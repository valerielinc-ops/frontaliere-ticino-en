Job ID: 41654 | Location: Pratteln, Canton of Basel-Landschaft, Switzerland | Work Model: Hybrid Clariant Position Grade: Level 10 Total Target Cash CHF180,000.00* Clariant is a focused, sustainable, and innovative specialty chemicals company headquartered near Basel, Switzerland, operating across more than 40 countries with a strong commitment to driving value through science and responsibility.

We are looking for a results-driven (Senior) Manager – National Tax & Tax Governance to join our Global Tax team within the Finance function.

In this role, you will take full ownership of Clariant's Swiss tax agenda while contributing to global tax governance, compliance, and process improvement initiatives.

Reporting directly to the Global Head of Tax and based at our site in Pratteln, Switzerland, you will play a pivotal role in shaping a robust and forward-looking tax framework for a multinational organization.

Responsibilities Swiss Tax: Own and manage Clariant’s overall Swiss tax position across all relevant taxes.

  • Cover corporate income tax, withholding tax, stamp duties, and the Swiss tax aspects of cash pooling and financing arrangements, including related compliance, assessments, audits, rulings, tax planning, and negotiations with tax authorities.
  • Serve as the primary point of contact for Swiss tax authorities and ensure that Swiss tax risks, positions, obligations, and opportunities are identified, evaluated, documented, and addressed.
  • Assess and manage the Swiss tax implications of group-wide activities, including transfer pricing, intercompany financing, cash pooling, restructurings, supply-chain changes, Pillar Two and Tax Governance: Own the end-to-end governance, strategic direction, supervision, and accountability for Clariant’s global minimum tax and Pillar Two process and coordinate selected tax-governance processes.
  • Set and maintain the Pillar Two methodology, governance, data flows, controls, timelines, review, escalation, and reporting framework.
  • Oversee and challenge calculations, tax-accounting inputs, and compliance work performed by local teams and external advisers, without directly executing each jurisdiction’s underlying calculations or filings.
