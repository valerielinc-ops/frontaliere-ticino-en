Main responsibilities

  • Together with the head of department, you coordinate daily business, act proactively and promote cross-departmental cooperation
  • You independently lead a team of 10 to 15 employees and ensure a smooth process
  • Active cooperation in the outgoing goods and in order picking is just as much a part of your tasks as the safe movement of goods by means of industrial handling equipment
  • Responsibility for the quick and correct storage of the goods in the right storage area
  • The performance indicators are continuously monitored and evaluated by you, and you also support the inventory together with your team.
  • All material movements are recorded in the system and delivery notes and loading papers are reliably processed
  • Work safety, order and cleanliness in the warehouse are ensured at all times and additional tasks are flexibly taken over Profile:
  • You have completed vocational training as a logistician EFZ or have at least five years of professional experience in logistics

  • Confident oral and written knowledge of German enables you to communicate clearly in everyday work
