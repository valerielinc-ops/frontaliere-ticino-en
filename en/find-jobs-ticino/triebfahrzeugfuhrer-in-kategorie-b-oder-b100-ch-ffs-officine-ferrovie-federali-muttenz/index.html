SBB | SBB Cargo International: Train driver category B or B100 (CH)

SBB Cargo International is the market leader on the north-south axis in European rail freight transport.

Thanks to our more than 1,000 employees in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Italy and France, we run around 550 trains every week from the North Sea ports to Italy and back.

Key requirements

  • You have a valid category B or B100 railway driving license.
  • If you have a qualification of category B100, you are ready to complete the training to category B.
  • You also have the willingness to qualify for at least one other network (Germany and/or France).
  • You work independently, reliably and in a solution- and performance-oriented manner.
  • Even in demanding situations, you maintain an overview and act responsibly.
  • You have good reaction and coordination skills and have very good knowledge of German.
  • Knowledge of Italian and French is an advantage.
  • We require you to be willing to work shifts and work on weekends and public holidays.
