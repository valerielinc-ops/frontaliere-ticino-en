Role overview

For our subsidiary Stardrinks AG, we are looking for an independent and motivated personality as Chauffeur Kat at the Fahrweid location.

C/E You are responsible for the punctual and on-demand delivery of all products from our range to our customers – mainly in the catering, hotel and retail sectors.

In doing so, you ensure that the vehicle is suitable for traffic in accordance with the requirements of the Road Traffic Act and that the legal provisions on loading (cargo securing, weight, weight distribution) are complied with.
