Company and context

  • The Organizational Development Directorate's mission is to lead institutional projects, optimize organization and flow, and strengthen quality and patient experience within the Riviera-Chablais Hospital.
  • Composed of experts in project management, organisational engineering, continuous improvement and quality management, it supports teams in implementing concrete solutions to improve operational performance and patient path.
  • At the heart of a modern and evolving hospital, the DDO is transforming itself into a strategic partner, capable of driving the major changes associated with major institutional projects, organizational innovation and the development of a global quality centered on patient experience.
  • This position is for those who share our belief that the health of the population is a collective responsibility, where every action is meaningful to make an ethical and lasting difference.
  • To complement the Organizational Development Directorate (DDO), we are looking for a: Project Manager M/F at 80%-100% The main tasks of this post are:
  • Lead cross-cutting and/or complex institutional projects by ensuring their integration into the institution's value chain and contribute actively to the achievement of the strategic plan.
  • Define objectives, scope, deliverables and milestones, plan and coordinate project phases and activities by integrating resource, time and budget constraints.
  • Establish, mobilise and coordinate multidisciplinary project teams, coordinate with external providers and manage tenders where necessary.
  • Monitor and control risks, boundary changes and interdependencies with other institutional projects, and ensure regular and factual reporting.
  • Listen to business needs, conduct studies and diagnoses, map processes and identify opportunities for optimization based on qualitative and quantitative data.
