A career at Lombard Odier means working for a renowned global wealth and asset manager, with a strong focus on sustainable investing. Business Transformation Manager – Operations (80-100%)

Operations is at the heart of the Bank's ability to deliver excellence to clients while adapting to an increasingly complex and evolving environment. To support our long-term ambition, we have launched the Operations 2030 Strategy , a transformative journey designed to build a more scalable, efficient, data-driven and future-ready organisation. This includes rethinking our operating model, accelerating the adoption of AI and automation, strengthening data quality and governance, modernising our technology landscape, and developing the skills needed for the future.

As a Business Transformation Manager, you will join the Operations Transformation Office, a small and highly visible team responsible for driving this strategic agenda. Working closely with senior leaders across Operations, Technology, Data Office, HR and Control Functions, you will help shape, coordinate and deliver initiatives that will have a lasting impact on the way Operations operates across the Bank.

This position offers a unique opportunity to combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution, while gaining broad exposure to senior stakeholders and key transformation priorities. Whether your background is in consulting, programme management or banking operations, you will play a central role in turning strategic ambitions into tangible business outcomes.

YOUR ROLE

As a Business Transformation Manager, you will act as a catalyst for change, ensuring that strategic initiatives are translated into measurable results.

You will

  • Drive the delivery of key initiatives within the Operations 2030 roadmap, from planning to implementation.
  • Coordinate complex, cross-functional programmes involving Operations, Technology, Data Office, HR and Control Functions.
  • Establish and maintain effective governance frameworks, steering committees and executive reporting mechanisms.
  • Monitor programme progress, milestones, risks, dependencies and value creation to ensure successful delivery.
  • Challenge existing processes and ways of working, identifying opportunities to simplify, optimise and enhance operational performance.
  • Facilitate executive decision-making through high-quality analyses, recommendations and presentations.
  • Support the implementation of initiatives related to operating model transformation, digitalisation, data, automation and AI.
  • Foster alignment and collaboration across diverse stakeholder groups in a matrix environment.
  • Contribute to change management efforts and help drive adoption across impacted teams.
