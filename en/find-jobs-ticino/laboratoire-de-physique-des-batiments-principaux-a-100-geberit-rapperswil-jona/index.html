Description

MAIN TASKS In our building physics laboratory, the foundations for innovative and high-quality Geberit products are created.

For the management of this competence center (acoustics, statics, fire protection) we are looking for a technically experienced and leadership-strong personality with a passion for building physics, innovation and interdisciplinary cooperation.

The function as Head Building Physics Laboratory includes in particular the following activities:

  • You manage the building physics laboratory in technical, business and human terms (3 employees)
  • You are responsible for the building physics know-how in the areas of acoustics, statics and fire protection and together with your team ensure professional measurements, tests and external fire tests including approvals
  • You actively drive innovation, bring new technologies and trends and make an important contribution to the further development of our products with innovation projects
  • As a competent contact person, you advise and support product development and sales in challenging building physics issues
  • You prepare technical results appropriate to the addressee and act as an interface to internal and external stakeholders
  • You maintain contact with universities and institutes and supervise and accompany interns ## PROFILE For this challenging and versatile task, you will bring a completed university degree (uni/ETH) in building physics, civil engineering or physics or comparable disciplines.
