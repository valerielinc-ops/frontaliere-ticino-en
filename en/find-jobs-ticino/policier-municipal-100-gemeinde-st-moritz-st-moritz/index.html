Description

For the municipal police of St. Moritz we are looking for you as a municipal policeman 100% (m/f) from now on or by agreement. Security as the basis of a tolerant and free society is the central concern of the police. The police departments in the Engadin are working around the clock for the protection and safety of the population. Police are firmly integrated and close to the population. This enables a high degree of personal exchange and creates a feeling of high security within the community. The municipal police of St. Moritz works closely with the cantonal police.
