Role overview

Tasks

  • Family-centered advice for pregnant women and mothers on the topic of breastfeeding and nutrition of the newborn in the women's clinic and children's hospital
  • Independent and structured day organization both in the outpatient consultation hours and in the stationary setting including patient documentation and lead the care process
  • Providing internal training and case discussions on breastfeeding, lactation, infant nutrition and aids
  • Coaching of employees on the stations
  • Close cooperation and regular, structured exchange with the experts of the Frauenklinik and Kinderspital as well as with the breastfeeding working group
  • Co-creating guidelines on breastfeeding, lactation, infant nutrition and breastfeeding aids

Requirements

  • Training as midwife or nurse FH / HF with the certificate breastfeeding and lactation consultant IBCLC or BSS
  • Professional experience in acute hospital in the field of postpartum bed
  • Strong organisational and communication skills
  • Joy, commitment and participation in multi-professional cooperation
