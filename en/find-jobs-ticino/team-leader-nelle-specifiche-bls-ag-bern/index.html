Original description

You make sure that our trains not only roll, but that every journey remains safe and reliable. With your team you set the pace for clear specifications and regulations - and thus ensure a strong foundation on which everyone can rely in railway operations. Your contribution? Mobility that creates trust.

Tasks

  • Managing a dedicated team professionally and personnelly
  • actively advancing the digital transformation of the area and developing processes, working methods and services
  • taking responsibility for the creation, development and updating of operating regulations, instructions and specifications
  • ensuring professional examination and approval of internal and external documents
  • actively participating in internal and external departments
  • ensuring timely distribution of regulations and specifications to driving personnel

Requirements

  • Completed technical studies and several years of experience in the railway environment
  • Safe handling of operational, regulatory and organizational conditions
  • Consulting competence and a clear view for complex relationships
  • Safe in the professional analysis and release of specifications and instructions
  • Very good German knowledge, French a plus
  • Pleasure to bear responsibility for a team and to design clear structures

We offer

  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st class or a half-taxabo.
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office.
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top- and job iv
  • There is the possibility to buy five to ten holiday days per year in addition
  • Financial support for external training as well as an exciting internal learning and course offer are available to you.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health.

About BLS With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second largest railway company.More than that: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight rail, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? The pride of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
