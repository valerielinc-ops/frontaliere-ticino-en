Employee / in sorting plant 60 - 80% (m/f/d)

Every year, the dress centre collects and sorts around 600 tons of used clothes and shoes for meaningful and targeted reuse at home and abroad. An important part of the collected clothes goes on sale in Switzerland. The operation includes the sorting plant, the warehouses, two textile sales stores and the administration.

Their tasks

  • Professional and organizational leadership as well as support of employees from different groups
  • Sorting of new and used second-hand clothing and shoes
  • Classification and categorisation of goods by size, sex, season and quality standards
  • Examination of incoming donations of clothes with regard to completeness, cleanliness and condition. Preparation of goods for transfer, storage or sale in shops: washing, labelling, merging, packaging
  • Promoting labour integration and supporting the induction and development of employees
  • Ensuring efficient, high-quality and smooth operations in the field of textile sorting and preparation
  • Co-responsibility for order, cleanliness and safety at work in the entire work area
  • Experience: No formal training required
  • Experience in stock, sorting or selling clothing / fashion knowledge of advantage
  • Physical capacity
  • Teamwork and reliability
  • Assumption of responsibility for sub-areas
  • Responsibility
  • Patience and social skills in dealing with volunteers, school classes, asylum seekers and non-profit workers
  • Good knowledge of German is advantageous
  • Interest in sustainability and recycling of textiles and footwear
  • Basic knowledge of merchandise, sorting processes and further education in the field of recycling and sustainability is advantageous
