Original description

Baloise Bank is part of the Helvetia Group – an innovative financial services provider that intelligently combines banking and insurance. We offer solutions that offer security and at the same time open up new paths. We are characterized by a unique culture in which the human being is at the center. We believe in the value of trust, respect and integrity. We are proud of an environment in which you can develop and contribute your ideas.

Tasks

  • Independent development of demanding, complex strategic financial planning for customers and employees of the bank as well as insurance against fee, including conducting the related customer meetings
  • Quality assurance in the team
  • Support for client advisors in the acquisition of potential financial planning clients and in the implementation of the concretely defined measures from financial planning
  • Professional support for client advisors
  • Active promotion of the “Financial Planning Plus” service within Helvetia
  • Presentations at regular customer events on the topic of "retirement planning", including event planning and organization as well as support and acquisition of the participants
  • Active participation in the further development of the “Financial Planning Plus” service within the bank and insurance

Requirements

  • Completed banking education or financial studies
  • Further education as a financial planner with federal certificate, federal dipl. Financial planning expert, CFP® or comparable qualification
  • Several years of in-depth professional experience (minimum 5–10 years) in financial planning or asset consulting
  • Strong expertise in the areas of investments, taxes, provision, financing and estate planning of complex issues (e.g. company succession)
  • Experience in dealing with demanding and wealthy customers
  • Strong analytical thinking and entrepreneurship
  • High customer orientation, trustworthiness and discretion
  • Very good communication and presentation skills
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method

We believe in cooperation at eye level, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
