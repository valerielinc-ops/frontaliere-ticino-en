Role overview

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. You are the central contact person for construction projects – advise, check, coordinate and accompany projects safely until successful implementation.

Your tasks

  • You advise clients and architects on planning, building law and architectural issues.
  • In doing so, you coordinate and carry out building permit procedures, examine and assess building applications professionally and prepare the corresponding applications to the city council.
  • In addition, you carry out construction inspections and acceptances and ensure a legally compliant implementation of the construction projects.

Your profile

  • You have an education in architecture, building or civil engineering (FH, higher technical school or comparable). Ideally, you have completed the module Construction at HSLU or are motivated to attend this course.
  • You are interested in planning and building law and work independently, structured and customer-oriented.
  • A safe appearance, teamwork, a quick comprehension as well as very good oral and written knowledge of German distinguish you.
  • In addition, you are digitally savvy and convince through organizational skills, initiative and a high resilience.

From the water tower to the KKL: The 25-strong team in the building permits department pays attention to the interaction between existing and new buildings while keeping an eye on the overall picture of the city. In exchange with building owners and planners, new buildings, conversions and the further development of monuments are implemented in high quality.
