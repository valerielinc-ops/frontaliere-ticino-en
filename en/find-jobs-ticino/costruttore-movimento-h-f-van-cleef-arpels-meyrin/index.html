Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities will include:
  • Designing and developing watch movements from concept to production.
  • Conducting feasibility studies and technical analyses.
  • Preparing detailed construction plans and bills of materials.
  • Performing simulations and tests to validate performance and reliability.
  • Collaborating with the design studio, casing department (case), and other builders (case, dial, and movement) to ensure the implementation of new movements.
  • Keeping up with technological and material advancements in watchmaking.
  • Participating in continuous improvement of design processes.
