Role overview

The Directorate-General for Mobility and Roads DGMR is seeking, in connection with the compliance under the Law on Equality for Persons with Disabilities (LHand) of bus stops and compliance with the Ordinance on Noise Protection (OPB), two project leaders specialists LHand and Noise, for its infrastructure division.

Within the noise section of the infrastructure division, planning and piloting projects related to LHand compliance of bus stops and compliance with the OPB Noise Protection Ordinance.

Ensure compliance with applicable orders, laws, regulations, directives, standards or procedures, in the context of the implementation of the directions chosen by management.
