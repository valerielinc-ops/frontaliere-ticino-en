Original description

With your tasks, FORTYSEVEN also becomes an oasis of pleasure

  • Preparation of high-quality, balanced and attractively presented food for the daily business of both restaurants and for events
  • Production and preparation of the mise-en-place as well as coordination of daily tasks with the kitchen management
  • Passionate, clean and professional work in front-cooking and in front of guests
  • Acceptance of goods including control of quantity, temperature, quality and weight as well as proper storage and marking
  • Regular stock control, product quality and expiration dates as well as support for orders
  • Consistent compliance with the HACCP concept, hygiene, quality and safety standards and cleaning plans
  • Appropriate management of allergies and intolerances and proper disclosure of relevant information
  • Cost-conscious handling of food, active reduction of food waste and correct waste separation according to the FORTYSEVEN system
  • Cleaning and disinfection of work surfaces and kitchen areas and reporting of defective equipment to kitchen management
  • Careful handovers for the following day as well as documentation of important information and guest feedback
  • Maintaining a friendly and professional guest contact as well as cooperation with other departments
  • Active participation in the further development of offers, processes and FORTYSEVEN standards

With your passion for fresh ingredients, you create well-being for the soul and palate.

What I want from you

  • Completed apprenticeship as chef/chef EFZ or EBA or a comparable qualification
  • At least one year of professional experience in a professional kitchen
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Other languages are beneficial
  • Sound expertise on hygiene, food safety, allergies and intolerances
  • Enjoy regular guest contact and clear, professional communication
  • Independent, efficient and resilient working method, even in the event of a higher workload
  • Open-minded, motivated, loyal, helpful and team-oriented personality
  • Interest in modern and health-oriented nutrition trends as well as high service and quality orientation
  • Cost awareness and responsible handling of goods, resources and infrastructure

Your wages

If you have the necessary knowledge and experience for this job, we would be happy to give you a fair salary offer between CHF 58’500 and CHF 65’000 (100% expense).

If you do not meet all the requirements, then we are also happy about your application. In this case, your starting wage may be slightly below that range.
