Role overview

Would you like to contribute your experience in the field of catering in the primary school of St.

Karli?

Support our team four to five days a week in the preparation of the food rooms, the delivery of meals and the necessary cleaning work – and together with us contribute to the welfare of children and adults.

  • Would you like to contribute your experience in the field of catering in the primary school of St.
  • Karli?
  • Together with the care team, you prepare the food room for the children and provide an inviting atmosphere during lunchtime.
  • You arrange meals in the Bain-Maries and accompany the food service for up to 30 children in the 3rd to 6th grade.

Main responsibilities

  • Together with the care team, you prepare the food room for the children and provide an inviting atmosphere during lunchtime.
  • You arrange meals in the Bain-Maries and accompany the food service for up to 30 children in the 3rd to 6th grade.
  • You contribute to a pleasant and appreciative food culture.
  • You support the children during lunchtime and are a friendly, reliable contact person in the dining room.
  • You take on independently incurred domestic work in the kitchen and dining sector.
  • After eating, you clean tables, chairs, work surfaces and the premises and ensure the proper cleaning of the Bain-Maries.
  • You ensure compliance with the cantonal hygiene guidelines and the hygiene concept of the elementary school in Lucerne and work responsibly according to the applicable standards.
  • You work mainly during the school weeks, an average of 9 hours per week, from 11.30 to about 2.30 p.m.
  • Your working days would be Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Requirements
  • Ideally, you will bring practical experience in the kitchen or housekeeping sector or enjoy working in this field.
