  • Work where others go on holiday.

Lenzerheide Bergbahnen AG connects people, nature and technology in one of the most impressive mountain worlds in Switzerland. With team spirit, innovation and passion, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests every day. Join our team and shape the future of mobility in the heart of the Alps.

Immediately or by agreement we are looking for you as

Sales Manager International

80 - 100% workload at Lenzerheide Bergbahnen AG

Your tasks

  • Active sale and positioning of the ski area in the focus markets Germany, Benelux and UK
  • Maintaining cooperation with service providers in the region in the field of international market development (tourism organisations, hotels, sports shops, etc. in Arosa and Lenzerheide)
  • Build, maintain and further develop long-term partnerships with tour operators, agencies and key accounts
  • Lead management and maintenance of business contacts in the CRM system
  • Identification of market potential, trends and new business opportunities in the international environment
  • Development and implementation of market-specific sales strategies to increase sales
  • Participation in B2B industry events and trade fairs, as well as planning and implementation of sales calls in the mentioned markets
  • In-depth experience in international market development, ideally in the tourism industry (mountain railways, destinations, hotels)
  • Demonstrable success in international sales and in building international partnerships
  • Entrepreneurial thinking coupled with strong implementation orientation and focus on results
  • Very good knowledge of the relevant markets, sales structures and partner landscapes
  • High communication and negotiation competence, secure appearance
  • Enthusiasm for alpine experiences and in particular skiing
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing, other languages are advantageous
  • Willingness to travel and flexibility in a dynamic, seasonal environment

As an annual employee: at Lenzerheide Bergbahnen AG you benefit from many benefits. If you have any questions, Manuela Pargätzi is at your disposal. We look forward to your application.
