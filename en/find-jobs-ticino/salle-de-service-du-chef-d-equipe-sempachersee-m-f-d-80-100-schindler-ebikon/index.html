Your new challenges

Do you have the motivation to develop into a leader, take responsibility, recognize opportunities and actively promote change? With us, you will lead a dedicated and diverse team of service technicians, stay close to daily business through regular assignments in the field (2-3 days per week) and at the same time actively shape the future of our service organization. Look forward to a varied management task, as a captain on the field, with a lot of creative leeway, in which your ideas are in demand and you can make a visible contribution to the success of Schindler together with your team.

  • Leadership, coaching and further development of a team of around 13 service employees
  • Ensuring high service quality and efficient and customer-oriented service provision
  • Promoting a positive team culture and supporting your employees in their professional and personal development
  • Regular assignments in the field for maintenance, troubleshooting and repairs to stay close to customers, employees and operations
  • Active support of the repair business by identifying and sharing repair potential
  • Monitoring relevant indicators and taking appropriate measures to continuously improve quality, productivity and customer satisfaction
  • Planning and organisation of the Pikett service including ensuring a reliable back-up regulation
  • Co-design and further development of processes, structures and organizational processes within the service area

Your ideal conditions

  • Basic technical training completed (e.g. polymechanic in, automotive mechatronics engineer in, plant and apparatus manufacturer in or electrical installer in)
  • Professional experience in the service, customer service or repair environment of elevator installations
  • First leadership experience or the motivation to make the next development step into a leadership function
  • Joy in motivating, developing and achieving success together
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and the willingness to take responsibility and actively shape change
  • Positive Creative Maker & Decision Maker Qualities
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Further language skills are advantageous

  • Diversity and Inclusion: A corporate culture that promotes diversity and sees different perspectives as strength.
