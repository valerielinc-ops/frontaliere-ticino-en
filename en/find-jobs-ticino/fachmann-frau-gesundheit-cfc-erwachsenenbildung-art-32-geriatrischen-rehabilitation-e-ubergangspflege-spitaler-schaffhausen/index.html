Role overview

In this two-year training, you will work on the station for geriatric rehabilitation and transitional care at the Cantonal Hospital Schaffhausen to learn the tasks of a health professional.

You will be accompanied and supported by your day caregivers every day.

In addition, you can focus on individual activities during regular learning days with education and specialists.
