Original description

  • Analyze and evaluate the organizational structure based on the strategic corporate goals and guidelines of the line
  • Manage organizational sub-projects and support organizational programs with regard to the further development of procurement ASTAB
  • Create, accompany and ensure concepts, standards and methods for the uniform handling of procurement / procurement projects and procurement strategies (automation and digitization of processes, simplification of processes, training & communication, innovation and risk management)
  • Elaborate, define and enforce policies and processes in sub-areas of sourcing (sourcing partial governance) taking into account the corporate strategy, structure and culture
  • Analyse, evaluate and implement tenders for the award of orders, service contracts and procurement in close cooperation with the recipients and on the basis of strategic corporate objectives
  • Further tasks in favour of operational and strategic procurement in accordance with the specifications of the line
  • University degree (uni / FH / ETH) or equivalent education and several years of professional experience, preferably in public procurement
  • High degree of independence, self-initiative, organizational and communication skills as well as pronounced qualities in process and specialist management
  • Knowledge of the procurement procedures and processes of the federal government (public administration) is advantageous as well as negotiating skills, assertiveness and very good knowledge of SAP and ActaNova
  • Experience in dealing with IT procurement challenges
  • Strong analytical and conceptual thinking skills as well as a high degree of service and customer orientation
  • Good active knowledge of a second official language and, if possible, passive knowledge of a third official language and passive knowledge of English
