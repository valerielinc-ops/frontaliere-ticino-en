Original description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Radiology with precision and responsibility – start your education with us! What makes us special? As a versatile peripheral hospital with heart and mind, we live a trusting coexistence and a working environment that is characterized by openness, respect and appreciation. Working in a modern way means for us: connecting high-tech with humanity, with freedom of design and an open ear for each other. Our employees are in the middle of us instead of just there – both professionally and personally. Family-friendly conditions and jointly designed planning are part of everyday life for us. Do you want to work in an environment where technical understanding, care and empathy are needed on a daily basis? With us you can expect an exciting and versatile education with insight into the latest imaging techniques such as X-ray, mammography, DL, CT and MRI as well as a collegial, supportive working environment in which you can develop professionally and personally. Then you are exactly right in our radiology! From September 2026 we offer a free apprenticeship place.

Creating precision, accompanying people – your field of activity in radiology As a radiology specialist HF / dipl. Radiology specialist HF you work with both people and with the latest medical technology. The interpersonal is just as central as dealing with high-precision cutting-edge technology. You look after and accompany patients, work together, think and take on responsible tasks in everyday diagnostics. Training as a radiology specialist HF or as a dipl. Radiology specialist HF takes three years. Theoretical and practical lessons take place in school blocks of several weeks on the medi as well as in several internships with us in radiology.

  • Patients are looked after professionally, safely and with heart – always oriented towards our values
  • You take responsibility for your learning process yourself – and actively shape your education – together with the vocational trainers
  • Step by step you develop into a qualified radiology specialist HF and strengthen your professional safe and empathetic working method in everyday hospital life.

You need this to be successful

  • Joy in dealing with people and interest in medicine
  • Good physical and psychological resilience
  • Skill in handling technical equipment as well as an exact and careful working method
  • Good spatial imagination
  • Self-employed, responsible and reliable working methods
  • Team spirit and the willingness to make a difference together
  • Higher secondary education (high school, secondary school FMS or 3-year vocational training or equivalent)
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
