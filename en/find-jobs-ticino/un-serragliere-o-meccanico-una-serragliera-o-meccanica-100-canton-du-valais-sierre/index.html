Role overview

A locksmith or mechanic (100%) with the Territorial Unit III Service (SUTIII), in Charrat and Sierre.

The Territorial Unit III Service is one of 11 units in Switzerland responsible for maintaining national roads.

SUTIII employs around one hundred employees who ensure 24/7 safety and operation of national roads in Valais.
