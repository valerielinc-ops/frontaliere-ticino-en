We are a dedicated Squad on a mission to redefine Swiss watchmaking, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision we create timepieces that combine modern-retro design with casual, approachable, and sustainable luxury — powered by passionate teams around the world. Join our vibrant team in Bern as a Sales Associate and be part of something extraordinary! Your Future Team: We are a team of 4 and are currently looking for our next colleague to complete our squad! You will work at Marktgass-passage, Breitling boutique in Bern. This is more than a Sales Associate role; it is an opportunity to share your knowledge of Breitling, its heritage, and its exceptional timepieces, while showcasing the craftsmanship and expertise of our watchmakers. Your mission will be to build and nurture lasting customer relationships by delivering an exceptional level of service and creating memorable brand experiences.
