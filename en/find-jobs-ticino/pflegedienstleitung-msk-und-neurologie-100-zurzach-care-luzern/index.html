Original description

Our rehabilitation clinic Sonnmatt offers specialized musculoskeletal and neurological rehabilitation at a high medical level. Through targeted diagnostics and individually tailored therapy programs, our interdisciplinary team ensures that our patients are optimally looked after on their way back to better health and quality of life. The first-class hotel ambience in the most beautiful viewing position above the Lake Four Forest combines maximum comfort with high therapeutic quality.

Your tasks

  • Leadership: Responsible for the personnel, professional and administrative management of our nursing team (40-50 people), the training managers and pharmaceutical assistants in the Sonnmatt rehabilitation clinic together with your two group leaders on two floors (musculoskeletal and neurological rehabilitation)
  • Design possibilities: As a member of the squad team, you play a decisive role in the design and further development of the location
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Work in an interprofessional team closely with other nurses, therapists and doctors
  • Care: Take responsibility for high-quality care with the care team

Your profile

  • Training: You have completed training as a qualified nurse/nurse specialist HF/FH with appropriate professional qualification (BAB)
  • Leadership experience: You already have experience leading a care team in the Swiss health care system
  • Personality: You enjoy working in an interprofessional team, thinking in a networked way and always looking for solution-oriented approaches. Your positive, communicative nature and your pronounced quality awareness characterize you as a personality
  • IT skills: You are well versed in dealing with common computer programs (Outlook, Clinical Information System (KIS), Planning Software Polypoint (PEP))

What we offer you

  • Co-creation: Work in a quiet working environment in the green and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: Receive a generous share of the costs of education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers or car dealers as well as a membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other perks at the FORTYSEVEN spa.

Are you ready for the next step in your career? Then we look forward to your application via online tool and to welcome you soon in our team!
