Introduction For our Production Division , we are looking for an Assembly Mechanic (H/F) whose main mission will be to guarantee the operational technical support of proximity to production operators. Responsibilities

  • First level intervention on technical hazards and coordination, if necessary, of the relays to the competent interlocutors
  • Ensure availability, proper use and monitoring of equipment, tools and consumables
  • Conduct field interventions: troubleshooting, adjustments, audits and 5S application control
  • Monitor indicators within its scope and contribute to continuous improvement
  • Participate in the development projects of the plateau by bringing its operational expertise. Profile
  • Holder, at least, of an AFP in a technical field
  • Professional experience in the desired industrial environment, ideally acquired in the watchmaking sector
  • Mastery of French, both oral and written
  • Good ease in using common computer tools
  • Excellent relational qualities and good communication skills
  • Ability to listen, analyse and understand situations
  • Autonomy, sense of responsibility and ability to step back. Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
