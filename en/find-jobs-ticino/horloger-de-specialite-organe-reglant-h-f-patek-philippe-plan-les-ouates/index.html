Role overview

Mission

As part of this function, attached to the Watchmaking Division, you will be responsible for carrying out the operations of virolating, pitoning and shaping the spiral on any type of steel spiral balance (flat and with Breguet curve from 1839 to the present) according to the Patek Philippe quality criteria.

In parallel, you will guarantee the know-how on the steel coils that you will pass on in the course of training.

  • As part of this function, attached to the Watchmaking Division, you will be responsible for carrying out the operations of virolating, pitoning and shaping the spiral on any type of steel spiral balance (flat and with Breguet curve from 1839 to the present) according to the Patek Philippe quality criteria.
  • Traditionally perform virolalage, pitoning and spiral shaping (flat spiral or Breguet curve).
