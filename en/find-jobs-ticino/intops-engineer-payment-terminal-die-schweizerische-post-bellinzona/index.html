Application process

  • You are responsible for application management for payment terminals and, in cooperation with internal and external partners, ensure smooth interface communication with the POS system.
  • The support and further development of the IT infrastructure, especially in the area of payment terminals, printers, client and network infrastructure, is one of your core tasks.

  • You will analyze complex problems in a structured manner, quickly identify connections and find efficient solutions.
  • In addition, you communicate with the addressee, take responsibility and manage several IT services at the same time in a dynamic environment, which allows you to bring your strengths optimally.
