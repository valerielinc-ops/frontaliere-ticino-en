Place of work: Hirslanden Salem Hospital | Bern

Occupation by: immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 69057

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Your Advantages in Us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in fitness subscription

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • Together with the team, you receive our patients, visitors and customers, represent the clinic and create a benevolent atmosphere.
  • You are the first point of contact, work closely with all departments and are responsible for an optimal flow of information (reception, patient admission, emergency, doctors and bed departments)
  • The operation of the telephone exchange is just as much a part of your tasks as various administrative tasks
  • Together with the team you cover early and late services as well as the weekends

Your profile

  • You are an experienced receptionist with several years of professional experience
  • Assignments in the evening as well as on weekends you are used to
  • Openness to new things, high flexibility and commitment are a matter of course for you
  • Communicating in German and French does not bother you (every other language, especially English and Italian, is an advantage)
  • You have good PC user knowledge and a technical flair for various operating systems
  • You are the first contact person and distinguish yourself through a high customer orientation and a well-kept appearance
  • With you, our reception team gains a team-capable and loyal personality, which is flexible and resilient and enjoys dealing with people

For more information, please contact Bruno Pereira Rico, Head of Guest Services Hirslanden Bern at bruno.pereirarico@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.
