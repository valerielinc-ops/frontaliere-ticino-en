Role overview

Mission

As part of this function, attached to our Movement Components Division, you will manage a workshop made up of Mechanic-Regulators and Operators in their production activities in décolletage and termination.

In close collaboration with the other workshops of the Wheels service and support services to ensure the production plan in the quality criteria and standards Patek Philippe, you will be responsible for achieving the results and facilitating the Experience Collaborator.
