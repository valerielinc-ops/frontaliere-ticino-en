Do you have extensive experience in Corporate Real Estate and Facility Management within Switzerland?? Would you like to take responsibility for ABB's Facility Management operations across Switzerland and lead a major FM transformation program? Then join our team in Baden as Lead Facility Management Switzerland .

In this key leadership role, you will be responsible for ABB's Facility Management operations across Switzerland, overseeing a nationwide portfolio of approximately 240,000 sqm of office, production, R&D and logistics space across multiple locations. You will oversee the delivery of integrated FM services through external partners, drive operational excellence and cost optimization, and play a key role in shaping the future FM operating model for ABB Switzerland.

Your

  • Responsibilities

- Lead Facility Management service delivery across ABB's Swiss portfolio , acting as the primary interface with the integrated FM provider and ensuring contractual compliance, governance, KPI achievement, and service excellence.

- Drive the completion of ABB's national FM transformation , including finalization of the FM tender, transition planning, and successful mobilization of the new FM partner across all Swiss locations.

- Optimize operational performance, compliance and cost efficiency , ensuring adherence to regulatory, HSE and ABB standards while delivering continuous improvements in service quality, sustainability and user experience.

- Support strategic real estate and facilities objectives , leveraging data-driven insights, stakeholder collaboration, and project leadership to enhance portfolio performance and deliver facility investments.

Your Background

- Bachelor or Master's degree in Facility Management, Real Estate, Engineering, Business Administration or a related field.

- Approximately 10 years of experience in Corporate Real Estate and/or Corporate Facility Management within Switzerland, preferably within a large multi-site organization.

- Proven experience managing outsourced FM contracts, external service providers, and integrated FM service delivery models.

- Experience leading FM transformation, outsourcing, mobilization, or transition projects, with strong project management skills and the ability to drive complex cross-functional initiatives.

- Strong understanding of operational Facility Management, contract management, performance monitoring, budgeting, forecasting, and cost optimization.

- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to influence and collaborate effectively across all organizational levels; proactive, customer-focused, and solution-oriented mindset.

- Fluency in German and English is required; experience driving digital transformation initiatives in Facility Management, including AI, smart buildings, predictive maintenance, workflow automation, and advanced analytics, is a strong advantage.

Why Join Us?

This is a unique opportunity to shape the future of Facility Management at ABB Switzerland. Beyond managing a significant real estate portfolio, you will lead the transition to a new integrated FM delivery model and establish the foundation for long-term operational excellence across the country.
