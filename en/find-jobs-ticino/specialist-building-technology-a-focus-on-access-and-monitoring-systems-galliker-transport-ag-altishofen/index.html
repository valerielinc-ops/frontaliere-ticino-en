Specialist in building technology (a) - focus on access and monitoring systems

Our diverse logistics buildings and building technology systems are monitored and controlled by a central department at the Altis-JOen-We are looking for you as a specialist building technology (a) with a focus on access and monitoring systems to support the various systems and the processing of projects in this area.

Field of responsibility

  • Support and development of access and authorization systems
  • Monitoring, optimization and assurance of the operation of temperature and monitoring systems
  • Maintenance, management and control of various building technology systems
  • Conducting fault searches and repair of faults at building technology installations
  • Planning, coordination and implementation of projects and support of project management

Requirements profile

  • Completed training in building technology, ideally with focus on electrical or access control systems
  • enthusiasm for building technology and technical connections
  • Self-employed, structured and solution-oriented working methods
  • Responsible, reliable and able to use the team
  • Good IT user knowledge of advantage

Offer

  • Varied task in an innovative logistics environment with Swiss-wide activity
  • Large scope for action and autonomous work
  • Design scope for technical projects and optimizations
  • Modern infrastructure and collegial working environment
  • Possibility to develop yourself as a project manager with perception of the client representation in new and reconstruction projects
