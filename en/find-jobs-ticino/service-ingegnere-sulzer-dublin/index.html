Original description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Are you passionate about engineering and looking for an exciting opportunity to grow your career? We have a vacancy at our Wexford Service Centre for a Service Engineer to join our dedicated team. This role involves servicing and installing industrial, municipal, and commercial wastewater and stormwater pumping equipment. Key Responsibilities:

  • Planned Inspections & Maintenance : Conduct effective planned inspections, fault finding, and maintenance of pumping equipment at customer sites.
  • Ownership & Initiative : Take an active role and ownership of your responsibilities.
  • Technical Expertise : Demonstrate strong skills in the service and repair of wastewater equipment.
  • Reporting : Complete repair, service, and technical reports accurately and timely.
  • Work Environment : Support the company's drive to create a better working environment.
  • Client Communication : Communicate effectively and liaise with our client base to ensure satisfaction and service excellence.

What We Are Looking For

  • Mechanical Ability : Strong mechanical skills and aptitude.
  • Health & Safety : A dedicated approach to health and safety practices.
  • Computer Skills : Proficiency in computer skills for reporting and communication.
  • Interpersonal Skills : Strong interpersonal and customer service skills.
  • Team Player : Ability to work alone or as part of a team, under pressure and to deadlines.
  • Attention to Detail : Excellent attention to detail in all aspects of the role
  • Applicants must hold a full manual driving license. Automatic‑only licenses cannot be accepted for this role.

What we offer you

  • A competitive salary based on experience.
  • An interesting and rewarding position with opportunities for career progression.
  • Performance related bonus scheme related to personal and company performance paid end Q1 each year.
  • Defined contribution pension scheme.
  • Access to our Employee Assistance Programme.
  • Additional ongoing training to enhance your skills.
  • A real opportunity to grow your career with a global engineering company
  • Working hours Mon-Fri hours 8:30-5:00 with flexi time depending on workloads.
