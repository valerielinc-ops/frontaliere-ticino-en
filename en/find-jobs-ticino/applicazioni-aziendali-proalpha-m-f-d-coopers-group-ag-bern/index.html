Role overview

For an established international customer from the industrial production environment, we are looking for an experienced personality in the field of business applications.

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for the operation and further development of the ERP system (proALPHA) and the BI landscape (Qlik Sense)
  • Analysis, configuration and optimization of business processes along the entire value chain
  • Coordination of application support (2nd/3rd level) as well as cooperation with external service providers
  • Collaboration on release changes, system upgrades and international rollouts
  • Support for digitalization and process optimization projects
  • Ensuring system stability, data quality and authorization management
  • Participation in planning and implementing the global business application roadmap
  • Participation in project and portfolio management in the field of business applications
  • Close cooperation with specialist areas to implement requirements in a structured manner

Your profile

  • Completed training or studies in business informatics, computer science or business administration
  • Several years of experience in the field of ERP or business applications, ideally in the industrial environment
  • In-depth knowledge of proALPHA ERP and BI platforms (e.g. Qlik Sense or similar)
  • Experience in system operation, customizing, support and the analysis of business processes
  • Understanding end-to-end processes in production, logistics, purchasing, quality and finance
  • Knowledge of project management (classic or agile)
  • Experience with release and upgrade projects is an advantage
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • International travel readiness of about 10-20%
  • Experience in managing or coordinating an application team is an advantage.
