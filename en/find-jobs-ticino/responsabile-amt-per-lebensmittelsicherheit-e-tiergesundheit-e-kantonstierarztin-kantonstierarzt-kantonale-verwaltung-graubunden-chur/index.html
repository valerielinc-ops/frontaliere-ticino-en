Role overview

The Food Safety and Animal Health Office is a department in the Department of Economics and Social Affairs.

In the cantons of Graubünden and Glarus, it ensures protection against animal diseases, enforcement of animal welfare and control of primary animal production.

In order to protect against unsafe and misleading food and consumer goods, it also monitors the production and distribution chains of the affected establishments.
