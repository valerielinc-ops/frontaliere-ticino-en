Main responsibilities

  • We are looking for seasonal security staff for the Billionaire Dinner Show for the 2026/2027 winter season. | Key Responsibilities:
  • Ensuring the safety of our international VVIP guests, performers and staff with the utmost discretion.
  • Monitoring entrances and VIP areas during the dinner show and subsequent events.
  • Carrying out regular patrols in the event area and securing escape and rescue routes.
  • Acting with confidence and courtesy in conflict situations whilst adhering to internal service standards.
  • Acting as the first point of contact in the event of medical emergencies or security-related incidents, working closely with the hotel management and local authorities.

  • Fluency in German and English; knowledge of other languages (particularly Italian, French or Russian) is a strong advantage.
