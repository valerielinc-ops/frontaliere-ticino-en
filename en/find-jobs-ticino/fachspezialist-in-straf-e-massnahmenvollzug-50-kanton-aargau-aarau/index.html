Original description

Assistant for medical study - Ausschreibung L’EOC, the multisite Ticino hospital, is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of more than 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing more than 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team of medical secretariats and outpatients of the Regional Hospital of Locarno la Carità we look for a Medical Study Assistant (50-70%) Profile: The person we would like to meet is flexible and can adapt promptly to different situations. Cordial and sociable, shows a strong proactive attitude. In addition to the autonomous management of medical-delegated acts, it has a good mastery of the main secretariat tasks (welcome the patient, drafting medical reports, billing, managing appointments). The ideal candidate is particularly sensitive in understanding the needs of the user and believes, like us, in the importance of putting the patient at the center of our attentions. It also understands the importance of confidentiality and professional secrecy.

  • Requirements: Federal Certificate of Medical Study Assistant
  • Good Computer Knowledge (Microsoft Office) and typographical
  • Italian mother tongue and good oral knowledge of German. Date of entry: to be agreed. For any questions or clarifications, the Head of the Medici Secretariats of the Regional Hospital of Locarno is available at 091 811 47 54.
