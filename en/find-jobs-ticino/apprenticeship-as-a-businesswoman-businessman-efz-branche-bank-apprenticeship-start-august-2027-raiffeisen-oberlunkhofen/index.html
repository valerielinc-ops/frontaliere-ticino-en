Original description

Do you value a solid basic education? Are you a well-established and enthusiastic personality who has good manners, an interest in banking and the pleasure of customer contact? Then you may soon be our new team member.

Tasks

During your three-year training, you will gain insight into different departments and will be employed in the following work situations:

  • Receiving and forwarding bank customers
  • Banking clients advise
  • Supporting bank customers via digital channels
  • Assistance in advising customers
  • Administrative preparation and processing of banking transactions

In the various departments, you will be accompanied and supported by competent and experienced practical trainers. You can complete the training with us either in the EFZ advanced basic education or with vocational maturity.

Requirements

School

  • Completed folk level at top school level with good grades
  • Very good knowledge of German, oral & written
  • Requirements for learning two foreign languages
  • With professional maturity: Very good performance at top school level, passed entrance examination

Personal

  • Contactful and communicative
  • Joy of learning
  • Teamwork
  • Reliability and exact work
  • Open to new

Interests

  • Enjoyment of everyday banking and customer contact
  • Trends and developments in the professional field and in the industry
  • Application of information sources and use of new technologies

We offer

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.
