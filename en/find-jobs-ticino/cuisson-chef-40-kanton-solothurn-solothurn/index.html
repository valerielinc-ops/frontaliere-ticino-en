Role overview

The Office for Transport and Civil Engineering (AVT) is responsible for the further development and value preservation of the cantonal roads and paths as well as their operational maintenance.

It plans and orders public regional and local transport and approves transport measures on municipal roads.

The aim is to ensure performance-appropriate, safe and sustainable transport mobility in the canton.
