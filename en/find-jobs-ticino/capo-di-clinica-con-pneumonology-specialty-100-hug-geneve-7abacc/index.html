Role overview

As a head or head of clinic with a Pneumonology degree, you ensure all the clinical activities of the service and evolve in a university environment, at the interface of care, teaching and research.

Thanks to a quarterly tournus within the various specialized units, you develop a diverse expertise covering both hospital practice and ambulatory with versatility.

You are involved in the management of interstitial pneumonitis, pulmonary complications in immunocompromised patients or patients, pulmonary transplantation, interventional pneumonology and sleep respiratory disorders, in close collaboration with the Sleep Medicine Centre.

Company and context

  • Thanks to your organizational sense and ability to prioritize, you effectively ensure your clinical activities while responding to multiple demands (patients and patients, telephones, correspondence, meetings).
  • You ensure that you maintain a clear, caring and appropriate communication with patients and patients, their relatives and with all the professionals involved in their care, adapting your speech to their understanding.
  • By relying on your team spirit, you show solidarity with the unexpected.
  • As head or head of clinic, you are actively involved in the supervision and training of internal doctors in the Department of Medicine in the field of pneumonology, adopting a mentoring posture and sharing your expertise.
