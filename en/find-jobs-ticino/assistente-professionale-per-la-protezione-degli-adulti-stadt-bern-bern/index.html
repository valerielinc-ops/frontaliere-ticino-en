Original description

Responsiblely accompanying, supporting and creating solutions

In the Office for Adult and Child Protection, you work in a dedicated and collegial team that shares responsibility and supports each other.

After a comprehensive training, you accompany and represent people who are dependent on support.

You want to accompany people and make a difference with your work? Then we are looking for you as a social worker in adult protection!

Tasks

  • Self-employed management of adult protection assistance
  • Advice and support of cared for persons in different situations
  • Management of income and wealth and the establishment of social security rights
  • Client-oriented cooperation with staff
  • Cooperation with public authorities, specialised bodies and the social environment

Requirements

  • Studying in social work or comparable degree
  • Experience in the social field, preferably in the field of adult protection
  • Knowledge in social security
  • Networked working methods, load capacity, great organizational skills and very good communication skills
