Original description

The Building Construction Office of the Canton of St.Gallen plans, builds and operates buildings that the canton needs to fulfil its tasks. The real estate portfolio is more diverse than any other and includes educational, health, cultural, agricultural, security and administrative buildings.

What to expect

  • Planning, organisation and coordination of furniture procurement for the central administration of the Canton of St. Gallen
  • Ensuring standardization and compliance with agreed contractual services in furniture management
  • Contracting, controlling and coordinating suppliers, craftsmen and service providers for new acquisitions, removals and repairs
  • Guidance, maintenance and ongoing updating of the furniture inventory
  • Independent support of assigned properties in object management
  • Planning, coordination and support of maintenance projects from demand assessment to order processing

What sets it apart

  • Completed vocational training (EFZ) in the field of planning, carpentry, interior design or a similar specialisation
  • In-depth knowledge of construction and real estate, ideally supplemented by further training in facility management, furniture management or interior design
  • Strong spatial imagination
  • Very good MS Office skills and high IT affinity
  • Experience with CAFM systems is an advantage
  • Organizational skills, service orientation and enjoyment of coordinating different stakeholders
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Flexibility, resilience and a high level of initiative

What we offer

  • Freedom of design in the further development of furniture management
  • Versatile and varied tasks
  • Self-employment and personal responsibility
  • Collegial and motivated team
  • Contemporary employment conditions with good further education and training opportunities
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Location: St. Gallen
  • Application via the official job portal of the Canton of St. Gallen (https://www.sg.ch/ueber-den-kanton-st-gallen/employer-canton-stgallen/job portal.html)
