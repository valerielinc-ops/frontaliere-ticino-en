Original description

Place of work: Hirslanden BernOccupation by: Immediately or by appointment Type of employment: Indefinite reference number: 67519

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

The physiotherapy team is an essential part of the interdisciplinary sports medicine competence center "Medical Center Wankdorf (MCW)".

The comprehensive portfolio ranges from prevention, check-up, diagnostics (radiology and sports laboratory), medical consultation hours around the musculoskeletal system, sports physiotherapy to performance diagnostics and rehabilitation. The physiotherapy is composed of three specialist teams and is responsible for the optimal care of the inpatient patients in the MCW as well as in the Hirslanden Clinics Beau-Site and Salem Spital in Bern. To complement our team, we are looking for a Dipl for inpatient physiotherapy. Physiotherapist.

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Training opportunity in your own fitness area
  • Discount on internal massages

YOUR TASKS

  • You treat inpatients from various specialties (heart, thoracic and visceral surgery, internal medicine, IPS) at the Beau-Site Bern
  • In addition, you are interested in other specialist areas, in particular orthopaedics and spinal surgery, the majority of which are located in the Salem Hospital.
  • Optional (only for a minimum amount). 60%) the treatment of outpatients from different specialist areas, mainly in the musculoskeletal area, is also one of your tasks
  • You are responsible for the professional and independent care of your patients taking into account individual needs
  • Together with your team you perform weekend services on a regular basis

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a physiotherapist (Diplom HF, FH, SRK recognition)
  • In addition, you ideally have experience in the treatment of inpatient and outpatient patients
  • Further developments in the areas of manual lymph drainage and/or internal medicine/IPS are advantageous
  • You value teamwork, are flexible, resilient and have a motivated personality
