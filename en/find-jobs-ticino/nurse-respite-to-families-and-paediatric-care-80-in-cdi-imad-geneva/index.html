Original description

Attached to the family respite team which extends its mission of supporting families by taking charge and developing pediatric care services, the nurse provides home care for children requiring technical care usually provided in a hospital environment. Your missions and responsibilities

  • Offer and guarantee individualized care to children, taking into account physical, psychological, emotional, spiritual, social and cultural needs
  • Exercise a referent role and make decisions in the context of interventions with families and children
  • Engage concretely in accompaniment and support for families by developing a bond of trust with each member of the family and promoting exchanges
  • Coordinate support with our partners within the network
  • Participate in the promotion of clinical practices in an interprofessional approach in order to promote the diversification and quality of services in complex situations
  • Keep your skills up to date in line with developments in professional practices
  • Participate in the institutional mission aimed at promoting home care in a safe and stimulating environment for children.
  • Bachelor’s degree in nursing or training deemed equivalent, supplemented by post-graduate training in the field of child, adolescent and family health
  • Experience in caring for families in crisis situations
  • Very good interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work independently
  • Hospital experience in pediatric care
  • Specific theoretical knowledge related to the impacts of illness on the family, such as the Calgary model and the systems approach
  • Contribute to the evolution and diversification of the team’s activities
  • Ability to work interdisciplinarily and interprofessionally with the healthcare network
  • Mobility throughout the canton, driving license required.
