Original description

PROFILE

  • Completed degree at university level (FH/university) in systems engineering, mechatronics, mechanical engineering or a comparable discipline
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of rail vehicles or in an industrial environment
  • In-depth understanding of mechanical and electrical systems as well as their integration into complex overall projects
  • Negotiation-proof German and good English skills (at least level B2) in word and writing
  • High personal responsibility and strong communication and coordination skills

TASKS

  • Responsibility for the technical support of the entire development process of door systems, from the concept phase to the customer’s type approval
  • Collection, analysis and coordination of customer, vehicle and system-specific requirements for the door system
  • Creation of system specifications, technical concepts and solutions taking into account customer requirements, norms and internal standards
  • Coordination and control of the implementation of the door system in close cooperation with internal departments, customers and suppliers
  • Assuming full system responsibility for the door system in terms of function, performance, quality, costs and appointments
  • Ensuring the technical integration of the door system and supporting reviews, validations, tests and approvals throughout the development process
