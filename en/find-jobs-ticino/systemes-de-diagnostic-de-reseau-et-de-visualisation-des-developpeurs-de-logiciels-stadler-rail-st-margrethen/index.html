Original description

PROFILE

  • Completed studies (FH/uni) in computer science, application development or a comparable field of study
  • Alternatively, a completed vocational training as a computer scientist: in EFZ field of application development with corresponding professional experience
  • Several years of experience in object-oriented programming, preferably with Java
  • Knowledge of Linux and GUI development is beneficial
  • Practical experience in the field of network and communication technologies

  • Technical support for sales, product management and project management
  • Technical support and support of diagnostic systems in vehicle projects

Your further development opportunities in the field of cyber security

  • Possibility to take responsibility for cyber security issues in the vehicle environment, depending on interest and personal development
  • Cooperation in the analysis of security requirements and the preparation of risk and threat analyses
  • Support in the definition, implementation and further development of security concepts for diagnostic and communication systems
  • Support of vehicle projects in the integration of cyber security requirements in close cooperation with engineering, customers and internal departments
  • Continuous expansion of expertise in a future-oriented and growing technology field
