Role overview

We are a growing Swiss company, enthusiastic about innovation and technology.

We specialize in producing small and medium-sized high-performance tools for the processing of the most difficult materials such as stainless steels, heat-resistant alloys and titanium.

In one of the most modern technological centers for micro-processing, holistic processes are also developed - also in the context of customer projects.

Application process

  • Technical junior salesman with knowledge of the Swiss-German language Apply Division Tool Function Sales/Marketing Related location Switzerland, Agno More than Tools Mikron Tool is international leader in the development and production of hard metal cutting tools.
  • During the first 12 months, the candidate will be included in the various technical and productive departments, with the aim of gaining in-depth knowledge of tungsten carbide cutting tools, manufacturing processes, applications and customer requirements.
