Original description

Work Flexibility: Onsite

You are a person who is friendly, likes to take responsibility and likes to work independently? We offer you a challenging training period with a versatile, practical and theoretical training at the workplace. You expect varied and interesting activities. In addition, you gain insights into various departments of our company. As a balance to vocational school and training company, we ensure a good exchange between our trainees with exciting activities.

This is what awaits you

  • In the 4-year training as a polymechanic, you will learn skills for manual and machine manufacturing of workpieces and components from different materials.
  • The focus of your training is the production of high-precision implants by turning, milling and drilling using computer-controlled machine tools (CNC).
  • In addition to the realization of the components, the operating, setting up, programming and maintenance of machines are also part of your training content.
  • You run through different production departments using a tailor-made through-plan, whereby we offer you the possibility of active participation.
  • In addition to training courses and the possibility of participating in interdisciplinary project teams in small location projects, you will receive regular feedback to support your personal development.

That's what makes you feel good

  • You have a good school leaving certificate.
  • You have fun in mathematics and physics.
  • A strong interest in technical issues as well as a high degree of care and organizational talent distinguish you.
  • You like to work in the team and want to contribute to our success with your skills.
  • Craft skills and good spatial imagination complete your profile.

We offer

  • Helpful colleagues & a friendly working environment
  • In-house training workshop with modern machines
  • Intensive exam preparation & varied run-through plan

Your application: Diversity is important to us. We are pleased to receive applications from people regardless of their ethnic, national or social origin, gender, disability, age or sexual identity.

We look forward to receiving your meaningful online application including all relevant documents. Please note that in our application process under the item "Curriculum Vitae, directly upload all attachments as one file.
