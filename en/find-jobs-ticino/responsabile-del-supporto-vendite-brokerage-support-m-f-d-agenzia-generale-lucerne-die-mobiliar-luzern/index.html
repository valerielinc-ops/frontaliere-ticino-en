Role overview

Tasks - As Head of Sales Support, you are responsible for the management, organization and further development of sales and broker support of the general agency. You ensure efficient and high-quality sales support, develop processes and structures and support the professional and personal development of employees. - You manage and develop the central hub between customers, broker partners, our specialist departments, the management and our insurance and pension advisors.

With the help of your team leaders, you will develop your department with 16 employees, ensure professional collaboration with the brokers, and be responsible for the quality and achievement of your departmental goals. As a member of the executive board of the general agency, you will actively work on the further development of the general genture. - As an organizational and executive talent, you are a role model for your team. You will ensure the professional development of your team and support your colleagues in their daily work.

  • Self-employed, customer-oriented and entrepreneurial working methods
  • Authentic, winning and loyal personality
  • Flowing German skills, knowledge in other languages are a plus
