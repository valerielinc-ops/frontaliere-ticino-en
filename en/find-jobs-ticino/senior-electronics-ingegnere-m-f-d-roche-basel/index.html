Application process

  • In the role of Senior Electronics Engineer (m/f/d) , you will take charge of the conceptualization and circuit design of analogue and digital assemblies for diagnostic devices in medical applications.
  • Building prototypes and demonstrators, conducting systematic troubleshooting at the assembly and component levels, and performing EMC pre-compliance testing will be part of your daily routine.
  • You will also actively contribute to risk analyses and Design FMEAs, implementing technical safety measures directly into the electronics design.
  • Additionally, you can look forward to the following exciting tasks:
  • Specifications & System Requirements: Define electronics specifications and interface documentation, actively contributing to global system requirements.
  • Standards-Compliant Design (IEC 60601): Design circuits in compliance with DIN EN 60601-1 (electrical safety, isolation concepts, MOPP/MOOP, clearance and creepage distances) and DIN EN 60601-1-2 (EMC).
  • Approval & CE Compliance: Prepare, execute, and document standards-relevant tests to support external CE certifications in accredited testing laboratories.
  • Process & Interface Collaboration: Create technical documentation, oversee the transition of designs into volume production (DFM/DFT), and coordinate closely with adjacent technical disciplines.

Additional details

  • From the initial concept to series production, we work hand in hand.
  • Mentoring: Conduct design reviews and support junior team members with your technical expertise.
