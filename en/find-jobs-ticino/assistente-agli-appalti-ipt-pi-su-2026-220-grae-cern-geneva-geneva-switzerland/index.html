Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Process, place, and follow up on purchase orders up to 3 000 CHF, ensuring compliance with CERN's Procurement Rules.
  • Request quotations from suppliers and conduct comparative analysis of offers received, supporting sound and cost-effective purchasing decisions.
  • Negotiate commercial terms: payment terms, delivery schedule and transport conditions, and general conditions.
  • Liaise with suppliers to resolve delivery delays, non-conformities, purchase order amendments and invoicing issues.
  • Liaise with technical officers to resolve discrepancies, inconsistencies, and operational issues arising during/ post purchase order processing, ensuring timely and accurate resolution.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders to ensure efficient procurement operations.
  • Provide direct support to suppliers throughout the registration process in CERN's supplier database / eProcurement, ensuring the accurate verification, maintenance, and completeness of supplier records.

Key requirements

  • Experience or exposure to procurement, purchasing, or contract administration (through studies, internships, or initial work experience).
  • Prior exposure to an international and multicultural working environment. Skills:
  • Excellent communication, being able to resolve supplier claims.
  • Attention to detail and the ability to deal with a large number of purchase orders.
  • Spoken and written English, with a commitment to learn French. Eligibility criteria:
  • You are a national of a CERN Member or Associate Member State .
