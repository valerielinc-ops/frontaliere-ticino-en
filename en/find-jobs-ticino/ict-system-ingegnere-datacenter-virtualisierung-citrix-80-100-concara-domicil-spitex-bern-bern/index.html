Original description

Tasks

As an ICT System Engineer, you play a central role in the operation and further development of our data center, virtualization and server infrastructure.

  • Operation, maintenance and further development of our virtualized server and workplace infrastructure
  • Administration and engineering of VMware vSphere, Nutanix and Citrix environments
  • Planning and implementation of infrastructure projects, migrations and lifecycle renewals
  • Establishment, configuration and operation of Windows server systems and central platform services
  • Analysis and resolution of complex 2nd and 3rd level disturbances
  • Ensuring availability, performance, security and recoverability of systems
  • Further development of our data center, storage, backup and network architecture
  • Automation of recurring operational tasks, for example with PowerShell
  • Preparation and maintenance of technical documentation, operating concepts and standards
  • Collaboration with internal departments, external partners and manufacturers
  • Technical cooperation in the strategic development of our hybrid ICT landscape

Classic end-user support is not the focus of this function. For complex questions, however, you support the support team with your in-depth technical know-how.

Requirements

You have in-depth experience in the engineering and operation of complex ICT infrastructures and want to take on technical responsibility.

  • Completed training as a computer scientist: in EFZ platform development, systems engineering or a comparable further education
  • Several years of professional experience as a system engineer, infrastructure engineer or data center engineer
  • Very good knowledge in at least one virtualization platform, preferably:
  • VMware vSphere and ESXi
  • Nutanix AHV and Nutanix Cloud Platform
  • Microsoft Hyper-V
  • In-depth experience with Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops, Citrix ADC or similar platforms
  • Very good knowledge of Windows Server, Active Directory, Group Policy, DNS, DHCP and certificate services
  • Experience with storage, backup, monitoring and disaster recovery solutions
  • Good knowledge of network, firewall, VLAN, routing and VPN
  • Experience with Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365 and hybrid identity environments is beneficial
  • Knowledge of PowerShell or comparable scripting and automation technology
  • Ability to analyze complex technical relationships and develop sustainable solutions
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Enjoy building technical standards, questioning existing environments and implementing improvements
  • Very good knowledge of German and good technical knowledge of English

Advantage Not all of the following knowledge is mandatory. However, experience in some of these areas is welcome:

  • Nutanix Prism Central and Lifecycle Management
  • VMware vCenter
  • Citrix Provisioning Services or Machine Creation Services
  • UNICON operating system and thin client infrastructures
  • Veeam or comparable backup and recovery solutions
  • Microsoft Entra ID, Intune and Azure
  • ITIL-oriented operational processes
  • Experience in healthcare or in environments with high demands on availability and data protection

We offer

  • Technically demanding and varied infrastructure
  • Freedom of design in the further development of our ICT architecture
  • Collaboration in modernization, virtualization and cloud projects
  • Direct influence on technical standards and future platform decisions
  • Modern work equipment and a future-oriented working environment
  • Collegial and supportive team
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • Short decision paths and space for your own ideas

Spitex Bern and Domicil have merged under the umbrella of the non-profit Concara Group. With more than 2000 employees, we are committed to integrated all-round care, both inpatient and at home. At Concara Services AG, we provide various services for all operating units of the Concara Group, implement support processes and provide professional support.
