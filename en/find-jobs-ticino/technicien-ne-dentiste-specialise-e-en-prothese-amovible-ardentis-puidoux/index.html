Role overview

As a Removable Digital Denture Technician, you will play a crucial role in the creation and manufacture of high-quality removable dentures.

Your responsibility will be to design and manufacture partial and complete prostheses using the latest digital technologies.

With your expertise, you will help improve the quality of life of our customers by offering them dental solutions that are both comfortable and aesthetically pleasing. Your Missions

Key requirements

  • Vocational training or equivalent.
  • Previous experience in computer-aided design (CAD).
  • Solid experience in removable dental prosthetics.
  • In-depth knowledge of 3Shape design software, as well as dental mills and 3D printers. A mastery of dental materials and digital manufacturing techniques.
  • Teamwork skills and excellent communication with colleagues and dentists.
  • Knowledge of safety standards related to digital dental prosthetics manufacturing.
  • Excellent command of milling machines and dental printers.
