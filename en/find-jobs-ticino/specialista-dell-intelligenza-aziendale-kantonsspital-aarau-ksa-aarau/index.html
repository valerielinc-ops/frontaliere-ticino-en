Role overview

Tasks

  • You design, develop and optimize data models, management dashboards and reports and create meaningful analyses for the financial management of hospital operations, focus on analytics and operational processes in controlling.
  • You will analyse the requirements of our departments and develop customised business intelligence solutions in close cooperation with internal and external stakeholders.
  • They integrate new source systems and continuously develop our modern data platform by implementing new data models, cubes and data warehouse solutions.
  • They are jointly responsible for the maintenance, operation and continuous development of our business intelligence landscape and ensure its quality, stability and sustainability.
  • They advise and empower our employees in dealing with business intelligence tools and support them in making data-based decisions in everyday work.

Requirements

  • Ideally, you will have experience in the development of data models as well as in the development and further development of data warehouse solutions and modern data platforms.
