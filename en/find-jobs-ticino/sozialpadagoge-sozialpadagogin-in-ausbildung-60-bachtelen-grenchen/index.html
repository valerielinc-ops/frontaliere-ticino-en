Original description

Do you want to strengthen young people in their growing up, to accompany them and to move and shape themselves? You have a guaranteed place of study and are looking for a training place that suits you? In the Bachtelen, children, young people and young adults are the focus – we accompany them in challenging life situations with heart, mind and a pinch of humor. In a humanly and professionally strong team, we work together with them to create spaces for development. The Foyer Däderiz in Grenchen is a 365-day offer for seven young people and young adults aged 14 to 20 years on their way to self-employment. Together with them we develop life and future perspectives. Our work is based on trust, participation and relationship design at eye level. We are guided by trauma pedagogical principles, life-world orientation and empowerment. Thus we create a secure, living framework in which young people can test their strengths and grow.
