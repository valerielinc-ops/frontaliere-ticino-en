Original description

At the Beilngries site, around 360 employees in the Grain Quality and Supply division are dedicated to plant and mechanical engineering in the fields of grain processing and malting and brewing. This includes cleaning, drying, transport (including conveying technology and ship loading), storage and digitalization to process grain from the field to the consumer. Always with a focus on efficiency, product quality and sustainability.

Let us make a difference at Bühler! Two billion people eat food produced on Bühler plants every day. One billion people travel in vehicles whose parts have been produced by Bühler technology. With every decision, Bühler aims to reconcile the needs of business, people and nature, and to develop solutions that open up sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed and mobility industries.

We want to create innovations for a better world, with a particular focus on healthy, safe and sustainable solutions. That's why we work with customers, start-ups, multinational corporations and scientists to achieve a faster impact together.
