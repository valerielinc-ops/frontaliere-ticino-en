Role overview

Introduction

As an Electrical Works Manager within the Cabling Section, you’ll play a key role in expanding, strengthening, and maintaining CERN’s control and power cable installations, required for various systems within the CERN accelerators, such as alarm systems, process control systems, beam instrumentation and magnet powering systems. Functions

Analyse installation and dismantling requests, liaising directly with requestors as needed to complete the required information.
