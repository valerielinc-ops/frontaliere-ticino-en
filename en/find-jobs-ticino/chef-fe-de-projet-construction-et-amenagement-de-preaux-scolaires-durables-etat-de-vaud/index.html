Role overview

The Vaudois Climate Plan aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and support adaptations to climate change.

For the 93 schools, the aim is to combat the phenomenon of heat islands by supporting the municipalities in their projects on soil dewatering, vegetation of outdoor spaces, installation of solar protections of buildings and in schools, recovery of rain water.

In a context of strong climate issues, you will be involved in making teaching places for environmental models that promote local biodiversity and improve the well-being of children and professional teams.
