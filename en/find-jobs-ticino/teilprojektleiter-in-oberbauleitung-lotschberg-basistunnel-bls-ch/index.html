Original description

While trains roll reliably every day in the Lötschberg Base Tunnel, you push ahead with the construction of the second tube – a masterpiece under full load. In advance, the Engstlige tunnel is growing, which will make Alpine traffic even more efficient in the future. Your commitment secures people and goods strong, storm-resistant connections and gives sustainable mobility strength.

This is waiting for you

  • Responsibility for the quality, time and cost-related execution of the assigned subprojects
  • Leading the local construction managers: inside and maintain a partnership with the companies
  • Ensuring the smooth transition from planning to execution
  • Active control of project progress and transparent reporting
  • Designing challenging construction work under ongoing railway operations and contributing to the safe realization of one of Switzerland’s most important infrastructure projects

This is what you bring with you

  • Degree as a civil engineer: in FH or ETH
  • Several years of experience in superstructure management or construction management of complex infrastructure, tunnel and railway construction projects
  • Experience in the management of demanding interfaces and ideally in construction under operation
  • Convincing communication and negotiation skills
  • Independent, solution-oriented and entrepreneurial working methods as well as very good knowledge of German

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st class. Class or half-tax subscription
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether at home office or office
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten days of holiday per year and to take a sabbatical of 40 working days every seven years.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health.
  • Financial support for external training, a leadership development program and an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.

About the BLS

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? Proud of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.
