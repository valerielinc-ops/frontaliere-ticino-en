  • Preparing and approving technical documentation, including quotations, work plans, project documentation, coating specifications, technical reports, and QA documentation
  • Analyzing laboratory results and process data, and compiling clear, reliable final reports
  • Providing technical consulting support to internal and external customers
  • Planning and coordinating coating trials and campaigns
  • Collaborating with other Coating Solution Centers to share knowledge, project experience, and best practices
