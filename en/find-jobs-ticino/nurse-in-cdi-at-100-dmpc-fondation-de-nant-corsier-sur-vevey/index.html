Original description

In order to strengthen its team of the Mobile Community Psychiatry Device (DMPC), the Nant Foundation is putting to the competition a position of 100% CDI nurse THE MISSION The mission of the DMPC nurses is to intervene, if indicated, with persons with psychological difficulties who encounter obstacles to access to the usual care and support structures. Their intervention consists of conducting a specialized clinical evaluation and providing appropriate support in the person's living environment. The population covered is between 18 and 65 years of age. The DMPC's nursing team also provides entry into care and specialized follow-up of youth integrated into the Young Adults Program (YMP). This three-year programme focuses on the early intervention of psychotic disorders (the nurse acts as case manager). WE OFFER The Nant Foundation is a private institution recognized in the public interest, mandated by the State to organize and provide psychiatric care in the eastern Vaud region. Working at the DMPC is evolving within the community psychiatry and psychotherapy department, carrying out a diversified activity in constant collaboration with an interdisciplinary team. WE SEEK A nurse with experience in psychiatry (minimum 2 years), holder of a Bachelor of Science HES-SO in nursing with Red Cross recognition (or equivalent). A good experience in psychiatry especially with young adults is desired. This position requires a common sense of organisation, autonomy in its work and good management of unforeseen and stress. Having a good capacity for interdisciplinary analysis and coordination is essential to work in this environment, as well as good relational, communication and negotiation capabilities. Mobility of the post means having a private motorized vehicle. Entry into office Immediately, or to be agreed upon Posting time Applications will be reviewed as they arrive Type of contract CDI Conditions of engagement According to the Collective Labour Convention in the Parapublic Sector of Vaud. Place of work: Corsier-sur-Vevey Information from Ms. Vanessa Baier, ICUS Community Mobile Team, 021 965 71 06 To the extent that all our applications are processed electronically, we would like to thank you for applying exclusively via the Internet, by sending us, at a minimum, a curriculum vitae accompanied by a cover letter. More information on www.nant.ch Nant Foundation — Psychiatric institution in the heart of the canton of Vaud, sites of Corsier-sur-Vevey and SPAUL (Lausanne).
