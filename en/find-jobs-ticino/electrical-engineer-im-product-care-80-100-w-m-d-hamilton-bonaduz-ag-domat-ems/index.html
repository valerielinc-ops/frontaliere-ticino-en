Role overview

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950.

We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Process Analytics business unit develops, produces and distributes the latest process measurement technology for customers in biopharmaceuticals, chemicals and breweries.

Company and context

  • For our product care team in the development department of the Process Analytics business unit, we are looking for a committed personality with technical understanding, organizational skills and enjoyment of cooperation across different departments.
  • In this role, you accompany our sensor products throughout their entire life cycle and ensure that product changes, enhancements and optimizations are implemented efficiently, sustainably and in high quality.
  • You work closely with development, production, quality, purchasing, regulatory affairs and other stakeholders. Your tasks
  • As Project Manager Product Care, you take responsibility for planning, coordinating and implementing product changes and product extensions.
  • Management and coordination of cross-divisional change and integration projects
  • Planning and implementation of product changes, product care and obsolescence projects
  • Analysis of the scope of change and assessment of impacts on stocks, production, resources, costs and deadlines
  • Coordination of interdisciplinary teams and ensuring a smooth project flow
  • Carrying out risk analyses and support for special releases in cooperation with quality managers
  • Ensuring compliance with processes and policies
