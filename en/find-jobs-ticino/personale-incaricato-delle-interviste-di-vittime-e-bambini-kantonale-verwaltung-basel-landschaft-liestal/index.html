Original description

Tasks

  • Independent planning, preparation and implementation of specialised hearings (written and audiovisual)
  • Advising case-leading organisational units
  • Participation in the development and updating of professional standards
  • Support for new employees and members of the militia pool
  • Participation in education and training
  • Participation in projects, working groups and specialist events
  • Participation in quality assurance and case analysis
  • Maintain cooperation with internal and external partner organisations within the scope of their responsibilities

Requirements

  • Completed training as a police officer/policeman with federal law. Technical certificate and verifiable experience in carrying out hearings
  • Good and secure knowledge of the Code of Criminal Procedure (StPO)
  • Strong social skills and an appreciative and respectful treatment of people
  • A solution-oriented, assertive and reliable personality
  • The ability to self-reflection and the willingness to critically question one’s own actions
  • High sense of duty, responsibility and quality
  • Readiness for professional and personal training
  • Readiness for personal development, flexible accessibility and the appropriate assumption of additional tasks

The Police Basel-Landschaft is a modern police corps with a wide range of tasks. More than 700 employees in the canton ensure a high level of security for its residents. With advanced technologies, equipment and vehicles and in close cooperation with the population and partner organisations, crimes are prevented, combated and clarified and intervened in emergencies. Targeted and efficient, the department of the Security Directorate makes an important contribution to the sense of security and the quality of life in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft.
