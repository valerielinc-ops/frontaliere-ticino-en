  • The clinic provides inpatient care for patients in 285 beds.
  • The clinic specializes in geriatric, internal medicine, cardiovascular and pulmonary rehabilitation, Long-Covid, psychosomatic medicine and psychotherapy, psychocardiology, and sleep medicine.
