Role overview

The Health Office is a central and versatile department of the Department of Justice, Security and Health of the Canton of Graubünden.

It implements federal and cantonal decrees and thus makes a significant contribution to high-quality, secure and sustainable health care in the canton.

The Office is divided into the following areas: health promotion and prevention, cantonal medical service, licensing and quality, rescue, legal service and staff.
