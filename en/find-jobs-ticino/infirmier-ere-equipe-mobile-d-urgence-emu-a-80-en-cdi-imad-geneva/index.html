Role overview

The mobile emergency team (EMU) was created in January 2025 after IMAD positioned itself by proposing its participation in the cantonal project "Geneva Emergency Network (RUG+)" (project to extend the emergency response to the canton) through the establishment of a mobile team to deal with non-vital emergencies.

Several public and private partners are involved in this project.

This nursing team is part of the Mobile Emergency Social Unit (UMUS).

Main responsibilities

  • Your missions and responsibilities
  • Evaluate the nature of the request from a professional at the Tri and Orientation plant attached to 144 and respond to it as soon as possible or direct the situation towards a more suitable service if necessary
  • Intervene and carry out a joint assessment with his nurse by providing clinical expertise by appreciating quickly the somatic and psychological health of the beneficiary.
  • Propose to the beneficiary ways to reduce the intensity of the emergency felt
  • Take all necessary measures to ensure a stable general physical and psychological state and, if necessary, to coordinate the intervention of medical or paramedical partners.

  • Coordinate and monitor situations within the Geneva health and social network
  • Use available computer tools to monitor situations
  • Assess the relevance of each intervention and participate in the quality process
  • Participate in seminars and supervisions in teams, outside intervention times
