Role overview

Ente Case Anziani Mendrisiotto (ECAM) publishes the competition "Concorso Generale Permanent 2026" for its own organic of houses for the elderly in Mendrisiotto.

The application must be sent through the online form indicated on the official employment opportunities page.

GENERAL PERMANENT CONCORSO YEAR 2026 The Mendrisiotto Entity makes known that the general competition for the recruitment of the following staff is open:

  • care and therapeutic staff (infirmaries, socio-sanitary workers, geriatric assistants, care assistants, sociosanitary care professionals, care assistants, animators, activation specialists, physiotherapists, ergotherapists)
  • personal service kitchen and service domestic economy (kitchen accessories, cook help, domestic economy auxiliaries, CFP domestic economy employees, AFC domestic economy employees, cooks, dietetic cooks)
  • technical service staff (technical and maintenance service, qualified craftsman, building and infrastructure operators)
  • administrative staff (assistant/specialists human resources, business employees, administrative staff, finance and accounting specialists) The positions are open to both male and female applications. Stages and apprenticeships The Ente Case Anziani Mendrisiotto offers internships in the administration services, domestic economy, care and technical service.
