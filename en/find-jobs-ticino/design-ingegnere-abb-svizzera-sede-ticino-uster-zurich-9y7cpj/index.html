Original description

At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

This position reports to:

Engineering Manager

Your role and responsibility:

In this role you have the opportunity to create the design of devices, systems or installations or to create ready-made drafts for • Modify tasks using standard concepts. Every day, you help plan technical solutions, including drawings and calculations according to regulations. You will also demonstrate your expertise by participating in design reviews, design verifications and customizing the design as needed.

The working model for the role is: hybrid.

This role contributes to the PMA business.

You will be primarily responsible for:

  • Supported in the development of cable protection products and systems used in power and data applications.

  • Contributes to product development projects from concept phase to first item testing.

  • Supports part testing and qualification.

  • Creates and maintains design documentation and data management.


Our team dynamics You become part of a dynamic team in which you can succeed.

Qualifications for the role

  • Educated an apprenticeship as a constructor at EFZ.

  • Founded knowledge and practical experience in product design and 3D CAD (preferably PTC Creo).

  • Experienced in MS Office applications, Windchill and SAP.

  • High motivation and commitment to a career at ABB.

  • Flowing German and good English skills.

  • You are either a Swiss citizen or have a valid residence permit for Switzerland.

More about us


We develop cable protection systems for our global customers in a variety of market segments. Our goal is to strengthen our position in our target markets through targeted development and improvement of our solutions.
