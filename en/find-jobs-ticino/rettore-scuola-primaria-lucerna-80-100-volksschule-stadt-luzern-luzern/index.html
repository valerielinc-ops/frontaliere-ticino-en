Role overview

Design the elementary school Lucerne of tomorrow.

Bring your vision, your leadership and your will to shape and move the future of education together with a dedicated team.

A key position awaits you with a great deal of room for manoeuvre in the exciting environment of education, politics and administration.

  • Design the elementary school Lucerne of tomorrow.
  • Bring your vision, your leadership and your will to shape and move the future of education together with a dedicated team.
  • You bear the overall responsibility for a high-quality elementary school in the city of Lucerne.
  • You lead the six-member management of the elementary school Stadt Lucerne and are also a member of the management of the Education Directorate.

Main responsibilities

  • You bear the overall responsibility for a high-quality elementary school in the city of Lucerne.
  • You lead the six-member management of the elementary school Stadt Lucerne and are also a member of the management of the Education Directorate.
  • You lead the rectorate of the elementary school with nine directly subordinate employees.
  • You are responsible for the strategic goals and performance mandate of the primary school with a global budget of over 125 million francs.
  • You represent the overall interests of the primary school and maintain an active network with offices in the city and the canton of Lucerne as well as with other cities in Switzerland.
  • You participate in various working groups and committees.

Company and context

  • Together with the management team, you will develop the elementary school in a forward-looking manner and provide impulses for innovative school and organizational development. Requirements
  • You are a committed and convincing leader with a pedagogical college degree or equivalent education.
  • You are interested in educational policy issues and the design of the public service in the education sector.
  • You know the internal administrative processes and have a good sense of political connections and decision-making processes.
  • You think ahead, develop visionary ideas and want to actively shape the elementary school.
  • You have proven experience in project and change management and move safely in change processes.
  • You recognize the opportunities of the digital transformation and shape it in a future-oriented way.
  • You communicate clearly, transparently and appropriately.
