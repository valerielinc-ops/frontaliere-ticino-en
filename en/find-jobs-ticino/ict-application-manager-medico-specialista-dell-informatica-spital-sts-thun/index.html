Application process

  • As an employee of our application management team, you are responsible for the stable operation of medical and non-medical applications operated at Spital STS AG.
  • You advise and support users in the areas of application and process handling.
  • By including specialist requirements, you coordinate further developments of applications and help with projects.
  • You will be involved in specification, testing and rollout to implement application releases.
  • Applicational disturbances and errors are analyzed and processed by you.
  • In the event of complex disruptions, coordinate between second- and third-level support (suppliers).
  • As a side task, you support the service desk on individual days in the quarter and take over Pikett services (about three weeks per year).
  • Creating, updating and managing the necessary documentation is also part of your job.
