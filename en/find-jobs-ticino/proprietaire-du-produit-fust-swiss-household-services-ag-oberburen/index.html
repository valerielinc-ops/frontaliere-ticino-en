Original description

Switzerland buys at Fust! We are proud to be a leader in electrical household appliances, multimedia/entertainment electronics and kitchen and bathroom remodeling, and with our comprehensive range of services and unique services we create real added value! Become part of our team and help us continue to create customer experiences! A job at Fust, and it’s running!

Tasks

  • Derivation of functional solution concepts from business requirements
  • Support in the definition of MVPs, prioritization of the scope and development of the product roadmap in close cooperation with the business owner, business project manager and specialists from business and IT
  • Translation of technical requirements in epics, user stories and acceptance criteria including management of backlog refinement sessions
  • Creation of the Project Delivery Plan as well as responsibility for the product backlog and the management of the Scrum Team
  • Preparation and implementation of sprint planning, presentation of sprint results and product demos, transparent communication to stakeholders and responsibility for the project budget

Requirements

  • Completed studies in business informatics or a commercial education, supplemented by further training in project management
  • Many years of experience in managing, controlling or coordinating complex business/IT projects
  • Experience as a product owner or scrum master in a comparable delivery-related role as well as sound practice in agile IT development, knowledge in lean management are advantageous
  • Strong communication and moderation skills in German and English
  • Structured, implementation-oriented working method with a high level of initiative

What we offer

  • Conditions of employment : Largely flexible working hours, free parking, internal co-op restaurant with a range of premium prices, modern and bright rooms
  • Discounts : Discounts in various coop formats, 5 times the number of coop super points, Up to 20% discount on various insurance services, Cheap mobile and prepaid subscriptions at Coop Mobile, Reka Card: Discounts on subscription, staff discount also for partners:in (living in the same household), discount on the travel offer ITS Coop Travel, CHF 650.- Voucher for the purchase of an SBB GA's
  • Training : internal training, support for external training, development opportunities
  • Communication & culture : DU culture throughout the company, orientation towards common values, internal app for communication with employees
  • Insurance : Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop (base), various savings plans at the pension fund, generous and super-compulsory insurance benefits in the event of accident and illness
  • Holidays : At least 25 days of holiday or according to valid GAV, if possible support for unpaid holidays
  • Health : Update Fitness: CHF 200.- Discount on annual subscription, free and anonymous company social consulting 24/7
