Role overview

The position is included within the Human Resources & Organization function of IBSA and is aimed at managing the activities related to the selection of staff (fixed and temporary), performance assessment, database management and line assistance for all issues related to staff in a HR Business Partner perspective.

  • Work closely with business function line managers to support them in HR areas.
  • Search, recruit, examine, interview and recommend external and/or internal candidates for all levels of work.
  • Publish job ads on the company’s dedicated platforms.
  • Use the services of employment agencies and Headhunting.
  • To process the organization's human resources information using the computer systems and applications available and support users in using HR IT tools.
  • Ensure management of temporary staff databases.
  • Advance and plan the medium-term (personnel) needs of human resources in collaboration with Management Control and business functions.
  • Manage processes related to technical training of employees and business language courses.
  • Support the organization of management training courses offered by the company.
