Your role and responsibilities

The mission of this team is to bring modern and disruptive power electronic solutions from research into product development. We improve our products by holistic optimization, pushing forward novel converter topologies as well as optimizing key components. By joining us, you will participate in idea generation at the frontier of academic research and industrial applications, as well as in the development of advanced technologies for ABB’s products.

Do you want to contribute to a greener future by utilizing advanced and highly efficient power-electronic solutions for various applications? Get in touch with us! A young, multinational team is looking forward to welcoming you as their latest team member!

The work model for the role is: #LI-Hybrid

The main responsibilities include

  • Own the lab infrastructure (Laborinfrastruktur), including coordination with maintenance (Instandhaltung), organizing calibration (Kalibrierung) of equipment, and managing lab supplies (Verbrauchsmaterialien).
  • Ensure lab safety (Sicherheit) and organize/structure lab spaces for multiple ongoing projects.
  • Support scientific work by designing and building power electronics prototypes (Leistungselektronik Prototypen) up to medium voltage (Mittelspannung) and megawatt power.
  • Assist in commissioning test setups (Inbetriebnahme von Prüfaufbauten) and perform testing together with scientific staff (Wissenschaftler).
  • Collaborate closely with internal and external stakeholders (Teamfähigkeit), and supervise apprentices (Lernende / Auszubildende) in daily tasks.

• Qualifications for the role

  • Completed apprenticeship as an automation technician (Automatiker EFZ) with practical experience in machining (Fräsen, Bohren, Drehen, Handarbeit), and be able to conversate in English.
  • Additional education from a Higher Technical School or University of Applied Sciences (Höhere Fachschule HF / Fachhochschule FH) in power electronics, energy systems, or similar is a strong advantage.
  • Experience with CAD design (CAD-Konstruktion) using tools such as Creo, Solid Edge, SolidWorks, or equivalent; PCB design (Leiterplattendesign) knowledge is a plus (Altium Designer).
  • Solid experience in electrical circuit design (Schaltungsdesign), grounding concepts (Erdung), and wiring standards (Verdrahtungsrichtlinien).
  • Hands-on experience building and prototyping power electronic systems (Leistungselektronik Systeme), including mechanical work, tooling (Werkzeuge), and implementing ad-hoc improvements.
  • Basic knowledge of electrical standards (e.g., NIV / NIN / SNR 464022) and familiarity with lab technologies such as 3D printing (3D-Druck), LabVIEW, and laser cutting/engraving (Laserschneider / Lasergravur).

More about us

At the ABB research center in Baden-Dättwil in the proximity of Zurich, we work on exciting technologies that shape the future of our products. Our international research teams focus on Power Electronics, Energy Storage, Intelligent Systems, and Switching.
