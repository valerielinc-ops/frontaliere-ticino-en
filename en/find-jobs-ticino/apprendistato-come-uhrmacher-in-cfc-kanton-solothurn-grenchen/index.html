Role overview

During your training as a watchmaker / in EFZ at the Vocational Training Centre Solothurn-Grenchen you will get an insight into the versatile working world of watchmaking.

With us at the ZeitZentrum Uhrmacherschule in Grenchen you have from 01.08.2027 the possibility of a varied training for the watchmaker / in EFZ. Your education

As a watchmaker/in EFZ you gain extensive knowledge of mechanical and electronic wristwatches as well as special watches with complications, e.g. chronographs and pendulum clocks.
