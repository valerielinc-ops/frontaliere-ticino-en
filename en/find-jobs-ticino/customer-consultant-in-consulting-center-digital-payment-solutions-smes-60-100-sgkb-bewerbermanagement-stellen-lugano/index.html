Original description

For our customers, we are the first bank. For us, being the first bank means that we focus on the wishes and goals of our customers and inspire them anew. To live this consulting philosophy, we need you! Do you want to enrich your know-how as our new colleague or our new colleague in our new team Digital Payment Solutions SMEs? We look forward to meeting you!

This is what you will do.

  • You dive deep into our digital payment solutions and become a specialist step by step.
  • After a comprehensive introduction, you will support with internal training and advise customers independently on topics such as EBICS, TWINT, eBill and card terminals (on site or via digital channels).
  • You convince our customers with individual advice and support them in the selection and use of the appropriate product solutions.
  • You accompany the onboarding of new customers, maintain inventory data and ensure first-line support for technical questions and problems.

You bring that with you

  • You have an IT or banking education, ideally with initial experience in digital payment solutions.
  • Great interest in digital payment solutions and technical understanding of interfaces such as EBICS or bLink.
  • In customer contact, you feel safe, look likeable and have perseverance in problem solving.
  • When dealing with our internal stakeholders, you convince by a secure appearance.
  • You are an independent, resilient and performance-oriented personality with high analytical and conceptual skills and enjoy dealing with other people and working together in a team.
  • Your commitment and openness will quickly make you a valuable team member.
