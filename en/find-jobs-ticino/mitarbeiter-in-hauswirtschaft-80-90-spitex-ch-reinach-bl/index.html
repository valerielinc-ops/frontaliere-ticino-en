Original description

Future-oriented, competent and professional – that is our non-profit organization Spitex Region Birs GmbH. Are you ready for an exciting challenge in an exceptional and diverse working environment? Then join us in the team! With flat hierarchies and a respectful cooperation at eye level, we attach great importance to professional skills and strong social skills. To strengthen our team, we are looking for you as Employee/housekeeping (80-90%)

What to expect

  • Accompanying, supporting and relieving elderly, ill and needy persons in the domestic sector to ensure a clean and safe environment (Monday to Friday on day duty)
  • Household cleaning services
  • Textile care (washing, ironing, small sewing work)
  • Shopping and preparation of simple meals, if possible with the involvement of clients
  • Correct documentation of the activities carried out in our electronic system
  • Compliance with hygiene guidelines and operational standards
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with nurses, relatives and other services
  • Approximately 1 x per month assistance with the weekly cleaning of our offices in Reinach and Dornach (evening service until 21.00h)

What you bring

  • Completed training as a specialist in housekeeping EFZ or a professional certificate as a housekeeping practitioner/in EBA
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in the home or care environment
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Driving licence Cat. B and readiness to travel by car
  • An independent, responsible way of working
  • Open-mindedness, flexibility and empathy

Our offer for you

  • No night and weekend service
  • When designing working hours, we take your personal needs into account
  • Space for personal initiative and professional development
  • A motivated team and an open, appreciative working atmosphere
  • A modern, rapidly growing and innovative organization

With us you will not only find a job, but a meaningful task with a perspective! Become part of our dynamic team and shape the future of healthcare with us. We look forward to your application!
