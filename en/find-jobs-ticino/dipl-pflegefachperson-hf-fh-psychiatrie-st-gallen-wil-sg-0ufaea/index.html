Main responsibilities

  • Treatment and support of patients with psychological and social stress in the daily clinical setting in a supportive interdisciplinary team with a versatile field of activity.
  • Independent nursing treatment as well as management of milieu therapy and other topic-specific group therapies.
  • Self-responsible and professional design of the nursing process with nursing diagnosis, treatment planning and preparation for withdrawal under weekly psychiatric psychotherapeutic supervision.
  • With your observations and professional assessments, you design the daily program and support the individual treatment process.
  • In exchange with medicine, psychology, nursing and other therapeutic areas, you participate in rapports, case discussions and further training.
  • You are responsible for a careful documentation of the course of treatment and a correct performance recording in the hospital information system. Requirements
  • Ideally, you will bring professional experience in psychiatry or be motivated to develop further in this field.
  • The pleasure of working with mentally ill people as well as the design and management of groups distinguishes you.
  • With your independent, responsible and resilient working method as well as your strong social and communication skills, you enrich our team.
  • Openness to new things and enjoyment of professional development will bring you as well as very good knowledge of German at at least C1 level and good IT user knowledge.

Key requirements

  • You have completed training as a nurse HF/FH (for a foreign diploma with an SRK recognition or a positive SRK PreCheck).
  • Ideally, you will bring professional experience in psychiatry or be motivated to develop further in this field.

  • Openness to new things and enjoyment of professional development will bring you as well as very good knowledge of German at at least C1 level and good IT user knowledge.
  • With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatments and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
