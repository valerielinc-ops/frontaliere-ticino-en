Role overview

In the 19th century, the site of Les Pâquis housed the first infirmary of Morges, then the first hospital of the city, before being converted to EMS in the 1970s.

With its renovated premises, the site hosts some of the administrative functions of the EHC, such as human resources, finance and information systems.

The site "Les Paquis" will return to its medical vocation with the Covid-19 pandemic in 2020, becoming one of the canton's most important testing and vaccination centres.
