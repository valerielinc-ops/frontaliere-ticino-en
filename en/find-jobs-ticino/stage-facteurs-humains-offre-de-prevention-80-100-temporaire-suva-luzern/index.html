Original description

Do you want to further develop prevention offers with psychological know-how and thus sensitize people to safe behavior? Then you are right with us. What you do

  • You work in projects for the prevention of occupational and leisure accidents and occupational diseases.
  • You support the development of prevention services in the areas of human factors, prevention culture and behavioural change.
  • You work on evaluations and on impact measurements.
  • You research, analyze and prepare insights for practice.
  • You will work with internal and external stakeholders and get exciting insights into Suva’s prevention work.
  • The internship lasts 6 to 12 months and starts from January 2027 or by appointment. What you bring
  • You bring an ongoing or already completed master's degree in psychology with a focus on work, social or organizational psychology.
  • You are interested in human factors as well as safety and prevention topics.
  • A high level of initiative, self-organization and pleasure in working independently characterize you.
  • You have a quick apprehension and willingness to familiarize yourself with new topics.
  • You master German at the native language level and ideally bring French skills.
