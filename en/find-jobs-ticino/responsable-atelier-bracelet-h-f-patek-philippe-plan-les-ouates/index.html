Role overview

Mission

As part of this function, attached to our Clothing Division, you will be responsible for the management of the team and all the production activities of the Bracelet 1 workshop combining the satining and polishing operations.

In close collaboration with the Bracelet 2 workshop, Fabrication Component Bracelet and the other workshops of the division, as well as the support services, you will ensure the performance of your workshop by guaranteeing the waiting of the production objectives and the respect of the Patek Philippe standards, while promoting the collaborative experience.
