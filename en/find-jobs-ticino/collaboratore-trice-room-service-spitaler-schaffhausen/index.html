Original description

Tasks

  • Care and support of patients from entry to exit
  • Ordering of all meals and advice on menus and diet
  • Serving and clearing of food and, if necessary, providing appropriate assistance
  • Medicaid for patients
  • Administrative work such as orders and documentation
  • Working days are from Monday to Sunday from 7:00 - 13:24 and 17:15 - 19:15

Requirements

  • Completed training in the hospitality, restoration or housekeeping sector (EFZ/EBA) with professional experience in the hospitality and catering sectors
  • Experience in healthcare and dealing with people who need support
  • Experience with Sanalogic desirable
  • Sound PC user knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Reliable, responsible and service-oriented

We offer

Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but does well.

The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
