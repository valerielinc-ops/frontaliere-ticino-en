Role overview

The Office for Migration and Civil Law currently has around 230 employees at more than 20 locations and is responsible for the entire area of responsibility in the area of migration, i.e. in particular granting and withdrawing permits, accommodation and support, executing expulsions and integration.

In addition, ID cards are issued to Swiss nationals, naturalisations are made and supervision of the civil status authorities is exercised.

The headquarters of the Office of Migration and Civil Law is in Chur.
