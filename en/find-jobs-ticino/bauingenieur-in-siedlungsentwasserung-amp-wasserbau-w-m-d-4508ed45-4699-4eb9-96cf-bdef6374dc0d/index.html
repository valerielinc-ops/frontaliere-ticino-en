Original description

Pensum: 80% or more | Bathing

In the Engineering division Hydroenergy and Biomass you realize challenging projects in hydraulic engineering and hydropower. In this way, you make an important contribution to a reliable and sustainable energy supply.

Your focus is on urban drainage and drainage planning for hydropower plants. As part of various renewal projects, the plant park of our hydropower plants is brought up to date with the latest technology. The existing drainage systems are also reviewed and adapted or redesigned as needed.

You can expect a versatile task at the interface of civil engineering, hydraulic engineering and hydropower. You work on projects with high technical relevance and actively participate in the renewal of important infrastructures. To strengthen our expertise in this area, we are looking for you.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You will work independently on various tasks in the area of municipal drainage for hydropower plants – from conception to implementation.
  • You create drainage and water protection concepts and project the necessary measures.
  • You take over the management of projects or sub-projects.
  • You coordinate and manage external planning offices and executing companies within the framework of the projects.
  • You work on other hydraulic engineering and hydropower projects.
  • You ensure compliance with quality, costs and deadlines.
  • You work closely with internal departments, authorities, associations and other external stakeholders.

Profile & Requirements

  • Completed studies as a Dipl. Civil engineer in ETH, FH or TU; alternatively a degree as a dipl. Cultural or environmental engineer/in ETH, FH or TU.
  • Broad expertise in construction technology as well as in-depth knowledge in urban drainage and general drainage planning.
  • Experience in project management is an advantage.
  • Open, communicative and solution-oriented personality.
  • Enjoy challenging construction projects and assignments throughout Switzerland.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage.

Is this new challenge electrifying you? Roger Hofer is looking forward to your application.

About the team

We work on highly topical topics that sustainably benefit our energy future and bring us forward socially. Our team in the construction department has broad expertise and successfully implements projects in the field of hydraulic engineering and hydropower, especially in the power plant park of Axpo. We offer an open, progressive and collegial working environment in a professional and dynamic environment. There is the possibility of up to 40% home office share.
