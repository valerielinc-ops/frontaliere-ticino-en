Original description

Automation specialist:in

Coop cooperative — Schafisheim, Aargau, Svizzera

Tasks

  • Responsibility for automation systems (OT) in the areas of bakery, logistics and building and infrastructure facilities
  • Support for operation in challenging incidents including second-level support
  • Operation and further development of higher-level automation and server systems such as HMI, SCADA, PLS and Data Historian
  • Central interface role between operation, technical maintenance, IT and external plant suppliers
  • Planning, implementation and management of changes and projects such as optimizations, extensions, modernizations or adjustments in ongoing operations
  • Ensuring high plant availability through monitoring, analysis and preventive measures
  • Active participation in the preventive maintenance of automation systems
  • Contribute your know-how to the technical support of maintenance and project teams
  • Take over depending on profile and interest You also have tasks in the areas of automation, project management, PLC programming, PLC support, service engineering or process informatics

Requirements

  • Studies as an engineer: in FH or training as a technician: in HF in the field of automation technology
  • Ideally, a completed apprenticeship as an electrician:in or automatic:in
  • In-depth experience in the commissioning and programming of PLC and visualization systems, in particular Siemens S7 (Step 7 Classic and TIA Portal) and WinCC
  • Very good knowledge of the fieldbus systems ProfiNet and ProfiBus
  • Experience in the IT-related environment of automation is advantageous
  • Practice in at least one of the following areas: food production, intralogistics, home engineering, process or industrial automation, plant automation
  • Experience with networked, modern industrial plant technology
  • Practical EMSR knowledge, ideally in special machine construction
  • Readiness for 2nd-level viewing services, mostly remotely (about one day per week, one weekend in five weeks)
