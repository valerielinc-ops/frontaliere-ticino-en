Original description

The psychiatry of the Emmental Hospital offers demanding jobs for doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers and administrative staff with the opportunity for personal development and takes into account their own resources. In our manageable company, the personality of each individual employee shapes the way we deal with each other and our cooperation. We regard individuality as enrichment. We offer jobs at the two locations Burgdorf and Langnau. We are a recognised SIWF training centre.

Your tasks within team-oriented processes

  • Conduct of the psychiatric consultation at the Langnau General Hospital

  • If interested: participation in the special consultation hours ADHD and ASD in adulthood
  • Establishment and expansion of the regional outreach supply structure
  • Interprofessional cooperation with care, therapies and somatic disciplines
  • Participation in the integrated psychiatric emergency service
