Original description

Who are we?

The IT Services Directorate (DIT) of the University offers a dynamic work environment, driven by a multidisciplinary team working closely with an academic community composed of leading researchers and teachers. Within the DIT, the IT support service for teaching and research (DIT-ER) supports researchers through specialized IT expertise, while providing digital tools designed to enrich and support the teaching practices of University teachers.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and diversity of perspectives because it is the diversity of backgrounds and profiles that makes the strength of the State of Fribourg. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link.

Your missions

  • Ensure the operation, maintenance and evolution of high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructure
  • Participate in the development of IT services and platforms dedicated to AI for teaching and research
  • Guarantee the performance, stability, security and optimization of computing resources for scientific and artificial intelligence needs
  • Deploy, configure and maintain servers, Linux systems, scientific libraries and software components necessary for research activities
  • Administer storage systems, backups, quotas and high-performance network interconnections
  • Define, implement and monitor security measures and compliance of infrastructure and data
  • Support research and teaching teams in the use of artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies
  • Contribute to the development of institutional services and platforms dedicated to artificial intelligence for teaching and research.
  • Ensure scientific and technical support for projects requiring HPC resources, AI or specialized computing environments
  • Deploy and maintain virtualized environments, virtual machines, containers and notebooks for research and teaching
  • Collaborate with other DIT teams to ensure the availability, security and performance of IT services
  • Participate in technological monitoring in the fields of scientific computing, artificial intelligence and Linux infrastructure
  • Write and maintain technical and user documentation

Desired profile

  • Master's degree in computer science or equivalent qualification
  • Experience in Linux system administration and scientific infrastructure
  • Scripting skills (Python, Bash or equivalent)
  • Experience or good knowledge of HPC environments, computing clusters and CPU/GPU resource management
  • Knowledge of virtualization, containerization and computing environments for research
  • Interest in artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies
  • Ability to interact with researchers and teachers from various disciplines
  • Service mindset, autonomy, analytical skills and ability to work in a team
  • Fluency in French or German, with good knowledge of the other language, as well as proficiency in written and spoken English
