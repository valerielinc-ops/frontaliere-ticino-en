Role overview

You will complete your internship in the customer center, the hub of the service department for urban space and events (STAV).

You take care of the parking space management and the granting of exemption permits in road traffic.

You will also get exciting insights into the approval processes for small actions and events that take place on public property in the city of Lucerne.

  • You will complete your internship in the customer center, the hub of the service department for urban space and events (STAV).
  • You take care of the parking space management and the granting of exemption permits in road traffic.
  • You advise and serve customers at the counter and provide comprehensive information on the phone.
  • You process electronic customer requests and create exception permits.
