Role overview

Job ID: 41889 | Location: Pratteln, Canton of Basel-Landschaft, Switzerland | Work Model: Hybrid Clariant is a globally leading specialty chemicals company committed to creating value through innovative and sustainable solutions for people and the planet.

As part of our sustainability strategy, we are looking for a motivated individual to join our Pratteln office in Switzerland as Intern – AI for Sustainability reporting , ideally starting November 15th, 2026, on a full-time basis, with a fixed-term contract.

We are seeking an Intern with strong AI and digital skills, excellent communication abilities, and a keen interest in sustainability and corporate reporting.

In this role, you will support our team in the implementation of the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), leveraging digital tools and AI where appropriate to enhance efficiency, structure, and insight generation, while also contributing to broader sustainability reporting activities, gaining a well-rounded understanding of regulatory, strategic, and operational aspects.

The Intern position is limited to 12 months in full time.
