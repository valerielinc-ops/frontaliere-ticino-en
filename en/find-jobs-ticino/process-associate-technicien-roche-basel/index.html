The Position

The Opportunity

We are currently looking for two Process Technicians to join our Process Engineering team on a 12-month contract , with the possibility of extension. Both positions will involve the same day-to-day responsibilities outlined below; however, one of the roles will also require working at our Dumyat location.

As a Process Technician you will work closely with the sensor manufacture and packaging teams and will become highly skilled and competent in all aspects of manufacturing (e.g. line operation & packaging, processes and procedures). Be able to diagnose product and process issues quickly and effectively using a logical approach. Support and drive continuous improvement initiatives while ensuring the highest levels of quality and productivity are maintained. Analyse manufacturing data and conduct product / process testing to ensure CTQs are met. Update documentation such as standard operating procedures, work instructions and bills of material.

This is an on-site role based at our Athena Motherwell location. Please note that you will spend approximately 70% of your time working from our Bathgate site, however, one of the roles will also require working at our Dumyat location.

Responsibilities

Technical

  • Work in close collaboration with manufacturing technicians and engineers to become highly competent in sensor manufacture line operation, processes and procedures
  • Support monitoring of product and process data using statistical analysis techniques
  • Quick and effective diagnosis and troubleshooting of problems using a methodical approach and report progress in a structured way
  • Conduct CTQ testing and material / product analysis techniques
  • Investigate and resolve non-conformances and help develop robust OCAP’s and preventative measures
  • Support NPD projects through transfer of pilot work to high volume manufacture
  • Support validation activities and completion of validation documentation in line with business needs
  • Help drive continuous improvement initiatives through engagement of production team on shift
  • Ensures highest levels of quality and compliance are maintained
  • Lead/support change control updates to manufacturing documentation.
