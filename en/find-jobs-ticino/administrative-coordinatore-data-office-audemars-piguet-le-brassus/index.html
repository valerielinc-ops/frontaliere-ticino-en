Original description

Integrated into the Data Office and under the responsibility of the Senior Director Data, your main mission will be to ensure the administrative, organisational and budgetary coordination of the department. A true partner of Management, you will contribute to the smooth functioning of the team by piloting various administrative processes, monitoring performance indicators and actively participating in the animation of the team's life. In this context, your main responsibilities will be:

  • Organize and coordinate meetings, events and activities contributing to the dynamics and cohesion of the Data Office
  • Assist management in the operational implementation of departmental activities and priorities
  • Monitoring of the team's performance indicators, including training, certifications and other KPIs defined by the Directorate
  • Coordinate administrative processes related to suppliers, contracts, orders, external providers and internal requests
  • Actively participate in the department's budget monitoring: expenditure control, tracking of provisions, orders and billing
  • Produce and update the dashboards, reports and presentations required to manage the activity
  • Contribute to compliance processes and continuous improvement of departmental administrative practices
  • Ensure a cross-cutting coordination role and foster collaboration between the various Data Office teams
  • For the benefit of a confirmed experience on a similar role, you have excellent organizational and planning capacity
  • You master the Microsoft Office suite perfectly and are comfortable with tracking, reporting and ERP systems
  • You have experience in budget tracking, order management and billing tracking
  • Rigoury and with an excellent sense of detail, you demonstrate a high level of professionalism and service
  • Your team spirit, patience, and ability to collaborate with a variety of partners allow you to build lasting relationships of trust
  • Proactive and powerful proposal, you like to improve processes and bring pragmatic solutions
  • You demonstrate a strong interest in IT, digital environments and new technologies
  • Curious and with a strong desire to learn, you enjoy evolving in a constantly changing environment
  • Finally, you are fluent in French and ideally have an English level B2 or equivalent
