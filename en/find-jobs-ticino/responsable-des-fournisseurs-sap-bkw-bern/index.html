Original description

Your field of activity

  • You control application service management across multiple services and are responsible for problem management
  • In overarching incidents, you will lead internal departmental task forces and ensure a rapid restoration of services
  • KPI establishes and monitors the role in order to make quality and efficiency in application service management measurable, derive development measures and take responsibility for implementation
  • You actively promote the continuous development of the service organization
  • As a central interface, you coordinate cooperation with SAP and other partners
  • You ensure that agreed services are adhered to and that further developments are specifically controlled

You bring that with you

  • Several years of experience in application service management as well as in vendor or partner management in the SAP environment
  • You understand IT contexts well enough to exchange views with technical experts
  • Communication strength is evident in dealing with different stakeholders in German and English
  • In challenging situations, you keep an overview and act in a structured and solution-oriented manner.
  • You act confidently, can represent interests and move safely in dealing with contracts and partners
  • An independent working method and a calm handling of pressure situations round off your profile
