Main responsibilities

  • You carry out audits and maintenance work on our production facilities independently and reliably.
  • You plan, document and realize line maintenance on schedule.
  • You will analyze faults sustainably, correct causes of errors and optimize plants through technical adjustments and conversions.
  • You support with machine and product changes as well as the installation and commissioning of new plants.
  • You produce special constructions, supervise external fitters and perform assignments in the emergency pikett. Requirements
  • Several years of experience: in the machine maintenance of a production plant, ideally in the food or pharmaceutical industry.
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): as a mechanic; further training as a maintenance specialist is an advantage.
  • Good knowledge of production facilities as well as practical experience in pneumatics and automation technology.
  • You tackle technical challenges systematically and are able to solve complex disruptions sustainably using drawings and revision documents.
  • You convince through a high customer orientation, a respectful treatment of others and an independent, solution-oriented working method.
