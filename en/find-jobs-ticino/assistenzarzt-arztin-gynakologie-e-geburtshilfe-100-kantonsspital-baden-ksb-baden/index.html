Original description

High-tech medicine and empathy: The gynecology at KSB offers you an exciting environment with minimally invasive surgical techniques, most modern therapies and individual care. You work interdisciplinary and can grow in areas such as oncology, birth aid and prenatal diagnosis. Your tasks:

  • You enter patients in the speaking hours and at the station and accompany the entire diagnostic and therapeutic process.
  • You assist in surgical operations, especially in minimally invasive surgery.
  • You focus on childbirth and support the team in the care of pregnant women and births.
  • You participate in in interdisciplinary meetings, tumour boards and further training.
  • You actively contribute to the optimization of patient care and consistently implement the Lean philosophy. Your profile:
  • You have a completed medical study (Switzerland or equivalent) and at least 1 year experience in a B-spital in the field of Gynaecology and Obstetrics as well as MEBEKO recognition.
  • You are interested in the entire range of gynecology and childbirth and ideally bring first practical experiences.
  • You are interested in innovative approaches, think entrepreneurial and like to work interdisciplinary
  • You are passionate about innovative approaches and are motivated by changes.
  • You have German knowledge at least at level C1 and communicate safely with patients and in the team. Their advantages: The KSB offers more than an exciting workplace with good working conditions.
