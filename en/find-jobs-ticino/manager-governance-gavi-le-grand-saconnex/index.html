Role overview

Position title: Manager, Governance Contract type / duration: 5 years Location: Geneva Department: Governance Team: Governance Reports To: Director, Governance / Head, Governance Career Step: 3 1.

The Manager, Governance ensures the effective functioning of the Gavi Alliance Board, its Committees and the IFFIm Board by delivering high quality governance processes, documentation and coordination.

The role sits at the centre of interactions with Board and Committee members, constituency representatives, Secretariat leadership, and Alliance partners including WHO, UNICEF, the World Bank, civil society and donor governments.
