Company and context

  • The Personnel and Organizational Service (SPO), which is a central service of the state of Fribourg, implements the management and conduct of human resources and the organisation of the canton.
  • The SPO acts according to the legal provisions as well as the policies and strategies decided by the Council of State.
  • In addition, it provides for the payment of pay at the operational level for all government personnel.
  • The main tasks of the Section for Personnel and Organisation Development are organisational and professional development, including initial vocational training and continuing training, management of business analysis and accompanying the budgetary procedure (post management).
