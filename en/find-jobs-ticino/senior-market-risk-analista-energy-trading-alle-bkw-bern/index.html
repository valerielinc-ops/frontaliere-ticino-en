Original description

Your field of activity

  • In this role, you will analyze and evaluate the performance of our trading activities and provide a sound basis for decision-making for trading.
  • Structured energy trading transactions, commodity derivatives and flexibility products are analyzed by you and consistently mapped along the trading and risk processes.
  • With P&L analyses and the plausibility checking and commenting on valuation effects and market movements, you ensure transparency and understanding.
  • Identify earnings and risk drivers, question trading performance using market analysis and KPIs, and derive valuable insights
  • As an interface between trading, risk management, reporting and systems, you work together across departments and continuously develop methods and processes.

Your expertise

  • Completed degree training in a quantitative or financial mathematical field (e.g. mathematics, physics, economics or finance)
  • Several years of experience in energy trading, risk management, structuring or middle office enables you to deal safely with complex issues
  • You have in-depth knowledge of commodity derivatives and complex energy trading transactions, as well as experience in mapping trading transactions in ETRM systems
  • Programming skills in Python, VBA or SQL help you work data-driven and optimize processes
  • Very good knowledge of English and good knowledge of German enable you to communicate clearly in an international environment
