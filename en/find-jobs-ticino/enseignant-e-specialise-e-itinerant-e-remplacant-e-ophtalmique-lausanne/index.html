Role overview

Recognized as a competence centre under the Specialised Pedagogy Act, the HLPF promotes the inclusion of people with visual impairments in Romandie.

It is part of the Asylum of the Blind Foundation, founded in Lausanne in 1843, which includes the Jules-Gonin Ophthalmic Hospital in Lausanne, the social service and low vision rehabilitation, the research centre for the sciences of vision and the EMS Clair-Soleil in Ecublens, a medical-social institution specially designed for visually impaired seniors. We're looking for a Specialty mobile teacher Your Mission

Ensure and ensure the availability of suitable teaching aids from 1H to 11H, with the support of a transcription service: enlargements, computer documents or Braille.
