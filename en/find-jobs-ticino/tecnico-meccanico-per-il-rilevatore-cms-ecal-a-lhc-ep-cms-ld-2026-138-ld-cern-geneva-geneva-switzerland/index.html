Role overview

Introduction

As a Mechanical Engineer, you will contribute to the development, procurement, assembly, testing and installation of innovative mechanical structures for the High Granularity Calorimeter (HGCAL) of the Compact Muon Solenoid experiment , working with advanced materials and challenging structural and thermal requirements.

You will help oversee the surface assembly and testing of the 250-tonne detector, including its CO ? cooling, dry gas, cabling and fibre services, as well as its installation and commissioning 100 metres underground.
