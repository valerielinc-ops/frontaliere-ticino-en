Original description

Bachtelen promotes and integrates children and young people in the canton of Solothurn with various offers. Among other things,

  • Early education, early logopaedia and day-care centres
  • School area with special schools and integrative special educational measures
  • Living with outpatient and (partial) stationary socio-pedagogical care
  • Therapy and counselling with diverse therapeutic supports

Your work with us makes sense and leaves a lasting mark. Become part of our team and shape the therapeutic work in Bachtelen with us!

Immediately we search at the location Solothurn

an occupational therapist (m / w / d) 80%

to strengthen our interdisciplinary team

What we offer

  • Collaboration in an interdisciplinary team – with various therapists and other specialists
  • Working with different children and adolescents – outpatients from public school, special school and early school
  • a supportive working environment – with space for your own ideas and independent work, intervision and exchange
  • Good employment conditions – according to cantonal guidelines with the possibility for further training
  • a professionally furnished room at the Solothurn workplace – for versatile ergotherapeutic work

Your tasks

  • Implementation of ergotherapeutic investigations
  • Therapies with children and adolescents
  • Advice for parents and caregivers
  • If interested: design of further development sequences

You bring that with you

  • A recognised training as an occupational therapist FH / HF or an SRK recognition
  • Professional experience of min. 2 years (this is necessary for the cantonal approval of the health directorate)
  • Enjoy working with children and young people as well as their environment
  • Interest in interdisciplinary, networked work
