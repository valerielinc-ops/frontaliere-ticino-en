Original description

You combine customer orientation with expertise in finance and technical affinity? Then a versatile role awaits you, in which you competently support customers, find solutions and actively contribute to the further development of our financial applications. Your role

  • You are the central contact person for customer concerns around our financial applications.
  • Together with the team, you ensure smooth and professional customer service.
  • You answer professional questions competently – by phone as well as in writing.
  • After a well-founded introduction, you will process customer inquiries independently and with a high level of service orientation.
  • You will analyze user problems, find suitable solutions and proactively inform our customers in the event of disruptions.
  • You pass on complex problems to internal specialist departments and clarify them in close cooperation.
  • You test new solutions and actively participate in projects.
  • You plan and conduct training for users on your own responsibility. Your profile
  • Completed commercial education and professional experience in finance and accounting (ideally in the municipal or cantonal environment).
  • Experience in customer support is beneficial, but not absolutely necessary.
  • High customer orientation and enjoyment of direct customer contact.
  • Enthusiasm for solving complex questions as well as the ability to keep an overview and act calmly even in hectic situations.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing. Your Benefits
  • Home office possible
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees We maintain an open you culture – so feel free to send us your application directly in the you form.
