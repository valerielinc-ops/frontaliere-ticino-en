The cooperative ZFV-Unternehmungen (ZFV) creates, in collaboration with its partners, inspiring meeting places that are enthusiastic about their hospitality and authentic experiences.

In more than 200 establishments in Switzerland, ZFV employees meet the needs of customers and guests.
