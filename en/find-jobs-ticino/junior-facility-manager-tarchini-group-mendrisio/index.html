The Tarchini Group, active in Ticino in various sectors, from design to construction and management of buildings (industrial, commercial, logistics and residential) as well as the management of FoxTown in Mendrisio, in the context of strengthening its own staff and to support the growth of the Group is seeking a Junior Facility Manager.

We are looking for a young and dynamic professional who is passionate about the construction and management of buildings and is able to work in a team.

The ideal candidate will have a strong technical background, excellent problem-solving skills, and the ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
