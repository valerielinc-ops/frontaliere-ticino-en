Original description

Data Engineer (m/f/d) - Asset Management Infrastructure Assets 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Data Engineer (m/f/d) - Asset Management Infrastructure Systems Bern / Work Smart, immediately or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. Welcome to the agile release train for technical operations of the SBB infrastructure! As a highly qualified data engineer, you will develop and support data-centric applications in the RAMSI team that significantly improve the operational operations of investment management. Together with the specialist departments, we make data from cancellations, train delays, inventory, weather and IoT usable via various channels to enable quick and precise problem solving

  • RAMSI is the fault memory of the SBB infrastructure. With your experience, you will strengthen our agile team and help to further develop RAMSI together using innovative solutions. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad
  • also for the family
  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • In this exciting role, you will build, operate and further develop data pipelines on SBB's enterprise analytics platform, which combines batch and streaming approaches.
  • You integrate a wide variety of data sources via APIs/Kafka, enabling analyses, dashboards and predictive maintenance.
  • You design data products and semantic models on Snowflake and Power BI.
  • You continue to develop RAMSI
  • from the web application (Java Spring Boot/Angular) to reports and dashboards for our specialists.
  • In close cooperation with the business, you record requirements and ensure sustainable solutions in the company.
  • You share your knowledge and experience in the team and contribute to professional development. This is what you bring with you.
  • Strong communication skills and team orientation with the willingness to work with our specialists to develop concrete solutions and promote the professional development of other data engineers.
  • Agile, independent and solution-oriented way of working with strong reliability and willingness to take responsibility for the quality and sustainability of technical solutions.
  • Several years of practical experience in designing, building, developing and operating cloud-based data platforms with a modern data stack (ETL/ELT, data warehouses, streaming, orchestration
  • e.g. Snowflake, DBT, Helm, Argo Workflows, Kafka).
  • Ability to design scalable and cost-effective data architectures and critically evaluate trade-offs between latency, consistency and cost.
  • Experience in data modeling (star/snowflake models) and reporting with Power BI, advanced SQL knowledge and knowledge of query performance optimization.
  • Software engineering skills in Python, including testing, CI/CD, packaging and deployment best practices.
