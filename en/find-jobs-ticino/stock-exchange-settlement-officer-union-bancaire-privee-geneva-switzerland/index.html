SECURITIES TRANSFERS OFFICER

6 MONTHS MISSION Mission

Ensure the clients' assets delivery/receipt for all the activities associated with the transfer of securities to a different financial establishment or within the Bank.

Main responsibilities

  • Main responsibilities
  • Crossed analysis of the received information: custodian’s swifts, agent prospectus, contract notes and financial providers such as: Bloomberg, Telekurs.
  • Perform control of reconciliation of holdings, entitlements determination, pending positions management.
  • Be able to manage and control securities transfer instructions received from Wealth Management and external parties (transfer IN/OUT/Internal and funds registration process).
  • Control transmission of the deposits to our correspondents/Transfer Agents/Administrators and control of their safe delivery and acceptance.
  • Follow-up of the topics and issues over the lifecycle of the securities transfer activities.
  • Good understanding of the risks inherent in the Corporate Actions environment related to ongoing securities transfers and demonstrate good discipline around control and escalation.
  • Ensure daily follow up in respect of pending issues and take appropriate measures for resolution.
