Role overview

You are on the pulse of time and provide the data for navigation systems, building plans and applications such as Google Maps. You travel a lot, missing construction sites, buildings, land and traffic routes. Your collected data is analyzed in geodatabases and processed in visualizations on the computer, in plans, maps and documents.

Tasks

  • On construction sites, you miss factory lines, axles and points precisely and reliably.
  • You collect relevant data with precision instruments and via satellite technology.
  • In the construction, engineering and safety measurement you actively participate.
  • For the planning of new buildings, you develop the basics, maps and plans.
  • You keep geometric data up-to-date in the GIS and prepare it professionally.
  • In customer service you maintain contact with customers friendly and competent.

Requirements

  • You enjoy technical design tasks and enjoy working on the computer.
  • With your initiative, curious and weatherproof nature, you bring a breath of fresh air to the team.
  • You approach your tasks with a precise and careful way of working.
