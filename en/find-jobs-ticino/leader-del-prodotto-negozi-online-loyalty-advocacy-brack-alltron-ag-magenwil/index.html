Original description

Tasks

  • Responsibility for product strategy and further development of loyalty, customer advocacy and the Brack App – in close coordination with marketing and on the basis of shared corporate and customer goals.
  • Development and communication of a clear product vision and a roadmap with a focus on customer benefit and business impact
  • Discover new opportunities and develop solutions together with stakeholders and the development team, from problem definition to feasible solution
  • Identification of customer needs, market potential and optimization possibilities along the customer journey as well as development of effective solutions
  • Prioritize initiatives based on customer value, business impact, effort and strategic relevance
  • Control of product decisions using KPIs, experiments and data
  • Working closely with the product leads of the other customer journey domains to ensure a seamless shopping experience across our B2C and B2B stores
  • Active stakeholder management between engineering, UX, analytics, marketing and other business areas
  • Working in an autonomous, cross-functional product team with short iterations and fast release cycles

Requirements

Product Management and Leadership

  • Completed study of business informatics, computer science, business administration or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience as a product manager or in a comparable role, ideally in the e-commerce or consumer environment
  • Demonstrate experience in the development of digital products with high user reach
  • Experience in product discovery, product initiative prioritization and data-driven decision-making
  • Experience with outcome-oriented product control and working with OKRs or comparable target systems
  • Experience in the definition and use of KPIs, experiments and A/B tests
  • Strong communication strength and very good stakeholder management skills
  • Ability to structure complexity, provide guidance and make informed product decisions

E-Commerce and Customer Experience

  • Passion for excellent digital customer experiences
  • Ideally, experience in at least one of the areas of loyalty, CRM, mobile apps, personalization or customer engagement
  • Understanding customer journeys and working with UX, Analytics and Engineering
  • Experience in the productive use of modern AI tools and a good understanding of how AI can support the development of digital products
  • Structured, independent and cooperative working methods as well as enjoyment of working together in an interdisciplinary product team

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Super-compulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business appointments.
