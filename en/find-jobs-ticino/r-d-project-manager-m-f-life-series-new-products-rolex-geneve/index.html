Role overview

Introduction

For our Research & Development division, we are looking for a (M/F) R&D Project Manager whose main mission will be to provide R&D support for process changes or differences in the performance of watch dressing detected in manufacturing flows.

As a team with our materials designers and engineers, and in close collaboration with the Quality and Production services, you will be responsible for leading the reliability approach.

  • Introduction
  • For our Research & Development division, we are looking for a (M/F) R&D Project Manager whose main mission will be to provide R&D support for process changes or differences in the performance of watch dressing detected in manufacturing flows.
  • Lead risk management in terms of design, industrial environment and performance targets to be achieved

Main responsibilities

  • Lead risk management in terms of design, industrial environment and performance targets to be achieved
  • Conduct, on the basis of numerical tests, experimental tests and experimental plans, an approach to quantify the robustness of the watch throughout its life cycle
  • Implement scientific, statistical and reliable approaches to support analysis and provide sound argumentation
  • To be a force of proposal and involved in the industrialization phases in order to participate in the analysis and treatment of the performance gaps in the production flow
  • Formalize results and communicate them through technical reports and presentations
  • Developing and sustaining business knowledge Profile
  • HES/EPF degree in physics, mechanics, microtechnics or equivalent training
  • Technical and scientific expertise, in particular on mechanical, measurement and characterization topics
  • Good knowledge of materials resistance, design and statistical analysis
  • Experience in project management, with the ability to manage multiple projects in parallel
