Original description

As part of the team of speakers, you will advise the department head FDFA in technical and political terms. The tasks shall include:

  • Examination and preparation of the dossiers of the assigned departments in the FDFA on the decision of the department head and the Federal Council;
  • Substantive examination and preparation of the assigned dossiers of other departments in the official consultation and for the Federal Council meeting;
  • Substantive preparation of the department head for his appearances in Parliament and its commissions;
  • interface between the assigned departments of the FDFA and the head of department and with contacts from other departments.
  • Master’s degree, several years of professional experience, preferably in a political environment (e.g. European policy, migration policy, UN system, IZA, parliamentary services, security policy, health policy, social policy);
  • in-depth knowledge of domestic processes and interrelations;
  • rapid comprehension and pronounced skills in analysis and synthesis – oral and written;
  • Pronounced quality awareness and the ability – even under time pressure – to check demanding documents for coherence, completeness and specifications;
  • structured working practices, loyalty, strong sense of responsibility and assertiveness;
  • high load-bearing capacity, stress resistance and flexibility in time-disposability;
  • excellent knowledge of at least two official languages;
  • Strong social skills and the ability to deal with contacts at all levels appropriately.
