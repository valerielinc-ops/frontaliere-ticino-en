Original description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. We are there for our customers with a lot of commitment. In our 31 cash-and-carry stores, via field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a diverse range, attractive own brands, professional advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet group. The company is a leader in Swiss delivery and collection wholesale and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).
