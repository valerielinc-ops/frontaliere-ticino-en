Original description

Reference code: JR133464 Since 1833, drive by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressly expressed its inventiveness through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication. YOUR MISSION: Support the Safety & Security department by leveraging your academic knowledge on a central occupational risk prevention project. Under the supervision of our Health and Safety Manager, your main mission will be to actively participate in the deployment of risk analysis in the manufacturing workshops of the Manufacture, directly contributing to the reduction of the risks of occupational accidents and to the improvement of the safety of our employees. HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT? Conducting field inventories: Develop hazard inventory in workshops, paying particular attention to specific hazards, and accurately estimate hazardous phenomena. Ensure regulatory compliance (SUVA method): Identify and ensure the application of all recognized safety rules, whether they are legal or derived from SUVA directives. Evaluate OHS skills: Define and structure the OHS skills required for field staff (e.g., chemicals handling or electrical pallets). Write and formalize: Develop security documentation (including job forms) and develop clear and applicable corrective action plans. Coordinate and lead: Plan, organize and conduct hazard inventory monitoring sessions with departmental managers. Leading Change: Actively participate in the operational deployment of selected technical and organizational improvements. HOW ARE YOU TO BE US? Training: Student in master's degree in engineering with specialization in HSE (Hygiene, Safety, Environment) or Risk Management. Technical knowledge: Good understanding of risk prevention concepts (ideally SUVA method) and mastery of common office tools (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook). Know-how: You have a high degree of autonomy, scientific rigour and a sharp analytical mindset. Communication: With an excellent sense of listening and communication, you appreciate contact with the field and have strong editorial skills to formalize clear relationships. Language: Perfect mastery of French in both oral and written terms. WHAT WE OFFER: A concrete and rewarding experience of 5 to 6 months in a renowned Manufacture. Proximity and quality coaching with our Health and Safety Manager to perfect your field skills. The opportunity to participate in an end-to-end HSE project with a direct impact on the health and safety of production teams.
