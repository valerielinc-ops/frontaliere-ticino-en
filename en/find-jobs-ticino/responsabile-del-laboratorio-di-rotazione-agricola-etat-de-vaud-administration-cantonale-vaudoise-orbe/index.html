Role overview

As part of the temporary strengthening of our team, we are looking for a future agricultural workshop manager in the Orbe Plains Establishments (EPO) under a one-year fixed-term contract.

As such, you accompany detainees in their day-to-day work by replacing the incumbent workshop managers in the various agricultural workshops.

You thus contribute to the continuity of activities, while ensuring that production, safety and supervision objectives are met.
