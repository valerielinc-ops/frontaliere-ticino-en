Main responsibilities

  • CORE JOB RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Provides the necessary technical leadership and subject matter expertise and oversees complex capital projects across all EPD sites to enhance capital execution predictability.
  • Defines and deploys enterprise standards, design criteria, and delivery expectations for site development, earthworks, foundations, buildings, cleanroom envelopes, structural systems, underground networks, construction sequencing, etc. for pharmaceutical manufacturing projects. Basic Engineering & FEP:
  • Lead civil input into feasibility, concept selection, site master planning, plot plans, utility corridors, logistics strategy, geotechnical assessments, constructability reviews, and early risk identification to avoid late design changes and delays.
  • Challenge concepts for simplicity, modularization potential, site access, laydown needs, crane strategy, prefabrication opportunities, and construction phasing to reduce cost, complexity, and schedule risk before projects are sanctioned. Detailed Engineering:
  • Ensure mature, coordinated detailed design packages are issued with clear construction work packs, interface management, clash resolution, permit inputs, and complete specifications to ensure competent field execution and reduce design gaps or rework.
  • Lead constructability reviews with engineering, site operations, EHS, quality, and contractors.
  • Verify that design reflects real field conditions, shutdown constraints, operational continuity, brownfield tie-ins, material flows, and GMP boundary requirements.
  • Commissioning, Qualification, Validation (CQV):
  • Ensure buildings, structures, drainage, roadways, access systems, equipment foundations, architectural finishes, and environmental conditions are completed to the required standard and handed over in a controlled manner to support safe commissioning starts.
