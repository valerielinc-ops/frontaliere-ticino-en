Original description

position in the development and production of high-quality industrial packaging and system solutions. Quality, innovation and sustainable growth form the basis of entrepreneurship. In the course of establishing a new production site in German-speaking Switzerland, we are looking for a committed and responsible personality as SHEQ Manager / Safety Officer (SiBe) 100% . In this responsible role, you assume overall responsibility for the areas of Safety, Health, Environment and Quality (SHEQ). You ensure that the local management systems comply with company standards as well as the relevant legal and normative requirements and are continuously developed. Your tasks:

  • Responsibility for the local SHEQ management system and its continuous development
  • Ensuring compliance with relevant legal requirements and the applicable management systems
  • Responsibility for quality management at the site
  • Processing of customer complaints and internal deviations including root cause analysis and implementation of appropriate corrective and improvement measures
  • Analysis of quality deviations and tracking of possible causes along production processes
  • Application of modern quality methods such as FMEA, MSA, 8D or 5 Why
  • Planning, implementation and monitoring of internal audits
  • Coordination of customer and certification audits
  • Further development and support of management systems, in particular ISO 9001 and FSSC 22000 as well as other relevant standards
  • Exercise of the function as Security Officer (SiBe)
  • Building and developing a sustainable culture of security and prevention
  • Planning and conducting SHEQ training
  • Close cooperation with the international SHEQ managers and promotion of cross-location knowledge exchange Your profile:
  • Completed technical studies or basic technical training with further training in quality management
  • Several years of professional experience in the quality management of a manufacturing industrial company
  • Ideally, experience in plastics, packaging, food or a comparable manufacturing industry
  • In-depth knowledge of the relevant management systems, in particular ISO 9001 and FSSC 22000; other standards such as ISO 14001, ISO 45001 or ISO 50001 are advantageous
  • Experience in planning, implementing and supporting internal and external audits
  • Strong technical understanding and experience in the analysis of quality deviations and production processes
  • Safe use of quality methods such as FMEA, MSA, 8D or 5 Why
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
  • Independent, structured and analytical working method
  • High level of initiative, responsibility and enforcement and communication This is what awaits you:
  • A responsible function with a great impact on quality, safety, environment and health
  • The opportunity to actively shape the development and development of local SHEQ management
  • International cooperation within a global group of companies
  • A modern industrial environment with short decision-making paths and open cooperation
  • A long-term perspective in a successful and sustainably growing company
