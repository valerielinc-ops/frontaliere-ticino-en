Original description

Who are we?

Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is a space for innovation and an important driver for economic and cultural life in the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Within the Administrative Directorate, you ensure the strategic and operational conduct (financial accounting, creditors, debtors) of the financial service
  • Development and implementation of a service strategy
  • Service Human Resources Management (approximately 15 people)
  • Closing of accounts and preparation of supporting documents
  • Specific financial monitoring and expertise, compilation of various counts and compilation of statistics
  • Monitoring of relations with external partners of the service (fiduciary, banks, state of Fribourg, Confederation)
  • Expert and sparring-partner for administrative management
  • Contribution to the University's overall strategic vision of finance
  • Contribution to the preparation of the financial plan and annual budget

Profile desired

  • You have training in finance and accounting (min. patent) and you have completed a continuing post-graduate training in the field of financial expertise and controlling
  • Ability to analyse results and control management
  • Mastery of SAP S/4 HANA software and office automation tools (Microsoft 365)
  • Several years of experience in project management and team management
  • Knowledge of or willingness to train in public administration processes
  • Excellent command of the French or German language with very good knowledge of the other language
