Role overview

As a digital health project leader within the Digital Health Centre, information system and monitoring, you are in charge of managing the implementation of the deployments of the electronic patient record (EDP), its various modules or other digital health tools according to the strategies of the intercantonal association Romande CARA or specific to the canton of Vaud.

Coordinate reflections within the Health Branch by making proposals to evolve the deployment strategy for digital health solutions
