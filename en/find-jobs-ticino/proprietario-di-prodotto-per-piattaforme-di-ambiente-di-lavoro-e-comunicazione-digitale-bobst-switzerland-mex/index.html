Digital Workplace & Communications Platforms Product Owner BSA - Bobst Mex SA Apply now Contribute to shape the future of work at BOBST At BOBST, we are transforming the way our employees collaborate, communicate and leverage AI to work smarter every day. Your key responsibilities Define and manage the Digital Workplace roadmap and priorities.

Lead the evolution of Microsoft 365 collaboration platforms (Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Viva).

Own and continuously improve the corporate Intranet and employee communication platforms.

Drive the deployment and governance of Microsoft 365 Copilot and other AI productivity solutions.

Ensure a high-quality employee experience through effective collaboration and communication tools.

Monitor platform adoption and performance through clear KPIs and insights.

Manage external partners, budgets and service providers.

Work closely with stakeholders across the organization to deliver impactful solutions.
