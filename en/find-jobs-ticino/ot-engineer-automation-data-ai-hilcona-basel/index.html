Role overview

You want to not only keep existing systems running, but also help shape the production of tomorrow? 🔍 As an OT Engineer, you will become part of our 6-member OT team and work at the interface between production, automation, data and IT.

Your sphere of activity extends significantly beyond a single production site: Together, we continue to develop our OT landscape throughout the Group and create the technological basis for more networked, automated and data-driven production processes.

A special focus is on the question of how we can use data, automation and in the future also AI in our industrial environment.
