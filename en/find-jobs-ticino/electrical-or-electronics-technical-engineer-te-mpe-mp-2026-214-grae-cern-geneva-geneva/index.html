Original description

If you enjoy understanding how things work, solving and learning through hands-on experimentation, this role could be for you. From testing advanced electrical and electronic systems, this role offers a unique opportunity to develop practical engineering skills in a world-leading research environment. As a member of the team developing CERN's 13 kA Energy Extraction System controls, you will contribute to system testing, troubleshooting, data acquisition, hardware installation and commissioning systems. Your responsibilities: • Set up instrumentation devices (oscilloscopes, measurement probes, function generators, etc.) and conduct commissioning and performance qualification tests. • Debug and troubleshoot electronic and electrical circuits. • PCB tests and validation. • Set up DAQ devices. • Data collection and analysis. • Test report preparation. • Assemble, test and install electronic chassis. • Test hardware performance validation. Your profile: • Experience in the installation, test, troubleshooting of electrical and/or electronic circuits and devices. • Experience with instrumentation (setting up measurement devices such as oscilloscopes, measurement probes, function generators, etc). • Experience of PCB production and tests. • Basic experience with set up DAQ systems and electrical/electronic signals data analysis. • Experience with test stations validating the performance of control electronic and electrical production would be an advantage. Skills: • Knowledge of electrical and/or electronic circuits, debugging and measurement techniques. • Basic knowledge of Electromechanical devices and maintenance. • Ability to write test reports and documentation. • Knowledge of using mechanical tooling during the installation of the equipment would be an asset. • Spoken and written English or French, with a commitment to learn the basics of the other language.
