Role overview

Tasks

  • You will lead the research department strategically and operationally and take responsibility for the professional and human leadership of your team.

  • Together with the head of the department, you will contribute to the establishment and establishment of Baselland Archaeology as a competence centre and research hub.
  • You lead and accompany research projects on findings and findings of all eras in the entire cantonal area as well as in the perimeter of the Roman city of Augusta Raurica.

  • You actively supervise and promote young scientists, for example by accompanying final theses and supervising internships.
  • You coordinate and participate yourself in the communication of our research results through lectures, scientific publications or the preparation for publication via our communication channels.
