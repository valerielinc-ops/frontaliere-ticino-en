The Position Data/Information Architect - Data Platforms

Roche Digital Technology (RDT) is where innovation meets purpose. Our mission is to power Roche through cutting-edge digital technologies, harnessing the potential of artificial intelligence, data, and scalable tech innovations. The specific office days may vary depending on business needs, such as workshops, conferences, town halls, team meetings, and other collaborative events.

We are seeking a visionary Data/Information Architect within the RDT Data Platforms function to lead the strategic design and implementation of our AI-driven Master Data Management (MDM) solutions. In this role, you will be the core architect enabling artificial intelligence across our MDM ecosystem, transforming legacy relational master records into intelligent, context-aware, and AI-ready data assets.

The first key priority of this architectural roadmap will focus on the modernization of Healthcare Professional (HCP) and Healthcare Organisation (HCO) master data programs , with a broader scaling strategy across alternative master data domains in future waves. A defining focus of your architectural design will be bridging the gap between traditional MDM platforms and advanced enterprise semantic layers, seamlessly transitioning master datasets into unified knowledge graphs that fuel cutting-edge Generative AI applications.

The Data Management and Data Quality Platforms area serves as the foundational intelligence engine of our global digital enterprise. Our mission is to scale a harmonized, FAIR-compliant master data framework that integrates heavily with an automated, AI-enabled Semantic Layer . By injecting contextual meaning, ontology standards, and relationship graphs into raw master data, we provide the enterprise with a single, high-trust, and discoverable view of our network. Joining this area means taking a leading role in ensuring that healthcare-specific data models remain contextually accurate, highly secure, compliant with global regulatory standards, and completely optimized for state-of-the-art machine learning deployment.
