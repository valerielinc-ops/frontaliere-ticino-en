Role overview

Gardener Role

Reporting to the Landscaping Team Leader, the gardener is responsible for the day-to-day maintenance of the green and landscaped areas of the Prilly site (flower beds, shrubs, lawns, turf, street furniture).

They are mostly out in the field and directly contribute to the aesthetic quality and safety of the site's outdoor areas.

Main responsibilities

  • and tasks
  • Routine maintenance: mowing, trimming of hedges and shrubs, weeding, watering, collecting leaves and green waste.
  • Specialised pruning: autonomous execution of training pruning, crown reduction and rejuvenation pruning on various shrubs, perennials and hedges, respecting seasonal cycles.
  • Lawn and soil management: diagnosing lawn problems (moss, diseases, thatch) and carrying out maintenance plans (scarification, fertilisation, overseeding).
  • Planting: setting up and maintaining flower beds and plants according to the plans defined by the Team Leader in agreement with the landscaping principal.
  • Winter service: participation in snow clearing and salting of the site's roads and paths.
  • Equipment maintenance: safe operation of machines and tools, daily technical maintenance (mowers, hedge trimmers, chainsaws, leaf blowers, etc.).
  • Efficient site organisation: following the schedule and tidying equipment, leaving the site clean and safe at the end of the day.
  • Safety: strict compliance with safety instructions, particularly when working with machinery or at height. Profile
  • Experience: 3 to 5 years of experience in professional garden and green space maintenance.

Key requirements

  • 3-5 years of experience in professional garden and green space maintenance
  • Good knowledge of plants, shrubs, hedges and related pruning techniques
  • Ability to work autonomously, rigorously and with a team spirit Valid driving licence
  • Availability for physical, outdoor work all year round
  • Ability to share know-how and support less experienced colleagues
  • SUVA certificates for working at height and/or chainsaw handling (appreciated plus) Strict compliance with safety instructions
