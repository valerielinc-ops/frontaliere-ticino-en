Original description

Tasks

  • Preparation and attractive preparation of food for the à la carte shop, seminar meals, banquets and aperitifs
  • Self-employed management of a kitchen post and support for other posts
  • Preparation of the daily mise en place for restaurant and event operations
  • Collaboration in the planning and implementation of seasonal menus, buffets and special events
  • Processing of fresh and, where possible, regional products

  • Shared responsibility for orders and economic use of goods
  • Compliance with HACCP guidelines and hygiene, safety and quality standards

  • Active cooperation with service, seminar and event organization to ensure a smooth process

  • Enjoyment of a varied cuisine with restaurant, seminar and banquet operation
  • Independent, structured and quality-conscious working method
  • Creativity and interest in the co-creation of food and offers
  • Teamwork, reliability and resilience even in hectic situations
  • Good knowledge of German, further language skills beneficial
  • Readiness for assignments on weekends and at events
  • High quality and hygiene awareness
  • Maintained appearance and a service-oriented personality
  • Discounts: up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop Group
  • Conditions of Employment: Loyalty Premium
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Further education: Support for further education and training offers from the LGAV and Coop
  • Sustainability: Hotel staff drink bottles: Together against plastic use
  • Communication: Du- & "Heartist" culture
