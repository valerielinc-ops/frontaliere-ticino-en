Main responsibilities

  • | What awaits you
  • As Global Lead, you will drive critical initiatives at the intersection of manufacturing innovation and sustainability, embedding green standards across our global sites.
  • You will take on the following key responsibilities:
  • Drive Manufacturing Innovations: Identify and guide technical studies enabling Design for Manufacturability —ranging from process engineering (e.g., particle technology, lyophilization/freeze-drying) to overarching technology topics such as solvent supply and disposal.
  • Provide Technical Guidance: Serve as a specialized advisor to project managers, offering expert scoping, optioneering support, and high-level troubleshooting across complex technical interfaces.
  • Lead Sustainable Manufacturing: Coordinate and lead a dedicated global matrix team in key technical domains (e.g., reagents, sensors) as part of the process Global Expert Panel (GEP).
  • Manage Sustainability Projects: Drive sustainability-focused project reviews (with a primary focus on Early Phases) and own key deliverables such as Sustainability One-Pagers .
  • Represent GCI Internally & Externally: Represent sustainable production within international networks, champion Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) commitments, and represent Roche in external expert forums (such as the Dechema Forum).
  • Stakeholder Management: Build and nurture strong, highly collaborative relationships with global partners across MSAT, R&D, and leadership.

Key requirements

  • | What you bring to the team
  • You combine analytical sharpness with enthusiasm, alongside a passion for driving meaningful, sustainable change.
  • Education: University degree (Master's/Diploma) or equivalent qualification in engineering, natural sciences, or a related discipline.
  • Professional Experience: 7+ years of relevant experience within engineering, manufacturing, or a highly regulated matrix environment.
  • Project Management & Sustainability Expertise: Proven track record in international technical project management, with deep knowledge of sustainability practices (including GHG reporting and Life Cycle Assessments / LCA).
  • Technical Knowledge: Broad understanding of modern manufacturing technologies and emerging industry trends.
