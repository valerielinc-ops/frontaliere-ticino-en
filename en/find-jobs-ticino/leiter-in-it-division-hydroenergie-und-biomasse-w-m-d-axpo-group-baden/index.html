Original description

Pensum: 80% or 100% | Bathing

Work in one of the most exciting industries in Switzerland – sustainable energy! Make a contribution to the energy future. In this role, you are the central interface between Business, Group IT and other divisions. You understand the requirements of the department, develop sustainable IT solutions and successfully advance digital initiatives. You assume responsibility for operational IT topics as well as for the strategic development of the division.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You work closely with internal stakeholders, Group IT and other IT leads
  • You translate business requirements into clear IT solutions
  • You lead or coordinate challenging IT and transformation projects
  • You are responsible for the entire lifecycle of divisons-specific applications and services
  • You ensure a stable and professional IT operation
  • You actively promote change and improvement processes
  • Keep an eye on budgets, risks and dependencies

Profile & Requirements

  • A completed degree in computer science, business informatics, engineering, business administration or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in an IT leadership role or at the interface between business and IT
  • Experience in project management and digital transformation
  • Strong communication skills and secure stakeholder management
  • Analytical thinking and a structured way of working
  • Joy of actively shaping change
  • Independent and solution-oriented working method
  • German, English and French

Are you a strategic thinker and at the same time a high-performance leader? Roger Hofer is looking forward to your application.
