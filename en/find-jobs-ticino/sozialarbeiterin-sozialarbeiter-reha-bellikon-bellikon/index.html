Original description

Membership, meaningfulness and competence With an experienced team, we accompany patients through ups and downs and help them to find their way back to life or work after an accident or illness.

accompanying people. Creating perspectives. Shaping the future. Want more than just advice? With us you accompany people on their way back to a life as self-determined as possible. With your social work competence, your empathy and your networked thinking, you support patients and their relatives in challenging situations. You will work closely with different specialist areas and can actively contribute to creating sustainable solutions for the period after rehabilitation. You can expect a varied and meaningful activity with a lot of creative leeway in a committed interdisciplinary team. If you want to use your expertise where it makes a real difference, we look forward to meeting you.

Tasks

  • You advise and accompany patients and their relatives on social and personal issues
  • Social security and financial concerns you clarify competently and solution-oriented
  • Organizing outpatient support for returning home is your responsibility
  • Suitable institutions and follow-up solutions you convey as needed and individual
  • With authorities, insurance companies and other organizations you network patients professionally
  • In the interdisciplinary treatment team you bring your social work expertise and work closely with different professional groups
  • Depending on the qualification and interest, you accompany and support students in their practical training

Profile

  • Completed training in social work (FH/HF) is mandatory
  • You already have in-depth knowledge of social security
  • Ideally, you have gained experience in clinical social work or healthcare
  • Even with difficult human fates, you act professionally, empathetically and solution-oriented
  • Flexibility, teamwork and resilience set you apart
  • A structured, independent and reliable working method is a matter of course for you
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Further language skills are advantageous
  • A CAS practical training is desirable

Pensum: 70%

Employment: indefinite
