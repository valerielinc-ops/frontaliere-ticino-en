Original description

Reference code: JR133762 Found in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the world At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timespieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches. Vacheron Constantin celebrates 270 years as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755. Based in Geneva, Switzerland, our House operates a worldwide network of nearly 200 outlets in 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional community – watchmakers, artisans and professionals – shares the same passion for our heritage and ambition to build an even brighter future. Within the Manufacturing department, the Product Line Manager is responsible for industrial processes (draft, termination, trimming) involved in the production of the same product family. In this context, it/it ensures a rigorous and daily management of its perimeter in terms of planning, resource management, management of corrective actions, productivity in compliance with the criteria of quality, quantity, costs, delays and safety. She/it accompanies both the industrialization of new product developments and the production in series life on her line and actively participates in the process of continuous improvement with the support of the Quality, Method and Technical Reference supports dedicated by process. The Product Line Manager leads a multidisciplinary team (from 20 to 35 people) representing several occupations in the machine and manual environment. YOUR ROLE TO PLAY?

  • Operational piloting Implement the production programme while ensuring daily compliance with the criteria of quality, quantity, costs, delays, security, Supervising the scheduling and supply of its perimeter, Coordinating, allocating the workload and ensuring the proper balancing of the line, Managing the activity and reporting (production tracking indicators), Achieving the progress of production in the ERP, Ensuring the consideration of the corrective and curative maintenance of its perimeter. Ensure the availability of equipment within its perimeter.
  • Coordination of problem solving according to the standard Maison Collaborate with the various departments concerned for the reliability and improvement of working methods and tools, Detect, report and manage technical problems specific to its perimeter, Collaborate with the various field partners (Quality, Methods, Technical Reference, Collaborators, counterparts...) to propose solutions allowing problem solving in relation to its field of competence.
  • Piloting the team Animating and coordinating the activity of the team daily (perf session, continuous improvement sessions...), Planning and organizing the work, supporting, empowering and motivating the team to achieve the results, Piloting the workforce (absences, leave, replacements, recruitment needs, rotations) and the means necessary to achieve the objectives, Conducting evaluation interviews (performance, skills) Promote the development of the team's skills under its responsibility, Defining and deploying the versatility plan necessary for the continuity of activity, Assessing the training needs (enriching the jobs, synergies and pooling of resources), Recruiting new collaborators in collaboration with HR, Guaranteeing compliance and enforcement of regulations (STS, internal regulations). QUALITY FOR EXCELLING? You come from an engineering study course or from the field of production management. You benefit from a solid expertise in industrial production management, ideally in a multisite context subject to short deadlines. You have evolved in an industry driven by the imperatives of high quality and excellence. You are able to mobilize, give meaning and accompany teams to collectively meet daily production objectives. You enjoy evolving in a challenging environment, requiring permanent agility. Ready to be part of our story? We will be delighted to discover your application.
