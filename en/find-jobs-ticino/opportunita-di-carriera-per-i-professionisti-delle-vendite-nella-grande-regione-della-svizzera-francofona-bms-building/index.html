BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire construction customers and private customers with a great deal of commitment on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

Career opportunity for sales professionals in the large region of French-speaking Switzerland!

You are a sales talent, passionate about strategic customer management, and you are looking for a new challenge from 1 January 2027? Then this post is for you! We are an innovative and forward-looking company, and we are looking for a National Key Account Manager motivated for the French-speaking Swiss market. In this function, you will be responsible for the monitoring and development of our main customer relations and will thus contribute decisively to the growth and success of our company. If you have a strong customer orientation, a real negotiating talent and strategic thinking, and you like to work in a dynamic team, then we'd like to get to know you! Be part of our success and actively build your professional future with us!
