  • In close cooperation with the co-management and the team, you represent a solution-oriented basic attitude.
  • They have a structured, strategic way of thinking and acting and have a good knowledge of ICT.
  • With the vision and core values of our school you can identify yourself.

Our school employs around 35 employees and teaches about 160 children in eight classes. The two kindergartens are organised in mixed years, while the primary school classes are run separately. Class lessons are supplemented by regular cross-class activities such as school house singing, project week, sports day, free activity, toy-free kindergarten and forest morning. Our primary educational goal is that children increasingly experience themselves as self-effective, creative personalities in their learning processes. To achieve this, we develop our teaching continuously and purposefully.
