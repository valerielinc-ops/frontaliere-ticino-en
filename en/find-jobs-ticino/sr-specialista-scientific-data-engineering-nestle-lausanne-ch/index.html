As a Scientific Data Product Manager at Nestlé Research in our Computational Sciences department, your mission will be to own the lifecycle of scientific data products across agriculture, packaging, clinical research, food safety, and health & nutrition scientific domains - reinforcing and increasing value of Nestlé Research's data foundations.
