Original description

In the cantonal public law institution, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implements public policies to promote and prevent mental health

  • It acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research.

The Human Resources and Relations Directorate (HRD) is seeking, as part of a digitalisation project of the HRD Directorate, its future:

Project Specialist Human Resources and Relationship Information Systems (HRIMS)

The HRS Information Systems Project Specialist contributes to the digitalization project of the Human Resources and Relations Directorate and, in particular, to the implementation of the HR core solution for the management of the institutional structure, personnel database data and payroll. Deputy to the RHR Management Officer in charge of project management, she is the second in the design and operationalization of the selected IT solutions, from defining needs to deploying an efficient and functional solution. In this context, it works closely with the HRR administration team, HRR executives, relevant internal stakeholders, the IT department and HRRD as the project mandator.

As part of your function you will be required to

  • identify HR management processes that require improvement or digitization to achieve service, quality, timeliness and efficiency objectives
  • design, as a team, the resulting target processes, forms, correspondence, etc.
  • obtain the opinions of key users in the design phase to meet their needs and facilitate their membership
  • ensuring the implementation, testing and continuous improvement of these innovations to the satisfaction of stakeholders
  • documenting know-how to ensure sustainability and repeatability
  • promote the adoption of new working processes both internally within the HR Directorate and with internal clients and contributors, in particular through communication and training actions
  • to provide and implement periodic checks and assist in the adoption of the work organisation essential for smooth implementation and quality of activities
  • monitoring control of new processes through quality controls of deliverables and data
  • give a structured feedback to the Head, the Director and the persons concerned.

As part of the project to design and deploy the new information system, you will also participate in:

  • develop and draft, in collaboration with internal and external stakeholders, a specification taking into account the existing mancos, the needs arising from the processes while taking into account the possibilities offered by the chosen solution
  • identify the impacts on other information systems and management processes and the resulting adjustment needs. Contributes to their implementation
  • Preparing for briefings / debriefing development
  • develop test scripts, run them both live and by involving key users, and formalize test returns
  • facilitate the adoption of the tool by the various stakeholders within the framework of the objectives set by its responsible (drafting of training materials, procedures, training facilitation, etc.)
  • identify project risks and set priorities

The competencies required for this position are

  • structure and manage an end-to-end project from design to deployment
  • understanding complex situations and obtaining robust, coherent and durable solutions
  • establishing forecasts, setting priorities and setting targets by coordinating resources and resources
  • know the HR business processes and the legal and regulatory requirements that govern them
  • make calculated decisions, initiatives and risks
  • seeking and obtaining an agreement between several interlocutors
  • develop clear procedures and understand how processes work
  • master document management and quality system logic
  • demonstrate an analytical and synthesis spirit to organize complex elements into a coherent and understandable whole
  • transfer knowledge or know-how by adapting its pedagogy to the audience concerned
  • adapt to changes in methods and tools, and approach the unknown with a positive spirit
  • be rigorous, with attention to detail and verification
  • love working as a team and working with colleagues to achieve common goals
