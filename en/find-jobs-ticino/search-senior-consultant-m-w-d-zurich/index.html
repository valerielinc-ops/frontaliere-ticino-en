More about this search for cross-border applicants

Zürich remains a magnet for senior consultants from northern Italy, where the demand for skilled professionals in finance, engineering, and IT aligns with the Swiss capital’s dynamic job market. With commutes between Ticino, Graubünden, and Valais often under two hours, many cross-border workers—known as *frontalieri*—leverage this proximity for career growth. The city’s international firms and startups offer competitive salaries, though tax obligations and residency permits require careful planning.
