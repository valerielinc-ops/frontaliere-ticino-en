Main responsibilities

  • On our reception and emergency station 60+, you will strengthen the team as a committed and experienced nurse HF or FH in the field of gerontopsychiatry.
  • As a caregiver, you assume the nursing responsibility for the individual care and care of our patients.
  • Even in complex situations, you accompany our patients safely through the nursing and therapeutic process.
  • Your versatile tasks include the design of everyday life, administrative activities and the implementation of medical and technical prescriptions. Requirements
  • Ideally, you have already gained professional experience in somatics or psychiatry or would like to deepen in this field.
  • Working in a multi-professional team and caring for people with age-related complexity interest you and motivate you in everyday work.
