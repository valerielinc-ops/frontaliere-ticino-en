Role overview

Tasks: Our newborns and premature babies are independently nurtured and cared for in all three shift services, with high personal responsibility and in close cooperation with our paediatricians and neonatologists.

  • You independently care for up to four premature or sick newborns and actively involve their parents in a holistic, family-centered care.
  • You carry out the care process according to the latest findings, supported by the clinic information system KISIM.
  • For the achievement of the divisional and departmental goals, you take on joint responsibility and are committed to the continuous optimization of care processes.
  • In addition, you support the Gebärsaal team in emergencies.

Requirements

  • You are a certified nurse (KWS / PFF HF / FH with focus F) and have at least six months of experience in neonatology.

Thanks to your professional and social skills, you can provide competent and sensitive care for premature and sick newborns.

  • You are a flexible, committed and motivated personality with a quick apprehension.
  • Good interdisciplinary cooperation is important to you.
  • The well-being of premature and newborns and their families is at the heart of your work.
