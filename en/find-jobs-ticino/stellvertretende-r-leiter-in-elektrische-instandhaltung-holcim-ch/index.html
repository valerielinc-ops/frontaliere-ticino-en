Original description

What to expect

  • You will independently maintain and control our electrical installations, measuring equipment and machines, always complying with safety regulations
  • You took over the deputy management of electrical maintenance and work closely with the division management
  • You plan and prepare maintenance projects and successfully commission new plants
  • You create and maintain high- and low-current systems and run circuits on high- and medium-voltage systems in compliance with regulations
  • The independent measurement and testing of low-voltage installations is in your responsible hands
  • You took the main responsibility for the training of our aspiring automatists:innen EFZ
  • You offer external service providers, supervise them on site and work constructively with production, quality management and other departments
  • You provide piketting services and take on additional flexible tasks when operationally necessary

What you bring

  • Basic technical training in the electrical sector (e.g. electrical installer in EFZ, automatic technician in EFZ) and a degree as a company electrician:in accordance with NIV Art. 13
  • At least 10 years of practical experience in control and electrical engineering
  • In-depth knowledge of motors, experience in the operation of high-voltage systems, shifting authorization and practice in working under voltage
  • Craftsmanship, an exact and precise working method and a quick understanding
  • Strong teamwork, independence, sense of responsibility, flexibility and strong planning and organizational skills
  • Good user knowledge in Google Tools, MS Office and SAP
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, good knowledge of English and driving licence/mobility in the care region
