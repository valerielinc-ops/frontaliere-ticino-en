Designer - manufacturer in Watchmaker Dress (M/F) - Swatch Group

Designer - manufacturer in Watchmaker Dress (M/F) Introduction of society Comadur SA, World leader in the production of hard materials components Swiss Made. We are part of the Swatch Group, the world's leading watchmaker. Recognized as the preferred partner of the Group's brands for the production of Ceramics, Sapphire Glaces and Clock Stones for the watch industry. We are always interested in meeting future colleagues wishing to contribute to the work of our teams with the help of their talents, ambitions and inspirations. As part of our growth and development, within the Mechanics Department on the Le Locle site, we offer a position of: Our profile

  • Your commitment will contribute to our growth as well as to your personal development through:
  • A modern and pleasant work space.
  • Social conditions of first order.
  • A dynamic, demanding team listening and always looking for new challenges.
  • A common will: to collaborate and grow together to become an indispensable partner.
  • Description of post
  • Realize the construction of the products according to the technical and aesthetic specifications of our customers and according to our construction rules
  • Make the technical dossier of the products: overview plans, nomenclature, detailed and operational plans, technical documentation and guarantee its availability through our PLM
  • Monitor and manage technical records throughout the life cycle of our products
  • Collaborate with Product Project Managers and Process Experts to define products and study their feasibility
  • Participate in defining and improving our industrial standards
  • Participate in continuous improvement projects
  • Profile
  • Basic training of designer-builder in microtechnics for the benefit of 5 years of experience in watchmaking design with very good knowledge of the dressing sector
  • Mandatory mastery of CAD software (ideally CATIA V5) and practice of using a PLM
  • Mastery of the Office suite, knowledge of an ERP is an advantage
  • Proposition strength, rigour, sense of detail, autonomy, team spirit and relational qualities
  • Contact person
  • We offer you a pleasant and stimulating work environment, as well as top social conditions.
