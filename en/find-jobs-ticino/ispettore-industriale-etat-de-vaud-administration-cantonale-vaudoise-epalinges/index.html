Role overview

As Director in charge of the Directorate for the Industrial, Urban and Rural Environment (DIREV) and within the Directorate-General for the Environment (DGE), you are part of its Steering Committee (CODIR) and are fully involved in its strategic management.

DIREV has a special mission within the DGE to protect the population and the environment against pollution, damage and nuisance caused by human activities.

DIREV has three divisions: Air, climate and technological risks (ARC), Water Protection (PRE) and Sanitation (ASS).
