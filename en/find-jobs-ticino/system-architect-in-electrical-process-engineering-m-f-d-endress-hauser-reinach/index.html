Original description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future of process automation. Whether developing new technologies, implementing innovative solutions or working with different global industries, our work is based on trusting relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and create a sustainable future for our employees, our customers and society. We are looking for a system architect* in Electrical & Process Engineering (m/f/d) to complement our team and to future-proof the technical architecture of our plants and equipment. At Endress+Hauser Flowtec AG in Reinach, we develop and produce innovative flow measurement technology for applications in a wide range of industries. What is this point about? Are you enthusiastic about seeing complex plants as a coherent overall system? Do you want to convert electrical, process and automation requirements into robust solutions? Do you enjoy shaping technical standards and bringing together different specialist areas? In this role, you develop holistic system architectures for plants and equipment and accompany important technical concept decisions. As part of our technical organization, you will work closely with specialists from mechanics, software, automation and engineering as well as with internal stakeholders from development and investment projects. What tasks will you take on? They shall: • Responsible for the electrical and signaling system architecture of plants and equipment • Develop, compare and technically evaluate plant and automation concepts • design complex plants at the overall plant level, taking into account process, functional and safety requirements, system and process engineering • Support development and investment projects in technical concept decisions and provide expert advice to internal stakeholders • Defining architectural standard
