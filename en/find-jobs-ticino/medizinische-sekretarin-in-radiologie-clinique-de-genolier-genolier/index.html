Original description

Medical Assistant •

  • Full time
  • Department: Genolier Clinic
  • Participation rate: 100 %
  • Start date: Next or to be agreed Company Description With a strong expertise, the Genolier Clinic is distinguished by its experienced and qualified team and its state-of-the-art medical equipment. The Genolier Clinic offers a wide range of services covering cardiology, neurology, oncology, orthopaedic surgery and plastic surgery. In addition, the clinic provides its patients with a wide range of prevention and rehabilitation services to promote their health and well-being. The reputation of the Genolier Clinic is based on its excellent medical infrastructure and cutting-edge technologies. These include a state-of-the-art diagnostic centre, a high-performance intensive care unit and an operating room equipped with the latest generation instruments and devices to ensure optimal quality care. Job Description Reception and Patient Relationship
  • Welcoming patients with professionalism, kindness and discretion
  • Ensure administrative registration and patient records management
  • Inform and guide patients throughout their care path Administrative management and organization
  • Plan and manage appointments by optimizing schedules
  • Manage phone calls and patient requests
  • Update medical records in computer systems (IPD)
  • Write and process medical and administrative correspondence
  • Order and inventory management (medical equipment, consumables, supplies), related to suppliers and the needs of the clinical assistance service
  • Preparing patients for consultations and examinations
  • Assisting health professionals in medical procedures
  • Perform some technical care (constant taking, blood taking, injections, dressings; ECG according to skills)
  • Ensure proper installation and patient comfort Coordination and communication
  • Liaising between patients, physicians, health care teams and external partners
  • Transmit information necessary for continuity of care
  • Participate in the coordination of patient journeys Billing and medical administrative management
  • Participate in billing and case tracking processes
  • Check insurance and care information
  • Ensuring compliance of administrative documents Quality, hygiene and safety
  • Respect existing hygiene, safety and protocols
  • Ensure confidentiality of medical data
  • Maintain a clean, organized and secure work environment Qualifications Training
  • Diploma of medical assistant or equivalent training (CFC of medical assistant or equivalent recognized in Switzerland)

  • Prior experience in medical, clinical or hospital practice
