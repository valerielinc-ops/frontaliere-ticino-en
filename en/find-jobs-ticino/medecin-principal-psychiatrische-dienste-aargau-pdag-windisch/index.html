Original description

For our center for developmental and neuropsychiatry (treatments for people with intellectual developmental disorder 18+) at the attractive location Windisch, which is unique in Switzerland, we are looking for a suitable reinforcement as of September or as agreed.

Tasks

  • Ensuring high-quality and contemporary psychiatric-psychotherapeutic care based on legal and operational framework conditions as well as specialist recommendations (inpatient, visiting and outpatient)
  • Leadership of the multi-professional and interdisciplinary team taking into account the values and leadership principles of the PDAG
  • Ensuring the achievement of current annual targets as well as other quantitative and qualitative goals of the organizational unit
  • Ensure careful structured case documentation
  • Ensuring professional training or "on-the-job training" of subordinate employees as well as training supervisions
  • Cooperation in the multi-professional and interdisciplinary team
  • Participation in PDAG’s initial, further and further training programmes
  • Public relations and networking in the context of different events
  • Participation in projects, orders and adjustments according to clinic management
  • Participation in the PDAG service system

Requirements

  • Completed further training for the specialist title Psychiatry and Psychotherapy
  • Interest in the special field of developmental and neuropsychiatry
  • Responsibility, reliability and teamwork
  • High social skills, independent and efficient working style and high resilience
  • Enjoyment of multi-professional and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Identification with our company values: courage, commitment and trust
  • Very good leadership skills (CAS Leadership)
  • Strategic and business competences
