Role overview

Mission

We are seeking a dynamic, detail-oriented and bilingual Internal Communications Specialist to join the Group Communications team on a temporary 6-month assignment.

The role is designed to provide hands-on support to the Internal Communications Manager in the day-to-day delivery of internal communications activities, employee engagement initiatives and editorial content across the organisation.

Main responsibilities

  • Main responsibilities
  • Support the Internal Communications Manager in the development and execution of the internal communications plan and related initiatives.
  • Draft, edit, proofread and publish internal news, announcements, and campaigns in English and French.
  • Prepare, coordinate and send the daily internal newsletter, ensuring accuracy, consistency and timely publication.
  • Act as a back-up for the preparation and distribution of internal communications when required.
  • Maintain and update the intranet, including editorial content, employee resources, calendar of events and other relevant pages.
  • Draft internal surveys, support the analysis of results and help identify insights to improve employee engagement and communication effectiveness.
  • Contribute to the internal magazine, including content planning, article drafting, coordination with contributors and follow-up with the graphic design team for visual content creation.
  • Support the preparation of communication materials for internal events, townhalls, campaigns and employee engagement initiatives.
  • Monitor internal communication channels and help ensure messages are clear, consistent, timely and aligned with the organisation’s tone of voice.

Key requirements

  • Minimum of 3-5 years of experience in internal communications, corporate communications or a related communications role, ideally within the financial industry.
  • Degree in Communications, Business Communication, Marketing, Journalism or a related field is a plus.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English and French, with the ability to write clear, concise and compelling messages for different internal audiences.
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple tasks, priorities and deadlines effectively.
