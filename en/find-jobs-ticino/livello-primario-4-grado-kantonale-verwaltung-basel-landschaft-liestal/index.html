The city of Liestal, capital of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, is one of the most important workplace and business locations in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft with around 16,000 inhabitants and around 16,500 jobs. Primary level Liestal is an innovative school and is organized in 5 school circles. We attach importance to good quality, which we also regularly have independently checked and are Q2E certified. Integrated training is an important focus of our pedagogical orientation. Dedicated teachers and around 1200 children experience a diverse and pedagogically motivated school day.
