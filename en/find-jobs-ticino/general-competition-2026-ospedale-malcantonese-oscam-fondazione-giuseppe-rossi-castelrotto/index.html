General Competition 2026 at the Malcantonese Hospital OSCAM (Fondazione Giuseppe Rossi), Casterotto (Malcantone, Canton Ticino).

OSCAM is a hospital of acute care based in Casterotto serving the Malcantone region.

The Giuseppe Rossi Foundation manages departments of internal medicine, surgery, psychiatry, obstetrics-gynecology and palliative care, as well as a first aid and outpatient services for the population of the Lugano-Malcantone district.
