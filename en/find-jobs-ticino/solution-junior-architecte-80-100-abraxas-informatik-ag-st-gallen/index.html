Original description

We connect Swiss administrations, authorities, companies and the population with efficient, secure and consistent IT solutions and services. To further expand our architectural and consulting expertise, we are looking for a strong, entrepreneurial and technically-savvy personality as a leader: in Solution Architecture. In this key role, you will take over the professional and personnel leadership of a team of 5 Solution Architects:innen. Together with your team, you will design future-proof IT architectures, cloud and outsourcing solutions and technical concepts for our customers in the public sector – especially for the federal government, cantons, municipalities and other public sector institutions. As a member of the Extended Sales Board, you actively bring the perspective of solution architecture to strategic, operational and business-relevant discussions. You combine technical excellence with leadership, customer orientation and entrepreneurial thinking. Your role

  • Personnel and professional management of 5 Solution Architects: including coaching, development, prioritization and resource management
  • Further development of solution architecture competencies within consulting services – both methodologically, technologically and in terms of supply and customer situations
  • Design (IT architectures, IT standards and technical concepts for offers) of on-premise & cloud solutions (private, hybrid and public cloud from Microsoft, AWS or Google)
  • Processing of public tenders and offers (technical lead, design and development of the technical solution, pricing, presentation of the solution to our customers)
  • Collaboration in the design of new IT solutions and future services
  • Advising and supporting our customers in their IT projects, architecture issues and strategic technology decisions
  • Development of concepts of complex IT platforms in cooperation with our engineers and external partners
  • Identify and introduce new innovations, technologies and tools that drive business success
  • Active participation in the Extended Sales Board with focus on strategic offerability, innovation potential and further development

Your profile

  • Completed degree in computer science or business informatics (FH/Uni/ETH) or equivalent education
  • Several years of experience in team management, professional management or development of architectural, consulting or technical expert teams
  • Several years of professional experience in IT architecture, ideally in the development of complex IT systems
  • In-depth knowledge of cloud technologies (e.g. Azure/Microsoft 365, Google, AWS) and their architecture principles
  • Strong communication and moderation skills to convey complex technical content in a comprehensible way, as well as analytical thinking, problem-solving skills and high quality standards
  • Very good communication and presentation skills in German (C1 level) and good English skills
  • Certifications in the area of public cloud or IT architectures (e.g. Azure Solutions Architect Expert / Enterprise Administrator Expert) or Togaf round off your profile.

Your Benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% workload
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or time training as well as active support for certification
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees
