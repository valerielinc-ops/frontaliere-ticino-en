Original description

Do you have a soft spot for the administrative handling of export transactions and would you like to assume responsibility in a dynamic environment? Then you are exactly right in our forwarding team in the field of Manufacturing and Logistics. In this versatile function in a dynamic environment, you can independently handle international export and cross-trade transports for machines and plants. You coordinate deliveries from various worldwide production sites and advise sales and project management on Incoterms, transport concepts and customs and document issues.

Tasks

  • They coordinate international deliveries from different production sites, organise multimodal transports and, in particular, manage international transport processes
  • They draw up and verify export-, customs- and accreditation-compliant transit and commercial documents
  • They collect transport quotes and evaluate them in terms of costs, duration, routing, equipment and possible risks
  • You hire freight forwarders, monitor shipping dates and coordinate the entire transport process
  • They ensure compliance with all relevant customs, export control, origin and preference requirements and communicate proactively with internal interfaces, international locations and external partners.

Requirements

  • Basic commercial training and several years of experience in administrative export processing
  • Further training in foreign trade or in the forwarding industry is advantageous
  • In-depth knowledge of Incoterms, export and customs documentation and international transport, especially in the field of ocean freight
  • Safe handling of transport documents, commercial invoices, packing lists and proofs of origin and preferences
  • Experience with Letters of Credit, project logistics or machine and plant export is an advantage
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method as well as strong communication skills and pleasure in working with internal and external partners
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing as well as good MS-Office skills; SAP experience is beneficial
