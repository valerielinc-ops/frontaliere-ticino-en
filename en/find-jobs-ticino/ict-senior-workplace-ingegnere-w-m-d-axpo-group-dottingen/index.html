Original description

In this role, you will design and optimize modern workplace services for Axpo’s nuclear energy computing with a focus on safety, automation and user experience. As an ICT Senior Workplace Engineer, you take over the quality management responsibility for the AI Workplace environment – from technology to operation – and ensure reliable, stable and traceably controlled service delivery. For the new nuclear energy informatics at the Leibstadt KKL, Beznau KKB and ZWILAG nuclear power plants, you provide workplace services that meet the high nuclear requirements for safety, compliance, availability and quality, use synergies with Axpo Group IT and ensure a consistent quality level on all devices. Through clear standards, clean documentation and close cooperation with relevant stakeholders, you ensure a stable, secure and sustainable virtual and physical workplace. With your expertise in virtualization, automation and security-by-design, you ensure that our highly critical systems are available at all times and that the high safety culture is lived in the field of nuclear energy. If you enjoy complex infrastructures, like to take responsibility from design to operation and feel comfortable in a technically demanding environment with a high safety culture, you are at the right place with us.
