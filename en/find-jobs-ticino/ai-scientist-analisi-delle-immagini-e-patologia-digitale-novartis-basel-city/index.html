  • This role will apply advanced AI approaches to generate insights from complex imaging datasets and support our oncology drug discovery programs. Job Description
  • Develop and apply AI / deep learning approaches to extract insights from digital pathology imaging datasets (e.g., H&E, IHC, mIF, Spatial transcriptomics).
  • Work with foundational models, including pre-trained, self-supervised, multi-purpose, and multi-modal models to advance generative AI applications in drug discovery.
  • Support imaging biomarker development efforts by analyzing pathology imaging data and collaborate with pathologists and translational teams to interpret image-derived biomarkers and biological findings.
