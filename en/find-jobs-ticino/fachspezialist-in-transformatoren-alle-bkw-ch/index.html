Original description

Your tasks

  • In this versatile function, you support the assembly as well as the maintenance, conversion and dismantling of power transformers in high-voltage systems
  • You carry out your tasks directly on site in BKW substations, power plants, in the workshop and at our customers:innen After a careful introduction and targeted training courses, you increasingly take responsibility on construction sites and make an important contribution to the successful implementation of our projects
  • In addition, if necessary, you support various activities in other areas of our organization. You have completed an apprenticeship EFZ in the electrotechnical field
  • You have experience with primary technology devices, experience and knowledge with power transformers are an advantage
  • Ideally, you have already gained initial experience in the service sector and are familiar with safety regulations in high-voltage systems
  • A driver’s license in the category BE (with trailer) is a requirement, with the category C/CE and truck crane you bring another advantage
  • You master the German language fluently in word and writing (min. B2 level) and have good IT knowledge in Office 365
  • You bring travel readiness within Switzerland and are flexible for assignments with occasional overnight stays during the week We do not want requests from personnel service providers. We look forward to your online application. BKW offers its customers internationally complete solutions in the fields of energy, buildings and infrastructure. We employ around 11,500 people. Together we build networks, produce energy, plan and realise energy-efficient buildings and design sustainable living spaces of tomorrow. For this we need you: people with ideas and commitment. Make a valuable contribution to society and our environment with us!
