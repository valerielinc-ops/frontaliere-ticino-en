Role overview

Reporting directly to the Digital PR & Special Events Manager, the Digital PR & Events Intern will assist the Press & PR team in all activities across 11 European markets, serving all Guess product categories.

The Digital PR & Events Intern will assist the team across both Press and Digital PR activities, including influencer collaborations, product seeding, creative mailers, press kit preparation, campaign and product launches, media monitoring and reporting, media and influencer research, as well as PR and special events.
