Role overview

Under the supervision of the medical and technical managers of the LTCHO, the tasks and responsibilities of the technician are to take charge of the entire process of preparing human tissues for their distribution for transplant purposes, that is, you receive the cells taken from the donor from Switzerland or elsewhere.

You prepare, analyze and store these cells in a controlled environment (clean room, GMP).

You prepare samples for flow cytometry analyses.

You produce production files before transmitting them to the quality control manager.

You prepare and freeze fetal-placental blood for the bank.

You prepare the feto-placental blood files for the bank before transmitting them to the quality control manager and sending them to the SBSC.

You collaborate in the proper functioning of the laboratory and its quality by ensuring the maintenance work of the materials and equipment used, including technical and biological validations, in collaboration with the staff of the LTCHO laboratory and that of the LTCC platform.

You participate in the administrative management of the laboratory.

Missions may vary depending on the specific needs of the laboratory.
