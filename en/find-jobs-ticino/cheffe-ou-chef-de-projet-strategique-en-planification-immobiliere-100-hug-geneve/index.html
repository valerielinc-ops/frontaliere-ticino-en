Role overview

The HUGs are implementing their new institutional strategy 2026-2030, structured around five strategic axes.

Research and innovation play a central role in stimulating research, innovation and digital transformation.

Within this framework, the flagship accomplishment 3.1 "Translational Research Programme" of axis 3 of the institutional strategy aims to better structure, simplify and connect the entire research and innovation ecosystem in order to accelerate the transfer of scientific discoveries to clinical practice.
