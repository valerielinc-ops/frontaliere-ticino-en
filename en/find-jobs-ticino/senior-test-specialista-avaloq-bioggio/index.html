Original description

Responsible for all test activities throughout testing life-cycle of Avaloq solutions for key customer accounts.

Customer facing role requiring excellent presentation and communication skills and ability to manage stress levels in demanding project contexts.

Your key tasks

  • Lead the project-based test team, enabling it to deliver the highest level of quality across our testing services and practices
  • Define Test Policy and Test Plan
  • Manage customer relationship with appropriate communication and coordinate testing activities with customer Test Manager
  • Monitor and control testing activities against planned budget and quality gates
  • Report progress to management and customer
  • Design, update and execute functional and business test scenarios and test cases based on functional and non-functional requirements, ensuring their re-usability
  • Manage test environments (baseline definition and maintenance, assessment on environment readiness)
  • Manage tools for testing, tracking defects, statistics and requirements coverage reporting
  • Documentation and evaluation the results of the testing

Qualifiche

  • University degree in Engineering, Banking, Finance, Business or Economics
  • Minimum of 5 to 8 years work experience within the banking/financial industry
  • In-depth knowledge of test concepts and methodology
  • Working knowledge of QC/ALM, JIRA, MS Office Suite
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving competences
  • Ability to work in a highly challenging, fast-paced and complex environment
  • Willingness to travel and work on-site with clients locally and in EMEA region
  • Excellent written/verbal communication skills with an emphasis on client service and presentation
  • Fluency in English

It would be a real bonus if you have

  • ISTQB Certification or equivalent
  • Experience with Avaloq banking system solution is a strong plus
  • Experience of testing in agile context: (Continuous Integration, Continuous Testing, TDD, BDD, API testing)
  • French, German, Italian language skills
