Original description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

Tasks

  • When loading and unloading trucks and loading train cars, you show your skills and work safely and efficiently
  • You complete storage and removals sovereignly and keep the overview at all times.
  • You carefully control goods and exchange equipment for completeness and condition
  • You accept incoming goods and process them routinely with the MDE device
  • Delivery notes and loading documents you process correctly and reliably
  • You consistently comply with ADR and storage regulations – Safety comes first for you
  • Forklift trucks, drawbar trucks and fast runners you serve safely, routinely and responsibly

Profile

  • Your working hours are from 05:00 to 14:00 or 14:00 to about 23:00 (two-weekly change)
  • You already have experience in the goods outlet and know what matters when loading
  • You are the proud owner of a forklift pass (R2/S2) - R1 is an advantage
  • Oral and written you can safely express yourself in German
  • Teamwork is yours, and you stay flexible and relaxed even in stressful moments
  • Physically, you are fit, resilient and tackle your tasks with energy

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
