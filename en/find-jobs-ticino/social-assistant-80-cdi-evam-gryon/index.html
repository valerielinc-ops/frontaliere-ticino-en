Original description

EVAM (Etablissement vaudois de l'accueil des migrants) is a major player in the voodoo public service. With more than 1,000 employees and an annual budget of 360 million francs, we welcome, support and integrate migrants in the canton of Vaud.

Every day, our teams work with openness and responsibility to address key social issues. Joining EVAM means putting its skills at the service of a human mission and of public utility, in a stimulating, diverse and constantly evolving professional environment.

As part of an internal change, we recruit one

Social Assistant (80%) – CDI Location: Blonay Entry into office: As soon as possible [Salary class: 7](https://www.evam.ch/app/uploads/2026/06/Bareme-salarial 2026.pdf) (This scale refers to validated prerequisites, failing which classification is done at grade level -1)

What you bring to our mission

In connection with a multidisciplinary team, you play a central role in providing individualised social support, organising care according to the standards in force, contributing to the life of the home and its proper functioning.

The word of your future leader, Fabien

"In the heart of the vineyards and overlooking the lake, the Foyer de Mottex welcomes a population with varied routes, carrying multiple needs and different realities of life. In this exceptional setting, I propose a working environment that is both demanding, stimulating and deeply human.

I am convinced that the quality of the accompaniment depends on the strength of the collective. That is why I prefer a multidisciplinary team, based on solidarity, respect for each other's roles and close collaboration in the service of the beneficiaries. Each professional plays an essential role and contributes fully to a meaningful common project.

Beyond individual accompaniment, I pay particular attention to the life of the home. The facilitation and development of initiatives promoting social bonding, participation and living together are an integral part of my approach.

Joining Mottex's home means integrating a committed and dynamic team into an environment where human beings, cooperation and the sense of mission add value to social work."

Your key missions

  • You are in charge of administrative monitoring and social interviews.
  • You set up a custom action plan.
  • You support beneficiaries in social, professional and administrative integration.
  • You coordinate with internal and external partners.
  • You ensure that the rules of the home are respected.

Your profile

  • You have Bachelor HES in social work (recognition SEFRI if foreign diploma).
  • Minimum. 3 years of experience in a similar function.
  • You have a good knowledge of the Vaud network and social insurance.
  • You have an excellent Frenchman (oral and written); Mastering English or other languages is an asset
  • You have a strong interest in migration.

What EVAM proposes

  • A committed working environment
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 from 50 years)
  • Very good social benefits
  • Meaningful projects in a stimulating intercultural context

The three-step recruitment process

  1. An interview with the head of the household and the.e.
  2. Situation
  3. Half a day of immersion to discover your future working environment

At the end of the process: request for an extract from the criminal record, special extract and certificate from the prosecution office (undocumented documents required).

Do you recognize yourself in this mission? We look forward to discovering your candidacy and building together a solidarity future.
