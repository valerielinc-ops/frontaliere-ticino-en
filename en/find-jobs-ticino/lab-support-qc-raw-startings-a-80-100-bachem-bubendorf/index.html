Original description

To strengthen our QC Raw & Startings team, we are looking for a new team member as Lab Support QC (a) 80%-100% to support laboratory activities.

Their tasks

  • General support activities in various analytical laboratories incl. Laboratory maintenance (disposal and cleaning)
  • Ensure supply of laboratory infrastructure such as procurement of consumables and chemicals, refilling of material cabinets and distribution of deliveries within the work area.
  • Equipment maintenance, maintenance and calibration of laboratory equipment (e.g. analytical balances, pipettes)
  • Documentation according to GMP guidelines

Your profile

  • Completed school education and experience in chemical-related professions
  • Engaged and motivated personality with pleasure in working in a motivated team
  • Quality-conscious, exact and service-oriented working method
  • Team-capable, reliable and flexible personality
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Motivated to participate in changes and contribute ideas
  • Knowledge of handling chemicals or food
  • Prior knowledge in the GMP environment
  • English skills in word and writing
  • Experience in dealing with a laboratory and information management system (LIMS / SAP) desirable

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus

Do you want to drive innovation together with us? Then we look forward to your application (incl. cover letter).
