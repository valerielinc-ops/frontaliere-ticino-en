Role overview

Become part of something bigger.

Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life.

On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you.

  • Become part of something bigger.
  • Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life.
  • Under the supervision of a cadre doctor, you supervise patients in the spinal surgery consultation and support them in the care of inpatient patients
  • You assist with operations, infiltrations on the spine and the large joints and can carry out these under supervision depending on your training status.

Main responsibilities

  • Under the supervision of a cadre doctor, you supervise patients in the spinal surgery consultation and support them in the care of inpatient patients
  • You assist with operations, infiltrations on the spine and the large joints and can carry out these under supervision depending on your training status.
  • Administratively, you will be relieved by the Department of Acute and Rehabilitation Medicine in the inpatient care of patients, which will allow you to focus more on medical matters
  • You work closely with colleagues in acute medicine, rehabilitation, pain clinic, plastic surgery, radiology, physiotherapy, occupational therapy and many other specialist areas
  • You actively participate in teaching, research and further education Requirements
  • You have several years of clinical experience with discernible spinal focus.
  • Ideally, you are close to completing your specialist training and intend to complete the specialty training spinal surgery SGNC/SO.
  • You have completed a medical degree (for foreign diplomas with MEBEKO recognition) and have very good oral and written knowledge of German (at least level C1).
  • You are a team-capable, committed and resilient personality with high social and professional competence.
  • Great sense of responsibility, an open and pleasant communication style and the willingness for interdisciplinary / interprofessional cooperation distinguish you.
