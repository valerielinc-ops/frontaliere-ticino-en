Your role and responsibilities
We are looking for a Power Systems Architect to join our Direct Current Data Center Incubator, playing a critical role in driving the transformational shift toward next-generation Direct current data center architectures. Working closely with strategic customers - especially hyperscalers - you will help shape the future of data center architectures by translating complex customer challenges into innovative, optimized electrical solutions.

In this highly visible role, you will act as a trusted technical advisor across the full project lifecycle, combining deep technical expertise with strong consultative skills. You will influence market opportunities, support customer adoption, reduce churn, and enable flawless execution of pioneering Direct Current data center projects.

The work model for the role is: hybrid (#LI hybrid)

This position reports solid line to the Solid State Transformer Program Manager and dotted line to the Direct Current Datacenter Incubator Manager.

Key • Responsibilities

  • In collaboration with ABB data center segment and account managers partners closely with key customers - particularly hyperscalers - to support their transition from conventional AC data centers to advanced Direct Current data center architectures.

  • Design and architect end-to-end electrical power system solutions for data centers, delivering competitive, scalable, and future-ready system architectures.

  • Design protection and control— Designing Direct Current ‑specific protection schemes and overall control schemes for the future architecture including sequence of operation, managing the load and active monitoring.

  • Manage customer and market requirements and align the priorities for the product development teams.

  • Collaborate with internal engineering and product teams to identify technology gaps and influence product roadmap evolution.

  • Lead and support pilot projects, ensuring successful early-stage execution and delivery of novel Direct Current data center solutions.

  • Act as a technical authority and customer advocate throughout the sales, design, and delivery phases.

  • Contribute to technical thought leadership in emerging Direct Current power architectures and industry trends.

  • Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering or a related discipline.

  • Experience: 5+ years of experience in power system design, consulting, or solution architecture.

  • Strong background in data center power systems, or exposure to Direct Current power solu-tions (experience in adjacent industries such as marine or industrial Direct Current systems is highly valued).

More about us

ABB's Distribution Solutions Division facilitates the efficient and reliable distribution, protection and control of power by improving electric power quality while strengthening the resilience of the grid. The Division offers segment-specific products and solutions that largely serve utilities, industry and infrastructure segments, often providing the requisite medium-voltage link between high-voltage transmission systems and low-voltage users. With ABB Ability™ enabled connected solutions at its core, the offering includes medium-voltage air- and gas-insulated switchgear (1 to 66 kilovolts), indoor and outdoor circuit breakers, reclosers, fuses, contactors, relays, instrument transformers, sensors, motor control centers, as well as low-voltage switchgear for the ANSI standard markets.
