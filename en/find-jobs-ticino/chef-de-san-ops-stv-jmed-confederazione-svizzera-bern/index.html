Original description

You can make this contribution

  • Design the management process at the operational level, assess the feasibility of operations at the medical level and represent JMed professionally and organizationally
  • Monitor the army's readiness to serve in the medical service, develop risk management and create the paramedic situation picture
  • Develop medical guidelines, concepts and decision-making bases for operations and coordinate implementation with internal and external partners
  • Analyze missions, define indicators and derive measures for the further development of the department and contributions to the army command
  • Representation of the medical department in projects, working groups and specialised committees, leadership of militia members and implementation of the safety requirements of the firearms

This makes you unique

  • University or Fachhochschul degree (Bachelor) or higher technical or business training with several years of professional experience
  • Training and practical experience in project and process management as well as sound knowledge of military management and medical processes at operational level
  • Several years of experience as a staff officer and strong analytical, conceptual and organizational skills
  • knowledge and practical experience of the medical profession in the international context and willingness to train in this field
  • High social and leadership skills, independent and responsible working methods and a secure attitude towards internal and external partners
  • Very good knowledge of two official languages and good English (at least B2)

Their mission for security and freedom The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss army. The Kdo Op includes the Air Force, the Army, the four Terri-torial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Centre, the Command Special Forces and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are civilian workers – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs over 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions – and this is done at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Young people are also provided for: The Swiss Army trains students in over 30 professions.
