The Directorate for Spatial Development, Infrastructure, Mobility and Environment (RIMU), through its offices, ensures responsible spatial development, mobility and adequate protection of the environment, provides efficient, secure and needs-based transport infrastructure and ensures efficient management of state-owned properties.

Sustainable development and agglomeration policy are also subordinate to it.
