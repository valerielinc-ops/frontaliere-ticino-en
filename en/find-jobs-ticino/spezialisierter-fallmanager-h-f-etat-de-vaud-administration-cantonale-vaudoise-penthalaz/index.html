Role overview

As an assistant to the Head of the Office for the Execution of Penalties (OEP), you play a central role in the implementation of criminal sanctions in the canton of Vaud, particularly in the area of alternative penalties and regimes.

In a demanding and multidisciplinary environment, you ensure that criminal convictions are managed in accordance with the legal framework, jurisprudence and the principles of public safety and reintegration.

You are also responsible for the development of electronic surveillance in the criminal or civil field, in particular in a form of active surveillance.
