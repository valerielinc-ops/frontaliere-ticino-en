Role overview

Within the Directorate-General for the Environment (DGE), the Directorate for the Industrial, Urban and Rural Environment (DIREV) is responsible for the protection of the population and the environment from pollution, damage and nuisance caused by human activities.

As a specialist in air quality measurement in a team of 8 people, you are responsible for integrated measurement by passive sensors and gravimetry.

You operate a measuring network extending throughout the cantonal territory and participate actively in monitoring air pollution and air quality.
