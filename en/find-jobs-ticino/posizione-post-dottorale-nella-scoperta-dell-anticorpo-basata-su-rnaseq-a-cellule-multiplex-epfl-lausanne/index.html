Role overview

Mission

The lab of biomedical microfluidics (LBMM; www.epfl.ch/labs/lbmm ) at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Lausanne (EPFL), is looking for postdocs with a primary training in single-cell biology, next generation sequential or antibody discovery.

Experience in droplet-microfluidics, including use of commercial devices such as the 10X Genomics platform is beneficial, but not absolutely crucial.

Application process

  • Having a comprehensive microfluidic toolbox at hand (and expanding it continuously), we are now inviting applications for highly multiplexed single-cell RNAseq-based antibody discovery.
  • The idea behind this project is to exploit transcriptomic data sets for the discovery of new therapeutic antibodies.
  • The lab has a very translational focus, running a tech development lab on the EPFL campus and a translational lab in the AGORA cancer research center (with direct access to patient material).
  • Furthermore, a strong network of clinical partners in Europe and the US has been established.
  • The successful candidate will closely interact with experts in microfluidics and bioinformatics, while speaking the above project.
