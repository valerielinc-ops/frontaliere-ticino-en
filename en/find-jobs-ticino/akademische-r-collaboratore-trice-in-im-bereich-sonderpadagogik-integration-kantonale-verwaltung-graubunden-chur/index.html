Role overview

The Office for Primary Schools and Sports (AVS) is part of the Department of Education, Culture and Environmental Protection of the Canton of Graubünden.

For the area of special education / integration we are looking for an academic employee on 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

This area plans school offerings for children and adolescents with special needs and supervises special schools.

  • The Office for Primary Schools and Sports (AVS) is part of the Department of Education, Culture and Environmental Protection of the Canton of Graubünden.
  • For the area of special education / integration we are looking for an academic employee on 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Application process

  • Processing applications for special training for pupils with mental, physical and perceptual disabilities or multiple disabilities
  • Processing of applications for measures in case of high funding needs
  • Development and enforcement of clear reporting and application criteria and guidelines
  • Development of appropriate strategies and concepts in the area of responsibility (analysis of development at cantonal and Swiss level)
  • Supporting the finance department in budgeting and billing the institutions of special training Requirements
  • University degree in special education or comparable education
  • good knowledge of the cantonal school system and/or affinity for educational issues
  • very good knowledge of German as well as skill in written expression

Contacts

  • person for specialised centres and institutions for specialised training
  • Management or participation in internal and external meetings and working groups in the area of responsibility
  • Preparation of responses to parliamentary initiatives
  • Cooperation in the development of messages, instructions, guidelines and recommendations
  • Dealing with complex penalties from day-to-day business
