# Learning: Computer science CFC Development of applications

He tuo futuro inizia who

In the development of applications, you realize software solutions for products, processes or services and are responsible for implementing functional solutions according to customer requirements. You're ready,

  • You have a close exchange with your clients in order to inform them at an early stage of the evolution of the demands of their ICT solution or the system context.
  • You analyze user models, create design sketches and develop prototypes. In close collaboration with your clients, you develop the conceptual implementation variant.
  • Finally, you implement them with the appropriate programming languages and control their quality and safety via a test concept.
  • You ensure the correct delivery of your solutions by defining a suitable process, testing them and finalizing your solution to your client.

What you bring

  • Compulsory completed, higher (secondary) degree with good to very good grades. Affinity for technical subjects such as mathematics and natural sciences
  • Creative and solution-oriented thinking capacity with a systematic and structured working method
  • Affinity for technical subjects and theoretical processes
  • Faculty of rapid understanding and high concentration capacity
  • Communicative spirit and sense of service
  • Team spirit
  • Enjoying to experience things
  • Your curiosity makes you constantly want to discover and learn new things
  • You know Swisscom's somewhat different training model and you want to be part of it

Duration: 4 years Professional maturity: possible

Diploma: SWC IT Application Development / SWC Computer Software Development of applications
