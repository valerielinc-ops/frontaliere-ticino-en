About the role

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. Your main tasks and responsibilities:

Portfolio & Project Management

  • Lead and guide overall execution of multiple IT Projects, ensuring alignment with Sulzer’s strategic priorities.
  • Monitor project progress, manage risks, and ensure timely delivery, budget adherence and manage interdependencies between projects.
  • Maintain project documentation, status reports, and dashboards for leadership and stakeholders.

Governance & Methodology

  • Apply and promote consistent project management methodologies, tools, and reporting standards.
  • Ensure compliance with governance frameworks and contribute to process improvement initiatives.

Stakeholder Management

  • Build strong relationships with business leaders and IT stakeholders to ensure project objectives meet business needs, leading the IT project Steering Committees.
  • Act as a key point of contact for project-related communication and issue resolution.
  • Proactively engage in business partnering, acting as a trusted advisor to business divisions and supporting the identification, prioritization, and management of IT demands to maximize value delivery.

Demand Management

  • Lead the demand management process by capturing, evaluating, and prioritizing business requirements, ensuring alignment with Sulzer’s strategic objectives and resource availability.
  • Facilitate transparent decision-making on project selection and resource allocation.

Vendor & Financial Management

  • Support vendor selection, contract management, and performance tracking for project-related services.
  • Assist in project budgeting, forecasting, and financial performance tracking.

Operational Excellence

  • Drive efficiency in project delivery and resource allocation.
  • Champion continuous improvement and simplification across project processes and tools.

Sulzer Core Ambition Program

  • Actively contribute to the SulzerCore Ambition Program by ensuring that all project and portfolio activities support the organizations goals and strategic ambitions.

Team Collaboration & Development

  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams and contribute to a culture of accountability and excellence.
  • Mentor junior project managers or team members as needed.
  • Drive efficiency across IT operations, including resource allocation, vendor management, and performance tracking.
  • Champion continuous improvement and simplification across the IT landscape, including consolidation of tools and platforms.

OneSulzer Culture

  • Promote and embody the OneSulzer mindset by fostering collaboration with an “Entrepreneurial spirit and teamwork”, challenging the status quo, and supporting a unified, “Customer focus” culture across all divisions and teams.

Desired Experience & Qualifications

  • 9+ years of professional experience in IT and business/industry environments
  • At least 5 years of experience in team management and leadership roles
  • Strong experience with project methods and methodologies
  • Solid knowledge of operating models (Ops Models)
  • Proven experience in financial management within IT or project-driven environments
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field
  • Project management certifications such as PMP, PRINCE2, or equivalent practical experience
  • Fluent in English
  • Strong leadership and communication skills
  • Proven ability to manage multiple projects and priorities simultaneously
  • Experience in stakeholder engagement and change management
  • Analytical mindset with strong attention to detail
  • Ability to work effective
