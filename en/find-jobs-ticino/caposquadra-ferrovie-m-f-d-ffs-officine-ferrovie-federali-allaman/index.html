Railway track foreman (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Make Switzerland move with us. Railway Track Foreman (m/f/d) Allaman, 01.11.2026 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you. Are you passionate about railway infrastructure and would like to join a dynamic team at the heart of railway maintenance projects in the Lake Geneva region? Would you be interested in the role of conductor, who coordinates the technical needs of railway maintenance sites, safety requirements as well as the management of the work? This position is made for you! To find out about social benefits, such as free GA and discounted travel abroad (also for the family), go to our website under Social benefits. What you can make move.

  • You carry out all maintenance and construction work in the field of railways independently, including the exchange or assembly of components on the tracks and track devices.
  • You manage and supervise the staff under your responsibility on the site.
  • You control the quality of execution of all supplier services as part of the project entrusted to you (framework contracts, construction machines, etc.). What you bring.
  • You demonstrate great motivation, you are ready to take responsibility and you invest yourself with passion in your tasks. You focus your work on the pursuit of your objectives and set high standards for its quality.
  • You have solid experience in the construction and maintenance of railway installations and good knowledge of the field.
  • You have obtained a CFC as a track builder, or in a craft sector.
  • You hold the railway construction foreman diploma (POS), obtained from the SSE (Swiss Society of Entrepreneurs). - You consider irregular hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. With us, working nights and weekends entitles you to additional pay, e.g. ex. CHF 16.– per hour for Sunday work. Time increases of 10 to 30% are granted for night work, which gives you more free time.

Your salary and benefits.
