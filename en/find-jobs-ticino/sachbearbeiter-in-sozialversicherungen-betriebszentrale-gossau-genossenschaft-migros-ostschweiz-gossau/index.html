Bring your expertise to Migros! You can expect a supportive team, exciting challenges and the opportunity to make a difference. Together we live "Tämä Erfolgrich": You support employees and managers, take on responsibility in the social insurance sector, carry out training courses and develop processes further. Become part of the Migros world and apply now!

Mansioni

Requirements

  • Professional experience: social security

  • You perform safely in front of groups and understand how to convey content in an understandable, target-group-oriented and practical manner
  • Customer and service orientation are a matter of course for you: you recognize needs, provide competent advice and find pragmatic solutions
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo
