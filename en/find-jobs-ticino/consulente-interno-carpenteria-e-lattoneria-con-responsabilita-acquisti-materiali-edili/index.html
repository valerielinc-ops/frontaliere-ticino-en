Main responsibilities

  • and responsibilities
  • As a customer advisor in our building materials store in Via San Gottardo 98 in Bellinzona, you are the stable, structured and reliable point of reference for our customers and within the company.
  • You will ensure operational complementarity with external department colleagues, ensuring continuity, precision and coordination in the entire business process. Your responsibilities include:
  • Customer management: technical-commercial support, order processing and offers, product advice.
  • Operational collaboration and structured support to external sales consultants: act as an internal contact person that guarantees continuity, preparation and coverage in business activities, ensuring smooth and punctual processes.
  • Understanding technical documentation: reading and interpretation of plans, diagrams and diagrams according to industry standards and standards.
  • Purchasing responsibility: planning and management of procurement, stock monitoring, definition of requirements.
  • Negotiation with suppliers: negotiation on prices, conditions, quality and delivery terms; evaluation and development suppliers.
  • Internal sales development: promotion of carpentry/lactonery assortment and identification of business opportunities.
  • Internal coordination: collaboration with logistics, warehouse and external sale.
