Original description

Duties

  • assists the Director in the administrative management of the cantonal socio-psychiatric organization (OSC)
  • coordinates and is responsible for the activity of the central services of the OSC: finances, personnel, finance, logistics and security, hotel and catering
  • supervises the financial management of the OSC, prepares forecast accounts and final accounts and monitors the evolution of expenses and revenues
  • maintains contacts with the central services of the cantonal administration and collaborates in the management of the related projects, for example in the IT and logistical
  • verifies the correct application of regulations in financial, human resources and purchasing management
  • participates in negotiations with health insurers for the definition of fees and tariffs and verifies the preparation of hospital analytical accounting and related reporting
  • participates in working groups at cantonal and federal level
  • Requirements
  • academic training (bachelor's + master's), preferably in economic sciences, or experience that allows to certify its equivalence
  • knowledge of the institutional context of the Canton as well as of the decision-making and legislative processes
  • aptitude for teamwork and the development of relationships within the cantonal administration and with partners in the area
  • spirit of initiative, analytical, decision-making and mediation skills
  • excellent knowledge of Office tools
  • excellent linguistic knowledge of Italian (conversation and drafting) and good knowledge (conversation and drafting) of a second official language
  • editorial skills Preferred qualifications:
  • experience in managing a large number of collaborators and/or in the field of hospital or social-health management
  • knowledge of SAP system, Rekole certifications and/or quality and pricing management systems in the hospital and social-health sectors. Special observations
  • place of work: Mendrisio
  • start date: to be agreed Conditions for submitting the application
  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application will not be considered valid
  • in addition to the documents to be forwarded at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and work certificates), further documentation may be requested at a later stage (criminal record with related self-certification and self-certification on the state of health) Conditions of employment
  • appointment or assignment: are those provided for by the Law on the organization of state employees and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulation. Any modification of the law concerning salary and organization remains expressly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment relationship), flexible or according to service needs. Expiry 6 July 2026 Further information can be obtained from Mr. Daniele Intraina, OSC Director, tel. 091 816 55 00.
