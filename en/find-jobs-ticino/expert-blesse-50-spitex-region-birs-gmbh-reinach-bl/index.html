Original description

Future-oriented, competent and professional – that is our non-profit organization Spitex Region Birs GmbH. Are you ready for an exciting challenge in an exceptional and diverse working environment? Then join us in the team! With flat hierarchies and a respectful cooperation at eye level, we attach great importance to professional skills and strong social skills. To strengthen our team in Reinach we are looking for you as Health professional (80-100%)

What to expect

  • Independent, competent and resource-oriented basic and treatment care for our clients of all age groups with a wide range of medical conditions
  • Together with a dipl. Nurse HF you take on case responsibility for a certain number of clients
  • Active participation in the care process
  • Support our clients in a self-determined everyday life at home
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with relatives, doctors and other services

What you bring

  • Completed vocational training as a specialist in health EFZ
  • An independent, responsible way of working
  • Open-mindedness, flexibility and empathy
  • Willingness to travel by e-bike or car

Our offer for you

  • No night service
  • When designing working hours, we take your personal needs into account
  • Space for personal initiative and professional development
  • A motivated team and an open, appreciative working atmosphere

With us you will not only find a job, but a meaningful task with a perspective! Become part of our dynamic team and shape the future of healthcare with us. We look forward to your application!
