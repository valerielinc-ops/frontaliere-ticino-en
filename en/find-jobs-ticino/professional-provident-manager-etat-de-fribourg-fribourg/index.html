Role overview

Who are we?

The CPEF (Provisional Fund of the State of Freiburg) is an institution governed by public law which provides all the staff of the State and some 75 institutions close to the State (municipalities, foundations, corporations, etc.) under the second pillar.

The CPEF has more than 22,000 active insured persons and more than 9,000 beneficiaries.
