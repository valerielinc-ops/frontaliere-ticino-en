Original description

The Office for Migration and Civil Law currently has 230 employees at more than 20 locations and is responsible for the entire area of responsibility in the area of migration, i.e. in particular granting and withdrawing permits, accommodation and support, executing expulsions and integration. In addition, ID cards are issued to Swiss nationals, naturalisations are made and supervision of the civil status authorities is exercised. The headquarters of the Office of Migration and Civil Law is in Chur.

We are looking for the Office for Migration and Civil Law from 1. December 2026 or by appointment a supervisor for the exit centre Flüeli in Valzeina.

Tasks

  • Assistance and assistance to persons subject to exit
  • Completion of administrative tasks and management of electronic files
  • Ensuring the smooth operation of operations
  • Documentation and reporting on relevant events and developments in the Centre
  • Occasional provision of piketting service outside operating hours

Requirements

  • Basic administrative, social or medical training completed
  • Resilience and empathy in dealing with different cultures and life stories
  • independent, goal-oriented and team-oriented work
  • Patience, flexibility and assertiveness
  • Knowledge of foreign languages and driving licence for PW is an advantage
