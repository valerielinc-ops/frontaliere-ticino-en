Role overview

Tasks

Not in the mood for an ordinary office day? For this reason, you deal with the management of real estate in your basic commercial training at the Apleona from day one. With us you not only check figures and balance sheets, but also get exciting insights into the real estate world.

During your 3-year education, you will

  • Gain many interesting and instructive insights
  • Getting to know different departments
  • Helping with the rental and transfer of apartments
  • Processing various customer requests
  • Employing craftsmen
  • Get a comprehensive insight into real estate accounting

In addition to passing on solid expertise, we also promote your human qualities such as team spirit, communication skills and responsibility. All this is the foundation for your future professional success!
