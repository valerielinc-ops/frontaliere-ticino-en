Original description

ELCO offers premium-grade heating solutions with first-class service, which is designed to meet the needs of our customers throughout the entire life cycle of the products and heating systems. The focus is on excellent consulting as well as convincing products and heating systems. ELCO has been a leader in burner, condensing and solar technology for decades and is represented throughout Europe with 1.7 million installed heating systems.

Since 1928, ELCO has been a leading European brand offering intelligent solutions and technologies tailored to the needs of private and commercial projects and ensuring maximum energy savings.

To strengthen our service group in the oil & gas sector, we are looking for a reliable and independent personality for the area of application in the Lucerne area.

Service team leader in the oil & gas sector (m/w)

We have many exciting tasks for you

  • Professional and personnel management of the service team and support in technical challenges
  • Responsibility for budgeting and ensuring achievement of the goal
  • Promoting motivation, quality and productivity within the team
  • Close cooperation with internal departments (back office & front) for optimal coordination of processes
  • Organization and take-over of the piket service within the team
  • Active implementation and promotion of our corporate culture
  • Competent customer advice on renovations and sales

Maintenance contracts and services

  • Processing and technical clarification of customer complaints

For our shared success story, you will bring with you

  • multi-year experience in heating service as well as completed training at FA level
  • Ideally first experience in a comparable leadership position
  • responsibility, flexibility and resilience
  • customer-oriented and professional appearance with a positive "hands-on, mentality"

What makes us unique

  • Dynamic working environment with a family atmosphere and team spirit
  • Service vehicle with private use as well as equipment with modern technology such as smartphone and tablet
  • Modern and future-oriented products in the premium area
  • Technical development opportunities
  • Start as a cross-country entrant at ELCO and develop in person and professionally

Apply now and become part of our ELCO family. Together we will shape the future.
