FaGe EFZ adult education apprenticeship in Thun Your training

  • It's best at home - you learn to respectfully care for and support people in their familiar surroundings
  • Time for the essentials – competently carrying out medical technical tasks as well as administrative and logistical services.

This is what you bring with you

  • You are mobile – with your own car, the Spitex e-bike or e-scooter

  • A well-founded two-year training with a high level of practical relevance, accompanied by committed vocational trainers as well as theory in vocational schools and inter-company courses

  • Compensation of CHF 400 for the school days
