As an up-and-coming residential community in the middle of Basel, Lausen offers an attractive living environment for around 6000 inhabitants. At the Lousen primary level, around 500 children are taught by just over 60 teachers in 7 kindergartens and 19 primary school classes. An attractive and modern infrastructure is available for this. The dedicated teaching team and the school management cultivate a school culture oriented towards commonality and look forward to reinforcement.
