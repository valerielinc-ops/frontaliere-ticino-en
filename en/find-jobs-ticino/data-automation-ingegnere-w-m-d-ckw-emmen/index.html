You can design it.

  • You analyze large amounts of data, link data sources and develop practical data products.

This is what makes you

  • You are interested in generative AI, AI agents and automation and develop your knowledge in these topics continuously.
  • Ideally, you will bring experience from the energy industry, grid or metrology or electrical engineering.
