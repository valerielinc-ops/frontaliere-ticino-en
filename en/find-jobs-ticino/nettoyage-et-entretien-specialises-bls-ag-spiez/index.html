Original description

The first impression counts. If our passengers feel comfortable and safe, then you have your fingers in the game. Because in fact you make sure that the train stations and stops give a really good business card. Happy travelers – that counts for you.

This is waiting for you

  • Cleaning of stations and stops
  • Proper disposal of waste
  • Performing small repairs
  • Maintain switches and signalling equipment
  • Care of ticket machines
  • Driving of cleaning machines

This is what you bring with you

  • Vocational training as a building cleaner: in or specialist in maintenance
  • Professional experience in cleaning technology
  • Category B driving licence
  • Good physical constitution incl. Hearing and vision
  • Knowledge of German at level B1
  • Readiness for irregular working hours and assignments on weekends

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • We promote the most flexible planning of your tours and shifts possible
  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st class. Class or a half-tax subscription.
  • You have free time when others work. For pennies at night and on weekends there are surcharges.
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year.
  • Financial support for external further education and an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health

About the BLS

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? Proud of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.
