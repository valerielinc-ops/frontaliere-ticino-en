Role overview

With more than 600 employees in the Schiers hospital, the three retirement homes Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, Spitex, living with service, crèche, parental counselling and the Klosters Medical Center, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in the Prättigau between Grüsch and Klosters.

The Medical Center Klosters (MZK) offers comprehensive family care for the population and guests of the region.

We work in an interdisciplinary team of family doctors, specialists from Spital Schiers and medical specialists to ensure patient-centered care.
