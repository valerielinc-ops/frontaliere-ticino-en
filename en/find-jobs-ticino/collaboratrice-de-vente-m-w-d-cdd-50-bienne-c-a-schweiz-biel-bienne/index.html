The future looks like you

C&A is a family business founded in 1841, which has established itself as one of the world leaders in the fashion industry. Present in 17 countries across Europe, Latin America and Asia, C&A is proud of its history and responsible business model, focused on sustainability and inclusion. A pioneer in its sector, the company has made accessibility, ethics and respect for the environment a real commitment. Through its collections, sustainable development initiatives and its commitment to equality, C&A embodies a vision of fashion that goes beyond the simple trend, valuing social and environmental impact. These strong values guide each of our actions and are at the heart of the evolution of our corporate culture.

  • You know how to work as a team, demonstrate autonomy and initiative.
