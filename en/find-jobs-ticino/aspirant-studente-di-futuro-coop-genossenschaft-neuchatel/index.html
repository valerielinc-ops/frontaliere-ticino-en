Original description

Aspirant (future student)

Coop Genossenschaft — Neuchâtel, Neuchâtel, Svizzera

Mission

  • After a period of training in the concepts of our company, you will have to take responsibility in the Neuchâtel region.
  • You will be in charge of offering the best possible shopping experience to customers through optimal management of the availability of goods, their presentation and promotion of sales
  • You will ensure that the objectives set for you are met, taking into account the main key figures (net revenue, personnel costs, liquidations, etc.)
  • You will ensure strict compliance with the quality and presentation guidelines: freshness, quality, hygiene, temperatures and expiry dates
  • You (co) will lead a team and ensure its supervision (training, motivation and follow-up)
  • You will provide, with competence and friendliness, irreproachable customer service in all circumstances

Qualifications

  • You are a professional in large distribution and benefit from a confirmed experience in the field
  • You have very good analytical skills and are guided by the results
  • You have a strong sense of initiative and responsibility
  • Without natural leadership, you have great relational ease and excellent communication.
  • You are available and know how to be flexible in terms of working hours
  • You are comfortable with computer tools and attentive to technological innovations
  • You are a merchant in the soul and have easy contact with customers
  • Discounts and discounts: 5 times more superpoints in supermarkets, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop group, mobile and prepaid subscriptions at a reduced price at Coop Mobile, discount to staff also granted to the partner (living under the same roof)
  • Conditions of employment: pension fund: taken over by Coop of 2/3 of contributions, attractive conditions of employment and guaranteed equal pay, collective labour agreement (CCT), 18 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, seniority benefits
  • Working time: 5 weeks of vacation
  • Continuing training: Over 600 internal training courses
  • Sustainable development: A commitment to man and animals and nature with more than 400 acts
  • Communication: internal app dedicated to staff
  • Health: sports activities: volleyball tournaments and hikes
