Company and context

  • You take on a central senior medical role in acute geriatrics and rehabilitation and further develop your ward medically and organizationally.
  • You lead your interprofessional team with clinical excellence, empathy, and clarity, creating an environment for everyone that promotes safety, a culture of learning, and mutual respect.
  • You treat our highly complex, often multimorbid patients according to our holistic geriatric approach (mobility, cognition, delirium, nutrition, function, quality of life) and accompany them evidence-based through acute and rehabilitation phases.
  • You conduct geriatric rounds, interprofessional case discussions, as well as family and goal-setting meetings—medically sound, ethically reflected, and humanly attentive—lead consultations and assessments, and represent geriatrics as a consultant within the clinic.
  • You further develop treatment paths, quality, and innovation projects (e.g., delirium management, early rehabilitation, interprofessional models).
  • You are a central training figure for assistant physicians: you supervise, coach, and specifically promote them in clinical decision-making, geriatric thinking, and professional attitude.
  • You participate in the senior medical on-call system (on-call duty, no night shifts) and bear responsibility for continuously high-quality care. Requirements
  • With your specialist title in General Internal Medicine (or shortly before) with proven interest or focus in geriatrics; a geriatric focus or corresponding further training is very welcome.
  • With your joy for acute and rehabilitation geriatrics and your interest in complex clinical questions beyond individual diagnoses.
  • Because you enjoy working interprofessionally and understand leadership not as hierarchical, but as a clinically present, attentive, and reliable responsibility for patients, the team, and the organization.
