Original description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2600 employees, including 245 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Electricity Distribution Directorate builds, maintains and operates the electricity distribution network. Through the development of innovative offers, it ensures the technological mastery of projects such as the realization of the intelligent network or public lighting.

Mission

  • Actively participate in the construction, renewal and maintenance of MT/BT, air and underground electricity distribution networks
  • Contribute to the reliability of power supply by acting on public lighting networks and installations
  • Diagnosing and resolving incidents to ensure quality service to our customers
  • Collaborate in the picket service (about 1 week out of 6) and play a key role in the continuity of electricity supply
  • Working in compliance with Group E safety and quality standards

Profile

  • Holder of a Swiss CFC of network electrician or related training in the field of electricity (electrician installer, automatician, assembly electrician, etc.), legal requirements relating to interventions on distribution networks requiring a recognised training in Switzerland
  • Interest in electrical distribution networks or willingness to develop its expertise in this specialized field
  • Good knowledge of French, both oral and written
  • Driver's licence required category B, category C1 an asset
  • Team spirit, sense of responsibility and desire to contribute to infrastructure essential to everyday life

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together for tomorrow's energy".
