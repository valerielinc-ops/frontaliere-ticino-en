Role overview

Tasks

Your expertise makes the difference!

Do you combine medical expertise with precision and keep track of complex cases? Then you are exactly right with us. As a medical coder, you make an important contribution to high-quality patient care and transparent, fair hospital financing:

  • You code stationary cases from all specialist areas independently according to SwissDRG and ensure high quality and accuracy.
  • You work closely with doctors, nurses and the finance department and proactively clarify medical and billing issues.
  • You will analyze challenging coding cases, identify ambiguities and develop comprehensible solutions.
  • You handle cost carrier rejections competently and ensure correct, transparent case handling.
  • You contribute your knowledge and ideas and actively shape coding standards and internal processes.

Requirements

  • You have several years of professional experience in medical coding according to SwissDRG – ideally with additional training as a Swiss Federal / Medical / Coder.
  • You bring a basic medical training, for example in nursing or as a doctor.
  • You master medical terminology safely and you can assess complex medical contexts in a well-founded manner.
