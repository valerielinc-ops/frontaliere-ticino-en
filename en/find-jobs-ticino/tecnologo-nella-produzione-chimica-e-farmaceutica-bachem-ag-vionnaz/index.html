Original description

In order to strengthen our Production team, we are looking for a Technologist in Chemical and Pharmaceutical Production (CFC) (a), 100% for our Vionnaz production site (Valais/Wallis, Switzerland). Your main missions

  • Know and follow existing HSE procedures and directives to ensure safe operations and environmental compliance.
  • Contribute to the manufacture of a pharmaceutical product, from the order of raw materials to the packaging of the finished product.
  • Carry out reactor loading operations within industrial facilities.
  • Carry out ancillary production activities: sampling, labelling, cleaning facilities and buildings, maintaining order according to 5S principles.
  • Detect and report any deviations.
  • Actively participate in continuous process improvement.
  • Working in teams (3x8) from Sunday evening to Friday evening Your profile
  • You have a Chemical and Pharmaceutical Technologist CFC or equivalent training with experience in the chemical or pharmaceutical industry for at least 5 years.
  • Family with GMP standards, you are rigorous and professional in the application and monitoring of manufacturing procedures.
  • You adhere to the HSE directives and apply them.
  • This position involves tasks requiring good physical condition, including handling loads, frequent movements also on stairs, extended standing station.
  • Capable of working independently, you demonstrate a healthy and motivated team spirit.
  • You use IT tools with ease Our offer We offer interesting and stimulating work in an innovative and growth-oriented company, which guarantees versatility and diversity. You will be part of a college environment and will have the opportunity to grow professionally in indeterminate employment. Good conditions of employment await you, including attractive supplementary and social benefits. We look forward to receiving your full application through our application portal. Learn more about the Bachem group and let yourself be inspired by our exciting work environment on our Vionnaz website!
