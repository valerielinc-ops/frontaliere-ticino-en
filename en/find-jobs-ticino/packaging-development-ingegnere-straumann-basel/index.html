Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Develop packaging solutions for existing and new products from concept through implementation, considering product, regulatory, manufacturing, sustainability, and customer requirements.
  • Take ownership of assigned packaging development and Life Cycle Management (LCM) projects and activities.
  • Collaborate with global cross-functional teams, including Engineering, Operations, Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Clinical Affairs, Procurement, and Marketing.
  • Translate technical and user requirements into robust, practical, and cost-effective packaging solutions.
  • Manage packaging development activities, including project planning, risk assessment, timeline management, technical documentation, and stakeholder communication.
  • Conduct feasibility assessments, engineering evaluations, and cost analyses to support packaging design decisions and identify suitable technical solutions.
  • Plan, coordinate, and evaluate packaging verification and validation activities, including transportation testing, shelf-life studies, sterile barrier validation, and compatibility testing.
  • Support packaging activities related to global product transfers, manufacturing site localization, supplier changes, business continuity, and Life Cycle Management initiatives.
  • Collaborate with suppliers and external development partners to evaluate and implement packaging materials, technologies, components, and manufacturing processes.

Key requirements

  • Requirements needed
  • Bachelor's or master’s degree in Packaging Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Materials Science, Industrial Engineering, or a related engineering discipline. Experience (with key words)
  • Typically, 2–5 years of relevant experience in packaging development, packaging engineering, product development, or a related technical field.
  • Hands-on experience with technical packaging development projects and working in cross-functional teams.
  • Good understanding of packaging materials, packaging technologies, manufacturing processes, and packaging test methodologies.
  • Experience in medical devices, pharmaceuticals, or another regulated industry is an advantage.
  • Ability to plan and coordinate technical activities, manage priorities, and deliver agreed results within project timelines.
  • Basic understanding of regulatory requirements, industry standards, and structured product development processes.
  • Knowledge of standards such as ISO 11607, ASTM, ISTA, MDR, or FDA requirements is an advantage.
  • Knowledge of sterile packaging systems, sealing technologies, and sterilization processes is an advantage and can be further developed within the role.
  • Experience with packaging verification and validation activities and related test methods such as transportation testing, seal strength, dye penetration, bubble emission, or visual inspection is an advantage.
  • Understanding Design Control processes, particularly within medical device development, is an advantage.
