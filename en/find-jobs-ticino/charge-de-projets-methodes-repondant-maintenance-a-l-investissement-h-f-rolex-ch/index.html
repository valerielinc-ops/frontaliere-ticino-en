Role overview

Introduction For our Procurement, Support and Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a Project Manager Methods – Responsive Maintenance to the investment whose main task will be to represent maintenance in investment projects, ensuring the preparation of machines for their future maintenance, monitoring their condition and performance and the evolution of the associated needs.

It will also lead the activities of AS&I partners integrated into projects.

As such, it will have to ensure good visibility on the progress of investments within its perimeter, including through monitoring of indicators and regular communication.
