Internship – Operations Support Specialist

As a member of our Operations team in Luxembourg, you will discover various Middle Office / Operations activities and learn how much they are central and critical to our Maison, worldwide. You will be an all-rounder and work with our main Operations teams, such as:

  • Security Master File: Opening and updating information in the Security Masterfile database on financial instruments held by our clients.
  • Income: Payment of dividends, coupons and redemptions on behalf of our clients for all markets and all types of financial instruments, including structured products and derivatives
  • Reconciliation: Reconcile cash, securities, listed derivatives, precious metals, etc. Comparing transactions and balances of the Bank’s books with our network of cash counterparties, securities depositories, Transfer Agents etc.
  • Operational Taxes: Preparation of tax calculations and statements for Europe- based retail clients and enterprises.

YOUR ROLE

  • Assist team members in daily tasks
  • Work on specific project and requests, while learning from senior peers

YOUR PROFILE

  • A keen interest in the Banking sector, especially in Middle Office / Operations domains
  • Collaborative team player with the ability to work independently
  • Motivated, detail-oriented, proactive, able to multi-task
  • Fluent in French, basic English proficiency

NB: Please be aware that in accordance with the current law and job requirements, the selected candidate will be requested to provide a criminal record (section 3).
