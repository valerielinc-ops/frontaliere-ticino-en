Role overview

Do you want to actively shape the automation of future machines and plants while setting technological standards?

Our Chocolate & Flavor Creation business unit develops innovative machinery, equipment and automation solutions for the processing of cocoa, chocolate, coffee and nuts.

As a Technical Lead Automation Engineer PLC in our R&D team, you will take over the technical responsibility for demanding automation developments and accompany new technologies from the initial idea through validation to market introduction.
