The Position The Planning and Material Management function within our Global Instrumentation Network (GIN)—part of Global Operations—ensures manufacturing continuity across EMEA, NAM, and APAC . By seamlessly connecting our global network of suppliers and production resources, we ensure flawless product lifecycles, maximized efficiency, and unyielding market supply of critical diagnostics products worldwide .

Operating as a Global Sourcing and Material Manager across our EMEA, NAM, and APAC regions, you will partner with global procurement and category management to orchestrate seamless material flows throughout the manufacturing network . This role entails managing critical A-supplier portfolios to maximize performance, achieve TCO efficiencies, proactively mitigate VUCA and geopolitical risks, and drive tangible gains in factory productivity .
