Role overview

Michel Gruppe AG comprises the psychiatric institution Privatklinik Meiringen, the rehabilitation clinic Hasliberg, the Helsenberg Foundation with dormitories and protected workplaces as well as the Haslital laundry and the Haslital Kita as well as services in other areas.

It employs a total of around 800 people at its locations in Willigen, Meiringen, Hasliberg, Ringgenberg, Interlaken, Thun and Bern.

The Kita Haslital is aimed at working parents from Meiringen and the surrounding area.
