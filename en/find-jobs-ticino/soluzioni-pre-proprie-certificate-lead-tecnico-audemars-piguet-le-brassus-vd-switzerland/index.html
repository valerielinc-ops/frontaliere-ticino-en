Role overview

Under the responsibility of the Domain Manager Retail Solutions, your main mission will be to ensure the technical leadership of the IT CPO (Certified Pre-Owned) ecosystem by defining and developing robust, secure and scalable digital solutions that are fully integrated into the application landscape of Audemars Piguet. This new role actively participates in Audemars Piguet's ambition in the second-hand market by supporting an innovative roadmap focused on customer experience. As the technical reference of the field, you guide development teams in architectural choices and structuring technical decisions. You guarantee the application of standards of quality, cybersecurity and development, while ensuring the excellence of deliveries. At the interface between business teams, business analytics, architects and technology partners, you contribute to the platform's technical roadmap and support the success of this strategic program for our company.

Within this framework, your main responsibilities will be

  • Pilot the technical guidance of the CPO platform to ensure the consistency, sustainability, security and scalability of deployed solutions
  • Assume the role of technical reference for the entire CPO perimeter, accompanying teams in architectural decisions and structuring technological choices
  • Ensure compliance with standards of architecture, development, cybersecurity and data protection
  • Lead the definition and evolution of the platform's technical roadmap, ensuring its alignment with business priorities, IT strategy and programme objectives
  • Work closely with Business Owners, Business Analysts and architects to translate functional needs into robust, efficient and sustainable solutions
  • Ensure delivery quality through good software engineering practices, testing automation and continuous integration
  • Ensure the proper integration of the CPO platform with the application ecosystem of Audemars Piguet (e.g. ERP, CRM, after-sales tools)
  • Piloting development partners and teams, while promoting skills building, sharing good practices and technical excellence
  • Advanced training in computer science, software engineering or equivalent field.
