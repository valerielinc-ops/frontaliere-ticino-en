Role overview

Your key responsibilities

  • As a painter, you will be responsible for painting, decorating, and minor repair work throughout the hotel as well as in the staff housing, ensuring that all areas consistently meet our high standards.

  • You will carry out high-quality interior and exterior painting and decorating work, including the professional preparation of surfaces through filling, sanding, priming, painting, and coating.

  • You will perform regular inspections and touch-up work in guest rooms, bathrooms, public areas, and back-of-house spaces to maintain an impeccable appearance and ensure the highest quality standards.

  • You will carry out minor repair and maintenance work on walls, ceilings, woodwork, and other painted or coated surfaces, selecting and using appropriate materials and techniques.

  • You will work closely with the Technical Services team and support painting, decorating, and finishing work during renovations, seasonal preparations, special projects, and events.

  • You will ensure that all work is carried out efficiently, safely, and with particular attention to detail, while minimising disruption to our guests and hotel operations.

  • You will take on occasional on-call duty shifts to respond quickly and reliably to emergencies as they arise.

• What you bring to the job

For this highly varied position, you ideally bring the following:

  • Completed vocational training as a painter, decorator, or an equivalent professional qualification.

  • Several years of professional experience in painting, decorating, renovation, and maintenance work.Previous experience in a luxury hotel or a comparable high-standard environment is an advantage.

  • Strong technical knowledge of painting and decorating techniques, surface preparation, coatings, materials, and finishes, combined with a high standard of craftsmanship.

  • Good command of German as well as spoken Italian; knowledge of English is an advantage.

  • A responsible, reliable, and independent working style, with excellent attention to detail and a strong commitment to maintaining the highest quality standards.

  • A professional and discreet approach, particularly when working in guest-facing areas and occupied spaces, with consideration for our guests and hotel operations at all times.

  • A flexible and solution-oriented mindset, together with the ability to remain reliable and efficient during busy periods.

Our offer

  • Permanent full-time position in St.Moritz.

  • Average 5-day working week with 43.5 working hours.

  • 5 weeks of vacation and 6 public holidays per year.

  • 13th salary from the start date (pro rata temporis).

  • Accommodation in one of our new staff houses at the beginning upon request.

  • Meals available in our new and modern staff restaurant.

  • Opportunity to work in an attractive environment with development and career growth opportunities.

If you are looking for an interesting, challenging, and varied role in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-established team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
