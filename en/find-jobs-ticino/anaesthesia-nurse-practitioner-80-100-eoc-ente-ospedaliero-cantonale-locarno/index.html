  • To complete the team of the nursing service of the Regional Hospital of Locarno La Carità we are looking for a nurse specialized in anesthesia (80-100%) Profile: The person we are looking for will be responsible for guaranteeing specialized nursing care in anesthesia for patients undergoing operations mainly in general surgery, visceral surgery, orthopaedics-traumatology and urology, actively collaborating with the anesthesiologist in the different phases of the perioperative process.

  • The ideal candidate is sensitive to interpersonal dynamics, actively contributes to teamwork and maintains a positive attitude even under pressure.
