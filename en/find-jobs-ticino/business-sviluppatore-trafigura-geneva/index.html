Role overview

Main purpose

This role supports Trafigura's Refined Metals trading desks.

The Business Developer will originate and grow commercial opportunities across Turkey and the surrounding region, acting as the primary bridge between local counterparties and the Geneva trading desks.

Main responsibilities

  • Key responsibilities
  • To Originate and develop business opportunities with Turkish and regional counterparties
  • Act as the primary liaison between local market relationships and the Geneva Refined Metals desks
  • Support deal structuring, contract negotiations, and execution alongside experienced traders
  • Coordinate with internal operations, logistics, trade finance, and contract administration teams
  • Provide regular market intelligence and counterparty insights to Geneva desk heads Required qualifications
  • Minimum 4 years' experience in business development, trading, commercial origination, or a related commercial role within commodities.
  • Demonstrated exposure to the full physical trading cycle — from pricing and contract negotiation with counterparties through to execution, shipment logistics, and settlement.
  • Ability to spot and act on relative-value opportunities by conducting market research and analyzing forward curves, delivery timing, geographic spreads, and storage economics.
  • Native Turkish, with fluent English — excellent oral and written skills in both languages. Preferred qualifications
