Role overview

Portrait

The BVS Lower Emmental provides training for around 120 cognitively impaired, socio-emotionally striking and neurodiverse children and adolescents in 21 classes at four locations in Burgdorf and Utzenstorf.

It forms an important pillar of the SAZ, the training and work centre Burgdorf, which has offered itself for more than 50 years cognitively as well as physically or mentally impaired people a place to learn, work and live.
