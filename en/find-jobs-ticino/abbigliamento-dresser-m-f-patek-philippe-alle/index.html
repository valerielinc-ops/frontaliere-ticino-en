Role overview

Mission

As part of this function, attached to the International Customer Service of our Alle site, you will ensure the maintenance and repair of modern watches, produced from 1980 to the present day.

  • Mission
  • As part of this function, attached to the International Customer Service of our Alle site, you will ensure the maintenance and repair of modern watches, produced from 1980 to the present day.
  • To perform disassembly, assembly, oiling, flat spiral adjustment and Breguet spiral, dynamic retouching, punching dials and hands and boxing.
  • Identify the origin of breakdowns.

Main responsibilities

  • To perform disassembly, assembly, oiling, flat spiral adjustment and Breguet spiral, dynamic retouching, punching dials and hands and boxing.
