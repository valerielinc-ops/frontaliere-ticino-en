Role overview

We take PostFinance’s customer service to a new level with intelligent, connected solutions.

For this purpose, we develop modern services, automate processes and connect cloud and on-prem systems to make our chat and voicebots even more powerful.

As an embedded tester / test automation engineer in the Next Gen Bot delivery team, you and our developers ensure the continuous quality of our AI-based bot platform.

  • We take PostFinance’s customer service to a new level with intelligent, connected solutions.
  • For this purpose, we develop modern services, automate processes and connect cloud and on-prem systems to make our chat and voicebots even more powerful.

Application process

  • Together with developers, you ensure the continuous quality of our product up to application integration
  • You design and maintain demanding tests for our application up to application integration based on
  • Requirements and risks and document test specifications and results in the corresponding test management tools
  • You automate regression tests for APIs, web applications and voicebot functionalities and continuously develop the existing test automation
  • You test voice dialogues and voicebot functionalities, taking into account different languages, formulations and customer situations
  • You analyze, reproduce and document errors and accompany their correction together with developers: inside also cross-team
  • You support triage and management of bugs in Jira and work with external teams and suppliers if necessary
  • You actively bring the customer perspective as well as usability and quality aspects into the team and get involved in the community of practice of embedded testers You bring that with you
