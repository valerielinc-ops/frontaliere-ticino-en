Role overview

Your Mission Within the Real-Time Network (RTN) team, you design, deploy, operate and evolve IP network infrastructures that transport the audio and video streams of our production environments: radio, filmed radio and television.

You intervene on high-performance and highly available network architectures, based mainly on Arista equipment and SMPTE ST 2110 standards.

These infrastructures support critical production services and real-time media flows.
