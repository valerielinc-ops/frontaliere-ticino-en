Introduction to the Team

  • Data Ops Services’ mission is to ensure adequate servicing of all data interfaces supported by our data platform. It covers a large spectrum of interfaces, from very critical order processing to more regular analytical systems.
  • The Data Ops team is supporting global systems serving the entire group worldwide.
  • As our platform and data landscape evolve, we would like to strengthen our team with a skilled Data Engineer specialised in Talend and enterprise‑grade ETL / ESB integrations.
  • As the technological landscape continues to evolve, our integration framework is modernising likewise. Microservices, API gateway management and Kafka are all technologies at the centre of this team.

Main Responsibilities

  • Design, implement and maintain ETL/ELT data pipelines (batch and near real time) using Qlik Talend and related integration components.
  • Develop and operate ESB‑style integrations between core banking systems, data platforms and downstream applications, ensuring robustness, scalability and reusability.
  • Take operational ownership of Talend (change management, data quality management, SLA monitoring, SOPs and runbooks).
  • Monitor and ensure the smooth operation of critical data pipelines, including incident analysis, root cause identification and implementation of preventive measures.
  • Apply CI/CD practices using the existing framework to automate and streamline deployments and configuration changes.
  • Implement controls using the observability framework, ensuring service continuity and accuracy.
  • Create, maintain and improve documentation and operational procedures for the data integrations under your ownership.
  • Collaborate with cross‑functional teams (application owners, architects, business analysts, data consumers) to design integration patterns and improve governance around data flows.
  • Contribute to the standardisation and industrialisation of integration patterns, promoting convergence, normalisation and reusability across projects.
  • Stay current with advancements in data engineering, ETL/ESB technologies and cloud‑based integration platforms to support continuous improvement of our DataOps capabilities.

Skills and Experience

  • At least 5 years of proven hands‑on experience in data engineering and data integration, with a strong focus on ETL/ELT pipelines and ESB‑style integrations.
  • Solid experience with Qlik Talend ETL and ESB on‑premise, including job design, orchestration, error handling and performance optimisation.
  • Strong ETL / data modelling skills, including handling of large datasets, complex transformations and integration of heterogeneous data sources.
  • Good knowledge of SQL and experience working with relational databases and/or cloud data platforms.
  • Experience with CI/CD practices and tools in a hybrid environment (e.g. Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions) applied to data integration components.
  • Familiarity with ESB concepts, API‑based integrations, messaging and event‑driven patterns is a strong plus.
  • Experience with data quality, metadata management and data lineage tools or frameworks is an asset.
  • Enjoy providing operational support on Talend jobs and data integration workflows (incident analysis, troubleshooting).
  • Good communication and collaboration skills, able to work closely with both technical and business stakeholders.
  • Rigorous and methodical in documentation, process creation and change management.
  • High interest in standardisation, convergence and reusability of integration components.
  • Curious, enthusiastic and proactive, with a hands‑on mindset and a strong desire to learn and grow in a demanding environment.
  • Passionate about data processing and integration technologies.
  • Fluent in spoken and written English; French is an asset.
