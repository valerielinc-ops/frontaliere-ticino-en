Main responsibilities

  • To excel in this role, you will bridge the gap between complex software engineering and laboratory research in drug discovery through the following responsibilities:
  • Design, program and troubleshoot high-throughput lab workflows.
  • Build orchestration systems, hardware abstraction layers, and instrument drivers that manage retries and allow unattended operation.
  • Implement logging, metrics, and alerts across hardware and software to identify failures.
  • Fix root causes using software or vision models instead of relying on manual workarounds or vendor support.
  • Build systems to maintain a digital representation of the lab, including sample identity, instrument state, and resource availability.
  • Connect instruments via APIs and integrate with LIMS and ELNs.
  • Manage hardware requirements, site constraints, reagent selection, and work cell integration from initial design to deployment.
