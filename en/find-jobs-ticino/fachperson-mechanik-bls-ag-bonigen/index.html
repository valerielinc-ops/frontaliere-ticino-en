Original description

You make sure that our trains are stormproof on the rails. Thanks to your reliable work, passengers travel safely and on time on the BLS network. With pride you can see the fruits of your labor at every point.

This is waiting for you

  • Breathing new life into driving motors – repairing them with precision, revising them and testing them powerfully
  • Dismantle highly complex drives, thoroughly clean them and prepare them for the next use
  • Documentation of maintenance work

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed apprenticeship as a polymechanic: in or an artisanal metal or mechanical profession
  • Experience in assembly and good mechanical knowledge, electrical knowledge advantageous
  • Basic knowledge of common PC applications such as Word, Excel and Outlook; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Craftsmanship and exact, safe and reliable working methods and desire to train regularly

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st class. Class or a half-tax subscription.
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year
  • Financial support for external further education as well as an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health.
  • 18 days of paternity leave and 18 weeks of maternity leave with the option of extending it by an additional 6 months as unpaid leave.

About the BLS

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? Proud of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.
