Role overview

In controlling, we use reliable data to create the basis for well-founded decisions.

This ensures that financial and corporate data are available in the right quality for reporting, analysis and day-to-day business.

We are looking for an experienced, careful personality with controlling or finance experience, banking expertise and a high technical affinity.

Application process

  • For interdisciplinary questions and projects, you contribute your understanding of banking and data and support the further development of processes and applications. You bring that with you
  • You have a higher business education, for example an FH degree, and bring several years of professional experience, ideally in the controlling, finance or banking environment.
  • Banking know-how and a good understanding of business relationships are familiar to you.
  • You have a high technical affinity and a good understanding of data structures, databases and reporting.
  • Computer science training is not required – basic SQL knowledge should be available.
  • Care, accuracy and reliability characterize your way of working.
  • You also perform recurring tasks concentrated and with the necessary quality standards.
  • You work independently, remain open to pragmatic solutions in the event of changes and maintain a respectful and professional approach to different stakeholders.
