More about this search for cross-border applicants

Morges, a charming town in the Swiss canton of Vaud, offers a range of job opportunities across various sectors. The town is well-connected by public transport, making it an ideal location for cross-border workers. From healthcare and education to finance and hospitality, Morges provides a variety of roles suitable for both local residents and commuters from northern Italy.
