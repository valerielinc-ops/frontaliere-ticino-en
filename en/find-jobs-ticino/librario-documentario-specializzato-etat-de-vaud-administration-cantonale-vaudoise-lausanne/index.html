Role overview

Due to the size of its collections, the Cantonal and University Library – Lausanne (BCUL) is one of the largest libraries in Switzerland.

A public institution with a heritage, cultural and academic vocation, the Cantonal and University Library – Lausanne (BCUL) is dependent on the Directorate-General for Culture (DGC).

Within the University campus of UNIL, the Unithèque site of the Cantonal and University Library
