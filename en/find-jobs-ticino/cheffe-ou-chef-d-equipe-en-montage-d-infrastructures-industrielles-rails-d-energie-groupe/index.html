Role overview

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our clients realize their projects by providing global, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether for the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment.

An exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2,250 employees, including 200 apprentices, as well as the balance between private and professional life.
