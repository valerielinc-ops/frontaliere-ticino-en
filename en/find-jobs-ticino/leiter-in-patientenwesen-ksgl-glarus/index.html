Original description

Your opportunities with us

  • You lead a team of 10 employees in the patient administration and reception, promote and develop them further
  • you drive the continuous optimization of processes and accompany digitalization projects in your area
  • Together with your team, you are responsible for a professional, efficient and patient-oriented process from admission to invoicing
  • you ensure that reception and patient admission are the first points of contact of our hospital with friendliness, competence and high quality of service
  • You work closely with medical, nursing and administrative areas, think across areas and clarify interfaces
  • you are responsible for the correct accounting of our services as well as the professional exchange with health insurance funds; the industry-specific billing systems you get to know with us
  • you coordinate the training of our learners in patient administration

You are fit for us if

  • you have already successfully led a team and know how to promote and motivate employees
  • you have experience managing projects and you have actively developed processes instead of just managing them
  • you convince with your communicative nature and this in exchange with different stakeholders
  • you think solution-oriented, set priorities and keep an overview even in hectic moments
  • you have already gained experience in the Swiss health care system, in a health insurance or in a related industry, and you have a basic commercial education or comparable qualification as well as knowledge of processes in the patient administration (reception, cost accounting, billing)

We reward your passion

  • with a versatile and responsible activity, including freedom of design
  • with a dedicated and motivated team
  • with a good interdisciplinary cooperation in a family environment
  • With attractive employment conditions
  • with a wide range of training opportunities

The Cantonal Hospital Glarus is located in the middle of the spectacular Glarner mountains – just one hour from Zurich. Here the high quality of life is combined with numerous opportunities for mountain sports and outdoor activities. With 65 acute medicine beds and six in the intensive care unit, the Cantonal Hospital Glarus AG offers a broad range of disciplines. As a medical competence centre, the hospital provides extended basic care for the Canton of Glarus and the adjacent regions.
