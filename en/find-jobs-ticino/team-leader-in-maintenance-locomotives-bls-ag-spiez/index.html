Original description

Reliable locomotives for a reliable timetable – with your leadership and technical understanding, you are leading a team that maintains our freight and passenger locomotives. You give direction, hold and trust and thus create the basis for a safe operation. Your contribution moves people and goods – day by day, train by train. This is waiting for you:

  • Personal management of the workshop team and professional leadership in close cooperation with the workshop technician
  • Targeted employee development and design of a modern team culture with self-responsibility and confidence
  • Ensure the availability of our locomotive fleet and of our third-party customers for smooth and economic operation
  • Planning and coordination of maintenance work in the daily and weekly rhythm
  • Active cooperation in the further development of maintenance processes and quality assurance, live the KAIZEN thought and implement measures for a new safety culture
  • Basic technical training at level HF or equivalent
  • Leadership experience as well as experience in industrial production, rail maintenance or vehicle construction
  • Networked thinking and solution-oriented action in day-to-day business and open communication
  • Good IT skills (O365, SAP)
  • Ready for individual late and weekend service Your advantages – you can look forward to this:
  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1. Class or half-taxabo.
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy five to ten holiday days a year in addition
  • Financial support for external training and an exciting internal learning and course offer are available to you.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health.
  • 18 days paternity leave and 18 weeks maternity leave with option to extend this for a further 6 months as an unpaid holiday. BLS With nearly 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. More: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight train, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What links us? The pride of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
