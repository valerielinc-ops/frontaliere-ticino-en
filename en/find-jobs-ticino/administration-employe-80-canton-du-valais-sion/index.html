Role overview

Economic employee (40-50%) at the Legal Affairs Section of the Department for Industry, Trade and Labour, in customs Their tasks:

They process the dossiers of the unemployment insurance in German, for which the section is responsible, namely the transfers for the decision of the clarification of the ability to placement, the pre-registrations of short-time work and the notifications of weather-related work loss as well as the request for remission You advise the employees of the regional employment centres as well as employers and job seekers in all matters of unemployment insurance You work together with the various partners (unemployment funds, etc.) They support the French-speaking economic employee of the Unterwallis and the other employees of the section if necessary
