Company and context

  • For our foundry, we are looking for an experienced and organizational shift leader who controls production efficiently, ensures quality and leads the way on the shop floor. Shift leader foundry (m/f/d) Their tasks:

  • Enforcement of all safety regulations, conducting tours and ensuring order
