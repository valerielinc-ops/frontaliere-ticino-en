Role overview

Contact with customers in the lively daily life of a café is a passion for you? Then we have the ideal opportunity for you. For our Caffè Spettacolo in Lausanne we are looking for backup for 4:00 p.m./week, paid per hour. You will be responsible for:

Missions

  • Welcome and advise our customers with professionalism and friendliness, as well as actively sell coffee specialties and the Italians' kindest ones
  • Freshly prepare hot and cold coffee specialties and bakery products
  • Transmit a true Italianità through excellent knowledge of the products and a genuine presence
  • Use the cashier with insurance
  • Present our offer in an attractive way and ensure cleanliness and order of the working environment (including the storage and restocking)

Profile sought

  • Experience or training in catering or sales
  • Strong customer orientation and sense of service
  • Passion for the caffè and an open and enthusiastic personality
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Flexibility to work on weekends and, in service in the morning and evening

What we offer

  • Be part of the main provider of Foodven's life and bring happiness to our customers
  • Exciting development opportunities and online training on our Valora Academy
  • Regular working hours from Monday to Sunday
  • A salary higher than the CCNT
  • An attractive supplement for the evening (20:00–23:00), in addition to night, Sunday and public holidays
