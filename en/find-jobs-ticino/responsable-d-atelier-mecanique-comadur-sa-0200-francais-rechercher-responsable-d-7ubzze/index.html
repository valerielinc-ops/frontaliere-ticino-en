Head of Mechanical Workshop - Swatch Group

Head of Mechanical Workshop Introduction of society Comadur SA, World leader in the production of hard materials components Swiss Made. We are part of the Swatch Group, the world's leading watchmaker. Recognized as the preferred partner of the Group's brands for the production of Ceramics, Sapphire Glaces and Clock Stones for the watch industry.

On our Boncourt production site, we offer you a post of

MECHANICAL WORKSHOP RESPONSIBLE (M/F) Main mission: Manager of mechanical workshop – Manufacturing and development of watch tools, managing and coordinating a team specialized in the manufacture, development and maintenance of tools for the watch industry. It ensures the quality, reliability and timeliness of production while actively contributing to the technical improvement and optimization of manufacturing processes. Description of post

  • Team management and coordination (20%)
  • Supervise, coordinate and accompany a team of specialized micromechanics/polymechanics.
  • Organize and allocate tasks according to production priorities and skills.
  • Ensure performance monitoring, transfer of know-how and skills development.
  • Promote a collaborative working environment within a small team of experts.
  • Production and manufacturing (80%)
  • Supervise the production of mechanical parts made on a fleet of 3-axis and 5-axis CNC machines, electro erosion, grinding machines and some manual operations.
  • Actively participate in the manufacture, development and maintenance of tools.
  • Perform or supervise high-precision machining operations for watchmaking tools.
  • Ensure conformity of parts produced according to technical and quality requirements.
  • Setting and monitoring machines
  • Adjust, program and control CNC machines to ensure optimal operation.
  • Ensure the smooth running of machining operations and intervene in the event of deviations or technical problems.
  • Optimize machining processes and production parameters.
  • Quality and control
  • Guarantee the quality of production through:
  • 3D dimensional controls.
  • The use of measuring instruments.
  • Ensure traceability and compliance of the parts produced.
  • Planning
  • Ensure compliance with production planning.
  • Anticipate manufacturing needs and coordinate priorities with relevant services.
  • Profile
  • Microtechnic technician or CFC micro mechanic, polymerician.
  • Federal Patent of Manager of Mechanical Workshop would be an additional asset
  • Work experience of at least 3 years.
  • Experience in team management.
  • Teacher's degree – apprentice engineer (an asset)
  • Good control of CNC machining 3 axes and 5 axes.
  • Knowledge of CNC and FAO programming.
  • Experience in electro erosion processes.
  • Experience in flat and cylindrical grinding.
  • Experience in dimensional control and precision measurement tools.
  • Qualifications
  • Autonomy and sense of responsibility.
  • Motivation, rigor and professional conscience.
  • Versatility and adaptability.
  • Team spirit and capacity for collaboration between sectors.
  • Proactivity and proposal strength for technical improvements.
  • Languages
  • French current indispensable.
