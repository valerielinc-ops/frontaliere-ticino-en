Main responsibilities

  • Be the preferred contact point for the specialist and generalist press, in direct contact with journalists as well as with our international network of press managers and distributors.
  • Develop, maintain and enrich a network of high-level press contacts (journalists, editors and content creators), ensuring careful monitoring of developments in the media landscape.
  • Contribute to the development and deployment of action plans and press communication strategies, bringing your knowledge of the media and their expectations.
  • Take part in the organisation of events — inaugurations, product launches, watch fairs, exhibitions, interviews — and ensure the qualitative and budgetary follow-up.
  • Design and disseminate press content adapted to different digital platforms and channels, consistent with the universe and brand codes.
  • Organize and coordinate press visits within the Manufacture and follow up after visits (press requests, spillovers, etc.).
  • Monitoring, analysing and valuing press returns in order to measure their impact and recommend optimisation actions. Profile sought
  • 5-7 years of experience in a similar function, within a home watchmaking an asset.
  • Excellent general culture and strong interest in the world of watchmaking.
  • Recognized relational strength and excellent ability to interact with various interlocutors, including journalists, media and external partners.
