Original description

Do you like contact and work as a team? Are you curious and open to novelty? Do you want to be able to move things and face challenges? So join our team and shape the future with us!

We offer comprehensive practical business training in the banking sector. With us, you will learn everything you need for a successful career and to shape your professional future at UBS.

Your training During your three years of learning, you will work in different teams within the bank and discover the many exciting areas of UBS. Our motivated trainers will always be at your side.

In addition to vocational school, you will benefit from practical CYP training in your region. You will meet other apprentices from different banks and learn the theoretical bases of the banking sector. CYP will prepare you perfectly for the final examination of the banking branch. At UBS, you will deepen your knowledge of our products and services and discover our customer advice approach. We will also attach great importance to the development of your social skills such as sales techniques and communication.

In our case, your apprenticeship will not simply end with the Federal Trade Employee Certificate (FCC with or without professional maturity type economy). Many former apprentices stay at home and start their careers with a fixed job or by deepening their knowledge through our two-year Youth Talent Promotion Programme (JUNA).

Your team You will be part of a large group of apprentices employed in UBS commerce. You will meet regularly during internal and external training, events of several days and on many other occasions. Friendships often form, going beyond work and training.

At UBS, we are committed to modern technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) to continuously improve our services – including within your team. This allows us to make processes more efficient, to use data in a smarter way, and to make everyday life easier for colleagues and clients.

Your knowledge and experience You have completed the highest compulsory level with good grades in mathematics, German, English and French. What is particularly important to us:

  • You like working with other people and you're a real Team player.
  • You are motivated and always take a positive attitude.
  • You like learning new things and are open to change.
  • You're interested in the world of finance.

Next steps Apply easily under the following link. On our website, you will also find more information about learning and information sessions near you.
