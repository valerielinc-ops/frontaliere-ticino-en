Application process

  • In this role, you will work closely with local Field Engineering Specialists (FES) and Field Applications Specialists (FAS) to hand over fully operational, validated mass spectrometry systems ready for customer assay implementation. The Opportunity
  • As a Field Service Installation Representative – Mass Spectrometry, you will:
  • Lead & Execute Mass Spec Installations: Perform site pre-inspections, physical installation, assembly, line leveling, and initial setup of cobas i601 mass spectrometry systems and automated liquid-handling front-ends across national customer sites with minimal supervision. Mass Spec
  • Hardware & Infrastructure Setup: Install and verify critical mass spec infrastructure, including ultra-high vacuum (UHV) pumps, atmospheric pressure ionization (ESI/APCI) interface modules, nitrogen and carrier gas supply lines, exhaust systems, and high-pressure liquid chromatography (HPLC/UHPLC) fluidic connections. Connectivity &
  • Software Integration: Install instrument connectivity solutions, network interfaces, and software drivers as part of the total cobas i601 platform integration.
  • Initial System Calibration & TSB Compliance: Perform initial physical alignments, basic vacuum leak checks, fluidic pressure testing, and implement mandatory technical service bulletins (TSBs) and engineering modifications during the installation window.
  • Troubleshoot Installation Issues: Identify, analyze, and resolve mechanical, fluidic, gas-line, electronic, and IT connectivity issues during the installation process to ensure on-time project completion.
  • Cross-Functional FES/FAS Handover: Partner closely with local FES and FAS teams to ensure seamless transition from physical installation to assay validation and user training, ensuring high customer satisfaction.
