Original description

Start your path to social education You are interested in training in the social field and want to find out if social education really fits you? You are at least 20 years old, have the school or teaching completed or are you looking for a meaningful interim solution with perspective? An internship with us offers you the opportunity to get to know the everyday work of social pedagogues: both realistic, practical and accompanied. With us, you will be prepared specifically for training at a university of applied sciences, higher technical school or other social education. If we have training places, you can complete your studies afterwards. Who we are The Bachtelen focuses on children, young people and young adults. We accompany them in challenging situations of life with expertise, commitment and an appreciation attitude. Together with the children, we shape everyday life and create spaces for development, stability and perspectives. Our work lives from relationship, reliability and professionalism – and from being able to accompany children in important stages of development. for the Huus am Schärme in Hägendorf we are looking for from 1.
