  • Planning and implementation of process, financial and compliance audits at national and international Group companies

  • Analysis and evaluation of financial and management reporting of the Group companies
  • Preparation of user-friendly audit reports and presentation of audit results to management qualifications

  • Sound understanding of business relationships as well as analytical thinking skills
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High communication and team skills as well as a secure presence in an international environment
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
