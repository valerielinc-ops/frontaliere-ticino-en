Role overview

As part of the temporary strengthening of our team, we are looking for a future agricultural workshop manager within the Orb Plains Establishments (EPO) under a one-year fixed-term contract.

As such, you accompany detainees in their daily work by ensuring the replacement of the incumbent workshop managers in the various agricultural workshops.

You thus contribute to the continuity of activities, while ensuring that production, safety and supervision objectives are met.
