Original description

Tasks

  • You will learn to manage medical devices such as instruments, appliances and devices, which are used in surgical procedures, diagnostic applications and general care.
  • You clean, disinfect and sterilize these products with the help of state-of-the-art facilities and subject them to careful quality control.
  • You contribute to the safety of medical interventions and the preservation of the value of various devices.

Requirements

  • You are tech-savvy and interested and have craftsmanship skills
  • You work hygienic, careful and precise
  • You are in good physical condition
  • You are reliable, resilient and responsible
  • You appreciate teamwork
  • You have no color vision weakness

More information on parplegie.ch/bonus.

The Swiss Paraplegic Centre in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for cross-sectional, back and respiratory medicine. As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the centre offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives. The clinic is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a worldwide unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with paraplegia.
