Role overview

Tasks

You will develop the company-wide goal and roadmap for the digital guest journey and assume user-related product responsibility for our core applications as well as the future digital guest platforms. This also includes:

  • Control and prioritization of the product backlog according to guest and business benefits, strategic relevance, effort and dependencies
  • Further development of our website, the digital booking and purchase routes as well as other digital contact points and the associated processes
  • Data-based optimization of the digital guest experience with focus on user-friendliness, personalization, conversion and direct business
  • Translating the needs of the business units into clear technical requirements as well as supporting the solutions from conception to successful implementation
  • Professional leadership of cross-divisional initiatives and coordination of cooperation between business units, marketing, CRM, IT and external partners
  • Defining relevant KPIs and ensuring measurable business value, clear priorities and higher time-to-market
  • Evaluation of new technological possibilities – especially in the field of artificial intelligence – and transfer of suitable approaches into concrete applications

Requirements

You have a higher specialist training or a degree in business informatics, digital business, business administration, marketing or a comparable qualification. In addition, you have

  • Several years of experience in digital product management, as a product owner, business analyst or in a comparable interface function between business and IT
