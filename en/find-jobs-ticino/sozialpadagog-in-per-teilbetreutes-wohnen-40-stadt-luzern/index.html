Role overview

Tasks

  • You accompany and advise young people and young adults on the way to an independent lifestyle and include their individual living spaces.

  • You work decentrally at various locations of partially supervised living in the city of Lucerne.
  • You will take part in team meetings and supervision sessions and assist with systemic and psychiatric specialist and case counselling.

  • You work solution-oriented, and also approach challenging situations with a pinch of humor and act calmly and decisively in conflict situations.
