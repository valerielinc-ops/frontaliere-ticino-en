Original description

Pensum: 100% | Stalden

Do you want to contribute to the energy future? Are you ready to become an indispensable part of our team? In this exciting function, you monitor and operate modern power plant plants in shift operation. You detect critical deviations at an early stage, react safely in the event of disruptions and actively contribute to ensuring that our systems run reliably around the clock.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Monitoring and operation of power plants
  • Ensuring safe and trouble-free plant operation
  • Control of installations in the event of extraordinary events and failures
  • Execution of circuits according to switching order or instruction
  • Resolving and preventing disruptions together with the Picettdienst
  • Documentation of operational data and events

Profile & Requirements

  • Completed technical training EFZ
  • Willingness to work alone in shift mode (24/7)
  • Experience in monitoring and controlling industrial plants
  • Knowledge of electricity production and energy transmission is advantageous
  • Good knowledge of Outlook, Excel and Word
  • Category B driving licence
