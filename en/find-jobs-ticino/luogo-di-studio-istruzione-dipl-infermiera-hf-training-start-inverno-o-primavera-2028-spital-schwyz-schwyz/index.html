Role overview

Tasks

HF care professionals bear the professional responsibility throughout the care process. They are responsible for the planning, execution, delegation and monitoring of nursing measures. After obtaining detailed information about the care needs and needs of patients, they create a care concept. In the team, they carry out the care measures and control their effect and success. They closely monitor the course of the disease and the recovery process. In the nursing documentation, they record the latest developments every day and share their knowledge of station reports and medical visits. RF nurses are confronted with challenging situations in their work: they assist patients in different physical and psychological conditions and accompany people in all life situations.

Requirements

For the shortened study (start winter & spring) a degree as a specialist / woman health EFZ, as well as a trial internship in the acute field is required. For the 3-year training (Start Winter) we are looking for people without FaGe degree, with the following requirements:

  • Completion of a min. 3-year basic vocational training with a Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ) or
  • Specialist school card or high school maturity
