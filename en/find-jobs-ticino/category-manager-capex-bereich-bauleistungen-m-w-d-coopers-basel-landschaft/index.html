Original description

position in indirect purchasing responsible You are responsible for strategic procurement and contracting for challenging new and existing construction projects. Your tasks:

  • Contract management: Professional responsibility for setting up German and English-speaking planner, work and supply contracts for new buildings and renovations.
  • Purchasing strategy: design and implementation of procurement strategies in strict compliance with SIA standards and internal guidelines.
  • Tendering: preparation, implementation and support of tendering procedures including the final award.
  • End-to-end processing: holistic monitoring of the purchasing process from the order to the supplement management to the invoice audit.
  • Performance monitoring: Continuous analysis and control of operational and strategic KPIs in our own portfolio.
  • Timeline Controlling: Proactive appointment management and initiation of targeted measures in the event of plan deviations.
  • Sourcing & Supplier Management: Strategic evaluation of sources of supply as well as continuous development and expansion of partner networks.
  • Stakeholder management: Close, interdisciplinary cooperation with the engineering, production and finance departments.
  • Market research: systematic monitoring of trends, price developments and supplier markets.
  • Process optimization: Continuous development of procurement processes, standard interfaces and contract templates.
  • Crisis Management: Fast and solution-oriented troubleshooting in the event of deadline or quality problems.
  • Project collaboration: Bringing in-depth purchasing expertise to various internal project teams.
  • Team Support: Ensure smooth mutual representation within the Capex purchasing team. Your profile:
  • Basic technical education with commercial further education (e.g. technical / merchant / woman, purchasing specialist / woman, business economist / HF) or a degree in industrial engineering, mechanical engineering or a similar discipline.
  • Your experience: At least 5 years of practice in purchasing the chemical or pharmaceutical industry, of which proven at least 2 years in the procurement of construction services.
  • Your know-how: Deep understanding of complex contract mechanisms for purchasing technical components and services as well as in-depth knowledge of SAP and MS Office.
  • Her way of working: Analytically strong, strategically thinking and solution-oriented with a healthy dose of pragmatism (hands-on mentality).
  • Your personality: strong communication, inclusive and winning personality with assertiveness and entrepreneurial drive.
  • Negotiable in German and English
  • Mindset: You are flexible, open-minded and curious. Your high willingness to perform and the joy of developing and implementing pragmatic solutions distinguish you.
  • Tools: You master SAP and MS Office absolutely safely and use these systems efficiently in your daily work environment.
  • Mindset: Your strong analytical skills allow you to think strategically and quickly penetrate complex issues.
  • Interaction: You convince with strong negotiating skills and a clear, targeted communication ability at all levels.
