Company and context

  • Our employees are committed to making a difference in our organization, for our client partners and the patients we serve.* The job:
  • The Regional Director, Engineering provides strategic engineering leadership across the region (Americas or EMEA) ensuring engineering capability, capital investment, asset reliability, infrastructure planning, and engineering governance support the long-term growth of the Clinical Trials Division.
  • Reporting to the Senior Director of Engineering for CTD, this role is accountable for developing regional engineering strategy, leading the regional capital portfolio, driving engineering standards, enabling operational excellence, and building organizational capability across the engineering network.
  • The Regional Director partners closely with regional business leaders, Operations, Quality, Supply Chain, Global Engineering and site leadership to ensure engineering investments are aligned with business priorities while delivering safe, compliant, reliable and scalable operations.
  • This role can be based at either our Allschwill (Switzerland), or Horsham (UK) sites.
  • It provides oversight to all CTD locations within the EMEA region.
  • Significant time may be spent at the appropriate CTD facilities within the region, including up to 50% travel to network locations globally.
  • What will you do? Strategic Leadership
  • Develop and execute the regional engineering strategy aligned with CTD business objectives, driving long-term infrastructure, engineering capability, and operational excellence across the regional network.
  • Lead regional engineering planning, including site master plans, multi-year capital investment strategies, and engineering roadmaps that support business growth, capacity expansion, and business continuity.
