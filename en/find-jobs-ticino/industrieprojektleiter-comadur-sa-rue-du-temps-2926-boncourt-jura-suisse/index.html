Industrial Project Leader - Swatch Group

Industrial Project Leader Introduction of society Comadur SA, World leader in the production of Swiss Made hard materials components. We are part of the Swatch Group, the world's leading watchmaker. Recognized as the preferred partner of the Group's brands for the production of Ceramics, Sapphire Glaces and Clock Stones for the watch industry.

  • As part of our development, we offer, on our Boncourt site, a position of:
  • Project Manager Industrialization M/F
  • Your commitment will contribute to our growth as well as to your personal development through:
  • A modern and pleasant work space.
  • Social conditions of first order.
  • A dynamic, demanding team, listening and always looking for new challenges.
  • A common will: to collaborate and grow together to become an indispensable partner.
  • Description of post
  • Manager and lead on several industrialization projects, from a technical and administrative point of view:

In the study phase: search for solutions, plans of experience in the field, writing of test reports, writing of specifications, calls for tenders, comparatives, choice of technical solutions, administrative installation of investment files.

  • In the production phase: supplier manufacturing, testing and development of machinery, training and production transmission.
  • Research and development of innovative and industrializable manufacturing processes for the manufacture of hard material components.
  • To guarantee the stability and technical reliability of the equipment and processes in place.
  • Support for production in terms of problem solving and continuous improvement.
  • Profile
  • You have a degree in HES Engineer or ET Technician.
  • You are multidisciplinary and experienced: mechanical, automation, processes, highly interested in both automation and processes specific to hard materials.
  • You have experience in monitoring industrial projects (automation + manufacturing processes).
  • You have mastered the common computer tools and native French language, as well as good knowledge of English and German.
  • You are flexible when travelling on different company sites.
  • You have a technical personality, resistant, constructive, proactive, communicative and structured.
  • Qualifications
  • We are looking for an organized person, able to work in multitasking with excellent relational qualities both with external and internal interlocutors.
  • Equipped with a good openness of mind, creativity and dynamism, knowing how to step back and listen.
  • Languages
  • English is an asset.
  • Contact person
  • If you are motivated by this new challenge, please send us your complete application form via our online platforms to Ms Perrine YALA.
