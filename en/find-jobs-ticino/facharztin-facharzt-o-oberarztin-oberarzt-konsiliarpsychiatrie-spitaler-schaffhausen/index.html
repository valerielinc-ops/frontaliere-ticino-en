Role overview

The Breitenau Psychiatry Center is, in addition to the Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service and the Cantonal Hospital, one of several locations of the Schaffhausen hospitals.

The Breitenau Psychiatry Center in Schaffhausen’s Breite Quarter consists of the Clinic for Psychiatry and Psychotherapy with its inpatient, day-clinical and outpatient treatment services as well as long-term psychiatry.
