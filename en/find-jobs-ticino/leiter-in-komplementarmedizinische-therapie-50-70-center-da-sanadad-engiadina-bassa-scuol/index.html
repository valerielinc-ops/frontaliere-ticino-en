Original description

As part of succession planning, we are seeking a committed individual to lead the complementary therapy department starting January 1, 2027, or as agreed, following the retirement of the current position holder. Together, we aim to further develop the future of complementary and anthroposophical therapy and establish inpatient pain therapy.

Responsibilities

Main tasks

  • You will take on the technical and organizational leadership of the complementary therapy department
  • You will lead, support, and promote your team, creating a respectful work environment where employees can thrive
  • You will assist with staff deployment planning, recruitment of new employees, and their onboarding and training
  • You will ensure well-organized processes and smooth daily operations
  • You will actively contribute to the development of the department as well as quality and organizational projects
  • You will collaborate closely with physicians, nursing staff, therapists, and other professional groups
  • You will represent complementary therapy in interdisciplinary committees and share your expertise with colleagues
  • You will continue to work in your therapeutic specialty and directly support patients in treatment
  • You will contribute to the development of innovative projects

Requirements

Profile requirements

  • You hold the Advanced Federal Diploma of Higher Education in Complementary Therapy (HFP) or are willing to obtain it
  • Ideally, you have recognition from EMR, ASCA, and/or EGK
  • You have leadership experience or are keen to develop in this area
  • You bring your own anthroposophical-complementary therapeutic specialty
  • You communicate openly, respectfully, and approach people with empathy
  • You enjoy working in an interdisciplinary environment
  • You work in a structured and responsible manner, maintaining composure in challenging situations
  • You possess organizational skills, initiative, and the ability to make clear and decisive decisions

We offer

Our offer

  • A responsible position (management role) with significant creative freedom
  • Clearly structured working hours, high quality of life in a vacation region with diverse recreational and sports opportunities
  • Integration into a committed, multiprofessional team in an inspiring environment in the Engadine
  • Excellent collaboration with the hospital and other operational units of the health center
  • Interdisciplinary work environment with short decision-making paths
  • Attractive employment conditions and support for further education and training
  • Opportunity to develop integrative concepts in pain medicine

Under the umbrella of the "Center da sandà Engiadina Bassa" (CSEB) / "Gesundheitszentrum Unterengadin," key regional providers in healthcare and wellness are working together. In the National Park region, you will find a wonderful environment with diverse recreational and sports opportunities.

The offerings of the regional hospital, emergency services, rehabilitation clinic, Spitex, care groups, nursing homes, and the Bogn Engiadina mineral baths are optimally coordinated. With this integrated care model, CSEB has been a pioneer in the region and beyond national borders.

Clinica Curativa combines excellence in conventional medicine with complementary medicine expertise. Our integrative clinic stands for patient-centered and holistic treatment in the fields of complementary medicine, psychosomatics, and oncology. It is currently developing an innovative and integrative pain center that provides inpatient care for patients with acute and chronic pain in an interdisciplinary and multimodal setting.
