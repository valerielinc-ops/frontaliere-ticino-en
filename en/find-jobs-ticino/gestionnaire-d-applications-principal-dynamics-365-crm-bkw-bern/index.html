Original description

Your field of activity

  • Ensure the stable and secure operation of the Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM, customer engagement and power platform
  • Together with internal and external partners, you coordinate and accompany changes, releases, tests and productive introductions and ensure reliable implementation of the solutions
  • Business requirements from different specialist areas are analyzed and prioritized and translated into viable technical solutions
  • You will advise departments, product owners and project managers on issues relating to processes, data, integrations, authorizations and automation
  • Platforms, integrations and automation are continuously developed and thus support a sustainable and efficient system landscape
  • You will continuously evaluate modern technologies, AI services and platform functions of the Microsoft Cloud and actively contribute their potential to the further development of the solution landscape.

You bring that with you

  • You have a higher education in computer science, business informatics or a comparable technical field
  • Collaboration with different stakeholders also succeeds in challenging and changing system landscapes, building relationships and maintaining an overview
  • Several years of experience in application management (ideally Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM) as well as in the operation and further development of corresponding platforms distinguish you
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Azure, the power platform and modern cloud, integration and automation solutions
  • You will analyze complex technical and technical relationships in a structured manner and find pragmatic and sustainable solutions.
  • Very good German and good English skills round off your profile
  • Certifications in the Microsoft Dynamics environment are an advantage

We do not want requests from personnel service providers.

We look forward to your online application.

About us
