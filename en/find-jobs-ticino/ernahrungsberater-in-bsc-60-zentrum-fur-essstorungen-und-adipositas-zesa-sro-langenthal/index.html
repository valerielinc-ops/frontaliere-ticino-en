Role overview

At the Centre for Eating Disorders and Obesity ZESA, we offer a multi-professional treatment concept for people with eating disorders and resulting weight problems.

By combining medical assessment, psychological and physiotherapeutic diagnosis, psychotherapy, nutritional counselling and physiotherapy, individual strategies can be developed.

We support and advise relatives of those affected in their role.

  • At the Centre for Eating Disorders and Obesity ZESA, we offer a multi-professional treatment concept for people with eating disorders and resulting weight problems.
  • By combining medical assessment, psychological and physiotherapeutic diagnosis, psychotherapy, nutritional counselling and physiotherapy, individual strategies can be developed.
  • In close cooperation with the medical service, you carry out medical checks at the obesity consultation.
  • They care for adults with overweight or obesity and corresponding comorbidities.

Main responsibilities

  • In close cooperation with the medical service, you carry out medical checks at the obesity consultation.
  • They care for adults with overweight or obesity and corresponding comorbidities.
  • They accompany patients in the context of conservative or drug-assisted weight reduction efforts or before and after a bariatric procedure according to SMOB standards and work in an interdisciplinary team.
  • As part of the multi-professional team, you will bring input and medical knowledge to the case meetings and weekly boards. Requirements
  • You have completed a master’s degree (MScN) in nursing or will soon complete it.
  • They have a particular interest and, if possible, knowledge of the counselling areas of obesity and bariatrics.
  • Motivating conversation is up to you.
  • They are conversational and keep calm even in tricky counseling situations.
  • When dealing with devices and software (Microsoft Office, Outlook, video telephony, clinic information system, voice recorder) you are fit after an introduction.
  • In addition to your in-depth expertise, your human qualities are just as important to us.
