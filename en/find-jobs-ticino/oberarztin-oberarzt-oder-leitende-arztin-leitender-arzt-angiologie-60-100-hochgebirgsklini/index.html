Role overview

Angiology and Cardiology work with us as a team.

Together, we care for patients with complex cardiovascular diseases in an outpatient and inpatient setting.

The Hochgebirgsklinik Davos is the only stationary angiological rehabilitation clinic in Switzerland.

  • Angiology and Cardiology work with us as a team.
  • Together, we care for patients with complex cardiovascular diseases in an outpatient and inpatient setting.
  • Care for complex vascular diseases: You treat patients with complex angiological diseases in an outpatient and inpatient setting.
  • Develop Angiology: You develop Angiology together with Cardiology in our cardiovascular team.

Main responsibilities

  • Care for complex vascular diseases: You treat patients with complex angiological diseases in an outpatient and inpatient setting.
  • Develop Angiology: You develop Angiology together with Cardiology in our cardiovascular team.
