Original description

Place of work: Hirslanden BernOccupation per: 01.12.2026 or by appointment type of employment: Indefinite reference number: 73482

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, the Hirslanden Group sets standards with its clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of the people are the focus.

Hirslanden Bern’s vocational training Human Resources accompanies around 160 trainees in 18 different training and further education courses in the fields of nursing, medicine, therapy and technology, domestic service, catering and administration. In this way, it makes an important contribution to achieving the overall corporate goals of the organization. Hirslanden Bern includes the three Bern clinics Salem-Spital, Beau-Site and Permanence as well as the outpatient centers Medical Center Wankdorf and OPERA Bern.

The vocational training team of Hirslanden Bern is looking for reinforcement! In your role as a vocational training officer, you support the vocational training team in administrative and organizational matters. You will work closely with the training managers and get exciting insights into 18 different professions at the EFZ and tertiary level.

  • Extensive benefits offer (e.g. publishing, public transport coupons, fitness subscriptions, etc.)
  • Discounted food options in restaurants in clinics

YOUR TASKS

  • Support for the administrative recruitment and support process of learners and students
  • Participation in the organisation and implementation of educational events
  • Contact person for internal and external stakeholders in vocational training by e-mail and telephone (educational institutions, vocational trainees, training managers, internal bodies, learners and students)
  • Management of the platforms for vocational internships and for the applications of our training professions
  • Taking on a wide range of administrative and organisational tasks in close cooperation with the training managers

Your profile

  • Professional experience, ideally in the educational, health or hospitality sector

  • Enjoy working with different stakeholders as well as administrative and organisational activities
