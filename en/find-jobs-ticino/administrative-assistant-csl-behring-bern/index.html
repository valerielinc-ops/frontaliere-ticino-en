Role overview

Under the direction of the Senior Executive Assistant, the Administrative Assistant provides comprehensive administrative support to ensure smooth daily operations for a team or department.

This role involves managing calendars, coordinating travel, organizing meetings, and preparing documents and reports.

The assistant serves as a key point of contact for internal and external stakeholders, handles correspondence, maintains filing systems, and supports onboarding activities.
