Original description

Arxada is a global leader in innovative solutions to protect our world. Our groundbreaking technologies, comprehensive regulatory expertise, and proficiency in production and process development help our customers efficiently safeguard food, health, and infrastructure – through chemistry and biotechnology that promote sustainability. We offer a broad portfolio of ingredients and services for various end markets, including Health & Nutrition, Home & Personal Care, Professional Hygiene, Paints & Coatings, and Wood Protection. With customers in over 100 countries, the company achieved sales of 2 billion CHF in 2024. Arxada's headquarters are located in Basel, Switzerland. Worldwide, the company employs 3,200 people at 24 production sites and 14 R&D centers, all committed to the success of our customers. To strengthen our team in Visp, Switzerland, we are currently seeking an employee for Laboratory Equipment Support / Support in Quality Control during daytime operations. As a team member in Equipment Support within Quality Control, you play a crucial role in ensuring stable and efficient laboratory processes. In this versatile technical role, you support our QC team by ensuring the functionality of our laboratory instruments, thus making a direct contribution to the quality of our products and the success of our customers. You will work in an environment that fosters collaboration, values technical interest, and provides space for continuous improvement. Here, you actively participate, drive topics forward, and help us go beyond the ordinary. ## Main Tasks and Responsibilities:

  • Support and ensure smooth operational processes within QC
  • Main responsibility for preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, and minor repairs of chromatography equipment
  • Ensure correct functionality of all laboratory instruments
  • Responsible for instrument qualification including organization and filing of documentation (electronic and physical) according to LIMS requirements
  • Communication with equipment suppliers and organization of services and repairs, including initiating corresponding SAP orders
  • Creation and improvement of relevant work instructions
  • Active support of QC strategy and participation in continuous improvement initiatives ## Qualification & Experience:
  • Completed vocational training, preferably as a chemical laboratory technician or in a technical/craft profession (e.g., mechanic)
  • Teamwork skills, sense of responsibility, resilience, and strong communication skills
  • High quality awareness as well as efficient and reliable working methods
  • Good comprehension and independent, structured working style
  • Confident handling of potentially hazardous substances
  • Good PC skills (standard software)
  • Excellent German language skills, both spoken and written; basic English knowledge is an advantage
  • Technical interest and enjoyment in working with delicate and sensitive equipment ## What We Offer Arxada provides first-class solutions in two clearly defined areas:
  • Microbial Control Solutions (MCS) focuses on threats posed by microorganisms to humans and the environment. This area includes five independent business units: Professional Hygiene, Home & Personal Care, Wood Protection, Paints & Coatings, and Material Protection.
  • Nutrition, Care & Environmental (NCE) supplies materials for the production of composites in electronics, aerospace, and other industries. The portfolio also includes vitamins and nutritional components, high-performance chemical intermediates, and raw materials for a wide range of demanding applications.
