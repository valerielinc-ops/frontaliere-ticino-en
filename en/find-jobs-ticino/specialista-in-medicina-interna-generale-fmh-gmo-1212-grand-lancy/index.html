Role overview

The Centre Santé de Lancy – Pont-Rouge is looking for a medical specialist in general internal medicine, wishing to practise as an independent, in order to complement his team of health professionals.

Ideally located in the heart of the dynamic district of Lancy Pont-Rouge, our Health Centre is a medical facility of proximity, accessible to both the inhabitants of the area and to people working there or coming from outside.

Our approach is based on quality family medicine, based on listening, continuity of care and a relationship of trust with patients.
