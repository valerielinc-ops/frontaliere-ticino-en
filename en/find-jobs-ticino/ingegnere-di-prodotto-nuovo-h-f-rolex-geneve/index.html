Introduction For our Plaque Milling sector, we are looking for a New Products (H/F) Professional Engineer whose main mission will be to be the technical sponsor and to coordinate the transition of new products into production. Responsibilities

  • Coordinate the passage of new products according to the production plan
  • To guarantee the relevance of our manufacturing line (tools and necessary documents) and the start-up of production

  • Participate in project risk analysis
  • Making the operating balance of a new product's registration
  • Ensure proper coordination between project respondents, operating technicians and scheduling
  • Representing the sector in new product bodies
  • Ensure communication with management (reporting, blocking points, ...)
  • Collaborate on the implementation of rigorous monitoring of workshop qualification orders
  • Conduct project sessions on the sector Profile
  • Microtechnic Engineer
  • Industrial experience required
  • With a strong team spirit and good communication
  • Ability to take back on complex projects and flows
  • Of a proactive nature, you have a very good spirit of analysis and synthesis
  • Flexible and responsive, ability to adapt to changing projects
  • Priority Direction and Stress Resistance Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits. Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
