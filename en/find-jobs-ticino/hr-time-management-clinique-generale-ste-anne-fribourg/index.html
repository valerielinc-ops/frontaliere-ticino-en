Mansioni HR-Zeitwirtschaft und Systemberatung Polypoint PEP Do you want to actively shape the HR-Zeitwirtschaft and improve processes sustainably? In this responsible position, we continuously develop our Polypoint PEP time management system and contribute significantly to efficient HR processes. As a central contact person for users in the field of quality management, our internal customers advise you competently. They support users in all matters relating to the deployment and planning system and bring their expertise to the continuous optimization of our work processes. Your area of responsibility also includes the maintenance of icons, supplements and piquet services according to the applicable requirements and regulations. In addition, you are a reliable support for our HR teams – especially in the areas of social security and lowering management. They capture wage-relevant mutations, process absentees and actively participate in monthly, quarterly and annual accounts. In addition, you control lowering and piquetting services before each payroll and continuously check the planning data for their accuracy.
  • Development and maintenance of the Polypoint PEP time management system

  • Care of icons, allowances and piquetting services according to requirements and regulations
