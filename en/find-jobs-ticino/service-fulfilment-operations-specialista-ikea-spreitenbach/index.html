Role overview

Who you are

The overall purpose of this role is last mile route planning for customers’ services.

You will be responsible for maintaining and changing various parameters in our transport management system to efficient routes and securing an excellent service experience for our customers.

Main responsibilities

  • Your responsibilities Responsible for route planning
  • adjusting fleet, approval, communication to Transport Service Providers
  • you will be an operating part of the team that monitors our route planning 6 days a week.
  • Implementing quality and processes with steakholders (training/follow-up).
  • Responsible for continuously fine-tuning our route planning tool to ensure optimal performance with our various Transport Service Providers.
  • Analyzing data sets, creating reports and bringing insights that support last mile operations and cost development.
  • Administrating and updating fleet data as well as adjusting capacity parameters in our route planning tool.
  • Documenting and optimizing all processes.
  • Ability to stay focused and think clearly when unexpected situations occur and take the needed corrective actions on various types of deviations to safeguard our last mile delivery precision and to meet our customer expectations.
  • It is essential that you have a very close cooperation with both our Remote Customer Meeting Point (RCMP), our different Transport Service Providers and the Fulfilment Units, as all these stakeholders can have input or changes to specific customer deliveries.
