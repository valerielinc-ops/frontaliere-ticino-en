Mission

The Professor Courtine's laboratory at EPFL is offering a position for a Test Engineer to work on the development of neurorehabilitation therapies for patients with motor disabilities.

Main responsibilities

  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • Support.
  • NeuroRestore researchers and clinicians at CHUV by verifying and validating robust software that contributors to various stages of therapy development (included: imagery, therapy optimization, interface with the electrical stimulus, home use)
  • Ensuring medical grade software quality following industry best practices for patients' use at home and for researchers in rehabilitation facilities
  • Designing, coding, and driving the automation of test suites across the software stack
  • Building and improving a hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) test bench for systems interfacing with sensors and spinal cord stimulators
  • Defining and implementing test strategies (unit, integration, system, regression) with a strong emphasis on traceability and reproducibility
  • Providing remote as well as on site support to researchers, clinicians and patients in using these tools and systems
  • Contributing to release testing, verification & validation documentation, and quality records in line with IEC 62304 or related standards
  • Working close with software engineers to identify risks early and effective meticular, high quality deliverables Profile
