Responsible for traction current installations (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Make Switzerland move with us. Responsible for traction current installations (m/f/d) Brig, 01.03.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you. We ensure the safety and availability of railway infrastructure by monitoring the condition of electrical installations (traction current, associated systems), engineering structures and access. We take care of the necessary immediate and planned measures. Customer satisfaction is essential to us, so we provide lifecycle managers with data to improve the quality of installations. We represent the SBB during work near electrical installations, verify the conformity of the equipment upon receipt and have any anomalies corrected. We examine projects to facilitate future maintenance and, in the event of incidents, identify the technical causes. To find out about social benefits, such as free GA and discounted travel abroad (also for the family), go to our website under Social benefits. What you can make move. - As manager of “Traction current” installations, you guarantee their safety and availability and defend the interests of the owner with internal and external services. You identify the causes of breakdowns, particularly on switching elements. - You analyze and evaluate the results from different monitoring (diagnostic vehicle, local measurements, visual inspections), taking into account the risks. You define and assign maintenance measures (quality, deadlines) to internal and external partners and trigger immediate actions if necessary.

  • You ensure that the new installations are fault-free, carry out the SIOP B examination if necessary, and, as representative of the project owner, defend its interests vis-à-vis third parties (individuals, municipalities, authorities). You ensure installation data is updated.
  • You technically receive maintenance and repair work and manage defects.
  • Depending on the installation, you plan, coordinate and control the execution work; you manage the mandated companies and guarantee the compliance of the work and safety. What you bring.
  • Thanks to your experience, you have developed excellent communication skills: you are comfortable searching for faults, particularly on switching elements, and know how to give clear and precise instructions to CFF colleagues and external companies.
  • In terms of training, you benefit from a CFC in a technical field and experience in leading contact line sites (team leader, foreman or project manager). You will follow the “contact line” and “cables and cable protection” expert certification training courses.
  • You have excellent knowledge of maintenance guidelines, facility components, hazardous situations, as well as construction and operation processes.
  • You have an excellent level in French or German (minimum C1 level), as well as a good level in the second language (ideally from level B2).
  • In addition, you have good IT knowledge (DfA, SAP, AMGT, ZMON and MS Office).
