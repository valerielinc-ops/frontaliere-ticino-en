Original description

Thanks to its 800 employees, the Intercantonal Hospital of Broye (HIB) provides an acute care and rehabilitation mission. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative health institution striving for excellence for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges that await it, the HIB is looking for, for its treatment and rehabilitation center, a: Community care and health assistant from 60% to 90% Your mission

  • Collaborate closely with the nursing team in the care of patients
  • Support patients, in a responsible and autonomous manner, for aspects of care relating to activities of daily life (toilet, elimination, meal assistance, mobilization)
  • Ensure the execution and monitoring of care delegated by the team of nurses
  • Provide medical-technical acts on delegation in stable care situations
  • Carry out administrative and logistical work inherent to your activity, mainly related to patient care Your assets
  • You have a CFC of Community Care and Health Assistant (ASSC) or title deemed equivalent recognized by the Red Cross
  • You preferably have experience in rehabilitation (an asset)
  • You are comfortable with IT tools, as well as familiar with the Soarian computerized patient file
  • With a great ability to work in a team, dynamism and autonomy, you demonstrate rigor and responsibility in carrying out your tasks
  • You know how to manage priorities and make yourself available when necessary
  • Excellent command of spoken and written French Starting position: immediately or to be agreed. We thank you for sending us your complete application file by applying online. For any additional questions about this position, please contact Mr. Jaouade Lakrouni, ICUS, on 026 662 70 13.
