Application process

  • High-temperature superconducting materials have interesting characteristics that make them suitable for future magnet applications.
  • Come join CERN and take part in this exciting development.
  • You will develop and implement electromechanical systems for the manufacture and assembly of high-temperature superconducting (HTS) magnets, as part of an ongoing research and development (R&D) programme.
  • Your work will involve coil winding and assembly, instrumentation and tooling, bringing together mechanical, electrical and control technologies. Functions
  • Design and implement dedicated systems to produce high-temperature superconductor-based (HTS) coils, considering mechanical, electrical and control aspects, according to applicable safety regulations and industry standards.
  • Conduct regular maintenance of machinery, electromechanical systems and technical infrastructure in the HTS magnet laboratory.
  • Prepare technical documentation, including procedures, test and measurement reports, and ensure traceability of components.
  • Coordinate component logistics and stock for assigned assemblies; initiate and follow up manufacturing requests and purchase orders.
