Company and context

  • Reporting to the Traction Service Sales and Marketing Manager you will support the Sales organization and customers throughout the sales process to determine customer specific needs and pricing.
  • You will be part of the Sales Team that focuses on Traction Transformer Service in Europe.
  • As Tendering Sales Specialist it is your exciting task to facilitate the sustainable mobility transition by a cutting-edge rail product and rail service portfolio for rolling stock equipment.
  • In this team, you will work with international peers having many years of experience within the on-board traction transformer service business for rolling stock equipment.
  • Based in Geneva, the role will assume responsibility for the rail customers in Europe (OEM train manufacturers, rail operators, leasing companies).
  • If you are customer-focused, highly organized, and thrive in a dynamic technical environment, we would love to hear from you.
  • How you'll make an impact Prepare and manage quotations for traction service activities (oil analysis, on-site customer Service interventions, workshop repairs, spare traction transformers and maintenance price catalogs) using MS Office and corresponding pricing calculation templates, ensuring accurate commercial and pricing information in Salesforce and other business systems Act as the primary point of contact for customer requests, proactively following up on quotations, opportunities, and commercial inquiries Coordinating communication and follow-up on cases with internal teams (sales, logistics, purchasing, finance, and technical support) to ensure the creation and processing of service offers Monitoring customer satisfaction and managing issues with escalation if needed.
  • Providing customer support including sharing information about service offerings, products, and prices based on current standard rates.
