Role overview

IT system administrator and application manager (m/f/d)

Do you love variety instead of a stubborn IT routine? You keep a cool head even when the servers glow and a colleague needs help at the same time? Then you are exactly right with us! Pfister Curtain Service is a market leader in the field of curtains and solutions around the window. With around 100 employees, we advise customers at home and assemble in our own company in Thörishaus. With us, you are not a small cog in the transmission, but the digital hub that keeps our entire IT landscape running from A to Z.

  • Responsibility for the operation and the ongoing development of the IT system landscape
  • Support, optimization and expansion of IT tools, applications and interfaces
  • Coordination and allocation of tasks to external IT partners across all levels of support as well as ensuring 2nd-level support
  • Management and implementation of IT and organizational projects
  • Creation and maintenance of process, system and IT documentation
  • Support and training of employees in the application of IT systems
  • Close cooperation with the departments and function as a link between business, IT and external partners
  • Management of IT hardware and related infrastructure
  • Completed training in computer science or business informatics, for example as a computer scientist in EFZ, or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in the IT environment, ideally as an application manager or in a comparable function, preferably in an SME or production company
  • Experience in dealing with ERP systems as well as a good understanding of applications, interfaces, data flows and operational processes
  • In-depth knowledge of Microsoft Windows, Microsoft 365, network technologies and server administration
  • Knowledge of CRM, production or infrastructure processes is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong responsibility and a service-oriented hands-on mentality
  • Ability to communicate technical connections in an understandable way, combined with a patient and user-oriented approach
  • native language German or French with good knowledge of the other language
  • English skills are beneficial
