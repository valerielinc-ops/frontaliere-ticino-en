SCI Finance Specialist

Your Mission

Attached to the Accountancy Manager , you , as a specialist in the Financial Internal Control System , help ensure the reliability of financial information , the control of risks and the efficiency of processes , in accordance with the applicable legal and regulatory requirements and the principles of the COSO benchmark . As such, you pilot and evolve the financial SCI , structuring and accompanying the updating process. You guarantee the integration of SCI into the company's quality management system by monitoring and evaluating its maturity and performance. You will also assist process managers in identifying and controlling risks and in the appropriation and application of internal controls.

In collaboration with the Accountant, you will monitor the internal and external audit points relating to the financial SCI. Finally, you are involved in cross-cutting projects involving supplier and debtor accounting and in monitoring developments in internal control and auditing standards, legal requirements and good practices.

Your profile

  • You have a Bachelor's degree in Business Economics or Business Management, an expert-accounting degree an asset
  • Confirmed professional experience of at least 3 years in a similar function
  • Good control of SAP software is an asset
  • You stand out by your ability to perform tasks accurately and rigorously
  • Very autonomous, you know how to get up and have an overview of the company
  • At ease in communicating with your partners, you understand the constraints of your internal clients and you show strength of proposal in a continuous improvement perspective
