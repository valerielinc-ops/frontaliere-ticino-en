Original description

Welcome to Medbase Gampelen

Our versatile medical center is located in the center of Gampelen, not far from the municipal administration. Here we care for patients in an interdisciplinary and quality-oriented manner. Our team, consisting of around 25 employees, includes specialists in general internal medicine, cardiology, dermatology, gynecology, pediatric and adolescent medicine, psychiatry and psychotherapy as well as MPAs. At Medbase Gampelen we pay attention to a good work-life balance. Part-time work is possible in all functions and working hours can be arranged flexibly.

You can move that

During your training to become an MPA EFZ, you will get to know all facets of practical work and become part of a supportive team. Medbase accompanies you in your training and gives you space to develop your strengths.

  • Receiving and caring for patients and preparing examinations
  • Supporting doctors during consultation hours and during procedures
  • Independently carry out laboratory tests and x-rays
  • Administrative tasks and appointment coordination
  • Independently taking on various tasks

What you bring with you

  • Good academic performance in secondary school A / level E
  • Interest in medicine and enjoy contact with people
  • Empathy and reliability
  • Well-groomed appearance and communicative personality
  • Willingness to work independently
  • Good knowledge of German (at least B2 level)

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from various disciplines work hand in hand to offer people throughout Switzerland integrated, first-class all-round medical care. We support doctors with logistics services and digital solutions so that they can fully concentrate on patients.

The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
