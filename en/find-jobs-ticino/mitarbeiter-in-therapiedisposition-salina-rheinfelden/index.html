Salina rehabilitation clinic

Employee therapy disposition 80-90% by appointment

  • limited until June 30, 2027
  • Rheinfelden

The Reha Rheinfelden, the Salina Rehaklinik and the Park-Hotel am Rhein AG employ around 1,000 people. To achieve the best possible rehabilitation, interdisciplinary teams work together, with each individual's specialist knowledge and experience contributing to success. The Salina Rehabilitation Clinic (rehab clinic, specialists and therapies) offers an interdisciplinary concept for prevention, diagnosis and treatment of the musculoskeletal system. The medical competence center includes modern diagnostic and therapy facilities with a competent team of specialists, therapists and nursing staff. Your team At the Salina Therapies location of Rehab Rheinfelden, we are looking for a committed and warm personality to join our team of outpatient and inpatient therapy scheduling as a temporary replacement who will actively support us. Do you like to keep track of things, enjoy contact with people and use your reliable nature to ensure that everything runs smoothly in the background? Then we look forward to getting to know you.

What awaits you

  • Receiving and processing therapy orders
  • Obtaining and processing cost approvals from health insurance companies and insurance companies
  • Planning and coordination of outpatient and inpatient therapy appointments
  • Friendly and competent care for our patients, in person and by telephone
  • Close collaboration with our therapists and other internal departments

What you bring with you

  • Training in the administrative area
  • Safe handling of IT and data systems
  • Interest in healthcare
  • Reliable and careful way of working
  • Enjoy personal and telephone contact with people
  • Teamwork skills
  • Flexible and resilient personality
