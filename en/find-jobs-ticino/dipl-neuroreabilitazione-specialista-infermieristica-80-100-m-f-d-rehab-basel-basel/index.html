About us Anything but everyday

The REHAB Basel, Clinic for Neurorehabilitation and Paraplegiology, treats and rehabilitates patients with brain injury, paraplegia and related clinical pictures after an accident or illness.

Our ultimate goal is, together with the people entrusted to us, to achieve the greatest possible independence and an optimal quality of life.
