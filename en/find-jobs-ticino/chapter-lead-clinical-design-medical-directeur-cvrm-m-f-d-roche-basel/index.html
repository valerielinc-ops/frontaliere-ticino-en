Here's what we're looking for

At Roche, we believe it is urgent to deliver medical solutions right now – even as we develop innovations for the future. We are passionate about transforming patients’ lives. We are courageous in both decision and action; we believe that good business means a better world.

At Roche Diagnostics, we are transforming diagnostics into an intelligent, data-driven system of care, powered by AI and digital solutions. Our ambition is to move from isolated clinical insights to continuous, data-driven decision support across the care continuum—with a strong focus on real-world adoption and impact.

The Clinical Design Chapter is a team of clinical, medical, and healthcare system experts. It ensures that Roche Information Solutions delivers clinically meaningful, system-relevant, and economically viable digital and AI-enabled healthcare solutions that are adopted and scaled in real-world care settings. The Chapter plays a critical role in shaping clinical strategy, embedding healthcare system insights into product development, and enabling effective go-to-market and adoption strategies.

The Chapter Lead Clinical Design & Medical Director CVRM combines enterprise leadership with direct clinical domain accountability. Reporting to the Head of Digital Technology & Health Information (DTHI), this role ensures that healthcare expertise is scaled across Roche Information Solutions (RIS) while directly owning the clinical strategy and medical leadership for the Cardiovascular, Renal, and Metabolic (CVRM) disease area—particularly in the context of AI-enabled, data-driven care models and their real-world adoption.

Your upcoming mission

Define and drive the global clinical value design strategy across RIS, with a strong focus on digital health, AI-enabled care transformation, and real-world adoption. Lead and develop a global team of healthcare experts and medical directors. Ensure clinical, medical, and healthcare system perspectives are embedded into AI-enabled product development, data-driven decision support, and adoption strategies.
