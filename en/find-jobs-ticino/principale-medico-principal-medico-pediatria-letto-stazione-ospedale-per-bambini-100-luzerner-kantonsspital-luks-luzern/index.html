Role overview

The Children's Hospital Central Switzerland of the Lucerne Cantonal Hospital (LUKS) is one of the largest children's hospitals in Switzerland and serves the entire region of Central Switzerland at the highest level.

Our employees work around the clock to ensure that children, young people and their relatives are measurably better off.

LUKS, with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen, is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. >For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.
