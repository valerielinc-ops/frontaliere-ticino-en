Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Hohe Promenade Middle school teacher mbA for French 50 - 60% Our school The Cantonal School Hohe Promenade is a long-term high school with an ancient and modern language profile, a German / English immersion course and a strong MINT offering.
