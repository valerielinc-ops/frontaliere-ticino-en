The assessors are non-professional judges who have decision-making authority in judicial matters (art. 4 LJ). An assessor is convened according to the needs of the authority concerned. He/she receives session allowances in accordance with art. 79a RJ. The assessors are elected by the Grand Council, upon notice from the Judicial Council which puts out a competition for judicial functions for the Grand Council (art. 12 LJ). The assessors of the industrial tribunals are chosen equally from within employers' organizations and within workers' organizations (art. 55 LJ).
