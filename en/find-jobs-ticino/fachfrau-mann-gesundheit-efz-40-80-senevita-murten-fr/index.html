Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. For the Senevita Résidence Beaulieu in Murten, we are looking for a mindful and team-oriented person as soon as possible. Professional Health EFZ 40-80 % Your tasks

  • Assuming daily or shift responsibility

  • You are committed and motivated in the nursing profession and are happy to pass on your knowledge to colleagues and learners

  • Ideally, you already have knowledge of RAI/RUG and CareCoach
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
