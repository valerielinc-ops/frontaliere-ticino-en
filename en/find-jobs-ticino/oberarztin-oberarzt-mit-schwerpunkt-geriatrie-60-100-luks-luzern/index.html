Role overview

The acute care department at the Lucerne site has 21 beds and provides interdisciplinary care for both surgical and internist patients.

The overarching goal is to maintain and restore independence in everyday life and the quality of life.

In addition to the treatment of the underlying disease, geriatric syndromes such as gait and balance disorder, cognition, polypharmacy, sensory deficits, swallowing disorder or malnutrition are clarified and treated.

Main responsibilities
