Discover the cooperative area as a truck driver at the cooperative Migros Aare!

Explore our beautiful region and help ensure that our products arrive safely and on time in the stores.

This activity offers an ideal opportunity to pursue an enriching part-time job in addition to study, family or other commitments.

  • Discover the cooperative area as a truck driver at the cooperative Migros Aare!
  • Explore our beautiful region and help ensure that our products arrive safely and on time in the stores.

  • You supply the sales outlets in the cantons of Aargau, Bern and Solothurn and ensure punctual deliveries
