Original description

Michael Kors is always interested in hearing from talented and globally oriented individuals with a passion for fashion. If you enjoy working in a creative and fast-paced environment, then we would like to hear from you! ### Main Objective of the Job To achieve sales targets and develop lasting customer relationships. These initiatives are achieved by providing the highest level of customer service and adhering to all company policies, procedures, and directives. ## Responsibilities The responsibilities of this role are included in 3 areas:

  • Sales Generation
  • Achieve sales targets
  • Utilize high levels of sales and service to maximize sales performance
  • Demonstrate a thorough knowledge of the merchandise
  • After closing a sale, monitor all details, including: shipping, alterations (if applicable), and special requests to ensure customer satisfaction
  • Adhere to all sales policies and procedures
  • Maintain a keen interest in the fashion industry and market trends
  • Customer Service
  • Provide the highest level of customer service
  • Build and maintain repeat clientele; use the customer book
  • Resolve all customer issues and complaints quickly and effectively, ensuring customer satisfaction
  • Operations
  • Keep the sales floor and merchandise well-stocked and organized
  • Assist in maintaining all inventories in the warehouse and on the sales floor
  • Assist in all areas of stock, shipping, receiving protocol/policy, and all related shipping/receiving documents
  • Participate in inventory counts
  • Adhere to all point-of-sale policies and procedures
  • Perform all cash register functions correctly
  • Adhere to the work schedule, including hours and attendance
  • Participate in all relevant training seminars, programs, and meetings, as directly indicated by the location management ## Requirements
  • Retail and luxury sector experience
  • Ability to work as a team member
