Description

For the Center for Neuropsychiatry in Brugg/Windisch we are looking for 1.

October 2026 or by arrangement one or Tasks

  • They offer individual and group therapies for people with intellectual developmental disabilities between the ages of 18 and 65
  • You implement the ergotherapeutic interventions within the treatment concept
  • You support animal-assisted therapy at holiday representatives (with option to take over the animal- and nature-related therapies in two years)
  • You are part of the inpatient, interdisciplinary treatment team and actively participate in the implementation of therapeutic goals
  • They support the interdisciplinary team in diagnosis by using their assessment instruments Requirements
  • You have completed studies or training in occupational therapy (BSc, FH, HF), preferably with experience in practical work in neuropsychiatry, old age psychiatry or geriatrics
  • You preferably bring a corresponding further development in animal-assisted therapy as well as experience in handling animals
  • They are characterized by high flexibility, resilience, personal responsibility and teamwork in an interdisciplinary context.
  • They are interested in deepening guidelines-based work in the field of developmental and neuropsychiatry We offer: Our employees are the heart of our company.
