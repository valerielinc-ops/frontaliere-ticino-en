Role overview

Tasks

  • Medical responsibility: Independent management of the angiological consultation and care of our inpatients with the entire spectrum of arterial and venous vascular diseases. If interested, it is possible to participate in minimally invasive varicose interventions and, if necessary, in the course of independent implementation.
  • Management tasks: Technical and disciplinary support as well as motivation of the assistant doctors. You are an important reference for the training and support of the team.
  • Continuing education: Engaged in the further education of our assistant doctors (ideally you have the FA Angiology or are in advanced training for FA Angiology).
  • Development: Active participation in quality management, development of clinic-internal treatment standards. Possibility for phlebological training.
  • Interdisciplinarity: Close cooperation with colleagues in vascular surgery, radiology, neurology and diabetology within the framework of the certified vascular center.

Requirements

  • Specialist title: You are a specialist in angiology (or are in advanced training for FA Angiology (FMH)) or have an equivalent foreign qualification.
  • Experience: Ideally, you have several years of well-founded clinical and interventional experience.
  • Competence: You master the entire spectrum of non-invasive vascular diagnostics (color duplex sonography).
  • Personality: You are a communicative and team-oriented personality with a positive and appreciative attitude. Your own initiative and pleasure in the further development of the department distinguish you.
  • Languages: You have very good knowledge of German (at least C1 level) to ensure smooth communication with patients and team.
