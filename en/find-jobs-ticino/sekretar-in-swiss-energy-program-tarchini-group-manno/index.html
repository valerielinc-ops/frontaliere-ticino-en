Role overview

GIG Europe SA, a company of the Tarchini Group that is involved in the planning, development and management of “green” energy plants, is now looking for a bilingual secretary in German-Italian as part of the expansion of its activities at national level in the field of energy storage through the implementation of BESS (Swiss Energy Program) plants.
