Original description

Join our team as an IT Process Specialist, where you'll play a key role in shaping and improving the business processes that drive our organization. Leveraging your SAP expertise, you'll collaborate with cross-functional teams to analyze, standardize, and continuously enhance end-to-end processes, helping create efficient, scalable solutions that support business growth. This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys solving complex challenges, driving operational excellence, and making a lasting impact through technology and process improvement.

Aufgaben

  • Supporting processes and applications (focus in SAP) in North America.
  • Supporting implementation and training for SAP modules and related applications.
  • Supporting rollouts of SAP in North America as part of the international SAP Team.
  • Developing relationships and works closely with relevant stakeholders both business and IT to evaluate and interpret business requirements.
  • Represent/lead discussions related to process and application.
  • Participates in testing of new functionality as well as regression testing for new rollouts and new functionality.
  • Coordinates and conducts implementation tasks.
  • Support super users with daily problems, also across different departments.
  • Close collaboration with regional process mangers.
  • Regional process and data flow improvements.

Anforderungen

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science/Engineering or equivalent.
  • Minimum 3 years' experience.
  • Very good knowledge of SAP (SD, FI, CO)
  • Project Lead experience is a plus.
  • Excellent communication skills.
  • High motivation and high-quality standards.
  • Project Management expertise is a plus.
  • Strong knowledge in integrated processes.
  • Has an understanding of how to proceed systematically/ methodically.
  • Fluency in Spanish or Portuguese is considered an advantage.
