Role overview

Are you passionate about designing efficient, scalable business processes and digital backbones for a global service organization? In this role, you will shape and
improve the end-to-end processes and supporting systems that enable workshop refurbishments, field service, and parts delivery.
You will connect service execution processes with commercial processes, from quotation and order handling through planning, execution, invoicing, and performance follow-up. Working closely with global business stakeholders and IT teams, you will translate operational needs into standardized processes, clear requirements, and prioritized digital improvements.
Join Bühler and help build a connected, data-driven, and customer-focused global Service organization, supporting our mission: “Partnering for better outcomes.”
