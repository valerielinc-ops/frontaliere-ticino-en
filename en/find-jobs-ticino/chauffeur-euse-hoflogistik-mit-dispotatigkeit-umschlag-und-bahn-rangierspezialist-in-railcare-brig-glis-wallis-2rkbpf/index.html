Combined transport from one source. With us, you combine driving (40) with coordination and planning (60) – and we train you in the railway sector!

Tasks Coordination (approx. 60%)

  • Central contact for control centre, transport disposition, grid management and locomotive personnel
  • Monitoring of processes and equipment with rapid, solution-oriented intervention in case of disturbances
  • Active participation in new hub and shunting concepts as well as continuous process optimization Operational day-to-day business (approx.

40)

  • Handling of swap containers with vehicles between lorries and railway wagons (horizontal handling)
  • Loading, unloading, provisioning and relinquishing of swap containers
  • On-time collection and delivery according to customer specifications
  • Effective assistance in general handling operations on the premises

Requirements

  • CH-Guide pass Cat. C or C/E as well as mandatory readiness for further training as a shunter A40 and car examiner
  • flexibility for shiftwork (Monday to Saturday incl. holidays) and good physical condition
  • Compulsory very good German knowledge in word and writing
  • Extensive organizational talent, networked thinking as well as high reliability and customer orientation
  • Routinized handling of modern communication means and openness as well as willingness to learn for digital systems
