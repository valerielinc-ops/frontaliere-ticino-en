Marketing Automation Specialist

Transgourmet Schweiz AG — Moosseedorf, Bern, Svizzera

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional consulting and innovative services.Transgourmet Schweiz AG belongs to the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You develop, plan and realize marketing measures and campaigns in our marketing automation platform (BSI)
  • You lead projects for the further development of our marketing automation solution and successfully implement new ideas
  • You work closely with internal stakeholders from sales, IT and communication as well as external agencies and coordinate the cooperation
  • You analyze customer behavior, customer data and the performance of our campaigns, derive optimization measures and continuously develop our processes
  • You advise and support our digital marketing team in the implementation of marketing measures, for example in newsletter or WhatsApp marketing

Requirements

  • You have completed studies in business administration, marketing or a comparable education and/or have professional experience in this environment
  • Ideally, you have experience in digital marketing and project management
  • You are interested in marketing automation and campaign management
  • First practical experience is a plus
  • You have a basic technical understanding of web and email technologies (HTML, CSS)
  • You convince with your organizational talent, your own initiative and your safe presence
  • You communicate fluently in German
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage
  • You work independently, pragmatically and solution-oriented and quickly find yourself in new topics
  • You think analytically and networked and move safely in exchange with different stakeholders

Your contact person

Lisa Röthlisberger HR Marketing
