The first integrated care network in Switzerland, the Arc Network is a key player in coordinated and community-based care in the somatic and mental health fields. With close to 1,300 employees, he is based on 2 hospital sites in Moutier and Saint-Imier, as well as a network of Medicentres, ambulance, EMS, radiology, a physical and cognitive therapy centre and a home hospital service. The Arc Network covers the medical-hospital needs of a population of nearly 80,000 and is characterised by its dynamism, excellent infrastructure and a constant concern to develop services of proximity and quality.
