Original description

The lettering of the S-Bahn vehicles radiates with the evening sun for the bet. That is your merit! During the last vehicle revision, your colleagues refreshed the mechanical and electrical parts while you took care of the professional relabeling. Thus, the vehicle not only works reliably, but also appears in a sympathetically new shine.

This is waiting for you

  • Create foil prints, fonts and pictograms on the modern plotter
  • Apply slides millimeter-accurate on BLS and third-party vehicles – your precise craft travels through the entire network
  • Living out creativity in the modernly furnished writing studio – here your ideas get space and effect
  • In direct exchange with internal and external customers, turn smart ideas into visible solutions
  • Shaping the field of labeling and bringing new fields of application to rail with innovative ideas

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training in advertising technology, polygraphy, writing, graphics or equivalent profession
  • Safe handling of common graphics programs and technology for precise work
  • Several years of experience in advertising technology, inscription or film processing
  • Strong customer orientation and enjoyment of direct exchange
  • Independence, a high degree of conscientiousness and openness to work in other workshop areas

About the BLS

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? The pride of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.
