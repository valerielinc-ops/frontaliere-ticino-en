Role overview

The Office for Water and Energy (AWE) works for safe and intact waters, for protection against floods and natural hazards, for the protection and sustainable use of rivers, streams, lakes and groundwater, and for the implementation and further development of energy and climate policy.

In order to achieve the energy and climate goals, the Canton of St.Gallen intends to consistently promote the implementation of the energy transition.
