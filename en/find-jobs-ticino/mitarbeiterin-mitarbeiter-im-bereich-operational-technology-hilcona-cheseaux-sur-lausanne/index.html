Main responsibilities

  • Integration and maintenance of systems based on Siemens CPUs
  • Participation in the support and life cycle of existing and new facilities
  • Operation and support of OT/IoT systems on site, including configuration, commissioning, maintenance, level 1/2 support (error correction, diagnosis, remote maintenance), monitoring and tracking of assets, management of backups and contingency plans, and system change and update management.

  • Technical training (EFZ, HF or equivalent) in the fields of automation, electrical engineering, industrial informatics or similar specialisations
