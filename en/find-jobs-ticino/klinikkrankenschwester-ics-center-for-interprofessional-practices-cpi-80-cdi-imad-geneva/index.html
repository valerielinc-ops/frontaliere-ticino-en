Original description

Introduced in Switzerland since 2009, advanced nursing practice continues to develop. The amendment of the Health Act, which came into force in October 2020 in Geneva, now allows for advanced practices under certain conditions. As an institution for health care, care and home care, IMAD is strongly committed to strengthening continuity of care, the efficiency of care and to supporting the development of staff skills; two central axes of the institutional strategy. To do this, IMAD is looking for a specialized clinician nurse (ICS) with a home health focus to strengthen its team within the Interprofessional Practice Centre. (CPI, under the direction of operations), which will contribute to the implementation of advanced nursing practice in the institution. Your missions and responsibilities As ICLS, you will play a key role in transforming home care by contributing to continuous improvement in the quality and safety of care. Your mission will be part of a collaborative, patient-centred approach as a full partner.

Your main responsibilities

  • Support the institutional mission to maintain home in a supportive, safe and patient-friendly environment.
  • Initiate, coordinate and support clinical interventions related to the function of ICLS with care teams and as appropriate to patient needs.
  • Assess complex care situations and respond with evidence.
  • Participate in interdisciplinary clinical decisions in the transition, in collaboration with care settings and partners, to develop individualized care pathways integrating clinical assessment, therapeutic education and professional support etc.
  • Actively contribute to the implementation phase of advanced nursing practice, by being a proposal force or any clinical implementation project.
  • Support the quality of clinical documentation.

Specific areas of intervention

  • Management of clinical projects , particularly related to the implementation of new professional practices.
  • Direct and indirect intervention in complex or fragile clinical situations, in support of transition teams or specialized colleagues.
  • Transformational leadership, for example: by facilitating communities of practice by clinical areas, responding to the specific needs of populations monitored at home.
  • Participation in clinical research, linked to evidence and aimed at continuous improvement of clinical competence.
  • Master of Science in Nursing Sciences (MScSI) ICS
  • An experience of at least 5 years as a nurse and at least 5 years as ICLS
  • Experience in home care or community health is an asset
  • Ability to work in interprofessionality within a multidisciplinary team
  • Good pedagogical capacity, flexibility and proactivity
  • Ability to position itself in line with the institutional line
  • Engaged, motivated with excellent relationship
  • Good knowledge of the Geneva care network.
