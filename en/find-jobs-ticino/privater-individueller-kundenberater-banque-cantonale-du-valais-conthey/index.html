Private/Individual Client Advisor

  • Conthey
  • 80-100%

Are you benefiting from commercial training and have an experience in the banking world? If you are looking for an opportunity to value your skills, this job is for you.

Specifically?

  • You will support clients in their requests for funding, investments and heritage planning.
  • You will acquire in an entrepreneurial spirit new customers.
  • You will establish and maintain a relationship of trust with clients.
  • You will proactively monitor customers following an offer/information request or personal contact.
  • You will train on an ongoing basis on changes to internal and external directives.
  • You will ensure the control of risks and compliance with legal provisions.

The challenge is tempting you? This post is for you if...

  • You have commercial and/or financial training.
  • You have good sales and advisory capacity on all investment, financing and heritage consulting products and services.
  • Dynamic and pragmatic, you benefit from a strong sense of initiative and contribute to the success of your team.
  • You have good analytical skills and are able to act quickly in unforeseen situations.
  • Your knowledge of English and/or German are real assets.

Would you like to join us? Let's meet! We look forward to discovering your application. To do this, send your complete file to Marisa Berclaz, our HR Business Partner, at: recruitment@bcvs.ch .
