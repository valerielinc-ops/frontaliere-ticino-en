Role overview

The Global Supply Chain Operations Organization centrally manages supply, inventory, and replenishment planning for drug products, connecting markets directly to the global manufacturing network. Within this end-to-end framework, the Customer Logistics Manager is responsible for trading and delivering semi-finished (SEMP) and finished products (FG) to third-party customer markets worldwide. This role ensures high service levels, right-first-time order fulfillment, and full compliance for product and document supply.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure global market supply to third party customers on time in full at the right quality and cost.
  • Partner with different interfaces for upstream replenishment coordination from central packaging
  • Continuously improve the cost-to-serve-model (incl. KPI metrics).
  • Leverage data for decision making by using e.g. analytics, simulation tools and customer insights
  • Drive efficiency through identifying value-adding and productivity-enhancing opportunities through technology and data.
  • Collaborate with different functions in the markets to understand the changes which may impact supply to the country and align with planning functions to adjust the Replenishment Plan.
  • Ensure appropriate supply chain set-up (incl. e.g. master data).

Requirements

  • Education: Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Business, or a related field (or equivalent specialized training).
  • Minimum 5 years of professional experience in Supply Chain Management and Operations within the pharmaceutical industry.
  • SAP, Microsoft Office, and familiar with AI-driven supply chain tools/analytics.
  • Professional fluency in English (written and spoken); German is a plus.
