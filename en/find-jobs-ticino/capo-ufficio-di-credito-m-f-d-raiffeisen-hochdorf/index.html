For Raiffeisenbank Oberseetal, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Hochdorf, Eschenbach and Inwil, we are looking for a personality as Head of Credit Office (m/f/d) on the basis of a reorganisation.

Main responsibilities

  • Professional and operational management of credit processing

Application process

  • Independent analysis and risk- and solution-oriented assessment of loan applications (private and small and medium-sized corporate customers, real estate or corporate financing)
  • Approval of credit applications in-house or preparation of the basis for the decision and recommendation to the competent authority
