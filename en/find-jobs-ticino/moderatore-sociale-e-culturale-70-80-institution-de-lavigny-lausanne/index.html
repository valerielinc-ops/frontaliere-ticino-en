The Département Plein Soleil de l'Institution de Lavigny, located in Lausanne, is dedicated to people with progressive neurological diseases or brain damage, and includes a 70-bed EMS, a 10-seat CAT, 12 sheltered accommodation and an outpatient therapy centre.

It is a unique place of life and care in the Canton of Vaud, which offers personalized accompaniment, with care services and therapies, educational and socio-cultural activities, workshops for personal development and well-being, spiritual support and a service dedicated to the relatives of the people welcomed.
