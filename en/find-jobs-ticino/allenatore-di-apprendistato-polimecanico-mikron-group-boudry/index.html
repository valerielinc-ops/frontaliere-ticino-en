Role overview

As a polymechanic apprentice trainer, you play a key role in the development of tomorrow's talents by accompanying them throughout their journey.

Through a committed and concrete pedagogy, you contribute to their professional success while ensuring learning aligned with the technical requirements and quality standards of our industry.

Main responsibilities

  • Key responsibilities
  • Give CIE courses (Manual, conventional, CNC, metrology, assembly)
  • Bringing apprentices to success through motivating pedagogy and valuing their work
  • Forward the information to management in the form of a cockpit (cost tracking, status of commitments, production of apprentices via SAP)
  • Manage investment requests (machines, accessories) and purchases.
  • Plan and organize the weekly workload for each apprentice (MAP, Use of machines, follow-up of manufactured parts) and sign the work logs and absences of apprentices.
  • Conflict management, applying internal rules in case of non-compliance with company values, organizing sessions with parents if necessary
  • Collaborate with training partners (COMEC, Swissmem, CPNE, schools)
  • Participate in biannual exchanges with local training centres
  • Organize open doors, internships for future apprentices, educational and recreational events
