Why do we need you?

As a Nurse in the Pool, you are involved in supporting the medical and surgical units according to the needs of the institution. This role, which requires adaptability and commitment, gives the opportunity to capitalize on varied nursing expertise in a dynamic environment, and offers a stimulating professional practice on a daily basis.

What will you have to do?

  • Perform technical and specific nursing care in accordance with existing protocols
  • Actively collaborate with the multidisciplinary team to ensure individual and quality management
  • Ensure strict traceability of care in the computerized patient record
  • Strictly apply hygiene, aseptic and safety rules
  • Integrate quickly the teams of services in which you intervene to ensure continuity of care with professionalism

Who are you going to work with?

The team consists of 10 collaborators. Their strength lies in being a team united with a sense of common values. Working in different units, they always enjoy meeting each other during breaks.

Audrey Laurent, nurse-cheffe of care unit and manager of the Pool, is strong from a course recognized in various surgical specialties. His leadership based on listening, benevolence and availability guarantees a respectful working environment, where each employee can flourish fully. Your well-being is at the heart of his concerns.

What do we expect from you?

The indispensables

  • Diploma of Nurse or equivalent recognized by the Swiss Red Cross
  • Minimum 3 years of professional experience, ideally in hospital
  • Strong general care skills, both in medicine and surgery
  • Excellent adaptability, autonomy and responsibility
  • Rigor, organization and effective stress management in a dynamic environment

Your assets

  • Experience confirmed in working in multidisciplinary teams
  • Excellent relationship and communication skills with patients and colleagues
  • Flexibility and flexibility for rapid integration within different services
