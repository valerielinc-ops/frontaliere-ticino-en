Original description

Description of your tasks Flexibility and enjoyment of variety are central points. Because that’s exactly what working in acute psychiatry means. If this applies to you, here are some of the most important tasks:

  • The individual care and support of patients at night
  • Conducting crisis discussions and reports
  • Arranging and planning the treatment with the responsible doctors and therapists
  • For better integration into the team and ensuring the processes, it may be that part of the introduction is planned during the day shift
  • Requirements
  • Your profile Professional competence, service orientation and a lot of humor are the most important qualities that you must have. With these characteristics we achieve the best treatment results for our patients and the team looks forward to having you become a part of them. If you have experience in nursing but not in the psychiatric environment, it is still possible to get started. What is then crucial is that you have a lot of motivation and commitment to acquire the specific skills.

  • Innovative employer with strong interprofessional cooperation
