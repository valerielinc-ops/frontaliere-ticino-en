Role overview

Your key responsibilities

  • Your expertise in the preparation and presentation of sushi and sashimi in the renowned Matsuhisa style, in full compliance with our quality standards, sets you apart.

  • You create new sushi rolls and dishes, continuously enhancing and evolving our menu.

  • You advise and engage with our guests at the specially designed sushi counter within our open show kitchen.

  • Ensuring cleanliness and hygiene in the kitchen in accordance with HACCP standards is one of your key responsibilities.


• What you bring to the job

  • Completed professional training as a Sushi Chef (certificate required).

  • At least 8 years of professional experience as a Sushi Chef (references required), including a minimum of 1 year’s experience in one of the NOBU / Matsuhisa restaurant operations.

  • Resilience and flexibility to thrive in a dynamic environment.

  • Excellent teamwork and communication skills.

  • A high level of attention to detail and precision in the preparation of dishes.


Our offer

  • Fixed-term seasonal position from the end of November 2026 until the end of March 2027.

  • Five-day working week on average, based on 43.5 hours.

  • Five weeks of holiday and six public holidays per year.

  • 13th month salary from the start date (pro rata temporis).

  • Accommodation in one of our attractive staff residences upon request.

  • Dining options in our new, modern staff restaurant.

If you value an interesting, demanding and varied role within a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-coordinated team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
