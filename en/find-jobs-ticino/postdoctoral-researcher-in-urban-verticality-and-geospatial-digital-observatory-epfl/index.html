Role overview

EPFL, the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Lausanne, is one of Europe’s most dynamic university campuses and ranks among the world’s top universities.

With more than 6,500 employees, EPFL advances its core missions of education, research and innovation within an international community of over 18,500 people, including more than 14,000 students and 4,000 researchers from over 120 countries.

Within this stimulating and international research environment, the Communauté d’études pour l’aménagement du territoire (CEAT) at EPFL is recruiting a postdoctoral researcher in urban verticality, Earth Observation, environmental modelling and geospatial data science.

Main responsibilities

  • and responsibilities WP1
  • Environmental indicators and remote sensing
  • Develop and harmonise Earth Observation workflows using Sentinel-1/2, Landsat, MODIS, GHSL, Open Buildings 2.5D, OpenStreetMap, Copernicus and other available datasets to characterise urban morphology, verticalisation and land-surface change, including the reconstruction of temporal dynamics through multi-temporal imagery and change-detection approaches.
  • Derive and analyse urban and environmental indicators related to verticalisation, including building height and density, built volume, vegetation and NDVI, imperviousness, albedo, Land Surface Temperature, climatic comfort, terrain and drainage characteristics, and flood hazard/exposure.
  • Assess the environmental impacts of verticalisation, particularly urban heat and flood risk, by combining Earth Observation, meteorological, topographic, hydrological and land-cover data, and applying approaches such as the InVEST Urban Cooling Model and relevant flood-analysis methods.
  • Coordinate field measurements and model validation along selected urban transects, including microclimate sensors, thermal observations, photogrammetric surveys and field validation, while establishing protocols for sensor calibration, spatial sampling, quality control and integration with Earth Observation data.
  • Develop spatial, statistical and predictive models to investigate relationships between vertical urban form and environmental conditions, assess uncertainty and transferability across the three cities, and develop baseline-to-2030 and 2030–2050 scenarios of vertical development and associated environmental impacts.
  • Contribute to short-term (2026 to 2030) and long-term (2030 to 2050) verticalisation forecasting models based on machine learning, and to their validation against official building height layers. WP4
  • Coordination of the digital observatory of urban verticalisation (DOUV)
  • Coordinate the scientific and geospatial development of the DOUV, integrating data, indicators and models generated across VERTICAF.
