Senior Ecotoxicologist Role Overview

At Huntsman, we are currently looking for a Senior Ecotoxicologist to support our Global EHS Division.

In this role, you will provide expert advice across Huntsman businesses by applying your professional judgement in ecotoxicology, exposure science and regulatory compliance.
