Role overview

Introduction For our Information Systems division , we are looking for a Business Analyst (H/F) whose main mission will be to design applications and maintain processes related to Purchasing activities.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure the operational link between the trade and Build teams
  • Contribute to the transformation program to implement management processes and tools between Rolex and its suppliers through an SRM portal (Ivalua)
  • Monitor business needs and requirements (functional specifications), in collaboration with Business Process Owners
  • Participate in the implementation of the solution, including testing and business support phases
  • Ensure compliance of established processes, including interfaces between Ivalua and several SAP instances
  • Coordinate actions with SI teams
  • Ensure that the standard good practices of the solution are respected
  • Develop and maintain applications
  • Dealing with incidents
  • Analyze, encrypt and plan evolution requests related to Purchasing Profile
  • University training in management computing, or equivalent training
  • Technical/functional experience in MRS (Ivalua) and/or ERP (SAP)
  • Ability to reformulate users' computer functionality requirements
  • Methodology and good knowledge of session and communication facilitation tools
  • Rigor and good analytical capacity
  • Meaning of communication and ability to work in a team
  • Good relational qualities and adaptation
  • Initiative
  • Respect for confidentiality Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
