About Arxada

Arxada is a leading global company for innovative solutions that protect our world. Our pioneering technologies, comprehensive regulatory know-how, production and process development skills help our customers to efficiently secure nutrition, health and infrastructure through chemistry and biotechnology while promoting sustainability.

We offer a wide range of ingredients and services for various end markets, including Human Health & Nutrition, Home & Personal Care, Professional Hygiene, Paints & Coatings and Wood Protection.

With customers in over 100 countries, the company generated sales of CHF 2 billion in 2024. Its headquarters are in Basel, Switzerland, and Arxada employs 3,200 people at 24 production sites and 14 R&D centers – all committed to the success of our customers.

The role

As a member of the Quality Control (QC) at the Visp site, you are a central component of our production and quality processes. In this role, you ensure that our products meet the highest quality and safety standards through precise in-process and release analytics. You work closely with production and interfaces and make an important contribution to the continuous improvement of our processes.

This position gives you the opportunity to contribute your expertise, take responsibility and actively contribute to the sustainable development of Arxada.
