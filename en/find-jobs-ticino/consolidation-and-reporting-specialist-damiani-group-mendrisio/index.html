Role overview

The resource will be part of the Consolidated team to carry the function manager directly.

It will support the activities relating to the drafting of the consolidated financial statements and will actively collaborate with the companies of the group and external consultants in order to ensure the monthly and quarterly closures of the Group in the expected time.

It will ensure alignment with the HQ Management Control function and will work closely with the Group Treasures team.
