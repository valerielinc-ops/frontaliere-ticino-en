Original description

SFS is a supplier of screws and tools, fittings, construction and civil engineering products, special parts and chemical-technical products throughout Switzerland. We offer our customers professional advice both online, by visits as well as in our local sales subsidiaries. Many external service employees deal with clients with recommendations and proposals for competent solutions. This includes an assortment suitable for the market. In the metal construction sector, we are the competent and reliable delivery partner for our customers. For the development of the canton of Geneva, we are looking for an experienced sales advisor in the external service.
