Role overview

Laboratory technician — MKS PAMP SA, Castel San Pietro (TI).

MISSION The resource will become part of the Analytical Laboratory by focusing on spectrometers by contributing its expertise to ensure reliable and high quality analytical results.

Through the use of advanced and cutting-edge laboratory tools, it will support the control of materials and production processes, transforming analytical data into fundamental information for quality and innovation.

  • Laboratory technician — MKS PAMP SA, Castel San Pietro (TI).
  • MISSION The resource will become part of the Analytical Laboratory by focusing on spectrometers by contributing its expertise to ensure reliable and high quality analytical results.
  • MAIN ACTIVITIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Prepare liquid samples in compliance with ISO 5667 , ensuring the correct execution of the preliminary phases for analysis;
  • Prepare metal samples according to ISO 11596 , ensuring the correct preparation of materials for subsequent instrumental analysis;

Main responsibilities

  • MAIN ACTIVITIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Prepare liquid samples in compliance with ISO 5667 , ensuring the correct execution of the preliminary phases for analysis;
  • Prepare metal samples according to ISO 11596 , ensuring the correct preparation of materials for subsequent instrumental analysis;
  • Prepare and manage laboratory reagents, ensuring quality, traceability and availability in accordance with operational procedures;
  • Perform chemical analysis by advanced spectrometric instrumentation, including Optical Emission Spectrometry (OES), SPARK, ICP, IC and ED/WD-XRF, ensuring accuracy and reliability of results;
  • Perform elementary analysis for the determination of Carbon/Zolph (C-S) and Oxygen/hydrogen (O-H);
  • Conduct gravimetric, volumetric and potentiometric analyses by applying consolidated analytical methods and in compliance with quality standards;
  • Interpreting and communicating analytical results, supporting verifications of conformity of materials and production processes;
  • Record, manage and track analytics through the LabWare LIMS system, ensuring completeness and integrity of information;
  • To contribute to the maintenance of the high quality standards of the laboratory, operating in compliance with the procedures, the Good Laboratory Practices (GLP) and the quality system.
