Original description

Do you want to actively shape the safe use of artificial intelligence in public administration? Are you excited about modern security architectures and cloud security? Then you are exactly right with us. In this role, you will assume a key position in the development and development of our Security Architecture. Your role

  • Responsibility for the definition and further development of our security architecture standards and reference architectures
  • Conducting security reviews and architecture assessments for projects, platforms and services
  • Build and develop safety standards for AI platforms, generative AI, co-pilots and agent systems
  • AI for proactive cyber defense: using modern AI models to detect and assess external vulnerabilities, simulating AI-enabled attack scenarios and supporting the rapid resolution of identified vulnerabilities
  • Development of architecture specifications in the areas of data security, data classification and data loss prevention (DLP)
  • Advising architecture, development and platform teams on security-related issues
  • Implementation of threat modelling, risk analysis and security assessments for new technologies and solutions
  • Technical and conceptual management of security initiatives and architecture projects

Your profile

  • Several years of experience as a security architect, security engineer or in a comparable role
  • Computer science degree or equivalent qualification
  • In-depth knowledge of security architecture as well as AI security required, as well as risk analysis and threat modelling
  • Several years of experience in creating security architectures and technical concepts
  • Know-how for the design of security architectures of on-prem LLMs and cloud LLMs
  • Recognized certifications such as CISSP, SABSA, CCSP, TOGAF, AZ-500 or similar certificates
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Structured, independent and communicative personality with high consulting and implementation competence

Your Benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% pension
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or temporal training
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees
