Role overview

The Office for Spatial Development Graubünden (ARE) is shaping the future of our unique Alpine habitat.

With around 35 dedicated employees, we are committed to using soil responsibly as a scarce resource and to reconciling the diverse demands on our living space in a balanced way.

  • The Office for Spatial Development Graubünden (ARE) is shaping the future of our unique Alpine habitat.
  • With around 35 dedicated employees, we are committed to using soil responsibly as a scarce resource and to reconciling the diverse demands on our living space in a balanced way.
  • First contact person for municipalities and their planning offices in questions of spatial development, use planning and construction outside the construction zones (BAB).
  • Examination of municipal use plans and development of corresponding decisions at the disposal of the licensing authority.

Main responsibilities

  • First contact person for municipalities and their planning offices in questions of spatial development, use planning and construction outside the construction zones (BAB).
  • Examination of municipal use plans and development of corresponding decisions at the disposal of the licensing authority.

Application process

  • Our tasks range from the implementation of the government’s spatial planning guidelines and the support of local and regional planning to the examination of building applications outside the construction zones.
  • The focus is on transparency, expertise and the protection of our natural livelihoods.
  • In order to strengthen our team in the usage planning and BAB department, we are looking for a room planner as a district planner (80 – 100%) by agreement.
  • Management of AAB procedures and examination of AAB applications and drafting of AAB permits.
  • Writing consultations on other spatially relevant projects.
  • Accompanying processes of particular spatial importance at municipal level. Requirements
  • Completed Bachelor's or Master's degree (or equivalent) in spatial planning, architecture or a related professional field, ideally combined with several years of professional experience.
  • Independent, structured as well as customer- and solution-oriented working method.
