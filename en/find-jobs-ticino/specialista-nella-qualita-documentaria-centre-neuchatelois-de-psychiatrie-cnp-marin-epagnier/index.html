Original description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. Specialist in documentary quality CDI - 60%

The documentary quality specialist contributes, in conjunction with her head, to the development and continuous improvement of the institutional documentary system. It supports teams in the creation, updating and dissemination of clear, structured and easily accessible documentation to support professional practices, quality of services and organizational efficiency. In cooperation with the various stakeholders, it ensures that documents are adapted to the needs of the field and promotes their appropriation by the users.

As part of your function, you will be required to

  • Draft, structure and update institutional documents in collaboration with relevant units.
  • Support teams in the production of clear, structured, accessible and tailored documentation to the needs of professional practices.
  • Ensure the quality, consistency and consistency of documents prior to their validation and publication.
  • Coordinate the validation process and ensure the publication of documents in institutional information systems.
  • Ensure the reliability, timeliness, accessibility and consistency of the documentary corpus.
  • Organize and coordinate periodic literature reviews to ensure relevance.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of the documentary system by proposing simplification, harmonisation and optimization measures.
  • Monitoring and analysing documentary quality using indicators and integrating user feedback into the evolution of documentation.
  • Promote good documentary practices and support teams in their ownership.
  • Monitor access rights to the institutional tree and contribute to the continuity of the activities of the Institutional Steering Office.

The profile we are looking for

  • Bachelor or equivalent training in a field related to quality, documentary management, information management or organisation.
  • Additional training in quality systems, audit, continuous improvement or process management is an asset.
  • Experience confirmed from 3 to 5 years in a similar role.
  • Experience in setting standards, quality controls or continuous improvement approaches.
  • Experience in an environment requiring cross-cutting coordination, analytical capacity and application of institutional rules and standards.
  • Experience in drafting, structuring or revising institutional documents is an important asset.
  • An experience with a GED/ECM tool (M-Files or equivalent) is an asset.
  • Have a sense of observation and concern for verification and control.
  • To be able to identify and relate the elements of a situation, to group them together, by structuring them, into a concise, coherent and understandable set.

We offer you

  • A stimulating and collaborative environment, serving a public health mission, with working conditions governed by the CTC21, with attractive social benefits.
  • Flexible work organisation.
  • An exceptional historic site in nature, on the lakeside, close to public transport, the motorway and shopping centres, with good parking and food service.
