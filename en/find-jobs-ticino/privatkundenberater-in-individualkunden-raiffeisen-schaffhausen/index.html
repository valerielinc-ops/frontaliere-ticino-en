Role overview

For Raiffeisenbank Schaffhausen , a modern, successful and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Schaffhausen and a branch office in Neunkirch, we are looking for a committed, customer-oriented and sales-strong personality as a private customer consultant 80-100% as a result of retirement of the previous customer advisor as of 01.03.2027 or by arrangement for our headquarters in Schaffhausen.

Since a substantial part of the existing customer portfolio is Italian-speaking, we are particularly pleased about applicants with good to very good knowledge of Italian.
