Role overview

Location: Geneva, Switzerland Work Pattern Percentage: 80-100% Advise international clients on cutting edge business areas At Deloitte, our Technology Strategy & Transformation specialists help organizations develop the strategies and implement the systems that build business value and drive performance.

We have the in-depth knowledge and experience gained from working on some of the most complex IT challenges around the globe, everything from system replacement to full IT transformation.

By enriching us, you will gain valuable insights into IT Strategy and Operating Model design, IT Architecture, IT Business Management, IT Agility Advisory, and IT M&A - great to have you with us!

How you can make an impact

  • Deliver transformational projects for clients using your expertise in IT infrastructure, project management, and interpersonal skills
  • Apply your knowledge of infrastructure technologies with client needs to identify and create a solution to resolve their challenges
  • Use your knowledge and experience, along with our international network of experts, to deliver IT infrastructure engagements that meets our clients’ needs
  • Develop and manage lasting trust-based relationships with a diverse set of internal and external stakeholders to drive business success
  • Collaborate with colleagues to develop market offerings to enhance how we best meet our clients’ needs Who are we looking for?
  • A strong technical background in IT infrastructure consulting
  • Excellent analytical / critical thinking skills, and can effectively communicate technical concepts both verbally and in writing
  • Strong sense of ownership based around customer-centricity and interpersonal skills
  • A proactive and entrepreneurial mindset with an interest in infrastructure and related technology trends
  • Previous experience in a Big Four consultancy or equivalent environment
  • Fluent in English and French (written and verbal), and based in the Romandie region
  • Certifications in IT infrastructure technologies / vendors are considered an advantage Your team By joining the team in our Geneva and Zurich offices, you will be integrated into a diverse group of like-minded colleagues right from the start.

As part of the Technology Strategy & Transformation team, you will work directly with our clients to optimize Technology Organisations and shape the way IT interacts with the business.

Our commitment to your professional development translates into an extensive internal and external training programme.

Joining us also means benefiting from our in-depth understanding of technology, excellent communication skills and broad business understanding.

Look forward to working together with some of the largest organisations in Switzerland and across the world to introduce the change that will create real impact for their business.

Life at Deloitte At Deloitte, your ideas create impact and spark meaningful change for our clients, people and society.
