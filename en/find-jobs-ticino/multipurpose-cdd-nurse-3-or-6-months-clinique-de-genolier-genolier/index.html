Multipurpose CDD Nurse - 3 or 6 months •

  • Part-time
  • Department: Genolier Clinic
  • Participation rate: 90 %
  • Start date: 1 September 2026 Company description With strong expertise, the Genolier Clinic is distinguished by its experienced and qualified team and its state-of-the-art medical equipment. The Genolier Clinic offers a wide range of services covering cardiology, neurology, oncology, orthopaedic surgery and plastic surgery. In addition, the clinic provides its patients with a wide range of prevention and rehabilitation services to promote their health and well-being. The reputation of the Genolier Clinic is based on its excellent medical infrastructure and cutting-edge technologies. These include a state-of-the-art diagnostic centre, a high-performance intensive care unit and an operating room equipped with the latest generation instruments and devices to ensure optimal quality care. Description of post Multi-purpose CDD nurses may be employed in different services (medicine, surgery, emergencies, etc.) depending on the needs of the institution. Its missions are broadly the same as an incumbent nurse, but with a rapid adjustment dimension as it may be required to change service. Key missions include: 1. Patient Care 2. Monitoring and security 3. Organization and coordination 4. Relationship and support 5. Adaptability
  • Quickly integrate into a service that she does not always know.
  • Being able to adapt to different audiences
  • Respond to the establishment's needs (replacement of a colleague, reinforcement during peak activity). The multi-purpose CDD nurse performs all the care and coordination tasks of a state graduate nurse, but with the particularity of being flexible, responsive and able to adapt quickly to different services and environments. Qualifications
  • To benefit from a CRS nursing degree or training deemed equivalent
  • High professional awareness, motivation, flexibility and dynamism
  • Ability to integrate into a group and team spirit
  • Ability to manage stress
  • Knowledge of English is an asset Further information
  • An activity in an exciting field, within a dynamic, stimulating and expanding company
  • Experience in a pleasant working environment within a multidisciplinary team
  • Wage and motivating social conditions We pay great attention to the diversity of life paths of our collaborators. Our positions are open to both women and men.
