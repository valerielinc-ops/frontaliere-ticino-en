Role overview

The Role: Mission

As the Audit & CAPA Lead, you will ensure an effective, pragmatic, and risk-based Audit & CAPA framework at Debiopharm.

Your focus will be on strengthening quality oversight, supporting inspection readiness, and driving continuous improvement for all our clinical trials programs across all GxP activities, and GCP/GcLP in particular.
