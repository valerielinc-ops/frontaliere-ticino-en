Main responsibilities

  • Job Responsibilities
  • To strengthen our mode-of-action and disease biology characterization efforts in CNS biology/pharmacology, we are offering a Postdoc position for a highly motivated neurobiologist.
  • The successful candidate will have solid experience with hiPSC-derived neuronal cocultures, as well as basic knowledge of neuronal circuit modeling and analysis.
  • In this role you will investigate the effect of innovative therapeutic agents in neuronal co-culture models of disease using a broad array of readouts.
  • You will complement this with data generated in in vivo disease models. include :
  • Design, perform, analyze, and interpret experiments in iPSC-derived neuronal co-cultures to characterize innovative therapeutic agents for CNS drug discovery
  • Set up and optimize electrophysiology, omics, biochemical and immunocytochemistry assays of neuronal cocultures for mechanism-of-action studies
  • Introduce disease-causing mutations into iPSC cells (CRISPR) to generate and characterize co-culture-based disease models for above experiments
  • Design, perform, analyze, and interpret experiments in rodent CNS disease models
  • Set-up autism-relevant behavioral pharmacology assays

Application process

  • Follow novel developments in the field and evaluate application opportunities for CNS drug discovery
  • Interact with biologists, pharmacologists, chemists, and computer scientists to drive innovation and advance drug discovery projects Candidate’s Requirements
  • PhD degree in neuroscience or related areas
  • Proven track record of generating, optimizing, working with and analyzing hiPSC derived neuronal co-cultures
  • Hands-on experience in using multi-electrode arrays for recordings from hiPSC-derived neurons and pharmacological manipulations of the culture conditions
  • Practical knowledge of in-vitro and in-vivo models of autism is highly advantageous
  • Wet lab experience with current molecular biology tools (e.g. CRISPR) is advantageous
  • Hands-on experience with current techniques of rodent handling, substance application, tissue sampling, and behavioral pharmacology
