Original description

What you achieve with us

  • As a Night Concierge, you are the central contact person for our guests during the night and are competent and discreet at your side for all questions, concerns and wishes.

  • With expertise, commitment and a pronounced sense of service, you look after and advise our guests during the night hours and provide personal and professional care.

  • Internal and external guest wishes are professionally organized and reliably implemented by you.

  • Through individual recommendations on restaurants, activities, transfers and other offers, you create an exceptional guest experience even during the night.

  • Reservations in-house and with external partners coordinate efficiently and service-oriented.

  • They ensure that our guests experience an excellent and personalized service during their stay.

  • In close cooperation with the Night Team and all relevant departments, you ensure a smooth process and a professional handover to the day team.

  • The Loge division is your responsibility, as is the leadership and motivation of employees such as Voituriers, Doormen, Bellmen and Drivers.

  • You take feedback and concerns professionally and ensure timely and sustainable solutions – always in the sense of highest guest satisfaction.

  • Through your proactive communication, discretion and attentiveness, you contribute decisively to a pleasant and safe atmosphere during the night.


You with

  • A completed training as a hotel manager or at a hotel management school forms the basis of your professional career.

  • Several years of experience as a concierge, ideally as a night concierge in the luxury hotel industry, ensures your safe and professional appearance in guest contact.

  • You inspire with excellent communication skills, a sovereign and professional appearance as well as a pronounced customer orientation.

  • Very good language skills in German, English and Italian – oral and written – are a matter of course for you; Any other language is beneficial.

  • Comprehensive knowledge of all processes at the reception and in the guest service distinguish you.

  • With good computer user knowledge in MS Office and ideally experience with Protel, you work efficiently and structured.

  • Flexibility and willingness for regular night work as well as a high resilience characterize you.

  • With a winning appearance and secure communication you convince both on the phone and in a personal conversation.

  • Discretion towards guests and loyalty to the company are a matter of course for you.

  • Organizational talent, service orientation and a strong sense of situations and people make you a warm host and experienced service provider.


Our offer

  • Temporary season point from the end of November 2026 to the end of March 2027

  • 5 days week on average at 43.5 hours

  • 5 weeks holidays and 6 holidays per year

  • 13. Salary from admission (pro rata temporis)

  • On request accommodation in one of our beautiful staff houses

  • catering opportunities in our new, modern employee restaurant

  • Work in an attractive environment with development opportunities


If you appreciate an interesting, demanding and versatile job in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-functioning team, then we look forward to your complete online application.
