Role overview

Mission

The École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) is a leading institution offering a dynamic and multicultural research and teaching environment.

Within the Institute of Physics (IPHYS, Faculty of Basic Sciences), we develop advanced electronic systems to support ambitious research projects for IPHYS laboratories ranging from scientific instrumentation within laboratories, to the realization or participation in instruments for international research infrastructures.
