Mission

Within the framework of this function, attached to the Flückiger Dials Dials Finishing workshop in Saint Imier, you will guarantee in complete autonomy the realization of a complex set of operations relating to the installation of appliqués, index, crimps, etc. on dials completed in compliance with our production and quality standards.
