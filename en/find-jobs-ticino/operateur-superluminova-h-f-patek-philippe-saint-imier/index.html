Mission

Within the framework of this function, attached to the Finish Dials workshop, you will independently guarantee the realization of a limited set of complex operations relating to the installation of Superluminova (SLN) on appliqués and dials in compliance with production standards (qualitative and quantitative) while ensuring the activities necessary for the proper management of the production activity.
