Original description

Shape the Future of Healthcare with Abbott

Abbott is a global healthcare leader dedicated to helping people live their best lives at every stage. Our portfolio spans life-changing technologies in diagnostics, medical devices, nutrition, and branded generic medicines. With 114,000 colleagues serving patients in 160+ countries , we are committed to advancing health worldwide.

Are you passionate about driving evidence-based strategies that improve patient outcomes? At Abbott’s Established Pharmaceutical Division (EPD) , we are looking for a Senior Manager in Health Economics & Outcomes Research to shape global initiatives that make a real impact. About the Role

Join us as a Sr Global HEOR & Payer Evidence Lead and help shape access to life-saving therapies in oncology and immunology. This is a unique opportunity to make a global impact while working in a collaborative, inclusive environment that values diverse perspectives. Reporting to the Director, Global HEOR , you will lead strategic and hands-on initiatives across emerging markets (Latin America, Middle East, Asia-Pacific, China, Russia, India, and Africa). Your work will directly influence patient access and affordability, ensuring innovative treatments reach those who need them most.

What You’ll Do

  • Lead with purpose : Oversee HEOR projects with external partners, ensuring scientific rigor and timely delivery.
  • Empower teams globally : Co-create country-level HEOR plans and mentor local professionals to build capabilities.
  • Drive innovation : Adapt economic models and guide Real-World Evidence (RWE) studies to meet diverse market needs.
  • Communicate impact : Translate complex data into clear, compelling value stories for HTA submissions and payer negotiations.
  • Collaborate across cultures : Work with cross-functional teams and external stakeholders to strengthen global access strategies.
  • Champion visibility : Lead publication planning and represent HEOR at international congresses.

About You

  • 8+ years’ experience in the biotech, pharma or healthcare sector (incl. consulting) with strong focus in Health Economics, Market Access, or related functions/fields
  • Experience across multiple global regions, especially emerging markets
  • Deep knowledge of healthcare system and evolving trends of the major HTA countries and Emerging countries
  • Strong knowledge of health economic modeling and RWE.
  • Proven leadership in cross-functional or cross-country initiatives.
  • Advanced degree (MSc, PhD, PharmD, MPH) in health economics, public health, epidemiology, or related field.
  • Oncology and/or immunology experience preferred.
  • Passion for improving patient access and affordability.

What We Offer

  • Inclusive culture : We value diversity and encourage different perspectives to drive innovation.
  • Career growth : Leadership development, mentoring, and exposure to strategic decision-making.
  • Impactful work : Contribute to therapies that change lives worldwide.
