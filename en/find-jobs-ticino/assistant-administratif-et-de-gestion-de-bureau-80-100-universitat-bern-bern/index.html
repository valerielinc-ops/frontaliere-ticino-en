The UniBE is versatile.
With us you can expect a wide variety of topics and a lot of design freedom. Tasks: In this role, you play a central role in the administrative support and operational processes of the DCR. They work closely with our professors and the management team and work in various cross-departmental tasks. 1. Administrative support of the DCR Directorate • Daily business including calendar and session management, correspondence and communication • Administrative support for the research groups of our professors • Preparation, organisation and maintenance of project-related documentation (e.g. applications, contracts, reports) • Track project schedules and milestones as well as follow-up with internal and external stakeholders • Support for internal and external communication and reporting on departmental initiatives • Initial research on potential cooperation partners and project opportunities • Organization of business trips and support in planning and conducting strategic meetings, workshops and events • Support for website and social media communication in collaboration with project teams • Support for the creation of communication materials such as flyers, LinkedIn posts, digital signage (TV on the DCR) and videos 2. Operational administrative support and office management • Processing of requests via the ticketing system and maintenance of project documents • Support in the onboarding of new employees and customers, including documentation, system access and introductory materials • Coordination with internal teams to coordinate operational processes and results • Support for event logistics and presence on site • Organisation of internal employee events and regular meetings • Office organisation and day-to-day operations (e.g. orders, mail processing, filing systems, service providers) • Contact person for the BNF programme in the DCR, including updating the BNF projects and coordinating discussions with potential candidates Your profile • Completed commercial or administrative training (KV EFZ or equivalent), Bachelor’s degree beneficial • Several years of professional experience in administration, ideally in the academic, research-related or medical environment • Negotiable German and English in word and writing • Strong organisational and coordination skills as well as a structured, independent and solution-oriented working method • Very good communication and team skills as well as a strong understanding of services • Very good MS Office skills (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneDrive, etc.) • High degree of reliability, discretion and detail • Interest in clinical research • Readiness for full presence on site (no home office) Requirements • A varied and responsible activity at the interface of research, teaching and administration • Insights into innovative clinical research • A dynamic working environment at one of the leading medical faculties in Switzerland • Participation in important, forward-looking projects • International working environment with short decision-making paths • Attractive employment conditions according to the guidelines of the University of Bern We offer: A dynamic working environment at one of the leading medical faculties in Switzerland
