Role overview

The Office for Commercial Registers and Notariates maintains a public register in the area of commercial registers and performs notarial reports in commercial registers, matrimonial property and inheritance law.

Furthermore, the Notariate is active in the field of administrative inheritance law and is responsible for inheritance security measures and official inheritance divisions. What you're expecting:

You will be involved in the implementation of legal safeguards rules (security inventories and inheritance administrations), official divisions and executions of wills
