Original description

Careers that change live start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a Mission to alleviate bread, restore health, and extend life. Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact. A Day in the Life Relieving pain, restoring health and prolonging life is guided by this mission that Medtronic continues to provide its innovative therapies to millions of patients. Today, you are given the opportunity to integrate the largest medical device company in the world and participate in this daily challenge. By joining our teams, you will be at the heart of our values! The Maintenance Supervisor controls daily reactive maintenance activities to ensure equipment availability and performance. A local leader, he coordinates technicians, manages operational priorities and provides technical support for complex interventions.

Responsibilities may include the following activities and other activities may be assigned

  • Organize interventions, allocate resources and adjust priorities to meet production needs.
  • Coach, support and develop technicians while promoting a culture of safety, quality, commitment and continuous improvement.
  • Provide technical support, structure troubleshooting and ensure effective communication at major events.
  • Conduct root cause analyses, pilot corrective actions, and contribute to reducing recurring breakdowns.
  • Follow key maintenance KPIs and define improvement plans necessary to achieve site objectives.
  • Ensure data quality, documentation and closure of interventions.
  • Deploy the initiatives "Medtronic Maintenance Excellence".
  • Communicate fluidly and efficiently with production and other support functions (Quality, Calibration, IT, etc.).

Knowledge and experience required

  • Technical training in industrial maintenance, mechanical, electrical or automation, with minimum 5 years experience in industrial maintenance.
  • Excellent diagnostic skills and problem solving.
  • Experience in team leadership or desired technical coordination.
  • Master's degree in GMAO/CMMS.
  • English in writing and oral skills
  • Agility and decision-making in a dynamic environment
  • Experience in a regulated industrial environment: an asset.
