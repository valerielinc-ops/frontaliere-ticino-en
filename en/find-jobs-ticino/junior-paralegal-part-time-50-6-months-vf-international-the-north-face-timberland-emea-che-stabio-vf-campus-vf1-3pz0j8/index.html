Main responsibilities

  • Primary Responsibilities: Contract Execution Management
  • Own the end-to-end contract execution workflow via Docusign, handling approximately 70–90 contracts per week with accuracy and within agreed turnaround times.
  • Prepare contracts for electronic signature: review routing logic, assign signing order, set expiry dates, and trigger execution cycles.
  • Monitor signature status, chase outstanding signatories, resolve routing issues, and escalate blockers to the responsible lawyer in a timely manner.
  • Archive fully executed agreements in the designated document management system (SharePoint / contract repository), ensuring consistent naming conventions and metadata tagging.
  • Coordinate wet-ink signature processes and notarisation / legalisation of documents where electronic execution is not accepted.
  • Support continuous improvement of the Docusign workflow, including template creation and process documentation. Legal Budget & Purchase Order Management
  • Serve as the Legal department's primary Coupa user for all external law firm engagements: raise purchase orders, manage PO amendments, and track spending against approved budgets.
  • Liaise with Finance and external law firms to ensure timely and accurate invoice submission, coding, and approval in line with company policy.
  • Maintain a spend tracker for external legal fees; provide regular budget status reports to the General Counsel and relevant budget owners.

Key requirements

  • Knowledge & Skill Requirements:
  • Bachelor’s degree.
  • Work experience as an assistant or similar role.
  • Hands-on experience with Docusign (or equivalent eSignature platform) is required;
  • experience with Coupa or another P2P / procurement system is highly desirable.
  • Well organized, with strong time management skills and the ability to act without close guidance.
