Original description

At ABB, we help industries run leaner and cleaner—and every person here makes that happen. You’ll be empowered to lead, supported to grow, and proud of the impact we create together. Join us and help run what runs the world.

This position reports to:

Warehouse/Internal Logistics Manager

__

Your role and responsibilities


In this role, you will have the opportunity to conduct all activities related to the handling of parts and semi-finished or final products to/from the production line or in the warehouse. Each day, you will be responsible for handling and moving material for production as per guidelines. You will also showcase your expertise by performing warehouse and logistics activities in close collaboration with our internal production teams.


The work model for the role is: Onsite; Baden and Untersiggenthal


This role is contributing to the Motion – Traction division in Switzerland.


You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Goods receipts handling from our internal shuttle trucks and external deliveries.
  • Sorting, arranging, and storing materials as per guidelines, and picking components for dispatch and final packing on pallets.
  • Executing logistics activities according to established procedures, adapting quickly to new processes as they evolve.
  • Handling goods with various handling equipment, such as forklift or indoor cranes (up to 16 to)

Our team dynamics


You will join a dedicated, service-oriented team at our Untersiggenthal and Baden site. We work in a fast-paced production environment where collaboration with manufacturing teams is key, and where every team member plays an important role in keeping operations running smoothly, while serving various internal customer and stakeholder needs.


• Qualifications for the role

  • You hold a qualification as a Logistics Specialist (EFZ or equivalent qualification), have several years of professional experience in logistics or a production-related environment, and possess a SUVA-recognized forklift licence for counterbalance and pedestrian-operated forklifts.
  • You enjoy working with warehouse and logistics processes in an industrial/manufacturing environment and are quick to absorb and adapt to new workflows.
  • You have a demonstrated track record of reliable, independent working practices and can manage your own workload in a structured environment.
  • You are highly adept in ERP/IT systems (e.g. SAP) and general computer tools – experience with logistics software is a clear advantage.
  • You are passionate about service orientation and customer focus, with a proven ability to prioritize and respond to the needs of internal production stakeholders.
  • You are at ease communicating in German (fluent). Every additional language is an asset.
  • Knowledge in Dangerous Good (ADR/IMDG/IATA) is highly recommended
