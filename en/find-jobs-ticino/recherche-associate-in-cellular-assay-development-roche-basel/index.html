Main responsibilities

  • Your key responsibilities include:
  • Developing, optimizing, and validating robust cell-based functional assays—including complex 3D models—using technologies such as HTRF, AlphaLISA, Luminescence, and High-Content Imaging.
  • Miniaturizing cellular assays into high-density plate formats (384-well and 1536-well plates), optimizing performance parameters such as Z’-factor and signal-to-background ratio.
  • Maintaining, characterizing, and performing quality control on a wide range of mammalian cell lines, including primary cells, continuous lines, and induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs).
  • Partnering closely with the High-Throughput Screening and Profiling team to seamlessly transfer new assays onto integrated robotic systems.
  • Running routine cellular assays to generate high-quality data supporting structure-activity relationship (SAR) studies for portfolio projects.
  • Analyzing complex screening datasets using modern scientific tools (e.g., Genedata Screener,
  • Spotfire) and documenting workflows meticulously in Electronic Lab Notebooks (ELN).
  • Serving as the primary cellular assay expert on multidisciplinary project teams, sharing insights across chemistry, High-Throughput Screening and Profiling, and therapeutic area groups. Who You Are
  • You are a curious, highly motivated scientist who thrives on solving complex biological challenges.

Key requirements

  • and skills:
  • Master’s degree in Cell Biology, Molecular Biology, or a closely related scientific discipline.
  • 5+ years of relevant industry research experience focused on advanced cell culture and cell-based assay development.
  • Demonstrated experience in miniaturizing assay workflows into 384- and 1536-well plate formats and utilizing automated liquid-handling systems.
  • Strong proficiency in data analysis, statistical evaluation, and presenting scientific findings clearly to multidisciplinary teams.
  • Fluency in English with strong interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Flexibility and a resourceful problem-solving approach tailored to a dynamic lab environment.
