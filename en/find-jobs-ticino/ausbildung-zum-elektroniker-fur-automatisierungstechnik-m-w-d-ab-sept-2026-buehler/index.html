Original description

At the Beilngries site, around 360 employees in the Grain Quality and Supply division are dedicated to plant and mechanical engineering in the fields of grain processing and malting and brewing. This includes cleaning, drying, transport (including handling technology and ship loading), storage and digitalization to process grain from the field to the consumer. Always with a focus on efficiency, product quality and sustainability.

Tasks

  • As an electronics technician for automation technology (m/f/d) you are part of the PLC programming team in automation.
  • You learn about the process in the areas of silo, rice and malting and image it in the PLC software.
  • Together in the team you ensure that the plants that Bühler supplies worldwide are breathed into life and can be controlled by the customers easily and efficiently.
  • The training usually takes 3.5 years, the vocational school is in Ingolstadt.

Requirements

  • You are interested in technology, are creative and want to program.
  • You are also committed and reliable, you enjoy teamwork.
  • You are right with us with the completion of middle-level and good grades in mathematics, science subjects and English.
  • And after the training?
  • There are a variety of applications for you.
  • As a project engineer automation you can program and start up programming and customer systems.
  • With the focus on "electrical engineering, you can plan electrical schemes for customer systems independently.
  • In addition, there is the focus on "application development, the possibility to put the focus even more on programming.
