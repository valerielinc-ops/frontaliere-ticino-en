Original description

Would you like to take the first steps in a modern and stimulating environment in the heart of the real estate sector? At Livit you will have the opportunity to learn from experienced professionals and actively contribute to managing the needs of owners and tenants. Thanks to your customer orientation, you will help build solid and long-lasting relationships based on trust, becoming an integral part of a team that puts people at the centre.

We are looking for a motivated person, ready to face with enthusiasm the challenges of today and tomorrow, and eager to make a difference in a constantly evolving sector. From Livit your commitment, your ideas and your potential really matter. If you want to learn, grow and build your professional future in a solid and innovative reality, we are the right place for you.

Aufgaben

  • Telephone connection with potential conductors, tenants, custodians, craftsmen, authorities, etc.
  • Management of lease cancellations
  • Support in the activities of renting primary objects and managing subentres
  • Management of the rental of secondary objects such as places in garages and parking spaces
  • Support in preparing and performing the shooting and deliveries of apartments
  • Processing of invoices and final accounts
  • Development of adjustments to the leases and contractual changes
  • Support in the assignment, coordination and monitoring of repair and maintenance operations
  • Matching management and various administrative activities

Anforder GEN

  • Training completed by the middle school of commerce (SMC part school)
  • Good and constant votes in the main subjects
  • Interest in real estate processes
  • Enthusiasm in using new digital technologies and tools
  • High commitment and motivation and friendly attitude
  • Reliability, responsibility, autonomy and excellent relational skills
  • Positive personality, well-groomed appearance and friendly and professional attitude

Wir biene

  • Internal offer of specialist courses and management
  • Financial support and/or timetable support for external specialist training courses and access to e- letti platforms for online language courses
  • Individual career planning and growth prospects
  • Home- / Mobile Office
  • Bonus related to business results and personal performance
  • From 5 weeks of leave and the possibility of purchasing a maximum of 10 days of additional leave (Ferienflex)
  • Over-average social security benefits: Livit bears 100% of daily sickness insurance and accident insurance premiums
  • 14-day paternity leave

We love real estate and we are committed with enthusiasm to preserving the value of the property of our customers.

If you work at Livit, you are part of our family. We appreciate what you are and what you do. In fact, we care a lot not only about your skills and experience, but also about you as a person. In our working environment you feel comfortable and have the opportunity to realize yourself. This is due, on the one hand, to our multiple professional profiles and the possibilities of growth within our company and, on the other hand, to the unmistakable corporate culture, which does not have equal in the real estate world.
