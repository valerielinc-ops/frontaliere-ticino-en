Role overview

Tasks

  • You are responsible for teaching in our "Alternative Learning Place Reinach" (ALOR). Our Pimar students enter these voluntarily or involuntarily if they need a "TimeOut" from standard lessons.
  • The ALOR strives for long-term integration into the rule class via a short-term separation. Central are the accompanying measures before, during and after the ALOR in order to sustainably improve the well-being of all actors.
  • Therefore, in addition to the support in the ALOR, the advice and support of teachers and parents are part of your tasks and our ALOR concept.
  • For the fulfillment of your pedagogical tasks in the leadership of the ALOR class and the contact and exchange with the participants (parents / teachers, class) a curative pedagogue / curative teacher is at your side.
  • Fulfilling as a joyful, committed and initiative personality You can perform your tasks with reliability, resilience and teamwork and help to implement and further develop the existing ALOR concept in practice.

Requirements

  • You have a recognised diploma in social pedagogy and have experience in the school environment.
  • They have experience in dealing with behavioral original and/or neurodivergent children as well as mentally distressed children and families. In addition, you also have an understanding of children’s learning processes.
  • They have consulting expertise or are ready for appropriate training.
  • You cultivate an appreciative cooperation with the students and the guardians and the pedagogical cooperation with colleagues is important to you.

The Reinach primary level comprises more than 80 classes with more than 1400 students, who are taught by more than 200 teachers and specialists. Primary level employees optimally design schooling for the children entrusted to them and respond competently and differentiated to the complex challenges of modern society. Our motto is: Working together to learn for the future!
