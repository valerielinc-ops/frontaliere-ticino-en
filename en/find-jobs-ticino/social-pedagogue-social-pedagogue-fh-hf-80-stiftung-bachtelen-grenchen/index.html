Original description

Do you want to strengthen children in their growing up, accompany them and also move and shape something yourself?

The focus of Bachtelen is on children, young people and young adults – we accompany and promote them with heart, mind and a pinch of humor. In human and professional teams, we shape their everyday lives together with them and create spaces for development.

On the residential group Weiher live during the school weeks from Sunday to Friday up to 8 children and young people of school age. We are a stable, motivated and committed team that works strongly systemically. Together with parents, legal representatives and, of course, children and young people, we are important partners in their development. Our work is based on trust, participation and relationship design at eye level. Professionally, we are guided by trauma-pedagogical principles, the principles of life-world orientation and empowerment. In this way, we create a structured, reliable framework and a safe place where children and young people can grow and develop their strengths.
