Role overview

Mission

As part of this function, attached to the International Customer Service Division (SCI), you plan, organize and direct the aesthetic and functional control activities of watches and components.

Within the SCI Quality Control workshop, you are the guarantor of the conformity of watches and components, in compliance with the Patek Philippe criteria.

  • Mission
  • As part of this function, attached to the International Customer Service Division (SCI), you plan, organize and direct the aesthetic and functional control activities of watches and components.
  • Federate, mentor and develop a team of quality controllers.
  • Ensure production within the expected level of functional and aesthetic quality and within the deadlines.

Main responsibilities

  • Federate, mentor and develop a team of quality controllers.
  • Ensure production within the expected level of functional and aesthetic quality and within the deadlines.
  • Pilot the optimization of watch and component control processes in close collaboration with other Division units.
  • Develop and implement relevant monitoring plans.
  • Define and maintain relevant indicators within your perimeter.
  • Participate in improvement projects within the Division. Profile sought
  • Technical degree ES, engineer in microtechnics or training deemed equivalent.
  • Prior experience in watch quality.
  • Knowledge of methodologies and quality tools (AMDEC, 8D).
  • Knowledge of an ERP (SAP ideally) and office tools (MS Office).

Additional details
