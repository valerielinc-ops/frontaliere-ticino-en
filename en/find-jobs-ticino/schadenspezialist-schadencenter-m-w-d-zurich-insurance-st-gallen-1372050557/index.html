Original description

136720 | 80 - 100% | St. Gallen | Hybrid

Are you fascinated by the world of motor vehicle damage and would you like to further develop your expertise in claims processing? Do you enjoy supporting customers in demanding situations in a solution-oriented manner and finding fast, efficient solutions?

As a damage specialist, Damage Center (m/f/d) at Zurich Switzerland, you will strengthen the damage division in the damage motor vehicle segment and make an important contribution to our joint success. In this role, you handle motor vehicle damage from capture to final regulation and ensure that the processes are efficient and customer-oriented. You will work in a dynamic environment in the St. Gallen & Help Points East damage center, which promotes collaboration and customer orientation and offers you the opportunity to continuously develop your skills. Through your commitment, you contribute to ensuring that our customers are supported quickly and competently in cases of damage.

This is what awaits you

  • You handle motor vehicle damage from capture to final regulation and ensure that the processes are efficient and customer-oriented.
  • You actively communicate with customers, workshops and other stakeholders in order to ensure a smooth handling of claims.
  • You analyze claims reports, check covers and make decisions for further processing with a view to the best possible solution for all parties involved.
  • You support the optimization of processes and submit suggestions to increase the efficiency and quality of claims processing.
  • You work closely with your team to solve complex cases and continuously improve customer satisfaction.

This sets you apart

  • You have a completed commercial education or an equivalent qualification, ideally with experience in claims processing or in the insurance industry.
  • You bring strong customer orientation and strong communication skills and act professionally and solution-oriented even in challenging situations.
  • You work independently and structured and have a high level of problem-solving skills.
  • You have team spirit and actively contribute to the cooperation and success of your team.
  • You have good IT knowledge and can quickly familiarize yourself with new systems.
