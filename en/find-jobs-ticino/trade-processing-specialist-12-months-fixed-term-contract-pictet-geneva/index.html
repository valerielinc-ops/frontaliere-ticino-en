Original description

Your team

Operations ensure secure and efficient processing of Pictet's banking and investment funds. Our teams ensure that all activities comply with regulations and manage risks. By developing and maintaining a modern and robust infrastructure, we guarantee reliable and high-quality services for asset management, asset management and real estate. As a member of the External Brokers Trade Processing team, you will be required to process transactions involving standard products (shares, bonds, investment funds, ETFs, etc.) carried out by our external managers and the Pictet Asset Management (MAP) entity in a multi-site environment. The quality of service offered and an attitude that promotes continuous improvement play a key role for our team, and will be expected from you.

Your role

  • Record DVP/RVP transactions on standard securities, with instructions sent to sub-depositories from external asset managers and WFP.
  • Monitor settlement operations and deal with defaults according to each market in order to ensure timely settlement and avoid any financial or regulatory impact.
  • Addressing activity-related incidents and exits with a critical look, proposing appropriate solutions that reduce operational risks.
  • Maintain excellent working relationships with other departments and departments of the Bank, providing reliable support and appropriate solutions for solicitations.
  • Understanding the value chain front to back and contributing to process optimization, ensuring procedural compliance and risk reduction throughout the business life cycle.

Your profile

  • Bachelor/HES in finance, or equivalent training.
  • Experience of at least 3 years in the trading type banking transaction.
  • Excellent knowledge of the settlement activities of transactions against payment and of the main regulations in force (notably CSDR on central depositories)
  • Knowledge of Avaloq banking software, an asset
  • Mastery of MS Office tools.
  • Ability to rigorously apply procedures and guidelines, while maintaining a spirit of continuous improvement.
  • Mastery of French and English in writing and oral.
  • Home in Switzerland required.

Your rigor, your sense of detail and your ability to adapt will be as much appreciated as your true team spirit and your willingness to ensure the satisfaction of our internal and external clients.

Note

We will not accept any CVs via agencies
