Original description

Bachtelen is committed to the individual promotion of children, young people and young adults in the canton of Solothurn. We are also committed to the integration of young people who are impaired in different ways in their behaviour, language, communication, interaction or cognition. Our wide range of offers includes daycare centers and day schools, residential offers as well as individual support, therapy and counselling offers that are tailored to the respective needs. We currently employ around 550 people.
