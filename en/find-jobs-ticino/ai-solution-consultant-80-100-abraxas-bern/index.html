Original description

Our team is actively shaping the future of artificial intelligence in the public sector. Together we develop innovative AI solutions that create added value for society and contribute our expertise in strategy, implementation and consulting. As an interface between technology, administration and specialist areas, we translate complex concepts into practical applications. Together, we ensure that our AI offerings are not only legally secure and impact-oriented, but also understandable and user-friendly. You accompany projects from conception to successful implementation, support customers: inside the introduction of AI and design sustainable innovations for the digital management of tomorrow. Your role

  • You are actively involved in shaping our AI market solutions: You will further develop the strategic orientation of our AI offerings and use your expertise in product development and positioning.
  • You make AI usable in the public interest: You develop solutions that create real social added value
  • You bring AI into implementation: You accompany administrations and organizations in implementing AI projects strategically, legally and impact-oriented
  • You connect people, technology and impact: You translate complex technologies into understandable solutions, moderate between stakeholders and set impulses for sustainable innovation
  • You show external impact: You represent our AI expertise, give presentations at events and customer events and write specialist articles with external impact

Your profile

  • Strategic thinking & implementation competence: You bring experience in consulting or digital innovation and can embed AI projects in existing strategies and set priorities in a comprehensible way. You also bring concrete implementation experience in AI projects and know the common stumbling blocks and how to skilfully bypass them
  • Technology understanding & project practice: You have in-depth knowledge of common generative AI technologies as well as classic machine learning topics and can realistically assess their potentials and limits.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking & analytical ability: You feel at home in financial metrics, market analysis and customer journeys as well as in prompt engineering sessions
  • Teamwork: You enjoy working in a team, exchanging ideas with experts from different disciplines and actively contributing to the exchange of knowledge. At the same time, it motivates you to help shape a growing AI team and further develop its competencies, processes and offerings.
  • Empathy, Resilience & Diplomatic Skill: You can listen, recognize needs, develop win-win solutions and deal with blurring, setbacks and skepticism. You understand the needs of our customers and can mediate between specialist areas, IT and management. Ideally, you already have experience in the field of public administration
  • Experience: university or university of applied science degree in computer science with focus on artificial intelligence or a comparable education as well as several years of professional experience in this field
  • Languages: You speak fluent German (C1) and have very good English skills

In addition, you bring several years of practical experience in the following topics:

  • Python and corresponding DataScience and GenAI libraries (Pandas, SciKitLearn, Pytorch, Tensorflow, Langchain, etc.)
  • Use and provision of locally operated LLM’s and machine learning models, including tuning and optimization of runtime
  • Implementation of RAG use cases with vector or graph databases on large amounts of data.
  • Optimization of data pipelines for use case-specific data extraction, chunking and embedding.
  • Implementation of classic machine learning use cases such as classification, regression or NLP

Your benefits

  • Up to 60% home office possible with a 100% workload
  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41h week with flexible working hours on a full-time workload
  • Mind. 25 days of holidays per year
  • Support for 100% financial or temporal training
  • Free SBB half-ax subscription for all employees
