Original description

We at Fielmann are more than colleagues – we are a team. Our cohesion makes us strong. Through new impulses, we continue to develop. With your commitment, we grow – sometimes faster, sometimes wisely. Together we find the right pace and set the standards of tomorrow.

We give everything – Their advantages

  • Attractive compensation package incl. Annual glasses discounts for you and your family as well as numerous other benefits
  • Professional and personal development opportunities
  • Family team spirit and a digital working environment
  • Secure workplace with extensive social benefits

Together we set the standards of tomorrow – Their tasks

  • Join the Employee Lifecycle as an all-rounder – from recruitment and recruitment to administrative personnel processes
  • You are the contact person and consultant for employees and managers in personnel issues
  • Take part in the further and new development of our HR processes
  • You are open to new tasks

They inspire us – Your profile

  • You have completed education or training in the HR area and several years of experience as an HR Business Partner
  • You are strong in communication, enjoy dealing with people and are a team player
  • You have very good knowledge of German and very good knowledge of French or Italian
  • English skills are beneficial
  • Commitment, reliability and a high level of comprehension distinguish you
  • You are confident in dealing with common computer programs and ideally have knowledge of Workday and SAP HCM

Sounds like a job in human resources where you can show your strengths? Then we look forward to your complete online application, as well as possible entry date and salary presentation.

The Fielmann Group is the third largest company in the eye-optical industry worldwide. The listed family company based in Hamburg provides its customers with glasses, contact lenses and hearing systems via an omnichannel business model. With 50 specialist shops in Switzerland and around 200 apprentices, we are one of the largest trainers in the industry.
