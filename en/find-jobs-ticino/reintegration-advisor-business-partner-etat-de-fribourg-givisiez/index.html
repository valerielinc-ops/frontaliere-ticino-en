Original description

Appointment authority / department / institution

The Kantonale Social Security Institute (KSVA) is an independent public-law institution that manages and coordinates activities of the Equalisation Fund of the Canton of Freiburg, the IV-Stelle and the Central Services. Thus, it forms a unique centre of competence for insured persons and the companies of the Canton of Freiburg for all social security benefits of the first pillar.

Become part of our team!

We offer a pleasant working environment with a modern infrastructure that is ideally connected to public transport. We promote the compatibility of private and professional life, training and mobility.

"To simplify the processing of applications, we ask all interested persons to attach only one PDF file with all diplomas, employment certificates and/or other documents".

Your tasks

In the section « Employment agency, labour market and contract management » within the corporatist insurance agency of the KSVA :

  • Initiate, develop and accompany projects for the social and professional reintegration of people with health problems
  • Insured persons advise, accompany and implement suitable measures for reintegration, vocational orientation or further training
  • Analyze and accompany situations to ensure optimal coordination between objectives, health restrictions and reintegration potential
  • Maintain active cooperation with the network, especially with employers, service providers and medical and social partners
  • To understand companies as partners, to take up their expectations and to create sustainable solutions for occupational reintegration through targeted negotiations
  • Work closely with the internal departments of the IV unit, in particular the medical and legal services
  • Actively participate in the team and in interdisciplinary exchange vessels, thus ensuring a holistic and coordinated support of the insured persons

Desired profile

  • Bachelor's degree in social work, health care or similar education.
  • Ideally, the C.S.A. in reintegration counselling or equivalent training.
  • Experience in the monitoring and/or reintegration of people with health problems
  • Interest in the social security system and in the application of the IVG
  • Extensive social and communication skills and the joy of contact with different stakeholders
  • Ability to work together constructively in teams and in interdisciplinary technical constellations
  • Extensive network competence, initiative, responsibility and independent working methods
  • Doing French or German with very good of the other language
