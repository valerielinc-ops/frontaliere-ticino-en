  • In your role, you independently carry out construction and maintenance activities on the railway electrical systems, primarily indoors.
  • You will be actively involved in economically ensuring the reliability, availability and safety of the railway infrastructure systems.
  • You provide on-call duty to resolve faults in the complex systems and ensure their availability.
  • Depending on the respective project, you will work sporadically at night and on weekends. Of course, these hours worked will also be rewarded accordingly.
  • So that you can successfully carry out this important function, you will undergo two years of part-time internal training. We train you so that you can independently locate and resolve faults in the indoor systems. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are a technically savvy personality, independent and proactive and are always flexible in your way of working for the tasks involved.
  • For this position you need a completed 4-year basic electrical engineering training EFZ (automatic engineer, electrical engineer, electrician or related professions).
  • The prerequisite for taking up the position is good hearing and vision so that you can recognize important acoustic and visual signals when working in the track area.
  • For this position you need a category B driving license. Ideally, you live in the Brugg region so that you can be at the location within around 30 minutes during on-call duty.
