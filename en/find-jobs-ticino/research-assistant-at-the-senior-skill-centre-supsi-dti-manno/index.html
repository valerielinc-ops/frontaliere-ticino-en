Main responsibilities

  • and activities
  • The selected person will contribute to the implementation of research projects (literature reviews, development of procedures and materials, collection of primary data, analysis of quantitative and qualitative data) and to the scientific production of the Center (reports, scientific articles, presentations at national and international conferences).
  • He will provide project management support and maintain relationships with colleagues from the university institutes involved, as well as with local partners.
  • He will also have the opportunity to develop specific skills in the field of research on aging and a training course will be provided to be defined jointly. Requirements
  • The selected person holds a Master's degree in social sciences, gerontology, or a related field, obtained no more than three years ago.
  • Demonstrates a strong interest in research on aging.
  • Possesses skills in quantitative and/or qualitative research (data collection and analysis), as well as good drafting skills.
  • Is available to carry out diversified tasks depending on project needs.
  • Transversal skills: strong communication, relational and social skills, with a predisposition for teamwork and collaboration, as well as working independently.
  • Digital skills: good knowledge in the use of computer programs and new technologies.
