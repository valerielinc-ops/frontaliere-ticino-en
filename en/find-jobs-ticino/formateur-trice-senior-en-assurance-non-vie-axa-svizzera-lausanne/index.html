Original description

Join AXA, Switzerland's first insurance company, as a retirement plan in early 2027, we are looking for the new link of our training team in Lausanne! Within our People & Organization team Development, bringing together training, leadership and recruitment activities, we are dedicated to the employees of the agencies in order to lead them towards their greatest successes. We would be happy if you would come and share your expertise in the field of non-life insurance, but also your skills in training methodology and your interest in teaching. Do you recognize yourself in the profile below? Do you have a big smile just to imagine joining a dynamic team and participants from all walks of life? Don't hesitate to apply! Our sector provides the interface with our customers. Every day, we present the work on the trade in order to best meet the needs of the clientele with consulting solutions oriented towards the human, action and future, in line with the motto "Customer First" of AXA. Your contribution to the company

  • Support our non-life external service employees in the acquisition of technical skills in insurance, products, systems, processes and sales methods.
  • Prepare and conduct face-to-face or virtual training and accompany participants throughout their journey as part of a structured individual tutoring.
  • Adapt your speech to train professionals and people who discover the profession.
  • Develop and adapt our training offer by following the agile method and objectives set, combining traditional methods, new digital tools and the latest techniques in knowledge acquisition.
  • Work closely with the entire team of trainers in Switzerland, interested in all new training techniques to offer the best possible quality. Your skills and talents
  • You have extensive expertise in the field of non-life insurance, ideally with previous experience in consulting or selling (insurance specialist's patent or equivalent). You'll be excited to expand your knowledge to other areas of insurance as needed.
  • You already have a first experience in the field of adult training on the topic of insurance and you ideally hold an adult trainer degree (FSEA 1 or equivalent). If this is not the case, the number of hours you have already accumulated allows you to make the equivalence.
  • With a focus on quality of service to our clients, you are able to create a climate of trust and exchange, you know how to make your trainings lively and rewarding.
  • Self-governing and highly structured, you have great individual responsibility. You adapt easily and quickly to a very dynamic environment, and you are able to moderate a wide variety of courses while maintaining a high level of quality and expertise.
  • Thanks to your mastery of the various digital technologies and tools, you can develop engaging and effective hybrid training.
  • You have perfect command of French; you can also speak internally with colleagues in German and ideally follow sessions in this language Our recruitment process Discover our application process and get answers to frequently asked questions.
