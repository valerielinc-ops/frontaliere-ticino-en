Original description

Do you have a heart for the elderly? Is the nursing profession your calling, which not only improves the quality of life of others but also creates satisfaction and meaning in your own life? Do you see opportunities to bring your skills and experiences to us to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are in the right place!

For the Park Ramendingen house in the Eastern Switzerland region, we are looking for, starting January 1, 2026 or by agreement, a

Healthcare Specialist EFZ 50–100%

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and nursing of guests according to our nursing philosophy and the nursing process
  • Taking on tasks as a nursing contact person
  • Participation in the nursing process and in the nursing needs assessment with RAI for the assigned guests
  • Electronic nursing documentation using CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach-Smartphone)
  • Planning and accompanying doctor visits
  • Taking on daily responsibility
  • Taking on tasks as a theme bearer of a specialist topic and participation in the group-wide network for nursing quality

Your profile

  • Completed training as a healthcare specialist EFZ
  • Work experience in long-term care preferable
  • RAI knowledge desired or willingness to attend corresponding courses (paid)
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Joy in varied challenges
  • Good German language skills, both oral and written
