More about this search for cross-border applicants

Zürich hosts a modest but steady stream of private-sector roles across finance, technology, and professional services. While the majority of positions serve the local German-speaking workforce, Italian-speaking professionals occasionally find openings in international firms, wealth management, and cross-border project teams. The city operates under Swiss employment law, with contracts typically structured for residents or holders of Swiss work permits.
