Original description

Who are we? The Children and Youth Services Department (SEJ) is committed to supporting the children and young people of the canton of Fribourg. It is the specialized cantonal service responsible for implementing policies on behalf of children and young people and for child protection. Why join us? The childcare sector (SMA) of the SEJ is composed of a small motivated team working to ensure the quality of childcare services. The SMA is the competent authority for authorization, evaluation, monitoring and advisory services for extrafamilial daycare facilities. In this varied and meaningful field, you will be able to apply and develop your social and professional skills. This position is a fixed-term contract until 31.12.2027. The State as employer encourages the reconciliation of professional and private life and offers an attractive working environment and conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link. Your responsibilities • Evaluate, authorize and monitor family childcare settings within the framework of the child placement ordinance • Provide information and advice to childcare settings Desired profile • Master's or bachelor's degree in social work or equivalent qualification • Interest in child protection • Ability to analyze complex situations with a synthesizing mindset • Strong written communication skills • Organizational skills, excellent stress resistance, ability to work as a team • Driving license required • Perfect command of French language (spoken/written) with knowledge of German language Contact information Michaëla Bochud, sector manager, T +41 26 305 15 30
