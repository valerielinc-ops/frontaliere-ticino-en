Role overview

Who are we?

The Cantonal Institution for Social Insurance (ECAS) is an autonomous public law institution that administers and coordinates the activities of the Compensation Fund of the Canton of Fribourg, those of the Cantonal Office of Disability Insurance as well as Central Services.

It thus constitutes a unique center of expertise in first pillar social insurance for insured persons and businesses in the Canton of Fribourg.
