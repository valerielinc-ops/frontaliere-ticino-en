Role overview

At our 4 stations of long-term psychiatry with 18 beds each, chronically mentally ill women and men with cognitive impairments, restrictions on independent life and everyday life or pronounced behavioral abnormalities are looked after.

Furthermore, the stations offer low-swelling rehabilitation programs for patients who then return to their ancestral conditions or transfer to another institution.
