Role overview

On our 4 stations of long-term psychiatry with 18 beds each, chronically ill women and men with cognitive impairments, limitations of independent living and everyday life or pronounced behavioral problems are cared for.

Furthermore, the stations offer low-threshold rehabilitation programs for patients who then return to their ancestral circumstances or move to another institution.
