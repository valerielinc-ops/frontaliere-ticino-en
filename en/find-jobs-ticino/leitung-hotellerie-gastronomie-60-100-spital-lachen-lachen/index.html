Main responsibilities

  • Your Responsibilities
  • With passion, you are a host for patients, employees, and guests.
  • You maintain the excellent reputation of Spital Lachen's hospitality and gastronomy and deliberately build on it further.
  • Together with your department heads, you lead your four departments — Kitchen, Restaurant, Guest Services, and Cleaning — and create a motivating, attractive working environment for around 80 employees.
  • You ensure high-quality and innovative services that you and your employees can be proud of.
  • In doing so, you succeed day after day in balancing service quality, cost-efficiency, and regulatory requirements.
  • You have the opportunity to successfully implement groundbreaking and innovative projects for the entire hospital, such as Serve-on-Demand or the planned kitchen renovation.
  • You report directly to the COO and develop relevant decision-making bases.

Key requirements

  • You are a professional and likeable hotelier.
  • Ideally experience in the hospital sector and knowledge of the specific requirements of services for patients
  • Proven experience in managing managers and developing teams of around 80 employees
  • In-depth skills in project management and change management
  • Digital affinity and ability to use key figures as management and steering tools
  • High resilience, hands-on mentality, and strong interpersonal skills
  • Fluency in German; additional languages are an advantage

Company and context

  • You have a successful track record as a leader in a complex organization with budget responsibility.
  • Thanks to in-depth knowledge of the relevant specialist areas, you act and communicate confidently at both operational and strategic levels.
  • Ideally, you are familiar with the hospital sector and the special requirements of services for patients.
  • You value interdisciplinary collaboration with doctors, nursing staff, technical teams, and other stakeholders.
  • You are accustomed to leading managers, promoting employees in a targeted manner, and developing teams further.
  • You bring in-depth knowledge in project and change management.
  • Thanks to your digital affinity, you drive the digitalization of processes and use key figures deliberately as a management and steering tool.
  • With your high resilience and hands-on mentality, your winning manner, and your very good social skills, you serve as a role model for our service culture.
