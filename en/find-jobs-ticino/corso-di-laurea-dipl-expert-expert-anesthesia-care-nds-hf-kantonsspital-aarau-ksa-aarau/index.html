Role overview

Tasks

As a student, you are jointly responsible for the implementation and monitoring of anesthesia in close cooperation with the medical service. Comprehensive patient care before, during and after the procedure. You benefit from a comprehensive 2-year postgraduate course.

  • We offer you a teaching and coaching by experienced vocational trainers and an accompaniment by specialists in practice. Together with the educational provider of the Aargau School of Anesthesia, Intensive Care and Emergency Care (afsain), which organizes theoretical education, we support and support our students in everyday life.

Requirements

You are a committed, open-minded personality and have a degree as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF, Bachelor of Science in Nursing FH or a foreign diploma in nursing recognised as equivalent and at least 6 months of professional experience in acute care in a hospital or clinic. You bring manual skill and technical flair, you are flexible, team-oriented, communicative and you want to develop further.
