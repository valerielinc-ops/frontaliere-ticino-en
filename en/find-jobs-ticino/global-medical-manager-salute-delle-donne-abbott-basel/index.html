Role overview

Abbott is a global healthcare leader committed to helping people live their fullest lives at every stage.

With a broad portfolio spanning diagnostics, medical devices, nutrition, and branded generic medicines, our 114,000 colleagues serve patients in over 160 countries, driving innovation that advances health worldwide.

Within Abbott’s Established Pharmaceuticals Division (EPD), we are expanding our Women’s Health team and are seeking an experienced Global Medical Manager – Women’s Health to support our continued growth.

Main responsibilities

  • Main Responsibilities
  • Responsible for specific activities and strategic projects within Medical Affairs for the Women`s Health Portfolio
  • Support the Global Medical Director in reviewing, approving and overviewing global and regional medical / clinical studies and publications relevant to the therapeutic area
  • In close collaboration with the Global and Regional Marketing Teams, plan and execute prelaunch activities of cross-regional products/brands
  • With collaboration of regional medical teams run Key Opinion Leader assessment and identification projects, organize Key Opinion Leaders database, ensure regular updates, identify potential global Key Opinion Leaders, and inform internal stakeholder on potential global Key Opinion Leader candidates
  • Work on medical maintenance of global brands by participating in product safety team meetings, Labeling team meetings, writing and/or reviewing scientific documents, updating product scientific library, etc.
  • Collaborating with marketing team on developing promotional materials, including sales aids, web pages, digital platforms, etc. and ensuring scientific validity of the contents
  • For geo-expansion of local products, update/upgrade scientific part of registration dossiers in order to get them into approvable standards in other countries.
  • Conduct medical assessments on new Licensing & Acquisition opportunities for portfolio expansion The profile
  • Medical Doctor degree and/or PhD in related life sciences with relevant pharmaceutical industry experience in regional or global roles
