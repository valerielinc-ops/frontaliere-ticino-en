Original description

Who you are

We are looking for a proactive, well-organized person who likes to work in a constantly changing environment.

  • You have a passion for inventory accuracy and understand how essential it is that our products be available at the right place and time to create a successful shopping experience for our customers.
  • You have great skills in processes, tools and working methods in the area of retail and/or logistics.
  • You know how to use forklifts safely and efficiently and, ideally, you have an up-to-date SUVA carist licence and an CFC with more than 2 years experience
  • You like to work with logistics in a fast and constantly evolving retail environment.
  • You're flexible, you like to work as a team and you're self-disciplined.
  • You have a friendly behavior that helps promote positive collaboration with your colleagues through your openness and communicative nature.
  • You are available to work early between 05h and 14h variable-

Your responsibilities

  • Work according to established routines to ensure accurate inventory and product availability for customers
  • Work together with your colleagues to ensure that the store is well stocked, in perfect condition and ready to welcome our customers.
  • Work carefully to ensure the safety and protection of your customers and colleagues in your area of responsibility.
  • Share your knowledge and skills to help your colleagues in the department and other areas of the store.
  • Be available to support your colleagues in the department and other areas of the store when necessary.

About this industry

We are the team that, behind the scenes, ensures that IKEA products are available for as many people as possible. We use our various procurement process expertise to adapt the range to our storage capacity through forecasts and orders, so that products are as available as possible for customers, while minimizing our expenses. If you ask yourself who is in charge of finding effective ways to prepare our customers' orders, it is us! Our passion: collaborate to improve the customer experience at IKEA.
