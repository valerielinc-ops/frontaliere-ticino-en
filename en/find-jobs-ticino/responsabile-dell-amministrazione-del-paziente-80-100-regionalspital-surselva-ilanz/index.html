Head of Patient Administration 80 - 100% - Regionalspital Surselva (RSS).

The Regionalspital Surselva AG is a regionally anchored hospital in Ilanz in the canton of Graubünden and provides the extended basic and emergency care for around 22,000 inhabitants and seasonally 20,000 holiday guests of the Surselva region.

As a modern health centre, we offer attractive employment conditions, advanced medical infrastructure and a dedicated, interdisciplinary team.
