Original description

Your profile Do you want to enter professional life directly after your maturity, assume responsibilities and discover the banking world in practice? As a member of our Junior Talents, you start your career at UBS. From the beginning, you are integrated into daily activities: you participate in processing customer or product-related requests, you analyse cases and you prepare interviews. You discover different fields through semi-annual rotations that will allow you to quickly develop a global understanding of our activity.

What awaits us

  • a training of 18 months with various insights in our various sectors and agencies
  • three six-month rotations during which you work in customer consulting and product consulting (present and digital). This will allow you to familiarize yourself with topics such as investments, credits, mortgages and payments
  • For each rotation, a practical training manager accompanies and supports you in your development
  • an excellent starting point in the banking sector, with real prospects for your future career

Your training Your training is practical, varied and focused on your personal development. In parallel with your work in the team, you acquire the theoretical bases of the banking field (cyp.ch), with people training other banks. Specialized in-house training complements this learning to deepen your understanding of our products and services. This allows you to apply your knowledge directly in your professional daily – this is an ideal preparation for your final exam.

At the end of your training, you will obtain the certificate "Initial Post-Maturity Banking Training (BEM)" issued by the Swiss Bankers Association (SBA). Many graduates are then employed at UBS in a fixed position or continue their development in our two-year succession promotion programme (JUNA).

If you want to know more about the BEM program and the documents needed for your application, click here.

Your team As a member of our UBS BEM community, you will meet regularly with the other BEMs during internal and external training, multi-day events and joint activities. Friendships and networks are often created on this occasion and continue well beyond the training period.

At UBS, we are committed to modern technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) to continuously improve our services – including within your team. This allows us to make processes more efficient, to use data in a smarter way, and to make everyday life easier for colleagues and clients.

Your skills and experience We are looking for young people who are motivated, curious, self-reliant, who like to learn, assume responsibilities and interact with others – both within the team and in contact with customers.

It is important that you have one of the following degrees

  • Gymnastics (preferably in economics and law)
  • SWC employee with professional maturity obtained in a sector other than the bank
  • Diploma from a business school with integrated maturity obtained in a sector other than the bank
  • Specialized maturity (only in German-speaking Switzerland)

Do you recognize yourself in this profile? Then you're at the right address at our house!

Moreover, you distinguish yourself by

  • your pleasure in human contacts and your strong team spirit
  • your motivation and positive attitude
  • your great desire to learn and your openness to new products
  • your interest in the world of finance

Perhaps you are already in higher education and realize that you miss practice? Or have you gained first experience in sales or customer-oriented activities? Think about how your experiences and successes match the skills we seek. Commitments for our BEM program take place in March and September. We remind you that you can only apply for one commitment date per year and only one region.
