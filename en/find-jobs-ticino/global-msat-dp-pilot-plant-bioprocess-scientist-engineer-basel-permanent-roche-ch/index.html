Within PTT, Roche’s global Manufacturing Science and Technology (gMSAT) department is the process and product owner for our commercial products, including our internal and external drug substance and drug product network, implementation of new technologies, network standardization, and mitigation of technical risks. The Global MSAT DP Pilot Plant Bioprocess Scientist, reporting to the Global MSAT DP Operations: Lead design & executes experiments to support process and product improvements, technology implementation, process validation, hands-on second-level commercial product support and process transfer to & from internal manufacturing and contract manufacturing sites, as requested.
