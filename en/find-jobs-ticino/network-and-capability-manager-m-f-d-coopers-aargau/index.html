Main responsibilities

  • & Responsibilities
  • You are responsible for overseeing and optimizing the distribution network, handling more than 100,000 shipments per year, to enable timely, reliable and efficient delivery for global clinical trials.
  • You partner closely with internal stakeholders in Supply Chain, Procurement and Quality, customers in Clinical Operations, and external partners such as third-party logistics providers and logistics service providers.
  • You contribute to and execute the Network & Capabilities strategy in alignment with the overall distribution strategy.
  • This includes ensuring future network and capability readiness, establishing new locations and maintaining a strong focus on Supplier Relationship Management.
  • You oversee, manage and optimize distribution costs and an annual spend exceeding CHF 50 million.
  • You drive best practices, standardization and optimization of distribution network processes in collaboration with Business Capability Leads and Quality Assurance to ensure supply chain reliability.

Company and context

  • You hold a bachelor’s degree, master’s degree or MBA in a technical, business or supply chain-related field, or possess equivalent relevant professional experience.
