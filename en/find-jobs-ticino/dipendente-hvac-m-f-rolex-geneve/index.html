Main responsibilities

  • Monitor the periodic operation of HVAC installations via the supervisory system
  • Optimize the operation of HVAC control loops
  • Analyzing, diagnosing and intervening on different technical failures
  • Carry out measurement campaigns and balancing of hydraulic and aeral installations
