Role overview

The technologist and milk technologist mainly deal with the transformation of milk into dairy specialties such as cheese and dairy products, consumption milk and dessert.

They work milk until they get different dairy specialties such as the vast types of raw or pasteurized milk cheese as well as various dairy products and desserts.

Milk technologists also take on tasks in the field of analysis, quality management, logistics and collaborate on the research and development activities of new products.

Additional details

  • Their main tasks can be described as follows: Receiving and storage
  • take milk samples, check its quality and draw reports;
  • put into operation the machines for the manufacture, program them and supervise the production chain; pack, stock and ship the products;
  • participate in the creation of new products; Hygiene and quality control
  • verify the hygiene of the staff.
