The Coercive Measures Court (ZMG) grants particularly drastic coercive measures in the field of criminal and administrative law for the entire canton at the request of the public prosecutor’s office, the juvenile justice or the juvenile justice, the police, the court authorities of first instance, the Office for Prison and Probation Assistance or the Office for Population and Migration. It shall make its decisions through a single judge.

  • Lawyer's patent, licence or master's degree and sufficient practical knowledge for the performance of the office
  • 60% position requiring high availability to ensure on-call time
  • The Judicial Council is looking for a bilingual judge. The position is also open to a German-speaking judge who is willing and able to handle French-speaking cases with the assistance of a bilingual court clerk. Good to very good oral knowledge of French is required, in particular good to very good understanding and sufficient oral expression
  • Voting rights in cantonal matters. Foreign nationals must hold a residence permit and have been resident in the canton for at least five years. There shall be no loss certificates against the persons. They must not have been convicted of acts incompatible with the judicial office
