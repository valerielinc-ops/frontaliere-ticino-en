Company and context

  • You take on a central medical and organizational leadership role in acute geriatrics, geriatric rehabilitation and outpatient geriatrics and shape the future of old-age medicine together with us.
  • You lead interprofessional teams with clarity, empathy and clinical presence, fostering a culture of responsibility, collaboration and education.
  • You care for highly complex geriatric patients and combine evidence-based medicine with people’s individual needs and life goals.
  • You will continue to develop care models, quality standards and technical focus areas and actively participate in the strategic development of geriatrics.
  • You participate in the cadre service system with pikettdienst and without night services. You inspire us
  • with your specialist title General Internal Medicine and focus on Geriatrics (SIWF) or a recognised equivalent qualification.
  • with in-depth clinical experience in acute and/or rehabilitation geriatrics as well as leadership experience at cadre level.
  • We enjoy complex medicine, people and geriatrics that take function, autonomy and quality of life as seriously as diagnoses.
  • with management training at least CAS level or willingness to complete such training.
  • with very good knowledge of German and a personality who combines professional excellence with empathy, reliability, curiosity and creative pleasure.
