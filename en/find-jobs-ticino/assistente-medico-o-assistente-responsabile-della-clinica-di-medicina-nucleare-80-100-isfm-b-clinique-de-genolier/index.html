Original description

The centre is looking for a doctor who wishes to obtain, in the long run, the post-graduate federal title of specialist in nuclear medicine. The position is open to candidates with or without prior experience in the discipline. Supervision is provided by experienced medical officers of the department.

The position guarantees

  • direct and progressive access to all diagnostic examinations;
  • active participation in the therapy and theranostic programme;
  • participation in multi-disciplinary colloquiums both internal and external to the Clinic;
  • integration into post-graduate training networks;
  • a structured framework that meets the requirements of the ISFM program.

The definition and implementation of a research project are also encouraged, in particular to meet the requirements of ISFM post-graduate training.

Swiss doctor diploma or MEBEKO equivalence

Entry into office: 1 May 2027, or to be agreed

Number of posts: 1

Activity rate: 80 to 100 %

Type of contract: 12-month fixed-term contract, renewable Enquiries: vdeffradas@swissmedical.net

Your application must include

  • a cover letter;
  • a resume;
  • a copy of your diplomas;
  • If applicable, the work certificates corresponding to your previous experiences, accompanied by the contact details of the reference persons.

This announcement is addressed to women and men alike.

The Genolier Clinic is part of the Swiss Medical Network, a network of nearly 25 hospitals and more than 70 health centres in Switzerland. The Nuclear Medicine Service of the Genolier Clinic is recognized as a Class B ISFM post-graduate training centre for up to two years. He proposes an assistant doctor or assistant head of clinic. The centre is located in the Genolier Innovation Hub building, in the heart of the Genolier campus, close to radio-oncology and radiology services. It is one of the two European centres of excellence of GE HealthCare in molecular imaging and theranostics, as well as one of the founding members of GE HealthCare's international network of innovation centres. The centre actively collaborates with several Swiss and regional hospital institutions, including the University Hospitals of Geneva, the Cantonal Hospital of Lucerne, the hospitals of Nyon and Morges, the Centre hospitalier Alpes Léman and the Centre Léon Bérard in Lyon. He is developing an active research program in advanced imaging, artificial intelligence and clinical trials. It also welcomes students from polytechnic schools as well as experienced researchers. The first commercial copy of the OMNI 128 cm total body PET, the StarGuide's first commercial upgrade to the GX Dual-Sided configuration, and a theranostic unit with radiation protection chambers will be operational in the coming months. The centre offers a full range of diagnostic examinations for adult patients, including oncology, PET cardiology and neurology. He also performs all conventional nuclear medicine examinations, as well as comprehensive multimodal imaging protocols including diagnostic CT. You will join a team of five senior doctors, two of whom benefit from an academic affiliation: one with the University Hospitals of Geneva and the other as head of the theranostic unit of the Léon Bérard Centre in Lyon. You will also be supervised by specialists with a dual FMH title, particularly in nuclear medicine and radiology or in nuclear medicine and cardiology.
