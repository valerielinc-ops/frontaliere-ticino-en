Original description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in active ingredient production & lyophilisation, we are looking for a dedicated chemical & pharmacologist in shift operation (a), full-time. For a structured and comprehensive training, you work during the first three months in the day service. After successful completion of the training phase, you will switch to the regular 6-4 shift operation (6 days of work – 4 days off).

Their tasks

  • Implementation of production work in the clean room incl. Working in the insulator with highly effective active ingredients
  • Operation, monitoring, arming and cleaning of production facilities (in particular lyophilisers) under GMP conditions
  • Preparation of the clean room and provision of materials, equipment and work equipment before production starts
  • GMP-compliant implementation and complete documentation of all work steps (manufacturing regulations, batch documentation, logbooks)
  • Safe and responsible handling of active substances and hazardous substances in compliance with all safety, health and environmental regulations (SGU)
  • Independent organization of the work area, proactive detection of deviations and submission of suggestions for improvement

Your profile

  • Completed training as a chemical and pharmatechnologist/login EFZ (or equivalent) or at least 3 years of professional experience in a chemical or pharmaceutical production environment
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Ideally, experience in the GMP-regulated environment as well as a strong understanding of quality and documentation
  • experience in cleanroom production
  • Knowledge of how to use insulators is an advantage
  • Structured, responsible working methods and teamwork
  • Readiness for 6-4 shifts (6 days of work – 4 days off)

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
