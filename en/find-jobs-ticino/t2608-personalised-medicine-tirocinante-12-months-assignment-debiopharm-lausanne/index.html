Application process

  • In this role, you will immerse yourself in the exciting and innovative field of liquid-biopsy based biomarker investigation in oncology with direct application to our internal project’s portfolio.
  • Liquid biopsies represent a major advance in the molecular diagnostic space and are increasingly used in clinical trials.
  • By replacing invasive tissue biopsies with minimally invasive blood sampling, They facilitate patient recruitment, enable earlier assessment of treatment response, and improve identification of appropriate patient populations.
  • In addition, they provide valuable insight into disease biology and support the development of predictive and prognostic biomarkers.
  • Debiopharm is now expanding the implementation of these technologies across our portfolio.
  • As an intern in the Personalised Medicine team, part of the Translation Medicine Team, you will participate in fore-front clinical investigations, contribute directly to patient-centric clinical trials design and help shape the future of targeted oncology therapies. Requirements
