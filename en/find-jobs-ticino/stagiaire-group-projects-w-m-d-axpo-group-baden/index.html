Original description

Pensum: 100%

Start your career at the Axpo Group and gain valuable practical experience in strategic projects around digitalization, sustainability and transformation. This 6-month internship offers you the chance to actively shape the energy future and learn from experienced experts.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Support for the implementation of projects across different work areas, including the definition of goals, milestone planning, risk and progress management.
  • Preparation and support of workshops as well as documentation and coordination with stakeholders in Group Functions and Business Units.
  • Participation in strategic initiatives
  • Analysis and preparation of data as well as creation of dashboards and presentations for decision makers.
  • Further development of project governance through templates, playbooks and KPIs.
  • Coordination of external partners and service providers where necessary.

Profile & Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in Economics, Engineering, Data/Informatics, Sustainability or a related field.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; safe handling of Excel/Google Sheets and PowerPoint.
  • Clear and effective communication skills to understand complex content.
  • Structured, proactive and detailed workflow with the ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously.
  • German at native language level, reliable knowledge of English
  • Further language skills are advantageous.

You will become part of an international and supportive team of experienced project managers and senior stakeholders. We value collaboration, innovation and continuous learning, offering you a hybrid working model and mentoring opportunities.
