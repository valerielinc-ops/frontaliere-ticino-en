Role overview

ZämeHalter is a development- and commitment-oriented group therapeutic offer for children and adolescents with escape experiences and mental stress.

Within a reliable group framework, relationship, exchange and individual development are promoted.

The offer is anchored in the children's and youth's service of the Asiatric and enables, if necessary, transitions into further therapeutic offers.

  • ZämeHalter is a development- and commitment-oriented group therapeutic offer for children and adolescents with escape experiences and mental stress.
  • Within a reliable group framework, relationship, exchange and individual development are promoted.
  • You take over the technical and personnel management of the ZämeHalter team and promote a joint development and commitment-oriented orientation.
  • You lead two groups of women and men together with the respective co-groups, one group for female and one for male adolescents, and remain directly connected to therapeutic practice.
