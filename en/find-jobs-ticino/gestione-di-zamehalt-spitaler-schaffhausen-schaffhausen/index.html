Role overview

ZämeHalt is a development- and attachment-oriented group therapy offer for children and adolescents with flight experiences and psychological stress.

In a reliable group framework, relationship, exchange and individual development are promoted.

The offer is anchored in the child and adolescent psychiatric service and enables transitions to further therapeutic offers if necessary.

  • ZämeHalt is a development- and attachment-oriented group therapy offer for children and adolescents with flight experiences and psychological stress.
  • In a reliable group framework, relationship, exchange and individual development are promoted.
  • You take over the technical and personnel leadership of the ZämeHalt team and promote a joint development- and retention-oriented orientation.
  • Together with the respective co-group leaders, you lead two ZämeHalt groups – one group for female and one for male adolescents – and thus remain directly connected to the therapeutic practice.
