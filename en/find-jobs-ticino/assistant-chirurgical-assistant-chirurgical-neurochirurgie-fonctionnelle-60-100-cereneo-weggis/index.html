Role overview

Tasks

  • Construction, nursing management of the operating room and management and if necessary Qualification of the OTA team
  • Preparation, organisation and support of functional neurosurgical procedures
  • Instrumentation and assistance in neurosurgical operations
  • Willingness to assist in neurosurgical procedures outside the actual operation (especially in treatments with focused ultrasound)
  • Care and preparation of the required special neurosurgical instrumentation
  • Support and maintenance of surgical technology (e.g. surgical microscope, navigation system, C-arm)
  • Support for stereotactic interventions
  • Experience in Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS)
  • Preparation and handling of stereotactic systems, implants and other OR-specific materials
  • Ensuring a smooth and efficient surgical procedure in close cooperation with neurosurgery, anesthesia and other participating specialist areas
  • Development and further development of process management in the OR
  • Willingness to flexibly adapt working hours to the needs of the OR in the case of part-time employment
  • Consistent compliance with hygiene, sterility and safety standards
  • Qualification in radiation protection using fluoroscopy (C-arc) in the operating room
  • Control of the completeness and functionality of instruments and technical equipment
  • Documentation of relevant perioperative processes
  • Predictive recognition of the surgeon’s requirements during complex interventions
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing are required to ensure secure and professional communication in an interdisciplinary and international environment.

Requirements

  • Completed training in surgical assistant with surgical experience or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of operations
  • Sound experience in neurosurgery
  • Ideally practical experience in functional neurosurgery
  • Experience with DBS, stereotactic methods and/or implantation methods of great advantage
  • Very good knowledge of sterile procedure and surgical hygiene
  • Safe handling of neurosurgical instruments and technical surgical equipment
  • High degree of precision, responsibility and ability to concentrate
  • Ability to work calmly and structured even during demanding and prolonged procedures
  • Strong team skills and professional communication with doctors, nurses and other disciplines are particularly important: For this position, we are looking for an experienced OTA with in-depth neurosurgical experience. Expertise in functional neurosurgery is particularly welcome, especially in DBS implantation and stereotactic procedures, a high degree of teamwork, pleasure in setting up a new operation, taking responsibility and the ability to think creatively outside the box.
