Role overview

The Directorate-General for Children and Youth (DGEJ) implements the cantonal policy on children and youth, which is structured around three tasks: prevention, protection and participation.

It ensures that children and young people in the canton receive appropriate support, especially when their development or safety is threatened.

The Finance Unit of the Directorate-General for Children and Youth (DGEJ) is putting in the competition a position of Head of the Debt Accounting and Parental Contributions Sector to complement its teams.
