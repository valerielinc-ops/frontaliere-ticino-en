Original description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

As part of Digital Solutions’ Quality & Compliance team, you will contribute to sustainably improving the quality of our products and solutions from the customer’s point of view and ensuring a high level of customer satisfaction.

What is at stake here?

Do you enjoy analysing software and hardware problems in a structured manner and establishing sustainable solutions? Do you like working at the interface between development, support and other areas? Do you want to actively contribute to continuously improving quality from the customer’s point of view?

In this role, you have the opportunity to actively shape our complaint management and directly influence customer satisfaction and the quality of our products.

What tasks will you take on?

  • You take on the responsibility and end-to-end management of customer complaints – from capture to analysis to a sustainable solution
  • You coordinate the handling of customer complaints in close cooperation with development, support, product management and other relevant stakeholders
  • They analyze causes of software and hardware problems (e.g. bugs, system behavior, process gaps) and ensure a sustainable solution
  • They drive structured problem-solving processes (e.g. 8D) and ensure their consistent implementation
  • They identify recurring or system-immanent patterns and derive proactive improvement measures to increase quality
  • They ensure transparency about the status and development of customer complaints through suitable key figures and reports
  • You actively participate in non-conformity management and the further development of the Complaint Management process

What do we expect from you?

They

  • Completed studies (e.g. computer science, engineering, quality management or similar)
  • Experience in dealing with support service or customer complaints, ideally in the software or IT environment
  • Understanding software development processes as well as typical error images in software and hardware solutions
  • Strong moderation and communication skills in an interdisciplinary environment (e.g. development, support, product management)
  • Pronounced analytical, structured and efficient working method with high solution orientation, even in time-critical situations
  • High level of initiative, assertiveness and responsibility
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English

What can you expect from us?

  • A dedicated team in which open communication, mutual support and ownership are lived even with high dynamics
  • A dynamic and innovative working environment at the forefront of digital transformation.
  • Opportunities for professional and personal development through versatile training and learning opportunities.
  • A flexible and family-friendly work culture that supports work-life balance.
  • An attractive compensation package with additional benefits, including health and wellness programs.
  • The opportunity to be part of a company that lives sustainability, diversity and a long-term vision.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.
