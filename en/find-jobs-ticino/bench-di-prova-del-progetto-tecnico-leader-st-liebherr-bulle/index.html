Role overview

Your Responsibilities

  • You plan and manage projects involving the new build, overhaul and upgrading of test benches for internal combustion engines, as well as for hydraulic and aeronautical components (from design through to handover), in collaboration with the production and development departments
  • You identify requirements and draw up preliminary designs and specifications
  • You oversee the implementation and commissioning phases
  • You collaborate with other internal departments, as well as with suppliers, and find innovative solutions to optimise the start-up phase
  • You provide support during maintenance operations and in their preparation Your Profile
  • You have at least five years’ professional experience in the commissioning of production facilities, ideally involving engine test benches or engine components.
  • Experience in project management and in-depth knowledge of combustion engine technology or hydraulics would be a significant advantage.
  • You hold a degree in mechanical engineering, electrical engineering or process engineering.

A technical qualification with relevant experience in the construction and operation of engine or engine component test benches will also be considered.

  • You have a very good knowledge of Windows operating systems and MS Office applications.
  • You have a good command of German or English; knowledge of French would be an advantage.
  • You are independent, well-organised and self-reliant in your work, and possess strong analytical skills.
  • You enjoy taking the initiative, are innovative and customer-focused.

You are highly committed and able to work as part of a team.
