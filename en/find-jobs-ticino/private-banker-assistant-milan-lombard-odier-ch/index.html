Private Banker Assistant - Milan

Provide support to Private Banker on client-focused activities. Assist with client administration and client management, manage workflows and ensure that processes remain timely as well as efficient and effective. As a Private Banker Assistant for our Italian branch, you'll be reporting to the local COO.

YOUR ROLE

Support Private Bankers with administrative duties pertaining to the clients’ accounts:

  • Account opening process, daily monitoring of the accounts, act as first contact for all administrative KYC file documentation
  • Process transfer orders and deal with client correspondence and all administrative matters
  • Ensure appropriate filling/archiving of documentation of all client account documentation
  • Liaise with internal service providers within the company to ensure timely and accurate execution/resolution of clients’ request
  • Notify Bankers of documentation issues/pending items encountered in the course of the development of business by the Private Bankers
  • Deal with the specific mailing for the clients
  • Collaborate with internal functions (Compliance, Operations, Legal…) on client onboarding and lifecycle activities

YOUR PROFILE

  • Fluent in English and Italian
  • Bachelor Degree Level a plus
  • 5-years' experience within financial industry, ideally international
  • Experience of direct client interaction and ability to maintain a professional approach
  • Organization with ability to prioritize workload accordingly
  • Strong collaboration/communication skills
  • Ability to work under pressure
  • Detailed-oriented
  • Client oriented
