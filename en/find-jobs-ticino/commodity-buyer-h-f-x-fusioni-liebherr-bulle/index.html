Role overview

Your Responsibilities In this position, you are responsible for the daily management of a purchasing portfolio,

  • You are in charge of analyzing the portfolio, sorting offers, updating prices, and handling the related administrative tasks,
  • You manage relationships with suppliers: sourcing, RFQs, price negotiations, contract management, long-term agreements, business reviews, QDCF, etc,
  • You are responsible for supplier performance and for drafting reports,
  • You manage supplier-related risks, handle resulting crises, and implement corrective actions,
  • You coordinate supplier evaluations with the supplier quality management department and support audits,
  • You implement cost-reduction activities and ensure their follow-up,
  • You look for the best technical solutions to guarantee design-to-cost (DTC).

Your Profile

  • You hold a bachelor’s or master’s degree in engineering, economics, management, project management, or purchasing,
  • You have 1 to 2 years of experience as a buyer in the automotive, construction equipment, aviation, or railway sectors,
  • You have good knowledge of French, German, and English,
  • You are proficient with an ERP system, SharePoint (intranet), and MS Office,
  • You are organized, flexible, have strong analytical skills, and an excellent ability to work in a team.

You are open to developing your skills in the field and are results-oriented.
