Application process

  • Keep plants and workstations in a very good condition through the application of 5S and TPM methods as well as actively participate in the continuous improvement process and contribute optimization potential.
  • What do we expect from you? They:
  • Have a completed basic education, ideally in a technical profession.

  • Possess a strong quality awareness, especially when assessing surfaces. Ideally have experience in assessing welds

Company and context

  • Then you can expect a versatile task in our production organization, in which you and your teammates produce innovative flowmeters and actively contribute to the continuous improvement of our processes.
  • What tasks will you take on? They shall:
  • Produce Coriolis flow meters as part of a team of around ten.
  • Loading and operating test, blasting and welding equipment
  • Weld seams and surfaces with regard to quality and execution.
  • Precisely and carefully assemble assemblies, cabling and soldering, and weld components together at the plants.
  • Work flexibly in two-shift operation and ensure a smooth production process.
