Role overview

Tasks

  • You accompany topics and projects communicatively and take over the planning, implementation and evaluation of target group-oriented communication and marketing measures.
  • You are responsible for selected communication tools and measures, including the viva! magazine, the annual report, events (e.g. the public lecture series and the Zebi) and brand management.
  • You develop the instruments and measures you are responsible for.
  • You develop communication content both independently and in cooperation with external partners such as agencies, photographers and editors.
  • You advise cadres on communication and reputation issues.

Requirements

  • You have a university degree or a comparable education in corporate communication, marketing, journalism or a similar field.
  • You bring experience in corporate communication and understand how to prepare complex content in an understandable and addressable manner.
  • You are familiar with the interaction of digital and analog communication measures and have a good understanding of the use of text, image, graphics and video.
  • You work independently and with a strong implementation – with the necessary sensitivity.
  • You are interested in topics related to health, age and social developments.
