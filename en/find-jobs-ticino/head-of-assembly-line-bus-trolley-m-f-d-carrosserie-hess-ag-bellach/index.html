Head of Assembly Line Bus & Trolley (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - for better mobility As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our modular system “Co-Bolt”. We are the only Swiss bus manufacturer to go far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The culture of innovation is the fuel of our company.

To support the increase in our production of electric buses, we are looking for a strong and motivated assembly line manager for bus assembly.

As assembly line manager, you will take responsibility for a production line in bus assembly and lead interdisciplinary teams from body shell construction to commissioning. With your organizational talent and leadership strength, you will coordinate the processes along the line, ensure that cycle times are adhered to and keep quality and deadlines under control at all times. They ensure that our buses can be handed over to customers on time and in perfect quality. Your tasks

  • Overall responsibility for the production line in terms of output, quality and cycle time
  • Monitoring and coordination of the production groups in line production and consistent processing of open points in the production chain
  • Leadership and development of line employees
  • Resource and deployment planning including absence and vacation coordination
  • Ensuring a timely production process by adhering to cycle times
  • Ensuring compliance with quality, safety and process standards
  • Monitoring and control of line metrics (hours, quality, etc.)
  • Coordination with various interfaces (planning, logistics, mechanics, electrical, diagnostics, quality)
  • Responsibility for lean management methods at line level including 5S, CIP and process optimization
  • Carrying out and participating in quality assessments and documenting quality requirements
  • Initiate and lead improvement projects
  • Escalation management in the event of technical or procedural deviations
  • Implementation and support of customer acceptances

Our requirements

  • Completed basic technical training
  • Professional training
  • Several years of management experience in industry
  • Knowledge of lean methods, process management and quality assurance
  • Assertiveness, strong communication skills and organizational skills

We offer you

  • Interesting and challenging permanent position in e-mobility
  • Responsible leadership position
  • Opportunity for further training and development
  • Flexible working hours
  • Performance-based employment conditions
