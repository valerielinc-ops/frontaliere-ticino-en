Original description

Introduction Are you a skilled and experienced electronics engineer? Do you want to work in one of the world’s leading scientific Intergovernmental Organisations? Come and join a unique environment whose core value is excellence, and whose defining characteristic is teamwork. CERN, take part! You will work in the TS-Group within the HSE Unit , which is responsible for occupational health and safety, including radiation protection, environmental protection, and emergency preparedness and response. Activities cover the monitoring and reporting of the environmental and radiological impacts of the Organization’s activities. In your role as an electronics engineer, you will be involved in the development and improvement of radiation and environmental monitoring solutions. This includes participating in the full lifecycle of hardware and embedded firmware development using modern methodologies and technologies. Functions

  • Design and develop mixed-signal electronics, with a focus on analogue sensor interfaces and ultra-low-current measurement circuits.
  • Develop and maintain embedded code in VHDL and C to support system control, data processing, and hardware interfacing.
  • Consolidate and maintain heterogeneous firmware and its development environment (tools, build flow, and supporting infrastructure).
  • Adapt the MPSoC architecture to support the required communication interfaces and onboard processing.
  • Develop and integrate firmware updates to meet system functional and performance requirements.
  • Perform functional tests to verify correct system behaviour under expected operating conditions.
  • Develop dedicated electronic test boards and complex test benches to evaluate electronic systems under radiation at CERN and at external irradiation facilities.
  • Characterise newly developed systems through measurement, analysis, and validation activities to ensure compliance with functional and performance requirements.
  • Identify issues during testing, troubleshoot root causes, and implement corrective improvements.
  • Collaborate with hardware, firmware, and software teams to ensure effective system integration and reliable operation.
  • Ensure proper documentation, version control, and traceability of developments using collaborative tools such as GitLab and Jira.

Master's degree or PhD or equivalent relevant experience in the field of Electronics Engineering or a related field.

Experience

The following are required for this post

  • Complex HDL firmware development for FPGA, including implementation of data readout and data processing, HDL simulation, timing, and placement constraints.
  • Software scripting and programming languages, particularly C and Python.
  • Familiarity with digital design methodologies and verification flows, with experience in ASIC development.
  • Practical knowledge of GitLab CI/CD pipelines, including build, test, and deployment automation.
  • Demonstrated experience in mixed-signal electronics design, including analogue and digital board development.
  • Experience in precision analogue front-end design and low-current measurement techniques.

The following are considered assets

  • Experience with radiation monitoring tests and system characterisation under pulsed radiation fields.
  • Ability to analyse test results, troubleshoot issues, identify root causes and implement corrective improvements.

Technical competencies

  • Design and simulation of FPGA-based electronics.
  • Design and simulation of front-end electronics for analogue sensors and transducers.
  • Development of software for embedded and real-time systems with/without OS.
  • Knowledge and application of instrumentation and measurement techniques.
  • Design of ultra-low-current circuits.

Behavioural competencies

  • Working in Teams: working well in groups and readily fitting into a team; participating fully and taking an active role in team activities.
  • Learning and Sharing Knowledge: creating or participating in forums to exchange experience, comparing techniques and learn from others.
  • Communicating Effectively: delivering presentations in a structured and clear way; adjusting style and content to the audience; responding calmly and confidently to questions.
  • Achieving Results: dividing extensive work / projects into manageable sub-tasks with clearly defined milestones.
  • Demonstrating Accountability: working conscientiously and reliably; delivering on promises.

Language skills

Spoken and written English or French, with a commitment to learn the other language.
