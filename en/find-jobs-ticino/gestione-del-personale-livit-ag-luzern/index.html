Original description

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? At Livit, you are committed to the concerns of owners:inside and tenants:in with an exemplary customer orientation and thus contribute significantly to a trusting, long-lasting customer relationship and a positive corporate image. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Tasks

  • You support specialists in matters of day-to-day business such as rental interest changes, apartment acceptances and incidental cost reports as well as in the preparation of final reports and in dunning
  • You advise tenants, housekeepers, craftsmen and authorities by phone and provide information
  • You are dealing with resignations
  • You prepare repair orders and issue them
  • You control and record the creditors
  • You prepare tenant statements, collect real estate status reports and are responsible for general data maintenance
  • You perform administrative tasks and process tenant correspondence

Requirements

  • You bring a completed CV apprenticeship EFZ or an equivalent education (e.g. building or civil engineering drawing:in)
  • You have in-depth business experience, benefit from our industry and are ready to learn new things
  • Your mother tongue is German and you can ideally communicate verbally and in writing in other languages
  • You have a high level of social and self-competence with the ability to self-control
  • You live the values of customer orientation, communicate professionally and enjoy dealing with people
  • You bring an affinity for technology and can apply new technologies
  • You have self-learning skills and it gives you pleasure to share your knowledge

We offer

  • Internal offer of our Livit Academy for specialist and leadership courses
  • Financial and/or time support for external specialist training and access to electronic learning platforms for online language courses
  • individual career planning and development prospects
  • Home / Mobile Office
  • Bonus depending on company success and personal performance
  • At least 5 weeks holiday and purchase of up to 10 additional days holiday (Ferienflex)
  • Above-average pension benefits and premiums for medical day allowance and accident insurance are 100% covered by Livit
  • 14 days paternity leave

We love real estate and are enthusiastic about preserving the value of our clients’ properties.

When you work at Livit, you belong to the Livit Family. You are appreciated for what you are and what you do. Because not only your skills and your experience, but also you as a person are important to us. In our working environment you feel comfortable and can develop. On the one hand, this is due to our versatile job profiles and the development opportunities within our company. On the other hand, there is also the distinctive corporate culture that is unparalleled in the real estate world.
