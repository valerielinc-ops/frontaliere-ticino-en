Role overview

The Transactional Function domain, responsible for delivering tailored solutions in the areas of Securities Operations, Payments, Treasury, Credits, and Cost & Fees, is seeking a Business & Technical Consultant to support requirements analysis, solution design, and Avaloq Core Platform parameterization for our customers worldwide.

In this role, you will collaborate closely with clients, product experts, and delivery teams to translate business needs into effective Avaloq-based solutions, ensuring high-quality implementation and customer satisfaction.

The team is responsible for all aspects related to these areas within the Avaloq Core Platform and the implementation baselines for our SaaS centres.
