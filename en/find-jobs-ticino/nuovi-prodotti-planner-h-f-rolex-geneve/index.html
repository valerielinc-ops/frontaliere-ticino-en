Role overview

Introduction

For our Production division, we are looking for a New Products Planner (H/F) whose main mission will be to ensure the production planning of new products in order to ensure their availability, while respecting quality and timeliness objectives.

You will coordinate the various players in Supply Chain and Production in order to anticipate needs, secure industrial launches and contribute to the overall performance of flows.
