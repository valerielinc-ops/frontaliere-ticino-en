Role overview

Mission

As part of this function, attached to our Development & Industrialization Service, you will be responsible for analysing the feasibility and manufacturing of prototypes (diameters and appliqués) for their future industrialization.

  • Mission
  • As part of this function, attached to our Development & Industrialization Service, you will be responsible for analysing the feasibility and manufacturing of prototypes (diameters and appliqués) for their future industrialization.
  • Exchange with the Creation service to establish requests for prototype dials.
  • Participate in the definition of production processes, feasibility studies and develop production records
  • Diploma of Engineer in Mechanical or Micromechanical or Diploma deemed equivalent.

Main responsibilities

  • Exchange with the Creation service to establish requests for prototype dials.
  • Participate in the definition of production processes, feasibility studies and develop production records
  • Be the guarantor of the basic data created in the ERP & PLM (gams, nomenclatures, technical criteria and other basic data for the manufacture of prototypes).
  • Define the means to be used for the start-up of the prototype (possages, tools, sub-assembly, supplies, etc.).
  • Coordinate the advancement of OFs and orders during the product development phase.
  • Be responsible for the validation of the prototype by the Creation department (compliance of delivered products).
  • Pilot the production of each prototype in project mode, with the collaboration of a multidisciplinary team (ERP basic data, responsible for industrialization, quality, logistics, production, prototype).
  • Ensure the quality of all project deliverables produced according to standards.
  • Organize and host recurrent project follow-up meetings and ad hoc technical sessions (development, production, quality, prototype, etc.).
  • Carry out regular reporting on project progress to internal clients and/or the OMP. Profile sought

Key requirements

  • Diploma of Engineer in Mechanical or Micromechanical or Diploma deemed equivalent.

Additional details

  • Carry out regular reporting on project progress to internal clients and/or the OMP.
  • Strong listening capacity, customer orientation and solution.
