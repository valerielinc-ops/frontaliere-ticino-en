Company descriptionThe hotel Mövenpick Hotel Geneva is a superior 4-star hotel with 350 rooms. Located just a few minutes from Geneva International Airport and Palexpo Exhibition Center, it offers an international working environment with a global clientele. Working here is to accompany business travelers, diplomats and tourists who are waiting for professionalism and reliable service. Whether at reception, in the organization of conferences, in the restoration or in the housekeeping, the team ensures that each stay takes place in an efficient, pleasant and welcoming way. The culinary offer includes several restaurants offering international and regional cuisine, including a fine dining offer, as well as an elegant bar. A generous buffet breakfast ensures guests an excellent start for their day. This establishment combines the internationality of a global city with a modern working environment. Employees here help create a place where the quality of service, team spirit and warmth of reception meet at the highest level. Position DescriptionServer / Leader (M/F)Main tasks:Responsible for the implementation necessary for the smooth running of the service (tables, consoles, back office). Ensure that facilities and premises are maintained in a hygienic and clean condition that meets the requirements of the establishment. Provides quick and courteous service by clearing, serving and setting tables, while respecting the instructions of the incumbent. Informs the person in charge of any customer complaint or remark promptly and fully, as well as any other significant problems. Helps maintain a positive working atmosphere and maintains good relations with the hierarchy and colleagues. Knows food and beverage cards in French and English, as well as special offers, and actively participates in the sale of products (fromage, desserts, promotions, etc.). Follows the training programs defined by management. May be required to perform other tasks according to the needs of the service, even not mentioned above.
