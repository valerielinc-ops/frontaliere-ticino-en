About United Machining

UNITED MACHINING is one of the world’s leading suppliers of complete solutions for manufacturers of precision components and tools as well as for mold making. The portfolio includes machines for milling, spark erosion, laser structuring, laser micromachining and additive manufacturing. In addition, the business unit offers spindles, automation solutions, tools and digitized solutions supported by unmatched customer service and support. UNITED MACHINING includes the brands AGIE CHARMILLES, CHARMILLES, MIKRON MILL, LIECHTI, STEP TEC and SYSTEM 3R. UNITED MACHINING is headquartered in Biel, Switzerland and currently employs over 3,000 people at more than 50 locations worldwide.
