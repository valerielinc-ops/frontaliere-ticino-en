Original description

Do you like to work with modern machines, take responsibility and ensure that our cantonal roads remain safe and maintained? Then you will fit in with us. The street district inspectorate Gossau is responsible for the construction and operational maintenance of around 150 km of cantonal roads.

What to expect

  • Operation of a modern equipment carrier in a team of two for green maintenance and winter service
  • Mowing embankments and banquets and carrying out hedge care work
  • Implementation of maintenance and minor construction work on roads and artificial structures
  • Renewal of signalling, carrying out cleaning and other operational maintenance work
  • Performance of winter service missions and participation in the pikett organisation

What sets it apart

  • Category C or CE driving licence
  • Completed training as a specialist in business maintenance, specialisation in workshop service, or craft vocational training is an advantage
  • Independent, physically resilient personality with an efficient way of working
  • Team-ready, collegial and helpful
  • Location with accessibility of the base within 20 minutes
  • Readiness for assignments outside regular working hours

What we offer

  • A versatile and contemporary workplace
  • A well equipped vehicle and machine park
  • Varied activities with responsibility
  • Attractive employment conditions and good training opportunities
  • A motivated and collaborative team

Contact: Michael Rüegg, Street District Inspector, telephone 058 229 05 80.
