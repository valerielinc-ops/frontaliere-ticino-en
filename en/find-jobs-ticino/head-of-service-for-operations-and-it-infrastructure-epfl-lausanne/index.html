Original description

Context and Mission At the interface of state-of-the-art research excellence, teaching, and major challenges such as the advent of AI or the management of sensitive data, the service for Operations and IT Infrastructure (ITOP) of EPFL's Information Systems (DSI) field is piloting the operation, maintenance and evolution of EPFL's cross-cutting critical IT infrastructure. As head of this service, you manage an average annual budget of about 8 MCHF and lead a team of 60 ETPs. Your teams cover the entire value chain related to the Institute's IT operations and infrastructures, ranging from desk service to the deepest layers of infrastructure, including data centers. You lead them and ensure that the services provided are robust, resilient and responsive to the needs of students, faculty and doctoral staff alike. The position is both strategic and operational. Under the direct supervision of the IOC, you help to define and implement the School's IT strategy, in a collegial manner with the other Chiefs of Service of the DSI, and in close cooperation with the other Vice-Presidents and the Faculties. In parallel, you build and maintain a strong partnership with key stakeholders in the Swiss Higher Education ecosystem (Swiss Universities, Swiss Higher Education) and strategic providers.
