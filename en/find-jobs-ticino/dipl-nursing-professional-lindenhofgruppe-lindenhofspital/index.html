Original description

What you do

  • Our newborns are nursing and looking after in all three shift services together with learners health and students HF nursing and FH midwives.
  • You coach and qualify the learners and students during their assignment.
  • You carry out the care process according to the latest findings.
  • You are committed to the continuous optimization of care processes.
  • You ensure the quality of care by implementing the respective standards.
  • You support and accompany the vocational trainees as well as the daily caregiver of the department in training issues and are jointly responsible for the training quality in the department.
  • As a member of the extended management team, you support and advise the department management on vocational training issues.

What sets you apart

  • You are a qualified nurse (AKP/DN II/HF/FH) and bring experience in the care and care of newborns.
  • Ideally, you already have the SVEB-I certificate or are ready to purchase it in the course.
  • You coordinate the training tasks efficiently and with the necessary organizational skill.
  • You will bring good IT knowledge to competently support learners and students in their digital day-to-day training.
  • Family well-being is at the heart of your work and you meet people with respect and appreciation.
  • You work conscientiously, independently, and with a high quality awareness.
