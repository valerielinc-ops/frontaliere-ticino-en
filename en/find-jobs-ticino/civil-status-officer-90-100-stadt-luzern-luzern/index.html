Role overview

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Do you want to combine office work with a meaningful activity and put customers first?

Your tasks

  • You record civil status events from birth, marriage to death, including court and administrative decisions.
  • You accept declarations (e.g. recognition, name declaration, gender change).
  • As part of marriage preparation, you check whether all marriage requirements are met.
  • You are conducting weddings.
  • You create excerpts from the civil status registers.
  • You advise our customers by phone or personally at the counter.

Your profile

  • You have completed basic commercial training, preferably in a public administration, as well as a degree as a civil status officer with a federal professional certificate.
  • You bring practical experience in the task area and have a flair for legal matters.
  • You express yourself in writing and orally in the German language and bring your foreign language skills.
  • As an emphatic personality, you work independently, reliably and service-oriented.
