Original description

Are you dynamic, motivated and passionate about cooking? As a kitchen employee, you are involved in the preparation of dishes, ensuring that hygiene standards are met and helping to provide our customers with a quality experience every day.

Mansioni

  • Support the service by ensuring the dressing of plates and kitchen support
  • Participate in the preparation of dishes and various implementation activities
  • Ensure compliance with existing hygiene and safety standards
  • Perform kitchen and fridge cleaning and ensure the kitchen's organizational and hygienic fit
  • Ensure cleanliness and organization of the kitchen, office and dining areas

Required

  • 1st experience in the field of collective restoration: an asset
  • Completed professional learning (AFP): in cooking & catering
  • You are versatile, physically resistant and able to work in a sustained environment, cold and hot.
  • You are rigorous, organizational and self-reliant in the performance of your duties.
  • You stand out for your sense of service: you are kind, helpful, punctual and flexible.
  • French (Very good knowledge)
