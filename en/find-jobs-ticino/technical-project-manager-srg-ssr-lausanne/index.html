Original description

Your Mission • Design the technical solutions of the broadcast and IT infrastructure projects (studios, production and distribution authorities, technical premises, mobile units), in pairs with the project manager who carries the steering, budget and planning. • Develop design files: functional synoptics, principle diagrams, low current and strong current wiring plans, space layout plans and bay face views. • Establish electrical and thermal load balances, size power supplies, protections, redundancy and cooling requirements of technical premises. • Define and track the racking: installation of equipment, paths, tracking, mixing, mass management and access. • Coordinate technical specialists (IT, network, audio and video broadcast, security) to ensure overall consistency of the solution, and interface with third-party companies (electricity, CVC, second work) as well as with project agents. • Follow-up on-site implementation: compliance check, receipt of work, commissioning, withdrawal of reserves. • Produce and maintain technical documentation throughout the life cycle: implementation records, plan updates, records of completed works, equipment inventories. • Participate in technology watch. Knowledge requirements: • Mastery of design and technical documentation tools (AutoCAD, Visio or equivalent). • Knowledge of broadcast infrastructures, IP networks and professional audiovisual environments. • Experience in structured copper and fibre optic wiring and integration into technical bays. • Electricity knowledge applied to technical infrastructure (power supply, redundancy, cooling). • Good understanding of professional audio and video protocols (SDI, ST 2110, MADI, AES67, PTP). • Project management concepts and experience in provider coordination. • Knowledge of Agile and ITIL methods.
