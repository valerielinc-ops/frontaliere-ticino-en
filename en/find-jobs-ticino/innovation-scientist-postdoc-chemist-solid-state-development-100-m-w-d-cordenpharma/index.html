Your Tasks We are seeking a highly motivated and scientifically curious Innovation Scientist with a strong hands-on mentality to join our Solid-State Development team at Corden Pharma Switzerland. Reporting directly to the Team Leader Solid-State Development, you will contribute to innovative research projects, experimental investigations, and scientific publications in pharmaceutical solid-state development. The position offers a unique opportunity to combine scientific excellence with practical industrial application in a dynamic CDMO environment.

  • Independently plan, execute, and evaluate scientific case studies related to pharmaceutical solid-state development
  • Identify and propose innovative research topics in collaboration with internal stakeholders and commercial teams
  • Conduct comprehensive literature reviews and develop scientifically sound experimental strategies
  • Perform solid-state screening studies including polymorph, salt, co-crystal, hydrate, solvate, and crystallization investigations
  • Design and execute crystallization, precipitation, and solid-state development studies from screening scale to laboratory scale
  • Design and execute experiments to understand, control, and optimize crystallization processes and solid-state properties of pharmaceutical compounds
  • Apply and interpret analytical techniques such as XRPD, DSC, TGA, DVS, Raman spectroscopy, FTIR, microscopy, and particle characterization methods
  • Analyse and critically evaluate experimental data and draw scientifically sound conclusions
  • Prepare technical reports, scientific presentations, and project updates for internal and external stakeholders
  • Contribute to scientific publications, conference presentations, white papers, and other technical communications
  • Collaborate closely with experts from Solid-State Development, Process Development, Analytical Development, and Manufacturing Science
  • Support the development of innovative scientific approaches and new service offerings in solid-state development.

  • Strong understanding of crystallization processes, thermodynamics, phase behaviour, and solid-state properties of pharmaceutical compounds

  • Ability to critically evaluate scientific literature and translate findings into practical development strategies
  • Strong interest in experimental laboratory work within an industrial research environment
