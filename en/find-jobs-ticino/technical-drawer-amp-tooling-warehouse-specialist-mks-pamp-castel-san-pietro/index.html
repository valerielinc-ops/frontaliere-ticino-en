Role overview

Technical Drawer & Tooling Warehouse Specialist — MKS PAMP SA, Castel San Pietro (TI).

MISSION The Technical Drawing & Tooling Warehouse Specialist is responsible for managing technical drawings, developing and maintaining operational procedures, and overseeing the tooling warehouse.

As part of a Center of Excellence, this role supports not only the Castel San Pietro site but also teams in the US and India, requiring strong technical competence, cross-regional coordination skills, and the ability to standardize practices across multiple locations.
