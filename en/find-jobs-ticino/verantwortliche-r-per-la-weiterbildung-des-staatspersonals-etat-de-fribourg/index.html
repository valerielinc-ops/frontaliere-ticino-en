Company and context

  • The Office for Personnel and Organization (POA), a central service of the State of Fribourg, is responsible for personnel administration and management as well as organizational management of the canton.
  • It is guided by the legal provisions as well as the political and strategic guidelines of the Council of State.
  • It also ensures the payment of the salaries of all state employees at the operational level.
  • The main tasks of the "Personnel and Organisational Development" section are organisational and professional development, including vocational education and training, the management of business analysis and the support of the budget procedure (job administration).
