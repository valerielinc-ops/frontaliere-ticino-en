Social worker* in FH 60 %

For the social service Integration & Migration we are looking for a committed social worker as of 01.11.2026 or by agreement.

Their tasks

On behalf of municipalities, you advise and accompany recognised and provisionally accepted refugees in the context of economic social assistance and their integration process. The basis is the cantonal guidelines and the integration agenda.

This includes in particular

  • The monthly calculation of the orientation of economic social assistance according to cantonal guidelines
  • Psychosocial counselling
  • Advising and accompanying measures for linguistic, educational, vocational and social integration, in particular in the context of the integration agenda
  • Cooperation with municipalities and other specialist bodies involved
  • Placement, information and triage in the fields of family, housing, health, insurance, finance, education and work
  • Information and orientation on the social and political system in Switzerland
