Role overview

The city of Lucerne organizes snow sports camps in the Melchsee-Frutt, Savognin and Arosa during the car night holidays for schoolchildren.

We are looking for warehouse managers with J+S recognition in the sports of skiing and snowboarding as well as (co-)main warehouse managers.

  • The city of Lucerne organizes snow sports camps in the Melchsee-Frutt, Savognin and Arosa during the car night holidays for schoolchildren.
  • We are looking for warehouse managers with J+S recognition in the sports of skiing and snowboarding as well as (co-)main warehouse managers.
  • As part of the management team, you provide professional and responsible support for the warehouse participants on and off the slopes.

Main responsibilities

Company and context

  • You support the main warehouse management in organizational tasks.

  • You have a great enthusiasm for snow sports.

  • You are a reliable, well-organized personality with a high sense of responsibility.
