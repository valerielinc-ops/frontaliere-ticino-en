Original description

MAIN PURPOSE OF THE ROLE Boarding House Tutors are key members of our boarding community, bringing energy, enthusiasm, and a flexible, student-centred approach to daily boarding life. Working closely with the House Parents, they provide pastoral care, support students' well-being and personal development, contribute to the smooth running of the house, and help create a safe, inclusive, and engaging environment. This role is ideal for people looking to gain valuable experience in education, youth development, or pastoral care. As trusted role models, Boarding House Tutors encourage students to develop independence, resilience, confidence, and positive life skills while contributing to a warm and supportive boarding community. KEY RESPONSABILITIES Pastoral Care: Act as a trusted point of contact for students, offering emotional support and practical guidance. Collaborate with House Parents and the Health Centre to ensure students’ emotional, social, and physical well-being. Community Leadership: Foster a positive and inclusive house environment, promoting teamwork, respect, and responsibility. Encourage student engagement in house activities, celebrations, and competitions. Activity Involvement: Participate actively in a range of student activities and excursions, setting a positive example. Support evening, weekend, and holiday activity schedules, promoting student participation. Operational Support : Assist with the daily operations of the house, including meal supervision, laundry oversight, and student wake-up and bedtime routines. Support House Parents in ensuring the house environment is clean, organized, and safe. Administrative Duties: Ensure accurate and timely record-keeping for attendance, student check-ins, and other administrative tasks. Contribute to student progress reports, sharing observations with House Parents and the senior boarding team.
