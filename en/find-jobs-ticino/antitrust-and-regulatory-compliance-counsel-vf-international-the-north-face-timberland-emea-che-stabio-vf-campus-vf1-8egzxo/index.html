Role overview

VF Corporation is looking for a highly skilled Antitrust and Regulatory Compliance Counsel to join our Legal team based in Stabio, Switzerland. Let’s talk about the role

This role plays a critical and specialized role in strengthening VF’s global Antitrust Compliance Program, with a primary focus on Europe and close alignment with global initiatives.

As a recognized antitrust expert, you will lead the design, implementation, and continuous improvement of VF’s antitrust compliance program framework across EMEA, and act as a key advisor to legal and business stakeholders on competition law risks arising from wholesale and distribution activities.
