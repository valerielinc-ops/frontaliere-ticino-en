Original description

80-100% at the department for basic registry, introduction and computerization Oberwallis (Brig and Leuk) The Land Registry Department is responsible for the state of the rights regarding the land (e.g. property, easements or liens). It consists of 6 basic bowers, which are distributed throughout Valais. Teams are responsible for the introduction and computerization of the Swiss Federal Land Register into a fully computerized land register.

Their tasks

  • They participate in the projects for the introduction and computerization of the Swiss Federal Land Registry (recording, cleaning up and tracking rights and obligations, entry and processing of land) You work in the harmonization and modernization of cantonal processes with you take part in the training and support of users They participate in the ongoing recording of the data in the databases

Your profile

  • You have a certificate of competence in a commercial or technical profession or have an equivalent education You have good knowledge and experience in the land register or real estate sector You have good computer knowledge (CAPITASTRA and SAP are advantageous) You have analysis and synthesis skills You are team-oriented They carry out their tasks precisely
  • You act customer-, process- and goal-oriented They are flexible, resilient and willing to participate in the realization of our projects. You are a German mother tongue and have a good knowledge of the second official language

Immediately or by agreement
