Original description

La Clinique de La Source is a unique multidisciplinary acute care facility. It belongs to the Fondation La Source, an independent and private non-profit institution that also oversees the Institut et Haute École de la Santé La Source. The independence granted by this status allows continuous investment in human and technological aspects to ensure the best possible care for its patients. The La Source affiliated Radiation Oncology Center offers quality care through its multidisciplinary team and equipment. The Center has a full range of radiotherapy treatments and is equipped with state-of-the-art linear accelerators.
