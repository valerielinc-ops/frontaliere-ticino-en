Main responsibilities

  • The responsibilities of a Senior Solids Launch & Transfer Manager include:
  • Project management in process transfers and validations of established products as well as new products from the galenic development
  • Independent planning, implementation and coordination of tasks in all areas of activity of the Solids Launch & Transfer department
  • Processing quality-relevant questions in production with the help of LEAN methods and tools for optimizing concepts, quality, plant efficiency, cost reduction and processes - either as SME or as project manager
  • Responsibility for the corresponding GMP-compliant documentation including management of SOPs, changes, deviations and corrective / preventive measures
  • Continuous improvement of the GMP standard of operation through proactive interdisciplinary teamwork
  • Development and establishment of workflows in the context of the introduction of clinical and commercial products
  • Active support for domestic and foreign official inspections, the existence of which is essential for the rapid approval of innovative medicines Who you are
  • You are a highly motivated, communicative and team-oriented personality who convinces through a very initiative, independent and structured way of working.
  • You always work carefully and reliably, keep the big picture in mind with your entrepreneurial and systemic view and thus contribute to the continuous improvement of processes.

Company and context

  • For a successful production site, the organization Manufacturing Science & Technology (MSAT) Kaiseraugst promotes and enables innovation and improvement through the use of science, technical knowledge and predictive services.
  • At Solids Launch & Transfer within MSAT, we are responsible for the processes for the introduction and optimization of products belonging to the field of solid drug form (synthetic molecule drug product).
  • Our function supports production and ensures the regulatory requirements throughout the entire life cycle for our products. The possibility
  • You work quickly and independently into new areas of responsibility and have a strong scientific analytical thinking your ability to communicate effectively at all levels of the organization and to influence others allows you to perform consistently in a constantly changing environment.
  • You will bring the following qualifications and experience:
  • Completed studies in pharmacy, chemistry or in the field of other natural sciences as well as excellent knowledge of English and German.
  • At least 5 years of relevant experience in the bio/pharmaceutical industry including significant matrix management in functional and interdisciplinary teams.
  • Proven strength in matrix leadership to motivate teams and courage to drive change while balancing risk.
  • Very good knowledge of cGMP and other relevant topics (e.g. the development, production and validation of solid drug forms)
  • Sound experience in change and deviation management
