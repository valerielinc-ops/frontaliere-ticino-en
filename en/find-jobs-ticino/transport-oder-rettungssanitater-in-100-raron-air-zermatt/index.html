TRANSPORT OR RESCOURCES:IN 100%

In order to strengthen our ambulance team, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement in Zermatt:

Your duties

  • You take over the function of a transport paramedic: in FA or emergency paramedic: in HF during rescue operations in the ambulance
  • Irregular working hours with night, weekend and public holiday work
  • Promoting Safety Culture, Crew Resource Management
  • Administrative tasks related to your function
  • Cooperation with the Air Zermatt Training Center
  • Participation in working groups
  • Participation in project work

Your profile

  • Certificate as a transport paramedic:in FA or diploma as a rescue paramedic:in HF
  • Valid driving licence C1/D1 with code 122
  • Level B2 in English and French is advantageous
  • Resilience and flexibility
  • Ability to set priorities
  • You maintain a respectful and professional approach and carry with you exemplary behavior
  • to a positive company image internally and externally

We offer

  • A collaborative team and a motivated working environment
  • An activity with high responsibility and a high level of technical and medical competence
  • Possibility to participate in the further development of the processes and quality cycles of the emergency service
  • Support for professional development and training
  • Attractive benefits thanks to our strong partnerships

What are you waiting for? Take advantage of the opportunity to become part of Air Zermatt AG. Send us your application documents by e-mail to personal@air-zermatt.ch or apply directly online via our website.
