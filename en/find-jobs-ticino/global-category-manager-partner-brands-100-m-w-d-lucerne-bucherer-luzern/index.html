Original description

Your role with us

  • Take responsibility for demand planning as well as the definition, development, and optimization of product assortments for our partner brands, including lifecycle management
  • Manage and monitor relevant KPIs (sales revenue, sales volume, inventory levels, stock turnover, and margin), and conduct regular assortment and product analyses
  • Act as the central point of contact for internal stakeholders and assigned partner brands

  • Identify market and sales opportunities and initiate as well as implement appropriate sales promotion activities

What you bring to the role

  • Commercial vocational training combined with further education in business administration
  • Several years of professional experience in Category Management, ideally within an FMCG, retail, or lifestyle environment
  • An entrepreneurial mindset, strong initiative, and a proven ability to execute and deliver results ("hands-on" mentality)
  • Advanced Excel skills and strong analytical capabilities
  • Excellent communication and negotiation skills, coupled with an appropriate level of assertiveness
  • Fluent German and English, both written and spoken
