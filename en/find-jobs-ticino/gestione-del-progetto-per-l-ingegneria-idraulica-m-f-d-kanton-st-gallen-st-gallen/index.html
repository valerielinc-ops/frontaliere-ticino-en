Role overview

The Office for Water and Energy (AWE) is committed to the protection against floods and natural hazards, the protection and sustainable use of rivers, streams, lakes and groundwater, the rational and efficient use of energy and the protection of the climate.

The main tasks of the Department of Hydraulic Engineering and Natural Hazards are to ensure modern flood control, to preserve or restore the waters as habitats, and to advise and support communities in dealing with natural hazards. What to expect:

Managing and accompanying water development projects (flood protection and revitalization)
