Original description

Sunday evening, after a wonderful day on Lake Thun. At Thun Station you waited on the train to Konolfingen. A BLS brazing machine starts – in the new, freshly worked BLS design, directly from the refit, ready for many more reliable years of use. With pleasure, you explain to your acquaintance that you had the threads in your hand as project manager: in the refit. Seeing the result makes you proud. This is waiting for you:

  • Guide refit, rebuild and modification projects in the field of railway rolling stock and answer project budgets, dates and quality
  • Develop innovation projects in the field of fleet management, examine new approaches and promote their implementation in cooperation with internal and external departments
  • Interdisciplinary project teams guide and ensure coordination between client and internal and external departments
  • All project phases run – from planning to building management and acceptance
  • Create cost proposals, applications and decision bases
  • Actively integrate stakeholders and promote an open, professional project culture
  • Complete technical training (FH) in technical direction or technician: in HF, continuing training in business management desired
  • Experience in project management of technical projects, ideally with IPMA certification
  • Technical understanding of complex vehicle systems as well as good knowledge of computer science in the environment of control/lead technology, data communication and IT/OT interfaces
  • self-initiative, decision-making, enforcement capacity and solution-oriented operation
  • German language skills Your advantages – you can look forward to this:
  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1. Class or half-taxabo.
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy five to ten holiday days a year in addition
  • Financial support for external training and an exciting internal learning and course offer are available to you.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health. With nearly 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. More: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight train, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What links us? The pride of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
