Role overview

The Intelligent Maintenance and Operations Systems (IMOS) Lab at EPFL is looking for a motivated and out-of-the-box thinking PhD researcher, (100%, in Lausanne, fixed-term) starting in November or upon agreement. PROJECT DESCRIPTION

The objective of this project is to develop a trustworthy AI framework for the autonomous operation of process plants.

The research will focus on AI agents that monitor the plant state, detect and diagnose abnormal situations, and recommend or execute operating actions, while meeting the safety, reliability and transparency requirements of safety-critical environments.
