Working in part of Stryker’s Peripheral Vascular division, a unique opportunity has arisen for a motivated, driven, and successful Regional Sales Manager to lead a team of Sales Representatives across Switzerland and Austria.

At Stryker, we look for sales leaders that can motivate, guide, and oversee the success of your region, whilst fostering a strong partnership with our customers and making healthcare better through the use of our industry leading products and services.

Main responsibilities

  • Key Responsibilities
  • Reporting directly into the regional BUM, you will lead a sales team to ensure that financial & performance objectives are met.
  • Accurately forecast business and drive a sustainable, long-term regional sales pipeline aligned with country and EMEA priorities.
  • Attract, develop, engage and lead a high performing sales team, supporting and driving them to accelerate business.
  • Support and collaborate with our Enabling Function teams (Marketing, Medical, Reimbursement, Order-to-Cash) to ensure a smooth Order to Cash process is maintained and our customers receive the highest levels of service.
  • Ensure positive and detailed account communication between the team and other in-house teams, providing feedback and insights to senior management as required.
  • Develop and maintain strategic relationships with key regional and national healthcare systems, key accounts, and influential stakeholders.
  • Monitor and analyse sales performance metrics (revenue, activities, conversion rates) and take corrective actions as necessary.
  • Embrace & enable change to simplify, scale & innovate, acting as a senior commercial role model within the region.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in leading complex sales cycles typically defined as six months or longer with multiple partners, decision-makers, and influencers at multiple levels.
