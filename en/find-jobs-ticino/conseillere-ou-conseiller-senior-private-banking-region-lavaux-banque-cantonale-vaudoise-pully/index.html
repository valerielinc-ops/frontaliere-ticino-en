Original description

Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, whose proximity to its clients is inscribed in its values.

  • Be part of the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poor's since 2011.
  • Contribute to the development of the Vaudois economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of your region.

Missions

  • Manage and retain an existing client portfolio.
  • Prospect and acquire new clients through your network.
  • Contribute to and execute a strategy for acquiring clients on defined segments.
  • Provide information and advice to clients on matters related to wealth management, pre-vision, taxation, succession, and real estate financing.
  • Master and respect the legal framework to minimize operational and legal risks.
  • Collaborate closely with natural business providers, namely counselors from other client segments.

Profile sought

  • Higher education (university or banking economist).
  • Solid technical knowledge in fiscal, legal, and financial matters, validated by SAQ certification.
  • Confirmed experience in the field of wealth management, global counseling, and/or pre-vision for a client base domiciled in Switzerland.
  • In-depth knowledge of the local economic fabric.
  • Good interpersonal skills, developed commercial sense, and genuine understanding of client needs.
  • Perfect mastery of French and very good knowledge of English (C1 level).

What we offer

  • An opportunity to be part of the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poor's since 2011.
  • The possibility of contributing to the development of the Vaudois economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of your region.
  • A dynamic team and passionate colleagues.
  • Training and professional development opportunities.
  • A pleasant and equitable work environment.
