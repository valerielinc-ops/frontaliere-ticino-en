Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock. So that our therapists can concentrate fully on the treatment, our secretariat ensures smooth processes – from the first contact at the reception to coordinated therapy planning. To strengthen our team, we are looking for you: a communicative and organizational personality who likes to be a hub and keeps an overview in lively everyday life.

For our team of the Secretariat of Physiotherapy at the locations Burgdorf and Langnau, we are looking for a:

Task area The missions take place from Monday to Friday during the opening hours of the secretariat. The working days are coordinated within the team. Operations at several locations are possible.

  • Reception and support of patients and other stakeholders
  • Organization and coordination of outpatient and inpatient therapy appointments
  • Processing of medical prescriptions and cooperation in performance recording and billing
  • Independent management of administrative processes in the secretariat
  • Support of the therapists in organizational and administrative matters
  • Close cooperation with various specialist areas for optimal therapy planning
  • Telephone and written correspondence with internal and external bodies

Profile

  • Basic commercial education, health education or equivalent qualification
  • Experience in administrative environment, ideally in healthcare
  • Joy of contact with people and a strong service orientation
  • Good IT user knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Even in hectic situations, you keep calm, priorities and overview
