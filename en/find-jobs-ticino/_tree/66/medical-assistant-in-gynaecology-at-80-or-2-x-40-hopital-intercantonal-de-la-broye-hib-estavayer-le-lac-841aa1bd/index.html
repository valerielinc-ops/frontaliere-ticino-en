Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the Broyard population. In order to meet the many challenges ahead, HIB is looking for: Medical assistant in gynaecology at 80% or 2 x 40% Your Mission

  • Physical and telephone reception of patients and their loved ones with professionalism and kindness
  • Organize appointments via Ultragenda software
  • Prepare, maintain and store the consultation room (replacement of protective paper, disinfection of surfaces and organization of space)
  • Ensure cleaning and disinfection of medical equipment
  • Attend gynecological-obstetric consultations: blood tests, injections, urine tests, dressings, samples, measurements of weight, size and vital signs and ensure the delivery of samples to the laboratory
  • Accompany the doctor to the consultation room when necessary
  • Prepare, update and scan patient medical records
  • Invoicing the medical assistant's services and checking the billing of the consultation
  • Miscellaneous administrative work (mail, mail and medical documents) Your assets
  • SWC medical assistant required
  • Prior experience in obstetric gynaecology
  • Excellent mastery of computer tools (Outlook, Word) and ideally Opale, Soarian, Ultragenda software
  • Ability to manage emergencies, organization and priority management, teamwork, flexibility and strict confidentiality Start: immediately or to be agreed.
