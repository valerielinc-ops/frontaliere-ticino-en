Intern Hospitality & Animations Retail (M/F)

GENERAL MISSION

Hermès Horloger's offer is present internationally through a network of Hermès branches and dealers, and a circuit of approved external retailers. These stores distribute all the Hermès watch offering. As part of his 6-month mission, the employee assists the team in the operational implementation of projects and activities for markets. It provides support for the logistical, administrative and event coordination of retail initiatives, assists in the management of customer experience tools and contributes to the monitoring of prototypes and support for the deployment of actions on the ground.

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES AND ACTIVITIES

  1. Hospitality: participation in the preparation and implementation of events or events to support dynamic retail.
  • Coordination of events: participation in the organisation and operational monitoring of retail events: incentives, visits to subsidiaries, seminars, etc.
  • Logistics preparation: coordination of upstream needs (material, support, delivery, planning and coordination of interlocutors).
  • Monitoring of providers and partners: participation in exchanges with internal and external interlocutors to ensure the smooth running of projects.
  • Budget/Rebilling: Provide operational support in tracking event budgets, controlling expenditures and preparing elements for rebilling processes.
  • Retex: contribution to the collection of returns and the production of post-event balance sheets.
  1. Operational support customer experience: contribution to the implementation of CX initiatives and to the operational support of business activities, ensuring the logistical coordination of tools and support for markets and retail teams.
  • Management of packages for exceptional parts: preparation and monitoring of packages dedicated to exceptional parts, including ordering printed media, assembly of components and coordination of shipments to markets.
  • Configurator's operational support: management of logistical aspects related to the configurator, including the sending and tracking of platelets, as well as the updating of certain elements of the tool.
  • Management of orders and shipments: monitoring of requirements, coordination of orders, control of deliveries and monitoring of the availability of support for retail teams.
  1. Prototype management & logistics monitoring: ensure the operational monitoring of prototypes used in retail animation and customer experience projects, ensuring their traceability, availability and good management.
  • Movement monitoring: organization and monitoring of loans, transfers, shipments and returns of prototypes to the various interlocutors.
  • Logistics coordination: anticipation of prototype requirements for events, presentations and animations.
