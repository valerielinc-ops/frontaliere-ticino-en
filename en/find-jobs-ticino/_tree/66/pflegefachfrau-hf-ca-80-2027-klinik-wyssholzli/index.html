Clinic Wysshölzli is a leading psychiatric and psychotherapeutic specialist clinic for women with addictions and eating disorders. Our location in the Oberaargau, Canton of Bern, in a quiet location convinces with wonderful nature and proximity to Bern and Olten. The clinic offers 37 inpatient treatment stations and two ambulatories. Assignments are made from all cantons of German-speaking Switzerland, the patients enter planned. We are looking for agreement a dipl. Nurse HF about 80% of your tasks

  • Crisis management
  • Assistance and support of students and trainees
  • Monitoring, monitoring and evaluation of the course of treatment

Your profile

  • Experience in the treatment of addictions and eating disorders is beneficial
