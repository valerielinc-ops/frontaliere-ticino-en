Full description

Your responsibilities

  • Case management in ambulatory patients: women in adulthood of the entire spectrum of mental illnesses within the framework of social psychiatric primary care under supervision and professional support
  • Taking over emergency services as part of our supply task
  • Make a valuable contribution to our interdisciplinary cooperation and open knowledge culture

Your skills

  • Started psychotherapeutic training as well as in the best case stationary psychiatric prior experience
  • Interest in socio-medical issues and innovative models of psychiatric care
  • Empathetic, creative and resilient personality with humor and a high level of teamwork
  • Patient-, team-, and network-oriented working methods
  • Great cooperation of all professional groups: Everyone talks about interdisciplinary and cross-professional cooperation - we do it too! Projects and working groups are deliberately put together in an interdisciplinary and professional manner. And we also consciously promote the informal exchange between professional groups through various activities.
  • Family, leisure and work (you): We offer you attractive working (time) models, the purchase of unpaid leave or the opportunity to work part-time in all areas, including in the squad. We are happy to respond to individual needs.
  • Working with quality of life (you): Working with a view to a unique mountain world? A bath in the lake at work? Unlimited outdoor activities on days off? Is it your desire to live and work in a great tourist and recreational region? We fulfill it to you!
  • Friendly working atmosphere (you): New employees already feel it on the first working day: With us you will be welcomed and supported. Even in everyday life you openly approach each other, greet each other and like to help. We are proud of the way employees treat each other and take care of it.

For questions about the job, please contact Dr. med. Tim Niemeyer, Chief Physician Psychiatry / Member PsychiatryHead.

About us

The hospital fmi AG with over 1,600 employees includes the two acute hospitals Interlaken and Frutigen, the psychiatry of hospital fmi AG, the health center in Meiringen, the senior centers in Frutigen and Interlaken, the emergency service with 5 locations, the walk-in clinic in Interlaken and various practices. As a modern hospital group, we provide outpatient and inpatient, multidisciplinary medical and psychiatric care in the eastern and central Bernese Oberland.

Location: Spital Frutigen, Spital Interlaken

The hospitals fmi AG include the acute hospitals Interlaken and Frutigen, the health center Meiringen, the senior parks Frutigen and Weissenau, the ambulance service and the walk-in clinic in the Bernese Oberland.
