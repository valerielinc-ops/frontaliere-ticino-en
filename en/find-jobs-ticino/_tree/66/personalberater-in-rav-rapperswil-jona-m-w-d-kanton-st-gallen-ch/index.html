Full description

The Regional Employment Centre (RAV) provides competent and comprehensive advice to job seekers, employers and other offices. It supports job seekers in their reintegration into the labour market with the measures of unemployment insurance. The focus is on rapid and permanent employment services.

What to expect

  • Advising and providing jobseekers
  • Conducting and recording consultations
  • Development and agreement of adequate reintegration strategies and qualification measures with job seekers
  • Cooperation with internal departments (logistics, labour market measures, legal service, employer service, etc.)
  • Checking compliance with legal requirements and initiating sanctions for abuses
  • Complete all administrative work related to the activities

What sets it apart

  • Basic commercial education or vocational apprenticeship with further commercial education
  • Bundesgenössischer Fachausweis as HR specialist, social security specialist or the willingness to undertake further training for HR specialist (direction B)
  • Professional experience from recruitment, recruitment consultancy, demanding customer advice or close connection to social security
  • Empathetic and solution-oriented communication with the most diverse interlocutors from different social and cultural circles
  • Flexibility and willingness to provide possible work assignments at the RAV sites Sargans and Wattwil

What we offer

  • High degree of independence and personal responsibility after professional training
  • Different working time models
  • Workstations in walking distance to the stations of the respective RAV locations
  • Discounted Ostwind company subscription
  • Modern infrastructure (multispace office)
