Full description

Migros Aare prepares you to run a Migros branch independently and successfully. The compact and practical training is individually tailored to you. Within 12-18 months, you will be fully prepared for your future vice leadership role. Your application will be considered for all branches in the vicinity of the desired workplace.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, MM Langenthal, Wiesenstrasse 28, 4900 Langenthal

Important information

Please submit CV, employment certificates and diplomas in full. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Customer-oriented management of the branch and of an area
  • Responsibility for freshness, quality and availability of products
  • Responsibility for the management and development of department managers, department managers and employees as well as for the promotion of young talent
  • Achieving revenue and cost targets
  • Ensuring the defined processes in your area

Requirements

  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade or equivalent training
  • Enthusiasm for the retail trade
  • You want to take responsibility and can already show initial leadership experience
  • Joy in leading and promoting employees
  • Strong customer orientation and convincing appearance
  • Readiness, resilience and flexibility
  • High social skills, team spirit and contact
  • Good PC knowledge
  • Ideally, more than 4 years of professional experience incl. First management experience
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Childcare: Generous financial cost sharing in external childcare services
  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks paternity leave
  • Mobility: participation of an annual subscription to public transport, discount on fuel (Migrol), reduced annual contribution for mobility carsharing and entitlement to a fleet discount
  • Mobile Subscriptions: Various Perks on Mobile Subscriptions
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest on capital or a discount on interest rates of newly concluded fixed mortgages
  • Employee offers: Preferential conditions for insurance and health insurance funds
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation on self-selected courses
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
  • Sports and fitness offerings: cost-sharing of subscriptions to over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, aquaparks
