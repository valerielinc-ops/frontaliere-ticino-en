Full description

Which optics have your future We are a global manufacturer of optical components and systems in the field of photonics. Our specialized teams meet the complex design and manufacturing needs of customers in the life sciences, advanced manufacturing and defense & aviation sectors, helping global leading brands meet their market potential. FISBA employs over 360 people from 30 nations worldwide. Field of work: For optical devices such as telescopes, microscopes, projection devices, medical diagnostic devices and astro-objectives, precision opticians produce lenses, prisms, plano- and round-optical components. They process the glass partly by hand, partly with modern computer-controlled machines (CNC), which they program, set up and also maintain. Accuracy and precision characterize the optical parts, therefore work is done on fractions of thousands of millimeters. Conditions:

  • Good eyesight without color problems, quiet hands
  • Joy of precision work
  • Technical understanding
  • Spatial imagination
  • Interest in Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics
  • Flexibility, creativity and teamwork
  • Good secondary school students or very good real school students
