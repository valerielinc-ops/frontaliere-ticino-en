The Position In the role of Laboratory Workflow Analyst (internally referred as Lab Workflow Consultant) , you will collaborate closely with project team members to deliver customer value and support laboratory consulting initiatives. Your responsibilities will include providing expert evaluation of laboratory workflow processes, anticipating customer requirements, and identifying areas for optimization based on mandatory key performance indicators (KPIs). You will design laboratory layouts, simulate diverse workflow scenarios, create customer presentations, and facilitate meetings and workshops to support the development and deployment of Roche solutions. Furthermore, you will assist customers with pre- and post-analytical instrument applications to ensure their configuration aligns with the recommended workflow design; in the context of total lab automation sites, this role may frequently overlap with project management responsibilities.

What you will be working on

As the Lab Workflow Consultant, you will

  • Conduct expert evaluations of customer workflow processes to pinpoint optimization areas that align with defined KPIs and client needs.
  • Coordinate with cross-functional internal teams to establish compelling customer value propositions across all stages of the Buying Cycle.
  • Advise and guide hospital clinicians on enhancing clinical decision support tools and refining the patient journey.
  • Model workflows and laboratory layouts, preparing client-facing presentations to support solution development and deployment.
  • Design customer proposals by leveraging Roche's advanced visualization and simulation tools, including turnaround time (TAT) models, 3D renderings, and virtual reality experiences.
  • Deliver client workshops, educational training sessions, product demonstrations, and presentations.
  • Oversee the deployment of benchmarking surveys and administer Roche's digital platform to share insights, case studies, and healthcare thought leadership.
  • Collaborate closely with the regional APAC Consulting Team to share best practices and ensure effective local service execution across affiliates.
  • Establish enduring, value-focused customer relationships by identifying evolving needs and continuously improving the user experience.
  • Work in tandem with clients and cross-functional teams to address operational demands and sustain excellent customer satisfaction.
  • Embody the traits of an agile leader, demonstrating a proactive growth mindset, curiosity, and a focus on continuous refinement.
  • Promote and safeguard organizational culture by modeling new methodologies and championing cross-functional collaboration to deliver exceptional customer experiences.
  • Lead and incorporate Lean Six Sigma methodologies into customer projects to deliver and quantify concrete benefits.
