Flagship Event Manager

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the planning and execution of flagship customer events.
  • Manage event timelines, milestones, and end-to-end delivery to ensure execution excellence.
  • Establish project governance and prepare and lead steering committee meetings.
  • Monitor project progress, manage risks and dependencies, and facilitate timely decision-making.
  • Align and coordinate cross-functional stakeholders, including Product Lines, Industries, Sales, and Regional MarCom teams.
  • Foster effective collaboration and manage project communications across all involved parties.
  • Manage event budgets, procurement activities, and agency selection and performance.
  • Monitor spending and forecasts while ensuring compliance with approved budgets.
  • Define and track KPIs, evaluate event performance and outcomes, and deliver post-event reporting.
  • Capture lessons learned and recommend improvements for future flagship events.

Competencies, Skills & Experience

Experience

  • Proven experience independently managing complex, large-scale events end-to-end, ideally B2B trade shows , exhibitions, or comparable multi-stakeholder programs
  • Demonstrated experience translating a strategic brief into a complete operational event plan and driving it through delivery
  • Experience managing multiple parallel workstreams with significant interdependencies
  • Proven ownership of substantial event budgets
  • Strong experience managing agencies and suppliers through production and delivery
  • Experience working in complex, international and cross-functional organizations
  • Experience in an industrial, technology or B2B environment would be a strong advantage

Skills

  • Advanced project and program management
  • Budget planning and cost management
  • Risk and dependency management
  • Agency briefing and performance management
  • Project governance and steering committee facilitation
  • Excellent stakeholder communication and coordination
  • Excellent written and verbal English
  • French and/or German would be an advantage

Competencies

  • High level of autonomy and ownership, with strong accountability for delivery
  • Ability to translate strategic direction into structured operational execution
  • Proactive and solution-oriented, able to anticipate risks and drive decisions
  • Strong stakeholder leadership, with the ability to align and mobilize cross-functional teams
  • Able to balance operational autonomy with strategic alignment, escalating with clear recommendations when needed
