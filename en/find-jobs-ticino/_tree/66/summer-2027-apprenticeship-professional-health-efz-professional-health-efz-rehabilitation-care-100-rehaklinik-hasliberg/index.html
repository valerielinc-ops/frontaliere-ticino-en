Full description

The Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of internistic-oncological, psychosomatic and musculoskeletal rehabilitation. The clinic has 83 beds and is located on the hospital lists of the cantons of Bern, Lucerne, Solothurn, Nidwalden and Uri. The ISO-certified company works process-oriented and interdisciplinary, based on the guiding principle of "REHA competence", with the characteristics: respect, efficiency, cordiality, attention and competence.

Tasks As a specialist health care specialist, EFZ supports, supports and cares for people. In addition, you take over domestic and administrative-logistical as well as medical-technical tasks. We offer you a comprehensive, varied training through a competent educational team in an interesting field of work. In addition, you get valuable insights into the diverse areas of our institution in the form of short internships. With the successful completion of this training, you have a versatile career field open to you.

Among other things, you have the opportunity to obtain a degree in the subject of HF / of HF.
