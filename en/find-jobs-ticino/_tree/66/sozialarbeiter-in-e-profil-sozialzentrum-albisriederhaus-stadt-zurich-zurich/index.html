Full description

Do you have experience in social welfare or adult protection? That is a good basis. Due to the temporary employment, it is possible to make your tasks and case allocation flexible. We focus on your expertise and interests. They do not have to be equally saddle-resistant in all areas.

For our committed team in the social centre Albisriederhaus we are looking for a reinforcement immediately or by agreement. The employment is limited until the end of December 2027.

  • They advise and accompany adults in economic welfare and/or in the protection of adults.
  • You take responsibility for your cases, make professional assessments and coordinate the necessary next steps.
  • They work closely with administrators, specialist offices and external partners.
  • They move between advice, legal mandate and different interests – and keep an overview.
  • You manage your cases independently and ensure careful and comprehensible documentation.
  • University of Applied Sciences degree in Social Work
  • Experience in economic welfare and/or adult protection
  • Commercial flair and good knowledge of financial topics
  • Rapid comprehension and security in dealing with complex situations
  • Sovereignty, clarity and a professional approach to different concerns and expectations
  • A strong team: You work in a dedicated team in which mutual support, professional exchange and the sharing of experiences are a matter of course.
  • Together on site, flexible in everyday life: You work in modern open-plan offices and benefit from flexible working models and the opportunity to do some of your work in the home office.
  • Insight with a future: You get to know the social services of the city of Zurich. As a large employer, the city always offers opportunities for further employment.

In the social services more than 1000 employees shape the social Zurich. We accompany and support people in different situations, promote participation and strengthen social cohesion. Our services include social assistance, child and adult protection, school social work, child and youth welfare and socio-culture. We do our work interdisciplinary and throughout the city: in school buildings, counselling centres, the five social centres as well as in facilities such as the Youth and Culture House Dynamo, the Debate House Karl der Grosse and the district centres Schütze and Bakeranlage.
