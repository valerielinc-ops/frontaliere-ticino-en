The Anker Swiss Group, born in April 2021, is growing and is now also present in ICTINO.

Do you want to be part of a future-oriented society?

On behalf of our esteemed customer, we are looking for the following professional figure immediately:

100% PIASTRELLIST

Work zone: Mendrisiotto *

Main handling

  • Pose of ceramic tiles, porcelain stoneware, marble, mosaic and natural stone.
  • Preparation and leveling of laying surfaces.
  • Execution of coating works for bathrooms, kitchens, terraces and commercial surfaces.
  • Cutting, shaping and adapting materials according to design.
  • Realization of escaping, sealing and fine finishes.
  • Reading and interpretation of technical plans and drawings.
  • Pose of large sizes and decorative coatings.
  • Removing and replacing existing floors and coverings.
  • Control of quotas, alignment and quality of finishes.
  • Safe use of industry-specific equipment and machinery.

Skills required

  • Proven experience as a tile player in civil and/or commercial yards.
  • Excellent laying capacity for flooring and coverings in gres, ceramics and natural stone.
  • Ability to perform precision work and high quality finishes.
  • Autonomy in the preparation of the bottom and in the laying operations.
  • Good reading of the technical drawing.
  • Great craftsmanship and attention to details.
  • Ability to work both independently and on the team.

Preferential requirements

  • Experience gained in the Swiss market.
  • Vocational training in the building or business sector.
  • Experience in laying large formats and mosaics.
  • Knowledge of different adhesives, sealants and modern laying systems.
  • Driving license category B and half.
  • Immediate availability.

We offer

  • Insertion into a firm and structured company.
  • Professional and dynamic work environment.
  • Long-term employment.
  • Interesting wage conditions tailored to the experience gained.
  • Constant support from the Anker Swiss team.

If you're a qualified Piastrellist with experience in laying floors and coverings and wishing to become part of a growing reality, send your application OGGI STESSO! *

The applications of professionals with proven experience in laying porcelain stoneware, ceramics, natural stone, mosaics and large sizes will be taken into consideration with priority.
