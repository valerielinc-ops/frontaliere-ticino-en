Carpenter CFC (M/F) back to list To strengthen our teams, we are looking for a CFC carpenter (M/F) Job description

Your mission

  • Manufacture various custom wood articles in the workshop, according to plans and specifications
  • Read and interpret technical plans, drawings and specifications
  • Choose materials suitable for each production
  • Conduct on-site measurements before manufacture
  • Perform flow, machining, assembly and assembly of parts on conventional and/or numerically controlled (CNC) machines
  • Installation and installation of on-site works
  • Control the quality of manufactured parts and laid structures and make necessary adjustments
  • Working with other trades on site

Profile

Profile desired

  • Holder of a carpenter's CFC, required
  • Prior experience in a similar position, workshop and/or on site in Switzerland
  • Good command of reading plans and tracing techniques
  • Knowledge of different wood species and their properties
  • Control of conventional wood machines
  • Good knowledge of CNC machines, an asset
  • Manual skill, precision and attention to detail
  • Good physical condition
  • Organizational sense and ability to work independently as a team
  • Driver's licence category B, an asset
