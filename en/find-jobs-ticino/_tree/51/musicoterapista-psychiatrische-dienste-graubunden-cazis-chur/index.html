Full description

Your environment: You will work as a holiday replacement in the Therapeutic Service of Clinic Waldhaus in Chur and Clinic Beverin in Cazis. You will experience a collegial environment with good collaboration and exchange. ## Your tasks

  • You will take over the holiday replacements of our music therapists
  • You will accompany mentally ill people in music therapy
  • You will bring musical skills in combination with solid music therapeutic expertise into your work
  • You will plan group activities or individual therapies and orient yourself to the treatment goals of the patients
  • You will network and work interprofessionally together
  • You will maintain an open, individual and person-centered communication ## Our requirements profile
  • You have a completed training as a music therapist (art therapist, specialization in music therapy or MAS in clinical music therapy)
  • You have experience in the guidance of mentally ill people
  • You are an innovative, creative and self-reliant personality
  • It brings you joy to work in different teams ## We offer you
  • You will take on a varied and responsible task
  • You can bring in your ideas and shape your field of activity
  • You will experience a diverse and cooperative collaboration with all professions
  • Use our internal training and further education offer – we also support your external training needs
  • With part-time working models and unpaid leave, an optimal work-life balance
