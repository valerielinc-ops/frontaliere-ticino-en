Full description

As part of the organisation of the Christmas Village of Sierre, we are looking for an Operational Manager to ensure the coordination and smooth running of the event on the ground. A real relay between the management, the teams and the various stakeholders, you will be at the heart of the daily operation of the Christmas Village. Your mission: to ensure the proper operation of facilities and activities, anticipate needs, coordinate stakeholders and contribute to providing a quality experience to visitors and partners. Your missions

  • Supervise and coordinate daily operations throughout the Christmas Village.
  • Ensure the proper functioning of the various spaces: chalets, catering, dome, bars and entertainment.
  • Organize and coordinate the work of the operational teams.
  • Be the preferred interlocutor for exhibitors, suppliers, suppliers and partners in the field.
  • Monitor logistical, material and technical requirements in collaboration with relevant stakeholders.
  • Coordinate the preparation, opening and daily closure of the site.
  • Anticipate difficulties and provide quick and adapted solutions.
  • Ensure the application of safety instructions and operating procedures, in collaboration with relevant officials.
  • Ensure effective communication with management and provide important information.
  • Ensure smooth organisation and optimal reception quality.

Your profile

  • Experience confirmed in the operational management of public events, festivals or events.
  • Excellent organizational, anticipation and priority management capabilities.
  • Leadership, autonomy and responsibility.
  • Dynamic, versatile and solutions oriented personality.
  • Excellent relational skills and ability to coordinate multiple contacts.
  • Good stress resistance and ability to take initiatives.
  • Ability to manage contingencies and situations requiring rapid response.
  • Availability to work according to a schedule including evenings and weekends.

An important asset is experience in operating Christmas markets, festivals or public events.

What We Offer

  • A fixed-term contract for the period of the Christmas Village of Sierre.
  • An undertaking subject to the National Collective Labour Agreement (NCTC) of the hotel-restoration.
  • Compensation adapted to the experience, qualifications and responsibilities of the position, in accordance with the CCTT.
  • A position with responsibilities with real operational autonomy.
  • Direct collaboration with the event management.
  • A dynamic business environment at the heart of a major event in the region.
