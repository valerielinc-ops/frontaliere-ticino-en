Full description

The Klubschule Migros is the largest provider of adult education in Switzerland. The Genossenschaft Migros Ostschweiz operates 7 Klubschulen, where our customers are offered over 600 different courses and training programs in a pleasant learning atmosphere.

Tasks

  • You design the lessons for the Dot Painting offer professionally according to adult education criteria, taking into account the different life experiences, learning styles, and needs of the participants
  • Comprehensive preparation and follow-up of the lessons are also part of your tasks
  • Your joy in teaching, your commitment, and your winning demeanor inspire the participants
  • Through regular coordination with the responsible contact persons of the Klubschule administration, you ensure smooth cooperation

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in the field of adult education as well as in the corresponding subject area
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): Ideally, you have the SVEB Certificate 1 or an equivalent qualification
  • You are characterized by self-reflection and empathy towards the participants
  • You carry out your work responsibly and independently
  • You communicate clearly, effectively, and respectfully
  • You bring a relevant professional qualification in acrylic painting
  • German (very good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Fair wages: Attractive employment conditions as well as certified equal pay
  • Insurance: Advantageous conditions at various insurance companies
  • Mobile subscription: Heavily discounted conditions on private mobile subscriptions also for family and friends
  • Cumulus points: Multiple Cumulus points on food and non-food items
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers and events
