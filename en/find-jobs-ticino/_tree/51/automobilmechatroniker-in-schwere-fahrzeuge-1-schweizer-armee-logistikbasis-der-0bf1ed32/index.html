Automotive mechatronics technician/\-in heavy vehicles 1

Chur \| 80\-100%\ |

You can make this contribution

  • Conduct diagnostics, repair and adjustment work on engine, drive assemblies, brakes, steering, chassis, subassemblies and safety/comfort electronics
  • Check and repair defects and repairs on non-marching vehicles
  • Perform revisions and replacement of individual assemblies
  • Provide technical support for material transfers and material returns with regard to driving safety, accident prevention, maintenance, cleaning and vehicle inventory
  • Record defect messages and spare parts in the SAP\ system
  • Caring for learners

This makes you unique

  • Completed vocational training as an automotive mechatronics specialist, specialisation in commercial vehicles or related professions
  • Cat B driving licence (cat C, CE and SUVA\-approved forklift)
  • Self-employed, forward working methods with good organizational skills
  • Pronounced service concept in contact with and in advising customers, for example the troop
  • Mutual support in the team is important to us

To the point

Do you enjoy engines and military vehicles and would like to benefit from a pronounced work\-life\-balance? Together as a team, you will help ensure that our bicycle and commercial vehicles for the Swiss Army are functional and ready for deployment.

This is what we offer

  • Work for Switzerland We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom

The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information

Employing the Swiss Army makes sense and brings many advantages. Convince yourself.
