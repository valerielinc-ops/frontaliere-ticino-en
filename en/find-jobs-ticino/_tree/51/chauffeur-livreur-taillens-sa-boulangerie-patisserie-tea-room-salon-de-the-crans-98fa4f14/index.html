Full description

To complete our delivery team we are looking for a 100% year round position, a Deliverer

Who are We? A family-owned company founded in 1943, Taillens has been transmitting its artisanal know-how in Valais for four generations. Bakery, pastry, confectionery and tea-rooms are part of our daily lives. The quality of our products extends in the care given to their delivery and the relationship with our customers.

Profile Wanted

  • -A valid driving licence
  • -A careful conduct and constant attention to the safety and care of the goods.
  • -Punctuality, reliability and the sense of priorities to organize tours and manage unforeseen events.
  • -The availability to work in staggered hours.
  • -Easy with handling and loading, in compliance with safety instructions.
  • -A good understanding of French to follow instructions and exchange with teams and clients.
  • -A spirit of initiative and natural ease to accompany your colleagues on a daily basis.
  • -Good stress management during periods of high activity
  • -Experience in delivery and knowledge of the region are essential
  • -The versatility needed to participate in other business activities in the low season

Main missions

  • -Contribute to the organization of tours, prepare orders and verify quantities and delivery documents.
  • - Charge and organize the products in the vehicle while preserving their quality.
  • -Ensure deliveries to our outlets and customers, according to schedule.
  • - Check the delivery of orders and promptly transmit any anomalies or particular requests.
  • -Manage return and return of containers according to company instructions.
  • -Maintain the vehicle clean, perform the usual checks and report any problems.
  • -Apply hygiene and product conservation instructions throughout the tour.
  • -Facilitate team coordination, transmit instructions and accompany new colleagues.

We offer

  • -An active and varied position within a family enterprise attached to the job.
  • -A schedule organized in advance to better organize your daily life.
  • Discounts in our outlets.
  • -A staff assistance programme available 24 hours a day and 7 days a week.
