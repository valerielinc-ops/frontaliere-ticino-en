# Stagist 52 weeks

The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI), puts in competition some places

Stage 52 weeks

at the Department of Business Economics, Health and Social (DEASS) and Human Resources (DIR) in Manno and the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI) in Lugano-Viganello. Degree of employment: 80%-100%. Entry into operation: July-August 2027, to be agreed.

Scope and purpose of position

The internship is intended exclusively for students currently enrolled in the third year of a Swiss Business Professional Centre, which from summer 2027 will have to carry out a year of professional practice aimed at obtaining the Federal Certificate of Capacity with professional maturity.

Responsibility and activity

The selected person will be involved mainly in administrative support activities, operating independently and under the guidance of our trainers/trainers. It will have the opportunity to concretely apply the theoretical skills acquired during the business journey, contributing to the achievement of the expected formative objectives.

Main activities include

  • Management and coordination of incoming and outgoing mail, calls and communications via email.
  • Administrative processing of invoices.
  • Management of stationery and office material.
  • Use of internal management systems for managing databases, documentation and archives.
  • Reservation of meeting rooms and classrooms.
  • Support for the promotion and organization of SUPSI events and initiatives.
  • Administrative and organizational support for different units (Direction, Communication Service, Human Resource Service, Basic and Continuous Training Secretariat, etc.) in the activities of competence.
  • Welcome, assistance and orientation of guests.
  • Management of requests from students, collaborators and external guests, ensuring a professional and customer care service oriented to the needs of the user.

Requirements

  • Transversal skills: precision, sense of organization and reliability, combined with good communication and relational skills. Prepared for teamwork and collaboration, as well as the ability to operate independently and responsiblely.
  • Digital skills: good knowledge in the use of computer programs and new technologies.
  • Language skills: excellent knowledge of the Italian language, school knowledge of a second national language and/or English.

SUPSI offers

  • A dynamic and changing working environment.
  • Entering a participatory work team.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
