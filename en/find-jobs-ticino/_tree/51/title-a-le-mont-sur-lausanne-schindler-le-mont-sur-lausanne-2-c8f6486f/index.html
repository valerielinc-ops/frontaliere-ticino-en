We are AS Lifts

As the second largest elevator company in Switzerland with 800 employees and more than 30 apprentices, we went to the individuality of an SME to the perspectives and safety of a large company. Ast offers an ideal start in working life. With us, you will receive a solid and varied training with professional support. After their apprenticeship, we offer talented graduates interesting opportunities for further training and career development.

In our company, we offer

SWC Trade Employee Learning for 2027 at Le Mont-sur-Lausanne

What are your tasks?

  • Self-execution of administrative tasks in different departments
  • An overview of areas such as contract management, product trade, sales and administration
  • Comprehensive and versatile training, supervised by experienced professionals
  • The possibility of practicing foreign languages through contacts with apprentices from other language regions

What are the requirements?

  • Autonomy, reliability and willingness to learn
  • A good team spirit and a sense of contact
  • Very good knowledge in French, both oral and written
  • Interest in trade relations
  • Good to very good grades in high school

Your benefits at home

  • A week of introduction for the start of your apprenticeship
  • Above average number of holiday days
  • Financial contribution for public transport subscriptions
  • Discounts for sport and fitness with selected partners
  • ... among other benefits that you can enjoy, and that you will find here
