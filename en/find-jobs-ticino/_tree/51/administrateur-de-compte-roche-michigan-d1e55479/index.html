The Opportunity

The Roche Core Lab Account Executive (AE) is responsible for meeting and exceeding sales objectives for Roche Core Lab (Chemistry and Immunoassay) in targeted accounts across the specified territory. The AE must develop customer prospects, create, and maintain relationships with decision-makers across the laboratory and key laboratory stakeholders. The AE visits existing and potential customers and works to build and maintain a network of colleagues, partners, and customers to share information and obtain prospects to grow Roche core lab business across the assigned territory. The AE works closely with an extended team to develop relationships and navigate IHNs, create successful sales strategies, provide technical and produce support for existing customers, grow reagent revenue, expand market share and create a positive customer experience. The AE often receives little instruction on day-to-day work, is provided general instructions on new assignments, and is expected to actively seek additional instruction when needed. The AE is responsible for the sales strategy development and achievement of the desired revenue, customer satisfaction, retention, and acquisition goals for Clinical Chemistry and Immunoassay instruments, Automation, and Middleware Solutions within assigned territory. Success requires a strategic, goal-oriented professional who thrives in a team environment and values lifelong learning.

This is a field based role the territory covers Michigan. You must live within 50 miles of the territory with ability to travel throughout the territory for customer meetings.

  • Contact new and existing customers to meet and exceed sales objectives in targeted accounts, by developing new business, growing existing business, maintaining customer relationships, and troubleshooting customer problems. This includes:
  • Conducting and organizing necessary territory planning and routing for efficiency. Conducts business analysis and planning.
  • Actively using Miller Heiman and other sales tools to ensure team alignment and customer-centric focus
  • Selling the primary differentiation of Roche products, overcoming and managing objections, and employing product knowledge to establish competitive barriers to market penetration by positioning Roche Professional Diagnostic's solutions.
  • Generating demand and maximizing product awareness by working with and providing education and information to customers.
  • Assist customers in achieving defined business/technical and personal goals, providing strategic business reviews coordinating services reviews and enhancing the customer experience.
  • Work cross-functionally with internal colleagues and peers to leverage the skills, experiences and perspectives of the larger team in order to increase sales and maintain customer base.
  • Work on problems of varying scope where analysis of data requires evaluation of identifiable factors. Demonstrates good judgment in selecting methods and techniques for obtaining solutions. Networks with senior internal and external personnel in own area of expertise.
