Full description

As a specialist in gynecology and obstetrics with a focus on gynecological endocrinology and reproductive medicine, you take on a central medical function in a specialized fertility center in the Lucerne region. In addition to the independent clarification and treatment of couples with unfulfilled desire to have children, as deputy medical director you bear joint responsibility for medical quality, professional standards and the further development of the center. The position is to be filled immediately or by agreement in a charge of 50 to 100%. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • Independent performance of investigations and treatments in the entire spectrum of gynecological endocrinology and reproductive medicine
  • Medical care for couples with unfulfilled desire to have children
  • Co-responsibility for the professional and strategic development of the fertility centre
  • Ensuring a high medical quality standard and a pronounced service orientation
  • Close cooperation with the medical management and the interdisciplinary team
  • Co-design of innovative treatment concepts and care models
  • Participation in the future direction and development of the Centre
  • Cooperation in a highly specialized field of reproductive medicine

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed specialist training in gynecology and obstetrics
  • Several years of experience in reproductive medicine and fertility treatment
  • High level of professional and social competence
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation and network maintenance
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

That sets them apart.

  • Entrepreneurship and willingness to innovate
  • High sense of responsibility and empathy
  • Objective-oriented and structured working method
  • Team-oriented leadership style
  • Flexibility and willingness

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Guide function with a high degree of design latitude
  • Possibility to actively shape the future of a fertility center
  • Highly specialized medical environment
  • Modern infrastructure and innovative treatment options
  • Attractive training opportunities
  • Progressive social benefits and attractive employment conditions
  • Contribution to mobility costs and other employee offers

Additionally for applicants from the EU
