Medico Caposervice (with possibility of development in the role) in Internal Medicine - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of more than 41,000 patients per year and providing more than 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team at the Department of Internal Medicine of the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona and Valli (ORBV), home of Bellinzona, we look for a (a) Medical Chief Service (with possibility of evolution in the role) in Internal Medicine (100%) The Department of Internal Medicine of the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona and Valli (sedi di Bellinzona, Faido e Acquarossa) is an integral part of the Cantonal Hospital (www.eoc.ch). It is recognized as a professional training center, category A, with a wide multidisciplinary case and involves employees, as well as in the field of care, even in those of research and training. The peripheral hospitals located in Leventina (Faido) and Blenio valley (Acquarossa) complete the profile of the case in a dynamic of greater autonomy. The medical team consists of 35 assistant doctors, 12 clinical chiefs and numerous superiors belonging to different disciplines of internal medicine. Profile: The doctor we seek has professional experience and autonomy, as well as motivation, own initiative and spirit of collaboration. He believes, like us, in the importance of putting the patient and his family at the center of all our attentions.

  • Requirements: The person we are looking for will have to own the medical title and a specialization title in Internal Medicine (for foreign titles necessary MEBEKO recognition already obtained before the beginning of the activity).
