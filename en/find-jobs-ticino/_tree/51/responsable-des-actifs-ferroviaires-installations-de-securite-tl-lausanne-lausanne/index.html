Rail asset manager (safety installations)

  • Your Mission

Within the Rail Directorate, you are responsible for the management of railway assets within your perimeter and ensure their sustainability, availability and performance, in accordance with safety requirements and the regulatory framework.

As such, you define and guide strategies for the maintenance, renewal and evolution of railway infrastructure and equipment, taking into account regulatory, operational and financial requirements.

You identify investment needs, contribute to budget planning, and track financial resources allocated to your perimeter. You analyze the condition and performance of the facilities in order to propose the measures necessary to maintain and improve them.

In close collaboration with the technical teams, maintenance, operation and the various internal and external partners, you coordinate the actions necessary for the proper management of the assets and contribute to the projects of modernization and development of the network.

You also ensure compliance with the legal, regulatory and normative requirements applicable to rail infrastructure and the technological watch of the field

  • Your profile

For the benefit of advanced technical training (HES, EPF or equivalent), ideally in the field of railway engineering or electricity, you have confirmed experience in the management of technical assets or infrastructure, ideally acquired in the railway sector.

You have strong knowledge of the issues related to maintaining the field's operational conditions, renewal and life cycle management of technical facilities. A good understanding of the Swiss railway regulatory environment is an important asset.

Recognized for your analytical capacity, overall vision and sense of responsibility, you know how to define priorities, anticipate needs and make relevant recommendations.

With excellent relational skills, you appreciate cross-cutting work and know how to work with people with diverse profiles. Your autonomy, rigor and orientation solutions allow you to evolve efficiently in a demanding technical environment.
