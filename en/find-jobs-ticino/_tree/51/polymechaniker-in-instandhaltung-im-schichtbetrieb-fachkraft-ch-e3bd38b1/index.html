Full description

Activities

  • You are responsible for the maintenance and revision of our production machines as well as of buildings and infrastructures.
  • You produce spare parts and assemblies independently.
  • You carry out systematic troubleshooting and repair technical problems on production machines.
  • You optimize wear parts and machines to continuously improve their reliability and performance.
  • You implement technical improvements to machines and systems.
  • You control and service our process control systems.
  • As a professional trainer you accompany and support our prospective production and polymechanics EFZ.

Requirements

  • Completed training as polymechanic, production mechanic or comparable technical training.
  • Good practical knowledge in the production techniques drilling, turning and CNC-freaks.
  • Professional experience in maintenance and in the maintenance of operations is of advantage.
  • High independence and self-motivation as well as a structured and solution-oriented working method.
  • High networked thinking and technical understanding.
  • Good German knowledge in word and writing; technical English understanding is of benefit.
  • willingness to work shifts and to make pike-ins. - good user knowledge in the current IT programs. - completed vocational training course.
