Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors. The Operations business unit produces parts for all Hamilton companies, from injection molded parts and pipette tips for liquid handling to metal and plastic parts. Our aim is to produce as many parts as possible in-house. This keeps us agile, independent and efficient. ## Mansioni

  • Operational implementation of lean initiatives directly in assembly and production
  • Active conversion and further development of existing workstations according to lean principles
  • Design and introduction of tool and material holders (5S, ergonomics, material flow)
  • Definition, visualization and optimization of the assembly sequence
  • Introduction and maintenance of standards (workstation layouts, labeling, visual guidance)
  • Implementation and coordination of changes during ongoing operations
  • Supporting the Lean Project Manager in the planning, implementation and follow-up of measures
  • Close cooperation with Production, Manufacturing Engineering, Purchasing, Logistics and Quality
  • Ensuring the sustainability of the implemented improvements (CIP / continuous development) ##
