Customer consultant / sales assistant / hearing acoustics (m/f/d), Landquart, (CH)

You want to make a difference. Then shape the future of hearing with us

Are you looking for a meaningful task where people, medicine and technology come together? With us you accompany people on the way to better hearing and a better quality of life.

We offer you the opportunity to contribute your strengths, learn new things and develop in a future-proof industry. Together we make an audible difference every day.

Become a customer consultant / sales assistant\in hearing acoustics (m/f/d) at Neuroth *

Penum 100 %

Location: Landquart

This is waiting for you

With your organizational talent, your service orientation and your commitment, you contribute significantly to a positive customer experience and a smooth listening center everyday life.

  • You support our customers by phone and personally, make appointments and provide first-class customer support.
  • You coordinate the schedule and perform administrative tasks as well as the cash management independently.
  • You perform service work on hearing aids and advise on accessories and additional products.
  • You support our hearing care professionals with hearing tests, ear impressions, hearing aid controls and annual services.
  • You take care of the warehouse management including orders, goods acceptance and control.

You bring that with you

You are communicative, service-oriented and enjoy dealing with people:

  • Enjoyment in sales and personal advice - ideally with experience in retail or retail trade.
  • Strong customer and service orientation as well as a high degree of empathy.
  • Independent, reliable and responsible working methods.
  • Interest in technical and electronic products as well as a good IT affinity.
  • Communication strength, team spirit and the motivation to continuously develop.
  • Confident knowledge of German in words and writing.

This is what we offer you

At Neuroth you will not only find one, but a task with meaning and real perspectives:

  • A start to a task where you help people to a better quality of life
  • Individual development that really suits you \- with exciting perspectives
  • 5 weeks holidays and scheduled working hours for your everyday life
  • Secure workplace in an internationally successful company with a future
  • Attractive compensation combined with versatile benefits and employee offers
