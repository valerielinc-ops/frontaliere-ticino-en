Full description

Portrait You are considering a job change for the new semester, come back from a holiday or would like to take over a great class from February, then contact us! We look forward to your application.

Welcome to the Belp Schools - help shape learning. At the primary school Mühlematt we accompany around 300 children in twelve standard classes and three kindergartens. We actively develop our school – in an appreciative and reliable environment that is open to educational innovations. The annual school shows; in our community ideas must not only emerge, but also become reality.

Tasks

Per 15 February 2027 we are looking for a class teacher in the school house Mühlematt.

  • From Monday to Thursday noon you will teach all subjects except music, French and English.
  • As a class teacher, you take over class 5b with 18 students.
  • Together with the step and specialist teams, you plan the next learning steps, exchange ideas about the learning development of the children and bring your perspectives to schoolhouse conferences.

Requirements

  • You have a big heart for kids this age.
  • You have completed training as a teacher for the appropriate level.
  • Cooperation and agreements with the other LPs are important to you.
  • An appreciative communication with the children, the parents and the team is a matter of course for you.
  • You bring an openness to new things and have the willingness to actively shape our school.

We offer

  • With us, a helpful team awaits you, which is characterized by a strong team spirit.
  • You get space to develop pedagogical ideas.
  • As a manageable school, we are characterized by short paths and the support of the community.
  • For your work, a modern IT infrastructure and the school administration platform Escola are available.
