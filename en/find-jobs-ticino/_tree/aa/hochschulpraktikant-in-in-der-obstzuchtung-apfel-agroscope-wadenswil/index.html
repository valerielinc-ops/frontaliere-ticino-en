Put to the Point

Our goal: Progress in production thanks to location-adapted varieties for sustainable fruit cultivation of the future. The university internship enables participation in apple breeding and other projects (pear breeding, genetic genetic resources) as well as an insight into related topics such as phytopathology, storage, sensory, phenotyping, etc.

Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.

We offer this

We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population. Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring different perspectives. We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Additional information

New, innovative fruit varieties combine the highest quality requirements of production, downstream stages and consumers with high productivity and robustness. This is the aim of fruit breeding at Agroscope and, together with its partner organisation VariCom, organises the breeding accordingly. In this context, Agroscope leads and develops breeding programmes for apple, apricot and new pear breeding.
