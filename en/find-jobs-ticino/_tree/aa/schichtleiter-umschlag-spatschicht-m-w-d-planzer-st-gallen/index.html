Full description

Planzer Paket stands for reliable and fast parcel deliveries in Switzerland. As part of our team, you actively contribute to further developing our high quality standards and service. We offer you a varied job in a future-oriented company that focuses on sustainable solutions. Become part of our team and shape the future of the package world!

What you move

  • You take over the leadership of a team of up to 20 people and, in cooperation with the shift manager team, provide an envelope for efficient planning
  • A smooth and efficient shift operation in the package envelope is your responsibility
  • You actively help with loading and unloading vehicles
  • In the case of non-compliant delivery of goods, you hold reservations
  • You ensure order, discipline in the envelope and ensure that work safety is correctly implemented in your area

Why do you succeed?

  • You have already gained experience in leadership, ideally in the transport industry
  • Working at night when others are sleeping is not a problem for you. Your working hours are from about 22:00 to about 08:00
  • You have successfully passed the truck exam (R1, R2 and S2)
  • Resilience, independence, teamwork and initiative are among your greatest strengths
  • A reliable and correct way of working is a matter of course for you
  • Thanks to your good physical condition, flexibility and organizational talent, you always have an overview
  • You express yourself safely in word and writing in German and use your good PC user skills daily

Your Benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop yourself - we promote and challenge talents
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
