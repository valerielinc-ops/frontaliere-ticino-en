Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The University of Economics (HSW-FR) Freiburg is a member of the University of Applied Sciences in Western Switzerland (HES-SO) and offers bi- and trilingual training at bachelor and master level.

Become part of our team!

HES-SO//FR has an equal opportunities employment policy. We focus on the complementarity of competences and the diversity of perspectives, because it is the wide range of careers and profiles that makes up the strength of the state of Freiburg. We promote the reconciliation of professional and private life as well as flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link.

Their tasks

  • Research & Innovation: Conception and implementation of projects of application-oriented research on the topics of anchoring sustainability in companies, impact/social entrepreneurship and circular economy in cooperation with partner institutes and laboratories. Writing and publishing articles in scientific journals
  • Institutional strategy: Implementation of the university’s sustainability strategy and coordination of activities with institutional partners
  • Teaching: Co-design and implementation of teaching modules on sustainability as well as management of awareness-raising workshops
  • Monitoring & network maintenance: ensuring regulatory and scientific monitoring as well as representation of the university in national and international networks

Desired profile

  • Master’s degree in economic, social or management sciences with a focus on sustainability or equivalent education
  • Strong interest in writing a doctoral thesis
  • First successful experience in the management of research projects and/or in strategic consulting in the field of CSR/sustainability in the public, private or academic sector
  • In-depth knowledge of environmental data analysis tools (ESG assessment, LCA, carbon footprinting, etc.)
  • Excellent competence in transdisciplinary work, ability to synthesise and an interest in sustainability values
  • Teamwork, organisational skills, independence and flexibility
  • Master of the German or French language and good knowledge of the other language, as well as very good knowledge of English
