Full description

In this role, you will take over the technical, financial and scheduling management of demanding projects in the area of security systems.

Tasks

  • Independent processing of demanding and complex projects in the field of security systems
  • Technical, financial and scheduled project management
  • Operational planning and organisation of material resources
  • Coordination of cooperation with customers and various contacts
  • Supporting the sales team in adjusting offers and maintenance programs
  • Development of processes and use cases in the concept and execution phase
  • Managing process and change management throughout the project cycle

  • Negotiation skills, reliability and independent working methods

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
