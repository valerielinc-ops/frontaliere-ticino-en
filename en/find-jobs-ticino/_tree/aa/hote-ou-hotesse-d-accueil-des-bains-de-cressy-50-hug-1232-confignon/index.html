Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The post is attached to the Department of Operations. The mission of this department is to ensure the stewardship and logistics necessary for the proper functioning of all HUGs, in the restoration, cleaning and disinfection of premises and hygiene of linen. It also manages the sterilization service, the biomedical service and equipment as well as Cressy-Santé. More than 1'300 professionals and professionals carry out their missions on a daily basis with a constant concern for requirements, with a sense of service and environmental responsibility. The Cressy Santé centre, the only balneotherapy centre in Geneva that receives people with reduced mobility for rehabilitation and rehabilitation, welcomes people who want to treat themselves and relax in a vast area of well-being. It has an area of 2400 m2 and includes, among others, a basin of 300 m2, a large jacuzzi, a rest room, a sauna, a hammam, a odorium, a fitness and a rehabilitation and physiotherapy pool of 27 m2. All Cressy Santé facilities are designed to be accessible to people with reduced mobility.

Description of post

As part of your mission, you welcome and inform all those who come to the reception of the Baths of Cressy (patient and heteroclite clientele). You have the task of cashing in entries, subscriptions and any other services (numerary, credit cards, gift vouchers). You ensure the hotline as well as the appointment of external providers such as aestheticians, massagers and masseuses, etc. and you set up the wellness rooms. You advise, promote and sell the various services of the Bains de Cressy and carry out various administrative tasks (accounting and billing, production of statistics, management of the parking system, management of stock and shop). You have great stress resistance, excellent ability to concentrate and cope with unforeseen events and proven versatility for a multi-tasking shift. You are able to defuse complex and/or conflicting situations with a heterogeneous clientele.

Qualifications

You benefit from an SWC of an employee or business employee, a diploma from a hotel or tourism school or a diploma deemed equivalent. You are comfortable with the IT tools, especially with the GTS application for managing the Baths (sale, cashing, billing, customer/patient flow management, cash management, management of external provider calendars) and the GatRelax application for managing the lockers. You have a thorough knowledge of the services of a health centre (physiotherapy, rehabilitation) and well-being (balneotherapy, massages and aesthetics). You have perfect command of the French language, both oral and written. Proficiency in English and another foreign language is an asset. You have an experience of at least three years in a reception and reception function with cashier's outfit.
