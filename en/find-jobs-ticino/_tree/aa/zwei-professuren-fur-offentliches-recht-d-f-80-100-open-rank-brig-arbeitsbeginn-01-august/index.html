· Job vacancies Place of work: Brig | Start of work: 01. August 2027 or by arrangement The FernUni Switzerland stands for completely online university courses and is accredited according to the HFKG. It combines flexible, location-independent distance learning part-time with an active, interdisciplinary research culture at Campus Brig. In five faculties – Law, Psychology, Economics, History and Mathematics & Computer Science – we have been shaping modern teaching, learning and research for more than 30 years and count over 2,700 students and around 550 employees. To strengthen teaching and research activities at the Faculty of Law, we are looking for Two professorships in public law (D/F, 80–100%, open rank) Place of work: Brig Start of work : 01. August 2027 or by arrangement Two professorships will be filled under this call. One professorship is filled with a German-speaking or bilingual personality, the other with a French-speaking or bilingual personality. Their tasks

  • Research at the highest level of excellence in Swiss public law with a specialisation in current and future transformation fields such as new technologies, sustainable development or digital learning and/or using innovative research methods
  • Promotion of young scientists
  • Supervision of bachelor, master and doctoral theses
  • Acquisition of third-party funds
  • Teaching in public law at bachelor and master level
  • Development and implementation of innovative teaching methods in distance learning
  • Establishment and maintenance of cooperation within the FernUni Switzerland as well as with other universities in Switzerland and abroad
  • Active support for the further development of the faculty and its degree programmes
  • Taking over tasks in academic self-management
  • Participation in training
  • Promoting equality, diversity and inclusion as well as good scientific practice

Your profile

  • Above-average dissertation in Swiss public law
  • Completed or advanced habilitation or an equivalent certificate (for an application for a full or extraordinary professorship) or started habilitation or postdoc project (for an application for an assistant professorship)
  • Research focus in current and future transformation fields of public law, such as e.g. B. New technologies, sustainable development or digital learning and/or application of innovative legal research methods
  • National and international visibility as well as proven ability for national and international cooperation
  • Experience in the acquisition of third-party funds
  • Leadership and management experience in research and teaching is an advantage
  • Teaching experience at university level in the main areas of the professorship
  • Experience in distance learning is an advantage
  • Ability to teach in German or French or both languages in the focus areas of the professorship
  • Good knowledge of English and native language in German or French and at least passive knowledge in the other language
  • Willingness to acquire active knowledge in the other language
  • Willingness and ability to participate in academic self-management

We offer

  • The positions will be designed in accordance with the qualifications of the respective future position holder as a full, extraordinary or assistant professor with tenure track
  • Support and funding for the development of a research and teaching team
  • Comprehensive distance learning training and support
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • An opportunity to work in a dynamic and changing academic environment in a modern university campus

Timetable The appeals will take place on Thursday, 11. March 2027 and Friday 12 March 2027 on the university campus in Brig. The online trial lectures will take place on Wednesday, 24. March 2027. Application and contact We look forward to your application by 01 at the latest. December 2026 by email to jobs@unidistance.ch. Please indicate in your application whether you are applying for a full professorship, extraordinary professorship or tenure-track assistant professorship. Please also indicate whether your application is for the German-speaking professorship, the French-speaking professorship or for both professorships.

We are happy to expect the following documents

  • Letter of motivation
  • Current CV with an explanation of your academic career and your three main research results
  • List of publications, lectures and teachings (including support activities)
  • Three scientific publications as PDF that document your suitability for the job
  • List of third-party funds raised
  • Teaching evaluations
  • Your research and teaching concept (max. 2 pages in total)
  • Overview of contributions to equality, diversity and inclusion, to good scientific practice (e.g. open science) and to participation in academic self-management

The FernUni Switzerland promotes diversity and equal opportunities and strives for a balanced representation in research and teaching. Applications from women and members of other under-represented groups are expressly welcome. If you have any questions, please always contact the following two contact persons:

  • Prof. Dr. Bruno Pasquier (bruno.pasquier@unidistance.ch), Dean of the Faculty of Law, and
  • Dr. Harry Kulczynski (harry.kulczynski@fernuni.ch), Deputy Head of HR and HR Business Partner

Applications from people who are resident in Switzerland or are considering this in the future will be considered. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
