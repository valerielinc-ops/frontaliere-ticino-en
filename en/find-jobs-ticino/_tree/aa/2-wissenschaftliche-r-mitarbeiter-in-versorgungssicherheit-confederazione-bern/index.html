Put to the Point

Are you concerned about the future care of patients with medical goods? In our interdisciplinary team, you will work on a wide range of political and technical issues and develop concrete solutions for reliable healthcare together with partners within and outside the federal administration.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21035

You can make this contribution

  • Participate in challenging and strategic policy dossiers as well as long-term implementation projects in the area of security of supply of medical goods
  • Help shape strategic and political business as well as develop reports and decision-making bases for policy makers
  • Analyze complex health policy issues and develop practical solutions, in particular to reduce the impact of shortages on health care
  • Working on parliamentary initiatives and participating in the further development of the legal and political framework
  • Coordinate and actively shape cooperation with relevant partners from administration, healthcare, science and industry

This makes them unique.

  • university degree in social, political, economic or natural sciences or Medicine or a comparable area of expertise
  • Experience with health policy, regulatory or care-related issues as well as managing complex projects
  • Strong analytical and conceptual thinking skills combined with the ability to grasp complex relationships and develop viable solutions
  • Rapid comprehension, independent and structured working methods with an above-average degree of personal responsibility.
  • Above-average editorial and communication skills
  • Active knowledge of two official languages and, if possible, passive knowledge of the third official language. Good English skills
