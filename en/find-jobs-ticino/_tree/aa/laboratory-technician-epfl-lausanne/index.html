Full description

Mission

EPFL's Thomä laboratory is looking for a motivated laboratory technician to support various research projects in the field of drug discovery. You will work with a team of postdoctoral researchers developing compounds of the "molecular glues" type. This work includes the development and optimization of high-throughput in vitro and in vivo screenings, as well as the early stages of drug discovery, including optimization of the initial active molecules into main candidates ("hit-to-lead"). If you wish to join a team working on interesting scientific projects that can lead to concrete applications, we invite you to submit your application.

Main tasks and responsibilities

Within our research team, you will provide experimental support to various projects. Your responsibilities will include:

  • Conduct molecular biology experiments, including DNA cloning, plasmids preparation and building validation
  • Participate in mammalian and insect cell culture, including routine crop maintenance and transfections
  • Contribute to the development and implementation of cellular or molecular screening tests
  • Prepare samples and reagents, and maintain accurate experimental records
  • Analyse experimental results and identify possible technical problems
  • Participate in the management of the laboratory's common stocks, the maintenance of equipment and the maintenance of a clean and well-organised working environment
  • Coordinate experiments and priorities with several researchers
  • Contribute positively to the scientific and social life of the laboratory

Profile

You are a motivated, reliable and curious laboratory professional who appreciates learning new techniques and working in a collaborative research team. You carry out the experiments carefully, take a critical approach in your work and are able to work independently once trained.

You must have the following qualifications

  • Training in biology, biotechnology, biochemistry, molecular biology or related disciplines
  • Prior practical laboratory experience
  • A good understanding of good laboratory practices, particularly as regards rigour and sterility
  • The ability to follow accurate and reproducible protocols
  • The ability to organize your work, manage multiple tasks and clearly document experiences
  • A thoughtful and systematic approach to experimental work, in particular when conducting screenings
  • Good communication skills and a collegial attitude, oriented towards teamwork
  • The desire to learn new methods and adapt to the needs of different projects

A good mastery of English

Experience in one or more of the following areas would be an asset:

  • Cloning Gateway, Gibson or based on restriction enzymes
  • Mammal and insect cell culture techniques
  • Microbiology techniques
  • Cellular or molecular screening techniques
  • Fluorescence-activated flow or cell sorting (FACS)

Candidates are not expected to master all the above techniques. Training will be provided. Motivation, reliability, scientific curiosity and the ability to become a fully committed member of the laboratory are essential qualities for this position.

We offer

EPFL is located in French-speaking Switzerland on Lake Geneva and offers:

  • Excellent international working conditions within a state-of-the-art multidisciplinary team with access to global infrastructure
  • A young, dynamic, flexible and highly motivated research group
  • Training in relevant molecular and cellular biology techniques
  • Competitive pay and social benefits
  • A rich cultural environment, campus life and vibrant urban life, as well as many opportunities for outdoor activities
