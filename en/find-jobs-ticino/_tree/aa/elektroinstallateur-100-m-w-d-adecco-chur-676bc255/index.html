Full description

Introduction

We are looking for an electrical installer (m/f/d) for a versatile application in the field of electrical installation. The position includes practical activities on changing construction sites and in different working environments. It is used in a professional environment with clear workflows and technical tasks related to installation, maintenance and testing of electrical systems. We are looking for a qualified professional who works carefully and implements technical specifications reliably. The customer offers long-term employment with a permanent employment contract. The activity is aimed at people who value structured work processes and want to contribute their experience in electrical installation to a varied field of work.

Tasks

  • They install electrical lines, sockets, switches and other components according to technical specifications.
  • They take over the assembly and commissioning of electrical systems in buildings and plant areas.
  • They check electrical installations for function, safety and compliance with applicable regulations.
  • They carefully perform maintenance and repair work on existing electrical systems.
  • They assist in troubleshooting and correct faults in electrical systems professionally.
  • They work according to schematics, installation plans and other technical documents.

Profile

  • You have completed training as an electrical installer or in a comparable electrotechnical profession.
  • You have practical experience in electrical installation and handling of electrical systems.
  • They work carefully, reliably and comply with technical and safety-relevant requirements.
  • They have a good technical understanding and can safely read schematics.
  • They are willing to take on varied electrical installation activities responsibly.
  • They like to work in a team and coordinate professionally with colleagues and clients.

Advantages

  • The remuneration is mainly based on the collective agreement and offers reliable framework conditions.
  • The task area is versatile and includes various activities in the field of electrical installation.
  • A permanent employment contract is offered.
