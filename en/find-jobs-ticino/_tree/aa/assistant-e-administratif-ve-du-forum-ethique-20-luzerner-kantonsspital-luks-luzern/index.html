Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 September 2026 or by agreement. Tasks: • They independently organize and coordinate the back office in the general and professional administration and work closely with the co-chair of the Ethics Forum – in particular in the planning and implementation of meetings, conferences, meetings and educational events. • They manage and maintain documents professionally in SharePoint, Microsoft Teams and the intranet and ensure structured and transparent storage. • They keep minutes at meetings and events and ensure structured and comprehensible documentation • They ensure clear, reliable and addressable communication between all stakeholders and thus promote efficient cooperation Requirements • You have an education in the administrative field – ideally in the health care sector – and bring your know-how professionally in a demanding environment • You have experience in protocol management as well as in the organization and coordination of meetings and work in a structured and reliable manner. • They are characterized by a distinctive organizational talent and keep deadlines, tasks and processes under control at all times. • You have in-depth knowledge of how to use Microsoft 365 and use digital tools in your everyday work We offer: • Look forward to working independently with clear responsibilities within a binding framework – embedded in an appreciative cooperation in an interdisciplinary team • You will get the chance to contribute your own ideas and actively shape the design of events and projects • You work in a value-oriented environment that actively promotes and lives dialogue, reflection and respectful exchange. • You benefit from a wide range of further education and training opportunities in the sense of company goals and actively shape your professional and personal development
