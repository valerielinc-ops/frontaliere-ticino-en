Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire with great commitment construction customers and private customers on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

Would you like to take on a strategic, varied and high impact position at the heart of the development of the product offer? Do you like to anticipate market trends, build successful assortments and work closely with sales, marketing, purchasing and supplier teams? Then this opportunity could perfectly match you. We recruit for the Bulle team our future collaborator as

Missions

  • You are responsible for product management for parquet, vinyl, laminate, doors and exterior fittings Wood
  • You develop and evolve an innovative, consistent, competitive and cost-effective assortment
  • You analyze market trends, the competitive environment and customer expectations to identify new development opportunities
  • You define and implement product, assortment and marketing strategies
  • You work closely with the Purchasing, Sales, Marketing and supplier teams
  • You actively support sales teams during product launches, training and customer projects
  • You develop and maintain strong relationships with suppliers, while negotiating commercial terms
  • You control the life cycle of the products, from their introduction to their evolution
  • You are involved in defining pricing strategies and the positioning of ranges
  • You conduct product training and make presentations to internal and external contacts
  • You travel regularly throughout Switzerland to stay close to customers, partners, sites and the market
  • You ensure active monitoring of innovations, materials, design trends and sustainable developments in the sector

Your Profile

  • You have SWC or technical business training, ideally complemented by continuous training in product management, marketing, sales or business management
  • You justify several years of experience in product management, category management or a similar function
  • You have strong industry knowledge in one or more areas: floor coverings, doors, interior design, garden products / outdoors
  • You are strongly oriented and know how to identify market potentials and customer needs
  • You stand out for your entrepreneurial, innovative and visionary personality
  • You show initiative, structure and a strong capacity to realize your ideas
  • You are at ease in the negotiation and know how to convince your interlocutors
  • You appreciate the cross-sectional collaboration and communicate easily with various profiles
  • You combine analytical mind, pragmatism and field approach
  • You are willing to make frequent trips nationally
  • You master French and German, both oral and written; any other language, including English, is an asset

We offer

  • A stimulating, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where active collaboration is valued while encouraging autonomy.
  • A comprehensive integration programme; our new colleagues also benefit from in-house training on products and systems
  • Support for the professional and personal development of our employees
  • A well-established network of craft centres and close collaboration with sales management
  • Collaborating discounts, various discounts such as fleet discounts, discounts in selected fitness centres and numerous offers to promote their mental and physical health
  • An attractive wage package combined with a solid pension fund, with flexible savings contributions and above average employer share
  • 5 weeks of holidays (6 weeks from 50 years) and the possibility of buying up to 10 additional days of vacation, as well as additional benefits such as a highly funded autonomous pension fund and discounts on our entire product range as well as external premium benefits
