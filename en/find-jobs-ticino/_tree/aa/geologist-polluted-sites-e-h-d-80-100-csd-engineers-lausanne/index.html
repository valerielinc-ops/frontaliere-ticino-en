More than 1,000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: the CSD Group is a dynamic and growing company. We are looking for our Environment branch in Lausanne (VD) or Geneva (GE), a Geologist - Polluted Sites (f/h/d) 80-100%.

  • Your missions
  • Pilot projects for the diagnosis and remediation of polluted sites (OSites, OLED)
  • Knowledge of environmental issues related to PFAS (environmental dissemination, PFAS behaviour) is a plus
  • Intervention on multidisciplinary projects in geology, geotechnical and hydrogeology
  • Conduct and supervise field investigations (drills, surveys, trials)
  • Develop concepts of management and recovery of excavation materials
  • Coordinate stakeholders (internal teams, clients, authorities) and mentor techniques to ensure the quality of technical deliverables
  • Active participation in the development and acquisition of mandates in the above areas
  • Your profile
  • Graduated geologist/hydrogeologist or equivalent
  • Min. 2 to 3 years of engineering office experience
  • Strong Geology and Regional Hydrogeology
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss romantic context, standards (OSites, OLED) and its actors
  • Good control of technical tools and software for data management and representation (ArcGIS, ....)
  • Dynamic and autonomous personality with initiative and appreciation for teamwork
