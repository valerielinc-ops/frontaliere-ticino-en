Full description

The House for Mother and Child is an institution for eight residents and their children between the ages of 0 and 6, who need support in everyday life due to their personal, psychological and social situation. The children and their mothers are supported, accompanied and cared for during their stay by an interdisciplinary team.

Job description ##

  • Day organization and information flow: Oversee the status of the day, reports, huddleboard, agenda, day planning, appointments, absences, conversations, offers, maternity instructions, office hours and breaks and coordinate them on a daily basis. Structuring morning and midday reports and handovers to late and night service.
  • Residents and house rules: take up organizational concerns of the residents, make daily tasks and offices transparent, pay attention to the observance of internal rules and forward open points to the responsible specialists. In difficult situations, act quickly within your own role and ensure the necessary consultation.
  • Interface Child Care: Take part in the child care rapport briefly, compare the planning with the main house and make sufficient coverage of child care visible. The professional assignment and task allocation in childcare remains with the responsible specialist or the co-institution management.
  • Housekeeping, meals and infrastructure: Compare the status of Ämtli, meal planning, food supplies and support needs with housekeeping. Pay attention to order, safety, room temperature, heating, strollers, visible infrastructure themes and the orderly condition of the house; forward the necessary measures to the competent authority.
  • Medication and documentation: Make sure that agreed procedures for drug delivery and documentation are visibly adhered to. The implementation and professional responsibility lie exclusively with the responsible and entitled employees.
  • Resources, breaks and telephone: conduct service telephone, receive inquiries and triage together with editing and competent specialists. coordinate breaks and daily resources so that there is sufficient coverage; clarify foreseeable gaps, additional hours or hour compensation at an early stage with the co-institution management.
  • Control, handovers and subsequent day planning: carry out room checks on the basis of the existing list, pay attention to complete progress and report documents and accompany the end of the day. Together with housekeeping and responsible child care, prepare and compare the planning boards, the huddleboard and the clipboard with the documents for the following day.
  • Friday and weekend: Check menu plan, weekend planning, office and service planning for completeness and prepare the necessary documents. In the event of uncertainties or resource deficits, consult the co-institution management or the background service at an early stage. Visits and location discussions accompanies the TV only after express commission and within its remit.
