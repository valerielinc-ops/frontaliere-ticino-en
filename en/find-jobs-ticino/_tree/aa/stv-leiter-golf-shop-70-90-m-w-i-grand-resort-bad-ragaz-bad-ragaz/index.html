  • Customer support and advice as well as handling of complaints
  • Ordering and assortment
  • Presentation and decoration of the Golf Shop

Requirements

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship in the retail trade (EFZ), preferably in the textile and fashion sector, or equivalent training
  • Professional experience in the sale of high-quality women’s and men’s clothing (ideally sports sector)
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English, further knowledge of foreign languages beneficial
  • Communicative with very good manners to advise the demanding clientele individually and personally
  • Flexibility and readiness for regular assignments on weekends and public holidays in 7-day operation during the golf season

The Grand Resort Bad Ragaz is the only resort in Switzerland with two golf courses. The 18-hole Championship Course of Golf Club Bad Ragaz is one of the best courses in the country and one of the "Leading Golf Courses". The restaurant gladys, awarded several times as "Best Swiss Golf Restaurant", spoils you culinary. In addition, the clubhouse offers an extensive golf shop range of golf and leisure fashion.

The Golf Club Heidiland offers with its 9-hole Executive Course and an exercise center with 20 covered and 50 lawn teeing areas optimal training opportunities and has established itself as a beginner club of the region. A bistro and a golf shop complete the wide offer.

Our golf shop at Golf Club Bad Ragaz is primarily available to our club members, but also to the international guests of our Grand Hotels and external customers. The range has elegant and sporty women's and men's fashion of renowned designers, golf equipment and accessories.
