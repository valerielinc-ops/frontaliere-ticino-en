Full description

You develop ideas that create spaces and buildings that move people. In our team in Chur you accompany construction projects from the first sketch to the convincing presentation and thus shape the face of our built environment in Graubünden and beyond.

You work closely with project managers, specialist planners and builders and bring your creative signature to challenging projects from the hotel industry, housing, business and infrastructure. If you enjoy conceptual work and like to think through your designs in detail, you will find the right setting for it with us.

Your tasks

  • Development of concepts/energy-optimized buildings and designs of buildings.
  • Location analysis and needs assessment.
  • Development of solutions and variant studies.
  • Preparation of explanatory reports and detailed studies
  • Preliminary clarifications authorities/neighbours/third parties.

Your profile

  • Dipl. Architect FH / TU / ETH / USI.
  • In-depth expertise in design theory.
  • 3-4 years of professional experience.
  • Analytical thinking, rapid comprehension, teamwork.
  • Understanding construction, design flair.
  • MS Office, ArchiCAD and Adobe desktop publishing skills.
  • Good knowledge of German (word and writing).

Your perspectives

  • Professional infrastructure and working methods.
  • Innovative and exciting projects.
  • Flat hierarchies and collegiality.
  • Individual development opportunities.
  • Working in interdisciplinary teams.
