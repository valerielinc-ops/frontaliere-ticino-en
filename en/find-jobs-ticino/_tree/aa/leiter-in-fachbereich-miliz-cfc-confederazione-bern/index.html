Full description

Put to the Point

As a dedicated specialist, you actively shape the future of the Swiss Army’s cyber militia. You coordinate militia missions, develop cyber education and connect the professional and militia organizations – also beyond national borders.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20832
  • Location: Papiermühlestrasse 20, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure coordination, planning, implementation and control of militia operations in collaboration with the Cyber Fusion Center (CFC) teams and the militia organization
  • Further develop the cyber training of the Swiss Army (cyber training course, repetition courses)
  • Develop operational concepts of the cyber militia for the benefit of the Swiss army (analyse capabilities, define structures and ensure alimentation)
  • Check training quality and identification of appropriate measures
  • Initialize and establish international cooperation
  • Ensure cooperation and communication between professional and militia organisations and act as a link between the agencies involved

This makes you unique

  • Graduate degree in computer science/information security or engineering
  • In-depth experience in a security organization (SOC, CSIRT, CERT)
  • Strong assertive personality that drives and motivates other people
  • Knowledge of military staff processes/organisations
  • Ideally has experience as a staff officer/in the Swiss Army
  • Good active knowledge of a second official language and good knowledge of English

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
