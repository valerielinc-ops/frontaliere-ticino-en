Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate - that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company today employs over 2500 people from more than 80 countries worldwide, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters is located in the canton of Glarus. With a breathtaking Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The «Retail» business unit includes over 250 Chocolaterien in over 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you want to be part of it? Then come to us. ##

  • Responsibilities
  • You lead and motivate a committed team of branch employees
  • You ensure the efficient organization and smooth operation of the branch
  • You offer competent, customer-oriented advice and support in selecting gifts from our diverse range of products
  • You are responsible for the appealing presentation of our extensive confectionery and chocolate assortment, including creative store design
  • You take on financial responsibility, ensure the proper processing of cash transactions, and guarantee correct billing
  • You handle ordering and ensure that sufficient goods are always available
  • You ensure that our hygiene and freshness concept is always adhered to
  • You take on personnel planning in shift operation and are actively involved in the recruitment process ##
  • Requirements

  • You have an EFZ qualification in retail
  • You prioritize high quality and customer orientation even in turbulent times
  • You are willing to take on responsibility, think proactively in a team, and promote positive collaboration
  • You have sound IT knowledge and work independently and solution-oriented
  • You appreciate open and respectful communication and speak German and English fluently; knowledge of French and other languages is an additional advantage
  • You are not afraid of flexible working hours and are willing to take on responsibility on weekends, Saturdays, Sundays, or public holidays ##
