Full description

Elsa Group is one of the leading Swiss dairy industry groups and is part of the Migros Group (Migros Industry). Elsa Group is an expert in the manufacture and packaging of dairy products as well as in the production, refining and processing of cheeses and the production of plant-based products.

Luogo di Lavoro: Elsa Group, Elsa Group – Estavayer-le-Lac, Route de Payerne 2-4, 1470 Estavayer-le-Lac

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Manage and perform preventive and corrective maintenance work, especially on your business line facilities, to ensure their optimal operation
  • Provide electrical and automation support to other maintenance services
  • Search for root causes of malfunctions using company methodologies and implement sustainable corrective actions
  • Propose, manage and perform improved maintenance work to optimize the performance of automated installations
  • Document work orders in SAP PM (part management, hours imputation, histogram data, etc.)
  • Collaborate with the technical store to identify critical spare parts to store and ensure their availability
  • Needle technical project leaders in component standardization
  • Ensure continuous operation of the equipment by participating in the picket service

Required

  • Experience confirmed: in industrial maintenance, ideally in the field of food (an asset)
  • Initial or continuing training completed: CFC and/or ES in the technical field (automatician, industrial systems technician) or training deemed equivalent
  • Anticipation capacity, organizational sense and autonomy
  • Spirit of analysis, rigour and orientation solutions
  • Knowledge of IT maintenance management tools such as SAP PM or other GMAO and MS Office
  • Home in the area due to picket service (max. 30 minutes drive)
  • French (Very good knowledge)
  • German (Basic terms)
  • English (Basic terms)

Recruitment process

  • Submit an application: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. It is not necessary to attach a photo to the CV or a cover letter. Applications received are reviewed as they progress. So you can count on a response within one to two weeks. We apply equal opportunities to all candidates. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application.
  • Pre-selection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Personal interview video or telephone: In the case of a large number of candidates, personal video or telephone interviews are optional. This allows us to get to know more candidates in a short time so as to invite the people concerned for the next round. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Interview with HR and the department: The interview is conducted in distanciel or face-to-face with the HR person responsible and/or the specialized department. It serves primarily to learn more about personal and professional skills and allows both parties to assess cultural compatibility with the company and the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and establishment of contract (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week
