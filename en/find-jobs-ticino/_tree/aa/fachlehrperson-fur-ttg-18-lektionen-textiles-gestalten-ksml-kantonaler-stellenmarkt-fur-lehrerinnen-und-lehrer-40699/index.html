Full description

Portrait We provide learning for all, maintain community and real relationships We are guided by clear structures. (Guideline)

Ringgenberg is an attractive place to work and live on Lake Brienzersee. At our school with a motivated school team and modern infrastructure, around 230 children and young people from kindergarten to 9th grade are currently taught in a total of 13 classes. They can look forward to well-established structures that also allow for innovation and development, for example, since 2025 Cycle 3 has been organised in a mixed age and we are a mosaic secondary school. As a result of retirement we are looking for a successor - as of August 2027 you will be part of our team as:

Tasks

They teach Textiles Gestalten in cycles 2 and 3 (3rd - 9th grade) They work closely with the instructor for technical design and with the cycle teams. You take responsibility for the specialist premises (compensated). This includes material ordering, budgeting and maintenance of equipment as well as material storage.

Requirements

You have a teaching diploma (primary level or sec I with TTG or a specialist diploma). We also welcome applications from students in the last year of study. They work independently, enjoy being in a team and enjoy designing with children and young people.

We offer

  • Good cooperation, support in the team and through the school management
  • appreciative, pleasant working atmosphere in a future-oriented school
  • A laptop is provided.
  • Our specialist rooms are bright and currently equipped with new sewing machines, overlook, cutting plotter, embroidery machine and well-stocked material storage.
  • permanent employment with perspective and space for own projects (exhibitions, project weeks, etc.)
  • The workload can be supplemented by agreement and possibility with further lessons and tasks.
  • Handover and familiarisation by the previous holder, possibility for a trial visit
  • Mentoring for new professionals
  • Location: a few minutes from Interlaken, easily accessible by public transport
