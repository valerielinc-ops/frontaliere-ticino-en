Full description

Become part of something bigger. Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life. On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you.

You would like to start your apprenticeship as an assistant in Health and Social EBA from the 1. Start August 2027 at the Swiss Paraplegic Centre and help us to do something meaningful? Then apply now – we look forward to your full application (cover letter, CV, trial assessments, school certificates, interlocking test). Tasks: • You accompany, care for and care for patients in acute situations as well as during rehabilitation in their everyday life. • You provide a safe and clean environment and participate in administrative tasks • You get an insight into various areas in the SPZ such as cleaning, bed center, hotel industry Requirements • You have completed the compulsory schooling and have good knowledge of German (minor level B2) • You bring good team and communication skills • You enjoy domestic activities, as well as being interested in working with people in need • Your motivation and willingness to learn for a 2 year training period round off your profile We offer: You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits. The Swiss Paraplegic Centre in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for cross-sectional, back and respiratory medicine. As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the centre offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives. The clinic is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a globally unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with paraplegia.
