Full description

"Expect to grow", personally and professionally: At Siegfried, we offer exciting challenges and many opportunities to showcase your talents. As a trusted and respected partner in the pharmaceutical industry, we meet the highest standards of safety, quality and sustainability, while cultivating a culture that allows you to thrive. Join a growing global company where diverse perspectives and experiences come together in a collaborative environment. Here you will find a workplace that values collective success as much as your individual contributions—giving long-term opportunities to evolve and have an impact.

Your Role

Do you want to put your analytical expertise at the service of the pharmaceutical industry? Join our Laboratory Stabilitys and Reference Substances.

Attached to the Manager Stabilitys and Reference Substances, you perform instrumental and physico-chemical analyses related to stability studies and standards in accordance with procedures, GMP requirements and safety rules.

Your responsibilities

Conduct and control instrumental analyses, including GC, HPLC, GC-HS and UPLC, as well as physico-chemical analyses.

Enter and verify the results in the LIMS and control the analyses in Empower.

Ensure the stability and exit of samples according to the established schedule.

Contribute to the management of reference substances.

Participate in investigations related to non-specific results and analytical deviations, propose hypothesis tests and contribute to corrective and preventive actions.

Provide laboratory maintenance and equipment maintenance.

Participate in drafting and updating working procedures and instructions.

Train new workers in analytical techniques.

Support, as required, raw material and finished product release analyses in collaboration with other quality control laboratories.

Your Profile

  • You have a chemical laboratory CFC or equivalent training.
  • You have a proven laboratory experience and master the above-mentioned analytical techniques.
  • You organize your analyses independently and manage priorities.
  • You are rigorous, critical and proactive in dealing with the technical problems of everyday life.
  • You are able to contribute to analytical investigations and propose concrete solutions.
  • You appreciate teamwork and the transmission of your knowledge.
  • You carefully apply GMP requirements as well as hygiene and safety rules.

Your Benefits

Temporary appointment post with possibility of fixed passage

Founded in 1873 in Zofingue, Switzerland, Siegfried has become a global network of 16 sites spread over three continents. With a team of more than 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to the industrial scale and manufacture safe medicines for patients around the world. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few organizations able today to ensure the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished galenic forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most reliable partner in the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operators manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
