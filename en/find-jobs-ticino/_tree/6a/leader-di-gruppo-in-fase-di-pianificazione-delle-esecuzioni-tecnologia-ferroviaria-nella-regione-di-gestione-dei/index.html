The «Project Management Region East» is responsible for the project planning and realization of complex multidisciplinary railway infrastructure projects with a total volume of approximately 300 MCHF per year. Bring in your talent as part of a motivated and ambitious leadership team and make a direct contribution to the further development of Switzerland’s transport infrastructure. In your exciting role, you will lead a team of execution project leaders who lead complex, multidisciplinary railway infrastructure projects together with various specialist areas over the various project phases. You can move that.

  • As a team leader: you lead and coach a team of 10-12 project leaders with main responsibility in the field of railway technology as well as in their coordination on the construction site. Here you will be guided by the values and leadership principles of SBB.
  • It gives you pleasure to organize and optimize workflows. In addition, you promote further education, knowledge transfer, collaboration and performance culture in your team and together with your peers in the region.
  • Together with your team, you ensure the success of the projects assigned to you. You are responsible for all project processes up to implementation and commissioning, in close cooperation with the project managers and internal railway technology specialists: inside and construction companies.
  • You are a member of the management team «Project Management Region East» and actively participate in the goal achievement of the region. You promote and support the further development of processes, project management and digitalization.
  • You take over the management of overarching development topics and actively promote the networking of the different regions and departments.

You bring that with you.

  • With strong social skills and communicative strength, you motivate your employees to achieve their goals and ensure their performance.
  • With your conceptual skills and your enthusiasm, you contribute profitably to the further development of the region and the business unit "Project Management".
  • You have a flair for the leadership and coaching of employees in a challenging environment.
  • You already have several years of experience as a project manager: in or specialist engineer: in the railway technology sector and preferably already have initial management experience or an appropriate training.
  • We presuppose higher education or university education with a focus on construction, economy or transport. In addition, you have completed further training in business administration or technology in order to optimally complement your profile.
  • In German, you are confident in negotiation (minimum C1) and preferably have a good knowledge of French or Italian.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
