About us

Volg is the specialist for village shops and small-format stores in German-speaking and French-speaking Switzerland. We are all about being close to our customers – both as a convenient local shopping option and through personal contact. Employees are the heart of the store, and we place great value on individuality and personality. That is why our motto is "fresh and friendly".
