Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic Sonnmatt in Lucerne combines medical rehabilitation in the fields of neurology and musculoskeletal with a first-class hotel environment in the most beautiful viewing position. Our patients benefit both inpatient and outpatient from personal, holistic care by an experienced, interdisciplinary team – with maximum comfort and high therapeutic quality.

Your tasks

  • Diagnosis: You are jointly responsible for neuropsychological diagnosis in people with acquired brain damage
  • Rehabilitation: planning of neuropsychological-therapeutic measures and psychological support for patients
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration: Work in our interprofessional team with other neuropsychologists, nurses, therapists and doctors. Your expertise is highly appreciated!
  • In-house training : Pass on your knowledge to the other employees of the interprofessional team

What you bring

  • Education: Completed psychology degree with appropriate recognition
  • Further qualification: Ideally, a specialist title in neuropsychology or alternatively the willingness to continue training for the specialist title
  • Personality: You are characterized by team spirit, initiative and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation in a rehabilitative environment. Your thinking is constructive and solution-oriented, and you also have a high willingness to learn.

Our offer to you

  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Co-creation: Bring your expertise to the further development of our therapy programs so that we stay up-to-date in neuropsychology
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
