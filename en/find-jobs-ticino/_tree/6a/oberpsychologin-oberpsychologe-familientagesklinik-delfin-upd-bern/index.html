University Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Would you like to make a decisive contribution to the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of children and adolescents of the Canton of Bern? Then engage yourself as a senior psychologist in the Dolphin Family Day Clinic of the University Hospital for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy of the UPZ in Bern. The team of the Delfin Family Day Clinic offers a semi-inpatient psychiatric treatment for children and their families. As part of multi-family therapy, children aged 9 to 13 are treated with a wide range of mental disorders, with the family closely involved in the therapeutic process. You have the chance to lead and shape this offer in a multi-professional team. Become effective where you need it most and bring your passion for psychotherapy to the care of patients, new diagnostic and treatment concepts and the training of young colleagues.

Your tasks

  • You take over the superpsychological management of the family day clinic Dolfin in an interdisciplinary management team
  • You lead a psychological team and work closely and multiprofessionally with the medical service as well as the staff of the clinic school, nursing and pedagogy
  • You are therapeutically active yourself
  • You play a key role in the training and guidance of assistant psychologists and assistant doctors in the diagnosis and therapy of children and adolescents. You actively include the extended system (family, school and education)
  • You take on conceptual tasks, continuously develop the diagnostic and therapeutic offer and carry out supervision
  • You will build up valuable networks with specialist offices and expert groups in the region and maintain existing contacts
  • You act at the interface between clinic and research and support the recruitment of research projects
  • You have the opportunity to get involved in the KJP’s teaching and internal training programs

Your profile

  • You have successfully completed your psychotherapy training
  • You want to apply diagnostic and therapeutic concepts based on current evidence-based guidelines, pass on your expertise and actively develop existing treatment concepts
  • You have experience in a comparable working environment and are motivated to break new ground together and develop innovative approaches
  • You appreciate the networked cooperation with patients, their families, schools, educational institutions and the extended healthcare system
  • You appreciate and promote teamwork and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • You are motivated to support treatment-related research projects

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
