Become part of our EMR team in Visp!

To strengthen our Engineering & Maintenance team in the area of Development & Manufacturing Solutions at the Visp site, we are looking for an electrical, measurement and control (EMR) engineer. The position includes responsibility for maintenance and various projects in mono- or multi-product facilities.

What we offer

  • An Agile Career and Dynamic Work Culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous advantages in the areas of lifestyle, family and leisure. Find the full list of our global services You are under:

Benefits - Visp

Your tasks

  • Planning and implementation of investment and conversion tasks in an automated production plant for pharmaceutical active ingredients
  • Continuous adaptation and improvement of equipment, processes and product changes with focus on quality, safety and performance
  • Ensuring the smooth operation and reliable performance of highly automated production facilities with DeltaV and MES systems
  • GMP-compliant performance of audits, documentation and change management using Kneat and Ubik
  • Creation and maintenance of plant documentation in SAP/COMOS to ensure regulatory requirements
  • Coordination and monitoring of projects in terms of functionality, performance, costs and deadlines
  • Support of projects from planning to successful commissioning
  • Efficient planning and organisation of maintenance to ensure plant availability and operational safety
  • Promoting technological advancements through innovative and proactive working methods
  • Active participation in interdisciplinary committees

Your profile

  • Completed Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical, Automation or Systems Engineering or Computer science
  • First professional experience in operation or project engineering, maintenance or new plant construction, ideally in a GMP-regulated environment of the pharmaceutical or chemical industry
  • Knowledge of GMP, COMOS, DeltaV, MES and SAP
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation and an independent and flexible working method
  • Strong teamwork and very good communication skills
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Business thinking, high motivation and willingness to change
