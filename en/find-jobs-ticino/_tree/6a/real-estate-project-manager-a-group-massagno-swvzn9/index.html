Company Description

A++ Group is a multidisciplinary international organization founded in 2000 with offices worldwide, from Europe to Asia, passing through America and the Middle East.

The Group consists of over thirty companies that deal with real estate investments, construction, architecture, interior design, building materials, product design and hospitality.

Position

We select two resources to support Real Estate for real estate project management: Real Estate Project Manager

Tasks and responsibilities

  • Business plan and real estate cost reporting
  • Brochure preparation
  • Provision of real estate ads/insertions
  • Market studies - industry analysis

Property Manager/Real Estate 100%

  • back office activities
  • Manage relationships with conductors (contractual, renewal, resolutions).
  • Support the search for new tenants.
  • Monitor the collection of rental fees.
  • Oversee ordinary and extraordinary maintenance, in collaboration with the external company responsible for facility management services.
  • Collect, analyze and verify the necessary documentation for acquisition or disposal of properties.
  • Create regular budgets and reports for the Management.

Requirements

Real Estate Project Manager

  • Bachelor in Construction Engineering and Architecture (EDA)
  • Minimum experience 5 years.
  • Knowledge of English, German preferential
  • Good competence use AUTOCAD and Office package
  • Excellent knowledge of the office package, especially Excel
  • At least 3 years of experience in similar roles, preferably at real estate funds or real estate structured in the real estate sector.
  • Great knowledge of English; knowledge of German or another national language is an advantage.
  • Solid mastery of economic-financial analysis and real estate management tools.
  • Knowledge of building, urban and management regulations.
  • Strategic approach, result orientation, trading capacity, operational autonomy and strong relational skills.
