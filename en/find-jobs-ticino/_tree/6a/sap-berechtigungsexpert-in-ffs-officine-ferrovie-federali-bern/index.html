SAP authorization expert:in 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. SAP authorization expert:in Bern / Work Smart, by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

In Identity and Access Management, you are responsible for the protection and control of around 80,000 identities and the secure authentication and authorization of around 1,500 applications. In a modern cloud environment for SAP and non-SAP, you play a part in shaping quality, security and innovation and actively shape SBB’s authorization system. You work closely with specialist areas, security and compliance and drive improvements with a noticeable impact. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As the person responsible for quality assurance in the authorization system, you ensure transport controls, compliance and secure access rights and thus strengthen the stability of our systems.
  • In close cooperation with the Access Compliance Manager, you support audits and controls and ensure transparent proof.
  • Independent adoption of projects and active support in the development of viable solutions.
  • Analysis and further development of existing process documentation for uniform and effective processes.
  • Support for security reviews such as Risk Analysis and Remediation RAR and User Access Reviews UAR.
  • Consulting on SAP security topics with focus on identity and access management processes.

You bring that with you.

  • With your communication strength and service orientation, you create clarity and promote constructive cooperation with different stakeholders.
  • Networked work is yours, you are happy to take on responsibility and cooperation across team boundaries is a matter of course for you.
  • Openness to change and joy in actively shaping new topics and technologies.
  • Several years of experience in SAP authorization as well as in SAP Access Control and SAP GRC Governance Risk and Compliance.
  • In-depth know-how in SAP Security in ABAP, S 4 HANA, Business Technology Platform and SaaS solutions as well as experience with auditing or Xiting solutions are beneficial.
  • Completed higher computer science education HF/FH/Uni or equivalent further education as well as German at level C1 and English at level B2.
