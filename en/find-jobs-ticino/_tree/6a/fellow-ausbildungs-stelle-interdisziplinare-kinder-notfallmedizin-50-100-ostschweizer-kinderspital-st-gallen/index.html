Full description

Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a Fellow / Training Position for Interdisciplinary Children’s Emergency Medicine (50-100%) to strengthen our medical team of the Interdisciplinary Children’s Emergency Response.

Their tasks

  • Pediatric internist and traumatological emergency patients as well as their relatives are looked after in collegial exchange with the cadre doctors of the interdisciplinary child emergency and the consultants of the house.
  • Support for the training of junior doctors in paediatric training
  • Structured training and supervision by the cadre doctors of the child emergency ensures targeted preparation for the examination on the focus of child emergency medicine

Your profile

  • Presence of the specialist title in pediatric and adolescent medicine or pediatric surgery, or in the last training year
  • Objective: Acquisition of the specialty child emergency medicine (two years of additional training)
  • Enjoyment at work in a collegial and committed team
  • Self-responsible and flexible working method
  • Perspective of joining the senior physician team after 12–18 months

We offer them

  • Responsibility because we trust in the competence of our employees
  • Perspectives, because we are constantly developing together with you
  • Knowledge because we promote internal and external training
  • Teamwork, because we know that we only achieve the goal together

Your work area The interdisciplinary child emergency takes care of the children in the foundation area and treats about 27,000 patients per year. For children with serious illnesses or injuries, specialists from different disciplines are available to offer the best possible treatment as a team. We are a training center for the focus of child emergency medicine category A (2 years).
