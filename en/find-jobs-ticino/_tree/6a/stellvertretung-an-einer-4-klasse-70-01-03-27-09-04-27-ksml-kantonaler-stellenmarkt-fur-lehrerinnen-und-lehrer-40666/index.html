Full description

Portrait At our school, students and teachers meet to learn, live and laugh. The Stegmatt School teaches pupils from kindergarten until the 9th year of school.

Tasks

You are working on a 4th grade in Lyss. Your workload includes 20 lessons. You teach German, Math, NMG, sports and music. Your working days are Monday morning, full Tuesday, Wednesday morning and Friday morning. The part-time teacher takes over the class teacher function during the deputy. You work together with a curative teacher and a teacher for BVSA accompaniment.

Requirements

You are a qualified teacher, or on the way to the teaching diploma and have the desire to start a new adventure together with the young, curious children. You fit in with us when you...

  • Go through life with a positive attitude.
  • like to discuss opportunities and opportunities for learning experiences for the children.
  • You like to bring your ideas to a team.
  • Be open to new things.
  • You can well imagine the work in a large, lively college.

We offer

  • A helpful and warm team
  • Close cooperation in cycle 2
  • Specialist teachers, school social work and school management who are happy to support you
  • very good accessibility with public transport, parking is also available
  • A community that values its school
