Full description

At the BWZ Rapperswil-Jona we train more than 1'200 apprentices in the departments Commercial-Industrial Professions, Commercial Professions/Detail Trade and Professional Maturity/IMS. We carry the whole range of basic education: certificate training, three- and four-year teaching, teaching with vocational maturity and since 2017 also a computer science school technology. In addition, we lead the pre-teaching and integration classes in the bridge offer. Our further education is led by the Academy of St. Gallen in Rapperswil, offers courses and courses and runs a Higher School of Economics. Due to retirement, we are looking as a complement to our team

What to expect

Accompanying young people on their way to education and work means more than teaching. It needs people who recognize potential, give orientation, formulate clear expectations and support young people to take their next step independently. For our bridge offers pre-teaching and integration classes, we are looking for a dedicated teacher with coaching tasks. A particular focus is on accompanying young people with different linguistic, educational and cultural conditions. Their tasks

  • Teach young people and young adults in pre-teaching and integration classes.
  • Planning and designing practical, competence-oriented and differentiated teaching.
  • accompany learners in their personal, school, linguistic and professional development.
  • Incorporate German as a second language and language promotion in class.
  • Support learners in the career selection process as well as in the search for internships and apprenticeships.
  • Assume class leadership and coaching tasks as well as conduct location, promotion and development discussions.
  • Collaborate with parents or caregivers, internship companies, career counselling and other specialist positions.
  • Actively participate in the further development of bridge offers and contemporary forms of teaching.

What sets it apart

  • A pedagogical training or a teaching diploma or a comparable qualification, preferably at the upper level.
  • Experience in teaching with young people and young adults.
  • Enjoy heterogeneous classes and different educational biographies.
  • Interest or experience in German as a second language, language promotion or integration work.
  • Interest in vocational training, career choice and the world of work.
  • Strong skills in relationship design, class leadership and coaching.
  • An appreciative and at the same time clear approach to young people.
  • Self-employment, flexibility and high team orientation. Particularly welcome
  • Experience in DaZ, integration classes, secondary level I, coaching, social pedagogy or career choice support is an advantage.

What we offer

  • A versatile field of activity at the interface of teaching, coaching, language promotion, career choice and integration.
  • Working in a dedicated team.
  • Space for your own ideas and innovative forms of teaching.
  • A modern vocational school with a close connection to the working world.
  • Current employment conditions.
