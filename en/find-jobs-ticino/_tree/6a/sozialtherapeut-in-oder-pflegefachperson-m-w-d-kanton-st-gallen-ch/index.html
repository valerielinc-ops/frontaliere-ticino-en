Full description

We create perspectives for people in a self-responsible life outside of crime and thus make an important contribution to a safe society. The Office for the Imprisonment of Justice of the Department of Security and Justice employs around 260 people in various professional fields. In the Bitzi Action Center, criminal measures for adult men are carried out on two closed and three open care departments as well as an external housing group.

What to expect

  • Empowerment of detainees to lead a life free from tort
  • Co-creation of a forensic milieutherapeutic setting
  • Intensive cooperation with internal and external bodies

What sets it apart

  • Social skills in relationship design
  • Respectful communication and appreciative attitude
  • Administrative and digital skills, process thinking
  • Studies in Social Work/Social Education or Nursing Professional HF

What we offer

  • Working in a socially important context
  • Varied and demanding work in an interdisciplinary environment
  • Interesting training and further education opportunities / supervision
  • Attractive working conditions in shift operation
