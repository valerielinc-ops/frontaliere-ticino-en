Team leader: in logistics fruits & vegetables, permanent night shift

Coop Genossenschaft — Schafisheim, AG, Svizzera

  • Job opening: by agreement
  • Specialist: Logistics / Transportation
  • Place of work: Coop, Rupperswilerstrasse 2, 5503 Schafisheim
  • Pensum: 80 - 100%

Tasks

  • Professional and personnel management of the team of the Fruits & Vegetables department in day-to-day business
  • Responsibility for efficient deployment and holiday planning taking into account operational requirements
  • Promotion and development of employees through regular employee, goal and performance discussions
  • Ensuring a smooth running of the storage processes incl. Management of order picking and implementation of random checks
  • Close cooperation and proactive exchange of information with incoming goods and sub-control room

Requirements

  • Completed training as a logistician: in EFZ, ideally with appropriate further training or comparable practical experience in logistics and management
  • High load capacity, reliability and an efficient, independent working method
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Secure handling of common IT systems and warehouse management software
  • Readiness to work at night (including Sunday assignments) and to work in a permanently cool working environment (13-15 °C)

What we offer

  • Perks: 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket
  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Annual subscription discount
