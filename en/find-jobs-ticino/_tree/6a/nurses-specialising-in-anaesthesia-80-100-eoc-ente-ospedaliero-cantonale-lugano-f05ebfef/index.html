Full description

Anesthesia specialist nurses - Ausschreibung L’EOC the Ticino multisite hospital is present with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team of the service of Anestesiology of the Regional Hospital of Lugano we look for a(a) Specialized nurses in anesthesia (80%-100%) Profile: organizes and manages multiple activities autonomously and with flexibility, knowing to establish priorities in an efficient and timely manner maintains a positive attitude even in complex situations, of strong stress and unexpected is able to work both autonomously and in team relationships

  • Required requirements: a diploma of infirmary (or recognised equivalent qualification) a diploma of infirmary/experienced in care anesthesia diplomat/a SPD SSS or certificate of infirmary/a specialized in anesthesia (ASI) work experience of at least 1 year in a service of Swiss anesthesia flexibility to work in both locations of the Regional Hospital of Lugano Comprehensive application of curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, copies of certificates of study and work, will be sent by 17 June 2026 only in electronic format via www.eoc.ch . Applications that will come beyond this date will not be considered, as well as those sent in paper form, by e-mail or that do not comply with the requirements indicated. Further information can be requested from Mr Geremia Gervasoni, Head of Nursing Department Anesthesia ORL (geremia.gervasoni @eoc.ch).
