Full description

The opportunity

The Factory Digital & AI Specialist turns manufacturing challenges and production data into practical Digital and AI solutions that improve daily factory performance. Working closely with Operations, Engineering and Quality, the role develops and industrializes solutions using manufacturing data, analytics, AI, machine learning and automation to improve yield, quality, equipment performance, productivity and manufacturing flow.

How you'll make an impact

  • Coordinate the Manufacturing IT Transformation and align digital initiatives with factory priorities
  • Identify and translate manufacturing improvement opportunities into practical digital, data and AI solutions
  • Develop solutions for yield, quality, downtime, productivity, flow and process stability, including rapid proof of value on the shop floor
  • Build manufacturing data and analytics solutions, including standardized Power BI dashboards, automated KPI reporting and real time monitoring
  • Develop and deploy AI/ML applications for process optimization, predictive quality, anomaly detection, root cause analysis and predictive maintenance
  • Industrialize and scale successful solutions by integrating them into factory systems and creating standardized, reusable and maintainable applications and models

Your background

  • Degree in Engineering, Data Science, Computer Science, Automation or related discipline
  • Strong interest or experience in semiconductor manufacturing
  • Practical experience with manufacturing data and analytics
  • Experience with Power BI, AI/ML or comparable technologies
  • Understanding of MES, equipment interfaces and industrial data architectures
  • Strong problem-solving capability and hands on execution mindset
  • Ability to collaborate directly with production and engineering teams while demonstrating strong business acumen
  • Very good English and good German language skills are required
