Full description

Introduction

For our Production division, we are looking for an Electroplast (M/F) whose main mission will be to analyze, process and protect the surfaces of the parts using galvanotechnical processes.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare, analyse and control electrolytic baths
  • Conduct and monitor automated operation of galvanic treatment plants
  • Conduct post-treatment washing, surface finishing
  • Determine the principal components of the electrolytes and calculate the quantities of chemicals required
  • Adjust and calibrate equipment, apparatus and machines
  • Maintain technical facilities and monitor their proper functioning
  • Calculate and determine surface treatment data: pretreatment, current density, deposition time, dial surface
  • Examine treated dials, identify anomalies and take action to address them
  • Manage stockpiles and waste chemicals.

Profile

  • CFC Electroplast or equivalent
  • Experience in surface treatments in industry, an asset
  • Chemical safety knowledge
  • Sense of responsibility, careful, rigorous, organized and methodical
  • Good relationship skills, availability and team spirit
  • Autonomy and quality orientation.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.
