Full description

Intern: in animal care (m/f/d | 100%) The Walter Zoo in Gossau SG is a scientifically managed zoo that cares for 1,000 animals from around 100 different species. It inspires more than 275,000 visitors annually for the wildlife and awakens interest and understanding for their needs and the protection of their habitats. The zoo is involved in various conservation projects and manages two care stations for native wildlife. We are looking for a motivated personality from February 2027. Their tasks

  • Carrying out all work in animal care (care of animals, feed preparation, cleaning of animal facilities, animal employment, commented feedings)
  • Care of pony and camel riding
  • Environmental care in and around the animal facilities (garden work, small maintenance work)
  • Participation in veterinary treatment and animal transport

What you bring

  • Joy of physical work with animals as well as with people
  • Visitor-oriented, friendly appearance
  • Teamwork, independence and commitment
  • Readiness for weekend and holiday service / flexibility

What we offer you

  • An extraordinary place to work in the zoo
  • Versatile and exciting field of activity
  • A dedicated and collaborative team
