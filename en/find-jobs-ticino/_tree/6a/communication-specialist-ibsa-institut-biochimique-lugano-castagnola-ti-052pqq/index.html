Full description

Location description

The position is included in the Corporate Communication & IBSA Foundation, as part of the management of business communication activities.

Responsibility and activity

  • Support in the creation of IBSA Group's institutional communication plans
  • Creating content and managing online and offline communication, both internal and external
  • Building and updating of materials and institutional communication platforms
  • Collaboration with the organization of events and meetings
  • IBSA Group PR activities management
  • Coordination of external agencies and partners for specific communication activities
  • Collaboration and interface with various business functions for the activation of required communication activities

Profile requested

  • Degree/Master in communication, humanities, scientific, economic or related subjects.
  • At least 3 years of experience in communication, content creation, digital communication and PR, ideally at communication departments of companies, associations or communication agencies
  • Experience in content planning, creating content for social media, storytelling strategies and coordinating external/client agencies
  • Experience in PR business management
  • Knowledge of the functioning of social platforms (LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram)
  • Italian compulsory, excellent knowledge of English written and spoken (C1)
  • Teamwork attitude, good organizational skills of personal work and agencies, dynamics and hourly flexibility

Nice to have

  • Basic knowledge of photography and video realization
  • SEO Knowledge
  • Good command of French and/or German
  • Experience using CMS (content management systems)
