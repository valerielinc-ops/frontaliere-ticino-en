Head of Party (M/F)

Are you a passionate cook, rigorous and eager to express your talent in a demanding brigade? We are looking for an independent and committed Head of Party to join our team. You will benefit from attractive working conditions in continuous schedules and will play a key role in the success of our culinary services.

YOUR CHALLENGES TO QUOTA

  • Production \& Excellence: Sublimate the culinary preparations and implement them in strict compliance with technical specifications and quality standards.
  • Hygiene \& Safety (HACCP): Ensure exemplary cleanliness of your position, ensure food labelling and rigorously enforce HACCP standards.
  • Inventory Management: Control receipt of goods, ensure optimal product rotation (FIFO) and prevent ruptures.
  • Leadership \& Autonomy: Provide leadership and overall responsibility for the kitchen in the absence of the Chef and Deputy Chefs.

VOTRE PROFIL

  • Training: Cook CFC holder (or equivalent training).
  • Experience: Minimum 3 years experience confirmed in the same position.
  • Knowledge: Proven mastery of culinary techniques, post management and hygiene standards.
  • Knowledge: Responsibility, rigour, autonomy, team spirit and excellent communication.

WHAT WE OFFER YOU

  • Quality of life at work: Post in continuous hours, guaranteeing a balanced rhythm of life.
  • Stimulating project: Quality cuisine that values teamwork and skills development.
  • Responsibilities: A central role within the brigade with real potential for autonomy.
