Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Niedersimmental, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Wimmis and Därstetten, we are looking for a personality as a customer advisor.

What awaits you?

  • Recognize and exploit the customer potential through holistic advice and support of customers in the basic products of the product ranges "Savings, figures, investment and provision"
  • Reception and service of customers in the customer zone
  • Active potential recognition and forwarding of customers to the responsible specialists
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures, in particular in contact campaigns
  • Participation in staff administration
  • Accompaniment of learners

What do you bring?

  • Completed vocational training as a businessman / business assistant ideally in the banking environment
  • Enthusiastic and customer-oriented personality with a winning appearance
  • Self-employed, exact and responsible work with pleasure in direct customer contact

Preferably

  • Completed training as a practitioner
  • Training in human resources

We do not focus on products, but on people. Anyone who enjoys contact with customers, takes responsibility and wants to be part of a collegial team will find an exciting environment with a lot of room for manoeuvre. Sarah Messerli Head of Customer Services Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We enable the purchase of additional holiday weeks

Our employees can purchase up to two additional holiday weeks per year in addition to the regular holiday days.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
