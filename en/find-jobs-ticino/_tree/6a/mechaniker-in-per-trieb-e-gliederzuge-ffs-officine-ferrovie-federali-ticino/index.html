SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. With your team and concentrated expertise, you make an important contribution to public transport. The Olten plant is responsible for revisions, repairs and modernisation of trains. With the maintenance team, we ensure the safe operation of our fleets. An exciting task awaits you. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • In this interesting function, you are responsible for carrying out commissioning, maintenance, repair and inspection work on train and articulated trains.
  • You work closely with technology, clarify mechanical interfaces, provide employees with relevant information and make smaller decisions independently within the scope of your responsibility
  • Independently or together as a team, you carry out the commissioning of train and link trains.
  • On individual components or on the complete system you diagnose and repair the existing faults.
  • You are responsible for the entrance examinations as well as the final inspections.
  • Safety and quality standards are always in the foreground at SBB and also at you.

You bring that with you.

  • Teamwork is our and your key to success. You are independent, flexible and approach things goal-oriented, with enthusiasm and as a team player. You are aware of quality and safety and remain calm, thoughtful and serene even in dynamic situations.
  • In your career so far, you have been able to gain several years of professional experience in the field of electrics, electromechanics, automatics or maintenance. In addition to your well-founded equipment, you can also have several years of experience in the commissioning of systems and plants. In order to optimally prepare you for this position, we train you internally, specifically as a professional.
  • All instructions and documentation are digital with us. You are not afraid of modern work equipment and computer science programs.
  • For this position, you need a completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ in the mechanical field and a degree as a mechanical engineer: in, or related university degree. The activities are primarily focused on the mechanical (70%). The electrical component is about 30%.
  • We expect good to very good oral and written knowledge of German. Other national languages such as French and/or Italian are a plus that we appreciate very much.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
