Full description

For Raiffeisenbank Rigi, a successful and regionally oriented bank around Rigi, we are looking for an independent, committed and service-oriented personality as a complement to the existing HR team. As a junior manager and HR specialist, you also live the values of Raiffeisen-bank Rigi: collaborative, authentic, committed and focused.

Tasks

  • Responsibility and supervision of our six learners over all apprenticeship years
  • Coordination and communication with the interfaces CYP, vocational school and teaching
  • First contact person for our practical trainees, control of the learning processes, implementation of experience exchanges/presentations, training courses, semester interviews, etc.
  • Coordination of up-and-coming-generation programmes (e.g. trainee, lateral entrant programme, etc.)
  • Opportunity for participation and implementation of junior projects
  • Support of the Raiffeisenbank Rigi HR team with 75 employees
  • Recruitment of various functions of the whole bank
  • Administrative activities related to staff

Requirements

  • Successfully completed banking training
  • Further training in the HR area (e.g. HR specialist certificate)
  • Diploma "vocational trainers in teaching enterprises" completed or willing to do so
  • Designated professional experience of several years in cooperation with learners
  • First experience with the KV reform as a professional or practical trainer:in
  • Self-responsible, solution-oriented and structured working method
  • Empathetic, discreet and flexible personality

We offer

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.
