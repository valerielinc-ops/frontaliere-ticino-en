Full description

Your heart beats for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to contribute your skills and experience to us in a meaningful way in order to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you fit perfectly with us!

For the Tertianum Kirchdorf / Kappelerhof in the Aargau region, we are looking for an

Head of Care and Care 100%

Their tasks

  • Responsible for the quality and implementation of the service mandate and the maintenance process
  • Further development of the care & care area for our number of care guests
  • Controlling quality and productivity (bed occupancy, performance recording in the CareCoach)
  • Adherence to budgetary requirements and achievement of the objectives of the area
  • Organisation and coordination of the various stakeholders (members, family doctors, other organisations and institutions as well as insurance companies)
  • Management of nurses
  • Recruitment and further development process of employees
  • Close cooperation with management

Your profile

  • Completed training for/for dipl. FH/HF/DNII nurse or equivalent training recognised by the SRC
  • Professional and leadership experience in long-term care
  • At least 1-2 years of management experience as management nursing & support or at least 3-4 years of professional experience as group management
  • Dynamic leader
  • Secure communication in the German language in word and writing
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • High empathy and special team skills

A regional network with further lines of care and support gives you exchange opportunities and mutual support. Tertianum also offers interesting opportunities in specialist and management careers.
