Your job: Everything about the tire

BestDrive by Continental is the new service brand of Continental AG with around 70 own branches throughout Switzerland. In addition, 47 FleetPartners are expanding the network. The new brand will gradually emerge from the Pneu Egger and Adam Touring brands and will visibly carry the long-standing membership of the Continental Group to the outside world. We offer car service, tires and accessories in our branches: for a perfectly maintained vehicle that guarantees great driving comfort and maximum safety in every traffic situation. In the tire trade, we have been the leading company in Switzerland for years.

This is to be done

Work on changing tires for people, delivery and trucks

  • Mounting and balancing of tyres and wheels
  • Repair of damaged tyres and hoses
  • Check the chassis and adjust the steering geometry
  • Easier control and service activities such as measuring oil levels, refilling oil, brake and battery fluid or checking exterior lighting.

This is what we expect

  • School education completed
  • Technical and craftsmanship
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Safe and well-kept appearance
  • Interest in the automobile or Tyre industry

In addition to a varied and interesting job in a well-established team, we can also offer you contemporary social benefits and an interesting remuneration in a modern and secure workplace.
