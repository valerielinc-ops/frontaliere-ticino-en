Full description

The place of work of this office is in Visp, Switzerland.

Are you interested in biotechnology, chemistry or biology and at the same time want to experience how digitalization and artificial intelligence can be used in a modern production environment? Then this internship offers you the opportunity to combine science, production and digital technologies.

This body requires a continuous presence on site. Personal collaboration enables close, timely cooperation and the technical precision required for the production of medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What we offer

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical working environment.
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance.
  • A variety of advantages, depending on function and location.

Their tasks

  • Support in the digitization and structuring of knowledge from different production areas
  • Participation in the development of a central knowledge or Information database
  • Support in the development of a solution with which information from different production areas can be queried easily and efficiently
  • Testing and using AI-based applications to make existing knowledge more accessible and usable
  • Collaboration with employees from production, technology and other specialist areas in order to identify relevant knowledge and structure it in a meaningful way
  • Support for further digitalization and optimization initiatives in the production environment

What we are looking for

  • Completed Matura or ongoing studies, ideally with an interest in biotechnology, biology, chemistry, life sciences or a related field
  • Great interest in digitalization and new technologies
  • IT affinity and interest in artificial intelligence are beneficial
  • Enjoy understanding, structuring and making complex information easily accessible
  • Independent, structured and curious working method
  • Good communication skills and enjoy working with different teams
  • Good knowledge of English
  • Knowledge of German is an advantage

About Lonza At Lonza, our employees are our greatest strength. At more than 30 locations on five continents, our globally networked teams work together every day to produce the medicines of tomorrow. Our core values of collaboration, responsibility, excellence, passion and integrity reflect who we are and how we work together. Every idea, big or small and from whomever it comes, has the potential to improve the lives of millions of people, and this is exactly the kind of work we want you to participate in.

Innovation thrives when people from different backgrounds bring in their unique perspectives. At Lonza, we value diversity and are committed to an inclusive environment for all employees. If you are ready to translate the groundbreaking ideas of our customers into practical therapies, we look forward to welcoming you to our team.
