Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Sensetal Freiburg-Ost is one of the largest Raiffeisen banks in Switzerland. With 118 employees, including 10 apprentices, we are an attractive employer with strong regional roots. We cultivate a corporate culture based on partnership, which is characterized by dialogue, fairness and social responsibility. The award with the Swiss Employer Award confirms this claim and makes us proud. As an innovative cooperative bank, we have consistently developed into a consulting bank in recent years. We actively seek encounters with people and realize dreams of our customers. For our successful, modern bank, we are looking for two independent, committed and service-oriented personalities as Junior Private Client Advisor Individual (m/f/d) with further development potential or as Private Client Advisor Individual (m/f/d) with experience.

What awaits you?

  • Recognizing and exploiting the customer potential through active and continuous support of the personal customer portfolio
  • Competent and comprehensive advice and support for private clients in all financial matters such as mortgages, investing, provisioning
  • Maintenance and expansion of an existing portfolio through active cross-selling and targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the individual needs, wishes, goals and living conditions of the customers as well as within the segment-specific product range
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures such as contact campaigns, events and representative tasks
  • Active representation of our bank in the local market

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training in or with corresponding further training in the financial sector
  • Several years and broad experience in private customer business are advantageous
  • Enjoyment in dealing with customers, a healthy dose of ambition and a safe and winning appearance
  • Flair for customer acquisition as well as customer and service orientation
  • Social competence and a high sense of responsibility
  • Regionally and locally well networked
  • Knowledge of French is beneficial
