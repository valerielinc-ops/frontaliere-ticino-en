Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process.

Your responsibilities

As a school social worker you work according to the methods and principles of social work and in close cooperation with parents, teachers and carers as well as the school management.

Your tasks

Your responsibilities include in particular

  • Advice and support for children in personal and social issues
  • Cooperation with parents, teachers, carers and school management
  • Advising groups and classes
  • Early detection and prevention
  • Networking and cooperation with specialist bodies and other institutions
  • Cooperation in projects and offers of school social work

Your profile

You are an initiative person who

  • has a diploma in social work and ideally experience in school social work or in the field of child and youth welfare.
  • Enjoy working with children and young people as well as working with their caregivers.
  • communication strong, resilient and flexible.
  • an independent, solution-oriented and interdisciplinary working method.
  • even in challenging situations acts professionally and calmly.
  • gladly takes responsibility and actively participates in the team and in the school.
  • is motivated and fulfills the entrusted tasks with commitment and joy.

More on the spot

Further information on school social work at the City of Lucerne Primary School can be found at www.schulsozialarbeit.stadtluzern.ch

We offer

  • a versatile, varied and interesting activity in the school environment.
  • current employment conditions according to cantonal specifications.
  • An appreciative and good working atmosphere.
  • professional support from experienced teammates.
  • the opportunity to contribute your expertise and ideas.
  • an activity with room for manoeuvre and direct contact with children and their environment.
