For sport and movement in Switzerland

The Federal Office for Sport (BASPO) promotes sport and its values - for all age and performance groups. The BASPO brings together sports policy, sports promotion and training: this includes the largest Swiss sports promotion programme Youth+Sport and training centres in Magglingen and Tenero. The Swiss Federal University of Sport Magglingen EHSM is the only Swiss university with education, development, research and services in the field of sport.
