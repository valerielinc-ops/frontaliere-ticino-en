# Generalist Human resources

The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI) puts in competition the position of at the

Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI) / Human Resources Office

based in Lugano. Degree of employment 100%, indefinitely. Entry into operation: from immediately or date to be agreed.

Professional background

The SUPSI Human Resources Service operates in a matrix organization: the Department Human Resources Service is the point of reference in Human Resources for staff and managers, to which it provides advice and administrative support, and collaborates with the Central Services to ensure consistency, quality and compliance along the staff life cycle. Within the DTI Human Resources Office, the selected person independently manages the administrative practices of the staff of the Department, responds to the demands of the staff and managers and ensures the correct collection, verification and transmission of data to the Central Services, in compliance with the values of professionalism, transparency and collaboration that characterize the SUPSI.

Main handling

  • Management of administrative practices related to the lifecycle of the staff of the Department: recruitments, contractual variations, terminations, certificates and data update.
  • Management of administrative practices relating to external mandates.
  • Management of presences, absences and holidays through dedicated systems, ensuring the correctness of data and their correct transmission to the Central Services.
  • Preparation and updating of contractual and administrative documentation, in accordance with the SUPSI directives and current legislation.
  • Advice and administrative support to staff and department managers on contracts, working hours, permits, allowances and internal procedures, as a reference point for proximity to Human Resources.
  • Support for recruitment and selection processes in collaboration with line managers and the Central Human Resource Service: announcements publication, application management, summons and communications.
  • Collaboration with the projects of the Service in the context of digitization, continuous improvement of processes and quality assurance system.

Requirements

  • AFC with professional maturity or academic qualification SUP (minimum Bachelor).
  • Federal Professional Certificate of Human Resource Specialist.
  • Minimum 4 years experience in function.
  • Applied and operational knowledge of Swiss labour law and social insurance.
  • Italian native language or level C1
  • English B1; ideally another national language.
  • Good IT skills (Office package) and management programs in Human Resources (Abacus, Microsoft Dynamics).
  • Accuracy, sense of responsibility, organizational and planning skills, excellent relational skills, attitude to group work and absolute discretion.

What we offer

A qualified and autonomous role within a dedicated team, in an institutional context attentive to the quality and continuous improvement of processes. The position is in Fascia 3 of the SUPSI Collective Contract (CCLSUPSI).
