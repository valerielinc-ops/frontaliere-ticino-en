Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for someone who knows how to truly put customer needs at the center, relating to them with friendliness, sensitivity, and empathy. Does the customer always come first for you, without losing sight of business dynamics and objectives?

Duties

  • Provide consultation, primarily by phone, and sell fuels, liquid fuels, and technical services to private and corporate clients
  • Proactively manage and develop the existing client portfolio
  • Acquire new clients, with a focus on the regional market, both corporate and private
  • Monitor the Ticino market, analyze trends and commercial opportunities, collaborating closely with regional management
  • Record and manage orders received by phone or in writing, ensuring correct updating of customer data in company operating systems
  • Prepare commercial offers and promote special actions through phone contacts and targeted email campaigns
  • Verify customer solvency in accordance with company directives, process credit requests, and contact delinquent debtors by phone for debt collection purposes

Requirements

  • A minimum of 3 years of practical experience in the sector: In telephone sales or another sales-oriented activity with direct customer contact
  • Completed professional apprenticeship (VET/AFC): Sales sector, commercial specialization
  • Italian (Excellent level) German (Good level): Ticino dialect (preferred)
  • Excellent sales skills
  • Resilient, dynamic, and enthusiastic personality
  • Good computer skills (SAP)
  • Good communication skills and customer-oriented interpersonal skills
  • Good ability to work in a team and team spirit
  • Solid network of contacts in the Ticino economic context
  • C residence permit or Swiss citizenship
