Full description

The movement is in our DNA - since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow up with us. Join our family and shape with us the future of transport and storage logistics.

What You Do

  • You discover the world of transport and logistics from A to Z
  • You help customers, process requests and organize orders as a team
  • You're comfortable with numbers, help with accounting and manage data.
  • You use foreign languages in a targeted way, you organize meetings and notes written information

Which will allow you to succeed

  • You like human contact, you like teamwork and you know how to adapt to others
  • You are interested in the economy, organization and modern IT tools
  • You're comfortable expressing yourself both orally and in writing and you communicate clearly
  • You're comfortable with the numbers.
  • You are a self-reliant, reliable and always ready to learn and you are flexible even when there is change

Your Benefits

  • Family Enterprise Culture: Respect, trust and fair play are important to us – we say "you" and we accompany you personally during your learning.
  • Vacations: In the first year, you have 8 weeks of vacation, in the second year 7 weeks and the following years 6 weeks.
  • Events: During your training with us, you will take part in many events such as the reception day, the deepening seminar, the apprenticeship camp, the end party, etc.
  • Financial support: At home, you get a fair salary with a thirteenth month. If you do a good job at school, in business classes and in the company, you can even get a bonus. In addition, we pay your tuition costs, we offer you a contribution to purchase a laptop and we give you a half-rate subscription for the duration of your apprenticeship.
  • Language stay: Want a language stay? With us, you can improve your language skills while living full of rewarding experiences!
  • Permanent employment: At the end of your apprenticeship, you have a good chance of getting an indefinite contract.

Who are we? As a company, we attach great importance to collaboration and a good working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, availability and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis of a reliable and customer-oriented method of work.
