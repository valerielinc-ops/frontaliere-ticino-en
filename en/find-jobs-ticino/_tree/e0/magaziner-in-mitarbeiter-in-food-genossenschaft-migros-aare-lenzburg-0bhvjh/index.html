Full description

Do you want to combine your sales talent with your coordination skills? Are you interested in warehousing and goods management and also like to communicate with our customers? As a magazine / employee of Food, you are responsible for ensuring that the right products end up in the right place.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, MM Lenzburg, Bachstrasse 15, 5600 Lenzburg

Important information

Start of work from 06:00 We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You coordinate the warehouse management
  • You load and unload the trucks
  • You advise and serve our customers with pleasure and your expertise
  • An attractive presentation of goods, efficient goods management and ensuring availability are your focus
  • You support actively and engaged in other departments

Requirements

  • Ideal professional experience: in the field of warehouse management & food
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade
  • As a customer-oriented personality with pleasure in sales, you make shopping a positive experience
  • You have a good physical condition
  • PC skills and high digital fitness set you apart
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks paternity leave
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation on self-selected courses
  • Sports and fitness offerings: cost sharing of subscriptions to over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, aquaparks
  • Mobility: participation of an annual subscription to public transport, discount on fuel (Migrol), reduced annual contribution for mobility carsharing and entitlement to a fleet discount
  • Mobile Subscriptions: Various Perks on Mobile Subscriptions
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest on capital or a discount on interest rates on newly concluded fixed mortgages
  • Employee offers: Preferential conditions for insurance and health insurance funds
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
