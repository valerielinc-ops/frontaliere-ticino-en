Full description

Construction Manager / Chief Installer / Electromechanics BSA Started in 1922 as a small family business, it has grown over the decades. Today, more than 80 Group companies from all areas of the construction industry belong to us. One of them is us, Technik AG. As an internationally active, innovative technology company, Technik AG offers various products and services in the fields of conveying technology, electrical engineering, formwork construction, special machine construction and infrastructure facilities. Our products and services are characterized by the highest quality, forward-looking technology and customer-oriented thinking. In order to strengthen our team in Upper Valais, we are looking for a committed and ambitious personality who, with passion and team spirit, advances our common goals. Construction Manager / Chief Installer / Electromechanics BSA You can move it with us. You manage your construction sites and ensure the professional and timely order processing. You competently represent Technik AG vis-à-vis the building owner, the planners and suppliers. With your competence and your appearance, you gain trust and thus follow-up orders.

  • You lead forward and plan employee resources. You promote and develop your team and make sure that the regulations are adhered to.
  • As part of the entire construction site process, you take responsibility for AVOR, extent and billing.
  • Additional tasks:
  • Electrical installations in a commercial and industrial building and in transport
  • Responsible for forward processing
  • Compliance with project specifications, quality and deadlines
  • Management of materials and tools
  • Management and control of Baujournal / Regierapporte
  • Assistance in commissioning
  • Planning and organisation of required acceptances
  • Responsibility for occupational safety on the construction site
  • What you bring with you
  • Completed training with EFZ as an electrical installer, network electrician or assembly electrician with several years of experience
  • Additional technical training and/or experience in group management are advantageous
  • Thanks to your flexible and responsible nature, you also master demanding situations reliably and quality-consciously.
  • When dealing with customers and suppliers, you convince through expert communication and negotiating skills
  • Read and understand plans and schemes
  • Reliable and independent work
  • Readiness for further training
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Computer knowledge
  • Live with us professionally
  • We offer you a versatile and varied task in a diverse environment, attractive conditions and very good social services.
