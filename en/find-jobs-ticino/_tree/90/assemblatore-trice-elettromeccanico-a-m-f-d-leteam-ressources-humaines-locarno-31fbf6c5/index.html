Company description

Our agency has more than 39 years of experience in research, selection and placement of permanent and temporary staff in the fields of construction, crafts, industry, technology, administration and pharmaceuticals. We are present in 28 branches throughout Switzerland and Liechtenstein. For our important Sopraceneri customer, we are looking for the (M/F/D) electromechanical/electromechanical assembly/machine.

Location description

  • Electromechanical assembly: Mount mechanical and electrical components of UPS modules and systems by following operational instructions, technical drawings and business procedures.
  • Electric cabling: Perform electrical connections, laying and fixing of cables and components according to technical specifications.
  • Quality control: Check the correct execution of the operations, carry out the planned self-controls and report any anomalies or non-compliance.
  • Using tools: Use manual, electrical and clamping tools correctly, respecting tightening pairs and marking procedures.
  • Material management: Check the availability and compliance of the components necessary for assembly activities.
  • Security and organization: Respect HSE procedures and maintain order and cleanliness of positions according to 5S principles.
  • Continuous improvement: Collaboration with the achievement of the productive objectives, respecting cycle times and contributing to the reduction of waste.

  • Experience in mechanical, electrical or electromechanical assembly in industrial contexts.
  • Good dexterity and familiarity with the use of tools and mounting equipment.
  • Ability to read and interpret simple technical drawings and assembly instructions.
  • Basic knowledge of mechanical and electrical components.
  • Precision, attention to detail and quality orientation.
  • Ability to work on team and respect production timing.
  • Reliability, flexibility and availability to work on shifts.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
