Full description

The Wellnesshotel Schweizerhof is one of the leading first-class hotels in Saas-Fee with the following capacities: 40 comfort rooms and 8 suites (90 beds), 120 seats in the Hofsaal restaurant, 40 seats in the lounge and bar. It is centrally located yet quiet with a fantastic view of the Allalinhorn. A vinothek with fine Valais drops, fine evening menus and specialties in the Hofsaal restaurant as well as 1000 sqm spa with swimming pool, whirlpool, sauna landscape and fitness room for relaxation round off the offer.

Explore more! Do you want to spend a new evening in the Valais mountains? We are a young, competent, established team looking for a new member. We strive for high guest satisfaction, love the service of the guest and stand for quality instead of quantity. Interested?

Your tasks

  • Cleaning and care of guest rooms according to the highest quality and hygiene standards
  • Making beds and changing bed linen to offer guests a fresh and pleasant stay
  • Bathroom cleaning, changing towels and filling toiletries
  • General cleaning work in public areas, such as lobby, stairwells and corridors
  • Cleaning and maintenance of hotel and table linen in the in-house laundry
  • Support for ironing and mangle work
  • Notification of defects or repair needs to the responsible departments
  • Compliance with HACCP and safety regulations

Your profile

  • Experience in a similar position in the hospitality or building cleaning industry
  • Responsibility and resilience – you work efficiently and conscientiously
  • Good knowledge of German is an advantage, English or other languages are a plus
  • Careful and fast working with an eye for details
  • Teamwork and helpfulness to maintain high standards together
  • Flexibility and willingness for different working hours
  • Compliance with the dress code and company guidelines
  • Knowledge and implementation of all safety regulations in the housekeeping sector
  • Friendly appearance and pleasure in dealing with international guests
