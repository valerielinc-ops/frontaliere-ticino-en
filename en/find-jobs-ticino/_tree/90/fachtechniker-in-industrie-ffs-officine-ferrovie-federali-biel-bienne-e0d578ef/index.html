  • As a specialist technician: in industry, you carry out demanding refurbishment and maintenance work, repairs and revisions of components of diesel and special vehicles.
  • You exchange and check components and implement control and change orders according to SBB specifications.
  • Deviations are detected early on and ensure seamless documentation.
  • If necessary, you will skillfully produce special constructions and thus make a significant contribution to smooth rail traffic in Switzerland.
  • You actively participate in continuous improvement processes, measure and document process steps and keep your work clean and traceable.
  • Together in the team you will find economic solutions and bring constructive suggestions for improvement.

You bring that with you.

  • You enjoy working in a team, but you also complete tasks independently and independently. Then you go with me.

Changes, are flexible and have fun in a versatile and varied field of activity.

  • Since all instructions are available electronically, you work safely with MS-Office and SAP applications.
  • You have a high level of safety and quality awareness, a quick apprehension as well as a willingness to learn and flexibility.
  • Due to safety requirements, good health is important: unrestricted hearing and vision as well as physical resilience.
  • For this function you have a completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ in the craft sector (e.g. polymechanic:in, production mechanic:in, agricultural machinery mechanic:in) and several years of experience in an industrial environment.
  • You also have good oral and written knowledge of German or French (at least B1). Other national languages are a plus.
