Specialist technicians: in industry 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Specialist technicians: in industry Biel/Bienne, from now on or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

In a dedicated and motivated team, you work with modern infrastructure and a versatile machine park on challenging craftsmanship tasks. You bring your craftsmanship and technical skills to bear in the processing of various components of diesel and special vehicles. With your use, you ensure that these can be used reliably and safely in operation and thus make an important contribution to public transport in Switzerland. In doing so, we support you in further developing your expertise and building new skills. Thanks to a wide range of internal training offers, you also have various development opportunities within SBB. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • As a specialist technician: in industry, you carry out demanding refurbishment, maintenance, repairs and inspections on various components, mainly bogies, of diesel and special vehicles.
  • You exchange and check different components and implement control and change orders according to SBB specifications. You recognize deviations early and document your work seamlessly.
  • If necessary, you will produce special constructions and thus make a significant contribution to smooth rail traffic in Switzerland.
  • You actively participate in continuous improvement processes, measure and document process steps and keep your work clean and traceable.
  • In the team, you bring economic solutions and constructive suggestions for improvement.

You bring that with you.

  • You are flexible, open to change and appreciate a varied and versatile working environment.
  • A quick comprehension, a high willingness to learn as well as a pronounced safety and quality awareness distinguish you.
  • You have a completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ in the craft sector (e.g. polymechanic:in, production mechanic:in, agricultural machinery mechanic:in, car mechanic:in) and some years of experience in an industrial environment.
  • For this activity, you bring the necessary physical fitness and resilience and meet the requirements for safety-relevant work (no color vision disorder).
  • In addition, you have good oral and written knowledge of German or French (min. B1).
