Then you are exactly right with us! This versatile and fulfilling task offers you the opportunity to work in the palliative care unit as well as in the acute hospital and outpatient area – in a team that lives cohesion, openness and mutual support. 🌸 Your tasks

  • Physiotherapeutic care of patients in the palliative care unit
  • Participation in interdisciplinary team meetings and quality assurance
  • Perform individual treatments in various departments in the acute hospital
  • Outpatient treatments in various specialist areas
  • Participation in group therapies, student education and exciting projects
  • Active exchange in the team, participation in training and internal intervisions

🌼 Your profile

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist (SRK-approved) or Bachelor in Physical Therapy
  • Ideally, experience in palliative care – or interest in further education in this field
  • You work resource-oriented, evidence-based and with heart
  • You are flexible, resilient and find joy in the lively hospital everyday life
  • You are digitally fit and appreciate the open exchange in the team
  • Re-entrants to physiotherapy are also welcome!

🌻 What we offer you

  • A motivated, interprofessional team with a lot of heart and humor
  • Bright, modern therapy rooms and a new MTT training room
  • An appreciative working environment with a family atmosphere
  • Support from our therapy secretariat and flexible working hours
  • Meaningful work with design freedom and development possibilities
