Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The University of Freiburg is an educational institution, research centre, employer and event organiser all in one. Its innovative power makes it an important engine for the economic and cultural life of the region. As an attraction for students and researchers from all over Switzerland and from all parts of the world, the university has shaped the life of the bilingual city of Freiburg since its foundation in 1889.

Become part of our team!

We focus on the complementarity of competences and the diversity of perspectives, because it is the wide range of careers and profiles that makes up the strength of the state of Freiburg. Their tasks

  • Department administration in all respects (study offer and student dossiers, personnel dossiers, business credit, third-party funding, exams, appointment calendar, website, protocol management, etc.)
  • Coordination and organisation of activities of the Faculty and Rectorate in the Department
  • Administrative cooperation with the Institute for Multilingualism (information, activities, etc.)
  • Uni-internal communication (students, departmental team, other departments, Faculty of Philosophy and Faculty of EduForm, central services)
  • External communication (prospective students, institutions, media, etc.)

Desired profile

  • EFZ as a merchant/merchant or equivalent training
  • Experience in administrative and organisational work
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Very good planning and organizational skills
  • Personal responsibility, flexibility and service orientation
  • Confident knowledge of German and French, solid knowledge of English
