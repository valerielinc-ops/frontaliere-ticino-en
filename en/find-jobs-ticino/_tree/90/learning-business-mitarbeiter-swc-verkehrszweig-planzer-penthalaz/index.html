Full description

The movement has been in our DNA since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow up with us. Join our family and shape with us the future of transport and storage logistics.

Aufgaben

  • You discover the world of transport and logistics from A to Z
  • You help customers, process requests and organize orders as a team
  • You're comfortable with numbers, help with accounting and manage data.
  • You use foreign languages in a targeted way, you organize meetings and notes information in writing

Profile

  • You like human contact, you like teamwork and you know how to adapt to others
  • You're interested in modern economy, organization and IT tools
  • You're comfortable expressing yourself both orally and in writing and you communicate clearly
  • You're comfortable with numbers.
  • You are a self-reliant, reliable, and always ready to learn and you are flexible even when there is change

Benefits

  • Respect, trust and fair play are important to us – we say "you" and we accompany you personally during your learning.
  • In the first year, you have 8 weeks of vacation, in the second year 7 weeks and the next 6 weeks.
  • During your training with us, you will participate in many events such as the reception day, the deepening seminar, the apprenticeship camp, the end of apprenticeship party, etc.
  • In our house, you get a fair salary with a thirteenth month. If you do a good job at school, in business classes and in the company, you can even get a bonus. Plus, we pay your tuition costs, we offer you a contribution to buy a laptop and we give you a half-rate subscription for the duration of your apprenticeship.
  • You are helped to study independently in the company, you are regularly offered study hours with other apprentices and you are encouraged to take support courses.
  • You can deepen your theoretical and practical knowledge in our apprenticeship spaces.

As a company, we attach great importance to collaboration and a good working atmosphere. This is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, availability and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis of a reliable and customer-oriented method of work.
