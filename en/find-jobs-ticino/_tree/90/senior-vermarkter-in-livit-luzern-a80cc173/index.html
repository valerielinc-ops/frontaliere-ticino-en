Rental management Lucerne | Pensum: 100%

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? In rental management, you are looking for the perfect match and bring residential and commercial properties together with the right tenants. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • Independent responsibility and project management for the marketing of residential and business properties
  • Development of creative offers for rental contracts. Location/market and object analyses in cooperation with the direct supervisor
  • Advising clients: inside (owner: inside) and tenants as well as conducting final negotiations
  • Preparation of rental quotes
  • Development of individual use and expansion concepts for business premises as well as advertising and marketing concepts
  • Planning, preparation and commissioning of marketing measures and organisation and implementation of marketing events
  • Controlling/Reporting of implemented measures and checking of relevance and periodic rental reports / regular activity reporting to the clients:
  • Personal handover of dismissals to tenants: incl. pre- and follow-up care
  • Assistance in the further development of the business area of first letting
  • Maintaining customer relationships and establishing new contacts to acquire new mandates
  • Professional management of the rental consultants:innen Remietung Lucerne

Your profile

  • You have a completed CV apprenticeship or equivalent education
  • You have an oath. Professional ID as a real estate marketer or an adequate training in marketing
  • You have several years of professional experience as a marketer in the real estate industry or as a real estate manager
  • You are a sales talent with strong persuasion and a strong service orientation
  • You have a professional and competent appearance combined with winning manners
  • You think and act entrepreneurially and maintain an independent and proactive way of working
  • You bring an affinity to technology
  • You are interested in digital marketing and work tools and have the ability to actively contribute to change
  • You can communicate verbally and in writing in German and English
