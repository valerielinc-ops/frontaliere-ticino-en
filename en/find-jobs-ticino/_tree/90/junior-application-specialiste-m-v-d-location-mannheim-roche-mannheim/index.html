What to expect We are looking for a product specialist (m/f/d) for the Core Lab Application area in the Regional Customer Service Center (RCSC) Mannheim. Based in Mannheim, we serve numerous customers in a total of seven countries and offer remote, telephone and email support for inquiries and complaints about our Roche analysis systems. In the advertised position you provide support with a focus on the German-speaking area (Germany, Austria, Switzerland). Your main tasks are:

  • Support our customers and field employees from Germany, Austria and Switzerland, if necessary also from another country of the RCSC.
  • You identify customer-specific problems, develop solutions and initiate appropriate measures.
  • You document your activities carefully in the supporting CRM system.
  • Occasionally, you also support field service colleagues during service missions in the customer laboratory on site.

Who you are

  • You speak and write the RCSC language German fluently. In addition, good technical and general language English skills in word and writing are necessary. Language skills of another RCSC language (Dutch, Greek, Turkish) are advantageous.
  • You have an education as a medical/biological technical assistant (MTA/BTA) or have successfully completed studies in biology, biochemistry or another scientific subject.
  • Ideally, you will bring your first professional experience in the medical and technical environment, which you have gained either by working in a medical laboratory or in customer support in sales.
  • Application problems fascinate you and you will always find fast and good solutions based on a methodical troubleshooting.
  • Customer satisfaction is your strongest motivation.
  • When working in a team Your strength: Together with others, complex problems can also be solved.
