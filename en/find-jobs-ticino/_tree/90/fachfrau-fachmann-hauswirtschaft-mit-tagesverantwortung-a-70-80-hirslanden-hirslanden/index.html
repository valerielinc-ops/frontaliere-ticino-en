Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Salem-Spital | Bern Occupation by: Immediately or by appointment

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

For our housekeeping team in the Salem Hospital, we are looking for a committed and reliable person who cares for cleanliness and hygiene with care and dedication – and thus contributes to the well-being of our patients.

Your Advantages in Us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in the Fitness Abo & Entrance to the indoor pool

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • You take on independent and professional maintenance, intermediate and basic cleaning work
  • You share the day-to-day responsibility with another specialist and take over these alternately
  • As a day-to-day responsibility, you coordinate the cleaning work and ensure that the specified quality, hygiene and safety standards are met.
  • You prepare the exit list and make sure that the upcoming exit cleanings are carried out accordingly
  • You support the team in the daily cleaning work and are a reliable contact person for questions
  • In the absence of the supervisor, you organize personnel bottlenecks or failures and ensure a smooth flow of day-to-day business.
  • In the daily responsibility until you are responsible for the cleanliness and order of the laundry room as well as for the professional washing and preparation of the daily laundry
  • You are jointly responsible for the maintenance of equipment and machinery as well as the management of the warehouse
  • You recognize defects, damages or special situations at an early stage, initiate the necessary measures and report them to the competent body
  • You support the implementation of quality controls and contribute to the continuous assurance of cleaning quality
  • You work closely with the various professional groups and departments and ensure the flow of information
  • You take on special tasks if necessary and support with organizational and operational concerns
  • You work efficiently, economically and responsibly with materials, equipment and resources
  • In addition, you take on a mission on Sunday once or twice a month.
  • Two to three times a year you take a piquette from Saturday to Sunday

Your profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as a specialist EFZ or a comparable qualification
  • You bring in-depth professional experience in the domestic sector, ideally in hospital or health care
  • Dedication and willingness to learn, as well as quality awareness are among your strengths
  • You work independently, reliably and are helpful
  • You are organizationally adept, set priorities right and can react flexibly even in the event of short-term changes
  • Even in stressful situations you keep a cool head and keep the overview
  • You have a strong hygiene and safety awareness
  • Teamwork and the joy of working with employees from different cultures distinguish you
  • You work customer-, service- and service-oriented and enjoy taking responsibility
  • A well-kept appearance and very good knowledge of German round off your profile

For more information, Nadine Stroh, Head of Housekeeping Salem-Spital, is available at T. We look forward to your contact.
