Full description

At Sunrise, we think bigger, go further and create new things. For us, working culture means: creating great things together. Here respect and fresh ideas meet real teamwork – every voice counts, every perspective makes us stronger. Our passion drives us to dare new things and to constantly grow. Sounds like you? Then become part of our success story.

Our front-line heroes are the flagship of Sunrise. You score with tailor-made solutions and top service - so every customer contact becomes a long-term connection.

Your challenge

  • You are the face of Sunrise and inspire our customers with your positive charisma, your targeted advice and the sale of our diverse Sunrise products.
  • You build long-term customer relationships and provide a first-class atmosphere in the shop.
  • You support customers with repair and service concerns and act professionally and solution-oriented.
  • You keep track of stock and inventory and perform administrative tasks efficiently.
  • You live and represent the Sunrise values – innovation, customer proximity and top service.

Your kills

  • You have completed training in the retail trade, ideally with experience in the telecommunications or multimedia industry – or you are a motivated lateral entrant with a sales flair.
  • You bubbly with enthusiasm for technology, telecommunications and digital trends.
  • Your passion for sales is noticeable and your professional appearance makes you a real sales professional.
  • You enjoy dealing with people and the ability to quickly recognize their needs.
  • Thanks to your patience and technical understanding, you will become an everyday hero for our customers.
  • You have very good knowledge of German, every other language is a plus.
  • You bring a great deal of flexibility, reliability and openness to a fast-moving industry.

At Sunrise, you work in a dynamic and multinational environment where every voice is heard, perspectives are shared and values are lived. Diversity is not just a program for us, but part of our DNA. Because we celebrate our differences, we achieve great things together.

Sunrise is a partner of Advance Gender Equality in Business, the Swiss LGBTI label and certified with Fair-ON-Pay Advanced for equal pay for work of equal value for all.

Are you ready for the challenge? Then become part of the team and actively shape our future!
