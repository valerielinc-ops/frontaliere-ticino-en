Full description

Profile

Have you already spent a few hours by the sea in your life and moved out from your «Mère»? Then perhaps NOW is exactly the right time for «more» in the 3rd/4th grade in Limpach at the Fraubrunnen schools.

Tasks

  • You teach the 3rd/4th grade in Limpach on Tuesdays (total 5 lessons: 3 lessons German, 2 lessons NMG).
  • You like thinking «Out of the Box» and have the ability to act and make decisions independently.

Requirements

  • You have a sense of humor and are a team player.
  • You enjoy working at a school that is evolving and likes to try new things.
  • You are interested in "alternative" forms of teaching.
  • Interdisciplinary competencies are a core value in your work.
  • You have experience teaching children.

We offer

  • A maternity cover at the Limpach school site from January - July 2027.
  • 5 lessons per week, every Tuesday from 9 - 12 o'clock and from 13.30 - 3 p.m.
  • A lively and energetic class.
  • A committed and helpful team. And that really is the case!
  • A school and staff culture based on presence, trust & appreciation.
