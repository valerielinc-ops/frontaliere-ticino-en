Since 1833, drive by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressed its inventiveness through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication.

YOUR MISSION

The Finis Product Quality Department was created in September 2025 with the mission of ensuring the excellence of our products at all stages of their life cycle, relying on three pillars: Manufacturing Quality, Improving the Reliability of Existing Models, and Market Quality. This last pillar is crucial to capturing the voice of our customers internationally.

As an International Market Quality Intern, you will actively support the development of this growing strategic activity. For 6 months, you will have the opportunity to work at the crossroads of project management, data analysis and overall customer experience strategy. You will provide essential support for structuring our returns and will actively contribute to the construction of the department's strategic roadmap.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT? •

Analyze returns markets and customer complaints: Collect, consolidate and analyse qualitative and quantitative data directly from our international stores and subsidiaries countries. Identify quality trends, map recurring problems and prepare regular summary reports for the department.

Coordinate and communicate internationally: Serve as a first-rate information relay to our international subsidiaries to disseminate quality initiatives, training and good practices. Work closely with internal teams (Quality Manufacturing, Reliability, Customer Service) to boost and optimize the processing of customer returns (quality feedbacks).

Contribute to continuous improvement: Formalize quality reporting processes, propose preventive or corrective action plans and monitor their implementation on the ground.

To achieve high-level sectoral benchmarks: To carry out an active strategic watch on best practices quality and customer experience in the field of luxury and comparable industries, in synergy with the other Houses of the Richemont Group.

Power the department's strategic roadmap: Structure the data collected, conduct preliminary studies and prepare key elements of decision support to define the medium- and long-term roadmap of the Quality Products Finis department.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE US?

Training: Student in Master in Quality, Project Management, International Trade, Marketing or Engineering School.

Technical Skills: Excellent mastery of Excel tools (manipulation and consolidation of complex data) and PowerPoint (creation of impacting decision support deliverables). You have strong analytical and synthesis capabilities.

Languages: English is mandatory (oral and written) to interact daily with our global subsidiaries and shops.

Personal qualities: Rigor, sense of organization and great autonomy. With an excellent relationship and a strong team spirit, you are proactive and oriented towards the satisfaction of the end customer. A marked sensitivity to the world of luxury is a real asset.

WHAT WE OFFER

The opportunity to take concrete action on the experience of our customers worldwide.

A training course with various missions, combining important analytical, relational and strategic aspects.

Benevolent support and supervision on a daily basis.
