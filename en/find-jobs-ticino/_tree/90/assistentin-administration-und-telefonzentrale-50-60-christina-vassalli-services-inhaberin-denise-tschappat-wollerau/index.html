Assistant Administration and telephone exchange 50–60 % Description Be one of our valued employees in our team soon. Are you organized, strong in communication and keep an overview even in hectic situations? Do you enjoy customer contact, administration and telephony and enjoy working in a motivated, experienced team? Then we look forward to meeting you. Our telephone order service and secretarial service is aimed at business customers who value serious, personal service, high quality and customer friendliness. In order to strengthen our team in Wollerau, we are looking for a flexible and committed assistant administration and telephone exchange. Their tasks The position combines administration with operations at our telephone exchange. They relieve the burden on the Wollerau site in day-to-day business, support our team and are a reliable contact for our customers, employees and partners.

Their tasks include

  • Support in administrative everyday life as well as general correspondence and organizational tasks
  • Creating offers and maintaining filings and internal processes
  • Receiving and processing calls for our business customers from a wide range of industries
  • Support of the team at the telephone exchange with fixed use as well as takeover in case of holidays, outages or special bottlenecks
  • Collaboration in a varied environment with clients such as lawyers, medical practices, therapists, real estate consultants, IT companies or construction companies

They are often the first point of contact and thus an important business card of our company. With your friendly, reliable and charming manner, you make a significant contribution to making our customers and employees feel in good hands. Your profile You are our perfect match because you...

  • Enjoy communication and customer contact in very different industries
  • are a resilient, flexible and humorous personality
  • Working independently, reliably and in a structured way
  • Stay calm even in hectic times and keep an overview
  • communicate stylishly and bring a quick understanding
  • like to work in a team, but also take responsibility independently
  • You may speak Swiss German. ‘un peu de Français’, ‘and of course some English’
  • have good IT and MS Office skills
  • are ready to take over holiday representatives as well as missions in the event of failures

Ideally, you have a commercial or equivalent basic education. Also lateral entrants with suitable experience, organizational talent and pleasure in administration and telephony are welcome.

We offer

Your advantages with us

  • Fixed working days
  • bright, modern and ergonomic workplaces
  • a long-standing, experienced and warm team
  • More holidays by arrangement
  • varied tasks with a lot of personal responsibility
  • Regular team events
  • Parking facilities
  • only 20-30 minutes drive from Zurich or Rapperswil
  • Good public connections

Take the chance now and become part of our team of office heroines. We look forward to fresh support with wind power from 50-60% immediately or by agreement.
