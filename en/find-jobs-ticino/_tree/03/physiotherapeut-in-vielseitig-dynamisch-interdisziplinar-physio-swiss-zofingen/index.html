With us, you will treat patients inpatiently and on an outpatient basis, from acute surgery to sports physiotherapy, in the strong team of the Zofingen Hospital. Your tasks

  • Outpatient physiotherapy: treatment of a wide range of patients, orthopaedics, neurology, internal medicine, sports physiotherapy, as well as therapeutic group training.
  • Inpatient care in the acute hospital: Independent physiotherapeutic care of patients in the areas of internal medicine, orthopaedics, neurology and geriatrics.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Close cooperation with doctors, nursing and other therapeutic professions in acute and rehabilitation setting.
  • Training and knowledge transfer: Possibility to participate in the supervision of physiotherapy students and to actively participate in internal further education.
  • Team and development: Active participation in team meetings, internal training and quality development.

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Physical therapist (SRK-approved) or Bachelor Physiotherapy.
  • Enjoy working with patients of all ages or clinical pictures.
  • Evidence-based, structured and patient-centred working methods.
  • Flexibility, team spirit and the ability to adapt quickly to new situations.
  • Secure handling of digital documentation and modern communication.

What we offer

  • A versatile location with a broad physiotherapeutic spectrum in a modern acute hospital.
  • A family, supportive team that actively lives cooperation and knowledge exchange.
  • Flexible working hours and relief by a dedicated therapy secretariat.
  • Modern, well-equipped therapy facilities.
  • Attractive internal and external training and development opportunities.
  • A working environment that offers professionalism, humanity and future orientation.

Your employer Spital Zofingen AG In the regional and supra-regional health network, the Zofingen Hospital plays an important role. With more than 750 employees and various partner organizations, we provide medical care for the population and have also specialized in high-quality old-age medicine.
