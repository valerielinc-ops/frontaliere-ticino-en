What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family business with 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare parts and accessories and the provision of financial services. The central administration of the Emil Frey Group, which controls the overall activities, is located at the headquarters in Zurich-Altstetten. The real estate department manages, maintains and develops all of the Emil Frey Group's properties and land in Switzerland and, together with the construction department, forms the competence center for all matters relating to real estate.
