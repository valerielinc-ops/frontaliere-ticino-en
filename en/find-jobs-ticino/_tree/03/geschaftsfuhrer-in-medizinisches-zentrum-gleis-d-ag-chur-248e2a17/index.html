Managing Director

The medical center Gleis d with the pharmacy is the largest outpatient health service provider in the region – with doctors from various disciplines under one roof. For many patients from Chur and the surrounding area, our medical centre, where around 70 specialists work, is an important point of contact in health matters.

Per 1\. 2027 or by agreement we are looking for an entrepreneurial and reliable personality as

Your tasks

  • Operational overall management of the Medical Center and the Pharmacy
  • Co-development and implementation of the strategy in close cooperation with the Management Board
  • Ensuring an economically and organizationally optimal service provision, ongoing optimization of processes and further development of the offer
  • Responsibility for budget, accounting, controlling and tariffs as well as transparent reporting to the Board of Directors
  • Leadership and development of the team, recruitment of doctors and specialists and strengthening of employer attractiveness
  • Further development of digital infrastructure and processes, including practice information system, data protection and information security
  • Responsibility for premises, medical technology, maintenance as well as expansion and renewal projects
  • Coordination of the diverse internal and external stakeholders – medical profession, insurers, authorities and partners in the healthcare sector
  • Your profile *
  • Higher degree in business administration or equivalent education
  • Proven experience in a management function or management
  • Health care and good knowledge of the Swiss health system
  • Sound know-how in finance, human resources, ICT and infrastructure
  • Consistency and willingness to develop: you are looking for a long-term commitment and bring the company forward with foresight
  • Strong communication strength and a benevolent, consistent type of leadership
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, implementation strength and contagious enthusiasm

We offer

  • A multifaceted management task in a successful, growing company of a future industry
  • Design room for manoeuvre and short decision paths to the Administrative Board
  • A dedicated, interdisciplinary team with comprehensive medical competenceA modern workplace in Chur and good employment conditions

Interested? Then we look forward to your complete application documents until on 8\. November 2026

For information, our Chairman of the Board of Directors, lic. iur. Cahannes, are available.
