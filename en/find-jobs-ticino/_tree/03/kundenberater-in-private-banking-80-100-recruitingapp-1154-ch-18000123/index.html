Full description

In our branch in St. Margrethen, you can expect a successful and committed private banking team that accompanies wealthy private customers with passion, competence and personal proximity. Together we are pursuing a clear goal: to be the first bank for our customers. We inspire them day after day with individual advice and a real understanding of their needs. Are you looking for a task with responsibility, development opportunities and a motivated team? Then you are exactly right with us.

This is what you will do.

  • Competently advise and continuously develop existing customer relationships in the region of St. Margrethen / Rheintal.
  • Comprehensive advice in the areas of investing, financing, provision and taxes ensures enthusiasm among customers.
  • Acquisition strength and regional networking contribute to the acquisition of new customer relationships.
  • Close cooperation with front-line and support units promotes success and efficiency within the bank.
  • Through active participation in public events and customer events, the brand is visible and further strengthened.

You bring that with you

  • A basic banking or business education and training.
  • Winning and communicative personality with high social competence for the needs of customers.
  • Proven experience in establishing and maintaining customer relationships in the area of wealthy private customers.
  • Customer-oriented and initiative personality with convincing expertise.
  • Very good knowledge of the regional and local market conditions in the St. Gallen Rhine Valley.
