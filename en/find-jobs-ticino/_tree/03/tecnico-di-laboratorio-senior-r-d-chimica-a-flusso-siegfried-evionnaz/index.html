Full description

"Expect to grow", both personally and professionally: At Siegfried, we offer exciting challenges and numerous opportunities to showcase your talents. As a trusted and respected partner in the pharmaceutical industry, we uphold the highest standards in safety, quality, and sustainability, while fostering a culture that allows you to thrive. Join a global company in expansion, where diverse perspectives and experiences converge in a collaborative environment. Here, you'll find a workplace that values both collective success and your individual contributions—offering long-term opportunities to grow and make an impact. ## Your Role

  • As a technician, you execute the syntheses of chemical intermediates, active pharmaceutical ingredients, or excipients in continuous flow chemistry on scales ranging from grams to kilograms (kilo-lab).
  • You are a point of reference based on your several years of experience with various types of reactors (CSTR, tubular, plate, catalytic bed) and other continuous work-up technologies (mixer-settler, extraction column, thin-layer distillation).
  • You participate in the selection of continuous flow chemistry equipment, their maintenance, and continuous improvement.
  • You protocol the procedures related to syntheses, results, and conclusions in the electronic laboratory notebook rigorously.
  • You work closely with other members of the continuous flow chemistry team and with other departments of the company: analytical, production, etc.
  • You carry out your activity in compliance with HSE and quality (GMP) rules, respecting Siegfried's procedures. ## Your
  • Profile
  • You hold a laboratory technician training in chemistry (CFC).
  • You have 5-10 years of experience in continuous flow chemistry.
  • You have experience with the use of automation systems and knowledge of analytical techniques (HPLC and PAT probes).
  • You master the classic computer tools such as Word, Excel, Labware, and Outlook.
  • Knowledge of active ingredient manufacturing under GMP is a plus.
  • Fluent in French, good knowledge of English is a plus. ## Your Benefits
