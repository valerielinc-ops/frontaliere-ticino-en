Full description

The Spitex of the Bülach Region is the outpatient division of the Old Age Center Region Bülach Foundation. As a public Spitex, we provide services with around 70 employees in the areas of regular care, palliative care, psychiatry, dementia, wound care and housekeeping. Our Spitex serves five communities with about 36'000 inhabitants. Nursing expert (60-80%) In order to secure and further develop our high quality standard, we are looking for a committed personality as soon as possible or by agreement. Nursing expert (60-80%) In the ambulatory setting, demanding care situations await you in which your professional expertise is in demand. You actively shape the care quality of our Spitex, bring scientific findings into practice and develop solutions for complex care situations in the home environment. Your everyday life with us

  • Development, implementation and evaluation of evidence-based guidelines as well as professional support for implementation
  • Initiation and implementation of measures for the further development of care quality, care standards and hygiene guidelines including ensuring traceable electronic documentation
  • Professional advice and support of the care team in demanding and complex care situations
  • Participation in optimization and quality assurance in the care process
  • Planning and implementation of training, training and case meetings
  • Active participation in cross-divisional projects of the Alterszentrum Region Bülach Foundation and the Spitex Region Bülach

Your profile

  • Bachelor / Master's degree in Nursing (MScN) with professional experience as a nursing expert - ideally with Spitex experience
  • Interest in nursing development, quality and evidence-based practice
  • Team-oriented, communicative and solution-oriented personality
  • High level of advisory competence
  • Category B driving licence

This is what we offer you

  • 38-hour week
  • Appreciative culture
  • 5, 6 or 7 weeks holiday (depending on age)
  • Above average insurance benefits
  • Miscellaneous benefits and allowances
  • Generous training and education opportunities

Interested? For further information, Nadine Wartini (Department Manager Care & Care) is at your disposal. Please apply exclusively via our online application portal at www.sarb.ch. Applications from recruitment agencies and applicant dossiers submitted by e-mail will not be considered.
