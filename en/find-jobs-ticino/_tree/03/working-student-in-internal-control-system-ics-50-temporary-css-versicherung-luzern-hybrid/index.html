From you comes a concentrated load of curiosity and desire to learn more about the topics Internal Control System (ICS) and Business Continuity Management (BCM). From us comes the appropriate environment and the necessary flexibility so that you can reconcile your working hours well with your studies. As a working student, you are involved with us like all other employees - that is really important to us. You can fully participate in our tasks, projects and processes. We look forward to your inputs! This is what your everyday life looks like

  • Digitalization is no foreign word for you and you are fit in dealing with modern office tools.

  • A high level of social competence is not only a buzzword for you, but a real strength.
