Full description

Klinik Im Hasel AG is a psychiatric-psychotherapeutic specialist clinic with a cantonal service mandate for the treatment of addiction and trauma disorders. As a health insurance-approved institution and part of the cantonal hospital list, we offer high-quality, evidence-based and human-oriented treatment for our patients. With around 170 dedicated employees at three locations – our inpatient clinic in Gontenschwil, the ambulatorium and the day clinic in Lenzburg and the trauma-specific ambulatorium in Baden – we combine professional competence with an appreciative and interdisciplinary cooperation.

Senior doctor:in Baden (m/f/d), 60 - 100%

At our location in Baden, we are building up a daily clinical, trauma-specific treatment offer. For this we are looking for on 01.01.2027 or by agreement a senior physician.

Your tasks

  • Medical care for patients
  • Further development and implementation of the integrative therapy concept
  • Training of the assistant medical team
  • Supervision and support of psychotherapies
  • Ambulatory activity possible at the Lenzburg location

Your profile

  • Proof of successfully completed medical studies CH/EU/EFTA
  • FÄ/FA Psychiatry and psychotherapy or advanced training
  • Experience in the field of addiction diseases
  • Experience in the treatment of trauma follow-up disorders
  • High social skills, teamwork, sense of responsibility and motivation
  • High identification with the mission statement and the conceptual basis of the therapeutic work of Klinik Im Hasel AG
  • We want a basic attitude that is characterized by appreciation and respect in dealing with our patients: inside.

Our offer
