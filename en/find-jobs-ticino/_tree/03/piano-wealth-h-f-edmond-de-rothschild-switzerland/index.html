Full description

Background: As part of the development of its international private banking activities, our institution wants to strengthen its Wealth Planning team by recruiting an experienced Wealth Planner, specialized in British clients and complex heritage structures, including trusts. The incumbent will join the Wealth Planning team to support private bankers and their clients in the design, structuring and implementation of cross-border heritage strategies. This function is intended for a senior professional with recognised expertise in heritage planning applicable to the UK market, trusts and international tax issues concerning UK customers or with links to the UK. The Wealth Planning function is organized at the Group level, offering an international platform of expertise and a framework for close collaboration between the different entities. As such, the Wealth Planner will join a team of specialists working in coordination with the Group's other Wealth Planners to promote knowledge sharing, the development of innovative heritage solutions and the implementation of a harmonized approach to international clients. The position also offers the opportunity to collaborate regularly with private bankers, product specialists and experts from the various entities of the Group, allowing exposure to a diverse clientele, complex heritage issues and many international development opportunities. Main missions Heritage Council and Structure UK market expertise Support for private banking teams Group Collaboration External Partner Network Governance and compliance Preparation of internal/external publications and thematic training modules. The incumbent supports private customers, entrepreneurs, international families and UHNWI, advises on trusts and heritage structures, participates in customer appointments, is oriented to solutions, leads in-house training and contributes to the Group's Wealth Planning standards.
