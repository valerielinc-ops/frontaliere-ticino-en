Full description

The Operations department is responsible for the national strategic management of the technological and productive orientation of SRG SSR. The People Pool Operations sector guarantees, through clear roles, a skill-based resource planning and regional language support, that employees can develop their own potential in a targeted manner and be committed where their skills generate maximum value. To strengthen the Network Core & Datacenter team, SRG SSR is looking for a Realtime Network & Orchestration Engineer, with a solid experience of zero critical media networks for broadcast production, orchestration through Nevion VideoIPath and network infrastructure based on Arista systems.

Your mission and responsibility

As a Network & Orchestration Engineer, you will join the Network Core & Datacenter team of SRG SSR, contributing to the development and evolution of network infrastructure that support modern radio and television production environments. In this role you will work in IP networking environments, datacenters and realtime media technologies, participating in the design, implementation and management of high-performance networks based on modern datacenter architectures, Arista products and IP media technologies.

Your main responsibilities will be

  • Design, implement and manage high availability IP network infrastructure to support SRG SSR's production and media distribution environments.
  • Administer and develop orchestration platforms and Software-Defined Networking (SDN), with particular attention to the Nevion VideoIPath system, promoting integration between networks, production systems and monitoring tools.
  • Operating on standard-based IP media infrastructure such as SMPTE ST 2110, multicast and Precision Time Protocol (PTP), contributing to their evolution and optimization.
  • Collaborate closely with radio and television production teams, broadcast specialists and colleagues from the Network Core & Datacenter team to ensure reliable, performing and scalable services.
  • Support operational and troubleshooting activities, analyzing and solving complex technical issues in mission-critical environments.
  • Contribute to the evolution of network architecture, standardization of solutions, management of the lifecycle of infrastructure and SRG SSR technological innovation projects.
  • Monitor availability, performance and infrastructure capabilities, developing and improving telemetry, automation and diagnostic tools, and keeping technical documentation up to date according to company standards.
  • After an appropriate training and insertion period, participate in a rotational availability service to ensure the operational continuity of critical infrastructure.

Professional profile and technical skills

  • We are looking for a person with a solid experience in IP networks and modern datacenter infrastructures, able to operate in high availability contexts and to contribute to the evolution of network platforms and orchestration in support of media production.
  • You have university education in computer science, telecommunications or networks, or an equivalent professional path. At least 5 years of experience in designing, managing and troubleshooting complex IP networks.
  • You have a solid knowledge of routing and switching technologies, multicast networking and modern datacenter architectures.
  • You have experience with Arista EOS and CloudVision environments and technologies such as BGP, EVPN, VXLAN, MLAG or equivalents.
  • Know network orchestration platforms and SDN controllers, ideally Nevion VideoIPath.
  • You are familiar with SMPTE ST 2110, AMWA NMOS, REST APIs and automation tools such as Python and Ansible.
  • Know the principles of PTP synchronization (SMPTE ST 2059) and ideally experience with SDI/IP gateways and radio or television broadcast infrastructure.
  • You stand out for the analytical approach, the method in troubleshooting and the ability to understand interactions between networks, datacenters and media production systems.
  • You have a good knowledge of English; knowledge of a second national language is a plus.
