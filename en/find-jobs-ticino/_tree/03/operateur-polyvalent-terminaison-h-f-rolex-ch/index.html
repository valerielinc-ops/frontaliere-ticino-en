Full description

Introduction

For our Production division , we are looking for a versatile termination operator (H/F) whose main mission will be to perform polishing and vibrating operations on watch-dressing components, while respecting strict quality criteria and established production rhythms.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out finishing operations, including polishing and vibrating of prepared surfaces
  • Ensure dimensional and aesthetic control of the parts produced
  • Actively participate in continuous improvement to optimize production processes

Profile

  • Experience in polishing and avivage of watchmaking clothing components, considered a major asset
  • AFP or SWC of Policeman an asset
  • Knowledge of automatic or semi-automatic termination an asset
  • Excellent visual acuity, remarkable dexterity, rigour and sense of detail
  • Easy with the use of production software
  • Autonomous, organized, and teamed up
  • Rigourous and procedurally respectful
  • Availability for shift work (2x8)

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.
