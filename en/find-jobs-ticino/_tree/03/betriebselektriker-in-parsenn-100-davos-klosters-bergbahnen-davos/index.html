Full description

Introduction The Davos Klosters Mountains are five mountain and ski resorts in the Bundner Alpen. In addition to the mountain railways, Davos Klosters Bergbahnen AG operates more than 23 accommodations under the brand Mountain Hotels and Resorts, from the simple group house to the upscale four-star superior hotel. From now on or by agreement we are looking for a person for our company Parsenn as:

Tasks

  • Maintenance and operation of cable cars and snow installations
  • Maintenance and operation of our building infrastructure incl. Gastronomy services
  • Assistance in troubleshooting of railway installation controls and periodic checks

Profile

  • Training: Electrician training completed, fulfilment of Article 13 Approval
  • Working method: You work reliably and independently, are motivated and have a high level of service and security awareness
  • Personality: You are open-minded, friendly, attentive, team capable and enjoy learning new things
  • Language: You have good oral German skills
  • Snow sports: You can ski or snowboard one of the two snow sports equipment to get to the workplace

We offer

  • Established, young team with a positive working atmosphere
  • Seasonal map of Davos Klosters mountain railways
  • Placement of staff apartments or rooms
  • Fitness room for special conditions
  • Attractive benefits for internal offers and services
