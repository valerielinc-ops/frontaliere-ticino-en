Become part of the energy future and expand your skills

Training as a grid electrician:in opens up new perspectives for Stromer:innen in times of energy transition and digitalization. Here you can expand your skills and develop professionally.

Your tasks

  • You build, operate and maintain the distribution network of BKW together with your colleagues
  • You carry out assembly work and are responsible for the maintenance and troubleshooting of our plants in the low and medium voltage distribution network
  • You lay cable lines, maintain overhead lines and are also there when it comes to repairing damage

You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational training as an electrical installer:in EFZ, or assembly electrician:in EFZ
  • You bring experience with electrical installations and are ready to take care of piquet and interference services
  • Team cohesion, mutual trust and secure working is just as important to you as your future work colleagues:
  • We generously support your desire for professional development
  • You have a category B or C driving licence
  • Good knowledge of German, every other language is an additional plus
