Full description

The health department ensures easy access to safe and high-quality health services for the Solothurn population. Our interdisciplinary team plans, analyzes and develops health care in the canton of Solothurn. In doing so, it develops well-founded decision-making bases, designs planning processes, makes collective bargaining decisions and deals with central healthcare and health policy issues. For the health care department, we are looking for a scientific employee at the Solothurn location, 80-100%, immediately or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • They are responsible for the conception and further development of health care in the canton of Solothurn, especially in hospital care and outpatient basic care.
  • They shape cantonal hospital planning for acute somatics, psychiatry and rehabilitation and further develop their processes and criteria. Supply-relevant data and statistics analyze and condense them into meaningful basis for decision-making.
  • Tariff approval and tariff setting procedures are carried out in all outpatient and stationary service areas.
  • They develop and control challenging projects, develop opinions and consultations and participate in legislative and regulatory changes.
  • The head of the department and the administration advise you on health and care issues and represent the Canton of Solothurn in cantonal and national bodies.
  • They communicate appropriately to stakeholders, institutions and the public.

Your profile

  • They have a university degree in economics, law or social sciences or a similar education.
  • Several years of professional experience in the health or hospital sector characterize your professional background. Ideally, you will have knowledge in hospital planning, tariffs and handling service orders.
  • They have proven experience in challenging conceptual work and in managing interdisciplinary projects. You are characterized by an independent, initiative and solution-oriented working method.
  • In the evaluation of large amounts of data and the creation of meaningful statistics, you are well-versed and understand how to prepare complex content succinctly.
  • They convince with analytical and scientific thinking, negotiating skills and assertiveness. Very good IT user knowledge (MS Office) complement your competences.
  • A confident appearance, high social competence and resilience as well as a stylish and addressable communication round off your profile.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity for mobile work
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
