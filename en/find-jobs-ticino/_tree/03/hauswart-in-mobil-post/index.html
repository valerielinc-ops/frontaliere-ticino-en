Full description

Are you competent in the area of home maintenance and maintenance and convince through customer orientation? Do you have an eye for shortcomings and are solution-oriented? Then apply now, because only with your keen eye for details can we ensure the quality of our plants.

You can do that.

  • Together with your team, you ensure that the buildings assigned to you are always in the best condition. This includes the care of the facilities, equipment and the entire appearance.
  • During your regular checks, you recognize defects, initiate appropriate measures and document them carefully.
  • Minor disruptions and repairs are done independently
  • If necessary, bring in specialists.
  • You are the central contact person for users on site and take care of the building access for external specialists.
  • You support object management in administrative work such as planning the building tours or using the meeting rooms.
  • In addition, you carry out inspections and maintenance on technical building facilities according to a defined service level.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed basic craftsmanship or technical training, supplemented with further training in the area of home maintenance / maintenance.
  • Your in-depth knowledge of technical and infrastructural facility management as well as initial home maintenance experience will help you to master this exciting challenge competently.
  • You are a flexible and resilient personality and enjoy daily customer contact.
  • With your category B driving license, you can flexibly reach our various locations.
  • You communicate very well verbally and in writing in German and can skillfully apply your good MS Office skills in your work.
