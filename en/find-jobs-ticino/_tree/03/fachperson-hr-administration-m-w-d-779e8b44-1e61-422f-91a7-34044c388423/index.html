Full description

Your tasks

  • You take responsibility for the administrative HR processes along the entire employee lifecycle: contracts, permits, certificates, confirmations – everything goes over your desk
  • You are actively involved in our HR projects, question existing processes and develop them with a view to digitalization, automation and self-service.
  • You use modern digital tools to make processes more efficient, reduce manual activities and identify potential for improvement
  • You are the contact person for the HR team, employees and managers on general HR questions
  • You take responsibility for the maintenance of personnel and wage mutations in the HR system

Your profile

  • You have a basic commercial education and a further education as an HR specialist
  • You have been working in the HR Service Center environment for several years, especially in HR Administration and have practical experience in project and process management.
  • Passion and high affinity for digitalization distinguish you. IT tools, such as the MS-Office programs, you serve with ease and with SAP and SuccessFactors you have ideally already worked
  • You communicate safely in German and can communicate well in English
