Full description

Tasks

  • Analyze technical customer requirements and translate them into suitable and economically viable solutions together with sales
  • Developing technical concepts and solution designs around data center and IT infrastructures
  • Support Specialized Sales and Key Account Manager in technical issues and customer projects
  • accompany technical customer meetings, workshops, webinars and presentations and communicate complex topics in an understandable way
  • Participate in the preparation and development of technical offers
  • Coordinate technical investigations with manufacturers and technology partners
  • Evaluate new technologies and assess their potential applications for our customers and our portfolio
  • Identify trends and innovations in the data center and infrastructure environment at an early stage and share your knowledge with the relevant teams
  • Continuously expand your technical know-how through further training and certification

Requirements

  • Practical professional experience in ICT, data center or infrastructure environment
  • Technical understanding and pleasure in translating customer requirements into concrete solutions
  • Experience in technical customer service, in presales or in a comparable interface function is an advantage
  • Experience as a system engineer or in a comparable technical function is a plus
  • Knowledge of manufacturers, technologies and solutions in the data center environment as well as experience in the distribution business are advantageous
  • Secure and professional appearance in exchange with customers, manufacturers and internal contacts
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English for exchange in a professional environment
  • basic technical training or equivalent qualification
  • Further education and manufacturer certification are advantageous
  • Enthusiasm for new technologies and the motivation to continuously develop your expertise
  • Readiness for occasional business trips of up to 10%.

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
