Full description

Diagnostic radiology at Muri Hospital is a modernly equipped centre that uses the latest X-ray technology. Every day, the highest quality is provided for the well-being of our patients. You will take over the team leadership in the Secretariat of Radiology, which consists of six employees.

Their tasks

  • Supporting employees in professional and organisational terms
  • Creation of service planning by the Secretariat (PEP)
  • Conduct of annual talks
  • Regular interdisciplinary exchange
  • Optimisation of processes
  • Ensuring patient reception
  • Answering telephone inquiries from patients and referees
  • Appointment coordination, patient admission and handling of examination formalities in the RIS-Medavis system
  • Completing various administrative tasks (post processing, e-mail, patient registrations, processing findings, HNET inquiries / sending pictures, etc.)
  • Training of new employees

Your profile

  • Basic medical education, further training as medical secretary or proven commercial professional experience with knowledge in medical terminology
  • Experienced PC skills
  • Resilient, team-capable, flexible and positioned personality with a high level of social competence
  • Independent and exact working methods as well as strong organisational skills

Your opportunity You can expect a responsible and varied job in a modern and future-oriented hospital with a wide range of services. The cooperation within the different areas, team spirit and a cross-divisional feeling of togetherness are consciously lived in our company.

Their advantages

  • Holidays: You benefit from holidays between 25 and 30 working days (depending on age) and 13 paid holidays. For this you have the possibility to buy 5 holiday days.
  • Working hours: The company’s normal working hours for a full-time workload are 42 hours per week (excluding doctors). The annual working time applies, which ensures the highest possible flexibility in the organization of working time. Service requests are implemented if possible and part-time employment is possible in many functions – also in management positions.
  • Education and further education: Your personal and professional development is important to us. We support participation in internal and external further education and training through generous participation in the form of working hours and/or course costs.
  • Food: In our staff restaurant – nominated with the “Best of Swiss Gastro” award – you can enjoy menus from the market-fresh cuisine at very attractive conditions.
  • Arrival/parking: Employees of Spital Muri benefit from cheap parking. The car park is located on the hospital area. If you come to work by public transport, bike, motorcycle or on foot, we will reward you with a monthly eco-bonus.
  • Social insurance: We attach importance to adequate social benefits. For night, weekend and holiday work, we pay you fair allowances. We insure your salary at the pension fund Profond without coordination deduction (excluding doctors). The wages of doctors are insured by the VSAO preventive foundation.

Pensum: 70-100 %
