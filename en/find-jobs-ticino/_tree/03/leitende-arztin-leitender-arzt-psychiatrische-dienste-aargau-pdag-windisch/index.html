Clinic for Aging and Neuropsychiatry (KAN)

For our center for developmental and neuropsychiatry (treatments for people with intellectual developmental disorder 18+) at the attractive location Windisch, which is unique in Switzerland, we are looking for a suitable reinforcement as of September or as agreed. The Psychiatric Services Aargau AG (PDAG) is responsible for the high-quality medical, therapeutic and nursing treatment and care of mentally ill people in the Canton of Aargau. They employ around 1,600 people in over 50 professions. Clinic for Aging and Neuropsychiatry (KAN) 5210 Windy Workload: 80 - 100 % 80 - 100 %

Over the area

The Center for Developmental and Neuropsychiatry (Stefanie Nil and Tabea Blatter) is part of the Clinic for Consiliary, Aging and Neuropsychiatry (Clinical Director Dr. med. Rafael Meyer). It offers a unique range of information and treatment for people with intellectual developmental disorder (mental disability) and mental illness throughout Switzerland. In addition to a neuropsychiatric outpatient centre, the centre has an interdisciplinary investigation centre, a consultancy and liaison service for facilities for people with intellectual developmental disabilities, and a specialized station that meets the latest scientific knowledge and quality standards in terms of facilities and treatment concept.
