Full description

Digital solutions, technology and people – connect everything in your ICT teaching! Are you interested in computers, networks and digital solutions? You like to help others and not only Google technical problems, but really understand and solve them? Then the apprenticeship as an ICT specialist at Hilcona offers you an exciting introduction to the digital world of work. During your three-year apprenticeship, you will learn to set up ICT workplaces, support users and analyze technical disruptions. You combine your technical interest with communication strength and solution-oriented thinking. Tasks:

  • You install, configure and manage ICT workstations
  • You support employees with questions about hardware and software
  • You analyze and repair technical disturbances
  • You set up user accounts, devices and accesses
  • You help manage and maintain ICT systems
  • Install Applications and Perform Updates
  • You document solutions, workflows and support cases
  • You will learn the basics of networks, operating systems and IT security
  • You work with internal and external ICT professionals Requirements:
  • You are interested in computer science, technology and digital solutions
  • You enjoy helping other people with technical questions
  • You think logically and like to work structured
  • You are patient, communicative and service-oriented
  • You work carefully and responsibly
  • You can work independently into new topics
  • You like to work in a team and stick to the troubleshooting
  • You have a good degree from the FL-Oberschule / CH-Realschule
