fenaco is the largest agricultural cooperative in Switzerland. With more than 80 subsidiaries, we operate in Switzerland and Europe. Together, we ensure that the valuable Swiss food is brought from the earth to the table – de la terre à la table. For the VAT & customs department in our internal customs clearance office in Basel, we are looking for a customs declarant with immediate effect or by appointment.

Stephanie Arnie HR Business Partner +41 58 434 08 01

These tasks inspire you

  • Technical support for our subsidiaries and strategic business and service units in all matters relating to Swiss and EU customs law
  • Independent preparation and declaration of import and export customs clearances
  • Further development and optimization of our AI solution for digitized customs clearance processes
  • Audit of commodity classifications, customs regulations, trade agreements and tariff rate quotas to ensure correct and efficient customs clearance
  • Collaboration in the overarching project "fenaco customs solution"
  • Participation in risk analysis and the further development of customs-relevant business processes

We are looking forward to this profile

  • Completed commercial training or comparable qualification
  • At least 5 years of experience as a customs declarant or similar position
  • Sound knowledge of national and international customs legislation
  • German mother tongue and good knowledge of French and English
  • Open-minded, reliable and conscientious personality with a high level of service and customer orientation
