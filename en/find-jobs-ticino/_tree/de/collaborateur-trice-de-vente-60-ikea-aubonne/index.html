Full description

Who you are

Promote the well-being of people, help them in their choices and of course sell IKEA products. If these activities inspire, we advise you to read the following.

People work better when they feel good. At IKEA, we believe that taking pleasure in his work and achieving common goals makes it possible to address challenges in a positive way. We want you to feel good among us and to be able to flourish personally and professionally.

What do you think? Do you recognize yourself in the following criteria?

  • You are interested in furnishing and interior design
  • You have experience in sales or customer service.
  • You like daily contact with many different people.
  • You speak French fluently, of good knowledge in English are a plus, just like computer knowledge.
  • You work autonomously and control time management.

Your responsibilities

  • Participate actively in the development of the IKEA shopping experience.
  • Advise clients on their purchases and ensure their satisfaction.
  • Know all the services and offer them to visitors.
  • Take responsibility for your purchasing sector.
  • Work with other departments to ensure a positive overall purchase experience.
  • Work with the logistics team to ensure product availability.

You think this could work for you?

As we have already indicated, we value the well-being of our collaborators.

So we want to say 'thank you'!

We offer as such

  • 28 days of vacation (based on 100 % shift)
  • 8 weeks paid parental leave for fathers; 20 weeks paid parental leave for mothers
  • Very attractive social benefits
  • 15% discount on your purchases - in each IKEA store in Switzerland.
  • Would you rather come to work on public transport? We cover between 85% and 100% of the price of an annual subscription.
  • Unlimited access to the LinkedIn learning platform
  • Favourable and balanced meals in our canteen (4.50 CHF for meat dishes and 3.50 CHF for vegetarian dishes).
  • Premiums paid to all employees

There are many possibilities of evolution in IKEA. We encourage and support our employees to try different roles, move from one job to another or even go abroad. Please submit the complete application online via our website. However, we cannot take into account applications and documents sent by e-mail or mail.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
