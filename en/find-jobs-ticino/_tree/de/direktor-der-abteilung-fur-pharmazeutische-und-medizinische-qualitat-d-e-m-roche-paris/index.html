Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you brag. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to three both personally and professionally. This is how we love to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position As Director of the Pharmaceutical and Medical Quality Unit (d/f/m), you will be required to manage the policy and quality management system of the Roche Pharma France subsidiary, ensure regulatory compliance and good practices (GxP) of the pharmaceutical establishment, and ensure the quality and safety of medicines and medical devices marketed in France.

You will represent quality in internal decision-making bodies and health authorities, while exercising strong leadership with your teams and more broadly in the organization. You will also contribute to the operational excellence of your unit, collective efficiency and the promotion of the group's quality culture.

Join us to shape the future together!

Your missions

Incarnate the role of People Leader

  • Creating conditions conducive to the People & Culture culture and the Roche Group: learning and agile organization, responsible development approach, networking, autonomy and accountability, self-organised team, and well-being at work;
  • Inspire, align and mobilize teams around common objectives;
  • Develop a medium- and long-term strategy for skills development;
  • Coaching in an inspiring way by embodying creative leadership to ensure the development of online teams with their potential and organizational needs;
  • Integrate feedback elements to feed the development and evaluation of the contribution of collaborators;
  • Effectively and seamlessly interact with all the leaders of the organisation in order to promote the achievement of the objectives and ambitions of the subsidiary.

Ensuring operational and strategic excellence

  • Develop and manage the quality assurance strategy and quality management system in accordance with applicable regulations and good practices;
  • Ensure the quality, safety and conformity of marketed products;
  • Ensure the efficiency of the subsidiary's quality system and its continuous improvement by hiring all the teams involved;
  • Design and manage the quality risk management strategy;
  • Ensure continuous procurement of the market and coordinate the management of supply tensions;
  • Coordinate with the depositary the quality of storage and distribution operations (BPD, BPD, transport);
  • Pilot the packaging, certification and release of lots for placing on the French market;
  • Ensure the quality and conformity of medical activities (medical affairs, clinical research, pharmacovigilance, medical information, real life studies), in collaboration with the Lead Medical Quality, local teams and the global;
  • Defining and monitoring indicators of piloting and compliance of activities and external partners;
  • Coordinate audits and inspections.

Facilitate cross-cutting collaboration

  • Representing quality in internal decision-making bodies;
  • Ensure the qualification and monitoring of suppliers, suppliers and CROs;
  • Actively contribute to the pharmaceutical responsibility community;
  • To be a strategic partner of other units and care routes for accelerating the availability of Roche products and the development of impacting solutions for patients and professionals
