Full description

Who are we?

The Cantonal Service of Contributions (SCC) is responsible for the taxation and collection of cantonal taxes and direct federal tax.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

The Cantonal Contribution Service (CSC) has created a sector responsible for relations with taxpayers. This sector ensures CSC's image and the cornerstone between taxpayers and the various sectors of CSC.

  • Receive calls from the CCS call centre and provide support to taxpayers
  • Welcoming Taxpayers at Window
  • Sort and respond to CSC e-mails and mail
  • Making various changes in the tax register
  • Perform various administrative tasks

Profile desired

  • SWC business employee or training deemed equivalent
  • Mastery of standard IT tools
  • Ease in human relations
  • Sense of welcome
  • Ability to work independently
  • French and/or German with very good knowledge of the other language

Note: This fixed-term contract is for a period of three years, renewable from year to year.
