Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Obwalden is looking for a person who will approach the interesting activities in customer consulting with enthusiasm and, as part of a team, will recognize and work on the needs of its customers.

What do you expect?

  • Varied activity with short consultations of our current customers and comprehensive discussions on the topics of basic services, provisions and investing
  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through ongoing support
  • Ensuring smooth switch operation incl. Management of our self-service equipment
  • Support for market development measures
  • Potential for further development in consulting

What do you bring?

  • Banking experience or the motivation to familiarize oneself with everyday banking (e.g. lateral entry from the close industry)
  • Joy of contact with customers
  • Open personality with a winning appearance
  • Self-employed and responsible working practices
  • Flexibility, helpfulness and team spirit

What I like about customer service is that you can advise, support and accompany people on exciting topics. Mounia Berqaoui Head of basic client services Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
