Put to the Point

They combine governance, risk and compliance competence with a sound technical understanding and manage to promote cyber and information security together with IT, data protection, specialist areas and management pragmatically and effectively.

  • Date of entry: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20811

You can make this contribution

  • Working in the operation and continuous improvement of the ISMS based on ISO/IEC 27001
  • Advise departments and management bodies on security issues, sensitize and train employees to implement technical and organizational security measures
  • Examine the documents prepared in accordance with the Federal Security Procedure for new projects and ongoing applications of the FOPH, assess security measures for adequacy and ensure the effectiveness of implementation
  • Maintain, improve and implement concepts and instructions for strengthening cyber and information security in the Office. Assess and accompany risk analyses and risk treatment measures
  • Coordinate and monitor audits, penetration tests and assessments and examine the implementation of defined improvement measures

This makes them unique.

  • University degree in Cybersecurity or Computer Science with further education and/or certification in the field of Information Security (CAS/MAS IT-Security, CISM, CISSP, CCSO or similar)
  • At least five years of professional experience in cyber and information security, ideally with experience in the operation of an ISMS
  • A committed, communication- and assertive, integrity-minded personality who likes to take responsibility and as a team player and maker actively contributes to the further development of cyber and information security
  • Good technical understanding of modern IT architectures and current security concepts, especially in the areas of cloud security, IAM, zero trust and network and application security. Familiar with frameworks such as ISO/IEC 2700x, NIST and CIS controls. Ability to assess technical risks and security requirements and communicate appropriately to management and departments
  • Active knowledge of two official languages and if possible passive knowledge of the third official language
