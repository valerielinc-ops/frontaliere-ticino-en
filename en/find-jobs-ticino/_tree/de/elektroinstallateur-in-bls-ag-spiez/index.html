Full description

You give our locomotives the power to roll reliably through Europe – and provide travelers and goods with safety and punctuality. With a feel for technology and your trained eye, you keep our fleet resilient on the rails. This keeps BLS moving, day and night.

This is what awaits you

  • Diagnose and analyze faults in locomotives and components
  • Carry out demanding repair and maintenance work on locomotives and components
  • Record maintenance data

This is what you bring

  • Completed apprenticeship as an Electrical Installer EFZ or Automation Technician EFZ
  • Enjoyment of combining electrical and mechanical work
  • Very good German skills and good IT user skills
  • You work reliably and independently and take responsibility
  • Willingness to work irregular hours, late shifts and weekends

Your benefits – this is what you can look forward to

  • Receive a GA 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a Half-Fare Travelcard.
  • Organize your working hours freely. We promote part-time work, top sharing and job sharing
  • It is possible to purchase an additional five to ten vacation days per year
  • Financial support for external continuing education as well as an exciting range of internal learning and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.
  • 18 days of paternity leave and 18 weeks of maternity leave, with the option to extend this by a further 6 months as unpaid leave.

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second-largest railway company. What's more: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
