Full description

As a family-run, high-tech company with almost 19,000 employees at 71 locations worldwide, we are looking for forward thinkers with unconventional ideas and drive to join our team. Our company culture, which values collaboration and mutual trust, creates the ideal framework for boldly trying new things and questioning the status quo. Our technologies inspire people to develop and produce things that are currently unimaginable. Whether lasers, machine tools, EUV or electronics - TRUMPF is building technological worlds for future generations. Are you ready for new challenges?

Your tasks

  • Planning and implementation of marketing activities and campaigns
  • Further development of our website and our marketing systems
  • Creating and optimizing landing pages
  • Support for lead generation and customer engagement (sales)
  • Establishment and further development of CRM and lead nurturing processes
  • Using automation and AI tools to improve marketing processes
  • Collaboration with social media, paid media, sales and other internal teams
  • Analysis and optimization of marketing measures
  • Support for content projects and marketing materials
  • Participation in the introduction of new tools and systems

What you bring Must-have

  • Completed studies in marketing, communication, business, media or a comparable field
  • At least 3–5 years of professional experience in marketing
  • Good understanding of modern marketing channels and customer journeys
  • Interest in new technologies, AI and automation
  • Experience with websites, CMS, CRM or marketing tools is beneficial
  • Analytical and at the same time creative thinking
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Good communication and team skills

Nice-to-have

Experience in one or more of the following areas is a plus

  • CRM and Marketing Automation
  • Website and CMS management
  • Landing Pages and Funnel Systems
  • E-commerce
  • AI tools
  • Web Analytics
  • Video and content production

What we offer

  • A versatile marketing role with the opportunity to further develop existing activities and contribute new ideas.
  • We are looking for someone who already has experience, likes to take responsibility and is open to trying out new tools, technologies and marketing approaches.
  • A lot of room for manoeuvre and the opportunity to actively shape an international sales network.
  • A dynamic, appreciative team with short decision paths.
  • Attractive employment conditions, flexible working hours
  • An environment that promotes innovation and development.
