Nursing assistant / nursing assistant or assistant health & social care on the emergency - Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

Regula Schlotterbeck, Management Emergency offers you a job with a selectable workload of 40-80% but without night services as

Nursing assistant or assistant health & social care on an emergency

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Support of the emergency team in the care of our emergency patients according to current standards
  • Support for patients in nursing activities as well as support and assistance in the activities of daily life
  • Takeover of general hospitality and housing activities
  • Co-responsibility for materials management and control and provision of needed materials
  • Preparation and cleaning of treatment rooms after patient exits in compliance with hygiene regulations
  • Carrying out patient transports within the hospital

Become part of our hospital emergency department, where we treat and care for more than 15,000 patients every year. The emergency department has 9 treatment stations and a rainy 24-hour operation. It is part of the Clinic for Internal Medicine with continuing education status A, which is a central and important center of service of the hospital.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

To make you feel comfortable with us, many benefits are waiting for you. Just to name a few:

  • Ecobonus of up to CHF 650 per year
  • Own pension fund with super-compulsory participation of the employer
  • Free use of the fitness room on the Zollikerberg site
  • Generous shift allowances

All amenities for employees can be found here . If you also want to be the best version of yourself, you will find the possibilities with us.

This convinces us

  • Completed training as an assistant / health and social assistant (AGS) or as a nursing assistant / nursing assistant SRK
  • Good oral German language skills
  • High resilience and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Careful, reliable and independent work
  • willing to provide services mainly in late service (also on weekends and public holidays)

For more information, please refer to Regula Schlotterbeck, head of emergency, T 044 397 22 26.

We look forward to your online application.

We would like to fill this position with direct applications, without the support of recruitment consultancies. Thank you in advance for your understanding.

About the Zollikerberg Hospital We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. With a public service mandate and 173 beds, the Zollikerberg Hospital contributes to the optimal medical care of the greater Zurich area. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
