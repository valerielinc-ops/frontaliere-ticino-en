SBB | The Central Railway: Automatics: in 80-100% - - - - Your ideas count. Join us – in Meiringen as of March 2027 or by appointment. With around 500 employees, we shape the future of Switzerland’s railway pearl. Together we move forward. You want to contribute your technical know-how, realize your own ideas and build something new together? Then become part of our new Refit team and actively shape the modernization of our rail vehicles from the beginning. Automatics:in 80-100% Meiringen, admission as of March 2027

You can do that with us.

  • Execution of demanding electrical, electronic and mechatronic conversion work on our rail vehicles
  • Modernization and optimization of electrical components, controls and vehicle systems
  • Independent analysis and sustainable resolution of complex technical failures
  • Carrying out electrical measurements, functional tests and commissioning
  • Analysis of vehicle data and preparation of technical documentation
  • Active participation in building the new Refit team and optimizing workflows

You bring this luggage with you

  • Completed apprenticeship as an automatic technician:in, automatic installer:in, electrical installer:in, electronics technician:in, automotive mechatronics technician:in or in a comparable technical profession
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of electrics or electronics, ideally in maintenance or in the railway environment
  • Technical understanding, craftsmanship and pleasure in creative problem solving
  • Independent, reliable and quality-conscious working method
  • Team spirit, initiative and motivation to build something new together
  • Good IT knowledge, ideally experience with industrial controllers (PLCs)
  • Good basic German and English skills and driving licence category B
