Full description

Are your sense of responsibility and self-initiative on your strengths? Would you like to be a member of a leading and successful team? Then fit us perfectly. As a Lindenhof group, we are only so successful because more than 800 nursing professionals, about 100 hospital doctors, 300 staff doctors and 1300 other employees work in hand – and can always rely on each other. The focus of high-quality medicine is always a strong team. And to strengthen this team we are looking for you! Mansioni

  • In your activity as FaGe:
  • You support nursing professionals in the care and care of our patients according to the latest findings.
  • In doing so, you carry out the care process (including performance detection of material and care) according to internal requirements, supported by the Clinic Information System (KISIM).
  • In addition to the personal care and support of other activities of our patients, you take tasks in the logistic, administrative and household sector.
  • In your field of competence, you carry out medical-technical orientations in everyday life.
  • Your job as a student from the 3rd year of study:
  • As a student BSc, you are increasingly carrying out the nursing process according to the latest findings by using qualified care professionals.
  • In order to achieve the area and department goals, you are increasingly taking part in responsibility and, in cooperation with your professional students, you are concentrating on the continuous optimization of care processes.
  • Requisiti
  • You have a completed training as a specialist in health with professional maturity.
  • You are in the admission procedure at the BFH for the part-time Bachelor of Science in Nursing BScN, have passed this already or stand in the middle of your studies.
  • Your training is a matter of course for you.
  • Cosa offriamo
  • Are you planning your future steps in the world of work? With us you will gain first or more experiences in everyday work, and this is only in an interdisciplinary hospital business.
  • At our three locations Engeried, Lindenhof and Sonnenhof we offer attractive internships and training centres for all levels of education.
  • You will be supported both in your professional and personal development.
  • With your own initiative, you can use the access to a wide and qualitatively high-quality training offer, work actively with our experienced employees and at the same time allow your colleagues a fresh outside view. Contatto
