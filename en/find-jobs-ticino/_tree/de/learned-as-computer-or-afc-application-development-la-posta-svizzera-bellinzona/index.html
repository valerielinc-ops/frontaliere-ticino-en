Full description

Do you want to be part of digitization and support users in the use of information and communication technologies? Then training as an ICT specialist is exactly what it does for you! During this three-year basic training, you will gain the skills needed to successfully enter the dynamic computing world.

Your training

  • In the first months of your basic training you will acquire your practical skills in modular learning units and small projects.
  • During training you will provide assistance to our customers independently or in collaboration with other specialists.
  • You will install hardware and software, optimize device configurations, train users and learn ICT support.
  • You will provide valuable help in the maintenance of ICT customer terminals.
  • In order to quickly provide quality assistance during installation and if necessary, you will be aware of the specific settings for the company of hardware used and standard and business applications.

Your skills

We are looking for young people who are enthusiastic, positive and motivated to make a difference with their actions and meet the following requirements:

  • have concluded the average level of compulsory school with good grades
  • you have a propensity for technical nexes
  • you are easy to communicate with people and work in team
  • you are a person with systemic thinking, organizational skills and reliability
  • excites your practice-oriented work
