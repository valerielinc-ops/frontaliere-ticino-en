Full description

The control and organization of CNC wood production is one of the main tasks. The company relies on the latest technologies and promotes the further development of production processes. If you want to contribute your experience and communication strength, there is an exciting position. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Planning, organizing and controlling CNC wood production
  • Create and improve NC hops programs for the machining center
  • Monitor production quality and timely execution
  • Assessing and introducing new machinery and technologies
  • Leading machine team with 3 to 4 employees

Requirements

  • Multi-year practice in manufacturing and CNC machining
  • Very good knowledge of CNC programming
  • In-depth expertise on wood, composites and surfaces
  • Strong communication and solution-oriented cooperation
