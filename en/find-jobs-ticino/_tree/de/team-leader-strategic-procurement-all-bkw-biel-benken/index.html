Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Design the shopping of tomorrow You lead the purchasing department of Hinni AG and develop effective commodity group strategies together with your team. In this way, you will strengthen supply security and economic efficiency and advance the digitalization of our purchasing processes.

Your tasks

  • You will analyze the procurement market and develop product group strategies within BKW’s lead buyer structures.
  • You control supplier management, are responsible for contracts and conduct price and performance negotiations.
  • You optimize material requirements and scheduling processes, accompanying prototypes, samples and zero series, thus ensuring availability and cost-effectiveness.
  • You will continue to develop purchasing processes in terms of quality, cost and time and drive forward digital solutions and KPI-based controlling.
  • You lead and develop your purchasing team with one or two employees in a technical and disciplinary manner.
  • You work closely in the international purchasing community of BKW and report directly to the Head of Procurement BKW Infra Services.

Your competence

  • You have completed a basic commercial or technical training and a further education in purchasing, supply chain management or business administration.
  • You have several years of experience in strategic and operational purchasing in an industrial or project-related environment.
  • You will ideally bring experience in the professional and disciplinary leadership of a small team.
  • You negotiate safely, assert yourself convincingly and act cost-consciously.
  • You think analytically, like to develop processes and systems further and work routinely with ERP systems and digital purchasing solutions.
  • You communicate stylishly in German, well in English and convince with team spirit.
