Our bank is strongly anchored in the region and is on a clear growth path in corporate banking. Our corporate customers appreciate personal advice and quick solutions. For this to succeed, strong support is needed in the background. That's what we're looking for.

What awaits you?

  • Support of corporate client advisors in administrative and organisational tasks

What do you bring?

  • Completed basic commercial training, ideally in banking
