Full description

More than 1000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: The CSD Group is a dynamic, constantly growing engineering company. Inventiveness, competence and multidisciplinarity characterize our teams, optimal solutions for our customers with a plus for the quality of life and the environment are our trademark. Do you enjoy working in a professional and agile environment and attach great importance to quality and efficiency? Become part of our success! We are looking for an environmental scientist, environmental engineer or equivalent as project manager for the environment in St. Gallen or Frauenfeld 80-100% (m/f/d).

Your responsibilities

  • Project management in the following topics:
  • Environmental impact reports with focus on infrastructure projects in the field of rail and road, in cooperation with an interdisciplinary project team
  • Environmental support, in particular for railway and road construction projects
  • Development of specialist amounts with focus on soil protection
  • Planning and implementation of soil, excavation and material sampling incl. Documentation
  • Participation in further multidisciplinary environmental projects (depending on suitability and interest)
  • Advising and supporting customers

Your profile

  • Study FH/ETH/University as an environmental engineer:in, environmental scientist:in or in a related field (geography, etc.)
  • Knowledge of environmental impact reports and environmental monitoring, ideally related to infrastructure projects and/or site developments
  • Appropriate expression in German, oral and written
  • GIS user knowledge is beneficial
  • Soil protection experience is beneficial
  • Connected to Eastern Switzerland
  • Possession of category B driving licence
  • Openness, communication skills, an exact working method and pleasure in dealing with customers

We offer

  • Exciting tasks in projects such as project management of environmental impact reports and environmental construction monitoring in infrastructure projects railway and road, as well as in area developments
  • Participation in further environmental projects (further specialist areas depending on interest and suitability)
  • Targeted and structured training by the team
  • Integration into the network of various departments
  • Professional and personal development
  • A dynamic environment and a respectful approach
  • Flexible conditions for optimal work and leisure

We look forward to your application! Offers from recruitment agencies are not considered in this recruitment. CSD will therefore in no way negotiate the payment of claims.
