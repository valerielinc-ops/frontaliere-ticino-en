Full description

Put to the Point

Become part of the Cyber Command as a Senior Test Manager and shape MSK’s overarching testing and validation strategy and ensure that the individual components, networks and services can be integrated, verified and validated for use as an end-to-end E2E system.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20616
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Comprehensive test strategy, test planning and test control of the Mobile Security Communication System (MSK) across all layers, systems, user organizations and suppliers
  • Design and coordinate integration, verification and end-to-end validation (E2E) including test levels, test environments, test data, test configurations, dependencies and test campaigns
  • Define test and acceptance methodology and ensure traceability between requirements, use cases, test cases, results and findings
  • Technically coordinate tests carried out by internal and external specialist bodies and consolidate results and findings for the assessment of the maturity and operational capability of the MSK overall system
  • Planning and controlling test activities in the proof of concept (PoC) and preparing later system, integration, interoperability and operational validations

This makes you unique

  • Bachelor’s or higher vocational training in computer science, telecommunications, system engineering or comparable discipline as well as several years of experience in the test management of complex ICT systems
  • In-depth experience with test strategy, system and integration tests as well as E2E verification and validation
  • Experience in coordinating tests across multiple systems, organizations, suppliers and test environments as well as structured findings management
  • Good knowledge of test automation, test frameworks, traceability and modern test and application lifecycle management tools
  • Strong leadership, coordination and communication skills, as well as the ability to combine technical and operational test results into a comprehensible overall assessment
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
