Full description

With a top vehicle park, we supply the whole of Switzerland with our products. This is not possible without our chauffeurs and chauffeurs. Experience your first Emmi moment and apply now!

You can do that.

  • You supply our customers throughout Switzerland according to tour planning.
  • You are responsible for ensuring the cold chain and compliance with hygiene guidelines during transport.
  • You reliably execute the orders according to disposition.
  • You guarantee a responsible and safe handling of vehicles and cargo in road traffic.
  • The friendly and professional contact with our customers is a matter of course for you.
  • We require the care and maintenance of the entrusted vehicle.

Why do you succeed?

  • You are in possession of a driving license Kat. C/E, a driver qualification certificate and a driver card (CZV).
  • You have completed the drawbar examination.
  • We benefit from your good/accident-free driving practice and your willingness to replace your vehicle.
  • In terms of afternoon and night driving, you are flexible, but prefer to drive at night.
  • You bring experience on trailers and semitrailers, in Emmen mainly trailer tours.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in words and writing.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You will receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Structured assignment planning for your truck tours: You will receive your assignment plan two weeks in advance with the planned tours, start times and destinations for the next two weeks.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
