Full description

The Schönegg residential community accompanies young people from the age of 16 and supports them on their training path. Further information can be found at: <a href="https://www.schlossmatt-bern.ch" target=" blank">www.schlossmatt-bern.ch</a>

Tasks

  • You accompany young people from the age of 16 through challenging phases of life and shape a reliable, supportive everyday life with them
  • You empower young people and their families to use their own resources, develop new perspectives and achieve their goals
  • Together with the participants, you design individual support processes according to the cooperative process design
  • You work closely with families, schools and professionals, document the socio-educational work and write reports and protocols

Requirements

  • Diploma of a Higher Technical School/Fachhochschule für Sozialpädagogik or Soziale Arbeit
  • Professional experience in the stationary sector with children and adolescents
  • High load capacity and safe handling of demanding situations
  • Flexibility for irregular work assignments (night and weekend assignments)
  • Flair for administrative work, as well as reliable, independent working methods
