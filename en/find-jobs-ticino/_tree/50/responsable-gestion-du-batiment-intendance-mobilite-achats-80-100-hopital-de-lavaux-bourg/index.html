Full description

The Lavaux Hospital is a private foundation recognized in the public interest, member of the Vaudian Hospital Federation. Located in Cully (Bourg-en-Lavaux), in the heart of the vineyards and close to public transport, school and Lavaux daycare. A modern and integrated regional hub, Lavaux Hospital is recognized for its excellence in community care, specialisation in geriatrics, rehabilitation and specialized palliative care, as well as its commitment to quality and innovation. As early as 2027, a project to expand the area was planned. We are looking for a Head of Building Management – Stewardship – Mobility – Procurement (80-100%). Attached to the Director General, you will join the Hospital at a key moment in its evolution and will actively participate in its development, particularly in the strategic projects related to the future establishment. Your main responsibilities

  • Ensure the safety, regulatory compliance, functionality and sustainability of hospital infrastructure.
  • Ensure the technical, operational and logistical management of infrastructure and services essential to the proper functioning of the institution.
  • Piloting the implementation of our new site, also orchestrating the technological transition.
  • Develop and deploy a maintenance strategy to ensure the maintenance and enhancement of existing assets.
  • Take over the technical operation of buildings using state-of-the-art digital tools (BIM, GTB, GMAO) ensuring reliability and data recovery.
  • Professionalize internal purchasing processes to optimize partnership and maximize the efficiency of our agreement with the purchasing centre (CAIB).
  • Conduct a full audit of the existing park and structure our investment plan at 5 years.
  • Optimize the energy performance of buildings and manage overall maintenance to actively reduce operating costs.
  • Ensure continuity of activity by piloting contingency plans and coordinating logistical emergency procedures.
  • Develop and lead quality partnerships with external suppliers and suppliers.
  • Supervise and unite building, stewardship and mobility teams to ensure excellence in services rendered.
  • Create a rejuvenating environment, both inside and outside, to replenish, relieve stress and promote the well-being, health and recovery of all users.

Your profile Technical expertise

  • You have a graduate degree (HES/EPF Engineer, Master or Federal Patent) in Facility Management, Technical Engineering, Property Management or equivalent training.
  • You benefit from a proven experience in managing a technical real estate service, ideally in a medical, institutional or industrial environment.
  • You have experience in managing complex projects and team management.
  • You master GTB and GMAO tools. Knowledge of BIM exploitation is an asset.

Human skills

  • Analytical and pragmatic spirit.
  • Rigor, sense of responsibility and reliability.
  • Strong leadership and ability to create cohesion within the team.
  • Proactivity, orientation solutions.

We offer

  • A fixed position within a human-sized institution, recognised for the quality of its services.
  • A dynamic, collaborative and multidisciplinary working environment within a committed team.
  • Working conditions governed by the collective labour agreement of the public health sector (CCT San Vaud).
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development.
  • A working environment located in the heart of Lavaux, easily accessible by public transport.
  • The opportunity to make a concrete contribution to the development of the Hospital and the projects related to the future establishment planned for 2027.

Effective date: to be agreed.
