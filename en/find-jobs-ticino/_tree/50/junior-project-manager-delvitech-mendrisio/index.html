Junior Project Manager

ABOUT DELVITECH

Delvitech stands at the forefront of technological advancement, having pioneered innovative Automatic Optical Inspection (AOI) technology for assembled printed circuit boards (PCB) utilizing artificial intelligence. Our accomplished team is driven by a commitment to exceed technological boundaries and consistently deliver exceptional results. We are actively seeking individuals who are eager to embrace challenges, contribute proactively to achieving our set objectives, and play a pivotal role in propelling the company’s growth. Presently, an opportunity exists for a proficient and motivated Junior Project Manager

JOB DESCRIPTION

We are looking for a highly motivated Junior Project Manager with at least 1-2 years of experience in project management to support the development team in our office located in Mendrisio. The ideal candidate will be familiar with project management tools such as Jira and Confluence (or equivalents) and will be able to monitor and track the tasks assigned to developers while collaborating with various business functions. This is an excellent opportunity for those looking to advance their career in project management in a dynamic and innovative environment

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Support the planning, monitoring, and execution of projects, ensuring deadlines and objectives are met.
  • Use Jira and Confluence, to manage the backlog, tasks, and project progress.
  • Collaborate with development teams, Head of Program Development, and other stakeholders to ensure proper communication and coordination.
  • Identify and resolve any issues or bottlenecks in the project workflow.
  • Create and update reports related to the status of projects.
  • Facilitate status update meetings, stand-ups, and reviews to monitor team progress.
  • Ensure the quality of project documentation and knowledge management.
  • Help identify risks, issues, and potential bottlenecks, proposing mitigation strategies.
  • Maintain and improve project documentation, reports, and knowledge bases to support project governance and scalability.
  • Contribute to the adoption and improvement of Agile methodologies and project workflows.
  • Support resource allocation, prioritization, and progress reporting to management.

REQUIREMENTS

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Electronics Engineering, Management Engineering, or related scientific fields (a Master’s degree is a plus).
  • At least 1-2 years of experience in project management or team coordination roles, preferably in a tech-related field.
  • Knowledge of Agile frameworks (Scrum, Kanban) and familiarity with software development processes.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills to engage with cross-functional teams and stakeholders.
  • Knowledge of project management tools like Jira, Confluence, or equivalents.
  • Excellent command of the English language (both written and spoken).
  • Ability to work in teams and interact with multiple stakeholders

If this position matches your skills and aspirations, please send your complete application, including CV and references, to career@delvi.tech with your name and “Junior Project Manager” in the subject line. Only short list candidates will be contacted.
