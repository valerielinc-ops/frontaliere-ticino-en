At Equans Switzerland, you shape the future of facility management – innovative, sustainable and customer-oriented. We are looking for you for our mandates in the Aargau region: an independent and service-oriented personality who takes responsibility and grows together with us.

You can move that with us.

In this versatile role, you will take over the management and coordination of facility management services for multiple customer locations. Among other things, the following tasks are part of your area of responsibility:

  • Preparation and monitoring of annual budgets per mandate
  • Cost and budget control and KPI monitoring
  • Preparation of reporting and analysis
  • Ensuring standardised service provision
  • Control and optimization of service level agreements
  • Support for the implementation and de-implementation of mandates
  • Maintaining and expanding customer relationships
  • Support for the acquisition of new mandates
  • Compliance with internal policies and conduct of security tours
  • Leadership and development of your employees
  • Technical cooperation and support in maintenance and repair
