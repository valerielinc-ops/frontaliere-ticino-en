A Sales Assistant

Founded in 1889, MAYE SA is one of the last large independent wine houses in Valais, still in the hands of the founding families.

After a profound transformation in recent years, MAYE is entering a new era: the House will soon move its seat to establish itself on the exceptional site currently occupied by the Regency Balavaud in Vétroz. In the course of 2027, it will be located in the heart of its historic estate, the mythical Clos de Balavaud, where it will welcome its clients in a unique and timeless setting, to experience fun and unforgettable experiences.

Through teams of agile professionals, complementary talents and skills, MAYE continues to innovate by combining tradition and modernity, while focusing on sustainable, responsible and long-term development.

In this attractive setting and challenging and demanding context, we seek to complement our teams:

80 to 100%

Your challenge

Attached to the Head of Finance \& HR and the Head of Marketing, you are the voice and face of MAYE with our clients. You receive and process orders (telephone, e\-shop, e\-mail, mail), advise, inform and sell professionally, and ensure that every customer contact is facilitated and cared for.

You rigorously manage the administration of sales, from order to invoicing: entry of orders in the ERP and coordination with our logistics providers, invoicing, updating of rates and customer data, management of promotions and flash sales, various counts as well as monitoring of debtors in connection with our sellers. You also support our external sales team.

Beyond these main missions, you support the marketing team – updating the catalogue, brochure and order card, listings for our mailings, followed by the shop and the stock of promotional material – and coordinate from A to Z our tastings and entertainment at points of sale. Occasionally, you strengthen the team at the store, during events or during the harvest.

Your profile

Holder of an SWC of a business employee, of professional maturity, of a business school diploma, of a Bachelor of Tourism degree or of an equivalent training, you justify at least 5 years of experience in a similar function, ideally acquired in a training company in the beverage or consumer goods sector (FMCG). A federal sales specialist certificate or similar training is an asset; a training or practical experience related to the world of wine would be a plus.

At ease with the usual IT tools (more Office), you are used to working with an ERP / CRM – the knowledge of Business Central is an asset – and are open to the use of artificial intelligence to increase efficiency. Your French spelling is sure.

Oriented customer, welcoming and smiling – including on the phone – you love contact and always look for the solution. Accurate, fast and effective, you just do it at first, have a sense of coordination and keep your calm when the pace accelerates. Autonomous, proactive and versatile, you appreciate teamwork, challenges and change.

Your mother tongue is French or German and you speak easily in the other language

  • English is a plus. You have a real affinity for the world of wine. The idea of joining a family historic house, currently in full transformation, you enthusiasm.

We offer you

The opportunity to join a well-known, independent company in full renewal and to be able to contribute fully to its impact. Autonomy in your tasks and a unique challenge in an exciting field. A dynamic, agile team and short and fast decision paths. A working environment on an exceptional site, open, close to nature and easy access. Modern and attractive conditions of engagement.

Your next steps

Do you recognize yourself in this announcement and want to share your motivation and talents with us? We look forward to meeting you.

Send us your complete application package (CV, cover letter, work certificates) via the platform only.
