Chef·fe de quartière production Lausanne Sorting 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head of quarter production Lausanne Triage Denges, 01.01.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

The western region, Switzerland's largest unit, offers high quality services to our customers. We manage the operation taking into account safety, savings and productivity aspects. A motivated team, interesting work by all time and responsibility to move Switzerland. At home, night and weekend work entitles us to extra pay. You also benefit from time increases. This means you have more free time than employees who do not work at night or on weekends. To find out what other benefits we offer, such as children's allowances, free G.A. 2nd class or foreign travel at reduced prices (also for the family), visit our website under "Social Benefits". What you can move.

  • As a Chief/Quarter's Officer, you act as operational director during your assigned service and represent your team leader/fe during this time.
  • In the team area, you are responsible for the operational management of personnel, trains and vehicles. You provide functional instructions and assign them to the group within your area of responsibility as required.
  • You ensure that security rules and processes are respected. You ensure punctuality and safety when preparing trains in your area.
  • In case of deviations, you take autonomous measures to minimize the impact on customers and operations.
  • You represent the interests of SBB Cargo in case of an incident. Based on your observations, you provide important guidance to improve processes and resource use.

What you bring.

  • SWC professional learning completed or several years of experience in an operational or disposal management service are required, as well as good knowledge of national languages (German and French). Ideally, you have completed training in the RCP branch and have completed the VOT (Operational Train Visit) training. If so, you are prepared to follow it.
  • You have broad social and professional skills. You enjoy making decisions about unforeseen situations.
  • You like to work with colleagues and respect different personalities. You work independently and quickly find good solutions, even in difficult situations. You are actively committed to the safety of your colleagues and set an example on a daily basis.
  • The use of IT tools is an obvious one for you, as is the application of the working time provisions of the CTC and the LDT.
  • You have strong railway skills and are ready to assume responsibilities throughout Romandie.

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the F level and is eligible for a Level 1 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits .
