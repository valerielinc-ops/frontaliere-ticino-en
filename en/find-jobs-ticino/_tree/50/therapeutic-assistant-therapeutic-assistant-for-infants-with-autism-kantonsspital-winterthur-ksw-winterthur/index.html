Full description

For the Early Autism Intervention Unit of the Social Pediatrics Center (SPZ) for Early Intensive Behavioral Interventions (FIBI), we are looking for a therapeutic staff member (ThMA) starting in January 2027. In an FIBI, each child receives targeted and individualized support for 15 hours per week. This intensive early intervention is based on the principles of ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis). The position is initially limited until the end of 2027. An extension and conversion to a permanent employment relationship will be considered.

Tasks

  • You independently conduct support sessions according to professional guidance at the children’s place of residence, at the SPZ and/or in a playgroup/daycare center.
  • You support parents in putting developmental and everyday goals into practice (parent training)
  • You document the individual support data according to a predefined structure and prepare the support documentation for therapy team meetings.
  • You participate in the biweekly 2.5-hour therapy team sessions per child at SPZ Winterthur
  • You take on vacation cover for the other ThMA’s and additional administrative tasks to keep operations running.

Profile

  • You have a basic education in psychology or education, e.g. a bachelor's degree in psychology, education or related disciplines, FaBe-K
  • You have experience with toddlers
  • You are inspiring, empathetic, resilient, organized and have a very quick grasp
  • You have further training in autism, ABA, ESDM, PECS or are willing to complete them
  • You are willing to travel to the various places of residence and funding (no fixed workplace). Therefore, your place of residence in the Winterthur region would be an advantage
  • Your mother tongue is German. Swiss German is an advantage.
  • You have a secure basic knowledge of Office

Benefits

  • Work and private life should be compatible. That's why the KSW offers you flexible and flexible working hours, home office, generous maternity leave, paternity leave and employment levels that can be adapted to different phases of life.
  • Develop professionally and personally: Every year, around 40 interdisciplinary and 50 professional training courses are open to you at the KSW. We also support external further training – in terms of time and/or financially.;
  • Sometimes support is needed. In the event of personal stress, prolonged illness or difficult experiences in your professional or private life, various confidential consultations are available to you at the KSW.;
  • At KSW, we work with and for each other. Appreciation, open and respectful communication as well as cooperation across professional groups and departments characterize our everyday work. In doing so, you should be able to contribute and develop with your ideas and strengths.;
  • Your future is also taken care of: You benefit from very good social benefits and a pension fund solution with BVK that is well above the legal minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, you are very well protected as a KSW employee.;
  • Your health is important to us. Benefit from discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training and courses as well as discounts in pharmacies and selected health insurance companies.;
  • Family and career can be easily combined with us. The La Luna daycare center with generous opening hours is located right next to the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports you financially - depending on your income - with the crèche costs.;
  • There is also something to discover outside of work: Thanks to our cooperation with cultural partners in Winterthur and the surrounding area, you will regularly benefit from discounts for concerts and theatre performances.
