Klipp & Klar School is looking for curative school teachers for cycle 1 & 2 with experience in the field of autism or readiness for professional training in this field

Tasks

Klipp & Klar Schule GmbH provides individual training services for normal and highly gifted neurodivergent children and adolescents at two locations. At a third location we offer transitional training. As a special elementary school bVSA approved by the Canton of Bern, we offer from 1.-10. Class individualised training in single and/or small group setting (max.5).

The focus of individualised training is on how to combine the existing framework conditions with the individual resources of the affected child or adolescent in order to open up the best possible development opportunities and promote reintegration.

We trust in the development of potential

Requirements

  • Ideally, experience in education with children and adolescents with special educational needs
  • Create experience, individual and holistic educational opportunities for children and young people
  • Openness to work in an innovative team and to contribute (social) pedagogical knowledge
  • Motivation to seek creative solutions
  • Interest in accompanying children and adolescents in challenging and stressful situations and crises
  • Willingness to work in a team and at the same time the ability to act independently

We offer

  • A setting that creates framework conditions for needs-based, individualized learning with a focus on resources and intrinsic motivation
  • Very motivated and competent school team
  • Collaboration with qualified, interdisciplinary network
  • Specialist advice in the field of autism and trauma
  • Regular supervision and case intervision
  • Internal training (trauma, ASS, de-escalation, etc.)
  • Good infrastructure
  • Workplace in a central location in the city of Biel
  • Remuneration by cantonal salary classification
  • Advanced working conditions
  • Regulated working hours
