What you do

  • Integral processing of property damages, in particular professional liability cases of the liberal professions (e.g. lawyers, notaries, trustees) as well as state liability cases (municipalities and cantons), and, depending on the profile, personal damages of all industries
  • Independent negotiation with policyholders, claimants, lawyers, authorities and insurers
  • Cooperation in projects and implementation of internal training
  • Professional support for employees of claims departments, underwriting and other internal departments
  • Area of activity: German-speaking Switzerland (place of work by agreement)

What you bring

  • Completed legal training, preferably with a lawyer's patent, or other qualified training (e.g. legal agent); Advantageous training in civil liability/insurance (e.g. CAS civil liability and insurance law)
  • Experience in civil liability, social security, administrative and/or business law
  • Enjoyment in the processing of damage cases in personal contact as well as in legal contexts
  • Style-safe expression in the German language, knowledge of English desired, further knowledge of foreign languages (especially F, I) advantageous
  • Solution-oriented, responsible and independent working method
  • Good negotiating skills and strong decision-making
  • High service mentality and customer orientation
  • Strong team player mentality
  • Openness to use AI tools (e.g. Microsoft Copilot)
