Full description

Would you like to make an impact on our Group’s sustainability governance and controls? Are you passionate about sustainability and ESG topics?

We’re looking for a Sustainability Controller like you to

  • enhance the governance framework over our Group's sustainability disclosures and reporting. This includes defining the governance process, timelines and documentation and control standards for metric owners and disclosure owners, as well as challenging and approving proposed metrics to ensure disclosures are fit for purpose
  • act as a SME on various sustainability reporting topics. Be our point of contact for multiple stakeholders on matters relating to metrics, disclosures, and reporting. Coordinate with data providers to educate, enhance, and embed the control framework for sustainability reporting
  • use your deep accounting and sustainability reporting knowledge to perform content and analytical reviews of our Group sustainability disclosures. Assess reporting revisions and impacts on reporting.
  • support assurance activities over sustainability reporting. Act as an interface between auditors and internal stakeholders
  • define, conduct and review metric reporting risk assessments
  • integrate new ESG regulatory requirements and/or standards into business as usual.
