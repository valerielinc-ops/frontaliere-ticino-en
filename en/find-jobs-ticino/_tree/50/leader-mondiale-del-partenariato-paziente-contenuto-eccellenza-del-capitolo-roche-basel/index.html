The Position As a member of the Global Patient Network (GPN) team within Roche's Product Development Medical Affairs (PDMA) department, the Content & Chapter Excellence Lead is a strategic role focused on elevating the GPN brand and professionalizing the function’s operational standards. You will serve as the primary architect for how GPN communicates its value and ensures global consistency through standardized training, onboarding, and procedural excellence. By driving the GPN congress strategy, Global Patient Week, and the ongoing maintenance of our functional narrative, you will ensure that the voice of the patient is communicated effectively across the organization. You will also play a critical role in fostering a culture of excellence by equipping patient-facing roles with the tools, training, and governance necessary to succeed. The Opportunity

  • GPN Congress & Global Patient Week Strategy: Design and implement a global congress strategy for Patient Partnership at Roche, including content development and speaker preparation, while leading the execution of Global Patient Week to drive internal and external engagement.

  • Training & Onboarding Excellence: Design and deploy the GPN Training Program and provide oversight for the global onboarding approach to ensure new hires in patient-facing roles are equipped for success.
  • Operational Governance: Oversee the management of the PP Hub and lead the refresh and maintenance of GPN Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), providing critical input into company-wide SOPs related to the function.
