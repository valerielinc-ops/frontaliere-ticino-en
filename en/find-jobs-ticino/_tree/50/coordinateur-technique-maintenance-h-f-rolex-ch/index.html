Full description

Introduction

As part of the development of our activities in the canton of Fribourg, we are a pool of talents to accompany the deployment of our new industrial site in Bulle. To this end, we will soon be looking for (M/F) Technical Maintenance Coordinator profiles to strengthen our teams.

Responsibilities

  • To conduct the discussions within the maintenance team and ensure compliance with the intervention standards
  • Coordinate actions with client operators, internal teams and external partners to ensure smooth delivery of interventions
  • Focus intervention priorities in collaboration with the scheduler and monitor progress in the sector's work orders
  • Identify blocking points, propose technical solutions and seek the necessary expertise
  • Ensure the interface with the Methods, in particular for processing requests for modification and the evolution of technical solutions
  • Support technicians, promote skills development and contribute to the integration of new employees
  • Conduct follow-up sessions, report to the manager and contribute to continuous improvement actions

Profile

  • Technical training in maintenance, mechanics, automation or equivalent
  • Experience in coordinating maintenance activities in the industrial environment
  • Technical versatility and ability to analyse problems related to production equipment
  • Mastery of an integrated management system, analytical tools and common IT tools
  • Knowledge of English and/or intermediate German (B1), an asset
  • Communication skills, sense of service and ability to collaborate with diverse stakeholders
  • Leadership, initiative, autonomy, rigour and strength of proposal
  • Availability for flexible schedules, intersite travel and periodic service as required

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
