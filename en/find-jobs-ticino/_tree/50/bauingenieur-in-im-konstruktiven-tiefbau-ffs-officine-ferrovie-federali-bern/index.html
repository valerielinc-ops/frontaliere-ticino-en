Civil Engineer: in the structural civil engineering 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Civil Engineer: in structural civil engineering Bern / Work Smart, 01.01.2027 or by agreement, 60-100% (job sharing possible) Apply now

You can look forward to that.

The SBB Infrastructure division is responsible for the construction, maintenance and operation of the infrastructure and strategic and technological asset management is ensured in the area of “network design, facilities and technology”. The "Engineering Construction" department is responsible for the SBB wide specialist guidance for tunnels, bridges, support structures, earthworks, noise barriers, drinking water supply systems and sewage disposal systems of artificial buildings. We develop techniques, specifications and methods for new construction, repair and monitoring of engineering structures. Our benefits such as child allowances, free 1. Class GA and discounted travel abroad, also for your family, can be found on our website under Benefits. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • In this exciting function, you will take over the specialist tour of support structures with a focus on structural civil engineering for new construction and maintenance projects of the SBB infrastructure and support the specialist areas of tunnelling, geotechnical engineering and special civil engineering.
  • Under your responsibility, you will review project dossiers from variant studies to preparatory and construction projects for support structures and opencast mining tunnels with regard to the correct implementation of technical regulations and qualitative requirements. You will be in close contact with project managers and monitoring specialists.
  • You will take on expert services within SBB for the assessment of existing support structures and strategic and operational advice for the implementation of construction measures.
  • In your role, you will continue to develop SBB technical specifications and guidelines for new construction and maintenance measures and monitoring for support structures. You conduct regular consultations with the authority and actively participate in working groups for standard development.
  • You initiate and accompany research projects for innovations and further developments in structural civil engineering and for the monitoring of support structures.

You bring that with you.

  • Your own initiative, independence and sense of responsibility characterize your personality.
  • You can draw on professional leadership experience and communicate appropriately. Within the department you have a broad network and are ready to expand it internally and externally.
  • You have several years of practical experience in structural civil engineering.
  • You are familiar with the state of science and technology in structural engineering, and you bring expertise in railway operations and relevant regulations.
  • For this role, we require a completed degree in civil engineering (FH/Uni/ETH).
  • You communicate fluently in German (min. C1) and your Italian or French skills are good (min. B2).
