Full description

We are looking for active and reliable support in logistics, which is available immediately. You like to take part and keep track even if it has to go fast? You want goods receipt, warehouse and shipping to run cleanly, reliably, safely and with a system? Then you fit us perfectly. This is what you do with us

  • You ensure that goods incoming and outgoing work smoothly – from acceptance to inspection and handover to freight forwarders
  • You pick spare parts, label, distribute and provide shipments on time
  • You keep our warehouse under control: proper storage incl. Documentation is your standard
  • You consistently pay attention to quality, safety and clean processes
  • You take over returns and simple administration (e.g. filing / documents) You bring that with you
  • Training as a logistician and experience in logistics (experience in picking is a plus)
  • SUVA recognised forklift and category B driving licence
  • You work independently, accurately and reliably – Order is yours
  • You work with quality and safety awareness
  • You show perseverance and can also cope with hectic days
  • Very good German
  • French is an advantage that we offer you
  • A job in which you have a visible effect: Without you it does not work
  • Varied tasks around spare parts, storage and shipping
  • A well-established team and an environment that lives together
  • A job in an innovative and stable family company Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your online application. For any questions, please contact Susanne Sulzer. One passion. Many opportunities. The company Liebherr-Baumaschinen AG, based in Reiden (Switzerland), is responsible for the sales and service of earthmoving and material handling machines, tower cranes, concrete technology, special underground construction machines and mobile and crawler cranes in Switzerland.
