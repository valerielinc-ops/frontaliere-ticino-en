Railway foreman (m/f/d) Apply » Date: 11 Nov 2026

Place

Allaman, Allaman, CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. Are you passionate about railway infrastructure and want to join a dynamic team at the heart of rail maintenance projects in the region of the German Arc? Would you be interested in the role of conductor, who coordinates the technical needs of railway maintenance sites, safety requirements and the conduct of work? This job is for you! To find out about social benefits, such as free AG and foreign travel at reduced prices (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits. What you can move.

  • You independently carry out all maintenance and construction work in the railway sector, including the exchange or assembly of components on track and track equipment.
  • You manage and supervise the personnel placed under your responsibility on the site.
  • You monitor the performance of all the services provided by the suppliers in the framework of the construction site entrusted to you (framework contracts, construction machinery, etc.).

What you bring.

  • You have great motivation, you are ready to take responsibility and you are passionately involved in your tasks. You focus your work on achieving your goals and setting high quality requirements.
  • You have a solid experience in the construction and maintenance of railway facilities and good knowledge of the terrain.
  • You obtained a CFC as a track manufacturer, or in a craft sector.
  • You have the diploma of foreman in railway construction (POS), obtained from the SSE (Société Suisse des Entrepreneurs).
  • You consider irregular schedules and teamwork as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. At home, working at night and on weekends is entitled to extra pay, e.g., for example: CHF 16.– an hour for Sunday work. Time increases of 10 to 30% are granted for night work, which gives you more free time.
