Full description

You like to tackle and keep an overview even if a lot is going on? At Lüchinger + Schmid AG, a subsidiary of MICARNA SA, you ensure that our eggs and egg products are in the right place at the right time. You expect a dynamic environment in which cohesion counts and you can take responsibility.

Luogo di lavoro: Lüchinger+Schmid, Toggenburgerstrasse 23, 9230 Flawil

Important information

Only complete application dossiers will be considered. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You load and unload trucks and store items from different product categories as well as packaging materials.
  • You pick customer orders and accompany the logistics processes from receipt to exit.
  • You carry out quality controls and operate forklift trucks.
  • You also work in the refrigerator and adhere to our high quality and hygiene standards.
  • You are in exchange with forwarding partners and support with inventories.

Requirements

  • You have ideally already worked in the food industry: You have an SUVA-approved forklift pass and are safe with forklifts and other equipment.
  • Completed basic or further training: You have completed an apprenticeship as a logistics specialist EBA/EFZ or have many years of experience in logistics.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German.
  • You are physically fit, resilient and enjoy working in a team.
  • You take responsibility, work independently and consistently pay attention to quality and hygiene.
  • You are familiar with MS Office and ideally have experience with SAP.
  • You are ready to work in two shifts between 05:00 and around 22:00.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Contatto

Application and contact Sigrid rooster HR recruiting and sourcing specialist
