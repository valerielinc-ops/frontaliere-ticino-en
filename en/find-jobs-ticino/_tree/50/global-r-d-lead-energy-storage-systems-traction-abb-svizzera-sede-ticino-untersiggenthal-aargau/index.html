Business and Division Description

ABB Traction plays a critical role in shaping the future of electrified mobility. Through our traction battery packs and energy storage solutions, we enable customers globally to achieve higher efficiency, reliability, and sustainability in rail and off highway applications. We combine global scale with strong local execution to deliver performance, innovation, and long-term value.

Location

This role is open globally for ABB employees, but location is set in Switzerland. The work model is hybrid #LI-hybrid.

Key • Responsibilities

  • Lead and develop the newly established ESS R&D organization together with the team leaders and around 30 individual contributors – aligned with the Division and Product Line strategy
  • Establish technical thought-leadership and expertise in all relevant disciplines spanning from battery cells to battery systems – nurture an innovation pipeline to stay ahead of the competition
  • Own the ESS R&D budget and manage technology roadmaps, projects, and priorities in close alignment with the ESS product management function.
  • Establish a strong outside-in and customer-oriented culture aligned with the ABB values, courage, care, curiosity and collaboration
  • Build, develop and maintain strong relationships with all relevant internal and external stakeholders – ensure clarity at the interfaces to other functions, such as engineering, production and supply chain management
  • Take full responsibility for a safe working environment and for the correct implementation of guidelines and processes.

• Qualifications

  • Master degree in a STEM discipline
  • Proven direct or indirect leadership skills, with a collaborative, solutions-oriented approach and strong communication skills
  • Experience in leading product or platform development in the field of propulsion or battery energy storage systems
  • Knowledge of industry standards and development processes relevant to railway and e-mobility applications
  • A high level of self-motivation, ownership, curiosity, and strong drive to stay one inch ahead
  • Must be fluent in English, fluency in German a strong plus
  • Candidates from the Traction Division will be given preferential consideration
