Full description

Within our Cybersecurity team, we are looking for an Application Security Expert passionate about application security, DevSecOps practices and securing modern development environments. In this key role, you will be responsible for defining, implementing and evolving our Secure Software Development Lifecycle (S-SDLC) approach. You will work closely with development, architecture and infrastructure teams to integrate security into our applications, APIs and delivery processes.

Your Mission

Ensure the safety of application developments throughout their life cycle by piloting the S-SDLC approach, supporting development teams in adopting best safety practices and contributing to the integration of security into DevSecOps processes.

  • Define, deploy and maintain an effective Secure Software Development Lifecycle (S-SDLC).
  • Integrate security from project and application design (Security by Design).
  • Design and promote secure application architectures.
  • Review source codes, development practices and production processes to identify and reduce risks.
  • Deploy DevSecOps principles by integrating safety controls into CI/CD pipelines.
  • Secure APIs through authentication, authorization and encryption standards.
  • Implement protective measures against DoS/DDoS attacks, WAF rules and detection mechanisms.
  • Support, train and sensitize development teams and Security Champions on good security practices.
  • Lead security governance through committees, action plans, reporting and performance indicators.
  • Ensure continuous improvement, technological monitoring and documentation to enhance the organization's cybersecurity maturity on a sustainable basis.
  • Advanced training in computer science, cybersecurity or equivalent field.
  • Experience confirmed in application security, DevSecOps or Software Security.
  • Excellent understanding of safe development principles and application security standards.
  • Good knowledge of CI/CD environments, modern architectures and APIs.
  • Mastering the concepts of authentication, authorisation, encryption and protection of web applications.
  • Knowledge of the standards and standards such as: OWASP, S-SDLC, DevSecOps and Secure Coding.
  • Excellent analytical, communication and outreach capabilities.
  • Collaborative spirit and ability to work with diverse interlocutors.
