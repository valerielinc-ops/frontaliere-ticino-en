Full description

Freshness in focus, processes under control - At the traditional location Ostermundigen, we passionately produce popular classics such as Emmi Caffè Latte, the finest yogurt and refreshing glace. In a dynamic environment that stands for freshness, speed and the highest quality, we are looking for people who want to think, shape and grow with us.

You can do that.

  • You always keep an eye on the filling plants and the accompanying processes. 🔎

  • You are always happy to support in the event of technical problems. ✔️

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
