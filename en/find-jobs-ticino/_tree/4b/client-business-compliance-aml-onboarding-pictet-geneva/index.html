Full description

Your team

Pictet Wealth Management (PWM) is a company that is based on a Swiss bank legacy of over two hundred years and on international expertise in investment. Pictet is a group owned and run by partners and offers a comprehensive range of financial services for for families and forsaken individuals, including discretionary management and investment consulting solutions as well as Family Office services.

In the PWM Risk & Control Functions department, as Business Compliance Manager, you will analyze all new client account opening files.

Your job

  • To analyse the files relating to the opening of accounts, both in terms of materiality (PEP, Close to Tweet, High Risk, Medium Risk & Low Risk) and on the formal and fiscal aspects (FATCA, CRS, etc.).
  • To draft consolidated documents concerning requests for opening accounts and to present them to the various bodies of the Bank.
  • To advise managers, in particular on the process of opening accounts.
  • To ensure compliance with regulations (LBA, OBA-FINMA, CBD), particularly in the context of the fight against money laundering and the financing of terrorism (AML).

Your profile

  • Experience of at least 5 years in a similar position in a private bank in Switzerland.
  • University or banking training; a certificate in compliance management is an asset.
  • Interest in regulatory aspects, particularly in the field of AML. Very good knowledge of the LBA, the OBA-FINMA and the Banking Due Diligence Convention, as well as its jurisprudence.
  • Mastery of the usual computer tools, including Avaloq and MS Office banking software.
  • Current English and French; mastering another language is an asset.
  • Home in Switzerland required. - Rhythmous, integral and discreet/pet, while having a curious mind and a real sense of customer service, you know how to demonstrate initiative and commitment. - You show a strong interest in all regulatory aspects and are able to enforce and enforce the rules and internal directives strictly, while placing business satisfaction at the heart of your priorities.
  • Organized and methodical, you appreciate teamwork and help maintain an excellent working environment through your attitude.
