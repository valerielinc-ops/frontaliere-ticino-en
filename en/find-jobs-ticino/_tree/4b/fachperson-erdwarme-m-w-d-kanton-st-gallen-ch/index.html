Full description

The Office for Water and Energy is committed to safe and intact waters, protection against floods and natural hazards, protection and sustainable use of rivers, streams, lakes and groundwater, rational and efficient use of energy and protection of the climate. The AWE Groundwater Section is responsible for the protection, monitoring and sustainable use of our groundwater resources. Among other things, we ensure the protection of groundwater in the use of geothermal energy.

What to expect

  • Technical responsibility for the area of deep geothermal use (natural heat probes)
  • Advising customers (municipalities, planners, builders) on applications for geothermal probe plants
  • Examination including water protection assessment of applications for geothermal probe and exploratory drilling
  • Implementation of concession procedures for the thermal use of groundwater

What sets it apart

  • Technical or scientific training with further education in the field of energy or the environment (bachelor or similar training)
  • Expertise in geothermal use and/or hydrogeology
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Teamwork and communication skills
  • Safety in the use of digital work equipment and openness to new technologies
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

What we offer

  • A demanding professional activity with scope for design
  • Diverse and exciting tasks in a dynamic subject area
  • A modernly equipped workplace in the city of St.Gallen
  • Collegial working atmosphere and a supportive team
  • Flexible working time models and individual training opportunities

Contact: Harry Eggenschwiler, Head of Groundwater Section, telephone 058 229 10 29
