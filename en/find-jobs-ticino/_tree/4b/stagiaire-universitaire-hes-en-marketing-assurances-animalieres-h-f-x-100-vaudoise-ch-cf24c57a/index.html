Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in vocational training, it promotes the development of young talent, with every year more than 120 learners trained in our departments. In order to strengthen our P&C Department, we are looking for a current Bachelor or Master intern for a period of 6 months from February 2027 in Lausanne. Within the Animal Insurance Marketing & Communication team (Animalia & Epona), the University Trainee/HES is involved in the development and implementation of marketing actions aimed at increasing brand awareness, supporting business objectives and improving customer experience. Integrated into a dynamic and multidisciplinary team, he/she contributes to a variety of projects related to marketing project management, performance analysis, content creation, partnerships and events. The course offers a comprehensive vision of marketing within a major player in Swiss insurance and enables the development of concrete skills in digital marketing, data analysis, communication and project management The missions that await you:

  • Participate in the management and monitoring of cross-cutting marketing projects
  • Conduct market analyses, competitive benchmarks and sector monitoring
  • Contribute to the creation, adaptation and updating of marketing content for different communication media
  • Participate in the preparation, monitoring and evaluation of marketing and communication campaigns
  • Analyze the performance of marketing actions using scoreboards and key indicators (KPI)
  • Contribute to the reflection and development of new concepts, products, services or customer journeys
  • Collaborate with marketing teams, product, customer service, sales and external partners according to projects Your profile:
  • You are in the course of academic studies (university or college) with an orientation Marketing, Communication, Business Economics or equivalent field
  • You have a strong interest in digital marketing and project management
  • You have good editorial skills in French
  • You have ease with Microsoft 365 office tools (Excel, PowerPoint, Word), and have an interest in analytics, reporting and artificial intelligence tools
  • You are able to analyze data and draw recommendations from it
  • You have knowledge of the basic principles of digital marketing and communication
  • You have a relational ease and a taste for teamwork You are also curious, autonomous, organized and have rigor in the execution and monitoring of tasks. We offer you :
  • A healthy working environment in a cooperative company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • A varied and rewarding work experience
  • A possible opportunity for placement at the end of the internship/training The HR team is looking forward to receiving your complete application package (CV, cover letter and 3 final transcripts).
