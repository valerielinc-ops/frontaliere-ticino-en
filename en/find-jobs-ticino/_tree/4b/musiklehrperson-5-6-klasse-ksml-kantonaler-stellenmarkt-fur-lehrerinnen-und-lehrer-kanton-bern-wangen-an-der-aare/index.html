Full description

Portrait Wangen an der Aare, which has a population of almost 3000 inhabitants, together with the municipalities of Walliswil near Niederbipp and Walliswil near Wangen, runs a well-organised and innovative elementary school. At three locations (Wangen an der Aare, Walliswil bei Wangen and in the district of Wangenried), around 400 pupils are taught from kindergarten to secondary school. Our school is run by a supportive school management team. The cooperation between the teachers is very much appreciated by everyone and the joint planning and teaching development are at the center of our schoolhouse culture at all levels. All classes are supported by IF, DaZ teachers and school social work.

Tasks

As a teacher with a partial course of 2 lessons, you are part of a team of three, which supports the class teachers of the primary level of our school. They teach the subject of music every Friday from 07.30 - 09.05 clock on the 5./6. a.

Continuation of employment and increase of the workload from August 2027 possible.

Requirements

They have solid music skills, have pedagogical skills and enjoy working in a team. They are creative, humorous and enjoy teaching. They accompany young people with enthusiasm and motivation.

We offer

  • Support in the preparation and follow-up of teaching by team at the same level
  • A dedicated, motivated and experienced college
  • Support in daily work (DAZ, IF, speech therapy, psychomotorics, school social work)
  • Accompaniment by school management
  • Contemporary infrastructure
  • Good connections to public transport and motorway
