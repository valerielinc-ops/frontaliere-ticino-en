Put to the Point

As a military licensing authority, the Army Road Traffic and Shipping Office (SVSAA) is organised like a cantonal road traffic and shipping office and therefore performs the same tasks in the area of licensing vehicles, ships and their leaders. Our customers are the Army and the Federal Administration. We are an experienced team of just over 20 people who work hand in hand with military and civilian partners in Switzerland and abroad.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20333
  • Location: Guisanplatz 1, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure the management, monitoring and updating of signalling and traffic management on the infrastructure of the VBS and on the military road network
  • elaborate concepts, signalling plans and road markings that comply with the legal bases and the applicable standards and at the same time accompany their implementation
  • Coordinate projects with authorities, cantons, municipalities and various partners to implement transport measures and military derogations
  • Manage and update the cadastre of signalling, ensuring the reliability, accuracy and tracking of data throughout the lifecycle of the facilities
  • Processing permit applications, developing and issuing transport decisions and accompanying and implementing project implementations of signalling and road markings
  • Leading IT projects in the field of signaling, developing digital tools and contributing to the continuous improvement and digitalization of processes

This makes them unique.

  • Higher vocational training in civil engineering or in a technical field related to road construction, traffic, civil engineering or traffic police with two to three years of functional experience
  • Good knowledge of legal bases, standards and road safety, as well as analytical skills to evaluate traffic data and develop effective road safety measures
  • Experience in project management with authorities, engineers and architects
  • Very good skills in teamwork, communication and coordination with different partners
  • Mastery of graphics software and signal planning
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German and good knowledge of at least one second official language

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
