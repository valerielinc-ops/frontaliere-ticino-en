The actual location of this job is in Basel, Switzerland. Relocation assistance is available for eligible candidates and their families, if needed.

This Administrative Specialist role provides high-level operational support to the Global Quality Leadership Team. You will act as a proactive coordinator, ensuring seamless daily operations and professional execution of activities across a fast-paced, global environment.

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture

What you will do

  • Proactively manage complex, fast-changing schedules for leadership, including meeting logistics and attendee coordination.
  • Arrange comprehensive domestic and international travel itineraries, including flights, visas, and accommodations.
  • Process expense reports and manage Purchase Requisitions via SAP Ariba to ensure budget visibility.
  • Maintain SharePoint sites and Intranet pages, ensuring document integrity and information accessibility.
  • Provide end-to-end support for on-site, off-site, and virtual leadership workshops and team meetings.
  • Lead the integration process for new hires, coordinating system access, equipment, and orientation materials.
  • Build collaborative relationships across functions and act as a key contact within the "One Lonza" Admin Community.

What we are looking for

  • Proven background supporting senior leadership in an administrative or Personal Assistant capacity within an international setting.
  • Strong command of Microsoft Office (Outlook, Excel, PowerPoint), SharePoint, and SAP Ariba.
  • Fluent in English with excellent written and verbal skills to interact with global stakeholders.
  • Ability to prioritize a heavy workload independently while maintaining high accuracy under tight deadlines.
  • A proven track record of handling highly confidential and sensitive information with absolute integrity.
  • Strong collaboration skills with the ability to navigate various organizational levels and functions.
  • A degree or apprenticeship in Business Administration, Project Management, or Event Management.
