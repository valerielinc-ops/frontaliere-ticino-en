Full description

Würth AG supplies craftsmen of all industries with quality products and coordinated system solutions for workshops, warehouses and on the road. The versatile range comprises over 150,000 items: from screws, dowels and fittings to tools, machines, chemical products and occupational safety to vehicle equipment and warehouse management. Proven since 1945 and at home in Switzerland since 1962, Würth AG offers tailor-made solutions with the aim of making everyday life easier for its partners. Würth AG, headquartered in Arlesheim (BL), is a company of the globally active Würth\ Group.

Tasks

  • You lead a team of around five sales representatives with a role model and accompany them through targeted, development- and performance-oriented coaching in the market.
  • You manage your own customer area and advise your customers as a trusted partner with the aim of establishing long-term and sustainable business relationships.
  • You ensure the implementation of the strategic goals and drive growth in your area
  • You promote the team spirit with regular district meetings and motivate/develop your salespeople: inside through individual measures
  • You actively maintain your network and work closely with our various customer contact points (Würth Shop, Telesales, E\-Business and Support)
  • You monitor and analyze the sales data of your salespeople: inside the sales force and derive the necessary development steps from this

Requirements

  • Completed vocational craftsmanship and business administration training (technical merchant, sales manager or comparable training)
  • Experience in sales, coaching, or management with corresponding proof of performance
  • Networked thinking with business understanding
  • Communication is a strength of yours \- Feedback you see as an opportunity to reflect and improve
  • Passion for craftsmanship and sales and experience in employee management and development
  • You are curious and possess the ability to identify problems, analyze them and develop effective solutions
  • Digital understanding and openness to new technologies and systems
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as excellent knowledge of French (at least level B2\).
  • Willingness to take responsibility and make a decision even in uncertain situations
  • Open to new things and see change as an opportunity
  • Category B driving licence

We offer

  • Compatibility of work and family is important to us as a family business
  • Versatile internal further education offers about our Academy Würth
