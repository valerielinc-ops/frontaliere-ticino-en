Full description

EVAM (Etablissement vaudois de l'accueil des migrants) is a major player in the voodoo public service. With more than 1,000 employees and an annual budget of 360 million francs, we welcome, support and integrate migrants in the canton of Vaud.

Every day, our teams work with openness and responsibility to address key social issues. Joining EVAM means putting its skills at the service of a human mission and of public utility, in a stimulating, diversified and constantly evolving professional environment.

What you bring to our mission

The word of your future leader, Ricardo

"I'm looking for an appointed and multi-purpose team with a sense of service and hospitality. I wish to welcome to my team a reliable person with a good relationship and motivated to contribute to the smooth functioning of our daily structure."

Through your commitment and technical skills, you contribute directly to the quality of life and hospitality in the Alagare home. You ensure its proper functioning and maintenance, the organisation of daily activities and contribute directly to the safety and well-being of both residents and teams. Your sense of service, rigor and relationship capacity contribute to creating a respectful, structured and secure living environment.

Your key missions

  • You ensure the technical management of the fireplace, including the maintenance of interior and exterior spaces and the maintenance of technical installations
  • You coordinate and supervise the cleaning activities carried out with the beneficiaries, and ensure the organisation of waste, recycling and stocks.
  • You organize the reception of newcomers, provide general information about the operation of the home and ensure compliance with the regulations in force
  • You ensure administrative follow-up, including the management of orders, mail, occupancy plan and periodic security checks.
  • You work closely with internal and external partners, and ensure the flow of important information about the life of the structure.

Your profile

  • You have an operating agent CFC or in a technical field useful to the function
  • You have at least 2 years of professional experience in building engineering
  • You have excellent organisational, relational and ability to work in a multidisciplinary team
  • You have the driver's license a plus
  • You master French in oral and written language, as well as the usual office tools.
  • Knowledge of English and of one or more other languages
  • An interest in migration and an intercultural environment is an asset.

What EVAM proposes

  • A committed working environment
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 from 50 years)
  • Very good social benefits
  • Meaningful projects in a stimulating intercultural context

The three-step recruitment process

  1. Interview with our recruitment officer, the head of the household and the head of the rotating stewards
  1. A situation with the business manager
  1. Half a day of immersion to discover your future working environment

At the end of the process: request for an extract from the criminal record, (virgin documents required).
