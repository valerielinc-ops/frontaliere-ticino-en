Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Salem-Spital | Bern Occupation by: by appointment

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

The Department of General Internal Medicine (DAIM) is a clinical competence center at three clinic locations in Bern (Klinik Beau-Site, Salem-Spital, Klinik Permanence) in which the entire spectrum of the discipline General Internal Medicine and the interdisciplinary clinical emergency medicine is covered. DAIM is a category A training centre for general internal medicine, category IV for outpatient medicine and SGNOR category 2 for clinical emergency medicine.

Your Advantages in Us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • A collaborative, dynamic team with a real opportunity to participate
  • Part-time work with fixed days possible by arrangement
  • No obligation to attend night services
  • Financial and time support for external training
  • Free time Plus – up to two weeks of additional holidays
  • Attractive perks: discount in the Salem pharmacy, participation in the fitness subscription, entrance to the indoor pool and participation in mobility costs

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • You are responsible for the emergency or station operation and look after patients interdisciplinary – if you are interested, also combined
  • You provide efficient, patient-centred care and close cooperation between emergency, ward and special disciplines
  • You take part in the medical background service – without night attendance
  • You lead, accompany and promote assistant doctors and students in education and training

Your profile

  • Emergency medicine: You are a specialist for general internal medicine or another specialist area relevant for emergency medicine (e.g. surgery), or just before you obtain the title. You bring knowledge of interdisciplinary emergency medicine – ideally supplemented by the additional designation clinical emergency medicine SGNOR – as well as experience in sonography
  • Stationary internal medicine: You have a specialist title General internal medicine if necessary. supplemented by another specialist title (e.g. infectiology, nephrology, cardiology)
  • You are interested in a wide range of internistic and interdisciplinary diseases and bring experience in their diagnosis and therapy
  • You are an organizational talent, capable of teamwork and strong in communication in interdisciplinary exchange
  • You take responsibility towards patients, assistant doctors and the entire organization and maintain an empathetic and sovereign approach
  • We value your conflict resolution skills, your sense of responsibility and your resilience

For more information, Dr. med. Stefan Trummler, Chief Physician ad interim Department of General Internal Medicine, is available under T. We look forward to your contact.
