Full description

Do you want to handle technical and commercial customer complaints professionally and actively shape sustainable solutions? In this versatile function, you work in a complaints team within our customer service hub in Uzwil. They coordinate the entire problem-solving process according to D1 to D8 problem-solving methodology, bring internal and external stakeholders together and ensure transparent and customer-oriented communication. At the same time, with targeted evaluations, quality measures and process optimizations, you make an important contribution to the continuous improvement of our customer service.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the professional and systematic processing of complaints and complaints and coordinate the entire problem-solving process according to D1 to D8 problem-solving methodology
  • Together with internal specialist departments, external partners and Complaint groups, they develop and coordinate suitable technical and commercial solutions and ensure timely, customer-oriented communication.
  • They prioritize complaints, coordinate cooperation with relevant interfaces such as purchasing, shipping, material planning and forwarding and ensure transparent documentation of the measures and results
  • They drive the continuous improvement of quality measures and processes, including through audits, Q-gates, initial sample tests and inventory controls and support EHS (Environment, Health & Safety) projects.
  • They moderate meetings, produce evaluations and statistics on the complaints system, derive improvement measures from them and carry out internal training on the complaint process.

Requirements

  • Technical training with commercial affinity or commercial training with technical affinity
  • Further education in quality management is an advantage
  • At least two years of professional experience in complaints or Complaint management and practical experience with D1 to D8 problem-solving methodology
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method combined with networked thinking, initiative and enjoyment of cross-sectoral cooperation
  • Very good knowledge of MS-Office products, especially Excel
  • Knowledge of SAP S/4HANA is an advantage
  • Excellent German and English skills in words and writing, strong communication and customer orientation, as well as flexibility to work in a 60% workload
