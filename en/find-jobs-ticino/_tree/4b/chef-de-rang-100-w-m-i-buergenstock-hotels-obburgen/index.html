Full description

An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Located on the Bürgenberg, a 500-meter-high rocky outcrop in central Switzerland, the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne stands out for its bold design, groundbreaking sustainability, breathtaking landscapes and state-of-the-art leisure activities. Around 700 employees from 54 nations provide excellent hospitality and provide the perfect setting for moments beyond imagination. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two accommodation options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Tavern 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence; at the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. For the third consecutive year, Bürgenstock Resort has been officially certified as a “Great Place to Work” in Switzerland – an award that fills the entire team with pride. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement:

Chef de rank 100% (f/m/i)

As Chef de Rang, with passion, professionalism and a keen sense of detail, you ensure that every visit becomes an unforgettable experience. This is what awaits you – Your stage for excellence

  • Service with style: You guarantee a smooth service process according to our high internal standards
  • Personal responsibility: You run your service station independently and look after our discerning guests with the utmost care
  • Excellent pleasure: you provide a professional food and drink service and embody the art of hospitality
  • Creating ambience: With charm, warmth and an eye for detail, you create an atmosphere in which our guests feel completely at home

Your profile – how you fit perfectly with us You have completed an apprenticeship in the service sector of the catering or hotel industry and already gained valuable experience in the upscale hotel industry. You master German and English safely and fluently. With your organized and reliable way of working, you bring exactly the right feeling for the wishes of our guests. Even in stressful situations, you maintain calm and sovereignty – you convince with a sympathetic attitude and pleasure in taking on responsibility. Your sales talent, your team spirit and your motivation to continuously develop make you the ideal member of our team.

In addition to a modern workplace, you benefit from numerous attractive employee offers and perks. The staff house has its own gym, a TV lounge, a pool room and a library. In addition, you will enjoy discounts in our restaurants as well as preferential conditions for you, your family and friends for overnight stays in our hotels. Other benefits include perks for tennis, golf, sporting goods, insurance, group courses and REKA checks. We also support your mobility: Public transport is financially supported and parking is available at attractive conditions. We invest in your future: With access to the Beekeeper employee app, a wide range of further education offers, internal development opportunities and first-class career prospects, we create the framework for your personal and professional development. She expects the chance to be part of a world-class resort, to actively contribute to the joint success and to continuously develop in an inspiring environment.
