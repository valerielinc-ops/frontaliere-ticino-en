Full description

Introduction

For our Purchasing Sector, we are looking for a Purchaser Acquisition Equipments (M/F) whose main mission will be to manage the acquisition of efficient means of production and participate in steering the panel suppliers dedicated to industrial equipment.

Responsibilities

  • Pilot projects to acquire industrial equipment, from identification of need to implementation of contracts
  • Ensure management, facilitation and development of the supplier panel
  • Support internal clients with a consulting, quality of service and value creation approach
  • Identify the best supplier solutions consistent with technical, qualitative, economic and calendar requirements
  • Conduct calls for tenders, analyse proposals and conduct trade and contractual negotiations
  • Develop sustainable and demanding relationships with strategic suppliers
  • Ensure panel performance against cost, quality, timeliness, innovation, reliability and sustainability criteria
  • Contribute to your category's procurement strategy in a logic of continuous improvement and operational excellence
  • Work closely with technical, industrial and project teams in a spirit of partnership and service

Profile

  • Advanced technical training, ideally supplemented by a specialisation Purchases
  • Experience in purchasing industrial equipment
  • Mastery of problems related to means of production, ideally in the fields of machining and maintenance
  • Experience in managing a supplier panel and conducting negotiations with a high technical and commercial dimension
  • Knowledge of the watch industry, which is a valued asset
  • Strong sense of relationship with the internal client, true service culture and excellent level of requirement
  • Ability to evolve in environments where precision, responsiveness and performance quality are essential
  • Rigor, autonomy, analytical spirit and ease in transversal relationships
  • Languages: current French, professional English, German an asset

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
