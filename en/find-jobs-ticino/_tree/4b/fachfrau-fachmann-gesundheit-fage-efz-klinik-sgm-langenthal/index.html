Clinic SGM Langenthal is a recognized specialist clinic with a cantonal service mandate for inpatient and outpatient treatments according to the biopsychosocial model. As a holistic institution, our integrative therapy offer includes biological, psychological and social aspects as well as the spiritual dimension. We live Christian values that become visible in daily coexistence, reliability, trust and mutual appreciation. To support our dedicated care team, we are looking for agreement reinforcement with flexible part-time and full-time opportunities to promote work-life balance.

Their tasks

  • Competent care & support: You take over the individual, address-oriented care and the support of our patients in everyday life and in the daily structure offer
  • Medical engineering & organizational tasks: You perform prescribed medical engineering tasks independently and carefully within the framework of the FaGe competence profile
  • Cooperation in the therapeutic environment in which you actively contribute to the design of a safe, healthy and reliable station atmosphere
  • Interprofessional cooperation: You work closely and at eye level with the qualified nurses HF/FH, doctors, psychologists/psychologists and specialist therapies

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as a specialist / health professional EFZ (FaGe)
  • Interest in psychiatric care as well as pleasure in accompanying people in psychological crises and change processes
  • High social and communication skills, empathy, reliability and team spirit
  • Independent, structured and conscientious working method.
  • Identification with the mission statement and Christian values of the clinic SGM

The clinic SGM Langenthal is a recognized specialist clinic for psychiatry, psychotherapy and psychosomatics with inpatient and outpatient treatment offers. The therapy offered is integrative and involves the spiritual dimension. We are there for people who are sick or in a life crisis. For them, we create a protected framework where recovery and change are possible. We think and act holistically and professionally. The view of our patients is shaped by biological, psychological, social and spiritual aspects. We live Christian values that become visible in our dealings with our fellow human beings We are committed and responsible in what we do.
