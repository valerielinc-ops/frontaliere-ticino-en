Full description

In a context of business development, you join a technical team specialized in telecommunications and videoconferencing composed of engineers and engineers as well as technician and technician. Your activity is divided between these two areas. This team plays a central role within the institution and works closely with DTN‐IA network, security, systems and applications teams. It values knowledge sharing, continuous learning and innovation for patients, patients and in-house teams. In this role, you master the architecture of the video conferencing system and participate in the coordination of the team's activities. You conduct end-to-end projects, including on-site studies, advice, orders, facilities, recipe, training and documentation. You also ensure the operational maintenance of hardware and software equipment, ensuring compliance with procedures and performance standards. Your role includes proactive and corrective management of fixed and mobile telephony equipment. You ensure the configuration of equipment, the diagnosis of network problems and the resolution of technical incidents. You are involved in the renewal, updating and evolution of network equipment, telephony and videoconferencing, while guaranteeing the updating of the inventory (CMDB). You handle incidents and service requests in collaboration with engineers. You intervene on all 30 sites of the HUG and collaborate with various interlocutors taking into account their business needs. Thanks to your ability to anticipate and organize your interventions, you minimize the impact on patient care. Your extensive knowledge of ICT allows you to intervene in collaboration with engineers on network incidents related to wifi antennas, switches or routers of our computer network. In parallel, you help to update the knowledge base and technical procedures, and participate in sharing good practices. You work closely with engineers in charge of architecture and solution design, which also offers opportunities for learning and evolution. You perform the necessary administration and setting operations and ensure Level 2 user support in conjunction with other DTN‐IA services. You follow service requests with a strong customer orientation and mobilize your synthesis capabilities and pragmatism to conduct your activities in a timely manner.
