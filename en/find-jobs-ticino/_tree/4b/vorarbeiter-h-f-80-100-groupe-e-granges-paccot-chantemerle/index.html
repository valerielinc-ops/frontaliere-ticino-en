Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2450 employees, including 245 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Thanks to efficient and innovative solutions, the Celsius Directorate specialises in the production, supply and distribution of renewable heat and natural gas, the company now operates some 50 remote heating networks.

Mission

  • Assist in the implementation of projects in the field of remote heating networks by ensuring customer satisfaction and the achievement of the technical objectives of the projects:
  • Establish operational planning and track progress on excavations
  • Participating in the metres
  • Order, receive and control equipment on site, ensure good logistics
  • Ensure safety and quality on remote heating sites.
  • Ensure support for construction drivers by highlighting their expertise in civil engineering and remote heating.

Profile

  • Federal Chief of Construction Team or foreman or deemed equivalent
  • Experience in conducting works or managing civil engineering projects
  • Good knowledge of the various actors in construction;
  • Ability to manage unforeseen events
  • Organizational sense, orientation solutions, proactive and comfortable in contacts.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success."
