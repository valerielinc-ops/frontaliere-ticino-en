Full description

About the role For our production site in Düdingen we are looking for 01. January 2027 or by agreement in the area of adhesive production (PUC LV) a plant operator. The following exciting works await you:

  • They are part of the security culture in the company
  • They are responsible for ensuring trouble-free operation on the allocated facilities in compliance with the production programme
  • They support the minimization of false batches, waste, etc. within the process team.
  • They are jointly responsible for achieving goals and eliminating losses with regard to the overall process
  • They comply with standards as well as work instructions, laws and regulations, in particular occupational safety regulations
  • They actively promote improvement measures within the entire processes

Your skills and experience

  • Completed vocational training and proven experience in industrial production
  • A good technical flair as well as manual skill and an independent, self-responsible, reliable working method
  • Very good knowledge of German (m/s)
  • Willingness to work in a 3-shift operation
  • Good knowledge of common office software programs; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Experience in handling chemical raw materials is an advantage

Why you should come to us The Sika is a kind of "world SMEs": locally anchored in over 101 countries, at home all over the world. This opens up exciting prospects in Switzerland and beyond the borders Uncomplicated leaders who trust, promote and actively support. A family atmosphere makes working at Sika special – many of us call it the Sika spirit If you have any questions, Jacqueline Zbinden, HR Manager, will be happy to provide you with further information (tel. 058 436 6105). Do you feel addressed? Then apply online. We look forward to meeting you!

We offer competitive remuneration based on local market standards and the specific responsibilities of each job. The remuneration shall be based on the skills, responsibility, education and/or training relevant to the position. We are committed to fair and equitable remuneration schemes in accordance with applicable laws, the Chemicals Tariff Agreement and regulations.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemicals company with a leading position in the development and manufacture of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in the construction and automotive industry. Sika has subsidiaries in 103 countries around the world and produces in over 400 factories. The more than 33,700 employees generated an annual turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
