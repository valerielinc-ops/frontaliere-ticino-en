Full description

Tasks

  • Co-responsibility for individual, need- and resource-oriented care and care of our patients based on process planning on delegation of the qualified nurse
  • Execution of nursing tasks as second reference person
  • Ensuring full care/care documentation
  • Assistance and implementation of diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • Compliance with hygiene directives
  • Solution-oriented teamwork in relation to a positive working environment

Requirements

  • SRC qualification certificate/recognition or completed apprenticeship Assistant/Health and Social EBA
  • Several years of professional experience in a similar position beneficial
  • Excellent social skills and high resilience

Klinik Lengg AG in Zurich is the centre of excellence for epileptology and neurological rehabilitation.

It is our aim to provide patients with neurological diseases with the widest possible rehabilitation and to ensure an accurate diagnosis and sustainable treatment of patients with epilepsy. Around 300 employees are committed to humanely, competently, innovatively and reliably ensuring that our patients regain the greatest possible independence - and thus their quality of life. Become part of one of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional teams.
