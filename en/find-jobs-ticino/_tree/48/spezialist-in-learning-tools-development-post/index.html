Full description

Are you enthusiastic about digital learning technologies and would like to actively shape Post’s learning landscape? You take responsibility for innovative learning tools, develop digital learning solutions and shape the future of learning at Post. With you, learning becomes more digital, connected and effective.

You can do that.

  • Your focus is on co-responsibility for Group-wide learning tools such as Viva Learning, SuccessFactors Learning and Author Tools, where you actively shape the post-wide system and tool landscape.
  • You advise different target groups methodically and didactically and develop effective learning formats together with internal and external partners.
  • You are responsible for the Group-wide learning opportunities, promote the learning culture of Post in consultation with the various stakeholders and thus shape the learning of Post of the future.
  • Subprojects around change, upskilling and reskilling you lead independently and moderate workshops and learning communities.
  • With Learning Analytics, you make the success of learning opportunities measurable and create a sound basis for their further development.
  • You develop the impact measurement (learning analytics) further and create transparency with data for further development.

You bring that with you

  • You have a higher degree in business administration, business informatics, business education, work and organizational psychology or comparable and bring experience in corporate learning as well as competence management.
  • Technology inspires you: You are familiar with digital learning platforms and know the potential of generative AI (e.g. Learning Agents) in the form of innovative learning solutions.
  • You know the current learning trends and formats (e.g. Open Badges, Barcamps, Learning Circles, LXP, Learning Analytics etc.) and want to further develop and promote them at Post.
  • Thanks to your winning appearance, you communicate and act in line with target groups and inspire a wide range of stakeholders.
  • Confident knowledge of German, good knowledge of a second national language and sound knowledge of English round off your profile.

You work flexibly in our hubs (Berne, Olten, Zurich) or in the home office. For the cooperation in the team we meet twice a week in Bern Wankdorf.
