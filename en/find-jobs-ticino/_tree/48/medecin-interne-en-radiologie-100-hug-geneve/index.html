Full description

Company Description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals of Geneva are a benchmark establishment at the national and international level. To find out more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here. The position is attached to the Diagnostic Department. The objectives of the department (bringing together 9 medical services) are to provide patients with results to help with diagnosis, prognosis, therapeutic choices, prediction and/or prevention of diseases. These results must be of high medical quality, reliable, delivered as quickly as possible and at an acceptable cost. The main mission of the Radiology Department concerns diagnostic radiology examinations and therapeutic interventions guided by different imaging techniques. This clinical activity includes approximately 220,000 examinations and 5,000 diagnostic and therapeutic procedures per year, a significant part of which is provided 24 hours a day.

Job Description

You carry out, under the supervision of the medical hierarchy, the practical activities, clinical and therapeutic interventions entrusted to you and engage in postgraduate training in radiology according to the ISFM program. You contribute to the training of trainee doctors. You progress in your training course according to the defined plan and/or the FMH requirements for the title of specialization in radiology.

Qualifications

You hold a federal medical diploma or have training deemed equivalent. If your diploma comes from a foreign country, you have obtained recognition by the Medical Professions Commission of the Federal Office of Public Health (MEBEKO) and your diploma is entered in the Register of Medical Professions (MedReg). A full postgraduate year in internal medicine and/or surgery is a prerequisite. With excellent communication skills in a multidisciplinary team and with patients, you are a dynamic, flexible and committed person. In addition, you have demonstrated strong adaptation skills. For non-French-speaking applications, French level C1 in the spoken and written test is required.

Additional information

As part of the training program for internal doctors, the service recruits around 15 new internal doctors during the year.

  • Entry into position: to be agreed, depending on the course of the selected person
  • Number of positions: 15
  • Activity rate: 100%
  • Function class: 20
  • Contract: 12-month renewable fixed-term contract
  • Request for information: Prof. P.-A. Poletti, head physician, tel. 022 372 70 01

Your application file must include a letter demonstrating your motivation, your curriculum vitae, copies of the diplomas and certificates required for the position and the last 2 work certificates.
