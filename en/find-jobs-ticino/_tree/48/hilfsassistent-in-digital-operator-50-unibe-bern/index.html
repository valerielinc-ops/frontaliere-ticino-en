Full description

The UniBE is in the middle of it. With us you benefit from the central location and conveniently located locations.

Requirements

  • Careful, structured and independent working method
  • Quality awareness
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Willingness to learn and high IT affinity
  • CRMS: User knowledge of Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Customer Insights desirable
  • CMS: User knowledge of content management systems desirable
  • Matriculation at a university

Tasks and responsibilities

  • Support for the operational implementation of communication and marketing measures (e.g. mailings)
  • Cooperation in the preparation, filling and triggering of media and information mails (e.g. newsletters, invitations, serial mails) via the CRMS
  • Cooperation in data maintenance
  • Implementation and filling of content and objects in the content management system according to content and technical specifications
  • Operational support in the cross-channel implementation of communication measures of the entire university

Meaningful work and fair remuneration Application and contact If you are a passionate digital implementer and would like to help shape the digital presence of the University of Bern, we look forward to receiving your application dossier (including letters of motivation, CV, certificates) at bewerbungen.komm@unibe.ch. Application deadline until 15.11.2026 With us, you create value. For themselves and society.

About us

With over 19,000 students and around 8,000 employees, the University of Bern is one of the largest universities in Switzerland. It is excellently networked nationally and internationally and at the same time anchored in the region. With her research she sets a worldwide accents – especially in medicine, space and climate research she is at the forefront. The Department of Communication & Marketing (AKM) is part of the Rectorate and is responsible for internal and external communication at the University of Bern. For online marketing, we are looking for a dedicated assistant for operational implementation in CMS and CRM systems. In this position, you are responsible for ensuring the careful and timely content filling and implementation of elements in the digital systems (especially CMS, CRMS). The term of office shall be two years.
