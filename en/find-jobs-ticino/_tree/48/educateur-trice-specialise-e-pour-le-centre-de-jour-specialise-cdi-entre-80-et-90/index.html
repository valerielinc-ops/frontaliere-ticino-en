Full description

Specialised teacher for the Specialised Day Centre - CDI between 80% and 90%, 80% to 90%

Deployed at several sites, the Lavigny Institution consists of a neurological hospital with outpatient consultations at two sites, two departments for adults with disabilities, with an intellectual disability with a physical problem, a specialized school and a workshop and vocational training sector respectively.

The socio-educational housing department is recruiting. If you want to work in interdisciplinarity around strong values that are:

Benevolence, fairness, respect and responsibility in a collaborative managerial approach.

You can join our educational teams.

We are looking for one

Specialised educator for the Specialised Day Centre - CDI between 80% and 90%.

At Lavigny's institution, we accompany adults with autism spectrum disorders (ASD) and/or challenging behaviours, including so-called complex situations for which auto- and hetero-aggressive behaviour disorders are associated. Our Specialised Day Centre is open Monday to Friday (and a few Saturdays per year). Their reality is to accompany men and women aged 18 to 80 by offering them access to activity.

Your Mission

As part of the development of our services, we are looking for a specialist educator. The aim is to accompany people, through individualised care, out of their home in a reassuring and secure environment.

Your main responsibilities

  • Provide individualised educational support tailored to the specific needs of each individual.
  • Design, implement and evaluate personalized educational projects.
  • Conduct structured activities, developing skills, providing physical and psychological well-being.
  • Preventing, understanding and managing crisis situations or challenging behaviours through appropriate educational approaches.
  • Work closely with the housing education teams and the various partners of the network.
  • Participate in team meetings, institutional reflections and the writing of observations and reviews.
  • Actively contribute to a secure, structuring and caring environment.

Your profile

  • Diploma ES/HES or equivalent.
  • Experience with people with intellectual disabilities, behavioural disorders and/or ASD.
  • Trained or sensitized to approaches: Functional, ABA, TEACCH, PECS, CAA (an asset).
  • Ability to analyse, manage stress and take back complex situations.
  • Excellent relational skills and ability to work in a multidisciplinary team.
  • A sense of responsibility, creativity and professional commitment.
  • Mastery of the usual computer tools and ease of writing.

We offer

  • Working conditions agreed by the Commission for the para-public social sector
  • A salary in line with the function and in line with the rates of the Vaudian parapublic sector's CLCs, with mobility-related benefits (parking, participation in public transport costs)
  • In-service training and specialized supervision (including TSA and challenge behaviour), internal mobility, possible funding of diplomas and certifications
  • A business restaurant offering varied meals at preferential rates, in a pleasant business environment encouraging exchanges
  • A wide choice of sports and relaxation activities (yoga, fitness, aquagym, pilates etc.) as well as massages, while enjoying regular business life events
  • The possibility of part-time work for optimal work-life balance
