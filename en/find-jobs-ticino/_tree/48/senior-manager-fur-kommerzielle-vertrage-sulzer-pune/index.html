Basically, answering the question: "Why does this position exist?” The Global Head of Commercial Operations is responsible for leading the commercial management of Sulzer’s global claims portfolio, driving strong commercial and customer outcomes across complex, high-value projects within the oil & gas, industrial, and energy sectors. This is a senior, strategic and operational role leading a team of commercial contract managers, ensuring robust contract interpretation, commercial positioning, and delivery of business objectives, whilst operating at the interface between customers, EPC partners, and internal stakeholders. Working closely with the Global Head of Legal Excellence, the role combines strategic customer relationship management with hands-on engagement in complex commercial matters, requiring strong international experience, sound judgement, and a collaborative leadership approach within dynamic, fast-paced environments.
