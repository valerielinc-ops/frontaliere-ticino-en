Full description

The University of Italian Switzerland (USI) is a young and agile reality in continuous development, launched to new challenges in the sign of its three guiding values: quality, openness and responsibility. Active in different areas of study and research, USI offers its students a training as protagonists, engaging and interdisciplinary, and its researchers and collaborators a privileged area of autonomy and freedom of initiative. The Faculty of Economics of the USI has an international and interdisciplinary approach that aims to form economists able to enter and operate competently in a global market. Considering that Lugano is Switzerland’s third financial centre, the Faculty is committed to training and research in finance, management and economy in general. One of the most important advantages for students of the Faculty of Economics is the easy relationship between student and teacher: the teaching system is designed to allow teachers to devote themselves to students individually. The areas of specialization for the Bachelor program include finance, quantitative methods, management and political economy. The Università della Svizzera italiana, Faculty of Economics, Lugano, for the spring semester 2027, presents the teaching of “Public administration, non-profit and sustainable development” for its Bachelor in Economics / Bachelor in Economics. Course profile The course “Public administration, non-profit and sustainable development” counts 6 ECTS and aims to provide the theoretical and practical principles of public administration management (PA) and the non-profit sector. At the end of the course, students will be able to:

  • Understand the main dimensions of public management and non profit
  • Analyze the structure of a public organization and performance management
  • Mastering concepts of strategy and reorganization of the PA
  • Applying theoretical concepts and tools to analysis of real case studies. Shipments
