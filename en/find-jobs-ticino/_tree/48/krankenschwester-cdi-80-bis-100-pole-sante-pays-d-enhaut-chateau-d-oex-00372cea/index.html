Full description

Located in Château-d'Œx, at an altitude of 1000 metres, the Pays-d'Enhaut Health Centre offers a wide range of hospital services, accommodation, support for the elderly, as well as home care. According to the so-called integrated care approach, this innovative organisation represents an opportunity for health professionals to work in small multidisciplinary teams, for better continuity of care and in response to the health needs of the population of the Pays-d'Enhaut. In the heart of a privileged natural environment, less than an hour from Lausanne or Bern, the Pays-d'Enhaut is renowned for the quality of its living environment and offers a very wide range of sports, leisure and cultural activities, to be experienced in summer and winter! To complete our team in the accommodation sector, which has just moved into a new residence for 50 residents, for short or long stays, we are looking for a suitable fit:

Nurse (permanent contract - 80 to 100%)

As a nurse attached to our Les Gentianes nursing home, you are committed to providing quality care, reception and support to guarantee the maintenance of health and well-being of residents. You will also contribute to the optimization of the quality/safety approach to care and its adaptation to the needs of residents and their families. On a daily basis, you will supervise the care teams and promote a culture of participative management, empowering and encouraging interdisciplinarity.

Your responsibilities

  • Organizing basic care, providing nursing care and delegated medical acts;
  • Actively contribute to team dynamics by organizing and supervising the care team in their day-to-day tasks
  • Work closely with the various neighbouring sectors (Cooking, Socio-cultural Activities, etc.)
  • Ensure the comfort and respect of residents, while respecting the instructions related to the constraint measures and hygiene rules in force

Your profile

  • ES or HES diploma in nursing, or qualification deemed equivalent by the Swiss Red Cross;
  • Ideally, a first convincing experience in a similar position in a nursing home;
  • Ability to listen, autonomy and ability to manage complex situations
  • Proactivity and ease in networking
  • Proficiency in common computer tools
  • Serious assets: you have a CAS as a Practitioner Trainer (FP), have experience on CareFolio and/or are a "Pleasure" evaluator

We offer

  • Participation in the development of an integrated care model, involving the coordination and collaboration of different health professionals
  • A job in a pleasant environment, within a human-sized structure
  • The advantages of living in the mountains while remaining close to urban centres (to be discovered under www.alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/stories/paysdenhaut)
  • Working conditions in accordance with the San-Vaud CLA (13th salary, training, 5-6 weeks of vacation per year, etc.)
