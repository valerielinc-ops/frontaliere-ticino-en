Full description

At the Bank Raiffeisen Vedeggio Cassarate we believe that the success of an organization is born from the commitment, cooperation and contribution of those involved. We offer a solid, dynamic and future-oriented working environment where trust, quality and team spirit guide our daily work. We value skills, support professional development and promote a corporate culture that encourages initiative, responsibility and collaboration. To strengthen our HR team we are looking for a competent, proactive and motivated person, eager to contribute with his experience to the development of our Institute.

What are you waiting for?

  • Management of recruitment and onboarding processes.
  • Management of the entire administrative cycle of staff.
  • Management of processes related to wages, social insurance and related administrative practices.
  • Organization and monitoring of internal training and assistance and management of young levers.
  • Collaboration in the implementation and optimization of HR processes.
  • Support for function managers in staff management issues.

What are you offering?

  • Business training with specialization in Human Resources, is a preferential qualification as a HR Specialist and/or a training diploma in the company (CYP or cantonal professional trainer course).
  • Multiannual experience in a HR function
  • Excellent organizational and planning skills.
  • High reliability, confidentiality and sense of responsibility.
  • Proactive attitude, spirit of initiative and ability to work both independently and in team
  • Explain relational skills and collaboration with interlocutors at all levels of the organization.
