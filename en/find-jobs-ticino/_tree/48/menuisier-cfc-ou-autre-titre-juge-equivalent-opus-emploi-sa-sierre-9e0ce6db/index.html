Full description

We are looking for a qualified carpenter with CFC or other title deemed equivalent, to the benefit of at least 5 years of experience, in order to strengthen our teams on various sites in Western Switzerland.

Your Mission

As an experienced carpenter, you will have to carry out various interior and exterior carpentry work, both in the workshop and on site. You will work independently on various projects, respecting plans, deadlines and quality requirements.

Your main tasks

In particular, you will be responsible for

  • Reading plans and taking action
  • Manufacture, assembly and installation of wood elements
  • Installation of doors, windows, cabinets, kitchens, fixtures and parquet floors
  • Renovation and interior development
  • Adjustments, finishes and retouchings on site
  • Use of carpentry machines and tools
  • Compliance with safety standards and site instructions
  • Cared, accurate and autonomous work

Your profile

  • Carpentry CFC or other equivalent
  • minimum 5 years experience in the field
  • Good control of laying and/or workshop work
  • Ability to read and interpret plans
  • Self-employed, accurate, reliable and organized
  • Good team spirit
  • Driver's license, an asset

We offer

  • An interesting mission within a serious company
  • Various construction sites in French-speaking Switzerland
  • Attractive working conditions according to existing conventions
  • Collaboration with a professional and dynamic team
