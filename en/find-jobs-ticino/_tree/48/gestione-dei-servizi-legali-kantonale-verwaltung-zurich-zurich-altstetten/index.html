Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Prison Implementation and Reintegration Probation and Implementation Services Head of Legal Service 60 - 80% Prison Implementation and Reintegration (JuWe) is responsible as part of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the execution of the various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have committed crimes, the prevention of recidivism and the detention and preparation of the departure of persons without a valid residence permit are of particular importance. The Probation and Enforcement Services (BVD) are one of seven main departments of JuWe and are responsible for the execution of prison sentences and court-ordered measures as well as for probation assistance. They make an important contribution to the prevention of crime and the social integration of criminals. The legal service in the area of enforcement 2 (V2) provides advice and support services on legal issues related to the core business of the V2 and thus supports the legally compliant implementation of criminal sanctions, in particular in the area of ambulatory and inpatient enforcement of measures. The team consists of three people, including the management and the auditorate. For the management of the legal service, we are looking for a socially competent, assertive and resilient personality.
