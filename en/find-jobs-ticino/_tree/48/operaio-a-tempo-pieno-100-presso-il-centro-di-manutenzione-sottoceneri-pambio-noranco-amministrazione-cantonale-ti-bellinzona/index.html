Full description

Tasks

  • performs the ordinary maintenance work of the road network, applying the directives and service procedures, in compliance with the instructions received and the established timing
  • contributes to guarantee the winter roadworthiness, using the means, equipment and material available in order to ensure the safety of road traffic
  • uses mechanical equipment along the highway and inside the tunnels, following the instructions received and operating according to the modalities and times established
  • laying the site signs and the relative elements in the presence of traffic, according to the provisions received and in accordance with the established laying plan
  • performs interventions also outside the ordinary working time, including public holidays and night hours, in case of need
  • carries out the service of shift picket during the year

Requirements

  • Federal certificate of capacity (AFC) in a profession of the technical or craft sector (building, civil engineering, forestry, metal construction, gardener, etc.) with previous practical activity in these same professional branches, or similar occupations according to orientation sheet. ch (detail/or road maintenance)
  • proven work experience in similar position and in the presence of road traffic
  • excellent knowledge of the territory where the main activity will be carried out (Sottoceneri)
  • possession of licences for light and heavy vehicles with trailer categories BE and CE with CQC certificate
  • spirit of initiative and good availability to teamwork

Special remarks

  • availability to intervene outside working hours in case of need and service of day, night and festive picket throughout the year
  • service office: Noranco
  • entry into service: Immediately

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), further documentation may be requested at a later stage (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements
