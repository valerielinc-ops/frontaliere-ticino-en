Full description

Become part of something bigger. Together with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions, you support people in highly specialized care areas on their way to health, independence and quality of life. The Swiss Paraplegic Centre offers a unique environment in which acute care and rehabilitation work seamlessly together.

As a nursing expert APN Clinical Nurse Specialist, you accompany complex patient situations in the areas of breathing and ventilation, back medicine and cross-sectional medicine. You support teams in everyday clinical life, bring scientific findings into practice and develop the care together with the specialist areas. Tasks: • You advise nurses and interprofessional teams in complex patient situations and work closely with APN’s, care specialists and other experts • You conduct in-depth nursing assessments and support clinical decisions • You strengthen clinical reasoning and nursing competence through coaching and specialist inputs • You accompany patients along the treatment path from acute care to rehabilitation • You bring research results into practice and contribute to standards and concepts • You recognize development potential and initiate improvements in the quality of care Requirements • Master of Science in Nursing (MScN) or equivalent qualification with emphasis on Advanced Nursing Practice • Sound clinical experience in a complex acute, rehabilitation or special care area • Broad nursing interest as well as the willingness to further develop expertise in various clinical focus areas • Strong clinical judgment and enjoyment of clinical reasoning • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation as well as advice, coaching and knowledge transfer We offer: You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/bonus. The Swiss Paraplegic Centre in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for cross-sectional, back and respiratory medicine. As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the centre offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives. The clinic is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a worldwide unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with paraplegia.
