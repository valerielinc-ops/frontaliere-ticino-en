Full description

CERCASI GOVERNMENT Addition to breakfast and rooms – 80–100% B&B “La Casa dei Gelsi” & Restaurant “Osteria Manciana” Scudellate / Valle di Muggio For our facilities in Scudellate, in the heart of the Muggio Valley, we look for a reliable, precise and dynamic person to be included in our team with the role of ruler, with a degree of employment from 80% to 100%, for the period from mid-March to mid-November. Your duties • Preparation and breakfast service for B&B guests. • Cleaning and reorganizing of the rooms, refurbishment of the beds and preparation of the rooms for the new arrivals. • Cleaning and care of the common spaces and the different premises of the structures. • Laundry management and, if necessary, ironing of the linen. • Care for details and environments, helping to ensure guests a pleasant and welcoming stay. The profile we seek • Serious, reliable, organized and autonomous person. • Hospitality attitude and attention to cleanliness and details. • Flexibility and availability to work also on weekends and holidays, according to the needs of the structure. • Experience in the hotel industry, professional cleaning or B&B management. • Knowledge of German and/or English is an advantage. • Automunita/o and autonomous/or in the movements: indispensable requirement given the location of the structure.

What we offer

• A degree of employment from 80% to 100%. • Seasonal employment from mid-March to mid-November. • A familiar and welcoming working environment, immersed in the nature of the Valle di Muggio. • A varied activity, in contact with the guests and with the possibility to contribute directly to the quality of the reception of our facilities. Important: accommodation is not available at the moment. It is therefore necessary to reside in a place from which you can reach Scudellate (30 minutes from Chiasso). Interested? If you love hospitality, you are a practical person and you like to take care of environments and people, we will be happy to meet you.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
