Polymechaniker in the field of erosion 80-100%, on site - Swatch Group Polymechaniker/in in the field of erosion 80-100%, on site Your future employer Our employees contribute to the success of the Swiss-Made watches, watches and components produced at ETA SA Manufacture Horlogère Suisse by their competence, passion and commitment. Our company is part of the Swatch Group, the world's largest watch group with 16 renowned brands and numerous production companies that are highly specialized both in watch production and in other industries. The Swatch Business Unit is responsible for the development, production, assembly and delivery of the watch heads for the brands Swatch and Flik Flak. The tool construction is responsible for supporting the injection molding tools. The sophisticated tools are provided for production by repairs, corrections, cleaning and modifications. In addition, the object area also includes the production of new injection molding tools or individual components.
