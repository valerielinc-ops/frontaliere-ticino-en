The Position

Please note this is a 2 year fixed-term position.

The opportunity

  • Building, leading, coaching and managing a strong and collaborative Accounts Payable team covering end-to-end AP support
  • Creating a safe and positive team environment and ensuring that the team demonstrates the right mindset and attitude
  • Setting up clear, right, challenging expectations and goals, with desired behaviors for team members and role modelling these
  • Through frequent check-ins, ensuring that ongoing dialog occurs on the topics of Career, Capabilities, Connections and Contributions; and developing the team members’ knowledge, skills, competency, etc. in order to retain and develop talented staff as well as building a team talent pipeline
  • Setting and monitoring team working plan and resource allocations
  • Developing and managing key stakeholder relationships, working with peers to develop future service portfolio in the area of P2P Services & Solutions
  • Ensuring high quality knowledge transfers and smooth transitions in case of transferring work to own area. Liaising with other Service teams for cross-functional end-to-end service delivery and process improvement

Who you are

  • University degree in Finance or relevant professional experience. Experience in working with SAP.
  • General understanding of finance processes and standards. 2-5 years of experience in a shared service center, with a proven track record of leading regional or global service delivery teams
  • Proficiency in English ( French is an advantage)
  • A growth & continuous improvement mindset, coupled with strong business acumen and an understanding of servicing impact
  • Customer centricity & accountability as well as excellent communication & problem-solving skills
  • Strong leadership competencies and demonstrated success in cross-cultural collaboration, change & stakeholder management
  • You bring a creative leadership style and a coaching mindset (empowering, facilitating accountability, collaboration, experimentation & continuous learning)

In exchange we provide you with

  • Development opportunities: Roche is rich in learning resources. We provide constant development opportunities, free language courses & trainings, the possibility of international assignments, internal position changes and the chance to shape your own career.
  • Excellent benefits: Competitive salary and cafeteria package, annual bonus, Private Medical Services, Employee Assistance Program, All You Can Move Sportpass, coaching / mentoring opportunity, buddy program, team buildings, holiday party.
  • Flexibility: We also support flexibility to help you find your balance. Home office is commonly available (typically 2 office days/week on average). We create the opportunity for freedom in working, where your corporate and private life coexist in harmony.
  • A global inclusive community, where we learn from each other. At Roche, we cooperate, debate, make decisions, celebrate successes and have fun as a team. Our leadership is very focused on people, creating a strong, inclusive culture, so you always have the chance to share your opinion.
