Full description

Portrait For the special elementary school Klipp & Klar, area transitional training in Ipsach we are looking for

A teacher with a socio-educational and/or psychological background for cycle 1 & 2

Tasks

As a special elementary school in the Canton of Bern (bVSA), Klipp & Klar offers individual training for normal and highly gifted children and young people. At the location in Ipsach we offer a school bridge for a maximum of 10 children and adolescents after the completed stay in a psychiatric offer for a maximum of 13 weeks.

The focus of individualised training in individual and group setting is to ensure continuity in the learning process. The aim is to promote stability and orientation as well as reintegration into a long-term school solution in the regular or special school sector. In addition to specialist teaching, everyday practice, social and emotional skills are also promoted.

We trust in the development of potential

Requirements

  • Experience in education with children and adolescents with special educational needs.
  • Experience to design individual and holistic educational offers for children and young people.
  • Openness to work in an innovative team and to contribute educational knowledge.
  • Open to seek creative solutions to various difficulties.
  • Open to teamwork and at the same time the ability to act independently.
  • Social pedagogical knowledge for the design of the daily structure.
  • Enjoy networking and writing reports.
  • Ability to quickly build a relationship.

We offer

  • A setting that creates framework conditions for needs-based, individualized learning with a focus on resources and intrinsic motivation.
  • Good infrastructure
  • Motivated and competent education and leadership team
  • Cooperation with a qualified, interdisciplinary network
  • Specialist advice in the field of autism, trauma and psychopathological peculiarities
  • Workplace in Biel and Ipsach
  • Remuneration by cantonal salary classification
  • Progressive working conditions, regular working hours at school
