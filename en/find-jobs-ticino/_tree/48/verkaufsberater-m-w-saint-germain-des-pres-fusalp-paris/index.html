Full description

As a sales advisor at Fusalp, you play a crucial role in the customer experience. You are responsible for welcoming customers, advising them on products based on their needs and preferences, and guiding them through the purchasing process. You are familiar with Fusalp products, as well as fashion trends and customer needs. You are able to create a welcoming and convivial atmosphere in the store, and provide high-quality customer service to ensure customer satisfaction and loyalty. ## Main responsibilities

  • Be an ambassador for the Fusalp brand: You represent and transmit the values of Fusalp to customers and new colleagues. You are able to welcome and advise our local and international customers. You build customer loyalty through retail sales and high-quality service. You create a memorable experience for customers.
  • Participate daily in the management of the store: You participate in the merchandising of our collections according to the brand's standards. You ensure the smooth operation of the point of sale and maintain a good store appearance. You participate in other operational activities of the store (customer orders, receipt of goods, labeling, inventory, stock management, after-sales / SAV management, cash management, opening / closing and maintenance of the store). You apply the store's safety guidelines (theft prevention and loss).
  • Be a versatile and competent salesperson: You follow sales performance indicators based on established objectives. You are proactive in marketing activations. Constantly on the move, you are alert to customer opportunities. The scope of these missions can evolve based on the needs of the company ## Requirements
  • You are passionate about skiing, fashion, and luxury, you know how to surpass yourself and overcome challenges to achieve objectives and KPIs.
  • You have at least 3 years of experience in a high-end premium environment.
  • You have a developed sense of excellence in service and customer experience in the store.
  • You are dynamic, versatile, and have a keen sense of performance and detail. ## Job information
  • Type of employment: Full-time 35h contract
  • Salary: From €1800 per month based on profile and experience, variable based on objectives
  • Language: French and English fluent ## What we offer
  • Fusalp gives equal importance to technical skills and soft skills in its recruitment. We believe that your personal qualities and human skills are crucial to strengthening team cohesion and ensuring a harmonious working environment.
