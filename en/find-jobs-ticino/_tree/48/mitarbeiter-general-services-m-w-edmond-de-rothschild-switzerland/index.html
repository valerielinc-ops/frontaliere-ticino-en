Full description

Attached to the Department of General Services, you will be in charge of managing all the Bank's archives.

Responsibilities

Archives

  • Provides overall management of the Bank's archives, both internal and external.
  • Makes and updates the inventory of archive boxes in the dedicated IT tool.
  • Centralizes the inventories transmitted by the various departments.
  • Ensures regular updating of the Bank's department's archival inventories.
  • Monitors and tracks archives through the internal program.
  • Responds to requests from other departments to search and locate boxes.
  • Replaces the Head of Archives in the performance of his duties in case of absence.
  • Participates, as required, in projects related to the activity of archives.
  • Scan and scan as required

Handling

  • Ensures the delivery of the requested archive boxes to the services concerned.
  • Collect the archive boxes from the departments, then arrange classification, storage and organization in the dedicated spaces.
  • Organizes, where necessary, the storage of archives on pallets.
  • Coordinates the transportation of archival boxes to requesting services.
  • Proceeds, on request and in accordance with the procedures in force, to the destruction of the expired archives.
  • Ensures that an up-to-date, structured and optimized archive is maintained.

Administrative

  • Place orders with suppliers as part of its business.
  • Contributes to the development and monitoring of statistics related to the activity of archives.
  • Participates in the drafting, updating and improvement of industry-specific directives and work processes.
  • Knows, applies and monitors compliance with internal directives and procedures within its area of activity.

Competences

  • 1 to 3 years' experience in a similar position
  • Very good knowledge of archival legislation
  • Aisance with the Microsoft Office suite's IT tools / good practice
  • Rigorous, organized and methodical profile
  • Good physical resistance
  • Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written
  • Driver's licence required
  • Residence in Switzerland compulsory

Without news from us within a month, you may consider that your application has not been accepted.
