Full description

The Fincons Group is an IT consulting company that has been shaping the digital future of leading companies in international markets for 40 years. The Fincons Group has made a name for itself by predicting and successfully interpreting new business models and the rapid development of IT systems. She has built a complete range of services: from research and consulting to design and development, from system integration of leading software solutions to application management, supporting her customers step by step on their digital transformation journey. Fincons is a multinational company with more than 2,600 employees and several offices worldwide (in Italy, Switzerland, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Belgium and the United States), but above all a family business where the founders play a strategic role with commitment and passion, building the company on the same principles of a united and caring family. Tasks:

  • Conception and further development of the delivery pipeline as well as build, CI and deployment automation
  • Further development of an OpenShift-based platform landscape taking into account stability, security and automation
  • Development of architectural concepts, standards and target images for CI/CD environments and deployment processes
  • Active technical cooperation through development, code reviews, process automation and integration of solutions
  • Responsibility for the architecture of the build, CI/CD and delivery landscape as well as co-design of the technical roadmap
  • Coaching and technical support of engineers and development teams in the areas of CI/CD, platforms and architecture

Requirements

  • At least 5 years of professional experience in software development, software architecture, platform engineering, DevOps or CI/CD with experience in complex platform and delivery landscapes
  • In-depth knowledge of OpenShift, Kubernetes, Container Technologies, GitOps, Argo CD, Helm and Infrastructure as Code
  • Current practical experience in software development as well as programming languages such as Java, Python or Go
  • Experience in technical leadership and in the coordination and control of architectural decisions in agile environments
  • Experience with highly available, business-critical systems as well as the automation of development and operational processes
  • Very good knowledge of German (C1/C2) and good knowledge of English.

Why Fincons?

  • Innovation & Diversity: Work on forward-looking technologies in a creative environment.
  • Career & further development: Benefit from individual development opportunities.
  • A corporate culture in which our highly qualified and satisfied employees are the engine of our success.
  • Flexibility: Hybrid working with home office option.
