Full description

A leading producer of meat, poultry, eggs and fish and as a member of M-Industry, Micarna set itself the goal of always being "just better". Do you share this vision? Join us and contribute to the success of Migros.

Luogo di lavoro: Micarna Group, Micarna SA - Courtepin, Rte de l'Industrie 25, 1784 Courtepin

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Ensure the achievement of the operational, financial, qualitative and human objectives of the sector, including costs, returns and production losses
  • Lead, support and develop managers, specialists and collaborators under your responsibility
  • Ensure optimum personnel planning and availability of facilities, machinery and materials for production
  • Ensure compliance with occupational safety, hygiene, quality, compliance and animal welfare requirements
  • Analyze claims and coordinate corrective action and continuous improvement with relevant sectors
  • Pilot cross-sectoral projects, contribute to the development of production capacities and ensure reliability of data in different systems
  • Coordinate external slaughters and ensure professional collaboration with internal and external partners

Required

  • Professional experience: Confirmed in a production environment, in team management and in continuous improvement
  • Higher school: Higher level training Higher school, higher professional examination or specialized college, ideally complemented by management training
  • French (Very good knowledge) German (Good knowledge): Ideally bilingual French German, with a minimum level required B1 to B2 in German
  • Strong managerial skills and experience in leading teams of varied profiles
  • Good control of common IT tools, including Microsoft Office and SAP
  • A sense of responsibility, autonomy and initiative
  • Analysis capacity, solution orientation and ease in managing complex situations
  • Flexibility, openness to change and entrepreneurial spirit
  • Excellent communication, collaboration and conflict management skills
  • High sensitivity to quality, safety, performance and customer satisfaction issues

Cosa offeriamo

  • Professional provision: Full coverage from the Migros Pension Fund – with first-rate and attractive benefits
  • Social benefits: 730 days of daily sickness benefits with full maintenance of net salary
  • Food: Balanced menu/buffet at the staff restaurant for a maximum of CHF 7.-
  • Points Cumulus: Cumulus points are doubled on food products and quintupled on non-food items.
  • Migros Bank: Migros offers higher interest rates on private accounts and investment accounts to its employees.
  • Migros Club School: 70% reimbursement of course fees, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Sport and fitness offers: Contribution to fitness subscriptions: maximum CHF 200.- per subscription per year
  • Leisure and culture: You benefit from discounted tickets for selected cultural events
  • Training and continuing training: Attractive offer of training and further training
  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave / 8 weeks of paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
