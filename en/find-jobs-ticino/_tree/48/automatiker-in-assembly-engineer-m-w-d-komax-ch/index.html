Full description

Your tasks

  • You mount mechanical, pneumatic and electrical assemblies as well as complete machines and plants.
  • You carry out pre- and final assemblies on the basis of technical drawings and production documents.
  • You wire control boxes and systems according to schematic diagrams and put assemblies into operation.
  • You perform functional, process and quality checks and document the results.
  • You will analyze errors and support technical changes and optimizations in cooperation with engineering, quality assurance and production.
  • You are actively involved in the continuous improvement of assembly processes and in error prevention.

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training EFZ in the field of electrical engineering, automation or in a comparable technical profession.
  • Experience in the assembly of machines, plants or technical assemblies.
  • Good knowledge of mechanics, electrical and pneumatics.
  • Safe handling of technical drawings, schematic diagrams and parts lists.
  • Independent, structured and quality-oriented working methods and a high sense of responsibility.
  • Teamwork, hands-on mentality and joy in working with other specialist areas to develop solutions and drive improvements.

Your benefits

Your new place of work in Dierikon is newly built and equipped with the latest technology and work equipment. Your workplace is located in one of the modern open-plan offices, where there are also inspiring meeting areas and a large terrace for various employee events. Komax AG offers its employees flexible working time models and home office. The further training of employees is important to Komax AG and offers, for example, free internal English courses or access to selected attractive learning platforms. At the Dierikon location, there are also modern break rooms and the possibility of a discounted lunch in a neighbouring partner restaurant.
