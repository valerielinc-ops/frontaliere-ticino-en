Full description

Personal – competent – close Welcome to the Hospital Uster, where modern medicine and family cohesion complement each other perfectly. As the central pillar of health care in the Oberland region of Zurich and the largest employer in the city of Uster, we offer a working environment characterized by helpfulness, honesty and appreciation. Here you experience the highest level of expertise every day in an environment where you are valued as a person and promoted in your professional development. Become part of our committed community and pursue with us the vision of a “regional and digital health hub in Züri East”! As of December 1, 2026 or by appointment, we are looking for a dedicated and motivated personality to strengthen our team in the hospital’s restaurant.

Exciting challenges await you

  • friendly service and care of guests in the self-service restaurant
  • Ensuring a smooth operation (mis en place, presentation of goods, etc.)
  • self-service and accounting of the cash register
  • Management of the Micomarket
  • Cooperation at various internal and external events
  • Cleaning work at the restaurant
  • Compliance with hygiene and quality standards

What sets you apart for the job

  • Completed training in the retail and/or catering sector and several years of professional experience
  • Team player with a positive attitude and pleasant manners
  • High customer and service willingness
  • Readiness for irregular working hours (Monday to Sunday between 06.30 - 19.00)
  • Good oral German language skills

5 Reasons to Work with Us

  • versatile and varied work in a highly motivated team
  • Numerous benefits and generous mobility concept
  • Contemporary remuneration and pension funds models
  • attractive holiday regime, including compensated changing time
  • Highly subsidized employee prices in catering
