Full description

Portrait The school Manuel is a managed quarter school (Schulkreis Kirchenfeld-Schosshalde) of the city of Bern. Since summer 2026 the school has run 44 classes from kindergarten to 9. School year with about 900 children and adolescents and 130 employees. The school location Manuel includes the school complex Manuel, the Wittigkofen School House, the Elfenau Quarter School House, the Kirchenfeld-Schosshalde full-day base level and day care in the Manuel, Wittigkofen and Elfenau School Houses.

Tasks

You teach at two seven. Classes total 17 lessons in the subjects M (5L), NT (5L) and MI (1L). You carry the individual and coaching discussions in the two classes. Co-organization of school events (including country school weeks) is just as much a part of your task as helping to integrate students with special needs.
