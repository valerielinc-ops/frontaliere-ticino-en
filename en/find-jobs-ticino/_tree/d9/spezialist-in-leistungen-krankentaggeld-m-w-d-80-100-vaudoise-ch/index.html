Full description

Vaudoise is one of the leading Swiss insurance companies and an attractive and future-oriented employer. In keeping with its cooperative identity and values, Vaudoise creates a healthy, inclusive and inspiring working environment in which you can develop further. As part of the continuous development of our team in German-speaking Switzerland, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality as a specialist in medical day allowance to strengthen our personal insurance team. In this demanding function, you take over the processing of complex performance cases and actively bring your expertise with you. The following exciting tasks await you:

  • Self-employed and qualified treatment of sickness benefits
  • Assessment of entitlements to benefits taking into account medical, insurance and economic aspects
  • Active and customer-oriented case management in close cooperation with care managers, consulting doctors and other specialists
  • Writing demanding correspondence and conducting complex telephone conversations and negotiations
  • Regular exchanges with insured persons, employers, authorities, doctors and legal representatives
  • Professional support and transfer of know-how within the team
  • Collaboration in the optimization of processes and further development of the faculty This distinguishes you:
  • A basic commercial education in the insurance sector as well as several years of experience in personal insurance, ideally in the medical day allowance industry
  • Private and/or social security certificate
  • In-depth knowledge of personal insurance and a strong understanding of complex benefits cases
  • High decision-making skills and analytical thinking.
  • Stylistic German, knowledge of French is advantageous
  • Communication skills, negotiation skills and assertiveness If you also like to take on responsibility, actively contribute your expertise and tackle complex questions in a solution-oriented manner, then you are the right person for this job! We offer you:
  • A pleasant working environment in a cooperative company that has been voted the best employer several times
  • Numerous benefits and employee benefits, including flexibility in work organization and various discounts
  • Support for further development and training
  • Employee well-being as a first priority We look forward to your complete application documents (with CV, letter of motivation, copy of employment certificates and diplomas).
