Working independently. Carried in a team. Close to people. At Spitex Regio Liestal, we accompany people where they feel at home. Our catchment area includes 16 municipalities with around 49,000 inhabitants. To strengthen our nursing team, we are looking for a Health Specialist (FaGe) EFZ at 50 – 100% Your task You will accompany and care for our customers holistically in their familiar living environment. Portrait SPITEX Regio Liestal is a non-profit organisation with a public mandate from 16 municipalities. For the approximately 49000 inhabitants of these municipalities, it provides a comprehensive range of non-hospital services in nursing, care and housekeeping with six interdisciplinary teams at three bases. In addition to help and care, it offers a meal service and a laundry service. SPITEX Regio Liestal employs 210 people and is an important partner in the regional health care system. Spitex Regio Liestal offers apprenticeships in the tertiary and secondary level and accompanies between 15 and 18 trainees every year, including external internships.
