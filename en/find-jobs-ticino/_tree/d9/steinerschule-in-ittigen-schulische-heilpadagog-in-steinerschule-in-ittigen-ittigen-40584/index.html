Full description

Portrait The Steinerschule Bern Ittigen Langnau is an educational institution characterized by its school philosophy and its special commitment to individual support. Our offer includes multi-level classes in which students with different learning requirements and age groups are taught together. This model offers an alternative to traditional school operations and enables a more personal and individually coordinated learning environment through small class sizes and differentiated teaching methods. This focuses on promoting the strengths of each child.

Tasks

  • Healing pedagogical accompaniment: Support of pupils:women with different learning requirements through targeted, creative and needs-based measures
  • Support sequences: Planning, design and implementation of support programs within and outside the classroom and in team teaching adapted to the individual needs of the learners
  • Advice and guidance: Support and guidance of teachers in the implementation of (healing) educational measures
  • Conducting and accompanying site and support discussions together with class teachers and guardians
  • Cooperation: Close cooperation with teachers, external therapists: inside, specialists, authorities and the school doctor: inside / therapeutic specialists of the school to develop holistic support plans
  • Recording of learning statuses and funding needs as well as preparation and evaluation of individual funding plans (e.g. funding planning)

Requirements

  • You have an EDK-approved diploma as a curriculum pedagog:in
  • They are experienced in dealing with different settings, age groups and learning requirements and appreciate intensive individual encounters.
  • Experience or interest in anthroposophical pedagogy

We offer

  • A diverse working environment: An inspiring working atmosphere in an environment characterized by mixed age groups and different learning requirements
  • A supportive support team: Close cooperation with a dedicated support team that supports the teachers in the individual support and support of children and young people.
  • Small Classes: They work in small groups and can thus specifically respond to the needs of all children / adolescents from cycles 1, 2 and 3
  • Innovative school concept: The opportunity to actively participate in the concept work and to participate in the development of an inclusive school
