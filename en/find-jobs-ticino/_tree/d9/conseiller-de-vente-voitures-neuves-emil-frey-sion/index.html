Full description

What you need to know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with more than 100 years of experience, but is also one of the leaders in the automotive sector. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the pillars of the company. Its main activities are the import of world brand vehicles, the operation of garages, logistics of spare parts and accessories and the provision of financial services. The Emil Frey Sion Automotive Centre is undoubtedly the largest car choice in the canton of Valais, both in terms of its importance and its number of vehicles, the services available and the quality of the staff.
