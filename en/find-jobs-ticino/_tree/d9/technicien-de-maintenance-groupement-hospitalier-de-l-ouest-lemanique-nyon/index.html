Full description

The Groupement Hospitalier de l'Ouest Lémanique (GHOL) brings together several health facilities in the region of La Côte and provides community care for the population.

Your mission

Within GHOL's biomedical service, you ensure the maintenance, preventive maintenance and routine repairs of the equipment used in the hospital and medical-social establishments of the group. As part of a team composed of the service manager and three biomedical technicians, you contribute to equipment reliability, user safety and continuity of care activities. This concrete and varied position is intended for a manual, curious and versatile person who wants to develop his technical skills in a stimulating environment.

Your main activities

  • Conduct preventive maintenance and routine repairs on a variety of equipment, including hospital beds, wheelchairs, health chairs and examination beds.
  • Monitor equipment performance, identify anomalies, and diagnose common failures.
  • Conduct necessary interventions in accordance with established procedures and safety requirements.
  • Document the interventions and monitor equipment in the GMAO (computer-assisted maintenance management).
  • Collaborate with health care teams and different services to meet their technical needs.
  • Participate in the management and monitoring of the equipment park at the various GHOL sites.
  • Depending on your progress and level of autonomy, you can also intervene on more complex equipment and contribute to technical projects.

Your profile

  • You have good manual skill and a strong interest in technique and maintenance.
  • You are versatile, pragmatic and able to seek solutions adapted to the problems encountered.
  • You show curiosity, rigour and autonomy, while knowing how to recognize your limitations and seek advice when necessary.
  • You appreciate teamwork and take a discreet and confidential attitude, essential in hospital settings.
  • You have mastered common computer tools, including Microsoft Word and Excel.
  • You are the holder of the driving licence, which is essential for travelling between the various GHOL establishments.

Diplomas/training

SWC or federal patent in a technical field (operating agent, polymerician, electronician, maintenance engineer, etc.)

Additional assets

  • A knowledge of GMAO software.
  • An experience in a hospital or medico-social setting, appreciated but not indispensable.

We offer you

  • A diverse position, at the heart of the operation of care facilities.
  • Closeness coaching and the support of an experienced team.
  • The possibility of developing your skills and gradually gaining autonomy.
  • Prospects for more complex interventions and participation in technical projects.
  • An opportunity to engage in CDI at the end of the CSD of one year, depending on your skills, your involvement and the results achieved.
