Full description

In this role, you support the department manager in the daily management of the department and actively contribute to the smooth running of production. In particular, you make sure that the recipes, quality standards and goals of the department are adhered to.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: day shift
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: French
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Chemin du Châtelard 5 1033 Cheseaux-sur-Lausanne

Tasks

  • Supports the head of department in the daily management of the department and in achieving the goals
  • Represents the head of department in his absence
  • Reviews, tracks and updates the department’s most important data, in particular revenues and time tracking data
  • Ensures optimal use of materials and raw materials according to recipes and specifications
  • Verifies and ensures compliance with the specifications and quality standards of the department
  • Oversees and manages the procurement of raw materials
  • Ensure compliance with hygiene and safety regulations
  • Collaborates with the various production areas and jumps in at other workplaces if necessary

Requirements

  • Basic vocational training, ideally in the food sector
  • Experience in food production
  • Training for the butcher or sausage manufacturer or experience in the field of sausage products is advantageous
  • Experience leading or supervising a team
  • Secure use of IT tools and interest in capturing production data
  • Organizational skill, care and accuracy
  • Local leadership and good interpersonal skills
  • Self-employment, responsibility and decision-making capacity
  • Very good knowledge of French

This is what we offer

  • Work-life balance: A balanced work-life balance is important to us. You benefit from at least 25 holiday days and a transparent, electronic time recording. So you can flexibly compensate for any overtime with additional free days.
  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: freshly prepared food in our staff restaurant, a lunch menu incl. Soup and salad, water & tea and free fruits as well as high-quality products at greatly reduced prices await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport, attractive subsidies for tickets and sufficient parking in the car park.
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive further education offers, high-quality training in the Coop Education Center and a wide range of personal development opportunities.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management, attendance bonus, discounted Reka checks, exclusive employee discounts and subsidized fitness subscriptions – for health and leisure fun!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments at our departmental event and strengthen the team spirit during the company run – celebrate, move and make contacts together!
  • Market-friendly wages and benefits: Secure your future with our above-average retirement savings, receive anniversary bonuses, Christmas bonuses and attractive employee referral bonuses. In addition, you receive the personal discount card from Coop and we cover two thirds of your pension fund contributions!
  • Family-friendly employer: Make your working hours flexible with part-time models, work in professional work clothes and experience an inclusive work environment where diversity is valued and encouraged!
