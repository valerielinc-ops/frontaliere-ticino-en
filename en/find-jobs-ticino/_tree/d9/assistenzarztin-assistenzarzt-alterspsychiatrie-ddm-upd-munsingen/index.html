Clinic for Aging and Neuropsychiatry

Your tasks

  • You support patients in finding a way out of the disease to a better quality of life and self-determination
  • You take on responsibility in day, night and emergency services
  • You make an important contribution to basic mental health care in the Canton of Bern
  • You work in the clinic for age and neuropsychiatry and support people 65 years and older and cognitively impaired with psychological challenges
  • Under medical guidance, you take on diagnostic tasks and therapeutic case management
  • You work closely with your interprofessional team and are in exchange with relatives and other practitioners
  • You get to know the treatment and the entire spectrum of mental illnesses in depth

Your profile

  • You have a medical degree recognized in Switzerland
  • You are an open, motivated and willing personality with social skills, empathy and observation
  • You appreciate interprofessional cooperation and are happy to take on responsibility
  • You have good oral and written knowledge of German (minor level C1)
  • Ideally, you have previous experience in the somatic field
  • You will actively contribute yourself and your knowledge to the interprofessional team consisting of medical service, psychology, social service and care and expand your own horizons

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
