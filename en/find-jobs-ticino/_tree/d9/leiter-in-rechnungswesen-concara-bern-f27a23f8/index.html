Full description

Tasks

  • You lead employees professionally and personnel.
  • You bring in ideas to make our accounting processes more efficient, digital and sustainable.
  • You ensure that our services are invoiced promptly and correctly.
  • You assume responsibility for monthly, annual and consolidated financial statements according to Swiss GAAP FER and OR.
  • You plan our liquidity forward and ensure that financial obligations can be met on time.
  • You will maintain and develop checklists, work instructions, processes and ICS requirements.
  • You ensure compliance with regulatory requirements, especially in the area of VAT (group taxation).
  • You create ad-hoc evaluations and financial reporting
  • You support the CFO and take on professional topics

Requirements

For this versatile leadership task, we are looking for a personality who combines professional security, leadership strength and a service-oriented attitude.

  • You have completed a commercial education and a higher education in accounting or trusting.
  • You bring several years of experience in accounting or trusting.
  • You have in-depth Abacus knowledge, especially in the modules FI, KRED, DEBI and facilities.
  • You work safely with MS Office, especially with Excel and pivot tables.
  • You communicate safely in German – verbally and in writing.
  • You convince with good manners and strong team skills.
  • You work independently, show your own initiative and think networked.
  • You are resilient, flexible and work efficiently and goal-oriented.
  • You are discreet, secretive and loyal.
  • You think service and customer oriented.
  • You bring understanding and empathy to older people.

We offer

Do you want to contribute your experience and to shape the future of accounting together with us? We look forward to your application. For further information, the supervisor is at your disposal.

Concara Services AG provides central services within the Concara Group. For our finance team, we are looking for a person who enjoys taking responsibility, leading people and actively developing the accounting of the Concara Group. If you like to work in a structured way, appreciate figures as much as you want to help shape the exchange with people and processes, we look forward to you as Head of Accounting.
