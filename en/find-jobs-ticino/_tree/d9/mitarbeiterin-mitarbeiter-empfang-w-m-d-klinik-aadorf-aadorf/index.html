Full description

We are a medically managed specialist clinic for psychiatry, psychotherapy and psychosomatics with a total of 58 beds. The focus is on eating disorders (anorexia, bulimia, binge eating disorder), obesity, depression / anxiety disorders / burnout and psychotherapy for people of the 50+ generation. To strengthen our team, we are looking for January 1, 2027 or by arrangement, an employee receiving (M/F/D) 40%.

ITS TASKS

  • Reception of patients and visitors
  • Telephone exchange operation
  • Orientation / Advice for patients, referees and other stakeholders
  • Scheduling with patients
  • Bed planning in cooperation with station managers
  • Collection of inpatient and outpatient data (case taking and case closure)
  • General administrative work

YOUR PROFILE

  • Commercial apprenticeship or comparable training completed
  • Several years of professional experience at the reception (advantageous in health care)
  • Very good IT knowledge (MS Office, of advantage OPALE and Polypoint RAP/DIS)
  • Professional, service-oriented and empathetic nature
  • Very good communication skills and high social skills
  • Well-groomed appearance and good manners
  • Independent, responsible, precisely working and resilient personality
  • Flexible time (various services from Monday-Friday). The normal workload is between 30-40% and increases to 50% and 60% during 14 weeks (holiday representation).

We beg you... ...a responsible and independent activity in a small team. This in a varied environment with attractive employment conditions, without weekend / evening service and a pleasant working atmosphere. If you have any questions, Thomas Ochsner, Head of Patient Administration, is at your disposal on +41 52 368 88 09. Have we aroused your interest? Then send us your application documents (please use the link below).
