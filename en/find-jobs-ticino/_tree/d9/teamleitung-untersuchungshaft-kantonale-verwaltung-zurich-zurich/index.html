Full description

As part of the UGZ, Zurich West Prison is located in the Zurich Police and Justice Centre and has 241 detention places. It specialises in temporary arrest and pre-trial detention and plays a key role in the reception and first-aid of detained persons. We are looking for a committed, dynamic and humorous leader for Zurich West Prison. You carry the personnel responsibility for the supervisory and support team and accompany persons admitted to prison in everyday life. You strengthen their resources, promote relationship building and shape group execution. You promote normality and stability, prevent crises and act sovereignly in difficult situations. You ensure the quality, develop the company further and work in the regulated shift service with weekend, night and special services. You do that with us.

  • As a member of the management team of the pre-trial detention, you lead your employees professionally and personnel and promote their professional and personal development through targeted education and training.
  • You coordinate and organize day-to-day operations and ensure smooth operations
  • You are responsible for the administrative processes and ensure forward-looking personnel planning
  • You recognize crisis and emergency situations early and act safely and consistently according to the applicable security protocols You bring that with you
  • You have a Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ), a (specialised) Maturity or an equivalent degree
  • You bring your first leadership experience or have completed a corresponding leadership training – Both are beneficial
  • You have very good knowledge of German, master at least one other language and are confident in dealing with IT applications
  • You bring resilience, assertiveness and conflict ability and meet your colleagues and instructed persons in challenging situations sovereignly and respectfully.
