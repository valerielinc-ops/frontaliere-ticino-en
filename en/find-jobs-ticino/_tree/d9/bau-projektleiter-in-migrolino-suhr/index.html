Full description

Variety, responsibility and projects that you can really see at the end! In construction project management, you accompany our migrolino shops through new construction and renovation projects from planning through implementation and thereby shape our appearance throughout Switzerland.

Place of work: migrolino AG, headquarters, Wynenfeldstrasse 3, 5034 Suhr

Duties

  • Responsible for the overall construction management of existing and new migrolino shops in the assigned region
  • Managing the planning teams from the preliminary project phase through to implementation
  • Calculation of rough costs as the basis for investment orders
  • Responsible for maintenance work on the shops
  • Ensuring implementation in line with our CI / CD concept
  • Coordination and ensuring construction-related interfaces with landlords
  • Monitoring adherence to construction costs
  • Conducting the acceptance, organizing the rectification of defects and handling the handover of the construction project to Sales
  • Coordinating schedules with external planners, landlords and sales staff
  • Taking on smaller site management tasks as required

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in a similar position in construction management as well as experience in site management for renovation and new construction projects
  • Higher technical college: Completed vocational training as a structural drafter with further training as a site manager or architect from a university of applied sciences
  • Several years of professional experience in a similar position in construction management as well as experience in site management for renovation and new construction projects
  • Experience in realizing small-format shops, preferably in the retail / convenience sector
  • Responsible and personable personality with a high level of assertiveness
  • Very good MS Office skills
  • Efficient and proactive way of working, as well as a high ability to work under pressure
  • Willingness to travel within Switzerland
  • German (business fluent)
  • French (an advantage)

What we offer

  • Mobile-flexible working model: Opportunity for flexibility in terms of time and location when performing the role.
  • Employee benefits: Lunchcheck 80.-/month, free parking, free coffee.
  • First-name culture: Our values are supported by our first-name culture.
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: Future-oriented environment with a high degree of personal responsibility and scope for innovative ideas.
