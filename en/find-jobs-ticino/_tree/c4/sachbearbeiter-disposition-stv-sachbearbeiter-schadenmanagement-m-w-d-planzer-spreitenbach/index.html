Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

Tasks

  • Our administration and disposition offer professional support
  • Keeping general correspondence and preparing statistics you can do with links
  • You attach great importance to accuracy in the cash management and the processing of expense reports
  • As Stv. Claims management you deal independently with claims cases in the areas of carrier liability, motor vehicle liability and company liability
  • You organize and coordinate appropriate immediate measures for loss management and solution of a wide range of customer concerns
  • Recording and approving claims invoices are also part of your recurring tasks.

Profile

  • You are required to bring a completed commercial basic education or equivalent education. If you have experience in transport logistics, this is a great advantage
  • We also welcome experience in claims management
  • Oral and written you can certainly express yourself in German, English and French are advantageous
  • Contact with chauffeurs, contract drivers and customers will make you happy
  • You have a healthy assertiveness, are communicative and decision-making
  • You work reliably, accurately and quickly adapt to new challenges
  • PC, phone, chat – there you are nimble like a weasel and serve all means of communication with links

Benefits

  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
