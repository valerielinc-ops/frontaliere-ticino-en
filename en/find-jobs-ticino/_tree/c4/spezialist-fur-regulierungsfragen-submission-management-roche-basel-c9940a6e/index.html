The Position As Senior Regulatory Affairs Manager (m/f/d), you will shape the global approval strategy for innovative in vitro diagnostics (IVD) and directly contribute to making life-sustaining technologies available on an international level. You act as a central interface between our local organization, international markets and the Global Regulatory Functions. Working in a committed, appreciative and collegial team You are open at eye level and advance complex approval projects in a solution-oriented manner.

Your central tasks

  • International market registrations: You will be responsible for the preparation, submission and management of international approvals for IVD products with a strong focus on the USA (FDA: 510(k), PMA, De-Novo, Q-Submissions) and MDSAP markets (e.g. Canada, Australia).
  • Strategic R&D Consulting & SOPs: Advise interdisciplinary development teams early on before finalizing design input specifications and create and approve SOPs in accordance with FDA 21 CFR Part 820 (Design Control, Risk Management).
  • Global interface function & strategy: You develop innovative solutions and manage interdisciplinary teams securely through complex regulatory hurdles.
  • Technical Documentation & Compliance: You create and maintain parts of the technical documentation in accordance with IVDR, carry out regulatory assessments for product changes (change control) and maintain existing approvals.
  • Contact with authorities & auditing: You strategically represent our interests in critical enquiries, coordinate feedback and actively participate in notified body audits, FDA inspections and MDSAP audits.

Your profile

  • You are a proactive personality who feels comfortable in a dynamic matrix organization and safely navigates complex stakeholder landscapes.
  • Completed scientific studies (Master or B.Sc. with many years of senior practical experience, e.g. in biology, chemistry, pharmacy, medical technology or regulatory affairs).
  • Many years (7+ years) of deep technical expertise and strategic experience in the area of regulatory affairs for in vitro diagnostics (IVD) or medical devices.
  • Demonstrated practical experience and professional responsibility for FDA submissions (e.g. 510(k), PMA, De-Novo, Q-Submissions) and in-depth knowledge of requirements for the US market.
  • Very good knowledge of FDA 21 CFR Title 21, IVDR, ISO 13485 and the MDSAP framework including practical experience in change control and audits.
  • Strong negotiating and communication skills (also at management level) as well as excellent leadership skills, analytical sharpness, solution-oriented working methods and fluent German and English skills in word and writing (C1 level).

This position is to be filled at TIB Molbiol in Berlin and requires a local presence of at least three days per week.
