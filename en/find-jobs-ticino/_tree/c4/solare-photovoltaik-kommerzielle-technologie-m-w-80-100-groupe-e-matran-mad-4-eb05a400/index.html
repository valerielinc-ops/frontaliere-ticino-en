Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Mission

As a solar photovoltaic Technico-commercial·e, you are actively contributing to the development of our photovoltaic activities in the Broye region. A true trusted partner, you support your customers throughout their project, from identifying needs to final reception of the installation, offering them suitable and sustainable solutions. Your main responsibilities:

  • Actively develop the region's photovoltaic activities by exploring new customers and developing your professional network.
  • Support incoming requests, analyse on-site customer needs and advise them on the photovoltaic solutions most suited to their situation.
  • Develop technical and commercial offers in collaboration with the technical back office and ensure their presentation to clients.
  • Ensure complete monitoring of projects, from the establishment of the offer to the final reception of the facilities, guaranteeing a quality customer experience.
  • Work closely with technical teams to ensure the feasibility, planning and successful execution of projects.
  • Develop a lasting relationship of trust with customers in order to support them in their projects and identify opportunities for the different solutions of the group: photovoltaics, heating, electric mobility, telecom-IT, electrical installations, etc.

Profile

  • Basic training in building technology, ideally CFC as an electrical installer.
  • Experience confirmed in a solar photovoltaic technical-commercial function.
  • Knowledge of the photovoltaic field and the ecosystem with which it can integrate (batteries, heating, charging stations, etc.).
  • Experience in consulting and customer contact.
  • Ability to understand customers' needs and offer global technical solutions.
  • Autonomy, organizational direction and results orientation.
  • French mastery and valid driving licence.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success"
