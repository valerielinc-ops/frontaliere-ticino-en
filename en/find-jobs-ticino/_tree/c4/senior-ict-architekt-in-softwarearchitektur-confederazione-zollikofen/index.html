Full description

Put to the Point

You actively shape the architecture of the IT systems of the EJPD and shape their strategic orientation. With your know-how, you ensure that complex applications are developed and operated in a sustainable, secure and sustainable manner.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 485-19983
  • Place of work: Eichenweg 3, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Assuming architectural responsibility as lead architect in demanding Java-based IT projects with a particular focus on security-by-design and resilient system architectures
  • Design and continuously develop software architectures for highly available, mission-critical applications
  • Ensure safety requirements from design to operation
  • Develop and develop strategic IT architecture for large-scale projects in close cooperation with customers, with cyber security requirements and regulatory requirements being an integral part of the architecture
  • Coach development teams on architecture and security topics as well as conduct architecture and security reviews
  • Define and further develop IT architecture principles and security standards and ensure their sustainable implementation

This makes you unique

  • University degree (FH, Uni, ETH) in computer science or comparable education
  • Several years of experience with highly available, safety-critical multi-tier architectures in the Java/Spring environment
  • Experience in managing architectural topics in complex projects as well as in stakeholder management
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills and enjoyment of strategic work
  • Communication strength and experience in consulting, coaching and knowledge transfer
  • Good active knowledge of two official languages and good knowledge of English

IT for Switzerland’s internal security

High customer satisfaction and economic service provision are among the most important success factors. The ISC-EJPD develops and operates specialist applications for the areas of police, justice and migration of the EJPD.
