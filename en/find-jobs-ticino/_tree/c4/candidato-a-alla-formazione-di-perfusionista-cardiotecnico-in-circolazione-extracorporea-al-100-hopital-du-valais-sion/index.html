The Hôpital du Valais is seeking for the Centre Hospitalier du Valais Romand a Candidate for Perfusionist Training (Cardiotechnician in Extracorporeal Circulation) 100% ## Mission

  • Ensure the maintenance of the medicotechnical equipment specific to the perfusionist activity. ## Profile
  • Tertiary nursing diploma (recognized by the Swiss Red Cross)

  • Ability to adapt to extremely rapid and highly complex situations.

  • Initiative spirit with the implementation of new solutions without any delay

  • Relational and social skills, continuous collaboration with the medical-surgical team.
  • Ability to integrate into a small team and work in close collaboration with flexibility in work time scheduling
