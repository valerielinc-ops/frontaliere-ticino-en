The Position

Who we are

Roche Pharma AG at its site in Grenzach-Wyhlen manages, among other things, the medical activities, marketing and sales activities for the German market. The Marketing division is responsible for the design and implementation of successful marketing activities for our drugs in therapeutic areas with high medical needs.

What to expect

Do you want to contribute your knowledge in an application-oriented environment? In this internship, you can expect a balanced relationship between your own projects and the support of product management leads in day-to-day business. During your internship, you will take on the following activities:

  • Support of product management leads in the implementation of marketing activities
  • Support of our own marketing projects (e.g. creation of materials, websites, banners) as part of our omnichannel communication in cross-functional cooperation and close coordination with advertising and PR agencies
  • Preparation and follow-up of internal workshops, market analyses, creation and merging of presentations
  • In addition, you are part of the Marketing & Customer Excellence community, where further education and joint learning is a priority

Who you are

You are interested in marketing in the pharmaceutical sector and ideally already have initial experience in marketing and project management. In addition, you are customer-oriented and communicative, work in a structured, independent and solution-oriented manner and enjoy solving tasks in a team.

Bringing for an internship in marketing You also have the following requirements and knowledge with:

  • General prerequisite for employment as an intern is regular enrollment during the entire internship or the internship is completed within a gap year, i.e. after the bachelor's degree in preparation for a master's degree
  • You are a student (m/f/d) with a focus on economics, communication or natural sciences or the like.
  • First experience in marketing or health management desirable
  • Digital skills and knowledge in dealing with e.g. Google gSuite or MS Office
  • Good knowledge of English and German in word and writing

Your application Please upload your CV and a short letter of motivation online. Please indicate your earliest start date and how long you are available for an internship.

Remember that this internship is aimed at students (m/f/d) with ongoing enrollment or alternatively at people in the gap year (between Bachelor and Master studies).

Period: from 01.10.2026 or by agreement for a period of 6 months.

What we offer you

  • Mobile work possible
  • Possibility to take advantage of internal further development offers
  • Stay in our boarding house for the duration of the internship (depending on the place of residence)
  • 2.268 € monthly. Compensation for full-time internship > 3 months
  • 2.5 days off work per month

We look forward to your application!
