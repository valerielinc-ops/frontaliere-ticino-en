Full description

Your tasks

  • Together with our interdisciplinary team, you will treat patients with subacute psychological and social problems in the daily clinical setting.
  • After a well-founded familiarization, you lead group therapies and accompany the patients through the structured therapy process.
  • As a nurse, you design the daily program, observe and analyze the group dynamics and document the course of treatment in our hospital information system.
  • In your varied everyday work, you will encounter the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases and work closely with our treatment team from medicine, psychology, care and therapy.
  • At our location in Uznach, you actively participate in rapports, case discussions and training courses and maintain a constant exchange with the other practitioners.
  • Together with your colleagues, you are involved in public relations work and represent the psychiatry of St.Gallen professionally and sympathetically. Your profile
  • You have completed training as a nurse HF/FH and ideally have already gained professional experience in psychiatry (SRK recognition or positive SRK PreCheck required for foreign diplomas).
  • Your interest is in day-to-day clinical, interdisciplinary treatment, which is based on the needs of patients and their environment.
  • Planning and leading group therapies gives you pleasure and the appreciative exchange with patients is yours.
  • You enjoy continuously expanding your expertise, taking up new developments in care and integrating them into your everyday work.
  • You work independently, are open to new things and appreciate working together in a motivated and committed team. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. So you can get a differentiated picture of the company and already prepare for the job. On the first day you can expect an onboarding in your new team and with other colleagues. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also get your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
