Full description

Storenmonteur:in (all genders) Join the Griesser Team! Since 1882 we have been pioneers in the sun protection industry and create favourite places – inspired by the sun. With quality, we are represented as an internationally active family company in 20 countries.

  • Become part of Griesser and create the perfect balance of sun and shadow with us. This is where your new professional chapter starts.
  • Your responsibilities
  • In this role, you are responsible for the professional and timely assembly of our products in the Malans region. The main activities concerned are:
  • Preparation, loading and transport of assembly material
  • Professional installation and installation of sun and weather protection systems (tors, shutters, lamellar doors, etc.)
  • Commissioning, functional testing and minor revisions to existing sun protection systems
  • Independent handling of on-site assembly orders
  • Professional documentation of the work carried out and feedback to internal bodies
  • Compliance with safety, quality and assembly regulations
  • What we offer
  • Personal & professional development: Diverse training and development opportunities
  • Work\-Life\-Balance: 40\-hours\workweek
  • Security \& Future: Comprehensive insurance packages, own pension fund, sickness allowance\-/accident insurance
  • Recreation & Leisure: At least 23 days of vacation, 9 guaranteed public holidays, 16 weeks of maternity leave and 3 weeks of paternity leave
  • Financial advantages: Attractive monthly package fees, annual remuneration and preferential conditions on Griesser products
  • What you bring
  • Completed craftsmanship training with EFZ (ideally as a poly builder specialising in sun protection or alternatively as a metal builder, carpenter, or similar)
  • Experience in assembly (e.g. store, sun and/or weather protection, facade construction, window construction or metal construction)
  • Technical understanding and strong craftsmanship
  • Physical and vertigo-free
  • Independent, reliable and customer-oriented working method
  • Customer-oriented appearance and professional communication with all contacts
  • Category B driving licence mandatory
  • Lifting platform test and PSAgA\ course are advantageous
