Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Are you enthusiastic about art and culture and would like to actively shape the cultural diversity of the city of Lucerne? In this versatile function at the interface of culture, administration and politics, you can contribute your expertise, set your own impulses and help shape urban cultural promotion.

Your tasks

  • You are responsible for individual project funding. This includes the processing of applications as well as the consultation and correspondence with applicants.
  • You prepare their meetings, accompany them and ensure a structured follow-up of the meetings and support decisions.
  • You organize the tendering and implementation of the jury for studio awards and cultural projects.
  • You work in working and project groups and support the implementation of the 2030 cultural agenda.

Your profile

  • You are enthusiastic about art and culture and want to actively shape their promotion and further development.
  • You have a university or university of applied sciences degree or an equivalent education.
  • You bring experience in project work in the cultural sector. Administrative experience is beneficial.
  • You know the cultural events in the city of Lucerne or have a connection to them.
  • You are interested in cultural policy issues and developments.
  • You quickly grasp complex relationships, work independently and have strong organizational skills.
  • You move confidently in dealing with different stakeholders and communicate appropriately.
  • You are a communicative personality, think creatively and act solution-oriented.
  • You safely deal with various means of communication, bring your own initiative and enjoy working in a team.

The Department of Culture and Sports sees itself as a hub for urban cultural and sports policy. Various financial support instruments as well as infrastructures, services and technical advice are made available to players in the sports and cultural sectors.
