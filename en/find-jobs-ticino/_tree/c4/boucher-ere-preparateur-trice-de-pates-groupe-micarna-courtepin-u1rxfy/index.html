Full description

Are you a professional butcher in a production environment? Join Micarna SA, leader in the meat sector in Switzerland as a Butcher / Pasta preparer and put your know-how at the service of the manufacture of our specialties. A concrete and varied position where precision, autonomy and team spirit make all the difference

Luogo di lavoro: Micarna Group, Micarna SA - Courtepin, Rte de l'Industrie 25, 1784 Courtepin

Mansioni

  • You prepare raw materials by sorting, unpacking and assembling them according to production needs.
  • You put your know-how into practice by using the chopping and mixing machines to prepare the different pastas for our sausages
  • You prepare and dose spices accurately according to different recipes
  • You ensure the weighing of the goods at the various manufacturing stages and enter the necessary data in SAP
  • You actively participate in the proper operation of the production by climbing, removing and cleaning the various machines used
  • You ensure the order and organisation of refrigerators and freezers, while respecting the hygiene and working rules in force
  • You work according to morning hours, from 5:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. or from 3:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., Monday to Friday. Work on Saturdays is scheduled occasionally, about 3 times a year

Requirements

  • 1-3 years: Experience in a similar position
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Butcher's-scarf CFC or equivalent training
  • You're an autonomous, conscientious and organized person that the team can count on.
  • You work rigorously and accurately and attach importance to the quality of the work done.
  • You are flexible, especially with regard to morning working hours.
  • You have a good physical condition and work in a cold environment does not scare you
  • You appreciate the fieldwork and fully invest in the different stages of production
  • You're comfortable with computer tools and you know SAP software ideally
  • French (Good knowledge)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave / 8 weeks of paid paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Occupational planning: Full coverage from the Migros Pension Fund – with first-rate and attractive benefits
  • Social benefits: 730 days of daily sickness benefit with full maintenance of net salary
  • Restaurant: Balanced lunch/buffet at the staff restaurant for a maximum of CHF 7.-
  • Points: Cumulus points are doubled on food products and quintupled on non-food items.
  • Migros Bank: Migros offers higher interest rates on private accounts and investment accounts to its employees.
  • Migros Club School: Refund of 70% of course fees, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and culture: You benefit from discounted tickets for selected cultural events
  • Sport and fitness offers: Contribution to fitness subscriptions: maximum CHF 200.- per subscription per year
