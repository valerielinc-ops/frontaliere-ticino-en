Full description

  • Main responsibilities
  • Development of intelligent control solutions to ensure mitigation of business regulatory risk by extracting, transforming, aggregating data into insightful visualizations for Executive Management and Wealth Management (WM) Front Office. You will be reporting to the Head of Business Monitoring in our Geneva office.
  • Analyse and understand the existing controls and processes performed within the WM Business Risk and Monitoring function, understand the data sets and business logic in the background, with the aim to automate the data extraction, transformation and visualisation.
  • Pursue data transformation and development of feeds to Business Control Monitoring tools, and to existing dashboards (platform: MS PowerBI).

  • Contribute to and implement performance & risk management measurement tools.
  • Any other task required by Management relating to the Mission of the employee.
  • Software Proficiency
  • Data extraction and transformation: SQL language. Nice to have: Aqua Data Studio, MS Power Query, SAP Business Object, R, Python, Shiny R Studio.
  • Data presentation: MS Power BI and MS Excel.

  • Assertive, open, and constructive communication, both written and verbal
  • An open, flexible, and confident team-player who can quickly adapt to new challenges
  • Self-disciplined, goal-oriented, having a "Problem-Action-Results" approach
  • Languages : French and English
  • Others
