TRAVECO is looking for you to join us as a project manager with a focus on transport and warehouse logistics, starting on a date to be agreed.

Ursula Bättig HR Business Partner +41 58 434 41 38

These tasks excite you

  • Analyzing and implementing our logistics requirements, with a focus on transport and warehousing, and in other specialist departments
  • Supporting specialist departments in preparing requirements specifications for solution approaches, taking technical system requirements into account
  • Developing proposals for optimization opportunities within the existing solution landscape, as well as contributing to the evaluation of new applications
  • Managing the project, subprojects and work packages; preparing the associated orders; evaluating suppliers and drafting contracts; planning personnel deployment (defining responsibilities, performance targets, personnel planning) for the project
  • Recognizing the impact of change requests, problems and changed framework conditions on the project
  • Testing, documenting and coordinating with internal and external partners
  • Communicating with internal and external stakeholders and presenting the results

This is the profile we are looking for

  • Completed vocational training in logistics with a technical aptitude or in IT with a logistics aptitude
  • A sound understanding of logistics processes and business management issues
  • The ability to grasp things quickly and analytical thinking, as well as an independent and precise working style
  • Curiosity and willingness to independently familiarize themselves with new topics and systems
  • Affinity for data, data models and visualization
  • Ability to question existing processes and propose meaningful improvements
  • Personality that enjoys taking initiative independently in a dynamic environment

Discover your benefits

  • Training and further education: We invest in the development of our employees. A broad range of internal educational opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external continuing education.
  • Attractive vacation policy: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. From 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks and from the 60. Year of age, even 7 weeks. In addition, it is possible to purchase additional vacation days.
  • Family time: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent enjoys 3 weeks of family time at full pay.
  • Insurance benefits: We provide our employees with above-average social security and pension benefits and offer special conditions on supplementary insurance with various health insurance providers for their entire family.
  • Discounts and rebates: Among other things, our employees benefit from discounts when shopping and refueling, credits for REKA money, and discounted mobile plans for themselves and their families.
