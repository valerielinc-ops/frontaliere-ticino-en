Full description

We're looking for an 80% nurse for our pediatric emergency department at the Clinic and Permanence of Onex SA. You will join a dynamic team in a stimulating environment where the quality of care, responsiveness and support of children and their families are at the heart of our priorities. Your missions

  • Reception, evaluation and care of children in emergency situations, in collaboration with the medical team.
  • To carry out the clinically appropriate nursing care of the child in accordance with the protocols in force.
  • Ensure clinical monitoring of patients and adapt the management according to the evolution of their condition.
  • Administer treatments and perform prescribed technical actions.
  • Participate in the management of paediatric emergency and acute care.
  • Accompany and inform parents throughout the care, with listening and benevolence.
  • Ensure the traceability of care and ensure the quality of documentation in the patient file.
  • Work closely with physicians and other health care professionals to ensure coordinated care.
  • Ensure compliance with hygiene, safety and good practices.
  • Participate in service life, team meetings and ongoing training. Your profile
  • Diploma of nurse recognized in Switzerland and right of practice in the canton of Geneva.
  • Experience of at least five years in pediatrics.
  • Obtaining the Pediatric Advance Life Support (PALS) certification or to perform.
  • An experience in paediatric emergency service or acute care is a real asset.
  • Excellent ability to manage emergencies and work under pressure.
  • A sense of responsibility, autonomy and good organizational capacity.
  • Team spirit, relationship ease and caring approach to children and their families. We offer
  • No night guards
  • Participation rate at 80%.
  • 5 weeks of vacation a year.
  • A friendly, dynamic and specialized working environment.
  • A start as soon as possible.
