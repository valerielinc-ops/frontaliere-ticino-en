Full description

The KM Channels & Production Department is responsible for the strategic and operational management of supraregional communication channels (Intranet, social media, newsletters, websites, etc.), Community Management and KM national partnerships. It ensures an attractive, coherent and responsive format and dissemination of communication content to target audiences.

Your Mission

Within the KM Channels & Production department of the SSR, from December 2026 (or date to be agreed), your specifications will include the following:

  • Collaboration within the Live Communication service, particularly in the organization and implementation of internal and external events (guest management, Evenito management, event organisation, etc.)
  • Participation in partnership management (communication, marketing and mobilization measures, banknote draw, etc.)
  • Participation in the development and implementation of communication measures for Publishing
  • Coordination and administration tasks

Your profile

  • Diploma (university/high school) in communication or media sciences, or comparable training in communication, sponsorship or Live Communication
  • First experiences in the areas of sponsorship, Live Communication or corporate communication
  • Interest in the Swiss media landscape, audiovisual media and communication
  • French or German mother tongue with good oral and written knowledge of the other language
  • Aisance with the usual means of communication and Office tools, ideally with good experience of CMS system

What we offer you

  • An overview of the different areas of communication of an electronic media company (newsroom) and the many partnerships and projects related to Live Communication
  • A multilingual working environment
  • A position within a motivating team, a good working atmosphere and an open mode of communication

Deadline for application: 12 November 2026. Thank you for your interest in SSR.

Our promise

Reaching the entire Swiss population with high quality formats requires expertise, enthusiasm and a stimulating and creative environment, in which everyone can invest and evolve. For the best to give the best of themselves, they need to have a source of inspiration: Welcome to the world of SSR!

  • Create Content: Quality journalism is the heart of our activity and passion. We enjoy complete freedom of writing, encourage creativity and grant autonomy that promotes novelty and quality.
  • Living digital: In our society, quality journalism can also take a digital form. This is why we are exploring new opportunities and experimenting with new formats and practices: digital, interactive and cross-media. Our content fits into the air of time and responds to public concerns.
  • Human being: We maintain informal relations, work independently and contribute to a good working atmosphere. Our success is based on the strengths of everyone and on the technical skills of all our employees.
  • Make sense: With our program, we reach all of Switzerland. We act independently and respectfully to encourage the formation of opinion and strengthen cultural diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

  • Intérêt prononcé pour le paysage médiatique suisse, les médias audiovisuels et la communication
