Team Leader Procurement Construction 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team Leader Procurement Construction Olten / Work Smart, suitable, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

You want to make a difference? Then you're in the right place. An extraordinary mission in a dynamic environment awaits you. In collaboration with your team, you organize the purchase of engineering and construction services so that construction projects can progress efficiently and travellers can travel without obstacles. A portfolio of varied projects awaits you: new constructions and transformations, as well as renovations of stations, tunnels, bridges and entire railway sections. You work closely with project managers, technical services, legal services and other interfaces, and assume responsibilities with a concrete impact. What you can move.

  • At the head of the Infrastructure Division's regional construction project procurement, you will be responsible for both personnel and operational responsibility.
  • You advise your eight project purchasers on procurement and contractual issues governed by public procurement law and define individual development measures.
  • You manage regional purchasing operations according to commercial principles and sign allocations up to CHF 10 million.
  • As a member of the management team, you participate in regional and national forums and actively engage with internal and external stakeholders.
  • In expert groups, you propose approaches for continuous improvement of processes and methods.
  • You are willing to travel within the region and perform regular missions in Olten, Lucerne and Bellinzone.

What you bring.

  • In this leadership function, assertive managerial skills, such as results-oriented leadership and development-oriented leadership, are of great importance.
  • You quickly become familiar with new and complex issues and act in a results-oriented and quality-sensitive manner. You have already gained experience in managing climbing and critical project situations at the management and management levels.
  • Your assets include entrepreneurial thinking and cost awareness, as well as excellent communication and animation skills.
  • Experience in project procurement and/or construction is desired
  • You have a degree from a high school of specialization (in the field of engineering, business economy or equivalent) or a higher federal patent (responsible for graduate purchases or equivalent training).
  • A master's degree in spoken and written German (C2) is required. You speak French or Italian with very good knowledge (B2) of the other national language.

Your salary and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This commitment is in accordance with the Code of Obligations, taking into account a comparison with the team, market and industry.
