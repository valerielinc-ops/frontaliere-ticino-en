Full description

Attentive and competent. We are a private clinic for psychosomatics, psychiatry and psychotherapy with 108 beds at a central location in Rheinfelden as well as two ambulatories in Rheinfelden and Aarau. The clinic is recognised by the FMH as a training centre for the specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy (2 years inpatient, 2 years outpatient), as well as by the SAPPM (2 years). Our treatments focus on individual and group psychotherapies. We work interprofessionally and resource-oriented, including physical and creative therapeutic offers. We attach great importance to somatic and psychosocial aspects. We are looking for 01. January 2027 or by arrangement for the stationary area Senior physician (80–100%) inpatient department for intensive psychotherapy for young adults In our department, we treat young adults aged around 18 to 35 years. The focus is on eating disorders, personality disorders, ADHD, autism spectrum disorders, depression and anxiety disorders.

  • We offer both diagnostics and therapy in the above disorders. The job becomes vacant due to an internal promotion.

We wish for an open and reliable personality with pleasure in interprofessional cooperation and deeper interest in psychotherapy and experience in the treatment of young adults. A completed specialist title psychiatry and psychotherapy is desirable, but not mandatory for advanced training. To your

  • In addition to therapeutic work, tasks include in particular the leadership of the interprofessional treatment team, the further development of treatment concepts and participation in overall clinical management tasks. A longer-term commitment is welcomed.

We offer you a versatile, multi-professional working environment with design possibilities and development perspectives. They benefit from attractive employment conditions on the basis of a 43-hour week and 6-week holidays, a wide range of further and further training opportunities as well as an open and appreciative company culture with regular events for employees. For further information, our chief physician and clinic director, Frau Dr med. Katharina Gessler , katharina.gessler@klinikschützen.ch , 061 836 26 62, is available.
