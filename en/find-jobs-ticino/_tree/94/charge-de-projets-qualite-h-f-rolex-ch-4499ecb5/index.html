Introduction

As part of the development of our activities in the canton of Fribourg, we are a pool of talents to accompany the deployment of our new industrial site in Bulle. To this end, we will soon be looking for profiles of (M/F) Quality Project Manager in order to strengthen our teams.

Responsibilities

  • Promote preventive quality in project groups dedicated to new products and processes
  • Implement industrial qualification processes
  • Collaborate with component suppliers, subcontractors and manufacturing process suppliers
  • Participate in the qualification of new products and processes
  • Conduct product and process risk analyses
  • Pilot specific or transversal quality projects
  • Contribute to continuous improvement of quality practices and processes

Profile

  • Technical and/or quality training at the Bachelor's level or equivalent
  • Experience in a similar function, ideally in an industrial environment
  • Knowledge of quality tools, including statistical analysis and risk analysis
  • Project management knowledge
  • Lean / Six Sigma training, an asset
  • Leadership, ease in communication, autonomy, rigour and organizational sense
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis, complemented by a capacity to popularize technical subjects
  • Availability for initial training in Geneva before continuing the mission in the canton of Fribourg

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
