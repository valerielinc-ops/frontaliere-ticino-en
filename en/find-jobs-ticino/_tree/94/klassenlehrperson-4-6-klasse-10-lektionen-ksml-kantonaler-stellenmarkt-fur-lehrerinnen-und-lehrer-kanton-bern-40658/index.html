Full description

Portrait The school 2556 Schwadernau / Scheuren is located on the Aare and offers with its great change a lot of space for learning and discovery.

The children from Schwadernau and Scheuren attend the kindergarten and the primary level at our school together. In the school building Schwadernau two kindergarten classes and two 1st – 3rd classes lead. The Scheuren School House has two 4th – 6th classes at home.

Currently, around 110 students are accompanied by 19 teachers.

Tasks

For our 4th–6th. Class b in Scheuren we are looking for a motivated and dedicated teacher for 10 lessons.

Do you enjoy accompanying children on their learning path, do you teach with enthusiasm and do you appreciate teamwork? Then this deputy could be just right for you!

  • Lessons in NMG, Mathematics, German, English, Music and Sports on Wednesday and Friday mornings 7:30 - 11:45
  • Takeover of 2,5 % class management
  • Accompanying the 4th to 6th class to the ski camp in March (08.03.27 - 12.03.27)
  • A varied and exciting time with a 4th – 6th class
  • An open, helpful and committed team

Requirements

  • A completed teacher training or you study at a Pedagogical College.
  • You are a humorous, uncomplicated and resilient personality with a lot of initiative.
  • Enjoy teaching and working with children.
  • An appreciative approach to students: inside and their parents is a matter of course for you.
  • They appreciate the constructive and solution-oriented cooperation in the team.

We are happy to consider applications from people with other training, provided they have extensive teaching experience.

We offer

In our small, family-run schoolhouse you can expect an open and appreciative school culture. A helpful team, a modern infrastructure with a screen and visualizer in each classroom and your own work equipment are at your disposal.

The location and transfer talks are already completed at this time.

Have you become curious? We look forward to your application and to welcoming you to our team!

If interested, the position can be combined with another deputy from January. This makes it possible to extend the workload.
