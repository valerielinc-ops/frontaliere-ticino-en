Full description

We make spaces where it is good to live.

Delémont (JU)

At Arnold, grid electricians build and maintain electricity distribution facilities. A varied and challenging daily work awaits you on well-educated teams that actively support you during your three years of training.

Your tasks

As a specialist in all electrical network work, you are responsible for the construction and maintenance of cables and overhead lines, coupling and processing stations, distribution booths, domestic connecting boxes and public lighting. Whether in the city or in the country, you are on a daily basis outside and in any season. In order to minimize the risks associated with your demanding activity, you learn how to use tools and machines safely, both in-house and in-house. Whether in the trenches or on the towers of the airlines, as a network electrician, you work exclusively in groups. Good cooperation and mutual trust are therefore essential. In our home, you can also do a second apprenticeship in CFC power/network.

Your profile

  • You finished high school or college with good math skills.
  • You have a good technical understanding.
  • You like and enjoy handwork
  • You have no trouble with color perception or vertigo
  • You're reliable and you like working as a team
