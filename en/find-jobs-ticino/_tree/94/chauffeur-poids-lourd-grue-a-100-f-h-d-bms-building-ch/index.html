Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire with great commitment construction customers and private customers on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

To strengthen our team based in Bulle from 1 January 2027, we are looking for a motivated personality with excellent knowledge as a Heavy Weight Crane Driver as

Missions

  • Heavyweight driving in compliance with safety standards
  • Inter-agency delivery as well as for our customers on construction sites
  • Arrays of goods
  • Loading and unloading of vehicles
  • Maintenance of vehicles (washing, lubrication)
  • Digital tablet handling and use (PTV)

Your Profile

  • Holder of a driver's licence for heavy goods vehicles with trailers (EC)
  • Being up to date with OACP courses
  • Compulsory crane licence, trailer and carist truck
  • Know how to work independently while easily integrating into a team
  • Demonstrate dynamism, flexibility and versatility
  • Having ease in contacts, good oral expression
  • Very good presentation

We offer

  • A challenge within a changing company
  • A comprehensive integration programme; our new colleagues also benefit from in-house training on products and systems
  • Support for the professional and personal development of our employees
  • Collaborating discounts, various discounts such as fleet discounts, discounts in selected fitness centres and numerous offers to promote their mental and physical health
  • An attractive wage package combined with a solid pension fund, with flexible savings contributions and above average employer share
  • 5 weeks of holidays (6 weeks from 50 years) and the possibility of buying up to 10 additional days of vacation, as well as additional benefits such as a highly funded autonomous pension fund and discounts on our entire product range as well as external premium benefits
