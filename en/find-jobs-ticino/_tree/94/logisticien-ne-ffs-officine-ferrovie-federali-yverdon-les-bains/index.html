Logisticsien·ne 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Logistician Yverdon-les-Bains, 01.03.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

Located in Yverdon-les-Bains, the SBB Workshops are a historic site where specialized teams maintain and modernize rolling stock. Looking to the future, the site is gradually evolving to accommodate new facilities and offer workspaces adapted to the requirements of tomorrow, while continuing its activities. Joining the Workshops is integrating an industrial environment rich in know-how, in full development, where teams contribute every day to the mobility of tomorrow. To find out about the benefits, such as free AG and low-cost travel abroad (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits . What you can move.

  • As a logistician, you are responsible for receiving goods for the maintenance of railway vehicles.
  • You properly control and condition the various items following returns from production for stocking.
  • You are in charge of the quantitative and qualitative control of the incoming goods, which you then register in the system before placing in stock.
  • In daily contact with internal and external customers, you prepare orders and make inventories.
  • You load the vehicles for shipping.

What you bring.

  • Thanks to your team spirit, you integrate perfectly into a new environment. You enjoy being a reference person for your colleagues and clients. In addition, you enjoy working independently on your daily tasks.
  • Your journey allows you to bring at least 2 years of experience in a similar position.
  • At the level of training, you benefit from a Logistician CFC.
  • You benefit from a counterweight, electric and retractable mast forklift licence. The crane license is a plus.
  • You master French and are able to communicate professionally and constructively, both with your clients and with your superiors.
  • You have a special affinity with the usual IT tools (MS Office). Ideally you have already worked with SAP.

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive salary system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the E level and gives entitlement to a level 0 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits .

Here's a good way to start.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the biggest motivation for us. We therefore waive our request for a cover letter for this position. If you wish, you can write to us in our application tool.
