Full description

Pensum: 100% | Kraftwerk Linth – Limmern | Tierfed

Make a contribution to the energy future. Use your know-how to ensure that our power plant infrastructure runs reliably. At Kraftwerke Linth-Limmern AG, you play a central role in a technically demanding power plant environment. With your expertise, you ensure that our systems for heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary and electric are safe and ready for use at all times. You get involved, find pragmatic solutions and actively shape the further development of our infrastructure.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You ensure the safe operation and technical availability of our HLKKSE and fire alarm systems
  • You analyze disruptions, fix them in a targeted manner and identify potential for improvement at an early stage
  • You plan and coordinate maintenance, maintenance and repair work
  • You assume professional responsibility for renewal, optimization and investment projects
  • You coordinate our HVAC employees as well as internal specialists and external specialists:
  • You work in a versatile environment where your experience and commitment work directly

Profile & Requirements

  • Completed basic training with EFZ in the field of HVACs
  • Several years of practical experience in the operation, maintenance, optimization and renewal of HLKKSE plants – ideally in a power plant or industrial environment
  • Experience with building control systems and the common MS-Office applications
  • Routine in cooperation and coordination with internal and external partners:
  • A category B driving licence
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • A further education in maintenance or building technology is a plus
  • A connection permit according to type. 15 NIV or an education as a waterkeeper are advantageous
