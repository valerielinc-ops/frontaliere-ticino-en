Full description

Located in Onex for 15 years, the DeltaPharm pharmacy is a neighborhood clinic integrated into a health home. It supports a local clientele and is part of an environment conducive to exchanges between health professionals. We are looking for a 100% responsible pharmacist to take responsibility for the official and accompany his team on a daily basis. Your conditions:

  • Permanent contract
  • Start: as soon as possible
  • Activity rate: 100%, based on 40 hours per week.
  • Right to holidays: 5 weeks per year Your mission: You ensure the professional responsibility of the pharmacy and ensure the quality of the services, the safety of the dispensation and compliance with the requirements applicable to the official. As such, you:
  • Welcome and advise patients, monitor and validate prescriptions and contribute to the quality of their care.
  • Coach and accompany the team, fostering collaboration and skills development.
  • Organize daily activity, including planning and managing absences.
  • Pilot the QMS Pharma quality management system and ensure that ISO 9001 certification is maintained, procedures are applied and practices are continuously improved.
  • Work with management to adapt the organization and the means to the needs of the official and develop its services. Your skills:
  • You have a diploma in pharmacists recognized in Switzerland and an authorisation to practise under your own professional responsibility.
  • You have a solid experience in team coaching.
  • You appreciate the contact with patients and the collaboration between healthcare professionals.
  • You are rigorous, organizational, flexible and proactive.
  • You master the usual computer tools
  • Tactil's knowledge is an asset.
