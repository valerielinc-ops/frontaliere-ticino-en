Description of post

As Restaurant Manager, you support the Manager in the management of the team and the smooth running of operations. You take on additional responsibilities to ensure quality, food safety, service and hygiene, and you contribute to the restaurant's success. You coach and motivate team members, coordinate priorities and promote a positive and respectful atmosphere.

Key responsibilities

  • Lead the team with empathy, respect and patience
  • Communicate openly and manage conflicts constructively
  • Define strategic priorities and coordinate day-to-day operations
  • Coach and support of Shift Leaders and Crews for optimal performance
  • Ensuring food safety, quality, service, cleanliness and hygiene standards
  • Use management tools for planning, cashing, ordering and inventory
  • Analyze KPIs and support restaurant development initiatives
  • Participate in recruitment, integration, training and staff engagement
  • Manage claims and create ‘feel-good' moments for hosts
  • Maintain equipment and solve problems

Qualifications required

  • Experience in team management or valued restoration
  • Excellent communication and conflict management skills
  • Analytic, organized and comfortable spirit with technology
  • Proactive, solutions oriented and eager to learn
  • Reliable, trustworthy and able to work under pressure
  • Good command of the local language (oral and written)

  • Training and opportunities for evolution – many Restaurant Managers have started as Assistant!

  • 50% on our products in restaurants in Switzerland

  • Access to Corporate Benefits platform
  • Additional holidays for over 50s
  • Free access to the largest online training library
