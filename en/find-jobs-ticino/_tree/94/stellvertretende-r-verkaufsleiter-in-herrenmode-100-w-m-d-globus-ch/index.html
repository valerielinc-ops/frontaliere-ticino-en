Deputy:r Sales Manager:in Menswear (m/f/d) With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the “place we love to be” to life together with us. Here you make the difference

  • Together with the sales management, you make sure that our customers not only shop, but also take a fashion experience that inspires and inspires.
  • In your absence, you take over the personnel and organizational leadership of the department and provide an environment in which consulting culture, style awareness and team spirit are lived.
  • You advise the team in day-to-day business, promote type-appropriate advice and bring your strengths to bear in visual merchandising and the staging of brand and style worlds.
  • Performance tracking and KPI analysis. You keep an eye on the performance of the team to make strategic decisions that will make us successful together.
  • You control processes efficiently, support events and administrative tasks – and ensure that digital tools are also used smartly.
  • You accompany new employees and learners, show leadership with heart and mind.

That draws you out

  • You work in a structured, entrepreneurial way and actively support the team.

  • You use digital applications with ease.
