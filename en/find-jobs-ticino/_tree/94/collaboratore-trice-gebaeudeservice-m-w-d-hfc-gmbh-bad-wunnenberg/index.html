Full description

As an employee for building services (m/f/d), you are responsible for the safety and smooth operation of the plant – this means the control of people and goods, the monitoring of our security facilities and the reception of visitors. In addition, you support our team with general tasks, are the contact person at the telephone exchange and take care of the procurement, configuration and administration of our IT. Tasks:

  • Your main task is to check deliveries and vehicles. Management and allocation of central keys (works vehicles) and their monitoring
  • Control of video surveillance, alarms and fire alarm systems. Carry out indoor and outdoor inspections.
  • Representative welcome of guests and forwarding to the responsible contacts.
  • Procurement, configuration and administration of all IT and telephony devices
  • Acceptance, sorting and distribution of mail and support for sample shipping Requirements:
  • You have completed an apprenticeship in the commercial/security sector with proven IT professional experience
  • Practical experience with IT ticket systems and hardware support of workstations
  • Fluent knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
  • High service orientation, pronounced communication ability, well-maintained appearance and absolute reliability in dealing with security requirements
  • Readiness for shift work
