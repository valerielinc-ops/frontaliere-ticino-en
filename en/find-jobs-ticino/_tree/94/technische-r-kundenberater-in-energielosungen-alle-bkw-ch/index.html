Full description

You have an electrotechnical background and would like to use your expertise in consulting and in the office in the future? In this function, you accompany customers in new forms of energy use and contribute your practical experience. You can expect an exciting environment around the energy transition

Your field of activity

  • You will advise and support our customers on technical and practical questions relating to energy communities such as associations for self-consumption (ZEV) and local electricity communities (LEG).
  • Thanks to your electrotechnical background, you understand their concerns and can classify technical issues in a practical way and convey them in an understandable way.
  • In difficult business cases, you support the customer consultants from the Customer Center and coordinate issues within BKW and its subsidiaries.
  • You maintain customer relationships in accordance with our guidelines and ensure high-quality services as well as professional process quality with quality controls.
  • You bring your practical experience in the further development of our processes, customer journeys and IT systems and help to improve processes.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as an electrical installer, assembly electrician or comparable and have experience in the electrical industry.
  • In the future, you would like to use your technical knowledge in an advisory function in the office.
  • You enjoy contact with people and support customers with technical issues.
  • You work independently, reliably and solution-oriented and at the same time appreciate the exchange in an open team.
  • Communication in German and French is easy for you.

With this function, we offer you the opportunity to further develop your experience from the electrical industry in a new professional direction. Instead of on the construction site, you will deal with topics that are becoming increasingly important for the future energy supply. The position is therefore particularly suitable for electrical specialists who want to remain close to the technology, but want to develop away from the construction site and towards a consulting activity. We do not want requests from personnel service providers.
