What you do

  • You lead and coach your team with an open, supportive leadership style and create a motivating work environment.
  • You take on the personnel and professional responsibility for your team and accompany your employees in their professional and personal development.
  • You control and lead your team using key figures and take responsibility for performance, quality and goal achievement.
  • You work operationally, support your team in day-to-day business and are the first contact person for specialist questions.
  • You are a role model for your employees and live a high service concept.
  • You ensure impeccable, comprehensive and solution-oriented customer service.
  • You actively promote digital innovations as well as process optimization in claims processing.
  • You work across departments with internal and external partners: together and bring your know-how effectively.
  • You participate in projects and participate as a member of the management team in strategic topics.

What you bring

  • Strong, winning personality with strong motivation for leadership tasks.
  • Several years of practical experience in claims regulation, ideally in the field of motor vehicles.
  • Experience in the management of a team as well as enjoyment of coaching and developing employees.
  • Organizational talent that keeps an overview even in hectic situations.
  • High customer and service orientation as well as enjoyment of contact with employees and customers.
  • Qualified education and training in the insurance industry (e.g. Swiss Federal Law). Professional ID, insurance operator: in HF.
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of French
  • Italian is beneficial.
