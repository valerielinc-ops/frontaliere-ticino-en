Full description

What to expect

  • You are responsible for the stable operation and further development of an SAP module in the area of SD/WM/EWM
  • They work in IT in a group of developers and module supervisors and are in exchange with the power users from the specialist departments.
  • Your tasks include analyzing user requests, specification for development, customizing, documentation, testing, and coordinating the implementation of changes and fixes.
  • In projects you work with or coordinate cooperation and ensure that deadlines are met and the quality is right

What do you bring with you

  • You have higher education in computer science and/or business administration
  • You have in-depth SAP knowledge, preferably you have experience in SAP consulting
  • You like to take responsibility and solve problems independently
  • They understand the requirements of the specialist departments and can translate them into the language of computer science.

You can look forward to that.

  • Working in the middle of beautiful natural and recreational areas
  • Exciting tasks in an international environment with short decision paths
  • Lots of personal responsibility and attractive management positions
  • Market-oriented remuneration, progressive social benefits and own pension fund with good benefits
  • Staff restaurant, free parking

About us

EMS has 25 production sites in 16 countries, employs around 3,000 people worldwide and, with around 140 apprentices, is the largest vocational trainer in the canton and winner of the National Education Award.

EMS-CHEMIE is an internationally successful company specializing in high-performance polymers and specialty chemicals. The headquarters is located in Domat/Ems, directly next to Chur.
