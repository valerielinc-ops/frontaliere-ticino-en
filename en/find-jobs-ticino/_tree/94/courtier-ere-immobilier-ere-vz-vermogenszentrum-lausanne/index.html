Full description

You're passionate about real estate, you advise clients equal to equal, and you want to actively support the sale of residential properties? Then join our growing team.

  • You accompany the owners in the sale of their residential properties, from the first council until the conclusion of the contract.
  • You evaluate real property, develop marketing strategies, coordinate visits and negotiate with interested people.
  • You work closely with our specialists in the fields of foresight, financial advice and real estate consultancy, which regularly identify real estate sales opportunities.
  • You maintain relationships with existing customers and develop your own network.
  • You represent our company professionally and contribute to the growth of our business.
  • You have at least 2 years of experience in marketing or selling real estate and ideally have the federal real estate broker's patent.
  • You know the regional real estate market and maintain a quality network.
  • You convince in advice and sales thanks to your skills and your insurance.
  • You work independently, structured and with a lot of initiative.
  • You are fluent in French, both oral and written. A basic knowledge of German, allowing internal exchanges and understanding, is desired.
  • You have a valid car driver's licence (category B).
  • You have the will to undertake and develop the activity and you can adapt in a changing environment.
  • You know how to collaborate, work as a team and take advantage of common experiences to make progress.
  • A business model with high growth potential and many opportunities.
  • A fixed annual salary, without dependence on commissions.
  • A dynamic and committed team you can count on.
  • A simple and open culture, in which you can actively engage.
  • Surveys regularly show that collaborators view VZ as a particularly positive environment, in which they can flourish fully. Join us!
