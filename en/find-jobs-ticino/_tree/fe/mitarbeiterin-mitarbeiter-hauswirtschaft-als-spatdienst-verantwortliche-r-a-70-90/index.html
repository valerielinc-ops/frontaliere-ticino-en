Location: Hirslanden Klinik Aarau | Aarau

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 61448

Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for cutting-edge medicine in a manageable, personal environment - true to our clinic goal "First-class medicine with personal treatment, as we wish for our loved ones".

YOUR TASKS

  • You work weekdays from 12:15 to approx. 21:00, during the introductory phase you also work in the morning
  • As a late service manager, you are jointly responsible for planning and organizing the evening service business and ensure a smooth process.
  • You take instructions in the field of cleaning
  • You work actively in the maintenance cleaning in the risk area and on the bed stations
  • You support the introduction of new employees
  • You support in the internal laundry and transport service
  • You routinely use the cleaning software for data acquisition and planning
  • We appreciate your commitment on all working days, occasionally also on weekends

Your profile

  • You have completed vocational training as a specialist hotel industry EFZ or building cleaner/in EFZ
  • You enjoy the instruction and promotion of employees
  • You are adept at handling MS Office
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • For you, the friendly treatment of patients and visitors is a matter of course, because we focus on people
  • We are also looking forward to applications from re-entrants and graduates

OUR OFFER

  • 5 weeks holiday
  • 4 additional paid days off as compensation for changing time• Market-based wages and very good social benefits• Various training opportunities• Subsidized daycare center (KITA) "Small Foot" next to the clinic• Various discounts (fleet discounts, fitness, mobile subscriptions and much more)

For additional information, Selma Utlu, Team Leader Housekeeping, under T, is at your disposal. We look forward to your contact.
