Full description

Do you know the field of Underwriting Life and want to bring your knowledge where it really has an impact? With us, you can take responsibility, bring your ideas and contribute actively to shaping the future of our life insurance business. Together, we create solutions that make a difference for our clients. And because we need a good balance, we offer you flexible working hours, a generous share of telework and a culture of equals. When do we meet?

Your mission

  • The heart of the subject: you yourself assess the medical, non-medical and financial risks and record your assessments in an understandable way.
  • You process life insurance proposals and ensure that compliance directives are respected at all times.
  • You actively request missing documents and ensure an effective risk review.
  • In exchanges with sales partners, clients, doctors and reinsurers, you clarify the situation and find suitable solutions.
  • From examination to conclusion: you establish policies, treat reluctance and ensure a high level of quality in new cases.
  • You support Sales for questions about new business and actively bring your know-how.
  • You work mainly in Lausanne or in telework and you are once or twice a month at the head office of Lucerne.

What you bring

  • Let's start with the main point: you have several years of experience in dealing with proposals and examining risk in the field of life insurance.
  • You are confident in assessing medical and financial risks and making informed decisions, even in complex cases.
  • You've completed business training in insurance.
  • German and French are part of your professional daily life
  • Italian or English make your profile even more interesting.
  • When you contact clients, advisors, service providers and reinsurers, you can convince by your tact and clear communication.
  • Cherry on the cake: you know automated Underwriting systems as well as MSG Life Factory and like to participate in projects, training and process improvement
  • Work flexibly: Adapts your work every year to your lifestyle, with opportunities for part time and other flexible models such as telework, to name but one.
  • Enthusiasm your purse: Benefits such as bonuses, Reka cheques, preferential health insurance premiums, etc., which can quickly reach several thousand francs per year.
  • Choose your holiday model: Five or six weeks of vacation? You choose. You work 41 or 42 hours a week, respectively.
  • Keep training: We want you to continue training internally or externally and generously support you with time and money.
  • Keep the form: ergonomic work places, promotion of psychic well-being and many other offers related to health.
  • Engage in social matters: Is life good for you? So make others enjoy it and broaden your horizons, alone or as a team.
