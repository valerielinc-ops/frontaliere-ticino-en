Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future of process automation. Whether developing new technologies, implementing innovative solutions or working with different global industries, our work is based on trusting relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and create a sustainable future for our employees, our customers and society.

We are looking for a system architect* in Electrical & Process Engineering (m/f/d) to complement our team and to future-proof the technical architecture of our plants and equipment. At Endress+Hauser Flowtec AG in Reinach, we develop and produce innovative flow measurement technology for applications in a wide range of industries.

What is this point about?

Are you enthusiastic about seeing complex plants as a coherent overall system? Do you want to convert electrical, process and automation requirements into robust solutions? Do you enjoy shaping technical standards and bringing together different specialist areas?

In this role, you develop holistic system architectures for plants and equipment and accompany important technical concept decisions. As part of our technical organization, you will work closely with specialists from mechanics, software, automation and engineering as well as with internal stakeholders from development and investment projects.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • Responsible for the electrical and signaling system architecture of plants and equipment
  • Develop, compare and technically evaluate plant and automation concepts
  • design complex plants at the overall plant level, taking into account process, functional and safety requirements, system and process engineering
  • Support development and investment projects in technical concept decisions and provide expert advice to internal stakeholders
  • Defining architectural standards, technical interfaces and mandatory guardrails
  • Ensure modularization, standardization and reuse of technical solutions together with mechanical, software, automation and engineering teams

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have completed engineering studies or comparable training in electrical engineering, automation engineering, process engineering or system engineering
  • bring several years of industrial experience in the development, planning or design of complex plants and technical systems
  • have in-depth knowledge in electrical system architecture as well as in fluid and process engineering and media management
  • are familiar with control and automation technology and the technical interfaces between the technical areas
  • have experience in the system engineering of industrial processes and in the creation of holistic plant concepts
  • Capture relationships across mechanics, electrics, software and processes and translate these into robust overall solutions with a structured, independent and communicative working method

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support a balanced work-life balance and offer flexible working conditions and mobile working where the task allows.
  • On Campus Reinach, we offer a staff restaurant with high-quality and discounted food options.
  • We support sustainable travel with a share of the costs of public transport and offer free parking on site.
  • We promote health and family with sports and relaxation offers, health promotions as well as support offers for child care and personal life situations.
