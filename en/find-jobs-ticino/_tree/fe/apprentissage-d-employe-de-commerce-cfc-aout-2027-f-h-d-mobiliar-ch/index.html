Full description

What awaits

During your commercial training at La Mobilière, you will be part of different teams and will take on interesting tasks from day one, in which you will be able to develop constantly. You will follow a varied training program and can actively shape your future. "To do an apprenticeship at home, you don't need to know everything. But you must be curious and thirst to learn! »

What you can do with us

During your apprenticeship, you will discover, through practice, the different facets of the private insurance sector.

By working successively in several services, you will have the opportunity to get an overview of the various areas of insurance and gradually discover the tasks of a daily insurance company.

Business training provides a solid basis for starting a career in the insurance profession. In addition, for motivated apprentices who provide excellent services, we offer the possibility to spend a year abroad or to benefit from a language stay with our headquarters in Bern either during or after the end of the apprenticeship.

What you bring

  • Interest in insurance
  • Good school performance (secondary level)
  • Enthusiastic, curious and motivated personality
  • Rigor in work, taste for performance and teamwork
  • Want to learn
  • Knowledge in German is a significant asset

Working with us

We offer strong basic training in the private insurance sector as well as a diversified training programme with individual supervision in a modern infrastructure.

During the 3 years of learning, you will be closely accompanied by a training manager and practical trainers. After learning, you will have multiple developmental opportunities at your disposal.

Your place of work is Nyon and your apprenticeship begins on 01.08.2027. During your training, we cover all tuition expenses, offer a free subscription for your smartphone and half-rate subscription, participate monthly in your health insurance premium and prepare you to pass your studies through different courses and exam simulations.

Learn more about SWC employee business learning at Mobilière.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
