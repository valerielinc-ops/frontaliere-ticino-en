QA Engineer

Madrid | working from home up to 40% | Reference 7968

Do you enjoy being the person who asks "are we sure?" before software reaches production? Join our RegTech team and help us build confidence in mission-critical SaaS solutions used by financial institutions for Transaction Cost Analysis, Best Execution and Trade Surveillance.

As AI-assisted development increases delivery speed, quality engineering becomes more strategic than ever. This is not a manual testing role. It is a technical engineering role focused on automated verification, regression prevention, release confidence and system correctness in a complex, data-heavy platform.

What You Will Do

  • Define and evolve the QA and verification strategy for our RegTech SaaS platforms.
  • Build automated test suites, regression checks, CI quality gates and release validation processes.
  • Design test scenarios for distributed, data-intensive systems, including edge cases and failure modes.
  • Work with developers to improve testability, observability and confidence in AI-assisted code changes.
  • Support performance, integration, data-quality and end-to-end validation across environments.
  • Help create a culture where quality is owned by everyone, but supported by strong tooling and expert guidance.

What You Bring

  • Strong technical background in software engineering, QA automation or reliability engineering.
  • Experience with automated testing frameworks, CI/CD pipelines and regression tooling.
  • Analytical mindset, attention to detail and curiosity for complex system behavior.
  • Ability to understand data flows, logs, APIs, databases and production-like environments.
  • Confidence to challenge assumptions constructively and help teams make better release decisions.
