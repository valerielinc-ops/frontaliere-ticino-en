Medical Assistant 80%

We are looking for an 80% medical assistant for a replacement from January to November 2027(CDD), with the possibility of extension in CDI.

What we offer

  • An atmosphere of benevolent work within a cohesive and dynamic team
  • A varied and stimulating position, combining administrative tasks and technical care
  • A pleasant working environment within a specialist gastroenterology centre

Your missions

  • Patient Reception, Telephone Call Management and Medical Secretariat
  • Reporting, medical records management and summons
  • Assistance with digestive endoscopy (gastroscopy and colonoscopy)
  • Preparation and administration of infusions of biological treatments
  • Participation in the sedation in Propofol, in collaboration with the doctor

Your profile

  • Holder of a medical assistant CFC
  • Organized, caring and staffed
  • Motivated to invest in a dynamic team

Only applications corresponding to the profile sought will be answered.
