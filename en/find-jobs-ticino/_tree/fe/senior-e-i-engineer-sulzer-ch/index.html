Purpose of the Job

As a Senior E&I Engineer, you will participate at the Sulzer projects execution at all execution stages (Conceptual design, Skid and Modular design, DD, Procurement / Construction / Commissioning support and et.) within discipline and responsible. Senior E&I Engineer reports to the Chief E&I Engineer and/or Engineering Manager.

主要任务与职责:

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Execute detail engineering of instrument design for process plant modular skids, including wiring line diagrams, instruments, panel and PLC control specifications.
  • Prepare technical specifications for inquiry, issuing requisition for instruments, related items and subcontracting work.
  • Preparation and design of Piping and Instrumentation Diagram, in conjunction with Process Engineers
  • Preparation of process plant operating control philosophy, including alarm and interlocking trip matrix in conjunction with Process Engineers
  • Develop SIS, SIF and SIL
  • Participate in HAZOP for process plant
  • Review and approve subcontractor engineering documents, including instrument calculation, selection and PLC programming subcontractor.
  • Supervision and support of subcontractors' activities
  • Technical support for costing of skid projects.
  • Support receiving inspection of project E&I items.
  • Technical support for workshop skid assembly.
  • Material selection within discipline
  • Value engineering within discipline
  • Interface management between disciplines
  • Participation in the development of design philosophy and internal standards within the discipline
  • Participation in the project meetings, 3D models review etc.
  • Contribute to development of the overall project schedule.
  • Tracking and managing changes within discipline
  • Participation in the project progress report development.

职位要求：

Qualifications

Education and Experience

  • Bachelor in Electrical/ Instrumentation Engineering
  • 10 years’ experience in the oil and gas industries.
  • Good knowledge of applicable industrial standards and best practices. (China & International)
  • Good knowledge of designing methods and economic analysis.
  • Good knowledge of skid and modular design
  • General knowledge of oil and gas plant construction.

Language

  • Fluent in Mandarin and English

Others

  • Ability to work under tight deadlines with multiple tasks.
  • Ability to work in multiple projects.
  • Ability to travel on business trips including lengthy business trips to site during construction and commissioning.
