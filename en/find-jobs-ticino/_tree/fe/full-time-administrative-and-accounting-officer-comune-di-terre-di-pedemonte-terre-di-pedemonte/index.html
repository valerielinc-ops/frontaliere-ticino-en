Salary conditions

Those provided for by the art. 38 ROD, cantonal salary class 4, minimum CHF 58'067.00 maximum CHF 90'237.00 per year over 13 months. In addition to the salary, those entitled will be paid allowances for their children. In the case of newly hired candidates, with little professional experience in activities of a similar level or scheduled for tasks that require an introduction period, the Municipality may establish, for a period of two years maximum, a reduction of 5% of the salary compared to that foreseen by the function (art. 41 ROD).

Entry into service and probationary period

To be agreed as soon as possible. The first year is considered probationary.

How to submit your application

The offers, in a sealed envelope with the external wording "Administrative/accounting official competition" and accompanied by the required documents, must reach the Municipal Chancellery, located in Piazza Don Gottardo Zurìni 2 in Tegna, by 11.30 am on Monday 9 November 2026. The Municipality will not take into consideration offers that reach the Chancellery after the date and time indicated, documents sent by email will not will be considered valid. We reserve the right to request additional documents or information from candidates during the examination of applications and/or at the time of hiring, as well as to call them to interviews or subject them to specific tests. Finally, the Municipality reserves the unquestionable right to cancel the competition. The municipal secretary is available for any further information (091/78560 05).

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
