What awaits you

Are you a committed and service-oriented personality that our brokers are happy to support? Then you have come to the right place.

This is what you do with us

You will independently create correspondence, create offers, record applications and coordinate between our specialist departments. In addition, you will be in direct telephone contact with our customers, answer their inquiries and ensure professional and service-oriented support.

What you bring with you

  • Completed training as an “insurance specialist with a federal certificate”
  • Several years of experience in the SME sector / business and personal insurance
  • High motivation to learn new things in a varied field of activity
  • Communicative and reliable way of working, team orientation, independence and a positive attitude

Work with us

Our customers and our employees are our focus. A motivated team awaits you at the Winterthur general agency and maintains an open and collegial exchange.

The start of the position takes place by appointment.
