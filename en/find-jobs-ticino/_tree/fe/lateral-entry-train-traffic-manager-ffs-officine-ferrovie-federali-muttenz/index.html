Lateral entry train traffic manager: in 100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Lateral entry train traffic manager: in Olten, by arrangement, 100% Apply now ## You can look forward to this. As a train traffic manager, you keep an eye on the route network assigned to you, direct and monitor the trains at your workplace with around eight screens. During normal operation, you monitor the traffic, control the processes and ensure that things run smoothly. In the event of disruptions, you take over operational management: you analyze the situation, initiate measures and resolve the disruption so that normality is restored as quickly as possible. This means you share responsibility for mobility in Switzerland. Without you the internet comes to a standstill. The job is system-relevant and immediately effective: you coordinate people and technology, make quick decisions and thus make a direct contribution to the safety and reliability of rail transport.
