Head of Partnerships - International Affairs (m/f/d) Apply now » Date: 18 Nov 2026

Place

Bern, CH

Company

SBB What we offer. As a Partner Manager, you will actively contribute to shaping the collaboration between FFS Infrastructure and our main infrastructure partners in France and Italy. Together we create conditions for efficient, reliable and attractive cross-border rail traffic. You will connect people, organizations and interests, promote joint initiatives and make a concrete contribution to the development of sustainable mobility in Europe. A role awaits you that offers a wide range of autonomy and initiative, the ability to achieve tangible and visible results, to operate within an international network and to further develop your skills in specialist management. What you can move.

  • As a Partner Manager, you will further develop bilateral partnerships with France (SNCF Réseau) and Italy (RFI), in line with the agreed objectives, and will identify, together with specialist sectors, opportunities for joint initiatives.
  • You will start, coordinate and help define joint projects and activities, promote dialogue and ensure effective and objective-oriented collaboration and communication.
  • You will develop solid and lasting relationships with partner organisations' interlocutors and decision-makers, promote high-level dialogue platforms, moderate joint exchange moments, and promote solutions in the presence of complex issues.
  • You will draw up periodic reports on the implementation of the activities agreed with the partners and, in case of deviations from the objectives, you will share with the specialist sectors responsible for the most appropriate measures.
  • Ideally, within the International Affairs and Technical Standards team, you will assume the National Contact Point alternate function for the TSI Telematics system task.

The one you bring with you.

  • You have a strong ability to develop and consolidate strategic partnerships and you can establish relationships of trust with interlocutors belonging to different interest groups, up to the levels of high management.
  • You have a strategic and systemic vision, you can recognize common interests and potential development and, thanks to your strong orientation to realization, you can translate them into concrete results.
  • You have an excellent presence, communicate convincingly and, thanks to your diplomatic skills, you can reconcile different interests even in complex or controversial situations.
  • You have a university degree at a professional university (SUP) or university in economics, international or engineering relationships, or you have equivalent tertiary level training.
  • Preferential title is the knowledge of the railway infrastructure sector and the experience of creating IT projects.
  • An excellent command of French and Italian (level C2) is required. You also have a knowledge of level C1 English and German at least B2 level, both oral and written.
