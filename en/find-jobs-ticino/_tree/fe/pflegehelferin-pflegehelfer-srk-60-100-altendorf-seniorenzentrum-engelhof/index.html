Full description

We are a medium-sized senior center on the upper lake of Zurich and offer 76 residents a contemporary and family home. In a protected department for people with dementia, 10 single rooms are available for individual care and care. In our day apartment, which is integrated in the Seniorenzentrum in the tract East, we offer a pleasant day stay in a supervised environment for a maximum of 6 people. To complement our team, we are looking for a general care department: Nursing assistant / nursing assistant SRK 60-100 %

Their tasks

  • You have the responsibility for a high-quality and individual care and care of our residents
  • They document care in electronic form
  • You are responsible for a dignified and professional treatment of our residents and their relatives

We wish

  • You have experience in long-term care
  • You enjoy working and treat your environment with respect and appreciation
  • You are committed and take responsibility for your work area
