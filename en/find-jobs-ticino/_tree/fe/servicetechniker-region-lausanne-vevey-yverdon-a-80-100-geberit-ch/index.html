MAIN TASKS

As a service technician, you will decisively shape the impression that Geberit customers will have. Your area of responsibility includes in particular the following activities:

  • Independent scheduling of orders and planning of day trips
  • Maintenance and repair of our products «Geberit AquaClean» toilet with integrated shower function
  • Professional support and advice to our customers

PROFILE

You bring a degree as a sanitary installer. In addition, the following points distinguish you:

  • Customer service experience is an advantage
  • Good manners and well-groomed and friendly behavior
  • Flexibility, resilience and teamwork
  • Very good knowledge of French in word and writing, knowledge of German is advantageous

If you are an independent and flexible person with a high service orientation and ideally live in one of the sought-after care areas " Lausanne-Vevey-Yverdon Region" or "Biel-Neuenburg-La Chaux de Fonds Region", then you may soon be our new team member.
