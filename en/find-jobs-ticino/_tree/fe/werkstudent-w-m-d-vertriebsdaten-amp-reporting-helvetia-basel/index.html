Full description

Customer Service / Service Center Pensum: 40-60% Place of work: Level of experience: Students FlexOffice: on site Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

What you do

  • You work in the clusters Data Foundation as well as Sales Analysis & Reporting. There you bring sales and customer data from the world of Helvetia and Baloise together to form a common data basis.
  • You develop and automate data preparations with SQL and R or Python on our data science platform, providing reliable data products for sales.
  • You check and monitor the data quality, analyze deviations and document tables and logics in a comprehensible manner.
  • You create and maintain reports and dashboards on sales, potential and goal achievement. This makes the sales performance measurable.
  • You support ad hoc analyses for general agencies, market regions and sales management.
  • You recognize potential for optimization and implement innovative ideas for automation and the use of AI tools.

What you bring

  • You will study Business Informatics, Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science or Economics with a quantitative focus in the Master and have at least 2 semesters ahead of you.
  • You have first experience with SQL and R or Python. Knowledge of shiny or git and dealing with AI coding agents such as Codex or Claude Code are a plus.
  • You think analytically, work carefully and structured and like to get to the bottom of data problems.
  • You are characterized by your own initiative, reliability and willingness to learn.
  • You communicate clearly and enjoy working in a team and with various internal stakeholders.
  • You have very good knowledge of German.
