Full description

As the leading employer in the canton, Valais Hospital employs 6,200 staff members. Each year, 41,500 patients are cared for, and nearly 652,700 outpatient visits are conducted. Valais Hospital is seeking, for the Valais Romand Hospital Center and its Psychiatry and Psychotherapy Unit, a Psychologist at 50-70% for mobile teams in adult psychiatry and psychotherapy in Central Valais ## Mission Under the direction and supervision of your managers, you will assume the following tasks:

  • Provide mobile care tailored to the needs of individuals suffering from mental disorders, promoting their autonomy and respecting their social and cultural characteristics
  • Conduct crisis interventions in the field, supporting teams involved in the care of complex patients
  • Ensure transition follow-ups, particularly hospital-to-outpatient, for as long as necessary to integrate the patient into a care network, promoting continuity of care and coordination among various partners
  • Assess and guide the patient within the socio-healthcare network in collaboration with family members and multidisciplinary teams ## Profile
  • Master of Science in Psychology awarded by a university or equivalent recognized qualification
  • Postgraduate training towards obtaining the federal specialization title in psychotherapy, either ongoing (with confirmed enrollment in the program) or completed
  • Minimum 1-2 years of clinical experience preferred
  • Strong knowledge of clinical psychopathology
  • Interest in psychotherapeutic work and adult psychiatry
  • Ability to collaborate within a multidisciplinary team
  • Team spirit and interest in institutional network work
  • Initiative and autonomy in work
  • Strong organizational skills and ability to manage demanding clinical situations
  • Valid driver’s license and access to a private vehicle Only applications matching the profile will be processed. ## Offer
  • A stimulating and diverse work environment with opportunities to apply your knowledge and experience
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Opportunities for continuous training and professional development Find all the benefits for our employees here.
