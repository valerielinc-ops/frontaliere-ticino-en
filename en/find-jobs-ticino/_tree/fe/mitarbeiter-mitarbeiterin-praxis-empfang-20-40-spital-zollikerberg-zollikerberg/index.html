Welcome to your new place of work.

The Kinder-Permanence of the Spital Zollikerberg offers you a job as an employee.

Employee / Practice Reception (20–40%)

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Cooperation in patient reception and support of the telephone exchange
  • Administrative recording and registration of patient data in SAP
  • Clarification and assumption of costs
  • Preparation and provision of necessary documentation and maintenance and updating of patient data
  • Independent coordination and planning of patient appointments
  • Responsibility of various administrative activities (reporting, ordering, reporting requirements, etc.)

Become part of the Department of Women's, Child and Obstetric Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. In our children - Permanence directly on the hospital grounds, with more than 11,000 visits per year, we look after children and young people both for acute health concerns and in the context of regular consultation hours. General wound care and care of fractures in interdisciplinary cooperation in the team surgery and the children's permanence.

This convinces us

  • Completed commercial training, ideally with professional experience in the health sector and experience in the reception area
  • Good English skills
  • Friendly, empathetic and open-minded personality with a strong service orientation
  • Enjoy working with children and their caregivers as well as the willingness to be actively involved in the cooperation and development of the team
  • Very good PC user knowledge and safe handling of digital work equipment
  • High flexibility, resilience and a quick comprehension, combined with an independent, careful and reliable working method
  • Readiness for flexible assignments from Monday to Sunday, including regular weekend services and holiday agencies (no night services)

About the Zollikerberg Hospital We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. With a public service mandate and 173 beds, the Zollikerberg Hospital contributes to the optimal medical care of the greater Zurich area. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
