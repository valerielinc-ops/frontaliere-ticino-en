Full description

Do you like to start your day early, do you love being outdoors, and do you enjoy putting a smile on people's faces? Then an apprenticeship in delivery (specialising in distribution) is just the thing for you!

During the three-year basic training programme, you will acquire the tools you need to successfully enter the diverse world of logistics.

Your education.

  • Early in the morning, you sort your letters and parcels and load your delivery vehicle for the subsequent delivery round.
  • After that, you will be out and about independently, delivering and collecting all kinds of consignments and taking care of our customers’ concerns.
  • Before you start your well-deserved evening off, you do various follow-up tasks at your apprenticeship placement.
  • During your training, you will be supported by professionals, introduced to practical work and continuously developed.
  • In addition, with financial support from Swiss Post, you will either obtain a category A1 motorcycle driving licence or a category B motor vehicle driving licence and learn to drive a forklift.
  • We will round off your broad logistics training with various internal placements, a placement in a warehouse operation and individual support measures tailored to your wishes.

We require from you

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference. You should meet the following criteria:

  • You enjoy physical activity and working outdoors.
  • In addition, you enjoy interacting with people and are reliable, honest and discreet.
  • You are ready to obtain a provisional driving licence for category A1 or B by the start of the apprenticeship.
  • You have completed compulsory schooling.
  • When you start your apprenticeship, you will have a B2 level of German.
