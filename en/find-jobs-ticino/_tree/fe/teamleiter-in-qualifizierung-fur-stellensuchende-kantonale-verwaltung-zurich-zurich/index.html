Full description

Canton of Zurich Economic Directorate Office for Labour Team leader/in qualification for job seekers 80 - 100% Which courses really bring job seekers further? How do we design accompanying measures that are on the pulse of the labour market? What do people who are looking for a return to work need? In the area of qualification for job seekers (QuS), we actively address these questions. Together with external partners, we develop and coordinate a tailor-made range of measures so that job seekers can make their way into the world of work stronger. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you as a dedicated leader to lead one of our three product teams. Their main tasks include:

  • Personnel and professional management of a team of 5 employees
  • Active participation in the strategic development of the area as a member of the QuS management team
  • Professional support and support of employees in the design, further development and control of the offer of labour market measures
  • Steering and overall responsibility for the team’s budget and resources
  • Overarching responsibility for tendering and procurement procedures
  • Managing projects and maintaining and developing cooperation with internal and external stakeholders For this function, bring the following profile:
  • University degree in a relevant discipline
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in a comparable function or environment, e.g. education, work integration, public administration, non-profit, etc.
  • Designated conceptual and business thinking and working methods
  • Interest in labour market policy issues
  • Experience in Project Management or Project Cycle Management
  • Public procurement experience is beneficial
  • Negotiation strength and strong communication skills
  • Integrative, resilient and solution-oriented personality
  • What awaits you: You can expect a versatile leadership function with a great deal of creative leeway. As a member of the QuS management team, you actively shape the strategic orientation and further development of the area and make a significant contribution to an effective qualification of job seekers. The Canton of Zurich as an employer has a lot to offer. Flexible working hours, contributions to the food costs and home office are a matter of course for us. Does this exciting challenge appeal to you and are you a leader with a collaborative mindset? Nicolas Schulthess, 043 257 64 05, is happy to provide you with further information. We look forward to your online application. You can find out more about us at www.zh.ch/afa.

Pensum: 80-100 %
