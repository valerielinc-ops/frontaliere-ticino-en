Career change as a shunting specialist 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Career change as a shunting specialist Rotkreuz, by agreement, 60-100% Apply now

Here's what you can look forward to.

At SBB Cargo, you are at the heart of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 170.000 tonnes every day, thereby taking 15.000 truck journeys off the roads per day and reducing CO2 emissions by 460.000 tonnes annually. An innovative working environment awaits you, where you will help drive SBB Cargo forward alongside your team members. You will also receive varied, paid training at the marshalling yard. In addition, various further training opportunities are available to you, such as training to become a shunting locomotive driver. Attractive working conditions are important to us, including when working irregular hours. Night and weekend work is additionally compensated - for example, at 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night shifts, you also receive time supplements of between 10 and 30%. This means you have more free time than employees without night and weekend shifts.

Possible start dates

  • 1 March 2027
  • 1 May 2027

Interested in finding out more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22. until 24. To welcome you in October 2026 at our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Main Station. You can find all information at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe . How you can make a difference.

  • As a shunting specialist, you help ensure that our customers receive reliable and punctual service by preparing freight trains for their journeys.
  • You take over the wagons, carry out safety checks and prepare the train for its next journey through complex shunting movements.
  • You record the necessary data for train operations using modern personal IT devices (e.g. a tablet).
  • By placing and collecting the wagons at customers' premises on time, you have direct contact with customers every day. You receive their feedback and, when necessary, forward it to the responsible department.

Find out more about the job profile here. What you bring.

  • Your attentiveness, concentration and physical stamina set you apart, enabling you to successfully perform this demanding role in a 24-hour shift operation. You also enjoy working outdoors in all weather conditions.
  • Since all instructions and documentation are electronic, you will quickly become comfortable using digital tools.
  • You have successfully completed initial vocational training (at least an EFZ or equivalent qualification) and ideally have professional experience in a skilled trade, logistics or a comparable field.
  • You have a good command of spoken and written German (at least B2).
  • Normal colour vision (no red-green colour blindness) is a prerequisite for this role, as is a clean criminal and debt enforcement record.
