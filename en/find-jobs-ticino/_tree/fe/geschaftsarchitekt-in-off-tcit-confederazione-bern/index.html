Put to the Point

SECO’s Labour Directorate is the Federal Government’s competence centre for labour market policy and unemployment insurance. As part of the insourcing strategy, the computer science department is in an important development phase. Structures, roles and forms of cooperation will be reoriented to create an efficient and forward-looking organization. As a business architect, you play a key role in the development and management of sustainable business architectures in the field of public employment and unemployment insurance (RAV & ALK). They actively shape the development of our newly emerging Enterprise Architecture Management and create the connection between strategic goals, professional skills, organization and IT.

  • Entry date: by immediate or by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20991

You can make this contribution

  • Building the business architecture together with the EA team and the necessary structures, methods and skills.
  • Development of professional target images, capability maps, value streams and other architectural artifacts for orientation for business and IT.
  • Translating strategic objectives and professional requirements into comprehensible business architectures and transformation paths.
  • Analysis of existing structures and capabilities, identification of fields of action and identification of concrete development opportunities.
  • Advising executives, departments, product managers and IT on strategic architecture and transformation issues.
  • Ensuring the understanding, acceptance and anchoring of architecture in the organization by conveying the added value of business and enterprise architecture.

This makes them unique.

  • Several years of experience in business architecture, enterprise architecture or a comparable strategic function in insurance, social security or a comparable environment.
  • In-depth knowledge in the development of business architectures, capability models, target images and transformation architectures as well as experience in the construction and sustainable establishment of new structures, methods and organizational skills.
  • Pronounced ability to move securely at the interface between business and IT and to communicate complex connections in an addressable, comprehensible and convincing manner.
  • Empathy and empathy, coupled with assertiveness, creative will and the ability to attract people to new approaches and goals.
  • Higher education in business informatics, business administration or a comparable field, as well as strategic and analytical thinking skills with strong decision-making competence.
  • Good active knowledge of two official languages in words and writing as well as English language skills for communicating complex technical issues.

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
