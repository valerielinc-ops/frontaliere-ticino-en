HR Manager / Payroll (BF) M/F

Company description

On behalf of our client, a company based in the region of Sion, active in a dynamic and demanding business environment, we are looking for a Responsible HR / Payroll to strengthen its team.

As part of this recruitment, you will join a structure that provides a professional, collaborative and varied working environment, in which HR skills and payroll process management are central.

Description of post

As Responsible HR / Payroll Specialist, you are responsible for the administrative management of human resources and the full processing of payroll.

Your main responsibilities will include

  • Managing the entire life cycle of employees, from entry to exit
  • Ensure administrative management of personnel and monitoring of HR files
  • Preparing and processing wages independently
  • Managing social insurance, allowances, absences and other pay-related items
  • Ensure compliance with existing legal provisions and regulations
  • Be the co-ordinator for all salary and HR issues
  • Monitor and update data in the various HR tools
  • Working with the various internal and external interlocutors
  • Participate in improving and optimizing HR and payroll processes
  • Provide support and advice in different HR themes

Profile sought

  • Federal Human Resources Patent
  • Experience confirmed in a similar function, with a solid Swiss payroll experience
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss legislation on wages and social insurance
  • Good control of HR business processes
  • Good knowledge of English, both oral and written
  • Mastery of common computer tools
  • Organizational sense, rigour and excellent priority management
  • Autonomy, discretion and sense of responsibility
  • Relationship and excellent communication
