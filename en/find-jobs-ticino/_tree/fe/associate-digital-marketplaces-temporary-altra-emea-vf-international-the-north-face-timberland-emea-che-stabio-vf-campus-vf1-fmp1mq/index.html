Altra Running was founded in 2009 with the purpose of unleashing human potential by inspiring the world to move naturally. We want everyone who is capable of running to experience the thrill of finishing their first race, setting a new personal record and shattering limits they never thought they could overcome.

Altra Running is looking for an exceptional Associate, Digital Marketplaces (Temporary) to join our eCommerce team based in Stabio, Switzerland.

Let’s Talk about the Role

As Associate, Digital Marketplaces for our Altra team, you will primarily support our Amazon business as it scales significantly next fiscal year.

You will independently manage day-to-day operational flows, solve issues and work directly with Amazon, ensuring alignment between brand strategy, commercial priorities, and customer needs. This role requires a more autonomous and structured contribution, with the ability to translate seasonal plans into impactful execution.

You will work closely with Sales, Product, and Brand teams, acting as a key operational point of contact to drive execution, monitor performance, and proactively recommend improvements that enhance brand visibility and commercial impact.

In addition, this temporary role will provide capacity for new Travel Retail initiatives currently in the development phase.

How You Will Make a Difference

You will make a difference by

  • Owning the day-to-day operational flows for Amazon, ensuring timely delivery, consistency, and alignment with seasonal brand priorities.
  • Monitoring performance and proactively identifying issues and opportunities, tracking execution, collecting market feedback and reporting on results to share learnings and recommendations for future plans.
  • Coordinating with internal teams to resolve operational, stock, content and commercial issues, ensuring clear communication, brand consistency, and effective follow-up across all touchpoints.
  • Working directly with Amazon and other external partners as a day-to-day contact, ensuring that agreed actions are followed through.
  • Supporting marketplace growth initiatives and commercial planning, contributing to seasonal planning discussions by bringing market insights, customer feedback, and executional learnings.
  • Supporting new Travel Retail and marketplace projects.
  • Helping improve processes as the channel grows and becomes more complex.
  • Bringing energy, ideas and a willingness to get involved wherever needed.

Skills for Success

  • A strong collaborator and confident communicator able to influence and coordinate different stakeholders across Sales, Marketing, Product, and external partners, and comfortable dealing with complex external partners.
  • Strong operational discipline and attention to detail , with strong project coordination skills and the ability to manage multiple workstreams, deadlines, and priorities with limited supervision.
  • Hungry, hands-on and proactive ownership mindset , relentless in following issues through, with the ability to anticipate needs, solve problems, and recommend practical improvements.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office (Excel, PowerPoint, Word), with the ability to prepare clear presentations, trackers, and reporting files.
  • At least 1 year of relevant experience, preferably in the eCommerce or marketplace field ; experience within the sports, outdoor, footwear, or lifestyle industry is a plus.
  • Fluent in English ; other European languages are an advantage.
  • Comfortable using data, feedback, and market insights to support decision-making and improve execution quality.
  • Strong cultural fit with Altra .

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
