The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their businesses. Together with the Volg, TopShop and AGROLA brands, the LANDI cooperatives also supply the population in rural areas of Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy. LANDI Aarau-West AG operates successfully in the Aarau, Olten, Suhren, Wynen and Ruedertal region. At the Kölliken location, we are looking for a sales employee for the LANDI store, starting immediately or by agreement.
