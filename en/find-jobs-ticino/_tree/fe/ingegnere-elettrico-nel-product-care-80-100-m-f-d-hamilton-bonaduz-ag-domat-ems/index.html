Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Process Analytics business unit develops, produces and distributes state-of-the-art process measurement technology for customers in biopharmaceuticals, chemicals and breweries. Our product portfolio includes sensors for critical process parameters such as pH, oxygen or sensors for measuring cell density, which are extremely important in biological processes and are unique worldwide with their innovative technology.

The Process Analytics business unit develops, produces and distributes state-of-the-art process measurement technology for customers in biopharmaceuticals, chemicals and breweries. Our product portfolio includes sensors for critical process parameters such as pH, oxygen or sensors for measuring cell density, which are extremely important in biological processes and are unique worldwide with their innovative technology. As an Electrical Engineer, you are part of our product maintenance team within the Product Development Process Analytics department responsible for the technical support of our digital sensors.

Your main tasks include

  • Troubleshooting and product analyses from production and products on the market
  • Development of improvements
  • Cross-divisional cooperation with: production, purchasing, quality, etc.
  • Installation, commissioning and testing
  • Documentation
  • Programming test sequences and performing automated tests (Ni test stand)

This is what you bring

  • Studies in Electronics/Electrical Engineering (TS/HF, FH)
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working methods and analytical thinking skills
  • Experience programming firmware (ARM Cortex M3/M4 beneficial)
  • Experience in circuit board design (advantageous with CAD Mentor Expedition)
  • Communicative, creative and team capable personality
  • Technically multidisciplinary, hands-on oriented
  • Several years of experience in industrial product development are advantageous
  • Good knowledge of German (English knowledge of advantage)
