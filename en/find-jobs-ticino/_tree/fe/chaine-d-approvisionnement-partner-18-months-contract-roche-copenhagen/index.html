The Position As a Supply Chain Partner at Roche Diagnostics, you will join our dynamic Supply Chain team, a key pillar within the Finance and Operations Chapter . In this role, you will report directly to the Supply Chain Team Lead.

You will be part of a high-performing, collaborative team of five dedicated colleagues who take immense pride in delivering world-class customer care. We operate in a modern, agile environment, leveraging cutting-edge digital solutions and a steadfast commitment to sustainability to support our internal and external partners. Our team moves seamlessly between local execution and global collaboration, maintaining a particularly close and strategic partnership with our Nordic counterparts.

The Opportunity

  • Master order-to-cash execution within a complex SAP landscape, maximizing the adoption and quality of our digital ordering portfolio (Webshop, eOrder, and Inventory Ordering).
  • Drive improvement initiatives and foster cross-functional collaboration , taking full accountability for end-to-end delivery.
  • Deliver exceptional supply chain support by swiftly fulfilling customer needs.
  • Optimize inventory management by rigorously applying FIFO (First-In, First-Out) principles to minimize waste and ensure reliable product supply.
  • Maintain and govern critical customer and material master data to ensure system accuracy.
  • Synthesize data to prepare high-impact reports and actionable insights for both internal and external stakeholders.
  • Proactively monitor system dataflows incl. IDOC handling , troubleshooting anomalies to maintain operational continuity.
  • Orchestrate logistics and transportation frameworks.

Who You Are

  • SAP Specialist : You bring solid experience with SAP—ideally SAP S/4HANA —coupled with a deep, functional understanding of its ecosystem.
  • Industry Insider : You possess a proven track record in supply chain or logistics, preferably within the highly regulated pharmaceutical or diagnostics sectors.
  • Tech-Savvy Analyst : You back up your logistics expertise with robust IT literacy, combining advanced Excel proficiency with a natural talent for data-driven, analytical thinking.
  • Bilingual Communicator : You possess exceptional written and verbal communication skills in both Danish and English , allowing you to collaborate seamlessly with diverse stakeholders.
  • Proactive Driver : You are a self-starter by nature—characterized by a resilient, goal-oriented mindset and a passion for moving projects forward.
