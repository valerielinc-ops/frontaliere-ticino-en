Your focus: Creating a digital batch documentation (Digital Batch Doku)

We are the experts in the manufacture and testing of electrochemical electrodes and thick-film sensor cartridges for our blood gas analyzers and support our point-of-care customers in monitoring and treating patients in an intensive care environment.

The possibility As an intern (m/f/d), you are significantly involved in the digitization and optimization of our production documentation:

  • Analysis and conception: Analysis of the existing paper-based batch documentation processes and development of concepts for their digital mapping (Digital Batch Record).
  • Prototyping and Implementation: Participation in the design and implementation of a digital batch documentation system.
  • Process optimization: Identification of potential improvements (CIP/LEAN) in the documentation process through digitalization.
  • Collaboration: Close coordination with production, quality assurance (QA) and IT to ensure GMP compliance and usability of the digital solution.
  • Documentation & training: Preparation of training documents and implementation of training for production staff to use the new system.
  • Support: support for data migration and the maintenance of digital documentation standards.

Who you are

You are matriculated or have Your bachelor’s/master’s degree at a university or college, preferably in the field of business administration, business engineering, business informatics, production engineering (or process engineering) or applied computer science within the last 12 months and are interested in applying your knowledge as part of an internship in a modern working environment.

As an ideal candidate, you bring the following

  • Technical understanding: affinity to digital systems, databases and ideally first contact points with MES, LIMS or ERP systems in the production environment.
  • Process knowledge: basic understanding of production processes and ideally initial experience with LEAN/CIP methods.
  • Quality awareness: High quality and responsibility awareness, especially with regard to regulatory requirements (e.g. GMP/GAMP).
  • Analytical and structured working method: You distinguish yourself through your independent, structured and reliable working method.
  • Communication & teamwork: Very communicative personality with pleasure in working with different departments
