Full description

Class and annual salary (including 13th monthly)

8 78'611.-- / 124'677.--

Tasks

  • is responsible, together with other members of the team, of the educational-therapeutic intervention of the cases entrusted to him/her, operating mainly in an individualized relationship and/or small group
  • maintains contacts with families according to the modalities established in the global program
  • search for situations useful to implement the external socialization program, in groups of activities or in external classes to facilitate the insertion of children who are entrusted to them
  • accompanies the child/a and the family at the time of dismissal by carrying out a post-care intervention where it is considered useful, or by cooperating with the eventual passage to the Medical-psychological Service (SMP) or to other situations
  • participates in the decisions on new admissions and cooperates, after a first period of observation to a synthesis of entry, to the elaboration of the therapeutic plan of intervention
  • on cases of which it prepares periodic synthesis to be discussed in team
  • according to the needs of the EPC, performs tasks related to its specific training of psychologist/s
  • guarantees the correct compilation of performance and the management of the billing process

Requirements

  • complete academic title (bachelor and master or license or diploma) in psychology obtained at a Swiss university or, if obtained from foreign university, federal recognition of diploma issued by the Psychological Professions Commission PsiCo (according to article 2 and 3 of the Federal Law on Psychological Professions (LPPsi)))
  • authorisation in Canton Ticino for the exercise of the profession of psychologist/activist in the health sector
  • authorisation as a psychotherapist
  • Postgraduate internship in institutional child psychiatric services and experience in team at equivalent degree of specialization in the pedopsichiatric treatment of severe childhood pathologies
  • qualified experience in team with equivalent degree of specialization in the pedopsichiatric treatment of serious childhood diseases
  • knowledge and familiarity in the use of the main projective and level tests
  • availability and motivation for multidisciplinary teamwork
  • knowledge of the territory, institutions and the social network of the Canton Ticino
  • excellent knowledge (conversation and editing) of the Italian language and knowledge (conversation and editing) of another official language
  • license to conduct category B

Special remarks

  • use: 1 place to 80%
  • Workplace: Lugano
  • availability by the candidate/to work on irregular shifts
  • start date: to be defined
  • to the candidates not yet in possession of the authorisation in the Canton Ticino for the exercise of the profession of psychotherapist are granted 4 years by the decision in charge for obtaining the same. During this period the recruitment will take place in the form of the assignment (art. 15 and 16 GROSS) which can be switched to the appointment of the authorisation in Canton Ticino for the exercise of the profession of psychotherapist.
