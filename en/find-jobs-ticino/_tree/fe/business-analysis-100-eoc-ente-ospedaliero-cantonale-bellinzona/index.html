IT Business Analyst - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite Ticino hospital is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our Applications Services team within the Digital Evolution and Technology Area of the Directorate-General we look for an IT Business Analyst (100%)

  • Profile: The person we are looking for is specialized in the analysis, mapping and improvement of business processes in the healthcare sector. It has the task of understanding existing, clinical and administrative processes, to identify inefficiencies, redundancies and optimization opportunities, translating them into effective and sustainable IT solutions. It analyzes and manages end-to-end processes: it collects requirements, facilitates workshops, maps and optimizes processes in collaboration with the development team, translates them into low-code tools and takes care of technical implementation, testing, user training and Change Management, gathering feedback for continuous improvement. It actively collaborates with the various Teams present within the Area, promoting coordination between the different functions involved and contributing to the continuous improvement of processes and digital solutions. The sought figure stands out for a strong analytical attitude, strong process modelling skills, and proven experience in the analysis and optimization of business processes. Excellent relational and communication skills, proactivity, orientation to the result and ability to facilitate organizational change in complex and multidisciplinary contexts. Main tasks: Map AS-IS processes by detecting, documenting and modeling existing business processes (clinical and administrative) using standard notations (BPMN). Identify actors, responsibilities, input/outputs and decision points. Analyzes mapped processes to identify bottlenecks, redundancies, low-value added activities and operational risks, supporting data and metrics evaluations. Design TO-BE processes by defining and formalizing target processes optimized, in collaboration with stakeholders and technical teams, ensuring consistency with strategic objectives and technological feasibility.
