Full description

In the idyllic landscape of Glarnerland is our renowned institution, the Braunwald Rehaklinik. We focus on the treatment of people with psychiatric diseases. Our team offers a versatile, interdisciplinary therapy based on a multimodal and integrative treatment approach.

Your tasks

  • Care: Caring for our inpatient patients in cooperation with the other responsible doctors
  • Rehabilitation: Planning of the rehabilitation steps in an interdisciplinary team with nurses and therapists
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Through close cooperation with various disciplines, you contribute significantly to holistic treatment and rehabilitation
  • Contact person: Responsible contact person for patients and their relatives with medical questions during their inpatient stay

Your profile

  • Qualifications: Federal or MEBEKO-recognized state examination, of advantage already experience in the field of psychiatry
  • Experience: You should already mind. 1 year of experience in psychiatry / psychotherapy
  • Personality: You bring professional and social competence for a good interdisciplinary cooperation and work in a structured and independent manner

What we offer

  • Mobility grant: Financial support for travelling by Braunwaldbahn
  • Recognition of further training: possibility of recognising 1–2 years of medical assistantship in the field of psychiatry
  • Working environment: Working in an idyllic, car-free recreation area in an appreciative and motivated team
  • Culinary offer: daily three freshly cooked menus at special prices and once a month a breakfast offered by the group of companies
  • Wellness: Free use of medical-therapeutic training and twice a week of the bathing area
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Pension fund contributions: above-average participation and management costs with attractive pension options
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies as well as our membership at Benefit@work and free admission and other discounts in the wellness spa FORTYSEVEN

Contact person Vasileios andriopoulos Chief physician Rehaklinik Braunwald Email: vasileios.andriopoulos@zurzachcare.ch
