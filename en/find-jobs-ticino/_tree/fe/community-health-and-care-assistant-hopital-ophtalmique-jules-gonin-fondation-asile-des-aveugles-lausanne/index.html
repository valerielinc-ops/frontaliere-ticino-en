Full description

Founded in Lausanne in 1843, the Asylum Foundation of the Blind includes the Jules-Gonin Ophthalmic Hospital, a university department of ophthalmology, a research centre, the Centre pédagogique pour écoles handicapées de la vue (CPHV), the social service, rehabilitation and low vision and the medical-social establishment Clair-Soleil.

More than 600 employees work every day in the service of visual health to provide diagnostic, treatment and support services to people with vision.

To complete our team at the hospital unit, we are looking for a

Community Health and Care Assistant

Your Mission

  • Ensuring quality reception and care for our patients
  • Working in pairs with HES nurses
  • Dispensing medico-technical acts
  • Contribute to the organisation of work and the smooth running of the Unit's activities

Your assets

  • SWC Community Health and Care Assistant
  • A first successful experience in a hospital environment, an asset
  • Sense of welcome and human contact
  • Ability to work as a team and independently
  • Versatility, great flexibility, intergent
  • Mastery of the French language, another language, an asset

We offer you

An enriching activity within a dynamic environment where you can showcase your talents, specific training for our mission, salary conditions related to your skills. Activity rate: 100% Nature of contract: CDI Entry into office: to be agreed Workplace: Jules Gonin Ophthalmic Hospital, Lausanne
