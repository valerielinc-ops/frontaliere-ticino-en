Head of department: in TK Hybrid (Dipl. nurse HF/FH), 90–100 %

Location: Basel

From 01.01.2027 or by agreement

Founded in 1900, the Klinik Sonnenhalde in Riehen is now an established private clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland and southern Baden. We pursue a holistic, human-centered, milieutherapeutic psychiatry that combines excellent professional quality with a Christian value base. The clinic has 68 stationary beds, 4 day clinics and 4 ambulatories. As one of the first clinics in Switzerland, we are actively promoting digital therapy offerings, such as blended therapy and virtual reality and opened the first hybrid day clinic in Switzerland in February 2023.

Take on a responsible leadership role in the psychiatric environment, actively shape the further development of the team and contribute your professional and personal skills to conceptual and innovative projects.

Your responsibilities

  • You have the leadership responsibility for an interdisciplinary team that can consist of assistant doctors:innen, psychologist:innen, nurses, social workers:innen and specialist therapists:innen
  • They promote employees through target agreements and treat them prudently, appreciatively and act result- and solution-oriented. In the professional management of professional groups that do not correspond to their own, you will be supported by experts:
  • You are responsible for occupying the day clinic, implementing the strategic objectives of the clinic and further developing the therapeutic offer with a focus on blended care and virtual reality (VR).
  • Together with their team, they implement the defined quality goals in patient-centered treatment and work with them in the therapies.

Your profile

  • You are a nurse HF or FH with leadership experience and would like to develop further in the field of leadership
  • You have professional experience in psychiatric setting and experience leading groups
  • You are a dynamic, innovative and communicative personality with a strong sense of self-reflection and responsibility.
  • They are interested in conceptual and project-related work and identify with the Christian values and the holistic approach of the clinic.
