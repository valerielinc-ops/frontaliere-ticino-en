Airbone Software Lead \-

Engineering \& Training Systems

Primary work location

All workplaces

Fly with us into the future and become part of the team at one of the most innovative aircraft manufacturers in the world.

Your Tasks

  • Establish and grow the Airborne Software team at our office, fostering a collaborative and high\-performing engineering culture
  • Act as the primary software focal point in and ensure close alignment with the Airborne Software organization at headquarters in Stans
  • Plan, coordinate, and lead software development and verification activities throughout the software lifecycle, including definition of tasks and mentoring of software engineers
  • Ensure compliance with aviation certification standards, development processes, quality requirements, budgets, and schedules
  • Drive collaboration with internal stakeholders, such as Systems Engineering and Program Management, as well as external partners and suppliers
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with certification authorities, vendors, and cross\-functional teams to support successful project delivery

With this you convince us

  • Degree in Engineering (Bachelor's level or higher) and extensive experience in software development within a regulated environment
  • Demonstrated success in leading multicultural and multidisciplinary engineering teams
  • Strong expertise in C programming, software architecture, software testing, and model\-based development (SCADE)
  • Proven experience applying DO\-178C and related standards (e.g., DO\-330, DO\-331\) in certified aerospace projects
  • Solid understanding of modern software development processes and tools, including Git, Jira, and DOORS
  • Fluent English (C1 or higher), Italian language knowledge highly desired

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
