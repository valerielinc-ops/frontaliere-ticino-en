Full description

Rapelli has been representing the Ticino tradition since 1929 and is recognized for high quality products made with selected ingredients, respecting tradition and with a constant attention to innovation. With the brands Rapelli and Ticinella, the company is ambassador of Ticino taste throughout Switzerland. Rapelli is part of the ORIOR Group, a Swiss company listed at SIX Swiss Exchange and specialized in the production and marketing of premium food specialities.

To strengthen our Retail Sales Department, we are looking for a solid, engaging and business-oriented business figure for the role of

Key Account Manager Retail (100%)

The position has the commercial responsibility of Retail customers assigned, with the aim of consolidating existing relationships, identifying new development opportunities and contributing to the growth of business and corporate profitability.

The person inserted, reporting to the Sales Manager, will be called to build value relationships with customers, understand their strategies and translate them into concrete growth opportunities for Rapelli, combining commercial vision, negotiating capabilities and results orientation.

Your main responsibilities

  • Key Account Management and Development: Take on the business responsibility of assigned customers, consolidating existing relationships and identifying new growth opportunities.
  • Strategic approach: building a qualified dialogue with Buyer, Category Manager and managers of retailer business strategies, including targets, priorities and category dynamics.
  • Business Development and Innovation: identify development opportunities, argue and present new concepts and product innovations, supporting their value through convincing arguments and economic analysis.
  • Commercial negotiation: to prepare and conduct, in the context of agreed mandates, even complex negotiations, including annual negotiations on prices, bonuses and contractual conditions, protecting corporate profitability and the quality of the relationship with the customer.
  • Responsibility for results: managing turnover, margins, budgets and business conditions of the assigned portfolio, monitoring performance and activating the necessary development initiatives.
  • Internal collaboration: working closely with Sales and Marketing and Category Management functions, Production, Supply Chain, Quality and Controlling to transform business opportunities into concrete projects and measurable results.

Your profile

  • Significant business experience in the management and development of Retail customers structured in the Swiss market, preferably in the food sector or FMCG, with ability to interact with decision-makers of different levels.
  • Spicy commercial sensitivity and ability to understand retailer strategies, class dynamics and business development opportunities.
  • Ability to build, submit and support innovative business proposals and projects, combining market vision, argumentation and results orientation.
  • Solid negotiating skills, also matured in the management of complex business conditions and negotiations.
  • Analytical capacity, mastery of major economic and commercial levers and attention to marginality.
  • Autonomy, initiative, relational authority and ability to collaborate effectively with different business functions.
  • Good knowledge of the main IT and management tools.
  • Excellent mastery of Italian and German, both written and spoken. Knowledge of English and French is a preferential element.
  • We appreciate curious people, ambitious in the positive sense of the term, able to question consolidated solutions and take on increasing responsibility.

We offer you

  • A major commercial role within a company with a strong identity, recognized brands and a consolidated presence in the Swiss market.
  • Autonomy and direct responsibility for results, with the possibility to actively contribute to the development of business.
  • A close collaboration with the Sales, Marketing and the main business functions.
  • A dynamic context in which competence, entrepreneurial spirit, ability to innovate and concrete results are enhanced.
  • Interesting prospects for professional growth.
  • Attractive employment conditions and a remuneration package tailored to experience and skills.

In Rapelli there is a modern and pleasant working environment, characterized by a friendly and respectful corporate culture. Our internal café offers a wide choice of fresh menus and coffee freshly prepared by the bartender. In addition, you will benefit from interesting discounts on the products of our assortment. Thanks to the Stabio railway station, located right next to the building, we are easily reachable; furthermore, we encourage car pooling to facilitate home-work travel. A dynamic environment with motivated teams awaits you, where collaboration and well-being are first.
