Office for Migration, Integration and Citizenship

The Office for Migration, Integration and Citizenship with its almost 60 employees is a department of the Security Directorate of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft. It enforces the rights of foreigners and asylum in the canton and is responsible for the naturalisation procedure at cantonal level. In the fulfilment of this task, the department examines the conditions of entry and residence of foreign persons and the naturalisation requirements in the civil rights procedure.
