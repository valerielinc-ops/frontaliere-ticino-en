Full description

Participate and support the activities of the Interreg project “Libervie, Libertà di muoversi: Vie culturali e letterarie transfrontaliere accessibili e inclusive” Collaborate on the management and development of the cultural heritage mapping project, updating the supporting documentation and drafting press releases and in-depth texts for media Review thematic maps and feed the cultural heritage databases Collaborate on the review of the website and the drafting of newsletters Support the organization of events, informative meetings, conferences and thematic training sessions Other activities may be assigned based on the interests of the intern ## Requirements

  • Academic education (bachelor + master), preferably in the humanities field
  • Knowledge of open platforms (Wikidata, OSM, etc.)
  • Excellent oral and written expression in Italian
  • Knowledge (spoken) of at least one official language and English
  • Interest in the cultural sector
  • Knowledge of the territory and its cultural heritage
  • Goal-oriented working method
  • Having experience in writing texts and/or managing geodata is a preferred title ## Special Observations
  • The internship (with a fixed-term auxiliary personnel contract) will last 6 months
  • Candidates who have completed their bachelor's and master's degrees more than a year ago cannot access the internship ## What We Offer
  • The Digital Cultural Heritage and Analysis Office (UAPCD) manages the Cultural Observatory of the Canton of Ticino (OC) and the System for the Valorization of Cultural Heritage (SVPC)
  • It is a multifunctional pole that promotes investigations and relationships in the cultural sector and develops projects to valorize cultural heritage ## Contact
  • Applications must be submitted online
  • In addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of online application (presentation letter and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and work attestations), further documentation may be requested in a second moment (judicial record with related self-certification and self-certification on health status)
  • Further information can be obtained from Mr. Roland Hochstrasser, Digital Cultural Heritage and Analysis Office (UAPCD), tel. 091 8143470, e-mail roland.hochstrasser@ti.ch ## Employment Conditions
  • Appointment or assignment: those provided for by the Law on the Organization of State Employees and Teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the related Regulation
  • Any modification of law concerning salary and organization is explicitly reserved
  • Working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment relationship), flexible or according to service needs ## Deadline 7 April 2026
