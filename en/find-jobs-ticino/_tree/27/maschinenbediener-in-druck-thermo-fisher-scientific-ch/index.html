Job description

Equipment Operator / Machine Operator:in print

The machine operator is responsible for the professional setup, operation and monitoring of our printing equipment for the production and/or printing of labels and booklets for clinical trials (flexo and inkjet technology with integrated camera systems).

Their tasks

  • Professional installation, operation and monitoring of our various printing systems
  • Ensuring on-time and quality production of products
  • Cleaning, maintenance and maintenance of machinery and systems
  • GMP-compliant documentation of the tasks performed
  • Active participation in process improvements
  • Working in a 3-layer model

Your profile:• Completed training as a flexographic printer, printing technologist or printer or at least 5 years of professional experience in the printing industry on in-line printing presses

  • Experience in the pharmaceutical or food industry is advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (English skills of advantage)
  • Experience in dealing with computers as well as basic knowledge in the common MS Office applications
  • Self-responsible and independent working methods, reliability and willingness to work in 3-shift operation
  • Initiative, flexibility and teamwork
  • Experience in handling machines
