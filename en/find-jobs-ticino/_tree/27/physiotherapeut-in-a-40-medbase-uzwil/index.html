Full description

Welcome to Medbase Uzwil

Our modern practice with our own X-rays and ultrasound is located in rural Uzwil, just a few steps from the train station. Around 20 employees from the disciplines of general internal medicine and physiotherapy look after our patients. After consultation, we also carry out home visits. At Medbase Uzwil we pay attention to a good work-life balance. Working part-time is possible and working hours can be arranged flexibly.

You can move that

As a physiotherapist at Medbase, you are at the heart of our origins, because Medbase began with the aim of promoting people's recovery and well-being through physiotherapeutic care. In a team that is characterized by commitment and collegial exchange, you can expand your professional skills and contribute to the continuous development of our quality standards.

  • Comprehensive, empathetic treatment and care for patients
  • Participation in the further development of quality standards
  • Participation in team meetings and training for professional development
  • Complete administrative tasks and documentation
  • Support sports clubs and associations if necessary
  • Participation in Medbase events and optionally carrying out performance diagnostic tests

What you bring with you

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist FH with SRK recognition
  • Further training in sports physiotherapy (SPT or ESP) would be an advantage
  • Team orientation and enjoyment of work
  • Good knowledge of German (at least B2 level)

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation.
