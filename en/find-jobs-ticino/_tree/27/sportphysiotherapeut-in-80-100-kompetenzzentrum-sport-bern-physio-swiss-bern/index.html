Sports Physiotherapist (80–100%) – Competence Center Sport Bern Help shape our competence centre Sport! We are looking for a trained sports physiotherapist. Bern, Bern Cooperation Permanent employment 80–100% Job: By appointment General physiotherapy Orthopaedics / Traumatology Sports medicine Musculoskeleton Contact details Employers Physiotherapy at the Bümpliz Centre Bümplizstrasse 124 3018 Bern Website Job title Create the competence center sport with us! You are a trained sports physiotherapist and would like to contribute your experience, realize your own ideas and build something together with us? Then this could be just the right challenge for you! This is what awaits you

  • Sports physiotherapeutic care of grassroots and competitive athletes
  • Sports Rehabilitation, Active Therapy and Return to Sport
  • Co-development of sports physiotherapeutic offers and concepts
  • Cooperation with sports clubs
  • Professional exchange with an experienced therapeutic director (MSc Physiotherapy, OMT)
  • Perspective of later assuming professional responsibility at the Competence Center Sport

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist with Swiss recognition
  • Completed further training in sports physiotherapy
  • Experience in Sports Rehabilitation
  • In-depth knowledge of clinical reasoning and active rehabilitation
  • Own initiative, sense of responsibility and pleasure in working together in a team

Your workplace – a health center with a heart In the middle of Bern-Bümpliz, our newly designed, interdisciplinary health centre with an area of around 900 m2 awaits you. Physiotherapy is at the heart of our center. Modern treatment rooms, spacious training areas, a separate gymnastics room and a versatile training and diagnostic infrastructure create ideal conditions for contemporary sports physiotherapy. Our equipment includes strength and training equipment, Dividat, SensoPro, InBody and functional diagnostics. Here you have space to implement your own ideas and actively develop sports physiotherapy. This is what we offer you

  • Attractive occupational provision (BVG)
  • 13. Monthly wages
  • Two weeks of paid training per year
  • One hour of paid administration time per working day
  • 15 minutes paid break per half day
  • Crediting of all public holidays of the Canton of Bern
  • Professional exchange and support by an experienced team
  • Modern infrastructure and diverse development opportunities
  • Possibility to co-create your own subject area

What is important to us We attach importance to well-founded diagnosis, comprehensible clinical reasoning, active rehabilitation and good cooperation. You don’t have to build everything alone – but you should enjoy developing something together. On approximately 900 m2, we combine modern physiotherapy, medical training, exercise and other health services under one roof. Spacious treatment rooms, an approximately 100 m2 training area, a separate gymnastics room and modern training and diagnostic equipment create optimal conditions for high-quality, active physiotherapy. Our therapeutic director has an MSc in Physiotherapy, an OMT qualification and many years of experience in clinical practice as well as training and supporting students. We attach great importance to well-founded clinical reasoning, professional exchange, self-responsible work and an appreciative team culture. Our aspiration: Professional quality, personal care and enjoyment of physiotherapy – in a centre that offers space for development.
