Full description

The activity includes the construction of complex tools and technical support of toolmaking. An innovative environment promotes self-responsible work on exciting production projects. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Designing punching, bending and follow-on tools and devices
  • Creating concepts, detailed drawings and parts lists
  • Projecting new tools and accompanying them until sampling
  • Optimize existing tools and technically support production and toolmaking

Requirements

  • Good 3D CAD knowledge, ideally with TopSolid
  • Experience in toolmaking, punching or forming is advantageous
  • Self-employed, exact and responsible work
