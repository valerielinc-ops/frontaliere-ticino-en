At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. The Position A healthier future. That’s what drives us. But we can’t do it alone. To succeed, we collaborate – with each other, with our partners, with governments, with patients.

We are seeking a highly motivated and organized individual to join our team as an Omnichannel Engagement Partner at Roche Pharma Turkey.

You will contribute to the development, deployment & adoption of the empowerment of healthcare professionals, improvements in stakeholders’ journeys, empowerment of healthcare systems & providers, stakeholder engagement, and medical content management projects. You will act as the owner of various projects, business & agility products, digital health solutions, and strategic initiatives.

Key Responsibilities

  • Contributing & executing cross-functional omnichannel customer engagement strategy and data initiatives by partnering with marketing & commercial members within the Disease Area Teams, connecting channels, and bridging between functions (marketing, sales, medical interface) to ensure a seamless, optimal customer engagement experience.
  • Developing & executing communication, enablement & adoption plans that support the establishment of an omnichannel approach through digital & data platforms/initiatives, including but not limited to digital customer engagement initiatives, online initiatives, social media channels, HCP portals, patient portals, and value-added solutions.
  • Running and providing omnichannel engagement performance monitoring and measurement reports (including both marketing and sales digital execution), executing and supporting outcome analysis, impact analysis, ROI analysis, and channel effectiveness analysis.
  • Ensuring and/or supporting the Disease Area team that the presentation of the company, brands, content, targeting, coverage, and communication is consistent across all channels and being accountable for results: executing specific, measurable, actionable, and time-related deployments with clear success and metrics. Managing assigned owned channels and supporting the management of other affiliate channels.
  • Developing, supporting, and executing training to enablers and PPoCs (Primary Point of Contacts) to identify, and optimally leverage, internal (cross-functionally and cross-brands) and external (competitors and other categories/industries) digital best practices to achieve optimal customer experience and positive behavioral change.
  • Improving capabilities within medical teams, disease area teams, finance teams, or other related teams regarding customer engagement channels & content lifecycle.
  • Directs the planning, execution, coordination, and support of digital platform features & operations. Works towards carrying out day-to-day business requirements of maintaining our digital platforms while working together with internal & external stakeholders.
  • Conducts and prepares feasibility studies or other research for new digital platforms, applications, or revisions to existing digital technologies. Develop new features/ projects and constantly endeavor to improve existing ones. Review the current status of digital platforms and applications and prepare recommendations for improvements.
  • Prepares project cost estimates, budgets, and schedules. Prepares and presents project proposals to management and user departments. Follow up the processes from A - Z such as planning, design, development, testing, launch, and promotion in coordination with related parties.
  • Establishes digital customer engagement solution standards
