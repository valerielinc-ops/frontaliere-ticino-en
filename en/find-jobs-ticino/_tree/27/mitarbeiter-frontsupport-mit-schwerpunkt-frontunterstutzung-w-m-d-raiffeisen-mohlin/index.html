Full description

12.11.2026 Möhlin

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Möhlin is a successful and locally anchored bank with headquarters in Möhlin and an office in Rheinfelden. Around 50 employees, including three apprentices, are committed to our customers every day. To strengthen our front support team, we are looking for a motivated, independent and service-oriented personality.

What awaits you?

  • You support our customer advisors in administrative and organizational tasks in day-to-day business.
  • You process various banking transactions independently and on time and ensure efficient and high-quality processing.
  • You take on tasks related to customer master data management (CLM) as well as the administration of accounts, cards and other banking products.
  • You work closely with internal specialist departments and contribute to smooth banking operations.
  • You are actively involved in the optimization and digitalization of our work processes.
  • If you are interested and appropriately suited, you have the opportunity to develop professionally in the direction of credit processing.

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking studies or several years of experience in banking.
  • Ideally, several years of experience in the administrative processing of banking transactions.
  • Good MS Office skills, Avaloq skills are beneficial.
  • Independent, structured and careful working method with high quality awareness.
  • Service-oriented, team-capable and reliable personality.
  • Openness to digital solutions, new technologies and the further development of processes.

You value a dynamic environment, work in partnership in a team and tackle challenges in a solution-oriented manner? Then look forward to a varied task in the diverse world of banking. Rolf Meyer Head of Front Support Why Raiffeisen?

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Aargau?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Aargau's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
