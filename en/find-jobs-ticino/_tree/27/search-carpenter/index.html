More about this search for cross-border applicants

Carpenter jobs in Ticino and Italian-speaking Switzerland include workshop production, interior fitting, timber construction, renovation, roofing support and on-site installation. The 93 listings aggregated for this search may suit qualified carpenters, cabinetmakers and candidates with practical experience in construction or furniture making. Employers commonly look for accurate measurements, technical drawings, safe tool use and the ability to work independently or in a team. Opportunities are concentrated around Lugano, Bellinzona, Locarno and Mendrisio, with some positions extending into nearby valleys and other Swiss regions. Italian is useful locally; German or French may be requested for projects outside Ticino.
