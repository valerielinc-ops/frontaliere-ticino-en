Company Description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals of Geneva are a benchmark establishment at the national and international level. To find out more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here. The Department of Oncology provides diagnostic and therapeutic care for adult patients suffering from cancerous diseases and provides support for patients and their loved ones. The objective is to ensure access to the best cancer treatment, to offer appropriate care and to anticipate the needs of hospitalized patients (marrow transplant, cell therapy, immunotherapy, etc.). Jointly, the Cancer Center allows coordination of transversal activities dedicated to the problem of cancer between services and departments. It brings together 4 departments (hematology, oncology, precision oncology and radiation oncology) and is made up of around 300 employees. The Hematology Department provides diagnostic and therapeutic care for patients suffering from benign or malignant blood diseases, whether hospitalized or outpatient. The department is involved in various clinical and translational research activities, in cooperation with the University of Geneva and also involved in different cooperative groups: SAKK, EBMT, GRAALL, SFGM-TC, HOVON, CML german study group, CLL german study group, etc. Several rotations are carried out during the semester, under the direct supervision of a clinical head and assistant physician. Rotations include hospitalization (intensive treatment, Allograft HSC, CAR-T & bispecific) as well as outpatient monitoring of allograft patients (consultations and HDJ). Rotations also include the lymphoma and myeloma unit as well as the myeloid pathologies and blood diseases unit, in outpatient clinics and hospitalizations. The program also provides for participation in integrative diagnosis in hematology, including cytology, flow cytometry and the possibility of contributing to intra-hospital consultation activity. Depending on availability, rotations may include immunohematology, transfusion medicine and apheresis, as well as hemostasis.

Job Description

You take care of patients treated by hematopoietic stem cell transplantation as well as hematological problems such as acute leukemia, lymphoma and other malignant and benign hematological diseases. You guarantee quality medical services, both clinically, administratively and organizationally. You contribute to the training of trainee doctors. In parallel with your activity, you have the opportunity to benefit from quality post-graduate training contributing to obtaining the title of specialist in hematology. As a reference center in French-speaking Switzerland for allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation as well as the coordination of two institutional boards for the care of patients with hematological diseases in the canton of Geneva, we offer you the opportunity to manage the entire spectrum of clinical hematology pathologies (malignant and benign). You are integrated into a dynamic team made up of 15 senior doctors and 7 intern doctors.

Qualifications

You hold a medical diploma or have training deemed equivalent by the Medical Professions Commission (MEBEKO). For non-French speakers, French level C1 in the spoken and written test is required. You have good knowledge of internal medicine. You demonstrate excellent communication skills in a multidisciplinary team and with patients. You are an autonomous, flexible and committed person. You have a curious, involved personality who enjoys teamwork
