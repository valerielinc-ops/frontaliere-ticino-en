Full description

MAIN TASKS

In our highly automated production of plastic components, you combine in-depth knowledge of plastics and injection molding with analytical and methodological competence. You will analyze complex technical issues around materials, processes, tools and machines and actively advance projects with internal or external partners. Your goal is to understand technical connections holistically and to develop efficient and sustainable solutions for our production. This includes in particular the following activities:

  • Interface management between process technology as well as tool and machine procurement and advance complex topics professionally
  • Assessment and further development of injection moulding tools with regard to design, function and process behavior
  • Systematic analysis of injection molding processes (by means of statistical experimental planning (DoE), technical evaluation of results and derivation of optimization measures)
  • Evaluation, design and further development of sensors as well as process monitoring and assistance systems for injection molding
  • Use of production and process data as well as further development of solutions for the analysis and visualization of data
  • Professional management and implementation of technical projects as well as their coordination with internal and external partners
  • Evaluation of innovative technologies and solutions, transfer to production and further development in terms of economy, quality and safety
  • Support for complex issues in the production environment as well as transfer of knowledge to specialist departments and production

PROFILE

You will bring a completed technical degree with a focus on plastics technology

  • Ideally, you have already completed an apprenticeship as a plastics technologist before your studies and therefore know injection molding from both a practical and an engineering perspective. Further, the following points distinguish you:
  • Several years of industrial experience in the plastics industry as well as leading technical projects
  • Analytical, practice-oriented working methods as well as the ability to solve complex challenges in the operational environment
  • Strong communication and enforcement skills
  • Self-responsible and structured working method with an eye for profitability, efficiency and sustainability
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

ADDITIONAL NOTES

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
