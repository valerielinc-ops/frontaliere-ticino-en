KNX system integrator 100% With around 50 employees, Robert Widmer AG is a leading provider of electrical installations, telematics and building technology in the Lucerne area. In order to strengthen our KNX department, we are looking for a committed and independent personality as a KNX system integrator.

Your tasks

  • independently program, commission and extend KNX systems
  • Execution of service and maintenance orders on existing KNX systems
  • Create documentation (e.g. function descriptions, plan corrections, schema comparison)
  • Cooperation in the development of technical concepts
  • Training and guidance of investment operators

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic electrical installer training
  • Experience in ETS programming and with visualization servers – or the willingness to acquire this know-how
  • Further education with focus on automation, computer science, system or building technology (advantageous)
  • High IT affinity and good knowledge in the common MS Office programs
  • Readiness for subject-specific training
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Open and communicative: You communicate clearly, transparently and tackle new challenges
  • Structured and solution-oriented: You work organized and find pragmatic solutions
  • Innovative: You are interested in new technologies and contribute your own ideas

You can look forward to this

  • An exciting, future-proof and digital working environment
  • Progressive 40-hour week
  • 27 holiday days per year (at 100% penalty)
  • High-quality work equipment and modern workwear
  • Flexibility thanks to annual working hours
  • Support and participation in training
