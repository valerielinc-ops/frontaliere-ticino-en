Full description

Who are we?

At a time when access to reliable and quality information is crucial, the BCU Fribourg offers a learning, discovery and memory space in the heart of the city and canton.

On-line and in its new building, it offers important documentary resources and provides access to scientific information. It also preserves cantonal cultural heritage and animates rich cultural programming.

Why join us?

We offer a dynamic environment, in the context of creating a new library in the city centre that provides for the development of modern services. BCU is a founding member of SLSP (national network) and is involved in the development of Swisscovery services.

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

You are actively involved in the logistics organization, monitoring and storage of the new cantonal and university library in Fribourg. Thanks to your posture and charisma, you contribute to compliance with the regulations for the use of this new place of life in the centre of Fribourg.

In collaboration with the Head of Infrastructure and the Concierges of the Buildings Service, you perform important tasks to maintain a pleasant and adequate working environment for the cantonal and university public.

In particular

  • Monitoring of public spaces and law enforcement
  • Organization and layout of the Das Kaleidoskop Hall
  • Furniture in reading spaces and group rooms
  • Logistics preparation for various cultural events of the institution
  • Visitor reception, information and orientation.

Profile desired

  • You have a CFC level training in a technical field or Documentary Information Officer
  • You have a strong interest in librarian environments
  • You are rigorous and have a sense of organization and a fast learning capacity
  • You are comfortable in the contact with end users: enterprising, aptitutde to negotiation, firmness
  • You are willing to work on expanded and variable schedules, depending on the needs of the service, including in the evening when the situation requires
  • You master the French language with knowledge of German

Competences

Flexibility Agility Empathy Service orientation
