Full description

Join our PROCUREMENT & NETWORKS team. We lead, develop, design and plan national infrastructure and energy projects. We support these projects from the planning phase to their completion. Taking environmental issues into account, we carry out our projects in an environmentally friendly and sustainable way.

Come and join and strengthen our team by taking on the following responsibility: PROJECT MANAGER AND WORKS MANAGER

  • HIGH VOLTAGE POWER LINE CONSTRUCTION
  • SUPPLY & (m/f/d) 80-100% NETWORKS
  • LAUSANNE

(overhead and underground high-voltage lines)

YOUR TASKS

  • Responsibility for the planning and monitoring of demanding projects, with particular attention to the construction of overhead lines and high-voltage cables, from design to completion
  • Autonomous execution of the technical tasks of the project, including coordination of the development of plans and models, participation in technical and coordination meetings, and preparation of calculations and reports
  • Development of schedules and cost estimates
  • Preparation of tender documents for our clients' construction projects
  • Technical management of works for high and very high voltage lines, with particular attention to overhead and underground lines
  • Coordination and control of the work on site until commissioning
  • Nationwide displacement (approx. 10-20%)

YOUR CHANCES

  • Development of the engineering team for French-speaking Switzerland with the possibility of becoming the future team leader.
  • Collaboration, knowledge sharing and mutual support are the pillars on which our way of working is based
  • Varied and interdisciplinary projects, in collaboration with specialists from different fields
  • A diverse team, bringing together various professional backgrounds
  • One-on-one support, whether through targeted training or professional development opportunities
  • Flexible working models, with the possibility of teleworking, as well as a modern infrastructure, all in a central location

YOUR PROFILE

  • Electrical Engineering Engineer (BSc/MSc) or equivalent
  • At least three years' professional experience in electrical engineering, with a focus on the construction of high-voltage overhead and underground lines
  • Good knowledge of MS Office; knowledge of PLS-CADD, EFC-400, FM-Profil and AutoCAD, or comparable CAD systems is an asset
  • Experience with BIM (Building Information Modeling) methodology is an asset
  • Customer-oriented mindset and the pleasure of collaborating within a team
  • Very good knowledge of French, oral and written (min. C1)
  • Knowledge of other languages (German, Italian, English) is an asset
  • Reliable, autonomous and precise way of working
