Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Department of Psychiatry, with more than 1,300 employees, is responsible for a three-pronged mission in the area of psychological treatment, training and research. Thus the department's mission is to effectively detect and treat increasingly frequent and polymorphic pathologies with respect for patients and their relatives. Organised in 6 services, the department has a very wide and diverse range of care services, including hospital care, multidisciplinary ambulatory care in various ways and intensities, organised according to the principles of community psychiatry, as well as specialized programmes and units.

Description of post

As part of your duties, you perform neuropsychological assessments of adults monitored in the various structures of adult psychiatry care, outpatient psychiatry centres and in the consultation campus biotech Clinical Neurosciences. Your activity revolves around patients with various psychopathologies (schizophrenia, tda-h, ...) that can affect cognition, motivation and emotional regulation. The work includes the use of structured and standardized psychometric tests (e.g. BACS, MATRIX MCCB) relevant to the standardized survey in a clinical as well as scientific context. Cognitive rehabilitation work is also to be developed.

You have to collaborate with different teams

Multidisciplinary outpatient care teams of the adule psychiatry department (transmission of results and conclusions of neuropsychological evaluation)

Liaison with psychologists working in the various outpatient facilities of the service

Collaboration with stakeholders (care teams, researchers, ...) of the consultation campus biotech clinical neurosciences - Psychiatry

You are also actively involved in the research activities of the adult psychiatry service. More generally, you are evolving in an environment that values collaboration, clinical expertise and knowledge sharing, serving demanding and humane management.

Qualifications

With a Master's degree in Clinical Psychology (or equivalent), you have a federal degree in neuropsychology. You have several years of experience in clinical neuropsychology and have advanced training in methodology and research. Prior experience with a population of adults with mental disorders and in the field of research would be an asset. You have an ability to work in a multidisciplinary team while demonstrating autonomy, responsibility and ability to work and contribute to the development of clinical projects and research.
