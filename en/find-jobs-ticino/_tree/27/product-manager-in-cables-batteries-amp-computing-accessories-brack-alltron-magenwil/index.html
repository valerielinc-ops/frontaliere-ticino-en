Full description

Tasks

  • Corporate responsibility for your product ranges including sales, margin, contribution margin, budget and pricing strategy across our B2B and B2C channels
  • Strategic development of a broad portfolio with more than 8,000 articles as well as identification of new market and product range potential
  • Co-responsibility for the own brand strategy in the area of ICT Volume in close cooperation with our private label team
  • Evaluation of new manufacturers, suppliers and competitive sources of supply
  • Independent support and negotiations with manufacturer and supplier partners as well as implementation of the procurement strategy together with purchasing
  • Development and implementation of range, pricing and procurement strategies
  • Conception of marketing measures together with category marketing and sales
  • Analysis of relevant key figures, preparation of reporting and derivation of concrete optimization measures
  • Responsibility for manufacturer billing and compliance with legal and regulatory requirements in your range areas
  • Collaboration and responsibility in strategic projects such as dropshipping, data connections or purchasing tools

Requirements

  • Completed basic training with EFZ or equivalent qualification, ideally related to trade or e-commerce
  • Further training in marketing, product management, purchasing or business administration
  • At least 3–5 years of professional experience in product management or category management, preferably in the e-commerce and/or distribution environment
  • Very good analytical skills and pleasure in working with key figures and deriving concrete recommendations for action
  • Excel knowledge and safe handling of MS Office
  • Experience in cooperation and negotiation with manufacturers and suppliers
  • Practice in dealing with ERP systems and master data as well as understanding of data-driven range control
  • Knowledge of cross-channel pricing and marketing
  • Very good German and English skills
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, own initiative and a structured and independent working method

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.

"Together we shape the trade of tomorrow" Brack.Alltron employs around 1300 employees and generates over one billion sales. The company exists because a person had the courage to find new ways and go them. Our success story shows that through each individual, something great is created together and we love our uniqueness. We set ourselves ambitious goals, but it is very important to us that we act responsibly, promote and challenge each other and enjoy what we do every day.
