Full description

For Migros Dragons in Basel, we are looking for a person who is responsible for the acceptance of goods, the order in the magazine and the independent management of the frozen and beverage department. This includes ordering goods, shelf management and stock control. Do you feel addressed? We look forward to your application!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Basel, Dragon, Aeschenvorstadt 24, 4051 Basel

Important information

Working days/times: Monday to Friday between 5:30 and 8 pm, Saturdays between 5:30 and 6 pm We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Responsibility for the acceptance of goods and the correct storage of the delivered products
  • Ensuring order, cleanliness and structure in the magazine
  • Independent management of the frozen and beverage department including ordering goods and shelf care
  • Control of stocks and ensuring optimum availability of goods and compliance with quality standards

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience: in retail or logistics an advantage
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EBA): an advantage in retail or logistics
  • Experience in logistics, warehouse management or in the retail trade is advantageous
  • Independent, reliable and careful working methods
  • Physical resilience as well as enjoyment of practical activities and work in a team
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension fund with above-average performance: 8.5%, AG share 17%
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
  • Employee offers: Various offers, discounts and preferential conditions
  • Leisure and cultural activities: various discounted or free cultural activities
