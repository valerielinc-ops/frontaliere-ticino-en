Full description

When you work on construction sites, you professionally implement sophisticated steel and metal constructions. The company provides you with up-to-date work equipment and a professional environment. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • mount demanding steel and metal constructions on construction sites
  • use railings, stairs, balconies and canopies professionally
  • carry out installations independently according to drawings and plans
  • align metal constructions precisely and anchor them firmly
  • make adjustments and rework directly on the construction site
  • Requirements
  • Training degree as a metalworker or metalworker EFZ or equivalent craftsmanship qualification
  • Experience in the assembly of steel and metal construction structures would be desirable
  • Technical drawings and assembly plans safely applied
  • Careful, independent and reliable working method
  • Driver's license category B rounds off the offer
  • Applications in versatile and demanding metal construction projects
  • Contemporary equipment and professional infrastructure
  • Opportunities for professional development
