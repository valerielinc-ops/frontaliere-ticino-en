Full description

Within movodis SA, the LEB links Lausanne to Gros-de-Vaud and supports the development of a growing region. By 2035, he plans to introduce a 10-minute pace between Lausanne and Echallens in order to offer ever more attractive and sustainable mobility.

This development is accompanied by many infrastructure projects and requires strict maintenance of the railway lines. To ensure the safety, reliability and sustainability of the network, movodis is seeking a motivated team leader to supervise and organise the maintenance of LEB tracks.

Team Leader Maintenance LEB

Your Mission

As part of the LEB's public transport service mission

• Coordinate, supervise and monitor daily track maintenance operations, as well as their infrastructure, with due regard for costs, time and quality • Daily support the team manager/manager leads in the monitoring and management of entity employees in the field • Conduct preventive and remedial track maintenance

Your activities

Field follow-up of the team: Ensures the operational conduct of its team: • By relaying the direction of the manager way • By taking a position of influence, participatory leader against the team • By relaying issues or proposals from the team to the hierarchy • By supervising the work and following the proper execution of the work • Monitoring the conformity of the work carried out

  • Providing expertise as a technical respondent

Administrative support to manager: Supports manager in administrative tasks related to track maintenance activity: • Searching for parts in SAP and following the movements of spare parts in the absence of the storekeeper (50%) • By ensuring the adequacy of the tools and by placing the necessary controls • By developing weekly schedules in collaboration with the team manager • Supporting team members in their development needs

JEPO Management: Treats JEPO events in SAP

  • Writing and processing fault notices (description, analysis, data verification)
  • Identifying appropriate interventions and creating work orders (OT)
  • By assigning EO to employees

Maintenance: Runs track and infrastructure maintenance operations:

  • By providing preventive maintenance
  • By performing corrective maintenance operations
  • Controlling the operation following interventions
  • By providing an agreed picket service and related interventions
  • By participating in the conduct of the work
  • Respecting quality and defined costs
  • By carrying out preventive and corrective maintenance of infrastructures (green spaces, curing and washing, small civil engineering, etc.)

Compliance with safety and quality standards: Meets and enforces required safety standards:

  • By applying the safety procedures and ensuring that they are respected by the staff of the team
  • Ensuring traceability of interventions carried out

Your profile

Training

  • CFCs in the technical field, ideally as automatician (electrician), electronician or equivalent
  • In-service specialised training (internal and external) in railway clearances and necessary for the practice of the trade

Professional experience

  • The trade requires between 3 and 5 years of professional experience, in a similar field as a railway technician
  • First successful experience of coaching

Authorizations, legal requirements and security

  • Compliance with company IT rules and data confidentiality
  • Project coordinator
  • Specific authorizations m1: Licence B80 according to OCVM and OASF (with addition for driving the TM); Chief safety officer according to R TEN 20100 (periodic training every 3 years);
  • Driver's licence category B (light vehicles)

Practical information

  • Quality working conditions and social benefits
  • Place of work : Echallens
  • Contract: 100% indefinite
  • Selection process: 1st interview with manager and recruitment specialist to get to know each other
