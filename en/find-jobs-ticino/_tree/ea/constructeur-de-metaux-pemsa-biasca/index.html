For over 35 years, Pemsa has been a leader in recruitment, management and commissioning of personnel specialized in building, civil engineering, electricity, building and industry technology in Switzerland. Our technical expertise and rigorous attention to the needs of the Swiss market allow us to effectively support customers and candidates in achieving their professional goals. To strengthen our team and ensure our highly qualified resource partners, we carefully select profiles in line with the highest standards and local regulations.

Mission

  • Efficient management of specific role-related activities, respecting the safety regulations in force in the construction and industrial sectors.
  • Proactive collaboration with the operating team to ensure compliance with Swiss made quality standards and times.
  • Monitoring and maintenance of the machines and equipment entrusted, ensuring their proper functionality and safety.
  • Application of Swiss technical and administrative procedures in the context of construction and project management.

Profile

  • minimal experience proven in the specific sector, framed in the Swiss context, which allows rapid operational autonomy.
  • dubious technical mastery and certified knowledge of the Helvetic regulations relating to safety at work on site.
  • ability to use specialized machinery with relative permits according to Swiss classification (M1, M2, M3 etc. depending on the specific job).
  • minimum experience of 3 years in the Swiss market, guarantee of knowledge of the company reality and local legislation.
  • good knowledge of the Italian language at level B1, for effective communication with the team and the working environment.
  • further professional certifications related to the building or industrial sector recognised in Switzerland.
