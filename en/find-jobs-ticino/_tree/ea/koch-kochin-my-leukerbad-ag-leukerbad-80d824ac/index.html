Full description

The self-service restaurant Rinderhütte has 300 indoor seats, 100 on the terrace and offers outdoors a snow bar and a food truck.

In the field of gastronomy, we offer an interesting seasonal location for the winter of 2026/27 in the restaurant Rinderhütte on Torrent (mountain gastronomy for skiing/hiking/enjoyment guests) as a chef / cook 90%. The self-service restaurant Rinderhütte has 300 seats indoors, 100 seats on the terrace and offers a snow bar and food truck outdoors.

Your tasks

  • You create the mise en place or help with equipment
  • You make hot and cold food or support it
  • You help with orders and handle the entrance control, storage and transport
  • As a cook, you represent the kitchen management if necessary
  • You work during cleaning work and in compliance with hygiene and quality standards

Your profile

  • Completed training as a chef/chef EFZ or proven experience of several years in the kitchen
  • Passion and fun for fresh and seasonal dishes and preparation of fresh food
  • Creativity in Menu Design
  • You communicate in German and ideally also in French
  • You are flexible at work depending on your needs and time of day as well as seasonality or on weekend events
