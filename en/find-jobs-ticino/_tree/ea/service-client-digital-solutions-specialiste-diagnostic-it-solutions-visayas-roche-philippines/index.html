Key Responsibilities

  • Install, maintain, and troubleshoot Roche IT solutions and software at customer sites, ensuring integration with Hospital/Laboratory Information Systems (HIS/LIS) via HL7 and ASTM standards.
  • Collaborate with Sales, Service, and Marketing teams to promote Roche IT solutions, providing product positioning, customer value propositions, and consulting support on connectivity solutions, data security, and regulatory compliance.
  • Offer workflow consulting services, including mapping and optimizing laboratory layouts and processes, leveraging tools such as turnaround time (TAT) simulations.
  • Support sales efforts by delivering product presentations, demonstrations, and training to end-users, while ensuring staff are well-versed in IT product strategies.
  • Drive the development of promotional concepts and marketing strategies in coordination with the Marketing Team to enhance the IT solutions portfolio.
  • Proactively key in activities in the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system, escalating and resolving customer issues in a timely manner.
  • Ensure compliance with Roche’s quality standards, regulatory requirements, and local SOPs. Maintain PPE usage and observe safety protocols during maintenance and service activities.
