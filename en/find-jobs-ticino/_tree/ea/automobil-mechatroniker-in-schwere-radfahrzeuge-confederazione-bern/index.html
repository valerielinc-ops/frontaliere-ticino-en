Put to the Point

Are you an automotive mechatronics specialist and looking for a job that is anything but 08/15? As a specialist, you are responsible for the maintenance of wheeled vehicles in the workshop. With its wide range of vehicles, the Swiss Army offers you a unique and varied environment.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20914
  • Location: Wylerringstrasse 36, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Conduct diagnostics, repairs and adjustments of engine, drive assemblies, brakes, steering, chassis, subassemblies and safety/comfort electronics
  • Check and repair defects and repairs on non-marching vehicles
  • Perform revisions and replacement of individual assemblies
  • Provide technical support for material transfers and material withdrawals in the field of rolling stock
  • Capture defect messages and spare parts in the SAP system

This makes them unique.

  • Completed vocational training as an automotive mechatronics specialist /-in EFZ with specialization in commercial vehicles or related professions (e.g. agricultural machinery mechanic /-in EFZ etc.)
  • Category C driving licence is mandatory, category CE desired
  • Self-employed and responsible work
  • Cooperative and collaborative behaviour
  • Customer-oriented service concept
  • Good knowledge of MS-Office

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom

The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3’500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
