Full description

The ability of a company like the CSR to fulfill its public service mandate rests on the competence and reliability of its services. Without forgetting an aspect that comes first of all: the human factor.

Shipments

The person we are looking for will take care of the provision of the assigned staff, in compliance with the “Federal Law on Work” (LL) and the “SSR Collective Contract of Work” (CCL’22). It will ensure compliance with the internal rules on the detection of working time/performances according to official SSR deadlines (Uman and Controlling Resources) and according to the analytical accounting guidelines (HKLR). It will provide advice to Line Managers for the provision of the assigned productive staff. It will be called to cover the weekly picket shifts according to an agreed plan.

Requirements

We ask for training in administrative/management, preferably with the fulfillment of professional maturity, skills in the planning of productive resources and in the organization of their work, as well as knowledge of legal issues related to staff management. We also call for good organizational and work skills for goals, excellent relational skills and predisposition to teamwork as well as good resistance to stress, mental flexibility and propensity to change. The profile includes the good knowledge of the Office package, Excel and the knowledge of the German language (level B1) or ev. of the French language (level B1).

What we offer

  • The opportunity to work in a challenging context, where teamwork is a fundamental value.
  • A continuous comparison with multiple interlocutors and different business realities.
  • Direct involvement in production resource planning processes.
  • The opportunity to actively participate in projects and decisions that have a direct impact on organization and production.

Our promise

What are the ingredients to reach the inhabitants of Switzerland with quality format? Know-how, enthusiasm and an environment rich in stimuli, in which everyone can grow. To give the best it takes a source of inspiration: Welcome to the world of RSI!

  • Produce content: Quality journalism is our main activity and our passion. We enjoy editorial freedom, promote creativity and give ample space to the production of new and high-level content.
  • Give shape to digital: Quality journalism is also digital. We explore new possibilities and experience innovative forms: digital, interactive and crossmedia. A look at tomorrow for our spectators today.
  • Valorize the person: We cultivate solidarity relations, work independently and contribute together with a good working climate. Our success is also based on the personal and professional qualities of all employees.
  • Make sense: With our programs we reach and thrill Switzerland. We act with respect and sense of independence; we promote the free formation of opinions and strengthen pluralism. We are proud of the contribution we make to Switzerland.
