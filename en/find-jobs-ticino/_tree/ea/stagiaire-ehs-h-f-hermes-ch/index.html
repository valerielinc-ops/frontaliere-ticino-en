Full description

Principal place of work: Le Noirmont (JU) Secondary place of work : Brügg (BE) Duration of the internship: 9 months General missions: Under the supervision of the Head of EHS, the intern is responsible for supporting the EHS department, in particular with the Health and Safety Specialist, and ensures that the tasks entrusted to him are carried out properly. Key responsibilities and activities: 1 - Risk analysis:

  • Support the Health Safety Specialist in the deployment of risk analysis at the workstations at both production sites (inventory, methodology, risk assessment, etc.).
  • Update workplace documentation and the single risk assessment document.
  • Propose action plans and follow up with workshop managers. 2 - Personal protective equipment:
  • Identify PPE currently available and used in workshops.
  • Analyse the suitability of PPE for the various risks identified (including chemical and mechanical).
  • Write a standardized reference catalogue of validated PPE within the company.
  • Update the EPI zoning mapping of the Noirmont site as part of the extension project. 3 - Chemical risk:
  • Support the Health Safety Specialist in the deployment of the new chemical risk management tool QUARKS, in collaboration with the project team of the Industrial Directorate of the Hermès Group (preparation and editing of data files).
  • Ensure operational compliance: labelling, simplified safety data sheets (SDS), training, exposure measurement campaigns, etc. 4 - Miscellaneous:
  • Contribute to the facilitation of prevention tools at the request of the Head EHS. Profile:
  • Master's degree or engineering school, in progress or completed, with a specialization in occupational safety and health protection (STPS).
  • Good sense of contact, sense of service and openness of mind.
  • Responsibility, listening ability and relationship ease.
  • Rigor, organization, method and strength of proposal.
  • Ability to work independently while effectively integrating into a team.
