Full description

The Tarchini Group seeks a new resource to be included in its staff, in support of the Group's growth.

Active in Ticino in the design, construction and management of industrial, commercial, logistics and residential properties, the Group also manages FoxTown Factory Stores Mendrisio.

For its activities as General Contractor, the Group seeks

TECHNOLOGY

Light construction and finishes

The selected person will manage light building orders, renovation and installation of spaces, following them from the inspection to the delivery. It will operate independently in the management of the assigned activities, in compliance with budgets, deadlines and procedures of corporate approval, and will refer to the Manager.

Requirements

  • Technical diploma in construction or equivalent vocational training, accompanied by proven experience in the management of yards
  • At least 5 years of experience in managing light building orders, renovations or interior fittings, preferably at a General Contractor
  • Direct experience in the management of workers' teams and in the coordination of artisans, suppliers and subcontractors
  • Practical knowledge of dry construction, walls and counterwalls, ceilings and finishes
  • Excellent readability of technical drawings and execution of reliefs and measurements
  • Ability to process estimates, estimate materials and labor, plan processing and monitor costs, advancement and delivery times
  • Knowledge of electrical installations and RVCS sufficient to coordinate interference with building works and with specialists in charge
  • Operational knowledge in the field of fire protection, with particular reference to compartments, fire locks, crossing seals and escape routes
  • Good organizational skills, authoritativeness in team management, aptitude to solve the unexpected and care of relationships with customers and suppliers
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office, especially Excel, in Macintosh environment; ability to consult and update technical drawings with AutoCAD
  • License to conduct category B

Main handling

  • Perform inspections and withdrawals, collect the customer's needs and identify the necessary processing, technical constraints and operating conditions
  • Prepare detailed estimates for materials, labour and external performance; request and compare suppliers and subcontractors offers
  • Plan the stages of work, teams, procurement and interventions of external enterprises, defining the program and delivery times
  • Organizing and managing workers' teams operationally, assigning tasks and coordinating craftsmen and subcontractors
  • Check the progress and quality of the work, as well as compliance with the safety provisions; manage unexpected and interference between the various activities
  • Monitor hours, consumption and costs compared to the estimate, promptly report to the Responsible any deviations and propose corrective measures
  • Quantify additional variants and jobs and submit them to the approval provided by company procedures before their execution
  • Care for operational relationships and correspondence with customers, suppliers, companies and consultants; update the Responsible for Advancement and any critical issues
  • Check the works at the end of the work, arrange the elimination of defects and treat the delivery, the minutes, the final documentation and the consuntivo
  • Manage requests and prepare fire tests in collaboration with the external recognized technician: collect documentation, organize inspections and checks and coordinate adjustments until the closure of deficiencies

The specialist evaluations and the release of fire certificates and certifications remain the responsibility of the external recognized technician.

It offers

  • An interesting and dynamic activity within the Group’s various activities
  • Retribution to skills and experience
  • Full-time use, with workplace in Manno / Mendrisio
  • Immediate start or agree

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
