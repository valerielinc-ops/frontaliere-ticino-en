Drafter at the 'Infrastructures, Mobility, Environment \& Sports' department

The City of Monthey places the following position in the competition:

Do you want to make a concrete contribution to the development and management of the infrastructure of a dynamic municipality? Do you like both field work and technical data analysis and management? Join our team and participate in the sustainable management of our communal networks and equipment.

Your missions

A varied role between field and office

  • Ensure data management of drinking water and sanitation systems
  • Monitoring road infrastructure, parks and gardens data
  • Managing Urban Furniture Data
  • Ensure the maintenance and maintenance of sports infrastructure data

Your profile

  • Holder of a geomatician CFC, civil engineering draftsman or equivalent technical training
  • Interest in field work and infrastructure
  • Experience in DAO and SIT
  • Rigorous, accurate and autonomous person
  • Good relationship and sense of public service

We offer you

  • A varied position at the heart of municipal technical services
  • Various activities combining fieldwork, technical design and geographic data management
  • The possibility of contributing to the quality and development of the municipality's infrastructure
  • A stimulating working environment within a committed team

Conditions of engagement

  • Depending on staff status and scale of communal salaries

On-going

  • Immediately or to be agreed

The specifications can be consulted with Mr Thierry Huber, SIT Manager, who is at your disposal for further information ().

Offers, with cover letter, curriculum vitae, references and copies of diplomas and certificates must be submitted on the website: until 29 November 2026.
