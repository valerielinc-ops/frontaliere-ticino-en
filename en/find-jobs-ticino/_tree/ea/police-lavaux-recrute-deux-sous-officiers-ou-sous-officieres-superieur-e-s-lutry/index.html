Police Lavaux recruits NCOs. e Police Lavaux recruits two non-commissioned officers or senior non-commissioned officers Participation rate 100% Police Lavaux develops, recruits and gives you the opportunity to join a world heritage area of Unesco. She places in the competition two executive positions with an activity rate of 100% for the intervention units of the Public Security Division (work in turns): Head of Unit and Deputy Head of Unit Degree Next

Your Mission

Your general tasks will be to ensure the safety of the population, to carry out emergency interventions and requests for assistance in accordance with the legal framework, to conduct preventive patrols, to establish fruitful collaboration with security partners and to carry out all the administrative tasks inherent in the activity. As Head of Unit, in addition to general security missions, you will organize the work of your team in accordance with your hierarchy, ensure the quality of the tasks being performed, create an excellent group dynamic, set an example and maintain collaboration in a healthy and trustworthy environment. As a replacement head of unit, in addition to general security missions, you will be a valuable support for your superior both organizationally and managerially. During absences from your chieffe you will replace him and maintain a collaboration in a healthy and confident climate. Your profile and skills In addition to the federal certificate for police officers (swiss diploma required), future managers must respectively hold the diplomas CCII (CEO IU) and CCI VD (CEO IU) and benefit from professional experience where their skills in staff conduct, natural leadership, relational ease, sense of priorities and responsibilities have been fully recognized. We offer you Prospects of evolution and an employer attentive to your aspirations. Attractive and modern in welded teams. An exciting and varied activity for the benefit of the people and the common good. A dynamic working environment in an exceptional region.
