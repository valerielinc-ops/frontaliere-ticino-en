Location: Alloga AG, Buchmattstrasse 10, 3400 Burgdorf

Are you interested in an apprenticeship as a logistician? Handles

The pre-wholesale company Alloga provides warehousing, logistics and distribution services as well as other services for the pharmaceutical industry. We primarily supply wholesalers, including Galexis’ own wholesale business, as well as hospitals, doctors and pharmacies throughout Switzerland. At Alloga, we work on behalf of around 120 national and multinational pharmaceutical companies to distribute their products on the Swiss market. Almost every second package for patients in Switzerland was previously stored at Alloga in Burgdorf.
