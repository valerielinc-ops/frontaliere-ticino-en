Full description

Are you passionate about fresh foods, especially meat? As a team leader* in the service counter, you can live out your craftsmanship and sales talent. In addition, you inspire our customers with your tips and tricks. We look forward to your application!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Basel, Paradise, Spitswaldstrasse 215, 4123 Allschwil

Important information

Working days/times: Monday - Saturday, between 06:00 - 20:00 We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the professional and personnel management of the service counter department.
  • You serve and advise our customers.
  • You are responsible for the management, goods management and presentation and actively work in the specialist area.
  • You promote sales by planning and executing actions.
  • You support and develop employees and are also responsible for the training of learners.

Requirements

  • 2 - 3 years of professional experience: in the retail trade / butchery / cheese shop
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): butcher's shop / gastronomy / cook, cook
  • You bring extensive experience around the topic of meat.
  • With your skill in planning and organizing tasks as well as your flair for interpreting key figures, you can contribute to success.
  • Your communication skills and the joy of dealing with people set you apart.
  • Experience in HR management is also one of your strengths.
  • You can show PC skills and digital fitness.
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)
  • English (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension fund with above-average performance: 8.5%, AG share 17%
  • Social benefits: In the event of illness, the salary for two years is 100%, in the event of accident we are insured SUVA
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
  • Migros Bank: Preferential conditions for employees
  • Employee offers: Various offers, discounts and preferential conditions
  • Club School Migros: cost share of 70% in club school courses up to max. CHF 1,000 per year
  • Leisure and cultural activities: various discounted or free cultural activities

Contatto

Application and contact Maria Aldao HR Business Partner
