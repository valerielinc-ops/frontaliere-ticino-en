Full description

MAIN TASKS

During a 6-month internship, you will support the Packaging Technology team in the further development of the packaging strategy and in the implementation of the new EU Packaging Regulation (PPWR). This includes in particular the following activities:

  • Strategic projects: Participation in projects for the strategic orientation of the company towards the PPWR
  • Packaging optimization: Support the team in coordinating and improving existing packaging, both in terms of cost and logistical efficiency
  • Development of new solutions: Participate in the adaptation and standardization of packaging for new products and materials
  • Knowledge transfer: Provide training and guidance for production staff to optimize their handling of packaging
  • Group-wide standardization: Contribute to the unification and optimization of packaging materials used worldwide

PROFILE

You are studying packaging technology or a related field. In addition, the following characteristics and abilities characterize you:

  • High communication skills and enjoyment of teamwork
  • Good knowledge in dealing with MS-Office and an affinity for IT-supported processes
  • Good English skills in Word and Writing

ADDITIONAL NOTES

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
