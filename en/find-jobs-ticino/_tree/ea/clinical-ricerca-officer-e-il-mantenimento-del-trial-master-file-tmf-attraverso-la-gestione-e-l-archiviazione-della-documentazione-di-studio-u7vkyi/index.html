Full description

We are looking for a Clinical Research Officer with many years of experience in the role of CRO or pharmaceutical companies, to be included in the Clinical Research team. The resource will support the Clinical Research Manager (CRM) in the operational and administrative management of clinical trials, contributing to their proper start, development and completion, in accordance with corporate SOPs and applicable legislation.

Main Responsibility

Trial Master File (TMF) The main responsibility of the role is to manage, organize and maintain the Trial Master File (TMF), both paper and electronic. In particular:

  • setup and organization of TMF paper (pTMF) and electronic TMF (eTMF) ;
  • verification of quality, completeness and correct storage of documentation;
  • TMF periodic checks and follow-up of missing documentation;
  • document tracker/TMF Workbook management and updating;
  • eTMF oversight and quality check activities, including documentation uploaded by CRO and other partners;
  • collaboration with CRO and stakeholders involved in eTMF management;
  • final verification of the completeness of the TMF and support to the closure and storage of the study.

Support for clinical studies

  • support to CRM in operational and administrative activities of studies;
  • collaboration with CRO, clinical centres and suppliers;
  • support for preparing documentation for regulatory submissions;
  • support for the management of documentation and activities related to medicines and study materials;
  • participation in the study team meetings.

Requirements

  • Multiannual experience as a Clinical Research Officer, developed at CRO or pharmaceutical industry;
  • concrete experience in the management of TMF, both paper and electronic (eTMF);
  • good knowledge of the processes and documentation of clinical studies;
  • excellent organizational skills, precision and attention to detail;
  • ability to work independently and in collaboration with different stakeholders;
  • English fluent (B2 advanced– C1)
  • good familiarity with IT tools and document management systems.

They complete the precision, reliability, autonomy and strong orientation to the quality and completeness of the study documentation.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
