Founded in 1932 and located in the heart of the city of Lausanne, in an exceptional setting facing Lake Geneva and the Alps, the Clinique de Montchoisi has always been one of the most famous establishments in the canton of Vaud. It brings together the best specialists of national and international renown, especially in its flagship specialties: ophthalmology, orthopaedics, ORL and radiology. The clinic makes every effort to provide excellent care and personalized, unique and warm care in a state-of-the-art environment. In 2003, the Montchoisi Clinic joined Swiss Medical Network, one of Switzerland's two largest private clinics.
