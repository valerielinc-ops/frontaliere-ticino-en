Full description

Are you interested in meaningful work, in which you allow children and young people a varied leisure time? With us you manage the holiday pass, the creative and sports weeks, organize, bring your ideas and shape with you. You will accompany the children and youth parliament of the city of Lucerne. If you do not shy away from customer contact, you are exactly right with us.

Tasks

  • You support in the organization, design and further development of leisure activities.
  • You create social media posts.
  • You make contracts and agreements.
  • You are in written and telephone exchange with the parents and the course leaders.
  • In addition, you work at the Children’s & Youth Parliament and help with the design, organization and accompaniment of events.
  • Your other tasks include managing our websites (www.freizeit-luzern.ch)

Requirements

  • You have successfully completed the secondary level II (Matura, intermediate school or similar).
  • You are self-employed and reliable.
  • You bring stylish knowledge of German and a safe handling of MS-Office.
  • You are team capable, resilient and flexible.
  • You are interested in topics for children and young people.
  • If you are also a communicative, creative and curious personality, you fit perfectly into our team.
