Full description

Bank, customer advice – sales Busy: 80 - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Are you convinced that first-class asset advisory services need more than investment expertise alone? We too. At Helvetia, we combine banking and insurance in a unique way. In this way, we support our customers comprehensively in all important financial issues of their lives. From investment to provisioning to estate and financing planning. For you, this means more opportunities, more room for manoeuvre and more entrepreneurial freedom. You shape your customer relationships independently and decide flexibly how best to look after your customers. Whether in the office, at the customer’s premises or digitally: Consulting takes place where it creates the greatest added value.

What you do

As a private banking consultant, you accompany wealthy customers in important financial decisions and develop long-term, trusting relationships. You benefit from our unique interaction between bank and insurance as well as from the know-how of specialized colleagues from both worlds. Together we create solutions that go far beyond traditional investment advice. Your tasks

  • You support and advise wealthy private clients holistically and position yourself as a trusted contact person in all financial matters.
  • You analyze the individual situation of your customers and develop tailor-made solutions for asset accumulation, asset preservation and asset transfer.
  • Together with specialists from the bank and insurance sector, you will develop individual investment, pension, financing and succession concepts.
  • You integrate insurance services and services from our partnerships into your advice and thus create real added value for your customers.
  • You actively monitor developments in the financial markets and regularly discuss their effects with your customers.
  • You actively gain new customers and develop your customer portfolio sustainably.
  • You represent our private banking at customer events and maintain your network in the Basel region and the surrounding area.

What you bring

  • You have several years of experience in the support and advice of wealthy private customers.
  • You have completed a banking education as well as a well-founded further education in the financial sector, for example as a financial planner: in a federal certificate, Certified Wealth Management Advisor (CWMA) or a Bachelor in Banking & Finance.
  • You are enthusiastic about holistic advice and recognize the opportunities offered by the interaction of bank and insurance.
  • You think entrepreneurially, act independently and have a strong customer orientation.
  • You convince through empathy, authenticity and the ability to build sustainable customer relationships.
  • Thanks to your regional roots, you have a valuable network and know the needs of the people and companies in the region.
  • Your professional appearance, reliability and team spirit round off your profile.
