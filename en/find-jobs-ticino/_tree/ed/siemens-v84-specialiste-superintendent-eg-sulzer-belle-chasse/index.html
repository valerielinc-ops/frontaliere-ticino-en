About the role

Job Summary The SGTS Superintendent oversees the daily operations of the SGTS GT Division with a focus on safety, quality, and efficiency. They cultivate a positive work environment, drive technical advancement, and ensure effective collaboration with customers and stakeholders. This role also involves optimizing processes, managing budgets, and leading Health and Safety initiatives to promote a culture of excellence and continuous improvement. Responsibilities

  • Manage day-to-day operations, prioritizing safety, environmental compliance, and adherence to quality standards.
  • Manage the Profit and Loss (P&L) of the division, ensuring financial targets are met or exceeded by effectively managing costs, maximizing revenue streams, and implementing strategies to enhance profitability.
  • Cultivate a positive work environment by actively engaging with employees, setting clear expectations, and promoting teamwork.
  • Drive technical advancement within the division by staying abreast of industry trends and continuously improving coating solutions.
  • Optimize work processes to enhance efficiency and profitability, ensuring alignment with monthly budgets.
  • Collaborate with customers, Sales Department, and vendors to meet project requirements and maintain strong relationships.
  • Coordinate scheduling and project timelines, regularly updating task status and completion dates for effective project management.
  • Conduct performance evaluations for Direct Reports and manage salary adjustments in line with company policies.
