Full description

10.11.2026 St. Gallen Zurich

Become part of our team!

As a domain architect of IT platforms, you are responsible for the architectural orientation and further development of strategic platforms and technology capabilities. You define target images, standards and guidelines for platforms in the areas of AI, DevOps, Engineering and Automation and ensure their sustainable, consistent and future-oriented use across the entire IT landscape. To strengthen our team based in St. Gallen (with the possibility of home office and workplaces in the Circle at Zurich Airport), we are looking for a personality as domain architect (m/f/d) from now on or by agreement.

What awaits you?

  • Responsibility for the architecture of our strategic platforms in the areas of AI, DevOps, Software Engineering and Application Platforms
  • Develop and develop target architectures, reference architectures, standards and guidelines for platforms and technology capabilities
  • Evaluating new technologies, architectural approaches and market trends in terms of added value, risks and sustainability
  • Co-shaping the technology management and control of the technology lifecycle from introduction to replacement
  • Conducting architecture reviews and supporting strategic technology and architecture decisions
  • Ensure consistent use of platforms, technologies and architecture standards across projects and organizational units
  • Act as a technical sparring partner for enterprise architects, solution architects and platform and engineering teams and promote standardization, reuse and innovative capacity of the platform landscape

What do you bring?

  • Completed degree in computer science, business informatics or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in software development, ideally with Java or Kotlin and Spring Boot, as well as as a solution or technical architect
  • In-depth experience in platform engineering and internal developer platforms, including Golden Paths, software catalog and self-service
  • Good knowledge of container and cloud native technologies such as OpenShift, GitOps with Argo CD and helmet, CI/CD and Infrastructure as Code
  • Experience with AI platforms, such as LLM integration, RAG, model hosting or MLOps, as well as understanding of AI governance
  • Very good understanding of service-oriented and event-driven architectures (for example Kafka) as well as integration landscapes
  • You convey architectural specifications pragmatically, enforce them effectively and inspire development teams for common standards (Developer Advocacy)
  • Very good German and English skills
