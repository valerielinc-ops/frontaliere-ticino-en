# Head of Basic Training for the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI)

The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI) puts in competition a position of

Head of Basic Training

at the

Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI)

based in Lugano. The selected person will be a member of the Departmental Directorate and will assume the strategic, organizational and administrative responsibility of the Department's Basic Training. The professional framework is provided for in the function of

Associate Professor/Professor or Ordinary

Lecturer or Lecturer/Docente-researcher

, according to the profile, qualifications and the degree of academic maturity of the selected person. Employment rate 80–100%. Entry into service 1 September 2027 or date to be agreed.

Shipments

  • Participate, as a member of the Directorate of DTI, to the definition and implementation of strategic addresses and departmental decisions.
  • Define and implement the development strategy of basic training, in accordance with the departmental and institutional addresses.
  • Coordinate the Bachelor and Master's educational offer, ensuring its quality, attractiveness and educational innovation.
  • Ensure the management and development of the managers of the undergraduate courses and the Secretariat of the DTI Basic Training, promoting the valorisation of skills and a culture of collaboration.
  • Ensure the continuous management and improvement of the administrative processes of the Basic Training.
  • Ensure planning and coordination of training activities and resources allocated.
  • Collaboration with the SUPSI's Directorate for Basic Training in Task Planning and the implementation of institutional addresses.
  • Represent the Department in the SUPSI Basic Training Commission and collaborate on transversal projects
  • To participate in dipartimental and interdepartmental working groups, contributing to the harmonization and improvement of training and administrative processes.
  • Promote relations with the professional world and synergies between training, applied research and innovation.

Requirements

  • Master's degree or equivalent in an area relevant to the activities of the Department; the doctorate is preferential.
  • Experience gained in university education and training, preferably accompanied by recent professional experiences gained in other university or business contexts.
  • Solid academic curriculum, with proven experience in research and development in university context.
  • Experience in the management of academic and/or administrative staff, team guidance and coordination of complex organizational processes.
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss university system, preferably of professional colleges.
  • Leadership, strategic vision, planning and management of change.
  • Explain relational, organizational skills and attitude to interdisciplinary and interdepartmental collaboration.
  • Guidance to quality, innovation and continuous improvement.
  • Educational qualification or availability to achieve it, pursuant to Directive 6C of the Internal Directives of SUPSI contracted personnel.
  • Excellent knowledge of Italian and English; knowledge of another national language is a preferential title.
  • Residence in the Canton of Ticino or availability to transfer your domicile within a period agreed upon by the intake of the function, in view of the responsibility and the need to ensure an adequate presence on the territory.

What we offer

  • The DTI Head of Basic Training has a four-year duration and is renewable.
  • Responsibility function as a member of the Departmental Directorate, with an active role in defining and implementing strategies for the development of professional university education, in a dynamic and innovative context.
  • Competitive salary, in line with Swiss standards and the experience of the candidate/a.

We value diversity

In all its activities, SUPSI guarantees an inclusive study and work environment, accessible and respectful of every diversity, with the aim of offering everyone a high quality university education. More information about diversity and inclusion are available to this link .

  • Titolo universitario Master o equivalente in un ambito pertinente alle attività del Dipartimento; il dottorato costituisce titolo preferenziale.

  • Comprovata esperienza nell’insegnamento universitario e nella gestione di attività formative, preferibilmente affiancata da esperienze professionali recenti maturate in altri contesti universitari o aziendali.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
