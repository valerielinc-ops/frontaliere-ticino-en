About us

The largest pharmacy network Amavita is a company of the Galenica network and, with Switzerland's largest pharmacy network, is an essential pillar of community health care. We offer a wide range of products ranging from prescription and over-the-counter drugs to natural medicine and cosmetics. In addition, we are experts in dermatological care. Amavita pharmacies are mainly present in western and northern Switzerland. They are usually located in attractive and lively places.
