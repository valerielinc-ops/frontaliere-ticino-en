Full description

Give your job a new look FISBA is one of the world's leading suppliers in the optics industry and stands for outstanding performance from optical design and system engineering to high-precision series production and coating. FISBA operates from its headquarters in Switzerland and branches in Germany, the USA and China. The company is privately owned. We employ over 360 employees from 30 nations worldwide. To strengthen our purchasing team, we are looking for This is your future activity

  • Procurement of optical components and optomechanical assemblies as well as contract and contract processing contracts in compliance with the corresponding quality, price and deadline specifications
  • Active communication and coordination with suppliers as well as internal interfaces such as quality management, testing centres, work preparation and project management
  • Monitoring delivery dates and ensuring demand-based material availability
  • Processing and coordination of complaints
  • Request, comparison and evaluation of tenders
  • Pricing and contract negotiations with suppliers as well as responsibility for order processing
  • Active supplier management including supplier evaluation, supplier visits, supplier development, portfolio optimisation and auditing
  • Participation in make-or-buy decisions, projects and offers as well as active participation in relevant shop floor meetings
  • Active participation in the development, implementation and further development of the procurement strategy

We make these requirements

  • Completed basic technical training, preferably as a precision optician, or comparable training and corresponding further training in the field of procurement (e.g. purchasing specialist)
  • Several years of purchasing experience in an international environment
  • Good technical know-how, preferably in the field of optics
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English
  • SAP and MS Office skills
  • Sympathetic and dynamic personality with outstanding communication skills and result-oriented negotiating skills
