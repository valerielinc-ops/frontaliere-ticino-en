Full description

We are looking for a dedicated candidate to join our Backoffice team at Cornèrcard.

In this role, you will play a crucial part in supporting the team with administrative tasks and ensuring the smooth operation of the department.

  • Handle customer enquiries received via email and written correspondence in German, French, Italian and English.
  • Maintain, update and manage customer records for credit and prepaid cardholders, ensuring data accuracy and completeness within the company's systems.
  • Receive, analyse and manage complaints, enquiries and requests relating to payment cards.
  • Ensure timely, accurate and customer-focused handling of all requests, contributing to a high level of customer satisfaction.
  • Comply with internal procedures, quality standards and applicable regulatory requirements.
  • Proactively identify appropriate solutions to customer issues and escalate more complex cases to the relevant department when necessary.
  • Perform administrative and back-office activities, including the preparation and processing of operational reports, lists and documentation.
  • Maintain accurate customer records and documentation across the company's information systems.

What do we offer?

  • Flexibility in working from home and the management of working hours in order to guarantee a healthy work-life balance
  • Numerous benefits and incentives
  • Well-being, motivational and team-building activities
  • Excellent knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel (data management, filters and list processing), Word (business correspondence) and Outlook (email management).
  • Strong IT proficiency, with the ability to work efficiently and accurately across multiple systems and applications simultaneously.
  • High level of accuracy, reliability and attention to detail.
  • Strong customer service orientation, organisational skills and problem-solving abilities.
  • Excellent command of German (Proficiency, Native), French, Italian and English, both written and spoken.
  • Previous experience in a similar customer service, back-office or administrative role, preferably within the banking or financial services sector.
  • Resident in Ticino or willing to relocate
