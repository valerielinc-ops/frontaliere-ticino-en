ASSC / Community Health and Care Assistant FEE at 80%

L的 EMS Residence Jean\-Paul , in Riddes, welcomes 55 residents in long stay and 1 in short stay. An institution of a human size, we favour individualized care, respecting the rhythm, needs and dignity of each person.

Within our EMS, proximity, goodwill and interdisciplinary collaboration guide our commitment to serving residents.

To complement our team and to contribute together to the quality of the accompaniment of our residents, we seek:

Your mission

  • Accompaniment and care of residents in everyday life
  • Provide basic and delegated care, in collaboration with the nursing team
  • Actively participate in monitoring and reporting
  • Contributing to the maintenance of a secure environment that respects institutional values
  • To provide pedagogical guidance and follow-up to apprentices (ASSC, ASA) in the sector

Your profile

  • SWC Community Health Care Assistant (CHS)
  • To benefit from the FEE certificate
  • Experience in geriatrics or EMS, an asset
  • Meaning of listening, observation and communication
  • Team spirit
  • Autonomy, sense of responsibility and ability to manage priorities
  • Mastery of the French language, both oral and written
  • Use of common computer tools

We offer you

A human-sized working environment

A constructive interdisciplinary collaboration

Wage conditions according to the CLC SLD

Free parking for employees

Committed to office: 1 January 2027
