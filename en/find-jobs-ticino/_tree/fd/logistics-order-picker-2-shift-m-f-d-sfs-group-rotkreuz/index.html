SFS is a nationwide provider of screws, tools, fittings, building ranges, special parts and chemical-technical products throughout Switzerland.   • Assistance with bottlenecks in other logistical workplaces
  • Responsibility for resolving investment disruptions in day-to-day business and ensuring trouble-free operation Your profile:
