Full description

Introduction

As part of the development of our activities in the canton of Fribourg, we are a pool of talents to accompany the deployment of our new industrial site in Bulle. To this end, we will soon be looking for (M/F) Maintenance Technician profiles to strengthen our teams.

Responsibilities

  • Corrective maintenance of production equipment, automatic inventory, autonomous mobile robots and self-guided vehicles
  • Apply diagnostic methodologies, identify the root causes of malfunctions and return equipment to service within the expected timeframe
  • Conduct preventive maintenance operations and help improve maintenance plans
  • Prepare interventions according to operational constraints, analyse equipment history and follow safety and environmental instructions
  • Propose and implement reliability actions, technical modifications and equipment improvements
  • Coordinate and support internal and external stakeholders by monitoring and validating their interventions
  • Share technical knowledge with teams and collaborate with internal partners in cross-cutting projects and work

Profile

  • Technical training in mechanics, automation, electrical engineering or equivalent
  • Experience in maintenance of automated industrial equipment, ideally in a demanding production environment
  • Confirmed technical versatility in mechanics, electricity, pneumatic and hydraulics
  • Knowledge of automation, digital controls, robotics and industrial computing
  • Mastery of common IT tools and integrated management system
  • Knowledge of English and/or intermediate German (B1), an asset
  • Communication, team spirit, rigor, autonomy and technical curiosity
  • Availability for flexible schedules, intersite travel and periodic service as required

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
