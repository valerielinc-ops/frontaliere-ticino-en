Their tasks

  • Execution of general plate laying work in new, renovation and renovation construction
  • Installation of ceramic wall and floor panels
  • Processing of porcelain, natural stone and mosaic panels
  • Plates in bathrooms, kitchens, living quarters and stairwells
  • Performing board laying work in the interior and exterior
  • Preparation, cleaning and priming of various substrates
  • Execution of compensation and spatula work
  • Creation of professional waterproofing in wet areas
  • Cutting and adapting plates with suitable machines and tools
  • Execution of jointing and silicone work
  • Laying of large-format panels and special panel coverings
  • Carrying out repair and refurbishment work on existing panel coverings
  • Creating clean closures, edges and transitions
  • Dividing and measuring plate areas according to building plans
  • Proper handling of plate-laying tools and materials
  • Carrying out quality controls and ensuring clean processing
  • Independent work according to building plans and specifications of the foreman
  • Compliance with safety and quality regulations on the construction site

  • Very good knowledge of general record setting work
  • Experience in laying ceramic wall and floor panels
  • Safe handling of porcelain, natural stone and mosaic panels
  • Good knowledge of laying large-format plates
  • Experience in indoor and outdoor laying work
  • Knowledge of professional sealing of wet areas
  • Experience in preparing and balancing substrates
  • Safe handling of plate cutting machines and other tools
  • Good knowledge of available and silicone work
  • Experience in the new, renovation and renovation sector
  • Knowledge of reading and implementing building plans
  • Craftsmanship and strong technical understanding
  • Clean, precise and quality-conscious working methods
  • Independent and professional work according to work orders
  • German skills are advantageous
  • Category B driving licence is advantageous
  • Own car not required
  • Physically resilient and reliable
