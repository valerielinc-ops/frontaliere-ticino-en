Full description

The movement is in our DNA - since 1936. As a family company, we give importance to people who want to think, work and grow with us. Join our family and build with us the future of transport logistics and storage.

What's going on?

  • Transportation the goods of our customers safely and punctually on trucks, trucks or self-articulates
  • Before any trip, check your vehicle, load it as a rule of art and download it once at destination
  • The national provisions on transport, including for the management of dangerous goods
  • You move within Switzerland, comply with road traffic regulations and drive environmentally friendly
  • Keep your vehicle in good condition, take care of it and ensure its operation
  • In the daily contact with customers you always feel friendly and helpful
  • During your internship you will also know a truck workshop

Because you will succeed

  • You are passionate about technique and you also have good manual skills
  • Your strengths are autonomy, reliability and calm when you are under pressure
  • Thanks to your physical shape and stress resistance you are able to manage your working everyday life outside the office
  • Opening, contact propensity and friendliness distinguish your relationship with other people

Your advantages

  • Family business culture: We have respect, trust and a correct relationship. We give you "you" and we follow you personally in your path.
  • Holiday: In the first year of apprenticeship you have 8 weeks of vacation, in the second 7 and in the following 6 years.
  • Events: During your training period, you will participate in various events such as Welcome Day, the study seminar, the field for apprentices, the apprenticeship end party, etc.
  • Financial support: We have a good pay with the thirteenth. If you're doing well at school, in inter-company courses and in the company, you'll have an extra bonus. In addition, we pay you the school material, we give you a contribution to buy the notebook and receive a half-price subscription for the apprenticeship period.
  • Safe training: With an experiential route and the “Fit for work & life” program, we help you improve your safety.
  • Talent development: We offer you good career and development opportunities.

Who are we? For us, as a company, collaboration and a positive working atmosphere are fundamental. For this reason we particularly appreciate quality such as team spirit, availability and motivation. Moreover, we attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and precision, since these values are the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
