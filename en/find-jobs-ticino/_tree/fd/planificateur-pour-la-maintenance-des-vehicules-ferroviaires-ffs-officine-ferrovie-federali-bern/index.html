Planner:in maintenance rail vehicles 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Planner:in maintenance rail vehicles Bern / Work Smart, 01.01.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

An exciting working environment at the interface between data, technology, production and management awaits you. You analyze complex data and, on this basis, dimension maintenance services for our fleets. At the same time, you are in regular exchange with different stakeholders from technology, planning and production. You explain complex data in an understandable way and create acceptance for data-based planning. In this way, you make a direct contribution to the safety, reliability and cost-effectiveness of the Swiss railway system. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • Maintenance services are dimensioned at different locations over different planning horizons and thus contribute to the reliability and safety of trains. Ensure adequate and economical planning.
  • You analyze the need for preventive maintenance and repairs for all fleets, structure the complex data situation in a targeted manner and use information from IT systems as well as the knowledge and experience of the team and the organization.
  • From large amounts of data, you condense decision-relevant insights and prepare them for the addressee.
  • With your self-motivation and personal responsibility, you define your tasks yourself to a large extent and work on further development topics and projects or initiate them and coordinate such projects across departments.
  • In financial planning, budgeting and reporting, you make your contribution to the services and locations you are responsible for.

You bring that with you.

  • Strong communication skills and secure stakeholder management help you to involve different stakeholders, communicate decisions in a comprehensible way and accompany their implementation. Thanks to analytical thinking, you reduce complexity and make informed decisions.
  • You have experience in tasks that require planning and thinking ahead of different time horizons. For example, in offer or operational planning. Knowledge of the railway and maintenance of vehicles is advantageous, but not mandatory.
  • Advantageously, you bring a degree at a high school or university of applied sciences (e.g. in business administration, computer science, mathematics / natural sciences, in the technical field or comparable further education).
  • German at level C1 is required. Other national languages are beneficial.
  • There is a flair for facts and figures, Microsoft Excel uses you in depth. In addition, you are interested in SAP, other planning applications and artificial intelligence and ideally have gained initial experience or acquire appropriate knowledge.
