Full description

The Pool of Caregivers provides clinical services with ad hoc resources from experienced collaborators in order to compensate for occasional absences, but also to strengthen teams during peak work. It is made up of flexible collaborators adaptable to different units.

To complete our service we are recruiting a

Pool Nurse - CDM 90% 3 months (ref: 2073)

Your Mission

In a state-of-the-art facility, you work in a multidisciplinary environment, in close collaboration with nurses, caregivers and physicians.

You ensure the overall care of patients with various conditions in the acute phase, ensuring the quality, safety and continuity of care.

As such, your main responsibilities are

• To receive, install and accompany patients with information on their stay and care. • Collect, evaluate and transmit medical and administrative data, while maintaining the computerized patient record. • Organize and plan care taking into account priorities, urgency and changing clinical conditions. • Perform medical prescription care in accordance with protocols, procedures and institutional requirements. • Ensure rigorous and complete documentation of the computerized patient record. • Monitor and evaluate the clinical status of patients on an ongoing basis, quickly identify any changes and adjust management accordingly.

Your profile

You are a committed professional who has excellent clinical sense and appreciates the diversity of care situations.

You have the following qualifications and qualities

• Diploma of Nurse. • Recognition of the Swiss Red Cross compulsory for holders of a foreign diploma. • Excellent sense of contact and service, with particular attention to patient satisfaction and well-being. • Autonomy, benevolence and sense of responsibility. • Great versatility and ability to adapt quickly to different working environments and organizations. • Excellent organizational, anticipation and priority management capabilities. • Team spirit, positive attitude and ability to build confidence with your colleagues and patients. • Great flexibility and availability to meet the requirements of a position in the Pool.

What we offer

• A varied position offering a wealth of clinical experiences. • The opportunity to develop and enhance your versatility within different units. • A stimulating working environment, in contact with experienced multidisciplinary teams. • An activity that promotes the autonomy, adaptability and development of your professional skills.

Post conditions

• Type of contract: Contract with maximum duration of 3 months • Participation rate: 90%. • Schedules : Variables, depending on the needs of the services.

Want to meet the challenge and join our Caregiver Pool?

We look forward to discovering your application and welcoming you to our teams!
