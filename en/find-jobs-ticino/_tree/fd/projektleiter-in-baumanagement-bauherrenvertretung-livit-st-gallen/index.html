Construction Management St. Gallen | Workload: 80-100% Are you open‑minded, ambitious and value a future‑oriented way of working at the pulse of the real estate industry? Whether repairs, modernisations or repurposing – in the construction management of Livit you will oversee a wide variety of properties throughout their entire life cycle. Are you ready to tackle today’s and tomorrow’s challenges and make a difference in the real estate world? Then Livit is the right place for you. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your Responsibilities

  • You work on a variety of tasks and construction challenges related to the preservation of property value throughout the life cycle.
  • You advise, plan and implement small and medium‑sized renovation projects.
  • You are responsible for timely, cost‑effective and quality‑compliant execution.
  • You advise and represent the client’s interests in medium‑sized renovation projects.
  • You support the property managers in construction‑related questions and budgeting.
  • You actively maintain internal and external client relationships.

Your Profile

  • You have completed a basic construction training and further education as a Techniker HF, Architect FH or Civil Engineer FH.
  • You bring several years of professional experience as a project manager of renovation projects.
  • You possess a strong technical understanding, have a pragmatic mindset and act solution‑oriented.
  • You are resilient and have excellent organisational skills for handling multiple projects simultaneously.
  • You are digitally savvy and have the ability to actively drive change.
  • You have a strong customer orientation and maintain a partnership approach.
  • You have an entrepreneurial mindset and conduct, as well as pleasant manners.
  • You are a team player and contribute your expertise profitably within the team.
