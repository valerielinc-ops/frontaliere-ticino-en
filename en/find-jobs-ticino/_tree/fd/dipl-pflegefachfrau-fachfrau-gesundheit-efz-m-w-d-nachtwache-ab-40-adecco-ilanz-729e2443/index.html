Full description

Are you looking for an activity at night that combines responsibility, independence and teamwork? Here you have the opportunity.

Tasks

  • You accompany people during the night and support them with mobility, personal care, toilet walks and food intake
  • You observe the state of health and carefully document changes
  • You care for safety and well-being
  • You plan and prioritize your tasks independently
  • You comply with hygiene standards and protective measures

Profile

  • You have completed training in nursing or a comparable qualification
  • You ideally have experience in caring for or dealing with older people
  • You have knowledge of primary care, observation and documentation
  • You work independently, structured and efficient
  • You bring empathy, professionalism and team spirit
  • You deal constructively with conflicts, are critical and discreet
  • You have good knowledge of German

Advantages

  • A collaborative, supportive team
  • An appreciative working environment with a long-term perspective
  • Fair employment conditions including social benefits
  • Opportunities for professional training
  • Flexible working hours in shift work
  • Combining space, work and personal free time optimally
