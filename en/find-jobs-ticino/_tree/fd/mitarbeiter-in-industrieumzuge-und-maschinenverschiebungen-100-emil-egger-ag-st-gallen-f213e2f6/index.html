Full description

You are skilled in craftsmanship, tech-savvy and like to join? Are you looking for a varied activity in which no day is like the other and teamwork is capitalized? Then you are exactly right with us! The EMIL EGGER Group is the leading provider of heavy goods logistics in Switzerland. In the areas of crane work, special transports, industrial removals and rental lifts, more than 230 employees are employed. With the comprehensive special know-how, individual and innovative customer solutions are developed every day. For our team in St. Gallen we are looking for reinforcements.

Your job with us

  • You support in the dismantling, displacement, assembly and removal of machines, plants and operating facilities.
  • You use special tools and technical aids professionally.
  • You work hand in hand with our transport managers, dispatchers and other specialists.
  • You are directly at the customer’s site and together with the team ensure a professional and smooth order processing.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed basic technical or craft training is mandatory (e.g. mechanic, carpenter or fitter).
  • You have craftsmanship and technical understanding.
  • You are physically fit, resilient and like to work practically.
  • You are ready to take on missions throughout Switzerland – including occasional overnight stays.
  • You work independently, reliably and solution-oriented.
  • You are capable of teamwork, flexible and enjoy direct customer contact.
  • You have a driver's license Cat. B. Categories BE or C are advantageous.

This is what awaits you

  • A strong team: We support each other – we count here.
  • Pure variety: Exciting and versatile applications with different machines, plants and challenges.
  • Modern work equipment: You work with professional equipment and modern infrastructure.
  • Good training: We make you fit for your new tasks and promote your further development.
  • Attractive framework conditions: progressive social benefits and a free parking space.
  • Safety and perspective: long-term employment in an innovative and successful family business with a lively du-culture.

Ready to move big with us? We look forward to meeting you! Send us your application or contact us directly by phone if you have any questions. We look forward to seeing you!
