Full description

Are you looking for a role that combines your technical expertise, project work, and communication skills? In this position, you will play a central role in introducing, developing, and integrating our HR systems. You will be the key interface between our external implementation partners and our internal HR departments: this combination makes the role very valuable for your future career.

  • Your responsibilities will be:
  • Supporting ongoing software projects in the HR area
  • with a focus on introducing and developing Abacus and SAP SuccessFactors.
  • Coordinating between our external partners and our HR teams
  • and keeping a cool head even in hectic phases.
  • Analyzing, documenting, and maintaining interfaces to adjacent systems (Finance, Time Management, etc.).
  • Collaborating with IT and our implementation partners to perform data migrations and ensure their quality.
  • Configuring field-level configurations and ensuring they are neatly embedded in global and local HR processes.
  • Creating and maintaining clear, understandable technical documentation and supporting tests, acceptances, and training.
  • Recognizing optimization potential
  • and actively driving the improvement of data quality, processes, and system integration. The position is currently limited until 30.06.2027, with the option of an extension.
  • Requirements:
  • Completed education or degree in Computer Science, Economics, Engineering, or a similar field.
  • Several years of experience in IT or HR digitalization projects
  • ideally with SuccessFactors and/or Abacus.
  • Good understanding of interface architectures, data models, and migrations.
  • Enjoyment of HR processes and representing them technically.
  • Structured and independent working style
  • combined with strong communication and coordination skills.
  • Very good German and English knowledge to navigate between all stakeholders.
