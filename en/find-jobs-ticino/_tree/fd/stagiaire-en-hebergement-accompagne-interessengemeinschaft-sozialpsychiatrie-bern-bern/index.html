Full description

In assisted living at igs Bern, we offer people with severe cognitive and psychological impairment a home. Five residents live in each of our four residential groups: we accompany them around the clock during 365 days of the year. The daily structure is adapted to their individual needs and possibilities. Self-determination, autonomy and quality of life are our focus.

For our residential groups in Moosseedorf and Fraubrunnen we are looking for 1. Febraur 2027 motivated interns:women for a training internship / practical module or a preliminary internship (6-12 months) in connection with training as a social pedagogue:in, social worker:in or specialist care. This is what awaits you

  • You accompany our residents in everyday life and support them in living as self-determined as possible.
  • You plan and design activities inside and outside the residential group.
  • You support the residents in their personal care.
  • Together with the team and the residents, you will perform everyday tasks such as cooking and shopping.
  • You participate in team meetings and supervisions and bring your ideas to the table. You bring that with you
  • Interested in working with people in need of support
  • Motivation to learn new things and develop yourself
  • Commitment, reliability and teamwork
  • Readiness for irregular assignments and weekend services This is what we offer you
  • Sound practical training with personal accompaniment
  • An experienced and appreciative team
  • A varied and meaningful activity
  • Pleasant service hours (no shared services)
  • Regular discussions with your practical instructions and support with your training goals
  • Paid food and coffee/tea during service hours Interested? We look forward to your full application. For further information, Rahel Hofer, HR manager, is at your disposal. Tel 031 370 79 79
