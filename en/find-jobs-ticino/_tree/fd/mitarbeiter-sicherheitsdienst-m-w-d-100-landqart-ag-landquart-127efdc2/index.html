Full description

The traditional Swiss values of quality, safety and reliability are combined with innovation. We are a supplier of banknote substrates and passport and visa documents in more than 50 countries around the world.

# Staff Security Service (m/f/d) 100% #

In this responsible position, you ensure plant protection and internal safety. In addition, you and your teammates are responsible for the operation of all security systems, guarding and accompanying services in uninterrupted shift operation.

Its responsibilities include

  • Monitoring compliance with safety requirements in terms of property protection, value safety, fire protection and occupational safety and health protection (ASGS)
  • Management of the alarm system and initiation of measures
  • Reporting of all deviations and assistance with corrective action
  • Access and personal controls and reception services
  • Member and support of the emergency alarm group in the event of an incident
  • Management of sample, acceptance and archive sheets
  • Control steps in value, occupational safety and fire protection
  • Assumption of safety logistical tasks

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic training EFZ completed
  • Medical training Level 1 IVR
  • Sound training in the security service
  • Secure handling of MS Office (especially Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Professional experience in a security-relevant environment
  • High sense of responsibility, role model, resilience, disciplined, assertiveness, teamwork
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • English skills A2, B1, additional languages beneficial
  • Readiness for 4 shift work and weekend services

We offer you

  • Responsible and varied activity in a systemically important area
  • Collaboration with experienced experts for value security, ASGS and fire protection
  • Attractive working conditions in a progressive industrial company
  • Exemplary shift plan with long-term predictability and good work-life balance
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation

Interested? Do you want to be in the middle of it instead of just being there? Then convince us today with your complete application dossier incl. Photo.

For more information, Markus Bischof, HR Business Partner (direct selection) is at your disposal.
