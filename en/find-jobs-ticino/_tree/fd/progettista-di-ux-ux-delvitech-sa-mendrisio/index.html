Full description

Design the industrial HMI and data-rich interfaces of Delvitech's AI inspection software, making complex workflows simple on the line. Job Summary Design the user experience and interfaces of Delvitech's AOI (Automatic Optical Inspection) software, making complex inspection workflows simple and efficient for machine operators, process engineers and our Application teams. The role goes beyond generic software design: it covers the industrial HMI used on production lines and the visualization of inspection data, working closely with our AI, software and Application Engineering teams and with real users in the field. Location: Mendrisio, Switzerland, 100% on-site. Reports to the Product Team lead. Key Responsibilities

  • Design the industrial HMI of our machines, with attention to operator ergonomics, touch screen use, response times and usability in production environments.
  • Design data-heavy interfaces: high-resolution images, heatmaps, quality KPIs, production statistics, defect classification and trend analysis.
  • Work with the AI and Application Engineering teams to represent defects, false positives, algorithm results, warnings and classification scores clearly.
  • Observe operators on production lines, take part in customer installations and training sessions, and collect real feedback during FAT and SAT.
  • Create wireframes, prototypes and high-fidelity UI designs.
  • Define and maintain a design system and UI guidelines.
  • Work closely with front-end and software developers through implementation.
  • Run usability tests and drive continuous improvement. Requirements
  • At least 3 years of experience as a UI/UX or Product Designer, with a portfolio.
  • Experience designing industrial HMI, SCADA or software for automatic machines and production environments.
  • Strong skills in data visualization and information design (dashboards, technical data and image visualization).
  • Proficiency in Figma or equivalent and in prototyping.
  • Willingness to work in the field with operators and customers.
  • Professional fluency in English. Nice to Have
  • Experience with B2B technical products or industrial software.
  • Basic front-end knowledge (HTML, CSS, React).
  • Experience designing interfaces for computer vision or AI-based products.
  • Italian or German. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
