Full description

Start on 1 January 2027 or by appointment as medical secretary: in paediatrics and adolescent medicine 30 - 30 %

The Department of Pediatrics and Adolescent Medicine is a professionally independent clinic. In addition to general pediatrics, including neonatology and intensive care, we offer a wide range of special consultation hours. To ensure that everything works smoothly, our stationary secretariat ensures that all entries and exits are carefully planned, coordinated and optimally coordinated.

Are you an organizational talent that values contact with our young patients, their family members and the medical profession? Then we have to get to know each other!

These are your tasks

  • You plan and coordinate the stationary entrances and exits and are the contact person for internal and external requests
  • You create, correct and send the medical correspondence
  • You perform your administrative tasks independently, including electronic archiving and scanning of medical records
  • You are actively involved in process improvements (lean management)

You bring that with you

  • You have an education as a doctor’s secretary: in or are an MPA with corresponding professional experience
  • You can quickly integrate into new tools thanks to your PC affinity
  • You work together in a structured, meticulous and reliable way in a team and communicate stylishly in German
  • Other languages are a plus
  • You are working on fixed days and are engaged with holiday and deputies

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!
