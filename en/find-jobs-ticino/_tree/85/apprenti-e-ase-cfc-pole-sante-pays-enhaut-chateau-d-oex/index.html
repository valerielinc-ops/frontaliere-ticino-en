Full description

Located at 1000 meters above sea level, the Country-D-Enhaut Health Centre offers a wide range of hospital services, accommodation, accompaniment for the elderly and home care. According to the so-called integrated care approach, this innovative organization represents an opportunity for health professionals to work in small multidisciplinary teams, for better continuity of care and in response to the health needs of the population of the Upper Country. In the heart of a privileged natural environment, less than an hour away from Lausanne or Bern, the Pays-d'Enhaut is renowned for its quality of life and offers a wide range of sports, leisure and cultural activities, to live summer and winter!

The Health Centre of the Country-d-Enhaut is looking for a

For its sector The Health Centre of the Upper Country is seeking support for:

Apprentices (Social and educational assistant)

During the three years of dual training, you participate in the sociocultural support and follow-up of the residents and beneficiaries CAT (Temporary Reception Centre) by offering individual or collective activities. Under the responsibility and delegation of the socio-educational assistants, you participate in the well-being and autonomy of the residents/beneficiaries through the organization of activities (celebrations, festivals, cultural and artistic events) involving families and relatives.

Your profile

  • Interest in the elderly and desire to accompany them on a daily basis;
  • Dynamism, reliability, discretion and availability;
  • Ability to work in a small team.

We offer

  • Possibility to develop knowledge and skills in different reception facilities (hospital, EMS, Day Centre) on an ad hoc basis thanks to the structure of the Health Centre of the Upper Country.
  • Regular support and supervision by trained staff;
  • A pleasant working atmosphere within a small, motivated team.
