System manager: traction current systems 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Person responsible for the system: traction current systems Brig, March 1, 2027 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. We ensure the safety and availability of the railway infrastructure by monitoring the condition of the electrical systems (traction current and associated systems), structures and access points. We take the necessary immediate and planned measures. The satisfaction of our customers is important to us. That's why we provide those responsible for the life cycle with relevant data to continuously improve the quality of the systems. We represent the interests of SBB when working near electrical systems, check the conformity of the systems during acceptance tests and arrange for defects to be rectified. We also examine projects with a view to optimal future maintenance and identify the technical causes of malfunctions. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • also for your family
  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • As the person responsible for the traction current systems, you ensure their safety and availability and represent the interests of the owner towards internal and external bodies. In the event of malfunctions, you identify the technical causes, especially in the area of ​​the switching elements.
  • You analyze and evaluate the results of various monitoring activities, such as diagnostic vehicles, local measurements and visual inspections, taking into account the existing risks. From this you derive the necessary maintenance measures, define quality requirements and deadlines and commission internal or external partners to implement them. If necessary, you trigger immediate measures.
  • You ensure that new systems meet the requirements and are free of relevant defects.

If necessary, you will carry out the SIOP-B inspection. As a client representative, you also represent the interests of SBB towards third parties such as private individuals, municipalities and authorities and ensure that the system data is updated.

  • You carry out maintenance and repair work professionally and ensure that any defects identified are corrected.
  • Depending on the system, you plan, coordinate and control the execution work. You provide technical management to the commissioned companies and ensure both the conformity of the work carried out and compliance with safety requirements. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are characterized by strong communication skills. You can give clear and precise instructions to SBB colleagues and external companies. Thanks to your technical experience, you can proceed systematically and safely when troubleshooting, especially in the area of ​​switching elements.
  • For this role, you have an EFZ in a technical area as well as experience in managing contact line installation work, for example as a foreman, construction manager or contract manager. You will complete the required certification courses as an expert in “contact lines” and “cables and cable protection” as part of your job.
  • Very good knowledge of the relevant maintenance guidelines, system components and hazardous situations as well as the corresponding construction and operating processes round off your professional profile.
  • You have very good knowledge of French or German (at least level C1) as well as good knowledge of the other language (ideally at least level B2).
  • You are experienced in dealing with the relevant IT systems such as DfA, SAP, AMGT and ZMON as well as with MS Office.
