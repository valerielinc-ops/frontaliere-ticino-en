Full description

As a Fusalp store manager, you are responsible for overseeing all store operations, from stock management to customer satisfaction. Your role is to ensure that our customers benefit from an exceptional in-store experience, offering personalized service and responding to their needs. In collaboration with your team, you promote the brand values, retain our customer base, and ensure that product presentation is impeccable and up-to-date, reflecting the quality and elegance of Fusalp. ## Responsibilities

  • Welcome and advise our local and international customers.
  • Enhance our collections through merchandising according to brand standards.
  • Follow key performance indicators of sales based on objectives.
  • Retain customers through retail sales and quality service.
  • Lead a sales team, recruitment, sales training and products, employee scheduling management.
  • Unite and motivate the team around a common goal, developing the skills of each team member.
  • Be proactive in marketing actions.
  • Participate in other operational tasks of the store (receiving goods, labeling, inventory, after-sales management, …)
  • The scope of these missions can evolve based on the needs of the company. ## Requirements
  • You are passionate about skiing, fashion, and luxury, you have a strong team spirit and will be a driving force for the team and sales.
  • You have at least 5-10 years of experience in team management in a high-end premium environment.
  • You have a developed sense of excellence in service and customer experience in the store.
  • You are dynamic, versatile, and have a keen sense of performance and detail. ## What we offer
  • Type of employment: full-time 39h
  • Salary: fixed according to profile and experience / variable on objectives
  • Language: French and English fluent
  • Good to know! Fusalp attributes equal importance to technical skills and soft skills in its recruitment. We are convinced that your personal qualities and human skills are crucial to strengthening team cohesion and ensuring a harmonious working environment.
