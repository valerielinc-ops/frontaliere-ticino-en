Put to the Point

They are happy to contribute to a well-coordinated future of mobility and space in Switzerland. You have good communication skills in Italian and a second official language (verbal and written) and want to become part of a dynamic and interdisciplinary team in a political environment.

  • Date of entry: 01.11.2026
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20445
  • Place of work: Worblentalstrasse 66, 3063 Ittigen

You can make this contribution

  • As a contact person for the Ticino agglomeration programs you work in the Italian-speaking area
  • Lead projects in transport and settlements to develop and assess agglomeration programmes
  • Further develop subjects within the highly dynamic program Agglomeration Traffic
  • Negotiating with project promoters and federal agencies
  • Write and present reports and arguments

This makes them unique.

  • a master’s degree or equivalent, preferably relating to transport, environment and space
  • Experience with new technologies is an advantage
  • First professional experience, preferably knowledge of political processes
  • Sense of the essentials, resilience, openness as well as pleasure in teamwork and high communication ability
  • Committed, innovative and loyal personality
  • Very good knowledge of Italian and at least good active knowledge (verbal and written) of a second official language and passive knowledge of the third official language

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Quality of life for today and tomorrow.

The Federal Office for Spatial Development (ARE) is committed to the sustainable development of Switzerland’s living space. It is part of the Federal Department of Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications (UVEK).

As an employee of the ARE, you have an eye on the Switzerland of today and tomorrow. They take responsibility in challenging projects that advance all regions and ensure the preservation of a high quality of life. The ARE offers you the opportunity to make a significant contribution to a sustainable Switzerland in a modern and flexible working environment.
