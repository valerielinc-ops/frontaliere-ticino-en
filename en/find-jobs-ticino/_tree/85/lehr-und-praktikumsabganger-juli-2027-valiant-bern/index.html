Full description

We are looking for you. You will soon complete your apprenticeship (apprenticeship or internship) and would like to continue working at Valiant Bank?

Your application dossier contains the following documents

  • CVC
  • Letter of motivation
  • All operational assessments (BKNW, education report, ALS, etc.)
  • All school certificates (vocational school or WMS/Gymnasium)
  • Possible further diplomas

Note: Valiant internal assessment forms and probationary time reports are voluntary.

Your CV contains, among other things

  • Personal data
  • Previous apprenticeships and learned activities
  • Professional strengths and weaknesses
  • Reference person (from the working environment)

Your motivation letter contains

  • Preferred activities or divisions
  • Employment level
  • Geographical flexibility
  • Training plans
  • Absences (military, travel, etc.)

What next?

  • Basic HR education reviews your dossier and gives you feedback on content and completeness.
  • Your dossier will be shared internally if there is a suitable position. In this case, information is sent to you.
  • If you see a job that interests you, contact HR basic education. Together with you, she decides on the next steps.
  • Ideally, you will find a suitable place for you. Otherwise, HR basic education will inform you personally about the further procedure.

About us Valiant is an independent Swiss financial services provider operating exclusively in Switzerland. It offers private customers and SMEs a comprehensive, easy-to-understand offer in all financial matters. Valiant is locally anchored in the following 15 cantons: Aargau, Basel Landscape, Basel City, Bern, Freiburg, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Vaud, Zug and Zurich. It is also present throughout Switzerland thanks to innovative, digital services. Valiant has a balance sheet total of 37.9 billion francs and employs around 1200 people – of which more than 70 are apprentices.
