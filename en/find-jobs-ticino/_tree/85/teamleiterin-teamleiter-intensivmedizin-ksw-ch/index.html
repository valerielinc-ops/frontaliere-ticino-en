Full description

Ready for the next career step with Impact? Our interdisciplinary Center for Intensive Medicine (ZIM) with integrated IMC station puts another one on it! After upgrading from 12 to 14 beds we start right now – soon it goes up to 17 beds. And we are looking for you – a motivated, committed personality with Bock on responsibility, teamwork and high-level car. What awaits you with us Welcome to a varied job with meaning, responsibility and a lot of team spirit. With us you can show what is inside you – learning and growing a lot. Tasks:

  • You take over the professional, organizational and personal leadership of a motivated care team – naturally in close cooperation with our management Care ZIM
  • Together, you provide the best possible care for our ZIM patients and patients from a wide range of disciplines
  • You keep the quality of care high – with heart, mind and eye for detail
  • Complex, rapidly changing situations? For you no stress – but daily business Profile:
  • You have an NDS in intensive care – without that, unfortunately, it is not possible
  • Leadership experience? Nice to have – but no need
  • Technology affinity, organizational skills and a structured way of working belong to your repertoire
  • You are communicative, team-oriented and brings positive vibes into interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Top & socially strong – a combination that counts with us Benefits: The KSW provides employees with conditions that enable them to meet their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, sliding working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and levels of employment adapted to different situations of life – this is all possible at the KSW if it permits the operation
  • Every year, the KSW offers 40 overtime and 50 professional training courses. We also support you in external developments in time and/or financial terms
  • In exceptional situations, e.g. longer illness, strong personal burden or difficult experiences in the private and in the everyday professional life, you have various conversation offers available. The valuable and friendly cooperation within the teams and across the departments is characterized by the working climate at the KSW. As a sign for a strong team idea, the "Du" was introduced as a standard at the KSW. In addition, the KSW supports annual team events financially and organizes a large staff festival for the whole house every four years; KSW employees benefit from very good social benefits. Thus, the pension funds solution with the BVK is far above the statutory minimum. Even in the event of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well secured
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training options and course offers, discounts in pharmacies and among the most popular health insurance companies. You can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health
  • Our La Luna daycare centre with generous opening hours is located right next to the KSW. You can also use the income-dependent, financial support provided by the KSW for the crib costs
  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theatre performances. The borrowing of media in the in-house library is also free for employees.
