Full description

We are looking for a motivated and detail-oriented professional to join our team in a dynamic and customer-focused environment. This role combines client interaction with financial analysis, offering the opportunity to support customers in identifying suitable solutions while ensuring accurate assessment of their financial capacity and risk profile.

Excellent knowledge of Italian and German minimum C1 level, both written and spoken, is required. Candidates who do not meet these language requirements will not be considered.

  • Collaborating with the team on case processing and monitoring

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
