Full description

The Kita Paradise is looking for 1 January 2027 or by arrangement limited to 30. September 2027 a motivated and committed personality.

  • You care individually and collectively within the framework of the pedagogical concept.
  • You promote self-reliance, language, motor and cognitive development, as well as integration into the group.
  • You can help shape a differentiated, binding pedagogical everyday life that gives the child security and enables the development of trust.
  • You take on independently delegated tasks, such as targeted observations, information sharing in the team, parents and professionals.
  • You guide learners in consultation with the vocational training manager of the daycare centre.
  • Completed training as a specialist Care EFZ, specialty children
  • Interest in education-oriented nursery education (BuLG, Pikler)
  • Joy of caring for young children (2 years to kindergarten entry)
  • Personal maturity, resilience and good organizational ability
  • Administrative and organizational skills as well as good PC knowledge

A challenging, varied challenge in a dynamic and pedagogically oriented environment, where working with the children is the focus. In our organization, we maintain open and transparent communication. We offer a flat hierarchy – embedded in the administration –, agile structures, self-responsibility, a learning culture and an appreciative approach in our organization. 80% is divided into 4 working days.

With around 2,200 employees, the Social Department of the City of Zurich works every day for a social Zurich. We are there for everyone and support children, young people and adults in different situations. We make an important contribution to social cohesion. And we contribute to a high quality of life in the neighbourhoods.

To accompany and support young children in their development in the important first years and thus give them a good foundation for their future life: we are committed to this with great commitment. In the city of Zurich, the childcare division of the social institutions and companies operates 11 daycare centres, a crisis intervention group and a residential group for infants, a family meeting place and at two locations "accompanied visiting meeting places" for families in conflictive separation situations.

The Paradise Kita in the Entlisberg Health Center in Wollishofen is located in the immediate vicinity of the forest. The nursery is surrounded by a garden with varied play opportunities and a small animal zoo on the site. Paradise daycare centre and Entlisberg health centre promote intergenerational contact between young and old.
