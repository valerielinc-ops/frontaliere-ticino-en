We are looking for talented service technicians to drive innovation and excellence at Otis Switzerland.

What to expect from us

You are responsible for the smooth operation of our facilities. This shall include the following tasks:

  • You perform regular maintenance and repair work on mechanical, hydraulic, electrical and electronic components of elevators and escalators
  • You independently identify repair and improvement work on the plants and order the necessary spare parts
  • You perform error searches with the help of modern tools and eliminate the disturbances

What we expect from you

  • You have a completed technical training such as electrician:in, electrician:in, polymechanic:in or similar. If you are already familiar with lifts, this is a great advantage.
  • You speak fluent German
  • You plan and coordinate your day independently and show a friendly attitude
  • We require a category B driving license, as well as a place of residence in the area of application so that you can comply with the pikett times

What we offer you

  • When you start with us, you will be accompanied by an experienced colleague who will give you valuable expertise on your way. In addition, you will be equipped with a mobile phone and your own company vehicle, which you can also use privately.
  • We care about your personal development. In concrete terms, this means that we support further training and internal promotion opportunities are open to everyone.
  • Good pension fund
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Food subsidy

Our company culture is our most important asset and is deeply rooted in the company and is lived and appreciated by all employees. More information about our «Otis Absolutes» can be found here.
