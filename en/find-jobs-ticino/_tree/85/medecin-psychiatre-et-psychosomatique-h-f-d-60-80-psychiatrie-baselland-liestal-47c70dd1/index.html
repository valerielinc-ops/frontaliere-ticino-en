Together.

For what really counts Psychiatric Psychosomatic Station UKBB

Child and adolescent psychiatry / UKBB

Do you want to co-manage a stationary department and participate in the further development of our dynamic clinic? For our psychosomatic department in the University Children's Hospital of Basel (UKBB), which is run together with the University Hospital for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, Baselstadt, we are looking for a senior physician.

Their tasks

  • Co-management of the Psychosomatic Station A3 together with two other senior physicians
  • Medical-therapeutic overall responsibility for the treatment of inpatients with a focus on eating disorders and their families
  • Depending on the level of employment participation in the outpatient psychosomatic consultation hours of the UKBB or at the KJP ambulatory in Binningen
  • Creation and further development of treatment concepts as well as responsibility for their implementation in cooperation with supervisors and the multi-professional treatment team
  • Planning & coordination of human resources deployment
  • Close cooperation with employees in other areas of child and adolescent psychiatry
  • Training of assistant doctors and assistant psychologists

Your profile

  • A specialist title in child and adolescent psychiatry (or equivalent specialist level) distinguishes you
  • Interest in psychosomatics, especially in the treatment of patients with an eating disorder
  • Working in multi-professional teams gives you pleasure; your quick comprehension, your independent working method and your responsible action convince you
  • Interest in milieutherapeutic and recovery-oriented work is a matter of course for you
  • Leadership skills and enthusiasm for the conceptual advancement of treatments are among your strengths

About us

The Psychiatry Baselland (PBL) is one of the leading psychiatric institutions in northwestern Switzerland. It offers comprehensive outpatient, daily clinical, home treatment and inpatient therapies for adults as well as children and adolescents. She supports people with mental and multiple disabilities in the areas of living, work and day-to-day planning (www.inclusioplus.ch). She also holds a stake in WorkMed AG, the Center for Work and Mental Health. At several locations in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, PBL employs around 1,300 people and treats, supports and advises more than 15,000 patients per year (www.pbl.ch).
