Full description

What awaits you with us

As a CV learner at SMG, you will get a comprehensive insight into the central commercial processes of a leading Swiss digital company. You are actively involved in HR, finance, reception and sales/marketing – accompanied by trained young talent coaches.

Diverse tasks that have an impact

You dive into four exciting areas and are in the middle of real work:

  • Reception/Office Management: Customer Reception, Organization & Communication
  • HR Operations: Working in HR dossiers, mutations, data maintenance
  • Payroll/Accounting: Support in the finance team, processing of receipts, data collection
  • Sales & Marketing: CRM maintenance, campaign support, content preparation

A supportive environment

You are not alone! You work with experienced professionals from different fields - on site and in the hybrid work model. We support you, give you space for your ideas and help you to grow and start right.

About us

SMG Swiss Marketplace Group AG is a pioneering network of online marketplaces and an innovative digital company that simplifies people’s lives with future-oriented products.

SMG Swiss Marketplace Group AG offers customers the best tools to help them make important life decisions. The portfolio includes real estate (ImmoScout24, Homegate, Flatfox, CASASOFT, IAZI), automobiles (AutoScout24, MotoScout24), general marketplaces (Ricardo, tutti, anibis) and finance & insurance (FinanceScout24, moneyland.ch).
