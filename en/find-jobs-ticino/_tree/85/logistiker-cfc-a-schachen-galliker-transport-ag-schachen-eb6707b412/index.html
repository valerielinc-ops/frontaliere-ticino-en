Logistician EFZ (a) Chess We are an initiative, modern and future-oriented transport and logistics company with headquarters in Schachen/LU. Thanks to our motivated staff and a high quality of service, we are on course for success! To strengthen our warehouse logistics teams, we are looking for a logistician immediately or by agreement (a) Task area

  • Versatile use within logistics with focus on incoming goods inspection and shipment provision
  • Control and collection of item master data
  • Detailed control, recording and recording of receipts of goods
  • Communication with customers by phone and email
  • Compression and provision of customer deliveries
  • Provision of consignments to geographical loading zones

Requirement profile

  • Completed basic training as a logistician EFZ or several years of professional experience in the field of goods receipt
  • Strong quality awareness coupled with high efficiency
  • Very good handling of all types of trucks of category R and/or S
  • Good PC user knowledge (MS Office, LFS from Erhardt+Partner is an advantage)
  • Good physical constitution
  • Team player with a distinct service character
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German

Offer We offer you a challenging, versatile activity. In addition to a modern infrastructure, free parking spaces are available. Of course, you can look forward to a motivated and competent team that will actively support you in all areas. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application.
