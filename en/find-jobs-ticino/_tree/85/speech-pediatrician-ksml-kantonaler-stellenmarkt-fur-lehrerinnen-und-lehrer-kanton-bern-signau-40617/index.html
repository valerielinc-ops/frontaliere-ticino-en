Full description

Portrait The municipality of Signau is nestled in the hilly landscape of the upper Emmental. It is easy to reach by public transport.

Tasks

As a specialist in our speech pediatrics team, from 01.02.2026 or n.V. you will support learners Z1-3 in single and/or small group setting in the linguistic/logopaedic area. Your workload is at least 6L and can be extended to 100% at will in the new school year.

Its tasks shall include in particular

  • Clarification of the therapy needs of individual students
  • Therapy and accompaniment of students
  • Advice for parents and teachers
  • Preventive activities where possible
  • interdisciplinary cooperation with the MR team, the school management and corresponding specialist departments
  • Cooperation with colleagues at our school within the framework of the vocational assignment

Requirements

Bachelor of Arts Speech Therapy, EDK- recognised diploma for Speech Therapy or in the last year of training. They are an eloquent, humorous, dutiful, interested and motivated personality and are accustomed to dealing with children and parents.

We offer

+ Permanent employment with fees from 6L (option up to 100% per). 1.08.27 + Group rooms furnished + an exciting field of work with a high level of personal responsibility + Good cooperation and acceptance from parents + Appropriate participation in meetings/conferences + a lot of support from MR and school management + Concentration on the core business of therapy and cooperation with teachers and parents + Exchange/ Collaboration with experienced speaker on site + Possibility of a mentorship for new professionals
