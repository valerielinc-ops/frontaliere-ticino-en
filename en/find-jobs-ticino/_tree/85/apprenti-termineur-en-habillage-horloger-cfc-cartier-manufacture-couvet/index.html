WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

Missions

From the beginning of August 2027, you will train for 3 years to obtain your Federal Certificate of Terminator Capacity in watchmaking dressing. During your apprenticeship, you will develop your skills in the field of finishing and decoration of watch components. You will learn to perform different finishing operations on dressing parts.

Responsibilities

· Perform different finishing and finishing operations on watch components · Perform polishing, satining and vibrating operations according to defined requirements · Make decorations and finishes · Read and interpret technical plans and documents · Quality control · Identify any defects and make necessary adjustments · Collaborate with different departments

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE · A good level of French in both oral and written language enabling successful vocational training · Interest in watchmaking, precision mechanics and crafts · Manual dexterity, excellent thoroughness and great attention to detail · Faculty of concentration, patience and aesthetic sensitivity · Autonomy and rigor · Team spirit and relationship ease
