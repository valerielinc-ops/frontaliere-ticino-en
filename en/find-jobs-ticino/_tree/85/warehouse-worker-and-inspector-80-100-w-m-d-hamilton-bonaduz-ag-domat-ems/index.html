Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

The Operations business unit produces parts for all Hamilton companies, from injection moulded parts to pipette tips for liquid handling to metal and plastic parts. Our goal is to produce as many parts as possible in-house. In this way, we remain agile, independent and efficient.

You can do that

  • Compliance with requirements and guidelines in accordance with internal regulations
  • Testing of delivered goods with the Babtec test software, according to the specifications in the test plan
  • Support of the team in the receipt of goods, on the ramp and in other areas to ensure a smooth process in the MFG IGI
  • Reporting errors in processes
  • Co-assessment of the quality of suppliers
  • Ensuring the shelf life of articles by labelling, repackaging, etc.
  • Ensure a fast and clean operation to minimize the throughput times of the articles
  • Implementation of various forklift operations
