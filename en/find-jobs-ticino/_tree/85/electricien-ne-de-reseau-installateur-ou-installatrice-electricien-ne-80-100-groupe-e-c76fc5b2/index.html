Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our clients realize their projects by providing global, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether for the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. An exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2,600 employees, including 245 apprentices, as well as the balance between private and professional life.

The Electricity Distribution Department builds, maintains and operates the electricity distribution network. Thanks to the development of innovative offers, it ensures technological control of projects such as the creation of the smart network or public lighting.

Mission

  • Carry out all construction and maintenance work on overhead and underground MV/LV distribution networks
  • Build and maintain public lighting (EP)
  • Troubleshoot networks
  • Participate in on-call service (approximately 1 week out of 6)
  • Apply and respect safety regulations and construction standards

Profile

  • CFC network electrician or equivalent training
  • Knowledge of distribution network equipment and training on live work
  • Good oral and written knowledge of French
  • Holder of a driving license (cat. B), the license (Cat. C1) is an asset

What we offer you

  • An evolving environment favorable to balance between professional and private life
  • The social benefits of a modern company, fair treatment, professional development opportunities
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: “Together, we move towards success”
