The Position Business Developer – Diagnostics (South region)

The Opportunity

In this high impact in-field hybrid role, you will be the driving force behind expanding Roche’s market footprint and maximizing the value of our Core Lab portfolio. Combining commercial vision with technical expertise, you will act as a strategic market builder and a trusted partner to laboratories across your assigned region.

Your key responsibilities will focus on

  • Market Expansion & Account Growth: Identify new business opportunities, execute strategic plans to target and acquire key accounts, and evaluate lead profitability. Prospect, qualify, negotiate, and close complex commercial deals to secure long-term sustainability and meet ambitious growth targets.
  • Technical & Scientific Excellence: Carry out system installations, configurations, parameterizations, software updates, and application troubleshooting with high technical competence. Lead system evaluations, customer demonstrations, and comprehensive training sessions.
  • Solution Selling & Value Proposition: Promote Roche’s Core Lab, digital, and service solutions, demonstrating their clinical and operational value to customers. Prepare content for tenders, deliver technical presentations, and share up-to-date scientific insights.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with internal teams—including other commercial lead roles, marketing, medical, service, and finance—to deliver cohesive customer experiences, map key decision-makers, and gather competitive market insights.

Who You Are

You are an entrepreneurial, resilient commercial visionary and a technical problem solver who thrives in a collaborative, fast-paced environment. As a strategic thinker and a resilient dealmaker, you are focused on creating a robust sales pipeline that guarantees our long-term success.

  • Education: Master’s degree and/or PhD in Life Sciences (or equivalent), engineering, or technical disciplines.
  • Experience & Expertise: Proven commercial track record of profitable client acquisition, high-value contract negotiation, and sales pipeline management in the IVD diagnostic healthcare market. Prior knowledge of Core Lab products, digital solutions, and technical/application support is a strong plus, though not a mandatory prerequisite.
  • Communication: Outstanding solution-selling, communication, and presentation skills in both Portuguese and English.
  • Commercial Drive & Agility: Growth-minded, proactive, and customer-focused with strong decision making and sense of urgency to deliver exceptional results. You bring sharp commercial acumen and an entrepreneurial spirit to uncover new opportunities and co-create sustainable value.
  • Collaboration & Relationship Building: A team-oriented professional with outstanding interpersonal and communication skills, adept at cultivating strong, trust-based relationships across and beyond the organization.
  • Territory Coverage: This role targets strategic accounts throughout the South region. Familiarity with the regional market landscape will be a plus.
