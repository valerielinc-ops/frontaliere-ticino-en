Full description

Tasks

As a specialist, you shape and coordinate everyday life in your area of competence and take on the responsibility for holistic, individual care and care of our residents with dementia: inside. The interdisciplinary cooperation is just as self-evident for you as the implementation of nursing measures, which you implement with high professional competence and empathy. You recognize changes in the state of the inhabitants: inside early and take the necessary steps to support. You actively involve relatives and trusted persons in the care process and maintain a professional and sensitive approach. Depending on requirements, you take on other areas of responsibility such as day-to-day responsibility in shift operations.

As a result of the replacement of the Domicil Bethlehemacker, your place of work will be in Bern-Bethlehem until the end of April 2027 and in Ostermundigen from May 2027. As part of the transition location, you will be faced with varied and instructive tasks with co-creation.

Whether experienced personality or ambitious young talent – we look forward to your application!

Requirements

  • Tertiary education (FH, HF, DNII, ACP, PsyKP, KWS)
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Open and constructive personality
  • Service and customer orientation
  • Conscientious working method

We offer

  • Competent, committed and collaborative team
  • Versatile and demanding work in an exciting and dynamic environment

Domicil offers senior citizens stationary services in care and care. With more than 20 locations and around 1,600 employees, Domicil is the number 1 in long-term care in the canton of Bern. Under the umbrella of Concara, we are working together with Spitex Bern for integrated all-round care.

Domicil Centre of Competence Dementia Bethlehemacker is designed to provide people with dementia with an emotionally experienceable living space.
