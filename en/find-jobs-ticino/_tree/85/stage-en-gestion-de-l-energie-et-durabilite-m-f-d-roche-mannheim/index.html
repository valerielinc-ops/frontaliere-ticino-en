Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position

You want to work on solutions that really make a difference – for people, the environment and the future? Then become part of our energy management team at the leading biotech site in Penzberg and help to achieve our ambitious sustainability goals. Here you will be involved in the implementation of our decarbonisation strategy, the optimisation of energy consumption and the further development of innovative supply systems – directly at the interface of technology, sustainability and industry. With your work, you support the team that is responsible for the Penzberg plant as a central service provider for the energy and media infrastructure.

Who we are

Roche is a global leader in pharmaceuticals and diagnostics with over 100,000 employees worldwide, improving people’s quality of life through innovation and sustainability. At our location in Penzberg near Munich – one of the largest biotech centers in Europe – more than 7,500 employees are working on innovative solutions for better healthcare. In our Energy Management & Sustainability department, we are working to minimize the ecological footprint of our production site – through technical innovations, intelligent energy monitoring systems and concrete CO2 reduction measures.

Your tasks

  • You develop smart energy KPIs and dashboards to visualize consumption in order to identify optimization potential.
  • You support the further development of our energy monitoring system and improve the data quality.
  • You support energy billing for transparent data determination and develop ideas for internal process optimization.
  • You will help to further develop our communication strategy in the field of energy & sustainability and create presentations for management.
  • You support the further development of the decarbonization strategy and track the progress of our projects on the basis of the CO2 roadmap.
  • You independently take on small sub-projects and implement your ideas directly.

This is what you bring with you

  • Studies: You are studying in the fields of energy, environmental, industrial engineering, mechanical engineering, supply engineering or a comparable degree program and are enrolled for the duration of the internship.
  • Enthusiasm: You bring passion for the topics of sustainability and technology.
  • Languages: You have very good language skills in German in word and writing and can safely use them in everyday work.

Duration of the internship: max. 6 months Start: from October 2026
