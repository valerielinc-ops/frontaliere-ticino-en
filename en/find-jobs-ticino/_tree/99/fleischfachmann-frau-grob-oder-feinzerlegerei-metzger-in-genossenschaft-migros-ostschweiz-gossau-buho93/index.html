Full description

Would you like to carry out the butcher craft in its versatility and with passion? We value real teamwork and further develop our employees. We look forward to your application as a meat specialist in our coarse or fine cutter shop – you decide what suits you more.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, Gossau headquarters, Industriestrasse 47, 9200 Gossau

Mansioni

  • Ground-breaking plant: professional boning and dressing of all cuts of the bovine, calf and pig genus
  • Fine cutting: preparation and portioning of meat for packaging or specialty production
  • Compliance with hygiene rules
  • If suitable, possibility for further development (e.g. professional or personnel management)

Requirements

  • Professional experience: as a meat specialist*woman beneficial
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): as meat specialist*woman
  • Team-capable, motivated and reliable personality
  • Efficient and clean work
  • Flexibility and mobility (start of work from 5:00 am)
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Targeted promotion: Promotion of our employees with targeted personnel development measures
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Food: 2 staff restaurants at the location
  • Mobility: Various advantages, e.g. when refuelling and renting vehicles
