Full description

Portrait Around 1200 children and young people are taught in 11 kindergarten classes, 32 primary classes, 14 secondary school classes, 2 KbFs and an IDK. The school's educational offer is supplemented by a day school and school social work.

Responsibilities

First of all, they are optimistic and convinced that support supports the development and opportunities of children. They see a way, an approach, they get down to business. They encourage, they test, they advise, they motivate. They have tips or ideas on how and what else could be tackled. With this perspective, they support children and young people with special needs. They are part of the committed and motivated team, work with them, but also like to sit comfortably with the team in the teacher's room.

Requirements

You think in a curative education and support-oriented way. You are a trained remedial teacher (or teacher in training as a remedial teacher or willing to continue your education in this direction). You understand learning, the basics and basics of mathematics and language. You like to exchange ideas in the MR team.

  • -From 1 February, an additional 7 language courses (IF, cycles 1 and 2)
  • -The 22 lessons can also be taken over as part of it.
