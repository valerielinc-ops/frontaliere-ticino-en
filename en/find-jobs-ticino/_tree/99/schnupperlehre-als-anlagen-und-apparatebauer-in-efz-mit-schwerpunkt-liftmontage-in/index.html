We are AS Elevators

As the second largest elevator organisation in Switzerland with 800 employees and over 30 apprentices, we combine the individuality of an SME with the perspectives and certainties of a large company. AS offers you the perfect start to your professional life. With us you will receive a well-founded, varied education with professional support. We offer talented graduates exciting further education and career opportunities after teaching.

We offer you one in our company

Schnupperlehre as plant and apparatus manufacturer*in EFZ with a focus on lift assembly in Küssnacht a. R.

What are your tasks?

  • 2 years of training in the workshop and 2 years of lift assembly
  • Working with your hands
  • Precise production of metal components and orders using various techniques
  • Installation and commissioning of lifts from A to Z: from empty concrete shaft to moving lift

In this video you get an insight into everyday work

What should you bring?

  • Craftsmanship and no fear of heights
  • Spatial imagination
  • Enjoy assembly work on construction sites
  • Responsibility, independence and reliability
  • Good to very good grades, especially in mathematical subjects

Your advantages with us

  • Above average number of holidays and J&S compensation
  • Financial share in the car inspection & own business vehicle
  • Financial advantage for public transport subscriptions
  • Sports and fitness compensation at selected providers
  • ... more benefits can be found here
