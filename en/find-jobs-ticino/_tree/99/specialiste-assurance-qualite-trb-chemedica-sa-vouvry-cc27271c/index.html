TRB Chemedica, a Swiss international pharmaceutical group active in more than 60 countries, has been a major player in the production and distribution of reference therapies for the treatment of ophthalmological, rheumatological and neurological disorders for 40 years. A human-sized company, TRB develops a culture where autonomy, trust and creativity are a true foundation for professional development. The organization of work in project mode allows talent to develop transversal skills, thereby promoting versatility and internal promotion. The Vouvry site specializes in the production of rheumatic injectables and a neurological active substance. It meets the highest requirements through its certification to GMP and ISO 13485\\. \\

As such, you manage several key processes, including Non\\-Conformities (NC), Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA), Change Controls (CC), Risk Management and Internal Audits. You ensure their effectiveness, timeliness and relevance of the associated performance indicators. Thanks to your quality expertise and your mastery of electronic tools for document management and quality, you are actively involved in modelling and optimizing workflows in the eQMS Ennov electronic system to support users and improve process efficiency. You track quality indicators (KPIs), analyze process performance, prepare quality reviews and maintain associated documentation. You also participate in quality activities related to reviewing batch files in the release process. Finally, you have a back\\-up role on several strategic quality processes, including document management, training management and vendor management. \\
