Working here is to accompany business travelers, diplomats and tourists who are waiting for professionalism and reliable service. Whether at reception, in the organization of conferences, in the restoration or in the housekeeping, the team ensures that each stay takes place in an efficient, pleasant and welcoming way. The culinary offer includes several restaurants offering international and regional cuisine, including a fine dining offer, as well as an elegant bar. This establishment combines the internationality of a global city with a modern working environment. Position DescriptionThe Sub-Chief Banquet is responsible for the organization, production and quality of culinary services for events (banking, weddings, seminars, receptions, cocktails, conferences, etc.). It ensures that quality standards, deadlines, costs and standards of hygiene and food safety (HACCP) are met.
