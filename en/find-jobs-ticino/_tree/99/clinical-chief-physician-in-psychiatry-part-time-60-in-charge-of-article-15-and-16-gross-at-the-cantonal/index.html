Full description

Class and annual salary (including 13th monthly)

Special Contract

Tasks

  • decides and manages the individual therapeutic plan for the patient according to the diagnostic and pharmacotherapeutic framework ensuring, in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team, the correct realization of the taking on the patient, as from protocol “medical responsibility and delegation”
  • ensures and performs specialist medical-psychiatric visits, where necessary in collaboration with psychologists, patients ensuring the correct diagnostic, psychopharmaceutical and rehabilitation framework
  • actively collaborates with physicians of psychiatric and somatic hospitals and doctors with private practice, in accordance with the medical chief OSC service. In relation to the somatic care necessary for the welfare of the patient organizes and co-manages the planned somatic care
  • ensures the FMH training of assistant doctors and other public or public professional figures. It is required to update its professional knowledge in compliance with the provisions of FMH for the maintenance of the specialty title
  • verify, in accordance with the doctor psichiatra added/a and/or the doctor psichiatra chief OSC service, that the ideal preconditions for network intervention on the territory in collaboration with the public and private structures involved
  • secures the pad on the territory and/or the guards in Cantonal psychiatric clinic (CPC) as provided by the “Regulation of OSC medical guards and spikes”
  • he/she has been entrusted to him/her and supervised by a medical psychiatrist head of service
  • guarantees the correct preparation of the TARDOC performance

Requirements

  • Medical diploma (or equivalent title)
  • FMH specialist title in psychiatry and psychotherapy (or equivalent title)
  • free exercise in Canton Ticino of the profession of doctor issued by the Health Office
  • medical-therapeutic work experiences in sociopsychiatric equipes
  • aptitude for group work
  • perfect mastery of the Italian language, knowledge of the English language and/or a second official language
  • knowledge of the territory and the network of sociopsychiatric assistance of the Canton Ticino

Special remarks

  • Degree of employment: part-time (60%) - in charge (Article 15 and 16 of the GROSS)
  • service office: Bellinzona
  • availability by the candidate/to work on irregular shifts
  • start date: to be agreed
  • the candidate not yet in possession of the authorization to free exercise in the Canton Ticino of the profession of doctor, the certificate of linguistic knowledge (Italian-B2) and, in case of foreign qualifications, the recognition issued by the Commission of medical professions Mebeko both of the diploma of doctor and the title of specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy (or equivalent titles), must obtain all the requirements within the beginning as established during the selection for the specific position. Failure to obtain the requirements for the assignment entails automatic decay.
  • the actual start of the activity of the chosen candidate/a is subject to the release of the authorisation to free exercise in the Canton Ticino of the profession of doctor issued by the Health Office of the Canton Ticino
  • further use: part-time (20%) at EOC/CREOC with Faido

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required additional documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
