Full description

Being able to continue operating the Lötschberg Base Tunnel while, just a few metres away, the second tube is being excavated and fitted out places the highest demands on planning, construction and safety. As Team Leader, Structural Works, together with your 2 direct reports, you will represent BLS's interests and play an active role in one of Switzerland's most challenging rail infrastructure projects.

What awaits you

  • Lead the detailed design for the structural works package and guide your project team and the external project team through the challenging project phase
  • Coordinate the interfaces with the existing tunnel facility, ongoing rail operations and railway systems
  • Manage the construction of the structural works in close cooperation with construction management and the contractors carrying out the work
  • Ensure that requirements are met and permits are complied with, construction proceeds according to plan, the required documentation is provided and the facilities are handed over for subsequent fit-out
  • Coordinate closely with the Alptransit team, BLS's specialist departments and other project stakeholders

What you bring

  • Completed degree in civil engineering from a university of applied sciences (FH) or ETH, ideally supplemented by further education in business administration
  • Several years' experience in the design and construction of tunnel structures, preferably on larger rail infrastructure projects
  • Sound knowledge of public procurement
  • Strong understanding of complex technical interrelationships and a structured, goal-oriented approach to work
  • Strong communication and negotiation skills, perseverance and enthusiasm for working with specialists from different disciplines
  • Negotiation-level German proficiency

Your benefits – what you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2. Class, a GA 1. Class or half of ataxabo
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether working from home or in the office
  • Organise your working hours freely. We support part-time work, top sharing and job sharing
  • You have the option of purchasing an additional five to ten days of holiday per year and taking a 40-working-day sabbatical every seven years.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.
  • Financial support for external continuing education, a leadership development programme and an exciting internal learning and course offering are available to you.

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second-largest railway company. What's more: We are also a shipping company, a real estate firm, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
