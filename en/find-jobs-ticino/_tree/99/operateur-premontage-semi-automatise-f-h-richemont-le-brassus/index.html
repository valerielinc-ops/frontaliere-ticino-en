Full description

Reference code: JR134864

Found in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the oldest watch Manufacture in continuous production for more than 270 years, actually perpetuating a proud heritage of watchmaking excellence and stylistic sophistication through generations of master craftsmen.

At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timespieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches.

FOR THOSE AND THOSE WHO RESEARCH EXCELLENCE

Join one of the iconic houses of high watchmaking, where excellence is a constant requirement. Take part in our collective success and the creation of our future heritage.

YOUR REASON TO BE IN THE HOUSE

We are looking for a semi-automated (M/F) pre-assembly operator.

At the heart of our production environment, you play a key role in the pre-assembly operations of our watch components.

By piloting semi-automated equipment, you ensure their setting and programming in order to ensure strict adherence to our quality standards and deadlines.

YOUR ROLE TO PLAY

Your role will be to

Assembly & Production

  • Perform pre-assembly of components according to operating ranges and work instructions.
  • Pilot, monitor and fluidize production cycles.
  • Adjusting and optimizing equipment: Programming, setting and adjusting semi-automated equipment according to the products and operations to be performed. Make the necessary adjustments and adjustments to the production process. Identify and quickly resolve technical uncertainties related to production settings and parameters. Propose and implement improvements to optimize process quality, reliability and productivity.
  • Control and quality: Perform visual and functional controls of pre-assembled components. Ensure the conformity of assemblies according to the defined criteria.

QUALITIES FOR EXCELLING

You benefit from a first successful experience in preassembly of watch components and enjoy ease in setting semi-automated means and reading planes.

You are recognized for your very good dexterity and accuracy,

your experienced eye in aesthetic control and your sense of detail

You have a high capacity for concentration and rigour to perform challenging and demanding tasks.

Your team spirit translates into sharing your know-how, listening to your colleagues and an active contribution to a strong and respectful team dynamic.

Vacheron Constantin celebrated 270 years as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755. Based in Geneva, Switzerland, our House operates a worldwide network of nearly 200 outlets in 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional community – watchmakers, artisans and professionals – shares the same passion for our legacy and ambition to build an even brighter future.

Ready to be part of our story? We will be delighted to discover your application.
