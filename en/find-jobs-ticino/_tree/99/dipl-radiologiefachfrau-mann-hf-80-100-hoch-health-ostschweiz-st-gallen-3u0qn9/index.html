Full description

Dipl. Radiology specialist HF 80-100 %

Clinic for Radiology and Nuclear Medicine

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Independent work in the fields of computed tomography and conventional radiology covering the entire spectrum of investigations
  • The third subject area either magnetic resonance imaging or interventional radiology (coverage of all subject areas possible on request)
  • Autonomous work on radiological devices of the latest generation, including intraoperative MRI and EOS
  • Individual promotion of one’s own competencies on the basis of a structured career and development model

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Radiology specialist HF with recognition in Switzerland or SRK-Precheck
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German, mind. Level B2
  • Motivated, reliable and competent personality
  • Flexible thinking and acting as well as good communication skills
  • Willingness to change and innovation
  • High social competence and high quality awareness

Our offer to you

  • 6 weeks of annual holiday time (23 days + 7 days of fixed planned compensation)
  • Collaboration in a collegial and permanent team with regular team events
  • Direct influence on everyday work through grassroots leadership and regular team meetings
  • Personally adapted and systematic induction
  • Interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation in one of the largest radiologies in Switzerland with new premises and state-of-the-art equipment park
  • Attractive discounts in the HOCH Health East Switzerland network including staff offers and a modern rooftop restaurant for relaxed breaks with a view
  • Attractive mobility benefits with a highly discounted East Wind annual subscription

Your application

  • Entry by arrangement
  • You can find out more about this exciting position from the leading radiology specialists in St. Gallen, telephone +41 71 494 66 96, e-mail: radiologie.mtra-leitungsg@h-och.ch
  • For questions about the application process, please contact Brigitte Kappler, HR Business Partner, telephone +41 71 494 68 65
