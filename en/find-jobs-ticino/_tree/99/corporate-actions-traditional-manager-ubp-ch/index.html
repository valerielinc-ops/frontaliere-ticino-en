Full description

  • Mission

Lead and oversee a team responsible for the accurate and timely processing of mandatory and voluntary corporate actions affecting equities, bonds, and mutual funds.

  • Main responsibilities
  • Perform daily activities and supervise workloads, controls, and service-level commitments.
  • Manage the full lifecycle of corporate actions, including:

o Review and validation of information from custodians, agents, and market-data providers. o Reconciliation of holdings and determination of entitlements. o Management of pending positions and external instructions. o Client and Relationship Manager notifications. o Transmission of instructions to correspondents, transfer agents, and administrators. o Booking and reconciliation of cash and in-kind proceeds.

  • Monitor outstanding issues and ensure timely resolution and escalation.
  • Ensure compliance with procedures, controls, regulations, and client SLAs.
  • Identify operational risks and recommend mitigation measures.
  • Communicate effectively with clients, internal departments, senior management, custodians, and external providers.
  • Contribute to projects, process improvements, and industry initiatives.
  • Manage, mentor, and develop the corporate actions processing team.
  • Your Profile
  • Proven experience in corporate actions, custody, asset servicing, fund administration, or investment operations.
  • Strong knowledge of corporate actions involving equities, bonds and mutual funds.
  • Previous experience managing or supervising an operational team.
  • Good understanding of reconciliations, entitlements, settlements, bookings, and instruction management.
  • Strong awareness of operational risk, controls, and regulatory requirements.
  • Excellent analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder-management abilities.
  • Client-focused, proactive, adaptable, and able to work effectively under pressure.
  • High attention to detail and commitment to accuracy and quality.
  • Ability to challenge constructively, propose solutions, and continuously improve processes.
  • Others
  • Swiss Residence
