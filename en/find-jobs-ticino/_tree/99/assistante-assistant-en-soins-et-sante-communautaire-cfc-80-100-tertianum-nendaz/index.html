Full description

The Tertianum Group is committed to the quality of life of older people, ensuring that they have comfortable residences. As a leading company in the Swiss market, we aim to give seniors a life that is worth living every day, thanks to our approximately 6400 collaborators, so that they can fully enjoy life. Thanks to our personalized services, we create a new home at all stages of old age and at nearly 100 sites that is conducive to a safe and independent life. Our daily motivation lies in our respect and sense of responsibility towards the elderly.

You want not only to improve the quality of life of others, but also to give meaning to your professional activity? Do you see opportunities to bring your skills and experience to us to have a positive impact on the lives of our residents? Then you're at the right address!

For Tertianum les Vergers in the region of the Central Valais in Aproz we are looking for immediately or to agree one/one

Community Care Assistant SWC from 80% to 100%

Your tasks

  • Guidance and support of assigned hosts
  • Support for body care, movement and other daily activities.
  • Responsibility for tasks as reference person
  • Participation in care reports and transmission of important observations
  • Writing care notes in our CareCoach care documentation system (possible for mobility with the CareCoach smartphone)

Your profile

  • ASSC training completed
  • Professional experience in the care of several years is an asset
  • A warm attitude
  • Pleasure meeting various challenges
  • Good knowledge of French spoken and written
