Full description

Pre-SUPSI - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite Ticino hospital is present with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 5,000 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC provides quality healthcare to 380,000 patients per year. The following position is addressed to candidates wishing to apply for a spontaneous pre-SUPSI training course at the EOC Rehabilitation Clinic (CREOC) in Faido Profile The pre-SUPSI internship is required by the Italian University School of Switzerland and is aimed at students wishing to pursue a Bachelor in nursing or physiotherapy

  • Requirements to be enrolled and attend the complementary modules of the Specialised School for Health and Social Professions (SSPSS) predisposition to human contact, motivation to work with patients, communication skills and flexibility excellent knowledge of the Italian language spoken and written and possibly of a second national language indispensable knowledge of the main IT means (Office package). Note The applications of those who do not have the above requirements will not be taken into account in any case and consequently will be deleted from our database. Applications will be assessed only in case of service requirements. As a position for spontaneous applications, telephone information is not released. EOC - our hospital Interested?
