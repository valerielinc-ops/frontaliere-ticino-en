Full description

Based in Plan-les-Ouates, attached to the Head of Service and linked to the business managers, you will be responsible for the following tasks:

Aufgaben

  • You apply your technical and architectural knowledge while applying the HVAC regulations;
  • You make drawings and drawings and provide the dimensioning of the work or component;
  • You draw up directly usable mount execution plans on site
  • You make manufacturing plans
  • You conduct on-site surveys;
  • You work closely with the business manager;
  • You participate in the orders of the distribution networks with the various stakeholders.

Anforderungen

  • You apply the regulations and follow the procedures related to HVAC installations,
  • You master the AutoCAD and REVIT drawing tools,
  • You know how to read and understand a schematic,
  • You are particularly attentive to respect for the guidelines,
  • You are rigorous and objective in your analysis of the information,
  • You are recognized for your excellent ability to listen and understand needs.

As the leading provider of global solutions for building and energy in Switzerland, we support the transformation and digitisation of our customers and work together on the energy transition.

Our services cover the entire life cycle of buildings, sites, infrastructure and neighbourhoods. As a market leader, our customers benefit from a full range of services from a single point of contact as well as solutions to their projects developed in an integrated and team-based manner.

We have a presence throughout Switzerland and employ 6500 people spread over 140 sites. This enables us to effectively implement both regional and country-wide projects.

In addition to building automation and technology as well as Facility Management, our offer also includes specializations such as energy supply, transport, telecommunications, digitisation and energy optimization. We design, build and implement projects that extend from intelligent buildings to future power plants through tunnels.
