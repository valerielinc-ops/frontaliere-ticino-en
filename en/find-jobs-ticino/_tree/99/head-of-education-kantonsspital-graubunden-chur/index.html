Full description

We are continuously looking for medical student assistants 100 - 100

In the Departments of Surgery, Internal Medicine, Paediatrics, the Women’s Clinic, the Institutes and ANIR (Anaesthesia, Emergency Medicine, Intensive Care Medicine, Rescue Services), more than 100 female and male students complete their practical training each year (block students, elective study year, PJ term).

Training placements are allocated in advance for a period of one, two, three or four months. They start on the first day and always end on the last day of a month. In addition to practical work, participants can attend weekly continuing-education courses.

Here you will find further information about block students and medical student assistants. The departments listed above offer positions for medical student assistants. The relevant contact persons for your application are listed below. They will also be happy to answer any questions you may have in advance.

Can you do something others cannot? Do you make the difference? Then you belong with us!

And this is what we offer you

  • Flexible working hours / home office / part-time work: If operational requirements permit, working hours and workloads can be flexibly tailored to your current life situation.
  • Catering options: All four sites of Kantonsspital Graubünden offer a wide range of catering options. With us, you can enjoy a varied, balanced and healthy diet.
  • Health: The health of our employees is important to us: In November 2022, Kantonsspital Graubünden was awarded the Friendy Work Space quality label.
  • Staff accommodation: Depending on availability, we provide our employees, interns and trainees with staff rooms and apartments at reduced rates.
  • Our values: The outstanding quality of our services and the health and satisfaction of our employees are equally important to us. An open, appreciative corporate culture, diversity, tolerance and sustainability are just as firmly embedded in our philosophy as progressive employment conditions and diverse development opportunities.
