Full description

Portrait The Salome Brunner Foundation supports around 300 children and adolescents with language development disorders and learning difficulties. With our various schools, we see ourselves as an important part of the educational landscape of the Canton of Bern.

The Language Healing School Wabern is a special elementary school for children and adolescents with a severe language impairment as well as a reduction in intelligence and comprises twenty-one classes from the basic level up to the vocational preparation school year. In our day school and boarding school, children receive socio-educational care. If the lessons continue in the afternoon, all the students eat: in small groups in their lunch rooms. Here they enjoy balanced, freshly prepared meals from our kitchen in a quiet atmosphere.
