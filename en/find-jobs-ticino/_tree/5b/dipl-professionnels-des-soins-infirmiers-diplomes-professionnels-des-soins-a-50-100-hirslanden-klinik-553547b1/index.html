As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

For our interdisciplinary surgical bed department with a focus on cardiac, thoracic and visceral surgery, we are looking for a dedicated Dipl. nurse.

Your benefits to us

  • Work-life balance: Look forward to scheduled working hours without night or shared services. So there is enough time for your hobbies, family and personal time out
  • At eye level and with each other: we support each other, meet with appreciation and do not lose our humor even in stressful times
  • Together it's more fun! You can expect a young, open-minded team with a lot of cohesion, joint breaks, regular team events and a collegial atmosphere.
  • The view is on top of it! Enjoy a beautiful view of Bern's old town at work
  • The benefits are also right: Remunerated changing time in the form of additional Fridays as well as a share in public transport costs and the fitness subscription

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • With expertise, commitment and heart, you provide professional and evidence-based care for our patients
  • Variety is guaranteed: You care individually and need-oriented and carry out diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • Teamwork is part of us! Together with our attending physicians, nursing specialists and other areas, you ensure holistic care with high care quality
  • Medical engineering activities, nursing documentation and service billing are also part of your versatile task area
  • You accompany and promote our learners and students and gladly pass on your knowledge and experience
  • Not just working, but helping to shape! Contribute your ideas, develop our nursing and organizational processes and look forward to a varied working environment in which your professional and personal development also has space

Your profile

  • You have a diploma as a nurse
  • The well-being of our patients is important to you and comes first for you
  • An appreciative and respectful treatment of people is a matter of course for you
  • You like to work independently, actively participate in the team and have a high quality standard
  • You bring good knowledge of German and feel safe in everyday linguistic life

For additional information, Norina Esaltato, Head of Bed Department, is available at T norina.esaltato@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact!
