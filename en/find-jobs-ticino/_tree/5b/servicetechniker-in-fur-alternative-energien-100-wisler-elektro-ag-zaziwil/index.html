  • Service technician for alternative energies
  • 100% Wisler Elektro AG in Zäziwil is your partner for electrical engineering at the building. We plan and implement future-oriented solutions from the fields of electrical installations, PV systems, electromobility, smart homes and lighting. We also carry out service, repair and maintenance work on existing electrical systems and systems of any size. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a service technician for alternative energies or a service technician for alternative energies. You install and configure charging stations for electric vehicles, inverters for photovoltaic systems, controllers for self-consumption optimization and self-consumption communities ZEV. The commissioning of the installations and the customer instruction are also part of your comprehensive task. As a trained electrical installer EFZ or learned electrical installer EFZ, you are characterized by your own initiative, good handling of customers and personal commitment. They are used to work independently and take responsibility. For this future-oriented task, you will also bring good PC skills and technical flair and enjoy new technologies such as electromobility and photovoltaics. Of advantage you have already made some further trainings in this area or are ready to attend trainings.
