Public Competition Public competition at the City of Mendrisio. ## Position

  • Position: Educator (70%). ## Description The Mendrisio Municipal Council is opening a competition for the hiring of an educator at 70% on an indefinite basis, at the Municipal Nursery School, under the conditions of the current Staff Regulations of the City of Mendrisio (ROD). ## Duties The holder of the position deals with educational activities with children from 3 months to 4 years at the Municipal Nursery School of the City of Mendrisio. Generally:
  • manages and verifies individualized educational paths, which aim at developing the child's potential to achieve increasingly higher levels of autonomy and well-being;
  • formulates pedagogical projects (development programs) using specific techniques and methods of educational intervention, both at an individual and group level;
  • reads, understands and supports complex relationships, translating them into educational interventions, of which he/she maintains awareness and control;
  • updates his/her training based on the specific fields he/she performs;
  • actively participates in meetings convened by the Direction and study days;
  • collaborates in the decision-making processes of his/her team, engaging to share professional knowledge and experiences;
  • contributes to creating a positive work environment and climate;
  • is responsible for the precise and timely transmission of information and communications, ensuring that they reach the recipients in an adequate manner. ## Requirements
  • Formal requirements:
  • Diploma of childhood educator SSS or equivalent title.
  • Diploma of educator SUPSI, EISS, or equivalent title recognized by SEFRI. ## What We Offer
  • Gross annual salary (thirteenth salary included and salary scale updated as of 01.01.2026):
  • Classes: (7*) 8
  • 10 ROD (function of Administrative Collaborator);
  • Minimum: CHF 72,975.-- per year at 100%, (* CHF 69,324.--);
  • Maximum: CHF 100,867.-- per year at 100%;
  • In addition to the salary, the allowances according to LAFam are paid to those entitled.
  • Working week at 100%: 40 hours.
  • Employment rate: 70%.
  • Start of activity: As of August 1, 2026.
  • The first year of service is considered as a probationary year. ## Contact
