Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. An exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, as well as the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Human Resources Directorate is the partner committed to anticipating, defining and developing the Group's culture, leadership and organization.

Mission

  • Participate in the management of HR applications and ensure their performance, evolution and consistency with business needs
  • Ensure availability, security and management of access to HR solutions, including SAP SuccessFactors and Timetool
  • Supervising interfaces and data flows between HR applications and other enterprise systems
  • Contribute to the coordination of SIRH developments and projects with HR, IT and external partners, while ensuring budgetary monitoring
  • Advise and support HR teams in the use of solutions, adoption of new features and sharing of good practices

Profile

  • Holder of a Master's degree in computer science, administration or equivalent field, or a Bachelor's degree in IT or project management
  • You have first professional experience in managing business applications, information systems or HR solutions.
  • You ideally have knowledge of HR processes or are motivated to quickly develop your HR skills
  • You are comfortable with project management tools and methods. Knowledge of SAP SuccessFactors and TimeTool is an advantage.
  • You are fluent in French and technical English. Knowledge in German is an asset.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success"
