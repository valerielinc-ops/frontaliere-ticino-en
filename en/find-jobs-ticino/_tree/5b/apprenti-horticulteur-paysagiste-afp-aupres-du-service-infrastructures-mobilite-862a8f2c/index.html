Apprentice Horticulture/Landscaper AFP at 'Infrastructures, Mobility, Environment'

The City of Monthey places the following 2 apprenticeships, which are equally accessible to women and men:

APPRENTI HORTICULTEUR/PAYSAGISTE AFPwith the "Infrastructures, Mobility, Environment" service Launch your career with an apprenticeship at the Municipality of Monthey! We are looking for young, motivated and curious talents, wishing to learn and contribute to the maintenance and beautification of our commune's green spaces.

Your profile

  • Completion of compulsory education
  • Interest in nature, plants and the environment
  • Pleasure working outdoors, all the time
  • Good physical condition and manual skill
  • Dynamism, motivation, curiosity and desire to learn
  • Team spirit, timeliness and reliability
  • Sense of observation, care and compliance with safety instructions
  • Adaptability and willingness to develop your autonomy

We offer you

  • Two-year vocational training AFP, varied and practical
  • The discovery of the various activities related to the maintenance and development of communal green spaces
  • To participate in plantation, mowing, plant size and maintenance work
  • Learning to use professional tools, machines and equipment
  • Regular and personalized support throughout your training
  • A respectful and stimulating framework within an experienced team
  • Contribute to the quality and value of Monthey's public spaces

On-going

  • August 2027

Will you fully invest? Apply now online on and attach to your file a cover letter, your curriculum vitae, copies of the school bulletins for the last two years and your diplomas and certificates of internship. Preselected people will have the opportunity to discover the trade and their future working environment during a compulsory internship. It will also provide an opportunity to get to know and assess their skills and motivation. We look forward to receiving your application by February 1, 2027\.
