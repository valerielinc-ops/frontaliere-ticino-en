The Position In Roche Diagnostics we move forward with our proposal to transform the health of patients. To contribute to this objective, we must incorporate Junior Technicians for our Repair Center through a Format Contract for the Obtainment of Professional Practice. It deals with an opportunity directed to recently qualified people who want to start a professional career in the field of maintenance, repair and installation of high-technology clinical diagnostic equipment, acquire practical experience in an innovative and advanced environment specialized.

During the training program, the selected person will gradually learn their technical knowledge based on the supervision and support of experienced professionals, participating in different strategic areas of the Repair Center.

Main responsibilities

  • Team adjustment.
  • Participate in the diagnosis, repair, preventive maintenance and preconfiguration of clinical diagnostic instruments.
  • Collaborate in response activities with loss-making teams on different product lines such as SWA (Serum Work Area), Molecular, Tissue and Coagulation.
  • Learn technical procedures, quality standards and working methods applied in the repair and maintenance environment.
  • Participate in continuous improvement initiatives aimed at optimizing processes and workflows within the height.

Technical support in the field

  • Accompany technical specialists and service engineers in interventions carried out in hospitals, laboratories and health centers.
  • Collaborate in installation activities, can operate and verify clinical diagnostic systems.
  • Acquire practical experience in real customer experience, without supervision and technical support.

Professional Desarrollo

  • Participate in a structured training course that combines technical learning, professional skills development and knowledge of the in vitro diagnostic sector.
  • Actively contribute to the identification of opportunities for improved operations and efficiency within the Repair Center.

Profile required

  • University title obtained in the last years compatible with the formalization of a Professional Practice Contract.
  • Grado en Ingeniería Electrónica Industrial y Automática, Ingeniería Eléctrica, Ingeniería Biomédica, Ingeniería Mecatrónica, Ingeniería de Tecnologías Industriales o titulaciones sobres.
  • Alternativamente, Ciclo Formativo de Grado Superior en Electromedicina Clínica, Automatización y Robótica Industrial, Mecatrónica Industrial or equivalent specialties.
  • Interested in maintenance, repair and installation of technological equipment.
  • Analytical capacity and problem solving orientation.
  • Interested in continued improvement and operational excellence.
  • Learning ability, working with a team and adapting to dynamic conditions.
  • Availability to carry out point placements with technical and service engineers.

  • Participation in projects with direct impact on health care.
