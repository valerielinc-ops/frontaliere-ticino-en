In addition to friendly and bright treatment rooms, we offer you an MTT area and a training room where various exercise courses are offered. We have a wide-ranging patient clientele and look after everyone from the athlete to the pensioner. It is important to us to accompany our patients in the activity and offer you all possibilities. We document digitally via Ipad and provide you with additional documentation times. In addition, our team meets regularly for an internal professional exchange, once a year we organize an in-house training. The patient organization (practical telephone, appointment, etc.) is completely taken over by our reception.

Your responsibilities

  • Patient management (findings, treatment planning, treatment, documentation)
  • Contact with the assigning doctors, writing progress reports
  • Taking over home visits in practice

What we expect from you

  • Trained physiotherapist with SRK recognition
  • Self-employment and professional experience

We offer you

  • Good employment conditions
  • Digital documentation via Ipad, additional documentation times
  • Patient organisation through our reception team
  • MTT area, training room
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Internal professional exchanges, in-house training, specialist lectures by our assigning doctors
  • Participation in movement courses within working hours

Start and duration of employment

  • 1. January 2027, for an indefinite period. Thereafter, the possibility of permanent employment.
