Logistics Project Manager - Neuchâtel direct line - La Chaux de Fonds Apply » Date: 20 Oct 2026

Place

Renens, CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. Take part in a unique project for a new major and strategic railway line (investment over CHF 1 billion). At the SBB, we continually upgrade the infrastructure to ensure fluid connections, point trains and welcoming stations. After the preliminary project has been completed, we will strengthen the team to take a key step: the preparation of the case file. Why would you like this job? It is a strategic and emblematic project comprising more than 30 km of tunnels, mostly carried out at the tunnel operator, and the management of about 1.5 million m3 of materials. The project is carried out according to a sustainable development approach: optimisation of the recovery routes, traceability of materials and reduction to the strictly necessary road transport to limit the impact on the environment and the residents. What you can move.

  • As a project leader, you lead the strategy and execution of materials logistics, from extraction to recovery or disposal.
  • Responsible for the strategy and implementation of actions related to the sustainability of the project, you define the environmental and social objectives, follow indicators and coordinate the various stakeholders.
  • You guarantee the smooth running of your project portion throughout the SIA phases (from planning to implementation) in terms of safety, quality, time and costs, while integrating logistics into the general site planning.
  • You are responsible for the entire tendering process for materials management, in accordance with the LMP/OMP and internal processes.
  • You pilot internal specialists and external agents by ensuring coordination and quality of deliverables.
  • On a daily basis, you lead the project until you obtain the various internal validations, represent the project in committees and bodies, and develop trusting collaborations with key partners, including the OFT, the Canton and the municipalities.

What you bring.

  • Thanks to your experience, you have developed an excellent sense of relationship and a strong team spirit, allowing you to easily coordinate the various internal and external stakeholders of the project.
  • Your proactivity and ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously distinguish you.
  • During long-term projects, you know how to stay on course and achieve the goals set while remaining motivated on a daily basis.
  • Professionally, you bring a proven experience in managing complex and multidisciplinary projects (from 5-6 years of experience)
  • A proven experience in planning materials management platforms and logistics is a major asset.
  • You have advanced technical training (HES, EPF, university) in a construction-related field, such as civil engineering.
  • You master French (minimum level C1); a good knowledge of German (from level B1) is an asset.
