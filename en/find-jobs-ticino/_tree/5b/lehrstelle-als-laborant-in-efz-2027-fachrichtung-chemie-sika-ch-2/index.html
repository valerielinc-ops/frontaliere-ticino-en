About the role This is what awaits you At the start of the apprenticeship in August 2027, we are looking for a motivated learner for training as a laboratory assistant / in EFZ specialisation in chemistry.

During your education

  • conduct chemical tests independently and learn about modern laboratory methods
  • Make chemical products and compounds
  • analyze samples and evaluate the results
  • You work with modern laboratory equipment and analysis methods
  • gain insights into an international company and work with professionals from different fields

Your skills and experience

This is what you bring with you

  • Interest in natural sciences, especially chemistry
  • Joy of experimentation, research and computing
  • Accurate and careful working methods
  • Rapid understanding and willingness to learn
  • Teamwork and reliability
  • Successful completion of secondary school (highest level of requirement)

Why you should come to us

With your application you send us

  • your CV
  • An insight into your motivation for teaching as a laboratory assistant
  • all secondary education certificates
  • your interlock test result and/or optionally the multicheck

You can look forward to this

  • Personal support by experienced trainers and practice trainers
  • varied training with a lot of practical experience
  • Family culture with the well-known Sika spirit
  • 40-hour week, attractive learning wages and 13. Monthly wages
  • Assistance in completing the Professional Maturity (BM)
  • Benefits worth over CHF 200 per month
  • Development opportunities after successful teaching

Sika is a great employer – especially for young starters. Find out more about teaching as a laboratory assistant and about our various teaching professions at www.sika.ch/lehrstellen. About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting for the construction industry and industry.
