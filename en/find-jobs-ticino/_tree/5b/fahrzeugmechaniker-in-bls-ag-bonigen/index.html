Full description

Technology excites you. Generally speaking. Doesn’t it always have to be cars or trucks? Our rail vehicles are packed with modern technology paired with proven pneumatic, hydraulic and mechanical parts. In this combination, the vehicles are comfortable and reliable workhorses at the service of our customers. And you, as part of a well-established maintenance team, are an important link in the transport chain.

This is waiting for you

  • Carry out and document challenging revisions, repairs and modifications to thermal infrastructure vehicles
  • Carry out maintenance work and take over mobile operations
  • Perform analysis, diagnosis and testing tasks
  • Working assignments at other BLS locations and providing piquetting services

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed mechanical-technical training, for example as agricultural machinery, construction machinery or commercial vehicle mechanic:
  • Good mechanical, hydraulic and pneumatic knowledge, electrical knowledge of advantage
  • Independent, exact and reliable working methods as well as the desire to train yourself regularly and are physically fit
  • Good knowledge of German language
  • Category B driving licence

About the BLS

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? The pride of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.

Requirements

  • Abgeschlossene mechanisch-technische Ausbildung, zum Beispiel als Landmaschinen-, Baumaschinen- oder Nutzfahrzeugmechaniker:in

  • Gute mechanische, hydraulische und pneumatische Kenntnisse, elektrische Kenntnisse von Vorteil

  • Selbstständige, exakte und zuverlässige Arbeitsweise sowie Lust, sich regelmässig weiterzubilden und bist körperlich fit

  • Gute Kenntnisse in deutscher Sprache

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
