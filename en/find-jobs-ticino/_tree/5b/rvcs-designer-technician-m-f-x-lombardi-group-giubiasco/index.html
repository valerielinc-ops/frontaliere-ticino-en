Full description

As an RVCS designer, you will lead the implementation of innovative plant systems, from design to delivery, ensuring quality, precision and coordinating all phases to guarantee the success of our projects. ##

  • Responsibilities
  • Design and sizing of RVCS systems for industrial and civil buildings
  • 2D drafting with AutoCAD, BIM modeling with Revit software
  • Cost, schedule and quality control of works from design to execution
  • Preparation of principle schematics
  • Drafting of specifications with Messerli CPN ##
  • Requirements
  • RVCS designer/drafter with technical apprenticeship or SSS/ST diploma and at least 5 years of experience in the field
  • Ability to work independently and manage projects
  • Knowledge of SIA-EN standards
  • Good knowledge of CAD programs and Office 365. Revit is a preferred qualification
  • Excellent knowledge of Italian and German languages
