Full description

Pensum: 80–100%, bathing

You will play a key role in Axpo Grid’s Energy Automation: Focusing on leading challenging projects, you will also shape our supply coordination and continuously develop our portfolio for energy and automation systems.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • The focus of your role is project and sub-project management: You lead projects and sub-projects with a focus on secondary technology and coordinate planning services. You control the scope of services, deadlines, costs and quality and coordinate technical interfaces with specialist offices, customers and suppliers.

In addition, you are the central contact person for the coordination of offers within the Energy Automation department: You create calculations, service descriptions and offer documents, coordinate technical, commercial and scheduling content with specialist departments and the central sale and ensure completeness and plausibility of the offers. In addition, you support in negotiations and coordinate suppliers in coordination with central purchasing.In addition, you are involved in the further development of our portfolio: You expand existing offers, develop new products and services and develop proposals for services, price logic and positioning. You standardize and modularize services and offers – including reusable offer modules and templates. Profile & RequirementsCompleted studies in electrical engineering, industrial engineering or a comparable qualification as well as several years of relevant professional experience and industry knowledge.Extensive experience in the design and engineering of energy systems and automation technology, ideally in energy production/transmission, industrial automation or public infrastructure.Experience in the further development and standardization of service offerings. Knowledge of architectures and security functions of critical infrastructures as well as relevant standards such as ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27019 and StromVV of advantage.Ability for the professional management of project and sub-project teams as well as the coordination of interdisciplinary contributions in matrix organizations. Independent, structured working methods, communication strength and teamwork

  • Secure use of Microsoft 365 and project planning tools. German and English are negotiable, knowledge of French is an advantage. About the teamYou will become part of an interdisciplinary team working together on future-oriented energy and automation solutions. We value initiative, open exchange and diversity in all their forms and support each other in successfully implementing challenging projects.
