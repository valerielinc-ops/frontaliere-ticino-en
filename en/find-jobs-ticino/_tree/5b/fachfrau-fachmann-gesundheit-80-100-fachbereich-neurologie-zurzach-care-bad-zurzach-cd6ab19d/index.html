Full description

On our station, we look after 32 neurological patients in twin rooms. The organization takes place in two areas, each led by interprofessional teams with different qualifications. As a committed personality for our diverse team with different age groups and cultural backgrounds, you are right because we maintain a humorous, respectful and appreciative relationship with each other.

Our patients often need comprehensive support in everyday life due to their illnesses. We ensure professionally competent, empathetic and respectful care according to recognized concepts such as Bobath, Kinesthetics and F.O.T.T.

A versatile range of tasks from basic and treatment care requires expertise and a sense of responsibility. An open and constructive culture of error is just as obvious to us as the close interdisciplinary cooperation that ensures holistic support.

Your tasks

  • Patient-oriented care: You accompany our patients with your expertise and support them in their recovery
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: In our interprofessional team, you work closely with other nurses, therapists and doctors. Your expertise is highly appreciated!
  • Diversity: You enjoy exciting challenges and continuous learning opportunities. Through the variety of clinical pictures you gain valuable experience and can constantly expand your knowledge

Your profile

  • Training: Completed vocational training as a specialist / health professional (FaGe) or equivalent training with at least one pos. SRK-PreCheck
  • Personality: You are a strong communication personality with a high level of customer orientation, social skills and team orientation. In addition, flexibility, independence and a competent and well-kept appearance distinguish you

What we offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Flexibility bonus: Voluntary participation in our internal care pool for additional shifts, which are additionally attractively compensated
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
