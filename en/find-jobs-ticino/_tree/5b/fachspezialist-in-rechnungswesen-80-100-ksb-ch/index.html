Full description

Do you want to drive forward projects, optimize processes and stay close to operational events? In KSB accounting, you assume responsibility for cross-departmental tasks and digitalization projects. With your experience and your sense of digital solutions, you make an important contribution to the further development of our financial processes.

Their tasks

  • They are responsible for cross-team projects and further develop internal processes.
  • You coordinate the migration of our ERP system to Dynamics 365 Business Central.
  • You take on special accounting tasks for the entire hospital.
  • You support as a jumper in all three teams of accounting.
  • You will take on assistance tasks for the Head of Accounting.

Your profile

  • You have completed a commercial education and a further education in finance or accounting (level specialist card).
  • They have several years of professional experience in accounting, ideally also with project experience.
  • You are familiar with ERP Navision and use MS Office securely.
  • They bring digital affinity and are open to new tools.
  • They communicate clearly, are flexible, like to work in a team and think solution-oriented.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
