Full description

For our Central Switzerland region, the unit «Baumeister Neubau» within the Buildings division, we are looking for you as our new construction guide (m/f/d). From our office in Gisikon, you take responsibility for exciting and challenging new construction projects in Central Switzerland. Together with an experienced team, you will design projects from planning to successful handover and contribute significantly to their economic success. Tasks: Construction manager of the new Gisikon (m/f/d)

  • Independent management of several parallel construction sites
  • Resource planning and use
  • Personnel and technical management of the construction site teams including implementation of occupational safety measures and quality assurance
  • Work preparation, construction schedule
  • Measuring, profitable billing of construction sites incl. Acceptance of guarantees
  • Contact person for builders, business partners and employees
  • Independent preparation and enforcement of supplementary offers
  • Implementation of Project Controlling Requirements: Construction Manager New Gisikon (m/f/d)
  • Completed training in the building industry with further training as a dipl. Construction manager, technician, HF or similar
  • Several years of professional experience as a construction manager in the realization of building construction projects, preferably new buildings
  • In-depth knowledge in construction site organisation, construction schedule planning and cost and appointment management
