Become part of our team!

For the Raiffeisenbank Regio Frick-Mettauertal, a successful and innovative bank with headquarters in Frick and offices in Eiken, Gipf-Oberfrick, Herznach, Gansingen and Stein, we are looking for an independent, committed and service-oriented personality as an intern: in 100%.

What awaits you?

80 employees, 6 offices, 1 mission: "Mis Fricktal, mini Bank". We maintain our core cooperative values, are anchored locally and create innovative solutions for our employees, customers and partners. During your two-year BEM internship, you will work closely with experienced professionals. You get to know different fields of activity, continuously expand your expertise and get exciting insights into the diverse world of the bank.

Your assignments are in the following areas

  • Human Resources / Employer Branding
  • Marketing
  • Hypocenter
  • Asset management services

In this way, you will discover different professional fields, develop your strengths further and create a solid foundation for your career entry.

What do you bring?

School

  • BEM-WMS: School part of the Wirtschaftsmittelschule completed
  • BEM: completed gymnasium maturity

Personal

  • Analytical and process-oriented thinking
  • Self-employment and responsibility
  • High willingness to learn and perform
  • Team and communication skills

Interests

  • Delight in everyday banking and customer contact
  • Trends and developments in the professional field and in the industry
  • Application of information sources and use of new technologies
