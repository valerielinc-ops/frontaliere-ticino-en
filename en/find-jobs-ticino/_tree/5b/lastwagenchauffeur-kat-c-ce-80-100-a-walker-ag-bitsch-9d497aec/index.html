Full description

The family business A. Walker AG is the partner in the Valais delivery market for the hotel industry, gastronomy, hospitals and homes. More than 100 employees at the Bitsch, Zermatt and Martinach sites do their best every day in the service of our customers.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a

Truck chauffeur Cat C/CE (80 -100%)

Their tasks

  • Delivery of food to our customers in Valais
  • Independent loading and unloading of the vehicle (rolling containers, boxes, etc.)
  • Ensuring compliance with the cold chain
  • Timely and correct delivery according to the tour plan
  • Caring and cleaning of the vehicle
  • Friendly and professional customer contact on site
  • Co-operation in the warehouse and order picking of customer orders

Your profile

  • Category C driving licence (mandatory)
  • CZV ID and driver card
  • Experience in delivery service of advantage (food sector a plus)
  • Physically fit and resilient
  • Reliable, independent and customer-oriented working methods

We offer

  • Early route planning with regulated working hours (mostly early in the morning)
  • Modern refrigerated vehicles and equipment
  • Family, experienced team
  • Secure employment in a stable regional company
  • Direct contact with exciting catering companies
