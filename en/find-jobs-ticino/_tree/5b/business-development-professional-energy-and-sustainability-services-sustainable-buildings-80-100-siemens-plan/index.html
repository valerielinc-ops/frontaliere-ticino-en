Full description

We connect real and digital worlds in collaboration with our customers. At Siemens Smart Infrastructure, we support our customers in creating smarter, more sustainable and more efficient buildings. In order to support the development of our activities in French-speaking Switzerland, we are looking for a Business Development Professional passionate about energy transition, sustainability and digital solutions. In this role, you will contribute to the development of our energy and sustainability services portfolio by identifying new business opportunities, supporting sales teams and advising our customers on energy performance and decarbonization projects. You will work closely with business, technical and sustainability teams to promote innovative solutions that meet market needs. Your Responsibilities

  • Develop energy services and sustainability activities with customers and partners in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • Identify, qualify and support business opportunities in collaboration with sales teams.
  • Advise customers on their energy efficiency, decarbonisation and sustainability needs, and offer suitable solutions.
  • Contribute to the development of offers, business boxes and the identification of financing mechanisms or support available.
  • Support client presentations, promotional campaigns and the development of the solution portfolio.
  • Analyse market trends, customer needs and competition activities to identify new growth opportunities.
  • Work with internal teams, portfolio experts and design offices to promote Siemens solutions and support projects. Your talents and experience
  • Technical degree in sustainability, engineering, energy, Facility Management or similar.
  • Experience in commercial development and/or sale of energy solutions, durability or intelligent buildings.
  • Good knowledge of energy performance, decarbonisation and digital solutions applied to buildings.
  • Experience in Consultative Selling and Value-Based Selling.
  • Excellent communication skills, presentation, negotiation and customer relationship development.
  • Team spirit, customer orientation, autonomy and ability to evolve in a collaborative environment.
  • Mastery of French and English. Travel Availability for regular travel (about 50% of the time) to customers and sales offices in French-speaking Switzerland, depending on the needs of business activities and collaboration with teams. Benefits
  • Public transport subscription at reduced prices and free parking
  • Many opportunities for development and further training
  • Access to employee share plans
