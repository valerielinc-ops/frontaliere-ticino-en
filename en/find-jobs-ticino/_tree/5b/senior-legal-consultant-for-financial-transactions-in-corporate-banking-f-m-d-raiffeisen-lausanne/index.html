05.08.2026 Lausanne Zürich Saint-Gall

Be part of our team!

Legal & Compliance Department deals with all legal issues and ensures that the behaviour and activities of the Raiffeisen Group comply with internal and external regulations. Within the Legal Service, we are looking for a specialist who can work as a team, independent and flexible to strengthen the Corporate Legal Group. The workplace is Lausanne, Zurich Airport (the Circle) and/or Saint-Gallen and it is possible to work at home.

What are you waiting for?

  • Support on the legal aspect of complex financial transactions in the corporate customer sector
  • Support from the customer sector undertaken in financing such as bilateral credits, Club Deals and consortial credits, including the corresponding guarantees, as well as in guarantee operations
  • Autonomous legal support of the customer sector companies in Romandie, with a headquarters in Lausanne
  • Structure, drafting and negotiation of complex contracts, sometimes in collaboration with external law firms
  • Participation in the development of new areas of activity and financial services

What are you bringing?

  • Experience in the field of financing and contractual and company law in Switzerland
  • Experience in developing, reviewing and negotiating financing documents, security rights and other contracts
  • Swiss lawyer's patent and several years of professional experience (at least 4 years) after obtaining the patent
  • Excellent mastery of French (main working language), as well as good knowledge of German and English
  • Autonomous, structured and oriented method of work solutions, with a high sense of responsibility and a pragmatic approach to complex issues

"You are facing a varied and stimulating working environment at the interface between law, finance and commercial development. You support important financial transactions and actively participate in the development of business customer transactions and the expansion of financial services."
