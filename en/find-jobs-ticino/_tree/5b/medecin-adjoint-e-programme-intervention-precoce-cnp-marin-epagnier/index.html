Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP is responsible for conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health. It offers an integrated and community psychiatry, in partnership with local institutions. The NPC is also a reference centre for training and research in psychiatry. The institution has more than 800 employees, spread over several sites.

The Medical Research Directorate, to lead the TIPP program

Assistant physician - Early Intervention Programme

The TIPP (Treatment and Precocious Intervention in Psychotic Disorders) programme is the Neuchâtel cantonal programme dedicated to the early detection and management of psychotic disorders among young people aged 15 to 35. Its aim is to intervene from the first signs of the disease in order to improve prognosis, prevent relapses and limit the consequences on the patient's life course. Based on a multidisciplinary approach, the programme combines specialized evaluation, case management, intensive monitoring and psychosocial rehabilitation, in close collaboration with relatives and the care network. It offers professionals the opportunity to contribute to a modern, proactive and recovery-oriented psychiatry. Composed of an interdisciplinary team (doctors, psychologists, social workers and nurses) representing 7 full-time equivalents, the unit is part of a logic of coordination and continuity of care, at the service of patients and their relatives.
