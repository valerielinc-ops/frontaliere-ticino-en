Full description

Facilities maintenance team leader 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Make Switzerland move with us. Facilities maintenance team leader Biel/Bienne, 01.11.2026 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you. Together with your team, you make an important contribution to the reliable operation of public transport. Thanks to your commitment, the facilities of the maintenance workshops and the maintenance center in Biel remain safe, available and operational. You work in close collaboration with the production units, workshop management and various specialized services and contribute to the economical and reliable operation of technical installations. An exciting and varied mission awaits you, in a collegial work environment. To find out about social benefits, such as free GA and discounted travel abroad (also for the family), go to our website under Social benefits. What you can make move.

  • As a team leader, you manage and develop a team of seven to nine employees according to the SBB management principles and you deduce, based on key figures, the appropriate measures which you implement in a targeted manner.
  • You plan, coordinate and assume responsibility for all maintenance activities carried out in the workshops and maintenance center in Biel, from preventive and curative interventions to special mandates as part of operational processes.
  • You assume responsibility for the installations from a technical and security perspective, ensure compliance with service level agreements and ensure the development of sustainable solutions through effective fault management.
  • You develop the maintenance strategy, ensure the interface between production and maintenance and bring your professional knowledge during workshop sessions, management meetings and specialized meetings as well as in discussions with site managers. In addition, you support the specialists on technical questions.
  • In collaboration with internal and external specialists, you optimize the maintenance of installations, buildings and railway infrastructure objects.
  • In close contact with regional management, you support audits, take on special tasks and participate in strategic and operational decision-making. You thus contribute to the continued development of the organization. What you bring.
  • Having a team spirit, you actively meet people, lead and motivate your team while maintaining an overview thanks to your interdisciplinary thinking.
  • You manage to communicate in a recipient-friendly and solution-oriented manner with different interest groups.
  • You have several years of professional experience in industrial maintenance, including driving experience in a similar function.
  • You have at least four years of technical professional training with a specialization in the field of electricity (ideally Art. 15 or 13 ITTO), solid knowledge of mechatronics and production and process technology as well as successfully completed higher professional education (e.g. federal diploma in a technical specialty, maintenance manager, Facility Manager or equivalent continuing education).
  • You have experience in fault analysis as well as technical assessment of installations and work safely with Micosoft 365. Knowledge of SAP R/3 or SAP S/4HANA completes your profile. - You have very good knowledge of German at level C1 and knowledge of French at level B1, both oral and written.
