About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics centre, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland, we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Transport House in Lucerne. More than 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

What you do with us

  • You take cocoa beans, store them and manage the silos
  • You make cocoa masses, cocoa blocks and nibs
  • You set up our highly automated production facilities, operate them and monitor the ongoing production
  • You control product quality and perform sampling and documentation
  • You carry out inspections and simple maintenance work on the plants
  • You consistently adhere to hygiene, quality, safety and 5S standards
  • You ensure a smooth flow of information during shift transfers
  • You clean plants and work areas and support large-scale cleaning
  • You are actively involved in improvements and optimizations

When you fit us

  • Good performance in Sek E or very good performance in Sek B
  • Good knowledge of German (at least level B1)
  • Interest in food and its composition
  • Enjoying machines, controls and modern high-tech systems
  • Craftsmanship and responsibility
  • Hygiene and cleanliness are a matter of course for you
  • Getting up early is no problem for you – work starts at 06.30

Family-friendly environment - properly insured Our social insurance, as well as maternity leave, goes far beyond the mandatory - for your own protection and for your loved ones.
