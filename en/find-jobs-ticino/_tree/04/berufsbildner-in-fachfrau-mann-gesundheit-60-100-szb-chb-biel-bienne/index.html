Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

The education of future FaGe's excites you? Would you like to pass on your expertise with pleasure, accompany learners on their way into working life and support them in realizing their potential? As a vocational trainer, you make an important contribution to the development of the next generation of FaGe's. You promote professional and personal skills, support learners in challenging situations and actively contribute to a high-quality education. For the accompaniment of our learners, we are looking for a committed and motivated personality as:

Professional trainer/specialist Health 60-100 %

You do

  • Accompaniment of learners during their training
  • Conduct of site, course and assessment discussions
  • Participation in practical qualification procedures as well as taking on experts Expert activities
  • Active participation and further development of training quality at the Biel Hospital Centre
  • Care of patients as a specialist / health specialist
  • Close cooperation with team leadership, nursing team and education team

This sets you apart

  • Health training completed
  • Completed vocational training course or the willingness to complete it
  • Ability to support and promote learners of all ages
  • Engaged personality with motivation and initiative to help shape the training of FaGe's on the bed stations
  • Interest in continuous professional and personal development
  • Conscientious work, team spirit and healthy resilience
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as basic knowledge of French

You will benefit from this

  • Attractive working conditions: a modern infrastructure, family-friendly personnel policy and fair compensation
