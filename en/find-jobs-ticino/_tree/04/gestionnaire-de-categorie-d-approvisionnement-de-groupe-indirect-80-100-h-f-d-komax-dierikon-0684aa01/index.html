Full description

The Komax group is a pioneer and market leader in the field of automated wire processing. As a globally active Swiss company, we develop and manufacture serial production machines, customer-specific systems, quality assurance modules, test systems, networking solutions, and services for various industries. The Komax group has more than 50 companies with 3500 employees across five continents and a worldwide sales and service network.

Your responsibilities

  • You develop and implement Group-wide strategies for indirect categories such as logistics, IT, telecommunications, travel, facility management and other indirect categories.
  • You analyze Group-wide spend, demand, specifications, supplier markets and total cost of ownership to identify opportunities for savings, standardization and bundling.
  • You build a transparent project pipeline and lead international tenders, sourcing and optimization projects from analysis through to sustainable implementation.
  • You are responsible for supplier selection and contract negotiations and establish framework agreements, preferred supplier models and suitable catalog solutions in close collaboration with stakeholder functions.
  • You manage strategic suppliers based on relevant performance indicators and drive improvements in cost, quality, service, risk and ESG.
  • You collaborate closely with IT, Finance, HR, Operations, Facility Management as well as local procurement and management teams and ensure compliance with Group-wide procurement standards.

Your profile

• A degree in Business Administration, Supply Chain Management, Procurement, Finance, Engineering or a comparable field • Several years of experience in strategic procurement or category management, ideally within an international industrial or matrix organization • Hands-on experience across several indirect categories as well as in spend analysis, strategic sourcing, tendering and contract negotiations • Experience of successfully led international and cross-functional projects and achieved demonstrable savings or sustainable process improvements • A combined analytical, pragmatic and results-oriented approach with strong negotiation and stakeholder management skills

  • Proficient in Power BI and experience with SAP S/4HANA, e-sourcing or procurement analytics solutions is an advantage

• Fluent in English, German and other European languages are an advantage; willingness to travel

Your benefits

You’ll take over a key role with real impact, international exposure and plenty of room to shape Group Procurement. You will drive strategic initiatives, collaborate across Komax and actively contribute to the development of our global procurement organization. Your new workplace will be either in Dierikon (Switzerland) or another of the mentioned locations, depending on your place of residence and work.
