Team leader: in Strategic asset management for access to mobility 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team leader: in Strategic asset management for access to mobility Bern / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

As a team leader: in strategic plant management for access to mobility, you and your team shape the future of public plants and low-voltage consumers and make an important contribution to a customer-oriented, efficient and sustainable railway infrastructure. You will lead your team through the next phase of organizational and professional development. As a member of the strategic asset management management team, you contribute to the further development of strategic asset management and ensure that strategy, financing and substance conservation are effectively coordinated. Our benefits such as child allowances, free 1. Class GA and discounted travel abroad – also for your family – can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • Within the newly created organizational unit, you will lead and develop the team "Strategic Asset Management Access to Mobility" and strengthen cross-divisional cooperation.
  • In this role, you are responsible for the strategic development of plant management for all public plants and low-voltage consumers at SBB. You ensure that our customers find an efficient, safe and sustainable infrastructure on the way to the train.
  • You ensure the long-term financial viability of maintenance and renewal. Your team is responsible for the technical preparation of the offer "Access to Mobility" for the service agreement with the BAV.
  • You drive the change from an operational to a more strategically oriented management and promote a common goal within the team and its partner organizations.
  • Together with your team, you ensure the implementation of the plant strategy by coordinating the strategic requirements with the plant managers in the four regions (West, South, Middle and East) and working closely with the project portfolio management.
  • You ensure that asset strategy, performance, customer benefit and profitability are in line in the long term.

You bring that with you.

  • You have several years of leadership experience and have successfully developed teams, ideally also in change or integration situations.
  • You analyze complex technical, organizational and financial issues quickly and derive pragmatic and strategically viable decisions from them.
  • You convince through pronounced stakeholder management, negotiate on an equal footing and manage to merge different interests into a common goal. Passing on knowledge and experience is easy for you.
  • You bring knowledge of strategic asset management in the infrastructure environment
  • Experience in the public transport or railway environment is an advantage.
  • You have a university degree (FH/ETH/uni) in technical or economic direction, ideally supplemented by further training in management or asset management.
  • You have very good oral and written knowledge in German. Knowledge of a second national language (F/I) is desired. You can apply your digital skills (IT, MS office and tools) on a daily basis.
