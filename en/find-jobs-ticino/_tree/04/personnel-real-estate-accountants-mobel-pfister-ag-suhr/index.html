At pfister, we pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today – will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Active participation in the development of central real estate management and participation in the future structures and processes
  • ensuring efficient and correct administrative management of real estate; the broad field of responsibility extends from contracting, to correspondence with tenant partners, to billing and debt collection
  • Cooperation in managing the real estate accounting of our business properties
  • Maintaining relationships and close exchange with our external partner in the area of facility management (technical building maintenance, construction management, cleaning, etc.)
  • You have a basic commercial training and already have further training and/or experience in administrative real estate management
  • You master the German language very well in word and writing
  • You master the French language well in word and writing
  • You have very good knowledge of the application of Microsoft Excel
  • You have a pronounced numerical flair and are stylish in commercial correspondence
  • If you are also an open and interested person who wants to develop ambitiously and grow in their task, then you are exactly right with us
