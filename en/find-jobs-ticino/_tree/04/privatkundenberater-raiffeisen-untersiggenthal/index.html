Full description

11.11.2026 Untersiggenthal

Become part of our team!

We – Raiffeisenbank Siggenthal-Würenlingen – are close to the people and strong in the region. At our three locations in Nussbaumen, Untersiggenthal and Würenlingen, we combine personal advice with a modern banking experience. As a hostess, we create spaces in which our customers feel comfortable and banking is experienced simply, humanely and authentically. Our attachment to the region, our commitment to the people and our enthusiasm for financial issues shape our everyday life. With around 48 employees – including five apprentices – we focus on continuous development, modern forms of work and real work-life balance. Flexible working hours, attractive social benefits and ergonomic workplaces are of course part of this for us. Ready to start with us? Then we look forward to you.

What awaits you?

  • You manage and develop your own customer book and maintain long-term customer relationships
  • You advise comprehensively on the topics of numbers, saving, investing, pension and daily banking transactions
  • You take responsibility for your own customer portfolio and continuously expand it
  • You recognize opportunities, create added value and inspire with individual solutions
  • You work with modern digital consulting tools and shape the banking consulting of tomorrow

What do you bring?

  • You have completed banking education or a comparable education
  • You are at least 30 years old and have several years of experience in banking customer advice
  • You love to advise and help people.
  • You are flexible, think along and act on your own initiative
  • You have good communication and social skills
  • You bring affinity and joy for digitalization in customer consulting

Close to people. Strong in service. Many as life. The job profile of a customer advisor is exciting and fulfilling. Martina Vago Head of Customer Consulting Basis Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We offer flexible working models

The work-life balance of our employees is important to us. That’s why we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time work.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.

We enable the purchase of additional holiday weeks

Our employees can purchase up to two additional holiday weeks per year in addition to the regular holiday days.

We promote the reconciliation of family and work

We support families. That is why we create an environment in which family and work can be reconciled in the best possible way, for example by working part-time, generous parental leave or paying a voluntary family allowance.if the ordinary (cantonal) family allowances are received via the Raiffeisen Family Compensation Fund.
