Full description

Key responsibilities This role supports the development of comprehensive factual records and narratives, and support the handling of regulatory, government, and civil litigation matters across a global financial institution. We are looking for an Authorized Officer to join UBS's Fact Finding Unit within Global Litigation, based in Switzerland. You will support day-to-day development of factual records as needed for civil litigation, regulatory investigations, and other similar matters, working closely with the attorneys overseeing and conducting the assessments. Document review, data processing, and AI tools will support that work. You will:

  • Organize, maintain, and manage electronic and hard-copy files, document repositories, and matter-related records for regulatory investigations, civil litigation, and other similar matters.
  • Prepare, format, and assemble binders, exhibits, chronologies, witness files, and other materials for interviews, depositions, regulatory inquiries, and litigation proceedings.
  • Coordinate document collection, processing, tracking, and production activities with internal stakeholders, IT teams, and external vendors.
  • Support document review and quality control efforts, including document organization, privilege log support, and production readiness checks.
  • Utilize litigation support tools, such as Relativity, Harvey, and Microsoft Copilot, to assist with document management, information retrieval, and workflow efficiency.

The team The Fact Finding Unit sits within Global Litigation, part of UBS's Group Legal function, and supports matters across all regions and business divisions. The unit supports litigation, regulatory, enforcement, and other high-risk matters by developing factual records, managing discovery and document review processes, organizing evidence, and leveraging legal technology and AI tools to support fact development.

Your skills and experience

  • Bachelor's degree required
  • Minimum 5 years of experience as a litigation paralegal, investigations paralegal, or in a comparable legal support role
  • Strong experience with document management, litigation support, discovery processes, and matter organization
  • Experience supporting document review and production workflows preferred
  • Demonstrated proficiency with AI-powered legal tools, such as, Microsoft Copilot, Harvey, or comparable technologies, and a strong interest in leveraging technology to improve legal workflows
  • Exceptional organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple matters and competing deadlines in a fast-paced environment
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills and the ability to work independently and collaboratively with attorneys, legal professionals, and business partners
  • Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite, particularly Excel, PowerPoint, and Word
  • Financial services, regulatory, or in-house legal department experience is a plus

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
