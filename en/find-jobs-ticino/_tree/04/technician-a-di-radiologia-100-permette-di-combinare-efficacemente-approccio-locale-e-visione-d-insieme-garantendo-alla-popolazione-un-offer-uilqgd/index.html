Full description

The multisite Ticino hospital, with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory, offers a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine a local approach and an overview, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. The Institute of Integrated Diagnostics of Italian Switzerland (IDISI) brings together the Clinics of radiology, nuclear medicine, pathology and laboratory medicine and, with its approximately 600 collaborators and almost 4 million examinations and analysis carried out each year, offers a comprehensive and high quality diagnostic service, responding to the needs of patients resident throughout the cantonal territory. Overcoming traditional specialist barriers, IDISI adopts a multidisciplinary approach to favor more efficient and personalized diagnostic-therapeutic paths, capable of responding to the specific needs of each patient. The Institute is also oriented to university education and to promoting biomedical research. The Nuclear Medicine Clinic performs approximately 8,000 diagnostic and therapeutic examinations each year, covering almost all available performance in nuclear medicine. Among the various awards, the Clinic is accredited to the EUMS/EBNM European Agency for Clinical and Training. To complete our team at the Nuclear Medicine Clinic in Bellinzona and Lugano we look for a Radiology Technician (100%). ##
