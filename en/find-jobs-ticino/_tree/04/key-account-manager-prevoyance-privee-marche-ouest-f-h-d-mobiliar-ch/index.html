Full description

What awaits

In this role, you accompany our general agencies in French-speaking Switzerland so that they can advise individuals and SMEs with competence on all issues related to foresight. As a professional coach, trainer or trainer and sparring partner, you support skills development and help strengthen the quality of advice offered to our clients.

What you can do with us

You design and animate practical training within our general agencies in French-speaking Switzerland. Through your support, you contribute to the development of the skills of our foresight specialists as well as our financial planners and planners. You ensure that you pass on knowledge that is directly applicable to everyday life and promote continuous learning within teams.

Thanks to your expertise and coaching, you're helping teams deal with challenging foresight issues. In complex customer situations, you participate in interviews, bring your expert look and help develop solutions tailored to the needs of individuals and SMEs.

You also give training to new counsellors and insurance and foresight advisors as part of our internal curricula in Bern. Finally, you encourage the sharing of knowledge by facilitating specialized forums and developing spaces for exchange that foster collaboration and learning between teams.

What you bring

  • Advanced training specialized in foresight or financial planning
  • Experience in adult training and commercial coaching
  • Experience in accompanying or advising SMEs is an asset
  • Interest in new approaches and ability to convey your enthusiasm
  • Open personality, loving contacts, autonomous, solutions oriented and enjoying teamwork
  • Excellent mastery of French with good knowledge of German

Working with us

You join a team of specialists in foresight and training that favours open communication, mutual assistance and the search for pragmatic solutions. Together, we contribute to the development of the competencies of general agencies and to the quality of advice provided to our clients.

Your workplace is Nyon, with flexible organisational possibilities. This function involves a travel activity of about 60% within French-speaking Switzerland, allowing you to work closely with our general agencies and their teams on the ground.

What we offer

Balance of working life - privacy

We apply flexible work models and allow 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. We also offer above-average family allowances and a sustainable pension fund.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of in-house courses and allow you to participate in possible programs abroad. We contribute generously to external continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, half-rate subscription, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.
