Full description

Vaudoise is one of the leading Swiss insurance companies and an attractive and future-oriented employer. In keeping with its cooperative identity and values, Vaudoise creates a healthy, inclusive and inspiring working environment in which you can develop further. To strengthen our personal insurance services area, we are looking for a group leader in training UVG/KTG services & quality. In this role, you will lead a team of specialists and play a key role in the further development of competencies, ensuring high quality standards and harmonizing professional working methods within the area The following exciting tasks await you:

  • They lead, accompany and develop a team of trainers and specialists in the field of UVG, KTG services and quality
  • They organize the activities of the team, ensure optimal resource planning according to operational priorities and take over administrative coordination
  • They identify the training needs and the technical specifications of the various units and coordinate the corresponding measures with the specialists and managers
  • They continuously develop the training and further education offers as well as the corresponding training documents and at the same time ensure a high quality of the onboarding and integration processes.
  • They lead projects to improve the learning experience and the efficiency of training measures
  • They participate in the preparation and further development of professional guidelines and ensure harmonisation of practices within the division
  • They accompany the teams in the implementation of professional specifications and ensure efficient communication and implementation of regulatory and organizational changes.
  • You control the activities in the area of quality control and monitoring and support the managers in the analysis of the results and in the definition of improvement measures
  • They actively contribute to the continuous improvement of processes, services and quality standards
  • With your leadership, expertise and ability to inspire teams for common goals, you will make an important contribution to the success of Vaudoise This is what distinguishes you:
  • Higher education or equivalent professional experience in insurance, training or management
  • Several years of in-depth experience in the field of UVG benefits and/or day sickness benefits
  • German as a mother tongue and very good knowledge of French (C1)
  • Successful leadership experience and the ability to accompany employees in a changing environment and actively shape change
  • Strong interest in knowledge transfer and skills development
  • Very good analytical, organizational and communication skills
  • Strong quality awareness, solution orientation and enjoyment of working with different contacts
  • Natural leadership, strong ability to connect people and create a trusting work environment
  • Secure use of digital tools as well as interest in digitalization and continuous improvement
  • A healthy working environment in a company that is certified as a "Great Place to Work"
  • Numerous attractive social benefits as well as flexible options for the organization of work
  • A corporate culture with strong human values and focus on the well-being of our employees
  • Working in a dynamic and committed team
  • A responsible position with high visibility and direct influence on the quality of our services as well as the further development of competencies
  • Exciting projects in a dynamic environment of transformation and digitalization
