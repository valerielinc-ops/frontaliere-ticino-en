Specialist:in integral safety 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Specialist: in integral safety Bern / Work Smart, 01.04.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

In the security organization of SBB, you take responsibility for the further development of security in a versatile company. In the Security division, you combine strategic issues with effective implementation in the 2nd Line of Defence and work closely with specialists and executives within and outside SBB. You can expect a challenging task area with creative leeway, in which you can contribute and further develop your leadership experience and your security knowledge. You play a central role in risk management security. Together with your partners, you will develop solutions that protect people, facilities and operations and thus make a tangible contribution to the reliability of public transport. You can move that.

  • As a specialist manager, you develop the security management in your area of responsibility and align it with the strategy, risks and goals of SBB, so that you strengthen the protection of people, plants and operations.
  • You identify and assess security risks, derive appropriate measures and check implementation and effectiveness.
  • You plan and carry out demanding audits, recognize the need for action and accompany the implementation of improvements.
  • You develop security concepts, policies and processes and ensure coordination across organizational boundaries.
  • You accompany changes from conception to sustainable anchoring and take over the management of projects and sub-projects.
  • Managers and specialists advise on complex security issues, represent your specialist topics and promote knowledge transfer.

You bring that with you.

  • With a high sense of responsibility and sovereign communication, you convince at all hierarchical levels and work cooperatively with different stakeholders.
  • Strong conceptual and analytical thinking as well as a structured way of working help you transform complex issues into effective solutions.
  • You will bring several years of experience in the security industry at a strategic level, have expertise in at least one area of physical security and skillfully integrate modern IT tools, including AI, into the work processes. Good legal knowledge and officer level experience in the army or police are beneficial.
  • You are experienced in the development and control of security management, as well as in risk management.
  • You know the basics of project, program and process management. Experience with agile forms of work such as SAFe is an advantage.
  • A university degree at FH or university level or an equivalent education distinguishes you and you have German at least C1 and French at least B2.
