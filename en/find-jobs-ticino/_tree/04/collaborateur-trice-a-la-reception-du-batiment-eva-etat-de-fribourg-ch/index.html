Full description

Who are we?

The mission of the Food Safety and Veterinary Service SAAV is to ensure, through its inspections and analyses, that food and usual items do not endanger or deceive the health of consumers. It ensures the welfare and health of animals for themselves and the benefit of human beings.

Why join us?

Do you want to work for the entire population of the Fribourg region and join a committed team of diverse and complementary profiles? Join us to practice a meaningful profession, within a service of human size, while benefiting from the stability and resources offered by a large company that is the state of Fribourg.

In addition to this stimulating setting, we offer you attractive conditions: pleasant working environment, business restaurant, ergonomic workplaces, state-employer services and continuing training. Promotion of bilingualism through training in the partner language. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

For this position, you will be hired as an administrative employee (reference function)

  • Provide and organize reception and reception of visitors to the EVA building (Food Safety and Veterinary Affairs Service) - SAAV and Environmental Service - SEN)
  • Ensure communication by responding to telephone calls and e-mails addressed to the SAAV
  • Manage incoming and outgoing mail
  • Apply visitor security procedures and manage building access
  • Provide logistical, organizational and administrative support to the administration and support sector at different levels of responsibility (correspondence, internal documentation, etc.)
  • Coaching and contributing to the training of VAS learners at reception
  • Manage orders for small office equipment (orders, tracking, delivery control, storage)

Profile desired

  • SWC or equivalent
  • Attestation of trainer in undertaking an asset or willingness to attend training
  • A few years of work experience in a reception function is an asset.
  • Oriented solutions, you know how to manage priorities with practical meaning and demonstrate availability and flexibility
  • Smileing and caring, you enjoy human contacts and are motivated to work in the public service
  • Good knowledge of common computer software and ease in learning new tools
  • Mastery of the German or French language with very good knowledge of the other language
