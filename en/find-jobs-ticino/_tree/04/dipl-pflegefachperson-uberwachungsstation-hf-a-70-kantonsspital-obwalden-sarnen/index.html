Full description

The Cantonal Hospital Obwalden is an innovative, versatile primary care hospital in central Switzerland with the clinics general, accident and visceral surgery, orthopaedics, internal medicine, ENT, urology, ophthalmology, spinal surgery, gynecology and obstetrics as well as anesthesia.

Are you ready to develop, to break new ground and are you looking for a varied new challenge? Then we really want to get to know you.

For our Competence Center Intermediate Care (IMC ) we are looking for an experienced personality as

Dipl. Nursing professional monitoring station HF (a) 70 %

Their tasks

  • Monitoring patient health after surgery
  • Treatment and care of seriously ill patients
  • Link between the intensive care unit and the departments as well as the emergency

Your profile

  • You have a tertiary degree and have at least one year of professional experience in nursing
  • Ideally, you have experience in monitoring care and have already completed the monitoring care course, or are ready to complete it.
  • You attach importance to a goal-oriented, multi-professional cooperation
  • Complement your empathetic, friendly appearance and independent work as well as your flexibility Your profile
  • You appreciate a dynamic everyday work and enjoy working in a small team

Our offer

  • A workplace in an attractive location in Sarnen by the lake, 5 minutes walk from Sarnen station
  • Professional training in your field of activity
  • Versatile, process-oriented and interdisciplinary organization as well as modern tools and technologies
  • Personal and professional development opportunities
  • A motivated and committed team
  • Advanced employment conditions such as discounted parking and canteen food, worldwide private accident insurance, free fitness subscription in the in-house physical fitness and discounted Reka checks
  • An interesting, varied task in a family, small hospital with short decision-making paths and lived du-culture
