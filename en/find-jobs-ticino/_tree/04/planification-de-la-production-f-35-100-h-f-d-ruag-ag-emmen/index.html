Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Planning and controlling production orders as well as throughput, schedule and capacity planning
  • Ensuring compliance with customer requirements and quality specifications
  • Monitoring, checking and reporting of production dates
  • Making make-or-buy decisions and selecting suitable manufacturing processes and suppliers
  • Preparation of production documents, work orders, parts lists and work reports
  • Creating offers for trials, prototypes, structural repairs and technical changes

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic technical or electrotechnical training EFZ as well as further training as process specialist or technical merchant (or in training)
  • Professional experience in production planning and control (PPS) as well as AVOR, knowledge in aeronautical engineering is advantageous
  • Experience in lean management, project management and CIP and 5S methodology desirable
  • Experienced SAP skills in the modules PP, PM and MM
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (B2) in word and writing
  • Team-capable, independent, structured and reliable personality

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
