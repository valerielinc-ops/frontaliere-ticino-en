Full description

Within the framework of our temporary Sigma department - building and civil engineering, we are pleased to collaborate with a renowned company in the construction sector based in Valais.

To meet its needs, we are actively looking for a (M/F) Box Mason.

Your responsibilities

  • Vertical and horizontal formwork for walls, slabs and pillars
  • Traditional formwork, metal and bandages (industrial vertical formwork)
  • Concrete work and concrete vibrating
  • Formwork, formwork maintenance and quality control
  • High precision formwork, especially for apparent concrete
  • Reading and interpreting plans, setting up and monitoring levels

Your profile

  • Excellent knowledge of metal and traditional formwork systems:
  • Sateco, Peri, Hussor, Doka
  • Confirmed experience in vertical and horizontal formwork
  • Ability to work independently as well as in teams and with other trades
  • Motivated, dynamic, careful and reliable worker
  • Compliance with existing safety standards and procedures
  • A base in French is recommended for a good understanding of the instructions
  • Essential and motor vehicle licence

What we offer

  • Active collaboration with a dynamic team, with technical know-how and advanced business skills.
  • Absolute discretion guaranteed, with an immediate entry date or to be agreed.
  • Attractive remuneration, related to the position and your skills.
  • Possibility of continuous training, to develop your talents and evolve.
  • Personalized coaching throughout your mission, with a dedicated contact.
  • Access to a network of diverse companies, offering opportunities in strategic sectors.
  • Prospects for evolution, with fixed job opportunities at our partner clients.
  • Simplified process for rapid and efficient integration.
