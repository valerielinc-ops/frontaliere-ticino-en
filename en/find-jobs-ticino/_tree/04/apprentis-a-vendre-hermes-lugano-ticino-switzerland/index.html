Full description

The company guarantees the transmission of exceptional know-how through strong territorial roots with respect for women, men and nature. Sixteen artisanal crafts fuel the creativity of the maison, whose collections are available in over 300 stores around the world. Hermès employs over 22,000 people in France and around the world who work to create "beauty with beauty" in a harmonious and environmentally friendly environment. Hermès is committed to finding women and men with curiosity, team spirit and attention to quality. A management based on the trust placed in each individual, which cultivates a collective spirit and continuous learning. Hermès has been present in Switzerland for over 70 years with a network of 8 stores distributed throughout Switzerland that sell all our creations. These stores are operated by Hermès retailers serving our customers. We offer an apprenticeship for a rich and varied experience as an AFC retail specialist, designing and delivering shopping experiences. Do you want to learn about the profession of sales and consultancy to demanding local and international customers? Have you developed a sincere interest in ready-to-wear, fabrics and silk? Do you want to learn how a store works and contribute both in the store and behind the scenes? We are looking for an apprentice who appreciates teamwork, is attentive to results, has an eye for detail and places high demands on the quality of their work. The apprentice should have good communication skills in German and English and, above all, be very eager to learn and want to actively participate in the life of a shop. In order for your application to be examined, it must consist of the following elements, which must be scanned into a single document:

  • Your CV
  • An authentic letter of application
  • The result of the Multicheck or EVA test.
  • Your last three school reports As a responsible employer, we are committed to promoting ethics, diversity and inclusion. Join the Hermès human adventure!
