Secretary for Port Affairs Secretary in charge of Port Affairs (CDD for student) Activity rate 30 to 40 % Police Lavaux is looking for a law student or with good legal knowledge to manage the administrative secretariat of the communal port of Lutry. Degree Next

Your Mission

  • Answer citizens' questions by telephone or e-mail;
  • Manage applications for berthing or wintering:
  • Preparing for removal decisions;
  • Keep records and databases up to date;
  • Conduct field checks;
  • Answer legal questions related to port affairs.

Your profile and skills

  • You are a student (last year of bachelor's or master's degree) in law or management;
  • You want a first professional field experience;
  • You are quickly autonomous;
  • You are comfortable in oral and written communication.
  • Knowledge in the field of navigation is a plus.

We offer you Schedules compatible with your studies. Attractive and modern conditions of employment within welded teams. An exciting and varied activity at the service of the population and the common good. A dynamic working environment in an exceptional region.
