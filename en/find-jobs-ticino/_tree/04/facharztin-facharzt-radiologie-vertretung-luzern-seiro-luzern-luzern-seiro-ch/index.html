Full description

As a specialist in radiology or specialist in radiology, you will take on sporadic representation assignments in Lucerne in the course of 2027 as required and individually agreed. A fixed job start and a fixed workload are not provided. The individual assignments usually last three to six hours per day of assignment and take place in particular in the event of absence from holidays, illness-related failures or short-term personnel shortages. Technically, the focus is on diagnostic radiology with MRI, CT, mammography and ultrasound. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • Taking over individual radiological missions in the event of absence from holidays, illness or short-term staff shortages
  • Diagnostic activity and diagnosis with focus on MRI, CT, mammography and ultrasound
  • Individually agreed assignment days without obligation to a fixed amount
  • Assignments of usually three to six hours per day of use
  • Introduction to operational processes and the existing radiological infrastructure
  • Professional exchange with other doctors on site so that you do not work as a sole medical specialist

This is what they bring with them.

  • Recognized specialist title for radiology
  • Fulfilment of the requirements for a professional practice permit in the Canton of Lucerne
  • Many years of practical experience in radiological diagnostics
  • Sound knowledge of MRI, CT and/or ultrasound
  • Willingness to take on short-term assignments sporadically and depending on availability

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition
  • Assistance in matters relating to the professional licence
  • Assistance in organising travel
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department

That sets them apart.

  • They make diagnostic decisions safely and in a structured way even during short missions
  • They communicate findings and questions clearly with assigning doctors and the radiology team
  • You contribute your experience in a targeted manner without presupposing a long-term employment scope
  • They value professional exchange and also work together with the existing team for individual representation missions.

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Flexible representation without a fixed workload
  • Individual agreement of the operating days according to your time availability
  • As a rule, three to six hours per day of use
  • Possibility to continue working in diagnostic radiology even after retirement
  • Privately run and independent radiological institute without externally specified sales targets
  • Modern diagnostic imaging with focus on MRI, CT and ultrasound
  • Cooperation with an experienced medical and radiological team
  • Opportunity to contribute different radiological focuses within the team

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
