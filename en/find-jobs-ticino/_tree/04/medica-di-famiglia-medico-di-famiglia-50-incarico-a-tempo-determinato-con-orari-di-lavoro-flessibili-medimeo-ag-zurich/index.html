Are you looking for a temporary family doctor position where you can arrange your workload and working hours flexibly? In a long-established practice in the Zurich Oberland, you take over an existing patient base and organize your consultation hours independently. The assignment is planned for about six months – with the possibility of a subsequent permanent employment. Here you will find a long-established family practice in the Zurich Oberland, in an easily accessible location with connection to public transport. The practice has a grown patient base with a high proportion of older people and attaches importance to personal, continuous medical care. You work with an experienced specialist in general internal medicine, two medical practice assistants (MPA) and an administrative management. Equipment includes its own office, laboratory, X-ray, ECG, lung function diagnostics and 24-hour blood pressure measurement. The practice is located in a historical building with a special character.

Their tasks

  • Independent family care and treatment of an existing patient base
  • Diagnosis, therapy and follow-up controls in acute and chronic diseases, especially in adult and elderly patients
  • Implementation of preventive examinations, small-surgical procedures and internal diagnostic investigations
  • Self-responsible organization of your office hours with sufficient time for individual patient care
  • Collegial cooperation with the practice owner, the MPA and the administrative management

Your Competences

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine or a corresponding qualification recognised in Switzerland
  • Experience in Swiss healthcare
  • Already existing MEBEKO recognition and ideally an existing professional practice permit (BAB) as well as OKP approval in the canton of Zurich or another canton
  • Knowledge of German at level C1 as well as an independent, patient-oriented working method
  • Short-term availability, ideally from now on or after a timely agreement

Their advantages

Temporary employment for around six months: an attractive opportunity to engage in an established family doctor’s office for a manageable period of time Flexible working hours: Whether individual mornings, entire working days or individual combinations – Your assignments can be adapted to your personal situation Perspective on permanent employment: In the case of mutual interest, it is possible to transfer the temporary cooperation into a permanent employment relationship. Independent work: own office room, self-responsible office hours planning and enough time for careful patient care Practical environment: long-term patient base, support from two MPAs and an administrative management as well as good accessibility with public transport
