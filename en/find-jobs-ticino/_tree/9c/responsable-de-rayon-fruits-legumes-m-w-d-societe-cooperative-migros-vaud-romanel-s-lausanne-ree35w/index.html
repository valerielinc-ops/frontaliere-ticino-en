Full description

Your commitment and personality make a difference! Passionate about retail, you're ready to coordinate a store and unite a team around ambitious goals. A true ambassador of the brand, you contribute to the performance of your store, to the satisfaction of our customers and to the development of your team.

Luogo di Lavoro: Migros Vaud Cooperative Society, MMM Romanel, En Félezin, 1032 Romanel s/Lausanne

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but we can only consider online applications. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Guarantee a warm customer experience and exemplary outfit of the ray
  • Coordinate daily operational activity: radius management, goods and flows
  • Developing business performance and contributing to achieving objectives
  • Budget compliance and cost analysis

Requirements

  • Necessary: Experience confirmed in retailing in the Fruit & Vegetables department
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): as a Retail Specialist
  • Trade and customer orientation
  • A sense of organization, autonomy and mastery of computer tools.
  • Active listening, constructive feedback and a culture of collaboration.
  • Adaptability in a changing environment (digital)
  • Product analysis and management
  • French (Very good knowledge)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks of maternity leave and 4 weeks of paternity leave, paid at 100%.
  • Occupational pension: Pension fund financed by the employer at 17%.
  • Cumulus Points: Additional Cumulus Points for Migros Employees
  • Migros Bank: Preferential rates at Migros Bank
  • Holidays: 5 to 7 weeks of vacation per year, depending on your age or seniority.
  • Migros Club School: Financial support for courses and training at Migros Club School.
