Career Service and Enlightenment Collaborator/Professor, Events and Communication (100%) The University of Italian Switzerland (USI) is a young and agile reality in continuous development, launched to new challenges in the sign of its three guiding values: quality, openness and responsibility. Active in different areas of study and research, USI offers its students a training as protagonists, engaging and interdisciplinary, and its researchers and collaborators a privileged area of autonomy and freedom of initiative. The Career and Enlightenment Service promotes privileged relationships and links of USI students and graduates with major professional realities, facilitating a qualified inclusion of graduates in the world of work. The Service is also dedicated to maintaining relations with USI graduates by establishing and consolidating contact networks among USI graduates, promoting interchange between graduates, companies and institutions and supporting professional development and networking of the Alumni community.
