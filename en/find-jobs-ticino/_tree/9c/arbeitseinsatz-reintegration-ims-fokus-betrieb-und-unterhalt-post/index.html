Full description

Post Immobilien (IMS) is the real estate specialist of Swiss Post. With around 850 employees, we develop and operate around 2,200 properties throughout Switzerland and thus create the real estate solutions for the Post of tomorrow.

IMS wants to support you on your way back to work. For this, we offer various practical activities in operation and maintenance for a gradual re-entry, which fit your current possibilities. Depending on the activity, an assignment in different regions of Switzerland is possible. The work assignment reintegration usually takes 3 to 6 months and is individually coordinated with you.

Together with you, we create new perspectives for your re-entry.

You can do that.

  • You take the first step back into the work process, bring in existing strengths and build new ones. So you actively shape your re-entry and create a good basis for your further professional path.
  • You work in our teams and take on manageable, clearly defined practical tasks in the operation and maintenance of our buildings.
  • For example, you can help us with manual and mechanical cleaning as well as minor service work. Which tasks you take on concretely, we tailor together to your experiences, strengths and current possibilities.
  • In this way, you make an important contribution to the clean and well-kept buildings of the Post Office.

You bring that with you

  • You want to gain a foothold in everyday work and bring the openness to get to know different tasks and to contribute in many ways.
  • You approach your tasks reliably and help ensure that the joint agreements work well.
  • You bring experience from a practical or operational activity, for example in technology, crafts or logistics.
  • You like to tackle and enjoy practical work.

The reintegration assignment at Post Immobilien with a focus on operation and maintenance is aimed at employees of Post who are unable to work at their current workplace. If this applies to you, contact your case management, HR consultancy or StayFit Services person before applying.

Are you interested in such a mission? Then please send us a short CV and answer the questions in the application form. We know you and your strengths better.

In a conversation, we then clarify the possible tasks, the workload, the location and the support.
