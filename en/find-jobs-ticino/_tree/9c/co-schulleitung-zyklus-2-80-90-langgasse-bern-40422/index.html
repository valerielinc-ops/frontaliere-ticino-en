Full description

Portrait The elementary school Länggasse is a lively, centrally located school in the school district Länggasse-Felsenau, which offers a wide range of learning opportunities and an appreciative cooperation. As a member of the co-directorship, together with the two other co-directors, you actively, creatively and sustainably design Länggasse Cycle 2 with 450 students and 65 employees. In doing so, you take on responsibility, provide impulses and continue to develop the school as a team.

Tasks

  • Together with a dedicated team, you will develop a future-oriented educational school and contribute current social, educational and school visions.
  • You take over the controlling and develop the concept work at the location.
  • You lead with clarity, esprit and empathy, provide orientation and manage to inspire employees for common goals – including responsibility for personnel management and difficult personnel situations.
  • You assume budget and financial responsibility for the location.
  • You are responsible for the administration and organization of the school, including the timetable, school room planning and infrastructure.
  • You lead location and school conferences.
  • Together with your team, you position the school convincingly and attractively – opposite the school commission, teachers, administrative offices and the district, and are the contact person for the school office and the inspectorate.
  • You are responsible for student and parental concerns such as career decisions and child welfare.

Requirements

  • You have an educational education, teaching experience and a proven track record in working with children of primary school age.
  • You think strategically, recognize developments early on and have the initiative to initiate things and promote them sustainably.
  • You have started or already completed a school management training or a comparable leadership training.
  • You are a team player, have a viable network and have a good feeling for taking people with you and making a difference together.
  • You approach people and meet them with interest and empathy.
  • You appreciate the close cooperation on an equal footing with the two co-school leaders and take an active role.
  • You enjoy a challenging environment and meet different cultures, perspectives and living environments with openness and interest.

We offer

Our school management consists of three people with clearly divided areas of responsibility. As the new co-director, you assume the main responsibility for the location and manage the Grosse Schulhaus. One co-director is responsible for the MR area and 20% co-director cycle 2 and the other co-director heads the school house Muesmatt, without administration. Together, you will form a closely collaborative team in which you support each other and develop the school as a whole.
