Full description

Your tasks You are passionate about challenging real estate portfolios, like to organize independently and enjoy looking after tenants and owners professionally? Then you actively shape the management of complex residential and commercial properties with us as real estate manager (m/f/d) and directly shape the quality of our service as well as the satisfaction of our customers. Due to a mandate access, we expand our team immediately and are looking for a person who takes responsibility, thinks ahead and contributes to the further development of our mandates with their understanding of services. If you have a structured way of working, entrepreneurial thinking and enjoy working together in a team, you are exactly the person we are looking for.

  • Self-responsible commercial and technical management of high-quality residential and commercial properties
  • Monitoring developments in the rental market and in rental law
  • Active support for new and existing tenants: as well as comprehensive lease contract management
  • Effective vacancy management including subsequent rental
  • Negotiation and drafting of business and supplementary lease contracts
  • Implementation of property acceptance and transfer as well as support for tenant development projects
  • Preparation and verification of detailed heating and operating costs accounts
  • Preparation of solution-oriented applications to contracting entities
  • Preparation of meaningful reports
  • Prediction, budgeting and planning with earnings responsibility
  • management and coordination of assigned clerks:inside and housekeeper:inside or FM service providers

Your requirements

  • Successful graduation of real estate manager: in with Federal Professional ID
  • Experience in residential and commercial property management
  • Stylistic German in Word and Writing
  • Good IT application knowledge (MS Office programs, ev. GREM)
  • Charismatic appearance and strong social skills
  • Valid driving licence and own car are required (free parking at Root LU location)
