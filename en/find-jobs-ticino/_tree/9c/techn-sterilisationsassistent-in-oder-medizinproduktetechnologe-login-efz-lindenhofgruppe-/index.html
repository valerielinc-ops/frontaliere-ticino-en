Their tasks

Behind every successful intervention is a safe and professional preparation of the instruments - you take on a central role in the process.

  • Preparation of all medical devices incl. Borrowing instruments from operation and flexible endoscopes
  • Complete documentation of the processing steps using batch documentation software BarCon (Asanus)
  • Operation of all devices used, including the system for processing flexible endoscopes
  • Responsibility for the proper care, maintenance and control of all equipment
  • Transfer of shift and weekend services

Your profile

  • Completed training as medical device technologist/login EFZ or as technical sterilisation assistant
  • Professional experience in all processing steps of medical devices in the hospital environment, also advantageous with flexible endoscopes
  • Good technical understanding and a reliable, careful working method
  • Flexibility and resilience in everyday work
  • Team spirit and enjoyment of working together in a diverse, multicultural environment
  • Good knowledge of German

Our offer

Our Spirit Group

The Lindenhof Group is one of the leading list hospitals with private sponsorship throughout the country. At our three locations Engeried, Lindenhof and Sonnenhof, we care for more than 170,000 patients every year, of which around 31,000 are inpatients. We attach great importance to a personal atmosphere that supports the professional and human exchange between our doctors, nurses and our patients. Thanks to modern treatment methods and a future-oriented infrastructure, we offer our patients high-quality care.
