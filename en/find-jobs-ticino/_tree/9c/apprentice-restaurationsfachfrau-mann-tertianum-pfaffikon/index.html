Full description

The Tertianum Residenz Huob, right in the center of Pfäffikon SZ, consists of 67 modern apartments with in-house Spitex service and a rehabilitation, nursing and dementia living area with 21 single rooms, internal restaurant and public restaurant ROSSOROSSO, physiotherapy, fitness, therapy pool, hairdressing salon, podiatry and cosmetic studio. Get your EFZ through training in the catering industry. With us you will start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as a restaurant specialist you can expect a supportive working environment from day one. During your training you will be accompanied and supported by our experienced vocational trainers.

We are looking for you for Haus Huob in Pfäffikon SZ on August 1, 2027

Apprenticeship as a restaurant specialist EFZ

You'll learn that with us

  • Looking after and entertaining our guests with professional service in the internal restaurant for our residents as well as in the public restaurant for external guests, where a la carte is served or banquets take place
  • Presentation of the food and beverage offerings
  • Conduct sales calls and place orders
  • Impeccable appearance as a hostess/host

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed schooling: You have completed secondary school A or B with good academic performance
  • Great willingness to learn, sense of responsibility as well as service and hygiene awareness
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Well-groomed appearance and good manners
  • Willingness to work irregular hours (we are open every day)
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy diverse challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German is important for this apprenticeship.

What benefits do we offer you? You are just as important to us as your training is to you! That's why we offer you the following advantages:

  • You get the day off on your birthday
  • CHF 500 towards your compulsory school materials
  • Free SBB half-fare subscription
  • Every lunch for CHF 5
  • Discounted cell phone subscriptions with Sunrise
  • Discounts and offers relating to fitness, leisure and your wellbeing
  • Up to 10% discount on your supplementary health insurance
  • Regular team events
  • And much more…

You start straight away with an exciting introductory day and halfway through your training we invite you to our summer camp for a whole week. Of course, your training completion must be celebrated properly, so we organize an unforgettable graduation party for all graduates. And maybe this is just the beginning of your career with us! We offer numerous opportunities to work within our company immediately after completing your apprenticeship.

You can find more benefits and insights on our website.
