Full description

The Office for Spatial Planning (ARP) is committed to a differentiated and sustainable development of the municipalities and regions as well as of the canton as a whole - the goal is a housekeeping approach to the non-replicable soil. For the Office for Spatial Planning, we are looking for a manager for spatial planning/canton planner, 100% at the Solothurn location as of 01.11.2026 or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • They lead an office with more than 30 employees in the specialist areas of spatial planning, including construction outside the construction zone as well as nature and heritage protection.
  • In your role, you will lead the Office in technical, human and financial terms and are responsible for key cantonal projects and the cantonal indicative plan.
  • The Head of the Department will assist and advise you in all areas of responsibility of the Office.
  • They represent the canton in working groups, parliamentary bodies as well as towards the neighbouring cantons and the Confederation.

Your profile

  • You have a university degree preferably of geography, jurisprudence or architecture with a master’s degree. This is complemented by additional training or proven experience in spatial planning;
  • High level of leadership and social competence, creative will as well as a strong communication ability and placement competence distinguish you.
  • They bring a high level of willingness and assertiveness as well as a deeper understanding of political processes. Strategic and networked thinking as well as accurate and stylish formulation distinguish you.
  • Many years of experience in negotiating with municipal authorities as well as public and private providers of large spatially effective projects bring you with you. In addition, you are adept at weighing conflicting interests and willing to make decisions.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
