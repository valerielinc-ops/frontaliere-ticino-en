Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

What is the role about?

They lead the test engineering team with eleven employees professionally and personnel and strategically develop team, competencies and test and test systems. In addition, you coordinate cooperation with the relevant specialist areas, ensure 3rd level support and drive forward the needs-based further development of the test infrastructure.

What tasks will you take on?

  • They lead the test engineering team with eleven employees professionally and personnel and develop structures, competencies and cooperation in a targeted manner.
  • They are responsible for the recruitment, training and sustainable development of new employees and thus strengthen the performance of the team.
  • They shape the strategic development of the team at the Reinach site and align it with future requirements.
  • They ensure close cooperation with research & development, industrialization, production, project management and software development and ensure competent 3rd level support for complex technical issues.
  • They identify future requirements for test and test systems, prioritize them together with the relevant interfaces and coordinate their successful implementation.

What do we expect from you?

  • You have completed a technical degree in electrical engineering, communications engineering, mechanical engineering or a comparable field of study.
  • You have several years of experience in the industrial environment, ideally in PCBA production, test development or industrialization, and understand complex production and test processes.
  • They have proven experience in project management as well as in professional or personnel management and understand how to develop teams in a targeted manner.
  • They communicate stylishly and negotiably in German and English and move confidently in an international, interdisciplinary environment.
  • They impress with an independent, creative and solution-oriented working method as well as strong communication and team skills.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run, committed company with a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport.
  • We support family-friendly policies, including childcare places and childcare services during the holidays.

How do you apply?

Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. Thank you for your understanding.
