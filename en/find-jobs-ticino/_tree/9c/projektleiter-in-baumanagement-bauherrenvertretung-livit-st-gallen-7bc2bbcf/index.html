Construction management in St. Gallen | Pensum: 80-100 %

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? Whether repairs, modernizations or reuse - in the construction management of the Livit, you will manage very different properties throughout their entire life cycle. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • You work on a wide range of tasks and structural challenges of preserving the value of real estate along the life cycle
  • You advise, plan and implement small and medium-sized renovation projects
  • You are responsible for the scheduled, cost- and quality-related execution
  • You advise and represent the interests of the client in medium-sized renovation projects
  • You support the managers in construction engineering issues and in budgeting
  • You actively maintain internal and external customer relationships

Your profile

  • You have completed basic structural training and further education as a technician HF, architect FH or civil engineer FH
  • You bring several years of professional experience as a project manager of renovation projects
  • You have a high technical understanding, have a pragmatic way of thinking and you act solution-oriented
  • You are resilient and have very good organizational skills for handling several projects simultaneously
  • You are digitally affine and have the ability to actively participate in changes
  • You have a high customer orientation and maintain a partnership relationship
  • You have an entrepreneurial way of thinking and trading as well as pleasant manners
  • You are capable of teamwork and bring your expertise profitably to the team
