Full description

Attached to the Medical and Administrative Service, the Medical Imaging Secretariat is present on the site of La Chaux-de-Fonds and Pourtalès (Neuchâtel). The medico-administrative staff deal with all requests from planning to reporting through the information system.

A medical-administrative worker at 70%

Your missions

  • Physical reception of patients
  • Provide telephone hotline and inform different internal and external partners
  • Administrative management of patient records
  • Processing of internal and external administrative requests
  • Various administrative tasks

Your profile

  • SWC business employee or training deemed equivalent
  • Diploma of medical secretary, an asset
  • Experience in a similar function (preferably in an Imaging Service)
  • Perfect mastery of the French language and medical terminology
  • Mastery of IT tools (MS Office)
  • Knowledge of institutional applications (OPALE, Xplore, Pacs Telemis), an asset

Your skills

  • Ability to collaborate with team spirit
  • Priority management
  • Flexibility and flexibility
  • Organizational sense and discretion
  • Ability to work in teams and networks

Further information

  • Location : site de Pourtalès, in Neuchâtel and site de La Chaux-de-Fonds
  • Entry into office: immediately or to be agreed on an indeterminate contract
  • Attractive framework and working conditions governed by the CTC health 21
  • Information: Ms. Aline Pereira da Costa, Medical and Administrative Officer, +41 79 559 42 67

Full applications (motivation letter, Curriculum Vitae, diplomas, work certificates) are due until 1 November 2026.

Any questions regarding the postulation procedure can be addressed by telephone to the Commitment Service at +41 32 713 30 13.

The Neuchâtel hospital network (RHNe) includes the sites of Pourtalès (Neuchâtel), La Chaux-de-Fonds, Val-de-Travers, Val-de-Ruz, Le Locle and La Chrysalide. More than 2,600 collaborators provide acute care, rehabilitation and palliative care for the Neuchâtel population.
