Full description

Put to the Point

As head of the Army Logistics Center Grolley, you run one of the Army’s major logistics sites and are responsible for ensuring that the troops have ready-to-use material at all times – both in everyday life and in crisis. Supported by an experienced cadre, you combine entrepreneurial leadership with proximity to the troop and, as a member of the management, actively shape the further development of the army’s logistics base. Their bridging role between the language regions and between troops, administration and partners is a key success factor.

  • Date of entry: 01.03.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20976
  • Place of work: Route du PAA, 1772 Grolley

You can make this contribution

  • The Army Logistics Center (ALC) Grolley with about 600 employees is technically, human and financially managed and responsible for the results for the main processes of replenishment, maintenance, provision and operation as well as support for the Air Force – in the normal as well as in the special and extraordinary situation
  • Ensure the provision of logistical services to the benefit of the troops in the first priority, ensure a high level of satisfaction of the recipients and support the neighboring army logistics centres if necessary
  • Responsible for budget, loans and resources, managing medium- and long-term human, financial and performance planning and ensuring the performance, earnings and cost balance of the ALC
  • Promote development of cadre and young talent, shape a motivating and performance-oriented leadership culture, assume responsibility for the training of learners and work in partnership with the personnel commission and personnel associations
  • Orient the provision of services to crisis and war, strengthen the self-protection of the center as well as the external locations and the overall responsibility for military security incl. Protecting information, property protection, accident prevention, fire protection and occupational safety
  • As a member of the LBA Management Board, help shape the further development of the Army Logistics Base (LBA), lead complex projects and represent the ALC to authorities, partner organisations and the region

This makes them unique.

  • University degree (university/ETH or FH) in business administration or technical direction, ideally supplemented by business education
  • Several years of experience in the management of a large organizational unit with more than 300 employees, preferably in the areas of logistics, maintenance or in a comparable production or service operation
  • In-depth experience in budget, credit and cost controlling, organizational development and management of complex projects
  • Experience working with an administrative centre is beneficial
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, enforcement and negotiation strength, sovereign appearance as well as pronounced decision-making strength and high resilience in a highly regulated, dynamic environment
  • Military cadre training desired (general staff officer advantageous), as well as the willingness to familiarize themselves with military structures
  • Very good knowledge of a second official language and passive knowledge of a third official language and good knowledge of English
