The Position

Missions

As a Field Network Engineer (Western Territory), reporting to a Territorial Manager of Services Teams, you will be required to collaborate with the support and IT teams on missions related to the installation and maintenance of Roche instrument networks in medical analysis laboratories (private and hospital).

Ideal location: Poitiers / La Rochelle / Bordeaux

Your main responsibilities

  • Design network diagrams, flow matrices and ensure their deployment. Management of its IT assets and updating of customer documents (Technical Architecture Document, Operations Document)
  • Install and maintain the Roche IT network of all ranges, in close collaboration with the Service Engineer and IT and Workflow Specialist teams.
  • Guarantee action plans related to cybersecurity. Act quickly when developing a PRI (Incident Response Plan)
  • Carry out hardware/software retrofits for Roche network IT, maintenance of instrument networks and updates of solutions for our customers.
  • Install and test Roche remote maintenance solutions.
  • Ensure traceability and precise reporting of your interventions and upgrades.

Your profile

  • Bac 3 or Master level training specialized in IT and networks.
  • Ability to install and configure the various components (networks, firewalls, access) of client IT infrastructures.
  • Oral fluency, raising objections being identified as the preferred contact for the IT Services Departments (ISD) to explain the prerequisites for installing Roche digital solutions and discuss customer needs.
  • Professional English
  • B license (territorial mobility essential).
  • Sense of results, taste for challenge and sense of responsibility.
