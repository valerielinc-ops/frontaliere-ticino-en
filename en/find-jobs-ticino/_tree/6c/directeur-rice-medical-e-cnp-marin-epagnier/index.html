Full description

The CNP is seeking a Medical Director for its mission in mental health.

Tasks

  • Define and deploy clinical governance policy.
  • Ensure the quality and security of medical services.
  • Promote innovation and development of medical skills.

Competences

  • Physician specialist in psychiatry with leadership experience.
  • Unifying leadership and strategic vision.
  • Excellent relational and representational skills.

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implements public policies to promote and prevent mental health

  • It acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research.

Join the Neuchâtelois Psychiatric Centre Directorate and help shape the future of mental health. In order to support its development and meet the challenges of tomorrow, the CNP seeks its future:

Medical Director

A member of the Executive Committee and strategic partner of the Branch, you carry the medical vision of the institution and ensure the quality, safety and relevance of medical and therapeutic services. In close collaboration with the Care Branch and Support Branches, you define and implement the NPC's clinical governance policy. You unite medical teams and psychologists around a common vision, foster innovation and ensure that services are continuously adapted to the needs of the population and to the evolution of modern psychiatry. You also play a major role in the development of medical skills, clinical research and institutional influence of the CNP at the cantonal, national and international levels.

As part of your function you will be required to

Medical leadership and clinical governance

  • Define and deploy the NPC's clinical governance policy.
  • Ensure the quality and security of medical and therapeutic services.
  • Promote continuous improvement of clinical practices and development of innovation.
  • Ensure compliance with professional standards, ethical principles and legal requirements.

Strategic piloting

  • Contribute to the definition and implementation of the institutional strategy.
  • Participate in the development of the annual and multi-annual objectives of the medical departments.
  • Develop indicators to assess the quality and performance of services.
  • Ensure efficient use of human, material and financial resources.

Management and skills development

  • Accompany doctors and psychologists head of department and unit managers under your responsibility.
  • Contribute to the planning of medical and psychological occupations.
  • Promote the attractiveness, development and sustainability of medical skills within the institution.

Research, education and partnerships

  • Supervise the Institute for Training and Research in Mental Health (IFRSM).
  • Support the development of clinical and translational research projects.
  • Promote academic, scientific and institutional collaborations.
  • Represent the NOC with health system partners and authorities.

You are also responsible for

  • Institutional pharmacy
  • Dietetics
  • The Office of Clinical Quality (OQC)
  • The Centre of Psychiatric Expertise

Your profile

  • Medical specialist FMH in psychiatry and psychotherapy or recognized equivalent title.
  • Further training in management, management or governance of health organisations.
  • Recognized and successful experience in managing a medical service, department or health institution.
  • Excellent knowledge of the Swiss health care system and its challenges.
  • Experience in strategic management, change management and multidisciplinary teams.
  • Strong interest in quality care, innovation, training and research.
  • Unifying and inspiring leadership.
  • Strategic vision and ability to anticipate developments in the sector.
  • Excellent relational skills and ability to build sustainable partnerships.
  • Ability to combine medical requirements, quality of care and institutional performance.
  • Political sense and ease in institutional representation.

Why join the CNP?

  • A strategic function within the Executive Committee of a reference mental health institution.
  • The opportunity to have a lasting influence on the development of psychiatry in the canton of Neuchâtel.
  • A stimulating environment combining care, training, research and innovation.
  • Wide autonomy of action and a high level of responsibility.
  • Rich collaborations with the actors of the Swiss health network.
  • An exceptional living environment between lake and mountains, in the heart of French-speaking Switzerland.
  • We look forward to receiving your application.
