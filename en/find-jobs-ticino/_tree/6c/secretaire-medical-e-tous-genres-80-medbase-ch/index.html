Welcome to Medbase Lausanne Gare

Our versatile medical centre with modern facilities is located at Lausanne station. Some 35 collaborators in general internal medicine, endocrinology, diabetics, osteology, gynaecology and obstetrics, ORL, orthopaedic surgery and traumatology, as well as physiotherapy and nutritional counselling ensure the interprofessional care of our patients, always oriented towards quality. Medbase Lausanne Gare favours a good balance between professional and private life. It is possible to work part-time in all functions and work schedules can be flexibly arranged. For doctors, collaboration is possible under employee or independent status, to be agreed upon during the interview.

Your Mission

  • Patient reception
  • Convocation of patients
  • Telephone centre management
  • Making and coordinating appointments
  • Processing of medical reports (formatting, sending, filing)
  • Administrative management of patient records
  • Collaboration with doctors and the healthcare team

Your profile

  • Diploma of medical secretary (or training deemed equivalent)
  • Medical experience
  • Knowledge of Axenita software an asset
  • Very good command of French (oral and written)
  • Responsibility, rigour and autonomy
  • Good organization and management of priorities
  • Team spirit, ease in collaboration and communication
  • Dynamic and flexible

#WeAreMedbase

To achieve great things, one must have a vision of the future. Twenty years ago, Medbase was founded with the idea of creating a health care network for all of Switzerland. From a medical practice with three collaborators in 2001, we have now become a 360 degree health provider, with about 4,000 employees spread over 170 sites, operating in four areas of activity: medical and dental centres, pharmacies, drug distribution and digital health platform. Take the opportunity to continue this success story with us, as Medbase continues to develop and improve every day the future of health care in Switzerland.

Über uns

Die Medbase Gruppe betreibt über 150 medizinische, pharmazeutische und zahnärztliche Standorte in der Schweiz. Sie hat damit ihre Stellung als führende Leistungserbringerin für ambulante, integrierte Medizin in den letzten Jahren stark ausgebaut. Rund 3200 Mitarbeitende betreuen die Patientinnen und Patienten ganzheitlich - von der Prävention über die Akutmedizin bis zur Rehabilitation. Über 480 Haus- und Spezialärztinnen und -ärzte, 340 Therapeutinnen und Therapeuten, 190 Apothekerinnen und Apotheker, rund 360 Zahnärztinnen und Zahnärzte sowie Spezialistinnen und Spezialien für Kieferrthopädie, Implantate und Dentalhygiene arbeiten koordiniert und interprofessionell in der Gruppe zusammen. Für Firmen bietet Medbase Corporate Health spezifische Beratungen und ganzheitliche Projekte im betrieblich Gesundheitsmanagement an. In ihren 12 Sports Medical Centern betreut und behandelt Medbase Sportlerinnen und Sportler in Freizeit- und Spitzensport. Medbase sorgt zudem für Nachwuchs im Gesundheitswesen und bildet rund 340 junge Frauen und Männer aus.
