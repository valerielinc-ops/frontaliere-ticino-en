Full description

Motivated by a Logistician's apprenticeship? Ready to become a reference in your profession through quality training in a leading company? Join us!

The Logistician for storage shall ensure the proper storage of the goods using wagons or complex handling systems. Its role is also to manage their monitoring using computer tools. Prior to distribution, he/she prepares the orders, packages the different products and loads them on the appropriate means of transport. The Logistician for storage is also the guarantor of the good supply of stocks.

This trade is suitable for young people who have a sense of responsibility, like working as a team and have good physical resistance.
