Full description

Introduction

For our customer, a renowned company in the construction industry, we are looking for a motivated and responsible personality as a project manager: in construction. You play a key role and control construction projects independently from start to finish.

Tasks

  • Management of construction projects over all phases - from preparation to realisation and final handover
  • Coordination of all project participants and ensuring a smooth project flow
  • Management of deadlines, costs and quality as well as proactive management of project risks and challenges
  • Ensuring compliance with standards, guidelines and safety requirements
  • Conduct and moderate meetings and document progress
  • Preparation of tenders, evaluation of tenders and monitoring of procurement processes
  • Efficient resource and scheduling in close cooperation with the construction management

Profile

  • Training in construction (e.g. civil engineer:in, architect:in, technician:in or construction manager:in)
  • Several years of experience in managing construction projects
  • Sound know-how in construction processes, standards and tenders
  • Experience in dealing with budgets, schedules and contracts
  • Safe in dealing with common tools
  • You are assertive, organized and keep track even in demanding situations
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Additional languages are an advantage.
  • Category B driving licence

Advantages

  • A lot of personal responsibility and the opportunity to actively shape projects
  • Flexible working hours and options for mobile work
  • Support for training and development prospects
  • Modern infrastructure and efficient decision-making processes
