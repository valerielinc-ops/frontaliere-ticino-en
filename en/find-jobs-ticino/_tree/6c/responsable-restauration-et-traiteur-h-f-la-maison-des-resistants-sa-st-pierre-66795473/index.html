Full description

Welcome to a living place

Here, in the heart of the vineyards — the House of Resistants grows much more than grapes. We cultivate an energy, an identity, a way of sharing it together. Our agritourism brings together a vibrant restaurant, a growing cellar, a homemade roasting, a brocante, "resistant" wines, bold spirits, original mistelles, homemade cocktails... And a team that beats the heart of the estate.

A living ecosystem where

  • the vines feed the cellar,
  • The cellar inspires the restaurant,
  • the restaurant makes the House shine,
  • and each person brings his touch to it.

About the restaurant: An unusual place, a large terrace, a garden with games, many park squares and space to imagine beautiful events, welcome artists and make the places vibrate.

The House of Resistants is looking for its/its future Restaurant and Catering Manager M/F.

At the heart of the field, you guarantee the proper functioning of the restaurant, the quality of the customer experience, the coordination of the teams.

Your missions

  • Lead the operational organization of the restaurant, catering services and events in collaboration with the team;
  • Ensure active field presence to ensure quality of service and smooth operation;
  • Organize, plan and coordinate teams according to the activity (planning, monitoring of overtime, extra recruitment, etc.);
  • Monitoring orders, stocks and inventories;
  • Ensure compliance with the House's quality standards, hygiene standards and procedures;
  • Participate in restaurant development, continuous improvement and customer satisfaction;
  • Monitor key management indicators and contribute to the overall performance of the activity

Your profile

  • Experience in service and management of a catering establishment.
  • You are an organized and autonomous person, able to coordinate the activities of the restaurant and caterer while promoting fluid collaboration between the teams.
  • Excellent communication, able to create a strong team cohesion.
  • A spirit of initiative and a sense of self-help.
  • Mastery of HACCP standards.
  • One more: knowledge in wine tourism
  • Mastery of a second language: German and/or English
  • Mastering basic IT tools

Further information

  • Location: Saint Pierre de Clages (VS)
  • 100% Post
  • Collective agreement: NCCT hotel / restaurant
  • Start date: end of January 2026
  • Salary: according to profile and experience
