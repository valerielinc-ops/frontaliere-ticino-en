Full description

For a long-term and technologically demanding transformation program, we are looking for an experienced ICT Safety Architect (aviation) for our customer from Bern.

In this role, you will support the development of state-of-the-art air traffic control and aviation solutions and ensure that systems, processes and architectures meet regulatory and safety requirements.

Your tasks

  • Define, develop and implement safety strategies and safety roadmaps
  • Analyze aviation regulatory requirements and safety-related standards and standards
  • Development of safety requirements for complex air traffic control and aviation systems
  • Creation of safety cases as well as safety-relevant proof and compliance documentation
  • Conducting and accompanying security analyses such as FHA, FMEA or similar methods
  • Supporting certification and certification activities in the regulated aviation environment
  • Coordination of technical and security issues with authorities, partners and internal stakeholders
  • Participation in interdisciplinary architecture, engineering and integration projects

Your profile

  • Several years of experience in the field of safety engineering or safety architecture
  • University degree in computer science, system engineering, electrical engineering or comparable qualification
  • Experience with safety-critical systems, ideally in the aviation, ATM, defence or transport environment
  • Knowledge of relevant safety methodologies and safety demonstrations (e.g. FHA, FMEA, Safety Case)
  • Experience with regulatory requirements and certification processes
  • Understanding complex system and integration architectures
  • Analytical, structured and quality-oriented working method
  • Very good communication skills and pleasure in working with different stakeholders
  • Negotiable knowledge of German mandatory
  • Proper reputation is mandatory
