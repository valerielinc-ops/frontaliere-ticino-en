Full description

"Expect to grow" , personally and professionally: At Siegfried, you can expect exciting challenges and numerous opportunities to demonstrate your talents. As a trusted and respected partner of the pharmaceutical industry, we maintain high standards in safety, quality, and sustainability, while fostering a culture that enables you to succeed. Join a rapidly growing, global company where diverse perspectives and experiences come together in a cooperative environment. Here, you'll find a workplace that values both collective success and your individual contributions, offering you long-term opportunities to develop and make a difference. ## Your Role

  • In our laboratories, more than 90 colleagues work on the development of new processes and the control of adherence to the highest quality standards, supporting our customer-oriented and efficient production. We offer our employees exciting tasks and a high degree of variety.
  • Overall responsibility for coordinating analytical tasks within your projects (e.g., method development, transfer, validation, and troubleshooting) throughout the entire lifecycle, from evaluation to commercial production.
  • Technical leadership and analytical expertise within the assigned projects and technologies in the field of Small Molecules.
  • Representation of Siegfried to customers in project-related meetings, both on-site and virtually.
  • Central interface between Analytics, Chemical Development, Production, Quality Assurance, and other departments.
  • Support in the transfer of knowledge from analytical development to quality control.
  • Creation, maintenance, and review of analytical documents (e.g., specifications, method descriptions, validation and transfer reports, development and campaign reports).
  • Active participation in customer audits regarding analytical issues.
  • Tracking and control of the analytical cost budget within the project plan.
  • Compliance with cGMP regulations and safety standards within your area of responsibility to ensure maximum safety and quality. ## Your Profile ### Technical Skills
  • A Master's or PhD degree in Chemistry, Pharmacy, or a comparable academic education forms the right basis for this role.
  • 2 to 3 years of professional experience in pharmaceutical analytics.
  • In-depth knowledge of chromatographic analysis techniques (e.g., HPLC, GC) in the field of Small Molecules is a must.
  • Knowledge of the application of spectroscopic and wet-chemical analysis techniques with Small Molecules is an advantage.
  • Experience working in a GMP-regulated environment is an advantage. ### Personal Strengths
  • Experience in project management helps you structure your workday.
  • You are service- and customer-oriented and enjoy working in a team.
  • Good German and English language skills in both written and spoken form. ## Your Benefits

  • Monthly mobility allowance.
  • Health insurance: discounts of up to 10% on additional insurances with our partners.
  • The share purchase plan is generous and individually customizable.
  • Child allowance: Siegfried AG increases the child allowance by 10% compared to the cantonal approach.
  • Individual further training opportunities upon agreement.
  • Paid rest breaks, the well-being of our employees is important to us.
  • Reka-Geld: Siegfried AG grants a 20% discount to employees.
  • Pension fund: Our company pension fund is attractive and contributions are individually customizable.
  • Company restaurant / cafeteria: Food at preferential conditions.
  • Central location: Station nearby with very good public transport connections.
  • Access to exclusive corporate benefits and discounts for our employees. ## About Us Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has grown into a global network with 13 locations on three continents. With a team of over 3,800 highly qualified professionals, we bring our pharmaceutical customers' innovations into industrial production and manufacture safe medicines for patients worldwide. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few companies today that can combine the development and manufacture of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and finished dosage forms under one roof. This unique combination of expertise and experience makes us the trusted partner of the pharmaceutical industry.
