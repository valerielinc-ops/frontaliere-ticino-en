Full description

The Seegarten restaurant offers its guests a modern and varied cuisine. To support the kitchen brigade, we are looking for a talented chef for the production and presentation of the food as well as cooperation in all areas of the kitchen. The shifts are mostly with through-service, but can also come to services with room hours.

Luogo di lavoro: Restaurant Seegarten, Rainstrasse 6, 4142 Münchenstein

Important information

Working hours: Monday to Friday between 08:00 and 22:00 We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Preparation / production of food for the self-service and the à la carte restaurant
  • Responsibility for the quality of the food on the respective item
  • Cleaning and control tasks as well as occupational safety within the framework of the HACCP / KOPAS concept
  • Active assistance in the further development of our culinary offer as well as banquets

Requirements

  • No professional experience required if training is available, otherwise 5-10 years: as cook/chef
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): as cook
  • Creativity, joy and passion for cooking
  • High level of teamwork and flexibility as well as independent and precise working methods
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension fund with above-average performance: 8.5%, AG share 17%
  • Social benefits: In the event of illness, the salary for two years is 100%, in the event of accident we are insured SUVA
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
  • Leisure and cultural activities: various discounted or free cultural activities

Contatto

Application and contact Enya Röthlisberger

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.

Grado di occupazione: 100 %
