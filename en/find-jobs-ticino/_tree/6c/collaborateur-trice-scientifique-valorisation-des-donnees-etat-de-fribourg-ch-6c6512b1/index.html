Full description

Who are we?

The Statistics and Data Service (SSD) coordinates the canton's public statistics system. We manage, store, document and disseminate cantonal statistical information. Our mission includes strengthening the use of data in public decision-making, in particular the use of cantonal data using Open Data and internal dashboards.

Why join us?

By joining our service, you will be joining a small team of creative and multidisciplinary people. We encourage reconciliation between work and private life and provide an attractive working environment and conditions for our employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Data recovery strategy: As functional manager, implement the strategy and ensure that projects meet the needs of the canton and users
  • Open Data and KPI: Promote Open Data and value relevant internal data in collaboration with administrative units
  • Data governance: Ensure governance to ensure value quality
  • Team Management: Lead a 2-3-person team in the Data section and coordinate projects
  • Communication: Establish and maintain constructive and collaborative relationships with stakeholders to ensure alignment of objectives
  • Pipelines: Design and maintain data pipelines using technologies like Python and CI/CD

Profile desired

  • Diploma of Tertiary Education (HES, UNI) in Data Science, Management Computer Science or equivalent experience
  • Strong experience in managing complex teams and projects, ideally linked to data products
  • Knowledge of Open Government Data Principles
  • Experience in data governance
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Mastery of Python and ideally Power BI skills
  • Mastery of French or German, with good knowledge of the other language
