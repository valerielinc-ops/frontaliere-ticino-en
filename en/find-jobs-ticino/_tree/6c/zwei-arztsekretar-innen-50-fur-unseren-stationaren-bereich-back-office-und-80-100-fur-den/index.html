Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement

Two medical secretaries, 50% for our inpatient area – Back Office AND 80% – 100% for the reception area Surgical Ambulatorium

You do.

Your tasks – our job 50% in the back office

  • Writing, correcting and formatting medical reports mainly in German (a small part in French)
  • Control and follow-up in the CIS of withdrawal and operations reports
  • Archiving (electronic) and general secretarial work in the stationary sector

Your tasks – our position 80 - 100% in the reception area Surgical Ambulatorium

  • Personal reception and first contact of patients in German and French
  • Telephone service, planning and preparation of office hours
  • Write, correct and format medical reports in French and German
  • Archiving (electronic) and general secretarial work
  • Close cooperation with medical and nursing staff

That sets them apart.

  • You have completed medical training as medical secretary or med. Practical assistant. Advantages with sound professional experience
  • You convince by your reliable and precise working method with which you can ensure high quality standards
  • They have excellent language skills in German for the back office area and French in the ambulatory area, with high style awareness
  • Your profile is rounded off by a team-oriented way of working as well as by commitment, resilience and flexibility.

They benefit from it.

The healthcare sector and thus also the Biel Hospital Center are in an exciting transformation phase in which initiative and self-responsible action are required. You will have the opportunity to actively shape the future of the hospital center with a motivated team. In addition to the employment conditions according to our GAV, we offer you various discounts (pharmacy, public transport or leisure), a restaurant with favorable prices and an internal crèche.
