[Association for the medical and social centre of the region of Sierre]( )

With our more than 560 employees, the Regional Medical and Social Centre of Sierre (Association intercommunale) has the mission of offering various services to the entire population of our 11 member communes. Our main benefits cover a variety of areas, including home care and assistance, social work, employment, prevention and childcare.

Our social services sector is looking for a

Administrative Assistant

50\-60% (over 4 days)

  • Your profile

\- You are in receipt of commercial training (CFC business employee, commercial maturity or equivalent training)

\- You have confirmed professional experience and appreciate the numbers

\- You have excellent mastery of computer tools

\- You show rigor and professional conscience

\- You know how to communicate and appreciate teamwork

\- You are capable of autonomy and flexibility

  • Your mission

\- You ensure the administrative follow-up of social assistance cases, in close cooperation with social workers

\- You ensure the development of monthly budgets, the processing of invoices and the management of administrative procedures related to health and financial benefits

\- You ensure proper record keeping and compliance with cantonal directives and internal procedures

The possibility of integrating a company into various fields of activity with multidisciplinary teams

\- Collaborating advantages with many partners

\- 5 weeks of annual leave, 6 weeks from 40 years

\- Fair and transparent wage policy
