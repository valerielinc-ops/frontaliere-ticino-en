Full description

Introduction

For our Production Division, we are looking for an Industrialization Project Manager (M/F) whose main mission will be to pilot investment projects, in order to ensure the integration and performance of the equipment concerned.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate the integration of new processes in line with project benchmarks, budgets and objectives
  • Animate needs analysis, integrate customer needs, technical benchmarks and propose project scoping
  • Justifying the need for change and contributing to the consolidation of continuous improvement plans and investment programmes
  • Managing information and decision-making, and facilitating reporting, gap resolution and decision-making
  • Build all contributors around goals and value teamwork
  • Documenting industrial scenarios and technical solutions
  • Capitalising on good practice and contributing to the development of benchmarks
  • Lead business planning and risk removal

Profile

  • Diploma of engineer HES, EPF or equivalent
  • Experience in industrial project management.
  • Ability to take a step backwards to steer a group of experts across the board.
  • Natural leadership and pedagogy to motivate and unite a team.
  • Autonomy, sense of responsibility and ability to be a force of proposal.
  • Relationship with clear communication and listening ability.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
