Full description

The gastronomy of the cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland is diverse, modern and dynamic. Whether Migros restaurant, Migros take away, shared gastronomy or catering services – we offer every customer a tasteful experience.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Restaurant, Migros Restaurant Calandapark, Pargherastrasse 1-3, 7000 Chur

Mansioni

  • You are actively involved and responsible for the presentation and staging of the food at the buffet depending on the time of day (breakfast, salad and dessert buffet, etc.)
  • You actively serve and advise guests in the buffet area and at the checkout to ensure perfect service.
  • With your help with equipment in the cold and/or warm kitchen, you help to create delicious food that gives you pleasure.
  • You make sure that the hygiene regulations according to food safety are adhered to and consistently implemented
  • You ensure that the dishes are always clean and ready for the next use – for a pleasant experience of our guests.
  • With your dedication to cleaning work in the guest room, restaurant and kitchen, you make everyone feel comfortable.

Requirements

  • Professional experience: an advantage in gastronomy
  • Completed basic or further training: in gastronomy or in the retail trade
  • You love being in touch with people and enjoy getting our guests excited
  • You enjoy working in a team, are reliable and support your colleagues
  • You are a team player, flexible and ready to go anywhere
  • Independent and accurate work is a matter of course for you
  • You are ready to continuously train yourself and learn new things
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • L-GAV: Attractive Land collective employment contract
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Insurance: Benefit Conditions at Various Insurance Companies
