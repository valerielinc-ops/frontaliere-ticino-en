Type of contract: CDI Rate: 100% Date of entry: 1 January 2027 Place : Rue de la Sionge 37, 1630 Bulle

Your role as Dental Assistant

  • Clinical assistance: To assist the dentist during the consultations, to carry out certain practices according to his specialization. Disinfect and sterilize instruments, perform x-rays
  • Care Management: Welcoming patients, assisting in the preparation and management of treatments, monitoring patient records and adherence to clinical protocols.
  • Technical Innovation: Use state-of-the-art equipment and adapt quickly to new dental technologies and methods. Collaborate with Dental Lab Ardentis.
  • Education and Prevention: Advise patients on good oral hygiene practices and contribute to their education.
  • Development and Training: Participate in ongoing training to keep up to date on the latest developments in the sector.
  • Various responsibilities within the clinic.

Your profile

  • Holder of a dental assistant CFC with confirmed experience (minimum 3 years).
  • Equivalence recognized by SEFRI with a minimum of 3 years of professional activity in Switzerland.
  • Passion for patient care, availability and ability to build a relationship of trust.
  • Aspiration to evolve in a dynamic environment, favouring optimal working conditions, promoting knowledge sharing and mutual assistance, while seeking a fulfilling and pleasant professional experience.
  • Perfectly comfortable in French, good English knowledge a bonus, and an easy and engaging approach.
  • Excellent mastery of computer tools.

Why join Ardentis?

  • As soon as you enter Ardentis, you will receive a personalized integration training tailored to your needs.
  • You will evolve in a benevolent environment, conducive to growth, where you can excel and innovate.
  • We offer you opportunities for continuing education and professional development to nurture your growth throughout your career at Ardentis.
  • By integrating Ardentis, you will be able to immerse yourself in a corporate culture that values sharing and team spirit, giving great importance to sustainability.
  • Very good working conditions and wages, with monthly premiums for responsibilities.
  • 5 weeks of vacation a year.

Join us and become a driving force in a community dedicated to dental excellence. The adventure begins here!
