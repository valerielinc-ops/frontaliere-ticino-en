Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Nursing specialist HF 70-100 %(m/f/d) Psychiatry Baselland On the open-guided ward for addiction diseases of Psychiatry Baselland you can expect a versatile and varied field of work. In this professional and supportive environment, you accompany patients who are impaired in their personal, social and professional lives as a result of an addiction.

Their tasks

  • Holistic care and care of patients who are impaired in their personal, social and professional life as a result of an addiction and who come to planned inpatient withdrawal
  • Holistic care and support of patients in substitution
  • Implementation of individual care and sociotherapeutic measures using evidence-based methods
  • Comprehensive support and networking through involvement and advice of relatives and external specialist bodies
  • Participation in the professional development of care and treatment concepts, as well as the co-design and implementation of new station-specific concepts

Your profile

  • Care professional HF / FH
  • Experience in the field of psychiatry, ideally in the field of addiction
  • Joy and motivation to work in the inpatient treatment setting
  • Enjoying innovative interprofessional collaboration in a team
  • Open and communicative personality and structured and precise working method
