Full description

At our location in Dintikon you check and document returns and warranty cases from the post office and from our retail locations. Together with your team and our suppliers, you ensure that every case finds the fastest way to solve it. The job is limited to 6 months – if it fits on both sides, let’s talk about an extension or permanent employment.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG main camp Dintikon, parallel road, 5606 Dintikon

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Timely recording and customer-oriented processing of returns and warranty cases in our ERP
  • Responsibility for quality and quantity of returns and guarantee cases, from completeness to the right solution for the customer
  • Forwarding the cases to the right place: Refurbishing, manufacturers, suppliers or service partners
  • Further processing of withdrawals from our retail locations
  • Control of incoming shipments from suppliers, manufacturers and service centres before they reach internal and external customers
  • Exchange with customer service, refurbishing and other internal teams

Requirements

  • 1-2 years: processing related to return and guarantee processes
  • Completed initial or continuing education: logistics specialist with commercial diploma
  • Good PC user knowledge in MS Office and ERP systems as well as the desire to acquire the necessary know-how in editing
  • Motivation, personal responsibility and readiness to keep a cool head even when the packages stack
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Taking calculated risks to create value and drive growth
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (very good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)

  • Personal responsibility & freedom: Our working environment is characterized by our values, an open feedback and a positive culture of error

  • Values instead of blabla: cooperative, innovative, pirate, self-responsible, ambitious – these five values we all demand and live.
  • Boldly think for yourself: We encourage everyone to think for themselves and actively. And love it when you think bravely, newly and differently.
  • Uncomplicated: We avoid the unnecessary and focus on the important. From mastering challenges to celebrating our successes.
  • Flexibility instead of bending (non-office jobs): Every position is also possible in 80%.
  • Rapid growth: With us, you are constantly growing and developing. And get EDU points (2'000 CHF for employees in Switzerland, 1'600 EUR for German employees and 700 EUR for our Serbian employees) for your further development, so that you do not stand still.
  • Further benefits Switzerland: Benefit from 25 days of holidays, exclusive employee prices in the shop, mobile, Internet and TV subscriptions at the company rate and other benefits.
