Full description

Psychotherapeutically shaped clinical medicine with clear treatment focuses and the time to really go into depth. In Aadorf, Canton of Thurgau, we are looking for a specialist doctor or a senior physician for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the amount of 70-100% by appointment. She expects a medically managed specialist clinic for psychotherapy, psychosomatics and psychiatry with an integrative treatment concept in a calm, appreciative atmosphere. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • You take over the integrated psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of inpatients in your specialist areas
  • They independently bear case responsibility from diagnosis to therapy planning to implementation
  • They treat people with depression, anxiety disorders and burnout, patients of the 50+ generation as well as with eating and personality disorders
  • You work closely interdisciplinary with the psychotherapy, care and therapy team
  • They take over supervision and professional support of assistant doctors* in the appropriate function

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy or advanced training
  • Experience in psychiatric-psychotherapeutic and psychosomatic treatment
  • Interest in an integrative, psychotherapeutically oriented treatment approach

That sets them apart.

  • Patient-centered, trusting basic attitude in psychiatric-psychotherapeutic care
  • Enjoy independent case management and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Careful, structured working method with high professional standards

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Responsible work in a medically managed specialist clinic
  • Clear treatment priorities and an integrative, psychotherapeutically oriented concept
  • No crisis intervention, emergency or closed station operation, thus planning work structures
  • Clear corporate structure with short decision-making paths and an appreciative working atmosphere
  • Diverse therapeutic environment with individual and group psychotherapy as well as body, movement and design therapies

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
