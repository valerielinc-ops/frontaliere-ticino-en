Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss delivery and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals and retailers).

Tasks

  • You advise our customers actively, friendly and expertly
  • You make sure that the product presentation is top, fresh and seasonal
  • You independently manage the ordering and ensure full availability
  • You monitor actions, labeling and sales places according to the concept
  • You recognize customer wishes and forward them to the department management
  • You support your manager in day-to-day business and train employees and learners “on the job”

Requirements

  • Completed training in the retail trade or in the food industry (e.g. chef, baker:in)
  • You work reliably, efficiently and have a well-kept appearance
  • You are fit to deal with MS Office
  • You approach people openly and enjoy customer contact
  • You are resilient and stay friendly even in hectic moments
  • You speak fluent German and enjoy working in a team

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
