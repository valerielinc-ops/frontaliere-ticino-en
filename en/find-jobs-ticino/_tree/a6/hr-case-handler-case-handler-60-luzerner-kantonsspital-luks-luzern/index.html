Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by agreement reinforcement. Tasks: • Responsible together in the team Reliable handling of administrative HR processes from entry to exit • They manage the personnel and wage administration and maintain all salary and social security relevant data carefully in SAP • You advise and support our employees on administrative issues relating to a wide range of HR topics competently and service-oriented • As part of the harmonization of HR processes, you are actively involved in the further development of processes and in cooperation with LUKS HR Requirements • You have a commercial basic education or an equivalent education, a further education as HR assistant / HR assistant or clerk / clerk HR management as well as experience in a comparable administrative HR function. We require very good user knowledge in Microsoft 365 and SAP, an HR certificate is an advantage • They organize different tasks, deadlines and concerns in a structured manner, set priorities in a targeted manner and work efficiently and independently. • Even with critical feedback or different expectations, you remain professional, respond appreciatively to your counterpart and maintain your pronounced service orientation. • With your understanding of processes, you recognize connections, keep an eye on the big picture and contribute constructively to the further development of processes. We offer: • You take on a versatile HR function with a wide range of tasks and direct contact with our employees • You actively participate in the harmonization and further development of HR processes and can use your experience and knowledge in a targeted manner. • In a motivated team, you work closely with different HR specialists and shape cooperation in the evolving HR environment • Through the exchange within the LUKS Group you benefit from a large HR network and versatile professional perspectives.
