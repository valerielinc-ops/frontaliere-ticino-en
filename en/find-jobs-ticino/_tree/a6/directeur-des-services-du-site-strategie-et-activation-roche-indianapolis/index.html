The Position Are you a visionary leader ready to drive transformational change at a global scale? The Director of Site Services, Strategy & Enablement will serve as a strategic architect for the Indianapolis Site, translating Roche's global mission into local operational excellence while strengthening our impact on patients, customers, and employees. This highly visible, senior leadership role offers the opportunity to design and execute innovative site strategies, lead a high-performing customer-focused organization, and represent Roche as a trusted partner within the Indiana business community—all while building the operational infrastructure that accelerates progress from vision to measurable outcomes.

The Opportunity

  • Develop, execute and maintain long-range strategic planning in collaboration with global standards, identifying opportunities that drive impact and experience locally, regionally, and globally while translating organizational strategy into actionable business goals for a high-performance, customer-focused site organization.
