Full description

The employer is looking for one

Responsible for cultural mediation

Mansions

  • participates, under the responsibility of the direction of the Museum, to the definition of programs of mediation actions;
  • defines, coordinates and evaluates the mediation activities and the necessary supports; in particular defines and realizes the program of specific days, involving adults, boys and families;
  • creates and maintains relations with organisms outside the Museum that act as intermediaries for the various public of the Museum (communication, promotion);
  • defines together with the direction of the Museum the necessary support for the promotion of cultural mediation activities and edits the promotional texts of them (for the printed, website, newsletters);
  • updates the website regarding cultural mediation activities;
  • recruits, forms and directs collaborators participating in cultural mediation projects (guides, animators, accompanying staff);
  • contributes, together with the direction of the Museum, to the formation of the reception and surveillance staff for its specific sector;
  • is responsible, under supervision and in accordance with the direction of the Museum, of the budget of cultural mediation activities;
  • is kept up-to-date on the latest news and trends concerning cultural mediation and deals with contacts with colleagues in the sector.

Requirements

  • academic title in history of art, history, anthropology or equivalent titles;
  • experience as a cultural mediator/mediator in a museum institute;
  • critical sense and creativity;
  • organizational skills and skills in the field of communication and in the drafting of texts;
  • the coordination experience of a team is a preferential title;
  • prepared to carry out works independently or in teams and organizational skills;
  • ability and ease in interpersonal relationships and attitude to contact with the user;
  • flexibility in the tasks entrusted to you/he;
  • availability at work outside hours (can be called/to work in the evening, on weekends and public holidays at events and openings);
  • knowledge of at least one second national language and/or English;
  • knowledge of the office package.

Details of use

  • Bellinzona workplace
  • employment at 50%
  • use from 01.01.2027 (or date to be determined), indefinitely
  • Sunday work and festive work
  • wages to be agreed

We offer

  • work in a hungry team;
  • diversified and stimulating activities;
  • the opportunity to participate in the development of the Villa dei Cedri Museum on the territory.

Conditions

The employment contract is subject to the Code of Bonds (CO). The wage conditions will be bilaterally agreed between the parties.
