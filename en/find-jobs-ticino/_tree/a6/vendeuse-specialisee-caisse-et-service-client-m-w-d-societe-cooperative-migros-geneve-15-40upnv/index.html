Full description

Looking for a versatile position where your rigor and ability to interact with customers make a difference? You will play a key role in providing an exceptional shopping experience. Your passion for service will be your best asset to succeed in this challenging position. Get out of here!

Luogo di Lavoro: Migros Cooperative Society Geneva, M Airport, Airport Route 25, 1215 Geneva 15

Important remarks

The use of women aims at simplicity, but our recruitment is open to all.

Mansioni

  • You welcome, you listen and you advise customers.
  • You ensure cash transactions and respond effectively to customer requests (returns, refunds, claims).
  • You perform the tasks of managing a cashier, customer service and flower department. You advise them to help them find the perfect floral compositions for every occasion.
  • You answer phone calls and you ensure the transmission of communications and information.
  • You contribute to the good organization of the caisse area, the souk area and you coordinate the breaks of the caisse team.
  • You work with the team to ensure transparent and seamless communication, ensuring an exceptional customer experience.

Requirements

  • 2-5 years of experience: in retailing with customer contact
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Retail Manager
  • Smile, versatility, patience and rigour
  • Excellent communication skills and natural ease in customer contacts
  • Orientation solutions, great ability to keep calm, comfortable in a dynamic environment, stress resistance
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Catering: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus Points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on the assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and free of charge for a savings account.

Contatto

Candidature and contact Djaina Julliard Talent Acquisition Specialist

Recruitment process

  • Preselection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Professional interview: During the professional interview, the manager and/or other specialized person, or even the team, asks specific questions about knowledge to accomplish the work. If possible, then there is more time to get to know the workstation and team in a broken stick discussion. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and contract preparation (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then launched by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week

Grado di occupazione: 85%
