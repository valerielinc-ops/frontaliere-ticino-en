Full description

Spital Oberengadin AG, a company of the KSGR Group, is responsible for basic medical care and emergency care for the population and guests in Samedan, with the largest hospital in southern Germany. We are searching for one by 1 April 2027 or by agreement

Their main duties

  • Care and support of patients in demanding situations
  • Vital function monitoring
  • invasive activities by competence profile
  • Administration of medicines by competence profile
  • independent documentation in the in-house patient documentation system (KISIM)
  • General administrative work
  • Support of the medical and nursing team in everyday work
  • Ensuring a high quality of care and treatment

Your profile

  • completed training as medical assistant (MPA) EFZ
  • Experience in emergency care is beneficial
  • Safety in handling medical devices and procedures
  • high social skills and empathy in dealing with patients
  • Responsibility, resilience and teamwork
  • independent and structured working method
  • High flexibility
