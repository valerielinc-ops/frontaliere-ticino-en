CAE System Specialist (a), 80-100% — Bachem AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals.

To strengthen our team in Engineering Services, we are looking for a dedicated CAD / CAE system specialist (f/m/d), 80 – 100%.

In this function, you are responsible for ensuring smooth operation and the further development of our AVEVA authoring tools as a key user and administrator, as well as for the creation of plans within the framework of projects for conversion or refurbishment. New construction of production or infrastructure facilities.

Their tasks

AVEVA Super-User / Administration

  • Support and administration of AVEVA authoring tools (e.g. E3D, Unified Engineering or comparable solutions)
  • First- and second-level support for users
  • Establishment, maintenance and further development of templates, standards and databases
  • Provision of training and support to new users
  • Interface management with IT, engineering and external partners

CAD/CAE

  • 3D installation and pipeline planning for complex chemical and pharmaceutical plants.
  • Creation of further design documents, such as R&I schemes, isometrics, parts lists and mounting drawings
  • the spatial coordination of all trades, such as buildings, electrical, EMSR, ventilation and clean room.
  • Monitoring of plant implementation
  • Support for the implementation of BIM

Your profile

  • Completed training e.g. as a constructor in plant or building planning or comparable
  • Several years of professional experience in plant planning, process and/or building technology (preferably in the chemical / pharmaceutical environment)
  • In-depth knowledge of AVEVA CAD/CAE tools, experience in the role of key user, CAD administrator or super user of advantage
  • Several years of experience in CAD design (2D/3D)
  • High load capacity and a weakness for digital planning
  • Fluent knowledge of German, very good knowledge of English in word and writing
