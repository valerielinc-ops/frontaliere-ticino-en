Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a Project Manager Applications (m/f/d) 100% to complement our project team in Reinach. In this role, you combine profound product, application and system knowledge in gas measurement technology with an active project management function. You take on the technical and commercial responsibility for customer projects and lead them from the first request and conception through the preparation of offers and implementation to the successful conclusion. You ensure that sophisticated solutions for gas analysis and continuous emission measurement systems (CEMS) are implemented in a customer-friendly, economical and time- and quality-compliant manner.

What is this point about?

Are you enthusiastic about sophisticated gas measurement technology and industrial emissions monitoring? Would you like to find solutions for individual customer applications and combine technical expertise with project management, consulting and commercial responsibility? As Project Manager Applications, you are both a technical contact person and a project manager for gas analysis and CEMS solutions. You control customer projects from request to completion, coordinate internal and external stakeholders and keep an eye on deadlines, costs, quality and scope of services. In this way, you make an important contribution to customer satisfaction and sales development in the Sales Center Switzerland.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • lead technical customer projects in the field of gas measurement technology, gas analysis and CEMS from planning to successful completion.
  • Develop, design and convert customer-specific measurement, application and system solutions into technically and economically convincing offers.
  • Advise customers and internal sales offices and support them with technical clarifications, customer visits and commissioning.
  • Actively manage the deadlines, costs, quality, scope of services and risks of your projects and coordinate the internal and external specialist departments involved.
  • Actively track offers, identify additional customer potential and document relevant activities and contacts reliably in the CRM system.
  • Continuously develop your product, application and standards knowledge and actively contribute to the team.

What do we expect from you?

  • have basic technical training, ideally in automation, electrical engineering, process engineering, environmental engineering or a comparable field.
  • bring experience in the management and handling of technical customer projects as well as knowledge of the main project management instruments.
  • Ideally have experience in gas measurement technology, gas analysis or industrial emission measurement (CEMS) as well as knowledge in the design and integration of analysis systems.
  • understand complex technical contexts and can convey them in a customer-oriented, solution-oriented and economical manner.

  • convince through a professional appearance and clear communication and have very good German and English skills
  • Other Swiss national languages are beneficial.

  • We promote lifelong learning through diverse training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We attach great importance to your work-life balance with flexible working hours and the possibility of home office
  • We offer a modern working environment with amenities such as an in-house canteen.
