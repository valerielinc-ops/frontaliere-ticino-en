The Position At Roche, patients come first. At the Basel Drug Substance (DS) Manufacturing site, we have around 600 employees to ensure commercial and clinical drug supply via three modern technology streams. Our Materials Management (MM) unit manages the seamless material supply and production planning for the site. We are looking for a strong, structured personality as Tech Transfer & Launch Manager. The focus of this challenging role is on technical transfers, scale-ups and product launches within our Small Molecules (SM) Value Stream in Bau 50.

The opportunity

In this function, you are the central interface for all MM activities of Small Molecules Technical Development (PTDC). You lead cross-product teams for launch projects and ensure highly efficient, GMP-compliant material delivery. Furthermore:

  • Subteam management & SPOC: Establishment, management and representation of the Materials Management Subteam as primary contact (SPOC) for Small Molecules transfer projects
  • E2E material control: coordination and operational planning of all direct materials (chemicals, starting materials, solvents and packaging)
  • BOM & Order Strategy: Close cooperation with operational and project chemists to create dummy BOMs, master data maintenance and definition of order strategies
  • Interface & Supplier Management: Management of supplier relationships in close coordination with global procurement and supplier quality management
  • Cost & risk control: calculation of direct material costs, risk assessments for recovery and implementation of lean process improvements
