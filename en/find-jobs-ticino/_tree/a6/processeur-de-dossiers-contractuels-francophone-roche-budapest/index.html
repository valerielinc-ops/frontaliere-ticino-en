The Opportunity

As a Contract Records Processor, you are responsible for establishing governance for contract records management, implementing modern archiving methodology, screening, summarizing, and entering legal contract data to provide complete, accurate profiles of contracts in Contract Lifecycle Management System (CLM) and ensuring adherence to document retention policies across Roche affiliates.

  • Utilizing ticketing system for incoming requests and issues
  • Support end users and requesters on system related questions, trainings, providing consultancy services on existing functionalities of the system
  • Utilizing multiple data sources to confirm and update contract data in contract data migration collection sheet
  • Reviewing data in database to check for completeness and accuracy
  • Managing multiple data cleanup for different Groups simultaneously
  • Work with team members within Global Procurement to achieve project uniformity and provide feedback of project tasks
  • Coordinate project duties with other team members

Who you are
