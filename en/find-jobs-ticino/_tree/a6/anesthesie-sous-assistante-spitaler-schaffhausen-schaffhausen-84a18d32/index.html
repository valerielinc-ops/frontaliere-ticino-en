In a modern environment, the Department of Anesthesia and Intensive Care Medicine performs approximately 6,000 anesthesias annually in a wide range of disciplines. The interdisciplinary intensive care unit with 8 beds and the ambulance service are also part of our area of responsibility.

  • Support for assistant doctors in everyday hospital life
  • Participation in training events

  • free accommodation in the nearby staff house
  • Regular working hours

This will take us further.
