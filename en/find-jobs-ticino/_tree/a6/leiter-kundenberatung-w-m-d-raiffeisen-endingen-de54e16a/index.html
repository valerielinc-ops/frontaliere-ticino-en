Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Surbtal-Wehntal stands for personal advice, regional ties and long-term customer relationships. With our locations in Lengnau AG, Endingen, Schneisingen and Niederweningen, we are close to the people and are actively involved in our region. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and customer-oriented leader as Head of Customer Consulting (m/f/d) 100%.

What awaits you?

  • You take responsibility for the area of customer service and lead your team professionally and personnel.
  • You ensure that everything runs smoothly in day-to-day business and use the human resources as required.
  • You are on the day-to-day business and ensure with a forward-looking assignment planning that our customers are reliably looked after.
  • In counseling, you yourself are in the middle of it. You accompany customers around saving, paying, investing and provisioning.
  • You recognize their needs and establish the connection to our specialist areas for further concerns.
  • At the same time, you will continue to develop existing customer relationships, recognize potential and win new customers with your team.
  • Because good customer advice never stops, you bring your ideas and actively shape the further development of the area.

What do you bring?

  • You have completed banking training and bring in-depth experience in customer advice.
  • Leadership is yours. Ideally, you could already gain initial leadership experience and successfully accompany and develop employees.
  • SAQ certification is an advantage.
  • You convince with an open, communicative way, think entrepreneurially and like to get involved.
  • Organizational talent and a good feeling for people help you to keep an overview even in a lively daily business.
  • You like to be visible, actively approach people and represent our bank at customer events and other internal and external events.

Are you looking for a job where every day is the same? Then we are probably not the right team for you. You want to shape, take responsibility for your team and our customers and jump into the cold water? Then we should get to know each other. Jens Eschweiler Head of Customer Services Why Raiffeisen?

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.
