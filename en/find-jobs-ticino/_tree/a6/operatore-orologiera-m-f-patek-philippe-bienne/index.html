Full description

Mission

As part of this function attached to Cadrans Fluckiger SA Site in Bruegg, near Bienne, you will be in charge of performing various operations using a brush, microphones and small machine on a variety of parts for making dials.

Responsibilities

  • Perform different production operations on machines.
  • Perform component washing operations.
  • Ensure bonding operations according to established procedures.
  • Ensure quality control of the parts produced.

Profile sought

  • 0 to 2 years of experience in a similar position, as Vernisseur a serious asset.
  • Habits working with chemicals.
  • Excellent visual acuity.
  • Good dexterity.
  • Computer skills confirmed.
  • Team spirit.
  • Rigor and accuracy.
  • Autonomy and organizational sense.
  • Ability to hold a conversation in German.
