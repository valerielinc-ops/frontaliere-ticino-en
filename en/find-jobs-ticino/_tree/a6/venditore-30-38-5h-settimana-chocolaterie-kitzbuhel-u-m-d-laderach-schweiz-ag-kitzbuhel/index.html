Full description

Our Swiss chocolate manufacturer, Läderach, is known for its high-quality fresh chocolate. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company today employs over 2500 people in over 80 countries, with a growing trend. Our headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates and develop new and innovative ideas in agile teams. The «Retail» department comprises over 250 chocolateries in over 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite our customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to be part of it? Then come to us. This is what you can expect. You advise and sell our diverse product range, for example, when selecting gifts. You take care of the cash handling and ensure precise and correct billing. You present our select confectionery and chocolate assortment in an inviting and tasteful way. You adhere to our high quality standards with dedication and care. You ensure that our hygiene and freshness concept is upheld. What you bring. You bring valuable professional experience in retail and have a genuine passion for sales. You love making our customers happy and offering them a unique shopping experience. Even in turbulent times, you prioritize quality and customer orientation above all else. You take on responsibility, are a true team player, and work hand in hand with your colleagues. You appreciate open and respectful communication and are fluent in German and English; knowledge of French and other languages is an additional advantage. What we offer. An exciting work environment with many opportunities for development. Motivated and committed colleagues (internal and external). Opportunities for further education. Daily chocolate enjoyment and a generous personal discount. Discounts in the areas of holidays, clothing, and much more (Benefits at work). Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh. For this position, we offer the KV minimum salary of CHF 2,251.- with a 100% workload. Depending on your experience and qualifications, a collective contractual overpayment is possible. Sounds interesting? Then apply with us. Mario Bromm, Branch Manager, Tel. +43535673427, is happy to answer any questions you may have.
