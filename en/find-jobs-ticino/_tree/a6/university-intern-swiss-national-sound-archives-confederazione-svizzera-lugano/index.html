Living memory of Switzerland for all

The Swiss National Library collects Helvetica - texts, images and sounds of Switzerland - and secures the collective memory of Switzerland with its collections. In cooperation with other institutions, it enables wide access to the cultural heritage of the country.

The Swiss National Library is a section of the Swiss National Library. It is also the sound archive of Switzerland and takes care of the sounding cultural heritage of our country. These include recordings of classical music and of rock, jazz and folk music, audiobooks, stories, plays, interviews, sound documents from science and research, field recordings or private collections.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
