Full description

More than 1,000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: the CSD Group is a dynamic and growing company. Thanks to the ingenuity, skill and multidisciplinarity that characterize our teams, CSD offers innovative and sustainable solutions for our customers with a "plus" for quality of life and the environment. We will search for our Road Infrastructure Department in Geneva (GE), a 3D Civil Projector & BIM (m/f/d) 80-100%. Your missions

  • Develop 3D plans and models for infrastructure projects
  • Develop and maintain BIM digital models
  • Achieving Phase, Implementation and Coordination Plans
  • Participate in the modelling of earthworks, networks, roads and works
  • Ensuring the graphic and technical quality of deliverables
  • Collaborate with different disciplines within multidisciplinary BIM environments
  • Participate in the continuous improvement of our digital methods and standards

Your profile

  • Training of draftsman in civil engineering, geomatics, projector or equivalent
  • Good control of Autodesk Civil 3D
  • High interest in BIM and new design technologies
  • Team spirit, autonomy and rigour
  • Want to learn and progress in a stimulating environment
  • Experience on infrastructure projects is an asset
