At STRABAG, around 89,000 people at more than 2,400 locations worldwide are making progress. Uniqueness and individual strengths characterize not only our projects, but also each and every one of us. Equal opportunities, diversity and inclusion are integral parts of what makes us a company and how we work. Together we implement projects successfully and in partnership and grow in new tasks. Together we create great things. Let's make progress!

Construction manager: in construction and engineering with ambition for branch management 80-100 % Your contribution with us

  • You take over as a construction manager: in the field of civil engineering and/or building construction
  • You handle construction projects independently
  • You take over the work preparation, logistics and construction site organization
  • You create dimensions and billings correctly and on time
  • You are responsible for meeting costs, deadlines and quality
  • You supervise and coordinate employees and subcontractors
  • You contribute to acquisition and calculation

What matters to us

  • You have completed training and further education as a construction manager: in (HFP/HF), technician: in HF, Dipl. Civil engineer: or comparable technical training
  • You bring in-depth professional experience in engineering and/or building construction
  • You work independently, committed and solution-oriented
  • You have a strong technical understanding and enjoyment of complex projects
  • You convince with analytical skills and leadership

Our added value for you

  • A responsible role with a lot of room for manoeuvre and short decision paths
  • Collaboration with a well-established team and close connection to exciting construction projects in the Mittelland region
  • Multifaceted projects in building and engineering with direct influence on their success
  • Attractive employment conditions as well as targeted training and development opportunities
  • Modern work equipment and flexible solutions for work-life balance
  • Health offers, employee discounts, company events and other attractive benefits
