Full description

You are responsible for Projects & Service High Voltage Systems with around 15 employees. Together they shape the cooperation and realize plant construction projects as well as maintenance services for BKW’s own high-voltage network and customers throughout Switzerland.

Your field of activity

  • For the Projects & Service High Voltage Plants division, you assume the professional, organizational, personnel and economic overall responsibility
  • You lead six project leaders directly and create framework conditions that enable orientation and decision-making spaces for self-responsible cooperation.
  • Together with the employees and the management team, you shape the orientation, cooperation and culture of the high-voltage equipment department
  • Together with your employees, you ensure that projects and service orders are handled securely, economically, in high quality and on schedule.
  • When acquiring, calculating and creating offers, you bring together technical solutions, customer needs and economic goals
  • You maintain long-term customer relationships throughout Switzerland and represent the area to internal and external partners

You bring that with you

  • You have completed a degree in electrical engineering at an FH, ETH or university
  • Experience in project management of infrastructure projects forms your professional basis
  • As an experienced manager, you promote personal responsibility and develop employees in a targeted manner.
  • You combine technical understanding with an eye for efficiency, quality and deadlines
  • In customer contact, you convince with negotiating skills, commitment and a strong customer orientation
  • You communicate negotiably in German and ideally have very good knowledge of French
