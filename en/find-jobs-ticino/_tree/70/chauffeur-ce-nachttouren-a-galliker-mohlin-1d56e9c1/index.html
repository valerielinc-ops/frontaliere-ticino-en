Full description

Chauffeur CE / Night Tours (a) Our branch in Möhlin AG is a centre of excellence for transport and logistics within the national Galliker network. Thanks to our location close to the border, we handle fruit and vegetables, cut flowers and plants and provide just-in-time service for automotive spare parts. To complement our team of drivers, we are looking for motivated chauffeurs.

Responsibilities

  • Carrying out refrigerated transports with semitrailer trucks throughout Switzerland
  • Shuttle service between the various locations in Switzerland
  • Pick-ups of flowers and plants
  • Assignment from Monday to Friday, start of work between 1:00 p.m. and 5:00 p.m.

Requirements profile

  • CE driving licence and valid driver qualification certificate
  • Reliable, conscientious, professional and passionate
  • Flexible, determined and independent
  • Enjoyment of a modern vehicle fleet
  • Very good knowledge of German, French is an advantage

Offer

  • Careful and thorough familiarization with the area of responsibility
  • Exciting and varied workplace with a lot of personal responsibility
  • State-of-the-art vehicle technology
  • asa-recognized training center for CZV
