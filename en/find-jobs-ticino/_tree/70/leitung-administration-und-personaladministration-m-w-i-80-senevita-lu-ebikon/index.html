Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita Pilatusblick in Ebikon we are looking for 15. February 2027 or by arrangement a management administration with HR tasks 80% Your tasks

  • You lead and organize the administration team and ensure smooth processes in day-to-day business.
  • You are the calling card of our house and the first contact person for residents, relatives and guests.
  • You ensure the correct settlement of residents and monitor the administrative financial processes.
  • You coordinate apartment visits, accompany interested parties and are jointly responsible for the rental of our apartments and care rooms.
  • You support the management in organizational, administrative and business tasks.
  • You are actively involved in the optimization and further development of our administrative processes.
  • You support HR managers in various tasks in personnel administration.

You bring that with you

  • Completed commercial training or equivalent qualification and further training in human resources
  • Several years of professional experience in administration and HR, preferably in the long-term care sector
  • You always meet other people with a smile and appreciate the interdisciplinary cooperation
  • You enjoy dealing with older people and working with different stakeholders
  • You think networked, recognize connections and understand operational processes
  • High commitment, teamwork and resilience distinguish you
  • An independent, structured and careful working method is a matter of course for you

We offer you

  • Attractive training offer of the Senevita Academy, which inspires ongoing development.
  • An environment where vocational training is written GROSS and brings a breath of fresh air into the house.
  • Free drinks and discounted food - heartily prepared by our gastro team.
  • Various perks (SBB half-ax, fueling, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 weeks, from 60 years 7 weeks).
  • Unique culture and various generous employee events
  • Very good accessibility (direct motorway connection, location directly at Ebikon station).
  • Our commitment: You as a person are important to us!
