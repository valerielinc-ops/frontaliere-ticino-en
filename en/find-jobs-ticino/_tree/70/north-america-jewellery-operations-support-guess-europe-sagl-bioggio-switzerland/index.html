Full description

The North America Jewellery Operations Support acts as a key link between the HQ Jewellery team in Bioggio and the North American local teams, ensuring accurate product set-up, order and shipment coordination, retail allocation activities and day-to-day follow-up across full-price stores, outlet stores, and e-commerce. As these activities have been progressively brought in-house, this role will play an important part in consolidating operations across North America.
