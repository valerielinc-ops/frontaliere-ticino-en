Full description

Versatile metal construction projects are managed independently from start to finish. The company attaches great importance to close cooperation with customers and partners. With your structured way of working and team orientation, you can successfully complete the project. We are looking for a job for our client

  • You will independently manage versatile metal construction projects from start to finish.
  • You will maintain relationships with customers, suppliers and other project partners and ensure constructive cooperation.
  • Together with the responsible design engineer, you will take part in construction meetings.
  • You will coordinate project execution closely with the assembly team and coordinate the deadlines with internal production.
  • You will monitor deadlines, costs and quality standards and ensure successful project completion.

  • Ideally experience in project or construction management.
  • Organizational talent, technical understanding and spatial imagination.
