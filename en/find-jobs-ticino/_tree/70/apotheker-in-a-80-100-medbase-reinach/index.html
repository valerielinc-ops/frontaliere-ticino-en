Welcome to the Medbase Pharmacy Reinach

Welcome to the Medbase Pharmacy Reinach We have been proud to count the Hornstein pharmacy/drugstore in Reinach as part of the Medbase Group since June. At this lively location, we are allowed to present pharmacies, drugstores and health food stores under one roof. It is impossible to imagine life without our long-standing and well-coordinated team, which accompanies us into the future. Our customer base appreciates the extremely competent and committed advice, which has resulted in close customer relationships, which we continue to cultivate with equal commitment. The pharmacy has made a name for itself with its extremely wide range of products from conventional medicine to complementary medicine to the regionally outstanding special offer with the health food store. We also boast free parking, employee-friendly opening hours and a location in the immediate vicinity of Migros and Coop Restaurant.

What you can do

The focus of your work at Medbase will be on holistic care and pharmaceutically competent advice for our customers on all health issues. As part of a dedicated team, you will actively shape everyday pharmacy life and make a significant contribution to the comprehensive care of our patients.

  • Competent and individual advice to our customers on health concerns
  • Active participation in day-to-day business – from prescription testing to quality assurance
  • Implementation of health services such as vaccinations or checks to promote health literacy
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration with doctors, therapists and other specialists for networked care
  • Commitment to the training of apprentices as well as support for pharmacists in the assistant year

What you bring to the table

  • A degree as a federal. Dipl. Pharmacist or a recognised equivalent training (MEBEKO)
  • The desire to further develop yourself in offical pharmacy as part of your further training to become a specialist pharmacist FPH
  • A customer-oriented, responsible and independent way of working
  • Enjoyment of teamwork and an open, committed personality
  • Good IT skills (e.g. ProPharmaXX) as well as German language skills at least at level B2

About Us

The Medbase Group operates over 150 medical, pharmaceutical and dental locations in Switzerland. It has thus significantly expanded its position as a leading service provider for outpatient, integrated medicine in recent years. Around 3,200 employees care for patients holistically - from prevention to acute medicine to rehabilitation. Over 480 general practitioners and specialists, 340 therapists, 190 pharmacists, around 360 dentists and specialists in orthodontics, implants and dental hygiene work together in a coordinated and interprofessional manner in the group. For companies, Medbase Corporate Health offers specific consultations and holistic projects in corporate health management. In its 12 Sports Medical Centers, Medbase looks after and treats athletes in recreational and elite sports. Medbase also ensures new talent in the healthcare sector and trains around 340 young women and men.
