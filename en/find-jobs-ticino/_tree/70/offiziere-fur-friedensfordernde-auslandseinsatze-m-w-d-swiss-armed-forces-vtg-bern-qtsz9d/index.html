Put to the Point

Promoting peace – our mission For various missions in Europe, Asia and Africa, we are constantly looking for officers for different functions. Interested women and men can apply to the SWISSINT Competence Center for a mission abroad on behalf of the Swiss Army to promote peace.

  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Place of work: Kasernenstrasse 4, 6370 Stans-Oberdorf

Their tasks

  • Assignment as an officer in NATO- or EU-led contingents, for example as location commander, team leader or staff officer
  • Deployment in UN missions, for example as a military observer to monitor compliance with a ceasefire agreement or as a staff officer in headquarters
  • Capacity building as a training officer and head of international courses in various training centres

This makes them unique.

  • Completed apprenticeship or study, practical qualification and good repute
  • Age between 25 and 55 years
  • Military degree of the Swiss Army as first lieutenant, captain, major or lieutenant colonel
  • Very good knowledge of English, French is an advantage
  • Category B ticket
  • Mental and physical resilience
  • Ability to put personal interests in the background
  • High willingness to work impartially for the mission in the international environment for several months

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

The SWISSINT Competence Center is the national operational command for the military peace promotion of the Swiss Army and is responsible for the planning, provision and national leadership of all Swiss military contingents and individuals in the army order peace promotion.

Additional information

For further information please contact the Competence Center SWISSINT, Tel. 058 467 58 58. Application forms can be found at: www.peace-support.ch

The Federal Administration respects the different needs and life experiences of its employees and promotes their diversity. Equal treatment is a top priority.
