Full description

Reference code: JR125584

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

The expectations of our collaboration

In a dial factory whose technical expertise is valued by more than 50 trades and manufacturing stages, your mission will be to provide one or more trades.

Attached directly to the Sector Manager, you will be responsible for:

  • Conduct manufacturing operations according to quality standards
  • Adjust, adjust and control parts
  • Ensure conformity of products according to technical requirements
  • Participate in continuous process improvement
  • Strictly comply with security instructions and internal procedures

The must haves and skills required for this position

  • Experience confirmed in production and more precisely on dials/applicants.

-Strong expertise on at least one trade (e.g. decal, galvanoplasty, polishing, CNC, zappon, sunbathing, etc.)

  • Good dexterity and a sense of detail
  • Rigor, autonomy, team spirit
  • High sensitivity to quality

We offer

  • A stimulating work environment, dynamic teams, demanding in search of new challenges.
  • A modern, pleasant work space and beautiful social benefits
  • Flexible hours (starts at 3:00 pm)
  • Opportunities for change, skills development
