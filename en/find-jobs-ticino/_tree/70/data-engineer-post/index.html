Data Engineer — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

In the course of digitalization, data plays a central role at Post. Do you want to make an important contribution to the further development of our mobility offers with digital solutions? Then apply and become part of the Post’s Data & Insights team! With you, data solutions are created that create noticeable added value.

You can do that.

  • As part of a DevOps team, you will be responsible for the development and operation of our AWS-based data infrastructure and data pipelines.
  • For efficient data processing, you ensure the operation of Spark ETL and optimize it continuously.
  • You work closely with Data Owner and Data Analyst to develop and maintain robust and reliable data pipelines.
  • You analyze requirements, use them to design data products and implement them in our Big Data Stack (AWS).
  • You will support and coach our data engineers in the automation of processes and thus contribute to smooth operation and the continuous improvement of our development stack.
  • In close cooperation with business stakeholders and the architecture team, you tailor technical solutions specifically to business needs.
  • Together with the team, you optimize the costs of existing AWS services and accompany migrations to new tools and technologies.

You bring that with you

  • You have experience in data engineering and a university degree in computer science or a comparable qualification.
  • Your strong expertise in data processing with Apache Spark, SQL and Python sets you apart.
  • With your experience in software development with Scala and/or Java as well as in dealing with AWS Analytics services such as Glue, SageMaker, Athena, Lambda or Batch, you can score additional points with us.
  • First knowledge of CI/CD, Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform) and DevOps Best Practices you have already built up and want to deepen it further.
  • Agile work is yours, you bring a growth mindset and you are happy to share your knowledge and experiences with your team.
  • Stylistic German and good English skills for working with your teammates in Lisbon round off your profile.

Due to the team composition, we are particularly pleased about applications from women.
