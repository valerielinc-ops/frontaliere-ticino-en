# Learning: AFC

Your future begins here

You are a multimedia professional and inspire your customers as a hub of design, communication, information technology and business administration. You're ready,

  • to inspire with your charm for new technologies, media and forms of communication.
  • to use your breath for multimedia trends in the ICT world
  • to design and produce a wide range of content - you will create raw digital data (photos, videos, animations, texts) and process them further
  • to use the right tool for work - such as HTML, CSS, Photoshop, InDesign or Flash
  • to approach internal or external customers effortlessly and work together to develop sustainable and convincing solutions

What you bring with you

  • Aver completed compulsory school with good and very good results

  • You will convince with your peculiar oral and written expression and your communication skills.
  • Thanks to your networked thinking and your focused way of working, complex situations and problems do not put you out of the game.
  • Your curiosity pushes you to constantly want to discover and learn new things
