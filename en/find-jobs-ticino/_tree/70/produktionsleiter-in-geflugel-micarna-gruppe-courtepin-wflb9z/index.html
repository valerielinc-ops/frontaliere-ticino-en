Full description

As a leading producer of meat, poultry, eggs and fish, Micarna as a member of the M-Industry pursues the goal of always being “simply better”. Do you share this vision? Then become part of our team and contribute to the success of the Migros company.

Mansioni

  • Ensure the achievement of the operational, financial, qualitative and human objectives of the area, in particular in terms of costs, revenues and production losses
  • Lead, accompany and promote responsible persons, specialists and responsible persons in their own area of responsibility
  • Ensure optimal personnel planning and the availability of plants, machines and the raw materials required for production
  • Ensure compliance with occupational safety, hygiene, quality, compliance and animal welfare regulations
  • Analyze complaints and coordinate corrective actions and continuous improvements with those affected
  • Manage cross-sectoral projects, participate in the further development of production capacities and ensure data quality in the various systems
  • Coordinate external slaughter and ensure professional cooperation with internal and external partners

Requirements

  • Professional experience: proven professional experience in a production environment, team leadership and continuous improvement process (CIP)
  • Higher technical school: higher education at the level of a higher technical school, a higher technical examination or a technical college, ideally supplemented by a management education
  • German (good knowledge) French (very good knowledge): Ideally bilingual French/German, with knowledge of German from at least level B1 to B2.
  • Strong leadership skills and experience in leading teams with different profiles
  • Good knowledge of common computer tools, especially Microsoft Office and SAP
  • High sense of responsibility, independence and self-initiative
  • Analytical thinking skills, solution-oriented working methods and safety in dealing with complex situations
  • Flexibility, openness to change and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Strong communication and cooperation skills as well as competences in conflict management
  • High awareness of quality, safety, performance and customer satisfaction

Cosa of friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave / 8 weeks paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Occupational pension: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages
  • Meals: Balanced lunch menu/buffet in the staff restaurant for max. CHF 7.-
  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees
  • Club School Migros: refund of 70% of the course costs up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and cultural offers: You can benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events
  • Sports and fitness offers: organisation of various sporting events with discounts
