The Position The Patient Insights Software Chapter, within the Architecture and Software Engineering global function, aims to leverage software engineering as a competitive advantage for Roche, empowering patients and healthcare professionals to deliver better care. We are looking for a Delivery Manager to play the Release Train Engineer (RTE) role for Accu-Chek Care to lead and improve software delivery management across its teams. Accu-Chek Care is the integrated digital ecosystem that empowers Healthcare Professionals (HCPs) — moving from standalone tools to a unified platform that enhances clinical decision-making and operational efficiency. As RTE you lead and take ownership of Product Delivery within Engineering. This is an operationally hands-on role focused on engineering best practices, tooling standards, regulatory compliance, project management and scaled agile leadership. You are the orchestrator with a single goal: ensure effective and efficient product delivery — on time, within budget, and with the expected level of product quality — while actively leveraging AI-enabled tools and analytics to enhance predictability, performance and operating model effectiveness at scale. If you are passionate about agile software delivery, a strong people leader, an empathic communicator, a team player, and excited to help build an AI-augmented delivery ecosystem, this role is for you.
