Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary The Sr. Project Engineer is responsible for managing technical projects or processes independently with limited supervision. The role includes coaching junior professionals and solving complex problems through investigation and analysis. This job profile is intended for specialist professionals who apply advanced knowledge of a job area. Required to manage projects / processes independently with limited supervision. Typically coaches and reviews the work of lower level professionals.

Your main mission

  • Embody the link and manage internal and external contacts with a view to delivering the contract / project on spare parts / retrofit / new projects activities.
  • Complete the project by satisfying the customer (cost, quality and deadline) and optimizing the financial result for Sulzer (revenue, margin and cash).

The experienced project manager works on high-stakes and complex projects in the company

Meaningful activities

  • Organize and supervise contract review meetings (handover), initiation and internal monitoring (KOM), integrating and managing all the transversal skills necessary to complete the project.
  • Carry out, from the initial phases and during implementation, risk analyzes of projects and their necessary updates; define and manage the corresponding corrective and preventive action plans
  • Ensure regular communication with clients (inception meeting, follow-up meeting, reporting, etc.), maintaining the right level of cooperation through appropriate interpersonal skills.
  • Create and maintain project schedules (MS Project), the main project management tool.
  • Manage and coordinate internal activities & teams, in accordance with project planning, in particular ensuring collaboration and interfaces between the BE, Production Management, Purchasing, Production, Quality, the sales force and Tendering.
  • Create and maintain key indicators (reliability of commitments, documentation, etc.) at the level of each project in charge.
  • Obtain documentary validations (technical, quality, supply) in conjunction with customers and suppliers.
  • Establish billing, margin and cash flow forecasts for each managed project.
  • Promote the negotiation of change orders with the client to improve the contractual conditions of the initial contract, the income and the margin of the project.
  • Document the project throughout its duration to prevent any dispute.
  • Deploy and formalize feedback on the project activity.

Business – technical skills

  • Mechanical skills (mechanical technical culture), acquired through engineering training and professional experience in the mechanical environment
  • Proven skills in project management (minimum of 7 years in the management of major projects in demanding technical environments, such as nuclear, Oil & Gas, precision mechanics internationally)
  • Financial skills / project management control: knowledge of major financial indicators
  • Management of key indicators / KPIs: OTD (On-time delivery) and Gross Profit (margin).
  • Customer & supplier relationship skills (the ability to manage supplier relationships is fundamental to the position)
  • Knowledge of project planning (“MS project” type)
  • Skills in ERP (project monitoring under ERP, SAP type).
  • Fluent English (read, written, spoken) Level C1

What we offer you

  • Advantage 1: Evolve on strategic projects for the Group
  • Advantage 2: Buchelay: nuclear reference within the Gr
