The Position The Division In the area of Global Operations - ADI (active diagnostic ingredient), our group is responsible for the provision of sera of human and animal origin. This very special working environment offers an enormously diverse range of work. Our team is responsible for the routine provision and analysis of human serums and plasmas, as well as animal serums from animal husbandry to finished pooled material. In addition to our routine, we are constantly working on process improvements. In addition, several automation and IT/data projects are underway, for which we need your support. We work with large amounts of data and are on the way to making them usable in the best possible way. For this we are looking for you, with experience and a pronounced affinity for data and automation solutions, and the willingness to tackle hands-on from time to time. We offer a diverse and exciting working environment where you can see the success of your work every week.

Responsibility

  • Product support & coordination : You take care of our products in the animal serum environment. Specifically, you control the production of some products (virtual collection of individual donations based on extensive analytical results) and coordinate with the team and the interfaces for further work steps.
  • Data Use & IT Solutions: You are actively involved in the further development of data use to increase efficiency. You collect large amounts of data and develop suitable solutions.
  • Project representation: You represent us in IT and partly also in automation projects as operator representatives. You communicate upcoming changes to the team and in turn take feedback from the group into the projects.
  • Digital Tools & Organization: You support the group in the further development of digital documentation in RAW and our work organization in monday.com.
  • Process optimization & Lean: You help us with your analytical skills to improve our processes, advance lean topics and keep track of the implementation of measures within the team and across interfaces. In addition, you maintain our document and process landscape.
  • Team Support: You support your colleagues in everyday life and coach them in dealing with new tools and processes. When it comes down to it, you are ready to work in the laboratory yourself – this gives you an even deeper insight into our processes.

Your profile

  • You have successfully completed your studies as a Bachelor/Master in Life Sciences and/or IT
  • You bring expertise in dealing with large amounts of data in the industrial environment.
  • You are very confident in dealing with standard EDP applications and can have initial experience in establishing new applications and have knowledge of programming languages such as Python and SQL.
  • Experience in process automation on laboratory or pilot scale.
  • You fit into a highly motivated team and understand how to communicate and communicate.
  • Personally, you convince us with strong organizational skills, a quick comprehension and the ability to work conscientiously, but also to find creative solutions “out of the box”.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English round off your profile.

This post is limited to 2 years.
