Full description

Head AVOR (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company.

To complement our Bus & Trolley division, we are looking for a dynamic leader in materials management as head of work preparation.

As head of AVOR, you and your team will take on important planning, control and control tasks for the production of our innovative and high-quality electric and trolley buses. Take on the challenge as an important link between our development and production and become part of the HESS family! Their tasks

  • Organizational, professional and personnel management of our team in work preparation

incl. Personnel Planning (6 MA)

  • Overall responsibility for operational activities in AVOR
  • Coordination of all AVOR activities for the BUS assembly sites
  • Responsibility for the scheduled and volume-based payment of all production orders
  • Monitoring target vs. IST order times, as well as classification of corrective measures
  • Organisation, implementation and monitoring of material disposition
  • Close cooperation with sales, project management, development and purchasing
  • Creating make-or-buy analyses in close cooperation with strategic purchasing
  • Efficient processing of production orders (checking, de-registering, closing production orders)
  • Additional tasks such as master data maintenance, article mutations, pre-calculations and development of key figures
  • Supportive cooperation in cross-departmental projects

Our requirements

  • Completed basic technical education
  • Further training as a technician for HF mechanical engineering or electrical engineering
  • Proven AVOR experience in a production facility
  • Leadership experience in a comparable function
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Sound IT user and ERP system knowledge
  • Resilience and teamwork

We offer them

  • Interesting, varied and challenging activity in e-mobility
  • Permanent employment in a successful traditional company
  • Assuming leadership responsibility
  • Opportunity for further training and development
  • Moving working time
  • Conditions of performance

If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
