Full description

As a university intern: in Product Owner, you actively shape the future of “feasible” and take responsibility for products and projects from the idea to the implementation. You work closely with colleagues from different areas, develop the portfolio further and bring new impetus to the strategy. In addition, you represent the "feasible" inside and out, for example at events or in exchange with interested parties: inside the IT entry program. This way you strengthen our network and make impact visible.

You can do that.

  • You run the backlog and plan the implementation of projects together with stakeholders
  • You are responsible for the product lifecycle of the feasible innovation portfolio across all phases
  • You design the product portfolio and the strategy of the feasible together with the management
  • You take over the marketing for the IT entry program (website, career fairs, video concept, etc.) and implement internal communication measures
  • Organize and moderate hackweeks and other potential events
  • You will lead and moderate workshops on process improvements or topics such as OKR, Design Thinking and Feedback

You bring that with you

  • Bachelor degree in Business Administration, Business Informatics or a comparable discipline or study from the 4th Semester
  • Interest in product development and innovation processes
  • Feel for communication and marketing
  • Strong performance and network competence
  • Willingness to try out new approaches and to proactively contribute and share ideas and insights
  • Very good knowledge of German and advantage through further language skills
