1st employer in the canton, Sion Hospital employs 6’200 staff. Every year, 41’500 patients are cared for and approximately 652’700 outpatient visits are provided. Sion Hospital is seeking for the Valais Romand Hospital Center Nurses at 50-100% in CDD and CDI for the Surgery Services of Sion Hospital. ## Mission

  • Ensure patient care in the service according to the HVS strategy and the Professional Practice Model

  • Tertiary nurse diploma (general care recognized by the Red Cross, HES, DNII)

  • Ability to promote and develop quality care

  • Mastery of computer tools and general office work
