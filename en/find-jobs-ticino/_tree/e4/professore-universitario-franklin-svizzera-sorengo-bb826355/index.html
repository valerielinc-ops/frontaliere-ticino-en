Full description

The employer is looking for one

Professor/Professor of History Assistant (marked version)

Mansions

  • teach university courses of History at different levels, including first year seminars, courses with an important component of academic writing and conclusive seminars of the Bachelor's course.
  • normally carry out six courses per year (18 US credits, equivalent to approximately 36–38 ECTS).
  • design and conduct courses with integrated academic travel component (Academic Travel).
  • develop interdisciplinary, experiential and project-based teaching activities.
  • carry out historical research activities and produce scientific publications, including academic articles, monographs, chapters of books and other contributions recognised in the discipline.
  • collaborate with colleagues from other academic areas and external partners.
  • provide academic advice and mentoring to students.
  • participate in academic committees and institutional activities, contributing to the development of university programmes.

Requirements

  • PhD (PhD) in History, obtained by the date of recruitment.
  • solid disciplinary preparation and strong interest in university teaching, preferably in small classes.
  • experience or teaching skills in different geographical areas and historical periods.
  • documented scientific activity, adapted to the stage of academic career, and clear research program.
  • interest in innovative and interdisciplinary teaching methods, including experiential teaching.
  • collaboration with other teachers and willingness to participate in the life of the institution.
  • excellent mastery of the English language, language of teaching.
  • experience in the American liberal arts university system particularly appreciated.
  • knowledge of a Swiss national language is a preferential title.
  • licence to conduct cat. B

Details of use

  • Sorengo workplace
  • 100% employment
  • use from 01.08.2027, indefinitely
  • wages to be agreed

Application

  • letter of motivation.
  • curriculum vitae.
  • Declaration on its teaching activities and the research programme.
  • evaluations of the teaching activity.
  • three professional references.

Documentation in PDF, DOC or DOCX format. Applications will be examined progressively until the position is covered.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
