Full description

Bethesda Spital AG | online since : 30.09.2026

As a successful Basel clinic with a private character, the Bethesda Spital combines top medicine with first-class service and a unique feel-good atmosphere. With the five focus topics of women’s medicine, pregnancy & childbirth, musculoskeletal system and rehabilitation as well as palliative care, our 6,000 inpatients annually benefit from state-of-the-art treatment centers under one roof – in the middle of one of the most beautiful hospital parks in northwestern Switzerland.

For our interdisciplinary nursing department with the main disciplines musculoskeletal rehabilitation, rheumatology, spinal surgery and internal medicine, we are looking for a health specialist (80%) from January 2027 or by appointment.

Their tasks

  • Daily coordination of the department
  • Individual nursing support for patients under the responsibility of the qualified nursing staff
  • Independent assumption of tasks in administration and logistics
  • Domestic activities

They bring with them

  • Completed health EFZ training
  • A noticeable identification with the nursing profession and after external pleasure in dealing with people
  • A patient-oriented and professional understanding of care
  • Flair for administrative and organizational tasks of the department
  • A strong willingness to learn and perform
  • The ability to meet the demands even in demanding, labor-intensive situations and to react flexibly
  • The ability and willingness to cooperate in an interdisciplinary team

We offer

  • Innovative field of work centred on the continuous development of nursing care for our patients
  • Organizational structures that promote interprofessional cooperation
  • Team atmosphere of togetherness and for each other
  • Attractive internal training programme
  • Contemporary employment conditions Here you come to the employment conditions at Bethesda
