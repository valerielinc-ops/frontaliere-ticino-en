Full description

University Hospital for Age Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Are you looking for a versatile and responsible task in outpatient retirement psychiatry? At the University Psychiatric Center in Bern, you work in a dynamic environment and accompany patients with gerontopsychiatric diseases. You are guided by a person-centred and recovery-oriented mission statement and take on a versatile and responsible area of responsibility in the outpatient setting.

Your tasks

  • You work in an exploring, mobile setting in interprofessional teams
  • You support the care teams in complex gerontopsychiatric and psychosocial situations in retirement and nursing homes
  • You design a person-centered, recovery-oriented care and support of patients
  • You keep the digital maintenance documentation and create reports
  • You recognize changes in the patient’s condition at an early stage and take over the case management if necessary
  • You accompany learners and students in their education
  • You are responsible for the TARDOC performance billing
  • You have the opportunity to participate in various topic responsibilities

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH
  • You bring several years of professional experience in psychiatry as well as experience in accompanying people with dementia
  • You have strong professional and social skills in dealing with complex psychiatric situations
  • You work independently, structured and responsible and organize your daily work reliably even in mobile settings
  • You appreciate interprofessional cooperation and communicate bindingly and solution-oriented
  • You communicate clearly, empathetically and professionally even in demanding situations
  • You are flexible, resilient and have a high level of social skills
  • You show your own initiative and gladly take responsibility
  • You enjoy continuously developing professionally and personally
  • You are an organizational talent and appreciate both independent work and active exchange in the team
  • You are digitally affine and open to modern work processes and new technologies in the healthcare sector

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
