Who you are

If it has to go fast, you can keep up and provide peace and order. The logistics team at IKEA wants to have just such a person on board.

People work better when they are well. At IKEA, we believe that challenges bring joy when you have fun at work and achieve goals together. We want you to feel comfortable with us and to be able to develop personally and professionally.

How do you see it? See if this suits you:

  • You already have experience in logistics or a similar field.
  • You are open-minded, communicative and have a friendly way of working positively with your colleagues.
  • You speak good German, further language skills are always a plus.
  • Hectic does not scare you and in a dynamic environment you feel comfortable.

You are flexible and can work from Monday to Saturday in the early shift from 05:00 to 10:00.

  • You are physically fit and ready to actively engage.

You are responsible for

  • Thanks to you, our many products are always available and always in perfect condition.
  • You ensure the timely provision of goods and a correct inventory management.
  • As a team, you work according to established routines to jointly guarantee product availability.
  • You work carefully and accurately, because safety comes first.
  • Support the department and other areas of business when assistance is needed.

Do you think that would fit?

We wrote above that we want our employees to be well. This includes saying “thank you”!
