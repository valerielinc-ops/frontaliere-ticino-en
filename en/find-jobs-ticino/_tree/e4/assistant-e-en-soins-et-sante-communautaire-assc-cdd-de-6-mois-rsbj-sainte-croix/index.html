We are looking for, for our EMS the tree of life, geriatric service: A Community Health and Care Assistant (CHS) 80% - CSD 6 months

Your missions

  • Ensure the care and support of residents during the day and at night (2 to 4 nights per month)
  • Organize and provide care in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team
  • Perform medical and technical care, assist AVQs and support on a daily basis to respond to the resident's care and life project
  • Collaborate with multidisciplinary team members

Your profile

  • Essential CFCs
  • EMS experience required
  • Very good management of priorities and organization
  • Team spirit, sense of communication, ability to work in interdisciplinarity
  • Adaptability
  • Access with IT tools
  • Perfect command of the French language
  • EEF training an asset

We offer

  • Working in a dynamic interdisciplinary team
  • An attractive atmosphere and working conditions within a human scale structure
  • Free parking and easy access
  • The possibility to receive discounts from more than 150 brands
  • A wage and social benefits according to the collective labour agreement in the public health sector (FHV).

Entry into office: Immediately or to be agreed

Full application file to be sent to: jobs@rsbj.ch / www.rsbj.ch
