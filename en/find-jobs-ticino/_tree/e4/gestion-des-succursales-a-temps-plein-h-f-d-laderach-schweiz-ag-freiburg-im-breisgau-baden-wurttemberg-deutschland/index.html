Full description

Branch management Münster full-time (m/f/d) Freshness, craftsmanship, Switzerland – this is what the chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, our family business today employs more than 3000 people from around 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of picturesque Alpine scenery, we produce our fresh chocolates here. The “Retail” division comprises more than 200 chocolateries in 21 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • Overall responsibility for the results of the sales outlet in compliance with the given budget
  • Leadership, management and motivation of the branch and branch employees
  • Ensuring quality standards
  • Ensuring and complying with internal branch requirements
  • Communication with corporate headquarters
  • Active cooperation in the branch and in the sales process

You bring that with you

Leadership competence

Team leadership and motivation: You should be able to lead, motivate and coach a team as needed to achieve goals together.

Communication capability

Open communication: You should be able to communicate clearly and openly, both with your team and with customers and the company headquarters.

Organisational talent

Time management: You need to be able to perform different tasks at the same time, such as personnel planning, merchandise management, and sales strategies, while keeping track.

Sales talent

Sales strategies: You should have a good sense of sales promotion and know how to present products attractively to increase sales. Retail experience Industry Experience: Retail experience, ideally in a senior position, helps you better understand and apply the different aspects of business operations.
