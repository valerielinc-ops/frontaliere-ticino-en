Team leader: in Technology Workshop Bicycle/E-Mobility

Coop Cooperative — Cheeks at Olten, SO, Svizzera

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Production / Technology
  • Location: Coop, Industriestrasse 17, 4612 Wangen bei Olten
  • Pensum: 100%

Tasks

  • Professional and personnel management of the workshop team (about 15 employees): including personnel planning, employee support and support of the learners
  • Ensuring a smooth, timely repair process in compliance with economic and qualitative requirements
  • Active participation in everyday workshop work as well as active support and advice for the team in complex repairs and issues
  • Continuous optimization of work and workshop processes according to lean standards (5S, CIP) to increase efficiency and quality
  • Close cooperation with internal interfaces (workshop, procurement, logistics, customer service) as well as consistent enforcement of quality, safety and environmental standards

Requirements

  • Completed basic training in the mechanical sector (ideally bicycle or motorcycle mechanic: in EFZ) or comparable qualification
  • Several years of practical workshop experience in the bicycle and e-bike sector with current technical expertise
  • Several years of proven professional and managerial experience in a craft/technical workshop environment
  • Vocational trainer ID is beneficial
  • Experience in the application of lean methods (5S, CIP) as well as experienced user knowledge in MS Office and common SAP/workshop systems
  • Solution-oriented, assertive and communicative leader with a high level of initiative and very good knowledge of German (word/script)
  • Discounts: Discount on the travel offer ITS Coop Travel, 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket, up to 20% discount on various insurance services, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop Group, cheaper mobile and prepaid subscriptions at Coop Mobile, Reka Card: discount on subscription, staff discount also for partners:in (living in the same household), discount on GA subscription, discounts for leisure activities
  • Conditions of employment: Pension fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, Attractive employment conditions and certified equal pay, collective employment contract (GAV), savings plan at the pension fund
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Training: support for external education and training
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription, free participation in various company runs
