Full description

This place of work includes a restaurant with an outdoor area. The meeting place in Bern creates an inviting meeting place for students, employees and external guests. Our team inspires an average of 76 guests here every day from Monday to Friday with a wide range of products - from fresh and aromatic cuisine to healthy, quick dishes and specialties with Far Eastern influences from Thailand, Vietnam and China. Are you our new team member who will support us as a service employee (temporary staff) with immediate effect or by appointment? You will work as a temporary worker with a workload of approx. 20% on an hourly wage basis between Monday and Saturday. Depending on the scheduling, the working hours are between 10:00 a.m. and 10:00 p.m. You can be flexibly deployed for assignments with room hours and in the evening. Your task menu:

  • Welcome, service and advice of guests
  • Perception of the host role
  • Ensuring a high level of guest satisfaction
  • Operation of the cash register
  • Creation of the mise en place
  • Ensuring an attractive presentation of goods
  • Collaboration in caterings and special events
  • Implementation of smaller aperitifs
  • Preparation of coffee specialties
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the working environment
  • Completion of general clean-up and cleaning work
  • Implementation and assurance of quality standards and ZFV guidelines (hygiene and occupational safety, service, etc.) Your recipe for success:
  • Basic training as a restaurant specialist: EFZ or basic gastronomic training EFZ with additional training
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Customer orientation
  • Ability to work in a team
  • Enthusiastic sales personality
  • Winning and charming charisma
  • This is what we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that is good for you. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 52,000 and CHF 54,400 (incl. 13. Monthly salary).
