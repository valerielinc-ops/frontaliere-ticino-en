Full description

Mission

As part of this function, which is attached to our Development & Industrialization Service, you will be required to lead product projects and/or production and transactional processes, support production and actively participate in continuous improvement actions based on the standards you can create and evolve, independently.

Responsibilities

  • Define, update and maintain the repository as well as production records, system data and tooling database.
  • Actively support production to ensure the perfect control and performance of existing processes by searching for and analyzing deviations to standards.
  • Process curative and corrective actions, conduct optimization tests, identify critical line operations and bring them to the Patek Philippe production system standard.
  • Pilot the acquisition and integration of means of production by ensuring compliance with the planning, budget, quality requirements and compliance of deliverables while monitoring the indicators of actions.
  • Pilot reindustrialization approaches (identification of critical operations, launch of 0 series, updating of production records), update and maintain the repository and production records, system data and tooling database.
  • Lead project monitoring committees and lead technical coordination sessions.
  • Coordinate and unite working groups (support functions, internal clients, suppliers) by assessing project and process risks (AMDEC) and proposing action plans.

Profile sought

  • ES technician or Bachelor in mechanical or micromechanical.
  • 5 years of experience in a similar function in the field of micromechanical, watchmaking or mechanical construction.
  • Control of project management in autonomy and associated risk management.
  • Knowledge of mechanical watchmaking technique.
  • Knowledge of the principles of a GPAO (Article, range, nomenclature, MRP, etc.).
  • Knowledge of CAD (ideally Solidworks).
  • Mastery of Quality tools (AMDEC, 8D, Capability, etc.).
  • Mastery of office tools (MS Office).
  • Very good knowledge of Lean tools - Six sigma (5S, SMED, process capability, etc.).
  • Good knowledge of English is an asset.
  • Ability to question and listen.
  • Orientation towards action, ability to convince.
  • Ability to master interpersonal relationships and manage complexity.
  • Strategic spirit and ability to manage uncertainty and ambiguity.
  • Oriented "customers" and "solutions".
