Full description

We are on an exciting path: In the coming school year, three new 1st secondary classes will be opened. Our school continues to grow and offers dedicated teachers the opportunity to contribute their ideas and actively shape the development of our school.

Our goal is a lively day school where pioneering spirit, optimism and fresh ideas open up new paths. Community and relationship strength shape our cooperation, while close cooperation forms the basis for holistic learning and the individual development of our students.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. August 2027 several motivated and experienced teachers who want to take joint responsibility for the new 1st secondary classes and actively shape the further development of our young school.

We are looking for didactically/methodically experienced and enriching teachers with a strong flair for collaboration.

  • You take on a shared class responsibility in a first secondary class.
  • You exchange ideas about the lessons in the year team and share responsibility for the lesson content.
  • You identify with the idea of day school and actively contribute to its success.
  • You motivate, accompany and support the students on their individual learning path and regularly lead coaching discussions.
  • You are present at the school for at least four days and are involved in school and teaching development as well as at school events.
  • A cooperative cooperation in the team, with external specialists, parents and authorities is a matter of course for you.
  • Teaching diploma (EDK recognized) for elementary school
  • motivated, flexible, creative, innovative and resilient personality
  • Broad fan profile
  • positive attitude in working with young people and parents
  • Good resource management and a large portion of humor
  • Of course in dealing with integration and heterogeneity

A challenging and versatile task awaits you in an open-minded and helpful team, which together breaks new ground and continuously develops the school. We attach great importance to a solution- and goal-oriented cooperation as well as to the development and development opportunities of all employees. You will be supported by a committed and experienced school management, a well-organized school secretariat and a constructive cooperation with the school authority.

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 18 school houses. These are connected to 43 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 14 organizational units, 11 are day schools.

At Zurichberg something new and big has been created: In August 2026, our newly founded secondary school opened its doors. Around 200 pupils, a motivated and manageable team and a modern schoolhouse in the countryside offer an inspiring framework for jointly designing and developing schools.
