Full description

The figure will take care of the different entities of the Group of the Ticino Region. Main responsibility

The role includes

  • the responsibility and supervision of the financial activities of the various entities of the Group in the Ticino Region;
  • financial planning and management of budgeting and forecasting processes;
  • the monitoring of results and analysis of deviations;
  • preparation of financial reports and KPI to support management;
  • cost monitoring and identification of optimization opportunities;
  • the coordination of the various internal interlocutors;
  • the contribution to the development and continuous improvement of financial processes.

The research profile

The ideal figure has

  • a higher education in finance, business economy, controlling or equivalent scope and a consolidated experience in Finance, Controlling or Business Partnering;
  • Diploma of Specialist in Finance and Accounting with Federal Professional Certificate, or equivalent Title;
  • solid analytical capabilities and good mastery of reporting tools;
  • Solid experience and expertise in accounting, with the ability to preside over and support the main accounting and financial closure processes;
  • the ability to interpret financial data and translate it into concrete information to support business and decision-making;
  • autonomy, precision and a strong sense of responsibility;
  • excellent relational skills and preparation for collaboration with different interlocutors;
  • Excellent mastery of Italian language and good knowledge of German language. French knowledge is a further advantage.

The position, together with the CFO, directly returns to the Ticino Region Directorate, plays a strategic role in the financial management of the different Group entities in the region, ensuring a qualified support for management and contributing to their development and achievement of business objectives.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
