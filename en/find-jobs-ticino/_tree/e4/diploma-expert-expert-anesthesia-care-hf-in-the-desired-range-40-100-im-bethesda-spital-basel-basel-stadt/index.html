Full description

Bethesda Spital AG | online since : 29.08.2025

As a successful Basel clinic with a private character, the Bethesda Spital combines top medicine with first-class service and a unique feel-good atmosphere. With the five focus areas of women’s medicine, pregnancy & childbirth, musculoskeletal system and rehabilitation as well as palliative care, our 6,000 inpatients annually benefit from state-of-the-art treatment centers under one roof – in the middle of one of the most beautiful hospital parks in northwestern Switzerland.

For our anesthesia care team, we are looking for a Dipl immediately or by appointment. Expert / Expert anesthesia care HF in the desired range 40-100 %

Their tasks

  • Independent performance of general anesthesia under specialist supervision
  • Comprehensive care for patients with regional anesthesia
  • Safe preparation and follow-up of all common forms of anesthesia
  • Collaboration in the in-house pain service and resuscitation team
  • Assumption of night and emergency service of the house
  • Co-responsibility for an efficient design of processes and a conscious use of resources
  • Care of our students

They bring with them

  • Completed postgraduate studies for / to the Dipl. Expert anesthesia care NDS HF and ideally professional experience in anesthesia care
  • Readiness for weekend and night services
  • Own initiative and self-employment
  • Readiness for further training
  • Process-oriented and economic thinking and action
  • Resilience and flexibility
  • Ideally, experience in the field of education or the willingness to develop further in this field (e.g. by obtaining the certificate SVEB 1) but not a must (!)
