Coop Cooperative — Cheeks at Olten, SO, Svizzera

Tasks

  • You take goods and unload them professionally
  • You check the delivery notes and compare them with the received goods

Requirements

  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Ideally, you will bring knowledge of SAP and/or WAMAS
  • You work independently and reliably and are physically fit and resilient
  • Discounts: discount on the travel offer ITS Coop Travel, 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop Group, cheaper mobile and prepaid subscriptions on Coop Mobile, Reka card: discount on subscription, discount on GA subscription, discounts for leisure activities
  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, Attractive Conditions of Employment and Certified Equal Pay, Total Employment Contract (GAV), 18 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses, support for external education and training
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with more than 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
