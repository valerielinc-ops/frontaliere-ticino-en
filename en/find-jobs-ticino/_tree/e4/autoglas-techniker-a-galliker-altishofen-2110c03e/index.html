Car Glass Technician (a) As a leading transport and logistics company in vehicle logistics, we offer our customers a wide range of automotive services in addition to the transport and storage of new and used vehicles, such as vehicle preparation. The wide variety of brands offers a varied and exciting everyday life. To strengthen our team, we are looking for you as an automotive glass technician.

Responsibilities

  • Replacement, repair and polishing of car glass panes
  • Calibration of driver assistance systems
  • Checking the delivered panes and consumables
  • Providing the necessary tools and materials

  • Sense of quality, order, cleanliness and precise work
  • Independent, dutiful and team player
  • Good knowledge of written and spoken German
  • Driving licence Cat. B

Offer

  • Personal development opportunities in a versatile company
  • Overtime can be compensated
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
