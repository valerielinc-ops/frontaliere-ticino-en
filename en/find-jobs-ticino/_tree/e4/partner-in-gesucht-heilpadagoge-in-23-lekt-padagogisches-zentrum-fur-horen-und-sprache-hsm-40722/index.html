Full description

Portrait The Educational Center for Hearing and Speech HSM Münchenbuchsee is a service center with a wide range of support and advice for children and adolescents with severe hearing and speech impairments and their environment.

We accompany normally gifted pupils with hearing and/or language impairments from basic to secondary level I. In multi-professional teams, we design individual learning and development paths based on joint educational planning. We focus on success, identify strengths and resources and work together to find suitable solutions.

For our language healing basic level in cycle 1 we are looking for 1. February 2027 a dedicated curative educator or a dedicated curative educator.

Tasks

  • Together with your co-class teacher, you will teach a class of 12 students with severe language impairments in Cycle 1.
  • You take responsibility for the class and design the educational work in the multi-professional team.
  • You participate in the creation and implementation of individual educational planning.
  • You work closely with speech therapists, psychomotor therapists and other professionals.
  • You create many lessons in team teaching.

Requirements

  • Ideally, you have completed your studies as a curative teacher or are ready to develop further in this area.
  • You have experience or great interest in working with language-impaired students.
  • You bring a great deal of serenity, humor and endurance.
  • You have the eye for success and focus your attention on the strengths and development opportunities of the children.
  • You enjoy working in a team, appreciate the professional exchange and actively participate in the cooperation.
  • You are resilient, flexible and maintain your confidence and humor even in challenging situations.
  • You like to take responsibility and bring your own ideas and pedagogical considerations.
  • You are open to different perspectives and appreciate the competences of other professional groups.
  • You enjoy designing individual learning and development paths and jointly developing educational planning.
  • You meet children, parents and colleagues with appreciation, patience and a positive attitude.

We offer

With us, you can work demandingly and meaningfully, be part of a team and be supported in your work.

  • A dedicated team and a supportive school management to accompany you.
  • A multi-professional environment with diverse professional competencies and opportunities for exchange.
  • Small classes and scope for your educational ideas.
  • Individual and collective training opportunities within and outside the HSM.
  • A beautiful school area in the historic part of Munich bushings with swimming pool, animals and own garden.
