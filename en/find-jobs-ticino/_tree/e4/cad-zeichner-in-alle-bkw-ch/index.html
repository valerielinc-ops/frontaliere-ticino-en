Full description

You think architecture not only in plans, but also in 3D? With us, you will help shape challenging construction projects, contribute your know-how in design and BIM modeling and actively develop digital planning processes. Your ideas and your own initiative count.

Your field of activity

  • Independently, you create challenging architectural and detailed plans in 2D and 3D and develop constructive solutions within the framework of the overall concept
  • With modern CAD and BIM tools such as Revit, AutoCAD and Navisworks, you can create and edit digital building models and ensure high planning quality.
  • For BIM collision checks, you keep an overview, coordinate 3D models of different disciplines and check and optimize external supplier data
  • Your ideas are in demand: You develop our 3D model libraries and digital BIM processes and bring new approaches to BIM 360 and cloud technologies
  • Quality comes first for you. You ensure compliance with relevant SIA standards and contribute to documentation, quality assurance and archiving
  • Step by step you take on more responsibility for your own sub-areas, share your expertise and support colleagues with technical issues

You bring that with you

  • A completed basic education as a draftsman: in EFZ with a focus on architecture forms your basis
  • In building construction and architectural planning, you are at home and bring in-depth knowledge in the elaboration of structural details
  • With Revit, 3D modeling and BIM methods you know your way around
  • You are familiar with the relevant SIA standards and attach great importance to precise and high-quality planning results
  • Independent, structured work is just as important to you as the exchange with various disciplines. You like to bring your own ideas and share your knowledge with others
