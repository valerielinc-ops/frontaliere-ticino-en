Full description

Mission As a Network and Voice Engineer, you will play a crucial role in managing and improving our Global Network Infrastructure. Your expertise will be essential in ensuring high availability, performance, and security of our IT services. Part a of Team, you will be charge of the Network activities at global level for the group, supporting LAN / MAN / WAN, WiFi, Security Firewall, Load Balancing, DDoS ect.. Challenges Evolve in a challenging, regulated environment for a fast and growing Private Bank. Support a large amount of technology in an international distributed environment. Main responsibilities

  • Manage incident queue and ensure these are resolved in due time (monitoring, investigation, resolution, follow-up)
  • Manage the life cycle of network environment (LAN/MAN/WAN/Wifi...)
  • Manage the life cycle of voice environment (CUCM, NICE Voice Recording solutions...)
  • Manage the life cycle of security environment (PaloAlto/Bluecoat/F5/IronPort/SandBlast...)
  • Work with the team on all infrastructure project where network/security is needed
  • Work with external vendor within incident/project/BAU (Service provider, Carriers, Telco…)
  • Ensure inventory updates, capacity planning, KPI tracking
  • Be part of a network team and security operation teams a great set of environments to manage
  • Willing to perform task out of office hours on the production environment
  • Be part of an on-call service rotation one week per month (24/24-7j/7)

Specific competencies

Must have strong expertise in

  1. Cisco (Catalyst, ASR, Nexus…)
  2. Palo Alto Firewall
  3. F5 LTM/APM/ASM

Nice to have

  1. CUCM / NICE
  2. IronPort
  3. DNA Center
  4. Devops skills will be appreciated

Education / Experience

  • At least 5 years of Experience
  • Experience in Banking Industry will be appreciated

Languages

Personal skills
