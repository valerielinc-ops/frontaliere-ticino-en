The Position Digital Campaign Delivery Intern

Position summary

As a Digital Campaign Delivery Intern in the Global Digital Hub, you ensure the successful delivery of digital campaigns content creation projects within the Digital Campaigns Chapter. The role plays a crucial role in ensuring timely and high-quality digital campaigns content creation coordination and completion.

Responsibilities

● Manage the affiliate or global digital campaigns content creation and delivery process, ensuring timely and successful completion of any projects from initiation to final implementation, while ensuring delivery standards are met. ● Supports Campaign Coordinators and Global Campaign Experts in client communications in the area of service offering, change management or communication ● Adoption and Center of Excellence: follow existing processes, establish process governance principles and maintain proper documentation and implementation within the scope of responsibility

Who You Are You are an enthusiastic, growth-minded individual eager to launch your career in project coordination, digital marketing, and content operations. You bring strong structural thinking, exceptional organizational skills, and a high level of learning agility. You thrive in structured environments, adapt quickly to changing feedback, and are excited to support cross-functional projects from concept to delivery.

Core Requirements & Mindset

  • Learning Agility & Curiosity: High capacity and enthusiasm to quickly master digital marketing workflows, content production processes, and new project management tools.
  • Organization & Structure: Exceptional attention to detail with the natural ability to break down projects, prioritize tasks, and manage personal and team timelines effectively.
  • Eagerness to Grow: A self-motivated mindset focused on learning how to manage project lifecycles, coordinate schedules, and deliver work on time.
  • Strong Communication: Clear verbal and written skills to collaborate seamlessly with team members, ask the right questions, and integrate feedback.
  • Team Collaboration: Ability to build relationships and support cross-functional partners, including designers, copywriters, and project leads.
  • Education & Background: Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or a related field (no prior professional experience required).
