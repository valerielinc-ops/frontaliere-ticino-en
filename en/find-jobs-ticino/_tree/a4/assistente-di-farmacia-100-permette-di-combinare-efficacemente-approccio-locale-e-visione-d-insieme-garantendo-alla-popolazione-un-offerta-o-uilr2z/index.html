Full description

Pharmacy assistant - Ausschreibung The EOC, the multi-site hospital in Ticino, is present with its institutes throughout the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1,000 beds. The organization allows us to effectively combine a local approach and an overall vision, guaranteeing the population a global and local hospital offer, regardless of the place where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 collaborators and their attention to human relationships, the EOC ensures quality healthcare by taking care of over 41,000 inpatients per year and providing over 600,000 outpatient consultations. To complete the team of the Hospital Pharmacy Service of the Pharmacological Sciences Institute of Italian Switzerland (ISFSI) at the Lugano Regional Hospital, we are looking for a Pharmacy Assistant (100%) Profile: The person we are looking for knows how to actively listen to their interlocutors, ensuring adequate advice, correct handling of medicines according to current directives and provisions and effective interaction with patients. Knows how to act quickly using their knowledge, technical and interpersonal skills to interact with the various hospital services and patients; maintaining effective and efficient relationships with the various interlocutors. Responds to requests with flexibility and competence. Actively participates in the centralized preparation of cytostatics and on-call service. He believes, like us, in the importance of putting the patient at the center of all our attention.

  • Necessary requirements: federal certificate of competence (AFC) as a pharmacy assistant, medical assistant, laboratory technician, laboratory technician in biology/chemistry or equivalent qualification; excellent command of the Italian language and good knowledge of German or French; good computer skills; good organisational, relational and communication skills. Entry date: 1 August 2026 or date to be agreed. The contract is intended as a fixed-term contract until 1 June 2027 at the latest. Forwarding of applications and information: Applications complete with curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, copies of study and work certificates, must be received by 19 July 2026 exclusively in electronic format via the website www.eoc.ch. Applications sent by post or e-mail will not be taken into consideration, as will those that do not comply with the required requirements. Further information can be requested from Davide De Stefani, ENT pharmacy manager (091 811 63 38; davide.destefani@eoc.ch).
