Full description

Located at 1000 meters above sea level, the Country-D-Enhaut Health Centre offers a wide range of hospital services, accommodation, accompaniment for the elderly and home care. According to the so-called integrated care approach, this innovative organization represents an opportunity for health professionals to work in small multidisciplinary teams, for better continuity of care and in response to the health needs of the population of the Upper Country. In the heart of a privileged natural environment, less than an hour away from Lausanne or Bern, the Pays-d'Enhaut is renowned for its quality of life and offers a wide range of sports, leisure and cultural activities, to live summer and winter!
