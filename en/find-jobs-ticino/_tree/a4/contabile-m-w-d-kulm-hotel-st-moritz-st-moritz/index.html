As a member of the Luxury Kulm Group you are part of the key to our success. Together, we make every stay deeply inspiring, unique and distinctive. And as such, we embrace our culture: understated luxury, inspiration, and a life experience. Together, we make it happen. This is your chance to be a member of our shaping culture: | This is what you move with us: You are passionate about numbers, work in a structured way, and are ready to take on responsibility in an exclusive environment? In our Finance Team, you ensure that two of the most renowned hotels in Switzerland are financially perfectly positioned. Your responsibilities – precise, diverse, impactful:

  • You actively contribute to the preparation of monthly and annual financial statements and ensure transparent, reliable financial data.
  • You are responsible for accounts payable — including payment transactions and timely payment runs.
  • You ensure clean accounts receivable processes and handle dunning procedures professionally and solution-oriented.
  • You manage lease agreements and handle invoicing for our residences using Opera Cloud.
  • Start date: January 2027 or by arrangement. | This is you:
  • You have a solid commercial education (or equivalent) and bring several years of experience in accounting.
  • You communicate effortlessly in German and have good command of English.
  • Excel is a tool you use efficiently — digital solutions excite you.
  • You work independently, precisely, and enjoy taking ownership of your area of responsibility.
  • Your professional demeanor, natural kindness, and polished appearance complete your profile.

Become part of our Group Finance Team and actively shape the future of two iconic Swiss hotels — Kulm Hotel St. Moritz and Grand Hotel Kronenhof Pontresina.
