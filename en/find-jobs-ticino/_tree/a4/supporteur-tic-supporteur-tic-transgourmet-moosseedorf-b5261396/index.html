Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You take over field support within headquarters and at external locations throughout Switzerland

  • You are ready to take care of piketting outside normal business hours

  • Experience in IT support and ticketing is an advantage

  • Working independently is a matter of course for you,
  • You are team capable and resilient, stay calm even in hectic situations
  • You are flexible in working hours in order to cover the piketting service as well as occasional assignments outside the regular working hours
  • You have good communication skills and manners
