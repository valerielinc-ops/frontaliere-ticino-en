Full description

Operator weighing (day work)

As Operator Logistics, you ensure the timely and GMP-compliant supply of production with raw materials, consumables and packaging components. With your reliable way of working, you ensure a smooth flow of materials and contribute directly to the production of high-quality medicines.

What we offer

  • Attractive remuneration and comprehensive social benefits
  • Training and development opportunities
  • Safe and modern working environment
  • Open, respectful and inclusive corporate culture
  • Cooperation in an international environment

The full overview of our benefits can be found here: https://www.lonza.com/careers/benefits

Their tasks

  • Implementation of receipts and exits
  • Control, storage and provision of materials for production
  • Implementation of material bookings in the ERP and warehouse management system
  • Monitoring of stockpiles and carrying out inventories
  • In-house transport of materials by forklifts
  • Management of deviations and support for improvement measures
  • Close cooperation with production, quality control and supply chain

This is what you bring with you

  • Experience in logistics, warehousing or material supply
  • Knowledge of ERP or warehouse management systems
  • Understanding GMP, quality and safety requirements
  • Careful, reliable and structured workflow
  • Experience in handling forklifts, ideally with forklift
  • Teamwork and good communication skills
  • Completed vocational training or comparable professional experience
