Area Sales Manager: in Coop Pronto

Coop Pronto AG — Allschwil, GE, Svizzera

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Specialisation: Other specialisations
  • Place of work: Coop Pronto AG, Hegenheimermattweg 65, 4123 Allschwil

Coop Pronto AG is a company of the Coop Group. As a leading Swiss company in the convenience, filling station and energy sector, we operate a network of more than 330 Coop Pronto Shops with or without filling stations in Switzerland and Liechtenstein.We are continuously expanding our Coop Pronto Shop and Coop filling station network.You accompany and develop the franchise partners in your area and ensure the business, legal and professional support of the Coop Pronto Shops. You monitor the key figures, willingness to sell and market trends and promote targeted sales, training and young talent in your area.

Tasks

  • Support and further development of franchise partners: inside
  • Ensuring the professional, legal and business support of the Coop Pronto shops (approximately 20-25 locations)
  • Monitoring of the measures taken to comply with the turnover, sales, QS, labour law, profitability and sales promotion requirements
  • Overall responsibility for willingness to sell in the sales area
  • Preparation of the budget specifications for the sales-relevant key figures within the sales area
  • Market monitoring and support of fuel pricing managers
  • Support for education and training as well as the promotion of young franchise partners:

Requirements

  • Completed training in sales/detail trade preferably in the food industry
  • Several years of professional experience in the retail trade, of which at least 3-5 years in a management position and successful track record with own turnover and cost responsibility
  • Further education in Swiss retail or business administration/management/marketing
  • Strong advisory and communication strength
  • Very good MS-Office knowledge, safe handling of electronic media
  • Great self-organization / self-motivation as well as high time flexibility and resilience
  • Very good knowledge of French in word and writing as well as good knowledge of German in word
  • Place of residence ‘ catchment area Lausanne/Geneva’ (otherwise a move is required)
  • A letter of motivation in German is desired
