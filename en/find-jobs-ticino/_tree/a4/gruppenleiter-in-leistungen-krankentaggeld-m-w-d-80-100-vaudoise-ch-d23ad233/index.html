Full description

Vaudoise is one of the leading Swiss insurance companies and an attractive and future-oriented employer. In keeping with its cooperative identity and values, Vaudoise creates a healthy, inclusive and inspiring working environment in which you can develop further. In the area of personal insurance, we are looking for a group leader (80 to 100%) with a place of work in Wankdorf to strengthen the team's sick day allowance. The role includes, on the one hand, the management of a team of specialized case handlers and, on the other hand, the handling of a small proportion of claims files. Their tasks:

  • You support and advise your employees professionally in the dossiers
  • You organize and coordinate the work of your group: conducting team meetings, one-on-one meetings, planning representatives, etc.
  • They actively participate in the cadre meetings and work closely with the head of department and other group leaders
  • You train your employees (8-10 people) and ensure the correct application of the processing guidelines
  • You plan, organize and control the handling of your team’s performance cases and actively participate in improving and simplifying processes
  • You independently and proactively handle a part of the claims of your own customer portfolio (from the opening, in close cooperation with the trusted doctors and our care managers)
  • You maintain regular contacts with partners, insured persons, employers and service providers your profile:
  • Professional certificate in insurance or equivalent education
  • Proven and in-depth experience in the field of day sickness benefits
  • Leader with experience in team leadership and decision-making
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage
  • Secure use of the usual computer science tools (MS Office) If you are also a committed, organized and performance-oriented leader with a strong customer orientation and positive attitude, then you are probably the person we are looking for! We offer you:
  • A performance-promoting, modern working environment in a cooperative company that has been voted the best employer several times
  • A success-promoting, above-average employment contract for sales professionals from the industry
  • Efficient, highly trained customer support team as well as specialist support in the corporate and pension business
  • Advantageous social benefits, flexibility in work organization and various employee discounts
  • Support for professional development and further education We look forward to your complete application documents by electronic means (with CV, letter of motivation, copy of employment certificates and diplomas).
