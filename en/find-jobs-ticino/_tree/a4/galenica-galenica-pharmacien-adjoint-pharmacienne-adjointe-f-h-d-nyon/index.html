About us

The strongest network in the Swiss health market. Galenica is the leading provider of health care in our country. The Galenica network offers work places for a wide variety of future-oriented occupations that have one thing in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate Switzerland's largest pharmacy network and offer well-known own brands and exclusive brands and products from business partners. In addition, we are the leading wholesaler in the health market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is in Bern, where all central functions are grouped. This is where we provide valuable services for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication and finance.
