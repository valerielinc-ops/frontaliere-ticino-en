Put to the Point

In a constantly changing security situation, it is all the more important to recognize connections before others see them. By analyzing, interpreting and prioritizing information from various sources, you create the situational picture, identify risks and create the basis for preventive protective measures to be targeted and effective. The Service for Preventive Protection of the Army (DPSA) forms the first line of defense of the army. It monitors and assesses the military security situation and takes precautionary measures to protect the army from espionage, sabotage and other illegal acts.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20331
  • Location: Papiermühlestrasse 20, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Creating, analysing and communicating a situational picture of the entire range of operations of the Army Preventive Protection Service (DPSA)
  • Interpret, compare, consolidate and evaluate data, identify gaps in the situational picture and derive targeted measures to close them
  • Analyze threats such as espionage, sabotage and other illegal acts as well as develop preventive protection concepts and measures
  • Develop intelligence products and decision-making bases for the operational level and represent the military defence situation in specialist committees
  • Continuously monitor, assess and prepare the internal security situation of the army in accordance with target groups

This makes them unique.

  • Officer in the Swiss Army (minor lieutenant), specialised training as intelligence officer
  • University/specialist university degree (preferably in political science, criminology, law or psychology) or multi-year functional professional experience in the intelligence service/state protection or police sector
  • Experience and competencies at operational level (staff work)
  • Strong analytical, methodological and editorial competence as well as step-oriented communication
  • Good oral and written knowledge of a second official language and good knowledge of English

Your commitment to security and freedom The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are civil employees – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
