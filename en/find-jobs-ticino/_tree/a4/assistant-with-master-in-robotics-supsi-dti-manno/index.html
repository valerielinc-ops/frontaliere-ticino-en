# Assistant with Master in Robotics

The University of Applied Sciences and Arts of Southern Switzerland (SUPSI) invites applications for an , at the

Dalle Molle Institute for Artificial Intelligence (IDSIA USI-SUPSI)

within the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI), located in Lugano. The degree of employment is 100%, with a contract starting on 01.01.2027, or on a mutually agreed date.

Duties and responsibilities

  • Design and develop navigation algorithms for autonomous self-balancing systems, including omnidirectional sensing-based navigation, fail-safe navigation, and shared autonomy functionalities.
  • Lead the integration and validation of the autonomy stack on a real robotic platform, from onboard hardware integration to testing in representative indoor and outdoor environments.
  • Collaborate with industrial partners to support the transition from research prototype to a product with TRL 6–7.
  • Collaborate with researchers, PhD candidates and Master's students, supporting their research activities and scientific development.
  • Contribute to the preparation and dissemination of research results, to be included in publications in high-quality international conferences and journals.
  • Support the search, management and execution of funded research projects, ensuring timely delivery of milestones and maintaining effective communication with academic and industrial partners.

Requirements

Technical background and skills

  • Master’s degree in Robotics or a related field, with less of three years of professional experience.
  • Solid programming skills in Python (and ideally C++) for robotics applications, with experience in ROS2-based development.
  • Proven experience in autonomous systems and robot perception and control, including areas such as navigation and/or human-robot interaction.
  • Excellent analytical, mathematical, and problem-solving skills.

Soft skills

  • Strong motivation for applied research at the intersection of AI, robotics, and real-world deployment, with genuine interest in bridging academic research and industrial innovation.
  • Ability to work independently while contributing effectively to multidisciplinary and international research teams.
  • Excellent communication skills in English, both written and spoken.
  • Strong organizational skills, proactivity, and a problem-solving mindset oriented toward impactful and high-quality research.

Desirable but not strictly required

  • Experience with deep learning frameworks (e.g. PyTorch, TensorFlow) applied to perception or robotics tasks.
  • Hands-on experience with rapid prototyping, including 3D printing and hardware integration.
  • Scientific publications in high-quality international conferences and journals in the relevant research areas.

What SUPSI Offers

  • A full-time employment contract, initially for two years, with possibility of extension.
  • Attractive salary, in line with Swiss standards and the candidate's experience.
  • An international working environment.
  • Travel support to participate to high-quality conferences and workshops.
  • Opportunity to develop professional and scientific skills.
  • Co-supervision of students.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
