About us

Be part of the energy transition! ETAVIS Bern-Mittelland AG is one of the leading companies in the Swiss electrical installation and telecommunications market. We provide all services related to consulting, project planning, execution, support and maintenance for electrical and building technology, telematics, telecom infrastructure and automation.

Tasks

  • Support customers from order acceptance through to reporting and build long-term relationships
  • Carry out high-voltage and low-voltage installations as well as repair and maintenance work
  • Inspect installations, perform measurements and tests, identify and fix possible causes of faults or errors

Requirements

  • Vocational training as an electrician installer EFZ, assembly electrician EFZ or an equivalent qualification recognized by ESTI.
  • Interest in new technologies
  • Category B driving licence

We offer

  • Open, family-like and collegial working environment

Who we are

ETAVIS has the right expertise for all topics in the field of electrical engineering. As part of the international VINCI Energies group, we have the power for innovation to remain the best solution provider in the future.
