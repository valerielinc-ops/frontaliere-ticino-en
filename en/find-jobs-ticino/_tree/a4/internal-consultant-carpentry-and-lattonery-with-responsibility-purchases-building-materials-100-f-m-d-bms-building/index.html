Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is one of the wholesalers active in the distribution of building materials for crude construction and interior finishes. Our company group includes renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco . Our proven experts offer advice, assistance and inspiration to building and private customers at 110 stores throughout Switzerland, all with great dedication. We are up to date on developments in the building sector and this makes us a reliable partner for each construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

What are you waiting for? Become part of our team and bring your energy and expertise to our store. Entry from 1 October 2026 or date to be agreed

To reinforce our Bellinzona team, we are looking for a(a)

Tasks and responsibilities

As a customer advisor in our building materials store in Via San Gottardo 98 in Bellinzona, you are the stable, structured and reliable point of reference for our customers and within the company. You will ensure operational complementarity with external department colleagues, ensuring continuity, precision and coordination in the entire business process.

Your responsibilities include

  • Customer management: technical-commercial support, order processing and offers, product advice.
  • Operational collaboration and structured support to external sales consultants: act as an internal contact person that guarantees continuity, preparation and coverage in business activities, ensuring smooth and punctual processes.
  • Understanding technical documentation: reading and interpretation of plans, diagrams and diagrams according to industry standards and standards.
  • Purchasing responsibility: planning and management of procurement, stock monitoring, definition of requirements.
  • Negotiation with suppliers: negotiation on prices, conditions, quality and delivery terms; evaluation and development suppliers.
  • Internal sales development: promotion of carpentry/lactonery assortment and identification of business opportunities.
  • Internal coordination: collaboration with logistics, warehouse and external sale.
  • Documentary management: updating lists, personal articles, contracts and commercial conditions.
