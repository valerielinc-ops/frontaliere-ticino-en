Full description

Radio Télévision Suisse is looking for several audiovisual technicians for the Production News department within the Production Unit.

Your Mission

The TAV will be trained in various aspects of the profession through theoretical and practical training.

The TAV News has to work on Radio, TV, Digital productions. It exploits the technical means dedicated to the acquisition of audiovisual data, the assembly, mixing, sound illustration, image control, the framing of cameras and the live broadcast of news broadcasts on all vectors.

Your profile

  • Recognized training in audio and/or video
  • Professional experience required
  • Knowledge of shooting and video editing
  • Confirmed ability to use new technologies
  • Autonomy, initiative and adaptability to the surrounding conditions
  • Ability to act quickly, while managing direct stress
  • Faculty to work in partnership and team
  • Good general culture and interest in current affairs
  • Availability and flexibility related to an activity with irregular schedules, including evenings and weekends

What we offer you

  • A stimulating and varied working environment within a public service media company.
  • A dynamic and collaborative team, where the spirit of self-help and the quality of service are essential.

Our promise

Reaching the entire Swiss population with high quality formats requires expertise, enthusiasm and a stimulating and creative environment in which everyone can invest and evolve. For the best to give the best of themselves, they need an inspiration: Welcome to the world of RTS!

  • Create Content: Quality journalism is the core of our activity and passion. We enjoy complete freedom of writing, encourage creativity and grant autonomy for innovation and quality.
  • Living digital: In our country, quality journalism can also take a digital form. This is why we are exploring new opportunities and experimenting with new formats and practices: digital, interactive and cross-media. Our content is part of the air of time and responds to public concerns.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
