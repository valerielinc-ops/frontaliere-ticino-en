To strengthen our electrical team, we are looking for a committed and competent personality in the electrical department as Project Manager Electrical 80-100% (m/f/d). Your

  • Responsibilities
  • Independent planning, design and further development of electrical systems and controls on our crushing and screening plants
  • Assistance in the creation and maintenance of technical documents, diagrams and parts lists in cooperation with our external control engineer
  • Technical support for projects, commissioning and internal clarifications
  • Assistance in the optimization of electrical components and processes as well as in the procurement of components
  • Independent coordination with internal and external bodies; control engineering, design, assembly and service, engine center, hydraulics
  • Ensuring reliable, practical and high-quality implementation
  • Deputy and support of the head of department in coordination, scheduling and personnel management What you bring to the table
  • Completed technical apprenticeship in the field of electrical and/or automatic engineering
  • Training or further education as an electrical engineer / automation specialist or a comparable qualification is an advantage
  • Professional experience in the control sector, ideally in mechanical and/or plant engineering
  • Understanding of technical contexts and enjoyment of practical solutions are required Independent, reliable and structured way of working
  • Ability to work in a team, commitment and enjoyment of a varied task
