Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for agreement reinforcement. Tasks: • As medical secretary, you are the first contact person of our clinic and shape the first impression with your professional appearance – thanks to you, everyday hospital life runs smoothly, efficiently and patient-oriented • In your turntable function, you take over the medical correspondence, are in telephone contact with patients and referees and organize the vascular surgery consultation hours from assignment via diagnostics and surgery to follow-up control, including maintenance of all data in the clinic information system (EPIC). • As part of clinical patient management, you will accompany and coordinate the individual patient progressions along the entire patient path and work closely with the medical service, patient disposition, surgical coordination and the office centre. • In addition, in close cooperation with the neighboring disciplines of the vascular center, you ensure a smooth progression of further diagnostics and therapy and coordinate the corresponding interfaces for optimal patient care. Requirements • With a completed medical education (MPA) or a commercial education including further training as medical secretary or Medical secretary and several years of experience in the medical environment provide you with the ideal basis for this demanding task • Commitment, reliability and an independent, organized way of working distinguish you as well as the ability to keep an overview even in hectic situations – supported by your resilient, structured and socially competent personality • Very good IT knowledge and enjoyment of administrative tasks are required and form the basis for efficient and precise work with modern digital systems • Well-founded know-how in medical terminology is essential to communicate safely, accurately and professionally in every situation – both in writing and orally. We offer: • We offer you an exciting, varied and challenging field of activity with development potential in a growing medical field • In a versatile area of responsibility with a great deal of room for manoeuvre, you can contribute your skills in a targeted manner and take on a high degree of self-responsibility. • Look forward to a motivated, collegial and competent team in the clinic for vascular surgery, which welcomes you and attaches great importance to a supportive cooperation • Thanks to regular working hours from Monday to Friday, you benefit from a balanced work-life balance as well as at least 25 holiday days and 13 paid holidays. Further information on our attractive employment conditions can be found at www.luks.ch/employment conditions
