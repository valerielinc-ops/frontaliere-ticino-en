Full description

What you do

Do you want to expand your experience from acute care and develop further in emergency care? With us, you can expect a practical further education with expert support. Depending on your training level, you take on step-by-step responsibility in emergency daily life.

  • As part of your acquired competencies, you actively participate in emergency situations, monitor and look after patients and accompany their relatives.
  • You use your expertise in everyday work and continuously develop your skills.
  • You will learn to triage according to ESI, assess the need for care and set targeted nursing priorities.
  • You work closely with different professional groups and experience interprofessional cooperation in dynamic emergency operations.
  • You work in shifts and get involved in everyday life.
  • Our vocational training team and the experts emergency care accompany you competently during the further education and support you in your learning process.

What distinguishes you

  • You are Dipl. nurse or dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH and bring at least one year of professional experience in acute care.
  • You are interested in specialized emergency care and complex patient situations.
  • Even in rapidly changing conditions, you maintain an overview and set priorities.
  • You enjoy working in a team, communicate openly and appreciate the exchange with different professional groups.
  • With your willingness to learn, reliable and appreciative personality, you also approach challenging situations calmly and responsibly.

Good to know

In the interdisciplinary emergency center with 21 treatment stations and a shock room, medical and surgical emergencies – from simple general and accident surgical injuries to complex internistic, acute-cardiac and surgical emergencies as well as multiple injuries – are treated. The further development of the Dipl. Expert/Expert Emergency Care NDS HF takes two years. You will acquire the theoretical basics at the Bernese Education Center Nursing (BZP), the practical skills at our Lindenhof hospital at a modern interdisciplinary emergency department.
