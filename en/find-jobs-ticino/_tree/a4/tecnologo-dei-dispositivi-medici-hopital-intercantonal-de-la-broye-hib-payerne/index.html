Full description

Thanks to its 800 employees, the Intercantonal Hospital of Broye (HIB) provides an acute care and rehabilitation mission. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative health institution striving for excellence for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges that await it, HIB is looking for a: 100% medical device technologist Your mission

  • Carry out the reprocessing of medical devices (pre-disinfection, washing, control, packaging, sterilization, storage, distribution)
  • Master all manual and automated medical device reprocessing techniques (surgical instruments and endoscopes) in compliance with quality and institutional directives
  • Guarantee the traceability of all medical devices
  • Manage the production unit's stocks of consumables in collaboration with the team manager
  • Carry out the usual checks on the equipment used and know how to detect non-conformities
  • Participate in the supervision and compliance with the application of the department's procedures and protocols as well as the development of good professional practices
  • Establish privileged and transversal links with the partners of the different departments and daily contacts (operating room, departments, staff technical)
  • Supervise TDM apprentices Your assets
  • You have a CFC as a medical device technologist
  • Experience in the sterilization and/or operating room sector is an asset
  • FEE training an asset or be willing to follow it
  • You are comfortable with IT tools
  • With a great ability to work in a team, you demonstrate rigor in carrying out your tasks
  • You know how to manage priorities and make yourself available when necessary necessary Entry into office: immediately or to be agreed. We thank you for sending us your complete application file by applying online. For any additional questions about this position, please contact Ms. Rabiaa Yahia, Head Nurse of the operating room and sterilization, on 026 662 81 79. In the event of technical difficulties, please contact our recruitment department on 026 662 81 91.

Requirements

  • Hold a CFC in medical device technology

  • Experience in the sterilization and/or operating room sector is an advantage

  • FEE training is an advantage or be willing to follow it

  • Be comfortable with computer tools

  • Be equipped with a great ability to work in a team, demonstrate rigor in the realization of their tasks

  • Know how to manage priorities and be available when needed
