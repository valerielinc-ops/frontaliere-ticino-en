Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

The ICT ERP Services team is responsible for the support and further development of SAP special applications. In cooperation with our partners in the specialist areas, we ensure the smooth running of numerous support processes within the hospital.

IT Business Partner SAP IS-H & Patient Management 80-100 %

You do

We are looking for an SAP Business Analyst whose main task is to ensure expertise in the area of the IS-H module, ensuring continuity and further development of the master data and patient management process. He/she will work closely with the specialist and IT teams, help resolve disruptions and implement change requirements. Our future colleague will also be responsible for the common medical applications associated with SAP (Ibicare, Diacos, Idefix, etc.) and will take on the following tasks:

  • Planning / implementation of the necessary updates
  • Management of incidents and new requirements
  • Coordination of external and internal resources
  • Definition of interfaces from / to SAP IS-H
  • Regular maintenance of systems outside regular working hours

Working in this role You work closely with the other teams of our Digital & Technology Directorate (technical support, medical services, network and infrastructure, security).

This sets you apart

  • You have in-depth knowledge of SAP and have at least 3-5 years of experience as a business analyst or similar.
  • You master the IS-H module in an SAP ECC EHP-8 environment (experience with SAP S4H would be an advantage).
  • Several years of experience as an SAP key user in the medical sector as well as a strong interest in computer science and the willingness to familiarize themselves with technical aspects would also be beneficial.
  • Knowledge of SQL and SAP queries as well as ABAP debugging
  • Ideally, You have several years of experience in the medical sector, especially in the hospital environment, and master the Swiss tariff systems (Tarmed and Tardoc).
  • In order to ensure effective communication with hospital staff and our partners in Switzerland, the candidate must be bilingual French/German (minimum B1-B2 in the other language).
  • Excellent interpersonal skills are also required to best accompany the change in the various contacts.
  • Readiness for pikettdienst (max. during 4 weeks per year)

You will benefit from this

  • A motivated team characterized by an open and collaborative working atmosphere
  • A thorough training in the new field of activity
  • An exciting job in a very innovative and future-oriented environment
  • Flexible and family-friendly working time models with in-house KITA
  • Development prospects and training opportunities
  • Collaborating with bilingual colleagues from many different professions in a dynamic environment
  • Modern employment conditions governed by a collective employment contract
