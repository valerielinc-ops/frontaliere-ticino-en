Full description

The Philanthropy & Engagement team, within the Private Bank's Wealth Planning business line, supports private clients throughout the Group's operations in implementing their committed projects, from idea to action. The Philanthropy & Engagement Advisor is part of the Private Bank's business and contributes to the holistic accompaniment of clients in a global view of their heritage.

Active in Swiss and international markets, he/she performs the following tasks:

Customer support

  • Along with a senior team, help support private clients in their philanthropic projects, from needs identification to implementation.
  • Produce synthetic content for customers: support proposals, tailor-made courses, thematic studies on causes, training, stakeholder mapping, etc.
  • Work with bankers and heritage engineers to co-build tailor-made accompaniments.
  • Design and conduct collective intelligence workshops.
  • Offer dating programs using your networks.

Contribution to the animation of the offer

  • Actively develop the network of key partners at European level (thematic experts, legal advice, foundations, associations, think tanks, etc.).
  • Ensure active monitoring of engagement (emerging models, impact investment).
  • Contribute to the monitoring of KPI activity.

PROFILE

  • Graduated in social sciences or economics/business.
  • Successful experience of at least 5 years in the field of public interest and/or impact investment. An experience in the board or bank would be a plus.
  • Knowledge base on philanthropy: regulatory framework, vehicles, causes, major donors.
  • Excellent mastery of Excel and Powerpoint tools
  • Current English and French, both oral and written. A third language (Italian or Portuguese) would be a plus.
  • Writing and synthesis capabilities , assertive business sense and result culture , organized , relational ease, sense of initiative and team spirit.
  • Travel to Switzerland and Europe: 1-2 days /month

We thank all applicants for their interest in our House. Only those profiles selected for further recruitment will be contacted.
