What to expect

  • Responsibility for the conception, creation and publication of our employee magazine
  • Writing and preparing editorial content, such as press releases, news releases and other communication contributions
  • Contact person for sponsorship including coordination and implementation of corresponding activities
  • Active participation in trade fairs, events and company events
  • Support in the training and supervision of our mediamatics learners

What do you bring with you

  • Training or initial professional experience in the fields of editorial, journalism, communication or a comparable environment
  • Enjoy writing and preparing editorial content as well as a strong sense of language, style and storytelling
  • Careful, flexible and team-oriented personality with a good eye for detail
  • Structured, independent and reliable working method
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing as well as good knowledge of English
  • Good user knowledge of common Office and Adobe programs
  • Basic technical understanding in the web/internet sector is an advantage

You can look forward to that.

  • Working in the middle of beautiful natural and recreational areas
  • Exciting tasks in an international environment with short decision paths
  • Lots of personal responsibility and attractive management positions
  • Market-oriented remuneration, progressive social benefits and own pension fund with good benefits
  • Staff restaurant, free parking

About us

EMS has 25 production sites in 16 countries, employs around 3,000 people worldwide and, with around 140 apprentices, is the largest vocational trainer in the canton and winner of the National Education Award.

EMS-SERVICES is responsible for the operation of the workshop with other local companies and provides its services in the areas of engineering, energy provision, maintenance, logistics, real estate, information technology, personnel management, accounting and controlling as well as environmental protection and security.
