Full description

You work independently when welding individual parts and assemblies. The company attaches importance to precise work and promotes your professional skills. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Parts and assemblies as shown in the drawing
  • Prepare components for the welding process
  • Precisely positioning workpieces
  • Check the mass with gauges and calipers
  • Supporting learners in everyday work

Requirements

  • Safe to read technical drawings
  • Teaching in the metal sector or equivalent welding practice
  • First experience in TIG and MAG welding
  • Experience in aluminum welding would be desirable
  • Valid welding test according to EN ISO 9606-1 counts as a plus point

Offer

  • Varied applications thanks to small series production
  • Thorough introduction to the task area
  • Opportunities for professional development
