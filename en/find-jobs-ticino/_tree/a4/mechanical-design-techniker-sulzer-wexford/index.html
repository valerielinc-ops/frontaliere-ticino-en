Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Sulzer is a global leader in fluid engineering, specialising in the design and manufacture of pumps and associated equipment for some of the world’s most critical industries. Founded in 1834 and headquartered in Switzerland, we employ over 13,500 people worldwide, operating in more than 40 countries, generating annual sales of approximately CHF 3.6 billion. Our pumping technologies support customers across the energy, water and wastewater, chemicals, industrial manufacturing, life sciences and food markets, enabling safe, reliable and efficient fluid handling in demanding applications.

The Wexford site is a key contributor to Sulzer’s global pumping portfolio, with a strong focus on product development and engineering support for pumps and associated mechanical equipment. The team works closely with international stakeholders, supporting the design and development of products supplied to customers across global markets About the Role & Opportunity

We are seeking a Mechanical Design Technician to join our Product Development team in Wexford. This role plays an important part in supporting the design and development of engineered pump solutions and related equipment, working closely with experienced Project Manager's.

You will be involved throughout the design lifecycle, contributing to products that are used worldwide in process‑critical environments. This is a development‑focused role within a structured engineering organisation, offering long‑term technical growth and progression opportunities.

Key Duties

  • Create and maintain 3D models and 2D engineering drawings for pumps and associated mechanical equipment
  • Produce detailed manufacturing drawings, assemblies and bills of materials
  • Support engineering change requests, design updates and product improvements
  • Collaborate with engineering, manufacturing, testing and quality teams
  • Ensure compliance with engineering standards, procedures and documentation requirements

What We’re Looking For

  • A qualification in Mechanical Engineering, Mechanical Design or a related discipline
  • Experience creating engineering drawings and CAD models within a mechanical or manufacturing environment
  • Understanding of mechanical assemblies, components and technical documentation
  • A structured, detail‑orientated approach with good communication skills
  • Motivation to learn, develop and build a long‑term career within engineering
