Full description

The Hospital Wallis is looking for a hospital for the Hospital Center Oberwallis in the Psychiatry Center Oberwallis (PZO) in the Labor Therapy Wood and Metal Workshop:

Work therapist/in 80-100 %

In the Upper Valais Hospital Center, the Upper Valais Psychiatric Center provides basic psychiatric care for the Upper Valais population (about 85,000 inhabitants) in the inpatient, semi-stationary and ambulatory areas.

Mission

  • You are responsible for the planning, implementation and evaluation of occupational therapy measures
  • They develop activities and work in therapy adapted to the resources of mentally ill people and carry them out together with patients.
  • They accompany and promote patients in order to use and expand their social and professional skills
  • They manage the sports and leisure activities of patient groups
  • You create documentation and reports and work in interdisciplinary teams

Profile

  • Completed vocational training with a federal certificate of competence in a craft profession and several years of professional experience
  • Additional psychosocial training or the willingness to undertake training
  • Professional experience in psychiatry desirable
  • Engaged, resilient, flexible and team-capable personality
  • Structured and patient-oriented working method

Offer

  • Interesting and varied area of responsibility
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Further education and training opportunities
