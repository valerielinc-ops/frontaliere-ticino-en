Your impact - your chance to make a difference

  • You audit annual and consolidated accounts of national and international corporations and companies in the fields of industry, trade and the service sector.
  • Business processes are reviewed by you and you provide assistance with accounting issues according to various standards (OR, IFRS, Swiss GAAP FER, US GAAP).
  • With the Engagement Manager you work on the planning, implementation and finalization of various test assignments as well as various special examinations and are in direct exchange with other specialists.
  • You are part of the digital auditing revolution by using and helping improve our digital auditing tools and approaches.
  • Acquiring in-depth knowledge of a wide range of customers and building and maintaining customer relationships appeals to you.
  • You will be supported by us in your professional and personal development through on- and off-the-job coaching to achieve your long-term career goals.
  • As part of an inspiring, diversified team, you will find yourself in an international network and receive regular feedback from colleagues and managers.

Your Skill Set - What You Bring

  • You complete the training for / to the Dipl. Auditors: in or have just completed these.
  • You will bring at least two years of professional experience in auditing and have gained insights into business audits of companies.
  • You have strong analytical skills, a high quality standard for you and the team and want to actively promote digitalization in auditing.
  • You are motivated and help design solutions to exceed the demands of our customers.
  • You are ready to take responsibility from the beginning and move in an environment with an active coaching culture.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.
