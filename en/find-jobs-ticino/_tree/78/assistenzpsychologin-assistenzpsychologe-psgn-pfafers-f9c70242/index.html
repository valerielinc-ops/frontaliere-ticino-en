Your tasks

  • As a member of our interdisciplinary treatment team, you support patients in the inpatient setting and actively participate in the design of the individual treatment process.
  • After a thorough induction, you will gradually take on case-leading tasks and carry out individual psychotherapeutic treatments in close cooperation with our interdisciplinary team.
  • Your varied day-to-day work covers the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases and offers you the opportunity to get to know different clinical questions and develop your specialist knowledge.
  • As an assistant psychologist, you coordinate treatments, plan individual therapies and carry out test psychological assessments to support the diagnostic assessment.
  • Participation in rapports, interdisciplinary case discussions and further training as well as the regular professional exchange with the other practitioners are integral parts of your work. Your profile
  • You have completed a degree in psychology (recognition by the PsyReg is required for a foreign degree) and are already in an advanced phase of your federally recognized psychotherapy training.
  • An assistant psychotherapy permit from the Canton of St.Gallen (permission to work under supervision) is already available or you meet the requirements to obtain it before taking up the job.
  • An independent and responsible way of working as well as openness, empathy and joy in working together in a committed team distinguish you.
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing at at least level C1 enables you to secure and professional communication in clinical everyday life. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also get the opportunity to get to know your future team. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. So you can get a differentiated picture of the company and already prepare for the job. On the first day you can expect an onboarding in your new team and with other colleagues. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also get your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
