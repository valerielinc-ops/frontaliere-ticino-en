Head of the Nursing Base Training Centre - Ausschreibung

L.EOC The multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes throughout the cantonal territory for a total of 1,000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring to the population a comprehensive hospital offer and proximity, regardless of the place where the services are required.

Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to human relationships, the EOC provides a quality health care care by taking care of over 41,000 patients per year and providing over 600,000 outpatient consultations.

The Nursing Departments of the Regional Hospital of Lugano and the Institute of Cardiocentro Ticino seek a:

Head of the Nursing Basic Training Centre since 2010

The Head of the Centre for Nursing Basic Training is the local coordinator for the quality and development of practical training and serves as a reference for professionals who play a role as trainers and TBs, who accompany the people in training.

Tasks

In particular

  • it translates the guidelines and the training strategy into concrete actions at local level, promoting organisational and training conditions conducive to a.learning experience; - it is the reference for professionals who, in departments and services, accompany people in training in clinical practice, supporting them in the performance of their role, promoting the development of the skills of turgidity and accompaniment through counselling, coaching and supervision; - it guarantees and monitors the quality of the accompaniment and learning in clinical practice, verifying the
  • maintains the link between the local headquarters and the Centre for Nursing and Medical-Technical Training EOC, ensuring alignment between institutional addresses and needs and specific needs of the local context; - maintains relations with educational and training institutions, promoting a constant dialogue and an effective link between training courses and the reality of clinical practice

Requirements and qualifications: Academic and advanced training

  • Bachelor in Nursing, Socrates in Nursing or equivalent qualifications.
  • FFA 1 and/or CAS in Clinical Tutor SUPSI and/or SUFFP course for the referent of the practical training of the latter.
  • Preferably, pedagogical qualification equivalent to 600 hours of training (Level II of the Higher School for Health Training. SSFS, Federal Professional Certificate of Trainer/Adult Trainers FSEA 2 or equivalent title), or willingness to complete their training course with such qualification.
  • Basic course.
