Over the area

The Center for Specialised Psychotherapy and Psychosomatics (ZPP) is part of the Clinic for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy at PDAG (Clinic Director Prof. Dr. med. Marc Walter). It summarizes the inpatient specifically psychotherapeutically oriented treatment offers for people with complex trauma follow-up disorders, chronic depression, personality disorders as well as problems to cope with acute life crises independently or with outpatient services. The offer also includes the special consultation hour pregnancy and breastfeeding. The ZPP is run by Dr. med. Nina Schweinfurth-Keck, Senior Doctor, Dr. phil. Armin Blickenstorfer, Senior Psychologist and Andrea Jaggi, Head of Care and Specialist Therapies, is headed and comprises three stations including a station for supplementary insured persons and an ambulatory. Part of the centre is also a training institute. As part of the Königsfelder seminars, it offers a modular certified curriculum with further training in scientifically based psychotrauma therapy. For doctors, the centre is a SIWF-approved training centre for psychiatry and psychotherapy category A inpatient (3 years).
