Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position A profession for open-minded pragmatists: women with a sense of order

Information & Documentation offers you varied tasks that provide you with insights not only into the history, but also into the past and present research work of the more than 125-year-old pharmaceutical company. In addition to signing and managing documents, you help customers get to the information they need - whether it's to help them use databases or provide them with relevant documents. With us, you will not only work with databases, but also with old files, photographs, VHS cassettes and various objects such as pharmacy ceramics or former laboratory facilities. Working in various internal internships will give you a comprehensive insight into the job profile.

Training The I&D training at Roche prepares you professionally for the working world. It not only promotes your professional development, but also your personal development. The professional maturity is possible after consultation during or after the apprenticeship. The training to become a:r specialist:frau I&D takes 3 years. In the vocational school and in the intercompany courses (üK) you will be taught important basics and skills. You can then apply this theoretical knowledge in practice and gain important professional experience at your line workplace.

What you should bring

  • The upper or middle school level with good to very good grades in the core subjects
  • Good general education
  • Language skills (German, English and French)
  • Analytical and systematic thinking
  • Openness and interest in new technologies
  • Service awareness
  • Communication and team skills
  • Precise and reliable operation
  • Strong sense of order
  • Good memory, independence and self-initiative

The training opportunities

After successful training, there are various further education opportunities at the Higher School of Applied Sciences (HF), University of Applied Sciences (FH) and at the University. For the latter it needs a professional maturity, for the university additionally a passerelle.

Further development options below

  • Professional maturity
  • BM1 (accompanying teaching, by agreement)
  • BM2 (after training)
  • Participation in the Erasmus+ mobility project

European Mobility Vocational Training (https://www.mobility-id.ch/)

  • Various training courses of companies and professional associations
  • Bachelor degree at a university of applied sciences, e.g. Bachelor FH in Information Science

Your advantages

  • You work in an international environment and get first-class insights into the company and everyday work
  • You attend a practice-oriented workshop school
  • You work with state-of-the-art facilities, equipment and future-oriented facilities
  • With corresponding services, there is the possibility that we will support you with various further education opportunities after the training.
  • Depending on the distance to the place of work, you have the opportunity to get a room in the Roche residential building in Basel

Your full application contains the following documents

  • CVC
  • Certificates of the last two semesters
  • Check S2, if you are from the education area Northwest Switzerland (if available)
