Full description

Aproz Sources Minerales SA produces and offers in its assortment major brands for gastronomy (Schweppes, Lipton, Orangina, ...). As an external sales advisor, you develop our presence with HoReCa customers by building lasting and trustworthy relationships.

Luogo di lavoro: Aproz Sources Minerales SA, Aproz, Route de Riddes 327, 1994 Aproz

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but we can only consider online applications. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Acquire new customers in the catering sector.
  • Develop sales activities with regional beverage distributors and develop the customer network.
  • Ensure market exploration in the assigned sales area and implement the commercial strategy.
  • Respect the region's annual budget for sales volume, conditions and services.
  • Observe the entire market and understand trends and developments.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in the field of external sales, in the sector of gastronomy, ideally drinks.
  • Initial or continuing training completed: in the field of sales.
  • A good network of relations and knowledge in gastronomy within the sales area.
  • Ability to conclude cases and be persistent.
  • Driver's licence category B required.
  • French (Very good knowledge)
  • German (preferably)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks maternity leave and 3 weeks paternity leave with pay
  • Mobile subscription: Private mobile subscriptions at reduced prices
  • Points: All employees* benefit from multiple Cumulus points
  • Offers for employees: Our employees* benefit from interesting discounts in many partner companies
  • Holiday: 5 weeks, from 50 years 6 weeks, from 60 years 7 weeks

Contatto

Candidature and contact Timothy of Riedmatten
