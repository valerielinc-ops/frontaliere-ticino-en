Are you looking for a unique professional challenge that not only reflects your passion for physical activity but also promotes your personal development? Welcome to our innovative physiotherapy team, which specializes in internal medicine and in which we not only promote and support patients, but also our therapists! We offer you the unique opportunity to become part of a dynamic environment consisting of three specialized teams (orthopedics/geriatrics, internal medicine, pediatrics) and a dedicated secretariat. We are looking for an immediately or by agreement DIPLOMIATED PHYSIOTHERAPETS/IN WITH SPECIALIZATION IN THE AREA OF SPORTS, 40% - 100% Your tasks – our job

  • You treat patients from the fields of sports, orthopaedics and traumatology, in particular the follow-up of patients under the protocol "Return To Sport"
  • You care for outpatients from different specialist areas
  • You also care for patient groups, especially in the fields of internal medicine (cardiology, pneumology, oncology) as well as geriatrics and orthopaedics
  • You independently record the services and documentation of patients
  • You represent the physiotherapy department

What makes us unique

  • A dynamic work environment: We believe in the power of change and promote an inspiring and dynamic work culture that leaves room for ideas and innovation
  • Further education: We support and encourage our physiotherapy team to continuously develop and deepen its competences. Whether further training, specialisations or participation in national workshops and symposia – we invest in your professional development
  • Bilingual environment: Our team is bilingual! We value the diversity of languages and cultures and provide an inclusive environment for patients and employees.

Your skills – our requirements

  • You are a qualified physiotherapist (recognition by the SRC and admission to practice the profession in Basel-Stadt are advantageous)
  • You have additional training in sports physiotherapy
  • You have additional training in manual therapy or are currently undergoing training in this area, which is beneficial for us
  • You are full of energy and enthusiasm and ready to continue your education
  • You are committed, motivated and proactive and can make a decisive contribution to the development of our organization in a rapidly changing environment.
  • You are team-oriented, open, committed, stress-resistant and communicative and used to working independently
  • You communicate fluently in German or French (in word and writing) and have a good knowledge of the other language

Your possibilities – our offer

  • Varied activities in the field of health sports and prevention
  • Collaboration with bilingual colleagues from many different professions in a lively environment
  • Diverse career perspectives with further education and individual development opportunities
  • Family-friendly personnel policy, company crèche, modern working conditions and good accessibility with public transport
