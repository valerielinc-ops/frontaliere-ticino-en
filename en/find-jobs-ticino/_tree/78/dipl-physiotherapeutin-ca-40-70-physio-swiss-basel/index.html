To strengthen our team, we are looking for a physiotherapist: in (m/f/d) preferably with further training or interest in sports physiotherapy and manual therapy.(HF/FH/BSc/SRK Partial decision) We are a centrally located practice in the heart of Basel about five minutes walk from the SBB train station, work with doctors from various fields and look after friendly patients with exciting and different diagnoses. Our focus areas are: orthopaedics, traumatology, sports physiotherapy, rheumatology, lymphology and domicile treatments. We offer a varied working day, beautiful bright spaces, good remuneration, high holiday requirements and support further training, both internally and externally. Would you like and enjoy supporting our dynamic and competent team? If you work reliably, motivated and value a problem- and goal-oriented treatment approach, then send us your application by post or e-mail. We would be happy to meet you. Your employer
