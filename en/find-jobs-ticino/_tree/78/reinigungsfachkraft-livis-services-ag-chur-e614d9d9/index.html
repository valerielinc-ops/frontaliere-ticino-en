LIVIS Services AG is a professional service company in the areas of cleaning, facility services, building management, care and consultation.

Your tasks include*

  • Technical and timely execution and implementation of various cleaning orders such as basic cleaning, maintenance cleaning, construction cleaning, etc.
  • Professional cleaning and maintenance of various properties
  • Compliance with the specified safety, hygiene and quality guidelines
  • Ensure proper use of available cleaning agents and cleaning machines
  • Material and storage management incl. Inventory control
  • Accurate and precise targeting of regulations
  • Participation in instructions and training
  • Occasional, pre-planned evening and weekend assignments as needed.
  • Winter work (snow clearance and smoothing)

Your profile

  • Experience in building cleaning (basic and maintenance cleaning) or training as an EFZ / EBA building cleaner is advantageous
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Compulsory category B driving licence
  • Teamwork, punctuality and reliability
  • Service-oriented and self-employed work
  • Careful and responsible handling of machines and tools
  • Replacement and operational readiness for planned evening missions from 17:00
  • Readiness for weather-dependent winter service missions, including early morning, evening and weekends
  • Flexibility and sense of duty in operations
  • Good Constitution and Physical Resilience
