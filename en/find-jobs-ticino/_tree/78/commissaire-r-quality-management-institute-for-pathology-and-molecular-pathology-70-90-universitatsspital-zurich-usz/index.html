Full description

Take on a central role in the quality management of a highly specialized university laboratory and actively contribute to ensuring the highest diagnostic standards. In this responsible role, you shape processes, drive further developments and work closely with interdisciplinary teams. Look forward to a meaningful task with effect.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • They are responsible for all aspects of accreditation according to ISO 15189 and inspection body according to ISO 17020 and the maintenance and further development of the QM system (DMS). • You maintain our intranet page in connection with the QM documents • You support our employees from the various areas in the preparation, updating and adaptation of QM documents • For internal and external audits, you ensure timely planning, preparation, monitoring and follow-up. • They communicate with the authorities, in particular the representatives of the Swiss Accreditation Body (SAS) • You are a committed member of our QM Group Laboratory Diagnostics Requirements • You have completed training as BMA HF and some years of professional experience and/or further training in the quality management of medical laboratories • You are an independent, resilient and effective personality, are responsible and have a good assertiveness • Thanks to excellent communication skills, you will be able to establish a trustworthy cooperation with all stakeholders. • You like to work interdisciplinary in a team and it gives you pleasure to pass on your own knowledge We offer: In the accredited future-oriented laboratory you can expect a varied, interesting and demanding activity. You will be able to supplement your experience and knowledge with regular further education and training. Furthermore, you can expect attractive employment conditions in a university environment and a motivated, committed team.
