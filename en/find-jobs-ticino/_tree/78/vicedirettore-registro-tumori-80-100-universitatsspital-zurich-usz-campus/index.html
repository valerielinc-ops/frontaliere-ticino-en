Full description

The core task of the register is to record the cancer-related diseases that have occurred in the population of the cantons ZH, ZG, SH and SZ and thus systematically document the cancer burden. This monitoring is a central basis for research, prevention and continuous improvement of care. Zurich University Hospital is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees are committed to the welfare of our patients every day.

Tasks

  • You already date cancer cases recorded in the database according to international standards (ICD-O, TNM) and document therapies and medical follow-ups.
  • As a competent contact person, you support registrant:inside with questions about registration and coding.
  • You ensure the data quality by careful checks and continuous verification of completeness.
  • You coordinate registration and coding processes in close coordination with the management and in compliance with the NKRS requirements.
  • You work closely with doctors who are subject to reporting, hospitals and laboratories.
  • You are involved in research projects of the register with internal and external partners.

As deputy director, you also perform the following tasks

  • You accompany new employees in their training and promote the transfer of knowledge within the team.
  • You actively participate in and work on further training.
  • In cooperation with the Cancer Register Management, you will conduct recruitment and annual talks.

Requirements

  • Completed studies in medicine, public health, epidemiology or life sciences
  • Multi-year experience in tumor coding and in-depth knowledge of medical terminology
  • Understanding epidemiological relationships and cancer registration
  • Safe handling of common IT applications (especially Microsoft Office) and experience with databases
  • Structured, careful and independent working methods

We offer: Look forward to a collegial, multidisciplinary team with complementary skills, an exciting universitary environment and attractive working conditions with flexible working models and training opportunities.
