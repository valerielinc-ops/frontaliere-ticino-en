Full description

The Swiss Bird Observatory is a non-profit foundation for bird science and bird protection that combines basic research, applied research and practical implementation under one roof. More than 170 permanent employees and 60 temporary workers are involved in a wide range of tasks for the benefit of birds.

In the regional office Valais, species protection plays a central role. To strengthen our team, we are looking from the 15th. February 2027 or by arrangement until 13. August 2027 one/n

Intern or Zivi in the AFP Mehlschwalbe, Mauersegler and dwarf ear owl, 100%

Their tasks

  • Field and office work in connection with the capture and protection of swallows and sailors in Valais.
  • Preparation of action dossiers for these species for submission to the affected communities.
  • Monitoring and management of the nesting box stock of dwarf ear owls.
  • Field work for the inventory of the dwarf ear owls in Valais.

Your profile

  • They like to be outside, physically resilient and enjoy field work.
  • They are flexible and weatherproof, their working hours depend on bird activity and weather.
  • They work independently, practically and reliably.
  • They want to actively work for the protection of endangered bird species in Switzerland.
  • They are well acquainted with birds and can observe and assess their behavior attentively.
  • You have the driving licence Cat. B.
  • You speak German and French.

We offer

An interesting field of work awaits you in a versatile environment. A company vehicle is provided. Good training opportunities and exemplary social benefits round off our offer. Place of work Sitten Valais
