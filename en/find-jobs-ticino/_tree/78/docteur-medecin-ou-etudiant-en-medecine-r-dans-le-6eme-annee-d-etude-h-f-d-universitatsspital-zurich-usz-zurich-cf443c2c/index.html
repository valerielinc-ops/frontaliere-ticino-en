The Institute of Family Medicine (IHAMZ) of the University Hospital Zurich (USZ) and the University of Zurich (UZH) is looking for a doctor or a doctor in the 6th year of study for the quality check of automated codings.

Their tasks

As part of a current research project, we are using large language models for ICD-10 coding of free text diagnoses from electronic medical histories. Your task is to check whether the assigned codes match the respective diagnoses. Your ratings serve as a reference to evaluate the accuracy of the automatic coding and optimize it in a targeted manner. The verification is carried out via a specially developed, intuitive user interface.

Your profile

  • Advanced or completed medical studies (6th year of study up to approximately 2 years of professional experience)
  • Very good knowledge of German (level C2)
  • Accurate, reliable and independent working method
  • Previous experience in internal medicine or family medicine is advantageous

We offer

  • A clearly defined task with direct reference to clinical practice
  • Insight into data-driven healthcare research and the use of AI in medicine
  • Employment in the hourly wage, classification according to cantonal guidelines on the HR of the University of Zurich
  • Central place of work: Institute for Family Medicine, Sonneggstrasse 6, 8006 Zurich (work is done on site for data protection reasons)
