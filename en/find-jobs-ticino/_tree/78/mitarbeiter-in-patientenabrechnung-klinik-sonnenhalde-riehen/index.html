Employee:in patient billing, 80-100 %

Location: Riehen

From 01.10.2026 or by agreement

Founded in 1900, the Klinik Sonnenhalde in Riehen is now an established private clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland and southern Baden. We pursue a holistic, human-centered, milieutherapeutic psychiatry that combines excellent professional quality with a Christian value base. The clinic has 68 stationary beds, 4 day clinics and 4 ambulatories. As one of the first clinics in Switzerland, we are actively promoting digital therapy offerings, such as blended therapy and virtual reality and opened the first hybrid day clinic in Switzerland in February 2023.

Do you have a flair for numbers and administrative processes? Then support our team in a reliable and efficient handling of our financial processes.

Your responsibilities

  • Invoicing & data maintenance: They record medical services, medicines and additional offers and ensure that everything is billed correctly
  • Financial processes: You support the booking of invoices, cash management and monthly accounts
  • Questions & Statistics: You process inquiries from insurers and patients and create evaluations
  • Administration & controls: You maintain relevant master data, carry out regular controls and support administrative tasks in the financial sector

Your profile

  • Education & experience: You have completed commercial vocational training as well as experience in health care
  • Focus on people: Dealing with patients, relatives and colleagues gives you pleasure – discretion and willingness to provide services are a matter of course for you.
  • Structure & initiative: You work in an organized, self-responsible and solution-oriented way – and keep an overview even in hectic phases.
  • Team player: communication strength and team spirit distinguish you – They support where it is necessary and are actively involved.
