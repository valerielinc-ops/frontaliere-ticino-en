The Position As a working group “Near Patient Care Biostatistics and Mathematics”, we are part of Roche’s research and development department. Among other things, this business area develops devices for blood, plasma and urine tests in laboratories, clinics and medical practices - from handhelds to large solutions for laboratories. Our team offers support for a wide range of mathematical/statistical questions in this business field. In addition, we work interdisciplinary on development projects and are characterized by our openness and mutual support. As part of an early development project, you have the opportunity to make a valuable contribution by deriving a predictive model. In addition, you gain insights into working with Real World databases, can use your skills in practice based on many challenges and get enough space for creative approaches.

Your tasks

  • You will work your way into the structure of a Real World database and develop an understanding of the underlying medical processes within the US healthcare system
  • You extract, examine and extract relevant information from the database above and prepare it for the efficient training of machine learning algorithms
  • You develop different partial and overall prediction models based on given biomarkers
  • You determine the predictive accuracy of your models based on suitable, previously defined, quality measures
  • You discuss your approaches in our team and present your results to internal stakeholders

Your profile

  • enrolled in a master's degree in mathematics, physics, computer science, engineering or similar degree programmes; as well as first practical experience in machine learning processes and artificial intelligence
  • Good programming knowledge in Python and its relevant libraries such as Pandas, Scipy, Pytorch, Tensorflow, etc. as well as basic knowledge in SQL
  • You will bring knowledge in the development of machine learning algorithms for the classification and regression of multifactorial problems, as well as basics in statistics
  • Ideally, you have prior knowledge of dealing with real world databases from a healthcare system and/or for modeling time-unevenly distributed data points.
  • You are communicative, enjoy working in interdisciplinary teams and show your own initiative
