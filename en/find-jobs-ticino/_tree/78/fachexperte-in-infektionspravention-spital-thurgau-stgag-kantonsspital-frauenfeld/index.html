For the Clinic of Infectiology & Infection Prevention, we are looking to complement our infection prevention team from 1. March 2027 or by agreement one or more

Expert in infection prevention

50-60%

You take over

  • Advising all areas on infection prevention issues
  • Creating and revising infection prevention policies
  • Development, implementation and evaluation of concepts and prevention strategies
  • Collection and analysis of data in the context of local and national surveillance projects
  • Conduct of training, workshops and audits
  • Outbreak investigation and crisis management assistance

You bring with you

  • Ideally, a completed training as a specialist for infection prevention (HFP), nurses with an interest in training will gladly be considered
  • Communicative, motivated and team-oriented personality

  • Very good computer user knowledge
  • Ability to understand English literature

The Cantonal Hospital Münsterlingen introduces itself

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
