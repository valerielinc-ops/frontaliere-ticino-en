Full description

Entero Klinik with the locations Niederlenz, Neuenhof and Egliswil is a recognized specialist clinic for the treatment of patients with dependency diseases. In Neuenhof we carry out qualified withdrawal treatment under medical supervision. With 13 beds, we are a manageable facility with a family character. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a responsible and resilient Specialist for the Permanent Night Watch (100%)

Place of work: Neuhof removal

Your tasks

  • Ensuring the night care of patients in the psychiatric setting
  • Monitoring the course of therapy
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Documentation of nocturnal incidents and handover to the early service team
  • Support for administrative tasks (night protocol, medication delivery, etc.)

Requirement profile

  • Completed training as a certified nurse/certified nurse HF/FH
  • Resilience, reliability and an appreciative attitude
  • Self-employment and ability to prioritize in calm phases
  • Willingness to work exclusively on night duty
