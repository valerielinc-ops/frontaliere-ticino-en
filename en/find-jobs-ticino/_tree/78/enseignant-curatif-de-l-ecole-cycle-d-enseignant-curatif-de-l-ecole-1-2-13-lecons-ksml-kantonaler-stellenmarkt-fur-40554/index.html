Tasks

We are looking for a teacher for curative pedagogy who, among other things, accompanies a 6th grade learner (bVSAint.). In cooperation with the teachers, you support and accompany the learners and the class. They draw up the promotion and education plan and are involved in discussions with parents and specialists. They advise the teachers, work with them and take responsibility for teaching together. As part of your employment, you are committed to the good of the school and to comprehensive school events and projects.

Requirements

You have a teaching qualification as a teacher or in school curative education, are in training or are ready to start training. Readiness for close cooperation and inclusive teaching. They like to work in a team, are a resilient and reliable personality. You see the diversity of learners as an opportunity.
