Full description

The company manufactures electrical distributions and undertakes independent project work. You will professionally connect PLC components and perform functional tests. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Construction of electrical distributions according to scheme and disposition
  • Independent processing of projects or sub-projects
  • Connect PLC components professionally

  • Perform functional checks and implement corrections
