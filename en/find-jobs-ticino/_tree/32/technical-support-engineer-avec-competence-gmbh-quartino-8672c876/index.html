Full description

You provide technical support and diagnostics for uninterruptible power supply and power conditioning systems, working closely with customers and internal teams.

Tasks

  • Provide hardware support for UPS and power conditioning products via ticketing system, phone, or email
  • Support partners and subsidiaries through a 24/7 diagnostic centre rotation
  • Perform complex system analysis and document findings for stakeholders
  • Travel occasionally to support complex installations such as large data centers
  • Audit service operations and develop competencies of local teams
  • Provide technical training on UPS and power conditioning products
  • Interact with quality and R&D departments to address product issues

Profile

  • Bachelor's degree, diploma, or higher qualification in electrical or electronic engineering
  • 2–5 years of hands-on experience in commissioning and after-sales technical support beneficial
  • Experience troubleshooting mission-critical applications such as data centers is a plus
  • English communication proficiency at B2 level or higher required
  • Knowledge of Italian or Spanish considered a strong asset
  • Ability to write clear technical documents, reports, and field support procedures
  • Good working knowledge of MS Office

Offer

  • Hybrid work model
  • Up to 15% travel commitment
  • 3–6 months interim, then a permanent position

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
