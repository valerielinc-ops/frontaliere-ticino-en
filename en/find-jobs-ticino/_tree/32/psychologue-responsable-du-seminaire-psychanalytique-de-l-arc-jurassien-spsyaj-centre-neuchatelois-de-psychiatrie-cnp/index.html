Full description

The CNP is seeking a psychologist for the SPsyAJ, a seminar of excellence.

Tasks

  • Pilot the seminar by guaranteeing the quality of the teachings.
  • Ensure financial sustainability by pooling resources.
  • Promote the sector to professionals and institutions.

Competences

  • Federal post-graduate diploma holder in psychoanalytic psychotherapy.
  • Clinical experience of at least 5 years as a psychologist-psychotherapy.
  • Organizational skills and partnership development.

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The Neuchâtel Centre for Psychiatry is looking for a post-graduate course in psychotherapy for psychoanalytic guidance, a

Psychologist in charge of the Psychoanalytic Seminary of the Jurassian Arc (SPsyAJ) 30%

Linked to the Institute for Training and Research in Mental Health (IFRSM), the SPsyAJ contributes to the post-graduate training of future psychologists with a federal post-graduate degree and to the development of clinical skills in psychotherapy for psychoanalytic guidance. We are looking for a committed personality, wishing to put its clinical, pedagogical and organizational expertise at the service of the development and outreach of this field.

Your missions

In close collaboration with the IFRSM team, you ensure the strategic, pedagogical and organizational leadership of the SPsyAJ, ensuring the quality of its teachings, its sustainability and its development.

As such, you are responsible for

  • Conduct and ensure the organization of the activities of the Psychoanalytic Seminar of the Jurassian Arc (SPsyAJ) in order to guarantee its functioning, under delegation of the Medical Directorate and the functional responsibility of the head of the IFRSM.
  • In close cooperation with the IFRSM's head, ensure the financial viability of the SPsyAJ, in particular through pooling resources with the IFRSM, with a view to rationalising and optimizing resources.
  • Promote the sector to other professionals, institutions and universities.
  • Coordinate and participate in the strategic reflections concerning the post-graduate and continuing training course of the SPsyAJ in connection with the Medical Directorate of the CNP, and the organisation responsible for the field (FSP).
  • Participate in strategic reflections on the operation of the post-graduate training of LWOP carried out by IFRSM
  • Ensure federal accreditation of the industry under the responsibility of the FSP.
  • Update the requirements for psychotherapist training by adapting regulations to the quality standards of the Confederation with the responsible organization, the Medical Directorate and the IFRSM.
  • To ensure continuous improvement of the quality of the industry by respecting the objectives and guidelines of the CNP, in line with the requirements of the FSP.
  • Ensure collaboration with the CNP interprofessional network and the responsible organisation as well as other accredited training courses.

The core competencies required are

  • You hold the federal post-graduate diploma of psychotherapist, with post-graduate training in psychotherapy psychoanalytic guidance.
  • You have at least 5 years of clinical experience as a psychologist/psychotherapist recognized at the federal level.
  • You have a good knowledge of the Swiss system of post-graduate training in psychotherapy, its regulatory requirements and professional bodies, including the FSP and OFSP.
  • You show a strong interest in the transmission of knowledge, teaching and accompaniment of professionals in training.
  • You have strong skills in organizing, coordinating projects and developing partnerships.
  • With a strategic vision and unifying leadership, you can mobilize different interlocutors around common objectives.
  • Your autonomy, rigor, sense of collaboration and excellent communication capabilities allow you to evolve smoothly in an institutional and academic environment.
