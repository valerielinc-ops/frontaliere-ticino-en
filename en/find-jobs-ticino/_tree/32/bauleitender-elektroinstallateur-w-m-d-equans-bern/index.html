Full description

In order to strengthen our electrical team at the Berne site, we are looking for a committed personality as a construction manager electrical installer (m/f/d).

You can move that with us.

In this exciting and varied function, you take on responsibility for projects in the field of electrical installations – from small conversions to complex new installations.

  • Independent management and coordination of electrical construction projects
  • Leadership, operational planning and support of the assembly team
  • Ensuring quality, deadlines and costs on site
  • Material management and close cooperation with suppliers
  • Contact for customers, planners and internal project management
  • Control of safety regulations (SUVA, internal standards)
  • Documentation and reporting

That's what makes you.

You are a tech-savvy personality who likes to take responsibility and keep track.

  • Completed training as electrical installer EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience in construction management
  • Organizational talent, assertiveness and quality awareness
  • Joy in dealing with customers and team members
  • Driving licence Cat. B

That's what we offer you.

At EQUANS Switzerland you benefit from attractive services, modern working conditions and development opportunities.

  • Participation in training (e.g. team leader, security consultant/project manager)
  • Attractive employment conditions, fair remuneration and expense regulations
  • Modern company vehicle (private use possible)
  • Digital tools (i-pad, mobile, etc.)
  • Exciting and challenging projects
  • Employee benefits and discounts
  • Secure and stable employer – part of the globally active EQUANS Group
  • Lived appreciation & team culture: We promote personal responsibility and cohesion
