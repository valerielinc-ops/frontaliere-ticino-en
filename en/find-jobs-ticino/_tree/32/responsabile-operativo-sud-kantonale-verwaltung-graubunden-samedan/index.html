Full description

The Road Construction Department is responsible for the planning, construction, maintenance, and operation of cantonal roads, water management, and slow traffic in Graubünden. Additionally, we are in charge of the operational maintenance of national roads within the canton. The canton's road infrastructure includes over 1,450 works of art, such as bridges with heights up to 90 meters and tunnels up to 3 kilometers in length. The challenges that the nature of our mountainous canton presents in summer and winter make our work demanding and diverse. As a Friendly Work Space certified office, we particularly care about the health and satisfaction of our approximately 480 employees. Therefore, we are committed to a systematic Occupational Health Management (OHM). We are looking for an Operations Manager South for the Road Maintenance Department in District 3 Samedan, starting on 1st July 2026 or upon agreement | Tasks: Technical and personnel leadership of the operational maintenance team South Planning and implementation of the operational maintenance of cantonal roads Scheduling of the three maintenance groups with a total of approximately 20 employees, vehicles, and equipment, in collaboration with your three directly subordinate foremen Supporting the Construction Department with construction supervision tasks for the maintenance of cantonal roads Participating in the patrol service as a deployment manager and being available for the avalanche service | Requirements: Degree as a civil engineer or construction manager Several years of experience in road construction or road maintenance Leadership experience and entrepreneurial thinking Assertiveness and initiative High self-reliance and sense of responsibility Good IT user skills Stylish written expression in German and Italian, knowledge of Romansh is an advantage
