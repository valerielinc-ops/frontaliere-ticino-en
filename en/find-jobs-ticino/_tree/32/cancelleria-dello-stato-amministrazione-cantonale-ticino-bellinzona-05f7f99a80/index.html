This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • manage the withdrawal, sorting and delivery of the correspondence for administrative units
  • ensure efficient management of outgoing correspondence flows from administrative units, ensuring withdrawal, sorting and verification activities, as well as prompt delivery to competent postal services
  • replace the staff of the absent messenger service in the different locations, in accordance with the plan of assignment of shifts
  • collaborate in administrative and postal activities

Requirements

  • Federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as an employee in logistics or certificate of specific and internal training issued by the Post (until 1997) resulting in a federal certificate of capacity obtained at the end of an internship of at least three years
  • multiannual experience in the postal sector
  • basic knowledge of digital innovation dynamics
  • good knowledge of the Office package and knowledge of the computer program Login Post
  • good knowledge (conversation and editing) of the Italian language
  • good communication skills, relationships, active listening and interpersonal relationships management, with particular attention to user orientation
  • analysis and synthesis, taking responsibility and decision-making autonomy in carrying out the activities assigned
  • aptitude for versatile work, both in autonomy and in team, an assertive personality, open to change, oriented to the resolution of problems and to the achievement of the objectives
  • license to conduct category B
  • availability of continuing training

Special remarks

  • entry into service: to be defined
  • service center Bellinzona
  • shiftwork: Monday to Friday from 05.45 to 18.00

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required additional documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

30 November 2026
