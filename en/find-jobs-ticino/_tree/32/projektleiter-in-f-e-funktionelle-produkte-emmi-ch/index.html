Full description

Do pleasure and health go together for you? For us too. At Emmi, you have the chance to break new ground with your scientific expertise and develop products with real added value. You can expect a versatile environment in which your ideas are in demand and you can actively shape the future of functional nutrition.

You can do that.

  • You develop innovative products, recipes and processes that combine pleasure with targeted nutrition and health benefits.
  • You contribute your scientific expertise to laboratory and pilot tests and turn your findings into practical solutions for our functional products.
  • You will discover and evaluate new raw materials and technologies around functional nutrition, high protein and nutraceuticals.
  • You will follow scientific, technological and regulatory developments, build a strong network and thus strengthen Emmi’s competence in the field of health-oriented products.

You bring that with you

  • You have a university degree in food sciences or a comparable field as well as a further education in human nutrition, biochemistry or related disciplines.
  • You bring several years of experience in product development.
  • Functional ingredients, nutraceuticals and nutritional relationships are your area of expertise.
  • A scientific, structured and independent working method distinguishes you.
  • You convince with your communicative and networked nature and enjoy working with different specialist areas as well as international partners.
  • You communicate safely in German and English.

This is what we offer you

  • 6 weeks vacation: Enjoy six weeks vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to seven weeks.
  • Sabbatical & Holiday Shopping: Give yourself unpaid holidays or extend your holiday entitlement by up to two additional weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Further education and development opportunities: At Emmi, the focus is on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
