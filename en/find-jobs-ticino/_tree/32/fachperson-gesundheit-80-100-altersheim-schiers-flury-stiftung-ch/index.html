Full description

COMPETENT AND WARM With more than 600 employees in the Schiers Hospital, the three retirement homes in Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, the Spitex, Wohnen mit Service, the daycare center, the parent advice center and the Klosters Medical Center, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in the Prättigau between Grüsch and Klosters. The Schiers retirement home, embedded in a beautiful mountain landscape, offers around 50 residents a loving home. We are looking for a health specialist 80 - 100% as of December 1, 2026 or by appointment

Your responsibilities include

  • Needs-oriented and individual support and care
  • Carrying out care in accordance with the care process and corresponding documentation
  • Taking on day-to-day responsibility within the team
  • Co-design and further development of the area of ​​care and nursing

Your profile

  • Completed training as a health specialist EFZ
  • Joy and patience in dealing with older people, ability to work in a team and reliability
  • High professional and social skills as well as a reliable and independent way of working
  • Interest in new challenges and a well-structured work organization

We offer you

  • A varied and challenging job in an innovative company
  • Working independently with a competent and motivated team
  • A family working environment
  • Attractive, contemporary employment conditions and generous social benefits
  • Our own crèche and other interesting benefits for employees

If you have any initial questions, please contact Ms. Lea Hohbach, Head of Division, Tel. +41 81 308 06 10. Further information on the Flury Foundation can be found on the Internet: flurystiftung.ch please send your application by e-mail to hr@flurystiftung.
