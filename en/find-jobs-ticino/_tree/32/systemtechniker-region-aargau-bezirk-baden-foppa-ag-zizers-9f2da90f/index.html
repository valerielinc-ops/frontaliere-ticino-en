System technician Region Aargau / District of Baden (m/f)

More than a workplace.

Wherever and fire can arise, we have come into play since 1958. Whether in the planning and implementation of \- and heat extraction systems, in preventive fire protection or in the equipment of fire departments. Our employees work every day with expertise, commitment and team spirit for the safety of our customers.

Behind this are people with different abilities and a common aspiration: taking responsibility, finding solutions and making a difference together.

Become part of our team and shape the safety of tomorrow with us.

For the Region Aargau / District of Baden we are looking for a technically experienced personality who can reliably look after our facilities and carry out service missions independently.

Your tasks.

  • After training, independent performance of service and maintenance work on our plants
  • Planning and organization of your service missions incl. Appointment and coordination with our customers
  • Assembly of drives, buttons and fans as well as troubleshooting, troubleshooting and minor commissioning of our plants
  • Gradual takeover of more demanding service orders up to independent commissioning and programming of larger plants

Classic installation work, such as cable pull-in and extensive wiring, is largely carried out by our partners. This allows you to focus on technically demanding and varied tasks.

Your qualifications.

  • Completed training as an electrical installer, electronics technician or equivalent with at least three years of professional experience
  • Self-employed, reliable and well-organized working methods
  • Technical interest, pleasure in direct customer contact and motivation to learn new things
  • Category B driving licence and good knowledge of German in words and writing

Your Foppa Plus.

  • Healthy, attentive and appreciative working environment with attractive working conditions and strong social benefits
  • Comprehensive training and individual support
  • Clear development possibilities from service work to commissioning and programming of complex plants
  • Working independently with responsibility
  • Modern company vehicle that can also be used privately

Benefit from our unique Foppafit\ program and discover about our activities, values and benefits.
