Full description

Are you enthusiastic about Windows Client Engineering and would you like to help design a modern workplace? Do you want to not only operate solutions, but really understand, automate and continuously improve them? With us, you bring together Windows, hardware, drivers, deployment, intune and automation into a stable and modern workplace. With you, we develop the Windows Workplace of tomorrow.

You can do that.

  • You’ll engineer and operate our Windows client platforms across the entire lifecycle, from Windows releases, feature updates and patching to productive deployment.
  • In addition, you automate technical processes with PowerShell and VB scripts and develop tools and processes further, where you can contribute C# for frontend adjustments.
  • You integrate and test Windows drivers and firmware and ensure their reliable deployment.
  • In addition, OS Deployment and Modern Management are helping to shape solutions with Microsoft Intune and existing software distribution and deployment mechanisms.
  • Examine new requirements for feasibility, complexity and impact on existing solutions, standards and policies and develop viable alternatives.
  • In addition, you will evaluate new Microsoft technologies, build prototypes, test under realistic conditions and decide what will bring the Windows Workplace further technically.
  • You will analyze complex faults to the cause, develop sustainable solutions together with the teams involved and keep technical documentation, system overviews and operational concepts up-to-date.

You bring that with you

  • You know Windows Client Engineering from practice and feel as comfortable with operating systems as you do with hardware, drivers and deployment.
  • In addition, PowerShell or VB script are part of your toolbox, where you think of reusability, robustness and automation in scripts and bring C# as a plus.
  • Understand the interplay between hardware, firmware and drivers and their impact on stability, compatibility and user experience, and learn about OS deployment, Windows releases, feature updates and patching.
  • In addition, you have experience with Microsoft Intune in the Windows Workplace or Modern Management and ideally bring knowledge of Windows 365.
  • You like to get to the bottom of technical problems, recognize dependencies and make comprehensible technical decisions. You combine engineering with operations and think automation, standardization, testability, security, performance and stable operation together.
  • In addition, you enjoy working with other engineers and disciplines, taking on responsibility and ideally know Scrum or SAFe to jointly advance solutions in an agile environment.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German and English. Any other national language is beneficial.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
