Full description

Mission

As part of this function, attached to our Watchmaking Division, you will perform assembly as well as finishing, starting and adjusting extra-flat movements. You will also make the installation of complications, such as universal time, the large instant date as well as great complications, including the conventional perpetual calendar and skeleton. These operations will be carried out in traditional assembly in accordance with the assembly, lubrication and control processes, while ensuring an optimal level of quality.

Responsibilities

  • Properly assemble the movements by following the production processes as well as the installation and control instructions put in place.
  • Manually and accurately perform lubrication operations.
  • Perform adjustment and completion in a traditional way.
  • Perform assembly and adjustment of complications and great complications.
  • Provide aesthetic and technical control of each assembly part of the movement in order to deliver a product that meets the specifications and criteria of Patek Philippe.

Profile sought

  • CFC Watchmaker or equivalent diploma recognized by SEFRI.
  • Work experience of at least 5 years in a similar job desired.
  • Knowledge of how the perpetual calendar works.
  • Mastery of lubrication, oiling and dandruff, as well as finishing, starting and adjusting.
  • Rigor, precision, concentration capacity.
  • Excellent dexterity.
  • Versatility.
  • Team spirit and sense of cooperation.
