Full description

As a Project Engineer, you will contribute to the design and management of RVCS plant projects, developing innovative, efficient and sustainable solutions. If you like to work in teams and face complex technical challenges, find out more. You can choose to work at one of our offices in Giubiasco or Rotkreuz.

Shipments

  • Management and coordination of RVCS plant projects in infrastructure, construction and industry.
  • Development of conceptual projects, technical feasibility, implementation of energyly sustainable solutions.
  • Design, technical calculations and documentation in phases SIA 3–5, from concept to executive planning.
  • Dimension of complex ventilation and cooling systems in infrastructure and industrial sectors.
  • Collaboration with project team and multidisciplinary specialists.

Requirements

  • Degree in RVCS Engineering, Mechanical Engineering or equivalent title with energy address, fluid and processes.
  • Experience in the design of RVCS or thermotechnical systems in infrastructure or industrial.
  • Knowledge of SIA and Office tools; knowledge of BIM (Revit) design is an advantage.
  • Good organizational skills, communication and teamwork attitude.
  • Excellent knowledge of Italian and fluent knowledge of German (min. B2); English and/or French represent an advantage.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
