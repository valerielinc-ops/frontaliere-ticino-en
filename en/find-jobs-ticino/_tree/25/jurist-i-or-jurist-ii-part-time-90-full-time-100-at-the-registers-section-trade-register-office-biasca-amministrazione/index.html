Full description

Tasks

  • co-ordinates and supervises the procedures for the registration of the business register, ensuring proper and uniform management, and autonomously treats the procedures of its own competence, taking care of education, legal examination and procedural follow-up
  • examines and processes the registration practices of particular complexity and related legal and procedural issues
  • performs preventive examinations of corporate acts and operations and provides legal advice to users, professionals and authorities in matters covered by the trade register
  • form and support officials in the examination of practices and counselling, contributing to the uniform application of legal provisions, practices and internal directives
  • cooperates with the Head of Office in organizational matters, operational priorities and in the handling of particular dossiers
  • replaces the Head of Office in case of absence, ensuring continuity in the management and management of the Office and assuming the tasks and responsibilities connected with the alternate
  • performs mandates, legal backgrounds, projects and other particular tasks assigned by the Head of Office as part of the function

Requirements

  • complete academic training (bachelor + master or license) in Swiss law
  • good knowledge of the trade register and corporate law
  • excellent linguistic knowledge of Italian (conversation and editing) and good knowledge (speaking and written) of German and French
  • spirit of initiative, autonomy, sense of responsibility, discretion, reliability and precision
  • flexibility, availability, capacity for collaboration and coordination and management of employees
  • no executive procedure in justified course and absence of certificates of lack of assets
  • no registration in the judicial box
  • Swiss citizenship
  • are preferential title:
  • patent lawyer
  • Notary patent
  • professional experience in the field of notary

Special remarks

  • the function of jurist I (class 10) can be attributed only to candidates who have complete academic qualifications: university license, bachelor's degree, Bachelor's degree + Master's degree or professional university (Bachelor + Master 270/300 ECTS credits) and who have gained at least 15 years of professional experience in the required field
  • the jurist function II (class 9) will be attributed to candidates with less than 15 years of professional experience in the required field and possess complete academic qualifications: university license, degree, consecutive master's degree or professional university school (Bachelor + Master 270/300 ECTS credits)

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required additional documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Terms of use

  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
