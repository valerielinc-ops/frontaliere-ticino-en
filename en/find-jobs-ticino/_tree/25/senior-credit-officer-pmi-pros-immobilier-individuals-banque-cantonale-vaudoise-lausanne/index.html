Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Put your managerial ambitions at the service of sound and sustainable performance by joining the canton of Vaud's first universal bank, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Credit Management Division , we are looking for one or one :

Senior credit officer

  • SMEs
  • Pros Immobilier
  • Individuals Your main missions:
  • Ensure autonomous analysis and decision-making flows on credit exposure of "standard" and "complex" files,
  • Deciding on the granting/refusal of credit within the framework of individual powers conferred or giving notice to decision-making bodies,
  • Ensure compliance with and compliance with existing rules and directives and the Credit Policy,
  • Support and advise front managers through the provision of technical expertise, preliminary analysis and occasional customer visit support;
  • Give notice to decision-making bodies, in the context of credit decisions, where appropriate, of the preparation of credit acts,
  • Take part in risk monitoring, at the Bank portfolio level, on the basis of input from within the department, proposing the actions necessary for each situation,
  • Take over the establishment of credit instruments,
  • Actively participate in internal projects related to the development of rules, tools and procedures,
  • Actively contribute to the process of training Associate Credit Officer (ACO), trainees, apprentices and anyone involved in the credit process. Provide continuous training. Your profile:
  • banking, commercial training, supplemented by higher vocational training (HES or diploma in banking economy),
  • experience from 5 to 10 years as credit analyst,
  • good knowledge of the risks associated with the financing of performance buildings,
  • rigor, analytical and negotiating capabilities,
  • team spirit, communication capabilities, focus and focus solution,
  • mastery of common IT tools, especially Excel and Access,
  • excellent mastery of French, knowledge of German and/or English a plus.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write three stories together: your career story, your bank story, our region story.
