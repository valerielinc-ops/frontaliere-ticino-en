Full description

Do you like to lead with foresight and keep an overview even with complex IT infrastructure? ####

At a public institution in the field of social insurance, you take over the leadership of a committed ICT team and actively shape the digital future - with responsibility, room for manoeuvre and a stable environment behind you.

The main tasks ####

  • Professional and human leadership of the team
  • Ensuring the operation of the IT infrastructure
  • Further development of client, server and network infrastructure
  • Coordination and management of external service providers as well as management and cooperation in projects
  • Budgeting, investment planning and cost control

The requirement profile ####

  • Completed computer science training with a higher degree in business informatics (HF/FH)
  • Several years of management and professional experience in ICT operations
  • In-depth knowledge of Windows client and server infrastructure
  • Strong organisational capacity and solution-oriented working methods
  • High service orientation as well as communication skills and assertiveness
