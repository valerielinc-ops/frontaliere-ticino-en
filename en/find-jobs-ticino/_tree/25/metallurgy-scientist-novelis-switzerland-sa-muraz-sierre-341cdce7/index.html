Novelis is driven by the desire to shape together a sustainable world.

As a world leader in innovative products and services and the world's largest aluminium recycler, we work in partnership with customers in the automotive, aeronautics, cans and specialty sectors to provide solutions that enhance the benefits of light aluminum.

We have a coordinated network of technically sophisticated rolling and recycling operations at 29 sites in 9 countries in North America, South America, Europe and Asia, with 12,750 employees worldwide. The county seat is Atlanta, USA.

Join our mission to create a sustainable world and start working on our Sierre site in Switzerland as soon as possible.

Metallurgy Scientist (m/f/d)

Our research and technology centre in Sierre, in the canton of Valais (Switzerland), is looking for a metallurgical specialist with expertise in the development of automotive alloys (series 5xxx and 6xxx) and in basic research. You will participate in the development of future generations of automotive aluminium products and the continuous improvement of manufacturing processes within the global network of Novelis plants.

Your responsibilities

  • Lead strategic projects to develop new laminated aluminium products for the automotive sector, focusing on mechanical resistance, crash performance, formability and durability of alloys.
  • Work closely with process, development and trade teams to identify high value-added products that meet market needs.
  • Establish collaborations with international universities and consortia to accelerate innovation and the development of sustainable solutions.
  • Provide technical expertise on the entire aluminium production chain, including hot rolling, cold rolling and finishing, to support production activities.

How to convince us:

  • Master's or Ph.D. in materials science, metallurgy or related fields; relevant professional experience is an asset.

  • A dynamic, flexible and innovative team spirit capable of learning quickly and easily evolving in multicultural environments.

  • Mastering French or German is an asset.
