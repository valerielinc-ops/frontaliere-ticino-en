Finance IT Expert – EPD Finance Transformation Team EPD Finance Transformation Team | Abbott At Abbott, our purpose is simple yet powerful: to help people live healthier, fuller lives. With more than 114,000 colleagues in over 160 countries, we work together with curiosity, compassion, and a shared commitment to meaningful impact. Our Established Pharmaceuticals Division (EPD) delivers trusted medicines to communities around the world, with a strong focus on fast‑growing emerging markets. About the Role We are looking for a Finance IT Expert to join our EPD Finance Transformation Team. This position sits at the intersection of finance and technology, offering the opportunity to design solutions that strengthen data quality, streamline processes, and empower teams globally. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys solving problems collaboratively, working with modern technologies, and contributing to digital transformation in a supportive, inclusive environment. What You’ll Work On Technology Enablement for Finance

  • Design, enhance, and support finance‑related IT systems, with a strong focus on SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC).
  • Contribute to functional and technical design while resolving defects and implementing improvements. SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) – Integration & Enablement
  • Data Modeling: Build and maintain planning data models and logics (e.g. SAC data actions) ensuring accuracy, consistency, and scalability.
  • Front‑End (UI/UX): Develop intuitive input templates, dashboards, reports, and visualizations that prioritize usability and process integration.
  • Translate business needs into clear technical specifications.
  • Ensure smooth integration between SAC and core systems (SAP ERP, HFM, AWS, VENA).
  • Monitor performance and proactively implement enhancements for user experience and data quality. Process Automation & Digitalization
  • Partner with finance teams to identify opportunities for automation (e.g., AI/ML) and implement solutions that increase efficiency and reliability.
  • Apply optimization techniques to improve system speed and responsiveness.
  • Stay up to date with SAP’s continuous releases and incorporate new features where beneficial. Data Strategy & Governance
  • Build and maintain strong data models supporting consistent, compliant financial reporting and analytics. Project Leadership
  • Act as a technical lead on cross‑functional transformation initiatives, ensuring alignment between business goals and system capabilities. Stakeholder Collaboration
