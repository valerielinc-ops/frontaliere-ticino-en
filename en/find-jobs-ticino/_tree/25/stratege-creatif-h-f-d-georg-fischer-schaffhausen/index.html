Creative Strategist (m/f/d)

Your tasks

  • Develop creative strategies, concepts, storylines, and campaigns that align with business objectives and strengthen our global brand
  • Shape, maintain, and further evolve our corporate design and brand identity, ensuring consistency across all channels, touchpoints, and markets worldwide
  • Translate market trends, audience insights, and business needs into engaging brand experiences and impactful creative solutions
  • Support and coordinate global marketing, communication, and employer branding campaigns in collaboration with internal stakeholders and external agencies
  • Act as the creative link between business teams and agency partners, providing guidance, feedback, and ensuring high-quality deliverables
  • Create and oversee visual assets for digital channels, social media, events, presentations, and marketing communications while contributing hands-on design expertise

Your profile

  • Bachelor's degree or a vocational apprenticeship with relevant further training in Graphic Design, Communication, Marketing, or a related field
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in graphic design, branding, creative strategy, marketing, communications, or a comparable role, ideally in an international B2B environment
  • Strong portfolio demonstrating expertise in graphic and corporate design, storytelling, campaign development, and creative thinking
  • Proven ability to connect strategic, big-picture thinking with hands-on creative execution and brand management
  • Experience collaborating with multiple stakeholders, international teams, and external creative agencies, combined with strong project management skills
  • Excellent English communication skills and a proactive, solution-oriented mindset; additional languages are an advantage
  • Please include work samples or a portfolio with your application
