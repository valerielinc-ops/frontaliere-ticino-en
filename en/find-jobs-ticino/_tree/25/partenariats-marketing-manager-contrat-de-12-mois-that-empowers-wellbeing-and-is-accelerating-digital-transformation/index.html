An exciting opportunity has opened to join us as a Partnerships Marketing Manager on a 12‑month contract.

This role will see you own strategic partnership marketing end to end, acting as the day‑to‑day marketing lead for GI Partners. You’ll drive growth, retention and brand outcomes through impactful partner campaigns, managing everything from concept and communications through to delivery and collateral. Using data, insights and ROI tracking, you’ll continuously refine activity, conduct post‑implementation reviews and uncover creative ways to deepen partner engagement. You’ll work closely with stakeholders and Marketing Chapter peers to identify new opportunities, ensure a consistent tone of voice, and protect both Zurich and partner brand assets—always aligned to brand guidelines and best‑practice go‑to‑market tools.

  • Proven experience in marketing strategy
  • TTM / campaign development capability
  • Content creation experience (particularly across general insurance)
  • Social media targeting and amplification experience
  • Strength in research, tracking, data analytics and insights
