Full description

Do you want to work in an environment that challenges, encourages and inspires you for more? Do you appreciate the diversity of surgical and medical specialties, like to work in an interdisciplinary manner and would like to develop personally and professionally? Then you've come to the right place.

We offer you

  • You will take on the responsibility of complex nursing tasks, including the option of taking on one of our many specialist responsibilities. No two days will be the same, as you will be caring for different patients from different surgical specialties.
  • In the nursing tandem, you will work together with a trained specialist in health, nursing assistance or together with students and apprentices. You can use your knowledge and skills to closely supervise the trainees.
  • Your ideas, your experience and your expertise are welcome from all sides – you can actively shape the further development of our care.
  • A structured and individual induction and a young, motivated, appreciative and collegial team
  • A culture in which thinking, co-creation and co-decision-making are expressly desired and accepted

What do you bring to the table

  • You meet people with professional competence, empathy and commitment
  • You will take responsibility for the entire nursing process and design patient-centered care together with the interdisciplinary team
  • Mutual support in the team is just as much a matter of course for you as it is for us
  • You actively contribute your initiative, take responsibility and appreciate collegial cooperation
  • Even in the varied working day, you will keep track of things and face changes with flexibility

Your benefits

  • Holidays to 49. Age: 25 working days 50. 59. Age year: 30 working days from 60. Age: 35 working days
  • Seniority gift from 5 years in the form of holidays or wages
  • Extensive training and further education opportunities in the form of working hours and/or cost sharing
  • Employee benefits Possibility of a free room upgrade at Lachen Hospital Permanent discounts on products and services from well-known providers Discounted meals in the staff restaurant Massages and fitness courses at our physiotherapy
  • Accident insurance Worldwide accident cover in the private department at no additional cost
  • Parking Variety of parking spaces at very fair prices

Requirements

  • Nursing diploma HF or a diploma as a qualified nurse recognized in Switzerland

  • Professional experience in the acute care sector is an advantage

  • Willingness to actively contribute to development processes

  • Sense of responsibility and ability to work independently

  • Resilient, flexible, and a team player
