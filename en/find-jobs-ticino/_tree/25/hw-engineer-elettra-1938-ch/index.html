Full description

HORIEN Salt Battery Solutions, FZSoNick's new business brand, is the world leader in the production and marketing of sodium nickel-chloride storage systems. It offers safe and completely sustainable energy storage and backup solutions: from raw materials to maximum recyclability of components, our products aim to promote the decarbonization process towards a more sustainable world.

HORIEN is an industrial group with manufacturing plants and commercial branches in Italy, Switzerland, United States, Mexico, Brazil, China, France and Germany. The group is active in the energy and automotive components sectors and is known for its commitment to supporting the transition to new developments and innovations.

Location For our headquarters in Stabio we are looking for an HW ENGINEER that will take care of the following activities:

  • Management of technical documentation (signs, separate base, costs);
  • Search for new components;
  • Management of testing systems;
  • Support for design (digital/analogue interface + CAN communication bus, Modbus etc) ;
  • Verification of product compliance with current regulations (IEC61000, IEC61508, UL1973 and other industry regulations);

  • Support for EMC tests at external laboratories;
  • Repair and search faults on existing products;
  • Graded IPC regulatory knowledge for electronic documentation
  • Basic knowledge of static conversion systems.
