Facility Manager

100%, Lugano, permanent

The Swiss Science Centre (CSCS) develops and operates state-of-the-art infrastructure for high-performance computing (HPC) and data research, providing them with the scientific community and contributing to the development of excellence research in Switzerland.

The CSCS scientific community infrastructure is accessible to national and international researchers from the academic world, industry and the economic sector.

The CSCS is run by the Swiss Federal Polytechnic of Zurich (ETH Zürich) and operates at its headquarters in Lugano, where the main computing centre is located, and Zurich .

For the management of the Cornaredo building, equipped with a complex technical infrastructure, from 01.11.2026 or from the date of convent, we are looking for the following figure:

Job description

  • Ensure the management, availability and security of the technical infrastructure of the building.
  • Plan and coordinate the ordinary and extraordinary maintenance of plants.
  • Manage faults and anomalies, coordinating the necessary interventions to restore plants.
  • Manage and supervise companies, suppliers and external partners.
  • Monitor plants through building supervision and automation systems.
  • Manage and update technical documentation and maintenance planning.
  • Participate in the realization, testing and commissioning of new technical installations.
  • Help optimize reliability, energy efficiency and plant safety.

Profile

Profil or

  • We seek a mature, charismatic and authoritative personality, able to inspire confidence and to exploit its experience to face with competence and serenity the challenges of the role.
  • Higher technical training in the field of planting (SSSti), or an equivalent or higher qualification, preferably with federal certificate of capacity in the electrical branch.
  • Good knowledge of electrical systems and RVCS (heating, ventilation, air conditioning and sanitary).
  • Good knowledge of automation, regulation and supervision of buildings.
  • Experience in the management and maintenance of complex technical infrastructure.
  • Experience in coordinating specialist firms and suppliers.
  • Interest in training and mentoring apprentices in the technical sector
  • Good knowledge of MS-Office applications; knowledge of CAFM and CAD systems are preferential title.
  • Great oral and written knowledge of Italian and English are indispensable; knowledge of German is a preferential title.

We are looking for an autonomous, reliable and problem-oriented person with good analysis, organization and priority management skills. The ability to work in teams and work in an international and multicultural environment is required.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
