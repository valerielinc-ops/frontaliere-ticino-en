Full description

As a nurse at the B2 medical unit, you provide personalized and comprehensive nursing care in a self-reliant and medically delegated manner in a curative, preventive and educational manner. Reactive or reactive, with good management of stress and priorities, you can assess, analyze complex clinical situations and adapt to the context as well as to unexpected and peak activity. You provide technical care and accompany the patient and their families in different settings of care, especially in acute or chronic phase in somatic or psychic fields. You have an interest in the care of paediatric medical pathologies and more specifically diabetics, children with autistic spectrum disorders and d-anorexia. You easily practice clinical nursing assessment and have knowledge of Therapeutic Education for the patient. Your ability to work collaboratively allows you to work hand in hand with the medical-care team. You ensure accurate and rigorous transmission of information within multidisciplinary teams. Of a benevolent and caring nature, you have extensive knowledge in relational care, allowing you to support parenting, care for children, adolescents, and their families, by showing listening, empathy, communication skills and emotional management. Your learning abilities allow you to continuously progress in your practice.
