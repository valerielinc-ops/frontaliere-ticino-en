Full description

Put to the Point

In case of interest and suitability, the selection as a professional subcommissioned officer can be sought and supported. With your commitment, you can actively and together with a committed team make an important contribution to Switzerland’s defence capability. Working with and supporting militia cadres must bring you joy.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21017
  • Location: Barracks Auenfeld, 8500 Frauenfeld

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure service operations: As a time military unit field woman in the Kdo Cyber/ FU Br 41/ FU Ber Kp 104/204 lead and support the operation during transition phases and in the absence of the militia
  • The higher non-commissioned officers of the militia technically train and support them in the targeted development of their competencies in the service operation
  • Coordinate logistics processes and ensure that personnel, materials and processes are optimally coordinated
  • Support the higher non-commissioned officers of the militia in the Commissariat service and contribute to the smooth running of administrative and organizational tasks
  • Take on additional tasks within the professional military team of the FU Ber Kp 104/204 and jointly ensure operational readiness

This makes you unique

  • Higher non-commissioned officer with good qualifications from the militia
  • 3-year vocational apprenticeship with federal certificate of ability, high school or professional maturity
  • High sense of responsibility and resilience
  • Readiness for irregular working hours and assignments also on weekends
  • Civil driving licence Cat B

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom

The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information

Employing the Swiss army makes sense and brings many advantages. See for yourself at www.vtg.admin.ch/en/zeitmilitar. The job is limited to 12 months (with an option for renewal). During employment, temporary military personnel are not exempt from compulsory service with their unit. Offers from recruiters are not taken into account.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
