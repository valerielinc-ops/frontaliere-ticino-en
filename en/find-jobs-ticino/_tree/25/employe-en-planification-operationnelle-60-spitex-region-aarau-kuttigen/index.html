Spitex Region Aarau: Health in the best hands! Working together for a better quality of life The Spitex Region Aarau is one of the largest providers of outpatient care services in the region and provides its services on behalf of the communities. Our dedicated and qualified employees are at the heart of our organization. With their daily commitment, they make a valuable contribution to people’s health, independence and quality of life. We accompany and support our clients in all phases of life – competently, individually and with a lot of commitment. To strengthen our planning team, we are looking for a motivated Employee in operational planning (60%) The workload can be extended on request by additional assignments in direct care. Are you an organizational talent, like to think ahead and keep an overview even in hectic moments? Then they are right with us! Their tasks

  • Planning and coordination of the missions of our three somatic care teams
  • Ensuring efficient and needs-based operational planning
  • Consideration of the needs of our clients and the professional competences of our employees
  • Flexible adjustment for short-term changes
  • Close cooperation with care teams and team leaders

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed training as a specialist in health
  • In-depth knowledge of Perigon software is advantageous
  • Ideally experience in operational planning
  • Organizational talent and independent working methods
  • Communication strength, team spirit and solution orientation
  • Enjoyment of a varied activity at the interface between organization and care

Are you interested? Then we look forward to your complete application documents to bewerbungen@spitexregionaarau.ch! For questions Karin Fehlmann, co-core service provider, on 062 839 11 70, is at your disposal.
