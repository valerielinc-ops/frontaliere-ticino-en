Start on January 1, 2027 as a nurse / nurse specialist Endoscopy 60 - 60 %

Our highly specialized and dynamic team of gastroenterology and hepatology diagnoses and treats diseases of the entire digestive system – from prevention and early detection to follow-up after procedures. We rely on the latest technologies and gentle anesthesia methods.

We are looking for a personality who can help with expertise and empathy and enrich our team in the varied area of intervention and investigation. Sounds like your environment? Perfect, then we look forward to your application!

These are your tasks

  • You are actively involved in endoscopic procedures and ensure a reliable examination and intervention process
  • You look after our patients: empathetically and professionally before, during and after the procedure
  • You are participating in the Pikettdienst to ensure emergency care

You bring that with you

  • A diploma as nurse / nurse specialist HF/FH as well as professional experience in the acute hospital
  • Experience in endoscopy or gastroenterology is beneficial
  • An exact, structured way of working and a quick understanding
  • An open, team-oriented appearance and willingness to take on pikett services
