Full description

The Weisse Arena Group is an integrated service provider in the tourism and leisure industry. We position ourselves as providers of alpine leisure experiences. The group includes a cable car and ski lift company, hotels and restaurants, a sports equipment distribution and rental company, a ski and snowboard school, an administration company, and a tourism destination marketing company for Flims Laax Falera. At Ristorante Camino, under the motto "l'arte della cucina italiana", pasta or risotto dishes, meat and fish dishes, and traditional wood-fired pizza are served. The selection of Italian wines and beverages completes the offering. Hotel guests enjoy their breakfast and dinner here. The venue offers space for around 110 guests in the restaurant area and for around 60 guests in the Camino Bar/Lounge. Additionally, various caterings and banquets are offered from Ristorante Camino. ## Responsibilities

  • Greeting and serving guests
  • Preparation and serving of hot and cold beverages
  • Monitoring and restocking the breakfast buffet
  • Mise en place and setting up for the lunch and dinner à la carte service
  • Lunch service and support at the buffet
  • Assistance with break refreshments during seminars
  • General cleaning tasks ## Requirements
  • Work experience in service from a comparable position
  • As a morning person, you actively approach our guests
  • Enjoyment of working in a lively environment
  • Overview and friendliness even in hectic moments
  • German mother tongue, good English skills, and advantageous Italian skills
  • Experience with computer cash registers ## What We Offer
