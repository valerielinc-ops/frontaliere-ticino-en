Full description

The figure will deal with the Galenic development activities, contributing to the different phases of the formulation development of pharmaceutical products, from the preparation and characterization of formulations to the support of the activities of industrial scale-up and registration.

Tasks

  • It will perform the preparation and characterization of formulations during the development phases;
