CFC Learning - (M/F) Production Clock to be filled for the August 2027 re-entry

GENERAL MISSION

Accurately and rigorously, the apprentice CFC Production Watchmaker performs adjustment operations to ensure the proper functioning of mechanisms and watches. He/she also carries out rigorous quality controls to ensure that watches comply with the House's quality standards and corrects any defects.

MAIN ACTIVITIES

  • Assembly and disassembly of movements
  • Assembly of components
  • Completion and adjustment operations
  • Participation in the production process
  • Participation in the life of the workshop

PROFILE

  • Good visual acuity and high concentration capacity
  • Patience and thoroughness
  • Logical and methodical spirit
  • Manual skill
  • Confirmed aptitudes for mathematics and physics
  • Attracted for watchmaking trades

CANDIDATURE

  • CV

  • Last 3 years of school bulletins
