Full description

MAIN TASKS

In this varied function, you are responsible for the scheduling of your assigned articles and ensure a needs-based material supply.

Your responsibilities include in particular

  • Disposal of the allocated items taking into account delivery readiness, stocks and capacity
  • Operational management and timely provision of raw materials, components and finished products
  • Daily coordination with production and internal interfaces
  • Ensuring optimal inventory and capacity planning
  • Inventory management of components, semi-finished and finished products
  • Maintenance and monitoring of master data in SAP and other systems
  • Cooperation in product changes, optimization projects and in the continuous improvement process (CIP)

PROFILE

You have a basic technical or commercial training and ideally bring a further education in the field of supply chain management (level HF desirable). In addition, the following characteristics and abilities characterize you:

  • 2 to 5 years of professional experience in distribution, production planning or supply chain management
  • Experience in dealing with SAP as well as good MS Office knowledge
  • Negotiation skills and an independent and structured working method
  • High level of initiative, resilience and responsibility
  • Networked thinking, pronounced process understanding and hands-on mentality
  • Very good knowledge of German
