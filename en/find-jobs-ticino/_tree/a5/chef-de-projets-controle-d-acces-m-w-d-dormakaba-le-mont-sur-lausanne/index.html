Job Overview

As part of strengthening our access control service, we are looking for a Project Manager to manage the implementation of our solutions. In this position, you will be responsible for the complete management of projects, from their launch to their finalization, ensuring compliance with deadlines, costs and quality. The Project Manager will be the technical referent of his/her solutions to our clients in French-speaking Switzerland.

What you will do

  • You are responsible for the planning, budget and quality of project execution.
  • You manage projects in accordance with the customer order and specifications, while identifying and implementing improvement opportunities.
  • You ensure the coordination of all project stakeholders: internal teams, clients, external partners and technical resources.
  • You analyze and clarify technical aspects with the various contacts in order to guarantee smooth execution of the project.
  • You ensure budgetary monitoring of projects and ensure cost control throughout their implementation.
  • You keep the technical documentation and associated deliverables up to date.
  • You act as the preferred technical contact with clients throughout the project life cycle.

What we ask

  • You hold a higher degree with a technical orientation, ideally in the fields of electricity, automation or construction.
  • You have at least 3 years of experience in project management in the areas of security systems, access control, building automation, construction or a comparable technical environment.
  • You are proficient in common IT tools and comfortable in a hybrid work environment.
  • You are fluent in French and have a good level of English and/or German.
  • You are customer oriented and know how to understand user needs in order to offer appropriate solutions.
  • You demonstrate autonomy, rigor and excellent organizational skills.
  • You are able to make decisions and effectively manage multiple priorities in a complex and evolving environment.
  • You have excellent communication and coordination skills with various stakeholders.

We offer

  • Attractive employment conditions with competitive advantages.
  • Possibilities for partial teleworking.
  • An innovative and exciting technological environment.
  • The opportunity to join an international group with a family spirit.
  • Support promoting personal and professional development.
  • A collaborative work environment offering autonomy and responsibilities.
