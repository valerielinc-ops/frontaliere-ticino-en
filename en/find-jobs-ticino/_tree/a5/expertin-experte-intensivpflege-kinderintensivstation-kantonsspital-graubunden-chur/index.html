Full description

Start by appointment as an expert / expert intensive care child intensive care unit 40 - 100%

Intensive care with heart and competence – for children in critical situations.

In this role, you will take on the demanding care of premature and newborn children up to older children with serious or life-threatening illnesses and injuries. You ensure their physical and psychological well-being and ensure their safety at all times. Dealing with complex and rapidly changing situations and understanding children and their families in extraordinary moments are part of your everyday life. This activity requires a high degree of flexibility, speedy yet prudent action and a strong sense of responsibility.

Sounds like your new challenge? Perfect – we look forward to meeting you!

These are your tasks

  • You work in an interprofessional environment and are assured of professional care for our patients and their relatives.
  • You master complex nursing situations professionally and react safely in acute situations
  • You take responsibility for the quality of care and ensure safety and well-being
  • You support students in their further education and promote their professional development

You bring that with you

  • You are Dipl. Expert / Expert Intensive Care NDS HF
  • You value independent and responsible work
  • You are a flexible personality and act as a team player:
  • You have a good technical understanding and manual skill

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!

And that's what we offer you.

  • Career and family: As a flexible employer, we promote different working models so that you can optimally combine work and family for yourself. We also offer you and your family a wide range of support services.
  • Holidays / loyalty leave: The annual holiday entitlement with us is at least 25 days. Long-term employees: insiders are rewarded with an additional loyalty leave.
  • Flexible working hours / Home Office / Part-time work: If the company allows it, working hours and expenses can be flexibly tailored to your current living situation.
  • Gastronomic offer: All four locations of the cantonal hospital Graubünden offer a wide range of catering options. With us you can eat varied, balanced and healthy.
  • Health: The health of our employees: inside is important to us: The cantonal hospital Graubünden was awarded the quality label Friendy Work Space in November 2022.
