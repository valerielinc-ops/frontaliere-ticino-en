Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company now employs over 2500 people from more than 80 countries across the group, and the trend is rising. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. Our Corporate Functions division bundles various activities. In addition to our finance and IT services, this area also includes global marketing and sales management as well as human resources. Fresh ideas are created here every day, which we lead to success together. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Do you want to be part of it? Then come to us. ## Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the daily export processing of all shipments (B2B, franchise, IC, retail) for air, road and sea transports
  • You control and monitor delivery dates and deadlines in cooperation with internal departments
  • After successful training, you take over your own markets in the franchise area
  • You maintain active customer contact via telephone, mail and teams with franchise partners, Country branches, regional sales managers and internal contact persons
  • You maintain business partner data and ensure archiving and filing correctly and completely
  • You are the point of contact for internal departments in the international dispatch of sample shipments, B2B shipments, special transports as well as in customer service and intercompany retail
  • You prepare all necessary export and customs documents such as health certificates, certificates of origin and proof of preference for a smooth export
  • You work closely with chambers of commerce, health authorities, freight forwarders, transporters, customs clearance agencies and other service providers
  • You recognise, analyse, initiate and monitor measures in the area of trade compliance in coordination with the head of department and the management of international transports ##
