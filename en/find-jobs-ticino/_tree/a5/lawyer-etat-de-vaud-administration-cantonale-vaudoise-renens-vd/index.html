Administrateur-rice Datacenter — État de Vaud (Administration cantonale vaudoise)

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you looking for a stimulating challenge in a dynamic and innovative environment? Put your energy to work as a Datacenter Administrator with the General Directorate for Digital and Information Systems (DGNSI)!

Missions

You ensure the proper operation and availability of the IT hosting centers (datacenters) of the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (ACV):

  • Plan regular maintenance of vital equipment (power supply, ventilation, cooling, anomaly detection)
  • Coordinate supplier interventions and ensure regular maintenance of the datacenters
  • Regularly check and test the proper operation of datacenter infrastructures
  • Monitor and analyze performance, publish reports and statistics on defined indicators

You carry out the day-to-day operational activities of the datacenters:

  • Manage and keep technical infrastructures (racks, cabling, equipment) up to date
  • Control and log access to the datacenters
  • Install, test and validate equipment and links
  • Handle incidents according to their priority within agreed deadlines
  • Monitor datacenter surveillance tools
  • Ensure the update of management tools, documentation and inventories

You contribute to the implementation of new solutions within the datacenters:

  • Provide infrastructure experts with the necessary support during the implementation and testing of new solutions
  • Contribute to the improvement of the DGNSI Management System

You participate in the on-call duty service

  • Proactively supervise the operation of the datacenters outside business hours
  • Resolve serious incidents and escalate complex incidents as quickly as possible
  • Grant access to people who need to intervene urgently outside business hours

Profile sought

Education

  • Diploma of Technician ES (Higher Technical School) in a technical, IT or electrical trade (required)
  • Bachelor's degree in electrical engineering or building services engineering (desirable)
  • Certification in operational datacenter management and/or ITIL Foundation certification (required)

Professional experience

  • 5 years of experience in operating datacenter-type environments
  • 3 years of experience in fire safety, flood safety and crisis management

Technical knowledge

  • IT hardware (network equipment, servers, storage)
  • Building services engineering (air conditioning, ventilation, electricity, surveillance and alarm)
  • Datacenter infrastructure (racks, cabling)
  • Technical English level B1 (required) – German level B1 (desirable)
  • Proficiency in ACV standard office tools (MS-Office suite, messaging, ITSM) (desirable)
  • Category B driving license required.
  • A spirit of mutual aid, adaptability, sense of responsibility, good organization and solid stress management are your strengths.
  • Your attention to detail and pursuit of perfection will also be highly valued.

Specific on-call duties

  • Work possible outside standard hours (night, Saturday, Sunday and public holidays)
  • Participation in the night and public holiday on-call service
  • Residence in Switzerland required
  • Police accreditation required

Complete application in PDF (CV in French, cover letter, certificates, diplomas). For any further information, please consult the job description by clicking here or contact the head of the Datacenter, Network entity, Giuseppe Catanese ( giuseppe.catanese@vd.ch ), who is at your disposal.
