Are you open-minded, motivated and looking for a modern training company on the pulse of the times? Livit offers you just that. Our interns are an important part of the Livit family. As an intern, you work in a team and work together with your caregiver to ensure that the properties of our customers and their tenants are in good hands. The tasks at Livit are varied and varied.

Your tasks

  • You have rain telephone contact with tenants, housekeepers, craftsmen, authorities, etc.
  • You support your caregiver in the preparation and implementation of apartment acceptances / transfers
  • You are handling various customer concerns
  • You help with the rental of apartments as well as additional objects, such as garages and parking spaces, including contract creation
  • You create accounts receivable and final accounts
  • You help with the issue of repair/renovation orders and their monitoring
  • You create various correspondence and complete general administrative tasks

  • You are an organizational talent and like to take on responsibility
  • You are curious and open to change
  • You enjoy applying new technologies and digital tools
