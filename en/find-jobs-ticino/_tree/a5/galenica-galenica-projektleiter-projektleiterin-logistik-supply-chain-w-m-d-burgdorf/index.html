Full description

Location: Alloga AG, Buchmattstrasse 10, 3401 Burgdorf

You are not waiting for something to happen. You're doing it. Do you love to move things forward? Challenges motivate you, responsibility is a matter of course for you and standstill is not an option? Then we are looking for you! At Alloga AG, we are shaping the logistics of tomorrow. In Circle Solution Design, part of our Partners & Solutions division, we develop and implement innovative solutions for pharmaceutical logistics. We work in a modern role model, live Servant Leadership and rely on people who want to take responsibility, courageously decide and make a difference together. Ready to make the difference?

You can do that.

  • You drive projects forward. You assume responsibility for demanding logistics and supply chain projects from the start of the project and consistently lead them to a successful conclusion.
  • You give results. You control several projects in parallel, set clear priorities and keep deadlines, costs and quality under control.
  • You remove obstacles. You recognize problems early, make decisions and bring solutions on the road.
  • You move people. You coordinate interdisciplinary project teams, inspire stakeholders and shape the logistics of the future with our project methodology PULS.

You bring that with you

  • You tackle and take responsibility. You act proactively, decide courageously and bring things to a consistent conclusion even in the face of headwinds.
  • You have the professional foundation. You have completed training in logistics, supply chain or business administration, ideally supplemented by further training at the level of HF, FH or comparable.
  • You know your craft. You will bring several years of project management and logistics experience, ideally with SAP S/4HANA, WM/EWM and knowledge in the pharmaceutical environment (GDP/GMP).
  • You communicate sovereignly. You have very good knowledge of German and English.

We fill this position without external personnel service providers.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

Logistics services for the pharmaceutical industry Alloga is a company in the Galenica network and, as a leading pre-wholesale service provider, provides comprehensive warehousing, logistics and distribution services as well as other specialized services for the pharmaceutical industry. Alloga thus assumes a central role for the safe and efficient supply of pharmaceutical products in Switzerland. We primarily supply wholesalers, including Galexis’ own wholesale business, as well as hospitals, doctors and pharmacies throughout Switzerland. At Alloga, we work on behalf of around 100 national and multinational pharmaceutical companies to distribute their products on the Swiss market. Almost every second package for patients in Switzerland was previously stored at Alloga in Burgdorf.
