Put to the Point

In this role, you and your team are responsible for various warehouse management tasks. Thanks to your work, the ready delivery of all material items in the army pharmacy Ittigen is guaranteed. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your complete electronic application!

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20805
  • Place of work: Worblentalstrasse 36, 3063 Ittigen

You can make this contribution

  • Leading a team of around ten employees, ensuring a balanced utilization rate and specifically promoting and demanding employees
  • Responsibility for the professional planning "quantity, time, personnel and resources"
  • Responsible for the implementation, coordination, optimization and control of freight forwarding in the entire army pharmacy
  • Actively support our own team in day-to-day business and ensure smooth running of the warehouse processes
  • Ensure the proper storage management independently, taking into account economic criteria (specialist inspections, safety audit, ISO audit)
  • Participate in projects, operational tests and process optimizations as well as coordinate maintenance and maintenance work

This makes you unique

  • Technical or commercial vocational training completed
  • Training as a logistics specialist or equivalent with several years of experience in the pharmaceutical industry
  • Several years of management experience with high social skills
  • Customer- and solution-oriented working methods, analytical and conceptual thinking
  • Comprehensive MS Office, SAP and TMS skills, readiness to carry a service weapon
  • Oral and written knowledge of a second official language

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3,500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
