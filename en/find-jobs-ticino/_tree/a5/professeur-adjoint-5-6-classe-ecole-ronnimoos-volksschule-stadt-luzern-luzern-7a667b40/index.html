We offer

  • Good accessibility with public transport

The Rönnimoos School Operation Unit consists of the Rönnimoos School House and the three kindergartens Matt, Michaelshof and Rönnimoos. Every day we welcome around 220 children at our school, who are taught and accompanied by around 30 educators. We want the children to look forward to kindergarten and school, so it is important to us that we respond to the children’s living environment and constantly develop our school. In order to strengthen coexistence and live common values and attitudes, we work according to the principle of the New Authority (according to Haim Omer). In addition, the further development of teaching and assessment practice is in focus. In August 2025 we started with our specialist areas Sawubona (Theme Behaviour - I see you!), IBBF (integrated talent and talent promotion) and digitality, which help shape our school day with consultations, support and projects.
