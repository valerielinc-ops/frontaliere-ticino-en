Full description

The Migros Club School is the largest adult education provider in Switzerland. The cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland operates 7 club schools in which our customers are offered more than 600 different courses and courses in a pleasant teaching atmosphere.

Luogo di lavoro: Club School Migros, Club School St. Gallen, Bahnhofplatz 2, 9000 St. Gallen

Mansioni

  • Your joy in teaching, your commitment and your winning appearance inspire the participants
  • Preparation, implementation and follow-up of teaching Pilates Reformer
  • Practice-oriented and health-promoting teaching design taking into account the different requirements of the course participants
  • Ensuring course quality and implementing club school standards

Requirements

  • Several years of proven experience: as a teacher in the teaching of Pilates offers, ideally of offers Pilates Reformer
  • Completed basic or further education: Dipl. Pilates instructor with training at a recognized training institution (e.g. SAFS, Art of Motion, Star Education, Pilatestrainer*in BGB)
  • Specific training in the field of reformer Pilates (e.g. reformer certificate or modular training)
  • Independent, flexible and reliable personality who imparts their knowledge and joy for Pilates courses with commitment
  • Winning appearance and good communication skills
  • Ability to lead groups safely, accompany participants individually and methodically correctly convey movement sequences
  • Proof of sound knowledge in safe handling of the Pilates reformer
  • Ability to scale exercises appropriately (beginners to advanced)
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses
  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
  • Insurance: Benefit Conditions at Various Insurance Companies
