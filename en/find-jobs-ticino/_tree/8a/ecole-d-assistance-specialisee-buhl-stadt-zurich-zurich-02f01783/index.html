About us

The school district Uto is one of seven school districts in the city of Zurich and comprises the city districts 2 and 3 with the districts Leimbach, WollisInsingen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon.

The Bühl School is a public school in the city of Zurich. It is located in the heart of the Wiedikon district at the foot of the Üetliberg. In the school year 2023/24, 485 children from kindergarten to 6th grade attend the school Bühl, which currently leads 18 primary classes, of which 9 are under and 6 are under and medium grade.

In addition to the school house and the gymnastics hall building, there are also 4 kindergarten locations in the quarters and 8 care facilities.
