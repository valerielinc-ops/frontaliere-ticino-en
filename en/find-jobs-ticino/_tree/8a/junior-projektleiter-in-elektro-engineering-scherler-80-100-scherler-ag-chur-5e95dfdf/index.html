Project manager in electrical engineering SCHERLER 80\-100 %

Shape the future with us. * SCHERLER is a modern family company with roots and vision. We believe in cooperation at eye level, in people with ideas and in an environment in which you can develop further – both professionally and personally.

With us, you work in dedicated teams on exciting projects, take responsibility and bring your strengths where they have an impact. Our diverse fields of activity open up real development prospects for you – and the space to actively shape your next career step.

Your tasks

  • Processing of the entire electrical technology (\- and weak current) over all construction phases, from the preliminary project to the final billing
  • Independent preparation and processing of plan documents and schemes for \- and civil engineering projects
  • Development of well-founded basics in a team with the project manager and visualization of them using the planning method BIM
  • Support of the project manager in the preparation of concept studies, tenders, project designs and specialist construction management

You bring that with you

  • Completed vocational training as electrical planner EFZ or electrical installer EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience in electrical engineering
  • Completed studies or current studies in the field of building\-electroengineering
  • Ideally first knowledge in planning according to BIM
  • Flexibility, teamwork and interest in technically demanding projects

What makes us special as an employer

  • Exciting projects in electrical engineering
  • Flexible working hours \& part-time models
  • 5\-6 weeks holidays, plus paid holidays
  • Further education & development
