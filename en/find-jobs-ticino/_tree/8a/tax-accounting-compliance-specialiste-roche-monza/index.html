The Position Within the Finance management, we are looking for a Tax Accounting Specialist to be included in our tax office with strong technical-accounting skills. The resource will be responsible for autonomous management of the fiscal compliance cycle and reporting for Italian companies of the Roche Group. The role requires a solid capacity to combine tax compliance with accounting records. Main Responsibility

  • Tax Compliance & Operations: Full management of periodic VAT obligations (LIPE, annual declaration), direct taxes (IRES/IRAP) and deemed to be in agreement, ensuring strict compliance with statutory deadlines.
  • Accounting & Tax Reporting: Accurate calculation of current taxes, deferred and anticipated for actual and forecast phases.
  • Budget preparation: Technical support in drafting the tax posts of the civil and consolidated financial statements, ensuring the correct application of accounting principles.
  • Group Reporting: Preparation of the periodic tax reporting in accordance with the parent company guidelines and local requirements.
  • Regulation Monitoring: Analysis of the impacts of tax innovations on business accounting processes and implementation of the procedures necessary to ensure compliance. Requirements Requested
  • Master's degree in Economics (preferably with diploma of Reason) ; the qualification to the profession of Commercialist is considered a preferential requirement.
  • At least 2-3 years of professional experience at consulting companies (Big4), specialized studies or within tax departments of structured multinational contexts.
  • Deep knowledge of Italian tax law and mastery of national accounting (OIC) and international (IFRS).
  • Advanced use of the SAP and Excel ERP system.
  • Italian mother tongue and good mastery of the English language (written and spoken).
  • They complete the profile: excellent analytical skills, aptitude to problem solving, precision and guidance to teamwork in a dynamic environment.
