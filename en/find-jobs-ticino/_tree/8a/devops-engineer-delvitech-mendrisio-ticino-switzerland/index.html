Full description

Build and run the CI/CD pipelines, containers and releases that power Delvitech's AI inspection software, from development to the customer site. Job Summary As a DevOps Engineer, you will support the development and deployment life cycle of our software platform, built on a microservices architecture. You will maintain CI/CD pipelines, manage containerized environments and help streamline the release, update and support of our software at customer sites. We currently use TeamCity for continuous integration, Git (Bitbucket) for version control and Docker Compose for container orchestration. Our infrastructure runs on Linux (Debian/Ubuntu). Key Responsibilities

  • Maintain and improve our CI/CD pipelines (TeamCity).
  • Support containerized service development and deployment with Docker/Docker Compose, including private Docker image registries.
  • Build installation packages (.deb or similar).
  • Administer Linux systems (Debian/Ubuntu).
  • Manage the release, update and support of software installed at customer sites (on-premise), including in isolated networks.
  • Apply Linux and container security fundamentals: secrets, privileges, network security.
  • Support the deployment and testing of software on physical AOI systems.
  • Contribute to continuous improvements in automation, scalability and reliability. Requirements
  • At least 3 years of experience as a DevOps Engineer, SRE or System Engineer.
  • Linux administration (Debian/Ubuntu): systemd, networking, permissions, apt, etc.
  • Experience with Docker/Docker Compose and virtualization tools.
  • Building installation packages (.deb or similar).
  • CI/CD tools (TeamCity or similar).
  • Git (Bitbucket).
  • Scripting in Bash and/or Python.
  • Private Docker image registries.
  • Linux and container security fundamentals: secrets, privileges, network security.
  • gRPC and MQTT.
  • Managing software installed at customer sites (on-premise): release, update and support, including in isolated networks.
  • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
  • Ability to explain technical choices to non-technical people.
  • Data-driven approach and ability to drive change.
  • Teamwork.
  • Fluent Italian and technical English. Nice to Have
  • Containers with NVIDIA GPUs.
  • Databases: MariaDB, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Redis, MinIO.
  • Java (Spring Boot) and Go.
  • Jira and Confluence. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
