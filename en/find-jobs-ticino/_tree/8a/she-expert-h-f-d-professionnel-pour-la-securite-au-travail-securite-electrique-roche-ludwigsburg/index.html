The Position SHE expert (m/f/d) professional for occupational safety / electrical safety

Your new working environment Roche Diagnostics Automation Solutions GmbH in Ludwigsburg is the market-leading centre of excellence in the field of laboratory automation. Here we develop and produce innovative systems that are used by laboratories all over the world. As a site management sustainability team at the site, we are committed and passionate about promoting sustainability issues. Comprised of SHE (Safety, Health, Environment), Mobility and Energy Management, we support and advise on the planning and implementation of sustainability standards and regulatory requirements.

In a dynamic development and production environment, we offer you the opportunity to actively contribute your expertise in occupational safety and electrical safety and to shape the future of our location.

Responsibility | This is what awaits you In this versatile function, you are part of the Safety, Health & Environment area of RDA Site Management in Ludwigsburg. You combine the classic consulting as a specialist for occupational safety with the technical responsibility as an overall responsible electrical specialist (GVEFK) and support the business units Site, Operations and R&D in the implementation and compliance with legal and internal company regulations. Your responsibilities and daily tasks include:

  • Operational support & prevention: You act as an appointed specialist for occupational safety and advise and train managers and employees directly on site in the safe design of workplaces and processes.
  • Risk minimization & protective measures: You carry out regular inspections, identify sources of danger at an early stage and create practical risk assessments together with the managers / supports and advises on the implementation of protective measures.
  • Cause analyses: In the event of accidents or safety incidents, you support the cause analysis and ensure that effective corrective measures are implemented in close coordination with the company doctor and the works council.
  • Overall responsible electrical specialist: You take over the technical supervision and responsibility for electrical safety at the site. This includes the operational control of the VEFK structures and the securing of a legally secure electrical organization.
  • Project support: You accompany local projects operationally to ensure that security aspects are taken into account from the beginning.

Qualifications | This is what you bring

  • You have successfully completed your studies in a technical field (e.g. engineering, electrical engineering, safety engineering).
  • You are already a qualified specialist in occupational safety (SiFa) according to §6 & §7 ASiG, ideally bring the necessary specialist knowledge for ordering to the GVEFK and have several years of practical professional experience, preferably in the (diagnostic or medical) industry.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of the current safety standards, regulations, laws and guidelines (BetrSichV, ArbSchG, GefStoffV, VDE) as well as experience in practical implementation of the regulations. Additional qualifications or experience in other SHE areas, such as security, health or environment, are beneficial.
