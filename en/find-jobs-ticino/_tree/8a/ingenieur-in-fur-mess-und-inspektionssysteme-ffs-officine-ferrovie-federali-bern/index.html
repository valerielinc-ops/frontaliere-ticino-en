Engineer:in for measuring and inspection systems 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Engineer: for measurement and inspection systems Bern / Work Smart, 01.02.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Move with us great things! Become part of a young and dynamic team. By supporting the measurement and inspection systems in coordination with our international industrial partners, you ensure the condition monitoring of the SBB infrastructure. In this way, you make a big contribution to our vision of “train control” and have the opportunity to shape the mobility of the future. Our benefits such as child allowances, free GA and discounted travel abroad – including for your family – can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • In the varied function as an engineer: in measurement and inspection systems, you are part of the industrial condition monitoring of the SBB infrastructure.
  • You are responsible for operating measurement and inspection systems on the diagnostic vehicles.
  • For this purpose, you take over/monitor the management of maintenance and life cycle management in cooperation with international industrial partners.
  • In your role, you create specifications for the further development of existing systems for condition monitoring.
  • Among other things, you are responsible for the partial / project management of optimizations and further developments of technologically sophisticated measurement and inspection systems.

You bring that with you.

  • With your independent, service-oriented way of working and communicative nature, you contribute to our organization.
  • You are a strong organizational talent and like to proactively approach people and maintain your network, whether inside or outside the organization.
  • You have completed studies in electrical engineering or computer science (FH/ETH) and preferably have several years of professional experience in project and/or life cycle management.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and English, knowledge of every other national language is a plus.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This position is in requirement level J, regional allowance level 1.
