Full description

We at CSS help people to lead a healthy life. Maybe our mission will soon be yours too? We would be delighted. We are a diverse group of ambitious people who want to make a difference. Some of us did the KV, others studied, still others come from the retail trade, the hospitality industry or from another professional corner. Everyone brings their experience. We always pursue our own goals, celebrate the successes as a team and come to work every day with joy and passion.

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • You lead your team of around 6 people, inspiring the people who work with you and helping them achieve their goals and develop.
  • Your leadership style can combine the needs of your employees and the objectives of your agencies for the benefit of all.
  • You are a professional in network maintenance - our relationships with private and framework contract customers continue to grow thanks to you.
  • Our professional appearance is external to our customers, but also to potential future employees.
  • In your role, you always look outside the box and think about future prospects and possibilities for your agency.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You realize that you like to work with people – you approach topics openly and your positive nature is contagious.
  • You are a motivated and profitable personality with a pronounced sales and goal orientation and ideally have a higher education in business administration or insurance.
  • You already bring experience in employee management in sales. You can respond well to your counterpart, are interested in the needs of your team and can bring about joint solutions.
  • Thanks to your stylish and addressable communication in German, the exchange over different levels is easy.
  • You have a large relationship network and preferably live in the region.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
  • Enjoy your family life: Make time for your offspring: 3 weeks for fathers, for mothers up to 20 weeks. There are also generous child and education allowances.
