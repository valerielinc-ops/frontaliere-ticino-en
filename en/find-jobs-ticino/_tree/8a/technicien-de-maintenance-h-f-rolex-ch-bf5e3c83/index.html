Full description

Introduction

As part of the development of our activities in the canton of Fribourg, we are building a talent pool to support the deployment of our new industrial site in Bulle. With this in mind, we will soon be looking for (M/F) Maintenance Technician profiles in order to strengthen our teams.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure corrective maintenance of production equipment, automatic stocks as well as autonomous mobile robots and automated guided vehicles
  • Apply diagnostic methodologies, identify the root causes of malfunctions and return equipment to service within the expected time frame
  • Carry out preventive maintenance operations and contribute to the improvement of maintenance plans
  • Prepare interventions according to operating constraints, analyze equipment history and comply with safety and environmental instructions
  • Propose and implement reliability actions, technical modifications and equipment improvements
  • Coordinate and support internal and external stakeholders, ensuring the monitoring and validation of their interventions
  • Share technical knowledge with teams and collaborate with internal partners as part of projects and cross-functional work

Profile

  • Technical training in the fields of mechanics, automation, electrical engineering or equivalent
  • Experience maintaining automated industrial equipment, ideally in a demanding production environment
  • Confirmed technical versatility in mechanics, electricity, pneumatics and hydraulics
  • Knowledge of automation, digital controls, robotics and industrial IT
  • Mastery of common IT tools and an integrated management system
  • Intermediate level knowledge of English and/or German (B1), an asset
  • Ease of communication, team spirit, rigor, autonomy and technical curiosity
  • Availability for flexible hours, inter-site travel and on-call service as needed

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and first-rate social benefits.

We thank you for sending a complete application file (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
