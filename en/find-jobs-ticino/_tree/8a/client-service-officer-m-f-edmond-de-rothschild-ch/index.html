Full description

Attached to the Department of Independent Fortune Managers (GFI), your role will be to contribute through your support to the commercial development of a fast growing business.

MISSION

Your main activities will be

  • Bring your support to Relationship Manager in its daily activity with GFIs
  • Exchange orders, exchange rates, trustees, payments
  • Preparation of accounts openings, closures, modifications, management of the documentary part
  • Trouble shooting, investigation, control
  • Daily GFI interaction (GFI, end customers, call back, GFI/client visits)
  • Administrative management of GFI files and clients, annual reviews, update KYC

PROFILE

  • You have a Banking CFC or professional maturity or equivalent with finance/economy specialization.
  • You justify a minimum 2 years experience on similar functions
  • You benefit from excellent banking and financial knowledge
  • French, with excellent English knowledge, any other language will be considered an asset.
  • Excellent team spirit, open mind and flexibility
  • Manages Windows desktop tools including excellent knowledge of Excel and Powerpoint
  • You are recognized for your relational qualities, your oral and written communication skills and your high sense of service. You have an ease of writing and a sense of synthesis. You are a force of proposal, rigorous (se), reactive (ve) and autonomous. You are able to work under pressure in a competitive environment and demand, with high volumes
  • Mastery of Avaloq considered an asset
