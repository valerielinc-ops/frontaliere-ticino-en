Nurse HF/FH - TK Angst, 60 %

Location: Basel

From 01.02.2027 or by agreement

Founded in 1900, the Klinik Sonnenhalde in Riehen is now an established private clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland and southern Baden. We pursue a holistic, human-centered, milieutherapeutic psychiatry that combines excellent professional quality with a Christian value base. The clinic has 68 stationary beds, 4 day clinics and 4 ambulatories. As one of the first clinics in Switzerland, we are actively promoting digital therapy offerings, such as blended therapy and virtual reality and opened the first hybrid day clinic in Switzerland in February 2023.

As a nurse HF/FH, you accompany people with expertise, empathy and heart through their everyday lives. In this versatile position at our TK Hybrid Basel location, you can expect a modern working environment in which your expertise is appreciated and you can actively contribute to the quality of life of your patients.

Your responsibilities

  • You work as a caregiver in the core team and take responsibility for accompanying your caregiver from entry to exit.
  • They plan the care process and ensure the implementation of the planned interventions both on site and digitally
  • They lead groups and together with the team shape the therapeutic milieu on the station

Your profile

  • In addition to a diploma as a nurse HF or FH have She has profound experience in psychiatric setting
  • You are a motivated, proactive and self-reflected personality with good criticism and communication skills
  • You enjoy leading groups and participating in the further development of digital offerings
