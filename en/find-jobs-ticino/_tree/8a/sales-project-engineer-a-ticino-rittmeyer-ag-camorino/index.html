Full description

For our internal sales team, consisting of seven people, we seek a motivated person who combines proactivity and precision in work and who plays their role with competence. In this position you act as an interface between our sales and operating department, responsible for the execution of our projects. A hungry team awaits you and a leadership based on an open and equal dialogue.

Rittmeyer Group

rethink. Automate. innovative.

As part of the BRUGG Group, we specialize in providing our customers worldwide with innovative solutions for measuring and automation in the water and energy supply sector, as well as data-based services, with particular attention to energy transition, sustainability and infrastructure security.

Your area of expertise

  • Get bid requests from sales department
  • Processing concepts of realization, calculations the necessary costs and reviews commercial offers
  • You consult directly with the customer and perform any inspections on site
  • For larger projects, build an internal team dedicated to the offer and hire the guide
  • Decide whether or not to submit an offer in accordance with the sales department
  • Identify and document risks
  • Ensure an orderly delivery of the project to the operating team after the order by the customer

What you bring with you

  • You have a degree in technical field (SUP or FH)
  • You have professional experience in automation and control systems or in a similar sector
  • You are distinguished by the team spirit, strong communication skills, working autonomy and mentality oriented to economic efficiency
  • You have an excellent knowledge of Italian (C2), a good knowledge of French (B2) and a communicative level in German

What we offer

  • Work with a future: Significant work in a future-oriented and environmentally friendly company
  • Work culture based on trust: Culture DU, peer communication and open doors (up to CEO)
  • Flexibility and balance between professional life and private life: Annual working time model, 5-6 weeks holiday, mobile and flexible work, home office, Third Place, part-time work
  • mobility: Privileged location of our offices with motorway connections and public transport. Three locations in Switzerland: Baar, Avry and Camorino. Seats all over the world: Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Croatia, Italy, France, United States and Singapore.
  • workplace: Great offices with ergonomic workplaces and modern IT infrastructure, modern co-working space at Baar headquarters for flexible work
  • facilities: From free fruit to discounted menu for lunch, workwear provided, telephone subscription, individual use of electric bikes or scooters and vehicles in pool.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status. Note: this listing allows remote work — teleworking above 25 % of your time can shift your social-security base to Italy, so confirm the agreed remote share with HR before signing.
