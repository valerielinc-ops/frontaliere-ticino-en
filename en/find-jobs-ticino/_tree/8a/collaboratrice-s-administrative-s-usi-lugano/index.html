Lugano University Library Collaborator/Administrative/or (50%) The University of Italian Switzerland (USI) is a young and agile reality in continuous development, launched to new challenges in the sign of its three guiding values: quality, openness and responsibility. Active in different areas of study and research, USI offers its students a training as protagonists, engaging and interdisciplinary, and its researchers and collaborators a privileged area of autonomy and freedom of initiative. The Lugano University Library (BUL) organizes documentary materials (monographs, periodicals, electronic media etc.) for the Lugano University campuses, responding to study, research and information. To complete the BUL team, USI opens an administrative/cooperator/or position (50%) Location
