Full description

Kummler+Matter EVT AG - a company of the Equans Group - has been involved in the planning and installation of electrical and electromechanical infrastructures in the fields of energy, transport and telecommunications for more than 100 years. The company offers interesting opportunities for education, growth and further development. Our customers have high quality standards that motivate us to perform at the highest levels every day. Competent, motivated professionals find exciting challenges with us. In order to strengthen our team at the Sois site, we are looking for an installer for cable construction (80-100%).

Your tasks

  • Installations in road tunnels such as lighting, smoke detectors, video cameras, SOS, fibre optic cable construction and central installation
  • Work on energy distribution networks
  • Cable construction and installation work for railway installations such as track lighting, station conversion, fibre optic cable construction and installation of emergency handrail systems
  • Installation and cabling of traffic signalling systems
  • New construction and maintenance of floodlights on sports fields and public lighting

Your profile

  • Completed basic electrotechnical training is advantageous
  • Experience in cable construction or similar function
  • You like working outside
  • You are ready for night work
  • You are resilient, flexible and safety-conscious
  • Knowledge of German in words and writing, knowledge of Italian advantageous
  • Cross-starter possible
  • Driving licence Cat. B

We offer

  • Varied and responsible tasks
  • Working on special equipment and non-ordinary tasks
  • Self-employed work
  • Young, dynamic and motivated team
  • Attractive social benefits and employment conditions
  • 4.5 days week
