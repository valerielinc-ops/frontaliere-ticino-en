Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Bern – Salem Spital

Composition by: 01. August 2027

Type of employment: temporary

Reference number: 69763

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Hirslanden Bern AG includes Klinik Beau-Site, the Salem Hospital and Klinik Permanence with a total of around 350 beds. At the Salem Spital site, we offer a freelance apprenticeship as a medical device technologist/technologist EFZ with training start on 01. August 2027.

The Hirslanden Group offers you a diverse and attractive education. You work in an experienced team and complete various internships, which give you an in-depth insight into various areas. You will be accompanied throughout the learning process by a vocational trainer.

YOUR TASKS

During your training, you are jointly responsible for the correct preparation of various instruments and apparatus, which are used for examinations and operations, among other things.

  • You manage the medical products
  • You organize the processing process of medical devices
  • You clean and disinfect instruments
  • You sterilize instruments
  • You contribute to quality assurance
  • You perform administrative and logistical tasks

Your profile

  • You have completed elementary school (secondary school or good real degree)
  • You are interested in healthcare
  • You work reliably and have a high sense of responsibility
  • You like to work precisely and have a manual skill
  • You like working in a team and are reliable
  • You are physically resilient
  • You have a good hygiene awareness

Our offer

  • Attractive and central workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Family atmosphere with you - culture
  • Professional support and individual support in the learning process
  • Extensive benefits offer (e.g. publishing, public transport coupons, fitness subscriptions, etc.)
  • Discounted food options in restaurants in clinics
  • Financial contribution to teaching equipment
  • Annual learning excursion

For additional information, Zahid Ascic, Head of AEMP under T, or Sarah Jäggi, Head of Vocational Education and Training under T, is at your disposal. We look forward to your contact.
