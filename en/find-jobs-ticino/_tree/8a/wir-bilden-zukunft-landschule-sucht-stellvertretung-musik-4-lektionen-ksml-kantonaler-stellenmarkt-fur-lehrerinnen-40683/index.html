Full description

Portrait Tuesday, shortly after 13:15 in the Arnissäge. The door to the music room is already open. A child sits on the xylofon and hesitates to strike the first note. Ten minutes later, a whole rhythm sounds out of the room – cheerfully carried by children from the 3rd / 4th grade. The school Arni-Landiswil is a rural school in the Emmental. We teach in multi-year classes - music connects.

Tasks

Compact and planable: 4 lessons on Tuesday afternoon Our music teacher is due to illness longer. Country School with Heart You take over the music lessons of our multi-year classes 3./4.Kl. and 7th–9th class Real and contribute your knowledge and skills.

  • Joy of accompanying children and young people
  • Communicative, engaged, team-oriented
  • Flexible and targeted use of own resources. This deputy can be combined well with a master thesis.
