Full description

About us

Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa's approximately 1,100 employees make crucial contributions to the well-being of society and to a future worth living. Empa is a research institution in the ETH domain.

Apprentice laboratory assistant EFZ specializing in textiles (a)

Versatile and practical vocational training is particularly important to us. With us you not only learn your craft, but also work actively in an innovative research environment. A successful apprenticeship at Empa requires motivation, initiative and flexibility.

Independence, a sense of responsibility, creativity and networked thinking accompany you throughout your entire training. With our internal “Saphir” support program, we provide you with additional targeted support in your development. If you are curious, enjoy working on future technologies and find research exciting, we look forward to getting to know you.

Tasks

  • EFZ Textil laboratory technicians learn not only how to safely handle chemicals, but also technical and dyeing processes.
  • External chemical and textile-specific introductory courses support the transfer of knowledge during your training.
  • You work on research projects and learn to plan and carry out experiments in the laboratory and evaluate the results.
  • Recording experiments is a central part of daily work.
  • In addition to in-house training, you will attend the St. Gallen Commercial Vocational and Further Training Center (GBS).

Requirements

  • You graduated from secondary school with good results in mathematics, physics, chemistry and English.
  • You have good color vision.
  • You have a willingness to learn and perform.
  • Networked thinking and an exact and reliable way of working are very important to you.
  • You like working on the PC and have a lot of perseverance for experiments that don't work right away.

We offer

  • We offer interesting training, a modern workplace and excellent employment conditions.
  • With us you have the opportunity to acquire a wide range of knowledge.
  • Empa is a lively and innovative research environment with an international flair.
  • You can find further information about Empa's teaching in our fact sheet and on our homepage.

“a” stands for “all” in our job advertisements. We live a culture of inclusion and respect. We welcome all people who are interested in innovative, sustainable and meaningful activities - that's all that counts.

Contact

We look forward to receiving your complete online application including a letter of motivation, CV, certificates, signal box test and contact details for two references. Please submit these documents exclusively via our application portal. Applications by email or post will not be considered.
