Assistant physician in urology - job advertisement

EOC, the multi-site hospital of Ticino, has facilities throughout the canton, with a total of 1’000 beds.

The organization effectively combines a local approach with an overall vision, ensuring the population a comprehensive and local hospital offering, regardless of where the services are needed.

Thanks to the commitment and expertise of its more than 6’500 employees and their focus on human connection, EOC provides quality healthcare, caring for more than 41'000 inpatients each year and providing more than 600'000 outpatient consultations.

Position

To complete our urology team at the Ospedale Regionale di Lugano, we are looking for:

Assistant physician in urology (100%)

The urology department of the Ospedale Regionale di Lugano is a rapidly expanding department with an ambitious growth plan at the cantonal and national levels.

Requirements

  • Essential requirements: a degree / diploma in medicine and surgery with the corresponding registration in MedReg before starting work.
  • For physicians with foreign qualifications: recognition by the Federal Commission for Medical Professions MEBEKO and the corresponding registration in MedReg before starting work are mandatory;
  • proven knowledge of Italian (minimum required level B2) with the corresponding registration in MedReg before starting work,
  • knowledge of English desirable;
  • proficiency with IT tools;
  • constructive and proactive attitude;
  • strong willingness and aptitude for teamwork;
  • interest in clinical / translational research in urology;
  • interest in training in urology (surgical and outpatient)

Start date

Start date: January 1, 2027, or to be agreed

Applications

Complete applications, including a curriculum vitae, a cover letter, and copies of educational and employment certificates, must be received by November 15, 2026, in electronic format only.

Applications submitted by post or email will not be considered, nor will applications that do not meet the required criteria.

Contact

Further information can be requested from

  • Prof. Dr. med. Andrea Gallina, Chief of Urology, Andrea.Gallina@eoc.ch
  • Prof. Dr. med. Nicola Fossati, Deputy Chief of Urology, Nicola.Fossati@eoc.ch

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
