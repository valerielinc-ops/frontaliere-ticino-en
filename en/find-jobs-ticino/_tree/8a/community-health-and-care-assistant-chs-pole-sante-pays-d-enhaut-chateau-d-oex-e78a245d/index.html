Full description

Located in Château-d’Œx, at an altitude of 1000 meters, the Pôle Santé Pays-d’Enhaut offers a wide range of hospital services, accommodation, support for the elderly, as well as home care. According to the so-called integrated care approach, this innovative organization represents an opportunity for health professionals to work within small multidisciplinary teams, for better continuity of care and in response to the health needs of the population of Pays-d'Enhaut. At the heart of a privileged natural environment, less than an hour from Lausanne or Bern, the Pays-d'Enhaut is renowned for the quality of its living environment and offers a very wide range of sporting, leisure and cultural activities, to experience in summer and winter! For its Home Care Sector, the Pays-d’Enhaut Health Center is looking for a:

Community care and health assistant (ASSC) Activity rate 80 - 100%

Entry into office: October 1, 2026 In principle attached to the home care sector, you will also be required to carry out occasional missions within the accommodation and hospital sectors.

Your mission

  • Provide basic care and medico-technical care within the framework of your skills
  • Support clients in activities of daily living
  • Observe the state of health of clients and transmit relevant information to the team
  • Participate in the implementation and monitoring of care plans
  • Promote autonomy and home support for clients
  • Ensure rigorous and complete documentation of the services carried out
  • Actively participate in the quality and continuity of care
  • Collaborate with the multidisciplinary team in taking care of the client

Your profile

  • Holder of a CFC Community Care and Health Assistant (ASSC) or a title deemed equivalent recognized in Switzerland
  • Strong interest in home care and support for people with varied needs
  • Autonomy, sense of responsibility and ability to adapt
  • Good interpersonal skills and listening skills
  • Ability to work in an interdisciplinary team
  • Organization, reliability and flexibility
  • Valid driving license and personal vehicle available, essential for the exercise of the function

We offer

  • Participation in the development of an integrated care model, involving the coordination and collaboration of different health professionals
  • Work in a pleasant environment, within a human-sized structure
  • The advantages of living in the mountains while remaining close to urban centers (to discover under www.alpesvaudoises.ch/fr/stories/paysdenhaut)
  • Working conditions in compliance with the San-Vaud CCT (13th salary, training, 5-6 weeks of vacation per year, etc.)
