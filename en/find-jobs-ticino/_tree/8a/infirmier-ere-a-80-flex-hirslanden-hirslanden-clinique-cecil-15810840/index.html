The Hirslanden Group is Switzerland's largest medical network. We offer all individuals, regardless of their situation, high-level individual medical care to improve their quality of life - from birth to old age and from prevention to cure. As part of our corporate strategy Together We Care, our ultimate goal is to improve the quality of life of people who trust us.

MAIN ACTIVITY OF THE POST

Within an oncology unit, the tasks are

  • Apply clinical therapeutic procedures according to quality criteria
  • Provide hygiene, comfort and maintenance care that meets the needs of patients, promotes their autonomy and respects their social and cultural characteristics
  • Ensure effective and personalized communication with patients and their environment
  • You will also have to perform replacements in surgical services

YOUR PROFILE

  • Nurse, you have confirmed professional experience in medicine. Knowledge of the field of oncology is an asset
  • For a foreign diploma, Red Cross recognition is required (in progress or obtained)
  • Excellent organizational and relational capabilities
  • Having a great sense of listening and empathy
  • With a high sense of responsibility
  • High flexibility

WE OFFER YOU
