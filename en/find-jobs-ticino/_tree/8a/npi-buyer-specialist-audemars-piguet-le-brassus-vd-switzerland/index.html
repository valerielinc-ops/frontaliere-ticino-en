Full description

In a demanding and constantly changing industrial environment, we are looking for an NPI Buyer Specialist to strengthen our purchasing organization. This strategic position combines a dual dimension: Procurement projects / development (NPI) and Purchasing amenities / long-term vision. It is at the heart of the interactions between product development, production and supplier strategy, with a key role in securing launches and the performance of the supplier panel. Attached to the NPI Manager, you will work in a matrix organization, in close collaboration with multidisciplinary teams. In this role, you will be led to:

  • Pilot supplier sourcing for new projects (RFP/RFQ, selection)
  • Build and develop a robust and efficient supplier panel
  • Conducting procurement negotiations (prices, tools, trade terms)
  • Ensure supplier performance (quality, costs, deadlines)
  • Identify and anticipate supplier risks (capacity, technique, dependence)
  • Support project teams by challenging specifications and feasibility
  • Coordinate internal stakeholders (engineering, quality, production, projects)
  • Ensuring the reliability of purchasing data and prices in the ERP (M3)
  • You justify 5 years minimum experience in industrial purchases
  • You have significant experience buying projects / NPI
  • You ideally have an exposure to shopping amenities / panel management
  • You are easily evolving in a demanding technical environment
  • You have strong negotiation and cost analysis skills
  • You're comfortable in a matrix and transverse organization.
  • You have excellent relational and collaborative qualities
  • You are recognized for your rigor, autonomy and ability to manage complexity What we offer:
  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual holiday weeks and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The ability to work remotely depending on the function
  • A business restaurant
