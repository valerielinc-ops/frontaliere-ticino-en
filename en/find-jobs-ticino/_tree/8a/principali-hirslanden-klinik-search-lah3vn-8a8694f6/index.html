The Hirslanden Group is Switzerland's largest medical network. We offer all individuals, regardless of their situation, a high level individual medical care to improve their quality of life - from birth to old age and from prevention to healing. As part of our corporate strategy "Sound We Care", our ultimate goal is to improve the quality of life of people who trust us.

PRINCIPAL TASKS

Within the department of the physiotherapy of the Bois-Cerf clinic, you will have the following main missions:

  • Management of patients in outpatient and stationary treatment - Care of patients in individual and group sessions (re-athletisation, preparation of sports season) in the room and in the pool - As part of the Swiss Olympic label: Close collaboration with sports doctors and orthopaedic surgeons Development and implementation of specific protocols for the rehabilitation of sports patients (prevention, rehabilitation and return to sport) Evaluation of performance in our Sportlab (VO 2 Max / lactate) Evaluation and rehabilitation with isocinetic equipment

YOUR PROFILE

  • Graduated physiotherapist with experience confirmed, for the benefit of a training in Sport physiotherapy, recognized by the Swiss Association of Sport Physiotherapy ASPS (level of, C minimum accreditation)
  • For foreign diplomas, recognition by the Swiss Red Cross is compulsory
  • With a common sense of communication and listening
  • Good faculties of adaptation, availability, flexibility and versatility
  • Strong motivation, high sense of responsibility and ability to collaborate in team and with doctors
