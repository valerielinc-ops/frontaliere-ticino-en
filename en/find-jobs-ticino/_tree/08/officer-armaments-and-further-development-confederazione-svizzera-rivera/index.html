Put to the Point

As a specialist for special forces equipment and devices, you work where the extraordinary is part of everyday life and make a direct contribution to the performance of the Swiss Army Special Forces Command with your know-how. In a dynamic environment, you actively promote the further development of a unique association, accompany it on its way into the future and use your skills specifically where the highest demands are placed on material and operational readiness.

  • Represent the operational needs and interests of the Special Forces Command in expert committees and project groups

This makes them unique.

  • Good oral and written knowledge of a second official language and good knowledge of English (minimum level B2)

Your commitment to security and freedom

The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are employed by civilians – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
