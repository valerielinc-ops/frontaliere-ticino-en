Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With great dedication, we are there for our customers. In our 31 cash & carry markets, through field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile assortment, attractive private labels, professional expert advice, and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in Swiss delivery and cash & carry wholesale, employing around 2,100 staff and serving 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, care homes, and hospitals, as well as retail). ##

  • Responsibilities
  • You will manage the department operationally according to economic principles and implement the defined company and market performance processes
  • You will promote and develop employees, including implementing staff and vacation planning as well as horizontal promotion (TOJ)
  • You will ensure compliance with internal and legal QS and ASIB guidelines as well as the implementation of internal regulations and instructions
  • You will maintain the flow of information and communication within the department, the market, and with MCM
  • You will build a network of relationships with our customers
  • You will be responsible for daily sales readiness and product presentation, executing sales activities according to the annual sales plan, and ensuring product availability ##
  • Requirements
  • You already have leadership experience and bring several years of experience in a similar role
  • You enjoy active customer interaction
  • You are proficient with the MS Office suite
  • You are excited by new challenges
  • You speak fluent German
  • Ideally, you already have forklift and/or pallet truck training (SUVA standard)
