HR Service Desk Specialist (m/f/d) 60-100%

Move Switzerland with us. Specialist HR Service Desk External (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, to be agreed, fixed duration until 31.12.2027, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

Join the growing "Service Desk External" unit, attached to Sourcing, Recruitment and Talents within the HR unit of the SBB since October 2021. Contribute actively, with autonomy and commitment, to the development of our services nationally. As a centre of expertise for external staff, we advise and support the specialized units of the SBB in a professional and harmonised way, from identification of needs to departure. In order to successfully manage the anticipated increase in the number of units we accompany, we are looking for several people who value teamwork, which will help ensure quality, integration and care. This position is of fixed duration until 31 December 2027. What you can move.

  • You assume administrative responsibility for the onboarding of new units (including data collection and analysis) and participate in the centralized management of external staff.
  • You professionally accompany the various internal and external partners (service rental companies, executives, external colleagues, Purchasing, Finance, etc.) throughout the onboarding process and help ensure partnership-based solutions.
  • You provide support throughout the recruitment process for temporary external staff (expenditure analysis, competitive examination, pre-selection, contract conclusion) to attract the best temporary staff for SBBs.
  • You are responsible for exciting projects on process optimization, quality assurance and implementation of new tools. You can also participate actively in the development of the External Desk Service.

What you bring.

  • We face multiple challenges every day. That's why we're looking for a number of solutions and customer service people, who are able to convince by their relationship ease, positive attitude and ability to stay cool in all circumstances.
  • We evolve every day and aspire to be among the best. You are therefore committed and consider the challenges as a source of motivation. You actively share your knowledge and are committed to contributing to the development of your colleagues' skills.
  • Excellent organization and strong IT knowledge (e.g. SAP HR, MS Office, SAP Fieldglass) are an asset in our growing industry. We are looking for several colleagues who already have professional experience in the commercial field and share our passion for human resources. Experience in the placement of temporary staff is a considerable asset but is not indispensable.
  • We work for Switzerland as a whole. That is why language skills are particularly important to us. You can communicate in German and have very good knowledge of French (level B2–C1). The mastery of Italian would be the cherry on the cake.

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive salary system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the F level and is eligible for a Level 1 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits.

Here's a good way to start.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the biggest motivation for us. We therefore waive our request for a cover letter for this position. If you wish, you can write to us in our application tool.
