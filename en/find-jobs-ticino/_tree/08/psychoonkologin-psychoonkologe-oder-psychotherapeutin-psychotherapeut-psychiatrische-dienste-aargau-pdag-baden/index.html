Clinic for Aging and Neuropsychiatry (KAN)

For our team at the interdisciplinary tumor center of the cantonal hospital Baden, we are looking for a reinforcement as The Psychiatric Services Aargau AG (PDAG) is responsible for the high-quality medical, therapeutic and nursing treatment and care of mentally ill people in the Canton of Aargau. They employ around 1,600 people in over 50 professions. Clinic for Aging and Neuropsychiatry (KAN) 5404 Baden Workload: 70 % 70 %

Over the area

The Center for Consiliar and Liaison Psychiatry and Psychosomatics (Director Dr. med.) Rafael Meyer is part of the Clinic for Consiliary, Aging and Neuropsychiatry (Clinical Director Dr. med. Rafael Meyer MHA). It includes an ambulatorium in Dattwil as well as the psychiatric-psychosomatic consultancy and liaison service for acute hospitals, rehabilitation clinics and long-term facilities for people with mental impairments. The center also offers various special consultation hours (e.g. Memory Clinic, Gynecosychiatry, Obesity, Psychooncology) with professional proximity to other medical disciplines. The centre is SIWF-approved training centre for psychiatry and psychotherapy, old-age psychiatry as well as for consiliar and liaison psychiatry.
