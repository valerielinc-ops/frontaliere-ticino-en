The Position Job description

As an Engineering Manager you will focus on capabilities development of your team members that align with the function’s vision. You will encourage your team to self-define their career journey and seek ways to enable development opportunities in the direction of the journey. You will support them in removing roadblocks and by setting focus in their performance management. You make sure to develop your team with the right skills, healthy dynamics and a sustainable pace. You support the software and/or service delivery as a leader setting focus, removing impediments, managing stakeholders and creating efficiencies in collaboration wherever possible.

Description of the area Workforce Enablement (WE) is the home for employee facing products, services, and experiences within RDT. We deliver digital tools, drive adoption, and provide the support necessary to enable the entire workforce, wherever they are, every single day.

The Team Lead Data Foundations & Governance is part of Workforce Insights & Oversight and, together with the team, is accountable for establishing and maintaining strong, AI-ready data foundations that ensure data is trusted, secure, and fit for purpose. The role defines and operates data governance frameworks, standards, and policies to ensure consistency and control across the data lifecycle. It leads data quality management, master data practices, and metadata management to maintain integrity and reliability. The role ensures compliance with privacy, security, and regulatory requirements, and provides the structures and controls that enable sustainable and scalable data use across the organization. This role will be essential in enabling the next AI S-curve by helping build a connected data fabric and enhancing the organization’s data foundations with rich business context, interoperability, and discoverability. By ensuring data is structured, governed, and accessible in ways that support intelligent automation and decision-making, the role creates the foundation required for an increasingly agentic AI world, where AI systems and autonomous agents can reliably reason, act, and generate value at scale.

This RDT leader is accountable for the people, budget, technologies, and services required to deliver a high quality offering for employees.
