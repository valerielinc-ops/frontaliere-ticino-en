Full description

As Assistant Tissue Development H/F (Internship), you will assist the Tissue Development and Eco-design Manager on development and testing of collection fabrics. You will follow the development of fabrics, jersey, lining throughout the collection development process. ## Responsibilities

  • Assist the team throughout the entire process, from initial research to final production
  • Contribute to the selection and preparation of fabric samples
  • Participate in sending technical specifications to suppliers and follow-up
  • Collaborate on creating color palettes and illustrating fabrics for collections
  • Help validate lab dips
  • Participate in researching alternative and eco-friendly solutions for our materials
  • Be in contact with our internal laboratory for preparing materials for testing and occasionally, for performing tests
  • Pilot the collection of certification and regulatory compliance information
  • Classify and set up our tissue library ## Requirements
  • Be a final-year student in textile engineering or fashion school
  • Have knowledge and appreciation for materials (fabrics, jerseys, lining)
  • Be curious, organized, and have excellent interpersonal skills
  • Current English written and spoken mandatory
  • Good proficiency in computer tools (MS Office minimum)
  • Knowledge of a PLM would be a plus ## What we offer
  • ## Contact Based at our Annecy office starting from September 2026
