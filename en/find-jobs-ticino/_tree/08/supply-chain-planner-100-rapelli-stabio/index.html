Full description

The resource will have the responsibility to define, monitor and optimize the production program of semi-finished products and finished products, ensuring an efficient balance between demand, production capacity and availability of materials.

Liability

  • Define the production program for semi-finished products and finished products with the aim of meeting demand and optimizing costs and levels of stocks.
  • Check with the office to check the availability of raw materials and components necessary to ensure the feasibility of the plan.
  • To set up a production programme in the short, medium and long term, adapting it to changes in expected demand, production capacity and company production strategy.
  • Translate ricelets into distinct production, set or define production cycles and management and launch logics of the MRP.
  • To monitor work orders and to analyse possible deviations from the standards, proposing and implementing improvement actions.
  • Manage security stock levels and monitor material rotation.
  • Evaluate risks related to possible inefficiencies of production flows and develop efficientation strategies.

Requirements

  • Bachelor in Management Engineering or equivalent title.
  • Previous experience of at least 3 years in production planning and in the end of the year.
  • Obtima knowledge of IT systems, with particular reference to SAP (planning modules and the Sigmund)
  • Ottima knowledge of English (German)
  • Analytical skills, problem-solving orientation and proactivity in proposing improvement solutions.
  • Excellent communication skills and aptitude for work in teams.

Soft Skills

  • Effective communication and work-skills in teams.
  • Installation and attention to detail.
  • Flexibility and ability to work under pressure.
