Full description

For our new Tertianum Le Rolliet establishment in Plan-les-Ouates (GE), which will open in April 2026, we are looking for a

100% nurse in charge of care unit (IRUS)

Your role

Under the responsibility of the Chief Nurse, you are a reference person close to the field and the teams. You ensure the smooth running of day-to-day care, support employees in their practices and help ensure the quality and safety of the support of residents.

You also ensure rigorous follow-up of care records and actively participate in the implementation and improvement of service processes.

Your main responsibilities

  • Participate in the construction, implementation and continuous improvement of service processes in the context of the establishment's opening.
  • Organize and plan field work in conjunction with the Chief Nurse in compliance with labour legislation, the CLC EMS Geneva and the Tertianum Guidelines.
  • Ensure a close presence with the teams and support the collaborators in their daily lives. Support and coach them so that they can build on clear processes, good practices and common benchmarks to act with confidence and autonomy.
  • Ensure quality and continuity of care documentation and support teams in good traceability practices.
  • To be a relay between the field and the chief nurse: to identify the needs of the teams, to trace the relevant information and to contribute to the search for suitable solutions.
  • Support teams in assessing and managing clinical situations, including in complex situations, by promoting secure, coherent and interdisciplinary management.
  • Participate in the reception, integration and development of skills of new employees, apprentices and trainees.
  • Contribute to interdisciplinary work and maintain trust with residents, their relatives and other sectors.
  • To ensure the responsibilities entrusted to you by the Chief Nurse and to support him/her in the management of the service, in particular in the event of his/her absence.

Your profile

  • ES/HES nursing degree or equivalent training recognised by the Swiss Red Cross.
  • Confirmed experience in caring for the elderly; experience in EMS, an asset.
  • Experience in a reference or coaching function, an asset.
  • Taste marked for the field, proximity to the teams and ability to accompany with kindness and demand.
  • Rigor in the management and documentation of care records.
  • Good organizational, communication and communication skills.
  • Sensitivity to interdisciplinary work and continuous improvement.
  • Empathy, listening and ease with residents, relatives and employees.
  • Very good command of French, both oral and written.
