Full description

Post "Assistant Learning in Community Health and Care SWC, RFSM Marsens" at RFSM - Fribourg mental health network, Marsens (FR). The RFSM is the mental health centre of the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions.

Who are we?

The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health.

Your missions

  • The training takes place over 3 years, starting on 01.08.2027

  • Organization of daily life
  • Stewardship
  • Administration and logistics
