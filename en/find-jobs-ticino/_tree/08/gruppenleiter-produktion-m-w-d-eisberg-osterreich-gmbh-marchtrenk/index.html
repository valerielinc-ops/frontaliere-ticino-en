Full description

As group management in production at Eisberg, you bring structure, quality and teamwork together – together to success! This position is full-time. Tasks:

  • Organization and management of production lines as well as employee allocation and break planning
  • Ensuring timely production in compliance with quality, hygiene, safety and allergen standards
  • Professional leadership, support and training of employees as well as conducting team meetings
  • Active participation in the production lines as well as rapid reaction in the event of disruptions and problems
  • Implementation of quality controls, product approvals and correct documentation including CSB bookings
  • Cooperation in improvement measures to optimize processes, order, safety and efficiency in production Requirements:
  • Several years of professional experience in production, ideally in the food sector
  • Initial management experience or pronounced experience in the management of employees
  • High understanding of quality and safety
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Resilient, team-oriented personality with hands-on mentality
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Two-shift operation from Monday to Saturday (5-day week)
