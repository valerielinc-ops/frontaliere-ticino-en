Full description

Start on 1 February 2027 as Head: in Care Development 40 - 80 %

The Department of Care and Specialist Support designs care at the Cantonal Hospital Graubünden in a future-oriented and evidence-based manner. In close cooperation with care experts, care management and interprofessional partners, you will continuously develop the quality of care. In this function, you actively shape the strategic care development together with the management of the Department of Care and Specialist Support, as well as the nursing cadre conference.

Do you want to rethink care, promote development and make a difference? Then come to us – and by the way: You can also take over the job with us in job sharing.

These are your tasks

  • You strategically develop the development of care and lead cross-divisional projects and quality processes
  • You design, implement and evaluate evidence-based care tools, treatment concepts and standards
  • You lead and control nursing, specialist and topic expert, advise nursing management and nursing staff and promote further development and knowledge transfer
  • You lead the nursing expert:innengroup and the committee nursing quality, work in interprofessional committees and represent the nursing experts:innen in the nursing cadre conference

You bring that with you

  • A degree as a qualified nursing specialist HF/FH as well as a Master of Science in Nursing or a comparable nursing master’s degree or PhD
  • Several years of professional experience in the Swiss healthcare sector with in-depth knowledge in nursing development, quality of care, evidence-based practice and research application
  • Experience in the independent management of complex projects as well as ideally in the professional management or control of experts: inside and interprofessional groups
  • A strategically thinking and communicative personality with experience in the conception and implementation of workshops and trainings

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!

And that's what we offer you.

  • Career and family: As a flexible employer, we promote different work models so that you can optimally combine work and family for yourself. We also offer you and your family a wide range of support services.
  • Holidays / loyalty leave: The annual holiday entitlement with us is at least 25 days. Long-term employees: insiders are rewarded with an additional loyalty leave.
  • Flexible working hours / Home Office / Part-time work: If the company allows it, working hours and expenses can be flexibly tailored to your current living situation.
  • Gastronomic offer: All four locations of the Cantonal Hospital Graubünden offer a wide range of catering options. With us you can eat varied, balanced and healthy.
  • Health: The health of our employees: inside is important to us: The cantonal hospital Graubünden was awarded the quality label Friendy Work Space in November 2022.
  • Accommodation for staff: For our employees, interns and apprentices, we provide staff rooms and apartments at a discount as far as possible.
  • Social benefits: In addition to the legally prescribed benefits, we offer you supplementary and generous non-compulsory benefits (such as 100% wage continuation, waiver of coordination deduction at PK).
  • Further education and personal development: We continuously invest in the education, training and further education of our employees. With us you will be supported, motivated, challenged and promoted.
  • Our values: The excellent quality of our services and the health and satisfaction of our employees are equally our focus. An open, appreciative corporate culture, diversity, tolerance and sustainability are just as much an integral part of our philosophy as progressive employment conditions and diverse development opportunities.
