Full description

Finance Directorate

Controlling plays a central role within the University Psychiatric Center (UPZ) Bern and is responsible for the financial management and support of clinics and directorates. For our dynamic and committed team, we are looking for an analytical personality who enjoys recognizing financial contexts, developing processes and actively shaping economic governance. In this versatile function, you contribute to the periodic financial statements as well as budget and financial planning, create meaningful reporting and analyze business key figures. You advise the clinics and directorates on financial issues, identify potential for optimization and prepare well-founded decision-making principles. In this way, you make an important contribution to ensuring the profitability and the further development of controlling in the UPZ.

Your tasks

  • You are jointly responsible for the timely execution of the periodic financial statements, including the necessary deferral entries
  • You create and update internal and external reporting, projections, forecasts and statistics and support the analysis and interpretation of the results
  • You support the clinics and directorates in financial management and advise them on business issues
  • You actively participate in the budgeting and planning process and ensure its timely implementation according to the requirements of the management
  • You carry out systematic evaluations of the cost centre and cost carrier calculation and support the identification of optimization potential in the processes and services of the UPZ
  • You contribute to compliance with legal and internal requirements as well as the applicable accounting standards
  • You will contribute to the further development of controlling and help to establish it as an effective management and control instrument for ensuring economic efficiency
  • You work in projects and actively contribute your business expertise

Your profile

  • You have a higher business education (e.g. university or university), ideally with a focus on controlling or finance
  • You bring several years of experience in controlling or finance - preferably in healthcare, but this is not mandatory
  • You are well versed in dealing with controlling tools as well as with relevant legal regulations
  • You have strong skills in the analysis and evaluation of large amounts of data and use appropriate statistical methods as well as tools for data processing and interpretation safely.
  • Your analytical thinking skills and ability to explain complex issues in an understandable way
  • You work structured, have a high degree of initiative and are a team player

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
