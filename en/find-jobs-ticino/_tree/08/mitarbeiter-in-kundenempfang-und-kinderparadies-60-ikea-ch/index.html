Full description

Who you are

We are looking for employees in customer service who enjoy standing by our customers with advice and deeds and offering them the best possible shopping experience. Thanks to you, our customers will not only visit IKEA with a smile, they will also leave our stores with a smile and look forward to coming back.

  • You have a strong service orientation, a friendly manner and a good feeling for the wishes of customers.
  • You always have an answer to requests and a solution to any problem – no matter how stressful the situation may be.
  • You have a flair for dealing with people and enjoy offering our customers the best possible shopping experience.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing and are confident in dealing with the PC.
  • You are numerical, can handle money well and feel comfortable on the computer.
  • You have a passion for home furnishings and the IKEA brand and look forward to getting to know the IKEA product range and service options in detail.
  • You feel safe in dealing with the English language, so that you can always be addressed by international customers.

You are responsible for

  • Advise and look after customers and always meet them with a friendly smile in the work areas described below:
  • In the entrance area: Welcome customers to ensure they feel welcome and well-informed right from the start, if necessary, to the right contact person.
  • In Småland: Ensure that the children aged 3 to 10 are in good hands.
  • In exchange: Based on our internal guidelines, find fast, uncomplicated and customer-oriented solutions for all challenges.
  • In the goods delivery: control and handover of the purchased goods to our customers and ensure that the waiting times are as short as possible.
  • Taking over the goods from the logistics department and checking their correctness.
  • Handing over the rental keys for our rental cars to our customers, checking and documenting them.
  • Every day anew prove that service can be fast and friendly and say goodbye to customers so that they want to come back: with a smile.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Basel?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
