Full description

Description of work EMIL EGGER SA is one of the leading Swiss logistics companies and offers its customers a full range of services in the areas of general freight, heavy transport and warehouse logistics. About 800 employees currently work in 11 locations for renowned customers in industry, crafts and commerce. Our heavy logistics department includes gruting services, special transport, industrial relocations and the rental of lifting platforms.

Tasks

  • Execution of special demanding transport of all types in semi-trailer
  • Delivery of various goods and construction machinery
  • Loading and unloading of goods using a crane or lift
  • Arrests of goods and control of loading
  • Compliance with instructions, delivery times and safety standards
  • Maintenance of vehicles (washing, lubrication, etc.).

Your profile

  • Experience in semi-trailer transport (exceptional transport is an asset)
  • EC driving licence
  • Up-to-date OACP courses
  • ADR permit (one plus)
  • Flexible and dynamic
  • Good stress resistance

Assets

  • Interesting and forward-looking activities in a dynamic company
  • Different tasks in an exciting environment
  • Modern vehicle fleet
  • Family business culture
  • Pay in line with responsibilities
