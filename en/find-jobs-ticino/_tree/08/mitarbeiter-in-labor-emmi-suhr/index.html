Full description

Emmi is all about quality – and that’s where you start. At our location, you work in the laboratory directly on the basis of our products and make a decisive contribution to ensuring that our milk products meet the highest standards. In this versatile function you are in the middle of it instead of just there: you analyze, check and make sure that our quality is right at all times. You work closely in the team, take responsibility and bring your care and expertise to bear on a daily basis.

You can do that.

  • You will become part of a dedicated team and perform exciting chemical, physical and microbiological analyses.
  • Independently you process the daily sample input, check the condition of the samples and record them conscientiously in our system.
  • You support in the development and improvement of our processes and products.
  • The care and calibration of laboratory equipment are also part of your tasks to ensure always accurate and reliable results.

Why do you succeed?

  • You have completed training as a dairy technologist:in, laboratory assistant:in or comparable qualification and have practical experience in the food sector or laboratory.
  • An independent and careful working method as well as your high sense of responsibility and quality characterize you.
  • Knowledge of test equipment monitoring or quality management is advantageous.
  • Technical understanding and interest in measurement and testing technology.
  • You are flexible and ready to work in a 2-shift model. Dealing with MS Office and SAP is a matter of course for you.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Aargau's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
