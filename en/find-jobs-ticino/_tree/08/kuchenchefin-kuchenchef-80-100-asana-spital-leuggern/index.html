Full description

Asana Spital Denuggern is the innovative health centre in Zurzibiet. In a family atmosphere, we guarantee the high-quality basic medical care of the region. We are a family-friendly employer with progressive employment conditions for around 450 employees. As of January 1, 2027 or by appointment, we are looking for a committed and organizational personality who leads, develops and actively shapes our kitchen with passion.

Departmental Description

Our hospital kitchen prepares approx. 400 meals for patients, residents, employees and guests. In the style of Mediterranean cuisine, we produce several different menus every day. We pay attention to the various diet forms. In addition, we supply our cafeteria with hot and cold food as well as pastries and patisserie and offer a meal service.
