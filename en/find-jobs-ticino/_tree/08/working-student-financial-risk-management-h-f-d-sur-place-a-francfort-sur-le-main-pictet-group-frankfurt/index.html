Full description

Your team

Join our European headquarters of Bank Pictet & Cie (Europe) AG (BPAG) in Frankfurt am Main as we are seeking a motivated Working Student Financial Risk Management (m/f/d) to support our strategic initiatives and daily business exclusively on site in Frankfurt am Main.

BPAG is a leading financial institution with a strong presence across Europe, operating branches in Luxembourg, Spain, Monaco, France, Italy and the UK. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to impactful projects within a collaborative, international environment at the heart of our Frankfurt office.

If you are passionate about Financial Risk Management and eager to make a difference in a prestigious, pan-European organization, we look forward to your application.

Your role

Learn about and support/contribute to

  • Execution and automation of control processes related to the Bank’s different activities.
  • Financial and non-financial risk modelling and stress testing.
  • Enhancement and automation of risk reporting.
  • Analyse on new risk metrics, new products/services, risk methodology enhancements and -implementations.

Your profile

  • Senior BSc or a MSc student in a preferably quantitative field of study e.g., Quantitative Economics, Quantitative Finance/Risk Management, Financial Technology, Applied Data Science, Computational Business Analytics, or equivalent.
  • Strong coding skills (e.g. Python, VBA).
  • Excellent knowledge of Microsoft Office suite.
  • Fluency in German or French next to English, would be a plus.
  • Strong analytical and communication skills.
  • Problem-solving mind-set and focussed on teamwork and collaboration.
  • Trustworthy and reliable.
  • You enjoy working in a multi-cultural and multi-lingual environment.

Note

At Pictet, you will find more than just a position—you will discover a unique opportunity to contribute to a distinguished private bank renowned for its tradition of independence, long-term thinking, partnership, responsibility and entrepreneurial spirit.

We invite you to join our engaged and collaborative team and look forward to welcoming you to Pictet.
