Lead Engineer OT-Engineering Buildings & Energy with Perspective Team Leadership

Coop Cooperative — Cheeks at Olten, SO, Svizzera

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Production / Technology
  • Location: Coop, Industriestrasse 17, 4612 Wangen bei Olten
  • Pensum: 80 - 100%

Tasks

  • You will strategically and operationally develop the area of OT-Engineering Buildings and Energy and significantly shape its technological orientation
  • You develop architectures, standards and technical foundations for building, energy and infrastructure systems
  • You are responsible for central systems, platforms and edge solutions for scalable OT and IoT application cases
  • You drive the development of the Coop Virtual Power Plant forward - including energy data consolidation from around 1300 locations as well as technical requirements for control energy and energy optimization
  • Define a modern tech stack and ensure that solutions are zero-touch provisioning and over-the-air update-ready
  • You work closely together with specialist areas, architecture, operations and security and prepare the setup and eventual management of the team.

Requirements

  • With a completed degree in engineering, computer science or a comparable education at bachelor or master level, you have a sound professional basis
  • You bring in-depth knowledge of computer science, system architecture, networks and technical solution development
  • You have already gained initial practical experience in the field of OT security and know the requirements of modern and secure system landscapes
  • You have experience in setting up, standardizing and further developing technical systems or platforms
  • In the field of tension between engineering, architecture, operations and security, you move confidently
  • You work in a structured, self-responsible and with high implementation power and convince with leadership potential and entrepreneurial thinking

Ideally, you will also bring this with you

  • Experience in OT, IoT, building automation, energy, infrastructure or industrial systems
  • Practice in the development of scalable edge or device-related architectures
  • Understanding central device management, monitoring and lifecycle management
  • Experience in defining technical standards and target architectures

  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, Attractive Conditions of Employment and Certified Equal Pay, Total Employment Contract (GAV), 18 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, savings plan at the pension fund
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses, talent management with a wide range of development opportunities
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription, free participation in various company runs
