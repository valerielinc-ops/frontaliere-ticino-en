ID: Category: Logistics - Spedition Region: Basel Reference: Scheduling International Land Transports 100% Basel Today in the middle of daily business. Maybe tomorrow in the forwarding management. For an established, owner-managed forwarding company in the Basel region, we are looking for an experienced personality who not only disposes of international truck traffic, but actively thinks and develops the business. We are not looking for a classic seller or someone who only passes on tasks. You work directly on the pulse of events, know the needs of the customer and recognize where a conversation can develop into a new assignment. At the same time, you can reliably keep an eye on drivers, shipments, dates, costs and documents. Therefore, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement Planning international land transport 100% - you do not only move freight. They move the business... Their tasks

  • They dispatch international land transports, record shipments in the forwarding system and carefully maintain customer, partner and address data. In daily contact with customers, drivers and transport partners, you ensure clear processes, reliable information and viable solutions
  • The TIMOCOM freight exchange is part of your daily working instrument. You procure freight space, check offers, calculate daily prices and support in the preparation of offers
  • An important part of your job is to look after existing customers. They should not simply remain an anonymous voice out of disposition, but be perceived as a competent and reliable contact person. At the same time, you enjoy winning additional shipments and building new customer relationships
  • They are familiar with the basic customs clearance mechanisms between Switzerland and the EU and know which documents are needed for a smooth settlement. There are also some traffic with Turkey
  • After familiarization, you create evaluations of shipment quantities, effort and yield and provide the management with sound decision-making basis. If necessary, you also support in billing and create invoices yourself If in the future your own fleet will be set up again, you will also take on organizational tasks around MFC, workshop and deployment dates as well as the monitoring of driving and rest times.

Your profile

  • They have several years of experience in the forwarding industry, preferably in the scheduling of international truck traffic. They know from their own practice what disposition means and can quickly take responsibility after a careful introduction
  • They work in a structured, economical and solution-oriented manner. Even at high speed, you keep an overview and act friendly, committed and assertive towards drivers, partners and customers.
  • You are not too sorry to be involved where it is necessary. In a manageable company, short distances, mutual support and the willingness to actually take on responsibility count.
  • We are explicitly not looking for a career starter, but an experienced personality with a hands-on mentality, entrepreneurial thinking and a secure sense of customer needs.

Your perspective

  • This position is deliberately not intended as a mere dispatch office. Those who prove themselves, build trust and advance the company technically and economically can gradually assume additional responsibility.
  • The long-term goal is to build up a personality for forwarding management who will reliably manage day-to-day business, relieve the burden on management and help develop new transport routes, customer relationships and partnerships. Therefore, it is not simply seeking reinforcement for disposition. Looking for a future key person
