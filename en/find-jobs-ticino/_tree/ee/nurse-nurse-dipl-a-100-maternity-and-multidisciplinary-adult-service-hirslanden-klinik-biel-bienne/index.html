As Switzerland's largest medical network, we apply high standards in our clinics, outpatient surgery centres and emergency services.

THE SCOPE OF ACTIONS

  • Care and care of women and newborns and gynaecological patients, focusing on clients' needs
  • Care and care of patients in multidisciplinary medicine and surgery, focusing on client needs

YOUR TIME

  • You play a central role in the personalized care and support of our patients in the maternity service as well as in the adult care service
  • In close cooperation with our accredited doctors and an engaged interprofessional team, you are actively involved in preventive, diagnostic and therapeutic measures.
  • You accompany our patients with professionalism, empathy and know-how throughout the care process – from childbirth to postnatal care to adult treatment
  • You work in pairs with a Nurse
  • You are enthusiastically involved in the training and supervision of learners on a daily basis, thus actively contributing to the evolution of our team.

TRAINING REQUIRED

  • You have a health care assistant degree (CFC)

PREVIOUS EXPERIENCE

  • Experiences in acute care and/or maternity hospitals are an asset

OTHER SKILLS AND KNOWLEDGE

  • You like to work as a team, you willingly take responsibility and you know how to guide and encourage others
  • Your keen sense of communication and empathy allow you to put patient needs at the centre of your action
  • You are flexible, stress-resistant and have a great sense of organization
  • You master German and/or French (minimum level B2) and are able to express yourself in the other language
  • Good knowledge of IT tools
