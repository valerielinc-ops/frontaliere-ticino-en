Your benefits at the USB

  • Attractive salary policy and exceptional social benefits
  • Discounts for shopping, sport and recreation
  • Support during relocation and apartment hunting
  • 25 vacation days, plus 5 flexible free days
  • Support in further training and education, plus career planning
  • Campus in the heart of Basel and the tri-border region
  • Comprehensive childcare options
  • Free-of-charge sport and fitness program

What you will be doing

  • Manufacturing of cell-based (primary human immune cells) medicinal products in a GMP facility
  • GMP-compliant documentation, including the accurate completion of production logs, batch records, deviation reports and more
  • Validation of new procedures and qualification of new equipment for GMP use
  • Material management for GMP manufacturing campaigns
  • Performing environmental monitoring of the cleanroom
  • Development of assays (viability, potency, identity, purity) for cell-based medicinal products

Your profile

  • Experience in cell culture of mammalian cells (preferably primary human immune cells) and standard analytical methods used in immunology and cell culture
  • A high degree of reliability, flexibility, a problem-solving attitude, the ability to work independently and excellent teamwork skills
  • Completed apprenticeship as a laboratory technician (EFZ) or a university degree in science (biological background)
  • Experience in the manufacturing of ATMPs according to GMP regulations
  • Motivation to work in a cleanroom environment and knowledge of GMP-compliant documentation
  • Excellent knowledge of German and English (written and spoken)

Benefits of this position

  • Participation in a broad spectrum of projects and tasks translating research into the manufacture of GMP-compliant medicinal products to be used in Phase I/II clinical trials
  • An interesting and challenging position within a dynamic, interdisciplinary team
