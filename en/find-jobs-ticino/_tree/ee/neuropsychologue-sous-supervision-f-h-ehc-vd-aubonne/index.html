Full description

The Memory Center located in Aubonne is dedicated to the evaluation, diagnosis and management of cognitive disorders in adults and the elderly (MCI, dementia syndromes, neurodegenerative pathologies). We favor a structured multidisciplinary approach, focused on clinical quality, close collaboration between professionals and respectful care of patients and their loved ones.

We are looking for a

Neuropsychologist under supervision (M/F) at 20-80%

Start of employment as soon as possible or to be agreed on a permanent or fixed-term contract (leave replacement)

Activity rate: to be defined (ideally 20–80%)

  • Organization of work: Freedom to organize schedules, within the opening days of the center (Monday to Friday) and presence on site required.
  • Complete infrastructure provided: Shared consultation booths, testing equipment, secretarial services, administrative management and invoicing support.
  • Financial terms to be discussed.

YOU OWN

  • University master's degree in psychology with specialization in neuropsychology or at the end of training
  • Specialist title in neuropsychology FSP an asset
  • Confirmed clinical experience in neuropsychology of adults and elderly subjects
  • Marked interest in cognitive disorders, neurodegenerative pathologies and support for loved ones
  • Ability to work in a network and in a multidisciplinary team
  • Autonomy, clinical rigor, sense of responsibility
  • Excellent interpersonal and communication skills

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Carrying out neuropsychological assessments in adults and the elderly and writing neuropsychological examination reports
  • Analysis, interpretation of results and writing of clinical reports
  • Diagnostic feedback to patients and their loved ones by integrating the detailed conclusions of the examination, making treatment proposals and ensuring follow-up
  • Implementation of neuropsychological monitoring and adapted cognitive support
  • Participate in the evaluation of new diagnostic and management tools
  • Close collaboration with partner doctors (neurologists, geriatricians, general practitioners), participation in the training of assistant doctors
  • Participation in clinical conferences and multidisciplinary meetings
  • Contribution to the training and supervision of psychologists or neuropsychologists in training
  • Ensure administrative management and invoicing of neuropsychological services

WE OFFER YOU

  • The opportunity to become a player in a stimulating, expanding institution capable of entrepreneurship.
  • A position in a human-sized establishment with a working atmosphere that values ​​exchange, kindness, proximity and teamwork.
  • Sports activities (running, discounts at various centers such as several fitness centers or Bains de Lavey), events for the children of employees, a company refreshment bar to share moments of conviviality on sunny days.

WE ARE

  • An institution comprising acute care and rehabilitation establishments, a private clinic, EMS, medical centers and hotlines spread across the La Côte region as well as the 1st school of clinical hypnosis dedicated to the healthcare sector in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A healthcare network allowing numerous opportunities for internal transfer according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees caring for patients and residents of the region, sharing values ​​of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment favoring the completion of projects.
  • A training company committed to training the next generation, with around 80 apprentices in 16 different professions, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
