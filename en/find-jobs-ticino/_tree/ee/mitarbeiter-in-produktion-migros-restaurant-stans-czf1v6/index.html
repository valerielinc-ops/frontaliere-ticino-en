Full description

Do you enjoy the preparation of food and contact with guests? Then you are exactly right with us. As an employee* in production, you prepare cold and warm food every day and provide an attractive presentation of our offer.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Restaurant, Migros Restaurant Community Gastronomy Pilatus Stans, Ennetbürgerstrasse, 6371 Stans

Mansioni

  • Preparation of cold and warm food
  • Customer service in compliance with the cash regulations and providing customer information
  • The Mise and Place
  • Miscellaneous cleaning work

Requirements

  • First professional experience beneficial: in gastronomy or as a cook/chef
  • Completed basic or further education: in gastronomy
  • A strong sense of quality and responsibility
  • Communicative and resilient personality with flexibility
  • Reliable and accurate working methods (including good HACCP knowledge)
  • German (very good knowledge)

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing.
