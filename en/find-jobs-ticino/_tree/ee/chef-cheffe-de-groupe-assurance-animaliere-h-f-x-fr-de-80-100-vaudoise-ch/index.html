Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. Epona is one of the Swiss leaders in animal insurance. We have been concerned about the health of all animals for over a century. Our values, our size, our financial strength and our independence are sure assets to offer you a top quality working environment. Since 1 January 2022, Epona has joined the Vaudoise Group, recognized as one of the best employers in the insurance sector. In order to strengthen our P&C Department, Animal Insurance Division, which provides our clients with high-level services in the management of benefits, we are looking for a Head/Group Head Animal Insurance . The various missions that await you

  • Drive, support and develop your team, fostering accountability, collaboration and positive collective dynamics.
  • Organize and coordinate the team's activities, allocate resources, set priorities and ensure the achievement of objectives.
  • Manage business activity by ensuring processing times, quality of services and client satisfaction.
  • Ensure the management, monitoring and coordination of complex files, while providing your expertise and support to staff as necessary.
  • Work closely with the various stakeholders, particularly in the areas of training and quality, to ensure the development of skills and the quality of services.
  • Actively contribute to continuous improvement, identifying opportunities for optimization and proposing changes in processes, practices and organisation. What makes you unique
  • You have a proven experience in driving and managing a team, ideally in insurance or benefits.
  • Health knowledge (veterinary or human) is an asset.
  • You have strong organizational, coordination and prioritization capabilities, and are comfortable managing complex situations.
  • In French, you have an excellent mastery of German: spoken and written level C1. You are also organized, comfortable in task coordination and able to prioritize, accustomed to making complex situations simple, but also endowed with an excellent sense of customer service and relationship ease. We offer you
  • A healthy working environment in a mutual company certified "Great Place to Work".
  • Multiple social benefits, including flexibility in the organisation of work.
  • Team management and accountability.
  • A work within a dynamic and passionate team. We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
