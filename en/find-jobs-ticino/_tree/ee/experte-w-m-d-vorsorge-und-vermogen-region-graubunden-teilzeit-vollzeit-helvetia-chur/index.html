Pensum: 50-100% Workplace: Experience level: With professional experience FlexOffice: on-site

Setting new standards together and shaping the future means much more to us than simply ticking off tasks. Here, you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards, and bring the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand, and your ideas, initiative, and contribution count. What you achieve

  • Professional Expertise: As an experienced insurance consultant focusing on the pension and financial business, you know the industry and its products. You advise our clients, develop financial and retirement plans, support and empower our customer advisors, conduct training courses, and serve as the primary contact for pension-related questions
  • Market Analysis: You conduct market analyses and develop market cultivation strategies to identify and utilize potentials and opportunities in the pension and finance sector
  • Expansion of Pension and Finances: You are involved from the very beginning and play a decisive role in building and shaping the area of insurance and banking solutions (e.g., asset management, funds) within pension and wealth. Planning marketing measures, their implementation, and controlling also fall within your responsibility
  • Building Customer Relationships: You maintain close relationships with the customer advisors you support, guide them on their path to success, and actively expand your network within Helvetia and in the market
  • Teamwork: As a member of our Pension and Wealth Expert Community, you make a significant contribution to the professional and sales-related development of these topics at Helvetia

What you bring

  • Expertise: You have a sound education or degree as well as several years of professional experience in the financial services industry
  • Entrepreneurial Spirit: You are enthusiastic and inspiring, enjoy developmental work, and like collaborating with various stakeholders
  • Strong Communication: You are an excellent communicator and can convey complex matters in an understandable way
  • Analytical Thinking: You have a flair for numbers, think in an interconnected way, and possess strong organizational and planning skills
  • Customer Orientation: The satisfaction of your customers is close to your heart, and you do everything you can to meet their needs in the best possible way
