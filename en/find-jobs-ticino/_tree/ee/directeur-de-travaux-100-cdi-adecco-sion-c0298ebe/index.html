[Adecco]

Director of works 100% CDI (M/F/D)

Want to participate in various construction projects within a dynamic and collaborative structure? We are looking for a committed professional to ensure coordination and follow-up of construction projects, in close connection with the teams and partner companies

Responsibilities

  • Provide direction and follow-up of construction sites, from preparation to receipt of works
  • Develop bids, analyse bids and establish comparisons
  • Planning and coordinating the interventions of enterprises and the various professions
  • Ensure that costs, timelines, quality of delivery and standards are met
  • Organize worksite sessions and draft minutes
  • Maintain trusting relationships with agents, companies and partners
  • Ensuring effective communication with all project stakeholders

Profile

  • Training of construction manager or training deemed equivalent
  • Experience confirmed of at least 3 years in bid and site follow-up
  • German mother tongue with good knowledge of French (level B1\-B2\)
  • Organised, autonomous and with an excellent sense of responsibility
  • Team spirit, relationship ease and ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously

Benefits

  • A stable position in a dynamic architectural office in central Valais
  • Various projects with construction sites mainly located in the Haut\-Valais
  • A collaborative working environment with autonomy and responsibilities
  • Entry into office: to be agreed
