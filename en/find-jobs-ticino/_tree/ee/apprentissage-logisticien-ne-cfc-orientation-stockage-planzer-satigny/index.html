Full description

The movement has been in our DNA since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow up with us. Join our family and shape with us the future of transport and storage logistics.

What You Do

  • You receive the goods, the controls carefully and the records in the system
  • You put each item in its place, from screws to medicines, using the forklift
  • You prepare orders, packages, labels and prepares them for shipping
  • You ensure the optimal maintenance of the stock of equipment
  • Whenever a problem occurs in the warehouse, you mark and report it on time

Your benefits

  • Family Enterprise Culture: Respect, trust and fair play are important to us – we say "you" and we accompany you personally during your learning.
  • Vacations: In the first year, you have 8 weeks of vacation, in the second year 7 weeks and the next 6 weeks.
  • Events: During your training with us, you will participate in many events such as the reception day, the deepening seminar, the apprenticeship camp, the end of apprenticeship party, etc.
  • Financial support: At home, you get a fair salary with a thirteenth month. If you do a good job at school, in business classes and in the company, you can even get a bonus. Plus, we pay your tuition costs, we offer you a contribution to buy a laptop and we give you a half-rate subscription for the duration of your apprenticeship.
  • Permanent employment: At the end of your apprenticeship, you have a good chance of getting an indefinite contract.
  • Support during learning: You are helped to study independently in the company, you are regularly offered study hours with other apprentices and you are encouraged to take support courses.

Who are we? As a company, we attach great importance to collaboration and a good working atmosphere. This is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, availability and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis of a reliable and customer-oriented method of work.
