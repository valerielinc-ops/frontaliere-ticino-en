Coop Cooperative — Basel, BS, Svizzera

  • Job opening: by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Computer Science
  • Location: Coop, Thiersteinerallee 12, 4053 Basel
  • Pensum: 80-100 %

Tasks

  • You take on the professional responsibility for the further development of our order-to-cash and sales processes in the SAP SD environment
  • Together with the relevant stakeholders, you will further develop the roadmap and derive concrete goals and priorities for implementation from this.
  • You analyze end-to-end processes in detail, identify optimization potential and develop suitable solutions together with business and IT.
  • You take requirements, question them and translate them into clear, testable and actionable user stories, features and acceptance criteria
  • You bring different stakeholders from sales, marketing, e-commerce, logistics, finance and IT together and advance topics in a targeted manner even without disciplinary leadership responsibility

Requirements

  • Sound SAP knowledge in the module SD
  • Experience in the analysis and further development of end-to-end processes as well as in requirements management
  • Experience with agile working methods, product backlogs, user stories and prioritization
  • Ability to quickly grasp, structure and communicate complex technical and technical contexts
  • Enjoy taking responsibility, making decisions and actively advancing topics

  • Working time: 5 weeks holidays, flexible working time models
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses, support for external education and training
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
