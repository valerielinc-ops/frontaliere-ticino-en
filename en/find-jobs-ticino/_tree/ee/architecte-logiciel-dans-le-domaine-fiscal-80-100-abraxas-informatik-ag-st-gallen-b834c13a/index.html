Full description

Do you also think that there is no way around modern software architectures? Are you a doer and visionary who wants to actively participate and develop and advance our architecture topics? Then you've come to the right place! Actively support us in the implementation of planned technical projects. You will work closely with your team colleagues and work with them to ensure efficient and sustainable software solutions in our modern tax solutions. We are a motivated and dynamic software development team and are looking for YOU to support us on our journey to high-quality applications that our customers will love. Your Role

  • You will support our customers in the introduction of new software releases and the Scrum teams in the implementation of the micro-service architecture
  • You will keep an eye on technical debt, prioritize its implementation and drive technical improvements to our tax solutions
  • You take over the technical lead in projects
  • You drive architecture discussions, make suggestions and discuss solutions for our products
  • You attach importance to clean architecture documentation and also implement it
  • You will support the further development of our CI/CD pipeline and the monitoring of the application within a Kubernetes cluster
  • You are a technical contact person and consultant for the agile software development teams and convey complex content and concepts in an understandable way
  • You advise and analyze customer inquiries and provide technical customer support

Your profile

  • At least 5 years of experience as a Senior Software Engineer or Technical Lead
  • Degree in computer science or equivalent professional experience
  • Very good knowledge of the following technologies: Java, Spring Boot, Micro-Services, Maven
  • Cloud technology, service-oriented architecture, microservices, containers and related activation systems or tools such as Kubernetes are not foreign words to you
  • Experience with SQL databases (Oracle, Hibernate, Liquibase)
  • Experience in platforms and tools such as GitLab, Docker, Kubernetes is a plus
  • You are a team player and a communicative personality with a high level of initiative who likes to develop technically and methodically
  • Very good language skills in German and English

Your benefits

  • Holiday purchase (2 weeks per year)
  • 41-hour week with flexible working hours with a full-time workload
  • At least 25 days of vacation per year
  • 100% funding for further training in terms of funding or time
  • Free SBB Half-Fare travelcard for all employees
