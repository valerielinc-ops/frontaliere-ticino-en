Full description

Start your career as a Department Manager Supermarket: With our junior program, you will be optimally prepared directly in practice for a period of 6–12 months. Develop yourself from a generalist to a leader, optionally in the areas of Food/Non-Food or Cash/Services. Your application will be considered for all branches near your desired workplace.

Responsibilities

  • Customer-oriented management of a department
  • Responsibility for managing and developing department managers and employees, as well as promoting junior staff
  • Achieving revenue and cost targets
  • Ensuring defined processes in your department

Requirements

  • Completed basic or further training: in retail or an equivalent qualification
  • Willingness to take on responsibility and already initial leadership experience
  • Customer-oriented personality with a passion for sales
  • Good PC skills
  • Willingness to work flexible hours
  • Ideally more than 4 years of professional experience including initial leadership experience
  • German (business fluent)

What we offer

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks of maternity leave and 4 weeks of paternity leave
  • Mobility: Contribution to an annual public transport subscription, discount on fuel (Migrol), reduced annual fee for Mobility Carsharing, and entitlement to fleet discounts
  • Mobile subscription: Various discounts on mobile subscriptions
  • Migros Club School: Generous financial contribution to self-selected courses
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, Interhome, and events
  • Sports and fitness offers: Cost contributions to subscriptions for over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, and aquaparks
  • Targeted promotion: Promotion of our employees at all levels with targeted personnel development measures
  • Training and further education: Promotion of job-related training and further education
  • Autonomy & freedom: Varied field of activity with high personal responsibility
  • Childcare: Generous financial contribution to external childcare offers
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits ensure the financial security of employees in retirement
  • Social benefits: Attractive working conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus points: Multiple Cumulus points on Food and Non-Food items
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest rates on capital, or discounts on interest rates for newly concluded fixed mortgages
  • Employee offers: Preferential conditions for insurance and health insurance
