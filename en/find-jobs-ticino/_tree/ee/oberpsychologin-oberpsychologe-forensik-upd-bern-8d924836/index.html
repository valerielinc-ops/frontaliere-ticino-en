Full description

University Clinic for Forensic Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

At the Forensic Psychiatric Specialty Unit Etoine, as a senior psychologist, you can expect a special field of activity in which psychotherapeutic work, psychiatric treatment and forensic issues are closely linked. They assume the professional responsibility for the psychological-psychotherapeutic care of patients in deprivation of liberty with complex psychiatric clinical pictures and work closely with the interprofessional treatment team and the specialist disciplines of the acute care unit. In the Forensic Day Clinic (FTK) in Burgdorf, you design and take responsibility for the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment in a day-clinical setting. In addition to your clinical work, you assume professional leadership responsibility, supervision and conceptual tasks and actively participate in the further development of the treatment offer. Both offers are part of the forensic care triangle of the UPZ and open up an exceptionally versatile field of activity for you as a senior psychologist. They combine clinical and forensic expertise with leadership responsibility, actively shape innovative care offerings and work in an interprofessional environment that offers close professional exchange and diverse development opportunities. Looking for a special task in Bern and Burgdorf? We look forward to your application. Become part of our team and actively shape the forensic care in the canton of Bern.

Their tasks

  • Superpsychological responsibility – You are responsible for the psychological-psychotherapeutic care of Etoine and FTK in dual management with the senior physician
  • Patient care – They ensure evidence-based and guideline-compliant forensic-psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment
  • Supervision and training – They supervise assistant psychologists and assistant doctors and accompany their clinical activities and actively shape their professional and psychotherapeutic development
  • Concept & Offer Development – They actively design and develop the treatment and care concepts
  • Research – They support the further development of evidence-based forensic care and actively participate in research and quality development projects if required.

Your profile

  • Professional qualification – You have a university degree in clinical psychology and a completed psychotherapy education
  • Interest in forensics – You would like to receive in-depth training in the field of forensics and ideally bring your first prior knowledge
  • Leadership & Organization – You have leadership experience, organizational skill and enjoy leadership and further education responsibility
  • Collaboration – Teamwork, communication strength and enjoyment of interprofessional collaboration set you apart
  • Development – You are committed to the continuous development of the department
  • Language – They have very good knowledge of German, ideally also good oral knowledge of French. Further language skills are advantageous

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
