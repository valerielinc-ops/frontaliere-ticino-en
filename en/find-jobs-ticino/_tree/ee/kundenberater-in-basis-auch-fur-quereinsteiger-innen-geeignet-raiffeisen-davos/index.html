12.10.2026 Davos

Become part of our team!

Do you inspire people, think service-oriented and like to advise? Then we offer you the opportunity to enter the banking world or to further develop your consulting expertise. For our headquarters in Davos we are looking for a committed personality as a customer consultant: in base.

What do you expect?

  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous customer portfolio support
  • Ensuring simple, standardised advice on numbers, savings, investing and provisioning
  • Cooperation in market development measures and customer events
  • Co-creating a positive customer experience and supporting our reception team
  • Do you not have banking expertise yet? No problem. We accompany you with a structured introduction, internal specialist training and individual development opportunities to become a professional customer consultant.

What do you bring?

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship
  • Experience in a customer-oriented service sector (e.g. tourism, hotel, insurance, retail or administration)
  • Strong customer- and sales-oriented thinking and acting
  • Open, sympathetic and communicative personality
