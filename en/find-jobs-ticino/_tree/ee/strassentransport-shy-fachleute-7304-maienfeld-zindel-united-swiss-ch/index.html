Full description

Road transport professionals About the profession Our road transport experts are on the roads every day in the field of construction logistics. Their work includes everything from order planning to calculation and determination of the distance to delivery to customers. They select the route, calculate the driving, loading and unloading time and check the transport documents. They are responsible for professional and safe loading and also carry out cleaning, maintenance and minor repair work on the vehicle. Activities Preparation of transport

  • Organising transport
  • Check the condition of the vehicle
  • Supervise the loading of the vehicle and assist if necessary
  • Know and comply with safety regulations.

Transport and delivery of goods

  • Transport to place of delivery
  • Observe traffic conditions (traffic, weather)
  • use electronic devices such as route plans and tachographs
  • Goods unloaded and check if everything is in order
  • Complete formalities
  • comply with occupational safety and environmental regulations.

Maintenance vehicles

  • Clean the vehicle inside and outside
  • Perform inspections and minor repairs
  • Maintain tools and tools
  • Environmentally sound disposal of waste

You bring that with you The basic prerequisite for road transport professionals is a great interest in vehicles. They like to travel and act independently and responsibly. Technical understanding is just as essential as craftsmanship. Road transport professionals are on their own on the road. This can trigger a feeling of freedom, but at the same time it also means taking responsibility in transport, driving attentively and concentrated, taking into account other road users, providing first aid in the event of accidents and much more.
