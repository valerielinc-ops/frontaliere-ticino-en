Place of work: Amavita Rigimärt, Im Rigimärt, Siegwartstrasse 11, 6403 Küssnacht am Rigi

Advice is your strength? Then you are right with us. In our highly frequented Amavita Pharmacy Rigimärt in Küssnacht, the focus is on personal and high-quality advice. With Consulting Plus, you listen, recognize needs and find the right solution together with our customers. Look forward to a lot of variety and an international team.

You can do that.

  • You advise our customers competently, personally and with pleasure.
  • You listen carefully, recognize individual needs and find suitable solutions.
  • You use your expertise in everyday pharmacy and contribute to high-quality advice.
  • You take on your tasks independently and responsibly.
  • You are actively involved in our international team and support an appreciative cooperation.
  • You will continue to develop your expertise and use opportunities for professional and personal further education.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed your training as a pharmaceutical assistant EFZ.
  • You enjoy dealing with people and personal customer advice.
  • You are friendly, open and well-groomed.
  • You work independently and take responsibility for your tasks.
  • You are team-oriented, flexible and committed, even if there is a lot going on in the pharmacy.
  • You are curious, like to learn new things and want to develop professionally and personally.

We fill this position without external personnel service providers.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday

  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Extend competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities

About us

The largest pharmacy network Amavita is a company in the Galenica network and, with the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland, forms an essential pillar of health care close to home. We stand for a wide range, ranging from prescription and over-the-counter medicines to care products to natural remedies and natural cosmetics. We are also experts in dermatological skin care. Amavita pharmacies are the most represented in Western and Northern Switzerland. They are usually located in attractive and busy locations.
