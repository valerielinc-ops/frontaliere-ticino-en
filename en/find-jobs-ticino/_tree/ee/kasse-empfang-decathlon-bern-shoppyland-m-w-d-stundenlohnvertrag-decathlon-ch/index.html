Mission Your future playing field You will become part of the store in Bern , which opened in 2022 and has a team of 25 employees. Your area at reception/checkout is at the heart of our local strategy. And now to your job: Are you looking for a dynamic job where no two days are the same? Would you like to share your passion for sport and offer every customer who enters the store an unforgettable experience? Do you love action, being active on the sales floor and teamwork? Then the position as a sports advisor in sales is exactly right for you. Why?

  • You are at the heart of the action: Your day-to-day work takes place 100% on the sales floor. You are the face of Decathlon. Whether you welcome beginners or advise experts, you share your passion and build a connection.
  • You actively help shape the retail of tomorrow: You are not content with selling a product from the shelf. You offer a sustainable omnichannel experience: digital orders, equipment rental, buyback of old products (Second Life)... You cater to all needs.
  • You have autonomy and influence: You are responsible for your sport! You ensure that your department looks appealing, manage your inventory and organize your sales campaigns. Your efficiency is directly reflected in your customers' satisfaction. What you will do every day: Your tasks
  • Omnichannel advice and sales: You welcome your customers like guests. You advise them, close sales (in person or through our digital tools), offer our loyalty program and handle checkout.
  • Commercial drive / merchandising: You place products according to the seasons and merchandising guidelines, take care of the "facing" so that your department is always impeccable, and highlight the bestsellers and new arrivals of the season.
  • Availability and logistics: You pitch in! From receiving deliveries to stocking the shelves, you ensure that your inventory is accurate and your products are quickly available. You also prepare web orders.
  • Circular economy and services: You are an ambassador for our new models: You manage the buyback of used goods and promote our rental solutions to extend the lifespan of our products.
  • Occasional assignments: Depending on your interests and development, you can take on tasks as the point of contact for a specific topic, act as a trainer for your colleagues, or even help with shift management at the store. This job is NOT for you if...
  • You would rather sit quietly behind a screen all day.
  • You do not like approaching others spontaneously.
  • You think that unpacking pallets, stocking shelves and handling merchandise are "not your problem".
  • You are not prepared to see 10'000 steps on your watch at the end of the day.
  • You are looking for fixed, regular working hours: Here, we live to the rhythm of sport and our schedules adapt to the activity.
  • You do not want to work on Saturdays. Profile What we expect from you:
  • You have excellent interpersonal skills. Customer satisfaction is your compass.
  • You are brave, curious, and always eager to learn.
  • You are organized, efficient, and know how to set priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • You are a sports enthusiast and an active person: That is the company's DNA!
  • You have perfect command of German. Good English/French skills are a real plus. What we offer you:
  • A flexible and secure hourly wage contract: As the name suggests, you will be paid by the hour. In other words: The more you work, the higher your salary! To provide you with security, we guarantee you a monthly minimum in any case (the workload agreed in the contract, which varies between 20 % and 50 %). During peak periods, we can offer you additional hours. No need to panic about your schedule: We will always let you know at least 48 hours in advance, and this will only happen if your availability allows it!
  • Hourly wage: The wage is 25 CHF gross per hour. In addition, compensation is provided for public holidays, vacation, as well as the 13. Monthly salary (if you have a permanent hourly wage contract). On each 15. of the month, you will be based on the hours worked in the previous month (between the 16. of the previous month and the 15. paid for the current month).
  • Employee shares: Once a year, you can become a shareholder in the company. This is an excellent investment, as the share's value has never been negative since Decathlon was founded 50 years ago!
  • Passion for sport: We support your sporting activity! After 1 month of service, benefit from a 25 % discount on your Decathlon purchases.
  • Training: You will take part in training courses to improve your technical knowledge of products, your sales techniques, or your interpersonal communication. Your career: With us, career development is not a myth You start with us in the store? You start as a salesperson? You can develop into a department manager, take on a leadership role as an Operations Manager, and even become a Store Manager! We have dozens of stories like his. EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
