Full description

Your mission

You love sales and advise customers with passion. You are thrilled to take on responsibility, learn new things and actively shape the development of one of the largest digital organizations in Switzerland. In this role, you are in daily contact with our customers, find suitable solutions and contribute significantly to the success of SMG with your sales talent.

Specifically, you will

  • Proactively advise our customers and accompany them in everyday life - personally on site, remotely or by phone
  • Recognize customer needs and offer and sell suitable solutions
  • Check incoming customer inquiries and make initial assessments
  • Actively develop existing customers and provide long-term support
  • Visit existing and potential customers and establish and maintain sustainable business relationships
  • Create offers, prepare contracts and accompany the entire contract process
  • Create reports and analyses for your area of responsibility and derive concrete fields of action from them
  • Plan presentations and carry them out with customers in order to convey our products and solutions convincingly
  • Actively shape the expansion of the business area and contribute your own ideas
  • Attend customer events to show presence and promote exchange

Qualificates

You bring that with you

  • Completed commercial or equivalent training
  • Two to three years of sales experience, for example in account management or sales management
  • Flowing knowledge in Swiss German and English
  • Enjoying active customer contact and a proactive, optimistic and team-oriented way of working
  • Strong communication skills
  • Secure handling of Microsoft Office, Google Workspace and CRM systems
  • Category B driving licence

Informazioni aggiuntive

This is what we offer you

  • 6 weeks holiday
  • 40-hour week
  • 18 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. It also applies to adoption.
  • Flexible working models (work-life balance is very important to us)
  • Access to mental health, well-being and counselling platforms
  • Half-tax subscription for public transport and 1st class travel by train between SMG locations in Switzerland

About us

SMG Swiss Marketplace Group AG is a pioneering network of online marketplaces and an innovative digital company that simplifies people’s lives with future-oriented products.

SMG Swiss Marketplace Group AG offers customers the best tools to help them make important life decisions. The portfolio includes real estate (ImmoScout24, Homegate, Flatfox, CASASOFT, IAZI), automobiles (AutoScout24, MotoScout24), general marketplaces (Ricardo, tutti, anibis) and finance & insurance (FinanceScout24, moneyland.ch).

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
