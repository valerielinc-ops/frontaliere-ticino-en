Full description

SFS is a nationwide provider of screws, tools, fittings, building ranges, special parts and chemical-technical products throughout Switzerland. We offer our customers professional advice both online and with local sales branches. Numerous employees in the field service provide you with competent recommendations and solutions. This includes a time and market range. Your Tasks:

  • Manual and automatic order picking and storing of goods
  • Packaging of consignments for dispatch
  • Carrying out rolling inventory
  • Assistance with bottlenecks in other logistical workplaces
  • Responsibility for resolving investment disruptions in day-to-day business and ensuring trouble-free operation Your profile:
  • Completed logistical or craft basic training (ideally logistician/in EFZ)
  • Experience in the logistics sector as well as an SUVA-approved truck pass
  • Basic PC knowledge (SAP knowledge of advantage)
  • Operational readiness in the flexible shift model (early shift: 05.30 – 14.15 / late shift: 13.45 – 22.30)
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Physically robust and physically resilient
  • Team player with open communication, flexible working methods and independent mentality We offer:
  • Employment in a future-oriented and innovative company
  • Six weeks of holidays and the option of additional holiday purchase
  • Annual participation in success
  • Clearly structured training phase with cross-department onboarding program
  • Many more benefits: https://join.sfs.com/ch/de/benefits/
