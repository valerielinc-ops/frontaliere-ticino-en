As the AI & Process Intelligence Lead , you will fundamentally shift how Roche manages organizational complexity by treating it as a data problem. Moving away from manual, human-heavy process mapping workshops, you will lead the strategic development and deployment of our Agentic AI and Natural Language Processing (NLP) diagnostic engines.
