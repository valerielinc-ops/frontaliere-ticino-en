External Sales Advisor – Valais (100%)

Univerre Pro Uva SA with more than 40 years of excellence is a major national player in the world of glass packaging. Indistinguishable in the distribution of glass packaging, a national leader for the industrial washing of glass containers and for decoration on bottles and table glasses, we offer our customers and partners the best packaging solution for each product.

We are looking for a

One day in the role

  • As a sales advisor, you are a privileged contact of our customers. You identify their needs, advise them with expertise and accompany them in the search for suitable solutions, while developing sustainable business relationships.
  • You create a unique customer experience by bringing a true advisory dimension. You analyse the needs of your customers and accompany them with a global approach: from the choice of product to the different techniques of customisation and washing, until the realization of their project.
  • You recommend custom solutions, develop offers with the support of our Customer Care team, negotiate contracts and track orders throughout the customer relationship.
  • You actively contribute to the commercial success of our products and services by providing your expertise and technical support in the development of tailor-made solutions.

The keys to success

  • You benefit from a proven experience of at least 5 years in selling, consulting and managing a customer, as well as acquiring new customers.
  • You are particularly comfortable in customer contact and are passionate about sales, advice and human relations. Experience in industry and knowledge of glass or printing techniques are valuable assets.
  • You are committed to understanding the needs of your customers and contributing to the development of their business by providing them with relevant and tailored solutions.
  • With a true commercial sense, you are guided by results, appreciate contact with the field and show initiative and perseverance.
  • Your professionalism, sense of service and your communication, negotiation and conviction capabilities allow you to develop and maintain long-term customer loyalty.
  • A good knowledge of the wine, brewing, spirits, events and agriculture sectors is an asset.
  • In the German or French mother tongue, you have a good knowledge of the other language.

At Univerre you will find

  • A modern, innovative and forward-looking working environment
  • A varied position with great autonomy and real responsibilities
  • The opportunity to develop your ideas and take initiatives
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development
  • Various projects and customized solutions for clients from a wide variety of sectors
  • Modern working tools to accompany you every day
  • A human-sized company with short decision circuits
  • Development prospects within a developing enterprise
  • An exciting sector around a durable, forward-looking material: glass

Date of entry: immediately or to be agreed upon

If you are ready to invest and contribute to the development of our company, then do not hesitate to send us your complete application and your wage claims to the Human Resources Department.
