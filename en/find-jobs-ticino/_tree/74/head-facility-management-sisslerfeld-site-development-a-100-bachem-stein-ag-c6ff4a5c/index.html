Full description

The Bachem Group is investing in a new planned location in Sisslerfeld in northwestern Switzerland. This step is part of the long-term strategy to expand production capacity by the end of the decade. The new production site will play a central role in achieving these goals. The planning phase of the project offers you unique opportunities to actively shape the organization of the future.

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are always looking for highly qualified specialists. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated personality as Lead Facility Management - Sisslerfeld Site Development (a) 100%

Their tasks

  • Participation in the project and development phase of the new location in the area of Facility & Utility Management
  • Support for the qualification, validation and commissioning of technical installations and systems
  • Participation in the implementation of the CMMS system with focus on facility management processes
  • Cooperation in the development and optimization of processes, standards and strategies in facility management
  • Preparation and coordination of work instructions in cooperation with the relevant specialist areas
  • Support in establishing an efficient and future-oriented facility management on site
  • Review and technical approval of project-relevant documentation
  • Ensuring compliance with safety, environmental and quality standards as well as close cooperation with interdisciplinary stakeholders

Your profile

  • Completed middle or higher education in facility management or equivalent qualification, e.g. industrial engineering
  • Professional experience in the chemical or pharmaceutical industry as well as knowledge of GMP processes
  • Experience in the project environment as well as in the development and further development of new structures
  • Very good IT user knowledge (MS Office) and experience with CMMS systems; SAP PM knowledge is a plus
  • High initiative, resilience and sense of responsibility combined with a structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Analytical and networked thinking skills as well as strong organisational skills
  • Communication-strong, team-oriented and assertive personality with pleasure in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Motivation to actively participate in the development of a new strategic location and to shape facility management processes sustainably, with a view to a later management function

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of the pension fund contributions by Bachem as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our election savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
