Technical Collaborator – Constructions

In order to complete its construction team, the Commune of Conthey is contesting the position of Technical Collaborator – Constructions 100%

Main function

  • Ensure the full management of construction and construction police files, in close collaboration with the Construction Commission and the relevant cantonal services
  • Supporting citizens in their administrative processes
  • Contribute to the monitoring and coordination of construction projects
  • Maintain the Federal Register of Buildings and Housing (RegBL)

Profile and skills

  • Designist CFC in architecture / building or training deemed equivalent
  • Control of relevant laws, directives and procedures
  • Experience confirmed in a similar activity
  • Service, rigor, solution oriented
  • Relationship strength, ability to communicate and organize
  • Mastery of computer tools and French (oral and written)

Working and information conditions

  • Entry into office: 1 February 2027 or to be agreed
  • To be available occasionally early evening for sessions
  • To be conveyed

The specifications and additional information can be obtained from Human Resources via the rh(at) email address.

Your complete application file (motivation letter, CV, diplomas and certificates, work certificates, wage claims) will have to be received by November 20, 2026\.
