Full description

12.10.2026 St. Gallen

Join our team!

The Raiffeisen Pension and Vested Benefits Foundation processes and manages pension and vested benefits positions. To strengthen our team based in St. Gallen, we are looking for you as a flexible, service-oriented and communicative person from now on or by appointment. Do you like to support our Raiffeisen banks and our customers? Then apply with us.

What should I expect?

  • Participation in day-to-day business (control and approval of openings, postings and balancing of vested benefits and pension accounts pillar 3a)
  • Case assessments and verification of compliance with legal provisions
  • Written and telephone advice to internal and external customers
  • Correspondence with authorities and courts
  • Home office option (max. 2 days per week)

What we expect from you

  • Commercial training or similar training
  • Enthusiasm for numbers
  • Very precise and independent way of working, high commitment and reliability as well as networked thinking
  • Sense of responsibility and ability to work in a team
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of French or Italian (oral and written)
  • Expertise in the field of 2. and 3. Advantageous pillar

"As a fulcrum between the Raiffeisen banks, customers and institutions of the 2. and 3. Pillar, you will make an important contribution to a good retirement provision for our pensioners." Ralf Krentz Head of Pension & Vested Benefits Foundation Why Raiffeisen?

We offer flexible working models

The work-life balance of our employees is important to us. That is why we are creating the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile working, working from home and part-time work.

We cultivate a family environment

The cooperative values are our cornerstones: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our dealings with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We support diversity and equal opportunity

We promote the different perspectives, experiences and competencies in our teams. Because this diversity makes us special and contributes decisively to our success.

We offer collective profit sharing instead of individual bonuses

We focus on collective performance. That is why we offer collective profit-sharing instead of individual bonus payments. This promotes a healthy corporate culture in which overarching goals are pursued together.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training courses as well as cost sharing for external training courses.

We make "healthy living & working" part of the programme

With our holistic "healthy living & working" offer, we support our employees in finding and maintaining a healthy balance between work and private life in the long term.
