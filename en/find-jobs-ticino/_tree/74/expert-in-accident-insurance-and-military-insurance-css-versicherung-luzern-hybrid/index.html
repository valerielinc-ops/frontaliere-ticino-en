Full description

Complex healing costs cases are our everyday life: Whenever it is unclear which insurance pays, we come into play. We clarify who performs – OKP, UV, MV or IV – and ensure that everything runs correctly and fairly. You dive deep into dossiers from German and French Switzerland, check the cases and make well-founded decisions. You can expect an exciting job with a lot of personal responsibility, strong colleagues and top employment conditions.

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: You clarify cases of delimitation for accident and military insurance and make clear decisions.
  • In professional and medical matters, you represent our position vis-à-vis other social insurers.
  • You exchange information in writing and verbally with customers as well as social insurers and service providers.
  • You will recover incurred costs from the responsible insurers and book them directly in the system.
  • You maintain your cases in the system, update our professional databases and keep track of many dossiers.
  • With your know-how, you get involved in team rounds, discuss tricky cases and help to further develop our processes.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: Your profound professional experience in a similar role in health and/or accident insurance forms the foundation for your function as a specialist.
  • In German you express yourself very well – verbally and in writing.
  • Your paramedical or commercial basic education forms a solid basis.
  • Do you already bring a further education in the social security sector or are you in the middle of it? That would definitely be a plus!
  • Teamwork is important to you: You get involved, share your knowledge and at the same time independently lead cases.
  • You work carefully, think networked, stay calm even in complex situations and enjoy taking on responsibility.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
  • Enjoy your family life: Make time for your offspring: 3 weeks for fathers, for mothers up to 20 weeks. There are also generous child and education allowances.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
