Das können Sie bewegen

  • Investigate mechanisms of response and resistance to immune checkpoint blockade in solid tumors, with a focus on head and neck and esophagogastric cancers
  • Characterize the tumor immune microenvironment using spatial proteomics, multiplex imaging and spatial transcriptomics
  • Integrate tissue-based immune profiles with blood-based biomarkers, immune repertoires and clinical outcomes to identify markers for patient stratification and treatment response
  • Establish patient-derived 3D tumor models and explants and perform functional tumor–immune cell experiments to validate resistance mechanisms and therapeutic strategies
  • Take an active role in planning and conducting the project, analysing complex datasets, collaborating with clinical and scientific partners, and presenting and publishing your findings

Das bringen Sie mit

  • Master's degree in biology, biomedical sciences, biochemistry, molecular medicine, immunology, cancer biology or a related life-science discipline
  • Strong interest in translational cancer research, tumor immunology and modern spatial profiling technologies
  • Solid hands-on experience in mammalian cell culture and molecular or cellular biology, ideally in the context of tumor biology
  • Initiative, scientific curiosity and the ability to work independently and collaboratively in an interdisciplinary and international environment
  • Excellent English language skills; experience with primary human samples, flow cytometry, spatial technologies, 3D models, functional assays or quantitative data analysis using R/Python is an advantage

Benefits dieser Stelle

  • Work at the interface of experimental tumor immunology, clinical research and patient care
  • Access to state-of-the-art technologies and infrastructure for spatial profiling, flow cytometry, sequencing and functional tumor models
  • Close collaboration with physicians, bioinformaticians and basic and translational research groups
  • Cross-disciplinary training and substantial opportunities for scientific development, independence and project ownership
