What you should know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family company with 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the main pillars of the company. The focus of the activities is on the import of car brands, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare and accessories as well as the provision of financial services. Emil Frey IT Solutions AG provides various central ICT services for the Emil Frey Group and manages the server backend systems in two data centers together with the hybrid cloud infrastructure for approx. 10,000 users who work at over 100 locations in Europe.
