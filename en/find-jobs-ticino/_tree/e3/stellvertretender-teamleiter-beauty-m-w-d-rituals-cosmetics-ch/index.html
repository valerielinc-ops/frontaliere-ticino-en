Full description

Happiness can be found in the smallest of things. It is our passion to turn everyday routines into more meaningful moments and connect with customers and colleagues. At Rituals, we blend a thriving culture with top talent. Join our fast growing wellbeing brand and nurture an impactful career with us.

  • Collaboration with the Floor Manager: Plans the personnel assignments together, develops sales strategies and concepts to inspire the team and the clientele.
  • Wellness expertise: Answer complex questions about treatments such as Brain Massages and Breathing Bubbles and present our unique relaxation experience.
  • Operational responsibility: Take care of rosters, inventory control and cash management and thus ensure the smooth running of the store.
  • Team development & goal achievement: Inspire with your coaching, let people grow beyond themselves and turn sales goals into exciting challenges.

  • You have at least 3 years of experience in retail or customer service.
  • You bring flexibility – also for early, late shifts and weekends and have a valid work permit or residence permit (C, B, L or G permit) or a Swiss citizenship.
  • You can’t stand to sit still all day. You are really excited about the retail trade and love the hustle and bustle and variety.
  • You are results-oriented and naturally turn conversations into inspiring sales conversations.
  • You have strong social skills and can adjust to different personalities. You know how to motivate your team, collaborate with your store manager and win the customer base for you.
