Full description

The Office for Energy and Transport is the cantonal competence centre for energy and public transport issues. In the area of public transport, we are responsible for the strategic planning and the mutual coordination of all public transport in the Canton of Graubünden. We coordinate the public transport of trains and buses and are responsible for the planning, project planning and implementation of projects for future-oriented public transport. Good connections to the national and international rail network as well as regular and frequent connections in regional and agglomeration traffic are of central importance for the maintenance and further development of the life, tourism and economic area Graubünden We are looking for the Office for Energy and Transport at 1. September 2026 or by agreement a project manager/in public transport ordering procedure. Tasks

  • Implementation of the ordering procedure for regional public transport (RPV) for road transport services in the Canton of Graubünden
  • Implementation of tenders and responsibility for concluding target agreements with the transport companies
  • Technical assessment of applications for operating materials according to ARPV
  • Technical assessment of applications for funding
  • Supervision of the tariff associations in the Canton of Graubünden Requirements
  • University or university of applied sciences degree in business administration or transport planning direction
  • Interest in interdisciplinary cooperation, negotiating skills and skill in dealing with transport companies, authorities and associations
  • Experience in the independent handling of conceptual issues
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of public transport of advantage
  • Style-confident command of the German language in words and writing
  • Good knowledge in several cantonal languages of advantage
