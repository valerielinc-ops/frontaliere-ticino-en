Full description

Motivated by an apprenticeship as a production watchmaker? Ready to become a reference in your profession thanks to quality training in a leading company? Join us!

The production watchmaker assembles and precisely adjusts the different components of watch movements. Its role consists of carrying out adjustment operations to guarantee the proper functioning of mechanisms and watches. Furthermore, the production watchmaker carries out rigorous quality controls to ensure that the watches comply with established standards and corrects any defects.

  • Logical and methodical
  • Manual skill and practical sense
