· Job vacancies Place of work: Brig (partially home office possible) | Start of work: as of now or by agreement (temporary to 18 months) The FernUni Switzerland stands for completely online university courses and is accredited according to the HFKG. It combines flexible, location-independent distance learning part-time with an active, interdisciplinary research culture at Campus Brig. In five faculties – Law, Psychology, Economics, History and Mathematics & Computer Science – we have been shaping modern teaching, learning and research for more than 30 years and count over 2,700 students and around 550 employees. To reinforce research activities at the Faculty of Psychology, we seek a dedicated personality as: Research staff: in (20-70%) Location: Brig (partially home office possible) Start of work: from now on or by agreement (limited to 18 months) They conduct research at the interface of learning psychology, technology and human-machine interaction and investigate how artificial intelligence is changing learning and how AI can be used to promote sustainable learning. Their tasks

  • Planning, implementation and evaluation of empirical studies
  • Conceptual and/or technical development of our AI-supported learning tool
  • Participation in the communication of research results in science and the public (e.g. scientific articles, conference papers, reports, social media)

Your profile

  • Master's degree in Psychology or Cognitive Science
  • Interest in current developments in the field of artificial intelligence
  • Very good knowledge of data analysis and statistics with R
  • Experience with git and programming of web applications
  • Very good knowledge of English or German

We offer

  • Collaboration on an innovative AI project at the Teaching & Research Interface
  • Great creative freedom: You take on a responsible position in a dynamic environment with plenty of room for initiative and development.
  • Research-strong academic environment: You work in a dynamic university context with proven scientific expertise and interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Attractive employment conditions: You benefit from contemporary working conditions, generous social benefits and a wide range of training opportunities.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
