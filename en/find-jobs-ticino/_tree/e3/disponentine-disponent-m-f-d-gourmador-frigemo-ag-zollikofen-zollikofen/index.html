For our location in Zollikofen BE, we are looking for an organized, independent and reliable person to complete our dispatch team, starting immediately or by agreement. Marisa Borer HR Business Partner +41 58 434 08 02

These tasks excite you

  • Ensuring and monitoring optimal delivery readiness
  • Planning and dispatching our distribution routes using various IT systems
  • Operational management of our chauffeur team in teamwork within the dispatch team
  • Technical and operational responsibility for the maintenance and repairs of our fleet of 23 vehicles, as well as claims processing
  • Monitoring our chauffeurs’ compliance with the ARV (Working and Rest Time Regulation), as well as their training
  • You actively support our warehouse processes and, in the event of bottlenecks, also work as a chauffeur in the delivery area on an exceptional basis

We look forward to applications from candidates who fit this profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship
  • Preferably, you have practical experience as a chauffeur and hold a category C truck driver's licence as well as a CZV certificate of competence
  • You have good local knowledge of the Bern region and the Swiss Plateau
  • You are IT-savvy and willing to familiarize yourself with new systems
  • You have very good German language skills
  • You are flexible with regard to working hours
  • You appreciate a varied “all-rounder role” and enjoy working efficiently

Discover your benefits

  • Education and professional development: We invest in the development of our employees. A wide range of internal educational opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external professional development.
  • Attractive vacation policy: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. As of 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks, and from the 60. Year of age, even 7 weeks. In addition, it is possible to purchase additional vacation days.
  • Family time: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent enjoys 3 weeks of family time at full pay.
  • Insurance benefits: We provide our employees with above-average social security and pension benefits and offer special conditions on supplementary insurance policies with various health insurers for their entire family.
  • Benefits and discounts: Among other things, our employees benefit from discounts when shopping and refueling, credits for REKA money, and discounted mobile subscriptions for themselves and their families.
