Full description

Within TND-IA, this position is attached to Operations. As the Area Manager, you are leading the operational security of information systems with the team of seven specialists while maintaining a strong technical involvement. As a result, you support your team in resolving complex incidents, participate in periodicity and intervene in security events. In your management role, you develop the skills and organization of your team to strengthen the capabilities of operational security and the Security Operations Center (SOC). You organize the day before and conduct awareness-raising activities with DTN-IA and trades. In terms of governance and strategy, you are contributing to the definition, implementation and compliance of information systems security policies (ISSPs) and the deployment of the cybersecurity strategy. You ensure compliance with operational governance, participate in the Major Change Management Committee (MMC) and lead the priorities, performance indicators and objectives in your area. With your team, you define and implement security projects to strengthen the institution's cybersecurity posture. You participate in the "Security by Design" reviews of new projects, validate the connection of technical and biomedical equipment according to the validation process and contribute to the Incident Recovery Plan / Incident Continuity Plan program. You manage vulnerability management, monitor remediation actions, conduct security investigations, and organize audits and regular and appropriate slopests. You also guarantee the maintenance of cybersecurity platforms and services in operational conditions, including firewalls, proxy devices, authentication and authorisation mechanisms, preferred access management solutions, PKI, Vault, IAM, EDR, as well as information systems hardening and protection. At the head of SOC, you oversee incident detection and response activities using the EMIS. You organize the analysis and qualification of alerts, coordinate technical investigations and response to incidents, participate in crisis management and develop capabilities for detection, investigation and response to cyber threats. Finally, you are contributing to the design and evolution of secure architectures in collaboration with the DTN-IA teams and the Information Systems Security Officer.
