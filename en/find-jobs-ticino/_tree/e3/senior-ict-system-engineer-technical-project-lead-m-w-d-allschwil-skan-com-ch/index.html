Full description

Founded in 1968, SKAN is a pioneer in cleanroom equipment and the development of isolators for the pharmaceutical industry. Innovative products, tailor-made solutions and an effective service organisation have made SKAN a market leader and a key partner to industry and research laboratories. Overview For our ICT Engineering & Operations Team, we are looking for an experienced Senior ICT System Engineer / Technical Project Lead with in-depth expertise in Microsoft-based hybrid cloud infrastructures. In this role, you will actively contribute to the further development of our ICT landscape and take technical responsibility for challenging infrastructure and cloud projects. You will connect on-premises systems with Microsoft Azure and Microsoft 365 and drive topics such as cloud integration, Identity & Access Management, security, standardization, and automation. You will combine strong technical engineering expertise with project responsibility.

Responsibilities

  • Lead and technically coordinate ICT infrastructure and hybrid cloud projects from planning through implementation
  • Design, operate, and further develop our hybrid Microsoft infrastructure, including on-premises systems, Microsoft Azure, and Microsoft 365
  • Administer and further develop Microsoft Entra ID, Identity & Access Management (IAM), as well as PKI and certificate services, including MFA, Conditional Access, role and authorization concepts, and certificate lifecycle management
  • Further develop and modernize central Windows Server, Active Directory, and cloud services, including the gradual migration to hybrid and cloud-based architectures
  • Integrate remote locations into a standardized, secure, and scalable ICT infrastructure
  • Contribute to the design and further development of security, governance, backup, recovery, and data concepts
  • Standardize and automate operational and administrative processes, particularly using PowerShell
  • Ensure stable, secure, and high-performing operations and support troubleshooting, root cause analyses, and complex 2nd- and 3rd-level issues
  • Create technical documentation and collaborate with internal stakeholders, external partners, and suppliers

Hardskills

  • Degree in Computer Science, higher professional qualification, or a comparable qualification
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in ICT infrastructure, ideally including experience in technical project management
  • In-depth experience with Microsoft-based hybrid cloud infrastructures
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, and Microsoft Entra ID
  • Experience with Identity & Access Management, MFA, Conditional Access, RBAC, and hybrid identity scenarios
  • In-depth experience with Microsoft PKI / Active Directory Certificate Services (AD CS), certificate management, and certificate lifecycle management
  • Strong knowledge of Windows Server, Active Directory, and Group Policy (GPO)
  • Experience in integrating and modernizing on-premises and cloud infrastructures
  • Experience with cloud migration projects and the automation of operational and administrative tasks
  • In-depth knowledge of ITIL and practical experience with IT service management processes, particularly Incident, Problem, and Change Management, is required

Softskills

  • Strong communication skills and the ability to explain complex technical topics in a way that is appropriate for different audiences
  • Analytical, structured, and solution-oriented approach, combined with a high degree of initiative and a hands-on mindset
  • Ability to effectively combine technical engineering, operational responsibility, and project management
  • Strong team spirit, collaboration skills, and service orientation when working with different stakeholders
  • Ability to prepare, justify, and confidently communicate technical decisions
  • Openness to continuous learning and new cloud, security, automation, and AI technologies

We offer you

  • An open and collaborative corporate culture
  • Freedom to contribute and develop ideas
  • Opportunities to participate in sports and social activities
  • Professional development and continuous learning opportunities, especially at our SKAN Academy
  • Comprehensive and career-focused onboarding
  • Quick decision-making paths and flat hierarchies
  • Transparent and open communication
  • 5 weeks of vacation, with the option to take additional leave

Additional information We look forward to receiving your complete application documents (CV, diplomas and employment references) via our career page. For this position, we are focusing on direct applications and are not working with external agencies. Any dossiers submitted by agencies will not be processed. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Basel?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
