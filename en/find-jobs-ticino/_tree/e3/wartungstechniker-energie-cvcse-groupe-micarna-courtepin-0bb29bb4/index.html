Maintenance Technician Energy (CVCSE) — groupe Micarna

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

A leading producer of meat, poultry, eggs and fish and as a member of M-Industry, Micarna has set itself the goal of always being "just better". Do you share this vision? Join us and contribute to the success of Migros

Mansioni

  • Preventive and corrective maintenance of the site's energy facilities, including heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold production, boilers, compressors and controls
  • Monitoring of the condition and operating parameters of the installations, detection of anomalies, carrying out diagnostics and implementing appropriate corrective actions
  • Implementation of planned maintenance operations, technical checks and safety and performance tests to ensure reliability of equipment
  • Participation in improving plant performance and optimising energy consumption through monitoring of consumption indicators and records
  • Participation in the commissioning, adjustment and control of new equipment and coordination of specialized interventions or external partners where necessary
  • Application of internal procedures, WES instructions and regulatory requirements while contributing to continuous improvement of maintenance and operation processes

Requirements

  • 1-3 years: Experience in the maintenance of technical or energy installations; experience in the industrial, tertiary or on-site environment is an asset
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): CFC or technical training in industrial maintenance, electrical engineering, climate engineering, energy, thermal or automation
  • Good knowledge of multi-technical maintenance as well as HVAC, electrical, thermal or refrigeration facilities
  • Mastering the reading and interpretation of drawings, electrical diagrams and technical documents
  • Knowledge of safety rules and regulatory requirements for energy installations
  • Good analysis and strong diagnostic capabilities to effectively solve technical problems
  • Mastery of computer maintenance tools; knowledge of SAP PM is an asset
  • Independent, rigorous and responsive personality, enjoying teamwork and having a common sense of service and communication
  • You are willing and able to provide a picket service according to a rotation system, including a daily picket at 6am and 6pm, as well as a night picket from 6pm to 6am, insured over a full week, from Friday to Friday
  • French (Very good knowledge)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave / 8 weeks of paid paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Occupational planning: Full coverage from the Migros Pension Fund – with first-rate and attractive benefits
  • Restaurant: Balanced lunch/buffet at the staff restaurant for a maximum of CHF 7.-
  • Social benefits: 730 days of daily sickness benefit with full maintenance of net salary
  • Migros Bank: Migros offers higher interest rates on private accounts and investment accounts to its employees.
  • Migros Club School: Refund of 70% of course fees, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and culture: You benefit from discounted tickets for selected cultural events
  • Points: Cumulus points are doubled on food products and quintupled on non-food items.
  • Sport and fitness offers: Contribution to fitness subscriptions: maximum CHF 200.- per subscription per year
  • Training and continuing training: Attractive offer of training and further training
