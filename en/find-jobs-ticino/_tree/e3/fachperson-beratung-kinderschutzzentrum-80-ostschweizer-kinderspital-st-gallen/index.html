Full description

As of February 1, 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a specialist counselling child protection center (80%) to strengthen our team.

Their tasks

  • Support for coping with the multiple consequences of physical, psychological and sexual violence against children and adolescents by advising those affected, their parents, caregivers and specialists in compliance with the Victim Assistance Act
  • Crisis intervention in acute hazard situations
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with various disciplines and bodies

Your profile

  • Successful degree in social work, social education, psychology or curative education or equivalent education
  • At least three years of professional experience in a similar family counselling function
  • Ideally, systemic counselling training and/or expertise in victim assistance
  • Openness to reflection and further education
  • Interest in complex tasks
  • Resilient, resource-oriented and optimistic personality that can distinguish itself and position itself clearly and independently

We offer them

  • Responsibility because we trust in the competence of our employees
  • Perspectives, because we are constantly developing together with you
  • Knowledge because we promote internal and external training
  • Teamwork, because we know that we only achieve the goal together

Your work area Children and adolescents affected by violence and their environment receive advice and support at the Child Protection Centre (KSZ). Great emphasis is placed on support for finding your own solutions. Professionals and third parties can also contact the KSZ if it is reasonable to assume that children or adolescents are affected by violence. Through the children and youth emergency call, help seekers receive telephone or personal support on site - anonymously, confidentially and free of charge. The Parents Hotline “Active” supports parents if they are strongly challenged with parenting tasks in the short term and it is no longer possible to solve disputes without violence. The interdisciplinary interaction at the OKS helps in situations with suspicion or certainty of violence against children and adolescents. Networking and cooperation are central to child protection. In addition to the advisory services, the KSZ trains and sensitizes various target groups with a variety of training and prevention services.

Comprehensive information about the child protection center can be found here: www.kinderschutzzentrum.ch/en
