Full description

Portrait The school Herzogenbuchsee is a lively and future-oriented school in the heart of the Oberaargau. Around 760 children from Herzogenbuchsee and Niederönz will be taken from kindergarten to 6. Class accompanied by a dedicated and versatile team. We attach great importance to good cooperation, individual support and a lesson that inspires children. Our school invests specifically in modern learning spaces and contemporary digital infrastructure. A special working environment awaits you at the Niederönz site: a new schoolhouse, which will be occupied in the summer of 2026, with bright rooms, modern facilities and a wide range of possibilities for contemporary teaching.

Tasks

You take on a maternity representation in a 1st / 2nd class at the Niederönz location with a total of 14 – 18 lessons. Depending on your wishes and possibilities, the position with or without co-class management is possible. You will design a varied, child-friendly lesson in the subjects German, NMG, Technical Design, MGS as well as movement and sport. You accompany the children in their development, bring your own ideas and work closely with the class team. Your working days are preferably Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. The deputy is expected to last from the Christmas holidays to the summer holidays.

Requirements

You teach with joy, patience and enthusiasm in Cycle 1 and create a learning environment in which the children can feel comfortable and develop. You enjoy working in a team, bringing your own ideas and shaping everyday school life in a structured, appreciative and creative way. An open exchange and the individual promotion of the children are important to you. Whether you already have experience or are at the beginning of your career: We look forward to a committed teacher who wants to shape our school day.

We offer

  • A new schoolhouse in Niederönz, which was moved into in the summer of 2026, with bright, modern and contemporary learning rooms
  • Modern digital teaching options with interactive multifunction screens and a personal laptop
  • A flexible set of 14–18 lessons, with or without co-class leadership
  • A dedicated, helpful college and supportive collaboration in the class team
  • Space for own teaching ideas, creativity and varied forms of learning
  • Technical support by DaZ, IF, psychomotorik, speech therapy and school social work
  • A future-oriented school that invests in its infrastructure and the further development of teaching
  • Good accessibility with public transport

New schoolhouse, modern teaching facilities and a team that designs school together – we look forward to meeting you! The interviews are ongoing.
