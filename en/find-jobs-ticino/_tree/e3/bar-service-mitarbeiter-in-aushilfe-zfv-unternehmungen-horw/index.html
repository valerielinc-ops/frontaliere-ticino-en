Full description

This workplace comprises a self-service restaurant with Free Flow, a cafeteria and a 24/5 self-service location. The meeting place in Lucerne creates an inviting gathering place for students, employees and external guests. Every day, our team delights an average of up to 400 guests with a varied offering – from seasonal menus and a Free Flow offering to vegetarian dishes. In addition, we offer a creative catering service tailored to a wide range of occasions and organize standing lunches, aperitifs and events. Are you our new team member who will support us as a bar / service employee immediately or by agreement? You will work approximately 20% on an hourly wage basis, every Thursday or Friday from 14:00 to 23:00.

Your task menu

  • Welcoming, serving and advising guests
  • Taking on the host role
  • Ensuring a high level of guest satisfaction
  • Operating the cash register and preparing the mise en place
  • Ensuring an appealing presentation of products
  • Assisting with catering and special events
  • Preparing coffee and beverage specialties
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the work environment and carrying out general tidying and cleaning tasks
  • Implementing and ensuring compliance with the quality standards and ZFV guidelines (hygiene and occupational safety, service, etc.)

Your recipe for success

  • Practical experience in gastronomy / hospitality
  • Good spoken German
  • Agile and innovative mindset
  • Teamwork
  • Enthusiastic sales professional
  • Passionate host
  • Engaging and charming presence
  • Enjoyment of good food

What we offer you

We create teams that support one another and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that promotes well-being. Among other things, we place great emphasis on pay transparency and pay equality. For this position, with a 100% workload, we offer an annual salary of around CHF 52'000, depending on your experience and qualifications, depending on your experience and qualifications (including a 13th month's salary).
