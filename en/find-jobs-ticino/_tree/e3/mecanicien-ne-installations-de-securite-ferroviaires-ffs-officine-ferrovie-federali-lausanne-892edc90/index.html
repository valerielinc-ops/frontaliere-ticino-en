What awaits you.

Looking for a job that makes sense in an innovative work environment? We have the offer you need! To find out what other benefits we offer you, such as children's allowances, free AG 2nd class or foreign travel at reduced prices (also for the family), visit our website under "Social benefits". What you can move.

  • You modify, construct and maintain a variety of rail traffic safety facilities such as ground and height signs, switch and needle motors, barrier and track occupancy control facilities.
  • You will independently perform delicate and complex mechanical work on outdoor security installations.
  • You manage the lifting of disturbances to mechanical devices.
  • After in-house training, you alone monitor and carry out installation work in our Romandie region.
  • You are willing to support the indoor assembly team and expand your knowledge.

What you bring.

  • CFCs in a mechanical industry (automotive mechanic, polymerician or equivalent profession, and with electrical bases).
  • Head of security TEN 20100 or ready to undergo in-house training of security technician.
  • Driver's licence category B compulsory You are domiciled at 30 minutes max. of Lausanne and are ready to do the lay-off according to the location of the site.
  • You have a strong sense of responsibility and team spirit. You have a high sensitivity to security issues. You are proactive and skilful, flexible and stress-resistant.
  • Knowledge of technical computer tools.
