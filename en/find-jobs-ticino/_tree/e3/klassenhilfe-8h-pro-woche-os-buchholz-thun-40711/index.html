Full description

Portrait Welcome to Thun! At the Buchholz High School, around 200 pupils are taught in 10 classes on three levels (real, sec and sSek classes). We are a dedicated team and look forward to seeing you!

Tasks

They accompany a single student in the 8. Class in everyday school life according to orders of the case-leading support teacher and class teacher. The subjects and times cannot be changed. Since continuity over the week must be guaranteed, applications for a partial workload are not possible.

Monday, 10:15-11:50 Math

  • Tuesday, 8:20-9:55 German
  • Wednesday, 8:20-9:55 German
  • Thursday, 14:00-15:40 RZG, ERG
  • Friday, 7:30-09:05 (may be omitted).

Requirements

  • Ideally, you have an educational background in the form of an appropriate education.
  • They bring experience in dealing with young people (outside the private family framework).
  • German as a mother tongue is a prerequisite.
  • They have good access to young people and understand how to build a trusting and sustainable relationship.
  • They approach adolescents with patience, empathy, appreciation and understanding.
  • They are characterized by a calm, balanced and reliable personality and maintain the necessary serenity even in challenging situations.
  • They have a good feeling for the individual needs of young people and can accompany them sensitively, but also clearly and consistently.

We offer

  • 8 hours of care (60 minutes) weekly until the end of school year 26/27
  • Cantonally regulated employment conditions according to LADV.
  • Very good infrastructure.
  • Good support from the entire team.
