Dipl. Nurse HF/FH - MH, 70-90 %

Location: Riehen

From 01.10.2026 or by agreement

Founded in 1900, the Klinik Sonnenhalde in Riehen is now an established private clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland and southern Baden. We pursue a holistic, human-centered, milieutherapeutic psychiatry that combines excellent professional quality with a Christian value base. The clinic has 68 stationary beds, 4 day clinics and 4 ambulatories. As one of the first clinics in Switzerland, we are actively promoting digital therapy offerings, such as blended therapy and virtual reality and opened the first hybrid day clinic in Switzerland in February 2023.

For the team of the open department Margarethenhaus we are looking for 01. October 2026 or by appointment a committed and empathetic personality.

Your responsibilities

  • You work as a caregiver in the core team and take responsibility for accompanying your caregiver from entry to exit.
  • They plan the care process and ensure the implementation of the planned interventions
  • They participate in the further development of nursing services and the therapeutic environment on the station

Your profile

  • You have professional experience in psychiatry or want to change the field of work and benefit from a structured familiarization in psychiatric setting
  • You are an open, motivated and proactive personality with good criticism and communication skills
  • Her strengths are self-reflection and enthusiasm for new challenges
