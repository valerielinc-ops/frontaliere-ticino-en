Full description

Our customer is an established, medium-sized building and civil engineering company. To strengthen the team, we are looking for a reliable person as a chauffeur category C/E.

Their tasks

  • Transport of materials and machines to construction sites and magazines
  • Driving other vehicles such as concrete mixers and tippers
  • Collaboration in the Winter Service (Pikettdienst)
  • Careful handling of vehicles, cargo and delivery documents

Your profile

  • Professional experience as a truck driver, ideally in a construction company
  • Valid CH driving licence of categories C and E (mandatory)
  • Valid CZV training and driver card (mandatory)
  • Readiness for Winter Service (Pikett)
  • Knowledge of German to understand delivery documents
  • Reliable, independent and careful working methods

We offer

  • Varied job in a solid construction company
  • Possibility of permanent employment after 3 months of temporary employment
