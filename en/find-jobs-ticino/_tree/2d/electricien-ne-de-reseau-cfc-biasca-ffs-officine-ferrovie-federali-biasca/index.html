Full description

The distribution network electrician internship is ideal for you if not only are you physically fit and don't suffer from vertigo, but also if you like working when others are asleep. Thanks to this training, you will take flight towards a future full of prospects in the world of transport. ## General information

  • Start of training/internship: Summer 2026
  • Location: Biasca
  • Work area: Contact lines
  • Language: Italian ## Your tasks
  • Establish and block sections of the route
  • Ground the contact lines along the blocked sections of the route
  • Build, check and repair the contact lines
  • Operate lifting platforms, cranes and other machines and tools
  • When the work is finished, free the section of the route again
  • You always work very reliably and with safety in mind ## What do you bring with you
  • The world of transport excites you
  • You like working outdoors
  • You are a true team player and a reliable person
  • You are skilled in manual work, physically fit and do not suffer from vertigo
  • You have good visual and hearing abilities and have no limitations in color perception
  • Irregular working hours during the day, at night and on weekends do not scare you
  • You have obtained a compulsory school leaving certificate (intermediate level or above)
  • You are in good health
  • You have a limited hearing ability, limited color vision or other health problems? In this case contact us. ## Your benefits
  • GA travelcard: Free GA travelcard for free travel in Switzerland
  • Tickets abroad: Reduced price tickets for train journeys abroad
  • Laptop + tablet: 300 francs for your laptop or tablet
  • Language study trip: Financial support for your language study trip
  • Teaching material: Contribution to the purchase of your teaching material
  • Badge with benefits: You receive numerous benefits thanks to your badge
  • Holiday: 1st year of internship 7 weeks of holidays, from the 2nd year of the internship 6 weeks of holidays
  • Thirteenth month's salary: You receive 13 months' salary ## What we have to offer you
  • The "login week" to kick off your internship together with all the other apprentices
  • A varied internship thanks to regular rotation between internship places
  • Career coaching and professional support during the internship
  • Excellent quality of training and passionate professional trainers
  • A promising career in an industry with a great future ## Orientation internship
  • 7th class: do you want to know more about this profession? At our events you have the opportunity to inform yourself and have an exchange with our specialists.
  • 8th grade: do you want to learn more about this profession? Then apply for an internship in your region.
  • 9th or 10th grade: Do you want to learn this profession? Apply for an internship position. You will complete an internship with us as part of the application procedure. ## Your complete application
  • Curriculum vitae
  • Copies of the last three report cards
