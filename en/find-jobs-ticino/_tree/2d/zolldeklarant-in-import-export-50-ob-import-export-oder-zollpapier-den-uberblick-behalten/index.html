ID: Category: Logistics - Spedition Region: Basel Reference: Customs declarant/in Import & Export 50% Our customer is an owner-managed service company from the Basel region, which specializes in customs and logistics services with a lot of commitment and high level of expertise. Quick decisions, pragmatic solutions and a pronounced customer orientation shape the company culture as well as a high quality standard and a personal service. Thanks to many years of industry experience and a strong national and international partner network, our customer accompanies his clients reliably along the entire transport and customs processing. For the continuous expansion of the existing team, we are immediately looking for a flexible, experienced and team-oriented personality. Customs declarant / in Import & Export 50% - whether import, export or customs paper, keep the overview here.

Tasks

  • Independent handling of import and export customs clearance via e-dec, ATLAS, NCTS and Passar
  • Ensuring timely and legally compliant customs clearance for national and international consignments of goods
  • Support of day-to-day operations from collection of shipments via aviation to billing and dossier management
  • Competent contact person for customers and business partners with questions about customs and logistics processes
  • Close cooperation with freight forwarders, transport partners and the competent customs authorities
  • Cooperation in administrative tasks and active support for a smooth daily routine
  • Holiday representation at the Basel Weil border office and taking over the customs clearance orders
  • Participation in the continuous optimization of internal processes and processes

Your profile

  • Completed basic commercial training, ideally supplemented by training as a customs declarant (e.g. Spedlogswiss) or a comparable further education in the field of customs and foreign trade
  • Several years of professional experience in import and export clearance as well as in the independent handling of customs formalities
  • In-depth knowledge of common customs systems such as e-dec, ATLAS as well as NCTS and Passar
  • Safe handling of customs tariffs, origin of goods, customs procedures and the relevant legal provisions
  • Experience in the preparation and processing of customs dossiers as well as in communication with customers, freight forwarders and customs authorities
  • Good knowledge of German and English in words and writing
  • Further language skills are advantageous

If you value an uncomplicated working environment with short decision-making paths and a pronounced service mentality, you will find the ideal conditions for taking on responsibility and getting involved in the long term.
