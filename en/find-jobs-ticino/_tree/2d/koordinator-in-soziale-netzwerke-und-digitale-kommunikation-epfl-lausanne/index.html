Full description

Mission

EPFL's communications department, Mediacom, is looking for an experienced part-time social networking and digital communications coordinator (40-50%) to strengthen its team.

The social networks and digital communication coordinator will help develop and strengthen EPFL's presence on social networks, ensuring the dissemination and exploitation of its content. He/she participates in the day before and following up on the School's online reputation and adapts the content, formats and tone to the specificities of each platform and its audiences, in accordance with EPFL's editorial strategy and identity.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Contribute to the publication and enhancement of EPFL content on social networks, including scientific and institutional news, portraits and other editorial content
  • Ensure the adaptation and dissemination of audiovisual content on the various platforms, including the writing of adapted texts and legends, the selection of visuals and the creation of thumbnails for videos
  • Participate on the eve, moderation and animation of the various EPFL platforms (LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Mastodon, X and Reddit), including responding to private comments and messages, identifying sensitive content or interactions and following publications from other entities of the School
  • Contribute to the monitoring and analysis of the performance of publications and platforms using key indicators (KPIs), and identify formats, topics and practices that can improve the visibility and commitment of content
  • Participate in the editorial planning of social networks and in the coordination of publications with other members of the Mediacom team

Profile

  • Excellent editorial skills in French and English
  • Experience in digital communication and mastery of different social media platforms
  • Knowledge and mastery of graphical content creation tools
  • Interest in scientific, technological and academic fields and ability to make complex topics accessible
  • Spirit of analysis and creativity
  • Ability to identify, anticipate and manage sensitive or potentially reputable subjects with discernment
  • Sense of aesthetics and visual consistency of digital content
  • Good relationship skills and sense of teamwork
