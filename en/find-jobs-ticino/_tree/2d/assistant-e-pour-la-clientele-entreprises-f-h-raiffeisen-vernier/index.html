Full description

22.10.2026 Vernier

Be part of our team!

Do you like to coordinate, organize and advance the files? Do you want to play a key role in supporting SMEs and entrepreneurs in the region? At the Banque Raiffeisen Cities and Vineyards Geneva, you support four Client Advisors Companies in managing their daily activities. Your contribution is essential: thanks to the quality of your operational and commercial support, they can devote themselves fully to consulting, developing customer relations and acquiring new business. At home, your work is not in the shadows. You are a trusted partner, a key link on the team and contribute directly to the quality of service offered to our customers.

What are you waiting for?

As Business Customer Assistant, you occupy a central position within the team. A true partner of Business Advisors, you ensure the coordination of business and administrative activities in order to guarantee an irreproachable service to our SME customers.

Specifically, you

  • Support four Business Advisors in managing and monitoring their client portfolio.
  • Support requests to open banking relationships and ensure the quality of the files.
  • Prepare customer appointments and associated administrative and business processes.
  • Ensure the processing of banking transactions and the administrative follow-up of customer relations.
  • Coordinate exchanges between clients, Bank specialists and various internal partners.
  • Identify contact opportunities and contribute to the development of business relationships.
  • Participate actively in the excellence of our service and the team's collective performance.

What are you bringing?

  • Banking or financial training (CFC banking, HEG, HEC or equivalent).
  • A first successful experience in a customer-contact function, ideally in the banking or financial sector.
  • A strong interest in SMEs and an understanding of their issues.
  • Excellent organizational, planning and priority management capacity.
  • Rigor, reliability and a high sense of quality in the processing of files.
  • A team spirit developed, combined with a proactive and solutions-oriented approach.
  • Very good relationship skills and a real sense of service.
  • A good knowledge of the Geneva economic fabric is an asset.
