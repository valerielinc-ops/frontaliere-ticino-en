Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Maintenance work on aviation components of the Swiss Air Force
  • IH/Overhaul F/A-18 Gun System
  • Repairs, fault detection and modifications of the upgraded aviation components
  • Testing and assessment of individual parts
  • Setting up and carrying out test runs on the corresponding test systems
  • Maintenance services of test systems and special equipment
  • Customer Support in Disruptions

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a polymechanic or mechanic (EFZ)
  • Ideally, professional experience in aircraft component maintenance
  • Enjoyment of complex mechanics
  • Fresh and exact working method
  • Reliable, responsible personality
  • Good technical knowledge of English
  • CH nationality (functional)

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
