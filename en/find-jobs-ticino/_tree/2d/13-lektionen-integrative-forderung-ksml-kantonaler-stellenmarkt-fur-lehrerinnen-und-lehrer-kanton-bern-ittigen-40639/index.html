Full description

Portrait honest - committed - strong. The School Ittigen attaches importance to openness and maintains a close exchange between the regular teachers, special teachers, the school social worker and the school management. The light-flooded and modern school house is very easily accessible by public transport. Information about our school can be found at www.schule-ittigen.ch.

Tasks

  • They shall perform the following tasks:
  • Support and advice for students with special needs
  • Implementation of diagnostic evaluations
  • Create funding plans and education plans
  • Support in class, in small groups or individually
  • Advising teachers and guardians
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with internal and external specialist bodies
  • Close cooperation with the teachers and the class team

Requirements

  • Ideally, they have a master’s degree in curative pedagogy, a teaching diploma with a curative pedagogical focus or are currently in training.
  • A solution-oriented approach, joy with children and young people is desired.
  • A respectful approach at eye level, serenity and patience, a good dose of humor makes it easier to deal with children and young people.

We offer

  • ... a supportive and open team with school social work and school management
  • ... an exciting field of work with a high level of personal responsibility
  • ... a very good infrastructure with its own workplace, beautiful classrooms and iPad-based teaching
  • ... good and fast accessibility by public transport (paper mill Ittigen) or car (parking available)
  • ... the hour planning can be arranged with the class teacher and the specialist teacher
