Full description

You are dipl. Specialist / specialist surgical technology HF and would like to continue working in the operating room, but is consciously looking for a very small and flexible workload? Then you are exactly right with our staff pool of surgical technology. We set up a flexible pool of employees for our operations and are looking for dedicated specialists who can help us with personnel bottlenecks.

Their tasks

  • Assignments as a specialist surgical technology HF in our operating room operation
  • Instrumenting and serving according to your expertise
  • Support in the event of increased operational utilization, sick leave and short-term staff shortages
  • Working in different specialist areas according to your experience and competences
  • Professional and patient-oriented perioperative care
  • Close cooperation with the various professional groups in the OP
  • The employment takes place in the 5% hourly wage rate. The pool should enable us to use employees flexibly and specifically where support is needed. We take into account both your personal availability and the needs of our operating room.
  • Employment in 5% hourly wage rate
  • Operations are planned according to availability and in consultation
  • Early and as planned as possible operational planning
  • Assignments in the case of increased operating utilization, illness and short-term failures
  • Possibility to take on individual additional services
  • Use according to your professional qualifications and experience
  • You remain part of our surgical team, even if you consciously only want to work in a small workload

Your profile

  • Completed training as a dipl. Specialist surgical technique HF or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • Professional experience in operations
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work
  • Enjoy working in an interprofessional team
  • Flexibility and willingness to respond to staff shortages
  • High level of technical and social competence
  • Willingness to contribute your knowledge and experience to our operation

Your opportunity You want to continue working in the operating room – but stay flexible? Perhaps you have reduced your workload, are already retired, want to re-enter after a professional break or are simply looking for a way to continue working in the operating room without having to take on a large amount of work. Then we look forward to you! Become part of our staff pool and support our surgical team exactly when your experience and skills are needed.

Their advantages

  • Holidays: You benefit from holidays between 25 and 30 working days (depending on age) and 13 paid holidays. For this you have the possibility to buy 5 holiday days.
  • Working hours: The company’s normal working hours for a full-time workload are 42 hours per week (excluding doctors). The annual working time applies, which ensures the highest possible flexibility in the organization of working time. Service requests are implemented where possible and part-time employment is possible in many functions – also in management positions.
  • Education and further education: Your personal and professional development is important to us. We support participation in internal and external further education and training through generous participation in the form of working hours and/or course costs.
  • Food: In our staff restaurant – nominated with the “Best of Swiss Gastro” award – you can enjoy menus from the market-fresh cuisine at very attractive conditions.
  • Arrival/parking: Employees of Spital Muri benefit from cheap parking. The car park is located on the hospital area. If you come to work by public transport, bike, motorcycle or on foot, we will reward you with a monthly eco-bonus.
  • Social insurance: We attach importance to adequate social benefits. For night, weekend and holiday work, we pay you fair allowances. We insure your salary at the pension fund Profond without coordination deduction (excluding doctors). The wages of doctors are insured by the VSAO preventive foundation.
