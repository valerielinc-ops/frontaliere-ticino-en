Full description

Fresh products that live up to their name. Shelves stocked. Orders placed on time. Area clean. Cash register correct. Customers satisfied. That’s our store. And soon, yours too. Switch now to the fresh winning team. ## Tasks

  • Operate and settle the cash register
  • Accept, check, and stock fresh products and all other goods
  • Check expiration dates on products
  • Ensure order and cleanliness in the store and warehouse
  • Implement store layout guidelines accurately ## Requirements
  • Ideally more than two years of professional experience: in retail (food)
  • Completed vocational training (EBA): in retail (food) or a related profession would be ideal
  • Willing to work beyond «nine to five» and enjoy free time when others are working
  • Physically fit, some parcels weigh up to 10 kilos
  • Familiar with PC, tablet, and smartphone – and with other digital tools (e.g., for inventory management), which you will quickly get comfortable with
  • Enjoy working with people, a good dose of friendliness, and a positive attitude
  • Good knowledge of German ## What we offer
  • Part of the Migros Group: a strong, retail-anchored brand under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Work healthily: high standards in occupational safety, ergonomics, and hygiene
  • Maternity/paternity leave: 5 additional weeks of parental leave (19 weeks) with full pay. Fathers enjoy 4 paid weeks of parental leave.
  • Fair wages: 13 monthly salaries and fixed salary increases for everyone instead of bonuses for a few
  • Mobile phone plan: your choice: with Galaxus Mobile, Wingo, or Sunrise, starting at CHF 10.-, stay connected
  • Migros Bank: better interest rates and lower fees on your private, investment, or mortgage accounts
  • Employee discounts: 10% off your next big purchase (twice a year)
  • Vacation: 6 weeks of vacation for everyone, with additional days for service anniversaries
  • Leisure and cultural offers: various discounts on car rentals, hotels, events, and more ## Contact ## Application & Contact Remo Herrmann, Sales Recruiter Middleland
