Full description

Your surroundings

You work in a small team at the Waldhaus clinic in Chur. Cooperative structures, motivated colleagues and a professional exchange at eye level shape our cooperation.

Their tasks

  • You work in the cost accounting for the health, disabled and special education sector
  • Support the team in preparing the monthly cost and performance reports
  • They create and assess ad hoc analyses for the business units and the management
  • You are actively involved in the further development of controlling services and the continuous improvement of processes
  • You work in various cross-sectoral projects

Our requirement profile

  • You have a commercial education and enjoy dealing with numbers
  • You are interested in health care and its economic connections
  • You think analytically, act proactively and bring an entrepreneurial mindset
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented
  • They are characterized by a high numerical affinity and a good understanding of business relationships

We offer you

  • Dynamic and supportive team culture, also cross-divisional in cooperation with finance, customer administration and human resources
  • A meaningful job, because the employees you serve make an important contribution to the mental health of the German population
  • An interesting and versatile field of activity with good personal development and development opportunities
  • Independent design of your tasks as part of a motivated team
  • Support in your personal and professional development through further education and training
  • Advanced working conditions with above average social benefits and at least 30 days of holidays
