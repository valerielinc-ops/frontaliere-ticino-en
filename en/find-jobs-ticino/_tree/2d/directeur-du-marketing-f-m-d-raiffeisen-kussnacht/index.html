Full description

For Raiffeisenbank Rigi, a successful and regionally oriented bank with six offices around the Rigi, we are looking for a committed, initiative and implementation-strong personality as a marketing manager. In this function, you strengthen the visibility of Raiffeisenbank Rigi in the market and actively shape our presence across all relevant channels. You support segment managers, consultants and campaign managers in the effective market development and ensure that marketing and communication measures are implemented in a targeted, professional and customer-oriented manner. Tasks:

  • You develop the bank-specific marketing plan including budget basics in cooperation with the segment manager and taking into account the marketing plan of Raiffeisen Switzerland.
  • You support the sales managers with sales concept and sales plan.
  • You plan and implement target group-oriented marketing and communication measures – from the idea to the success control.
  • You support the departments in the efficient implementation of marketing and sales activities.
  • You are responsible for means of communication in compliance with the corporate design guidelines of Raiffeisen Switzerland.
  • You maintain and update our market presence through various channels such as website, customer zone, social media, shop window, and advertising.
  • You ensure targeted media deployment and coordinate external partners, agencies and service providers.
  • You monitor measures and budgets and bring in ideas for optimization. Requirements
  • Completed basic training and further education or studies in marketing, communication or business administration (HF/FH or comparable).
  • Several years of experience in the marketing and communication environment, ideally with conceptual and implementation responsibility.
  • Broad marketing understanding via analogue and digital channels as well as flair for target group-oriented communication.
  • Structured, exact and independent working method with high quality standards.
  • Joy of interdisciplinary cooperation, service orientation and a secure appearance.
  • Initiative, maker mentality and the ability to combine implementation with conceptual thinking.
  • Digital affinity and interest in the development of processes. This is a plus.
  • Experience in the financial or banking environment.
  • Basic knowledge of Adobe InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator or similar tools.
  • Experience with CMS, social media, newsletter tools or other digital marketing tools. We offer: Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.
