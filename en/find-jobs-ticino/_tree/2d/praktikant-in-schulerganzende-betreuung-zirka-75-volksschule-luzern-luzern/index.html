Full description

You are on your way to an educational education. Children fascinate you. You dare to accompany them on their way. In the school supplementary care you are in the middle of it.

Tasks

  • Together with the pedagogical team, you will look after and support the children during lunchtime and in the afternoon – you will be carefully and qualifiedly guided.
  • You will accompany the children in the everyday transitions, excursions and group activities.
  • You support with domestic tasks and share responsibility for hygiene and cleanliness.
  • During school weeks, you usually work from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m.

Requirements

  • Bustle and children’s laughter are no problem for you – you keep an overview even in lively situations.
  • You are happy to spend time with children and are a reliable contact person for them.
  • Responsibility and reliability set you apart.
  • You like to take initiative, be communicative and work in a team.
  • Housework is a matter of course for you and gives you pleasure.
