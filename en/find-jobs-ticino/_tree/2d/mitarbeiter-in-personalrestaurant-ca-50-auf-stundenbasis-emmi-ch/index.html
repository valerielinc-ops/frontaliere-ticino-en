Full description

Are you looking for a varied task in a lively environment in which you can actively participate? In our staff restaurant you are part of a dedicated team and ensure every day that our guests experience fresh and pleasant food.

You can do that.

  • You support our team in the preparation and delivery of around 150 fresh lunches and ensure satisfied guests.
  • In the morning you will get our coffee corner (06:00 am tour) underway and make a successful start to the day.
  • You actively help with the preparation of cold cuisine and fresh produce and pay attention to quality and presentation.
  • Whether cash register, service at internal events or general work - you are in versatile use.
  • You ensure order, fill up food vending machines (also on Saturdays) and keep the operation running.

You bring that with you

  • Experience in gastronomy and pleasure in tackling it as a team.
  • You work reliably, think along and set priorities right in hectic situations.
  • Friendly appearance, cultivated appearance and pleasure in guest contact.
  • Flexibility for operations and willingness to work between 06:00 and 14:30.
  • Very good knowledge of German for a smooth working day.

This is what we offer you

  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
