Full description

Our intensive care unit with a bed capacity of up to 10 beds is equipped with the most advanced technology and state-of-the-art facilities. We treat, care, look after and advise the patients entrusted to us according to the latest state of knowledge and take into account the patient’s environment and their individual life story according to our understanding of care.

Tasks

  • · Care, monitoring and treatment of patients in critical situations
  • Assistance/care for the first care of emergency patients
  • Commitment to continuous optimisation of care processes
  • Early detection of clinical changes and initiation of appropriate measures
  • Implementation and monitoring of diagnostic and therapeutic measures in cooperation with the interprofessional treatment team
  • Safe handling of monitoring and medical devices within the scope of your competences
  • Accompaniment, advice and support of patients and their relatives
  • Active interprofessional cooperation with doctors, therapists and other professional groups
  • Participation in the training and support of new employees and students as part of your skills
  • Commitment to continuous optimisation of care quality and care processes

Requirements

  • You have a degree as a dipl. Nurse HF/FH and bring at least six months of professional experience
  • You are interested in intensive care and are ready to develop professionally, for example with NDK monitoring care (ÜWP) or NDS HF intensive care
  • You work responsibly and independently and recognize changes in the condition of your patients at an early stage
  • You are a committed, flexible and team-oriented personality and appreciate interprofessional collaboration
  • You communicate clearly and appreciatively and meet patients and relatives with empathy and professionalism
  • You also keep track of dynamic and demanding situations and set priorities
  • You are open to new things and would like to actively participate in the further development of our intensive care unit

We offer

You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits.

The Swiss Paraplegic Centre in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for cross-sectional, back and respiratory medicine. As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the centre offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives. The clinic is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a globally unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with paraplegia.
