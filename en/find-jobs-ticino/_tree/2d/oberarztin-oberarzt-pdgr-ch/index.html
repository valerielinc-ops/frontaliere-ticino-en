Full description

Your surroundings

You work in the private clinic MENTALVA in an interdisciplinary team of adult psychiatry. You can expect a collegial environment that actively supports exchange, further education and the participation of the treatment offer.

Their tasks

  • They ensure the inpatient care and case management of semi-privately and privately insured patients of adult psychiatry
  • They independently carry out psychiatric diagnosis and psychiatric and psychotherapeutic therapies
  • They treat patients with diseases from the entire diagnostic spectrum of adult psychiatry
  • They maintain contacts with treatment partners, relatives and authorities and participate in public events

Our requirement profile

  • You have a completed degree in human medicine and have specialist training in psychiatry and psychotherapy or have completed with a focus on psychosomatics
  • They have solid clinical experience
  • You have a high level of leadership and social competence, and enjoy working in a team
  • You have the willingness for interdisciplinary cooperation
  • You are motivated and willing to take responsibility
  • You have good communication skills and show entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Good English skills are beneficial

We offer you

  • A versatile job in a pleasant and collegial working atmosphere
  • You can contribute your ideas and shape your field of responsibility
  • You will experience a diverse and cooperative cooperation with all professional groups
  • Take advantage of our internal training offer – we also support your external training needs
  • With part-time work models, regular working hours and unpaid leave, an optimal work-life balance
