Full description

Portrait We teach at our school from kindergarten to secondary school eleven mostly mixed-year classes (two school years). The day school is also integrated in the schoolhouse.

Tasks

You teach as a class teacher about 75 - 95% in a 3rd / 4th grade. In parallel, a second 3rd/4th class is led. Applications as a team are also possible. We also support applications from students, who wish to teach in parallel with the training. You work in an experienced and motivated cycle team. As a class teacher, you accompany and guide children in their social, emotional and cognitive development. Collaborating with your colleagues and working on school events are also part of your job. Subjects: German, Math. NMG. BG, Sport, Music

Requirements

  • Ideally, you have a Bachelor for the primary level or a seminary primary teacher patent.
  • You are a communicative, team capable and dutiful personality.
  • Working with the children is your focus; you accompany and promote them effectively.
  • You enjoy working in a dedicated, thinking and helpful team (level / cycle / overall college).
  • You are committed to good parental work and actively participate in school projects.
  • You can also look outside the box and are not afraid to bring in new ideas.

We offer

  • Open, active and very helpful college
  • Good cooperation and many opportunities for co-creation (e.g. permanently installed vessels for cooperation such as working in cycle groups)
  • Close cooperation with parents and authorities - we pull on a rope.
  • Many offers of the school in the musical and creative field (band, choir, school garden, potters, etc.) - a good balance to the top-heavy subjects is important to us.
  • Modern school house with excellent infrastructure (e.g. computer science or school-owned winter sports equipment)
  • School in the rural area with well-developed tourist infrastructure (winter sports / hiking area) - if you like to be sporty, you are right with us - otherwise of course.
  • For new entrants: Accompaniment through experienced in-school mentoring
  • Housing assistance
  • Accessibility possible via public transport
