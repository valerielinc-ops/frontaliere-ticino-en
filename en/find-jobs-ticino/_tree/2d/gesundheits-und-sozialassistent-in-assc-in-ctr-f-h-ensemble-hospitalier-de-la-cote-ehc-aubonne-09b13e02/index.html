Full description

Aubonne Hospital has been serving its community since 1895. Ideally located near vineyards and the town center, it is one of the three founding hospitals of the EHC. It consolidates all EHC missions in a single location: whether for primary care with its medical center, acute care with its recognized internal medicine beds and palliative care unit, the multisite treatment and rehabilitation center, or long-term care services with its EMS and Temporary Accommodation Center (TAC).
