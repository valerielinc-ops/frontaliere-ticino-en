Welcome to Medbase Bulle

Located in the heart of the southern extension of Gruyère Centre, the Medbase Bulle medical center covers 650 m² and benefits from new, bright and well-equipped infrastructure, including a laboratory and a radiology department. The site welcomes a rapidly expanding team, currently made up of around fifteen employees (doctors and medical assistants) engaged in interprofessional care, mainly in general internal medicine and rheumatology. A medical hotline provides emergency care from Monday to Saturday.

Missions

  • Medical care of your patients under your own responsibility
  • Collaboration within a team of internal and external specialists

Profile sought

  • Several years of experience in outpatient care
  • Committed person aware of their responsibilities, interested in interdisciplinary collaboration and teamwork
  • Great social skills, team spirit, flexibility and enjoyment of working independently
  • Holder of a cantonal practice authorization responsible for basic insurance or meets the conditions for granting cantonal authorizations to practice

What we offer

  • Particular importance of the balance between professional activity and private life
  • All positions are open to part-time work, with flexible hours
  • For doctors, collaboration is possible under employee or independent status, to be agreed during the interview

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Fribourg?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Fribourg's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Fribourg's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
