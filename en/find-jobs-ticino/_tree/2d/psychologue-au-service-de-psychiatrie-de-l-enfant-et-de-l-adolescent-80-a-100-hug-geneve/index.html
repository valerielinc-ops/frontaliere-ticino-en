Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The mission of the Department of Women, Children and Adolescents is to provide quality care tailored to the needs of women at every stage of their lives and to the health problems faced by newborns, children and adolescents from birth to age 16. He works on the development of state-of-the-art services and specialist expertise. The Child and Adolescent Psychiatry Service (SPEA) is responsible for the day hospital or outpatient care of children, adolescents and adolescents with mental disorders. It provides holistic psychological and child-psychiatric care, the patient is taken into account as a whole, including her family and social network. SPEA is a Category A training centre for the post-graduate training of physicians. The service occupies the new premises of the Maison de l'enfance et de l'adolescence (MEA), which brings together all the units of the service in a particularly warm setting, designed specifically to improve the quality of care of the most vulnerable young patients.

Description of post

You will join the Child and Adolescent Nutrition Unit (AliNEA), a unit specializing in the assessment and management of eating and eating disorders among children and young people aged 4 to 16. You are responsible for the management of children, adolescents and adolescents with eating disorders (ASD) and eating disorders (ASD). This activity is part of an integrated multidisciplinary approach involving child psychiatry, paediatrics, psychology, nursing and working with families. You conduct psychotherapy of children, adolescents and adolescents, and their families. You participate in supervision, in different meeting times and present clinical situations to your colleagues.

Qualifications

You have a MAS in psychotherapy (or have been in the MAS training process for at least one year) or an equivalent title. You have experience and interest in working with children and adolescents and their families. You present an opening to working with other professionals as well as for multidisciplinary work.
