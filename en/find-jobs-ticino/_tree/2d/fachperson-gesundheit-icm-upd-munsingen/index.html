Clinic for Depression and Anxiety

Do you want to professionally accompany people in mental crises and also make a valuable contribution in demanding situations? On our Intensive Care Münsingen (ICM) station, you support people with acute psychiatric conditions on their way to stabilization and strengthen them in dealing with their illness. Together with a committed interprofessional team, you will design individual, person-centered care in a demanding and versatile environment. Our modern station concept offers you the opportunity to contribute your skills and actively shape the further development of care.

Your tasks

  • You accompany and care for our patients individually, appreciatively and person-centered – even in challenging situations
  • Together with your team, you ensure a safe environment for people in acute psychiatric, social and somatic crises.
  • You support our patients together with the nursing care provider in developing helpful strategies for dealing with their disease and strengthening their resources
  • You work in tandem with a dipl. Nursing professional
  • You are involved in the implementation and further development of our recovery-oriented ICM station concept and the therapy offers
  • Together with your team, you contribute to comprehensive care and to ensuring our high quality standards

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a health professional EFZ
  • You enjoy working with people with challenging diseases and appreciate the interprofessional cooperation
  • You meet our patients with appreciation, empathy and a person-centered attitude
  • You think goal- and people-oriented, have a high level of social competence and are actively involved in the team
  • You are resilient, committed and flexible and appreciate the professional and human challenges of a psychiatric acute care unit
  • You are motivated to contribute to changes and to actively shape the further development of our station concept

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
