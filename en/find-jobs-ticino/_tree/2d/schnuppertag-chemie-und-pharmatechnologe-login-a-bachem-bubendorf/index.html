Full description

Are you looking for an apprenticeship and interested in a trial day as a chemical and pharmatechnologist/login? Then you are exactly right with us!

Professional description As a chemical and pharmacologist/login (CPT for short), you are the specialist in our chemical production plants. They worked in the production of peptides, oligonucleotides and complex organic compounds used as drugs. They operate and monitor the semi-automated reaction apparatus, measuring and regulating devices, pumps and valves. They also control the production plants where the chemical reactions and technological processes take place from modern control consoles. The maintenance and maintenance work on equipment and chemical plants is part of the CPT’s remit.

Your profile

  • Technical interest and understanding
  • Craftsmanship
  • Team capable, reliable, flexible personality
  • Endurance and physical resilience
