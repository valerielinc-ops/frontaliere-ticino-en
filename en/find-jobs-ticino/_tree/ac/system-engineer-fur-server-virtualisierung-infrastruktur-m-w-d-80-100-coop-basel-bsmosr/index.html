System Engineer for server/virtualization/infrastructure (m/f/d); 80-100 %

Coop Cooperative — Basel, BS, Svizzera

  • Job opening: by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Computer Science
  • Location: Coop, Tellstrasse 60, 4053 Basel
  • Pensum: 80-100 %

Tasks

  • Operation, administration and further development of the KVM-based virtualization platform incl. Provisioning, lifecycle management, patching, performance optimization and troubleshooting during operation
  • Administration of Linux and Windows servers in physical and virtualized environments, including installation, configuration, hardening, patching, monitoring and incident/problem management
  • Ensure stable, secure and available operation of the compute platforms incl. Capacity management, performance analysis and sustainable troubleshooting at server, hypervisor and operating system levels
  • Cooperation in the automation and standardization of operational tasks, e.g. with scripting or infrastructure-as-code, to increase quality, speed and traceability
  • Participation in infrastructure projects such as migrations, renewals, rollouts and technical changes in the compute, storage and backup environment as well as the documentation in Confluence and the update of existing entries

Requirements

  • Completed computer science training (EFZ or comparable) and 3-5 years of practical experience as a system engineer in the infrastructure environment
  • Good to very good knowledge of KVM / QEMU / libvirt or comparable virtualization technologies as well as understanding of cluster, HA and resource management
  • Practical experience in administration of Linux and Windows servers, ideally incl. OS lifecycle, security hardening, patch management and troubleshooting in enterprise environments
  • Basic to good understanding of storage and backup technologies, such as SAN/NAS, snapshotting, replication, restore, recovery and DR testing
  • Structured, reliable and team-oriented way of working, good communication skills and willingness to take responsibility in day-to-day operations and in changes/projects
  • Discount on the travel offer ITS Coop Travel, 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket, up to 20% discount on various insurance services, Reka-Card: Discount on subscription
  • Conditions of employment: Pension fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, collective employment contract (GAV)
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Annual subscription discount
