Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Clinique des Grangettes According to agreement Type of employment: Permanent

Reference Number: 71321

As Switzerland's largest medical network, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centres and emergency services. The primary medical quality and well-being of the individual are at the centre of our concerns.

MAIN ACTIVITY OF THE POST

  • Care oriented needs and clients and care of patients in accordance with the care process and in collaboration with our partner doctors and an interprofessional team.

MAIN TASKS

  • Effective analysis and management of specific medical and surgical emergencies
  • Sort patients admitted to emergencies
  • Collect, analyze and synthesize all relevant clinical data
  • Implement immediate diagnostic and therapeutic measures in case of vital emergencies
  • Know the etiology and semiology of emergency pathologies
  • Collaborate with physicians by combining coherence, complementarity and trust
  • Ensure the safety of the patient's care and inform him of his rights.

TRAINING REQUIRED

  • Swiss or foreign nursing degree, approved by the Swiss Red Cross
  • Experience confirmed in emergency service
  • Knowledge of the Swiss EST® sorting scale
  • Have a high sense of responsibility and flexibility
  • Ease of communication and collaboration between different institutional professionals, doctors, surgeons
  • Excellent stress and crisis resistance

PREVIOUS EXPERIENCE

  • Desired experience in acute care clinic

In addition to an attractive workstation, you will integrate a dynamic team that is looking forward to welcoming you into a human-sized structure.

You will benefit from attractive conditions of employment with, among other things, opportunities for continuing training and development throughout your career with us, as well as social benefits and other interesting benefits.

For an optimal balance between work and private life, we offer our employees various contractual models, including part-time and FLEXible contracts. There is also the possibility to enjoy up to 7 weeks of holiday thanks to our offer "Congé-Plus".

YOUR CANDIDATURE

The Human Resources Directorate is pleased to receive your complete application (CV, cover letter, diplomas and work certificates).

Please note that for data protection reasons, we cannot take into account paper files (which will not be returned) or applications sent by e-mail. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via our recruitment site.
