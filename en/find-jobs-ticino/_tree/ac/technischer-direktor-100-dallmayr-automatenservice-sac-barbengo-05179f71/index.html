Technical Director (m/f/d) – 100%

Introduction

We are looking for a strong and future-oriented personality who is responsible for and actively develops our technical organisation throughout Switzerland. In this key position, you report directly to the CEO and help shape the technological future of Dallmayr Vending & Office Switzerland.

Your tasks *

  • You develop the technical strategy and drive the further development of our machine park.
  • They are responsible for the evaluation and introduction of new automatons, technologies and technical solutions.
  • They control payment systems, connectivity and IoT and recognize the potential of new technologies such as AI for our processes and services.
  • They define uniform technical standards, processes and quality requirements throughout Switzerland.
  • You lead your own team as well as the regional technical managers and promote cooperation between our offices.
  • They lead technical and cross-divisional projects and work closely with sales, manufacturers, suppliers and technology partners.

Qualifications

  • Technical education or studies, e.g. in engineering, electrical engineering, automation or mechatronics.
  • Several years of management and professional experience in a comparable technical function.
  • Good knowledge of automata, IoT, connected systems and digital technologies
  • Knowledge of AI and payment systems is beneficial.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, a high level of initiative, flexibility and a strong hands-on mentality.
  • High social and communication skills as well as an appreciative and motivating leadership style.
  • German or ösisch as mother tongue with very good knowledge of the other language
  • Italian is beneficial. High performance and willingness for regular travel within Switzerland.
  • Category B driving licence

We offer

A responsible key position with a direct reporting line to the CEO, great scope for design and the opportunity to actively shape our technical organization and future technologies.

They work in a modern environment around machines, payment systems, IoT, connectivity and digital innovations and benefit from attractive employment conditions including business vehicles.

Application information

We look forward to your online application and getting to know you personally. Please indicate your availability in your application.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
