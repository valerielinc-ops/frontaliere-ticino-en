Film and video producer — Brack.Alltron AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • Develop creative moving image concepts for our internal and external communication and translate ideas into convincing storyboards, scripts and production concepts
  • Film and video productions independently plan, organize and realize – including camera, light and sound
  • Responsible for the entire post-production – from video editing and color correction to motion and sound design to animation and compositing
  • Producing versatile content such as social media clips, campaign and image films, interviews and event content and optimizing it for different channels and platforms
  • Use Generative AI specifically in film, photo and content production and combine new technologies with classic production methods
  • Further develop AI-supported workflows and thus make conception, production and post-production more creative and efficient
  • Keep an eye on new technologies and trends around Generative AI, Virtual Production and Synthetic Media and actively contribute your knowledge to the team and to the advice of our internal stakeholders

Requirements

  • Completed studies in the field of film and video production or comparable training and relevant professional experience of several years
  • Solid experience in conventional camera and sound guidance as well as in post-production
  • Very good knowledge of Adobe Creative Cloud, especially Premiere Pro and After Effects
  • Experience with VFX (Visual Effects) is an advantage
  • In-depth practical experience in film and photography as well as a good understanding of current AI tools, their possibilities and limitations
  • Experience in prompt engineering and in connecting AI-supported creative processes with classic film production
  • Creativity, a high visual quality standard and an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Confident knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Negotiating knowledge of English is an advantage.
  • Valid category B ticket and enjoyment of working with different teams and stakeholders

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Super-compulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business appointments.
