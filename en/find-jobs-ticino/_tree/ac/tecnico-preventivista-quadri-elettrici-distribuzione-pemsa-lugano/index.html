Mission

  • Direct recruitment at the customer company.
  • Analysis of customer requests.
  • Evaluation of technical feasibility.
  • Production of commercial offers and quotes for electric paintings (low/medium voltage, automation or distribution).
  • Active collaboration with designers, workshop and suppliers.
  • Analyze specifications, technical specifications, electrical schematics and supply requests (RFQ) from customers.
  • Configure and dimensionalize the quadristic by identifying the suitable carpentry and components (changers, PLC, inverter, relay, etc.).
  • Draw out the base (BOM) of materials and calculate labor costs for wiring.
  • Development of the final economic offer and sending the quotation to the customer.
  • Interface with electrical material suppliers for requesting dedicated quotations or updating of lists.
  • Offer pre-sales technical support to customers and the commercial network.

Profile

  • Diploma in Electrical / Electronics or Bachelor of Electrical Engineering.
  • Excellent readability and interpretation of electric patterns and unifilari/multifilari designs.
  • Knowledge of the main preventive software and CAD tools.
  • Deep knowledge of industry regulations.
  • Guidance to the result, precision, analytical skills and strong attitude to teamwork.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
