Full description

Are you looking for a professional challenge parallel to your studies? Are you interested in marketing and brand management? Do you want to use your knowledge in practice, help shape diverse marketing measures and gain valuable professional experience?

Then we offer you an exciting perspective: With us you can contribute your skills in a motivated and versatile team, take responsibility and gain valuable experience for your further professional path.

For the Corporate Service Center in Dübendorf we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement one/one

Working student Marketing Services, 60%

Your area of responsibility

  • Under professional supervision, you will manage a defined region, including our companies in Ticino, and you will be the contact person for a wide range of marketing concerns.
  • You support in the planning, control and coordination of marketing measures at local and regional level and implement selected measures independently, coordinated with central campaigns
  • You develop and realize target group-specific content, such as stories and image material
  • You help to ensure a CD/CI-compliant brand presence across all touchpoints and advertising materials, such as brochures, advertisements, website and merchandising
  • As part of your tasks, you coordinate external partners from the areas of text, image and graphics
  • If necessary, take on smaller design tasks in InDesign and Photoshop

Your profile

  • Ideally, you already have a bachelor’s degree in marketing, communication, business administration or a comparable field and are currently in the master’s degree program.
  • You have ideally already gained practical experience in marketing
  • You have very good MS Office knowledge and good user knowledge in Adobe InDesign
  • You enjoy project management and organization and also keep an eye on priorities and deadlines for several parallel tasks
  • You work in a structured, independent and conscientious manner and bring a pronounced hands-on mentality as well as service orientation
  • You are a communicative and team-oriented personality and enjoy working with different internal and external contacts
  • German and Italian you master at native language level
  • Ideally, you also have good knowledge of French.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status.
