Full description

For our care department we are looking for an immediately or by agreement Stv. station line 80\-100% (m/f/d) with prospect of station line function

Do you want to take on responsibility in everyday care, support a team and develop into a leadership function? Then we look forward to meeting you.

Your main tasks

  • You support the station management in the professional and organizational management of the nursing department and represent them in their absence.
  • You coordinate the daily processes and use employees according to their competences.
  • You take care of reference care, plan and control the care process and ensure careful care documentation as well as the BESA\ recording.
  • You accompany new employees and learners, promote cooperation in the team and participate in case discussions.
  • You are actively involved in the project to convert BESA to interRAI LTCF.

Your profile

  • Dipl. Care specialist HF/FH with professional experience in care in Switzerland, preferably in long-term care
  • Developing interest in leadership
  • Enjoyment in accompanying and developing a team organizational skill, clear communication and an independent, responsible working method
  • Safe handling of digital applications and very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Our offer *
  • A versatile task with room for manoeuvre and prospect of taking over the station management
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation and the possibility of unpaid vacation
  • Modern, innovative workplace with very good public transport connections
  • Free use of the existing wellness area and cost reduction during consumption in the associated gourmet restaurant VA BENE

If you are interested, we look forward to meeting you personally. Please submit your complete online application with photo via our homepage

For further information, Mrs. Cindy Flury \- management care \& care is at your disposal.
