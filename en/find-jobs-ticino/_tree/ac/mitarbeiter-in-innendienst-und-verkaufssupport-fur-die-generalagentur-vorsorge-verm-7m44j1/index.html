Full description

80 - 100%, Location: Basel

Shape your future – together with us!

To strengthen our office team, we are looking for an upbeat, flexible personality.

Start your career in an agency at AXA, the No. 1! We are at our customers' side as a competent partner – in every situation. Are you too?

This is what awaits you

  • Advice and support for customers, mainly on the telephone or at customer reception
  • Support for pension advisors in arranging customer appointments
  • Pre- and post-processing of customer appointments (sales support)
  • Administrative support for pension advisors
  • Preparation of offers and applications
  • General correspondence

What you bring to the table

  • Commercial training in the insurance industry or many years of experience in the insurance industry is mandatory
  • Passing the intermediary examination (VBV) is an advantage
  • Willingness to undergo regular further training
  • Enjoyment of expanding existing customer relationships
  • Reliable, resilient personality combined with a quick way of working
  • High level of commitment as well as customer and service orientation
  • Convincing appearance with pleasure in dealing with people
  • Positive and winning manner, salesy flair
  • Good knowledge of written and spoken German, other languages are an advantage

What we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you the space to develop and fully exploit your potential. As a leading Swiss insurer, we offer ambitious people attractive career opportunities and entrepreneurial freedom.

  • Discount of up to 30% on AXA products
  • 5 weeks vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-fare travelcard/GA (pro-rata)
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Scope for independent action
  • Innovative structures, processes and modern tools
  • Well-founded, professional training and further education opportunities
