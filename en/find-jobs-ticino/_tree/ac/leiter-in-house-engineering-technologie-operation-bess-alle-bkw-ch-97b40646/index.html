Full description

Large-scale battery storage systems are an important component of the energy future. In this key role, you and your team will ensure the technological competence of BU BESS and ensure that our facilities meet technical requirements, are safely operated and economically optimized.

Your field of activity

  • You build in-house engineering organizationally and methodically, lead your team and create clear roles, processes, methods and decision-making processes.
  • You will further develop the employees technically and methodically and establish the necessary BESS competence for an independent technical assessment
  • Employer’s requirements and standards define the requirements for general planners, EPC contractors and suppliers and establish effective reviews and verification processes for our new construction projects
  • You design operational preparation, establish operational KPIs and create structures for systematic analysis of plant performance
  • Together with the energy industry, you will develop methods for the techno-economic optimization of performance, degradation and production costs
  • You will establish technical and cyber/OT security governance in cooperation with Group Security and ensure that experience is systematically incorporated into standards and requirements.

Your expertise

  • You have a university degree in electrical engineering FH/ETH or a comparable education and have experience with complex energy systems and technical tenders
  • Sound (BESS) competence enables you to independently assess external technical solutions and verify their implementation
  • You bring leadership experience and enjoy building a new unit, developing employees and effectively delegating responsibility
  • With conceptual thinking, you create pragmatic methods, processes and structures that give your team orientation and enable reliable decisions - combining technical understanding with business perspectives and moving safely between technology, project, EPC and operation
