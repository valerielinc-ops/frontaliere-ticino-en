Team leader Caritas Care – carers 80-100 %

With us, you take on responsibility in a versatile and meaningful environment. Their tasks are close to people, varied and of immediate importance. In the offer "Care for relatives", we enable clients in need of care to receive the best possible, person-centered care from their relatives. The caring relatives are not alone: they are professionally advised, accompanied and supported by our care specialists HF/FH. For your valuable work, they are honored and insured accordingly.

A role – as individual as you

For the management of our offer in the canton of Bern, we are looking for a team management care HF/FH by agreement. In this versatile function, you will take over the professional and personnel leadership of a team of case-leading nurses HF/FH. At the same time, as a member of the super-cantonal committee Pflege, you actively participate in the design and further development of the offer. Direct work with clients also remains part of your task: As a case-leading care specialist, you control and accompany the care process and ensure a high, person-centered care quality. In doing so, you advise and support the caring relatives competently and according to needs.
