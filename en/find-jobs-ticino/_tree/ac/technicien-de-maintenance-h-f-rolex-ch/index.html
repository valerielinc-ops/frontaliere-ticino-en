Full description

Introduction

For our Procurement, Support & Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a Maintenance Technician (M/F) whose main mission will be to ensure the corrective and controlled maintenance of equipment under our responsibility by taking into account all tasks, condition and changing needs.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure corrective maintenance of equipment through full analysis by identifying the root cause
  • Conduct preventive maintenance
  • Make proposals for reliability and participate in changes
  • Apply current diagnostic methodology
  • Participate in equipment acquisition projects
  • Ensuring the transfer of skills to colleagues
  • Partnering with the various Rolex services during group work (industrialization, B&I, IT, environmental security) and with suppliers
  • Respect Rolex safety and security guidelines in the performance of its work

Profile

  • Automatic CFC + ES Technician
  • Technical versatility confirmed
  • Electrical, mechanical, pneumatic, hydraulic skills
  • Skills in automation, digital controls and robotics
  • Team spirit, listening, sociable and friendly, easy communication
  • Spirit of synthesis, rigorous, technical curiosity, entrepreneurial and autonomous
  • Team hours 2X8

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
