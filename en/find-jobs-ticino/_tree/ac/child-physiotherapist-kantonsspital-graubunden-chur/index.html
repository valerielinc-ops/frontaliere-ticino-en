  • You treat inpatient and outpatient patients – in individual and group therapies with a focus on medicine, intensive care medicine and neurology
  • You work closely and interdisciplinary with the therapy team and other professional groups and ensure professional communication

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training as a physiotherapist / physiotherapist
  • You will bring several years of professional experience as well as relevant further training in the specialist area and in the hospital environment
  • You show commitment and joy in the education of students and actively contribute to the transfer of knowledge in the team
