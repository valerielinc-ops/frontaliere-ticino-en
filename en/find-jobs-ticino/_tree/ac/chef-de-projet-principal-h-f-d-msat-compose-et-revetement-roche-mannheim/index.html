The Position About Roche – Near Patient Care Operations Doing now what patients need next. The Customer Area Near Patient Care (NPC) brings together Roche’s point-of-care and diabetes care portfolios. Our goal is to improve dieselase management by bringing critical responses and personalized care closer to the people we are committed to. With our passion, we bring tests closer to patients, empower people to better monitor their own health, and help healthcare professionals make faster and safer decisions right at the site of treatment. Within NPC Operations, we are globally responsible for the end-to-end production of solutions for continuous glucose measurement (CGM), blood sugar measurement (BGM) and insulin delivery systems (IDS), as well as point-of-care (POC) products. The most important internal production sites are Mannheim in Germany and Indianapolis in the USA.

Your new team As Manufacturing Science and Technology (MSAT) in NPC Operations, we build the bridge between development and production. With around 200 colleagues at the Mannheim, Indianapolis and Rotkreuz sites, we lead the technology strategy, the industrialization of product design and the cooperation with external partners throughout the entire product lifecycle. As a project-based organization, we enable scalable product designs for mass production, ensure robust processes and facilities for profitable results, and ensure scalable quality control (QC) processes. In cooperation with external networks, we ensure competitive production costs and reliable production. We are committed to building a global, diverse and inclusive team that brings together diverse perspectives and skills to drive innovation and create an excellent working environment for all.

The position As Senior Project Lead (m/f/d) in our Project Delivery Team within MSAT You have end-to-end responsibility for handling highly complex, cross-functional projects in NPC operations, such as technology transfers or product care projects. These are usually large, global and cross-divisional projects within NPC Operations with investment volumes of more than 20 million euros. The teams work very closely with R&D, quality assurance and local location functions. You take over the functional matrix management of large teams consisting of various members such as technical specialists and sub-teams and ensure smooth and efficient project management within NPC operations and beyond. You are a project management professional and anchor an end-to-end ownership mindset in your project team. You ensure that projects are not considered completed until the desired business results are fully achieved. Do you want to passionately advance complex projects in a very dynamic environment? Then become part of MSAT and unleash your potential in our Project Delivery Team!
