Full description

Rivera (and work at home)

What you can contribute to

  • Prepare arms projects, accompany them and introduce them into training after their acquisition
  • To assume responsibility for concepts and development in the field of special forces and their implementation
  • Carrying out projects within the various specialised fields
  • To serve in various specialized bodies and project groups as a representative of operational needs and interests of the Special Forces Command
  • Develop and implement training plans, training concepts and curricula in assigned training areas, and support the planning, organization and conduct of large-scale training and intervention exercises

What makes you unique

  • SWC followed by advanced vocational training and several years of professional experience in project management or armaments
  • In-depth knowledge in the field of weapons of special forces, preferably in command assistance, transmissions, sensors for exploration and ability to combat by all weather and night
  • Good oral and written knowledge in a second official language and English (minimum B2 level)
  • Officer or non-commissioned officer in the Swiss Army with a readiness to be (re)assigned to the troop within the Special Forces Command; fitness for military service and medical fitness (to be monitored during the recruitment process)

In a few words

As a specialist in equipment and aircraft engaged by special forces, you contribute, through your expertise, directly to the performance of the Swiss Army Special Forces Command, for whom extraordinary events are part of everyday life. In a dynamic environment, you are actively involved in the development of unique training by meeting its high material and operational readiness needs. You help him build his future and put your knowledge into practice.

Your commitment to security and freedom

The Command of Operations (cdmt Op) is responsible for all operations and military commitments of the Swiss Army. The CDmt Op includes the Air Force, the Land Forces, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Skills Centre, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. About 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are employed in civilian occupations, from engineers to analysts to mechanics on aircraft.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 employees in four military professions and around 200 civilian professions, at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. It is also responsible for succession by training apprentices in more than 30 trades.
