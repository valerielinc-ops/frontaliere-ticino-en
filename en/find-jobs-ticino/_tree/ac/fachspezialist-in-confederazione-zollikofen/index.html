On behalf of the Federal Council, the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDA) coordinates and shapes Swiss foreign policy, safeguards the country’s interests and promotes Swiss values.

The Directorate for Resources (DR) manages the use of personnel and finances to implement foreign policy goals. It operates the worldwide Swiss representation network. The Directorate shall provide the logistics, telematics, management and controlling services necessary for the management of the department. The department’s legislation, application and legal advice also fall within the remit of the Resources Directorate. It is also subordinate to the Federal Travel Office, which organises and buys international business trips.
