You want to change to the practice after your years in the hospital, but not start alone? In a modern, interdisciplinary medical center, you can expect a collegial team, your personal ultrasound device and a workload that fits your life. The ideal introduction to outpatient gynecology. An established practice group with several group practices in German-speaking Switzerland and more than 200 employees stands for comprehensive outpatient care. Multilingual teams of doctors, doctors and medical assistants: women take care of the well-being of patients at generous opening hours. Your future place of work is a modern medical center over three floors in a central location. Under one roof, family medicine, gynecology, cardiology, dermatology, orthopaedics as well as psychiatry and psychotherapy work closely together. The gynecological offer includes, among other things, pregnancy prevention, pregnancy ultrasound including 4D and colposcopy. The region in the Swiss Mittelland combines the advantages of a small town with the best accessibility: Zurich can be reached by train in about half an hour, Basel and Bern in about one hour.

Their tasks

  • Independent management of gynecological and obstetric consultation hours, from diagnosis to treatment planning
  • Ultrasound examinations with your personal, modern device, including in pregnancy prevention
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with colleagues from family medicine and other specialties
  • Professional guidance and supervision of the assistant staff
  • Documentation in the electronic patient record as well as participation in quality assurance and internal training

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in gynecology and obstetrics as well as at least three years of professional experience in the field at a recognised continuing education institution in Switzerland
  • Flowing knowledge of German (level C1), English and other languages of advantage
  • Patient-oriented, quality-conscious action, combined with empathy, team spirit and communication strength
  • Specialist title from abroad? In the case of MEBEKO recognition and vocational training permits, we provide you with advice and financial support.

Their advantages

  • Choose your workload between 40 and 100% yourself, part-time and flexible working hours included
  • Plannable working hours and employment conditions that adapt to your life
  • Own modern ultrasound device and contemporary equipped practice rooms
  • A dedicated, cross-disciplinary team in which open and collegial communication takes place
  • Further education and professional development specifically promoted
  • A compensation that rewards your performance fairly
  • Versatile everyday practice with insight into numerous specialist areas
