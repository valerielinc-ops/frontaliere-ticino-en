Full description

Weisse Arena Gruppe in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and a passionate community for unique mountain adventures. The group includes various hotels and gastronomy businesses, sports and rental shops, a ski, snowboard and bike school, as well as a management and a construction company. Our success? A motivated and committed team that lives their passion every day. Do you want to contribute with digital solutions to ensure a consistent and engaging experience for guests and employees? Join our team at Weisse Arena Gruppe and help us further develop our digital platform. We are looking for a highly motivated Lifecycle Marketing Manager with strong customer understanding and analytical expertise. As an important part of our E-Commerce team, you will work closely with the Product Owner, Data Analyst and marketing stakeholders to engage, inspire and retain customers with smart, data-driven campaigns. ## Tasks

  • Planning, building, implementing and optimizing email and push campaigns throughout the entire customer lifecycle (onboarding, activation, retention, reactivation)
  • Future development and maintenance of automated marketing processes (e.g., welcome flows, cart abandonment, re-engagement campaigns, On Mountain campaigns)
  • Segmentation and personalization of target groups based on customer behavior, purchase history and interests
  • Creation and analysis of A/B tests to optimize open, click and conversion rates
  • Ensuring a consistent brand message
  • Close collaboration with design, content and marketing to ensure consistent brand communication
  • Reporting & KPI tracking for continuous development of the lifecycle strategy
  • Evaluation of new tools and trends in CRM, automation and customer engagement ## Requirements
  • Completed education or degree in Communication, Digital Business or professional experience in campaign management
  • Analytical and economic mindset
  • Motivated to drive goals and KPIs such as Customer Lifetime Value
  • Basic technical understanding of engagement campaigns via CRM tools
  • High level of personal initiative – you recognize what needs to be done and take action before being asked
  • Strong communication skills – you proactively engage with people and ask the right questions
  • Structured, solution-oriented and positive work approach
  • Good proficiency in German and English, both written and spoken ## What we offer
  • An attractive workplace in the midst of breathtaking mountain scenery
  • The opportunity to grow within our dynamic, vibrant team and lively corporate culture
  • Numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, discounts in our shops, hotels and gastronomy businesses, as well as advantages for your family ## Contact
