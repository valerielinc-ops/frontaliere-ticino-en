Full description

Mission

The assistant of the Civil Engineering Section is responsible for the follow-up of bachelor's and master's courses in civil engineering at the ENAC faculty. He or she supports the section director by providing an overview of the teaching programs and contributing to their consistency and quality in order to provide students with the best possible educational conditions.

In the course of his or her duties, he or she has a close and constructive working relationship with the students, teachers and teachers of the section, with the services of EPFL (academic service, vice-presidency for education, decant of ENAC, among others), as well as with the assistants and assistants of the other sections of ENAC.

The Assistant ensures the operational functioning of the Section and leads the Section Office team. He or she also assists the Director in the development and implementation of the section's strategic vision and in the conduct of other strategic projects.

Main tasks and responsibilities

Guidance

  • Informing and advising students on the administration of studies
  • Participate in the development and implementation of annual study plans
  • Ensure consistency of the section's operation with the other sections of the ENAC faculty and EPFL
  • Coordinate the organisation of study visits
  • Manage the Civil Engineering Section Secretariat
  • Analyze student results and work with the academic department to modify their study plans

Promotion

  • Evaluate and develop existing and potential exchange programme partnerships with the Civil Engineering Section and EPFL Studies Promotion Service
  • Participate in the promotion of studies at EPFL to attract young people to studies in civil engineering.

Support

  • Assist the section director and link teachers to students and/or their representatives
  • Assist in the development of in-service training in civil engineering and interdisciplinary minors in collaboration with other sections of the ENAC Faculty and EPFL
  • Prepare teaching statistics and various reports
  • Prepare presentations by the section director for academic sessions

Profile

  • Doctorate/Post-Doc/ in the field of engineering or Master in civil engineering with relevant professional experience
  • Knowledge or interest in the civil engineering curriculum
  • Experience in setting up a monitoring and monitoring system for studies, an asset
  • Good oral and written communication capacity in French and English
  • Accuracy, rigor, student orientation and solutions
  • High autonomy and proactivity
  • Languages: full mastery of French and English (working languages)
