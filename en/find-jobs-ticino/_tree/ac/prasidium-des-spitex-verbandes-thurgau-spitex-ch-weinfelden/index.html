Full description

In healthcare, Spitex operates in an environment of constant change and new challenges. It is becoming increasingly important in ambulatory care. The Spitex Association Thurgau is an important point of contact for care, help and care. The election will take place at the Annual Meeting of the 27th. April 2027. Executive Committee of the Spitex Association Thurgau

Their role
