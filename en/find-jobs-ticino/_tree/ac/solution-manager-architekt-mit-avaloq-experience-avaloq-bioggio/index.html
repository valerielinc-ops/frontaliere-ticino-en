Full description

Founded and headquartered in Switzerland, Avaloq is continuously expanding its global footprint with around 2,500 colleagues in 10 countries, and more than 160 clients in 35 countries. We are an industry-leading provider of wealth management technology and services for financial institutions around the world, including private banks and wealth managers, investment managers, as well as retail and neo banks. Our research led approach and continual innovation is powered by the passion and creativity of our colleagues. We are always looking for talented people to join us on our mission to orchestrate the financial ecosystem and democratize access to wealth management. Avaloq offers the opportunity to work closely with some of the world’s leading financial institutions as we jointly develop and shape careers. Championing a collaborative, supportive and flexible work environment empowers our colleagues to reach their full potential.

We are seeking an experienced Solution Manager - Architect with deep Avaloq expertise to join our organization. In this role, you will design, architect, and oversee the implementation of comprehensive banking solutions using the Avaloq platform. You will serve as a technical leader, bridging the gap between business requirements and technical execution while ensuring solutions are scalable, efficient, and aligned with organizational objectives.

  • Design and architect end-to-end banking solutions leveraging Avaloq platform capabilities and best practices
  • Analyze complex business requirements and translate them into detailed technical specifications and solution designs
  • Lead solution implementation projects, ensuring timely delivery, quality standards, and stakeholder satisfaction
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including business analysts, developers, and quality assurance professionals to execute solution roadmaps
  • Provide technical guidance and mentorship to development teams throughout the solution lifecycle
  • Conduct solution reviews and assessments to identify optimization opportunities and areas for improvement
  • Manage stakeholder communications, presenting technical concepts and progress updates to both technical and non-technical audiences
  • Customize and configure Avaloq modules to meet specific client and organizational requirements
  • Document solution architectures, design decisions, and implementation approaches for knowledge transfer and future reference
  • Stay current with Avaloq platform updates, features, and industry trends to inform solution strategy
  • Support pre-sales activities by providing technical expertise and solution recommendations to prospective clients

Qualifiche

  • Minimum 5+ years of professional experience with Avaloq platform implementation and configuration
  • Proven expertise in banking and financial services solution architecture
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to design scalable technical solutions
  • Demonstrated experience managing complex solution implementations from design through deployment
  • Proficiency in system integration, APIs, and data management within banking environments
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, with ability to present technical information clearly
  • Experience with project management methodologies and tools
  • Knowledge of banking regulations, compliance requirements, and industry standards
  • Strong organizational and time management skills with ability to manage multiple priorities

It would be a real bonus if you have

  • Avaloq certification or formal training credentials
  • Experience leading technical teams or mentoring junior architects
  • Background in change management and organizational transformation initiatives
  • Experience with multiple banking system implementations and migrations
  • Fluency in German
