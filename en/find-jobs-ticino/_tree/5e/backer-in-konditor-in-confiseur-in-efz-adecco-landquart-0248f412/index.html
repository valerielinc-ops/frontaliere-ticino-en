Introduction

For our customer, an established company in the food trade, we are looking for a committed and quality-conscious personality as a baker/in-confector/in-confiser/in EFZ. In this versatile function, you are responsible for the production of high-quality baking, pastry and confectionery products and actively contribute to ensuring consistently high product quality.

Tasks

  • Manufacture of bread, small bread, pastry and seasonal specialities
  • Production of pastry and confectionery products according to specified recipes
  • Professional processing and preparation of raw materials
  • Ensuring quality, hygiene and food standards
  • Control and monitoring of production processes
  • Cooperation in the development and implementation of new products

Profile

  • Completed training as baker/in-confectioner/in EFZ
  • Professional experience in a comparable function is beneficial
  • Independent, careful and reliable working methods
  • High quality awareness and a strong understanding of hygiene
  • Team-capable, resilient and motivated personality
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Advantages

  • Current employment conditions
  • Attractive compensation with good social benefits
  • Modern work equipment and professional infrastructure
  • Thorough training and support by a collegial team
  • Opportunities for further education and development
  • Long-term perspective in a stable working environment
