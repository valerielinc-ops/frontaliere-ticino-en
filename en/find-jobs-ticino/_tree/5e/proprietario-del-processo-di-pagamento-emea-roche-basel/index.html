The Position The Opportunity As a Payroll Process Owner, you will be a key driver in streamlining and optimizing the delivery of payroll, time, and benefits (PTB) services across the EMEA region. Reporting to the Global PTB Lead, you will act as a strategic bridge between regional operational teams and global product management to ensure our solutions are both efficient and scalable.

  • Act as a strategic partner and regional representative for PTB operational teams, ensuring alignment with Global Best Practices.
  • Define end-to-end processes in collaboration with local PTB leads and cross-functional partners in Finance, Treasury, and IT.
  • Prioritize regional requirements by collecting business needs and balancing them against global priorities and roadmap planning.
  • Drive harmonization and simplification of processes and practices across the region to foster a "OneRoche" approach.
  • Collaborate with Product Owners (specifically Workday Time & Absence) to support technical discussions and system round tables.
  • Manage vendor relationships alongside global leadership, interfacing with key partners such as Workday, ADP, and Deloitte.
  • Support service governance by overseeing regional KPIs, Service Catalogs, and compliance audits (e.g., ICFR and SOD controls).
