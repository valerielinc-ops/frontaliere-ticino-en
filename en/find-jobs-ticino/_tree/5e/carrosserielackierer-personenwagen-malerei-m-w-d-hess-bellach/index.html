Full description

Car body painting (m/f/d)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. We also offer a comprehensive range of repairs and services for vehicles of all kinds (buses, commercial vehicles, passenger cars, campers, etc.).

To complement our team in the Repair & Service division, we are looking for a motivated and experienced car body painter for P ersonenwagen painting. Do you have an apprenticeship as a car body painter and do you have a passion for cars? We offer you an interesting activity in our PW painting. With your expertise, you make the cars of our customers appear in new glory! Their tasks

  • Independent processing of paint jobs on passenger cars and campers
  • Perform alternative repairs (e.g. smart repair)
  • Responsibility for the timely delivery of the painted vehicles

Our requirements

  • Apprenticeship degree as a carrosserie painter (Carrossier/in Lackiererei)
  • Professional experience of several years
  • High level of readiness
  • Independence, reliability and teamwork

We offer them

  • Interesting, varied and challenging activity
  • Moving working time
  • Performance-based conditions of employment
  • Opportunity for further education and development

If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
