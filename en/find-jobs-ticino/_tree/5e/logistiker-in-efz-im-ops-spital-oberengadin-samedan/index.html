Full description

The Spital Oberengadin AG in Samedan, a company of the KSGR Group, is responsible for basic medical care as well as emergency care for the population and guests. We are looking for the surgical team at 1. March 2027 or by agreement a motivated

Their tasks

  • Independent assumption of the role as a care assistant in the OPS
  • Ordering, incoming goods inspection and order booking
  • professional storage of the product groups under management
  • Management and monitoring of stocks of all consumables for the OPS and anesthesia area
  • Checking the expiration dates of all implants, consumables and sterile goods
  • Implementation of the annual inventory

Your profile

  • Completed training as logistician/EFZ
  • Professional experience, beneficial in the hospital sector
  • Knowledge of logistical requirements for medical consumables and warehouse management
  • Good computer skills (MS-Office)
  • Strong cost awareness
  • Teamwork and flexibility (optimal willingness to provide services depending on seasonal load)
  • German in word and writing, Italian of advantage

Pensum: 40-60 %
