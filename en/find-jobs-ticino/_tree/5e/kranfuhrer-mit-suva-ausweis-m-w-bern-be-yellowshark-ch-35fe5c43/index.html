Crane operator with SUVA licence (m/f)

3020 Berne

Building construction

  • Temporary

Ofis

01 Future employer

Are you curious and ready for new challenges? We are continually looking for motivated crane operators with a SUVA licence for temporary positions on exciting assignments with our clients. If you enjoy taking responsibility and working with modern machinery, this position could be exactly right for you.

02 Your task

  • As a crane operator with a SUVA licence, you discuss the work process and the tasks to be completed with the site manager or foreman every day in advance.
  • Your main responsibility is operating top-slewing and bottom-slewing cranes, both with and without remote control.
  • It is important that you always work with focus and safety awareness to ensure smooth operations on the construction site.
  • In addition to your work as a crane operator, you will also carry out general construction-site work as needed, completing it with the necessary care and sense of responsibility.
  • Our clients place great value on a friendly, customer-oriented manner, which you can demonstrate through your communication skills and professional conduct.

04 Your profile

  • SUVA crane operator licence
  • Several years of professional experience as a crane operator
  • Good physical fitness
  • Aged between 25 and 50
  • Good German language skills
  • Knowledge of occupational safety

05 Good to know

Only electronic applications can be considered. Applications sent by post will not be returned.
