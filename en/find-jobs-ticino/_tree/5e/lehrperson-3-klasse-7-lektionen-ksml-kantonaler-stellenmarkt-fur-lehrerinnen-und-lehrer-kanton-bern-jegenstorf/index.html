Full description

Portrait The municipality of Jegenstorf (about 5800 inhabitants) has two school facilities. In the comprehensive school, about 680 school-aged children and adolescents are taught in 32 classes in year classes. Day school and school social work are part of our school offer. The school location Säget is in the center of the village and easily accessible by public transport.

Tasks

They teach 7 lessons in a 3rd grade with 16 SuS (Franz, NMG, music and sports) as a part-time teacher. There is also a close integrative cooperation with the responsible curative pedagogue for this class. School social work is available. Collaboration in the class team at school events and special weeks. Participation in school house and cycle conferences. Working day: Motion: 08.20-11.50 hrs. 13.30-16.05 hrs.

Requirements

  • Bachelor of Arts in Primary Education, a primary teaching patent or equivalent degree are desired.
  • Great pleasure in the profession and the teaching of heterogeneous classes. Teaching experience desired.
  • Team capable, flexible, communicative, dutiful and positive personality

We offer

  • Established interdisciplinary cooperation in a committed college
  • Exciting field of work with a wide range of design options for teaching
  • Good connection to public transport, free parking available
  • Work laptop of the school, Microsoft 365
  • temporary employment according to cantonal employment conditions (LAG), ev. Extension possible
