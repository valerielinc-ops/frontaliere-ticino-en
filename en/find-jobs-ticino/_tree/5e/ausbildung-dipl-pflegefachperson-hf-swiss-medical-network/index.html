Full description

  • Training to become a qualified nursing specialist takes 2-3 years, depending on previous training
  • Responsibility for the care and support of people in different life spans.
  • Professional responsibility and discussion of the nursing process
  • Accompanying patients and their relatives in difficult situations
  • Close collaboration between intra- and interdisciplinary teams
  • Sense of responsibility, physical and psychological resilience and quick comprehension
  • Service orientation, patience and sensitivity in dealing with people
  • Enjoyment of nursing and medical-technical activities and teamwork
  • good manners as well as adaptability and communication skills
  • Completed professional apprenticeship with federal certificate. Certificate of competency (health specialist EFZ is an advantage) or a school qualification FMS or Matura
  • Passed or ongoing aptitude test (the form can be downloaded directly from bz-gs.ch)
  • 2 or 3 years full-time training to become a qualified nursing specialist HF (shortened course of study possible as a health specialist EFZ)
  • Training positions in the field of somatics
  • A well-positioned, competent team that accompanies and supports you during your training
  • Varied training in a lively but manageable environment with an internship in another field of work
  • You benefit from a wide range of discount offers from the Obach Private Clinic and the Swiss Medical Network group
  • 5 weeks vacation

The Obach Private Clinic is something very special in the Swiss hospital landscape: a small institution that has been committed to providing medical care at the highest level for 100 years. An institution that, thanks to its ideal location, is both close to the city and in a relaxing, green environment. A little gem that fulfills its cantonal service mandate with personal care in a private atmosphere for patients from all insurance classes. The Obach Private Clinic offers a wide range of services with a particular focus on orthopedics and urology.
