As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

YOUR TASKS

  • Head of the anesthesia care team with direct management and support of the approximately 30 employees at the St. Gallen site
  • Economic management of the department as well as qualified personnel planning
  • Ensuring interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation in the OR and related interfaces
  • Co-responsibility for developing and monitoring the required safety and quality standards
  • Co-responsibility in further education of Dipl. Experts Anesthesia Care NDS HF
  • Working as a specialist in anesthesia care in customer-oriented professional care of our patients

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Expert / Expert Anesthesia Care NDS HF with several years of professional and managerial experience
  • Management training, preferably level CAS Leadership, or the willingness to complete it
  • Hands-on mentality with the willingness to respond to changes and carry them together with the team
  • You are an independent, resilient and responsible personality with a high level of professional and social competence
  • Strong organizational and communication skills, good assertiveness and well-versed in dealing with MS-Office and clinic information systems

Do you want to work in a dynamic and innovative environment where you can fully develop your strengths, know-how and competencies? We create an inspiring environment where your ideas and contributions are appreciated and you have the chance to make a positive impact on people’s well-being.
