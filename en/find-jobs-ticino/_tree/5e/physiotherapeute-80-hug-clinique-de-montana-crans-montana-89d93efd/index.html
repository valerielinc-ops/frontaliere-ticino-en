Full description

You develop, propose, apply and evaluate physiotherapy treatments in accordance with the physiotherapeutic assessment, diagnosis and medical prescription to recover, improve or maintain the bio-psycho-social functions and activities of patients.

You ensure individual or group management of patients and ensure their physical and psychological comfort throughout the therapeutic process. You also use educational approaches such as therapeutic education, promoting treatment, prevention and health education.

You are actively involved in interdisciplinary exchanges, symposia and case discussions. You work closely with doctors, health professionals and health professionals and all care partners to ensure coordinated and high-quality care.

You ensure the traceability of interventions in the patient file, participate in the management and maintenance of therapeutic equipment, and contribute to the continuous improvement of service delivery.

You participate in the reception, integration and supervision of new collaborators, students and trainees. You also contribute to the development of professional knowledge through your participation in training activities and, where appropriate, clinical research projects.

Rigourous or rigorous, autonomous and organized or organized, you demonstrate excellent relational skills and enjoy working as an interprofessional team in a dynamic environment.

You have a Bachelor's degree in physiotherapy or a diploma deemed equivalent recognised for the practice of the profession in Switzerland. Specialized post-graduate training, such as a CAS as a trainer, is an asset.

You have a strong interest in the rehabilitation and interdisciplinary care of the patient. An experience in hospital or rehabilitation is an asset.

You have an excellent command of oral and written French (minimum level C1 or mother tongue).

You are comfortable with computer tools and electronic care documentation. You have autonomy, rigor, organization and good relational abilities.

You appreciate working as an interprofessional team and recognize yourself in the values of trust, respect, team spirit, recognition, equality and inclusion.
