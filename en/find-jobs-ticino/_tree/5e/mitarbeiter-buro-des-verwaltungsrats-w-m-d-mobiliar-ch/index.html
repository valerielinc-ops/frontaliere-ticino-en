Mitarbeiter Büro des Verwaltungsrats (w/m/d) — die Mobiliar

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

This is what awaits you

In this versatile function, you are part of the VR office and work close to the top organs of the furniture. You can expect a varied area of responsibility with an insight into the corporate governance of a cooperative company.

You work in a small team and are in close contact with different contacts: inside.

This is what you do with us

Together with internal and external bodies, you will organize the delegate meeting and regional delegate events and ensure a smooth process. You prepare invitations, detailed programs, presentations, scripts and other documents and coordinate their dispatch. You also support the preparation and follow-up of the meetings of the Board of Directors of the Swiss Mobiliar Cooperative Society.

You look after our delegates in administrative matters, manage the member and address administration and take care of compensation and other organizational tasks. You also participate in the organization and administration of the delegate elections and maintain the corresponding correspondence. In addition, you support the Secretary General in organizational and administrative matters.

This is what you bring with you

  • Commercial training and several years of experience in a demanding assistant, secretarial or comparable function
  • Independent, reliable and careful working methods, strong organisational talent and the ability to keep track of several parallel tasks
  • Discretion and a sovereign approach to different contacts:
  • Very good user knowledge of Microsoft 365 applications, including Microsoft Copilot, and ChatGPT; Openness and interest to actively use the possibilities of AI in everyday work
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of French

Working with us

You can expect a small, experienced team with short distances and close cooperation. You will serve as assistant to the Secretary General together with a colleague on a part-time basis. Due to the appointments of our organs, an assignment in the evening is necessary. Incidentally, the function offers a well-planned part-time workload and a lot of variety and personal contact.

Your place of work is Bern and the workload is 60% or 70%.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
