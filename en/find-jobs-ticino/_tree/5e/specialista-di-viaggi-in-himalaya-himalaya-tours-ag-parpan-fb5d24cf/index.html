Himalayan travel specialist (80 \- 100%)

Himalaya Tours is one of the leading Swiss tour operators for trekking, hiking and cultural tours in the Himalayas. We only offer our own trips, which we design ourselves from A to Z and organize together with our local teams – no third-party products.

Our daily work is as varied as our travel programme: we develop new trips, advise our customers, look after our website, are in close contact with our local employees in the Himalayas and take care of much more. No day is like the other! *

For 2027 we have put together a fresh, versatile travel program with numerous new and extraordinary trips to the most remote regions of the Himalayas. That’s why we want to expand our team and look for:

Job opening from January 2027 or by appointment

This is what we offer you

  • A varied and independent activity with a lot of personal responsibility
  • A small, family and uncomplicated team
  • A quiet workplace in a beautiful environment, without switch operation
  • Attractive employment conditions and additional benefits
  • A good work/life balance
  • Optional five or seven weeks holiday

You bring that with you

  • Own travel experience in the Himalayas
  • Ideally experience in tour operating – motivated lateral beginners are equally welcome
  • Enjoying contact with our customers and local partners in the Himalayas
  • Reliability, flexibility and resilience
  • Stylistic German and good English skills
  • Good user knowledge in MS Office and a secure handling of the computer

By the way, our office is located in (20 minutes from Chur) in the middle of the ski, bike and hiking region of Lenzerheide. *
