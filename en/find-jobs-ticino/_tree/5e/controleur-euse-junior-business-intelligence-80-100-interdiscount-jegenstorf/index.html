Full description

  • Job opening: 01.01.2027
  • Specialization: Category Management / Procurement
  • Location: Interdiscount, Bernstrasse 90, 3303 Jegenstorf

At Interdiscount, we offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Take responsibility, be proactive with us and develop further.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the information- and data-based control of central value-added areas along the procurement market, processes and sales market. You prepare relevant data, analyses and models in such a way that action priorities become visible and informed decisions can be supported.
  • You create ad hoc analyses, reports and KPI dashboards and continuously develop them to create transparency about sales, flow of goods, promotions, procurement, processes and performance.
  • You analyze and validate data, recognize patterns, deviations and optimization potentials and translate them into understandable decision-making bases for departments and leadership.
  • You support the prioritization and control of measures by showing data- and model-based where action is needed and what effect possible measures can have.
  • You work interdisciplinary in projects, clarify requirements with the departments, coordinate analytical tasks and present results in a target group-oriented and action-oriented manner.

Requirements

  • You have a completed degree or a comparable education in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Business Informatics, Computer Science, Business Administration/VWL or a related field.
  • You bring a strong analytical mindset and enjoy structuring, analyzing and comprehensible economic issues based on data.
  • You have good basics in data analysis, data validation and quantitative methods. First experience with Python, SQL, BI tools or similar analytical applications is an advantage.
  • You are interested in business processes, procurement and sales markets, and how data- and model-based insights can contribute to better decisions.
  • You work in a structured, independent and solution-oriented way, set priorities and move safely between day-to-day business, ad hoc inquiries and project tasks.
  • You are team-oriented, strong in communication and can present analytical results appropriate to target groups, understandable and action-oriented.
  • You have very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English. French or Italian are beneficial.
