Full description

Location: Medifilm AG, Ostringstrasse 10, 4702 Oensingen

Do you want to operate and technically develop business-critical production IT while managing a small team? With us, you are actively involved in the operational IT operation, solving demanding technical challenges and taking over the technical and personnel management of a three-person team.

You can do that.

  • You lead and develop our three-member IT Operations team and work actively in day-to-day business.
  • You work hands-on on our server, infrastructure and system landscape.
  • You ensure the stable, secure and GxP-compliant operation of production IT.
  • You will analyze and repair complex disorders, identify their causes and implement sustainable improvements.
  • You optimize existing operational processes and advance standardization, automation and monitoring.
  • You coordinate internal and external IT partners and ensure reliable support operations, including mission planning and pikett organization.

You bring that with you

  • Education or training in computer science (HF, FH or equivalent).
  • Several years of experience in engineering and operating server and infrastructure environments.
  • In-depth knowledge in Microsoft infrastructures, virtualization and production-related IT systems.
  • You bring the willingness to offset daily working hours, adapted to the operating hours of our production, as well as to occasional cooking
  • Enjoying technical challenges and a hands-on working method.
  • Initial leadership experience or motivation to assume professional and human leadership responsibility for a small team.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Extend competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-shaping healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
