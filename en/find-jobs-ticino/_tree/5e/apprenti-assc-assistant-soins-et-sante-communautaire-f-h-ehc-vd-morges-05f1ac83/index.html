Ensemble Hospitalier de la Côte

  • EHC Ensemble Hospitalier de la Côte — EHC is a healthcare network that has been serving you for over 20 years.   • Assistant care and community health (F/H) for our Medical-Social Establishments (EMS) in Morges and Aubonne:

  • A training plan that involves discovering 2 missions (geriatrics and advanced age psychiatry) within at least 2 different sites, between Morges and Aubonne.
  • The opportunity to discover the world of work within an institution in full evolution and whose mission is to take care of all our patients and residents.
  • The opportunity to benefit from a close follow-up during your apprenticeship, by experienced and involved trainers who will accompany you to the success of your CFC.

  • Free hot drinks in the morning and meals at CHF 8.50 in our cafeterias.

  • The opportunity to benefit from a defined time on the workstation for the preparation of your final exams.
  • A privileged access to fixed posts open on the entire network of our sites, from the end of your apprenticeship. What you have
  • A compulsory school leaving certificate or you are about to reach the end of your studies.
  • A well-defined professional project, thanks to an observation internship in EMS care that allowed you to confirm your choice, required.
  • A genuine motivation to engage for 3 years in the accompaniment of elderly and fragile people in their health, within our EMS in Morges or Aubonne.
  • A good adaptability capacity that allows you to follow a rotation of training involving a passage through 3 training services during your apprenticeship.
  • A dynamic, voluntary and empathetic nature, showing maturity and ease in teamwork.
  • A very good level of French, a good learning capacity and a sense of listening. Your objectives
  • Ensure the physical, social and psychological well-being of residents in our EMS by being attentive to their needs and habits.
  • Participate in the accompaniment of residents in daily life activities, as well as in the adaptation and maintenance of their environment, with their collaboration.
  • Provide care, apply certain treatments and perform medical-technical acts by delegation and under the responsibility of the nurse. Who we are
  • An institution that includes acute care and rehabilitation establishments, a private clinic, EMS, medical centers and permanences scattered throughout the La Côte region as well as the 1st clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care milieu in the Swiss Romand region.
  • A healthcare network that allows for numerous opportunities for internal mobility according to the aspirations and development projects of the collaborators.
  • Over 2,000 collaborators taking care of patients and residents in the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment that favors the realization of projects.
  • A training company committed to training the next generation, with around 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES interns and around 300 doctors in training.
