Medical/practical assistant/in 40%

Non-invasive cardiology

Place of work St. Gallen

Medical assistant or health professional

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Coordination of appointments in cooperation with different professional groups
  • Reception, care and advice of patients in close cooperation with doctors
  • Assistance of doctors in various cardiological examinations
  • Implementation of ECG, Holter plants and removals and other cardiological diagnostic measures
  • Performation of blood sampling

What you bring for this job

  • Training as medical/practice assistant
  • Medical expertise and interest as well as motivation to constantly develop
  • High quality awareness
  • Flexible and resilient personality
  • Very good PC user knowledge
  • Experience in cardiology is beneficial

Our offer to you

  • Various employee offers in the areas of health promotion, job and family, mobility and attractive benefits
  • Easy access to an extensive internal training and further education offer as well as subject-specific training after successful introduction to promote individual further development
  • Comprehensive implementation concept
