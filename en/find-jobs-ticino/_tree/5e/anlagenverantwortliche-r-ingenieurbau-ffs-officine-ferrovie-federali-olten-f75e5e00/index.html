Plant Manager: Civil Engineering 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Plant manager: Civil engineering Olten / Work Smart, November 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. A meaningful job awaits you in a versatile and varied field of work in the field of civil engineering. In this position you can apply and deepen your knowledge of civil engineering and develop and implement economical solutions for the maintenance and repair of bridges, culverts and supporting structures. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • also for the family
  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • In your role as plant manager, you monitor and assess the condition of the engineering structures in your area of ​​responsibility. You rely on a well-rehearsed inspection team and also carry out assessments yourself.
  • If there are deviations from the target condition, you plan, budget and commission deeper condition checks and suitable maintenance measures.
  • As the person responsible for the system, you carry out monitoring and maintenance measures on your own responsibility. You commission large repair measures and monitor compliance with costs, deadlines and quality.
  • You assess SBB's own construction projects and third-party railway-related projects with regard to compliance with standards and regulations as well as ensuring the safety and availability of the railway infrastructure.
  • You are open to innovations and provide impulses to ensure that the operation and maintenance of buildings can be carried out more safely, efficiently and economically. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are characterized by your independent and reliable way of working as well as your analytical, creative and forward-looking thinking.
  • You also have an awareness of costs and responsibility, assertiveness and willingness to make decisions. You communicate with a wide range of stakeholders in an appropriate and solution-oriented manner.
  • In order to carry out railway-related control activities and maintenance measures, we require that you complete internal training in the area of ​​workplace safety (self-protection, safety manager) and can therefore move around the track area independently.
  • You have completed a degree in civil engineering (ETH/Uni/FH) or comparable qualification. Experience in the area of ​​monitoring and maintaining engineering structures is an advantage.
  • You communicate fluently in German (min. C1). You also have a Category B driving license and have in-depth knowledge of the Microsoft Office range.
