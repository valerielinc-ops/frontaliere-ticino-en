Full description

Certificate physician vascular surgery 100%

Clinic for vascular surgery

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Self-responsible care of outpatient and inpatient patients with vascular surgical diseases as well as independent management of a specialized consultation at the main location and, if necessary, at external locations
  • Use in the operating room, in the emergency department and in endovascular procedures with independent surgical-operative and endovascular treatment of vascular-surgical clinical pictures
  • Close supervision of the assistant doctors and the Physician Associate in the department, in the operating room and during their consultation hours
  • Medical representation (inside and outside) of vascular surgery at various interdisciplinary colloquia (e.g. vascular, neurovascular, dialysis and diabetic foot) and on emergency
  • Deepening of independence in a defined vascular surgical spectrum

What you bring for this job

  • Completed medical studies recognised in Switzerland and specialist title for vascular surgery with proven experience over large parts of the vascular treatment spectrum
  • Ability to and enjoy patient-oriented, interprofessional and interdisciplinary cooperation as well as demanding open and endovascular surgery with appropriate manual skill and commitment
  • Good communication skills and a high level of social competence as well as resilience and flexibility
  • Enjoy working in a small and motivated team as well as enjoying the sharing of skills and knowledge
  • Interest in rapid personal and professional development and taking on responsibility

Our offer to you

  • Further and further training at one of the leading interdisciplinary vascular centers with a full range of treatments including specially maintained open vascular surgery
  • State-of-the-art infrastructure with hybrid hall and professionalized station support with Physician Associates
  • Used as part of the lecture activities at the Medical Master of the HSG/UZH School of Medicine with regular student support
  • Building a long-term work perspective with a specialized spectrum including special consultation hours
  • Possibilities for use at our external locations under independent responsibility
