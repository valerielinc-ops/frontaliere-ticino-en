Description of post Join and strengthen our TECHNICS & TECHNOLOGY team. We ensure the safety of road and rail operations through modern operating and safety equipment - from energy supply to intelligent traffic regulation.

Join and strengthen our team and assume responsibilities such as: PROJECT HEAD IN ELECTRICITY (INGENIEUR / TECHNICIAN) (M/F/D) 80-100% - NEUCHATEL

YOUR TACHES

  • Responsibility for power plant planning mandates (rail and motorway)
  • Independent execution of the technical tasks of the project entrusted, ensuring that quality, costs and deadlines are respected
  • Coordination and management with the client
  • Advise and support clients in all phases of the project, from strategic planning to commissioning and final optimization
  • Preparation and drafting of reports on selected technical concepts
  • Design and realization of detailed electrical diagrams

YOUR CHANCES

  • Development of the engineering team for French-speaking Switzerland with the possibility of becoming the future team leader.
  • Collaboration, knowledge sharing and mutual support are the pillars of how we work
  • Various and interdisciplinary projects, in collaboration with specialists from different fields
  • A diverse team, bringing together various professional backgrounds
  • Individual support, whether through targeted training or career development opportunities
  • Flexible work models, with the possibility of telework, as well as modern infrastructure, all in a central location

YOUR PROFILE

  • SUP academic training, SSS technician in electrotechnical or equivalent qualification
  • Minimum 3 year professional experience in managing railway or motorway projects is preferred
  • Knowledge of SIA – CPN standards in the field of electricity and public tenders (SIMAP)
  • Knowledge of software for the design of medium voltage electrical networks and CAD programs
  • Knowledge of building electrical installations is an advantage
  • Controlling MT/BT transformer dimensioning
  • Experience with BIM (Building Information Modeling) methodology is an asset
  • Control of low voltage distribution networks and selectivity management settings
  • Ability to organise work and manage projects independently
