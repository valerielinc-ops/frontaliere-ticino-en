Your tasks

  • Care and service of our guests in the restaurant, in the hall and on the terrace
  • Independent management of a service station including ordering, serving and cashing
  • Preparation and follow-up of the service and ensuring a smooth operation
  • Assistance with events, banquets and special occasions
