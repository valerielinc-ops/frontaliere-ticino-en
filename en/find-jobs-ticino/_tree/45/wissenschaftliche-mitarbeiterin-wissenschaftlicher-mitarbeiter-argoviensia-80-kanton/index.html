Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • You are the first contact person for specific inquiries about the Aargau Cultural Heritage (Argoviensia) and advise customers
  • They plan and design the programme for the education of the Aargau cultural heritage – including the selection of topics and speakers
  • They arrange Argoviensia independently in the context of events, guided tours, training courses and specialist articles
  • They maintain the Aargau bibliography and are responsible for the selection, acquisition and development of Argoviensia, including the evaluation and acceptance of donations and estates.
  • You work in customer service at the Cantonal Library and State Archives locations (regular Saturday and evening services)

What you bring

  • Graduate degree at master level (humanities, ideally history, German or cultural studies)
  • CAS in Information Science
  • Good knowledge of the history of the Canton of Aargau and its cultural scene
  • Proven experience in a similar environment, knowledge in dealing with the Alma and scopeArchiv software is advantageous
  • Knowledge of the Latin language and the German script is advantageous

Library and Archives Aargau is a central memory institution and coordination centre for the further development of the library and archive landscape of the canton. It combines the cantonal services of the Cantonal Library, the State Archives and library promotion. Library & Archive - Canton of Aargau (ag.ch) (https://www.ag.ch/en/verwaltung/bks/kultur/kulturpflege/bibliothek-archiv/kantonsbibliothek)
