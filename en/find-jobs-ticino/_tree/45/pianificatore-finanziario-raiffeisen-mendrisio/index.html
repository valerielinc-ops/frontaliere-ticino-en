Join our team!

For the Bank Raiffeisen Mendrisio and Valle di Muggio, a successful bank oriented to the local clientele, we look for a dynamic and motivated person, with solid knowledge of the Swiss banking system as a Financial Planner

What's up?

  • Analyze the financial, social, tax, financial and family situation of customers
  • Develop custom financial plans and identify solutions tailored to customer needs
  • Provide specialist counselling on pensions, taxation, succession, investment and financing of housing property
  • Develop pension simulations and pension assessments
  • Support for consultants in customer management and development
  • Maintain updated regulatory, tax and social security knowledge relevant to the business

What do you offer?

  • Solid financial, social and tax knowledge
  • Customized Solution Analysis and Processing Capacity
  • Empathy, listening skills and customer orientation
  • Excellent relational, communicative and trading capabilities
  • Organizational autonomy and strong sense of responsibility
  • Ability to work in teams and collaborate with different specialist figures
  • Banking or economic training (Bachelor, Master or equivalent title)
  • Professional experience in financial, banking, social security or insurance consultancy
  • They constitute preferential title certifications or specialized paths such as: Certified Financial Planner (CFP), MAS in Financial Planning, other equivalent qualifications in financial planning

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
