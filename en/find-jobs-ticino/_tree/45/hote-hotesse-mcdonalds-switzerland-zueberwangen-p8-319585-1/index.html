At McDonalds, every visit is a fun and friendly time. Our team loves welcoming customers and turning every moment into a memorable experience. If you like human contact and look for a living, dynamic and opportunityful job, you're in the right place!

Description of post

As a guest or hostess, you are the face of the restaurant. You welcome the guests with a smile, organize anniversaries and creative activities, and make everyone — the younger ones in particular — have a great time. You bring your energy, creativity and good mood to the team.

Key responsibilities

  • Welcome guests and create a relaxed and friendly atmosphere
  • Organize anniversaries and creative events for children
  • Cooking tours during guided tours
  • Support the team at the counter, at the McDrive or in the room during rush periods
  • Assist management with administrative tasks

Qualifications required

  • Open and positive personality
  • Love contact with people, especially children
  • Creative and full of ideas
  • Team spirit, reliable and motivated
  • Good communication skills (French required, other languages one plus)
