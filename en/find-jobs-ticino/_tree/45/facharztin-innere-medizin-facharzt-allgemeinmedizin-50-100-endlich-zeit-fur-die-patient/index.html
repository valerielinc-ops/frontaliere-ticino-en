Looking for a health system in which medical quality, enough time for your patients and medical responsibility are actually lived? Switzerland convinces you as an attractive place of work with clear structures, modern infrastructure and excellent working conditions? Or are you, as a specialist, looking for a practice in which you can contribute your own ideas, help shape innovative approaches and develop both professionally and personally? Then this owner-managed basic care practice opens up a convincing professional perspective. Here you benefit from a cleanly organized practical environment, digitalized processes and fair, transparent framework conditions – including high job satisfaction, predictable work-life balance and excellent development opportunities. You can look forward to an experienced and appreciative supervisor who involves his team in the decisions. It will not be decided over your head!
