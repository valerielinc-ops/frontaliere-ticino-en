Full description

For our oncological ambulatory we are looking for 01. January 2027 or by agreement

Medical Secretary or Med. Practical assistant/oncological ambulatorium

40-80%

They take over

  • Reception and care of our patients
  • Independent planning and coordination of the complex oncological consultation
  • Friendly and competent telephone contact with patients, family doctors as well as hospitals and insurance companies
  • Versatile administrative and organisational secretarial work
  • Power measurement
  • Writing and processing of medical and insurance reports

They bring with them

  • You have several years of professional experience as medical secretary or medical assistant
  • They convince with stylish German, good knowledge of medical terminology and a safe handling of EDV applications
  • They meet patients with warmth and empathy, think customer-oriented and like to contribute as a team-capable personality.
  • They work independently, responsibly and accurately, have a quick understanding and also keep an overview of challenges.
  • You are a motivated and committed personality who keeps calm even in hectic situations and acts in a solution-oriented manner.
  • You feel comfortable in a lively, high-frequency ambulatorium and appreciate a varied and dynamic working environment
  • They are flexible and show a high level of commitment, support us during school holidays and take over holiday representatives if necessary

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
