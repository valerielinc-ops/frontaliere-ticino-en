Full description

The Domus Foundation, a Valais institution for psychosocial rehabilitation, supports adults Facing significant mental health challenges. Nearly 150 collaborators are committed with conviction and professionalism. Our vision: self-determination for everyone, a solution-focused approach, and professional practices marked by kindness and responsibility.

We are looking for a

Social Worker for Home Socio-Educational Follow-ups (60 to 80%) Immediate start or to be agreed

We offer you to

  • secure client support at their home in daily tasks and activities according to the institutional rehabilitation concept, staff regulations, and safety standards;
  • development documented individual projects with your referees, ensuring the interdisciplinarity of the projects. Guarantee their follow-up;
  • manage the individualized follow-up file of your references;
  • collaborate with the network of your referees.
  • join a foundation guided by participatory management, a positive dynamic, and a strong spirit of openness;
  • integrated an institution certified by Health Promotion Switzerland, concerned with the well-being of everyone and the teams where listing, collaboration, mutual aid, and friends are cultivated;
  • make your place in a structure that regularly runs many projects to which everyone can contribute;
  • benefit from a pleasant working environment with an annually planned team schedule and good social and salt conditions;
  • receipt continuous training each year through our "Acting Together" training catalogue.

Your profile

  • you hold a diploma as a social worker HES / ES or an equivalent qualification. A CAS in psychiatric is an asset;
  • you have confirmed work experience in psychiatric or social work in a residential home and/or home follow-up;
  • with a very good sense of organization, you enjoy working independently and in a team and can take on responsibilities;
  • you are professional with common IT tools such as MS Office;
  • holder of a driving license, you have a private vehicle.

Interested Further information can be obtained at 027 205 75 00. Your complete application with CV, photo, cover letter, diplomas, and work certificates must be sent by 30 October 2026. Applications will be treated confidentially.
