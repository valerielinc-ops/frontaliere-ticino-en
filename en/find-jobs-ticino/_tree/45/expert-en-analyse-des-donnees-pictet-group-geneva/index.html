Full description

Your team

Corporate Functions encompasses the Group’s non core-banking activities, with over 900 professionals supporting all business units, including Communications, Compliance, Finance, Human Resources, Information Security, Internal Audit, Legal, Corporate Office & Digital, Risk, and Tax departments. You will be part of the Group Internal Audit Department in Geneva, within the Corporate Functions Division.

You will play a key part in the Data Analytics team for Internal Audit, contributing to the department’s mission by leveraging advanced data and AI solutions, and innovative analytics.

Your role

  • Working closely with lead auditors and stakeholders to transform strategic objectives and regulatory needs into effective analytics and data-driven solutions for audit and assurance activities.
  • Building and maintaining scalable data pipelines that bring together information from various sources, including legacy and cloud-based systems, to support audit processes and enable the delivery of actionable insights through analytics and data visualization reports tailored to audit needs.
  • Participating to the modernization of the department’s analytics infrastructure by streamlining data flows, supporting migration to cloud platforms, and introducing new technologies such as AI, automation, and intelligent agents to enhance audit analytics.
  • Developing business cases with the Audit team by identifying and processing relevant data.
  • Conducting and documenting procedures to assess and ensure data quality within audit assignments.
  • Building strong partnerships with data stakeholders throughout the Pictet Group.

Your profile

  • Engineer-related academic background with a master’s degree in data science or computer science.
  • A minimum of five years’ experience in a similar role.
  • Solid programming languages skills such as Python, and SQL.
  • Hands-on experience with cloud data platforms and contemporary analytics practices; experience with Snowflake is a must.
  • Practical experience applying AI/automation and intelligent agents to enhance analytics efficiency and capabilities.
  • Good knowledge of Business Intelligence tools such as Tableau, and experience with ETL tools such as Alteryx is a plus.
  • Strong initiative, adaptability, and a proactive approach to problem-solving.
  • Creative and solution-oriented, with strong communication skills and teamwork spirit.
  • Fluent in English and French.
  • Must be resident in Switzerland.
