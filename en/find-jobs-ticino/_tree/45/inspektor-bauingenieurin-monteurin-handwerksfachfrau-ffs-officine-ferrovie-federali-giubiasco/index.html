What we offer you.

SBB designs and realizes not only galleries and stations, but develops the Swiss infrastructure. For our team we look for a motivated person who guarantees the safety and availability of the works of the civil genius/factory. What you can move.

  • Ensure the safety and availability of the works of the civil genius/ artifacts through regular inspections and small maintenance operations performed as a rule of art.
  • Mainly perform inspections, controls and maintenance work on artifacts such as bridges, supporting walls, wall units, tunnel portals, tombs, noise walls etc.
  • Create reports on the status of works and lists of defects, sending them to the capoteam for subsequent processing.
  • Collaboration in the planning and execution of maintenance measures and construction operations.
  • Coordinate the various internal and external services involved in the interventions on the artifacts.
  • Support plant managers in infrastructure management and monitoring.

The one you bring with you.

  • Pluriennial experience in the construction and/or maintenance of artifacts or concrete and steel works.
  • Ability to follow continuous training courses (e.g. suspension work with strings, SBB safety courses) in parallel with professional activity.
  • Basic knowledge of MS Office applications and good attitude to the use of computer equipment and tools.
  • Good judgment and decision in critical situations of plants, good health, absence of dizziness and claustrophobia.
  • Excellent knowledge of the Italian language and good knowledge of the German language level B1.
  • Professional internship completed in the technical-artisanal branch (e.g. metallurgical, designer/trice, mason/masonist, mason/masonist, blacksmith/a or equivalent training) with practical experience.
