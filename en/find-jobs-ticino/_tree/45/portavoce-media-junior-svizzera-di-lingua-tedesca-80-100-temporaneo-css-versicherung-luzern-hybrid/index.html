Full description

We at CSS give every day gas for a strong, fair health care – and talk about it openly. In the Media Relations team, you will meet people who love media, find politics exciting and like to be in the middle of the action. You will delve deep into the world of health insurance, take on current topics and learn how professional media work works in one of the largest health insurance companies in Switzerland. This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: In direct exchange with journalists, you answer media inquiries, get input from specialist departments and ensure clear, understandable statements.
  • Together with the team, you write media releases, Q&A, arguments and texts for various channels and bring complex topics to the point.
  • In the newsroom you plan topics, develop content and help to build up exciting topic dossiers and keep them up-to-date.
  • With a close eye, you follow social, political and health policy developments and recognize where opportunities or risks arise for CSS.
  • Thanks to your flair for figures and details, you can create media analyses, reports and short management updates and thus support the management of media work.
  • In cross-departmental projects, you advise internal stakeholders on communication issues and contribute to concepts and measures in sensitive situations. You bring that with you
  • First things first: Safe, professional appearance and enjoyment of contact with media professionals distinguish you and you are noticeably interested in media work, politics and the healthcare sector.
  • You have completed a bachelor’s degree, ideally in media or communication studies, journalism, business administration or a similar field.
  • 3 years of professional experience in communication, media relations or in cooperation with journalists, for example in a media office, corporate communication or agency, you bring along.
  • Very good editorial skills in German, a stylish writing feeling and a structured, independent and team-oriented working style are among your strengths.
  • With common office tools you are confident, use monitoring or media monitoring tools and ideally have n language skills in French or Italian.
  • Dynamics, resilience, curiosity and openness to new developments in communication drive you. Even under time pressure, you keep a cool head and deliver reliably.
