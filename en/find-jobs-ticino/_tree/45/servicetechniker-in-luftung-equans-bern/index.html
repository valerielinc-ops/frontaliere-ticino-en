Full description

Are you looking for a varied job with a lot of personal responsibility and direct customer contact? Then you are exactly right with us in Bern. We are looking for you as a ventilation service technician.

You can move that with us.

  • Performing maintenance and maintenance work on ventilation and air conditioning systems
  • Resolving malfunctions and repairs of all kinds in the field of ventilation
  • Acquisition of repair and maintenance contracts
  • Small installations and additions to existing installations
  • Performing of Picett Service

That's what makes you.

  • Completed vocational training as a ventilation system manufacturer or similar (car mechanic, precision mechanic, etc.)
  • In-depth professional experience in this field as well as in the field of electrical and control systems
  • Self-employed and responsible working practices
  • Strong customer orientation and communication skills
  • Flexibility, entrepreneurial thinking and good organizational skills
  • Readiness for Pikett service
  • Category B driving licence
  • Good knowledge of German

That's what we offer you.

  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Experience our DU culture at eye level
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Above-average social benefits and attractive employment conditions
  • Company cars (private use possible)
  • The working atmosphere as in a family business, the latest work equipment and the safety of a large corporation.
