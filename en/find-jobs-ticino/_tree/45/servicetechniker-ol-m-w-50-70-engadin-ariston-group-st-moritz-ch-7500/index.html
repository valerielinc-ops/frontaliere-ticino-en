Full description

ELCO offers premium heating solutions with first-class service designed to meet the needs of our customers throughout the entire product and heating system lifecycle. The focus is on excellent consulting as well as convincing products and heating systems. ELCO has been a leader in burner, condensing and solar technology for decades and has 1.7 million installed heating systems throughout Europe. Since 1928, ELCO has been a leading European brand offering intelligent solutions and technologies tailored to the needs of private and commercial projects, ensuring maximum energy savings.

Together we shape the energy future! In the Engadin we look for you as

Service technician oil (m/f) 50-70 %

This is what awaits you with us

  • Commissioning of modern heating systems in the area of oil, later also heat pumps and solar systems
  • Independent performance of maintenance, repairs and troubleshooting
  • Responsibility for demanding heating systems
  • You will advise our customers about their heating system
  • Close cooperation with installers and the sales and service team

What you should bring

  • Experience as a service technician is a plus
  • Completed training EFZ, e.g. as an electrical, heating or sanitary installer, car industry or comparable technical apprenticeship in the HVAC or electrical engineering sector
  • Enjoyment of customer contact as well as a structured and independent working method
  • Readiness for Picetting
  • You are safe in German and Italian
  • Residential in Engadin

You can look forward to this

  • Modern service vehicle – also for private use
  • Innovative heating solutions and future-oriented products in the premium segment
  • Further education opportunities and targeted personal support
  • Attractive social benefits and fair employment conditions
