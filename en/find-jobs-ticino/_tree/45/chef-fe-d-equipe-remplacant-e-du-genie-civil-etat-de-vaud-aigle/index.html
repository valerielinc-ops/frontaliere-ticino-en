Full description

As a replacement maintenance team leader in civil engineering, you alone or as a team carry out civil engineering work related to the development and improvement of the road network and its annexes, supporting and replacing the team leader.

  • To carry out civil engineering work on its own or as a team in connection with improvements and improvements to the road network and its annexes, particularly in the masonry sector.
  • Second and replace the Civil Engineering Team Leader, by order or in case of absence.
  • Participate, in collaboration with the teams concerned, in road maintenance and maintenance. All the work - varied and interesting - is carried out with respect for the environment, safety rules and principles and procedures. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr. Sébastin Tacchini, deputy of the road haulage company, at 021 557 87 89.
  • SWC of mason, road builder or other technical or craft profession useful to the function.
  • Driving experience and desired team management.
  • Driver's licence for light vehicles and trailers (BE) required
  • Personal vehicle essential.
  • A machinist's license and/or heavyweight license, a real asset.
  • Good health and fitness for outdoor work.
