Full description

Medacta International is looking for an SQL & BI Developer /Analyst that will be inserted into the IT team with the goal of developing and maintaining business intelligence and reporting solutions.

The resource will take the following activities

  • data modeling and data processing;
  • development dashboard and analytics (Qlik Sense);
  • advanced SQL query design and optimization;
  • realization of structured reports (SSRS);
  • data infrastructure management and optimization on Microsoft SQL Server;
  • will play a key role in ensuring the consistency, reliability and performance of data used globally, intervening both in application and infrastructure.

Hard Skills

  • Degree in Computer Science;
  • excellent knowledge of the English language;
  • at least 3 years of experience in a similar function in enterprise environments with high data volumes;
  • concrete experience with:
  • Advanced T-SQL
  • stored complex procedures
  • query tuning and performance optimization
  • experience with SQL Server Reporting Services (SSRS);
  • Qlik Sense experience (data load, scripting, dashboard);
  • good data modeling ability (star schema, normalization);
  • knowledge of:
  • execution plan and performance analysts
  • management indexes and statistics
  • backup/restore and recovery strategies
  • SQL Server monitoring (DMV, native tools)
  • experience in managing databases on large data volumes.

Soft Skills

  • practical and solution-oriented approach;
  • ability to work on complex systems and critical data;
  • aptitude for problem solving and autonomous management of activities;
  • strong attention to the quality and reliability of the data.

What we offer

  • contract indefinitely;
  • flexible working hours;
  • carpooling organized by Medacta;
  • incentives for mobility with public transport;
  • advantageous business conventions;
  • growth opportunities, working in an environment in continuous improvement and expansion;
  • Annual review of the salary related to performance and professional development;
  • continuing training.
