Full description

What awaits you in our rehabilitation clinic: An everyday work day that fits your life perfectly. As a rule, no overtime, no unnecessary pressure – real time for our patients: inside. We plan ahead and flexibly so that your free time does not fall short. What distinguishes us? A strong team that supports together and a working environment in which medical technology and relationship care are in balance. Have you become curious? We look forward to your application.

Your tasks

  • You contribute to ensuring quality care
  • You work in a microteam with a qualified nurse and a health care professional (FaGe) and actively support the care process
  • You organize the housekeeping tasks and the cleaning and disinfection measures (acceptance of day-to-day care assistance)
  • Assistance in the training and accompaniment of apprentices, trainees: inside and new employees

Your profile

  • You have completed training or SRK recognition
  • You are an established, winning personality who likes to work in a team
  • You like to pass on your knowledge and think solution-oriented

Our offer

  • You expect a balanced relationship between medical technology and relationship care as well as an interdisciplinary cooperation
  • You get the space to make decisions independently and actively contribute your ideas
  • A forward-looking and flexible service planning that supports your personal lifestyle – with annual working time models and planning six weeks in advance
  • Plannable work and leisure – overtime usually does not apply to us
  • After a break back to work? With our individual re-entry program we accompany you back into everyday work
  • Do you want to get ahead? We offer you targeted training and further education opportunities
  • Relaxing rest (fitness classes, free wellness offers in our partner companies and discounts on fitness subscriptions)
  • Contemporary employment conditions with progressive non-wage benefits

Interested? Then we look forward to your online application. Klinik Schönberg AG, Estelle Fuchs, Human Resources, Schönbergstrasse 40, 3654 Gunten
