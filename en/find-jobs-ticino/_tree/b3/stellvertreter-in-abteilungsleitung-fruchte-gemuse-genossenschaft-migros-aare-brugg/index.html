Full description

Are you our new sales talent? Fresh fruits and crisp vegetables are the basis for a healthy and balanced diet – and the Migros offers a colourful variety. Help our customers expand their culinary possibilities and become part of our team! 🥦🍒🥑

Mansioni

  • You advise and serve our customers with joy and expertise
  • An attractive product presentation, efficient goods management and availability are your focus
  • You keep the defined processes in the department and consider a smooth process
  • You're safe to hire the department manager
  • You support active and engaged in other departments

Requisiti

  • Ideally several years of professional experience: in the area of fruit & vegetables
  • Completed basic or advanced training: in retail
  • As a customer-oriented personality with pleasure in selling, you make shopping a positive experience
  • PC skills and a high digital fitness
  • You have leadership experience
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks paternity leave
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus points: multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Migros Club School: Generous financial allocation to self-selected courses
  • Sports and fitness offers: cost participations in Abos of over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, water parks
  • Childcare: Generous financial contribution for external childcare services
  • Mobility: Participation of an annual subscription to public transport, discount on fuel (Migrol), reduced annual contribution to mobility carsharing and entitlement to fleet rebate
  • Mobile phone subscription: various discounts on mobile subscriptions
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest on capital or a discount on interest rates of newly completed fixed mortgages
  • Leisure and cultural offers: various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, Interhome and events
