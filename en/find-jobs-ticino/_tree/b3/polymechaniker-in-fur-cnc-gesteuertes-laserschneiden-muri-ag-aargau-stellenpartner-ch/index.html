Full description

The task includes the independent programming and operation of modern laser cutting machines. The company offers an innovative environment with development opportunities. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Self-responsible activity on the Bystronic laser, folding robot and Trumpf punching laser (i.e. programming, setting up and operating)
  • Production of high-quality individual and series parts according to workshop drawing and instructions
  • Self-testers for the workpieces produced (i.e. measuring)
  • Provision of materials and post-processing and transfer of products
  • Optimization of existing program
  • Responsibility for the maintenance and maintenance of machinery
  • Documentation of initial orders / prototypes
  • Participation in the continuous improvement process (CIP)

Requirements

  • Advanced training in CNC programming and experience in the application of TruTopsBoost and the Bysoft7 program
  • Experience in handling CNC controlled laser cutting and combi punching
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of mechanical engineering (sheet processing)
  • Very good technical understanding
  • Responsible and quality-conscious working methods
  • Structured and efficient work organization and team-oriented thinking
  • Good knowledge in reading plans / sketches

Offer

  • After a thorough induction, you take on a versatile and responsible area of responsibility
  • Positive working atmosphere in a motivated and competent team
  • Personal training and development opportunities
