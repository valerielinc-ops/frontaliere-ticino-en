Description of the Office

The expropriation commission decides on all valuation questions which are not transferred by the law on expropriation to another authority, as well as on compensation requests for material expropriation. It also exercises the powers which other provisions of cantonal law expressly or mutatis mutandis assign to the expropriation judge – for example the request for compensation of an owner vis-à-vis his neighbour, in application of the spatial planning and building law.

Desired profile

  • The Judiciary Council is seeking a Vice-President or a vice-president with attorney’s patent, license or master’s degree in rights or experience in jurisdiction with knowledge of Freiburg administrative law, in particular in the field of construction and spatial planning (planning). Some availability for sessions and text writing is required. It is a part-time job (about 10%), which is carried out at irregular intervals.
  • Voting rights in cantonal matters. Foreign nationals must hold a residence permit and have been resident in the canton for at least five years. There must be no loss certificates against the person. You must not have been convicted for acts incompatible with the judicial office
  • Mastering the German language with very good knowledge of the French language
