Product Manager: in overhead lines 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Product Manager: in overhead lines Bern / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

We keep Switzerland moving - without electricity, no train runs. Run the Swiss railway network with us and ensure that we can distribute the electricity to the overhead lines of the railway infrastructure operators. Further information about our projects and activities can be found here: Energy | SBB Our benefits such as child allowances, free GA and discounted travel abroad – including for your family – can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As an expert for high voltage lines and cables, you are the central contact person within SBB.
  • Together with the users, you develop and control the product strategy for high-voltage lines and cables over the entire life cycle.
  • You define the internal technical specifications for the planning, construction, operation and maintenance of high-voltage systems and ensure their sustainable further development.
  • For the energy supply of SBB, you are responsible for the technical approval of high-voltage lines and cable systems.
  • You represent SBB Energie in national commissions and working groups, actively contribute the interests of SBB to the further development of standards and industry standards and maintain a strong network with users, manufacturers, authorities and other partners.

You bring that with you.

  • You are a convincing and integrating personality with high communication skills, implementation orientation and structured approach.
  • Several years of experience as a product developer: in, -designer: in and / or in-depth user knowledge of high-voltage equipment are required.
  • A higher education (FH / ETH / technical university) in electrical engineering enables you to successfully perform this challenging task.
  • You are familiar with the relevant norms and laws in energy technology as well as the applicable domestic and cross-border safety standards and regulations.
  • You have very good knowledge of German (min. C1) and good knowledge of French (min. B1).

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This job is in requirement level K, regional allowance level 1.
