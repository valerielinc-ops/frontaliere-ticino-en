Company Description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals of Geneva are a benchmark establishment at the national and international level. To find out more about our institution, take a few minutes to discover the highlights of the year 2025 by clicking here. The Department of Women, Children and Adolescents' mission is to provide quality care adapted to the needs of women at each stage of their lives and to the health problems faced by newborns, children and adolescents from birth to the age of 18. For many years, the Department of Women, Children and Adolescents has continued to develop cutting-edge services and centers of expertise in several medical specialties. The General Pediatrics Department provides outpatient and hospital care for the health problems of newborns, children and adolescents until the end of their growth and contributes to maintaining health. The General Pediatrics Department is an establishment recognized by the FMH for obtaining the title of pediatric specialist.

Job Description

Your commitment is part of a comprehensive five-year pediatric training course, including one or two years in one of our partner hospitals (Tour Hospital, Friborg Hospital, Sion Hospital, Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Neuchâtel Hospital, Nyon Hospital). It is also possible for you to commit only for three years, in order to obtain validation of the years in an A center. You progress through a training course according to a predetermined plan in the sectors below: Inpatient units

Pediatric emergencies

Pediatric intensive care

Neonatology

Maternity and developmental consultation

Pediatric specialties and certain partner entities (adolescent medicine, allergology, cardiology, endocrinology, gastroenterology & hepatology, infectious diseases, nephrology, neurology, onco-hematology, ENT, orthopedics, pulmonology, rheumatology, Médecins sans frontières) Under the supervision of the medical hierarchy, you carry out the practical and therapeutic activities entrusted to you and you engage in post-graduate training in pediatrics according to the ISFM program. You must work in a multidisciplinary team and contribute to teaching and supervision linked to a university structure. At the end of your training, you are ready to take the FMH pediatrics exam and have completed all FMH requirements for the pediatrics specialty title. For more information on our training program, do not hesitate to visit our website here.

Qualifications

You hold a federal medical diploma or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Federal Commission for Medical Professions (MEBEKO). For non-French-speaking candidates, certified level C1 in the spoken and written test is required.
