Full description

Attached or attached to the Digital Transformation and Enhanced Intelligence Directorate (DTN-IA), you take responsibility for the User Relations Service and actively contribute to the quality, performance and evolution of the institution's digital services. A preferred interlocutor for trades, the User Relations Service provides the interface between users and DTN-IA teams. It ensures that business needs are taken into account in all management activities while supporting the development of digital usages and continuous improvement of services. The Service is structured around three complementary areas: Service Management, Project Management and Digital Management. In this function, you ensure the strategic, operational and financial management of the service. You set directions, oversee the benefit portfolio, implement performance indicators, and ensure that quality of service objectives are met in a continuous way. You develop trusting partnerships with business managers, departments and representatives to collect, analyze and prioritize their needs. You support the teams in identifying the opportunities offered by digital technology and contribute to the development of roadmaps to support institutional transformation. At the head of multidisciplinary teams, you ensure the supervision and development of employees of the service. You promote a user-centred culture, innovation, collaboration and quality of services. As a member of the Management Committee of TND-IA, you are involved in defining and implementing the management strategy. You are helping to drive performance, prioritize investments and identify value creation opportunities for HUGs.
