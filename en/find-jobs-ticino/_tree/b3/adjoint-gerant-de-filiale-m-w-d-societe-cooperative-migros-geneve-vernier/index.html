Full description

Do you want to give your career at Migros a new boost? As an assistant manager, you will play a key role in operational management and performance development. This position will allow you to take on concrete challenges: leading teams, optimizing results, and ensuring an impeccable customer experience. If you want to progress and have a real impact, this role is for you.

Duties

  • Ensure store management in the absence of the manager. You take the reins to ensure optimal functioning and continuity in operations.
  • Manage assigned sectors and achieve profitability goals. You analyze performance and implement concrete actions to maximize results.
  • Supervise your team by fostering autonomy and initiative. You create a positive work climate and engage each employee to give their best.
  • Analyze performance and implement corrective actions. You identify gaps, adjust strategies, and ensure rigorous monitoring of key indicators.
  • Ensure the skill development of employees and apprentices. You support their progress, set clear goals, and value their efforts.
  • Ensure the application of quality, safety, and HACCP standards. You ensure that every procedure is respected to guarantee a safe and compliant environment.

Requirements

  • 3 years of minimum experience: in the cooperative with team management.
  • Leadership: You inspire your team, give constructive feedback, and encourage collaboration. You know how to mobilize energy to achieve ambitious goals.
  • Analysis and management: You interpret figures with precision and make strategic decisions based on data. You know how to anticipate and adapt to unforeseen events.
  • Customer orientation: You listen, advise, and offer impeccable service. You make every customer visit a positive and memorable experience.
  • Completed studies (Uni/VET, UAS, PET)
  • French (Good command)

What we offer

  • Occupational pension: Contribution primacy, contribution from age 20 doubled by Migros, and possibility of early retirement from age 58.
  • Cumulus points: 2% and 5% discount depending on the assortment.
  • Catering: Meal discount of Fr. 4 (on purchases from 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Migros Bank: Preferential interest rate and no fees for a savings account.
