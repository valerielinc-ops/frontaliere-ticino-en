Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 17,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a Technical Lead Embedded C++ SW developer/architect from the industrial sector to complement our team and use their experience to shape our software landscape in a sustainable way and enrich it both technically and humanly.

What is at stake here?

Are you someone who likes to take bold technological steps? Do you enjoy shaping clear software concepts for tomorrow from ideas? And do you enjoy accompanying others on their way and setting standards together? If you find yourself in it, you’ll find yourself in a role where you shape our future generations of devices and have a real impact on the technologies that people around the world encounter every day.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • assume functional and technical software leadership in the context of our current and future software platforms.
  • In the team, we design new software concepts, system architectures and processes in the software environment and thus lay the foundations for future device generations.
  • shape the strategic development within the software, establish processes and new methods and continuously develop them further.
  • Professionally develop employees through active coaching and mentoring.
  • play a central role in establishing and maintaining networks within and outside the company and represent our interests in standardization organizations.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have a university degree (Master, Doctor of Technical Sciences ETH or equivalent) in the field of Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering or Automation Engineering.
  • have at least 15 years of experience in the development, architecture and real-time design of embedded systems.
  • have excellent knowledge and experience in the use of embedded C/C++, in security and robustness methods, in software quality metrics and in test-driven development.
  • bring in-depth knowledge from an industrial environment and are familiar with requirements in regulated application areas such as functional safety or metrology.
  • have experience in group or committee work as well as very good English and at least good German skills.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through diverse training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We invest in your health – with comprehensive health management.
  • We support you with a company pension and offers for the compatibility of family and work.
  • You benefit from a working environment in which technological curiosity, appreciation and creative freedom come together.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.

Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding.
