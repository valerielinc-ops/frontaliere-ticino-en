Full description

Are you technically talented, skilled with your hands, and motivated by varied tasks? Then an apprenticeship in automation is exactly right for you! During the demanding four-year basic vocational training, you will acquire the skills needed for a successful start in the world of automation.

Your training

  • During your training, you will learn a great deal about complex systems, electronics and automation.
  • You are the specialist responsible for the maintenance and functionality of the sorting and conveyor systems and help ensure trouble-free operation.
  • Project-oriented, productive and service-related tasks as well as problem analyses and their practical resolution shape your area of responsibility.
  • In the two-year basic training, you will learn the fundamentals of creating, commissioning and maintaining automated systems and discover other exciting topics at the partner company login Berufsbildung AG in Olten.
  • During the last two years of your apprenticeship, you will apply your knowledge in a sorting centre and consolidate and deepen the knowledge you acquired in the basic training.

What you bring

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, passion and motivation who want to make a difference. You should meet the following requirements:

  • You have very good mathematics and physics skills, can think logically and are good at solving problems and tricky tasks
  • You have completed compulsory schooling.
  • You have technical understanding, are interested in digital technologies and have a high level of perseverance and concentration.
  • In addition, you enjoy working in a team and are reliable and responsible.
