La RSI, Swiss Radiotelevisione di lingua italiana, is a public service broadcaster whose mandate is enshrined in the Federal Constitution, the Radiotelevision Act (LRTV) and the Concession. Independent, it is a pillar of the Swiss media landscape, as it plays a key role in promoting open dialogue and disseminating accurate information. Thanks to a wide range of radio, television and digital programs, the CSR offers a universal service in the media field, reaching and informing the population in a capillary way. This function is essential both for democracy, and to ensure that the Swiss public is constantly updated and informed, with coverage ranging from politics to culture, from economy to entertainment, from sport to education. The ISR provides an accurate picture of Swiss reality on a national, linguistic and regional scale, facilitating dialogue and exchange between the language regions of Switzerland.
