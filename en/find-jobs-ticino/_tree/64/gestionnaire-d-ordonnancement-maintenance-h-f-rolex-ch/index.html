Full description

Introduction

As part of the development of our activities in the canton of Fribourg, we are a pool of talents to accompany the deployment of our new industrial site in Bulle. To this end, we will soon be looking for (M/F) Maintenance Scheduling Manager profiles to strengthen our teams.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and communicate the short-term maintenance plan taking into account priorities, resource availability and scheduling strategy
  • Analyze the workload and capacity of the sector, propose suitable smoothing solutions and collaborate with medium-term planning teams
  • Ensure the procurement of work orders interventions and components, in coordination with suppliers and procurement teams
  • Follow the order confirmations, deliveries and merchandise entries, and perform the necessary restarts
  • Monitor daily the implementation of the maintenance plan, carry out the necessary reordering and communicate any delays to stakeholders
  • Ensure consistency of management data related to work orders and follow up on sector outstandings
  • Develop and update activity indicators, report to management and contribute to maintenance scheduling projects

Profile

  • Technical, logistical or administrative training, complemented by experience in scheduling activities
  • Experience in an industrial environment, ideally in the areas of maintenance and supply
  • Mastery of the principles of planning, monitoring of load and capacity, and prioritization of interventions
  • Data analysis capability and ease with reporting tools
  • Mastery of an integrated management system, common office tools and planning tools
  • Good command of English and/or German, an asset
  • Organizational sense, rigour, autonomy and ability to work in a demanding environment
  • Communication, teamwork, sense of service and ability to offer solutions
  • Availability for flexible schedules and intersite travel as required

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
