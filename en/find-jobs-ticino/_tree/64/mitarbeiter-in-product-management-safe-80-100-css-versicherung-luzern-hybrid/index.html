Full description

At CSS, we are committed to ensuring that people can stay healthy and become healthy. Do you also want to make a difference in health care with your work? As an employee of Product Management SAFe, you support our Product Managers in further developing products and processes. You bring structure to complex topics, provide the basis for decision-making and coordinate cooperation with different areas. In this way, you help to advance joint projects and focus on the essentials. Sounds like a task for you? Then we look forward to meeting you.

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • Together with the product managers, you will continue to develop the ART vision, product strategy and roadmap. For this, you prepare information and decision bases understandably.
  • In the ART backlog, you create order and prepare features for prioritization.
  • For risks, dependencies and open points, you coordinate the necessary clarifications and ensure that everyone keeps track.
  • For PI Plannings and PI System Demos, you prepare progress, results and decisions. Communication and tracking the next steps are also part of this.
  • With product and ART metrics you make developments visible. You create regular reports and support product managers in continuously improving products and processes.

You bring that with you

  • A completed education or study in business administration, business informatics or a comparable field as well as initial experience in project management, business analysis or a similar function belong to your profile.
  • Ideally, you already work in an agile environment and know SAFe or a comparable framework from practice.
  • You structure complex information in an understandable way and convey it appropriately for different target groups.
  • Independently, carefully and structured you also work on several topics at the same time. You appreciate working with different roles and areas.
  • You enjoy product development, processes and continuous improvement as well as a good sense of connections and priorities.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
