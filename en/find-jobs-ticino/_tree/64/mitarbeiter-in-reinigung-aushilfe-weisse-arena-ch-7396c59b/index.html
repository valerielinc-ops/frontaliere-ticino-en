The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. In the signinahotel, the rocksresort and the Riders Hotel, alpine lifestyle meets modern hotel experience and individual hospitality. From the stylish hotel to high-quality serviced apartments to the urban meeting point – each house has its own character and demands. As a housekeeping team, we make sure this character is felt in every room. With a trained eye, high quality standards and a lot of heart, we create spaces that are clean, well-kept and inviting – and thus the basis for an all-round successful stay. | Imagine you are strengthening our team as an employee: in cleaning and supporting us on the weekends in the rocksresort.
