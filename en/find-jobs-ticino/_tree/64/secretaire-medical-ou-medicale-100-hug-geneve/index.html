Full description

Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is attached to the Diagnostic Department. This department, consisting of nine medical services, is responsible for providing diagnostics, therapeutic products, therapeutic procedures, and specialized, high-quality medical clinical consultations, as soon as possible and at an acceptable cost, to guide the management of patients and contribute to therapeutic decisions and actions. The Clinical Pathology Service provides clinical diagnostic activity, mainly on tissues from biopsies or operating parts. This activity helps to guide the management of patients and contributes significantly to therapeutic decisions. By carrying out medical autopsyes and by its research and development activities, the department participates in understanding the physiopathological mechanisms involved in injury processes. The service also has research and teaching missions.

Description of post

As part of your mission, you will ensure the administrative handling of requests for pathology examinations addressed to the department, from receipt and registration to transmission of results and follow-up of supplementary applications, in collaboration with medical and medico-technical teams. You shall ensure the completeness and consistency of the information, the reliability of the identification of patients and patients and the traceability of applications, in accordance with the procedures and rules of confidentiality. You support telephone calls, emails and mail addressed to the service, participate in the formatting of medical reports and ensure their safe transmission to the recipients concerned. You also contribute to the preparation of files for multidisciplinary symposia, the management of second notice requests and the traceability of diagnostic equipment. You work in a team of 9 collaborators, under the supervision of the medical-administrative manager, to ensure the continuity of all medical-administrative flows, the quality and standardization of practices, the respect of deadlines and the proper management of priorities.

Qualifications

You have a Federal Certificate of Capacity (FCC) and/or, ideally, a medical secretary's degree or equivalent administrative training. You have at least two years of experience in a secretariat, ideally in a medical, hospital or laboratory environment. A good mastery of medical terminology and a certain interest in the diagnostic field are expected. You have a very good command of the French language, both oral and written. Basic knowledge in English and German is an asset. You master the usual computer tools (Microsoft 365) with a standard ECDL level for Word, Excel and PowerPoint, as well as an advanced level for MS Outlook. You are also comfortable using several information systems. Knowledge of the institutional tools of HUGs, including DPA and IPR, as well as laboratory management systems, is an asset. You are rigorous, precise, autonomous and have an excellent sense of organization. You can manage several activities simultaneously, identify priorities and adapt to the demands of a demanding hospital environment. Your team spirit, relationship qualities, sense of responsibility and vigilance with regard to confidentiality, identity and data protection are essential to the performance of this function. You are motivated or motivated to contribute to the efficiency, quality and excellence of the missions of the Clinical Pathology Service, for the benefit of patients and all employees, in a key position within an interprofessional team composed of administrative collaborators, technicians and laboratory technicians, doctors, researchers, etc. We offer you a stimulating working environment in a dynamic team and in close collaboration with medical-technical professionals and professionals, where you will have the opportunity to develop your skills and contribute to the excellence of the services and organization of the service. We are committed to support your professional development and value your contribution.
