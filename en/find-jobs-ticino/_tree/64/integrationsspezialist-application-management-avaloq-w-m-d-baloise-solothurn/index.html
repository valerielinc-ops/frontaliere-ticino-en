Full description

We are the bank of Helvetia – an innovative financial services provider that intelligently combines banking and insurance. Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development

Tasks

Join our application management team at Baloise Bank and help us develop and maintain innovative integration solutions. Share your ideas, be curious and implement IT projects in close cooperation with IT specialists and the business.

With a focus on Avaloq, Baloise Bank’s application management team is responsible for the smooth operation of all IT applications. We are looking for you to strengthen our team in the application operation of our core banking system (Avaloq), the surrounding systems and integration projects.

  • Analysis, design and implementation of integration projects between Avaloq and various environments
  • Close cooperation with departments, IT teams and external partners
  • Parameterization, configuration and continuous development of the Avaloq Banking System
  • Operation, maintenance and optimization of the integration platform and interface solutions
  • Ensuring stable application operations including monitoring, incident and problem management
  • Collaboration in 2nd-level support and implementation of housekeeping and lifecycle management tasks
  • Support for security reviews, authorization concepts, vulnerability fixes and the implementation of security-by-design principles
  • Active participation in the technical and organizational development of our application landscape

Requirements

  • Higher degree in computer science, business informatics or comparable training
  • Several years of professional experience in the banking environment
  • In-depth knowledge of the Avaloq Banking System
  • Good knowledge of Windows, Linux and SQL database environments, experience with container technologies such as Docker or OpenShift
  • Analytical, structured and independent working method
  • Enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation and exchange with different stakeholders
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English

We believe in cooperation on equal terms, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
