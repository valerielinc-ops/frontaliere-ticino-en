The Opportunity This role sits within the Materials, Manufacturing & CAPEX Procurement Delivery group, which covers the core procurement activities from category management, strategic and operational sourcing, supplier management, contracting, content strategy definition, and execution. The Materials, Manufacturing & CAPEX Procurement Delivery group comprises many communities grouping categories of the same value chain or having commonality, including Raw Materials, External Manufacturing Services, Logistics, Research & Laboratory Materials, and Engineering (CAPEX). Within Research & Laboratory Materials, we cover two categories:

  • Lab Chemicals & Consumables (LCC)
  • Lab Equipment & Maintenance (LEM) This structure enables strong category expertise while allowing flexibility in the procurement delivery response to the evolving needs of our customers.
