Full description

In order to strengthen our team at the Brig site, we are looking for a service technician immediately or by agreement.

You can move that with us.

  • Responsible for installation and maintenance of small conversions and repairs at the end customer
  • Care of our customers and development of further customer relationships
  • Organising and managing the material independently for their own orders
  • Independent planning and execution of assigned contracts
  • Responsible for work preparation (AVOR), creating additional tasks such as cost estimates and further tasks according to the instructions of the superior

That's what makes you.

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship with federal certificate of ability as electrical installer/EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience in Switzerland as a service technician
  • You feel comfortable working independently, handling the orders conscientiously and correctly and also supporting customers with advice
  • Personal initiative, personal commitment and accuracy distinguish you
  • You are service-oriented, reliable and responsible
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Category B driving licence

That's what we offer you.

  • Varied activities
  • Set up young team
  • Lively and challenging environment
  • Attractive social benefits and conditions of employment
  • Awarded the Friendly Work Space Label
  • Internal and external training opportunities
