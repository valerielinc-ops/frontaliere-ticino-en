We are looking for a

Apprenti Production Clock - CFC (M/F/X)

Position based in La Côte-aux-Fées

HOW DO YOU HAVE AN IMPACT?

Production Clock assembles the different components of watches of all kinds (electronics, manual or automatic mechanics), controls quality at different stages of production and corrects any defects. He is a versatile professional and performs all production operations (assembly, finishing, adjustment, dial pose, switch, interlocking, swivelling).

In the specific field of watchmaking, watchmakers mainly carry out the following activities:

  • Micromechanical
  • Assembly
  • Completion, start-up, adjustment, swivelling
  • Coupling and dressing
  • Alignment of movement and dressing
  • Final checks

HOW ARE YOU GONNA USE US?

  • In the 11th year of high school, you are passionate about the world of watchmaking
  • Motivated and thorough person with great concentration capacity and excellent dexterity,
  • Advanced general education

The CFC is a Federal Certificate of Capacity. The training takes place according to the dual system (school – company) and is strongly oriented towards the practical dimension of the profession.

The Production Clock is a three-year course alternating technical courses within the ValFleurier Manufacture (3 days a week) and theoretical courses at the Technical School (CPNE – 2 days a week).

YOUR TRAVEL WITH US

We propose to evolve in a stimulating working environment, you will be integrated into our internal training center located at the Côte-aux-Fées. Your apprenticeship course will be delivered by two accredited trainers, in a team spirit and benevolence.

We look forward to receiving your complete application on our career site (cv, cover letter and last 3 school bulletins). If your application meets the entry criteria for this training, you will find below the main steps in the recruitment process:

  • first phone contact with our HR team,
  • theoretical assessment,
  • practical evaluation,
  • interview with our HR Business Partner and the training manager will complete the recruitment process.
