As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital Zurich, we are committed to showing commitment with our professional diversity in the top medical environment and always keeping up with the times. Want to be part of our team? With us you can make a difference!

  • You actively shape evidence-based nursing practice: You identify, evaluate and implement current scientific findings and empower nursing teams to consistently align their actions with evidence
  • You empower employees in their professional development: Through coaching, consulting and knowledge transfer, you strengthen the skills of care professionals and promote a culture of continuous learning
  • You think visionarily and develop the care of tomorrow: You recognize future challenges early on and initiate innovative concepts that combine nursing practice, research and teaching
  • You promote interprofessional cooperation on equal terms: Together with clinical, scientific and academic partners, you develop sustainable solutions for high-quality patient care
  • You act flexibly in a dynamic university environment: you accompany change processes in an agile and solution-oriented manner and ensure that new requirements and insights are effectively integrated into practice.
  • You have completed a postgraduate course in supervision care. You have also completed a master’s degree in nursing science or are ready to complete one
