Full description

For our customers in the region we are looking for immediately or by agreement motivated and reliable carpenters (m/f/d) for varied applications in the field of workshop and assembly.

Your tasks

  • General carpentry work in workshops and on assembly
  • Manufacture and assembly of furniture, doors, windows and interior fittings
  • Processing of various wood-based materials
  • Work according to plans and technical drawings
  • Operation of machines and tools
  • Installation of kitchens, cabinets and other interior equipment
  • Repair, service and adaptation work
  • Self-employment of assigned work
  • Compliance with quality and safety rules

Your profile

  • Completed training as Schreiner EFZ/EBA or comparable training beneficial
  • Alternatively several years of professional experience as a carpenter
  • Experience in workshop and/or on assembly
  • Independent, exact and reliable working method
  • Craftsmanship and technical understanding
  • Good physical condition
  • Good knowledge of German is advantageous
  • Advantageous driving licence Cat B
  • Motivated and team-capable personality

We offer

  • Personal support by our recruitment team

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed.
