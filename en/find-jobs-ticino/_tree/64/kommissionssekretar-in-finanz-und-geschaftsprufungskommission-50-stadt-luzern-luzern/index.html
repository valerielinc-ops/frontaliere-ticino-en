Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Are you interested in political processes and would like to actively shape the work of the Grand City Council? In this versatile function, you support and advise the financial and business audit committee and, with your organizational talent and careful working methods, ensure a smooth commission operation.

Your tasks

  • You support the Finance and Business Audit Committee, its members and the Grand City Council in administrative and professional matters.
  • You plan and organize the meetings of the financial and business audit committee as well as any sub- and special commissions.
  • You will keep the minutes of the meetings of the commissions, subcommissions and special commissions of the Grand City Council.
  • You draft the commission correspondence and ensure addressee-friendly communication.
  • You advise and accompany the Commission Bureau before, during and after the meetings on Commission and procedural issues.
  • You draft media releases and prepare the Commission’s communication to the outside world.
  • You will assist and advise the Commission in particular in the exercise of its parliamentary oversight.

Your profile

  • You have professional experience in public administration, ideally in the Commission or Parliament service.
  • Completed political science, law or comparable studies are beneficial.
  • You enjoy working in a political environment and are interested in the work of Parliament.
  • You work precisely and discreetly and have excellent knowledge of German.
  • You are flexible in terms of time and can adapt to changing requirements.
  • Ideally, you live in the Lucerne area.
