Full description

At the SIX staff restaurant, fresh and seasonal dishes are offered daily. Our guests can choose from a wide range of options throughout the day. To complement our team, we are looking for a service- and front-oriented personality who brings enthusiasm for the profession.

Responsibilities

  • Preparation and production of dishes for banquets and events in high quality and large quantities
  • Organization of mise en place and planning of workflows within the team depending on the size of the event
  • Close coordination with kitchen management and collaboration with service and event management
  • Plating and serving dishes according to specifications
  • Contribution to menu creation and consideration of special requests and dietary requirements
  • Compliance with GMZ quality standards and HACCP hygiene guidelines, as well as goods control and inventory management

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience in the required field
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ) as a chef
  • Motivated, organized, and humorous team player personality
  • Efficient, forward-thinking, and orderly working style
  • Enjoy working with colleagues from diverse backgrounds and cultures, as well as interacting with guests
  • Flexible availability for daytime shifts from Monday to Friday (no split shifts)
  • German (very good proficiency)

What we offer

  • Part of the Migros Group
  • Maternity/paternity leave
  • Attractive working hours from Monday to Friday without split shifts
  • Fair wages
  • Occupational pension: excellent pension benefits with high employer contributions
  • Social benefits: fair social benefits
  • Cumulus points: earn additional Cumulus points while shopping
  • Employee offers
  • Mobile subscription: special conditions for mobile subscriptions
  • Migros Bank: preferential interest rates on private and investment accounts, free account management, discounted mortgages, and more
  • Migros Club School: contribution to course costs at the Club School
  • Sports and fitness offers: redeem a leisure bonus of up to CHF 1000.–, e.g., for an annual subscription to the M-Fitness Center
