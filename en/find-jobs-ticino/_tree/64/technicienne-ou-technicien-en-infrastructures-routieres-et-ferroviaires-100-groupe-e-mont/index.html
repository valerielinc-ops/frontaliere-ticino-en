Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as maintenance of installations.

Mission

  • Develop implementation plans and records with technical drawings and details for our projects related to road and rail electrical infrastructure
  • Ordering equipment for construction sites
  • Ensure consistency and consistency of technical documentation of facilities
  • Follow-up of technical changes and incorporate adaptations to plant plans
  • Working closely with project leaders
  • Technical support to assemblers
  • Participate in the monitoring of road and rail electrical infrastructure projects
  • Participate in the calculation of bid and bid files

• Ensure compliance with electrical infrastructure standards, guidelines and requirements

Profile

  • CFCs of electrician or electrician planners
  • To benefit from initial professional experience
  • At ease with computer tools and drawing software (Revit, Autocad, etc...)
  • Organized, autonomous and precise person

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success"
