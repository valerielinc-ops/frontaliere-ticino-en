Introduction

For our Planning sector, we are looking for a (M/F) Project Manager whose main mission will be to lead the governance projects of the supply chain.

Responsibilities

  • Piloting projects (realising frameworks, setting schedules, coordinating teams, guaranteeing deliverables, instructing performance indicators);
  • Support the preparation of governance bodies and follow-up of actions;
  • Contribute to the structuring of supply chain topics on the Biel site;
  • Participate in updating supply chain processes;
  • Participate in the establishment of the supply chain skills development network;
  • Supporting change with stakeholders;
  • Identify and propose improvement axes in order to optimize the processes and tools of supply chain.

Profile

  • Diploma of engineer EPF/HES or equivalent training with specialization in supply chain and/or production management
  • Experience in planning, supply chain or production management
  • Good mastery of project management methods and tools
  • Excellent communication skills both oral and written
  • Very common sense in relation to the ability to link, federate and facilitate coordination between teams
  • Spirit of analysis, capacity for synthesis and backwardness

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
