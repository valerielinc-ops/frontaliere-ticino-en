Tile setter (m/f)

5000 Aarau Tile coverings

  • Temporary

Ofis

01 Future employer

Are you fit and looking for a new challenge? For our clients, we are looking for committed and reliable tile setters for temporary or permanent positions.

02 Your task

  • As a tile setter, you lay floors and walls using materials such as ceramic, mosaic, or natural stone.
  • You work both on new construction and renovation projects and cut the raw material precisely to fit the respective subfloors.
  • You enjoy laying tiles on floors, walls, or stairs, and do so in a clean, efficient manner.
  • You work independently with your personal tools and distinguish yourself through open communication and a customer-oriented approach.

04 Your profile

  • Vocational training as a tile setter in Switzerland or an EU country
  • Knowledge of occupational safety
  • Friendly and professional manner
  • Independent, clean, and efficient way of working
  • Aged between 20 and 50
  • Physically fit and resilient
  • Good knowledge of German or Italian
  • A driving licence and own car are an advantage

05 Things to know

Only electronic applications can be considered. Applications sent by post will not be returned.
