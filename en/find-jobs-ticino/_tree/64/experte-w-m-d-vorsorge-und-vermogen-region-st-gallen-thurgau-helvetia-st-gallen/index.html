Customer Consulting – Sales

Workload: 50-100% Location: Experience level: With professional experience FlexOffice: on-site

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, that means much more than simply ticking off tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards, and bring the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand, and your ideas, initiative, and contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to truly make a difference for our customers, your team, and your personal development.

What you achieve

  • Professional Expertise: As an experienced insurance consultant focusing on the retirement provision and financial business, you know the industry and its products. You advise our clients, develop financial and pension plans, support and empower our client advisors, conduct training sessions, and serve as the primary contact for retirement provision questions
  • Market Analysis: You conduct market analyses and develop market penetration strategies to identify and seize potential and opportunities in the area of retirement provision and finance
  • Expansion of Retirement Provision and Finance: You are involved from the very beginning and play a key role in building and shaping the area surrounding insurance and banking solutions (e.g., asset management, funds) in retirement provision and wealth. Planning marketing measures, implementation, and controlling are further within your responsibility
  • Building Customer Relationships: You maintain close relationships with the client advisors you support, assist them on their path to success, and actively expand your network within Helvetia and in the market
  • Teamwork: As a member of our Retirement Provision and Wealth Expert Community, you make a significant contribution to the professional and sales-related further development of these topics at Helvetia

What you bring

  • Expertise: You have a sound education or degree as well as several years of professional experience in the financial services industry
  • Entrepreneurial Spirit: You are enthusiastic and inspiring, enjoy building things from scratch, and like collaborating with various stakeholders
  • Strong Communication Skills: You are an excellent communicator and can convey complex matters in an understandable way
  • Analytical Thinking: You have a flair for numbers, think in an interconnected way, and possess strong organizational and planning skills
  • Customer Orientation: The satisfaction of your clients is close to your heart, and you do everything you can to meet their needs in the best possible way
