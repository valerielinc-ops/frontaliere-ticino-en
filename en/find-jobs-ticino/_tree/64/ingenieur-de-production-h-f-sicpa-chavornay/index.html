Full description

PRODUCTION INGENIEUR/E (1-YEAR FIXED-TERM CONTRACT)

Your Mission

We are looking for an Industrial Engineer Projects with 3 to 5 years of experience in industrial project management to join our Chavornay site and manage a diverse portfolio of projects in a chemical production environment.

A true cross-cutting coordinator, you intervene at the interface between the operational and technical teams in order to transform the needs of the field into concrete and lasting solutions.

Key responsibilities

  • Pilot technical and organisational projects from definition to implementation.
  • Manage multiple projects simultaneously within defined timelines, costs and priorities.
  • Collect and analyse the needs of different stakeholders in order to propose suitable solutions.
  • Ensure coordination between the various departments: Production, Engineering, Maintenance, HSE, Quality, Supply Chain and external suppliers.
  • Monitoring the progress of projects and ensuring the proper implementation of action plans.
  • Identify risks, anticipate blocking points and implement the necessary corrective actions.
  • Support operational teams in the deployment of improvements and new processes.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of the site's industrial performance.

Profile sought

  • Degree in Chemistry/Chemical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Agri-Food or Pharmaceutical.
  • 3-5 years of experience in project management in an industrial environment, ideally chemical.
  • Excellent ability to manage multiple projects in parallel and effectively manage priorities.
  • Strong ability to work cross-sectionally with various stakeholders (Production, Maintenance, Engineering, HSE, Quality, Suppliers).
  • Autonomous, structured and pragmatic personality, with strong field orientation and results.
  • Professional mastery of French and excellent communication and coordination skills.

Why join us?

  • Join a global leader in trusted technologies, committed to a high-impact mission.
  • Move to the heart of a strong, financially and resolutely innovative business strategy.
  • Collaborate with high-level experts and contribute to international projects with high added value.
  • Work in a multicultural, integrity-based environment where autonomy, accountability and leadership are encouraged.
