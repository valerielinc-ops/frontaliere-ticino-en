The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

We are looking for our team on January 1, 2027 or by appointment. This allows you to combine professional activity and leisure activities • Your further development is important to us: Thanks to our wide variety of internal educational offers, you benefit from targeted and individual competence development and promotion.
