Full description

Your surroundings

Her future workplace is the Psychiatry Center Nordbünden. It offers outpatient psychiatric treatments as well as daily clinical services for adults. The Nordbünden Psychiatry Center comprises two day clinics with locations in Chur and Thusis as well as two ambulatories in Chur and Cazis.

Their tasks

  • You will take over the professional, personnel and organizational management of the general psychiatric day clinic
  • They ensure professional, individual and patient-centered care and care
  • They participate in treatment planning and work in the reference care system
  • You help shape the milieutherapeutic offer and take over the management of therapeutic individual and group offers
  • You promote an appreciative interprofessional cooperation and network with external treatment partners
  • They ensure and continuously develop the quality of care as well as the standards and processes
  • They accompany change and development processes and participate in the implementation of company and process goals

Our requirement profile

  • You have a degree as a qualified nurse HF/FH and have several years of professional experience in psychiatric care
  • Ideally, you will have leadership experience as well as completed leadership training or are ready to complete it.
  • They are characterised by strong social skills and appreciate interprofessional cooperation.
  • You communicate clearly, securely and appreciatively
  • You act goal- and solution-oriented and have a strong organizational skill
  • They convince through high professional and social competence and bring knowledge in quality management

We offer you

  • You take on a varied and responsible activity
  • A diverse and cooperative cooperation with all professional groups at all levels
  • With part-time work models, regular working hours from Monday to Friday and unpaid holidays, an optimal work-life balance
  • You benefit from progressive working conditions (above average social benefits, holiday credits of at least 30 days)
  • Take advantage of our internal training and further education offer – we also support your external training needs
