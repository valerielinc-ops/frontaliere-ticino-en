Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process.

Your tasks

  • Choral conduction with focus on boy and male voices
  • Cooperation in the management team and with the singing teachers
  • joint further development of the Lucerne cantoery

Your profile

  • several years of experience in choral work with boy and young male voices
  • several years of proven conducting experience with supra-regional broadcasting
  • excellent artistic personality with dedication, enthusiasm and pedagogical skill
  • University degree in choral conducting (optional also in singing and piano)
  • Flexibility for use on rehearsal weekends, in choir camps and at performances

More on the spot

We offer

  • a choir in the best condition, with great charisma and strong networking with cooperation partners such as the Lucerne University of Music, the Jesuit Church of Lucerne, the Lucerne Theater and the Lucerne Symphony Orchestra
  • a motivated team with the spirit for further development and exciting joint projects and further developments
  • if qualified and desired, the opportunity to extend the workload with vocal training lessons

The interviews will take place on Friday/Saturday, 11./12. December 2026. The trial conductors will take place on Friday/Saturday, 8/9 January 2027. Trial days (afternoon and evening)

  • Mondays and Thursdays (boys and men's choir), at most Fridays

The Lucerne Music School is one of the largest music schools in Switzerland. 163 teachers teach children, adolescents and adults between the ages of 2 and 86 in around 3,500 subjects. The Music School Lucerne deals intensively with the topics "new forms of teaching" and "digital transformation". Contemporary music lessons, variety of musical styles and a great enthusiasm for music characterize the teachers of the Lucerne Music School.
