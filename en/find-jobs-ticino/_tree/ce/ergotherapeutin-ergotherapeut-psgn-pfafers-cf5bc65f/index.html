Full description

Look forward to an exciting area of work in which you will work in the day-to-day clinical and inpatient area. In a pleasant working atmosphere, a varied environment awaits you, in which you can contribute your expertise and help shape the future of psychiatry in St.Gallen together with us. Your tasks

  • Together with our interdisciplinary treatment team, you will support patients with subacute psychological and social problems in the daily clinical setting and actively contribute to their individual treatment.
  • After in-depth training, you will lead ergotherapeutic group therapies in the daily clinical setting and accompany the patients in a structured way through the entire therapy process.
  • In the inpatient acute psychiatric setting, you take over ergotherapeutic treatments in various individual and group therapies in the gerontopsychiatric area and bring your expertise specifically into the treatment.
  • As part of a dedicated interdisciplinary treatment team, you accompany patients with psychiatric diseases and related health problems and support them in their social and professional reintegration.
  • Your versatile day-to-day work covers the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases and offers you the opportunity to use your ergotherapeutic expertise in different treatment contexts and to coordinate closely with other departments and assignments. Your profile
  • You have completed training as an occupational therapist HF/FH and have a professional training permit from the Canton of St.Gallen or meet the requirements to obtain it before taking up the job.
  • Ideally, you will have professional experience in adult and/or gerontop psychiatry as well as experience in carrying out group therapies and enjoy a versatile field of work and appreciative treatment of patients.
  • Interest in the implementation of ZEPS groups (experience, perspective creation) with a focus on social participation and everyday activities is beneficial.
  • Creativity, innovation and design skills as well as the motivation to actively develop therapy offers and treatment pathways characterize your work method. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. We are on the road digitally and are committed to sustainability, so we look forward to your online application via our homepage. As soon as your application has been received by us online, you will receive a confirmation of receipt by e-mail. We are happy to get to know you based on your dossier and check whether your qualifications, skills and you as a person fit the advertised position and the psychiatry St.Gallen. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also get the opportunity to get to know your future team. You will receive a telephone confirmation from us if we decide for you after the second interview and ask you if you are still convinced of us. If we agree, we will draw up your contract. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. So you can get a differentiated picture of the company and already prepare for the job. On the first day you can expect an onboarding in your new team and with other colleagues. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also get your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
