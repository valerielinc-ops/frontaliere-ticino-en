Full description

We're here to make you feel sexy, empowered & UNSTOPPABLE. Founded in 1984, Tally Weijl has been on a crazy journey to celebrate fashion, independence, fun, love, & community for close to 40 years. Today we're present in 35 markets with over 700+ stores, online presence, and 3m+ fans across social channels. Show us how #UNSTOPPABLE you are! ## STORE MANAGER (m/f) ### YOUR CONTRIBUTION TO OUR COLLECTIVE SUCCESS Under the direct responsibility of the Regional Manager, you are responsible for your store and your goal is:

  • To lead and train your team towards success.
  • To ensure, with your team, optimal customer service.
  • To achieve sales targets, wage management, and inventory and markdown rates.
  • To ensure the implementation of omni Channel in your store.
  • You are responsible for the respect of safety measures in the store.
  • You oversee the optimal management of merchandise and ensure a clean store visual in line with internal guidelines.
  • With your help, your team gains new customers and you perpetuate relationships with existing customers.
  • You act as an engine and know how to motivate your team. ### WHAT CHARACTERIZES YOU
  • You have basic commercial training in retail.
  • You have already 2-3 years of professional experience in a similar position, preferably in the young fashion sector.
  • You like to work result-oriented and measure your success by the objectives achieved and key figures.
  • Organization, planning, and communication are among your strengths.
  • You have very good knowledge of English, any other language is an advantage.
  • You are up-to-date on fashion, creative, and 100% oriented towards sales and customers.
  • In a rapidly evolving environment, you remain calm and flexible. ## YOUR ADVANTAGES WITH US
  • We encourage professional development and offer many career opportunities.
  • Varied work in a dynamic, open, and team-oriented company culture.
  • Attractive discounts for employees (50%).
  • A work environment where fashion and the pleasure of working are at the forefront.
  • International professional experience.
  • Minimum of 25 days of vacation (+ 3 days of presence bonus).
  • Base salary and possibilities of bonuses/rewards. ## Contact Do you want to be part of our Fashion Story and become an enthusiastic member of our #TALLYFAMILY? Then share your personal talent with us and apply now to HR J.
