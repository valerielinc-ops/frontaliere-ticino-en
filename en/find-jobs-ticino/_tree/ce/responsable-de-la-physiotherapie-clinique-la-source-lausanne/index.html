Full description

La Source Clinic is a unique, multidisciplinary acute care facility. It belongs to the La Source Foundation, an independent, private, not-for-profit institution that also oversees the La Source Institute and High School of Health. The independence conferred on him by this status allows him to invest continuously in human and technological terms in order to guarantee his patients the best possible care. We are looking for a Head of physiotherapy from 80 to 100%

Key Position Information

Reference : CLS/RH/2026.793 / Type of contract : Contract with indefinite duration from 80 to 100% / Date of entry : as soon as we find the right person / Location : in the heart of Lausanne (M2 stop « Riponne » or Bus 2, 3 or 21 stop « Beaulieu-Jomini »)

Why do we need you?

As part of the replacement of the current holder, we are looking for our future Head of Physiotherapy.

This position allows you to combine clinical practice and managerial responsibilities, with an activity mainly dedicated to the physiotherapeutic management of patients. You also ensure the coordination and management of the service (about 30% of the activity), contribute to its development and ensure the quality of the services, in collaboration with the various medical and institutional interlocutors.

What will you do?

  • Ensure physiotherapeutic management of patients and adapt treatments to their evolution
  • Collaborate with doctors and other health professionals in patient monitoring
  • Organize and coordinate the activities of the Service
  • Provide guidance to employees (nearly 7 EFAs), manage planning and conduct annual interviews
  • Overseeing administrative and budgetary aspects of the service
  • Develop service activities by offering innovative services and partnerships
  • Contribute to the development of practices and continuous improvement of the quality of services
  • Represent service to professional bodies and maintain knowledge in the field of physiotherapy

Who are you going to work with?

We are a team of 11 physiotherapists who appreciate as much teamwork as exchanges and moments of conviviality. Everyone brings his personality and skills, in a dynamic and warm atmosphere. We look forward to welcoming our future manager to share new ideas and develop the service together.

What do we expect from you?

The indispensables

  • Holder of a Bachelor's degree in Physiotherapy or equivalent
  • Have confirmed professional experience in physiotherapy and exercise autonomy in practice
  • Demonstrate good organisational and coordination capacity
  • Have first experience in coaching or managing a team
  • Demonstrate leadership, communication and relational ease

Your assets

  • Have experience as a responsible person or physiotherapist
  • To benefit from further training in management or team management
  • Knowledge of the functioning of a hospital environment
  • Focus on practice development and continuous quality improvement

Why choose The Source Clinic?

  • Join the largest private clinic in the canton of Vaud and evolve in a dynamic environment, recognized for its expertise and know-how
  • Benefit from a human-sized work environment, while benefiting from the wealth and diversity of activities of a future-oriented health institution
  • Benefit from attractive terms of engagement and advantageous social benefits (reception and integration, LaMal participation, advantageous LPP, discount, etc.)
  • Reconciling work and private life, through opportunities for part-time work, up to more than 7 weeks of vacation, unpaid leave, compensated holidays and valuing exchange time
  • Sharing similar values: a Unique, Respectful, Innovative and Responsible Clinic
