Full description

Have you always wanted to drive trucks and get to know the road from a new perspective? Then your new challenge awaits you. You acquire the C/E driving license in the first month and get to know the tasks as a chauffeur directly on the job.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operations headquarters Schönbühl, Industriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

Duration of training from theory course: about 6-12 months We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You supply our sales outlets in the cantons of Aargau, Bern and Solothurn
  • You drive truck with trailer and ensure safe transport
  • Secure the cargo and check it before each trip
  • You carry out the daily checks on your assigned vehicle
  • You load and unload goods and returns
  • You are on day trips and start and finish your working day in Schönbühl

Requirements

  • No professional experience: necessary as a chauffeur
  • Initial or continuing education: or several years of professional experience
  • You are willing to work at irregular hours
  • You convince with your teamwork, resilience and flexibility
  • You act responsibly and have a strong security awareness
  • You have a category B driving licence
  • You are enthusiastic about trucks, road traffic and logistics
  • German (very good knowledge)

Contatto
