The Position The Opportunity The NPC Commercial Lead owns the end-to-end commercial responsibility on Near Patient Care products in Kuwait, with a special focus on the CGM. This is the central leadership role in the proposed structure, consolidating direct team leadership, distributor and channel management, and MOH disease-committee relationships under a single point of accountability. The Lead drives HCP adoption through close, hands-on coaching of the Senior HCP Adoption Sales Specialists, while personally owning the distributor team management and the committee pathways needed to unlock MOH penetration.

Key Responsibilities

  • Team Leadership & Coaching: Provide direct leadership and coaching to the full NPC team, driving

performance and building the team’s readiness to scale as MOH penetration unlocks growth.

  • Distributor & Channel Management: Own the distributor relationship end-to-end, including the distributor’s

salesforce and nurse educators, and lead joint commercial planning.

  • MOH Committee Management: Own and coordinate relationships across the different MOH disease committees, orchestrating the requests needed to unlock the penetration pathway.
  • KAM Coordination: Coordinate with Key Account Managers (KAMs) to identify and unlock commercial opportunities across all accounts.
  • Reporting & Planning: Track the CGM sales trajectory against the agreed 2030 target and prepare the business case for further headcount investment volume justifies it.

Who you are

Required Experience and Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in life sciences, business, or a related field; advanced degree a plus.
  • 10+ years of experience in medical devices, diagnostics, or pharmaceutical commercial roles, ideally including diabetes care, CGM, or near-patient testing with a min of 5 years in a leadership position.

-Fluent in English and Arabic.

Required Competencies and Skills

  • Demonstrated success managing direct and indirect team members and navigating MOH or other public-sector stakeholder committees in Kuwait.
  • Prior people-leadership or field-coaching experience, with the ability to drive performance through direct, hands- on management of a small team.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills across MOH, CMS, KAMs, and distributor organizations.
