Full description

The Department of Neuroorthopaedics of the University Children's Hospital Beider Basel (UKBB) supervises patients and their families with a dynamic team in over 4500 consultations/years, including transition to the adult area of the University Hospital Basel (USB). We are an expert center for orthopaedic deformities in rare neurological and neuromuscular diseases, and are thus part of several multidisciplinary speaking hours (with colleagues in spinal surgery, neurosurgery and various paediatric areas). We have a state-of-the-art clinical trial laboratory for neuroorthopedic patients, as well as patients with general deformities, sports injuries and spinal pathologies. In addition to patient care, research projects and teaching also belong to the everyday life of our university children's hospital. The UKBB is located in the heart of the city of Basel for innovative strength, further development possibilities and interdisciplinary cooperation. It awaits you an exciting and varied activity in a cross-departmental clinical and research environment (Clinical Neuroorthopaedics and Motion Analysis in cooperation with the Department of Bioengineering and the Department of Clinical Research at the University of Basel).
