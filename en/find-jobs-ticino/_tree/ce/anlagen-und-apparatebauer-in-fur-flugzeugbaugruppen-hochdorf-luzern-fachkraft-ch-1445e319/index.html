Full description

The operation offers a challenging activity in the construction of aircraft assemblies. You work independently on mechanical connections and seals. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • to set up aircraft assemblies according to specifications in the assembly gauge
  • to drill parts, deburring and connecting them with riveting systems
  • to carry out sealing work according to process-related regulations
  • to actively participate in 5S and CIP
  • to ensure quality through self-checks and deviation messages
  • Requirements
  • Basic technical training such as plant and apparatus manufacturers or metalworkers
  • Independent working methods with pronounced personal responsibility
  • Practical thinking with focus on viable solutions
  • Knowledge of German for everyday work
  • Careful introduction to the specialized field of work
  • Work in a technically characterised production environment
