Take on new challenges: 100 % healthy Kirchweg 1, 6022 Grosswangen Networking is your thing and you want to work in an entrepreneurial capacity? Welcome to the CONCORDIA family! We are looking for you for our branch office in Grosswangen.

What you can expect

  • You independently advise and look after our customers at your branch office or in their homes.
  • You acquire new customers and sell needs-based health insurance and pension solutions.
  • You proactively arrange appointments, prepare quotations and complete the associated administrative tasks.
  • You implement sales activities, build a network and represent CONCORDIA in Grosswangen and the surrounding area.

What you bring

  • You have completed vocational training in a service-oriented or sales-oriented environment and live in the region.
  • Customer contact, sales and advice are your passion, and administrative skills round out your profile.
  • You have very good German language skills; any additional language is an advantage.
  • You are communicative and sociable, and enjoy working independently.
  • You have completed the VBV training or are willing to complete it - we will support you.
