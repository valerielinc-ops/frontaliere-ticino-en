Full description

The company offers a wide range of tasks in technical support and repair of high-quality kitchen equipment. You get the opportunity to expand your knowledge in training courses and pass it on professionally. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Provide technical support by telephone, online and in writing
  • Carry out workshop repairs of inductions and kitchen appliances
  • In challenging cases, take on missions on site
  • Conduct face-to-face and online training for specialist partners
  • Create repair offers and process complaints

Requirements

  • Degree as electrical installer EFZ, auto mechatronics engineer or automatic engineer
  • English skills of at least A2
  • Knowledge of MS Office such as PowerPoint and Word
  • Readiness for initial assignments as field service technician
  • Interest in didactics, knowledge transfer and presentation techniques

Offer

  • Attractive framework conditions with good social benefits
