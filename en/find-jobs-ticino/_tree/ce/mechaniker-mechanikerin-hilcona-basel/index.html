Full description

Do you want to work for a company that stands for quality, sustainability, tradition and enjoyment? Do you convince through a responsible way of working and enjoy new challenges? Then we are looking for you as a mechanic for our cutting plant area in Basel. In this role, together with your teammates, you are responsible for the maintenance and maintenance of the automatic conveyor systems as well as the carrying out of repairs throughout the operation.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: day shift
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Hagenaustrasse 3 4056 Basel

Tasks

  • Maintenance and repair of various conveyor systems and machines
  • Carrying out repairs and troubleshooting of mechanical and pneumatic components of the supervised facilities
  • Conversion/optimisation of existing facilities
  • Coordination of work with production and external bodies
  • Responsible for safety in the area of responsibility with regard to occupational safety and health protection
  • Responsible for anchoring and further developing TOP X in the area of responsibility

Requirements

  • Completed vocational training as a mechanic or mechatronics specialist with at least two years of professional experience, ideally in the field of electrical engineering and as a maintenance mechanic
  • German in word and writing, knowledge of French advantageous
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Independent, reliable, resilient, flexible and motivated
  • Team-ready, well-maintained appearance and compliance with hygiene regulations
  • Open to new things and changes as well as cost-conscious action and decision-making

  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments at our departmental event and strengthen the team spirit during the company run – celebrate, move and make contacts together!
  • Market-friendly wages and benefits: Secure your future with our above-average retirement savings, receive anniversary bonuses, Christmas bonuses and attractive employee referral bonuses. In addition, you receive the personal discount card from Coop and we cover two thirds of your pension fund contributions!
