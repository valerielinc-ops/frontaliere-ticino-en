Full description

ZURZACH Care is the leading company group in Switzerland for prevention, rehabilitation, sleep medicine, and professional and social reintegration. The group is headquartered in Bad Zurzach and currently employs around 1300 people at more than 20 locations. Thanks to the combination of different specialist competencies, partnerships with upstream and downstream institutions, and our own research department, we can offer new, demand-oriented care models.
