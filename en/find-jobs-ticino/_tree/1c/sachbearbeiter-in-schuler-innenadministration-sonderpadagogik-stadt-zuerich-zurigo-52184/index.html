Full description

To complement our dedicated team in the field of pupils and special education, we are looking for 1. February 2027 or by appointment a student administration special education.

  • They shall be responsible for drawing up special training orders in a signed form.
  • They take over the monitoring and controlling in the area of special training, embedded in the overall coordination of the management.
  • They coordinate the transport of the children through the organization for school and therapy trips.
  • They write demanding correspondence and take over the protocol management.
  • They support the head of the department of special education in various tasks and actively contribute to the continuous development of the department.
  • Completed commercial training (EFZ) and some years of professional experience, ideally in the administrative and/or educational field
  • Open-minded and resilient personality with a high service orientation, organizational talent and precise and independent working method
  • Enjoyment in dealing with different stakeholders and adequate communication skills at different levels
  • Self-employed work and high flexibility (large workload in the spring quarter, holidays preferably in school holidays)
  • Stylistic, exact written and oral expression in German

a versatile, responsible and exciting task in a committed team on site and with committed school leaders in the school circle. You have the opportunity to make an important contribution to the well-functioning school system of the city of Zurich. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions according to the city guidelines. Your workplace is centrally located at Enge station.

The school district Uto is one of the seven school districts of the city of Zurich. It comprises the districts 2 and 3 with the quarters Leimbach, Manegg, Wollishofen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon.

Over 6,000 students are taught in the 19 school units. More than 1,450 employees from the teaching, care, administration and domestic service groups work in our schools.
