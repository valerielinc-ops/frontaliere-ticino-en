Full description

Do you want to evolve within the canton's reference university medical institution? As a proposaler, you want to contribute to the projects of a large institution such as the HUGs and you recognise your institutional values (respect, team spirit, recognition, trust, equality and inclusion). You supervise and ensure quality medical services, under the supervision of the medical hierarchy, of a care unit within the rehabilitation and geriatric department. You coordinate practical activities, clinical or therapeutic interventions, as well as administrative and organizational. You participate in the training of intern physicians and have the opportunity to receive quality post-graduate training contributing to the qualification of specialist in internal and general medicine (service of general internal medicine category B), the qualification of specialist in geriatrics (service of geriatrics category A), the qualification of specialist in physical medicine and rehabilitation. In addition, you contribute to a noble mission of major utility to the present society and benefit from a diverse training framework within the department. A multidisciplinary and highly qualified team will accompany you. The development of our employees in their mission is an important value for us with a personalized career path and several opportunities for evolution within the institution.
