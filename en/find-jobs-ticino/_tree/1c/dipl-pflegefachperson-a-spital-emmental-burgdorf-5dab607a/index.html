Full description

The psychiatry of the Emmental Hospital offers demanding jobs for doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers and administrative staff with the opportunity for personal development and takes into account their own resources. In our manageable company, the personality of each individual employee shapes the way we deal with each other and our cooperation. We regard individuality as enrichment. We offer jobs at the two locations Burgdorf and Langnau. We are a successful training facility of the BZ Pflege.

For our openly managed crisis intervention station at the Burgdorf location, we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by agreement

Task area

Our department is guided by the following principles

  • Person-oriented attitude according to Recovery and Open Dialogue
  • Relationship-oriented intensive care
  • Extensive treatment settings from stationary to semi-stationary to ambulatory in the sense of social psychiatry

Profile

  • You have completed training as a nurse HF/FH with a focus on psychiatry.
  • You appreciate a dynamic and evolving working environment and enjoy actively shaping it.
  • You enjoy working in a team and at the same time bring a high degree of independence.
