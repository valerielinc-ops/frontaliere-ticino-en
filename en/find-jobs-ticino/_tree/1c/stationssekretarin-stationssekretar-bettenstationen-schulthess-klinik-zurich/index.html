Full description

Tasks

  • Relieving the nursing team and department management by independently assuming administrative, organisational and logistical tasks
  • Ensure smooth management of station materials, equipment and furniture as well as coordination of orders and premises
  • Competent support of the station telephone including independent processing and targeted forwarding of requests
  • Friendly and appreciative reception of patients, relatives and external contact persons
  • Maintaining open, direct and solution-oriented communication and promoting constructive cooperation in the team

Requirements

  • Completed training in the commercial or health sector
  • Professional experience in health care or in the commercial sector
  • Good IT skills, especially in MS Office
  • An open-minded, committed and resilient personality
  • Independent and responsible working methods as well as strong teamwork
  • Good knowledge of German, both oral and written (other languages are advantageous)
  • Working days: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday

We offer

  • 40 hourly week
  • Attractive social benefits and numerous benefits
  • Paid or compensable changing time (15 min./day / 6.5 Fridays/year)
  • 15 minutes paid break per day
  • ZVV BonusPass / ZVV BonusPass Flex
  • No weekend use

The Schulthess Clinic in Zurich is one of the leading orthopaedic clinics in Europe with over 1,100 employees. Our central concern is to free people from their pain and restore their mobility. As a specialist clinic, we focus on surgical orthopedics, neurology, rheumatology and sports medicine. Schulthess Clinic is Swiss Olympic Medical Center.
