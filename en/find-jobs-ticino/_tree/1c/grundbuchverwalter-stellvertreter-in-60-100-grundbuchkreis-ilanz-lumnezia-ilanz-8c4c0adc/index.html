Land register administrator deputy / notary / lawyer (60 – 100 %)

Are you looking for a new, exciting challenge in the heart of Graubünden? We are located in the middle of the urban center of the Surselva, nestled in well-known tourist areas and rural regions with untouched nature.

As of 01.01.2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a competent, self-responsible personality who is actively and actively involved with us.
