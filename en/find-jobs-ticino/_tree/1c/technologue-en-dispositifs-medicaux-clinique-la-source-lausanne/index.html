Full description

La Clinique de La Source is a one-of-a-kind multidisciplinary acute care facility. It belongs to Fondation La Source, an independent, private, nonprofit institution that also oversees the Institut et Haute École de la Santé La Source. The independence afforded by this status enables it to continually invest in its people and technology in order to ensure that its patients receive the best possible care.

We are looking for a

Medical Device Technologist at 100%

  • Reference: CLS/RH/2026.762 /
  • Contract type: Permanent contract at 100% /
  • Working hours: according to schedule from Monday to Friday between 7:00 a.m. at the earliest and 8:00 p.m. at the latest + Saturday on-call duty on a rotating basis (1 every 2 months on average) /
  • Start date: as soon as we find the right person /
  • Location: in the heart of Lausanne (M2 stop “Riponne” or Bus 2, 3 or 21 stop “Beaulieu-Jomini”)

Why do we need you?

Following the incumbent’s departure, we are looking for a Medical Device Technologist to join our Sterilization team. Some opportunities allow you to take a step forward: a modern technical environment, close collaboration with clinical teams and a genuine impact on the quality and safety of care. This is what we would like to offer our future Medical Device Technologist.

What will you be expected to do?

  • Ensure the complete reprocessing of medical devices, from collection to sterilization, in compliance with quality standards and hygiene and safety rules
  • Identify, sort, pre-treat, disinfect, package and sterilize medical devices according to their specific characteristics and applicable protocols
  • Use and supervise washing, disinfection and sterilization equipment to ensure its performance and prevent malfunctions
  • Carry out quality checks and interpret technical tests (Bowie-Dick, leak tests, soiling checks) to ensure sterilization cycles comply
  • Organize activities according to elective programs, surgical priorities and emergencies
  • Ensure the computerized traceability of every step in the one-way workflow
  • Work closely with the teams from the operating room, care units, ambulatory surgery and radiology, as well as with external partners, to ensure an efficient service
  • Participate in the continuous improvement of practices, compliance with ISO 13485 requirements and staff training
  • Participate in the department’s bio-cleaning and ensure the maintenance of a working environment that complies with institutional procedures

Who will you be working with?

Our team of 11 colleagues stands out for its solidarity, empathy, availability and dynamism. The team’s motto: Together, it’s better!

In this position, you will be supervised by Monica Catarino, Head of Sterilization, who combines professional standards, organization and kindness, paying particular attention to listening and teamwork in order to ensure reliable processes and a calm working environment.

What do we expect from you?

The essentials

  • Hold a CFC as a Medical Device Technologist or have completed training as a Sterilization Technical Assistant (level 1 or 2)
  • Demonstrate initial experience in hospital sterilization or in an operating room
  • Be comfortable in a dynamic environment requiring responsiveness and a sense of priorities
  • Demonstrate rigor in applying protocols, quality standards and hygiene rules

Your strengths

  • Have good knowledge of medical devices and sterilization processes
  • Demonstrate autonomy, versatility and a great capacity for adaptation
  • Enjoy teamwork and communicate constructively with various stakeholders
  • Have an eye for detail, organizational skills and a sense of responsibility
  • Stand out through reliability, punctuality and flexibility in organizing work

Why choose Clinique de La Source?

  • Join the largest private clinic in the canton of Vaud, recognized as a training center
  • State-of-the-art medical technology equipment (7 operating rooms) and a great diversity of surgical activities (primarily orthopedics, neurosurgery, digestive and urological surgery, plastic surgery, gynecology and obstetrics)
  • The atmosphere of a human-scale clinic with the challenges of a future-oriented healthcare institution offering a wide range of activities
  • Attractive employment conditions and social benefits
