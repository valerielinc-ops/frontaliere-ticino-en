Full description

La Source Clinic is a unique, multidisciplinary acute care facility. It belongs to the La Source Foundation, an independent, private, not-for-profit institution that also oversees the La Source Institute and High School of Health. The independence conferred on him by this status allows him to invest continuously in human and technological terms in order to guarantee his patients the best possible care. We are looking for a 100% medical device technologist

Key Position Information

Reference : CLS/RH/2026.762 / Type of contract : Contract with Indeterminate duration 100% / Hours : according to schedule from Monday to Friday between 7h00 at the earliest and 8h00 at the latest + pick on Saturday according to turns (1 every 2 months on average) / Date of entry : as soon as we find the right person / Location : in the heart of Lausanne (M2 stop « Riponne » or Bus 2, 3 or 21 stop « Beaulieu-Jomini »)

Why do we need you?

Following the departure of the incumbent, we are looking for a Medical Device Technologist to join our Sterilization team. There are opportunities to move forward: a modern technical environment, close collaboration with clinical teams and a real impact on the quality and safety of care. This is what we want to offer our future medical device technologist.

What will you do?

  • Ensure complete reprocessing of medical devices, from collection to sterilization, in accordance with quality standards, hygiene and safety rules
  • Identify, sort, pre-treat, disinfect, condition and sterilize medical devices according to their specificity and current protocols
  • Use and supervise washing, disinfection and sterilization equipment to ensure performance and prevent malfunctions
  • Perform quality checks and interpret technical tests (Bowie-Dick, leakproofness tests, dirt checks) to ensure compliance of sterilization cycles
  • Organize activities according to elective programs, operational priorities and emergencies
  • Ensure IT traceability of all steps of the march forward
  • Collaborate closely with the O.R. teams, care units, outpatient surgery, radiology and external partners to ensure effective service
  • Participation in continuous improvement of practices, compliance with ISO 13485 requirements and training of employees
  • Participate in the bio-cleaning of the service and ensure the maintenance of a working environment in accordance with institutional procedures

Who are you going to work with?

Our team of 11 collaborators is distinguished by its solidarity, empathy, availability and dynamism. Team's slogan: Together, it's better!

In this position, you will be supervised by Monica Catarino, Head of Sterilization, who combines professional requirements, organization and benevolence, paying particular attention to listening and teamwork, in order to ensure reliable processes and a serene working environment.

What do we expect from you?

The indispensables

  • Holder of a Medical Device Technologist CFC or a Technical Assistant in Sterilization (Level 1 or 2)
  • To justify an initial experience in hospital sterilization or in an operating unit
  • Be comfortable in a dynamic environment that requires responsiveness and focus
  • Strict application of protocols, quality standards and hygiene rules

Your assets

  • Have good knowledge of medical devices and sterilization processes
  • Demonstrate autonomy, versatility and high adaptability
  • Appreciating teamwork and communicating constructively with different interlocutors
  • Having a sense of detail, organization and responsibility
  • Distinguish itself by its reliability, punctuality and flexibility in the organisation of work

  • The ambiance of a human-scale clinic with the challenges of a health institution with a variety of future-oriented activities
