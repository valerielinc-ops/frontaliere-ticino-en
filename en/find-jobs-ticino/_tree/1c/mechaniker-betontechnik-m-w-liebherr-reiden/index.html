Full description

Screws, tinkering, finding solutions and in the end see what has been created: Exactly your thing? With us, you can expect a varied workshop day in which your skills are in demand. In a well-rehearsed team, you take on responsibility and together with your colleagues ensure that everything fits in the end. These are your tasks

  • You take over the assembly and final assembly of our truck mixers on truck chassis.
  • You install mechanical, hydraulic and electrical components and put them into operation.
  • You perform functional checks and commissionings and make sure everything works reliably.
  • You maintain and repair truck mixers and concrete plants and repair technical faults independently.
  • You perform general locksmith work and sheet metal processing. You bring that with you
  • Completed basic technical training, for example as a construction machinery, agricultural machinery, truck or car mechanic EFZ or comparable training.
  • Delight in mechanics and technology as well as interest in vehicle bodies, structures and the construction industry.
  • An independent working method and pleasure in working together in a team and finding solutions.
  • Driving licence category B/BE and C are advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage. You can look forward to that.
  • Versatile tasks in a highly equipped workshop
  • A motivated team that tackles together
  • A responsible task where you see in the end what you have done
  • The safety and prospects of an internationally successful family business Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your online application. For any questions, please contact Susanne Sulzer. One passion. Many opportunities. The company Liebherr-Baumaschinen AG, based in Reiden (Switzerland), is responsible for the sales and service of earthmoving and material handling machines, tower cranes, concrete technology, special underground construction machines and mobile and crawler cranes in Switzerland.
