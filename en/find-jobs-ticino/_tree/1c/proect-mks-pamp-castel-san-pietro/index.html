Project Engineer — MKS PAMP SA, Castel San Pietro (TI).

MIXI

The Project Engineer, a direct reference of the Technical Services Manager, will be the reference point for the design and implementation of the service and distribution facilities of the site of Castel S. Pietro. Plants cover areas ranging from energy to industrial fluids, to chemicals, such as acids and reagents used in manufacturing processes. The ricera will follow complex projects from the first idea to the implementation, coordinating different people, suppliers and skills and ensuring that each intervention respects time, costs and technical standards. The Project Engineer will help make the site safer, more efficient and more sustainable, in full compliance with the technical requirements and ESG regarding the environment, health and safety.

MAIN ACTIVITIES AND RESPONSIBILITY

  • Comprehensive management (end-to-end) of complex plant projects (from concept phase to commissioning)
  • Planning, monitoring and control of budgets, timing and resources
  • Technical design and supervision of utilities plants
  • Coordination of suppliers, contractors and internal stakeholders
  • Production and validation of technical documentation
  • Supervision of installation, commissioning and start-up activities
  • Management of technical issues and project risks
  • Identification of opportunities for improvement and efficiency
  • Take risk assessments and define mitigation measures
  • Follow sustainability initiatives and energy efficiency

TRAINING AND EXPERIENCE REQUEST

  • Master Degree in Engineering
  • 5–8 years of experience in similar roles

QUALIFIES AND COMPETENCES

  • Solid skills in utilities and project management
  • Knowledge of HSE and ESG legislation
  • Excellent communication, interpersonal skills and collaboration with colleagues from different cultures, respecting diversity
  • Sense of responsibility, spirit of initiative and ability to work independently
  • Comprehensive attitude to continuous improvement and innovative thinking
  • Great organizational capacity and attention to detail
  • Resistance to stress and tight deadlines
  • Full professional mastery of Office 365
  • Great knowledge of English. Knowledge of other languages will be considered a plus
  • Swiss citizenship or EU state, or valid Swiss working permit
  • They constitute preferential title: knowledge of Swiss regulations (authorization procedures and technical documentation for plants and buildings), knowledge of ATEX environments, certifications in Project Management

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
