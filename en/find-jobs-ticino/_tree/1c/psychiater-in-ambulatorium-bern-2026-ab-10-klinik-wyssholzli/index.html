Full description

The Wysshölzli Clinic is a leading psychiatric and psychotherapeutic specialist clinic for women with addiction disorders and eating disorders. Our main location in Oberaargau, Canton of Bern, in a quiet location convinces with beautiful nature and proximity to Bern and Olten. Our outpatient clinic in the city of Bern is centrally located near the train station. The clinic offers 37 inpatient and numerous outpatient treatment places, with referrals from all cantons of German-speaking Switzerland. We are looking for a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy by appointment, from 10% for the outpatient clinic in the city of Bern Working days and workload can be flexibly arranged individually and also monthly

  • We offer you a high degree of flexibility. Workload, working days and scope of work can be agreed individually and, if necessary, also flexibly arranged on a monthly basis. This makes the position ideal for combining with a practical activity, another job, family obligations or other professional commitments.
  • Your tasks ‐ Case-leading outpatient psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of adults with disorders from the entire psychiatric diagnostic spectrum, in particular eating disorders and addiction disorders ‐ Discussions with relatives as required ‐ Collaboration in the interdisciplinary team of therapists ‐ Documentation in the electronic clinic information system ‐ Substitute in the inpatient area on request and consultation
  • Your profile ‐ Specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy ‐ Empathetic approach to patients ‐ Integrative and flexible personality, independent, resilient ‐ Experience in the areas of eating disorders and addiction disorders is an advantage Your opportunity ‐ Very high flexibility in terms of workload and working days ‐ Additional assignments and deputies in the inpatient area on request ‐ A challenging, varied and trusting job with a lot of personal responsibility ‐ Work in a motivated, interprofessional team and a very good working atmosphere, which is characterized by transparency and mutual respect Ms. Barbara Ammann, Director, will be happy to provide you with more information: Tel. 062 510 58 06, Please apply via the online portal on our website or send your application to personal@wysshoelzli.ch.
