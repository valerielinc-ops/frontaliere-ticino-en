Full description

In this role, you accompany our patients during breakfast and lunch and help them to eat and drink safely and as independently as possible. With your attention and empathy, you contribute significantly to their well-being in everyday life.

Your tasks • Accompaniment during meals: You support our patients with safe and dignified food intake and promote their independence when eating and drinking • Professional support with FOTT: You apply the principles of Facio-Oral Tract Therapy (FOTT) and especially accompany people with swallowing disorders or neurological limitations • Attention and safety: You observe the eating and swallowing behavior attentively and are sensitive to possible difficulties to ensure safe food intake • Collaboration in the team: You work closely with speech therapy, medical team, care, nutritional advice and kitchen and contribute your observations to the treatment process and participate in rapports • Create a pleasant eating situation: You provide a calm, supportive atmosphere during meals and pay attention to a suitable sitting position and individual diets

Your profile • Qualification: Completed training Specialist Health / Specialist Health EFZ and you have completed or are ready to complete a FOTT course (Facio-Oral Tract Therapy) • Experience: 2 years of professional experience • Personality: You are an empathetic, patient and responsible personality who enjoys supporting people in everyday life • Working method: You work attentively, reliably and appreciate cooperation in an interprofessional team • Motivation: With your commitment, you want to contribute to ensuring that our patients can consume their meals safely, pleasantly and as independently as possible. • Openness: Also re-entrants and are welcome

Work assignments • Regular working hours from Monday to Friday • In addition to the meal accompaniments, you support the care team

We offer

• Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care • Flexibility bonus: Voluntary participation in our internal care pool for additional layers in care, which are additionally attractively compensated. • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
