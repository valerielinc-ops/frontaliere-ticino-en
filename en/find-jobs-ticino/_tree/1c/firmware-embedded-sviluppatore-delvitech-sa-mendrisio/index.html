Full description

Develop the firmware of Delvitech's embedded vision systems, working in C/C++ and Linux close to the hardware of our AI inspection machines. Job Summary We are looking for a Firmware Embedded Developer with hardware skills to join the Embedded team in our R&D department. The role requires strong programming skills and expertise in machine vision, image processing and embedded computing, with a patient, organized and problem-solving approach. Location: Mendrisio, Switzerland, 100% on-site. Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and optimize firmware and software for embedded systems using C/C++ and Python.
  • Work with Linux-based environments, focusing on system performance and efficiency.
  • Develop and integrate device drivers for microcontrollers and embedded platforms.
  • Optimize memory usage and handle cross-compilation for embedded architectures.
  • Conduct debugging and memory leak analysis to ensure system stability.
  • Work with U-Boot, Linux Kernel, kernel drivers and Device Trees to enhance system functionality.
  • Collaborate with R&D teams on machine vision and image processing solutions.
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to system architecture discussions.
  • Work on real-time video acquisition, analysis, recording and streaming. Requirements
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electronics or a related field; a Master's degree is a plus.
  • At least 3 years of experience in firmware and software development for embedded systems.
  • Strong proficiency in Linux, C/C++ programming and microcontrollers; good knowledge of Python.
  • Experience with device drivers and memory optimization.
  • Knowledge of cross-compiling for embedded systems.
  • Competence in real-time video acquisition, analysis, recording and streaming.
  • Experience with version control systems (Git).
  • Advanced English, written and spoken. Nice to Have
  • Experience with U-Boot, Linux Kernel, kernel drivers and Device Trees.
  • Experience with ARM processors in Linux environments.
  • Expertise in memory leak analysis and performance tuning.
  • Familiarity with Rust for embedded development. Soft Skills Strong problem solving, creativity in approaching technical challenges, teamwork, clear communication, a proactive and results-oriented approach, passion for technology and strong planning skills. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
