Full description

With us, you ensure that high-quality products are bottled reliably every day. In a modern production environment, you take responsibility for high-performance plants, contribute your technical understanding and have the opportunity to sustainably improve processes. Become part of a team that values quality, values innovation and together ensures that everything runs smoothly – from the first shot to the clean shift transfer.

You can do that.

  • Take on an important role by using your flexible nature to serve and monitor the entire repackaging line.
  • Ensure first-class quality by carefully conducting product and packaging testing.
  • Support our plant operator and help to rectify faults to ensure a smooth process.
  • You keep an overview because cleaning work and independent plant maintenance are your responsibility.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed vocational training as an investment manager: in EFZ or valuable practical experience in the food industry.
  • Your technical flair for machines and systems as well as your high hygiene awareness set you apart.
  • You are flexible You love the challenges that the job brings and are ready for our 4-shift operation.
  • With your good knowledge of German, your physical fitness and your positive attitude to life, you fit perfectly into our team at Emmi!

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed.
