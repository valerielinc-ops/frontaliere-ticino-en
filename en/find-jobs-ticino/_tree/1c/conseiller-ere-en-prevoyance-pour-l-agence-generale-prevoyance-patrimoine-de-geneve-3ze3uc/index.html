Do you live in the Geneva area and have you ever been able to practice your sales, negotiation and networking skills? Our Agence Générale Prévoyance et Patrimoine de Genève wishes to strengthen its team and we would be delighted to receive your file!

What's waiting for you

  • Advice oriented and personalized solutions in individual and collective foresight
  • Mortgage and investment consultancy
  • Loyalty of your customers and development of your own customer portfolio
  • Acquisition of new customers to ensure the growth of your region's customer portfolio
  • Placement of other AXA products (non-life, health)
  • Performing administrative tasks related to your position, with the support of the entire AXA structure and modern monitoring tools

What you bring

  • For the benefit of a professional degree, you have first experience at your disposal in the service sector (advice and sales), experience in the field of personal insurance is welcome as well as obtaining AFA is an asset
  • Your knowledge of the services, insurance or banking sector and your indisputable affinity for financial and foresight products are necessary
  • Your personality is service oriented and you have ease in contacts with others, you seek solutions for your clients and you know how to adapt to any situation
  • You master French both orally and in writing and you stand out for your good level of English, any other language is an advantage
  • Your method of work is autonomous and oriented towards the objectives
  • Your home must be located in the area of activity concerned, you have a very good knowledge of the local economic fabric and a wide network in the Geneva region.
  • You have ease with common computer systems as well as learning new digital tools

We create framework conditions that give you the opportunity to fully develop and exploit your potential. As a Swiss insurance leader, we offer ambitious people interesting career opportunities with great freedom of action.

  • Up to 30% discount on products from AXA
  • Five weeks of vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-tariff subscription/participation in general subscription costs
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Freedom of action and self-employment
  • Innovative structures, processes and tools
  • Strong and professional training and development opportunities

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
