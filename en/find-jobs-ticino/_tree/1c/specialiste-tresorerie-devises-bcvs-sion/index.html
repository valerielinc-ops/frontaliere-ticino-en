Specialist Treasury & Currency

  • Zion
  • 80-100%

Experienced in currency trading, do you also have the talent to manage bank cash flows? You have at the BCVS the unique opportunity to make a difference and impact transactions at the bank level. Join the Treasury & Currency team where you will be a full member, recognized for your talents.

Specifically?

  • With rigour in the supervision of cash flows, you will manage the liquidity, rates, investments, etc. of our BCVS through the mastery of trading platforms and tools.
  • Skilled in trading, you will conclude the best currency/metal/derivate transactions for our customers and the bank.
  • You will monitor financial market developments and will also act as an internal expert in projects related to cash and currency.
  • Your versatility will allow you to complete the accounting and fiduciary, controlling and administration operations of the banking network and manage post-trade activities.
  • Finally, your vigilance will clearly define the limits of action in strict compliance with directives and regulations.

Do you want the challenge? This post is for you if...

  • Having completed graduate business studies (HES, Federal Diploma in Economics or equivalent) you have additional training (AZEK or CIIA level), a REPO SIX degree and extensive market experience.
  • At the same time, you master financial trading platforms, information systems (Reuters, Bloomberg) and have a certain agility in IT tools (Finnova, VBA, Excel expertise, MasterFinance being an asset).
  • A lively person and with an understanding, you are perfectly comfortable in the financial sector and discover future trends.
  • Your communication and negotiation skills are available in both French, German and English.
  • Finally, you complete your qualities with strong analytical skills.

Let's meet! Would you like to join us? We look forward to discovering your application. To do so, send your complete file to: recruitment@bcvs.ch .

Requirements

  • Ayant suivi des études commerciales supérieures (HES, Diplôme fédéral en économie ou équivalent) vous disposez de formations complémentaires (de niveau AZEK ou CIIA), d’une licence REPO SIX et d’une expérience approfondie dans les marchés.

  • Parallèlement, vous maîtrisez les plateformes de négoce de produits financiers, les systèmes d’informations (Reuters, Bloomberg) et disposez d’une agilité certaine dans les outils IT (Finnova, VBA, expertise Excel, MasterFinance étant un atout).

  • Personne vive d’esprit et avec de l’entregent, vous êtes parfaitement à l’aise dans le secteur financier et décelez les tendances à venir.

  • Vos capacités de communication et de négociation se déclinent aussi bien en français, qu’en allemand et en anglais.

  • Finalement, vous parachevez vos qualités avec de fortes capacités analytiques.
