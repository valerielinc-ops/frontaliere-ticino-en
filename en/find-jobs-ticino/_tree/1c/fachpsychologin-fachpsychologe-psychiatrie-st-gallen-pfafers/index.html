Your tasks

  • You will take over the independent case management and integrative psychiatric psychotherapeutic treatment of adult patients in individual setting and group setting.
  • In the interdisciplinary team, you work closely with doctors, psychologists, nurses and social workers.
  • The professional support and supervision of our assistant psychologists is just as much a part of your tasks as the support of your psychotherapeutic development.
  • You actively shape the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic care in Toggenburg, foster cooperation with regional health and support services and contribute your specialist focus and ideas to the further development of our evidence-based treatment offer.
  • You benefit from the broad expertise of the Psychiatry of St. Gallen as well as from regular case meetings, external supervision and individual professional support. Your profile
  • You have successfully completed your psychology studies and already gained experience in stationary adult psychiatry.
  • You have completed your Swiss-approved psychotherapy training or will complete it in the foreseeable future.
  • Outpatient, interdisciplinary treatment oriented to the needs of patients and their relatives arouses your interest.
  • Interprofessional teamwork, short decision-making paths and flat hierarchies are highly appreciated.
  • This position requires German language skills at C1 level to communicate professionally with patients, colleagues and external partners. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. You will receive a telephone confirmation from us if we decide for you after the second interview and ask you if you are still convinced of us. If we agree, we will draw up your contract. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. So you can get a differentiated picture of the company and already prepare for the job. On the first day you can expect an onboarding in your new team and with other colleagues. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also get your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
