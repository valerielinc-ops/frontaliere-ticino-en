Full description

As a specialist in environmental safety in the field of safety, health & environment (SHE), you take over the technical management of environmental safety in the Migros industry, you anchor environmental protection and environmental law with heart blood and pioneering spirit!

Mansioni

  • You guide the field of environmental safety and the Migros industry's environmental management team, which acts as a decision-making body for environmental safety issues.
  • In accordance with statutory regulations (GGBV, VeVA, StFV, GSchV, ChemV, LRV, emissions, environmental risks, chemical safety, special waste).
  • You work closely with specialists from AS-GS, environmental safety and operational sustainability of Migros Industrie and the Migros Group.
  • You are the contact point, advise and support Migros Industrie's companies on all issues relating to environmental safety.
  • You support the SAP systems professionally.

Requisiti

  • Multiannual professional experience in the SHE field in an industrial company: Group experience and experience in the management of lateral teams of advantage
  • Completed vocational education (EFZ): or completed studies of engineering, environmental sciences or a comparable course of study.
  • You know very well with the legal framework (Chemikalienrecht CH/EU, Dangerous Goods regulations, Special Waste, Environmental Protection Act, Air Purification Regulation, Water Protection Act, Environmental Risks, StFV, ISO14001).
  • You bring a firmed personality with you and you stand confidently.
  • You are highly resilient, have a strong ability to integrate and convince with your enforcing power.
  • You communicate clearly and convincingly, can argue well and have experience in stakeholder management.
  • You are ready to travel within Switzerland.
  • You have the qualification as a Dangerous Goods Officer (according to GGBV) or Environmental Officer or a comparable qualification.
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (very good knowledge)
  • English (of advantage)
