Full description

Actively shape the future of trade and take responsibility for several Migros teo locations in the Canton of Thurgau. We are looking for a dedicated management succession that actively shapes our teo’s and further develops operations at a high level. Do you feel called? We look forward to your application.

Mansioni

  • Lead over several Migros teo locations. This includes in particular the financial and personnel responsibility of the cluster (geographical area with several locations).
  • Sales, cost, quality, assortment and inventory management are one of the core tasks. You always have the overview and control specifically.
  • Ensuring management, quality standards, order, optimization as well as the management of the staff as well as the interpretation of key figures and the derivation of adequate measures.
  • Introduction and further development of efficient management processes with coordination between logistics & personnel. With your organizational talent, you ensure a smooth process.

  • Participation in small-quantity logistics including the driving of a small van (cat. B)
  • You also lead the way in challenges and lead your team with a positive attitude.

Highest motivation to take over the management of several Migros teo locations in a young, timely sales format and to further develop this together.
