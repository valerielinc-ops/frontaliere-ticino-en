Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 1,800 employees for the health of our population? The emergency department at the Biel Hospital Center is open to patients 24 hours a day, 365 days a year and is one of the largest emergency centers. More than 25,000 patients are treated each year. The Emergency Department is an SGNOR-recognized training institution of category 2 for the acquisition of the interdisciplinary focus clinical emergency medicine, general internal medicine category IV ambulatory as well as for the training sonography abdomen and POCUS.

To strengthen the medical team we are looking from 01. January 2029

Interdisciplinary emergency department 60-100 %

Your tasks – our job

  • Care of ambulatory and inpatient emergency patients under supervision
  • Possibility for training for the interdisciplinary focus clinical emergency medicine SGNOR and comprehensive insight into acute medicine with a very wide range of patients
  • In-depth ultrasound training (FA Abdominal Sonography, FA POCUS: chest sonography, basic emergency onography, echocardiography); Participation in internal ultrasound courses) as well as numerous training opportunities (shock room simulations according to ATLS and ACLS, skills training etc.)
  • Possibility of participation in rescue operations preclinical
  • Possibility of rotation in anesthesia, intensive care & emergency practice
  • New training concept according to SIWF and SGNOR requirements with an additional internal training day per month
  • Integration of EPAs.
  • Integration in the emergency team consisting of NF cadre physicians, rotational assistant physicians and rotational senior physicians, emergency care and sub-assistant physicians
  • Assistance in the care of sub-assistant doctors

Your skills – our requirements

  • The position is aimed at candidates for the focus of clinical emergency medicine, but also at experienced assistant doctors with a professional goal of family practice
  • At least one year of clinical experience in internal medicine, like additional experience in surgery, emergency medicine, intensive care medicine or orthopaedics
  • Great interest in acute medicine
  • Engaged personality with motivation and initiative for significant cooperation for the further development of our emergency department in a changing environment
  • Fluent language skills in German or French (word/script). Good knowledge of the other language at least level B2

Your possibilities – our offer

In the SZB we live diversity: bilingual colleagues from many different professions in the multicultural city of Biel/Bienne. We work together: a large interdisciplinary cooperation and a lot of mutual support. We continue to develop: further education opportunities and the opportunity to actively shape the future of the hospital center. In addition to the employment conditions according to our GAV, we offer you various discounts (pharmacy, public transport or leisure), a restaurant with favorable prices and an internal crèche.
