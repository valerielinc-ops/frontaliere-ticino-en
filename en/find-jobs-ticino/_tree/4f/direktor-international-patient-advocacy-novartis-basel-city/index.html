Salary Range: CHF137,900.00 - CHF256,100.00 Job Description Summary Director, International Patient Advocacy #LI-Hybrid Location: Basel, Switzerland Are you passionate about amplifying the patient voice and shaping healthcare systems at a global level? In this pivotal leadership role, you will drive the International Patient Advocacy strategy for a key Therapeutic Area, translating patient insights into impactful action that improves access, outcomes, and trust. Working at the heart of a highly matrixed organization, you will partner with senior leaders and external stakeholders to shape the environment, influence launch success, and embed innovative, data-driven approaches across the product lifecycle—ultimately making a meaningful difference for patients worldwide. Job Description Key Responsibilities

  • Lead execution of international patient advocacy strategy for the assigned Therapeutic Area, across pre-launch, launch, and in-market brands
  • Scale Patient Activation (IPEX) for priority brands, including early pre ‑ launch insight generation, AI ‑ based message testing, personalized content, and PESO activation models including oversight of country implementation
  • Partner with Public Affairs and Policy Communications to align advocacy with access and environment shaping priorities
  • Advise priority countries on advocacy plans, ensuring alignment with global frameworks and local adaptation
  • Build and manage strategic partnerships with patient organizations to strengthen external engagement and impact
  • Translate patient and AI-enabled insights into actionable strategies that improve access, outcomes, and launch readiness
  • Embed data-driven ways of working to monitor performance and anticipate external trends influencing decisions Essential Requirements
  • Bachelor’s degree in life sciences, public policy, communications, business, or related field
  • Extensive experience in patient advocacy, public affairs, access, or healthcare communications
  • Proven track record leading global advocacy strategies for launches or enterprise initiatives
  • Strong ability to build trusted relationships with patient organizations or external stakeholders
  • Experience working cross-functionally with commercial, medical, public affairs, and access teams
  • Demonstrated ability to operate effectively in highly regulated environments with governance and compliance requirements
  • Strong strategic thinking and data fluency with the ability to translate insights and complex information into actionable behavior change
