About Us Casale SA is a leading firm specializing in designing and implementing innovative plants to produce green ammonia, ammonia, nitrates, phosphates, urea, methanol, melamine, and syngas. With a solid commitment to sustainability and cutting-edge technology, we strive to deliver high-quality solutions to our clients globally. In this frame, the company is seeking a dynamic HSE Specialist.The offered position is within the QA/HSE Department. ## The Role In this role, you will be responsible for ensuring that all Health, Safety, and Environmental activities are managed in full compliance with applicable laws and in alignment with our internal HSE Management System. You will play a key role in driving the continuous improvement of our HSE practices, helping to refine and evolve our systems to meet the growing demands of our business and the environment in which we operate. Beyond compliance, we’re looking for someone who is passionate about building a strong safety culture. You will act as an ambassador for HSE, promoting awareness and proactively engaging employees at all levels to embed a culture of responsibility, prevention, and environmental stewardship throughout the organization. ## Key

  • Responsibilities - Develop HSE Policy, Procedures and Objectives in line with applicable laws and Company
  • Requirements and cascade them with the Company
  • Monitor the effective implementation of HSE requirements.
  • Ensure that all Company activities are subject to proper Risk Assessment
  • Identify HSE training needs, develop HSE training programs and carry out HSE trainings.
  • Ensure that Project HSE Plans and Procedures are developed and implemented throughout the entire project life
  • Verify that HSE personnel assigned to projects are suitably qualified by education, training and experience.
  • Provide technical support to Project and Site HSE teams.
  • Plan and perform HSE audits and inspections, both internal and toward subcontractors
  • Coordinate accident /incident investigation process
  • Manage safety and environmental statistics and perform performance analysis, identifying trends and suggesting improvements plans.
  • Prepare and perform internal /external presentation on HSE topics
  • Develop and promote safety and environmental campaigns to enhance Company culture and awareness. ✔️ Experience: Minimum 8+ years in a similar position in construction industry✔️ Soft skills:Flexibility, empathy, efficient communication, ability to work with interdisciplinary and international partners, problem solving and troubleshooting, risk management, good team player. ✔️ Qualification:Lead Auditor ISO 14001 / 45001 – NEBOSH (preferred)✔️ Language Skills: Fluent written and spoken English / Italian (Level C1) ✔️ Education:Master Degree in Engineering (Environmental, Energy, Civil,…)
