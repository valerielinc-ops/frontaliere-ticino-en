Your assignment

As an expert driver in possession of the SUVA M1, M2, M3, M6 and M7 licences, working in civil engineering yards, earth movement, public works and outdoor accommodation. Versatile and perfectly at ease in the driving of different construction machinery, you actively contribute to the realization of the work ensuring safety, precision, quality and productivity.

  • Drive and use different construction equipment corresponding to the categories SUVA M1, M2, M3, M6 and M7, in strict compliance with the safety standards and procedures in force.
  • Perform earth-moving, excavation, trenches, leveling, filling, loading and handling materials in accordance with executive projects.
  • Make precision adjustments, soil modeling and finishing work in compliance with altimetric quotas, gradients and defined tolerances.
  • Participate in the construction of civil engineering works, underground networks, pipelines, road infrastructure and external accommodation.
  • Adapt your working methods to the characteristics of the land, to the technical constraints and to the specific needs of each yard.
  • Operate precisely in proximity to existing networks and sensitive works while guaranteeing the safety of people and plants.
  • Ensure loading, handling and displacement of materials in order to optimize the proper work.
  • Check the quality of the works realized and ensure the perfect compliance with the executive projects and requirements of the construction site.
  • Perform daily checks, ordinary maintenance and the first level of machinery to ensure availability and performance.
  • Locate and report any technical abnormalities, failure or situation that may affect the safety or progress of the work.
  • To cooperate closely with leaders, managers of the work and teams on the field in order to ensure effective coordination and optimal execution of the operations.
  • Strictly apply the SUVA directives, internal procedures and all safety, quality and environmental protection standards.

And now we're talking about you?

  • You have one or more SUVA driver licenses (M1, M2, M3, M6 and/or M7). Any additional category is a real advantage.
  • You have gained significant experience in driving construction machinery, ideally acquired in civil engineering, earth movement or public works.
  • Versatile, self-contained and meticulous, you can handle different types of machinery and adapt to the needs of each construction site.
  • Demonstrate an excellent sense of responsibility and pay particular attention to the quality of the works performed.
  • Enjoy a good physical form and enjoy outdoor work in challenging contexts.
  • Strictly apply the SUVA directives as well as all safety standards and procedures in force.
  • In possession of the B-class driver's license and, ideally, a vehicle, you are mobile to intervene on different yards.
  • Fast availability and mobility in the regions of Lausanne and Geneva are a real advantage.
