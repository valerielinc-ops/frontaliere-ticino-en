at Energy Markets

You can expect a small architecture & security team at Energy Markets with a wide range of tasks at the interface between business and IT.

Your field of activity

  • You will recognize the need for architectural action at an early stage and develop the IT architecture in line with the requirements of the energy market.
  • As a process owner, you work with the stakeholders to develop and evaluate solution options until they are transferred to solution design.
  • You accompany agile projects and ensure an architecture-compliant implementation, also as a Scrum Product Owner.
  • In your role, you represent and synchronize IT architecture across domains, technologies and internal partners.
  • You ensure governance and security within the domain in collaboration with business, development, operations and group security.

Your competence

  • Several years of experience with complex IT and business architectures, ideally with commercial applications and customer solutions in the energy sector.
  • Good understanding of the requirements and developments of the energy market as well as strong customer and stakeholder orientation.
  • Extensive experience in agile projects and enjoyment of networking within the BKW Group.
  • Higher education in business administration, business informatics or a similar discipline.
  • Strong communication and presentation skills as well as negotiated German and English.
