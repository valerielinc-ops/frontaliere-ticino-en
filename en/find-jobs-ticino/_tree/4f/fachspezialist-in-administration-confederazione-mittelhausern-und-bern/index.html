Put to the Point

They assume a versatile function at the interface of finance, procurement, administration and logistics. With your independent and careful working methods, you ensure that central processes at our locations function reliably and efficiently.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20783
  • Location: Sensemattstrasse 293, 3147 Mittelhäuser

You can make this contribution

  • Financial administration with focus on debtor and creditor processes, invoicing and technical issues in SAP/S/4HANA
  • Coordinate and process procurement and ordering processes for the Mittelhäuser and Bern locations
  • Perform professional and organisational responsibility for reception at the Mittelhäuser site
  • Coordinate and ensure postal, goods and consignment logistics at Mittelhäuser
  • Organise events and coordinate administrative processes within the institute
  • Participate in projects and other cross-divisional administrative tasks

This makes them unique.

  • Basic commercial training with several years of professional experience in accounting, procurement and administration
  • Functional training or in-depth user knowledge in the field of finance, SAP or procurement
  • Very good knowledge of SAP/S/4HANA as well as experience in debtors and creditors
  • Experience in procurement, order and supplier management as well as strong organisational and coordination skills
  • Good active knowledge of two official languages and oral knowledge of English as well as driving licence category B
