Full description

Assistant secretary or assistant secretary (80%, one-year fixed-term appointment) in the administration and legal affairs unit of the cantonal police

Your tasks

  • You ensure the administrative work for the Office of Conduct of the Unit Administration and Legal Affairs and coordinate its action with the other units of the cantonal police You keep a watch on the files of the interpreters who work for the justice and the cantonal police of their registration for their accreditation
  • You ensure the updating of the files relating to the communal regulations for the whole canton via the computerized validation system You support the Head of Unit in the preparation of replies to parliamentary files and to federal and cantonal consultations You organize the digitisation, classification and archiving of the various structures of the unit You participate in the working groups related to the field of activity of the unit You provide the secretariat of the Legal Affairs Section, including the keeping of statistics, ensuring legal and disciplinary records, taking minutes, updating internal processes, preparing correspondence, sending letters
  • You support the other sections at the request of the Head of Unit

Your profile

  • SWC business employee or training deemed equivalent with experience in the legal environment Knowledge of the operation of the cantonal administration Police security skills would be an asset Ability to work as a team and autonomously Adaptability to change, good stress resistance and high workload
  • Administrative and editorial skills Mastery of common computer tools
  • Pro-active, conscientious and discreet behaviour sought French mother tongue with good knowledge of the second official language

Entry into office

  • and
  • January 2027 or to be agreed
