The EVAM (Vaudian institution for the reception of migrants) is a major player in the voodoo public service. With more than 1,000 committed collaborators and an annual budget of 360 million francs, we ensure the reception, support and integration of migrants in the canton of Vaud.

Every day, our teams work with openness and responsibility to address key social issues. Joining EVAM is putting its skills at the service of a human mission and of public utility, in a stimulating, diversified and constantly evolving professional environment.

What you bring to our mission

You guarantee fire safety at all EVAM sites by ensuring compliance with the AEAI requirements. You advise the various areas of the institution, guide quality assurance processes, conduct audits and contribute to the development of a true prevention culture among employees and beneficiaries.

Your key missions

  • You ensure compliance with the AEAI requirements on all buildings operated by EVAM.
  • You carry out regular audits, inspections and checks to ensure the compliance of installations and organisations with regard to fire protection.
  • You identify risks, propose corrective actions and accompany their implementation.
  • You are involved in construction, renovation and development projects to integrate fire protection requirements from the moment they are designed.
  • You develop, update and follow quality assurance concepts, response plans and safety procedures.
  • You organise and conduct training, evacuation exercises and awareness-raising activities for employees and beneficiaries.
  • You are the preferred interlocutor with ECA, authorities, relief services and institutional partners.
  • You analyze incidents, monitor indicators and contribute to the continuous improvement of security features.

Your profile

  • Technical or safety training (CFC or equivalent).
  • Federal Fire Safety Officer Certificate AEAI (mandatory).
  • Minimum 8 years of experience in an operational field related to fire protection, safety or protection of property and persons.
  • A complementary AEAI certification (or willingness to obtain it) is an asset.
  • Experience or ease in the animation of appreciated training.
  • Good control of common computer tools.
  • Driver's licence category B essential.
