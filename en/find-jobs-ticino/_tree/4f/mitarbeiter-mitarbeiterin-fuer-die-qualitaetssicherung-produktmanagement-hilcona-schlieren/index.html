Full description

You have an eye for detail, a sense of quality and joy in making food always better? GOU (by Hilcona) is an innovative manufacturer of ultra-fresh food in Schlieren ZH, where high-quality daily specialties are produced every day with a lot of commitment and craftsmanship. From now on or after agreement we are looking for an integrity and decision-making personality as an employee quality assurance and product management. In this versatile function, you ensure compliance with our standards in product safety, quality and legal requirements and contribute your expertise to the development of new products. You coordinate projects, evaluate new raw materials and maintain an exchange with our suppliers. With us, you take responsibility and actively shape the quality and further development of our products.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 80% - 100%
  • Working time model: Flexible time
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Hilcona Gourmet S.A., Ifangstrasse 9 8952 Schlieren

Tasks

  • Taking microbiological samples, carrying out environmental monitoring and sensory assessment of sales items in the context of end MHD tastings
  • Implementation of internal process audits, inspections and hygiene controls
  • Training and awareness raising of employees in the areas of quality, hygiene and food safety
  • Processing internal error messages and complaints, carrying out root cause analyses and initiating improvement measures
  • Search and evaluation of new raw materials according to requirements profile
  • Review and maintenance of specifications and article master data
  • Support and cooperation in the development team for innovations
  • Accompanying the introduction of new articles

Requirements

  • Completed training in the food industry, such as food technician HF, ideally with further training in the field of quality assurance or similar
  • High affinity for foods, their composition and application
  • Experience in the QS/QM of a food processing establishment
  • Experience in project management as well as an independent, structured working method
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, English and French skills are advantageous
  • High sense of responsibility, accuracy and communication
  • Experience with MS Office (especially Excel), experience with ERP systems is an advantage
