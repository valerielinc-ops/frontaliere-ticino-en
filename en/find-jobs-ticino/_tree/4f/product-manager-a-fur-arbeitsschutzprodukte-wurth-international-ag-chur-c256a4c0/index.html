Full description

Your duties:

  • They are responsible for and develop an assortment in the field of occupational safety – including products for hand, head, eyes, breath and face protection as well as for fall protection.
  • They observe the market, understand the needs of our customers and derive potential for existing and new products.
  • They accompany the development and introduction of new products – from the first idea to the market launch.
  • Together with internal and external partners, they ensure that our products comply with the Würth quality standards as well as the relevant legal and normative requirements.
  • They coordinate the required documentation while keeping an eye on quality, deadlines and cost-effectiveness.
  • Greater product launches Together with project teams from purchasing, marketing, sales and other specialist areas.
  • They support supplier negotiations, the creation of marketing and sales documents as well as product training.
  • You are available to our internal contacts as a competent point of contact for questions about your range.

This is what you bring with you:

  • They are interested in occupational safety and enjoy getting involved in technical, legal and application-related issues.
  • Ideally, they bring with them experience with occupational safety products – for example from the application, development, production, procurement or distribution. Another professional background with a high willingness to learn is also welcome.
  • Experience in product management, project management or in a comparable interface function is an advantage.
  • You enjoy working on the connection between procurement, product development and sales and have suitable training or training – or comparable practical experience.
  • They take responsibility, work in a structured manner and make decisions with a good eye for customer needs and economic contexts.
  • They like to tackle, actively drive forward topics and reliably complete tasks.
  • They enjoy working in a team and understand how to bring different interests together.
  • They communicate safely in German and English
  • Other language skills are a plus.

We offer this:

  • Corporate culture that lives the following values: optimistic, straightforward, dynamic, respectful
  • Our most important word in dealing with each other is THANK YOU
  • Flexible working hours and hybrid working models
  • Interesting training programs as well as 24/7 access to our e\-Learning platform
  • Occupational health management with a 24/7 employee counselling program
  • Above-average social benefits
  • Discount lunch offers
  • Sports and massage offer at very favorable conditions
