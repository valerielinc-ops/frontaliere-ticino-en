Since 1833, drive by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressed its inventiveness through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication.

YOUR MISSION

At the heart of our Integrated Manufacture, the flow that guides the management, monitoring and re-conformity of our industrial components requiring repair is a major lever of our operational and financial performance.

Today, this flow faces challenging challenges: heterogeneous processes according to the workshops, a lack of traceability of responsibilities (Manufacture vs Suppliers), a large administrative burden and too high stocks of spare parts. Your main objective will be to fully rethink this flow to simplify it, clarify decision-making governance and implement robust, dynamic and visual performance management. This major project will bring you a cross-cutting vision of our organization and develop your project management and change management skills.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT? •

Analyze the existing (Lean Diagnostic): To thoroughly audit the current repair process in the various workshops of the Manufacture, to map out inefficiencies (bottlenecks, low value added tasks, administrative burdens) and to identify the main areas of improvement.

Design the target stream: Define a harmonized global operating strategy and model the new target stream optimized, more fluid and transparent.

Clarify governance and responsibilities: Structure decision rules for the repair of components and define a clear and equitable framework for costing (internal responsibility Manufacturing vs. external supplier liability).

Deploy visual control (KPIs): Define and implement relevant key performance indicators (KPIs) and then set up visual animation routines to effectively track the status of spare parts stocks.

Support implementation: Translating optimizations into concrete actions on the ground, drafting new operational standards and accompanying teams (workshops, quality, logistics, procurement) in the adoption of these practices.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE US?

Training: Master's student in engineering school with specialization in Industrial Engineering, Operations Management, Continuous Improvement or Supply Chain.

Technical Skills: Strong theoretical background in Supply Chain and continuous improvement methodologies. Excellent ease with encrypted data, flow modelling and the creation of control tools.

Spirit of analysis: Structured, rigorous and pragmatic profile capable of translating complex issues into simple operational solutions.

Know-how: With an excellent relationship, you can listen and communicate diplomatically with a wide variety of professions (operators, buyers, logistics specialists, workshop managers). You have a great autonomy and are a force for proposals to challenge existing ones.

WHAT WE OFFER

Comprehensive management of a strategic continuous improvement project with high financial and human impact.

A unique opportunity to acquire a global vision of the logistical and industrial flows of a leading watchmaking Manufacture.

A collaborative, innovative and structuring framework for your future engineering career.

Community coaching to develop your change management skills.
