Job Summary

Use specialized machinery for industrial welding and oversee machines that perform the same job. Maintain and repair all machinery.   • Work closely and communicate clearly with the other departments, Weld personnel, Supervisor and Lead Man

  • Proactively engage and support company initiatives designed to promote a culture of safe working, environmental protection and continual improvement.
