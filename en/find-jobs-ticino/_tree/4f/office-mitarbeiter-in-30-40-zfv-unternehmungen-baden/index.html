Full description

The Power Inn restaurant offers space for 252 guests in the self-service area and has 86 seats on the terrace. Every day, between 500 and 600 people are served meals ranging from traditional to exotic. Standing lunches and aperitifs are also held. In keeping with the ZFV philosophy, fresh food is cooked on site using regional, ecologically responsibly produced and fairly traded ingredients. Are you our new team member who will support us as an office employee (30%) immediately or by agreement? You will work on an hourly wage basis with a workload of approx. 30-40% between Monday and Friday. Working hours are mainly during lunchtime, between 11:00 and 16:00. You are available flexibly to provide vacation cover. Your task menu:

  • Washing dishes, cutlery, and all kitchen utensils
  • Operating the dishwasher
  • Disposal and waste separation
  • Maintenance and care of the machines in the dishwashing area
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order in the work environment
  • Carrying out general tidying and cleaning work
  • Implementing and ensuring compliance with the quality standards and ZFV guidelines (hygiene and occupational safety, service, etc.) Your recipe for success:
  • Basic knowledge of the hospitality / hotel industry
  • Experience in a dishwashing area is an advantage
  • Spoken German skills
  • Ability to work in a team
  • Willingness to learn and resilience
  • Hygiene-conscious
  • Structured and independent What we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you can develop. We work together and focus on everything that is good for us. Among other things, we place great importance on salary transparency and equal pay. For this position, with a 100% workload, depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary of around CHF 52'000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
