Full description

Founded in 1913 in Italy, the Prada Group was built on a tradition of excellence and with a vision of innovation. The Group, a world leader in the luxury sector, operates in over 45 countries with the brands PRADA, Miu Miu, Versace, Church's, Car Shoe and Luna Rossa and employs over 100 nationalities.

SCOPE

The resource will be included in the Purchases Office, in particular in the team dedicated to tissue supply.

During the course, you will have the opportunity to join the company tutor and support it in the activities related to the management of purchases of raw materials.

RESPONSIBILITY

Support in the following activities

  • Research, selection and coding of raw materials
  • Issue of purchase orders
  • Insertion and management of order confirmations
  • Monitoring and prompting of delivery dates
  • Unlocking and verification of materials
  • Analysis and reporting activities
  • Correspondence management with suppliers and internal departments

REQUIREMENTS

It is preferable to have gained a first experience in the role

Good knowledge of the Office package, especially Excel

Accuracy, attention to detail and preparing for work with numbers

Ability to work under pressure and comply with close deadlines, collaborating with the company tutor and following the indications

Flexibility and dynamism

Excellent knowledge of the Italian language and good knowledge of the English language, both spoken and written

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
