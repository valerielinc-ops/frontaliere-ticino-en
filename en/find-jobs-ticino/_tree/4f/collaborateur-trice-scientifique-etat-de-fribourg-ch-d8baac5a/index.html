Who are we?

Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is an area of innovation and an important driver for the economic and cultural life of the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889.

The Master of Advanced Studies (MAS) in Health Psychology is a federally recognized post-graduate course (OFSP). It includes three Certificates of Advanced Studies (CAS), which can also be followed independently of MAS. Its mission is to train health psychologists capable of practising their profession under their own responsibility and leads to the Federal Title of Health Psychologist. It is delivered by the University of Fribourg (leadinghouse) in collaboration with the Universities of Lausanne and Geneva.

To ensure the follow-up and development of training, the Steering Committee (Prof. C. Martin Sölch, President) places the position of Training Coordinator, located at the University of Fribourg, in the competition.
