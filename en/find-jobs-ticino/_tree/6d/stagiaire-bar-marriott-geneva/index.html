As a Bar Intern, your main tasks will be

  • Welcome clients with courtesy and professionalism.
  • Assist barmen in the preparation and service of drinks.
  • Take orders and ensure their correct transmission.
  • Serve drinks and ensure customer satisfaction.
  • Participate in the establishment and resupply of the bar.
  • Maintain clean working spaces, equipment and equipment.
  • Perform storage and cleaning of tables and counter.
  • To comply with hygiene, food safety and internal procedures.
  • Participate in inventory inventories and inventory control under team supervision.
  • Discover and learn the techniques of mixology, service and standards of the establishment.
  • Contribute to sales promotion by offering bar offers and specialties.
  • Work with catering teams to ensure a quality customer experience.
