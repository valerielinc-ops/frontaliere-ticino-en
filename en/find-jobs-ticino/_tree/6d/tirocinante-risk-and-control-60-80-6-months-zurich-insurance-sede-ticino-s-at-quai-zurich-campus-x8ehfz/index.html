Full description

Interested in exploring the world of risk management? Curious about working in a stimulating and challenging environment of a multinational company? We are looking for a talent with a positive can-do attitude who is able to think "out of the box”.

Apply for our internship and support our Next Generation Talent Strategy. Key stakeholders are based in Zurich, Switzerland.

What you will do

  • Support Zurich Internal Control Integrated Framework (ICIF) across the organization
  • Collaborate with other team members and a risk community around the globe to identify best practices in documenting and assessing risks and controls in financial reporting and operational risk area
  • Collaborate with other team members to develop training content and communication material
  • Contribute to overall innovation activities
  • Hands-on work in operational day to day tasks related to Risk Management

What you bring

  • Current enrolment in a University/Institute of higher education for the duration of the internship, specifically in the field of business, accounting, finance, economics or information systems
  • A team-oriented individual and a strong communicator, able to work in a dynamic and everchanging environment and prioritize and balance multiple tasks simultaneously
  • Proactive, customer focused and responsible attitude
  • Strong skills of Microsoft standard software such as Excel and PowerPoint
  • Business fluent in English (written and spoken), other languages would be of advantage
  • You must be located in Switzerland and able to travel to the office for the duration of the role
