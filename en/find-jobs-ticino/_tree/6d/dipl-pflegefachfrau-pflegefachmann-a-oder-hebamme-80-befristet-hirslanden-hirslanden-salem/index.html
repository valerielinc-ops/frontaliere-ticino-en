Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Salem Hospital | Bern

Composition by: 01. January 2027 or by agreement

Type of employment: Temporary until 30 April 2027

Reference number: 75041

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

For our postpartum department with small neonatology and surgery area, 19 beds and about 830 births annually we are looking for a Dipl. Nurse or midwife as support for our interdisciplinary team.

Your Advantages in Us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in the fitness subscription & entrance to the indoor pool

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • You are responsible for an individual and family-centered care and accompaniment of newborn women and their newborns
  • You support and advise the parents in their start as a family, in the nutrition and care of the newborn and promote the mother-father-child relationship
  • The care of prematurely born and sick newborns from the age of 35. SSW in our small neonatology area is also one of your tasks, such as the holistic and individual care of patients with small gynecological and surgical procedures
  • In addition, you help to accompany the learners and students in their education
  • High quality of care, safety and customer satisfaction are important to us

Your profile

  • Completed training as a nurse (HF/FH) or Dipl. Midwife
  • Several years of experience in the field of postpartum bed and/or in the care of newborns
  • The holistic care as well as individual care and advice gives you pleasure and you are characterized by a high flexibility
  • Through your socially competent, committed and resilient personality, you are a win for our team
  • Good knowledge of German (min. B2) we presuppose

For additional information, please contact Regula Ingold, Head of Maternité under T. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by e-mail for data protection reasons. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
