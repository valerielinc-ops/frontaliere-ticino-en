Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Cooperation in the design of mediation and exhibition projects
  • Implementation of placement offers for school classes and adults
  • Assumption of administrative tasks and search tasks
  • Collaboration in museum operations and guest service (reception)
  • Organisation and organisation of events

What you bring

  • Master’s degree in History, MAS Cultural Studies or Pedagogy
  • Interest in the public-effective conveyance of historical content
  • Creativity, flexibility, organizational talent and team spirit
  • Open and committed personality with a high level of oral and written communication
  • Readiness for work on weekends

With around 300,000 visitors, Museum Aargau is one of the largest historical museums in Switzerland. At ten original locations you can experience history. In the castles Lenzburg, Hallwyl, Wildegg and Habsburg, in the monastery Königsfelden, on the monastery peninsula Wettingen, on the legionary path Vindonissa and in the Vindonissa Museum, on the IndustriekulTOUR Aabach and in the collection center Egliswil. Attractive offers invite you to unique journeys of discovery from antiquity to today. www.museumaargau.ch
