We make habitats worth living.

Shape the future of building technology with us! Innovation, sustainability and modern technologies inspire you? Then you are exactly right with us! You can expect a wide range of tasks in heating technology, a well-founded introduction to photovoltaics as well as exciting possibilities.

Your field of activity

  • As a ventilation system manufacturer, you work in the preparation and implementation of assembly work in commercial, industrial and residential construction as well as private customers. As a construction manager, you also take over the construction site organization and the coordination of the work.
  • You ensure a smooth process in the assembly team. In the building management function, you lead the team and dispose of materials and tools to match the construction progress and delivery times.
  • You mount and install ventilation systems according to plans and technical drawings.
  • You work sheet metal and other materials, install plant parts in the workshop and ensure the professional insulation of ventilation channels and pipes.
  • You comply with safety regulations and quality requirements. As a construction manager, you ensure their implementation in the team and keep the construction journal.
  • You will advise our customers expertly and assist with the acceptance of the equipment and the instruction of the users.

Your expertise

  • You have completed training as a ventilation system manufacturer: in EFZ. We are looking for specialists for assembly as well as professionals with experience in construction management. A further education as chief fitter or chief fitter is welcome, but not a requirement.
  • You bring good technical understanding, craftsmanship and spatial imagination and work carefully, efficiently and quality-consciously.
  • You are a team player with your own initiative and take responsibility for your work. For the building management function, you also bring organizational skills and joy in team leadership. Even if you want to develop in this direction, we look forward to your application.
  • You communicate with customers, suppliers and service partners in a friendly and knowledgeable manner, have good oral knowledge of German and have a category B driving licence.
