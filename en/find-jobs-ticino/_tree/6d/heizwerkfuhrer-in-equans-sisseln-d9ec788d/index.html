Full description

We are looking for you as a dedicated heating plant operator who takes responsibility and ensures that our thermal systems run safely, reliably and efficiently at all times. In this function, you work with modern steam, hot water, biomass and energy plants, monitor complex processes and contribute significantly to the high plant availability.

Tasks

  • You operate and monitor our steam, hot water, biomass and heat plants and ensure safe and stable operation
  • You detect deviations, carry out safety measurements and take the right steps in the event of faults.
  • Depending on your focus, you work with industrial thermal engineering plants or thermal waste recycling plants, including flue gas cleaning.
  • You regularly perform chemical analyses of boiler and feed water as well as firing control measurements.
  • You contribute to optimizing processes and ensuring consistently high plant availability.
  • You work shifts and appreciate the variety between routine and responsibility.
  • Tours and partial support in the operation of disposal are also part of the activities

Requirements

  • You have completed technical or craft training, e.g. as a mechatronician, mechanic, electrician, locksmith or heating installer.
  • You have experience in the operation of thermal systems or would like to develop further in this field.
  • Additional training as a heating plant guide is an advantage or you have the motivation to acquire it.
  • You work reliably, flexibly and responsibly and observe safety regulations consistently.
  • You feel comfortable in shift operations and keep an overview even in challenging situations.
  • An exciting, responsible task with a lot of creative freedom
  • An experienced team with an open corporate culture
  • Contemporary employment conditions and good social benefits

We specialize in holistic facilities management services for every phase of the business real estate and area lifecycle. Buildings and infrastructures are complex systems. Whether technical operation, strategic planning or infrastructural services – we ensure maximum availability, increase operational reliability and improve comfort in buildings. In this way, we minimize costs and create optimal conditions so that our customers can concentrate on their core business.

As a company of Equans Switzerland, the leading full-service provider for holistic building and energy solutions in Switzerland, we are also driving the transformation and digitalization of our customers and working together on the energy transition.
