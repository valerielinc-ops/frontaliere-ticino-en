Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

You want to be used in various areas of the women’s clinic and determine for yourself when you work. Are you looking for employment on an hourly basis? Then our Pool MPA is just right for you! The Pool MPA in the Biel Hospital Center offers flexible working conditions for medical practice assistants. This flexibility facilitates the reconciliation of professional life with non-professional activities and projects. In addition, it opens up the opportunity to gain professional experience and develop specific competencies in various disciplinesThe work assignments take place at the following locations: -Gynaecological Ambulatorium, Hospital Center Biel -Gynaecology, MEDIN, Bahnhof Biel -Gynaecology Schüpfen, Medi Zentrum Schüpfen

Medical / Practical Assistant / Women's Clinic Pool

You do

  • Reception and care of the patients of the three locations
  • Support of doctors for a smooth running of the consultation hours
  • Operating the telephone
  • Assistance in consultation hours
  • Simple laboratory activities
  • Administrative tasks

This sets you apart

  • Completed training as a medical practice assistant (mandatory for foreign diplomas SRC recognition)
  • Professional experience in an ambulatory or medical practice in Switzerland is absolutely necessary
  • Secure IT application knowledge (e.g. MS Office, KISIM, etc.)
  • High social competence and personal responsibility as well as patient orientation
  • Teamwork, flexibility, openness and enjoyment of human contact as well as resilience
  • Availability, versatility and flexibility
  • Native language: German or French, with knowledge of the second language (level B2 required)

You will benefit from this

  • Meaningful work for high patient satisfaction on the pulse of the regional population
  • Working in different teams
  • Flexible working conditions that facilitate the reconciliation of professional life and non-professional activities and projects
  • regular working hours,
  • Various attractive benefits
