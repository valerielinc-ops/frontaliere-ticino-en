"Quenching thirst together" – this is our mission. Join us and become part of one of the largest Swiss beverage producers. In a down-to-earth environment with flat hierarchies, where personal responsibility is highly valued and collaboration is actively practiced, we are looking for additional support.

Jasmin Strotz HR Business Partner +41 58 434 41 53

These tasks will inspire you

  • You produce high-quality products in accordance with our high production and quality standards
  • You operate, monitor and control our modern production facilities
  • You ensure a smooth production process and respond to deviations independently and with a solution-oriented approach
  • You reliably record and check the flows of goods and materials in the SAP system
  • You ensure that our quality, hygiene and safety regulations are consistently complied with
  • You consistently implement our quality management requirements in your daily work
  • You actively participate in the continuous improvement process (CIP)
  • You contribute your ideas and expertise and thereby help continuously optimize our production processes
  • With your careful approach to work, you make an important contribution to ensuring consistently high product quality

We look forward to applicants with this profile

  • You have completed training as a brewer, dairy technologist, food technologist or equivalent
  • training in the food, beverage or production sector
  • Ideally, you have experience operating and monitoring production or process systems
  • You have a good technical understanding and enjoy working with modern production systems
  • You work independently, reliably and responsibly
  • You have a strong awareness of quality, hygiene and safety
  • You are willing to work in a 3-shift operation

Discover your benefits

  • Training and further education: We invest in the development of our employees. A broad range of internal educational opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external further education.
  • Attractive holiday arrangements: Our holiday policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of holiday. As of 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks, and from the 60. Year of age even 7 weeks. In addition, it is possible to purchase additional vacation days.
  • Family time: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent enjoys 3 weeks of family leave at full pay.
  • Insurance benefits: We protect our employees with above-average social security and pension benefits and offer special conditions on supplementary insurance with various health insurers for their entire families.
  • Benefits and discounts: Among other things, our employees benefit from discounts when shopping and refuelling, credits for REKA money, and discounted mobile subscriptions for themselves and their families.
