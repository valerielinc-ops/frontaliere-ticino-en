Full description

Technical solutions are developed and implemented with innovative 3D CAD models. A structured exchange with production and customers ensures smooth project processes and high-quality results. For our client, we are looking for an activity

  • Development and construction of components, assemblies and plants in 3D CAD
  • Creation of production, assembly and detailed drawings
  • Accompanying projects from the concept phase to implementation
  • Technical coordination with production, project management and customers
  • Optimization of existing designs with regard to function, quality and manufacturability
  • Support in technical clarifications and finding solutions
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a constructor, technician HF or comparable qualification
  • At least 10 years of professional experience in metal or mechanical engineering
  • Very good knowledge in SolidWorks 3D
  • Experience in the construction of machines, plants or metal construction components
  • Structured and independent working methods
  • Strong technical understanding and solution-oriented thinking
  • Team-capable and responsible personality Offering
