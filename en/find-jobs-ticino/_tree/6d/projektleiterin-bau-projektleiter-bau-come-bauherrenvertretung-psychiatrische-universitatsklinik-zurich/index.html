Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 100% In order to strengthen our construction project management team, we are looking for a committed and team-oriented personality as soon as possible. Their tasks

  • Independent management, organization, coordination and supervision of construction projects through phases 31 – 53 according to SIA 112
  • Consideration of the strategic requirements of the hospital council and the management
  • Assumption of the client function vis-à-vis planners, external companies and other stakeholders
  • Development of decision-making bases for the various internal and external committees / project participants
  • Ensuring a transparent flow of information in time for the necessary decisions
  • Representation of requirements by construction (construction project and object management, medical technology access control, security, etc.), users, operation and ICT

Your profile

  • Completed training in architecture, civil engineering or construction project management at university / university of applied sciences level (ETH/FH) or a similar degree
  • Sound knowledge and practical experience in construction, process and project management
  • Experience in client management of complex building construction projects
  • Knowledge of healthcare and public procurement services and support processes
  • Communication strong and team capable personality with commitment and assertiveness
  • Connects thinking and solution-oriented with a strong quality awareness

Our offer

  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Space for ideas: High personal responsibility and design possibilities
  • Strong in the team: appreciative cooperation and mutual support
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • More advantages for you: Discounts and discounts at numerous partner companies
