Full description

Tasks

  • collaborates with the inspector/trice in the management of the inspectorate ensuring equity of action on the whole territory of the surrounding area according to cantonal indications
  • exercises the psychopedagogical and organizational direction of the regional pedagogical support group ensuring coordination and ensuring relations with other school operators, specialist services and external bodies. The management of the regional group can be carried out in collaboration with another group leader or independently depending on the internal organization of the service and/or according to the indications of the Section
  • decides on admission/admission to/from the service of pupils and students
  • assists teachers and operators by monitoring their work and exercising supervision over the activities of members of the group by means of visits and personal interviews
  • (at the request of the inspector) the decisions of the inspectorates and the directorates of the institution concerning pupils and students followed by the service
  • supervises or performs maturity tests
  • takes care personally if necessary, cases requiring special interventions, cares for relations with families and reports to other agencies or services the situations that arise from the competences of the pedagogical support service
  • performs administrative practices related to the activities of support teachers, logopaedists, psychomotricians and other professional figures
  • collaborates on the promotion of the study plan as well as the implementation of the innovation projects of the compulsory school

Requirements

  • Comprehensive academic training (bachelor and master or license) in the field of education or psychology
  • in-depth knowledge of the Ticino and Swiss educational system, with particular focus on childhood school and elementary school
  • multiannual experience and recognized in the management and management of groups
  • Language skills: native language or excellent mastery of Italian. Knowledge (conversation and editing) of German and/or French
  • strong decision-making skills, mediation, negotiation and interpersonal communication
  • strong attitude to teamwork, listening and managing relationships
  • high spirit of initiative, operational autonomy, planning, design and management of administrative processes
  • high sense of responsibility, flexibility and ability to manage stress and priorities even in dynamic or high-work environments

Special remarks

  • degree of employment required by 80%

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

28 October 2026
