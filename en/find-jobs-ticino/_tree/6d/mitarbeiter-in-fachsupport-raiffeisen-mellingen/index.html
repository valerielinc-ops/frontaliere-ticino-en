Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Aare-Reuss, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Mellingen, Lupfig, Schinznach Dorf, Wildegg and Rupperswil, we are looking for a personality as an employee* in specialist support.

What awaits you?

  • Ensuring compliance with the applicable regulations in the areas of VSB, cross-border and tax compliance and carrying out the associated control tasks
  • Responsible for checking the conformity of the opening formalities of new customers and investment products
  • Ongoing monitoring of data quality and placing of the associated cleanup orders
  • Support of the compliance officer in various control tasks, preparation of reporting and general administrative tasks
  • Co-support in the area of securities/trading settlement and management of bank-owned trading assets
  • Further development of internal processes in your own area of responsibility
  • Information provision to internal and external bodies as well as training for employees
  • Assistance in cash management of offices

What do you bring?

  • Completed vocational training as a businesswoman/merchant
  • Independent, exact and responsible work
  • Service-oriented thinking and action
  • High quality awareness
  • Raiffeisen experience is beneficial
