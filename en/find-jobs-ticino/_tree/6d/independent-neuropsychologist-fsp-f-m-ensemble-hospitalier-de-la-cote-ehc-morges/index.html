Full description

Located in the charming municipality of Lavigny, in the heart of the canton of Vaud, the Pietro Sarto Medical Centre aims to improve the care and follow-up of children with neurological disorders, development and behaviour. By working in synergy with various healthcare professionals, we provide comprehensive and personalized care.

We are looking for a

Neuropsychologist FSP at 20-100%

We have the possibility to offer you several formats of collaboration (employment contract or self-employment)

Positioned in January 2027.

WE OFFER YOU

  • The opportunity to become the actor of an expanding stimulating institution, capable of undertaking.
  • A position in a human-sized establishment with a working atmosphere that values exchange, benevolence, proximity and teamwork.
  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of redeeming an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (child care, unpaid leave, part-time, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • A mobility plan including participation in your cycling or basketball trip or in your public transport subscription, with a view to the environment following an institutional sustainability road map.
  • Sports activities (Pilates, walking races, discounts at various centres such as several fitness centres or Bains de Lavey), events for the children of collaborators, a company drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Hot drinks offered in the morning and meals at CHF 8.50 in our cafeterias.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Internal transfer facilitated according to your desire for development.
  • Many in-house or certifying training courses available to you.
  • A programme dedicated to managers to help them acquire the skills needed to manage their teams.

YOU MAY

  • A master's degree in Psychology with specialization in neuropsychology
  • A title of psychologist specialist in neuropsychology FSP
  • A clinical experience of at least 3 years in child and adolescent neuropsychology
  • Dynamic, flexible and independent
  • A natural pro active, open and you have at heart to offer a care for the paediatric patient based on benevolence and respect
  • Rigor and good management of priorities allowing you to cope with an ever-changing activity
  • A good team spirit and the desire to evolve in an environment with collegiality at heart

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Conduct neuropsychological assessments of children in outpatients at the Medical Centre and prepare reports
  • Perform neuropsychological care for children
  • Working in a multidisciplinary team
  • Develop your professional network around the Centre's missions
  • Provide administrative management of ongoing management and writing of neuropsychology reports
  • Provide supervision sessions

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
