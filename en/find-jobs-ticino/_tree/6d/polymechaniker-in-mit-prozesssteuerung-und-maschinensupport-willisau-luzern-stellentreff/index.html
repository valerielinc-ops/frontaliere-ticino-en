Full description

Responsible work at modern production facilities awaits you. The company attaches importance to quality and a good working environment with attractive social benefits. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Ensure smooth operation of the allocated production facilities
  • Management of the respective machine group in compliance with the specified objectives
  • Responsible for compliance with production management and quality requirements
  • Execution of maintenance, service, repair work including preventive maintenance work
  • Assistance in projects in the engine room and in the development of solutions for improving machine performance
  • Co-responsible for training and support of employees at the facilities

Requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship as a polymechanic or similar training
  • Professional experience in production engineering and/or maintenance
  • Experience in the field of process control and automation, hydraulic and pneumatic knowledge are advantageous
  • Good German skills in word and writing, oral English skills are advantageous
  • Readiness to work in shift operation is required (early shift rotating from 05.30 to 2 p.m., late shift from 14.00 to 22.30 as well as night shift from 22.30 to 5.30 p.m.)

Offer

  • 40-hour week, at least 5 weeks holiday
  • Canteen
  • Super Benefits and Social Services
  • Fitness room and sports courses
  • Very good compensation
