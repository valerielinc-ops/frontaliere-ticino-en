Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the place we love to be to life together with us. Here you make the difference

  • With style and consulting expertise, you accompany our customers through the range – personally, competently and occasionally.
  • You put together outfits, give styling tips and make sure that customers feel understood and inspired.
  • Building long-term customer relationships is just as much a part of your everyday life as actively promoting our customer card program.
  • You actively contribute high-quality presentations, visual merchandising and your own ideas – always with an eye for details.
  • In the team, you work independently, support your colleagues and visibly contribute to the success of the area.

That draws you out

  • A completed training in the retail trade and several years of experience in the fashion sector form your basis.
  • You are familiar with brands, materials and trends – and work with a high standard of style, service and consulting culture.
  • Joy in customer contact, clarity in appearance and respect in dealing with your advice.
  • You bring reliability, initiative and teamwork – and actively use it in everyday work.
  • Very good German, good knowledge of English and commitment from Monday to Saturday you bring with you.

BENEFITS FOR YOU Together with around 1,700 other detail lovers, you are an important part of our fascinating premium and luxury world. You experience real appreciation, work in a great team and we trust you so that you can use space and make a difference with your skills. If you understand your craft and bring a large portion of passion, then many career doors are open to you. We are happy to support you with internal but also external training and further education.

Also, look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment with flat hierarchies in the heart of Bern.
  • Benefit-based premiums and attractive social benefits.
  • Flexible working time models and at least 5 weeks holidays (PS: You get more free time thanks to the option "unpaid holiday" - from 7 to 365 days).
  • up to 50% discount on products from the globe world for you and your family.
  • Much more...
