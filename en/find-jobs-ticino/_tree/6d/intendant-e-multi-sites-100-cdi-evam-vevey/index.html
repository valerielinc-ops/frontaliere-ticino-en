Full description

EVAM (Etablissement vaudois de l'accueil des migrants) is a major player in the voodoo public service. With more than 1,000 employees and an annual budget of 360 million francs, we welcome, support and integrate migrants in the canton of Vaud.

Every day, our teams work with openness and responsibility to address key social issues. Joining EVAM means putting its skills at the service of a human mission and of public utility, in a stimulating, diversified and constantly evolving professional environment.

As part of a start, we recruit an e : Intendant.e multi-site (100%) CDI

[Salary class: 4](https://www.evam.ch/app/uploads/2026/06/Bareme-salarial 2026.pdf) (This scale refers to validated prerequisites, failing which the classification is at grade level -1)

Location : Multi-sites (Vevey, Territet, Villeneuve and Aigle). Entry into office: 01 December 2026 ideally

What you bring to our mission

You are responsible for the maintenance, cleanliness and maintenance of premises and buildings, in particular by helping to clean and tidy up common spaces, both indoor and outdoor.

The word of your future leader, Pascal

" I prefer management based on trust, respect and direct exchanges. My goal is to provide a clear framework while promoting autonomy and accountability.

The intendant trade is above all a field trade, versatile and essential to the proper functioning of our buildings and to the quality of the living environment of those welcomed. It requires organization, responsiveness and ability to manage unforeseen events.

By joining our team, you will find listening management, a clear framework and the opportunity to leverage your ideas and skills. The aim is to build lasting collaboration, serving the quality of our services. »

Your key missions

  • Maintain indoor and outdoor spaces, maintain facilities and monitor breakdowns.
  • Organize sorting, recycling and waste management.
  • Perform various administrative tasks, manage orders and track maintenance product stocks.
  • Perform periodic inspections, including fire protection.
  • Maintain good relations with partners, neighbourhoods and collaborators, and respond to requests by directing them where necessary.
  • Ensure the flow of information useful to all trades, linked to the hierarchy.

Your profile

  • SWC operating agent or in a technical field useful to the function.
  • Minimum 2 years experience in building technical management and cleaning.
  • Driver's licence, category B.
  • Experience in inventory management and orders.
  • Basic safety knowledge (including fire).
  • Very good knowledge of technical installations.
  • Ability to work independently, organizational sense and versatility.
  • Excellent relational skills and taste for teamwork.
  • Good command of French (oral and written), and computer tools.

What EVAM proposes

  • A committed working environment
  • A policy of continuing training to evolve in your career
  • Telework policy
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 from 50 years)
  • Very good social benefits
  • Meaningful projects in a stimulating intercultural context

The three-step recruitment process

  1. Interview with the manager and the recruitment officer.
  2. Situation with the person in charge and the recruitment officer.
  3. A moment of immersion to discover your future working environment.

At the end of the process: request for an extract from the criminal record, special extract and certificate from the prosecution office (undocumented documents required).
