Full description

Pelco SA, an electrical and automation engineering company located in Collombey\-Muraz, Valais, is looking for an(\-e) electrical planner(\-tre)\(\-ne).

Profile desired

  • Experienced SWC planners, or training deemed equivalent
  • Electrical engineering experience, ideally in construction and industry

Your skills

  • Positive, open, creative, evolutionary, conscientious and collaborative spirit
  • Curiosity for new technologies
  • Imagination, rigor, accuracy and reliability
  • Writing capacity, ability to synthesize and communication ease
  • A sense of responsibility and organization

Key Responsibilities and Tasks

  • Study, design and scale high current and low current electrical installations
  • Develop developed schemes
  • Develop plans, one-off diagrams and synoptics
  • Edit calls for tenders, analyse bids and propose tenders
  • Write technical reports
  • Conducting metres and on-site surveys
  • Monitoring and coordinating business work
  • Organize and perform table receptions in workshop
  • Organize and perform on-site facility receptions

Competencies and Requirements

  • Knowledge of electricity standards and standards.
  • Mastery of CAD/DAO tools (AutoCAD, Eplan, Winprogitel etc.).
  • Methodical spirit, precision and autonomy in work.

We offer

  • A motivating and varied activity within a young and dynamic team
  • Continuing training courses
  • Responsibilities and salary adapted to your skills

Occupancy rate: 80 to 100%, to be agreed

Date of entry: to be agreed
