Full description

The technical platform of our endoscopy center has the mission of providing high-quality care in collaboration with the various services of Hôpital de La Tour. With a high-level technical platform, many diagnostic, follow-up and interventional examinations are performed. In order to complete our service, we are looking for a: Duties

  • Ensure all administrative tasks of the reference service.
  • Welcome patients.
  • Make appointments and organize the doctor's agenda.
  • Organize examinations and tests.
  • Update the service's files and databases.
  • Manage incoming and outgoing correspondence.
  • File, classify.
  • Print reports.
  • Invoice consultations in Tardoc and in private tariff on Opale.
  • Requirements
  • Excellent mastery of medical secretarial work.
  • Holder of a medical secretary diploma is essential.
  • Experience in Switzerland is essential.
  • Good computer skills.
  • Perfect mastery of French and English (ideal).
  • Mastery of MS Office tools, knowledge of Carefolio & Opale is a real asset.
  • Good understanding of the billing system and the Swiss healthcare system in outpatient and private settings.
  • Dynamism, autonomy and ability to organize quickly and effectively.
  • Discretion and versatility, good stress resistance.
  • Good patient contact and sense of service.
  • Excellent presentation.
  • Available quickly.
  • What we offer
  • Contract type: Fixed-term contract of 3 months
  • Percentage: 85%
  • Start date: As soon as possible
