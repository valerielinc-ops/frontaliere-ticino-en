PROJECTIVE/IN AVOR, on site - Swatch Group

PROJECTIVE/IN AVOR, on site Your future employer Through their competence, passion and commitment, our employees contribute significantly to the success of the Swiss Made watches, movements and components produced by ETA SA Manufacture Horlogère Suisse. Our company belongs to the Swatch Group, the world’s largest watch group with 16 renowned brands and numerous production companies, which are highly specialized in both watchmaking and other industrial sectors. The Swatch business unit is responsible for the development, production, assembly and delivery of watch heads for the Swatch and Flik Flak brands. At our plant 18 in Grenchen, state-of-the-art technologies are used to produce the most diverse dials of these attractive products.
