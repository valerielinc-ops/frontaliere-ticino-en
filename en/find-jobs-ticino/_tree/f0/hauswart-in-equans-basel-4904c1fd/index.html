Full description

Do you want to take responsibility and make a real difference? Then you are exactly right with us!

You can move that with us.

In this versatile function, you manage an object on site and are the contact person for our customers.

Your tasks include

  • Maintenance and supervision of building installations
  • Repair and maintenance work
  • Coordination and control of service and maintenance work (heating, ventilation, air conditioning, sanitation)
  • Cooperation with external service providers and coordination of different service providers
  • Execution of administrative tasks as part of your activity
  • Pikett inserts

That's what makes you.

You live service and work with conviction for quality and reliability. You bring with you:

  • Completed technical training (ideal in the HVAC area) or basic crafts training with further training in building management
  • Several years of proven professional experience in the field of home maintenance
  • Independent, structured working method and sense of responsibility
  • Strong service mentality and organisational talent
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as good PC knowledge (digital working)
  • Valid driving licence Cat B and residence in the Basel/environment region

That's what we offer you.

  • An exciting, responsible task with plenty of room for manoeuvre.
  • An experienced team with an open corporate culture.
  • Contemporary employment conditions and good social benefits.
