Head: in E-Business & Loyalty Platforms

Coop Cooperative — Basel, BS, Svizzera

  • Job opening: by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Computer Science
  • Location: Coop, Thiersteinerallee 12, 4053 Basel
  • Pensum: 100%

Tasks

  • Leadership and further development of the Customer Processes division with the teams E-Commerce, Internet/Intranet and CRM/projects with around 45 employees
  • Overall responsibility for the strategic and operational development of customer-oriented IT solutions, platforms and processes
  • Responsibility for project portfolio, prioritization, budget and management of complex projects in terms of deadlines, quality, functionality and costs
  • Further development of central digital platforms of the Coop Group in the areas of e-commerce, CRM/Loyalty and content management
  • Managing the solution architecture with a focus on scalable, modular and sustainable systems as well as ensuring cooperation with internal and external stakeholders
  • Participation in strategic committees and active further development of agile working methods

Requirements

  • Completed studies in computer science, business informatics or a comparable education, ideally with further training in IT management
  • Several years of leadership and management experience in an IT or digitally shaped organization
  • Sound experience in IT project, portfolio and stakeholder management
  • Very good knowledge of e-commerce, CRM, web and CMS-related system landscapes
  • Experience in architectural topics, interface management and cross-divisional cooperation
  • Strong social skills, a secure appearance and a structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High resilience, determination and a very good understanding of the business impact of technological and organizational decisions
  • Perks: 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop Group, participation in the subscription, perks for cultural events, perks for leisure activities
  • Conditions of employment: Pension fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, Attractive employment conditions and certified equal pay, savings plan at the pension fund
  • Working hours: 6 weeks holidays
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription, in-house sports offers
