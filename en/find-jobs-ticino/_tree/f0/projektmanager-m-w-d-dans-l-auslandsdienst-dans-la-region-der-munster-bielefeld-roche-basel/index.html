The position Your team As an application specialist (m/w/d) in the field of field services in the Münster / Bielefeld / Dortmund region, you will be part of Roche Diagnostics Deutschland, a world leader in the fields of pharmaceutical and diagnostics. We develop and distribute state-of-the-art diagnostic systems for laboratories and clinics. Your task is to ensure, with passion and know-how on site, that our systems run reliably, that our customers are supported and that they experience excellent service.

  • Responsibilities You're expecting As an application specialist (m/w/d) in the field, you are the face of Roche on site and thus contribute significantly to customer satisfaction. You perform your tasks both in direct customer contact and in close coordination with your team:
  • Comprehensive customer support and training: within Roche system lines (pathology and/or molecular diagnostics/PCR) with the focus on application, operation and follow-up.
  • Ensuring system availability and quality of results at the customer.
  • Own-responsible analysis and correction of errors at application level in close cooperation with internal expert:inside.
  • Processing customer requests (on site and by telephone) with a focus on the optimal solution finding.
  • Application technical advice for customers:in order to optimally use the systems.
  • Active support of the sales team and regional system management with expertise and application knowledge.
  • Implementation of system installations, commissioning and software modifications of selected systems as well as documentation of applications in the CRM system.
  • Qualifications | That brings you
  • Complete training as MTA/BTA with corresponding professional experience or scientific studies (Bachelor or Master degree).
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in the diagnostic laboratory or as an application specialist:in, ideally focusing on pathology and/or molecular diagnostics (PCR).
  • Good application and quality assurance knowledge of diagnostic analysis systems as well as deepened knowledge in human diagnosis.
  • IT basic knowledge (networks) and safe handling of MS Office/Google Suite; ideally CRM experience.
  • High solution-finding competence, methodological approach to tricky problems and the ability to understand complex content.
  • Embossed customer orientation, team skills, communication strength as well as a result-oriented and independent working method.
  • Flowing German and good English skills (approx. B1/B2 level) to understand technical documentation and to exchange you with the team.
  • For the optimal support of the region, you have a valid driver's license and ideally live in the Münster / Bielefeld / Dortmund region and bring a high readiness to travel to our customers: inside the region.
