Consultant/Customer Center

  • Sion
  • 60%

Are you interested in answering customer inquiries, advising customers by phone, email and chat while ensuring a high quality of service? Our Customer Center is looking for a customer advisor!

What does that mean in concrete terms?

  • They systematically identify the customers, provide information, communicate the conditions and information about the various products and services and use the sales potential.
  • They share information necessary to update IT data regarding customer service and market management.
  • They advise by phone about basic products/services (accounts, cards, e-banking) and provide initial information about other banking products.
  • They accompany and support our customers with our digital products and thus create added value for their customer experience.
  • You plan the appointments with our consultants.

Do you like challenges? This job is as created for you if...

  • You have a commercial education (EFZ, MPC, ESC, Maturity type E).
  • You have experience in a customer center and excellent knowledge of office communication tools.
  • You communicate efficiently in German and French. Other languages are beneficial.
  • You are a pragmatic and disciplined person who makes constructive suggestions for improvement and is adroit in telephone contact.
  • They value teamwork and work effortlessly with colleagues.

Then we should get to know each other! Do you want to be part of our team? We look forward to your application. Please send your complete application documents to the address: recrutement@bcvs.ch .

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed.
