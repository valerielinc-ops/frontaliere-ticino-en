Full description

Mission

The photovoltaic research laboratory (PVlab) specializes in the manufacture and reliability of solar cells and panels. To strengthen its team, the PVlab is looking for a motivated technician or engineer to support research in renewable energy. As a member of the technical team, you will work to support and develop the laboratory's scientific equipment and contribute to the development of new photovoltaic technologies.

Main tasks and responsibilities

Within our team, you will provide technical support to various projects. Your responsibilities will include:

  • Technical support to the group's employees
  • Maintenance of scientific equipment (PVD, PECVD, etc.)
  • Equipment fault analysis and correction
  • Maintenance of laboratory infrastructure (special gases, DI water)
  • Development of research equipment

Profile

You are a motivated, reliable and curious professional who appreciates learning new techniques and working in a collaborative research team. You take a critical approach to your work and are able to work independently once trained.

You must have the following qualifications

  • Training in electronics, automation or mechanics and knowledge in other fields
  • A level of ES technician or HES engineer training
  • Ability to organize your work, manage multiple tasks independently
  • English mastery, level B2 or higher

Experience or knowledge in one or more of the following areas would be an asset:

  • Vacuum technology
  • PLC Programming
  • High frequencies (HF and VHF)

Training in the above-mentioned techniques will be offered in the context of taking up office. We attach particular importance to motivation, reliability, curiosity, team spirit and the willingness to actively contribute to laboratory activities.

We offer

At EPFL, we are convinced that everyone can accomplish great things when he or she evolves in a caring, respectful and successful environment. By joining our institution, you will help shape the future of society through education, research and innovation.

  • Excellent working conditions within an international, multidisciplinary and state-of-the-art team, with access to high-level infrastructure
  • A young, dynamic, flexible and stimulating research group
  • Competitive pay and social benefits
  • A rich cultural environment, continuing training courses, access to these sports infrastructures, a half-tariff SBB subscription.

Beyond your professional activity, our campuses welcome a vibrant community within a unique framework, driven by many initiatives to promote equality, inclusion, diversity, well-being and sustainability. Throughout the year, a varied program of cultural, sports and community activities also strengthens campus life.
