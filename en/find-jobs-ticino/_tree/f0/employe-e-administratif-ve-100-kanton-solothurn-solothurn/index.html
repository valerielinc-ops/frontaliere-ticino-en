Full description

The Richteramt Dorneck-Thierstein is the first judicial authority in the office. For the Civil and Criminal Department, we are looking for a 100% clerk at the Dornach site as of 01.02.2027 or by agreement until 31.07.2027.

Your responsibility

  • They process the incoming mail, open court dossiers and write orders and general correspondence.
  • They make appointments and set up court hearings.
  • You are also responsible for sending all correspondence, in particular decisions, judgments and certificates of legal force, as well as case accounting and archiving.
  • They shall carry out checks on time limits.
  • On the telephone and counter you provide information and serve the reception.

Your profile

  • We expect a commercial degree, if possible with experience in civil and/or criminal law (in particular procedural law).
  • They have good IT knowledge, like to work independently and take responsibility.
  • Legal issues interest you.
  • They are skillful in oral expression and appreciate contact with customers and authorities.
  • An efficient working method, stylish German as well as flexibility, resilience and teamwork round off your profile.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
