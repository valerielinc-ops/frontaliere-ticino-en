What if your professional history starts at the BCV? Training is at the heart of the priorities of the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud. Starting his BCV career is having all the cards in hand to build his professional future For our Private Banking division, we are looking for one or more:

EHL intern in Bachelor's degree - Private Banking Your missions :

  • support advisors in administrative management and client portfolio development,
  • offer wealth management solutions and a comprehensive heritage approach, aiming at optimising the profitability of customer portfolios both qualitatively and quantitatively,
  • processing mortgage credit files on behalf of clients,
  • Participation in compliance processes (KYC, AML, LSFin)

  • Bachelor EHL,
  • strong interest in banking,
  • knowledge of markets and finance,
  • dynamism, proactivity, reliability and relational ease,
  • mastery of common computer tools,
  • good knowledge of English and German.
