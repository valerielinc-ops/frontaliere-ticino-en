Full description

What you do

  • In close cooperation with qualified nurses, you will make an important contribution to competency-based and holistic patient care.
  • You actively shape your everyday work and carry out nursing and medical tasks professionally.
  • You will carry out the nursing process according to the latest findings, including performance recording of materials and care, and document your activities in KISIM.
  • You will also be responsible for everyday support as well as logistical, administrative and housekeeping tasks.
  • As a shift manager, you will also take on responsibility for the organisation and smooth running of the shift. This will require your organisational skills, initiative and overview.

What sets you apart

  • You are a health specialist EFZ, ideally with experience in the acute care sector.
  • You focus on our patients and treat them with respect and empathy.
  • You remain flexible even in changing situations and want to develop further.
  • You enjoy working in a team, take on personal responsibility and have a sense of quality.
  • As an organizational talent, you keep track of things even in hectic situations.
  • Good knowledge of German rounds off your profile.
