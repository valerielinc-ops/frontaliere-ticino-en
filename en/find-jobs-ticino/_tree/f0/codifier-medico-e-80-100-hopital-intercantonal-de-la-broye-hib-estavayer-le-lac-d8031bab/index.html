Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. His ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges that lie ahead at the Payerne and Estavayer-le-Lac site, HIB is looking for a medical codifier 80% - 100% Your mission Within the Medical Coding Unit, attached to the Finance Department, you actively contribute to the quality, reliability and value of the institution's medico-administrative data. Thanks to your expertise, you are involved in ensuring accurate coding of hospitalizations in accordance with the directives of the Federal Statistical Office (FOS) and the current classifications. Your tasks:

  • Ensure medical coding of hospital stays according to SwissDRG and ST-REHA classifications.
  • Analyze and interpret clinical data to ensure reliable, consistent and consistent coding in accordance with regulatory requirements.
  • Collect, monitor and validate information required for quality medical and administrative documentation.
  • Work with medical, health and administrative teams to ensure the accuracy of coded data.
  • Manages exchanges with insurance companies and monitors applications related to medical coding.
  • Participate in continuous process improvement and support cross-cutting service activities as required. Your assets
  • Medical coding training.
  • Bachelor of nursing or equivalent training.
  • Federal patent of medical coding specialist, an asset.
  • Experience in medical coding or coding, an advantage.
  • Good knowledge of anatomy, physiology, pathology and diagnostic and therapeutic procedures.
  • Analytic spirit, rigour and organizational sense.
  • Ability to work independently while fostering collaboration.
  • Experience with common computer and office tools. We offer you
  • A stimulating position within a committed, collaborative and passionate team.
  • A human-sized working environment that promotes proximity, autonomy and the quality of exchanges.
  • Opportunity to evolve in a dynamic institution, oriented towards innovation and continuous improvement.
  • Telework opportunities that promote flexibility and work-life balance.
  • Attractive conditions of engagement and quality social benefits.
  • A workplace in Estavayer-le-Lac. Entry into office: 1 January 2027 or to be agreed Thank you for sending us your current application form by applying online.
