You can look forward to that.

Many exciting challenges await you in a railway production environment. As an HOF specialist (Human and Organizational Factors), you actively contribute to the development of processes, organizations and applications. You bring the perspective of the users in a complex environment and are committed to an optimal interaction of people, technology and organizations – with the aim of sustainably promoting the safety, efficiency and performance of the work system. You ensure a systematic and demonstrable integration of HOF in all phases of change and development processes. You can move that.

  • You advise and accompany different projects from a user-centred perspective and work in interdisciplinary teams on new processes, applications and organizational changes.
  • You work closely with various business partners and support them in the analysis and definition of requirements for people, technology and organization – always with a holistic view.
  • Project leaders, epic owners, business owners and executives advise and empower with regard to HOF and actively contribute to the anchoring of the HOF mindset in the organization.
  • In projects, you recognize needs in the area of education, communication and change and derive concrete recommendations for action.
  • You create HOF proof for the approval of functions / systems by the Federal Office of Transport (BAV), implement topics according to agile methods, document, visualize and present your results.

You bring that with you.

  • You have already accompanied development processes – from vision development to concrete implementation and can derive targeted requirements and recommendations using HOF methods.
  • You enjoy working interdisciplinary and conceptually, quickly recognize connections and dependencies and think holistically.
  • You bring a high degree of personal responsibility, initiative and independence as well as customer orientation, have assertiveness and a structured way of working.
  • You have a pronounced ability to communicate and conflict and can communicate appropriately and convincingly.
  • Ideally, you will bring experience in the agile working environment, move safely in dynamic structures and have the willingness to systematically acquire railway-specific know-how.
  • Completed training in human and organizational factors or work and organizational psychology, at least at university level, ideally with additional training in security and risk management or organizational development and transformation round off your profile.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This position is in requirement level J, regional allowance level 1.
