Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) provides a mission of acute care and rehabilitation. His ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges ahead, HIB is looking for:

Medical secretary in orthopaedics at 60%

Your Mission

  • Welcoming and guiding patients with professionalism and kindness
  • Announce patient arrival to physician
  • Manage appointments via Ultragenda software and organize the schedule of consultations and interventions
  • Coordinate orthopaedic, pre-hospital and surgical procedures
  • Prepare, update and archive patient medical records
  • Conduct invoicing monitoring of consultation and administrative follow-up (miscellaneous mail, mail, medical documents, on-call lists)
  • Preparation of reports and operating protocols
  • Collaborate with medical and paramedical teams to ensure fluid and quality management
  • Participate in office automation equipment management

Your assets

  • SWC of a commercial employee or equivalent supplemented by a medical secretary degree or SWC of a medical assistant
  • Prior experience in an orthopaedic secretariat
  • Excellent mastery of computer tools (Outlook, Word) and ideally Opale, Soarian, Ultragenda software
  • Good knowledge of medical terminology and administrative procedures in hospitals
  • Organizational sense, rigour, priority management and good stress resistance
  • Team spirit, flexibility and strict respect for confidentiality

Start: immediately or to be agreed.
