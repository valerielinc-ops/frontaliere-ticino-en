SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. You play a key role in the overall coordination and optimization of ongoing railway production in Switzerland. If terms such as disruption management, customer orientation, collaboration and decision-making competence are not foreign words for you, then you fit perfectly into our team! Do you also have a passion for tackling challenges and finding pragmatic solutions? Then we look forward to meeting you! You can move that.

  • As Network Manager:in guides and controls the overall operational situation of current rail traffic on the SBB network. In doing so, you analyze and assess the development of the situation in a forward-looking manner and proactively influence rail production.
  • You work closely with other control centres, railway companies and infrastructure operators at home and abroad.
  • For questions about traffic control and disruptions, you decide throughout Switzerland.
  • Within the SBB you inform verbally and in writing about current disruptions and events.
  • You also moderate briefings and agreements with various stakeholders.
  • In projects and interdisciplinary working groups, you represent production-specific interests.

You bring that with you.

  • Your communication strength, resilience and fast comprehension are complemented by your assertiveness.
  • A high customer orientation characterizes you, and you stick with your solution-oriented way of working.
  • You have several years of scheduling experience from an operations centre or control centre of a railway company and have very good knowledge of the regulations, processes and operational processes in railway production.
  • Working in shifts and taking on irregular working hours at night and on weekends gives you pleasure.
  • You have German (minimum C1) and knowledge of French and Italian, or you are ready to acquire this knowledge up to level B1.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
