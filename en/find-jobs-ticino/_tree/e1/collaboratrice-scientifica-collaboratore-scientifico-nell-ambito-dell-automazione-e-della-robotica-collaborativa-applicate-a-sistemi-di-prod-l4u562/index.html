Scientific Collaborator in the field of automation and collaborative robotics applied to human-centred production systems”

The University of Applied Sciences and Arts of Southern Switzerland (SUPSI) is advertising a position as Scientific Collaborator in the field of automation and collaborative robotics applied to human-centred production systems” , at the

Institute of Systems and Technologies for Sustainable Production (ISTePS) of the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI), based in Lugano. Employment rate of 100%. Start date 1 December 2026 or a date to be agreed.

Responsibilities and activities

  • Devote a significant portion of the work (approximately 50%) to the development of the DemoStART demonstrator, a platform of the SPS laboratory dedicated to collaborative robotics and human-centred production systems, currently under development and also used in the Bachelor's degree in Management Engineering. The activity includes the design, implementation, updating and maintenance of the demonstrator, as well as demonstration activities for industrial, academic and institutional partners.
  • Carry out applied research, experimental development and demonstrator development activities in collaborative robotics and automation for human-centred production systems, with particular reference to human-robot collaboration (HRC), human-robot interaction (HRI) and digital twins of production systems, including prototyping, conducting experiments, and collecting and analysing data, based on objectives defined with the team.
  • Contribute to the technical development of European and national research projects, particularly in software development and systems integration, in collaboration with team members and under their supervision.
  • Manage the integration of sensors, actuators and communication systems between devices.
  • Develop software for the integration, programming and control of robotic and automated systems, particularly in Python and/or C/C++, also using programming and simulation environments such as RoboDK.
  • Contribute to the testing of vision systems, data acquisition and sensor technology, as well as the use of tools for systems integration and orchestration (e.g. Node-RED or equivalents) and middleware for data transmission (e.g. MQTT).
  • Explore the use of artificial intelligence and human-machine interfaces applied to collaborative robotics and human-centred production systems.
  • Support the dissemination of results through the drafting of scientific articles, presentations at conferences and technology-transfer activities aimed at local companies.
  • Occasionally support teaching activities and the supervision of students (Bachelor's/Master's theses), in line with the needs of the laboratory.

Requirements

  • University education (Master's degree or equivalent) in robotics, mechatronics, automation, electronics, computer science, mechanical engineering, management engineering or related disciplines.
  • Experience in research activities (including experience gained during a Master's thesis), preferably acquired within a university research group or a research and development center.
  • Scientific publications and/or contributions to conferences in fields such as robotics, automation, mechatronics, production systems or related disciplines will be considered an advantage.
  • Good programming skills in Python and/or C/C++ and the ability to develop software for the integration and control of robotic and automated systems.
  • Experience or a strong interest in programming industrial and/or collaborative robots and their related programming and simulation environments, such as RoboDK or equivalent tools.
  • Familiarity with some of the technologies underpinning the laboratory's activities is considered an advantage: automation and systems integration (sensors, actuators, communication between devices), vision systems and data acquisition, orchestration tools and middleware for data transmission (e.g. Node-RED, MQTT), digital twin (demonstrator and robotics activities); machine learning, deep learning, generative AI and agentic systems (applied research); Java, containerization (Docker), relational or NoSQL databases (software development and project activities).
  • Interest in research activities in the areas of collaborative robotics, HRC, HRI, robot programming and human-centred production systems.
  • Strong independence in learning and using new technologies, including those not previously known, and the ability to independently complete the entire experimental cycle: from design and simulation to implementation, programming, integration and validation of a system.
  • Ability to work effectively in small teams and multicultural contexts, completing assigned tasks without the need for continuous supervision. Ability to create experimental prototypes, with willingness to use (including learning it quickly) CAD software for designing supports and basic mechanical interfaces, and 3D printing.
  • Excellent command of English and proficiency in a national language. Knowledge of a second national language is considered an advantage.

What we offer

  • A fixed-term contract for a maximum duration of 2 years, with the possibility of renewal.
  • A stimulating work environment in an applied research team active in national and European projects, with access to experimental infrastructure and industrial use cases.
  • The opportunity to contribute to the development of new lines of research and to technology transfer to local companies in the context of Industry 4.0/5.0 and Circular Economy.
  • Opportunities for professional growth through progressive responsibilities in project activities, publications and the acquisition of competitive funding.

  • La familiarità con alcune delle tecnologie alla base delle attività del laboratorio costituisce titolo preferenziale: automazione e integrazione di sistemi (sensori, attuatori, comunicazione tra dispositivi), sistemi di visione e acquisizione dati, strumenti di orchestrazione e middleware per la trasmissione dei dati (ad es.
