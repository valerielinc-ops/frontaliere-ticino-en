Full description

Location: Vers-chez-les-Blanc, Switzerland Work model: On site Contract type / duration: Internship – 6 months Target start date: February 2027

Be a force for good and transform your potential into something extraordinary.

As a Creative Food Lab Intern at Nestlé Research, your mission is to help imagine, develop and bring to life new food concepts that connect culinary creativity with scientific insight. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with scientific teams and food innovation specialists to create prototypes, support internal events and contribute fresh ideas to product development. This is your opportunity to make an impact while gaining hands on experience and mentorship to kick-start your career.

How you’ll make an impact as a Creative Food Lab Intern

  • Creating innovative food concepts and prototypes that translate scientific ideas into engaging culinary experiences.
  • Collaborating with scientific teams to explore how research can shape meaningful product development opportunities.
  • Preparing culinary prototypes and product demonstrations for internal events, tastings and innovation discussions.
  • Contributing fresh ideas and practical solutions while learning how innovation is developed in a scientific environment.
  • Presenting your concepts with clarity and confidence, adapting your message to different audiences and project needs.

What will make you successful

  • Current student or fresh graduate in culinary creation, food innovation or another relevant field, with a strong interest in how research supports new product ideas.
  • Being proactive, curious and open to innovation, with the ability to generate ideas and turn them into tangible concepts.
  • Having resilience, flexibility, agility and patience to develop products in a collaborative environment where ideas evolve over time.
  • Communicating fluently in English
  • French is an asset.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning.
