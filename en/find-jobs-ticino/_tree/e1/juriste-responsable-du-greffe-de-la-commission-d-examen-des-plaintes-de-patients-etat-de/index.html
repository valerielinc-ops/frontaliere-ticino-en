Full description

According to the Public Health Act (PHA), the Commission for the Review of Complaints of Patients, Residents or Users of Health and Socio-Educational Institutions (COP) is mandated to ensure respect for the rights of patients and residents and to deal with complaints about the handling of human rights violations by health professionals and health institutions or institutions. As the head of the COP registry, your mission is to ensure that the registry is managed and organised to ensure the judicial functioning of the COP.

  • Ensuring the control of the registry in order to ensure the judicial functioning of the registry
  • Define the organisation of the registry, processes, specifications and working rules
  • Develop and ensure administrative simplification and optimization of registry processes and tools
  • Conduct analysis, document, consolidate and synthesize completed files for Commission members
  • Draft notice, decisions, letters and determinations
  • Assisting members of the Commission in the processing of cases
  • Ensuring legal compliance of projects submitted to the committee
  • Assisting members of the Commission in the processing of cases
  • Conduct legal research, draft legal opinions, participate in working groups
  • Represent the Registry with internal and external partners. For more information, please consult the Terms of Reference by clicking here or contact by email Céline Amy, Human Resources Advisor: Celine.amy@vd.ch .
  • Master in law or equivalent
  • Training in health law
  • Patent of lawyer desired
  • Management training and experience in team management
  • Professional experience where possible in a relevant registry, lawyer's study or field of law
  • Excellent analytical and synthesis capabilities
  • Excellent editorial skills
  • Good oral skills: ability to popularize technical information
  • Accountability and ethics of the public service
  • Adaptability and flexibility
  • Good listening and communication In order to have all the necessary information to analyse your file, please send us a letter of motivation, as well as your work certificates and diplomas, in a single pdf file.
