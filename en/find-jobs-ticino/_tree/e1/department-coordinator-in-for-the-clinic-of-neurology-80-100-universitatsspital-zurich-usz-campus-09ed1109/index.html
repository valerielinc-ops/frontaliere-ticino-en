Full description

You keep track when it runs smoothly and especially when it becomes challenging? Communication is your strength and you want to actively contribute to relieving the burden of everyday care? Then you are exactly right with us.
To strengthen our team in neurology, we are looking for a committed, responsible personality for departmental coordination.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • You are the central hub of our department and ensure a smooth daily routine. • You coordinate processes, plan ahead and keep an overview even in a dynamic environment. • You communicate professionally with the patient: your relatives and internal and external contacts. • Even in challenging situations, you remain calm, set priorities and find pragmatic solutions. • You receive our patients warmly and ensure a pleasant arrival and an appreciative farewell. • You support your team if necessary by taking on nursing tasks and thus promote good cooperation. Requirements • You have completed training as a medical practice assistant:in, health specialist or pharmaceutical assistant:in. • You like to organize, work in a structured way and keep track of several tasks at the same time. • You communicate openly, friendly and professionally. • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing. • The handling of digital work equipment and administrative systems is a matter of course for you. • You like to work in a team, take responsibility and actively contribute your ideas. We offer: With us, you can expect a versatile task in a challenging university environment. Together we are committed every day to high-quality patient care and good interprofessional cooperation. You become part of a motivated team that supports each other and counts on each other. In addition, we offer you a broad learning and development environment with numerous opportunities to develop yourself professionally and personally and to contribute your ideas. Do you want to work in an exciting medical environment, take responsibility and make a difference? We look forward to your application.
