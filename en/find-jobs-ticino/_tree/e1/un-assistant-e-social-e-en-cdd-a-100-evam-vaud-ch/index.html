Full description

Join EVAM and make sense of your expertise!

What if your next professional challenge has a real social impact? At EVAM, each employee contributes to an essential mission: to accompany people in situations of migration with openness and responsibility.

As part of a replacement, we recruit

A Social Assistant in 100% CDD

[Salary class](https://www.evam.ch/app/uploads/2026/06/Bareme-salarial 2026.pdf): 7(This scale refers to validated prerequisites, failing which classification is at grade level -1)

Place : Lausanne Entry into office : To agree

The word of your future leader, Cecile

" For several years, our structure has been accompanying migrants in their journey of integration, with humanity, professionalism and commitment.

Each day is different: social and administrative support, access to rights, coordination with our partners... rich and meaningful work.

You will join a multidisciplinary and supportive team, where mutual assistance and experience sharing are essential. We are looking for a motivated person, willing to commit to the and respect for human dignity.

To the pleasure of getting to know you".

Your key missions

  • You are in charge of administrative monitoring and social interviews.
  • You set up a custom action plan.
  • You support beneficiaries in social, professional and administrative integration.
  • You coordinate with internal and external partners.
  • You take care to bring expertise and advice to the cantonal social counter to guarantee quality support

Your profile

  • You have Bachelor HES in social work (recognition SEFRI if foreign diploma).
  • Minimum. 3 years of experience in a similar function.
  • You have a good knowledge of the Vaud network and social insurance.
  • You have an excellent Frenchman (oral and written); Mastering English or other languages is an asset
  • You have a strong interest in migration.

What EVAM proposes

  • A committed working environment
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 from 50 years)
  • Very good social benefits
  • Meaningful projects in a stimulating intercultural context

The three-step recruitment process

  1. An interview with the antenna manager and the recruitment officer.
  1. Situation
  1. Half a day of immersion to discover your future working environment.

At the end of the process: request for an extract of the criminal record (virgin documents required).

Do you recognize yourself in this mission? We look forward to discovering your candidacy and building together a solidarity future.
