Physiotherapist (20-100%) We attach importance to a holistic, cause-oriented physiotherapeutic treatment. The well-being of our patients and our team is our priority. Are you looking for a varied job, a nice place to work and excellent working conditions? Then you are exactly right with us.

What to expect

  • A bright, spacious and newly renovated practice with closed therapy rooms, including MTT training area and fitness center (complete new equipment park)
  • A mixed, experienced and established team
  • Competent, flexible and committed practice management (incl. full-time secretary)
  • Versatile and varied field of application
  • Good and close cooperation with doctors
  • Close team meetings and internal training
  • Further education support both financially and temporally (flexible and individual solutions also for more extensive training)
  • Individual, flexible working hours
  • in Swiss comparison very generous remuneration and 5 weeks holidays
  • Gifts of seniority in the form of wages or holidays
  • If desired, physiotherapeutic work in retirement and disabled homes is also possible

What we expect

  • Committed and resilient personality with pleasure in the profession
  • Completed training as a physiotherapist (Diplom, BSc. or MSc.)
  • For foreign diplomas optimal with SRC recognition or partial decision
  • Enthusiasm and innovation for new challenges
  • Patient-oriented and active work
  • Readiness to continue to train
  • Conscientious administrative work
  • A team player whose harmony in the workplace is important
  • Also welcome deeper knowledge in manual lymph drainage, pelvic floor and dizziness therapy (not necessarily necessary)
