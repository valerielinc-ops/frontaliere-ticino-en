Full description

Are you familiar with pipeline and plant construction, do you like to work practically and at the same time take responsibility for coordination? In this versatile function, you are the important interface between external service providers, our technical team and production.

You can do that.

  • The work of external employees in pipeline and plant construction is coordinated and monitored reliably and ensures professional execution.
  • You measure pipelines, prepare them and independently carry out practical TIG welding as well as repair and locksmith work on our plants.
  • In the support and maintenance of our media supply, including for ice water, water, softened water, compressed air, steam, condensate and chemical media, you bring your expertise.
  • You keep the various media and their pipeline networks up-to-date and ensure that changes are documented cleanly and comprehensibly. You also see possible optimizations.
  • You coordinate tank inspections and pipeline insulation as well as smaller projects and support the commissioning of pipelines and plants.
  • In close cooperation with mechanics, electrical engineering and production, you ensure safe processes and consistently implement the requirements for occupational safety in your area.

You bring that with you

  • Completed technical vocational training and maintenance experience in the food or pharmaceutical industry form a good basis for this task.
  • A further training as a maintenance specialist or process specialist with federal law. Professional card is an advantage.
  • In TIG welding you bring practical experience as well as a valid test according to EN ISO 9606-1 for the welding process 141 / material group FM5.
  • You are familiar with the handling of various supply media and you recognize optimization possibilities in the pipeline network thanks to your experience.
  • An independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working characterizes you and the coordination of different work lies with you.
  • Interest in your personal and professional development, good knowledge of German and a secure handling of MS Office round off your profile.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Sabbatical & Holiday Shopping: Give yourself unpaid holidays or extend your holiday entitlement by up to two additional weeks.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on your business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Individual development plan: With tailor-made development plans, we specifically promote your individual skills and support your long-term growth – a central component of our sustainability strategy. Learn more .
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
