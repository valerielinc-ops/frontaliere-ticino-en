Full description

The activity includes the repair and optimization of injection molding tools. The company offers a demanding working environment with close cooperation for technical support of production. You benefit from great personal responsibility and good development opportunities. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • repairing, optimizing and converting injection moulding tools
  • manufacturing spare parts and coordinating repairs
  • technically supporting production in fault analysis
  • maintaining tools, storing them and keeping them ready for use
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a polymechanic or comparable
  • Experience in mould construction and metalworking
  • Knowledge in the production, assembly and maintenance of injection moulding tools
  • Self-contained, structured and reliable working method Offer
  • Versatile activity with high personal responsibility
  • Demanding
  • Tasks related to toolmaking and maintenance - Collaboration with production and project management - Modern technical environment with development possibilities
