Coop Genossenschaft — Lausanne, VD, Svizzera

  • Entry into office: to be agreed

  • Place of work: Lausanne, St-François

Mission

  • Welcome and advise our clients with friendliness and competence, mainly at the stationery department
  • Make the store attractive and manage the assortment with care
  • Ensure control and implementation of the goods according to Coop presentation concepts
  • Manage orders, tracking and inventory rotation
  • Cash purchases and participate actively in sales promotion

Qualifications

  • You like to work with others and communicate your passion to the team and customers every day
  • You benefit from a professional degree in sales and have very good knowledge in the field of stationery
  • You are a smiling person with great ease in contacts. You are looking for a varied and versatile job. You have a sense of responsibility, are dynamic, organized and effective on the ground
  • You have a command of French, both in writing and oral.
  • You are of good physical constitution and handling is no problem

What We Offer

  • Discounts and discounts: 5 times more superpoints in supermarkets, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop group, mobile and prepaid subscriptions at a reduced price at Coop Mobile, discount on the Reka Card, discount to the staff also granted to the partner (living under the same roof), discount voucher valid on a general private subscription, discounted admissions to leisure activities
  • Conditions of employment: pension fund: taken over by Coop of 2/3 of contributions, attractive conditions of employment and guaranteed equal pay, collective labour agreement (CCT), 18 weeks of paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, seniority premiums
  • Working time: 5 weeks of vacation, flexible time models
  • Continuing training: Over 600 internal training courses
  • Sustainable development: A commitment to man and animals and nature with more than 400 acts
  • Communication: Internal app dedicated to staff
  • Health: Update-Fitness: Fr. 200.– discount on annual subscription, sports activities offered by the company
