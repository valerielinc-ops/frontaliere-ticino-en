Portrait The school Ersigen-Oesch, a rural, manageable school with two locations: Ersigen and Niederösch At the Ersigen location, where the position is advertised, approximately 160 children are taught in 8 classes by 20 teachers. They work in a great team and benefit from an excellent infrastructure. Ersigen can be reached from Bern in 20 minutes by car but also well by public transport.

Tasks

Due to the traineeship of the teacher, the position is advertised.

The lessons are 9 lessons and the lessons take place on Tuesday.

Subjects of teaching

07:25 - 08:10: Media and computer science 08:15 - 09:50: Visual design 10:10 - 11:45: Nature and technology

13:30 - 15:05: Textile designs 15:20 - 17:05: Movement and sport

From August: Tuesday afternoon falls away

Requirements

Are you motivated to commit to the 789R?

Have you completed your training as a teacher with Focus Z3 or are you completing it next year? Are you committed, open and proactive?

Are you familiar with the digital media (smartboard, visualizer, iPad, etc.) and like to use it in class?

If you were able to answer these questions with "Yes", I would be very happy about your application!

We offer

  • A great, motivated class
  • Established, helpful team

  • Very good infrastructure
  • Modern concept media and computer science (work with office, escola, smartboards, ipads, visualizer)
  • School supplements such as school social work and day school
