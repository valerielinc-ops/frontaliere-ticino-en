The opportunity

As an Employee Lifecycle Management Specialist - German speaking you are part of a dynamic, vivid and spiritful Organizational Management and Compensation Team supporting our German affiliates. The Organizational Management and Compensation Team delivers customer service and advisory support to HR and business partners on global HR systems and processes, ensuring end-to-end process completion in line with our customer-focused principles.

Please note that this is a fixed-term opportunity, until December 31, 2028.

Your main responsibilities will be

  • Providing customer service on HR related activities, such as inquiry resolution, request management, and troubleshooting in a proactive and customer focused way

  • You will strongly collaborate with the compensation and payroll teams on different topics
  • You might prepare reports and analytics to support team dynamics and decision making as well as advice if needed
