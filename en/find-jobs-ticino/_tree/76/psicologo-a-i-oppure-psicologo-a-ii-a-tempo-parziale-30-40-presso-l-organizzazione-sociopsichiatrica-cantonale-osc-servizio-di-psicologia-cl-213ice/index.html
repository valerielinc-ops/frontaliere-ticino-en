Full description

Duties

  • carry out psychodiagnostic and psychotherapeutic indication activities aimed at assessing psychological functioning and to guide the intake of the patient(s)
  • carry out psychological and/or psychotherapeutic support activities both individually and in groups, where necessary in collaboration with physicians, nurses, educators, social workers and social workers, for patients, following the therapeutic plan and implementing it in accordance with his or her professional competencies
  • being responsible for the preparation of reports on cases followed or peritiated
  • ensuring the updating of medical records and other registration and/or statistical tasks required as well as the proper compilation of services and the management of the billing process
  • providing counseling with regard to his or her specific competencies, at agencies, institutions and facilities, state parastatal and private after favorable notice from the head of the Psychology Service and/or the head of the team in which he/she is placed
  • update his/her professional knowledge in compliance with federal and cantonal regulations for the maintenance of the specialty title
  • is responsible for the training and supervision of psychologists and psychotherapists in training and other personnel
  • participate in team meetings in order to ensure shared multidisciplinary care and operates within joint working tables contributing to the development of interprofessional / intersectoral / interdepartmental projects
  • participate in initiatives related to the field of mental health in order to develop new preventive or rehabilitative measures and/or subject to authorization of the Directorate may carry out research activities ##
  • Requirements
  • diploma or license or bachelor+master's degree in psychology obtained at a Swiss university or, if obtained at a foreign university, federal recognition of the diploma issued by the Commission of Psychological Professions PsiCo (pursuant to Art. 2 and 3 of the Federal Law on Psychological Professions (LPPsi))
  • authorization in the canton of Ticino to practice as a psychologist active in the health care field (see details particular observation)
  • authorization in the canton of Ticino to practice as a psychotherapist (see details particular observation)
  • experience in the field of eating disorders (more than 3 years) may be an asset
  • ability to work in multidisciplinary teams, proactive attitude, ability to adapt, empathy and interpersonal skills
  • possess adequate psycho-aptitude profile for the practice of the profession
  • knowledge of the territory, social services and the socio-health network of Canton Ticino
  • perfect command of the Italian language and knowledge of another official spoken and written language ## Special remarks Candidates with psychologist title:
  • applicants with a title of psychologist with recognition issued by the Commission of Psychological Professions (PsiCo) in the case of foreign qualifications but not yet in possession of authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice as an active psychologist in the health care field, are granted 6 months from the decision of assignment to obtain the same
  • applicants with a title of psychologist obtained at a foreign university, but not yet in possession of recognition by the Commission of Psychological Professions (PsiCo), are granted 6 months from the decision of assignment to obtain the same together with the authorization in the Canton of Ticino to the practice of the profession of psychologist active in health care
  • the actual start of the activity of the successful candidate is subject to obtaining the authorization in the Canton of Ticino to the practice of the profession of psychologist active in health care
  • failure to obtain the requested requirements within 6 months of the decision of appointment will result in its automatic forfeiture
  • within 4 years of the start of activity, it is necessary to obtain authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice as a psychotherapist
  • during this period, employment will take place in the form of appointment (art. 15 and 16 LORD) commutable in appointment upon obtaining authorization in the Canton of Ticino to practice as a psychotherapist
  • failure to do so will result in the permanent termination of the employment relationship Candidates with a psychotherapist title:
