About us

High quality fresh chocolate – this is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company employs more than 2,200 people from more than 80 countries across the group, and is now increasingly active. The company's headquarters is located in the canton of Glarus. In front of the picturesque alpine scenery, we produce our fresh chocolates here and develop new innovative ideas in the teams of the Alain.

The "Retail" division comprises over 250 chocolateries in over 30 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialities and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you want to participate in this? Then come to us.

Tasks

  • You advise our customers and sell our diverse range of goods, for example in the selection of gifts
  • You actively engage our customers, recognize their needs and offer suitable solutions
  • You take care of the cashier processes and ensure precise and correct billing
  • You present the exquisite confiserie and chocolate assortment in a tasteful and inviting way
  • You also take over production activities as well as the filling and maintenance of the sales areas
  • You set our high quality standards with dedication and

Care for - You consistently pay attention to compliance with the hygiene and freshness concept - You work hand in hand with your team and contribute to a positive working environment

Requirements

  • You have professional experience in retail and are enthusiastic about sales and direct customer contact - You quickly recognize customer needs and advise friendly, individual and convincing - You have very good German and English skills
  • French and other languages are of benefit
  • You work responsibly, reliably and quality oriented and consistently adhere to hygiene and cleanliness standards
  • You are a team player, you are happy to take responsibility and want to develop yourself in sales and service
  • You are flexible and

ready for weekend and public holidays
