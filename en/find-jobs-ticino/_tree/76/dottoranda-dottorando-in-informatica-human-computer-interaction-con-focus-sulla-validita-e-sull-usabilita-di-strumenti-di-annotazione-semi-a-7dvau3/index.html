# PhD / PhD in Computer Science (Human-Computer Interaction), with focus on the validity and usability of semi-automatized annotation tools for clinical and therapeutic contexts

The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI), Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI) and Department of Education and Learning / High Educational School (DFA/ASP),

Educational, school and society skills centre (CC BESS)

, puts in competition at the DFA/ASP of Locarno a position of

PhD or PhD

. Degree of employment 100% and entry provided for on 1 December 2026 or date to be agreed. We are looking for an interested candidate or a candidate interested in performing a

PhD in Computer Science (Human-Computer Interaction)

on the validity and usability of human-in-the-loop interfaces for therapists and clinicians, within a multimodal analysis platform of child behaviour .

Professional background

The doctorate will be supervised/or by Prof. Angela Pasqualotto (Co-responsible Center for Educational Needs, School and Society BESS, SUPSI DFA/ASP,- Locarno) and Prof. Monica Landoni (Educational Science Faculty, USI - Lugano). The position is part of the SNSF SEMANTIC project (SEmi-automated Multimodal ANalysis Toolkit for Individualized Characterization), jointly conducted by DTI and DFA/ ASP with the collaboration of clinical and socio-sanitary institutions in Switzerland and Italy. SEMANTIC develops an open source platform that integrates multimodal signals to produce semi-automated notes of child behaviour in therapeutic contexts. The annotations generated by the models are presented to professionals through an interface, where they can be validated and correct according to a human-in-the-loop approach. The quality and usability of this interface are decisive for the adoption of the platform by non-computer users and constitute the subject of the PhD.

Shipments

The selected person will be involved in all phases of the project. In particular, he will have to deal with the design, prototyping and iterative evaluation of the interface addressed to professionals, in co-design with therapists and clinical partners; the analysis of the requirements and workflows of annotation and correction; the conduct of usability studies in real context, with both quantitative measures (e.g. timing of annotation, perceived load, standardized scales of usability) and qualitative; the contribution to the definition of the scientific functions The main office is Locarno with lessons and research activities also carried out in Lugano.

Requirements

  • Master in Computer Science or in a strictly connected field.
  • Interest in the design of interfaces and for human-machine interaction aimed at clinical users. Experiences in observation and coding of behavior, or in clinical validation studies, are preferential title.
  • Advanced language skills in English and Italian.
  • Group work skills, self-employment, flexibility and motivation.

The recruitment of the candidate is subject to admission to the PhD in computer science at the University of Italian Switzerland. The requirements and procedures for admission to the doctorate are available on the web page PhD in the Faculty of Computer Science | USI - Informatics
