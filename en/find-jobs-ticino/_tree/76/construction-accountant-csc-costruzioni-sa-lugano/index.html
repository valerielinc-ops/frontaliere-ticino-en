Full description

For our construction site within the new industrial and railway plant SBB in Arbedo-Castione we are looking for a yard accountant. The resource will be responsible for the main administrative and accounting activities related to the management of the yard, contributing to the proper monitoring of the financial flows and the management of the accounting documentation. Responsibility

  • Management of the passive cycle and registration of the passive invoices in the management system;
  • Verification, control and accounting of the invoices, ensuring the correct attribution;
  • estate and management of the yard;
  • Checking and checking of SALs (Advancing Works) and of the suppliers boards;
  • Reconciliation of banking movements and verification of accounting records;
  • Management and storage of administrative and accounting documentation through business tools;
  • Collaboration with the construction team and with the central administrative functions to ensure the correctness and punctuality of accounting processes.
  • Requirements
  • Degree in Economics, Business Administration or related disciplines;
  • At least one year of experience in similar roles in administrative-accountable;
  • Excellent knowledge of Microsoft Excel;
  • Knowledge of SAP and DocuWare is a preferential requirement;
  • Precision, reliability and attention to detail;
  • Good organizational skills and shortterm management skills;
  • Ability to work in teams and to interface with different interlocutors;
  • Knowledge of a second language between English, French and German is a plus;
  • The origin of the construction industry is an added value. #CSC #LI-LB2

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
