Full description

Particularly recognized for its rapid diagnostic approach and for the quality of care, the Internal Medicine Service has a central role in our institution. Its mission is to receive patients without diagnosis, often referred by the emergency department.

In the context of the development of new lines of care, particularly in geriatrics and palliative care, our service is called upon to evolve and strengthen its skills in the care of elderly, poly morbid patients with complex care situations.

In order to complete our service we recruit one

Day Nurse in Medicine - CDM 6 months 70% (ref: 2014)

In a state-of-the-art institution you have a variety of positions on a multidisciplinary team including nurses, health care workers, doctors.

You will need to accommodate patients with multiple pathologies as part of a comprehensive acute phase management.

As such you are in charge of;

  • Install the patient and present the administrative and care organization information
  • Collect and transmit medical and administrative data and complete the computerized care file
  • Provide and organize care planning according to priorities and urgency
  • Perform care on medical prescription and in accordance with unit and institutional procedures
  • Maintaining the Informatized Patient Record
  • Continuously assess the patient's clinical condition and its development in acute conditions
  • Identify situations of fragility, loss of autonomy and complexity requiring an interdisciplinary approach;
  • Participate in the care of elderly and polymorbid patients, as well as in the support of patients and their relatives who are confronted with palliative situations;
  • Collaborate with the various stakeholders to ensure continuity and coordination of care;
  • Ensure inter-service coordination for patient movement management
  • Coordinate inputs and outputs: internal mutations, external patient transfers in collaboration with the health framework.

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • Diploma of Nurse
  • In possession of Red Cross recognition if foreign diploma
  • Having experience in internal medicine, geriatrics, palliative care or a complex area of care, an asset;
  • Having a strong interest in the management of elderly, polymorphic and/or palliative patients;
  • Sensitive to the patient's holistic and interdisciplinary approach;
  • Meaning of contact and service in a private establishment
  • Self-governing, benevolence, versatility, adaptability, organization and sense of priority are essential qualities
  • Positive attitude within a team and are able to generate a climate of confidence
  • Able to help build confidence with patients, their loved ones and different professionals.

A changing environment

Joining our team is integrating a dynamic internal medicine service, at the heart of a developing environment, offering the opportunity to contribute to the evolution of new lines of care. We value employees wishing to develop their skills and invest in projects related to geriatrics, palliative care and care for patients with complex situations. Training or experience in these areas is an asset, but motivation and interest in these specialties are also particularly appreciated.
