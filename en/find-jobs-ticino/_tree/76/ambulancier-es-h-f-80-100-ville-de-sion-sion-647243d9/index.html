Dominated by its two iconic castles, Valère and Tourbillon, the city of Sion is distinguished by its dynamism, conviviality, sunny climate and rich historical and archaeological heritage. So many assets make it a quality destination. The Centre de Secours et d'Urgences (CSU) of the City of Sion is a reference actor in pre-hospital relief in central Valais. Dynamic and resolutely forward-looking, it places continuing training, innovation and quality and safety of care at the heart of its priorities. Each year, its teams carry out more than 5,200 interventions on the districts of Sion, Herens and Conthey, serving the population. Thanks to a modern planning system that allows everyone to indicate their availability, the CSU promotes a flexible work organization, best aligning the needs of the service with those of its collaborators.
