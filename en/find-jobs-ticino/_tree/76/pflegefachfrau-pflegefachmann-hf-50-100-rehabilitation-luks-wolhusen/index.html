Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 01.09.2026 or by agreement. Tasks: • They accompany our patients during their rehabilitation stay of several weeks and design the care process within the framework of the reference person concept individually, holistically and resource-oriented • Together with doctors and therapists, you ensure close coordination and effective interprofessional support. • You actively contribute your professional ideas, develop care concepts and work processes and contribute together with the station management to a high quality of care • Together with a versatile team, you actively shape a future-oriented specialist area with attractive participation, development and growth opportunities. Requirements • You have completed training as a qualified nurse or qualified nurse HF/FH. Rehabilitation experience is welcome, but not a requirement • They value personal contact and enjoy accompanying patients over a longer period of time on their way back to an independent everyday life. • They work responsibly, reliably and in a structured way, keep an overview even in dynamic situations and bring a good organisational talent with them • You are an open, empathetic and communicative personality, enjoy working in a team and feel like taking new paths and actively shaping the further development of rehabilitation. We offer: • At the Wolhusen rehabilitation centre, you can expect a diverse and dedicated team in which care, therapies and medicine work closely together. Together you actively shape the future of a growing specialist area • The new construction of LUKS Wolhusen creates a modern and patient-oriented working environment with high workplace quality. You will be given the opportunity to help shape contemporary care concepts, processes and workflows • As part of the “Magnet LUKS Group” initiative, you benefit from attractive working conditions – including increased allowances for late, night and weekend services and paid changing time. • Individual career models as well as a wide range of internal and external training opportunities support you in your professional and personal development – at a natural workplace and within one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland
