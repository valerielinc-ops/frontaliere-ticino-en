Full description

The Rorschach District Court offers two internships for trained lawyers. Internships usually last one year.

What to expect

  • Insight into judicial decision-making activities at a court of first instance
  • Participation in civil, criminal and SchKG proceedings
  • Recording of negotiations with an advisory voice in the decision-making process
  • Reasoning for decisions in single judge cases

What sets it apart

  • Completed Law Studies (MLaw)
  • Independent, precise work
  • Enjoyment of working together in a team
  • Confidentiality

What we offer

  • Internship as preparation for the examination for the acquisition of the St.Gallian bar patent
  • Interesting, varied activity
  • Various insights into court practice
  • Current employment conditions
