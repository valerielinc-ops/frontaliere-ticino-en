Full description

As a university of applied sciences, the FH Graubünden relies on dynamic thinking and proactive action. We develop over 2,400 students in education and continuing education into responsible personalities. In our inter- and transdisciplinary research, we focus on applied future technologies, development in alpine regions, and entrepreneurial action. As an employer, we promote equal opportunities and diversity. ## Tasks In this versatile role, you will ensure the smooth operation and technical maintenance of our new university of applied sciences center currently under construction. You will independently carry out inspections, maintenance, and repair work on HVAC systems, photovoltaic systems, sprinkler systems, and other safety- and operation-relevant systems. You will check the condition of the systems, document any anomalies, rectify faults, and initiate appropriate measures independently. You will also contribute to the establishment and further development of our new CAFM system, ensuring the complete and accurate recording of all maintenance and system data. Together with your team and other professionals, you will optimize maintenance processes and maintenance strategies. If necessary, you will also carry out tasks at other locations of the FH Graubünden. ## Requirements

  • Technical vocational training (e.g., maintenance technician, building services technician, electrical installer, or equivalent training)
  • Professional experience in inspection, maintenance, and repair, preferably in building technology or infrastructure operations
  • Knowledge of building management systems and experience with CAFM systems, advantageous
  • Service-oriented personality with fluent German language skills
  • Willingness to perform duties outside regular working hours, e.g., in case of faults or events ## What we offer
  • A well-established and collegial team
  • An exciting and varied job
  • Support for personal development
  • Modern employment conditions and numerous sports and cultural offers ## Contact (Not specified)
