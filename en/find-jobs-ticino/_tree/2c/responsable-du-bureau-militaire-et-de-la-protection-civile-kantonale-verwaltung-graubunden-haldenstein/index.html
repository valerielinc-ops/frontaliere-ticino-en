Full description

The Department of Justice, Security and Health of the Canton of Graubünden comprises eight offices, including the Military and Civil Protection Office. As the head of this office, you report directly to the head of the department and are responsible for protective measures, disaster management, and the leadership and training of command staff. You coordinate preventive planning in civil protection, lead the Civil Protection

  • Construction and Operations/Training departments, and are responsible for the military tasks assigned to the canton. In this role, you maintain intensive contact with federal offices, cantonal services, municipal authorities, and partner organizations in civil protection. Together with your team, you ensure a solution-oriented and appreciative working environment. ## Tasks
  • Organizational, professional, personnel, and financial responsibility for the office with around 30 employees
  • Leadership and coordination of the Cantonal Command Staff in disasters and emergencies in the Canton of Graubünden
  • Ensuring the implementation of tasks assigned to the canton under the Military Act and primary responsibility for civil protection
  • Leading, accompanying, and implementing projects
  • Initiating and conducting strategic processes
  • Representing interests in commissions, working groups, and roundtables
  • Maintaining contact with federal offices, cantonal services, municipal authorities, and partner organizations ##
  • Requirements
  • Tertiary-level education and experience in managing emergencies / crisis management
  • Several years of professional and leadership experience, as well as staff work in a civil protection organization and/or a blue-light organization
  • Knowledge and experience of cantonal political and administrative structures and procedural processes
  • Good knowledge of the geographical, political, and social conditions in the Canton of Graubünden
  • Excellent communication skills, high social competence, and the ability to remain calm in hectic situations
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is an advantage
