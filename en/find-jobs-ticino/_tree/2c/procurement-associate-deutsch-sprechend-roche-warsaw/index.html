The Position As a member of the Delivery Support Team (DST), you will act as a reliable global procurement partner, delivering end-to-end process expertise and operational excellence. We are looking for a proactive professional who can empower Procurement Managers to focus on high-value impact activities, contributing to operational efficiency through automation and process simplification.

The Opportunity

  • Provide end-to-end operational support for initiatives from project creation through to savings reporting and signature.
  • Create projects, RFx, and contracts in myBuy, and coordinate communication with vendors and stakeholders.
  • Manage vendor onboarding and maintain savings records using the IEM Tracker.
  • Handle standard activity clusters, including CDA processing, RSS escalations, audit support, and tail-end sourcing.
  • Maintain a continuous dialogue with Procurement Managers and category teams regarding demand, spend, and savings.
  • Support digital-solution implementations and contribute to internal automation and process-simplification initiatives.

Who you are

  • You have completed second-level education or equivalent - hold a Bachelor’s degree or MBA in Operations & SCM.
  • You hold a professional training qualification, such as a 3-year apprenticeship as an office communication clerk.
  • You possess additional training as a specialist in purchasing and materials management, or a Bachelor’s degree.
  • You have at least 2 years of procurement experience (experience in Capex Engineering is a plus).
  • Fluent in English and German

What you get

  • Hybrid work model (2 days per week in our Warsaw office).
  • Opportunities for professional development and soft skills training to help you advance.
  • Access to tailored workshops and training designed to build your expertise within the Regulatory area.
  • A unique chance to gain hands-on experience within a leading pharmaceutical organization.
  • A competitive package including Luxmed private healthcare, Multisport card and much more!
