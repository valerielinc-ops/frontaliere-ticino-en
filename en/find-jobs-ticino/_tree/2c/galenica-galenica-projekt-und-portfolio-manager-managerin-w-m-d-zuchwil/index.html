Full description

Location: MediService AG, Ausserfeldweg 1, 4528 Zuchwil

Do you want to manage complex cooperations economically and successfully transfer cross-divisional projects into operation? At MediService AG you shape the product, service and cooperation portfolio over the entire life cycle. You will combine project management, analytical thinking and digital solutions in a versatile environment in the Swiss healthcare sector.

You can do that.

  • You manage your product, contract and service portfolio throughout the entire lifecycle and coordinate introductions, adjustments and orderly terminations.
  • You plan and manage cross-divisional projects, monitor milestones, dependencies and risks and ensure the structured transition to operational operations.
  • You create business cases, profitability analyses, forecasts and management reports and thus create sound decision-making bases.
  • You monitor relevant KPIs and implement measures to sustainably improve sales, revenue and efficiency. You also contribute to budget and resource planning.
  • You will analyze and optimize processes with regard to quality, cost-effectiveness and regulatory requirements and use suitable digital solutions as well as meaningful AI applications.
  • You coordinate cooperation with pharmaceutical companies and internal departments and ensure that contracts, information and measures are implemented reliably and on time.

You bring that with you

  • You have profound experience in project management and combine it with strong business and analytical skills.
  • You know the Swiss healthcare system
  • Experience in pharmaceuticals, life sciences, healthcare or commercial excellence is ideally also available.
  • You are confident with data, reporting and digital tools and are interested in integrating AI applications into processes.
  • You work in a structured and independent manner, set priorities reliably and keep track of parallel contracts and projects.
  • You communicate convincingly, enjoy working with different teams and move safely in a matrix organization.
  • You communicate in German and English in a negotiated manner
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage.

We fill this position without external personnel service providers.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Extend competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

Individual pharmaceutical care Mediservice is a company in the Galenica network and supports people with chronic or rare illnesses through specialized pharmaceutical care - at home, but also by phone and video. As employees of Mediservice, we have the task of making the lives of our patients at home easier. Furthermore, we deliver complex and expensive medicines, offer various specialized services and impart health knowledge as well as tips and tricks for everyday use with medicines. Mediservice employs pharmacists, nurses and logistics staff. You make your very personal contribution to our patients to increase their independence and improve their quality of life.
