Senior Quality Engineer Outbound Operations (w/m/d) — Galaxus

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

In our E-Commerce Fulfillment Center we ship tens of thousands of packages every day. In the role of Quality Team, you optimize shipping processes, develop standards for packaging and delivery quality and sustainably strengthen our shipping quality with your outbound experience from e-commerce or retail logistics. With KPIs and data analysis, you increase efficiency and reduce errors.

Mansioni

  • Use dashboards and KPIs to identify trends in shipping errors, transport damage, false deliveries and packaging issues
  • Make sound decisions for process improvement based on data analysis
  • Perform root cause analyses in the event of quality problems in the goods outlet, for example in the event of packaging errors, damage during shipment or pick errors
  • Derive correction and preventive measures and accompany their implementation in order to reduce returns and costs of poor quality
  • Planning and managing projects to optimize picking, packing and shipping processes
  • Apply quality tools such as FMEA, Control Plans, CAPA or Gemba Walks to measurably reduce error rates
  • Collaborate with outbound operations, process engineering, software engineers and transport service providers to optimize end-to-end shipping processes
  • accompany automation and build scalable quality mechanisms

Requirements

  • 3 years of experience in: Quality management, operational excellence or in quality-related roles in logistics
  • Student (Uni/FH/HF): Engineering or Supply Chain Management
  • Completed studies in engineering, logistics, supply chain management or comparable as well as several years of experience in quality management in the outbound area of e-commerce, retail distribution or fulfillment centers
  • In-depth knowledge of Lean, Six Sigma or structured problem-solving methods as well as secure handling of SQL, Excel, Power BI, WMS and automation for the analysis and optimization of shipping and quality processes
  • Very good knowledge of German in order to convey quality and process topics in an understandable way and to communicate securely with different stakeholders in everyday operations
  • Clear communication ability, a high level of initiative and the ability to convince stakeholders in the fast-moving warehouse environment and to accompany changes pragmatically in everyday operations
  • Identify potential for improvement and implement changes to increase productivity and effectiveness
  • The ability to adapt to continuous changes in the working environment
  • German (flowing)
  • English (advantageous)
