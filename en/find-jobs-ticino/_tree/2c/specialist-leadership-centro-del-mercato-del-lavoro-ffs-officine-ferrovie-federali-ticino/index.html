In this key role, you will lead the team of consultants in the labor market center professionally and ensure that employees are competently, uniformly and effectively accompanied in the professional reorientation after job loss. You are the first contact person for the consultants in technical questions, coach them in demanding cases and develop their consulting competence further. You combine individual and appreciative support of employees with the entrepreneurial and labour law requirements of SBB. In this way, you make an important contribution to the social responsibility of our company. You can expect a dynamic environment with creative leeway and a noticeable effect for employees and companies. You can move that. - As a specialist guide labour market center you lead the consultants of the labour market center professionally

  • You set consulting standards, ensure uniform case management and equal treatment of employees and ensure a high quality of support from prevention to the completion of the professional reorientation.

- You coach and support the consultants in complex cases, lead case discussions and intervisions and promote their professional and methodological development. - You take on consulting and coaching mandates yourself and accompany employees after job loss on the way to a new sustainable solution. - Together with the line, you coordinate internal temporary assignments, conduct location interviews and ensure that employees are used productively during the job search and are integrated into the SBB. - You are the contact person for HR and managers in questions of professional reorientation in reorganizations and ensure good cooperation at the interfaces. - With key figures and evaluations, you keep an eye on the impact of consulting and develop consulting offers, processes and instruments continuously in a practical manner. You bring that with you. - As an integrative, resilient and conflict-affected personality, you bring experience in professional leadership, preferably from consulting or coaching teams, and are able to differentiate yourself personally and professionally. - You have well-founded consulting and coaching competence and several years of practice in accompanying people in professional change situations, ideally with a recognized further training in coaching, career consulting or supervision. - With communication strength, empathy and presence, you perform confidently even in difficult conversations and give constructive feedback. - You enjoy working in a network and maintain a trusting cooperation with consultants, HR, executives and external partners. You work in a structured way and use key figures and evaluations to ensure the quality and impact of the advice. - For this function we require a university degree at master level in business administration, psychology, work / social sciences or a comparable field. In addition, you have knowledge of the topics of work integration, vocational reorientation, reintegration, job loss as well as collective employment contract (GAV) and regional employment centres (RAV). - Negotiable written and oral knowledge of German (C2) is required in this position. Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
