Description of post

It is a university-hospitalized post of Chief Medical Officer or Chief of the Addictology Service, including a fraction of a faculty position. This position is attached to the Hospital and Academic Departments of Psychiatry.

The candidate must have recognized managerial, communication and leadership qualities and extensive clinical experience in addiction psychiatry, to run a university clinical service that provides diagnosis and therapeutic care for patients with addiction problems within the framework of the Swiss four-pillar policy on addictions. The service is a pioneer in its field and has made important innovations in such areas as alternative treatment, street medicine, home hospitalization and behavioural addiction. The candidate will continue to evolve the service to adapt to changing needs and therapies. Through his or her work, he or she will supervise, organize and develop the activity within his or her department, which includes mobile, outpatient and hospital teams. He or she will have to develop existing relationships with the Dependencies Unit of the Primary Medical Service, other HUG services and the Cantonal Health Office.

The ability to lead high-level research in the field of addiction psychiatry is required, as well as recognized skills in mentoring, team management and pre- and post-grade education.

Qualifications

You are in possession of a Doctorate in Medicine and specialization in psychiatry or equivalent. In-depth training in psychiatry and psychotherapy of addictions, obtained or in the process of obtaining, or title deemed equivalent. You justify a relevant experience of research and teaching direction. Publications in major international journals.
