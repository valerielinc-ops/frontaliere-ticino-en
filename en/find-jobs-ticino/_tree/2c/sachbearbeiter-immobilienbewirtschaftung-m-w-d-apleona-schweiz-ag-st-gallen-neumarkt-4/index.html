Full description

Your tasks

  • Real estate management: Independent processing of versatile administrative and organisational tasks in commercial and technical management.
  • Rental management: Active participation in the re-letting process, from tendering to visits to the conclusion of the lease agreement.
  • Rental Contracts: Independent preparation and processing of rental contracts, supplements and other contract documents.
  • dwelling acceptances and transfers: organisation and support of dwelling acceptances and
  • -transfers and their independent implementation after appropriate training.
  • Change of tenant: administrative processing including final statements and rental deposits.
  • Technical management: handling damage and defect reports and coordinating and monitoring repair and maintenance orders.
  • Financial management: participation in debtor and creditor management, in particular in invoice control, accounting and dunning.
  • Communication and cooperation: Competent support of tenants and external partners as well as close cooperation with the management team.

Your requirements

  • Completed basic commercial training or a comparable training, ideally with further training in the real estate sector
  • First professional experience in real estate management and ideally basic knowledge of Swiss tenancy law
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as a stylish and addressee-oriented communication
  • Safe handling of MS Office and digital work equipment
  • Knowledge of the real estate software GREM is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and careful working methods as well as strong organisational talent
  • High service orientation, sense of responsibility and pleasure in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Team-capable, resilient and solution-oriented personality who keeps an overview even in hectic situations
  • Valid category B driving licence and own vehicle for outdoor dates
