Full description

Calibrate and test Delvitech's AI optical inspection machines before delivery, combining mechanics, PLC configuration and software. Job Summary The Calibration Technician will be responsible for the setup, calibration, testing and validation of our AOI machines in production, using mechanical tools, measurement instruments and the machine's dedicated software running on Ubuntu. The technician will work closely with the Quality department and, when needed, with R&D. The role also includes drafting production documentation, such as test reports, checklists and updates to calibration procedures, and completing the final checklist required to approve the shipment of machines to customers worldwide. Key Responsibilities

  • Configuration: manage the basic machine configuration, including control PLC, safety PLC and PC (Ubuntu).
  • Machine calibration: perform manual calibrations using mechanical tools and software calibrations using dedicated tools.
  • Testing and validation: execute electrical safety tests, ESD tests, functional tests and product validation.
  • Troubleshooting: diagnose, report and resolve identified issues.
  • Documentation: support the creation and updating of production and calibration procedures.
  • Reporting: complete reports, documentation and test results.
  • Collaboration: work closely with teams outside production, such as Quality, Application Engineering and R&D.
  • Cross-functional operations: perform assembly tasks when needed, particularly on optical vision systems, to meet production targets. Requirements
  • Technical diploma or degree in engineering, IT or a related field.
  • Knowledge of the Linux environment (Ubuntu).
  • Strong computer skills.
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work both independently and as part of a team.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Good command of English. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
