Put to the Point

Would you like to use your IT expertise to support our investigation or migration authorities in investigating crimes or identifying people? Then bring your know-how and become part of our team.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 485-20869
  • Place of work: Eichenweg 3, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Install, configure and integrate purchased, window-based specialist applications into the existing infrastructure
  • Ensure the stable, secure and highly available operation of the supported applications
  • Plan and implement updates, maintenance and migrations
  • Analyze and resolve technical failures and ensure 2nd and 3rd level support.
  • Participate in projects for the introduction, further development and replacement of specialist applications
  • Participate in regular piketting service and thus ensure operation outside regular working hours

This makes you unique

  • Completed computer science training EFZ
  • In-depth work experience with Windows Server 2022 or newer versions
  • In-depth knowledge in the operation of Windows-based applications
  • Analytical and networked thinking as well as an independent, structured and responsible working method
  • Enjoy solving technical challenges, learning about new technologies and developing your own expertise
  • Good written and oral English skills

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

IT for Switzerland’s internal security

The Computer Science Service Center ISC-EJPD is the central computer science service provider in the Eidg. Department of Justice and Police. High customer satisfaction and economic service provision are among the most important success factors. The ISC-EJPD develops and operates specialist applications for the areas of police, justice and migration of the EJPD. Our applications and infrastructures meet the highest security and availability requirements.
