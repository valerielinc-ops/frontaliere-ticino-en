Full description

The Pallas clinics are specialized in ophthalmology and aesthetics. With over 350 employees and 17 locations, we are one of the leading providers in Switzerland.

Our medical staff plays an indispensable role in the customer care process. They act as direct contacts and provide professional and individual support. The focus is always on the well-being of our customers. Through close cooperation with our specialists, we guarantee a holistic supply. Our team is characterized by high commitment, regular training and the pleasure of the interdisciplinary cooperation with our customers.

In order to strengthen our team in Bern, we are looking for a motivated/- n

Medical assistant in 80-100% (ophthalmology)

Responsibility

  • Management of office hours and operations and ensuring a smooth daily routine
  • Patient Care and Administration
  • Friendly reception and first contact person for our patients
  • Information and preparation of patients before surgical procedures
  • Administration of eye drops
  • Provision, cleaning and ordering of consumables, medicines and tools for consultations
  • Follow-up of patients (instruction, answering questions, etc.)
  • Performing measurements (Autoref., NCT, Octopus)
  • Pre- and post-operative care of patients

Requirements

  • Completed education as a Med. Practical assistant/in EFZ or equivalent training
  • Several years of professional experience in the health sector, ophthalmology beneficial
  • Open, dynamic and independent personality with a lot of pleasure in dealing with people
  • Team-oriented working method and high customer orientation
  • A well-groomed appearance with impeccable manners
  • Readiness for flexible use within practice activities
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing, French is an advantage

Benefits

  • Varied and challenging activity with a lot of personal responsibility and independence
  • Miscellaneous employee benefits
  • Performance-oriented team bonus system
  • Regulated working days
  • Five weeks of holidays per year
