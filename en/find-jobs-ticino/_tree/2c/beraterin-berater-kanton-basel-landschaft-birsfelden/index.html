Full description

In a versatile and exciting working environment, you accompany and advise young people on their entry into professional life. They support them in discovering their strengths, developing perspectives and finding their own professional path step by step. You can expect a varied and meaningful activity with a lot of creative scope and direct contact with young people. We offer you a temporary workload of 75% with the prospect of a subsequent permanent employment of 60%. Vocational training, secondary schools and higher education services Birsfelden by 1 February 2027 or by agreement

Your responsibility

  • Counselling and support for young people and young adults who cannot manage the transition to basic vocational training without assistance
  • Case-related coordination and interdisciplinary cooperation with specialist bodies (e.g. welfare state, education system, health sector) in the sense of case management to ensure needs-based support for young people
  • Statistical and technically comprehensible case documentation in the digital case handling tool
  • Active participation in project and working groups (e.g. professional development)

Your background

  • Sound knowledge of the educational landscape, in particular vocational training
  • Experience in advising and accompanying young people in challenging life situations that are at the transition to basic vocational training.
  • Tertiary degree in social work, psychology or pedagogy
  • Additional training in the field of counselling/coaching (or willingness to complete this part-time)

Vocational training, secondary schools and higher education services

Within the Educational, Cultural and Sports Directorate (BKSD), the Department of Vocational Education, Secondary Schools and Higher Education (BMH) is responsible for the post-compulsory school sector at both secondary and tertiary level as well as for career guidance for the entire population of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft. As part of the Main Department of Vocational Education and Training (HABB), the Centre for Vocational Integration makes an important contribution to the professional integration of young people and young adults between the ages of 15 and 25.
