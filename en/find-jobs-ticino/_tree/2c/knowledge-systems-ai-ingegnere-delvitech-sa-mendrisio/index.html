Full description

Build Delvitech's internal knowledge platform with LLMs, RAG and knowledge graphs, turning company know-how into reliable AI tools. Job Summary We are looking for a Knowledge Systems & AI Engineer to design and develop a company-wide knowledge infrastructure that collects, organizes and connects information from different sources, systems and people, and makes it usable by people, software and AI systems. The infrastructure must represent our product as a cyber-physical system, connecting software, electronics, mechanics, components, configurations, calibrations, requirements, tests, failures and procedures, in the correct context of each product, version and configuration. It will support requirements definition, software development, impact analysis, troubleshooting, documentation, training and decision making. Key Responsibilities

  • Define the architecture and technical roadmap of the company knowledge infrastructure.
  • Design a structured knowledge model of products, subsystems, components, configurations, software, requirements, tests, calibrations, failures and procedures.
  • Integrate sources such as Confluence, Jira, GitHub, technical databases and documentation, including engineering data such as BOMs and hardware revisions.
  • Design retrieval, semantic search and grounding systems based on verifiable company sources, evaluating RAG, knowledge graphs, vector databases and ontologies.
  • Make knowledge securely accessible to AI tools, internal applications and coding assistants, with appropriate access and permission levels.
  • Design AI applications for technical troubleshooting and AI workflows to capture and validate knowledge from experts.
  • Implement human-in-the-loop validation, safeguards against hallucination, data leakage and prompt injection, and metrics for accuracy and usefulness.
  • Collaborate with Quality, R&D, Application Engineering, Production and Technical Writing, avoiding dependency on a single AI vendor. Requirements
  • Strong ability to design and develop complex software systems integrating data, AI and services.
  • Practical experience with AI, Large Language Models or knowledge management solutions.
  • Good knowledge of APIs, databases and system integration.
  • Knowledge of RAG, semantic retrieval, grounding and knowledge graph principles.
  • Ability to model complex technical domains and cyber-physical products.
  • Good understanding of LLM risks and of access control and permissioning principles.
  • Strong analytical and systems-thinking skills; ability to work with subject-matter experts.
  • Professional working proficiency in English. Nice to Have
  • Hands-on experience with RAG, knowledge graphs, vector databases, semantic search or agentic workflows.
  • Familiarity with MCP or equivalent protocols.
  • Experience integrating Jira, Confluence, GitHub or enterprise platforms.
  • Familiarity with Systems Engineering, SysML, PLM or BOM data.
  • Experience with AI evaluation, observability and monitoring. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
