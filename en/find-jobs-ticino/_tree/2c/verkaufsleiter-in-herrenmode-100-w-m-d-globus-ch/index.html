Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the “place we love to be” to life together with us. For our menswear sector we are looking for you: an experienced sales person with a feeling for people, materials and looks. You like to work with high-quality products, recognize what customers need – and find stylish solutions. You understand fashion as an expression – and advice as an encounter. If that suits you, please keep reading. Here you make the difference

  • You make sure that our customers not only shop, but also take a fashion experience that inspires and convinces.
  • You take over the personnel and organizational leadership of the department and provide an environment in which consulting culture, style security and team spirit are lived.
  • You advise the team in day-to-day business, promote type-appropriate advice and bring your strengths to bear in visual merchandising and the staging of brand and style worlds.
  • You control processes efficiently, support events, reporting and administrative tasks – and ensure that digital tools are also used smartly.
  • You accompany new employees and learners and show leadership with heart and mind.

That draws you out

  • You bring several years of professional and first management experience in upscale fashion sales – ideally as Stv. Sales Manager:in or Team Leader:in.
  • Your basis is a completed training in the retail trade EFZ – supplemented by further education
  • You have a sense of trends, brands and fits – and understand how to advise people in a type-appropriate, authentic and enthusiastic way.
  • You work in a structured, entrepreneurial way and actively support the team.
  • You communicate safely in German, further language skills are a plus.
  • You use digital applications with ease.

BENEFITS FOR YOU Together with around 1,700 other lovers of detail, you are an important part of our fascinating premium and luxury world. You experience real appreciation, work in a great team and we trust you so that you can use space and make a difference with your skills. If you understand your craft and bring a large portion of passion, then many career doors are open to you. We are happy to support you with internal but also external training and further education.

Also, look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment with flat hierarchies in the heart of Bern.
  • Benefit-based premiums and attractive social benefits.
  • Flexible working time models and at least 5 weeks holidays (PS: You get more free time thanks to the option "unpaid holiday" - from 7 to 180 days).
  • up to 50% discount on products from the globe world for you and your family.
  • Much more...

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
