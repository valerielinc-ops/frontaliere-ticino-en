Founded in 1900, the Klinik Sonnenhalde in Riehen is now an established private clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland and southern Baden. We pursue a holistic, human-centered, milieutherapeutic psychiatry that combines excellent professional quality with a Christian value base. The clinic has 68 stationary beds, 4 day clinics and 4 ambulatories. As one of the first clinics in Switzerland, we are actively promoting digital therapy offerings, such as blended therapy and virtual reality and opened the first hybrid day clinic in Switzerland in February 2023.

With your warm and attentive nature, you create a pleasant atmosphere for our guests, respond to their individual needs and contribute together with the team to a smooth and hospitable restaurant operation.

Your responsibilities

  • They look after our guests in a friendly, attentive and professional manner and respond to individual needs such as allergies
  • You take responsibility for your assigned service area and ensure a smooth process
  • They take care of ordering for drinks, food/non-food items, kiosks and consumables
  • You ensure a well-kept restaurant area and work closely with the kitchen and your teammate

Your profile

  • They convince through a friendly and professional approach to patients
  • You have an empathetic and service-oriented attitude as well as experience in customer contact or service area
  • They work reliably, independently and responsibly
  • You have a solid knowledge of German in words and writing as well as teamwork and flexibility in terms of tasks and working hours
