Full description

Tasks

  • carry out computer-forensic analysis in the context of the activities of Police and Public Ministry
  • provide support, as a specialist technician, in the conduct of judicial police investigations
  • to collaborate in technical-informatic projects according to the tasks of the service
  • optimize computer-forensic analysis methods and automate work processes, proposing improvements
  • collaborate in the relevant section trainings
  • participate at the cantonal, national and international level in working groups and/or in projects in their specialist field

Requirements

  • higher education (APF, SSS) in computer science, data analysis, or business environment
  • multiannual experience in the field of computer security
  • in-depth knowledge in the field of security of networks and systems, data analysis and programming
  • analytical and interdisciplinary approach, psychophysical resistance and ability to work independently and in team
  • excellent knowledge (conversation and editing) of the Italian language, knowledge (written and spoken) of at least one second official language and English
  • license to conduct category B
  • willingness to follow training courses and achieve their specialist certifications
  • Swiss citizenship
  • good conduct and absence of precedents incompatible with function
  • is preferential title:
  • possession of certifications and diplomas in the field of forensic information

  • flexibility of use according to need (extended hours, holidays, night)
