What tasks await you?

  • Working with microorganisms and cell cultures
  • Application of biochemical, immunological and molecular biological methods to develop new diagnostics and therapeutics
  • Manufacture biotechnological products to detect, prevent and treat diseases
  • Laboratory animal work in accordance with the Animal Protection Act
  • Documentation and evaluation of the tests using digital systems

​ Throughout the entire training period, you will take part in various internships in the training laboratory, e.g. B. Microbiology, cell culture, fermentation, pharmacology, histology, biochemistry/immunology, molecular biology, hematology, physical and analytical methods, instrumental analysis/chromatography.

Your advantages with us

  • Development: Intensive exam preparation, technical college entrance qualification (optional) and a wide range of further training.
  • Extras: International working environment, assignments abroad and discounted canteen meals.

What requirements do you have to meet? At least intermediate level of education

How long does your training last? 3.5 years, with good performance 3 years
