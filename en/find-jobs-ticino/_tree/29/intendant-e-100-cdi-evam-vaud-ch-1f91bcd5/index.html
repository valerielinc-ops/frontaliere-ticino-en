What you bring to our mission

Through your commitment and technical skills, you contribute directly to the quality of life and hospitality in the Gland home and in the various homes where you intervene in reinforcement. You ensure its proper functioning and maintenance, the organisation of daily activities and contribute directly to the safety and well-being of both residents and teams. Your sense of service, rigor and relationship capacity contribute to creating a respectful, structured and secure living environment.

Your key missions

  • You ensure the technical management of the fireplace, including the maintenance of interior and exterior spaces and the maintenance of technical installations

  • You ensure administrative follow-up, including the management of orders, mail, occupancy plan and periodic security checks.
