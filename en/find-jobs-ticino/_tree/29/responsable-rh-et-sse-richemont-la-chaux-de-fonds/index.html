Full description

Reference code: JR135178

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

How will you flourish by our side?

A close partner of the Director of Manufacturing, you manage all HR and Health, Safety and Environment activities of the site. You combine global vision, operational excellence and field presence to provide long-term support to teams and organization.

This role is both strategic and operational, providing full support for the HR cycle and a central position for the company's Management Committee. You will also be the manager of the HR-SSE team.

In order to ensure a strong performance of the company, you will need to manage daily HR operations and relationships with employees, while establishing a trusting relationship with the various operational managers to design and manage a strong HR strategy that ensures compliance with Richemont Group policies and current legislation.

You will actively contribute to the continuing evolution of our organization by promoting and promoting a creative, stimulating, healthy and equitable environment, while accompanying teams to act and make their decisions in line with this culture.

How will you make a difference?

  • Actively contribute to the site management team to effectively implement the HR vision and mission, in full alignment with the Program Strategy.
  • Build trust and trust with business leaders to provide advice and lasting solutions on workforce planning, attraction and retention strategies, the development of a culture of continuous learning and skills development, while ensuring sound and effective succession plans.
  • Lead our annual HR processes by ensuring their understanding and realization by all our employees and managers (performance management, individual development plans, talent management, compensation processes).
  • Build strong relationships with teams to have a clear vision of key HR topics, develop appropriate action plans and deliver successful results.
  • Structure and mentor the HR-ESS team by clarifying their roles and responsibilities to empower them and ensure quality deliverables on the HR Roadmap. Act as an ambassador of change to successfully embark the team on the evolution of the company.
  • Contribute to the development of the HR Roadmap shared with other manufacturing sites by conducting projects that are beneficial to all.
  • Conduct a demanding and pragmatic ESS approach, closely linked to operational teams. Identify risks, coordinate prevention actions and promote continuous improvement. Ensure regulatory compliance and develop a shared culture of safety and environmental responsibility.

Your profile

  • Higher training in human resources, management or equivalent.
  • Experience confirmed in a general HR function, ideally in an industrial or manufacturing environment.
  • Strong knowledge of Swiss HR fundamentals and strong interest in Health, Safety & Environment issues.
  • Ability to combine strategic vision, operational rigour and field sense.
  • Collaborative leadership, listening, diplomacy and the ability to influence diverse interlocutors.
  • Excellent organizational, analytical and communication skills in French.
  • English mastery.
