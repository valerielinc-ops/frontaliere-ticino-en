Full description

We gather what belongs to the whole!

The Anker Swiss Group, born in April 2021, is growing and is now also present in ICTINO. Do you want to be part of a future-oriented society? On behalf of our esteemed customer, we are looking for the following professional figure immediately: Black Metal Constructor 100% Working area: Mendrisis

Main handling

  • Construction and assembly of metal structures in iron, steel and aluminium.
  • Production and assembly of gates, parapets, stairs, wall units, railings and heavy metal carpenters.
  • Reading and interpretation of technical drawings and workshop patterns.
  • Working of profiles, tubulars and sheets by means of specific machinery.
  • Welding performance MIG, MAG, TIG and electrode.
  • Assembly and assembly of metal artifacts in workshop and construction.
  • Use of drills, saws, bending machines, shears and other professional equipment.
  • Quality control of manufactured products.
  • Maintenance and repair of existing metal structures.
  • Collaboration with the workshop manager and construction staff.
  • Compliance with safety regulations and company quality standards.

Skills required

  • Proven experience as a Metal Builder.
  • Excellent readability of the technical drawing.
  • Good knowledge of metal processing and assembly techniques.
  • Experience in MIG, MAG and TIG welding.
  • Ability to work independently both in the workshop and in the yard.
  • Accuracy, reliability and attention to detail.
  • Excellent craftsmanship and organizational skills.
  • Problem solving and teamwork.
  • Good physical form and prepared for manual work.

Preferential requirements

  • AFC of Metal Builder, Blacksmith or equivalent title.
  • Experience gained in the Swiss market.
  • Welding certificates valid.
  • Driving license category B and half.
  • Immediate availability.

We offer

  • Insertion into a firm and structured company.
  • Professional and dynamic work environment.
  • Interesting and diversified projects.
  • Long-term employment.
  • Interesting wage conditions tailored to the experience gained.
  • Constant support from the Anker Swiss team.
