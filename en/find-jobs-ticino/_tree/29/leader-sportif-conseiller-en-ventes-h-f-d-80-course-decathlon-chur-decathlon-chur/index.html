Full description

Your future playing field: Decathlon Chur You will become part of our well-established team at the Decathlon Store in Chur. Your department is at the heart of our local strategy in the city. Here you will meet a highly motivated team of passionate athletes who equip locals and tourists with the perfect equipment every day. Become part of a Swiss success story Decathlon Switzerland is growing rapidly: Since the start of 2017, we have expanded our network to around 53 sales outlets. And we continue to expand – especially in German-speaking Switzerland. For you, this means a crisis-proof workplace with real, nationwide career opportunities. Why this job is perfect for you You are not looking for a classic 08/15 job, but want to share your passion for sports from morning to evening? You love the action on the sales floor and want to see your clientele break up with a smile? Your daily action: The tasks

  • Enthusiastic advice: You are the first point of contact for our customers. You listen, analyze the needs and find the perfect equipment.
  • Your area: Your focus is on running – you help our customers find the perfect running shoe and the right equipment. If you can also help out in cycling, this is a huge plus point.
  • A store to feel good: You provide a complete presentation of goods ("facing") and make your department dynamically and seasonally attractive.
  • Hands-on mentality: From incoming goods to placement on the shelf – you actively tackle where it burns and ensure a smooth customer experience.
  • Circular Economy: You don’t just “sell.” You drive forward sustainable circular models and inspire customers for our rental and second-life offers. This job is NOTHING for you if...
  • You prefer to sit quietly behind a screen all day.
  • It is difficult for you to approach people openly and actively.
  • You think unpacking pallets and racking up shelves is “not your job.”
  • You would rather have free Saturdays.
  • You are looking for rigid, unchanging working hours.
