Full description

The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. With mountain railways, hotels, gastronomy, sports and rental shops and the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive workplaces for our employees. | Weisse Arena Bergbahnen AG is a subsidiary of the Weisse Arena Group, Laax. It is responsible for the construction and operation of the tourist transport facilities and has 30 facilities (cable cars, gondola cars, chairlifts, ski lifts). Furthermore, the snowmaking, the preparation of the 215 km slopes as well as the parks and pipes, the rescue service and the lift ticket sale. | Do you like to work independently, take on responsibility and enjoy dealing with people? At our mountain railway coffers in Falera, you can manage your point of sale independently, keep an overview even in lively situations and find the right solution for our guests. If you work reliably, confidently and responsibly, a varied seasonal position awaits us as an employee: in Guest Service & Ticketing! | What you do:

  • You self-manage our mountain railway fund in Falera and are the first point of contact for our guests on site.
  • You advise our guests competently about the offers of the destination LAAX and find the right solution for their concerns.
  • You sell tickets, subscriptions and other products and help with questions about online bookings.
  • You organize your everyday work independently and ensure a smooth operation of the sales point.
  • You handle guest concerns such as Lost & Found, refunds and claims professionally and solution-oriented.
  • You run your cash register responsibly and ensure a correct daily statement.
  • You ensure that sales and consumables are available at all times.
  • For questions or special concerns, you work closely with the POS team and other internal departments. What makes you:
  • You have completed vocational training and ideally already have professional experience in the service, sales or tourism sector.
  • You like to work independently, take on responsibility and can organize your daily work independently.
  • You are reliable, conscientious and feel comfortable looking after a sales outlet independently.
  • Even in hectic or unexpected situations, you stay calm, keep an overview and find suitable solutions independently.
  • You enjoy direct guest contact and act friendly, safe and professional.
  • You speak fluent German and English. Knowledge of Italian or French is a plus.
  • You are also a team player and get support from the POS team if necessary.
  • Irregular working hours and assignments on weekends and public holidays are a matter of course for you. | An attractive workplace in the middle of a breathtaking mountain world is certain for you. You also have the opportunity to develop in our dynamic, bright team and vibrant company culture. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family.
