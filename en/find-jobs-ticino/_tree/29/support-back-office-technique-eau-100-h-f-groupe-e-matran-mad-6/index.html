Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. An exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2300 employees, including 210 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life. Group E Tech, thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment and maintenance of installations. Join a team specialized in water treatment solutions and take on a key role, at the heart of technical projects. Combining expertise, precision and versatility, this position will allow you to contribute to various achievements, ranging from small installations to industrial and collective applications. A true reference in technical and administrative support, you will benefit from an environment where quality, mutual assistance and knowledge transfer are central. Support Back-office Technical Water 100% (H/F) Mission: Within the Water Unit, you ensure a technical and administrative support role that is essential to the smooth running of the projects. Your main responsibilities are:

  • Develop commercial offers and costing of sanitary and industrial water treatment facilities.
  • Analyze tender documents, identify elements within our field of activity and prepare technical and commercial proposals.
  • Management of supply models in the ERP database.
  • Administrative monitoring of offers, tariff catalogues and technical documentation.
  • Provide technical support to technicians by answering their questions and accompanying them in choosing suitable solutions.
  • Ensure the back-up role of the Water Technical Office Manager.
  • Participate in the monitoring of water quality contracts, organize sampling, coordinate analyses with the partner laboratory and carry out some analyses on an ad hoc basis.
  • Collaborate with suppliers to monitor technical files.
  • Contributing to continuous process improvement and maintaining a high level of quality in case processing.
  • Participation in water unit marketing activities. Profile:
  • SWC of sanitary installer with a strong interest in administrative and technical activities or SWC of commercial employee supplemented by experience in building, sanitary or water treatment technology.
  • An initial experience in technical back office or health project management is a real asset.
  • Training specialized in water treatment or experience in water analysis: benefits appreciated.
  • Very good mastery of computer tools, including Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, Outlook)
  • Aisance with administrative tasks and simultaneous management of several files.
  • Excellent command of French, appreciated knowledge of English.
  • Driver's licence category B.
  • You stand out for your rigor, your sense of organization and your constant concern for quality.
  • Curious and autonomous, you like to understand technical aspects, seek solutions and develop your knowledge. What we offer:
  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate "Together, we are moving towards success" Need more information?
