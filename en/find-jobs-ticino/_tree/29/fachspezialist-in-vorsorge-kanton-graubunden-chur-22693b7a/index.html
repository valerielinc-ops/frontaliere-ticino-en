The Graubünden Pension Fund is the largest pension fund in the canton of Graubünden. It serves more than 10,000 insured persons, more than 4,000 pensioners and 130 affiliated employers. We manage a pension fund of 4.5 billion francs. Among our insured are the employees of the canton of Graubünden, the public institutions, municipalities, schools and institutions from the health and social sector. This diversity makes our work varied and creates exciting and challenging questions every day.

  • For our pension team we are looking for 1\. October 2026 or by agreement a dedicated, service-oriented personality as specialist /\-in provision.
  • In this versatile job, you are the contact point for our insured and employers. With your competent and solution-oriented advice, you shape the first impression of the PKGR.
  • Your responsibilities
  • Management of the insured and employer portal "myPKGR"
  • Processing of monthly pay interfaces
  • Processing of incoming and outgoing payments (incoming/outgoing services, purchases, WEF\ advance payments and \-liabilities)
  • Implementation of pension compensation in the event of divorce as well as processing of declarations of beneficiary and civil partnership
  • Support for benefit cases in cooperation with our reinsurance
  • Planning and implementation of employer visits, information events, presentations and webinars
  • Active participation in projects, the further development of our processes and digitalization
