Full description

Class and annual salary (including 13th monthly)

3 52'756.-- / 81'560.--

Tasks

  • participates on the basis of the directives issued by the Director/trice of the CARL and within the framework of the specific competences, the drafting of the rehabilitation and socio-educative development plan of the residents for which it acts as a reference figure supported by a nurse/a and/or an educator/trice
  • performs the necessary interventions to properly manage the problems arising from the behaviour of the residents, while promoting their autonomy and psychophysical well-being.
  • participates in all those rehabilitative activities, social and professional integration indispensable to make operational the objectives established by the educational work of each individual
  • cooperates with operators/internal and external operators involved in taking charge of residents
  • performs and guarantees medical prescriptions and therapeutic interventions authorized by its specific training or other received instruction
  • organizes its activity within the four areas of presence, including nights, in order to ensure continuity of educational care

Requirements

  • Federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as a socio-assisted operator (address for persons with disabilities)
  • flexibility and good ability to adapt
  • availability at shiftwork
  • motivation to work in team
  • knowledge of the territory, institutions and the social network of the Canton Ticino
  • perfect mastery (conversation and editing) of the Italian language and knowledge (speaking) of another official language
  • proven psychiatric experience

Special remarks

  • degree of employment: a 100% place and a 90% place. In its application the candidate is invited to indicate the desired percentage
  • Workplace: Mendrisio
  • availability by the candidate to work at irregular times and to work at shifts (festives and night)
  • start date: to be defined

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required additional documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements
