Full description

Do you feel at home at the interface between business and IT and do you quickly recognize the essentials in complex questions? Do you want to actively shape the digitalization of our post offices and self-service offerings? You can expect a versatile environment around stable product teams, retail core systems and solutions for our physical access points. With you, we develop sustainable IT solutions for the access points of the future.

You can do that.

  • You will actively shape your role on our journey towards stable product teams and work with your team to develop future solutions for our physical access points.
  • You will drive forward topics such as digitalization, the redesign of the retail core system and self-service solutions.
  • In your function in the field of computer science/technology, you combine the requirements of business with the possibilities of IT.
  • In addition, as a member of a business-oriented cluster around our post offices and self-service offerings, you support projects and support services from the definition of requirements to the introduction.
  • You are specifically involved in the development of sustainable IT solutions and drive them forward together with the relevant contact persons.
  • You also take responsibility for pilot projects and participate in the evaluation of innovative ideas.

You bring that with you

  • You have a commercial or technical basic education, supplemented by a further education in computer science or business informatics.
  • With your many years of professional experience and your enthusiasm for the diverse topics of IT, you are ready for this exciting challenge.
  • You have experience with agile methods and use them successfully in projects.
  • At the same time, you convince with an addressee-friendly appearance and assume responsibility for several IT services in a complex IT landscape.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German and English. Any other national language is beneficial.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
