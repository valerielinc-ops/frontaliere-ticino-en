Location: Hirslanden Clinic Stephanshorn | St. Gallen

Occupation by: by agreement

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 75265

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

You want to do a commercial apprenticeship and are looking for the right start? The internship at the Hirslanden Klinik Stephanshorn gives you a real insight into the commercial clinic routine during a year – ideal as a practical part of a bridge offer (e.g. pre-apprenticeship at GBS St. Gallen) or as an intermediate year. After a careful introduction, you will actively participate in our teams and take responsibility step by step. If you perform well, you will have the opportunity to start your apprenticeship as a merchant/merchant EFZ.

YOUR TASKS

  • Collaboration in various administrative areas of the clinic, e.g. reception/patient admission, clinic secretariats, human resources, purchasing or management
  • First contact person on the telephone and at the counter for patients and visitors
  • Completing correspondence, post and filing as well as collecting data in our systems
  • Assistance in organising meetings, training and internal events
  • Support for smaller projects and everyday commercial tasks

Your profile

  • Completed compulsory schooling (secondary level I) and participation in a bridge offer (e.g. pre-teaching GBS St. Gallen) or comparable situation
  • Clear professional goal Kauffrau/Kaufmann EFZ
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as basic knowledge in MS Office
  • Joy of contact with people as well as a friendly and well-groomed appearance
  • Reliability, willingness to learn and a careful working method
  • Teamwork and interest in healthcare

Please apply online only with the following documents

  • Short motivation letter: Why would you like to become a merchant/merchant?
  • Tabular CV incl. Email address and photo
  • Certificates of upper level or bridge offer
  • Test reports and results of aptitude tests (e.g. multicheck, basic check), if available

Do you want to contribute your skills in a modern and future-oriented hospital environment? With us, you can expect a family working environment in which personal responsibility, team spirit and personal development are promoted. Together we make a valuable contribution to the well-being of our patients every day.

You can rejoice.

  • Prospect of an apprenticeship as a merchant/merchant EFZ (H+ Health industry) at Hirslanden
  • Personal accompaniment by a permanent reference person and regular location meetings
  • Varied insight into several commercial areas of an acute hospital
  • Support with your applications and in cooperation with your school
  • Motivated, helpful team
  • Restaurant with up to 50% discount
  • An own staff restaurant with low prices, free coffee & tea, offered monthly breakfast buffet
  • Generous public transport participation, cheaper Reka checks and much more!

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed.
