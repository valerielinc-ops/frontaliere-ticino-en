Full description

About Our Client Our customer is a player in the financial services sector, recognized for the quality of its positioning in recognized markets. It operates throughout the value chain, serving a loyal clientele. In a context of sustained transformation, our customer is pursuing an ambitious strategy combining growth, innovation and strengthening of its steering functions. The work environment is characterized by a high level of requirement, high exposure to regulatory issues and a constant desire to improve processes and tools. Joining this organization is integrating a solid and evolving structure, providing development opportunities within a finance function at the heart of the Group's strategic stakes.

Job Description Attached to the financial management, you intervene at the heart of the Group's activities with a responsibility over a broad accounting area. You frame a dedicated team and ensure the quality, consistency and transparency of all financial information produced. This position offers a wide exposure to different activities and is a natural transition opportunity for an experienced profile from external audit (minimum Senior Manager level), wishing to evolve towards an operational role with a managerial and strategic dimension. You actively contribute to the production and reliability of the Group's financial information, including:

  • Coordination of periodic closure work and securing financial deliverables within time constraints
  • Preparation and review of accounts and consolidated statements
  • Monitoring of accounting frameworks applicable to the activity and their proper implementation within entities

You also oversee processes to ensure a consolidated reading of performance:

  • Organization of consolidation cycles and validation of reported data
  • Production of analyses for management and supervisory bodies
  • Contribution to the quality and relevance of financial reporting

In a highly regulated environment, you participate in controlling financial risks:

  • Regular interaction with oversight functions and external stakeholders
  • Monitoring of applicable regulatory obligations
  • Guidance on tax matters related to specialist experts

Finally, you play an active role in the evolution of the finance function:

  • Optimization of processes and strengthening of controls
  • Participation in tools and systems improvement projects
  • Contribution to transformation and automation initiatives

As a manager, you support your employees in their development:

  • Organization and management of team activity
  • Skills development and increased expertise
  • Creating a structured and collaborative working environment

The Successful Applicant You justify significant financial experience, acquired in a leading audit firm, financial services structure or comparable environment. Experience in external audit at an advanced level is a particularly relevant asset for this position, including a transition to operational responsibilities.

You also have

  • A strong understanding of financial production and consolidation issues
  • A good mastery of multi-entity and multi-currency environments
  • An ability to capture diversified financial activities and their accounting impacts
  • Easy with financial information systems and reporting tools
  • Ability to frame and structure a team
  • High quality and reliability requirement
  • Analytical spirit and critical sense developed
  • Ability to step back and prioritize in a complex environment
  • Taste for continuous improvement and transformation environments
  • Ability to interact with various interlocutors
