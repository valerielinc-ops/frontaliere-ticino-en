Full description

Our five-star hotel in the heart of Zermatt has welcomed guests from all over the world since 1879. The Grand Hotel Zermatterhof combines tradition, timeless elegance and personal hospitality with the unique mountain scenery around the Matterhorn. Our varied gastronomy ranges from fine dining with Valais ingredients to a French brasserie to traditional Swiss specialties such as fondue and raclette. To strengthen our team, we are looking for warm, motivated personalities. We look forward to your application.

Their tasks

  • Professional support and advice for our guests around all concierge services
  • Acceptance and processing of guest requests, reservations and special requests
  • Assistance with check-in and check-out as well as organization of transfers, excursions and tickets
  • Coordination with internal departments to ensure smooth running for our guests
  • Ensuring the highest quality standards according to LQA guidelines
  • Close cooperation with the front office and other service areas, especially for VIP and special requests
  • Maintaining discretion, professionalism and a well-groomed appearance at all times
  • Readiness for night and shift work as well as flexible use according to guest requirements

Your profile

  • Completed education and in-depth professional experience in a comparable concierge or guest relations position, ideally in the 5-star hotel industry
  • Very good German and English skills, French or other foreign languages are advantageous
  • Strong service orientation, empathy and professional guest care
  • Maintained appearance, confident appearance and high communication ability
  • Independent, structured working methods, teamwork and responsibility
  • Open-minded, dutiful personality with pleasure in demanding guest care
  • Experience working according to LQA standards and high quality awareness
  • Resilience and flexibility, including night and shift services

We offer

  • Temporary employment in an appreciative and collegial working environment
  • An attractive, performance-related compensation
  • Accommodation and food are available
  • A versatile, exciting and varied activity

A comprehensive benefit package within the Zermatt Hospitality Group AG with attractive discounts in our catering companies as well as special overnight rates for family and friends

  • Further education and development opportunities, including through Typsy, with recognized modules
  • Internal language courses to promote your personal and professional development
  • Free use of fitness and wellness areas at marginal times
