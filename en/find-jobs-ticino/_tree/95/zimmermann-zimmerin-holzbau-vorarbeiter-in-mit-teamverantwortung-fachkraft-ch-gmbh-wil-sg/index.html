Full description

Self-responsible management and coordination of wood construction projects are in the foreground. The company is characterized by modern working methods and flexible working hours. You will receive support in the training phase and development opportunities. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • Self-responsible management of a team in erecting and assembly work of wooden system buildings
  • Implementation of demanding interior constructions and renovation projects
  • Ensuring compliance with deadlines and quality standards
  • Requirements
  • Completed training as a carpenter EFZ
  • Experience as a foreman in timber construction combined with relevant school training
  • Self-employed, careful and reliable working methods
  • Ability to motivate a team through clear leadership and fair handling
  • Possession of a category B driving licence Offer
