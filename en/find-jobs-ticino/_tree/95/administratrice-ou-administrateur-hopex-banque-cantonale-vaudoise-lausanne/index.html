Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV IT? Put your skills and experience at the service of one of the few banks that develops, maintains and ensures the security of its own Information System. The endless and varied professional opportunities within the 3 IT departments offer you the opportunity to carry out strategic projects, which will contribute to the success of the entire bank. For our IT Departments, we are looking for one or more:

Administration or HOPEX Administrator Your main missions

  • changing the Architecture Reference (Hopex Platform) by finding the best approaches between attributes, relationships, pages, scripts, diagrams and reports to meet modeling needs;
  • to develop the public website of the Reference to present new objects and relationships and describe its content in different formats (lists, pages, dashboards, trees),
  • ensure the proper daily operation of the Reference to its users and its operation (extractions, imports, site generation),
  • produce specific exports in the form of DataSets exposed by REST APIs or Excel export, and import data for mass correction,
  • collaborate with the BCV editor and IT consultants to develop the solution, extract relevant logs, install patches, make changes between environments. Your profile:
  • knowledge of the internal functioning of Hopex, ideally of several versions of the product, and mastery of the Oriented-Object concepts underlying this operation (metaclasses, meta-associations, meta-attributes),
  • knowledge of ERQL and scripts (VB, javascript, PowerShell) for forms, macros, extractions, and public website generation,
  • perseverance and ability to dig to understand the functioning of Hopex,
  • knowledge of the Privacy Management Module, a plus,
  • knowledge Swiss Bank, one plus,
  • certification in Enterprise Architecture (TOGAF) and/or ArchiMateTM, a plus.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let us write together three stories, one of your career, one of your bank, one of our region.
