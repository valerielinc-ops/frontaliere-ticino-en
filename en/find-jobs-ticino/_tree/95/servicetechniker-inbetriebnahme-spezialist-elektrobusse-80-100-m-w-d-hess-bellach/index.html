Full description

Service technician/commissioning specialist international electric buses 80 - 100% (m/f/d)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. We also offer a comprehensive range of repairs and services for vehicles of all kinds (buses, commercial vehicles, passenger cars, campers, etc.).

To strengthen our Bus Hot Commissioning Team, we are looking for an experienced practitioner from the field of vehicle or commercial vehicle technology. Experience with electric or trolley buses is not a requirement. If you master the basics of vehicle technology and are interested in modern electromobility, we will introduce you to our vehicles, systems and technologies. Their tasks

  • Troubleshooting and diagnosis of traction components and other systems of electric and trolley buses at the customer
  • Carrying out repair work on the entire vehicle
  • Accompanying supplier visits to the customer
  • Maintenance of renovations / modifications from the aftersales
  • Implementation of conversions and modifications in the aftersales
  • Feedback of your experience from field use in development, production and aftersales for continuous improvement

Our requirements

  • Completed technical training, e.g. as an automotive mechatronics specialist specialising in commercial vehicles, agricultural and construction machinery mechatronics specialist or a comparable qualification.
  • Several years of practical experience in vehicle, commercial vehicle, agricultural machinery or construction machinery technology
  • Enjoying troubleshooting, diagnosis and independent problem solving
  • Interest in electromobility, high-voltage technology and modern vehicle systems
  • Willingness to travel at a high level of 80-100% (Switzerland and Europe)
  • Your assignments and stays out of the house can be planned in the long term, and the weekends are usually free. In this way, you combine a varied activity at different locations with reliable personal planning.
  • Good IT knowledge
  • English skills and other foreign languages are beneficial

We offer them

  • Exciting, varied activity with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Flexible annual working hours
  • Opportunity to work part-time
  • Modern electric vehicle as a business car
