What you do

  • Our patients you care for and look after in shift services with high personal responsibility.
  • In doing so, you will carry out the care process (including performance recording of materials and care) according to the latest findings, supported by the clinic information system KISIM.
  • For the achievement of the divisional and departmental goals, you take on joint responsibility and are committed to the continuous optimization of care processes.
  • In addition, you support our learners and students in their education.

What distinguishes you

  • You are a qualified nurse (AKP/DN II/HF/FH) and ideally have experience in the acute field.
  • The well-being of patients is at the heart of your work and you meet people with respect and appreciation.
  • Teamwork as well as self-responsible and quality-conscious work distinguishes you.
  • Good German skills round off your profile.
