Full description

The Spitex Region Entlebuch supports and enables with its services living and living at home for people of all ages who need care, care or help. Our comprehensive range of services includes somatic and psychiatric care, palliative plus and domestic services for eight communities in the region. Head* of Team Entlebuch Your task

  • Personnel and professional management of the nursing team at the Entlebuch base
  • Creating and coordinating service and holiday planning
  • Ensuring a needs-based and high-quality care and support of the clientele
  • Responsibility for economical and efficient service provision as well as client documentation
  • Implementation and compliance with operational requirements and processes at the base
  • Conducting needs assessments and reassessment with InterRAI HC and creating care planning
  • Carrying out all care services at the clientele
  • Active cooperation with external persons and specialist bodies

Your profile

  • Diploma completed as a nurse HF/FH or comparable qualification
  • InterRAI HC course or willingness to complete it
  • Leadership experience is beneficial
  • Further education in the field of leadership or motivation for professional development in the field of leadership
  • Readiness for flexible working hours and assignments
  • Joy of self-responsible and independent work
  • Good communication skills and an appreciative, motivating, clear and targeted way of working
  • High load capacity and flexibility
  • Advanced computer skills and driving licence Cat. B

Our offer

  • Demanding, interesting and versatile activity
  • Careful introduction and support during the training period
  • A motivated, collegial and supportive team
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • Progressive and attractive employment conditions
