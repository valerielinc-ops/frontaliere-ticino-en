Specialist technicians: in industry 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Specialist technicians: in industry Biel/Bienne, from now on or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Working in the area of maintenance and inspection of company vehicles makes sense because you ensure that the vehicles are safe and reliable. You can expect a varied everyday life with repairs, mechanical work and precise electrical tasks on different vehicles. Regulated working hours and good team cohesion create a reliable framework. However, physically demanding work is just as much a part of it as careful and concentrated work. With suitable further training, you can deepen your technical knowledge of vehicles and also develop further in topics such as occupational safety. You can move that.

  • Your main tasks include repair, maintenance and inspection work, as well as conversions to various service vehicles so that the vehicles remain safe and reliable.
  • If the situation requires it, you are flexible and ready to help out at our outdoor locations.
  • You actively participate in continuous improvement processes, measure and document process steps and keep your work clean and traceable.
  • Deviations are detected early and ensure seamless documentation.

You bring that with you.

  • We write teamwork and helpfulness great! You enjoy working with your own team, engineering and other teams and get involved independently.
  • A careful and reliable way of working distinguishes you. You work precisely, comply with safety standards and deal responsibly with the vehicles and tools.
  • For this function, you bring a completed technical vocational training: for example as an automotive mechatronic engineer:in, agricultural machinery or construction machinery mechanic:in, polymechanic:in, automatic engineer:in, automatic installer:in, electrical installer:in, assembly electrician:in or in a comparable mechanical or electrical profession.
  • Practical knowledge in mechanics and electrics as well as interest in carrying out repair, maintenance, inspection and conversion work and working out solutions together with the engineering we presuppose.
