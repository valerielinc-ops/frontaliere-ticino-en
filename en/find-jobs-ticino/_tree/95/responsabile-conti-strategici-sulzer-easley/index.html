Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary The Lead Strategic Account Manager is responsible for managing and growing relationships with key clients. This role ensures that client needs are met, identifies new business opportunities, and coordinates with internal teams to deliver tailored solutions that drive long-term value. This job profile is intended for recognized subject matter experts in a job area. Required to manage large projects or processes with limited oversight from manager. Coaches, reviews and delegates work to lower level professionals. Summary: Assigned accounts, with strategic importance to the company, specifically for the chemical process industry (CPI) and fertilizers segments in Western USA. Accounts may be local, national, international or have other important factors that classifies it as a strategic account. Also, promotes the sales and service of Sulzer products and accessories (new equipment & after-market: spare parts, repairs & field service) to established and new customers by providing technical and commercial proposals in response to inquiries, and interfacing between customers and company facilities, following written policies and procedures. Assigned territory and segments would be covered by a mix of direct accounts and distribution (indirect).
