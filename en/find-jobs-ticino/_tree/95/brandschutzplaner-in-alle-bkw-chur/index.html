Full description

Join our inspired team at our innovative engineering company and become part of the future of living spaces. Benefit from our experience, skills and many employee benefits.

Your tasks

  • Preparation and processing of fire protection plans for a wide variety of construction projects
  • Development of firefighting plans as well as escape and rescue route plans in accordance with applicable guidelines and guidelines
  • Support of project managers in the drawing implementation of fire protection concepts
  • Maintenance and updating of plan documents during all project phases
  • Participation in the development of efficient and practice-oriented planning solutions
  • Close cooperation with project managers

Your strengths

  • Completed training as draftsman, designer or equivalent technical training
  • Further training as a fire protection specialist VKF or interested in completing it
  • Very good knowledge in the CAD program NOVA
