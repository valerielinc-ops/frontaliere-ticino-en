Full description

Are you motivated to start your professional career in a multinational environment? Join our internship program to launch your career with us and benefit from a practical and training approach. We are looking for candidates available for one year from February 1, 2027. Your missions

  • You create and modify hydraulic product lines,
  • You ensure the management of product ranges and data,
  • You create documents (work instructions, plans) and collaborate in the design of workplaces based on scientific criteria,
  • You ensure the design and purchase of manufacturing and control means (gabarits, machining tools, specific devices, measuring means) and calculate the operating costs,
  • You guarantee the management of continuous improvement projects and define and improve control plans,
  • You are involved in updating the AMDEC (Analysis of failure modes, their effects and their criticality),
  • You implement projects to streamline and optimize costs. Your profile
  • You are in progress or have completed your engineering studies in Mechanical Engineering, Production Process or Industrial Engineering. You also have good knowledge of machining,
  • You have very good knowledge of French and English and/or German in terms of language,
  • The mastery of the following programs is a plus: MS Office, SharePoint, Albatros and an ERP,
  • You stand out for your enthusiasm to take on new responsibilities, your ability to work as a team and in a way, autonomous if necessary. In the face of tasks, you are competent, flexible and adapt to the dynamic environment of LMB. Our offer Can we motivate you to take on a key role in a family, dynamic and independent business? We encourage you to share and develop your ideas and offer you a safe job, modern working conditions that are compatible with family life such as flexible working hours, the possibility of working in Home Office, a business restaurant, opportunities for continuing training and many other benefits. The workplace is located in Bulle, in the beautiful Gruyère region. Plan your personal and professional development with us. In order to ensure a fair application procedure, we do not accept applications by mail or e-mail. Have we raised your interest? If so, we would be happy to receive your application online. If you have any questions, please contact Sabrina Sotheary BEAN. One Passion. Many Opportunities. *A common passion. So many opportunities. Liebherr Machines Bulle SA is the competence centre for combustion engines (diesel and gas) and hydraulic components. The company develops and manufactures diversified products used in civil engineering, mining, agriculture, decentralized energy, mechanical and industrial engineering and aviation.
