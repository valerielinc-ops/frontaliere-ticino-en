Team leader: in construction site safety 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team leader: in construction site safety Ostermundigen / Work Smart, 01.03.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you can drive major changes and thus contribute to the digitalization of SBB. Together with your teammates, you develop solutions for current challenges and implement them in a team. Further education is also not neglected for individual professional development. You are flexible and use the possibility of “Work Smart” to work from home or on the road. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits and more information about the profession can be found here. You can move that.

  • You are responsible for the technical and personnel management of a team of about 15 employees in the field of construction site safety.
  • Conducting recruitment, development and leadership interviews as well as personnel assessments is also part of your area of responsibility.
  • In addition, you take responsibility for the organization of human and material resources for the various railway construction sites.
  • You are present on the construction sites, taking care of the organisation and implementation of safety audits and ensuring the safety of railway construction sites.
  • You participate in various working groups and thus ensure the continuous improvement of the existing processes.
  • Completing administrative tasks such as reporting, billing and time controls, monthly billing etc. are also among your strengths.

You bring that with you.

  • You are self-reliant, dynamic and flexible and have the ability to adapt to different workloads and unforeseen events and to organize yourself.
  • As an initiative and communicative personality with a high partner orientation, dealing with people is easy for you.
  • For this position, you need basic construction, technical or commercial education (EFZ), supplemented by a function-specific further education.
  • First experience in the management of employees distinguishes you.
  • In recent years, you have built up knowledge in railway construction or are ready to acquire it.
  • You are sure to negotiate in German (level C2) and basic knowledge in French and / or Italian (level A2) is a plus.
