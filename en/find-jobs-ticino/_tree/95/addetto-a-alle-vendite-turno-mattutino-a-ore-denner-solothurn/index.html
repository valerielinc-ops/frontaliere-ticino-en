Full description

Fresh products that live up to their name. Shelves stocked. Floors clean. Cash register balanced. Satisfied customers. That’s our store. And soon it could be yours. Join our fresh and winning team now. ## Responsibilities

  • Operate and balance the cash register
  • Receive, check, and stock fresh products and all other goods in your store
  • Check product expiration dates
  • Ensure cleanliness and order in the store and storage area
  • Correctly implement store layout guidelines ## Requirements
  • Ideally, some initial work experience in retail
  • Completed basic or further training in retail is an advantage
  • Willingness to work beyond the usual "nine to five" hours – and enjoy your free time when others are working
  • Physically fit, as some packages can weigh up to 10 kilograms
  • Familiar with PCs, tablets, and smartphones; you quickly adapt to other digital tools (e.g., for inventory management)
  • Enjoy working with people, with a good dose of friendliness and a positive attitude
  • German (good knowledge) ## What We Offer
  • Part of the Migros Group: A strong, retail-rooted brand under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Healthy working conditions: High standards in occupational safety, ergonomics, and hygiene
  • Maternity/Paternity leave: 5 extra weeks of maternity leave (19 weeks total) for mothers with 100% pay. Fathers enjoy 4 paid weeks
  • Fair wages: 13 monthly salaries and fixed salary increases for everyone instead of bonuses for a few
  • Mobile subscription: Your choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo, or Sunrise, you’re well-connected starting at CHF 10.-
  • Migros Bank: Better conditions for interest rates and fees on personal, investment, or mortgage accounts
  • Employee benefits: 10% off your next big purchase (twice a year)
  • Vacation: 6 weeks of vacation for everyone, with additional days for work anniversaries
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on car rentals, hotels, events, and much more ## Contact ## Application & Contact Remo Herrmann Recruiter Sales Mittelland
