Do you have a heart for the elderly? Is the care profession your calling, which not only improves the quality of life of others but also brings satisfaction and meaning to your own life? Do you see opportunities to bring your skills and experiences to us to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are in the right place!

The family residential and care center Sternmatt in Lucerne offers people with dementia a safe, life-affirming home in an environment characterized by respect and care. In the protected living areas, our care guests receive the care they need, tailored to their individual needs.

For Tertianum Sternmatt in the Lucerne region, we are looking for immediately or by arrangement a

Healthcare Specialist EFZ 40-100%

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and nursing of guests in accordance with our nursing philosophy and the nursing process
  • Taking on tasks as a nursing contact person
  • Participation in the nursing process and in the nursing needs assessment with RAI for the assigned guests
  • Electronic nursing documentation using CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach-Smartphone)
  • Planning and accompanying doctor visits
  • Taking on daily responsibility
  • Taking on tasks as a theme bearer of a specialist topic and thus participating in the group-wide network for nursing quality

Your profile

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
