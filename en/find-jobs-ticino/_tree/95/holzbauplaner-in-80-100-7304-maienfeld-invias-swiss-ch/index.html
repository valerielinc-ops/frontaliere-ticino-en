Full description

WOOD PRODUCER:IN 80 – 100% In order to continue and expand our success story, we are looking for an experienced personality as Wood construction planner: in 80 – 100% immediately or by arrangement at one of our locations in Maienfeld or Savognin What you do with us As part of a creative team, you plan and realize modern wood construction projects. From initial designs to workshop planning, you bring your expertise and work on a variety of exciting construction projects.

Your tasks include

  • Diverse project planning – Whether multi-family houses, halls or impressive event buildings – together with the team you develop versatile and sustainable construction projects that inspire.
  • Detailed planning with effect – Your precise and thoughtful workshop plans form the basis for a smooth project process.
  • Technology meets practice – With your digital 3D models and precise production plans, you can lay the foundation for smooth production.
  • Together for success – You will become part of a dedicated team that supports you during training and projects – and appreciates your active participation, supports each other and builds on your ideas.

What we offer you

  • INVIAS offers you a modern, innovative working environment in the midst of an inspiring mountain world. Probably the most varied canton in Switzerland is our home where we work and live.
  • Benefit from numerous fringe benefits, attractive social benefits, training opportunities and flexible working models.
  • 5 weeks holidays and 3 weeks winter bridge
  • Enthusiasm, dynamism, sustainability, connection and trust are the values that shape our corporate culture.

What you bring

  • Completed basic training as carpenter/roomkeeper EFZ
  • Technical training, e.g. as a timber construction technician:in or timber construction polisher:in
  • Several years of experience in timber construction, in particular in production, assembly and the creation of workshop plans (2D/3D) for timber constructions
  • In-depth knowledge in a CAD program, ideally Cadwork
  • Self-responsible and goal-oriented working method
  • Structured and networked thinking and action
  • High flexibility, strong reliability and a strong team orientation
