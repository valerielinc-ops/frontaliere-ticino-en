Full description

Buyer Your tasks

  • Issue and manage purchase orders in SAP based on MRP requirements for assigned commodities, monitoring confirmations and deliveries and managing advances, postponements and cancellations.
  • Negotiate trading conditions with suppliers, update lists and record info and control costs, reporting price discrepancies (MCI).
  • Manage administrative anomalies and unblock invoices and coordinate the activities of expertise Purchases in non-compliance management.
  • Search and evaluate new suppliers along with Quality and Engineering and monitor their performance in terms of punctuality, quality, lead time and reliability.
  • Support Global Sourcing initiatives, identifying saving opportunities and collaborating with Production, Logistics, Quality and Engineering to optimize supply processes.

Your profile

  • Federal ability certificate (AFC) of equivalent trade or technical training employee; a SUP title in business economy or management engineering is a plus.
  • At least 2--3 years of experience as Buyer, preferably in industrial contexts and in the purchase of mechanical and electromechanical components.
  • Excellent knowledge of SAP, in particular the management of purchase orders and MRP processes; good mastery of Microsoft Office, especially Excel.
  • Good trading and cost analysis skills; organized, precise and proactive person, oriented to results and with teamwork attitude.
  • English and Italian B2.

About us AGIE CHARMILLES, inventor of EDM (Electrical Discharge Machining), is known as a premium brand and innovation leader in wire, die-sinking, and hole-drilling EDM. Its automation-ready solutions are used in mold making and the production of high-end components, offering Swiss precision, reliability, and performance. UNITED MACHINING is one of the world's leading providers of complete solutions for precision components and tools manufacturers and the Moldova-making industry. The portfolio includes milling, EDM, laser texturing, laser micromachining, and additive manufacturing machines. Additionally, the division offers spindles, automation, tooling, and digitalized solutions backed by unrivaled customer services and support. UNITED MACHINING includes the brands AGIE CHARMILLES, CHARMILLES, MIKRON MILL, LIECHTI, STEP TEC and SYSTEM 3R. UNITED MACHINING is headquartered in Biel, Switzerland, and currently has over 3'000 employees on over 50 sites worldwide.
