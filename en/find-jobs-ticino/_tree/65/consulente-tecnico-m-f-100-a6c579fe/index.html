Full description

We move and inspire people, simply, in a sustainable way

The AMAG Group is one of the 50 largest Swiss companies. Our vision is to become the leading provider of sustainable individual mobility. Approximately 7,600 employees work every day to achieve this goal with great expertise and passion, ensuring that our customers are always on the road. Do you like to get in touch with people and live customer service passionately? Then we absolutely want to meet you! For our Porsche Lugano Centre, we look for a motivated, reliable and flexible person who joins our team as a 100% technical consultant (m/f). Your homework

  • Customer Relations Manager: You will be the point of reference for our customers, you will provide a targeted and competent advice by coordinating appointments of mechanics and body shop until the return of the vehicle and maintaining active relationships
  • Commercial interface: You will work closely with the sales team, acting as a link between the workshop and our customers
  • Expert in quotes: You will process offers in line with the requests, guarantee the cleanliness and completeness of the vehicles and you will manage the workshop orders professionally until the billing to reach the maximum satisfaction of the customer
  • Mobility Expert: You will create a welcoming atmosphere and convince our customers thanks to your expertise and your passion for mobility

Your profile

  • You have training as a certified technical advisor or an AFC of maintenance mechanics or mechatronics of cars
  • You have matured at least 5 years of experience as a technical or receptive consultant
  • You are a person with a sense of responsibility and have strong organizational skills that also keeps in moments of intense activity
  • Resistant to stress, you are flexible, dynamic and proactive
  • You have affable ways and relationships with people easily
  • You are comfortable with digital processes, virtual communication with customers and the use of artificial intelligence tools
  • Please express yourself correctly in German or English
  • You are willing to perform courses and professional updates
  • You have a valid driving license of category B

We offer

  • An interesting activity in the fascinating world of automobile in a premium environment
  • A job with prospects: we support the professional development of our employees
  • A dynamic working environment
  • Open and transparent communication at all levels
  • Interesting conditions for all Group brands

You think we could cooperate?

Then we look forward to receiving your application online. By the way: we do not need a letter of motivation. Just give us three convincing reasons why you are exactly the right person for this position.
