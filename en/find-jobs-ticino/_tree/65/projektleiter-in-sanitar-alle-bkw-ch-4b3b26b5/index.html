We make habitats worth living.

Are innovation, sustainability and design more than just buzzwords for you? Then you are exactly right with us. Become part of our dynamic and committed team and shape the solutions of tomorrow with us.

Your tasks

  • You manage and handle sanitary projects independently – from planning to successful handover.
  • You plan, coordinate and monitor new and renovations and ensure compliance with costs, deadlines and quality standards.
  • You create offers, supplements and billing and are responsible for material orders and scheduling.
  • You coordinate the cooperation with builders, architects, specialist planners and entrepreneurs.
  • You guide and support our installers on the construction sites and participate in construction meetings and site visits.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training as a sanitary installer EFZ and have ideally completed further training as a sanitary planner, project manager for building technology or a comparable qualification.
  • You bring experience in project management of sanitary facilities.
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented and like to take on responsibility.
  • You convince by a secure attitude in dealing with customers, partners and other project participants.
  • You have good MS-Office knowledge as well as the driving license category B.
