Full description

Per il settore della produzione di birra presso la sede di Chur (Sudostschweiz) cerchiamo una persona autonoma, motivata e con spirito di squadra. Be sotto la direzione del Manager Brewing e lavori in un team esperto e motivato di circa 12 birrai. Il tuo ambito di attività comprende principalmente:

  • Use in brewhouse as well as in fermentation and storage cellar and filtration
  • Control of most automated systems and identify, analyze and solve problems
  • Ensure optimal production process
  • Working with TPM Methodology (Lean)
  • Technical service support for mechanical work as well as Utilities support

Requisiti

  • Teaching as brewers, resp. Food Technology FR Brauen
  • 2-3 years of professional experience, ideal in a brewery
  • knowledge of an automatic brewhouse (e.g. Brewmaxx)
  • Data maintenance in modern information systems such as Proleit Integrate
  • Experience in handling modern control systems and good IT skills
  • Your technical affinity and willingness to take over tasks on the actual task profile of a brewer is desirable
  • Workest Do you like working together?
  • Are you initiative, resilient, compelling and ready to work in 3-shift operation?
