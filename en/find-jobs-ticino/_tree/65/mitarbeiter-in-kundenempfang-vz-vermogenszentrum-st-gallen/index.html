Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We advise individuals and companies about money, manage assets and offer attractive financial services. Since we are growing vigorously, we are looking for reinforcements in our branch in our central location in St. Gallen as of January 2027.

  • You are the first contact person for our customers, whom you look after at the reception and on the phone – courteous and with much joy.
  • You are an important interface to marketing at the headquarters and process the returns from our regular shipping campaigns.
  • You support your colleagues in the office with administrative tasks.
  • You are responsible for various internal tasks.
  • You have a fixed amount of one day per week (Monday) and take over holiday representatives (about 6 weeks a year).
  • Experience in customer reception
  • Excellent communication in Swiss German and German
  • Serenity in stressful situations
  • Good organisational skills
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office programs
  • You work in an uncomplicated and open environment.
  • You can quickly take responsibility and help shape a unique company
  • Surveys on satisfaction regularly show that employees rate the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become a part of it!
