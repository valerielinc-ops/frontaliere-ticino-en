Put to the Point

In this key role, you will become the organizational hub of the Economic Crime Department – in direct cooperation with department management and procedural teams. You will gain a 360-degree insight into the internal processes of the Federal Public Prosecutor’s Office, expand your interdisciplinary competences and become part of a well-established team in which cooperation and exchange of experience are lived daily. Does that sound like your next challenge? We look forward to your application.

  • Date of entry: 01.03.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 110-20735
  • Place of work: Werdstrasse 138 + 140, 8036 Zurich

You can make this contribution

  • Relieve departmental management and those responsible for the field of crime in organisational and coordination matters
  • Create correspondence, e-mails and presentations and maintain management and departmental filing
  • Plan, organize and follow-up meetings, business trips and events – including protocols and pendant management
  • Support human resources and management processes, manage general applications of the department and training applications
  • Responsible for departmental administration – from expenses and budget to the further development of templates and working instruments

This makes them unique.

  • Commercial basic training, supplementary further education in administration, IT or languages of advantage
  • At least 3–4 years of experience in an assistant role, ideally at a public prosecutor’s office, court or law firm
  • Strong organisational talent, independent and careful working methods and a high level of service orientation
  • Strong communication, resilient personality with discretion, flexibility and enjoyment of a turntable function
  • Secure handling of MS-Office and interest in processes, quality assurance and digital work equipment
  • Good active knowledge of at least two official languages
  • English skills are beneficial

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

The Federal Public Prosecutor’s Office (BA) is the Federal Public Prosecutor’s Office. Its core tasks include protecting the state under criminal law and combating serious cross-border crime in the areas of organised crime, terrorist financing, money laundering, corruption and economic crime.
