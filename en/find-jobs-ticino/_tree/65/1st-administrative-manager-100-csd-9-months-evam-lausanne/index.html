What you bring to our mission

You ensure the administrative and financial management of the property's housing stock, with the aim of guaranteeing the availability, quality and compliance of the accommodation services for the beneficiaries.

You also help to ensure the adequacy, efficiency and compliance of the administrative areas available to employees, while optimising the use of real estate and financial resources in accordance with the legal framework and the mandates entrusted.

The word of your future leader, Skender

"To join a team is not only to occupy a position, it is to choose an environment in which you will learn, contribute and evolve. *

As a manager, what I'm looking for is not just skills, but people: curious, involved, able to question each other and move forward with others. The rest is learned.

  • Here we value ideas, initiative and the right to error. We're working hard, without taking ourselves unnecessarily seriously. Everyone has a place and can have a real impact. *

If you want to invest in a concrete project, with a team moving forward together and just saying things, then you might feel in your place among us.

To the pleasure of talking with you".

Your key missions

  • Ensure administrative and financial management of the housing stock (budgets, invoices, accounts, cost tracking) and analyse variances
  • Assess the use of assets (occupation, rotation, costs, claims) and make recommendations
  • Manage the lease life cycle (analysis, negotiation, renewal, termination) and process stakeholder requests
  • Planning and monitoring work, managing maintenance contracts and insurance
  • Updating housing availability as required
  • Using real estate data to support decision-making and improve processes
  • Collaborate with internal and external partners, process requests and manage complex or conflict situations

Your profile

  • SWC of trade employee or equivalent
  • Minimum 3 year work experience in real estate management
  • Good knowledge of lease law (additional training, an asset)
  • Mastery of office automation tools and, ideally, property management software
  • Excellent analytical, synthesis and organisational capabilities
  • Team spirit, collaboration and flexibility
  • Meaning of negotiation, assertivity and rigour
  • Interest in migration

What EVAM proposes

  • A committed working environment
  • A policy of continuing training to evolve in your career
  • Telework policy
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 from 50 years)
  • Very good social benefits
  • Meaningful projects in a stimulating intercultural context
