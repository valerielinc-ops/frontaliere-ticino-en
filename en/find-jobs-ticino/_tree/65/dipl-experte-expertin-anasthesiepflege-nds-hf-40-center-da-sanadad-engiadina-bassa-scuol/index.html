Full description

For the anesthesia department in the Ospidal we are looking for 01. January 2027 or by agreement a/- n

Tasks

Our focus is on traumatology, orthopaedics with endoprosthetics, gynecology and visceral surgery. Furthermore, there is a gastroenterology, pneumology with the corresponding endoscopic examinations and pain therapy interventions.

Main tasks

  • Preparation of anesthesia procedures, provision of drugs, materials and monitoring
  • Participation in anesthesia as part of the assigned responsibility and assistance in anesthesia and interventional pain therapies
  • Accompaniment of sedations at div. Endoscopies, HIFU
  • Materials and medicines management incl. Settlement
  • Active contribution to the implementation of quality and safety standards

Requirements

Requirement profile

  • Competence certificate Dipl. Expert anesthesia care NDS HF or equivalent foreign degree
  • A communicative, team-oriented personality who also likes to work actively with colleagues from other disciplines (emergency station, GEBS, etc.)
  • Responsible and patient-oriented professional
  • High level of technical and social competence, resilience and flexibility
  • Readiness for regular piketting services on weekends and public holidays
  • Computer skills

Our offer

  • Sound technical training

  • Internal and external training

  • Modern infrastructure adapted to the range of services
  • Working and living in the intact natural landscape of the Engadin (National Park Region) with high sports and leisure value

Under the umbrella «Center da sandà Engiadina Bassa» (CSEB) / «Health Center Unterengadin» key regional providers in the areas of health care and wellness are taking a common path. In the national park region, a wonderful environment awaits you with versatile recreation and sports opportunities.

As a regional hospital, Ospidal is responsible for basic and emergency medical care in Lower Engadin. It offers state-of-the-art school and complementary medicine and plays a pioneering role in the field of patient safety in Switzerland. The areas of surgery, traumatology, orthopedics, gynecology and obstetrics, internal medicine and palliative care, integrative medicine, anesthesiology and pain therapy including an IMC ward are offered. The hospital has state-of-the-art infrastructure with KIS, RIS, PACS including numerous advanced telemedical solutions as well as a modern surgical tract with 2 operating rooms.
