Full description

The Nidwalden Hospital is a modern acute care hospital with 90 beds, around 600 committed employees and is responsible for extended basic care. As a LUKS Group company, we are part of a powerful network that is one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. Together, we provide coordinated, digitally networked and accessible healthcare around the clock with passion and commitment. We are looking for a task (s) immediately or by appointment:

  • On weekends and holidays, you ensure that our emergency practice runs smoothly – from telephone triage to patient care
  • You support wound care, professionally apply bandages and fixations and independently carry out emergency laboratory tests (blood collection, urine, etc.)
  • You ensure order and cleanliness in the treatment rooms and always keep an eye on the emergency pharmacy
  • In addition to patient care, you coordinate patient transfers and take on versatile administrative tasks in everyday practice Requirements:
  • You have completed training as an MPA and ideally have several years of professional experience in your area of expertise or in a comparable function
  • You are a service-oriented, empathetic personality and appreciate working in a team
  • You enjoy working in a dynamic environment
