Full description

Your tasks Your next career step as a Senior Property Management Manager (m/f/d) with a focus on commercial properties! You take on a central role in the successful management of a demanding portfolio. You are the first contact person for our business customers, ensure the smooth operation of the properties and contribute with your expertise and your sense of economic connections significantly to the value of the properties. If you enjoy complex leases, long-term customer relationships and a responsible job in a professional environment, this position is the ideal next step in your career. This position must be filled by 01.02.2027 or by agreement.

  • Implementation of various administrative tasks in the field of commercial and technical management
  • Active participation in vacancy management through participation in visits of apartments and ancillary objects with potential interested parties in the context of re-letting
  • Responsible for drawing up rental contracts for dwellings and ancillary properties, including related annexes and supplements
  • Support for the acceptance of apartments and ancillary objects, partially independent implementation of these processes
  • Preparation of final statements for tenants: inside
  • Participation in debtor and creditor management in the real estate sector
  • Maintenance of written and oral communication with tenants:inside, craftsmen:inside, suppliers:inside, offices and housekeepers:in

Your requirements

  • Successfully completed commercial apprenticeship or comparable training in the real estate sector
  • Industry knowledge mandatory
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Good IT application knowledge, especially MS-Office
  • Experience in GREM
  • Organizational talent and exemplary manners
  • Possession of a valid driving licence required

Please send us your complete application documents stating your salary expectations.
