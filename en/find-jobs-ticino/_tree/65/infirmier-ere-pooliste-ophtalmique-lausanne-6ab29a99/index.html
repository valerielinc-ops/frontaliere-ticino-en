Full description

Founded in Lausanne in 1843, the Asylum Foundation of the Blind includes the Jules-Gonin Ophthalmic Hospital, a university department of ophthalmology, a research centre, the Centre pédagogique pour écoles handicapées de la vue (CPHV), the social service, rehabilitation and low vision and the medical-social establishment Clair-Soleil.

More than 670 employees work every day in the service of visual health to offer diagnostic, treatment and accompaniment services to people with vision.

To complete our team (24/24h and 7/7j), we are looking for a

Poolist Nurse

Your Mission

  • Care and support for patients and their families, including paediatrics, outpatients and hospitalizations
  • Contribute and promote interprofessional work, integrated and individualized care
  • Develop nursing, eye care and help promote evidence-based practice
  • Promote clinical evaluation, therapeutic education and develop the autonomous role

Your assets

  • Bachelor of Science HES-SO in Nursing or equivalent title (with Red Cross recognition)
  • Professional experience in acute care, paediatrics an asset
  • Ability to work independently and as a team
  • Decision-making and analysis capacity, good stress management
  • Sense of observation and human relations
  • Good knowledge of common computer tools
  • Mastery of French, German and Italian level B2, English an asset

We offer you

An enriching activity within a dynamic environment where you can showcase your talents, specific training for our mission, salary conditions related to your skills.
