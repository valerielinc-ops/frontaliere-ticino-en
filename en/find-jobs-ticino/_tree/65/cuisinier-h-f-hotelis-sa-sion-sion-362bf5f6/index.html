Full description

Your main tasks will be the setting up, preparation and preparation of the day's dishes. In close cooperation with management, you will perform the tasks assigned to you quickly and professionally. You will be able to assume the proper functioning of the kitchen as a whole in accordance with the applicable HACCP procedures and standards.

You benefit from a cook CFC AND a confirmed experience of at least 5 years as a responsible cook in Switzerland. You are able to take up a position in a kitchen independently and integrate into new people. Of calm and laid character you are dynamic, organized, and have easy contact. You see your job as a passion. You have good stress resistance. Community knowledge is also required. Driver's licence required (delivery by van), you must speak correctly in French (minimum B2).
