Full description

For our customers in the region, we are looking for motivated and reliable sanitary installers for temporary assignments as well as long-term use possibilities.

Their tasks

  • Installation services of sanitary facilities for new and renovated buildings
  • Installation of water, sewage and gas pipelines
  • Installation and installation of lavabos, toilets, showers and other sanitary appliances
  • Service, repair and maintenance work
  • Installation and connection of pipelines
  • Work according to plans and technical specifications
  • Performance of functional and leakage checks
  • Compliance with applicable safety and quality rules

Your profile

  • Completed Sanitary installer or equivalent training beneficial
  • Alternatively, several years of professional experience in the sanitary sector
  • Independent and reliable working method
  • Craftsmanship and technical understanding
  • Good physical condition
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Category B driving licence is advantageous
  • Teamwork, flexibility and responsibility

We offer

  • Varied assignments with different customers
  • Contemporary and fair employment conditions
  • Attractive remuneration according to qualification and experience
  • Personal support throughout the operation
  • Possibility for longer-term assignments
  • In the case of mutual interest, there is the possibility of permanent employment depending on the customer
