Full description

At one of our clients in Neuchâtel and under the direct authority of the Head of Sector, we are looking for a 100% CCV technician. CCV Technician (H/F) As part of your missions, you intervene on industrial, tertiary and hotel sites to ensure:

  • Preventive and curative maintenance, troubleshooting and repairs to the facilities under your contracts;
  • Maintenance of HVAC equipment: cold units, systems with direct release (Split, multi, VRV), fan-convectors, single blocks, cooling towers;
  • Detection and reporting of malfunctions, including those related to energy consumption, in a logic of energy transition;
  • The rigorous administrative monitoring of your interventions via the GMAO;
  • On-site interventions, including as part of the periodic penalty payment service;
  • Processing of non-compliances on the facilities you are responsible for;
  • The strict observance of hygiene and safety rules, ensuring the safety of installations and persons.

What characterises you You have received technical training in the field of CVC (CFC or equivalent) You have strong CVC skills, as well as air and hydraulic skills

  • You are authorized to handle refrigerant fluids in accordance with applicable regulations;
  • You have at least 5 years of experience in a similar function, ideally in the industrial environment;
  • You have a good team spirit, while being able to work independently;
  • You have an excellent sense of service and a good customer relationship.

Here's what we offer you

  • Internal training programme
  • Funding for various training courses
  • Good social benefits

Equals As the leading provider of global solutions for building and energy in Switzerland, we support the transformation and digitisation of our customers and work together on the energy transition.

Our services cover the entire life cycle of buildings, sites, infrastructure and neighbourhoods. As a market leader, our customers benefit from a full range of services from a single point of contact as well as solutions to their projects developed in an integrated and team-based manner.

We have a presence throughout Switzerland and employ 6500 people spread over 140 sites. This enables us to effectively implement both regional and country-wide projects.

In addition to building automation and technology as well as Facility Management, our offer also includes specializations such as energy supply, transport, telecommunications, digitisation and energy optimization. We design, build and implement projects that extend from intelligent buildings to future power plants through tunnels.
