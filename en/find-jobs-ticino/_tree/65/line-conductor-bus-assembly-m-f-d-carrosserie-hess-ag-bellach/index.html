Assembly electrician for bus construction (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our modular system “Co-Bolt”. We are the only Swiss bus manufacturer to go far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The culture of innovation is the fuel of our company.

We are looking for several motivated electricians with professional training in electrics/electronics in the 24V electrical department of our bus production.

Are you looking for a varied job in an industrial environment? You will find your new challenge with us! We are looking for motivated specialists who want to take on exciting tasks in the electrification of our innovative electric buses! Your tasks

  • Independent wiring and assembly of various electrical components in and on electric buses according to the diagram
  • Connecting and wiring dashboards, heaters and driver's cabs according to specifications and electrical circuit diagrams
  • If necessary, troubleshooting and rework on already assembled components

Our requirements

  • Completed basic vocational training in an electrical engineering field (electrical fitter, automatic fitter or assembly electrician)
  • Professional experience in assembly
  • Independent and reliable way of working
  • Teamwork and flexibility
  • Interest in learning new things

We offer you

  • Varied and interesting activity for the production of innovative electric buses
  • Internal training and further education
  • Permanent position in a successful, traditional company
  • Flexible working hours
  • Performance-based employment conditions

If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including a letter of motivation via link (Apply now).
