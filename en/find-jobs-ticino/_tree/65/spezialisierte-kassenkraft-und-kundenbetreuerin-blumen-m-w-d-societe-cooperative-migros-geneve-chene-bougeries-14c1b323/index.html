Full description

Are you looking for a versatile position where your rigor and sense of service make the difference? Between flower worlds, customer advice, cashing and managing financial flows, each day is unique. Join our passionate Migros Gradelle team and make a memorable customer experience!

Mansioni

  • You welcome, listen and advise customers.
  • You ensure cash transactions and respond to customer requests efficiently (returns, refunds, claims).
  • You perform the tasks related to the management of a cashier, customer service and flower department. You advise them to help them find the perfect floral compositions for each occasion.
  • You contribute to the good organization of the cashier area, the subitio area and you coordinate the breaks of the cashier team.
  • You work with the team to ensure transparent and seamless communication, ensuring an exceptional customer experience. - You are involved in managing financial flows within the store's safe.

Requisiti

  • 1-3 years of experience: in the field of retail with customer contact
  • Completed professional learning (CFC): Retail Manager
  • Sourure, versatility, patience and rigour
  • Excellent communication skills and natural ease in customer contacts
  • Solutions orientation, great ability to keep calm, comfortable in a dynamic environment, stress resistance
  • French (Good control)

Cosa offriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contribution from 20 years doubled by Migros and possibility of early retirement from 58 years.
  • Restoration: Reduction of meal of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cum Points (2008): Reduction of 2% and 5% according to assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.
