Full description

Together with us, you are shaping the future of two important locations: together with the overall project manager, you will promote Dreispitz Nord and the revitalization of Claramarkt. The two projects comprise an investment volume in the three-digit million range and are to be realized within eight years.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Basel, BZM GMBS, Ruchfeldstrasse 15, 4142 Munichstein

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • In close coordination with the overall project manager, you control two large construction projects with an investment volume in the three-digit million range. You share responsibility for the budget and keep an eye on deadlines, costs and quality
  • You design your area of responsibility independently, make decisions within the scope of your competences and proactively advance your topics
  • You control your projects from the first feasibility study through planning and approval to implementation and handover
  • You coordinate internal and external stakeholders and ensure smooth cooperation with specialist areas, authorities, planners and entrepreneurs
  • You are responsible for awards, contract negotiations and the choice of the appropriate commissioning model
  • You recognize risks early, initiate the right measures and provide transparent decision-making basis and reports

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: Project manager in the client representation, ideally at an institutional client or real estate company
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): civil engineer, architect or equivalent training in the construction sector
  • You have managed large, complex construction projects over several SIA phases and are familiar with investment volumes in the three-digit million range
  • You are familiar with Swiss building standards and have solid knowledge of work contract, planner and building law
  • You are well versed in dealing with approval procedures and authorities at the municipal and cantonal level
  • You work safely with MS Project as well as common cost and tendering tools
  • You convince with negotiating skills, entrepreneurial thinking and a pronounced awareness of costs and deadlines
  • You lead several projects in different phases in parallel and set clear priorities
  • German (flowing)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of friamo

  • Modern infrastructure: Inviting and functional office world, modern IT infrastructure
  • Occupational pension fund with above-average performance AN share 8.5%, AG share 17%
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
