Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. Start with us into the future of logistics and assume responsibility.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (Suhr), Wynenfeld, 5034 Suhr

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You monitor the flow of goods on the highly automated system and ensure a high system performance as well as a timely tour skill
  • In case of problems, you analyze the flow of goods and take necessary measures
  • You carry out the order loading, monitor it and escalate in case of a problem
  • You inform stakeholders about the situation in day-to-day business
  • You are responsible for the analysis and solution of plant-related deviations as well as for the processing of back office requests at the sewage treatment site (test and correction station).
  • In a dedicated team, you actively contribute to the realization of optimization processes

Requirements

  • 3 to 5 years: Logistics
  • You find your way around in a complex system landscape
  • You are experienced in handling warehouse management systems and have in-depth knowledge of Microsoft Office
  • You work analytically and process-oriented, think ahead and can communicate complex relationships in an understandable and clear way.
  • You are able to handle physical tasks, such as sorting or aligning pallets.
  • You have good communication skills and master the German language in word and writing
  • You have the SUVA forklift pass or are ready to purchase it
  • You are ready to work in shifts: early, late and night shifts
  • Shift coverage from Monday to Saturday in 24-hour operation (05:00
  • 14:06 / 14:00
  • 23:06 / 22:00
  • 06:15). Seasonal assignments on Sunday
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ)
  • German (very good knowledge)
