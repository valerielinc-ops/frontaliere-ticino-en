Full description

To strengthen our teams in commercial cold in the cantons of Neuchâtel and Freiburg, we are looking for a 100% refrigerant technician.

In our house, you can move things.

  • Conduct preventive and corrective maintenance of cold equipment in accordance with the manufacturer's specifications;
  • Diagnosis and resolve technical problems, including failures, leaks and malfunctions;
  • Collaborate with clients to provide recommendations on necessary improvements and upgrades;
  • Ensure accurate documentation of the interventions, service reports and materials used;
  • Work in accordance with existing safety standards and regulations.

Which is what you do.

  • Minimum of 5 years experience as a refrigerator technician;
  • Knowledge of HFO/CO2/NH3 refrigerant facilities;
  • Ability to read and interpret technical drawings and diagrams;
  • Strong diagnostic and problem-solving skills;
  • Excellent communication skills to interact effectively with clients and the team.

Here's what we offer you.

  • Opportunities for in-house or external in-service training
  • Good social benefits
  • Diversified and stimulating projects
