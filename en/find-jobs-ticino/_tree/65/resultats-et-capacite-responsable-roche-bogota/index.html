The Position As the Outcomes & Capability Lead, you will work with the affiliate’s Enabling Team to provide strategic guidance. Your primary role consists of driving business effectiveness and catalyzing innovation to deliver our Pharma Vision by embedding new skills and innovation frameworks to support team effectiveness and performance, as well as developing the overarching operations framework and fostering collaboration with internal and external stakeholders. Secondly, you will be responsible for defining and implementing the strategy for capability development (mindset, knowledge, and skills) across the organization based on customer needs.

Your Opportunity

The responsibilities for this role include

  • Vision Realization: Drive the realization of our organization’s vision and defined outcomes of the Colombia Enabling Team (CET) while ensuring operational effectiveness of the leadership team.
  • Strategic Partnering: Partner closely with the General Manager and Chapter Leads to enable the achievement of Colombia Outcomes.
  • Ambition Management: Plan, track, and provide project management support to monitor progress and delivery of short-term and long-term ambitions, priorities, and outcomes of the affiliate.

  • Coaching & Ways of Working: Coach, train, and educate teams for high performance, guiding them in finding their own journey to self-organization, self-sufficiency, and adopting new ways of working and business principles.
  • Mindset Transformation: Leverage a range of tools and practices to enable sustainable change of mindsets and behaviors as required on best practice and be a champion of connecting dots and activating the learning loop across the organization.
