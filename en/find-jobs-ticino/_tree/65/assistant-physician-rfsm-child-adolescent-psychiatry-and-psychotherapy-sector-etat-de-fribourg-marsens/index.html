Full description

Post "Assistant physician, RFSM - psychiatry and adult psychotherapy" at the RFSM - Frebourgeois mental health network, Marsens (FR). The RFSM is the mental health centre of the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions.

Who are we?

The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

Your area of activity

  • Care and treatment of patients and assigned to your unit under the direction of Prof. Isabelle Gothuey, Medical Director, and the respective/respective person in charge of the assigned care chain
  • Coordination and monitoring of treatment stages in interdisciplinary setting
  • Active participation in day and night care within the institution

Our offer

  • Recognized post-graduate training institution with different rotation possibilities, validation possibilities for:
  • Title of specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy FMH
  • In-depth training in consulting psychiatry and FMH liaison
  • In-depth training in psychiatry and forensic psychotherapy FMH
  • In-depth training in psychiatry and psychotherapy of FMH addictions
  • In-depth training in psychiatry and psychotherapy of the elderly FMH
  • Attractive conditions of employment (safe work place, pension fund, benefits as State employee)
  • Encouragement and support for post-graduate and continuing training and regular supervision, possibility of scientific research, offer courses in French or German

Profile desired

  • Swiss doctor diploma or equivalent (Mebeko recognition)
  • Willing to be positively involved in the functioning of the RFSM and to preserve its values ( https://www.rfsm.ch/you-are-patient-e-or-un-proche/our-chart-ethics )
  • Very good knowledge of French or German (C1), good knowledge of the other cantonal language desired

Contact details
