Full description

Responsibilities

  • Work directly in the field to analyze and optimize assembly processes, reduce production cycle times, and implement time-study standards
  • Conduct workstation studies and flow analyses to identify waste and propose concrete improvements
  • Perform value stream mappings (VSM) in collaboration with shop-floor teams
  • Evaluate production costs and contribute to planning by defining realistic target standard times
  • Recommend and implement ergonomic, safety, and organizational improvements
  • Define and deploy standardized, sustainable industrial best practices
  • Train and support operators and supervisors in adopting new standards and methods
  • Document processes to ensure operational continuity and knowledge transfer

Profile

  • Engineering degree in mechanical engineering or production engineering
  • Good knowledge of engine manufacturing and assembly processes
  • Skills in time measurement and work-time analysis
  • Fluency in French and knowledge of English and/or German
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team

Our offer

For this position, open only internally, LMB offers internal training to develop skills for the position:

  • REFA Technician in Industrial Engineering
  • Methods Time Measurement (MTM-1, MTM-2 and MTM-UAS)
  • Support for analyses through coaching if necessary

A training agreement covering the costs will be established between LMB and the person who will hold this position for a period of 3 years.
