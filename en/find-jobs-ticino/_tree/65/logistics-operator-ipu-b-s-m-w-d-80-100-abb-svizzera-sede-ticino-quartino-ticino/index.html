Your r ole and r esponsibilities

In this role, you will have the opportunity to carry out all activities related to the handling of parts, semi-finished products, and finished goods within the warehouse and between the warehouse and production lines. Each day, you will be responsible for sorting, storing, and organizing materials according to established guidelines. You will also support a variety of warehouse operations by performing routine tasks in line with defined procedures.

The work model for the role is: Onsite

This role is contributing to the IPU B&S team in Quartino , Switzerland .

You will be mainly accountable for

- Sorting and arranging materials for storage according to established guidelines.

- Executing routine warehouse and logistics activities in line with defined procedures.

- Picking components from stock for dispatch and preparing final palletized shipments.

- Transporting boxes and carrying out various receiving, storage, inventory, and warehouse-related activities.

  • Qualifications for the role

- Experience in warehouse and logistics operations.

- Good knowledge of SAP.

- Experience with material handling, picking, packing, shipping, and inventory management.

- Ability to work accurately, prioritize tasks effectively, and collaborate within a team environment.

- Forklift license is an advantage.

- Good communication skills in Italian; basic English skills are preferred.
