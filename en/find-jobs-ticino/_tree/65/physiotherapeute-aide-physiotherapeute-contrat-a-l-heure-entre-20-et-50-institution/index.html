Full description

Physiotherapist/physiotherapist (contract per hour, between 20% and 50%), 20% to 50% Are you looking for a dynamic and caring working environment where your physiotherapy expertise can be leveraged? Plein Soleil, one of the departments of the Institution of Lavigny, is a unique medical-social establishment in the Canton of Vaud, with more than 40 years of experience in the treatment and support of people with neurological diseases. Located in the upper part of Lausanne, Plein Soleil offers a place of life and care adapted to each person welcomed, providing them with a comprehensive and personalized care to meet their specific needs, depending on the type of reception (accommodation or outpatient). The ambulatory centre of Plein Soleil offers a rich range of medical-therapeutic services (physiotherapy, occupational therapy, neuropsychology, logopedia, dietetics and medical). In order to ensure a replacement within our team, we are looking for a physiotherapist or physiotherapist in December 2026. The post is open to different profiles, including advanced physiotherapy students in their curriculum.

Physiotherapist / Physiotherapist (contract per hour, between 20% and 50%)

Your mission

  • Provide specific, individualised and high-quality physiotherapeutic services in accordance with the values of the Institution, in order to ensure optimal care for patients at the outpatient centre, the beneficiaries of the Temporary Reception Centre and residents of Plein Soleil.
  • As a physiotherapist: take care of your own patientele independently.
  • As a physiotherapist or advanced student: perform treatments under the supervision of a senior physiotherapist of the team.
  • Join a team of physiotherapists with expertise in neurorehabilitation, who can support and advise you when needed.

Your profile

  • You have a degree in physiotherapy (recognition by the Swiss Red Cross is required for foreign degrees. In case of a positive pre-check, a suitable engagement may be considered, or you are a student in well advanced physiotherapy in your curriculum (minimum 3rd year Bachelor)
  • You have an experience (including training) or a keen interest in neurological rehabilitation.
  • You appreciate the interdisciplinary team work and are curious about new technologies.
  • You have autonomy, good adaptability and organization, and natural relational ease.

By joining our team, you will benefit from

  • A stimulating, human and varied working environment.
  • The support of a team experienced in neurorehabilitation, with supervision by a senior physiotherapist for physiotherapists and students.
  • Easy access to continuing training and welfare benefits.
  • Advantages related to sustainable mobility (reductions on public transport tickets and possibly parking space).
  • A restaurant of quality hotel, at preferential rates.

Practical information

  • Contract paid per hour, from December 2026 (date to be agreed).
  • Activity rate: 20% minimum, which can be increased depending on your availability. (50% to be filled)
  • Place of work : Lausanne
  • Information: Ms. Aline Gronchi-Perrin, Head of the Medical and Therapeutic Sector – Tel. : 021 651 28 73

If you are ready to meet this challenge and help provide quality support to people with neurological diseases, apply now and join our dynamic team.
