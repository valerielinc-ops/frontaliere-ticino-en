Automatician:in / Business Electrician:in \- VALSER (m/f/d) / Vals

The opportunity to make a significant contribution to advancing our world-class business and delivering iconic drinks to customers and consumers who have a big/strong relationship with our brands. You will benefit from a family-like environment, full of supportive colleagues who will help you overcome major challenges to achieve results you could not have imagined. No day will be like the other. You will be challenged, you will be encouraged and you will grow.

As an automatic engineer, you will carry out the electrical maintenance and maintenance of the plant and infrastructure at the Vals site within the defined guidelines.

Your new tasks:

  • Performing all electrical maintenance and maintenance work
  • Fixing disruptions to facilities and infrastructure, partly also to weekend deployments
  • Repair and dismantle operations and infrastructure
  • Build, calibrate and test new facilities in close cooperation with the production team
  • Report the work carried out and ensure efficient cooperation and communication, especially within production
  • Implementation of improvement measures (process flows, automation, etc.)
  • Instruct production staff for simple disruption and maintenance work on the plants
  • Creating and maintaining cross-divisional contacts with production employees, suppliers and external fitters
  • Ensuring compliance with occupational safety and health requirements

Are your secrets ingredients?

  • Completed vocational training as an electrical installer, automatic technician or similar training
  • Expertise in signal, control and control technology
  • Experience in SPS STEP7/TIA is an advantage
  • Reliable, independent and precise working methods paired with
  • Flexibility and resilience
  • Quick comprehension, organizational flair as well as strong service and team thinking
  • Willingness to do shift work and piquetting
  • Location: Accessibility of production in 30\-45 minutes
  • Experience in dealing with SAP and MS Office
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Please apply online and provide us with your complete application documents incl. Letters of motivation and employment reports in a single PDF. We do not accept applications via email. For this position, we do not receive dossiers from recruitment agencies.
