Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

The family practice Praxis à la Gare, centrally located at the Biel station in the MEDIN health centre, serves a catchment area with 70,000 inhabitants and 60,000 commuters.In addition to basic care including a large laboratory and conventional X-ray, our practice offers quick access to a variety of family medical services and emergency care (walk-in). Thanks to our close cooperation with the other MEDIN units at the site, we guarantee comprehensive medical care. Our multilingual family doctors also use an internal network to arrange special treatments such as nutritional advice, physiotherapy, radiology, gynecology or cardiology directly in the same building. In order to strengthen our committed and dynamic team, we are looking for several committed teams immediately (temporary, Try&Hire also possible).

Medical practice assistants (MPA), 50-100 %

You do

  • Reception, care and administrative reception of our patients
  • Appointments and coordination of office hours
  • Ensuring a friendly and professional patient reception
  • Support for administrative activities
  • Performing medical activities such as blood sampling, infusions, dressing changes, pulmonary function tests and other diagnostic examinations
  • Performance of ECGs, 24-hour blood pressure measurements and digital X-ray
  • Cooperation in the in-house laboratory including sampling and preparation of external analyses
  • Management of the practice pharmacy including ordering and warehouse management
  • Shared responsibility for smooth and efficient practical operation

This sets you apart

  • Completed training as medical practice assistant EFZ or comparable training
  • Professional experience in a medical practice or group practice is beneficial
  • Very good language skills in German or French with good knowledge of the other language
  • In-depth computer skills (experience with the practical software CuraMed is an advantage)
  • Joy in dealing with patients and teamwork
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Discretion, sense of responsibility, empathy and resilience

You will benefit from this

  • Modern practice space with contemporary infrastructure
  • Varied and responsible activities
  • Support for professional training and development opportunities
  • Attractive employment conditions and above-average benefits
  • 6 weeks holidays
  • Centrally located workplace directly at the station Biel/Bienne
