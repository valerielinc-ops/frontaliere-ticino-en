Full description

Is technology your passion? Then move with us big things. You don’t just screw machines – you make sure that the latest construction machines perform at their highest levels every day. Whether new equipment, complex repairs or complete overhaul: We offer you a versatile working day with state-of-the-art technology, exciting challenges and a team that supports each other. If you like to work independently, take responsibility and achieve visible results, you are exactly right with us. These are your tasks

  • You ensure that our new, used, rental and customer equipment is ready for use at all times – from deployment to complete revision.
  • You analyze disturbances, make diagnoses and get machines running quickly.
  • You carry out service, maintenance and repair work on the latest construction machinery technology.
  • You equip machines with special bodies such as hydraulic hammers, concrete hammers, quick changers or LIKUFIX systems.
  • You work in a structured way, document your assignments and contribute to a professional and well-organized workshop. You bring that with you
  • Completed training as construction machinery mechanic EFZ, agricultural machinery mechanic EFZ or truck mechanic EFZ
  • Experience in construction machinery or related vehicle technology is advantageous
  • Category B/BE driving licence
  • Enjoying modern technology and complex technical challenges
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Team spirit, sense of responsibility and a high level of customer orientation
  • Basic knowledge of the common PC applications You can look forward to
  • Versatile tasks in a highly equipped workshop
  • A motivated team that tackles together
