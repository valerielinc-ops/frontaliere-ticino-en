Move Switzerland with us. Lead Production logistics and location development (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, 01.03.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You enjoy the development of strategic topics in close cooperation with employees from the operative business and you are able to recognize the connections between qualitative basics and existing business data and to draw your conclusions from this? Then the position as specialist leader in production logistics and location development (m/f/d) in an interdisciplinary team at SBB Infrastructure Availability and Maintenance offers you an exciting career step. We are looking for a solution-oriented personality with high social and methodological competence, who leads topics and interfaces confidently and effectively shapes change processes. The Infrastructure Availability and Maintenance division is responsible for monitoring, suppressing and maintaining SBB’s railway infrastructure. As specialist leader (m/f/d), you are responsible for the further development of the logistics processes in the production of the business unit. In close cooperation with operational and tactical units, you will develop strategies for the development of locations and parking facilities. At the same time, you are close to operational issues in day-to-day business. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • You will lead the development of a business unit location and shutdown strategy to optimize production logistics over different time horizons from short to long term. In doing so, you take into account the production conditions of the business unit operating throughout Switzerland and draw the right conclusions on the basis of qualitative and quantitative analyses.
  • Necessary adjustments to standards and processes can be recognized independently. Through discussions and the analysis of relevant data sources, you will gain a sound understanding of the processes in the maintenance of the railway infrastructure.
  • The implementation of changes can be achieved through targeted interface management with various partners within the SBB infrastructure.
  • When building new buildings or renewing locations and parking facilities, you act as the central contact person for the operational representatives in the projects and ensure compliance with standards throughout Switzerland.
  • You act as a consumer: in the targeted expansion of charging infrastructure for the future electric rail vehicle fleet for the maintenance of the SBB infrastructure.
  • In operational business, you technically manage the scheduling and capacity management of the existing parking facilities (processes, tools). You lead the Swiss-wide community of track planners:innen.

You bring that with you.

  • You have a very independent, goal-oriented working method, actively assume your responsibility within your field of activity and are strong in stakeholder management. Your pronounced analytical, conceptual and methodological skills will help you, also with regard to professional leadership.
  • In cooperation with different stakeholders, you communicate openly and profitably, act affable and convince through your assertiveness. Your addressees include both employees: women at operational level and higher levels of management.
  • Due to your experienced use of Excel, Power BI & Co, it is easy for you to analyze data and process it in an understandable way.
  • Experience in strategy/location/capacity planning, asset/process management or industrial operation/maintenance are beneficial.
  • You have a degree FH/uni in engineering, industrial engineering, logistics, business sciences or the like.
  • In the German language, you communicate with confidence in negotiation (C1) and you master another national language at level B1 (French or Italian).
