Full description

For a modern, cross-locational institute for radiology and nuclear medicine in the city of Zurich, we are looking for 1. September 2026 or by appointment a dedicated senior physician or senior physician radiology with a focus on breast imaging, 80-100%. With around 200,000 examinations and interventions every year, the institute is one of the largest hospital radiologies in the Canton of Zurich. What awaits you in this challenging task

  • You strengthen the team in breast imaging with your expertise and assume an important role in a clinically relevant focus area
  • You work closely with the head of mammary imaging and actively shape the further development of breast imaging
  • You are active in the clinically linked early detection of breast cancer as well as in the diagnostic clarification and bioptic confirmation of conspicuous findings
  • They maintain a close interdisciplinary collaboration with gynecology, oncology, tumor boards and external assignees
  • They remain broadly integrated in general radiological terms and participate in clinical operations incl. Emergency radiology and, if interested, develop further areas of focus, especially in the field of female imaging
  • You are happy to engage in the further education of assistant doctors

This is what they bring with them.

  • Swiss or Swiss recognised specialist title for radiology
  • Experience in breast imaging or a clear interest in specializing specifically in this area
  • Broad general radiological expertise and enjoyment of working in a clinically versatile center hospital
  • Knowledge of radiology of the female pelvis or interest in female imaging is beneficial

That sets them apart.

  • Careful, patient-oriented working method with high professional quality standards
  • Clear, binding and appreciative communication
  • Strong team orientation as well as enjoyment of design, development and responsibility
  • The desire to actively shape an area with a future

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Attractive, technically viable position in an institute of continuing education category A for radiology
  • Perspective for further development towards conduction function in breast imaging with appropriate fit
  • Collegial, family working atmosphere with an open du-culture
  • 13. Monthly wage according to transparent urban wage system, fair and non-discriminatory remuneration
  • Above average pension benefits
  • Generous parental leave arrangements and city-run daycare centres
  • Center hospital among the top 10 Swiss hospitals
