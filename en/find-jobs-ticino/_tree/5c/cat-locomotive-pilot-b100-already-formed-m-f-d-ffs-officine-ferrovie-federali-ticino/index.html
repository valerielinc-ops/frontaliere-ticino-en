Place

Denges, CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you can implement major changes and thus make a decisive contribution to SBB Cargo. In collaboration with your team's colleagues, you develop and implement innovative solutions to meet existing challenges. We offer a wide range of continuing training to support your professional development. What you can move.

  • Movement of goods to ensure quality of life in Switzerland.
  • Make sure along the Swiss railway lines that our customers' goods arrive at their destination safely and on time.
  • Lead the convoy and respond to the safety and punctuality of freight trains on a busy rail network.
  • To act as a specialist in safe, appropriate (articulate) and ecological driving of different freight trains.
  • Conduct complex manoeuvring movements, act autonomously, capture business and operating data in digital systems and ensure customer relationship in this context.

What you bring.

  • Cat. B100 locomotive driver licence.
  • Provision for necessary training, such as remote control and level 3 technical control.
  • Meticulity, reliability, team spirit and team layout (over 24 hours).
  • Physical and psychic resistance; independent and responsible working methods; ability to act correctly and deliberately.
  • Oral and written understanding of French (mother tongue level); knowledge of a second national language (certified level A1).
  • Arrangement to work on irregular schedules and on weekends.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
