The Position As Sales Analyst (d/f/m), you will lead the strategic analysis and financial planning of the Neurology and Cancer of the Breast portfolios by working closely with the transverse teams to provide a complete view of performance (sales, KPIs) and guide investment decisions. You will also lead the sales forecasts to match them with the forecast spending, and contribute to the overall sales consolidation for the entire Roche France portfolio.

This role is not only a position, but a stepping stone to a long-term potential career in financial leadership at Roche. We offer accelerated development through immersive learning experiences, global missions and cultural commitment.

Why join Roche as part of this role / initiative?

  • Strategic Impact: Play a crucial role in formulating financial strategies and decisions that drive our business forward;
  • Global Opportunities: Participate in missions offering an international exhibition, expanding your professional network and experience. Take advantage of our strong development plan, including local and global projects, designed to improve your skills and accelerate your professional growth;
  • Inclusive culture: Be part of a diverse and inclusive environment that values different perspectives and encourages innovation.

Your main responsibilities

  • Collaborate with Finance Enterprise Partners, business leaders and members of the Neurology transverse team to provide strategic information and support decision-making regarding sales performance;
  • Work closely with Insights and Analytics to provide a complete view of Neurology performance (sales data, qualitative insights, field activity);
  • Work closely with the Finance Manager to provide a view of product sales performance in breast cancer;
  • To advise and assist the Neurology team in making informed decisions on sales management, key performance indicators (KPI) and investments;
  • Lead sales forecasts (volumes and net sales) and necessary investments over a three-year horizon, transforming commercial assumptions and current product performance into relevant and well-founded sales forecasts, and presenting them to the executive committee of the subsidiary if necessary for the Neurology and Breast Cancer portfolios;
  • Associate these sales forecasts with the expenditure forecasts over the same period, based on the analysis of the activities carried out, the current action plans and the evolving strategies;
  • In addition to your business responsibilities, work closely with the Analyst Business Planning responsible for the consolidated view of net sales for the entire Roche France portfolio. Contribute by providing and conducting short-, medium- and long-term analyses, as well as sales journal planning.

Your profile

  • You have first experience consulting, market research and sales forecasts, ideally in the pharmaceutical industry;
  • You are curious and possess a strong analytical mind with excellent deductive abilities. You have the ability to self-assess yourself
