Experienced CFC Automatician (M/F) back to list In order to strengthen our teams, we are looking for an experienced CFC automatic operator (M/F) Job description

Your mission

  • Design, model and commission automated installations and their electrical controls (robots, elevators, sliding doors, etc.)
  • Programming and setting automation to optimize the operation and energy efficiency of machines
  • Diagnosis and resolve electrical and automated failures
  • Participate in continuous improvement and investment projects
  • Collaborate with production teams, methods and engineering
  • Write technical documentation (schedules, operating procedures, reports, etc.)
  • Provide technical support to operators and train users

Profile

Profile desired

  • Holder of a (M/F) automated CFC, required
  • Confirmed experience from 3 to 5 years minimum in an industrial environment
  • Solid knowledge in wiring, electrical assembly and technical diagram reading
  • Experience in commissioning, troubleshooting and maintenance of automated equipment
  • Knowledge of programmable controllers (Siemens, Beckhoff, Schneider or equivalent), an asset
  • Good diagnostic skills for electrical, pneumatic and automated faults
  • Autonomy, methodical mind and orientation solutions
  • Ability to work as a team and to intervene effectively in the industrial, automated production or technical maintenance environment
