Full description

You have a passion for high-quality meat products and want to contribute your expertise? As Head of Meat, you play a central role in the management and organization of our meat department. Are you a responsible person with leadership experience and enthusiasm for the food industry? We look forward to your application!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Basel, Station SBB Basel, Centralbahnstrasse 10, 4051 Basel

Important information

Working days/times: Monday to Sunday between 05:15 and 22:10 We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Professional leadership of a small team
  • Staff deployment planning and monitoring
  • Preparation and presentation of the meat plates for commercial sale in compliance with hygiene requirements
  • Disposal, presentation and maintenance of goods
  • Quality, data and freshness control and inventory management

Requirements

  • 2-3 years of professional experience: as a butcher
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): butcher
  • Reliability, flexibility and high operational readiness
  • Customer-oriented personality with pleasure in sales
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Occupational pension fund with above-average performance AN share 8.5%, AG share 17%
  • Social benefits: In case of illness, the salary is 100% for two years, in case of accident we are insured SUVA
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
  • Employee offers: Various offers, discounts and preferential conditions

Contatto

Application and contact Enya Röthlisberger
