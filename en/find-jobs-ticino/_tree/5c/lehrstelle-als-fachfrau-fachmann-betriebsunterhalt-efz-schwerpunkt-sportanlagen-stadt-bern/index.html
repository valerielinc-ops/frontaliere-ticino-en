Full description

Do you like to get involved and learn how sports and leisure facilities are professionally operated, maintained and maintained so that they can be used safely and smoothly?

Then apply now! We look forward to your application documents. Tasks: • You operate, control and maintain machines and equipment as well as sanitary, ventilation and bath water systems including pumps and measurement and control technology • You build ice sheets and nurture them • You do the Life Saving Brevet and supervise the bathers in the indoor and outdoor pools • You maintain football fields and other green areas • You take care of cleaning work and waste management • You pay attention to the work safety of employees Requirements • You have completed secondary school or real school with good grades • You are physically fit and can swim well • You are interested in craft activities inside and outside and are skilled at crafting • You enjoy dealing with people • You are capable of teamwork, independent and reliable We offer: Diverse and fair More than 20 exciting apprenticeships await you with us – from crafts to administrative. As a versatile training company, we value the different role models and pay all apprenticeships the same wage – and learners from 25 even a little more. The city of Bern is committed to sustainable vocational training and offers you a wide range of opportunities for your career. Around 200 apprentices are currently completing their training in over 20 different professions – accompanied by more than 300 experienced employees who pass on their knowledge and enthusiasm. With us you not only learn your dream job, but also actively shape life in our city. Become part of a team that works with joy, responsibility and heart for Bern.
