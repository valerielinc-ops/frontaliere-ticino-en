Full description

You want to make buildings safer - and take responsibility yourself? ####

At a specialized engineering office for fire protection planning, you will accompany challenging building construction projects from the concept phase to commissioning.

The main tasks ####

  • Project management of fire protection projects in all phases
  • Advising architects and specialist planners
  • Coordination with competent fire protection authorities
  • Conduct of inspection visits on construction sites
  • Organisation of fire safety commissioning and quality management

The requirement profile ####

  • Completed studies or technical training
  • Flowing knowledge of German
  • Professional experience in construction planning or execution, ideally experience in managing large projects
  • Knowledge of fire protection is advantageous, ideally fire protection expert
  • Presentation talent, personal responsibility and teamwork
