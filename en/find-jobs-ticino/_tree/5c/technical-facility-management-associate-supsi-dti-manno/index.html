# Collaborator / Technical Collaborator / Facility Management

The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI) puts in competition a position of

Collaborator/Technical Machine/Facility Management

at the

Real Estate & Facility Management Service (RE& FM)

, with a degree of employment of 100% and entered service in February 2027 or date to be agreed. The selected person will contribute to the daily management of the SUPSI offices of Luganese, with a task mainly on the field of custody, ordinary maintenance and logistic support.

Main responsibility

  • Perform small maintenance, repair, installation and assembly.
  • Perform periodic checks on buildings and technical installations, perform preventive maintenance and report anomalies that require specialist interventions.
  • Check the opening and closing of the buildings and collaborate on access management.
  • Set up and reconfigure classrooms, offices, laboratories and event spaces; move furniture, materials and equipment.
  • Manage warehouses, warehouses and consumables.
  • Collaboration with the care of external spaces and waste management.
  • Provide operational support to external companies.

Requirements

  • Federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as an operator/processor of buildings and infrastructures or in an artisanal or technical field (e.g. electrician, installer/health machine, carpenter, gardener, mechanic/a); profiles with similar professional experience will be evaluated on dossiers.
  • Explain manual skills and practical experience in maintenance and repair.
  • Autonomy in the organization of work and in the resolution of simple technical problems.
  • Spirit of service, reliability and attitude to teamwork.
  • Basic computer knowledge.
  • Excellent knowledge of the Italian language.
  • License to conduct cat. B.
  • Availability to move between SUPSI locations.
  • Availability to occasionally serve outside ordinary hours (events, movings, extraordinary situations).

Constitute preferential title

  • Federal Professional Certificate (APF) of Housing.
  • Safety training at work, fire or emergency management.
  • Experience with access control systems or maintenance management software.
  • Knowledge of a second national language and/or English.

We offer

  • Indefinite contract.
  • The position is in Group 2 of the SUPSI collective labour agreement (CCLSUPSI).
  • Activities vary in a university context, within a team dedicated to managing the SUPSI real estate.
  • Possibility of continuing training.

Incomplete applications, sent to other addresses or beyond the agreed deadline will not be considered. For information contact Livio Berardo , Responsible for Real Estate & Facility Management of SUPSI.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
