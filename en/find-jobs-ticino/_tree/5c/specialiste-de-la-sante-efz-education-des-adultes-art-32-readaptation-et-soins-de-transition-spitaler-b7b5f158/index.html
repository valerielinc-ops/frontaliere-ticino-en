Full description

During this two-year training, you will work at the Geriatric Rehabilitation and Transitional Care Station of the Cantonal Hospital Schaffhausen in order to learn the tasks of a health professional. You will be accompanied and supported by your day caregivers every day. In addition, you can focus on individual activities during regular learning days with education and specialists. After your successful apprenticeship, you are highly trained and can do the tasks of a health professional independently.

All fees and expenses are covered by us as a training company.

Requirements for the start of training:
  • A job of 6 to 12 months in the Cantonal Hospital Schaffhausen, depending on professional experience
  • This employment serves as the basis for an aptitude test by us in order to decide whether we want you for adult education according to Art. 32 BBV recommend or allow
Tasks: • Professional care and care of sick and elderly people • Interesting nursing and medical tasks • Domestic and administrative activities • Responsibility for cleaning, disinfecting and operability of instruments and apparatus as well as material procurement Requirements • At least 3 years of full-time professional experience • Older than 25 years • Workload of at least 80% and maximum 100%, supplemented by one school day per week (20%) • Good knowledge of German (at least B2) We offer: Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well. The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
