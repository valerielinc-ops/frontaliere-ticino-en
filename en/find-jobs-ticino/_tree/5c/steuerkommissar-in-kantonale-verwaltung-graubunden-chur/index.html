Full description

The tax administration is looking for an active and motivated person, starting immediately or by agreement. We offer comprehensive onboarding into an interesting and demanding field of activity and a collegial working environment. Tasks: Determination of tax factors for income and wealth tax of self-employed individuals in the agricultural sector

  • Accounting audits in the agricultural sector
  • Written or personal clarifications with taxpayers, tax advisors and authorities
  • Processing of appeals, handling of objections, participation in consultations regarding appeals and complaints in court. Requirements: Higher education in business administration (e.g. trustee with federal certificate, specialist in finance and accounting with federal certificate or equivalent qualification) and preferably knowledge in agriculture
  • Several years of professional experience, preferably in accounting, fiduciary and/or tax services related to agriculture
  • Quick comprehension, fluency in oral and written expression in German
  • Knowledge of Romansh and Italian is an advantage
  • Willingness to make decisions
  • Negotiation skills and assertiveness combined with good manners and a confident presence
  • Professional commitment and willingness to continue education
  • Resilience
  • Organizational skills
  • Teamwork ability
  • Discretion and reliability.
