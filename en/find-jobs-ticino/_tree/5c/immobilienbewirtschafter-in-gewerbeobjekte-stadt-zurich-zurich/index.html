Full description

In our management department, we are looking for a real estate manager for a portfolio of commercial properties in the Gastronomy and Retail team.

  • Managing a portfolio of retail space
  • Preparation of rentals including contract negotiations and drawing up lease, lease, land and utility loan contracts
  • Ensure and coordinate ongoing maintenance and create and monitor budget
  • Participation in construction projects in cooperation with our portfolio management and the Office for Building Constructions
  • Responsible for rental interest calculations, rental interest adjustments and incidental cost statements
  • Processing of legal and insurance cases in cooperation with our legal department and the municipal insurance department
  • Eidg. Professional property management card
  • Commercial property management experience
  • Good knowledge of tenancy law and construction
  • High service and customer orientation
  • Sociability, high social skills and communication skills
  • Solid computer science user knowledge, also beneficial with SAP

a diverse and collegial working environment in a growing organization. Our employees benefit from attractive employment conditions and good social benefits. The centrally located workplace at Hallwylplatz and a wide range of training and further education are further qualities.

Properties for a livable city of Zurich

With its portfolio of 5.8 billion francs in insurance value, the City of Zurich is one of the major real estate organizations in Switzerland. In addition to the management of around 10,300 apartments and 1,600 commercial premises, we are responsible for the comprehensive ownership representation, the long-term value preservation and the further development of this versatile portfolio. In addition, we manage the car parks, the municipal land reserves and building rights and are responsible for municipal real estate transactions.
