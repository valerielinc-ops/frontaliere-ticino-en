Arosa Energie is an independent public institution and belongs to the municipality of Arosa. It employs 23 people who are responsible for secure power supply and the operation of the hydropower plants in the region.

For our metrology, we are looking for a tech-savvy personality who wants to combine electrical engineering, IT and energy data.

With us, you accompany the entire process – from the installation of the smart meter to the provision of data for energy billing.

Specialist in metrology 80–100 %

Your duties

  • Assembly, connection and commissioning of smart meters
  • Parameterisation and troubleshooting of measuring instruments and communications
  • Operation and support of our smart metering systems HES, MDM and EDM
  • Monitoring and ensuring measurement data quality
  • Support of the data chain from smart meters to billing
  • Further development and automation of measurement processes
  • Willingness to cooperate in the Pikettdienst with attractive additional remuneration
  • Depending on the training, participation in control and reporting

This is what you bring with you

  • Training as an electrical installer, automatic technician or equivalent basic electrotechnical training
  • Enjoyment of a varied activity between electronics and IT
  • Interest in smart metering, data communication and modern IT systems
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Good IT\ user knowledge
  • Category B driving licence
  • Further training as an electrical project manager/in installation and safety / electrical safety consultant/in is an advantage
  • Experience with Smart Metering, HES, MDM or EDM is welcome, but not a requirement

You can look forward to this

  • Versatile activity with modern smart metering technology
  • High independence and short decision-making paths
  • Attractive compensation and benefits (check, discounted e\-car charging, telephone compensation, assumption risk BVG etc., special social allowance for families with children, benefit premium)
  • Support for professional training
  • Accommodation on request
  • Work and live in Arosa – in the middle of one of the most attractive and leisure regions in Switzerland
  • Skiing, biking and hiking right on the doorstep – and Chur can be reached by car in around 45 minutes
