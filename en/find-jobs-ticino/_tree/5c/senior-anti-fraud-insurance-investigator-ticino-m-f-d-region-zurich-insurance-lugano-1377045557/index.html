Full description

138143 | 80 - 100% | Lugano, Ticino | Hybrid

Are you motivated by the idea of finding complex cases of suspected fraud and actively contributing to the fight against insurance abuse? Would you like to make available your investigative experience and analytical skills to find fair solutions and protect our honest customers?

In this role you will strengthen the Sinistri sector of Zurich Switzerland within the Unit specialized in the fight against insurance fraud (LFA). You will examine complex sinister cases, characterized by suspects of fraud in the insurance of things and civil liability, autonomously conducting investigations both nationally and internationally. In collaboration with a team of expert specialists, you will help to quickly identify fraud cases, ensure the facts professionally and further develop effective prevention measures.

What's waiting for you

  • You will self-examine complex left-hand cases with suspicion of insurance fraud in matters and civil liability insurance.
  • You will analyze the facts, carry out targeted investigations and develop effective and practice-oriented solutions.
  • You will communicate professionally with all parties involved and represent Zurich's interests in civil and criminal proceedings.
  • You will work closely with specialized internal services and external partners, ensuring accurate and transparent case management.
  • You will provide advice and training to our employees in the management of the claims, helping them to recognize suspicious situations of fraud and to take appropriate measures.

What makes you stand out

  • Multiannual experience in judicial police investigations or in the fight against insurance fraud and solid skills in the professional conduct of investigations.
  • Exploited analytical and interdisciplinary reasoning skills as well as an approach to structured, autonomous and oriented work in search of solutions.
  • Ability of negotiation, determination and authority, accompanied by a sure and respectful attitude in relations with interlocutors of different nature.
  • High sense of responsibility, integrity and discretion, as well as a strong focus on the quality of work and customer satisfaction.
  • Excellent mastery of Italian and German languages, both written and spoken, as well as ability to document complex situations in a clear, precise and understandable way.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
