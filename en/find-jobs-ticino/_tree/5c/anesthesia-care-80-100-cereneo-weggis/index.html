Full description

cereneo – Centre for Neurology and Rehabilitation is a world-leading centre in the field of neurological rehabilitation. Our location on the beautiful Lake Vierwaldstättersee offers not only a breathtaking view of Pilatus and Rigi, but also a highly modern, accessible infrastructure. We focus on excellence, warmth, motivation, teamwork and innovation – values that determine our daily actions and ensure the quality of our care. For our location in Weggis-Hertenstein, we are looking for an experienced anesthesia care expert as of July 2027 for a versatile 100% function with an application of approximately 50% in the surgical / anesthesia area and 50% at the IMC.

Tasks

  • Varied activity in a combined function with an application of approximately 50% in the surgical/anaesthesia area and 50% in the Intermediate Care Unit (IMC)
  • Independent and competent care of patients in the context of anesthesia and at the IMC
  • You are responsible for the independent execution, monitoring and control of anesthesia under the responsibility of the anesthesiologist
  • Monitoring and care of patients before, during and after surgical or interventional interventions
  • Active participation in the development and development of our new operational area, which will open in 2027
  • Close interprofessional cooperation with the medical service, nursing and other specialist areas

What should you bring?

  • For this versatile function we are looking for a Dipl. Expert (see title), who moves safely in the anesthesia and surgical environment as well as in the care and monitoring of patients on an IMC
  • Several years of professional experience and high professional competence in anesthesia care
  • Experience in the care of IMC or intensive care patients is beneficial
  • Enjoyment of a combined activity with approximately 50% use in the operating room and 50% at the IMC
  • Self-employed, responsible and structured working methods
  • Flexibility and pleasure in working together in an interprofessional and dynamic environment
  • Willingness to actively shape the interface between anesthesia and IMC and to contribute to the further development of the corresponding structures and processes

We offer

  • Modern infrastructure: Perfectly located working environment with a view of Lake Vierwaldstättersee, Pilatus and Rigi.
  • Development: Personal training and development opportunities.
  • Social benefits: Good social benefits (NBU, employer share pension fund 60%).
  • Parking & public transport: Free parking and access to e-charging stations, subsidy for public transport.
  • Food: Lunch check with 50% employer participation, free coffee, tea, water & fruit.
  • Kita: Discount for our connected Kita.
  • Discounts: Various discounts for leisure activities.
  • Fitness: Sharing fitness equipment.
  • Our corporate culture:
  • EXCELLENCE: Using the latest research findings and first-class service
  • CHARACTERISTICS: We are always there with all our heart. Of course, this also includes the sincere treatment of our patients: inside, but also between our staff
  • MOTIVATION: Joint goal achievement and support of patients: women to improve their quality of life
  • TEAM WORK: Reliability and mutual support in the team
  • INNOVATION: Research and testing of new therapeutic approaches and devices
