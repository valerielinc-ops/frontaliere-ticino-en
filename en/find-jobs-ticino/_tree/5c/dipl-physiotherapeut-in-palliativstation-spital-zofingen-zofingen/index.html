Full description

Their tasks

  • Physiotherapeutic care of patients in the palliative care unit
  • Participation in interdisciplinary team meetings and quality assurance
  • Implementation of individual treatments in various departments in the acute hospital
  • Outpatient treatments in various specialist areas
  • Participation in group therapies, student education and exciting projects
  • Active exchange in the team, participation in training and internal intervisions

Your profile

  • Completed training as a physiotherapist (SRK-approved) or Bachelor in Physical Therapy
  • Ideally, experience in palliative care – or interest in further education in this field
  • You work resource-oriented, evidence-based and with heart
  • You are flexible, resilient and find joy in everyday hospital life
  • You are digitally fit and appreciate the open exchange in the team
  • Re-entrants to physiotherapy are also welcome

Personal, Family, Competent In the regional and supra-regional health network, the Zofingen Hospital plays an important role.
