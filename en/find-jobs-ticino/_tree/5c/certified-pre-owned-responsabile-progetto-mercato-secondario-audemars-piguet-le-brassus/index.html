Full description

Reporting to the CPO (Certified Pre-Owned) Director, you will be part of the development of a new strategic capability dedicated to understanding and strengthening market dynamics around the brand. As such, you will act as a structured and business-oriented Project Manager to support the deployment of a cross-functional roadmap. You will contribute to the implementation of a global strategic framework aimed at strengthening market visibility, supporting internal alignment, developing organizational capabilities, and preserving, in the long term, the desirability and perceived value of the Maison’s timepieces. This role is at the intersection of strategy, business analysis, project management, stakeholder coordination, and executive reporting, in a rapidly evolving and highly demanding environment.

  • Responsibilities
  • Deploy the global market strategy into structured initiatives (milestones, deliverables, monitoring)
  • Coordinate cross-functional projects ensuring alignment, ownership, and progress tracking
  • Structure and manage project tools (plans, governance, trackers, executive reporting)
  • Develop market intelligence capabilities (dashboards, performance, analyses, benchmarks)
  • Contribute to internal initiatives that enhance understanding of market dynamics and customers
  • Facilitate collaboration between key functions (business, retail, client, product, communication, data)
  • Define and monitor KPIs ensuring clear visibility on performance and impact
  • Prepare support for governance bodies and management (committees and other steering committees)
  • Identify risks and propose solutions to ensure proper execution, while maintaining confidentiality
  • Requirements
  • You hold a higher education degree (business school, engineering, or equivalent)
  • You have proven experience in project management, strategy, consulting, or transformation (luxury, retail, client, or market intelligence)
  • You have a good understanding of the watch industry and its dynamics
  • You are able to structure complex issues and translate strategic vision into concrete action plans
  • You demonstrate excellent analytical skills and ease in handling data and performance indicators
  • You are able to produce clear, concise, and impactful deliverables for senior stakeholders
  • You have worked in matrix and cross-functional environments involving multiple stakeholders
  • You combine strategic thinking with strong execution capabilities, while demonstrating organizational skills in managing multiple initiatives
  • You have perfect command of English
  • What we offer
  • Health insurance coverage (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 weeks of annual vacation and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the accumulation of extra minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The possibility, depending on the role, to work remotely
  • A company restaurant
