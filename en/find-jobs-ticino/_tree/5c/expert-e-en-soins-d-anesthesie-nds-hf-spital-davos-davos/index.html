Full description

Expert nurses and experts in anesthesia NDS HF can handle rapidly changing situations and complex issues. They enjoy independent and interdisciplinary care of people of all ages, with different socio-cultural backgrounds and various degrees of illness and injury. As a regional hospital/acute hospital with an emergency service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for the medical basic care of the Davos population and guests. Just as important to us is the well-being of our employees. Working with us means being in a small but extremely important operation for the region. We maintain a collegial work climate and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities in Davos. ## Tasks

  • Independent performance of general anesthetics under medical supervision
  • Comprehensive care of patients in regional anesthesia
  • Safe preparation and follow-up of all anesthesia forms
  • Participation in the internal pain service and interventions in the shock room
  • Shared responsibility for the efficient design of processes and conscious use of resources ## Requirements
  • Completed training as a dipl. nurse/expert in anesthesia NDS HF or equivalent training and further education
  • Professional experience as a dipl. nurse/expert in anesthesia
  • Responsible, independent and patient-oriented working method
  • Reliable, team-oriented and flexible personality, willing to work in all shifts
  • Very good German skills, good English skills, any other language is an advantage ## What we offer
  • Varied, responsible work
  • Structured, committed, dynamic and collegial team
  • Attractive employment conditions and social benefits; hospital-owned nursery up to kindergarten age
  • Interesting further training and education opportunities ## Contact
