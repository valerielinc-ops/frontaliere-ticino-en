Full description

In the technical environment, you will experience varied activities from assembly to commissioning. The company offers innovative projects and short decision paths for your further development. For our client, we are looking for an activity

  • Installing, wiring and putting electrical switch cabinets into operation
  • Elaborating electrical schemes together with the development
  • Building prototypes, testing functions and recording results
  • accompanying type tests internally and in external test laboratories
  • inspecting goods incoming and carrying out incoming tests
  • Requirements
  • Basic technical training as an automatic technician EFZ, electrical installer EFZ or equivalent qualification
  • Practical experience and solid expertise in the construction of switch cabinets
  • Experience with wiring, testing and commissioning of electrical systems or assemblies is advantageous
  • Knowledge of power electronics or high-voltage technology is welcome
  • Good oral and written German language skills Offer
  • Strong technical environment with short decision-making paths
  • Multifaceted area of responsibility along the entire manufacturing process
  • Innovative technology field with long-term development opportunities
