Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For Clienia Privatklinik Schlössli we are looking for 1 January 2027 or by appointment a

Their tasks General internist care and treatment of psychiatric patients with diseases from the entire spectrum of psychiatry. Close cooperation with psychiatric colleagues as well as indication and coordination of specialist diagnosis and treatment. performance and evaluation of apparatus diagnostics (ECG, 24-h BD measurement), Laboratory, ultrasound, spirometry. Participation in the clinic's internal resuscitation team. Teaching and supervision of assistant doctors of General Internal Medicine (Further Educational Center General Internal Medicine Category C, 1 year).

Your profile We expect from you the specialist title for General Internal Medicine, clinical experience in a wide range of internal medicine and ultrasound diagnostics. Practical proficiency certificate is desirable. A high degree of independence and social competence as well as interest and pleasure in the integrative care of psychiatric patients.

Our offer We offer attractive working conditions at an innovative private psychiatric clinic, which attaches great importance to the holistic care of general and additional insured patients. Working in an interprofessional environment. Regular working hours on weekdays from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., no on-call time. Generous promotion of further education and training.

Further information is available to you Dr. med. Verena Niggemeier, Chief Physician of Internal Medicine, Tel. +41 44 929 82 10 or Dr. med. Christine Poppe, chief physician special offers, tel. +41 44 929 84 50.
