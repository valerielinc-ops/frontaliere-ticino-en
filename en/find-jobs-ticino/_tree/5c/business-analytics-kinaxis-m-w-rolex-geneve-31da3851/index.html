Introduction

For our Information Systems division, we are looking for a Kinaxis Business Analyst (M/F) whose main mission will be to work with business and IT teams on the implementation of our Supply Chain planning solution.

Responsibilities

  • Participate in workshops/workshops with the trade to help them define their needs
  • Design and document business processes
  • Working with stakeholders on processes to solve problems
  • Develop initiatives to improve overall data quality, improve time processing and anticipate future needs
  • Participate in inter-domain projects
  • Help coordinate the transition to the new Kinaxis RapidResponse (Maestro) system by supporting supply chain continuity
  • Maintain data governance with the trade
  • Support the team's skill development on the tool
  • Mastering the evolution of the solution and the correct fit with the company's roadmaps

Profile

  • University training in computer science or Supply Chain, EPF engineer or equivalent
  • Minimum experience of 3 years in Supply Chain, Planning or Production
  • Experience confirmed in the use and configuration of RapidResponse / Kinaxis Maestro
  • Project Management Skills: Organizational and Planning Capacity, Flexibility and Teamwork
  • Critical spirit and autonomy in the ability to understand and analyse problems and find solutions
  • Excellent interpersonal skills and team spirit
  • Strong communication skills: be able to lead meetings, produce and present syntheses
  • Rigor and confidentiality
  • Mastery of French and English

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
