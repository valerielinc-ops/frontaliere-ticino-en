Full description

We combine Swiss tradition with innovative expertise to produce unparalleled fondue specialties and creamy processed cheese. With our many years of expertise and passion for quality, we create products that inspire the whole world. Become part of our team to develop the taste of Switzerland together.

You can do that.

  • You lead product development projects for our fondue and processed cheese specialties.
  • You develop recipes for new products and take over the planning, implementation and evaluation of pilot and operational tests.
  • You implement product innovations in close cooperation with the clients, quality management, technology and production.
  • You test and optimize existing recipes and processes and thus actively contribute to continuous improvement.

Why do you succeed?

  • You have completed vocational training or study in the food sector.
  • You already have several years of experience in product development.
  • You bring in-depth knowledge with SAP RD and feel comfortable in a technologically demanding environment.
  • You know how to manage different stakeholders and keep them up to date.
  • You have reliable knowledge of German and very good English.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
