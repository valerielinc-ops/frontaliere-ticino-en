Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Step into a leadership role where you can drive quality, compliance, and continuous improvement at Sulzer’s Leeds Service Centre — a key facility specialising in the repair, overhaul, and maintenance of rotating equipment for customers across power generation, oil & gas, water, and other heavy industries. Known for its technical capability and fast‑turnaround support, the centre plays a vital role in helping customers minimise downtime and maximise performance.

In this position, you’ll have the opportunity to make a meaningful impact by strengthening quality standards, shaping improvement initiatives, and supporting a high‑performing, customer‑focused operation.

Your main responsibilities

  • Investigate and resolve quality issues using structured root‑cause analysis, ensuring robust corrective and preventive actions
  • Lead and champion continuous improvement initiatives using LEAN methodology, working closely with the local management team
  • Oversee the internal audit programme and support successful outcomes in customer and third‑party audits
  • Monitor supplier performance and collaborate with procurement to enhance quality, conformance, and reliability

To succeed in this role, you will need

  • A recognised qualification in mechanical engineering or significant experience in a relevant service or repair environment
  • Strong working knowledge of ISO 9001 and experience maintaining or managing quality management systems
  • Proven success in leading LEAN or continuous improvement projects, with strong structured problem‑solving abilities
  • Excellent communication, leadership, and influencing skills
  • An understanding of repair processes, rotating equipment, and supplier quality standards
