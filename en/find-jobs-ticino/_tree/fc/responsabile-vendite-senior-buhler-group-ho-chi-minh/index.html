Full description

Aufgaben

CS Part Selling

  • Define and implement the strategic and operational plan
  • Analysis of the inactive/lost customers for CS products and define proper action plan to gain back the business
  • Analysis of the demand for CS products – understand customer pain points and build spare part package according to their needs
  • Develop the activity of Customer Service in coordination with Area Sales Managers, Area Service Managers, Head of Sales and the SAS Manager
  • Build an action plan to increase the users for myBuhler and encourage customers to transact via myBuhler
  • Develop new business, collect and evaluate about competitors, customer and market trend
  • Review operational records and reports to Head of Sales & SAS manager
  • Attend trade shows or seminars to promote products

Service Agreement

  • Listen and proactively re-solve customer complaints regarding spare parts and services
  • Obtain and check all relevant information of complaints and to determine possible problem causes
  • Understand customers' complaints, root cause of the problems and paint points from the customers and tailor Service Agreement to their needs
  • Actively coordinate with CSOP Managers for the dispatch of FSE to perform problem solving or service sales

Channel Management

  • Build – up, develop and manage parts channels for business
  • Follow to execute the process of parts channel management with regional team

CS Team Leading

  • Build the budget and goals for team and individual people
  • Plan and prepare work schedules, and assign employees to specific duties
  • Responsible for the budget of sales and keep costs of the CS department within benchmarks.
  • Monitor and assess employees performance
  • Join the recruitment process, follow any promotions and prepare training plan for the development of the people
  • Ensure security and optimal working conditions for the employees.

Anforderungen

  • Bachelor's Degree in engineering / businees or equivalent
  • 5 years experience in Technical Sales and in Customer Service
  • 3 years experience in Management
