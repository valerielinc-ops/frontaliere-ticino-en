Since 2017, the Futuro Therapy Center in Liestal has become an established address for physiotherapy and medical training therapy in the Baselland region. As we have grown and expanded our modern premises, we want to strengthen our team. We are looking for a personality who loves her profession, values an individual, holistic treatment and wants to actively participate. From January 2027 or by arrangement. What to expect from us (your benefits)

  • First-class training surface (a real difference): We have very nice, spacious and diverse premises for active training. This gives you the optimal opportunities for functional and movement-oriented treatments in everyday work.
  • Broad range of subjects: With us, you cover the entire spectrum of all common physiotherapeutic areas and manage a versatile, varied patient product.
  • A strong team & real cooperation: You can expect an open, dynamic and very likeable team that welcomes new ideas and wants to make a difference together.
  • Plenty of space for your design: With us you work independently in your personally designed treatment room as well as on the modern training area. You can actively shape the center, set your own accents (e.g. osteopathy) or offer your own sports and training courses in the evening.
  • Flexible workplace to measure: We offer you a flexible workplace design that fits your life. With us there is no shift or weekend work
  • Focus on therapy: Thanks to your own administrative staff for patient planning and billing, you can concentrate fully on your patients – and in a pleasant 30-minute cycle.
  • Medical network: We maintain a close interdisciplinary cooperation with our cardiological practice as well as regional specialists (surgery, orthopaedics, obesity). This ensures short communication paths and exciting professional perspectives.

How we work We rely on evidence-based, modern and technically sound physiotherapy. At the same time, we give you the freedom to design your treatments individually and appropriately, because we value independent thinking and tailor-made therapy approaches. Modern training software helps you create uncomplicated home programs for your patients. Who we are looking for

  • You are dipl. Physical therapist (with SRK recognition or the requirements for it).
  • You are an open, empathetic and team capable personality with a committed, independent way of working.
  • Whether you already have a lot of experience or want to start as a motivated career starter: What is important to us is your pleasure in the profession and your willingness to learn new things.
  • Note: Direct participation in outpatient cardiac rehabilitation and care of heart groups is possible, but not mandatory.

What we offer you in addition

  • Contemporary, fair remuneration and 5 weeks holiday for your recovery.
  • Annual training days and financial support for your further education (CHF 600.- per year).
  • Excellent connection to public transport as well as the option of an employee parking space.
