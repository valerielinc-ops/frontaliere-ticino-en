Full description

Tasks

  • Professional and technical responsibility for our tracking infrastructure: from the data layer in shop and app to Tealium to Adobe Analytics and the other systems that consume this data
  • An honest starting point: increased complexity, real dependencies on advertising platforms and third-party systems as well as a team that supports you in your further development and not only manages it
  • An accompanied training by our external experts. You start with direct access to his knowledge instead of from zero
  • A strong analytics team with which you build the tracking data products on our Databricks Platform and are supported by our data platform team. In everyday life, you work closely with our data analysts and marketing
  • Real design leeway: You’ll help shape how we monitor tracking quality, how data products are created from Adobe and Tealium, and how we evolve the lifecycle of our tools

Requirements

  • Bring strong experience in web tracking: You understand how client and server-side tag management systems work (ideally Tealium, alternatively Google Tag Manager, Adobe Launch or Segment), how a clean data layer is built, and where the dirty realities of the web world lie (ITP, ad blocker, bots, etc.). Mobile app tracking doesn’t scare you either
  • In-depth knowledge of client- and server-side tracking for common ad platforms (Google, Meta, TikTok & Co.) and directly in the browser finds out which data goes where
  • Are secure in JavaScript and bring advanced SQL knowledge
  • Understand data quality as your responsibility and not as a task you do when there is time left. Tracking errors are rarely noticed by themselves and cannot be repaired retroactively
  • Have a “Serving the Analyst” mentality: You’re not building for yourself, but for analysts and marketing teams to rely on the data
  • Cleanly document and actively share your knowledge. This is a core requirement in this role, not a side issue.
  • Experience with Adobe Analytics from an implementation point of view (variables, processing rules, classifications)
  • Know Python, Databricks or PySpark or feel like working your way in. On this stack we build the tracking data products in the future
  • Uses AI tools in the workflow and trains yourself on your own, simply because you enjoy growing professionally
  • Enjoy working with marketing, analysts and developers to explain complex tracking topics so everyone understands what to do
  • Speak English fluently and understand German well. Flowing German is a plus

We probably don’t fit together so well if you

  • See tracking as a side task that you do when nothing else is pending
  • Switch between tool worlds, between tag management configuration, analytics suite and data platform
  • Prefer frameworks such as SAFe or projects that start with a specification
  • Are more frustrated by imperfect systems than motivated - Spoilers: We are not there yet
  • Seeing Documentation as an Annoying Duty to Do When Time Is Left
  • Work and good mood strictly separated from each other

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
