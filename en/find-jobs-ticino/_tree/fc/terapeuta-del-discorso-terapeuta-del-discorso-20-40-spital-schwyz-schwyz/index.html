Full description

Welcome to the hospital Schwyz. Health is our highest good. That’s why we do everything every day to help people – professionally and personally. As a regionally anchored hospital, we care about the direct relationship with the population of the region and their trust. The extensive range of medical care and modern infrastructure make the Schwyz Hospital attractive for people from all over Central Switzerland – and beyond. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a professionally experienced and empathetic personality for speech therapy / speech therapist / speech therapist 20%-40%. Your challenge

  • Independent diagnosis, counselling and therapy of patients in the acute care unit and rehabilitation with neurogenic speech, speech, voice and swallowing disorders (dysphagia)
  • Conduct of clinical swallowing examinations
  • Advice and guidance for relatives
  • Concept revision
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with the medical service, nursing and other therapeutic areas (physical and occupational therapy) and nutritional advice
  • Complete documentation of the course of therapy and preparation of reports Your profile
  • Completed and EDK-recognized training as a speech doctor (bachelor or equivalent diploma) with Swiss recognition
  • Clinical experience (acute symptoms and dysphagia management) is beneficial
  • High social skills, empathy and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing Our offer
  • A versatile and responsible job in a motivated, collegial team
  • Flexible working time arrangement that is ideally compatible with other obligations or a further part-time job
  • Advanced employment conditions and contemporary social benefits
  • Support for internal and external training
