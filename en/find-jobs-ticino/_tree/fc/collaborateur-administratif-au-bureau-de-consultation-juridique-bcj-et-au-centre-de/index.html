Full description

Just act. In Switzerland and in almost 20 countries on four continents. Engage with us to realize the vision of a solidarity society, as The Office of Legal Consultation (BCJ) for asylum seekers of Caritas Switzerland offers a professional legal service in the fields of asylum law and the law of foreigners for persons domiciled in the cantons of Fribourg, Jura and Neuchâtel. Caritas Switzerland's Centre de Compétences (CdC) Droit & Poverté offers a professional legal service "low-threshold" in the area of debt relief, social assistance and social insurance for people affected or threatened by poverty in Switzerland. Your tasks

  • To ensure professional reception of beneficiaries, particularly during permanences
  • Administrative management of Permanences
  • Provide administrative support to BCJ and CoC (lawyers and other specialists)
  • Manage and track files administratively using a database (eCase)
  • Coordinate employee agendas and set appointments to beneficiaries
  • Manage information flows (mails, e-mails, phones) and ensure their efficient processing
  • Support the administrative officer in the organization and proper functioning of the secretariat
  • Participate in process optimization and formalization

Our requirements

  • SWC business employee or experience deemed equivalent
  • Minimum 2 years of work experience, preferably in the fields of migration or legal
  • Mastery of French and good knowledge of German and/or English
  • Knowledge of one or more foreign languages is an asset for this function
  • The use of IT tools, particularly in database management (knowledge of eCase, an asset)
  • Excellent intercultural communication skills
  • Interest in work in the migration sector

The workplace is Fribourg. Entry into office: 1 January 2027 or to be agreed. Caritas offers interesting hiring conditions and an open working environment in an international context. For the active development and implementation of digital transformation, we rely on employees with a digital and agile mindset who are willing to participate in change processes. For more information on the position, please contact Ms. Gemma Rosell, Head of Administration, BCJ Romandie, tel. 026 552 50 46. Thank you for your interest and wait for your file by November 8, 2026 through the online portal. Caritas Switzerland, Adligenswilerstrasse 15, 6002 Lucerne www.caritas.ch
