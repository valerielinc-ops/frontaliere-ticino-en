Full description

Your surroundings

You run the private clinic MENTALVA with an interprofessional team of doctors and psychologists, nursing, therapy, hospitality and other professional groups. The professional and collegial environment supports you with a great deal of room for manoeuvre, cooperation and a wide range of development opportunities.

Their tasks

  • You take over the operational and professional management of the private clinic Mentalva working closely with the care service management and the station management
  • You are responsible for the personnel management and development of your employees
  • They ensure the inpatient care and case management of the demanding patients
  • They ensure the further development of an optimally networked, high-quality treatment and care
  • They independently carry out psychiatric-psychotherapeutic diagnosis and treatments
  • You are in contact with the large national network of treatment partners
  • In cooperation with the Medical Director, you participate in the strategic positioning of Privatklink Mentalva and participate in public relations work.
  • You participate in the clinic’s internal background service

Our requirement profile

  • You are a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • You have several years of experience in a senior position in clinical care in a psychiatric institution
  • They cultivate a cooperative leadership behavior and the willingness for interprofessional, process-oriented cooperation
  • You have a broad psychotherapeutic experience
  • You have good communication skills as well as entrepreneurial thinking and acting
  • They enjoy aligning the treatment with the established guidelines of evidence-based care and the high demands of the special patient group.
  • Good English skills are beneficial

We offer you

  • Dynamic and supportive team culture, also across departments
  • A meaningful job, because the employees you lead make an important contribution to mental health
  • An interesting and versatile field of activity with very good personal development and development opportunities
  • Job and/or top sharing models are possible
  • We offer them appropriate attractive and flexible employment conditions and the possibility of working time and earning models
  • With part-time work models, regular working hours and unpaid leave, an optimal work-life balance
