Biochemical Laboratory Technician — IBSA Institut Biochimique

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The resource will be inserted into the protein development team and will be responsible for managing eukaryotic cell cultures, with a particular focus on CHO lines. It will contribute to the development, optimization, and maintenance of in vitro production processes, collaborating with the protein biochemistry and downstream processing teams. ## Main Responsibilities

  • Cell culture, maintenance, and manipulation of eukaryotic cell lines (particularly CHO).
  • Management of bioreactors in batch, Fed-batch, and perfusion.
  • Cell transfers and selection of productive clones.
  • Optimization of growth and protein production conditions.
  • Monitoring of cell viability and density through standard techniques (e.g., cell counting, microscopy).
  • Support for scaling up from flask cultures to bioreactors.
  • Preparation and updating of SOPs, technical reports, and laboratory documentation.
  • Collaboration with multidisciplinary teams (protein biochemistry analysis, downstream process, biological analysis).
  • Compliance with safety regulations and GMP/GLP where applicable. ## Requirements
  • Bachelor's degree in Biotechnology, Biology, Pharmacy, or related fields; a PhD is considered a plus.
  • Knowledge and experience in managing laboratory bioreactors in batch, Fed-batch, and perfusion, and scaling up cell cultures.
  • Proven experience (at least 1-3 years) in eukaryotic cell cultures, preferably CHO.
  • Knowledge of cell transfer, clone selection, and maintenance of stable cell lines.
  • Basic knowledge of protein purification and analysis techniques (SDS-PAGE, Western Blot, ELISA).
  • Good problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
  • Excellent communication skills and teamwork abilities.
  • Scientific English (written and spoken). ## What We Offer
  • Integration into a dynamic and innovative research environment.
  • Opportunities for professional growth in the biotechnological and pharmaceutical field.
  • Collaboration with a multidisciplinary team on high-tech projects.
