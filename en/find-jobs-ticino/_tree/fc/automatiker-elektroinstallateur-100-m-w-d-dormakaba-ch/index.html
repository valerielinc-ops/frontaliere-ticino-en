Full description

Become part of our team Bring your ideas to life – from electrical equipment to the finished special machine!

At dormakaba, not only high-quality locking systems are created, but also the machines with which they are manufactured. In internal special machine construction, you actively shape this production world: You build switchgear cabinets, wire electrical components, integrate controls and, together with our engineering, make individual machines and prototypes work. At the same time, through maintenance work and troubleshooting, you ensure that our existing production facilities run reliably.

Open the door to your future: your tasks

  • You build and assemble individual special machines and prototypes for our internal production of keys and lock cylinders.
  • You build switch cabinets, wire electrical components and integrate controls into our self-developed machines.
  • You bring your ideas and expertise to mechanical engineering projects as well as to the further development of our production processes.
  • You will analyze and repair electrical and mechanical faults on production machines and plants.
  • You carry out maintenance, inspections and repairs and independently take care of the required spare parts.

Hence, we trust you: your capabilities

  • Completed technical training, for example as an automatic technician or electrical installer.
  • Professional experience in electrical assembly, mechanical engineering, special machine construction or maintenance.
  • Knowledge in control cabinet construction, control technology, robotics and sensor technology is desired.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working methods as well as initiative in technical challenges.
  • Teamwork, enthusiasm and resilience – even if it becomes demanding.
  • Quick comprehension, flexibility, reliability and service orientation.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing.

Reduced layer operation

So that we can optimally support our production, you work in a 6-week cycle with the following working hours:

  • Four weeks of standard working hours
  • One week early shift from 06:00 to 15:00
  • One week late shift from 15:00 to 23:00

You Can Leave Yourself: A Motivating Work Environment

  • Varied tasks in internal special machine construction – with space for your own ideas and practical solutions.
  • Exciting projects in which you build electrical systems, integrate controls and put machines into operation.
  • An innovative team.
  • Attractive salary package and modern employment conditions.
  • A first-class staff restaurant.
