Full description

Getreu der Vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives" liefern die Hamilton Unternehmen seit 1950 Lösungen für die Gesundheitsbranche. Wir sind ein innovativer Vorreiter was Beatmungsgeräte, das automatisierte Pipettieren und Lagern von Proben sowie die Entwicklung von Prozesssensoren anbelangt.

Die Business Unit Robotics entwickelt, produziert und vertreibt Pipettier-Roboter, um Liquid Handling Prozesse für die Pharma-, über die Nahrungsmittelindustrie bis hin zur Zell- oder DNA-Forschung zu automatisieren.

Das kannst du bewirken

  • Manage the assigned product portfolio from a technical perspective
  • Work closely with colleagues from different organizations to improve the technical aspects and the conformity of the assigned product portfolio
  • Technical lead for design control, construction materials, product functionalities and extensions / modifications required to meet customer requirements in coordination with the R&D PD organization
  • Constantly interact with the product management, manufacturing, supply chain, service organization and quality functions
  • Coordinate change projects with interfaces to our suppliers and internal customers
  • Ensure transparent and accountable customer-centric technical product management
  • Support product with maintenance, error analysis and troubleshooting for existing automation solution
  • Analyze errors and troubleshoot in case of problems in product and production and work out solutions
  • Participate in the continuous improvement process regarding design optimization
  • Coordinate customer complaints (root cause analysis, reporting)
  • Analyze, optimize and document product production
  • Support in the analysis of defective parts including the derivation of necessary steps and improvements
  • Implement product changes and document them
  • Test and introduce products
  • Provide technical support for products in the product life cycle
  • Write CAPA reports in English
  • Mainteain testing procedures in production

Das bringst du mit

Education

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering (System, Software, Mechanical, Electrical, Mechatronics, Automation, Industrial Engineering, or similar)

Technical Skills

  • Root cause analysis and problem solving
  • Error analysis and troubleshooting
  • Programming knowledge (Python or similar)
  • Product testing and validation
  • Documentation and technical report writing
  • Materials and component knowledge
  • Automation systems knowledge

Soft Skills

  • Strong analytical thinking
  • Structured problem-solving approach
  • Good communication skills
  • Ability to coordinate multiple stakeholders
  • Ability to work independently but also in a team
  • Continuous improvement mindset

Language Skills

  • Fluent written and spoken German and English
