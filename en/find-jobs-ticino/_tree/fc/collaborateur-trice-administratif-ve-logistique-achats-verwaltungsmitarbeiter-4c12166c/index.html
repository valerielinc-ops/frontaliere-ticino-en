Your missions

  • Administrative management of procurement: purchase orders, confirmation and document management.
  • Follow-up of suppliers: requests for tenders, monitoring of conditions and coordination of deliveries. According to profile: participate in trade and contractual negotiations with suppliers
  • Ensure logistical follow-up: stock movement, resupply and updating of data in the ERP.
  • Participate in the processing of supplier disputes and the monitoring of non-compliances.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of the purchasing processes and the previous suppliers/products.

Your profile

  • SWC trade employee or training deemed equivalent.
  • Experience from 2 to 5 years in a similar function, ideally in procurement or procurement.
  • Knowledge of procurement processes and ease in administrative monitoring.
  • Good command of office tools (Excel, Word, Outlook) and knowledge of an ERP (e.g. Opale).
  • Organizational sense, rigour, autonomy and good relational abilities.
  • Mastery of French, good level of German or English an asset.
