Full description

An auditorate at a district court enables people with completed legal studies to enter the civil and criminal justice department in Zurich. The auditorate shall be counted as a net year within the meaning of the admission conditions for the bar examination. Auditorate at Horgen District Court (100%) As an auditor, our pool system gives you insight into all areas of activity of a Zurich district court and works with all our 11 judges and 15 court clerks. They participate in negotiations, draw up the relevant protocols, participate in decision-making reasons and provide legal information. You will get an in-depth insight into procedural law, civil law (e.g. family law, claims including labour law and rental law disputes), criminal law as well as debt enforcement and bankruptcy law.
