Full description

The Tertianum Residence Huob, in the center of Pfäffikon SZ, consists of 67 modern apartments with in-house Spitex service and a rehabilitation, care and dementia living area with 21 single rooms, internal restaurant and public restaurant ROSSOROSSO, physiotherapy, fitness, therapy bath, coiffeur salon, podology and cosmetics studio.

For the Tertianum Residence Huob in the Zurich region we are looking for 01.02.2027 or by arrangement a

Stv. Head of Housekeeping 100%

Their tasks

  • Supporting the Housekeeping Management in the management and organisation of the Housekeeping Team and their deputies in absence
  • Organization and coordination of day-to-day work processes as well as support in the assignment planning of employees
  • A responsible and versatile function with personal responsibility and development opportunities
  • Responsible for the professional cleaning of all assigned premises and facilities
  • Ensuring the textile supply with the areas of company and professional clothing (order, coordinate and distribute) as well as guest clothing
  • Sorting, washing, drying, distributing and labelling of guest linen
  • Implementation of domestic work according to order
  • Professional and proper handling of materials and equipment
  • Compliance with hygiene regulations and personal hygiene standards in accordance with the instructions of the company

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience in housekeeping (home, hospital or hotel)
  • Specialist housekeeping EFZ or housekeeping practitioner EBA
  • High service and service readiness with a love for detail
  • Reliability, responsibility and physical resilience
  • Enjoyment of dealing with senior citizens
  • Ready for weekend and public holiday assignments and support from other departments as needed
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoyment of various activities
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
