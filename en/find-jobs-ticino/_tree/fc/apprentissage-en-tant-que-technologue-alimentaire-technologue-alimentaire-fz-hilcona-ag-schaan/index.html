Full description

Produce food, design processes and guarantee quality! Are you interested in how food is produced? Do you want to combine science and practical work? As a food technologist/login FZ, you will learn how to produce safe, high-quality and tasty products from carefully selected raw materials. During your three-year training, you will get exciting insights into food production. You will work with modern equipment, monitor production processes and learn how quality, hygiene and efficiency work together. Tasks:

  • You produce food according to given recipes and procedures
  • You weigh raw materials and prepare them for production
  • You operate and monitor production facilities
  • You control temperature, weight, consistency and other quality features
  • You perform simple analyses and quality controls
  • You document production data and work steps
  • You recognize deviations and react to them professionally
  • You comply with strict hygiene, safety and quality regulations
  • You support cleaning, conversion and maintenance requirements:
  • You are interested in food, science and technology
  • You work carefully, cleanly and responsibly
  • You have a good understanding of numbers and relationships
  • You are practical and like to take part
  • You can concentrate well and work reliably
  • You are curious and ready to get to know different production areas
  • You like working in a team
  • You have a good degree from the FL-Oberschule / CH-Realschule
