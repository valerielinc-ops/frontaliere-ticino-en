The RFSM is the mental health centre of the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions. Who are we? The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health. Bilingual MSRF is continuously developing and remains at the forefront in the therapeutic field.
