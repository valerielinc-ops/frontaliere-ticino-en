Full description

For our House of People team, we are looking for a colleague to take on the role of HR Generalist Care Management.

Tasks

  • You support employees with health-related concerns (illness, accident, and psychosocial stress) in close cooperation with managers, HR Business Partners, and external bodies.
  • You take over the care and support for long-term absences, including individual case management, coordination of reintegration measures, and consulting on salary, insurance, and time management issues.
  • You are responsible for the administration and coordination of complex health cases and ensure coordination with the various stakeholders.
  • You work closely with social insurance institutions (IV, KTG, UVG) and advise the line management and our employees on social insurance matters.
  • You coordinate and monitor IV reintegration measures and process the associated correspondence.
  • You are responsible for the monthly processing of sickness and accident daily allowances as part of the payroll run.
  • You advise the line management on health topics and absence management and further develop the Care Management.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience in a similar position.
  • Excellent knowledge of the social insurance system and law (ATSG).
  • You enjoy supporting employees, convince through high empathy, and act in a solution-oriented and trustworthy manner even in demanding situations.
  • Your flair for administrative and organizational tasks, strong communication skills, as well as structured and efficient work characterize you.
  • You have high digital competence and understand how to make the world easier with digital HR tools.
  • Aligning actions and decisions with your personal convictions and values.
  • The ability to adapt to continuous changes in the work environment.
  • No specific education/training required.

  • Uncomplicated: We avoid the unnecessary and focus on what is important. From mastering challenges to celebrating our successes.

  • Further benefits Switzerland: Benefit from 25 days of vacation, exclusive employee prices in the shop, mobile, internet, and TV subscriptions at company rates, and other advantages.
