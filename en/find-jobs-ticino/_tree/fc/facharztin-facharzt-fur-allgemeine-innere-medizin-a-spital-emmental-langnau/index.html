Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Your vision, your career and your decision: practice or hospital? Both! You do not want to decide between the dynamics of everyday hospital life and the continuity of family medicine? We have both. At the Langnau site, we combine modern inpatient medicine with a lively, growing family practice in the middle of the Emmental, in a warm, family environment. For this hybrid position we are looking for an agreement:n

Your sphere of activity – helping to shape instead of just working

  • Co-create & take responsibility: You bear co-responsibility for the medical care of our patients and actively shape the internistic and family medical offer with your professional expertise
  • Connecting Praxis & Spital: Together with a dedicated team, you will continue to expand our family practice with plenty of room for your medical ideas and experience. At the same time, you provide networked, high-quality care in close cooperation with the medical department and the hospital specialists.
  • Innovation & further development: You will actively develop the internistic offer at the Langnau location and bring new ideas to the practice and hospital work
  • Training with heart and mind: You accompany assistant doctors on their way, pass on your knowledge with joy and foresight and promote professional development in the team
  • Connected & human: As a medical contact person, you maintain an open, appreciative exchange with family doctors in the region and, if necessary, take care of home care in cooperation with nursing institutions

What distinguishes you – technically strong, humanly clear

  • You are a specialist for general internal medicine and would like to contribute your competence in a lively, future-oriented environment
  • You are team-oriented, empathetic and enjoy interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • You want to build something and take responsibility, both in practice and in the hospital. You create two worlds under one roof in which you can bundle your knowledge and create synergies
