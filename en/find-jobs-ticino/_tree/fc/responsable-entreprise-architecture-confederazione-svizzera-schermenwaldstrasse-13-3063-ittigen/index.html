Full description

  • Professionally, human and financially manage the corporate architecture sector across the five federal offices of the Swiss Army in the various architectural sectors (military ICT, civilian ICT, armaments and digital transformation)
  • Responsible for the management of corporate architecture for the entire Defence Group, involving the partners; overarching management of domain and business unit architectures
  • Design and take responsibility for the governance, processes and products of the architecture as well as create the basics and guidelines for the projects
  • Develop architectural specifications and guidelines for action coordinated with the strategy and business requirements and contribute them to the responsible management bodies
  • Manage internal, external and international stakeholders (e.g. industrial partners) of the architecture defense and communicate the content of the architecture in favor of the "Federated Mission Networking"
  • University or university of applied sciences degree with computer science and business administration specialization, good knowledge in the field of (company) architecture and analysis as well as very good knowledge of military structures and processes
  • Proven several years of leadership experience in corporate architecture and its methods in large organizations
  • Ability to communicate complex issues in a clear, structured and addressable manner and to combine different interests
  • Very good methodological competence (e.g. TOGAF, HERMES, project or program management certifications such as IPMA/MAS) and further education and experience in business analysis
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and passive knowledge of a third official language and very good knowledge of English (C1)
