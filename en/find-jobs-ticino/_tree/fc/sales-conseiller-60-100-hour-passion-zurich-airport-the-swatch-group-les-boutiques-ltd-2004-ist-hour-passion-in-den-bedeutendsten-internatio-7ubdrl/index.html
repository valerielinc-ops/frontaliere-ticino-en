Sales Advisor 60-100% Hour Passion Zurich Airport - Swatch Group Sales Advisor 60-100% Hour Passion Zurich Airport The company Hour Passion, a retail brand of Swatch Group, the world leader in the watch industry, offers you the opportunity to operate in a dynamic, international and demanding environment. Specializing in the multi-brand sale of watches and jewellery of renowned brands, Hour Passion inspires a discerning international customer base through innovative store concepts and first-class customer service. The brand carries prestigious collections of Blancpain, Glashütte, Omega, Longines, Rado, Tissot, Balmain, Certina, Mido and Hamilton.
