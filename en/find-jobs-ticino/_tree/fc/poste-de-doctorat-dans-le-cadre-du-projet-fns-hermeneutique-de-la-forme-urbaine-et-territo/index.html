Posizione Aperta Posizione aperta presso Università della Svizzera italiana. ## Informazioni Generali

  • Dipartimento/Istituto: Institute for the History and Theory of Art and Architecture (ISA).

  • Sede: Mendrisio, Svizzera (Canton Ticino). ## Descrizione PhD position as part of SNSF project “André Corboz’s hermeneutics of urban and territorial form: research practices, concepts and methodologies” (100%) The Università della Svizzera italiana (USI) is a young and lively university, a hub of opportunities open to the world where students are offered a quality interdisciplinary education in which they can be fully engaged and take centre stage, and where our researchers can count on having the space to pursue their initiatives freely. Established in 1996, USI is in constant transformation, always taking on new challenges while remaining true to its three guiding principles: quality, openness, and responsibility. The Institute for the History and Theory of Art and Architecture (ISA) has a profile inspired by long-established institutions, such as the History Theory and Criticism of Architecture and Art of the MIT in Boston or the Institute for the History and Theory of Architecture (gta) of the ETH Zurich. Without limiting their fields of study to any period or geographical area in particular, the purpose of ISA is to foster a fuller understanding of the complex meanings of artistic and architectural action. ## Il Progetto SNSF The SNFS project “André Corboz’s hermeneutics of urban and territorial form: research practices, concepts and methodologies” will investigate the research methodologies and practices of André Corboz (1928-2012), one of the most distinguished personalities in the field of urban and territorial studies. Corboz, initially trained in law, became an academic only in his late thirties. After having taught architectural history at University of Montréal from 1967 onwards, he held the chair for the history of urbanism at ETH Zurich from 1980 to 1993. His research and theorising demonstrate a high degree of independence from the disciplinary codes of their time. Privileging transversal methods of speculation and open forms of interpretation, he brought together various strands of knowledge from the fields of art, architecture and urban history in his scholarly work. The project will examine for the first time Corboz's entire intellectual trajectory, from his early works in the second half of the 1950s to his late writings and research projects at the beginning of the 2000s. This will be done by means of a systematic analysis of Corboz’s methodological position, balancing it with Corboz’s research practices and their particularities. The main research corpus is Corboz's estate, held at the Library of the Academy of Architecture. The project is led by Prof. Dr. Sonja Hildebrand (USI) and co-led by Adjunct Prof. Dr. Elena Cogato Lanza (EPFL). ## Il Progetto di Dottorato The SNSF project comprises three interconnected subprojects. The PhD project is part of Subproject 3 “Corboz’s reading of territorial form” which will investigate Corboz’s research on territorial configurations and contemporary urbanisation phenomena, examining how the large scale impacted his research practices, methodologies and theoretical framework and placing him in the national and international discourse on urbanisation. The PhD research will be supervised by Prof. Dr. Sonja Hildebrand and co-supervised by Adjunct Prof. Dr. Elena Cogato Lanza. ## Mansioni
