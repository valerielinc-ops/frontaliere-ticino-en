  • Together with the team, you will carry out the demanding maintenance and renewal work as well as troubleshooting on our catenary systems.
  • You perform the operational control during construction, maintenance and maintenance work on the catenary systems.
  • To ensure the reliability and safety of the catenary systems economically, you install our contact wires and support cables professionally. You operate specialized tools of the catenary assembly machines on the respective vehicles with lifting, pulling and crane function.
  • You lead employees professionally, manage work assignments and monitor the correct execution of the work, as well as compliance with the safety measures on the construction sites.

You bring that with you.

  • For this exciting challenge, you bring a high resilience and great security awareness. We also attach great importance to teamwork and independent working methods.
  • You have a certificate of ability in the electrical or construction industry and have several years of experience in catenary construction incl. shifting authorization and expertise.
  • Good PC and user knowledge (MS-Office, TUSP, etc.) makes your job easier. You have already proven your experience in planning (AVOR 3) and implementing construction sites.
  • Irregular working hours and piketting services do not matter to you and can communicate very well in German. Ideally, you have an A40 or higher train ID and a Cat driver’s license. B.
