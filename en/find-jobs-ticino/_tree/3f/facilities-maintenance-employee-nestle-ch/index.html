Location : Vevey, Switzerland Work Model: On-site / Full-time (100%), Type of contract / duration : Contract of indefinite duration

Application deadline: 2 weeks

Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet

As a technician in Nestlé's Infrastructure-Sanitary team, you play a central role in building on your health skills complemented by knowledge in electricity and HVAC to ensure technical and operational support.

You will make a difference by working closely with technical teams, campus collaborators and our external providers. This is an opportunity to take responsibility, develop your expertise and achieve concrete results that make a big difference.

Your Impact as a Health Technician

  • Ensure the availability and reliability of campus technical facilities by piloting day-to-day operational activities and tracking requests for intervention, and providing picket service.
  • Ensure continuity of services by deploying the preventive and corrective maintenance plan, in close collaboration with the electricity and heating, ventilation and air conditioning teams.
  • Maintain compliance of safety equipment (lifelines, dry columns, fire extinguishers, fire hoses) by planning and coordinating regulatory controls.
  • Secure each intervention through risk analysis, monitoring of internal and external activities and the application of existing standards.
  • Provide an appropriate working environment for more than 3,000 employees by identifying their needs and assessing the impact of the facilities on their operations.
  • Advance performance and operational efficiency by offering continuous improvement solutions.
  • Technical picket service.

The keys to your success

  • Make use of sound technical expertise in health care or building technology; A technical CFC is an asset.
  • Provide experience in a demanding service environment, with a strong sense of customer service and communication.
  • Very good knowledge of electrical and CVC techniques
  • Fire protection certification is an important asset.
  • Mastering of SHE requirements.
  • Demonstrated ability to effectively manage emergency situations on critical facilities in a demanding environment.
  • Manage your priorities independently, master IT tools and demonstrate proactivity, tact and team spirit.
  • Demonstrate courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value
  • Communicate fluently in French; professional knowledge of English.
