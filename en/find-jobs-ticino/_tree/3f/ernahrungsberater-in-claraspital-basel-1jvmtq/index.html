Full description

The Claraspital's nutritional advice supports patients of the Tumor and Abdominal Center during their inpatient stay and, if necessary, also accompanies them in an outpatient setting after they leave. We also specialize in caring for patients before and after bariatric surgery. Tasks:

  • Preliminary outpatient assessment and follow-up care of bariatric patients in an interdisciplinary team
  • Care and advice for outpatients with a focus on oncology, visceral surgery, obesity, diabetes and bariatrics
  • Conducting internal and public lectures as well as further training
  • Participating in and implementing projects Requirements:
  • Completed professional training in BSc Nutrition and Dietetics (or equivalent)
  • Enthusiasm for obesity, diabetes, bariatrics and clinical nutrition with a focus on visceral surgery and oncology, experience is an advantage
  • Social skills with strong teamwork and communication skills
  • Flexibility, resilience, commitment as well as an independent and responsible way of working
  • Knowledge of foreign languages (particularly French, English and Italian is an advantage) We offer:
  • Depending on age, 28-33 paid vacation days per year
  • Extensive opportunities for further education and training, e.g. our own seminar program with a wide range of educational offerings
  • Company health management with preventive measures such as: Ergonomic advice in the workplace
  • Part-time work and the possibility of job sharing, including in management positions
  • Bright, friendly rooms with modern infrastructure
  • Distinctive values and actively practiced corporate culture
  • Employees shape and help shape the Claraspital The Claraspital, Basel's largest private hospital, is a university teaching and research hospital with a comprehensive service mandate for highly specialized abdominal surgery and a national reach of its "abdominal" and "Tumor" and its specialties. At Claraspital, employees find jobs where they can develop both professionally and personally. At Claraspital, we attach great importance to consciously maintaining and developing our corporate culture and actively living the cultural criteria we have developed together: “together – competently – responsibly”.
