Construction manager 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people – now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by agreement we are looking for a permanent employment a 1 – 3 years experience as a construction manager

Your challenge

  • to operate optimal AVOR, to obtain documents, to visit the future construction sites, to take care of the entire construction processes
  • To organize the construction sites with all employees, machines and inventory optimally, economically and entrepreneurially in compliance with the rules of the building customer
  • Monitoring reporting

This is what you bring with you

  • 1 – 3 years of experience as a construction manager
  • High performance and flexibility
  • Punctuality and reliability
  • Independent, reliable and exact working method

What we offer you

  • Great working environment
  • Variable payments for temporary bets (monthly or weekly selectable)
  • Known customers
  • Fast-growing company
  • Long-term deployments
  • Attractive employment conditions with good pay
  • A team that is there for you and your needs
