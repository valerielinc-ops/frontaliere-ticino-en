No desire for classical clinical structure and rigid processes? Here you can expect a modern psychiatric setting, which lies between outpatient and stationary – with an everyday work day that also takes you out to the patients. They work in an innovative treatment model that is becoming increasingly important in Switzerland: inpatient equivalent treatment in the home environment. Your patients stay in their usual environment and are accompanied there therapeutically – a real alternative to inpatient stay. For you, this means less classic “station routine”, but more direct work on people – in the everyday life of patients. They travel regularly, carry out home visits and experience psychiatry in a much more practical context than in clinic settings. The team is deliberately small and interdisciplinary: doctors, psychologists and nurses work closely together, the leadership takes place in tandem between medical management, psychology and nursing. Hierarchies are flat, decisions are shared.

Their tasks

  • Initial assessment of new patients in the home setting (variant, 1-3 per day)
  • Technical supervision of assistant doctors: inside
  • Participation in treatment planning and therapy structure
  • Medicinal adjustment and follow-up
  • Participation / leadership of interdisciplinary team meetings and supervision

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Psychiatry and Psychotherapy
  • 1-3 years of experience after specialist training, ideally with upper medical proportions
  • Interest in a new, mobile treatment setting
  • Joy of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Openness for a co-leader role in the team
  • Structured and self-responsible working method
  • Good knowledge of German (min. B2)

Their advantages

  • Entry into a management function with a development perspective
  • Innovative treatment concept between ambulatory and stationary
  • Mobile working day with a high degree of variety
  • Small teams with close cooperation and flat hierarchies
  • Very good work-life balance
  • At least 25 days of holiday
  • Staff room available for entry into Switzerland
  • Above-average participation in the pension fund
  • Additional family and care allowances
  • Support for further education and training
  • Miscellaneous benefits (sports, pharmacy, mobility)
