Full description

Do you want to take responsibility, look after varied objects and make a real difference? Then you are exactly right with us!

You can move that with us.

In this versatile function, you manage several objects in the St. Gallen and Rhine Valley area and are the first point of contact for our customers. Your tasks include:

  • Supervision and supervision of building services (HLKSE)
  • Carrying out repair and maintenance work, loading and triggering orders to third parties, final inspections of the work
  • Coordination and control of service and maintenance work (heating, ventilation, air conditioning, sanitation, etc.), obtain offers, exchange with customers and administrations
  • Cooperation with external service providers and coordination of different service providers
  • Execution of administrative tasks as part of your activity
  • Pictet service
  • Gardening and site cleaning services
  • Holiday representation Postal services

That's what makes you.

You live service and work with conviction for quality and reliability. You bring with you:

  • Completed technical training in the HVAC sector, as an electrical installer or basic craftsmanship with further training in technical building management
  • Several years of professional experience in building technology
  • Independent, structured working method and sense of responsibility
  • Likes to work in a team as well as alone
  • Strong service mentality and organisational talent
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing and good IT skills
  • Residency in the St. Gallen region (Pikettdienst)

That's what we offer you.

  • An exciting, responsible task with a lot of creative freedom
  • An experienced team with an open corporate culture
  • Contemporary employment conditions and good social benefits
