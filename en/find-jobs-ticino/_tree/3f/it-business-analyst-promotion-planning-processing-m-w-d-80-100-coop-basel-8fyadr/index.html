IT Business Analyst Promotion Planning & Processing (m/f/d), 80-100 %

Coop Cooperative — Basel, BS, Svizzera

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Computer Science
  • Location: Coop, Thiersteinerallee 12, 4053 Basel
  • Pensum: 80-100 %

Tasks

  • You will analyze end-to-end processes in the SAP environment, identify optimization potential and develop suitable solutions together with business and IT
  • You question professional requirements and translate them into clear, testable user stories, features and acceptance criteria
  • You actively shape our SAP-based solutions around promotion planning, volume forecasting, volume processing and allocation
  • You will analyze SAP processes, data flows and interfaces, develop solution concepts and accompany their implementation
  • You bring different stakeholders together, drive topics independently and accompany the quality assurance

Requirements

  • Education or study in (economic) computer science or comparable professional experience
  • Experience in business analysis, requirements engineering and the further development of SAP-based business processes
  • Good SAP knowledge, ideally in the SAP retail environment as well as in quantity processing, allocation or associated logistics processes
  • Experience with agile working methods, user stories as well as Jira and Confluence
  • Analytical thinking, initiative and the ability to communicate complex technical and technical contexts in an understandable way
  • Discount on the travel offer ITS Coop Travel, 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket, up to 20% discount on various insurance services, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop Group
  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, Attractive Conditions of Employment and Certified Equal Pay, Total Employment Contract (GAV)
  • Working time: 5 weeks holidays, flexible working time models
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses, support for external education and training
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription, free participation in various company runs
