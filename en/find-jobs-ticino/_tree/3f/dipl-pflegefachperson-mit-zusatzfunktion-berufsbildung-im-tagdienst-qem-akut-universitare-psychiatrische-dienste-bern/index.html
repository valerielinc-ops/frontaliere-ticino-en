Clinic for Psychosis and Dependence

Do you want to combine your nursing experience with your commitment to the education of the next generation? At the Clinic for Psychosis and Dependence of the University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ) you can expect a versatile and responsible task as a dipl. Nurse with an additional function vocational training. You will work in a demanding psychiatric environment and combine the professional support of our patients with the targeted promotion and support of learners and students. In doing so, you play an important role in practical vocational training and, together with the interprofessional team, contribute to ensuring that prospective specialists are optimally prepared for their everyday work. You can expect a varied working environment in which you can contribute your professional skills, your pedagogical skills and your joy in the development of other people.

Your tasks

  • Together with your teammates, you create a safe and pleasant environment for our patients.
  • Through competence and successful relationship formation you contribute to a high patient satisfaction
  • With your professional, appreciative attitude and your constructive, critical approach, you actively contribute to the further development of our structures and processes.
  • In this demanding and exciting function, you take on the role of nursing caregiver and care for dependency-affected people with comorbidities both in acute crises and up to rehabilitation.
  • You are responsible for the care process and actively participate in the interprofessional treatment of patients
  • You take on complex care situations, lead discussions and patient groups as part of the socio-military therapeutic offer after recovery, safewards and motivating discussions
  • You accompany the learners and students in the training and can pass on your experience to the next generation
  • You work closely with education
  • As a vocational trainee you are responsible for the learning process support of the learners and students HF on the station in different training phases. You are guided by their resources and competences

Your profile

  • You have a completed training for the Dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • You will ideally bring professional experience in psychiatry
  • You think goal- and result-oriented, work patient-oriented and have a high level of social skills
  • You are interested in the entire spectrum of adult psychiatry, especially for patients with an addiction disease
  • You convince through your open, constructive communication and consider interprofessional cooperation as a matter of course
  • You can already have completed further training in the field of vocational training or have an interest in completing it

The UPZ

The University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
