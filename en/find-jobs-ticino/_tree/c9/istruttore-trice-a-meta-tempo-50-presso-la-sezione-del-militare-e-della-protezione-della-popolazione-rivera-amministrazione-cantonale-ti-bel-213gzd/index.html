Full description

Tasks

  • instruct the militia, specialist staff and population protection paintings, in particular civil protection
  • manage the assigned specialist service, ensuring the updating of the training of instructors, teaching documentation, directives and educational materials
  • participate in the organization and management of the conduct and intervention exercises curated by the Section
  • collaborate with the Head of Education Service of the PCi in the drafting of civil protection education programs
  • co-operation in the management of civil protection militia education projects
  • participate in committees or working groups
  • carry out the duties provided for in the Section's picket

Requirements

  • Federal certificate of capacity (AFC), cantonal/federal maturity
  • Federal diploma of civil protection instructor or federal diploma of part-time civil protection instructor with commitment to obtain full-time diploma
  • proven experience in civil protection education
  • strong spirit of initiative
  • aptitude for group work
  • availability to work at flexible times and to make additional hours or picks
  • excellent linguistic knowledge of Italian (conversation and editing) and written and spoken knowledge of a second official language
  • good knowledge of Office package (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • versatility and independence in work
  • availability to follow training courses
  • license to conduct category B
  • Swiss citizenship
  • a proven experience in the education of military training or in another partner organization of population protection

Special remarks

  • entry into service: 1 January 2027

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), further documentation may be requested at a later stage (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements
