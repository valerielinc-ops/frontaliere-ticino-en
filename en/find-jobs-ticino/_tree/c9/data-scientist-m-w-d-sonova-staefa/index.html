More about the role

Join our R&D organization and help build a deep understanding of how our products are used in the real world. This role sits in a technical team and focuses on translating complex product and user data into clear, decision-shaping insights for functional owners, senior leadership and product marketing.

In this role, you will

  • Lead the analysis of product usage data to uncover meaningful patterns, behaviors, and segmentations
  • Build a deep understanding of our datasets and apply statistical methods to generate robust, decision-relevant insights
  • Translate complex technical findings into clear recommendations that support R&D decisions, product direction, and business priorities
  • Communicate usage insights in a simple, credible, and actionable way to technical teams, senior leaders, and product marketing stakeholders
  • Create practical analyses and dashboards that make product usage visible and easier to act on
  • Partner closely with R&D teams, engineering, product management, senior leaders, and product marketing to frame the right questions and connect data to decisions
  • Stay current on relevant developments in analytics, statistics, and AI, and apply them where they improve insight quality and business value

More about you

  • Master’s degree or PhD in Psychology, Behavioral Science, Health Science, Audiology, or a related field
  • Several years of experience in data science, product analytics, user insights, or a similar role
  • Strong expertise in statistics, segmentation, behavioral analysis, and extracting insight from complex real-world datasets
  • Strong hands-on skills in Python and dashboarding, with the ability to move between strategic insight generation and practical delivery
  • Proven ability to translate deep technical analysis into clear recommendations for both technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • Experience working with complex datasets and turning them into clear recommendations for non-technical stakeholders
  • Confidence working with R&D and engineering stakeholders while also communicating clearly with senior leaders and product marketing
  • Experience in MedTech, healthcare, or another scientific or regulated environment is a plus
  • Clear, structured communication and a strong instinct for connecting data to product and business decisions
  • Fluent in English

More about what we offer

As one of the world’s leading hearing care providers headquartered in Switzerland, we’re committed to building an inclusive culture. We want to create an environment where you can balance a successful career with your commitments and interests outside of work, through our flexible hybrid working model.

We offer a wide range of training opportunities for both your professional and personal development, and there are exceptional growth opportunities with individual development plans.
