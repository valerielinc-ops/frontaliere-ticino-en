Full description

Are you enthusiastic about digital solutions and want to achieve more than just solving technical problems? At Schutz und Rettung Bern, you will combine platform development, ICT support and digitalisation. You will develop smart solutions, optimise processes and actively shape the digital working life of tomorrow. Contribute your ideas and make our organisation simpler, more efficient and fit for the future.

Responsibilities

  • You will ensure reliable ICT support and process inquiries about Windows workstations, Microsoft 365 and specialist applications
  • In the event of disruptions, you will analyse the causes, find solutions and, if necessary, coordinate the escalation to the IT Department of the City of Bern or to external service providers
  • You will manage our specialist applications and develop pragmatic digital solutions with Power Apps, Power Automate, Teams and SharePoint
  • You will take on board the needs of our departments and translate them into digital solutions and optimized business processes
  • You will accompany ICT, migration and digitization projects and participate in tests, changes and rollouts
  • You will support our employees with understandable instructions and training and promote the safe use of digital tools

Requirements

  • You have completed training as an ICT specialist EFZ, computer scientist EFZ specialising in platform development or comparable training
  • You have practical experience in ICT support as well as good knowledge of Windows workstations and Microsoft 365
  • You're familiar with Teams, SharePoint, and OneDrive. Experience with Power Apps and Power Automate is an advantage or you're willing to delve into it
  • You are familiar with Intune, Entra ID, permissions and IT security. Basics in networks and IT service management are also welcome
  • You analyze problems systematically, work in a structured manner and translate the needs of different user groups into practical solutions
  • As a service-oriented and proactive personality, you communicate in an understandable way, enjoy working in a team and share your knowledge with others

We offer

For Sense & City Life With us, you make a difference. You help shape city life, bear responsibility and make Bern more livable.

In the city of Bern, more than 4,500 people work together across five directorates and more than 80 professions. We are committed with passion and conviction to the people who live here, who work here. Together, we shape the community and the future of this city – visibly, tangibly, sustainably. For our city. For Bern.
