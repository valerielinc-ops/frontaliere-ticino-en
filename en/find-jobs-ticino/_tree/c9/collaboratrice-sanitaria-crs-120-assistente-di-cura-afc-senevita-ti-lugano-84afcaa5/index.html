Full description

Senevita Casa is the private Spitex service of Senevita, dedicated to outpatient care at home. We offer customized performance to promote the autonomy and quality of life of older people in their home environment. As part of the largest private provider of elderly services in Switzerland, Senevita Casa integrates our network of assisted residences and care facilities, bringing professional support directly to home. For the headquarters Senevita Casa Ticino – Region Luganese -, let's look at: Health worker CRS 120/ Care assistant AFC
