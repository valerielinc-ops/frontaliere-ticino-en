Full description

Welcome to the hospital Schwyz. Health is our highest good. That is why we do everything every day to help people – professionally and personally. As a regionally anchored hospital, we care about the direct relationship with the population of the region and their trust. The extensive range of medical care and modern infrastructure make the Schwyz Hospital attractive for people from all over Central Switzerland – and beyond. The field of internal medicine includes infectiology as well as travel medicine, cardiology, nephrology, gastroenterology and oncology. Consiliarly, endocrinology, pneumology, hematology, neurology, rheumatology and liaison psychiatry are covered. The Hospital Schwyz Palliative Care Station has eight beds. Our interprofessional palliative care team consists of specialists with special training in the nursing, therapeutic and medical fields.
