Attached to the Vaudois Judicial Order, the nine cantonal justices of peace rule at first instance in civil matters . They are also authorities for the protection of adults and children . The justice of peace of the district of Lausanne, which employs more than 50 magistrates and collaborators, search for a head of chancery at the registry of the wards . Missions

  • To ensure the proper functioning of the registry of the wards .
  • To organize the activity of the clerks , the distribution of their work and the monitoring of cases at all stages of the proceedings .
  • To act as a reference to the managers of the files .
  • To deal with particularly complex files by mastering the procedures and business processes relating to the field .
