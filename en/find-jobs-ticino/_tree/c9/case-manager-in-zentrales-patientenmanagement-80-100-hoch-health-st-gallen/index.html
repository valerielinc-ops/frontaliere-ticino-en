Full description

Case Manager/in central patient management 80-100 %

Clinic for Radiology and Nuclear Medicine

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Participation in our central administration with clearly recognizable service character and high call volume
  • Contact person for our patients, external customers and internal clinics
  • Complex scheduling and coordination of all outpatient and inpatient examinations
  • Responsibility for running smoothly, taking into account medical, organisational and patient-related aspects
  • Patient care at the reception
  • General administrative work in the field of findings, reporting and import and export of image material

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as a Med. Practice assistant/EFZ, pharmaceutical assistant/EFZ or specialist health EFZ
  • Experience in a similar function, preferably in the hospital environment
  • Verified in medical terminology and very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Strong customer and service orientation as well as high networked thinking skills
  • Team-oriented and resilient personality with an independent and responsible way of working
  • Willingness to change and innovation
  • Readiness for cross-site cooperation in the Radiology and Nuclear Medicine Network (operation sites St.Gallen and Rorschach)

Our offer to you

  • Constructive and committed cooperation in an appreciative and collegial team
  • Comprehensive introductory concept and regulated working hours without weekend service
  • Varied challenges in a highly specialized interdisciplinary environment
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
  • Various offers for employees in the field of health promotion, job and family, mobility and various benefits
