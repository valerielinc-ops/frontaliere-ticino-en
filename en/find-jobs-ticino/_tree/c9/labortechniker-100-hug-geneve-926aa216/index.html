Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is attached to the Diagnostic Department. This department, consisting of nine medical services, is responsible for providing diagnostics, therapeutic products, therapeutic procedures, and specialized, high-quality medical clinical consultations, as soon as possible and at an acceptable cost, to guide the management of patients and contribute to therapeutic decisions and actions. The Clinical Pathology Service provides clinical diagnostic activity, mainly on tissues derived from biopsies or operating parts. This activity provides guidance for the management of patients and makes a major contribution to therapeutic decisions. By carrying out medical autopsyes and by its research and development activities, the department contributes to the understanding of the physiopathological mechanisms involved in injury processes. The service also has research and teaching missions. Since 2019, the SPC has been making its diagnostics according to a fully digital and accredited workflow. The Laboratory of Computational Pathology and Clinical Bioinformatics (COBIO) deals with all aspects of digital pathology from the beginning of the digitisation process, the development, validation and implementation of computer-assisted image analysis, data management, to the archiving of digital data. The COBIO laboratory works closely with other laboratories in our department such as the tissue and cell pathology laboratory or molecular pathology laboratory or the molecular and genomic pathology laboratory.

Description of post

Within the COBIO team, you participate in daily clinical/diagnostic activities in digital pathology and digital pathology and contribute to the development, deployment and monitoring of innovative tools and algorithms for diagnostic routines. This leads for the flow of digital pathology to provide support and training for users in digital pathology tools, viewers and software for image analysis (pathologists/doctors, biologist(s) technician(s) and trainees), to coordinate with the manufacturers and other services of the HUG the maintenance of the scanners and the computing infrastructure of COBIO. You are also required or required to evaluate and propose new instruments and procedures, notably for diagnostic scanning, performing quality control of blades and scans and contributing to the quality approach and ISO 15189 accreditation of the laboratory and service. Concerning the development of tools and algorithms for the diagnostic workflow you are led or led to lead projects of digital pathology in close collaboration with pathologists and other members of the COBIO laboratory and other laboratories of the service, develop and train algorithms of analysis of images and deep learning (deep learning) on whole blades (Whole Slide Images), adapt, test and reliability of research tools for clinical use. You also need to integrate the tools into the existing infrastructure (e.g., Sector), in collaboration with HUG computing if necessary, validate the tools for clinical use in an ISO 15189 accredited framework and ensure continuous monitoring and improvement of the tools in production. For the Academic Plan you will participate in the implementation of the development, clinical innovation and research projects in close collaboration with the medical officer, you will participate in the congresses, seminars and teaching activities of the service and collaborate with the research group in Computational Pathology and take part in its scientific meetings and exchanges. We offer you a position in a pioneering service in digital pathology, integrated with the University Hospitals of Geneva (HUG) with active participation in the daily workflow in digital pathology, in direct collaboration with pathologists and other laboratories of the SPC. You will have the opportunity to participate in the development, validation and integration of innovative software solutions within the diagnostic process while being part of a modern digital infrastructure based on a proven validation framework. You have a sense of responsibility, an awareness of clinical issues, a team spirit and a capacity to listen and communicate and know how to collaborate in a multidisciplinary environment and have the ability to manage routine and projects in parallel. Pragmatic and with a sense of service, you have a strong interest in innovation and continuous improvement.

Qualifications

You have a Master's degree and a PhD in Biology. You master common Git-type development tools, Docker. Current French (C1) and good command of English (B2). You have a good experience in Python programming, including Python environment management and with the usual scientific libraries (NumPy, pandas, scikit-image or OpenCV), as well as a practical experience in image analysis, ideally biomedical. You also have a deep learning experience (PyTorch or TensorFlow), from training to model validation, with entire blades (WSI): proprietary formats and DICOM, libraries such as OpenSlide. An experiment is also required with Databases and SQL, using REST API, QuPath, image analysis laboratory, as well as clear background and fluorescence microscopy and project conduct. You have knowledge of pathology image management systems (e.g., Section) and blade scanners.
