Full description

The Office for Industry, Trade and Labour (KIGA) currently employs around 135 employees in six regions in the canton of Graubünden. In addition to the public employment service (RAV), we organise labour market measures for jobseekers and run the Cantonal unemployment insurance fund. We also carry out various tasks in the field of labour market and occupational safety. We are looking for labour market measures as of 1 October 2026 or, by agreement, for a head/in kind work and assistant in department manager (80-100%). Working conditions

  • Professional and personnel management of around 5 employees
  • Coordination of work and, if necessary, cooperation in the field of expertise
  • Ensuring close cooperation within the department and with assigning bodies
  • Supporting department management for coordination and planning of labour market measures
  • Advising RAV employees and jobseekers for work market measures
  • Ensuring the external quality in application processing and user applications
