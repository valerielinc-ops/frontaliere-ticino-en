Full description

In a context of internal mobility and transformation of our organization, we are looking for a Quality Supplier Facilitator to strengthen our Quality Supplier Assurance team. The position is based in the Brassus as a first step and is intended to evolve in the long term towards our Meyrin site, as part of the development of our industrial activities. Attached to the Quality Supplier Manager, you act as a quality reference with our external partners, with a key role both operational and strategic. You drive and animate the quality suppliers on your perimeter, from the development phase to the follow-up in serial production. In this context, your main responsibilities will be:

  • Ensure conformity of products and drive the achievement of GO Quality suppliers
  • Manage, analyze and track non-compliance suppliers
  • Deploy problem solving and continuous improvement tools
  • Perform quality product and process audits among suppliers
  • Monitor supplier performance through indicators and reporting
  • Contribute to the validation of new developments (capability, control plans, risks)
  • Provide operational support to internal control and production teams
  • Transverse coordination with internal teams (quality, industrialization, procurement, technical office, logistics, production)
  • Engineering training in mechanics, microtechnics or equivalent
  • Experience confirmed in quality assurance suppliers
  • Good control of quality tools (AMDEC, capability, problem solving, etc.)
  • Knowledge in metrology
  • Experience in a demanding industrial environment (watchmaking an asset)
  • Ability to manage a supplier portfolio with autonomy
  • Excellent communication skills and reference posture
  • Structured, rigorous profile with both field and strategic vision What we offer:
  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual holiday weeks and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The ability to work remotely depending on the function
  • A business restaurant
  • An advantageous PBL Would you like to express your expertise at Audemars Piguet? Don't hesitate and apply online! We look forward to discussing the opportunities that might match your next professional adventure. The male is the female.
