Full description

In our own canteens at various locations, we ensure the well-being of our employees every day. With fresh ingredients, a lot of passion and a well-established team, we create places of recreation and enjoyment. Become part of our gastro family and bring your talent to our kitchen!

What you move

  • In the warm and cold kitchen you prepare our food independently and with much pleasure (Monday to Friday from 6:00 to 15:00)
  • During the lunch service you set the plates in front of the guest and can recommend your delicious dishes and actively sell them.
  • You take care of the daily administrative tasks on the PC: help with quotation planning, writing menu plans, supplier orders, etc.
  • Apéros, office supplies, creative event meals and our rich snacks you prepare with a lot of commitment and a sense of pleasure
  • In the kitchen you pack everywhere, support with the dishes and cleaning work and ensure order
  • You ensure compliance with our regulations and quality standards and ensure that the cleaning concept is implemented reliably

Why do you succeed?

  • The passion for cooking and your strong service orientation distinguish you
  • You bring a completed training as a chef as well as several years of professional experience. Experience in Community Gastronomy is a Plus
  • When dealing with digital systems you feel comfortable, have very good PC knowledge and like to do administrative tasks
  • You like to get involved, be flexible and resilient and appreciate teamwork
  • An independent, reliable and well-organized way of working is a matter of course for you. You also attach importance to cleanliness and orderliness
  • In the absence of the restaurant manager, you take responsibility for the kitchen and ensure that it runs smoothly.

Your Benefits

  • Generous holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Modern work equipment: We keep up with the times – modern and safe work equipment is available to you
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
