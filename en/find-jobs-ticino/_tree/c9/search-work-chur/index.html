More about this search for cross-border applicants

Chur is the capital of Graubünden and a regional employment centre, with 163 vacancies currently grouped under this search. The local market includes opportunities in healthcare, retail, hospitality, logistics, construction, administration and technical services, with both entry-level and experienced roles appearing depending on the employer. Candidates should check each advert for working hours, qualifications, location and language requirements. The city’s rail connections and road links also make commuting from nearby Graubünden communities practical, although daily travel from northern Italy is substantially longer.
