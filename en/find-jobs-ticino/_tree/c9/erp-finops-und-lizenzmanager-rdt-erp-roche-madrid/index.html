The Position Job description The Senior ERP Systems Engineer (ERP FinOps and License Manager) leads the strategic financial governance, cost optimization, and license/credit/subscription utilization for the organization's global ERP and SAP environments. This role bridges the gap between IT, Finance, and Procurement to ensure the company remains fully compliant with vendor licensing rules while actively eliminating overspending, cloud waste, and token consumption leakages. Acting as both an analyst and a strategic advisor, you will govern holistically software assets alongside utility-based consumption models (such as SAP Private and Public Cloud subscriptions, entitlements and AI Units). By turning complex system-utilization data into actionable cost-containment frameworks, you will maximize the long-term return on investment (ROI) for the entire enterprise application ecosystem. Description of the area The RDT ERP Systems Management is the foundational custodian of the organization’s enterprise application landscape. We oversee the stability, security, compliance, and lifecycle evolution of critical global templates (including SAP S/4HANA Digital Core and legacy environments). Within this area, the FinOps and License Management becomes the intersection of platform license integrity and corporate financial efficiency. Moving away from manual legacy tracking, this role leverages system usage telemetry, automation tools, and predictive data modeling to govern entitlements, optimize utility-based cloud consumption (such as SAP RISE, SaaS portfolio (SAP and non-SAP), and BTP/Generative and Agentic AI pipelines), and maintain permanent audit readiness in a highly regulated corporate landscape.
