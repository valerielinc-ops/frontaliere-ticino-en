Technician/in Building Automation/MSRL 100% Place of work: Landquart We are a Swiss company with competences in building automation. With our location in Landquart and 8 employees, we are active throughout the canton of Graubünden and in adjacent areas. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the leading provider of cross-trade building technology in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. Are you ready for the next career step? We are looking for a technician in building automation / MSRL (100%). Your tasks

  • The quality, schedule and cost-related programming and commissioning of control and visualization software for complex HVAC systems and room automation
  • Technical support for the development of specifications, specifications, schedules and schedules
  • Optimization, maintenance and support of existing systems
  • Optionally, with appropriate experience, the complete project management from planning to acceptance

Your profile

  • Several years of experience in the areas of PLC programming, building technology, building and room automation with KNX and Dali
  • Knowledge of text-based programming for example C#, C++ or ST
  • Practical experience in construction and commissioning
  • Good understanding, analytical thinking and action
  • Planning and organizational skills, as well as teamwork skills
  • Communication skills and responsibility

Your advantages

  • above average salary
  • Flexible working hours
  • 5+ weeks of vacation
  • An established and motivated team
  • A versatile area of responsibility
  • Modern workplace
  • Start your day healthy with fruits, vegetables, coffee, tea etc.
