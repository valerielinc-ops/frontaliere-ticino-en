Clinical specialist (a) 50\-100 %

Location: Seewis rehabilitation clinic Where your job is doing rehab.

Cardiology, Internal Medicine / Oncology and Psychiatry / Psychosomatics. First-class rehabilitation, embedded in a magnificent mountain landscape. With over 50 years of experience. The environment in the Seewis rehabilitation clinic also benefits the approximately 135 employees.

Here you will be inspired interdisciplinary and work at eye level with doctors and therapists. Subject, leadership, education: Freely shape your career.

You can look forward to this

  • Support and close cooperation with the doctors of psychosomatics in direct contact with the chief physician psychiatry

  • Support in disease and crisis management, recognition of early warning signs and initiation of appropriate measures
  • Ensuring drug management and professional medication
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors: inside, psychologist: inside, therapist: inside, relatives and external bodies as well as securing the flow of information

  • Further education as APN, PA in psychiatry is advantageous
  • Strong social and relationship skills
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Regular presence from Monday to Friday without shift work, if possible with daily presence also in partial work.
