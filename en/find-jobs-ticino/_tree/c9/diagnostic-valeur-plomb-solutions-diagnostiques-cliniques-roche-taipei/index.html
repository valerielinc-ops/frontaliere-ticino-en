The Position The Diagnostics Value Lead (DV Lead) for Clinical Diagnostics Solutions must have a strong purpose to enable and empower the team to deliver better outcomes under the motto: “To Improve lives by Pioneering Diagnostics and Shaping Effective Healthcare in Taiwan.” As the Lead, you will think and act entrepreneurially, taking a whole-system perspective. You will translate strategic thinking into rigorous and thoroughly developed business and marketing strategies specific to the Clinical Diagnostics portfolio, ensuring that your team thrives in both the near and long term.

The Clinical Diagnostics Solutions portfolio includes Liver disease portfolio, navify AlgoSuite, Neurology & Infectious Diseases, Cardiac & Critical Care, Women’s Health & Health Check, Precision Oncology (Foundation Medicine, Avenio portfolio, navify Clinical Hub, Axelios1) and Clinical Research. These portfolios include instruments, assays and associated digital solutions.
