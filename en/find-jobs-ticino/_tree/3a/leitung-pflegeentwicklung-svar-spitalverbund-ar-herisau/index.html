Your tasks

  • Accompanying change processes and supporting teams in the sustainable implementation of new standards
  • Develop, update and implement nursing standards, policies and concepts
  • Conducting case meetings in complex care situations
  • Derive and implement improvement measures from quality data, audits and evaluations
  • Implementation of scientific findings in nursing practice
  • Professional management and further development of the responsible care
  • Management of working groups (care-related and/or cross-sectoral tasks)
  • Active participation in the teams and support of the teams in the care process
  • Evaluation, controlling and further development of the used nursing assessments
  • Coordinating, Planning and Teaching Students FH (Bachelor of Science in Nursing)
  • Support for the further development of the Department of Care
  • Constructive cooperation with all interfaces of the department
  • Initiation, conception and implementation of new care services and care models within the Herisau Hospital
  • Defining, collecting and evaluating care-sensitive quality indicators

Your profile

  • Completion of the Master of Science in Care
  • You have practical experience in a Swiss hospital
  • Interest in cooperation in the interprofessional team
  • You enjoy developing new things and contributing your ideas
  • Responsible and patient-oriented basic attitude
