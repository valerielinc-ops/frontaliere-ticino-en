Client Consulting – Sales

Workload: 50-100% Location: Experience level: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means much more than simply completing tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards, and bring the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand, and your ideas, initiative, and contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to truly make a difference for our customers, your team, and your personal development.

What you achieve

  • Consulting & support with foresight: You accompany private and corporate clients in all insurance matters with genuine commitment and ensure tailor-made solutions for every individual need. As a client advisor, you are there when it matters.
  • Building long-term relationships: Trust is at the center of your work – you cultivate and develop sustainable customer relationships based on your excellent service.
  • Actively shaping growth: You have a good network and contribute your energy to the acquisition of new customers. Your commitment ensures that our customer base grows steadily. How can we support you in this? By providing you with your own customer base of private and corporate clients.
  • Marketing & sales support: With fresh ideas, you support our marketing campaigns, attend trade fairs and events to strengthen our network.
  • Always on the pulse of time: Through our basic training and continuous further education, you stay professionally up to date and offer the highest level of consulting competence.

What you bring

  • Experience & expertise: You bring experience in customer consulting or sales – ideally in the financial or insurance industry. Ambitious career changers who are ready to hit the ground running are also very welcome.
  • Communication talent: Inspiring and winning people over – it's in your blood. You shine through communication and negotiation strength. As a client advisor, you can argue convincingly and have proficient knowledge of German.
  • Service is your strength: Field service is your passion, and your high customer orientation and service readiness distinguish you.
  • Commitment & personal initiative: With above-average motivation and entrepreneurial spirit, you are ready to achieve great things and exceed targets.
  • Regional anchoring: Your place of residence is in the Rapperswil-Jona region, where you look after your customers. You have a strong network in this region and appreciate building and maintaining new contacts.

Ready to inspire customers with passion and grow with us? Then use your talent in a targeted way and apply now!
