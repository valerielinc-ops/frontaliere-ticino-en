80% - 100% | Sales and customer service | Dübendorf | Experienced professionals Our team of five is responsible for individual advice and support for wealthy private clients in the fast-growing Dübendorf region. We promote an inspiring and appreciative cooperation with internal and external contacts. Together with the branches Volketswil and Fehraltorf, we form a branch group in the market area Oberland-Pfannenstiel. The competence and commitment of our employees contribute significantly to our track record. Good teamwork and passionate commitment are our key factors for success. Take the opportunity to succeed together with us and our strength-based leadership culture. Your tasks

  • Supervising and developing existing wealthy private clients
  • Acquisition of new customers in the catchment area
  • Exploring customer needs taking into account individual wishes, goals and life situations – if necessary in cooperation with internal specialists
  • Distribution of standardised products and services in the areas of investment, real estate financing, provision and monetary transactions
  • Complete all front-related administration tasks
  • Implementation of long-term confidence building and asset preservation on the occasion of the generational change
  • Perceiving demanding representation tasks in the sense of "the near bank" and participation in customer events
  • Opportunity to take on training or a specialised role in financing, investing or pension schemes

Your profile

  • Banking education or equivalent
  • Further education e.g. HFBF / FA financial planner:in / Bachelor in Banking & Finance
  • Several years of experience in personal customer service
  • Team capability and willingness to go the extra mile to achieve ambitious team growth goals
  • Affinity and joy in using digital technologies in the consulting process
  • You score with your trusting, communicative and empathetic nature
  • Your acquisition-strong personality with a strong sales flair rounds off your profile

Our offer

  • Access to internal and external networks and mentoring programs (e.g. Queers & Peers, Fund Women Membership, Network "Human" and others)
  • Long-term prospects in a stable, sustainable environment at one of the safest universal banks in the world
