The Position Summary of the position

As the Senior Legal Counsel for the Legal entities across Pune, Hyderabad and Chennai Sites, you will serve as the primary legal partner for Roche’s growing Global Capability Center (GCC) as it scales to 1,000+ employees. You will provide strategic legal and business support to the Site Leadership team, including key organizational units, across Hyderabad, Pune and Chennai sites, such as Roche Services and Solutions (RSS), Roche Information Solutions (RIS), Roche Diagnostics Solutions (RDS), Roche Digital Technology (RDT) and future teams that start operations. As the first legal hire at this site, you are expected to have a “hands on” approach to your work, as outside counsel support will be limited. You will be responsible for architecting the site’s legal framework, managing corporate and commercial agreements, and navigating the interface between local operational requirements and global standards. You will liaise with counsel at Roche’s local Indian affiliate supporting its pharmaceutical and diagnostics businesses, as necessary. This role requires a pragmatic, collaborative approach to risk management, government engagement, and stakeholder networking, ensuring the Indian site’s ecosystem expansion is legally sound and operationally compliant.

Main tasks of the position

  • Strategic Legal Partner: Act as a key member of the Site Leadership Team, providing counsel on corporate and operational matters, including crisis management and site strategy.
  • Commercial & Operational Support: Lead the review, vetting, and drafting of essential site agreements, including service agreements, vendor contracts, and lease agreements for the Hyderabad facility.
  • Government & Regulatory Engagement: Manage relationships with relevant government and regulatory stakeholders. Provide expert advice on local regulations, compliance, and government affairs to safeguard Roche’s interests.
  • Compliance: Partner closely with Compliance to implement and maintain a culture of integrity.
  • Data, Digital & Privacy: Support digital, IT, and privacy-related legal inquiries, including interaction with Roche’s Global Legal Data, Digital & Privacy team.
  • Global Networking: Act as the Single Point of Contact (SPOC) for legal topics at the site. Network with global stakeholders to align on best practices, transfer pricing, and cross-divisional partnerships.
  • Education & Enablement: Lead legal training sessions for site employees and ensure adherence to company policy.
  • Change Management: Support the rapid scaling of the site (target: 1,000+ employees), addressing employment law requirements and operational legal needs as the business grows.
