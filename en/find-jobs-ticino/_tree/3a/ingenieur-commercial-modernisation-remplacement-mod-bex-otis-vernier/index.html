Your role

As a MOD/BEX Commercial Engineer, you link technology, customers and business development by identifying and developing opportunities for modernization and replacement in the Geneva region. Through a consultative sales approach, you analyze your customers' needs, develop tailored Otis solutions, negotiate contracts and work closely with operational teams to create lasting value for our customers.

Your main responsibilities

Sales & commercial development

  • Manage and develop a portfolio of existing and potential clients in the Geneva region.
  • Identify opportunities for modernization, replacement and upgrading of existing facilities.
  • Develop business strategies and action plans tailored to each client.
  • Conduct customer visits in accordance with the business plan.
  • Prepare technical and commercial offers tailored to the needs of customers.
  • Manage tenders and price requests, from initial analysis to tender and negotiation.
  • Negotiate commercial terms and conclude MOD/BEX contracts.
  • Develop a strong commercial pipeline and achieve agreed sales, orders and profitability targets.
  • Analyze the market and monitor the evolution of customer needs, competition and business opportunities.

Customer relationship management

  • Develop and strengthen sustainable relationships with clients and key partners.
  • Act as a trusted technical and commercial partner by proposing suitable modernization solutions.
  • Proactively manage opportunities and customer communication throughout the sales cycle.
  • Support clients in planning their short- and long-term modernization investments.

Technical coordination & projects

  • Assess client needs and existing facilities in collaboration with technical and operational teams.
  • Translate technical needs into attractive and commercially viable solutions.
  • Ensure that offers, contracts and documents meet internal and regulatory requirements.
  • Ensure structured transmission of projects to MOD/BEX operational teams.
  • Collaborate with operational teams on client feasibility, planning and commitments.

Performance & reporting

  • Achieve agreed sales, orders and profitability targets.
  • Maintain commercial pipeline, sales forecasts and customer data.
  • Monitor business opportunities, offers, orders and performance.
  • Communicate business results in accordance with internal processes.
  • Continuously develop knowledge of products, modernization solutions and the market.

Your profile

  • Advanced technical training in engineering, building technology or similar field.
  • Experience in technical sales, ideally in the modernization or replacement of elevators (MOD/BEX), building technologies or comparable technical solutions.
  • Strong business skills and ability to transform technical needs into customer-friendly solutions.
  • Experience in client portfolio management, bidding, tendering and contracting is an asset.
  • Excellent customer orientation and strong communication, negotiation and business relationship development skills.
  • Proactive, autonomous and results-oriented personality, with a good team spirit and excellent organizational sense.
  • Good digital skills, including CRM and Microsoft Office tools.
  • Mastery of French and good English
  • German a plus.
  • Driver's licence category B valid and available for travel in the area.
