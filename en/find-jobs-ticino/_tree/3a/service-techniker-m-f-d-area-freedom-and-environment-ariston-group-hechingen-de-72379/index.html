Full description

ELCO has been a premium provider of heating solutions since 1928. With our first-class services, we meet the needs of our customers throughout the entire life cycle of the delivered systems and products. With our durable, climate-friendly and highly efficient heating solutions and our multi-award-winning heat pumps, we are pioneers in the heat transition. Together with our specialist partners, we plan and implement customized solutions for private households, businesses, municipalities, industry and the housing industry. With one of the largest, comprehensive service technician networks in Germany, we offer a wide range of excellent services. Design your and our future sustainably with ELCO and become an ELCO thermal hero!

For the expansion of our team we are looking for a

Service technician / plant mechanic SHK in the heating plant customer service (m/f/d) area Freiburg and the surrounding area

  • Commissioning, maintenance and interference elimination, including in the field of regenerative energy

ITS QUALIFICATIONS

  • Experience in refrigeration is desirable
  • Customer-oriented appearance and behaviour
  • driving licence category III, or B
  • Place of residence in the service area
