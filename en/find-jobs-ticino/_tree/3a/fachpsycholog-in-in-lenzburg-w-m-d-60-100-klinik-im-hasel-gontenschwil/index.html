Full description

Klinik Im Hasel AG is a psychiatric-psychotherapeutic specialist clinic with a cantonal service mandate for the treatment of addiction and trauma disorders. As a health insurance-approved institution and part of the cantonal hospital list, we offer high-quality, evidence-based and human-oriented treatment for our patients. With around 170 dedicated employees at three locations – our inpatient clinic in Gontenschwil, the ambulatorium and the day clinic in Lenzburg and the trauma-specific ambulatorium in Baden – we combine professional competence with an appreciative and interdisciplinary cooperation.

Specialist psychologist: in Lenzburg (m/f/d), 60-100 %

For our Ambulatorium Lenzburg we are looking for reinforcement in the field of psychotherapy immediately or by agreement.

Your tasks

  • Independent implementation of outpatient psychotherapies in cooperation with psychiatric specialists:
  • Independent management and co-management of psychotherapeutic group therapies
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with internal and external bodies
  • Participation in team meetings, intervision and supervisio

Your profile

  • Psychology degree or equivalent and advanced or completed psychotherapy training
  • Experience in the field of dependency diseases and/or trauma follow-up disorders is advantageous
  • Desire to contribute yourself and your own ideas in order to be a good companion to the affected people:
  • Openness, creativeness, collegiality, sense of responsibility, resilience and motivation
  • High identification with the mission statement and the conceptual basis of the therapeutic work of Klinik Im Hasel AG
  • We expect a basic attitude that is characterized by appreciation and respect in dealing with our patients: inside.

Our offer

  • Self-employed and responsible activities
  • Dedicated and established team
  • Further education opportunities and employee benefits
  • 5 weeks of holidays (from 60 years of age 6 weeks)
  • Well-developed occupational health management
