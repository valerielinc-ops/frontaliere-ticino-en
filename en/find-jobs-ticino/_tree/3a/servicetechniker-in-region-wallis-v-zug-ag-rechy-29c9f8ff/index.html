Service technician:in – Region Wallis

You work with a 100% workload from your home base in Réchy – with a fixed service vehicle and an area of application that extends from Gletsch to Montreux

V\-ZUG Field Service Switzerland

The Field Service Switzerland ensures with passion and expertise that our high-quality devices run impeccably and provide our customers with pleasure every day. With 16 locations throughout Switzerland, we are always close by: reliable, solution-oriented and personal. Whether installation, maintenance or repair – we stand for first-class service directly on site. Those who work with us are on the road a lot, close to the people and part of a well-established team that supports each other and celebrates successes together.

This gives you the job

  • You will be responsible for the diagnosis, repair, inspection and maintenance of washing machines and dryers – directly at customers’ homes, in an area of application close to home. No day is like the other: every mission brings a new situation, a new device, a new challenge
  • You make binding repair offers and recognize when a repair makes sense – and when an exchange is the more honest recommendation. This assessment is in your hands, and you carry it with the necessary expertise and backbone
  • You are not only a technician, but also a consultant: you instruct customers about the operation, care and maintenance of their devices, clarify connection options and take the time to really answer questions – not to tick off
  • You document your bets digitally via tablet: service reports, daily deals, offers – clean, complete and timely. This is part of the craft
  • You’ll interact with your team every day in the service center – in the morning meeting – and stay up-to-date with service bulletins and technical updates. There is no standstill here, neither technically nor personally

You bring this for the job

  • You have completed basic craft or electrotechnical training (EFZ or EBA) – ideally as an electrical installer:in, electronics technician:in or in a comparable profession
  • You’ll bring solid electrotechnical knowledge and know how to approach an unknown device – systematically, safely and without starting from the worst case. Ideally, you already bring the NIV 15 permit or are motivated to complete the required further training with us
  • You enjoy direct customer contact and have the ability to explain technical issues so that they arrive at someone who is not a technician – friendly, clear, at eye level
  • You work independently and reliably: You know that on some days it gets tight with the schedule, and you still find a way to reconcile quality and deadlines.
  • You are physically fit and do not have to face demanding assignments – whether it is a laundry room or installation situation that seems puzzling when you first look at it.
  • You communicate safely in German and ösisch
  • Other languages are a real plus
  • You have a category B driver’s license

As part of the V\-ZUG family, you benefit from contemporary employment conditions, diverse development opportunities, state-of-the-art equipment and numerous offers and services for your personal well-being.
