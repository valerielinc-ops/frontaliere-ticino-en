Full description

Enter the world of the Nestlé shop and contribute to the sale of various products, including chocolate, specialty coffee, frozen food and pet food.

  • Location: Nestlé Shop, Landquart
  • Employer: Randstad
  • Contract: Contract of indefinite duration
  • Pensum: 20% - 100%

The Nestlé Shop stands for the diversity and excellence of the brands of the Nestlé Group. In the shop in Landquart, customers are offered exclusive access to the entire product ranges (coffee, chocolate, food, nutrition, etc.).

As Nestlé seller (m/f/d)

Share your expertise and passion for the Nestlé Group brands by creating a welcoming and professional shopping experience every day. As an ambassador for the Nestlé Shop, you promote and sell a wide range of products ranging from popular classics to the latest innovations.

As the first point of contact, you guarantee impeccable service. Your daily tasks include:

  • Advice and sales: They recommend, present and sell the products of the various Nestlé brands in order to respond optimally to customer needs.
  • Tasting: You revitalize the shop by inviting customers to discover new products during tastings.
  • Warehouse management: They manage orders, take delivery and ensure the constant availability of products on the shelves.
  • Merchandising: They ensure a perfect presentation of the sales area and an attractive design of the displays.
  • Logistics and handling: You take over the regular replenishment of stocks (this includes moving loads, especially beverage crates).
  • Administration: You are responsible for the cashier process and the daily administrative support of the shop.

Your profile

  • Experience: You have experience in direct sales or in the field of fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG / retail trade).
  • Languages: fluent knowledge of German is mandatory.
  • Physical condition: A good physical constitution is essential for this position, as regular handling and moving of goods and drink packages is part of the job.
  • Soft skills: They are characterised by team spirit, a strong service orientation, proactivity and integrity.

Why Randstad? When selected for use in the Nestlé Shop via Randstad, you become part of a dynamic environment. You benefit from a stimulating working framework and the opportunity to represent globally renowned brands. Randstad accompanies you throughout your assignment to ensure successful cooperation.
