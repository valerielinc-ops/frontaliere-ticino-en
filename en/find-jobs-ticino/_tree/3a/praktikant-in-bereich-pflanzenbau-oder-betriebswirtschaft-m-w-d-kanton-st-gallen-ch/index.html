Full description

The Agrarliche Zentrum St.Gallen (LZSG) is part of the Department of Agriculture. It fulfils a versatile task in agricultural consulting as well as in education and training.

What to expect

Focus on crop production

  • Supporting the specialists in plant protection, fruit and agriculture in their diverse fields of activity (consultation, field trials, monitoring)
  • Collaborators in the Plant Health Service Vegetable Farming
  • Supervision of the practical test plots
  • Accompanying projects in the field of crop variety and plant breeding or focus on business administration
  • Support the Business Administration Department in individual consulting and in courses
  • Processing of business and legal issues
  • Accompanying experienced consultants (development of operational concepts, financing plans, assessments regarding the sustainability of investment projects, construction consultancies, yield and market value estimates as well as lease and land law)

What sets it apart

  • Student in agronomy or equivalent degree programmes
  • Interest and pleasure in plant cultivation and variety of crops or business administration

What we offer

  • Insights into agricultural advice
  • Competent, professional and personal internship support
  • Motivated, committed and collaborative team
  • Valuable contacts for further professional career
  • Accommodation with cooking facilities
