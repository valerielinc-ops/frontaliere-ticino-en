In Process Inspector Operations / Employee Process Control

Summary of function

As an In Process Inspector, you are responsible for the print releases in the print shop, and the IPC Inspector creates

Reprint files and reinsert the reprinted labels at the original position.

He/she checks the completeness and correctness at the end of each print job and then releases it in the system.

All finished print jobs are prepared by the In Process Inspector for internal or external shipping.

The In Process Inspector operates the machines for automatic camera control of the labels.

Your tasks

  • Verification and release of print jobs
  • GMP-compliant documentation of the tasks performed
  • Creating Reprint Files
  • Printing documentation verification
  • Release of controlled and finished print jobs in the Clinical Label Services System
  • Provision of goods for external and internal dispatch
  • Operation of machines for automatic camera control of labels

Your profile

  • Experience in quality assurance is beneficial
  • Experience in handling machines
  • Experience in the pharmaceutical or food industry is advantageous
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (English knowledge of advantage)
  • Experience in dealing with computers / Basic knowledge of MS-Office applications
  • Personal responsibility, independent work and reliability
  • Initiative, flexible and capable of teamwork
