Mandated by one of our recognized customers and leading player in Suisse Romande we are looking for a Heavy Weight Driver (m/f) for a fixed engagement for early 2027\.

Heater Heavy Weight (m/f)

Your missions and responsibilities

Ensure the safe transport, delivery and unloading of steel and aluminium-treated metal structures and industrial profiles to customers and construction sites in the region by means of an articulated package.

  • Drive the articulated assembly safely, economically and in accordance with the road code
  • Perform complex back-up and docking manoeuvres on construction sites and small delivery areas
  • Ensure that parts are secured and that the elements transported are protected in order to avoid friction, scratches or damage during journeys
  • Actively participate and supervise cargo loading and unloading
  • Provide basic technical monitoring of the truck (levels, pressures, cabin cleanliness) and report any anomalies
  • Maintain transport documents, road maps and comply with legislation (driving and rest hours)

Your profile

You have a proven experience in heavy-duty driving in industrial environments, metal construction, wood frame or other special structures in construction and have ease in customer relations

Here are the mandatory prerequisites

  • Driver's licence of the current EC category.
  • Digital driver card and current OACP capacity certificate.
  • Experience confirmed for several years in driving and handling trailers in Switzerland.
  • Perfect command of the French language (oral and written) for direct contact with customers.
  • Flexibility, good physical condition, autonomy and a strong sense of responsibility.
