Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland.
The interdisciplinary emergency department at the Sursee location stands for competent, patient-oriented and interprofessional emergency care – around the clock. Thanks to a central triage and the Fast Track and Nuclear Emergency treatment pathways, patients of all urgency levels benefit from efficient and needs-based care at a high quality level. Every year, more than 20,000 patients of all emergency levels are treated over 24 hours / 365 days.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 September 2026 or by agreement. Tasks: • As part of our interprofessional team, you will work closely with nursing experts and contribute to competent initial care in a nuclear emergency. • In the established Fast Track you act independently, work closely with the medical service and actively participate in the continuous development • They are in direct patient contact and support our patients and their relatives in demanding situations. • In addition, you take on tasks in housekeeping, administration, medical technology and logistics and thus contribute to a smooth process throughout the hospital operation. Requirements • You have completed your training as a FAGE EFZ and ideally bring your first experience in the acute field • They convince with high social skills and real team spirit • Your quick comprehension, your understanding of people in exceptional situations and your high resilience round off your profile • A real added value: You are happy to take over night and weekend services and with great flexibility We offer: • Enjoy a dedicated team and a professional working environment with flat hierarchies and a culture at eye level • As the largest employer in Central Switzerland, you benefit from numerous benefits – including internal education and attractive social benefits that go beyond the standard • Our modern clinic information system supports you in managing all patient data and processes digitally and efficiently
