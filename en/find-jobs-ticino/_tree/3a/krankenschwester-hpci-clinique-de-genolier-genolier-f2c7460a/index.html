Full description

Implement, communicate and ensure compliance with hospital hygiene measures and infection prevention protocols within the clinic, in collaboration with the clinical management team, infectious disease specialist, hygiene committee and field representatives. Develop, review and update hygiene policies and procedures in accordance with current best practices and regulatory requirements, ensuring harmonization across all clinical services. Conduct regular audits and monitoring activities to assess compliance with hygiene standards, identify areas for improvement and implement the necessary corrective measures. Provide training to clinical and non-clinical staff on hygiene protocols, infection prevention measures and standards of good practice to foster a culture of compliance. Investigate, manage, document findings and recommend preventive measures to minimize future occurrences of hygiene incidents and near-accidents. Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams and external stakeholders to promote hygiene awareness, share expertise and facilitate knowledge exchange within the organization. Participate in institutional projects and quality improvement initiatives to bring your specialized expertise and support the achievement of organizational objectives.

  • Diploma of recognized nurse, preferably Swiss resident
  • Diploma in hospital hygiene
  • Sense of priorities in acute situations and good stress management
  • Self-organisation and work
  • Sense of multidisciplinary collaboration
  • Availability, flexibility and versatility
  • Willing to expand your skills in a specialized sector.
  • A varied activity in an exciting field, within a dynamic, stimulating and expanding clinic
  • A pleasant work environment
  • A human-sized structure
  • Wage and motivating social conditions

We pay great attention to the diversity of life paths of our collaborators. Our positions are open to both women and men.

Founded in 1972, the Genolier Clinic is one of Switzerland's largest private clinics. With a strong reputation, it brings together specialized medical care, state-of-the-art technology, an experienced medical team and top-quality hotel service under one roof. She is committed to putting each patient at the centre of her concerns thanks to her signature specialties such as oncology, orthopaedic surgery, back centre, plastic and aesthetic surgery, radiology, radiotherapy and health/check-up capital management. It also has a polyclinic. Integrated into the Swiss Medical Network SA in 2002, it is the flagship of the private clinic group.
