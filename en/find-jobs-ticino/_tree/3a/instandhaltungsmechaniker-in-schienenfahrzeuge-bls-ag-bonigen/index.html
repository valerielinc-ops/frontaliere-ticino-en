Full description

When trains are on the road reliably, there is also precise mechanical work behind them. In this role, you ensure that important railway components and mechanical systems of rail vehicles are professionally overhauled, repaired, tested and made ready for use again. You work on brake valves, couplings, sliding steps and brake units - and see directly what you are doing with your work. With your work, you make a direct contribution to the safety, reliability and availability of rail vehicles. Career changers are welcome - the necessary railway knowledge grows with your experience in daily work.

This is waiting for you

  • You will repair, overhaul and maintain mechanical and pneumatic railway components such as control and driver's brake valves, couplings and e-couplings, traction and shock devices, sliding steps and braking units
  • You will analyse and diagnose faults in mechanical, pneumatic and mechatronic components and independently carry out maintenance, revision and repair work on components of rail vehicles
  • After overhauling or repairing the components, you will test their function and quality on modern test benches
  • You will ensure functional checks, quality checks in accordance with specifications and complete documentation
  • Through close teamwork, technical skills and thinking, you contribute to smooth processes and improved processes

What you bring to the table

  • Apprenticeship with EFZ in the mechanical or mechatronic field, e.g. as a polymechanic, automotive mechatronics technician, agricultural machinery or construction machinery mechanic
  • Experience in servicing, servicing, repair, revision, mechanics or pneumatics will make it easier for you to get started
  • Knowledge of rail vehicles or railway technology is welcome, but not a requirement.
  • You like to work practically and have a good technical understanding as well as manual dexterity
  • You take a structured approach to troubleshooting and work precisely, reliably and quality-consciously, the ability to work in a team, a sense of duty and a positive work attitude characterize you
  • Basic knowledge of MS Office, good knowledge of German for working in a team, knowledge of SAP as well as reading and understanding technical documentation is an advantage

Your benefits – here's what you can look forward to

  • Get a GA travelcard 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare card.
  • Organize your working hours freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy five to ten additional vacation days per year
  • Financial support for external training courses as well as an exciting range of internal learning and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.
  • 18 days of paternity leave and 18 weeks of maternity leave with the option to extend it for another 6 months as unpaid leave.

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland.
