Full description

Motivated to tackle an apprenticeship as a production mechanic? Ready to become a reference in your profession through a quality education? Then come to us!

As a production mechanic, you produce movement and equipment components using conventional machines and CNC-controlled systems. They are able to read technical drawings and prepare the tools and materials needed for parts manufacturing. They are responsible for the adjustment and control of the machines as well as the quality of the parts produced.

This training is aimed at young people who are interested in working with metal and working with industrial plants and enjoy precision work.

Profile

  • Logical and methodological thinking
  • Craftsmanship and sense of precision
