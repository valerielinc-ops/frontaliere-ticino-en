Your Mission

The Health, Prevention and Control of Infection Service (HPCI) aims to improve the quality and safety of care by controlling and preventing infections in hospitals. Infectious risk prevention applies to patients, visitors and staff.

Main missions

  • Participate in the definition and development of the HPCI programme at the institutional level
  • Guarantee the relay between the HPCI cantonal unit and medical-care professionals
  • Monitoring and prevention of hospital, nosocomial or endemic infections
  • Ensure detection, investigation, correction and prevention of epidemic infections
  • Control the resistance and use of anti-infective agents against multi-resistant microorganisms
  • Solve hospital hygiene problems by adapting the recommendations to the specific sectors
  • Continuously improve the quality of medical and technical care and procedures against infectious risk
  • Provide training, education and education to medical, nursing and therapeutic personnel in hygiene and infection prevention
  • Participate in the management of projects and cantonal studies under the HPCI
  • Provide expertise in internal and/or external committees or working groups

Your assets

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent
  • Holder of a degree in Infection Prevention and Control or deemed equivalent (EPIAS diploma, HPCI junior training, or HPCI qualifying certificate wishing to attend at least junior training)
  • 4 year general care experience
  • Experience in acute care
  • Being able to work independently and in an interdisciplinarity
  • Demonstrate understanding and diplomacy
  • Demonstrate a spirit of collaboration and negotiation
  • Have teaching and clinical leadership skills
  • Advanced knowledge of common computer tools (Windows environment, Microsoft Office: Word, Excel, Access)
  • Mastering oral and written French
  • Knowledge of professional English
  • Have a valid driver's licence (cat. B).
