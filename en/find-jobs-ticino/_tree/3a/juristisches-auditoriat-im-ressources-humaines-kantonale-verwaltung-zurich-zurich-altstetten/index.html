Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Justice and Reintegration Legal Auditoriat in HR 80 - 100%

''An employee gives a prisoner a high-five, is this personal-legally critical?'' You deal with these questions in everyday life. Does that make you curious? Then keep on being. Perhaps this is the right place for you. In the case of judicial enforcement and reintegration (JuWe), around 1,400 employees from around 40 professions work. We in the Human Resources (HR) department are responsible for all matters of concern to and in connection with the employees. As of 1 November 2026 or after agreement, the team is looking for a new team member.

A 12-month AudiJOIT awaits you with exciting tasks and a wide range of insights into legal practice in the public service.

Tasks

  • You clarify legal issues in a diverse and non-everyday environment and advise in personnel, social security and general administrative law
  • You prepare and participate in employee discussions
  • You write regulations, agreements and leaflets, check work certificates and support in HR projects
  • You fill and update the internal knowledge database
  • You work with the design and implementation of internal training courses in the field of personnel law

Requirements

  • Completed legal studies
  • Interest and curiosity in legal topics around employees
  • You are open to new things, ask questions and communicate with different people
  • Work in a team is important to you
  • And last but not least: A perfect reputation is absolutely necessary for this activity

We offer

  • cooperation in an uncomplicated, reliable and full-blown team
  • work close to people
  • insights behind the prison walls
  • Lets talk about money: your annual gross wage is over CHF 70`000
  • your lunch card costs half as much
  • For the examination of the lawyer you can charge yourself a part for the required practice year
  • Central workplace 2 min from the train station Zürich Altstetten

Contact Are you attracted by legal work close to life and in a non-everyday environment? Then send us your application via our online tool. We are happy!
