Become part of our team! We are looking for a person as of February 1, 2027, combined as: Management Support 48% & Hortleitung 12 %

Four days per week

In the school hall "Hafen" we look after up to 150 young people from the 1st to 3rd secondary school over lunch and after school.

  • Personnel management, promotion and development of employees*
  • Recruitment and introduction of new employees*
  • Quality assurance and development in care in cooperation with school management
  • Service and operational planning of employees, securing deputies
  • Administrative work related to personnel management, budget management and student allocation (using various tools such as peps.NET, PSL, etc.)
  • Coordination of training places and organisation of operations
  • Leadership of challenging parental talks
  • Contact person for external specialist offices / institutions
  • Organisation and securing of catering loans and their settlement
  • Graduate degree in pedagogy or social pedagogy at tertiary level (diploma of a higher technical school, teaching office, university or university)
  • Completed management training
  • Personnel management experience
  • Leader with high social skills
  • Professional experience in school or similar field
  • Organizational and communication skills, team experience
  • High motivation and interest in actively shaping new processes in cooperation with the school management
  • Responsible, versatile leadership task
  • Exciting, challenging field of impact in the school sector
  • A dedicated school team
  • Well-functioning processes in school
  • Central location with good public transport connection
  • Contemporary employment according to the guidelines of the city of Zurich

Zmitzt in the heart of Züri - there(s) are me! Every day at the pulse of education and where our future is growing and learning.

The Zurich-Limmattal school district (city districts 4, 5 and Sihlfeld district 3) is a school district in the middle of the city of Zurich with its 14 schools.
