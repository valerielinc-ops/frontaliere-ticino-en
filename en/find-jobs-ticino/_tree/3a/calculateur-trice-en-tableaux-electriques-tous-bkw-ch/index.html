Full description

We make spaces where it is good to live.

Beyond the post, it is above all a team that you join: a welded team in which the exchanges are direct, self-help is part of everyday life and everyone can really bring their ideas. We are working on great technical projects, and are growing!

Your tasks

You are at the heart of the preparation of our electrical painting projects. You analyze the needs of our customers and turn a technical file into a reliable, competitive and achievable offer.

Specifically, you will

  • analyse tenders, specifications, plans and electrical diagrams
  • complete encryption of low voltage electrical panels and cabinets, material as time of assembly
  • develop specifications, technical offers and bids
  • follow up on offers and carry out the necessary reviews with customers
  • consult suppliers and seek the best technical and tariff solutions
  • participate in technical exchanges with our clients and partners
  • work closely with the design office, project leaders and our manufacturing workshop
  • to monitor technical and tariff developments in electrical equipment
  • bring your technical expertise to project managers and the business department.

Your profile

You know the world of the electrical board and like to understand technically what you are encrypting.

Ideally

  • you are the holder of a CFC for automatics, assemblers, installers, or equivalent training
  • you have experience in the construction of electrical panels, automation or industrial electricity
  • you know the requirements and standards applicable to low voltage equipment sets, in particular EN/IEC 61439
  • you master reading electrical diagrams
  • you have already made calculation, encryption or technical offers
  • you are comfortable with Excel and IT tools; knowledge of VOLTA is an advantage
  • you are autonomous, organized and able to accurately estimate assembly times
  • You like contact with customers, suppliers and technical teams.
