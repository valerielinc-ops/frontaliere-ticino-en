Full description

Introduction

You also keep track of complex processes, lead your team in a goal-oriented manner and ensure that all logistical processes are integrated efficiently? For our customer we are looking for you as logistics manager (m/f/d). In this role, you take overall responsibility for goods receipt, warehousing, picking as well as shipping and transport - always with regard to quality, deadlines, costs and customer satisfaction.

Tasks

  • Leadership and further development of the logistics team (professional and personnel)
  • Ensuring a smooth material flow including internal supply
  • Responsibility for inventory management: definition of target values, implementation of inventories and clarification of deviations
  • Analysis and control of relevant performance indicators and derivation of improvements
  • Coordination of transport: planning, scheduling and cooperation with external service providers
  • Ensuring a high level of service
  • ensuring quality and safety guidelines in everyday operations
  • Compliance with dangerous goods specifications

Profile

  • Basic training as a logistician/EFZ; additional qualification in logistics is an advantage
  • Several years of experience in a management function within the logistics of an industrial company
  • Good knowledge of bearing control, material flow optimization and lean approaches
  • Secure handling of ERP and WMS systems as well as mobile detection devices
  • Structured and precise working method
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Advantages

  • Modern working environment with contemporary tools and processes
  • Support for further education and attractive development opportunities
  • Attractive and fair employment conditions
