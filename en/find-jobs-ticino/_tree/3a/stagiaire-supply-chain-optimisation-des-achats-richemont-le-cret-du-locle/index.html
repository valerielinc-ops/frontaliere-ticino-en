HOW DO YOU HAVE A IMPACT?

As the Supply Chain & Purchasing Intern, you will play a key role in the transformation of our department. You will intervene directly on the continuous improvement of our processes by working closely with our Purchasing teams and applying the principles of Lean Management. Your missions will include:

  • Analysis of current processes: Diagnosing inefficiencies and identifying areas for improvement in the management of component purchases and in our interactions with partners.
  • Process Optimization: Simplify and streamline administrative processes with advanced analytical tools and decision support tools to improve our overall efficiency.
  • Implementing Change: Supporting operational teams and our partners in the transformation of their practices, eliminating low value-added tasks and facilitating the adoption of new working methods.
  • Performance monitoring: Develop and deploy performance indicators (KPIs) to effectively manage processes and ensure alignment with the company's strategic objectives.

WHAT ARE THE CLES OF YOUR SURVEY IN OUR HOUSE?

  • Student in engineering school with orientation in Supply Chain, Purchasing or Industrial Engineering
  • Independent, curious, organized, rigorous and proactive person
  • Team spirit, relationship ease and creativity
  • Good knowledge of Lean Manufacturing concepts/tools
  • English (C1)

WHAT HAVE WE OFFERED TO YOU?

As part of this internship, you will develop your knowledge by:

  • Project management
  • Procurement management and optimization
  • Use of SAP tools and annexes
