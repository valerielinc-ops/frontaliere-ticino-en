With fun and skill do you work on machines and technical plants? Do you enjoy using your technical know-how every day?   • Conversion, inspection and construction work

  • Completed vocational training in the field of metal construction e.g. locksmith/metal builder (m/f/d), or industrial mechanic (m/f/d) or comparable qualification
  • Professional practice in the field of operational and building technology
  • Welding knowledge (E-hand and TIG) desirable
  • Good MS-Office knowledge
  • Independent and reliable working method
  • Teamwork capability
  • Flexibility
