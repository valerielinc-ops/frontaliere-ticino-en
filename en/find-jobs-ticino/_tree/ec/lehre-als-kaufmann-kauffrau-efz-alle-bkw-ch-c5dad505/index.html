Your field of activity

Hinni offers an apprenticeship with the two compulsory elective areas second foreign language (with or without vocational training) or individual project work. In the 3rd year you have the opportunity to deepen various competences (communication in German or French, finance, technologies). As a merchant or merchant, you independently perform a wide range of administrative and organizational tasks. You will gain insight into various departments such as back office, purchasing, financial accounting and marketing. The variety of these fields of activity guarantees you an exciting and challenging teaching, during which motivated and professional practice and vocational trainees accompany you. Together with your team, you take care of the concerns of our employees, applicants, customers and suppliers. So no day is like the other and you are always challenged.
