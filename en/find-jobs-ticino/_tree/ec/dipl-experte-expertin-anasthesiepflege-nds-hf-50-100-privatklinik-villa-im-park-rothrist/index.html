Business description

In the private clinic Villa im Park, whose character is characterized by the stately, historic property, around 40 observing physicians perform more than 1,800 procedures per year. The three integrative, fully digitalized operating rooms of the clinic are among the best equipped of their kind. Major medical specialties include general surgery, anesthesiology, orthopaedic surgery and traumatology of the musculoskeletal system and gynecology. As a Listespital, the private clinic Villa im Park in the canton of Aargau has a public service mandate and treats patients from all insurance classes.

Job description

  • Care of patients during regional or general anesthesia under medical supervision
  • Coordination of interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • Handling and provision of all respirators, monitoring equipment and materials
  • Support for organisational and administrative activities
  • Participation in the Picett Service System (Monday-Friday), no services Saturday/Sunday

Qualifications

  • Dipl. Expert/Expert Anesthesia Care NDS HF
  • High level of technical, action and social competence
  • Flexible, resilient and solution-oriented personality with a high sense of responsibility and great willingness to provide services

Additional information

  • A varied area of responsibility in a lively but manageable environment
