Full description

Post "Nurse FNPG Marsens and Villars-sur-Glâne - for spontaneous applications" at RFSM - Réseau fribourgeois santé mentale, Marsens (FR). The RFSM is the mental health center of the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions.

Appointing authority / department / institution

The Freiburg Mental Health Network (FNPG) is administratively assigned to the Directorate for Health and Social Affairs. The FNPG is a medical, nursing and psychosocial competence center that specializes in psychiatry and psychotherapy and, more broadly, in mental health. The bilingual FNPG is continually developing and staying up to date in the therapeutic area.
