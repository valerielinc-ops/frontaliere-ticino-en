Naturex AG, a company of the Givaudan Group, is a leading supplier to the food and, among others, the baby food industry. At the Bischofszell and Burgdorf sites, we produce high-quality fruit and vegetable powder, which is sold worldwide. For our production site in Burgdorf we are looking for a plant operator (m/f/d) immediately or by agreement. You are mainly responsible for the support of sub-plants for raw material processing and spray drying in shift operation.

The function shall include the following main tasks

  • Monitoring, operation and cleaning of raw material processing, spray drying and ancillary installations in compliance with quality, time and hygiene requirements
  • Continuous monitoring of critical control points (CCPs) and corresponding action initiation according to a defined scheme in case of deviations
  • Carrying out IPC control measurements for process control
  • Logging of production processes based on Q specifications
  • Recording and reporting of plant defects, defects or faults according to Q specifications for process control
  • Provision of raw materials and spray mixtures
  • Active cooperation of all work during production (including plant and building cleaning)
  • Performing periodic cleaning work

Your professional profile

  • Completed vocational training with technical understanding
  • Experience in the food sector desirable
  • Independent, precise work according to specifications
  • Driving knowledge in forklifts
