Profile

The Krauchthal schools, with school locations in Krauchthal and Hettiswil, are situated between Burgdorf and Bern at the northern gateway to the Emmental. Around 180 pupils from kindergarten through 6th grade are taught in multi-age classes by 24 dedicated teachers.
