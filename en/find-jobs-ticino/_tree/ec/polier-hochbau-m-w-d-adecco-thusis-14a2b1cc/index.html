Full description

Introduction

For our customer, an established company in building construction, we are looking for a practical and reliable personality as a polisher: in building construction. In this function, you take responsibility for your construction sites and keep daily processes on track efficiently.

Tasks

  • Operational overall responsibility on the construction site - from the facility to the handover
  • Ensuring quality, deadlines and costs
  • personnel and equipment and material management
  • Clearly structure AVOR and daily routines
  • coordination of subcontractors and suppliers
  • Ensuring smooth construction site logistics
  • Design according to plan according to SIA standards and consistent compliance with SUVA/HSE requirements
  • Quality assurance on the construction site: self- / external checks, defect detection and follow-up of measures
  • Rapporte & documentation: director and performance reports, building diary, protocols
  • Cooperation in cost and scheduling controlling
  • contact person to construction management, building manager and authorities
  • Active participation in construction meetings
  • You lead, instruct and promote your team. Learners and live occupational safety

Profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship in the building industry (e.g. mason: in EFZ or equivalent)
  • Further training as a building polisher: in with Federal FA or dipl. Polisher:in; advantageous technician:in HF construction management
  • Several years of experience in building construction
  • Strong enforcement, structured and solution-oriented
  • High level of personal responsibility and teamwork
  • Very good German in word and writing
  • Cat-B driving licence

Advantages

  • Collegial working environment with an open corporate culture and short decision-making paths
  • Modern and dynamic working environment
  • Flexible working hours for a good work-life balance
