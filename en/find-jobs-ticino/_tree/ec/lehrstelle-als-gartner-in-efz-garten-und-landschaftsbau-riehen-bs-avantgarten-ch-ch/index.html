Full description

AvantGarten AG is a horticultural company operating in the Basel area. We design and maintain gardens in both private and urban contexts in harmony with nature. Our team is characterised by creativity, commitment and diversity.Every year we offer an apprenticeship as a gardener in our company in Riehen and Binningen. Gardeners in horticulture and landscaping ... design, create and maintain private, as well as public green area plants roof terraces, place spaces and water plants grow wooden decks and privacy wall cutting hedges and woodland advice the clientele in competent garden questions and have knowledge of biological connections and processes, as well as plant science and ecologyWe expect from you:You are enthusiastic about nature and plant worldYou are physically resilient and weatherproofYou have craftsmanship and technical skillsYou like to work in the teamYou have fun designing and observingAs a gardener with an EFZ degree the following wage awaits you: - 4550 CHF entry wage- After three years of professional experience 4'800 CHF From the age of 20 you will receive a higher apprenticeship wage than usual. In the first year of apprenticeship, 1500 CHF, in the second 1600 CHF and in the third 1700 CHF.In our company, you will be optimally prepared for the final exam. Everything you need to know, you learn in the practical. Together with other apprentices, you take part in extracurricular plants and horticulture training courses and get insights into other horticulture companies. All costs for school documents and study trips are covered by us.We also pay you an external examination simulation before your final exam so that you know what you have to pay attention to during the final exam.Send us by e-mail or by post, together with the information about your person (curriculum vitae) and your school diploma (certificates), a short letter of motivation, why you are interested in an apprenticeship as a gardener and landscaping. Are you interested in an apprenticeship at AvantGarten?Then we look forward to receiving your application documents (application letter, CV, school certificates, references) by e-mail to bewerbung@avantgarten.ch or simply apply here via the application form.
