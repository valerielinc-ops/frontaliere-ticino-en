Full description

As part of our Quality Department, your main mission will be to address non-compliance in collaboration with the various departments.

In this context, you will be in charge of

  • Analyze non-conformities, identify root causes and define problem-solving action plans with all stakeholders
  • Propose, deploy and monitor actions through audits
  • Coordinate all corrective and preventive actions
  • Participate in and monitor the implementation of indicators
  • Categorize problems, define actions to be carried out and coordinate projects for continuous improvement produced
  • You have a Master's or Master's degree in Microtechnics and have specialised quality training
  • You benefit from 3 to 5 years of experience in a similar position, in the field of watchmaking
  • You master the quality tools (8D, Ishikawa, Pareto, SPC, etc.) and are able to perform quality audits
  • With a good analytical capacity, you are rigorous and independent.
  • You enjoy working as a team and cross-cutting

What we offer

  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual weeks of leave with 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • The ability to work remotely depending on function
  • Contributions to your sports or cultural activities
  • An LPP with favourable rates
