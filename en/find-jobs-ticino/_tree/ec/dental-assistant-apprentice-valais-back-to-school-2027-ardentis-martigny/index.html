Full description

What if you become our next apprentice dental assistant? Are you looking for an apprenticeship where you can really develop and learn with the best? The Ardentis Group, leader in the Swiss Romande with its 20 dental clinics, invites you to join its teams for the re-entry 2027. What you will learn with us: During the 3 years of your training, you will follow a complete course that combines theory and practice.

Under the direct supervision of your learning manager and the clinic team, you will be trained in the various facets of the dental assistant trade, with personalized follow-up throughout your career. You will specialize in:

  • Kind patient care
  • Administrative records management and firm organization
  • Assisting dentists in the wheelchair for care
  • Sterilization of equipment and radiology Thanks to rigorous supervision by our team of specialists, you can develop solid skills in a modern and stimulating environment. Ardentis is committed to providing you with quality training and we are proud to announce a success rate of 89% at the CFC! And after your apprenticeship? With your CFC in pocket, the opportunities open to you! As a qualified dental assistant, you will have prospects for hiring not only at home, but also in the entire dental sector.

Ardentis, in particular, offers opportunities for evolution within the group, and we value our young graduates. How to apply? It's simple: send your CV, a cover letter, your school bulletins for the last 3 years, your internship reports and, if applicable, a copy of your residence permit. Why choose Ardentis?

  • A varied and stimulating learning, with concrete missions from the first day
  • A friendly and dynamic team that will support you throughout your training
  • Modern clinics equipped with the latest technologies
  • Personalized coaching with a dedicated learning manager who will closely follow your progress Join our clinic and start your career with us! You may be our future collaborator!

If you are motivated in nature, have a focus on detail, well-made work and the demands of the trade do not scare you, we offer you the opportunity to train with the best specialists and, ultra-modern technology.

We offer

  • The strength of a group of 20 dental clinics in Suisse Romande
