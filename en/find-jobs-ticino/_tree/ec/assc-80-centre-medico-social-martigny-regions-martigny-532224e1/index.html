You want to give meaning to your professional practice by accompanying people in their daily lives, with respect and benevolence. By listening carefully to their needs, you offer appropriate care, both preventive and supportive, and help maintain their autonomy every day.

About us

By joining our team, you will not simply be a care professional: you will play a key role in improving the quality of life of our clients. Your mission will be to provide quality care and assistance directly at home.

In addition to the care, you will provide practical daily help, by accompanying our clients in household tasks and everyday activities.

You will work closely with a multidisciplinary team and will actively contribute to the well-being of our clients.

  • Knowledge of the canton's health, social and voluntary network;
  • Reflexivity and adaptability;
  • The mastery of common computer tools;
