Full description

The estate department is the centre of excellence for the various tax issues related to deaths. For this newly created position, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality who wants to contribute her previous professional experience and expand her tax law expertise. You can expect a versatile and varied job in an interesting tax law environment. Tasks

  • You check tax returns and make assessments for state and municipal taxes as well as direct federal tax in simpler cases.
  • You are dealing with supplementary property tax cases.
  • You support the department in the area of inheritance and gift taxes and assess cash gifts.
  • You process file access requests and provide telephone and written information.
  • You process requests from municipal tax offices. Profile
  • You have a completed commercial basic education (EFZ) and a federally recognized professional certificate or an equivalent qualification.
  • You will bring two to four years of professional experience, including at least two years in a relevant field, such as public administration, finance or taxation.
  • You have basic knowledge of tax law and are interested in the in-depth examination of tax law issues.
  • You are motivated to acquire further expertise and develop professionally.
  • You work independently, structured, carefully.
  • You have very good oral and written knowledge of German as well as good MS-Office user skills.
  • You are a reliable, team-ready and service-oriented personality. An exciting task with many design possibilities and a well-coordinated team in which appreciation and mutual support are lived.
