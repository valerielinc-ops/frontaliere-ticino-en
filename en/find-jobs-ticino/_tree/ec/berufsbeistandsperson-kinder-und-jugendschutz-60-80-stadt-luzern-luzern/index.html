Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Are you looking for a meaningful activity? With your open and positive nature, you accompany children and young people in special life situations.

Your tasks

  • You carry out child protection measures on behalf of the child and adult protection authority.
  • You advise, support and empower affected persons and initiate change processes.
  • You produce reports, statements and motions.
  • You work with internal and external interfaces.

Your profile

  • You have a university degree in social work
  • You have attended further training in child protection or in systemic/solution-oriented counselling.
  • You have professional experience in legal social work
  • You are an empathetic and well-organized personality with pleasure in advising and accompanying children and young adults.
  • You are confident in solution-oriented discussion and have a high degree of self-management and reflective ability
