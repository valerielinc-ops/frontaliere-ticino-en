University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

We are looking for you! Together with a dynamic team, do you want to set a significant milestone in the University Clinic for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy? Then apply to us. We, the Station Marbach, distinguish ourselves in the care of people in crisis situations. Our core business is the support of 20 patients in the psychotic experience as well as with general psychiatric diseases. In addition, we offer an individualized psychotherapy program for people from the schizophrenic form circle. The focus of our work is active relationship work and the design of the milieu in everyday life. Are you looking for a professional interdisciplinary team at a high level, where close cooperation and community are central? We look forward to your application.

Your tasks

  • You are the trusted contact person for people in crisis situations and create a space in which patients feel safe and respected
  • With your expertise, you take on central tasks in nursing-therapeutic treatment and contribute significantly to recovery.
  • Together in the interdisciplinary team, you implement the latest, evidence-based care standards and provide impulses for innovative care concepts
  • You play an active role in the care development of the UPZ and bring fresh ideas to further develop our standards
  • Carefully create individual care plans and document the progress of our patients to make their development transparent

Your profile

  • You have a diploma as a nurse HF / FH and bring experience in psychiatry or are ready to open up this fascinating world for you and deepen your knowledge
  • You are motivated to actively contribute to improving mental health care and make a positive difference together with us
  • You appreciate the close cooperation with professionals from different professional fields and enjoy the exchange of ideas and experiences
  • In challenging situations, you keep calm and show your balance so that you are a stable anchor for our patients.
  • Your interest and ambition drive you to constantly develop personally and professionally and to discover new perspectives
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
