Full description

Reference code: JR135909

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

Join our Research and Innovation team to shape the future of luxury materials. This unique position offers the opportunity to work on the development and implementation processes of new alloys for the Group Houses.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

You will join the Research and Innovation Team Materials Chemistry and Surfaces in which you will contribute to improving the mastery of the processes of making alloys dedicated to jewellery and watchmaking products.

You will be the operational and technical reference for the transformation of our metals: from fusion alloying and thermomechanical transformations to powder metallurgy techniques. You will also ensure the operational efficiency and continuous improvement of our R&I workshops.

Your main responsibilities will include

  • Metallurgical development and implementation: Conduct processes of casting, thermomechanical transformation and metallurgy of powders (casting, deformation by rolling and stretching, thermal treatment furnace, ...).
  • Operational management of the workshops: Ensure the proper functioning of the equipment, the first level maintenance, the management of consumables and play the role of technical reference.
  • Support for innovation projects: Conduct testing campaigns for R&I project managers and Houses, capitalize data and coordinate analyses with the laboratory.
  • Continuous improvement, material flow & safety: Optimize processes and yields, ensure strict traceability of precious metals and ensure strict compliance with safety and security rules.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE USED WITH US?

To succeed in this role, you will need to demonstrate the following skills and experience:

  • Diploma of Technicien.ne ES in Microtechnique or Mechanical, CFC of Polymechanic or equivalent technical training
  • First successful industrial or academic experience on metallurgy equipment.
  • Technical expertise:
  • Solid practical knowledge in general metallurgy, thermomechanical processing and powder metallurgy.
  • Control of equipment and process instrumentation.
  • Aisance with common computer tools (MS Office suite: Excel, Word, PowerPoint).
  • Autonomy, curiosity and excellent ability to solve technical problems on the ground.
  • Rigor, high accuracy and strong sense of safety.
  • Team spirit, listening and service orientation towards project partners
  • Rigor, high accuracy and strong sense of safety.
  • Very good command of French (oral and written); good level of understanding of technical English.

WHAT DISTINGUES OUR GROUP?

Our true strength lies not in our similarities, but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures and human skills, as well as our specific ability to encourage untapped potentials.

  • We value empathy, humility and integrity.
  • We encourage collegiality, curiosity and courage.
  • We take care of the world we live in.

Based mainly in Glovelier (JU), you will also have the opportunity to meet colleagues from other research sectors at our hub in Microcity, Neuchâtel. We offer a 12-month term, renewable position.
