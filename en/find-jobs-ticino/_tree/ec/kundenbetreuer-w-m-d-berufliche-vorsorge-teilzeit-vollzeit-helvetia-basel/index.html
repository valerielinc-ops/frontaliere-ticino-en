Full description

Customer Service / Service Center Busy: 50-100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Immerse yourself in the world of Servisa, where world-class service is at the forefront. We are looking for team players with hands-on mentality who understand prevention as a promise and are passionate about our customers. The proximity to the insured and the person himself is consistently the focus of our work. At Servisa you enjoy freedom to contribute your own ideas. We offer you real creative leeway so that you can actively shape the future of Servisa.

What you do

  • You independently manage a defined contract portfolio and are an important contact person for our customers.
  • You will process various requests from companies, insured persons, their representatives and brokers and find suitable solutions.
  • You perform mutations and handle challenging business cases such as payouts, distribution of free funds, purchase calculations or homeownership promotion (WEF).
  • You ensure a correct and timely handling of the debt collection, dunning and operation system.
  • You work closely with internal specialist positions and contribute to a high quality of service with your expertise.
  • You are actively involved in the further development of our processes and our customer support.

What you bring

  • You have a basic commercial education.
  • You have experience in social security, ideally in occupational pensions.
  • You work independently, reliably and solution-oriented and enjoy direct customer contact.
  • You communicate stylishly in German; good knowledge of French and / or Italian is an advantage.
  • You are well-versed in dealing with IT applications and enjoy working with new systems. Experience with MS Pension is a plus.
  • You are a committed, team-oriented personality who keeps track of more complex issues.
