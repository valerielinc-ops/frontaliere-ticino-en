The profession As an ICT specialist at Roche, you are responsible for the installation and configuration of PCs, tablets and mobile phones and ensure that they work properly and safely. You are also responsible for connecting peripherals such as notebooks and printers to the network infrastructure and solve any problems that occur.

In IT support, you work closely with end users and advise them on the optimal use of ICT systems and tools. If problems arise, you analyze them thoroughly and work closely with other specialists to find suitable solutions. In addition, you take on administrative tasks such as ICT orders and support customers in the procurement process of ICT devices.

Training At the beginning of your three-year apprenticeship as an ICT specialist / ICT specialist EFZ learns You first know the computer and network basics at the workshop school. After an introduction to the Roche IT environment, you can already take on initial tasks in IT support. You will learn how to work in service teams and will gain practical experience in at least two of the following areas over the course of your apprenticeship:

  • IT Experience Center Basel/Kaiseraugst: Advising end users on handling Roche IT systems
  • Laboratory Support in Research – Use in the pRED Center in Basel: Integration of laboratory equipment into the IT infrastructure
  • Labor Support in Production: Integration of end-user devices such as PCs and printers into the production plant.

Depending on personal interest, further topics can be developed and deepened independently during the entire apprenticeship period.
