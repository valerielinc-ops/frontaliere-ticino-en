Full description

As an environmental safety specialist in the field of Safety, Health & Environment (SHE), you assume the technical responsibility for environmental safety in Migros Industry and anchor environmental protection and environmental law with passion and pioneering spirit!

Mansioni

  • You are functionally leading environmental safety and the Migros Industry Environmental Management Team, which acts as the decision-making body for environmental safety issues.
  • You develop cross-cutting guidelines and concepts in accordance with legal requirements (OCS,OMoD, OPAM, Waters, OChim, OPair, emissions, environmental risks, chemical safety, special waste).
  • You work closely with specialists in ST-PS, environmental safety and operational sustainability of Migros Industry and Migros Group.
  • You are the contact point, you advise and accompany Migros Industry companies on all environmental safety issues.
  • You represent environmental safety in accordance with the legal requirements of external bodies and within the framework of the ISO 14001 certification of the Migros Industry, and you coordinate the ISO14001 activities of the Migros Industry.
  • You bring your expertise to SAP systems.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience in the SHE field in an industrial enterprise: An experience within a group and an experience in the direction of cross-sectional teams is an asset.
  • Completed vocational learning (CFC): or completed engineering, environmental sciences or comparable curriculum.
  • You have very good control over legal frameworks (CH/EU chemicals law, dangerous goods, special waste, LPE, LPair, LEaux, environmental risks, OPAM, ISO 14001).
  • You have an assertive personality and you are confident in your behavior.
  • You are very resistant to stress, demonstrate a strong capacity for integration and convince by your persuasion strength.
  • You communicate clearly and convincingly, you know how to argue well and you have experience in stakeholder management (stakeholder management).
  • You're ready to travel to Switzerland.
  • You have the qualification of safety adviser for the transport of dangerous goods (in accordance with the SCO) or environmental manager, or a comparable qualification.
  • French (Very good knowledge)
  • German (Very good knowledge)

English (preferably)
