Your heart is beating for the elderly? Is the profession of caregiver a real vocation? Do you want to put your skills and experience at the service of a meaningful mission, and make a concrete contribution to the well-being of our clients? Then you're the person we're looking for!

For Tertianum La Jardinerie in the Jura region, we are looking for a suitable one/one

Community Health Assistant ASSC 80 to 100%

Your tasks

  • Guidance and support of assigned hosts
  • Support for body care, movement and other daily activities. Organization and proposal of daily activities for our hosts.

Your profile

  • Professional experience in the care of several years is an asset
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Pleasure meeting various challenges
  • Good knowledge of French spoken and written
