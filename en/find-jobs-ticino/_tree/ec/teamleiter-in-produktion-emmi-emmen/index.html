Full description

At the Emmen site, the heart of our milk processing beats. Here we combine many years of experience with modern production and a lot of passion for our products. As a team leader: in production, you become part of Emmi and, together with your team, create an environment in which quality, cooperation and responsibility count. Want to make a difference with us?

You can do that.

  • As a team leader: in production, you play a central role in our company. You contribute your expertise, leadership skills and commitment to ensuring efficient, high-quality and safe production.
  • You are the central point of contact for your team and promote the optimal cooperation and performance of all team members through your motivating and appreciative leadership.
  • You ensure the smooth running of production processes by monitoring compliance with standards and workflows and proactively identifying and implementing optimization potential.
  • With your in-depth knowledge of food technology and production experience, you solve problems efficiently and implement innovative solutions to improve processes and plants.
  • You are responsible for compliance with all quality standards and requirements in production and carry out regular inspections and audits.

Why do you succeed?

  • Your completed vocational training EFZ as a dairy or food technologist gives you solid expertise that you have continuously expanded through relevant further training (HF/FS1/2).
  • Leadership skills are in your blood and you already have experience in team management. With your appreciative and inspiring nature, you motivate your employees and create a positive working environment in which the potential of each individual can be fully realized.
  • Communication is one of your outstanding strengths. You always keep your team informed, present results clearly and understandably and promote cooperation and team spirit. Through these skills and your enjoyment of interpersonal exchange, you are an important link between everyone involved.
  • Reliability and commitment set you apart. Structured and proactive work as well as the willingness to take responsibility are a matter of course for you.

This is what we offer you

  • 6 weeks vacation: Enjoy six weeks vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to seven weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You will receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Further education and development opportunities: Emmi focuses on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
