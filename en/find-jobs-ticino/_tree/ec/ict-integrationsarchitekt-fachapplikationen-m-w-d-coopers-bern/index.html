Full description

For a long-term and technologically demanding transformation program, we are looking for two experienced ICT integration architects (specialist applications) for our customer from Bern.

In this role, you will be responsible for the architecture and integration of specialist applications within a modern platform landscape and contribute significantly to the successful implementation of complex digitalization projects.

Your tasks

  • Development of integration architectures for specialist applications and digital services
  • Analyze and evaluate integration requirements as well as technical solution variants
  • Creation of integration concepts, architectural specifications and technical target images
  • Define interfaces, integration standards and architecture principles
  • Accompanying projects and specialist areas in architectural and integration issues
  • Conducting architecture reviews and ensuring compliance with defined standards
  • Coordinating solutions with platform, infrastructure, security and IAM managers
  • Support in technological evaluations, feasibility analyses and architecture decisions

Your profile

  • Higher education in computer science, business informatics or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of experience as an ICT or integration architect in complex system landscapes
  • In-depth knowledge of system integration, interfaces and enterprise architectures
  • Experience with modern integration platforms, APIs and service-oriented architectures
  • Familiar with cloud, security and identity management topics
  • Experience in creating architectural concepts and technical documentation
  • Analytical and structured working method with high consulting competence
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Proper reputation is mandatory
