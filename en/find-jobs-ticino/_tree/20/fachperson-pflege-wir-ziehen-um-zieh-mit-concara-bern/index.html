Domicil offers senior citizens stationary services in care and care. With more than 20 locations and around 1,600 employees, Domicil is the number 1 in long-term care in the Canton of Bern. Under the umbrella of Concara, we are working together with Spitex Bern for integrated all-round care.

In May 2027 we pack our suitcases: The Domicil Competence Center Bethlehemacker moves for almost three years to the former retirement home Steingrüebli in Ostermundigen. The reason? Our home is being rebuilt – to a state-of-the-art dementia center.

For this, we are looking for nurses and employees of all functional levels who do not just want to run along, but to make a difference. People with heart, attitude, humor and an affinity for dementia. Because this project is more than a move. It is a journey with many exciting stages: from setting up and commissioning our transitional location, through the further development of our care, to helping to shape a new dementia center.

HF, FaGe, SRK or nursing assistance? No matter where you are professionally and how much you want to work: We want you!
