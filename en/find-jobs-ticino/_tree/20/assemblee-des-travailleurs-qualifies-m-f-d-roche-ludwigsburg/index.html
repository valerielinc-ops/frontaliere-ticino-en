The Position Assembly of skilled workers (m/f/d)

Work You like to be precise, technically interested and a real team player? Then you are exactly right with us! Apply and do your part for success as an assembly skilled worker (m/f/d)!

Responsibilities and What to Expect

  • Assembly: You assemble assemblies, modules and systems based on clear specifications and approved documentation (assembly instructions).
  • Testing: You test the assemblies and systems in the four-eyes principle with test equipment or on the PC – of course according to the corresponding specifications.
  • Documentation: You carry out all work steps cleanly and GxP-compliant using our checklists.
  • Management of deviations and disturbances:
  • Recognize and document: You recognize deviations, fill out blocking sheets and document everything completely.
  • Fix: You solve simple problems independently, while you pass on more complex topics to the assembly specialist.
  • Working environment: You keep your workplace clean and safe – because that goes without saying for you.
  • Optionally, You take on more:
  • Assumption of roles: In agreement with your superior can You take on additional roles and expand your responsibility – according to our “Roll Catalogue for Assembly Specialist”.

Who are you and what qualifications do you bring with you

  • Graduation: You have a qualified primary school degree or a real school degree in your pocket.
  • Training: You have successfully completed a 3-year vocational training as an industrial mechanic, mechatronics engineer, (fine) mechanic, electronics engineer or in a similar field.
  • Experience: You know You are ideally equipped with the assembly of assemblies, modules or systems – ideally in medical technology.
  • Language skills: Your German skills in words and writing are great – so that you can communicate safely and clearly in everyday work.
  • IT knowledge: You master simple IT applications and have already gained experience with SAP.
  • Soft Skills: You are tech-savvy, work structured and precise? Then we look forward to welcoming you to our team!
