Full description

Become part of our team As a global company that pursues very clear growth goals, we need employees who want to advance themselves and the company. For our St. Gallen location, we are looking for you as a technical modernization officer (m/f/d) 100% immediately or by appointment. In this role, you are responsible for the commercial and technical processing of modernization orders in the area of ​​automatic door systems and ensure timely and economically successful order processing. Your tasks

  • You are responsible for the commercial and technical processing of modernization orders in the area of ​​automatic door systems.
  • Preparation of offers and orders for the modernization of automatic door systems.
  • You ensure that the orders are completed in an economically successful manner for the company.
  • Commercial and technical clarification of any deviations from the offer in collaboration with the service account manager.
  • Appointment responsibility.
  • Billing of orders and additional orders.
  • Substitute for processing maintenance contracts. ,
  • Complaint management.
  • Master data maintenance.

Your profile

  • Technical vocational training with further training in the commercial area.
  • 3-5 years of experience in a similar position.
  • Flair for technology.
  • Organizational skills and good communication skills.
  • Good computer skills (MS Office & SAP knowledge).
  • Customer-oriented, independent and motivated personality.
  • Excellent knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage.
  • Friendly demeanor and solution-oriented way of working.

What we offer

  • Best opportunities in a global corporation with the culture of a family business.
  • Work locations well served by public transport and home office options.
  • Attractive salary package.
  • Transparent and clearly defined development opportunities.
  • Support for militia work and charitable projects, even during working hours.

Who we are For every place that matters. From automatic doors to cloud-based access management, as a global leader in access solutions for schools, banks, airports, hospitals, hotels and more, we enable safe and sustainable places where people can move seamlessly.
