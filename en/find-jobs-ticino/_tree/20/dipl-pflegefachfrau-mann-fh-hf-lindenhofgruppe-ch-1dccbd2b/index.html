Full description

What you achieve

  • You will care for and look after our patients in shifts, with a high degree of personal responsibility.
  • In doing so, you will carry out the nursing process in line with the latest findings, supported by the KISIM hospital information system.
  • You will share responsibility for achieving the goals of the division and department and will be committed to the ongoing optimisation of care processes.
  • You will also support trainees and students in their education.
  • You will work collaboratively in a large interprofessional team and attend further training courses, case discussions and supervision sessions with us.

What sets you apart

  • You are a qualified nurse (AKP/DN II/HF/FH) and ideally have experience in acute care.
  • Experience in the field of palliative care and further training in PC B 1 or CAS Palliative Care are advantageous.
  • The well-being of patients is at the heart of your work, and you treat people with respect and appreciation, and are empathetic and resilient.
  • You stand out for your ability to work in a team, as well as your independent and quality-conscious way of working.
  • Good knowledge of German rounds off your profile.
  • Permanent night shift possible after induction.
