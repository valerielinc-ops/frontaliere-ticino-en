Full description

You lead projects in the field of ventilation and air conditioning technology technically and commercially, from the offer phase to the handover to the client.

Tasks

  • Professional and commercial management of projects from the offer phase to the handover to the client
  • Construction site management with material procurement and appointment and quality monitoring
  • Management of employees incl. Employee interviews and recruitment assistance
  • Disposition of the assembly groups
  • Customer support and negotiations with suppliers, engineers, architects and construction professionals
  • Controlling your own objects
  • Implementation and monitoring of health and safety objectives

Profile

  • Completed training as a ventilation system engineer
  • Several years of professional experience in the industry
  • Sound industry knowledge in ventilation and air conditioning technology
  • Further training for project manager building technology or building technology planner is advantageous
  • Confident knowledge of German
  • Good MS-Office knowledge
