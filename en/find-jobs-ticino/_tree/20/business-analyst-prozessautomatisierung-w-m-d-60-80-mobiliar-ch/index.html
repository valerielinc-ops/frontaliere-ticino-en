Full description

This is what awaits you

You like to analyze business processes and want to implement improvements yourself? As a business analyst with a focus on process automation, you will develop suitable solutions together with the specialist areas and implement them on our robotics platform.

In our team, you accompany automation from the first idea to reliable operation.

This is what you do with us

For more than seven years we have been automating business processes with Robotics. You accompany projects from start to finish and work closely with specialist areas throughout the company.

You identify automation potential, analyze processes and develop business cases. Together with the departments, you raise requirements and develop target processes, solution concepts and specialist specifications. You implement the solutions yourself on our robotics platform, test them and accompany the productive introduction. You will also support specialist departments and general agencies in the associated changes. In addition, you monitor existing automations and ensure their reliable operation with the team.

This is what you bring with you

In addition to your training path, what counts for us is your curiosity and your drive to improve processes together with others.

  • Higher education or further education, such as in business administration, business informatics, process management or a comparable discipline
  • Analytical thinking, rapid comprehension and pleasure in questioning and optimizing business processes
  • Strength of communication and pleasure in working with different specialist areas
  • Interest in technology and digitalization, high willingness to learn and motivation to implement and further develop solutions themselves
  • Independent, solution-oriented working method, initiative and entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

A classic computer science education or experience in the field of robotics are advantageous, but not a requirement. If necessary, we support you with targeted training and further education as well as sufficient time for a careful familiarization.

Working with us

You can expect an experienced, motivated team that works together easily and supports each other. At the interface of business, processes and technology, you will independently develop solutions whose added value you see directly in everyday work.

Your workload is between 60% and 80% selectable, your place of work is Bern. For suitable activities, you can work flexibly in the home office.

We fill the position immediately or by agreement.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
