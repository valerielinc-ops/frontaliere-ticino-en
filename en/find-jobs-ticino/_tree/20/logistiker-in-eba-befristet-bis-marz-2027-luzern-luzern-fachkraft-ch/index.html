Full description

In this role, you operate the picking, testing and packaging of materials. The company attaches importance to reliable storage organisation and precise support for stock censuses. We are looking for an activity for our client

  • picking, testing and packaging materials and spare parts
  • storing and retrieving goods
  • providing material internally
  • participating in inventory censuses
  • maintaining order in the storage area
  • Requirements
  • Practice in logistics is required
  • Apprenticeship as a logistician or logistician EFZ would be desirable
  • Valid truck pass is required
  • Driving practice with the truck rounds off the profile
