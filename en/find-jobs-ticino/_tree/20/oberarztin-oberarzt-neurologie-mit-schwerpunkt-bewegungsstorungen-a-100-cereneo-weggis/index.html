Full description

As one of the world’s leading clinics for neurorehabilitation, we develop an individualized treatment program with the aim of providing our patients with the highest possible degree of independence and freedom of life, thereby optimizing their quality of life. We are aimed at both patients from the Swiss cash system and international patients. cereneo offers highly specialized rehabilitation programs for people with neurological diseases and injuries, including stroke, brain injury, neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson’s, multiple sclerosis and many other neurological disorders. For our medical team, we are looking for a dedicated senior physician who works independently, actively shapes the area and wants to develop professionally. A special focus is on expanding our range of services for patients with movement disorders in close cooperation with neurosurgery, especially in the field of modern interventional therapy procedures. Here we offer the opportunity to contribute our own specialist focuses and to help develop new clinical concepts.

Tasks

  • Responsible medical care for inpatients and outpatients: taking into account all medical-rehabilitative aspects
  • Care of patients: insiders with movement disorders
  • Preparation, implementation and evaluation of appropriate examinations and tests before and after therapeutic interventions, for example focused ultrasound (FUS) or deep brain stimulation (THS/DBS)
  • Proactive participation and further development of your own area of responsibility
  • Assumption of personnel and technical responsibility in one area
  • Ensuring quality, patient-centred care
  • Active interdisciplinary cooperation with medical and non-medical professional groups
  • Further education, guidance and supervision of assistant doctors: inside

What should you bring?

  • Specialist title Neurology with MEBEKO recognition
  • Certificate of the German Society of Neurology (DGN) in the field of movement disorders or corresponding experience of advantage
  • Pronounced interest in movement disorders and willingness to promote participation in relevant courses and training courses
  • Experience in neurorehabilitation, beneficial but not a requirement
  • Enjoy working in an interdisciplinary environment
  • High communication skills, sense of responsibility and organisational talent
  • Process-oriented, reliable and team-oriented way of working
  • Open, committed and resilient personality with own initiative
  • Readiness for continuous professional training
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, other languages are an advantage

We offer

  • Design leeway: possibility to actively build and further develop a specialized clinical focus together with experienced colleagues and interdisciplinary partners
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Close cooperation with neurosurgery and other disciplines in the development and implementation of modern treatment concepts
  • Research and innovation: a closely networked, scientifically active environment with an affiliated research institute as well as numerous national and international research cooperations. Opportunity to participate in research projects, in particular in the fields of technology, therapeutic research and nutrition
  • Modern infrastructure: Perfectly located working environment with a view of Lake Vierwaldstättersee, Pilatus and Rigi
  • Development: Supported individual training and development opportunities
  • Social benefits: Good social benefits (NBU, employer share pension fund 60%)
  • Parking & public transport: Free parking and access to e-charging stations, subsidy for public transport
  • Food: Lunch-Check with 50% employer participation, free coffee, tea, water & fruit
  • Kita: discount for our connected Kita
  • Discounts: Various discounts for recreational activities
  • Fitness: sharing fitness equipment

Our corporate culture

  • EXCELLENCE: Using the latest research findings and first-class service
  • HEARTH: We are always there with all our heart. Of course, this also includes the sincere treatment of our patients: inside, but also between our staff
  • MOTIVATION: Joint goal achievement and support of patients: women to improve their quality of life
  • TEAM WORK: Reliability and mutual support in the team
  • INNOVATION: Research and testing of new therapeutic approaches and devices
