Full description

Deputy workshop manager (a) Galliker Service AG prepares the new cars from various car manufacturers for the Swiss market. In addition, it performs order-specific series, conversion and various workshop work on behalf of importers, corporate customers, dealers as well as rental car and leasing companies. For the car brands in question, Galliker Service AG is also responsible for delivering and taking back the rental cars and leasing vehicles and carries out all kinds of workshop, car body and paint work in the service of the customers. As a small, experienced team, we attach great importance to short decision-making processes, uncomplicated cooperation and mutual support. In order to strengthen our branch office in Schötz, we are looking for a committed personality who supports our workshop manager and takes over the deputy in his absence. Task area

  • Supporting the workshop manager and taking over the deputy in his absence
  • Coordination and planning of workshop orders and workflows
  • Repair and maintenance work
  • Execution of initial structures in cooperation with technical support of manufacturers
  • Implementation and support of versatile technical projects
  • Performing diagnostic work with brand-specific test equipment
  • Assembly and conversion work for fleet and emergency vehicles
  • Technical guarantee work and general workshop work

Requirement profile

  • Completed training as an automotive mechatronics specialist EFZ or automotive specialist EFZ
  • Enthusiasm and interest for automobiles of various vehicle brands
  • Self-employed, reliable and structured working methods
  • Teamwork, resilience and flexibility
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Driving licence Cat. B

Offer

  • Varied activity outside the classic garage routine
  • Working in a small and experienced team
  • Exciting insights into various exclusive vehicle brands
  • Possibility to attend brand specific training and courses
  • Working on the pulse of the latest technologies and digitalization
  • Generous, modern and bright workshop infrastructure
  • Personal and professional development opportunities in a versatile company
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Possibility to compensate for extra hours worked
  • Free parking for employees
