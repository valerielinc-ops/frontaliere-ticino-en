Full description

More than tools Mikron Tool is an international leader in the development and production of hard metal cutting tools. We are a growing Swiss company, enthusiastic about innovation and technology. We specialize in producing small and medium-sized high-performance tools for the processing of the most difficult materials such as stainless steels, heat-resistant alloys and titanium. In one of the most modern technological centers for microworking, processing processes are also developed in the context of customer projects. For our headquarters in Agno, Ticino, we are looking for a manager of the logistics area with a spirit of leadership and desire to assume a managerial role in the future development of our organization. Main handling

  • Management, coaching, development of employees, driving and growing their group
  • Use of Kardex vertical storage systems and its spatial optimization
  • Using laser marking and robots dedicated to tool processing
  • Use of ERP systems and warehouse management software for handling and tracking materials.
  • Knowledge of the identification, coding and traceability of tools and semi-finished products.
  • Coordinate and develop staff, ensuring compliance with business procedures and standards.
  • Knowledge of safety regulations at work and the correct handling of materials.
  • Accuracy, reliability and strong orientation to order and quality, indispensable for the management of high added value products.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively with production, planning, purchases and shipments to ensure continuity of the logistics flow.

Fundamental requirements

  • Exploited organizational skills and activity planning.
  • High sense of responsibility and reliability.
  • Accuracy and attention to detail in the management of materials and data.
  • Ability to work under pressure while maintaining quality and compliance with deadlines.
  • The resolution of the problems and the effective management of the unforeseen.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration with the internal departments.
  • Guidance to continuous improvement and process optimization.
  • Decision-making capacity and initiative in its own sphere of responsibility.
  • Flexibility and ability to adapt quickly to organizational and productive changes.
  • Knowledge of Italian and good knowledge of compulsory English; knowledge of German is a preferential title.
  • Bookings between Agno and Rottweil (DE) locations.
  • Oral flexibility and availability to adapt its presence according to operational and productive needs.
  • Residence in Canton Ticino

What we offer

  • Part of a strong Swiss group with an international focus
  • Business environment with horizontal hierarchies and a strong team spirit, positive corporate culture
  • Short decision-making and exciting and interesting activities with great possibilities for action and design
  • Continuous training measures such as language and technical training, employee events and excellent social benefits
