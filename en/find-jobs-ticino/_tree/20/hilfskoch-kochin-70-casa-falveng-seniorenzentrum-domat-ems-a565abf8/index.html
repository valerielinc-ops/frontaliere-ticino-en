Assistant cook/chef 70% – Cold kitchen

Cooking with pleasure, creating creatively and giving our inhabitants daily pleasure. In our modern senior center casa falveng in Domat/Ems we look after and accompany 99 residents. Around 170 employees are committed to their well-being every day. A balanced, varied and lovingly prepared meal contributes significantly to their quality of life. To complement our kitchen team, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality as an auxiliary cook with a workload of 70%, specialized in the cold kitchen. Your tasks

  • Preparation and pleasing of cold foods, salads, appetizers and desserts
  • Preparation and provision of cold components for daily meals
  • Composition and preparation of cold plates and other food according to menu plan
  • Support in the preparation and production of daily meals
  • Proper storage and careful handling of food
  • Compliance with hygiene, quality and food regulations (HACCP)
  • Ensuring order, cleanliness and a well-organized work environment
  • Collaboration with the kitchen team for a varied and needs-based meal for our residents

You bring that with you

  • Experience in the kitchen, ideally in the cold kitchen or in community gastronomy
  • Joy in the preparation and creative presentation of food
  • Independent, reliable and careful working methods
  • Strong hygiene awareness and responsible handling of food
  • Flexibility, teamwork and readiness
  • Friendly and respectful treatment of our residents and employees
  • Good to very good knowledge of German

This is what awaits you

  • A motivated, established and collegial kitchen team
  • A varied area of responsibility with a focus on cold kitchen
  • A secure workplace in a modern senior center
  • A modern infrastructure and well-organized workflows
  • Contemporary employment conditions and generous social benefits
  • A meaningful activity in which you contribute daily to the well-being of our residents

Interested? Then we look forward to your application with photo and the usual documents by e-mail or by post. casa falveng – Senior Centre Via Musel 21 7013 Domat/Ems

For further information, our chef or our manager, Mrs. Patricia Brenn, are at your disposal by telephone. We look forward to meeting you!
