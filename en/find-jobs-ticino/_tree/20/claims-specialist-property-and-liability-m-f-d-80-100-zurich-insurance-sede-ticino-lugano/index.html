Are you fascinated by the idea of proactively advising clients in post-claim situations? Do you have experience managing multiple tasks simultaneously in a constantly evolving work environment? The Property Insurance team handles all complex claims within the property and asset branches in a stimulating and dynamic environment. If customer excellence, quality, and timely delivery are as important to you as team spirit, then this could be your next career opportunity. As a Complex Claims Specialist, you have the opportunity to apply and develop your consulting skills. Thanks to your strong communication skills, you are instrumental in negotiations and discussions, always ensuring professional, customer-service-oriented assistance. Find out here why Zurich Switzerland is among the "Top 5" in the "Best Employers" award in Switzerland. Be part of this group too and apply as a Property and Liability Claims Specialist. What you will do:

  • Support and assist individuals in the event of claims and manage claims
  • Fully handle complex claim cases within the property and asset branches
  • Ensure the management of claim notifications, assess insurance coverage, and examine the question of liability
  • Analyze compensation claims and determine the extent of benefits
  • Carry out necessary investigations with involved parties and manage relationships with policyholders, injured parties and their representatives, repairers, and other Companies. What you bring with you:
  • Swiss qualification (Matura, commercial apprenticeship with federal certificate of proficiency or insurance expert with federal professional certificate)
  • Knowledge of insurance products and possibly experience in claims management
  • High level of resilience, initiative, strong decision-making skills, determination, collegiality, and flexibility
  • High aptitude for working with IT systems and using new technologies
  • Excellent command of spoken but especially written Italian. Good knowledge of spoken and written German, with written German being a preference.
