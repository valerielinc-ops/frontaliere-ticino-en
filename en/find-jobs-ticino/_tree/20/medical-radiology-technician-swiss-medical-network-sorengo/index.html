Full description

We are looking for a Medical Radiology Technician to be included in our team at Sorengo, Switzerland. The selected person will be responsible for carrying out diagnostic tests for images, use and management of radiological equipment and the correct handling of patients, ensuring high quality, safety and professionalism. Liability

  • Use and contribute to the proper maintenance of image diagnostic equipment, including conventional radiology, magnetic resonance (RM) and computerized tomography (TAC), in compliance with internal protocols and manufacturer indications.
  • Prepare and place patients accurately and safely in order to obtain high quality diagnostic images and minimize radiation exposure.
  • Strictly apply radioprotection and security protocols, in compliance with current regulations and standards.
  • Check the quality of the images acquired and collaborate with radiologist doctors for any insights, repetitions or further examinations.
  • To welcome and prepare patients for exams, providing clear information on procedures, answering their questions and helping to create a reassuring and professional environment.
  • accurately manage and update clinical documentation and exam information through RIS and PACS systems
  • Collaborate daily with radiologist doctors, hospital doctors, nursing staff and other health professionals, promoting efficient work organization and patient care.
  • Contribute to the maintenance of high quality standards, hygiene and safety within the radiology service.

The profile we are looking for

We look for a responsible person, precise and patient-oriented, with good relational skills and predisposition to work in team. They will be particularly appreciated operational autonomy, attention to detail and ability to work in a dynamic and multidisciplinary health context.

Professional Requirements

  • Ability to use, solve problems and carry out quality controls of diagnostic equipment for images (rays X, TAC, RM, Mammography)
  • Support for IT systems RIS PACS
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work effectively in teams
  • Excited attention to detail, commitment to guarantee high quality diagnostic images and patient safety

Requirements

  • Certification or license valid as a radiology technician, recognized in Switzerland - Master of patient positioning techniques, anatomical knowledge and safety principles
  • Knowledge of Italian language.

The Sant’Anna Clinic is part of the Swiss Medical Network: one of the largest groups of Swiss private clinics founded in 2002.
