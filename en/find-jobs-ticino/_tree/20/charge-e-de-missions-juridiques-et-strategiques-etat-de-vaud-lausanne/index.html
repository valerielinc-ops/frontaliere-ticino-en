Full description

At the heart of the canton's legal, economic and institutional issues, you intervene on a variety of topics that involve both your legal skills, your analytical capacity and your understanding of public issues. In direct contact with the department's management and many partners, you help prepare decisions, change legal bases and coordinate strategic issues. An opportunity to combine legal expertise, strategic thinking and concrete impact on public action.

  • Advise management and managers on legal and policy issues, analyze complex situations and prepare legal opinions, briefs and proposals on issues of high institutional, economic or political importance.
  • To contribute to the elaboration and development of the legal and regulatory bases and to the preparation of the files submitted to the cantonal authorities and to ensure legal and legislative oversight.
  • Process legal requests from the administration or external partners of the service and legal challenge procedures.
  • Ensure the treatment of parliamentary objects and federal and cantonal consultations by coordinating with relevant partners and meeting deadlines.
  • To manage and monitor financial and personal contributions to the service in coordination with the relevant interlocutors.
  • Participate in cross-cutting work and follow up on agreed decisions and actions. For further information please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Ms Alice de Benoit, Chief of Staff, at 021 316 39 57.
  • Master in Swiss law
  • Lawyer's patent, an asset
  • At least 5 years' experience as a lawyer
  • Excellent capacity for legal analysis and synthesis
  • Clear and appropriate communication to various interlocutors
  • Strategic vision and direction
  • Interest in public policy and knowledge of Vaud institutions and the economic fabric is an asset

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
