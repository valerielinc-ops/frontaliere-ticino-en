Full description

Your key responsibilities

As a butler, you embody exceptional guest service and make a significant contribution to our guests’ satisfaction:

  • Personalised care and attention to the wishes of our discerning guests are at the heart of your service.

  • With your thorough knowledge of our guests’ preferences and ability to adapt quickly, you ensure seamless and discreet in-room service.

  • Special requests and individual suite set-ups are handled in close cooperation with the Housekeeping team.

  • Packing and unpacking services, along with other specialised suite services, are also part of your role.

  • The highest standards of discretion and professionalism are always upheld.

• What you bring to the job

Ideally, you bring the following qualifications and qualities:

  • Completed vocational training in hospitality or an equivalent qualification

  • Several years of experience in a similar role in luxury hospitality

  • Excellent guest-relations skills and a strong service orientation

  • Discretion and the ability to respect and protect guests’ privacy

  • A confident manner, impeccable etiquette and a well-groomed appearance

  • A combination of two of these three languages; German, English, Italian

  • Flexibility, resilience, a strong team spirit and a solution-oriented approach

What We Offer

  • Fixed-term seasonal position, from the end of November 2026 until the end of March 2027

  • Five-day working week on average, based on 43.5 hours per week

  • Five weeks of annual leave and six public holidays per year

  • 13th salary from your start date, paid pro rata

  • Accommodation in one of our attractive staff houses, if required

  • Meals available in our new, modern staff restaurant

  • Work in an attractive 50Best environment with opportunities for development

If you are looking for an interesting, challenging and varied role in a dynamic environment and would like to join a well-established team, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
