You can look forward to that.

Discover an exciting challenge in project and plant accounting! You can expect varied tasks, a lot Personal responsibility and the opportunity to actively shape financial processes. Together with committed colleagues, you will receive exciting insights into various areas of SBB. As a clerk: in project and plant accounting you set the correct financial Mapping of projects and plants securely and supports the further development of our processes. You can move that.

  • The project and plant accounting accounts for around 2,500 projects (real estate, IT, construction and vehicle projects) every year. You coordinate administrative activities, work closely with project leaders and create the conditions for activations.
  • You accompany projects from opening to completion, carry out project completions in SAP and ensure the correct and audit-proof mapping of projects and plants in accordance with Swiss GAAP FER, OR and internal guidelines.
  • You check and record asset inflows and outflows, assess activation issues and ensure the correct mapping of business transactions in asset accounting.
  • Project manager supports you expertly with questions about project and plant accounting.
  • You ensure first-level support for SAP project and asset accounting applications.
  • Together with your colleagues, you analyze and optimize existing processes and participate in the further development of instructions, specialist concepts and process specifications.

You bring that with you.

  • You work independently, reliably and in a structured manner and also keep track of a large number of projects.
  • With clear, address-oriented communication, you actively approach project leaders and other contacts and enjoy working in a team.
  • You are interested in business and technical contexts and ideally bring initial experience in the project or asset accounting.
  • SAP knowledge and a high level of IT affinity make it easier for you to get started with the versatile tasks of project and plant accounting.
  • A completed commercial training and a completed further education as a clerk: in accounting (edupool) we presuppose.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing (at least level C1). Ideally, you are bilingual (German/French) or have very good knowledge of French. Further language skills are advantageous.
