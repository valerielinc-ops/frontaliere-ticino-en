Full description

Start of internship: February 1, 20277 Duration: 6 months Within the Research team, your main mission will be to define design guidelines to optimize the resistance of watches to accidental shocks. The FEM watch head model will make it possible to investigate and optimize concepts in order to ensure compliance with shocks. These investigations will be carried out from the exterior (absorbent architecture, shapes, materials, etc.) to the movement (movement/exterior fastenings, robust bridge architecture, etc.)

In this context, you will be in charge of

  • Evaluate parameters influencing acceleration
  • Seeking innovative solutions to dampen shocks through dynamic FEM simulations
  • Implement new guidelines on a prototype in terms of construction
  • Perform sheep-pendulum tests and verify the correlation of the FEM model
  • While obtaining a Master's degree, you are attracted by the world of watchmaking
  • You are following a specialization in numerical simulation of structures
  • You are proficient in LS-DYNA (self-explanatory) and are comfortable with CAD software (ideally Inventor)
  • You ideally have knowledge of Python or Matlab
  • You are fluent in French and have a good knowledge of English
  • Attentive, proactive and rigorous, you have excellent interpersonal skills and enjoy teamwork

We look forward to receiving your application documents and we will process them as soon as possible.
