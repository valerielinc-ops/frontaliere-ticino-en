Full description

As a specialist doctor or specialist for general internal medicine You combine medical quality with responsibility and use your expertise in a versatile clinical environment. For a modern acute care hospital in the canton of Aargau, we are looking for a senior physician or a senior physician for general internal medicine in a workload of 80 to 100%. They play a central role in inpatient and outpatient care, accompany assistant doctors in their further education and actively shape the further development of the internist clinic. What awaits you in this challenging task

  • Responsibility for the clarification, treatment and care of general internistic patients in the inpatient and outpatient area
  • Working in an interprofessional team with close professional exchange
  • Rotation in the context of the upper medical curriculum on bed stations, intermediate care unit, emergency center and outpatient areas
  • Active participation in the education and training of assistant doctors
  • Participation in the further development of the clinic and medical processes
  • Opportunity for scientific cooperation with renowned partner institutions

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine
  • Alternatively, you are close to completing the specialist title General Internal Medicine
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Interest in clinical development and medical quality

That sets them apart.

  • Commitment to quality patient care
  • Joy of teaching, knowledge transfer and supervision
  • Team-oriented, structured and responsible working methods
  • Interest in generalism and interdisciplinary cooperation

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Attractive employment conditions in a modern, certified acute care hospital
  • Clear training structure with rotations and development opportunities
  • Support for further education and training as well as leadership skills
  • Collaboration with renowned partners from research and teaching
  • Subsidized child care and modern infrastructure
  • Flexible working time models and attractive additional services

In addition for EU citizens

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition
  • Support for SRC recognition, travel organisation and accommodation by SEIRO (internal rental department)
