Your commitment to security and freedom

The Army Logistics Base (BLA) is there for the troop. With some 3,500 employees and 12,000 militia personnel, it provides all logistical services for the Swiss Army and manages its equipment, systems and infrastructure. The BLA offers a wide range of workstations throughout Switzerland. Whether you prefer to work in a workshop, in an office or in the open air, you will certainly find the right position for you.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 employees in four military professions and around 200 civilian professions, at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. It is also responsible for succession by training apprentices in more than 30 trades.
