Would you like to use your physiotherapeutic skills in a versatile environment, actively accompany people on their way to better health and quality of life and work in a committed team? Then this job could be just right for you. Stronger combines modern physiotherapy with targeted training and a great enthusiasm for exercise and sport. We offer you a dynamic working environment, versatile development opportunities and plenty of space for independent work and your own ideas. To strengthen our family team, we are looking for a motivated, committed and humorous personality who practises her profession with conviction and enjoys making a difference together with us. Of course, the pleasant sides of everyday work are not neglected: coffee, tea, protein shakes, an uncomplicated togetherness and your own music choice are part of us. ;- What to expect from us

  • A really great, uncomplicated and family team
  • Independent and flexible working in a cool practice in the middle of Zurich-Höngg
  • A very well-equipped training studio, which you can use at any time for your own training (racks, long and dumbbells, cable trains, etc.)
  • The opportunity to conduct personal training with exciting people
  • Versatile and interesting patients: from athletes (including personal training) to seniors and domicile therapy to movement muffles, everything is included.
  • 5 weeks of holidays and an additional week for further training including financial support
  • A good and fair wage

Who we are looking for

We look forward to seeing you if you

  • Bring a completed, Swiss-recognized physiotherapy education and get you excited about sports and exercise. A sports physio training is a plus.
  • Your patients also like to sweat and motivate them to grow beyond themselves.
  • Treat with passion and love to accompany people of all ages on their way to greater health and quality of life.
  • Bring team spirit, work independently and responsibly and perform your tasks efficiently and carefully.
  • Go through life openly, uncomplicatedly and humorously.
  • Want to develop professionally and personally.
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing.

In short, we are not looking for a perfect superhero or a perfect Superman, but a person who suits us professionally and humanly and who enjoys making a difference together. :- Sounds exciting?
