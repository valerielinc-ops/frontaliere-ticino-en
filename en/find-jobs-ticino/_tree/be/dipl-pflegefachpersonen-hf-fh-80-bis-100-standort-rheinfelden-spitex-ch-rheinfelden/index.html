Full description

Spitex Fricktal AG is an open-minded, modern and innovative non-profit Spitex. With around 100 employees and 20 apprentices, we ensure every day that people in the Fricktal can live at home in a self-determined and safe way. For more than 600 customers, we provide around 140,000 assignments every year – from somatic care and psychiatric services to specialized palliative care. We are in an exciting development phase and invest in people, processes and digitalization. For this we are looking for personalities who want to take responsibility, think and shape the future together with us. To strengthen our team in Rheinfelden, we are looking for committed personalities who will take on responsibility and shape the Spitex Fricktal of tomorrow together with us. Dipl.
