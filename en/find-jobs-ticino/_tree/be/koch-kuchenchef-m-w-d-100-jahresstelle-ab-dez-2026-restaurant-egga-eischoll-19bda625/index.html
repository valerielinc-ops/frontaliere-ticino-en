Full description

For our lively mountain restaurant Egga in Eischoll (two-season operation in the middle of the skiing and hiking area) we are looking for 15. December 2026 an independent and dutiful personality as chef / chef (m/f/d) in a permanent annual position.

Their main tasks

  • Self-responsible management, organization and structuring of the entire kitchen area
  • Preparation of freshly cooked, bourgeois dishes, daily menus and seasonal specialties
  • Menu planning, purchase of goods, calculation and efficient warehouse management
  • Consistent implementation and control of HACCP hygiene standards

Your profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as chef/chef EFZ or an equivalent, well-founded training
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally already in a management position or as a sole cook in the Swiss mountain hotel industry / gastronomy
  • Structured, clean and independent working methods
  • Resilient, team-capable personality with a high quality orientation

We offer

  • A long-term secure all-year job (100%) in a well-established family business
  • Modern kitchen infrastructure in top location directly on the piste and the hiking trails
  • Varied working between lively winter business and idyllic summer season
  • Current employment conditions according to L-GAV
