Are you passionate about insurance and have solid expertise in disaster management?

Join the Sinister sector, within the complex Sinister team, and actively contribute to the evolution of Zurich Switzerland.

As a complex disaster specialist, Things and Civil Responsibility, you play a key role in analyzing, managing and resolving complex cases. You evaluate insurance coverage, coordinate exchanges with internal and external stakeholders and find suitable solutions. With your expertise and sense of service, you contribute directly to the satisfaction of our customers.

Your missions

  • Management and resolution of complex "Whats and Liability" claims for private and corporate insurance
  • Analysis of claims and determination of applicable coverage
  • Transparent and effective communication with clients throughout claims processing
  • Negotiation with lawyers, injured persons, other insurers and other interested parties
  • Processing and implementation of appeals

Your profile

  • SWC or equivalent
  • At least five years experience in disaster management Things and Civil Liability as a disaster manager or inspector
  • Strong customer orientation, excellent relationship skills and very good editorial skills
  • Autonomy, flexibility and team spirit
  • An excellent mastery of French, knowledge of another national language is an asset.
