Full description

Nurse / ASSC for our Staff Pool, 10% to 100% The site of Plein-Soleil in Lausanne is a unique place of life and care in the canton of Vaud. It offers short-term or long-term accommodation, socio-cultural activities, workshops and day-time reception for people with progressive neurological diseases or brain damage. Do you have an interest in the field of neurology and want to work in a meaningful environment, driven by strong values, in a pleasant setting where residents are at the centre of our concerns? So we offer you to join our pool of staff and contribute to the quality of care and accompaniment of our residents!

Nurse / ASSC for our Staff Pool

Your mission

Depending on your training and skills, you will be responsible for:

  • Provide care and support to residents in daily living, taking into account their needs and abilities.
  • Contributing to the maintenance of autonomy, comfort and well-being of accompanied persons.
  • Participate in the implementation and follow-up of individual projects of care and support.
  • Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams to ensure quality management.
  • Ensure the transmission of information relevant to continuity and security of care.
  • Adapt yourself to the needs of the service and contribute to the smooth functioning of the teams.

Your profile

  • You have a Bachelor of Nursing degree or an SWC from ASSC, or an equivalent recognized designation for the position.
  • You show interest in the field of neurology and accompanying people with progressive neurological diseases or brain damage.
  • You show autonomy, flexibility and a strong sense of responsibility.
  • You appreciate teamwork and have good relationship skills.
  • You are a committed, caring and caring person.

We offer you

  • The possibility of practising an exciting profession in an institution recognised for the quality and diversity of its services.
  • A flexible activity thanks to our staff pool, depending on your availability and service needs.
  • The possibility to consult the proposed missions and position yourself directly via the HUBLO platform.
  • A stimulating working environment, fostering exchanges, proximity and teamwork.
  • The opportunity to develop your skills in the specialized field of neurology.
  • Working conditions according to the CLC of the parapublic health sector Vaudois and the provisions applicable to your commitment.

Type of engagement: one-off missions via the personnel pool. Entry into office: immediately Place of work : site de Plein-Soleil, Lausanne. Information: Mr Abdelilah Nouassi, Head of the care sector, at 021 651 27 20.

Do you want to complete your activity, diversify your experience or work with more flexibility? Join our staff pool and put your skills at the service of our residents!
