Full description

MAIN TASKS

In your role, you are responsible for order-related test setups in the field of static and acoustics. This includes in particular the following activities:

  • You coordinate test assignments and carry them out according to standards, guidelines and specific requirements
  • You support development engineers in the documentation and evaluation of the test results
  • You work closely with product management, development and quality management
  • You bring the user view into the new and further development of our products
  • You recognize and evaluate the needs of our users at home and abroad

PROFILE

You bring a degree as a Spengler sanitary installer or sanitary fitter EFZ. An additional further development in the field of building technology is advantageous. In addition, the following characteristics and abilities characterize you:

  • Several years of professional experience in the field of sanitary installations
  • Precise working methods and a good technical understanding
  • Good MS Office knowledge (Word, PowerPoint, simple Excel tables)
  • Communication strength and good English skills are beneficial

ADDITIONAL NOTES

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
