Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss delivery and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals and retailers).

Tasks

  • You are the central point of contact for our suppliers and internal interfaces and ensure the smooth flow of information
  • You open and maintain our article master data accurately and reliably
  • You support Category Management in product range design
  • You help with the price calculations
  • You create and analyze various statistics and evaluations
  • You help to stay up-to-date and identify trends early

Requirements

  • You have a degree as a merchant/merchant or an equivalent education
  • You will benefit from professional experience in product management or procurement in general
  • You are well-versed in MS-Office applications, especially Excel
  • You distinguish your highly structured, exact and independent working method
  • You are a team capable and reliable personality
  • Thanks to your flexibility, openness and fast expression, you can find your way around our very dynamic matrix organization.
