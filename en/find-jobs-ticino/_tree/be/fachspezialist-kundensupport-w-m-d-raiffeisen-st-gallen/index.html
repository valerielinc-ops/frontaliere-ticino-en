08.10.2026 St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

The Customer Service Center area is in an exciting further development phase. As part of our new group, we are creating the conditions for our employees in the Customer Service Center to have up-to-date expertise at all times, to comply with high quality standards and to be able to provide services in a professional, efficient and customer-oriented manner. Timely processing of bank and technical inquiries via the ticket system as well as independent processing of administrative and complex business cases, including telephone support of the customer service center (KSC) during peak hours. We are looking for a committed, analytically strong and implementation-oriented personality as a specialist customer support (m/f/d) at our St. Gallen location with home office possibility.

What awaits you?

  • Ensuring a current, understandable and practice-oriented knowledge transfer
  • Cooperation in quality assurance in day-to-day business as well as in the implementation and further development of projects and process optimizations
  • Processing of customer feedback and employee ideas in the voice-of-customer database

What do you bring?

  • Basic commercial training with banking orientation
  • Several years of professional experience in the banking environment (customer service or contact center) and a good understanding of banking services and customer-related support processes
  • Strong end-to-end thinking as well as banking and technical skills combined with high performance orientation
  • Experience in quality management, coaching or quality assurance is advantageous
