The Position The Global Clinical Supply Chain Operations (GCSC-O) packaging facility ensures timely and quality packaging of clinical samples for global clinical trials.

In this versatile key role, you combine production and value stream planning with operational work preparation (AVOR). They coordinate complex production processes and ensure smooth order processing – from preparation to system completion.

Promote as a central interface between packaging specialists and adjacent departments Efficient cooperation and a respectful and motivating working environment.

The possibility

Work preparation and order processing

  • Preparation, provision and technical completion of production orders in the ERP system
  • Clarification of deviations and technical and organizational challenges in coordination with relevant interfaces
  • Ensuring full and GMP-compliant documentation

Production & Value Flow Planning

  • Preparation and control of production and work plans taking into account human, machine and material resources
  • Monitoring and flexible adaptation of planning in the event of changes in priorities or bottlenecks
  • Close cooperation with adjacent departments to ensure a transparent flow of information

Process optimisation

  • Identification of bottlenecks and implementation of sustainable improvement measures
  • Analysis of production indicators (KPIs) to increase efficiency
  • Active participation and moderation of planning and CIP meetings

Who they are

  • Training: Completed vocational training or studies in logistics, business administration, industrial engineering or equivalent
  • Experience: 3–5 years in production planning, control and work preparation (AVOR), ideally in the pharmaceutical or clinical GMP environment
  • IT skills: Sound SAP or comparable ERP skills and secure handling of Google Workspace and MS Office
  • Methodological competence: experience with Lean Management, Six Sigma and process optimization projects
  • Personality: Analytical thinking, pronounced problem-solving skills, organizational talent and high social skills

You are a strong communication team player who works in a structured way, keeps an overview even in demanding situations and convinces through a solution-oriented, empathetic cooperation.
