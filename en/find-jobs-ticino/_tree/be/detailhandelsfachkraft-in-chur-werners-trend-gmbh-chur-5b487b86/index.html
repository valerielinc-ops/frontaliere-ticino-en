Since 1983, Werners Head Shop has been the leading family business in the field of head shop, e-cigarettes, shishas, CBD cannabis and many other lifestyle products. We are professional, but not uptight. We are trend hunters and trendsetters. Not least for these reasons, our shops in our product segments are number one in the Swiss market. For our branch in Chur we are looking for a motivated retailer (m/f/d).

Retail specialist (m/f/d) in Chur

  • Need-oriented customer advice and sales of our versatile products
  • Goods management & cash management
  • Assignment support
  • Appealing presentation of goods \& Store care
  • Compliance with ethical standards, guidelines and legal regulations

Your profile

  • You have completed an apprenticeship in the retail trade and ideally have been working with passion in our profession for years
  • You bring high social and communication skills
  • With your open-minded and cheerful personality, you fit great into our teams
  • You love working with a variety of people on a daily basis and have no fear of touching the diversity of Chur
  • You have a high memorability and complete your tasks with a lot of care and accuracy
  • You like to get involved and be a good team player
  • Ideally, you have experience in professional shopping and basic knowledge of our range
  • You are motivated, physically fit and have a well-groomed appearance

What you can expect from us

To make you feel really comfortable with us, you can rely on us to offer you an interesting working environment in which:

  • You can be yourself and be perceived by us as an individual
  • You work in a culturally diverse environment
  • You are promoted and have a long-term cooperation with high job security
  • You get a family environment with a sympathetic open team
  • In a growing market where there is room for ambition and creative ideas, you have the chance to actively engage
  • You can be part of a stable, sustainable and innovative company
  • You have up to 7 weeks vacation
