WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

Mission

Attached to the Vie-Série and Innovation department within the Development Directorate, your main mission will be to contribute actively to the management and coordination of the key projects of our roadmap. You will be a central player in our transformation plan, especially on the Manufacturing and BCP (Business Continuity Plan) performance initiatives. In close collaboration with internal and external interlocutors, you will coordinate with multiple actors in the advancement of projects.

Your Key Responsibilities

Coordinate and manage the Life-Series projects of the Swiss Jewellery Centre (hereinafter "PJS") – 60%

  • Piloting affected projects: transfer between sites, integration of new production processes...
  • Develop and ensure the smooth running and management of these projects at interfaces.
  • Pilot the project management process: milestones, deliverables, resources, interfaces, etc.
  • Provide a privileged relay between the Technical Project Managers and the Life Series & Innovation Manager.
  • Bring its "PMO" expertise to the PJS and Life-Series & Innovation.
  • If necessary, support strategic partners in the adoption of new technology/process bricks.
  • Participate in the EPM (Project Manufacturing Team) Life-Series and be guarantor of the project data in the Digital Weather Life-Series.

Coordinate, animate and ensure life-series changes in the PJS - 20%

  • Co-animate the amendment committee with the Technical Data Administrator.
  • Report the relevant changes to the Management Committee and the Technical Committee.
  • Disseminate and transversate relevant changes.

Report and communicate about the projects carried out - 20%

  • Coordinate with Technical Project Leaders the reporting of projects through dedicated committees.
  • Develop projects within the different sharing bodies.
  • Help strengthen the image of the Pole as a pioneer, agile and industrially effective player, both in the House and in industry.

WHAT ARE THE CLES OF YOUR SURVEY IN OUR HOUSE?

With engineering training or an equivalent course, you ideally have a proven experience in managing transversal, technical, R&D or Methods projects, acquired in a mature industrial environment with significant stakes. This experience has allowed you to develop strong leadership and a strong expertise from PMO.

You master project management methodologies and have an excellent knowledge of product development processes. You are also comfortable with the office tools (Pack Office) and SAP. Your experience has ideally taught you how to also handle PLM / CAD / FAO software (Windchill – CREO).

With a strong sense of organization, rigour and autonomy, you have excellent written and oral communication skills, as well as a sense of diplomacy. Your ability to work in project mode and to unite different actors, combined with your proactivity, your proposal strength and your results orientation, is an essential asset. Finally, your team spirit and relationship ease will allow you to integrate harmoniously and collaborate effectively with all actors, both internal and external.
