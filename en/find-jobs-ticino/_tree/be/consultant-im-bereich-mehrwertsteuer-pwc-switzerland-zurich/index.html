Payroll Consultant — PwC Switzerland

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Responsibilities

  • Perform the monthly payroll on a mandate basis, taking into account all applicable regulations and laws.
  • Prepare the withholding tax and social security declarations as well as the payslips.
  • Be the primary point of contact for clients of various sizes and industries—from small SMEs to larger branches of internationally operating companies.
  • Support clients with their personnel administration and proactively inform them about professional updates.
  • Maintain contact with clients, social security authorities and withholding tax offices.
  • Assist with the payroll of expatriates and create the shadow payroll.
  • Reconcile pension fund invoices, financial accounting and payroll accounting. ## Requirements
  • Hold a completed commercial basic training, preferably in the fiduciary sector.
  • Have at least two years of experience in payroll accounting and are familiar with personnel administrative tasks.
  • Deepen your expertise in payroll/social security and pursue further training to become a Payroll Specialist; aim for the federal certificate as an HR or social security professional as a long‑term goal.
  • Proficient in German and possess very good English skills.
  • Have good knowledge of SAP or the Abacus payroll module and strong Excel skills.
  • Work accurately and efficiently and are keen to continuously expand your knowledge.
  • Be motivated to work in a dynamic environment and gladly apply your payroll expertise as a consultant for our clients and their interesting requests.
