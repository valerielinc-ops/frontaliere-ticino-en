Full description

What to know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a family-run Swiss company with over 100 years of experience, but also one of the leading companies in the automotive industry. Quality, tradition, innovation and attention to the customer constitute the milestones of the company. Its activities focus on the import of vehicle brands from all over the world, the management of workshops, the logistics of spare parts and accessories and the provision of financial services. The Emil Frey SA Noranco is official dealer of the sale and services of the Toyota, Lexus, Kia, Suzuki, Volvo, Jeep, Jaguar and Land Rover brands and has a warehouse spare parts and a body shop. Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
