Tasks

  • Friendly reception and professional care of patients and visitors
  • Telephone exchange operation
  • Admission accompaniment of patients
  • Ensuring a smooth patient entry and exit process
  • General administrative activities and other organisational tasks

Requirements

  • Completed basic training as hotel specialist EFZ or as business assistant EFZ
  • Professional experience at reception or in a comparable service-oriented function
  • High service orientation as well as a friendly, respectful and professional appearance as well as excellent communication skills
  • Strong empathy and sensitivity in dealing with patients and relatives
  • Organizational talent as well as the ability to keep an overview and set priorities even in hectic situations
  • Team-oriented, reliable and cooperative way of working
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing; further language skills in French, Italian and English are beneficial
  • Willingness to work flexible hours

We offer

  • 28-33 paid leave days per year
  • Extensive opportunities for further education and training, e.g. own seminar program with a wide range of educational opportunities
  • Occupational health management with preventive measures such as ergonomic advice in the workplace
  • Part-time work and possibility of job sharing also in management functions
  • Bright, friendly rooms with modern infrastructure
  • Strong values and actively lived corporate culture
  • Characterisation and participation of the Claraspital by the employees
