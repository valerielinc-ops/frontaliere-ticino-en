Full description

Within the IT Service Management team, your main responsibility will be to lead and evolve the Request & Demand Management processes. You will be the key point of contact between the IT teams and the business to ensure effective, consistent, and high-quality request management.

Responsibilities

In this context, your main responsibilities will be to

  • Define and evolve the Request & Demand Management processes
  • Support IT teams in applying processes and ITIL best practices
  • Ensure the coordination and follow-up of business requests in collaboration with the various IT domains
  • Lead the service catalog and associated forms in ServiceNow
  • Produce monitoring indicators and contribute to the continuous improvement of processes
  • Facilitate governance forums and ensure effective communication with stakeholders
  • Train and support teams in adopting the processes

Candidate profile

  • With a higher education qualification in computer science or equivalent, you have 8 to 10 years of experience in a similar role
  • You have a strong command of ServiceNow and ITIL best practices
  • You have proven experience in Demand Management, Request Management, or IT Service Management
  • You are comfortable working in a cross-functional environment involving multiple business and IT stakeholders
  • Recognized for your organizational skills, analytical mindset, and solution-oriented approach, you are proactive
  • Agile and adaptable, you enjoy working in environments undergoing transformation
  • You have an excellent command of French and English, both spoken and written

What we offer

  • Health insurance coverage (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 weeks of annual vacation and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution toward your sports or cultural activities
  • The possibility of working remotely, depending on the position
  • A company restaurant
  • An advantageous LPP plan
