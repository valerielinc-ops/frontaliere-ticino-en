Full description

The canton's first employer, the Valais Hospital has 6,200 employees. Each year, 416 500 patients are cared for and nearly 652700 outpatient visits are provided. The Valais Hospital is looking for the Valais Romand Hospital Centre 60% medical secretary for the Flux Management Unit (FMU) service. Mission:

  • Manage, plan, organize applications for elective admission to hospital for CHVR.
  • Manage, plan, organize outpatient admission requests requiring a bed for the CHVR.
  • Identify, plan, correct, modify all elective entries and inform relevant CHVR services.
  • Ensure compliance with established procedures. Profile:
  • For the benefit of training as medical secretary and/or medical assistant or title deemed equivalent.
  • Mastery of one or both languages of the canton of Valais (French/German) and good knowledge of the second language an asset.
  • Mastery of computer tools (office automation) and typing.
  • Discretion and respect for professional secrecy.
  • A sense of organization and priorities.
  • Flexibility and versatility.
  • Positive and constructive attitude.
  • Meaning of dialogue and argumentation.
  • Good stress management.
  • Ability to work as a team while being autonomous.
  • Experience and knowledge of the hospital field. Only full bids and applications that meet the profile will be processed. Offer: A stimulating and diverse environment with the opportunity to leverage your knowledge and experience; attractive conditions of employment; opportunities for continuing training and further training.
