Full description

The University Children's Hospital of Basel (UKBB) is the university centre of excellence for pediatric and adolescent medicine as well as for teaching and research in northwestern Switzerland. For our social counselling / care management department we are looking for a motivated and committed personality as a social worker as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement.

Entry date: as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement

Their tasks

  • Inform, advise and support patient families in challenging situations
  • Advising patient families on various matters (social insurance, family and civil law, relief offers, contacts with employers, financial issues)
  • Networking of patient families with external specialist centres
  • Support of the social counselling team in all areas of responsibility
  • Participation in the child protection group UKBB
  • Participation in the Care Team UKBB. Appropriate training is required and can be completed if necessary.

Your profile

  • Social Work Training HF/FH
  • Some years of professional experience, possibly in clinical social work
  • Flexible and resilient personality with a positive attitude and friendly manners
  • Structured professional with high social and self-competence as well as networked thinking
  • Friendly dealings with patient families and in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Competence in solution-oriented discussion
  • Good knowledge of foreign languages (French, English, Italian) and good ICT skills
  • Willingness to take over Pikett service in the Care Team UKBB

Our offer

  • A dynamic and humorous team in a professional working atmosphere
  • An interesting, varied and meaningful activity
