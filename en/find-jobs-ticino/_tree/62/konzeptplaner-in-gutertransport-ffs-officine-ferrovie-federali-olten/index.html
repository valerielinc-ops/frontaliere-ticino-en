Concept planner: in freight transport 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Concept planner: in freight transport Olten / Work Smart, 01.02.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You will become part of a motivated and solution-oriented team in a dynamic environment where no day is like the other. Together with customers and internal contacts, you will develop optimal and sustainable transport solutions for single cargo transport and cleverly integrate them into our production concept. In this way, you make a tangible contribution to our customers and ensure that overall solutions are safe, economical and can be produced on time. Single freight wagon transport refers to the shipping of individual wagons or small groups of wagons in freight transport and combines flexibility with efficiency. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • In cross-process cooperation, you ensure optimal freight transport performance and anchor solutions end-to-end.
  • In the event of changing needs or framework conditions, you carry out feasibility tests, calculate costs and ensure operational feasibility.
  • You coordinate schedule changes with internal and external partners and ensure a smooth implementation.
  • Responsibility for single cargo transport is assumed by ordering the annual timetable as well as during the year concept adjustments including cost calculations, track rentals, adjustments of transport chains and the development of customer concepts.
  • Development topics in concept planning will drive forward and translate customer requirements into practical solutions.
  • Transparent coordination with sales, production and infrastructure operators is ensured and promotes continuous improvements along the planning process.

You bring that with you.

  • Strong conceptual skills as well as networked and interdisciplinary thinking set you apart.
  • Your confident appearance and your strong communication ability support you in exchange with different partners and finding common solutions.
  • With your planning and organizational skills, you control others and yourself effectively and keep an overview even for parallel tasks.
  • You approach your tasks with determination and a pronounced solution and customer orientation.
  • You bring a university degree (minimum bachelor) in the field of logistics, economics or traffic planning as well as experience in a planning activity in the transport or production industry. Knowledge of railway operations is a big plus.
  • Confident oral and written knowledge of German C1 as well as good knowledge of French at least at level B1 are required in this function.
