Full description

Fine manual work and precision play a central role in this task. The company attaches great importance to care and a good working environment. You can apply your concentration to challenging activities. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Partial covering of aluminum workpiece surfaces by means of film, manual brush painting or closing of bores and threads according to customer specifications
  • Upstream covering work for the blasting process
  • Optical inspection of the finished parts according to specified test criteria
  • Careful packaging of components

Requirements

  • Pronounced fine motor skills as well as craftsmanship and pleasure in careful manufacturing work
  • High reliability and a strong sense of duty
  • Good endurance and concentration ability in repetitive activities over several hours
  • Quick comprehension and ability to precisely implement shown work steps
  • Good oral knowledge of German and sufficient written knowledge

Offer Our client offers you a very varied and interesting task in an exciting environment. An environment that will excite you!
