Project management – (80-100%)

  • Project manager* in "Advertising Technology"
  • To support our project team "Advertising Technology", we are looking for a strong personality with professional and industry experience.
  • This is what awaits you

You will help with our challenging projects, advise our customers by phone, in writing and personally, calculate prices and independently handle orders from the idea to implementation. Thanks to your education and/or professional experience in the field of advertising technology, you are professionally at the side of our customers with your know-how. You know how to independently coordinate assignments, monitor appointments and competently tackle challenges. Your profile We are looking for a reliable, resilient and flexible person who convinces with his fast comprehension and his organizational skills. You master the MS Office programs and are familiar with industry-standard ERP systems. Since you are in direct customer contact, you bring very good oral and written communication skills in German and preferably also in French and English. We require a completed apprenticeship as an advertising technician or several years of experience in the field of advertising technology. This is what we offer you We offer you a varied and challenging job where you can contribute yourself and your skills every day. Since you are the interface between customer and production and support our sales and project team, you see many exciting business areas and work in a young, motivated team. The collegial working environment, the sympathetic DNA of our company and the state-of-the-art new building are other reasons why you should work with us. Further information
