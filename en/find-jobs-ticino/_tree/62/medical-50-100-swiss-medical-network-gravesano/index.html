Full description

To complete our team, we are looking for a doctor/a hospital/a interested in the perioperative grip of orthopaedic patients. The position provides for a clinical activity of department and within the I Intermediate Care, in an interdisciplinary context and oriented to the quality of the patient’s taking into account.

Its main activities

  • clinical perioperative management of patients undergoing orthopaedic surgery;
  • pain management;
  • evaluation and treatment of internal problems that may arise during the decency;
  • surveillance of vital parameters within Level I Intermedies;
  • interdisciplinary collaboration with the different professionals involved in the treatment path.

Your profile

  • degree in medicine and qualification of medical specialist already recognized in Switzerland and registered in MedReg at the time of application;
  • authorisation to exercise the medical profession in Canton Ticino;
  • excellent knowledge of the Italian language;
  • proven experience of at least 6 months in Intensive Therapy or at least 2 years in Anesthesia, indispensable requirement;
  • attitude to interdisciplinary work;
  • professional autonomy and good organizational skills.

Our offer

  • Contractual terms attractive;
  • integration into an interdisciplinary professional context and oriented to the quality of the patient's grip.

The Ars Medica Clinic is the reference pole in Ticino for orthopaedic surgery and traumatology of the locomotor apparatus, for the surgery of the spine and sports medicine.
