Through its projects and services, Caritas Bern combats poverty and social exclusion, promotes integration and stands up for people. To this end, it works with various partners and undertakes public-sector mandates. Join us in promoting solidarity and respectful coexistence as…

Team Lead Caritas Care – Family Caregivers 80-100%

At Caritas Bern, you will take on responsibility in a diverse and meaningful environment. Your tasks are close to people, varied and immediately impactful. Through the «Caring for Relatives» program, we enable clients in need of care to receive the best possible, person-centred care from their relatives. Family caregivers are not left alone: our nursing professionals HF/FH provide them with professional advice, guidance and support. They are compensated and appropriately insured for their valuable work.

A role – as individual as you are

To lead our program in the Canton of Bern, we are looking for a Team Lead Nursing HF/FH, starting by agreement. In this diverse role, you will be responsible for the professional and personnel management of a team of case-managing nursing professionals HF/FH. At the same time, as a member of the inter-cantonal nursing committee, you will actively contribute to shaping and further developing the program. Direct work with clients also remains part of your role: as a case-managing nursing professional, you will manage and support the care process and ensure high-quality, person-centred care. You will provide family caregivers with competent, needs-based advice and support.

You are a good fit for us if you

  • hold a valid driver's license and have your own car
  • For this responsible role, we require an HF/FH degree in nursing and leadership experience, preferably in Spitex.
