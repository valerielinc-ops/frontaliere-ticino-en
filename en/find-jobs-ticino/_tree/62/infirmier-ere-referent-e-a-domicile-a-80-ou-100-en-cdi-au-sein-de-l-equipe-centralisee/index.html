Full description

If you are looking for a dynamic environment where your care expertise will be used for the well-being of patients, join our team within the centralized long-term pool (PCLD)! As a member of the PCLD, you will be required to work throughout the Canton of Geneva for long-term replacements, varying from 6 to 15 months, within our branches. Your main mission will be to ensure continuity of patient monitoring, by acting as a referent in the management of their files and by providing quality services.

We are looking for committed people to fill a position where you will have the opportunity to:

  • Take on stimulating challenges and have enriching professional experiences.
  • Broaden your horizons by working over a long period of time in different sectors in Geneva, within various teams and with different types of patients.
  • Acquire in-depth knowledge of the Geneva health and social network, as well as our institution and the field of home help and care
  • Develop your theoretical and practical skills through adapted training.

By joining the PCLD team, you will be able to

  • Assume the role of referent in assessing patient needs, in accordance with the ethical principles of the nursing profession, within the home care team model.
  • Provide personalized nursing care, autonomous or by medical delegation, covering a full range of preventive, curative, educational and palliative services, directly to patients of all ages, at home.
  • Work in close collaboration with care and community health assistants as well as multidisciplinary assistants, delegating care according to established procedures.
  • Organize medico-social procedures and ensure coordination with all stakeholders involved in the situation of a given patient, by mobilizing the available resources of the primary and secondary network.
  • An HES-SO Bachelor's degree in nursing or training deemed equivalent, as well as recognition from the Red Cross.
  • Excellent ability to adapt and delegate, with flexibility and demonstrated availability.
  • A sense of responsibility and solution orientation, essential to meet the challenges encountered.
  • An ability to work independently and interprofessionally within a multidisciplinary team.
  • Essential mobility, which may involve travel on foot, by bike, by public transport (TPG) or by car depending on the missions.
  • Entry into office: to be agreed
  • Please attach to your file:
  • an up-to-date curriculum vitae,
  • a cover letter,
  • a copy of your diplomas and work certificates.
  • We only consider complete application files

We guarantee total confidentiality in the processing of your file.
