Medtronic, a pioneer in medical technology, is looking for a dedicated technical consultant for our team of sacred neuromodulation (Pelvic Health). German-speaking Switzerland.

The role of the Technical Consultant (TC) is to support our customers with applications related to the Sacred Neuromodulation (SNM) product area, especially implantation and aftercare. This is done in close coordination with the area responsible, the Therapy Sales Specialists. They build close relationships with customers, provide on-site technical support, act as a link between customers and internal teams in order to be able to offer our service and product solutions to customers in a timely and successful manner.

Become part of a team that changes patients’ lives: Support professionals inside and outside the operating room and bring innovation to the healthcare industry. Build trusting partnerships with healthcare professionals and advance groundbreaking technologies. Make the difference – apply now!

This position covers the regions – Central Switzerland (Luzern, Zurich) and is therefore a remote position. Remote means that your home address is considered the primary place of work (home office). Meetings may occasionally require your presence in the Medtronic Office.

Responsibilities include as follows

  • Technical surgical support for doctors and surgical staff, mainly urologists/gynecologists as well as surgeons and proctologists in hospitals, clinics and outpatient facilities.
  • Support of our customers through surgical aftercare in the form of product instructions and programming, patient training as well as assistance with inquiries from doctors, nurses and patients.
  • Implementation of training courses, workshops and product training for customers, participation in doctor training and accompanying doctors.
  • Market monitoring, exchange and dissemination of current market information
  • Participation in national and international congresses and events
  • Ensuring a high level of customer satisfaction through close support and excellent service
  • You are the product specialist of sacred neurostimulation or Sacred nerve stimulation and one relies on your knowledge.

Required knowledge and experience

  • Studies in business administration, or medical/medical engineering
  • 2-5 years of experience in comparable positions, preferably in medical technology, with a demonstrable success in customer support and loyalty
  • German and English fluent in words and writing, knowledge of Italian would be an advantage
  • Secure appearance, strong communication strengths and team spirit
  • Good MS Office and IT skills
  • Very good presentation skills
  • High operational readiness, flexibility and resilience, combined with high travel activity in Switzerland
  • Independent working methods as well as efficient time and task planning are among your strengths
