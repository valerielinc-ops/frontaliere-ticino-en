About us

The research project Nutrition System Biel – Intercantonal Model Region for Sustainable Nutrition (term 2026 - 2029) aims to establish the region from Jura to Biel to Seeland and parts of the Mittelland as a model for a sustainable, regional food system by increasing the proportion of sustainably produced food in community catering and gastronomy. The bilingual project is transdisciplinary and involves actors from municipal and cantonal administrations, natural parks and stakeholders along the food value chain as project partners. There is also the possibility of participation in program evaluations.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
