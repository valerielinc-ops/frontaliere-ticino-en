A / A specialist in research and analysis of audience data — SRG SSR

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The Audience & Offer Portfolio department brings together strategic functions for the development of a modern SRG SSR, oriented to the public and ready for the future. It deals with public and market research and analysis in all regions of Switzerland, providing strategic insights to support decisions. It also coordinates SRG SSR content portfolio quality development and evaluation processes on all channels, in line with corporate strategy and public service mandate.

Tasks, skills and responsibilities

  • Contribute to the future development of the SRG SSR portfolio of content and services, with particular attention to RSI, integrating the needs and behaviors of the public in strategic decisions and improving the individual offers.
  • Connecting research specialists and teams dealing with content, distribution, portfolio management and strategy, translating analysis results into clear and concrete recommendations.
  • Analyze, interpret and visualize data related to the public to identify trends, respond to business needs and contribute to reporting on content and audience performance.
  • Collaboration with the design and implementation of qualitative and quantitative studies, developing research tools and dealing with the analysis and interpretation of results.

Requirements

  • University degree, preferably in social sciences, sociology, economics, psychology, communication, media studies or related disciplines.
  • Strong analytical skills, curiosity and interest in data analysis and understanding of public behavior.
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to explain complex concepts and transform search results into practical recommendations.
  • Structured working method, autonomy, teamwork attitude and initiative in proposing new ideas and solutions.
  • Good knowledge of German or French, ideally at level B1/B2, and mastery of Microsoft Office tools, especially Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Experience in public research, study design, survey analysis, or using tools such as Python, R and Power BI is an advantage. We also appreciate the willingness to acquire new skills and knowledge of RSI content and services.

What we offer

  • The opportunity to contribute concretely to the future of public service media in Switzerland, bringing the public perspective in the strategic decisions of SRG SSR.
  • A diversified role, ranging from research on public behavior to content analysis and the development of new offers.
  • The possibility to work in a motivated and interdisciplinary team, collaborating with colleagues from all the language regions of Switzerland.
  • An environment that enhances personal ideas, taking responsibility and professional development in public research and data analysis areas.

Our promise

What are the ingredients to reach the inhabitants of Switzerland with quality format? Know-how, enthusiasm and an environment rich in stimuli, in which everyone can grow. To give the best it takes a source of inspiration: Welcome to the world of RSI!

  • Produce content: Quality journalism is our main activity and our passion. We enjoy editorial freedom, promote creativity and give ample space to the production of new and high-level content.
  • Give shape to digital: Quality journalism is also digital. We explore new possibilities and experience innovative forms: digital, interactive and crossmedia. A look at tomorrow for our spectators today.
  • Valorize the person: We cultivate solidarity relations, work independently and contribute together with a good working climate. Our success is also based on the personal and professional qualities of all employees.
  • Make sense: With our programs we reach and thrill Switzerland. We act with respect and sense of independence; we promote the free formation of opinions and strengthen pluralism. We are proud of the contribution we make to Switzerland.
