Full description

Eisberg AG is one of the leading producers of ready-to-eat salads, fruits and vegetables in Switzerland. According to our motto 'Colour Your Life', our over 800 employees at the locations Dällikon, Dänikon and Essert-sous-Champvent produce with passion and commitment up to 60 tons of fresh food daily. Eisberg is proud to be part of the internationally renowned Eisberg Group, which is considered the number one in the production of ready-to-eat salads in Central Europe. The Eisberg Group operates its own state-of-the-art production facilities in Switzerland and Austria. As a member of the Bell Food Group, which combines the renowned brands Bell, Hilcona, Eisberg and Hügli, we strive to set high quality standards and to delight the taste of our customers. To temporarily replace part of our team, we are looking for a reliable and independent person to join our 'Quality' team at our Essert location as: ## Tasks

  • Quality and food safety management system: support to the quality department employees in various tasks related to the monitoring plans (analyses and controls).
  • Update, improvement or creation of the operational documentation related to the IFS Food Standard and our customers' requirements (quality management system documentation).
  • Training of our employees regarding the existing documentation as well as the regulations for hygiene and food safety.
  • Food safety management system used in the production facilities: active participation in the various control plans and monitoring of the quality system and involvement in the corrective measures and corrective actions.
  • Participation in the various on-site controls and inspections.
  • Accompaniment and verification of the application of hygiene and food safety regulations on-site.
  • Update of the training materials and participation in the training of operators and area managers.

  • Control plan for phytosanitary control of raw materials.
  • Control plan for allergens.
  • Control plan for hand washing and GHP.
  • Plan to combat food fraud.
