Full description

Portrait In the school year 2026 2027 126 children and young people go to school in Eriswil. 24 children attend the kindergarten at Ahornstrasse 15. 102 students enter and leave the school. In the Eriswil School House, newly renovated classrooms, a well-equipped library, workrooms, group rooms and the school kitchen offer space and the opportunity to make classes in mixed-age classes varied and exciting.

Tasks

You take on about 4–6 lessons of speech therapy and accompany students with bVSA int status in their linguistic and communicative development.

To your

  • Tasks include logopaedic promotion and therapy as well as – as required – clarifications and consultations.

You work closely with the relevant teachers, IF teachers, parents and other specialists involved.

The pensum offers you a manageable introduction with a lot of design freedom. At the same time, there is the possibility that the scope of logopaedic activity at our school will expand in the medium term. If you wish, there is a chance that you will expand your workload with IF lessons.

Requirements

? You have training as a speech therapist or are in appropriate training.

? You like to work independently, contribute your professional ideas and at the same time appreciate the exchange in a multi-professional team.

? An appreciative and resource-oriented support of our students is important to you.

We offer

A small, personal school environment, in which cooperation is uncomplicated and you can actively shape your work.

We offer you about 4–6 lessons with flexible design options and the interesting perspective of expanding your workload in the medium term
