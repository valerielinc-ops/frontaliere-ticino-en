You work in international teams with clients from all over the world. We are solution-oriented and offer a flexible working environment that allows hybrid work. Fill your backpack with practical experience and knowledge from all over the world and develop your tax advisory skills every day with us. How you can make an impact

  • You will join our dynamic tax advisory team and will deal with tax advisory projects in a wide range of areas: Swiss and international taxation of cross-border employment structures and planning, equity incentives, pensions, rulings, etc.
  • You will have the opportunity to manage projects with the support of our leadership team from day one, to be involved in client-facing activities in projects in the field of individual taxes.
  • You can participate and help shape solutions: we advise our clients ad hoc because the general information and easy solutions are already available on the Internet. For this, you will work with our national tax office to do research, write and prepare tailored advice.
  • You can learn and apply your technical knowledge directly on fascinating cases. You help prepare and deliver presentations to clients and colleagues.
  • Combine your strengths and skills with the know-how of our experienced teams – in this power combination you can develop your full professional and personal potential and help our clients in a wide range of individual tax topics. Who We Are Looking For
  • You hold a bachelor's or master's degree from a university or university of applied sciences in business administration, law or a similar field.
  • You have a minimum of 5 years of professional experience in providing tax advisory services to international clients.
  • You are a team player who appreciates the daily contact with clients and colleagues in our network. You like to work independently, efficiently and question the "status quo".
  • You enjoy writing, doing research, exploring options and help design solutions. You are eager to learn and willing to develop and would like to benefit from our tax trainings.
  • You are outgoing and enjoy helping people solve their challenges and you also have a thirst for knowledge that will bring new perspective to our projects.
  • You are able to manage tasks with competing deadlines and prioritise accordingly as well as like to work in teams and have experience in managing them.
  • You speak German or French fluently and have very good English skills. Your Team Become part of a diverse, dynamic, and highly motivated team that is growing!
