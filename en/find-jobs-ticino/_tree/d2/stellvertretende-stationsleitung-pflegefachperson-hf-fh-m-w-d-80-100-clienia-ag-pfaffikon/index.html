Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With more than 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in ambulatory, day-clinical and inpatient settings. The Group employs around 1800 people and achieved a turnover of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For the area of adult psychiatry Station Park D with treatment focus crisis intervention and treatment of dependency diseases, we are looking for 1. February 2027 or by agreement

Taking responsibility together - accompanying people professionally and appreciatively. For our psychiatric acute care unit, we are looking for a committed, responsible and leadership-interested personality as deputy station manager. In this versatile function, you support the station management in the professional, personnel and organizational management of the care team. Together, you will create a professional, safe and appreciative treatment environment for people in acute psychological crises. Station Park D offers support and assistance in mental health crises to adults between the ages of 18 and 60. We work according to current clinical guidelines and findings of evidence-based medicine. The recovery-oriented therapeutic concept of the station combines disorder-specific therapeutic elements with established crisis intervention techniques and milieutherapeutic action factors. In addition, Park D offers qualified withdrawal treatment.
