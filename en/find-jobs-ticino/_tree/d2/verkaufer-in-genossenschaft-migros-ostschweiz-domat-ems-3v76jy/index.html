Full description

Do you have a feeling for high-quality basic foods and are enthusiastic about the variety of our supermarket products? 🥦🍫 You are strong sales 💪 and like to communicate safely with our customers? 😊 Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be «tämä success». 🤝🧡

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, M Domat Ems, Via La Val 1, 7013 Domat Ems

Mansioni

  • You make sure that all shelves are always well filled and the goods are optimally managed so that customers always find what they are looking for. 🛒
  • Friendly and competent customer service and advice 😊🛍️ are a matter of course for you and you advise the customers with your expertise.
  • You ensure that the products are presented attractively and efficiently arranged to create a pleasant shopping experience. 🛍️
  • You take responsibility for ordering and checking the goods and ensure a quick and friendly conclusion to the purchase at the checkout. 📦💳
  • With your commitment, you help ensure that the entire shop remains neat, clean and inviting ✨.

Requirements

  • Professional experience is advantageous: preferably in the retail trade
  • Completed basic or further training: preferably in the retail trade
  • Reliability, flexibility and a high level of commitment 💪⏰ distinguish you – you are always there when you are needed.
  • With your independent work and your friendly approach 😊🤝 you create a pleasant atmosphere for customers and team.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses
