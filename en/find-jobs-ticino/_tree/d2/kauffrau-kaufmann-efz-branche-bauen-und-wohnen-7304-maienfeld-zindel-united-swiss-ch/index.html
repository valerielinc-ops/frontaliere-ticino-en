Kauffrau/Kaufmann EFZ Industrie Bauen und Wohnen About the profession As a merchant EFZ, you are the linchpin of a company, organizing, coordinating, planning and handling a wide range of administrative tasks. In addition to the general secretarial work such as receipt of orders, data collection and maintenance as well as billing and customer reception You also engage in more challenging activities such as business correspondences, recording bookings, checking payments, organizing sessions and writing minutes.   • Amounts recorded
  • Checking accounts
  • Send payment reminders

Secretariat General

  • Manage schedules for meetings and appointments
  • Updating databases such as customer, order and supplier directories
  • Receive customers or visitors
  • Accept telephone calls and process emails

Other activities

  • Implement marketing and communication activities, prepare multimedia content
  • Organisation of meetings and events
  • Cooperation in the IT environment

You bring that with you As a merchant / businesswoman EFZ you have a flair for numbers and can also express yourself well in writing and orally in German.
