Full description

The Cantonal Tax Administration is responsible for the collection of direct taxes in the Canton of Graubünden insofar as the legislation does not provide for a competence of the Federation or the municipalities. The Commissariat assesses the income and property taxes of natural persons (direct federal tax and cantonal tax).

We are looking for a tax administration, department commissariat, as of 1 December 2026 or by agreement an intern. The internship lasts at least six months and can be completed up to one year. There is the possibility of an extension of the internship or a subsequent further employment.

Tasks

  • Support for pre-processing and post-processing work related to the assessment of natural persons
  • Support for clean-up work
  • Examination and determination of tax factors for income and wealth taxes of natural persons (unemployed persons) on the basis of tax returns

Requirements

  • In ongoing academic or vocational training (Bachelor/Master, University/Fachhochschule or Berufsmaturität)
  • Rapid comprehension and reliable, careful and organized working methods
  • Joy of working independently
  • Interest in taxation and readiness for professional training
  • Good computer skills
  • Style-confident oral and written expression in German
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is advantageous
