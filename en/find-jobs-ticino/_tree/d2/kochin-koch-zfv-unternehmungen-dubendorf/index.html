Full description

The Allegra restaurant of Helsana Versicherungen AG consists of a self-service section with around 252 seats and the Cafeteria Oase with also 240 seats. In addition, catering and aperitifs are carried out on request. True to the ZFV philosophy, we cook fresh on site with regional, ecologically sensibly produced and fair-trade food. Are you our new team member who supports us as a chef: (80%) with immediate effect or by appointment? You work from Monday to Friday at an 80% hour, usually from 06:00 a.m. to 03:00 p.m. For events, working hours may vary until 07:00 p.m. Your task menu:

  • Creation of the mise en place on the assigned item
  • Production of the food at the assigned post (entremetier, gardemanger, saucier, etc.)
  • Ensuring replenishment at the menu lines
  • Regeneration / preparation of the menus during the lunch service
  • Collaboration at catering, aperitifs and special events
  • Goods receipt and warehouse management
  • Contribute ideas for menu design
  • Ensuring cleanliness and order at the assigned post
  • Completion of general clean-up and cleaning work
  • Implementation of ZFV quality and service standards
  • Compliance with and implementation of hygiene and occupational safety guidelines Your recipe for success:
  • A completed vocational training as a cook: EFZ
  • 2-4 years of professional experience in the kitchen
  • Enjoyment of cooking and a high standard of quality and presentation
  • Team spirit and a reliable, independent way of working
  • Customer orientation and the willingness to adapt to new situations
  • Openness to new ideas and enjoyment of professional development
  • Good oral and written German skills What we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that is good for you. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 56,000 and CHF 60,000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
