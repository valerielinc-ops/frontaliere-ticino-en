Full description

Our five-star hotel in the heart of Zermatt has welcomed guests from all over the world since 1879. The Grand Hotel Zermatterhof combines tradition, timeless elegance and personal hospitality with the unique mountain scenery around the Matterhorn. Our varied gastronomy ranges from fine dining with Valais ingredients to a French brasserie to traditional Swiss specialties such as fondue and raclette. ur Reinforcement of our team we are looking for warm, motivated personalities. We look forward to your application.

Their tasks

  • Exclusive care and personal advice of our international guests at the highest level
  • Acceptance and processing of individual guest wishes with the utmost care and discretion
  • Coordination and communication with travel agencies, VIP customers and external partners
  • Management of reservations and correspondence in German, English and French
  • Professional implementation of check-in and check-out, including own cash management and billing
  • Ensuring a smooth process at the front office through independent and structured work
  • Translation and adaptation of guest information for an international audience
  • Working to the highest standards (LQA) to ensure an exceptional guest experience
  • Working closely with the front office team and other departments to ensure first-class service for VIP and regular guests

Your profile

  • Commercial or comparable training completed
  • Several years of experience as a receptionist, ideally in the upscale hotel industry or in a 5-star environment
  • fluent knowledge of German, English and French
  • Other foreign languages are beneficial
  • Safe handling of MS Office
  • Knowledge of Fidelio Front-Office Suite 8 is desirable
  • Pronounced service and guest orientation, sovereign appearance and a well-kept appearance
  • Highest discretion, empathy and empathy in dealing with discerning guests
  • Structured, independent working method paired with flexibility and resilience
  • Experience working to LQA standards and striving for excellence in guest service

We offer

  • An attractive, performance-related compensation
