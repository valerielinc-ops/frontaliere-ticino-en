Full description

Medical genetics at a clinic where genetic diagnostics and counselling come together from the prenatal period to high adulthood. In Bern, we are looking for a senior physician or a senior physician for medical genetics in the amount of 80 – 100% by appointment. In a university clinic for human genetics with full training authorization of category A, you can expect an interdisciplinary, scientifically integrated environment with a broad diagnostic spectrum. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They look after outpatient patients in a lively consultation session across the entire spectrum of genetic diagnosis and counselling, from the prenatal period to late adulthood.
  • They participate in several interdisciplinary special consultation hours, including together with cardiology, dermatology, nephrology, neurology and ophthalmology
  • They take over the consiliar assessment and clarification of inpatients in the children's clinics as well as in the adult sector
  • They work closely with the medical profession of the other hospitals of the hospital
  • They are involved in scientific questions and university training

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for medical genetics or equivalent foreign specialist title with federal recognition
  • Ideally, several years of professional experience
  • Interest in scientific questions and pleasure in personal training
  • Very good knowledge of German

That sets them apart.

  • Openness, collegiality and willingness to integrate into the existing team
  • Teamwork and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level
  • Responsible, careful work with high professional standards

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Demanding, responsible work in a dynamic, varied working environment
  • Excellent university work and training environment as a training company of classification A with promotion of individual career and career paths
  • Opportunity to participate in national and international research networks together with the University of Bern
  • Comprehensive training and development opportunities with very good scientific links
  • Various perks, including in the hospital pharmacy, in the staff restaurant, for sports offers, concert tickets and mobile subscriptions

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for the SRK or MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
