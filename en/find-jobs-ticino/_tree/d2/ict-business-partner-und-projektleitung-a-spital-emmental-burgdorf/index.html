Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

In the middle of life. In the middle of the action. Create the digital future of tomorrow with us. You know Navision or Business Central inside out and want to use your know-how where it makes a difference? Instead of changing customer mandates, you can expect a company, an ERP and projects whose impact you see every day – in a hospital that is there for the people of the region. For our ICT at the Burgdorf location, we are looking for one from 1 January 2027 or by agreement:n

Tasks

  • You are the link between business and ICT: as a central contact person for the administrative departments, you understand their processes, take up needs and translate them into viable ICT solutions
  • You will continue to develop our ERP: You will play a key role in shaping the further development of our ERP landscape based on Microsoft Dynamics NAV / Business Central and promote the migration from Navision to Business Central in a professional and project-oriented manner.
  • You lead projects to the goal: You lead ICT projects, sub-projects and general ICT projects from initialisation to implementation, manage costs, deadlines, quality and risks and coordinate internal teams as well as external partners and suppliers
  • You create the basis for decision-making: With business analyses, requirements and process recordings as well as solution concepts, you lay the foundation for well-founded decisions and report transparently up to the level of management
  • You continue to think about processes: You recognize optimization and digitalization potential, standardize processes and advance the consolidation of our application landscape
  • You ensure operation: Together with Application Management, you ensure tests, change and release processes, user training and clean documentation

Profile

  • You know Navision and Business Central from practice: You bring several years of in-depth experience with Microsoft Dynamics NAV and/or Business Central – whether as a consultant, project manager, application manager, or experienced:r Key User:in
  • You have successfully implemented projects: You have several years of project management experience in the ERP or ICT environment, ideally with introductions or migrations. A certification (e.g. HERMES, IPMA, PMI or Scrum) is a plus
  • You think business: You have a degree at the level of HF, FH or university in business informatics, computer science or business administration or a comparable career and understand business processes in detail
  • You know the health care system – or want to get to know it: Experience in the hospital or health environment is an advantage, but not a requirement. Knowledge of ITIL rounds off your profile
  • You convince at all levels: You communicate clearly, translate confidently between specialist and ICT language and create acceptance – from the department to the management. You speak and write very good German
  • You keep the overview: Independent, structured and solution-oriented you set priorities and keep a cool head even in several parallel projects
