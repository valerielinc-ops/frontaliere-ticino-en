Put to the Point

Does your heart beat for logistical topics in a unique environment? With this task, the logistics base of the Swiss Army offers you the opportunity to ensure the proper functioning of the reception, storage, dispatch and delivery as well as the handover of army material in the Oberland camp. An exciting challenge awaits you as well as the opportunity to contribute and further develop your expertise.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-19892
  • Location: Behind the Egg 123c, 3815 Gündlischwand

You can make this contribution

  • Ensure the deputy of the chief Lager Oberland in all respects in case of their absence
  • Planning and coordinating dates and work processes in materials management
  • Multi-faceted orders in the area of replenishment process independently (the work is done to a large extent underground)
  • Ensure proper storage management on site and ensure planning and implementation of inventories in all warehouses
  • Ensure quality of goods stored

This makes them unique.

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a logistics specialist with further training as a logistics specialist
  • Several years of experience in logistics and management experience desired
  • Good organisation as well as forward-looking and conscientious working methods
  • Required category B driving licence and SUVA-approved forklift training
  • Category C driving licence is beneficial and to be appropriated if necessary
  • Very good MS Office and SAP skills, English B1 is an advantage
  • Readiness for coercive and weapons training required

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
