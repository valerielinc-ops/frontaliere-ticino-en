Full description

Location: Denner SA, Aclens Distribution Centre, Chemin du Bochet 2, 1123 Aclens

Join the adventure of Denner's first distribution centre in French-speaking Switzerland! As a team leader in the receipt of goods, you guide your teams, optimize flows and guarantee quality and timeliness. Each action makes a difference and helps make Denner's heart beat.

Luogo di lavoro: Denner SA, Aclens Distribution Centre, Chemin du Bochet 2, 1123 Aclens

Important remarks

Schedules Monday to Friday 6:30 am/16:15 am, about 6 Saturdays to manage peaks related to holidays. All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Lead and develop a team of collaborators (approximately 8 people)
  • Ensure the management of goods entries (control, system update, stocking) and the recovery and processing of packaging
  • Organize evaluation interviews and conduct annual assessments of staff competencies
  • Participate in the implementation of the Continuous Improvement Process (CAP) in operational activities
  • Planning and carrying out inventories
  • Participate in specific projects

Required

  • Professional experience: 3-5 years: In a comparable function
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): in the field of logistics, supplemented by a Federal Certificate of Specialist in Logistics
  • A proven experience in team leadership
  • Analytical mind, process oriented and networked thinking
  • A capacity for reflection and cross-cutting actions
  • Very good communication skills and high social skills
  • French (Very good mastery)
  • German (Basic terms)
  • English (preferably)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Company parking: Free parking: Do you come by car? We offer free parking.
  • Maternity/paternity leave: 5 additional weeks of maternity leave (19 weeks in total) with a salary of 100% and 4 weeks of paternity leave.
  • Mobile subscription: The embarrassment of choice: subscription Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise from 10 francs.
  • Migros Bank: Better interest rates and less charges on your private, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Holidays: 6 weeks of vacation for everyone and extra days for anniversary service.
  • Individual continuing training: In-house training and financial support for useful external training.

Contatto

Candidature and contact Ines Colorado HR Business Partner Logistics

Recruitment process

  • Interview with HR and the department: The interview is conducted in distanciel or face-to-face with the HR person responsible and/or the specialized department. It serves primarily to learn more about personal and professional skills and allows both parties to assess cultural compatibility with the company and the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we conduct an additional step, the personality test (BIP, scale test or similar) to support the selection. The results of this test are discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Test work: A day or half-day on trial allows you to become familiar with the future working environment and team members. Conversely, we have the opportunity to see how compatible the candidates are with the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and establishment of contract: The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by post. Return time: up to 3 weeks
