Your tasks and perspectives

  • Accompanying and supervising complex patient situations throughout the entire treatment path on our certified oncological bed station

What you bring for this job

  • Interest in the field of oncology and motivation to constantly develop
  • Empathetic personality with high social skills and balance
  • High flexibility and adaptability in the design of everyday life
  • Strong skills in self-organisation and prioritisation

Our offer to you

  • Flexibility in service planning and openness to different working time models within the framework of the service plan concept
  • Well-coordinated, helpful and warm team
  • Individual introductory programme including targeted introduction to the field of Oncology
  • Easy access to an extensive internal training and further education offer as well as subject-specific training after successful introduction to promote individual further development
  • Engaged collaboration in a highly specialized interdisciplinary and interprofessional environment
