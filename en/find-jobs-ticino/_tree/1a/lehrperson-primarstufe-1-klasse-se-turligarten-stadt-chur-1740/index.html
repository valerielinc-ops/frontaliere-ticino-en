Full description

Teacher Primary level 1st grade, SE Door League Types With 41,000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees. The Chur City School is the largest public school in the canton of Graubünden with numerous attractive offers from kindergarten to secondary school.

The Türligarten School House is one of the smaller primary school houses in Chur and is located on the edge of the old town. In the corresponding 3 kindergartens and 6 primary classes, around 170 children are taught by about 22 teachers.

At the Türligarten School House, the professional, targeted and uncomplicated cooperation of all participants is of great importance. Small but fine, so the school house can be briefly described.

To complement our team, we are looking for a school year 2027/28 (August 1, 2027).
