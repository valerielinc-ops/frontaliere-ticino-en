Full description

Within the framework of our Temporary Sigma Building department, we are pleased to collaborate with several companies recognized in Valais, active in the fields of electrical installation of the building, renovations and new constructions. To meet their needs, we are actively looking for a: CFC installer (M/F)

Your responsibilities

  • Reading and interpreting electrical drawings and diagrams
  • Construction of electrical installations in residential, commercial and administrative buildings
  • Installation of ducts, tubes, pipes and cable paths
  • Drawing and connection of electrical cables
  • Installation and connection of electrical panels
  • Installation of electrical appliances, sockets, switches, lighting and equipment
  • Processing, renovation and extension of existing facilities
  • Controls and checks before commissioning
  • Troubleshooting and troubleshooting
  • strict compliance with NIBT standards, safety rules and quality requirements

Your profile

  • Electrician Installer CFC or equivalent
  • Experience confirmed in building electrical installations
  • Very good control of reading electrical drawings and diagrams
  • Good knowledge of existing standards and requirements
  • Autonomous, rigorous and conscientious
  • Good organizational capacity and sense of responsibility
  • Team spirit and good relationship
  • Essential driving licence

What we offer

Active collaboration with a dynamic team, with strong technical know-how and recognized skills. Absolute discretion guaranteed, with an immediate start or to be agreed. Attractive remuneration, in line with your skills and experience. Continuing training opportunities, in order to develop your knowledge and develop professionally. Personalized coaching throughout your mission with a dedicated contact person. Access to a network of recognized companies offering varied opportunities in industry, energy and technology. Prospects for evolution, with real opportunities for fixed engagement in our partner clients. Simplified recruitment process, allowing rapid and effective integration. You're interested in this job?
