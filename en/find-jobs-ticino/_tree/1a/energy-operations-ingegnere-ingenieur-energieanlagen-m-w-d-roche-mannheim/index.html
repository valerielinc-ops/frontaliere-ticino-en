The Position

Your new team

The Energy Supply department is the energy centre of the Roche site in Mannheim and is responsible for the secure, economical and sustainable supply of electricity, heat, cold, compressed air and other media. Together with our external operator, we ensure the availability of the critical energy and media infrastructure for research, production and site operations around the clock. In addition, we are responsible for strategic energy procurement, energy management, the further development of our energy infrastructure and the implementation of decarbonisation and sustainability measures. With innovative projects, we actively shape the energy supply of tomorrow and make a significant contribution to achieving the Roche sustainability goals. You can expect a motivated, interdisciplinary team with varied tasks, a high level of personal responsibility and the opportunity to actively shape the energy future of one of Roche’s largest locations.

Responsibility | This is what awaits you Taken over in the role You are responsible for the safe, economical and sustainable operation as well as the continuous development of the energy and media supply of the Mannheim site. In addition, you will take on the following exciting tasks:

  • Manage operations: You control the external operator technically and operationally and continuously develop the energy infrastructure with regard to security of supply, plant availability and energy efficiency.
  • Manage projects: You plan and coordinate cross-divisional new, renovation or expansion projects (infrastructure and transformation projects) in close cooperation with project teams, construction management and management.
  • Develop concepts & sustainability: You design and implement sustainable energy, sustainability and decarbonization concepts to improve profitability and actively drive CO2 reductions.
  • Responsible for maintenance: You ensure the functionality and availability of the technical equipment in your area of responsibility and recognize potential for optimization with regard to complex technical innovations.
  • Guarantee Compliance & Safety: You ensure full compliance with regulatory requirements as well as internal requirements for occupational safety, quality assurance and environmental protection.
  • Manage Budget & Partners: You are responsible for the needs-oriented budget planning of the technical services, monitor the costs and ensure the professional qualification and performance monitoring of contracted companies.

Qualifications | This is what you bring

  • You have successfully completed a degree in engineering (Dipl.-Ing. TU/TH/FH or Master/Bachelor of Science) in the field of energy engineering, energy process engineering, mechanical engineering or supply engineering or comparable.
  • You bring relevant professional experience in the field of energy technology.
  • You have desirable special knowledge in energy generation plants, especially in the heating, cooling, steam or water supply
  • Ideally, you have already gained experience in project management and convince through entrepreneurial thinking.
  • You are fluent in German and have a good knowledge of English to operate successfully in an international corporate environment.
