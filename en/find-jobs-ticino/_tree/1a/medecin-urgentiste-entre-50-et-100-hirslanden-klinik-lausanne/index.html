Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Clinique Cecile Cecile - Lausanne Occupation by: From 1 January 2027, or to be agreed Type of employment: Permanent

Reference Number: 74428

The Hirslanden Group is Switzerland's largest medical network. We offer all individuals, regardless of their situation, high-level individual medical care to improve their quality of life - from birth to old age and from prevention to cure. As part of our Corporate Together We Care strategy, our ultimate goal is to improve the quality of life of people who trust us.

MAIN ACTIVITY OF THE POST

As part of the development of our activities, we are looking for an emergency doctor to strengthen our team. Your activity will be spread over several sites:

  • the emergency department of our clinics;
  • emergency consultations in a group office in the canton of Vaud;
  • post-emergency consultations and patient follow-up in a practice located near the clinic.

MAIN TASKS

  • Ensure quality management in line with current professional standards
  • Establish a relationship of trust with your patients and support them throughout their care journey
  • Working closely with our network of accredited medical specialists
  • Participate in the establishment, supervision and optimization of the Centre's processes
  • Provide services on a continuous basis, 24 hours a day and 7 days a week, including public holidays, in 10 hours a day, including nights

YOUR PROFILE

  • Certificate of recognition of diplomas (including post-graduation) with the Commission des professions médicales (MEBEKO) indispensable, and registration of post-gradatory diploma compulsory
  • Specialization in general or equivalent internal medicine and in-depth training in hospital emergency medicine (AFC-SSMUS or DESC) desired
  • Charismatic and positive personality, you have leadership that motivates and unites caregivers around the goals set
  • High ability for teamwork and multidisciplinary
  • Dynamics, Quick Adaptation and Flexibility
  • A sense of initiative, autonomy and resilience to stress
  • Anticipation, effectiveness, large organization
  • Accountability
  • Relationship qualities, empathy and ease in communication

WE OFFER YOU

In addition to an attractive workstation in the heart of Lausanne, you will integrate a dynamic team that looks forward to welcoming you into a human-sized structure.

You will benefit from attractive conditions of employment with, among other things, opportunities for continuing training and development throughout your career with us, as well as social benefits and other interesting benefits.

For an optimal balance between work and private life, we offer our employees various contractual models, including part-time and FLEXible contract. There is also the possibility to enjoy up to 7 weeks of holiday thanks to our offer "Congé-Plus".
