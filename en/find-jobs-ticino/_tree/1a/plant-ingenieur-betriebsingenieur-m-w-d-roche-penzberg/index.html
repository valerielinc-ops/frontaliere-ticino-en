Responsibility | This is what awaits you Taken over in the role You are responsible for ensuring the functionality, technical availability and continuous optimization of process and building facilities at our locations. In addition, you will take on the following exciting tasks:

  • Ensure plant availability: You ensure an economical and highly available operation of the buildings and plants through continuous monitoring, reliability studies and error considerations.
  • Manage maintenance: You plan, commission and coordinate efficient maintenance measures and monitor them independently in terms of quality, costs and deadlines.
  • Develop concepts: You develop sustainable service concepts and coordinate appropriate maintenance strategies closely with the operators you maintain in the SAP system.
  • Managing projects: You plan and control building and process engineering measures and take over the (sub)project management for a professional and timely implementation according to the state of the art.
  • Safety & Compliance: You ensure compliance with all laws as well as requirements for occupational safety, environmental protection and quality assurance and assume the delegated plant responsibility.
  • Budget & documentation: You support with technical budget planning, monitor the costs of your measures and ensure a complete, up-to-date CV documentation of the technical facilities.

Qualifications | This is what you bring with you

  • You have successfully completed an engineering degree (Dipl.-Ing. FH / Bachelor / Master) in the field of mechanical engineering, process engineering, supply engineering or a comparable qualification.
  • You bring several years of professional experience in plant construction, in the support of complex technical systems or in building operations.
  • You have in-depth expertise in the maintenance and operation of technical systems as well as ideally experience in the regulated environment (GMP / GEP).
  • You are characterized by a strong economic thinking, like to make decisions based on data and bring a high IT affinity (e.g. SAP) as well as chemical-process engineering knowledge.
  • You speak negotiable English and generally have excellent communication skills. As a team player, you are able to work effectively with different stakeholders and communicate complex relationships in an understandable way.
