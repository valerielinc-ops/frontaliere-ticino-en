Full description

The activity includes setting up and programming CNC machines for the production of aluminium profiles. The company is characterized by short decision-making processes and the opportunity to participate in product development. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • set up and program CNC machines at the control
  • sawing aluminium profiles with straight and miter cuts
  • manufacturing drilling and threading in aluminium
  • planning and controlling production according to customer orders
  • manufacturing new parts and patterns exactly according to drawing
  • Requirements
  • Teaching degree as a polymechanic or polymechanic EFZ or equivalent training
  • Multi-year practice at CNC plants in production
  • Ideally experience in the processing of aluminium
  • Technical drawings safely read and implement
  • Good oral and written basic German language skills Offer
  • Collaboration in the development of new products
  • Small business with short decision-making paths
  • Space for professional further education
