The Position

We are looking for a results-driven Key Account Specialist to join our team. In this high-impact role, you will support and manage designated customer accounts, serving as a vital link between Roche and key healthcare partners. Working closely with cross-functional teams and key account leads, you will execute tactical account plans, strengthen formulary positioning, and drive patient access to our innovative portfolio.

Your Impact

  • Account Execution & Support: Manage day-to-day engagement for assigned customer accounts, ensuring seamless execution of business plans and delivery against access and sales metrics.
  • Formulary & Access Operations: Support formulary positioning and pull-through initiatives to maximize patient access to Roche products and services.
  • Relationship Building: Establish and maintain strong, collaborative relationships with key operational stakeholders, healthcare professionals, and account decision-makers.
  • Tactical Implementation: Translate overall account strategies into practical, day-to-day field activities and targeted engagement strategies.
  • Market Insights: Gather and share account-level insights, competitive dynamics, and market trends to support continuous optimization of account strategies.

Who you are

  • Medical background –Bachelor degree of Pharmacy
  • Industry Experience: 1-2 years’ experience in pharmaceutical/healthcare sales, key account support, or customer-facing commercial roles.
  • Execution Focus: Demonstrated ability to execute commercial plans, track performance metrics, and deliver on sales targets.
  • Relationship & Communication Skills: Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to engage, influence, and build trust with diverse healthcare stakeholders.
  • Account Management Fundamentals: solid understanding of hospital systems, formulary access processes, and customer ecosystem dynamics.
  • Collaborative Mindset: Strong team player capable of working seamlessly with cross-functional teams (Medical, Market Access, Brand Marketing).
  • Excellent Command of English Language
  • Excellent communication skills to facilitate complex information messaging.
  • Entrepreneurial attitude, e.g. challenges the status quo and takes calculated risks
  • Encourage highly motivated and effective teams with a performance-led culture.
  • Ability to prioritize across multiple competing priorities
  • Excellent networking abilities & exceptional ability in working across matrix organizations and leading cross-functional initiatives.
