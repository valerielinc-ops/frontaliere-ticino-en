Full description

At our location in Olten, which focuses on commercial real estate, we address the individual and complex needs of our customers with specialist expertise and negotiating expertise. As a sparring partner at eye level, we focus especially on office and commercial properties in the larger regional catchment area. With targeted and tailor-made marketing strategies, we pursue the ambition to successfully lease any area and at the same time build sustainable, long-term relationships with owners and commercial tenants.

If your heart beats for real estate, you bring a maker mentality and also like to work in a team, then we look forward to your application as

Senior manager commercial real estate (STV site management) (m/f/d)

Olten, 80-100 %

This is what moves you

  • As deputy location manager (Alten and Zug location), you are responsible for the professional management of employees at your location. You recruit, empower and develop your employees
  • In addition, you ensure a perfect communication flow, compliance with processes and specifications as well as performance management
  • As a role model and through active promotion and control, you ensure the quality of service at your location
  • You are responsible for the holistic management of a portfolio of commercial properties, including rental, contract and key management and ensuring proper condition
  • Through your expertise and negotiating skills, you lead successful negotiations and create business leases as well as supplements
  • Communication with the owner, the tenant, authorities and other stakeholders as well as the organization of meetings and tenant orientation are your responsibility.
  • You are responsible for obtaining and evaluating offers, awarding contracts to companies and controlling and releasing creditor orders and invoices
  • As a professional point of contact in the team, you are responsible for the training and guidance of employees
  • The preparation of the annual budget is also within your area of responsibility

This is why you succeed

  • You have a basic commercial education, you are a real estate manager with a federal professional certificate and ideally in possession of the diploma as a real estate trustee
  • You also have several years of professional experience in the management of commercial properties
  • Coaching, motivating and pragmatic work count among your strengths
  • It is easy for you to build, maintain and develop trust with different internal and external steakholders.
  • Digital affinity and in-depth PC and software knowledge are skillfully used daily in working with different systems
  • An efficient and goal-oriented working method as well as good communication skills with negotiating skills qualify you in dealing with different and demanding target groups
  • A high service and customer orientation are a matter of course for you
  • Good manners, high load capacity as well as flexibility and endurance round off your profile
  • You communicate verbally and in writing fluently in German

Here are some of our benefits

  • Working hours: We live mobile work with individual (year) working hours and/or part-time work. Up to 40% home office is possible. Work-life balance is a priority for us, because balanced and relaxed employees are important to us. The purchase of additional holidays is possible.
  • Mobility and food: You will receive a contribution for the mobile phone. For your food, we also make a contribution. commute = working time, if worked on commute this is considered as working time
  • Education and training: Promoting employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support in internal and external education and training. We offer career opportunities in management, specialist and project functions
  • Salär: With us, you can expect market-based remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. The pension benefits are attractive and you can choose the right one for you from different models. The accident protection insurance covers the private hospital department.

More information about our benefits can be found on our career page.
