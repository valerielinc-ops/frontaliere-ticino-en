Specialist Operational storage with support tasks in the med. Procurement of materials

Do you appreciate the familiarity of a small acute care hospital with 56 beds and around 120 employees?

Would you like to actively participate in our care team in a beautiful location and help shape this area? Then we may soon welcome you to Kreuzlingen.

Their tasks

You are part of a small and motivated team and work in a modern surgical operation with a focus on orthopedics, gynecology, surgery and urology. You support surgical storage as a specialist and ensure a professional and smooth surgical procedure. Together with the entire surgical team, you ensure patient safety and operational processes. They are also jointly responsible for the procurement of materials in the operating room and in the central warehouse. They know the upstream and downstream procurement processes and support beyond their own area.

Your profile

You are a socially competent, team-oriented and positioned personality and already have professional experience as a specialist in operations storage. You have in-depth IT knowledge and act economically and solution-oriented. They appreciate the cooperation with documentary doctors in a manageable and personal working environment. They communicate appreciatively, have a winning appearance and like to work across interfaces and think long-term.

Our offer

With us, you will find a varied and demanding task in a family atmosphere with advanced infrastructure and contemporary working conditions. We are committed to a high quality of work and good cooperation.

About us

Klinik Seeschau AG is a modern private clinic in the document doctor system with a cantonal service mandate. Our areas of expertise are in gynecology, orthopaedics, visceral surgery, hand surgery, plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic surgery, urology and anesthesiology. We are a member of the renowned association “SLH, The Swiss Leading Hospitals”, independently and privately managed in the third generation.
