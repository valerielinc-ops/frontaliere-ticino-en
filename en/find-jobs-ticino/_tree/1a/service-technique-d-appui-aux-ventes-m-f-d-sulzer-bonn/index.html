Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

In this position, you will be responsible for the nationwide creation of service and maintenance offers in the segments of municipal drainage and house and property drainage. You will actively support our technical service employees and make an important contribution to the smooth processing of customer inquiries. Your tasks

  • Technical and commercial processing of inquiries and orders in the service environment
  • Creation and development of commercial and technical offers for our customers
  • Determination of delivery times via SAP and in coordination with our suppliers
  • Close communication with international factories and external suppliers
  • Support and collaboration with internal specialist departments to optimally design offers

Your profile

You impress with your commitment, your structured way of working and your strong service orientation. You also have the following qualifications:

  • Completed technical or commercial training
  • Ideally, initial knowledge or experience in technical customer service
  • Strong customer orientation and the ability to keep an overview even in stressful situations
  • Ability to work in a team and have a clear, friendly manner of communication
  • Confident handling of common IT systems and applications (e.g. SAP, Salesforce, MS Office)
  • Good knowledge of English, especially in a technical environment

What we offer you

  • Varied and responsible tasks in a professional working environment
  • Working independently with clear responsibilities
  • Collaboration in a collegial team
  • Compensation in line with the market
  • Secure prospects in a long-term company
  • JobRad leasing
  • Free drinks (coffee & water), fruit basket and discounted lunch menu
