Full description

For a renowned, specialized trust company in the Solothurn region, we are looking for a committed personality as a case manager. With modern digital solutions and personal customer proximity, the company primarily serves customers from the agricultural and regional SME environment in all tax and accounting matters. In a collegial team, you take on varied tasks with a high level of personal responsibility and benefit from flexible framework conditions and targeted further education. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Taxation: Independent preparation of tax returns for natural and legal persons.
  • Customer accounting: cooperation in the management and preparation of customer accounting, including preparation of financial statements.
  • Trust administration: Support for general trust and administration work as well as digital mandate processing.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: Completed basic commercial education (EFZ) or equivalent qualification.
  • Experience: In-depth practical experience in completing tax returns as well as basic knowledge in the escrow or accounting sector.
  • Industry reference: knowledge or affinity for agriculture is a welcome advantage.
  • Working methods & IT: enjoyment of modern digital tools, independent working methods combined with high quality awareness and efficiency.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Culture & Team: Varied and responsible work in a motivated, helpful team.
  • Flexibility: High flexibility in the design of workload (60–100%) and working hours.
  • Development & prevention: Attractive development prospects with targeted support for further education and 5 weeks holidays (from 60 years 6 weeks) in the Solothurn region.
