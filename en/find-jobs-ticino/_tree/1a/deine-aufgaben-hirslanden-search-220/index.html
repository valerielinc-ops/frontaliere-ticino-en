Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Linde | Biel / Bienne

Occupation by 1 September 2026 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 70762

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments.

Clinic Linde is the leading private hospital in the Biel-Seeland-Jura area. With its high medical quality, its selected offers and the supra-regional reputation, it is the first choice for patients and very well-qualified medical doctors.

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional, personnel and organisational management of the Gebärsaal
  • Coordination, organisation and continuous optimisation of workflows and processes
  • Competent and professional cooperation with our doctors: gynecology and pediatrics
  • Cooperation and co-responsibility in cross-divisional issues and projects
  • Management, support and implementation of projects as well as change processes
  • Ensuring and further developing a high quality and service orientation

Your profile

  • Completed training as a qualified midwife FH/HF or equivalent training
  • Higher education in management (e.g. MSc, MAS, MBA) as well as several years of professional and managerial experience
  • Strong business understanding, organizational skills as well as structured and solution-oriented working methods
  • Enjoyment in the further development of employees, processes and quality standards
  • High social skills, communication skills and the ability to motivate and promote employees
  • Experience in interdisciplinary and process-oriented cooperation
  • Very good knowledge of German and/or French in words and writing as well as good knowledge of the other language

Why does it work for us?

  • We work respectfully and respectfully at eye level with each other
  • Possibility of working with flex and leisure plus models
  • Up to 4 additional free days per year as compensation for changing time (pension-dependent)
  • Above-average night and weekend allowance of CHF 10.- per hour
  • Many discounts and perks in the fitness center, over 100 service providers, leisure events and mobile subscriptions

Benefit as a Hirslanden employee: in many more benefits!
