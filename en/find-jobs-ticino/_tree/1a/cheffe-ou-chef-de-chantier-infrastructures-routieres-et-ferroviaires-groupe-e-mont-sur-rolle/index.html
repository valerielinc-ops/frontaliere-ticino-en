Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as maintenance of installations.

Mission

As the construction manager, you organize, supervise and monitor the daily execution of work on road and rail works. You ensure that the facilities are properly completed in accordance with plans, defined methods, safety rules and deadlines.

Your responsibilities

  • Prepare, plan, organize, perform and control the work, from the start of the project until its final delivery.
  • Lead, coach and motivate assembly teams, ensuring optimal division of tasks.
  • Check the conformity of work (lighting, ventilation, signalling, wiring, safety equipment, etc.) according to current standards.
  • Communicate, document and report project changes for review files.
  • Provides connections, commissioning of facilities, testing and storage.
  • Monitor the evolution of construction site costs, analyze the results with your manager and manage the management vouchers.
  • Strictly apply safety rules, QSE standards and road and rail environment directives.

Profile

  • TECHNOLOGY TECHNOLOGIES
  • Confirmed practical experience on roadworks, tunnels or railway infrastructure: major asset.
  • Strong technical skills, mastery of execution methods and understanding of field constraints.
  • Leadership affirmed, rigour, responsiveness and ability to meet deadlines while ensuring quality and safety.
  • Clear communication, sense of inter-industry collaboration and ability to represent the entity in construction sessions.
  • Careful, organized, flexible and exemplary person in the application of internal guidelines and values.
  • Driver's license.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success"
