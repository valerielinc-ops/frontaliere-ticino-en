Start your paid adult apprenticeship now as a network electrician: in EFZ 100% ## This is what you can do:
  • As a network electrician, you and your colleagues carry out all maintenance, renewal work and troubleshooting on overhead line systems.
  • During the course of your training you will learn to operate the aerial work platform.
  • Under the supervision of an expert, you carry out switching operations in the contact line network. After your apprenticeship, you will be authorized to use the SBB Infrastructure network.
  • You are willing to work at irregular hours, especially at night and sometimes on weekends. You can organize your free time more flexibly during the week. Night and weekend work is paid additionally. You also benefit from time bonuses. This means you have more free time than employees without night and weekend shifts. ## This is what you bring with you:
  • You are at least 25 years old and would like to learn a new profession.
  • You are technically skilled and ideally have practical experience in catenary, overhead line or metal construction.
  • You like being outside in wind and weather.
  • Network electricians can rely on each other because team spirit is just as important as safety regulations.
  • You have good eyesight and hearing and no color vision impairment ## This is what you can look forward to: As a network electrician, you ensure that the electricity flows every day so that our customers reach their destination on time and safely. You work with your own physical strength or let the most modern machines do the work for you. Your professional experience is worth something. The more relevant professional experience you have, the higher your salary will be during and after your apprenticeship: in the first year of your apprenticeship your salary will be 80%, in the second year of your apprenticeship it will be 90% and in the third year of your apprenticeship it will be 95% of your calculated salary after completing your apprenticeship. We would be happy to make you a provisional salary suggestion during the initial consultation. You will complete your three-year training in a contact management team at SBB Infrastructure, and you will be employed by login, our partner for vocational training. You will be accompanied by a qualified vocational trainer. At the same time, you attend vocational school. Interested?
