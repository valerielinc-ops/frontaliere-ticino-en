Marketing & Sales Support Assistant — Pictet Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your team

Founded in Geneva in 1805, the Pictet Group is now one of the main independent players in wealth management and asset management in Europe, with funds under management and in deposits exceeding EUR 878 billion at the end of June 2026. It is run by seven partners, who are both owners and managers.

Pictet Asset Management (Pictet AM) is dedicated to managing assets on behalf of third parties through the management of mandates and investment funds. Present in 18 financial centres around the world and employing more than 1100 employees, Pictet AM has been working to deliver an excellent service to a leading professional and institutional clientele since more than 1980.

This internship will allow you to acquire an overall vision of the ecosystem of investment funds, to know the different investment strategies of Pictet AM, to have a good understanding of the current economics, to develop a rigorous business methodology and to develop your marketing creativity.

Your role

Within the marketing and marketing team of Pictet AM, based in Paris, you will assist the team as follows:

Marketing and customer service (40%)

  • Preparation of management point summaries on Pictet funds.
  • Dissemination and programming of communications on the investment funds and the macroeconomic views of Pictet AM.
  • Creation and updating of marketing documents (product presentation, banner, brochure, documentation).
  • Creation of emailing campaigns.
  • Organization and logistics during customer events.
  • Production of audio & video content (recording, editing).
  • Maintenance and maintenance of the client database.

Commercial support (30%)

  • Support of the commercial team in the maintenance of the customer relationship (wholesale customers, institutional, retail) in the process of selling Pictet funds (emerging, thematic, bond, alternative and ETF funds).
  • Preparation of sales media (PowerPoint, e-mails, newsletters).
  • Response to client questionnaires and requests.
  • Writing of summaries in French of technical content received from management teams.
  • Keep an eye on the news of some management expertise.

Assistance to the Investment Advisor (30%)

  • Contribution to the analysis and drafting of macroeconomic issues.
  • Analysis of statistics.
  • Support the strategist on various requests for analysis, including the production of graphics, visuals and presentation materials.

Your profile

We are looking for a person interested in financial markets, with very good relational qualities, who is bilingual in English, who knows how to adapt to his environment, who is flexible, autonomous and curious, who has the spirit of synthesis and who knows how to manage different files simultaneously while respecting deadlines. An experience with a CRM system would be a plus.

REF: MSSA/SGV/MH

Start date: January 1, 2027
