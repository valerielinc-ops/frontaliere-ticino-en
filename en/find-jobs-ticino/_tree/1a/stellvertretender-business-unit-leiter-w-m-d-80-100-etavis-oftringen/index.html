Full description

Electric projects are your passion and you are ready to take on more responsibility? At ETAVIS, as a deputy, you actively shape the business unit in Oftringen. As Deputy Business Unit Manager: at ETAVIS you are the right hand of the head of our business unit in Oftringen. You are responsible for your own area and represent the Business Unit Manager in case of absences.

  • You manage several projects in parallel and are the first point of contact for builders and GUs, from planning to handover.
  • You maintain existing customer relationships, actively gain new customers and lead the negotiations until the conclusion.
  • You plan the deployment of your installers and project teams and coordinate resources across multiple projects.
  • You accompany your team professionally and support them in their further development. This will make it easier for you to get started.
  • Expertise according to NIV (Eidg. Dipl. Electrical installation and safety expert / expert / practical examination)
  • Several years of professional experience as a project manager: with proven management experience, ideally in the customer segment industry and public buildings
  • Clear communication, sales-oriented and winning appearance and team spirit
  • Good market knowledge, ideally with relationship network in the Greater Region of Mittelland
  • Responsibility and interest in strategically and innovatively shaping the business unit This is what awaits you
  • Great personal responsibility and real design possibilities
  • A corporate culture built on respect, trust and collaboration
  • Experienced team and a noticeable team spirit
  • Stability and future thanks to the strength of the ETAVIS Group
  • Modern employment conditions with attractive salary, business vehicle and lucrative profit participation Still questions?
