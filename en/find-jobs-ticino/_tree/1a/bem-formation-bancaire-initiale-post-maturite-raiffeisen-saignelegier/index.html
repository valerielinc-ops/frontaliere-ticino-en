14.10.2026 Bleeding

Be part of our team!

The "BEM - Initial post-maturity banking training" course at the Banque Raiffeisen Franches-Montagnes allows you, who are mature (professional or specialized) or student of a business school, to start your professional career in an ideal setting.

What are you waiting for?

Looking for a well-established training company locally? Do you like contact with customers and want to practice your knowledge of banking? Then you may soon join our team! During your 24-month training, supervised by experienced and motivated trainers, you will discover different services and become familiar with the concrete daily life of a Raiffeisen Bank. In addition, you will study banking theory and techniques at CYP (Challenge Your Potential) in Lausanne.

What are you bringing?

  • specialized maturity or SWC trade employee with professional maturity;
  • analytical and process-oriented thinking;
  • autonomy and responsibility;
  • strong willingness to learn and great commitment capacity;
  • ability to work as a team and communicate.

Start your professional career in an ideal setting and opt for an exciting and motivating banking internship! Carine Beuret Member of the Bank's management Why Raiffeisen?

We work in a friendly environment

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.

We live in diversity and equal opportunities

We encourage diversity of perspectives, experiences and skills within our teams. This diversity makes us rich and makes a decisive contribution to our success.

We encourage personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is an integral part of our culture. We create a space for the professional and personal development of each individual and support our employees in a targeted manner, through a wide range of in-house training and participation in external development costs.

We offer attractive social conditions and benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages over banking products, various discounts from third parties, as well as above average financial stability and old age insurance.
