Dermatology and aesthetic medicine under one roof, in one of the leading private clinic groups in Switzerland. In Lucerne, we are looking for a specialist in dermatology in the range of 60 – 100% by appointment. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They take care of dermatological patients independently in the ambulatory setting

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist titles in Dermatology and Venerology
  • Completed specialist training
  • Recognition for Switzerland for foreign diplomas. Provided at least three years of work at a recognized Swiss training institution
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

That sets them apart.

  • Strong social and communication skills in dealing with patients, referees and colleagues
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary collaboration in a growing team

  • Very modern infrastructure
  • Possibility for use at several locations in German-speaking Switzerland
