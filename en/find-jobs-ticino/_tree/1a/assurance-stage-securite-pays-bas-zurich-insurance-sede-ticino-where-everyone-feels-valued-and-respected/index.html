Full description

Your career in insurance starts here

Are you a recent graduate or a young professional looking to build a meaningful career in Surety Underwriting ? Zurich Insurance Benelux invites you to join our Insurance Traineeship (Surety) program: a 16‑month program designed to help you grow into a future insurance professional through hands‑on experience, learning on the job, and close collaboration with various teams and departments. As insurance trainee at Zurich Benelux, you will become part of the Surety home team while also getting the chance to rotate to 2 other host teams (e.g. Claims, Sales etc) to gain a better understanding of our business.

This program offers a strong focus on practical learning and deep integration with our business, allowing you to build expertise while really making an impact.

About Zurich Insurance Benelux

Zurich Insurance Benelux is part of the Zurich Insurance Group, and a leading Commercial insurer supporting local and international businesses. We are in the business of helping customers manage risk and build resilience in an ever‑changing world. Zurich employs over 60,000 people globally, creating a space where you can develop professionally, push your limits, and contribute in meaningful ways. Your perspectives are valued at Zurich. We support curiosity and empower you to be the CEO of your own career.

As trainee in Surety Underwriting , you are involved in both the guarantee underwriting & issuance as well as the client underwriting. Our Surety team is unique within Zurich as we issue bonds (bank guarantees) rather than traditional insurance, supporting mid-size and large corporates across sectors. Client underwriting is related to assessing the financial standing and performance of our clients by analysing their financial information, including balance sheets, cash flow and financing. In this role, you work closely with CFOs and other senior finance decision-makers.

The traineeship program at a glance

  • 16‑month structured program (via an open-end contract)
  • Start in September 2026
  • Fully based out of our Dutch office in The Hague
  • Combination of Surety home team and host team rotations
  • Preparation for a long-term (junior) role within Zurich Benelux

Program structure

  • Home team (6 months)

You start in Surety Underwriting as Home Team where you take on core responsibilities and build a solid foundation. This is also the team you will return to after completing the program.

  • 2 host team rotations of 5 months each

The host team rotations are designed to broaden your perspective by exposing you to different sides of our business. You will work on hands‑on, value‑adding assignments, develop new skills, and gain deeper business insight.

  • January 2028

You return to your Surety home team, ready to start in a junior role with a strong foundation of experience.

You bring

  • Bachelor or Master degree (preferably in Economics, Finance, Accounting, Business Law or equivalent) and 0–2 years of work experience
  • Fluency in Dutch and English (non‑negotiable)
  • You reside in the Netherlands (and available to start in September 2026)
  • A collaborative mindset and willingness to learn
  • Interest in insurance, finance, and personal development

What we offer

  • A structured development program with real responsibilities
  • On‑the‑job coaching and mentoring by experienced colleagues
  • Exposure to different teams and business perspectives
  • Training opportunities to support your professional growth
  • A supportive and inclusive work environment
  • A strong starting point for a long‑term career within Zurich Benelux
