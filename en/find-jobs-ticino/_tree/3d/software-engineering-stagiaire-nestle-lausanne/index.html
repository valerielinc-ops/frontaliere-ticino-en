Full description

Location: Lausanne, Switzerland Work model: On-site Contract type / duration: Internship, 6 months Start date: Between February and June 2027

Be a force for good and transform your potential into something extraordinary.

As a Software Engineering Intern within the Proteins and Carbohydrates Analytics Department at Nestlé Research , your mission will be to develop a tailored software solution for the analysis of complex mass spectrometry data.

You will work closely with scientists and laboratory colleagues to understand their analytical needs and translate them into a practical, user-friendly tool. The solution will support the identification and quantification of target proteins and their associated proteoforms, while generating clear and comprehensive reports for R&D teams.

This is an opportunity to apply your programming skills to a meaningful scientific challenge, gain exposure to advanced analytical technologies, and help developing a solution that can directly support research activities across Nestlé.

How you’ll make an impact as a Software Engineering Intern

  • Understand the needs and workflows of scientists working on intact protein analytics
  • Translate scientific and user requirements into a tailored software solution
  • Help develop a tool to process and analyse complex high-resolution mass spectrometry data
  • Support the identification and quantification of target proteins and their associated proteoforms
  • Create clear visual outputs and comprehensive reports for R&D users
  • Collaborate closely with scientists and laboratory colleagues to collect feedback
  • Test, refine and improve the software solution to ensure it is reliable and fit for purpose

What will make you successful

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s student, or recent graduate, in Computer Science, Data Science, Bioinformatics or a related field, with demonstrated programming skills, particularly in Python or R
  • Ability to translate data-related challenges into functional software solutions, identify patterns, and draw meaningful conclusions from complex information
  • Curious and analytical mindset, with strong problem-solving capabilities, autonomy, attention to detail and a proactive approach to progressing a project
  • Ability to understand users’ needs and collaborate effectively with scientists and technical specialists, and interest in applying software engineering to scientific and analytical challenges
  • Fluency in English; knowledge of French would be an advantage

At Nestlé Research, science and technology come together to create meaningful impact.

You will join a collaborative scientific environment combining fundamental research, advanced analytical platforms and strong prototyping capabilities. Working alongside experts in protein analytics and proteomics, you will have the opportunity to develop your technical skills while contributing to a software solution with practical applications for Nestlé R&D.

Our benefits are designed to support your growth and wellbeing, so you can thrive at work and in life:

  • Learning and development opportunities to grow your career
  • Exposure to advanced scientific and analytical technologies
  • Collaboration with experienced scientists and technical specialists
  • Healthcare contribution and access to the Employee Assistance Program
  • Attractive employee discounts across various products and services
