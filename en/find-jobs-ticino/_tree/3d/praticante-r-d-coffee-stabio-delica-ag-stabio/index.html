Full description

In Delica we invent the pleasure of tomorrow together. During this 12-month course you will enter the heart of Research & Development Coffee, contributing to concrete projects of innovation, packaging and raw materials. A unique opportunity to learn, grow and leave your mark in a team that enhances collaboration, Swiss quality and sustainability.

Workplace: Delica AG, Via Baragge, 6855 Stabio

Important information

Every application we receive makes us very pleased, but we can only consider those online. Mail applications will be deleted for data protection purposes and will not be returned.

Shipments

  • You will support R&D Project Managers in developing new products and improving existing products.
  • You will prepare and perform laboratory tests, measurements and controls according to defined procedures.
  • You will participate in industrial trials and collect, process and interpret experimental data.
  • You will provide technical reports, presentations and project documentation, contributing to the development of research and development activities.
  • You will work with Production, Quality and Product Management to ensure the success of your projects.

Requirements

  • Up to 3 years: professional experience, internships or project in laboratory, quality, production or product development.
  • Bachelor's degree (university/university school): •Technical diploma or vocational/university training (SUP/FH, HFP or equivalent) in Chemistry, Food Sciences, Packaging, Materials or related disciplines.
  • You are a proactive, collaborative and flexible person, able to communicate openly and professionally in a dynamic environment.
  • You stand out for analytical skills, precision, organization and attention to detail.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office
  • Italian (Great Level)
  • English (Great Level)
  • German (in preference)
  • French (in preference)
