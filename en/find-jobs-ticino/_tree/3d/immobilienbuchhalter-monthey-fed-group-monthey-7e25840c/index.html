Real Estate Accountant \- Monthey

Do you have experience in real estate accounting and want to join a dynamic and stimulating environment?

On behalf of our client, a recognized real estate player in Valais, we are looking for a real estate accountant to strengthen his team based in Monthey. Take over as soon as possible.

Your responsibilities

\- Independent management of the accounting of a stewardship portfolio of buildings. \- Processing of current invoices, payments and accounting operations. \- Preparation of accounts for management, heating and ancillary costs. \- Monitoring of tenant accounts and owner cash. Participation in quarterly and annual closures. \- Accounting management of takeovers and mandate purposes.

Your profile

\- Experience from 4 to 5 years minimum in real estate accounting. \- Accounting training, federal patent an asset. \- Good control of computer tools; knowledge of Quorum appreciated. \- Rigorous, organized personality with a good analytical spirit. \- Self-reliance and sense of teamwork.

We offer you

\- A varied position within a recognized and well-established structure. \- A dynamic and collaborative professional environment. \- 5 weeks of vacation and flexible schedules. \- Fast start.
