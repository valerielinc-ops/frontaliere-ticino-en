Full description

As part of an eight-month replacement, you are responsible for the audiovisual production of the HUGs. You take charge of the entire process, from design to distribution, in order to guarantee the continuity and quality of the video content of the institution. You inform the internal and external audiences about the activities of the HUGs by producing appropriate and engaging audiovisual media. You design and implement the various audiovisual productions, whether they are:

  • TV pulsations
  • institutional or promotional videos
  • educational content
  • formats for social networks
  • gatherings of events and conferences You will also ensure the editorial management of projects, coordinate external providers, collaborate with internal services and stakeholders involved in filming, follow the production steps, validate deliverables and ensure budget compliance. In parallel, you advise the medical and institutional services of the HUGs on their audiovisual needs and accompany them in defining their projects.
