Full description

Lucerne Psychiatry AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and is represented with its offers at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professional groups and departments are humanely and competently committed to the well-being of patients. The offers are aimed at children, adolescents and adults up to old age. Assistant doctor (m/f/d) 60 - 100%

Their tasks

  • Indication, diagnosis and implementation of psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment in "Home Treatment" under medical supervision
  • Participation in reports and case meetings
  • Preparation of reports for the cooperation partners (doctors, psychologists, insurance companies, offices and authorities)
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation in the treatment team as well as active cooperation with external institutions and the patient environment

Your profile

  • Completed medical studies (MEBEKO recognition mandatory)
  • Advanced psychotherapeutic training
  • Mind. 1-2 years of professional experience in psychiatry
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Flexible, team-capable and organized personality with independent and efficient working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German

Benefits

  • Appreciative and benevolent working environment
  • Collegial, interdisciplinary team
  • Mind. 5 weeks holidays, loyalty day and unpaid holidays possible
  • Attractive internal and external training and supervision
  • Free Use of Nextbike
  • Special family allowance for parents
  • Case-related cooperation with child and adolescent psychiatry
  • If interested, expert opinions are possible
