Full description

Start your career as a specialist in the field of fruits and vegetables with our 6-12-month junior programme – practical, with a full salary and a clear leadership focus. Learn all about fresh products, perfect presentation and team leadership. Bring your passion for food, organizational talent and responsibility and become an expert in your field!

Mansioni

  • You ensure freshness, quality and availability of products
  • With a focus on customers, you lead the department
  • As a leader, you promote your team in a targeted manner
  • You act entrepreneurially and keep the goals in mind
  • You ensure efficient and smooth processes

Requirements

  • Ideally more than 2 years of professional experience: in the retail trade specialty food
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade or equivalent training
  • Customer-oriented personality with pleasure in sales
  • Well-founded PC skills that make your day-to-day work easier
  • The willingness to work flexibly, even at different times
  • Good physical fitness to manage daily tasks efficiently
  • German (very good knowledge)
