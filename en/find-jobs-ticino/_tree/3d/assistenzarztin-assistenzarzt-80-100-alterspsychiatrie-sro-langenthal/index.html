Full description

Printing place Post as PDF The psychiatric services of SRO AG provide psychiatric care for around 80,000 inhabitants with modern, integrated outpatient, semi-stationary and inpatient structures. Within this network, the age psychiatric services offer comprehensive diagnostics and therapy for people aged 65 and over. These include outpatient consultation hours, a day clinic as well as consiliary support for the Langenthal Hospital and regional retirement and nursing homes. Our integrated Memory Clinic works closely interdisciplinary and is officially certified with the Swiss Memory Clinics quality label. Our department is recognized as a SIWF training center for the focus on old-age psychiatry and psychotherapy (category B, 1 year outpatient - also eligible as an additional year for the specialist title of psychiatry and psychotherapy). In addition, the psychiatric services of SRO AG have a total of category A for 3 years on an outpatient basis and 3 years in adult psychiatry. To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement one/one

They do it with us.

  • They carry out integrative age psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatments in the outpatient and daily clinical setting.
  • You work interdisciplinary in our certified Memory Clinic in the neuropsychiatric clarification of brain performance disorders as well as behavioral or personality changes.
  • If you are interested, you will be involved in the treatment of treatment-resistant depression (esketamine treatment in particular) and will conduct home visits to regional institutions.
  • They work closely with specialist nurses, neuropsychology, the internal social service, family doctors, Spitex and regional retirement and nursing homes.

This is what they bring with them.

  • You have a Swiss or MEBEKO-approved medical diploma and are in further training for psychiatry and psychotherapy or for the focus on old-age psychiatry and psychotherapy.
  • The position is also expressly suitable as a foreign year for the focus on geriatrics, general internal medicine and for (re)entry students from other disciplines.
  • They bring personal responsibility as well as the willingness to cooperate across sectors and to participate in the Pikettdienst.
  • They have a very good knowledge of German in word and writing (at least level C1).
