Capo del servizio del personale a tempo pieno (100%) presso l’Istituto delle assicurazioni sociali (IAS) / servizi centrali, servizio del personale, Bellinzona — Amministrazione Cantonale Ticino

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • manages, coordinates and controls the activities of the IAS Personal Service by consolidating and optimizing existing activities in human resource management
  • plan and manage human resources in accordance with IAS principles and strategies and in accordance with the order of state personnel
  • develops the human resource management model according to the guidelines of the Directorate
  • guarantees the consistency, effectiveness and sustainability of the processes of human resource management and the IT tools available
  • coadiuva the IAS Directorate in the creation of new synergies with the Central Services of the staff of the cantonal administration
  • check and verify the activity of the Service employees
  • determines key processes, puts them in place and presides over their implementation in an integrated and strategic way
  • cooperates with the Institute's Management and Executive FDs in current management and complex cases
  • updates on the legislative, legal and practice changes in force at the cantonal administration

Requirements

  • bachelor in Economics, Law or Psychology or Training of Specialist in Staff Management with Federal Professional Certificate
  • master in Advances Studies in Human Capital Management is a preferred title
  • proven and multiannual experience as responsible for human resources in medium and large companies
  • consolidated experience in the management of groups or services
  • good knowledge of the MS Office package
  • independence, sense of responsibility, initiative, reliability and precision
  • decision-making, editorial, relational skills and group work
  • predisposition to change, good stress management and flexibility
  • excellent knowledge of the Italian language (conversation and editing) and good knowledge (conversation and editing) of one of the official languages (French or German)

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required additional documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

16 October 2026
