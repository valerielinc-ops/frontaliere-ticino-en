Mission Your future playground You will join the shop in Bussigny, opened in 2018, made up of a team of 60 collaborators. Your ski area is at the heart of our local strategy. The team is composed of 8 enthusiasts and realizes a total CA of 6,000,000 CHF . And now, you're looking for a dynamic job where no day looks alike? Do you want to share your passion for sport and offer an unforgettable experience to every customer who crosses the store doors? Do you like action, terrain and teamwork? So the Sales Advisor position is for you. Why?

  • You're at the heart of the action. Your daily life is 100% in the field. You're Decathlon's face. Whether it's welcoming a beginner or advising an expert, you share your passion and create the link.
  • You're the actor in tomorrow's business. You're not just selling a product on the shelf. You offer an omni-channel and lasting experience: digital orders, hardware rental, take-over of old products (Second Life)... You answer every need.
  • You have autonomy and impact. You guarantee your sport! You make sure your department makes you want, you manage your stock and you set up your business operations. Your efficiency can be seen directly on the satisfaction of your customers. What You Will Do Every Day: Your Missions
  • Advice and omnichannel sale: You welcome your customers as guests. You advise them, you conclude sales (in physics or through our digital tools), you propose our loyalty program and you manage the cash flow.
  • Commercial Dynamism / Merchandising: You install the products according to the seasons and logical implementation, you manage the "facing" so that your radius is always impeccable, and you highlight the best sales and new products of the season.
  • Availability and logistics: Put your hand to the dough! From receipt of the delivery to dispatch, you guarantee the accuracy of your stocks and the rapid availability of your products. You're also preparing web orders.
  • Circular economy and services: You are an ambassador of our new models: you manage the purchase of used equipment and you push our rental solutions to extend the life of our products.
  • Specific missions: Depending on your desires and development, you will be able to take reference missions on a specific topic, become a trainer for your colleagues, or even attend the shop's permanence. This job isn't for you if...
  • You'd rather sit behind a screen all day in a quiet place.
  • You don't like to go spontaneously to others.
  • You think unpacking pallets, tidying a radius and dealing with merchandise "that's not your problem."
  • You're not ready to see 10,000 steps on your watch every night.
  • You are looking for fixed and routine schedules: here we live in the rhythm of sport and our schedules adapt to the activity
  • You don't want to work on Saturdays. Profile What we expect from you:
  • You have an excellent relationship. Customer satisfaction is your compass.
  • You're audacious, curious, and you always want to learn.
  • You're organized, efficient, and you know how to manage your priorities in a fast moving environment.
  • You're passionate about sports: company DNA!
  • You have perfect command of French. Proficiency in English/German is a plus. What we offer:
  • A flexible and secure hourly contract: As its name indicates, you are paid on time. In other words, the more you work, the higher your salary! To bring you security, we guarantee you no matter what happens a minimum per month (rate agreed in the contract and which varies between 20% and 50%). During peaks of activity, we can offer you extra hours. Don't panic about your organization: we will always warn you at least 48 hours in advance, and it will only be done if your availability allows you!
  • Hourly wage: The salary is CHF 2 5 gross per hour. In addition to this are allowances for holidays, holidays and the 13th (if you are on CDI hourly contract). Every 15 of the month, you will be paid according to the hours worked the previous month (between the 16th of the previous month and the 15th of the current month).
  • Shareholding: Once a year, you can become a shareholder of the company. This is an excellent investment since the value of the share has never been negative since the creation of Decathlon 50 years ago! (CDI only)
  • Passion sport : We support your practice! Benefit from 25% discount on your Decathlon purchases after 1 month of seniority
  • Continuing training: You take part in training to boost your technical knowledge about products, sales techniques or interpersonal communication. Your career: in our home, evolution is not a myth Do you join us in store? You start as a seller? You can evolve as Rayon Manager, take over as Operations Manager, until you become Store Manager! EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
