Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Put your managerial ambitions at the service of sound and sustainable performance by joining the canton of Vaud's first universal bank, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Strategy and Organization department, we are looking for one or more:

Expert adviser or expert adviser - Real Estate Clientele Your main tasks:

  • manage and develop its portfolio credits for the Real Estate Client,
  • prospecting, using and developing its customer network in order to acquire a new customer base,
  • apply value proposals (quality of service, number of visits, operational efficiency) and optimize cross selling,
  • develop financing solutions for clients in collaboration with credit analysts and real estate experts, get involved in depth within the business community with real estate orientation. Your profile:
  • higher banking training or higher education (HES or HEC, etc.)
  • minimum 5 years of customer experience in SME, Private Banking or Fortune Management clients or in the area of financing to investors and real estate developers,
  • knowledge of the Vaudian real estate market and its actors,
  • proactivity, service sense, results orientation, excellent communication and negotiation capabilities, risk control.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let us write together three stories, one of your career, one of your bank, one of our region.
