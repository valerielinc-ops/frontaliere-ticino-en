Shape your future with us!

Do you live in the Neuchâtel area and have you ever been able to practice your sales, negotiation and networking skills? Your interest in the field of foresight is strong and you may have professional experience in this sector at your disposal? Are you a customer-oriented and objective person and have a strong professional commitment?

We have an opportunity to train you regularly, develop your skills and demonstrate your abilities!

Our Neuchâtel agency wants to strengthen its team and we would be delighted to receive your file!

What's waiting for you

  • Personalized and oriented advice on individual and collective foresight solutions
  • Mortgage and investment consultancy
  • Loyalty of your customers and development of your own customer portfolio
  • Acquisition of new customers to ensure the growth of your region's client portfolio
  • Placement of other AXA products (non-life, health)
  • Perform administrative tasks related to your position, with the support of the entire AXA structure and modern monitoring tools

What you bring

  • With a professional degree, you have first experience in the service sector (advice and sales), experience in the field of personal insurance is welcome
  • Your personality is service oriented and you have ease in contacts with others, you seek solutions for your customers and you know how to adapt to any situation
  • You speak French both orally and in writing, any other language is an advantage
  • Your method of work is autonomous and oriented towards the objectives
  • Your home must be in the area of activity concerned and you have a very good knowledge of the local economic fabric and a wide network in the region.
  • You have ease with common computer systems as well as learning new digital tools

What we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you the opportunity to develop and fully exploit your potential. As a Swiss insurance leader, we offer ambitious people interesting career opportunities with great freedom of action.

  • Up to 30% discount on products from AXA
  • Five weeks of vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half rate/participation at general subscription costs
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Freedom of action and self-employment
  • Innovative structures, processes and tools
  • Strong and professional training and development opportunities
