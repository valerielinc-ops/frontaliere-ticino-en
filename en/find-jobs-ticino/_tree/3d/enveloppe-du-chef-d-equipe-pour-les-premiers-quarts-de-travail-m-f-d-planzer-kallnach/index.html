Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Marti.

As a firmly rooted company in the Seeland region, we offer comprehensive transport and storage solutions and ensure cleanliness in over 30 communities with our waste transports. Our success is based on motivated employees and modern infrastructure. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

Tasks

  • You lead a team of 7-12 employees and motivate them to do their best every day
  • You check and check the delivered goods carefully and make sure that everything is correct
  • You sort packages efficiently and carefully according to specified processes
  • Correct scanning of the shipments in the system and ensuring a seamless recording
  • You ensure that the goods are loaded safely and quickly onto the right vehicle. Chaos? Not with you!
  • The goods you move mainly with floor conveyors from A to B
  • You are responsible for a smooth process and a clean and orderly goods handling

Profile

  • Working at night is your thing? Perfect! (working hours from 01:00-09:00)
  • You are already a proud owner of a forklift card
  • In German you are saddlefest - verbally as in writing
  • As a team-oriented personality, you bring your first leadership experience or feel like taking on responsibility

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • We keep up with the times - you have modern and safe work equipment at your disposal
  • Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
