Technical Collaborator En/All 8

If you wish to actively contribute to the processing of construction files, to collaborate with the municipal and cantonal authorities and to play a key role in the application of legislation in the field of spatial planning, then don't wait to apply!

On behalf of the State of Valais, we are looking for a

Responsibilities

  • Analyse and investigate technical files related to construction and spatial planning projects
  • Assess project compliance with applicable legislation and regulations
  • Prepare the necessary advance notice, technical reports and draft decisions for the processing of cases
  • Ensure administrative and technical follow-up of files throughout their processing cycle
  • Conduct site visits and monitor the correct application of permits issued
  • Support and advise the various partners on technical and regulatory aspects
  • Participate in coordination sessions and contribute to exchanges between the various bodies concerned
  • Collaborate in the development of a harmonious territory by ensuring compliance with existing standards and procedures

Profile

  • Hold a Graduate of Architect EPF/HES, a Federal Diploma of Director of Works (Building) or a CFC of draftsman in architecture; a certificate of safety training for buildings is an asset
  • Experience confirmed in the processing of construction authorization files, architecture, construction management or site monitoring
  • Have strong knowledge of the legislation applicable to constructions outside the building area and an excellent capacity to analyse technical records
  • Be able to accurately control and interpret construction and execution plans
  • Completely master French (level C2\) and have good knowledge of German (minimum B1\)
  • Demonstrate excellent communication capabilities and a real ease in working with diverse interlocutors
  • Mastering common IT tools, including Microsoft Office; knowledge of SAP is an asset
  • Demonstrate autonomy, rigour, organization and a solution-oriented approach

Benefits

  • Attractive employment conditions conducive to professional development and balance between private and professional life
  • Various field missions, technical analysis, administrative coordination and collaboration with many partners
  • An activity offering autonomy, responsibilities and participation in projects with a concrete impact on spatial planning
  • A stable and stimulating working environment within a public administration serving the territory of Valais
  • Entry into office: to be agreed
  • CDD of a minimum duration of 4 years
