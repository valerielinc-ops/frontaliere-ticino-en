Team Leader Strategic Facilities Management Access to mobility Apply » Date: 5 Nov 2026

Place

Bern, CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. As a team leader, Strategic facility management for the "Access to mobility" division, you and your team are shaping the future of public facilities and low voltage installations, and making an important contribution to a customer-oriented, efficient and sustainable railway infrastructure. In doing so, you will guide your team during the next development phase from an organizational and specialized perspective. As a member of the "Strategic Facilities Management" management team, you are involved in the development of strategic facility management and ensure effective coordination of the strategy, funding and maintenance of network quality. To find out what benefits we offer you, such as children's allowances, free first class AG or travel abroad at a reduced price (also for your family), visit our website under Social benefits. What you can move.

  • Within the new organizational unit, you lead and develop the team "Strategic Facilities Management Access to Mobility" by strengthening intersectoral collaboration.
  • In this role, you are responding to the strategic development of facility management for all reception facilities and low voltage consumers of SBB. You ensure that our customers have a high-performance, safe and sustainable infrastructure when they train.
  • You ensure the long-term financial viability of maintenance and renewal. Your team is responsible for the technical development of the "Access to Mobility" offer for the benefit agreement with the OFT.
  • You are moving forward from operational management to more strategic management and fostering a common vision within the team and its partner organizations.
  • With your team, you ensure the implementation of the facility strategy by coordinating strategic direction with facility managers in the four regions (West, South, Central and East) and working closely with project portfolio management.
  • You ensure that the installation strategy, performance, customer utility and profitability are in a sustainable way.

What you bring.

  • You have several years of driving experience and have successfully developed teams, ideally also in situations of change or integration.
  • You quickly analyse complex technical, organizational and financial issues and draw from them pragmatic and strategic decisions.
  • Thanks to your strong stakeholder management, you are convincing, you negotiate equally, and you manage to have different interests converge to achieve a common vision. It is easy for you to pass on your knowledge and experiences.
  • You have knowledge of the strategic management of facilities in the infrastructure environment. Experience in public transport or the railway environment is an asset.
  • You have a graduate degree (HES/EPF/university) in a technical or economic field, ideally complemented by continuing training in the field of driving, management or asset management.
  • You have very good knowledge of German, both oral and written. Knowledge in a second national language (French/Italian) is an asset. You can use your digital skills (informatics, MS Office and tools) daily.
