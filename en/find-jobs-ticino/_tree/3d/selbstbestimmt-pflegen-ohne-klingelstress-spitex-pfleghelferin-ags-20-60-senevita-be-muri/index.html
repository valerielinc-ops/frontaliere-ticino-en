Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is one of the leading providers of private Spitex services in Switzerland.

The Spitex Senevita Casa Emmental – Oberaargau offers you

Spitex - caregiver/AGS 20 - 60 % Your important contribution

  • You can focus on one person. With us, you care for one person after another – without the bell calling in two other rooms at the same time.
  • You build personal relationships. In the reference care you get to know our clients, their habits and needs and design the care individually.
  • You are self-employed in Burgdorf and the surrounding area on Spitex tour. You take responsibility, have freedom in the design of work and get fresh air between your assignments.
  • You are part of a motivated team. We exchange experiences and support each other.
  • You use your expertise in primary care and care where it helps directly: with people who can live as independently as possible at home thanks to your support.
  • You bring your observations and ideas in exchange with the case management and actively shape the care process.
  • You document your care carefully and independently and thus create a reliable basis for further care.

Here you enrich our team

  • You have completed training as a nursing assistant or AGS
  • You enjoy dealing with older people and are committed to the various tasks
  • You have a category B driver’s license and advantageously own your own car
  • You are available for assignments on two to three mornings and/or two to three evenings.
  • You work independently and quality-consciously and attach importance to careful care
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing

What we are proud of

  • A dedicated team that supports each other and exchanges expertise.
  • No night shift: Your bets are between 07:00 and 22:00.
  • One person at the center: You can focus on the individual needs of your counterpart during your assignment.
  • Freedom in everyday life: You work independently and contribute your interests and strengths.
  • A motivated and helpful team: You benefit from close cooperation, exchange of experience and mutual support.
  • Shared employee events and a corporate culture in which cooperation counts.
  • The extensive training offer of the Senevita Academy is available free of charge.
  • Attractive employee benefits and partner benefits complement our offer.
