Full description

As the leading employer in the canton, Valais Hospital employs 6,200 staff members. Each year, 41,500 patients are treated and nearly 652,700 outpatient visits are conducted. Valais Hospital is seeking, for the Valais Romand Hospital Center, Chief physicians or hospital physicians for medical on-call duty at Sierre Hospital, with a workload between 20% and 100%. ## Mission

  • Providing care for walk-in outpatient patients at the Sierre on-call service
  • Possible consultation activities at the Monthey, Martigny, and Sion sites as part of the deployment of a new primary care medicine service
  • Participation in training sessions and conferences organized by the interprofessional service
  • Participation in on-call duties to support Migration Medicine and Prison Medicine
  • Opportunity to participate in primary care medicine deployment projects and contribute to training by supervising interns and assistant physicians ## Profile
  • Swiss federal medical diploma or equivalent EU qualification
  • FMH specialist title in Internal Medicine or equivalent training (MEBEKO)
  • Strong interprofessional communication skills
  • Proficiency in French (German is an asset)
  • Autonomy, flexibility, and teamwork
  • Valid driver’s license
