Full description

As team leader of our caring team for cardiac rehabilitation, you lead and support your team in demanding day-to-day care and together provide professional, empathetic care for our patients. The team accompanies people holistically through the rehabilitation process, prepares them for an independent everyday life and works closely with doctors, therapists and other specialists. Start: from August 2026 or by arrangement. | Tasks:

  • Professional and personnel management of a team of around 25 employees
  • Active promotion, development and support of your employees
  • Close cooperation with the management care and the treating doctors: inside
  • Cooperation in the direct care and support of the team in everyday life
  • Contact person for relatives and external institutions
  • Accompaniment of patients: women on the way back to an independent life
  • Implementation of qualitative, strategic and economic corporate goals
  • Co-design and further development of the department | Requirements:
  • Completed training as a Dipl. Care professional HF/FH
  • Ideally, first leadership experience incl. Leadership training or willingness to complete it
  • Experience in rehabilitation is beneficial
  • High social skills and strong team skills
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Joy of responsibility and active participation
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing | We offer:
  • Work-life balance: scheduled services, hardly any overtime, little to no night services
