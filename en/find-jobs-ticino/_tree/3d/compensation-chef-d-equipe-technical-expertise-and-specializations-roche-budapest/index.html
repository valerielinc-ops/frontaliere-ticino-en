The Position In this role, you will lead a team of Reward Analysts covering the Compensation area while at the same time you will be actively driving and collaborating on technical improvements and innovation. As a Team Lead in Global Chapter Services, you are part of the PR&R chapter and are leading a global team that covers the following areas:

  • Technical, system-related activities: Configuration of the Workday Job Catalog, eligibility rules, grades, grade profiles, bonus plans, allowances etc. and active support of our Annual Performance and Compensation round as well as involvement in projects
  • Executive Compensation: A dedicated role in your team, is collaborating with the Reward Partner for executive compensation for the preparation of compensation proposals for offers as well as related system configuration
  • Stock: Process support for the Roche Connect program, including requirements gathering and testing of the technical solution, custom object loads and daily monitoring during enrolment period. Audit- and integration-related checks of reports, exception logs and related data maintenance in collaboration with other teams in P&C.

We expect this leader to have a strong passion and expertise in people leadership paired with experience in total rewards and technology. We look for a person who has the ability to motivate, support and coach the team, while at the same time actively work with Technology Product Owners and Reward Partners on our Technology and AI solutions.

The opportunity

  • Demonstrate a strong focus on driving future technical enhancements through both direct and matrix leadership of the specialized Technical Reward Analysts and wider project teams, while ensuring high-quality delivery in the Stock and Executive Compensation area through dedicated support and coaching of these specialized colleagues
  • Building an engaging culture to both attract and retain top talent, coaching team members regarding their individual development, career plan and fostering a growth mindset
  • Connecting your team members located across multiple locations through regular team meetings, sharing of wider information and ensuring equity in opportunities
  • Through frequent check-ins, ensuring that ongoing dialog occurs on the topics of Career, Capabilities, Connections and Contributions; and developing the team members’ knowledge, skills, competency, etc. in order to retain and develop talented staff
  • Understanding and supporting the team in their day-to-day activities related to technology (e.g. Workday Job Catalog configuration), reporting and auditing as well as providing Tier 2 support during the annual review process
  • Connecting the dots with bigger technology initiatives and strategic projects (including AI) with the drive to push the team out of their comfort zone to improve processes
  • Being a trusted partner to leaders and partners in the chapters, leading others through complex P&C topics by understanding the philosophies, approaches, global policies and systems-relevant configurations of relevant topics
  • ​Developing and managing strong partnerships with key internal organizations and stakeholders as well as external vendors to co-create technology solutions and drive continuous improvements to increase productivity, quality and efficiency locally and globally
  • Being an escalation point for related topics to your area of responsibility
