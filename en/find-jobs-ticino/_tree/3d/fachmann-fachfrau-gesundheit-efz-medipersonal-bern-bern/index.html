Location: Bern and the surrounding area Start of deployment: now or by agreement MediPersonal is a temporary office that specializes in the placement of qualified specialists in the medical industry and thus efficiently supports medical institutions. For our customers, renowned clinics, hospitals, nursing homes and outpatient services, we are looking for qualified and dedicated health care professionals EFZ (m/f/d) who work with heart and competence in the healthcare industry.

Their tasks

  • Holistic care and care of patients and residents in various specialist areas
  • Support of the qualified nursing staff in medical-technical measures, such as blood sampling, infusion therapy and wound care

  • Assistance in mobilisation, storage and support of patients in everyday life
  • Communication with relatives, interdisciplinary specialists and doctors for optimal care of patients
  • Documentation of care measures and ensuring compliance with quality and hygiene standards
  • Supporting patients in health promotion and prevention

  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
