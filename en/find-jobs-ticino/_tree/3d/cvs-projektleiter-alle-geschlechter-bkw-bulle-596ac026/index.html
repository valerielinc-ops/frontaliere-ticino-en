Full description

We make spaces where it is good to live.

As Health Project Manager, you are responsible for the full management of your projects, from acquisition to final delivery.

Your tasks

  • Develop and maintain customer portfolio, establish offers and negotiate prices.
  • Pilot health projects from A to Z, from design to delivery.
  • Coordinate the various stakeholders (technical office, architects, agents, suppliers and subcontractors).
  • Organize assembly teams and ensure the smooth running of construction sites, including equipment controls.
  • Financial monitoring of projects (budgets, costs and billing).
  • Ensure the quality of facilities and participate in internal coordination sessions.

Your profile

You are an organized, autonomous and solutions oriented person.

You bring in particular

  • An installation CFC or projector in sanitary building technology or equivalent training.
  • Experience in managing health projects of all sizes and excellent organizational and planning capacity.
  • Good knowledge in heating and ventilation
  • A common sense of negotiation and a strong sensitivity to costs.
  • Ease of dealing with clients and partners.
  • Natural leadership and teamwork.
