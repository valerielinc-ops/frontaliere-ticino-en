Student Assistant for Data and Literature Research (m/f/d)

10%-20%, Zurich, fixed-term

The Chair of Public Finance at the Department of Management, Technology and Economics (D-MTEC) is now looking for a student assistant (HiWi) for data and literature research and website administration with a workload of about 6 - 8 hours per week.

Job description

They research, collect and prepare data that can then be used for further scientific analysis. For literature research, you are familiar with the handling of scientific sources. In addition, preparations for presentations can be part of your assignments.

Profile

These tasks require good application knowledge of the standard software programs of Microsoft Office, as well as

  • Stata
  • Python
  • TeX
  • Knowledge required in ArcGIS

They operate independently, accurately and systematically.

We offer

  • Become part of ETH Zurich, which not only supports your professional development, but also actively contributes to positive changes in society
  • You can expect numerous perks, such as public transport subscriptions and car sharing, a wide range of sports offers from the ASVZ, child care and attractive pension services.
