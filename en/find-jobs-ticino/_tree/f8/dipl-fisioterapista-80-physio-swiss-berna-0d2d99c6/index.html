Physiotherapie Monbijou is a sports physiotherapy practice specializing in orthopedic conditions in young and old alike. We are a well-coordinated, motivated team of 4 therapists and are looking for number 5!

  • The practice is spaciously laid out, all rooms are filled with natural light, and it is very conveniently located.
  • The 5 weeks of vacation with good pay, as well as flexible work planning, are further advantages of this position.
  • The MZM (Medizinisches Zentrum Monbijou) is also integrated into the practice. These sports physicians and rheumatologists have been working directly with the physiotherapy department for years.
