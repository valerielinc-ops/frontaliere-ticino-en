Does your passion for cranes always push you up? Give new impetus to your career by joining our team as a spare for Liebherr tower cranes. Here's what you'll do with us.

  • Intervention planning for assemblers, service technicians and loading station
  • Assignment of teams according to priorities, skills and availability
  • Coordination of installation, troubleshooting and repairs
  • Organization of logistics, tools, equipment and transport
  • Management of cargo transport inputs and exits
  • Help to order spare parts and check corresponding invoices
  • Processing of parts and equipment returns
  • Communication and coordination with clients, assemblers, suppliers and internal services
  • Processing of technical requests and assistance with troubleshooting where possible
  • Monitoring of services, contracts, intervention reports and billing
  • Performance of administrative tasks related to the function What you bring
  • Full or solid technical training in the field
  • Experience in managing and coaching a team
  • Mastery of German is an asset
  • Excellent customer orientation and strong team spirit
  • Preferably domiciled in the Lausanne region
  • Good control of computer tools What we offer you
  • A varied position where each day brings new challenges
  • Innovative machines and exciting technologies that inspire enthusiasm
  • Flexibility through modern working hours and infrastructure
  • A strong team within a successful business group
