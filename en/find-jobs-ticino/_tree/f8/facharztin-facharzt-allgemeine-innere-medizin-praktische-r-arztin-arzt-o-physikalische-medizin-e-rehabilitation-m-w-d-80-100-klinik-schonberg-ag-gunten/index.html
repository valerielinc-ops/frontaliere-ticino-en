Full description

Would you like to combine medicine with meaning and accompany people on their way back to a better quality of life? Then you've come to the right place. In our familiar medical team with nine colleagues, you will work in an environment with interdisciplinary collaboration, short decision-making processes and an appreciative culture. Instead of hectic everyday life in an acute hospital, you can expect predictable working hours, a clear daily structure and a lot of continuity in your everyday work. Our location on Lake Thun, the special Schönberg spirit and the combination of medical quality and human closeness make us a special place to work in the Bernese Oberland. Your tasks

  • Independent care and support of your patients
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration with therapy, nursing and other specialist areas
  • Participation in rounds, reports and medical meetings
  • Helping to shape high-quality, humane rehabilitation
  • Careful medical documentation and communication
  • Participation in the medical on-call service
  • Active professional development and participation in further training
  • Thinking, helping to shape and further develop our medical offerings Your profile
  • Specialist title in “General Internal Medicine”, “Practitioner” or “Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation”
  • Recognized license to practice medicine in Switzerland
  • Several years of clinical experience
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German (at least level C)
  • Enjoy interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Independent, responsible way of working
  • Empathetic and professional interaction with people Our offer
  • Meaningful work with time for people
  • Clear daily structure with predictable and reliable working hours
  • Hardly any overtime - additional assignments usually only as part of on-call duties
  • High level of planning and good work-life balance
  • Support with integration in Switzerland for applicants from abroad
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • Relaxing recreation (fitness courses, free wellness offers in our partner companies and discounts on fitness subscriptions)
  • Contemporary employment conditions with progressive fringe benefits Interested? Then we look forward to your online application. Klinik Schönberg AG, Estelle Fuchs, Human Resources, Schönbergstrasse 40, 3654 Gunten
