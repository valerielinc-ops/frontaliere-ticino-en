Flexible part-time work

We are looking for people with passion. For their profession, for the team and our guests. As an innovative and successful company, we offer attractive employment conditions in one of the most beautiful resorts in Eastern Switzerland. We owe this success to our approximately 270 friendly and motivated employees with a great team spirit. These are your tasks

  • Performing the entire spectrum of medical massages, in particular classical massage, manual lymph drainage, foot reflexology and electrotherapy
  • Professional, dedicated treatment and care of our rehab patients and guests
  • Implementation of preventive treatments, especially for FX-Mayr cures and medical wellness guests
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with physiotherapists, doctors and nursing staff
  • Complete documentation of findings and treatments in the digital documentation system
  • Active participation in a positive, appreciative and collegial team climate

That is what we want from you.

  • You have completed training as a medical masseur and have already gained professional experience
  • You have a strong interest in the musculoskeletal system as well as in medical and therapeutic connections
  • You enjoy personal guest contact as well as individual support and advice
  • You have a high service orientation, commitment and enthusiasm for the profession
  • You have a valid registration with the EMR
  • You are interested in a flexible part-time workload
  • You speak German – other languages are a plus

This is what we offer you

  • Promotion: We support your professional development by sharing costs in further education

  • Food: our kitchen also conjures up fresh, healthy and subsidized meals for our employees
  • Team Spirit: open, respectful team culture with pleasure in working together
  • Discounts : you benefit from discounts in our hotel, in the region and at partner companies of the ERFA Group and Private Selection Hotels
  • Further benefits: weekend allowances and free parking
