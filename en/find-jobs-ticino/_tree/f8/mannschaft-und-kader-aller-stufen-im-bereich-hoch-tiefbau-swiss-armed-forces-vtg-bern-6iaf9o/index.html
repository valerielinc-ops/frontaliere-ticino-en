Put to the Point

Promoting peace – our mission The Swiss Armed Forces Competence Centre SWISSINT is constantly recruiting experts for temporary missions abroad to promote peace in the Swiss army. Based on the voluntariness of these assignments, interested women and men can apply for various functions. The prerequisite for obtaining an assignment contract is the successful completion of the mission-related training at the SWISSINT site in Stans-Oberdorf.

  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Place of work: Kasernenstrasse 4, 6370 Stans-Oberdorf

Their tasks Your duties as a member of the team

  • Establishment, dismantling and conversion of national and international infrastructures on behalf of the KFOR Joint Logistics Support Group (JLSG)
  • Preservation of value and permanent improvement of KFOR’s own hardening measures for self-protection
  • Specialist in the association of a Freedom of Movement Detachements (FoMD) of KFOR
  • Complete service providers in the field of construction

Your duties as a cadre

  • Management of assigned staff and economic use of resources as well as responsibility for occupational safety and health protection in their own area
  • Operation planning and management in the international framework of a FoMD as well as in the provision of services for the benefit of the JLSG of KFOR
  • Participation in international exercises and subsequently the operational processing of lessons learned within the framework of the FoMD of KFOR
  • Planning and realization of national and international construction projects and ensuring maintenance

This makes them unique.

  • Completed vocational training in construction or completed studies in civil engineering
  • For their own safety and for the safety of comrades as well as a smooth cooperation with Austrian colleagues and local employees, knowledge of the German language (level depending on the function) is desired for this function.
  • Knowledge of the English language (level depending on function)
  • Civil driving licence (cat B)
  • Age between 18 and 65
  • Completed recruiting school of the Swiss army, women with Swiss citizenship without basic military training are trained militarily according to their function

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

The SWISSINT Competence Center is the national operational command for the military peace promotion of the Swiss Army and is responsible for the planning, provision and national leadership of all Swiss military contingents and individuals in the army order peace promotion.

Additional information

For further information please contact the Competence Center SWISSINT, Tel. 058 467 58 58. Application forms can be found at: www.peace-support.ch

The Federal Administration respects the different needs and life experiences of its employees and promotes their diversity. Equal treatment is a top priority.
