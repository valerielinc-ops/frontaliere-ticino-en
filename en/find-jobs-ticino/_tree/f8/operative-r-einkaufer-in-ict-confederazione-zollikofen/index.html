Full description

Put to the Point

You help design procurements from planning to processing and help ensure that they are implemented legally, efficiently and on demand. Bring your purchasing experience and your IT affinity and develop procurement together with us.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 485-20825
  • Location: Eichenweg 3, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Procurement projects in the ICT environment below the WTO thresholds across the entire source-to-pay process
  • Support and advise needers in procurement planning
  • Implement legally secure procurement in accordance with the requirements of public procurement
  • Represent the ISC-EJPD vis-à-vis external partners for procurement-related issues
  • Continuous improvement of procurement processes, systems and documentation

This makes you unique

  • Commercial or technical training with further training in purchasing
  • Knowledge in operational purchasing and preferably in public procurement, or in the IT environment as well as basic business knowledge
  • Strong communication and team skills
  • IT affinity, good knowledge of MS Office applications and knowledge of SAP S/4HANA
  • Good knowledge of a second official language

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

IT for Switzerland’s internal security

The Computer Science Service Center ISC-EJPD is the central computer science service provider in the Eidg. Department of Justice and Police. High customer satisfaction and economic service provision are among the most important success factors. The ISC-EJPD develops and operates specialist applications for the areas of police, justice and migration of the EJPD. Our applications and infrastructures meet the highest requirements in terms of security and availability.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
