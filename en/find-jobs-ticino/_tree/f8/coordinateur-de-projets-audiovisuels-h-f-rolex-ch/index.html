Full description

Introduction

For our entity The Rolex Film Company, we are looking for an Audiovisual Project Coordinator whose main mission will be to ensure the processing of project applications and the daily coordination of the team's administrative activities.

Responsibilities

  • Receive and sort project submissions by checking file completeness and ensuring responses
  • Conduct a first reading of projects and write reading notes
  • Maintain bid log, documentary versions and deadlines
  • Centralize, classify and secure access to documents exchanged with internal and external teams
  • Coordinate meetings, projections, schedules, travel and administrative deadlines
  • Prepare agendas, reports, decision files and reporting materials

Profile

  • Training in cinema, audiovisual, communication or equivalent
  • First experience in coordinating, assisting in production or administrative management of projects
  • Excellent mastery of French and English, oral and written
  • Organized and reliable
  • Sense of detail and rigour
  • Discretion and sense of confidentiality

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
