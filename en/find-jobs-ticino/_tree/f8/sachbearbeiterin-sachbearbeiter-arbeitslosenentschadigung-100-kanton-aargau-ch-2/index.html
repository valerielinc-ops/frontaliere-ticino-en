Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Acceptance of new registrations for unemployment compensation
  • Demand for necessary documents from job seekers, employers and other stakeholders
  • Examination and assessment of applications for benefits from job seekers
  • Calculation of compensation, initiation of payments
  • Competent advice to job seekers on unemployment insurance issues

What you bring

  • Commercial vocational training EFZ (E-/M-profile) with proven successful professional practice
  • Pronounced flair for figures and legal contexts
  • Correct repute (Criminal Register/Employment Office)
  • High resilience and pronounced customer orientation in direct contact
  • Enjoying targeted working methods and working together in a motivated team

Your working environment

At the Office for Economic Affairs and Labour, we enforce federal laws such as the Unemployment Insurance Act (AVIG), the Employment Services Act (AVG) and the Labour Law (ArG). The main tasks are to supervise industry and industry with regard to occupational safety and health protection, to advise and place job seekers and to pay unemployment allowances for beneficiaries.
