Full description

Welcome to Landquart, the largest cheese factory in Switzerland. Every year, we process more than 100 million kilograms of milk into Raclette and Switzerland Swiss. Our team is characterised by its creative spirit and collegial environment. In order for everything to run smoothly, we need dedicated specialists like you.

You can do that.

  • Ensure a smooth production process by monitoring pasteurisation, CIP and whey processing.
  • Responsible implementation of the work according to the weekly and daily schedule.
  • Close coordination of the processes with the finishers, especially in the case of finishing and CIP cleaning.
  • Careful inspection of equipment and equipment before each production start.
  • Transparent documentation and good communication between control room and production.

You bring that with you

  • training as a dairy technologist or similar.
  • Willingness to be used flexibly in the entire production area.
  • High quality and safety awareness as well as a careful working style.
  • Joy of teamwork and a clear, solution-oriented communication.
  • Responsibility in dealing with plants, processes and schedules.
  • Openness to take on additional tasks as directed.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You will receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Individual development plan: With tailor-made development plans, we specifically promote your individual skills and support your long-term growth – a central component of our sustainability strategy. Learn more .
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
