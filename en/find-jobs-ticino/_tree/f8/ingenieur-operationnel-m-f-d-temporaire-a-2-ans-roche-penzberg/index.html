The Position We advance science so we can all spend more time with the people we love.

These are us You will become part of the Building Operation / Technical Services area at the Penzberg location. We are a committed, interdisciplinary team that passionately ensures that the infrastructure for our scientists works smoothly. In a culture of trust and respect, we work hand in hand to meet daily technical challenges

Responsibility | This is what awaits you In this role, you are responsible for the technical availability of all devices and systems in the laboratory building and in the pilot plant area. You will take on the following exciting tasks:

  • You coordinate internal and external trades for maintenance, inspections and maintenance.
  • You work closely with building operations engineers to ensure seamless interfaces.
  • You independently initiate user-specific projects and involve engineering in complex projects.
  • You analyze disruptions, develop sustainable solutions and derive preventive measures.
  • You continuously optimize technical processes in terms of energy efficiency, costs and plant life.

Qualifications | This is what you bring with you

  • You have successfully completed studies in the field of engineering (e.g. supply engineering, electrical engineering, process engineering or mechanical engineering).
  • You bring professional experience in maintenance, ideally in the laboratory or pilot field.
  • You have a sound technical understanding of process engineering plants and laboratory equipment.
  • Your way of working is structured and you have a strong sense of responsibility and decision-making pleasure.
  • You are strong in communication and appreciate the interdisciplinary cooperation with different disciplines.

Duration: This vacancy is limited to 2 years.
