At pfister, we pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today – will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Enthrall visitors with creative ideas around our diverse range of home accessories and household goods
  • Identify customer needs, provide competent advice and develop equipment proposals
  • Presentation of our range, based on the needs of our customers
  • Conducting goal-oriented sales talks
  • Helping to build an inspiring and promotional product presentation
  • If necessary, assist in arranging, decorating and marking the products
  • If necessary, help at the cashier
  • Experience in sales and customer advice
  • Enjoy regular customer contact and professional and comprehensive advice
  • Flair for design and beautiful living
  • Communicative, convincing and friendly personality
  • Completed vocational training in the retail sector, preferably in the household/glass/porcelain/cutlery sector
  • Experience in industries with a strong customer orientation
