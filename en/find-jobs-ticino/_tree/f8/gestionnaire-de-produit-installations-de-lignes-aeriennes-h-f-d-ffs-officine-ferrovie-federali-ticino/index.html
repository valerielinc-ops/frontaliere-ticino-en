Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. We are moving Switzerland — without electricity, no trains are running. Join us to operate the Swiss railway power grid and ensure that we can deliver the power to the railway infrastructure managers' contact lines. Here you will find more information about our projects and areas of activity: Energy To find out what benefits we offer, such as children's allowances, free GA or foreign travel at a reduced price (also for the family), visit our website under Social benefits. What you can move.

  • As an expert on high voltage lines and cables, you are the main contact person within the SBB.
  • In collaboration with users, you develop and manage the product strategy for high voltage lines and cables throughout the life cycle.
  • You define the internal technical specifications for the planning, construction, operation and maintenance of high voltage installations and ensure their sustainable development.
  • For the power supply of SBBs, you are responsible for the technical approval of high voltage lines and cable systems.
  • You represent SBB Energy in national committees and working groups, you actively pursue SBB interests in the development of industry standards and standards, and you maintain a strong network with users, manufacturers, authorities and other partners.

What you bring.

  • A convincing and inclusive person, you have strong communication skills, a strong sense of implementation and a structured approach.
  • You bring several years of experience as a product designer or designer and/or have extensive knowledge of the use of high voltage means of operation.
  • Higher education (HES/EPF/technical university) in electrotechnics will enable you to carry out this demanding mission.
  • You are familiar with key laws and standards relating to energy technology as well as relevant national and international safety standards and requirements.
  • You have a very good knowledge of German (minimum level C1) and a good knowledge of French (minimum level B1).

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
