In this role, you’ll help run what runs the world, by taking on meaningful work that drives real impact. We are looking for an R&D Senior Hardware Engineer to join our Electrification business. The selected candidate will join a team working on UPS product lines.

Work model: hybrid

Locations: Quartino, Ticino, Switzerland

• Your responsibilities:

  • Execute a variety of complex technology and engineering assignments. Understanding and applying applicable processes, standards, guidelines, and requirements, including architectural and design constraints.
  • Define technical specifications, perform circuit simulations, and develop projects requiring expertise in analog, digital, and power systems.
  • Lead design optimization, identifying advanced technical, architectural, and circuitry solutions to maximize mechanical, electrical, and PCBA integration in collaboration with cross-functional product development teams
  • Develop and maintain comprehensive technical documentation for systems, components, and interfaces.

• Qualifications for the Role

  • Master’s degree in Electrical or Electronics Engineering.
  • 5+ years of experience with electrical systems and complex power electronic systems.
  • Proven experience in digital and analog circuit design and simulation and proven experience with EMI design, mitigation, and troubleshooting.
  • Strong knowledge of environmental, reliability, and safety requirements for industrial products.
  • Familiarity with product regulations and industry standards (including IEC and UL).
  • Proficiency in documentation systems, including Engineering Change Notifications (ECNs) and Bills of Materials (BOMs).
  • Experience leading failure investigations and Root Cause Analysis (RCA).
  • Excellent command of English (both written and verbal).
