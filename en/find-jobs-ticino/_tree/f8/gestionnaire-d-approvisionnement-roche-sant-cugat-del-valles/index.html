From locations around the world and with a diverse and multicultural team, we source, manage our supplier base, develop & execute category strategies and constantly develop & digitize processes that enable us to best support our business to grow sustainably. The Opportunity

Are you ready to optimize the sourcing channels for the cutting-edge hardware, sensor technologies, and core chemical components that drive our next generation of diagnostic innovations? As a Sourcing Manager in Sant Cugat, Spain, you will execute end-to-end tactical procurement within our diagnostic portfolios. In this role, you will elevate routine transactional requests into strategic, value-add solutions, driving bottom-line cost savings and ensuring global supply reliability. To support our rapid growth, we are hiring a cohort of Sourcing Managers who will be aligned to one of two specialized technology teams: Near Patient Care (NPC), Sensors & Cartridges, and External Manufacturing (EM) or Active Diagnostic Ingredients (ADI), Reagents, and Instruments.

  • Reporting Line: The position directly reports to the Sourcing Lead, and is part of the Procurement Center of Excellence (CoE).
  • Wider team and stakeholders: You will partner seamlessly with critical global cross-functional teams, including Category, Delivery, Accounts Payable (AP) and RSS Demand to Pay, to clear supply bottlenecks and inform future procurement strategies. You will also manage operational relationships with a diverse base of external suppliers.
  • Role Type: An Individual Contributor role focused on end-to-end sourcing execution, process optimization, master data management, and value creation.
  • Impact: You drive global procurement efficiency, value execution, and compliance across your portfolios. Specifically, you are accountable for:
  • Sourcing Execution: Executing global sourcing activities, standard processes (RfIs, RfQs, RfPs), and market benchmarking for on-market products and New Product Introductions (NPI).
  • Demand Aggregation: Analyzing user requirements to identify value-driving methods, aggregating global demand to maximize leverage, and escalating non-standard requests to Category Management.
  • Project & Risk Management: Developing comprehensive project plans (timelines, milestones, resources), participating in project teams, and proactively identifying and mitigating risks to objectives.
  • Contract Lifecycle: Setting up template-based contracts (such as CDAs and SOWs) and managing their full lifecycle, repository tracking, and potential renewals or improvements.
  • Operational Excellence: Utilizing enterprise tools (SAP, myBuy) to simplify new and existing workflows, managing purchasing documentation, and ensuring strict compliance with global procurement policies.
  • Metrics & CSR: Tracking and delivering on key operational targets and customer service metrics (such as cycle times) while championing supplier diversity, sustainability, and ethical practices.
  • Conflict & Exposure: Managing process expectations and competing activity priorities, while presenting regular KPI and project updates to the Sourcing Lead and global stakeholders.
