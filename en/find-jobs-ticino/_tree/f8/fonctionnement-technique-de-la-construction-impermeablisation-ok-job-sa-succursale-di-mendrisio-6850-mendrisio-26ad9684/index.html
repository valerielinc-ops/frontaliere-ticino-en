We have more than 30 years of experience in the field of fixed and temporary placement. Description du job We are looking for a TECHNICAL OPERATION OF CONSTRUCTION ? IMPERMEABLIZATION ? What do we ask?

  • Preparation of the supports and preparation of the planned laying system.
  • Pose of bituminous and synthetic membranes (PVC, TPO, EPDM).
  • Hot air welding of the membranes and use of the flame reed.
  • Realization of turns, joints and construction details, ensuring the continuity of the mantle.
  • Welding tests and quality control.
  • Warm work management, properly adopting the planned fire safety systems.
  • Work at altitude in compliance with anti-duty security procedures.
  • Reporting to the person responsible for any anomalies, risks or dangers.

Profil

Your profile

  • Experience in laying bituminous and synthetic waterproofing and membranes.
  • Knowledge of monolayer/double layer laying techniques, flame, grip, zavorrata and mechanical fixing.
  • Capacity for preparation and treatment of supports (primer, steam barriers, etc.).
  • Good mastery of hot air welding, preferably with experience in using Leister type machines.
  • Knowledge of safety procedures for high altitude work and hot work, anti-duty DPIs and fire station.
  • License B; licence C is a preferential title.
  • Accuracy, reliability and safety at work.

Your prospects

  • A serene climate of work in a company with experience in the field
  • Possibility of personal growth
  • Remuneration for experience and skills
