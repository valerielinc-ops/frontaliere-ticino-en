Commercial Representative (Photovoltaic) EN/ALL

Your profile

  • Experience: A proven experience in sales (ideally in the solar, building, iron or structural sectors)
  • Perfectly bilingual French/German
  • Knowledge: Mastery of photovoltaic planning tools and management software (CRM, ERP)
