Full description

About the position As a maintenance and maintenance technician, you ensure the high availability, reliability and operational reliability of our production facilities, automation systems and technical infrastructures at the Wettingen site. You support production through professional troubleshooting, preventive maintenance and technical optimizations. In doing so, you work closely with different specialist areas and actively contribute to the continuous development of our plants and to make our production processes more efficient and reliable.

You can do that.

  • Ensuring reliable operation of our production facilities, automation systems and technical infrastructure as well as carrying out plant inspections and condition assessments
  • Analysis and correction of mechanical, electrical, pneumatic and automation malfunctions including root cause analysis and sustainable implementation of corrective measures
  • Implementation of preventive maintenance and inspection work according to maintenance plans as well as continuous improvement of maintenance standards and maintenance processes
  • Supporting production in the event of technical problems and ensuring compliance with technical standards, quality requirements and safety requirements
  • Identification and implementation of technical improvements to increase plant availability, productivity and quality as well as participation in lean, TPM and operational excellence initiatives
  • Support for plant optimisation, conversion, modernisation and technical investment projects
  • Cooperation in the management of critical spare parts and ensuring the technical availability of necessary components
  • Documentation of faults, maintenance work and improvement measures in the maintenance system as well as compliance with safety and compliance requirements

You bring that with you

  • Completed technical vocational training as an automatic, mechatronic, polymechanic, electromechanic or equivalent technical training
  • Experience in maintenance of industrial production facilities and knowledge of mechanics, pneumatics, electrical engineering and automation
  • Knowledge of PLC controls, robotics and industrial automation systems is advantageous
  • Experience with SAP PM, maintenance management systems or similar tools as well as an understanding of TPM, lean and operational excellence methods
  • Structured, analytical and solution-oriented working method with high problem-solving competence and strong security awareness
  • Teamwork, service orientation and willingness to cooperate in shift operations and pikett service

More about us Hitachi Energy is shaping the energy future with innovative technologies for a sustainable and reliable energy supply. With us, you can expect a modern working environment, a strong team and the opportunity to actively participate in future-oriented solutions.
