Full description

Do you want to take responsibility and make a difference? With us, you run your sales office entrepreneurially, continue to develop employees and talents, control key figures and set impulses for sustainable success with targeted sales activities.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, MM Zweisimmen, Thunstrasse 11, 3770 Zweisimmen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You manage the sales outlet independently and are responsible for the earnings and sales targets including budget planning and key figures analysis
  • You lead, develop and promote your employees and young talents.
  • You ensure the sustainable economic success of the sales outlet
  • You develop your sales outlet further, implement targeted sales promotion measures and align the offer to the needs of the market
  • You make sure that the defined processes are adhered to in the appropriate areas

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in a comparable management position
  • Completed basic or further education: in the retail trade specialization Lebensmitttel / Non Food and higher education e.g. retail specialist
  • You think networked, plan ahead and convince with your structured and efficient way of working
  • You lead goal-oriented, communicate appropriately and convince with your safe, professional and responsible approach
  • You have a good business understanding and enjoy dealing with key figures
  • You convince through high customer orientation and a strong understanding of services
  • PC skills and high digital fitness set you apart
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks paternity leave
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation on self-selected courses
  • Sports and fitness offers: Cost sharing in subscriptions of over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, aquaparks
