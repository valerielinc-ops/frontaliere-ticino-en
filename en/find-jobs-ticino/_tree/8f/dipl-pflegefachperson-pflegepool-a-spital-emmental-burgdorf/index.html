With hospitals in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides first-class medical care for 150,000 people. Thanks to a diverse team of specialists, state-of-the-art infrastructure and a family working atmosphere, we offer high-quality care around the clock.

Care with heart, mind and flexibility – welcome to the Emmental Hospital. What makes us special? As a versatile peripheral hospital, we value an uncomplicated, family working environment.
