Full description

The Directorate-General for Digital and Information Systems (DGNSI) is looking for a Business Analyst to join the Directorate for SI Management and Business Relations (GRM), within the entity Institutions, Partners. Do you want to contribute to the digital transformation of the cantonal administration of Vaud and put your expertise in business analysis, consulting and evolving information systems at the service of projects with high added value? Do you like to understand complex environments, interact with diverse interlocutors and support organizations in the evolution of their information processes and systems? Then join us!

  • Advise recipient departments and services in the analysis of their organizational and trade issues. Understand their functioning, identify problems and propose ways of improving them in line with their strategic and operational objectives.
  • Carry out studies and expedient analyses to identify, evaluate and compare different scenarios for the evolution of information processes and systems. Analyze the value, costs, benefits, risks and impacts of proposed solutions.
  • Develop recommendations and transformation strategies taking into account the business, organizational, financial, regulatory and direction issues of the cantonal information system.
  • Support beneficiary services in defining their target: analyse existing processes, identify areas for optimization, formalize target processes and contribute to the definition of functional needs and directions for the development of the IS.
  • Producing decision support deliverables: preliminary studies, opportunity analyses, business boxes, decision files, value analyses, business plan and strategic roadmaps.
  • Facilitate the convergence of stakeholders by presenting the results of the analyses to the management and steering bodies, conducting workshops and promoting collective decision-making.
  • Ensure the transition from studies to project implementation, working with information systems managers and project managers to ensure a clear understanding and consistency of the directions chosen. Complete files with a CV in French, cover letter, diplomas and work certificates. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact the head of the business relationship of SI Institutions, Partners, Fabrice Sirey ( fabrice.sirey@vd.ch ) is available.
  • Master in computer science, information systems, management or title deemed equivalent.
  • At least 7 years of professional experience in Switzerland in consulting, business analysis, strategic studies or accompanying organizational and digital transformations.
  • Experience confirmed in conducting expedient studies, developing business boxes, decision files and strategic recommendations.
  • Experience in the analysis and optimization of business processes as well as in the support of recipient departments and services.
  • Good understanding of information systems issues and their role in organizational transformation.
  • Experience in complex IT projects and understanding the life cycle of SI solutions.
  • Mastering the desired German, ideally allowing effective interaction with German-speaking partners and interlocutors.
  • Excellent analytical, synthesis, writing and presentation capabilities. The following skills are assets:
  • CBAP or CCBA certification (BABOK).
  • IREB certification.
  • Knowledge of business architecture and urbanization of information systems.
  • Practice design thinking, collective intelligence and facilitation methods.
  • Knowledge of agile approaches (Scrum, FASe, Lean). You are recognized for your ability to heighten, quickly understand complex environments and turn problems into concrete and reasoned recommendations. With an excellent relational sense, you can interact with people at different levels, show diplomacy and promote consensus-building. Autonomous, rigorous and results-oriented, you combine global vision, critical spirit and pragmatism. Special penalties:
  • May be required to work outside normal working hours (night, Saturday, Sunday or holiday)
  • Home in Switzerland

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
