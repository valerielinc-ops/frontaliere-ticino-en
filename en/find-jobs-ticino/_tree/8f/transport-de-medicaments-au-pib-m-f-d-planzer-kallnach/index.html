Brand of the Planzer Group: Marti.

Tasks

Profile

  • Oral and written you can safely express yourself in German
  • You work reliably, accurately and quickly adapt to new challenges
  • Even if it gets stressful, you stay cool and keep track.

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation.
