Fresh products that deserve their name. Shelves stocked. Orders triggered on time. Floor clean. Cash correct. Customers satisfied. That's our store. And soon, it will be yours too. Join the fresh winning team now. ## Roles

  • Operate the cash register and settle payments
  • Receive, check, and shelve fresh products and all other items in your store's shelves
  • Check minimum durability dates on products
  • Ensure order and cleanliness in the store and warehouse
  • Implement store layout specifications correctly ## Requirements
  • Ideally more than two years of professional experience: in retail (food)
  • Completed vocational training (EBA): in retail (food) or a related profession would be ideal
  • Willing to work beyond «nine to five» – and enjoy free time when others work
  • Physically fit, some packages weigh up to 10 kg
  • Familiar with PC, tablet, and smartphone
  • and with other digital helpers (e.g., for inventory management) you adapt quickly
  • Enjoy being around people, a good portion of friendliness, and a positive attitude
  • German (good knowledge) ## What we offer
