Full description

You enjoy working in sales, are reliable and meet customers with a smile? In our Coop Pronto shop with gas station in Landquart, you can expect a varied day-to-day work with a lot of customer contact. We count on dedication, team spirit and a friendly attitude. If you like to sell, take responsibility and keep an overview even in lively moments, you are exactly right with us. ###

Tasks

  • They advise our customers: competent, friendly and with a smile - and ensure that they are happy to come back
  • You sell our convenience products with conviction
  • You take on cash stakes and process payments reliably and efficiently
  • They implement sales promotion measures attentively and effectively
  • They consistently ensure compliance with all relevant shop guidelines, in particular quality and youth protection regulations
  • They provide an attractive presentation of goods and a well-kept, inviting shop layout

Requirements

  • Ideally, experience in retailing
  • Completed basic education beneficial
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Customer-oriented thinking and acting as well as a friendly and well-groomed appearance
  • Joy in dealing with people and working together in a team
  • Flexibility in work assignments, including on Sundays and public holidays

  • Fair surcharges for Sunday and public holidays
  • Fair remuneration with an entry-level wage of CHF 4'200.- plus 13. Monthly wages
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • 41 hours Week
  • Flexible operational planning wherever possible
  • Early operational plans for better predictability
  • Option on part-time models
