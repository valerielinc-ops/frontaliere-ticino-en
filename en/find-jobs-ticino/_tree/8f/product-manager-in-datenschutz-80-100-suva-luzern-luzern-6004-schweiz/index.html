Full description

Do you want to shape the digital development of data protection and make the handling of information sustainable? Then you are right with us. What you do

  • You are responsible for the strategic and digital development of the product groups “Records Management and Archive System” and “Compliance and Data Protection”.
  • In an agile SAFe delivery organization, you develop the product vision and feature roadmap together with the product owners, SAFe teams and specialist departments.
  • You develop concrete, practical solutions for the legally compliant management of data and information over the entire life cycle, from creation and use to storage, deletion or archiving.
  • You define the Suva-wide retention and deletion requirements and ensure effective information lifecycle management and sustainable data governance. What you bring
  • You have a degree in law, business informatics, business administration, information management or a comparable education.
  • You have experience in product management, data protection or the development of digital products and processes. Knowledge of records management, compliance or governance is a plus.
  • You have managed projects, are familiar with agile working methods such as SAFe and can structure and prioritize complex professional, legal and technical requirements.
  • You have a pronounced conceptual thinking ability, initiative and assertiveness and convince through a solution-oriented working method.
