Full description

Elsa Group is one of the leading Swiss dairy industry groups and is part of the Migros Group (Migros Industry). Elsa Group is an expert in the manufacture and packaging of dairy products as well as in the production, refining and processing of cheeses and the production of plant-based products.

# Mansioni

  • Implement before service
  • Control the quality of goods and manage stocks
  • Cook the various menus within the specified time limits and quantities, in accordance with hygiene and safety standards
  • Provide noon and/or evening service
  • Participate in the development of special meals during visits
  • Control, maintain and clean kitchen equipment
  • Participate in cleaning
  • Set up the rooms
  • Participate in the training of employees and apprentices

Required

  • Experience confirmed: in a similar position
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Cook/Earth
  • Excellent knowledge of hygiene and safety standards
  • Creative and equipped with good cooking skills
  • Impeccable hygiene
  • Ability to work in groups
  • French (Very good knowledge)
