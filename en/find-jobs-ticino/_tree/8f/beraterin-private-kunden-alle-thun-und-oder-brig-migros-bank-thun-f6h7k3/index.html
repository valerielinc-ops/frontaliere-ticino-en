Full description

You can get involved with us and, together with your team, inspire customers and help shape the customer advice of the future. We are looking for you: a positioned personality who thinks ahead and is proactively involved.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Thun Branch, Bälliz 36, 3600 Thun

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Migros Bank customers, through your comprehensive and professional advice in the core areas of investing, financing and pension provision.
  • Your team, with which you take care of the further development and expansion of team-managed customers.
  • New customers because you can easily acquire and delight them with your winning and positioned style.
  • Challenging clients by meeting their client needs together with our financial planning experts.

Requirements

  • 1 - 3 years: Customer Consulting Retailbanking
  • First experience in private client consulting as well as the necessary expertise in the areas of saving, financing, investing with funds and pensions, so that you can advise our customers professionally.
  • A flair and joy in recognizing customer needs so that it is easy for you to inspire customers for the right financial solutions.
  • Experience in the successful acquisition of new customers and an open, positioned personality.
  • A responsible, communicative and resilient personality that doesn’t get things off balance so quickly, because you won’t only our customers, but also your team.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ)
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Child care: up to CHF 300 per month per child to external child care
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: accounts, mortgages and financial services with preferential terms
  • Central workplace: Optimally accessible, centrally located workplace

Contatto

Application and contact Patrick Käch HR Business Partner / Junior Manager

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Grado di occupazione: 50-100 %
