Entry into office : to be agreed Activity rate : 60% With its unique offer of benefits – prevention, insurance, rehabilitation – the Suva offers its insured persons comprehensive health protection. The main mission of the Suva Clinics is the rehabilitation of injured persons. In addition to the traditional disciplines of neurological rehabilitation and the musculoskeletal system, our two clinics in Sion (RRC ) and Bellikon ( RKB ) offer specific benefits for the rehabilitation of patients burned, technical orthopaedics and vocational rehabilitation, early rehabilitation, para/tetraplegia patients.
