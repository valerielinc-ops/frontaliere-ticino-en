The Position The Opportunity

As our Compliance & Safety, Health & Environment Specialist, you will play a central role in cultivating a culture of integrity and well-being across our Pharma and Diagnostics divisions in Hungary. You will drive key compliance processes, shape holistic employee health strategies, and modernize record management practices.

Compliance (50%)

  • Act as Compliance Specialist to shape a culture of ethics through training, awareness campaigns, and preventive risk mapping.
  • Manage EFPIA transparency disclosures, consent collection, grievance management, and process simplification.
  • Oversee conflicts of interest processes and utilize AI-driven tracking tools to eliminate manual data entry.
  • Adapt global regulations to domestic policies, manage data privacy administration, and update risk registers.
  • Partner with the Roche Services & Solutions (RSS) compliance monitoring group and support the local 1T initiative.

Safety, Health, Environment (~40%)

  • Serve as OneRoche SHE Specialist across Pharma and Diagnostics, updating local policies and managing global reporting.
  • Design and execute holistic employee health, mental well-being strategies, and non-mandatory medical services.
  • Direct annual occupational health exams, mandatory labor safety trainings, case inspections, and workplace accident administration.
  • Oversee SHE purchase orders in alignment with global directives and maintain safety standards across all locations.
  • Collaborate with regional/global SHE networks and RSS to eliminate operational silos and optimize resource flexibility.

Record Management (~10%)

  • Formulate and maintain a modernized record management strategy focused on lean, redundant-free workflows.
  • Adopt AI and automated lifecycle tools to streamline record-keeping, compliance purging, and monitoring.
  • Update local retention schedules, conduct routine vulnerability assessments, and execute systematic documentation purging.
  • Deliver staff training on document creation, retention, archiving, and compliant storage tools.
  • Guide the team on retention policies and assist with physical and digital archive management.

Other

  • Apply specialized functional expertise to support affiliate objectives and act as a trusted best-practice advisor.
  • Participate actively in cross-functional, local, and global project teams to drive strategic targets.
  • Formulate innovative solutions for complex issues while taking end-to-end process ownership.
  • Leverage international networks efficiently to optimize operational outcomes.
  • Stay current on all relevant guidelines while proactively managing intricate organizational processes.

Who You Are

You are a proactive, tech-savvy professional who thrives on building trust, simplifying processes, and creating a safe, ethical workplace.

  • Higher education degree in business, public/social administration, or a related field.
  • 2+ years of professional experience in compliance, SHE (safety, health, environment), or a related administrative discipline.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder engagement skills with a track record of translating goals into practical plans.
  • Proficiency in English and Hungarian, with a passion for leveraging technology and automated tools to optimize workflows.
