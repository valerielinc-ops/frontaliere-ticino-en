Full description

In this versatile function, you and the team ensure the perfect operation and maintenance of the entire building infrastructure at the new building in Oensingen. You ensure that plants, facilities and outdoor areas are maintained, functional and operational at all times.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: day shift
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Südringstrasse 14 4702 Oensingen

Tasks

  • Implementation of regular inspections, maintenance and maintenance work on building infrastructure
  • Independent execution of minor repairs and repair of failures in technical installations and equipment
  • Proactive detection of defects and initiation of appropriate maintenance measures
  • Coordination and monitoring of external service providers and craft businesses
  • Support for the operation and monitoring of HVAC installations and the control of the building control system
  • Management of spare parts and participation in waste management on the Südring site

Requirements

  • Completed craftsmanship
  • Several years of experience in maintenance or in a comparable function
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Knowledge in dealing with building control systems is advantageous
  • Readiness for Picett services and flexible response to out-of-schedule situations
