Put to the Point

Do you want to play an active role in shaping the mobilisation of the Swiss army and make a significant contribution to operational readiness? Do you enjoy working at the interface between operational planning, military staff work and preparations for mobilization? Then we look forward to meeting you.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20982
  • Place of work: Papiermühlestrasse 20, 3014 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Working independently on action plan planning, situation monitoring and follow-up in the field of mobilisation
  • Write reports, statements, concepts, studies and decision-making bases for mobilization as well as coordinate leadership products with internal and external partners
  • Prepare and accompany mobilisation exercises, support training in the field of mobilisation and plan, implement and evaluate test alerts
  • Represent the Department of Mobilization in working groups and committees, implement guidelines for integral security and manage the business area according to the applicable administrative orders
  • Supporting the Chief of Situational Response in ongoing operations, missions and support services of the Army, as well as assisting in special events as a Picquet Officer or Operation Officer

This makes them unique.

  • experience in project management and knowledge of the organisation and processes of mobilisation
  • Military experience in this area is beneficial
  • Good organizational, analytical and conceptual skills as well as an independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Very good editorial skills and very good user knowledge of MS-Office products
  • Good knowledge of a second official language and English at level B1
  • Experience as a staff officer or as unit or troop body commander is an advantage

Your commitment to security and freedom

The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are employed by civilians – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
