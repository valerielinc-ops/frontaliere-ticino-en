Full description

The offer of Migros gastronomy is diverse. In the restaurant Seegarten we pamper our guests with freshly prepared food and an attractive à la carte offer. To support our team, we are looking for a motivated and reliable person who contributes to a first-class service and a successful gastronomic experience.

Luogo di lavoro: Restaurant Seegarten, Rainstrasse 6, 4142 Münchenstein

Important information

Working hours: Monday to Sunday between 07:30 and 19:00 (in two shifts) We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Management of the buffet: installation and dismantling and regular filling of food and drinks
  • Ensuring cleanliness and compliance with hygiene regulations in the buffet and working area
  • POS operation: self-contained handling of the cash register
  • Friendly and professional treatment of customers

Requirements

  • No professional experience required if training is available, otherwise 3-5 years: in gastronomy
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade / in gastronomy
  • Customer-oriented, resilient and team-capable personality
  • Independent and exact working methods
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Employee offers: Various offers, discounts and preferential conditions
  • Social benefits: In the event of illness, the salary is 100% for two years, in the event of accident we are insured SUVA
  • Occupational pension fund with above-average performance: 8.5%, AG share 17%
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees

Contatto

Application and contact Enya Röthlisberger

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.

Grado di occupazione: 90 %
