We are looking for a person immediately or by agreement as

Physical therapist FH/HF in special education, 70–90 % 📚 Your tasks:

  • You take over the physiotherapeutic assessment, planning, treatment, documentation and evaluation according to medical prescription.
  • You accompany children and young people individually and resource-oriented in their motor development, mobility and independence.
  • You work interdisciplinary with the various specialist areas, relatives, medical specialists and external bodies.
  • You are responsible for the organization and correction of therapeutic aids in medical consultation such as orthoses, wheelchairs and standing boards.
  • You advise and support the environment of children and adolescents in physiotherapeutic questions and in dealing with aids.
  • You bring your expertise to meetings, team meetings, and the further development and quality assurance of physical therapy.
  • You have the opportunity to contribute your own ideas and to actively shape the physiotherapeutic department.

Happiness What you bring

  • You have a recognised, completed training as a physiotherapist FH/HF.
  • Ideally, you will have practical experience and/or further training in (neuro-)pediatrics or special education.
  • You are interested in working with children and adolescents with cognitive and multiple disabilities.
  • You are familiar with different pediatric conditions or motivated to familiarize yourself with them.
  • You are resilient and flexible with challenging behavior and short-term changes in everyday work.
  • You work independently, responsibly and at the same time enjoy working in an interdisciplinary team.
  • You are ready to actively participate and develop professionally and personally.

You can expect a small, humorous, motivated and collegial therapy team that accompanies you with expertise and heart in your tasks. You have plenty of room for initiative and flexible work design as well as the opportunity to contribute your own ideas and actively participate in the further development of the department. During the school holidays you have no work, of which seven weeks are paid. In addition, we support your professional development through internal and external further education. From the beginning of 2027, we will move into our new building with spacious therapy facilities, modern working conditions and plenty of space for your professional and personal development.
