Full description

Do you want to make a technological difference and make an important contribution to the future of the Swiss healthcare system? For the implementation of the uniform financing of ambulatory and inpatient services (EFAS) as of 1. January 2028 CSS develops a new application system in a greenfield project. For this we are looking for you as Senior Java/JEE Software Engineer (m/f). You have the opportunity to actively shape the architecture from the ground up and develop a sustainable solution together with a newly assembled team. When do we meet?

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: In an agile Scrum team, you develop challenging, architecture-compliant solution variants according to CSS and team-internal specifications.
  • You program sustainable solutions and ensure high software quality through quality assurance, automated testing and product regression.
  • In your work you use modern technologies and architectures such as Java/Jakarta, event- and workflow-based architectures, Docker and Kubernetes.
  • You use modern software engineering practices including AI-Driven Development, Clean Code, Clean Architecture, Test-Driven Development, Behaviour-Driven Development and Domain-Driven Design.
  • According to the principle "You build it, you run it", you accompany the software as a member of the DevOps team from conception to operation.
  • You work closely with the business and other development teams, bring your ideas and experiences and share your knowledge. At the same time, you are continuously expanding your expertise around EFAS.

You bring that with you

You have several years of experience in software development with the Java/JEE stack as well as with Kubernetes and DevOps. Experience in web development, with Angular and in the health insurance sector is a plus.

  • In your work, you enjoy complex issues and develop high-quality, sustainable and maintainable code.
  • You use AI-supported development tools and AI agents like GitHub Copilot specifically in your everyday development. You critically examine their results and take responsibility for quality, safety and professional correctness.
  • In everyday work, you act independently, creatively and solution-oriented and actively participate in the agile team.
  • You are ready to familiarize yourself with new technical topics around EFAS.
  • You communicate openly, share your knowledge and enjoy working with different stakeholders. You bring a lean-agile mindset and are open to working together in a SAFe environment.
  • Work flexibly: Your team often works in the home office or on the road. Thanks to laptop with integrated SIM card and other technical possibilities no problem.
  • Stay up to date thanks to our Software Engineering Academy, the Innovation Days and the visit of external conferences.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Take a break: house renovation, world travel or learning languages - what is your dream? Celebrate your 10th anniversary with a two-month sabbatical.
  • Enjoy your family life: Make time for your offspring: 3 weeks for fathers, for mothers up to 20 weeks. There are also generous child and education allowances.
