Full description

Spitex Pfaffnau-Roggliswil-Altbüron is a small organisation with 10 innovative employees. Do you want to lead and stay close to care at the same time? With us, you take responsibility for a well-established team and can actively shape everyday care. We are looking for agreement a committed and leadership-strong personality as Team Leader Care + Housekeeping (HF / DN II / AKP)

What you do with us

Care and support of our clients at home incl. fall management; management responsibility and planning and securing human resources; ensuring the quality of care and care within the team; contact person for customer matters

  • Controlling the care processes including qualitative documentation; close cooperation with the Registrar and external specialists
  • Further development of the organisation

What you bring

Completed nursing training HF/DN II/AKP and many years of professional experience; pleasure in dealing with people; ability and joy motivating to lead

  • Driving license / own car
  • What we offer:

A motivated team and an open, appreciative working atmosphere; a varied and responsible task; no night service; training opportunities; contemporary employment conditions; regular team events

  • Fair payment
