Specialist technician: in electrical and telecom 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Specialist technician: in electrical and telecommunications Brig, November 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. You can learn technology. Not a strong team. What really sets us apart are the people. We enjoy working together, discussing things openly and supporting each other. Because good technology comes not only from specialist knowledge, but above all from a strong team. Your day-to-day work is particularly diverse: from railway and business communication to clock systems to medium voltage, building automation and card terminal systems. With us you won't just work on one system, but on a variety of different technologies. With 25 electrical and 35 telecommunications platforms, an exceptionally broad and varied technical environment awaits you. Together as a team, you take on responsibility and use your efforts to ensure that the systems function reliably and that the trains run safely, on time and connected. You not only keep existing systems running, but also actively shape their further development. You can expect varied technical challenges and the opportunity to take on responsibility and develop professionally and personally. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • You have completed basic training as an EFZ electrician, EFZ assembly electrician or comparable electrical engineering training. Knowledge of telecommunications is an advantage.
