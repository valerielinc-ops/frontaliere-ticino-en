Full description

Location: Medifilm AG, Ostringstrasse 10, 4702 Oensingen

Medifilm is growing and with us our projects and structures are also growing. That is why we are creating a new position for an experienced project manager Strategic Projects. You actively shape our project and portfolio management, create the necessary structures for our further development and advance strategic, cross-divisional projects together with CEO, management team and specialist departments.

You can do that.

  • You will continue to lead and develop the strategic project and transformation portfolio and create transparency about priorities, dependencies, resources, risks and milestones.
  • You prepare strategic portfolio decisions for the CEO and leadership team, develop the basis for decision-making and moderate initiatives to decision-making maturity.
  • You establish and maintain an overarching roadmap and make resource bottlenecks and dependencies between operations, quality, market, IT and other areas visible at an early stage.
  • You will take over the management of selected strategic cross-cutting projects and coordinate participants from different specialist areas and interfaces within the Galenica Group.
  • You recognize risks and conflicts of goals at an early stage and prepare the most structured prioritization, de-prioritization and escalation decisions.
  • You ensure that decisions, responsibilities and milestones are documented, communicated and tracked consistently.

You bring that with you

  • Several years of experience in project or program management and ideally in portfolio management in a complex or regulated environment.
  • In-depth understanding of project and process management as well as experience with classical and agile methods, business cases, roadmaps, RACI and risk and dependency management.
  • An understanding of regulated environments such as Pharma/GxP and a good understanding of ERP, production and IT systems.
  • Strong ability to quickly structure complex relationships, connect different interests and derive clear basis for decision-making.
  • Sovereign appearance, convincing communication and the ability to drive decisions and changes without direct line responsibility.
  • High personal responsibility, analytical thinking and a structured, pragmatic and solution-oriented working method.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. The Galenica network is the market-defining healthcare provider in our country. The Galenica network offers a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire network, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
