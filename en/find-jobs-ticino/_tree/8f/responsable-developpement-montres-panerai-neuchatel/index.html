Full description

Reference code: JR126755

Found in Florence in 1860, Panerai forged its reputation by developing precision instruments and professional watches for the Royal Italian Navy, renewed for their reliability and functional design. Rooted in performance, purpose, and precision, the Maison embraces its heritage while pushing the boundaries of innovation, combining Italian design with Swiss expertise. Today, Panerai continues to create tool watches that serve as trusted companions for every adventure, even in the most demanding environment.

Linked to our Research & Development Director within our Project Department, we are looking for our future:

Responsible Development Watches

Your Mission

Federate and animate the team composed of Project Manager Watches & coordinate the development of the new Panerai according to the criteria of price, quality and delay.

How are you going to make a difference?

Team management

  • Lead the team of project leaders by ensuring that the launch objectives are met,
  • Organize regular reporting of projects to R&D, Operations and House Directorates (planning, results, problems, budget and risks) and ensure communication with key stakeholders,
  • Provide technical support to team members and ensure the quality of deliverables,
  • Set and evaluate team objectives, manage their performance and develop their skills,
  • Pilot the load/capacity of the team, build the budget (and its updates) and regularly monitor it,
  • Promote the values of the Brand and the Group on a daily basis.
  • Animation of interfaces with the main internal partners (Product, Quality, Supply Chain & Production)
  • Consolidate needs (marketing briefs, medium / long term orientation) as upstream as possible to give maximum visibility on the launch plan,
  • Ensure a quick response to the briefings by proactively proposing industrial and efficient solutions in terms of cost/quality and deadlines,
  • Encourage interactions between the Maison's stakeholders on the development of new products (marketing, creation, technique, quality, procurement, customer service, finance and logistics),
  • Anticipate blocking points and seek the necessary arbitrations,
  • Provide technical support for creation and analyse needs in relation to industrial possibilities. Effective communication of constraints while at the same time making proposals,
  • Ensure planning compliance based on launch date,

Monitor and monitor the progress of the portfolio projects produced, communicate progress and critical points (reporting, dashboards, flash...) and anticipate risks.

Continuous improvement

  • To monitor and improve the development method, define and implement project management tools,
  • Ensure compliance with the various milestones (in particular in terms of deliverability) of the development method,
  • To define and implement and monitor relevant team performance indicators,
  • Ensure the accuracy and optimization of cost prices (with the Purchasing Department),
  • Identify improvement levers (sourcing, technical choices, construction changes) to optimize the cost price of products and/or improve quality.

Transverse project management

  • Organize follow-up of new suppliers and competitors for sharing with the entire department and marketing and creation partners,
  • Frame/lead cross-cutting projects to improve and implement product improvements.

What are the keys to your success in our House?

You benefit from an engineering training in microtechnique or title deemed equivalent and have confirmed experience in project management, Excellent communicator and federator, you have at least 3 years experience in team management, You are comfortable coordinating transversal technical projects (knowledge in watchmaking is mandatory) and have a great ability to adapt and popularize information,

  • Force of proposal, you are curious & open mind. You demonstrate an excellent team spirit and a good relationship,
  • You master the usual IT tools (excel & PPT ++). Programming skills (VBA, PBI...) are a plus,
  • You have perfect command of French and English, both oral and written. Knowledge in Italian is an advantage.

What makes Panerai unique?

  • You will be part of a dynamic House where your skills and talents will be recognized.
  • You will share with us the same values such as "enrichment of communities, benevolent attitude, continuous improvement and individual responsibility".
  • You will work on a multicultural team and develop an international experience.
  • You will navigate with us on the path of sustainable development. You will develop and advance your career through opportunities within Maison and the Richemont Group.
