Blue Physio is a modern and dynamic physiotherapy firm located in the heart of Monthey, in the Valais Chablais. Our team of passionate professionals offers personalized and quality care, both at the clinic and at the patient's home. As part of the development of our activities, we are looking for a 40% graduate physiotherapist, with the possibility of progressive evolution up to 100%.

Your Mission

  • Provide a variety of physiotherapeutic care at the firm and at home.
  • Carry out reviews, develop individualized treatment plans and assist patients in their rehabilitation.
  • Intervening with a diverse patientel: orthopaedics, traumatology, neurology, geriatrics and functional rehabilitation.
  • Collaborate with a multidisciplinary team and different health partners.
  • Actively participate in the development and quality of the firm's services.

Your profile

  • Diploma in physiotherapist recognized in Switzerland (recognition Swiss Red Cross for foreign degrees).
  • Authorization to practice in the canton of Valais or ability to obtain it.
  • Independent, dynamic, caring person with an excellent relationship.
  • Team spirit, sense of responsibility and commitment to quality of care.
  • Driving licence for home interventions.
  • A beginner or experienced candidate, your application is welcome.

We offer you

  • An initial activity rate of 40%, evolving up to 100% according to the development of the activity.
  • A modern, pleasant and well equipped working environment.
  • A varied activity combining practice care and home care.
  • A welcoming, dynamic and collaborative team.
  • Professional autonomy and the opportunity to develop your skills and specializations.
  • Opportunities for continuing training and vocational development.
  • Attractive terms of engagement.
