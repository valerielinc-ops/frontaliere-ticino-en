Full description

Your Mission

Dive into the heart of the exceptional universe of our home and become a key ambassador of our collections. Your role is to offer every customer a rare and personalized experience, valuing the richness and diversity of Hermès know-how through our 16 trades. You create a unique moment for every customer, accompanied with attention and sensitivity in the discovery of our universes. You know how to convey the beauty of the materials, the quality of the finishes and the history behind each creation.

Your Daily

  • Welcome each customer with generosity and create a warm, attentive and caring environment conducive to personalized advice.
  • Discover our collections in their diversity, highlighting the pieces, materials and know-how.
  • Advise with precision and enthusiasm, offering harmonious associations adapted to the needs and lifestyle of each customer through our trades.
  • Develop sales across all categories, integrating a fluid and modern omnichannel approach.
  • Maintain in-depth knowledge of collections, novelties and trends while training you regularly
  • Collaborate and support the team to ensure a harmonious customer experience and collective success.

The keys to your success

  • Significant experience in high-end retail, with a strong understanding of the requirements of the customer relationship.
  • Solid product culture and ability to evolve with ease between several business worlds, thanks to a real sensitivity to materials, finishes and quality.
  • Clear and professional communication in French and English, allowing to accompany and retain a demanding clientele.
  • Active listening and adaptability to accurately meet varied expectations and adjust the customer experience.
  • Autonomy, agility and mastery of digital tools, supported by a natural curiosity that favours a continuous rise in competence.
  • Team spirit and open mind, contributing to a fluid collective dynamics between different professions.
  • Service and retail sense, ensuring an irreproachable customer experience that is consistent with home standards.

Responsible employer, we are committed to ethics, diversity and inclusion. Join the human adventure Hermes!
