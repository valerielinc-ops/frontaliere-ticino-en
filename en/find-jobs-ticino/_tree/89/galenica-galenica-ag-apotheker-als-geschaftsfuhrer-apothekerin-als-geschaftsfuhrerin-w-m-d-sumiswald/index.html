Full description

Location: Coop Vitality Sumiswald, Dorfgasse 2, 3454 Sumiswald

From January 2027 a new chapter begins: With the takeover and the conversion to Coop Vitality Pharmacy, a modern location with a lot of potential is being created. As Managing Director or Co-Managing Director, you accompany this new start, lead your team through the change and shape the pharmacy from the beginning.

You can do that.

  • You are technically responsible for the pharmacy and manage it independently
  • Every pharmacy is different: you reinforce what makes your location special
  • You actively contribute suggestions and ideas and help to optimize the processes throughout the group
  • Employees lead and coach, recruit and integrate: You coordinate the tasks and projects in the team
  • You accompany the students or learners expertly
  • Nothing escapes your watchful eyes: You make sure that the quality is right at all times

You bring that with you

  • Completed pharmacy studies with FPH or BAB
  • You want to make a difference and can handle responsibility well
  • With your motivating and appreciative nature, you bring the pharmacy and your team further
  • You like to share your know-how and appreciate the mutual exchange
  • It appeals to you to work for a group-wide company and to use the synergies from it
  • German at least level B2

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and at other top brands incl. Coop Personal Discount Card
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

A powerful joint venture At Coop Vitality, Coop and Galenica work together. Coop Vitality is part of the Galenica network and combines the strength of two partners to offer customers a modern pharmacy experience with a high level of expertise. The network’s pharmacies are usually located in or directly next to larger Coop branches – especially in the German-speaking part of Switzerland. The range includes a wide range of products for health, beauty and disease prevention, complemented by expert advice and pharmaceutical services. At Coop Vitality, you can expect an extraordinary customer orientation. This in the points of sale as well as in the online shop.
