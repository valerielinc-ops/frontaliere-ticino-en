Engine operator back to list We are looking for one of our customers, a versatile gear driver in the waste sector Job description As part of our waste management and treatment activities, we are seeking a (M/F) gear driver to intervene on our waste sorting and recovery platform.

Within the operating team, you will be responsible for

  • Drive various construction equipment (pneumatic roller with grapple, loader, hydraulic excavator...) to ensure waste handling operations.
  • Receive, move and power sorting and recovery facilities.
  • Sort and orient different materials...
  • Ensure the proper functioning of the equipment and carry out daily checks (greasing, levels, cleaning, checking of safety components).
  • Report any anomalies or malfunctions
  • Respect operating procedures, safety, quality and environmental protection rules.

Profile

You have experience driving machinery in the environmental, waste, public works, quarries or industry sectors. You control the conduct of a grapple shovel and/or loader. You are the holder of the valid CACES construction equipment. You are autonomous, rigorous and appreciate teamwork. You are sensitive to recycling and environmental issues. Gear Driver / Temporary
