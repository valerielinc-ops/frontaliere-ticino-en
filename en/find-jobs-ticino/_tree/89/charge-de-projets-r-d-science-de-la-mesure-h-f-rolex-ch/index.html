Full description

Introduction

For our Research & Development division, we are looking for an R&D Project Manager in Measurement Science (M/F) whose main mission will be to define, qualify and operate equipment dedicated to measuring the accuracy and reliability of our watches, thus contributing to the achievement of high quality requirements.

Responsibilities

  • Identify metrological needs and define appropriate methods and measurement methods, in collaboration with project teams.
  • Develop equipment testing plans and qualification protocols.
  • Organize, monitor and supervise test campaigns, including the identification of reference piece requirements.
  • Develop tools for processing and analysing measurement data.
  • Analyze results, assess their relevance, and prepare qualification reports.
  • Ensure planning and reporting of activities, while proposing improvements for existing equipment.
  • Contribute to the definition and application of business rules within the team.

Profile

  • Master's degree in physics, microtechnics or equivalent training.
  • Experience in metrology, including experience plans, uncertainty budgets and R&R studies.
  • Strong data analysis skills and knowledge of associated tools.
  • An experience in integrating sensors and actuators, an asset.
  • An organized, autonomous and rigorous person with a strong spirit of analysis and critical common sense.
  • Excellent team spirit, relationship ease and clear communication.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
