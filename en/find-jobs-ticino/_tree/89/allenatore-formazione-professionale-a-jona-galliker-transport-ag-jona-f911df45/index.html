Full description

Chauffeur/vocational trainer (a) Jona At our location in Jona, we carry out general cargo and partial cargo transports with a balanced vehicle fleet of around 40 vehicles. Our catchment area extends throughout Switzerland with the exception of peripheral regions where we work with our distribution network. Our customers include well-known companies from the region and transport various goods of all kinds. You love life on the road, keep a cool head even in tight corners and want to pass on your valuable knowledge to the next generation? Then you are just right! We are looking for an experienced and motivated personality who accompanies our learning road transport professionals on their way to becoming a professional and at the same time actively participates in day-to-day business. Task area

  • Distribution of general cargo
  • as a knowledge mediator and mentor, you train our learners in practice and teach them the tricks of everyday work
  • As a preliminary model, you will convey safety standards, economical driving and the correct handling of the vehicles and the load
  • Assistance in loading the trucks
  • Ensure daily order and cleanliness in the transhipment warehouse
  • Compliance with the correct handling of the transported goods and an expert loading activity
  • Compare transport goods with transport documents and, if necessary, correct them
  • Active promotion and support of our learners during training

Requirement profile

  • CE driving licence
  • Industry knowledge in logistics and road transport
  • Basic course for trainers
  • Very good knowledge of German (word / writing)
  • Professional appearance and pleasure in customer contact
  • Reliable, flexible, independent, capable of teamwork, forward-looking, quality-conscious
  • a lot of passion, initiative and enthusiasm for the transfer of knowledge and to our learners

Offer

  • top modern infrastructure
  • internal and external training opportunities
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Free employee parking
