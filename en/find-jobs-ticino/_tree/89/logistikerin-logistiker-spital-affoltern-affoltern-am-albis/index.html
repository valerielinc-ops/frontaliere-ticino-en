Full description

Their tasks

  • Ensuring seamless and quality-assured station supply
  • They support the installation and conversion of bearings and ensure their order, cleanliness and optimal use
  • They receive deliveries and ensure their correct distribution throughout the hospital
  • They check the daily arriving goods, book them and store them in the right place or forward them directly
  • In case of delivery deviations, you clarify matters independently with the purchase and other contact persons
  • Performing the correct control of the expiry dates of consumables
  • Execution of administrative work (order monitoring, returns, inventory, communication with internal bodies) on the PC or by telephone
  • Professional disposal of waste and recyclables Your profile
  • You have a degree as a logistician / logistician EFZ or an equivalent education
  • You have several years of professional experience in logistics, ideally in the hospital environment
  • They work independently, reliably, structured and exact and also like to physically participate
  • They are customer- and service-oriented, work fast and keep an overview even in hectic situations
  • You have MS-Office knowledge and a very good knowledge of German Our culture – Your benefits «Bi öis can mer no»: With us it is family and personal.
