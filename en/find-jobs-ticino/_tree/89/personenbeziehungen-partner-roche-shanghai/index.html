The Position Position: People Relations Partner Location: Shanghai, China Scope: All legal entities of China

About the Role We are looking for a proactive and collaborative People Relations (PR) Partner. By collaborating with People & Culture (P&C) colleagues, you will provide trusted solutions to elevate the employee experience, empower business leaders to support and drive organizational changes, and ensure a fair, inclusive, and psychologically safe workplace.

Key Responsibilities

  • Change Management: Work together with other P&C colleagues and act as a sparring partner to drive organizational transformation and business changes.
  • Employee Relations & Conflict Resolution: Consult on complex performance and health issues; lead conflict prevention and resolution; and address general employee queries regarding working relationships.
  • Policy & Compliance: Review HR contracts/agreements, design and evaluate PR policies, and manage communications with employee representative bodies (e.g., labor unions) and relevant government authorities.
  • Investigations & Corrective Actions: Conduct workplace incident investigations, provide compliance-based corrective recommendations, and prepare for potential labor dispute litigation.
  • Enablement & Insights: Deliver PR-related training, and leverage data and technology to generate insights that inform management decision-making.

Qualifications & Experience

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Law, Business, or related fields.
  • 8+ years of working experience in multinational companies, including at least 3 years dedicated to Employee/Labor Relations. Experience in a healthcare commercial organization is highly preferred.
  • Fluent English skills (reading, writing, listening, speaking); experience in designing and drafting corporate-level policies is preferred.
  • Expert knowledge of relevant employment legislation, with proven success in leading organizational transformations or reorganizations.
  • Outstanding consultation, influencing, and negotiation skills; an enterprise mindset with the ability to properly handle highly sensitive employee relations crises.
  • Passionate about building a diverse and inclusive work culture, and enhancing psychological safety and a sense of belonging within the team.
