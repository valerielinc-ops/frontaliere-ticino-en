Siamo una PMI con circa 30 collaboratori e forniamo ai nostri clienti dell’industria e dell’edilizia, in Svizzera e all’estero, prodotti di alta qualità per la tecnica di ventilazione, climatizzazione e fissaggio, oltre a utensili e materiali di consumo.

  • Il tuo obiettivo principale è costruire partnership durature. Grazie alla tua empatia e alla tua capacità di persuasione individui nuovi potentiali di vendita e ampli costantemente il tuo portafoglio clienti.

Technical field service consultant for the Ticino (m/f/d), 100%

Entry as of now or by arrangement

Airproduct AG is a Swiss trade and service company for the HVAC sector (heating, ventilation, air conditioning, sanitation) with headquarters in Oberwil\-Lieli near Zurich. We are an SME with around 30 employees and supply our customers from industry and construction in Switzerland and abroad with high-quality products in ventilation, air conditioning and fastening technology as well as tools and consumables.

In order to strengthen our sales team in Switzerland, we are immediately looking for a convincing and sensitive personality with a strong service orientation and solid administrative skills (create offers, evaluate tenders, record orders in the ERP\ system, deal with Office\ programs, etc.). After training at our headquarters, you will work in Ticino, with the customers and in the home office.

Tasks

  • You are in daily contact with our customers, on the construction site, in the warehouse or in the office, and meet construction managers, project managers and other contact persons. On site, you present our products, explain how they work, propose tailor-made solutions and show their advantages. So you convince the customers of their added value, negotiate the conditions and conclude contracts.
  • You work close to the market: You take up the needs and suggestions for improvement from users and pass them on to product management.
  • Your main goal is to build lasting partnerships. With your empathy and persuasiveness, you recognize new sales potential and continuously expand your customer base.

Profile

  • You have experience in the field or in a structured sales activity.
  • You are motivated to independently expand your customer base and increase sales.
  • You have a good technical understanding
  • Industry experience and product knowledge are beneficial.
  • You enjoy working in a team, are communicative and have a strong flair for attracting new customers.
  • You appreciate the daily customer contact and regular trips in the area.
  • Languages: Italian (native language) and German (advanced level).

We offer

  • A family working environment in a growing SME in the HVAC sector.
  • Flat hierarchies and interesting development and career opportunities.
  • Various benefits and personnel discounts.
  • Healthy food: fresh fruit, FELFEL\ refrigerator, drinks and more.
  • Social and insurance benefits above the legal minimum.
  • Work clothes free of charge.
  • Business vehicle, IT equipment, expense compensation and much more.
  • Above-average wage with profit-sharing.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
