Full description

Their tasks

  • They are oriented towards the patient’s treatment goals and accompany and treat mentally ill people with sports therapeutic methods.
  • They carry out sports therapeutic activities (game sports, functional training, fitness, Pilates, etc.) in individual and group setting
  • You work interdisciplinary with the different stations
  • They document treatment courses in the clinic's electronic patient dossier

Our requirement profile

  • You have a bachelor's or master's degree in sports and movement sciences or sports, exercise and health of a state college, college of applied sciences or university
  • You have experience in guiding and accompanying people with mental illness
  • You have knowledge and skills in the administrative field
  • You are an innovative, creative and self-employed personality
  • You have a high degree of empowerment and bring professional skills

We offer you

  • You take on a varied and responsible job in a motivated, interdisciplinary team
  • You can contribute your ideas and shape your field of responsibility
  • You benefit from progressive working conditions (above average social benefits, holiday credits of at least 30 days)
  • You have the opportunity to attend further education courses
