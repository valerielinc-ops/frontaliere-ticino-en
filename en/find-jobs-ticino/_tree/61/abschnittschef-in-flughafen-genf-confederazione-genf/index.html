Put to the Point

A leadership position at operational level and in the midst of an international environment: Do you want to take on a leadership position with responsibility in a dynamic, international and constantly evolving environment? Do you like to make decisions, motivate teams and actively contribute to achieving operational and strategic goals? Then apply to the Federal Office for Customs and Border Security (BAZG) as Head of Department at Geneva Airport. As Head of Department, you are responsible for the operational and organizational management of your department. You are responsible for using your employees and the funds entrusted to you effectively and efficiently, as well as for achieving the sector-specific goals. Become part of a changing organization where your ability to motivate a team, make decisions and accompany change processes directly contribute to the quality and efficiency of the services provided. Are you ready for this challenge? We look forward to your application.

Security for the population, economy and state

The Federal Office for Customs and Border Security provides comprehensive security at the border – for the benefit of the population, the economy and the state. It is well connected nationally and internationally. Its core tasks include the proper and swift handling of commercial goods, the speedy handling of travel, the areas of security police and migration, and it has its own investigative authority with law enforcement. The BAZG is in the midst of digital transformation. The DaziT program simplifies, optimizes and digitizes all customs, duties and control processes.
