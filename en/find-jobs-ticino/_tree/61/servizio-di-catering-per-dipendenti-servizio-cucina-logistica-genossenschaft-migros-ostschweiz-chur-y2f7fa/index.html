Full description

The AREON Event Hall Chur opens in autumn 2026 – be there from the beginning! As an employee in the catering service, you are the heartbeat of our events and provide dedication and charm for the well-being of our guests. Whether for large or exclusive events: With a passion for hospitality, you make every event a highlight. Become part of the AREON family - "kämä Erfolgrich"!

Luogo di lavoro: Catering Services Migros, Catering AREON Eventhalle Chur, Pulvermühlestrasse 86, 7000 Chur

Important information

The assignments are based on the event utilization and take place in consultation with the employees.

Mansioni

  • Depending on the application area, you can take on tasks in the service, in the kitchen or in logistics at various events
  • Serving food and drinks, supervising guests, presenting and managing food in the buffets
  • Preparation of cold and hot food in the manufacturing kitchen
  • Establishment and dismantling of events (infrastructure/dishware, etc.)

Requirements

  • Professional experience: an advantage in gastronomy
  • Completed basic or further education: advantageous in gastronomy
  • You are flexible in working time management
  • We can rely on you and convince you through a friendly and open-minded approach
  • You like to work in a team - because this is the only way we are "tämä Successful"
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • People at the center: We maintain a strong identity and offer meaningful activities
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers and events
