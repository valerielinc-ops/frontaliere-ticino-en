Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the gerontopsychiatric services offered by Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse range of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in outpatient, day clinic and inpatient settings. The group employs around 1800 people and generated sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.

For our Privé Station D2 with a treatment focus on psychotherapy, we are looking for a

The range of therapies on this private ward is aimed at patients with depression, burnout and stress crises, anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders, sleep disorders and psychosomatic complaints. In addition to a high therapeutic treatment quality, with a focus on psychotherapeutic offers, patients are offered a courteous and cultivated atmosphere. In addition to occupational therapy and physiotherapy, the range of therapies also includes complementary medical offers and psychosocial tasks. Our patients are cared for in close cooperation with the case therapist in the caregiver system. The atmosphere on the ward is characterized by a respectful attitude and the careful cultivation of interpersonal relationships.
