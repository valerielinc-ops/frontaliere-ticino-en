Full description

Do you want to help patients return to their familiar environment? We offer a training position as a professional nurse HF with an annual salary starting at 40'300 CHF. Your main responsibilities include: As a future professional nurse HF, you are responsible for our patients from their arrival to their departure. You will learn to shape the care process so that our patients experience holistic rehabilitation. This includes collecting patient data, organizing and performing nursing and medical-technical activities, as well as documenting and evaluating them. With your professional assessment and analytical skills, you will actively work in an interdisciplinary team. What you should bring:

  • For the three-year training: completed vocational training with federal professional certificate or high school diploma
  • For the two-year training: health and social care assistant (AS) with federal vocational certificate (FC)
  • Passed aptitude test, e.g. at the Higher School of Health and Social Care in Aarau (the aptitude test process can also start during the application process)
  • Enjoyment of working with and for people
  • Motivation to accompany and support patients during their recovery process
  • High degree of personal initiative and independence, as well as good observation and reflection skills
  • Very good knowledge of German (C1 level) What we offer: During your training, you will be supported and promoted by our committed training team to successfully complete your training. You will also benefit from our employee benefits.
  • Inspiring and collaborative working atmosphere: Work in a supportive and respectful team that is committed to the best possible patient care
  • Room for innovative ideas and long-term involvement opportunities
  • Better social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Discounts: Use discounts at various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealerships, pharmacies, and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free entry: Enjoy free entry and other benefits at the thermal bath FORTYSEVEN An internship is possible at any time. We look forward to your application. Your contact:
