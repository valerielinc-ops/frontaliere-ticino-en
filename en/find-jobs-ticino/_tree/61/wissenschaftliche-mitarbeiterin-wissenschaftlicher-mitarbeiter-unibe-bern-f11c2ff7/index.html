Tasks

The aim of the Interfaculty Centre for Educational Research (ICER) is to promote analytical-empirical knowledge in educational research at the interface with educational policy. A central task of ICER is the coordination of large-scale assessments (LSA), including the “monitoring of basic competencies” (www.monitoring-der-grundkompetenzen.ch) and the “Programme of International Student Assessment” (PISA, www.pisa-schweiz.ch).

  • They support the program management in the (further) development of the overall design of the monitoring of the basic competencies.
  • They develop and further develop didactic principles for monitoring the basic competencies, such as the specialist frameworks, in exchange with other employees, cooperation partners and experts (e.g. from school practice) from the different language regions of Switzerland.
  • They independently coordinate specialist groups and sub-projects (e.g. the design of threshold setting procedures or the specification of the description of basic competencies)
  • They operate the interface to other specialist groups as well as to program and other sub-project management independently (e.g. in the reviews of the specialist frameworks by experts or in the implementation of the threshold setting procedures).
  • They participate in the acquisition, conception and implementation of research projects in educational research.
  • They write scientific reports and publications and contribute to the transfer of knowledge between research, school practice and education policy.
