The Position EHS Engineer Projects / SHE Partner CAPEX Projects: Professional for Occupational Safety (m/f/d) Your new team At the Management Mannheim/Penzberg site, we are a good 1,300 specialists for a wide variety of infrastructure and professional services, which they provide for the Mannheim and Penzberg sites. In a dynamic environment, we promote seamless collaboration across business units and functions, offer innovative solutions and improve the working environment of employees at the Mannheim and Penzberg sites. In this role, you are part of Risk Management & SHE Compliance, and you belong to the team SHE Partnering Mannheim. Here we assume with real passion responsibility for people, the environment and society. We not only manage safety, we empower our business partners on site: through proactive advice and training in all SHE issues as well as through tailor-made, pragmatic roadmaps for risk assessments and inspections. We also support the company in partnership for large CAPEX projects, so that our high standards are successfully lived on the factory site. What makes us special? Despite our local responsibility at the Mannheim plant, we are connected by a close, cross-location exchange and a strong team spirit with colleagues in adjacent teams. We work virtually and real hand in hand. Responsibility | This is what awaits you Taken over in the role You are responsible for ensuring occupational safety at the site by advising on, implementing and reviewing future-oriented safety concepts in new and conversion projects (Capex) from early planning to execution on the construction site. In addition, you will take on the following exciting tasks:

  • Project consulting & partnership: You advise project teams in partnership regarding occupational safety, process and plant safety as well as the reduction of accident numbers and occupational health hazards.
  • Legal Occupational Safety: You fully perform the tasks of an appointed occupational safety specialist (SiFa) in the project and ensure compliance with relevant regulations (e.g. BetrSichV, GefStoffV).
  • Document Management & Coordination: You are responsible for the creation, testing, moderation and legally secure archiving (SAP / DVS) of SHE concepts, construction site security concepts, checklists and reviews.
  • Scientific assessments: You are responsible for well-founded investigations as well as biological, thermal or toxicological assessments in the workplace as a basis for seamless risk assessments in the project environment.
  • Audits & visits: You act as a SHE contact partner beyond your own area, plan project- and site-specific security visits as well as audits and keep deviations consistently up to the realization.
  • Cultural work & coaching: You coach and moderate programs to continuously improve our proactive safety culture (behavior-based safety) and pass on your knowledge in subject-specific training. Qualifications | This is what you bring
  • You have successfully completed a degree (bachelor/master) in engineering (e.g. process engineering, chemistry, civil engineering, safety engineering), a master in natural sciences or a comparable technical qualification and have the training as a professional for occupational safety according to § 7 AS
