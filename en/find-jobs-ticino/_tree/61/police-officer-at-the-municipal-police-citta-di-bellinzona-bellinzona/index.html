Full description

General Administration Dicastery Human Resources In Piazza 10 6528 Camorino AVVISO DI CONCORSO FOR PERSONAL ANSUNTION The City Hall of Bellinzona opens a public competition for the appointment, with an occupational degree of 100%, of: a Agent and police at the Municipal Police (with “Agent” is meant the function and not the degree). Main tasks: - carry out tasks as police officers within the Outer Service of the City of Bellinzona Police Corps; - perform other services according to the needs.

  • - Federal professional certificate (APF) as police officer; - licence to conduct category B.
  • Aptitude and preferential requirements:
  • availability to provide service for shiftwork, on weekends (Saturdays and Sundays), on public holidays, during nights and for non-clockwork;
  • attitude to group work;
  • adaptability, flexibility, confidentiality, personal responsibility and discretion;
  • good psycho-physical skills;
  • good skills in the use of computer programs;
  • Italian mother tongue and knowledge of national languages;
  • availability to obtain specializations in relations at the Service Maintenance Order (MO);
  • license to conduct category A, or willingness to pursue it. Documents to be produced: 1. accompanied letter; 2. curriculum vitae; 3. copy of the identity document; 4. Photography format jpg; 5. copy of the certificates of study (with exam notes) and work; 6. self-certification relating to the judicial box; 7. self-certification relating to the certificate of solvency (UEF); 8. self-certification relating to health. Self-certification is available on www.bellinzona.ch. Entry into service:
  • date to be agreed. Special conditions:
  • the recruitment refers to the Organic Regulation of employees of the City of Bellinzona (ROD);
  • the obligations and duties of the municipal police officer are those provided by the Law and the Police Regulation as well as the ROD and any internal regulations to the Municipality;
  • applications with incomplete documentation will not be taken into account;
  • will not be considered valid offers and documents sent in paper, fax or via e-mail. Wages conditions:
  • the conditions of the ROD and the Regulation concerning the uniformity of the ranks and salary conditions of the cantonal and municipal police bodies (RUGraS). Deadline:
  • employment offers accompanied by the requested documents must be sent through the application form online by Monday 05 October 2026, at 23.59. This notice of competition is also visible on www.bellinzona.ch/asks and can be found at the multifunctional offices of the districts. For further information please contact the Municipal Police, Vicecomandante Iten Andrea Cremonini (tel. 058 203 25 32). The City Hall reserves the right not to proceed with the absence of candidates/responsible for the purpose. THE MUNICIPIO Bellinzona, 18 September 2026
