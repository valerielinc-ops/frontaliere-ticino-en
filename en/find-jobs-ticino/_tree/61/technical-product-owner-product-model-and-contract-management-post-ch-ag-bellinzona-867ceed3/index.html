Full description

Do you want to help shape how products and agreements in logistics will be managed digitally, modularly and consistently in the future? In this role, IT is responsible for technical product management for the modular product model with product management system as well as for the contract management system. You combine the technical target images with architecture, platform, delivery and operation and bring the commercial stack from proof of concept into a robust implementation.

For one of our teams, we are looking for a committed personality who takes initiative, structures complex relationships and promotes viable solutions together with different specialist areas. With you, we bring together different specialist areas and develop viable solutions for product and contract management.

*You can do it *

  • You will develop the technical product vision, roadmap and the prioritized backlog for product management and contract management system together with business owners, business engineering, architecture and implementation teams.
  • You are responsible for the end-to-end delivery capability of the IT solution from product and service cataloging to product offerings to structured, versioned agreement data and the contract lifecycle.
  • You translate technical requirements and ZAM design principles into actionable features and enablers and ensure clear acceptance criteria, dependencies and release goals.
  • In addition, you ensure integration with pricing, billing, CRM, customer channels, operational systems, APIs, events and data products and clarify mastership, limits of responsibility and transition architectures.
  • You control manufacturers and internal delivery teams, prepare technical decisions and ensure quality, operational readiness, safety, maintainability and lifecycle management.
  • In addition, you measure the impact on the basis of concrete reference products and contracts and gradually develop the solution based on the MVP findings.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of experience as Technical Product Owner, IT Product Manager or in a comparable role for complex business solutions.
  • You will also have experience with product catalogs, product information management, CPQ/CPQC or contract lifecycle management as well as the introduction of standard software.
  • You can safely handle agile product and delivery models, backlog management, prioritization, MVPs, dependencies, and stakeholder decision-making.
  • You also have a good understanding of domain models, APIs, events, data responsibility, integration, and non-functional requirements.
  • You are able to align business, architecture, development, operations and suppliers with a common goal and to decide conflicts of goals transparently.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German and English. Every other national language is a plus.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
