Full description

Vaudoise is one of the leading Swiss insurance companies and an attractive and future-oriented employer. In keeping with its cooperative identity and values, Vaudoise creates a healthy, inclusive and inspiring working environment in which you can develop further. The following exciting tasks await you:

  • You will lead, prioritize and organize the activities of a team of nine specialists
  • Through close support in everyday life as well as targeted professional support, you create the conditions for joint success
  • They translate strategic guidelines into clear goals and ensure the sustainable management of team performance
  • You promote the further development of your employees, strengthen their independence and ensure the uniform application of the underwriting guidelines
  • You edit selected underwriting dossiers and participate in technical decisions
  • They work closely with the other teams in the field and engage in cross-sectoral projects This is what distinguishes you:
  • Swiss Federal Insurance Professional Card or equivalent education
  • In-depth professional experience in property insurance for corporate clients as well as a strong understanding of the assessment and subscription of risks
  • First or already sound leadership experience as well as the ability to make decisions and take responsibility
  • Mother tongue German or French (or level C2) as well as very good knowledge of the other language (at least level B2, ideally C1) in word and writing.
  • Secure handling of the common MS Office applications If you are also a committed, available and structured leader, show initiative and convince through your strong customer orientation as well as your positive and solution-oriented way of thinking, then you may be exactly the person we are looking for. We offer you:
  • A pleasant working environment in a cooperative company that has been voted the best employer several times

  • Responsible management tasks with scope for design
  • An appreciative corporate culture that focuses on the well-being of employees We look forward to your complete application documents by electronic means (with CV, letter of motivation, copy of employment certificates and diplomas).
