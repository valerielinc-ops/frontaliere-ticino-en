Full description

Thanks to its 800 employees, the Intercantonal Hospital of Broye (HIB) provides an acute care and rehabilitation mission. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative health institution striving for excellence for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges that await it, the HIB is looking for a: Nurse at the Treatment and Rehabilitation Center from 80% to 100% Your mission

  • Guarantee preventive, curative and educational nursing care through mastery of clinical evaluation with patients
  • Ensure patient care by providing autonomous nursing care in a logic of safety, efficiency and comfort
  • Carry out procedures medical delegates and master the resulting technical actions
  • Actively participate in the evaluation, analysis of clinical situations and the development of care projects
  • Guarantee the effectiveness of therapeutic projects by integrating the requirements of interdisciplinary work into your daily practice
  • Ensure good information and the quality of reception for patients and their loved ones
  • Be proactive and ensure initiatives to improve the efficiency of care
  • Apply and enforce the rules hospital hygiene and safety in force in the institution Your assets
  • You have a HES diploma in general care (or title deemed equivalent)
  • Experience in rehabilitation would be an asset
  • Proactive in nature, your technical, organizational and relational skills allow you to offer quality care
  • With a good sense of communication, you have a marked interest in multidisciplinary collaboration and demonstrate speed and precision in the execution of your tasks
  • For this position, an excellent command of French (spoken and written) as well as IT tools are essential Entry into office: to be agreed.
