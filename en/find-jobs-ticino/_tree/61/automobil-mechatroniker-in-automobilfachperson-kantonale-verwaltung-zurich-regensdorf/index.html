In the field of Economics & Labour (W&A) of JVA Pöschwies, trained persons work in various companies and work areas. Work is an important part of the process and makes a valuable contribution to reintegration into society. For our car garage in the area of Economics & Labour (W&A), we are looking for a committed personality who combines technical expertise with a high level of social competence. You do that with us.

  • You supervise and accompany instructed persons in everyday work, guide them professionally and promote their professional and personal development
  • Regular, constructive feedback on work and behavior as well as documentation of individual development are also part of your tasks
  • You carry out maintenance, maintenance and repair work on company-owned and private vehicles and equipment
  • Promoting the overall personality in the focus area of work with the aim of reintegration
  • With your expertise and practical experience, you support the execution of work orders and control the results
  • The promotion of self-employment and the personal skills of the admitted persons is important to you. This is an important contribution to reintegration.
  • Together with the other areas, you ensure safety and order in everyday work.
  • Completed training as an automotive mechatronics engineer or automotive specialist EFZ, specialising in passenger cars
  • You bring several years of professional experience in the maintenance, maintenance and repair of vehicles
  • With the common MS Office applications you work safely and independently
  • Your high level of social skills and calm, calm manner help you to keep track even in demanding situations.
  • You communicate very well verbally and in writing in German
  • Further language skills are advantageous
  • Physical and psychological resilience as well as a high sense of responsibility distinguish you
  • An impeccable reputation (no entries in the criminal and enforcement register) and a minimum age of 25 round off your profile Your contacts Still questions?
