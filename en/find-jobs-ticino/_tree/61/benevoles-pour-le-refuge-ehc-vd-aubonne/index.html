Full description

Located in the heart of the Aubonne Hospital, Le Refuge is a new palliative care unit project of Ensemble Hospitalier de la Côte (EHC), a warm and soothing place. Inspired by the world of mountain refuges, it is intended to offer patients and their loved ones a welcoming environment, where listening, presence and moments of sharing occupy an essential place. Much more than a place of care, Le Refuge wants to preserve the quality of life and foster human ties, respecting the rhythm and needs of each person.

In order to live the family lounge and to accompany patients and their relatives, we seek:

2 to 4 volunteers for the Refuge - Palliative care (M/F)

Start as soon as possible or agree.

WE OFFER YOU

  • A rewarding human experience in a team of committed and caring volunteers.
  • Regular engagement, one day a week or every two weeks, depending on your availability.
  • An unpaid volunteer activity, with compensation for kilometric costs.
  • Reimbursement of compulsory training costs after two years of volunteering, according to the conditions defined.

YOU MAY

  • A real desire to engage with people in palliative care and their loved ones.
  • Essential human qualities: empathy, listening, discretion, respect and benevolence.
  • Regular availability and willingness to invest in the Refuge.
  • The motivation needed to complete the mandatory 6-day training with Volunteer Vaud, initially at your charge (minimum presence of 5 days out of 6)
  • No prior experience in care is required. Your commitment and your human qualities are essential.

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Live the family lounge by helping to create a warm, welcoming and friendly space.
  • Provide a attentive presence, benevolent listening and comfort to patients and their loved ones.
  • Promote exchanges, meetings and moments of sharing, respecting the rhythm and wishes of each.
  • Participate in the reception of relatives and contribute to their well-being throughout their presence in the Refuge.
  • Collaborate with the Refuge team, respecting the role of everyone, without intervening in care.

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
