You have your graduation in your pocket and are interested in computer science or studying at a university is too theoretical for you? With our practice-integrated Bachelor's degree in Computer Science (PiBS), you can apply your newly acquired university knowledge directly at the pulse of computer science and are prepared to support the BIT from the second year of study in advancing digitization in Switzerland. You can make this contribution

  • Monitor information technology infrastructure and develop security measures to ensure data security

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
