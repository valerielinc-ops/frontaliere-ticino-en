Put to the Point

The Swiss population has a safe place of protection at all times – thanks to you! With your commitment, you make a significant contribution to securing and further developing these critical infrastructures and actively contribute to ensuring the operational readiness and resilience of Switzerland’s protective facilities in the future.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20956

You can make this contribution

  • supervise new construction, renovation and extension projects of protective buildings in cooperation with the cantons and planners; conduct negotiations, check, control, monitor and monitor the project flow until completion
  • Develop concepts for the further development of the systems in the construction work section and lead projects, represent experts in scientific and technical working groups
  • Planning, monitoring and checking payment for maintenance flat rates of all cantons
  • Advise public authorities, building owners, planners and internal bodies as an expert and organise and carry out technical training
  • Analyze technical developments, develop, plan and implement strategies and measures for protective systems

This makes them unique.

  • University degree / University of Applied Sciences degree as civil engineer or diploma as construction manager and proven proof of performance
  • Proven project management and management experience of protective construction projects
  • Technical understanding in the areas of HVAC, power and telematics, as well as willingness to acquire the necessary special knowledge for civil protection protective buildings
  • Communication and negotiation skills, persuasion and assertiveness, ability to produce written reports, concepts and studies
  • Open and initiative personality with a strong sense of interdisciplinary work, as well as affinity in the areas of IT and digitalization
  • Active knowledge in two official languages, passive knowledge of the third official language desired

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

The Federal Office for Civil Protection BABS is the nationally recognized coordination office for civil protection between federal, cantonal and third parties. It provides fundamentals and coordination, particularly in the areas of risk analysis, warning and alerting, training, telematics and protective infrastructure, as well as disaster medicine, ABC protection and disarmament. Together with the cantons and other national and international partners, it has the capabilities, current technologies and infrastructure for dealing with disasters and emergencies.
