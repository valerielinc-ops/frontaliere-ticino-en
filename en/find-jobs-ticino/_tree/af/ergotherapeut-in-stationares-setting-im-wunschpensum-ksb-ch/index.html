Full description

In the inpatient area, you accompany patients with acute and chronic diseases on their way back to everyday life. Together with an interdisciplinary treatment team, you plan and design individual ergotherapeutic measures and thus contribute to a holistic care. The position is available immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • You treat inpatients with different medical conditions.
  • You work closely with the interdisciplinary treatment team.
  • You plan, document and evaluate ergotherapeutic treatments.
  • You create electronic history documentation and reports.
  • You actively contribute your technical expertise to the treatment and exchange in the team.
  • You can actively participate in projects if you are interested.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as an occupational therapist FH.
  • You enjoy working in an interdisciplinary environment and appreciate working with different professional groups.
  • You are enthusiastic about the ergotherapeutic treatment of patients with acute and chronic diseases.
  • You meet people with appreciation, motivate them on their way and promote their independence.
  • You work independently, responsibly and have a high level of social skills.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
