Full description

The activity includes the maintenance and repair of vehicles such as forklifts and tugs. Complex technical systems and faults are systematically analyzed and corrected. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Maintenance of forklifts, tugs and other vehicles
  • Perform demanding repairs independently
  • Fix malfunctions in mechanical, hydraulic and electronic systems
  • Systematic diagnosis of errors in vehicle technology
  • Accompany technical questions and complaints professionally

  • Knowledge of industrial trucks would be desirable
