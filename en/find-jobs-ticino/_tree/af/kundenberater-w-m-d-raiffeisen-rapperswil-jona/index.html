Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Rapperswil-Jona, a successful and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Rapperswil-Jona SG and offices in Egg ZH, Hombrechtikon ZH and Rüti ZH, we are looking for a committed, service-oriented and independent personality as customer advisor (m/f/d) for our headquarters in Rapperswil-Jona SG.

What awaits you?

  • Comprehensive and needs-based advice to customers on the topics of basic products, savings, payments, investment and provision
  • Operation and comprehensive customer service at our consulting desk
  • Active cross-selling in all products including fund savings
  • Demand recognition and forwarding of customers to the responsible expert advisors
  • Proactive implementation of sales and market development measures

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training, experience in a comparable function (client advice)
  • Enjoyment of intensive customer contact
  • Service-oriented thinking and action
  • Winning, competent and professional appearance
  • Good communication and social skills
  • Reliability, trustworthiness, resilience
  • Great flexibility and teamwork

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed.
