Municipality of Serravalle info@serravalle.ch Via Orino 11 6713 Malvaglia Municipio 091 870 1145 COMPETITION FOR THE RECRUITMENT OF A MUNICIPAL TECHNICAL COLLABORATOR The Municipality of Serravalle opens the competition for the recruitment of a collaborator at the municipal technical office at 100% employment under the conditions provided for by the Organic Regulations of Municipal Employees (ROD). Duties

  • management of procedures relating to private construction;
  • collaboration in the management of the external team, in the maintenance of infrastructures

public and planning practices;

  • collaboration in the implementation of specific projects under the competence of the Technical Office.

General requirements

  • Federal VET Diploma (EFZ) in the technical field or higher qualification;
  • editorial skills (reports, reports, correspondence, etc.);
  • collaborative attitude and spirit of initiative.

Preferential requirements

  • experience in the field of municipal technical office;
  • diploma of municipal technician or commitment to obtain it;
  • good computer skills, in particular with Office and Autocad applications.

We offer

  • dynamic, collaborative and flexible work environment;
  • opportunities for continuous training and prospects for professional and career growth.

Start and working hours As soon as possible or date to be agreed. Salary conditions The salary is defined by the ROD, class 6, of the scale valid for civil servants. The starting salary will be defined on the basis of professional experience and knowledge. Applications Applications in a sealed envelope with the external wording "Municipal technical competition" must be delivered by 1 7.00 a.m. on Friday 6 November 2026 accompanied by the following documents:

  • cover letter and curriculum vitae with photograph;
  • certificates of study, training and work;
  • health questionnaire (self-certification) to be downloaded from our website;
  • Solvency Certificate (UEF).

Applications sent by email or that do not meet the requirements will not be considered. Recruitment will take place at the sole discretion of the Municipality. The Serravalle Municipality, 14 October 202 6

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
