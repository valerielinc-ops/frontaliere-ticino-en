Identity & Access Management Administrator (80-100%)

Within the IT Operations department, you will join Lombard Odier’s Identity & Access Management (IAM) team, responsible for managing identities, access, and associated security for more than 3,000 employees.

In the context of a transformation toward Zero Trust, RBAC, and cloud/hybrid models (Entra ID / Azure AD), you will actively contribute to securing and automating the identity lifecycle.

YOUR ROLE

Identity & Access Management

  • Process and analyze access requests via ITSM tools (ServiceNow)
  • Ensure access provisioning / deprovisioning and resolve IAM incidents
  • Manage identities, accounts, and groups in Active Directory & Entra ID (Azure AD)
  • Administer access rights (NTFS, file shares, business applications)
  • Monitor Joiners / Movers / Leavers (JML) processe

Automation & Continuous Improvement

  • Contribute to IAM process automation (workflows, scripting, APIs)
  • Support the improvement of access models (RBAC / ABAC)
  • Integrate new applications into the IAM ecosystem (SSO, provisioning, federation)
  • Participate in the transition toward Zero Trust and identity-first architectures

Identity Governance & Security

  • Manage and improve access assignment workflows (IGA)
  • Participate in access recertification campaigns
  • Administer privileged access via a PAM solution (e.g., CyberArk, BeyondTrust, etc.)
  • Contribute to the management of sensitive access and secrets (Vault)
  • Support compliance controls and audits (FINMA, ISO, etc.)

Support & Collaboration

  • Provide Level 2/3 support on IAM-related issues
  • Collaborate with Security, Infrastructure, Application, and Risk teams
  • Write and maintain technical procedures and IAM documentation

YOUR PROFILE

  • Strong experience in IAM (Identity & Access Management)
  • Excellent knowledge of Active Directory (on-prem) and Entra ID / Azure AD
  • Good understanding of RBAC / ABAC concepts, SSO (SAML, OAuth2, OIDC), provisioning / SCIM
  • Experience with an IAM / IGA tool (e.g., SailPoint, One Identity, Saviynt, ServiceNow IAM)
  • Experience or exposure to PAM solutions
  • Strong knowledge of Windows Server environments and access security
  • Scripting / automation / GenAI knowledge (PowerShell, APIs, Copilot) is highly valued
  • Experience in a banking or regulated environment
  • Knowledge of Zero Trust / Identity Security models
  • Familiarity with ServiceNow and monitoring tools (Splunk, Grafana, Elastic, SIEM)
  • Experience in application integration (SSO / federation)
  • Understanding of Cloud & Hybrid Identity challenges
  • Attention to detail and strong security mindset
  • Analytical skills and ability to solve complex problems
  • Team player with excellent interpersonal skills
  • Autonomous and proactive
  • Ability to work in a demanding and evolving environment
  • Fluent French and professional English required
