Full description

Dipl. Physiotherapist or Dipl. Movement Therapist (50%) Sensitive and competent. We are a private clinic for psychosomatics, psychiatry and psychotherapy with 108 beds in a central location in Rheinfelden and two outpatients in Rheinfelden and Aarau. The clinic is recognized by FMH as a training centre for the specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy (2 years inpatient, 2 years outpatient), as well as by the SAPPM (2 years). Our treatments focus on individual and group psychotherapy. We design the therapeutic environment in the interprofessional team resource-oriented, including body and creative therapeutic offers. Somatic and psychosocial aspects are highly important. To strengthen our therapist team, we are looking for a Dipl. on November 1, 2026 or by agreement. Physiotherapist or Dipl. Movement therapy (50%) Their main task is movement-based group and individual therapy with patients in the stationary setting. Additional tasks include participation in interprofessional rapport, networking with case-leading therapists and the treatment team, as well as documentation of the therapy processes. A medium to long-term commitment is desirable. They are involved in the team of physiotherapy with regular team meetings. We expect a completed training and at least 5 years of professional experience. Clinical experience in a psychiatric institution is an advantage, and we also wish to have high professional and social competence as well as a flexible personality with pleasure in interprofessional cooperation.

  • We offer an autonomous work area, a pleasant work environment and attractive employment and training conditions based on a 42-hour week, 6 weeks vacation plus a training week as well as internal training.
