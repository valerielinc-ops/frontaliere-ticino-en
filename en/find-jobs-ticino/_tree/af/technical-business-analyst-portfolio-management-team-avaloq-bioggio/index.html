Technical Business Analyst - Portfolio Management Team — Avaloq

Full description

Founded and headquartered in Switzerland, Avaloq is continuously expanding its global footprint with around 2,500 colleagues in 12 countries, and more than 170 clients in 35 countries. We are an industry-leading provider of wealth management technology and services for financial institutions around the world, including private banks and wealth managers, investment managers, as well as retail and neo banks. Our research led approach and continual innovation is powered by the passion and creativity of our colleagues.

We are always looking for talented people to join us on our mission to orchestrate the financial ecosystem and democratize access to wealth management. Avaloq offers the opportunity to work closely with some of the world’s leading financial institutions as we jointly develop and shape careers. Championing a collaborative, supportive and flexible work environment empowers our colleagues to reach their full potential.

We are looking for a committed and competent Technical Business Analyst specializing in Portfolio Management and Rebalancer to carry out requirement analysis, solution design and parameterization (product customization) activities. We work closely with regional support, project and client teams, so in addition to being self-directed, we are looking for someone who enjoys collaborating with others. Your key tasks

  • Analyze business requirements, as well as design, develop and implement complex software systems under the Portfolio Management and Rebalancing module
  • Undertake testing activities as part of the development process to progress task completion and solution releasing post development completion
  • Participate in client and prospects workshops explaining Avaloq features and embracing clients' needs, evaluate possible scenarios and recommend best business and IT solutions
  • Actively manage client relationship by communicating regularly and providing business consulting services to Avaloq customers
  • Prepare reports and project documents concerning new client requirements, ongoing activities and project status

Qualifiche

  • Degree in IT, Computer Science, or related discipline
  • Minimum 5 years of relevant experience in IT or Financial Services industry
  • Solid business analysis skills and development experience working in the Portfolio Management domain with technical exposure working on applications
  • Well-developed verbal and written communication skills with a service-oriented attitude
  • Experience working within Agile processes
  • An affinity for self-improvement continuous learning

It would be a bonus if you have

  • ACCP Certification and Avaloq Parameterization experience in Projects (ABR-S based customization would be a plus)
  • Business Analysis experience
  • Experience with Portfolio Rebalancing
  • Exposure or experience in PL/SQL and/or any other programming language, ideally with some development skills from prior experience, and the ability to query
