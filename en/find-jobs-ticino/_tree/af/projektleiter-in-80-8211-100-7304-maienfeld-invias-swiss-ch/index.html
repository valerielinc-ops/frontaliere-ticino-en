Full description

PROJECTOR:IN 80 – 100% In order to continue and expand our success story, we are looking for an experienced personality as Project manager: in 80 – 100% immediately or by agreement at our location in Maienfeld What you do with us In this responsible position, you take over the management and further development of demanding construction projects – from the idea to the successful implementation. With your technical know-how, your organizational talent and your eye for the whole, you ensure that our projects are not only realized, but also become real success stories.

Your tasks include

  • You develop complex construction projects independently and contribute significantly to their success
  • You ensure compliance with legal regulations, especially with regard to occupational safety
  • You control projects taking into account deadlines, quality, effort and costs and solve any problems you encounter.
  • You coordinate planners and construction managers, promote cooperation in the team and create a motivating working environment
  • You bring projects in execution planning and implementation clearly to the point
  • You keep complex construction details traceable and ensure transparency and structure
  • You recognize the needs of our clients and put them into tangible results
  • You ensure that our projects meet the highest technical, economic and time-related standards

What you bring

  • Completed studies as a technician: in HF construction planning or a comparable training
  • Several years of experience in project and/or construction management
  • Sound technical understanding and enjoyment of complex tasks
  • Structured, networked and solution-oriented working method
  • Experienced use of common software tools
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action
  • Independent working methods and a high sense of responsibility

What we offer you With us, you can expect a varied role in which you can take on responsibility and contribute your own ideas with a lot of creative freedom. You work in a dynamic environment characterized by exciting projects in the areas of construction, innovation and sustainability. Our modern way of working, short decision-making paths and high independence offer you the opportunity to make a difference every day and develop yourself personally and professionally. In addition, attractive development prospects within our group of companies open up for you. Do you want to become part of our corporate culture and live our values – enthusiasm, dynamism, sustainability, connection and trust? Then become part of a dedicated team that builds a better future together! Have you become curious? We look forward to your contact.
