Full description

Freshness in focus, processes under control - At the traditional location Ostermundigen, we passionately produce popular classics such as Emmi Caffè Latte, the finest yogurt and refreshing glace. In a dynamic environment that stands for freshness, speed and the highest quality, we are looking for people who want to think, shape and grow with us.

You can do that.

  • You are responsible for the independent operation and monitoring of the plants in the yogurt preparation.
  • You ensure a smooth production process in terms of quality, quantity and deadlines.
  • You carry out recipe approaches according to production planning.
  • You take over cleaning and maintenance work on the plant and ensure consistent compliance with the specified hygiene and safety measures.
  • You support in the process of continuous improvement of the plants (CIP) and actively work on problem solving in the event of emerging disruptions.

Why do you succeed?

  • You have completed vocational training, ideally in the food sector or in a technical environment.
  • You gain points by first experience in the manufacturing industry.
  • You have a strong technical understanding and enjoy working with modern production facilities.
  • Your responsible, reliable and team capable personality will take you further with us.
  • You are flexible and ready to work in shift mode (3-4 shifts).
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing (min. B2).

This is what we offer you

  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Further education and development opportunities: At Emmi, the focus is on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You will receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
