Full description

You are communicative, solution-oriented and enjoy acting as a hub for our ICT customers? You don’t just want to edit tickets, but create real added value? Then you are exactly right with us! To strengthen our ICT Services division at the Olten site, we are looking for a motivated and future-oriented personality who would like to become part of a dynamic team.

You can move that with us.

  • First-level support with heart and mind: You are the first point of contact for all incoming requests – by phone, by e-mail or via our ticket system.
  • Analysis & Problem Solving: You collect and classify tickets, carry out initial analyses and solve problems and service requests independently.
  • Proactive communication: You always keep our customers informed and ensure transparency.
  • Teamwork: For more complex cases, work closely with 2nd and 3rd level support and coordinate troubleshooting.
  • Documentation: You create and update customer documentation to share knowledge.

That's what makes you.

  • Completed training as a computer scientist: in EFZ or ICT specialist EFZ.
  • Initial experience in IT support and knowledge of ITIL processes are a plus.
  • Fit in Microsoft 365 and Windows Workplaces.
  • High motivation, customer orientation and pleasure in service.
  • Readiness for piquetting.
  • Good knowledge of German and English.
  • Driving licence Cat B

That's what we offer you.

  • Varied tasks: No day is like the other – you are in the middle of it instead of just there.
  • Development opportunities: We promote your further education and offer perspectives for your career.
  • Modern working environment: top infrastructure and tools that make your work easier.
  • Team spirit: A collegial environment with a real DU culture and plenty of room for your own ideas.
  • Attractive benefits: Good social benefits and the security of an international corporation.
