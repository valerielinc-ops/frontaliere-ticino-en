WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

Mission

Attached to the Digital department of our Manufacture, your main mission will be to develop concrete solutions directly related to the needs of our businesses, and to successfully pilot the deployment of the already initiated roadmap. In a spirit of pragmatism and facilitation, you design agile solutions to simplify the day-to-day lives of our teams, especially in the field of execution logistics, while ensuring the proper execution of planned projects.

Your Key Responsibilities

Development and configuration of business applications: Design, configure and develop agile applications (especially on Low-Code/No-Code technologies) to respond reactively, tailor-made and concretely to the operational problems of our workshops.

Success and delivery of the roadmap: Take charge, pilot and complete the various steps of the digital roadmap already initiated, respecting the triptych CDQ (Cost, Time, Quality) and ensuring the transition to the field.

Engaged member of trade committees: Actively participate and be a key contributor to local committees with trades in order to share developments, capture the evolution of operational needs and consolidate new applications.

Ensuring continuity of service, support and enrichment: Ensure the operational support of deployed tools while actively piloting the enrichment and continuous improvement of existing solutions (updates, new features). Provide documentation to ensure efficient service continuity.

Continuous prioritisation and framing: Collaborate with the trades to capture needs, identify friction points on the ground and prioritize the sprints of achievements according to a logic of impact Gains / Efforts.

Relais Manufacture et Groupe: Be the relay of the Manufacture to the Groupe (Richemont / Cartier Industrie) to align local developments, accelerate digital transformation and enhance our success.
