What awaits you.

Looking for a job that makes sense in an innovative work environment? We have the offer you need! At the SBB, you have the opportunity to provide your know-how while acquiring new skills, which allow you to enrich yourself and progress professionally. To find out what other benefits we offer, such as children's allowances, free AG 2nd class or foreign travel at reduced prices (also for the family), visit our website under "Social benefits". What you can move.

  • To carry out all maintenance and construction work in the railway sector in full autonomy.
  • Mainly assume the role of team leader/fe.
  • As Chief/Security Officer, establish and monitor compliance with security measures in accordance with the security arrangements.
  • Report on the work carried out for the mandates, prepare the implementation report and verify the reports prepared by the partner companies.
  • Remove the inconveniences in connection with the organization of the picket service.

What you bring.

  • SWC or SWC learning in a craft sector. SSE team leader/fe school course completed successfully (VOS).
  • Provision for irregular working hours and category B driving licences.
  • Good user knowledge in computer science and written and oral knowledge in French.
  • Pleasure working outside.
  • Chief Security Officer according to TEN20100 is an asset.
