Full description

Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The post is attached to the Directorate of Care. The Care Branch embodies the authority for care. Its mission is to ensure the quality and safety of care within the institution. As such, the Directorate of Care has the right to look at all the activities of care (nurses, nurses and various health professionals). To fulfil its mission, it relies on three areas of expertise: quality, sustainability and digital innovation; clinical expertise and professional practices; management and transformation. The social service consists of several committed teams, operating in an environment rich in interprofessional collaborations. You intervene with patients who are hospitalized or in consultation faced with social, administrative, psychological or family difficulties, requiring a social assessment and close support of their care situation.

Description of post

Most of the population with accompaniment is in very precarious situations. Socio-financial assessments are frequent, as are regularisation, social benefits or coordination with the canton's aid schemes. In this context, active listening, psychosocial support, the ability to establish a quick bond of trust, identify issues and develop concrete and customized solutions are key competencies. You play a key role in ensuring access to care, continuity of journeys and human support that respects the social and administrative realities of patients and their families. You bring your expertise to the medical-care teams and ensure, in multidisciplinarity, the liaison with the intra- and extra-hospital network.

Qualifications

You hold a Bachelor's degree in social work or have an equivalent title. You have a successful experience in social work in hospitals. You have mastered the usual approaches to social work and the procedures for reporting to the Adult Protection Authority (ADPA). You have good knowledge of the Geneva social health network and social insurance systems. You are a person with excellent capacity to constructively defend social expertise in a demanding interdisciplinary environment. You demonstrate strong analytical, synthesis and discernment skills in sensitive or high humane situations, as well as excellent editorial skills and computer tools. Additional training for trainers, social insurance, psychology, experience in group coordination and language skills in Spanish and/or Portuguese are particularly appreciated.
