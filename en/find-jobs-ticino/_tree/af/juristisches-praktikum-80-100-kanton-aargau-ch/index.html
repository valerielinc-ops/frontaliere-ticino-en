Exciting tasks await

  • Participation in appeal proceedings before all instances
  • Comments on complaints against our decisions
  • Participation in on-site inspections and complaints hearings
  • Cooperation with other cantonal authorities
  • Addressing legal issues within the department
  • Support various departmental projects, such as drafting regulations and enforcement assistance

What you bring

  • Completed Law Studies (Master)
  • Good knowledge of public law, in particular spatial planning, environmental and administrative procedural law
  • Communicative and committed person
  • Self-employed and efficient working practices

In the Department of Construction, Transport and Environment, we are actively developing the diverse habitat Aargau in the areas of space, energy, environment and mobility – today and for future generations. At the Department of Building Permits, we examine complex building applications that require cantonal approval and draw up the corresponding decisions.
