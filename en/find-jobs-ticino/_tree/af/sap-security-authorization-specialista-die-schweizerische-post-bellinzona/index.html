Full description

Are you interested in SAP Security and modern authorization concepts? Are you excited about combining information security, automation and innovative SAP technologies? In this versatile role, you actively shape the security of our SAP landscape and contribute to a stable and compliant operation. You work closely with specialist departments, operations and development and contribute your know-how to challenging projects. With you, we strengthen the security and future viability of our SAP system landscape.

You can do that.

  • You support permission and security management across all relevant SAP systems in on-premises and cloud environments.
  • In addition, you develop, implement and test SAP roles as well as authorization models based on business requirements and bring your expertise to bear.
  • You manage user roles and permissions, ensure functional separation and support second- and third-level support in user access management.
  • In addition, you analyze authorization errors, derive suitable measures and provide expert advice to specialist departments and technical teams, which makes an important contribution to safe operation.
  • You will accompany users in SAP GRC and IAG processes as well as in role-related questions and evaluations.
  • In addition, you will process security incidents, new security requirements and revision requests and document the results in a comprehensible manner, which will enable you to contribute your expertise effectively.
  • You identify potential for artificial intelligence and automation in the SAP authorization environment and implement suitable solutions responsibly.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of professional experience as an SAP Professional or Senior Consultant in the area of functional security and authorizations.
  • In addition, you will bring in-depth experience with SAP ECC, SAP S/4HANA Private Cloud as well as in user and role management and convince us.
  • You are familiar with security management, the maintenance of SU24 tables and the development of role and authorization concepts.
  • In addition, you have extensive experience with SAP Fiori Launchpad, technical catalogues, business catalogues and the analysis of complex authorization errors, which allows you to bring your know-how optimally.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of SAP system architecture as well as practical experience with artificial intelligence and automation in the environment of SAP roles and authorizations.
  • In addition, you communicate very well verbally and in writing in German and have good oral knowledge in English, which makes you move safely in the working environment.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
