SAP Security and Authorization Specialist — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you interested in SAP security and modern authorization concepts? Are you enthusiastic about combining information security, automation and innovative SAP technologies? In this versatile role, you will actively help shape the security of our SAP landscape and contribute to stable and compliant operations. You will work closely with specialist departments, operations and development and contribute your expertise to challenging projects. With you, we will strengthen the security and future viability of our SAP system landscape.

What you can achieve

  • You support authorization and security management across all relevant SAP systems in on-premises and cloud environments.
  • In addition, you develop, implement and test SAP roles and authorization models based on business requirements and contribute your expertise in a targeted manner.
  • You manage user roles and authorizations, ensure segregation of duties and support second- and third-level support in user access management.
  • In addition, you analyze authorization errors, derive appropriate measures and provide specialist departments and technical teams with expert advice, thereby making an important contribution to secure operations.
  • You support users with SAP GRC and IAG processes as well as role-related questions and evaluations.
  • Furthermore, you handle security incidents, new security requirements and audit requests, and document the results in a comprehensible manner, thereby making effective use of your expertise.
  • You identify potential for artificial intelligence and automation in the SAP authorization environment and implement suitable solutions responsibly.

What you bring

  • You have several years of professional experience as an SAP Professional or Senior Consultant in the area of functional security and authorizations.
  • In addition, you have sound experience with SAP ECC, SAP S/4HANA Private Cloud and user and role management, and convince us.
  • You are familiar with security management, maintaining SU24 tables and developing role and authorization concepts.
  • You also have extensive experience with SAP Fiori Launchpad, technical catalogs, business catalogs and analyzing complex authorization errors, enabling you to apply your expertise optimally.
  • You have sound knowledge of SAP system architecture as well as practical experience with artificial intelligence and automation in the context of SAP roles and authorizations.
  • In addition, you communicate very well in German, both orally and in writing, and have good spoken English skills, enabling you to navigate the working environment with confidence.
