Senior Physician / Senior Physician Internal Medicine (AIM) 80 - 100% Back to the list On behalf of a modern rehabilitation clinic in Basel, we are looking for a qualified senior physician for general internal medicine. The clinic specializes in internistic and oncological rehabilitation and offers a structured, patient-centered environment with short decision-making Job description The clinic offers a combination of internistic-oncological rehabilitation and post-acute care. In this challenging field of activity, you can expect a versatile day-to-day work in which you can contribute your professional competence and actively participate in the further development of processes and structures.

  • Medical care for patients in inpatient internist-oncological rehabilitation
  • Interdisciplinary planning of rehabilitation measures in cooperation with nurses and therapists
  • Supervision of medical teams

Profile

  • Medical degree with MEBEKO recognition
  • Advanced training or completed specialist title in General Internal Medicine
  • Ideally, experience in oncological rehabilitation or in the internistic-oncological environment
  • Responsible, team-oriented personality with high professional and social competence
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and structured patient management
  • High flexibility, commitment and a professional appearance

This is what the clinic offers you

  • Co-creation possibilities: Active integration into a modern, newly built working environment
  • Working time models: Selection between different models for optimal planning of your work-life balance
  • Appreciative working atmosphere: A motivated, collegial team that lives a high quality in patient care
  • Flexibility bonus: Voluntary participation in the internal care pool with attractive additional compensation
  • Above-average social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Generous participation in education and training
  • Discounts with various partners
