Quality assurance specialist (m/f/d)

Three locations. One team. Your future.

The PLAST Group unites Stüdli Plast AG in Romanshorn, Grischa Plast AG in Maienfeld and Küng Plast AG in Uznach. With around 76 employees and 64 machines, we combine many years of experience with modern plastic technology and innovative manufacturing solutions.

From large components and assembly to precision injection molding, toolmaking and 3K technology, to medical technology, micro injection molding and measurement technology, our three locations offer a versatile working environment.

As a certified group of companies, we stand for quality, innovation and continuous development. At the same time, we focus on modern workplaces, flexible working time models, short decision-making processes and a corporate culture in which our employees are valued as people and specialists.

Discover your possibilities at the PLAST Group – and shape the future with us.
