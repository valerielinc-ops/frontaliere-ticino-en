Full description

Do you enjoy personal customer contact and are looking for a task where you are independently on the road? Do you bring a qualification for bus driving? Then we are looking for exactly you, because with you at the wheel our passengers arrive on time and safely to their destination.

Your advantages in the post office.

We offer a fair wage, which ranges between CHF 68’100 and CHF 78’100 per year (100% fee) depending on experience and skills. Also applications of talents with different competencies are welcome, in such cases your wage can be slightly below or above the bandwidth.

With us, you work in a future-oriented, dynamic environment in which you are involved and with which you continue to grow. With us, you can expect various benefits, with which you can flexibly design your everyday life according to your needs.

Holidays

We will send you 6 weeks on holiday.

Education and training

We support you with training and further education, temporary job changes and offer you promotion opportunities. We train drivers in our own training centre.

Family friendly

We offer 18 weeks of fully paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of leave for the second parent. We also contribute to the costs of family-supplemented childcare.

Total employment contract

The collective employment contract protects, strengthens and creates clear rules – for fair employment and working conditions.

Mobility offers

We offer you driving discounts (FVP) as well as attractive additional offers for family members. You only pay CHF 10 per year at PubliBike or Velospot.

PostMobil and PostFinance

We offer you an attractive employee subscription for CHF 12.95 per month through PostMobile. With PostFinance, you also benefit from numerous advantages, such as a free private account.

You can do that.

  • On our varied regional lines you bring our passengers to their destination from Monday to Sunday.
  • You are the host: in our post buses and advise our customers on tariff and timetable issues.
  • The daily contact with your passengers enriches your everyday life with exciting encounters and you create positive experiences for our customers.
  • As a driver: you move much more than just your vehicle on the road. In direct customer contact, you experience new and varied situations every day.
  • You sell tickets and support our passengers with tariff and timetable issues.

You bring that with you.

  • You have driving practice in passenger transport and enjoy the variety that irregular working hours (including Sundays and public holidays) bring with them.
  • For this job, you need a category D driver’s license and a valid driver qualification certificate.
  • Reliability and enjoyment of daily customer contact are among your strengths.
  • You have a good knowledge of German and are well versed in using digital devices.
