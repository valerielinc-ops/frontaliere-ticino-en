Full description

The Migros Club School is the largest adult education provider in Switzerland. The cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland operates 7 club schools in which our customers are offered more than 600 different courses and courses in a pleasant teaching atmosphere.

Mansioni

  • Your joy in teaching, your commitment and your winning appearance inspire the participants
  • You design the lessons in the offer Painting and Drawing / Aquarelling and Acrylic Painting according to adult educational criteria, taking into account the different life experiences, learning styles and needs of the participants
  • The comprehensive preparation and follow-up of the lessons is also one of your tasks
  • You create a positive learning atmosphere that encourages the motivation of learners and build relationships based on trust and respect.
  • Regular agreements with the relevant contact persons of the club school administration ensured a smooth cooperation

Requirements

  • Experience: In the field of adult education and in the corresponding subject area
  • Completed basic or continuing education: Professional qualification with federal recognition EFZ or degree at a technical or university in the subject area to be taught
  • In-depth expertise in the relevant field of drawing and painting, acrylic painting, aquarelling
  • Ideally, you will have the SVEB certificate 1 or an equivalent degree.
  • You distinguish yourself through self-reflection and empathy towards the participants
  • You design your work responsibly and independently
  • You communicate clearly, effectively and respectfully
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Cumulus Points: Multiple Cumulus Points on food and nonfood items
  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses
