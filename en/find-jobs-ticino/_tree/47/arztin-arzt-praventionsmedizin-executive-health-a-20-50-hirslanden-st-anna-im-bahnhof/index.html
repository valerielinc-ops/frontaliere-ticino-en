Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73391

Internistic medicine is different: more prevention, more time for consultation, flexibly combinable with your current medical activity.

With the expansion of St. Anna im Bahnhof, the outpatient medical center of the St. Anna Clinic in Lucerne and part of the Hirslanden Group, we are further developing our competence center for prevention medicine, executive health and individual health care.

For this purpose, we are looking for a specialist in general internal medicine who would like to engage in this future-oriented area with a workload of 20–50% and actively shape it.

ITS TASKS

  • Implementation of comprehensive executive health and check-up programs with an internalist focus
  • Assessment of diagnostic findings and early detection of internal, cardiovascular and metabolic risks
  • Creating individual health profiles and personalized prevention strategies
  • Demanding advice for our patients in German and English
  • Coordination of further investigations and interdisciplinary cooperation within the specialist network of Klinik St. Anna and the Hirslanden Group
  • Co-creation of our offerings in prevention medicine, executive health and healthy aging

YOUR PROFILE

  • Specialist title FMH General Internal Medicine or an equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland
  • Clinical experience in outpatient and/or inpatient internal medicine
  • Strong diagnostic competence as well as holistic and networked thinking
  • Interest in preventive medicine, executive health, lifestyle or exercise medicine – appropriate experience is welcome, but not a requirement
  • Enjoyment of a consulting-intensive activity and the individual support of health-conscious patients
  • High social and communication skills, professional appearance and very good knowledge of German and English

Our offer

  • Flexible workload of 20–50%, ideally combinable with your existing clinical or outpatient activity
  • A varied activity in preventive medicine with time for well-founded diagnosis and individual advice
  • High medical responsibility and the opportunity to actively shape our competence center
  • Access to the interdisciplinary specialist network of Klinik St. Anna and the Hirslanden Group
  • Modern infrastructure in a central location directly at Lucerne Station
  • Attractive employment conditions and flexible working models in a professional, quality-oriented environment

Your chance

Whether as a new medical focus or as a supplement to your current job. With us, you can contribute your internistic experience specifically to preventive medicine and support people in maintaining and actively promoting their health in the long term.

If you have any questions, Pirmin Bühler, Head of Sports Medicine, Therapy and Training, is at your disposal at pirmin.buehler@hirslanden.ch or T. We look forward to meeting you.
