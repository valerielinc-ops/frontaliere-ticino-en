Business description

dasteam ag is one of the leading personnel service providers in Switzerland with over 35 years of experience. With 28 branches throughout Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein, we arrange qualified specialists in the areas of main and secondary construction, crafts, technology, industry and medical & care.

For our delivery companies in the Rhine Valley, Sarganserland and Chur region, we are constantly looking for experienced and reliable crane operators for interesting missions on varied construction sites.

Job description

  • Operation of upper and/or lower rotary cranes by radio or cabin
  • Safe lifting, transporting and positioning of loads
  • Support and cooperation with construction site teams
  • Daily inspection and maintenance of the crane
  • Compliance with safety rules on the construction site
  • Assistance with general construction work if necessary

Requirement profile

  • Valid SUVA crane driver pass category A or B
  • Several years of experience as a crane operator* is advantageous
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work
  • German language skills
  • Teamwork and high security awareness
  • Driving licence Cat. B is advantageous

We offer

  • Professional and personal support by team ag
