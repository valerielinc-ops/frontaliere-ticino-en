Description of post

As part of your function, you are responsible for the collection, sorting and preparation of laundry. You ensure the washing, drying, calendering, folding, packaging and storage of hospital and professional laundry, strictly respecting the standards of quality and hygiene in force. You are evolving in an industrial, organized and dynamic structure, where you can evolve in a stimulating work environment.

Qualifications

You have a good knowledge of textiles and a good command of the French language, both oral (level B1) and written (level A2). You have an experience of 2 to 3 years in a laundry, a logistics or comparable sector, would be an asset. You are able to adapt to the requirements of an industrial environment. Excellent physical condition is essential for the job. We are looking for a rigorous, organized person with a good team spirit and a real sense of service. You show versatility and flexibility. You are committed to contributing actively to the quality of service and the satisfaction of our customers.
