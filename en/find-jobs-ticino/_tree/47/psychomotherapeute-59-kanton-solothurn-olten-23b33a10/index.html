Full description

The Healing Education School Center (HPSZ): One school - four locations. We teach and support our students from kindergarten age to school leaving holistically and individually. Our diverse educational offer enables a good school, social and personal development. For HPSZ Olten we are looking for a psychomotor therapist, 59%, immediately or by appointment.

Your responsibility

  • In your role, you support children through movement and play in the psychomotor room provided for this purpose at the Sälipark and Usego locations. They design a movement room for up to 8 children taking psychomotor aspects into account. The percentages to be paid are designed for Wednesday mornings, as well as Thursdays and Fridays (13-17 lessons).
  • They perform varied psychomotor lessons in small groups and design movement landscape and following psychomotor aspects such as vestibular stimulation, promotion of cognitive, sensory and motor development, creation of offers for social interactions among children, promotion of emotional development and emotion regulation, offering material for safe-place installations, promotion of exploration ability also in the sense of self-efficacy.
  • In exchange with the existing psychomotor team of 2 people as well as in close cooperation with the class teachers They elaborated the funding goals in the learning report of children in single and small group therapy. Teachers and social educators are available for advice.
  • They do administrative work in the context of teaching (hour planning, where necessary writing short reports, agreements with teachers, etc.).

Your profile

  • You have a certificate as a psychomotor therapist.
  • Collaboration in a small team and working with children in the field of cognition and behavior bring you joy. You are characterized by your communication skills.
  • You get involved in the individual needs of our children and the close cooperation in an interdisciplinary comprehensive school team.
  • You are a resilient, creative, flexible and humorous personality.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
