Full description

At the heart of the issues of equality between women and men, you are contributing to the implementation of cantonal public policy to combat domestic violence and violence against women. While conducting projects, you support the project managers of the Office and facilitate the coordination of their work. At the interface between analysis, project management and communication, you help transform prevention objectives into concrete actions, in conjunction with network partners.

  • Conduct medium-sized projects, define their structure and stages and ensure that the objectives, timelines and resources allocated are met.
  • Collaborate with the Office's project managers in the implementation of major projects, including the preparation of reports and follow-up documents.
  • Contribute to awareness-raising campaigns, public events and information campaigns on violence prevention and equality.
  • Research and analyse information, make inter-cantonal or international comparisons, collect and update statistical data.
  • Conduct working groups and sessions and present projects to relevant audiences.
  • Participate in the organization and coordination of events and the operational functioning of the Office. For further information, please consult the terms of reference by clicking here or contact Ms Raphaela Minore, project leader at BEFH, at 021 316 60 70.
  • Advanced training, ideally complemented by certification in project management.
  • 2 years of experience in project management, an experience in communication or awareness is desired.
  • Good analytical and synthesis skills, organizational sense, autonomy and initiative.
  • Adequacy of teamwork and constructive collaboration.
  • Excellent writing and oral expression skills.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
