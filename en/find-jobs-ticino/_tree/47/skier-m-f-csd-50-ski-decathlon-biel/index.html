Full description

Mission Your future playground You will join the Biel store, opened in 2019, made up of a team of 22 employees. Rich in its positioning between lake and mountain, the enthusiasts of gliding, outdoor sports or cycling find themselves at home. Its linguistic richness requires being bilingual in German and French in order to share our advice without moderation. Your sports area is at the heart of our local strategy. The team is composed of 5 enthusiasts. The gliding period during the winter is constantly active whether in customer advice or in sales preparation. And now, let's do this: Are you looking for an intense challenge for the next few months when no day looks alike? Do you want to share your passion for sport and offer an unforgettable experience during your mission? Do you like the action, the terrain, and lend a hand to a team that is welded together? Know that here, a CDD is not just a "crossing": you are an integral part of the family and your energy is extremely valuable to give a real boost to the store! Why is this job for you?

  • You're at the heart of the action. The time of your mission, your daily life, is 100% on the ground. You're Decathlon's face. Whether it's welcoming a beginner or advising an expert, you share your passion and create the bond.
  • You're the actor in tomorrow's business. You're not just selling a product on the shelf. You offer an omni-channel and lasting experience: digital orders, equipment rental, recovery of old products (Second Life)... You answer every need.
  • Even in temporary contracts, you have autonomy and impact! You guarantee your sport. You make sure your department makes you want, you manage your stock and you set up your business operations. Your efficiency can be seen directly on the satisfaction of your customers. What You Will Do Every Day: Your Missions
  • Advice and omnichannel sale: You welcome your clients as guests. You advise them, you conclude sales (in physics or through our digital tools), you offer our loyalty program and you manage the cash flow.
  • Commercial dynamics: You implant the products according to the logics of implantation, you manage the "facing" so that your radius is always impeccable, and you highlight the novelties and essentials of the moment.
  • Availability and logistics: Put your hand to the dough! From receipt of the delivery to dispatch, you guarantee the accuracy of your stocks and the rapid availability of your products. You're also preparing web orders.
  • Circular economy and services: You are an ambassador of our new models: you manage the purchase of used equipment and you push our rental solutions to extend the life of our products. What this mission will bring you (the little plus):
  • A real springboard: Working at Decathlon is the assurance to boost your employability. We are known to be a true "school" of business and customer relationship: what you will learn on the ground with us will be a major asset on your resume!
  • A la carte mission: Depending on the length of your contract and your involvement, you can take the lead on specific topics or support the team on specific store projects. This job isn't for you if...
  • You'd rather sit behind a screen all day in peace.
  • You don't like to go spontaneously to others.
  • You think unpacking pallets, tidying a radius and processing merchandise "that's not your problem."
  • You're not ready to see 10,000 steps on your watch every night.
  • You are looking for fixed and routine schedules: here we live in the rhythm of sport and our schedules adapt to the activity (even if we communicate them to you 6 weeks in advance so that you can organize!).
  • You don't want to work on Saturdays. Profile What we expect from you:
  • You're passionate about skiing: it's company DNA!
  • You have a great ability to adapt: You learn fast, you're proactive, and you're ready to throw yourself straight into the big bath to help the team.
  • You have a real team spirit: you come to share and bring your positive energy and freshness to everyday life.
  • You have an excellent relationship: you easily go to others and customer satisfaction always remains your compass.
  • You are organized and efficient: you know how to manage your priorities in a very dynamic environment where you must know how to juggle between customers and logistical tasks.
  • You have perfect command of French. Mastery of English/German is a plus.
  • You are bilingual in French/German What we offer: (Small accuracy: the amounts below are valid for 100% and will logically be proratized according to your activity rate!). We list below the main advantages.
  • Salary and bonuses: We offer you a fixed salary of 4100 gross per month. To this is added a monthly bonus (up to 10% of your gross salary) directly linked to the store's collective perfs from the 4th month of contract.
  • Passion Sport: We support your practice! Benefit from 25% discount on your Decathlon purchases after 1 month of seniority.
  • Flexibility: We operate with an annualised working time: your schedules adapt to the activity while guaranteeing a stable and guaranteed monthly remuneration. Your career: evolution in us is not a myth A temporary mission... and more so affinities! Technically, this contract has an end date. But in fact, at Decathlon, a successful CDD is often the beginning of a beautiful story. The story that proves everything: our current boss at Decathlon Switzerland started at home at 18... with a student salesman job! Very many routes began with a reinforcement of a few months. If you prove yourself on the ground and the "fit" is there, our network opens up to you: you can be recommended in other stores, recalled as a priority for other needs, or even transform the trial over the long term. The journey: how do we meet?
  • Your application: CV + answers to the short questionnaire (no need for a cover letter!).

  • Interview: A concrete discussion with your future manager to see if the "match" works.
