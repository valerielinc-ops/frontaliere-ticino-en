Full description

Portrait The municipality of Jegenstorf with about 5800 inhabitants has two school facilities. The Gyrisberg I school complex is located near the train station. In the comprehensive school, about 690 school-aged children are taught in 34 classes. Day school and school social work are part of the offer of our school.

Tasks

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 30. November 2026 a dedicated teacher for our 5th class with around 20 students.

Their tasks shall be

7 lessons per week in the subjects French, English and M&I. 2 lessons French in half class, 1 lesson in whole class, 2 lessons English in half class and 1 lesson in whole class. M&I is teaching with the whole class. The cooperation with the class teacher as well as the cooperation on school events and projects are also part of your area of responsibility. Lesson days: Monday, 9:10-11:50 and Tuesday: 11:05-11:50 ; 13:30-16:05

  • In terms of timetables, easy adjustments are possible.

Requirements

This is what they bring with them.

  • Teaching diploma for primary or comparable education
  • Joy of working with children in cycle 2
  • Teamwork and willingness to cooperate in everyday school life
  • Commitment to a positive and supportive learning culture

We offer

This is what we offer you.

  • An exciting field of work with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Diverse possibilities for teaching
  • Support for competent health care teachers
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in a well-coordinated and supportive team
  • A modern school house with modern infrastructure
  • Electronic wall panels in the schoolrooms
  • 1:1 equipment for students with digital devices and an additional iPad pool for teaching
  • Temporary employment according to cantonal employment conditions (LAG) until the end of the school year with the prospect of permanent employment

Applications are processed on an ongoing basis. An early application is worthwhile.
