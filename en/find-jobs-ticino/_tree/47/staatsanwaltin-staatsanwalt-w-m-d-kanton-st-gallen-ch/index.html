Full description

The investigation office Uznach conducts all criminal proceedings in the area Gaster/See, Obertoggenburg, Neutoggenburg and Sarganserland. The team of around 30 people comprises ten prosecutors, as well as case handlers with prosecutorial powers, procedural assistants and trainees. It currently has two locations in Uznach and a branch office in Flums.

What to expect

  • Independent conduct of criminal investigations in core and secondary criminal law incl. Piketting services
  • Order of coercive measures and conduct of hearings
  • Issuing of final decisions
  • Personal representation of the prosecution in court
  • Close cooperation with partner organisations
  • Participation in committees and working groups
  • In case of interest and suitability, it is optionally possible to lead a team of procedural managers in terms of personnel and expertise.

What sets it apart

  • Master’s degree in law, preferably with a lawyer’s patent and some years of professional experience in adult law enforcement
  • Very good knowledge of criminal law and criminal procedure law
  • Correct writing of decisions and writings in linguistic and legal terms
  • Conscientious and forward working methods even under pressure
  • High resilience, strong assertiveness, determination, independence, teamwork and pronounced discretion

What we offer

  • Complex and interesting task area in an interdisciplinary environment
  • Contemporary employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • Careful training
  • Pleasant working atmosphere with motivated and collaborative team
  • Modern workplace with attractive training offer
