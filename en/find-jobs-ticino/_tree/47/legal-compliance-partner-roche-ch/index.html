Job Description: In-House Counsel (Legal & Compliance Department) Purpose and Mission of the Role

Under Roche Diagnostics K.K.'s corporate philosophy of "Doing now what patients need next," this role contributes to business development by appropriately managing the company's legal and compliance risks as a member of the Legal & Compliance Department. Our organization values contributing through teamwork. We are seeking an individual who will not only interpret the law but also work as part of a united team to actively engage in operational improvements, including process institutionalization, AI utilization, and the promotion of legal DX (Digital Transformation).

Key Responsibilities You will be entrusted with a well-balanced and wide range of responsibilities in both legal and compliance (legal compliance and corporate ethics) operations. Legal Consultation and Contract-Related Matters

  • Responding to legal consultations from various business divisions and providing support as a trusted partner to advance business initiatives.
  • Supporting the drafting, reviewing, and negotiation of contracts (handling highly specialized areas, contracts involving overseas parties or specific jurisdictions, and matters with potential litigation risks).
  • Collaborating with external counsel and managing outsourced services.

Compliance and Organizational Foundation

  • Promoting overall legal compliance and corporate ethics, and conducting various training sessions and audit operations.
  • Supporting investigations related to internal whistleblowing (ensuring appropriate response to reports).
  • Participating in internal systematization (improving the efficiency of legal operations), such as maintaining a "shared repository for judgment criteria."

Required Experience and Skills Required Qualifications

  • Bengoshi Qualification: Must hold a Japanese lawyer qualification (licensed to practice law in Japan).
  • Japanese Proficiency: Native-level to handle complex legal documentation and manage internal stakeholders
  • English Proficiency: Business-level English proficiency or willing to learn English (used for global collaboration, investigation responses, etc.

Guide: ability to communicate effectively in meetings in addition to reading and writing).

  • Collaboration: Strong collaboration skills to work harmoniously and complement each other as a member of the organization
