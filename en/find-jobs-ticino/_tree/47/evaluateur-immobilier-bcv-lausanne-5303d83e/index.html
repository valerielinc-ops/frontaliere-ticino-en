Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Credit Management Division , we are looking for one or more:

Announcement Real Estate assessor or assessor Your main tasks:

  • To carry out, through a credit process and through input into the internal estimation tool, real estate estimates of individual housing objects (villages and apartments) and small yield buildings,
  • To carry out, within the limits of the powers granted, real estate appraisals of the same objects where automatic estimation cannot be used (e.g. in case of submission to the LDFR or to a particular legal framework),
  • Support and advise front managers through technical expertise,
  • Exchange with fellow real estate experts for the most complex cases. Your profile:
  • Knowledge of legal and technical aspects in the real estate sector
  • Personal Credit Knowledge

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
