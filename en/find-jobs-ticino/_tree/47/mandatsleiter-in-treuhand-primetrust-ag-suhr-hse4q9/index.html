Full description

You ensure the proper accounting of the customer accounting and are a trusted contact for our franchise partners. At the same time, you have the opportunity to contribute your own ideas to continuously improve our services and processes.

Luogo di lavoro: primetrust AG, headquarters, Wynenfeldstrasse 3, 5034 Suhr

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Independent management of the financial and operating accounting of clients in accordance with Swiss accounting standards
  • Independent management of payroll accounts, as well as general personnel administration, including year-end processing and declarations, creation of payroll statements
  • Preparation of interim and annual accounts
  • Preparation of periodic reports and analyses
  • Preparation of annual accounts, tax declarations and VAT accounts
  • Monitoring the e-workflow and the completion dates

Requirements

  • 2-4 years: in general ledger and/or payroll accounting
  • Eidg. Professional ID: Trust or Finance/RW
  • Good knowledge of accounting according to OR
  • Self-employment
  • IT affinity
  • Precision, reliability
  • Transport mode
  • SAP and MS Office skills
  • Knowledge or experience in personnel administration and social security (personnel/HR)
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Modern infrastructure: Inviting and functional office world, modern IT infrastructure.
  • Flexible work location / home office: Possibility for spatial flexibility in fulfilling the function.
  • Handy subscription: discounted conditions for the private mobile subscription with digitec connect or Swisscom.
  • Occupational pension: We are insured with Migros Pension Fund: share 8.5%, share 17%.
  • Social benefits: In case of sickness and accident, the salary is 100% for two years, in case of accident we are privately insured.
  • Employee offers: Our employees benefit from attractive discounts at many partner companies.
  • Free on your birthday: Additional free day on your birthday.
  • Education and training: We offer a wide range of attractive education and training courses.
  • Du culture: Our values are supported by the lived Du culture.

Contatto

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We continuously review applications received, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
