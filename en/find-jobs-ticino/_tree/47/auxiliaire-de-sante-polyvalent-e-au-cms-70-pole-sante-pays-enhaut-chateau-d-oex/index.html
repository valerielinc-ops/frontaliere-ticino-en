Full description

Located in Château-d-Oex, at an altitude of 1000 metres, the Country-d-Enhaut Health Centre offers a wide range of hospital services, accommodation, accompaniment for the elderly and home care. According to the so-called integrated care approach, this innovative organization represents an opportunity for health professionals to work in small multidisciplinary teams, for better continuity of care and in response to the health needs of the population of the Upper Country. In the heart of a privileged natural environment, less than an hour away from Lausanne or Bern, the Pays-d'Enhaut is renowned for its quality of life and offers a wide range of sports, leisure and cultural activities, to live summer and winter! For its Home Care Sector, the Country Health Department is looking for:

Multi-purpose health assistant 70% activity rate, consecutive or to be agreed

Your mission

  • Provide basic care
  • Maintaining the client's living environment
  • Collaborate with the multidisciplinary team to transmit the information inherent in the care of the client

Your profile

  • Diploma of Auxiliary of Health of the Swiss Red Cross
  • Interest in the elderly
  • Driver's licence and essential vehicle

We offer

  • Participation in the development of an integrated care model, involving the coordination and collaboration of different health professionals
  • Work in a pleasant environment, within a human-sized structure
  • The benefits of living in the mountains while remaining close to urban centres (to be discovered under www.alpesvaudises.ch/en/stories/ paysdenhaut)
  • Working conditions according to the CLC san-vaud (13th salary, training, 5-6 weeks of vacation per year, etc...)
