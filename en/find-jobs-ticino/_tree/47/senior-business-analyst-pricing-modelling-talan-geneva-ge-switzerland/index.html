Full description

Background As part of the modernization of its energy trading information system, our customer is looking for a Senior Business Analyst specializing in Pricing & Modelling. The company develops its electricity trading activities and invests in various energy assets, including renewable energy, batteries and electrical production infrastructure. A strategic project to migrate to a new ETRM (Energy Trading & Risk Management) platform is underway to replace the historical system with a modern and scalable solution. You will intervene at the interface between business teams, IT and publishers, to translate functional needs into tailored solutions.

Responsibilities

Business analysis and functional design

  • Collect, analyze and formalize business needs in the form of functional specifications, functionality and User Stories.
  • Work with business teams to understand their processes, objectives and priorities.
  • Participate in the ETRM platform settings: configurations, workflows, templates and pricing.
  • Work with technical teams and publishers to design and implement solutions.
  • Propose improvements in business processes and ensure consistency of solutions with the IT strategy.

Tests and quality

  • Define and execute functional testing and non-regression strategies and plans.
  • Support users during recipe phases (UAT).
  • Coordinate the monitoring and resolution of anomalies.
  • Participate in production and migration activities.

Project management and support

  • Participate in the migration project to the new ETRM platform.
  • Contribute to change management: communication, training and user support.
  • Monitor the backlog of developments.
  • Provide functional support and participate in incident resolution.
  • Becoming the referent on the topics Pricing & Modelling.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in the energy sector or the Commodity Trading.
  • Experience on ETRM platforms (Orchestrade, Calypso, Murex, Endur...).
  • Good knowledge of European electricity markets and trading processes.
  • Expertise in Pricing & Modelling: MtM, DCF, Forward Curves, valorisation and risk management.
  • Understanding Front-to-Risk flows, physical and financial instruments, and P&L calculations.
  • Mastery of functional analysis, specifications and UAT tests.
  • Knowledge of Agile and DevOps methodologies.
  • Advanced computer or engineering training.
  • Common English and French.
