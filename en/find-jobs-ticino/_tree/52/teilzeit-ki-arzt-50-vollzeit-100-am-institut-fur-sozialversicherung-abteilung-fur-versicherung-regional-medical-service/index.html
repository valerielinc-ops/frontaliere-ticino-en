Full description

Tasks

  • assess the medical conditions of the right to benefits of invalidity insurance, in particular capacity/workability, functional restrictions, insurance resources, the probable evolution of work capacity, and draw up the necessary medical reports
  • provide advice and support to UAI services for medical requirements related to different insurance benefits (e.g. integration measures, reintegration measures or professional measures, health measures and returns)
  • complete the medical information necessary for the evaluation of the cases by consulting the attending physicians, arrange the collection of the necessary documentation to third parties and/or if necessary visit the insured persons, perform internal evaluations and/or propose the conduct of external surveys
  • develop, acquire and/or maintain their medical skills (continuous training), insurance medicine and the special legislation applicable to the sector (general expectations)
  • participate in working groups at cantonal and/or federal level

Requirements

  • Federal diploma as a doctor or title of equivalence issued by MEBEKO
  • authorisation for the exercise of the medical profession issued by the Health Office of the Canton Ticino
  • are preferential title: the multiannual clinical experience (in the hospital or private study) in Switzerland, the title of specialist doctor (FMH or equivalent) in general internal medicine or rheumatology, certification as a SIM expert and experience in Switzerland in insurance medicine
  • proficiency of the Italian language (conversation and editing) and knowledge of a second official language, is a preferential title of German knowledge
  • result orientation, decision-making, flexibility and good relational skills
  • good synthesis and editorial skills
  • good use capacity of the Office package
  • interest and availability in insurance training and insurance medicine

Special remarks

  • possibility of teleworking within the meaning of the Teleworking Regulation

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements
