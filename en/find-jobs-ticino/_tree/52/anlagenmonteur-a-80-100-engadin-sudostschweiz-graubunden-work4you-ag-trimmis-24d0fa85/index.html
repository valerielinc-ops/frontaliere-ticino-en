Plant fitter (a) automatic doors 80–100% – Engadin / Southeast Switzerland / Graubünden

You are skilled, like to travel and want to work independently with customers on site? In this versatile function, you install automatic door and gate systems, take responsibility for your assignments and are an important contact person for the customer. A well-founded introduction as well as ongoing training ensure that you are professionally well equipped for your tasks.

Company information

Our customer is an established company in the field of automatic door and door systems. You can expect a modern working environment with a lot of personal responsibility, direct customer contact and varied assignments in the Engadin, Southeast Switzerland and Graubünden region.

This is what the company offers

  • Self-organized and self-responsible working method
  • Own business vehicle for your daily assignments
  • Sound introduction to products and systems
  • Ongoing professional education and training
  • Good opportunities for professional and personal development
  • Trustworthy and open corporate culture
  • Varied assignments directly at customers on site
  • Above average pension benefits
  • Versatile activity with high independence

Our customer wishes

  • Completed basic craftsmanship training in the field of construction, electrical, metal, wood or in a comparable profession
  • Good mechanical or electrical understanding
  • Experience on construction sites and knowledge of the processes there
  • Interest in further development in the field of automatic doors and door systems
  • Category B driving licence
  • Driving licence category BE or readiness to retrieve it
  • Residence within the intended area of operation
  • Professional and convincing appearance
  • Flexible, reliable and resilient personality
  • Enjoy independent work and direct customer contact

This is what awaits you

  • Installation of automatic door and door systems at customers on site
  • Independent organization and implementation of your assembly operations
  • Commissioning and transfer of the finished installations
  • Instruction of customers to operate the systems
  • Clarification of individual customer wishes directly on site
  • Implementation of technical adaptations and customer-specific requirements
  • Close coordination with internal offices and contacts
  • Responsible work with a lot of creative leeway and direct customer contact

Would you like to use your craft skills in a varied field service function and at the same time further develop your technical knowledge? Then this position offers you an exciting combination of assembly, customer contact and independent work.
