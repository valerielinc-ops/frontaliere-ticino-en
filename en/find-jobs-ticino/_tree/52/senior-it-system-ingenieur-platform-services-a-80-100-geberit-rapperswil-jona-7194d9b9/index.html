Full description

MAIN TASKS

You work in a highly qualified team and in an innovative and versatile IT environment. Your tasks include:

  • Conception and further development of the Microsoft Azure platform
  • Development and maintenance of Infrastructure-as-Code modules with Terraform
  • Provision of standardized platform modules, interfaces and technical specifications for product and implementation teams
  • Advising and empowering application, data, AI, development and infrastructure teams
  • Analysis and processing of service requests and incidents (2nd / 3rd level)
  • Participation in innovative and international projects

PROFILE

You have a completed IT education with professional experience or a completed study of computer science. In addition, the following qualifications distinguish you:

  • In-depth experience in the design and standardization of Microsoft Azure platforms, such as landing zones
  • Knowledge of cloud platform architecture, experience with other hyperscalers and a solid understanding of basic IT architecture principles
  • Basic knowledge of Azure AI, Microsoft Foundry and API Management
  • Practice with Infrastructure as Code, preferably Terraform, and Azure DevOps and/or Git
  • Understanding Cloud Operating Models, Shared Responsibilities and Delivering Standardized Platform Building Blocks to Deployment and Operations Teams
  • Knowledge of software development or scripting, for example with PowerShell or REST APIs
  • Basic knowledge of Microsoft Entra ID
  • Strong analytical mindset, structured approach and high quality standards
  • Enjoy cross-team cooperation and knowledge transfer as well as a high degree of independence and personal responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, at least at level B2

ADDITIONAL NOTES

For this position, we only consider direct applications.
