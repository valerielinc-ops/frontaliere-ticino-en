Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2300 employees, including 210 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech, thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment and maintenance of the installations.

Do you like various technical environments and field interventions? Join a team specializing in mobile boilers and cold machines, where autonomy, versatility and initiative are part of everyday life. In this function, you play a key role with customers: installation, troubleshooting, maintenance and optimization of mobile thermal systems. You intervene on modern and diversified technical equipment, mainly in the Fribourg region, with possible trips throughout Switzerland. You operate in a human-sized structure that offers real technical support, development opportunities and autonomy in the organization of your interventions.

Mobile Heating Technician and Cold Machinery 80-100%

Mission

  • Prepare, install and commission mobile boilers and cold units
  • Ensure preventive and corrective maintenance of facilities
  • Perform technical troubleshooting directly on site
  • Make hydraulic, electrical and fuel connections
  • Achieve combustion settings and participate in energy optimization of installations
  • Intervening on cold industrial groups
  • Perform electromechanical diagnostics and automation systems
  • Install machines, lay pipes and perform hydraulic fillings in winter
  • Participate in the maintenance of the machinery fleet during the summer: upgrade, technical corrections and equipment reliability

Profile

  • CFCs in one of the following technical fields: heating, sanitation, electricity, automation, electrical engineering, motor or agricultural mechanics, or equivalent training
  • Electrical, hydraulic and mechanical knowledge
  • Ability to read and understand technical diagrams
  • Easy with basic computer tools (tablet, Outlook)
  • Good command of French
  • Driver's licence, trailer licence an advantage
  • Self-employed, voluntary and motivated, sense of respect, trust and good work
  • Technical versatility and interest in multitasking activities, comfortable with outdoor and workshop work

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate "Together, we are moving towards success"
