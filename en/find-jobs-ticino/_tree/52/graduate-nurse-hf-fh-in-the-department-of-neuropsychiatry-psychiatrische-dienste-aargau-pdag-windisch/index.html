Over the area

The Center for Developmental and Neuropsychiatry (Stefanie Nil and Tabea Blatter) is part of the Clinic for Consiliary, Aging and Neuropsychiatry (Clinical Director Dr. med. Rafael Meyer). It offers a unique range of information and treatment for people with intellectual developmental disorder (mental disability) and mental illness. In addition to a neuropsychiatric outpatient centre, the centre has an interdisciplinary investigation centre, a consultancy and liaison service for facilities for people with intellectual developmental disabilities, and a specialized station that meets the latest scientific knowledge and quality standards in terms of facilities and treatment concept.
