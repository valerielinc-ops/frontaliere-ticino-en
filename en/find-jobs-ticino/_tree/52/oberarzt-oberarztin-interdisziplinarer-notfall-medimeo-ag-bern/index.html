Do you want to work in emergency medicine and at the same time benefit from an exceptionally broad clinical spectrum? In this position, you work within a cross-location team at three clinics that are only a few minutes apart and work closely together. As an SGNOR-recognized training institution category A, the environment also offers ideal conditions for your professional development in emergency medicine. You will become part of a cross-location emergency team within an established private clinic association in the Bern region. The three clinics are geographically close together and ideally complement each other medically. While one location is known in particular for orthopaedic interventions and diseases of the musculoskeletal system, another one focuses on cardiac medicine, visceral surgery, thoracic surgery and intensive care medicine. The third location is established as an important point of contact for primary care and sports medicine. In this way, you will encounter an extraordinary range of clinical questions and patient groups in everyday life. Organisationally, emergency medicine is located in the Department of General Internal Medicine. The management team consists of a chief physician, a deputy chief physician and two location-responsible co-leaders. They are supported by around 15 senior physicians and around 45 assistant physicians. The culture is described by many employees as extremely familiar. The hierarchies are deliberately kept flat, the handling is uncomplicated and characterized by a pronounced du-culture. Particularly appreciated is the close cooperation with the numerous documentary doctors from different disciplines who work at the various locations. This results in short decision-making paths, quick consultations and a professional exchange that enriches everyday clinical life. We are looking for personalities who like to take on responsibility, change flexibly between different locations and enjoy practical and performance-oriented emergency medicine.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of internist and surgical emergency patients
  • Diagnostic, triage, reception, transfer and release
  • Care of a broad emergency medical spectrum
  • Treatment of cardiological, neurological, gastroenterological and intensive care medical conditions, trauma patients and surgical emergencies
  • Performing sonographies and other diagnostic procedures
  • Support and supervision of assistant doctors

Your Competences

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine or shortly before graduation
  • Interest in a broad spectrum between emergency medicine and internal medicine
  • Joy of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Willingness to operate at multiple locations
  • German skills at level B2
  • For applicants from Germany: Medimeo provides you with advisory and financial support in the recognition of your diploma as well as in all steps relating to MEBEKO recognition

Their advantages

  • SGNOR-recognised training centre category A
  • Broad clinical spectrum across three different clinic locations
  • Family culture with flat hierarchies and du-culture
  • High personal responsibility and wide scope for action
  • Attractive salary at Swiss level
  • Entry as a senior physician / senior physician or – if appropriate – as a deputy senior physician / deputy senior physician possible
  • For senior physicians no night services on site, but background services
  • Part of a large Swiss clinic network with a wide range of employee benefits
  • Work in the beautiful city with excellent transport links, also to rural areas
  • Very low tax burden and attractive pension and pension solutions
