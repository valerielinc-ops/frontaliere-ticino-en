Data Engineer — Pictet Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your team

Pictet Tech designs, integrates and operates software applications ranging from portfolio management systems and trading platforms to banking and corporate solutions. The division is responsible for some of the Group’s key innovations while sustaining business operations.

The Tech division and its Analytics platform are looking to hire a Data Engineer to reinforce their team to strengthen the data foundations and services offered to the Group’s business lines.

You will contribute to building and evolving scalable, secure and reliable internal platforms that enable reporting, automation and data-driven services across the division. Working closely with a wide range of stakeholders, you will help deliver robust self-service capabilities, improve developer experience and support the operational and technology teams with modern platform solutions that create tangible business value.

Your role

  • Maintaining, enhancing and industrialising internal platforms and shared services supporting technology and data use cases across the division.
  • Identifying and documenting requirements related to process automation, infrastructure evolution, observability and internal developer tooling.
  • Using Python, Prefect and DBT to build and orchestrate reliable data pipelines and platform services for ingesting, transforming and serving operational metrics.
  • Promoting engineering best practices including CI/CD, infrastructure as code, testing, documentation and platform standardisation.
  • Keeping abreast of current trends and advances in platform engineering, cloud-native technologies and automation relevant to the Group’s operational needs.

Your profile

  • A Master’s degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics or a related field.
  • Experience in platform engineering, DevOps or software engineering roles.
  • Strong knowledge of SQL, Python and automation frameworks.
  • Experience with CI/CD, API-based integrations, infrastructure as code and event-driven architectures.
  • Advanced understanding of data platforms, system integration, monitoring and service reliability principles.
  • Initiative, commitment, self-motivation and the ability to work independently.
  • Fluent in French with an excellent command of English.
  • Must be resident in Switzerland.

Ref. DE/CRO/AB
