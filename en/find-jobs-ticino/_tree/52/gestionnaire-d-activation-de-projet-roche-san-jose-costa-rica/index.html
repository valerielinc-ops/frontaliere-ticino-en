As Project Enablement Manager within the RSS Project Management organization, you report to the Chapter Lead for Project Enablement and deliver evolving, tailored, best-in-class project management services to Roche partners across diverse projects, customers, scopes, and engagement durations.

The Opportunity

  • Orchestrate implementation activities for the assigned scope of work, supporting the design and conceptualization of the project/program implementation strategy, roadmap, and methodology.
  • Engage and communicate effectively with relevant customers and stakeholders, collaborating with internal and external teams to ensure implementation goals and strategies are effective and reach the target audience.
  • Coordinate planning of implementation waves and work packages, facilitate relevant workshops, and ensure workstream tasks are delivered in accordance with the deployment methodology.
  • Monitor milestones, deliverables, project documentation, and outputs against the project charter to ensure timely, high-quality delivery, while adhering to internal RSS or program-specific methodologies, governance, cost, and service-level parameters.
  • Ensure all go/no-go criteria are met and approved by relevant stakeholders, and manage the appropriate transition of deliverables to the support organization.
  • Within Learning & Skills Development, apply project management principles, processes, and tools to deliver training programs/projects on time, on target, and within budget; prepare and update plans, schedules, agendas, and reports; track risks/issues/actions; coordinate resources and meetings with agile practices; and develop structured templates, documentation, and processes to enable team performance and business continuity.

Who you are

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business or Economics with 5+ years of project-related work experience, including proven experience in Agile, Waterfall and Hybrid setups; background in corporate learning or commercial training programs, Pharma industry or consulting experience, and Organizational Change Management expertise are pluses.
  • One or more areas of business functional expertise (e.g., Commercial, Marketing, Supply Chain, R&D) and proficiency with tools such as Google Suite, video conferencing platforms, Visio, MS Project, Smartsheet, Trello, or similar.
  • Strong project delivery capabilities, with demonstrated ability to execute, coordinate and integrate multiple streams of information and activities at various scales, while maintaining service orientation to shape high-quality, reliable project delivery.
  • Excellent communication skills, including influencing, negotiating and effective global collaboration, with strong organizational/time-management skills, attention to detail, and ability to manage multiple activities simultaneously.
  • Self-driven, flexible, adaptable and resilient professional with a growth mindset, passionate about service design and delivery, impact-driven and eager to take smart risks, able to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment, with creative problem-solving and strategic thinking skills.
