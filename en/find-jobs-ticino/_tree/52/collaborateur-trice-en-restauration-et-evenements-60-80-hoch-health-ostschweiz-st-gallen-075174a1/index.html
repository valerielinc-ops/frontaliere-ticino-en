Employee/in Restoration & Events 60-80 %

Hospitality Management

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Professional and service-oriented guest care in the various restorations as well as active advice and sales
  • Operation and accounting
  • Replenishment of drink and food expenses as well as display cases incl. Labelling of prices and presentation of goods
  • Execution of cleaning work according to our HACCP concept
  • Cooperation at banquet events

What you bring for this job

  • Completed vocational training as a restaurant or hotel specialist EFZ, job-specific further education such as Barista SCA Latte Art are advantageous
  • Professional experience in the gastronomy, hotel industry or in the retail trade and knowledge about coffee and milk varieties, as well as coffee specialities
  • Knowledge of Community gastronomy and experience in hospital gastronomy are desirable
  • Flexible operational readiness from Monday to Sunday in different shifts (early, medium and late services)
  • Motivated and organized team player personality with a humorous and winning nature
