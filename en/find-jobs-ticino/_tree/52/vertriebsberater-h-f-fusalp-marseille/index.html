Full description

As a sales advisor at Fusalp, you play an essential role in the customer experience. You are responsible for welcoming customers, advising them on products according to their needs and preferences, and guiding them in their purchasing process. You know about Fusalp products, fashion trends and customer needs. You are able to create a welcoming and friendly atmosphere in store, and provide high quality customer service to ensure customer satisfaction and loyalty. #Mansioni Your main tasks will be:

  • Be an ambassador of the Fusalp brand: You represent and communicate Fusalp's values to customers and new employees.
  • You are able to welcome and advise our local and international clients.
  • You loyalty customers through retail and quality service.
  • You give clients a memorable experience.
  • Participate in the daily life of the shop: You participate in the merchandising of our collections according to the standards of the brand.
  • You ensure the proper functioning of the point of sale and ensure the proper running of the shop.
  • You take part in other operational tasks of the shop (customer orders, receipt of goods, labelling, inventory, inventory management, after-sales management / VAS, cash management, opening / closing and maintenance of the shop).
  • You apply the shop's security directives (theft and loss prevention).
  • Be a versatile and competent seller: You follow the sales performance indicators according to the objectives set.
  • You're proactive in marketing activations.
  • Constantly on the move, you're in the throes of customer opportunities.
  • The scope of these missions may evolve according to the needs of the company ## Required
