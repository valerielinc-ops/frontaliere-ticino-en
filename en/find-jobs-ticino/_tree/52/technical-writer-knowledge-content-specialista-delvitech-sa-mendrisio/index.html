Full description

Capture expert know-how and turn it into clear, structured technical content for Delvitech's AI inspection machines and knowledge base. Job Summary We are looking for a Technical Writer & Knowledge Content Specialist to transform complex technical knowledge into clear, structured, maintainable and reusable content for customers, Application Engineering, Production, R&D and other functions. The role goes beyond writing standalone documents: it builds and maintains a structured technical content system, connected to product knowledge and designed to be used by both people and AI tools, in close collaboration with technical experts and the Knowledge Systems & AI Engineer. Key Responsibilities

  • Create and improve technical manuals, installation, maintenance and troubleshooting guides, operating procedures, application notes and training materials.
  • Transform information from R&D, Production, Application Engineering and other experts into clear and accurate content for each audience.
  • Organize documentation into modular content, define templates, editorial standards and terminology, and link content to machine models, configurations and software releases.
  • Define review, approval, versioning and publication processes, and keep documentation updated with product and process changes.
  • Turn resolved field issues into reusable troubleshooting guides and knowledge articles, and support production and inspection instructions.
  • Support multilingual content with consistent terminology.
  • Structure documentation so it can be used effectively by search systems, knowledge bases and AI agents.
  • Use AI daily for drafts, summaries, restructuring, translation and gap detection, always verifying technical accuracy. Requirements
  • Excellent technical writing and synthesis skills, with the ability to simplify complex subjects.
  • Experience creating and managing technical documentation, procedures, manuals or knowledge content.
  • Ability to interview subject-matter experts and clarify ambiguities.
  • Good understanding of structured documentation, content life cycle, versioning and governance.
  • Strong familiarity with Generative AI tools and the ability to validate their output critically.
  • Strong attention to accuracy and terminology consistency.
  • Excellent written English. Nice to Have
  • Experience with documentation for industrial machines, complex software, machine vision or integrated hardware and software systems.
  • Familiarity with Confluence, Markdown, Git, documentation-as-code or knowledge management systems.
  • Experience with structured authoring or single-source publishing tools.
  • Experience creating videos, diagrams or interactive training content. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
