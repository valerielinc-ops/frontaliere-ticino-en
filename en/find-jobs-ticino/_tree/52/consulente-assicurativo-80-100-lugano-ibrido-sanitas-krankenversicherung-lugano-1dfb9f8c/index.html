Insurance consultant (m/f/x), 80\-100%, Lugano / hybrid

With passion and team spirit, in Sanitas we promote innovations that actively and sustainablely shape the future of disease insurance. In this way, today and tomorrow we offer our customers and our customers exactly the support they need for health.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a motivated and results-oriented partner, specialized in consulting and selling health insurance solutions.

  • Your mission:
  • Professional and personalised advice to customers in health insurance
  • Analysis of customer needs and identify the most appropriate insurance solutions, ensuring a high quality service
  • Management and consolidation of relations with business partners, ensuring effective management of inbound and outbound leads
  • Active collaboration with the team, sharing skills and helping to achieve common goals
  • Constantly expand your network of contacts through recommendations, networking and building trust relationships with customers and partners

Because you're the right person for this job: *

  • successfully completed a commercial training and you have the AFA certificate, plus you have experience in insurance, preferably in the health insurance sector
  • You reside in Ticino and have a solid network of contacts on the territory that represents an important strength for the development of commercial activity
  • You like to work in teams and collaborate with colleagues to achieve common goals, without sacrificing your operational autonomy, demonstrate flexibility, ability to adapt and a strong customer orientation
  • In addition to being an autonomous, responsible and objective-oriented person, able to organize your work in a spirit of initiative, you are familiar with digital tools, CRM systems and the main computer applications used in consulting
  • You are characterized by excellent relational skills, entrepreneurial spirit, proactivity and a natural predisposition to create and consolidate relationships of trust with customers and partners
  • an excellent knowledge of Italian and English, both spoken and written; knowledge of a third language is an added value

What we offer you as an employer: *

With around 900 000 customers, Sanitas is one of the largest health insurance companies in Switzerland. In our modern and flexible working environment you can further develop your skills.

  • Flexible annual working time model with minimum 5 weeks of holidays
  • Modern offices in central locations
  • Generous regulations for work in Home Office
  • Participation in the sickness insurance package
  • Employer's contributions to the pension fund higher than the legal minimum
  • Future-oriented themes and a wide range of training and updates

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
