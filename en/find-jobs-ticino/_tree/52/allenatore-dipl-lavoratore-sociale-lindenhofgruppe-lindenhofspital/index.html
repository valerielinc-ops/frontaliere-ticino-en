Full description

Tasks

  • You are responsible for the comprehensive and individual management of the patient process from entry to exit by professional support of the patients and their system. The patient-related problems as well as the business aspects are taken into account. The patient coach is the central contact person for all interfaces and coordinates them.
  • You will conduct consultations and assessments in psychosocial, legal and financial situations related to the hospital stay.
  • As a patient coach / dipl. Social worker advises patients, relatives and the interprofessional team in the planning of individually adapted and scheduled follow-up solutions.
  • You organize, lead and record interprofessional conversations and round tables with relatives and aftercare providers.
  • You advise and support the medical profession in adult protection measures.
  • You actively help to develop the field of patient coaching.

Requirements

  • You have completed a degree in Social Work (FH/BSc.)
  • Ideally, you will bring experience in clinical social work and / or knowledge in the medical or nursing field and are interested in working as a practice trainer.
  • You are ready to expand your skills in one of our private hospitals and further deepen your expertise.
  • You have a quick apprehension to recognize difficult situations in time and can therefore prioritize.
  • Contact with people gives you pleasure and you meet them with respect and appreciation.
  • You enjoy working in a team and represent the interests of patients in the interprofessional team.
  • You like to actively participate in the further development of your area of responsibility.
