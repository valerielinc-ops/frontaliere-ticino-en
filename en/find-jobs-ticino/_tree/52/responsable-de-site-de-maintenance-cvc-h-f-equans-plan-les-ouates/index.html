Full description

As part of the development of our multi-service activities, we are looking for a CCV Site Maintenance Manager to ensure the technical, operational and human management of a fixed site. A true technical reference, you guarantee the quality of maintenance services, customer satisfaction and compliance with safety, quality and environmental requirements.

In our house, you can move things.

Your missions will be as follows

  • Ensure technical responsibility for site facilities.
  • Plan, organize and monitor preventive and corrective maintenance activities.
  • Ensure that maintenance plans are followed and that GMAO is used rigorously.
  • Monitor the interventions, monitor their performance and ensure their traceability.
  • Write reports and intervention vouchers and have them validated by the client.
  • Pilot subcontractors and receive work done.
  • Monitoring of facilities and works guarantees.
  • Participate in picket service according to the requirements of the contract.
  • To supervise, animate and accompany the technicians of the site.

Which is what you do.

  • Graduated in the CCV field (heating, ventilation, air conditioning, sanitary or technical building).
  • Professional experience from 3 to 5 years minimum in the technical maintenance of buildings.
  • Excellent mastery of at least two technical areas: heating, ventilation, hydraulic or sanitary.
  • Good mastery of computer tools and maintenance management software (GMAO).
  • Experience in team coordination and coaching

Here's what we offer you.

  • A position of responsibility within a recognized company.
  • A varied and stimulating technical environment.
  • A great autonomy in managing your site.
  • Opportunities for professional development and further training.
  • Integration into a committed, performance-oriented team and customer satisfaction.
