Full description

The new Tertianum Usego Park in Olten opened on April 1, 2024. With its 60 nursing beds and 24 apartments, it offers people in the 4th stage of life a comfortable home. The Tertianum Usego-Park is centrally located and is ideally connected to public transport with a bus stop in front of the house.

We are looking for someone to join our team immediately or by appointment

Nursing assistant / nursing assistant SRK 60-100%

Your tasks

  • Independent care and support of guests in everyday and demanding situations
  • Electronic care documentation using “careCoach”
  • Taking on tasks as a caregiver following instructions
  • Help with household chores

Your profile

  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Completed SRK nursing course with completed internship in long-term care
  • Experience in long-term care (advantageous)
  • Competent and loving interaction with our guests
  • Confident communication in German (oral and written)

Our team is looking forward to energetic and charismatic co-creators. Do you recognize yourself? We look forward to getting to know you personally!

If you have any questions, Olivera Kostic, Head of Care and Support, is available on 062 545 70 12.
