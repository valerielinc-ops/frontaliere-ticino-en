Full description

ADAMS Schweiz AG has been an innovative and internationally active company for more than 50 years in the development, planning and production of valves for hydropower plants. With our many years of experience, our technical know-how and our high flexibility, we set standards in the industry.

Every day, our employees make an important contribution to the realization of future-oriented projects in the field of renewable energies. Become part of a dedicated team that shapes the energy supply of tomorrow with passion, precision and innovative spirit.

Your tasks

  • Production and assembly of welding structures according to technical drawings
  • Tearing, straightening, stitching, assembly and welding of components
  • Timely implementation of production orders in compliance with quality standards
  • General construction work for locksmiths and metalworks

Your competencies

  • Completed training as plant and apparatus manufacturer EFZ or comparable qualification
  • In-depth knowledge in the welding processes MAG and WIG
  • Safe reading and implementation of technical drawings
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing (minor level B)
  • Independent, precise and quality-conscious working methods as well as teamwork and responsibility

Our offer

  • Exciting projects and versatile activity with technologically demanding products in the field of renewable energies
  • Independent work with a high degree of personal responsibility and scope for design
  • A 40-hour week with flexible working hours, pre-cooking time and additional free days through bridge days
  • Long-term perspective with further education and development opportunities
  • Above-average pension benefits thanks to higher employer participation
  • A collegial team, short decision paths and joint company events such as the summer grill and Christmas party
