Full description

You want to find out how an engine works? The world of machines awakens your curiosity? Do you want to integrate a dynamic team of apprentices into a modern and state-of-the-art environment? Liebherr Machines Bulle SA has been training apprentices since 1981 and every year some 20 young people get their CFC after 3 or 4 years of apprenticeship in our company. Your missions:

  • You manufacture parts, tools and mechanical devices
  • You read and interpret technical drawings drawn up by our designers and engineers
  • You make assemblies of devices, machines and templates
  • You choose machining tools or even factories in some cases
  • You perform tests, check the quality of the parts produced and eliminate machining defects
  • You perform repairs and commissioning of production devices Your profile:
  • You have completed your compulsory education, and you have good mathematics and technical drawing skills.
  • You have good academic performance
  • You show interest in mechanics and mechanical parts design
  • You have a good spatial representation capacity.
  • You show manual skill, dexterity and a good spirit of collaboration
  • You work with care, accuracy and accuracy
  • You have a logical and methodical mind Our offer: In our production workshop and thanks to various stages in manufacturing, you will benefit from a complete and varied training. Your trainers are qualified and will be able to guide and support you throughout your apprenticeship. We are looking for several apprentices for the start of the 2027 school year, and we are happy to expect your online application. So that we can take into account your application, think about attaching your CV, your cover letter, copies of your academic results and other diplomas. Contract Date: August 1, 2027 to July 31, 2030. In order to ensure a fair application procedure, we do not accept applications by mail or e-mail. In case of questions, do not hesitate to contact Lea GRIVEL. One Passion. Many Opportunities. Liebherr Machines Bulle SA is the competence centre for combustion engines (diesel and gas) and hydraulic components. The company develops and manufactures diversified products used in civil engineering, mining, agriculture, decentralized energy, mechanical and industrial engineering and aviation.
