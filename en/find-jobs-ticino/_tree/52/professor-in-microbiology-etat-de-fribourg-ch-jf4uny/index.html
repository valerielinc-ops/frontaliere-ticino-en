Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The University of Freiburg is an educational institution, research centre, employer and event organiser in one. Its innovative power makes it an important engine for the economic and cultural life of the region. As an attraction for students and researchers from all over Switzerland and from all parts of the world, the university has shaped the life of the bilingual city of Freiburg since its foundation in 1889.

Having signed the CoARA declaration, the University of Fribourg emphasizes qualitative assessment of academic achievement.

Their tasks

Qualified applicants must have an outstanding track record of research in the field of microbiology that is relevant for human health, human disease and medical sciences.

Desired profile

  • The candidate will teach microbiology, with a (bio)medical focus at the Bachelor level to students of Medicine and Biomedical Sciences and contribute to the Master program of the University.
  • The successful candidate, MD or PhD, will develop an internationally recognized competitive research program sustained by external funding. Relevant teaching experience at university level is expected.
  • The University of Fribourg is bilingual, French and German. Courses are generally taught in French or German, with some master’s courses taught in English. Administrative languages are German and French. The ideal candidate is expected to speak French or /and German and English. Knowledge of the second language, French or German, (level B1 oral) is expected. If this is not the case, it must be acquired within two years of taking up the position.
