Full description

We are looking for our cosmetics specialist school immediately or by agreement: Specialist Physics teacher at Beauty School, Part-time, m/f Physics as part of laser hair removal courses

Tasks

  • Training of the students of our beauty courses in theory, in particular. In physics
  • Preparation of documents as well as pre- and post-processing of your own lessons
  • Performance of theoretical tests

Requirements

  • Language skills: German and advantageously available in other languages (especially in English, French, Spanish and Portuguese)
  • Studies in physics or a similar discipline and at least 3 years of professional experience
  • In the case of a foreign education, we require proof of equivalence, i.e. the recognition of foreign educational qualifications in Switzerland
  • Alternatively, a teacher with a PH degree and knowledge of physics (optical radiation lung and frequencies) can also be considered.
  • Good presentation technology and the ability to explain complex relationships in simple words
  • At least 2 years of experience in training apprentices or adults
  • Natural Authority and Patience
  • Optional further education as coach, instructor or trainer in the field of methodology & didactics

We offer

  • Strong team with a pleasant working atmosphere

Interesting Conditions of Employment

  • Potential for further development and assumption of greater responsibility
  • Part-time workload of approximately 10 – 30% (depending on language skills and number of classes)

Do you live in Switzerland or the EU and have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete application with photo by email for this job. Swiss Education Company, Dübendorfstrasse 2, 8051 Zurich, email: hr@sbacademy.ch
