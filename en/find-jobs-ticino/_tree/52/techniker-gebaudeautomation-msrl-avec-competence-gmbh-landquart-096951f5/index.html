Full description

In this function, you program and commission control and visualization software for complex HVAC and room automation systems.

Tasks

  • Quality, scheduled and cost-based programming and commissioning of control and visualization software for HVAC systems and room automation
  • Technical support for the development of specifications, specifications, schedules and schedules
  • Optimization, maintenance and support of existing systems
  • Optional with appropriate experience complete project management from planning to acceptance

Profile

  • Several years of experience in PLC programming, building technology and building and room automation with KNX and Dali
  • Knowledge of text-based programming, for example C#, C++ or ST
  • Practical experience in construction and commissioning
  • Good understanding, analytical thinking and action
  • Planning and organisational skills and teamwork skills

Offer

  • Penalty 100%
