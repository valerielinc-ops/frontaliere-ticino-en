Make the difference - in the heart of the Surselva!

Dipl. Specialist surgical technique HF or Dipl. Nurse care professional surgical care HF 60-100% immediately or by agreement Regionalspital Surselva AG provides the extended basic and emergency care for approximately 22,000 inhabitants and seasonally around 20,000 holiday guests of the tourism region of Bündner Oberland. Our approximately 320 employees treat with modern infrastructure (CT, MRI, IMC, etc.) approximately 2,600 inpatient and 16,000 outpatient patients annually in the clinics of surgery and orthopaedics/traumatology with neurosurgery and urology, internal medicine with cardiology, oncology, gastroenterology, pneumology and rheumatology, gynecology and obstetrics, pediatrics and anesthesia. Regionalspital Surselva AG is awarded the “Swiss Olympic Medical Base approved by Swiss Olympic” label. Through the cooperation with the Center Hospital in Chur, an extended range of services can be offered.

Surselva Regional Hospital is more than just a workplace – it is a place where team spirit, responsibility and humanity are lived. Nestled in the breathtaking mountains of Graubünden, we offer not only first-class medical care but also space for development, innovation and personal balance.

To strengthen our dedicated surgical team, we are looking for YOU – a motivated and competent personality who is passionate about the operation.

Your tasks - exciting and versatile

  • Preparation, assistance and follow-up during surgical interventions in various disciplines
  • Independent responsibility for a smooth operation in the operating room
  • Ensuring compliance with hygiene, safety and quality standards
  • Cooperation in the AEMP: co-responsibility for the professional preparation, sterilization and provision of medical instruments
  • Collaboration with an interdisciplinary team at eye level
  • Participation in regular on-call duty

This is what you bring - professionally strong, humanly committed

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Specialist surgical technique HF or Dipl. Nurse/nurse specialist Operational care HF
  • Professional experience in the operating room is advantageous, but not mandatory
  • Enjoying teamwork, reliability, resilience, commitment and a high quality standard
  • Interested in developing yourself professionally and personally

This is what we offer you - more than just a job

  • A modernly equipped workplace with a diverse operating spectrum
  • Family atmosphere in a collegial and motivated team
  • Attractive employment conditions with regulated working hours and good piket damage
  • Support for further education and personal development
  • Living and working where others go on holiday – in the middle of nature, with a high quality of life and short distances
  • Employee benefits
