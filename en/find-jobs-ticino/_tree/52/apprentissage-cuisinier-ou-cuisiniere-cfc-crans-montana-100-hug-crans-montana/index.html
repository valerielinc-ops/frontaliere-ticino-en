Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . Crans-Montana Clinic, nestled in the heart of the Valais Alps, is a hospital site of the HUG. Its hospital duties consist of caring for patients for rehabilitations in general and psychosomatic internal medicine, multi-purpose post-operative or post-hospital rehabilitations in acute settings.

Description of post

At the Crans-Montana Clinic, the kitchen team prepares about 180 meals every day for patients and staff. It also offers a small restaurant at the cafeteria. Its mission is to ensure a balanced, varied and tasty diet, responding to Swiss nutritional recommendations and developed in accordance with the standards of hygiene and food safety. Labelled "Cooking our region" and "Green Range", the service focuses on fresh and local products to enhance the flavours of the region. As an apprentice or apprentice cook, you are involved in the preparation and production of dishes for a varied clientele, while taking into account specific diets related to the hospital environment. The diversity of benefits and the requirements of our care environment allow you to benefit from a rich and comprehensive training. You discover all the positions in the kitchen and gradually develop your technical and organizational skills. During your training, you learn the techniques of preparing and cooking food, the rules of hygiene and food safety, as well as the basics of organising and managing a professional kitchen, according to the objectives of your curriculum.

Curious or curious and creative or creative, you like to discover new flavors, products and culinary techniques. You have a rigorous, organizational sense and a good learning capacity. You are interested in the hospital environment and are ready or ready to invest in practical training in business and school education. You are committed to contributing to the well-being of patients by participating in the preparation of quality meals. If you recognize yourself in these qualifications, don't hesitate to join us!
