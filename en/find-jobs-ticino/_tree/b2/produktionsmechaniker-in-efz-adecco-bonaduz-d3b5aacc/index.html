Introduction

For our customer, an established company in the industrial environment, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality as a production mechanic / in EFZ (m/f/d). In this exciting role, you are responsible for the production, assembly and maintenance of mechanical components and assemblies and make an important contribution to efficient production.

Tasks

  • Setting up, operating and monitoring production facilities and machinery
  • Manufacture and assembly of mechanical components according to technical drawings
  • Carrying out quality controls and measurement tests
  • Maintenance and maintenance of machinery and equipment
  • Resolving simple disruptions and participating in optimizations of production processes
  • Ensuring order, cleanliness and safety at work in the production area

Profile

  • Completed training as a production mechanic or equivalent basic technical training
  • Experience in mechanical manufacturing and assembly is advantageous
  • Safe reading of technical drawings and plans
  • Precise, independent and quality-conscious working methods
  • Technical understanding and skill
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Advantages

  • Varied activity in a modern production environment
  • Careful training and support from an experienced team
  • Current employment conditions
  • Modern work equipment and infrastructure
  • Opportunity for professional and personal development
  • Collegial working environment with short decision paths
