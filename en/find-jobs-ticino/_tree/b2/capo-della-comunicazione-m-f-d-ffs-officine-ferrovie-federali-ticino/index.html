SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. A lively and exciting working environment awaits you. As part of the communication team of SBB, you are on the pulse of events and get insight into many topics. You will work closely with the Head of Communication and the Communications Management and get up-close to strategic, operational and political issues in corporate communication. You take on responsibility in a central hub function and contribute your ideas for modern digital working methods and AI-supported tools. You can move that.

  • In this important hub function, you comprehensively support the Head of Communication at SBB in day-to-day business, keep his agenda, edit the inbox and prepare all relevant documents for the future.
  • You prepare meetings of the management communication, keep minutes and always keep the overview of all committee meetings.
  • The preparation of management documents is just as much a part of your job as the preparation of reports and presentations.
  • Independently and professionally, you lead the secretariat, organize smaller events and workshops, coordinate appointments and support the team in all administrative matters.

You bring that with you.

  • A pronounced organizational talent as well as a structured, service-oriented and reliable way of working enable you to keep an overview even in hectic situations.
  • Addressable, clear and sovereign communication with different stakeholders is one of your strengths.
  • You actively take on change, you recognize the need for action at an early stage, work independently, think ahead and implement solutions independently and purposefully.
  • A basic commercial training is required, ideally supplemented by additional training in office management or higher education. Several years of experience in a comparable function make you the ideal cast of the role.
  • You master German at least at level C1 and French at least at level B1.
  • Thanks to your IT affinity and your safe use of modern tools (M365), you are easy to get started and work efficiently. In addition, you are interested in new digital working methods and AI-supported tools and use them sensibly.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
