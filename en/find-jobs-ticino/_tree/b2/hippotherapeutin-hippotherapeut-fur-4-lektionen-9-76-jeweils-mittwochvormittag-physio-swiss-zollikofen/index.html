As a hippotherapist/hippotherapist, you will treat children and adolescents individually, carry out the corresponding documentation and work closely with the pedagogical riding therapist, the horse guides and the volunteer helpers on site. The therapies are currently carried out on Wednesday morning. For the future, there is the prospect of additional lessons and thus a possible increase in the level of employment. The exact scope and timing of an enlargement are currently still open.

This is what you bring with you

They have a diploma in physical and hippotherapy for children and – if possible – experience in the therapy of children and adolescents with disabilities. They are an open and flexible personality and enjoy working with different stakeholders (parents, curative educators, doctors, IV). The safe handling of common office programs is a matter of course for you.

You can look forward to this

We offer a varied and responsible activity with well-trained therapy horses and an optimal infrastructure. They work together with experienced horse drivers. The working hours fall on the regular school operation, i.e. no therapies take place during the school holidays. The employment conditions are based on the cantonal framework conditions and can be seen. Further information can be found on our flyer.

We are the foundation for blind and visually impaired children and adolescents Zollikofen - a private, party-political and confessionally neutral organization with a charitable purpose. Our task is to support and accompany people with special needs from birth to young adulthood. We direct additional offers to parents, teachers of public schools and special elementary schools as well as to professionals. Our core competencies lie in the promotion and support of young people with visual impairment, blindness, multiple impairment or from the autism spectrum. Address
