Full description

Do you love variety, diverse tasks and individual support? Then you are right with us. In the residential group Chrosle, no day is like the other. In our family setting, relationship work is always the focus. Support us in the committed and appreciative care of the five residents: women (27-70 years old) with mental and cognitive impairment.

Place of work: residential group Chrosle, Moosseedorf (5 minutes walk from the station) Job start: by 1. December 2026 These are your tasks

  • Together with the residents, you shape everyday life – from low-threshold leisure activities to household tasks such as shopping or cooking.
  • You accompany the residents in the group and in the 1:1 setting and support them in the basic care.
  • You strengthen their participation, open up new learning fields and promote individual resources.
  • Time for relationship – thanks to generous care keys, you can respond individually to the residents and accompany them carefully
  • No shared services – a good work-life balance is important to us
  • Appreciative team – you can expect a supportive team, regular team meetings and supervision
  • Best long and flexible travel – optimal connection to public transport
  • Attractive employment conditions – these include good social benefits and free meals You bring that with you
  • Completed training as a specialist (FaBe) or specialist health (FaGe) EFZ.
  • Experience in accompanying people with disabilities and enjoying responsible caregiver work.
  • Readiness for cooking and weekend services (with compensation during the week).
  • Team spirit, high social competence and interest in professional and personal development. Interested? Barbara Wehrli, head of the residential group Chrosle, is at your disposal. Tel. 079 810 29 35 / barbara.wehrli@igsbern.ch We look forward to meeting you! Team of igs Bern | info@igsbern.ch
