Full description

A good feeling for numbers, an eye for detail and an eye for the big picture: This helps our projects to be commercially successful.

Tasks

Which tasks await you as a commercial project employee bilingual (m/f/d) Opfikon (Zurich) or Onex-Genève?

  • With your bilingual competence, you support regional and project management in organizational and administrative project tasks in German and French.
  • You create billing basics including the necessary documentation, prepare supplements and follow up their further processing consistently.
  • The preparation of outgoing invoices as well as the consistent tracking of payment receipts are part of your area of responsibility.
  • You check the incoming invoices and write bill rejections if necessary.
  • In Controlling, you support commercial project management in project-related evaluations, reporting and other controlling tasks.
  • You act as a communicative interface between internal departments, project participants and customers.

Requirements

What requirements do you bring as a commercial project employee bilingual (m/f/d) Opfikon (Zurich) or Onex-Genève?

  • Flowing bilingual German and French skills in word and writing are a prerequisite
  • Completed commercial training, ideally with a focus on accounting, or a comparable qualification
  • Initial professional experience in finance and accounting
  • Strong communication strength and pleasure in working together in a team
  • Structured, reliable and solution-oriented working method
  • Interest in technical connections and processes in construction logistics
  • Secure handling of common MS Office applications; SAP knowledge is an advantage

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
