Full description

Tasks

  • ensure the general management of the four cantonal libraries and the Ticino librarian system (SBT) in the biblioctic, financial and administrative context
  • overseeing conservation, valorisation and growth of librarian and documentary heritage
  • develop, promote and accompany processes of development, innovation and reorganization in response to social, cultural and technological evolution
  • ensure management and professional development of human resources
  • care and intensify the network of relationships with partners and homologues at cantonal and national level

Requirements

  • complete academic title (bachelor and master or license) of preference in a humanistic discipline
  • librarian/or specialist in information and documentation or training considered equivalent, is preferential title
  • proven professional experience in management functions (conduction and coordination) at a library or other cultural institution and good knowledge of the Ticino and Swiss librarian
  • good knowledge of the public administration and the territory
  • excellent knowledge (conversation and editing) of Italian, good knowledge of German and/or French (conversation and editing)
  • spirit of initiative and excellent attitude to group work
  • strong relational, communication and mediation skills

Special remarks

  • in operation: 1. May 2027 or date to be agreed
  • willingness to work outside the mandatory presence bands for representative tasks and on the occasion of cultural events
  • the degree of employment to 50% is possible only in the form of job sharing with two partial times. In this case, candidates must submit their individual application and indicate in the letter of motivation the intention to take the job sharing function together with the generalities of the candidate(s) with whom they intend to share the function
  • main office: Lugano
  • secondary offices: Bellinzona, Locarno and Mendrisio

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

24 November 2026

Further information can be obtained from Mr. Massimo Zenari, Director of the Division of Culture and University Studies, tel. 091 814 13 00, massimo.zenari@ti.ch .

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
