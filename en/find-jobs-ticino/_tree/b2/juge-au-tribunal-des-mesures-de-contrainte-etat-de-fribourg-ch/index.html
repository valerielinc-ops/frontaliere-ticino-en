Description of post

The Court for Coercive Measures (TMC) is the cantonal authority competent to approve, at the request of the Public Prosecutor's Office, the Judges of the Juvenile Criminal Court, the Police, the Court of First Instance, the Service for the Execution of Criminal Sanctions and Probation, and the Service for Population and Migrants, particularly incisive coercive measures, both at the criminal and administrative level. Its decisions are made by a single judge.

Profile desired

  • Lawyer's, Bachelor's or Master's degree, and sufficient practical knowledge to perform the duties
  • Demonstrated jurisdiction in criminal law
  • 60% post, requiring high availability to ensure permanent
  • The Judicial Council is seeking a bilingual judge or judge. The position is also open to a German-speaking judge who, with the support of a bilingual clerk, would also be able and motivated to deal with cases in French. Good to very good oral knowledge of French is required, including good or very good understanding and sufficient oral capacity.
  • Citizenship active on the cantonal level. Persons of foreign nationality must hold an establishment permit and have been domiciled in the canton for at least five years. Candidates must not be the subject of an act of lack of property or have been convicted of acts incompatible with the function
