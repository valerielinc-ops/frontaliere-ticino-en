Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Care with heart, mind and flexibility – welcome to the Emmental Hospital. What makes us special? As a versatile peripheral hospital, we value an uncomplicated, family working atmosphere. Our employees report on excellent development opportunities, a balanced mix of experience and fresh forces as well as an appreciative approach. Lean management is not a foreign word for us, and family-friendly working conditions are just as much a part of our profile. You appreciate the varied work in an emergency department, keep an overview even in challenging situations and enjoy working in a committed team? Then you are exactly right with us! In order to strengthen our emergency team at the Langnau site, we are looking for a motivated and responsible personality as soon as possible or by agreement.

Your tasks As a health care professional you are an important part of our emergency team.

  • Together with the qualified nurses, you provide professional, safe and patient-oriented care during the night.
  • You take on a wide range of tasks within your area of competence and contribute to a smooth running of our emergency department with your reliable and committed work.
  • You take on delegated medical engineering tasks within the scope of your competences
  • You support the care process and ensure careful documentation and performance recording in our clinic information system.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a health professional EFZ
  • Ideally, you already have experience in the acute area or in an emergency department.
  • You work responsibly, reliably and conscientiously and gladly take on responsibility within the framework of your competences.
  • Even in hectic and unpredictable situations you keep calm and the overview
  • The well-being of patients comes first and you meet all people with respect and appreciation.
  • You enjoy working at night and appreciate the special challenges of permanent night service.
  • Constantly developing yourself is a matter of course for you
  • A stylish German rounds off your profile (for foreign language degree level B2 is required)
  • You are ready to take on day and late duty assignments in order to get to know our processes, the different interfaces and the team comprehensively.
