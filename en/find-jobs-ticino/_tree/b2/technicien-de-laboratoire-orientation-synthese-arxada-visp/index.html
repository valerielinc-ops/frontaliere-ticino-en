Full description

Arxada is a global specialty chemicals company committed to solving the world's most challenging preservation issues through better science. [With a proud innovation history spanning over a century], we aim to help our customers develop more sustainable solutions, protect and preserve human health and well-being, extend the lifespan of critical infrastructure, while reducing our own environmental footprint and that of our customers. Sustainability is one of our top priorities. We make significant strategic investments not only to reduce our own environmental footprint, but also to help our customers adapt to long-term ecological and social change by developing cleaner and more environmentally friendly solutions. We are looking for a graduate laboratory technician. ##

  • Responsibilities
  • Planning and conducting chemical experiments in collaboration with chemists and engineers in process development and scale-up
  • Collection and evaluation of process data at laboratory scale (0.5–5 liters)
  • Supporting process transfers and scale-ups into our multipurpose facilities
  • Analysis and characterization of chemical products
  • Independent and solution-oriented work within projects
  • Maintenance and further development of the laboratory equipment fleet, as well as operation of laboratory automation systems
  • Training and onboarding of new team members (laboratory technician apprentices) ##
  • Requirements
  • Vocational training as a laboratory technician (EFZ)
  • High motivation to promote a safety culture
  • Extensive experience in laboratory synthesis and handling of production processes
  • Proactive work approach and enjoyment of interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Very good knowledge of German or English
  • Proficiency in MS Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) and affinity with computer-controlled laboratory equipment
  • Experience in flow chemistry, laboratory automation, and/or NMR spectroscopy is an advantage ##
  • What we offer
  • Arxada offers top-tier solutions in two clearly defined areas:
  • Microbial Control Solutions (MCS) focuses on threats posed by microorganisms to humans and the environment. This area includes five independent business segments: Professional Hygiene, Home & Personal Care, Wood Protection, Paints & Coatings, and Materials Protection.
  • Nutrition, Care & Environmental (NCE) provides materials for the production of composites in electronics, aerospace, and other industries. Additionally, the portfolio includes vitamins and nutritional components, high-performance chemical intermediates, and raw materials for a wide range of demanding applications.
