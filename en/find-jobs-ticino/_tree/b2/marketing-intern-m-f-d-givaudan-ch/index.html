Full description

Join us and celebrate the beauty of human experience. Create for happier, healthier lives, with love for nature. Together, with passion and creativity, we deliver food innovations, craft inspired fragrances and develop beauty and wellbeing solutions. There’s much to learn and many to learn from, with more than 17,000 employees around the world to explore ideas and ambitions with. Every day, your talent, your innovative spirit, and your determination will shape our future, making a positive difference on billions of people. Every essence of you enriches our world. We are Givaudan. Human by nature.

The ideal candidate must have strong skills in both PowerPoint and video editing. In this role, you will primarily assist our marketing managers in creating and distributing regional trend presentations to various markets and key clients. Also ideally to have a passion for Marketing and Consumer Insights and a strong desire to learn and grow in this space. Additional to creativity ideal candidate should have strong business acumen and be able to interpret performance numbers.

This is an exciting opportunity to gain hands-on experience.

In this position you will work closely with Regional Innovation Marketing team in Europe.

This position for a 12 months’ internship, and it is based in Kemptthal, Switzerland.

Assist the Marketing Manager on the following activities

  • Your primary responsibility will involve crafting, producing, and enhancing PowerPoint presentations.
  • Moreover, we expect you to come up with unconventional approaches for these presentations.
  • In addition to this, you will play a crucial role in supporting the implementation of various marketing initiatives as outlined in the roadmap, using innovative and creative presentation decks.
  • Support is necessary for other marketing materials: like internal and external events
  • Support in organizing exhibitors and speaking opportunities
  • Work with Digital tools and Consumer programs to build consumer knowledge and reasons-to-believe for projects: use multiple Consumer & Market intelligence digital tools for consumer research, build and apply consumer knowledge and insights for selected regional programs or customer projects
  • Pull out performance data from internal system and interpret them

Your profile

  • University Degree or Masters student in marketing;
  • Work experience in graphic design, and other creative fields is preferred.
  • You should proactively explore innovative methods for presenting trends by adeptly editing videos, images, and, most importantly, PowerPoint presentations.
  • This role necessitates a robust creative mind-set.

What do we offer you?

During your assignment you will get the opportunity to work with impactful roll-out campaigns and digital instruments and apply your analytical and creative skills to impact future innovation in an exciting field of the food industry. You will work with a team of professionals, passionate about creating the best food experiences for consumers today and anticipating the needs & trends of tomorrow.

At Givaudan, you contribute to delightful taste and scent experiences that touch people’s lives.

We value the different perspectives that come from diverse cultures, backgrounds, and experiences.

Everyone — regardless of race, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, culture, religion, or any personal circumstances — is warmly welcomed at Givaudan, a place where we all love to be and grow.

Every essence of you enriches our world. Join us in making a difference together.
