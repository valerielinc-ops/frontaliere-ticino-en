Full description

Introduction

For our Production division, we are looking for a (M/F) Terminator whose main role will be to participate actively in the termination activity of the stranded and clasped components.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure polishing and finishing operations across the entire product range;
  • Ensure daily production in line with expectations (quality and quantity) according to the objectives set;
  • Perform self-control of produced parts.

Profile

  • SWC and/or significant experience in the field of termination;
  • Mastery of polishing techniques: emeralding, felting, stone stoning, polishing, satining, vibrating on strands and clasps;
  • Demonstrated skills in plan reading and dimensional control;
  • Knowledge of a computer system related to the trade;
  • Strong team spirit, versatility and flexibility;
  • Rigor, sense of organization, consistency and attendance.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
