Full description

Property manager/in 100% With 41,000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees. The Real Estate and Management department manages the municipal real estate portfolio, which comprises both financial and administrative assets, as well as the properties of the residential cooperative of the city of Chur (WSC).

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 01. January 2027 or by agreement a dedicated and competent personality as

IMMOBILITY MANAGEMENT/IN 100% Their tasks

  • Commercial and technical management of the municipal real estate portfolio
  • Organisation and implementation of re-letting (visits, lease negotiations, conclusion of contracts, acceptance and transfer of rental premises)
  • Advice and provision of specialist information
  • Budgeting of ordinary maintenance, organisation and implementation of maintenance work and condition monitoring of the properties
  • Establishing a basis for decision-making for superiors

Your profile

  • Completed training as a property manager of advantage or comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in property management
  • Profitable, communicative, independent and team-oriented personality
  • Customer and service-oriented thinking and acting
  • Perseverance with sensitivity
  • Style-confident written and oral expression in German language Good PC user knowledge

Our offer We offer you a versatile and interesting activity in which you can work independently and actively contribute to the development and value preservation of urban real estate.
