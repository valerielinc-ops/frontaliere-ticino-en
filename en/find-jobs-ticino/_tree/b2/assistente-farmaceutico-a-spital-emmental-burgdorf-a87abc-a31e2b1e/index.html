Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

For our innovative hospital pharmacy in Burgdorf, we are looking for a committed maternity representation from January to April 2027.

Task area

  • You manage the drug and material storage and ensure a seamless supply of the stations
  • You pick medicines, set orders and carefully control incoming deliveries
  • In close cooperation with pharmacists: you support the safe supply of medicines on the wards
  • You take on administrative tasks around ordering, warehouse management and documentation
  • You are involved in the production and provision of medicines according to internal specifications

Profile

  • You have completed training as a pharmaceutical assistant: in EFZ
  • First experience in the hospital or pharmacy environment is advantageous, but not a must
  • As a real:r team player:in you are actively involved and support your colleagues gladly
  • You are a reliable, careful and service-oriented personality with an exact way of working
  • You work quickly into new processes and are ready to take responsibility for the duration of the representation
