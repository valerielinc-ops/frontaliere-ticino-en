Full description

We are looking for

Doctor specialist in rheumatology (M/F) at 50-100%

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Provide medical care for patients
  • Ensure the proper functioning of the Medical Centre in a collegial manner

YOU MAY

  • A federal rheumatology specialist or equivalent
  • For applicants for foreign diplomas, a MedReg (MEBEKO) recognized specialist qualification
  • Ability to work as an interdisciplinary team
  • A spirit of initiative, organisation and collegiality
  • An ease in human relations
  • An enthusiasm for maintaining, developing and teaching the knowledge of his field

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.

WE OFFER YOU

  • A team of highly qualified trainers, teachers and doctors, providing high-quality coaching and professional development.
  • Up to 5 days' leave per year for training courses are granted and training costs up to a maximum of 1'000 CHF are reimbursed for 12 months of activity.
  • More than 20 recognized "training centre" disciplines.
  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50, as well as 10 holidays recovered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time leave, etc.).
  • Sports activities (Pilates, walking races, discounts at various centres such as several fitness centres or Bains de Lavey), events for the children of collaborators, a company drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience.
