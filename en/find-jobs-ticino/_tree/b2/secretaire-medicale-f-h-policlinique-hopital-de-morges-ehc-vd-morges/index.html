Full description

Policlinic, emergency, outpatient care, hospitalizations: from birth to old age, the Morges Hospital offers a wide range of care services. The establishment has 240 acute care beds, one maternity, with about 1,500 births per year, and nearly 30,000 emergency consultations. In all, more than 40 medical disciplines have been practiced in its walls for more than 150 years. Surrounded by vineyards, with an incredible view of the mountains and the lake, the Morges Hospital welcomes you in a warm and dynamic setting.

We are looking for one for the Policlinic of the Hospital of Morges:

Medical Secretary (M/F) - 50%

8-month CSD, taking up office to be agreed.

WE OFFER YOU

  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of redeeming an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time leave, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • Sports activities (Yoga, Pilates, walking races, discounts at various centres such as several fitness centres or the Bains de Lavey), events for the children of collaborators, a business drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Internal transfer facilitated according to your desire for development.
  • Many in-house or certifying training courses are available to you, as well as a training grant.

YOU MAY

  • A medical secretary's training, or title deemed equivalent.
  • A prior experience of at least two years in a medical secretariat in Switzerland, an asset, coupled with an excellent knowledge of medical terminology.
  • Availability to work on Tuesday, Thursday afternoon and Friday
  • Recognized skills in the use of computer tools and excellent spelling.
  • Flexibility and versatility in order to provide doctors with quality support in the execution of all the activities of a medical secretariat.
  • A strong team spirit, autonomy and the desire to evolve in an environment conducive to collegiality.
  • Rigor, detail care and good management of priorities allowing you to cope with an ever-changing activity.
  • A relational ease allowing harmonious collaboration with the various medical-care workers.

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Ensure patient reception and orientation. e.s.
  • Monitor all day-to-day administrative tasks involved in the proper functioning of the unit, such as managing emails and calendars, telephone response and appointment making.
  • To guarantee the preparation, monitoring and archiving of patients' physical medical records
  • To guarantee the preparation of consultations.
  • Ensure receipt of billing benefits and transmission of medical reports and test results to internal, external or insurance physicians.

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
