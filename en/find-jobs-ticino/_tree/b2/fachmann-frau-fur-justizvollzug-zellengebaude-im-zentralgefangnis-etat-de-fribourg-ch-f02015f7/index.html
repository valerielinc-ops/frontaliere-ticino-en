Appointment authority / department / institution

In the Freiburg prison (the institution) the prison facilities of the canton of Freiburg are combined. It has the legal form of a public institution with its own legal personality. It is administratively assigned to the Security, Justice and Sports Directorate and is under the supervision of the State Council. The institution shall ensure the guarding, accommodation, care and treatment of prisoners in its custody and contribute to the implementation of the objectives of imprisonment and measures.

Their tasks

  • On-call duty and continuous surveillance of detainees, taking into account the different conditions of detention
  • Maintain the reception of the prison (entrances and exits, cameras)
  • Instruct prisoners for maintenance and renovation work
  • Working on the rehabilitation of inmates and having a positive effect on them
  • Ensure discipline, order and security among detainees

Desired profile

  • EFZ or training considered equivalent with several years of professional experience
  • Swiss Federal Professional Card for Law Enforcement Officers of the Swiss Centre of Competence for the Implementation of Criminal Sanctions, or be prepared to participate in training
  • Good physical and mental fitness, flexibility and stress resistance
  • Sense of human relationships, communication and conflict management
  • Willingness to work shifts (irregular working hours, including Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays)
  • Willingness to intervene in cases of force majeure outside working hours
  • Good knowledge of IT tools (MS Office)
  • Empty extracts from enforcement and criminal records
  • Mastery of the German or French language with good knowledge of the other language
