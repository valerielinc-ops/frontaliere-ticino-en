Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement at the two locations Lucerne and Sursee Reinforcement. Tasks: • They analyze performance recording and medical documentation, identify potential for optimization and sensitize doctors and nursing teams to a correct and complete recording • The verification and post-processing of faulty or incomplete performance measurements is also part of your versatile task area. • In close cooperation with the clinic management, the coding, the medical service and the care you contribute to a high quality of performance recording • You will take on further administrative and organizational tasks and work at the locations in Lucerne and Sursee. Requirements • You have completed training in the health sector or have several years of professional experience in performance management, health insurance or health care • In-depth experience in ambulatory service billing as well as good knowledge of the tariff systems TARMED and/or TARDOC distinguish you • Thanks to your strong communication skills, you can safely move at different hierarchical levels and convince with your cooperative and conflict-able personality. • An analytical, structured and independent working method rounds off your profile We offer: • Look forward to a varied and responsible work in a dynamic, collegial environment • You can expect regular working hours as well as attractive employment conditions and further education and development opportunities. • The opportunity to participate in a large, appreciative team at the Center Hospital of Central Switzerland • You bring your experience and ideas to the further development of the processes
