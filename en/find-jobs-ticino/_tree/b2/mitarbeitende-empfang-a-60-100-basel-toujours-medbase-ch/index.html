As a medical secretary at Medbase, you take care of the smooth running of the practice administration. Your organizational talent and discretion help ensure that the practice works optimally and that the team can concentrate on the patients.

  • Support for doctors in administrative tasks
  • Coordination of everyday practice and appointment management
  • Processing of patient requests by telephone and e-mail
  • Management and maintenance of patient data

What you bring

  • Training as MPA or FaGe, ideally further training as medical secretary
  • Good IT knowledge (MS Office, Outlook)
  • Flexibility for shift work and structured working methods
  • Responsibility and discretion
  • Very good knowledge of German (minimum B1 level), knowledge of English is advantageous

About us
