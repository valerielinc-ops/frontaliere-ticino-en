Drillmaster:in Your contribution to us

  • You lead and coordinate your team on the construction site
  • You organize and monitor the drilling work on site
  • You operate drilling machines and ensure their professional and safe use
  • You coordinate the use of personnel, machines, equipment and materials
  • You monitor the workflows and ensure a professional execution
  • You lead rapports and document the work carried out
  • You control the machines and equipment used and coordinate necessary maintenance work
  • You ensure compliance with our quality and safety requirements

What matters to us

  • Completed training in the construction industry or a comparable qualification
  • Professional experience in drilling or special engineering
  • Experience in the management and coordination of a construction site team
  • Technical understanding and safe handling of drilling and construction equipment
  • Independent, responsible and solution-oriented working method
  • High sense of responsibility for occupational safety and quality
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Category B driving licence

Our added value for you At STRABAG you work with modern drilling technology, high-performance construction machinery and high-quality equipment. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Graubünden?

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Graubünden's migration office after the contract is signed.
