Full description

Social security consultant ( Lugano area)

Are you looking for a new professional challenge in the security industry? Convit gives you the opportunity to exploit your skills or acquire new skills. Join our team and help ensure the financial security of our customers.

Main information Employer: Convit International GmbH Workplace: Via al Mulino 22a, 6814 Cadempino, Switzerland Recruitment area: Lugano and surroundings – the office is our headquarters in Cadempino. Contract: Indefinite recruitment; degree of employment according to the approved position Retribution (OTE): CHF 1'500–7'500 per month (fix + variable part, depending on the degree of employment) Language: Italian B2 or higher

Activities

  • Analyze individual customer social security needs.
  • Prepare customized social security solutions.
  • Provide professional advice within approved solutions.
  • Carefully document consulting processes.
  • Keep your knowledge up to date in the social security sector.

Your profile

A VBV qualification, at least in the Life profile, is an advantage, but is not required to start. If you already have it, attach the document and information about your professional update. Without qualification, the availability of training and the commitment to acquire the necessary qualifications are indispensable. Counselling and intermediation activities begin only after fulfilling the applicable requirements and internal authorization.

  • Customer orientation and strong communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and responsiblely.
  • Accuracy and attention to detail.
  • Availability for continuous learning.

What we offer

  • A structured insertion and a constant comparison with experienced colleagues.
  • Avant-garde digital tools and clear procedures for preparation and documentation.
  • A clear learning path with practical support if the qualification is still missing.
  • Hybrid working methods agreed, with presence in the reference location.

Training and qualification

If you still have a VBV qualification, the VBV admission exam preparation course is free for you: Convit supports course costs. The qualification requires overcoming the relevant examination and compliance with the applicable requirements.

Right to work

Applications of persons resident in Switzerland are welcome. For this campaign, those who reside in Italy must already have a valid G permit. The individual situation is verified before hiring.

Application

Send the resume and any certificates via the application form or to Recruiting@convit.ch.
