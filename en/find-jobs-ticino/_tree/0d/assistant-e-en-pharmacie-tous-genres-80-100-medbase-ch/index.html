Welcome to Medbase Lausanne Blécherette

In Lausanne's new Métamorphose des Plaines-du-Loup district, a new Medbase Pharmacy was created in November 2024. In addition to a wide range of traditional medicine, natural medicine and cosmetics products, we offer services such as vaccination, health check-ups or specialist advice. We look forward to being able to provide our clients with motivation in this new neighbourhood of La Blécherette and the surrounding area in a modern and bright setting. Medbase supports graduate pharmacists in their post-graduate FPH. We seek to form a versatile and dynamic team. We look forward to receiving your application!

To achieve great things, one must have a vision of the future. Twenty years ago, Medbase was founded with the idea of creating a health care network for all of Switzerland. EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
