Full description

Your team The Pictet Group is one of the world’s leading independent wealth and asset managers. Founded in 1805 and headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, the Group is represented in 31 offices in financial centres worldwide, currently employing over 5,000 people. Pictet has been present in Singapore for more than 25 years where it employs over 200 people. Pictet Wealth Management (PWM) combines more than 200 years of Swiss banking heritage with global investment expertise. The Partner-owned financial services group offers a comprehensive service for wealthy individuals and families, including discretionary and advisory investment solutions and family office services. Your role We are seeking a highly motivated Financial Analyst to join our Asia 2 team as part of a unique, structured 4-year rotational programme designed to provide a comprehensive foundation in private banking and investment management. At the end of this programme, you will be equipped to take on the role of Private Banker, supported by a robust in-house network of specialists and an understanding of our business and clients. This permanent position offers a 48-month journey through three key areas of our Wealth Management business, allowing you to build expertise and develop a strong professional network:

  • Year 1: Immersion with Client Relationship Officers to discover client relationship management and daily operations.
  • Years 2 & 3: Integration within the Investment Platform to deepen your knowledge of investment and portfolio management, and to understand the needs of portfolio managers.
  • Year 4: Gaining practical experience in high-level wealth management advisory through the management of an existing client portfolio. Throughout the programme, you will be mentored by a Senior Banker and will collaborate with professionals across the banking sector, support the implementation of wealth management strategies, and contribute to the delivery of tailored solutions for our clients. You will also have the opportunity to complete professional certifications, such as the CFA, and participate in ongoing training and development activities. Your profile
  • Master’s or Bachelor's degree in Finance or Management with 3 to 5 years of professional experience.
  • Enthusiasm, interpersonal skills, and a strong interest in client relations.
  • Highly organized, detail-oriented, and able to manage multiple projects with competing deadlines.
  • Strong communication skills, both written and verbal.
  • Team player with a positive attitude, strong work ethic, and the ability to adapt quickly.
