Full description

Do you know our yellow vehicles? They are always in use where we, K. Schweizer AG – Your reliable electrical partner on site – carry out sophisticated electrical installations or correct faults.

In order to strengthen our dedicated team in the field of industrial technology, we are looking for a service-oriented, entrepreneurial and motivated personality as a project manager / in electrical engineering.

Their tasks

  • Management and coordination of installation projects with a clear focus on economic efficiency
  • Ensure smooth implementation of projects in terms of offers, costs, schedule, quality and staffing, and monitoring of cash payments and debtors
  • Responsibility for strategic planning and coordination of human resources
  • quality assurance and time monitoring
  • Development of solutions for deviations
  • Control of project-related invoicing to ensure transparent billing
  • Maintenance and support of existing customers as well as active participation in the establishment and expansion of new business relationships

Your profile

  • Basic electrical installer training completed
  • At least 5 years of experience in project management at project manager level
  • Strong quality awareness and a high level of initiative
  • Leadership and team spirit to achieve success together
  • Professional and sovereign attitude towards customers, suppliers, employees and superiors
  • Negotiation skills and sound written and oral language
