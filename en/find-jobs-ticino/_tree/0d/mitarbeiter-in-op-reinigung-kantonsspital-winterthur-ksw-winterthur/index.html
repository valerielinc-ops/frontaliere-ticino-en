Full description

Our area of operation is looking for a:n flexible:n and dedicated:n employee for cleaning and disinfection. Tasks:

  • Execution of change between operations
  • Final cleaning after completion of the operation programme
  • Maintenance cleaning and disinfection of all rooms belonging to the operating area, such as operating rooms, inlets and outlets, embedding areas, surgical garderobes (including washing and disposal of the surgical shoes), offices, station rooms, watch room and other rooms according to work plan and hygiene guidelines
  • Ordering cleaning and consumables
  • notification of repair requirements to the competent authorities
  • Participation in internal team meetings and training
  • Shift operations between 06:00 and 23:00, 365 days a year (including weekends and holidays) Profile:
  • Multiannual professional experience in cleaning, ideally in healthcare
  • High quality and service awareness and good physical resilience
  • High flexibility in changing working hours and readiness for weekend services
  • Motivated, reliable and team-capable personality with responsibility
  • Experience in handling cleaning materials, machinery and equipment
  • Good oral and written knowledge Benefits: The KSW provides employees with conditions that enable them to meet their professional goals and personal needs. Flexible, sliding working hours, home office as well as a generous maternity leave and levels of employment adapted to different situations of life – this is all possible at the KSW, provided that it allows operation
  • Every year, the KSW offers 40 advanced and 50 professional training courses. We also support you in external developments in time and/or financial terms
  • In exceptional situations, e.g. longer illness, strong personal burden or difficult experiences in the private as well as in everyday professional life are available to you. The valuable and friendly cooperation within the teams and across the departments is characterized by the working climate at the KSW. Thus, the pension funds solution with the BVK is far above the statutory minimum. Even in the case of illness or accident, KSW employees are very well secured
  • With discounts on fitness subscriptions, internal training options and course offers, discounts in pharmacies and among the most popular health insurance companies. You can benefit from a comprehensive offer for your health
  • Our La Luna daycare centre with generous opening hours is located right next to the KSW. You can also use the income-dependent, financial support provided by the KSW for the crib costs
  • In cooperation with cultural partners in and around Winterthur we regularly offer discounts for concerts and theatre performances. The borrowing of media in the in-house library is also free for employees.
