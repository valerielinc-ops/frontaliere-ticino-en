# Assistant with Bachelor of Science in Visual Communication

The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI), competes at the Department of Environment Construction and Design (DACD) based at the Mendrisio Campus, a position of , with a degree of employment from 80% to 100%. Entry into operation on 1 February 2027.

Scope and purpose of position

The assistant accompanies students in three years of study in Visual Communication in their practical activities. He collaborates with the teachers of the professional area and assists them in the preparation and performance of the teaching activities, and in tutoring (revisions) and in the supervision of projects (quality control). Its contribution is central to coordinating resources and tools for all kinds of teaching activities. The Assistant with Bachelor is a training course: 20% of the degree of employment is dedicated to personal training, according to a training and work plan defined annually with the direct superior.

Responsibility and activity

In the field of disciplinary teaching assistance at the Bachelor in Visual Communication, the person selected:

  • He collaborates with professors in the preparation and conduct of teaching activities;
  • assists, supports and accompanies students during exercises, workshops and projects;
  • is an operational and autonomous reference figure for disciplinary teaching;
  • can be a person with great spirit of initiative, precise, punctual and oriented to work for objectives;
  • independently manages the planning of resources, tools and spaces necessary for teaching;
  • is part of the Bachelor's work group and contributes to innovation of content and teaching processes.

Requirements

  • Bachelor in Visual Communication or equivalent.
  • Less than 3 years of professional experience after the end of studies.
  • Excellent knowledge of professional computer programs ( Adobe suite), digital prototyping programs, audiovisual and photographic equipment.
  • Excellent knowledge of the Italian language and good in English. Knowledge of a second national language is desired.
  • Communication and organizational skills and to work independently respecting the established deadlines;
  • Group work attitude, with a strong focus on collaboration.
  • Ability to confront different situations and activities, adapting with openness and flexibility to the needs of bachelor.

We offer

  • A fixed-term contract of up to 3 years.
  • A structured training course, with 20% of the degree of employment dedicated to personal training.
  • Possibility to contribute to the development and growth of the degree program in Visual Communication.
  • Insertion in a young, dynamic and constantly evolving context.
  • Direct contact with local professionals.
