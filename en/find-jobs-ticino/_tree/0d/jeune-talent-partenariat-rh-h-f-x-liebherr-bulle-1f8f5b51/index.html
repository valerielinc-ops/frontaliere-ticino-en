Full description

Are you eager to kick-start your professional career in a multinational environment? Join our internship programme and benefit from a hands-on learning experience to develop your skills and gain valuable professional experience. We are looking for candidates available for a 12-month internship starting on March 1, 2027. Your responsibilities

  • You support the HR Business Partnering team in the day-to-day administration of all matters related to the employee lifecycle.
  • You are responsible for drafting and publishing job advertisements in French and English for various positions.
  • You assist the HR team throughout the entire employee lifecycle, including recruitment, onboarding, development, employee relations (absences,conflicts, disciplinary matters) and offboarding.
  • You manage end-to-end recruitment processes for Young Talent positions, including kick-off meetings, job postings, application screening, interviews and job offers.
  • You prepare engaging presentations, communications and reports on HR-related topics to support decision-making and internal communication.
  • Depending on your qualifications and interests, you will have the opportunity to contribute to a variety of exciting cross-functional projects. Your profile
  • You are currently completing or have recently completed a university degree in Business Administration, Psychology, Marketing or a related field.
  • You have an excellent command of French and English. German is an asset.
  • You are motivated to work in Human Resources and available for a 12-month internship.
  • You have excellent writing skills, are proficient in MS Office and can easily adapt to different IT systems.
  • You stand out for your autonomy, teamwork, strong communication skills and proactive attitude.
