Back to Overview Employee/administration 60% Job opening Immediately or by agreement Employment level 60% Place of work/region BE-Bern For our customer, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality for administration and coordination at an authority in the city of Bern. The temporary project starts in July and lasts until the end of the year (motherhood representation).

Your responsibilities

  • Recording and handling of business cases
  • Collection and maintenance of metadata
  • Scanning of documents incl. Quality assurance
  • Cooperation in archive management
  • Cooperation in specification management
  • Independent processing and management of internal inquiries and pendencies
  • Creation and maintenance of lists and overviews as well as support for internal processes and projects
  • Coordination of translations
  • Coordination and coordination of tasks within the team and with internal stakeholders

Your profile

  • Commercial training (EFZ) or equivalent qualification with at least 5 years of proven professional experience in administrative activities, ideally in an authority
  • Careful, structured and proactive working method with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Secure handling of digital business applications, in particular Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, Outlook, PowerPoint), M365, Business Case Management Systems (Required SAP Experience) and Document Management Systems
  • Experience in digital process processing and document management (mandatory SharePoint experience)
  • Very good written and oral German skills, good reading comprehension in French and English, ideally basic skills in oral communication
  • Analytical thinking and ability to capture professional and organizational contexts and implement them in a structured way
  • High reliability, efficient operation, fast comprehension and absolute discretion
  • Teamwork as well as strong service and service orientation
