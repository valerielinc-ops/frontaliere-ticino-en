Full description

Tasks

  • Administrative support for prosecutors and investigators in case handling
  • Rotating the investigation files for indictment, recruitment and criminal order proceedings
  • Completion of requests for access to files
  • Processing of procedure entries and exits with the help of our electronic business control
  • Friendly and professional telephone and counter service
  • Processing of incoming and outgoing mail

Requirements

  • For this challenging and versatile task, we want an open-minded and resilient personality with a commercial professional degree.
  • They express themselves verbally and in writing in the German language. Foreign language skills are a plus.
  • They have an interest in criminal law and prosecution.
  • They preferably bring with them professional experience in the judiciary, legal profession or administration.
  • They are experienced in dealing with digital media and office programs.
  • Independent and exact work is a matter of course for you.
  • They are concealed, flexible, resilient and have a sense of organization.
  • Sociability, teamwork and customer-oriented appearance are among your strengths.
