Full description

Are you passionate carpenter and ready for the next step? From the workshop to the finished branch, you bring your skills to production, assembly and organization and actively design new, renovated and expanded Migros branches.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operations headquarters Schönbühl, Industriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You produce high-quality carpentry work and independently realize assembly work in shop construction, interior construction and maintenance
  • You represent the department manager in his absence and support him in planning, organizing and coordinating assignments
  • You lead employees and learners professionally and promote successful cooperation in the team
  • You plan work preparation, plan materials and ensure efficient order processing
  • You ensure quality, adherence to schedules and work safety and actively participate in renovations, renovations and maintenance work

Requirements

  • Ideally more than 5 years of professional experience: in the field of assembly / workshop
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EFZ): as a carpenter
  • You have a good technical understanding and work precisely and responsibly
  • You think and act customer-oriented and respond to the needs of our clients
  • You have a category B driving licence
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Employee offers: Preferential conditions for insurance and health insurance funds
  • Sports and fitness offers: Cost sharing in subscriptions of over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, aquaparks
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
