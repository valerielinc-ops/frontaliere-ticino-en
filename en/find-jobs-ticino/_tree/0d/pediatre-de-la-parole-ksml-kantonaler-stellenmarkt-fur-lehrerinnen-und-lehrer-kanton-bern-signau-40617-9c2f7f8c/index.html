Tasks

As a specialist in our speech pediatrics team, from the new school year 2026/27 onwards, they support learners Z1-3 in single and/or small group setting in the linguistic/logopaedic field. Your workload is at least 10L and can be extended to 100% at will.

Its tasks shall include in particular

  • Clarification of the therapy needs of individual students
  • Therapy and accompaniment of students
  • Advice for parents and teachers
  • Preventive activities where possible
  • interdisciplinary cooperation with the MR team, school management and relevant specialist bodies
  • Collaboration with colleagues at our school within the framework of the professional assignment

Requirements

Bachelor of Arts Speech Therapy, EDK- recognized diploma for Speech Therapy or in the last year of training. They are an eloquent, humorous, dutiful, interested and motivated personality and are accustomed to dealing with children and parents.

We offer

+ permanent employment with workload from 10L (up to 100%) + Group rooms furnished + an exciting field of work with high personal responsibility + Good cooperation and acceptance from parents + Appropriate participation in meetings/conferences + a lot of support from MR and school management + Concentration on the core business of therapy and cooperation with teachers and parents + Exchange/ Collaboration with experienced speaker on site + Possibility of a mentorship for new professionals
