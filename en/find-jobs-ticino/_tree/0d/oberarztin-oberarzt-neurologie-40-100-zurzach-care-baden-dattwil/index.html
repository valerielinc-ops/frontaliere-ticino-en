Full description

ZURZACH Care Rehaklinik Baden-Dättwil offers a versatile neurological working environment at the interface between acute medicine and rehabilitation on the campus of the Cantonal Hospital Baden (KSB). We treat patients with complex neurological and musculoskeletal diseases as well as increased medical monitoring needs. The close interdisciplinary cooperation, the cooperation with the Cantonal Hospital Baden and the connection of inpatient and outpatient neurology open up interesting professional and personal development opportunities.

Your tasks • Station management: Medical management of a neurological rehabilitation station including supervision of assistant doctors • Conversation: Conducting discussions with patients, their relatives and members of the interdisciplinary teams • Rehabilitation: management of rehabilitation planning and interdisciplinary rehabilitation reports • Further education and training: Participation in the in-house interdisciplinary training as well as in the structured SIWF training in the field of neurology • Outpatient consultation: independent guidance of an outpatient neurological consultation • Background services: participation in the neurological background services in cooperation with the KSB • Further development: co-creation and professional development of our neurological offering with the opportunity to contribute our own focus areas • Sleep medicine (optional): Ambulante sleep medicine activity in the near ZURZACH Care Klinik für Schlafmedizin (KSM) in the Bäderquartier in Baden

  • Possibility to obtain the Sleep Medicine Certificate (SGSSC)

Your profile • Qualifications: Specialist title Neurology (SIWF) or an equivalent foreign specialist title recognized by MEBEKO • Approvals: You have a Professional Permit (BAB) or are willing to apply for it; OKP approval (AG) is advantageous • Language: Knowledge of German Level C1 •

Experience (advantageous, but not a requirement)

• Neurological medical activity • Rehabilitation work • Knowledge of clinical neurophysiology and neurosonology • Personality: You are responsible and organizationally strong and think economically and solution-oriented • Communication: You have a high level of social competence, enjoy working in an interdisciplinary manner and communicate in a step-by-step manner

What we offer

• Multifaceted activity spectrum: combination of inpatient neurorehabilitation, outpatient neurology and optional activity in sleep medicine • Design leeway: High professional independence and the opportunity to develop personal clinical focuses • Professional development: support for professional in-depth and additional qualifications as well as generous cost-sharing in education and training • Co-creation: Actively design a modern work environment • Inspiring and collaborative working environment: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care • Social benefits: Benefit from above-average social security benefits and comprehensive benefits • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners such as wholesalers, car dealers and pharmacies as well as our membership at Benefit@work • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.

Job opening: immediately or by agreement
