As an employer in Germany, we bear responsibility for this and maintain a fair treatment of our approximately 180 employees. Together we are committed with pleasure and passion every day for the production of high-quality Bundner meat specialities.

We are looking for an engaged, customer-oriented and communicative personality immediately or by agreement as

You are an important contact person for our customers and ensure with your commitment that everything runs smoothly in the sales department. From competent customer advice and order processing to coordination with operations and logistics, you accompany the entire sales process. You also keep an overview in a dynamic environment and contribute significantly to customer satisfaction with your service-oriented approach.

What you do move

  • Active customer support and telephone advice as well as sustainable customer relationships
  • Independent preparation, sending and tracking of offers
  • Acceptance of orders as well as complete order processing and billing in SAP
  • Processing of customer complaints incl. Credits in close cooperation with Quality Management
  • Coordination and distribution of customer orders in coordination with operations and logistics
  • Preparation of transport orders and processing of customs documents for the export business
  • Support for sales promotions, markup controls and price adjustments
  • Observation of market and competitive developments and assumption of various administrative tasks

What you bring

  • Completed training in the commercial sector, retail trade or food environment
  • Professional experience in a comparable function, ideally in the sales department or in the food industry
  • Strong customer orientation and service orientation as well as pleasure in telephone customer contact
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method with a high quality awareness
  • Communicative, resilient and flexible personality with a strong team spirit
  • Good MS\Office\ knowledge, ideally experience in dealing with SAP
  • Very good knowledge of German, knowledge of German is advantageous
