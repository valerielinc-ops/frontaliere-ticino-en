Full description

At the Beilngries site, around 350 employees in the Grain Quality and Supply division are dedicated to plant and mechanical engineering in the fields of grain processing and malting and brewing.

This includes cleaning, drying, transport (including conveyor technology and ship loading), storage and digitalization, with a focus on efficiency, product quality and sustainability, to process and handle grain from the field to the consumer.

Let us make a difference at Bühler! Two billion people eat food produced on Bühler plants every day. One billion people travel in vehicles whose parts have been produced by Bühler technology. With every decision, Bühler aims to reconcile the needs of business, people and nature, and to develop solutions that open up sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed and mobility industries.

We want to create innovations for a better world, with a particular focus on healthy, safe and sustainable solutions. That's why we work with customers, start-ups, multinational corporations and scientists to achieve a faster impact together.

Tasks

  • Professional and disciplinary management of the welding team
  • Planning, coordination and monitoring of welding work in production
  • Ensure compliance with quality, occupational safety and deadlines
  • Checking and release of welds according to drawings and welding instructions
  • Training and further development of employees in the field of welding
  • Participation in the optimization of welding processes and production processes (KVP)
  • Preparation of working instructions and welding data
  • Documentation of quality problems and processes

Requirements

  • Completed training in metalworking
  • Perennial experience in welding
  • First management experience (team leadership, foreman, master) of advantage
  • Good knowledge in reading drawings and welding plans
  • High level of quality and responsibility
  • Strength of assertiveness, communication strength and team orientation
  • Safe handling of SAP benefits
