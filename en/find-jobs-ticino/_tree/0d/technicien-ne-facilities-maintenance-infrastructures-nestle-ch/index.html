Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet

As a Technician in the Infrastructure-Sanitary team at Nestlé, your mission is to use your skills in locksmithing, carpentry or cabinetmaking, complemented by knowledge in electricity and CVCS, to ensure technical and operational support.

You will work together to ensure the smooth operation and continuity of our Vevey campus, in order to provide a safe, functional and efficient working environment for some 3,000 people. You will make a difference by working closely with the technical team, campus collaborators, business interlocutors and external service providers. This is an opportunity for you to take responsibility, develop your expertise and achieve concrete results that make the difference on a large scale.

How you will have an impact as an Infrastructure Technician

  • Perform maintenance and repair work in lock, carpentry and cabinetmaking, as well as ensure the continuity of technical installations.
  • Plan, coordinate and supervise maintenance interventions (preventive/corrective) as well as technical stops.
  • Ensure availability and reliability of facilities by managing day-to-day operations and follow-up of requests for intervention.
  • Strengthen the safety and compliance of each intervention through risk analysis and the application of standards and good practices.
  • Support the technical team on a day-to-day basis and ensure follow-up of interventions carried out by external providers.
  • Improve performance and operational efficiency by proposing concrete and sustainable solutions.
  • Technical picket service

What Will Make Your Success

  • Take advantage of a solid technical experience in lockmaking, carpentry or cabinetmaking, or in a field of building technology; A technical CFC is an asset.
  • Very good knowledge of electrical techniques and CVCS; Fire protection certification is an important asset.
  • Mastery of SHE requirements.
  • Demonstrated ability to effectively manage emergency situations at critical facilities in a demanding environment.
  • Demonstrate courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while transforming ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.
  • Provide quality service to a demanding customer, with tact, focus and understanding of the impact of facilities on business.
  • Use IT tools independently, with a proactive spirit, a strong team spirit and a continuous improvement approach.
  • Communicate fluently in French; have basic knowledge in English.
