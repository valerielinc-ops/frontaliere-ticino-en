Become part of our team!

Are you looking for a varied job where you can make a difference with your 40 or so colleagues? Then come to Raiffeisenbank Wasserschloss – a successful, locally anchored bank with offices in Gibstorf, Windisch and Brugg! Whether full-time or part-time, with us you will find the perfect balance between job and free time.

What awaits you?

  • With us you will find short decision paths and the opportunity to actively contribute your experience. You can implement ideas, take responsibility and shape your everyday work.
  • Digital First is part of our everyday life: You work with modern tools and processes and are open to using digital solutions in customer consulting.
  • Space for further development: We offer you an attractive overall package including training opportunities and fringe benefits, so that you can develop professionally and personally.
  • Du-culture and cooperation at eye level: Open communication, mutual respect and collaborative cooperation are important to us.

What do you bring?

  • You have a sound banking education and have gained several years of successful experience in the financial sector
  • Certification as a private client advisor (SAQ) is an advantage
  • As an experienced customer consultant with strong sales competence, you convince through a sustainable, needs-based and individual support of your customers
  • Thanks to your strong communication skills and your confident appearance, you build long-term customer relationships and inspire your counterpart.
  • You are an experienced, entrepreneurial personality who proactively contributes ideas, takes responsibility and contributes to the further development of the business
  • Connection to the region is beneficial

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Aargau?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Aargau's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
