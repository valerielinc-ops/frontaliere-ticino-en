Energy Supply Manager

Description of post

Occupancy rate: 8

Onward: to be agreed

Workplace: Monthey or Bulle

Your mission

  • Define, with management, the INERA partners' electricity supply strategy, coverage policy and risk limits.
  • Carry out futures, day\-ahead and intraday purchases, deposit appointments and manage flexible assets in order to reduce imbalances at the lowest cost.
  • Negotiate and monitor supply contracts (framework, bilateral, PPP) and acquire original guarantees.
  • Establish the purchase budget and cost of energy, monitor the outcome of the portfolio and report to management and partners.
  • Operate the EDM Energy system, manage the\-balance sheet group with Swissgrid and manage consumption forecasts with the data team.
  • Be the preferred interlocutor for partners, shareholders\-clients and suppliers, and ensure the day before markets and regulation (LApEl, OApEl, ElCom, REMIT).

Profile sought

Your profile and skills

  • Higher education in economics, finance, energy, electrical engineering, data science or related fields, with a solid knowledge of the functioning of energy markets
  • 3 to 5 years of experience with a GRD, supplier, aggregator or electricity trading desk, in energy purchase, portfolio management or spot markets.
  • Strong knowledge of Swiss and European electricity markets, balancing mechanisms, hedging instruments and energy contracts (EFET, PPP).
  • Very good mastery of Excel and reporting tools (Power BI, Grafana or equivalent), notions of Python; the practice of an ETRM system is an asset.
  • A sense of negotiation, decision-making capacity in a volatile market, rigour and excellent communication.
  • Common English and good knowledge of German (level B2\).

We offer

We offer you exciting and varied work, in a field of activity that shapes the energy transition and within a small team with a high level of skills. We offer flexible and modern working conditions, with the possibility of teleworking.
