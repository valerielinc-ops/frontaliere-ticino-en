Full description

Ready for new horizons? ####

It offers an exciting and versatile job with attractive employment conditions in a well-known industrial company in the Chur region.

The main tasks ####

  • Project management for the development of innovative plastic solutions in the fields of industry, consumer goods and mechanical engineering
  • Technical advice to customers and sales organisations on material selection and processing
  • Planning and implementation of tests and inspections at the pilot plant and at customers
  • Development of technical basics, feasibility analyses and cost accounting
  • Cooperation with various areas of expertise such as development, production and sales
  • Observation of market and technology trends as well as presentations and internal training

The requirement profile ####

  • Completed studies in mechanical engineering, plastics engineering, materials engineering, etc.
  • Several years of professional experience in injection molding technology and in the design of technical components
  • Good English skills in Word and Writing
  • Decision-making, purposeful and entrepreneurial-minded personality
  • High initiative, willingness to learn and flexibility
