Project Manager Switchgear Construction (80–100 %) (m/f)

6000 Lucerne Electrical engineering

  • Permanent position

Ofis Salary band 90,000 - 110,000 CHF

01 Future employer

For an established company in the field of electrical engineering and automation, we are looking for a dedicated professional who enjoys managing switchgear projects holistically and leading a team in both technical and personnel matters. You can expect a varied role with a high degree of personal responsibility, direct customer contact and exciting technical challenges.

02 Your role

  • You will independently manage several switchgear projects, from technical and commercial preparation through to successful handover.
  • In doing so, you will coordinate material procurement, resources and personnel deployment and ensure that the project runs smoothly.
  • You will lead the employees assigned to you and support your team with technical and organizational questions.
  • You will keep costs, deadlines and quality under control at all times and initiate suitable measures at an early stage in the event of deviations.
  • You will maintain professional relationships with customers and actively develop existing business relationships.
  • Thanks to your technical understanding, you will identify additional customer needs and thereby contribute to winning follow-up and additional orders.
  • You will work closely with internal departments and other project participants and ensure efficient collaboration.

04 Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as an automation technician EFZ, switchgear assembler or in a comparable electrical engineering profession.
  • Further training in switchgear construction, project management or electrical engineering is an advantage.
  • Professional experience in the planning, design or implementation of switchgear and control systems.
  • Initial or extensive experience in managing employees, as well as enjoying taking responsibility for a team.
  • Good knowledge of using CAD applications and a strong affinity for digital tools.
  • An independent, solution-oriented and structured approach to work.
  • Strong organizational skills and a good technical and commercial understanding.
  • A communicative, proactive and team-oriented personality who enjoys driving things forward.

05 Things to know

  • A responsible position with considerable scope for shaping things and streamlined decision-making processes.
  • Technically diverse projects in switchgear and control systems construction.
  • The opportunity to further develop both your technical and leadership skills.
  • A modern working environment with flexible working options.
  • Attractive conditions and performance-based compensation.
  • Individual training opportunities and prospects for your professional development.
  • A collegial and appreciative environment in which initiative is expressly encouraged.
  • The opportunity to take on long-term responsibility in a successful company and actively contribute to its further development.
