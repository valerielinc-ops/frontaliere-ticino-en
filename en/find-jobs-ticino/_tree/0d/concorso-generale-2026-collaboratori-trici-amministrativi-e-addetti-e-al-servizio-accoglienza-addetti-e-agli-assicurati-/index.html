General competition 2026

Administrative employees, reception service staff, Insured Persons III staff, telephone consultants, collection officers, bankruptcy administrators, secretaries, assistant secretaries, tax assessors and Administrative Manager at the Cantonal Administration

Grade and annual salary (including 13th monthly salary)

2 47'348.-- / 72'822.-- 3 52'756.-- / 81'560.-- 4 58'067.-- / 90'237.-- 5 63'297.-- / 99'178.--

Duties

  • perform administrative and secretarial activities in the various services of the Cantonal Administration or the Social Insurance Institute

Requirements

  • Federal Certificate of Competence as a Commercial Employee, or recognized commercial certificates/diplomas deemed equivalent
  • for the position of Telephone Consultant, a Federal Certificate of Competence as an Operator for Customer Communication is required
  • good command of Italian
  • basic knowledge of the other official languages
  • adequate IT skills
  • flexibility, organizational skills and interpersonal skills

Particular observations

  • depending on the needs of the services within the Cantonal Administration and the Social Insurance Institute, candidates for the indicated positions may be contacted for possible part-time/full-time employment, either fixed-term (substitutions or projects) or permanent
  • the place of work will vary depending on the positions available (preferences may be expressed in the application form)
  • the list of functions mentioned in the notice is not exhaustive; additional functions may be provided for within the salary grades listed above, depending on the needs of the various services and subject to assessment by the central services
  • assignment to a position corresponding to grades 3–5 is subject to several years of professional experience in the public and/or private sector

Particular conditions

Observations

  • as this is an annual competition, with the possibility of being hired during 2026, candidates will not receive a positive or negative response. They will only be informed about the validity of their application in relation to the required qualifications.

The Cantonal Administration will hire candidates based on the needs and professional profiles required by the various services.

Candidates who took part in the 2025 General competition, if still interested, must submit their application again together with all the requested documentation.

Conditions for submitting applications

  • applications must be submitted online via www.ti.ch/concorsi. Other forms of application will not be considered valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted when applying online (cover and motivation letter, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and employment certificates), further documentation may be requested at a later stage (criminal record extract for private individuals with the related self-declaration and self-declaration concerning health status)

Applications lacking the cover letter, curriculum vitae (CV) and the certificates/diplomas/certificates listed in the requirements will be rejected administratively. The possibility of resubmitting the application with all the requested documentation remains unaffected.

Employment conditions

  • appointment or assignment: these are governed by the Law on the organization of State employees and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and its related Regulation. Any legislative amendment concerning salary and organization is expressly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment relationship), flexible or according to service needs

Deadline

30 October 2026

Further information can be obtained by contacting the Administrative Management Area of the Human Resources Section, tel. 091 814 43 52/39.
