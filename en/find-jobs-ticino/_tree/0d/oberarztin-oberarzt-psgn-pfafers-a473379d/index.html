Your tasks

  • Together with the treatment team, you share responsibility for ensuring outpatient psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of patients with diseases from the entire diagnostic spectrum.
  • In the administrative area, you support the department-leading senior physician in the relevant activities and thus make an important contribution to the organizational processes of the department.
  • You play an active role in the organisational design of the ambulatory and ensure smooth interdisciplinary cooperation together with the team.
  • You accompany the assistant doctors assigned to you in their professional development, take direct responsibility for their further education and ensure continuous supervision.
  • In addition, you will participate in the recording and console service and contribute your technical expertise to the corresponding tasks. Your profile
  • As a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy, you have the necessary professional qualifications.
  • You bring a professional training permit from the Canton of St. Gallen or meet the necessary requirements to obtain it before starting your job.
  • Experience in the treatment of psychiatric patients forms an important basis for your work and enables you to deal safely with different treatment situations.
  • Your commitment and pleasure in working with patients distinguish you as well as your interest in working in a multi-professional team.
  • As a flexible, team-capable and communicative personality, you convince through your strong social skills and your motivation to continuously develop professionally and personally.
  • Working independently, assuming responsibility and making decisions safely are among your strengths and shape your professional way of working. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. We are on the road digitally and are committed to sustainability, so we look forward to your online application via our homepage. As soon as your application has been received by us online, you will receive a confirmation of receipt by e-mail. We are happy to get to know you based on your dossier and check whether your qualifications, skills and you as a person fit the advertised position and the psychiatry St.Gallen. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also get the opportunity to get to know your future team. You will receive a telephone confirmation from us if we decide for you after the second interview and ask you if you are still convinced of us. If we agree, we will draw up your contract. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. So you can get a differentiated picture of the company and already prepare for the job. On the first day you can expect an onboarding in your new team and with other colleagues. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also get your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
