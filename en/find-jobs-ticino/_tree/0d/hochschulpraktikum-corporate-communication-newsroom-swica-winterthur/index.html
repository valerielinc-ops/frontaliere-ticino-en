Full description

We are committed to health – because health is everything. We are committed to this, also as an employer. We are convinced: Only in a healthy working world do the best talents feel comfortable and move forward: professionally and personally. For that we give everything. You are part of the SWICA Corporate Communication team and take over the content management on the website for the Newsroom department. You make sure that the editorial quality requirements are met and are responsible for ensuring that the content is prepared attractively and played out via social media. In addition, you support the various communication channels of SWICA (website, customer magazine, Benevita app, etc.) editorially. After an introduction, the work can also be done in the home office. This is how you shape health

  • Management of the CMS and responsibility for publishing content from the newsroom
  • Editorial quality control of content from the newsroom
  • Image search and simple graphic processing of the image material used
  • Preparation and publication of social media contributions
  • Editorial support of the Corporate Communication team of SWICA incl. Benevita app

This sets you apart

  • Currently in or between a Bachelor's or Master's degree (Uni/FH) in Communication Sciences or a communication-related discipline
  • Excellent editorial skills, creative flair and interest in health topics
  • Experience working with MS Office, CMS tools, Adobe Creative Suite and Canva
  • Stylish-confident German in word and writing (level mother tongue), good French and English skills are advantageous

Why SWICA

  • We build on humanity. That is why we focus on openness, respect and trust in our everyday work. We tackle this, take responsibility and think in terms of solutions – together, for our customers. This is also why we have been number 1 in customer satisfaction for many years.
  • We trust you and give you space. Annual working hours, at least 5 weeks of holidays, part-time models and generous home office within the scope of the possibilities of the job – you make your everyday life largely flexible.
  • We value and reward your work with a fair market wage.
  • Because health is everything. With our company health management and region-specific offers, we take care of your well-being – so that you are healthy and energized.
  • We think long term. With three savings plans, pensum-dependent coordination deduction and benefits well above the legal minimum, you ensure good for your future.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed.
