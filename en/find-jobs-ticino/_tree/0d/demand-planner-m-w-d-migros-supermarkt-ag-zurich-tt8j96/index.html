Full description

The Demand & Distribution Management of the Operations Directorate is responsible for national demand planning and branch relocation processes. With the introduction of Relex and modern planning processes such as S&OE and S&OP, we are raising demand planning to a new level. If you are passionate about innovative technologies and data-based decisions, we look forward to your application.

Mansioni

  • Creation of data analysis and use of retail planning tool Relex for planning future customer needs and market trends
  • Efficient optimization of the necessary capacities and resources along the value chain
  • Identification of planning risks, development of risk mitigation strategies and preparation of sound decision bases for S&OE and S&OP taking into account relevant KPIs
  • Cross-departmental coordination and communication with relevant stakeholders
  • Communication of forecast deviations, validation of necessary adjustments and implementation along the entire value chain (E2E)

Requisiti

  • Mehrjähirge Professional experience: in demand planning with S4/Hana and by advantage Relex
  • Completed degree (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): in Business Administration, Supply Chain Management or Economic Engineering or equivalent training
  • Structured problem solving capability and the ability to present solutions clearly and convincingly
  • Constructive consensus between different stakeholders, even in challenging situations (e.g. in workshops)
  • Finded understanding of the business processes along a retail scale chain and ideally have experience with S4/Hana/IBP
  • First experience with planning solutions, for example Relex
  • German (flowing)
  • English (good knowledge)
  • French (of advantage)

Cosa offriamo

  • Modern infrastructure: varied working zones and modern co-working spaces with advanced equipment
  • Insurances: Up to 25% discount for various insurance companies such as car, private liability and household, legal protection and many more
  • Occupational pension: above-average benefits and three different savings plans to suit the individual life situation
  • Central place of work: Central place of work in the city of Zurich (Limmatplatz or near Hardbrücke)
  • Sports and fitness offers: Generous financial participation in courses of the Migros Club School
  • Design and decision-making space: possibility to design and develop the task field independently
