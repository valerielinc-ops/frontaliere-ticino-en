Full description

You enjoy the craft, like to work independently and value a clean execution? To strengthen our team, we are looking for trained record runners for permanent employment. At STAUB Ofenbau & Plattenbelag AG, you can expect varied tasks in new and renovations. With your expertise and craftsmanship, you will help to carefully implement the wishes of our customers. Your tasks

  • Plates on walls, floors and stairs
  • Working with ceramics, mosaic and natural and artificial stone
  • Preparation and inspection of substrates
  • Execution of joint and closing works
  • Working independently according to plans
  • Careful handling of materials, tools and machines

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as an EDC or equivalent
  • Reliable, punctual and independent work
  • Joy of craftsmanship and a good eye for details
  • Team spirit and friendly treatment of our customers
  • Responsibility and quality awareness
  • Category B driving licence

This is what we offer you

  • An annual job of unlimited duration
  • Varied work on different construction projects
  • A collaborative team that supports each other
  • The opportunity to work independently and contribute your expertise
  • Current employment conditions
