Salary Range: CHF65,870.00 - CHF122,330.00 Job Description Summary Support the operational implementation of biosafety and laboratory safety requirements within R&D facilities. The role focuses on hands-on biosafety activities, laboratory HSE inspections, and support of incident investigations. The position is designed as a temporary role (12 months) and requires a proactive, flexible, and team-oriented mindset. Job Description Work Arrangement: Onsite, #LI-Onsite Location: Basel, Switzerland Relocation Support: This role is based in Basel, Switzerland. Novartis is unable to offer relocation support: please only apply if accessible. Key Responsibilities:

  • Drive biosafety and laboratory safety excellence by supporting implementation of standards, ensuring compliance, and promoting safe and sustainable lab operations.
  • Strengthen risk management and prevention by conducting inspections, supporting risk assessments, and proactively identifying safety risks in laboratory environments.
  • Enable a strong incident management framework by supporting investigation of HSE incidents and near misses, driving root cause analysis, and ensuring timely corrective and preventive actions.
  • Enhance continuous improvement in safety performance through trend analysis, lessons learned, and embedding best practices across laboratory operations.
  • Collaborate closely with scientists, laboratory teams, and HSE colleagues to build a proactive and accountable safety culture across the organization.
  • Support and represent the team in selected leadership forums, contributing to decision-making and operational alignment through deputizing responsibilities where required.
  • Deliver impactful training and capability building in biosafety and lab safety, enhancing awareness and ensuring consistent adherence to standards.
  • Develop and continuously improve safety procedures, guidelines, and training materials, ensuring alignment with regulatory, HSE, and organizational requirements.
  • Foster a culture of collaboration, reliability, and structured execution, ensuring flexibility and responsiveness in a dynamic operational environment.
  • Contribute to personal and team growth by actively engaging in continuous learning and professional development within HSE and biosafety disciplines. Essential Requirements:
  • Initial professional experience in laboratory, research, or biosafety environments
  • Interest in lab safety and/or biosafety is an advantage
  • Strong communication and presentation skills
  • Confident presence in operational laboratory environments
  • Ability to work independently and in a structured manner Desirable Requirement:
  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in life sciences, biology, biomedical sciences, safety, or related field, or laboratory associate with relevant professional experience
