Key responsibilities Do you appreciate personal contact? You know the basics of banking and would like to develop further in consulting or in other areas of the bank? Do you feel comfortable in the digital world? Then contact us!

These are your core tasks

  • In the office, you represent UBS, warmly welcome customers and take on the role of host.
  • You advise customers on the topics of precautions and investing and develop your expertise in these areas further.
  • And finally, enthusiastically You are the customer for the digital world and help them deal with e-banking, mobile banking or TWINT.

The team We are a successful team. You can find us at the Heerbrugg/Heiden/Altstätten offices. Together with our colleagues, we serve our international customers and now want to have you in the team. Together we ensure that our customers leave the store satisfied. We like the proximity to the customers, the diverse area of responsibility and the good development opportunities in consulting and in other areas of the bank.

Your skills and experience

  • Banking interests you, and you ideally have an education and/or experience in the financial sector

  • You can inspire customers for digital banking
  • Working in a team gives you pleasure and you like to work towards common goals
  • You have stylish German and English in word and writing
  • If you have additional language skills, the better!
