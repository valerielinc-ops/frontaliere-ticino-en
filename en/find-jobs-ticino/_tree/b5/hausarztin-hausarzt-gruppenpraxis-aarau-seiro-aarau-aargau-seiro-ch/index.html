Full description

In a group practice in the region of Aarau, Canton Aargau, your patients are cared for by a family doctor over the entire breadth of general internal medicine, from prevention to diagnosis and long-term care of chronic diseases. We are looking for you immediately or by agreement as a specialist for general internal medicine in the amount of 60 – 100%. Medical practice assistants take over the laboratory, administration and appointment organization, so that you can concentrate on the office hours. What awaits you in this challenging task

  • Independent family practice with diagnosis, therapy and prevention in the field of general internal medicine
  • Holistic care and advice for patients in the ambulatory setting
  • Working with modern infrastructure and digital tools to support everyday practice
  • Close cooperation with an interprofessional team and various specialist areas
  • Active participation in the further development of medical quality and processes

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed specialist training in internal medicine
  • Experience in family care is beneficial
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods

That sets them apart.

  • High social competence and empathetic treatment of patients
  • Enjoying evidence-based medicine and quality care
  • Teamwork and interest in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Commitment and responsibility

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Modern group practice with digital infrastructure and efficient processes
  • Administrative discharge by supporting departments
  • Regulated working hours with high predictability
  • Attractive employment conditions and performance-related additional remuneration
  • Extensive training and development opportunities
  • Collegial, family working environment with motivated team
  • Central location with high quality of life and versatile leisure opportunities

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition
  • Assistance in organizing travel to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation
