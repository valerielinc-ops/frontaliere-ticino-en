Full description

You guide the customer competently through the entire ordering process. The company attaches importance to independent work and timely execution of missions. We are looking for an activity for our client

  • professionally handling service and industrial orders
  • accompanying customers through the entire ordering process
  • taking responsibility for material management
  • scheduling required materials in good time
  • carrying out assembly and repair orders on time
  • Requirements
  • Teaching qualification as an electrical installer or electrical installer EFZ or equivalent degree
  • Multi-year professional practice in electrical installation
  • Ideally already experience in management
  • Mandatory with driver's license category B
  • Self-employed and well-structured working methods Offer
