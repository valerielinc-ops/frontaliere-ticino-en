Head of Customer Service Sales Railway Infrastructure 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head of Customer Service Sales Railway Infrastructure Bern / Work Smart, October 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. In this key role, you will design customer service in sales in the railway infrastructure and ensure that service provision and billing are transparent, compliant and geared towards customer satisfaction. You work closely with sales and partner management, service providers and customers with a noticeable influence on the quality and impact of the services. You can expect a motivated team that relies on trust, service orientation and collaboration, and in which you actively shape culture and performance. You will further develop processes in a targeted manner in order to support processes even more effectively and use digital sales solutions in a targeted manner. Internal training and professional development paths support you in expanding your sphere of influence. You can move that. - As a manager, you are responsible for the customer service area in infrastructure sales and, together with your team, take on the hub function between sales and partner management, service providers from the overall infrastructure portfolio and customers. - You drive the constant optimization and digitalization of everyday work processes with practical sales applications and ensure the development of binding sales guidelines

  • You keep an eye on compliance and quality throughout the entire sales process, ensure documentation that complies with specifications and ensure consistent compliance with the framework conditions. - You are responsible for the consistent data quality of the product portfolio, customer and product master data right through to billing and work closely with finance and the infrastructure business areas across departments.
  • You will be responsible for central contract management with third-party customers and ensure complete, correct and compliant billing, coordinated with the relevant stakeholders.
  • As a leader, you establish and develop a trusting and performance-oriented team culture and specifically promote skills. This is what you bring with you.
  • You lead with clarity and empathy, communicate at eye level and approach conflicts in a solution-oriented manner.
  • You combine strategic and conceptual thinking with decisive action, a high level of service orientation and quality awareness.
  • You have several years of management experience in a comparable function as well as experience in sales and specialist management.
  • Confident handling of SAP and CRM systems as well as routine implementation of compliance and audit requirements.
  • Completed business training or further education at the college/university level. Very good knowledge of German at level C2 and ideally French at level B1. Knowledge of English or Italian is an advantage.
