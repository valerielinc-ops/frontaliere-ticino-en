Capoteam Strategic Plant Management Access to mobility (m/f/d) Apply now » Date: 5 Nov 2026

Place

Bern, CH

Company

SBB What we offer. As capoteam Strategic Management of Systems for "Access to Mobility", with your team, you contribute to shaping the future of plants open to the public and low voltage current utilities and provide an important contribution to an efficient rail infrastructure, oriented to customers and sustainable. As a member of the management team Strategic plant management, you contribute to further development of strategic plant management and ensure effective harmonisation between strategy, financing and maintenance of existing infrastructure. Find our benefits, such as children's allowances, 1. free class AG and discounted travel abroad – even for your family – on our website at the Benefit page. What you can move.

  • Within the new organizational unit directs and develops the strategic management team of plants Access to mobility thus strengthening intersectoral collaboration.
  • In this role you are responsible for the further strategic development of plant management for all plants open to the public and low voltage current utilities of SBB. Make sure our customers find an efficient, secure and sustainable infrastructure on the train route.
  • Take long-term financing of maintenance and renewal. Your team is responsible for the technical preparation of the offer for the maintenance of the existing infrastructure “Access to mobility” under the UFT performance agreement.
  • Keep moving from operational management to a more strategic one by promoting common goals within the team and partner organisations.
  • Together with your team ensure the implementation of the plant strategy by harmonizing strategic requirements with plant managers in the four regions (West, South, Center and East) and working closely with project portfolio management.
  • Make sure your plant strategy, performance, customer benefits and profitability are in line over the long term.

The one you bring with you.

  • To possess several years of management experience and effectively grow teams, ideally even in situations of change or integration.
  • Quickly analyze complex technical, organizational and financial issues and, on this basis, adopt pragmatic and strategically valid decisions.
  • Convince us thanks to a strong stakeholder management, you are able to negotiate at the same time and bring together different interests in a common goal. Transmitting knowledge and experiences has never been so easy.
  • You have knowledge of strategic management of infrastructure plants; experience in public transport or rail is a preferential title.
  • You have a university degree (SUP/Higher School/University) with technical or economical address, preferably integrated with continuous training in management, management or asset management.
  • You have excellent knowledge of written and oral German. Knowledge of a second national language (F/I). You can use your digital skills daily (IT, MS Office and tool).

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
