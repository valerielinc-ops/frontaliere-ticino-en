Full description

First try and then study. Imagine knowing exactly what your next step in your professional journey looks like. It would be clear to you which area of activity in health care suits you best. You would have a hot wire to a cool employer before starting your studies. Sounds like a moment of happiness, right?

Internship Care FH - Additional Module A Seize the opportunity and shape your future with us! At Merian Iselin Clinic, we are at your side with expertise and passion to support you on your way. The Merian Iselin Clinic is a state-of-the-art private clinic based in Basel. Our focus is on orthopaedics, surgery and urology. Are you interested? Then apply now.

Your main tasks

  • You accompany our nurses in their daily tasks
  • You listen attentively, observe, ask, learn and help – where possible

Your internship path

  • Two-month internship (additional module A) with us at the Merian Iselin Clinic
  • This internship fulfils the prerequisite for the Bachelor's degree program "Science FH in Pflege"
  • Certain (professional) degrees such as doctoral students in health and persons with an eidg. Certificates of competency Professionals Health and completed professional maturity do not require a nursing internship

You bring with you

  • Maturity certificate or certificate of proficiency
  • Federal certificate of competence

Our commitment to your internship

  • Refreshing insight into our care in a state-of-the-art clinic – up close, exciting, varied, up-to-date
  • Working in a family atmosphere
  • Excellent lunch in the restaurant at discounted prices
  • The Merian Iselin Clinic also offers internships during the full-time course of study for the additional module B as well as for the practice module elective mandatory
