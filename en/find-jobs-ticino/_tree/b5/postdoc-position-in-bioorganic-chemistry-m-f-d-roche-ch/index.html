The Position Are you interested in developing novel reagents and workflows for next generation sequencing? We are looking for a highly talented and motivated postdoctoral fellow (m/f/d) to join our team on this mission.

The Chemistry Technologies department provides the full spectrum of chemical diagnostic reagents, ranging from small molecules to biooligomers and polymers. Leveraging modern synthetic and analytical methods we design and develop tailor-made molecules to find the right solutions for diagnostic questions and beyond. We work as a team, are driven by passion for science and commitment to continuous learning to stay at the forefront of research.

Our culture is defined by trust, transparency, and a strong collaborative spirit. We are looking for a proactive colleague who balances independent critical thinking with team spirit and the flexibility to collaborate across diverse departments and interdisciplinary teams.

Your tasks

  • Design and synthesize complex oligonucleotide-conjugates by contemporary bioconjugation techniques and small molecular entities by chemical synthesis
  • Establish DNA templated proximity reactions for initial testing of your new reagent classes
  • Collaborate proactively with project partners from biology, analytics and the sequencing test development teams to gain a deep insight into the relationship of structural design and assay performance
  • Independently plan, execute and analyze experiments to effectively advance the projects
  • Present scientific progress through internal meetings and visit scientific conferences to ensure the state-of-the-art knowledge in the field of bioorganic synthesis, chemical biology and surface chemistry
  • Be part of a highly motivated and cross-functional team

Your profile

  • You hold a PhD in the field of (bio)organic chemistry, chemical biology or life science.
  • You possess profound knowledge in the synthesis of oligonucleotide-conjugates, preferably in context of biomolecules (proteins, peptides, nucleic acids)
  • You are well experienced in organic synthesis
  • Hands-on experience in the field of DNA-templated reactions, proximity reactions and nucleic acid detection completes your profile.
  • Your qualification should be clearly demonstrated by at least one first author paper published or accepted in a high impact journal.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills are your strength. You are sensitive to intercultural and interdisciplinary matters - working in a team comes natural to you.
  • You are a proactive individual with passion and commitment to innovate healthcare through our Roche Diagnostics Postdoc Program.
  • Excellent English skills are required, German is not mandatory.
