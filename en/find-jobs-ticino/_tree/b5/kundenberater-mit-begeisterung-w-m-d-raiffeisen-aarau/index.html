14.10.2026 Aarau

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Aarau-Lenzburg, a successful, cooperative and locally anchored bank with four offices, we are looking for a motivated personality who, with enthusiasm and curiosity, shapes the world of customer service personally, modernly and regionally.

What awaits you?

  • You accompany our customers in basic services such as numbers, saving and investing; always with a view to their individual goals.
  • You carry out basic advice on asset accumulation and optimization as well as provisioning and specifically address new opportunities.
  • You develop needs-based solutions that create real customer benefits.
  • You gain additional products and volumes through active cross-selling and a trusting customer relationship.
  • You are dynamically involved where you are needed; whether at the front desk, cash management or administrative tasks.

What do you bring?

  • Your foundation: A completed banking education with several years of experience in banking customer advice and enthusiasm to use your knowledge in every encounter.
  • Consulting is in your blood: You recognize customer needs quickly and develop suitable, creative solutions.
  • Your appearance makes the difference: With your positive charisma, you create an open, reliable and appreciative cooperation.
  • Flexibility and readiness: you think along, tackle and take responsibility
  • Even if it becomes dynamic.
  • Ideas are welcome: You actively bring your perspectives and act forward-looking.

With us you shape the future

We give you freedom for your personal development and your ideas, open up perspectives for individual careers and live closeness in daily cooperation. Together we shape a dynamic, modern and competent Raiffeisenbank – with flexibility, responsibility and enthusiasm for our region. Steffen Kauschka Head of Aarau Office Why Raiffeisen?

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer flexible working models

The work-life balance of our employees is important to us. That’s why we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time work.

We make “healthy living & working” a program

With our holistic offer "healthy living & working", we support our employees in finding a healthy balance between work and private life and maintaining it in the long term.

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
