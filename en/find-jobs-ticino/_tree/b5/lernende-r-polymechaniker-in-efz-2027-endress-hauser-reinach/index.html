Full description

Shaping the future together – your teaching at Endress+Hauser At Endress+Hauser, you don’t just work anywhere – you are part of a team working on exciting technologies for industry worldwide. Our approximately 18,000 employees develop, build and improve measuring instruments that are used all over the world. Collaboration, trust and team spirit. Because together we achieve more – and every idea counts. As a family-run company, we think long-term and invest in your future. With us you not only get a top education, but also many opportunities to develop and shape yourself.

What is the point about? Are you technically interested and fascinated by machines and manufacturing processes? Do you like to work precisely and want to know how high-quality components and devices are created from raw material? Then training as a polymechanic* in EFZ is just right for you!

In this training, you will manufacture mechanical components, set up CNC machines, build prototypes and support the commissioning and maintenance of machines and plants. You will gain in-depth insights into modern manufacturing technologies and work closely with different teams.

What tasks will you take on?

You will

  • machining of workpieces manually and mechanically.
  • Set up, operate and program CNC machine tools.
  • Install measuring instruments and equipment.
  • assemblies with welding technology.
  • Build prototypes and participate in commissionings.
  • Planning, monitoring and supporting production processes during maintenance work.

What do we expect from you?

You

  • You are in middle or upper school.
  • achieves good performance in mathematics, technical design and STEM subjects.
  • have craftsmanship and pleasure in technology.
  • You have good logical thinking and technical understanding.
  • Work carefully, team-oriented and, above all, reliable.

What can you expect from us?

  • Security and stability of an internationally operating family business
  • Very good takeover opportunities after training
  • A motivated and dedicated training team that will accompany you
  • Costs of the U-Subscription (Public)
  • Delicious and cheap lunch menus from 5.50 CHF in the company's restaurant

For your application we need

  • Letter of motivation
  • CVC
  • All upper secondary education certificates
  • S2-Check with evaluation in the profession of polymechanic
