Appointment authority / department / institution

The Jugendamt (YES) is committed to the children and young people of the canton of Freiburg. It is the cantonal specialist for the implementation of child and youth policy and the protection of minors.

Become part of our team!

JA’s External Family Care (SMA) sector consists of a small, dedicated team dedicated to the quality of child care. The SMA is the competent authority for the clarification, approval, supervision and accompaniment of the external family care. In this varied and meaningful area of activity, you can contribute and further develop your social and professional skills. The employer promotes the compatibility of work and private life and offers its employees a modern working environment and attractive working conditions. You can find out more about this on the following page.

Their tasks

  • Detection, authorisation and fishing of day parents within the framework of the implementation of the foster child regulation
  • Accompaniment and advice of care settings

Desired profile

  • Master or Bachelor in Social Work or Education Recognised as Equivalent
  • Interest in child protection and social education
  • Ability to analyze complex situations and ability to recognize the essentials
  • Sense of organization, resilience, teamwork
  • Driving licence is a prerequisite
  • Mastering the German language (verbal/written) with good knowledge of the French language
