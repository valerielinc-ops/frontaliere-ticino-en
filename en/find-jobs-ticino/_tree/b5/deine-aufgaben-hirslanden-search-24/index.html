Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna | Lucerne

Occupation by September 2027

Reference number: 52516

There is no more variety anywhere. Hirslanden Klinik St. Anna has 13 operating rooms in which, apart from cardiosurgical operations, all other surgical specialties are covered.

Do you want the exciting training to dipl. Complete surgical technique?

YOUR TASKS

  • You participate directly in the operation in the OPS and are responsible for the structural, human and material organization during operations
  • You look after and accompany patients during an operation
  • You practice instrumentation and service taking into account hygiene, asepsis and antisepsis
  • With in-depth expertise and trained observation skills, you ensure a smooth operating procedure

Your profile

  • Higher secondary education (completed vocational apprenticeship with a federal certificate of competence or a specialised school card or maturity)
  • Existing aptitude assessment
  • Joy of teamwork, exact working methods and a high sense of responsibility characterize you

Our offer

  • Since the end of April 2025, we have been working in a state-of-the-art surgical tract – become part of our team and meet professional, committed and open-minded teammates.
  • Long-term service planning and a good “work-life balance”
  • For only CHF 5.- a day you are provided with a meal in our varied staff restaurant, coffee, tea, mineral and seasonal fruits You are available for free
  • Attractive mobility concept
  • Paid changing time in the form of up to 4 additional free days
  • Further employment conditions and employee offers can be found here

Are you still undecided whether this profession really meets your expectations or you want to get to know your future place of work? We offer you a half-day insight day in our operating room.

For additional information, Kornelia von Arx, Head of Vocational Training Specialties at T / kornelia.vonarx@hirslanden.ch or Merlin Abgottspon, HR recruitment partner, at merlin.abgottspon@hirslanden.ch is at your disposal. We look forward to your contact.
