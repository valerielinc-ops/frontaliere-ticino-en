  • Our practice, in the centre of Kriens, has spacious treatment rooms with plenty of daylight, a large exercise hall, is wheelchair accessible and easily reachable by public transport.

In the 30 years that the practice has existed, we have built up a large patient base and work with all doctors in Kriens. Our patients come from all age groups, although the "more mature" patient is somewhat more represented. The range of treatments is very broad, which makes our work very interesting and varied. Our practice philosophy regarding the treatment of our patients is that, in cooperation with the patient and the prescribing doctor, we develop a realistic goal. The working atmosphere is very important to us, which is why we are looking for a colleague who fits well into our team. We look forward to a colleague who has life experience, is flexible and independent, and has empathy, team spirit and a sense of humor. Address
