Full description

Raben Sieber AG is a modern, forward\-thinking logistics company specialising in international transport and freight forwarding solutions. As part of a strong European transport group, we ensure that goods reach their destinations reliably, efficiently and on time every day.

Our success is driven by dedicated employees who take ownership, work together and are passionate about shaping the future of logistics. At Raben Sieber AG, you can expect short decision\-making processes, a supportive team environment and a wide range of development opportunities within an international network.

To strengthen our team in Bedano, we are looking for a motivated and committed individual to join us as a:

Dispatcher 100% (m/f/d)

Your Responsibilities

  • Independently plan, coordinate and monitor our groupage transport routes, ensuring efficient and cost\-effective operations.
  • Prepare transport documents and coordinate customer notifications and delivery announcements.
  • Support and coordinate our drivers, acting as an important point of contact in day\-to\-day operations.
  • Handle order entry, invoicing and amendments while maintaining a clear overview of all operational activities.

Your Profile

You are highly organised, able to keep a cool head even in stressful situations and enjoy finding practical solutions to challenges.

Ideally, you bring:

  • Completed vocational training and experience in a similar position.
  • Good geographical knowledge and a genuine interest in the transport and logistics industry.
  • Willingness to work shifts.
  • A reliable, open\-minded and responsible strong team spirit, fairness and excellent interpersonal skills.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
