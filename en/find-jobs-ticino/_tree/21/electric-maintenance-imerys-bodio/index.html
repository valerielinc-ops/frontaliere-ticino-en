The Company

Imerys is the world’s leading supplier of mineral-based specialty solutions for the industry with €3.4 billion in revenue and 12,300 employees in 40 countries in 2025. The Group offers high value-added and functional solutions to a wide range of industries and fast-growing markets such as solutions for the energy transition and sustainable construction, as well as natural solutions for consumer goods. Imerys draws on its understanding of applications, technological knowledge, and expertise in material science to deliver solutions which contributes essential properties to customers’ products and their performance. As part of its commitment to responsible development, Imerys promotes environmentally friendly products and processes in addition to supporting its customers in their decarbonization efforts. Imerys is listed on Euronext Paris (France) with the ticker symbol NK.PA.

The Position Electric Maintenance

Job Internationally recognized as a market leader, Imerys Graphite & Carbon serves various expanding industrial sectors such as electric vehicles, polymers, engineering materials and construction refractories. Research and innovation are the pillars of the offer of graphite and carbon based solutions, developed at our laboratories and produced at our plants equipped with the most cutting edge technologies. As part of the solid Imerys International Group, the Graphite & Carbon division has a Swiss headquarters and is globally present with a wide range of commercial and productive locations. Electric Maintenance (100%)

Your role and responsibilities

  • Execution of corrective, preventive, self-improved maintenance operations
  • Diagnosis of abnormalities, their causes and execution of repairs necessary for the installation/machine.
  • Replacement of electrical and mechanical components and related adjustment activities necessary for the installation/machine.
  • Drafting of the necessary documentation at the end of each intervention
  • Operational procedures
  • Piquet interventions
  • Participation in the study and implementation of projects in collaboration with the technical office
  • Development and processing of solutions for control and automation problems

Profile required

  • Federal Capacity Certificate (AFC) or Federal Practical Training Certificate (CFP) as Electromechanical / Operator in Automation / Mechatronic / Mounting Electricity
  • Detentor of Art. 13 OPI (authorization for special installation works).
  • Three-year experience in industrial maintenance
  • Excellent readability and interpretation of P&I, mechanical drawings, electrical schematics and tire patterns
  • Good knowledge of the usual computer media
  • Knowledge of industrial information and automation is considered a preferential element
  • Excellent knowledge of the Italian language, knowledge of the German and/or English language is preferential title
  • Flexibility and availability to carry out the service of picket (previous after an adequate period of introduction); to ensure the promptness of intervention required by the security protocols during availability, residence is necessary within 1h from the seat
  • Ability to work both in teams and independently
  • Spiccata proactivity in dealing with daily tasks, spirit of initiative and precision
  • Organized and with sense of responsibility
