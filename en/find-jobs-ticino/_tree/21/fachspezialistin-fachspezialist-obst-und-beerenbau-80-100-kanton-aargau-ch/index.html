Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Advice at eye level: You are the direct contact for companies and support them in all essential questions regarding fruit and berry cultivation
  • Sharing and moving knowledge: You plan and lead further education offers and make current specialist information practical and understandable
  • Drive innovation: Whether variety selection, new cultivation systems or modern crop protection – You work on current topics and contribute your own ideas to further develop the sector
  • Living networking : They work closely with research institutions, authorities and specialist organisations and build a bridge between theory and practice.
  • Using data with meaning: They coordinate surveys and evaluate specialist data in such a way that they offer real added value for production

What you bring

  • Your Foundation: A completed university or technical college degree in agronomy, horticulture or a related field
  • Your expertise: You are well-versed in fruit and berry cultivation and know where the shoe presses in practice
  • Your type: You communicate safely, enjoy being among people and enjoy communicating knowledge
  • You work in a structured and reliable way, but at the same time love to immerse yourself in new topics and actively shape things
  • Your motivation: You are looking for a varied mix of office, field and direct exchange with the producers

The Aargau is an important and versatile agricultural canton. As the agricultural center Liebegg, we are committed to an entrepreneurial and sustainable agriculture as well as a high level of competence in the areas of housekeeping and nutrition. Our core task is the transfer of knowledge in vocational training, further education, counselling and enforcement in specific areas. Our comprehensive education and counselling offer puts people first and leaves room for personal encounters. Become part of the Liebegger family!
