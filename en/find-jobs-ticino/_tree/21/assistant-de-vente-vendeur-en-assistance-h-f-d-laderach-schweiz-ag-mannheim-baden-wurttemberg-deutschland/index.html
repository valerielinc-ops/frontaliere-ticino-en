Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The “Retail” division comprises more than 230 chocolateries in more than 25 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again.
