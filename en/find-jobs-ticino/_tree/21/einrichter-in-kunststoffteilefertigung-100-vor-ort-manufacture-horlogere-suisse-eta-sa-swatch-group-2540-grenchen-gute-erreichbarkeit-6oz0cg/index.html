Installer/in plastic parts production 100%, on site | Manufacture Horlogère Suisse | ETA SA

Setter/in plastic parts production 100%, on site Together with your teammates, you work shifts and ensure the demanding qualitative and quantitative production standards. This includes the following activities:

  • Setting up and programming the injection molding machines and handling devices
  • Collaboration in samples / optimizations
  • Cooperation in the automation of injection molding processes and rework
  • Support of the shift leader (e.g. in the training of employees, reporting of deviations and irregularities in production)
  • Cleaning work on plants and in the department
  • Complete order documents and check for completeness
  • In addition, you have the opportunity to participate in projects and are in exchange with the departments of quality assurance, laboratory, toolmaking and logistics.
  • Your skills and competencies plastics technologist or installer with min. 5 years of professional experience in the field of injection molding
  • Enthusiasm for plastics technology, work in the micro sector and modern technologies
  • Readiness for shift operation (including readiness for night shift)
  • Enjoying process optimization, actively participating in improvements
  • Flair for Quality
  • High degree of independence and pleasure in working with different areas
