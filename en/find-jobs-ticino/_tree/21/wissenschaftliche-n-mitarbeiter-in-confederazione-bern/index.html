Put to the Point

The service area Bilateral Economic Relations (BW) promotes and maintains Switzerland’s economic relations with other states. The Asia and Oceania Department (BWAO) is one of four country competence centres and acts as a liaison and coordinating body for the protection of Swiss economic interests within the federal administration, vis-à-vis the partner countries and the private sector.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027 or by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20736
  • Place of work: Holzikofenweg 36, 3007 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • promotion and representation of Swiss economic and trade policy interests vis-à-vis the countries of Asia and Oceania
  • Coordination and handling of country-specific economic issues and concerns of Swiss economic actors
  • Close cooperation with FDFA, Swiss representations abroad and other federal agencies
  • Preparation and implementation of official visits, bilateral economic missions and joint economic commissions
  • Maintaining contact with other domestic and foreign authorities as well as the economy
  • Analysis of the economic policy situation and development and implementation of coherent foreign economic policy strategies towards partner countries

This makes them unique.

  • University studies, preferably in economics
  • 3-5 years of professional experience in the international environment and/or in economic diplomacy, ideally in the region
  • Economic and geopolitical knowledge of Asia and its role in the global economy
  • Strong analytical skills with a sense of essentials
  • Very good coordination and planning skills, structured working methods
  • High load capacity, time flexibility, good time management
  • Broad range of interests
  • Own initiative, commitment, discretion
  • Very good active knowledge of two official languages in word and writing. Very good oral and written English skills

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
