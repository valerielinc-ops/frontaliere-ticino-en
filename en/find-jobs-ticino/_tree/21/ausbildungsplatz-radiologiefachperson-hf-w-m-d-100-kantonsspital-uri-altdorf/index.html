Full description

The Cantonal Hospital Uri offers you a comprehensive range of services and provides the ambulatory and stationary extended basic care for the Canton Uri. Education is of great importance to us, and we attach great importance to individual support and promotion. For the start of training in September 2027, we are looking for your committed, responsible and motivated personality who is enthusiastic about an exciting and versatile education. YOUR TASKS

  • In diagnostic radiology, you conduct examinations of bones, internal organs, vessels or neural pathways
  • In radio-oncology, you use high-energy radiation specifically against pathologically altered tissue, plan radiation therapies and carry them out independently. You accompany and care for tumor patients over a longer period of time.
  • In nuclear medicine, using radioactively labeled substances, you represent the function and metabolism of organs and organ systems
  • Regardless of the respective area, you care, monitor and examine healthy and sick people professionally
  • You will always meet patients responsibly, in accordance with the situation and with high professional competence
  • You work interdisciplinary with other specialist areas to ensure the best possible care OUR REQUIREMENTS
  • Sek II degree (Federal Certificate of Competence, High School Matura, FMS)
  • Technical understanding, manual skill
  • Abstract logical and networked thinking
  • Good observation skills, quality awareness
  • Team and communication skills, independence and reliability
  • Flexibility and improvisation
  • Existing aptitude procedure of the Higher School YOUR PERSPECTIVES
  • You will receive a future-oriented education as a radiology specialist HF (m/f/d) in a modern and innovative working environment
  • You work with advanced technologies and diagnostic procedures at the latest state of the art
  • You benefit from a structured familiarization and close support by experienced specialists
  • You use internal training and further education to promote your individual strengths and achieve your career goals
  • Become part of a collegial and supportive team in an open and appreciative working atmosphere
  • Start your career in radiology and actively contribute to the health and well-being of our patients with our Co-Head of radiology, Oliver Marbet, will be happy to provide you with further information. Further information on the application and aptitude assessment as well as training offers can be obtained from the Center for Medical Education Medi Bern (www.medi.ch).
