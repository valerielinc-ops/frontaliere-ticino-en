Tasks

  • As a logistician, you will be used where consumer and production goods, packages and other goods are transported, stored and distributed.
  • At the University Hospital, you are responsible for the professional management of goods (e.g. medical disposable materials, laboratory, household articles, office materials, laundry, forms and food).
  • You receive them, check and register them in the system. When an order arrives, you prepare the corresponding goods for storage.
  • You also regularly check the stockpiles so that there are no overstocks or shortages.
  • In all areas, you use computers, smartphones and tablets as important tools. You use it to order goods, carry out stock controls, plan stock management and produce statistics. Requirements
