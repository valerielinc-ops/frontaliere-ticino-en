Put to the point

Promoting peace – our mission The SWISSINT Competence Center is constantly recruiting medical professionals for temporary missions abroad in favour of the promotion of peace of the Swiss army. Based on the voluntary nature of these missions, interested women and men can apply for various functions. The basic prerequisite for obtaining an assignment contract is the successful existence of the employment-related training at the SWISSINT site in Stans-Oberdorf.

  • Employment type: indefinite
  • Place of work: Kasernenstrasse 4, 6370 Stans-Oberdorf

Your tasks

  • Responsibility as driver for all transports with the ambulance Sprinter and maintenance of Swiss ambulance vehicles
  • Cooperation at the Swiss Medical Center in Camp Film City in Pristina and acquisition of orders from Chief Nurse
  • Support in the management of patients,
  • Co-responsibility for medical supplies and medicines, as well as planning of reserve materials and control of all medical devices
  • Assistance in medical emergencies (legs of poor access, wound care etc.)
  • Close cooperation with Austrian comrades

This makes you unique

  • Completed vocational training or study, ideally in the medical field
  • Knowledge of the German language (min. B2) is desirable for this function for the safety of the comrades and for the safety of their own and for a smooth cooperation with Austrian colleagues and locally employed persons.
  • Knowledge of the English language (min. B2)
  • Civilian driver's pass (C1/D1)
  • Age between 18 and 65
  • Completed recruiting school of the Swiss army, women with Swiss citizenship without basic military training are trained according to the function military

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to the success model of Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Lived diversity Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

SWISSINT is the national commando of operations for the military peace promotion of the Swiss army and is responsible for the planning, provision and national leadership of all Swiss military contingents and individuals in the army mission to promote peace.

Additional information

For further information please contact the Competence Centre SWISSINT, tel. 058 467 58 58. Application forms can be found at: www.peace-support.ch

The Federal Administration respects the different needs and life experiences of its employees and promotes their diversity. Equal treatment is the highest priority.
