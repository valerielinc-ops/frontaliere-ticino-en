Full description

Do you have a feeling for high-quality grill products and are enthusiastic about offering our customers a unique taste experience? 🍖🔥 You are creative, strong sales 💪 and enjoy convincing with your passion for good food and perfect service? 😊 Then you are exactly right with us! ✅ Let us be "tämä Successful". 🤝🧡

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, M Beringen, Schaffhauserstrasse 72, 8222 Beringen

Mansioni

  • You prepare the sausages 🌭 on the outside fresh and with much care – of course in compliance with the hygiene regulations.
  • You greet our customers with a smile 😊, advise them competently and ensure a great shopping experience at the sausage stand.
  • You keep the barbecue stand clean and tidy so that everything remains hygienic and inviting 😊️ and take care of the construction before the beginning and the dismantling at the end – thanks to you everything runs smoothly.
  • You serve our customers at the checkout 💳 reliably and correctly.

Requirements

  • Experience: as a griller or in the retail trade an advantage
  • You are physically fit and bring a high level of commitment 💪🚀.
  • You convince through your reliability and flexibility. 🤝✨
  • Friendly and competent treatment of our customers 😊🛍️ are a matter of course for you.
  • You love to stand on the grill 🔥, make the sausages perfect and ensure a good mood.
  • No education/training required
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Handy subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses
