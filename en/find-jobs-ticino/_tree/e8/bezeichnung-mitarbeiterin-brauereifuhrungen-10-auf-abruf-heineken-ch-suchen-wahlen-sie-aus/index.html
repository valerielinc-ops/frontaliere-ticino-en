At the location in Lucerne, of course, the traditional brand Eichhof is at the heart of the event. You have great pleasure in dealing with people You have a great interest in the world of beers and can convey emotions and knowledge well and gladly You are an excellent host and love to inspire fellow human beings You are independent of time and flexible You are ideally living in the Lucerne region (Swiss German important) If you reach the full score, then read more... In this lively and extremely communicative task you become the heart of our brewery world. With your open style and flair for host culture, you will accompany our visitors professionally through the fascinating world of brewing and make every guided tour a special experience. After the tour you turn the stay of our guests into a delightful finish: you enter the beer tasting, explain the peculiarities of our varieties and soar with a small snack for the appropriate culinary framework. With enthusiasm, authenticity and a lot of joy in sharing with people, you can create an unforgettable experience that keeps our guests in the best memory for a long time. After the tour and tasting, you are responsible for the premises being clean and neatly available for the employees the next day.
