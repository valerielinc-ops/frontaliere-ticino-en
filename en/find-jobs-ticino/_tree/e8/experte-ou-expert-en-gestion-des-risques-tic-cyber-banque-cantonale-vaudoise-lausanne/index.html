Full description

What if your professional history goes on at BCV's IT? Put your skills and experience at the service of one of the few banks that develops, maintains and ensures the security of its own Information System. The endless and varied professional opportunities within the 3 IT departments offer you the opportunity to carry out strategic projects, which will contribute to the success of the entire bank. For our Internal Audit, we are looking for:

Announcement ICT & Cyber Risk Management Expert or Expert for Internal Audit Your main missions:

  • planning, conducting and carrying out internal audit missions specific to ICT (information and communication technologies) & cyber risk management, business continuity and business resilience of the BCV Group
  • providing ICT & cyber expertise and expertise in cross-cutting audits
  • verify that the elements of the ICT & cyber system, its protection measures and the internal control system in place are complete, appropriate and effective, and in accordance with existing guidelines, standards and good practices (COBIT, ITIL, NIST, ISO, etc.)
  • actively contribute to the operation of technological and regulatory monitoring and assess its impact on existing audit areas
  • contribute, as Subject Matter Expert (SME) to the development and modernization of audit techniques and methodologies
  • participate actively in the preparation and presentation of reports to auditees and bodies
  • contribute to the deployment and evolution of the application portfolio operated by the Internal Audit Department. Your profile:
  • advanced computer and/or information security training EPFL/HES, CISSP certification(s), CCSP, CISA, CISM and/or AAIA an asset
  • proven practical expertise in the operational management of ICT and/or cyber risks (including aspects of cloud computing and artificial intelligence), business continuity and/or operational resilience (ideally in banking or with high normative and service requirements)
  • ability to prioritize in a demanding environment
  • rigor, analytical mind, capacity for synthesis, easy communication
  • very good writing skills in French, good knowledge of English and/or German, an asset
  • residence in Switzerland.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
