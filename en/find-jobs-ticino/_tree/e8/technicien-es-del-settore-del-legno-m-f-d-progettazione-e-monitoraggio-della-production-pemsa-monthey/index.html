We at Pemsa offer the perfect balance between the flexibility of temporary work and the safety of indefinite employment. Forty of 30 years of experience, we strongly support hundreds of Swiss companies specialized in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry, providing them with high-level talents. Our standards are high: we value motivation and enthusiasm, because they are the basis of excellence. If you want to join a dynamic and motivated team, the position of (M/F/D) wood industry ES Technician – Design and production monitoring awaits you to face exciting challenges.

Your assignment

  • Ensure the technical design of wood construction elements.
  • Make production and execution drawings on Cadwork.
  • Prepare processing data for CNC machines with Lignocam.
  • Monitor the progress of the works in the workshop and in the yard.
  • Check the quality of the works and compliance with deadlines.
  • Participate in technical training of apprentices.

And now we're talking about you?

  • ES technical diploma in wood / carpentry or equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland.
  • At least 3 years of experience in Switzerland in the wood construction industry.
  • Cadwork's mastery.
  • Good knowledge of Triviso and Lignocam.
  • Master of MS Office tools.
  • French level B2 minimum, both written and spoken.
  • License B and personal vehicle indispensable.
  • Residence no more than 30 km from the workplace.
