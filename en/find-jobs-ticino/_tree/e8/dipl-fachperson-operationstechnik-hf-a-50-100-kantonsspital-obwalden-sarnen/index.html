Dipl. Specialist Operating technique HF (a) 50 - 100 % Immediately or by agreement The Cantonal Hospital Obwalden – versatile, personal, close. As a modern primary care hospital in Central Switzerland, we offer a broad range of operations and a family working atmosphere with short decision-making paths. With us, you are an important part of the team – both professional and human.

To strengthen our surgical team, we are looking for a committed personality with pleasure in precision, responsibility and interdisciplinary cooperation.

Your tasks - meaningful and versatile

  • Independent instrumentation and service in surgical procedures in the fields of surgery/traumatology, orthopaedics, gynecology and obstetrics, ENT, urology, spinal surgery and ophthalmology
  • Ensure smooth operation of the operating room in close cooperation with all professional groups involved
  • Preparation, operation and maintenance of equipment, instruments and consumables
  • Complete documentation of nursing services incl. Power measurement
  • Active responsibility for patient safety and high quality standards
  • Support and support of students HF in everyday surgery

This is what you bring - technically strong, humanly convincing

  • Completed training as a dipl. Professional surgical technique HF or equivalent qualification
  • Professional experience in the operating room is beneficial – motivated new entrants are also welcome
  • High sense of responsibility and strong technical and social competence
  • Structured, independent and reliable working method
  • Teamwork, flexibility and joy in a dynamic environment

What to expect from us - because your work is valuable

  • A workplace in an attractive location in Sarnen on the lake, 5 minutes walk from the train station
  • A team that holds together, supports each other and laughs together

Modern infrastructure and a professional, well-organized working environment Careful training and targeted promotion of your professional and personal development
