Full description

You also keep track of several construction sites at the same time and love to lead projects step by step to success? ####

With an established real estate service provider, you take on versatile building construction projects with a high level of personal responsibility - from calculation to acceptance - and actively drive modern BIM processes forward.

The main tasks ####

  • Independent organisation and management of the construction site
  • Cost calculation, tendering, submissions and supplementary management
  • Management and control of deadlines, costs and quality
  • Coordination of companies, specialist planners and project participants as well as management of construction meetings
  • Acceptance, defect management and construction administration and project documentation

The requirement profile ####

  • Completed training as construction manager, technician HF, draughtsman architecture or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in construction management in Swiss building construction
  • Good knowledge of Swiss construction practice and standards
  • Experience with Messerli Bauad and MS-Office
  • Structured operation and communication strength
