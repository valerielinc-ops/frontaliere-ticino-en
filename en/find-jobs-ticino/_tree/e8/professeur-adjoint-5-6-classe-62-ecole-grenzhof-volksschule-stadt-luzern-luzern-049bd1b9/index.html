More on the spot

Further information about the Grenzhof school operation The school Grenzhof includes six primary school classes and two kindergartens. In our small and family school, we attach great importance to a lively school culture. In mixed-age school families, our students practice co-determination and experience democratic processes. We are also musically engaged: around 50 children are currently singing in the school choir. Pedagogically, we are guided by the concept of the New Authority. Our schoolhouse is located directly on the Gütschwald, which is regularly visited by our classes and used as a learning and experience space.
