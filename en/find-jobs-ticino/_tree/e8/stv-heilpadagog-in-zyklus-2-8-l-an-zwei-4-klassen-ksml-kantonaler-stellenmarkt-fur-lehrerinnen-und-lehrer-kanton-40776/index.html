Full description

Portrait The school Manuel is a managed quarter school (Schulkreis Kirchenfeld-Schosshalde) of the city of Bern. From the summer of 2026, the school will lead 46 classes from kindergarten to 9. School year with about 900 children and adolescents and 130 employees. The school location Manuel includes the school complex Manuel, the Wittigkofen School House, the Elfenau Quarter School House, the Kirchenfeld full-day base level and day care.

Tasks

They accompany and support the children in two 4th classes and their individual support needs (8 L. MR). They work closely with the class team. They maintain an exchange with all parties involved. They work in the college and participate in the conferences (block on Mondays 16.45 to 18.00).

Requirements

Master of Arts in Special Needs Education or in Training Master's programme School curative education The joy of promoting and teaching Teamwork, flexibility, resilience Experience Cycle 2 Humour

We offer

  • A motivated and helpful team
  • Submission of the SuS dossier
  • Good infrastructure
  • Support from the school management MR on site
