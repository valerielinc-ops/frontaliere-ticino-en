Full description

You have a solid experience and you want to share this expertise with our customers? Do you like working in a context where every day is different? Are you in the morning and available from 5:00? This opportunity is for you! Join our team and help make the customer experience unique!

Mansioni

  • You're the first to arrive at the store and arrange for the loading and unloading of trucks.
  • You're in charge of handling stains in the back and sorting pallets.
  • You participate in the management of freshness quality by ensuring that each product meets our quality standards.
  • You ensure that the products of the food department, bakery and butcher shop are placed on the shelf.
  • You can cook bakery products.
  • You help set up promotions and commercial actions.

Required

  • 1-3 years of experience: in the retail sector
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Retail Manager
  • Rigor, organization and autonomy
  • Excellent communication skills and strong team spirit
  • Versatile, dynamic and always with a smile
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Food: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.
