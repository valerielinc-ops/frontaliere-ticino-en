The Position

Your mission

As a Key Account Manager, Public Accounts & Lead Spectrometry of Mass (d/f/m) and attached to the SubChapter Lead Key Account Manager, you are a dynamic team and a Strategic Account Expert. You are the voice of customers and represent Roche with them. As such, you have a perfect understanding of the mapping of stakeholders and the decision-making matrix of accounts, the environment, and the strategic issues and visions of clients. You also have an accurate view of Roche's competitive position vis-à-vis these issues, allowing you to define and take responsibility for the commercial and financial strategy of existing and future accounts. Finally, you define and implement the negotiating strategy.

This hybrid position combines responsibility for a portfolio of Strategic Hospital Units of Territory (GHT), distributed mainly in a sector covering the Centre de la France and some neighbouring regions, with the role of Lead Spectrometry of Masse for Roche Diagnostics France. In addition to your Key Account Manager activity, you manage this activity at national level by coordinating strategic projects, bringing together the relevant business teams and ensuring the interface between Roche Diagnostics France and international teams, in accordance with the strategy defined by the subsidiary.

Your main responsibilities Key Account Management - Public accounts

  • You determine the Roche Diagnostics France strategy to deploy for the major public accounts under your responsibility, identifying the levers supporting the development of a strong partnership that can generate value for all parties.
  • At all stages of the business relationship, you identify and seize the different opportunities within the accounts to contribute to the achievement of Roche's strategic objectives; in collaboration with internal experts, including business teams, you co-design and propose Roche solutions tailored to the needs of customers and differentiating. You are responsible for the achievement of business objectives, the evolution of turnover and the profitability of accounts.
  • In close collaboration with the Offer Call Project Leader, you coordinate the project team involved in building the response and ensure that it will achieve the expected commercial and financial goals. You also make sure that you have the right contract in line with the rules of the Group and the subsidiary.
  • Responsible for the proper execution of the framework contract and customer satisfaction, you set up and follow the relevant performance indicators for the accounts under your responsibility and engage the teams on continuous improvement of the actions. You are the guarantor of the relevance and effectiveness of the communication scheme within the accounts.
  • Squad Lead: In collaboration with Chapter/SubChapter Leads, you identify the skills required and build a team committed to client needs, business strategy, achievement of objectives and action plan.
  • You make sure that the members of the squad understand the current account and our competitive position so that the team can prioritize its actions. You keep the team informed
