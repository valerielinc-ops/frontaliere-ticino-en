POSITION: Mechanical Design Intern

SUMMARY

The Product Development (PD) group is involved in the design, validation and documentation of new product design and upgrades to existing product ranges. The successful candidate will work on project support as assigned by Engineers in the PD group.

Position is full-time during the summer. Half time during the school year.

ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS

Training will be given to accomplish the following

· Hands-on support of component testing and data collection. · Drafting support using NX 3D/2D system. · Engineering support work for projects as assigned. · Release of drawings / documents through SAP & Teamcenter ERP systems.

SECONDARY RESPONSIBILITIES

· Adheres to company and department policies, procedures, and standards · Supports, cooperates with, and participates in company-wide ESG & EHS Management systems, which includes following all safety, health and environmental policies and procedures. Supports and cooperates in company-wide Quality Management effort. · Performs other duties as assigned

QUALIFICATIONS

· Completion of freshman year of BSc Mechanical Engineering degree. · Excellent interpersonal, communication and analytical skills. · Sound computer skills in Word and Excel. · Experience with 2D and 3D CAD systems · An Interest in rotating machinery and manufacturing is beneficial.
