Your surroundings

They work in a modern environment on the wards of Klinik Waldhaus in Chur and are part of a dedicated hotel industry team. In direct contact with our patients, you help shape the stay, take on domestic tasks and benefit from a supportive, collegial working environment.

Their tasks

  • They look after our patients about food (ordering meals, serving meals, etc.)
  • They carry out all domestic activities incl. Basic cleaning on the respective stations independently and professionally
  • Her area of activity is working in the lounge, room and station kitchen
  • You are responsible for compliance with our high quality standards in the area of room maintenance, cleanliness and sense of order
  • With your committed and heartfelt nature you are an enrichment for the operation and thus for our patients

Our requirement profile

  • You preferably have completed training in the field of hospitality / catering
  • You are a calm, patient and physically robust personality with a high level of commitment and service awareness.
  • High operational readiness and flexibility in a 7-day operation and thus also the weekends are a matter of course for you
  • Teamwork, loyalty and flexibility are important to you
  • They are reliable, dutiful and work speditively
  • They have a well-kept appearance as well as very good German and computer skills

We offer you

  • A versatile and varied activity in a lively operation
  • You can contribute your experience in various areas of the hospitality industry
  • You work in a pleasant, social environment
  • You benefit from progressive working conditions (above average social benefits, holiday credits of at least 30 days)
  • You have the opportunity to attend further education courses
