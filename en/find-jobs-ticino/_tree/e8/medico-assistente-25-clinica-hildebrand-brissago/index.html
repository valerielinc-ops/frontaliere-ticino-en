Full description

The Hildebrand Clinic Brissago Rehabilitation Centre is one of the most important centres of Swiss Rehabilitation. Our clinic stands out for the modern, innovative and perfectly equipped infrastructure that supports universally recognized diagnostic and therapeutic methods. The range of performance ranges from neurology, physiology, cardiology, geriatrics. The Clinic has 120 beds, operating according to the mandates of the Cantons Ticino and Graubünden under rehabilitation, is a member of REHA TICINO and the Swiss Rehabilitation Clinics Association (SW!SS REHA). We are Recognised Postgraduate FMH Training Center:

  • in Neurology, category D 2 (2 years)
  • in Physical and Rehabilitative Medicine, category B (1 year)
  • in Neuroriabilitation (1 year) To complete our team we try: Medical assistant training (100%) Profile: The assistant doctor we seek will be able to directly manage the clinical activity of department (17 beds), subjecting to the direct supervision of the medical chief clinic and chief of reference service. It stands out for having a good spirit of initiative, the ability to work both autonomously and interdisciplinaryly to optimally interface with the other professional figures of the rehabilitation team (physiotherapists, ergotherapists, neuropsychologists, speeches, social and dietary assistants). It will be given value to strong communication and empathy skills to relate with patients suffering from severe disability both acute and chronic and with their family members. Requirements:
  • Medical title (or equivalent title with MEBEKO recognition)
  • Work experience in clinical hospitals
  • Spirit of initiative, strong relational and communicative and empathic skills
  • Organizational capacity and preparation for interdisciplinary collaboration with a view to continuous improvement of service development
  • Flexibility and positive attitude, pleasure to work in a team and collaborate
  • Curiosity for the continuous innovations of the medical-rehabilitative field and desire to personally contribute in the internal training activities (reduction of articles, preparation of presentations for the Journal Club of continuous training)
  • Knowledge of the Italian language or availability to improve it in a short time (B2 level required by Mebeko). Date of entry: to be agreed. If you recognize yourself in the profile and in the values sought, submit your application with the words “medical assistant in training” to the following e-mail address: candidature@clinica-hildebrand.ch Corredate by letter of motivation, curriculum vitae, copies of the qualifications and the certificates of work. Applications submitted in paper form will be deleted without further information. For further information please contact Dr. med. Paolo Rossi, chief, tel. +41 91 786 86 86 86

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
