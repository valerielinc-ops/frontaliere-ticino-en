Put to the Point

We are looking for a motivated and talented learner who supports the EJPD in various commercial tasks. You will not only be accompanied and supported by our motivated practical trainees, but also have the opportunity for internal networking opportunities with the other learners and interns. Your education will also be enriched by the annual learning camp.

  • Date of entry: 01.08.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: 401-20495

You can make this contribution

  • Administrative tasks in the asylum procedure (e.g. processing of entry and exit, recording of personal data, identity documents, evidence)
  • Organisation of appointments (including surveys, registration of age reports, transfers, internal events)
  • Processing external requests
  • Material management
  • Perform commercial controlling tasks (e.g. create evaluations and prepare them graphically)
  • Assistance in the organisation of events and other staff tasks
  • Write documents such as letters, emails, logs and fact sheets
  • Provide telephone and written information

This makes you unique

  • You are a motivated, ambitious and responsible person
  • You are capable of teamwork and have good communication skills
  • You have a high level of learning and commitment.
  • You master the ten finger system
  • You have basic knowledge of the MS-Office programs

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Vocational training FYD – We form the future!

The training company EJPD promises excitement and diversity. It deals with socio-political issues such as the coexistence of people of Swiss or foreign nationality, asylum issues, internal security or the fight against crime. Civil status or civil rights issues are just as much a part of the FJPD’s remit as the supervision of gambling. The optimal start to the world of work in August 2027 for committed and motivated young people.

Are you interested now? Then apply for the apprenticeship with us at Eidg. Department of Justice and Police – we are delighted.
