Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. His ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges ahead, HIB is looking for:

Medical Assistant/Medical Secretary Multipurpose 80%

Your Mission

To ensure the proper functioning and continuity of the various medical secretariats, by replacing the medical assistants who are absent or by providing ad hoc support according to the needs of the various departments (secretariats and medical archives) at our various sites.

Your activities

  • Patient reception
  • Managing various internal and external medical appointments

Administrative management

  • Write medical reports (consultations, operating protocols, exit letters, etc.)
  • Ensure that medical reports are sent to the various recipients
  • Follow-up of interventions and coordination of different pre- and post-operative appointments
  • Administrative monitoring of medical records
  • Scanning of external medical documents
  • Check the billing of the services performed and bill the services if necessary

Communication

  • Ensure communication between patients, physicians and other health professionals
  • Taking and transmitting telephone messages effectively

Assistance

  • Blood take, TA, injections, ECG, Holter, bandages
  • Send samples to the laboratory and prepare the necessary materials for consultations
  • Clean, disinfect, and store consultation materials and rooms after each patient

Miscellaneous

  • Managing medical and administrative equipment orders related to consultations

Your assets

  • SWC of medical assistant, CASS or SWC of commercial employee supplemented by training of medical secretary, with experience confirmed in technical gestures (ECG, blood tests, blood pressure)
  • Prior experience of at least 1 year in a medical secretariat
  • Good knowledge of medical terminology and hospital administrative procedures
  • Aisance with the usual computer tools (Outlook, Word, Excel) and ideally the following software: Opale, Soarian, Ultragenda, Medex
  • Organizational sense, ability to manage priorities and good stress resistance
  • Versatility, rigor, team spirit and strict respect for confidentiality
  • Flexibility required in working days.

Start: to be agreed.
