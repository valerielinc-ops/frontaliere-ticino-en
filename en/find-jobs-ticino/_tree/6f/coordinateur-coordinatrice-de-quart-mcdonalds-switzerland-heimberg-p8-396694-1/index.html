Description of post

As a Watch Coordinator, you are the benchmark for your team and hosts. You organize and direct your shift, make sure that everyone feels safe and well. You motivate the team, help where needed and bring your positive energy to each shift. You are an example of food safety, quality, service, cleanliness and hygiene, and you are always ready to coach and support your team.

Key responsibilities

  • Organize and direct your shift for a smooth experience at the restaurant
  • Ensure a safe and welcoming environment for customers and the team
  • Maintain high standards of quality, service, cleanliness and hygiene
  • Support the team in rush moments – in the kitchen, in the counter or in the room
  • Promote teamwork and motivate employees
  • Open communication and conflict management with empathy and respect
  • Use management tools (planning, cash, orders, inventory)
  • Support training and development initiatives

Qualifications required

  • Reliable, responsible and positive attitude
  • Love working with people and leading a team
  • Open, empathic and respectful communication
  • Proactive, inventive and eager to learn
  • Analytical and comfortable mindset with technology
  • Resistant, flexible and optimistic
  • Good local language proficiency

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
