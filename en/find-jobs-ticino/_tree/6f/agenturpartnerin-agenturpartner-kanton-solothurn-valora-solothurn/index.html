Are you looking for an entry into self-employment? Valora as an established convenience and food service provider A turnkey and over years proven and successful business model. We support you beyond the new opening of your store, so that you not only successfully master the start, but also feel comfortable as an agency partner at our side in the long term.   • Managing one or more well-frequented Valora stores as an independent entrepreneur under your own responsibility
  • Staff management incl. Assignment planning, recruitment, training and training of employees
  • Goods management (inventory, stock, orders)
  • Proactive customer advice and development / maintenance of customer relationships
  • Close cooperation with the suppliers and the responsible sales management

  • Interested in self-employment and the management of one or more stores

  • Entrepreneurial thinking and acting, team spirit and organizational talent
