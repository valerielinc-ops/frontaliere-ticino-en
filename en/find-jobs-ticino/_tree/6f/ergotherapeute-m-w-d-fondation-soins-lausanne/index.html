Full description

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and fairness. By joining the Lausanne Care Foundation, you are committed in a concrete way to promoting health and well-being in the canton of Vaud, within expert, motivated and enthusiastic teams, in a varied activity rich in human contacts. Our office of transport of people at Mobility Réduite (TMRL) in the Lausanne region has the task of coordinating transport for people with mobility difficulties. This office brings together the home care organisations of the Lausanne agglomeration (Foundation Care Lausanne, APROMAD, APREMADOL) and Pro Infirmis.

In order to complete our teams following the departure of a collaborator, the CMS of the Lausanne Care Foundation is looking for a:

Ergotherapist (M/F)

Participation rate 20% Fixed-term contract

Your Mission

  • Record and follow up evaluations conducted by CMS.
  • Interview with evaluators (e.g. occupational therapists, nurses and social workers) of the CMS in Lausanne and surrounding area and Pro Infirmis when the assessments are incomplete or inconsistent.
  • Validate the allocation of one or more transport providers for customers with reduced mobility.
  • Ensure monthly updating and transmission of re-evaluation lists for the next three months to CMS and TMRL respondents.
  • Provide support and training to evaluators through regular presentation of TMRL benefits.
  • Provide training to drivers involved in TMRL races.
  • Collaborate, in an advisory capacity, with the TMRL Manager to improve the performance of the TMRL Office in terms of evaluation.

Your profile and skills

  • Bachelor's degree in occupational therapy or equivalent (foreign diploma recognized by the CRS)
  • Authorization to practice or ready to initiate the proceedings - conditional on the undertaking
  • Experience in physical or psychiatric hospital service, rehabilitation service, home care or private practice an asset
  • Strong interest in community health
  • Ability to collaborate in networking and multidisciplinarity
  • A sense of responsibility, ability to organize independently and set priorities

We offer

  • A stimulating and warm work environment where the caregiver's autonomy is emphasized
  • A working environment that favours human contact and exchange
  • Exciting work in a pleasant environment for the well-being of clients
  • Fair working conditions and remuneration
  • Conditions of occupational security which extend beyond the minimum benefits provided by law
  • Flexibility of working hours

Benefits of joining us

  • Plan for integration by occupation, offers of rich and varied continuing training, opportunities for vocational development
  • Gender pay equity (fair-on-pay certification)
  • Remuneration according to the Collective Labour Convention (CCT) of the public health sector of voodoo
  • Favourable offers with preferential rates in service companies around sport, health and welfare and leisure
  • Soft mobility policy and participation in public transport costs
  • Five weeks of vacation per year and six weeks from 50 years
