Full description

In this environment, you set up CNC flat and profile grinding machines independently. The company supports you with systematic training and offers modern production conditions. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • In-depth knowledge in measuring and testing technology
  • Readiness for use in 3-shift operation Offer
  • Modern working environment in industrial production
  • Structured introduction to the new task
  • Possibility of actively shaping process improvements
