Full description

Introduction

For our Communication & Image division, we are looking for a (M/F) Content Editor whose main mission will be to produce, adapt and optimize high quality content in English, consistent with the brand's editorial line. You will guarantee linguistic excellence (grammar, spelling, style, tone and fluidity) on all communication media.

Responsibilities

  • Produce, adapt and optimize high quality English content consistent with the brand's editorial line
  • Guaranteeing linguistic quality: grammar, spelling, style, tone and editorial fluidity
  • Write or revise press releases, brochures, exhibitions, content for digital platforms and advertising campaigns
  • Monitoring of text production processes between external editors, agencies and internal validators
  • Cross-check with stakeholders and ensure that validation returns are taken into account
  • Collaborate with internal (sponsoring, graphic designers) and external (agencies) teams
  • Managing production times and following several editorial projects simultaneously
  • Participate in continuous improvement of content processes and strategy.

Profile

  • Perfect command of written English, mother tongue; very good command of the desired French
  • University degree in English studies, communication, journalism or similar fields
  • Experience confirmed in writing, editing or communication for a demanding international brand (5 to 10 years)
  • A good knowledge of the fields of culture, the preservation of the planet, sport and affinity with the world of watchmaking are an asset.
  • Creativity, curiosity and adaptability to produce engaging texts according to audiences and markets
  • Excellent editorial rigour, detail and critical mindset to ensure reliable and error-free content
  • Autonomy, organization and collaborative spirit to manage time, priorities and team returns
  • Excellent communication, active listening and ability to understand the needs of different interlocutors
  • Intellectual curiosity, creativity, intercultural openness and taste for continuous learning
  • Reliability, autonomy, focus, stress resistance and ability to receive feedback constructively.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
