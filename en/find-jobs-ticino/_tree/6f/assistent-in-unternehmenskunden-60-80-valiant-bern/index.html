Full description

Simplify together. Because life is full of possibilities. At the same time, it became complex. That is why we are looking for employees who work with us to promote simplicity.

They shall

  • Receive an attractive entry opportunity into corporate customer business
  • independently prepare loan templates (including mortgage loans, operating and investment loans, etc.)
  • the customer advisors in all administrative and organizational activities as well as in the creation or Support the development of real estate estimates, analyses and templates
  • complete the preparation and post-processing of customer visits and, if possible, participate in customer meetings
  • support our customers on the telephone and maintain contact with internal and external bodies
  • assist in the organization of customer events as well as administratively prepare network events and visit if interested
  • in the medium term have the opportunity to develop into a junior corporate client consultant

They bring

  • a completed banking apprenticeship (apprentices welcome)
  • Interest in corporate banking
  • Dealing safely with customers
  • Enjoying service-oriented, telephone customer contact
  • a customer-oriented, sociable and helpful personality
  • Flexibility and good communication skills
  • an independent, efficient and reliable way of working
