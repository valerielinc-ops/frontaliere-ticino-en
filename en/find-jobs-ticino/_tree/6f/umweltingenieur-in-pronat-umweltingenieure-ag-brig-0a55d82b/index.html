Company description

Pronat Umweltingenieure AG is a renowned environmental engineering office based in Oberwallis (Brig), where most of our work takes place. Our field of activity extends from environmental impact reports and environmental construction support, through various expert reports and mappings to cultural landscape projects, success checks or water revitalizations.

We are a small team with experts in various environmental areas. This broad expertise benefits both our customers and our employees, who are happy to support each other in their collegial dealings.

Job description

Become part of our young, dynamic team. Take responsibility as a project manager: in varied projects that combine office work with field work. Contribute to the environmentally compatible design of small and large projects in the Alpine region with effects on the environment, climate and energy supply in Switzerland.

Your tasks

Depending on your education, your strengths and preferences, you can work with us in a varied, tailor-made combination of the following:

  • Project management: Area environment from pre-project, circulation project to the execution of various construction projects. Participation in project planning, preparation of environmental impact assessments and reports, assumption of environmental construction support
  • Fieldwork in the field \& Fauna
  • Processing of Altlastenprojekte: historical and technical preliminary investigation, planning and implementation of renovation projects in cooperation with the Department for the Environment Wallis
  • Work in the field of water ecology: renaturation and revitalization projects with IBCH\ images, projects on sunk\-shall renovations, fish gestation and residual water calculations

Your profile

  • University degree: Environmental sciences, environmental engineering, biology or similar discipline
  • Communication and team skills both within our small team and in contact with our customers as well as our interdisciplinary project partners and clients
  • Pragmatic and solution-oriented working method, where you recognize connections and quickly find viable solutions for interdisciplinary tasks
  • Project management experience preferred, but we are also willing to gradually introduce you to this work with support
  • Very good German knowledge (C2\) in word and writing, good ösisch knowledge preferred

This awaits you with us

  • Flexibility and trust: You can have a say in your workload as well as your working hours and days. You can also combine home office and office hours.
  • Further development: You can expect a professional and personal environment in which we actively promote your further education
  • Reliable insurance: In the event of illness, you will receive 100% of your wages, even in the event of prolonged absence
  • Job entry: as of now or by appointment

Are you interested?

Then we would like to meet you. Send us your application dossier.
