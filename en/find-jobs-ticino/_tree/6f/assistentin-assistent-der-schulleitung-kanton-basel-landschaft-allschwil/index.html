Secondary School Allschwil

The Allschwil secondary school comprises 38 classes with around 750 pupils, which are taught by around 100 teachers. Our school carries three trains (A, E and P), small classes and a foreign language class. Both the socio-pedagogical and the curative pedagogical structures help to support learning setting. In addition, school social work advises, accompanies and supports personal and social issues. We offer a responsible, versatile and varied activity in a lively environment.
