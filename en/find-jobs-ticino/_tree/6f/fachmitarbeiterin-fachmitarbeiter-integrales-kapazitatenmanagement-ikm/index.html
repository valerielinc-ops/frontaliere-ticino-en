Full description

Create an efficient and patient-oriented care with us. Integral capacity management ensures the central and cross-divisional planning of all hospital capacities. The goal is a balanced transparent and efficient control of operations, beds and consultation hours along the entire patient path in close interdisciplinary cooperation. It is the hub of our hospital and the first point of contact for patients with questions regarding registration and entry of a hospital stay.

Their tasks

  • Ensuring optimal patient care and planning
  • Contact person for patients with questions regarding registration and admission
  • Efficient scheduling in the area of peri-op and surgical management
  • Capacity planning of consultation hours
  • Patient-oriented disposition of beds
  • Anesthesia consultation hours
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation

Your profile

  • Dipl. Nursing professional HF
  • Completed training in health at HF/FH level
  • Professional experience
  • Experience in scheduling, operating or capacity planning is advantageous
  • Organizational strong, solution-oriented and resilient personality
  • High social skills as well as strong communication and team skills
  • Networked thinking, joy of co-creation
  • Good IT user knowledge

Your opportunity As of 1 February 2027 or by appointment, you can expect a versatile and demanding job in a modern and future-oriented hospital with a wide range of services. The cooperation within the different areas, team spirit and a cross-divisional feeling of togetherness are consciously lived in our company. They are in direct contact with our patients and work closely with the anesthesia doctors. You can expect a role in which, on the one hand, you can make a significant contribution to service-oriented patient care and, on the other hand, you can actively participate in the area of IKM. They work during office hours from Monday to Friday.

Their advantages

  • Holidays: You benefit from holidays between 25 and 30 working days (depending on age) and 13 paid holidays. For this you have the possibility to buy 5 holiday days.
  • Working hours: The company’s normal working hours for a full-time workload are 42 hours per week (excluding doctors). The annual working time applies, which ensures the highest possible flexibility in the organization of working time. Service requests are implemented where possible and part-time employment is possible in many functions – also in management positions.
  • Education and further education: Your personal and professional development is important to us. We support participation in internal and external further education and training through generous participation in the form of working hours and/or course costs.
  • Food: In our staff restaurant – nominated with the “Best of Swiss Gastro” award – you can enjoy menus from the market-fresh cuisine at very attractive conditions.
  • Arrival/parking: Employees of Spital Muri benefit from cheap parking. The car park is located on the hospital area. If you come to work by public transport, bike, motorcycle or on foot, we will reward you with a monthly eco-bonus.
  • Social insurance: We attach importance to adequate social benefits. For night, weekend and holiday work, we pay you fair allowances. We insure your salary at the pension fund Profond without coordination deduction (excluding doctors). The wages of doctors are insured by the VSAO preventive foundation.
