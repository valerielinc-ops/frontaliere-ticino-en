The actual location of this job is in Stein, AG, Switzerland. Relocation assistance is available for eligible candidates and their families, if needed. In this position, you will act as a central point of contact for GMP-related topics, support inspections, and ensure that all regulated systems meet internal operational requirements as well as external regulatory standards.
