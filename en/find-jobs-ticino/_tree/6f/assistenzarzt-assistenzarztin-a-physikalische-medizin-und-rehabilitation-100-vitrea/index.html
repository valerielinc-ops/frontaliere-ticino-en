Full description

Assistant doctor (a) Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation 100% Place of work: Dussnang rehabilitation clinic Where your job is doing rehab.

State-of-the-art robotic therapy for musculoskeletal and geriatric rehabilitation, surrounded by beautiful parkland. The first gerontotraumatological rehabilitation department in Switzerland. The environment at the Dussnang rehabilitation clinic also benefits the approximately 270 employees. Here advanced medicine meets flat hierarchies and a lot of room for manoeuvre. Through close exchange with care and therapy, you and your ideas advance.

You can look forward to this

  • Compliance with the VSAO requirements (42/4-hour week) and discharge of administrative tasks
  • Care of a musculoskeletal rehabilitation unit under specialist supervision
  • Ensuring high-quality and safe patient care in the inpatient rehabilitation setting
  • Active participation in the restoration of mobility, function and independence of our patients
  • Treatment of a wide range of patient goods in the fields of orthopaedics, rheumatology, traumatology as well as physical medicine and rehabilitation
  • Early detection of complications and initiation of adequate diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • Close interprofessional cooperation with care, physio- and occupational therapy as well as other specialist areas in a committed and collegial team

We are pleased about this

  • Successfully completed medical studies (Swiss medical diploma or medical diploma recognised in Switzerland)
  • Interest in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation, Orthopaedics, Rheumatology or General Internal Medicine
  • Delight in the care of a demanding musculoskeletal patient with concomitant internist diseases
  • Interest in rehabilitative medicine and a holistic, patient-oriented treatment
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (at least level C1)
