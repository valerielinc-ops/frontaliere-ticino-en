Full description

Do you love to open doors, inspire customers and launch innovative mobility solutions? As a Sales Manager: in new business in the Arc region, you are the driving force behind our growth – with a focus on acquisition, consulting and tailor-made offers in the B2B sector. With you, we win new customers for smart mobility solutions and drive the growth of Post Company Cars AG decisively!

You can do that.

  • In the Arc legal region, you will be responsible for the acquisition of new customers and the implementation of the outsourced business processes into the ecosystem of Post Company Cars.
  • With your commitment, you actively shape the growth of Post Company Cars AG and bring fresh impetus to the business.
  • You regularly visit potential customers, analyze customer needs and create customized offers in terms of company mobility.
  • After successful implementation, you hand over the customer base to your team in the region for further support and development.
  • With your eye for detail and your structured way of working, you create reliable reports that serve as a basis for decision-making.
  • For the preparation of offers, the contracting, the processing of tenders and the implementation of new customers, you are in close cooperation with the customer project managers.
  • You get involved in project groups and support marketing activities at events and trade fairs.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed an apprenticeship in the commercial or technical sector and have trained in marketing, sales or business administration.
  • You bring in-depth experience in customer acquisition – ideally from the service environment such as fleet management or leasing, the vehicle-related financial services sector or the B2B sales of company vehicles.
  • A high degree of final orientation as well as strong negotiating and sales skills are among your strengths.
  • You are a cooperative and competent sales personality with a flair for business contexts and a large personal network.
  • You communicate very well verbally and in writing in French and have good oral knowledge in German.

Further information about Post Company Cars AG can be found here; www.post.ch/companycars
