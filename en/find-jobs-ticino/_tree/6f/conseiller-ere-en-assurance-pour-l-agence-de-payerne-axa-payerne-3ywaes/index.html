100%, place of work: Payerne

Shape your future with us!

Do you live in the Broye region and have you already been able to put your sales, negotiation and networking skills into practice? Are you a customer-oriented and goal-oriented person? Do you take your work seriously?

With us, you have the opportunity to develop your skills and demonstrate your abilities! Following a strategic development, our agency in Payerne would like to strengthen its team and, if you recognize yourself in this profile, we would be delighted to receive your application!

Are you an insurance professional or from another background? Start your career in field service at AXA, the number 1. We are the trusted partner of our customers in all situations in life. Do you too?

What to expect

  • Oriented and personalized advice on insurance solutions for the customers you meet at their home, at the branch or by phone
  • Follow-up and professional development of your own customer portfolio
  • Acquisition of new customers to ensure the growth of the customer portfolio in your region
  • Placement of other AXA products (finance and pensions, health services)
  • Carrying out administrative tasks related to your position, with the support of the entire AXA structure and modern monitoring tools

What you bring

  • With a professional degree and a first experience in consulting or sales, you have a strong interest in training in the field of insurance
  • You have completed a professional apprenticeship and have a first experience in the insurance sector, the AFA is an additional asset
  • You can prove that you have a very good knowledge of the local economic fabric and a vast network of this region because you live there
  • Your personality is service-oriented and you have an ease in contact with others, you look for solutions for your customers and you know how to adapt to any situation
  • You are fluent in French both orally and in writing, any other language is an advantage
  • Your working method is autonomous and goal-oriented, remuneration based on your own performance is an additional source of motivation
  • You are comfortable with common computer systems as well as in learning new digital tools

What we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you the opportunity to develop and exploit your full potential. As Switzerland's leading insurance company, we offer ambitious people interesting career opportunities with a high degree of freedom of action.

  • Up to 30% discount on AXA products
  • Five weeks of vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of the Half-Fare Travelcard/contribution to the costs of the GA Travelcard
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Freedom of action and independent working
  • Innovative structures, processes and tools
  • Strong and professional training and development opportunities
