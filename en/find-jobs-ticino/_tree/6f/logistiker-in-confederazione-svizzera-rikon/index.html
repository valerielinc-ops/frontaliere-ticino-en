Full description

Put to the Point

As a key function, you ensure the proper functioning of our acceptance, the storage, shipping and delivery, as well as the handover of military equipment in your assigned area of expertise.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20875
  • Place of work: Überlandstrasse 17, 8340 Hinwil

You can make this contribution

  • Remove, store and relocate materials and ammunition professionally, inventory them and provide them in accordance with applicable specifications
  • Proper coordination and command of civilian and military personnel, in favor of a timely delivery or Take-back of military equipment
  • To act as a link between the army logistics centre and military or civilian payers
  • Performing tours and monitoring and control activities on military infrastructure, readiness to perform work outside regular working hours
  • Provide armed service and pikett service in accordance with our orders and specifications
  • Ability to work in enclosed spaces and rock installations

This makes you unique

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as logistician/in EFZ
  • Pronounced service orientation in contact with the troops and in their professional advice
  • Independent, efficient and reliable working method with a strong organisational talent
  • Mandatory driving licence of categories B, BE and C, CE and F advantageous
  • Good knowledge of SAP (S4/HANA) and secure user knowledge of MS Office products

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom

The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3’500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information

Employing the Swiss army makes sense and brings many advantages. Convince yourself of Army.ch/advantages. Offers from recruiters are not taken into account.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
