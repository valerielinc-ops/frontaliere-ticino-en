You can look forward to that.

Technology can be learned. Not a strong team. What really sets us apart are the people. We like to work together, talk things openly and support each other. Because good technology is not only created by expertise, but above all by a strong team. Your day-to-day work is particularly diverse: from railway and business communication to watch systems, medium-voltage, building automation and card terminal systems. With us you work not only on a plant, but on a variety of different technologies. With 25 electrical and 35 telecommunications platforms, you can expect an exceptionally broad and varied technical environment. Together in the team, you take responsibility and ensure with your commitment that the systems work reliably and that the trains are safe, punctual and networked. You not only keep existing plants running, but also actively shape their further development. You can expect varied technical challenges and the opportunity to take on responsibility and develop yourself professionally and personally.   • With the construction, operation and maintenance of electrical and telecommunications systems of the railway infrastructure, you make an important contribution to safe rail transport.
  • You carry out commissioning, conversion and maintenance work.
  • Analyse disruptions carefully and find sustainable solutions.
  • Technical orders and smaller projects are coordinated from planning to implementation.
  • With suggestions for improvement, you shape the railway infrastructure of tomorrow.
  • You work with different specialist areas and ensure the highest quality and safety.

You bring that with you.

  • Teamwork is important to you and you can rely on your reliable way of working.
  • You like to get involved, think ahead and take responsibility for your tasks.
  • Even in technically demanding situations, you stay calm.
  • Joy in learning and interest in your personal development distinguish you.
  • You will bring a completed basic training as an electrician: in EFZ, assembly electrician: in EFZ or a comparable electrotechnical training. Knowledge of telecommunications is an advantage.
  • You have knowledge of German at least level B2 and have the driving license category B.
