Full description

As a universitary centre for pneumology, comprehensive clinical services are offered to patients with all forms of lung disease and respiratory disorders. A special focus is on the care of patients in the field of lung transplantation – from clarification and listing to care before and after transplantation to long-term follow-up care and patient: in a highly interdisciplinary and interprofessional environment with bronchiectases (cystic fibrosis and other etiology).

At the same time, our clinic is full of all other areas of pneumology. We are intensively involved in the training of medical students, prospective specialist doctors for pneumology and in medical training. We conduct research at an internationally competitive level in various areas of research. The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and about 700 trainees are committed to the welfare of our patients every day.

Tasks

  • Care for outpatients and inpatients from the entire spectrum of pneumology with a focus on lung transplantation
  • Care of patients in the evaluation and evaluation of lung transplantation and of patients in the early and long-term aftercare
  • Engaged cooperation in the lung transplantation department in close interdisciplinary exchange with pneumology, thoracic surgery, transplantation center and other disciplines involved
  • Senior medical co-responsibility in the inpatient and outpatient pneumological care of a universitary tertiary centre including participation in the upper-medical service or background service for all patients with lung diseases
  • Commitment to the training and further training of assistant doctors and students
  • Cooperation in clinical projects, research projects and quality assurance processes
  • Active co-design and further development of the focus on transplantation spneumology

Requirements

  • Completed training to become a specialist in pneumology (FMH or internationally recognised equivalent specialist title)
  • Clinical experience at the level of the senior physician in the care of pneumonia patients
  • Ideally experience in transplantation spneumology, in the care of patients before or after lung transplantation or related area of pneumology (e.g. bronchial ictases, CF)
  • Interest in research in the field of transplantation spneumology, aspiration to habilitation
  • Very good German language skills in word and writing, at least level B2
  • Committed, able to work and resilient personality with high social competence
  • Enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation in a universitary environment
  • Interest in teaching, further training and quality assurance

We offer: We offer you a sophisticated and varied activity in a highly specialized universitary environment. You work in an experienced, interdisciplinary team and get the opportunity to contribute and develop your clinical expertise in transplantation spneumology. Thanks to the close cooperation with qualified specialists from different fields of expertise, you benefit from a wide range of professional exchanges as well as from attractive development opportunities in clinic, teaching and research.

A modern workplace in a central location in Zurich completes the offer. USZ pneumology is also described as a training centre with a center function, which also underlines the academic and clinical framework of the position.
