Full description

Our Sonnmatt rehabilitation clinic in Lucerne combines medical rehabilitation with a first-class hotel environment in the most beautiful viewing position. Our patients benefit both inpatient and outpatient from personal, holistic care by an experienced, interdisciplinary team – with maximum comfort and high therapeutic quality.

Your tasks

  • Speech therapy : You are independently responsible for the clinical therapy of speech, speech, voice and swallowing disorders - You carry out both individual therapy and, if necessary, group therapy
  • Advice and guidance : You are responsible for advising the patients and their guidance for self-training and take over the advice of the relatives
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Therapy, care and medical service work hand in hand. Your expertise flows actively into the exchange and contributes significantly to the successful rehabilitation
  • Training: If you are interested and needed, you can look after our speech pedia students

Your profile

  • Education: You are a trained speech therapist / trained speech therapist with EDK recognition, even professional or re-entrants are welcome
  • Recognition: You have a valid professional license (BAB)
  • Personality: You are a committed and curious person who enjoys working with neurological patients and is enthusiastic about their rehabilitation. It also motivates you to actively shape professional developments and initiate change processes.

This is what we offer you

  • Co-creation: Work in a quiet work environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN thermal bath
