Put to the Point

The personnel of the army (Pers A) accompanies the members of the army from recruitment to dismissal from military service. Design an apprenticeship with a Swiss-wide impact: You empower the moderators who are working on the cantonal orientation days. They combine course management and didactics with quality assurance and direct exchange with the cantons.

  • Date of entry: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20866
  • Location: Guisanplatz 1, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Planning and coordinating the basic and further training of the moderators for the cantonal orientation days throughout Switzerland and ensuring a uniform implementation
  • Organize and lead basic and refresher courses, accompany course cadres and participants, and ensure course administration and documentation
  • Advise the course cadre methodically and didactically as well as create and update practical teaching materials, course documents and digital learning content
  • Review the course quality on the basis of evaluations and performance proofs, evaluate results and implement agreed improvement measures
  • Coordinate operational exchanges with cantonal OT managers, organise votes and contribute feedback to the further development of training
  • Participate in projects and working groups and, in the absence of the senior management, take over the technical deputy within the delegated framework

This makes them unique.

  • university or technical college degree or equivalent education; further training in adult education, pedagogy or didactics is advantageous
  • Several years of practical experience in training, moderation, course management or the organisation of education and training
  • Experience in course planning and administration, evaluation and quality assurance as well as safe use of digital tools
  • Very good moderation and communication skills, convincing verbal expression and enjoyment of writing comprehensible training documents
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working methods, skill in dealing with different stakeholders and willingness to travel regularly to the cantons
  • Good oral and written knowledge of a second official language and, if possible, knowledge of a third official language

Your commitment to security and freedom The training command (Kdo Ausb) is responsible for training within the Swiss army – from recruitment to general basic training to cadre training for the militia and the professional military. The Kdo Ausb includes the higher cadre training, the personnel of the army, five teaching associations, the training center as well as the Women in the Army and Diversity Department. One third of the approximately 2,350 employees work in civilian professions in the scientific, technical, medical, psychological, administrative or sports sector and contribute to success together with the professional military.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
