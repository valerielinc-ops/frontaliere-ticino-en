Scientist QC - Late Phase 100% (a) — Bachem AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. Become part of a dedicated team that is responsible for quality control in the late development phase with the highest standards of precision and reliability. To strengthen our team in the QC Late Phase, we are looking for a dedicated Scientist Quality Control with focus on GC, GC-MS & IC (a), 100%

Their tasks

  • They monitor analytical activities with focus on GC, GC-MS & IC in the context of release analyses, development projects or analytical orders. You are the contact person for specialist questions of internal and external customers and authorities. Through well-founded professional assessment and secondary inspections, you ensure that all results meet our high quality standards – scientifically sound, comprehensible and precise.
  • They develop GC, GC-MS and IC-analytical methods and carry out method validations and transfers according to regulatory requirements (cGMP).
  • In interdisciplinary project teams, you contribute your expertise and take on professional responsibility. They communicate clearly with internal interfaces such as QA, production or regulatory affairs and thus contribute to quality along the entire value chain.
  • They comply with all relevant instructions, regulations and guidelines to ensure the correctness of daily work according to cGMP, occupational safety and environmental protection, actively implement them and are a model for the team.
  • You take responsibility for the processing and conclusion of Q-Events (OOX’s, Deviations). You create and control QC data, documents and process incidents and technical reports.
  • They review and create QC documents and consistently implement regulatory requirements (cGMP). Your forward-looking view allows you to identify compliance changes and establish them at an early stage.
  • They are committed to the continuous development and optimization of QC processes, document professional findings systematically and strengthen a common quality culture in the team through active knowledge exchange.

Your profile

  • Completed studies MSc / PhD in Natural Sciences (FH, Uni)
  • Professional experience in the pharmaceutical industry and in the GMP environment
  • Expertise in the analytical environment in dealing with chromatographic methods, GC, GC-MS & IC desirable
  • Knowledge of Analytical Method Validation & Transfers in the Regulated GMP Environment
  • Strong analytical thinking skills
  • Communicative and well-structured organizational talent
  • Convincing and safe appearance
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
