Full description

The work involves setting up machines for production and independent production of series in production. In an established team, you can use your experience in CNC processing and optimize processes. For our client, we are looking for an activity

  • elaborate CNC programs for turning, milling and drilling processes
  • equip machines and plants for production
  • specifically optimize processes in production
  • manufacture small and medium-sized series independently
  • analyze and correct production disruptions
  • Requirements
  • Completed apprenticeship as a polymechanic or polymechanic Specialty Manufacturing
  • In-depth experience in CNC machining
  • Structured and self-responsible working method
  • Open appearance with high operational readiness
  • Ready for use in 2-shift operation Offer
  • Multifaceted area of responsibility
  • Collaboration in a routine team
  • Fair remuneration according to market standard
