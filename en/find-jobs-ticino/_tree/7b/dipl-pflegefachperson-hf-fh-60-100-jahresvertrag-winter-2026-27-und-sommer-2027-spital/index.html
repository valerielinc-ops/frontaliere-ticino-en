Full description

Their nursing expertise, competent care and planning assistance are appreciated not only by our patients in the acute care unit, but also by our medical team. As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable, but for the region extremely important company. We maintain a collegial working atmosphere and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos. | For our interdisciplinary acute care unit we are looking for agreement |
