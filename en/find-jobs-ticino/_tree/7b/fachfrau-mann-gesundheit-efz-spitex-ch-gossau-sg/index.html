Quality with heart

The foundation VitaTertia with the houses Gerbhof and Weiher looks after 90 bright and spacious apartments, which are oriented to the needs of primarily older people. VitaTertia offers a specialized nursing and care area, as well as a residential group for people with dementia, with 16 beds each. The house enables its residents individual outpatient and stationary services in the areas of care and care, housekeeping and gastronomy in order to lead a self-determined and autonomous life.
