SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. Do you want to make a difference in an exciting and innovative working environment that makes sense? Then you are exactly right with us! Operation Center Technik ensures the smooth operation of all technical facilities around the clock and compliance with the defined service level agreements for the Swiss railway network. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even for irregular working hours. Night and weekend work is also paid for. Depending on the stakes, we pay between 300 and 700 CHF per month. You also get 10-30% time surcharges. This means you can compensate for these time surcharges with free time. Our other benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under " Benefits". You can move that.

  • In your role as 1st-level support in the Operation Center Technik, you visually monitor the traction current and railway systems using alarm systems.
  • In the event of disruptions, you analyze and identify alarms and events and take necessary measures.
  • You will be the first point of contact for infrastructure fault reports and coordinate cooperation with various organisations such as the area, 2nd-level support and escalation levels.
  • In disruptive situations, you ensure time- and target group-friendly communication with customers as well as partners as well as management.
  • With your forward-looking actions and rapid reaction to events, you make an important contribution to the safety, stability and availability of the railway infrastructure.

You bring that with you.

  • You are customer-oriented, have a strong technical understanding and master the common MS Office applications. Your working style is characterized by independence, efficiency and solution orientation.
  • In a dynamic, technical 24/7 environment, you flourish. Even in hectic times, you keep the overview and stay calm.
  • This position requires teamwork, decision-making and flexibility.
  • You have basic training (preferably in the electrical industry) and corresponding professional experience.
  • This position requires a reliable knowledge of German and preferably a good knowledge of a second national language (French and/or Italian).
