Full description

Medacta International is looking for a Measuring Instruments Responsible for Quality Control.

Returning to Quality Control Manager, you will be responsible for:

  • Manage, maintain and calibrate measuring instruments according to targets shared with Quality Control Manager, current procedures, best practices and industry standards;
  • Coordinate and supervise the CQ staff involved in the management, maintenance and calibration of measuring instruments;
  • Prepare and maintain updated management, maintenance and calibration procedures for measuring instruments;
  • Propose Quality Control Manager process improvements related to the management, maintenance and calibration of the tools by involving the staff involved;
  • Ensure that users have compliant measuring instruments and calibration systems at their disposal;
  • Ensure the order and cleaning of the department;
  • Ensure compliance with business operating procedures;
  • Ensure compliance with the corporate regulation;
  • Perform formations to your CQ team on the management, maintenance and calibration of measuring instruments;
  • Perform training to users on the use of measuring instruments;
  • Other activities related to function and according to business needs.

Hard Skills

  • Mechanical/mechatronic expert or technical or scientific degree;
  • English language knowledge with minimum A2 level;
  • Good knowledge of mechanical technical design;
  • Good knowledge of the Microsoft Office package;
  • Pluriennale experience, 2+ years, accrued use of the main measuring instruments (course gauge, centesimal and vintage altimeter, micrometer for interior and exterior, profile projector, wrinkle meter, TF, TL, P/NP control blades, coordinate measuring machines, optical measuring spots);
  • Multiannual experience, 1+ years, matured in the visual quality control of mechanical parts;
  • Knowledge of the fundamental principles of industrial metrology.

Soft Skills

  • Prepared for precision work;
  • Problem Solving and result orientation;
  • Activity to group work;
  • Spirit of initiative, autonomy and analytical capacity;
  • Good learning, flexibility, adaptability, punctuality and compliance with procedures;
  • Short-term availability in case of business needs,

What we offer

  • Contract indefinitely;
  • Carpooling organized by Medacta;
  • Mobility incentives with public transport;
  • Advantageous business conventions;
  • Growth opportunities, working in an environment in continuous improvement and expansion;
  • Annual review of salary related to performance and professional development;
  • Continuing training.
