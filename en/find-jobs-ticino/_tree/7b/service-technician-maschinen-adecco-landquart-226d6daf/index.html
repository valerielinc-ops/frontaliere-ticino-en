Full description

Introduction

To strengthen the service team, we are looking for a technically skilled specialist as a service technician (machines). The focus of this position is the maintenance, maintenance and troubleshooting of machines as well as the technical support of customers.

Tasks

  • Performing service, maintenance and repair work on machinery and equipment
  • Analyze and resolve technical failures
  • Customer commissioning of new machines
  • Performing functional and safety tests
  • Documentation of the work carried out and service missions
  • Technical advice and customer support on site
  • Ensuring high plant availability and customer satisfaction
  • Cooperation with internal departments

Profile

  • Completed technical training in mechanics, mechatronics, automation or equivalent
  • Professional experience as a service technician
  • Good technical understanding and analytical thinking
  • Independent, solution-oriented and customer-oriented working method
  • Readiness to travel for service missions
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Category B driving licence

Advantages

  • Varied and responsible work
  • Modern equipment and careful introduction
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Collegial and professional working environment
  • Development opportunities in a successful company
  • Long-term perspective with permanent employment option
