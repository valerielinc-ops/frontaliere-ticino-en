Put to the Point

They are responsible for ensuring that the head of the department has all the necessary documents in good time to respond adequately to advances and questions from Parliament. You coordinate the work between the experts in the offices and in the General Secretariat and ensure a smooth process. As part of a motivated team, you act professionally competent and are ready to participate in mutual assistance.

  • Entry date: 01.12.2026 / by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20661
  • Place of work: Inselgasse 1, 3011 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • sifting, assigning and coordinating parliamentary initiatives between decision-makers in the department, including coordinating the transfer of transactions between departments
  • Experts within the EDI advise on formal copies of the applications to the Federal Council and ensure the formal quality of these, this also includes comparing texts in all official languages for agreement
  • Substantive assessments of proposals submitted by the Federal Chancellery to the Federal Council from the point of view of the department, as well as drawing up decision-making and overview bases for the department management
  • participate in various coordination groups of the EDI and the further development of digital systems
  • responsible for the training of commercial learners in the field of business planning
  • possibly Deputy Head of Business Planning in all her duties

This makes them unique.

  • Commercial education with further education (e.g. federal law). Professional ID, BA or similar
  • Willingness to work administratively and flexibly
  • Understanding and interest in political processes
  • Vocational trainer card and experience in training
  • Active knowledge of two official languages and passive knowledge of the third official language

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

We at GS-EDI...

... work on the pulse of everyday life. The Secretariat-General shall be the department of the department. It performs planning, coordination and control tasks and is the point of contact between the department head and the federal offices. It coordinates resources and provides advisory services for the entire department. Decisions are prepared here that directly influence the lives of the Swiss population, their everyday life and living conditions. Modern employment and working conditions, further education and much more make the GS-EDI an attractive employer. The GS-EDI, like all other administrative units of the department, is awarded the label Friendly Work Space.

Additional information

The Business Planning Team is the hub of the General Secretariat, which ensures the coordination and organisation of all political affairs in the department. This includes documents for Federal Council meetings, answers to parliamentary initiatives and questions, performance information such as Federal Council goals and controlling, as well as the coordination of the extra-parliamentary bodies and those between the EDI and Parliament.
