Careers at Assura Work at Assura Positions to be filled Consultant Nurse experienced in psychiatry and home care Vacancy Nice to meet you! Nurse Consultant Experienced in Psychiatry and Home Care Joining Assura is evolving in a collaborative and dynamic environment where everyone plays an essential role and contributes to a meaningful mission. Every day, we put our energy and expertise at the service of our clients, to support them in their health decisions. Want to join us? So, nice to meet you! As a nursing counselor, you join a small committed team whose mission is to ensure that both home and EMS nursing services meet quality of care requirements and comply with the legal framework in force. What you do every day

  • At the heart of the activity, monitoring of medical mandates, required care assessments, and billing for psychiatry and somatic home care allows you to actively contribute to the quality and compliance of benefits provided to insured persons. You also ensure that providers comply with legal and treaty provisions.
  • You manage complex nursing records. Based on documents provided by providers or in home assessments, you assess and analyse the care needs of insured and established persons and prepare the care guarantees granted.
  • Collaboration with care partners, as well as meetings, is part of your role. These interactions make it possible to agree on care in accordance with the legal framework and good business practices, as well as to provide advice on LAMal benefits or other social insurance.
  • At the same time, you perform administrative tasks related to the function, and actively invest in continuing training, working groups and internal projects, thus contributing to the evolution of practices.
  • Your clinical expertise is a real support for the employees of the various departments, especially on the specific aspects related to home care.

What you bring to the team

  • Holder of a Bachelor of Science HES in nursing or equivalent, you have at least 2 years of confirmed experience in psychiatry and 3 years in home care in Switzerland. An experience in EMS is an additional asset for this position.
  • At ease in both oral and written communication and negotiation, you can defend your point of view in a structured and reasoned way. Your mind of analysis and synthesis allows you to effectively manage complex files.
  • Recognized for your sense of organization, you work autonomously, while fully engaging in team dynamics.
  • Your mastery of French and your ease with the usual computer tools are essential to succeed in this function. The knowledge of Italian and/or German, as well as the Syrius tool, is a real plus.
