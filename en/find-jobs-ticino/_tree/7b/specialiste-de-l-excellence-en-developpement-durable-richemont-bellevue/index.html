Full description

Reference code: JR128360 Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity. BACKGROUND Our approach to sustainability is inspired by our history and purpose: ‘We Craft the Future’. In FY26, we continued building on our responsible business values and heritage. We initiated comprehensive change across our Group functions and regions, as well as our Maisons to integrate ESG principles right across our business. This past year, we undertook a systematic reshaping of Richemont’s environmental data reporting framework. We have also reinforced the foundations of our ESG framework with priorities drawn from our FY25 double materiality assessment and laid down the foundations of our ESG Impact, Risk & Opportunity Assessment. HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT? Reporting to the ESG Excellence Director, this position will be instrumental in translating sustainability data into actionable insights. Your work will directly support Richemont's non-financial reporting and contribute to robust performance management. This includes overseeing the planning and execution of sustainability data collection, ensuring data consistency and accuracy, and contributing to the continuous improvement of sustainability data management and reporting processes across the Group. This role will be in charge of the following responsibilities, including but not limited to:

  • Lead the quarterly consolidation of environmental data, whose scope may expand to include selected supply chain data, working closely with reporting entities and Maisons to ensure accurate and complete data
  • Maintain and update reporting documentation and all related operational instructions, ensuring alignment with evolving reporting standards and internal sustainability frameworks
  • Maintain an open communication with reporting entities, Maisons and Regions, providing information and guidance for specific and complex environmental reporting aspects, including GHG accounting treatment
  • Maintain the inventory of the Emission Factors, in collaboration with other Group expert functions and Maisons
  • Ensure data consistency and accuracy across different reporting entities/Maisons and data sources, and contribute to creating control procedures
  • Analyse the environmental reporting categories and provide Group Sustainability management with clear and meaningful information on significant movements and deviations
  • Operate the data corrections and restatements processes allowed under specific conditions (e.g. errors, change in calculation methodology, changes in reporting scope)
  • Contribute to preparing the consolidated annual environmental disclosures, as part of the Group Non-Financial Reporting process, as well as ad hoc local reporting requirements
  • Facilitate the interim and annual external audit process
  • Provide instruction and training to regional and local finance controllers, particularly with regard to quarterly analytical reviews and year-end data submission
  • Contribute to the implementation and continuous improvement of the sustainability data management platform Sustaira
  • Contribute to the development and implementation of product and project GHG accounting methodologies to support the Group's climate change strategy
  • Contribute to the execution of quantitative assessments of physical risks and transition risk scenario analysis in close collaboration with Functions and Maisons, and collaborate with Group Finance to translate economic and physical projections into impacts on financial statements
  • Contribute to integrating operating frameworks and processes (e.g., Circularity) into sound inputs to continuously evolve accounting processes, internal performance management and digitalisation
  • Lead the annual submission of the CDP questionnaire and potential future similar third party questionnaires as deemed necessary HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?
