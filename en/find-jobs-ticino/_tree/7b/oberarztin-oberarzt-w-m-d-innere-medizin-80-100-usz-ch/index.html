Full description

The Clinic for Internal Medicine (KIM) offers an exciting, acute medical activity with a wide range of complex internal diseases. To strengthen our team, we are looking for senior doctors with a special focus in Geriatrie. The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and about 700 trainees are committed to the welfare of our patients: inside.

Tasks

  • Geriatric Consil Service at the USZ in cooperation with the City Hospital Waid (University Clinic for Age Medicine, Zurich)
  • Care for the elderly traumatological patient:in the Center for Age Traumatology in interdisciplinary cooperation with the Clinic for Traumatology
  • Care of patients: women in geriatric ambulatory, especially in the context of preoperatively-sexed patients
  • Comprehensive and professional care of our patients: inside and their relatives
  • Rotating operations on our interdisciplinary pool stations and on our own, the "Pfi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-fi-
  • Participation in the Senior Medical Officer's Office of the Clinic for Internal Medicine
  • Supervision and teaching of assistants:in-service training, as well as commitment to further training

Requirements

  • Specialist title of general internal medicine
  • Focus in Geriatrie already acquired or shortly before its completion
  • Wide and multi-year clinical experience in General Internal Medicine, ideally with experience in the inpatient area and/or on an IMC/Intensive Unit
  • Holder:in the Sonography/POCUS ability card or shortly before obtaining it
  • Doctorate to/to Dr. med. completed or shortly before his graduation
  • Reliable, team-oriented and communicative personality with joy in interdisciplinary cooperation and the training of the young doctor
