Put to the Point

Do you want to use your IT knowledge to advance research? At Agroscope, you put your technical expertise at the service of scientific research. You supervise various IT systems, participate in their further development and seek concrete solutions for the challenges of researchers and laboratory employees. A body that combines personal responsibility, technical diversity and concrete effect!

Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.
