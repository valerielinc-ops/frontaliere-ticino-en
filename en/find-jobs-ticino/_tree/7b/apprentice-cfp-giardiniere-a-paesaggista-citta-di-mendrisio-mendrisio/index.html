The Town Hall of Mendrisio opens the competition for recruiting:

  • 2 AFC apprentices employed at the municipal administration of the

City of Mendrisio. They constitute preferential title the profiles with maturity;

  • 1 AFC apprentice landscape gardener at the Environment Division and

infrastructure;

  • 1 apprentice CFP landscape gardener at the Environment Division and
  • 1 intern 52 weeks for the attainment of professional maturity as

employed by trade at the Dicastery Planning and public spaces and the Division Environment Infrastructures. The entry into operation will be to be agreed with the relevant service. The school office will be defined by the Vocational Training Division. Expiry of the competition The employment offers must be obtained through the application form online by Monday, January 4, 2027 at 23:59, accompanied by the following documents: ▪ letter of application; ▪ curriculum vitae; ▪ intermediate school statement for companies (from requesting to the secretariat of their school office); ▪ final school results of the eighth year of school (average) for those who had not yet finished the school Applications submitted in another form will not be considered. This notice of competition and its application form are also available on www.mendrisio.ch/concourses. City of Mendrisio Competition The Human Resources Section remains at courteous disposal (tel. +41 58 688 33 10) of those who do not have a device (PC, tablet, smartphone, scanner, etc.) through which to send their application. It is the right of the City Hall to immediately proceed to the examination of the applications received and to call to an interview the candidates pre-selected without waiting for the expiry of the contest. The City Hall reserves the right not to proceed in the absence of candidates eligible for the function. Legal remedies Against the notice of competition shall be the right to appeal to the Lodevole Council of State within 30 days of the date of publication. Detailed information may be requested from the Human Resources Section of the City of Mendrisio, tel. 058 688 33 10, or by consulting the website: www.orient.ch. The Town Hall
