Full description

Located in Château-d’Œx, at 1000 meters altitude, the Pôle Santé Pays-d’Enhaut offers a wide range of hospital services, accommodation, elderly care, as well as home care. According to the so-called integrated care approach, this innovative organization presents an opportunity for health professionals to work within small multidisciplinary teams, ensuring better continuity of care and responding to the health needs of the Pays-d’Enhaut population. In the heart of a privileged natural environment, Pays-d’Enhaut is renowned for the quality of its living environment and offers a very broad range of sports, leisure, and cultural activities, to be enjoyed both summer and winter! As part of our training mission, we invite interested medical assistants to apply through this advertisement. Medical Assistant Your Mission:

  • Participating in continuous medical and interdisciplinary training Your
  • Profile:
