Full description

We are one of the most strategic security platforms of the Swiss Post. Together with your team, you are implementing one of the first public Corporate Zero Trust Edge approaches in Switzerland and working with the most modern network, security and cloud technologies based on cloudflare.

You Can Make That Do

  • As part of the Zero Trust Edge team, you develop, operate and automate the central Swiss Post's network and security platform based on Cloudflare.
  • Together with your teammates: inside, you make sure that users, devices, applications and partners can communicate with each other safely and reliably at any time.
  • The further development of network and Internet connectivity, DNS-Services and traffic management is part of your diverse area of responsibility.
  • You implement modern zero-trust concepts and ensure secure access control, as well as protection of applications, APIs and networks.
  • By using GitOps, Terraform Enterprise and other automation solutions, you standardize and optimize operating processes.
  • You continuously develop monitoring, visibility and service reliability and thus contribute to stable and efficient platform operation.
  • Together with specialists:inside the areas of authentication & authorization, PKI, cloud, security, workplace and architecture, you ensure reliable 24/7 operation of a mission-critical platform.

You bring this with you

  • A completed training in computer science forms the basis of your technical knowledge, supplemented by the willingness to continuously develop.
  • Well-founded know-how in the areas of routing, DNS, proxy, firewalls or secure Web Gateway distinguishes your profile.
  • With modern security concepts and the basic principles of Zero Trust, you have already dealt with yourself and want to deepen your knowledge.
  • Ideally, you will have experience with cloud and platform technologies and their secure operation.
  • GitOps, Infrastructure as Code and automation are familiar to you and you understand their added value for stable platform operation.
  • Analytic thinking, pragmatism and the ability to understand complex relationships help you to develop sustainable solutions.
  • Good English skills enable you to work together in an international technology environment
  • German skills are an advantage.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
