Zero Trust Edge Engineer — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

With you, we will shape one of the strategically most important security platforms of the Schweizerischen Post. Together with your team, you will implement one of the first public Corporate Zero Trust Edge approaches in Switzerland and work with state-of-the-art network, security and cloud technologies based on Cloudflare.

What you can achieve

  • As part of the Zero Trust Edge team, you will develop, operate and automate the central connectivity and security platform of the Schweizerischen Post based on Cloudflare.
  • Together with your team colleagues, you ensure that users, devices, applications and partners can communicate with one another securely and reliably and securely at all times.
  • Your diverse range of responsibilities includes the further development of network and internet connectivity, DNS services and traffic management.
  • You implement modern zero-trust concepts and ensure secure access control as well as the protection of applications, APIs and networks.
  • By using GitOps, Terraform Enterprise and other automation solutions, you standardize and optimize operational processes.
  • You continuously develop monitoring, observability and service reliability and thereby contribute to stable and high-performing platform operations.
  • Together with specialists from the areas of Authentication & Authorization, PKI, Cloud, Security, Workplace and Architecture, you ensure the reliable 24/7 operation of a business-critical platform.

What you bring

  • A completed education in computer science forms the foundation of your technical knowledge, complemented by your willingness to continuously develop.
  • Your profile is characterized by in-depth expertise in connectivity topics such as routing, DNS, proxy, firewalls or Secure Web Gateway.
  • You have already explored modern security concepts and the core principles of Zero Trust and would like to deepen your knowledge further.
  • Ideally, you have experience with cloud and platform technologies as well as their secure operation.
  • GitOps, Infrastructure as Code and automation are familiar to you, and you understand their value for stable platform operations.
  • Analytical thinking, pragmatism and the ability to make complex interrelationships understandable help you develop sustainable solutions.
  • Good English skills enable you to collaborate in an international technology environment
  • German skills are an advantage.
