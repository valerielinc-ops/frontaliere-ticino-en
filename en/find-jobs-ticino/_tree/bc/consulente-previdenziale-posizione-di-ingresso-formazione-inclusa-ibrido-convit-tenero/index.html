Social security consultant – entry position, training included (hybrid)

Would you like to start a new professional path in the social security sector? In Convit you acquire the necessary skills step by step, with the constant support of our team.

Main information Employer: Convit International GmbH Area: Tenero and surroundings · hybrid work, Homeoffice possible Training and team days: Via al Mulino 22a, 6814 Cadempino, Switzerland Contract: Indefinite recruitment; degree of employment according to the approved position Retribution (OTE): CHF 1'500–7'500 per month (fix + variable part, depending on the degree of employment) Language: Italian B2 or higher

Activities

  • Learn the basics of Swiss pension during the initial phase
  • Support documentation preparation and internal organization
  • Conduct advice independently after qualification and internal qualification

Your profile

Pre-existing VBV qualification is not required to start the route. The availability of training and the commitment to acquire the necessary qualifications are essential. Counselling and intermediation activities begin only after fulfilling the applicable requirements and internal authorization.

  • Spiccata aptitude for communication and active listening
  • Reliable, precise and continuous learning approach
  • Good ability to self-organize your activities

What we offer

  • A clear learning path with practical support
  • Structured insertion and comparison with experienced colleagues
  • Clear Growth Path: junior → senior team leader
  • Hybrid working model: Homeoffice possible, appointments with customers in your area, training and team days in our Cadempino headquarters.

Training and qualification

The VBV admission exam preparation course is free for you: Convit supports course costs. The qualification requires overcoming the relevant examination and compliance with the applicable requirements.

Right to work

Applications of persons resident in Switzerland are welcome. For this campaign, those who reside in Italy must already have a valid G permit. The individual situation is verified before hiring.

Application

Send the resume and any certificates via the application form or to Recruiting@convit.ch.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status. Note: this listing allows remote work — teleworking above 25 % of your time can shift your social-security base to Italy, so confirm the agreed remote share with HR before signing.
