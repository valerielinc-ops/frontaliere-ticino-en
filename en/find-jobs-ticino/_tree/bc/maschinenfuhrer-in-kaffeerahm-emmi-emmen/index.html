Full description

You feel at home in production, like working with modern systems and are a real team player? Then come to Emmen and become part of our coffee cream production. Together we ensure that quality, technology and teamwork work together perfectly.

You can do that.

  • You serve and monitor our production facilities in the coffee cream department and ensure that our products are produced in the best quality.
  • You consistently adhere to the high hygiene and quality standards in food production and actively contribute to a flawless production environment.
  • Together with your team, you ensure a smooth production process and support your colleagues in everyday work.
  • You are actively involved, thinking and using your technical skills to continuously improve processes.
  • You take responsibility for your tasks and work reliably, carefully and independently.
  • You work in 3-shift operation and are ready for assignments from 06.00 to 2 pm, 2 pm to 22 pm and 10 pm to 6 pm.

You bring that with you

  • You have a good knowledge of German in words and writing in order to safely understand work instructions and ensure smooth communication in the team.
  • Ideally, you already have experience in the food industry or in a production company.
  • A pronounced hygiene awareness and consistent compliance with quality requirements are a matter of course for you.
  • You are physically resilient and ready to perform physically demanding work. This also includes lifting and carrying loads up to 20 kg.
  • You are a reliable, motivated and team-oriented personality who likes to get involved.
  • You work closely, like to take on personal responsibility and bring your own ideas to improve our processes.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
