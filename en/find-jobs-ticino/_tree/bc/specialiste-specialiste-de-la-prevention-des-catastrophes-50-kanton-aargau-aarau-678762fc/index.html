Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Planning, creation and implementation of training courses for partner organisations and emergency responders, especially the Regional Management Bodies and the Cantonal Heads of Operations
  • Creation and implementation of staff framework and operational exercises for staff and civil protection partner organisations
  • Participation in training and exercises as instructor, class teacher, instructor and observer
  • Participation in the management of regional governance bodies
  • Management and participation in projects of the section according to the specification of the section head.

What you bring

  • Sound experience in Swiss civil protection, e.g. from fire brigades, police, military service or civil protection
  • Experience and training in the field of adult education (methodology, didactics, practical implementation, etc.), for example as an instructor
  • Experience in project management and recognized project management certification
  • Conceptual, independent work and high organizational capacity
  • Sound knowledge of staff work is an advantage

The population is at the heart of the activities of the Department of Health and Social Affairs. For the safety of their areas of life, we work sustainably and purposefully. The Military and Civil Protection Department performs tasks in the fields of military service, military service substitutes and civil and civil protection. We are also the administrative hub for the members of the army and civil protection in the canton of Aargau. The employees are responsible for the training and coordination of missions within the framework of civil protection.
