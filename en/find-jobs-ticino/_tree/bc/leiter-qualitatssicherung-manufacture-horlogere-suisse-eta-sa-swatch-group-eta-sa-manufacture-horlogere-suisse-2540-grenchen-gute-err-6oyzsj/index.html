Head of Quality Assurance | Manufacture Horlogère Suisse | ETA SA Head of Quality Assurance

  • Description du posteLeadership, motivation and further development of the quality assurance team
  • Overall responsibility for quality management (development, project, production and customer quality)
  • Definition, implementation and monitoring of testing and release processes
  • Analysis of quality indicators and initiation of corrective and preventive measures
  • Active participation in product and process optimization in close cooperation with production, development and project management
  • Implementation and/or monitoring of the site's internal audits (5S, process, workplace, product) and monitoring the implementation of the necessary measures.
  • Controlling the processing of non-conformities at the site
  • Preparing the site's quality reports (CNQ...) and tracking deviations from the targets
  • Managing the metrology department on site (calibration, approval...)
  • Training and raising awareness of employees on quality-related topics
  • Participation in supplier evaluation by assessing the quality aspect of the deliveries
  • Profile Basic technical training with further training to become a technician HF / engineer FH
