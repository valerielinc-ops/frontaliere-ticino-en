Full description

The CSS supports in all life situations. Perhaps you will soon be contributing to this as well? We would be delighted. Because here you can make a difference and ensure that our supplementary insurance products remain competitive and customer-oriented. Always at your side: a team of professionals in this field. We exchange ideas and support each other, because a good team spirit is important to us. Ready to move big things together with us? We look forward to meeting you! This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: Together with your 8-member team, you are responsible for offering and portfolio management in the supplementary insurance sector (healing costs, material and liability and a few more). You ensure that our products remain competitive and customer-oriented.
  • It is your responsibility to identify market trends, analyze the competition and drive targeted further developments in the portfolio.
  • Product campaigns are planned and controlled by you. In addition, you ensure compliance with compliance and data protection and are the contact person for customer and sales inquiries.
  • As a professional hub, you coordinate the interfaces between sales, offer development, service purchasing, controlling and other stakeholders.
  • With a clear view of quality and profitability, you analyze the performance of our products, derive insights and strategically develop the portfolio.
  • You are actively involved in projects, workshops and working groups and are responsible for active account management with internal and external partners. You bring that with you
  • First things first: You have a degree at the Higher Technical University level and bring several years of industry and specialist knowledge in the health, social or private insurance sector.
  • You already bring your first leadership experience in smaller teams. Project management – whether classic or agile – is familiar to you, and you act flexibly and solution-oriented.
  • Analytical and conceptual thinking are among your strengths. You work independently, efficiently and take responsibility with self-image.
  • You are communicative, provide constructive feedback and inspire your team. Empathy and appreciation are a matter of course for you.
  • With data and IT applications (Teams, PowerPoint, Excel, Word) you are confident.
  • Network building and maintenance will bring you joy. You encounter changes openly, and your personal and professional development is important to you.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
  • Enjoy your family life: Make time for your offspring: 3 weeks for fathers, for mothers up to 20 weeks. There are also generous child and education allowances.
