Your team

Senior Private Banker - Caribbean Market

Your role

  • Proactively build and manage a portfolio of High-Net-Worth clients in the relevant markets, with a strong emphasis on business development and client acquisition.
  • Prepare tailored investment proposals and contribute to the development and implementation of best-in-class solutions, ensuring alignment with clients’ objectives, assets, and investment policies.
  • Monitor client portfolios, provide regular performance reviews, and keep clients informed of relevant market developments.
  • Meet with prospects and clients, prepare for meetings, and manage follow-up actions to ensure a high standard of client service.
  • Collaborate closely with Investment Specialists and the Investment Platform to remain up-to-date with the latest investment solutions and guidelines.
  • Oversee the efficient setup of new client accounts and address related administrative matters as required.
  • Maintain a strong compliance culture, ensuring adherence to all relevant local laws, regulatory requirements, and in-house directives.
  • Uphold Pictet’s mission and core values, demonstrating professionalism and integrity in all activities.

Your profile

  • Bachelor’s degree
  • Post-graduate degree such as a Master’s degree, CFA, CIIA or MBA
  • Minimum ten (10) years of of business development experience, preferably within Private Banking
  • Solid track record of developing a book of business consisting of High-Net-Worth clients within relevant markets.
  • Proficiency in a variety of software applications including Microsoft Suite.
  • Fluency in English and other languages as required by the markets covered
  • Excellent negotiating skills
  • Knowledgeable across asset classes and investment vehicles
  • Solutions-oriented and proactive mindset
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills, both written and oral
  • Strong sense of ethics, aligned with Pictet’s values and partnership culture
  • Ability to travel internationally

We will not accept any CVs via agencies
