Full description

Rittmeyer's control systems, regulators and measurement techniques are the basis for innovative solutions in energy and water supply systems, hydropower plants and waste disposal facilities around the world. As an engineering and service company meeting the highest quality standards, Rittmeyer specializes in the integration of software and systems for turnkey systems in the infrastructure sector. In order to strengthen our team within the Services division, we are looking for a collaborator for the position of: Service technician Automation & Industrial Networks (a) Region Fribourg, Bulle, Yverdon and about Romandie, Switzerland 100% Your field of activity Installation and commissioning of demanding control-command systems for water and energy supply facilities, hydropower plants and water disposal plants Will assume responsibilities within the team and actively contribute to its development Competent advice to our clients for the maintenance of control-command systems Service and maintenance works with the competent support of our headquarters in Baar. After a period of integration and in-depth training, you are ready to provide a picket service What you bring Successfully completed electrotechnical training (e.g. Automatician, electrician or installer) Ideally, a few years of professional experience and continuing training, for example at the ES level (higher school) You have strong knowledge of OT and IT networks (TCP/IP, switching, routing, VPN) and are comfortable with APIs, PC systems and industrial control-command systems. You have a team spirit and you stand out for your sense of responsibility and initiative You have good knowledge in German You can go to the intervention sites from your home what we offer Very autonomous work in a modern and environmentally friendly company, with a motivating and collegial work culture. Our flat hierarchies and short decision-making channels allow us to quickly and effectively achieve the goals we set ourselves together. Attractive conditions of employment await you in a well-positioned company in a forward-looking market environment. Interested? We look forward to receiving your application!
