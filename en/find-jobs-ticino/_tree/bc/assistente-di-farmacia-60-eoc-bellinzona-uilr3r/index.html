Full description

A pharmacy assistant - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete the team of the Hospital Pharmacy Service of the Institute for Pharmacological Sciences of Italian Switzerland (ISFSI) at the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona and Valli we look for a pharmacy Assistant (80%) Profile: The person we are looking for can actively listen to their interlocutors, ensuring proper advice, correct handling of drugs according to current directives and regulations and effective interaction with patients. He can act quickly using his own knowledge, technical and relational skills to interact with the different services of the hospital and with the degent patients; maintaining effective and efficient relationships with the different interlocutors. Responds to stresses with flexibility and competence. It actively participates in the centralized preparation of cytostatics and in the service of picket. He believes, like us, in the importance of putting the patient at the center of all our attentions.

  • Requirements: Federal Certificate of Capacity (AFC) as an assistant pharmacy, medical assistant, laboratory technician, biology/chemistry or equivalent qualifications; mastery of the Italian language and good knowledge of German or French; good computer skills; good organizational, relational and communication skills. Date of entry: 1. November 2026 or date to be agreed Exit date: 31 December 2027. Applications and information: Comprehensive application of curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, copies of certificates of study and work, will be sent by 31 August 2026 only in electronic format via www.eoc.ch . As a result, applications sent by mail or e-mail will not be considered, as well as applications that do not meet the requirements. Additional information can be requested at Dario Caronzolo, head of drugstore OSG (091 811 88 18; dario.caronzolo@eoc.ch).
