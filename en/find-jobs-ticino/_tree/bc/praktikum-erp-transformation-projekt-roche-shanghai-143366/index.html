The Position Internship - ERP Transformation Project

Role Overview

We are seeking a highly motivated, detail-oriented ERP Transformation Project Intern to join our global transformation team. In this role, you will sit at the heart of our SAP S/4HANA & EWM deployment, gaining hands-on exposure to a large-scale digital + business transformation. You will directly support the Project Management Office (PMO) and work cross-functionally across critical workstreams - including system integration, system testing, master data, user training, organizational change management, and technical/business go live. This is an exceptional opportunity to learn how a multi-million dollar technology implementation operates from the ground up and build highly marketable enterprise project management skills.

Job Requirements

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in project management, IT, engineering, biotechnology, or related business and technical disciplines.
