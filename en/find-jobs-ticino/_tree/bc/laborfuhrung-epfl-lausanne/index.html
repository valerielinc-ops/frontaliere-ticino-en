Full description

Mission With more than 100 apprentices in training in a dozen professions, EPFL is a fully-fledged training company. Regardless of the apprenticeship chosen at EPFL, apprentices carry out their vocational training in an extraordinary environment composed of research laboratories in fields as varied as engineering, the exact sciences and architecture. They benefit from all the dynamism and diversity of a university campus that offers them many opportunities, advantages and opportunities for personal development. At the École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), you have the opportunity to embark on an exciting apprenticeship in the field of chemistry. Meeting with Sébastien, apprentice Laborantin in chemistry Main tasks and responsibilities The apprenticeship place is offered at the Central Environmental Laboratory (CEL) of the Faculty of Natural, Architectural and Built Environment (ENAC). CEL is a multidisciplinary analytical platform active in the fields of environmental chemistry, molecular biology and microbiology. With advanced analytical instruments, the laboratory develops and applies methods to characterize and quantify a wide range of chemical compounds and trace elements of major environmental interest. CEL's research activities include studying the fate, transport and impact of pollutants in aquatic and terrestrial environments. As an apprentice in chemistry, you will participate in a wide range of practical and analytical activities, including:

  • Prepare samples for analysis (extraction, digestion, filtration)
  • Learn to use high-tech analytical instruments (LC-MS/MS, GC-MS/MS, IC, ICP-OES, ICP-QQQ, etc.) for characterization and quantification of organic and inorganic substances.
  • Identify and characterize molecules using different methods of mass spectrometry.
  • Conduct analyses on environmental samples (water, soil, sediment), biological samples, as well as on various materials from EPFL laboratories or other research institutions.
  • Interpret analytical results to assess the accuracy, reproducibility and reliability of measurements.
  • To participate in the proper functioning of the laboratory by applying the existing quality and safety procedures.
  • Profile The apprenticeship of Laborantine orientation Chemistry is accessible to young people at the end of compulsory schooling (VP, VG2, gymnasium ...), and which show a strong interest in the sciences and laboratory professions.
