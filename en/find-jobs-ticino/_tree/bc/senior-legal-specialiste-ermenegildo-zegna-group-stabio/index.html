Full description

Descrizione BASIC PURPOSE OF THE JOB As Senior Legal Specialist, he/she will be responsible for providing legal advice and support on commercial and privacy related matters. MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES Working under the supervision of the Group Legal Affairs & IP Director for the Zegna Segment, he/she will provide legal guidance and advice to management and other departments on a wide range of legal and privacy matters affecting the business, in particular:

  • Draft, review, and negotiate contracts, agreements and other legal documents, such as supply and purchase agreements, services and consultancy agreement, lease agreements, franchise, agency and wholesale contracts
  • Manage the commercial contract lifecycle (negotiation, conclusion, follow-up of execution, renewal, termination) in compliance with applicable laws and internal policies, and maintain the contract lifecycle database up-to-date
  • Draft, review or update policies, agreements, documents and projects related to collection, use and disclosure of personal information, in accordance with changes in privacy laws and regulations and/or changes in business; manage a periodical assessment of the privacy governance
  • Provide legal advice on privacy matters to management and various departments
  • Working closely with the Group Compliance & Risk Manager, ensure compliance with relevant privacy legislation across the organization of the Zegna Segment and communicate privacy guidance to various stakeholders and departments
  • Support the Group Legal Affairs & IP Director in managing litigation related to his/her scope of responsibility
  • Seek appropriate guidance and support from external counsel or expert when necessary
  • Conduct legal research and stay updated on relevant laws and regulations in his/her scope of responsibility
  • Assess and manage legal risks associated with business operations and transactions in his/her scope of responsibility BACKGROUND AND PRINCIPAL SKILLS REQUIRED
  • Solid knowledge of contracts in both Italian and English language and of privacy laws and international requirements
  • Excellent business sense and problem-solving skills
  • Proficiency in legal research and drafting in Italian and English
  • Ability to work autonomously and as part of a team
  • Strong organizational and time management skills
  • Agility and openness to digital tools
  • Strong attention to detail
  • Ability to handle multiple projects simultaneously and capacity to work under pressure and/or with tight deadlines
  • Proactive approach to identifying and addressing legal issues
  • Ability to explain complex legal concepts to non-legal stakeholders
  • Knowledge of the fashion or luxury sector is preferable
  • Familiarity with cross-border operations for companies with global operations and/or listed companies will be considered a plus
  • At least 5 years of legal experience, preferably including both in-house experience and experience in a law firm
  • Master's Degree in Law
  • Outstanding written and verbal communication abilities both in Italian and English
