The Position

Geograpics (departments): 18, 23, 28P, 36, 41, 45 (P=partial)

As a Commercial Engineer (d/f/m) – Diabetes Care, you are the ambassador of our solutions to pharmacists and healthcare professionals in your area. You play a key role in promoting our product range, both on distribution channels and prescription channels, and actively contribute to the growth of our business.

Your main responsibilities

Trade Development (Distribution)

  • Develop sales in your sector by ensuring a quality relationship with the agents, in compliance with RDF's trade policy and agreements;
  • Build customer-specific action plans to ensure customer loyalty;
  • Propose and negotiate suitable offers or partnerships
  • Monitoring, piloting and animating clients from national or regional groupings;
  • Contribute to the performance of the brand's product and service lines;
  • Train compendial teams on products, services and tools through demonstrations.

Medical promotion (Prescription)

  • Develop prescriptions and sales to hospitals, liberal specialists and general practitioners;
  • Develop action plans to strengthen the growth of turnover and market shares while ensuring the retention of healthcare professionals;
  • Maintain and develop trust relations with opinion leaders (KOL) in the sector;
  • Train hospital and liberal professionals in RDF products, services and devices.

Reporting and monitoring

  • Regularly update business data in existing reporting tools;
  • Participate in customer events and represent the company externally;
  • Ensure active monitoring of the competitive environment and the diabetes market.

Who are you?

  • Higher commercial training
  • Successful experience in field sales, ideally in the medical device or consumer sector
  • A good knowledge of pharmaceutical and medical issues would be ideal
  • Excellent conviction capacity and results orientation;
  • Strong relational ease and listening
