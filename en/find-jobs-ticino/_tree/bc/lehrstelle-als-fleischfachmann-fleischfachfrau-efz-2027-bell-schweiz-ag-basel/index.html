Full description

Bell Schweiz AG, a leading food brand in Switzerland, offers you the perfect start to your professional life with an apprenticeship as a meat specialist EFZ. With our support, you will become part of the Bell Learning community and start your career in a varied environment.

  • Contract type: apprenticeship
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Job opening: 01.08.2027
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Alsace Road 174 4056 Basel

Tasks

  • You choose a profession with a future in an extremely varied industry.
  • Meat specialists/meat specialists receive basic training from us. It deals with the basic competencies such as boning, cutting and dressing cuts of meat, production of food ready for kitchen and pan food and compliance with hygiene, safety and sustainability requirements.
  • In this profession, you choose a focus that suits you best:
  • In the "meat harvesting" you will learn everything about animal husbandry and the professional slaughter, cutting and boning.
  • When focusing on harvesting with "specialization poultry" you will learn the meat harvesting and processing of poultry. You process, package and declare the products for the end user. Ensuring sustainability and quality and hygiene requirements are also important learning objectives.
  • The focus of "commercial meat processing" is the production of meat and other raw materials for high-quality food.
  • In the "meat refinement" your creativity is required. For example, you can prove these with Traiteurwaren, cold plates and ready meals.
  • The production of ready-to-sell products and the dynamic manufacturing process in large-scale industrial operations round off your teaching.

Requirements

  • Completed elementary school
  • Enjoyment in dealing with food and a careful way of working
  • Physical fitness and craftsmanship

This is what we offer

  • Work-life balance: With us you benefit from 35 days of vacation, so that you have enough relaxation time and are then fit for everyday work and school.
  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: freshly prepared food in our staff restaurant, a lunch menu incl. Soup and salad, water & tea and free fruits as well as high-quality products at greatly reduced prices await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from our contribution to environmental mobility, convenient connection to public transport and free SBB general subscription (GA)!
  • Professional development opportunities: We take over the school materials and participate in the amount of CHF 700.- in a notebook of your choice. After your training, we also offer you exciting prospects in an international environment and you can develop further through training in the Coop Education Center.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management and a free subscription at Update Fitness - for health and leisure fun!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments with our learning community and every two years at our learning event with over 150 learners from all over Bell Food Group!
  • Market-friendly wages and social benefits: With a good academic and operational performance, you can look forward to your performance premium. Twice a year!
  • Family-friendly employer: Work in professional workwear and experience an inclusive work environment where diversity is valued and encouraged!
