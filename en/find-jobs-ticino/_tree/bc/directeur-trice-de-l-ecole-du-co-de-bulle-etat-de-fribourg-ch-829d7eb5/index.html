Full description

Who are we?

The Service de l'enseignement obligatoire de langue française (SenOF) is responsible for the pedagogical conduct and management of education at different levels of compulsory education in the French-speaking part of the canton. It also supervises private schools and home education.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • In accordance with the guidelines laid down by the Compulsory Education Service and the DFAC, the CO Director shall manage the organisation, operation and administrative and pedagogical management of his/her institution, the conduct of staff, the quality of teaching and education, and the collaboration with the school partners with whom he/she represents the school.
  • He/she also pays particular attention to the quality of the climate within the institution and the well-being of the people working there. Where appropriate, he/she resolves any difficulties that may arise
  • He/she works closely with the Association of CO Municipalities in carrying out his/her tasks.

Profile desired

  • Secondary education diploma I
  • If possible CAS in management and management of training institution or training deemed equivalent
  • Minimum 8 years of teaching experience
  • Desired experience in conducting educational projects
  • Ability to build quality human relations
  • A sense of responsibility and collaboration
  • Mastery of the French language with good knowledge of the German language
