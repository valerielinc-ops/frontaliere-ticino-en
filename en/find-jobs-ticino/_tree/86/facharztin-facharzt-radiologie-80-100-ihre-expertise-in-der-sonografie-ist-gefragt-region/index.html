Do you want to work radiologically versatile and not only CT and MRI images? In a regional acute care hospital near Bern you can expect a broad diagnostic spectrum with CT, MRI, conventional radiology and mammadiagnostics. A special focus is on the sonography, which you perform independently. You benefit from the direct exchange with the assigning departments and a collegial hospital environment. The position is aimed at radiologists who like to work clinically and patient-friendly. Also specialists: women from Germany with practical sonography experience are welcome. The institute is part of a regionally anchored acute care hospital near Bern. Radiology serves outpatient and inpatient patients and covers a broad diagnostic spectrum. Infrastructure includes CT, MRI, conventional radiology, ultrasound and mammadiagnostics. Sonography is particularly important because it is performed independently and is closely connected to the clinical questions of the assigning departments. The team works in manageable structures with short decision-making paths and direct professional exchange. Depending on experience, individual focus areas can be introduced or further developed, for example in mammadiagnostics, MSC or interventions.

Their tasks

  • Results of CT, MRI and conventional radiological examinations
  • Independent conduct and diagnosis of sonographic examinations
  • Radiological care of outpatients and inpatients
  • Participation in interdisciplinary reports and case discussions
  • Close cooperation with the assigning departments
  • Depending on experience Opportunity for cooperation or specialization in mammadiagnostics, interventions or other focus areas

Your Competences

  • Radiology, German or EU-approved
  • Support for recognition in Switzerland by Medimeo
  • Practical experience in sonography and enjoyment of independent performance
  • Safe knowledge of CT and MRI diagnostics
  • Interest in broad clinical radiology in a Swiss acute care hospital
  • Independent, careful and structured working methods
  • Motivation to establish yourself in Switzerland in the long term
  • Very good knowledge of German
