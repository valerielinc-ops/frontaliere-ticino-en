Full description

Migros Aare prepares you to independently and successfully manage a Migros branch. The compact and practical training is tailored to your needs. We train you over a period of 12-18 months with full salary. Your application will be considered for all branches in the vicinity of the desired workplace.

Responsibilities

  • Customer-oriented management of the branch together with the market management
  • Responsibility for freshness, quality, and availability of products
  • Co-responsibility for the management and development of department heads, team leaders, and employees, as well as for talent development
  • Management of a department
  • Ensuring defined processes
  • Acting as a substitute for market management during absences

Requirements

  • Ideally more than 3 years of professional experience: in the supermarket sector with several years of management experience
  • Completed basic or advanced training: in retail or an equivalent qualification
  • Passion for retail
  • Enjoyment in leading and promoting employees
  • Commitment, resilience, and flexibility
  • Strong customer orientation and convincing demeanor
  • German (business fluent)

What we offer

  • Maternity/Paternity leave: 18 weeks of maternity leave and 4 weeks of paternity leave
  • Childcare: Generous financial contribution to external childcare services
  • Mobile subscription: Various discounts on mobile subscriptions
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits ensure financial security for employees in retirement
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with excellent social benefits
  • Cumulus points: Multiple Cumulus points on food and non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest rates on capital, or a discount on interest rates for newly concluded fixed-rate mortgages
  • Employee offers: Preferential conditions for insurance and health insurance
  • Sports and fitness offers: Contributions to subscriptions for over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, and aquaparks

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
