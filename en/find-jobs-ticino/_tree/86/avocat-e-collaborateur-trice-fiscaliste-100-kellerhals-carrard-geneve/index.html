Your tasks

You like complex cases in the areas of advice and tax litigation. You are comfortable writing and analyzing financial elements.

Your profile

  • Legal training in Swiss law (essential prerequisites), holder of a Swiss lawyer's patent;
  • At least two to three years' experience in the tax field;
  • Perfect command of the French language;
  • Very good command of English;
  • Good synthesis, sense of responsibility;
  • Good management of stress inherent in the activity of a lawyer's study and flexibility;
  • Management of priorities and timelines;
  • Ability to work as a team but also independently in case management;
  • Mastery of common computer tools, in particular Excel (Suite Office, conference tools, messaging, PDF reader, etc.)
  • Switzerland or valid work permit

About us

Kellerhals Carrard is one of Switzerland's most important business lawyers with offices in Basel, Bern, Geneva, Lausanne, Lugano, Sion and Zurich. Our clients are private individuals, businesses and institutions, both in Switzerland and abroad. With its dynamism and entrepreneurial spirit, Kellerhals Carrard has experienced an evolution without comparison in recent years.
