More about this search for cross-border applicants

Mendrisio, nestled between Italy and Switzerland, offers a dynamic job market for professionals who commute across the border. With a mix of healthcare, education, engineering, and administrative roles, the area attracts cross-border workers—often called *frontalieri*—seeking stable employment near home. Whether you’re in nursing, IT, or teaching, local demand and flexible work arrangements make Mendrisio a practical choice for career-focused expats.
