More about this search for cross-border applicants

Mendrisio, nestled between Italy and Switzerland, offers a dynamic job market for professionals who commute across the border. The city’s proximity to Lugano and Bellinzona, along with robust public transport links, makes it an ideal hub for those balancing career and daily life. Whether you’re in nursing, IT, or teaching, local demand and flexible work arrangements make Mendrisio a practical choice for career-focused expats.
