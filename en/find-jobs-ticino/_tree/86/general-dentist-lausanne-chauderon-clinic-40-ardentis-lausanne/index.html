What we offer

Type of contract: CDI Rate: 40% Date of entry: Immediately or to be agreed Places : Place Chauderon 16, 1003 Lausanne

Your daily role

  • Clinical Excellence: Accurate diagnosis, development of interdisciplinary treatment projects and high quality care (composites, onlays, endocrowns, crowns on natural teeth, facets, crowns on implants, removable prostheses, etc.). To benefit from the support of a treatment coordinator can propose and frame bold treatment projects.
  • Technological innovation: Use advanced equipment such as smile design tools, conical beam volume tomography, intra-oral scanners, intra-oral cameras, lasers or guided surgical planners.
  • Continuing Education: Actively participate in training programs to stay at the forefront of the latest dentistry techniques and innovations. Get in touch with the most relevant training in the field. Access the mentoring program and benefit from the sharing of expertise among fellow experts in their field.
  • Sustainable Commitment: Adopt environmentally friendly practices and promote sustainability in dental care.
  • Collaboration and Leadership: Work as a team to ensure interdisciplinary management and share your knowledge with your colleagues.

Your profile

  • Diploma: Federal degree in dentistry or equivalent recognition MEBEKO.
  • Language: French mother tongue or level C1 with DALF certificate.
  • Passion: You are passionate about innovation and aim for excellence in your practice.
  • Proactivity: You are ready to evolve professionally and actively contribute to the growth of our team.
  • Long-term vision: You are motivated by the sustainability of your career and the lasting success of your dental practice, as well as the overall success of Ardentis.

Why join Ardentis?

  • As soon as you enter Ardentis, you will benefit from a personalized integration training tailored to your needs.
  • You will evolve in a benevolent environment, conducive to growth, where you can excel and innovate.
  • By integrating Ardentis, you will be able to imbue yourself with a corporate culture that values sharing and team spirit, placing great importance on sustainability.
  • Very good working conditions and wages.

Join us and become a driving force in a community dedicated to dental excellence. The adventure begins here!
