Group management Kita

Group Leader / Group Leader 60 %

Children do not need perfect adults, but real people with humor and joy in day-to-day daycare. Do you recognize yourself in it? Then apply now! *

From 1\. January 2027 or by appointment we are looking for you as a group leader! You lead the group in job sharing together with another group leader.

Your tasks

  • Professional and organizational management of your group
  • Leadership and support of the team in everyday educational life
  • Ensuring professional, loving and age-appropriate child care
  • Planning and design of a varied group everyday life
  • Conducting door and fishing discussions as well as location determinations with parents
  • Responsibility for processes, deadlines and organisational tasks within the group
  • Training of learners
  • Cooperation with the other group leaders and management

Your profile

  • Completed training as a specialist / specialist supervision EFZ or a comparable recognised pedagogical training
  • You bring at least three years of professional experience
  • Joy of taking responsibility and leading a team
  • Independent, reliable and organized working method
  • Flexibility, resilience and the ability to keep track even in lively situations
  • Your working days are ideally Monday to Wednesday, other combinations are possible by arrangement
  • A lot of room for manoeuvre and personal responsibility
  • Individual training opportunities
  • Three fixed working days per week
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Paid break

Interested? Then we look forward to your application documents!
