Full description

Are you interested in the strategic design of digital collaboration in a large corporation? Are you excited about combining business needs and IT opportunities into an effective product vision? In this role, you are responsible for Microsoft 365 as a central productivity platform for 50,000 users: inside, continuously. With you, the digital workplace becomes a productivity platform for the entire Group. You can do that.

  • You will bear product responsibility for digital collaboration within the Group and control the strategic development of the platform in cooperation with Microsoft, the business areas and IT. In addition, you will work in tandem with the Product Owner M365 in the Agile Release Train (ART) Workplace and the Topic Lead Collaboration, develop vision, goals and roadmap and ensure coordinated prioritization with clear business and customer benefits.
  • Ensure that digital collaboration for office and frontline workers is user-oriented, pragmatic and effective. In addition, you use data and key figures to specifically control the use, added value and further development of the platform and derive well-founded optimization decisions.
  • You are responsible for security, governance, stability and compliance and create clear standards, transparent decision-making processes and clean documentation.
  • Drive automation and efficiency potential, integrate licensing and cost-benefit considerations into planning and work closely with stakeholders and user adoption. You bring that with you Several years of experience in product management or in a similar role set you apart, taking responsibility for the entire product lifecycle, including roadmap management and priority of initiatives.
  • You also have a technical background or experience in the field of IT architecture, which allows you to work on an equal footing with business, engineering and architecture.
  • You have experience in agile working, ideally in the SAFe environment. An in-depth understanding of modern digital collaboration as well as in-depth knowledge in Microsoft 365 or comparable collaboration platforms bring you or are ready to deepen this know-how.
  • You convince with strong stakeholder management, a structured and pragmatic working method as well as analytical thinking in dealing with dependencies, risks, benefits and regulatory requirements.
  • Microsoft 365 Copilot or similar AI tools are used safely in everyday work, understand their interaction with M365 data and communicate well orally and in writing in German, have good (verbal B2) knowledge in a second national language and good (verbal) knowledge in English. At this point, we waive the dossier of recruitment agencies.
