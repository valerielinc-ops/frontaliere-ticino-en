Full description

The UniBE is versatile. With us, you can expect a wide variety of topics and a lot of design freedom.

Tasks

  • Processing of applications for admission of persons with a Swiss qualification certificate for Bachelor and Master
  • Enrollment and supervision of students of continuing education and contact with the program lines of the offers
  • Supervision of already admitted students: change of personal data, treatment of change of subject and leave of absence, renewal of matriculations or exmatriculations
  • Providing general factual information as well as information and brief advice on questions of admission and enrolment by telephone. Email or even at the counter
  • As required, use for further tasks within the framework of ZIB

Requirements

  • Commercial education with professional qualification or equivalent
  • Some years of professional experience with direct customer contact
  • Excellent knowledge of the German language in word and writing, very good active and passive knowledge of the English and French language
  • Other languages are beneficial, but not a requirement
  • Very good IT user knowledge
  • Careful, exact and independent working method
  • Resilience, even in hectic times and situations
  • Flexibility
  • Customer orientation
  • Teamwork
  • Communication capability

Multifaceted task at a central location of the University of Bern Application and contact Please send your application to Daniela Egger-Mikic by 26.11.2026 With us, you create value. For themselves and society.

About us

The Department of Admission, Matriculation and Counselling (ZIB) is responsible for all processes and questions relating to registration and admission to study at the University of Bern and semester enrolment. For the processing of the registrations of prospective students we are looking for 01. April 2027 or, by arrangement, a reliable case handler.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
