Full description

The Hirslanden Primar Day School belongs to the beautiful Zurichberg School District. The primary school consists of four kindergarten classes, six secondary and six intermediate classes. The total of 400 students are taught by about 40 teachers. The care area of the Hirslanden Primar Day School comprises three childcare centres for kindergarten children, one childcare centre for lower school children and two childcare centres for middle school children and a morning table.

The care of the Hort Hegibach and Hofacker is looking for reinforcements immediately or by agreement.

  • They support the pedagogical specialists in operational, organizational and pedagogical tasks. They help shape the activities in the lunchtime. They support and accompany the students. They do domestic activities such as: meal preparation, general maintenance and clean-up tasks. They perform household tasks as well as cleaning the kitchen and its furnishings
  • They are creative, proactive and active and actively involved in the design of the offers at lunchtime.
  • In the housekeeping sector and in cleaning, you perform upcoming tasks independently, quickly and efficiently
  • You are flexible, resilient, humorous and a team player
  • Professional experience in pedagogical work and interdisciplinary cooperation are beneficial
  • You have very good knowledge of German (level B2)

A varied and independent activity in a motivated and committed team, which is looking forward to working with you.

They receive competent support from the management and a progressive salary as well as social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

Your assignment days/times are: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday from 10.30 a.m. to 2 p.m. without assignment during school holidays.

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 18 school houses. These are connected to 43 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 14 organizational units, 11 are day schools.
