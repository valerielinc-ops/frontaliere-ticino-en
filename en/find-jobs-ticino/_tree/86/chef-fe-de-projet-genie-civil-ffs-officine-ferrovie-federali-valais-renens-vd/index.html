Project leader Civil engineering 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Civil engineering project leader Valais / Renens / Work Smart, 01.02.2027 or suitable, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

You help design safe and reliable rail infrastructure with a direct impact on customers. You will evolve in a varied environment within the Ingénierie des œuvres de la Romandie team, where collaboration and knowledge transfer take place on a daily basis. During the first two years, you will receive in-depth train training focused on rail and you will develop continuously. The position we propose mainly concerning the Valais Line with several projects in progress in the Haut-Valais region. The workplace is therefore located between Valais and Renens. At the level of the organization two days are planned in Renens and the rest of the week can be organized in a presentation in Valais and partly in a home office. Here you can find more information about this position: Project leader in civil engineering, SBB, or other information about projects underway in French-speaking Switzerland: French-speaking Switzerland and Valais: Rail projects and regional construction programmes To find out about the benefits, such as free AG and low-cost travel abroad (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits . What you can move.

  • As a project leader, you manage a portfolio of multidisciplinary projects and conduct engineering works from the preliminary project to commissioning.
  • You are responsible for the overall direction of projects and ensure that costs, deadlines, quality and safety are respected.
  • You coordinate internal specialists as well as external agents and various stakeholders, and ensure an effective interface with railway operations.
  • You control and validate the designs, dimension the supporting structures and conduct, if necessary, internal expertise.
  • Depending on the file, you take overall responsibility for the execution of the work and represent the projects in external bodies or organizations.

What you bring.

  • Through your proactive and committed work, you lead interdisciplinary teams and convince various stakeholders.
  • In demanding project situations, you resolve problems in a structured way and communicate clearly and appropriately to the interlocutors.
  • You bring several years of professional experience, for example in the engineering office, in the conduct of works or in project management; an initial experience in managing railway projects is an advantage.
  • You have solid technical knowledge in the calculation, design and execution of carrier structures, for example with experience in reinforced concrete and metal construction.
  • You have a degree in civil engineering (ETH, HES/HEI or equivalent qualification) and use French at level C1 and German at level B2 in the professional setting.

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive salary system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the K level of requirements and is eligible for a level 1 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits .

Here's a good way to start.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the biggest motivation for us. We therefore waive our request for a cover letter for this position. If you wish, you can write to us in our application tool.
