Introduction

For our customers in the electrical industry, we are looking for a qualified and responsible personality as an assembly electrician. You take on varied work in the area of new installations, conversions and service.

Tasks

  • Installation of electrical equipment in new and altered buildings
  • Laying of wiring, assembly of distributors and control cabinets
  • Performing simple troubleshooting
  • Support during test and measurement work
  • Close cooperation with construction and project managers
  • Ensure compliance with all safety standards

Profile

  • Completed training as an assembly electrician or equivalent experience
  • Practical experience on construction sites or in the service sector
  • Technical understanding and careful working methods
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Team-oriented, reliable and independent
  • Enjoyment of versatile installation tasks

Advantages

  • Modern tools and high-quality equipment
  • Varied locations
  • Development opportunities in the electrical sector
  • Current employment conditions
  • Collegial, supportive environment
