Company description ##

dasteam ag is one of the leading personnel service providers in Switzerland with over 35 years of experience. With 28 branches throughout Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein, we arrange qualified specialists in the areas of main and secondary construction, crafts, technology, industry and medical & care.

For our customers in the Rhine Valley, Sarganserland and Chur region, we are constantly looking for motivated and experienced masons for varied assignments on new and renovations.

Job description ##

  • Independent masonry and formwork work on civil engineering sites
  • Creation of wall, ceiling and staircase formwork with system and conventional formwork
  • Concrete, compacting and reworking
  • Masonry work with various building materials
  • Reading and implementing building plans
  • General construction work and support of the construction site team
  • Compliance with safety rules on the construction site

We are expecting ##

  • Completed training as a bricklayer or several years of professional experience in building construction
  • Experience in formwork construction (e.g. Doka, Peri or conventional formwork)
  • Independent, reliable and careful working methods
  • German language skills
  • Physical resilience and skill
  • Teamwork as well as a high quality and safety awareness
  • Category B driving licence is advantageous

We offer ##

  • Attractive and performance-based remuneration
  • Long-term and varied deployments
  • Modern construction sites and high-quality work equipment
  • Pleasant working atmosphere in a motivated team
  • Personal and professional support by team ag
  • Possibility of permanent employment if appropriate
