Full description

In a nutshell

Would you like to put your military policy expertise to practical use in an international environment? If so, join International Relations Defence, which represents the Army’s interests in the international arena at the interface between the military, law and foreign affairs.

  • Start date: by agreement
  • Employment type: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-20637
  • Workplace: Guisanplatz 1, 3003 Bern

This is what you can contribute

  • Lead and coordinate the Armed Forces/Defence Group’s military relations within the thematic portfolio
  • Analyse developments at international level with a view to your own portfolio, particularly in the areas of security policy developments, situation developments and armed forces development; prepare and advocate corresponding decision-making documents within and outside the Federal Administration
  • Be responsible for managing the international contacts of the Armed Forces/Defence Group (including annual planning for INTAKTA, approval procedures and reporting); decide on conflicts of interest at Armed Forces/Defence Group level in your own portfolio or prepare the necessary basis so that the higher-level authority can decide
  • Conduct negotiations with foreign partners and within the Federal Administration as part of the international contacts of the Armed Forces/Defence Group in your own portfolio
  • Maintain relations with stakeholders in the Armed Forces/Defence Group, with SEPOS, the FDFA and foreign representations in Switzerland (in particular defence attachés); manage other assignments within the scope of your own portfolio

This makes you unique

  • Completed university degree in international relations, law, political science, history or economics (master's degree or equivalent)
  • Additional training in international relations desirable
  • In-depth knowledge of how the Army Staff operates, particularly in army planning and/or army development, desirable
  • Analytical and editorial skills, networked thinking, as well as a high level of resilience, precision, independence and sense of responsibility
  • Further military training (staff officer or unit commander) an advantage
  • Very good active knowledge of a second official language and very good spoken and written English; passive knowledge of a third official language an advantage

What we offer

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Diversity in action Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and contribute different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees on physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Army Staff (A Staff) supports the Chief of the Army in managing the Defence sector of the department and is responsible for the Swiss Armed Forces' overall military planning and development. In accordance with political guidelines, the strategic foundations, armed forces development, and army and armaments planning are developed here, and tasks in the international security context are carried out. The management, control and coordination of all cross-cutting areas (personnel, finances, real estate) also fall within the remit of the Army Staff.

The Swiss Armed Forces employs over 9'000 employees in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also catered for: The Swiss Armed Forces trains apprentices in over 30 occupations.
