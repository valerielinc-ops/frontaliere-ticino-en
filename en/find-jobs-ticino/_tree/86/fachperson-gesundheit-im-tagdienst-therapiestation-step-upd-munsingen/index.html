Clinic for Psychosis and Dependence

At the STEP (Specialised Therapy for Psychotic Illnesses) Clinic for Psychosis and Dependence of the UPZ, you accompany people with psychotic diseases in their everyday lives and support them in strengthening their resources and developing new perspectives. We meet our patients at eye level, take their individual experiences seriously and actively involve them in the design of their treatment. Together with an experienced nursing team and other specialists, you will create a safe, appreciative and recovery-oriented environment in which self-determination, personal development and respectful cooperation are the focus. With your personality, your attentive awareness and your commitment, you make an important contribution to individual and human psychiatric care.

Your tasks

  • You support our experienced care team in the individual care and support of our patients
  • You accompany them in the activities of daily life, promote their existing resources and strengthen their independence
  • You actively shape the daily routine and plan and accompany group offers, such as cooking groups or activities outside the institution
  • You implement the nursing and milieutherapeutic measures defined in the treatment plan professionally and evidence-based
  • You contribute to entry and exit and support the design of a structured and therapeutically beneficial everyday life
  • You are actively involved in the design of the therapeutic environment, the daily structure and the leisure activities
  • You notice changes in the well-being and behavior of our patients attentively and contribute your observations to the interprofessional cooperation
  • You work closely with the interprofessional team and contribute to a holistic support
  • You take on department-specific administrative and domestic tasks and thus support a smooth station day

Your profile

  • You have a health EFZ degree and bring professional experience in psychiatry
  • You have profound experience in psychiatric care and care and are interested in accompanying people with psychotic diseases
  • You have a high level of technical and social competence and are ready to reflect on your actions regularly
  • You keep calm even in challenging situations and meet our patients sovereignly, empathetically and respectfully
  • You work responsibly, structured and team-oriented
  • You appreciate interprofessional cooperation and enjoy actively shaping a therapeutic environment
  • You want to accompany our patients individually, strengthen their resources and promote their independence in everyday life

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
