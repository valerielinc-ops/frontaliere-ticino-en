Your career journey belongs to you. Our ambition is to help you write its next chapters. As a Housekeeping Supervisor Intern, your main responsibilities will include:

  • Assisting the Housekeeping team in coordinating the department's daily operations.
  • Monitoring and updating room status (arrivals, departures, priority rooms, and "Do Not Disturb" rooms).
  • Preparing and distributing daily worksheets and room assignments for the housekeeping teams.
  • Coordinating requests between Housekeeping, Front Office, Maintenance, and Laundry departments.
  • Following up on guest requests and contributing to guest satisfaction.
  • Ensuring administrative tasks are properly completed and maintaining the required reports.
  • Reporting equipment malfunctions, maintenance needs, and safety concerns.
  • Ensuring compliance with the hotel's quality, hygiene, and safety standards.
