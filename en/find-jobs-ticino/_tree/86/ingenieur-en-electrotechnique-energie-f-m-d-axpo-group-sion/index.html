Full description

Position: 100%

Do you want to participate in the design of technical projects, assume responsibilities and work on future energy installations? Then you're at the right address at our house.

For our hydropower plants, we are looking for an engineer to contribute to the operation, maintenance and evolution of our developments. You will intervene at the intersection of electrical engineering, automation and the use of data from our installations to improve their reliability and performance.

Tasks and responsibilities

  • Plan, organize and monitor projects in the areas of automation, telecommunications and maintenance 4.0
  • Structure, process and analyse data from power plants
  • Develop or improve databases, queries, reports and tables for technical monitoring of facilities
  • Reliate data, monitor consistency, document sources and contribute to their archiving
  • Technical monitoring and regular updating of existing systems
  • Providing technical support for our power plants
  • Ensure availability, cyber security of industrial systems

Profile and skills

  • HES engineering diploma or equivalent
  • Experience or interest in databases and data analysis. Mastering SQL language and reporting tools is an asset
  • Interest in modern energy and automation systems
  • Ability to work independently and structuredly
  • Responsibility and teamwork
  • Mastery of French and German
