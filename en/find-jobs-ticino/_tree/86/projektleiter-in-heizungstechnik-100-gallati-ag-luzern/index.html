Full description

We are a Swiss company with competences in heating/cold, ventilation/air conditioning as well as sanitary technology and planning. With headquarters in Lucerne and around 80 employees, we operate throughout the Canton of Lucerne. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the leading provider of cross-trade building technology in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. Our goal is satisfied customers, employees, suppliers and environmentally friendly work. We achieve this by doing our best every day, providing optimal advice, handling the orders with the necessary care and exceeding the highest demands.

Are you ready for the next career step? We are looking for a project manager in heating technology (100%).
