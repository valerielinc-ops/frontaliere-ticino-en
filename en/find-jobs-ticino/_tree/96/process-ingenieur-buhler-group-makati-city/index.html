Process Engineer — Bühler Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Assist the Technology Department in providing correct information according to Buhler standards for the design of concept diagrams and correct machine specifications. Provide technical support to sales, quotation, and customer service department. Establish solutions for customer opportunities and projects. Commissioning and mill surveys of simple plants and single machines. Commissioning support for standard and complex plants.

Aufgaben

  • Support technology department with sharing knowledge and skills, establish diagrams for simple and standard projects
  • Establish concept flow diagrams according Buhler standards (simple projects and single machines)
  • Test software program before going for commissioning
  • Assess mechanical and electrical installation and communicate with personal in charge. Resolve questions or problems that occur during installation and commissioning. Verify that direction and IO test has been done before doing a dry run
  • Take the lead and manage the project during commissioning and communicate with the customer, project manager and team manager on a regularly basis to ensure projects run smoothly with normal progress.
  • Start up the plant or equipment with product and adjust process to ensure plant is running efficiently on contractual performance.
  • Record data during commissioning, and mill surveys. Reports during commissioning and after return in a qualified manner. Capture all relevant data on Buhler system
  • Record any project deviations, technical issues and product improvements in the Bühler CAQ system

Anforderungen

  • Graduate of Engineering degree or related field
  • Minimum 1 year experience in flour milling
  • A miller apprenticeship is preferred
  • Strong interpersonal and teamwork skills
  • Excellent problem solving skills
  • Ability to work in a matrix organization
  • Good written and oral communication skills

Two billion people eat food every day that was produced with Bühler equipment. One billion people drive vehicles whose parts were manufactured with our machines.
