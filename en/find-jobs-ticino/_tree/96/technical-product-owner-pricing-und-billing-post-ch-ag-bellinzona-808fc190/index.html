Full description

You want to shape how prices, services and invoices will consistently play together in the future? As Technical Product Owner, you are responsible for technical product management for pricing and billing in the commercial stack.

We are looking for an initiative personality that quickly grasps complex relationships, takes responsibility and develops viable solutions. You combine technical understanding with a sense of business requirements and bring business, finance and IT together. We combine pricing logics, contract terms, usage and event data as well as rating, charging and invoicing into a scalable end-to-end solution.

*You can do it *

  • You will develop the technical product vision, the roadmap and the backlog for pricing and billing together with business owners, business engineering, finance, architecture and implementation teams.
  • You will lead the technical implementation of standard and contract prices, price rules, discounts, surcharges, squadrons and customer-specific conditions up to the billable position and invoice.
  • You ensure the clear reference between product offering, agreement, pricing logic, use or event as well as rating, charging and billing.
  • In addition, you coordinate integrations for product and contract management, performance data, SAP or billing components, analytics, support and customer channels.
  • You prioritize features and enablers, control delivery teams and vendors, and ensure operational capability, traceability, auditability, performance, and troubleshooting.
  • In addition, you validate the end-to-end billing flow on the basis of reference products and contracts and derive the gradual industrialization from this.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of experience as Technical Product Owner, IT Product Manager or Solution Lead in the field of Pricing, Billing, Order-to-Cash or Revenue Management.
  • In addition, you will have knowledge of pricing, rating, charging, discounting, invoicing and the necessary data and integration flows.
  • You have experience with complex standard solutions and integration landscapes, ideally with SAP BRIM, S/4HANA or comparable platforms.
  • You can also safely handle agile delivery models, backlogs, technical enablers, MVPs, releases and operational requirements.
  • You convince with analytical strength and the ability to understand professional and technical dependencies and make decisions.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German and English. Every other national language is a plus.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
