Teacher for German and German for foreign speakers

Bündner Kantonsschule \| 80\-100% \| Chur

Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us.

The Bundner Kantonsschule is an important educational institution with around 1,000 students from all three cantonal languages. As a department of the Office for Higher Education, it offers secondary education courses at the \- and short-term high school as well as at the specialist, commercial and computer science schools. As a teacher for German and German for foreign languages, you teach learners at the secondary school as well as at the specialist, trade and computer science schools.

We are looking for a teacher for German and German for foreign speakers for our school as of 1\.2027.

Your responsibilities

  • Teaching in the subject area German and German for foreign speakers at the secondary school as well as at the specialist, trade and computer science school
  • Organization and implementation of project weeks, class events and excursions in a team with other teachers
  • Taking over various work within the student body
  • Participation in development projects of the comprehensive school

That is what we want from you.

  • Completed university studies in German as well as a teaching diploma for Maturity schools
  • Additional qualification in German as a second language (DaZ) at secondary level II desired
  • Flexible, initiative and team capable personality who likes to work with young people. In addition, we want a communicative person who takes responsibility and likes to be involved in school life.
  • As a teacher at the trilingual school, knowledge of other cantonal languages round off your profile

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to shape the future of the canton

Place of work
