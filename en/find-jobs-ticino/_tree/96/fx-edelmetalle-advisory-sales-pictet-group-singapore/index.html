Full description

Your team

The Pictet Group is one of the world’s leading independent wealth and asset managers. Founded in 1805 and headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, the Group is represented in 30 offices in financial centres worldwide, currently employing over 5000 people. Pictet has been present in Hong Kong for more than 25 years where it employs more than 200 people.

Pictet Wealth Management combines more than 200 years of Swiss banking heritage with global investment expertise. The Partner-owned financial service group offers a comprehensive service for wealthy individuals and families including discretionary and advisory investment solutions and family office services.

Main Duties & Responsibilities Mission

  • Deliver proactive, actionable FX & Precious Metals advice to Investment Specialists, Relationship Managers and clients, aligned with the House view and market opportunities.
  • Create and disseminate high-quality investment content and trade ideas across all internal and external channels.
  • Identify business opportunities and manage risk through systematic analysis of client activity and portfolio exposures.
  • Drive asset class development by collaborating on cross-asset projects, enhancing tools, and ensuring robust compliance and risk management.

Responsibilities

  • Proactively advise internal clients (relationship managers and investment specialists) and selected active clients on their FX and PM trading, investment and hedging needs.
  • Create timely investment advice in line with the House view and taking advantage of prevailing market conditions.
  • Create and distribute FX & PM communications (publications, broadcasts ideas …) through all available medias and channel (intranet, chat rooms, conference calls, investment meetings, one-on-one meetings …)
  • Analyze the book of business for opportunities and risk management.
  • Support RMs in monitoring FX margin positions and assist RMs to explain the situation in case there are margin calls.
  • Collaborate with Fixed Income, Equities, and Structured Product specialists across Asia and Geneva to deliver cross-asset solutions and drive business synergies.
  • Contribute to the development of the asset class through project work on tools, systems, risk framework, training material and marketing documents.
  • Support the development of structured products across asset classes.
  • Ensure optimal compliance and risk management that takes account of all relevant local laws, regulatory requirements, in-house directives and procedures, as well as possessing adequate knowledge to apply these effectively and ensure fair treatment of all clients.
