Full description

Founded and headquartered in Switzerland, Avaloq is continuously expanding its global footprint with around 2,500 colleagues in 11 countries, and more than 170 clients in 35 countries. We are an industry-leading provider of wealth management technology and services for financial institutions around the world, including private banks and wealth managers, investment managers, as well as retail and neo banks. Our research led approach and continual innovation is powered by the passion and creativity of our colleagues. We are always looking for talented people to join us on our mission to orchestrate the financial ecosystem and democratize access to wealth management. Avaloq offers the opportunity to work closely with some of the world’s leading financial institutions as we jointly develop and shape careers. Championing a collaborative, supportive and flexible work environment empowers our colleagues to reach their full potential.

We are looking for a passionate Cloud Network Architect to join our Cloud Architecture team. This role combines cloud network architecture with security engineering and automation, to help design and secure next-generation cloud platforms and services.

Your Key Tasks

  • Design, govern, and continuously evolve secure cloud architectures for SaaS, PaaS, and internal platforms
  • Define and maintain cloud network standards, reusable architecture patterns, reference architectures, and security blueprints
  • Embed security-by-design principles into landing zones, tenancy structures, network segmentation, identity models, and cloud guardrails
  • Drive the adoption of Zero Trust, least-privilege, and defense-in-depth security strategies across the cloud landscape
  • Integrate security controls and compliance requirements into CI/CD pipelines, Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC), and platform automation frameworks
  • Provide architectural guidance for network security and connectivity designs across internal, SaaS, and PaaS environments
  • Partner with the Security Architecture team to define enterprise network standards and reusable architecture patterns, ensuring secure, scalable, and compliant connectivity solutions across all platforms and environments
  • Ensure alignment with regulatory, compliance, and security requirements, translating them into practical and implementable architectural solutions
  • Evaluate emerging cloud network technologies and drive continuous improvements in the organization's security posture

Collaboration & Mentoring

  • Work closely with Cloud Architects, Platform Engineers, DevOps teams, Security Operations, and Product teams
  • Provide guidance and coaching on cloud adoption
  • Help drive cloud transformation and modernization initiatives
  • Collaborate with engineering teams to improve automation, reliability, and operational security

Qualifiche

  • Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Security, Information Technology, or a related field
  • Minimum 5 years of proven experience as an IT Cloud Architect, Cloud Network Architect, or in a similar senior security or architecture role
  • Experience designing and governing enterprise cloud architectures in Cloud Infrastructure (OCI preferred)
  • Experience with Cloud Security, managing complex architecture topologies and related topics
  • Hands-on experience with Infrastructure as Code and automation technologies such as Terraform/OpenTofu, CI/CD pipelines, GitOps, and Policy-as-Code

It would be a real bonus if you have

  • OCI Security Architect Professional
  • OCI certifications in Cloud Security and Network Architecture

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
