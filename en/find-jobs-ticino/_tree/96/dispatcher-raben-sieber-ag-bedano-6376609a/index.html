Dispatcher (m/f/d)

Raben AG is a modern, forward\-thinking logistics company specialising in international transport and freight forwarding solutions. As part of a strong European transport group, we ensure that goods reach their destinations reliably, efficiently and on time every day.

Our success is driven by dedicated employees who take ownership, work together and are passionate about shaping the future of logistics. At Raben AG, you can expect short decision\-making processes, a supportive team environment and a wide range of development opportunities within an international network.

To strengthen our team in Bedano, we are looking for a motivated and committed individual to join us as a:

Dispatcher 100% (m/f/d)

Your Responsibilities

  • Independently plan, coordinate and monitor our groupage transport routes, ensuring efficient and cost\-effective operations.
  • Prepare transport documents and coordinate customer notifications and delivery announcements.
  • Support and coordinate our drivers, acting as an important point of contact in day\-to\-day operations.
  • Handle order entry, invoicing and amendments while maintaining a clear overview of all operational activities.

Your Profile

You are highly organised, able to keep a cool head even in stressful situations and enjoy finding practical solutions to challenges.

Ideally, you bring:

  • Completed vocational training and experience in a similar position.
  • geographical knowledge and a genuine interest in the transport and logistics industry.
  • Willingness to work shifts.
  • A reliable, open\-minded and responsible attitude.A strong team spirit, fairness and excellent interpersonal skills.

  • Flexible working hours to support a healthy work\-life balance.
  • A high level of responsibility and the trust to make independent decisions.
  • Room for initiative and the opportunity to contribute your ideas and actively improve processes.
  • A motivated team where colleagues support one another and work together.
  • \-term career prospects and development opportunities within a growing company.
  • International collaboration within our European network.
  • A modern and dynamic working environment with short decision\-making processes and flat hierarchies.
  • A varied and interesting role where no two working days are the same.
  • Open communication and quick decision\-making, ensuring that good ideas can be put into action without unnecessary delays.
