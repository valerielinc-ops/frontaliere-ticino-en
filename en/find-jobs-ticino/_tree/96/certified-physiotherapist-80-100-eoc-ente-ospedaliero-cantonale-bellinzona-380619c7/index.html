Physiotherapist - Job Posting The EOC – Ente Ospedaliero Cantonale is a multisite hospital in Ticino with a total of 1,000 beds. Our organization effectively combines a local approach with an overall vision, providing the population with a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and competence of over 6,500 collaborators and their attention to human relations, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41,000 inpatients per year and providing over 600,000 outpatient consultations. To complete our physiotherapy and occupational therapy team at the EOC Rehabilitation Clinic, Faido site, we are looking for a Physiotherapist (80-100%).

  • Profile: The future physiotherapist is an action-oriented person who can establish effective and constructive relationships with internal and external clients. They have good listening skills and can carefully evaluate every situation before acting. They believe, like us, in the importance of putting the patient and their family at the center of all our attention and are committed to their personal development.

  • Necessary requirements:
  • Obtained a physiotherapist diploma (if foreign, recognized by CRS), preferably with training in a hospital setting in the orthopedic-traumatological, geriatric, respiratory, and neurological sectors.
  • Organizational and administrative skills with a strong predisposition to interdisciplinary collaboration, adaptability, and flexibility.
  • Excellent knowledge of the main computer applications and national languages. Entry date: May 1, 2026, to be agreed upon.
