Full description

As a firmly rooted company in the Seeland region, we offer comprehensive transport and storage solutions and ensure cleanliness in over 30 communities with our waste transports. Our success is based on motivated employees and modern infrastructure. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • You ensure the reliable operation of our automated, cube-based storage and order picking plant for small parts
  • You monitor the system, document important data and recognize early when action is required
  • With your technical know-how, you carry out maintenance and take care of the maintenance of our plants
  • In the event of disruptions, you are in demand: find causes, implement solutions and put the plant back into operation
  • Your skills are also required in the fleet – Support for repairs and maintenance work on vehicles up to 40 tonnes
  • Variety is part of this: help with warehouse conversions as well as various maintenance work around our infrastructure
  • You provide a well-kept environment with your commitment – from the outdoor facility to our indoor camps

Why do you succeed?

  • You have an education as a mechatronician or a comparable technical training
  • Enjoying versatile tasks where technical know-how, craftsmanship and practical handling are required
  • Desire for a varied everyday life with exciting challenges instead of a classic 08/15 job
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented working methods as well as a good feeling for technical connections round off your profile
  • Collegiality, sense of responsibility and joy in making a difference together in a team
  • Willingness to provide flexible assignments outside the usual working hours if necessary

Your benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Modern work equipment: We keep up with the times - you have modern and safe work equipment at your disposal
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
