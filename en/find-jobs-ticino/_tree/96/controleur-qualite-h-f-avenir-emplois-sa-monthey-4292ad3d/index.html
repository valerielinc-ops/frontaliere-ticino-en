Full description

Introduction

Avenir Emploi SA is a personnel placement company founded in Lausanne in 2002\. Since March 2008, our company has refocused its activities on the Vaudese and Valais Chablais as well as on the central Valais, with a representation in Aigle (VD) and the other in Sion (VS).

Our objective is to provide a high-quality service for the placement of fixed and temporary staff, particularly in the industrial and construction sectors. Our strength: a team of effective and professional advisors, listening to customers and candidates. Our specificity: the size and structure ofAvenir Jobs SA allows it to adapt quickly to market developments and to make quick decisions to best meet customer and candidate requirements.

We are currently looking for a H/F quality controller to join a dynamic team.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct quality controls on finished products and production processes.
  • Identify and report non-compliance and propose corrective actions.
  • Participate in continuous improvement of production processes.
  • Prepare monitoring reports and monitor quality indicators.
  • Work with production teams and other departments to ensure compliance with quality standards.
  • You will need to use control instruments such as micrometers, slide feet, gauges, buffers, binoculars, magnifying glass, etc.

Qualifications

You have been trained in quality control and/or first experience in watchmaking or metalworking.

  • Ability to read and interpret technical plans.
  • Spirit of analysis, rigour and sense of detail.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
  • Good knowledge of measuring devices.

Benefits

  • A stimulating and dynamic working environment.
  • Opportunities for professional development and continuing training.
  • Competitive pay and attractive working conditions.
  • A caring and listening team.
  • A company of human size, responsive and close to its employees.

Information to apply

If you are motivated to join a dynamic company and contribute to its success, we invite you to apply now. We look forward to discovering your application!
