Full description

The Clienia Group is one of the largest private providers of psychiatric services in Switzerland. With over 20 locations – including the private clinics Clienia Littenheid and Clienia Schlössli as well as the geriatric psychiatric offer of Clienia Bergheim – it offers a diverse spectrum of psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment methods in an outpatient, day clinic and inpatient setting. The group employs around 1,800 people and achieved sales of CHF 240 million in 2025.
