Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement. Tasks: • As part of a job sharing, you act as an administrative hub for the head of the Department of Surgery and support him in all administrative and organizational matters. • With commitment, you are responsible for the scheduling and together with the team ensure a smooth daily routine, with Monday and Wednesday being your fixed working days. • During the holiday absences of your job-sharing partner, you take over the deputy and are ready to temporarily increase your workload • The independent writing of correspondence in German and English, the organization of business trips as well as the planning and implementation of internal and external events are part of your versatile tasks and contribute to the success of the department. Requirements • A completed commercial training with experience as an assistant or a long-term job as chief medical secretary form the basis of your career • As a communicative and team-oriented personality, you enjoy working in an exciting hub function. • Flexibility, adaptability and the ability to react quickly to changes and prioritize your proactive and solution-oriented way of working • With very good IT knowledge (Microsoft Office 365), excellent German and English skills, the willingness to develop further and active commitment, you will contribute profitably to the team. We offer: • You can expect varied activities in a dynamic environment • We offer you the opportunity to participate in a large field of activity with a lot of personal responsibility and design possibilities. • You benefit from a large network and numerous benefits at the largest employer in Central Switzerland
