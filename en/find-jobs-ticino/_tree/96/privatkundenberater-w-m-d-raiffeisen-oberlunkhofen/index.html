Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Kelleramt-Albis, a successful and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Oberlunkhofen and headquarters in Affoltern am Albis, we are looking for a personality as a private customer consultant.

What do you expect?

  • Recognizing and exploiting the customer potential through active and continuous support of the personal customer portfolio
  • Ensure competent advice on financing issues (housing) as well as standardised advice on investing and provisioning
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the individual needs, wishes, goals and living conditions of the customers as well as within the segment-specific product range
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures such as contact campaigns, events and representative tasks

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking or similar training and completed financial training
  • In-depth expertise in investing, pensions and financing
  • Good communication skills, high social skills, teamwork skills and flexibility
  • Dynamic, entrepreneurial working method with a strong customer and sales orientation
  • Enjoying proactive customer contact
  • Solution-oriented negotiating skills with final strength

You love customer contact, are a team player and are looking for a varied job at a regionally anchored bank? I look forward to meeting you. Anna Huber Head of Private Clients Why Raiffeisen?
