You...

... are passionate about taxation and customer advice? ... look for an atmosphere of stimulating, motivating and collaborative work? ... would like to put your skills at the service of a diverse clientele composed of individuals, SMEs and groups of companies?

In order to strengthen the team of our office of Sion, would like to welcome an experienced tax expert.

Your Mission

  • Advise clients of individuals and businesses on complex tax issues.
  • Carry out tax planning and business restructuring.
  • Prepare and supervise tax returns of natural and legal persons.
  • Monitor taxation decisions and monitor complaints and appeals procedures.
  • Draft legal notices, tax studies and rulings.
  • Participate in customer development and retention.

Your profile

  • Higher education in taxation, economics or law (graduate tax expert, CSI tax expert, Master, Bachelor, or other).
  • Professional experience confirmed for several years in the Swiss tax law council.
  • Good control of Swiss taxation of natural and legal persons.
  • Writing experience.
  • French mother tongue
  • German and/or English are an asset.
  • Responsibility, autonomy, discretion, rigour and teamwork.

We offer

  • A dynamic working environment within a recognized trustee.
  • Various files with high added value.
  • A great autonomy in the management of mandates.
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development.
  • Attractive and flexible conditions of engagement.

Place of work

Sion, with occasional telework or activity in another group office.

To be agreed.
