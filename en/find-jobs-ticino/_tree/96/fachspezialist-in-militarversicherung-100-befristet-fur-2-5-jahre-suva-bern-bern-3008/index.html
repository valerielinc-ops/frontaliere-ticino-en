Full description

You work at Military Insurance, which provides insurance coverage for accidents and illnesses in military, civil protection and civil service. Suva manages the military insurance on behalf of the Federation. Do you value versatile tasks and like to contribute your insurance knowledge to different teams? Then you are right with us. What you do

  • You support the service and claims team of the military insurance in a cross-team jumper function and bring your expertise where it currently creates the greatest added value.
  • You process insurance cases with consequences of illness and accidents according to the Military Insurance Act (MVG) and check liability and benefit claims.
  • You check medical expense bills on the applicable rates and calculate and pay daily allowances.
  • You run your own cases in which you deal with insured persons, service providers, employers and insurance companies.
  • You work closely with insurance physicians: legal representatives, social insurance companies and other internal and external specialists. What you bring
  • You have a completed commercial, medical or paramedical training.
  • You bring your first professional experience in the benefit or case management of a health, accident or social insurance.
  • Ideally, you have knowledge of tariffs, such as TARDOC, and experience in handling insurance claims.
  • You communicate safely and appropriately with insured persons, service providers, employers and insurance companies.
  • You quickly work your way into new areas of expertise and keep track of changing tasks.
