Full description

Your surroundings

Her future workplace is the year-round clinical school for 28 pupils of the Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Clinic, which opened in Chur in spring 2026.

Their tasks

  • They teach in basic subjects (German, Mathematics, English) and other foreign languages and also design thematic teaching units
  • They are in close contact with young people’s home schools
  • They regularly exchange views with the treatment team

Our requirement profile

  • You have the teaching diploma (ideally upper school) as well as the degree in curative education
  • They are mostly used in upper school, individual assignments are also possible at primary level
  • You enjoy working in a motivated teaching team and are characterized by very good contact and communication skills
  • You are experienced in interdisciplinary cooperation

We offer you

  • An extraordinary and exciting workplace at the interface between school and child and adolescent psychiatry
  • Varied interdisciplinary cooperation in a professional team consisting of specialists from child and adolescent psychiatry, psychotherapy, specialist therapies, social education and care
  • A meaningful work, because you make an important contribution to the mental health of children and adolescents
  • Versatile internal training and team supervisions – we also support your external training needs
  • Advanced working conditions
  • With part-time work models, regular working hours and unpaid leave, an optimal work-life balance
