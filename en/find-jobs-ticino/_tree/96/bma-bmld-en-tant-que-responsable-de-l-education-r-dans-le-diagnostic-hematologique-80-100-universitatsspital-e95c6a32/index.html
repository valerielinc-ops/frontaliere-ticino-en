USZ Campus

By agreement

As a person responsible for education: r you actively shape the quality of training in haematological diagnostics and empower vocational trainers and students to develop their potential in the best possible way. In this way, you not only strengthen the professional development of individuals, but also make a sustainable contribution to the success of the entire laboratory team.

You can do that.

The function includes a workload of 30% as an educational manager:r. The other 50-70% you work in the hematology laboratory. Your responsibilities as an educational manager: r

  • You will design and coordinate the practical training of our students, combining education, expertise and everyday laboratory life
  • You participate in the further development of educational concepts and take responsibility for their coordination and implementation in hematological diagnostics
  • You train, accompany and coach the vocational trainers: women in the fields of hematology / hemostasis and immunohematology / transfusion medicine / stem cell laboratory in educational matters
  • You work closely with vocational trainers: inside, the department and the leadership
  • You ensure the training quality as well as compliance with the relevant requirements and educational standards
  • You work in the admission procedure HF together with the education managers of the other clinics and institutes

Your tasks in the field of hematology / hemostasis

  • You work actively in the hematology laboratory and bring your technical expertise into everyday laboratory life
  • Conducting a broad range of routine and special analyses
  • Morphological assessment of blood counts, punctures and bone marrow
  • Yield Collaboration in late, weekend and night service

You bring that with you

  • You are a biomedical analyst: in HF or biomedical:r laboratory diagnostician: in FH and have a well-founded educational training (FA trainer:in), respectively you are ready to complete this
  • You have experience as a vocational trainer: in, know the Swiss education system well and bring practical experience in hematology / hemostasis
  • It gives you pleasure to accompany students in their professional development and actively shape educational processes
  • You appreciate the expertise of the specialists and vocational trainers: inside and actively involve them in the design of the training
  • You realize that you are employed both as an education manager and as a member of the routine laboratory team and you can consciously combine both roles
  • You communicate clearly and bindingly, take a position and can justify decisions in a comprehensible way. Thanks to your good relationship and conflict ability, you understand how to accommodate, moderate and bring together different perspectives.
  • You have a high degree of self-motivation and innovative ability

This is what awaits you with us

Good education is a common task for us. As an education manager, you work closely with vocational trainers, specialists, the management and the higher education responsibility. For your function, we create clear framework conditions with a defined mandate, clarified roles and competencies as well as fixed contact persons in education, discipline and leadership. You can count on visible support, an open and direct communication culture and an environment in which different professional and educational perspectives are valued and jointly viable solutions are developed. In addition, you benefit from a state-of-the-art infrastructure with the latest technologies for research and diagnostics, flexible working hours with flexibility and only one weekend service per month. We give you the opportunity to work carefully into the function and develop it together with us. You like to connect people, expertise and education and would like to actively shape the training of future biomedical analysts and biomedical laboratory diagnosticians?
