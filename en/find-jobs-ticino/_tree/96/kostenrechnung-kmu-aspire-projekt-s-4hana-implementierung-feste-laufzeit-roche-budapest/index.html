The Position

This is a fix ed-term opportunity until the 31th of December, 2029

The Record to Report (R2R) Department is a global organization of over 650 experienced finance professionals working together to deliver financial services and solutions for Roche across the EMEA region. Our services cover financial management, period-end closing, capital and lease accounting, credit and receivables, manufacturing finance, and divisional reporting, ensuring compliance for 263 reporting entities in 96 countries with accounting standards (IFRS, GAAP) and providing assurance of financial integrity.

The Opportunity

As an ASPIRE SME you actively contribute to the successful implementation of the ASPIRE Global Templates together with SAP S4Hana in the companies supported by RSS. You closely work together with the Regional Deployment Team, IT teams and the RSS Operations colleagues impacted by the separate waves of the Program.

Onboarding

  • Collaborating with the Program, embracing its vision, reviewing documents, global templates, and tools, and participating in workshops for complex scenarios including data mapping.

Impact assessment

  • Engaging with R2R-MSC (Manufacturing Supply Chain) colleagues and actively contributing to Fit To Template workshops, articulating critical local business and reporting needs.

Data mapping

  • Contributing to master data scoping and migration including data enrichment and data migration dress rehearsal, including identification & collaboration with data owners

Testing

  • Executing or reviewing the execution of UAT and "Day in Life" simulation in collaboration with Operations colleagues

Training

  • Coordinating and deliver end-users training

Business Cutover

  • Participating in business readiness activities and coordinating continuity plans within Operations.

Post Go-live activities

  • Updating process documentation and providing post go-live support in collaboration with the Regional Deployment Team and IT.
