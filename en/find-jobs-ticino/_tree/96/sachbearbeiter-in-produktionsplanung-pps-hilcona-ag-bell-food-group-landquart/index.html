Full description

At the Zell site, the PPS team plans the demand for poultry slaughter animals and the production volumes for processing and packaging. We produce fresh, chilled, and frozen products. As a PPS clerk, you independently plan the required quantities in collaboration with sales and production. You monitor inventory levels and sales figures, continuously adjust the planning, create evaluations and analyses, and represent your team colleagues' processes during their absences. ## Responsibilities

  • Management of sales planning and quantity planning on a daily, weekly, and monthly basis
  • Creation of production plans considering capacities, availabilities, priorities, etc. in collaboration with departments
  • Timely creation of production orders for departments after clarifying production capacities
  • Ensuring the flow of goods and communication throughout the company
  • Reporting plan deviations and implementing agreed measures
  • Monitoring and recording actual key figures in the area
  • Reporting deviations and implementing agreed measures
  • Taking responsibility for fresh inventory in all warehouses, including coordination, determination of measures, and control of implementation in case of deviations
