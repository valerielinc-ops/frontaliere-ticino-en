Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. Located in Yverdon-les-Bains, the SBB Workshops are a historic site where specialized teams maintain and upgrade rolling stock. Looking forward, the site is gradually evolving to accommodate new facilities and offer workspaces adapted to the requirements of tomorrow, while continuing its activities. Joining the Workshops is integrating an industrial environment rich in know-how, in full development, where teams contribute every day to the mobility of tomorrow. To find out about social benefits, such as free AG and foreign travel at reduced prices (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits . What you can move.

  • As a logistician, you are responsible for receiving goods for the maintenance of railway vehicles.
  • You properly control and condition the various items following returns from production for stocking.
  • You are in charge of the quantitative and qualitative control of the incoming goods, which you then register in the system before placing in stock.
  • In daily contact with internal and external customers, you prepare orders and make inventories.
  • You load the vehicles for shipping.

What you bring.

  • Thanks to your team spirit, you integrate perfectly into a new environment. You enjoy being a reference person for your colleagues and clients. You also enjoy working independently on your daily tasks.
  • Your journey allows you to bring at least 2 years of experience to a similar position.
  • At the level of training, you benefit from a Logistician CFC.
  • You are entitled to a counterweight, electric and retractable mast forklift licence. The crane license is a plus.
  • You have a command of French and are able to communicate professionally and constructively with both your clients and your superiors.
  • You have a special affinity with the usual IT tools (MS Office). Ideally you have already worked with SAP.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
