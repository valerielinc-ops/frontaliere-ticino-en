Full description

Operational Risk Manager • Geneva, CH • Full-time • Unlimited Your Role As Operational Risk Manager at Bank J. Safra Sarasin, you are an experienced individual seeking to contribute to the Bank’s operational risk framework in alignment with FINMA and provide support to the consolidated supervision duty of the Group.

Your Responsibilities

  • Maintain the operational risk framework, including the following:
  • Review and challenge the Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA)
  • Collaborate with stakeholders across the three lines of defense
  • Perform test of controls
  • Contribute to relevant risk reviews

Your Profile

  • University degree in Finance or Economics
  • 5 years of practical experience in the field of Audit or Operational Risk in a bank in Switzerland required
  • Experience with implementation of regulatory framework and operational risks
  • Ability to weight problems and set priorities, excellent organizational skills
  • Excellent Microsoft Office skills, knowledge in Avaloq is an advantage
  • Ability to work with various stakeholders
  • Excellent interpersonal, communication and presentation skills
  • Capacity to work independently
  • Fluency in English, very good command of French, fluency in German is an advantage

Your Benefits • Entrepreneurial spirit in a family owned bank • Central city office locations across Switzerland • Above average insurance coverage fully borne by the bank • Contribution to health insurance and meal allowance • Global growth strategy and stable environment • Competitive offering About us As a leading sustainable private bank, we offer dynamic and personalized services in investment advisory and asset management for private and institutional clients.
