Intern Activation Event (M/F)

GENERAL MISSION

Within the Horloger Hermès trade and attached to the Communication Pole, you provide operational support to the event team in organizing the highlights. You intervene in particular on the management of hospitality, event logistics, relations with suppliers and partners, as well as on the organization of internal events and with our subsidiaries.

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES AND ACTIVITIES

  1. Watches & Wonders:
  • Management of reservations related to hospitality for all markets and headquarters (hotels, restaurants, transport...)
  • Event logistics management (delivery of equipment, flow coordination)
  • Operational support in connection with hospitality (F&B, hosts, requests for contracts, accreditations, gift management)
  • Support for post event rebilling
  1. Supplier & Partner Relations:
  • Order management from our various suppliers
  • Coordination with the Fondation de la Haute Horlogerie
  • Benchmarking and recommendations for improving our events
  1. Event Support:
  • Participation in the organization of our events with subsidiaries (logistics, hospitality, travel of craftsmen, etc.)
  • Support daily hospitality operations, anticipating the needs of guests and teams (visits, seminars, etc.)
  • Gift management
  • Participation in inventory management and inventories
  • Writing reports of our events

PROFILE

  • Student or graduate of a Bachelor's degree or equivalent, with a specialization in hospitality, communication or marketing;
  • First successful event experience, ideally within the luxury sector;
  • Appetence for the world of luxury or watchmaking;
  • Rigor, sense of detail, proactivity and excellent sense of organization;
  • Team spirit, ability to work with various interlocutors, in a multi-project context;
  • Excellent knowledge of computer tools and special ease with Excel and Powerpoint;
  • Common French and English.
