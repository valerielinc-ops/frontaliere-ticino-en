Full description

The employer is looking for one

Psychologist/worker (marked)

Mansions

  • conduct evaluation, guidance and professional accompaniment talks.
  • analyze the functioning profile of the person in relation to the demands of the role and the working context.
  • assess skills, resources, interests, motivation, self-efficiency, adaptation strategies and factors that can foster or hinder professional integration.
  • define, with the person and with the network, individualized objectives of development and reintegration.
  • assess the suitability of professional contexts consistent with the profile, skills and health conditions of the person.
  • monitoring and supervision of internship paths, professional assessments of work capacity, work training and courses of employment research techniques.
  • to accompany companies in the definition of tasks, framework conditions, adaptations and appropriate supervision.
  • observing and evaluating work performance directly in business contexts, drafting conclusions on intermediate and final reports at the request of institutional bodies.
  • support the maintenance of the workplace and intervene early in the presence of criticality.
  • collaborate with the interdisciplinary team of the SIL and participate in the methodological and qualitative development of the service.

Requirements

  • is a preferential title of the cantonal qualification of clinical authorization for free exercise.
  • experience in the evaluation of work performance, work capacity, motivational interventions with individuals and companies, professional orientation, selection, professional coaching, case management or work integration.
  • knowledge of the Ticino labour market and the main organizational processes and human resource management.
  • experience in collaboration with companies and employers.
  • the knowledge of invalidity insurance, measures for occupational reintegration and work with persons who have physical or mental health problems.

Key skills

  • ability to carry out analysis of the workplace or training, evaluating the compatibility between person, task and organization.
  • manage complex conversations and situations characterized by ambivalence, reduced motivation, conflict or high emotional or relational load.
  • work on the network and coordinate actors with different perspectives and mandates.
  • safely use current IT tools (MS Office, Word, Excel, Power Point and OneDrive)
  • excellent mastery of the Italian language, oral and written.
  • licence to conduct cat. B
  • availability to travel on the cantonal territory.
  • ability to listen and build the interpersonal alliance.
  • orientation to the resources and evolutionary possibilities of the team.
  • analytical thinking, ability to judge, concreteness and orientation to solutions.
  • organizational autonomy, ability to define priorities and flexibility in managing complex situations.
  • balance between support for the person and realistic comparison with the demands of the labour market.
  • negotiating skills and professional communication with companies and institutions.
  • reliability, precision, respect for confidentiality and attitude to interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • reflexive capacity and availability to continuous supervision and training.

Knowledge considered an advantage

  • clinical knowledge of psychiatric disorders and their potential impact on work functionality.
  • ability to translate clinical and psychosocial information into concrete indications the path of reintegration of the staff, respecting the roles and skills of the different figures involved.
  • knowledge of other national languages and/or English.

Details of use

  • Workplace Balerna
  • occupation from 50 - 100%
  • use after agreement, indefinitely
  • wages to be agreed

We offer

  • a specialized activity with a concrete impact on the professional paths and social participation of people.
  • an interdisciplinary context in which to integrate job psychology, clinical psychology, human resource management, skills development, reintegration coaching.
  • autonomy in case management, accompanied by professional comparison and teamwork.
  • the possibility of contributing to the development of innovative tools, procedures and projects.
  • supervisory, intervision and continuing training opportunities.
  • contractual conditions tailored to function and professional experience.

Selection procedure

Application

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
