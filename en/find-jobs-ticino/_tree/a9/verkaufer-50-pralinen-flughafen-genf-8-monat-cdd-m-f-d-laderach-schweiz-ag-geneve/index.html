Full description

High quality fresh chocolate - This is what the Swiss chocolate factory Läderach represents. Founded in 1962, the family business now employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries throughout the group, and the trend is on the rise. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glaris. Here, in the picturesque setting of the Alps, we produce our fresh chocolates and constantly develop new and innovative ideas within agile teams. The company's retail sector comprises more than 250 chocolate shops in more than 30 countries. This is where we offer our chocolate specialties and invite our customers to rediscover fresh chocolate constantly. You want to enjoy it too? Then join us. What awaits you
