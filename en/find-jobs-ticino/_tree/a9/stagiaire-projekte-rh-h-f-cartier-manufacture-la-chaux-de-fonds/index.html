Full description

The story of Cartier is founded on curiosity and passion. For more than 170 years we have embraced a bold, pioneering spirit that continues to inspire our teams across all Métiers from our boutiques to our workshops and corporate offices. Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striving to continuously enrich our Maison’s heritage by pushing the boundaries of creativity and innovation. Within the Etudes et Méthodes Traditionnelles department, you participate in the development of new horological products, modeling and creating the plans of new products and/or tools, integrating the technical constraints according to the defined project. HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

  • Establish production plans from the technical dossier
  • Create the marking phase plans
  • Define and build the cutting tool plans necessary
  • Define and build the component phase plans in collaboration with MP-programmers and MQ-methods quality technicians (trinity)
  • Ensure the consistency of the industrialization dossier
  • Build the tools
  • Collaborate with the concerned departments (quality, production, mechanics, etc.) to gather the necessary information
  • Analyze the needs and build the tools
  • Build the production dossier and coordinate its provisioning
  • Ensure the proper functioning of the tools for production
  • Rationalize and standardize the tools in relation to mechanics and production (quality, costs, and deadlines)
  • Manage the production plans in the database
  • Bring the modifications and make the updates to the plans in the system
  • Respect the life cycles in the PLM
  • Participate in the product and process improvement
  • Participate in the continuous improvement processes
  • Accompany the improvements in production, the optimization of products and processes WHAT ARE THE KEYS TO YOUR SUCCESS IN OUR MAISON? To the benefit of a CFC designer or equivalent, you justify a first experience in the industrial sector. You master the techniques of drawing, also possess knowledge of CAD software and are at ease with IT environments. Of a methodical nature, you are endowed with an autonomous, rigorous personality and have an interest in teamwork. WHAT WILL WE OFFER YOU? We create a positive environment where we are proud to employ talents from different backgrounds, experiences, and identities. We are convinced that when diversity and inclusion are fully accepted and reinforced, creativity and knowledge emerge to achieve excellence. YOUR JOB PROFILE WITH US: If your candidacy corresponds to what we are looking for, you will have a first telephone contact with our HR team. Then, you will access an individual interview with our Industrialization Technical Responsible and our HR Business Partner.
