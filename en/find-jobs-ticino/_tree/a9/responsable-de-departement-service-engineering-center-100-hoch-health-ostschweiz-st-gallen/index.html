Head of Service Engineering Center 100%

Technology Management

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Head of the Service Engineering Center department with five group leaders and around 50 employees
  • Ensure operational maintenance of medical and building facilities and systems, taking into account existing regulations and resources
  • Ensure and develop standards, processes and methods in close cooperation with system engineering and project management
  • Active participation in corporate and divisional projects with a focus on the representation of operational interests
  • Ensure high service quality as well as customer- and solution-oriented service provision

What you bring for this job

  • Several years of management and service experience in the technical area, beneficial in the hospital landscape or in a comparable organization
  • University degree (FH/university) in electrical engineering, system engineering, building technology or comparable discipline
  • Experience in the planning, coordination and implementation of commissioning as well as maintenance measures of plants and networked systems
  • Integrative and assertive leader with high social skills as well as strong communication and motivation skills
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation with our partners and customers

Our offer to you

  • Engaged collaboration in a highly specialized and interdisciplinary environment
  • Own area of responsibility in which you can develop your potential through independent, committed and flexible work
  • An appreciative cooperation and an open cooperation at eye level, in the team as well as at management level
  • Structured training so that you can start well accompanied and safe in the new role
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities to promote your individual strengths
