Company description

As part of temporary missions during the winter season, we offer opportunities to our customers, including hotel establishments of all categories in the different stations in the region. These missions are mainly in the mountains, in the canton of Valais. In this context, we are actively seeking:

Women / Room Valets on Call M/F

Description of post

You will be responsible for cleaning and range hotel rooms after use of guests or for daily service. You will take care of change sheets, redo beds, vacuum, dust, clean the bathroom as well as public places and commons. You will control and restock minibars, linens, towels and bathrobes. You also know how to cover in 4/5 star hotels.

Profile sought

You benefit from a experience confirmed as a woman or valet, ideally acquired in luxury hotels 4\/ 5\. You are a dynamic person, flexible and conscientious, with a real attention to detail and quality.

Having your own vehicle is a real asset to facilitate your travel to different mission locations. Please note that no accommodation is provided for these missions, so you must have your own accommodation solution.
