Firefighters - Locomotive engineer 100% intervention - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Firefighters - Locomotive engineer Intervention Biel/Bienne, 01.02.2027 or suitable, 100% Apply here

What awaits you.

At 15 locations in Switzerland, the SBB - Intervention firefighters, with their 350 employees and modern response vehicles, provide rapid and safe incident management. We are both railway specialists, locomotive drivers and firefighters, we ensure the safety of our customers 24 hours a day. Thanks to your cooperation, you are helping to ensure that rail traffic resumes as quickly as possible. As part of incident management, SBB firefighters - Intervention support public emergency organisations. You consider irregular schedules and teamwork as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. At home, working at night and on weekends entitles us to additional remuneration, for example CHF 16.– an hour for Sunday work. Time increases of 10 to 30% are granted for night work, which gives you more free time. What you can move.

  • In case of alarms and exercises, you intervene as a locomotive engineer with the extinguishing and rescue train with your team and ensure the safety of our customers.
  • In the event of an incident affecting rail traffic, you assume joint responsibility for the protection of persons, animals, the environment and railway infrastructure.
  • On-site, you ensure the safety of all travellers, fight fires on and around the tracks, provide oil and chemical defence and technical assistance. The use of drones makes it possible to quickly assess the situation.
  • You regularly train in different working processes and techniques to ensure that events are managed without any problems. In this context, you work by team, 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday, and you provide the picket service on weekends. Irregular working hours are the norm. They have the advantage of allowing you to practice winter sports during the week when the tracks are clear and to organize your free time flexibly.

What you bring.

  • Thanks to your keen sense of responsibility and security, you understand the interdependencies and consequences on your team and on your work. For us, the team spirit and the community on which you can rely at all times is paramount. This implies that you enjoy working independently and that you are physically and mentally resistant.
  • You completed an apprenticeship with the Federal Certificate of Capacity (FCC). You already have a B100 motor vehicle licence or are willing to take the corresponding paid in-house training.
  • Holder of a category B driving licence and, ideally, category C1 118.
  • You have respiratory protection skills and experience as a firefighter.
  • You can communicate in French (oral as well as written) and are ready to graduate in German at the A1+ level.
  • As part of the application process, we will ask you for a current extract from your criminal record and the prosecution and bankruptcy register (less than three months old). This document may be ordered at the time of a personal interview.
