Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic located in Crans-Montana, in the idyllic setting of the Valais Alps, is looking for one/one chief doctor.fe.e assistant in neurological rehabilitation 100%

Your activities

  • Manage the clinical activities of his unit, in direct collaboration with the physician-head of department
  • Develop interdisciplinary collaboration with medical, therapeutic, administrative and logistical teams
  • Establish an outpatient consultation related to its specialization
  • Supervising post-graduate teaching and training of assistant doctors
  • Participate in the 2nd level picket service (nights, weekends and public holidays)

Your profile

  • Swiss doctor diploma or equivalent (Mebeko recognition)
  • Holder of an FMH (or equivalent) title in physical medicine and rehabilitation (European Bar) or neurology
  • Rehabilitation hospital experience of at least five years, including several years as head of clinic
  • Ability to exercise autonomously and interest in coaching and training physician assistants
  • French mastery at the minimum B2 level for non-French speakers (certification required)
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German or willingness to acquire the necessary skills

We offer

  • A professional activity in an establishment recognised for the quality of care provided, within a motivated, committed and dynamic team
  • Very attractive conditions for engagement
  • Interdisciplinary work within the entire rehabilitation team
  • Opportunities for internal and external training
  • Accommodation and on-site dining opportunities

You're interested in this job? For more information, call: Dr. med. Maria Dan, Physician in Neurological Rehabilitation, tel. +41 (0)27 485 51 21 or Prof Philippe Eckert, Director, Tel. +41 (0)27 485 50 39
