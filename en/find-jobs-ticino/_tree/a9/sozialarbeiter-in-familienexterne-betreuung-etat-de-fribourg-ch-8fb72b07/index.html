Appointing authority / department / institution

The Youth Welfare Office (JA) is committed to the children and young people of the canton of Friborg. It is the cantonal specialist office for the implementation of child and youth policy and youth protection.

Become part of our team!

The JA's non-family care sector (SMA) consists of a small, dedicated team committed to the quality of childcare. The SMA is the responsible authority for the clarification, approval, supervision and support of care outside the family. In this varied and meaningful area of ​​activity, you can contribute and further develop your social and professional skills.

This position is temporary until December 31, 2027.

The state employer promotes the compatibility of work and private life and offers its employees a modern working environment and attractive working conditions. You can find out more about this on the following page.

Tasks

  • Clarification, approval and elimination of childminders as part of the implementation of the foster child regulation
  • Support and advice on care settings

Requirements

  • Master or Bachelor in social work or training recognized as equivalent
  • Interest in the areas of child protection and social education
  • Ability to analyze complex situations and ability to see what is important
  • Sense of organization, resilience, ability to work in a team
  • Driver's license is required
  • Proficiency in the German language (oral/written) with a good knowledge of the French language
