Full description

Materials science and technology are our passion. With our cutting-edge research, Empa’s approximately 1,100 employees make decisive contributions to the benefit of society for a future worth living in. Empa is a research institution of the ETH Domain.

Engineer in Acoustics (a)

For our acoustics / noise reduction department, we are looking for reinforcement in the environmental acoustics group.

Their tasks

  • Collaboration in research and development projects in environmental acoustics for the measurement and modeling of road traffic noise and the characterization of sound sources and acoustic material properties.
  • Provision of sophisticated technical acoustics services with field measurements, laboratory experiments and data analysis
  • Representation of Empa in the field of expertise vis-à-vis external bodies such as federal offices and specialist groups
  • Maintenance of knowledge transfer within the department and Empa
  • Presentation of technical-scientific results through publications and lectures

Your profile

  • Recently completed scientific and technical university studies
  • Technical flair
  • Independent initiative Personality with rapid comprehension and thorough working methods
  • In-depth knowledge of metrology, signal processing, programming (Matlab, Python), data analysis and the development of computer-aided forecasting models
  • Knowledge in different fields of acoustics is an advantage
  • Experience in written reporting and presentations
  • Very good oral and written knowledge in German and English, ideally basic knowledge in French
