Full description

The Hasliberg Rehabilitation Clinic is a renowned Swiss clinic in the field of psychosomatic, internist-oncological and musculoskeletal rehabilitation. The clinic has 83 beds and is on the hospital lists of the cantons of Bern, Lucerne, Nidwalden and Uri. The ISO-certified company works in a process-oriented and interdisciplinary manner, based on the mission statement of "rehab competence" with the characteristics: respect, efficiency, cordiality, attention and competence.
