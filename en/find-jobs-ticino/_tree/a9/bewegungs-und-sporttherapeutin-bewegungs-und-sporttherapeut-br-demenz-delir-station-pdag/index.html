Over the area

As part of the Clinic for Consiliary, Aging and Neuropsychiatry (Clinical Director Dr. med.) Rafael Meyer fulfills the Center for Age Psychiatry (directed by Kristine Ewert, Dr. med.) Andrea Wald and Susanne Spring) with his offers a valuable task for the mental health of older people and their relatives. It has four specialized stations and two ambulatories with memory clinics at the Aarau and Windisch locations. In addition to consultation hours and counselling services for relatives, the outpatient area includes the old-age psychiatric consultancy and liaison service for nursing homes. The centre is SIWF-approved training centre for psychiatry and psychotherapy, old-age psychiatry, consiliar and liaison psychiatry and general internal medicine.
