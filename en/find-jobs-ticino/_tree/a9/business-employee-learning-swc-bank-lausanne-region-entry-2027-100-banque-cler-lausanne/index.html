Commercial Employee Apprenticeship VET Bank – Lausanne Region (Start 2027) 100%

Do you want more than just an office job (i.e. a commercial employee VET apprenticeship in the Lausanne region)?

We are Bank Cler. A Swiss bank that has been calling things as they are for nearly 100 years. In fact, in Romansh, "cler" means clear, simple, and understandable. We operate based on these principles. That is why we bring financial clarity – regarding savings, payments, investments, pension provision, and financing. We provide advice, whether digitally with our Zak app or on-site in our 23 branches, and ensure that our apprentices are involved from the very beginning. You assume responsibility, you are close to our clientele, and you help make financial matters more understandable. Want to make things happen? If you know how to think globally and assume responsibility, you are a match for us. Let's move forward together!

Your apprenticeship

During your three-year training, you will have numerous opportunities, you will be right at the heart of the action rather than just a spectator, you will gain valuable experience through six varied assignments, and you will fully immerse yourself in the banking universe, for example:

  • Personal customer service at the counter or by phone
  • Account openings, payments, and payment transactions
  • In-depth advice on credits, investments, and banking products for private and corporate clients
  • Exciting projects and insights into digital banking solutions

And because you push yourself, other great things await you!

  • Exciting opportunities to advance: running a branch in the future? It might sound unusual, but by committing fully, you can evolve within the BKB Group and discover new and fascinating fields. Check out the portraits of our employees here.
  • Events that forge connections: meet other apprentices, colleagues, and staff members at numerous events throughout the BKB Group.
  • Benefits for your wallet: for example, through the Coop discount card, sports offers, and other benefits reserved for employees.
  • Generous support for everything you need for your training: from the subsidy for your laptop to the language stay at vocational school and the SBB general abonnement.

Your learning locations

During your apprenticeship period, you are not alone! At the learning locations, you will meet many other trainees, coaches, and colleagues with whom you can exchange ideas, network, and learn.

📍 Our branches in Western Switzerland (Lausanne, Vevey, Geneva) Your training place with us, where you are supported and accompanied by experienced practical trainers.

📍 EPCL Vocational School (possible with additional professional baccalaureate) You attend vocational school up to twice a week and have the opportunity to complete the professional baccalaureate if your academic results correspond.

📍 CYP (Challenge Your Potential) This is where you will attend inter-company courses and deepen your banking knowledge using modern learning methods.

📍 Internal sales training and specialized courses We offer you various training sessions during which you prepare for consulting, customer communication, and the sale of banking services.

Required profile

  • You attend a secondary school (VP or VG2 tracks) or a comparable school in the Canton of Vaud with good to very good academic results in the following subjects: German, mathematics, English, and French.
  • You have a keen interest in economics and the banking sector.
  • You express yourself fluently in German and voice your opinions constructively.
  • You are reliable, independent, and organized in your work, and you are ready to acquire new knowledge.
  • You enjoy actively meeting people from diverse backgrounds and making contacts openly.
  • You are interested in digital trends and enjoy trying out new technologies such as artificial intelligence.

Does this sound like you?

If so, apply promptly for your apprenticeship starting in August 2027 and submit the following documents:

  • CV
  • Report cards from the last two school years

And no cover letter is needed. Instead, answer three brief questions in the tool – we prefer authentic answers over a perfect formulation.

P.S.: Selected candidates will then be invited to a recruitment interview followed by a discovery day in Lausanne.
