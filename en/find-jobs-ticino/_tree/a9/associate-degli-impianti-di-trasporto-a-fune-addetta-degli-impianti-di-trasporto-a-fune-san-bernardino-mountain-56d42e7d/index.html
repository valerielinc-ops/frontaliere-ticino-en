Full description

For our ski resort in San Bernardino, we are looking for dynamic and motivated figures to be included in the organic ski lifts for the winter season.

Open positions

  • Engineers: operator of controls, control of motors and mechanical-electronic lift systems, as well as the execution of ordinary maintenance recorded on official operating registers;
  • Station/Pedana Agents: passenger direct assistance staff during boarding and landing (seggiovie/skilift) and visual monitoring of the lines, ensuring the application of safety and behaviour rules.

Technical Requirements and Certifications

  • for Engineers and Agents: basic technical training (mechanical, electrical, or Swiss vocational training CFP/APF in cable transport) is a preferential title;
  • Medical and Physics suitability: perfect psychophysical fitness (UFT requirement for safety-relevant tasks), long-term outdoor work resistance under alpine climate conditions and emergency management;
  • Flexibility and LDL Regulation: availability at work on weekends and holidays. Service shifts and rest periods will be structured in full compliance with the LDL in public transport;
  • Motion skills: good ability to ski or move in snowboard to carry out line controls and operational movements safely on the slopes;
  • Activities and Languages: strong sense of responsibility, absolute reliability and guest orientation. The excellent knowledge of the Italian language is required, the knowledge of German and English is a plus.

What we offer

  • seasonal contract of work in accordance with the legal and economic provisions of the Swiss cable industry;
  • continuous training, specific education on our plants and regulatory updates according to UFT standards;
  • integration into a solid and expanding business context.

Working period (indicative): 20.11.2026 - 15.04.2027
