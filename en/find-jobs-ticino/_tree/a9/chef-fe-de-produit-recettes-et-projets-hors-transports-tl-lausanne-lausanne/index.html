Full description

As a reference player in public transport, each customer is transported by making the heart of the Lausanne region beat by a commitment to simple, innovative and sustainable mobility.

To strengthen our teams, and also contribute to this experience, join us.

Become our future

Head of product, revenue and projects excluding transport

Your Mission

At the heart of the development of Tl's complementary revenues, you identify, develop and value new business opportunities in order to sustainably strengthen off-transport revenues and support the company's ambitions.

You ensure the complete management of the life cycle of products and services generating off-transport revenues, from design to making them available on the market. As such, you manage the portfolio of products and activities that generate additional revenues, including advertising concessions, commercial partnerships, deported sales and information devices, temporary trade space occupations, and the management of commercial contracts and associated services.

In collaboration with internal and external stakeholders, you will analyse market needs, design suitable offers, set revenue targets and guide their implementation in accordance with deadlines and targets. You contribute to the development of turnover, set tariffs, monitor the performance of products and actions developed using relevant indicators. You are involved in promoting the products and activities implemented, analyzing the results achieved and making the necessary recommendations to improve their effectiveness.

You also ensure the prospecting and monitoring of clients and business partners, the piloting of cross-cutting projects and the monitoring of budgets and contracts.

You also ensure the proper distribution of revenues according to internal rules. Finally, you plan, organize and participate in the different B2B tl events.

This position requires a perfect mastery of French, both in writing and oral, as well as a good ease in using the usual computer tools. Knowledge of German and English is an asset.

Your profile

  • You have a higher education (patent, bachelor's degree, master's degree, etc.) with specialization in marketing, or a training deemed equivalent.
  • You justify from 3 to 5 years of marketing or sales experience in a public service or transport company, as well as experience in B2B services.
  • Experience in managing projects and business partners is an important asset.
  • At ease with numbers and data, you have strong analytical skills, and performance monitoring.
  • Your analytical capacity, organizational sense and outcome orientation allow you to design relevant offers, make operational decisions and meet deadlines.
  • You're comfortable with running transversal projects.
  • With an excellent sense of service and a strong relational ease, you can argue your proposals, unite cross-cutting teams and develop lasting relationships with customers and partners.
