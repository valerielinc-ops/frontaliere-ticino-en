Full description

What you do

Want more than routine? With us, you can expect a diverse environment with different specialist areas, varied procedures and various anesthesia procedures. You work independently, take responsibility and bring your expertise where it counts.

  • You accompany patients safely through the entire perioperative phase and create trust with your calm, attentive nature.
  • General anesthesias are carried out independently; for regional anesthesias, you take over the competent supervision.
  • You recognize changes at an early stage and also act quickly, safely and focused in critical situations.
  • You bring your expertise to the accompaniment of students and pass it on in a practical manner.
  • With your open and appreciative communication, you strengthen cooperation in the team and help shape the further development of our anesthesia care.

What distinguishes you

  • You are Dipl. Expert/Dipl. Expert anesthesia care NDS HF and ideally bring experience in pediatric anesthesia – or want to develop in this area.
  • Variety is up to you: different areas of expertise and multiple challenges motivate you.
  • Responsibility, independence and a reliable way of working distinguish you.
  • Even if it becomes demanding, you keep track and make safe decisions.
  • You like to develop professionally and personally, appreciate the interprofessional exchange and contribute to a positive team culture.
  • "Round-the-clock assignments" (night, weekend, holidays) are part of it for you to guarantee seamless and high-quality patient safety.
