Full description

RUAG Real Estate AG is a real estate competence centre and a subsidiary of RUAG MRO Holding AG. We plan and implement tailor-made room, construction and service solutions within our own real estate portfolio at central locations in Switzerland. Our focus is on the duality of investment and operational properties, especially for special properties in the areas of aviation, armaments technologies and highly secure infrastructures. In addition, our room solutions and operator services create optimal conditions for the business success of our customers. With a holistic approach along the entire real estate lifecycle, digital solutions and long-term customer relationships, we stand for the highest level of professionalism and sustainable value creation.

You can move that

  • Personnel and technical management, development and administrative support of the TGM team of around 10 employees, including operational planning and coordination of the Pikettdienst
  • Responsibility for the maintenance and operation of energy and media systems (electricity, heat, compressed air, air conditioning, ventilation, MSRL technology) on the entire site
  • Planning, coordination and acceptance of all building installations for new construction, conversion, extension and renovation including legal controls
  • Project management and controlling in technical building management (HLKSE) as well as support for external planners, architects and installers
  • Responsibility as an electrical network operator (medium and low voltage) incl. Circuitry operations, inspection and servicing services of high and low current installations
  • Energy management (electricity, heat, water, waste water, compressed air) and polluter pays billing to tenants / billers
  • Ensuring compliance with all relevant policies, regulations and internal standards (IMS, safety and environmental requirements)

You bring that with you

  • Completed training in the field of electrical engineering, building technology, facility management or a related discipline within HLKSE as well as ideally further training as a federal dipl. Electrical installation and safety experts
  • Several years of practical experience in technical building management including previous management function with personnel responsibility
  • Sound know-how in the planning, operation and maintenance of electrical systems, HVAC systems as well as media and MSRL technology, including VDI and GEFMA knowledge
  • Experience in project management and controlling of construction and conversion measures as well as in cooperation with external service providers and authorities
  • Safe handling of legal requirements (network operation, installation permits (NIV), tests) and experience in energy management
  • Strong leadership and communication skills, assertiveness and team and customer orientation
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing; MS Office and common FM software knowledge are advantageous
  • Structured, independent and proactive way of working with a high service mentality, logical thinking and willingness to perform Picettdienst

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
