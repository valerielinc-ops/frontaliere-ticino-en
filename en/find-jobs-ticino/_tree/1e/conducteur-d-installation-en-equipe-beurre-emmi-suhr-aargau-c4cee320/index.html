Full description

Butter is not just a product for us - it is teamwork, technology and passion. In our team Butter, you ensure that our high-quality products are produced in the best quality every day. With your eye for detail and your willingness to take responsibility, you contribute significantly to the success of our production.

You can do that.

  • You and your team guarantee a smooth operation on the line and observe all safety guidelines and quality requirements in the production processes.
  • It is fun to bring in new ideas and suggestions for continuous improvement and thus to continuously optimize the processes.
  • For the production of the products in the best quality, compliance with the given recipes, procedures and working regulations is very important and you are aware of this.
  • Your efficient way of working and the presence on the lines is important and so you support your team.

You bring that with you

  • You have a basic training EFZ in the food sector (cook, butcher or in food or milk technology) and have perhaps already gained experience in industry.
  • Working with various operating systems (MES, SAP, Office) is no problem for you - and what is not, can still be.
  • You are a communicative, reliable person and have knowledge of German in words and writing.
  • You show your flexibility in the readiness for 4 shift incl. Weekends.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
