Full description

Pfister Curtain Service is a market leader in the field of solutions around the window. With more than 120 dedicated employees, we give our customers new feelings of living. For the start of the apprenticeship in 2027, we are looking for one for our headquarters in Thörishaus. 3-year education Your tasks

  • During your 3-year apprenticeship, you will learn how to make textile window decorations, raffle blinds, pillows and other home accessories
  • The trained specialists will provide you with their knowledge in ready-made assembly consulting and sales
  • In addition, you are involved in the entire production process from cutting the fabrics, through the production of the decoration to ironing and packaging.

You bring that with you

  • You attend Real or Secondary School and write good grades
  • In addition, you are an open and winning person who enjoys working in a team
  • You use your flair for shapes and colors with passion to sew atmospheric decorations
  • Your already existing craftsmanship serves as a basis on which you can build the skills of a living textile designer
