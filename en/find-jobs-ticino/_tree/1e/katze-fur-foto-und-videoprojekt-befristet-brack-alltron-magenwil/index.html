Full description

Tasks

  • You take entrepreneurial responsibility for your appearance and know how to keep quiet. Also over a whole minute. Or five.
  • You let yourself be convinced that leisure is not a flaw, but a valued expertise.
  • You ensure consistently high quality in the areas of eye contact, sitting position, tail posture and general grandeur.
  • If necessary, you make self-initiative acoustic statements in the form of meows, purrs or meaningful silence.
  • You work independently and reliably – or at least convincingly convey the impression that everything was planned exactly as planned.
  • A humble and cat-fearing camera team is always at your side.
  • There is no classic salary – but a Brack voucher worth 1,000 francs for your biped.

Requirements

  • You have completed basic training in the kitten field
  • Cross-enterers with a distinct natural talent are also welcome.
  • Further training in the field of two-legged management, posing and catwalk is advantageous.
  • Thou art a sure meow in word and writing.
  • You have over 2-3 years of professional experience in dealing with bipeds.
  • Cameras and cameramen are not scratch trees for you. Please.
  • You can sit still and keep the equipment at least 70% safe. Or 60% ... by 60% we are already happy.
  • You use your chocolate side immaculately – in case of doubt also the other.
  • Even with spontaneous strokes and snack breaks you keep the peace.

Important for your application Only an animal cat is sought, applications from bipeds are not sighted. Keepers can fill out the application form from the point of view of their cat. Only a small profile to the cat and a picture are required, an application dossier is not necessary. With the application, the owners agree that the pictures and videos submitted by them together with the name of the cat are excluded from the data protection declaration and may be further used and published. By participating in the casting, the owner of the winning cat additionally agrees that the photo and video recordings of the cat taken during the shooting may also be used and published.

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Super-compulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business appointments.

"Together we shape the trade of tomorrow" Brack.Alltron employs around 1300 employees and generates over one billion turnover. The company exists because a person had the courage to find new ways and go them. Our success story shows that through each individual, something great is created together and we love our uniqueness. We set ourselves ambitious goals, but it is very important to us that we act responsibly, promote and challenge each other and enjoy what we do every day.
