Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here. The Department of Medicine (DMED) provides comprehensive and specialized care for patients in hospitals and outpatients. Driven by a holistic, human approach based on best medical practices, it offers reference care in many specialties (cardiology, gastroenterology, pneumology, angiology and nephrology), with 24/7 availability. Engaged in clinical excellence, teaching and research, DMED promotes innovation, continuous skill development and patient-centred care, while ensuring the quality of life at work and the efficiency of benefits. Within this department, the Department of Gastroenterology and Hepatology takes care of patients and patients suffering from diseases of the digestive tract, the liver, the biliary tract and the pancreas, taking into account the services provided by the other services of the HUG. It provides the endoscopic, percutaneous and transhepatic interventional services of the above-mentioned organs. The Department of General Internal Medicine (SMIG) provides diagnostic and therapeutic support to adult patients and patients with a problem or assignment related to internal medicine, in collaboration with other units of the HUG, in particular the Department of Medicine. This position is newly created and is part of the ALTER deployment, which aims to ensure that work organisation complies with the Labour Act (LTr).

Description of post

As an internal physician, you want to prepare your post-graduate degree in general internal medicine and have a strong interest in specialization in gastroenterology and hepatology. You make a journey within the Department of Medicine in the Gastroenterology and Hepatology Service and the General Internal Medicine Service and evolve in both departments through rotations lasting 6 months. Under the supervision of senior doctors, you take care of the patients entrusted to you rigorously and clinically. You develop your analytical capabilities by dealing with sometimes complex clinical situations. Thanks to your organizational sense, you ensure practical activities, clinical and therapeutic interventions, while ensuring strict adherence to internal rules and procedures, with a constant concern for the quality of medical services provided. Evolving in a challenging and demanding academic environment, in a multidisciplinary team, you also contribute to the training of trainees. You work closely with the entire medical-care team and ensure that you maintain clear, fluid and respectful communication with your colleagues and with patients, adapting your speech to their needs and understanding. In parallel to your activity, you will receive a quality post-graduate training that will help you achieve your FMH qualification. As such, you develop your training according to the training plan and FMH requirements.

Qualifications

You have a federal degree in medicine or have training deemed equivalent by the Medical Professions Commission of the Federal Office of Public Health (MEBEKO). You have a minimum experience of 2 years of general hospital medicine. You have a perfect command of the French language (level C1 required) and a very good written and oral capacity. With excellent communication skills in a multidisciplinary team and patient, you are a dynamic, flexible and committed person. In addition, you have demonstrated strong adaptability.
