Full description

We are summit strikers by profession. Because the Alps show us every day what top performance means. With more than 90 vehicles and direct rail connections, we bring goods quickly, reliably and environmentally consciously to every corner of Southeast Switzerland. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • Based on the scheduling you carry out the transport orders in regional transport in Graubünden and deliver the goods on time and professionally to our customers
  • Loading and unloading? No problem for you - you get involved and make sure that everything is safely stored
  • You make sure that the cargo is perfectly secured – Nothing is left to chance
  • Together with you, your vehicle is the most important and always visible business card of our company – so you take care of its care and maintenance

Why do you succeed?

  • You have the driver's license Kat. C in your pocket and ready to take the tax

  • Your driver permits are all up to date

  • Oral and written you can safely express yourself in German
  • You are reliable, flexible and willing to give full throttle even in stressful moments due to your resilience

  • Quality bonus: Quality is written in our capital and rewarded

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere.
