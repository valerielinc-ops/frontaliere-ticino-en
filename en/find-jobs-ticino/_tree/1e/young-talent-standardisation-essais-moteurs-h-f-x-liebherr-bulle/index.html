Full description

Are you passionate about engineering, mechanical design and process optimization? Do you want to meet a concrete technical challenge in an international industrial environment? Join Liebherr Machines Bulle SA as a Young Talent – Engine Testing Standardization and take part in our engine development activities by actively contributing to the analysis, standardization and optimization of our test facilities and processes. We are looking for a candidate available from February 2027 for a 12-month internship. Your Responsibilities

  • You analyze the existing instrumentation configurations and set standards for different engine families to improve the repeatability of the tests.
  • You standardize the interfaces and connections of test benches to reduce installation times and optimize their preparation.
  • You analyze transmission and coupling systems and identify opportunities for standardization and reduction of variants.
  • You design and develop mechanical solutions using 3D CAD tools, from modelling to field implementation.
  • You develop and harmonize the technical documentation (plans, drawings, nomenclatures and instructions) to ensure the reproducibility of the installations.
  • You identify opportunities for continuous improvement, propose concrete solutions and measure their impact on the efficiency of engine testing.
  • You coordinate the project activities with the technical teams, monitor the actions and present the results achieved. Your profile
  • You have a higher technical background or have completed engineering studies in mechanics, electromechanical or similar fields.
  • You master 3D CAD software and have good knowledge of mechanical design and technical standards.
  • You have a strong interest in engines, test benches and field work.
  • You are autonomous, organized, proactive and appreciate teamwork.
  • You have good skills in both English and French, both oral and written Have we raised your interest? If so, we would be happy to receive your application online. If you have any questions, please contact Lisa SCHNEIDER. One Passion. Many Opportunities. *A common passion. So many opportunities. Liebherr Machines Bulle SA is the competence centre for combustion engines (diesel and gas) and hydraulic components. The company develops and manufactures diversified products used in civil engineering, mining, agriculture, decentralized energy, mechanical and industrial engineering and aviation.
