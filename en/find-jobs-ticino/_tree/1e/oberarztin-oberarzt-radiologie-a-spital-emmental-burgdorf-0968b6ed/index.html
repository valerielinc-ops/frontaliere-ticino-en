With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 residents – around the clock.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement:

Tasks

  • Independent radiological diagnosis of outpatients and inpatients at both locations
  • Competent radiological advice and contact maintenance with the clinical referral
  • Participation in clinical radiological subspecialization
  • Demonstration of findings on interdisciplinary reports
  • Participation in the further education and guidance of assistant doctors
  • Participation in radiological services

Profile

  • Specialist title FMH radiology with or is about to graduate. Alternatively, you have a foreign title recognized as equivalent
  • Knowledge or interest in a radiological subspecialization, in particular special knowledge in neuroradiological diagnostics (or focus „Diagnostic Neuroradiology) are advantageous
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and strong social skills
  • Independent, careful and patient-oriented work
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
