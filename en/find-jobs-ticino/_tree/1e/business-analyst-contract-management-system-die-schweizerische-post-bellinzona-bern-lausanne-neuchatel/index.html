Full description

Do you want to further develop contracts from isolated, document-oriented solutions to structured, digitally usable agreements? As a business analyst, you will design the professional model and requirements for the future contract management system and the end-to-end contract lifecycle in the commercial stack.

For one of our teams, we are looking for a committed and initiative personality who takes a structured approach to complex relationships, brings together different perspectives and develops viable solutions together with business and IT. With you, we create the basis for consistent and sustainable contract management.

You can do that.

  • You will analyze today’s contract processes, pain points, systems and data and develop coordinated target processes for creation, testing, completion, activation, modification, renewal and termination.
  • You specify structured agreement data, contract components, mandatory and optional elements, clauses, relationships, status, versions and technical rules.
  • You ensure consistent referencing of products, services, prices, service levels, billing and payment terms in the contract.
  • In addition, you specify roles, responsibilities, approvals, exceptions and transfers to offer, pricing, billing, legal, customer portal, support and service provision.
  • You formulate features, user stories, acceptance criteria and tests and accompany PoC, MVP, system configuration and technical acceptances.
  • In addition, you will moderate interdisciplinary workshops and ensure that specialist areas, legal, architecture and IT develop a common understanding of the contract.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of experience in business analysis or requirements engineering, ideally in contract, offer or customer management.
  • You will also have knowledge of contract lifecycle management, CPQ/CPQC, digital contract processes or data-based agreement models.
  • You have experience in process and information modeling as well as specification of business rules, workflows and approvals.
  • In addition, you convince with your strength in moderation, stakeholder management and the structured processing of professional and legal requirements.
  • You will have a good understanding of system integration, data quality, versioning, auditability and technical testing.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German and English. Every other national language is a plus.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
