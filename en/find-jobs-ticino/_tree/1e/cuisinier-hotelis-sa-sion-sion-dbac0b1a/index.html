Full description

The position of community cook is to ensure the preparation of dishes in accordance with hygiene, quality and sustainability standards. Your role will be to support the chef in the daily organization and replace the second kitchen in his absence in order to guarantee the continuity of the service. You will participate in inventory management and orders. Autonomous and versatile, you ensure the presentation of the dishes and the smooth running of the kitchen operations. The post includes the realization of live cooking, with preparation of dishes in front of the clientele. As such, you actively contribute to the image, transparency and friendliness of the institution.

Cook CFC or equivalent training, confirmed experience in community cooking or system restoration, ability to work independently, knowledge and mastering of standards of hygiene and food safety (HACCP), relational ease and careful presentation, including for live cooking activities

Organizational sense, teamwork, flexibility and stress resistance

50% post 5 days a week

French minimum level B2 - salary according to CCNT
