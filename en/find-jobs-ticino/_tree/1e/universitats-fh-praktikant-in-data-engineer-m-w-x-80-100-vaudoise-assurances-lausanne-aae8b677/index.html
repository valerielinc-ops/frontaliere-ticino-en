Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in vocational training, it promotes the development of young talent, with every year more than 120 learners trained in our departments. In order to strengthen our Digital Transformation & Information Systems department as part of the evolution of an operational platform, we are looking for a Master's intern for 6 months from February 2027 in Lausanne. The missions that await you:

  • Help ensure data quality and consistency
  • Participate in data integration and processing within MongoDB and Databricks
  • Contribute to the exposure and availability of data through REST API and asynchronous mechanisms
  • Participate in the design and implementation of Event-Driven architecture
  • Putting into practice the principles of architecture Medallion to structure, transform and enhance data Your profile:
  • You are in the course of academic studies (Master University or High School), ideally in an engineering school with specialization in computer science
  • You have strong knowledge of Python and Java
  • You are familiar with Kafka and Databricks
  • You are fluent in French (C1) and have good knowledge of English (B2) You are also autonomous and curious. We offer you:
  • A healthy working environment in a mutual enterprise
  • A varied and rewarding work experience
  • A possible opportunity for placement at the end of the internship/training The HR team is looking forward to receiving your full application package (CV, cover letter and 3 final transcripts).
