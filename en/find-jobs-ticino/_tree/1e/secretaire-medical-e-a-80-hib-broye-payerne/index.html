Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the broyard population. To meet the many challenges ahead, the HIB is looking for:

Medical Secretary at 80%

Your Mission

Ensure a quality reception for patients and their loved ones, in order to ensure a multidisciplinary administrative management of the metabolic centre, as well as the consultation of endocrinology allowing also to support the various stakeholders: dietician-ne, physiotherapist, nurse-ère in diabeticology and psychologist in their daily activities.

  1. Patient reception and coordination
  • Welcoming patients with professionalism and kindness.
  • Manage phone calls, emails and message transmission.
  • Organize and plan appointments and consultations via Ultragenda, based on medical availability.
  • Ensure coordination between patients, physicians and other health professionals.
  1. Administrative management and monitoring of medical records
  • Verify and update patient administrative data.
  • Write, format and transmit medical reports and letters to insurance.
  • Ensure the filing, digitization and monitoring of medical records.
  • Control billing and bill certain services.
  1. Organization and logistics of the secretariat
  • Manage orders for medical equipment and administrative supplies.
  • To contribute to the proper functioning of the secretariat and to the quality of administrative support.

Your assets

  • Business employee with CFC, supplemented by medical secretary training, or medical secretary degree
  • Confirmed experience of at least 2 years in a medical secretariat.
  • Good knowledge of medical terminology and hospital administrative procedures.
  • Easy to use with the usual computer tools (Outlook, Word, Excel) and ideally the following software: Opale, Soarian, Ultragenda, Medex.
  • Organizational sense, ability to manage priorities and good stress resistance.
  • Versatility, rigor, team spirit and strict respect for confidentiality.

Entry into office 15 January 2027 or to be agreed
