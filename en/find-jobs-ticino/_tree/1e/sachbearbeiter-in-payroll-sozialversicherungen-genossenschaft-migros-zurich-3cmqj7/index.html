Full description

At the cooperative Migros Zurich you can expect an exciting position as a clerk in Payroll & Social Insurance. In a modern working environment, you take on responsibility, contribute your know-how and take care of various topics in the field of payroll accounting. Become part of a dedicated team and contribute to our joint success.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Zurich, Pfingstweidstrasse 101, 8005 Zurich

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You handle social insurance issues – with a focus on MSE, EAE, EO, BUE, AHV and family allowances
  • You edit the absences in the time recording system MAPS/E3 as well as in the UKA
  • You book the daily allowance statements as well as overdraft bills and reconcile the pay-relevant FIBU accounts monthly
  • You correspond with employees and other contact points, such as HR services, compensation funds and insurance
  • You work on the annual work
  • You optimize payroll processes and get involved in payroll projects

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of professional experience: In the required area
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): As a merchant* clerk, ideally a further education or profound experience in the field of payroll, HR or social insurance
  • German (very good knowledge): Knowledge of other national languages is an advantage
  • First professional experience in a comparable position, preferably with focus on social insurance and absence management
  • Strong IT affinity and secure handling of systems such as SAP HCM or SuccessFactors, Excel and UKA
  • Reliable, independent and structured working method, combined with networked thinking and a high level of initiative

Cosa of Friamo

  • Handy subscription: You can look forward to special conditions for the Handy subscription

  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits

  • Club School Migros: Participation in the course costs in the club school
  • Leisure and cultural offers: You can benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Assessment centre: An assessment centre is a part of the selection process during the application process. With the help of various tasks and tests, the qualifications and behavior of the applicants are to be examined. Depending on the case, the assessment center can take up to one day. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
