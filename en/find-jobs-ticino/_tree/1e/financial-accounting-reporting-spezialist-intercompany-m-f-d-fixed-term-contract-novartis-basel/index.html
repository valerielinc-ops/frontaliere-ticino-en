Ready to bring clarity, control, and smarter ways of working to global intercompany accounting? As a Financial Accounting & Reporting Specialist, Intercompany (m/f/d), you will help ensure accurate accounting, reconciliation, and reporting of intercompany transactions across legal entities. You will partner with Finance, Tax, Treasury, Order to Cash, and Procure to Pay teams to resolve complex differences, strengthen controls, and support reliable financial close and reporting. You will also act as a subject matter expert, driving standardization, automation, and digital improvements that reduce manual effort and make intercompany processes more efficient and scalable.

Job Description

Key

  • Responsibilities

· Manage accurate and timely accounting, reconciliation, and reporting of intercompany transactions across legal entities. · Resolve intercompany mismatches, out-of-balance positions, and reconciliation differences in collaboration with global counterpart teams. · Coordinate intercompany close and elimination activities, investigating billing, cross-charges, allocations, transfer pricing, and foreign-exchange differences. · Execute key financial controls and maintain robust documentation to support compliance, audit readiness, and reporting requirements. · Partner across Finance, Tax, Treasury, Order to Cash, and Procure to Pay to strengthen end-to-end intercompany processes. · Drive standardization, automation, and digital improvements while providing intercompany accounting guidance and knowledge sharing.

Essential

  • Requirements

· Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration, or a related field. · Relevant experience in financial accounting and reporting, with strong exposure to intercompany accounting and reconciliations. · Strong knowledge of financial close, general ledger accounting, intercompany eliminations, internal controls, and compliance requirements. · Advanced proficiency with SAP or comparable enterprise resource planning systems and Microsoft Office tools. · Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities, with the ability to investigate and resolve complex accounting differences. · Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills, with professional English proficiency and experience collaborating across global teams.

Desirable

  • Requirements

· Experience with Sarbanes-Oxley controls, transfer-pricing impacts, foreign-exchange differences, or complex cross-border accounting. · Experience driving process automation, standardization, or digital transformation within Financial Reporting and Accounting, with a strong focus on leveraging AI solutions, including Microsoft Copilot, AI Agents, and intelligent automation technologies.
