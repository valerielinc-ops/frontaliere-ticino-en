Full description

We are looking for a passionate developer who loves designing, building and testing every layer of the system they work on. You care about code maintainability and about continuously improving software that has already shipped. We need your help to expand and evolve our modules to make them more flexible, open and robust.

You'll see the products you work on adopted and turned into powerful systems that support the critical business needs of our customers (some of them are listed here: http://www.answermodules.com#somecustomers).

Being a full-stack developer at AnswerModules means committing to quality and performance in everything you write. That holds whether your code will be used by your colleagues, by our partners or by all of our customers: your work is going to make the difference.

How we work: AI-assisted engineering We believe AI is changing software engineering as deeply as high-level languages once did, and we have chosen how to embrace it. We don't do "vibe coding". We practise AI-assisted engineering: AI is a powerful collaborator, but it doesn't replace engineering judgment.

  • People stay in control. Engineers make the decisions and own what ships.
  • Understanding comes before acceptance. If you can't explain it, it isn't done.
  • Quality is non-negotiable. AI makes us faster; discipline makes us better.
  • We learn together. AI should speed up learning, not replace it, and we share what we discover as a team.
  • If you want it even shorter, the opening paragraph can stand alone, followed by just one line: "People stay in control, understanding comes before acceptance, and quality is non-negotiable.
