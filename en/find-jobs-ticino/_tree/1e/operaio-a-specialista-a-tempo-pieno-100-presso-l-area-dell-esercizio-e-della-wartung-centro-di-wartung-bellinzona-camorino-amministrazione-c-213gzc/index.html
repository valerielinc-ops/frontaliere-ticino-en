Full description

Tasks

  • perform maintenance and repair work of vehicles, heavy vehicles owned by the State assigned to the Area of Exercise and Maintenance
  • perform maintenance and repair of special equipment and aggregates dedicated to the maintenance of green spaces, winter service and cleaning of roads
  • collaborate with the office of vehicles and machines of the State and with third parties in the execution of repair work
  • also perform work on the outside and on shifts; intervention by out-of-time needs (night and/or festive)
  • management of the warehouse of the maintenance center of Camorino with tasks of purchase of consumables and spare parts for vehicles and equipment, purchase and distribution material for the cleaning, collaboration in the management of the warehouse for the entire territorial unit, disposal waste

Requirements

  • Federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as a mechanic of cars (heavy vehicles and/or light vehicles) or as electric cars
  • proven experience in the maintenance and repair of different machines and equipment (falciers, aggregates cleaning tunnels and green cutting, sandblasters, means to clear snow)
  • experience in managing spare parts and different material (control of stocks, purchase, counting invoices).
  • licence for cat drivers BE and CE with CQC
  • license for forklift drivers
  • good computer knowledge package Microsoft Office
  • initiative, prepared to work in teams, organizational skills at work and priority management
  • flexibility and availability at high working rates, irregular and out of time (night and/or festive)

Special remarks

  • service office: Camorino
  • entry into service: 1. January 2027 or date of receipt

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), further documentation may be requested at a later stage (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

4 November 2026
