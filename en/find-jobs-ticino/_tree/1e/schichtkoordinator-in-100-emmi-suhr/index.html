Full description

You are not looking for routine, but responsibility? In this role, you are not simply part of the production – you actively shape it. As shift coordinator, you take on a key position between people, technology and processes and ensure that everything reliably interlocks. If you like to get started, keep track and make a difference, you are exactly right with us.

You can do that.

  • You are the linchpin of our production and ensure that everything runs smoothly - from quality to safety.
  • When things get hectic, you run to the highest form: You create structure, set clear priorities and give your team orientation.
  • You bring people, machines and processes in harmony – and are in the middle of it yourself instead of just there.
  • Disruptions are not a problem for you, but an opportunity: you tackle them systematically and find sustainable solutions.
  • You make improvements visible and actively drive them forward - whether through shop floorboards, POKA or through your own initiative.

You bring that with you

  • You are flexible, tackle and stay confident even in a dynamic 3-shift operation - new tools such as MES or SAP do not deter you.
  • You know the production from your own experience - as a plant manager: in EFZ or food technology: in EFZ.
  • The food industry is your home - ideally, you even bring experience in the dairy environment.
  • You think and act technically: you understand what is behind plants (e.g. pneumatics, sensors, motors), recognize connections and are able to not only fix faults, but really understand them.
  • You lead people with clarity and respect and create an environment where collaboration works.
