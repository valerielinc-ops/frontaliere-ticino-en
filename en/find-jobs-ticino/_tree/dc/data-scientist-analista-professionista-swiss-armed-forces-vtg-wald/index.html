Full description

Put to the Point

As an analyst in the Data Analysis and Intelligence (DAI) section, you are part of a team of news experts who analyze, evaluate and process news from worldwide open information sources around the clock. In close cooperation with our partners, you will also be involved in the administration and continuous development of the underlying situation tracking software and the products based on it. The technical focus of your activity can be individually designed according to your interests, knowledge and skills. Depending on your profile, you will take on tasks with a focus on product ownership, data science and/or software development.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20989
  • Location: Eichacher, 3086 Forest

You can make this contribution

  • Assess multilingual information from the open, worldwide news volume (OSINF) in real time and alert customers within the Federal Administration in the event of special security policy events
  • Manage the underlying OSINF tracking software and further develop it in cooperation with the manufacturer and internal partners
  • Develop various OSINF reports – for example using NLP – from proof-of-concept to mature product
  • Advising customers within the Federal Administration on the use of OSINF messages in everyday work and supporting them in the use of the situation tracking software
  • Creating multilingual information bulletins on current affairs

This makes them unique.

  • Higher education degree or equivalent higher education
  • Very good knowledge of current world affairs, international political contexts and relevant actors in the field of international security policy
  • Knowledge of programming concepts and data science, especially in the field of natural language processing (NLP), as well as experience in software development projects are advantageous
  • Willingness to shift according to fixed schedule, including night, weekend and holiday services. mobility also outside public transport hours
  • Having your own vehicle is an advantage
  • Very good active knowledge of a second official language and very good knowledge of English

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
