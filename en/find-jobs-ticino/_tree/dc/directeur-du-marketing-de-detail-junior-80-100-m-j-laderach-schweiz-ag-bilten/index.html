Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for it. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries, increasing trend. The company is headquartered in the canton of Glarus. Before picturesque alpine scenery we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new creative ideas in agile teams. Various activities are bundled in our Corporate Functions division. In addition to our finance and IT services, global marketing and sales management and human resources are also located in this area. Fresh ideas are created here every day, which we jointly lead to success. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Do you want to participate? Then come to us.
