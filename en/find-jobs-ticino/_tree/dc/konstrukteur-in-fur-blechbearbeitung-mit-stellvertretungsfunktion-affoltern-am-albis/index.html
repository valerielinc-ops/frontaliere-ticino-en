Full description

The construction of sheet metal parts is carried out using modern 3D CAD technology. You use your know-how for programming laser, punching and folding systems in the production process. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Designing sheet metal parts with 3D CAD and creating workshop drawings
  • Prepare winding-up and production documents
  • Programming lasers, punching and bending devices
  • Further develop products and represent the management of pre-production

Requirements

  • Training as a constructor or equivalent
  • Good 3D CAD knowledge, ideally with Solid Edge
  • Programming knowledge for sheet metal processing centers, preferably TRUMPF Boost
  • Motivated, responsible and team-oriented way of working

Offer

  • Pleasant working atmosphere in a motivated team
