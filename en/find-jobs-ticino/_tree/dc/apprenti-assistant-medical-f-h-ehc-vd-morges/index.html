Full description

The Hospitaller of the Coast — EHC has been a health network at your service for over 20 years. It brings together institutions with complementary missions of acute care, rehabilitation, accommodation in EMS and first resort medicine. We deliver more than 40 medical disciplines in order to care for each patient and resident, thanks to more than 2,000 professionals working on a daily basis to provide them with quality care.

We are looking for our medical centers in Aubonne, Bussigny, Nord-Sud and Arbora in Crissier, from Simplon to Renens and Mapraz to Ecublens and the Permanence des Halles de Morges:

6 Apprentices medical assistants (M/F) 100%

From 1 August 2027.

WE OFFER YOU

  • The opportunity to discover the world of work in an evolving institution whose mission is to take care of all our patients and residents.
  • The discovery of different disciplines within one of EHC's medical centres.
  • The opportunity to benefit from a close follow-up during your learning, by involved and experienced trainers, who will accompany you towards the success of your SWC.
  • 6 weeks of annual holidays, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • A mobility plan including participation in your trip by bike, basketball or public transport.
  • Financial participation in sporting events such as the 20km of Lausanne or Bike to Work, a catalogue of advantages offering discounts in the fields of sport, well-being, leisure, mobility, insurance and finance.
  • The possibility of having a fixed time at the workplace to prepare your final exams.
  • Professional opportunities within the institution after your apprenticeship.

YOUR POSITIONS

  • A compulsory school leaving certificate or you will soon be completing your schooling.
  • A real motivation to get involved for 3 years in a human-sized medical centre.
  • A well-defined professional project, thanks to an observation course carried out in the care.
  • A dynamic, voluntary and empathic nature, you are well managing your stress, maturity and ease in teamwork.
  • A very good level of French, a good learning ability and a sense of listening.

OBJECTIVES

  • Receive phone calls, analyze requests, if necessary and with the advice of the doctor, set appointments;
  • To welcome patients, install them, reassure them, help them prepare for the various examinations planned;
  • Perform various treatments such as dressings, blood samples and injections;
  • Assist the doctor in small surgeries;
  • Perform on request various examinations such as electrocardiograms and x-rays;
  • Perform various dry chemistry, hematology and rapid tests.

WE ARE

  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
