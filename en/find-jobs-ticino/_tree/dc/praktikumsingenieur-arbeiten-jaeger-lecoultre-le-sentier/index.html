Reference code: JR133466 Since 1833, drive by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressly expressed its inventiveness through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication. YOUR MISSION: The Wheel and Control Body sector is at the heart of our home's know-how: it ensures the manufacture and assembly of the watch's vital components. To meet the strategic challenges of manufacturing, your mission will be to design and plan the relocation of the industry's workshops. In close collaboration with production managers, support functions and building teams, you will act through four key phases ranging from flow diagnosis to the logistics plan for moving. HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT? Phase 1 – Setting and Objectives: Define the mix-product and target volumes precisely, then formalize the specifications by integrating all technical, human and spatial constraints. Phase 2 – Analysis of Flows and Process: Decoritize the operating ranges, map the actual production flows via a VSM (Value Stream Mapping) approach and perform the load/capacity calculations necessary for future dimensioning. Phase 3 – Implementation Design: Design the overall layout of the workshops (Macro-location/Zonage) and then model the ergonomic and detailed layout of the workstations (Micro-location), while participating in the preparation of the budget estimate. Phase 4 – Technical Planning & Logistics: Prepare equipment integration plans (networks, energies and utilities) and design a phased relocation plan (phasing) to ensure continuity of production. HOW ARE YOU TO BE US? Training: Student in the final year of master's degree in engineering (mechanical engineering, industrial engineering) in search of a graduation internship. Specialization: Strong orientation or specialization in Continuous Improvement. Personal qualities: Rigor, proactivity, curiosity of mind and strong analytical capacity. Relationship: Excellent team spirit and relationship ease. HOW ARE YOU TO BE US? A project of completion of strategic studies with high industrial added value. The opportunity to collaborate with multidisciplinary teams at the heart of an exceptional factory. A responsible learning framework.
