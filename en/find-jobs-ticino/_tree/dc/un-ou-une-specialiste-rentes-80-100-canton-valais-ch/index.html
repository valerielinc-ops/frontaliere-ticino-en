The Office AI du Valais is the cantonal centre of competence for the Swiss Invalidity Insurance with more than 190 employees in various fields. He is responsible in Valais for individual insurance benefits for people affected by their health. To strengthen our team, we are looking - for an entry into office to be agreed - an Annuity Specialist 80%-100% Your activities You process AI applications to define entitlement to AI pensions and vocational rehabilitation.
