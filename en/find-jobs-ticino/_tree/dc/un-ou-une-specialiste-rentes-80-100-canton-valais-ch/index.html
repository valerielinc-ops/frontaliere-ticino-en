Full description

The Office AI du Valais is the cantonal centre of competence for the Swiss Invalidity Insurance with more than 190 employees in various fields. He is responsible in Valais for individual insurance benefits for people affected by their health. To strengthen our team, we are looking - for an entry into office to be agreed - an Annuity Specialist 80%-100% Your activities You process AI applications to define entitlement to AI pensions and vocational rehabilitation. You decide the orientation of the file from a medical, legal and vocational rehabilitation perspective. You are actively collaborating with the regional medical service Rhône, as well as with the rehabilitation service. You analyze and discuss issues in a multidisciplinary team, if necessary in collaboration with other social insurance companies.

Your profile

You have a diploma from a higher business school, an SWC or equivalent training. A federal patent in social insurance is desired. Professional experience in social insurance is required. You also have an interest in legal aspects. There is also a need for excellent writing skills and good mastery of basic computer tools (MS Office). Your personality You are a person able to work independently and make decisions. You have good stress resistance and a capacity for interdisciplinary collaboration. With a very good analytical spirit, you also show flexibility. Open to changes, you are dynamic, with ease in contacts. Your mother tongue is French and you are ideally familiar with German.
