Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

As part of the Shared Services (SHS) business unit of Hamilton AG, this area assumes central tasks for the Hamilton companies. Teams in HR, ICT, Corporate Communication, Facilities and Finance ensure that processes run smoothly and employees worldwide are optimally supported.

You can do that

Main tasks

  • Overall responsibility for ICT service and technology architecture as well as its strategic development and governance. Ensuring alignment with Enterprise Architecture Responsibility in Business IT in terms of business requirements and technological solutions to support business objectives
  • Definition and enforcement of architectural principles, standards and target architectures in coordination with the ICT strategy
  • Ensuring architecture and service governance taking into account established frameworks (ITIL, TOGAF)
  • Establishment and governance of a company-wide service catalogue and service portfolio
  • Ensuring regulatory compliance (MDR, GxP, ISO 27001) in architecture and service management
  • Ensuring transparency and controllability through KPI-based reporting on architecture compliance and service quality

Additional tasks / Special tasks / Projects

  • Participation in the Change Advisory Board (CAB) and other process-related bodies
  • Advising ICT management and project managers on architecture and process issues
  • Further development of the ITSM platform (especially ServiceNow) from an architectural perspective with focus on integration, data consistency and automation
  • Accompanying ICT projects with focus on service transition and architectural conformity
  • Coordination of improvement initiatives to increase operational efficiency
  • Derivation of architecture principles from ICT strategyResponsibility for ICT technology and service target architecture as well as the associated technology and platform roadmaps

This is what you bring

  • Higher education in computer science, business informatics or comparable qualification (FH/HF/Uni)
  • Experience in IT architecture or system design
  • Several years of experience in IT (e.g. system engineering, architecture, IT service management or application management)
  • Good understanding of the relationships between systems, processes and business requirements
  • In-depth understanding of IT service management processes (ITIL) and their integration into the overall architecture and system landscape
  • Structured, analytical and solution-oriented working method.
  • Good communication skills in German and English (min. B2)
  • Ability to understandably structure and communicate complex topics
  • Interest in architecture, governance and strategic issues
  • Willingness to familiarize yourself with new topics (e.g. architecture methods, compliance)
  • Perseverance combined with a pragmatic, solution-oriented attitude

Benefits

  • Knowledge of frameworks such as ITIL, TOGAF or COBIT
  • Experience with ITSM tools (e.g. ServiceNow)
  • Knowledge in regulated environments (e.g. ISO 27001, MDR, GxP)
  • Experience in collaborating or managing projects
