Your Mission

Learning French is an integral part of the process of social and vocational integration of migrants and allophones. The Basic Training Unit (UFB) is a unit of the Measurement and Training department of the ISO Directorate of the General Hospital, a social action institution in Geneva. The UFB's activities are based on respect for institutional values and the application of educational principles in adult education, French as a language of integration (FLI) according to the principle of fide and the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFRL). LUBB is certified EduQua, a fide examination centre and its course offer has been certified as a fide quality since 2019. The UUFB manages the French language and basic skills system, adapted to the needs of participants, in accordance with the institutional, political, social and quality criteria. The UFB approach responds to the participant's need for language autonomy and contributes to the construction of its social and/or vocational integration path. Your main activities

  • Organize and plan evaluation sessions;
  • Strengthen the evaluation cluster in place;
  • Receive and evaluate participants in individual interviews or group meetings;
  • Promote guidance in line with the needs of the evaluated participants and draft the assessment reviews;
  • Propose guidance solutions tailored to the specific needs of the institution's public;
  • Contribute to the development of specific offer partnerships with the network of external providers;
  • Collaborate with social workers and vocational integration advisers;
  • Collaborate in the administrative monitoring of participants in the monitoring tools provided for this purpose (inscriptions, reports, results, statistics);
  • Participate in the evolution and adaptation of the unit training system;
  • Collaborate with the training centre as a whole.
