Full description

The EGGER Group is one of the top players in the Swiss logistics industry and moves, lifts and stores pretty much everything – from the standard range to the complete industrial plant. With 800 employees at 11 locations, we ensure smooth transport throughout Switzerland.

Your mission

  • We are counting on you! As our chauffeur or as our chauffeuse, you reliably bring cargo from A to B – with team spirit and driving pleasure.
  • You are the connection between our customers and their delivery – reliable, on time and with a smile.
  • Whether solo or with a pendant (Cat. C or Cat. CE) – you always keep control.

What you should bring

  • Experience as a truck driver – you know how to get heavy cargo safely to its destination.
  • Flexibility & resilience – traffic jams, weather & appointments won’t get you off track.
  • Readiness to stay overnight – because you are looking for adventure
  • Customer orientation and good manners – because we not only deliver goods, but also a first-class service.
  • Driving licence Cat C/E or Cat. C – you can’t do it without it.

What we offer

  • A job with variety – no day is like the other, every tour a new adventure.
  • A modern vehicle – your truck is your workplace, so there is only the best equipment.
  • Tours to the most beautiful corners of Switzerland – mountains, lakes and endless roads – you get everywhere!
  • A strong team & family culture – with us you are not just a number, but a valued colleague.
  • Progressive employment conditions & social benefits – because your commitment should be rewarded.

Ready to start with us? Then pack your driver card and get in touch – we look forward to meeting you!
