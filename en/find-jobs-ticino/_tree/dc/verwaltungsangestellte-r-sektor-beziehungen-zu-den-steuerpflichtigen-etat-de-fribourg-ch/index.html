The Cantonal Tax Administration (KSTV) assesses and obtains the cantonal taxes as well as the direct federal tax.

We focus on the complementarity of competences and the diversity of perspectives, because it is the wide range of careers and profiles that makes up the strength of the state of Freiburg. Their tasks

The Cantonal Tax Administration (KSTV) has created a new sector that takes care of relations with taxpayers. This sector is the guarantor of KSTV's good image and the link between taxpayers and the various sectors of KSTV.

Area of activity

  • cooperate in the KSTV call centre and answer telephone inquiries from taxpayers
  • receiving taxable persons at the counter
  • KSTV emails and letters and answers
  • Perform various mutations in the control register
  • Complete various administrative tasks

Desired profile

  • EFZ as a businesswoman or equivalent training
  • Mastering common IT tools
  • Skill in dealing with people
  • Courteous and courteous behavior
  • Ability to work independently
  • Mastering the German or French language with very good knowledge of the other language

Note: The post shall be filled under a fixed-term contract for a period of three years and may be renewed annually.
