The Opportunity

As an eLearning Specialist, you are responsible for executing Learning system data maintenance and administrative activities in a timely and accurate manner in support of Learning business processes. You will perform functionality testing on previously created eLearning materials You will gain a broad understanding across global Learning processes and tools, as well as local and global downstream impacts on compliance, audits and right-to-operate, as well as finance, IT etc. This is coupled with strong customer focus, communication skills and solution orientation.

In this role, you will be

  • Supporting the Global Learning Center of Excellence with system updates, process enhancements, and cross-functional projects.
