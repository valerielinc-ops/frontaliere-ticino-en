Spital Langenthal Printing place Post as PDF SRO AG is the regional hospital center in Oberaargau, Canton of Bern. The Langenthal Hospital and its two health centres in Huttwil and Niederbipp, as well as the Panorama Park in Herzogenbuchsee and the family practice in Wynau, provide comprehensive and high-quality medical care for the population of the region. The satisfaction of our patients as well as our employees is our focus. To strengthen our team, we are looking for an agreement

  • Conduct of a special consultation hour and consultancy services under supervision of the medical officers

  • Care of patients in the field of orthopaedics in the ambulatory and inpatient area
