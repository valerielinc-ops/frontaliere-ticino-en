Full description

Portrait Our school mats is located on the Bödeli. We currently lead 21 classes from kindergarten to high school with 390 students. We employ a total of 59 teachers, two school social workers and a day school team. We are looking for a healing teacher for 8 lessons. A continuation of the employment is possible. They accompany students in cycle 1 in two mixed classes. Are you interested and would like to know more about the job or our school? Visit our homepage (www.schulematten.ch) or contact us directly with your questions: V. Rüfenacht, 033 828 10 30, info@schulematten.ch

Tasks

You teach as an MR teacher in cycle 1.

Requirements

You have completed training as an MR teacher or are in training at a higher education institution. Students are also welcome! With commitment, you accompany your students individually, promote and challenge them and support them on their learning and educational path.

They are a responsible and communicative personality with experience in dealing with children and their guardians. Collaboration in the team is a matter of course for you.

We look forward to your application!

We offer

  • An employment as a deputy for an SHP on maternity leave, from 01.02. to 31.08.2027.
  • An IF room for teaching and preparation.
  • Good cooperation in the team and support from the school management.
  • A school culture characterized by clear structures and direct communication.
  • An appreciative, open and humorous cooperation.
