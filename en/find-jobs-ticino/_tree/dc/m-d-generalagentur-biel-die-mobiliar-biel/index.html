Full description

What awaits you

In this role, you offer our customers insurance and pension solutions that are tailored to the respective needs. In doing so, you are actively pushing ahead with the expansion of Biel General Agency’s customer portfolios.

This is what they do with us

The focus of this position is on the new acquisition of private and corporate customers, as well as the support and active expansion of your existing customers.

Thanks to your courteous nature, you always have an open ear for your customers, whom you always support with personal advice and individually tailored solutions from the entire insurance and pension sector.

As an established and communicative person, it is easy for you to build and maintain a customer network. You also appreciate the open exchange within a dynamic team.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed vocational training or studies
  • Several years of professional experience, advantageous in insurance and pension counselling
  • Enjoyment in attracting new customers through active networking
  • Independent, responsible and customer-oriented working methods
  • Authentic, winning and empathetic personality
  • Fluent German and French skills, every other language is a plus
  • Affinity for digital work

Working with us

We offer you versatile, varied and independent work in a motivated team, a modern workplace in a central location in the city of Biel as well as excellent working conditions and further training opportunities.

The area of activity includes the city of Biel with its surrounding municipalities. The job starts immediately or by agreement.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
