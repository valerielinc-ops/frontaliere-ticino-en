Customer service consultants Commercial Vehicles & Passenger Cars (m/f/d)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction Carrosserie Rothenburg is a branch of HESS AG, one of the leading Swiss vehicle manufacturers, and offers a varied working environment. In Rothenburg, our customers have a comprehensive range of repairs and services for vehicles of all kinds at their disposal – from buses and commercial vehicles to passenger cars and campers. Our orders are as diverse as the vehicles themselves.

To complement our team in Rothenburg (LU), we are looking for a motivated and qualified personality as customer service consultant Commercial Vehicles & Passenger Cars. Do you have industry experience in the repair of commercial vehicles and passenger cars and are you looking for a new challenge? As a customer service consultant Commercial Vehicles & Passenger Cars, you help to expand and develop our services in the area of services. If you combine administrative tasks with technical know-how and want to play a key role in a dynamic company, we look forward to receiving your application! Tasks.

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for coordinating and carrying out repairs and maintenance of commercial vehicles and passenger cars
  • Performing order entry, order processing and repair calculations
  • Scheduling for the repairs of customer vehicles (Spenglerei, Lackiererei & Schlosserei)
  • Acceptance and delivery of customer vehicles
  • Ensuring smooth customer service and excellent customer experience
  • Assisting the manager in the management and organisation of the holding
  • Maintenance of insurance, financing and garage partners (15 active partnerships)
  • Ensuring compliance with quality standards and guidelines
  • Assistance in the management, motivation and further development of employees
  • Taking over the deputy manager of Carrosserie Rothenburg

Our requirements

  • Apprenticeship degree as a carrosseriespengler EFZ, carrosseries painter EFZ or vehicle closer EFZ
  • Industry-specific training is advantageous
  • Professional experience in customer service or vehicle repairs
  • Experience in creating calculations with SilverDat is beneficial
  • Organizational talent with high problem-solving competence
  • High degree of customer orientation and excellent communication skills
  • Resilience and flexibility for a dynamic working environment
  • Team spirit and the ability to work effectively with different departments
  • Self-employment and efficient working methods

We offer them

  • Responsible, varied and challenging work in a growing company
  • Possibility for co-design and further development of our services in the field of passenger car repairs
  • Management responsibility in deputy for the manager
  • Moving working time
  • Performance-based conditions of employment
  • Opportunity for further education and development in the HESS Group (1100 employees)
