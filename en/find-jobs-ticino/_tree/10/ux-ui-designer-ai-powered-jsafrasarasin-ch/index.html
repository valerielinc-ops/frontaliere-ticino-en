UX/UI Designer - AI-Powered • Geneva, CH • Full-time • Unlimited Your Role As UX/UI Designer in our Artificial Intelligence Department, you own the user experience of a strategic legacy transformation program: one design system shared across all rebuilt modules, used by relationship managers, compliance officers and back-office teams. You will use the bank's internal AI tools and knowledge to accelerate research, prototyping and design work - AI empowers your craft, while you design the product. You will join our newly established Artificial Intelligence and contribute to a strategic, multi-year legacy transformation program that modernizes core banking processes for our private banking business. You will be part of the AI team, leverage the bank's internal AI knowledge, tools and private-LLM ecosystem, and use AI to power your day-to-day work - while the focus of the role is on delivering the transformation, not on building AI products.

Your Responsibilities

  • Create and evolve a single design system shared across all functional modules of the new platform
  • Run user research and workshops with relationship managers, compliance officers and back-office users across our international locations to simplify complex, process-heavy workflows
  • Design target-state journeys that eliminate manual workarounds and enable straight-through processing, in close collaboration with the program's process-redesign stream
  • Prototype before build: validate designs with users before each module enters its build phase, and support UAT with the Product Owner and QA
  • Use AI-powered tooling from the bank's internal AI ecosystem for research synthesis, usability analysis, prototype generation and design documentation
  • Ensure accessibility, usability and compliance-friendly design of regulatory forms and approval workflows

Your Profile

  • Degree in Interaction Design, HCI, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in UX/UI design for complex enterprise or banking applications
  • Proven track record building and maintaining design systems, ideally with MUI and React-based component libraries
  • Strong skills in user research, journey mapping, prototyping and usability testing in regulated or process-heavy environments
  • Hands-on experience using AI tools (LLM assistants, AI prototyping/research tools) in your design workflow
  • Ability to translate regulatory and compliance requirements into simple, human-centered interfaces
  • Fluency in English; other languages are an advantage
