On behalf of an important client, we are looking for the following:

IT SECURITY ARCHITECT

Responsibilities

  • Define and maintain enterprise IT security architecture standards, frameworks, and

guidelines.

  • Ensure compliance with regulatory, and industry best practice standards (PCI-DSS,

NIST CSF).

  • Design and review IT systems, applications, and infrastructure by translating

business requirements into secure technical solutions.

  • Apply architectural principles such as Zero Trust, Defense in Depth, and integration

with Automated Detect & Response.

Requirements

  • Degree in computer science, cybersecurity, IT architecture, or equivalent

specialization.

  • 5 years of proven experience as an IT Security Architect or in a similar senior

security or Architecture role.

  • Experience with risk assessments, threat modeling, and security architecture

reviews.

  • Strong knowledge of security frameworks and standards such as NIST CSF.

Experience with PCI-DSS would be a plus.

  • Security certifications such as (CISSP, CISM, SABSA, TOGAF, or equivalent) are a

plus.

  • English is mandatory, with Italian being a strong advantage. German is a plus.

Soft Skills

  • Strong communication capabilities and teamwork skills.
  • Leadership personality and capability at structured analysis and abstraction.
