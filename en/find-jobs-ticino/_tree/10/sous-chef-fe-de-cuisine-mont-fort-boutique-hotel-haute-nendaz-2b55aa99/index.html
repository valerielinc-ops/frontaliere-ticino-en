Full description

  • Mont Fort Boutique Hotel will open in Haute-Nendaz in December 2026. To give life to this new address, we are looking for committed and dynamic collaborators.
  • Ideally located in the heart of the Domaine des 4 Vallées, our hotel offers 44 rooms, a 120-room restaurant, a bar, a terrace, a seminar room and a wellness area.
  • What makes us different?
  • The opportunity to participate in the opening of a new hotel 4*
  • A human-sized establishment with a dynamic and welded environment
  • Prospects for change as we develop
  • An authentic atmosphere, a motivated team: Welcome to Mont Fort Boutique Hotel

To strengthen our team, we seek

1 Deputy Chef de Cuisine

Your profile

  • A positive and caring team spirit.
  • Polyvalence, autonomy and flexibility.
  • Passion for cooking, sense of detail and concern for quality.
  • Experience as Head of Party or in a similar function.
  • Dynamics, organization and ability to work effectively during periods of high activity.
  • Good command of culinary techniques, hygiene rules and HACCP standards.
  • Ability to work independently while ensuring good coordination with the rest of the team.
  • Mastery of French.

Your missions

  • Ensure the preparation, installation, production and dispatch of the dishes of its part, in accordance with the quality standards and requirements of the establishment.
  • Organize and supervise its part, ensuring the proper preparation of products, respect for recipes and regular presentations.
  • Ensure the smooth running of the service, coordinate preparations and ensure the timely dispatch of dishes.
  • Ensure quality, freshness and good product conservation, as well as stock management and waste control.
  • Participate in inventory and order management and supply tracking.
  • Work closely with the Chef de Cuisine and other team members to ensure good coordination between the different parts of the kitchen.
  • Participate in the supervision of the kitchen staff and ensure the daily functioning of the kitchen.
  • Conduct hygiene checks and ensure compliance with HACCP standards.
  • Participate in the development of menus, new proposals and continuous improvement of dishes.
  • Ensure the maintenance and cleaning of its workstation, equipment and kitchen spaces, in accordance with HACCP hygiene, safety and procedures standards.
  • To carry out its tasks under the direction of the Chef de Cuisine.

Entry into office : December 2026
