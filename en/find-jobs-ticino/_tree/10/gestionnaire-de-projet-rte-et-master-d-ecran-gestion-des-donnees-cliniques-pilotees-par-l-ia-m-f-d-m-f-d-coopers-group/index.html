For our client, we are looking for an experienced Program Delivery Manager / Scrum Master who confidently drives delivery roadmaps across multiple agile teams, coordinates cross-functional dependencies and releases, and brings strong expertise in agile methodologies, stakeholder management, and cross-team collaboration, in order to ensure transparent, efficient, and high-quality delivery of complex programs from MVP through multiple release cycles.

Start date: 01.07.2026 End date: 30.11.2027 Workload: 100% (up to 50% remote working possible) Contract Type: Contracting Location: Kaiseraugst

Your Tasks

Program & Delivery Management

  • Own and drive delivery planning across multiple agile teams from MVP through subsequent releases
  • Manage cross-team dependencies, risks, and blockers to ensure smooth delivery execution
  • Lead release management activities, including release planning and coordination with Development, QA, and Operations
  • Ensure structured scope and requirements management processes
  • Provide transparent program status reporting to stakeholders and leadership

Scrum Master & Agile Coaching

  • Facilitate Scrum ceremonies for multiple delivery teams (Sprint Planning, Daily Stand-ups, Reviews, Retrospectives)
  • Remove impediments and protect teams from external disruptions
  • Coach teams on agile best practices and continuous improvement
  • Support Product Owners with backlog refinement and story definition
  • Track and communicate key agile delivery metrics (velocity, burndown, blockers, etc.)

Cross-Team Coordination

  • Coordinate collaboration and alignment across multiple delivery teams
  • Facilitate Scrum of Scrums and dependency management sessions
  • Maintain Jira as the central source of truth and ensure consistent Jira governance across teams
  • Enable effective collaboration between onsite and distributed international teams
  • Coordinate with adjacent programs and platform teams on integration dependencies

Your Profile

  • 7+ years of project management experience in software delivery, including 3+ years managing programmes with multiple parallel teams
  • Demonstrated Scrum Master experience - you have facilitated agile teams, not just observed them. You know how to run an effective retro and how to coach without directing
  • Pharmaceutical or Life Sciences industry experience with understanding of GxP requirements and what inspection readiness means in practice
  • Hands-on Jira experience - you have configured and managed Jira projects, not just consumed reports. Xray test management experience preferred
  • Experience with scaled agile delivery (SAFe or similar) * and the pragmatism to know when to adapt the framework to the situation
  • Strong risk and dependency management skills with a bias toward action
  • Experience managing distributed hybrid teams across multiple time zones
  • Fluent English communication - written and verbal
