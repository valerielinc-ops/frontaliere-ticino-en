# Treasury Specialist

Il tuo futuro inizia

You are responsible for tasks in the Group Treasury with a focus on liquidity management and administration. You plan the short-term liquidity and control the liquid funds in CHF and foreign currencies on the money market. You also manage the corporate cash pools in Switzerland and Europe. In addition, you advise the Group companies on treasury issues and support Group loans as well as bank guarantees/guarantees. In a committed team, you work independently and maintain a clear, reliable cooperation.

  • Short-term liquidity planning at Group level and management of liquid funds in CHF and foreign currencies on the money market
  • Supervision of Group Cashpools Switzerland and Europe
  • Administration of group loans and issuance of bank guarantees/guarantees
  • Group-wide coordination of account connections, including new openings and updates of signature regulations, as well as superusers in e‐banking systems
  • Heavy users in the Treasury Management System Quantum and use of AI tools such as Copilot or Swisscom’s own AI applications.

Skills

  • Degree (FH/HF) in business administration, banking diploma or similar
  • Several years of experience in treasury and/or banking environment (corporate clients)
  • In-depth knowledge of financial instruments
  • Versed in e-banking systems as well as in cash management and money market transactions and with payment transactions topics (SIC, SWIFT, SEPA etc.)
  • Connected and analytically thinking, you recognize connections and solve problems
  • Precise and efficient operation
  • You are customer-oriented and as a strong communication personality you will find the right balance in representing our interests
  • Digital affinity and a good sense of automation and optimization potential in treasury processes
  • Very good German and English in word and writing

With us you have the opportunity to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or in the home office. You will come into contact with agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours to meet your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you can look forward to a variety of attractive services that will enrich your work experience. This includes a pleasant working environment, financial benefits and exciting opportunities for professional development. Benefits.
