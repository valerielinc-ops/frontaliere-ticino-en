Full description

Portrait We are a cantonal vocational training center in the Bernese Oberland with around 300 employees and 3,500 learners. At several locations, we offer training and further education in the fields of industry, service and fashion design (IDM).

Is your heart beating for the educational world? Then you are exactly right with us. We are looking for you as

Tasks

Are you ready?

  • Teaching a BPI class in the subjects language/communication (German as a second language), profession and society (professional world and integration), accompanied individual work
  • Shared class teaching and teach in team teaching
  • 1 to 1 Coaching lessons and supervision of learners
  • Starting class: Our class starts acyclically and therefore you are there from the beginning
  • Expected class size: approximately 16 learners
  • Promotion of educational, linguistic and personal skills
  • Support for learners in vocational orientation, internship and apprenticeship search
  • Cooperation with specialist bodies, partner organisations, parents/guardians and enterprises
  • Engagement within the framework of the vocational mission for a school that is developing

Requirements

You bring that to me.

  • Teaching diploma (preferably for upper secondary level I)
  • Experience with teaching in DaZ
  • Innovative and open attitude
  • Joy of working with young people and young adults
  • Enthusiasm and team skills
  • Digital Affinity
  • An independent and responsible personality
  • Teamwork is a central part of your working attitude

We offer

This is what we offer you.

  • Accompany young people on their career path in a meaningful working environment
  • Trust and appreciation shape our leadership: Together we focus on development and innovation, act sustainably, pay attention to health protection and live a strong coexistence
  • We live commitment according to the motto "We do what we do because we like to do it".
  • Benefit from internal course offers as well as from support for external education and training
  • We pursue a requirement-based, market- and performance-related salary policy (Law on the employment of teachers of the Canton of Bern)
  • Through mobility and exchange, we regularly maintain regional, national and international relations
