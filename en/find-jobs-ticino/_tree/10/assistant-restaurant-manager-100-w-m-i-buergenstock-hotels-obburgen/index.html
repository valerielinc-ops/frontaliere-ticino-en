Assistant Restaurant Manager 100% (m/f/i) - Bürgenstock Resort An icon among Swiss hotels: the history of the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne began in 1873 – a story that continues to impress and touch to this day. High up on the Bürgenberg lies an oasis of relaxation and a place of first-class hospitality. The Bürgenstock Resort and its approximately 700 employees from 54 nations offer the perfect setting for vibrant moments that become memorable. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two overnight options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior), and the Taverne 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these establishments knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Ten restaurants, bars & lounges serve as showcases for culinary excellence; guests can find ultimate relaxation in the award-winning Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa. The Bürgenstock Resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from around the world but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. The resort ranked 4th among the best employers in Switzerland in 2023 in the categories of gastronomy, tourism, accommodation, entertainment, and leisure, and received the official certification as a "Great Place to Work" in Switzerland in 2025. Two awards that fill the entire team with pride. To strengthen our team, we are looking for an immediate or by agreement: Assistant Restaurant Manager 100% (m/f/i) Our restaurant combines excellent cuisine with warm hospitality in a unique atmosphere. As Assistant Manager, you support the Restaurant Manager in operational management, ensure the highest quality standards, and promote the development of the team. Support the Restaurant Manager in the daily operation of the restaurant and ensure smooth processes. Assist in creating duty rosters with Mirus 3.0 and optimize staffing resources. Develop and conduct internal training sessions on operational guidelines, our offerings, and the ongoing development of employees. Ensure and monitor all hygiene and quality standards for impeccable guest service. What you bring – Your recipe for success: Completed training in gastronomy/hospitality or a degree from a hotel management school. Professional experience in a comparable position and confident handling of Micros Symphonie. Experience in upscale hotel operations. Fluent German and English skills; any additional foreign language is a plus. Sense of responsibility, flexibility, resilience, and a strong awareness of quality. Sensitivity when dealing with international guests, enjoyment of leading staff, and a charismatic demeanor. What we offer you – Work where excellence is at home: You can expect a varied role in a dedicated team and in a work environment that combines style, quality, and well-being. Our modern staff house offers space to relax and recharge, with a fitness studio, TV lounge, billiard room, and library. As part of the resort, you benefit from attractive discounts in our restaurants and bars, hotel stays, leisure activities such as tennis or golf, and selected insurance options. We support your commute with public transportation and offer parking at attractive rates. Through our Beekeeper app, you stay connected at all times, and exciting further training and internal advancement opportunities open up new perspectives for you. If you share our values of enthusiasm, commitment, responsibility, and entrepreneurship (BEvU), you are exactly right with us. Become part of a team that lives hospitality at the highest level – with heart, standards, and passion. Now it’s your turn! Become part of a team that inspires daily with passion, creativity, and a sense of excellence. At Bürgenstock Resort, elegance, views, and atmosphere come together to create a special place for demanding moments of enjoyment – make it your stage! Show us how you want to enrich this place with your personality, style, and experience. For us, it’s not just about what you do – but how you do it: with heart, sensitivity, and genuine hospitality. Apply now with your motivation letter, CV, work references, and a recent photo. Let’s create unforgettable experiences together – for our guests and for you. Awards:
