Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2,250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Directorate for Energy operates hydraulic and thermal production infrastructures, develops production facilities from renewable energy sources and provides electricity, heat and gas supplies under the best conditions.

Mission

  • You ensure the processing, tracking and control of customer billing, in collaboration with internal and external partners.
  • You identify and process billing anomalies and contribute to continuous process improvement.
  • You support business leaders on billing issues and participate in financial loops.
  • You participate in customer billing projects by bringing your professional expertise.

Profile

  • You have an SWC of a trade employee, a commercial maturity or equivalent training.
  • You have at least 3 years of professional experience in an environment involving billing processes.
  • You are comfortable with computer tools and complex processes and master SAP IS-U. D365 mastery a real advantage.
  • With a very good knowledge of the French language, you are recognized for your rigor, your sense of organization, your analytical spirit and your relationship ease.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and team: "Together for tomorrow's energy".
