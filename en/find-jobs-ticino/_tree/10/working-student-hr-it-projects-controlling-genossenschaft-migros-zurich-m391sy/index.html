Full description

The HR Directorate ensures modern personnel management through professional services. To further drive digital transformation in HR, we are looking for a customer-oriented and IT-savvy individual to join the HR Projects & Systems team.

Responsibilities

  • HR Controlling & Reporting: Create and update reports and dashboards for management and specialist departments.
  • First-level support for our HR systems: Act as the point of contact for user inquiries, assist with error analysis, and collaborate closely with IT and HR departments.
  • Participate in the implementation of SAP SuccessFactors, particularly in the area of Story and Canvas Reporting.
  • Support digitalization projects in the HR environment – from automation to improving data flows.
  • Documentation and process maintenance to ensure quality and optimize our HR data landscape.

Requirements

  • Initial experience: with HR systems (e.g., SAP HCM or SAP SuccessFactors) and a good understanding of data, reporting, and processes.
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): Commercial training with experience in HR or IT.
  • Student at the beginning of studies (University/FH/HF) in Business Informatics, Business Administration, or HR Management.
  • Proficient in Excel/Power BI; SAP knowledge (HCM or SuccessFactors) is a plus.
  • Analytical thinking, high IT affinity, and enthusiasm for independently driving topics forward.
  • Team-oriented, reliable, and communicative.
  • German (fluent)
  • English (advantageous)
