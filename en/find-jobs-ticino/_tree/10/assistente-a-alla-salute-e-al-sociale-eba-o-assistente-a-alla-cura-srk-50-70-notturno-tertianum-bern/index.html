For the «Fischermätteli» in Bern, we are looking for a Health and Social Care Assistant EBA or SRK Care Assistant 50-70% night shift starting January 1, 2026 or by agreement.

Your tasks

  • Care and support of assigned guests

  • Participation in nursing reports and forwarding of important observations

  • Assistance with logistical and domestic tasks

  • Completed SRK nursing course with practical training in long-term care
  • Completed training as «AGS»
  • Experience in long-term care is an advantage
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoyment of varied challenges

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
