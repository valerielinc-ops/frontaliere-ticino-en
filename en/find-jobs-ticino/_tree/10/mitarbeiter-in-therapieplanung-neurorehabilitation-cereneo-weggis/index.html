Full description

As one of the world’s leading clinics, we develop an individualized treatment program with the aim of enabling our patients to enjoy the highest possible degree of independence and freedom of life, thereby optimizing their quality of life. We address both patients from the Swiss cash system and international patients. cereneo offers highly specialized rehabilitation programs for people with neurological diseases and injuries, including stroke, brain injury, neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson’s, multiple sclerosis and many other neurological disorders.

Your new role

  • Creation, coordination and continuous adaptation of individual therapy plans for patients: in neurorehabilitation
  • Coordination of therapy times, premises, resources and participating professional groups
  • Consideration of medical, therapeutic, nursing and organizational requirements in planning
  • Ensuring a balanced, realistic and targeted therapy planning taking into account the individual resilience and daily form of the patient:
  • Early identification of planning conflicts, bottlenecks and priorities and development of pragmatic solutions
  • Professional processing of short-term changes, outages and rescheduling
  • Close cooperation with doctors: inside, care, therapy teams, administration and other interfaces
  • Documentation of relevant planning information and transparent communication of changes
  • Participation in the further development of planning processes, standards and digital planning systems
  • Support for the evaluation of planning indicators, capacities and loads

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed education or studies in medical, therapeutic, nursing or administrative fields
  • Professional experience in therapy coordination, deployment or scheduling or in a comparable clinical environment is advantageous
  • Understanding interprofessional processes in healthcare. Experience in neurorehabilitation is a plus
  • Strong organisational and planning skills as well as a good understanding of complex processes
  • Ability to capture information quickly, prioritize it meaningfully and transform it into workable solutions
  • Secure handling of digital planning, calendar and documentation systems
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing
  • Further language skills are advantageous

Your personality

  • They work in a structured, reliable and responsible manner.
  • They also keep track of high dynamics, short-term changes and conflicting goals.
  • They communicate clearly, bindingly and respectfully with different professional groups.
  • They act solution-oriented, pragmatic and make well-founded decisions in the defined area of responsibility.
  • They have diplomatic skills and an adequate level of enforcement.
  • They value the interprofessional cooperation and bring a pronounced patient:inner orientation.
  • They deal discreetly and professionally with confidential information and particularly sensitive personal data.
  • They question existing processes constructively and actively participate in improvement processes.

Our culture – that connects us Excellence, warmth, motivation, teamwork and innovation are more than just values for us.
