The Davos Klosters Mountains are five mountain and ski areas in the Swiss Alps. In addition to the mountain railways, the Davos Klosters Bergbahnen AG operates over 23 hotels and resorts, from simple group accommodations to luxurious four-star superior. To support the team at the Club Hotel*, we are looking for a person for the role of: Head of Service 100% The Hotel. Our 3-star Club Hotel has 145 spacious double rooms for families and sports enthusiasts. The cozy rooms and the hotel's offerings invite a longer stay. The Jakobshorn with the Bolgen training area is just a few minutes' walk away. Your profile: Communication skills: You are articulate, open, and friendly Work style: You have a high level of commitment and a structured and autonomous work style Personality: You have excellent manners, a well-groomed appearance, and a positive aura. Commitment: You are flexible, resilient, and willing to work on weekends and public holidays Language: You have good knowledge of German and English, both in writing and in speech. Other foreign languages are an advantage.
