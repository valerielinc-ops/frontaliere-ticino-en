Homepage Team Leader Support Rothenburg, Switzerland Want to lead a dedicated support team? Do you attach great importance to correct and lasting solutions to problems? Do you enjoy working with employees to find solutions for our customers? Then apply now! Your everyday life

  • In order to ensure the operation of our enterprise software applications (OpaccOXAS, OpaccERP, OpaccEnterpriseCRM, OpaccEnterpriseShop) in the day-to-day business of our customers, You have the following tasks:
  • Personnel and professional leadership of the support team with more than 10 employees
  • Managing personnel and deployment planning as well as conducting individual meetings with employees
  • Ensuring and building a solution-oriented support organization
  • Participation in processing escalated calls
  • Support in the day-to-day business of support (1st and 2nd level)
  • Ensuring 1st level support for the system platform of our GU customers
  • Further education, training and strength-based promotion of employees
  • Constant optimization of support processes, instruments and internal documentation
  • Conducting call reviews and support briefings with our customers

Your background In order to grow professionally and personally with this task, you bring the following:

  • Solid basic education with further education or studies in the direction of (economic) computer science or similar
  • Leadership experience of specialist employees
  • Enjoyment of planning and organization
  • Team capable, resilient and you can think and work in contexts
  • A high level of social competence and pleasure in leading a high-performing team
  • Experience in a comparable task, especially in the enterprise software (ERP, shop) environment

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed.
