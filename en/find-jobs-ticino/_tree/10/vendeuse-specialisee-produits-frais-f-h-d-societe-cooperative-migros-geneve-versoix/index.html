Full description

Do you have a solid experience and would like to share this expertise with our customers? Do you like working in an environment where quality and freshness are paramount? This opportunity is for you! Join our team and help make the customer experience unique!

Mansioni

  • You ensure the supply and management of the shelves by guaranteeing the availability and presentation of dairy products.
  • You manage stocks and orders based on performance indicators.
  • You participate in the management of freshness quality by ensuring that each product meets our quality standards.
  • You advise customers and share your dairy expertise.
  • You rotate the products in order to ensure irreproachable freshness.
  • You help set up promotions and commercial actions.

Requirements

  • 2-5 years of experience: in the retail sector
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Retail Manager
  • Rigor, organization and autonomy
  • Excellent communication skills and strong team spirit
  • Versatile, dynamic and always with a smile
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Catering: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus Points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on the assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and free of charge for a savings account.
