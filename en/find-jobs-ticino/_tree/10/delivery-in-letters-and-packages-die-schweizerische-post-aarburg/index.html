Full description

Are you a morning person and do you like working outside? Then you are exactly right with us, because with you the post of tomorrow remains in motion.

Also lateral entrants: insiders are welcome with us!

You can do that.

  • Early in the morning (about 6 a.m.) you load the delivery vehicles together with your team and prepare your delivery tour.
  • Through your work, you shape the streetscape of the Olten region and embody the values of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the punctual and impeccable delivery of the parcels and letters in your area.
  • On your tour, you answer questions from our customers and contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • You work regularly on Saturdays, with the services divided into the team.

You bring that with you

  • With your physical fitness, you can easily load and lift packages up to 30 kg on a regular basis.
  • You like to be on the road, have the category B driver’s license and ideally A1 or are ready to catch up before starting your job.
  • You appreciate the work in the team, but you also like to travel independently and feel comfortable with large vehicles in road traffic.
  • You are reliable and work fast.
  • Good oral German skills as well as an experienced use of digital devices round off your profile.
