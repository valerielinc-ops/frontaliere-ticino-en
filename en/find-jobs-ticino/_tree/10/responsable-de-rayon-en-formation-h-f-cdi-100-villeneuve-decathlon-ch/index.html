Full description

Your future playground You will join Villeneuve's shop, made up of a team of 25 employees. Atypical boutique, located in a shopping centre between Lake Geneva and the mountains. The store produces an overall CA of 11 million CHF and ticks all the boxes of a destination store: one of the largest average basket in the country, low margin, larger increase in quantities of the region, etc. Your mission? Make your sport the absolute reference for all local practitioners. Join an adventure that continues to grow Decathlon in Switzerland, it is a success story that started in 2017 in Neuchâtel. Since then, we have opened around ten stores a year to reach 50 outlets today. And we're not going to stop there! Our expansion continues, especially in German-speaking Switzerland, offering career opportunities throughout the country. And now, you're looking for a job where you won't spend your life behind an office? Do you want to make decisions, be in the field and manage your department like your own business? You want to coach a team to take him to the tops? Then the position of Rayon Manager (=Team Leader) is for you. Why?

  • You'll spend 80% of your time out of the office. Your radius is your playground. Whether it's spending time with your team or with your clients, you're at the heart of the action. You keep strategic time, but we offer you a field job first.
  • Your success is directly related to your team's. As a manager, your role is not to control everything but to facilitate everything. You give your team the reason, and your team decides how. You accept the right to error and grow up with them. If your team wins, you win.
  • You are the boss of your activity. Here, you're a real intrapreneur. You anticipate the whole cycle of activity of your ray today and tomorrow, you recruit your talents, you define your business strategies... Anyway, you're in charge. What you will do on a daily basis: You will have 3 large caps. #1: Your MANAGER cap:
  • Commercial Pilotage (ton P&L): You are the owner/manager of your business. You write and animate your trade policy, choose your ranges, analyze your economic performance and manage your stocks (including receiving goods).
  • Permanent Store (Captain): By running with other leaders, you take overall responsibility for the store (opening, closing, security). You are the guarantor of the smoothness of the game throughout the day. #2: Your MANAGER cap:
  • Coaching and management: You're here to advance your team every day. You conduct monthly individual interviews with each of your teammates to support them in their development.
  • Recruitment: You are the one who is looking for and choosing your future game partners to build a diverse team of enthusiasts.
  • Training: You continue to learn every day (leadership, finance, product technology, etc.) and you also pass on your knowledge to your team. #3: Your COMMERCANT cap:
  • Customer trade: Customer satisfaction must be the priority! As an exemplary manager, you put your hand in the dough: you advise your customers, you manage the reception, you receive goods and you guarantee a radius always at the top.
  • Transverse Missions: As soon as you have mastered your profession, you can get out of the box to boost regional projects, sporting events or club partnerships. Why "in formation" manager? As Head of Department (=Team Leader) in training:
  • You're in pairs for several weeks/months with an expert section manager.
  • You follow an integration and training plan adapted to your previous experiences and current skills. The goal? Give you the keys and gradually prepare yourself to manage your own ray independently. This post IS NOT for you if...
  • You wait till we tell you what to do every morning.
  • You're looking for a daily job.
  • Your sneakers don't want to do 10,000 steps a day...
  • You don't want to work on Saturdays.
