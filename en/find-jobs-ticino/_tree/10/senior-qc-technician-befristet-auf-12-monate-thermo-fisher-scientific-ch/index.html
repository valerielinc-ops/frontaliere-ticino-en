Job description

Position summary: The Senior Quality Control Technician (m/f/d) ensures compliance with the highest quality standards in the pharmaceutical production environment. This position covers a variety of tasks related to the inspection, monitoring and documentation of processes and materials and ensures compliance with GMP standards.

Main responsibilities

  • Start-up Release: Preparation of space, machines and materials before the start of production.
  • In-process controls: carrying out regular in-process controls to ensure compliance.
  • Final controls: conduct final inspections of packaged study medication (blind control).
  • Quality decisions: making quality decisions in the respective work areas / cabins.
  • Prevention of deviations: Implementation of measures to avoid process deviations.
  • Deviation Management: Initiating Deviation Reports in Global Trackwise.
  • Microbiological monitoring: performing microbiological measurements to ensure a sterile environment.
  • Material management: managing the quarantine and release of materials in the operation.
  • Inspection tours: conduct weekly inspection tours in the production areas.
  • Machinery monitoring: support and implementation of equipment monitoring and calibration.
  • Documentation: Creation of standard operating procedures (SOPs) and forms in the electronic document management system (eDMS).
  • Environmental monitoring: Review of weekly and monthly temperature and humidity readings.
  • Light measurements: conduct light measurements as required.
  • Support requests: Processing and support for internal and customer requests.
  • Logbook management: controls and archiving of logbooks.
  • Reporting problems: information direct supervisors about all problems that have occurred.
  • Training needs: identifying personal training needs and supporting the training of new employees in the workplace.

Qualifications

  • Experience in pharmaceutical manufacturing and/or quality control.
  • Knowledge of Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP).
  • High degree of accuracy and efficiency.
  • Strong sense of responsibility and reliability.
  • Ability to work shifts (06:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00).
  • Flowing knowledge of German, English to the advantage
  • Experience with Microsoft Word and Excel.

Additional information: This job description does not include all the tasks and responsibilities required for this function. Any additional duties, tasks or responsibilities required by the team leader or manager must be performed accordingly.
