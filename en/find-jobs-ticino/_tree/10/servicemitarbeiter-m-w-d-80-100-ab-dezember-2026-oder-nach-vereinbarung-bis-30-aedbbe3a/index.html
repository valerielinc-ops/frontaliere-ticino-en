Full description

The Wellnesshotel Schweizerhof is one of the leading first-class hotels in Saas-Fee with the following capacities: 40 comfort rooms and 8 suites (90 beds), 120 seats in the Hofsaal restaurant, 40 seats in the lounge and bar. It is centrally located yet quiet with a fantastic view of the Allalinhorn. A vinothek with fine Valais drops, fine evening menus and specialties in the Hofsaal restaurant as well as 1000 sqm spa with swimming pool, whirlpool, sauna landscape and fitness room for relaxation round off the offer.

Explore more! Do you want to spend a new evening in the Valais mountains? We are a young, competent, established team looking for a new member. We strive for high guest satisfaction, love the service of the guest and stand for quality instead of quantity. Interested?

Your tasks

  • Competent guest care, running your own station
  • Support for the service management and its deputies
  • Shared responsibility for the smooth running of day-to-day business
  • Co-responsibility for daily accounts, inventories and orders
  • Cooperation at banquets and receptions (birthdays, weddings, business events)
  • Participation in seminars and conferences (coffee break, lunch and dinner)
  • Implementation of seasonal events (Christmas, 1 August, Easter)
  • Host and contact person for guests in his station
  • Control of the Barin in relation to ambient, cleanliness and order
  • Very good knowledge of the F&B and wine offer
  • Constant adherence to quality standards through high self-initiative
  • Carrying out cleaning and maintenance work
  • Compliance with HACCP guidelines

Your profile

  • Completed training and experience in a comparable position
  • Good knowledge of wine and spirits
  • Positive and dutiful personality with the will to go new ways
  • Awareness of quality and responsibility
  • Creativity and pleasure at work
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.
  • Knowledge of French or other languages is advantageous
  • High level of initiative, independence and flexibility
  • Enthusiasm and team skills
  • Flexibility and resilience
