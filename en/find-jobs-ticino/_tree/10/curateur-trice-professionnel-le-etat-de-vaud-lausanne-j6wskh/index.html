Full description

We are looking for a motivated person to engage in a meaningful mission at the service of the most vulnerable. As a professional curator, you have a demanding, useful and deeply humane profession. Every day, you accompany and protect people who are vulnerable in a variety of social, administrative, financial, legal and health issues. The diversity of people and situations makes the trade rich and offers a stimulating, varied and constantly changing working environment. You work in a structured professional setting, in a department that implements the Conference's inter-cantonal recommendations on the protection of minors and adults (COPMA), particularly those concerning the number of files per curator. You benefit from the support of internal legal, financial, tax and insurance support. You contribute, on a daily basis, to the prospect of a dignified and autonomous life for all, thus participating in social justice.

  • Carefully manage measures for the protection of adults entrusted by the cantonal justices of the peace, in accordance with the legal and regulatory framework.
  • Manage the assets of the persons concerned and represent them legally.
  • Manage recipient revenues and expenses.
  • Develop an active partnership with professional networks and beneficiaries' relatives.
  • Create a relationship of trust with beneficiaries.
  • Invest in the initial training necessary for the function and in continuing training relevant to the development of the service. For more information, please consult the specifications, or watch the videos of this trade "Curatelles et tutelage : A mission of proximity, diversified and rewarding". By joining our service, you will be able to do telework. Indeed, we encourage flexibility to better reconcile work and personal life. For further information, please contact Vanesa Casas group leader at 021 316 67 14.
  • Bachelor's degree in social, legal or financial fields or equivalent.
  • 3 years of professional experience in relation to service missions or significant experience in accompanying vulnerable adults.
  • Further training related to health, addiction, migration, law, budget management and social insurance is welcome.
  • Excellent organizational capacity, administrative and financial rigour.
  • Autonomy, stress resistance, sense of responsibility and priorities.
  • Open mind, listening, flexibility, great adaptability and learning.
  • Disclosure of possible criminal history and an extract from the Prosecution Office is required for this function.
  • Driver's license.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
