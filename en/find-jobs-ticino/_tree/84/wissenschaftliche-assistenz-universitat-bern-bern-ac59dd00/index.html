Full description

The UniBE is networked. With us, you are part of an international community and benefit from interdisciplinary cooperation.

Tasks

  • Cooperation in the writing of scientific publications and opinions
  • Assistance in the organisation of scientific conferences
  • Participation in research projects
  • Independent scientific research
  • Participation in the Human Rights Law Clinic of the University of Bern
  • Assistance in the preparation and implementation of courses (lectures, seminars) and the supervision of students
  • Assistance in the development and correction of examinations and written work

Requirements

  • Outstanding editorial skills, stylish German and skillfulness in written expression (experiences in writing or editing scientific texts are advantageous)
  • Enjoyment of migration law and human rights issues
  • Interest in creative and scientific thinking and motivation to write a dissertation
  • Enjoyment of working together in a team
  • Independent, structured and careful working methods
  • Good study performance

Good research infrastructures and international network Application and contact Send your application including a detailed CV, certificates and a writing sample of max. 1 page (e.g. excerpt from a seminar or master thesis) please send a PDF to Marlen Weibel (marlen.weibel@unibe.ch) by e-mail until 31 October 2026. About us

The Center for Migration Law of the University of Bern is affiliated to the Institute of Public Law. It is dedicated to research in the entire field of migration law, taking into account human rights, international and European law and constitutional references. The Center for Migration Law organizes the Swiss Migration Law Days and publishes the Yearbook for Migration Law. BENEFRI Centre for Migration Law: www.ius-migration.ch
