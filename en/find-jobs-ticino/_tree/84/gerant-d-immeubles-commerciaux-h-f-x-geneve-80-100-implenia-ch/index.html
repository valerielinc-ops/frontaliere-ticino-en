Full description

For our Geneva branch, in a team dedicated exclusively to the management of commercial buildings, we respond with expertise and negotiation to the individual and complex needs of our institutional clients. As a strategic partner, we focus on offices and commercial goods.

Through targeted and tailor-made marketing strategies, we pursue the ambition to rent each surface successfully while building lasting and trusting relationships with business owners and tenants.

If you're passionate about real estate, you're entrepreneurial and you like to work as a team, we'd be happy to receive your application as:

Manager of commercial buildings (m/f/x) Geneva, 80-100%

What you contribute to

  • You are responsible for the complete management of a portfolio of commercial real estate, including leasing, contract and key management, and the guarantee of an irreproachable state
  • Thanks to your expertise and negotiation skills, you conduct successful negotiations and write commercial leases and amendments
  • You coordinate and carry out the records of the places of entry and exit of commercial surfaces and keep watch at a professional closing of accounts
  • Communication with landlords, tenants, authorities and other stakeholders, as well as organizing meetings and information sessions for tenants, is your responsibility
  • You are responsible for soliciting and evaluating offers, assigning mandates to providers, and monitoring and validating creditors' orders and invoices

What you bring

  • You have basic business training and you are a manager of federal patent buildings.
  • In addition, you have several years of professional experience in the real estate sector and in managing commercial buildings
  • Your affinity for digital and your strong computer and software knowledge allow you to use different systems with ease every day
  • You are able to work as a team, stress-resistant and distinguished by your customer-oriented personality and service
  • An effective, objective-oriented method of work and good communication skills qualify you to interact with demanding interlocutors
  • You speak fluently in French, oral and written. German and English are a plus.

Some of our benefits include

  • Working time: we live mobile work with individual (annual) hours of work and/or part-time work. Home Office possible up to 40%. Work-Life-Balance is crucial to us, because balanced and relaxed collaborators are important to us. Additional holiday purchase is possible
  • Allocation: you receive a contribution for the mobile phone. We also contribute to your meals
  • Training and development: encouraging employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support for internal and external training and development. We offer career planning opportunities in leadership, specialists and projects
  • Salary: We offer a market-compliant remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. Pension benefits are attractive and you can choose from different models. Accident protection insurance covers private division in hospital
