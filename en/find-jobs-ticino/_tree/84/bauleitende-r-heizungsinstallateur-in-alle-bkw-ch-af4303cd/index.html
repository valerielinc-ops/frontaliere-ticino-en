About us

Guggisberg Kurz AG, based in Ostermundigen and Urtenen-Schönbühl, has stood for the highest quality and reliability since 1926. As a traditional company, we offer home technology solutions in the areas of heating, sanitation, ventilation, spenglerei and thermal solar systems. Our customers benefit from comprehensive consulting and the use of the most modern and energy-efficient techniques and solutions – because environmental awareness and energy saving are particularly important to us.
