Every year you count the weeks until the next ski week in the Alps? And sometimes wonder why the mountains are actually just your holiday destination and not your home? Here you put on your ski boots in the free afternoon or stand on a summit at the weekend, and during the week you work as a senior physician in a psychiatry where you still know each other personally. Between two lakes and with four thousand meters on the horizon lies the psychiatry of a regional hospital group with around 1,600 employees. It is treated stationary at two locations, in a team that really lives the cooperation between the professional groups. The hierarchies are flat, new ideas are welcome, and the clinic is a training center of the highest category. Bern can be reached by train in about an hour.

Their tasks

  • Psychiatric expertise in the interdisciplinary treatment team, mainly at one location and with representation at the second
  • Own case management in a wide range, from basic care to specialized services, including in the parent-child sector
  • Guidance and supervision of doctors in further education, without personnel responsibility
  • Participation in the service and pikett system (pikett = on call)

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in psychiatry and psychotherapy or the degree is imminent
  • Does your specialist title come from Germany? The MEBEKO usually recognizes EU title, and Medimeo accompanies Step by step through the procedure
  • Safety in psychopharmacology and curiosity about tricky differential diagnoses
  • A person-centered attitude and enjoyment of working with different professional groups
  • Openness to new things and desire to shape processes

Their advantages

  • Living where others go on holiday: lakes, glaciers, ski resorts and hiking trails right on your doorstep
  • Clinical work at the center, without personnel responsibility
  • Part-time and job sharing possible, also at cadre level
  • Unpaid leave for longer breaks
  • Further education institution of the highest category A, stationary and outpatient
  • A warm climate in which you greet and gladly help

Your way to the mountains with Medimeo

  • A personal contact person, from the first question to the first working day
  • Support for MEBEKO recognition, with contribution to the costs
  • Help with moving and finding accommodation in the Bernese Oberland
  • Other suitable places from our network, if this is not the right one
