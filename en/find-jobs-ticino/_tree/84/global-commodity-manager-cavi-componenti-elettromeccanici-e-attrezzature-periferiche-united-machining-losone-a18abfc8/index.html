Global Commodity Manager - Cables, Electromechanical Components & Peripheral Equipment

Pensum: 100%

Location: Losone

Type of employment: Perpetual

Their tasks

  • Develop and implement the global sourcing strategy for the Cable Commodity across all UMS sites worldwide. In addition, lead the sourcing strategy, supplier management, and cost optimization initiatives for Electromechanical Components and Electrical Cabinets within UM.
  • Drive cost optimization and value creation initiatives, including supplier negotiations, cost reduction programs, volume bundling, should\-cost analyses, and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) improvements.
  • Manage and develop the supplier portfolio, including supplier selection, qualification, performance management, strategic partnerships, risk mitigation, and continuous supplier development.
  • Lead contract, risk, and compliance management activities, ensuring robust commercial agreements, supply continuity, and adherence to corporate procurement policies and regulatory requirements.
  • Collaborate cross\-functionally with Engineering, Operations, Quality, Supply Chain, and site leadership teams worldwide to support business growth, innovation, operational excellence, and the successful execution of procurement initiatives.

Your profile

  • Minimum 5 years of experience in Strategic Procurement, Commodity Management, or Category Management within an international industrial environment.
  • Ownership of the global Cable Commodity across all UMS sites worldwide, including strategy development, supplier management, cost optimization, and risk management.
  • Strong Negotiation, Supplier Management, and Stakeholder Management Skills, with a proven track record in leading complex international sourcing initiatives and contract negotiations.
  • Fluent and English language skills (written and spokenn) are mandatory
  • French and/or Italian are considered a strong advantage.
  • Excellent analytical and strategic thinking capabilities, combined with experience in cost optimization, Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), project management, and cross\-functional collaboration within a global matrix organization.

About us

UNITED MACHINING is one of the world’s leading suppliers of complete solutions for manufacturers of precision components and tools as well as for mold making. The portfolio includes machines for milling, spark erosion, laser structuring, laser micromachining and additive manufacturing. In addition, the business unit offers spindles, automation solutions, tools and digitalized solutions supported by unrivalled customer service and support.

UNITED MACHINING includes the brands AGIE CHARMILLES, CHARMILLES, MIKRON MILL, LIECHTI, STEP TEC and SYSTEM 3R.

UNITED MACHINING is headquartered in Biel, Switzerland and currently employs over 3,000 people at more than 50 locations worldwide.
