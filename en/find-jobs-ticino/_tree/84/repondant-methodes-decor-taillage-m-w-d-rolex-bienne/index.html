Full description

Introduction

For our Supplies sector, we are looking for a (M/F) Response methods whose main mission will be to study, design and carry out manufacturing processes in the fields of decor and cutting.

Responsibilities

  • Define programming strategies, associated tools and influential parameters
  • Perform equipment start-up, develop programs and certify them
  • Ensure the correct definition of the specifications produced in the operations, in close collaboration with industrialists, draftsmen and method workers
  • Share strategies and processes throughout industrialization phases
  • Train and support users in process transfers between industrialisation and production
  • Ensuring technological intelligence

Profile

  • Polymechanic CFC or micromechanic CFC or equivalent
  • Experience confirmed in the fields of Decor and Cutting of watch components
  • Knowledge of the manufacture or control of watch components
  • Technical culture, curiosity and openness to new technologies
  • Communicative, rigorous, organized, methodical and autonomous
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis
  • Ability to evolve within multidisciplinary and transversal teams

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
