Put to the Point

Are you an all-round talent and like to join? As an expert / woman company maintenance you have a special position. You are the good soul in the company and always there when something does not work. Do you feel addressed? Then you are exactly right in our diverse company. During your 3-year apprenticeship, you will get exciting insights into the varied tasks surrounding our buildings and facilities.

  • Date of entry: 01.08.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20324
  • Place of work: Military airfield, 3857 Meiringen

You can make this contribution

  • Maintain and maintain building technology (heating, ventilation, air conditioning, sanitation and electrical equipment)
  • Do small maintenance and repair work in buildings and accompany and support external craftsmen
  • Carry out cleaning work indoors and outdoors with modern cleaning machines and look after the disposal site
  • Care for the plants in the buildings and the environment outside
  • In winter, help with snow clearance and ice control
  • Carry out maintenance work on machinery and equipment and small equipment such as lawnmowers, brush cutters or leaf blowers

This makes you unique

  • Compulsory schooling completed
  • Logical thinking and exact working methods
  • Reliability and responsibility
  • Teamwork and good manners
  • Technical understanding and skill

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Diversity, dynamism, perspectives – teaching professions of the Swiss Army The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
