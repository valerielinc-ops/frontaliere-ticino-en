About us

The school district Schwamendingen comprises the entire district 12 of the city of Zurich. Schwamendingen is a lively, multicultural residential quarter. With public transport we are easily accessible via Stettbach station and Oerlikon with various tram and bus lines.

The Stettbach Day School is a QUIMS secondary school in Zurich-Schwamendingen. It manages two separate departments A and B and works with both the integrated promotion and the schoolhouse-internal learning and promotion center. The school Stettbach currently comprises sixteen classes, one of which is for the DaZ initial lessons and the lunch care.
