Front Office Manager (marked version)

Mansions

  • lead the Front Office department during the pre-opening phase, launch and ongoing operations, ensuring an impeccable experience for guests through reception, guest relationships, concierge coordination, central and presence in the lobby
  • define and implement high standards of service, operational procedures and contact points of the host path that reflect the position and ambition of the Grand Hotel Locarno as a five-star destination
  • select, insert, form and motivate the Front Office team, creating a culture of excellence, discretion, emotional intelligence and authentic hospitality
  • maintain a strong and visible presence of leadership in lobby and Front Office areas, supporting the team in daily activities and constantly acting as hotel ambassador
  • supervise arrivals, departures, VIP guests, allocation of guest rooms and preferences with extreme attention to detail, in order to offer a personalized and intuitive service at every stage of the stay
  • collaborate closely with Housekeeping, Reservations, Revenue, Spa, Food & Beverage and other operational departments to ensure impeccable coordination, accurate communication and uninterrupted luxury experience throughout the structure
  • promote guest satisfaction, quality control and upselling performance while maintaining operational efficiency, team productivity and compliance with hotel standards and procedures
  • support the implementation of systems, reporting tools and organisational structures relevant to the Front Office function, actively contributing to the success of the positioning and opening of this iconic hotel
