Specialist SAP Logistica - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team at the SAP Competence Center of the Digital Evolution and Technology Area we look for a(a) SAP Logistics Specialist (100%) Profile: The position is part of the SAP Competence Center and provides a mainly focused activity on MM and SD logistics modules in S/4HANA environment. The function is oriented to the support of logistic processes, the resolution of problems and the development of evolutionary solutions. It includes the management of different design phases, from analysis and definition of solutions to configuration, process documentation (BBP), testing, user training, data migration and go-live and post-go-live phases. The position also requires close collaboration with the different business departments and with the external partners involved. Main tasks and responsibilities: Ensure the correct operation of the MM and SD logistics modules business side. Provide 2nd and 3rd level assistance on SAP modules and related processes. Evaluate and coordinate new implementations or SAP processes, both internal business side EOC and resource management side suppliers. Manage documentation and training for users both for new functions and existing processes. Functional and technical analysis. Take care of system parameterization by complying with user requirements. Collect requirements. Identify and propose improvements: operational and process techniques, new features and/or new technologies. Support the technological evolution of architecture by suggesting valued updates such as improving and performing.

  • Requirements: Diploma SUP or higher education or equivalent in computer science. Professional experience on MM and SD logistics modules of at least 3 years. Knowledge of normal logistic processes (moving goods, quality control, ...). Good team and communication skills. Knowledge of Italian, English and/or a second national language. Good relational and trading skills. Good ability to solve problems, autonomy and orientation to service. They constitute preferential title: APM, FI, CO, HCM, IS-H, PS. Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
