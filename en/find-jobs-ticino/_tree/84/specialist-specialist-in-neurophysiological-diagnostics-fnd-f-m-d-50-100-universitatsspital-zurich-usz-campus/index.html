Full description

Zurich University Hospital is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks:

  • Independent preparation and implementation of the various EEG examinations as well as further neurological functional diagnostics
  • Co-responsible for planning in the application area and a smooth daily routine
  • Administrative work according to department-specific guidelines
  • Co-responsible for the material control and the operational readiness of the apparatus in the various laboratories and equipment warehouses of the EEG department
  • Teamwork and continuous training
  • On-call time during the week and on scheduled weekends, maximum 5-7 days per month Requirements
  • You are electrophysiological:r technical:r assistant:in or specialist in neurophysiological diagnostics (EPTA / FND / MTA-F) or equivalent medical training, professional experience beneficial
  • Independent, resilient and flexible personality who likes to deal with people competently
  • Safe handling of PC applications and readiness for familiarization with EEG/EP device software or already existing user knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • English skills as well as other foreign language skills are advantageous
  • High service orientation and teamwork We offer: The USZ offers you one of the most versatile and interesting FND jobs in Switzerland. As a new member of a dynamic, motivated and progressive team, you can expect exciting and varied assignments every day, which continuously expand your professional knowledge and skills. An integrated neurophysiological and epileptological training programme offers space for personal and professional development. The visit of internal and external training is encouraged. We live on the pulse of research and support team members with a research interest. The versatility of the University Hospital creates an attractive interdisciplinary working environment with ongoing contacts with other medical disciplines. A professional and competent introduction to your field of activity is guaranteed by our experienced team. In addition, if you are interested, we offer you exciting perspectives in sleep medicine with the opportunity for further training and active cooperation in our sleep laboratory.
