Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary The Machine Operator is responsible for operating and monitoring machinery used in the manufacturing process. This role ensures that machines run efficiently, products meet quality standards, and safety protocols are followed. This job profile is intended for support staff that follows established procedures and works under close supervision. Smaller/easier decisions are made autonomously. Summary: Works under limited supervision, sets up and operates machine tools without assistance, following department policy and procedures. Manufactures and reconditions components parts to customer, OEM, and industry standards using conventions, machine tools, including VTL, lathes, milling machines, grinders, etc. Interprets mechanical drawings, blueprints, sketches, and performs precision metal cutting, grinding, shaping and fitting. Constantly measures components, parts and raw materials with precision measuring instruments, gauges and tools.

Essential Functions

  • Sets up and operates conventional machine tools, under limited supervision.
  • Plans sequence of own work operations and methods of set-up, including methods of mounting, leveling aligning, holding and fastening work.
  • Interprets blueprints, charts, tables, handbook formulas, sketches, and written and verbal orders. Lays out work according to requirements. Selects and grinds own cutting tools.
  • Works within close tolerances to meet customer/contract specifications and applies knowledge of qualities of metals and alloys being machined.
  • Performs any bench work necessary to finish and fit parts to required customer specifications.
  • Follow procedures according to ISO 9001.
