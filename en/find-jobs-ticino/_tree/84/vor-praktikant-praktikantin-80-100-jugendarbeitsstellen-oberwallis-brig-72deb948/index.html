The youth work centre BRIGLINA is committed to open youth work in the district of Brig and offers a dynamic environment in which young people are supported and accompanied. The youth meeting place and visiting activities in the region are just as much a task as the development and implementation of projects on current youth topics.

Your profile: ###

  • You like working with young people and bringing your own ideas
  • You have basic knowledge of social media
  • You are flexible, open and ready to work even in the evening or on weekends
  • You work reliably and can organize well
  • You want to gain experience in the social field and take responsibility
  • Completed vocational training, secondary education or students at tertiary level (FH/HF)

What we offer: ###
