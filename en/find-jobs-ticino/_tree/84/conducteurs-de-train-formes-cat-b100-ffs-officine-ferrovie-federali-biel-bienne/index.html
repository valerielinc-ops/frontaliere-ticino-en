Trained train drivers: in Cat. B100 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Trained train drivers: in Cat. B100 Biel/Bienne, 01.12.2026 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

At SBB Cargo, you are at the heart of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 185,000 tonnes a day, relieving the roads of 15,000 truck trips a day and the environment of 460,000 tonnes of CO2 a year. An innovative working environment in which you and your teammates transport the goods of SBB.You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid for, for example, an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a train driver: in category B100 Cargo, you travel regionally at a speed of up to 100 km/h and deliver freight wagons to customers and pick them up. This makes you an important economic contribution.
  • You take over the head of the train and the responsibility for the safety and punctuality of freight trains in the busy rail network.
  • Your high level of independence helps you to supply the construction sites with the necessary materials on time.
  • You manage complex shunting movements, act independently, collect commercial and operational data in digital systems and ensure customer contact.

You bring that with you.

  • For this demanding work you have to work conscientiously, reliably, in a team and ready in 24-hour shifts.
  • You are physically and psychologically resilient and can act consciously and correctly with your own responsible work.
  • You have successfully completed the training as a B100 train driver according to the criteria of the BAV. You are ready to acquire the necessary skills for the complete execution of the transport order (radio remote control, VOT, EWC).
  • In the language of the training location, you are sure to negotiate both verbally and in writing (level C1), and have a knowledge of French (level A1).

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This post is in requirement level G, regional allowance level No regional allowance. More about wages and benefits.
