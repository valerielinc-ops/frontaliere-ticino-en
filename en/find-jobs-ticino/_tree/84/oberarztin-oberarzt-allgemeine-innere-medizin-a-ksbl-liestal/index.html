Full description

Entry as of 1 January 2027 or by appointment · Bruderholz, Liestal, Internal Medicine · Job reference 13484 · Permanent employment

Tasks

  • Varied clinical activities in an open and experienced team with a friendly working atmosphere at the KSBL Universitary Centre for Internal Medicine, primarily at the Liestal site and, as required, at the Bruderholz site
  • Clinical support for inpatient general internist patients with a broad spectrum of diagnosis
  • Supervision and teaching of doctors in further education and students
  • Participation in the service system in the stations and in the emergency department
  • Optional participation in research projects
  • Performing POCUS sonographies in clinical everyday life

Requirements

  • Specialist title in General Internal Medicine (or foreseeable acquisition)
  • Sound knowledge in internal medicine as well as experience in emergency medicine and POCUS emergency onography
  • Empathetic, committed personality with high service and patient orientation
  • Enjoying the teaching and further development of young colleagues
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional and interdisciplinary cooperation

Benefits

All-round care: discounted day care on site, full parenting allowances, paid short absences in case of emergency care

Area: Internal medicine

Employment level: 60-100 %

KSBL Kantonsspital Baselland — Orten Bruderholz, Liestal, Laufen. ~4'500 employees at 3 locations in the Basel region.
