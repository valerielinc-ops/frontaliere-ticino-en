Full description

This place of work includes a cafeteria. The meeting place in Zurich creates an inviting meeting place for students and external persons. Our team inspires an average of 690 guests here from Monday to Friday with a wide range of products - from daily changing menus to a fresh salad buffet to regional and fairly produced dishes. Are you our new team member who will support us as Head of Gastronomy as of February 1, 2027 or by agreement? Do you want to take on responsibility where education, encounters and hospitality meet? We are looking for you as Head of Gastronomy for the gastronomy businesses of the Zurich University of Teacher Education. Together with your team, you will ensure a diverse gastronomic offer, inspiring guest experiences and manage several business locations with a focus on quality, profitability and lived host culture. Your task menu:

  • Management and organisation of four cluster companies with a total of around 30 employees
  • Perception of the host role and first contact person for the clients on site
  • Ensuring a smooth service process as well as active cooperation on the front line
  • Planning and further development of an inspiring, varied range of products and suitable sales promotion measures
  • Conducting sales and negotiation talks
  • Responsibility for the planning, organisation and implementation of catering
  • Preparation of the annual budget together with the supervisor and ensuring budget compliance
  • Completion of administrative tasks in the areas of purchasing, accounting, and people and culture
  • Recruitment of new employees in cooperation with the Department of People and Culture
  • Review of operating costs and key figures as well as derivation and implementation of targeted measures
  • Ensuring compliance with ZFV quality and service standards
  • Implementation of hygiene and occupational safety guidelines and monitoring of compliance
  • Promotion of sustainable day-to-day operations through careful handling of food, consumables and consumables as well as equipment and machines Your recipe for success:
  • Several years of experience in operations management in community or system catering as well as management experience with teams of 20-30 employees
  • Completed training EFZ in the gastronomy or hotel industry
  • Completion of a hotel management school or comparable further training is an advantage
  • Several years of experience in the event, banquet or catering business are mandatory
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, business know-how and adept handling of budgeting, cost control and key figures
  • Strong leadership skills as well as the ability to build, promote and inspire a high-performing team for common goals
  • Clear, appreciative communication as well as joy in empowering people and bringing different perspectives together
  • Structured, quality-conscious and solution-oriented way of working as well as flexibility, resilience and enjoyment of continuous development
  • Very good German and good written and spoken English skills as well as sound IT skills What we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that is good for you. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of between CHF 85,000 and CHF 100,000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary).
