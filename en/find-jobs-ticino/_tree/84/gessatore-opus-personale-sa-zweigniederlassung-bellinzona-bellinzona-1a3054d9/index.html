Full description

The employer is looking for one

Gessatore (marked)

Mansions

  • surface preparation: Cleaning, preliminary treatment and preparation of supports (internal and external walls, ceilings)
  • plaster application: Coating and finishing plastering (by hand or through plastering machines)
  • shaving and finishing: Application of shaves, stucco and veils to get ready surfaces for paint
  • thermal and acoustic insulation: Pose of thermal coats and restoration of ammalorate facades
  • wall mounting and plasterboard ceilings
  • use and maintenance of tools: Safe use of construction equipment (coppers, bridges, scaffolding, shooting) and proper maintenance
  • compliance with safety standards: Unscrupulous compliance with current health and safety regulations on workplaces

Requirements

  • with AFC certificate
  • experience 1-3 years in the role of plasterer/rapper within construction sites
  • technical competence: Excellent knowledge of the different building materials (calce, cement, chalk, premixed plasters, mineral and synthetic rasants)
  • manual capacity: High precision in the use of tools of the craft (fractazzi, cazzuole, staggie, scrapers)
  • autonomy: Ability to autonomously manage assigned work ensuring quality timing and standards
  • good knowledge of Italian
  • licence to conduct cat. B
  • self-employed person

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
