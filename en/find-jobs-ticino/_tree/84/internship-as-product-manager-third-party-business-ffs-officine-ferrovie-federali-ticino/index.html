SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. A lively working environment at the interface of product management, sales and innovation awaits you. You will work closely with internal and external partners, gain insights into strategy, implementation and market mechanics, and help generate new returns for SBB. A collegial and active team supports you, and you develop both professionally and personally. You can move that.

  • In the third-party business team, you support operational and strategic tasks with a focus on digitalization and the further development of products.
  • You participate in negotiations on products, processes and business cases with internal and external partners.
  • You analyze market and product information and derive ideas for new, innovative offers along the extended mobility chain.
  • You support in the monitoring and control of existing products in the submarkets of the third-party business (e.g. change, insurance, lockers, SBB-Shop, tourism, Swiss Bankers and others).
  • After consultation, you independently take on product management tasks in a submarket and are responsible for defined work topics.
  • You will actively build and expand your network and gain in-depth insights into the strategic and operational tasks of third-party product management.

You bring that with you.

  • You bring communication, negotiating skills and success orientation.
  • Reliability and responsibility as well as a structured time and work management distinguish you.
  • You have already gained experience in dealing with various stakeholders such as external partners, sales management, finance, IT development, sales space, sales control center and others.
  • Several years of professional experience in the serviced sales of SBB (BEV) is required.
  • You have completed an apprenticeship as a retail specialist: public transport consultancy or a commercial apprenticeship in the travel or mobility industry. An ongoing or completed further education in the field of business administration or marketing is advantageous.
  • You master German at least at level C1. Knowledge of French and English at level B1 is an advantage.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
