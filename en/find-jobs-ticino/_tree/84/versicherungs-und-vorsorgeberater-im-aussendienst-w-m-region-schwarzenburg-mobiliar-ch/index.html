This is what awaits you

You want to get out to the people, understand their situations and find suitable solutions? Then you are right with us.

For our general agency Bern-West, we are looking for a personality in Schwarzenburg who takes responsibility, lives close to customers and is present in the market. The position is created due to the pleasing growth of our agency in Schwarzenburg.

This is what they do with us

They look after and develop a portfolio with private and corporate customers: inside.

They conduct consultations, identify needs and show understandable solutions.

You are close to your customers, maintain your network and develop existing and new contacts.

This is what you bring with you

  • Sales experience is beneficial

• Open and communicative personality with pleasure in dealing with people • Joy of advice, sales and personal contact • High willingness to learn and motivation for personal development • Perseverance, commitment and great diligence • Independent and structured working method • Good organisational capacity and personal responsibility

  • Rest in challenging situations

Working with us

You take on a task with a lot of personal responsibility and good support. Sales support, sales management and the team of the general agency Bern-West are at your side in everyday life.

You get clear structures, short paths, freedom for your work and the support of a strong brand with a sympathetic image.

Whether career changers: in or insurance professional, with us you will be thoroughly trained and carefully introduced to the task. In order to perform this function, the Insurance Supervision Act requires the conclusion of an insurance intermediary/in VBV, Profil Allbranche. If this is not available, we are happy to support with tailor-made internal training. Performance, reliability and good cooperation are important to us. We work in a committed team, celebrate successes together and speak openly with each other.
