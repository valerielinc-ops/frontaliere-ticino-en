Bobst Italia Spa, site of San Giorgio Monferrato (AL) is looking for a s upplier quality engineer who will be responsible for quality assurance of suppliers. It will ensure that supplied suppliers and components/services comply with product quality and process standards. He will work closely with the purchasing team, R&D, production and suppliers to monitor, evaluate and continuously improve performance throughout the supply chain. Main responsibilities: Manage quality issues related to the components provided by the supplier.

Analyze and solve non-compliance of supply through structured problem solving methods.

Coordinate containment activities and define corrective and preventive actions with suppliers.

Monitor suppliers quality KPIs (PPM, non-quality costs, response times, performance audit).

Perform qualification, process and monitoring audits with suppliers.

Support the selection and qualification of new suppliers in collaboration with Procurement and Operations.

Validate manufacturing processes of suppliers, process changes and introduction of new components.

Participating in industrialization and development projects new products ensuring compliance with the quality requirements of the purchased components.
