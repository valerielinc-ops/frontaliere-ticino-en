Full description

This is what awaits you

In this function, you are the central point of contact for our customers and support our insurance and pension consultants in all sales processes. With your work you contribute significantly to a high quality of service and confidence in the furniture.

This is what you do with us

The core of your function is the competent advice and support of our customers on the phone, by mail and at the counter.

In addition, you support our insurance and pension advisors administratively and organizationally. You create offers, process applications and coordinate appointments. This ensures an efficient and smooth handling of customer business.

This is what you bring with you

  • Commercial basic education or insurance education completed
  • Professional experience in the insurance industry
  • Completed training «Insurance intermediary/in VBV Allbranchen»
  • Strong customer and service orientation
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Teamwork and communicative competence
  • Flowing knowledge of German, other languages are an advantage

Working with us

At the Balsthal General Agency, you work in an experienced team of eight employees. Short decision-making paths, close cooperation and a collegial working atmosphere shape our everyday life.

We offer you a careful training, flexible working hours with fixed attendance days, the attractive employment conditions and further training opportunities of the furniture.

For the exercise of this function, according to the Insurance Supervision Act, the insurance intermediary/in VBV, Profile Allbranchen, is required.

Job starts on 1 July 2027 or by agreement.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Solothurn?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Solothurn's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Solothurn's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
