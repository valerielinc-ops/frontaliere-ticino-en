What you do

  • You actively support the sales force throughout the sales process and thus contribute to joint success
  • As a central contact person, you will answer professional and technical questions in the areas of non-life private customers, non-life corporate customers, individual lives, accident, sickness and BVG
  • You support our private and corporate customers by telephone, in writing and personally with great competence and commitment
  • With a sense of individual needs, you develop customized concepts and solutions
  • Together with the team you actively shape the market development and sales promotion
  • You accompany our learners in everyday work and promote their professional and personal development.

What you bring

  • Commercial insurance training and/or several years of experience with an all-sector insurer
  • Good knowledge of corporate banking is an advantage
  • Enjoying direct customer contact and a pronounced service orientation
  • Sales-oriented thinking and enthusiasm for the development of individual customer solutions
  • Efficient, solution-oriented cooperation with the field service and relevant departments
