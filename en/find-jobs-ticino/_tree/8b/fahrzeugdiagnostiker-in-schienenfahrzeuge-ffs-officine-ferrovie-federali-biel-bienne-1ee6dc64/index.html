Full description

Vehicle diagnosticians: in rail vehicles 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Vehicle diagnosticians: in rail vehicles Biel/Bienne, 01.02.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Help ensure that our trains run safely, on time and in perfect condition through Switzerland. In this function, you are significantly involved in ensuring that the trains run and can be quickly put back into service in the event of disruptions. This includes that you also perform night and weekend shifts in our 7x24 hour operation. In order to gain the necessary specialist knowledge in this function, we train you internally for about 9 months, at full wage. More about the profession as a diagnostician: at SBB you can find under this link. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even in the case of irregular working hours. Night and weekend work is also paid for with us

  • e.g. with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. You will also receive time allowances. This means you have more free time than employees without night and weekend assignments. Our other benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad
  • also for the family
  • can be found on our website under « Benefits ».

You can move that.

  • As a vehicle diagnostician, you will make fault diagnoses on the rolling stock of passengers using modern diagnostic devices during operation and during downtimes and thus make an important contribution to the operation of SBB.
  • With your commitment and your work, you also contribute significantly to the safety of our company.
  • You arrange for the quick and sustainable elimination of the problems or carry out the repairs independently on site and are responsible for the quality.
  • You support the operation in the area of increasing the availability of trains and minimizing disruption times.
  • Taking on site-specific tasks is also part of your varied workday.

You bring that with you.

  • You like to learn and are ready to complete a subject-specific training, which we finance.
  • Strong analytical skills, a quick comprehension, networked thinking and decision-making are among your strengths.
  • For this position, you bring a completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ in a technical or electronic direction, preferably as a mechatronics technician:in, automotive diagnostician:in, car, agricultural machinery, truck mechanic:in, electrician:in, automatic technician:in, heating installer:in with experience or comparable basic training in the field of electromechanics and you want to move Switzerland with us.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (min. C1) as well as good IT user knowledge round off your profile, knowledge of French is an advantage.
  • You are open for night and shift work and have the driver’s license Kat. B.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This job is in requirement level F, regional allowance level 0. More about wages and benefits.
