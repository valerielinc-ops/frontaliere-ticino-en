The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their businesses. Together with the brands Volg, TopShop and AGROLA, the LANDI cooperatives also supply the population in rural areas of Switzerland with food, everyday necessities and energy.For our TopShop in Steg, we are looking for a motivated and independent person to start immediately or by agreement.

These tasks will excite you

  • Stock management and replenishing shelves and coolers, including checking freshness
  • Baking bread and preparing lunch offerings
  • Ensuring tidiness and cleanliness in the shop, warehouse and at the service station

We look forward to candidates who match this profile

  • Flexibility and resilience are among your strengths

Discover your benefits

  • Training and professional development: We invest in the development of our employees. A wide range of internal training opportunities is available to everyone, and we support external continuing education.
  • Attractive vacation policy: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. From the 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks and from the 60. Year, even 7 weeks. In addition, it is possible to purchase additional vacation days.
  • Family time: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent receives 3 weeks of family leave at full pay.
  • Insurance benefits: We provide our employees with above-average social security and pension benefits and offer special terms on supplementary insurance with various health insurers for their entire family.
  • Discounts and special offers: Our employees benefit, among other things, from discounts when shopping and refueling, credits on REKA-Geld, and discounted mobile subscriptions for themselves and their families.
