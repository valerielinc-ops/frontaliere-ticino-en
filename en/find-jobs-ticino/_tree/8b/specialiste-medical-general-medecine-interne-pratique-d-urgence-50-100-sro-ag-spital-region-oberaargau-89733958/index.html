Spital Langenthal Printing place Post as PDF Are you looking for a job where you can combine family care with the infrastructure of a modern hospital? Then the emergency practice of SRO AG in the Langenthal hospital offers you a clinically versatile challenge in a collegial and interprofessional environment. We are looking for an emergency practice immediately or by agreement

They do it with us.

In the emergency practice, you take care of outpatient emergency patients independently in the entire general medical spectrum. The emergency practice takes care of pre-registered and walk-in patients in the premises of the SRO Emergency Response Center. They can use the infrastructure of a regional hospital and work closely with the specialists of the emergency center.

This is what they bring with them.

They have specialist training in general internal medicine (FMH or MEBEKO-approved) and ideally bring experience from family care or emergency medicine. They are willing to make decisions, enjoy working in a dynamic clinical setting and appreciate interdisciplinary collaboration. Very good knowledge of German in word and writing is required. In addition, you bring personal commitment and pleasure in the profession. They enjoy working in a well-functioning team and are ready to help develop the practice and the staff. Their advantages
