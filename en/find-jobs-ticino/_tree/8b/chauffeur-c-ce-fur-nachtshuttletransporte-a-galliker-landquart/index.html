Full description

Chauffeur C / CE for night shuttle transport (a) For the daily supply of customers in the extensive valleys of the canton of Graubünden we have an efficient delivery platform in Landquart. With modern vehicle park, we deliver the chilled and frozen food and normal goods professionally and on time. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a chauffeur or a chauffeuse cat. C/CE, immediately or by agreement, the night shuttle transports between Landquart and Altishofen. Task area

  • Start of work at 17.00
  • Implementation of shuttle transports by trailer between Landquart and Altishofen
  • Unloading of the shuttle and assistance in loading the platforms
  • Replacement of our platform employee (entry and outsourcing, general). Cleaning up, support of our employee maintenance

Requirement profile

  • Driving licence C or CE
  • Reliable, independent and dutiful
  • Good knowledge of the German language in word and writing
  • Professional appearance and pleasure in customer contact

Offer

  • A future-oriented company
  • top modern infrastructure
  • A cool, motivated and collaborative team
