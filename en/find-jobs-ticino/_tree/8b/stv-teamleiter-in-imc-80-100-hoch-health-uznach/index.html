Stv. team leader/in IMC 80-100 %

Intermediate Care

Place of work Uznach

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Support the team management in all tasks related to the organization of the department as well as cooperation in direct care
  • Ensuring and developing the station-related care and documentation quality
  • Implementation of a care organization system based on the primary care on the IMC as well as planning, organizing and ensuring a smooth process with the given personnel key
  • Active participation in achieving corporate goals
  • Participation in various project groups for the future-proof design of the department

What you bring for this job

  • Training for/for the Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH with SRK recognition and monitoring course IMC or IPS intensive care training NDS
  • Leadership training of advantage or willingness to complete it
  • Strong social skills, dialogue and motivation as well as enjoyment of interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • Enjoyment of handling medical-technical devices and systems
  • Willingness to work in all shifts

Our offer to you

  • Responsible activity, a versatile area of responsibility and design possibilities to contribute your ideas
  • Varied challenges in a dynamic working environment
  • Collaboration in a motivated and collegial team
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
  • Various offers for employees in the field of health promotion, job and family, mobility, various benefits, etc.
