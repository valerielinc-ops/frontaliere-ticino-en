Full description

Purchasing Manager (M/F), 80% Deployed at several sites, the Lavigny Institution consists of a neurological hospital with outpatient consultations at two sites, a housing department for adults with disabilities, intellectual impairment or physical problems, a specialised school and a workshop and vocational training sector. From 1 January 2027, for our Lavigny site, we are looking for:

Purchasing Manager (M/F)

Attached to the Directorate of the Logistics Department, you are the institution's purchasing authority. You ensure administrative coordination of purchases, follow-up of suppliers and contracts, as well as administrative control of investments. Through your expertise and your sense of service, you accompany the various services in their acquisition processes and ensure that the procedures and conditions negotiated are respected.

Your missions As a privileged partner of the Institution's services, your main responsibilities are:

  • Procurement coordination: ensuring the role of procurement focal point, accompanying services in their requests and coordinating institutional orders in connection with central purchasing centres and referenced suppliers
  • Vendor management and negotiation: establishing bid comparisons, negotiating prices and contractual terms within the framework of your delegations, ensuring the reference of suppliers and monitoring of framework contracts and their deadlines
  • Order and invoice tracking: coordination of orders, control of deliveries, processing of claims and verification of invoices according to institutional procedures
  • Investment monitoring: administrative monitoring of validated investments, maintenance of dashboards, financial data and equipment and equipment guarantees
  • Compliance and continuous improvement: ensuring compliance with procurement procedures and applicable legislation, including public procurement, and contributing to improved monitoring processes and tools
  • Cross-cutting administrative support: support the Logistics Department, including monitoring sponsorship contracts, negotiating employee benefits and coordinating files with relevant departments

You work closely with the various departments of the Institution, the Department of Finance, the Logistics Secretariat, suppliers and purchasing centres.

Your profile

  • You have an EWC or training deemed equivalent
  • You benefit from proven experience in procurement, procurement or negotiation with suppliers
  • Additional procurement training is an advantage. The Federal Patent of Procurement Specialist/Supply or Procurement Specialist is an additional asset
  • You have good knowledge of the purchasing processes and, ideally, the regulation of public procurement
  • You master office tools and are comfortable with data processing, tracking tables and administrative management tools.

Your assets

  • Organized and rigorous, you know how to manage priorities and ensure reliable monitoring of your files
  • With a good spirit of analysis, you are discerning and able to identify situations requiring arbitration
  • Your sense of negotiation and relationship ease allow you to develop constructive relationships with your internal and external interlocutors
  • Oriented by solutions and service, you appreciate the cross-cutting work and know how to accompany the various services with pragmatism
  • Self-sustaining and reliable, you are the driving force behind process optimization and resource efficiency.

What we offer you

  • A varied cross-sectional function at the heart of the institution's activities as well as real autonomy in the organisation and management of your activities
  • A stimulating working environment based on the collaboration and values of the Institution
  • The social benefits of a large institution (staff restaurant, cafeteria and employee prices, free parking, public transport costs, etc.)
  • Flexible working hours, wages and social benefits in line with the function and standards of the CLC in the Vaudian public health sector.
