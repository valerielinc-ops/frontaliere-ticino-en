Pemsa has been a reference point in Switzerland for more than 35 years in the recruitment, management and commissioning of personnel specialized in crafts applied to building, civil engineering, electricity, building technology and industry. Our strength lies not only in the deep technical knowledge of the reference sectors, but above all in the ability to select highly qualified profiles that meet the specific needs of our customers. To strengthen our team, we are looking for candidates with motivation and solid skills to cover key roles in construction and transport sectors.

Mission

  • Produce commercial vehicles and heavy vehicles in compliance with Swiss regulations.
  • Perform efficient and safe transport in urban and extra-urban areas, ensuring punctuality in deliveries.
  • Maintain the vehicle in optimal conditions, carrying out daily checks and promptly reporting any anomalies.
  • Collaborate actively with the logistics team to optimize the routes and improve customer service.
  • Ensure compliance with occupational safety standards and road legislation specific to the transport sector.

Profile

  • Possession of category C driving licence, indispensable for the management of heavy commercial vehicles in Switzerland.
  • CE driving licence for combined vehicles (tractor and trailer), required for transport of larger goods and weight.
  • Knowledge of Italian language at B2 level, essential for effective communication within the company and with customers.
  • Proven experience in professional driving in compliance with Swiss regulations on freight transport.
  • Accuracy, reliability and ability to independently manage your vehicle and delivery times.
  • Availability to work on shifts and long-range, in compliance with Swiss regulations on work and driving time.
