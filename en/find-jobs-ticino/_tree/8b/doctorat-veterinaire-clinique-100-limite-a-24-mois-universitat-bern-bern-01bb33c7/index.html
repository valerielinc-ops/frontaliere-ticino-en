We are looking for motivated veterinarians who want to gain their first professional experience and combine a clinical activity with scientific work in the field of MRI and/or CT diagnostics. About us

The Department of Clinical Radiology of the Vetsuisse Faculty Bern is a dedicated team of 24 employees, consisting of veterinary radiologists and radiologists as well as specialists in medical-technical radiology, veterinary practice assistants and doctoral students. We offer the full spectrum of modern imaging diagnostics. In addition to the diagnostic care and advice of our clinic-internal and external partners, we are involved in teaching and research.
