Full description

Every day, thousands of travellers rely on the route around Frutigen and the Lötschberg. What seems natural to them is the result of carefully planned and implemented infrastructure projects. With your know-how, you will shape the road of tomorrow and help BLS to remain reliably on the road in the future.

This is waiting for you

  • You carry out complex construction projects with a focus on roadways and participate in the planning of the execution
  • Coordinating railway technology together with specialist departments
  • Taking responsibility for costs, quality and deadlines in railway construction
  • Plan personnel, machines and intervals in advance and efficiently
  • Actively shaping safety on construction sites and consistently implementing standards

What you bring to the table

  • Further training as a construction foreman, HF technician or equivalent qualification
  • Technical understanding in the construction or infrastructure sector, experience in the railway environment is an advantage
  • Reliably coordinate different stakeholder groups with a clear appearance
  • Independent, structured way of working and a high sense of responsibility for quality, deadlines and safety
  • Stylistically confident written and spoken German skills
  • Driving licence Cat. B

Your benefits – here's what you can look forward to

  • Get a GA travelcard 2. Class, a GA travelcard 1. Class or a half-fare card.
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether in the home office or in the office.
  • Organize your working hours freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy five to ten additional vacation days per year
  • Financial support for external training courses as well as an exciting range of internal learning and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. What's more, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
