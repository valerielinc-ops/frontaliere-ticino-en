Full description

Who we are

Rapelli has represented the Ticino charcuterie tradition since 1929 and is recognized for high quality products made with selected ingredients, respecting tradition and with constant attention to innovation. With the Rapelli and Ticinella brands, the company is an ambassador of Ticino taste throughout Switzerland. Rapelli is part of the ORIOR Group, a Swiss company listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange and specialized in the production and marketing of premium food specialties.

Position sought

In a context of growth and organizational evolution, we are strengthening our Stabio Production team and we are looking for a

Production Intermediate Manager, 100% (m/f/d)

Role description

This is a full-time, on-site role as Intermediate Production Manager at Rapelli SA in Stabio. The role involves overseeing production activities, managing production schedules, ensuring compliance with quality standards, coordinating teams, and optimizing production processes to achieve business goals.

Liability

  • Ensuring the operational functioning of the Area
  • Ensure that all activities carried out under the framework are carried out in accordance with internal principles and provisions and on the basis of objectives.
  • Ensure that all activities carried out in the field are carried out safely as defined.
  • Guarantee the degree of Quality of the products.
  • Coordinate and control compliance with the weekly production plan;
  • Manage, motivate and develop the human resources available to the work area.
  • Regularly inform and provide the necessary support to direct and indirect subordinate collaborators;
  • Ensure the exceeding of the degree of Supply defined by the company and guarantee compliance with and continuous improvement of the cost structure of the products.
  • Propose and implement process improvements.
  • Ensure the implementation of the corporate strategy defined by management;
  • Ensure that the use of materials and ingredients in products corresponds to the predefined standard and guarantee the final quality of the products;
  • Collaborates in the preparation of the annual MDO budget for the respective area
  • Contribute to the development and maintenance of a corporate culture oriented towards open communication, team collaboration, respect for infrastructure, plants, raw materials, accessories and people, positively influencing the good working climate.
  • Actively participate in projects of corporate interest;
  • Organization and operational management of the departments and the area of competence, the working climate, the safety of its collaborators, professional training and excellent information for staff and superiors;
  • Compliance with the Budget of the Area of competence
  • Profile
  • Age 35-40 years
  • Education: Bachelor's degree (food technology, natural sciences, chemistry, engineering...).
  • Work experience: 5-10 years of experience in the processing industry, of which at least 2/3 years in the food or pharmaceutical sector.
  • Experience in leading a group of at least 20 people
  • Good sensitivity to hygiene issues (flows of goods and personnel, HACCP, etc.).
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Decisive and authoritative character
  • Good learning and adaptation skills (open to change, proactive).
  • Flexible working hours (shifts if necessary)
  • Knowledge of basic office packages
  • Languages: English (German plus)
  • SAP Knowledge (Plus)

Skills

  • Ability to work independently
  • At least 5 years' experience in a similar role in structured companies in the food or pharmaceutical sector

What we offer

  • A varied and stimulating function within an evolving business reality
  • Direct collaboration with the Head of Production
  • Involvement in cross-cutting projects and organizational development initiatives
  • Attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits
