Full description

On behalf of a Swiss industrial company renowned for its know-how and high-precision automation solutions, we are looking for a Ingénieur(e) \- Constructor(s) to strengthen its technical team.

Manufacturer

Your responsibilities

  • Ensure the implementation and development of tailor-made client projects, in close collaboration with the Project Leader.
  • Actively participate in the technical monitoring and development of machines and sub-assemblies during the assembly and assembly phase at the workshop.
  • Ensure technical compliance, feasibility and optimization of solutions designed.

Your profile

  • ES engineering or technician training in mechanics, microtechnics or equivalent.
  • Minimum 5 years of practical experience in a similar industrial function, including at least 2 years in robotics and automation.
  • Excellent mastery of CAD tools (specifically SolidWorks) as well as common office tools.
  • Ability to manage and coordinate technology.
  • Rigor, precision, method and autonomy in the organization of your work.
  • Initiative, adaptability, flexibility and a great sense of responsibility.
  • Mastery of French; good knowledge in German and/or English is an important asset.

What we offer you

  • The opportunity to work on various and technologically stimulating projects.
  • A dynamic working environment within a passionate and motivated multidisciplinary team.
  • Continuing training programs to support your professional development.
  • Attractive conditions of engagement and a salary in line with your skills and experience.
