Full description

The newly created discharge prosecutor’s office conducts criminal investigations against adults for the entire cantonal area of Zurich. As of February 1, 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a motivated personality as a prosecutor with jumper function (80-100%) to complement our dedicated team in Department A. These are your tasks

  • Conduct and conduct of criminal investigations
  • Providing temporary representation services to the regional public prosecutor’s offices throughout the Canton of Zurich and, associated with this, providing regular care services
  • Planning of investigations, gathering of evidence, conducting examinations, obtaining expert opinions, enforcement of coercive measures
  • Representation of the prosecution before district court and in cantonal appeal proceedings before high court, as well as conduct of follow-up proceedings
  • Management, instruction and supervision of law firm staff
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with police and other offices
  • They are locally flexible and have very good knowledge of criminal and criminal procedure law as well as practical court experience and have completed the CAS Forensics course
  • From a formal point of view, you have the elective certificate for prosecutors in the Canton of Zurich
  • They conduct investigations systematically and efficiently, with decision-making, assertiveness and high willingness to prosecute.
  • They are characterised by teamwork, resilience even in demanding situations, diplomatic skills and a high level of social competence and can contribute these strengths to interdisciplinary cooperation. We offer this
  • A varied and meaningful area of responsibility in a collegial, interdisciplinary environment
  • You have a competent procedural assistance at your side
  • Employment in wage class 24 and offers for further professional development
  • A modern workplace in the middle of the city of Zurich with good connections to public transport For further information, see Nathalie Godan Gacesa, Head of Department Abt. A (nathalie.godan@ji.zh.ch) is available. Information about the Public Prosecutor’s Office of the Canton of Zurich can be found at www.zh.ch/sta. The Zurich Public Prosecutor’s Office (STA.ZH) is responsible for prosecuting crimes committed by adults in the Canton of Zurich. It employs around 500 people in nine offices and the Higher Public Prosecutor’s Office. The discharge prosecutor’s office of the Canton of Zurich contributes to a sustainable settlement of cases among the 5 regional prosecutors’ offices. She handles criminal proceedings of mass delinquency, which are conducted by commercial personnel, and coordinates assignments of prosecutors with a Springer function at the regional public prosecutor’s offices. In the middle of life: The Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs of the Canton of Zurich covers a wide range of tasks. The management thus contributes to the functioning of the canton in important areas.
