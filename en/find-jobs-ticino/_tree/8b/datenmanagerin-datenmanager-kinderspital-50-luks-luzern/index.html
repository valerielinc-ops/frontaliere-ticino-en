Full description

The Children's Hospital Central Switzerland of the Lucerne Cantonal Hospital (LUKS) is one of the largest children's hospitals in Switzerland and serves the entire region of Central Switzerland at the highest level. Our employees work around the clock to ensure that children, young people and their relatives are measurably better off. LUKS, with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen, is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. >For our team we are looking for 1. September 2026 temporary for 1 year reinforcement.

Tasks

  • You are the central contact person for investigators, sponsors, clinical research organizations, authorities, laboratories and the Swiss Childhood Cancer Registry (SKKR).
  • You will be responsible for preparing and accompanying pre-study visits, initiations, monitor visits, audits and Swissmedic inspections.
  • They are responsible for organizing and participating in internal training on study protocols and archive and manage the investigator folders.
  • You provide administrative support and documentation as part of clinical trials

Requirements

  • You have completed training in the medical field, for example as a medical documentary, nurse or medical assistant (MPA), ideally with knowledge of medical terminology
  • They are characterized by an independent, structured and quality-conscious working method and have a high IT affinity as well as sound knowledge in the MS Office applications (Outlook, Word, Excel and PowerPoint)
  • You are a flexible and team-oriented personality, open to change and engaged in a dynamic working environment
  • Ideally, you have a ICH-GCP Basic Course Certificate or are ready to purchase it after starting your job.

We offer

  • You can expect a wide range of tasks with a lot of independence in the paediatric oncology center of Central Switzerland
  • We offer you timely employment conditions as well as individually coordinated training and further education opportunities
  • Your working hours are regular and therefore well plannable
  • You benefit from a great deal of existing know-how at Switzerland’s largest center hospital
