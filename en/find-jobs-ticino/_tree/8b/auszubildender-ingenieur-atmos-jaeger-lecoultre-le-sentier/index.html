Reference code: JR133470 Since 1833, drive by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressly expressed its inventiveness through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication. YOUR MISSION: The Atmos clock is a masterpiece of technique and an absolute icon of our House. To ensure its sustainability and optimize its manufacturing costs, we are launching a manufacturing improvement project. As an Engineer Trainee, you will join our industrialization team to audit our traditional manufacturing process and actively transition our key components to lead-free substitution materials. HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT? Audit and analyse the existing: Mapping our current manufacturing processes, in-depth analysis of our component nomenclatures and study the effectiveness of Atmos' historical operating ranges. Conduct the "Plomb-Free" transition: Methodically identify all components containing lead, conduct applied research on advanced substitution materials and propose sustainable technical alternatives. Innovate on manufacturing processes: Propose and define new modern manufacturing processes adapted to our needs. Optimize polishing of parts: Study and design technical solutions to facilitate delicate manual polishing of Atmos components, while ensuring irreproachable aesthetic quality. HOW ARE YOU TO BE US? Training: Student in master's degree in engineering (mechanical engineering, micromechanics, materials science or industrial engineering) in search of a 6-month term of study. Technical skills: Solid baggage in mechanical design, metal science, understanding precision manufacturing processes and reading technical drawings. WHAT WE OFFER: The rare opportunity to work directly on Atmos, an exceptional product unique in the world of Haute Horlogerie. A transversal project with high technological value, combining material engineering, production methods and R&D. Immersion in a prestigious Manufacture combining cutting-edge craftsmanship and modern industrial technologies.
