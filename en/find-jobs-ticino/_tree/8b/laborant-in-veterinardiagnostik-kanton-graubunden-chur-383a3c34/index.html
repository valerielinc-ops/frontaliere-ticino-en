Laboratory in Veterinary Diagnostics

Veterinary and Food Office \| DVS \| 80\-100% \| Chur

Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the cantonal administration Graubünden together with us.

From the PCR to the dissection table – with us, no day is like the other! In our laboratory for veterinary diagnostics, we make an important contribution to animal health and animal disease control in the cantons of Graubünden and Glarus and beyond.

ELISA, molecular biology, microbiology, parasitology and pathology – with us you do not always do the same, but are everywhere. In our small, experienced team, you can expect varied tasks, a lot of personal responsibility and a collegial working environment.

We are looking for a veterinary and food office as of 1\.2027 or by agreement a laboratory assistant in veterinary diagnostics.

Your responsibilities

  • Conduct and evaluate diagnostic studies in serology (ELISA), molecular biology (PCR), microbiology and parasitology
  • Cooperation in pathological\-anatomical investigations and sections
  • Independent processing of samples – from sample receipt to results acquisition
  • Cooperation in the quality assurance and further development of our quality management system
  • Maintenance and maintenance of laboratory equipment
  • Participation in the optimisation and further development of diagnostic methods and laboratory procedures

This is what we offer you

  • Change instead of routine: With us you are not limited to a single specialist area, but work in the entire range of veterinary diagnostics.
  • A small, experienced team: We support each other, maintain an uncomplicated approach and appreciate short decision paths.
  • Responsibility and participation: Your ideas are welcome and you can actively shape our workflows.
  • A meaningful task: With your work you make a direct contribution to animal health and the fight against animal diseases.
  • Development opportunities: We help you expand your expertise and learn new methods.
  • Attractive place to work: Your workplace is located in the Alpine city of Chur – with an urban flair, the mountains on your doorstep and countless possibilities for sports and nature experiences at any time of the year.

That is what we want from you.

  • Completed training as a biology laboratory technician, biomedical analyst, bachelor in biomedical laboratory diagnostics or comparable training
  • Ideally, experience in laboratory diagnostics – but you do not already have to master all our methods
  • An independent, careful and reliable way of working
  • Delight in varied tasks and interest in learning new things
  • Team spirit, flexibility and the willingness to go beyond your own specialist area
  • Interest in veterinary issues

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to shape the future of the canton

Place of work

Veterinary and Food Office Ringstrasse 10 7001 Chur

For technical questions, you are welcome to Dr. med. vet. Zumthor and Dr. med. vet. under the phone number and available.

Application deadline: 06\. December 2026
