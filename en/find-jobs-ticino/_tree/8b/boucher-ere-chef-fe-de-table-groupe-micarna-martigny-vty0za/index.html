Full description

You're passionate about meat trades and you want to take a new step in your career? At Mérat SA, we value craftsmanship, quality and team spirit. As head of the table, you are taking advantage of your butcher's expertise while gradually taking on responsibilities in organizing and accompanying your team.

Luogo di Lavoro: Micarna Group, Mérat AG - Martigny, Rue des Finettes 45, 1920 Martigny

Mansioni

  • Lead, support and train employees of a team of 10 employees
  • Perform meat cutting and preparation according to defined standards
  • Ensuring product quality and compliance with hygiene and food safety standards
  • Organize and coordinate production table activity to ensure quality, timeliness and performance objectives
  • Manage your raw materials, packaging and consumables stocks
  • Anticipating requirements and ensuring the IT controls necessary for the smooth running of production
  • Actively participate in continuous improvement

Requirements

  • Minimum 5 years: Experience confirmed in cutting and processing meat
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Butcher's CFC – Charcutier*ère or equivalent training
  • You combine butcher's know-how and leading fiber
  • You know how to motivate a team or you want to develop your management skills
  • Autonomy, organization and initiative are part of your strengths. You also have a strong team spirit and is passionate about meat
  • You like inventory management and you work hard
  • Dynamic and stress-resistant, you know how to keep the course even in intense moments
  • You have good knowledge of cuts and cutting techniques as well as HACCP and food safety standards
  • Prior experience in a semi-industrial environment in the agri-food sector is an asset
  • French (Good knowledge)
