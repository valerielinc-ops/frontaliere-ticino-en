The Position Your team As Nurse Liaison Manager (NLM), you will become part of our Disease Area Neuroimmunology. In your role as an expert: in the field You actively collaborate with the nurses community and our sales, medical and marketing teams. We see ourselves as a reliable partner for treaters in order to jointly promote the best possible care for patients with multiple sclerosis. Responsibility | This is what awaits you

  • Counselling & training: You inform and train nurses and doctors about the use of B-cell and IL6 therapies, including preparation, implementation and complication management.
  • Organizational consulting: You advise practices and clinics on the optimization of workflow organizations around our neuroimmunology products.
  • Digital solutions: You accompany the implementation of our CE-certified medical products (e.g. Brisa) for therapy support.
  • Event management: You actively plan and design local user meetings, best practice sharing concepts and moderate workshops.
  • Networking: You identify local customer needs and actively contribute your insights to the Disease Area Squad.
  • Cross-functional cooperation: You work closely with all representatives of the Customer Pacing Functions, Medical, Marketing and Patient Partnership areas and support the creation of account plans. Qualifications | This is what you bring
  • Completed training as a health and nurse: in, medical specialist or comparable qualification as well as several years of professional experience (minimum 4 years), ideally in neurology.
  • Experience in the treatment of multiple sclerosis (MS), in particular in dealing with MS patients and experience in the management of practical procedures and the guidance of nurses (practice manager:in)
  • Medical practice: safe handling of vein indwelling cannulas, conducting infusion therapies and complication management.
  • Additional qualification: Knowledge of quality management (QSM) and training as a medical device consultant are advantageous.
  • High social competence, solution-oriented working methods, initiative and team spirit.
  • Safe handling of Google Workspace/MS Office as well as good knowledge of English and a valid driving license. Your application We ask you for the following documents: Current CV.
