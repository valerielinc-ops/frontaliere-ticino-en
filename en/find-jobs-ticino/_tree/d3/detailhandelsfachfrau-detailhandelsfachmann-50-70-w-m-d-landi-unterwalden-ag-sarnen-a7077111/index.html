Discover your benefits

  • Education and continuing education: We invest in the development of our employees. A broad range of internal educational opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external continuing education.
  • Attractive vacation policy: Our vacation policy guarantees all employees at least 5 weeks of vacation. Starting from 50. Year of age, it is 6 weeks and from the 60. Year of age, even 7 weeks. In addition, it is possible to purchase additional vacation days.
  • Family leave: Mothers receive 100 percent of their insured salary during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent enjoys 3 weeks of family leave at full pay.

  • Perks and discounts: Among other things, our employees benefit from discounts when shopping and refueling, credits for REKA-Geld, and discounted mobile plans for themselves and their families.
