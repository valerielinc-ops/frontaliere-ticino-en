Full description

During your apprenticeship, you will discover all the activities related to the cooking profession within our catering service. Accompanied by experienced professionals, you will gradually develop the skills needed to practice your future profession.

Your main missions will be as follows

  • Follow the training plan.
  • Participate in the preparation and production of meals for patients, collaborators and visitors.
  • Apply the culinary techniques taught during training.
  • Observe existing hygiene, food safety and procedures.
  • Collaborate with the hotel and catering teams.
  • Develop your professional and personal knowledge throughout your learning.
  • You are interested in cooking.
  • You are dynamic and motivated.
  • You show respect, courtesy and teamwork.
  • You are eager to learn and progress.
  • You have a satisfactory level of education.
  • You are organized and conscientious in your work.
  • You appreciate practical work and dynamic environments.

During your apprenticeship, you will have the opportunity to discover different fields, gradually develop your autonomy and build a solid professional experience.

We offer you

  • A variety of training in institutions recognized and close to their patients.
  • The opportunity to discover the professional world in a stimulating and constantly evolving environment.
  • Personalized support throughout your learning by committed and experienced trainers.
  • Conditions that encourage learning, progressive autonomy and the development of your skills.
  • The possibility of making a concrete contribution to the quality of the services offered to the population of the region.
