Full description

The possibility

We are looking for a committed personality as a Transport, Trade & Logistics Specialist Export who actively shapes and optimizes our international transport processes. In this role, you will be responsible for organizing and supervising global air, sea, road and courier transport. They work closely with carriers and internal stakeholders to ensure smooth handling.

You can do that.

  • Organization and monitoring of worldwide transport (air, sea, truck, courier)
  • Contracting, preparation, scheduling and monitoring of shipments
  • Settlement of accreditations, letters of credit, carnet ATA and insurance certificates
  • Control of performance billing
  • Issuance of shipping documents via ERP and other tools (GTS/SAP, Excel etc.)
  • Evaluation of transport quotes and verification of freight bills
  • Continuous exchange with internal and external stakeholders
  • Application of your expertise in export control, rules of origin and freight transport with all modes of transport

You bring that with you

  • At least completed KV training, further education in the field of transport, trade & logistics advantageous
  • 2–3 years of professional experience in the relevant area
  • Sound know-how in the logistics industry
  • Digital Competences (API, EDI, AI): Standard
  • Experience in stakeholder management (SCM, planning, suppliers, PMs, engineering, quality, import/export, finance)
  • Knowledge of SAP TMS & GTS as well as MS Office (Excel, SharePoint etc.)
  • Language skills: German fluent, English B2 required, French/Italian advantageous
  • Skills: problem-solving skills, good communication skills, initiative
