Reference code: JR135140

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

“Take your chance and join this unique opportunity of a 360-finance journey!”

BACKGROUND

Picture yourself at the core of Richemont’s strategy in the place where key decisions are made, impacting our multi-billion Euro listed company, contributing directly to our business. With a transversal view across our Group and strong exposure to Sr Management, you will understand our activities inside and out and build your 360-degree finance skills.

Be ready to embark in an exciting first 24 months’ tour through key HQ finance functions such as Group FP&A, Group Accounting, Tax, and Treasury, with a special backstage access pass to a Maison and a Region!

Make this dream a reality by joining our Finance Accelerator Program!

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT

The scope of this program will cover the following

· Collaborating daily with global colleagues on regular reporting, rolling forecasts, budgets, analysis of key business drivers and ad hoc value creation projects · Exposure to and “learning by doing” the key financial processes involved in the global consolidation and external reporting under Stock Exchange requirements of our multi-division and multi-product Group · Immersing yourself in understanding the treasury processes, both client-facing and internal monitoring as well as risk & opportunity management · “Hands-on” practical experience of day-to-day finance life in one of our Maisons, whose focus is on product innovation and quality as well as excellence in client service · Deep understanding of the Group’s operating model and the importance of tax compliance

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

Are you up for the challenge?

  • in possession of a master’s degree in finance, controlling, economics or associated fields
  • bringing at least two to three years of post-master professional experience in finance
  • hungry to learn
  • bright-minded
  • embodying an agility mindset
  • a smooth talker in more than one language, including English
  • and a data analysis guru

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed.
