Job Description Summary #LI-Hybrid Location: Basel, Switzerland

Join Novartis Internal Audit at the forefront of technology innovation, where you will help safeguard and shape the future of data, Artificial Intelligence, cybersecurity and cloud transformation across a global organization. As a Senior Internal Auditor, Technology, Data & AI, you will partner with senior leaders, assess emerging risks, and provide valuable insights that strengthen business performance, governance and compliance. This is a unique opportunity to combine deep technical expertise with strategic influence, working across diverse functions while helping Novartis deliver better outcomes for patients worldwide.

Job Description Key Responsibilities

  • Deliver technology-focused audits across data, Artificial Intelligence, cloud, cybersecurity and wider digital risk areas.
  • Assess technology risks and evaluate related controls to identify gaps, themes and improvement opportunities.
  • Contribute to annual risk assessment and audit planning by sharing insights on emerging technology risks.
  • Participate in multiple audits each year, providing subject matter expertise to other audit teams where required.
  • Analyse root causes of control issues and recommend practical, business-focused actions to strengthen risk management.
  • Engage confidently with stakeholders to discuss findings, challenge constructively and agree clear outcomes.
  • Support continuous improvement across Internal Audit by bringing technical insight, curiosity and independent thinking.

Essential Requirements

  • Strong technical experience across cloud, Artificial Intelligence and data with cybersecurity experience a bonus.
  • Ability to apply technical knowledge to risk, controls, governance or assurance-related activities.
  • Proven self-starter who can work independently, manage ambiguity and deliver quality outcomes with limited supervision.
  • Strong analytical skills, with the ability to investigate complex issues and identify practical solutions.
  • Strong stakeholder engagement and communication skills, with the ability to build credibility, challenge constructively and influence outcomes through thoughtful, evidence-based discussion.
  • Strong communication skills in English, with the ability to explain technical topics clearly to different audiences.

Desirable Requirements

  • Professional certification in cloud technologies, auditing, information security, cybersecurity or technology risk management.
  • Demonstrated expertise in cloud technologies, Artificial Intelligence, data platforms or cybersecurity with relevant industry certifications preferred.
