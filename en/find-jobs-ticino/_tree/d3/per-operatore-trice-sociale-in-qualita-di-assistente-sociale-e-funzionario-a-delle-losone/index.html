Full description

COMMON OF MUNICY LOSONE COMPETITION OPERATOR/ SOCIAL COURT (Social Assistant) The City of Losone opens the competition for the recruitment of a social worker/woman as a social worker and official of the Social Institutions for the Municipality of Losone 100% under the conditions laid down in the Organic Regulation for municipal employees (ROD). Main activities (not exhaustive list)

  • take charge of preventive and global action, aid, coordination and

promotion of social, individual and/or collective actions on the territory;

  • assume the function of antenna and person of active contact on the municipal territory, constituting

a point of reference for all those who are confronted with situations of discomfort or need, in collaboration with the Municipal Administration and the existing social network;

  • disbursement of the practices of the AVS Agency, LAPS regional office and municipal social contributions.

Basic requirements

  • Bachelor in Social Work SUP or equivalent title in social counselling or

multiannual experience in the social sector, with good knowledge of the network of social services of the region and the Canton. Preferential requirements

  • knowledge of Swiss laws and social insurance;
  • sensitivity to social, youth and health promotion issues;
  • licence to conduct cat. B.
  • Swiss or foreign citizenship with “C” residence permit;
  • knowledge of national languages, including excellent knowledge of the Italian language and good

knowledge of the German one;

  • strong relational qualities;
  • autonomy and ability to group work;
  • flexibility (in working hours but also in the type of activity);
  • creativity and entertainment
  • The detailed list of functions and activities (chapter) can be obtained from the Municipal Chancellery (tel. 091/785.76.00) or can be downloaded from www.losone.ch. Required documents ▪ Handwritten letter with indication of the term of availability ▪ Curriculum vitae ▪ Certificates of study (with notes) ▪ Certificates of work ▪ Individual certificate of civil status or family act▪ Certificate of good conduct ▪ Judicial box extract ▪ Questionnaire on health status ▪ Recent photography passport format.
  • Dispense for domiciled in the municipality.

The individual certificate of civil status or family act, the certificate of good conduct and the extract of the judicial box must be issued for less than 6 months. Incomplete applications or documents which have expired will not be examined. Yearly wage, 13th monthly, as at the salaries scale for municipal employees:

  • with academic title: class 9 (min. Fr. 85’558.--, max. Fr. 136’380.--),
  • without academic title: class 7 (min. Fr. 75’250.--, max. Br. 118’744.--).

In addition to the salary, children's allowances are paid. Entry into service: as soon as possible, to be convent First year of trial. Applications for recruitment, together with the documents mentioned above, must be sent to the City Hall of Losone, in closed envelope and with the external wording “Incarious operator/social worker”, by 16.00 on Friday 4th December 2026. Although not included in this notice of competition, the Organic Regulation of employees of the Municipality of Losone was adopted. The task will be decided at the sole discretion of the City Hall, which reserves already until now to cancel the competition if the applications do not meet the requirements. Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
