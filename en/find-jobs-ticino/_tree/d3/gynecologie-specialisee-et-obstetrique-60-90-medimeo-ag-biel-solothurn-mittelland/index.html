Would you like to work on an outpatient basis and at the same time develop your profile in the field of endometriosis? In this modern practice in the Mittelland you combine general gynecology and pregnancy care with the opportunity to engage in the care of patients with endometriosis and chronic pelvic complaints. Experience in this area is welcome, but not a requirement. The established, further education-enabled practice in the Swiss Mittelland combines classical primary gynecological care with the care of patients with endometriosis and chronic pelvic complaints. Interdisciplinary cooperation and short decision-making paths shape everyday practice. Particularly important is the personal cooperation in the team: You know each other, take care of each other and support each other in everyday work. Joint lunches and uncomplicated exchange are just as much a part of this as a pleasant atmosphere in which you like to work together.

Their tasks

  • Independent management of a gynecological consultation
  • Care of pregnant women in the ambulatory setting
  • Depending on the interest Clarification and treatment of patients with endometriosis and chronic pelvic complaints
  • If interested, stimulation treatments in the context of fertility care
  • Participation in interdisciplinary case meetings
  • Participation in the development of the practice

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in Gynecology and Obstetrics or an equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland
  • Ideally, professional experience in the Swiss healthcare system
  • Advantageous Pregnancy Ultrasound
  • Interest in the field of endometriosis, previous experience is not a requirement
  • Independent, conscientious and patient-oriented working method
  • Enjoy working in an interdisciplinary team

Their advantages

  • Scheduled office hours from 8.00 to 17.00
  • Very good support from an experienced MPA team
  • Scheduled time for administration
  • Targeted training in the field of endometriosis
  • Very fair fixed salary plus revenue share
  • 6 weeks of holidays and 1 week of paid training
  • Modern and high-quality equipped practice rooms
  • Personal cooperation with respect, mutual support and common lunches
  • Private accident insurance and attractive social benefits
