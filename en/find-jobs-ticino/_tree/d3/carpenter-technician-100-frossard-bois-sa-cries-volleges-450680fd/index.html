Full description

To complete our team, we are looking for a 100% (M/F) Charpenter Technician or equivalent training.

Contract Type: Indeterminate position (IDC)Continued or to be agreedLocation:Val de Bagnes (VS)

About us

Born in 1974, the company Frossard Bois SA, Charpente et Menuisserie is composed of 45 employees, including 19 dedicated to the carpentry sector. Passionate about wood, we manufacture buildings and create furniture in the image of our basic material: with nobility and beauty. In order to satisfy its clients, to maintain an irreproachable quality of work and to ensure the well-being of its employees, our company is constantly evolving.

  • Your main missions

As a professional in design and construction, you intervene on sustainable wooden construction projects.

  • Your responsibilities are as follows:

Design and technical management:

Study the plans and specifications of clients, architects and engineers by integrating technical, budgetary and normative constraints. Perform static calculations. Draw accurate execution plans using the Cadwork software while ensuring safety, quality and durability standards are met.

Project monitoring and logistics:

Estimating quantities, evaluating the costs of materials and carrying out the necessary orders.Cooperate with the various actors of the project and ensure compliance of the execution.Diriger and monitor the work in workshop as on site, participate in the meetings and control the quality. Ensure strict compliance with budgets and deadlines separate invoices (drafting of texts and metering).

*Secondary missions (in-house training):*

Calculate prices, draft and format offers via software Triviso.Program the CNC machining center for solid wood elements via software Lignocam. Follow-up and training of apprentices within the company (regular interviews, monitoring of the programme).

  • Your profile

You have a training course in Wood Technician / Charpente or a title deemed to have a thorough knowledge of building materials, techniques and tools master the CAD software Cadwork as well as basic computer tools (Word, Excel).You are recognized for your rigor, accuracy and sense of responsibility with excellent team spirit, initiative, and capacity to anticipate and share our sensitivity to ecology and sustainability.

What we offer you

Interesting and varied challenges within a company recognised and firmly rooted in the Val de Bagnes.A dynamic and human-sized environment where decisions are made quickly and where each passionate colleague collaborator and a friendly working atmosphere on a daily basis.
