Full description

Tasks

  • support insured persons in their professional reintegration, provide them with personalized and targeted advice
  • contribute to the investigation and evaluation of the claims for the right to AI benefits
  • treat relationships with employers and external stakeholders in AI
  • to monitor the application and good performance of professional measures
  • to activate young people insured with psychiatric or physical problems in the field of first training (Youth Service and Integration) or to activate adult insured persons in the context of early intervention or professional reformation (Professional Integration Service)

Requirements

  • university education (bachelor academic or professional university), preferably in the psychological, educational, social or economic field
  • previous work experience: it is preferential to work with young people or people with psychiatric problems, or experience in the management of staff and/or social insurance
  • knowledge in the field of social insurance and labour market
  • proficiency of the Italian language (conversation and editing) and good knowledge of one of the official languages (French or German)
  • good ability to listen active, editorial, organizational, autonomy in work management, stress management and difficult interviews
  • strong decision-making capacity
  • good basic knowledge of computer tools
  • preparing for change and flexibility
  • availability to follow the continuing training
  • availability at travels outside Cantone
  • permit to conduct motor vehicles

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

22 October 2026
