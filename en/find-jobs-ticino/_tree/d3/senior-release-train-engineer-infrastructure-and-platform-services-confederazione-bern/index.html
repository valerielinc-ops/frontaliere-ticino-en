Full description

Put to the Point

Become part of the Cyber Command and actively shape the digitalization of the Swiss Army. Get involved in this unique task – building the new digital platform – in a dynamic working environment.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20504
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Control and optimize the Agile Release Train (ART) as well as responsible for the implementation and moderation of the central SAFe events (PI Planning, Inspect & Adapt)
  • Proactively identify, track and eliminate risks and impediments, and manage obstacles and dependencies across team and ART boundaries
  • Planning and controlling the capacities of the Agile Release Train as a whole and designing ART-internal prioritizations and coordination within the framework of Lean Portfolio Management requirements
  • Establish, control and further develop agile metrics to increase delivery quality and performance
  • Actively design and coordinate collaboration with product management, domain architecture, business owners and agile teams
  • Scrum Master/Team coaches, product owners and agile teams as well as actively shaping the RTE community

This makes you unique

  • Completed studies (Bachelor/Master) in the fields of computer science, business informatics or a comparable degree program
  • Several years of experience as a Release Train Engineer in a larger company, in public administration or in a comparable complex environment with demanding governance and stakeholder structures
  • Very good communication skills, high assertiveness and a structured working method
  • Current certifications as SAFe RTE and SAFe SPC are desirable
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and good knowledge of English

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information
