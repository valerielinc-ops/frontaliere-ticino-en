Full description

In the Canton of Solothurn, BACHTELEN is committed to the individual promotion and integration of children, young people and young adults.

In order to achieve our goals, we run day care centers, day schools, housing offers, offers of individual support as well as therapeutic and advisory offers.

We are looking for the school year 2026/2027 at Bachtelenstrasse 38 in Grenchen on 22.02.2027

Internship: school social pedagogy 70%

The areas of responsibility shall include

  • Accompaniment and support of children in school everyday life
  • Collaboration in midday care
  • Cooperation in the learning and leisure studio
  • Participation in organisational and pedagogical meetings
  • Participation in internal training and in the monthly internship

We are happy about personalities who

  • Have interest and pleasure in working with children with challenging behavior
  • Want to gain experience in the field of school social education
  • are open to new experiences
  • Appreciating a cooperative and self-responsible working method
  • Bringing motivation and initiative
  • are reliable, flexible, humorous and resilient

We offer

  • a goal-oriented and future-oriented task
  • A well-accompanied familiarization
  • Close cooperation in the multi-professional team
  • An insight into various areas of expertise
  • Conditions of employment according to cantonal guidelines. The monthly internship wage is approximately 75%. CHF 1’600 gross
  • working days are Monday to Friday; compensated in school holidays
  • The internship is recognized as a preliminary internship for training in social work, speech therapy, etc.
  • the possibility of completing this internship also as a training internship
