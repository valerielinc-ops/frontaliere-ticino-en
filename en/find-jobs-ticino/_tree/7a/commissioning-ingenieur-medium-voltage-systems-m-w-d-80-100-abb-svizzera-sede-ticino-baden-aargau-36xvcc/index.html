Commissioning Engineer (Medium-Voltage Systems)

This position reports to: Engineering Manager

Your Role and • Responsibilities

To strengthen our Engineering team in the field of protection technology, we are looking for an experienced Commissioning Engineer for Medium Voltage Switchgear.

You will apply your technical skills across various phases of customer projects, ensuring that all commissioning activities are completed efficiently and cost-effectively in accordance with contract specifications, quality standards, safety requirements, as well as cybersecurity guidelines and standards. You will also demonstrate your expertise by supporting the continuous improvement of project processes, product, and system design.

Work Model: hybrid

You will be primarily responsible for:

  • Independent commissioning of medium voltage switchgear as well as protection and control systems, taking into account customer requirements, technical specifications, and applicable quality, safety, and regulatory standards.

  • Parameterization and configuration of ABB protection relays and RTUs, as well as execution and documentation of functional, protection, and secondary tests.

  • Analysis and diagnosis of technical faults in protection and automation systems, support in second-level support, and participation in on-call duty (piquet service).

  • Supporting sales and project management in technical reviews and in technical discussions with customers and partners; conducting customer training on the operation of protection relays.

• Qualifications for the Role

  • Technical foundation education and a completed degree in Engineering, Automation Technology, or a related technical field.

  • Minimum 3 years of professional experience in commissioning or engineering of systems in the field of energy and protection technology.

  • Solid knowledge of software programming using FUPLA as well as communication protocols such as IEC 61850, Modbus, and IEC 60870-5-104.

  • Strong focus on safe working practices and commitment to compliance with all relevant safety guidelines.

  • Independent, structured, and responsible working style, high self-motivation, openness to new topics, and willingness to continuously acquire new technologies.

  • Very good German and good English language skills. French is an advantage.

  • High willingness to travel (approx. 60–70%) within Switzerland, as well as willingness to take on on-call duties (approx. 7 times per year).

More About Us

ABB's Distribution Solutions division provides medium voltage components, switchgear, and solutions that connect and protect the evolving electrical grid, ensuring reliable and predictable energy distribution. We link high-voltage systems to medium and low-voltage consumers, ensuring continuous power delivery to industrial, commercial, and residential users while enabling the integration of renewable energy sources into the grid. Our smart solutions, powered by AI, enhance grid resilience and optimize the usage of the electrical system.

With ABB Ability™ enabled connected solutions, the offering includes medium-voltage air- and gas-insulated switchgear (1 to 46 kilovolts), indoor and outdoor circuit breakers, protection relays, instrument transformers, sensors and solutions for distribution stations.

We support our customers and partners in staying ahead of increasing electrification needs, technology, and regulatory changes, providing the power to adapt to a rapidly evolving energy landscape.
