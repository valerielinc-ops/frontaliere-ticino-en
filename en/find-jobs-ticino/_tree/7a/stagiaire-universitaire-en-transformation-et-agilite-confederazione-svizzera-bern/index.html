Put to the Point

The Communication and Information System Operation Center (CISOC) is the operational centerpiece for the stable, secure and continuous operation of the new Swiss Army digitalization platform. It coordinates critical military IT infrastructures and guarantees maximum availability and information security. In this environment, agile principles (SAFe, Kanban, DevSecOps) will be further developed in a targeted manner in order to strengthen military leadership capability.

  • Entry Date: By Agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21022
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • accompanying, evaluating and further developing the transformation process psychologically, organizationally psychologically and methodologically
  • Strengthen team effectiveness and psychological safety as well as promote an agile mindset
  • Build change-readiness and resilient leadership competencies in an agile context
  • Adapt Agile methods (SAFe, Kanban, DevSecOps) to the specific requirements of military IT operations
  • Ensure continuous improvement of information security and operational availability
  • Promote knowledge exchange and best practice transfer in the interdisciplinary CISOC team

This makes them unique.

  • University degree in applied psychology (focus on organizational, occupational and business psychology) or related disciplines
  • Officer training or in-depth knowledge of military organizations (desirable)
  • Specialist areas of focus: organizational development, change management, agile transformation, psychological security, leadership development, human factors/performance; in-depth knowledge of agile frameworks (Scrum, Kanban, SAFe, LeSS, Nexus, DevOps/DevSecOps), including SAFe scaling (ART, Solution Train, Portfolio) and ITIL as well as secure handling of Confluence / Jira
  • high level of social competence, communication skills, analytical thinking, independent, structured and results-oriented working methods
  • Responsibility, discretion and integrity

Your commitment to security and freedom

The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
