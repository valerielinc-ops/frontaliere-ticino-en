Full description

ELCO offers premium heating solutions with first-class service designed to meet the needs of our customers through the entire life cycle of products and heating systems. The focus is on excellent consulting as well as convincing products and heating systems. ELCO has been a leader in burner, condensing and solar technology for decades and has 1.7 million installed heating systems throughout Europe. Since 1928, ELCO has been a leading European brand offering intelligent solutions and technologies tailored to the needs of private and commercial projects, ensuring maximum energy savings.

Together we shape the energy future! For the Oberwallis we are looking for you as a service technician (m/f)

This is what you expect from us

  • Commissioning, maintenance and troubleshooting of regional operations
  • Independent performance of maintenance, repairs and troubleshooting
  • Technical responsibility for complex plants
  • Advising our customers on the optimal setting of their heating systems
  • Close cooperation with installers and the sales and service team

What you should bring

  • Experience as a service technician is an advantage
  • Completed training EFZ, e.g. as an electrical, heating or sanitary installer, car industry or comparable technical apprenticeship in the HVAC or electrical engineering sector
  • Enjoyment of customer contact, structured and independent work
  • Readiness for Picetting
  • Place of residence Upper Valais or adjacent

You can look forward to this

  • Modern service vehicle – also for private use
  • Working with innovative and future-oriented products in the premium sector
  • Further education opportunities and targeted personal support
  • Attractive social benefits and fair employment conditions
  • A motivated team that supports, promotes and welcomes you

Ready for the next step? We look forward to your application and getting to know each other personally.
