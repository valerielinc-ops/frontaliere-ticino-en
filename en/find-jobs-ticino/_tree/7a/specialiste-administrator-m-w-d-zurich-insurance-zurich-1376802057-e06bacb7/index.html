Do you already have experience in the insurance industry and are an organizational talent that can not be brought out of peace even in hectic situations? Are you looking for a diverse challenge?

In this role, you are part of the P&C underwriting business unit in the area of corporate underwriting services at Zurich Switzerland. Summary of responsibilities and tasks. In this role, we support the teams in administrative and organizational tasks and are in close contact with brokers and various internal interfaces.

This is what awaits you

  • You are responsible for the triage of electronic, telephone and physical inquiries from brokers to the relevant specialist departments
  • You create proofs and "green cards" in the MF fleet area, by phone and by mail
  • You create regulatory, assessment and surplus accounts for personal and liability insurance
  • You record the redundancies & reductions in the notice period
  • You will perform various administrative tasks and provide general information to our brokers

This sets you apart

  • Communication skills in German and good knowledge of French and/or Italian are advantageous
