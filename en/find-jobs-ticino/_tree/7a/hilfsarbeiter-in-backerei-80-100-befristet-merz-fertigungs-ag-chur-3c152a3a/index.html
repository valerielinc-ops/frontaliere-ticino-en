Full description

CRAFTSMANSHIP. QUALITY. TEAM SPIRIT. Do you love genuine baking craftsmanship, high-quality ingredients and working in a well-coordinated team? Then join our day bakery and make baked goods with us every day that delight our customers.

ABOUT US Since 1946, we have been the innovative and dynamic bakery-confectionery-café family business of Southeastern Switzerland. Our team of over 150 employees and 14 apprentices bakes, decorates and sells our high-quality products every day with dedication at 10 locations in the Chur and St. Gallen Rhine Valleys. Our customers are at the center of everything we do. But an appreciative working relationship is just as important to us: with us, you are part of the Merz family. A special highlight is our glass-walled bakery at Chur's Rossboden, which offers a view of our work from the outside and a view of nature from the inside.

YOUR MAIN TASKS

  • -You work in our day bakery at Chur's Rossboden (working hours generally 07.00 to 16.00).
  • -You produce our high-quality bread and bakery products with skilled craftsmanship and great care.
  • -You take on classic production tasks such as dough processing, baking and finishing our products.

YOUR PROFILE

  • -You are willing to take on a fixed-term position of 16. November to 28. February 2027.
  • -You are a reliable, independent and quality-conscious individual.
  • -You enjoy teamwork and contribute to an open and respectful working environment.

Even if you do not meet all the requirements: we still look forward to receiving your application.

OUR BENEFITS

  • -Work planning four weeks in advance.
  • -Modern workplace in our bright and spacious production facility at Chur's Rossboden.
  • -30 % employee discount as well as free drinks during working hours.
  • -A close-knit team that supports one another and sticks together.

Interested? We look forward to getting to know you.
