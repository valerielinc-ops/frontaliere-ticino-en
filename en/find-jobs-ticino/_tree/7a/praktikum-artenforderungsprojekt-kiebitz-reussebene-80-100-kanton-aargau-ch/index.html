Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Species protection in action: observation, documentation and reporting of Kiebitz brood in the Reus Valley
  • Project management in the field: coordination and operational implementation of measures for the targeted promotion of the Kiebitz population
  • Commitment to biotope management: Active participation in the care and maintenance of valuable nature reserves

What you bring

  • Studies/experience: FH degree or experience in scientific writing (e.g. bachelor thesis)
  • Nature know-how: You are familiar with birds (ornithology) Botany knowledge is a welcome extra
  • Outdoor spirit: You like to be outside and look forward to exciting observation tours in the area
  • Mobile: You have the driving license Kat. B in the pocket
  • Mindset: You are a team player who at the same time likes to take responsibility independently

In the Department of Construction, Transport and Environment, we are actively developing the diverse habitat Aargau in the areas of space, energy, environment and mobility – today and for future generations. Nature and landscape provide habitat for animals and plants as well as recreational space for people. We in the Landscape and Waters department are committed to responsible development of the landscape, ecological enhancement and networking of diverse habitats for biodiversity, sustainable use of river waters and comprehensive flood protection.
