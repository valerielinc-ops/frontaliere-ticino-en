Full description

As of 01.01.2027 or by agreement You would like to become a specialist in pneumology and get to know sleep medicine from the ground up? With us, you will complete up to two years of your pneumological training and work in a sleep medicine center with five closely networked disciplines. Our Clinic of Pneumology and Somnology offers a wide range of outpatient and inpatient pneumological as well as sleep medical diagnostics and therapy. In addition to comprehensive lung function diagnostics, the diagnostic and therapeutic spectrum also includes bronchoscopies including EBUS, the care of patients with respiratory insufficiency and the initiation and control of non-invasive ventilation procedures. A particular focus of our clinic is sleep medicine. Our sleep medicine center is of supraregional importance and offers the entire spectrum of diagnosis and therapy of sleep and sleep-related respiratory disorders. Pneumology, psychiatry, neurology, ORL and dentistry work closely together in the team. As a result, complex sleep medical clinical pictures can also be comprehensively clarified and treated. Adjustment to CPAP and BiPAP devices as well as the initiation of non-invasive ventilation (NIV) and their long-term care are also part of daily routine. Our clinic is recognized as a category B training facility for pneumology with a two-year qualification as well as a training facility for sleep medicine.

Their tasks

  • Under close specialist supervision, they look after outpatient and inpatient patients with pneumological and sleep medicine diseases.
  • You will learn and deepen structured pneumological diagnostics including spirometry, body plethysmography, diffusion measurement, blood gas analysis and stress diagnostics.
  • They are gradually incorporated into bronchoscopy and gain insight into further endoscopic procedures including EBUS.
  • As part of our sleep medicine center, which operates across regions, you diagnose and care for patients with sleep medicine clinical pictures.
  • You will gain practical experience in performing and interpreting respiratory polygraphy and polysomnography.
  • You will learn how to adjust to CPAP and BiPAP devices, how to initiate non-invasive ventilation (NIV), and how to structure follow-up and optimize these therapies.
  • They participate in interdisciplinary meetings, rapports and case conferences.
  • Depending on your level of training, you participate in the internal further training of the clinic and in the further development of our medical offers. They shall have at their disposal:
  • a completed training as a specialist doctor / specialist for general internal medicine or are about to complete
  • a strong interest in pneumology and sleep medicine as well as the desire to acquire the specialist title pneumology
  • Teamwork as well as an open, committed and flexible personality with a high level of personal responsibility
  • very good knowledge of German in word and writing; for foreign medical diploma the necessary recognition by MEBEKO Your further education with us Structured further education has a high priority for us. They work in a manageable team with direct contact to the cadre doctors and receive continuous specialist supervision. Due to the wide range of pneumological and sleep medicine patients, you can gain extensive theoretical and practical experience within your training period. Through the close cooperation of pneumology, psychiatry, neurology, ORL and dentistry, you get a comprehensive insight into interdisciplinary sleep medicine. We offer you a very interesting and versatile activity in an advanced, dynamic and multi-professional working environment characterized by high quality, respect and appreciation.
