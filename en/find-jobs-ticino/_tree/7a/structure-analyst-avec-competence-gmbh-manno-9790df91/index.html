Full description

You perform structural stress analysis on metallic and composite airframe structures, with a focus on wing and landing gear, in a development and certification environment.

Tasks

  • Perform classical static stress analysis and finite element analysis of metallic and composite airframe structures
  • Focus on wing and landing gear structures
  • Prepare stress notes, formal engineering analysis reports, test plans and test reports
  • Support CS 23 compliance demonstration activities
  • Support structural testing definition and execution

Profile

  • University degree in Aerospace Engineering or a related field (Bachelor's or Master's degree)
  • Minimum of 2 years post-graduate experience in structural stress analysis in aerospace
  • Recent graduates with outstanding academic record may also be considered
  • Familiarity with NASTRAN (MSC/NX), FEMAP or comparable FEA software
  • Solid working knowledge of Microsoft Office
  • Mathcad experience an advantage
  • Knowledge of CS-23 certification requirements an advantage
  • Fluent English required

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
