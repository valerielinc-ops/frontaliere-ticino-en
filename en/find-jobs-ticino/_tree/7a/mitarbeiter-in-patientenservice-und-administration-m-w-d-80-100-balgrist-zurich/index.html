Full description

Welcome to Balgrist Tec AG – the leading competence center for orthopaedic aids and medical retailers in Zurich. As a subsidiary of the Balgrist University Hospital, we combine precision craftsmanship with the latest medicine. >The mobility and quality of life of our patients are our focus. You are the first point of contact at Balgrist Tec: With your friendly nature, you give our patients a good feeling and at the same time ensure professional administrative support.

Tasks

  • First point of contact: At the reception and on the phone you greet our patients friendly and professionally. You take care of the data collection, appointment coordination and management of the electronic patient record.
  • Aid Service: You handle the rental of aids (rollers, wheelchairs) from the drafting of the contract via handover/return to invoicing.
  • Administration: You take care of the administrative handling of supplies by creating cost estimates, making clarifications with the cost carriers, coordinating orders and appointments and invoicing completed supplies.
  • Turntable function: You are the first point of contact for patients and payers and work closely with the employees of the business areas (shoe technology, orthopaedic technology and rehabilitation technology). You take care of a forward-looking scheduling in the agenda of the business areas.
  • Process support: In addition, you actively support master data maintenance and document your processes for a smooth mutual representation in the team.

Requirements

  • Training: Successfully completed commercial basic training.
  • Experience: Ideally, you will bring experience in patient service as well as in administrative handling with payers (health insurance, IV and UVG).
  • IT & languages: You communicate stylishly in German (every other language is a plus) and are well versed in dealing with ERP systems and MS Office.
  • Personality: You are characterized by empathy, resilience and a high service orientation. Daily patient contact gives you pleasure.

We offer

  • Meaningful environment: You can expect a versatile position in a renowned, future-oriented company in the university environment.
  • Variety: You experience the perfect balance of direct customer contact, the coordination of different interfaces and demanding administration.
  • Attractive employment conditions: These include, among other things, a 50% discount on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to a GA, a paid day off in the birthday month, discounted food in the employee restaurant, various shopping discounts and a non-compulsory pension fund solution.
