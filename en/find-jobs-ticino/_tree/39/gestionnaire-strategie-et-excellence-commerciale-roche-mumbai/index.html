The Position

  1. Strategic Planning & Execution
  • Lead the development and continuous refinement of long-term strategic roadmaps, tracking organizational progress against core business objectives.
  • Drive high impact strategic projects including market entry, portfolio expansion, strategic partnerships and M&A assessments.
  • Design and facilitate executive alignment workshops, translating complex discussions into structured, actionable directions.
  • Strategy Realization Office (SRO): Serve as the custodian and Single Point of Contact (SPOC) for the SRO, driving disciplined execution across all initiatives.
  1. Commercial Excellence (CommEx)
  • Serve as the dedicated CommEx partner for assigned business franchises driving overall commercial strategy and execution.
  • Oversee the end-to-end design, governance and disbursement of commercial sales incentive schemes.
  • Direct business planning processes, workload design and performance tracking across teams.
  • Drive Management Information Systems (MIS) enhancements and process automation to optimize reporting efficiency.
  • Harmonize disparate data sources (CRM, sales performance, in-market metrics) into a unified source of truth.
  1. Market & Competitive Intelligence
  • Monitor healthcare economics, In-Vitro Diagnostics (IVD) trends, competitor strategies and regulatory shifts to anticipate market dynamics.
  • Act as the custodian for in-market data, continuously validating business hypotheses and market assumptions.
  • Market Research: Lead end-to-end market research initiatives to deliver precise, high-value insights that inform commercial strategy.
  1. Insights & Analytics (Internal)
  • Synthesize complex data into concise narratives and strategic recommendations for executive leadership.
  • Build dynamic financial, forecasting, and scenario models to anchor robust business planning.
  1. Stakeholder Engagement
  • Collaborate with senior leadership, Marketing, Sales and Support functions to guide decision-making through structured, data driven rationales.
  • Author high-impact presentation materials, executive briefs and strategic communications for leadership forums.

Qualifications & Experience

  • MBA, Master’s in Healthcare Management, Life Sciences, Economics, or related field.
  • 8 -10 years’ experience in strategy consulting, corporate strategy, in healthcare, pharma, diagnostics, or medtech.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with proven ability to manage complex projects.
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to influence senior stakeholders.
  • Exposure to healthcare markets, regulatory frameworks, and digital health trends preferred.

Key Skills & Competencies

  • Strategic mindset with structured thinking.
  • Strong business and financial acumen.
  • Proficiency in data analysis, market modeling, and scenario planning.
  • Ability to manage ambiguity and drive clarity.
  • Collaborative, resilient, and results-oriented.
