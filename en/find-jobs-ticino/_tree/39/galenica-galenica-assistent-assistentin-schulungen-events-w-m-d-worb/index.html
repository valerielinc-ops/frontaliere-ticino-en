Full description

Location: Spagyros AG, Neufeldstrasse 1, 3076 Worb

Do you love it when many small details create an all-round successful event? At Spagyros, you’ll keep the threads around our training and courses together. With your organizational talent, you ensure that everything is right from planning to implementation to follow-up. You are an important point of contact for participants as well as our marketing and sales teams.

You can do that.

  • You organize our courses and training courses from A to Z and ensure that everything runs smoothly from participant management to post-processing
  • You are the organisational hub of our training courses: you coordinate, negotiate, book and communicate with different stakeholders
  • You keep an eye on training budgets and costs and ensure transparent and reliable cost control
  • You check, create and record invoices relating to our training courses and ensure careful handling
  • You support our marketing and sales teams with administrative issues and contribute to efficient processes

You bring that with you

  • You have completed a commercial or paramedical education and bring initial experience in organizing training or events
  • You are well-versed in using MS Office, feel comfortable in digital tools and use our CRM profitably
  • You work in a structured and reliable way, like to take on responsibility and keep track of several parallel tasks
  • You communicate sovereignly with different stakeholders and are open for occasional outdoor or evening assignments
  • German is your feel-good language and also in French you communicate safely, at least on level B2

Applications from recruitment agencies cannot be considered for this position.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

Spagyros AG is an emerging company in the field of complementary medicine, production and marketing of spagyrics, gemmotherapy, phytotherapy, homeopathy and synbiotics. Spagyros AG has been part of the Galenica Group since 2021 and has its locations in Worb (BE) and La Malmaison, St. Brais (JU). It is a GMP-certified pharmaceutical company that manufactures and distributes all medicines in Switzerland by hand. Spagyros medicines - created by nature - Completed by hand.
