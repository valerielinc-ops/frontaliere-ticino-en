Full description

Do you have a flair for basic foods and are you interested in the variety of supermarket products in the field of food and non-food? Do you also bring a sales talent and communicate safely with our customers? Then you are exactly right with us.

Mansioni

  • You advise and serve our customers with great pleasure and your expertise

Requirements

  • Ideally, a few years of professional experience: in the supermarket sector
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade specialty food
  • As a customer-oriented personality with pleasure in sales, you make shopping a positive experience
  • PC skills and high digital fitness set you apart
  • You have leadership experience
