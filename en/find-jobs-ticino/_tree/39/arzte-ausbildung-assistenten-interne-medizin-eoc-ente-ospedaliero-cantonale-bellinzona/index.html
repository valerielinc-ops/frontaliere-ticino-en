Doctors in training (Medicina interna) - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite Ticino hospital is present with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. The following position is addressed to candidates who wish to apply spontaneously. The Department of Internal Medicine of the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona and Valli is looking for Doctors Training Assistants (Internal Medicine) Profile The Department of Internal Medicine of the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona and Valli (Sedi of Bellinzona, Faido and Acquarossa) is an integral part of the Cantonal Hospital (www.eoc.ch). It is recognized as a professional training centre, category A, with a wide range of multidisciplinary cases and involves employees, as well as in the field of care, also in research and training. The peripheral hospitals located in the Leventina valley (Faido) and Blenio valley (Acquarossa) complete the profile of the case in a dynamic of greater autonomy. The medical team consists of 34 assistant doctors, 15 clinical chiefs and superiors belonging to different disciplines. Profile: The person we seek has professional experience and autonomy, as well as motivation, own initiative and spirit of collaboration. He believes, like us, in the importance of putting the patient and his family at the center of all our attentions.

  • Requirements: Degree/diploma in medicine (Switzerland title or foreign equivalent title); for doctors with foreign qualifications the recognition/registration by the Federal Commission for medical professions MEBEKO is required and its registration to MedReg before the start of the activity. Experience in a B or C training institute, in internal medicine preferably matured in Switzerland, or adequate clinical experience developed in other countries. Good knowledge of the Italian language (B2) and if possible knowledge of a second national language (German and/or French) and English. Language skills must in turn be recorded in MedReg. Our offer: Structured continuing training program, multidisciplinary and multi-professional approach, individualized paths, development and career opportunities, hospitality culture and relationship, collaboration with the new faculty of biomedicine.
