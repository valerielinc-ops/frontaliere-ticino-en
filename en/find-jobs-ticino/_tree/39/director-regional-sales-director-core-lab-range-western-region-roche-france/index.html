The Position

Missions

  • As Regional Sales Director for the Core Lab Range, you are responsible for the quality of the service produced by the team and the continuous improvement of the processes you supervise.
  • VACC Leader (Visionary, Architect, Catalyst and Coach), you know how to engage around a strategic vision, and structure activities in an efficient and collaborative manner.
  • As a coach, you support the development of your team and know how to identify and develop talent.
  • Multiplier of agile culture, you establish within your team a framework of increasing autonomy and create the conditions for effective delegated decision-making; you recruit the talents that the organization needs, anticipate new skills thanks to monitoring market developments and your connections within the group,
  • Finally, you embody Leadership commitments.

Your main responsibilities

  • You participate in projects/squads within your Chapter/FI Branch/Region
  • You manage the commercial activity of local accounts and implement the evolution of the practices and skills of the CSM (Customer Success Managers) with regard to the strategic objectives of the subsidiary and the group (particularly in connection with the Key Account Managers).
  • You are also responsible for strict compliance with compliance for your team and compliance with pricing policies and processes for the ranges, by segment.
  • You cooperate with the Network Leads and the Resource Manager in order to guarantee the right level of skills and resources for the SPS while respecting the financial objectives.
  • Change agent, you are responsible for the proper adoption of new sales processes in the subsidiary and inter-Chapter cooperation

Who you are

  • You benefit from a solid culture of the diagnostic industry and, more broadly, the healthcare ecosystem
  • Experienced in team management, you master the positions of leader and coach to inspire, engage and grow your teams
  • Equipped with strong systemic thinking, you excel in managing complex environments and manage your projects with agility
  • Your high-level negotiation skills and business acumen are recognized
  • You have excellent communication (written and oral), a keen sense of synthesis
  • Your English is fluent

Let’s build a healthier future, together.
