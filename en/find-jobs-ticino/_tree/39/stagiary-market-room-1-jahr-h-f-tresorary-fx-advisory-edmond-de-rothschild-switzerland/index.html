ACTIVITIES

  • The trainee will be integrated into the cash and FX advisory teams and placed under the joint supervision of those responsible for both activities in Switzerland. Treasury
  • The Treasury is part of the Clean Account Operations business line, run from Switzerland, which brings together several complementary trades: trading of currencies for own account, consulting on the foreign exchange and OTC products market, financial engineering and treasury.
  • Upon completion of the training, the trainee will be required to assist the team in the following activities:
  • Treatment of customer fiduciary investment orders with authorized counterparties in interaction with clients, advisors and portfolio managers
  • Updating and making available to customers daily lists of terms and conditions for fiduciary deposits and refinancing rates for credit operations.
  • Daily support in the management of cash positions and in the processing and seizure of intra-group transactions with our entities. FX Advisory
  • Development of both internal and external DCD (Dual Currency Deposit) structures. Verification of such valuations, detection of errors and corrections
  • Perform daily middle-office spots related to DCD expirations
  • Assist traders Advisory Forex in the accounting and maintenance of accumulator-type operations, decumulators, PIVOT, TARF, TDRF with underlying currencies, precious metals and equities. This includes the following activities:
  • Fixing and controlling the booking of these in the core tool as well as the bank's risk tool
  • Maintenance of Trade Records
  • Operate deactivations during events of "KO", termination or "unwind"
  • Verification of the quantities of underlying fixeds and settlement dates
  • Daily support in the processing of transactions by establishing customer confirmations via our internal tool and sending customer confirmations
  • Seizure of operations in bank risk systems
  • Updating the volatility curves of underlying products action on an automatic basis or more, in the event of difficult market conditions
  • Assist traders in the production of internal research documents and FX strategies PROFILE
  • Studies in finance and/or business school
  • Knowledge of financial markets
  • Mastery of the Office suite (Excel, Word, Power Point), good VBA knowledge
  • Control the use of information systems such as Bloomberg
  • Good stress management
  • Autonomy, rigor and sense of organization
  • Relationship strength
  • Master's in English and French (written and oral) Without news from us within a month, you may consider that your application has not been accepted.
