Full description

USZ Campus

From 01.01.2027 temporary until 30.11.2028; subsequent continuation in part-time possible

The Clinic for Radio-Oncology at the University Hospital Zurich stands for cutting-edge medicine, pioneering research and committed training. With passion and scientific curiosity, we work every day to further improve the treatment of cancers and to give our patients the best possible chance of healing. Clinical excellence, innovative research and interdisciplinary cooperation form the basis of our actions.

Their main tasks

  • Preparation of study submissions to the Ethics Committee in cooperation with the project managers and the CTC
  • Development of SOPs
  • Monitoring adherence to study protocols and regulations
  • Identification of potential study candidates: in cooperation with the study doctor
  • Notification of study-specific studies
  • Collection of data and preparation of study documentation during the study and aftercare
  • Co-development of study protocols and CRFs, as well as processing and sending of samples
  • Preparation and implementation of monitoring and inspections, as well as contact with sponsors and authorities (EK, Swissmedic)
  • Quality assurance, control and implementation of legal regulations

Your profile

  • You have training in a medical science profession (e.g. biologist:in, BMA, nurse, MPA or data manager:in) or a completed specific training as / to the study coordinator
  • Previous experience in study coordination/implementation is advantageous
  • You have a high organisational talent and high flexibility and can manage several projects at the same time
  • You work actively and have clear communication and strong social skills
  • You feel comfortable in a small team, but also in interaction with a large group of clinic employees
  • You have stylish knowledge of German and good knowledge of English
  • You have good IT knowledge and are well versed in dealing with MS-Office and other database systems
