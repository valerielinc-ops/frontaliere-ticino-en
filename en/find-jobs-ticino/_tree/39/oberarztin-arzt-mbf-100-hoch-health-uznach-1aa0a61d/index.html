Full description

Senior physician 80-100 %

Clinic of Anesthesiology, Rescue and Pain Medicine

Place of work Uznach

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Implementation of all common anesthetic procedures of general and regional anesthesia in the operating room and in interventional procedures
  • Postoperative care and care of patients on our IMC station
  • Participation in on-call services
  • Theoretical and practical training of young assistant doctors and anesthesia nurses through active knowledge transfer of their broad technical expertise and their supervision

What you bring for this job

  • Swiss Specialist in Anesthesiology or a comparable title recognised by MEBEKO with proven and broad professional experience
  • Broad anesthesiological expertise in the care of older and multimorbid patients
  • Experience in the care of IMC or intensive care patients
  • High degree of self-organization and social competence in dealing with our patients as well as active promotion of a positive, appreciative working environment in a young, committed team
  • High self-motivation, commitment and pleasure in working in a dynamic, collegial team

Our offer to you

  • Possibility to work independently after the specialist examination with comprehensive support from the cadre doctors
  • Broad clinical spectrum and participation in all areas
  • State-of-the-art technical equipment and electronic patient data management system
  • Strong and positive team and clinic culture with close cooperation between all departments
  • Large range of internal and external, both professional and personal training opportunities
