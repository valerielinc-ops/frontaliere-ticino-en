Internship – Computational Design & Digital Fabrication for Sustainable Concrete Construction (Living Walls) Academy of Architecture, Università della Svizzera Italiana (USI) Università della Svizzera italiana (USI) is a young and lively university, a hub of opportunity that is open to the world, where students are offered a quality interdisciplinary education in which they can be fully engaged, and where our researchers can count on having the space to freely pursue their initiatives. Established in 1996, USI is in constant evolution, always taking on new challenges while remaining true to its three guiding principles: quality, openness, and responsibility. At the Academy of Architecture in Mendrisio, the Fabrication and Material Aware Architecture (FMAA) research group, led by Ena Lloret Fritschi, investigates sustainable design methods through advanced material systems, computational design, digital fabrication, and robotic construction. The group develops innovative approaches to architecture that integrate ecological performance, material experimentation, and emerging technologies. Candidate Profile The FMAA group is seeking a highly motivated intern to contribute to an exciting research and development project exploring living wall systems integrated into railway sound barriers. The project investigates how existing infrastructure can be transformed into multifunctional ecological systems that enhance biodiversity, improve environmental performance, and contribute to climate resilience. The internship will contribute to a feasibility study carried out in collaboration with SBB Swiss Federal Railways and the Swiss Ornithological Institute. The research focuses on evaluating and testing different living wall designs suitable for railway sound barriers, considering structural integration, constructability, ecological performance, maintenance strategies, and compatibility with railway infrastructure. What We Offer The successful candidate will become part of a multidisciplinary research team and support the development, prototyping, documentation, and evaluation of innovative façade and planting systems. Depending on the project's progress, the intern may also assist in preparing mock-ups, conducting laboratory and field tests, documenting experimental results, and developing digital models and visualizations. We are looking for candidates with a strong interest in sustainable architecture, material systems, and environmental design. The position offers the opportunity to work at the intersection of architecture, engineering, ecology, and digital fabrication while collaborating with academic researchers and external partners. pagina 2 / 3 This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to research with direct real-world application and impact on the future of sustainable infrastructure in Switzerland.
