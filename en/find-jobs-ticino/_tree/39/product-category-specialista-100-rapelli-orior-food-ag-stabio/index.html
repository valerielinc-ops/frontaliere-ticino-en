Full description

Rapelli, a symbol of Ticino’s charcuterie culture since 1929, is renowned for its high-quality deli products made with the finest local ingredients. The company’s strong values and traditions are passed down from generation to generation, ensuring the creation of exceptional delicacies. With the brands Rapelli and Ticinella, Rapelli is a renowned ambassador of Ticino, offering a unique gastronomic experience. ## Product & Category Specialist, 100% Role Description The Product & Category Specialist is a key figure in the development, operational management, and launch of new products. Acting as a bridge between the market, product development, production, and marketing, this role ensures timely project progress and technical accuracy of activities. The specialist actively contributes to food scouting, trend analysis, and continuous improvement of the product portfolio, proposing concrete and applicable ideas. ## Responsibilities

  • Independently manage product development projects from ideation to launch, coordinating internal stakeholders (R&D, Production, Marketing, Sales).
  • Ensure project timelines are met and PLM documentation is updated accurately, verifying the completeness and accuracy of information shared by various stakeholders.
  • Propose process and product improvements, contributing to cross-company projects.
  • Collaborate on pricing, packaging, and commercial positioning, evaluating the economic impact of development choices.
  • Analyze product performance (sell-in / sell-out) and support margin and cost evaluations, proposing optimization actions.
  • Conduct continuous food scouting and market benchmarking, translating insights into actionable proposals.
  • Actively participate in cross-functional meetings with clear, proactive, and timely contributions. ## Profile
  • 2-4 years of relevant experience in structured environments
  • preferably in the food industry (Category Management, R&D, Product Management, or Trade/Brand in FMCG).
  • Proven experience in independently managing product development projects.
  • Ability to work autonomously and effectively interact with production, marketing, and cross-functional teams.
  • Preferable experience with SAP or similar ERP systems and PLM management.
  • Degree in food, economics, or engineering fields (Gastronomic Sciences, Food Technologies, Management Engineering, Economics, Marketing, or related fields).
  • Genuine interest in food products and market dynamics. ## MUST HAVE
  • Excellent knowledge of Excel.
  • Ability to analyze and interpret sales data (sell-in / sell-out).
  • Ability to structure clear, concise, and action-oriented presentations.
  • Operational autonomy and prioritization skills.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with a cross-functional working attitude.
  • Precision and attention to detail in managing data and documentation.
  • Adaptability to change.
  • Curiosity about food products and market orientation.
  • Italian and English proficiency required. ## NICE TO HAVE
  • Knowledge of SAP systems or other ERP platforms.
  • Knowledge of the German language.
  • Familiarity with the Swiss food market and its consumption dynamics. ## Contact
  • Applications can be submitted via the “Apply Now” link addressed to Annalisa Sattin – HR & Development Manager. Please provide your resume, recent work references, and a brief statement explaining why you wish to join us. Only applications meeting the required criteria will be considered.
