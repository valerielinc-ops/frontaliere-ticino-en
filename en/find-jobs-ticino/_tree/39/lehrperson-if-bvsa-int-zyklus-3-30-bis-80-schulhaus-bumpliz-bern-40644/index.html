Full description

Portrait The school Bümpliz/Höhe is one of four schools in the school district Bümpliz in the west of the city of Bern. 800 children and young people are taught here by around 100 teachers.

The 15 classes of cycle 3 are located in the school house Bümpliz. We carry Model 4 with mixed-level classes in all subject areas.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for as of February 1, 2027:

Tasks

Our pedagogical concept provides for various forms of teaching:

  • Self-regulated learning in professional worlds
  • Interdisciplinary teaching in thematic worlds
  • Year-round project teaching
  • Ateliers on art and crafts
  • Workshops on interdisciplinary competences
  • Rituals at the beginning of the day

The IF teachers accompany and support students in particular with self-regulated learning and interdisciplinary skills.

In the role of learning coaches, all teachers contribute to the learning success of young people. The learning coaches are the contact persons for the guardians.

Detailed information on the pedagogical concept of our school can be found online at: schulkreis-buempliz.ch/buempliz hoehe/konzept z3

Requirements

You have a diploma in curative education or a comparable education or are in the process of completing your studies. We are also open to rule teachers who want to immerse themselves in curative education. Even lateral entrants with teaching experience are welcome to apply.

They are open to innovative forms of teaching and enjoy working in multi-professional teams.

We offer

  • A very committed, helpful and humorous college.
  • An educational concept for the modern school.
  • Involvement and exchange in specialist groups and teams.
  • Multi-professional cooperation with standard teachers and school social work.
  • An environment for actively shaping the digital transformation in schools.
  • A place of work in the heart of Bümpliz with good public transport connections.

The workload can be flexible between 30 and 80 percent, depending on availability.

We offer the possibility of permanent employment as SHP from August 2027.
