Full description

Childcare intern (80%) With 41,000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees.

The school-supplementary childcare of the Chur City School currently offers for 800 kindergarten and school children at various locations lunch and afternoon care as well as care during school holidays in the day care centers.

Would you like to gain practical experience in the social and educational field and prepare yourself optimally for an education in childhood pedagogy or social pedagogy HF/FH? Then you are exactly right with us!

Childcare intern 80% (6 months)

Your tasks

  • You accompany the children through everyday life, create an atmosphere of trust and make them feel comfortable, safe and taken seriously.
  • Together with the team and the children, you create a varied, creative free time in which play, fun and your own ideas have space.
  • You promote participation by actively involving the children in decisions, taking their needs seriously and giving them space to shape their everyday lives.
  • You bring your own ideas and strengths and implement smaller projects independently and enthusiastically.

You bring that with you

  • A teaching degree, a secondary school or a maturity degree.
  • Interested in education in the social or educational field.
  • Joy in working with children as well as an open, respectful and responsible way.
  • Team spirit, commitment and reliability.

This is what awaits you

  • An exciting and versatile internship with valuable insights into the pedagogical and social field.
  • Personal support and support during your internship period.
  • A motivated team and an appreciative working atmosphere.
  • The opportunity to take responsibility and contribute your own ideas.
