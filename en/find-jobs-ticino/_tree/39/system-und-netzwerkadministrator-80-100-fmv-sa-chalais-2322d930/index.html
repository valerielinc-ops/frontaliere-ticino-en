We are the Valais-based company that will become the Swiss market leader in hydropower production. In order to strengthen its "networks" department, FMV is looking for a

System and network administrator/in 80\-100 %

In this function, you work in the area of network systems from FMV.

Your mission

Their main task is to install, configure, manage and maintain the company’s IT\-OT\ infrastructures, including networks and servers, to ensure their proper operation and availability. They work with the OT\-CISO to define the concepts used in the OT\-IT\ infrastructure. You will also play a critical role in protecting the organization’s data and systems by implementing appropriate security measures, monitoring potential threats and responding to security incidents. They assess and integrate new technologies to improve the efficiency and performance of existing infrastructures.

  • Your profile *
  • HES\ degree or federal certificate of competence in computer science or a diploma recognised as equivalent
  • 3 to 5 years of experience in similar roles, such as system administrator or network administrator
  • Knowledge of IT/OT\ architectures and information systems in the industrial environment (SCADA)
  • Good experience in Windows\-\& Linux\ system administration
  • Experience in the network\-\& Firewall\ administration (CISCO \- PaloAlto)
  • Category B driving licence
  • Accuracy and precision, analytical skills
  • Self-employment, initiative and flexibility
  • Ability to work both independently and in a team
  • Mother tongue French or German with good knowledge of the other language (B1\) as well as good knowledge of English (B2\)
