Full description

Experience a modern workplace where your precise work is appreciated. You are actively involved in the quality assurance and optimization of CNC programs. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Setting up and operating CNC rotary milling machines
  • Quality assurance and quality control of manufactured parts
  • Optimize and adapt existing CNC programs
  • Preparation of production documents and performance of first-piece inspections
  • Active participation in continuous process improvement
  • Monitoring of production machines and manufacturing processes

Requirements

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a polymechanic (mandatory)
  • Independent, structured and exact working method
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Readiness to work in regulated two-shift operation (early shift 06:00-15:00, late shift 15:00-00).

Offer

  • Exciting, varied activities with responsibility
  • Internal training opportunities
