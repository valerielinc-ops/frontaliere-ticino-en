Salary Range: CHF137,900.00 - CHF256,100.00 Job Description Summary Director, International Business Communications #LI-Hybrid Location: Basel, Switzerland Shape how a global business connects, engages, and inspires—this is your opportunity to make a meaningful impact at scale. As Director, International Business Communications, you will play a pivotal role in driving strategic communications across a diverse and fast-evolving International Business Unit, reaching over 220 million patients worldwide. Working at the heart of leadership decision-making, you will translate complex business priorities into compelling narratives, lead high-impact campaigns, and harness innovative approaches including artificial intelligence to elevate engagement, alignment, and performance across markets. This is a unique opportunity to partner closely with senior leaders, influence transformation, and help bring to life the International ambition through powerful storytelling and communication strategies. Job Description Key Responsibilities

  • Translate complex strategies into clear, compelling narratives, editorial plans, and scalable content across markets
  • Drive high-impact content programs, including International Ambition and Artificial Intelligence strategy initiatives
  • Lead communications for major business moments and events, including flagship International engagements
  • Partner with regional communication teams to co-create assets, share best practices, and enable local adaptation
  • Collaborate with senior leaders to align executive, leadership, and business communications around shared priorities
  • Use insights, analytics, and industry trends to optimize communication strategies and measure engagement impact Essential Requirements
  • Degree required; advanced degree preferred (Public Policy, International Relations, Political Science, Communications, or related field)

  • Proven expertise developing and delivering international communication strategies across multiple markets
  • Strong experience partnering with senior executives, providing strategic counsel in complex, matrix organizations
  • Demonstrated ability to translate business strategy into compelling narratives and high-impact content
