Full description

Klinik Arlesheim is a leading centre for integrative medicine. For more than 100 years we have successfully combined conventional medicine with anthroposophical medicine. Inpatient care is provided to up to 90 patients in the fields of oncology, internal medicine and psychiatry/psychosomatics. Our offer is complemented by an oncological day clinic and other outpatient offers such as family medicine and nine specialist outpatient clinics. We are there for our patients around the clock, whether in our 24-hour emergency, through acute care at home (Hospital at Home) or in direct personal contact. For our clinic we are looking for a civil service provider in the area of patient transport from 28.06.2027 or by arrangement.

Their tasks

  • Preparation and support of patients for upcoming transports
  • Carriage of bed and wheelchair transport and accompanying services for examinations, therapy and exits
  • Support for operational logistics tasks and implementation of internal scheduling
  • Provision, cleaning and inspection of wheelchairs, beds and other means of transport
  • Assistance in station work, especially in the distribution of meals and drinks
  • Carrying out general clean-up and cleaning work

They bring with them

  • Rapid understanding and communication
  • Mastery of the German language in word and writing
  • Connected and social thinking
  • Flexibility, reliability and resilience
  • Independence and team spirit

We offer

Well-founded introduction to a varied and responsible activity. You can expect a good working atmosphere in a value-oriented hospital, a good public transport connection and our job ticket.
