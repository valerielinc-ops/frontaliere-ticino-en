Full description

"Expect to grow", personally and professionally: At Siegfried, we offer exciting challenges and many opportunities to showcase your talents. As a trusted and respected partner in the pharmaceutical industry, we meet the highest standards of safety, quality and sustainability, while cultivating a culture that allows you to thrive. Join a growing global company where diverse perspectives and experiences come together in a collaborative environment. Here you will find a workplace that values collective success as much as your individual contributions—giving long-term opportunities to evolve and have an impact.

Your Role

o ensure the coordination, quality and regulatory compliance of your perimeter activities, in accordance with GMP requirements and HSE rules. You supervise a team of 5-7 employees, and support their development.

A privileged partner for internal and external clients, you work closely with Quality Control, Analytical Development, Quality Assurance, Regulatory Affairs and Project Management teams.

Your main responsibilities

Conduct stability studies: costing and rating, protocol writing, planning, sampling organization, sample management, results review, trend studies, reporting and communication to clients.

Ensure regulatory follow-up: stability of site monitoring studies, updating of retest dates and storage conditions, contribution to Product Quality Reviews and participation in audits and inspections.

Coordinate analytical activities related to stability studies and the characterization of reference substances, in collaboration with relevant teams.

Ensure reliability of equipment and availability of resources: monitoring of areas and equipment monitored for temperature and humidity, maintenance and organisation of maintenance, monitoring of IT solutions, supply of equipment and consumables, and availability of valid reference substances.

Animating and developing the team: organizing activities, accompanying employees, monitoring performance indicators and updating working procedures and instructions.

Your Profile

  • Chemical engineer training, ideally with specialization in analytical chemistry.
  • Expertise in stability studies and reference substances, with good control of applicable GMP and regulatory requirements.
  • Ability to coach a team and coordinate activities involving several services.
  • Rigor, organizational sense and ability to manage priorities in a demanding environment.
  • Team spirit, relationship ease and ability to convince in a matrix organization.
  • Autonomy, discernment and sense of responsibility in your area of expertise.
  • English

Founded in 1873 in Zofingue, Switzerland, Siegfried has become a global network of 16 sites spread over three continents. With a team of more than 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to the industrial scale and manufacture safe medicines for patients around the world. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few organizations able today to ensure the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished galenic forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most reliable partner in the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operators manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
