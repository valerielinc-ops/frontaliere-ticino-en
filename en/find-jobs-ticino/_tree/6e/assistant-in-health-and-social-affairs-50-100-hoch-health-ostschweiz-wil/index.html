Assistant doctor / doctor 100%

Fellow Emergency Medicine

Interdisciplinary emergency centre

Place of work Wil

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Care of patients in the interdisciplinary emergency center and in the integrated emergency practice (INP) with around 25,000 patient contacts per year
  • Participation in the resuscitation team
  • In case of interest and appropriate suitability cooperation in the emergency medical service
  • Development perspective for / to the senior physician / senior physician (i.V) possible

What you bring for this job

  • Completed medical studies with at least two years of clinical experience in emergency medicine relevant subjects such as surgery, internal medicine, anesthesia, intensive care medicine, pediatrics, cardiology or neurology
  • Ideally experience in emergency medicine and interest in the career track of interdisciplinary emergency medicine
  • Completed courses such as ACLS, ATLS and basic course Emergency Sonography are beneficial
  • Flexible, team-capable, self-employed, committed and responsible personality
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Our offer to you

  • Special highlight in German-speaking Switzerland: Interdisciplinary emergency medicine and treatment of patients of all ages
  • Personal and professional support on the way to / to the emergency medicine specialist incl. structured career planning
  • Wide range of internal and external training courses including daily 30-minute structured training (Mo–Fr), CRM training in the shock room, ACLS, ATLS, basic course Emergency Sonography, Adaptive learning about NEJM Knowledge+ and NEJM Resident 360°
  • Continuing education authorizations: Category 2 Clinical Emergency Medicine SGNOR (1 year), Emergency Medical Location, General Internal Medicine Category IV (1 year) and Ultrasound - FA Sonography, POCUS Basic Emergency Onography Component 1
  • Temporary position for one year – with option for extension and further development in the house

Your application

  • Entry as of 1 October 2028 or short term by agreement
