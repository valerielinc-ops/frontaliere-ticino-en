Full description

The Office for Energy and Transport is the Cantonal Competence Center for Energy and Public Transport Issues. The use of hydropower and other renewable energy sources as well as a sustainable and efficient use of energy are among the core topics of the Energy and Transport Office. We primarily process the approval of concessions and projects to use hydropower. ## Mansioni

  • Cooperation in the implementation of the cantonal hydropower strategy
  • Project management of the various homefalls, on the one hand as project manager, on the other hand as representative of the cantonal in the home commissions
  • Thematic leadership in matters of the recovery and operation of the cantonal power plant portfolio, and in the construction of the corresponding structures
  • Development of decision bases in connection with home cases or Concession renewals
  • Economic evaluations of power plant companies
  • consultation of municipalities in home affairs, communication with municipalities
  • coordination with external service providers and industry partners in matters of home affairs
  • data management of individual power plant installations (procurement, evaluation and management) with regard to home cases ##
  • Requisiti - Higher education or technical college degree in the field of construction or energy July 2026 or by agreement a project manager/in home cases.
