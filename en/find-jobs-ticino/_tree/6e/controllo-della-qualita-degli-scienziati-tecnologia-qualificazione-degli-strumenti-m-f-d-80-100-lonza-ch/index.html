As a member of the QC Biologics, PhysChem Tech Team group in Visp, the Scientist (f/m/d) supports in analytical instrument qualification, promotes the adherence and continuous improvement of Quality Control and ensures lifecycle of the equipment

Key responsibilities

  • Initial qualification, requalification, service of the equipment, for the physical chemistry laboratory (centrifuges, balances, watherbaths, UV/VIS, photometer, turbidity, osmolality etc.) according to guidelines and procedures
  • Planning and coordination of related activities
  • Ensuring communication with the responsible QA and QC employees. Cooperation with laboratory analysts
  • Triggering and processing of change requests, CAPA, deviations. Working in LIMS, Documentation Management System, and other required system, software
  • Troubleshooting for the laboratory equipment
  • Training for the laboratory analyst in usage of the equipment

Key requirements

  • Bachelor in chemistry, biotechnology, life science, or at least 1 year experience in laboratory with technical knowledge for the instruments
  • At least several years of experience in the pharmaceutical industry
  • Knowledge of analytical instrument qualification
  • Strong background in cGMP regulations
  • Structured, focused and well-organized working attitude. Open-minded for new ideas and suggestions, highly motivated and dynamic drive. Teamwork, as well flexibility for various tasks in technology team. Willing to learn
  • Good verbal and written communication in German and English.
