Full description

The Bündner Cantonal School is an important educational institution with about 1'000 students from all three cantonal languages. As a department of the Higher Education Office, it offers middle-school courses at the long-term and short-term school as well as at the Fach-, Handels- and Informatikmittelschule. The school secretariat is the first contact point for the learners and parents, processes central processes of the annual school business and supports the management in administrative tasks and in the implementation of various projects. We are looking for our secretariat by 1. August 2026 or by agreement a school secretary/school secretary.
