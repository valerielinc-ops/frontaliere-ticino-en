Full description

Your tasks

Become the heartbeat of our production! As a maintainer (f/m/div.), it is your mission to keep our facilities running. Together as a team, you try not only to repair the plants, but also to solve preventive problems. Your tasks essentially include:

  • You assume responsibility for the maintenance and maintenance of our machine park. Whether a mechanical problem or an electrical challenge
  • you find the cause and solve it sustainably
  • With an analytical sense you show potential for improvement and implement it
  • On predefined days (twice a year) you work with the whole team on investment audits and self-responsible pikettdienst (4-5 weekends a year)

Your profile

  • Training: Basic technical training such as automatics, electricians, polymechanics (m/f/div.) ideally with a further training as a maintenance specialist
  • Personality and working method: Flexible, self-responsible, independent personality with a systematic and precise working method and the willingness for shift work (3-shift) and Pikettdienst
  • Experience and know-how: SAP and MS Office skills are advantageous
  • Languages: Good language skills in German

Contact and additional information

Benefits

  • A young, dynamic and experienced team with a collegial approach in a family environment awaits you
  • In this position, you can free your creativity for solutions and benefit from personal scope for action
  • The shift work (3-shift) allows you to reconcile work and private life
  • You can look forward to advanced training and further education opportunities With us you have the opportunity to make the most of your ideas and skills. Progressive employment conditions and benefits, a high level of technology, innovative machinery from our own special machine construction and diverse career opportunities.
