Full description

In your capacity as head or head of clinic with a specialty in gynaecology and obstetrics at the Reproductive Medicine and Gynaecological Endocrinology Unit, you ensure, under the supervision of the medical hierarchy, the care of patients and you contribute to the supervision and training of internal physicians and trainees. You are in an outpatient consultation activity in the following areas: Reproductive medicine and fertility preservation, Gynecology and menopause,

  • Gynecological imaging (infertility tests, specialized ultrasounds, diagnostic hysteroscopy).
  • Your mission is to ensure technical consultations and gestures in the field of reproductive medicine, gynaecological endocrinology and gynaecological imaging.
  • You are involved in pre- and post-graduate education, as well as in unit research projects.
  • You take part in gynaecology and obstetrics guards and reproductive medicine pickets. Evolved in a stimulating academic environment, within a multidisciplinary team, you supervise and supervise intern physicians and trainees, thus contributing to their training.
