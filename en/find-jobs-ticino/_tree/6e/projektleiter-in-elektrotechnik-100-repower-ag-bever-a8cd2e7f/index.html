Project manager: in electrical engineering 100%

We are looking for an immediately or by agreement

Repower is one of the leading energy companies in Switzerland. The 120-year history of Repower begins in the very south of Graubünden in Valposchiavo. In addition to the headquarters in Poschiavo, we have further locations in Landquart, Zurich, Ilanz, Küblis, Disentis, Grono and Bever. As an employer, Repower relies on initiative and trust to promote the creativity of its approximately 730 employees. The energy industry of tomorrow needs fresh ideas. We offer you an inspiring working world in which you and your team develop future-oriented solutions for a sustainable energy future.

The "Electrical Engineering" planning department consists of 15 employees and is responsible for the technical project planning of new plants, conversions and expansions as well as renewals in the field of electrical engineering. The employees work at various locations: Poschiavo, Ilanz, Bever, Landquart and Küblis.

You will do this

  • Leadership and responsibility of projects in substations, from planning to successful implementation
  • Technical project planning of new plants, conversions and expansions as well as renewals in the \ and medium voltage range (substations/power plants)
  • Independent planning and design of systems, components and concepts
  • Calculation, identification and assessment of plant design
  • Preparation of tender documents, tender requests and evaluation

You bring that with you

  • Studies / further education in the field of electrical engineering (FH/HF) or comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in the engineering of substations and in the management of projects in the field of energy distribution
  • In-depth electrotechnical knowledge in medium and high voltage installations
  • Negotiation-proof German (min. B2\), Italian advantageous
  • Independence and reliability distinguish you
  • Networked thinking, teamwork and high commitment
