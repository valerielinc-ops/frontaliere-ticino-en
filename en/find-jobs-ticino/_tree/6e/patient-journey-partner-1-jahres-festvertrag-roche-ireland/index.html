The Position Promotional customer facing role unlocking value along patient journeys

The Patient Journey Partner (PJP) is at the centre of the new Roche organisation, directly accountable for delivering better outcomes for patients, their community, the ecosystem and the organisation, faster. The PJP is the primary point of contact and the local face of Roche, partnering closely with all stakeholders who directly touch the patient journey in a given therapeutic or disease area, gaining a deep understanding of their needs in order to maximise value creation.

This role is field-based and locally embedded in the ecosystem.

Mission and responsibilities As Patient Journey Partner, you drive the field-based realisation of disease area outcomes, leveraging local network expertise to deliver a 'Triple Win' for patients, the ecosystem and Roche. Your key responsibilities will include:

Bringing True Value to Patients and Stakeholders

  • Responsible for the field-based realisation of outcomes for a given therapeutic area, creating value via a Triple Win approach.
  • Responsible for promotional engagement as well as gathering related insights to help inform the development and delivery of therapeutic area outcomes.
  • Acts as a system thinker, overseeing the patient journey in its entirety and identifying shared purpose among stakeholders.

Trusted Partnership & Stakeholder Engagement

  • Acts as a trusted partner and the primary (though non-exclusive) point of contact between stakeholders and Roche.
  • Co-creates solutions in partnership with external stakeholders, in compliance with applicable rules and regulations and in alignment with internal stakeholders.
  • Brings the outside in, working with partners to uncover opportunities to transform outcomes for patients via the Triple Win, faster, drawing on local and network expertise and collaborating with other PJPs where needed.
  • Liaises closely with internal and external stakeholders to realise sustainable access to healthcare for patients across the patient journey for products post-license.
  • Ensures great experiences whenever stakeholders in the ecosystem engage with Roche employees, products and services.
  • Lives the Visionary, Architect, Coach, Catalyst (VACC) model.

Product Stewardship & Lifecycle Support

  • Stewards Roche's products through their lifecycle and supports their appropriate use in a factual and balanced manner.
  • Facilitates the provision of information on disease area and relevant Roche solutions upon request.
  • Strategically prioritises projects and initiatives and delivers on these to improve patient outcomes.
