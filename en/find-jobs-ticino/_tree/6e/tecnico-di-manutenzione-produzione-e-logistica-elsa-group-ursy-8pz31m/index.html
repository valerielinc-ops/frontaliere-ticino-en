Full description

Elsa Group is one of the leading Swiss dairy industry groups and is part of the Migros Group (Migros Industry). Elsa Group is an expert in the manufacture and packaging of dairy products as well as in the production, refining and processing of cheeses and the production of plant-based products.

Luogo di lavoro: Elsa Group, Elsa Group - Ursy, Rue du Plattiez 11, 1670 Ursy

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Manage and perform preventive and corrective maintenance work on production and logistics facilities
  • Diagnosing faults, performing repairs and ensuring the return to service of equipment
  • Identify root causes of malfunctions and implement sustainable corrective actions
  • Propose and implement technical improvements to optimize the reliability of installations
  • Participate in the development and improvement of preventive maintenance plans
  • Document the interventions in SAP PM (work orders, rooms, hours, technical data)
  • Manage spare parts in collaboration with the technical store (orders, inventory, inventory)
  • Actively collaborate with different departments and pass on technical knowledge to teams
  • Ensure continuous operation of equipment by participating in the team schedule (3x8)

Required

  • 1 to 3 years experience: in industrial maintenance, ideally in the agri-food sector
  • Initial or continuing training completed: in a technical field (polymechanical, automation, electricity) or equivalent training
  • Mastery of IT tools including MS Office and GMAO, ideally SAP PM
  • Knowledge in automation and industrial maintenance, with a strong focus on fault analysis and resolution
  • Independent, organized, rigorous and able to manage stress and priorities in a demanding environment
  • Spirit of analysis, sense of responsibility and ability to propose continuous improvements
  • Available to work in shifts (3x8)
  • French (Very good knowledge)
  • German (preferably)
  • English (preferably)

  • Submit an application: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. It is not necessary to attach a photo to the CV or a cover letter.
