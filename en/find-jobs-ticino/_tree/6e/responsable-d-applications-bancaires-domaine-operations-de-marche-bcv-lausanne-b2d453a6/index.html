Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. At the border between operations and IT Linked to the Operations Operations and Projects Support Sector of the Operations Department, the ROA service (operational responsibility applications) - DOP (Operations Department) manages Department applications. To strengthen this team, based at the Prilly Administrative Centre, we are looking for a Announcement Responsible for bank applications - Market Operations domain Your main missions:

  • Ensure operational and functional responsibility for several applications.
  • Be the preferred contact point for Front-Office , Back Office , Accounting and external suppliers users for all applications related issues under your responsibility.
  • Ensure the availability, stability and performance of applications by ensuring daily monitoring of production, incident management and coordination of corrective actions.
  • Piloting the functional evolution of applications: gathering and analysing needs, drafting specifications, coordinating developments, organizing tests and accompanying change.
  • Coordinate production, release and relations with external publishers and data providers.
  • Identify, analyse and reduce operational risks by implementing controls, monitoring mechanisms and automation solutions.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of processes by simplifying manual activities and offering high added value optimizations.
  • Ensure documentation, application governance and support during internal and external audits. Your profile:
  • University, HES or equivalent training in finance, management computing or similar fields.
  • Experience in the role of Application Owner, Responsable Applique or Support Applique in a banking institution, a market room or a publisher of financial solutions.
  • Good knowledge of financial products and processes Front-to-Back.
  • Strong skills in functional analysis, specification writing, test management and change management.
  • Demonstrated ability to analyze complex incidents, coordinate multiple stakeholders, and ensure continuity of service for critical applications.
  • Spirit of continuous improvement, autonomy, sense of responsibility and internal customer orientation.
  • Excellent communication capabilities to interact with business and technical interlocutors.
  • Perfect mastery of French and good professional English (minimum B2); German is an advantage.
  • Swiss resident or willingness to settle permanently in Switzerland.
