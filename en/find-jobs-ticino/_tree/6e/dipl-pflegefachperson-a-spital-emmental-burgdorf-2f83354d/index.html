Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

You like it when no day is like the other and still everything is well organized? In our day clinic, you will look after patients from different specialist areas who enter and leave on the same day. Plannable working days from Monday to Friday, without night and weekend services, are important to you? Then you will fit us. For our day clinic at the Burgdorf location we are looking for 1. March 2027 or by agreement

Tasks

  • You look after patients of various specialties before and after outpatient procedures, examinations and therapies
  • You conduct the initial interviews, prepare the patients for the procedure and then monitor them until exit.
  • You plan, manage and document the care process independently in the clinic information system
  • With doctors, the surgical and examination areas as well as other specialist services you coordinate closely and ensure a smooth daily routine
  • When leaving, you inform and instruct patients and relatives so that care at home continues safely.

Profile

  • You are dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH and bring experience in acute care, ideally from a day clinic, surgery or wake-up
  • A high patient pass and changing areas of expertise motivate you. You keep track and set the right priorities
  • You communicate clearly and sensitively, even if there is little time for a conversation
  • You work independently and structured and like to take on responsibility
  • Further education is a matter of course for you and you are actively involved in the further development of the processes
  • You master the German language stylishly (B2 proof for foreign-language applicants is the minimum requirement)
