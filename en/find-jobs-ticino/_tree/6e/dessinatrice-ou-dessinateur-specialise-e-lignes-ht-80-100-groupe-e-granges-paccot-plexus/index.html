Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2450 employees, including 245 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Energy Solutions Directorate develops global offers combining consulting, design, execution, maintenance and operation. It is also the single entry point for major projects, energy efficiency and automation, electrical engineering, facility management and innovation.

Mission

  • Achieving 2D technical plans for overhead and underground lines and associated energy infrastructure;
  • Produce the implementation plans, details and execution necessary for the implementation of the projects;
  • Integrate cadastral, topographic and technical data to ensure quality and compliance of deliverables;
  • Participate in field surveys and ensure that plans are updated in accordance with project developments and site returns;
  • Ensure document management, archiving and traceability of technical drawings;
  • Collaborate with project leaders and ensure quality, safety, cost and timeliness requirements are met.

Profile

  • SWC of draftsman in civil engineering or geomatics, with specialization or experience in infrastructure, or training deemed equivalent;
  • Professional experience from 3 to 5 years minimum in the realization of technical plans;
  • Very good knowledge of AutoCAD for the design and development of 2D plans;
  • Experience in infrastructure, network or civil engineering projects; experience in power lines is an asset;
  • An advantage is the knowledge of AutoCAD Civil 3D, 3D modeling or metal construction;
  • A valid driver's license.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together for tomorrow's energy"
