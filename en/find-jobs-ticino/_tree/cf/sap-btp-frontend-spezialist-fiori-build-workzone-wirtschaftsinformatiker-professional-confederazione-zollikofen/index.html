Full description

Put to the Point

You take over the holistic configuration and administration of the SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP) as well as the design and maintenance of the SAP Build Workzone and the Fiori administration. In this key position, you ensure that the SAP environment is optimally operated and further developed.

  • Date of entry: 01.04.2026
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 609-17170
  • Location: Eichenweg 3, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Set up and manage sub-accounts, cloud services and entitlements, carry out monitoring, optimize performance
  • Process incidents as well as implement and maintain security specifications and role/authorization concepts
  • Develop guidelines and basics in the area of SAP BTP governance and configure and operate workzone sites including workspaces and pages
  • Adapt and further develop themes, navigation and widgets in SAP Build Workzone, integrate third-party applications and services, develop concepts and provide technological consulting
  • SAP Fiori Launchpad including Spaces, Pages and Business Catalogs configure, maintain and operate, activate standard Fiori apps and implement customer-specific requirements via UI adaptations, app variants and translations
  • Design and maintain Fiori role concepts and authorizations using PFCG as well as coordinate cooperation with development, module consulting, system teams, authorization and security teams and develop technical solutions with relevant stakeholders

This makes you unique

  • Completed university degree (FH/uni) in computer science, business informatics or a related discipline or an equivalent higher education with corresponding professional experience of several years
  • Several years of practical know-how in the administration and operation of SAP environments, in particular SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP), SAP Build Workzone and SAP Fiori
  • In-depth knowledge in the configuration, further development and operation of SAP BTP, SAP Build Workzone and SAP Fiori Launchpad Experience in dealing with SAP role and authorization concepts as well as security requirements
  • Basic knowledge of SAP development (ABAP, JavaScript, SAPUI5, OData services) is an advantage
  • Experience with agile working methods (SAFe, Scrum, Kanban) as well as knowledge in the use of CI/CD and monitoring tools
  • Analytical and conceptual thinking, pronounced customer and service orientation as well as independent and solution-oriented working methods, teamwork and ability to collaborate in interdisciplinary teams as well as the competence to convey complex technical issues appropriate to the addressee
  • Active knowledge of two official languages and good knowledge of English
