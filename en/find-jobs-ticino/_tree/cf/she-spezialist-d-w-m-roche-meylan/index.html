The Position

Missions

As part of the application of French regulations, Group directives and standards, the SHE specialist defines and guarantees the proper functioning and monitoring of procedures related to Health, Safety, Hygiene and the Environment (SHE) in order to prevent and reduce SHE risks within the company. Furthermore, the SHE specialist brings the Roche Group's SHE policy to life within the subsidiary.

Main Responsibilities

DU and Prevention of Physical and Psychosocial Risks at work

  • Generally speaking, the SHE specialist is responsible for defining and implementing the professional risk management and prevention policy within the company and with respect to our customers, in compliance with applicable standards and procedures and the dissemination of a culture of prevention in the company. The SHE specialist also manages the OHS organization and guides and stringent lines (training, zoning, etc.)
  • He is also responsible for the analysis and prevention of risks inherent to workstations, whether at headquarters, roaming or with our Customers, in connection with our DUERP, in order to carry out targeted actions (e.g. road risk, gesture and posture, PPE, ergonomics, etc.)
  • He participates in the detection, management and monitoring of potential cases of RPS in conjunction with stakeholders such as HR, elected officials and the health center
  • He has shared responsibility for monitoring accidentology and associated administrative management (accident investigation, declaration, record keeping, reporting, etc.) and for identifying, if necessary, immediate corrective actions in order to limit the risk - particularly in relation to group initiatives.
  • He organizes periodic visits to the facilities with stakeholders to ensure proper compliance and application of site security measures.

Onboarding of new employees and regulatory monitoring

  • Organization of SHE training: identification of needs, planning, monitoring of the welcoming process for new hires, but also monitoring and organization of refresher training (electrical clearances, SST, etc.)
  • Is responsible for ensuring that the company is always up to date with regulatory developments in the areas of personal safety and the environment, in its practices and procedures as well as in updating the Group's platform.

Waste management

  • Improvement of our processes and solutions to reduce the production of our waste on site and monitoring of waste at our service providers (notably WEEE)

Reporting

  • Defines and carries out on time the reporting associated with its activity
  • (SHE reporting, and contribution to the CSR report, BDES etc...)

Internal and External CSR

  • Participates in the CSR steering committee and in the preparation of the annual report
  • Communication actions across all activities
  • Customer part (prevention plan)
  • Prepares CSSCT subjects related to their expertise

SHE and SQB audit

  • Implementation of audits with critical suppliers (as part of a qualification or monitoring: documentary or physical audit)
  • Participates in the Supplier Quality Board (SQB)

Biosafety Officer

  • Assessment and management of biological and chemical risks
  • Compliance and regulations: ensure strict compliance with local and international laws
  • Raise awareness and train employees in good practices
  • Supervise decontamination and sanitation protocols
