Full description

Join our team as a career advisor! Are you passionate about career choice issues and supporting young people at the end of compulsory schooling motivates you? Want to help them find their way and build their professional future? This job is for you! As a guidance counsellor, you will accompany students at the end of compulsory schooling by conducting career guidance interviews with students in the last years of compulsory education. You will conduct workshops and classroom sessions and collaborate with the various partners in the school, training and professional world. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Xavier de Montmollin, Head of Centre, 022/557.59.58

  • Conduct individual interviews to support students and adults in their career and career choices
  • Provide reliable and up-to-date information on training, occupations and the labour market.
  • Analyze beneficiary profiles, interests and skills to help them define a realistic project.
  • Conduct and interpret tests and guidance reviews to inform the skills, motivations and potential of users.
  • Work with education, training and economic actors to ensure consistent follow-up of courses.
  • Ensure continuous professional monitoring to maintain knowledge and improve practices.
  • Master of Science (MSC) in psychology, counselling and guidance or specialized vocational, academic and career guidance training provided by a high school or an institution recognized by the SEFRI required.
  • Master of Advanced Studies (MAS) in management, human resources and career management or training deemed equivalent desired.
  • Relevant professional experience
  • Good knowledge of the regional economic fabric
  • Good knowledge of the Vaudian training system
  • Basic knowledge of user computing required
  • Driver's licence required Disclosure of possible criminal history is required for this function.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
