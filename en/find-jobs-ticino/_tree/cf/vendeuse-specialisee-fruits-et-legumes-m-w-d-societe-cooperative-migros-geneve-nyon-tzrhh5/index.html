Full description

You know the fruits and vegetables on your fingertips and you like contact with customers? Join a team where your business sense, energy and expertise contribute every day to customer satisfaction. Between product development, advice and radius management, no day is similar. Ready to take on the challenge? Apply now!

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Cooperative Society Geneva, MM Porte de Nyon, Chemin de la Fontaine 5, 1260 Nyon

Important remarks

The use of women aims for simplicity, but our recruitment is open to all.

Mansioni

  • Every day you guarantee an attractive fruit and vegetable section, well stocked and in accordance with quality standards.
  • You advise clients with professionalism to help them choose and value seasonal products.
  • You ensure the freshness of the products by regularly checking their quality and presentation.
  • You are actively involved in the implementation of commercial actions and the theatricalization of the radius.
  • You help limit losses by ensuring rigorous inventory management and commodity rotation.
  • You help maintain a clean, welcoming and pleasant sales environment for customers.

Required

  • 1-3 years of experience: in the field of retailing fruit and vegetable products.
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Retail Manager
  • You use your expertise in fruits and vegetables to ensure a quality offer and meet customer expectations.
  • You bring your sense of service, dynamism and relationship ease to create a positive shopping experience.
  • You contribute to the success of the department thanks to your team spirit, rigor and autonomy in daily tasks.
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Food: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.

Contatto

Candidature and contact Michaël Lassère Talent Acquisition Specialist

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Professional interview: During the professional interview, the manager and/or other specialized person, or even the team, asks specific questions about the knowledge to accomplish the work. If possible, then there is more time to get to know the workstation and team in a broken stick discussion. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and establishment of contract (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week

Grado di occupazione: 80-100%
