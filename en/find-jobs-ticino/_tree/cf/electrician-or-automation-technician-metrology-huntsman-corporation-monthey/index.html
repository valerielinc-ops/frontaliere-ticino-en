We are looking for an Electrician or Automation Technician (Metrology) for our site in MONTHEY Electrician or Automation Technician (Metrology) ##

  • Responsibilities
  • As part of a specialized team, you will be responsible for implementing metrology: manage the integration of advanced metrological concepts throughout the site
  • Calibration of instruments: ensure the precision and reliability of our equipment by managing the calibration program
  • Repair and maintenance: intervene on electrical and pneumatic systems to ensure uninterrupted productivity, from production to laboratories
  • Participation in projects: collaborate in the study and commissioning of systems
  • Supervision and documentation: manage with precision the work of external collaborators and maintain a rigorous documentation, in accordance with Huntsman standards and directives, particularly in the field of EHS. ##

  • Education: CFC in electricity or automation, or equivalent training

  • Industry knowledge: OIBT Art. 13 certification is a benefit
  • IT skills: Office tools and maintenance management through SAP PM ## Desired skills

  • Autonomy and flexibility: ability to work independently and adapt to different situations
  • Rigor and method: a methodical approach for precise and reliable execution of activities
  • Proactivity: anticipate needs and problems before they occur
  • Problem-solving skills: ability to identify and effectively solve problems
  • Availability: openness to participate in out-of-hours assistance service ##
