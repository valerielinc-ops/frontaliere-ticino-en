Your field of activity on the psychotherapeutic station

At our psychotherapeutic station, we focus on treating patients with the following diagnoses: F3: Affective disorders (e.g. depressive episodes, recurrent depressive disorders) F4: Neurotic, stress and somatoform disorders (e.g. anxiety and stress disorders, somatisation disorders) F6: Personality and behavioral disorders Together with the interprofessional treatment team, you create a framework in which recovery, stabilization and sustainable change are possible.

Their tasks

  • Personnel, professional and organizational leadership as well as further development of your nursing team including needs-based personnel deployment planning.
  • Ensuring and quality development of evidence-based, person-centered relationship and psychiatry care.
  • Design of the therapeutic environment in close coordination with the medical-psychotherapeutic service and the specialist therapies
  • Employee development and team development: Active support for employees and support for the practical training of learners and students
  • Collaboration in development projects: Constant optimization of station concepts, management processes and treatment processes.

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience in psychiatric or psychotherapeutic care.
  • Leadership experience as well as completed leadership training (or willingness to complete one).
  • High social and communication skills, resilience and a solution-oriented and appreciative leadership style
  • Identification with the mission statement and Christian values of the clinic SGM
