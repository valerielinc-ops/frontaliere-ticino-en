Full description

Become part of our logistics team in Visp as logistics specialist for external logistics service providers 80-100% (m/f/d). In this position, you will design the interface between internal departments, customers and international logistics partners. In this varied role, you take on the operational control and optimization of complex inbound and outbound processes, accompanying implementations at external 3PL service providers and actively contribute to ensuring the highest GMP and GDP standards. Look forward to a dynamic environment with international projects and a great deal of scope for design.

What we offer you

  • An Agile Career and Dynamic Work Culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous benefits in the areas of lifestyle, family and leisure. The full list of our global services can also be found at: Benefits - Visp

Your tasks

  • Coordination of logistical tasks between logistics, internal departments and external customers and service providers.
  • Responsible for operational control, coordination and continuous optimization of inbound and outbound processes within intralogistics and production supply in compliance with GMP and GDP requirements.
  • Implementation, handover and sustainable assurance of Lonza processes at external logistics service providers (3PL).
  • Operational responsibility for compliance and continuous improvement of the defined Lonza processes at the 3PL partners.
  • Presentation and coordination of standard processes at customer, project and kick-off meetings.
  • Close cooperation with internal stakeholders, customers, project managers, quality assurance and external logistics partners.
  • Proactive support of cross-divisional logistics and supply chain teams.
  • Ongoing review, standardization and optimization of shipping and logistics processes taking into account regulatory and operational requirements.

Your requirement profile

  • You have a completed commercial, logistical (preferably logistics specialist) or CPT vocational training with eidg. Certificate of ability and ideally already have professional experience in a pharmaceutical company
  • You have practical experience with SAP EWM / WM / MM
  • You are flexible, communicative, resilient and reliable
  • 40-60% willingness to travel throughout Switzerland for implementations, process transfers, audits, training and operational support at various 3PL locations.
  • Readiness for 2-shift operation
  • English skills round off your profile

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
