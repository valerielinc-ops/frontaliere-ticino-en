Full description

This is what awaits you

You value direct customer contact and provide competent and personal service with your commitment. In the customer service of our general agency, you look after our customers and support our insurance and pension consultants in day-to-day business.

This is what they do with us

You are an important contact person for our customers – on the phone, at the counter and in written exchange. They accept requests and process them independently. With your friendly and solution-oriented approach, you contribute to a high level of customer satisfaction.

Your tasks include the creation of offers, the processing of applications and the processing of contract changes and other customer concerns. You recognize the need for advice and make appropriate appointments with our insurance and pension advisors.

In addition, our consultants support you administratively and organizationally and work closely with the internal specialist departments. The support and support of our learners is also part of your task. Together with the team, you ensure reliable processing of business cases and a smooth process in customer business.

This is what you bring with you

  • Commercial training completed
  • Professional experience in the insurance industry, ideally in customer service or sales support
  • Enjoying personal and telephone customer contact as well as a pronounced service orientation
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Customer and sales-oriented thinking as well as enjoyment of working together in a team
  • fluent knowledge of German
  • Further language skills are welcome

Working with us

We offer you a versatile and responsible activity with direct customer contact and a varied area of responsibility. You will become part of a dedicated team that supports each other and pursues a high standard of service and quality together.

For this function, the degree as an insurance intermediary VBV, profile non-life and life, is planned. If you do not yet bring it, we support you with tailor-made internal training on the way to the necessary qualification.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Basel?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
