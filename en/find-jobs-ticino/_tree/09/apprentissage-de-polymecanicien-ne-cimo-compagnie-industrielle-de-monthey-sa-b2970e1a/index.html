Full description

Polymechanical learning CIMO Compagnie industrielle de Monthey SA is a Swiss company founded in 1997 with 450 employees in 80 specialized trades. As part of its strategy for sustainable development and innovation, CIMO is active, inter alia, in the fields of energy production and distribution, waste treatment, technical maintenance, engineering, automation, hygiene and safety, environment, laboratory and occupational medicine.

  • We are looking for candidates for one: Polymechanic Learning
  • Your profile
  • Good spatial representation
  • Methodical spirit
  • Accuracy and thoroughness
  • Interest in metal working and machine handling
  • Manual skill
  • Technical sense
  • Ability to calculate
  • Ability to work as a team
  • Training plan
  • The learning takes place over 4 years and leads to the Federal Certificate of SWC Capacity.
  • Theoretical training:
  • Courses are given at the Vocational Technical and Trades School (EPTM) in Sion for one or two days a week.
  • Practical training:

The first 2 years of your apprenticeship will take place at the workshop\-school of the Intercantonal Vocational School of Chemistry (EPIC) in Monthey. The last 2 years will take place at CIMO under the responsibility of a trainer.

  • The inter-company courses (CIE) take place at the Vocational Technical and Trades School (EPTM) in Sion.
  • The trade
  • After the first 2 years at the EPIC workshop where you will have become familiar with the basics of the trade, you will integrate one of the sectors of the CIMO Mechanical Workshop.
  • As part of your apprenticeship, you will be asked to discover different areas of competence such as assembly, machining and pumps.
  • Your job will be to repair, revise, control and monitor industrial equipment.
  • Future prospects
  • After getting your SWC, you can consider the following enhancements:
  • Federal patent
  • Federal diploma
  • Diploma of technician
  • Bachelor of Arts HES
  • Etc.
