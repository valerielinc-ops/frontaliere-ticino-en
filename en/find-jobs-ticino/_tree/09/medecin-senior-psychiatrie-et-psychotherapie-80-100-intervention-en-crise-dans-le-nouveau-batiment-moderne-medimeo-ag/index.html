Competently absorb psychological crises and spend the evening in Basel: As a senior physician, you bear the medical responsibility on a modern acute care unit of the crisis intervention and work in a multi-professional team. Some patients come in the middle of the night, others after weeks in which simply nothing went. Here they end up with people like you who are familiar with acute crises: in their own focus for crisis intervention, with their own chief doctor and in a house that was built precisely for this purpose. Those who arrive very ill are closely accompanied in the acute care unit with integrated intensive care. If it goes up, no one has to change the clinic. The treatment continues in the day clinic, on an outpatient basis or at the patient’s home, in the same area. So you not only experience the most difficult moment, but also keep an eye on the way afterwards. And after work? Work is done in the Basel bid. If you prefer to live in the city, you can take the train within 10 minutes in Basel. There you can drift in the Rhine in the summer after work, and with the tram you are in Weil am Rhein in about ten minutes, so already in Germany.

Their tasks

  • They are responsible for the diagnosis and treatment of patients in acute psychological crises.
  • They assess the patient: clinically, provide the indication and plan and review the therapy, up to the transition to a day-clinical, outpatient or visiting treatment
  • They supervise assistant doctor: inside and psychologist: inside and train them further
  • They work closely with care, therapies, social work and relatives
  • You develop treatment concepts together with your supervisors and the team

Your Competences

  • Psychiatry and psychotherapy - already acquired or within reach
  • Ideally experience in acute psychiatry or crisis intervention and rest even in tense situations
  • Enjoyment of multi-professional cooperation, an independent, well-organized and responsible way of working
  • German skills at a high level
  • Foreign diploma? You will be supported organizationally and financially in obtaining your MEBEKO permit by us

Their advantages

  • Lots of free time: 25 days of vacation, two additional days after the third year of service, plus eleven holidays per year
  • City within reach: Basel in about 15 minutes by S-Bahn
  • You live in the city and commute relaxed
  • Clear salary: 13 monthly wages in a transparent wage system with defined wage bands
  • Professional support: own medical director for the crisis intervention and a multi-professional team in which responsibility is shared
  • Modern workplace: new building on a green campus, easily accessible by public transport, parking garage on site
  • Well-insured: 100% wage up to 730 days in case of illness or accident
