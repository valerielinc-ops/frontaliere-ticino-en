Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for 1. December 2026 or by agreement reinforcement. Tasks: • Together with your colleagues, you will provide paediatric radiological care at the Children's Hospital Central Switzerland. Paediatric Radiology is integrated into the Institute of Radiology and Nuclear Medicine at the Lucerne Cantonal Hospital • Experienced specialists train you in all areas of paediatric radiology. You will deepen your knowledge in diagnostic imaging from the newborn to adolescence • Thanks to our large catchment area and about 16'000 to 17'000 child radiological examinations expected per year You have a broad and varied range of investigations • They are actively involved in clinical everyday life, participate in reports and interdisciplinary conferences and are involved in teaching radiology assistants as well as in research activities. Participation in the weekend service of General Radiology is expected Requirements • You have the title of specialist radiology or are close to it and are seeking a specialization in paediatric radiology • You have a great interest in pediatric radiology and would like to develop further in this versatile focus • Ideally, you already have initial experience in paediatric radiology • Your initiative, team-capable and independent working method as well as your enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation round off your profile We offer: • You can look forward to working in a collegial and professional environment in one of the most modern departments for pediatric radiology in Switzerland with its own equipment park which includes 2 X-ray machines, 1 fluoroscopy, 2 ultrasound machines, a 3 Tesla MRI and a photon counting CT. In addition, there is a close connection to the Institute of Radiology and Nuclear Medicine • You can expect a challenging and varied job with a wide range of paediatric issues in one of the largest children’s hospitals in Switzerland. • Further and further training in all areas of paediatric radiology as well as a structured further education in the focus of paediatric radiology are of course part of our job offer • You also benefit from attractive employment conditions, numerous benefits and the opportunity to participate in clinical research and teaching projects.
