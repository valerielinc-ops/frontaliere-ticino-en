Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic in Bad Zurzach offers rehabilitation at the highest medical level. The range includes neurological, musculoskeletal, cardiovascular, and rheumatological rehabilitation. In our modern infrastructure, we combine medical excellence with individual care. Our interdisciplinary team accompanies patients with a holistic approach on their way back to daily life. As a deputy to the head of nursing, you actively contribute to leadership and represent me in all relevant matters during my absence. You share responsibility for the personal, professional, and administrative leadership of our management team. Together with the head of nursing, you accompany, promote, and strengthen employees in their professional and personal development and contribute to an open, respectful, and appreciative work culture. You will have the opportunity to shape and accompany change processes. You are part of the management team of the Bad Zurzach rehabilitation clinic and significantly contribute to the development of the location. You work in an interprofessional team with other nurses, therapists, and doctors. What you bring Qualification: Completed training as a certified nursing professional HF/FH including NDS as Dipl. Department Head HF. Permits: You have a professional practice permit (BAB) or are willing to apply for it. Experience: You have professional experience and leadership experience. Personality: You enjoy working in an interprofessional team, think in a networked way, and always look for solution-oriented approaches. Your positive, communicative nature and strong quality awareness distinguish you. IT skills: Proficient in the use of common computer programs (Outlook, clinical information system, planning software Polypoint). Collaboration: Work in a modern working environment and actively contribute. Inspiring and collegial work atmosphere: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care. Above-average social security contributions: Benefit from attractive social security contributions and comprehensive benefits. Training and further education: Generous co-financing of training and further education. Discounts: Use discounts with various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies, and our membership at Benefit@work.
