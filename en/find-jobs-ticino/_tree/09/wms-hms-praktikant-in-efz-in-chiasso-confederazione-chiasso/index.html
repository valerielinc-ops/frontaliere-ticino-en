Put to the Point

This internship program offers students in the third year of school at a commercial school (SMC) the opportunity to complete a 52-week internship in the area of commercial middle school (MP1) in order to obtain the Swiss Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ) and Professional Maturity (BM2). During the internship you will deal with various administrative tasks in different areas.

  • Date of entry: 01.08.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: 401-19419
  • Place of work: Via Milano 23, 6830 Chiasso

You can make this contribution

  • Support for requests from internal and external users
  • Implementation of administrative and organisational tasks
  • Collaborate with other departments to ensure an integrated approach to administrative activities
  • Contribution to the planning of visits and events
  • Participation in internal training sessions to develop specific skills

This makes you unique

  • You have good academic achievements

  • You have excellent interpersonal and communication skills

Vocational training FYD – We form the future!

The training company EJPD promises excitement and diversity. It deals with socio-political issues such as the coexistence of people of Swiss or foreign nationality, asylum issues, internal security or the fight against crime. Civil status or civil rights issues are just as much a part of the FJPD’s remit as the supervision of gambling. The optimal start to the world of work in August 2027 for committed and motivated young people. Our experienced trainers promote the development of professional, methodological, social and personal skills of the trainees and provide effective support during the internship in a multilingual environment. The opportunity to interact and relate with other interns and learners further enriches this education. The entire internship takes place in the State Secretariat for Migration, Region of Ticino and Central Switzerland, in the administrative building of the Federal Asylum Centre (FOZ) in Chiasso. Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
