Who are you?

As a collaborator or collaborator IKEA Food, you will strive to make people smile and at the same time provide irresistible food offerings in a welcoming atmosphere that gives our customers a good reason to return.

  • You have experience in a high-flow customer-oriented environment, ideally in hospitality or sales.
  • You are a affable person, you like to work directly with customers and you always strive to provide them an excellent service.
  • You are physically durable and able to stand for a prolonged period of time even without the presence of customers.
  • You are flexible and able to work during the opening hours of the store.
  • You are a strong team player, but you also like to assume responsibility for working individually.
  • You are comfortable in a highly dynamic and stimulating environment and work well under pressure.
  • You have a good knowledge of computer systems, especially MS Office.
  • You speak fluently Italian and have a good level of English. Knowledge of another national language is an advantage.

Your responsibilities

  • Serve and meet customers with an empathetic and caring attitude based on IKEA values.
  • Ensure that food, beverages and products in your area of responsibility are of the highest quality and freshness and are presented in the most attractive way.
  • Always keep clean, organized and inviting your area of responsibility.
  • See the sales area through the eyes of our customers to continuously improve and meet their needs.
  • Receive payments and manage cash transactions at the cashier.
  • Find more effective ways of working to reduce costs, including waste control, portion size correction, production level control and the choice of the most suitable methods for preparing dishes.
  • Respect and follow the internal rules, policies and daily operating routines regarding issues such as quality, food security and stocks.
