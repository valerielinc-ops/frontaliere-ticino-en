Full description

Help us shape a strong safety culture from the start as we open our new warehouse in Neuchâtel next year. You’ll actively support managers and employees in developing workplace safety and health protection and putting them into practice every day.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Neuchâtel am Rhein, Hans-Buck-Straße 3, 7939 Neuchâtel am Rhein

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Point of contact for concerns and issues relating to health and safety at the site, supporting the responsible safety officer (if you have not yet completed your training)
  • Collaborating with internal and external stakeholders, authorities, auditors and service providers
  • Implementing projects in the field of occupational health and safety, and ensuring these aspects are taken into account in the implementation of general projects
  • Ensuring compliance with legal, regulatory and internal requirements in the field of occupational health and safety in collaboration with the relevant stakeholders and support functions (Sifa, fire safety officer, etc.)
  • Conducting, developing and refining training programmes, briefings, risk assessments, incident and accident analyses and audits, and providing support in the implementation and follow-up of the resulting measures
  • Raising staff awareness of safety and general compliance issues, and implementing, establishing and embedding a safety culture

Requirements

  • 3 Years: Safety
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EFZ): Safety
  • Strong communication skills and a hands-on approach
  • Experience in an operational or logistics environment, ideally in the field of health and safety
  • Very good written and spoken German and English
  • French would also be an advantage
  • Team spirit and a willingness to work with relevant stakeholders to find meaningful solutions
  • Identifying opportunities for improvement and constantly striving to further optimise health and safety
  • Analytical thinking and a structured, independent and solution-oriented approach to work come naturally to you
  • Qualification as an occupational safety specialist or the desire and willingness to obtain this
  • Training as a fire safety officer
  • Use creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • German (good knowledge)
  • English (flowing)
