Full description

Shaping efficiency together Mikron Machining is the leading operator in customized and highly productive manufacturing systems for the production of complex and high precision metal components For our headquarters in Agno we look for a Machine Testing Engineer 100% (m/f/d) Main responsibilities

  • Perform commissioning, testing and optimization of machines and plants at Mikron and at the customer
  • Actively collaborate with the project leader during the entire project implementation cycle, supporting planning, advancement, acceptance and resolution of technical issues, ensuring effective communication between project and internal organization
  • Check the correct mechanical, productive and qualitative operation of the machines according to the technical and contractual specifications
  • Adjust and optimize mechanical, CNC, handling, peripherals and machining processes
  • Realize, validate and document sample pieces and production processes
  • Support and perform FAT and SAT activities
  • Install and start machines at the customer
  • Perform preventive maintenance, corrective, troubleshooting and technical support on the installed base
  • Form customer personnel on the use, management and maintenance of plants
  • Collaboration with Manufacturing, Engineering, Product Care and Service for the validation of technical changes and continuous improvement of products
  • Draft test reports, technical documentation and intervention reports
  • Operating in compliance with safety, quality and environment
  • Support, depending on company priorities, assembly, testing, installation and technical assistance outside the main allocation area

Your profile

  • Proven experience in the function
  • Mechanical study title
  • National and international travel availability
  • Strong customer and service orientation
  • Good technical analysis and problem solving
  • Ability in the interpretation and application of drawing regulations
  • Ability to read and interpret CQ reports on pieces made
  • Competence in programming on board ISO CNC machine (Bosch or Siemens)
  • Good mastery of the English language

What we offer

  • Innovative and dynamic work environment
  • High technological product portfolio
  • Access to cutting-edge tools and technologies in the industry
  • Collaboration with a team of passionate and competent professionals
  • Continuing training on new technologies
