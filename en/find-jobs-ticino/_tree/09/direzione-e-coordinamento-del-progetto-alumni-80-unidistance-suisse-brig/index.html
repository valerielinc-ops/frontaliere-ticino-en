· Job vacancies Place of work: Brig | Start of work: from now on or by agreement The FernUni Switzerland stands for completely online university courses and is accredited according to the HFKG. It combines flexible, location-independent distance learning part-time with an active, interdisciplinary research culture at Campus Brig. In five faculties – Law, Psychology, Economics, History and Mathematics & Computer Science – we have been shaping modern teaching, learning and research for more than 30 years and count over 2,700 students and around 550 employees. To strengthen our service services and alumni, we are looking for a dedicated personality as: Alumni project management and coordination (80%) Place of work: Brig Start of work: from now on or by agreement Do you want to actively design alumni work, promote projects independently and make a difference for the university? For the expansion of our alumni work, we are looking for a committed personality who leads projects independently from the idea to the implementation and develops and continues the alumni work together with the management of the service. Their tasks

  • Operational implementation of the alumni strategy in coordination with the management of the service
  • Management of projects from conception to implementation to evaluation
  • Building and maintaining relationships with alumni and maintaining and developing the alumni database
  • Design, organisation, implementation and further development of measures and innovative formats for the active retention and involvement of alumnae and alumni, the promotion of knowledge transfer and the strengthening of added value for alumni and universities
  • Identification and activation of relevant alumnae and alumni for projects and collaborations
  • Collection, maintenance and evaluation of relevant data as well as monitoring and evaluation of measures to verify the achievement of targets and for reporting
  • Promoting a culture of giving back and a commitment of alumnae and alumni to the higher education community
  • Further development of processes, instruments and communication measures in alumni work
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation within the service, with other services and with the faculties

Your profile

  • Completed higher education at master level
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in a university environment, in alumni work or in project management
  • Strong project management and organizational competence as well as an independent, structured and cooperative working method
  • Conceptual skills and pleasure in developing and implementing new formats and measures
  • Secure handling of data, digital tools and contact management systems as well as experience in the collection and evaluation of personal data and in measuring success
  • Enjoy building and maintaining sustainable relationships and networks, as well as developing an active alumni community
  • Engaged personality with pleasure in interdisciplinary cooperation and different stakeholders
  • German at native level as well as very good knowledge of French, good knowledge of English advantageous

We offer

  • Research-strong academic environment: You work in a dynamic university context with proven scientific expertise and interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Attractive employment conditions: You benefit from contemporary working conditions, generous social benefits and a wide range of training opportunities.
  • Dedicated team: You become part of a qualified team that actively lives innovation, quality and collaboration.
  • Inspiring working environment: Our campus in Brig is located in close proximity to the old town, the SBB railway station and attractive natural and mountain regions. It is a place of academic exchange with a high quality of life.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
