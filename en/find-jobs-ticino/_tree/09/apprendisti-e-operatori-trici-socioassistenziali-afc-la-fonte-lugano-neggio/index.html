Full description

Over the course of three years of training you will be able to achieve the objectives set by the Ordinance on the formation of OSA operators/trices, address people with disabilities. The apprentices are part of the group of residents and, together with a practical manager, develop their skills through the progressive performance of the training curriculum. It is possible to carry out the apprenticeship at the housing facilities Fonte 3 in Neggio and Fonte 8 in Lugano.

Conditions

  • absolving compulsory schooling

  • preferential title will be given to applications that have already had first social experiences, such as volunteering, summer colonies, orientation internships

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
