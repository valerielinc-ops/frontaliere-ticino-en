Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

Surgery is an interdisciplinary field of activity that requires an optimal interplay between medical, paramedical and logistical competences. We search immediately or by agreement

TOA – technical assistant or IDDO – instrumental nurse, 50-100%

You do

  • Instrumentation and assistance in operations in various specialist areas in compliance with the prescribed hygiene standards
  • Implementation of all preparatory and post-operative measures
  • Proper preparation and use of necessary equipment, instruments and sterile materials
  • Careful documentation of all care activities, including performance monitoring
  • Assistance in ensuring a smooth daily routine in the operating room
  • Professional and close interdisciplinary cooperation with various specialist groups
  • Professional care, maintenance and disposal of instruments, implants, equipment and sterile materials

This sets you apart

  • Completion as surgical assistant or surgical nurse or equivalent training
  • Diversity in various areas of expertise is advantageous
  • Readiness for learning and further development
  • Teamwork, cooperation and motivation to develop in a multidisciplinary surgical team

You will benefit from this

  • A versatile activity with a wide range of applications (general surgery, visceral surgery, gynecology, obstetrics, vascular surgery, neurosurgery, orthopaedics, traumatology, paediatrics, ENT, etc.)
  • An environment that promotes skills development and continuous training
  • Working with bilingual colleagues in a dynamic environment
  • Structured familiarisation and individual support
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of work processes and actively contribute to the quality of care.
