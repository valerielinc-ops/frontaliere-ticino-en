The Position Field Service Engineer (Lisbon/South area) Roche Diagnostics, Portugal

Are you an experienced field service engineer seeking a meaningful role in the healthcare industry? Are you excited about the prospect of joining a forward-thinking, high-tech business that is passionate about improving patients' lives? If so, we have the perfect opportunity for you at Roche Diagnostics, the world's leading provider of diagnostic equipment.

The Opportunity We are looking for a Field Service Engineer to join our Lisbon/South team . In this position, you will be responsible for the installation, maintenance, and repair of high-tech laboratory equipment at our customers in laboratories and hospitals, maximizing customer satisfaction and ensuring that Roche Diagnostics systems meet the manufacturer’s specifications. You will be responsible for site visits for troubleshooting, training & follow-ups, ensuring immediate and proper scientific support. Beyond the technical scope, you will contribute to customer long-term loyalty by adopting a commercial mindset, understanding their business needs, and identifying opportunities to add value while documenting calls and activities on the Roche CRM System.

Who you are

To be considered for this role, you should be able to confidently represent the company at client sites. We are looking for someone who combines technical expertise with strong social soft skills—you must be able to communicate effectively, build rapport, and handle productive customer relationships in a complex environment. You are resilient, able to work under pressure, and possess a customer-oriented attitude. You are a team player that can also work autonomously, and ideally have experience working in an agile organization. You are able to carry out complex tasks with attention to detail, assess a situation, find alternative solutions, and deliver them in accordance with the objectives and values of the organization.

Besides that, it’s required that you have

  • Bachelor or Master degree in Engineering (preferably electronics, mechanical or related)
  • Knowledge of IT network infrastructure, firewalls and installation of Windows and Linux operating systems
  • Experience with laboratory devices (previous experience with Roche equipment is a significant asset)
  • Good level of written and spoken Portuguese and English
  • Driving license
  • Place of residence in the greater Lisbon area
  • Availability for national travel (site visits) and international travel (training)

At Roche Diagnostics, we are committed to invest in our people as well as our business. In addition to a competitive compensation and benefits package, we offer a recognized product training program and continuous support to help you develop your knowledge, skills, and potential. If you are ready to take the next step in your professional development and make a positive impact in the healthcare industry, we encourage you to apply for this exciting field service engineer opportunity.
