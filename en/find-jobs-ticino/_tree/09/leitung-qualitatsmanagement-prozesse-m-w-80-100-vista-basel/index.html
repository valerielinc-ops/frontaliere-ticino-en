Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking at our headquarters in Basel as of 01. August or by agreement a qualified and committed personality as Head of Quality Management & Processes (m/f) 80-100% Vista Eye Practices & Clinics, with its 35 locations and approximately 500 employees, are the largest provider of eye medicine in Switzerland. We offer first-class medical services to our patients and set standards in the disciplines of ophthalmology, eye surgery and laser eye medicine. You lead our team Quality Management & Processes and, together with medical management, site management and specialist areas, develop our quality and process landscape. Their tasks

  • Further development of the existing quality management system and the QM strategy
  • Further develop and ensure a high quality of service along the patient journey with a focus on patient satisfaction and continuous improvement of the patient experience.
  • Establishment and establishment of a binding, cross-site process management
  • Management of audits, CIRS, complaint management and patient surveys
  • Development and tracking of relevant quality and process indicators
  • Group-wide monitoring of sites for improvement and change projects
  • Ensuring regulatory and internal quality requirements that you bring with you
  • Studies or equivalent training in health care, business administration or comparable
  • Several years of experience in quality and process management in the healthcare sector, ideally in a hospital, clinic or outpatient medical service organization
  • Knowledge of the Swiss healthcare system
  • Sound QM training, e.g. CAS/DAS, Quality Manager
  • Experience in the analysis, optimization and standardization of processes
  • Leadership experience or management of complex cross-cutting projects
  • Implementation strength, persuasion and a pragmatic working style We offer you
  • Benefit from at least five weeks of holidays, strong social benefits such as an above-average employer participation in your occupational pension and a private accident insurance worldwide.
  • Work-Life Balance: We offer flexible working hours and home office, at least five weeks of holidays, attractive pension and insurance services as well as professional training support
  • Active participation in an innovative environment with a focus on quality and precision
  • No mere QM management function: You get creative leeway and a clear mandate to further develop quality and process management across our locations and actually bring improvements into implementation
