Full description

Do you like the health interventions in the new and the renovation? This function covers installations, connections and leak detection.

For one of our customers, we are looking for a CFC or equivalent sanitary installer

Responsibilities

  • Construction of sanitary facilities (nine and renovation)
  • Ensure installation and connection of various appliances and valves
  • Read the plans and execution diagrams
  • Troubleshooting and leaking
  • Welding (steel, copper, crimping, PE)
  • Ensure the installation of drinking water pipes and sewage disposal
  • Order and manage equipment on site

Your profile

  • CFC sanitary installer (an asset) or equivalent experience of several years in Switzerland
  • Ability to work independently and organizedly
  • Person motivated, serious, conscientious, with initiative
  • Good team spirit and sense of collaboration
  • Driver's licence (an asset)

We offer

  • A long-term mission with the possibility of a fixed place. A 100% personalized follow-up by your advisor throughout your journey.

We support you in your professional development through ongoing training in relation to your profession, fully supported by the Temptraining Training Fund.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
