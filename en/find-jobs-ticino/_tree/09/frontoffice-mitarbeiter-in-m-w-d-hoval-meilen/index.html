Full description

To strengthen our service team, we are looking for YOU!

As a leading technology company, Hoval has been inspiring for decades with groundbreaking system solutions for heating and air conditioning technology. With us, you make the difference. With your commitment, your knowledge and your personality, you help to offer our customers the best possible service every day and to actively shape our success story.

Your main activities

  • You are in contact with our customers and partners by phone and email (B2C and B2B)
  • You actively contribute to day planning and ensure a smooth flow in day-to-day business
  • You accept customer concerns and faults and maintain them in our system
  • You also take on administrative tasks

We offer you

  • Varied and responsible tasks in a collegial and helpful team
  • Successful family business with du-culture
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Location: field miles

Your profile

  • Excellent communication skills oral and written in German, other languages beneficial
  • Experience as a call center agent (out-/inbound, front office, etc.)
  • Experience in complaint management
  • Very resilient even in hectic situations
  • Basic commercial training or equivalent experience
  • SAP knowledge is an advantage

Take the chance to contribute your strengths and make a difference together with us. Look forward to exciting tasks and a motivated team. Apply online now and become part of Hoval!
