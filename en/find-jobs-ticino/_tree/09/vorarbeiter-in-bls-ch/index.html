Full description

Do you like to lead a team, lend a hand yourself and feel at home on construction sites? Then an exciting task awaits you with us in a unique infrastructure. Whether from track construction, civil engineering, road construction or a related construction profession: If you have management experience and want to take on responsibility, you have come to the right place. Together with your team, you will ensure that our railway infrastructure remains safe, reliable and in the best condition.

This is waiting for you

  • Technical management of a working group together with the foreman
  • Organization and coordination of daily work on the construction site
  • Assistance in maintenance and renewal work on tracks and points
  • Collaboration in maintenance work along the railway infrastructure (e.g. embankments, drainage, winter service)
  • Ensuring efficient, high-quality and safe construction site operation
  • Rectification of faults on the assigned railway network (on-call service)

What you bring to the table

  • Completed apprenticeship in track construction, civil engineering, road construction or a comparable construction profession
  • Further training as a foreman or the motivation to complete it
  • Experience in leading employees or construction site teams
  • Category B driving licence
  • Enjoy working outdoors and be willing to work in shifts and on call
  • High sense of safety and responsibility
  • Structured, reliable and independent way of working
  • Ability to motivate employees and achieve goals together

Your benefits – here's what you can look forward to

  • We promote the most flexible planning of your tours and shifts
  • Get a GA travelcard 2. Class, a GA travelcard 1. Class or a half-fare card.
  • You have free time when others are working. There are surcharges for workloads at night and on weekends.
  • It is possible to buy five to ten additional vacation days per year.
  • Financial support for external training and an exciting internal learning and course offer are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. What's more, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
