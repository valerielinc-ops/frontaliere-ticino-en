Full description

Area: Food & Beverage / Restaurant Service

For our customer, an exclusive luxury hotel in St. Moritz, we are looking for a dedicated and experienced Chef de Rang (m/f/d) to strengthen the restaurant team.

Their tasks

  • Independent support and management of its own service station.
  • Professional welcome, advice and support of international guests.
  • Acceptance of orders and professional serving of food and drinks.
  • Ensuring a first-class and individual guest service.
  • Preparation and control of the entire service area.
  • Compliance with the highest quality, hygiene and service standards.

Your profile

  • Completed training in the gastronomy or hotel industry is beneficial.
  • Professional experience as Chef de Rang or in a comparable position.
  • Experience in upscale gastronomy or luxury hotel industry desired.
  • Good knowledge of German, English or Italian.
  • Careful and professional appearance.
  • Strong guest and service orientation.
  • Teamwork, flexibility and responsibility.

We offer

  • 13 monthly salaries.
  • 35 days of vacation per year.
  • 43.5 hours working time per week.
  • Staff accommodation if needed.
  • training and development opportunities.
  • Perks in the F&B area.
  • Free sports activities.
  • High quality working uniform.

Application Please send your current CV with information about your professional experience, your language skills and your availability to our recruitment agency.
