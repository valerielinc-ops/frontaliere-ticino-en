The Position Job Overview The Facilities Engineer is responsible for delivering engineering projects, coordinating technical maintenance activities, and ensuring the reliable operation of building systems and critical utilities at the Roche Sant Cugat site. Reporting to the Lead HARD Services, the role acts as the technical interface between Roche and external service providers, ensuring maintenance activities, engineering projects, compliance requirements, and technical documentation are executed safely, efficiently, and in accordance with Roche standards. The position combines technical expertise with strong project coordination and stakeholder management, focusing on execution rather than strategic ownership.
