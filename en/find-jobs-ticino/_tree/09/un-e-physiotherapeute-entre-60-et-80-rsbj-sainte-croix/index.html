Full description

Type of Contract: CDI Rate: 60% Date of entry: Immediately or to be agreed

Within the RSBJ, your missions will be particularly varied

  • As part of the transversal geriatric program and in order to prevent functional decline, you will intervene with hospitalized patients
  • You will also provide support to people hospitalized in medicine or surgery, and will contact the person at home to prepare for their discharge from the hospital.
  • Within the tree of life EMS, you will collaborate with the interdisciplinary team working with the 70 residents
  • By accompanying outpatients, you will also have to intervene with our clients at the Temporary Reception Centre.
  • In an interdisciplinary approach, you will organize interventions with the aim of preserving the development, independence and autonomy of people
  • You will be actively involved in the development of the institution's service and projects by working on integrated care and will be involved throughout the health path.

Your profile

  • Bachelor of physiotherapist HES or equivalent
  • Diploma recognized by the Swiss Red Cross
  • Experience confirmed in hospital service and geriatrics an asset
  • Use of IT tools
  • Organizational and priority management capacity
  • Adaptability and openness to change and innovation
  • Interest in teamwork and sense of communication

We offer

  • A variety of work within a network enabling integrated care across all areas covered by the RSBJ
  • The opportunity to participate actively in the deployment of our strategic plan focused on values of proximity, responsibility and respect.
  • Working in a dynamic and interdisciplinary team
  • The opportunity to work alongside ASPMAD, a home care and assistance organization in the Nord Vaudoise region.
  • Free parking and easy access
  • Discounts to more than 150 brands
  • A wage and social benefits according to the collective labour agreement in the public health sector (FHV).

Entry into office: immediately or to be agreed Full applications to: SRBJ – Human Resources - Roses 29 – 1450 Ste-Croix, jobs@rsbj.ch
