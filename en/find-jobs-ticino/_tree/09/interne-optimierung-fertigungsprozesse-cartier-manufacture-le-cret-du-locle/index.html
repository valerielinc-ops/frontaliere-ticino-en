Missions Within the Department of Studies & Methods – Traditional Watches, we work to continuously improve products and processes related to watch design and industrialisation. In this context, we offer an internship aimed at optimising our choices in the production of 3D printing and conventional machining.
  • Responsibilities To carry out an inventory of the positures and their manufacturing range (3D printing vs conventional machining) in collaboration with the Industrialization and Mechanical teams. Analyze and characterize available 3D printing types. Analyze the functional requirements of the positions as well as the technical, economic and logistical criteria associated with each manufacturing process: precision, life, time, cost, material and frequency of renewal. Identify the limits, optimisation opportunities and positivity families best suited to each technology. Define and formalize a decision-support matrix to guide the choice between 3D printing and conventional manufacturing from the design stage. Test this matrix on concrete cases, adjust it with the different professions and accompany its integration into the practices and reference points of the department. WHAT WE RECEIVE?
