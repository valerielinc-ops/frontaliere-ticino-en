Full description

As a firmly rooted company in the Seeland region, we offer comprehensive transport and storage solutions and ensure cleanliness in over 30 communities with our waste transports. Our success is based on motivated employees and modern infrastructure. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • You ensure that our conveyor belt and storage system runs reliably and everything is ready in the morning
  • The conveyor belt system requires your technical know-how – Maintenance, cleaning and repair of disturbances include
  • Starting at 1:30 a.m.: Vehicles are parked, damages are documented and minor repairs are done directly yourself
  • Before the operation starts, take care of the recurring maintenance work on our state-of-the-art storage facility
  • Variety is guaranteed: assistance with shelving conversions, cleaning work with the machine as well as various cleaning work indoors and outdoors, also the toilets must be clean
  • With your commitment, you keep our operations running and ensure that plants, vehicles and infrastructure remain in operation.
  • Your working day starts at 01:30 and ends at around 10:30, including an hour break. Usually Monday to Friday, but can also be a Saturday

Why do you succeed?

  • You have a basic craftsmanship and enjoy technical and practical tasks
  • Technical understanding and ideally experience in reading schematics
  • Minor repairs, moving vehicles or refilling liquids? No problem for you
  • Working independently is just as important to you as working together in a team
  • Flexibility and responsibility are among your strengths
  • The common MS Office programs you master
  • You can express yourself well orally and in writing in German - further language skills are beneficial

Your benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Modern work equipment: We keep up with the times - you have modern and safe work equipment at your disposal
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
