Put to the Point

Do you have debt collection experience, negotiating skills and a good sense for demanding cases? With us, you take responsibility for complex federal demands and find viable solutions.

You can make this contribution

  • prepare payment proposals
  • Make installment agreements and assess applications for reorganisation and deferral
  • Independently create definitive and provisional legal openings of all administrative units in French, German and Italian
  • Written and telephone contact with debtors, authorities and legitimate third parties

Shaping the future with us

The Federal Financial Administration (EFV) is an office of the Federal Department of Finance and monitors the federal finances. We create the budget, the financial plan and the bill of the federal government. All important projects of the departments with an impact on the federal finances go over our table. The work in the EFV is diverse: we also take care of financial statistics, accounting and national financial equilibrium, we raise funds on the money and capital markets and ensure the willingness of the federal government to pay. The financial administration is characterized by good cohesion among employees, flat hierarchies and modern, centrally located workplaces. Become part of our team!
