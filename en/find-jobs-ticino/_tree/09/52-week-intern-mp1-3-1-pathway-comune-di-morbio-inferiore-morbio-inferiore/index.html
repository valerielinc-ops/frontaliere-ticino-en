Recruitment of an intern 52 weeks – MP1 3+1 course The Municipality of Morbio Inferiore hires an intern from the Middle School of Commerce and who needs a 52-week commercial practice to obtain the Federal Certificate of Competence and the Commercial Vocational Baccalaureate. Period and location of the internship The internship will take place from 1. September 2027 to 31 August 2028 at the Financial Services of the Municipality. Submission of applications Applications must be submitted by 30 November 2026 to the Municipality by letter or e-mail to the address morbioinf@morbioinf.ch attaching:

  • the curriculum vitae with a recent photograph;
  • copy of the most recent school evaluations (second year school report

and evaluations available for the third year). The Municipality reserves the right to request any additional documentation. Conditions Recruitment is subject to passing the third year of the Middle School of Commerce and will take place at the sole and unquestionable discretion of the Municipality. The Municipality reserves the right to proceed with recruitment before the deadline for the submission of applications in the presence of a candidate deemed suitable. Other information Any further information can be obtained from the Municipal Chancellery, tel. 091 695 46 10. For the Municipality The Mayor Claudia Canova The Secretary Giovanni Keller

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
