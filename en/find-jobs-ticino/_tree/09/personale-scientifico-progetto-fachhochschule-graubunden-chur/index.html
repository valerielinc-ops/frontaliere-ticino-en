Full description

As a university, FH Graubünden focuses on dynamic thinking and proactive action. Our more than 2400 students in education and training develop into responsible personalities. In our interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary research, we focus on applied future technologies, development in the Alpine region and entrepreneurial activity. As an employer, we promote equal opportunities and diversity. To strengthen the User Experience Team at the Swiss Institute of Information Science (SII), we are looking for a user experience team immediately or by agreement. In this role, you participate in innovative research, development and service projects. Together with interdisciplinary teams, you will develop user-centred solutions at the interface of people, design and technology. They plan and conduct user research studies as well as usability tests and UX evaluations, analyze user needs and derive requirements for digital products and services. On this basis, you will develop wireframes, prototypes and interaction and design concepts. In teaching, you accompany students in project and bachelor theses and support the further development of courses in the field of user experience design. | Your profile

  • University degree (Bachelor or Master) in Human-Computer Interaction, User Experience Design, Psychology, Information Design, Design, Computer Science, Information Science or a comparable discipline
  • Experience in user research, usability engineering or UX evaluation
  • Knowledge of user-centered design as well as the design and prototyping of digital products
  • Ideally, experience with accessibility, AI-supported methods and tools (e.g. Figma Make) along the user-centered design process (e.g. for research, ideation, prototyping or evaluation)
  • Enjoying scientific work and working with companies and research partners
  • Strong communication personality with organizational skills | We inspire you with
  • a motivated and interdisciplinary team within an innovative working environment with modern UX methods and technologies
  • varied research and service projects with renowned practice partners
  • the opportunity to further develop your own research interests and publish them scientifically
  • annually three weeks of time credit for personal further training as well as the opportunity to complete part-time master's studies or a doctorate
  • modern employment conditions and many sports and cultural offers
