Full description

Your team

Operations ensure secure and efficient processing of Pictet's banking and investment funds. Our teams ensure that all activities comply with regulations and manage risks. By developing and maintaining a modern and robust infrastructure, we guarantee reliable and high-quality services for asset management, asset management and real estate. In this context, we are looking for a Corporate Equity Specialist on Investment Funds within the Clients & Asset Services department. You will be responsible for various types of billing and customer notifications related to investments in traditional and alternative funds. Your mission will be to provide quality services to all stakeholders, to ensure a high level of customer satisfaction and to contribute continuously to the optimization of our services.

Your role

  • Ensure the execution and monitoring of current cash transactions, in accordance with internal and external procedures, directives and conventions.
  • Notify investors of changes affecting their funds (events for information and event of choice).
  • Process instructions related to elective events and instruct the administrators of the respective funds.
  • Process complex internal requests from the Front Office.
  • Identify and manage transaction risks across all investment fund activities.

Your profile

  • Strong experience in banking (3 to 5 years), and ease with figures.
  • Experience in investment fund transactions (traditional, alternative and/or private equity).
  • Team spirit, sense of collaboration and customer service.
  • Versatility and ability to manage multiple tasks with rigour and organization.
  • Proactivity, curiosity and interest in innovation and continuous improvement.
  • Curiosity for IT tools and proposal strength to improve them.
  • French and English.
  • Database knowledge, an asset.
  • Knowledge of the Avaloq tool, an asset.
  • Home in Switzerland required.
