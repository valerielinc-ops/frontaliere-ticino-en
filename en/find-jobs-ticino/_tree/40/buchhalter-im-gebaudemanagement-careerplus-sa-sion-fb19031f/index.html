Accountant in Building Management

Have you developed a strong expertise in real estate accounting and aspire to autonomously manage the accounting of a stewardship portfolio? This opportunity may interest you!

Our customer is a real estate company located in the Valais region. In order to complete his team of about a dozen people, he is looking for an accountant.

Your duties with your new employer

  • Complete accounting management of a portfolio of buildings and commercial complexes
  • Seizure, control and tracking of records related to managed assets
  • Annual leases of owner-owned real estate companies
  • Preparation of financial statements and accounts for owners
  • Close collaboration with the management team and the role of contact or interlocutor for owners

You offer to your new employer

  • A proven experience in real estate accounting, ideally three years or more
  • A proven practice of stewardship accounting
  • A first experience of real estate company loops
  • Knowledge of Quorum or other property management software
  • A strong sense of contact and clear and direct communication
  • Rigor, concentration and ability to conduct your files independently

You can also enjoy the following benefits

  • 5 weeks of vacation
  • Flexibility of working time
  • A human-sized agency that favours direct trade

We've awakened your interest? So apply today!
