Full description

Do you like to get up early and look for a physically challenging activity? Then you're a perfect fit for us! With you, we bring a little more yellow to the streets.

This is what you can make happen

  • Early in the morning (around 6:00 AM), you load the delivery vehicles together with your team and prepare your delivery route.
  • Through your work, you shape the streetscape in the Rhine Valley and embody the values of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the timely and flawless delivery of packages and letters in your area.
  • On your tour, you answer questions from our customers and contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • You regularly work on Saturdays, with shifts divided within the team. You usually work a 5-day week, usually Monday to Friday or Tuesday to Saturday.

What you bring with you

  • With your physical fitness, you can easily load and lift packages up to 30 kg.
  • You enjoy being on the road, have a category B driver's license and ideally A1, or are willing to catch it up before starting your job.
  • You value working in a team, but also enjoy working independently and feel comfortable with large vehicles on the road.
  • You are reliable and work efficiently.
  • Good spoken German skills as well as proficient use of digital devices complete your profile.
