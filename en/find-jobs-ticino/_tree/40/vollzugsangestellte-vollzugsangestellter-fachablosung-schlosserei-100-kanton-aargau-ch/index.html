Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • General supervision, support and control activities in the prison security service
  • Supervision, support and cooperation in the business "Schlosserei"
  • They act without prejudice at any time and consistently implement legal requirements, internal instructions and safety regulations. With your professional actions, you make a significant contribution to the safety of the public, employees and instructed persons.
  • They work three weeks a month at regular working hours and one week at irregular working hours with 2 to 3 night services and a weekend assignment.

What you bring

  • Completed vocational training with a Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ) as a metalworker, plant and apparatus builder, polymechanic, production mechanic, or similar
  • Additional qualification as a specialist / expert for the enforcement of justice with federal law. Professional card or willingness to complete training
  • Reliable and independent working methods, high social skills and emotional stability
  • Understanding and tolerance in dealing with people of different backgrounds, beneficial oral knowledge in a foreign language
  • Minimum age: 28 years, good repute (current extract from the penal and enforcement register)

Your working environment

The Lenzburg Prison provides care for persons who have been sentenced to imprisonment or to a custodial measure. The offer includes 380 prison places for closed prison, pre-trial detention and short sentences.
