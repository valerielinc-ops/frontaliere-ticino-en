Full description

Desideri contribuire allo sviluppo dei veicoli ferroviari del futuro e mettere ogni giorno in pratica le tue competenze tecniche? In questa funzione variegata partecipi a progetti di trasformazione e ammodernamento della flotta e supporti l’analisi e la risoluzione dei guasti tecnici. In collaborazione con diversi settori specialistici contribuisci a realizzare i progetti in modo efficiente e a garantire l'affidabilità dei nostri veicoli. Ti assumi responsabilità, porti le tue idee e partecipi attivamente allo sviluppo della flotta RhB del futuro.

Cosa puoi fare

  • Diagnosis, repair, testing and maintenance of electronic assemblies, printed circuit boards and equipment
  • Independent processing and coordination of internal and external repair orders as well as assessment of new repair options
  • Analysis of recurring error patterns and development of sustainable improvement and repair solutions
  • Technical support for various workshops in the event of complex faults, damage analysis and commissioning of traction vehicles
  • Support engineering projects, tests and measurement drives as well as participation in the optimization of maintenance and repair processes.

Ecco perché sei la persona giusta’

  • Completed training in electrical engineering, electronics or automation with further training in electrical engineer HF or with an equivalent qualification
  • In-depth knowledge in troubleshooting, metrology, repair and testing of electronic components and systems
  • Experience in dealing with technical documentation
  • Diagnostic systems and electronic assemblies
  • Independent, analytical and solution-oriented working method as well as high responsibility for quality and safety
  • Communication strength and pleasure in working with workshops, engineering, specialists and external partners

Cosa ti offriamo’

  • 2. Class General Subscription
  • Free tickets and discounts on rail travel abroad
  • Paid mobile phone and subscription for business and private use
  • Flexible part-time models
  • Attractive social benefits and pension fund with very good benefits
  • Additional purchase of holidays
  • Possibility for internal and external training with financial support from RhB

Ulteriori informazioni If you are looking for a non-ordinary job that is characterized by challenges that cannot be done routinely, then we can certainly offer you this in this function.
