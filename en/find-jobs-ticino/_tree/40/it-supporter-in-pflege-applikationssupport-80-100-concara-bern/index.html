Full description

Tasks

  • You support our employees in general IT first-level support and help with questions about workplace devices, Microsoft 365, Outlook, teams, printers, mobile devices and standard software.
  • You are the contact person for support requests about our care and patient information systems Perigon and EasyDoc.
  • You support the users with questions and problems in the daily use of the two systems.
  • You analyze error messages and narrow down whether it is a user problem, a permission question, a process or a technical failure.
  • Known and standardized problems are solved independently.
  • More complex technical, application or technical issues are escalated to the responsible application managers, IT specialists or manufacturers.
  • You provide support for entry, mutations, exits and user and authorization issues around Perigon and EasyDoc.
  • You will introduce new employees to the basic application of our care information systems and support training and user instructions.
  • You create and maintain simple instructions and assistance for our employees.
  • You support tests for updates, releases or changes and bring your experience from everyday care.
  • You recognize recurring problems and potential for improvement and pass them on in a structured manner to the responsible bodies.
  • You accompany open support cases reliably until the solution and ensure an understandable communication to the users: inside.

Requirements

  • You have completed training in the nursing or health sector, for example as a health care professional EFZ, a qualified nursing professional HF/FH or a comparable education.
  • You bring practical professional experience from nursing or health care.
  • You have already worked with Perigon or EasyDoc – Experience with at least one of the two systems is a prerequisite for this job.
  • You are ready to get deeper into the other system and to support both applications in the future.
  • Experience in Spitex, long-term care or a comparable environment is an advantage.
  • You are very interested in computer science, digital tools and processes and want to develop professionally in the direction of IT.
  • You are safe with PC, smartphone and common Microsoft 365 applications.
  • You can quickly familiarize yourself with new systems and processes.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in words and writing.

This will convince us

  • You enjoy helping other people with digital issues.
  • You can explain technical topics easily, patiently and understandably.
  • You understand the processes and challenges of everyday care and can classify concerns from practice well.
  • You work structured, carefully and independently.
  • When problems arise, you proceed analytically and take pleasure in finding the cause.
  • You know when you will progress yourself and when it makes sense to bring in other specialists.
  • You are service-oriented, reliable and enjoy working in a team.
  • The careful and discreet handling of particularly protective personal and health data is a matter of course for you.

We offer

  • Responsible and varied activity with plenty of room for manoeuvre
  • Participation in innovative digitalization projects in the healthcare sector
  • Close exchange with care, ICT and external partners
  • Modern work equipment and contemporary IT infrastructure
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Individual training and development opportunities
  • Appreciative corporate culture with short decision paths
  • Possibility to actively shape the future of digital care

Spitex Bern and Domicil have merged under the umbrella of the non-profit Concara Group. With more than 2,000 employees, we are committed to integrated all-round care – stationary, outpatient and at home. At Concara Services AG, we provide various services for all operating units of the Concara Group, implement support processes and provide professional support. You come from nursing, know Perigon or EasyDoc from your everyday work and want to develop professionally in the direction of IT?

Then this job could be your next step. For our central IT, we are looking for a person from the care environment who supports our employees in daily IT support and at the same time becomes an important contact person for our care and patient information systems Perigon and EasyDoc.
