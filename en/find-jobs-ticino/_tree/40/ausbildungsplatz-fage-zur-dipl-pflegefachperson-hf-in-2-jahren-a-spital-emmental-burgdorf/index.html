Full description

In the middle of the beautiful Emmental – your new challenge is waiting for you! With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Care with heart and responsibility – become part of our team! What makes us special? As a versatile peripheral hospital with heart and mind, we live a trusting coexistence and a working environment that is characterized by openness, respect and appreciation. Working modernly for us means: acting humanely, with freedom of design and an open ear for each other. Our employees are in the middle of us instead of just there – both professionally and personally. Family-friendly conditions and jointly designed service planning are part of everyday life for us. Are you a health professional and want to take the next step? Then the diploma education is just right for you! With attractive employment conditions, we offer you the start of training in March 2027 or September 2027.

Create care, strengthen people – your field of action with us

  • Patients are cared for and cared for professionally, safely and with heart – always oriented to the care process
  • In the interdisciplinary team, you are actively involved and also involve relatives and caregivers.
  • You take responsibility for your learning process yourself – and actively shape your education
  • Weekend and shift services are part of this – together we ensure continuous and high-quality care and support
  • Step by step you develop into a qualified nurse HF and strengthen your professional safe and empathetic working method in everyday care.

You need this to be successful

  • You have successfully completed your education as a health professional
  • You are communicative, open and meet people with respect and appreciation
  • Careful work is a matter of course for you – quality and responsibility come first.
  • Teamwork is yours: you value the exchange and enjoy working interdisciplinary
  • Even in challenging situations, you stay calm, committed and professional
