Regional Support Team Member for Corporate Clients and Oberwallis Region Visp 60-100% You have a commercial education and experience in the banking sector? If you are looking for an opportunity to showcase your skills, this is your chance. ## Responsibilities

  • You will support the regional manager in all administrative, organizational, and coordinating tasks.
