Diet chef EFZ (a) 80 - 100% Immediately or by appointment

Whether you are a young professional, an experienced professional or looking for a new challenge, what counts for us is the joy of craftsmanship and togetherness.

Obwalden Hospital is an innovative primary care hospital in Central Switzerland. In our hospital kitchen, we attach great importance to quality, variety and a family environment in which people of all ages feel comfortable and work together.

Your tasks with us

  • Culinary diversity: You will ensure healthy, varied catering for our patients, employees and external guests.
  • Special food under control: You process food orders for diet food confidently via our "LogiMen" catering system.
  • Precision & enjoyment: You can prepare diet diets independently according to the specifications of nutritional counselling with an eye for quality and taste.
  • Teamwork: You will actively participate in quality assurance and support the preparation of meals on the conveyor belt.

What you bring to the table

  • Training: Completed apprenticeship as a cook EFZ. Further training as a diet chef (EFZ/FA) is ideal, but not a must.
  • Experience: Sound knowledge of the gastronomy, hotel industry or community catering.
  • All-round talent: You have mastered the work processes in all kitchen areas, and you also enjoy the tasks in the patisserie.
  • Personality: You are flexible, hands-on and bring a lot of team spirit and a healthy dose of humor.

Our offer to you

  • Family environment: A manageable hospital with short distances, quick decisions and a warmly lived informal culture.
  • Dream location: A modern workplace directly in Sarnen am See (only a 5-minute walk from the train station).
  • Good induction: Our motivated kitchen team will train you in a structured manner and at eye level.
  • Attractive benefits:

🏋️ Free fitness subscription in our in-house physio fitness 🚗 Discounted parking & delicious employee discounts on catering 💼 Progressive Social Benefits, Company Child Allowances & Worldwide Private Accident Insurance 🎟️ Discounted REKA cheques and individual training opportunities
