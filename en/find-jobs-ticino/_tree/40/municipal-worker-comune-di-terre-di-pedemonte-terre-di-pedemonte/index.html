Workplace: the entire municipal area (fractions of Tegna, Verscio and Cavigliano)

Duties and tasks

. execution of the tasks assigned within the external service activity of the municipal technical office; . maintenance and care of greenery; . various seasonal jobs; . small maintenance of work equipment and machines; . care and maintenance of municipal infrastructure and buildings; . winter service; . availability to carry out activities even outside normal working hours and on holidays if necessary; . everything not contained in these duties, but which will be deemed appropriate by the Municipality.

Those provided for by the Employees' Organic Regulations (hereinafter called ROD) and in particular:

a) impeccable moral conduct; b) healthy physical constitution and predisposition to carry out outdoor work; c) if general worker: demonstrated experience in various sectors; if qualified worker: federal certificate of professional capacity; d) driving license, cat. B; e) aptitude for independent and team work, organizational skills, ease and friendliness in contacts with citizens, sense of responsibility, availability and flexibility on a personal level and in relation to working hours; f) availability to follow refresher and specialization courses; g) Swiss citizenship, subject to the application of international agreements; h) domicile preferably in the Municipality of Terre di Pedemonte

The first year of employment is considered a probationary period. During this period the relationship can be terminated by either party at the end of each month with thirty days' notice. The Municipality has the right to extend the probationary period to a maximum of two years.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
