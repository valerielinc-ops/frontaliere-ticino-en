Mission

  • Operating and technical support in the daily management of the construction site and civil engineering.
  • Conduction and maintenance of specific machinery for construction, in accordance with Swiss technical and safety standards.
  • Coordination with the working teams and supervision of the activities to ensure compliance with the timing and regulations in force in Switzerland.
  • Assistance in the control of the quality of the materials and the processing carried out, with particular attention to the technical requirements of the building sector.

Profile

  • Minimum experience of 3 years in construction or civil engineering, preferably matured in Switzerland.
  • A native language knowledge of the Italian language, indispensable for communication in teams and relationships with local customers.
  • Possession of a vocational training qualification (AFC) in the building or technical sector as a guarantee of solid technical skills.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical drawings as well as good mastery of Swiss safety and quality regulations.
  • Teamwork attitude and responsibility in the management of construction work.
