Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in vocational training, it promotes the development of young talent, with every year more than 120 learners trained in our departments. In order to strengthen our Main Subscription & Pricing Division, we are looking for a Master's intern for a duration of 6 months from September 2027 in Lausanne. In a dynamic and growing environment, you take part in various actuarial work in coordination with the employees of the pension and personal insurance service. Master's work includes a review of a model for predicting social illness and analysis of an individual accident portfolio. The missions that await you:

  • Taking charge of the new database: analyze its structure, available variables and their quality
  • Recalibrate the disease onset model: model intensity.
  • Improve the estimation of the number of times: methodology to be determined by the form of distribution of dates of appearance
  • Improve the estimate of the amount of recoveries: develop a robust statistical method (credibility, Bühlmann-Straub, minimization of prediction error, etc.) to replace the current decision threshold
  • Analyze the individual accident portfolio: data ownership and cost-effectiveness analysis Your profile:
  • You are in course of academic studies (Master University or High School)
  • You have excellent mathematics skills and very good Python programming knowledge. Knowledge of the VBA and SQL languages would also be appreciated.
  • Autonomous, you have good management of priorities and timeliness
  • You have excellent command of French. Good knowledge of English and German is an asset. We offer you :
  • A healthy working environment in a cooperative company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • A varied and rewarding work experience
  • A possible opportunity for placement at the end of the internship/training The HR team is looking forward to receiving your complete application package (CV, cover letter and 3 final transcripts).
