Here's what we're looking for

As the Business Analytics Lead , you will be the strategic architect of the data roadmap shaping the Patient Insights portfolio and strategy at Roche Information Solutions (RIS). You will serve as the single source of truth for the RIS Patient Insights portfolio metrics and act as the orchestrator of Patient Insights-wide business analytics. You will create effective strategies to collect data, analyze information, conduct research and implement analytics. You will bring a keen strategic focus and ability to translate data into actionable insights, championing data-driven decision making and the journey at RIS Patient Insights to become the leading chronic disease management company. We are looking for a proven business leader. You demonstrate energy, passion, courage, vision and the drive to be part of an organization at the forefront of the industry, delivering benefits for patients, our people, and our business.

The Business Analytics Lead will report to the Head of Digital Solutions Analytics. The Position is based in Vienna, Austria. This is a global role and up to 30% travel (largely international) is required.

Your upcoming mission

Within this role, your responsibilities will fall into these main areas: Business Analytics & KPI Strategy & Orchestration

  • You explore, develop, implement and evaluation innovative business analytic solutions to meet customer needs (internal and external)
  • You act as a bridge between traditionally siloed data sets (e.g. Finance, Portfolio, CX, Sales) in order to inform RIS Patient Insights business decisions and promote alignment with RIS Patient Insights Strategy
  • You lead key strategic initiatives related to RIS Patient Insights-wide data analytics (e.g. executive dashboard creation; transversal governance data requirement / definition) – developing and coordinating project plans with stakeholders to deliver to expectations
  • You collaborate with other business intelligence functions (incl. Finance, Commercial) to harmonize and simply the KPI identification, gathering, collection and visualization processes
  • You work with technical colleagues in R&D and IT, identifying business requirements related to data analytics and supporting development of solutions that meet customer requirements (internal and external)

Customer Solution Business Analytics & KPIs

  • You serve as the single source of truth for RIS Patient Insights customer portfolio metrics
  • You collaborate with product, service and solution development and pipeline key stakeholders to define key performance metrics for all RIS Patient Insights solutions – tracking product development, commercialization and use
  • You serve as the accountable business owner of key reporting solutions (e.g. Tableau), serving as a ‘super user’ able to interrogate data and translate it into actionable insight
  • You provide expertise with respect to portfolio analytics and key strategic recommendations that facilitate trade-off discussions
  • You serve as an extended member of RIS Patient Insights’ solution development team at distinct points in product development – providing Business Analytic expertise
  • As required, you act as a ‘visitor’ to RIS Patient Insights solution development Agile Release Trains (ARTs), participating in standard SAFe ceremonies and providing ‘hands on’ support
