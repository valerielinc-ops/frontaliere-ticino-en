Full description

Vaudoise is one of Switzerland's leading insurance companies and an attractive, forward-looking employer. True to its cooperative identity and values, Vaudoise creates a healthy, inclusive, and inspiring work environment where you can develop further. We are looking for a dedicated and entrepreneurial personality as Head of Accident Claims Department German-speaking Switzerland, starting January 1, 2027, or by arrangement. In this central role, you will contribute significantly to providing our customers with a first-class service, even in demanding situations. Together with your team, you will promote customer-oriented, efficient, and forward-looking service delivery. Furthermore, you will actively drive the further development of the department and make a substantial contribution to the continuous optimization of our services as well as the future-oriented development of Vaudoise. In doing so, you will recognize potential for improvement, provide impetus, and actively help shape changes sustainably. These diverse tasks await you:

Tasks

  • Professional, personnel, and organizational management of the Accident Claims Department German-speaking Switzerland
  • Leadership, promotion, and further development of three team leaders as well as around 30 employees
  • Ensuring high-quality, efficient, and customer-oriented claims processing in the area of accident insurance
  • Responsibility for goal achievement, productivity, quality, and customer satisfaction
  • Active quality and complaints management as well as monitoring of relevant key performance indicators
  • Representation of the department vis-à-vis internal and external stakeholders
  • Participation in projects and active further development of processes, organization, and digitalization
  • Support of the Head of UVG Switzerland in the implementation of the divisional strategy This is what sets you apart:

Requirements

  • Several years of management experience in a comparable role as well as the ability to successfully develop teams further
  • Several years of professional experience in the claims department of accident, personal injury, or social insurance
  • You hold a Swiss federal certificate of proficiency (Eidgenössischer Fachausweis) or comparable education
  • You convince through your pronounced leadership and communication skills as well as your decisiveness
  • Analytical thinking skills, high customer orientation, and enthusiasm for change and optimization processes
  • Very good written and spoken German skills, as well as good French skills, are an advantage
  • Confident handling of common MS Office applications
  • If you inspire and motivate your team to peak performance every day with your passion for the profession, then you are the right person for us! We offer you:

We offer

  • A pleasant working environment in a cooperative company that has been voted the best employer multiple times
  • Numerous social benefits and employee advantages, including flexibility in work organization
  • Responsible management tasks with room for maneuver
  • An appreciative corporate culture that places employee well-being at the center We look forward to receiving your complete application documents electronically (with CV, cover letter, copies of work references, and diplomas).
