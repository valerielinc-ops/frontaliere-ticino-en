Place of work: St. Anna in the train station | Lucerne

Occupation by: by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74807

You would like to contribute your rheumatological expertise in a professional clinical environment and attach importance to individual, high-quality patient care? At St. Anna in the train station, you can expect a versatile activity with an outpatient focus, professional exchange and close interdisciplinary cooperation.

Our rheumatological offer includes the clarification and treatment of a wide range of rheumatic diseases and musculoskeletal dysfunctions. In addition to outpatient office hours, we also provide rheumatological care for inpatient patients if necessary.

ITS TASKS

  • Independent implementation of rheumatological consultation hours and care of a versatile spectrum of patients
  • Adopting rheumatological consultancies and working closely with other disciplines
  • Possibility to perform pain interventions with existing BV
  • Professional exchange within the Rheumatology Association as well as with other specialist areas, in particular the osteoporosis center and physiotherapy
  • Active participation and continuous development of patient care
  • Participation in the specialist medical service system of the rheumatology section
  • Participation in case discussions within the Rheumatology section

YOUR PROFILE

  • Specialist title Rheumatology and Certificate of Competence Arthrosonography
  • At least three years of experience in a Swiss hospital
  • Sound clinical experience as well as an independent and evidence-based working method
  • High patient orientation and enjoyment of interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • Strong social and communication skills
  • Very good German and English skills

Our offer

  • Varied rheumatological spectrum with an outpatient focus
  • Design leeway to contribute your professional expertise and actively develop patient care
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation within Klinik St. Anna and the Hirslanden Group
  • Access to a broad network of specialists
