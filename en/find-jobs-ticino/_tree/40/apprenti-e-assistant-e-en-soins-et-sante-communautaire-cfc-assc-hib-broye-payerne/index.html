Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the Broyard population. The HIB places great emphasis on training and support for the succession. It offers apprentices a varied environment enabling them to gradually develop their skills, autonomy and professional experience. For the beginning of August 2027, the HIB offers learning places at its sites in Payerne and in Estavayer-le-Lac.

Apprenticeship Community Care and Health Assistant (CFC CSSA)

Your Mission

Your HIB learning

During your three years of training, you will be accompanied by experienced professionals and trained in coaching.

As you learn, you will gradually develop the skills necessary to practice the profession of ASSC and gain autonomy.

You will have the opportunity to

  • receive and accompany patients throughout their hospitalization;
  • provide care, apply certain treatments and perform medical-technical procedures, depending on your level of training and supervision;
  • support patients in the activities of daily life by valuing their abilities and autonomy;
  • observe their health and transmit relevant information to the team;
  • collaborate with the various professionals of the interdisciplinary team;
  • participate in administrative, logistical and materiel management tasks.

Your assets

  • You have completed or are about to complete your compulsory education and you want to train in health.
  • You have completed at least one discovery internship in the field of care to confirm your interest in the profession.
  • You are motivated to invest fully during the three years of training, both in theoretical learning and in practice with patients.
  • Dynamic, voluntary and empathic, you show maturity and a sense of responsibility.
  • You appreciate contact with people and teamwork.
  • You are comfortable with administrative tasks, be organized and are open to feedback in order to progress and gradually develop your autonomy.

How is the selection going?

If your file is selected, you will be invited to participate in a HIB observation workshop.

This internship will allow you to discover our working environment and the profession of the ASSC. It will also allow us to get to know and confirm together that this training corresponds to your professional project.
