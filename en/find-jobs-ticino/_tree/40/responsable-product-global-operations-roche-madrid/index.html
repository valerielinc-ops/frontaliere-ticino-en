Description of the area

Digital Operations and Reliability (DOR) exists to radically transform how we support and operate systems and platforms by leveraging knowledge, automation, and AI across a headless enterprise landscape. It is the canonical single point of aggregation for operational data to enable a move from reactive, "manual-heavy" operations models to a software-defined enterprise. The DOR function ensures the proactive management and maintenance of the optimal performance, reliability, and availability of key RDT enterprise-wide digital platforms and Infrastructure, minimizing disruption to critical business processes across the value chain, and guaranteeing compliance. The Global Operations Lead is a member of the DOR LT and primarily owns the Incident Management & Continuity (24/7 365) Global Operations Support for critical systems and beyond. To assure these products and services, In addition, this team will champion the design and implementation of good Change Management, Problem Management, and Operational Data Quality & Enablement: ensuring high quality operational data standards and practices enabling asset management and compliance for practices for alignment and adoption across relevant RDT organizations. Global Operations service provides 24x7 service support and acts as the primary interface for end users, executing initial ticket handling, device alarm monitoring, and basic troubleshooting to ensure swift service restoration. Critically, it also owns the Major Incident Management (MIM) for high-severity outages, chairing the Change Advisory Board (CAB) to govern planned changes, and leading Problem Management to conduct root cause analysis and implement permanent preventative measures, thus proactively improving overall system stability and service quality.
