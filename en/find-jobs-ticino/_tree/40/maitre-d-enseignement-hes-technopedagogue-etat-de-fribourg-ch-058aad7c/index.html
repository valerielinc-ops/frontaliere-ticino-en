Your missions

The HES/Technology teacher contributes to the implementation of training strategies using information and communication technologies. Is it involved in the development of digital education in order to contribute to the evolution of teaching methods?

Areas of activity/expertise

  • Advise and assist teaching staff in the design, implementation and evaluation of e-learning tools integrated into teaching
  • Analyze educational innovation opportunities, conduct targeted technological monitoring and propose recommendations to the dean of training
  • Providing technical-pedagogical activities (Learning platforms including Moodle)
  • Teaching at the main and post-graduate level in its field of expertise
  • Develop and manage digital tools for teaching evaluation

Profile desired

  • Master level training in technology pedagogy or deemed equivalent
  • Strong experience in e-learning and technology pedagogy
  • Use of specific software and interest in activities requiring excellent knowledge of IT tools
  • Confirmed capacity to organize, plan, conduct projects
  • Autonomy, flexibility, sense of responsibility and teamwork
  • Mastery of the French or German language with good knowledge of the other language
