Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", the Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in ventilation equipment, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors. The Business Unit Process Analytics develops, produces and sells state-of-the-art process measurement technology for customers in biopharma, chemistry and breweries. Our product portfolio includes sensors for critical process parameters such as pH, oxygen, or sensors for measuring cell density, such as are enormously important in biological processes and unique with their innovative technology worldwide.
