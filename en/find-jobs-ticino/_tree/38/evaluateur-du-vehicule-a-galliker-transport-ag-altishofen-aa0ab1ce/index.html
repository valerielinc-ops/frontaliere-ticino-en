Full description

Vehicle evaluator (a) Galliker Service AG prepares the new cars from various car manufacturers for the Swiss market. In addition, it carries out order-specific series, conversion and various workshop work on behalf of importers, corporate customers, dealers as well as rental car and leasing companies. For the car brands in question, Galliker Service AG is also responsible for delivering and taking back the rental cars and leasing vehicles and carries out all kinds of workshop, car bodywork and painting work in the service of the customers. To complement our team in the field of returns for rental and leasing vehicles, we are looking for you as a vehicle evaluator (a). Task area

  • Assessing the condition of vehicles according to brand-specific instructions
  • Executing calculations via tablet and calculation software
  • Creation of damage logs on occupation, rental and service vehicles
  • Detecting the Repair Need for Carrosserie and Painting
  • Coordinate, monitor and plan the return process Requirement profile
