Full description

Introduction

For our customer, a solid and well-established company in building construction, we are looking for you as a construction guide. In this function, you lead the construction sites operationally and ensure compliance with quality, deadlines and costs over all phases.

Tasks

  • Operational site management: deployment and resource planning, coordination of entrepreneurs, trades and service providers
  • Cost, schedule and quality control according to project specifications
  • Reports, construction journals, construction meetings and minutes
  • Active interface management with building manager, planning, authorities, neighbourhood and internal bodies
  • Ensure quality, safety and environmental protection in accordance with SUVA requirements
  • Checks, partial/final acceptances and defect management until handover
  • Construction administration and scheduling

Profile

  • Completed construction-related basic training (e.g. building draftsman/in EFZ, Zimmermann/Zimmerin EFZ, mason/in EFZ) with further training as a construction guide
  • Several years of experience in building management (new, renovation or renovation projects)
  • Versed in construction administration and IT tools
  • self-employed, structured and forward-looking working methods
  • Strong communication and negotiation skills
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Driving licence Cat B

Advantages

  • Flexible working hours and good work-life balance
  • Long-term prospects in a stable company
  • Support for personal and professional development
  • Structured familiarisation and support in everyday work
