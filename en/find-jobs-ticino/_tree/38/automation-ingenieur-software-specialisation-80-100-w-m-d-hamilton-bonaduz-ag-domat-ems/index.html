Full description

In line with our vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in ventilation devices, automated pipetting and sample storage, as well as the development of process sensors. The Business Unit Process Analytics develops, produces and distributes state-of-the-art process measurement technology for customers in biopharma, chemistry and breweries. Our product portfolio includes sensors for critical process parameters such as pH value, oxygen, or sensors for measuring cell density, which are extremely important in biological processes and unique worldwide with their innovative technology. ## Job

  • Responsibilities
  • Further development, adaptation and optimization of the software of our production plants, especially assembly and testing plants
  • Programming in C# to implement new functions, automation logics and process improvements
  • Analysis, identification and elimination of software errors in the production environment
  • Active design of completely new production plants
  • from the first idea to prototypes to series maturity
  • Ideally, support in the hardware-related further development of our plants (mechanics, electronics, sensors)
  • Maintenance and optimization of existing mechatronic systems as well as participation in conversions and upgrades
  • Carrying out tests, validations and functional tests of new software modules
  • Close cooperation with special machine construction, process engineering, production and maintenance
  • Collaboration or project management in interdisciplinary projects for the modernization and automation of our production processes ##
  • Requirements
  • Degree or technical training in the field of system technology, electrical engineering, mechatronics, computer science or comparable
  • Very good knowledge of C# and object-oriented software development
  • Technically multidisciplinary, with a passion for both software and hardware topics
  • Interest or experience in handling assembly and testing plants as well as automation technology
  • Practical experience in the field of mechatronics (e.g. mechanics, electronics, pneumatics, sensors) is an advantage
  • Independent, structured, solution-oriented and practical way of working
