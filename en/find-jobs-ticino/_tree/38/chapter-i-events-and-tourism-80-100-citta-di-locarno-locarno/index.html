27 November 2026 NOTICE OF COMPETITION

THE MUNICIPLE OF LOCARNO makes known that the contest for recruiting one/one is open:

Chapter I – Events and Tourism (80-100%) indefinitely at the General Administration, Tourism and Planning, of which he is a member of the superior paintings. The owner of the function will be responsible for managing and coordinating the activities related to the tourist and leisure events taking place in Locarno, to promote the FEVI Palexpo and to assist the municipal secretary in the activities concerning the formalities of the protocol. Main tasks

  • It acts as coordinator of the different services involved in organizing events and can come

called to participate in the organization of events managed by various bodies.

  • Plan the annual calendar of events and the use of the necessary resources.
  • It oversees the evasion of administrative practices (current compliance, reports, pre-accession

of payment, various statistics), prepares technical pre-arrangements and drafts of municipal messages.

  • It provides data collection and participates in the preparation of annual and annual estimates

ensures management control of your own cost centers.

  • Ensures contacts with various agencies that promote events in Locarno.
  • It sets out the authorisation of the various events and provides for verification of existence

the conditions for their performance, with particular reference to the safety standards, and the inclusion in the urban fabric of the city.

  • Promotes new events of interest to the City, enhancing its national image and

international as an important tourist and recreational centre.

  • It provides the management and promotion of the Palexpo FEVI Locarno, guaranteeing its full

exploiting and enhancing its potential as an infrastructure for events and congresses.

  • Support, on indication of the municipal secretary, to the functions of protocol of the municipality.
  • It can be called to represent the City Hall within associations or various bodies.
  • It deals with the management of the staff of its own sector for the human resources areas of its

competence, in addition to overseeing and managing the work of its employees. 2 Professional Requirements

  • Master SUP in business management, tourism, communication science or marketing. The

lack of the degree of study can be be be begged by a multiannual experience in similar function.

  • Comprehensive experience in managing and organizing events and in managing

personal.

  • Explain organizational skills, spirit of initiative and decision-making.
  • Great knowledge of the Italian language and good knowledge spoken and written of the languages

national and English.

  • Availability and flexibility for shiftwork even at night and on public holidays.
  • Prepared for contacts with the public.
  • Familiarity with new media and knowledge of the main computer programs.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
