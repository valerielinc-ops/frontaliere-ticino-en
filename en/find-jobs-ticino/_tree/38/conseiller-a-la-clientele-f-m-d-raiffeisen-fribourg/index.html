Banque Raiffeisen Sensetal Fribourg-Est is one of the largest Raiffeisen banks in Switzerland. With 118 employees, including 11 apprentices, we are an attractive employer with strong roots in the region. We cultivate a corporate culture based on partnership, dialogue, fairness and social responsibility. The Swiss Arbeitgeber Award confirms this ambition and makes us proud of it. As an innovative cooperative bank, we have been committed to a constant evolution towards an advisory bank in recent years. We actively seek contact with people and make our clients' dreams come true.

Our modern and efficient bank is looking for an autonomous, motivated and service-oriented personality to strengthen its team as a customer advisor (m/f/d).

  • Identify individual customer needs and organize appointments for advisors
