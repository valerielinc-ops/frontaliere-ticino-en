Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement. Tasks: • You take responsibility for the entire care process of newborns and interdisciplinary patients • The active participation in shaping the quality of care and the effectiveness of nursing measures is also part of this versatile task area. • They accompany students carefully and thus make a valuable contribution to their professional and personal development • Engaged participation in change processes as well as in the further development of a dynamic team rounds off the field of activity Requirements • You have completed training as a nurse / nurse specialist HF/FH; with a focus on KJFF or experience in the obstetric environment • With warmth, empathy and a high level of social competence, you will meet newborns, interdisciplinary patients and their relatives professionally and appreciatively. • Flexibility, resilience and the willingness to get involved in adjacent specialist areas, if necessary, characterize your way of working • You are self-confident, actively participate in changes and gladly take on responsibility in a changing team We offer: • She expects a varied and responsible work in our obstetric and gynecological department with around 1,000 births per year as well as in interdisciplinary special areas. • Working independently in a committed, motivated and networked team shapes everyday work • You benefit from targeted promotion of your professional and personal development through further education and training • Attractive social benefits and employment conditions, especially our shift allowances, complement this exciting overall package.
