In order to strengthen our team at AMEOS Spital Einsiedeln, we are looking for an

(m/f/d) Financial Accounting - 80 - 100%

Their advantages We offer an interesting and versatile job, a pleasant working atmosphere as well as contemporary employment conditions and an attractive offer for further education and training. The place of work is at the Einsiedeln site.

Their tasks

  • Accounting for debtors including dunning
