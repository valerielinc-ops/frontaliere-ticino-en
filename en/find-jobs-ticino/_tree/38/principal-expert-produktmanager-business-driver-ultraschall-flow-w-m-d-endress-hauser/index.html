Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a Principal Expert Product Manager & Business Driver Ultrasonic Flow to complement our team and drive the strategic development of our ultrasonic flow portfolio worldwide. In this key role, you will combine product management, market understanding and business development to enable sustainable growth in international markets. You will become part of an innovative organization that sets standards worldwide with leading measurement technologies and designs solutions for the process industry together with customers.

What is this point about?

Do you want to actively shape the future of ultrasonic flow measurement? Does it appeal to you to develop global market strategies and successfully manage products over their entire life cycle? Are you excited to work with international sales teams to unlock new market potential and successfully introduce innovations?

In this globally oriented role, you combine technical expertise with entrepreneurial thinking. They work closely with product management, sales, marketing, industrial management and international sales centers and play a central role in the further development of our ultrasonic flow portfolio and the successful market positioning of new and existing solutions.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • The product areas of the ultrasonic flow portfolio, in particular Prosonic Flow Clamp-on, are strategically responsible for the entire product life cycle.
  • Analyze market trends, customer requirements and developments in the target industries and derive sustainable product and growth strategies from this.
  • Ensure portfolio competitiveness through market analysis, product roadmaps, business cases and strategic pricing.
  • Plan, control and successfully implement market launches of new products and functions together with international stakeholders.
  • Develop global marketing and sales strategies for ultrasonic flow solutions and advance them in close cooperation with the sales centers.
  • Impart product and application knowledge worldwide and help sales organizations successfully understand, apply and market our solutions.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • Have a university degree in electrical or automation engineering, mechanical engineering or a comparable discipline.
  • Bring further training in the area of marketing and sales as well as demonstrable success in a sales-related or market-oriented function.
  • Have in-depth knowledge of instrumentation and process automation.
  • Combining technical understanding with strong customer orientation and a clear focus on sustainable business growth.
  • Communicate securely with different stakeholders in an international environment and work successfully across organizational boundaries.
  • Master German and English and are willing to travel internationally (about 15–30%); Further language skills are advantageous.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We offer you an international expert role with high visibility and the opportunity to actively shape the future of a global product portfolio.
  • We value a balanced work-life balance and offer flexible working models that balance professional and personal requirements.
  • We ensure that our employees work in an innovative, modern working environment and work together with colleagues worldwide.
  • We create space for personal development, personal responsibility and the implementation of new ideas in an environment characterized by trust and respect.
