Full description

How should SRG evolve over the next few years? How should you place yourself to react in advance to developments in the media market? And how can it strengthen its role as a public service company to maintain long-term support of the population? That's exactly what you're gonna be working on. Within the SRG Staff, under mandate of the Management, we accompany the transformation of the entire company and contribute to making it ready for the future. As a Business Development Specialist, you will be an internal consultant: you will connect strategy, transformation and implementation, helping to successfully carry out transversal issues to the different sectors of the organization. Your role will be deliberately broad and dynamic: depending on your needs, you will work on different themes, areas and contexts.

Your duties

You will work where the organization needs support for complex issues or change projects:

  • You will work on projects related to the most important strategic challenges of SRG and, together with the company development team, you will contribute to the evolution of the company.
  • You will bring structure and clarity to complex and transversal challenges to the organization, developing future visions and concrete solutions.
  • You will work with the strategic process of SRG or translate the company strategy, along with other SRG experts, into strategic initiatives, operational measures and guidelines for sectors and functions.
  • Through market analysis, trends and context, you will monitor the evolution of the environment in which the SRG operates, transforming the analysis into operational recommendations for the Management and the various departments.
  • You will facilitate dialogue between different perspectives, promoting alignment between business areas and building a shared understanding.
  • You will bring the themes to the decision-making stage, creating commitment and responsibility even without a formal hierarchical function.
  • Optionally, you will be able to take additional specific roles, for example by supporting in the long term certain regions or organizational units in their transformation processes or by engaging in innovation activities.

What we expect from you

  • University degree or higher vocational training and experience in a similar function in the field of business development or strategy.
  • Ability to think in a systemic and interdisciplinary way, including interdependence and working with serenity even on problems still undefined.
  • Excellent knowledge of Italian and at least good of a second national, German or French language (min. level B2).
  • Experience in the management of change, with solid methodological skills and change management and a good sensitivity to the market and trends.
  • Ability of transversal leadership, strong communication and authority in relations with different hierarchical levels.
  • Rapidity in analyzing complex themes and ability to make them understandable both orally and in writing.
  • Entrepreneurship, strong sensitivity to economic and sustainability aspects and goals.
  • Ability to build lasting relationships and trust and act as a credible interlocutor and a comparison partner; knowledge of the media and in particular of public service media is an advantage.

What we offer

  • A key role as an internal consultant, in close contact with strategic themes and the direction of SRG.
  • Large margin of manoeuvre within a newly established business development team.
  • Great variety: You will work on the most important themes of the organization and actively contribute to defining its future.
  • Possibility to participate in the construction of the future of the SRG in a dynamic context.
  • Hybrid work model, with possibility to carry out part of the activity in home office.

What are the ingredients to reach the inhabitants of Switzerland with quality format? Know-how, enthusiasm and an environment rich in stimuli, in which everyone can grow. To give the best it takes a source of inspiration: Welcome to the SSR world!

  • Produce content: Quality journalism is our main activity and our passion. We enjoy editorial freedom, promote creativity and give ample space to the production of new and high-level content.

  • Make sense: With our programs we reach and thrill Switzerland. We act with respect and sense of independence; we promote the free formation of opinions and strengthen pluralism. We are proud of the contribution we make to Switzerland.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
