Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Do you want to use your power to implement projects from acquisition to successful commissioning? Then we will tackle it together!

Your tasks

  • Project management with enthusiasm: You assume responsibility from the acquisition to the successful commissioning of medium-sized and large construction projects in industrial and residential construction.
  • Coordination is your thing: you are the link between customers, suppliers and colleagues in the team. Unforeseen challenges? No problem for you - you will find solutions!
  • Consulting with know-how: Your expertise and service concept inspire not only our customers, but also your own team.
  • The numbers under control: You calculate offers for customers and ensure smooth billing until the final fact. For ongoing projects, keep an eye on budget, resource planning and progress at all times.

We support you with state-of-the-art equipment, a company vehicle for private use and individual training. A companion team, above-average social benefits and numerous employee benefits await you.

You bring that with you

  • With a completed training in the electrotechnical field and further education as an electrical project manager Installation and Safety FA, you are the ideal complement for our team. You have not yet completed the training as a project manager? No problem – we still want to get to know you!
  • You have a “feeling” for schedules, budgets and the right balance between detail and an uncomplicated hands-on mentality.
  • You are not only a communication talent but also a real team player and know how to bring all project participants together into a strong team.
  • Your own initiative and your entrepreneurial thinking and action distinguish you.
