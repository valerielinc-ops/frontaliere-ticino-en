Full description

Your tasks

  • You check and process customer inquiries, offers and orders for machines, modules and other equipment.
  • You collect, process and monitor customer orders in the relevant ERP and CRM systems and ensure a high quality of order data.
  • You clarify technical, commercial and order-related questions with sales, product management, customer service, export and other specialist areas.
  • You monitor the relevant dates from acceptance of the order via material availability and production to handover and readiness for shipment.
  • You coordinate the project-related customer communication in coordination with the responsible Area Sales Manager as well as order-related shipping, export and delivery requirements.
  • You maintain and check customer-, offer- and order-relevant data in the ERP and CRM systems and support in their cleaning.

Your profile

  • Completed basic technical training, commercial training is advantageous.
  • Experience in internal sales, order processing, production planning, supply chain or a comparable function, ideally in an industrial environment.
  • Technical understanding and pleasure in handling complex customer orders.
  • Good knowledge of ERP and CRM systems
  • Experience with SAP S/4HANA is an advantage.
  • Independent, structured and careful working methods as well as pronounced customer, deadline and quality orientation.
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English, at least level B2; other foreign languages are advantageous.

Your benefits

Your new place of work in Dierikon is newly built and equipped with the latest technology and work equipment. Your workplace is located in one of the modern open-plan offices, where there are also inspiring meeting areas and a large terrace for various employee events. Komax AG offers its employees flexible working time models and home office. The further training of employees is important to Komax AG and offers, for example, free internal English courses or access to selected attractive learning platforms. At the Dierikon location, there are also modern break rooms and the possibility of a discounted lunch in a neighbouring partner restaurant.
