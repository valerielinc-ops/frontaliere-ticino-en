Welcome to Medbase Schaffhausen

Our modern, brightly furnished medical center with its own laboratory is located directly at Schaffhausen train station, near the Rhine and the picturesque old town. The team of around twelve consists of specialists in general internal medicine and MPAs and covers the entire spectrum of family medicine. Active involvement in the well-being of patients and a good work-life balance are very important to us.

You can move that

As an MPA, you are the first point of contact for our patients and ensure a pleasant practice experience. Medbase offers you a dynamic team that values ​​quality and collegiality.

  • Reception and care of patients, including agenda planning and appointment coordination
  • Performing laboratory tests, EKGs and x-rays
  • Assistance with minor surgical procedures
  • Management and electronic billing of patient records
  • Ensuring smooth practice processes
  • Cooperate with the team on administrative tasks

What you bring with you

  • Training as a medical practice assistant EFZ
  • Willingness to undergo further training in x-ray technology
  • Organizational skills and enjoyment of patient care
  • Empathy and flexibility
  • Teamwork and humor in challenging situations
  • Good knowledge of German (at least B2 level)

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medication supply and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from various disciplines work hand in hand to offer people throughout Switzerland integrated, first-class all-round medical care.
