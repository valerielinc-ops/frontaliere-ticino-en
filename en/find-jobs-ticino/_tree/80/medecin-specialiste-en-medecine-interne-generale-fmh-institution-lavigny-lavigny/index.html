Full description

General Internal Medicine (GHM) physician, 50% to 80% Deployed at several sites, the Lavigny Institution consists of a neurological hospital with outpatient consultations on two sites, a housing department for adults with disabilities, with intellectual or physical impairment, a specialised school and a workshop and vocational training sector. The Institution of Lavigny is looking for a medical specialist in general internal medicine, from 1 January 2027, to join its teams and intervene with several populations, at different sites (Lausanne, Lavigny and Morges).

General Internal Medicine Specialist (GHM)

Missions

You work closely with the medical director, the medical teams, the medical specialists and the departments. You participate in a care centered on the needs, projects and quality of life of each person accompanied.

  • EMS In Plein Soleil, Lausanne

You act as a physician responsible for EMS with people with disabilities as a result of an acquired neurological impairment. A mission that combines general medicine, long-term follow-up and close collaboration with interdisciplinary teams (rather: caregivers and therapeutics, as well as social).

  • Socio-educational accommodation in Lavigny and Morges

As necessary, you ensure the medical follow-up of residents of our socio-educational accommodation, in collaboration with the referring doctor already in place. You accompany people with congenital disabilities in a comprehensive, individualised and coordinated approach.

  • Hospital - University neurorehabilitation service in Lavigny

You bring your expertise in internal medicine to the approximately 65 patients hospitalized in neurorehabilitation, whose co-morbidities regularly require a specialized opinion. You work closely with the doctors in the department.

An outpatient consultation activity for outpatients can also be considered, as well as complementary missions according to a distribution adapted to your activity rate.

Profile

  • Title of FMH specialist in general internal medicine or title deemed equivalent and recognized by MEBEKO
  • Significant interest in interdisciplinary work and complex management
  • Taste for local, diverse and person-centred medicine
  • A particular experience or interest in neurology or disability is an asset.

We offer

  • The opportunity to practice general internal medicine in an environment where clinical diversity, interdisciplinarity and a holistic approach to the person make sense
  • The opportunity to discover exciting work within an institution belonging to the Vaudian neuro-rehabilitation sector recognised as to the quality and diversity of its services
  • Social benefits of a large institution: restaurant, parking, public transport nearby

Why join us? This position offers the opportunity to practice a rich and diverse general medicine, at the crossroads of health and medical-social, with populations with special needs. You join experienced teams in which medical, healthcare, therapeutic and socio-educational perspectives complement each other on a daily basis, with a common goal: to offer quality medical support, adapted to each life course.
