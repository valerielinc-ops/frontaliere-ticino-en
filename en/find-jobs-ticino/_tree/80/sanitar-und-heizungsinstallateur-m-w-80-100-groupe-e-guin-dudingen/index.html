Full description

We operate in Western Switzerland and support our customers in the implementation of their projects by offering comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, gentle mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, as well as the reconciliation of private and professional life.

Thanks to its expertise, Groupe E Tech is a customer-oriented and powerful partner for interdisciplinary projects in the areas of electrical installations, switchboards and electrical infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar systems, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, refrigeration, sanitation, water treatment and plant maintenance.

Are you at home in the sanitary area and also have experience with heating installations? For our branch in Düdingen, we are looking for a dedicated specialist who uses his craftsmanship in new buildings and renovations – from raw installation to commissioning.

Plumbing and heating installer (m/f) 80-100 %

Their tasks

  • Self-contained implementation of sanitary installations from raw assembly to completion
  • Installation and connection of sanitary equipment
  • Installation and commissioning of heating systems, heat pumps, boilers and heat distribution
  • Work on new and renovation projects
  • Service, maintenance and repair work (no piquetting)
  • Conduct of pressure tests, commissioning and functional checks
  • Support for the training of new employees
  • Compliance with applicable safety, quality and environmental standards
  • Ongoing further training and active further development of one’s own professional competences

Profile

  • You have an EGZ as a sanitary installer, ideally supplemented by an EGZ as a heating installer, or an equivalent training.
  • You have several years of professional experience with a focus on the sanitary sector as well as additional experience in the heating sector.
  • You have a category B driving licence.
  • You are an open and flexible personality with a strong team spirit.
  • They work independently, keep an overview and set the right priorities.
  • They have a good knowledge of German and value a friendly, customer-oriented service.

Benefits

  • With us, you are the focus as a human being. We attach great importance to making you feel comfortable with us and offer you numerous advantages:
  • A young and dynamic working atmosphere with a team that supports each other
  • Working hours of 40-hour week with the free Friday afternoon so you have more time for yourself
  • A company vehicle that you can also use privately unlimited
  • Attractive development opportunities within the company and support in your professional development (financial participation in courses and training)
  • Above-average pension benefits (60/40 scheme)
  • Employer contributions of CHF 60.- per child
  • Exclusive discounts at over 150 partner companies in Switzerland

... and several other advantages
