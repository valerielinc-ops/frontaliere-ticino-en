Full description

Immediately or by agreement we will search for a limited period until 16. April for the day school Neubühl

As a specialist care provider ISR, you support and care for children with special needs. They work together with people from the field of curative education, teaching and care. Their working hours are as follows: Mo: 8-18 / Di: 8-12 / Mi: 8-12 / Do: 8-18 / Fr: 8-18. You take your holidays in the school-free time.

Their tasks include

  • Majority 1:1 care in the ongoing teaching and care of children with special needs; e.g. support in communication
  • Medical assistance and support in the care and hygiene of children with special needs
  • Support for challenging children’s constellations
  • Continuous consultation and cooperation with professional management (SHP-ISR) and other professionals
  • Educational degree as a professional carer or specialist for child education EFZ
  • For applications with a foreign diploma, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached.
  • Joy and interest in the promotion of children with special needs
  • Willingness to provide professional and reliable support in care and hygiene
  • Enjoyment in dealing with people of different cultures, as well as readiness for interdisciplinary cooperation in the school team
  • Professional experience in caring for children, ideally also in caring for children with special needs
  • Solid knowledge of German
  • A varied and meaningful activity in a lively environment
  • A motivated and open-minded school and management team
  • A good working environment
  • Established structures of the day school 2025
  • Attractive employment conditions

The school district Uto is one of 7 school districts in the city of Zurich and includes districts 2 and 3 with the districts Leimbach, Wollishofen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon. The day school Neubühl is located on the outskirts of Zurich-Wollishofen and is a public primary school with an integrated care offer as well as courses in the musical, sports and craft sector.
