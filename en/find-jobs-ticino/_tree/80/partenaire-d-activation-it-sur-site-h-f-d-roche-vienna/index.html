Here's what we're looking for

English version below *

You are tech enthusiastic, proactive and looking for a role in which you can really make a difference? We are looking for someone who not only manages our IT on site, but makes it a real experience. As an IT Onsite Enablement Partner, you ensure that our colleagues have the best digital tools to realize their full potential. Your focus is on smooth processes, satisfied employees and a modern working environment. Since we are present at two locations in Vienna (in 1010 and 1210 ), you are regularly on the road between the two locations – a varied role that also offers you great development opportunities within the global team of Roche Digital Technologies.

The focus of this role is not classic IT support – we already have great onsite support teams on site for operational inquiries. Instead, you act as a strategic business partner: your mission is to actively shape our digital transformation, promote the acceptance of modern solutions and develop future-oriented IT enablement strategies directly on site.

Your mission: shaping the digital work experience

  • You are the face of IT: you provide an excellent digital experience at our locations.
  • You advise and accompany: You support our teams in the use of Group-wide IT solutions and processes.
  • You promote knowledge: Through training, you help everyone to work safely and gladly with our IT.
  • You coordinate: Whether external partners or internal remote teams – you keep all the strings together and make sure that the service is right.
  • You drive quality: Using data to identify trends, improve our processes and ensure that our goals (SLAs) are achieved.
  • You are actively shaping: from IT purchasing to asset management to automation – you tackle where processes can become more efficient.
  • You build bridges: You work closely with global IT teams to introduce new standards and implement exciting local projects.
  • You keep track: Whether budget planning, IT audits or disaster recovery – you are the reliable support on site.

What you bring

  • You have experience in IT service management (e.g. ITIL) and are very familiar with MacOS.
  • You enjoy managing projects and you approach challenges in a solution-oriented manner.
  • You have an agile, entrepreneurial mindset and enjoy working together across silos.
  • You are curious, act with integrity and burn for innovative solutions.
  • Hardware, software, mobile devices – you feel at home in common IT environments.
  • You can certainly communicate in German (B2) and English (C1).
  • Bonus: Have your first experience with AI-powered workflow automation? Then you are exactly right with us!

What to expect from us

  • A team you can count on: We are ambitious, passionate and together develop products that make a difference worldwide.
  • An environment that inspires: We work agile, cross-functional and live the exchange of knowledge.
