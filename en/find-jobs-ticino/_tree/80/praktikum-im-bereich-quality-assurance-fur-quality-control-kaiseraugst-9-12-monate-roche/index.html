Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position The Quality Unit in Kaiseraugst/Basel consists of Quality Assurance (QA), Quality Control (QC), Quality Systems (QS) and Quality Compliance and Issues. We guarantee the quality and compliance of over 40 product families for more than 120 countries in one integrated drug product and packaging site. In addition, we are responsible for the centralized QC and the release and stability testing of sterile and non-sterile products as well as raw materials and packaging materials. Our philosophy is that quality is the right of every patient, that we take responsibility for it and do our part.

The possibility

  • You drive the harmonization of processes and processes through targeted process improvements – first in QA for QC and perspectively within the entire Quality Unit.
  • You show great interest in analysing and optimising upstream and downstream steps, always maintaining a team-oriented mindset in the sense of increasing quality and efficiency.
  • You support us in the conception, design and training for SOPs as well as other GMP-relevant documentation and processes.
