Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual region of Biel-Seeland-Berner Jura (Switzerland). It provides the entire population with comprehensive care in all areas of modern medicine. Every year, more than 100,000 patients trust us. In the Biel Hospital Center, around 1,800 employees in over 40 different professions find an attractive job.

At the Biel Hospital Center, angiology, vascular surgery and interventional radiology work together in an independent vascular center. With this daily lived interdisciplinarity, we offer our patients the entire range of clarifications and therapies in vascular medicine. The large catchment area contributes to a varied and technically challenging angiology. Our core competencies include all endovascular procedures with a focus on arterial revascularisation and embolisation in a brand new catheter laboratory of the last generation, as well as the minimally invasive treatment of varicose veins. As part of the development of our team, by 01.10.2025 or by agreement, search for a

Senior physician / senior physician angiology 60-100 %

Your tasks – our job

  • Independent, varied outpatient office hours
  • Supervision of the assistant doctors of the vascular center, participation in the further training of assistant doctors (WB category B)
  • Strongly networked, interdisciplinary environment: close cooperation with fussorthopedics with joint diabetic pedicure, nephrology, neurology with joint neurovascular consultation, internal medicine and many other disciplines
  • With appropriate prior knowledge independent implementation of catheter interventions
  • and/or phlebological interventions, or learning these skills under supervision
  • Versatile career perspectives with the possibility of competence expansion in the entire angiological spectrum as well as, depending on the skills, option for promotion to lead doctor / lead physician
  • Possibility of setting up your own office hours at an external location
  • Good work-life balance
  • Bilingual environment (German and French)

Your skills – our requirements

  • Swiss Specialist in Angiology (alternatively MEBEKO-approved), or shortly before
  • Team player who also likes to work independently
  • German or French language fluent, good knowledge of the other language
  • Several years working at a Swiss hospital resp. Knowledge of the CH health system is beneficial

Your possibilities – our offer

At the SZB we live diversity: bilingual colleagues from many different professions in the multicultural city of Biel/Bienne. We work together: a large interdisciplinary cooperation and a lot of mutual support. We continue to develop: further education opportunities and the opportunity to actively shape the future of the hospital center. In addition to the employment conditions according to GAV, we offer various discounts (pharmacy, public transport, leisure), a restaurant with low prices and an internal crèche.
