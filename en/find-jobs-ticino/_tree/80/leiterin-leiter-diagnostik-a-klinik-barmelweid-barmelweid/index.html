Full description

People lead. Shaping the future. We want to know you. Employment Level: 80–100% Entry by Agreement

You don't have to know everything with us. But you can ask anything. You can try out ideas and take responsibility. For us, lean means: giving responsibility, trusting and becoming better together. In our teams, we live an open cooperation at eye level and pull together in the same direction. To strengthen our diagnostics, we are looking for an experienced and motivating leader with pleasure in the further development of people, processes and offers. You move that with us

  • You lead and support your team leaders in medical diagnostics and lung function in personnel and organizational issues.
  • You work closely with the specialist areas to further develop the diagnostic services offered.
  • You are responsible for the economic management of the department and control budget and cost development.
  • You ensure compliance with quality standards, standards and legal requirements.
  • Together with the team leaders, you ensure smooth processes as well as a high-quality performance of diagnostic examinations and therapies.
  • You promote the professional competence of employees through targeted training and further education activities.
  • You work on versatile clinical projects and accompany changes.
  • Together with medical technology, you assume responsibility for the procurement, maintenance and further development of the diagnostic infrastructure. You inspire us...
  • with your completed training as a Dipl. Radiology specialist HF, biomedical analyst HF, nursing specialist HF/FH or a similar education in health care.
  • with your further education in the field of leadership or the willingness to complete a corresponding further education.
  • with your organizational skills as well as your high quality and responsibility awareness.
  • with your experience in planning, coordinating and optimizing processes and resources.
  • with your safe use of medical technology as well as your interest in innovations and the further development of diagnostic offers.
  • with your networked thinking and your holistic view of clinical processes.
  • with your pleasure in the interdisciplinary cooperation with the medical profession, therapies, care and other specialist areas. Barmelweid offers its more than 750 employees an attractive, interdisciplinary working environment in a family atmosphere. This also includes advanced employment conditions, advantageous social benefits, a versatile leisure offer and an in-house daycare centre.
