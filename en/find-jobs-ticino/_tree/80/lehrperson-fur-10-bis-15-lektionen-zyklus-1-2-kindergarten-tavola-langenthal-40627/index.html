Full description

Portrait Since August 2021, the special educational offer TAVOLA of Schoio AG exists, a center in the family help from the Oberaargau Langenthal region. TAVOLA offers separate, tailor-made and flexible educational settings according to LP 21 for students in special education status with conspicuous social and learning behavior (including ASS, ADHD). In the design of learning processes, the promotion of children and young people and the activation of family systems according to their will and resources is in the foreground. The aim is Community return to regulatory systems.

To complement the team in the BILDEN department, we are looking for a second semester of the school year 2026/27.

Tasks

  • Teaching in a special educational environment incl. Class instructor
  • Individualized lessons according to LP 21
  • Responsibility for several subjects
  • Cooperation with parents and specialist bodies

Requirements

  • Teacher’s diploma and/or master’s degree in curative education
  • Motivation and ability for individualized teaching
  • Responsibility for several subjects
  • Cooperation with parents and specialist bodies
  • Systemic-solution-oriented basic attitude
  • Creative and reliable personality

We offer

  • Challenging work in an innovative organization with contemporary employment conditions and a transparent wage model
  • We pay 60% of pension contributions
  • Space for creativity and self-organization
  • Support through clear structures, supportive reflection and technical inputs
  • Culture of appreciation, transparency and orientation towards success in the interdisciplinary overall team
  • Regular lunch table with informal team quality
