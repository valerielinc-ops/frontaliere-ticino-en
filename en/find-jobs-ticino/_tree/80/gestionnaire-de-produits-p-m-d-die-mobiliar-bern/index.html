Full description

This is what awaits you

In the agile, multidisciplinary Value Proposition environment in Bern, you will develop software solutions for our insurance products using modern technologies. The ambition of the Value Proposition teams: close to the customers, a common language between business and IT as well as an architecture and methodology that are continuously developed. The teams are responsible for the modernization of insurance products in private individuals and SMEs. You design, develop, test and operate applications across the entire life cycle (DevOps) working closely with experts, stakeholders of the agile organization and other IT teams.

This is what you do with us

As part of the SAFe framework, you will develop complex technical applications and continue to develop them. You take an active role in the implementation of technical features, from the creation of offers to the processing of business cases, and are the central contact for the teams and other stakeholders in technical and IT-specific questions, such as feasibility, dependencies and requirements.

In close cooperation with platform teams and quality engineers, you ensure smooth integration and stable operation of your solutions.

By participating in Code Reviews, you contribute to the continuous improvement of code quality, architecture and development processes. In addition, you take responsibility for requests and incidents on a regular basis and thus ensure the reliable operation of the applications.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed computer science degree or comparable training
  • In-depth knowledge of agile software development with Javascript/Typescript and Angular
  • Experience with automated tests (e.g. Jest); Knowledge of test-driven development is a plus
  • Secure use of development support tools such as IntelliJ, Git, GitLab and CI pipelines
  • Experience with cloud services (Microsoft Azure) and DevOps tools
  • Implementation-oriented, communicative and customer-oriented personality
  • Curiosity and joy of complex questions
  • fluent knowledge of German
  • English skills are beneficial

Working with us

Strong teams are waiting for you, who are happy about reinforcements. Together we live a proactive, solution-oriented and communicative way of working.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programmes abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
