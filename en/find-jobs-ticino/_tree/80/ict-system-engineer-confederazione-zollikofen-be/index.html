Full description

Put to the Point

You want to design platforms that enable the reliable operation of important GEVER systems. You combine system engineering with automation and release management and ensure that technical changes are implemented safely and repeatedly. With us, you work at the interface between development, architecture and operations. You’ll help teams integrate their solutions efficiently into operations and create the conditions for predictable releases in different environments. You can help shape technical solutions and contribute your experience to the further development of our platforms and standards. Are you interested? Our team is looking forward to you!

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20922
  • Location: Eichenweg 3, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • You design, integrate and operate cloud, infrastructure and container platforms for GEVER systems.
  • You will analyze requirements, evaluate technical solutions and take into account architecture, security, risks and operations.
  • You will develop and optimize automated build, test, release and deployment processes and support development teams in CI/CD and operational integration.
  • You plan and accompany introductions, migrations and release changes in test, acceptance and production environments.
  • You will analyze faults and vulnerabilities, develop preventive measures and document technical solutions. You work closely with development, architecture, operations, projects and external service providers.

This makes you unique

  • You have a degree in computer science or a comparable education and training.
  • You have several years of experience as an ICT system engineer or in a similar function and have designed or operated cloud, infrastructure and container platforms. You know CI/CD, build and deployment processes and their automation from practice.
  • Understand how development, architecture and operations work together, and translate requirements into safe, stable and efficient solutions.
  • You have experience with scripting or programming languages and automation of deployment, configuration and operation.
  • You work independently, structured and solution-oriented and can communicate technical connections in an understandable way.
  • You have good knowledge of an official language as well as knowledge of English
