Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Use in the Optics specialist group for various maintenance and adjustment work
  • Maintenance and adjustment work on field stingers of the Swiss Army
  • Maintenance and adjustment work on optical and optronic instruments of the Swiss Army
  • Assistance with entry-exit control of army equipment
  • Assistance in various projects such as material provision of new material
  • Implementation of condition checks on army equipment on site at LBA operations
  • Production of protective masks
  • Operation of the US system including gluing of the glasses
  • Protective and special eyewear production
  • Use at WEMA' of the LBA
  • If necessary, use in the specialist group Eyewear for: production of visual aids for protective masks, operation of the US system including gluing of glasses as well as production of visual aids for protective and special glasses

You bring that with you

  • Completed technical training, ideally in optics, optronics, precision mechanics, mechatronics or equivalent
  • Professional experience in the maintenance, adjustment or maintenance of optical or technical equipment
  • Experience in using optical and/or optronic instruments is advantageous
  • Strong technical understanding and enjoyment of precise work
  • Skill in handling fine mechanical components and tools
  • Willingness to work with army equipment and compliance with appropriate quality and safety requirements
  • Flowing German, as well as good English skills in word and writing

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
