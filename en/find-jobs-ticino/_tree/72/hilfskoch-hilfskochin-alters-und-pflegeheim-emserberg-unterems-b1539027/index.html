Full description

From now on or by agreement we are looking for an auxiliary cook at 80-100%.

Their tasks

  • Preparing and equipping food
  • Preparation of meals (breakfast, lunch, z Vieri, dinner)
  • Compliance with and assurance of quality standards and hygiene rules
  • Participation in supply acceptance and stock management
  • Taking over the food order in the absence of the chef
  • Miscellaneous cleaning work
  • Sporadic missions as a kitchen aid

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training as a cook is advantageous but not absolutely necessary
  • Professional experience of several years
  • Self-employed, dutiful, friendly and committed personality
  • Flexibility in work assignments (Monday to Sunday)

We offer you

  • An independent and varied workplace in a small team
  • Current employment conditions according to AVALEMS Staff Regulations
  • Free parking spaces on the site
  • Discounted food in the in-house gastronomy
