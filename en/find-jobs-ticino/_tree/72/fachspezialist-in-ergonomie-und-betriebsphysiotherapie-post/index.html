Full description

Do you want to help shape health, ergonomics and prevention in everyday work? Are you enthusiastic about supporting employees and managers with your expertise? With you, we create healthy and safe working conditions.

You can do that.

  • You advise employees and managers in the areas of ergonomics, health and prevention.
  • In this role, you are responsible for carrying out workplace analyses and deriving improvement measures.
  • You plan and conduct training and awareness-raising measures.
  • The support of employees and managers in reintegrations and health promotion measures is up to you.
  • If necessary, you will conduct individual physiotherapeutic consultations.
  • You also use your creative leeway and take responsibility for your tasks.
  • You will be deployed in the Härkingen parcel centre and the Härkingen parcel centre with a 20% contribution each, and in the Buchs AG regional parcel centre with 10%.

You bring that with you

  • You have a degree as a physiotherapist: in FH or an equivalent education.
  • Several years of experience in ergonomics, health management or occupational medicine are among your strengths.
  • You work independently, solution-oriented and proactively.
  • Strong communication skills and consulting skills round off your profile.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German. Any other national language is beneficial.

In order to promote linguistic and gender diversity (and due to the current team composition), we are particularly pleased about applications from women or people who speak French or Italian.
