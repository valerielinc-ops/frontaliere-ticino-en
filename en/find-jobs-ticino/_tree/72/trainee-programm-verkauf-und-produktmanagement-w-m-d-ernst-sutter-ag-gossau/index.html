Start your career with our trainee program During 18 to 24 months, we offer committed young talents a comprehensive insight into the entire value chain of meat and fresh meat production. At the heart of the trainee program are sales and product management. In addition to production, further assignments are based on your interests and current company needs. You will thus get to know different divisions and gradually take on responsibility.In doing so, you will go through several stations at our locations in Gossau, St. Gallen and Bazenheid and work closely with experienced employees.The assignments are structured and enable you to understand connections along the entire value chain and to participate cross-divisionally. With an individually tailored program, we prepare you optimally for a future specialist or management function in our company.

Milos Makuljevic HR Business Partner +41 58 476 34 62

These tasks inspire you

  • Supporting product management in the implementation of product launches
  • Assistance in the implementation of product and range concepts
  • Analysis of sales, sales and assortment data and support for the creation of reports
  • Assistance in the development of marketing and sales promotion concepts
  • Participation in customer projects and annual meetings
  • Active participation in the production of meat products and fresh meat
  • Support for cross-divisional projects and process optimizations

We look forward to this profile

  • Completed apprenticeship in the food industry
  • First professional experience in sales or in an administrative function beneficial
  • Readiness for further training e.g. BP, HF, FH or the like (or in training or already completed)
  • Great interest in trade and business connections
  • Analytical thinking and own initiative
  • Willingness to continuously develop
  • Independent, exact and solution-oriented working method
  • Good MS Office knowledge / SAP knowledge is an advantage
