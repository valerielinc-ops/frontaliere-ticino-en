Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a

Senior physician Physical Medicine (PMR) or Rheumatology / General Internal Medicine

Your tasks

  • Care: Medical care of patients in the field of inpatient musculoskeletal and internist rehabilitation
  • Rehabilitation: Planning of the rehabilitation steps in an interdisciplinary team with nurses and therapists
  • Leadership: Support and deputy of the medical leadership
  • Supervision: Technical support and supervision of our assistant doctors
  • Further development : With suitable qualifications, there is an opportunity to assume a managerial function

Your profile •

  • Qualifications: Recognized specialist title in General Internal Medicine (AIM) and Rheumatology or in General Internal Medicine and Physical Medicine (PMR) with MEBEKO recognition or federation. Diploma
  • Permits: Professional Permit (BAB)
  • Personality: You enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation, convince through a sense of responsibility and have open, appreciative communication skills

What we offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial working atmosphere: Working in a supportive and appreciative team
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: We offer you a generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts at various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers and pharmacies
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits in the thermal baths FORTYSEVEN and the thermal bath Bad Zurzach
