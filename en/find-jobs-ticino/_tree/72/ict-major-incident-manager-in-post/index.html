Full description

When a serious IT disruption occurs, it takes someone to take responsibility, provide guidance and bring people together. This is where you come into play: As an ICT Major Incident Manager in the Operati-on Control Center (OCC), you coordinate the joint management of critical situations and get our IT services back on track with the specialists. With your overview and your determination, you also keep those involved under pressure capable of acting – until the affected IT services are available again.

You can do that.

  • You take the lead in the event of major IT disruptions, create orientation, bundle the forces of all parties involved and control the joint work until the situation is stabilized.
  • With clear communication, you ensure that technical teams and management keep an overview even in demanding situations and know the next steps.
  • You bring together the right experts, coordinate their deployment across technical boundaries and with the aim of restoring the affected IT services.
  • In addition, you use your experience from critical situations, recognize optimization potential and develop the processes in Major Incident Management in a targeted manner.
  • You will participate in the IT crisis team and contribute your experience in dealing with critical IT disruptions.
  • In addition, you have the opportunity to participate in projects and IT projects and to contribute your experience from Major Incident Management.

You bring that with you

  • You have in-depth training or further education in the field of computer science, speak the language of technology and understand the underlying processes.
  • Ideally, you have experience in Major Incident Management and have an ITIL Foundation certificate or are ready to acquire it.
  • You can communicate complex topics in an understandable way, convince with diplomatic skills and build bridges between different teams and management levels.
  • You recognize connections, act forward-looking, put yourself well in different perspectives and find solution-oriented approaches even under pressure with a cool head.
  • Proactive, team-oriented action as well as the willingness to take on responsibility also outside the regular working hours as part of the Pikettdienst distinguish you.
  • You communicate well orally and in writing in German, have a good knowledge of English and every other national language is an advantage.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
