Nestled in the heart of the Valais Alps at 1,400 m above sea level, the Leukerbad Clinic enjoys an exceptional natural setting and unique thermal water at 51°C. Rehabilitation clinic, we welcome patients in stationary and outpatient hospitalization in a high-level care environment.

As part of the strengthening of our organization, we put the position of Administrative/Logistic/Current co-ordinator. We offer a stimulating and meaningful position, attractive conditions and many advantages, within a dynamic team guided by strong values: empathy, commitment, evolution, professionalism and competence.
