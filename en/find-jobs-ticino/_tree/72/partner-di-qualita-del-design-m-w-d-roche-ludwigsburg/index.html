The Position We are seeking a highly motivated Design Quality Partner (DQP) to join our Product Development Quality organization. In this role, you will be a key enabler of project acceleration, balancing workload across customer areas through flexible allocation and ensuring effective partnerships within Q&R. You will be an example of living collective success—working, trusting, and empowering each other while the team builds an ambitious, next-generation AI tool.

Your Impact: What you will achieve

  • Milestone Governance & Risk Management: Partner with Strategic Quality Partners (SQP) and cross-functional stakeholders to review, approve, and ensure the completeness of milestone deliverables, acting as a pragmatic Risk Enabler in decision-making.
  • Process Excellence & Audit Readiness: Oversee the timely execution and quality management of design change requests, Non-Conformances (NC), and CAPA, while actively supporting internal and external regulatory audits.
  • Continuous Improvement & Change Leadership: Lead effective change management across R&D and Quality functions to reduce complexity, drive standardization of deliverables (templates, processes), and implement agile solutions.
  • Matrix Leadership & Vision Alignment: Build, inspire, and provide effective matrix leadership for global project teams, ensuring customer solutions deliver optimal contribution to the Roche Diagnostics Vision.
  • Coaching, Mentorship & Knowledge Management: Foster a learning-oriented environment by coaching colleagues—especially junior team members—and embedding continuous knowledge sharing and retention practices within Pre-Market Quality.

Who you are

You are a proactive, agile professional who thinks in terms of impact and outcomes rather than tasks. You are comfortable in dynamic environments and possess a unique blend of quality rigor and a tech-forward mindset.

Core Requirements

  • Education: Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Science, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • Experience: 3+ years of professional experience working with Quality Management Systems (QMS) and design controls.
  • Language Proficiency: Business fluent in German (C1) and at least intermediate proficiency in English (B2).

Skills and attributes that will elevate your impact

  • Regulatory Knowledge: A strong understanding of Quality and Regulatory requirements for reviewing Design History File (DHF) content (such as ISO 13485, FDA, GMP, or experience in other heavily regulated industries).
  • Professional Skills: Exceptional technical writing, data interpretation, communication, collaboration, and facilitation skills, along with the ability to focus on the right degree of detail.
  • AI & Tech Savviness: A solid understanding of or curiosity about AI capabilities and models, enabling you to support our tech-driven compliance initiatives.
  • Mobility: Willingness to travel internationally depending upon business needs and activities.
