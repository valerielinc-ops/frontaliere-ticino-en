Full description

Whether it is the Lötschberg Summit Tunnel, the Kander Viaduct near Frutigen or historic retaining walls along our railway lines: You recognize where action is needed and ensure that our structures remain safe and reliable. In doing so, you combine practical work on site with professional responsibility and actively help shape the maintenance of our railway infrastructure.

What awaits you

  • Inspect and assess tunnels, retaining structures and other engineering structures
  • Derive suitable maintenance measures and carry out maintenance work independently
  • Help shape norms and standards for engineering structures and historic natural stone structures
  • Support project managers in collaborating with external specialists
  • Take on technical leadership and represent BLS in specialist groups
  • Willingness to work occasional night shifts

What you bring

  • Training as a construction manager, site manager or HF technician
  • Several years of experience in the maintenance of structures, ideally also as a technology manager, client or project planner
  • A feel for historic natural stone structures and their proper maintenance
  • Assertiveness, negotiating skills and enthusiasm for interdisciplinary collaboration

Your benefits – things you can look forward to

  • Receive a GA travelcard 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare travelcard.
  • Work wherever it suits you best. Whether working from home or in the office.
  • Arrange your working hours freely. We support part-time work, top sharing and job sharing
  • You have the option of buying an additional five to ten days of vacation per year
  • You have access to financial support for external training and an exciting range of internal learning opportunities and courses.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second-largest railway company. More than that: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and safely transport cars through the Lötschberg and Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.

Requirements

  • Ausbildung als Bauleiter:in, Bauführer:in oder Techniker:in HF

  • Mehrjährige Erfahrung in der Bauwerkserhaltung, idealerweise auch als Technologiemanager:in, Bauherr:in oder Projektverfasser:in

  • Gespür für historische Natursteinbauwerke und deren fachgerechte Erhaltung

  • Durchsetzungsvermögen, Verhandlungsgeschick und Freude an der interdisziplinären Zusammenarbeit
