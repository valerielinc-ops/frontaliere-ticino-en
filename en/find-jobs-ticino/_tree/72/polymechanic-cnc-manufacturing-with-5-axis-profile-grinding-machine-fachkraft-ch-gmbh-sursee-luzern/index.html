Full description

5-axis profile grinding machines are constantly being improved and programmed. The company works with a 6-axis articulated arm robot for efficient order processing. Are you willing to take advantage of flexible working hours and contribute your experience? We are looking for a job for our client:

  • Developing and improving processes on the new 5-axis profile grinding machine
  • Developing and improving grinding programs at the CAM workstation
  • Transferring existing products to the new system
  • Processing grinding orders independently with 6-axis articulated robots
  • Carrying out minor repairs on the machine yourself
  • Requirements
  • Basic technical training such as polymechanic or production mechanic
  • Practice in grinding is mandatory
  • Good oral and written German skills
  • Structured and precise way of working
  • High awareness of quality offer
  • Scope for further development of the division
  • Flexible working hours thanks to annual working hours
  • Operations without shift operation
