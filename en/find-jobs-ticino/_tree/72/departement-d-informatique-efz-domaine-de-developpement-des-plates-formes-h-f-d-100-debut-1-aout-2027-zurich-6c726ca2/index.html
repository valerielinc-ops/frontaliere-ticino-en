Full description

What awaits you with us

An exciting introduction to the world of computer science: In the first year, you will learn the basics of IT at the Vocational Training Center Zurich Altstetten. From the second year onwards, you will be fully involved with us at the SMG in Zurich-Oerlikon and put your knowledge directly into practice - accompanied by trained young talent coaches.

Cool tasks

  • You build and operate virtual servers (Linux and Windows).
  • You plan, maintain and monitor networks (LAN).
  • You work with modern tools like Notion and ServiceNow.
  • You are part of exciting IT projects – sometimes you even manage them yourself!
  • You help us to implement the IT wishes of our customers.

A supportive environment

You are not alone! You work closely with experienced developers in the areas of IT helpdesk, IT application management and IT operations (also in the “hybrid work” setting). Our team is always at your side, giving you space for your ideas and helping you to reach your full potential.

Qualificates

This is what you bring with you

  • Talent for tinkering, analyzing and enjoying technology and modern technologies
  • Enthusiasm about programming modern technologies
  • Curiosity and courage to try something new
  • Good written German and English and the appropriate grades
  • Have fun working in a team
  • Responsibility, reliability and motivation

About us

SMG Swiss Marketplace Group AG is a pioneering network of online marketplaces and an innovative digital company that simplifies people’s lives with future-oriented products.

SMG Swiss Marketplace Group AG offers customers the best tools to help them make important life decisions. The portfolio includes real estate (ImmoScout24, Homegate, Flatfox, CASASOFT, IAZI), automobiles (AutoScout24, MotoScout24), general marketplaces (Ricardo, tutti, anibis) and finance & insurance (FinanceScout24, moneyland.ch).
