Full description

For our maintenance department, we are looking for an experienced electromechanical maintainer.

The resource will have to take care of the maintenance work of the general plant of factory, such as: electrical plants, plants of production and distribution of pharmaceutical water, air conditioning and treatment plants for production premises and white rooms, production and distribution plants compressed air, inert gas distribution plants, etc...; moreover, the tasks also include the management of the preventive and extraordinary maintenance of the machine park, for the production of medical devices

Hard Skills

  • proven experience in the role of at least 5 years;
  • welcome knowledge and experience to operate on numerical control machinery on first-order machines (Star, Citizen, Biglia, Hermle, DMG Mori, Sodik, Hardinge);
  • appreciate the knowledge of the English language.

Soft skills

  • orientation to problem solving taking into account the final goal, costs and timing;
  • to achieve the objectives;
  • leadership skills and team working;
  • availability to work both on day and on 3 shifts according to needs.

What we offer

  • Contract indefinitely;
  • Carpooling organized by Medacta;
  • Mobility incentives with public transport;
  • Advantageous business conventions;
  • Growth opportunities, working in an environment in continuous improvement and expansion;
  • Annual review of salary related to performance and professional development;
  • Continuing training.
