Technician Allrounder (100%) – City Pop | Zurich

We are looking for an experienced and versatile technician who wants to strengthen our team in the long term. If you combine craftsmanship with technical know-how and like to work independently – then you are exactly right with us.

✔️ Their tasks

  • Implementation of technical repairs (electrical, artisanal)
  • Ongoing maintenance and control of our objects (including equipment, outdoor areas)
  • Rapid error analysis and troubleshooting
  • Regular inspections of building systems (heating, ventilation, air conditioning, etc.)
  • Ensuring the correct technical condition of all installations
  • General maintenance of buildings
  • Documentation of work and reporting to supervisors
  • Cooperation with internal departments
  • Contact person for technical concerns of our customers
  • Participation in the 24/7 call in alternation

  • Readiness for emergency services outside working hours
  • Age preferably in the active occupational phase
  • Residence in Zurich or the surrounding area
  • Category B driving licence
  • Good knowledge of German + basic knowledge of English (or vice versa)
