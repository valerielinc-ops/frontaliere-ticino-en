Responsible for organizational development and skills - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite Ticino hospital is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team of the Human Resources Area of the Directorate-General, we look for a Responsible for organizational development and skills (100%) Purpose of the function: Support the Human Resources Area and the EOC Directorate General in the implementation of the People's and Culture Strategy, promoting initiatives aimed at supporting the evolution of the organization and development of people. Ensure coordination of the Organizational Development and Skills Service, promoting the integration of organizational development initiatives, management development, training and development of skills, in order to accompany the evolution of the EOC and contribute to achieving strategic objectives. Main tasks and responsibilities: Organizational development, Leadership and expertise Promote organizational development initiatives consistent with the People and Culture Strategy, in support of the Directorate-General and Human Resources Managers. Conduct organizational and climate analysis, identifying opportunities for improvement. Contribute to the evolution of human development systems, performance and potential evaluation. Promote the use of HR Analytics and decision support indicators. Define and coordinate leadership development initiatives and managerial and professional skills. Ensure an integrated training offer consistent with strategic priorities. Promote the enhancement of skills and the culture of continuous learning. Overseeing apprenticeship courses, ensuring quality and training consistency. Partnership and Governance Collaboration with Hospital and Institute Managers, with Area Leaders and Structure Managers to promote the development of people, leadership and collaboration. Coordinate the Organizational Development and Skills Service, ensuring consistency with the EOC strategy. Collaborate with Directorate General, Human Resources and other functions on transversal initiatives. Being an active member of the EOC Human Resources Board. Applicant: It is identified in the Values and Mission of the EOC. It builds effective and trusted relationships with interlocutors belonging to different professional contexts. Possiede authoritavolezza, capa
