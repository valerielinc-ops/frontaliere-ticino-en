Back to Overview Production planner - process specialist (a) 80-100 % Job opening Immediately or by agreement Employment level 100% Place of work/region BE-Bern For our client, an established Swiss production company in the Berne region, we are looking for a committed and structured personality for production planning.

Their tasks

  • Planning production orders and coordinating production processes
  • Monitor deadlines and ensure cooperation between production, logistics and supply chain
  • Analyze planning processes and optimize the use of the ERP system
  • Manage articles, parts lists and master data in the ERP system
  • Responsibility for various production areas
  • Ensure material availability and support purchasing in operational tasks

Your profile

  • Basic technical/mechanical training (e.g. polymechanic) with further training as process specialist (a) or technical merchant (a)
  • Experience in production planning, AVOR or PPS
  • Good ERP and MS Office skills
  • Very good German and English skills
  • Teamwork, flexibility and assertiveness

We offer you

  • Working hours and a 40-hour week
  • Direct communication channels and an uncomplicated working environment
  • Space for personal and professional development
