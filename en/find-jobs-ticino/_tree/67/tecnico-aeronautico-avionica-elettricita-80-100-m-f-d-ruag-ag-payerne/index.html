Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

In your function, you work directly on the military jets and mainly take on the following tasks:

  • Maintenance and modification work: implementation of planned maintenance intervals incl. Exchange & testing of avionics and electrical systems (instruments, electronics, radio and avionics)
  • Repairs & troubleshooting: analysis of error messages, troubleshooting processes incl. Repair of defects
  • Change and control orders: visual and functional tests after each flight (aircraft and system condition)
  • Working documentation and quality management
  • Visit to internal avionics training and training

You bring that with you

  • Technical / Electrotechnical vocational training EFZ (e.g. automatic technician, electronics technician, diagnostician or similar discipline)
  • Experience in aviation and/or vehicle electrics helpful, but not condition
  • Strong technical skills
  • Conversation-safe knowledge of English (level B1/B2) desired (for payers additionally knowledge of French)
  • Category B driving licence
  • High operational readiness and flexibility (possibly for possible shift and piquet work)
  • Resilient personality with team player qualities, great responsibility / security awareness and exact and reliable working methods
  • Swiss citizenship (required by function)
