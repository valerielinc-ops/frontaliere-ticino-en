The Swiss Competence Centre for Prisons (SKJV) is the platform in the field of prison in Switzerland. Based in Fribourg, we form the link between German-speaking Switzerland and Latin-speaking Switzerland. As an integral part of the SKJV, the BiSt Education Centre ensures the implementation of basic education for adult detainees in the penal and measures facilities in Switzerland.

We are looking for the Cazis Tignez*, Realta (GR), from 1\. February 2027 or by agreement

Teacher Education in Prison 40 or 60 %

Prison Education 40 or 60%, JVA Cazis Tignez

Your tasks

  • Promoting competences in the following areas: German and German as a second language (DaZ), general education topics (ABU), literacy, basics of mathematics, ICT\ fundamentals;
  • accompanying detainees from different cultural backgrounds on their individual educational path;
  • compliance with quality standards;
  • further development of educational opportunities, including in the digital sector;
  • Cooperation with the various stakeholders and participation in intercantonal meetings with other prison teachers.
  • Your profile *
  • upper secondary education master or equivalent;

  • affinity for digital teaching methods;
  • Strong communication capability;
  • Strong social skills and ability to awaken the joy of learning;
  • Good knowledge.
