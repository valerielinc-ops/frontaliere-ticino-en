  • As a technician:in catenary and train driver:in you carry out maintenance and renewal work with your team with a focus on chain plant and contribute to a safe and punctual railway traffic.
  • Safety is a top priority and you pay attention to yourself and the team in all missions.
  • Switching work and earthing work on catenary and railway power plants are carried out professionally.
  • Special vehicles for catenary construction are safely operated and carry out the associated journeys on the assigned routes.
  • You report technical defects promptly and actively bring in improvements.
  • You use location and plant knowledge specifically and adhere to the specifications and processes.

You bring that with you.

  • With a careful and responsible way of working, you protect yourself and your team.
  • You are attentive, focused and physically resilient to successfully handle this demanding work in 24-hour shift operations. You also like to work outside in any weather. You see irregular working hours, sometimes also on weekends, and/or shift work as beneficial for a flexible design of your free time.
  • Several years of practice in the maintenance and renewal of catenary systems as well as in switching and earthing.
  • Completed training as a train driver: in category B100 according to the specifications of the Federal Office of Transport.
  • Required vehicle, route and station knowledge you bring with you.
  • You have knowledge of German at level C1.
