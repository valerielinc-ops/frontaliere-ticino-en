Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The Directorate of Human Resources and Relations (DRHR) is looking for one:

Master Trainee – RH support 100% s/ref: R2026241

You are currently studying to obtain a Bachelor's degree (HEG, political sciences,...) or a Master's degree (human resources management, labour psychology, social sciences, etc.) and would like to join a recognized institution specialized in mental health.

Your main responsibilities

Support for key HR processes

  • Participation in the management of accompanying interviews (organisation, monitoring, data consolidation)
  • Contribution to evaluation campaigns (preparation, coordination, support for managers, reporting)
  • Participation in the monthly staff review (preparation, statistics, materials)
  • Contribution to the HR budget process (data preparation and consolidation)

Analysis and continuous improvement

  • Analysis of HR indicators related to accompanying processes
  • Identification of areas for improvement (benchmark, research, good practice)
  • Contribution to the implementation of concrete actions for improvement
  • Active participation in optimizing unity processes and tools

Project management

  • Participation in cross-cutting HR projects
  • Independent (supervised) piloting of a key project for HRR support unit

Your profile

  • Training in progress or recently completed: Bachelor's degree (HEG, political sciences or equivalent) or Master's degree (Human resources, labour psychology or similar field)
  • High interest in human resources, data analysis and employee support
  • Aisance with IT tools (Excel in particular)
  • Good editorial and analytical capacity
  • Organizational sense, rigour and timeliness
  • Initiative and direction continuous improvement
  • Excellent relational skills and a sense of confidentiality
