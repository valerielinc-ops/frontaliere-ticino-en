Specialist in pediatric surgery IPSI - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite Ticino hospital is present with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team at the Clinic of Paediatric Surgery and Orthopaedics of the Istituto Pediatrico della Svizzera Italiana, we look for a Medical Specialist in pediatric surgery IPSI (100%) Profile: The Pediatric Institute of Italian Switzerland, with its headquarters at the San Giovanni Hospital in Bellinzona, is the center for surgical and orthopedic care of Italian Switzerland and includes treatments in pediatric surgery, orthopaedics and pediatric traumatology throughout the territory with a cover 365 days a year, 24 hours a day. The new collaborator/collaborator will cooperate within the pediatric surgery clinic, to take care of all patients with pathologies of pediatric surgery. The person we seek needs professional experience, spirit of initiative and collaboration. He will have to be able to work in a team, have gentle ways with both the team and patients and a high resistance to variability of (ai) workloads. It also needs to be able to work in a structured and independent way and have an interest in teaching and research. He believes, like us, in the importance of putting the patient and his family at the center of all our attentions. The function involves activities in the outpatient and stationary field in the vast field of orthopaedics and pediatric traumatology, participation in teaching and research activities in collaboration with the Faculty of Biomedicine of the University of Italian Switzerland (USI).

Have excellent language skills in at least another Swiss national language as well as English. Free exercise certificate in the Canton Ticino obtained before the beginning at our Institute.
