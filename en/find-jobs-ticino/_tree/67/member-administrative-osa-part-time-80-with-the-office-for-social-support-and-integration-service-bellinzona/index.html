Full description

Tasks

  • assess the financial situation and the requirements of the applicant for social support services
  • (ii) to determine, on the basis of the principle of subsidiarity, the social support benefits available under the Law on Social Assistance (Las)
  • assess the personal and social situation of the applicant and if necessary direct it to the services/supporting entities present on the territory (social network)
  • to provide the applicant with advice on its rights and obligations under the Las, appropriate management of its financial resources and other benefits to which it may be entitled
  • properly manage any emergency situations
  • assess and define the individual project of social and/or professional inclusion of the applicant
  • manage the applicant's paper with the help of the appropriate means and tools

Requirements

  • Federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as an employee of trade, with commercial professional maturity or commercial maturity (SCC or high school) or federal certificate of capacity (AFC) employed or trade with professional experience documented in the management of social benefits
  • ability to manage stress and important workloads
  • ability to work in teams and to independently organize work
  • user orientation and assertivity
  • good ability to conduct complex interviews with users in difficulty
  • availability to follow the continuing training paths
  • the proven professional experience in social benefits will be a preferential title
  • good knowledge (conversation and editing) of Italian and spoken knowledge of a second official language

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

16 November 2026

Further information can be obtained from Mrs. Francesca Gaudenzi, Head of the Office for Social Support and Integration, Tel. 091 814 71 69.
