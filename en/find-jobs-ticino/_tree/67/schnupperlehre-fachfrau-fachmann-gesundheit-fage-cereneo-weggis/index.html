Full description

As one of the world’s leading clinics, we develop an individualized treatment program with the aim of enabling our patients to enjoy the highest possible degree of independence and freedom of life, thereby optimizing their quality of life. We are aimed at both patients from the Swiss cash system and international patients. cereneo offers highly specialized rehabilitation programs for people with neurological diseases and injuries, including stroke, brain injury, neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson’s, multiple sclerosis and many other neurological disorders. Are you interested in care and would like to find out whether the profession of specialist / health care suits you? During your trial apprenticeship at cereneo, you will get an exciting insight into the daily care life and get to know the various tasks of a health professional. You accompany our care team and can experience how we look after and support our patients in everyday life.

This is what awaits you with us

  • Insight into the varied everyday care
  • Getting to know the professional health EFZ
  • Accompaniment by experienced employees
  • Insight into cooperation within an interprofessional team
  • Personal exchange with our vocational trainers:
  • Opportunity to ask questions about education and working life

This is what you bring with you

You attend high school and are interested in training in the health care system. Contact with people makes you happy and you are open, reliable and respectful. In addition, you would like to learn new things and find out whether an education in care fits you.

Did we get your interest? Then we look forward to meeting you! Apply for a trial apprenticeship and send us your CV and your current school report via our website.
