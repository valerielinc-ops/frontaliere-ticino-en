Full description

Does your heart beat for training in the nursing profession? An engaged and passionate team is looking for reinforcement for an exciting workday in which the accompaniment, promotion and development of our learners and students are at the center. With us, you not only support our patients on their way to rehabilitation, but also accompany learners and students competently, valuingly and with pedagogical skill through their training. You promote professional, methodological and social competencies and actively shape a high-quality practical training. Care, responsibility, joy in guiding and a big heart for people are important prerequisites for optimally promoting our learners and students and caring for our patients. Your tasks

  • Training: You take on the responsibility for the practical training and accompaniment of several learners and students of the department.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You work independently in an interdisciplinary team and maintain close cooperation with vocational trainers, nurses, nursing managers, therapists and doctors.
  • Nursing: You continue to be active in nursing (approx. 20%) and ensure goal- and need-oriented, activating rehabilitation nursing.
  • Diversity: You take on varied and responsible tasks, take responsibility for the training of nursing professions including quality assurance and are directly involved in the care of our patients. Your profile
  • Training: You have a completed training as a certified nursing professional or an equivalent training recognized by SRK.
  • Further training: You have completed the vocational training course SVEB 1.
  • Experience: You have at least one year of experience in the nursing field and ideally experience as a daily accompaniment person. Personality: You are a communicative personality with high customer orientation, social competence and team spirit. Flexibility, independence and a competent and well-groomed appearance distinguish you. Moreover, you enjoy training and accompanying young people and young adults. What we offer
  • Co-design: Work in a dynamic working environment and actively contribute.
  • Working time model: You can choose between different working time models to create a balanced work-life model for yourself.
  • Inspiring and collegial working atmosphere: Work in a supportive and valuing team that works together for the best possible patient care.
  • Flexibility bonus: Voluntary participation in our internal nursing pool for additional shifts, which are additionally attractively remunerated.
  • Above-average social security contributions: Benefit from attractive social security contributions and comprehensive benefits.
  • Training and further education: Generous cost participation in training and further education.
  • Discounts: Benefit from discounts with various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work.
  • Free entry: Enjoy free entry and further discounts in the wellness-therme FORTYSEVEN. We look forward to your online application. Contact person
