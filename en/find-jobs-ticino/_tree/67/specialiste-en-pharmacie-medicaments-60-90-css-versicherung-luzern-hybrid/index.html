Full description

We at CSS are a health partner for 1.7 million insured persons. And maybe soon your partner for your professional future? We would be delighted. Because here you can make a difference: With your medical expertise and your enthusiasm for the topic of medicines and pharmaceuticals, you help us decide on the cost of applications for medicines. You can expect a motivated team of medical professionals, flexible working hours and space for your personal development. Does that sound good? Then we look forward to meeting you!

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: You decide on the cost assumption of medication applications based on medical reports and diagnoses. Here you can fully use your expert knowledge.
  • Answering requests for medications in your focus area is completely in your hands. You determine your focus with your group leader and team based on your skills and preferences.
  • You shape the cooperation with pharmaceutical companies in partnership and on an equal footing.
  • In unclear cases, you work closely with your team and our trusted medical service.
  • You have the opportunity to take on special tasks.
  • Your ideas are in demand! When it comes to optimizing existing processes, you always think one step ahead and contribute to the further development of the department.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You have completed basic training in the medical or nursing sector, e.g. nursing specialist, specialist pharmacy (pharma assistant), pharmacist – this forms your solid foundation.
  • You have at least 5 years of professional experience and have collected this in a medical institution (hospital, medical practice), a pharmacy or a health insurance company.
  • You express yourself very well in German (verbal and written). Maybe you can even speak French or Italian? All the better!
  • In case processing, you work independently, take responsibility for your decisions and always keep an eye on quality and customer orientation.
  • You go the extra mile: At times there is a lot to do and you can handle it well.
  • For you, teamwork means not only cooperation, but real cooperation.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
  • Engage Socially: Does Life Mean Good to You? Then let others share your happiness and expand your horizons alone or in a team.
  • Enjoy your family life: Make time for your offspring: 3 weeks for fathers, for mothers up to 20 weeks. There are also generous child and education allowances.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
