Full description

Are you looking for a training course? We are a manufacturing watchmaker of high-end products growing, established for several years in Fleurier, Val-de-Travers. Our ambition is to be a centre of excellence in the development and industrialization of mechanical movements. You have a good level of education and are (or will be) promoted from your 11th year of school. Your strong interest in the branches of mathematics and physics naturally directs you towards a learning place in a technical profession. With a good visual acuity, you are interested in working on high-end watch products. Of a patient nature, you love precision work and are sensitive to care and cleanliness. We will be happy to welcome you at the beginning of August 2027 in a pleasant setting equipped with latest generation machines to accompany you for 4 years of training to obtain the CFC: Micromechanic CFC (M/F/D), option Manufacturing and CNC You will be trained in the manufacture of parts and components for our watchmaking and decoration workshops on conventional machines or digital commands. If you are ready to take up the challenge, we look forward to your complete application (CV, cover letter), along with a copy of your school bulletins for the last 3 years to send us by logging on to our website at the following address: https://www.chopard.com/intl/careers
