Full description

Assistant for medical study - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team of the medical secretariat of the EOC Rehabilitation Clinic (CREOC), headquarters of Novaggio we look for a(a) Medical Study Assistant (50%) Profile: The people we wish to meet have excellent organizational and communication skills; they are able to respond to the many demands with flexibility and courtesy and they can interact positively with interlocutors from different fields, both professional and hierarchical, contributing to create a collaborative climate and demonstrating sensitivity in interpersonal relationships. They operate with a high degree of autonomy in the management of activities and priorities, while maintaining a strong orientation to teamwork. Reliability and ability to focus their efforts, both independently and in teams, on the basis of priorities, combined with an overview and sensitivity to meet the needs of the user, complete the required profile.

  • Required requirements: Federal certificate of capacity as a medical assistant AFC (or equivalent title) attested to additional training for medical assistants in extended conventional radiographic techniques is a preferential professional experience in the health sector is a preferential title excellent spoken and written knowledge of the Italian language excellent spoken and written knowledge of a national working language is a preferential experience at the front office and predisposition to the contact with the master’s office. Date of entry: From 1 November 2026 or date from convent Applications and information: Applications, complete with curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, copies of certificates of study and work, will have to be sent by 5 July 2026 only in electronic format via www.eoc.ch
