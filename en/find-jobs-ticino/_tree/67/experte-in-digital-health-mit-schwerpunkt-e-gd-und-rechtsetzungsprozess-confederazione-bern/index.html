Full description

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20555

You can make this contribution

  • Addressing complex technical and technical issues relating to the electronic health dossier (E-GD) within the framework of the Swiss Health Data Space (SwissHDS)
  • Analysis of technical and technical problems as well as the development of solutions and concepts within the framework of the development of the legal requirements of the E-GD
  • Accompanying the certification and operation of the federally managed systems for today's electronic patient dossier (mainly the certification test system provided by the FOPH)

This makes them unique.

  • University degree (ideally in computer science, medical informatics or business informatics) and at least 7 years of work experience in the field of eHealth
  • In-depth knowledge in the technology, development and operation of large IT solutions in the healthcare sector and ideally also in the procurement of such solutions
  • In-depth technical knowledge of EPD components, engineering and architecture
  • Practical experience in handling complex projects and multi-stakeholder management related to the Swiss health system
  • Further interest in and knowledge of the legal and organizational framework of eHealth, public services and the political system of Switzerland
  • Rapid comprehension, organizational skills, independent and structured working methods with an above-average degree of personal responsibility and high resilience
  • Very good editorial and communication skills and confident appearance as well as active knowledge of two official languages and, if possible, passive knowledge of the third official language

  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.
