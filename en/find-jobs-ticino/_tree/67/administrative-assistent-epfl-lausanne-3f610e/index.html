Full description

Mission

For the Laboratory of Sustainable and Catalytic Processing, led by Professor Jeremy Luterbacher within the School of Basic Sciences at EPFL, we are looking to recruit an Administrative Assistant at 50%.

Main duties and responsibilities

  • Administrative management of staff members and interns, support for the hiring process, accreditation, absences follow-up, onboarding of new lab members, employee lifecycle.
  • Accounts management (e.g., account follow-up, budget forecasting, financial reporting) of the lab’s funds.
  • Expenses and credit card management and follow-up
  • Provision of general support and logistics for the lab, purchases, invoicing, office space, etc.
  • Administrative support with memos and reports
  • Administrative tasks in relation to classes (exams’ scheduling, course administration, archival, interaction with expert examiners and SAC)
  • Organization of events (planning, reservations, purchasing, reporting), including support in managing Professor’s agenda
  • Organisation of travel for lab members (reservations for travel and accommodation, reimbursements)
  • Organization of academic guest visits including seminars (visas, logistics, travel and accommodation)

Profile

  • Self-driven, rigorous, organized, proactive, and with great attention to detail
  • Previous experience as an administrative assistant at EPFL and/or experience with SAP is an asset
  • Full proficiency in both French and English is mandatory (both written and spoken)
  • Experience with billing, accounting, funds management (e.g. SNF and third-party)
  • Computer proficiency, including strong proficiency in Excel or Google sheets
  • Able to interact in a multicultural / international environment
  • Strong communication and interpersonal abilities
  • Commercial background or education preferred (CFC de Commerce or equivalent)

We offer

At EPFL, we believe that people achieve great results when they feel supported, respected and enabled to succeed. By joining our community, you will help shape the future of society through education, research and innovation.

We offer an inspiring, international and collaborative environment where your work has real impact and your ideas are valued. Combined with flexible ways of working and attractive employment conditions, we aim to create an environment where everyone can contribute, grow and make a difference.

Beyond work, our campuses host a vibrant community and unique environment, enriched by initiatives promoting equality, inclusion, diversity, well-being and sustainability as well as an engaging program of cultural, sports and community activities throughout the year.
