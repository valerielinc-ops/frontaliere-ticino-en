Full description

Are you a thrilling organizational talent with civil engineering experience and ready to lead construction projects to success on your own responsibility? ####

Our client is an innovative construction company with exciting projects, flat hierarchies and a modern, team-oriented working environment.

The main tasks ####

  • Independent management of demanding civil engineering projects
  • Coordination with representatives of the building authority
  • Organisation of construction site processes and management of employees
  • Responsibility for the professional use of machinery and equipment
  • Participation in construction meetings and work preparation (AVOR)

The requirement profile ####

  • Basic training as mason, road builder or similar
  • Sound professional experience in civil engineering required
  • Completed training as a polisher or foremen
  • Organisational strength in site management and preparation
  • Communication ability in dealing with building owners
  • High operational readiness and resilience
