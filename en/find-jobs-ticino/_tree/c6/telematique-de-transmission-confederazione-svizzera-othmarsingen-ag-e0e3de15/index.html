Put to the Point

With your technical expertise, you make an important contribution to the operational readiness of the Swiss Army and ensure that modern transmission and communication systems function reliably. You can expect a versatile technical working environment in which you can contribute your experience, take on responsibility and further develop your expertise in a targeted manner.

  • Date of entry: 01.04.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20309
  • Location: Lenzburgerstrasse 86, 5504 Othmarsingen

You can make this contribution

  • Check, maintain, repair and ensure the technical readiness of the Swiss Army transmission, communication and telematics systems
  • Systematic analysis of malfunctions in transmission and communication systems, localization of errors and professionally carrying out necessary repairs
  • Inspect, exchange, configure and control electronic assemblies, devices, wiring and system components
  • Perform periodic maintenance and technical inspections in accordance with specifications and professionally correct detected defects
  • Document maintenance work and technical findings as well as reliably return executed work and work orders in the SAP system
  • Assist military personnel as well as internal and external payers with technical issues and ensure professional handling of the systems under management

This makes them unique.

  • Complete vocational training as a telematician, electronics technician, multimedia electronics technician or equivalent technical training
  • Professional experience in maintenance, repair and troubleshooting of electronic, communication or transmission systems
  • Have good technical understanding and knowledge in measurement, network and communication technology and quickly grasp complex technical relationships
  • Have good IT user knowledge and ideally have experience with SAP
  • Have category B driving licence and ideally bring category C1 driving licence
  • Working independently, carefully and solution-oriented, taking on responsibility and working together reliably in a team
