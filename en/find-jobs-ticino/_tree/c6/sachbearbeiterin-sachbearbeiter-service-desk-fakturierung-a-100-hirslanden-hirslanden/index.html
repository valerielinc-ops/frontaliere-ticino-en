Place of work: Hirslanden Corporate Office | Home Office

Composition by: 01. February 2027 or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

Our focus is not only on the well-being of our patients, but also on that of our employees. As a traditional Swiss company with an international connection to the Mediclinic Group and its divisions, we benefit from a worldwide exchange and continuous development.

YOUR TASKS

  • Information provision and processing of outpatient billing inquiries and patient inquiries
  • Treatment of all bill rejections in the ambulatory service area
  • Obtaining information from providers
  • Cancellation and refactura
  • Monitoring of open pendencies
  • Ensuring timely information to patients, insurance companies, clinics and doctors

Your profile

  • Commercial and/or basic medical education completed
  • Professional experience in health or insurance
  • In-depth knowledge of ambulatory tariffs (paramedical tariffs, KVG and UVG)
  • Good MS Office Knowledge
  • Networked thinking, social skills and responsibility round off their profile
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, French and/or English is advantageous
