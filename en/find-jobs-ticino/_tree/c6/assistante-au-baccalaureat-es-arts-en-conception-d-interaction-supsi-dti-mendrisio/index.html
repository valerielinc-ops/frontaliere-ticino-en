# Assistant with Bachelor's Degree / Master of Arts in Interaction Design

The Department of Environment, Construction and Design, based in Mendrisio, invites applications for the position of

Assistant with Bachelor's Degree in the Master of Arts in Interaction Design programme

, at the

FabLab Digital Fabrication Laboratory and the Institute of Design

. Start date: September 2026. Employment rate: 62.50%.

Responsibilities

  • Participating in the organisational management, dissemination, and maintenance of the FabLab Digital Fabrication Laboratory.
  • Participating in and providing support for the development of projects within the interaction design research area.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Visual Communication, Interior Architecture, Architecture, Product Design, Media Interaction Design, Design Engineering, or Design and Technology, obtained no more than three years ago.
  • Excellent proficiency in leading graphic design, image editing, and desktop publishing software.
  • Strong aptitude for learning prototyping technologies.
  • Knowledge of digital communication, web design languages and techniques, and/or computer programming, as well as 3D modelling and rapid prototyping technologies (3D printing, laser cutting) is considered an asset.
  • Creative mindset, initiative, and strong communication skills.
  • Precision and attention to detail.
  • Team-oriented attitude with a strong inclination towards collaboration.
  • Ability to work independently on design projects within set deadlines.
  • Flexibility and availability in pursuit of project objectives.
  • Good command of the English language. Knowledge of Italian is desirable and/or willingness to learn the language in order to integrate into an Italian-speaking work environment. Proficiency in a second national language will be considered an advantage.

We Offer

  • A fixed-term contract of 3 years.
  • The opportunity to share activities and collaborate with a team of experienced researchers working on research and development projects in interaction design, digital fabrication, and related design disciplines.
  • Direct contact with local and international professionals.
  • Opportunities to develop new competencies, both practical and theoretical.
  • Integration into an international, dynamic, and continuously evolving work environment.
  • Stimulating and creative work within an academic setting.
  • A flexible study pathway leading to the Master of Arts in Interaction Design.

Additional Information

This position is closely linked to enrolment in the Master of Arts in Interaction Design programme (120 ECTS), offered on a part-time basis over a period of 3 years.

For further information, please contact

Massimo Botta. Only applications submitted by

5 July 2026

via the designated application form will be considered.
