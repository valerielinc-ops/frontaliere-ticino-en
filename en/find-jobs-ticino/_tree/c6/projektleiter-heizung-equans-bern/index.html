Full description

In order to strengthen our team at the Berne location, we are looking for a project manager/in heating immediately or by agreement

You can move that with us.

  • Implementation of demanding heating and refrigeration systems in the field of trade and industry
  • Planning and project planning of direct customer orders
  • Responsible for the quality, time and cost-related implementation of projects
  • Acquisition of new construction projects and maintenance of relationships with customers, architects and suppliers
  • Use of the latest findings from research and development regarding improved materials, products, processes, etc.
  • Installer management

That's what makes you.

  • Basic heating training (building engineering planner/heating EFZ)
  • Leadership experience already in a similar position or as a construction site manager is advantageous
  • Exemplary and working methods and competent performance
  • Business and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Willingness to take responsibility and also lead employees
  • Organisational and negotiation skills
  • Strong team and customer orientation
  • German in Word and Writing and Ticket Kat. B is mandatory

That's what we offer you.

  • Varied activities
  • Established team
  • Attractive social benefits
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Assistance in the development of the heating department
