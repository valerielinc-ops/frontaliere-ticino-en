Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for you for the predominantly open acute ward KPP-4 with a focus on psychosis, immediately or by appointment, as tasks:

  • You support and promote the social abilities and skills of patients in a milieu therapeutic setting as part of caregiver work
  • You competently implement the nursing process and promote the health and resources of patients
  • You care for people with mental illness and involve relatives and follow-up carers in your work
  • Act in crisis situations You are appropriately competent and convey security to the patients
  • You see yourself as part of an interprofessional treatment team with responsibility for the optimal implementation of the concept and the PDAG mission statement
  • You receive an acute allowance on our ward Requirements:
  • Qualified nursing specialist (HF), preferably with professional experience in (acute) psychiatry
  • You are an enthusiastic professional with a high level of technical, social and self-competence and are interested in actively developing the treatment concept with us
  • You have a resource- and solution-oriented understanding of care. We offer: Our employees are the heart of our company. That's exactly why we offer you a lot: we strongly encourage further training. We also invest a lot in team cohesion and offer all of our teams access to the “Moodtalk” software, which strengthens team communication. We also offer attractive discounts on well-known brands such as Ikea, Zalando, Coop, Lidl and many more. Employees who commute to us by public transport receive an impressive eco-bonus at the end of the year. We also offer an attractive pension fund solution. We also promote work-life balance: We are always open to part-time work and offer the opportunity to buy a holiday.
