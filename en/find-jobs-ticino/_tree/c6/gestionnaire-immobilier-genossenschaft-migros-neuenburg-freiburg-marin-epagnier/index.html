Full description

With 1,800 employees and 51 sales outlets, Migros Neuchâtel-Freiburg combines humanity, innovation and sustainability to further develop the region. Your talent counts! Become part of a dynamic community and develop in an environment that supports your goals. Let us shape the future together.

Mansioni

  • Manage a portfolio of commercially used properties – mainly Migros centres and some mixed-use and residential properties – independently.
  • Be the first contact person for our tenants and build trusting relationships with them: Negotiation, drafting and support of rental contracts as well as management of supplements, extensions and terminations.
  • Monitor all administrative tasks related to management: invoicing, incidental cost accounting, indexing and collection of turnover figures.
  • Work closely with internal teams (projects and construction, facility management, accounting, center management) to ensure smooth and coordinated management.
  • Act as a landlord or tenant – with accuracy, reliability and a sense of responsibility.
  • Actively contribute to the marketing of the space: tenant search, vacancy management and market analysis.

Requirements

  • At least 2 years of experience in a comparable function: property management
  • Eidg. Technical card: Real estate management
  • German (negotiable): French is advantageous
  • In-depth knowledge of commercial tenancy law and management tools, ideally SAP RE-FX.
  • A good sense of priorities as well as clear and constructive communication.
  • You take your own initiative and actively share your knowledge in a collaborative and interdisciplinary environment.
