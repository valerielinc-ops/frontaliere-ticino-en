Sustainability Hub

Job as of 1 February 2027 or by arrangement, temporary

The Sustainability Hub has the task of coordinating and promoting aspects of sustainability in research, teaching and operational operations at the University of Zurich. For this purpose, its employees are in exchange with researchers and lecturers from all faculties and employees of administrative and technical staff, as well as with the media and the public. We are looking for a person who develops, coordinates and advances sustainability measures in university teaching.

Tasks

Her tasks include in particular the development and implementation of further education offers and workshops to reflect on the topic of sustainable development in teaching. Further tasks are the development of teaching materials and the coordination of an expertise pool. You will work with another person from the Sustainability Hub (responsible for research and teaching).

Profile

  • You have a university master’s degree preferably in social sciences; A doctorate is beneficial.
  • They have knowledge of scientific concepts of an education for sustainable development.
  • They have experience in university teaching and didactic methods.
  • You have experience in interdisciplinary communication and project work.
  • They are familiar with university structures and processes, knowledge of UZH is an advantage.
  • They have experience in attracting people to change towards more sustainable behavior and processes and accompanying them in the process of change.
  • They are accustomed to working independently and are able to set priorities even in hectic times.
  • They have the talent to communicate clearly verbally and in writing. We presuppose style-confident and fluent German and English.
