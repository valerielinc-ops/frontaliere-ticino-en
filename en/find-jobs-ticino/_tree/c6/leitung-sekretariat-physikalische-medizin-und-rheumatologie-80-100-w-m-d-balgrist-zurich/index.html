Full description

Balgrist University Hospital is one of the most renowned orthopaedic clinics in Switzerland with an affiliated centre for paraplegia and international recognition. Our medical secretariat is the first point of contact for our patients and handles all administrative work around the medical secretariat. We are looking for one or more by 1 January 2027 or by agreement

Tasks

  • Leadership and coordination of the secretarial team, including personnel planning, monitoring of working hours and conducting employee interviews
  • Support in the recruitment process and training of new employees
  • Organisation and logging of team and secretarial meetings
  • VIP management and expense reporting for the chief physician
  • Organisation and coordination of office hours including scheduling
  • Complete a wide range of administrative tasks such as correspondence, postal processing and the writing and editing of reports
  • Support for smooth running of office hours

Requirements

  • Commercial/medical background with several years of professional experience in a clinic environment
  • Several years of management experience required or several years of professional experience in the secretariat, ideally in a leadership role
  • Excellent organizational skills and a structured way of working
  • Strong social and communication skills as well as a secure and assertive appearance
  • Experienced handling of the common MS Office applications and ideally with the KISIM and PEP time recording system

We offer

  • Regulated working hours
  • Mind. 26 holiday days per year and gifted free day for birthday
  • 50 % discount on the ZVV bonus pass or an annual contribution to a general subscription
  • Enjoy a healthy and balanced lunch at discounted prices at our company-owned staff restaurant
  • 20% discount in the Balgrist pharmacies as well as other various shopping discounts
  • Working in a prestigious university orthopaedic clinic with an excellent reputation
