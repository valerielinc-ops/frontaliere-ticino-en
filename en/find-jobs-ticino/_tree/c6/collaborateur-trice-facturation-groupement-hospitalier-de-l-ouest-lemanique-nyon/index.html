Full description

GHOL is the reference hospital for the German West with its structures on Nyon and Rolle. Tomorrow, we propose to continue your professional development by involving you in your new missions and by participating in the development of GHOL, in its new hospital building at the Nyon site.

Description of position

In your new duties, your missions have an impact on the quality of patient care. Your role will include:

  • Seizing outpatient and stationary medical services, drugs and other non-medical services
  • Control administrative data of patient records
  • Ensure full seizure of medical fees
  • Ensure completeness of benefits entered from files
  • Generate outpatient and stationary invoices from all GHOL medical disciplines
  • Process returns of invoices and their justification with our paying partners by defending the best interests of the Institution
  • Keep up to date on developments in its business sector
  • Process additional requests from patients and third parties
  • Answering telephone calls
  • Work closely with other services in Patient and Medical Care Management.
  • Participate in updating work procedures and continuous improvement of practices
  • Actively participate in monthly, quarterly and annual billing loops
  • Anticipating the new features of tariff catalogues as a whole

Profile required

We are convinced of your rapid integration into your future functions through your:

  • Minimum experience of 3 years in hospital or in the field of sickness/accident insurance
  • In-depth knowledge of tariff structures (CPTMA, Tarmed, SwissDRG)
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss airframe system (LAMal, LAA, LCA)
  • Analysis capacity and proposal strength
  • Flexibility and good stress management
  • Very good computer tools mastery (Opale, MOY, Excel)
  • Interest in innovation and new developments related to your sector
  • Good knowledge in English, German an asset

Level of training

SWC administrative employee or equivalent

Benefits

A pleasant working environment with flexible working hours and the possibility of working at home; A human-sized team that promotes exchanges and your progress

  • Collaborate with innovative managers, active in their environment and creative to meet current and future challenges
  • The opportunity to carry out projects to develop in a strategic sector
  • Participate in the development of artificial intelligence tools in the implementation of data science within the institution

The dynamic operation of a private company whose recognition of the public interest guarantees the benefits of the CTC applicable in the parapublic health sector.
