Full description

Experis is the IT & Technology company of the ManpowerGroup. We partner with innovative companies to identify and attract top ICT talent, supporting both professionals and organizations in their digital growth and transformation.

For a leading international company based in the Lugano area, we are looking for a:

We are looking for an IT Security Engineer to join our Security Architecture team.

The successful candidate will be responsible for designing, implementing and continuously improving enterprise IT security solutions, helping protect the company's infrastructure and services through secure architectures and effective security controls.

Key Responsibilities

  • Review IT architectures and technical solutions to ensure compliance with security standards and best practices.
  • Define security requirements and recommend risk-based security controls.
  • Implement, manage and optimize enterprise security solutions across SIEM, EDR/XDR, IAM/PAM, Vulnerability Management, Network, Endpoint and Cloud Security.
  • Develop and maintain security standards, guidelines and reference architectures.
  • Conduct security assessments, threat modelling and technical risk analyses.
  • Collaborate with Infrastructure, Cloud, Network and Development teams to integrate security into systems and processes.
  • Support security incident investigation and response activities.
  • Promote Secure by Design, Zero Trust and Defence in Depth principles.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Security, Engineering or a related field.
  • At least 5 years of experience in IT Security, Security Engineering, Network Security, Application Security or Cloud Security.
  • Hands-on experience managing enterprise security technologies and reviewing IT architectures from a security perspective.
  • Good knowledge of security frameworks and principles, particularly NIST CSF, Zero Trust and Defence in Depth.
  • Experience in at least one of the following areas: SIEM, EDR/XDR, IAM/PAM, Vulnerability Management, Network Security, Cloud Security or Endpoint Security.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a collaborative approach.
  • Availability to participate in an on-call rotation outside standard business hours.
  • Fluent Italian and good command of English.

Nice to Have

  • Security certifications such as Security+, CySA+, SC-200, Azure Security Engineer, CISSP Associate, CCSP or equivalent.
  • Experience with Microsoft Azure security services.
  • Knowledge of automation, DevOps practices, CI/CD pipeline development and Agentic AI technologies.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
