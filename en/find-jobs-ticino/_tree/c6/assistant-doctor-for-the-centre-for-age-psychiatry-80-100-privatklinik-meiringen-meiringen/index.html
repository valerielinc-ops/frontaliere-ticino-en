Full description

The Meiringen Private Clinic is located in the impressive landscape of the Haslital, in the Bernese Oberland. Here people with all forms of psychological suffering find reception, treatment and care. The third-largest psychiatric clinic in the Canton of Bern is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for the inpatient primary psychiatric care of the eastern Bernese upper country. Care and care take place according to current medical-psychiatric and nursing knowledge. As a member of Swiss Leading Hospitals, Privatklinik Meiringen is one of the leading private clinics in Switzerland.

The team from the Center for Old Age Psychiatry in Lenggasshüüs looks after people with diseases from the entire spectrum of psychiatry who require extensive psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment. The center has a 24-hour emergency admission requirement for retirement psychiatry and is protected and run openly. Patients and employees benefit from a modern infrastructure that is adapted to the needs of older people. The service mandate is based on the latest scientific findings in an individually defined treatment and care process. Psychosocial reintegration is considered a priority treatment goal.

Their tasks

  • Self-responsible care of the assigned patients with full assumption of case responsibility under specialist supervision
  • Conduct of psychiatric examinations and preparation of findings
  • Diagnostic clarification including preparation of diagnoses and differential diagnoses
  • Initiation and interpretation of neuropsychological and test-psychological additional examinations
  • Planning and coordination of individual treatment and therapy concepts
  • Psychotherapeutic indication and participation in the determination of suitable forms of therapy
  • Performing psychotherapeutic treatments under supervision
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation

Our expectations

  • Medical diploma recognised in Switzerland (MEBEKO)
  • Started psychotherapy training desirable
  • Experience in diagnostics and treatment of mental and medical conditions is advantageous
  • Beginners or foreign specialists with a high willingness to learn and interest in the field welcome

Our offer

  • Further training for 6 months (mandatory for the specialist in psychiatry and geriatrics)
  • Further training for the specialist psychiatry and psychotherapy for 3 years inpatient (A), 2 years inpatient (C), 3 years outpatient (A)
  • If interested, we also offer the opportunity for scientific work (promotion)
  • Generous financial support for further education, supervision and psychotherapy training
  • Direct psychiatric and internistic supervision
  • Attractive employment conditions, including the possibility of ambulant additional earning opportunities
  • Extensive benefits, including a free day on the birthday, various free sports offers as part of health promotion and varied staff events
