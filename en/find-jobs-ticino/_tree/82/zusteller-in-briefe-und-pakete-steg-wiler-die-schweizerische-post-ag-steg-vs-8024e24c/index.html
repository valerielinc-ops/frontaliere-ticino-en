Full description

Do you like getting up early and looking for a physically challenging activity? Then you fit us perfectly! With you, dreams arrive unharmed and on time.

*You can do it

  • Early in the morning (about 6 a.m.) you load the delivery vehicles together with your team and prepare your delivery tour.
  • Through your work you shape the streetscape of Steg or Wiler (Lötschen) and embody the values of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the punctual and impeccable delivery of the parcels and letters in your area.
  • On your tour, you answer questions from our customers and contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • You also work on Saturdays, with services split up in the team.

You bring that with you

  • With your physical fitness, you can easily load and lift packages up to 30 kg on a regular basis.
  • You have the driving licences of categories A1 and B. If you do not yet have the A1 card, you bring the willingness to purchase it later.
  • You appreciate the work in the team but you also like to travel independently and feel comfortable with large vehicles in road traffic.
  • You are reliable and work fast.
  • You communicate very well verbally and in writing in German and have an experienced handling of digital devices.

Your advantages in the post.

We offer a fair wage, which ranges between CHF 57’700 and CHF 67’700 per year (100% fee) depending on experience and skills. Also applications of talents with different competencies are welcome, in such cases your wage can be slightly below or above the bandwidth.

With us, you work in a future-oriented, dynamic environment in which you are involved and with which you continue to grow. With us, you can expect various benefits, with which you can flexibly design your everyday life according to your needs.

Holidays

We will send you 6 weeks on holiday.

Education and training

We support you with training and further education, temporary job changes and offer you promotion opportunities.

Family friendly

We offer 18 weeks of fully paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of leave for the second parent. We also contribute to the costs of family-supplemented childcare.

Total employment contract

The collective employment contract protects, strengthens and creates clear rules – for fair employment and working conditions.

Mobility offers

We offer you a free half-tax subscription, a discounted GA or a commuter bonus. You pay only CHF 10 per year at PubliBike or Velospot.

Vehicle benefits

We offer you attractive advantages when buying a vehicle: You benefit from fleet discounts and special conditions on new models. With Yellow Drive, we offer you attractive advantages on electric car subscriptions.

PostMobil and PostFinance

Through PostMobile, we offer you an attractive employee subscription for CHF 12.95 per month. With PostFinance, you also benefit from numerous advantages, such as a free private account.

Advantages and rebates

We offer you numerous perks on brands and products. At the staff restaurants of the Post Office and other restaurants you also eat and drink at discounted prices.

Vouchers

We give you up to CHF 500 personal credit, which you can use for all products in the PostShop (including gift cards) as well as for products from SBB and Reka.
