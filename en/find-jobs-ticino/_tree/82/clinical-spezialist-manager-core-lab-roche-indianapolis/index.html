The Position Clinical Specialist Manager, Core Lab

The Opportunity The Clinical Specialist Manager, Roche Core Lab is a national business leader responsible for translating clinical capability into measurable commercial impact. This role leads a team of Clinical Specialists to develop and execute a clear clinical strategy aligned to enterprise priorities, driving growth of High Medical Value Assays (HMVAs) across the Core Lab portfolio. This individual operates at the intersection of clinical insight and business strategy—partnering across Sales, Marketing, Medical & Scientific Affairs (MSA), and Corporate Accounts to shape opportunities, influence decision-making, and deliver against revenue and financial objectives. The ideal candidate is not purely clinical or technical, but a strategic operator who can synthesize complex information, align stakeholders, and lead with discipline to scale impact nationally.

This is a field based role - you may live anywhere in the United States.

Cross-Functional Leadership
