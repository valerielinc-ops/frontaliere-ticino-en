Full description

Do you want to perform a varied, demanding and responsible function at the heart of the functioning of the Orb Plain Establishments (EPO)? Join our team and put your administrative, organisational and editorial skills at the service of an essential mission. In close connection with the Directorate, the judicial authorities and internal and external partners, you ensure the administrative and operational follow-up of detainees, coordinate procedures related to their journey and ensure that the legal and security framework is respected. You contribute to the proper functioning of the Registry by monitoring deadlines, transfers and authorizations, as well as the production of official documents and statistics. You also support the administrative sector and members of the Directorate. A versatile position that requires rigour, discretion, organizational sense and ability to manage complex situations. You act at all times in accordance with our values: "Loyality, Solidarity, Credibility".

  • Analyse individual situations and deal with all the letters received from different criminal authorities and sectors of the institution in connection with the detention of detained persons. Verify the legal and administrative conformity of the parts;
  • Prepare files for decisions of the Directorate and authorities (exits, transfers, execution of sentences, etc.);
  • Establish, maintain and store individual records of detainees;
  • Managing visits and hearings authorizations and determining their feasibility: comprehensive situation and judgement analysis, advance notice, organization, coordination and production of associated documents.
  • Manage the Directorate's agenda and arrange appointments and sessions;
  • Take and write minutes;
  • Ensure, develop and maintain contacts with bodies, services, agencies, institutions and persons directly related to the activities of the SPEN, the lives of prisoners.
  • Participate in the training of business apprentices within the sector
  • For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Didier Gauthey – Managing Director at 024 557 24 04.
  • SWC trade employee or equivalent;
  • With at least 3 years of confirmed experience in a criminal chain entity;
  • Very good organizational skills;
  • High discretion in the processing of sensitive data;
  • Excellent relational skills, teamwork and empathy;
  • Good mastery of French in both oral and written terms and mastery of the usual computer tools. Our terms and conditions of employment require you to be a Swiss citizen or holder of a licence C, free of property default (an excerpt from the Office of Prosecutions must be presented during the process) and your criminal record must be blank. There is a right to consult the criminal record under the Federal Criminal Records Act by the engagement authority for this function.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
