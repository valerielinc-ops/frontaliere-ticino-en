HR Team Barmelweid Resource Therapist (a) Saturday services and public holidays We are looking for a resource therapist with enthusiasm for experience- and action-oriented activities, who accompanies our patients on 3-4 Saturdays per month and individual holidays per year. Employment level: 20% admission immediately or by appointment More holidays on request, public transport contribution, Reka checks, snow sports day or by you with the boss? These and other advantages are offered by the Barmelweid. Our resource-oriented therapeutic offer on Saturday should conclude the therapy week positively for all patients who are treated in the Department of Psychosomatic Medicine and Psychotherapy. Our resource therapy team is small but fine – motivated, reliable and characterized by broadly covered expertise. We work hand in hand and live the interprofessional exchange. Are you interested in contributing your ideas and creativity and jointly developing the offer of resource therapy? We look forward to your application. You are independently responsible for the preparation and implementation of the resource therapy offer on Saturday. Planning and implementation of experience- and action-oriented activities with patient groups of 13 people. You are in active exchange with the care team/resource activation. You inspire us... Due to your education in social education or socio-cultural animation, activation therapy, special therapy or similar professional fields. Because you have experience in the psychiatric/psychosomatic field or you are interested in this field. Because you have the willingness to engage with the concept of positive psychology and salutogenesis and are interested in further developing the resource-oriented internal concept. Due to your high ability for self-reflection and personal development and for you proactive, thoughtful and prudent action are self-evident. Barmelweid offers you an attractive, interdisciplinary working environment in a family atmosphere. This also includes advanced employment conditions, advantageous social benefits, a versatile leisure offer and an in-house day care centre.
