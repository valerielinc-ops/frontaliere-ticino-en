Full description

The North Face EMEA is looking for an exceptional Digital Content Operations Coordinator to join our Digital Marketplace Team in Stabio, Switzerland.

Let's Talk about the Role

The Digital Content Operations Coordinator supports the end-to-end execution of product content workflows, ensuring PDP assets - images and descriptions are delivered accurately and on time.

This role partners closely with ecommerce merchandising, content, and creative teams as well as external agencies / photo shooting studio to maintain data quality, manage operational processes, and support content readiness across digital channels.

How You Will Make A Difference

  • Coordinate product sample ordering, tracking and management throughout content production workflows.
  • Own the coordination and shipment of marketing samples, ensuring products are delivered on time and aligned with campaign, PR, and business requirements.
  • Maintain and validate product line lists, ensuring accuracy and readiness for content production.
  • Manage the tracking and delivery of digital assets and product content to internal systems and stakeholders.
  • Partner with content, creative, ecommerce, and external vendors to ensure timely delivery of content assets.
  • Coordinate product description creation, review, and translation activities.
  • Identify, investigate, and resolve product, content, and asset discrepancies.
  • Perform quality checks to ensure content accuracy, completeness, and consistency.
  • Support process improvements and operational reporting.

Skills for Success

  • 2-3 years’ experience in eCommerce, digital content operations, merchandising operations, or a related field.
  • Excellent interpersonal skills with the ability to collaborate across teams and stakeholders.
  • Strong organizational and project coordination skills.
  • Excellent attention to detail and problem-solving abilities.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and other Office applications.
