Full description

Planzer Paket stands for reliable and fast parcel deliveries in Switzerland. As part of our team, you actively contribute to further developing our high quality standards and service. We offer you a varied job in a future-oriented company that focuses on sustainable solutions. Become part of our team and shape the future of the package world!

What you move

  • Early in the morning, pick up your ready-loaded vehicle and set off to deliver packages in your delivery area.
  • You deliver packages reliably to our valued customers and accept returns when needed
  • Together with you, your vehicle is the most important and always visible business card of our company – so you take care of its care and maintenance
  • Are you ready for more? Exciting additional tasks await you

Why do you succeed?

  • You have owned your driving licence Kat. B for at least 3 years and are well versed in driving manually switched vehicles
  • You are fit and like to pack – packages up to 30 kg do not get you out of breath
  • If you already have experience in the package service: Jackpot! If not, you learn quickly
  • Your reputation is impeccable, there is nothing wrong with you.
  • Oral and written you can safely express yourself in German
  • Saturday assignments? No problem, you are at the start!

Your benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Quality bonus: Quality is written in our capital and rewarded
  • Central introductory training: You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Good transport connections: Top transport connections - faster to work, more free time

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
