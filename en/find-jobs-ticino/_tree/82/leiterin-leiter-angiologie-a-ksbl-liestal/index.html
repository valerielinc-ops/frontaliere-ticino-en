Full description

Admission as of 1 March 2027 · Bruderholz, Liestal, Innere Medizin · Job reference 13223 · Permanent employment

Tasks

  • Take over the personnel, technical and organisational management of the Angiology Department at all locations of the KFSL
  • Ensure high-quality, patient-centered angiological care
  • Care and treatment of hospitalized and outpatient patients with the entire spectrum of angiological diseases
  • Perform and diagnose the entire non-invasive angiological diagnosis, in particular duplex sonography and ABI
  • Contribute to the further development of the diagnostic and therapeutic services and the

interdisciplinary cooperation

  • Close cooperation with vascular surgery, interventional radiology, cardiology, internal medicine and other specialist areas (USGup-certified vascular centre)
  • involvement in the clinic services
  • supervision and further training of assistants and specialists
  • ensuring high quality and safety standards
  • participation in further training, further training activities and in the interests of clinical research projects

Requirements

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine and Angiology with MEBEKO recognition.
  • Several years of clinical experience and proven expertise in the entire spectrum of angiology
  • Management experience (with appropriate further training) and joy in developing a team and a modern department
  • entrepreneurship and willingness to strategically develop angiology at the KWSL
  • Experience in own research projects and interest in the cooperation of ongoing projects
  • Empathic, committed personality with a high level of service and patient orientation
  • Expressed communication skills, networked and goal-oriented

Operation

3 years: Up and of which: "gap". 5 weeks holidays, loyalty holidays, 11 paid days of celebration and bridge

Field: Internal Medicine

Employment rate: 80-100%

KWL Kantonsspital Baselland — Locations Bruderholz, Liestal, Laufen. ~4'500 employees at 3 locations in the Basel region.
