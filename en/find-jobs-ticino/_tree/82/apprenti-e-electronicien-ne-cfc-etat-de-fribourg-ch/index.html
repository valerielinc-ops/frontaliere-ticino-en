Who are we?

The University of Engineering and Architecture Fribourg (HEIA-FR) is a member of the University of Applied Sciences of Western Switzerland (HES-SO) and offers bilingual training at the Bachelor and Master level.

Why join us?

The HES-SO//FR adopts a recruitment policy for equal opportunities. We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

The four-year training covers all fields of the electronics industry: production, circuit and measurement techniques, and microcontrollers. It takes place at the Electrical and Electronics Construction Service (SCEE) of the HEIA-FR electrical engineering industry, according to the dual model, with professional courses at CPNV in Yverdon-les-Bains and at EPAI in Fribourg. We ask apprentices to follow the integrated technical professional maturity program.

We offer a framework for vocational training, with the support of experienced trainers, specialists in the trade. You will thus develop knowledge and skills in line with the needs of the job market, while enjoying the ideal preparation to continue a higher education at the University of Engineering and Architecture in Fribourg after learning.

Additional information

  • Duration of apprenticeship: 4 years
  • Full application file: Letter of motivation, CV, and depending on your situation note bulletins (for students) or copies of diplomas and work certificates (for people already in operation)

Profile desired

  • Compulsory education completed (pregymnasial or general section), with very good results in mathematics, physics, chemistry and languages. The completion degree is required to start learning
  • Interest in electricity, electronics and information technology
  • Sense of organization, observation, rigour, perseverance and precision
  • Taste of experimentation, manual skill and ability to work in abstract
  • Mastery of French or German with good knowledge of the other language, knowledge of English is an asset
