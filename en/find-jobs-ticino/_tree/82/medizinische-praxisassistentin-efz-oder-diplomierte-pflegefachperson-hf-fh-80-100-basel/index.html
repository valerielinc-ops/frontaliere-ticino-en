Full description

Bethesda Spital AG | online since : 24.07.2026

As a successful Basel clinic with a private character, the Bethesda Spital combines top medicine with first-class service and a unique feel-good atmosphere. With its five focus areas: women’s medicine, pregnancy & childbirth, musculoskeletal system, rehabilitation and palliative care, our 6,000 inpatients benefit from state-of-the-art treatment centers under one roof – in the middle of one of the most beautiful hospital parks in northwestern Switzerland.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated personality who enjoys a varied job in a modern gynecological outpatient clinic in the hospital. As a member of our team, you help to provide women with acute and short-term gynecological complaints quickly, competently and empathetically. At the same time, you have the opportunity to actively participate in the development and development of a new ambulatory care offer.

Their tasks

  • Care of our patients from reception to conclusion of the consultation
  • Triage, organization and coordination of appointments – by telephone and via digital appointment booking systems
  • Assistance in gynecological examinations as well as invasive diagnostic examinations (assistance in the insertion of spirals, smaller biopsies, punctures of the mamma, wound rinses)
  • Perform blood sampling and urine analysis
  • Preparation of instruments and study rooms
  • Administrative activities and electronic documentation

They bring with them

  • Completed training as medical practice assistant EFZ or qualified nurse HF/FH
  • Professional experience in a gynecological practice, women’s clinic or emergency outpatient care is beneficial
  • Knowledge of SAP, as well as safe handling of the common MS Office applications
  • Enjoying a dynamic working environment with varied tasks
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • High patient orientation as well as team and communication skills
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Other language skills are from Vortei

We offer

  • The opportunity to actively participate in the expansion and further development of a new ambulatory care offer.
  • A modern working environment with the aim of continuously expanding digital processes
  • A varied and responsible field of activity
  • A motivated, experienced gynecological team with short decision paths
  • Attractive working hours in the context of the operation of the gynecological ambulatory
  • Opportunities for professional and personal development Here you come to the employment conditions at Bethesda
