Put to the Point

In the ICT Warrior Academy, you play a central role in bringing people, education and organization together effectively. You ensure that challenging courses and further education are not only professionally planned and implemented, but also continuously developed and adapted to the needs of the ICT and cyber world. You work with different people. From course leaders and lecturers to participants and executives and other stakeholders. With your organizational talent, your proactive actions and your sense of people, you create the conditions for training to succeed and for learners to develop professionally and personally. You will actively shape the next generation of cyber professionals and make an important contribution to ensuring that the ICT Warrior Academy continues to provide modern, practical and effective training in the future. Do you want to take responsibility, bring people together and actively shape the future of cyber education? Then we look forward to you and to developing the ICT Warrior Academy together with you!

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20738
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Responsible for planning and organizing the courses and further education of the ICT Warrior Academy including material and organizational resources
  • Develop organizational, course and training concepts independently and shape their continuous development together with the head of ICT Warrior Academy
  • Prepare and implement planning workshops and convert the resulting results into concrete courses and training measures
  • Ensure course organization including learning management system, resource management as well as coordination and supervision of course leaders and participants
  • Planning, implementing and evaluating the selection process for course participants
  • Design and implement own training in the field of methodology, organization, working methodology and workshop facilitation as well as support the ICT Warrior team in agile working and innovation methods

This makes you unique

  • Degree as a specialist / in corporate organization EFA or university degree (uni/ETH/FH), ideally in business informatics, business administration or psychology
  • Have in-depth knowledge and experience in corporate organization, project management, structural and process theory as well as organizational optimization
  • Experience in workshop facilitation, in particular in the moderation of planning workshops and the development of course concepts
  • Show knowledge and experience in agile working and innovation methods and enjoy their practical application
  • Bring an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method as well as a strong organizational and communication skills
  • Good active knowledge of a second official language

Diversity, dynamism, perspectives – teaching professions of the Swiss Army The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
