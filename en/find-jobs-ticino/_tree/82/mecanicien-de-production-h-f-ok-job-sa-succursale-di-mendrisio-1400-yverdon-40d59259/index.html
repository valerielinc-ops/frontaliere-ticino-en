Production Mechanic (M/F) back to list To strengthen our teams, we are looking for a (M/F) production engineer. Job description

Your mission

  • Manufacture the machining of precision mechanical parts using conventional machines and CNC machining centres
  • Make adjustments, start-up and production monitoring of manufacturing equipment
  • Read and interpret technical plans, manufacturing ranges and production documents
  • Participate in simple programming and optimization of CNC machines in collaboration with technical teams
  • Ensure monitoring of productions and make necessary adjustments to ensure conformity of parts
  • Control the dimensions, tolerances and quality of components using the usual measuring means
  • Perform assembly, assembly or finishing operations according to production requirements
  • Participate in the first level maintenance of equipment and ensure the proper functioning of facilities
  • Apply quality, safety and continuous improvement procedures within the company

Profile

Profile desired

  • Production mechanic CFC or equivalent training
  • Multi-year experience in precision machining on conventional and/or CNC machines
  • Experience in high precision watchmaking, microtechnical or mechanical environments
  • Knowledge of FAO and optimization of manufacturing processes, an asset
  • Excellent mastery of technical plan reading and metrology
  • Aisance in setting, setting up and monitoring production at machining centres
  • Good knowledge of machining processes, cutting tools and mechanical manufacturing techniques.
  • Ability to control the quality of the parts produced and to intervene on the settings when necessary.
  • Technical sense developed and strong interest in modern industrial environments
  • Ability to work independently while working effectively with production teams
  • Driver's licence (cat. B) and vehicle, an asset
