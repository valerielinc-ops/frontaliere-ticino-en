Institute of Italian Studies (ISI) Position of assistant-doctor at the Institute of Italian Studies (ISI), Faculty of Communication, Culture and Society of the University of Italian Switzerland (USI) The University of Italian Switzerland (USI) is a young and agile reality in continuous development, launched towards new challenges in the sign of its three guiding values: quality, openness and responsibility. Active in different areas of study and research, USI offers its students a training as protagonists, immersive and interdisciplinary, and its researchers and collaborators a privileged area of autonomy and freedom of initiative. The Institute of Italian Studies (ISI) offers a training focused on the study of Italian literature and language, within a broader area of civilization defined through a communion of knowledge that not only refer to the sphere of the word, in its different declinations, but also to that of artistic language, music and sciences. The Institute promotes its PhD students both through the training activities organized within it and collaborations with the Institute of History and Theory of Art and Architecture (ISA-ARC- USI), both through the doctoral teaching offer of the Faculty of Communication, Culture and Society and with the support of participation in external (summer/winter schools). For the publication of the best research, the ISI offers its own editorial Necklace “Officina” (Florence, Olschki). The Institute of Italian Studies (ISI) puts in competition a position of Assistant-doctorate/a in Italian Literature and Civilization with any specializations (preferential but not binding) on the Renaissance period or Italian poetry of the twentieth century, also in relation to other literatures or artistic forms. The position of Assistant-doctoral Doctorate will be written to the doctoral program in Italian Language, Literature and Civilization, according to the Faculty of Communication, Culture and Society. It will carry out its research activity under the scientific supervision of Prof. Stefano Prandi (http://usi.to/hd). The PhD will contribute to the development of the Institute’s research agenda. He will have the task of drawing up his doctoral thesis and drawing up scientific articles related to his research. From a didactic point of view, she will collaborate with Bachelor and Master's education programs in Italian Language, Literature and Civilization (assistance to courses and exams). The PhD will also be invited to collaborate in the organization of cultural activities and the third ISI mission. page 2 / 3 The candidate/selected/or will work as an assistant-doctor at the Institute of Italian Studies and will have the opportunity to interact with an international network of collaborators and collaborators. For further information on the PhD in Italian Language, Literature and Civilization, please refer to https://www.isi.usi.ch/doctorate-language-literature-civilized-Italian. Profile Ideal applications should meet the following requirements:

  • Master / Master's degree or equivalent title in Italian Literature.
  • Italian L1 or excellent Italian knowledge (C2).
  • Advanced knowledge of a second language of scientific expression.
  • Preferential title is the publication in scientific journals of essays or articles on themes in line with the focus of the doctoral position.
  • The candidate must demonstrate autonomy, spirit of initiative and collaboration. Applications of persons already in possession of a PhD (PhD) will not be considered. Contractual Terms Admission to the PhD program is highly competitive. Admission decisions are based on the training path, interests, attitude and academic potential of the candidate or candidate. The enrollment in the doctoral program and the position as an assistant PhD student is not compatible with the performance of a further professional activity. The position is financed internally and the wage levels correspond to those established by the Swiss National Science Research Fund for PhD researchers. The workplace is the USI Università della Svizzera italiana, Lugano, Switzerland. Availability is required to travel to other regions of Switzerland and abroad for collaborative and research purposes. The scheduled start date is 1 September 2026.
