Shape your future with us!

Are you a professional insurance professional or from another horizon? Start your career in the external service at AXA, number 1. We are the trusted partner of our clients, in all situations of life. You too?

Do you live in the Nord-Vaudois region and have you ever been able to apply your sales, negotiation and networking skills? Are you a client-oriented and objective person? Are you serious about your work?

You have the opportunity to develop your skills and demonstrate your abilities! Following a strategic evolution, our principal agency in Grandson wants to complete its team and, if you recognize yourself in this profile, we would be delighted to receive your file!

What's waiting for you

  • Directed and personalized advice of insurance solutions to clients you meet at home, at the agency or by phone
  • Tracking and professional development of your own client portfolio
  • Acquisition of new customers to ensure the growth of your region's customer portfolio
  • Placement of other AXA products (finance and provision, health services)
  • Performing administrative tasks related to your position, with the support of the entire AXA structure and modern monitoring tools

What you bring

  • To benefit from a professional degree and a first experience in relation to advice or sale you have a keen interest in training in the field of insurance
  • You can justify a very good knowledge of the local economic fabric and a vast network of this region because you live there
  • Your personality is service oriented and you have ease in contacts with others, you seek solutions for your clients and you know how to adapt to any situation
  • You speak French both orally and in writing, any other language is an advantage
  • Your work method is self-sustaining and objective-oriented, remuneration based on your own performance is an additional source of motivation
  • You have ease with common computer systems as well as learning new digital tools

What we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you the opportunity to fully develop and exploit your potential. As a Swiss insurance leader, we offer ambitious people interesting career opportunities with great freedom of action.

  • Up to 30% discount on products from AXA
  • Five weeks of vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-tariff subscription/participation in general subscription costs
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Freedom of action and self-employment
  • Innovative structures, processes and tools
  • Strong and professional training and development opportunities
  • Professional foresight worthy of the best companies in Switzerland
