Full description

This is what awaits you

Are you a committed and service-oriented personality who enjoys supporting our brokers and insurance and pension advisors? Then you've come to the right place.

This is what you do for us

In this role, you are the central interface between our brokers, insurance and pension advisors and our customers.

You will assist our brokers and insurance and pension advisors as a direct and competent contact person. Your diverse area of ​​responsibility includes preparing offers, processing tenders and applications, and coordinating with our specialist departments.

In addition, you will be in direct contact with our customers, answer their inquiries and ensure professional and service-oriented support.

This is what you bring with you

• Completed commercial vocational training, preferably in the insurance industry • High motivation to learn new things in a varied field of activity • Independent and proactive way of working • Responsible, empathetic and open personality • Fluent spoken and written German and knowledge of other languages ​​are a plus

Work with us

At the Aesch general agency you will be welcomed by a motivated and dynamic team. Together you will experience a committed, service-oriented and collegial working atmosphere.

Your workload is negotiable from 50% and your place of work is Aesch. The position can be started immediately or by arrangement.

In order to exercise this function, the Insurance Supervision Act requires the qualification of insurance intermediary VBV, non-life profile. If this is not available, we will be happy to support you with tailor-made internal training.

Profit sharing

If we are successful, our employees also benefit. Every year a decision is made about the distribution of a profit share and its amount.

Further training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We participate in job-relevant further training.

Perks
