Full description

The Rote Kreuz Baselland (SRK BL) has a wide range of services for people in need of support. Around 120 employees and more than 500 volunteers help in emergency situations, are involved in the training, health promotion and integration of the population in Baselbiet. Posizione Head of Department Home Support For our department support at home we are looking at 01. September 2026 or by agreement a new manager. Mansioni

  • You ask for the proper and smooth handling of the offers of your department: «Rotkreuz-Notruf», «Pflege and Care» and «Family Relief»
  • You take over the technical, organizational and personnel management of the team "Support at home" in the office (6 persons) and in the field of external services (60 persons)
  • You keep in mind the administrative processes and numbers of your department and provide all necessary information to the management
  • You will keep you on the current market environment, recognize important developments and guide the strategic development of the department and, as a member of the management, the entire SRK BL
  • You are well connected, behave and join new contacts and represent your department as well as the SRK BL on occasions and in specialist groups, where you actively introduce our offers
  • Requisiti
  • You have a degree as a nurse at tertiary level and Spitex experience
  • You have completed a training in management and experience in personnel management
  • You live a leadership culture that strengthens your employees, always have an open ear for them and see you as a backbone of your department
  • You have acquired profound knowledge of life situations of people of age or with a disease and families and have the necessary empathy in dealing with these groups of people
  • The offers for vulnerable people in the health field of the canton of Basel-Landschaft are familiar to you
  • You have the pleasure to deal with various emergency call systems
  • Cosa offriamo
  • We cultivate an open corporate culture characterized by respect and appreciation
  • You are part of the four-headed Management Board, which, together with the Managing Director, forms and develops the SRK BL on the basis of its principles
  • There are currently various pioneering challenges in your department that need your input
  • A motivated, committed team awaits you in your department
  • The place of work is Liestal. The team appreciates your presence on site, but of course we also offer flexible work opportunities Contatto Are you willing to make a real difference and become part of the largest humanitarian organization in Switzerland?
