The Position Lead IT Expert - RDT Data Platforms

Roche Digital Technology (RDT) is where innovation meets purpose. As a global team at the heart of Roche, we are a community of business-minded technologists committed to help shape tomorrow’s digital future of healthcare. Our mission is to power Roche through cutting-edge digital technologies, harnessing the potential of artificial intelligence, data, and scalable tech innovations. Driven by purpose and passion, we’re building a future where digital is a core strength across all of Roche, enabling smarter ways of working, unlocking human potential, and driving breakthroughs that truly matter for millions of patients around the world.

The Lead IT Expert (Tech Lead) for DataOps will drive the technical direction of our custom platform engineering solutions. You will not be building standard data pipelines; instead, you will architect, maintain, and evolve DataOps.Roche—the critical CI/CD ecosystem, infrastructure, and deployment tooling that enables hundreds of data engineers to process data safely across our global organization. This role requires deep expertise in Python, Infrastructure as Code (Terraform), and modern CI/CD ecosystems to lead massive architectural shifts, including migrating to an orchestrator-agnostic framework and transitioning from GitLab to GitHub. You will ensure the resilience of our platform while seamlessly integrating enterprise tools like Snowflake, Collibra, Monte Carlo, and HashiCorp Vault.

We are a platform team within RDT Data Platforms at Roche, operating under a decentralized Data Mesh architecture. Our core mission is to act as the foundational platform team, delivering the custom DataOps solution to various data product teams across the enterprise. We treat our internal tools, CI/CD pipelines, and infrastructure as a product, empowering these decentralized teams to build, deploy, and scale their data products efficiently and securely within a highly regulated environment. We are currently expanding our mission to serve additional platforms beyond the initial scope, making this a pivotal time for the team.

The Opportunity

  • Scope : Lead the technical roadmap for the DataOps.Roche platform. Drive the critical refactoring of our core solution from native GitLab CI structures into an orchestrator-agnostic Python framework. Establish a centralized data modeling service for the broader ecosystem.
  • Accountability/Problem Solving: Manage and maintain our complex Terraform-based infrastructure, which provisions GitLab/GitHub groups, projects, HashiCorp Vault engines, and dedicated runners. Ensure regulated releases are seamless by maintaining our automated validation processes and custom autovalidate package.
  • Stakeholder Management: Act as a product owner for the platform. Liaise with decentralized data product teams to understand developer experience (DevEx) bottlenecks. Guide senior leadership on platform strategy, tooling evaluation, and infrastructure investments.
  • Impact/Strategy: Lead high-impact strategic initiatives, including the complex migration from GitLab to GitHub and the evaluation/implementation of Dagster as our next-generation data orchestrator. Identify and integrate applied AI into the DataOps lifecycle to boost developer efficiency.
  • Complexity / (Product Size): Manage an enterprise-grade platform integrating multiple major technologies: Snowflake (data platform), Collibra (data catalog), Monte Carlo (observability), HashiCorp Vault (secrets management), and Talend
