Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 80-100% On Station A2, patients with all psychiatric diagnoses are treated. The therapeutic offer includes differentiated clarification and treatment related to the respective patients. One focus of the station is in the area of psychotherapeutic treatment of trauma, another is the treatment of patients with a disorder from the affective spectrum. For our Center for Depression and Anxiety, with a focus on Trauma and Affective Disorder, we search immediately or by agreement. Their tasks

  • Implementation of the care process within the framework of the reference person system
  • Cooperation within the framework of a multimodal, multiprofessional psychiatric treatment
  • Co-responsibility in the recovery-oriented design of the therapeutic process
  • Modular application of elements of DBT, skills training and cognitive behavioral therapy
  • Support patients in their daily structure and in leisure activities
  • Support for vocational training in the training of learners and students on the station

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training in the field of care HF/FH, (certificate of recognition of the Swiss Red Cross for foreign diplomas)
  • High level of technical, social and self-competence as well as high resilience and flexibility
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • High motivation to engage in a demanding working environment and take on responsibility
  • Interest in the psychotherapeutic, psychiatric specialty
  • Readiness for night and weekend services

Our offer

  • Knowledge that advances: Extensive initial, further and further training offers
  • Focus on health: Various sports offers, green oases and an in-house care team
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • Combining family and work: access to the company’s own crèche
  • Well taken care of in everyday work: discounted food offers or lunch checks
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
