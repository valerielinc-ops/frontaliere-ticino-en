Reference code: JR134717 The story of Cartier is founded on curiosity and passion. For more than 170 years we have embraced a bold, pioneering spirit that continues to inspire our teams across all Professions from our boutiques to our workshops and corporate offices. Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striking to continuously rich our Maison

CONTEXT

Within the Department of Human Resources, we are looking for our two future Interns Maturity Professional Commercial (3+1) to join our teams. Depending on your profile and your aspirations, you will evolve within the HR administration or HR learning division, while discovering the entire life cycle of the employee and interacting with all our staff and external partners.

HOW DO YOU HAVE AN IMPACT?

Depending on your orientation (HR Administration or Learning), you will be required to contribute to one of the following missions:

VOLET HR ADMINISTRATION – La Chaux-de-Fonds

Participation in the administrative recruitment process (preboarding, reception and integration of new employees)

Permanence at HR reception to meet the daily demands of our employees

Monitoring, updating and digitizing employees' electronic records

Management of greeting cards and gifts related to life events (marriage, birth, birthday, etc.)

Processing and follow-up of spontaneous applications

Organization of internal events (ex: Future in all kinds)

One-time collaboration with our interim agencies (temporary contracts and hours monitoring)

General administrative support and HR correspondence writing

VOLET LEARNING - Institut Horlogerie Cartier (IHC) – La Chaux-de-Fonds/Couvet :

Participation in the administrative management of apprentices

Organization of internships discovered or recruited

Management and organisation of learning-related events

Continuous improvement of processes and documents

Participation in various project developments

WHAT KEYS ARE YOUR SUCCESS IN OUR HOUSE?

As part of your business training with Maturity Commercial Professional (MP 3+1), you are looking for a practical internship lasting 12 months.

You have perfect command of French, both oral and written, and are particularly comfortable with the Microsoft Office suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook).

With very good relational skills and an appreciation of human contact, you have absolute confidentiality and discretion.

Your rigor, your sense of organization and your versatility make you an effective person. Finally, you want to enthusiastically invest in teamwork in a dynamic and demanding environment.

WHAT HAVE WE OFFERED TO YOU?

We create an innovative and positive environment where we are proud to employ talents from different backgrounds, experiences and identities. We are convinced that when diversity and inclusion are fully accepted and strengthened, creativity and knowledge emerge to achieve excellence.

YOUR CANDIDATE COURSE

We look forward to receiving your complete application (Letter of motivation, CV, bulletins of notes) on our career site. Please specify in your cover letter whether you have a preference for the Administration or Training component.

If your application meets the criteria of this internship and what we are looking for, you will have a first telephone contact as soon as possible with our HR team and then you will access an individual interview with our HR.

Entry into office : August 2027 (or to be agreed)

Duration of internship: 12 months
