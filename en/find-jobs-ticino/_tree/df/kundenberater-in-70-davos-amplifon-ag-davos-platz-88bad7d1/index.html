Full description

Amplifon is a quality provider and world-leading specialist in hearing solutions with 70 years of experience. Our passion is to create positive customer experiences and improve the quality of life of our customers with listening services. In Switzerland we are represented with more than 100 specialty shops and we offer local services almost everywhere.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a 70% customer advisor for our location in Davos by appointment or immediately.

Your tasks

  • Sale and advice of our services and accessories
  • Assistance of the hearing aid acoustician
  • All administrative work in the specialist business
  • Implementation of sales promotion measures in specialist business
  • Performing service work on hearing aids
  • Managing the Specialty Business Agenda
