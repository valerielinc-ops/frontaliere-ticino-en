Management Bern | Pensum: 100%

Are you open-minded, ambitious and value a future-oriented way of working on the pulse of the real estate industry? At Livit, you are committed to the concerns of owners and tenants with an exemplary customer orientation and thus contribute significantly to a trusting, long-lasting customer relationship and a positive corporate image. Are you ready to tackle the challenges of today and tomorrow and make a difference in the real estate world? Then you are exactly right with Livit. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Your tasks

  • You are fully responsible for managing a portfolio of residential properties
  • You develop and control the annual budget incl. Forecasts and controls and contracts creditors
  • You lead and train your subordinate employees (including goal agreement and control of goal achievement)
  • You look after and advise tenants and owners and control the facility managers
  • You are responsible for securing, executing and accompaniment of maintenance as well as for periodic condition assessments including reporting
  • You make lease contract changes, rental rate changes, and indexed rental rate adjustments
  • You monitor the current rental policy and advise homeowners on how to set the rental rate

Your profile

  • You have an oath. Real estate management or adequate further education in the real estate sector
  • You have several years of professional experience in the real estate industry, experience in rental law and basic building knowledge
  • Your mother tongue is German and you can ideally communicate verbally and in writing in other languages
  • You have a high level of social and self-competence with the ability to self-control
  • You live the values of customer orientation, communicate professionally and enjoy dealing with people
  • You bring an affinity for technology, can apply new technologies and are open to new things
  • You have self-learning skills and you enjoy sharing your knowledge with others.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
