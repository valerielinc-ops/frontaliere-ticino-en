Cook / Chef EFZ, 80-100 %

Location: Riehen

From 01.12.2026 or by agreement

Founded in 1900, the Klinik Sonnenhalde in Riehen is now an established private clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland and southern Baden. We pursue a holistic, human-centered, milieutherapeutic psychiatry that combines excellent professional quality with a Christian value base. The clinic has 68 stationary beds, 4 day clinics and 4 ambulatories. As one of the first clinics in Switzerland, we are actively promoting digital therapy offerings, such as blended therapy and virtual reality and opened the first hybrid day clinic in Switzerland in February 2023.

To strengthen the kitchen team we are looking for 01. December 2026 a committed and reliable personality.

Your responsibilities

  • Ensure proper food production in compliance with quality, manufacturing and hygiene standards
  • Independent management of the assigned kitchen post including day-to-day business, mise en place and participation in weekly menu planning
  • Responsibility for the procurement of goods, checks on receipt of goods and correct storage and checks on minimum durability data
  • Professional use and care of kitchen appliances and machines as well as implementation and documentation of cleaning processes

Your profile

  • Completed apprenticeship as chef/chef EFZ
  • High level of teamwork and motivation to work together
  • Loyalty to the company and superiors
  • Flexibility, resilience, reliability and good knowledge of German
