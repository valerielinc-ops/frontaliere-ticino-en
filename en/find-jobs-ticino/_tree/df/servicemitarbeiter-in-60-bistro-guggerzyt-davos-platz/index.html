Service staff:in (60-80%) As a result of retirement, we are looking for reinforcements for Zentrum Guggerbach as a service employee on 1 November or by agreement. The centre offers people an integrated range of services and helps them to maintain their individual lifestyle. Guggerbach has 82 single rooms and 5 double rooms in the residential group as well as 50 supervised apartments. A restaurant, a cafeteria and the bistro “Guggerzyt” are among many other offers. There are about 160 residents in the centre. More than 120 employees are working for their benefit. As part of a structural extension, currently attractive 3.5-room apartments with additional living space are being built. Their tasks

  • In our restaurant and in the Bistro Guggerzyt You have all the service work
  • They look after our residents courteously and serve the food / drinks professionally
  • For larger occasions and banquets you help with
  • They prepare the service for the ordered meals of our residents and offer support and care

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed training as a service professional of advantage
  • Experience in Service
  • Guest-friendly, customer-oriented thinking
  • Flexible, open and warm personality
  • Good manners and a high understanding for elderly people
  • fluent knowledge of German is an advantage

We offer them

  • Versatile, responsible and varied activity
  • Motivated and collegial team
  • Current employment conditions
  • Discounted food in the in-house gastronomy
  • Own fitness room
