Employees: in cage preparation and cleaning (m/f/d)

80%-100%, Zurich, permanent

Working where research shapes the future: At the ETH Phenomics Center (EPIC) at ETH Zurich, we make an important contribution to modern biomedical research. We specialize in the husbandry and care of laboratory animals – always under the highest quality, hygiene and animal welfare standards.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated employee who is enthusiastic about this specialized and responsible task.

Job description

In addition to the acceptance, cleaning and disinfection of the goods to be sterilized, her responsibilities include logistics and communication with researchers and animal keepers within the SPF barrier.

Profile

  • Open-minded, resilient and communicative personality who enjoys working in a team in a lively, international university environment
  • High willingness to take responsibility, resilience and reliability
  • Good knowledge of German and English for capturing, understanding, following and implementing hazard statements and operating instructions
  • Readiness to operate large electronic devices reliably and appropriately
  • Good physical constitution since it is physical, mainly standing and walking activities
  • Working under personal protective clothing such as laboratory clothing, mouth mask, vinyl gloves
  • User knowledge of common office programs (Outlook, Word)
  • Independence and flexibility round off your profile

We offer

  • Interesting and varied work at one of the most renowned universities in the world
  • A comprehensive and structured familiarization by experienced and qualified colleagues:
  • An appreciative, collaborative working environment and participation in a committed, open-minded and multicultural team
  • Attractive employment conditions and social benefits according to the guidelines of ETH Zurich
  • Attractive pension services through the PUBLICA pension scheme
