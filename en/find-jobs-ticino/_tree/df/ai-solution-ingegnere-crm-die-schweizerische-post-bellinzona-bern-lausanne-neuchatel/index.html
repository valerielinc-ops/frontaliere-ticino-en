Full description

Do you want to develop AI solutions that really make a difference in everyday life? With you, we bring modern generative AI technologies along the entire customer journey from idea to productive operation. You develop AI agents, integrate large language models into the CRM environment around support, sales and marketing and connect their interfaces with internal data and business applications. You work in an agile DevOps team that is jointly responsible for its solutions.

You can do that.

  • You develop AI agents, assistants and automated solutions and integrate them into CRM-related processes, internal applications and customer-centric workflows.
  • You connect large language models with APIs, data sources and tools and work with tool calling, structured outputs, context management and MCP servers.
  • Develop automated evaluations and regression tests, compare models and versions and assess quality, reliability, latency and cost.
  • The entire lifecycle of AI solutions is accompanied by analysis of the effects of model and API upgrades and optimization of prompts, context, model selection and application logic.
  • You develop prototypes quickly, translate technical requirements into feasible technical solutions and recognize when AI, deterministic logic or a combination of both makes sense.
  • You work in an agile DevOps team, run the solutions together with your colleagues and support the team through pairing, code reviews and technical discussions.
  • As a link between business and computer science, you are on the road to consulting in projects regarding the use of AI and ML topics and develop innovative and digital instruments with the help of modern technologies.

You bring that with you

  • You have a university degree in computer science or a comparable technical education and bring experience in the implementation of AI integration projects throughout the entire life cycle.
  • In-depth practical experience with LLM-based applications sets you apart, and you have a good understanding of modern AI agent concepts such as tool calling, context management, evaluations, fallback mechanisms and human-in-the-loop processes.
  • Experience in the integration of AI solutions into CRM or customer-related processes, for example in customer service, the processing of customer concerns or in chat solutions, rounds off your profile.
  • With your know-how in the systematic evaluation of LLM solutions, you develop evaluation datasets, automated tests and robust release criteria for model, prompt and application changes.
  • Thanks to your knowledge of prompt and context engineering, you can optimize AI solutions in terms of quality, token consumption, latency, costs and model dependencies.
  • You are familiar with basic RAG concepts such as embeddings, chunking, vector search, metadata filtering and source information
  • An understanding of data semantics, ontologies or knowledge graphs is an advantage. You are familiar with Git, REST APIs, SQL, containers, existing Kubernetes deployment templates and basic CI/CD operations.
  • Good German and English skills round off your profile. Knowledge of French is an advantage.
