1-year temporary mission: training specialist

Do you have experience in training management? Are you available quickly? This job is for you!

For our client, a company active in the industrial field in Sion, we are looking for a specialist training for a temporary mission of one year and a start-up in January 2027\.

Your duties with your new employer

  • You lead the implementation of the training concept and translate the needs into concrete programmes
  • You organize the trainings from A to Z, from reservations to convocations, in accordance with the budget
  • You develop synergies with partners and value the potential of internal trainers
  • You are key\-user of training management tools and accompany users daily
  • You evaluate the quality of training, from satisfaction to ROI, and propose targeted adjustments

You offer to your new employer

  • You have training in human resources, pedagogy or project management
  • You have experience in training projects and an FSEA trainer certificate is an asset
  • You are native French and your knowledge of German is an asset
  • You master MS Office and ideally tools like SAP SuccessFactors, Cornerstone or Moodle
  • You are autonomous, organized and curious, with a good spirit of analysis and a taste for teamwork
