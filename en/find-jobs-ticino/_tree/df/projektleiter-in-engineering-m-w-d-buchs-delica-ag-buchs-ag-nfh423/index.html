Full description

Do you want to drive technical innovations and actively shape the future of our production facilities? With your know-how, you create real changes and contribute to developing sustainable solutions. Together as a team, we focus on pioneering spirit, celebrate successes and create moments of enjoyment – become part of our success story!

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG Buchs, Bresteneggstrasse 4, 5033 Buchs AG

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You plan and realize challenging investment projects from budget creation to handover to operations and ensure timely implementation.
  • You will support operational operations and optimize production facilities through evaluation processes, efficiency improvements and innovative solutions.
  • You ensure technical operational readiness through larger conversion and revision projects and support the maintenance staff and line managers in repairs and smaller projects.
  • You drive automation and growth projects and take our production processes to the next level.
  • You ensure that internal regulations and quality standards are met and ensure sustainable improvements.

Requirements

  • 4-5 years: in the management of technical projects or in the field of engineering (ideally in the food environment).
  • Completed studies (Uni/ETH, FH, HF): in a technical area, supplemented by plant-specific further education.
  • Communication strength and the ability to work on several projects in parallel.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and an efficient, precise way of working with a strong goal orientation.
  • You bring a pragmatic approach that speeds up decisions and automates routines efficiently.
  • You are data-savvy and system-friendly (Office/BI, SAP & Co.), quickly acquire new tools and use AI responsibly.
  • In-depth knowledge of processes and processes, also across departments.
  • in German (negotiation confident)

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary.   • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing.
