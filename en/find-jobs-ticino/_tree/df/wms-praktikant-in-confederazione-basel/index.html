Put to the Point

In the HR Center in Basel you have the opportunity to complete a one-year internship, which leads to the Swiss Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ) Kauffrau/-mann according to the model 3+1 and to vocational maturity. In this field of activity, we offer a varied internship year in a small team and an in-depth insight into HR tasks at the Federal Office for Customs and Border Security (BAZG).

Security for the population, economy and state

The Federal Office for Customs and Border Security ensures comprehensive security at the border – for the benefit of the population, economy and state. It is well connected nationally and internationally. Its core tasks include the proper and rapid clearance of commercial goods, the speedy handling of travel, the areas of security police and migration, and it has its own investigative authority with law enforcement. The BAZG is in the midst of digital transformation. The DaziT program simplifies, optimizes and digitizes all customs, duties and control processes.
