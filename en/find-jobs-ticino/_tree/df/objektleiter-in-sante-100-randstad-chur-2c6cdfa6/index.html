Full description

For a renowned and Switzerland-wide leading service company in the field of modern facility management, we are looking for an experienced and service-oriented personality.

Object ladder in 100%

Your activity

  • You assume operational, qualitative, financial and human responsibility for the support services for several customer mandates in the healthcare sector.
  • You will continuously lead, promote and develop your team of around 55 employees.
  • You maintain a constant, close customer contact and act as a competent contact for technical questions.
  • You are responsible for all support services, ensure a high-quality service and guarantee strict compliance with all hygiene guidelines.
  • You regularly carry out quality controls, actively coach your employees and support them in day-to-day business.
  • You work closely with other disciplines and interfaces.
  • You plan, organize, coordinate, monitor and optimize all workflows in your objects.
  • You independently recruit new workers for your teams.
  • You manage and coordinate all material, machine and device orders efficiently.
  • You perform all administrative tasks reliably and take full budget responsibility.

Qualifications

  • You bring a completed education or training in the field of housekeeping, cleaning or facility management as well as several years of profound professional experience.
  • You have already gained valuable professional experience in healthcare and/or the upscale hotel industry.
  • You have proven, several years of leadership experience and are characterized by a pronounced hands-on mentality.
  • You are a human, communicative and solution-oriented personality and have a strong sense of organization and coordination.
  • You convince through your structured, solution-oriented and exact working method.
  • You are characterized by a high service orientation, flexibility and independence.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing (other languages are of great advantage).
  • You are absolutely well-versed in dealing with the common MS Office applications.
  • You must have a category B driving license and your own car.
  • You bring the necessary flexibility for 24/7 accessibility and occasionally accompany your cleaning staff during evening assignments.
