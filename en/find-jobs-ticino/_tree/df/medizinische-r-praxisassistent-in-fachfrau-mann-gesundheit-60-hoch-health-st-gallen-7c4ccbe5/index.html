Medical / Practical Assistant / Specialist Health 60%

Eastern Swiss vascular centre

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Execution of oscillographies of the extremities and ultrasonic Doppler shutter pressure measurement as well as assistance in interventions (endoluminal laser therapy, sclerosing of the leg veins)
  • Cleaning and professional care of wounds, with the help of existing dressing and wound care materials
  • Preparation of patients for medical examination and participation in ensuring patient safety, quality and safety at work
  • Demand- and situation-appropriate assessment of patient situations and implementation of measures within the framework of the capacity to act
  • Ensuring patient-related information flow

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as medical/practical assistant/in EFZ (MPA) or as a specialist health EFZ
  • A team-capable, flexible and resilient personality with pleasure in dealing with patients
  • Mastering medical terminology and style security in German correspondence
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office and safe handling of electronic work instruments
  • Organizational talent with an eye for the whole – you think in a networked and cross-divisional way

Our offer to you

  • Regulated working hours from Monday to Friday - no weekend and night service
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
  • Varied challenges in a dynamic working environment
  • Close cooperation in a motivated and interdisciplinary team
  • Diverse employee benefits around health, work and family – including attractive perks and more
