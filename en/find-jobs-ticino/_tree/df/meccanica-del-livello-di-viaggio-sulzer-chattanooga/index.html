Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. About the role Job Summary The Assembly Mechanic will be responsible for backfiling and balancing impellers, installing pump components, performing hydrostatic tests, installing pumps into test pit, and assembling full pumps for shipments. Other duties may be required as assigned by the Supervisor. ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS:

  • Using common hand, air, and power tools, assemble horizontal and vertical pumps per Engineering Instructions, Drawings, and Work Orders.
  • Disassemble and inspect pumps for repairs.
  • Perform hot work for installation of subcomponents.
  • Coordinate work with other Assembly Mechanics, and shop personal to assists them in accomplishing tasks.
  • Resolves issues with work instructions to shop, includes interfacing with engineering, purchasing, quality, planning, and project coordinators.
  • Perform grinding operations and balancing on pump impellers.
  • Regular and reliable attendance.
  • Supports, cooperates with, and participates in company-wide LEAN and EHS Management System which includes following all safety, health and environmental policies and procedures. Supports and cooperates in company-wide Quality Management effort.
  • Adheres to company and department policies, procedures, and standards
  • Performs other duties as assigned QUALIFICATIONS:
  • High School diploma or equivalent
  • Recommended 5 years experience in related environment, preferably working with pumps
