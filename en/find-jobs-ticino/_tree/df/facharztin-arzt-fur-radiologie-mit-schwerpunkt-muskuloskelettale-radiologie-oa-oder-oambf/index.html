Full description

In order to strengthen our team at the hospital locations Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, we are looking for an

Radiology specialist with a focus on musculoskeletal radiology (OA or OAmbF)

80-100%

They take over

  • Independent planning, implementation and diagnosis of radiological examinations with focus on MSC examinations
  • Member of the musculoskeletal expert network of the Radiologie Plus team
  • Representation of the musculoskeletal focus competence towards clinicians and referees
  • Instruction and training of assistant doctors
  • Participation in interdisciplinary meetings and reports
  • Participation in the hospital service system

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist in radiology
  • Musculoskeletal diploma of a recognised European or national professional society (e.g. ESSR or DGMSR)

The Radiology Plus team introduces itself to you

The Radiology Plus team is an association of several established Radiological institutes in Eastern Switzerland, including the radiological departments of Thurgau’s cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen under one roof. The Radiologie Plus team also provides teleradiological care for several clinics and medical facilities in the cantons of Thurgau, St. Gallen and Graubünden. Our portfolio includes the complete range of imaging diagnostics, interventional radiology, nuclear medicine and radiation therapy.
