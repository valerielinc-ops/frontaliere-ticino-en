Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Handling of entry and exit of male prisoners of investigation and conviction
  • Supervision and care of prisoners
  • Implementation of instructions and security rules
  • General cleaning work and control and implementation of internal hygiene rules
  • Completion of various administrative and operational tasks

What you bring

  • Successfully completed 3- to 4-year vocational apprenticeship EFZ and several years of professional experience
  • Willingness to perform irregular working hours in 24-hour operation (including night services and weekend assignments)
  • High load capacity, balance and teamwork
  • Understanding and tolerance in dealing with people from different backgrounds
  • Minimum age 30 years

Your working environment

The Office for Prisoners brings together institutions and services that deal with the execution of penalties and measures and thus contribute to social security.

This also includes the District Prisons section with prisons at the four locations Aarau, Baden, Kulm and Zofingen. Employees shall ensure the execution of pre-trial detention, short prison sentences, early commencement of sentences and half-detention. The rule-compliant and safe accommodation and care of prisoners is in the foreground.
