Automatician (m/f/d)

Their tasks

  • Carry out electrical installation, maintenance and repair work on all production lines in coordination with the supervisor
  • Independent diagnosis and correction of electrical disturbances and technical problems
  • Ensuring safe, reliable and efficient operation of production facilities
  • Participation in the Pikettdienst to resolve urgent technical problems outside regular working hours
  • Documentation of work carried out and detected disturbances

Your profile

  • Completed training as an automatic technician/EFZ or related profession with corresponding training
  • Experience in programming with Simatic Manager and TIA portal
  • Knowledge of CNC machines is an advantage
  • Team-capable, flexible and responsible personality
  • Electrical authorization according to NIV 15 or NIV 13 desirable
  • Readiness for piquetting and valid passenger car ticket

No applications from recruitment agencies shall be considered for this call.
