Full description

For a dynamic trust company, we are looking for a committed, independent and service-oriented personality as a clerk: in Treuhand. In this role, you look after customers from a wide range of industries and assume overall responsibility for smaller mandates within a motivated team with short decision paths. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Finance: management of financial accounts, accounts receivable and accounts payable, preparation of VAT accounts and assistance in interim and annual accounts.
  • Payroll: self-employed management of wage accounting including the entire social security system (AHV, withholding tax, etc.).
  • Tax & Support: Support for tax returns for natural and legal persons as well as general trust and administration work.
  • Contact: Direct, professional exchange with customers, authorities and insurance companies.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training: basic commercial training, ideally supplemented by further training as a clerk: in trust / finance and accounting (or currently in training).
  • Experience: Several years of in-depth professional experience in escrow as well as solid knowledge in finance, accounting and wage administration.
  • IT & Language: Secure handling of MS Office and common accounting programs as well as impeccable knowledge of German in word and writing.
  • Working method: An exact, reliable and independent working method combined with real pleasure in customer contact and teamwork.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Culture: A collegial, appreciative working environment with flat hierarchies, short decision-making paths and modern digital work processes.
  • Flexibility: Timely employment conditions with flexible working hours and the possibility for home office by arrangement.
  • Development: A varied activity with self-responsible work and active support in your external further education.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
