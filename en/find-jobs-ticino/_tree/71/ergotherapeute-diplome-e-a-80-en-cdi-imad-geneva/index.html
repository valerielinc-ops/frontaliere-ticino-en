Full description

Occupational therapy aims to maintain, acquire or recover optimal autonomy in daily, professional and leisure life, with the greatest possible safety. It acts according to the potential and constraints of the person's environment. Based in temporary reception units or in home support units located in the canton, the occupational therapist provides services independently, respecting the customer's lifestyle, beliefs, values and culture.

He/she works in interdisciplinarity and interprofessionality, respecting the mission and objectives of the institution. Your missions and responsibilities

  • To provide, in collaboration and synergy with the multidisciplinary team, on medical prescription, occupational therapy benefits to people limited in their ability to act by illness, accident or age-related problems.
  • Promote the development and/or maintenance of independence, patient autonomy in the activities of daily life, through rehabilitation and rehabilitation measures
  • Coordinate the group and community activities of the unit.
  • Occupational therapist degree
  • Recognition of the Red Cross
  • Right of practice (for the Canton of Genéve) or steps already under way
  • Diverse experience of at least two years in hospital, EMS or home
  • Experience in animation of therapeutic groups
  • Ability to work in a multidisciplinary and autonomous manner
  • Good knowledge of the health-social fabric and the Geneva-based care and support network will be an asset.
  • Have a driver's license.
  • Deadline for application: 15.11.2026
  • Entry into office: to be agreed
  • Please attach to your file:
  • an updated curriculum vitae,
  • a cover letter,
  • a copy of your diplomas and work certificates and, if applicable, a copy of your recognition of the Red Cross Foreign Diploma
  • A copy of your right of practice for the Canton of Geneva.

We only consider full applications We guarantee complete confidentiality in the processing of your file.
