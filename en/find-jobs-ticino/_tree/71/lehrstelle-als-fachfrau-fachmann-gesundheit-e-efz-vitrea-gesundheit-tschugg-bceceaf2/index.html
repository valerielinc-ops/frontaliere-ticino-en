Rittmeyer's control systems, control devices and measurement technology form the basis for innovative solutions in the fields of water and energy supply, hydroelectric power plants and wastewater treatment plants worldwide. Rittmeyer is an engineering and services company specializing in the integration of software and systems for turnkey plants, guaranteeing the highest quality standards. For our internal sales team, consisting of seven people, we are looking for a motivated person who combines a proactive approach with precision in their work and competently performs their role. In this position, you act as the interface between our sales department and our operations department, which is responsible for executing our projects. You can expect a close-knit team and leadership based on open, equal dialogue.
