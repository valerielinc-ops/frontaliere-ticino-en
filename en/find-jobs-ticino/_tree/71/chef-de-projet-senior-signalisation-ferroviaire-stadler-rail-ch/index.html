Full description

EXPERIENCE

  • - Diploma of engineer HES, EPF or university in a technical field (mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, mechatronics, industrial engineer or equivalent) supplemented by experience in project management
  • - In-depth experience in project management in a complex or specific technical field in the rail sector:
  • Proven success in carrying out complex and multidisciplinary projects, with responsibility for scope, timing, budget, quality and customer satisfaction
  • Project experience in on-board systems (notably ETCS, CBTC) and more generally in the field of railway signalling is an obvious asset
  • - Good knowledge of relevant standards and regulations in the rail sector (CENELEC, Dir. IS, OCF/DE-OCF, TEN among others)
  • - Strong leadership, stakeholder management, communication and negotiation skills
  • - Proven ability to work as a team and to promote knowledge sharing and best practices
  • - A proactive, strategic, structured and results-oriented mindset with a strong interest in continuous improvement and development of organizational processes
  • - Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Office, including Microsoft Project
  • - Excellent mastery of French and ability to evolve effectively in a multilingual project environment in English and/or German
  • - Travel to other sites (mainly Switzerland 10-25%)

LIABILITY

  • - Technical and commercial management of complex integration projects and programmes for embedded systems (ETCS type, CBTC)
  • - Responsibility for projects throughout the life cycle of the project, from the delivery by sales to the end of the warranty period
  • - Ensure that project objectives are met in terms of time, budget, quality, security and customer satisfaction
  • - Identification of implementation strategies and coordination of all project activities for successful implementation
  • - Support and coordinate clients, suppliers, partners and internal stakeholders, as well as building trustworthy and sustainable business relationships
  • - Proactively manage risks, opportunities, changes and project dependencies
  • - Monitor and manage project finance, including forecasts, costs, revenues and profitability
  • - Ensure compliance with contractual obligations and accurate project management at all times
  • - Coordinate the multidisciplinary activities of the project team, maintain an effective atmosphere of collaboration and a strong team culture
  • - In the longer term, coach and mentor project leaders and new team members, as well as promote the sharing of knowledge and good practices
  • - Participate in continuous improvement of methodologies, project management tools
  • - Contribute to the development and professionalization of the project management organization by designing standards, processes and operational excellence
  • - Participate in the achievement of the company's strategic objectives by improving the project performance and the organization's overall performance
