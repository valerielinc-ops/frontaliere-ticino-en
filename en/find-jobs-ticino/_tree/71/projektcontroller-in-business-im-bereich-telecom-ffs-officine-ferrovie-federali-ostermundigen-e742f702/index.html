You can look forward to that.

Telecom provides us with connectivity services, both mobile- and landline-based. You take on a central role in ensuring transparent financial leadership in one or more programs at SBB Telecom. You are part of the program management team, which supports each other in the respective tasks. The complex and dynamic environment makes your work exciting, varied and challenging. When fulfilling the tasks, you enjoy great scope for action and design. Become part of this exciting team and get the opportunity to actively participate in the future of Telecom. Great project teams with a lot of experience are waiting for you. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • You ensure proper financial management within programs. You create transparency and thus make an important contribution to the goal achievement of the programs.
  • As a sparring partner of the program managers as well as of all involved bodies in financial matters, you are the central point of contact and responsible for all the data assigned to you.
  • You are responsible for controlling and reporting programs and projects, your data is the basis for steering committees and other committees.
  • You accompany and control the change and risk management, track the initiated measures with the aim of reducing risks.
  • You actively help define standards in financial leadership and controlling.
  • Accompanying project managers and contractors, assessing developed documents and recording projects in SAP as well as general data maintenance are also part of your daily task.

You bring that with you.

  • With your confident appearance, you also convince critical voices and persistently search for common solutions that you can enforce.
  • In order to be successful in this position, you are used to working in interdisciplinary teams at different hierarchical levels. Ideally, you are familiar with classical and agile project and process methods.
  • With great passion, you operate demanding stakeholder management at very different hierarchical and know-how levels, taking into account the respective interests and goals. Your analytical and systematic way of thinking supports you in making rapid progress with pragmatic solutions.
  • The safe handling of large amounts of data as well as the necessary structuring do not bother you. Good knowledge of SAP makes it easier for you to perform this function, we consider advanced Excel knowledge necessary.
  • We require a completed degree in business administration, finance & controlling at FH/HF level, ideally you also have several years of practical experience in a comparable function, preferably in a telecom or IT environment.
  • In German you are sure to negotiate, good knowledge of French (B2) is another advantage.
