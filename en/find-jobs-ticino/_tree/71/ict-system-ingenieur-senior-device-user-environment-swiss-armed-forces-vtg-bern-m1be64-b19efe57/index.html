Full description

Put to the Point

Become part of the Cyber Command as a Senior Lead Engineer and shape an open and interoperable endpoint ecosystem for mobile security communication and create the conditions for different user organizations to use their endpoints securely and reliably with the common MSK services.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20754
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Working in a team according to agile DevSecOps methods and actively participating in ceremonies on the level of Agile Team and Agile Release Train
  • Work closely with the product owner to understand user requirements and develop complex solutions
  • Configure mobile devices and associated solutions for mobile device management and unified endpoint management
  • Analyse disruptions of large ICT platforms or applications and actively contribute to troubleshooting (including Pikett service)
  • Maintaining system technical documentation

This makes you unique

  • Higher vocational training in computer science, mobile computing, system engineering or comparable discipline as well as several years of senior experience in the device / enterprise mobility environment
  • In-depth knowledge of Mobile Device Management and Unified Endpoint Management as well as Android Enterprise, iOS/iPadOS, Secure Workspace and mobile application environments
  • Several years of experience with device security, identities, certificates, eSIM and the integration of heterogeneous end device ecosystems
  • Very good understanding of the end-to-end relationships between terminals, radio access, applications, security, core and platform
  • In-depth experience in the technical evaluation of end devices as well as in the implementation of integration, compatibility and proof-of-concept tests
  • Good active knowledge of a second official language

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
