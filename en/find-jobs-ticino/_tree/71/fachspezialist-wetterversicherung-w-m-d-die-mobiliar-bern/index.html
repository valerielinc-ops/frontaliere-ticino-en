Full description

This is what awaits you

Mobiliar offers its agricultural customers insurance to protect against crop failures due to weather-related events. As a specialist, you are jointly responsible for the further development of claims regulation for the product of weather insurance in agriculture.

This is what you do with us

In your role, you independently handle complex damage cases, which includes both damage assessment in the field and administrative processes. As part of the weather insurance team, you will be involved in the further development of damage recovery methods in the field of arable crops.

Your activities also include the development, training and professional support of the militia damage organization.

As a link between the claims organization and the product management, you contribute significantly to the further development of the product and the claims processes.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed studies in agronomy or equivalent education
  • Comprehensive horticulture skills with field experience
  • Good knowledge of Swiss agriculture with a deeper understanding of practical agriculture
  • Personality with a structured, independent and solution-oriented way of working
  • Strong negotiating skills
  • Mother tongue German or French with good oral knowledge of the other language

Working with us

Dedicated colleagues are waiting for you. Together we live an open and communicative working atmosphere that puts the customer at the center. In addition to familiarization with the topics of weather insurance, a well-founded insurance education awaits you.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
