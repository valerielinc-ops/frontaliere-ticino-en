Über de Rolle Sika waterproof coats have been tested worldwide for several decades on multiple structures.

Our "Made in Switzerland" roof systems are sustainable and reliable. Thermal accessories and insulation complete the product range. Whether it's green roofs, with gravel or without a layer of wear, we cover the whole request. Our consultancy and sales team is looking for a figure for the (m/f/d) region:

Your duties

  • Consulting and sale of floor roof systems to architects, designers, builders and entrepreneurs
  • Consolidating relationships with existing customers, acquiring new customers and projects of new construction and regeneration
  • Preparation of tenders and tender specifications, support in the main yards
  • Present high quality Sika solutions for constructions at information events and events for customers

Ihre Fähigkeiten und Erfahrungen

  • Responsible for constructions or projects, designers, engineers, SSST technicians, construction designers, lattonieri, panel for roofs
  • Knowledge of German (speaking and written)
  • Experience in the waterproofing industry
  • Knowledge of the CRB system for the preparation of specifications
  • With your experience and pleasure of communication, convince others and bring solutions through consulting
  • Business thinking, targeted action and perseverance are among your strengths. As a networker, you like to approach people and build relationships through your direct personality
