R&D Battery Engineer (m/f/d) — Sonova

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

At Sonova, we believe breakthrough products start with breakthrough technologies. As an R&D Battery Engineer , you will drive battery innovation across our Hearing Instruments and Charging Systems, bringing next-generation energy solutions from technology scouting to product integration.

This is a unique opportunity to combine deep battery expertise with innovation, engineering, and data-driven development. Working in a highly collaborative and regulated medical device environment, you will help shape the battery technologies powering the next generation of hearing solutions and chargers and improving the lives of millions of people worldwide

More about the role

  • Scout, evaluate, and qualify next-generation battery technologies, chemistries, packaging concepts, and manufacturing innovations
  • Collaborate with leading battery suppliers to assess new technologies and successfully introduce them into Sonova hearing instruments and chargers
  • Lead battery characterization, verification, validation, reliability and lifetime assessments throughout product development
  • Drive battery integration across Hearing Instruments and Charging Systems, working closely with electrical, mechanical, software, system, manufacturing, and quality engineers
  • Contribute to Battery Management System (BMS) concepts, including charging strategies, battery modeling, and State of Charge (SoC) and State of Health (SoH) estimation
  • Develop Python tools to automate testing, data analysis, and engineering workflows
  • Apply statistical methods and use Design of Experiments (DoE) to analyze complex engineering data, optimize battery performance, and support robust, data-driven decision-making
  • Stay at the forefront of battery innovation and translate emerging technologies into competitive product solutions

More about you

  • Master's or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Materials Science, Physics, Chemistry, or a related field
  • Proven experience in battery technology development within medical devices, consumer electronics, wearables, automotive, or another high-tech industry
  • Experience working with battery suppliers and introducing new battery technologies into products.
  • Good understanding of Battery Management Systems (BMS), battery modeling, charging strategies, and battery estimation algorithms such as State of Charge (SoC) and State of Health (SoH)
  • Deep understanding of battery technologies, electrochemistry, battery manufacturing processes, reliability, aging mechanisms, and verification methodologies
  • Strong Python programming skills for engineering automation and data analysis
  • Experience with JMP, statistical analysis, and Design of Experiments (DoE)
  • A collaborative mindset, strong analytical skills, and a passion for innovation
  • Practical, hands-on attitude and ability to work on-site in a laboratory setting
  • Independent, structured, and reliable work style
  • Strong communication skills and team orientation
  • Fluent spoken and written English

More about what we offer

As one of the world’s leading hearing care providers headquartered in Switzerland, we’re committed to building an inclusive culture. We want to create an environment where you can balance a successful career with your commitments and interests outside of work, through our flexible hybrid working model. We offer a wide range of training opportunities for both your professional and personal development, and there are exceptional growth opportunities with individual development plans.

  • Exposure to industry-grade test systems and analytical tools
  • Opportunity to contribute to meaningful products improving people’s lives
  • A collaborative environment with mentoring from skilled engineers
