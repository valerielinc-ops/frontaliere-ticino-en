Full description

For our modern plant in Seewis in beautiful Bündnerland we are looking for an injection moulding team leader. In this position, you will take on the disciplinary and professional leadership of around twelve setters and process technicians. You create clear orientation, promote the further development of your team and ensure quality-appropriate, timely and economical production. You combine technical know-how with strong leadership skills and clear communication. They work in the day shift and report directly to the head of the spray shop.

Their main tasks

  • Disciplinary and professional leadership, motivation and development of the team
  • Ensuring efficient production in a two-shift operation according to specifications (time, quality, set-up plans, occupational safety)
  • SMED analyses and continuous improvements such as standardization of setup processes
  • Ensuring efficient raw material use including traceability, scrap reduction and early detection of material shortages
  • Close cooperation with colleagues from other areas such as quality, production planning, maintenance and logistics
  • Order-oriented personnel planning, including structured shift transfers

Your profile

  • Completed technical training, ideally as a plastics technologist
  • Several years of experience in injection molding, ideally with leadership responsibility
  • A strong sense of quality, safety and responsibility
  • Knowledge of lean management and continuous improvement, ideally SMED and 5S
  • Winning communication and assertiveness
  • Secure use of ERP and MES systems and other digital tools
  • Enjoying an operational leadership role with presence in production
