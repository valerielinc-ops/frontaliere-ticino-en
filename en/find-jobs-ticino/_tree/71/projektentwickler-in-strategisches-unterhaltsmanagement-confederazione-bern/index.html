Put to the Point

As a project developer with a focus on maintenance management, you shape the long-term development of our real estate portfolio. They combine strategic real estate development with forward-looking maintenance management and lay the foundations for economic and sustainable investment decisions.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 620-19927
  • Location: Fellerstrasse 21, 3027 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Developing and assessing real estate projects in early project phases as well as developing sound decision-making bases for strategic investment decisions
  • Steering and accompanying feasibility studies as well as creating project definitions as a basis for successful project implementation
  • Assume professional responsibility for strategic maintenance management and ensure sustainable implementation of maintenance projects
  • Assessment of condition analyses, prioritisation of long-term maintenance needs and management of the multiannual action plan
  • Managing a higher-level controlling of the maintenance projects as well as involving and advising the project participants

This makes them unique.

  • Completed university degree (university or university of applied sciences) in architecture, civil engineering, business administration, facility management or a comparable discipline
  • Further education in real estate economics or business administration
  • Several years of professional experience in project and maintenance management in the real estate environment
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills as well as experience in the development of decision-making bases and the management of complex projects
  • Independent, responsible and solution-oriented working method with high communication and consulting skills
  • Good knowledge of a second official language
