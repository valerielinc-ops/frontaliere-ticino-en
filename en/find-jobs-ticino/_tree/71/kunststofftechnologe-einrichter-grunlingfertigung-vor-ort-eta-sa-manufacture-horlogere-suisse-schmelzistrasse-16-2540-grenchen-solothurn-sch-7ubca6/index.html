Plastic technologists / inverters Greenling production, on site - Swatch Group Kunststofftechnologe / Einrichter Grünlingproduktion, on site Your future employer Our employees contribute to the success of the Swiss-Made watches, watches and components produced by ETA SA Manufacture Horlogère Suisse. Our company is part of the Swatch Group, the world's largest watch group with 16 renowned brands and numerous production companies that are highly specialized both in watch production and in other industries. The Swatch Business Unit is responsible for the development, production, assembly and delivery of the watch heads for the brands Swatch and Flik Flak. Our plant 08 in Grenchen uses state-of-the-art technologies to produce high-quality stainless steel watch shells using MIM (Metal Injection Moulding) and shape the future of innovative and high-quality products as your future tasks Together with your team colleagues, you will work in day-to-day operation or in shift operation as required and ensure the demanding quality and quantitative production standards. This includes:

  • Installation and programming of injection moulding machines and automation systems
  • cooperation in the entire sampling process and optimization of existing production processes
  • Ensuring the production quantities and recording of operating data (e.g. SPC, MDE)
  • Support of the group leader, especially in the processing and training of employees
  • Detection and reporting of deviations and ensuring a stable production process
  • Co-responsibility for maintenance In addition, you work closely with the areas of quality assurance, laboratory, tool construction and logistics.
