Full description

Working as a compliance officer with a focus on data protection You are independent, competent and efficient as an important member of our Compliance Advisory Team. We are looking for an honest and agile personality who is very knowledgeable about data protection and wants to actively shape this and other compliance topics.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Wallisellen, Industriestrasse 17-19, 8304 Wallisellen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Advice on data protection issues and professional support of data protection projects and projects.
  • Updating instructions on data protection and setting up and implementing corresponding controls. Preparation of training, risk assessments and reporting in the area of data protection.
  • Responding to requests from data subjects and requests from public authorities. Cooperation with the Legal Service, Information Security and other specialist areas.
  • Implementation and coordination of data protection requirements (e.g. data protection impact assessments, editing directory, etc.) and data protection measures (e.g. implementation of compliance processes).
  • Advising on data protection-compliant development of new products and services.
  • Advising, monitoring and implementing various compliance topics outside the data protection area.

Requirements

  • three to five years: data protection
  • Master's degree (university, university): Law
  • High level of expertise in Swiss data protection (especially DSG and AI governance as well as cloud issues).
  • Professional experience in data protection at a bank, law firm or leading consulting firm.
  • Independent, efficient and accurate work.
  • Team player with interest and knowledge of various compliance topics.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • French (advantageous)
  • Italian (advantageous)
  • English (advantageous)
