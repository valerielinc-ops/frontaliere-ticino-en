Head of SAP Technology — BLS AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

BLS relies on SAP and therefore fully on you! As head: in SAP technology, you have an influence on the entire company with your area of 13 employees. Whether on trains, workshops or business processes. Thanks to your commitment, we manage to get our passengers to their destination safely and on time every day. Your work is everywhere – a great feeling!

This is waiting for you

  • You will lead and develop an experienced team of experts in the areas of SAP Basis, Authorizations and Development and create the framework for responsible and effective work
  • You will be responsible for the technological architecture and further development of our SAP landscape and ensure that on-premise, cloud and integration solutions work together in the long term.
  • Together with your team, you take on project management tasks in the technology environment, ensure stable, secure and high-performance SAP operations and continuously develop operating models, platforms and technical standards.
  • You are responsible for technological solution concepts, architectural decisions, operating concepts as well as security and compliance requirements and represent them to specialist departments, IT and external partners
  • As a member of the management team of the SAP Competence Center, you actively shape the technological orientation of SAP and participate in strategic projects and cross-divisional decisions.

This is what you bring with you

  • Further education at FH level Computer Science and/or Business Informatics with affinity to technology
  • Several years of experience leading SAP technology teams (base, permissions and development)
  • In-depth understanding of modern SAP architectures and the ability to assess technological dependencies holistically
  • Flair to structure complex technological issues, to bring about architectural and solution decisions and to represent them clearly and convincingly to different stakeholders
  • Solution-oriented action complements with the ability to maintain an eye for the overall system, even with different interests and high complexity

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2nd class, a GA 1st class. Class or half-tax subscription
  • Work where it suits you best. Whether at home office or office
  • Share your working time freely. We promote part-time work, top and job sharing
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten days of holiday per year and to take a sabbatical of 40 working days every seven years.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health.
  • Financial support for external training, a leadership development program and an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? Proud of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.
