Full description

Welcome to Medbase Lausanne Gare

Our versatile medical centre with modern facilities is located at Lausanne station. Some 35 collaborators working in general internal medicine, endocrinology, diabetes, osteology, gynaecology and obstetrics, ORL, orthopaedic surgery and traumatology, as well as physiotherapy and nutritional counselling ensure the interprofessional care of our patients, always oriented towards quality. Medbase Lausanne Gare favours a good balance between professional and private life. It is possible to work part-time in all functions and work schedules can be flexibly arranged. For doctors, collaboration is possible under employee or independent status, to be agreed upon during the interview.

Your tasks

During your learning as a medical assistant, you will discover all the facets of the profession in a multidisciplinary medical centre and integrate a dynamic and caring team. We will accompany you throughout your training to enable you to develop your skills and gradually gain autonomy.

  • Welcome and accompany patients and prepare them for exams.
  • Assist doctors in consultations, care and various medical procedures.
  • Perform laboratory analyses and x-rays in accordance with procedures.
  • Manage various administrative tasks, including appointment making, telephone reception and case tracking.
  • Gradually take over different missions independently, with the support of your team.

Your profile

  • You report good performance in all subjects at section A/E high school after orientation.
  • A strong interest in the field of health and the taste of human contact.
  • Empathy, reliability and a good team spirit.
  • A careful presentation as well as excellent relational and communication skills.
  • Organization, autonomy and a sense of responsibility.
  • A good mastery of computer tools.
  • A good mastery of German (minimum B2 level) is an asset.

#WeAreMedbase

To achieve great things, one must have a vision of the future. Twenty years ago, Medbase was founded with the idea of creating a health care network for all of Switzerland. From a medical practice with three collaborators in 2001, we have now become a 360-degree health provider, with approximately 4,000 employees located in 170 sites, operating in four fields of activity: medical and dental centres, pharmacies, drug distribution and digital health platform. Take the opportunity to continue this success story with us, as Medbase continues to develop and improve every day the future of health care in Switzerland.
