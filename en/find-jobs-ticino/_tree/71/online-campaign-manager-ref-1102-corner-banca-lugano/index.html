Full description

We are looking for an experienced Online Campaign Manager to plan, execute, and optimize digital marketing campaigns across multiple channels. This is a mid-level role suited for someone who already has hands-on experience managing campaigns and is ready to take ownership of performance, strategy, and cross-market coordination.

  • Plan, launch, and manage online marketing campaigns across channels (e.g., paid search, paid social media, display…)
  • Optimize campaigns continuously based on performance data (CTR, CPA, ROI, conversions)
  • Coordinate campaign execution in Switzerland in four languages (IT, DE, EN, FR)
  • Collaborate with internal teams (creative, content, analytics) and external partners/agencies
  • Analyze campaign results and provide actionable insights and recommendations
  • Manage budgets, timelines, and campaign deliverables
  • Conduct A/B testing and implement data-driven improvements
  • Ensure campaigns align with brand guidelines and overall marketing strategy

What do we offer?

  • Flexibility in working from home and the management of working hours in order to guarantee a healthy work-life balance
  • Numerous benefits and incentives
  • Well-being, motivational and team-building activities
  • 3–5 years of experience in digital marketing or campaign management
  • Proven track record of managing and optimizing online campaigns across multiple channels
  • Strong analytical mindset with experience using tools like Google Analytics, ad platforms, and reporting dashboards
  • Experience working across multiple markets or languages is a strong plus
  • Excellent project management and organizational skills
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple campaigns simultaneously
  • Resident in Ticino or willing to relocate
