At the subsidiary Scintilla AG in St. Niklaus in the canton of Wallis, a workforce of around 800 employees worldwide develops, manufactures and distributes accessories for power tools.

Job description

As a manufacturing and problem-solving mechanic (f/m/div.) in the field of hard metals in the MFO22, you take on the responsibility for supporting and ensuring the functionality of a wide variety of plants and processes. With the work preparers you maintain a close cooperation. You are the link and contact person for technical aspects in production (MSE 2\).

Your tasks essentially include

  • Problem identification, problem analysis and problem solving fall within your responsibilities
  • Submitting optimization suggestions, process and process development as well as carrying out production tests are part of your workspace
  • Training of machine operators and Teamleader Production will lead you
  • You are responsible for managing and replacing wear parts
  • Carrying out maintenance and repairs are among your responsibilities
  • You support in improving the 5S\ work design and work ergonomics
  • When converting equipment and ensuring availability, you help
  • Documentation of the work and processes round off your area of responsibility

Qualifications

  • Training: Basic technical training (polymechanic/automatician/automechanic (f/m/div.))
  • Personality and working method: Communicative, solution-oriented and team-capable personality with an independent, systematic and precise working method
  • Experience and know-how: Experience in production technology is an advantage
  • Languages: Good communication and expressiveness in German
