Full description

Portrait For our high school at Schulhaus Graben, we are looking for a committed and heartfelt teacher who enjoys accompanying young people on their learning path and developing school together with a motivated team.

Tasks

You will teach a total of 8 lessons

  • 3 lessons in French in the 7. Category
  • 3 lessons in English in 8. Category
  • 1 lesson RZG in the 8. Category
  • 1 lesson in French in 9th grade

An increase in the burden in the field of self-organized learning (SOL) is possible - a reduction as well. · The lessons are currently scheduled on Monday (full day) and Thursday morning. When designing the timetable, we are flexible and take your wishes into account as far as possible. Thursday afternoon or Friday afternoon is also possible. · You design a varied lesson that encourages young people to learn independently and strengthens their own responsibility. · You create a positive learning atmosphere with clear structures and an appreciative attitude. · You enjoy working in a team and bring your own ideas to the teaching development.

Our upper school is changing

Self-organized learning and first age-mixed learning groups are already part of our school day. From summer 2027, we will switch to age-mixed classes (7th to 9th school year). We are happy if you shape this path with your ideas.

Requirements

You bring with you

  • A teaching diploma for upper secondary level I or equivalent qualification.
  • The joy of teaching and dealing with young people.
  • Heartiness and humor: You meet the young people with openness, patience and a positive attitude.
  • Clarity and structure: You provide orientation and create a reliable framework for successful learning.
  • Team spirit: You enjoy working with others and working together with the team.
  • Openness and curiosity: You have the desire to try new things, to think lessons further and to help shape the age-mixed upper school.

We offer

  • A school that would rather ask: “How could we make this possible?” instead of “Why can’t we?”
  • A warm, helpful team, in which laughter, personal exchange and commonality have their place.
  • The opportunity to actively shape the further development of our upper school with our own ideas.
  • Lots of room for manoeuvre, mutual support and appreciation.
  • Teenagers who challenge you – in the best sense – and show you why you love this profession.

And a place where others go on holiday!
