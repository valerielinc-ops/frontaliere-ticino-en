We make spaces where life is good.

Planner/Designer-Electricity Projector

Join Solstis SA and actively contribute to the energy transition! At Solstis SA, you will have the opportunity to participate concretely in the realization of photovoltaic projects and to accompany the energy transition of our customers.

Your tasks

Within our technical team, you will be responsible for

  • Perform and/or verify the electrical dimensions of residential photovoltaic installations.
  • Establish connection requests from Distribution Network Managers (DRMs) via their dedicated platforms and the ElektroForm tool.
  • Design, establish and control electrical diagrams of installations.
  • Prepare technical files and ensure compliance with existing standards and requirements.
  • Work closely with project managers from different departments to ensure the smooth running of projects.
  • Provide technical support to installation teams in the field and conduct site visits on an ad hoc basis.
  • Actively participate in the continuous improvement of the department's working methods and processes.

Your profile

  • Training of HES Engineer, ES Technician, Electrical Planner, Electrical Designer or equivalent training.
  • First experience in the field of building electrical installations or strong motivation to develop your skills in this sector.
  • AutoCAD mastery.
  • Good knowledge of ElektroForm.
  • Knowledge of photovoltaic installations, battery storage systems and charging infrastructures for electric vehicles (a serious asset).
  • Good understanding of existing Swiss standards (NIBT/ITTO).
  • Experience with IT tools and DRM platforms.
  • Team spirit, autonomy, rigour and sense of responsibility.
  • Excellent communication skills in French.
