Full description

Do you already have an idea of what you will do after obtaining your school-leaving certificate? Embark on your future with us and take advantage of the development opportunities at Lidl Switzerland. Our stores are at the heart of our business. As an AFP or CFC retail apprentice (with a focus on creating shopping experiences), you contribute to the success of your Lidl store. Start/End AFP: August 1, 2027 – July 31, 2029 Start/End CFC: August 1, 2027 – July 31, 2030

Your mission

  • Cash register management and preparation of cash statements
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Stocking and presenting merchandise
  • Checking the receipt of goods in the stockroom and assisting your team during inventory counts
  • Helping to plan and implement promotions and seasonal product displays
  • Determining merchandise requirements and ordering merchandise in the 3rd year of apprenticeship (CFC)
  • First experience managing a team in the 3rd year of apprenticeship (CFC)

Your profile

  • Average to very good marks in the lower secondary school-leaving certificate
  • AFP: A1 level in a foreign language and CFC: at least A2
  • Enjoyment of working with people
  • Independent working style and quick thinking
  • Team spirit, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and availability
  • Physical stamina
  • Good knowledge of French

Our offer An apprenticeship that opens many doors for you. After successfully completing your apprenticeship, and if you have good personal skills, we will help you progress to a management position in the store. In addition, you will benefit from the Lidl CCT and numerous attractive benefits
