Coop Cooperative — Ilanz/Glion, GR, Svizzera

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement

  • Location: Coop, Via Crappa Grossa 24, 7130 Ilanz/Glion

Tasks

  • You will be introduced step by step into the processes and management tasks around rayon meat/convenience.
  • You ensure optimal goods management, ensure the high quality standards and are ready to take responsibility in the organization of the rayon.
  • You are responsible for the competent advice of our customers.

Requirements

  • Completed basic training in the retail trade, as a meat specialist (m/f/d), cook/chef (m/f/d) or corresponding professional experience in the field of meat/convenience.

  • You have a high willingness to learn and are motivated to take on a management task as rayon leader: in meat/convenience in the medium term.
  • You love advising customers and sharing your interest in quality products and culinary knowledge.
