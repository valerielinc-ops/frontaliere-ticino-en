Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Salem Hospital | Bern

Occupation by 1 August 2027

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 69757

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Hirslanden Bern AG includes Klinik Beau-Site, the Salem Hospital and Klinik Permanence with a total of around 350 beds. In the kitchen of the Salem Hospital we offer a free apprenticeship as a learner / cook EFZ with training start on the 1st. August 2027.

You work in a team and learn different forms of preparation – from preparation to presentation. In practice, you apply your knowledge from the vocational school and the intercompany courses (UK) continuously and according to your training status. You will be accompanied by a vocational trainer throughout your learning process.

Your profile

  • You have completed elementary school (secondary school or good real degree)
  • You are enthusiastic about gastronomy in healthcare
  • You enjoy handling food and preparing food
  • You like to work creatively and are experimental
  • You like working in a team and are reliable
  • You are physically resilient
  • You have good manners.

Our offer

  • Attractive and central workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Family atmosphere with you - culture
  • Professional support and individual support in the learning process
  • Extensive benefits offer (e.g. publishing, public transport coupons, fitness subscriptions, etc.)
  • Discounted food options in restaurants in clinics
  • Financial contribution to teaching equipment
  • Annual learning excursion

For more information, Thomas Strausak, Head of Kitchen, under T, and Sarah Jäggi, Head of Vocational Education and Training, under T are at your disposal.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by e-mail. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
