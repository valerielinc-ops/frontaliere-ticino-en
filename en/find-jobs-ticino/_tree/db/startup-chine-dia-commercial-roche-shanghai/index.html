The Position Join the StartUp Roche Diagnostics China Development Program and embark on an accelerated career path designed for future leaders. We are seeking individuals with High Personal Drive, High Potential, and High Mobility, who have no more than 3 years of professional experience in the healthcare industry.

As a Trainee, you will have a unique opportunity to gain a global view of Roche's business landscape, breadth of experience across the value chain in Roche Diagnostics China, and depth of knowledge in the specific function, given the China In Vitro Diagnostic (IVD) market dynamics. This program will empower you to shape the company by making significant contributions to our purpose: "Doing now what patients need next."

The program includes the following development journeys: 1) Commercial and 2) Digital & AI. Over the two-year duration, you will complete up to four rotations, each with tailored goals and plans. Please apply for the development journey that best matches your educational background and career interests.

As a Trainee of the program, you will be expected to

  • Complete tasks and learning objectives for each rotation as defined by the program framework;
  • Proactively leverage your expertise and business knowledge to deliver patient- and customer-centric outcomes;
  • Connect ideas and resources to generate value, prioritize investments, and make strategic trade-off decisions;
  • Take responsibility for your continuous growth, ensuring your skills and capabilities align with both your passion and organizational needs;
  • Maintain a high degree of flexibility for potential relocation assignments and business travel.

In the Commercial development journey, we are looking for ambitious professionals passionate about business strategy and commercial excellence. The development journey empowers talent to translate high-level strategy into market execution, leveraging scientific expertise and digital innovation to drive sustainable growth in China's evolving healthcare ecosystem.
