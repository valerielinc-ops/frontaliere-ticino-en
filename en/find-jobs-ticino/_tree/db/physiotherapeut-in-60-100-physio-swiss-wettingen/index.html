Your profile You are interested in the whole spectrum of physiotherapy and would like to continue your education. Your expertise includes musculoskeletal physiotherapy, manual therapy, treatment of diseases and injuries to the musculoskeletal system, lymphatic drainage and rehabilitation training. You have fun at work, a broad interest and show commitment and motivation for further education in neurorehabilitation and geriatrics. Then you are right in Team PhysioMed. You bring a completed education as a Dipl. Physical therapist/FH/HF, physiotherapist/BSc or equivalent training with SRC recognition. Your professional competence is accompanied by high social competence, as well as excellent communication and team skills. A pleasant appearance, well-kept manners, as well as independent work round off your profile. You are a digital native and master foreign languages in addition to German: Italian and English welcome. Additional requirements for working hours: In order to optimally complement our offer for patients and customers, we are looking for support on two to three days each until the practical deadline at 7 pm. The specific distribution of working hours will be coordinated in a joint discussion so that you and we fit well together. We provide PhysioMed offers a competitive salary also for newcomers, depending on qualification and professional experience, free weekends, a fixed work plan, 5 weeks holidays, 10 paid holidays, free parking, attractive BVG services, Swibeco, and free use of the fitness area in practice. At PhysioMed, further education is at the forefront. We regularly organize internal workshops and participate in your external training. We also support you with paid training leave. You can focus fully on your patients and always have at least 30 minutes for a treatment. The administration team keeps your back free and takes over patient registrations, telephony and bureaucracy. Your chance PhysioMed is a modern, well-equipped practice for physiotherapy with closed individual treatment rooms, large open movement and gymnastics room and well-equipped fitness area. You can expect a modern and dynamic working environment in which you can contribute your skills and personality.

  • We offer a versatile, independent and responsible activity in a pleasant and appreciative working atmosphere.

We are happy to await your written application via e-mail to: monica.obodny@physio‐med.ch Your employer PhysioMed Wettingen AG PhysioMed Wettingen AG is a competence center for outpatient physiotherapy, neurorehabilitation & sports physiotherapy PhysioMed offers modern physiotherapeutic treatment concepts, manual therapy, active movement therapy and functional training. The three basic principles of our thearpie offering are: prevention, rehabilitation and training. PhysioMed treats problems and dysfunctions from a wide variety of fields of medicine: orthopedics, rheumatology, traumatology, sports medicine, physical medicine, internal medicine, cardiology, pneumology, neurology, occupational medicine and ergonomics. PhysioMed specializes in manual therapy SAMT IMTT, Bobath IBITA, neurodynamics NOI, sports physiotherapy, multifunctional joint training TRX, medical training therapy MTT, training counseling, instruction and coaching, physiotherapeutic massage, lymph drainage and complex physical decongestion therapy KPE, physiotherapy in geriatrics, myofascial trigger point therapy and dry needling.
