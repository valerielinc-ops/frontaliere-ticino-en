Full description

HUGO BOSS is one of the leading fashion and lifestyle companies in the premium segment with more than 19,000 employees worldwide. As versatile as we are, we share a common goal: We love fashion, we change fashion!

Become a brand ambassador and part of a team that works with passion, ambition and know-how to create excellent customer experiences. Be among the first to bring our collections from the Runway to the customers! Become part of our team and discover your customized career opportunities.

From now on, we are looking for a dedicated assistant store manager full-time (m/f/d) for our BOSS Outlet team who will inspire customers with passion and inspiration for our BOSS brand. As an assistant store manager at HUGO BOSS, you will help store management lead a dynamic team, create world-class shopping experiences and embody our brand values through excellent product presentation and customer support. We look forward to receiving your application and welcoming you to HUGO BOSS.

This is what awaits you with us

  • Support the Store Manager:in by taking on the disciplinary and professional responsibility for a dynamic and motivated team
  • Carefully recruit new employees: join them through onboarding and coaching to shape a strong team that embodies our values
  • Steer together with the Store Manager: in the store and optimize work processes through targeted key performance indicator analyses (KPIs) to achieve sales goals and ensure the success of the store
  • Through an attractive product presentation and the implementation of all visual merchandising guidelines, you ensure a first-class appearance of the store and make our customers: insiders to fans
  • Optimize customer contact and customer relationship management by providing an appealing product presentation as well as comprehensive advice and support.
  • Creates shopping experiences and long-term customer relationships

This is your profile

  • With at least 1-2 years of relevant professional experience as a fashion retail executive or in a comparable position, you will bring valuable expertise
  • Successfully completed training in the field of commercial business administration, such as as a retail businessman or a comparable qualification
  • Thanks to your fluent knowledge of German and English, you can also optimally look after our international customers
  • Ideally, you will speak another foreign language.
  • Sound IT knowledge (MS-Office applications and ideally SAP) as well as digital affinity round off your profile
  • Your pronounced sales orientation and organizational talent allow you to always keep an overview and focus on customer needs
  • Your love of sales space and hands-on mentality make you a driving force that motivates your team to perform on a daily basis.
  • Through personal qualities such as safe appearance, positive charisma and empathy, you not only ensure that our customers feel comfortable, but also move forward as an inspiring role model for your team.

This is what we offer you

  • We focus on competitive remuneration as well as individual commissions and attractive additional services: from family support to our SHIP share program.
  • So that you can also represent our new collections on the floor, you benefit as a member of our team of high-end teamwear.
  • Your personal development is important to us: Learn more about our collection with trainings on the surface and in our HUGO BOSS University with Training Guides & Trainings.
  • We love fashion and digitalization: individual tools such as StorIQ, Click & Collect or CRM, RFID and iPads let you work not only manually, but also digitally and get in touch with your customers.
  • Exclusive access to fashion: Benefit from employee discounts and Family & Friends Days.

We are a global company and our employees represent the whole world. Our inclusive culture respects and values the authenticity and individuality of each individual. We are committed to equal opportunities in the hiring process – and we believe that our equal working environment helps you reach your full potential and inspire you to succeed.
