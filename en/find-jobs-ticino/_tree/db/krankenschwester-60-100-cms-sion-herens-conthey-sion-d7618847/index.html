Full description

The CMS Sion\-Hérens\-Conthey offers medico-social benefits, assistance and social and vocational integration that meet the expectations and needs of the population.

Its mission is to foster home maintenance, autonomy and social integration by providing individualised support in each situation and by offering a full range of services.

With its 5 sites and close to 550 collaborators, the CMS Sion\-Hérens\-Conthey accompanies more than 5'700 beneficiaries on all 16 communes in the region.
