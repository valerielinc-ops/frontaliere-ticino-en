Full description

The Office for the Environment (AFU) is committed to a careful handling of the environment and a sustainable use of natural resources. The Department of Industry and Commerce is responsible for the implementation of the various environmental regulations in industry and commerce. With customer-oriented enforcement work and advice, it ensures that companies can develop without unduly burdening the environment.

What to expect

  • Granting of authorisations in the environmental fields of water protection, air pollution control, noise protection, incident prevention and waste management
  • Environmental assessment of construction projects and technical procedures in industrial and commercial enterprises
  • Implementation of operational controls and rehabilitation
  • Advising industrial and commercial enterprises and municipalities on environmental issues
  • Cooperation with internal and external specialist offices as well as with companies and planning offices

What sets it apart

  • Completed studies in the technical or scientific field or completion of a higher technical school or higher specialist examination (HF/HFP) with several years of professional experience
  • Experience in the environmental assessment of plants and processes in industrial and commercial enterprises or in-depth professional experience
  • Strong consulting competence as well as a high resilience and endurance
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation and understanding of legal issues
  • Motivation, reliability and initiative as well as strong negotiating skills
  • High social skills and very good oral and written communication skills

What we offer

  • A responsible specialist position
  • Versatile and challenging subject area
  • A modernly equipped workplace in St.Gallen
  • Collegial working atmosphere and a supportive team
  • Attractive employment conditions with flexible working time models
  • Individual training opportunities and development prospects
