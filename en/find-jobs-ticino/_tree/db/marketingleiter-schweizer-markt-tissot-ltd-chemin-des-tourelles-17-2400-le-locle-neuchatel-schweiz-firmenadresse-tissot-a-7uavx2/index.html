Sales & Marketing Manager Swiss Market - Swatch Group

Society Founded in 1918, MIDO combines high precision, tradition, technical excellence and timeless design to create timepieces designed to accompany important moments of life. MIDO is present in 70 countries through a network of 2,300 official outlets. Based at the Locle, the brand is part of the Swatch Group, the world leader in watchmaking.

Description In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a Sales & Marketing Manager Swiss Market, based in the Locle.

Mansioni

  • Overall responsibility for turnover, sales volumes and profitability in the Swiss market
  • Management and development of approximately 70 dealers and key accounts
  • Acquisition of new trading partners and optimization of the existing distribution network
  • Guarantee of optimal stock levels and quality merchandising at points of sale
  • Planning and organizing training for sales staff
  • Development and implementation of marketing and marketing plans for the Swiss market
  • Preparation and deployment of actions to launch new products
  • Preparation and monitoring of the annual budget
  • Management of an administrative/sales assistant and a decorator

Profile

  • Persuasive personality with excellent communication skills
  • High autonomy and ability to clearly define priorities
  • Strong sense of service and excellent negotiation skills
  • Performance orientation and excellent analytical capabilities (comprehension of figures, KPIs, forecasts)
  • Ability to develop long-term partnerships and reason strategically
  • Availability to travel about 80 per cent in Switzerland and 20 per cent in the seat at the Locle

Required professionali

  • Minimum 5 years of successful experience in selling in fashion, luxury, accessories, cosmetics or watchmaking, with a solid marketing experience
  • Training in sales, marketing, business management or related fields

Lingo

  • French or German mother tongue, with excellent knowledge of the other language (oral and written)
  • Good knowledge of English

Place of work

  • Chemin des Tourelles 17
  • 2400 Le Locle (Neuchatel)

Switzerland

  • Company address: Tissot SA, P.O. Box 399, CH-2400 Le Locle
