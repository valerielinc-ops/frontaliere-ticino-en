Full description

The Cantonal Hospital Uri, embedded in the impressive mountains at the southern end of Lake Vierwaldstätter, provides basic medical care for around 38,500 inhabitants of the region. In the medical field of gynecology and obstetrics, a versatile and demanding field of activity awaits you. The region also offers you numerous sporting (e.g. skiing only 30 minutes away) and cultural leisure opportunities as well as excellent connections to the city regions of Zurich and Lucerne. We are looking for motivated personality as assistant physician / assistant physician (m/f/d) 100% in the field of gynecology and obstetrics at the following times:

  • April 2028 What You Expect In our clinic, you can expect a modern and versatile working environment characterized by a young, dynamic team. Together with a chief physician and four specialists and three assistant physicians, we look after our patients holistically and with the highest commitment. Our gynecological and obstetric ambulatorium offers you the opportunity to work in a wide range of gynecology. Every year we accompany around 300 births from the 36th week of pregnancy and work closely with the Children’s Hospital and the Neonatology of the Lucerne Cantonal Hospital to ensure optimal care for mother and child. In addition, we perform a wide range of gynecological operations. A particular focus is on the comprehensive treatment of mammary carcinoma, from diagnosis to surgery and follow-up. Advanced endoscopic procedures as well as corrections of genital lowering (both vaginal and laparoscopic) are regularly performed. With more than 9,000 outpatient consultations per year in our ambulatory, we offer the entire spectrum of outpatient care. As part of fertility medicine, we work closely with the fertility clinic Cada, so that all fertility treatments can be offered in our clinic. You have your state examination in your pocket and want to start or deepen your specialist training in gynecology and obstetrics? Perhaps you would like to learn the basics of this exciting discipline during a foreign year? Then you are exactly right with us! You appreciate working in a regional hospital that convinces with a family atmosphere and a wide range of medical services. A first-class education and cooperation in an interdisciplinary team are important to you. In addition, you will have a good knowledge of German (at least level C1) in order to easily exchange ideas with colleagues and patients. If you feel like working in a dynamic environment and driving your professional development in a supportive team, we look forward to meeting you! Our chief physician Gynecology and Obstetrics, Dr. med. Panagiotis Kanellos, MHBA will gladly provide you with further information.
