Full description

Learning neurology. Take responsibility. Shaping the future.

Do you want to complete your neurological training in a modern, collegial environment and combine clinical experience with practical neurological diagnostics? Then you are right with us.

Our rehabilitation clinic in Bad Zurzach offers a broad spectrum of neurological rehabilitation. We work medically and therapeutically interdisciplinary and accompany our patients back into everyday life.

This is what awaits you

  • 2 years of continuing education permit neurology (SIWF D2)
  • Close specialist supervision and personal support
  • Introduction and practical application of instrumental examination methods (EEG, ENMG, ultrasound)
  • Weekly internal neurological training and case discussions
  • Much contact with different neurological diseases and their rehabilitation
  • Gradual assumption of responsibility according to your training status
  • A dedicated, competent and interdisciplinary team

Your tasks

You look after our inpatients together with our senior doctors and senior doctors.

Among other things, you will

  • Entry and exit investigations
  • Visits and medical checks
  • Rehabilitation planning and therapy objectives
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration with nursing, therapy and psychology
  • Exchange with assignees, family doctors and relatives
  • Participation in night and weekend services

You bring that with you

  • Federal State Exam or MEBEKO-approved foreign diploma

Important: A MEBEKO recognition is absolutely necessary for employment at ZURZACH Care. A mere registration of the diploma without recognition is not sufficient.

  • Interest in Neurology and Neurological Rehabilitation
  • Readiness to learn, initiative and responsibility
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Are you at the beginning of your neurological training or looking for a neurological foreign year? No problem – we are also happy about your application.

Your advantages

  • Modern infrastructure and varied neurological everyday life
  • Individual professional development and supervision
  • Collegial and appreciative work climate
  • Discounts at various partners such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission to the FORTYSEVEN spa and other benefits

Fancy neurology with practice, responsibility and personal development?

Then we look forward to your online application!
