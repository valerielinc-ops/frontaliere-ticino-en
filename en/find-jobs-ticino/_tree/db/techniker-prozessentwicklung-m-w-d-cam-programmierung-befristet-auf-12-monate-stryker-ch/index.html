Full description

Work Flexibility: Onsite

Objective/purpose of the post

  • Supports the development of production processes for new products as part of the design transfer
  • Execution of assigned technical tasks focusing on the manufacturing process and the technical documentation required to define and specify the manufacturing processes
  • Programming with CAM system and launching new products
  • Continuous improvement of processes

Main activities

  • Technical support in implementing new processes, ensuring completion and documentation of all tasks in accordance with Stryker’s new product development procedures
  • Assurance of process and product quality according to the specifications of the corresponding operating and material specifications
  • Preparation of technical documents such as work plans, parts lists, control plans or work instructions under the supervision and guidance of project engineers and/or process development specialists.
  • Supporting process development specialists in the creation of CAM/CNC and/or CMM programs and in the development of associated devices and tools
  • Setting up the manufacturing systems and running-in, qualifying and documenting the created CNC programs with all associated tasks
  • Perform process studies in collaboration with project engineers and/or process development specialists.
  • Support or implementation of the relevant qualification and validation activities on manufacturing systems
  • Carrying out maintenance and targeting maintenance plans
  • Ensuring compliance with GMP standards and safety procedures
  • Further work according to the instructions of the supervisor

Competences

  • Signature authorized in accordance with the Financial Competence Code and Stryker Quality Directives.
  • 4-year-old mechanic teaching
  • Good CNC ISO programming knowledge or good experience with a CAM software
  • Good control knowledge (e.g. Heidenhain, Siemens, Fanuc, TB-Deco)
  • Good German skills, English skills are advantageous
  • General PC knowledge (MS Word, Excel, Outlook)
  • 2+ years of professional experience
  • Experience at 5-axis milling or turning centers
  • Ability to operate and set up manufacturing machines and execute manufacturing jobs
  • Knowledge of testing of manufactured products with standard test equipment
  • Good ability to read and interpret complex drawings (including models) as well as geometric dimensioning and tolerance (including GD&T)
  • Knowledge of manufacturing processes of medical devices
  • Willingness to train in the field and to know the current industry and industry standards and trends
  • Experience with process validations is beneficial
  • Experience in medical technology is an advantage
  • Willingness to shift work if necessary

Personal Properties

  • Flexible, resilient, enduring, capable of teamwork, self-employed
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work successfully in a team-based environment that places great emphasis on quality, performance and accountability
  • Analytical, logical, solution-oriented and strategic thinking
  • Ability to efficiently handle multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment
