Store: Lausanne, VD, Switzerland. Job ID: R2363.

Happiness can be found in the smallst of things. It is our passion to turn every day routines into more memorable moments and connect with customers and colleagues. At Rituals, we blend a thiving culture with top talent. Join our fast growing well-being brand and nurturing an impactful career with us.

  • In this versatile role, you are the link between team and customer. With your Store Manager, you work on staffing plans, goals and strategies. A perfect mix of responsibilities and customer contact.
  • Your main missions
  • Working with your Store Manager: working together on staff plans, sales strategies and initiatives to make your colleagues and customers even happier.
  • Be the product expert: manage complex customer issues, demonstrate products as a pro, and embody our Feel Good Experience.
  • Managing the operational aspect: dealing with planning, inventory, cash management, and all the essential administrative tasks that ensure the operation of the shop.

  • Have at least 3 years experience in sales or customer service.
  • Don't imagine sitting behind a desk all day. You truly love retail – effervescence, variety, problem solving.
  • Be oriented results, always looking for your next victory, and naturally turn conversations into purchases.
  • Have excellent relational skills and adapt easily to different personalities – know how to inspire your team, collaborate with your Store Manager and create a relationship with customers (whether they are chatter or more reserved).
  • Have a valid work or residence permit (C, B, L or G) or Swiss nationality.
