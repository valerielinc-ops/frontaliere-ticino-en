Full description

As a specialized case manager within the registry of the Detention Facility for Minors and Young Adults in Palézieux, you will ensure the complete and autonomous management of the files of detained individuals throughout their detention journey. In this specific context, you contribute to the administrative and judicial follow-up of minors and young adults, in close collaboration with criminal authorities, institutional partners, and various sectors of the facility. You contribute to the proper functioning of the registry by ensuring the monitoring of deadlines, transfers and authorizations, as well as the production of official documents and statistics. You also provide administrative support to the management, participate in the transmission of knowledge within the team, and apply the confidentiality and security requirements specific to the penitentiary field. You act at all times in accordance with our values: "Loyalty, Solidarity, Credibility".

  • Analyze individual situations and handle all correspondence received from various criminal authorities and sectors of the facility related to the detention of individuals. Verify the legal and administrative compliance of documents;
  • Prepare files for decisions from management and authorities (releases, transfers, execution of sentences, etc.);
  • Establish, maintain, and archive individual files of detained individuals;
  • Manage visit and hearing authorizations and determine their feasibility: complete analysis of the situation and judgments, decision notice, organization, coordination, and production of associated documents;
  • Manage the management's agenda and organize appointments, meetings, seminars, and travel;
  • Take and write minutes;
  • Ensure, develop, and maintain contacts with instances, services, organizations, institutions, and individuals directly related to the activities of SPEN and the lives of detainees;
  • Ensure the training of commercial apprentices within the sector and conduct regular evaluations and grading. For more information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact the management of the facility at 021 316 83 08.
  • CFC of commercial employee or equivalent title;
  • Strong confirmed experience of at least 3 years within an entity of the criminal chain;
  • Apprentice trainer in the workplace (FPT);
  • Very good organizational skills;
  • Great discretion in handling sensitive data;
  • Excellent interpersonal skills, team spirit, and empathy;
  • Good command of French both orally and in writing;
  • Proficiency in common IT tools. Our employment conditions require that you be Swiss or holder of a C permit, exempt from acts of default of goods (an extract from the Office of Debt Collection must be presented during the process), and your criminal record must be clean. There is a right to consult the criminal record under the Federal Law on Criminal Records by the hiring authority for this position. Position within the Vaud Cantonal Administration (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
