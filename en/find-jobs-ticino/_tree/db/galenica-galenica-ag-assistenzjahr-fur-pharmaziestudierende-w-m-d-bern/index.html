Full description

Location: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

Start your assistant time exactly where it suits you – in one of our approximately 400 pharmacies throughout Switzerland. Benefit from comprehensive support from our headquarters, an internal eLearning program and the individual support of selected external training courses. We accept applications for each region. Just upload your CV, answer 2–3 short questions and we will match you quickly and easily with the ideal pharmacy.

You can do that.

  • You use your knowledge in practice every day and gain step by step more safety in everyday pharmaceutical life
  • You get to know modern laboratories and gain valuable experience in the production of Magistral recipes and medicines
  • You will further develop your consulting and triage skills – supported by experienced coaches who will accompany you through the assistant period
  • You will get exciting insights into pharmaceutical services such as vaccinations or allergy checks and learn to use them confidently.
  • Take a look behind the scenes of our logistics at Galexis and the listing at Medifilm and discover how versatile the profession really is

You bring that with you

  • You are looking for an exciting assistant position as part of your pharmacy studies
  • You bring a strong customer orientation and enjoy the exchange with people
  • You are open to learning new things, taking on responsibility in the team and actively thinking
  • You want to benefit from a wide range of training offers and take as much knowledge as possible for your further path

Ready to get started? Apply now – quickly, easily and without long searches. We will contact you within one week at the latest and find the perfect pharmacy for you together. More information about our training program can be found here.

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss healthcare market. As the Galenica Group, we are the market-defining healthcare provider in our country. Our companies offer a rich variety of future-oriented professions that have something in common: Our employees are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. For this purpose, we operate the largest pharmacy network in Switzerland and carry well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. Furthermore, we are the leading wholesaler in the Swiss healthcare market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is located in Bern, where all the threads converge. Here, valuable services are provided for the entire group, including HR, IT, marketing, communication or finance.
