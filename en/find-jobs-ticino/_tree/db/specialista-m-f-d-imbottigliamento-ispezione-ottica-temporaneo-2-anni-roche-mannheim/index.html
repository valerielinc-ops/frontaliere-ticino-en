Value streams

With the organization in value streams, we focus on the essential, value-adding activities. In addition to reducing duplicate or unnecessary work, the focus is on improving interfaces and processes between the participating organizational units.

As skilled worker (m/f/d) bottling/opt. Control - limited to 2 years you are responsible for the mechanical filling and optical control of sterile solutions in syringes. This includes the preparation, monitoring, cleaning and disinfection of the filling machine and the optical control machines.

Responsibility | This is what awaits you

  • Ensure that the filling and visual inspection of sterile solutions in syringes run smoothly, taking into account the aseptic procedure as specified in the manufacturing instructions and compliance with GMP and hygiene regulations
  • You carry out microbiological environmental controls and are responsible for troubleshooting even difficult faults at the bottling and optical control system
  • An important part of your task is also the GMP-compliant documentation and control of the work carried out in production protocols (in the future electronic), electronic logbooks and document books, etc.
  • As part of work preparation, you clean, disinfect and sterilize equipment and small parts
  • In addition, you support both in the operation of the bottle task and in qualification, validation, maintenance and repair work.
  • You are responsible for the continuous maintenance and updating of SOPs in the workspace and support the creation of GMP documents (SOPs, forms)
  • In addition, you will be involved in our CIP process to improve our workflows

Qualifications | This is what you bring
