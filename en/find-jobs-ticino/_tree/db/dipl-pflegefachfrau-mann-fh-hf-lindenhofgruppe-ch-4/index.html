Tasks

  • You support our patients in shift services with a high level of personal responsibility.
  • You carry out the care process professionally and according to current standards – supported by our clinic information system KISIM.
  • You are actively involved in the further development and optimization of our care processes.
  • In addition, you accompany and support learners and students in their education.

Requirements

  • You are a qualified nurse (AKP/DN II/HF/FH) and ideally have experience in the acute field.
  • The well-being of patients is at the heart of your work
  • You meet people with respect and appreciation.

  • Good German skills round off your profile.
