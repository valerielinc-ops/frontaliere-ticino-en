Full description

We treat people with respect and appreciation. As a holistic person, we take you seriously.

60 - 80%

As a recognized specialist clinic for psychiatry, psychotherapy and psychosomatics with a cantonal service mandate, we set standards in the treatment and care of our patients. With around 175 employees, we offer inpatient and outpatient treatments according to the bio-psycho-socio-spiritual model at various locations in German-speaking Switzerland and integrate Christian values and perspectives into our therapies on request. The synergy of expertise, personal support and holistic support makes us a valued address for people seeking support in challenging life situations.

This is what they do

  • Professional and individual care of patients on night duty
  • Close cooperation with the night watches in the house and the doctors/therapists on duty
  • Perform night watch specific tasks
  • Assistance for learners and students in practical training

No night recordings take place and our psychotherapeutic offer is aimed exclusively at patients with self-motivation for treatment.

Your profile

  • Completed training as a nurse HF
  • Interested in working with mentally ill people
  • Teamwork, resilience and flexibility
  • Christian attitude

With us you will come up.

Clinic SGM Langenthal is a recognized specialist clinic for psychiatry, psychotherapy and psychosomatics with inpatient and outpatient treatment offers. The therapy offered is integrative and involves the spiritual dimension. We are there for people who are sick or in a life crisis. For them, we create a protected framework where recovery and change are possible. We think and act holistically and professionally. The view of our patients is shaped by biological, psychological, social and spiritual aspects. We live Christian values that become visible in our dealings with our fellow human beings We are committed and responsible in what we do. Trust and appreciation towards our fellow human beings are important to us. We work together with our partners in a solution-oriented manner. Important attributes for us are reliability, quality and fairness. We focus on profitability, sustainability and a future-oriented training and education culture.
