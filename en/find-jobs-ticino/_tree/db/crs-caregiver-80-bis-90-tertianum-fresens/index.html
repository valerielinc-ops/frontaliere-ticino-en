Tertianum Chantevent in Fresens search for

ASSISTANT: CRS (80% to 90%) in CDI

Located in a green setting in Fresens , Tertianum Chantevent offers a peaceful place of life with stunning views of Lake Neuchâtel. Specializing in the reception of elderly people for long stays, the establishment has 35 medical beds.

Your missions

  • Ensure the comfort and well-being of residents on a daily basis;
  • Carry out hygiene and comfort care in collaboration with the nursing team;
  • Observe and transmit important information on the health status of residents;
  • Promote self-reliance and contribute to the quality of life by offering appropriate activities;
  • Participate in the social and relational life of residents, creating warm moments;
  • Collaborate with a committed multidisciplinary team and participate in innovative projects.

Your profile

  • Certificate of compulsory or equivalent Red Cross care;
  • Experience confirmed in geriatrics or psychogeriatrics;
  • Mastery of oral and written French;
  • Listening, benevolence and team spirit;
  • Motivation to work in a human and stimulating environment;
  • No one's being transported.

Conditions

  • Hours: from 8:12 a.m., working days vary according to the activity rate.
