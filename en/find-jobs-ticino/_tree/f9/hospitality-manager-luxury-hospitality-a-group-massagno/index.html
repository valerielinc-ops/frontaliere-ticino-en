Company Description

A++ Group, an international company headquartered in Massagno and active in architecture, real estate, design, construction and hospitality, is looking for a Hospitality Manager with operational, organizational and economic responsibilities on an exclusive portfolio of properties.

Position

Hospitality Manager – Luxury Hospitality

Requirements

Main responsibilities

  • Operational and strategic management of the properties.
  • Coordination and supervision of personnel.
  • Management and development of the Booking & Revenue team.
  • Organisation of resources and optimization of operational processes.
  • Control of budgets, costs, revenues and profitability.
  • Monitoring major operational and economic KPIs and defining corrective actions.
  • Supervision of service standards and guest experience.
  • Supervision of pricing, distribution strategies, Revenue Management and sales strategies.
  • Management of relations with suppliers, partners and external collaborators.
  • Processing of directional reports and presentation of results to the property.
  • Ability to start and manage new facilities, developing effective Guest Relations strategies and ensuring a high quality of guest experience.

Profile searched

  • Proven experience and availability to provide professional references in the management of hotels, resorts, villas or luxury facilities.
  • Experience in Cluster / Multi-Property Management.
  • Comprehensive management skills and leadership of managers and multidisciplinary teams.
  • Solid budgeting skills, cost control, operations and performance analysis.
  • Ability to process reports and present results to the property.
  • Leadership, autonomy, strategic vision and strong orientation to the result.
  • High attention to quality and guest experience.
  • Excellent knowledge of Italian and English
  • German knowledge is a plus.

Other information

We offer

  • Indefinite Contract
  • Sept./October
  • Management role with wide autonomy and direct responsibility
  • Insertion into a project Luxury Hospitality booming
  • Remuneration and incentive package for experience and skills
