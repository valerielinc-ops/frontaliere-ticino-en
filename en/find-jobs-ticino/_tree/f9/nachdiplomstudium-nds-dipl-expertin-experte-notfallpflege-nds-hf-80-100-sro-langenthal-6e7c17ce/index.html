Full description

We offer the postgraduate degree program NDS Emergency Care What you do with us

  • Professionally high-level care and care of our emergency patients of all ages (including pediatric)
  • They are responsible for the physical and psychological well-being as well as for the safety of patients
  • You will learn the first care of critically ill or injured patients
  • You will acquire skills in the triage of emergency patients according to ESI and in due course assume the function of shift management of the emergency center
  • Electronic patient documentation management
  • Promoting interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Diploma degree in nursing at tertiary level (HF or FH) or recognised foreign diploma in nursing
  • At least 1 year of professional experience in the acute care sector of a Swiss hospital
  • Resilience, a flair for technical concerns and autonomous ability to act
  • Solid personal and social competence as well as motivated learning behaviour and willingness to persevere
  • Individual and learning-promoting supervision and support, theoretical lessons at the Bernese Education Center Nursing in Bern, as well as further lessons at SRO AG (in the form of learning transfer training, clinical teaching, AIN simulation training and emergency-specific Mega Code training)
  • Internships in our cooperation hospitals (in the University Emergency Center of the Inselspital Bern and in the Emergency Center for Children and Adolescents Inselspital Bern)
  • Insight internships in the anesthesia, the intensive care unit and the rescue service of SRO AG
  • Current employment conditions according to GAV
  • Responsible and interesting work in a team SRO AG Spital Langenthal, St. Urbanstrasse 67, CH-4900 Langenthal T 062 916 31 31, F 062 916 31 12, info@sro.ch, www.sro.ch
