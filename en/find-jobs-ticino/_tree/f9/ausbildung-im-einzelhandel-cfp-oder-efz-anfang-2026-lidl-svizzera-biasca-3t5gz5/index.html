Full description

Introduction Do you have any ideas for after you graduate from school? Start your future with us and take advantage of the development opportunities at Lidl Switzerland. The branches are our center of activity. As an apprentice in retail (focus on designing shopping experiences), whether EBA or EFZ, you contribute to the success of your Lidl branch. Start/End EBA: August 1st, 2026 - July 31st, 2028 Start/End EFZ: August 1st, 2026 - July 31st, 2029

  • Your tasks
  • Operating cash registers and creating invoices
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Clearing goods and presenting goods
  • Checking the receipt of goods in the warehouse and supporting your team with inventories
  • Support in the planning and implementation of campaigns and seasonal placements
  • Determining the need for goods and ordering the goods in the 3rd year of training (EFZ)
  • Taking over the first shift management in the 3rd year of training (EFZ)
  • Your profile
  • An average to very good high school diploma
  • Foreign language A1 for EBA and at least A2 for EFZ
  • Enjoyment of contact with people
  • Independent way of working and quick comprehension
  • Ability to work in a team, reliability and sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and work hard
  • Physical Resilience
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • We offer an apprenticeship that has many options open to you. After successfully completing your apprenticeship and if you have good personal suitability, we will develop you into a management position in the branch. You also benefit from the Lidl GAV and many attractive benefits.
