Full description

Mission

We are offering a full-time (100%) postdoc position at the Laboratory for the Chemistry of Materials and Manufacturing (ALCHEMY) at EPFL Neuchatel. At EPFL ALCHEMY, we are focused on developing accessible chemistries and processing strategies that can enable the fabrication of a wide variety of advanced functional materials and devices.

We are seeking a candidate with experience either in a) the development of (macro)monomers for the synthesis of microstructured polymers or b) the development of novel photopolymerization/photocrosslinking strategies.

Main duties and responsibilities

As a postdoc, you will be expected to

  • Have full responsibility for your own research
  • Provide mentorship on chemical synthesis and characterization
  • Research in close collaboration with other lab members and international collaborators
  • Experiment design and execution
  • Analyze and interpret experimental results
  • Write scientific articles for publication in peer-reviewed journals
  • Present at international conferences

Profile

  • A PhD degree in the fields of chemistry or polymer science. Materials science is possible if the candidate has the required experience listed below
  • Experience with small molecule synthesis and characterization is required
  • Experience with polymer characterization is required
  • At least 2 peer-reviewed scientific articles
  • Excellent comunication skills in English

We offer

  • A 1-year fixed-term contract (CDD), renewable.
  • Competitive remuneration
  • International collaboration and visiting opportunities
  • A world-class research and training environment with access to state-of-the-art research facilities

Informations

Only applications submitted through the online platform are considered . Please apply by uploading a single PDF file that contains the following:

  • Cover/Motivation Letter that discuss your fit for the position (1 page).
  • Detailed CV
  • A research statement that discusses two novel small molecules and/or polymers that you have synthesized and characterized (3 pages maximum)
  • Names and contact information of at least 2 references willing to write recommendation letters for you

For any further information, please contact: daryl.yee@epfl.ch
