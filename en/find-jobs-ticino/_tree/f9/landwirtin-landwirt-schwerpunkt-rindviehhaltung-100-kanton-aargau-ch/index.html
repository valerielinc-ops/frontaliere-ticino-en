Exciting tasks await

  • Responsible for cattle rearing/milk farming
  • Independent care of the dairy herd (40 GVE) incl. Breeding in the 2 team
  • Collaboration in other branches of the agricultural holding (farming/feeding, vineyards, fruit growing, etc.)
  • Guidance for learners and support staff

What you bring

  • Completed training as a farmer with federal law. Certificate of competence
  • Enjoyment of animal husbandry and practical work experience in the field of animal husbandry
  • Good physical condition, independence, reliability and critical ability
  • Willingness to provide irregular services (in particular weekend services and assignments on public holidays). A 100% fee corresponds to a 42-hour week
  • Minimum age: 25 years, good repute (current extract from the penal and enforcement register)

Your working environment
