Are you looking for a new, responsible challenge in an exciting environment? Then bring your knowledge and commitment. For our operations in northeast Switzerland, we are looking for a dedicated personality for commissioning in Axpo’s distribution network and customers’ plants. We provide an environment for independent work with comprehensive technical equipment, including a company vehicle, which can optionally be used privately.

Tasks & Responsibilities

Commissioning of protective equipment and control systems in the substations and power plants on site

Control and conduct of periodic maintenance tests of protective systems

Setting/parameterizing protection and control systems

Support for error analysis and troubleshooting in the context of protective technology

Participation in KVP and Q-Circle Measures in the field of engineering and commissioning

Profile & Requirements

You are in possession of a successfully completed degree or comparable qualification in electrical engineering

You have experience in the parameterization, testing and commissioning of protection and control systems in the environment of medium and high-voltage systems

You have an independent, efficient and responsible way of working and have a high demand on your work quality

You have a good knowledge of German (English/French skills are an advantage)About the team:We offer you a team-oriented environment with a collegial working environment, where you can actively contribute your strengths and contribute to the success of Axpo. You will be comprehensively introduced to your responsible and versatile tasks.
