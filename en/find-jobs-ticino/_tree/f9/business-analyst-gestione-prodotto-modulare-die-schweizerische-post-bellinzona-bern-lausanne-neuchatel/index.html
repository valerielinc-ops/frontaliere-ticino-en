Full description

Do you want to turn grown product logics into an understandable, modular and reusable model? Do you want to help shape how products, product offerings, services and other product components will be mapped in the product management system in the future and managed over the entire life cycle?

For one of our teams, we are looking for a committed and analytical personality who likes to permeate complex relationships, bring different perspectives together and develop viable solutions together with others. With you, we create the basis for a consistent, modular product model and its consistent use.

You can do that.

  • You collect and structure requirements from product management, sales, operations and IT and bring together different terms and perspectives in a consistent professional model.
  • You break down reference products into reusable product, service and resource building blocks and document properties, variants, rules, dependencies and responsibilities.
  • You model target processes for product development and product maintenance, states, events and business information objects and derive epics, features, user stories and acceptance criteria from them.
  • In addition, you make sure that the product logic for offer, contract, pricing, billing, customer channels and operational systems is clearly usable.
  • As a link between departments and IT, you work closely with product owners, product management, architecture and DevOps teams and accompany the implementation of professional requirements until successful validation and acceptance.
  • In addition, you will maintain the traceability of the target image and design principles up to the implemented requirements and the validated reference products.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of experience in business analysis, requirements engineering or product data/process modeling in a complex IT environment.
  • You also have experience with modular product models, product catalogues, PIM, CPQ/CPQC or comparable product management solutions.
  • You have knowledge of process, information and domain modeling as well as experience with user stories, acceptance criteria and professional tests.
  • In addition, you convince with a structured working method, moderation strength and the ability to process complex product logics in an understandable and decision-making manner.
  • You are interested in technical contexts and understand how products, processes, systems and data are linked.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German and English. Every other national language is a plus.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
