Field Service Engineer Where do people love what they do and are great at it? At Swisslog, of course! Our teams are at the heart of a world-leading data- and robot-driven company that harnesses the power of technology to shape the future of intralogistics. The result? We deliver performance and efficiency to our customers around the world, giving them the decisive advantage in their markets. Job description Here you do something

  • You carry out maintenance, repairs, conversions and troubleshooting at our logistics facilities - independently and in a team
  • You ensure the satisfaction of our internal and external customers
  • You analyze the condition of existing plants and support the installation of new systems
  • Your area of application includes Switzerland and neighbouring countries
  • Occasionally you are also in use in other European countries

Applicant profile This is what you bring with you

  • Completed mechanical training (e.g. polymechanic, mechanic, production mechanic, locksmith)
  • Ideally, professional experience as a service technician, preferably in the field of pallet or container conveyor technology
  • You like to work independently and use your knowledge flexibly and safely according to the changing requirements
  • You have good PC skills and document your work cleanly and completely
  • Flexible working hours are a varied and exciting challenge for your job
  • You are dizzy and generally consider yourself healthy
  • Category B driving licence
  • Good English skills are required
  • Knowledge of French is not mandatory, but it is an advantage
