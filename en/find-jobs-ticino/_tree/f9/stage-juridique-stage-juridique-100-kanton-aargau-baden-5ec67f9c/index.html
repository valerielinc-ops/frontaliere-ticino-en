Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Preparation of negotiations and processing of treated transactions
  • Reading files, making legal clarifications and recording negotiations
  • Prepare documents (protocols, comparisons, calculation tables, etc.)
  • Editing of judgments - in the dispositive, - with reasoning
  • Participation (with recording) in negotiations, hearings, inspections, etc.

What you bring

  • Completed law degree (lic. iur./ MLaw) or shortly before graduation
  • Interest and ability to deal with complex legal issues in a solution-oriented manner
  • Precise, forward-looking, reliable and independent work and good editorial skills
  • Rapid comprehension
  • Legal, administrative or legal practice beneficial

The courts make judgments every day in a wide variety of areas of law.

The focus is on an efficient and citizen-friendly justice system for peaceful coexistence in the Canton of Aargau.
