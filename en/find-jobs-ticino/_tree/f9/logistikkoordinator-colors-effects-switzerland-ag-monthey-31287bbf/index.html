Logistics Coordinator (m/f/d)

About us

We give color to the world! Chemical is a member of the CIS Group and is a world leader in printing inks and pigments. Present in 63 countries, more than 22,000 employees work on 250 sites every day to serve many industries, from cosmetics and packaging to agriculture and automobiles. Together, we have an annual turnover of approximately US$8.5 billion and are developing innovative and sustainable solutions with our customers.

Integrated with the Supply Chain service of our Colors \&Effects site in Monthey, you will be responsible for coordinating material management activities as well as administrative monitoring of import and export declarations.

Your main missions

  • You set up export documents in collaboration with our partners, create export declarations in Passar, and archive the documentation.
  • You coordinate with the different users the input material streams on the site and are involved in the import formalities of these.
  • You are responsible for various administrative tasks related to the logistics activities of the Monthey site.
  • You ensure the control, imputation and tracking of invoices for both import and export logistics activities.

PROFILE RESEARCH

  • You have an SWC of a business employee or an equivalent training.
  • You have a minimum experience of 3 years in the field of logistics/supply chain related to chemicals.
  • You have a good mastery of MS Office and SAP software.
  • You master French and English perfectly.
  • You are organized and methodical, you quickly adapt to priorities and you know how to put your professionalism at the service of teamwork.

Chemical lives diversity and, with all employees, we are actively committed to equal opportunities. Therefore, we follow the principle that candidates are selected and engaged solely on the basis of their individual performance and competence in relation to the position to be filled.

If you are interested in this position, please send your complete file (motivation letter, curriculum vitae, copy of your diplomas, work certificates and certificates of continuing training).
