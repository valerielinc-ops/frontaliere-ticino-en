Become part of a dedicated team! If you want to contribute your expertise in a holistic and interdisciplinary environment and support people in actively and self-determined participation in everyday life, you are exactly right with us. Your tasks – meaningful and versatile

  • You design physiotherapeutic treatments in single and small group setting.
  • You support our residents in developing or maintaining their mobility and participation in everyday life.
  • You accompany the supplies of aids from clarification of needs to application in everyday life.
  • You will advise caregivers and relatives on mobility, storage, transfers and other physiotherapeutic questions.
  • You work interdisciplinary with occupational therapy, speech therapy, social pedagogy, specialists and specialists from orthopaedic and rehabilitation technology.

You bring with you

  • A completed degree in physiotherapy (BSc FH or equivalent recognised degree; for foreign diplomas with SRC recognition).
  • Experience in neurology is beneficial.
  • An independent, structured and responsible way of working.
  • Joy of accompanying people with physical and multiple impairments.
  • Teamwork, resilience, flexibility, creativity and humor.

You can look forward to that.

  • A versatile activity with a noticeable effect in the everyday life of the accompanied people.
  • A dedicated, interdisciplinary team.
  • Space for personal initiative and professional development.
  • Contemporary employment conditions with development opportunities.
  • Attractive employee benefits via brands for employees.
  • Daily fresh lunch food at attractive conditions.
  • Good accessibility with public transport and the possibility to rent a parking space.
