At pfister, we pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today – will you join soon?

For our team in Category Management Food we are looking for a junior buyer (m/f/d) for our headquarters in Suhr.

Your new tasks

  • Capture and mutation of our range of tables & chairs on our SAP and DIVA systems
  • Preparation of sales documents and assortment grid
  • Preparation and maintenance of data to strengthen the online shop
  • Ongoing contact with our suppliers and branches
  • Administrative handling of our advertising inputs
  • Support of the buyer in the assortment planning
  • After thorough incorporation, gradual adoption of its own product group
  • Planning, development and procurement of innovative, market- and profit-oriented products in close cooperation with the Senior Buyer
  • Analyze and realize purchase-relevant KPIs such as sales, margin, warehouses
  • Purchasing activities at home and abroad (supplier visits, trade fairs)
  • Close cooperation with internal and external interfaces such as visual merchandising, marketing, branches, e-commerce and suppliers
  • Commercial education with business education
  • several years of professional experience in the field of purchasing in the Swiss retail/consumer goods market
  • Rapid comprehension, structured and reliable working methods and strong networked thinking
  • Very good MS Office knowledge, especially Excel, SAP knowledge are a plus
  • Good knowledge of English
  • Initiative, a healthy assertiveness and responsible
  • Team player with passion and motivation
