Full description

The Municipality of Collina d'Oro opens the competition for the recruitment of a student who is attending the 3rd year of the Middle School of Commerce and needs 52 weeks of commercial practice to obtain the federal certificate of competence and the commercial professional baccalaureate.

School year

Place: at the Municipal Administration, under the conditions of the Organic Regulations of Employees (ROD) and this notice of competition.

General requirements

Those provided for by the Staff Regulations for Employees and in particular:

  • Swiss citizenship, the application of international agreements is reserved;
  • irreproachable moral conduct and physical constitution compatible with the function;
  • good communication skills;
  • proactive attitude, precision and reliability;
  • knowledge of the Office package.

Tasks

The selected person will work at the counter and switchboard of the Administration, with frequent direct contact with users. He will also carry out administrative procedures related to the counter and stationery and, on the recommendation of colleagues, will also support other municipal services. Excellent interpersonal skills, courtesy, predisposition to contact with the public and a positive and collaborative attitude are required.

Salary conditions

Monthly salary (plus 13th month's salary) CHF 1,300.00, from the 7th month and CHF 1,500.00, subject to internal evaluation and whether the set goals have been achieved.

Entry into service

From 1 July or other date to be agreed

Degree of employment and working time

100%, from Monday to Friday, according to the work schedule established by the chancellery. Working hours are 40 hours per week. Availability to work on Saturdays and Sundays may also be required, on the occasion of operational needs or specific events.

Documents to be submitted

  • cover letter;
  • curriculum vitae with recent photograph;
  • school reports from the Middle School of Commerce
  • Montagnola, 23 October 2026 Notice of competition specifications for the recruitment of an intern for the achievement of the commercial professional high school diploma (52 weeks) at the Municipality of Collina d'Oro
  • self-certification relating to the criminal record (). The original document "extract from the criminal record

will be requested at the time of scheduling the interview (validity of the document 6 months from issue);

  • self-certification of the state of health (*) (), certifying the aptitude for work related to the function;
  • copy of the permit, if foreigners.

(*) The Municipality reserves the right to request the official report of the attending physician. () The form is available on the Municipality website in the "Competitions" section.

Competition deadline

The applications, in a sealed envelope with the words «Competition specifications for the hiring of an intern to obtain the professional commercial high school diploma (52 weeks) at the Municipality of Collina d'Oro», must be received at the Chancellery in Montagnola in Piazza Brocchi 2, 6926 Montagnola, by Thursday 25 March 2027 at 11.00. The Municipality reserves the right to request other documents when examining the offers, to proceed with a pre-selection or not to proceed with the hiring if there are no suitable candidates. For any questions or clarifications you can contact the Deputy Secretary, Mrs. Tatiana Balmelli-Lucchini on 091 986 46 46 or via email at ufficiopersonale@collinadoro.swiss. The assignment will take place based on the provisions of the ROD and at the exclusive and unquestionable judgment of the Municipality which reserves the right as of now to cancel the competition should the applications not satisfy the required needs. Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
