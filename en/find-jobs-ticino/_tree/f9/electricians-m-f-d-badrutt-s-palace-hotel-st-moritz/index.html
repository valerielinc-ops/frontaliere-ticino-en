Full description

What you can do with us

  • As an electroinstaller, you are responsible for repairs and smaller electrical installations in the hotel and in our employee accommodation and thus contribute to smooth operation.
  • They control and maintain our electrical systems, including lighting, energy distribution and emergency power supply, and ensure their safe and efficient operation.
  • At events you support the technical setup (e.g. audio, light and video technology) and ensure that all electrical
  • requirements are professionally implemented.
  • You carry out regular checks in our guest rooms and public areas and thus contribute to a safe and comfortable stay of our guests.
  • In addition, you participate in snow clearing and are part of the fire safety group to guarantee the safety of our guests and employees.
  • They take over sporadic piket services and ensure fast and reliable support in case of technical faults.
  • Your profile

For this varied position, you ideally bring the following

  • They are a service-oriented, efficient and reliable personality with high self-response.
  • Completed vocational training as an electro fitter, an electro fitter or a comparable qualification.
  • Approval as an NIV 13.
  • professional experience in a larger hotel or similar environment.
  • Good German and oral language skills in Italian, English skills are of benefit.
  • High sense of responsibility and an independent, precise working method.
  • load capacity as well as strong team and solution orientation.

Our offer

  • Permanent office
  • 5-day week on average at 43.5 hours
  • 5 weeks holidays and 6 holidays per year
  • 13. Salary from entrance (per stage of the course)
  • On request at the beginning accommodation in one of our beautiful staff houses
  • Food in our new, modern staff restaurant
  • An attractive and international working environment in the luxury hotel industry

If you appreciate an interesting, demanding and versatile activity in a dynamic environment and want to join a well-trained team, then we look forward to your complete online application.
