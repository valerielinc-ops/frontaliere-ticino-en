Full description

We are looking for a reinforcement for our team in Basel, who with passion for quality and precision ensures our high quality standards and thus makes an important contribution to our innovative medical technology products.

Address these values, then apply now as a Quality Inspector. The position is limited in time for 6 months.

Quality Inspector 100%

Your responsibilities

Perform all tests in the value chain to ensure the quality standard of our products Support for Quality Engineering and Q-Engineering Implementation responsibility for the individual quality checks according to specifications Completion of the necessary documentation in coordination with the Q technician of the production cells Steering of defective products in coordination with the Q technician of the production cell Preparation of quality deviation reports for internal and external purposes Implementing responsibility for lot release (DHR)

  • Your qualifications

Technical or commercial training completed Technical background is advantageous Quality verification training or comparable practical experience of several years Very good German in word and writing (B2), English is advantageous MS Office, SAP, CAQ (User Knowledge)

About Medartis

Medartis is one of the leading manufacturers of medical products for osteosynthesis in the area of the facial skull and extremities. For the benefit of the patient, Medartis is committed to providing surgeons and surgical staff with the most innovative titanium implants and instruments and providing the best service.
