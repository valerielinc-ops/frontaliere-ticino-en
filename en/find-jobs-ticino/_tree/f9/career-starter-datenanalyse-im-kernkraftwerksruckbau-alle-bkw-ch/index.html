Full description

Do you want to participate in the dismantling of the first decommissioned Swiss nuclear power plant? In the 18-month Career Starter Program in Mühleberg, you will analyze radiological measurement data and gain special practical experience for your further career. Start: 1 March 2027.

Your tasks

  • You evaluate measurement data and technical information for the radiological characterization of repository containers, check results for plausibility and document assumptions
  • You support the quality assurance of the data and help to further develop evaluations with digital tools and specialist software
  • You work with specialists from the fields of radiation protection, disposal and dismantling and get to know their processes
  • You document your results in a comprehensible and well-founded manner in technical reports. These form an important basis for the official documentation of the repository containers

You bring that with you

  • You have completed a university degree at master level in physics, chemistry, computer science, mathematics, engineering or a related natural science or technical discipline
  • You are interested in the analysis of radiological measurement data and bring an affinity for programming
  • You have a basic understanding of statistical methods and work carefully and structured
  • You are curious, willing to learn and enjoy working with experts from different fields
  • You have good knowledge of German (minimum level B2) and/or very good knowledge of English (minimum level C1)

We do not want requests from personnel service providers.
