The Position We are looking for a dedicated and reliable production expert for the powder filling of our proven antibiotic Rocephin. In this important role, you contribute to ensuring that our patients worldwide have access to high-quality, life-saving medicines. As part of a self-organized team, the production expert in the Rocephin plant is responsible for the GMP-compliant production of sterile medicines. This includes the implementation and coordination of all activities on the production lines, the complete and correct documentation and accounting of production orders and the monitoring of compliance with all relevant regulations.

The possibility

  • Independent cooperation in the production of sterile medicines on a highly technical production line as part of a self-organized team
  • Operation of production and inspection facilities
  • Ensuring the quality of production steps through GMP-compliant work
  • Provision of primary packaging and GMP-compliant documentation using electronic systems
  • Cleaning of production facilities, filling equipment and premises
  • Implementation of line clearance and IPC sample trains
  • Transport and disposal of goods
  • Reporting of faulty conditions of equipment or materials used (GMP, quality, safety)
  • Assist in activities during disruptions in cooperation with interfaces such as technology or the compliance team

Who you are

In order to succeed in this exciting role, you ideally bring the following qualifications:

  • Completed training as a chemical and pharmacologist:in, pharmacist:in or similar, with at least two years of relevant professional experience in the pharmaceutical industry (bottling or visual inspection)
  • GMP knowledge, as well as technical and IT basic understanding are required
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Communication skills and a high level of commitment to the job and the employer
  • Solution-oriented and creative thinking, as well as conscientious, systematic and precise work
  • Readiness to work across plants and divisions (polyvalence)
