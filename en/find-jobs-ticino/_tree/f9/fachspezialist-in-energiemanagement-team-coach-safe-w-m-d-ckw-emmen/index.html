Full description

Pensum: 80-100 %

You can design it.

  • You will accompany service customers in setting up and setting up their ZEV and VEWA objects and advise them reliably.
  • You ensure smooth operation throughout the entire lifecycle and coordinate cooperation with customers, suppliers, local distribution operators, ownership and administration.
  • You analyze data deviations, develop suitable solutions and ensure high data and process quality – from the maintenance of master and measurement data to invoicing.
  • You take on responsibility in optimization and process improvement projects and promote knowledge transfer through training in the team and with customers.
  • You accompany the agile team as a team coach: You promote lean-agile principles according to SAFe, a trusting cooperation and the continuous improvement of working methods.
  • You will moderate team events such as planning, reviews and retrospectives, support obstacles and conflicts and coach team members and product owners in cooperation with stakeholders.

This is what makes you

  • You have a recognised degree at tertiary level, for example a federal professional certificate or a bachelor's degree in technical and/or commercial direction.
  • You have experience as a Scrum Master, Team Coach or in a comparable agile role
  • Knowledge of SAFe is especially welcome.
  • You enjoy moderating workshops and team events, communicating appreciatively and finding constructive solutions for challenges together with others.
  • You work structured, customer-oriented and enjoy data-driven processes and different interfaces.
  • Ideally, you will know the energy industry as well as the ZEV/VEWA environment or the incidental cost accounting.
  • You communicate very well in German and English.
