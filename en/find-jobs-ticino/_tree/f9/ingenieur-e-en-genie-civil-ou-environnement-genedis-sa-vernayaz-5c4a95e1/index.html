Genedis is an active company from central Valais to the Vaudois East in the energy and multimedia sectors. It is made up of more than 210 collaborators and training staff from a variety of fields such as electricity, new renewable energies, water management and telecommunications.

Civil engineering or environmental engineer

Description of post

Occupancy rate: 100%

Onward: Onward or to be agreed

Workplace: Vernayaz

Your Activities

  • Conduct studies on the planning and development of drinking water infrastructure
  • Develop pre-projects, construction projects and implementation projects in the fields of civil engineering and hydraulics
  • Prepare and coordinate procedures for requests for building authorizations and invitations to tender
  • Provide general direction of work in the fields of civil engineering and hydraulics
  • Contribute to the implementation of quality assurance systems and the operation of municipal drinking water supply infrastructures
  • Representing the contractor and ensuring coordination with the authorities, administrative services and various agents

Profile sought

Your profile and skills

  • Engineer EPF/HES in civil engineering or environment, or equivalent training
  • Confirmed experience in water management, including the conduct of projects related to drinking water infrastructure
  • Knowledge of legal requirements on foodstuffs, SVGW directives and SIA standards
  • Experience in the areas of remediation and/or direction of work, an asset
  • Independent, rigorous person with a strong sense of responsibility and capable of integrating economic issues into the conduct of projects
  • Driver's licence category B

We offer you exciting work in a company where continuous development and technological intelligence play an important role. Join a dynamic team and contribute to the development of its activities!
