Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP is responsible for conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health. It offers an integrated and community psychiatry, in partnership with local institutions. The NPC is also a reference centre for training and research in psychiatry. The institution has more than 800 employees, spread over several sites. The CUP, composed of a multidisciplinary team of approximately 26 EFAs, is responsible for the evaluation and management of psychiatric emergencies at the Neuchâtel and La Chaux-de-Fonds sites. It allows rapid response to crisis situations and short-term outpatient follow-up.
