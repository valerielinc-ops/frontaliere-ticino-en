Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Cantonal Tax Administration (KSTV) assesses and obtains the cantonal taxes as well as the direct federal tax of natural and legal persons. For the latter, it also levies the church tax. On the basis of a corresponding agreement, it collects the taxes of numerous municipalities and parishes.

Their tasks

The main task of the withholding tax department is the collection and control of the withholding tax deduction, which is carried out by the employer (SSL) on the income of foreign employees.

  • Taxation of income subject to withholding tax, in particular for foreign persons who do not have a residence permit
  • Contact with employers, other debtors of the taxable benefit and the persons concerned
