Full description

To complement our team, we will be looking for a leader with numbers flair from 1 January 2027 or by agreement. Its tasks include:

  • Accounting management
  • Managing a team of three employees
  • Conducting team meetings and employee assessments
  • Responsible for the accounting of the Office of Governor and the District Council
  • Preparation of monthly turnover reports, interim and annual accounts, budget and wage budget, statistics
  • Accounting for incoming payments and incoming invoices
  • Keeping the cash register and the cash register records
  • Implementation of the payment order
  • Operation and bankruptcy
  • Ensuring Internal Control System (ICS)
  • Management of loss certificates (conclusion of payment agreements)
  • Debt collection
  • Correspondence related to the management of loss certificates and debt collection
  • Concept development
  • Participation in working groups
  • Logging This is what you bring with you:
  • Several years of leadership experience
  • Completed commercial training with further training in accounting / accounting
  • Very good knowledge of accounting in a public administration (especially Juris, SAP)
  • Several years of professional experience ideally also in the debt collection sector incl. Loss note management (good knowledge of SchKG)
  • Good editorial skills
  • Good IT user knowledge (Juris / Helium)
  • Friendly, customer-oriented action with good manners
  • Rapid comprehension as well as exact, forward-looking and independent working methods
