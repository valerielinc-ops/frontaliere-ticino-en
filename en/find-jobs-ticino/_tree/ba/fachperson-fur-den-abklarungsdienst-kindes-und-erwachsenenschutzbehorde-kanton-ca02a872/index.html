Specialist for the Child and Adult Protection Service

Child and adult protection authority Graubünden \| 60\-100% \| Chur

Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the cantonal administration Graubünden together with us.

The child and adult protection authority KESB is there for people in need. Those who need help and support can count on KESB. There is no day like the other. In our interdisciplinary team, we actively shape a secure future for vulnerable adults, families, children and young people.

We are looking for the KESB Graubünden, Branch Northbünden per 1\. January 2027 or by arrangement a expert for the clarification service. *

Your responsibilities

  • With great independence and autonomy, supported in a service with a great deal of expertise, case analyses must be prepared with appropriate application to the authority
  • Contact and conversations with directly affected persons and relatives, often directly in their living space
  • Situational adjustment of the individual clarification steps while respecting the legal basis and in consultation with the procedure management
  • Obtaining relevant information from experts, institutions and offices involved
  • Clarification of subsidiary and complementary support offers
  • Implementation of adapted crisis interventions

That is what we want from you.

  • Degree in Social Work BSc/FH/HFS or equivalent education
  • Experience in the areas of child and adult protection or willingness to receive further education
  • Very high communicative and social skills with a broad repertoire of methods
  • Empathy towards people in difficult life situations
  • Analytical skills to structure, define complex social situations and initiate necessary interventions
  • Reliable and resilient, with a great sense of responsibility
  • Independent, fast-learning and networked-thinking personality
  • Flexible with high service orientation and teamwork in an interdisciplinary context

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to shape the future of the canton
