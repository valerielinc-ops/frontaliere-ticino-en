General information Code 2026-245 DESCRIPTION OF THE AUTHORITY Job Title Product Innovation Manager 80-100% m/f/d Description of tasks

  • Identification and development of innovation potential through the analysis of trends, technologies, markets, materials and business models across all four product categories.
  • Leading and supporting innovation initiatives – from ideation and validation to transfer to the value creation process.
  • Translation of findings and results into Opportunity Briefs, concepts, experiments, pilot projects and clear recommendations for action.
  • Assess innovation opportunities in terms of attractiveness (desirability), viability, feasibility and sustainability, including customer needs, business potential, technical feasibility and sustainability impact.
  • Moderation of cross-sectoral collaboration and structured innovation formats to align stakeholders, create a common understanding and accelerate decision-making processes.
  • Control of internal and external idea management activities – from the evaluation and structuring of ideas to the identification of promising opportunities and their transfer to innovation projects or exploration formats.
  • Building and maintaining knowledge resources in the innovation field, such as material libraries, scouting reports, benchmark references, templates, playbooks and knowledge management tools.
  • Evaluating and supporting the introduction of new technologies, digital tools and AI-enabled innovation methods. Profile
  • Bachelor degree in innovation management, engineering, business administration/strategy, industrial or product design or a comparable field of expertise.
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in innovation, product development, product management, design or technology scouting, ideally in the consumer goods industry.
  • Experience leading cross-divisional innovation initiatives, workshops and stakeholder alignment processes.
  • Strong analytical, strategic and problem-solving skills, as well as the ability to translate insights into concrete business opportunities.
  • In-depth knowledge of innovation frameworks, agile methods, prototyping, business model development and product development processes.
  • Experience in trend, technology, market, material or future scouting.
  • Good understanding of sustainability aspects in product development, including materials, durability, circular economy and trade-offs.
  • Excellent communication, moderation and stakeholder management skills across different disciplines.
  • Entrepreneurial, collaborative and customer-oriented mindset with a high level of curiosity for new technologies, digital tools and AI applications.
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English (at least C1) in word and writing. Benefits
