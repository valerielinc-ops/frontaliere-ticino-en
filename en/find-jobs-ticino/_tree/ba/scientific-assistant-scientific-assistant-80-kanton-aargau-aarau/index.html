Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Management of the Political Affairs and Projects Unit
  • Coordination and management of the department’s political affairs, including timetables, reports and statements, and responses to political initiatives
  • Management of legislative revisions in the area of civil protection and management or cooperation of projects and sub-projects
  • Collaboration in the preparation of the annual report and the task and financial plan of the department
  • Advising and supporting the head of the department, the heads of the department

What you bring

  • Completed studies in humanities, politics or law
  • Stylistic German and fluency in written expression
  • Knowledge of cantonal administration and political processes in the Canton of Aargau is an advantage
  • Experience in the development of decision-making bases and reports
  • Rapid comprehension and precise, independent and structured work

The population is at the heart of the activities of the Department of Health and Social Affairs. For the safety of their areas of life, we work sustainably and purposefully. The Military and Civil Protection Department performs tasks in the fields of military service, military service substitutes and civil and civil protection. We are also the administrative hub for the members of the army and civil protection in the canton of Aargau. The employees are responsible for the training and coordination of missions within the framework of civil protection.
