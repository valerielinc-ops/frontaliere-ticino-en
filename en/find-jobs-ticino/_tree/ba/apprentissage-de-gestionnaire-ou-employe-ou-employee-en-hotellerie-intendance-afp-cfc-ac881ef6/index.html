Description of post

At the Crans-Montana Clinic, the hotel service team contributes daily to the comfort, hospitality and well-being of patients. It is responsible for the maintenance of rooms and premises according to the standards of hospital hygiene in force, participates in the service of meals in the dining room and in the cafeteria, and ensures a pleasant and quality environment. As an apprentice or apprentice manager in hospitality and stewardship, you have to discover and carry out many activities in different sectors of the institution. You are involved in cleaning and maintenance, serving meals and drinks, managing laundry, receiving patients at the reception and in certain cooking preparation activities. You also perform various administrative tasks, including inventory management and orders. The diversity of the missions proposed and the requirements specific to the hospital environment allow you to benefit from comprehensive and varied training, encouraging the development of both technical and relational skills.
