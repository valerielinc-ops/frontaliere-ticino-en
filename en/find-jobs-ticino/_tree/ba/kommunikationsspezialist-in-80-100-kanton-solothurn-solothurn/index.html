Full description

In the area of communication and media, the police canton of Solothurn informs the public about police events, the diverse tasks of the police and strengthens the confidence of the population in the organization. For the police command department, we are looking for a communication specialist at the Solothurn location with a focus on internal communication, 80-100%.

Your responsibility

  • They are responsible for internal communication and continuously manage and develop the intranet
  • You also take over the editorial CO management of the employee magazine.
  • Planning and publishing target group-oriented content for social media channels is just as much a task as implementing structured content planning.
  • The team supports you in writing media communications, editing and answering media inquiries competently and ensure professional event communication as a media spokesperson.
  • They support the organisation of media events, actively participate in projects and coordinate cooperation with external partners and agencies.
  • The analysis of current topics, trends and media reporting is just as much a task as deriving suitable communication measures.

Your profile

  • They have completed training and several years of professional experience in the areas of communication, media, PR or corporate communications.
  • They bring in-depth experience in internal communication and social media and use common platforms and content management systems securely.
  • Her experience in media production and project management is complemented by an analytical, creative and networked way of thinking.
  • A high level of communication and social competence distinguishes you as well as the pleasure of teamwork and active networking within the organization.
  • An independent working method, resilience as well as the willingness to offer pikett and weekend services round off your profile. In addition, you convince by a safe appearance and a clear, addressee-friendly expression.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
