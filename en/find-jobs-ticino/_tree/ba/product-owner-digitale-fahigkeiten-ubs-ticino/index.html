Full description

Do you have an entrepreneurial mindset and are you eager to understand and fulfill our clients’ needs? Do you want to play a key role in transforming our firm into an agile organization? Are you enthusiastic about the future of working in an AI driven world?

At UBS, we re-imagine the way we work, the way we connect with each other – our colleagues, clients and partners – and the way we deliver value. Being agile will make us more responsive, more adaptable, and ultimately more innovative.

We’re looking for a Product Owner to

  • Co-develop and maintain a clear product vision, strategy and roadmap for building out agentic capabilities in our core platforms.
  • Define and maintain Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) aligned to product and business strategy
  • Collaborate with end users in IB Operations to communicate art of the possible and identify business processes that can benefit from AI tooling
  • Assess potential use cases objectively and understand business value vs cost of solution, looking to reuse or extend existing capabilities where feasible
  • Influence the design of new solutions to deliver benefit for multiple stakeholders where possible
  • Maintain pod and iteration backlogs in Gitlab to a high standard
  • Obtain necessary governance approvals for new use cases / solutions
  • Keep current with industry trends and emerging technologies in AI / ML space, and apply external perspectives and insights to improve our offering
  • Mitigate impediments to the team’s goals, align across pods and with other crews where needed and act as your pod’s ambassadors towards internal stakeholders and clients
