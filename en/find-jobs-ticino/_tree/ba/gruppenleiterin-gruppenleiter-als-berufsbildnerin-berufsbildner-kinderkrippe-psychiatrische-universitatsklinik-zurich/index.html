Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 80-100% For our crèche we are looking for an immediate or by agreement Their tasks

  • Independent management of a group of children according to the socio-educational principles
  • Accompaniment of the children in free play and participation in activities
  • Implementation and integration of the pedagogical crib concept in everyday life
  • Care of appreciative parental contact as well as regular exchange
  • Taking oversight and safety responsibility
  • Completion of general household tasks

Your profile

  • Completed training as a specialist (child)
  • Completion of vocational training
  • Experience in group leadership or willingness to assume leadership responsibility
  • They are sensitive, patient and happy to take responsibility for children, parents and in the team
  • Creativity and a cheerful natural character distinguish you
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Our offer

  • More time for relaxation: at least 25 days of holiday and possibility for unpaid leave
  • Focus on health: Various health-promoting offers, green oases and an in-house care team
  • Future provision with added value: 60% employer contribution to the pension fund
  • Sustainable travel: participation in the ZVV-BonusPass or general subscription
  • More advantages for you: Discounts and discounts at numerous partner companies
