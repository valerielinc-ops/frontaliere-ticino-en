Full description

Introduction

For our Information Systems Division, we are looking for a Business Analyst – Forecast and Planning to strengthen the "Request" team. Within an Agile product-oriented organization, you will contribute to the design, evolution and security of business processes supported by our forecasting and planning solutions, including Maestro and SAP. The main mission will be to liaise between trade and technical teams to ensure a good understanding of needs.

Responsibilities

  • Collect, analyse and challenge business needs in the areas of forecasting and planning
  • Contribute to the definition of solutions tailored to business needs, by promoting the use of the standards of the selected tools
  • Participate in process design and evolution in the APS Maestro, SAPERP and associated solutions
  • Perform or coordinate the necessary functional settings in selected solutions
  • Write user stories, detailed functional specifications and contribute to technical specifications
  • Monitoring, testing and validation of team developments
  • Participate in projects for deployment, transformation and continuous improvement of IT solutions
  • Support end-users in testing, training, deployment and stabilization phases
  • Contribute to the quality of the solution, documentation and supported processes
  • Monitor good practice in forecasting, planning and using APS solutions.

Profile

  • Diploma of Engineer, Master or equivalent training in computer science, logistics, planning, supply chain, trade or related field
  • Experience of at least 3 years in an APS, ERP or forecasting/planning solution environment
  • Knowledge of Maestro or other advanced planning tool: SAP APO, SAP IBP, OMP, Blue Yonder or equivalent
  • Ability to write specifications, user stories, test scenarios and user support
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis
  • Ability to understand and structure complex processes
  • Rigor in documentation, testing and follow-up of decisions
  • Service sense and ability to work with various trade partners
  • Curiosity for advanced planning solutions and APS standards
  • Ability to adapt to evolving functional perimeters.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
