Full description

The Migration Office operates daily in the exciting political environment of all migration issues. We are responsible for the permits under foreigners law, enforce the Swiss alien and asylum law, issue the biometric Swiss passports and foreigner ID cards and provide care and integration in our asylum centres.

What to expect

  • You are responsible for the medical care of residents
  • You advise and support residents in health matters
  • You organize and coordinate appointments with doctors, specialists and other specialists
  • You ensure that the residents have health insurance
  • The care and contact with external contacts of the health sector are part of your tasks
  • You manage the house pharmacy and support with preventive health measures
  • You are the internal contact person for medical questions at the center

What distinguishes you

  • You bring a completed education as a dipl. Medical professional HF with or are a health professional (FaGe) with several years of professional experience
  • You distinguish yourself through a clear way of communication and are open in dealing with people from different cultures
  • You can set priorities, manage short-term changes and keep a cool head
  • Good MS-Office user knowledge of foreign languages is an advantage

What we offer

  • Benefit from regulated working hours from Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00
  • Possibility of employment in job sharing
  • A varied activity in an unusual environment
  • A family atmosphere with a lived du-culture
  • A motivated and interdisciplinary team
  • Diverse training opportunities
