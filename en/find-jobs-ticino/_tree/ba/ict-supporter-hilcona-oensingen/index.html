Full description

Are you looking for a varied role with technical depth? A position that challenges you and the IT infrastructure? A site that is expanding rapidly and relies on the latest technology? Then you've come to the right place!

We can only consider applicants who are authorized to work in Switzerland (EU/EFTA). We only consider direct applications.

  • Contract type: Permanent
  • Workload: 100%
  • Working time model: Hybrid working hours by agreement
  • Start date: Immediately
  • Language: German
  • Work location: Bell Schweiz AG, Dünnernstrasse 31 4702 Oensingen

Tasks

  • Installation, maintenance and support of workstation systems, mobile devices, servers, network and communication components, as well as associated software and interfaces
  • Implementation of installation and configuration specifications (e.g. directory structures, user interfaces, menu systems)
  • Analysis, isolation and resolution of faults in hardware/software, networks and communications, including escalation management
  • Advising and supporting users with IT questions, procurement, system introductions, and the design and implementation of solutions
  • Coordination and management of external service providers, suppliers and support partners
  • Ensuring a stable IT infrastructure, including availability monitoring and maintenance of inventory, documentation and operating manuals
  • Development of simple scripts and tools for automation and to support daily work on workstations and mobile devices
  • Handling interface and conversion problems as well as evaluation of standard and custom software
  • Documentation of incidents, changes and system states in the ticketing system, including outage and processing logs
  • Involvement in IT projects and coordination between users and internal IT departments
  • Carrying out planned system work as part of early/late shifts and maintenance windows
  • Participation in the 7x24 on-call service on a rotating basis

Requirements

  • Completed IT training or experience as an ICT support specialist
  • Several years of experience in IT – knowing what matters
  • Solid knowledge of Windows operating systems and MS Office
  • Good knowledge of hardware and networks
  • Very good German skills, English skills are a plus
  • Team-player mentality, an analytical approach and personal responsibility
  • Technical aptitude and a solution-oriented mindset
  • Place of residence no more than 45 minutes from Oensingen and a driver's license

What we offer

  • Work-life balance: A balanced work-life balance is important to us. You benefit from at least 25 vacation days as well as transparent, electronic time tracking. This allows you to flexibly compensate for any overtime with additional days off.
  • Meals: We place great importance on food: freshly prepared food in our staff restaurant, a lunch menu including soup and salad, water & tea, and complimentary fruit, as well as high-quality products at heavily reduced prices, await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient access to public transport, attractive ticket subsidies and sufficient parking in the parking garage.
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive further training opportunities, high-quality training at the Coop Education Center and diverse personal development opportunities.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management, attendance bonus, discounted Reka-Checks, exclusive employee discounts and subsidized fitness memberships – for health and fun in your free time!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments at our department event and strengthen team spirit at the company run – celebrate, get active and make connections together!
  • Market-based salaries and social benefits: Secure your future with our above-average retirement provision, receive anniversary bonuses, Christmas bonuses and attractive bonuses for employee referrals. You will also receive the Coop employee discount card, and we cover two thirds of your pension fund contributions!
  • Family-friendly employer: Organize your working hours flexibly with part-time arrangements and experience an inclusive work environment in which diversity is valued and promoted!
