Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss delivery and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals and retailers).

Tasks

  • Representation of the head of department in his absence
  • Active sales and consulting activities, ensuring optimal sales readiness
  • Recognizing, capturing and forwarding customer needs to the superior department manager
  • Ensuring the presentation of goods
  • Implementing self-employed ordering
  • Ensuring 100% availability of goods
  • Inventory recording according to specifications
  • Compliance with the prescribed internal and legal QS and ASIB guidelines
  • Efficient cooperation with internal and external suppliers and customers

Requirements

  • Completed training in the food industry (gastronomy, retail trade)
  • Experience in a comparable capacity as well as technical and product knowledge
  • Joy of personal customer contact
  • Enthusiasm for new challenges
  • Readiness for further development
  • Networking gastronomy/detail trade is an advantage

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Basel?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
