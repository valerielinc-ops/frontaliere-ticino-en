Full description

To complement our team, we are currently looking for a person Under the umbrella of "Center da sanadad Engiadina Bassa" (CSEB) / "Health Center Unterengadin", important regional providers in the fields of healthcare and wellness are taking a common path. In the National Park region, you will find a wonderful environment with numerous leisure and sports opportunities. The Hospital is responsible for the medical basic and emergency care in the Unterengadin. It offers advanced but also complementary medicine and takes a pioneering role in Switzerland in the field of patient safety. The fields of Surgery, Traumatology, Orthopedics, Gynecology and Obstetrics, Internal Medicine and Palliative Care, Integrative Medicine, Anesthesiology and Pain Therapy incl. an IMC station are offered. The Hospital is equipped with modern infrastructure with KIS, RIS, PACS incl. numerous advanced telemedicine solutions and a modern operating theater. ## Tasks
