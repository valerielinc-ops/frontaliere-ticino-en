AFP or CFC Retail Learning

Introduction

Do you have any idea what you'll do after graduation? Start with us in your future and take advantage of the development opportunities at Lidl Switzerland. Shops are at the heart of our business. As an apprentice in the retail business (focusing on creating buying experiences), AFP or CFC, you contribute to the success of your Lidl store.

Start/End PFA: 9 Start/End CFC: 0

Your Mission

  • Cash management and accounting
  • Guarantee of freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Storage and presentation of goods
  • Verification of receipt of goods in the reserve and assistance from your team during inventories
  • Assistance in planning and implementing seasonal promotions and investments
  • Identification of Goods Requirements and Ordering of Goods in Grade 3 of Learning (CFC)
  • First Team Leadership Experience in Grade 3 Learning (CFC)

Your profile

  • Average to very good grade at high school I
  • AFP: level A1 in foreign language and CFC: at least A2
  • Pleasure working with people
  • Self-employment and a lively spirit
  • Team spirit, reliability and responsibility
  • Will to learn and availability
  • Physical resistance
  • Good knowledge in French

Our offer

An apprenticeship that opens many paths for you. After successfully completing your apprenticeship, and if you have good personal skills, we will move you to a management position in the store. In addition, you will benefit from the Lidl CTC and many interesting advantages
