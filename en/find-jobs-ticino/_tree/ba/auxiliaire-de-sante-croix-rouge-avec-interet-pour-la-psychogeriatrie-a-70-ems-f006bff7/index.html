The EMS Riond\-Vert, which celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2025, is distinguished by its human approach to medical and social care. More than just an establishment, we are a house welcoming 93 residents with respect and benevolence.

In order to strengthen our team of care and accompaniment of Myosotis, a hypostimulating place of life, we seek immediately or to agree a

Auxiliary of health Red Cross with interest for 70% psychogeriatry

Your main tasks:

  • you frame and accompany the residents in the gestures of daily life
  • you collaborate in the development and implementation of projects accompanying residents and their relatives
  • you create a warm and supportive environment for the well-being of residents
  • you contribute to the smooth functioning of the service and team

Your profile:

  • you benefit from a Red Cross health extension or equivalent training
  • you have a minimum experience of a year confirmed in an EMS or similar structure
  • you have a clear interest in the elderly and psychogeriatry
  • you have a great ability to adapt and are able to work in a multidisciplinary team
  • you are oriented and have a common sense of human relations
  • you have a good command of French (spoken and written)

We offer

  • a pleasant and stimulating working environment, close to our residents
  • A context for skills development
  • opportunities for continuing training
  • social and wage conditions according to the CLC SLD
