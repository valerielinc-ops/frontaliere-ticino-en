Full description

In order to strengthen our Industrial Technologies department within our production site in Chavornay, we are looking for a:

Electrician (CDD 12 months)

ROLE

  • Expanding production machines and various installations;
  • Conduct preventive maintenance according to schedule;
  • Modify or install new machines;
  • 2x8 team schedules;
  • Occasionally provide night picket service.

PROFILE

• Technical training in mechanical-electric, automation or polymechanical - holder of art. 15 ITTO (an asset);

  • 3-5 years of experience in industry, industrial maintenance or similar positions;
  • Knowledge of hydraulics, pneumatics, mechanics and other fields (PLC, ATEX) is considered a real asset;
  • versatility, a strong sense of organisation and priority management;
  • Rigourous and precise, able to solve problems as quickly as possible, resistance to stress;
  • Confirmed user of office automation tools;

• Valid driving licence required.

  • French current / level of technical English, one plus.
