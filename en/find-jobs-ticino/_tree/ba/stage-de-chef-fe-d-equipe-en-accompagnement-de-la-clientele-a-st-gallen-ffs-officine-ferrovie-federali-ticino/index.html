SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. Move Switzerland – close to the pulse, among travelers and in the team. The customer support ensures that a train journey becomes a positive travel experience. With presence, service orientation and competence, it shapes the face of SBB – day after day. Take responsibility for a committed team and help shape SBB’s customer support. You can move that.

  • You will lead a team of around 40 employees – consisting of Head of Customer Support: SBB and Customer Support: SBB –, promote their professional and personal development and create the conditions for successful cooperation.
  • You are actively involved in attracting new customer companions: with and strengthen your team with an active health management.
  • Together with your colleagues, you will continue to develop your team and location (personal, professional and cultural, business) along the lines of strategy and ambitious goals and ensure high-quality service provision.
  • With the right decisions, your drive and your strategic skills, you lead, challenge and develop your team so that you can make a difference.
  • You actively shape the further development of customer support and contribute your experience to projects, working groups and committees.

You bring that with you.

  • You have several years of experience in customer support and direct customer contact. A strong customer, communication and conflict competence distinguishes you.
  • With your professional appearance, your decision-making strength and a healthy assertiveness, you are leading the way as an example.
  • You work in a trusting and cross-divisional manner with your colleagues in the management team. Together they take responsibility, represent joint decisions and continuously develop both the location and processes and interfaces.
  • Even in challenging situations, you act calmly, deliberately and keep an overview.
  • The handling of digital work equipment is a matter of course for you.
  • You master German at the native language level. In addition, you have knowledge of French at least level B1
  • Further language skills are advantageous.
