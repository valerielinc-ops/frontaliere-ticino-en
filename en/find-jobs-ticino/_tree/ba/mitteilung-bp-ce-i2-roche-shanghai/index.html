The Position This role serves as the strategic Communications Business Partner for the Infectious Disease and Immunology of Customer Engagement Unit (CE-I2). We are looking for a forward-thinking communications leader with an agile, innovative mindset to elevate our public disease awareness campaigns and maximize brand resonance. The ideal candidate will seamlessly blend traditional healthcare communications with dynamic, omnichannel strategies to support our flagship products and upcoming pipeline launches within a strict compliant framework.

Key Responsibilities

  • Strategic Planning: Work closely with CE I2 marketing team to develop integrated communication strategies based on a deep understanding of business needs, tailored for blockbuster products and upcoming new pipeline launches.
  • Innovative Disease Awareness & Digital Communications: Lead end-to-end communication campaigns by leveraging digital ecosystems and omnichannel strategies. Drive engaging, compliant content creation and public health education initiatives to maximize brand awareness.
  • Cross-Functional Agility: Work closely with cross-functional teams to maximize communication impact, blending traditional Rx compliance with innovative public communication approaches.
  • Agency Management: Manage and coordinate external agencies (PR, Social, Digital) to ensure smooth implementation of fast-paced communication events and track the effectiveness of agency expense.
  • Impact Measurement: Measure and assess the impact of communication initiatives using data-driven insights and digital metrics.
  • International Communication : Work closely with CE Head on CE-level strategic internal communication initiatives.
