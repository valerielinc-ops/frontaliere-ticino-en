Full description

Le Mirabeau Resort and SPA Superior is a family-run resort hotel of the 3rd generation of the Julen and Reichenbach family with a total of 87 residential units. Located in the quieter part of the village, the hotel offers a majestic view of the Matterhorn and the surrounding Mischabel mountain range.

The rooms and suites are divided between the main building, the Alpine annex and the Etoile annex opened in 2024 – the latter in the charming French country house style. In addition, the serviced apartments are available in the opposite chalet Antoine.

Culinary – The Art of Life

Our four restaurant concepts combine tradition, modernity and haute cuisine:

Veranda – Alpine & Casual

L’Epicure – elegant & upscale

Marie’s Deli – charming Deli concept

Le Bar – stylish, cozy, lively

Josef’s Wine Lounge invites you to events, wine & dine and special moments of pleasure.

Alpine Hideaway Spa – Indoor & Outdoor Pool

Highlights of the spa: several saunas, steam baths, rest rooms and one of Zermatt’s largest indoor pools. This is complemented by an outdoor pool with an unobstructed view of the Matterhorn and an inviting hot tub.

Treatments such as wine therapy or chocolate massage round off our exclusive wellness offer.

Your Chance: Souschef (m/f/d)

Would you like to take the next step in your career, take responsibility and actively shape the kitchen philosophy?

As a sous chef, you support the chef in the operational and organizational management of the kitchen, contribute your creativity and ensure the highest culinary quality together with the team.

Join our international and dynamic team and actively shape the culinary experience at the Mirabeau.

Their tasks include

  • Ensuring the operation even in the absence of the chef
  • Recruitment and training of new staff
  • Check daily tasks and coordinate them with the chef
  • Purchasing food and non-food items for the kitchen within the given budget requirements ) in coordination with the chef
  • Share responsibility for the use of goods
  • Working independently in the management of a post
  • Entire organisation of the mise-en-place in the assigned post
  • Bring creative new ideas and optimal delivery of posts in absence
  • Compliance with our quality standards
  • Outstanding commitment, willingness to provide services and great interest in a team-oriented way of working
