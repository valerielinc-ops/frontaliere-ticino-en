Full description

Ambassador of the Ensign and trusted person, you take care of the children of our clientele and watch over their satisfaction and loyalty through high quality service, from opening to closing the stop-game

Luogo di lavoro: Société coopérative Migros Vaud, MMM Crissier, Chemin du Closalet 7, 1023 Crissier

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • You warmly welcome guests and children
  • You provide children with the playing material (crayons, drawing paper, toys, etc.) and prepare the coloring drawings.
  • You care for the well-being of the child (drink, hygiene, etc.) and monitor the games of children by intervening in the event of conflict or dangerous games.
  • You guarantee compliance with current hygiene standards and ensure the disinfection of equipment and play areas.
  • You maintain order and cleanliness and support all areas of the store if necessary, while respecting the procedures in force.
  • You're in charge of opening and closing the game stop.

Required

  • First confirmed experience in childcare: expected
  • Completed vocational learning (CFC): as a socio-educational assistant (SEA) or equivalent training
  • You benefit from several years of experience in accompanying children
  • You're organized, self-sustaining and able to take initiatives.
  • You benefit from a first aid training
  • French (Very good knowledge)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Pension fund financed by the employer at 17%.
  • Restaurant: 20% discount in Migros Vaud restaurants.
  • Cumulus Points: Additional Cumulus Points for Migros Employees
  • Migros Bank: preferential rates at Migros Bank
  • Offers for employees: And much more!
