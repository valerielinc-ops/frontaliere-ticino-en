Full description

cereneo – Centre for Neurology and Rehabilitation is a world-leading centre in the field of neurological rehabilitation. Our location on the beautiful Lake Vierwaldstättersee offers not only a breathtaking view of Pilatus and Rigi, but also a highly modern, accessible infrastructure. We focus on excellence, warmth, motivation, teamwork and innovation – values that determine our daily actions and ensure the quality of our care. We are looking for an experienced dipl(s). OP specialist who supports our neurosurgical team in highly specialized surgical procedures. We are looking for a professionally experienced, precise and responsible personality who is familiar with the special requirements of functional neurosurgical procedures and works in a structured and forward-looking manner even in complex operating situations.

Tasks

  • Construction, nursing management of the operating room and management and if necessary Qualification of the OTA team
  • Preparation, organisation and support of functional neurosurgical procedures. Care and preparation of the required special neurosurgical instrumentation
  • Instrumentation and assistance in neurosurgical operations
  • Readiness to assist in neurosurgical procedures outside the actual operation (especially in treatments with focused ultrasound)
  • Support and maintenance of surgical technology (e.g. surgical microscope, navigation system, C-arm)
  • Preparation and handling of implants and other surgical-specific materials
  • Ensuring a smooth and efficient surgical procedure in close cooperation with neurosurgery, anesthesia and other participating specialist areas
  • Development and further development of process management in the OR
  • Willingness, in the case of part-time employment, to flexibly adapt working hours to the needs of the OR
  • qualification in radiation protection for the use of fluoroscopy (C-arc) in the OR; if the qualification is not yet available, readiness for appropriate training. The costs may be covered by us.
  • Very good German and English skills in words and writing are required to ensure secure and professional communication in an interdisciplinary and international environment.

What should you bring?

  • Completed training in Dipl. OP specialist/expert
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of operations
  • Sound experience in neurosurgery
  • Ideally practical experience in functional neurosurgery
  • Experience with DBS, stereotactic methods and/or implantation methods of great advantage
  • Experience in Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS)
  • High degree of precision, responsibility and ability to concentrate
  • Ability to work calmly and structured even during demanding and prolonged procedures
  • Strong teamwork and professional communication with doctors, nurses and other disciplines

We offer

  • Modern infrastructure: Perfectly located working environment with a view of Lake Vierwaldstättersee, Pilatus and Rigi.
  • Development: Personal training and development opportunities.
  • Social benefits: Good social benefits (NBU, employer share pension fund 60%).
  • Parking & public transport: Free parking and access to e-charging stations, subsidy for public transport.
  • Food: Lunch check with 50% employer participation, free coffee, tea, water & fruit.
  • Kita: Discount for our connected Kita.
  • Discounts: Various discounts for leisure activities.
  • Fitness: Sharing fitness equipment.

Our corporate culture

  • EXCELLENCE: Using the latest research findings and first-class service
  • HEARTH: We are always there with all our heart. Of course, this also includes the sincere treatment of our patients: inside, but also between our staff
  • MOTIVATION: Joint goal achievement and support of patients: women to improve their quality of life
  • TEAM WORK: Reliability and mutual support in the team
  • INNOVATION: Research and testing of new therapeutic approaches and devices
