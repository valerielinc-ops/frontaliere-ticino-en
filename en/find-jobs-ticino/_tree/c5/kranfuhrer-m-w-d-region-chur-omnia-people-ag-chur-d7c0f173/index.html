Full description

For a longer-term temporary use in the Chur region, we are looking for two experienced crane operators.

their duties;

  • Operation of upper and lower rotary crane
  • Safe lifting, moving and placement of building materials
  • Cooperation with construction workers and other professionals on the construction site
  • Checking the crane before work starts
  • Compliance with applicable safety rules
  • With the help of general construction work if necessary

Your profile

  • Several years of experience as a crane operator on construction sites
  • valid Swiss crane driver’s certificate of category B
  • Self-employed and responsible work
  • Reliability and good cooperation in the team
  • Good knowledge of German for understanding on the construction site
  • Physically resilient and vertigo-free

We offer

  • Long-term temporary use
  • Current employment conditions
  • Interesting construction site in the Chur region
  • Personal support throughout the operation

We look forward to your full application documents.
