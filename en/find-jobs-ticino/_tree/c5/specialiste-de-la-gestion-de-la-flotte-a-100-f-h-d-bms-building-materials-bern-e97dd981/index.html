BMS Building Materials Suisse est un grossiste en matériaux de construction pour le gros oeuvre et le second oeuvre. Notre groupe d’entreprises comprend les spécialistes de renom que sont Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton et RegusciReco . Nos spécialistes expérimentés conseillent, soutiennent et inspirent avec beaucoup d’engagement les clients du secteur de la construction et les clients privés sur 110 sites dans toute la Suisse. Proches du quotidien de la construction, nous sommes le partenaire local et fiable pour chaque project de construction. Rapides, simples and compétents.

For our fleet department (about 950 units, cars, trucks, LNFZ, trucks) with place of work in Bern we are looking for 1. January 2026 a service-oriented personality as As a Fleet Management Specialist, you are the central contact person for all topics relating to our trucks, vehicle and truck fleet: Independently ensuring day-to-day operations for the Swiss fleet of around 950 units (trucks, trailers, cars, LNFZ, forklifts, etc.) as well as coordinating and prioritizing all ongoing tasks in compliance with legal requirements and internal guidelines. Completion of the entire vehicle life cycle from ordering to registration, takeover, transfer and user change to return in cooperation with importers, leasing companies and internal bodies. Monitoring lease contracts, terms, mileages, budgets and ongoing costs as well as processing of contract adjustments, multi- and shorter kilometres and early returns. audit, control and processing of fleet-related invoices. Coordination of maintenance, repairs, tyre changes, breakdowns and vehicle damage as well as handling and tracking of claims and insurance cases in cooperation with internal and external stakeholders. Central contact for vehicle users as well as internal specialist offices such as Finance, Controlling, HR and Payroll and operational interface to leasing companies, garages, insurance companies, tire partners, fuel card providers and other service providers. Analysis, standardization and digitalization of fleet processes as well as ensuring a current and reliable database. Development of automated workflows, key figures and reports to reduce costs, error rates, lead times and administrative effort. Implementation of the step-by-step electrification of the vehicle fleet as well as coordination and tracking of vehicle conversions, charging infrastructure, location requirements, service providers, internal specialist departments, deadlines, costs and project progress. Support in collective bargaining with service partners, independent implementation of smaller contract negotiations. Close cooperation with the Fleet Manager on optimization measures, projects and cross-divisional topics. In order to successfully master these versatile challenges, you bring the following competencies: In-depth experience in fleet management, automotive or administrative functions with high coordination density In-depth knowledge in the automotive industry Good to very good knowledge of German, French and English in word and writing Experience in leasing and claims management is an advantage Technical affinity and analytical skills Digital affinity and secure handling of MS Office and IT/ERP tools Strong organisational skills and pleasure in dealing with people Communication strength, service orientation, project management experience and negotiating skills Precise, conscientious, independent, structured and responsible working methods Flexibility, resilience and teamwork a challenging, versatile and interesting job in a good working environment in which active cooperation and self-employment are emphasised Intensive training is a program, whereby our new colleagues can benefit from product and system training within the company Support our employees in their professional and personal development Basic wage gross x 13 plus variable wage share 5 weeks holiday (6 weeks from 50 years of age) and holiday shopping up to max. 10 days, additional benefits such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and discounts on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits We look forward to your complete application documents in electronic form.
