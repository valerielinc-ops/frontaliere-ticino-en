Full description

Introduction

For our customer, a company in the field of care and care in old age, we are looking for a nursing assistant SRK or AGS (m/f/d). You support older people in everyday life and help them feel safe, well cared for and respected. A meaningful task awaits you in a motivated team.

Tasks

  • Care and guidance of the residents in everyday life
  • Support for personal care, exercise and daily tasks
  • Co-creation of simple activities such as conversations, walks or creative offers
  • Disclosure of important observations to the care team and short entries in the care documentation
  • Assistance with simple domestic and organizational tasks

Profile

  • Completed SRC nursing course with internship or training as AGS
  • Experience in long-term care is beneficial
  • Joy of working with older people
  • Warm, patient and humorous nature
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Advantages

  • Meaningful work with contact to people
  • Motivated and supportive team
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Additional free day in the birthday month
  • Reduced lunch and other employee benefits
