SALES REPRESENTATIVE - OMEGA BOUTIQUE ZERMATT Introduction de la société OMEGA is a prestigious global brand that has been striving for more than 170 years for the highest quality, extreme precision and ever new innovations. Our watches are among the best in the world, they are the result of technologies that are leading in the watch industry and are characterized by a timeless design. Over the course of our long history, we have set up precision records, played a decisive role in sporting time and we continue to play a leading role in high watchmaking. As an international brand, we are always interested in getting to know future colleagues who are willing to support our teams on virtually every continent through their talents, ambitions and inspiration.
