Put to the Point

Experience a piece of the world at the FDFA – discover exciting topics and take a first look behind the scenes of Switzerland and other countries.

  • Date of entry: 01.08.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-19558
  • Place of work: Eichenweg 5, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Writing various documents (emails, letters, minutes)
  • Processing requests by email, MS teams and telephone
  • Organising and organising meetings
  • Process various work processes via IT applications

This makes you unique

  • Completed commercial school or business school with sufficient grades in German, French and accounting
  • Taking responsibility for your own work
  • Joy in dealing with and working with people of different generations
  • Be motivated to learn foreign languages (mainly French/German) and use them in everyday life.
  • Bring good manners and professional appearance

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

On behalf of the Federal Council, the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDA) coordinates and shapes Swiss foreign policy, safeguards the country’s interests and promotes Swiss values.

Additional information

The EDA is looking for you! From 1 August 2027, we offer 10 internships for students of a commercial (medium) school who need an annual internship for their EFZ degree. You will complete the inter-company courses in the service and administration industry. You will do your long-term internship in an exciting apprenticeship – for example in the Directorate for Resources, the Directorate for Development and Cooperation (SDC) or in the Consular Directorate. You will participate in various workshops and thus get a comprehensive insight into the versatile tasks and areas of the entire department.
