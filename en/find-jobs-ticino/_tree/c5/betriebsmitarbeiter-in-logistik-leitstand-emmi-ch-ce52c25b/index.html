Logistics Operations Employee, Control Center — Emmi

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

In our logistics in Emmen we load 2500 pallets daily with the finest Emmi products which are delivered by our great team throughout Switzerland and thus reach our customers.

You can do that.

  • You ensure that the picking and loading run smoothly and set the right priorities.
  • You coordinate the truck deliveries and ensure efficient loading and unloading.
  • Telephone and written inquiries are solution-oriented and initiate the necessary steps.
  • You keep an eye on the entire flow of goods and ensure a trouble-free process from goods receipt to goods exit.
  • With your know-how, you support your colleagues in day-to-day business and provide advice on technical issues.
  • In addition, you organize advertisements for external goods and take care of the management of transport aids.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training in logistics or have several years of professional experience in a comparable environment.
  • Even in hectic situations, you work in a structured way, set priorities correctly and keep a cool head.
  • You communicate clearly and appropriately and enjoy working with various internal and external contacts.
  • The handling of digital systems is easy for you and you are well-versed in the common MS Office applications.
  • You take responsibility, think 2 ski rides ahead and teamwork is important to you.
  • Working shifts (early, late and night shifts) is a matter of course for you and you bring the necessary flexibility.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Service Anniversary: Loyalty pays off: Every five years, we reward your loyalty with a premium, which you can also receive in the form of holidays or as a one-time contribution to the pension fund.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
