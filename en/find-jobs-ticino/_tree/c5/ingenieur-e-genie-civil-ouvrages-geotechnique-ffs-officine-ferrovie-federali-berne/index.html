What awaits you.

The SBB Infrastructure Division is responsible for the construction, maintenance and operation of the infrastructure. The "Network Development, Facilities and Technology" sector provides strategic facility management. In this division, the "civil engineering" unit is the centre of competence responsible for technical management at the SBB level in the field of civil engineering for tunnels, bridges, retaining walls, earthworks, noise barrier walls, drinking water and wastewater installations. We develop techniques, methods and guidelines for new construction, repair and supervision of works of art. To find out what benefits we offer you, such as children's allowances, free class AG 1 or foreign travel at a reduced price (also for your family), visit our website under Benefits. . What you can move.

  • In this role, you take on the technical direction specialized in supporting structures, with an orientation towards geotechnical structures, for the projects of construction and maintenance of the SBB infrastructure. You also support the Tunnel, Geotechnical and Special Work fields.
  • You check project files, from alternative studies to pre-projects and construction projects, for supporting structures and covered trenches to ensure the correct application of technical requirements and qualitative requirements. To do this, you work closely with project managers and monitoring specialists.
  • You provide expert services within the SBB for the evaluation of existing support structures and for the strategic and operational advice on the implementation of construction measures.
  • In your role you are responsible for the development of SBB technical guidelines for the design and construction of support structures and for conservation, maintenance and maintenance measures. In this way, you are actively involved in extending their duration of use and you are organizing the in-service training of SBB specialists in this field.
  • You manage research and development projects in the field of geotechnical works and support walls and their inspection.

What you bring.

  • A responsible, independent and independent approach to your work characterizes you.
  • You have experience in leading technical teams and communicate appropriately with different stakeholders. You have a wide network in the technical field that you are ready to develop both internally and externally.
  • You bring several years of practical experience in geotechnical civil engineering.
  • You are familiar with the state of art and technology in the field of geotechnical civil engineering and have specialized knowledge in railway operations and regulations.
  • You have advanced training in civil engineering/geotechnical engineering (HES/university/EPF).
  • You have an excellent level in German (level C1) and have good knowledge (level B2) in Italian or French.
