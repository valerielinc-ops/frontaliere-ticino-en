Full description

About Our Client Our client is a recognized organization based in French-speaking Switzerland. As part of a transition within her management team, she wishes to join the services of an acting Director of Human Resources to ensure the continuity of the management of the function.

Job Description A member of the management team and a member of the Directorate General, you take responsibility for the Human Resources function for a transitional period. You ensure continuity of HR activities, support teams in their operational priorities and ensure an effective and collaborative working environment.Your key responsibilities include:

  • Manage the HR function and support the teams in their daily activities;
  • Advise the Directorate-General on human capital issues;
  • Ensure continuity of ongoing projects and strategic files;
  • Promote constructive collaboration with different stakeholders;
  • Prepare the conditions for a smooth transition to the future incumbent of the position.

The Successful Applicant You benefit from a proven experience in leadership and have evolved within organisations with multiple stakeholders and significant human issues. You are recognized for:

  • Your leadership and your ability to unite;
  • Your ability to evolve quickly in new environments;
  • Your ability to analyze, prioritize and decide;
  • Your relational intelligence and your sense of dialogue;
  • Your discretion, integrity and pragmatism.

The perfect mastery of French is indispensable. A good knowledge of English is an asset.

What's on Offer
