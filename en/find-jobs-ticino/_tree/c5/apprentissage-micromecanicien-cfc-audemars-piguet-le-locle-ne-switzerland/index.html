Full description

Would you like to move towards the field of micromechanics and are motivated by quality training, structured and varied? We offer you the opportunity to integrate an apprenticeship of Micromechanic (CFC), starting at the beginning of the school year 2026, for a duration of 4 years. In a stimulating and caring work environment, you will discover and perform the following missions during your training:

  • Learning and practice of conventional machining: turning, milling, adjusting, mounting, etc.
  • Reading plans and design analysis
  • Learning and implementation of FAO programming for shooting and milling 3 and 5 axes
  • Setting and running CNC machines for turning and milling
  • Milling of unit parts and small series
  • Quality control and monitoring throughout the manufacturing process
  • Successfully completed secondary school, you have very good academic results and have been able to carry out internships discovered in technical trades
  • You are recognized for your motivation and curiosity and are ready to engage in an exciting and demanding CFC
  • You have perfect command of French and good English skills that you are ready to develop
  • With a good view of space, you are recognized for your logic and methodical analysis.
  • Finally, you have good visual acuity, excellent dexterity, and are careful

What we offer

  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual weeks of leave with 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • Contributions to your sports or cultural activities
  • Possibility of working remotely from several sites (GE/VD/NE)
  • An LPP with favourable rates
