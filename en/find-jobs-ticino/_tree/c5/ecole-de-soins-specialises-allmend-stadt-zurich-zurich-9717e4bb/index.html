Full description

We are looking for the support facilities of the school Allmend with immediate reinforcement.

Tasks

As a specialist you are responsible as a springer 2 days per week (Thursday and Friday) at various locations for the care of students in kindergarten or primary school age. Together with other social pedagogues and specialists you ensure a smooth daily operation. Precise information on working hours can be discussed during a personal conversation with the management of supervision.

Their tasks include

  • You plan, organize and lead together with your colleagues the extracurricular care offer of the day school
  • You plan teaching-supporting as well as social and leisure pedagogical activities and implement them
  • They take responsibility for caring for children with special needs in close cooperation with internal and external professionals and teachers
  • Ensure adequate parental work, taking into account different cultural, social and linguistic origins
  • They participate in school development. The school Allmend is a young school and you will be actively involved in the further development processes

Profile

  • You enjoy working with children of kindergarten / primary level
  • You have a professional qualification as a specialist carer or childcare provider (EFZ) or equivalent
  • They are flexible, resilient and keep an overview even for lively groups of children
  • They enjoy working in a team and appreciate working with teachers and parents
  • You are administratively experienced and quickly integrate into new areas of responsibility

We offer

A varied and meaningful activity in a living environment. Our employees also benefit from attractive employment conditions. A motivated and open-minded team is looking forward to your application!

About us

The school district Uto is one of seven school districts in the city of Zurich and comprises districts 2 and 3 with the districts Leimbach, Wollishofen, Enge, Friesenberg and Alt-Wiedikon.

The school house Allmend was completed shortly before the sports holidays in 2023 and has started operation after these. Psychomotorik, speech therapy, school social work, the MKZ and the curative school have also moved into their own rooms in the school building. A sports hall, a gymnastics room, an outdoor sports field on the roof of the schoolhouse, a library, a large cafeteria, a multipurpose room and many adjoining rooms round off the offer.

The school Allmend comprises a total of six kindergartens, two of them in the schoolhouse, four more in the quarter. The school Allmend has been a day school since August 2023. The Department of Home Service and Technology and its employees ensure the maintenance of the school facility.
