Full description

Part-time or external role: maintain Delvitech's ISO 9001, 14001 and 27001 management systems, lead audits and drive continuous improvement. Job Summary We are looking for a QMS & Compliance Specialist (part-time or external) to develop, maintain and continuously improve the company's management systems, keeping processes, procedures, responsibilities and evidence aligned with the applicable ISO and IEC standards and with the way the organization actually works. The role maintains the existing ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 systems and plays a key role in the design, implementation and certification path of an ISO/IEC 27001 Information Security Management System, translating requirements into practical, auditable and sustainable processes without unnecessary bureaucracy. Key Responsibilities

  • Maintain and continuously improve the ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certified management systems.
  • Support the design, implementation and certification preparation of the ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, including risk assessments, policies, procedures and evidence.
  • Translate ISO/IEC requirements into practical processes, responsibilities, controls and objective evidence.
  • Perform gap analyses, map company processes and define improvement actions with clear owners.
  • Plan and coordinate internal and certification audits and manage findings, non-conformities and corrective actions.
  • Verify that documented processes are effectively implemented and supported by up-to-date evidence.
  • Collaborate with IT, R&D and technical functions, while technical implementation of security controls stays with the technical owners.
  • Define compliance KPIs, support training and awareness, and coordinate external specialists when needed.
  • Use AI and digital tools to support gap analyses, audit preparation and document review, with human validation of conclusions. Requirements
  • Practical experience in managing ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 management systems.
  • Good practical knowledge of ISO/IEC 27001 and Information Security Management Systems.
  • Experience with audits, process governance and management-system compliance.
  • Ability to translate ISO/IEC requirements into clear, practical and auditable processes.
  • Strong process analysis and mapping skills.
  • Structured, precise and evidence-driven approach; ability to work with technical, operational and management stakeholders.
  • Comfortable with digital and AI tools.
  • Professional working proficiency in English. Nice to Have
  • Direct experience in an ISO/IEC 27001 implementation or certification project.
  • Knowledge of IEC 62443 or other industrial cybersecurity standards.
  • Internal Auditor or Lead Auditor training or experience.
  • Experience with management systems across multiple sites or international production locations. What We Offer A part-time or external collaboration with a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
