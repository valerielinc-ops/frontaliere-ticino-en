Full description

Whether small or large, simple or complex – we transport and store goods. With motivated employees and modern vehicles, we at Emmen serve not only the region, but also the rearmost corners of Switzerland. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • With your technical skills you keep our plants, machines and facilities running at the Emmen and Root locations
  • Whether repairs, maintenance or installation work – you can help and support our technical team in disruption services.
  • You also ensure a well-kept appearance: Building maintenance and cleanliness on the entire area (inside and outside) are part of this
  • As a versatile all-rounder, you support our technical departments and, if appropriate, the internal IT
  • Variety is guaranteed: Wherever helping hands are required, you are actively involved

Why do you succeed?

  • Completed basic training in company maintenance, logistics or in a comparable area
  • Technical flair and ideally first professional experience in the technical field distinguish you
  • Independent and reliable work is a matter of course for you
  • As a team player, you are flexible, like to take part and appreciate varied tasks
  • You also feel comfortable on the computer – good IT knowledge is an additional plus

Your benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Modern work equipment: We keep up with the times - you have modern and safe work equipment at your disposal
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
