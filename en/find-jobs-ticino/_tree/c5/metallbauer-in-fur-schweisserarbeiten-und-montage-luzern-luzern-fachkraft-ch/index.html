Full description

You will contribute your expertise to the welding and assembly of industrial plants. The company offers you modern workplaces with diverse tasks.

  • Tasks. We are looking for a job for our client
  • Welding work (EN ISO 9606-1, 141) from pipe sections to Isometrix, Inox
  • Reading drawings and P&ID diagrams
  • Measurement of pipe systems
  • Creating isometrics
  • Assembly and disassembly of plants, reservoirs and industrial facilities
  • Support in apparatus construction, workshop and assembly
  • Participating in the commissioning of plants
  • Perform pressure tests and measurements
  • Support for quality management
  • Requirements
  • Completed vocational training as a plant and apparatus engineer
  • Excellent welding skills (valid welding certificates must be available)
  • For workshop and mounting
  • Independent reading of plans/drawings
  • Ability to work independently
  • Driving licence Cat. B mandatory offer
  • Varied and responsible work
  • Modernly furnished workplace
  • Modern pay and a good working environment
