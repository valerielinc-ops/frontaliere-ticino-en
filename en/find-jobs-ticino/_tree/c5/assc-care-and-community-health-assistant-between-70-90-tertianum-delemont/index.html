Do you want to not only improve the quality of life of others but also give meaning to your professional activity? Do you see opportunities to bring your skills and experience to us to have a positive impact on the lives of our residents? Then you are in the right place!

Community Health Care Assistant CFC from 80% to 100%

  • Handling tasks as a reference person
  • Participation in care reports and transmission of important observations

  • Warm attitude
  • Enjoyment of tackling various challenges
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written French
