Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic Stephanshorn | St. Gallen

Occupation by: by agreement

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 72014

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

The preliminary internship in the Hirslanden Klinik Stephanshorn serves as admission for the Bachelor of Science degree program in nursing at the University of Applied Sciences OST. It aims to provide you with a differentiated insight into the everyday work of care. After appropriate introduction, you will actively work in the care team and take responsibility for the work you have received.

YOUR TASKS

  • Care and care of our patients together with qualified nurses
  • Providing a clean and well-maintained environment
  • Supporting and advising patients on food selection taking into account the state of health
  • Taking over administrative and logistical tasks

Your profile

  • Gymnasiale Matura, specialist degree in a direction other than health, or a non-professional certificate of competence (EFZ) with BMS.
  • Provisional confirmation of registration of the University of Applied Sciences
  • Willingness to learn and motivation
  • Empathy in dealing with patients and their relatives
  • Good written and oral German skills
  • Reliability, as well as friendly and open appearance
  • Physical and mental health
  • Teamwork

Do you want to work in a dynamic and innovative environment where you can fully develop your strengths, know-how and competencies? We create an inspiring environment where your ideas and contributions are appreciated and you have the chance to make a positive impact on people’s well-being.

Our offer

As an acute hospital, we offer you a versatile and exciting time. You will be professionally supervised and are entitled to personal and individual learning support during the entire internship by well-trained and motivated vocational trainers.

  • Motivated, innovative and experienced team
  • Possibility of up to 7 weeks of holidays per year with the «Leisure Plus» model
  • Paid changing time in the form of up to 4 additional free days
  • Free coffee, tea and mineral water
  • Restaurant with up to 50% discount
  • More information about our benefits can be found HERE

The following documents shall be submitted

  • Tabular CV incl. Email address and current passport photo
  • Certificates of the secondary school
  • Reports of trial days and/or trial weeks (if any)
  • 2 reference persons with telephone number and e-mail address
  • Letter of motivation or similar

Damaris Trachsel, Education Manager and Michaela Eugster, Education Manager are always at your disposal for additional information.

Ausbildungmes.stephanshorn@hirslanden.ch
