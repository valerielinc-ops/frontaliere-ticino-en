Full description

As a reference player in public transport, they transport each customer by making the heart of the Lausanne region beat by a commitment to simple, innovative and sustainable mobility.

Every day, our 2,000 collaborators contribute to sustainable mobility and accompany clients in their journeys to the heart of the Lausanne region.

Every year, we train more than 100 drivers and accompany professional conversions as well as career paths in service occupations.

In order to support the development of our network, we are looking for people who are recognized for their sense of responsibility, their customer orientation and their motivation to work for the public.

Important: This announcement is intended exclusively for candidates with professional experience in the transport of persons who already hold a D licence.

Confirmed bus drivers and trolleybuses (permit D)

Your daily role

As a bus driver and trolleybus driver, you ensure the transport of our customers on the tl network, respecting the safety rules of the schedules, and the quality of service standards of the company.

On a daily basis, you contribute directly to providing our customers with a safe, enjoyable and reliable transport experience. You are in constant contact with our customers and represent the tl on the ground, with professionalism, courtesy and sense of service.

Before exercising your activity independently, you follow a 3-month paid vocational training course designed to familiarize yourself with the specifics of operating the network.

In particular, you will deepen the knowledge of the network and the lines, the commercial driving according to the tl standards and the obtaining of the necessary authorisation to drive the trolleybuses (D110).

What we offer you

  • Support by a dedicated team throughout your integration
  • Paid training including obtaining trolleybus authorization (D110), learning the tl network and accompanying business driving
  • A future job, rich in human contacts
  • Multiple opportunities for change in a growing company
  • Quality working conditions and social benefits
  • An activity that contributes to the sustainable and eco-friendly development of the agglomeration

Discover all our working conditions, benefits and benefits on our dedicated page: Becoming a driver - Lausanne public transport

Your journey and the prerequisites of the trade

The following prerequisites are essential at the time of postulation:

  • Being at least 21 years old
  • Residing in French-speaking Switzerland, less than 50 minutes by car from Perrelet Tl depot
  • Be entitled to a residence permit valid in Switzerland (permis B, C or L) or a Swiss identity card
  • Hold a valid Swiss driving licence of category D
  • OACP qualification holder up to date an asset
  • Justify professional experience in bus and bus driving
  • Have not had a licence withdrawal in the last 2 years; this period shall be extended to 5 years for any withdrawal exceeding 3 months
  • Do not submit any criminal record *

Your assets to succeed in the profession

  • You already have experience in the professional driving of people
  • Even as an experienced driver, you are ready to follow a three-month training course and adapt your practices to the requirements of the network.
  • You have a sense of service, appreciate human contacts and show relational ease
  • You are able to manage complex or unforeseen situations with calm and professionalism
  • You are willing to work in irregular hours

Your application

Please attach to your application a complete file including

  • Your current CV and cover letter
  • Your work certificates or work certificates
  • A copy of your Swiss driver's licence D front/side
  • A SIAC extract, formerly ADMAS, dated less than 3 months
  • A duplicate copy of your residence permit or Swiss identity card
  • If holder, a copy of your OACP card

Before applying, please make sure that your file contains all the requested documents. Incomplete applications will not be taken into account and will require a new application .

*Our conditions of employment require a blank criminal record. Criminal record documentation is not mandatory at the time of application, but will be requested during the selection process.

The SIAC extract is an official document attesting to a history of licence withdrawals and driver licence warnings. You can obtain it from the Automotive Service in your area.
