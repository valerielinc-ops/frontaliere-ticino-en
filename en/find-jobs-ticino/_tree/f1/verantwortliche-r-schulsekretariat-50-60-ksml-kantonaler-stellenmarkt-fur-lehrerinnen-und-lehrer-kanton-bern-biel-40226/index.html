Full description

Portrait The Salome Brunner Foundation supports around 300 children and adolescents with language development disorders and learning difficulties. With our various schools, we see ourselves as an important part of the educational landscape of the Canton of Bern.

The Language Healing School Biel-Seeland / Classes de Langage Bienne is a special primary school for children and adolescents with a complex language development disorder and comprises eight classes in cycle one (5 German, 3 French) and two classes (1 German, 1 French) in cycle two. Small classes make it possible to respond to the individual needs of the students: inside. Speech therapy and psychomotor skills take place as individual therapy.

By 01.08.2027 or by agreement we search for:e

Tasks

They support the school management in administrative and organisational matters. They operate the telephone, write correspondence independently or after submission, edit texts, create, control and mutate lists and manage the students’ files. You are responsible as a link for the ICT issues of the school.

In addition, you provide spontaneous administrative and organisational services to relieve the burden on school management in everyday school life.

Requirements

  • Basic commercial training and some years of professional and life experience
  • Efficient use of office applications
  • Adaptive use of mail and Internet
  • Affinity to support and promote the digitalization of foundation administration
  • Joy, interest and motivation for working at a diverse school with children with special needs in language, communication, behavior and learning
  • Service orientation and participation in foundation-wide projects
  • Very good French skills in word and writing
