Full description

What to expect

  • Managing projects for the development of innovative solutions with our customers in the field of Industry & Consumer Goods or Industry & Mechanical Engineering
  • Technical advice to our customers and sales organizations in the areas of material selection, component and material analysis and injection molding processing
  • Implementation of tests and inspections on our modern facilities in the injection molding plant and at customers
  • Development of technical basics, feasibility analyses and cost accounting
  • Working in cross-functional teams with research & development, production, materials analysis, marketing & sales
  • Market monitoring to identify new trends, technologies and opportunities for EMS
  • Lectures and internal training

What do you bring with you

  • Completed studies in the field of plastics engineering, materials engineering or mechanical engineering
  • Professional experience in injection molding technology and design of technical components made of plastic
  • You are a decision-making and entrepreneurial personality with a strong will to achieve goals and are looking for the challenge in the areas of technology and project management.
  • You convince us with your commitment and willingness to learn as well as your own initiative and flexibility
  • Good English skills in Word and Writing

You can look forward to that.

  • A responsible activity with exciting and varied projects in an international environment
  • Modern infrastructure for demanding process tests, analyses and developments
  • A lot of room for manoeuvre and a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Personal and professional development opportunities in the areas of technology and project management
  • Attractive career prospects within our company
  • Working in a region with a high quality of life and diverse nature and recreation opportunities
  • Market-oriented remuneration, progressive social benefits and a corporate pension fund with attractive benefits

About us

EMS has 25 production sites in 16 countries, employs around 3,000 people worldwide and, with around 140 apprentices, is the largest vocational trainer in the canton and winner of the National Education Award.

EMS-GRIVORY is part of the EMS Group’s high-performance polymers business unit and employs more than 1000 people worldwide. The largest development and production centre is located in Domat/Ems in Switzerland. Further production facilities are located in Germany, China, Taiwan and the USA.
