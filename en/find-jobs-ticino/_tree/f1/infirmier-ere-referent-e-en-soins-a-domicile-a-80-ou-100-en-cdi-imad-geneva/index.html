Full description

The nurse intervenes at home with clients, with the whole population, all age groups, requiring nursing care. The specificity of home work is linked to the unpredictability of the context, the nature and variety of situations encountered, both socio-economic, environmental and psycho-physical. The services are performed independently, respecting the customer's lifestyle, beliefs, values, culture, autonomy and integrity. Your missions and responsibilities

  • Exercising a status referent role within the home retention team model and acting in accordance with the principles of the professional code of ethics for nurses
  • Providing comprehensive, preventive, curative, educational and palliative personalized nursing services to clients of all ages at home, independently and/or on medical delegation
  • Organize medico-social approaches, co-ordination and co-operation with all those involved in the situation of a particular client, mobilizing available resources and guiding care performed or delegated to the SASCs
  • Work in interdisciplinarity and interprofessionality, respecting the mission and institutional objectives
  • Participate in training and research activities.
  • Bachelor of Nursing or equivalent training
  • Minimum two years of professional experience
  • Knowledge of the Geneva care network
  • Ability to work independently and as an interdisciplinary team
  • Excellent adaptability and delegation
  • Responsibility and direction solution
  • Essential mobility (city bike / walking / car / TPG).
  • Entry into office: to be agreed
  • Please attach to your file:
  • an updated curriculum vitae,
  • a cover letter,
  • a copy of your diplomas
  • your work certificates
  • We only consider full applications

We guarantee complete confidentiality in the processing of your file.
