Full description

The food service at HelvetiCafé & Restaurant at Monbijoustrasse 74 in Bern provides catering for employees of the Federal Office of Culture (BAK) as well as guests of the Swiss National Library. A varied offering awaits them on the 1st floor, with freshly prepared menus every day, vegetarian and vegan dishes, salads, sandwiches and snacks. Catering is available around the clock via fresh-food and snack vending machines as well as coffee machines. In addition, the operation offers catering services, aperitifs and refreshments for meetings and events throughout the entire building. Freshness, quality and sustainability are at the heart of everything. Seasonal and regional products, together with a conscious approach to food, shape the offering.

Are you our new team member who will support us as an Operations Manager with cooking duties (80%), starting immediately or by agreement? You will work in an 80-100% position from Monday to Friday. Depending on the workload, your working hours are generally from 07:00 to 16:30, from 08:30 to 18:30 or from 10:00 to 22:00.

Your menu of responsibilities

  • You take over the organization and management of the new operation and, together with your team, ensure smooth day-to-day operations
  • You actively pitch in with the day-to-day business and support your team in service and in the kitchen, with preparation and reheating of the delivered dishes during the lunch service
  • You are an attentive host, maintain contact with our guests and clients and ensure a high level of satisfaction
  • You contribute your own ideas, further develop the offering and new activities, and implement sales promotion measures
  • You plan and organize catering services, events and special occasions and oversee their implementation on site
  • You lead, support and develop your employees and foster appreciative collaboration within the team
  • You take care of orders and administrative tasks, plan the budget together with your manager and keep an eye on the key figures and costs
  • You ensure compliance with our quality, hygiene and occupational safety standards and handle food and resources responsibly
  • You work closely with your manager and internal contacts and actively contribute to the further development of the operation

Your recipe for success

  • A completed EFZ vocational qualification and experience in the hospitality industry
  • Further training at hotel management school level or Gastronomy Basic Seminar G1, G2 or G3
  • Knowledge of French is an advantage
  • Initial management experience or the motivation to take the next step into a leadership role
  • Good basic knowledge of kitchen operations and a willingness to lend a hand with food preparation and reheating
  • Host qualities and enjoyment of interacting with guests and various stakeholder groups
  • Organizational talent, initiative and an independent way of working
  • Enthusiasm for helping shape a new operation with your own ideas and commitment
  • A good understanding of numbers and a willingness to take responsibility for costs and operational processes
  • Team spirit, flexibility and a solution-oriented attitude
  • A sense of responsibility when handling food, quality and hygiene

What we offer you

We create teams that support one another and in which you are encouraged. We make an impact together and focus on everything that does you good. Among other things, we place great emphasis on pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, at a 100% workload, depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary between CHF 70'000 and CHF 78'000 (including 13th month's salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
