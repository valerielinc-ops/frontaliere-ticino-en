Full description

Admission immediately or by agreement · Bruderholz, Liestal, Orthopaedics & Traumatology · Job reference 13130 · Temporary

Tasks

  • Support in the planning of clinical trials
  • Preparation of study protocols and other input documents for the Ethics Committee as well as the support of our medical and student staff in their preparation
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation as well as communication with external partners/sponsors to activate the study and ensure correct study implementation as well as study documentation incl. Reporting of relevant events
  • Inclusion and care of study patients in cooperation with the investigators:
  • Coordination, implementation and documentation of patient visits
  • Implementation of monitoring visits
  • Registration and data management as well as the associated obligations to our external partners and support clinical processes
  • Statistical evaluations and further development of quality assurance
  • Conduct of staff training
  • The position is limited to 2 years with prospect of extension

Requirements

  • Professional experience in clinical trials and/or data management
  • CAS studies, studies in human medicine, biology, nursing sciences, life sciences or related courses with professional experience in clinical trials
  • Self-employment, initiative and a structured and organized working method are among your strengths
  • Engaged personality with high performance, team spirit and social competence in dealing with sick people
  • Knowledge in database structures or in the creation of databases as well as in statistical evaluations
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Regular working hours from Monday to Friday
  • Flexibility in terms of a workplace with two hospital locations in Liestal and Bruderholz as well as the willingness to work even after 17:00

Being fit & staying fit: Attractive offers around sports, relaxation & nutrition
