Working Where Life Happens

Ours beats for the Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss delivery and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Job opening

Tasks

  • You create an environment in which our customers find their way around well and like to shop and communicate with experts at eye level
  • You ensure compliance with the internal and legal QS\ and ASIB guidelines
  • In addition to ensuring the freshness and presentation of goods, you are responsible for a correct labeling
  • You work in different departments
  • You ensure the constant availability of our products and regularly carry out inventories
  • You take complaints professionally and forward them accordingly
  • At the checkout you scan the goods

Requirements

  • You have completed training in the food industry (chef, baker, retailer, )
  • You enjoy offering our customers a great shopping experience
  • You distinguish your highly reliable and efficient work method
  • You are a team capable personality who is enthusiastic about new challenges
  • You communicate perfectly in German
  • Advantageously, you have already completed the forklift and/or drawbar training (SUVA\ standard)

What we offer

We offer you a modern, dynamic working environment in which you can contribute your skills and personality every day and make a difference. For joint success. For a common future.
