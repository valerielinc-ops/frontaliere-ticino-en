Full description

We make spaces where life is good.

Start of learning 2027

As a heating installer, you keep your customers comfortable. You are responsible for the installation, maintenance, maintenance and repair of heating and air conditioning facilities. Thanks to your expertise, you distribute heat consistently.

Your area of activity

  • Assembly of various components of heating installations
  • Installation of modern thermal systems (e.g. heat pumps, solid fuel boilers, solar installations)
  • Interventions in enterprises of different sizes
  • Work on construction sites and in company workshops
  • Guarantee of heating supply and comfort in living, working and leisure areas
  • Guarantee of a homogeneous distribution of heat (e.g. via soil or radiators)
  • Contribution to a high quality of life and to the satisfaction of the occupants
  • Priority given to innovative and energy efficient solutions
  • Important role in the implementation of the energy strategy

Your profile

  • Manual skill
  • A practical sense
  • Technical knowledge
  • A good spatial perception
  • Good physical agility
  • Team spirit
