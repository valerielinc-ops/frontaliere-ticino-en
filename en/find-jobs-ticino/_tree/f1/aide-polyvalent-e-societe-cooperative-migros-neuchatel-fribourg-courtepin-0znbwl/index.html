Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Personal interview video or telephone: In the case of a large number of candidates, personal video or telephone interviews are optional. This allows us to get to know more candidates in a short time so as to invite the people concerned for the next round. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Test work: A day or half-day on the test allows you to become familiar with the future working environment and team members. Conversely, we have the opportunity to see how compatible the candidates are with the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and establishment of contract (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week
