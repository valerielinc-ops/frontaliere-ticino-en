Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for someone for the service department to support our assignment planning team with a 100% effort in the Sales Center Switzerland in Reinach.

What is the role about?

They coordinate service missions and are the central contact person for customer inquiries regarding faults, repairs and maintenance.

What tasks will you take on?

Size

  • accept customer requests by phone
  • Open service, repair and fault orders
  • collect technical data according to customer specifications
  • plan the deployments of our technicians throughout Switzerland efficiently and economically with the related communication to customers
  • forward technical requests to customer service
  • Plan and monitor maintenance orders
  • Plan and coordinate assembly and commissioning
  • work closely with all interfaces

What do we expect from you?

  • have completed commercial training
  • have several years of professional experience in a similar function
  • have the enthusiasm for measurement technology and master the common MS Office software and SAP programs
  • can easily communicate in German and French, English is an advantage
  • think service-oriented, analytical and reliable
  • have a communicative and networked thinking personality

What can you expect from us?

  • Security of an international family business
  • CVT offers
  • Occupational retirement provision and health management
  • Flexible working hours
  • Offers to reconcile family and professional life

How do you apply?

To apply, please upload your complete documents to our career portal. Applications that reach us by e-mail cannot be considered for data protection reasons.
