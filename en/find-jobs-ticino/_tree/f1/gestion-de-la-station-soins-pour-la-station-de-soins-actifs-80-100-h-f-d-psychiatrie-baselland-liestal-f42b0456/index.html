Full description

Care for the acute care unit 80-100 % (m/f/d) Psychiatry Baselland The protected acute care unit G1 with 24 treatment stations specializes in older people with organically induced mental disorders, especially with dementia or delirium. We also include older people in acute psychological crises. The focus is on diagnostic clarification, stabilization and individually coordinated psychiatric, psychotherapeutic and nursing treatment. As a station manager, together with the medical station manager, you are responsible for reliable station operation, interprofessional cooperation and the further development of the treatment offer.

Their tasks

  • Professional, human and organizational leadership and development of the nursing team including needs-based personnel planning.
  • Ensuring and further developing a person-centered, relationship-oriented and evidence-based care as well as the therapeutic environment.
  • Support the team in dealing professionally with challenging behavior, delirium and complex crisis situations as well as promoting a reflective practice.
  • Control of quality and development processes on the station.
  • Promoting cooperation with relatives and caregivers, as well as with internal and external care partners

Your profile

  • Diploma as a nurse HF or FH or an equivalent foreign degree recognised by the Swiss Red Cross.
  • Several years of professional experience in psychiatric care. Experience in age psychiatry is beneficial.
  • management experience as well as a completed leadership training or the willingness to complete a corresponding further training.
  • Clear, appreciative and participation-oriented leadership style.
  • Organizational skills and safe action in demanding situations.
  • Interest in evidence-based care, quality development and change processes.

Job opening: The job is to be filled on 1 January 2027 or by agreement.
