Occupation per: by arrangement

Employment type: Temporary

Reference number: 64311

As Switzerland's largest medical network, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centres and emergency stations. First-class medical quality and the well-being of humans are our main focus.

The internship at the Hirslanden Klinik Stephanshorn serves to clarify the suitability and motivation of a profession in the health care sector. It aims to give you a differentiated insight into the everyday work of the care. After a corresponding introduction you actively participate in the nursing team and assume responsibility for the tasks assigned to you.

YOUR TASKS

  • Care and care of our patients together with qualified nursing professionals
  • Ensure a clean and well-kept environment
  • Support and advise patients in choosing meals taking into account their health
  • Administrative and logistical tasks

YOUR PROFILE

  • Completed 3-year technical secondary school
  • Learning readiness and motivation
  • Empathy in dealing with patients and their families
  • Interest in a profession in the health sector
  • Good written and oral knowledge of German
  • Reliability, as well as friendly and open appearance
  • Physical and mental health
  • Teamwork

Do you want to work in a dynamic and innovative environment where you can fully develop your strengths, know-how and skills? We create an inspiring environment where your ideas and contributions are appreciated and where you have the chance to have a positive impact on people's well-being.

OUR OFFER

  • Motivated, innovative and experienced team
  • Paid change in the form of up to 4 additional days off
  • Free coffee, tea and mineral water
  • Restaurant with up to 50% discount
  • As an acute hospital, we offer you a varied and exciting time. You are professionally looked after and are entitled to personal and individual learning support throughout the internship by well-trained and motivated professional educators
  • Support in the acquisition of the technical nature work
  • Advice on the further development (possibility of running in other areas)

The following documents shall be submitted

  • Tabellarian CV including e-mail address and current passport photo
  • Certificates of the secondary school
  • Reports of hatching days and/or hatching weeks (if available)
  • Indication of 2 reference persons (teacher) with telephone number and e-mail address
  • Letter of motivation (1 A4 page, font size 12pt). In this letter, please explain why you want to complete an FMS-Praktikum

Since Maris Trachsel, training manager and Michaela Eugster, training manager, are at your disposal for additional information at any time.
