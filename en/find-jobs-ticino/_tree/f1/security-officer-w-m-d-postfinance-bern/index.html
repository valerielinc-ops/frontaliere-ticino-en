Full description

As a Security Officer, you ensure security in the area of fraud management and protect our customers from attacks on our digital and card-related services. Your focus is on fraud and risk management as well as on supporting projects and advising on security issues.

You can do that.

  • You are committed to fraud management and support the fight against cybercrime
  • You will advise and support our partners on security issues relating to fraud management, work across departments and ensure that business and infrastructure projects are safely and successfully implemented.
  • Thanks to your well-founded risk assessments, investments are made specifically where they offer the greatest protection.
  • With new developments in fraud management, you are involved right from the start and actively shape innovative security solutions

You bring that with you

  • Higher education (HF or Uni/FH), ideally with supplementary security training (e.g. CAS Information Security, CISSP)
  • Several years of experience in the field of fraud and risk management as well as in-depth knowledge in security issues
  • Open personality that is resilient and ready for new challenges
  • Very good knowledge of German and English

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
