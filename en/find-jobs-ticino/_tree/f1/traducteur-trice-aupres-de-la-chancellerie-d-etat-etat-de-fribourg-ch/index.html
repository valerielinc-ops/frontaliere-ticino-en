Full description

Who are we?

The State Chancellery CHA offers a stimulating working environment at the heart of the cantonal government's activities and transversal to the cantonal administration. It serves as the secretariat of the Council of State, organizes its events and is responsible for informing the public, coordinating external relations and relations with the Grand Council. It shall ensure the unity and quality of the legislation of the Fribourg region. It organizes elections and votes and deals with official publications, state archives, legalization, printed matter and internal mail.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

You ensure the quality of official documents and texts in the German language of the cantonal administration, in particular: • you ensure, control and correct the translations from French to German for the Council of State, the Chancellery of State and the Grand Council • you ensure that both language versions of official texts match • you review legislative acts and activity reports • you contribute to the terminology work

Profile desired

  • Master's degree in translation
  • Several years of professional experience, preferably within the cantonal administration
  • Writing experience in the German language
  • Mastery of common computer tools and strong interest in new technologies (among others, AI)
  • Service sense, attention to detail and initiative
  • Discretion, reliability and understanding
  • Flexibility and ability to work innovatively
  • Perfect mastery of German with very good knowledge of French
