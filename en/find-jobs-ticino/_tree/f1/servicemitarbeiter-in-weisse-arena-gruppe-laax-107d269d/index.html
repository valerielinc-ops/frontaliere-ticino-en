Full description

The Weisse Arena Group is an integrated service company in the tourism and leisure sector. We position ourselves as providers of alpine leisure experiences. The group includes a cable car and ski lift company, hotels and restaurants, a sports equipment store and rental, a ski and snowboard school, and an administration company. Additionally, the Weisse Arena Group is responsible for the marketing of the tourism destination Flims Laax Falera. At Ristorante Camino, pasta or risotto dishes, meat and fish dishes, and traditional wood-fired pizza are served. The selection of Italian wines and beverages complements the offer. Hotel guests can enjoy their breakfast and dinner here. The venue offers space for around 110 guests in the restaurant area and for around 60 guests in the Camino Bar/Lounge. Additionally, various caterings and banquets are offered from Ristorante Camino. Imagine being part of our service team and delighting our guests with your sense of pleasure, warmth, and a service that stays in their memory. ## What drives you

  • You assist our guests in the à la carte and/or bar area with friendliness, attention, and a high level of professionalism
  • You support our guests in choosing dishes and beverages and convince them with active sales of our specialties
  • You ensure an organized service and a well-maintained, inviting atmosphere
  • You participate in events, themed evenings, or special actions in the restaurant and at the bar
  • You take care of a perfectly prepared service area (Mise en Place) and ensure cleanliness and order
  • You operate our cash register system safely and reliably and conduct the collection independently
  • You strictly adhere to our quality and hygiene standards (HACCP) ## What makes you
  • You bring practical experience in the service sector and know what matters
  • With your positive aura and team spirit, you create a pleasant atmosphere
  • You keep a cool head even in stressful situations and adapt flexibly to different requirements
  • You have excellent German skills, English skills are an advantage
  • further languages are a plus
  • You work reliably and independently, without losing sight of the details ## An attractive workplace
  • Amidst a breathtaking mountain world
  • Additionally, you have the opportunity to develop yourself in our dynamic, lively team and the vibrant company culture
  • Enjoy numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain trips, discounts in our shops, hotels, and restaurants, and benefits for your family ## Contact Start your professional adventure with the Weissen Arena Group now with your complete online application with photo.
