The Position The possibility

Roche Kaiseraugst is a cornerstone of Roche’s global production and logistics network.

In your role, you will be part of the QC Digital Backbone (Team Application Support) department in Kaiseraugst. Among other things, the Application Support team is responsible for providing high-performance and high-quality end-user support for most of the software applications used in QC Basel/Kaiseraugst.

As an intern, the following main tasks await you

  • Participation in support & project activities for all systems managed by Team Application Support
  • Support for the collection and analysis of relevant data
  • Support for collecting and updating master data and/or system data
  • Support in the planning and implementation of training and workshops
  • Support in communicating project activities to the organisation (e.g. via newsletter, info events or G-site)
  • Administrative support of the Application Support team

Your profile

As a successful intern in QC Digital Backbone (Team Application Support), you are enrolled at a university or university of applied sciences in the fields of pharmacy, chemistry, biology or related disciplines. You are open to new ideas and question conventional ways of thinking. You value working in a performance-driven environment characterized by mutual respect, open discussion and strong collaboration. Then Roche is the starting point for you for an exciting and international working environment with a wide range of development opportunities.

Ideally, you already have initial experience in quality control.

In addition, you round off your profile with the following competencies:

  • In-depth knowledge of Google Office Suite or MS Office applications (PowerPoint, Excel, Word)
  • Good knowledge of the use of AI tools (especially Google Gemini & Notebook LLM)
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills
  • Conscientiousness in dealing with business documents
  • Strong customer orientation
  • A high level of initiative and the ability to work independently
  • Creativity, inventiveness and innovation
  • Good communication skills in German and English as well as good presentation skills

Additional information

The preferred start date for the internship is January 2027 or by arrangement. Workload: 80-100 % Internship duration: at least 6 months, with the possibility of extension up to 12 months. Non-EU/EFTA citizens (m/f/d) must attach to the application documents a confirmation from the university that a mandatory internship is part of the education.
