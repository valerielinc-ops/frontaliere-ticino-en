Full description

In order to assess the state of the lakes and rivers, aquatic biologists need in-depth knowledge of species. Graduates can correctly collect and determine samples in our water protection laboratory in concrete field and laboratory everyday life – supported by experienced experts – and thus consolidate and expand their species knowledge. An internship at AWEL thus offers an ideal complement to the basic academic education. Their tasks

  • Data evaluation, research and creation of documentation on current issues. The focus is on evaluations in connection with the unexpected development of macrophyte stocks in the Greifen and Pfäffikersee.
  • Support for sampling and determination of macrozoobenthos by module level concept (MSC). Evaluation according to IBCH and according to the reference system for rivers in Zurich and further provisions and evaluations.
  • Support for mapping and evaluation of macrophytes by module level concept (MSK)
  • Support limnology team by taking on various administrative tasks Your profile
  • At least bachelor’s or equivalent education in environmental sciences, biology or comparable discipline
  • In-depth knowledge in limnology and aquatic ecology through in-depth studies, bachelor/master thesis or internships
  • Safe determination of macrozoobenthos at family level (IBCH)
  • Good knowledge in R (and Python), ArcGIS or Qgis desired
  • Independent, systematic and exact working methods You will learn the method Macrophyte MSK river waters from planning to mapping to evaluation. This also includes getting to know the most common river species. They deepen the methodological knowledge on macrozoobenthos sampling for MSK river waters and the species knowledge of selected groups of organisms. You will get an insight into the activities of the aquatic protection laboratory, including sampling on lakes, rivers and ARA as well as the related chemical and biological studies.
