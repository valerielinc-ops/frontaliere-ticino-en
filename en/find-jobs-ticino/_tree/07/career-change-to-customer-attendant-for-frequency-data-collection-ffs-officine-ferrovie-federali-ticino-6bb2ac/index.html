After a successful final exam, you will be part-time (50%) in shift operations nationwide as a customer companion, with shifts beginning and ending at the same training location. The customer support career offers you interesting further development opportunities such as the four-month training for / to the customer companion:in for ticket control or the eight-month training for customer companion:in. As a customer companion: for frequency acquisition, you contribute to our big goal: enthusiastic customers. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage because you can enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid, for example with an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
