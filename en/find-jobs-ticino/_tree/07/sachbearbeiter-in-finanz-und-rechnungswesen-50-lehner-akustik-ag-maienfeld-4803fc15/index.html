Full description

You are numerical, like to work independently and take responsibility?

Then this job could be just right for you!

Lehner Akustik AG is an innovative, owner-managed company with around 35 employees. We realize high-quality audio, video and multimedia solutions for private and business customers and are also active in event technology.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for an experienced personality who manages our ongoing accounting largely independently and convinces with its reliable and efficient working method.

Your tasks

  • Self-employed management of current financial accounts
  • Accounts payable and payable, including accounting and booking
  • Preparation and implementation of payment transactions
  • Monitoring of incoming payments and independent processing of the dunning system
  • Regular reconciliation and control of bank, cash and balance sheet accounts
  • Operation and maintenance of plant accounts
  • Independent preparation of quarterly VAT accounts
  • Control and accounting of expense and credit card statements
  • Implementation of periodic theses and participation in the financial statements
  • General administrative activities in the financial sector

Requirement profile

  • Completed commercial training, ideally with further training in finance and accounting
  • Several years of sound professional experience in independent accounting
  • Good knowledge of Swiss accounting and VAT
  • Very good user knowledge in Abacus and safe handling of MS Office
  • An exact, structured and efficient way of working
  • Self-employment, initiative and a high sense of responsibility
  • Strong numerical understanding and analytical thinking
  • Absolute reliability, discretion and trustworthiness
  • Team spirit, flexibility and joy of working in a vibrant SME

We offer

  • Responsible and versatile work with a workload of 50%
  • A high degree of independence and personal responsibility
  • A modern workplace with contemporary infrastructure
  • Short decision-making paths and uncomplicated cooperation
  • A family and collegial environment in an owner-managed company
  • A dynamic team and a varied working day
  • Attractive employment conditions
