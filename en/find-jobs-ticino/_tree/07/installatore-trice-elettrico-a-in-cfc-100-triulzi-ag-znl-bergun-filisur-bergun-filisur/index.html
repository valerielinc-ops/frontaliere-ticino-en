Full description

Triulzi AG is an established provider of electrical installations, telematics and building technology in the Albulatal and Bergün. To strengthen our team in Bergün, we are looking for an electrical installer EFZ 100% immediately or by agreement.

What to expect

  • You independently carry out installation work in new and conversion buildings as well as in residential and commercial buildings
  • In our service organization, you handle maintenance, disruption and small orders and support our customers reliably and solution-oriented
  • You test, measure and document equipment and thus contribute to the high quality of our work
  • If necessary, work closely with your team and support joint projects

What you bring

  • Electrical installer/EFZ training completed
  • Technical flair, high reliability and enjoyment of varied tasks
  • Independent and structured working methods and teamwork
  • Friendly, professional treatment of customers
  • Driving licence Cat. B

What we offer you

  • A motivated team and an appreciative working environment
  • Modern infrastructure and high-quality tools
  • Varied projects and the opportunity to take on responsibility
  • Attractive employment conditions with fair pay and flexible working hours
  • Support for further education and space for professional development

We look forward to your application by post or email.
