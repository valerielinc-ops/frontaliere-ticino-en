Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Are innovation, sustainability and design as important to you as they are to us? Then you are at the right address with us. Become part of our dynamic and dedicated team.

Your field of activity

  • As a refrigeration system installer: you take over the professional installation of air conditioning and commercial refrigeration systems. As a construction manager, you also coordinate the work and organize the construction site.
  • You ensure a smooth process in the assembly team. In the building management function, you lead the team and dispose of materials and tools to match the construction progress and delivery times.
  • You put air conditioning and refrigeration systems professionally into operation and support the acceptance and instruction of the users.
  • You also carry out service and repair work.
  • You comply with safety regulations and quality requirements. As a construction manager, you ensure their implementation in the team and keep the construction journal.
  • Pikettdienst you perform according to a regulated operational plan.

Your expertise

  • Ideally, you will bring an apprenticeship as a refrigeration system installer: in EFZ and a few years of professional experience in the refrigeration/air conditioning industry. We are looking for both specialists for assembly and professionals with experience in construction management.
  • You have a good technical understanding, enjoy craftsmanship and work carefully, efficiently and quality-consciously.
  • You enjoy working in a team and taking responsibility for your work. For the building management function, you also bring organizational skills and joy in team leadership. Even if you want to develop in this direction, we look forward to your application.
  • You communicate with customers and service partners in a friendly and knowledgeable manner, have good oral knowledge of German and have a category B driving licence.

We do not want requests from personnel service providers.
