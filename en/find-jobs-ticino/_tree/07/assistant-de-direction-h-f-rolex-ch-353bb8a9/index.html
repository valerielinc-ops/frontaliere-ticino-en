Full description

Introduction

For our Communication & Image division, we are looking for a Management Assistant (H/F) whose main mission will be to support the Head of the Perpetual Arts Initiative department in the administrative management and monitoring of projects.

Responsibilities

  • Manage management agenda (meeting planning, travel, appointments)
  • Organize and prepare internal meetings, seminars and events
  • Write and format letters, minutes, internal notes and reports
  • Monitoring and filing of administrative documentation
  • Manage phone calls, e-mails and incoming requests
  • Liaising between the different departments of the organization
  • Contribute to the writing of communication content (newsletters, institutional presentations)
  • Prepare PowerPoint presentations on different artistic disciplines (cinema, music, architecture, etc.)
  • Participate in administrative and logistical monitoring of artistic and cultural projects
  • Prepare and develop grant files, applications for funding and partnerships
  • Ensure timely and effective stakeholder coordination
  • Process expense notes and track management expenses
  • Update and maintain databases (contacts, partners, providers)

Profile

  • Experience confirmed in management assistantship ideally in an international environment and within a communication department
  • An interest in the arts is an asset
  • Knowledge and practice of a CRM tool
  • Perfect mastery of office tools (Suite Office, Powerpoint)
  • Excellent mastery of written and oral English and French
  • Sense of discretion and confidentiality
  • Rigor & organization: you manage multiple tasks simultaneously without losing precision
  • Team spirit: you collaborate naturally and contribute to a positive collective dynamic
  • Good presentation, excellent relationship and sense of service
  • Responsiveness and adaptability in a creative and dynamic environment

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
