Research: A neuropsychologist with the federal title

The Mentalis Centre, located at Avenue de la Gare 41 in Sion, is a centre for human-sized psychology and psychotherapy. In a caring, modern and collaborative setting, we offer quality psychotherapeutic follow-up.

To strengthen our team and develop our evaluation team, we are looking for: A neuropsychologist with the federal title

Your missions

Actively participate in the centre's life and team dynamics (clinical meetings, practice exchanges, intervisions).

Participate in clinical reflection on complex situations and contribute to the quality of care within the centre.

Write reports and ensure practice-related administrative follow-up.

Collaborate with the various partners of the network (doctors, institutions, insurance, etc.).

Contribute to the development and continuous improvement of clinical practices and care offerings at the centre.

Independent planning, implementation and documentation of diagnostic tests for adult and possibly adolescent patients.

Writing neuropsychological reports

Profile sought

Master in Clinical Psychology in Switzerland and MAS in Neuropsychology

You have several years of practical experience in neuropsychological diagnosis and master the autonomous planning, execution, evaluation and documentation of neuropsychological assessments, as well as the preparation of the corresponding reports.

Experience in the diagnosis of ADHD and Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is requested

Mastery of ADOS-2 (or voluntary training)

Neurofeedback experience is also an asset

Mastering cognitive remediation is another asset

Excellent relational skills

A sense of responsibility, autonomy, rigour and capacity to take initiatives.

Motivation to invest sustainably in a collective project

Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written; knowledge of other languages is an asset.

We offer

An employee position within a dynamic, collaborative and caring team.

Bright, modern and warm rooms, ideally located in the centre of Sion, close to the train station.

Administrative support (secretariat) for patient record management and billing.

Psychiatrist at the premises to cross eyes on diagnosis and management

A motivating remuneration, adapted to experience and responsibilities, with the possibility of varying parts depending on the annual results of the centre and the employee.

13th salary.

An improved social plan (LPP and Insurance Accidents).

A flexible activity rate, to be agreed (between 60% and 100%).

25 days of vacation per year (for full time).

A gold team

Start: to be defined
