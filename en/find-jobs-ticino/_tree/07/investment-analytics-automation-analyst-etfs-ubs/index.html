Full description

Are you interested in working in the fastest growing area of passive investments? Are you highly motivated by financial markets and portfolio construction? Do you enjoy working in a high performing, diverse and international team? Do you have a flair for numbers and presenting to stakeholders and clients? Then this is the role for you!

UBS Asset Management is seeking an Investment Analytics & Automation Analyst – ETFs to join the ETF & Index Fund business which is one of the key growth areas of the company. Index tracking investment solutions have been a core competence of UBS Asset Management for over 30 years.

Key tasks

  • monthly Analytics deliverables which requires running reports,
  • maintaining and updating collateral material,
  • performing in-depth analysis on products and competitor offerings,
  • clearly articulate and present the findings of the analysis to internal,
  • strong MS Office skills, in particular Excel and PowerPoint,
  • programming skills, with R coding skills being highly advantageous,
  • motivated to enhance and automate processes involving data and displaying results in reports and presentations,
  • having a positive attitude and willingness to learn and contribute to a high-performance team.

You will be supporting the ETF & Index Fund Investment Analytics team

UBS has a long track record of specialising in a quality indexing approach, which has contributed to them being one of the fastest growing index managers globally. Assets under management across index strategies amount to more than USD 1tn and USD 170bn in ETFs (as at Q4 25).
