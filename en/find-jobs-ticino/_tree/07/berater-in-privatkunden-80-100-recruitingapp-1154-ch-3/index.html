Full description

«We are the first bank for our customers». For us, the first bank means constantly inspiring our customers by focusing on their needs. We need you for that! Do you want to support our private customer team at the Wittenbach branch as our new colleague? Then come to us!

This is what you will do.

  • You offer your customers first-class advice and attentive support, taking into account the digital possibilities as well as the financial advice of St.Gallen and ensure the target-oriented market development in the Wittenbach region.
  • You understand how to inspire customers and distribute our products and services in the areas of investing, provisioning and financing.
  • You take over an interesting customer book, which you expand through active market development (acquisition).
  • You are actively present in the market area (public events, events, etc.).
  • You work in a motivated team and at an attractive location of the St.Galler Kantonalbank.
  • You bring your ideas to further develop the team and the office.

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed banking apprenticeship (or comparable basic training) as well as several years of banking experience in customer consulting.
  • You enjoy customer contact, good communicative skills and stand out for your degree strength.
  • You have a good knowledge of regional and local market conditions and are well established in the market area.
  • You are a team player with a high level of social competence, exemplary commitment and personal responsibility.

Information Thomas Widrig, Head of Wittenbach Branch, is happy to answer your questions by telephone 071 292 39 21.

Application www.sgkb.ch/karriere, Sandra Schefer, Stv. For this position, no applications from external personnel service providers are considered.
