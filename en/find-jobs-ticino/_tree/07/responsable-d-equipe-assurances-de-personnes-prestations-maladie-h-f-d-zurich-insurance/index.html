Are you passionate about personal insurance and want to play a key role in managing and optimizing sickness benefits? Do you like to develop a team, share your knowledge and support collaborators in their daily evolution?

At Zurich Switzerland, we work every day to build a more serene future for our customers. In order to strengthen our West-PGM Personal Insurance Services team, we are looking for a dedicated team leader with a strong organizational sense, unifying leadership and strong customer orientation.

In this function, you lead a team of claims managers and manage benefits tailored to the needs of our clients. With your team, you guarantee quality, clear, reliable and timely treatment of sickness benefits. You are actively involved in the continuous improvement of our business processes and work closely with other internal teams and departments in a modern and dynamic environment.

Your missions

  • Organization and distribution of work within the group, ensuring an equitable division of labour and timeliness
  • Management of the team for all activities related to the life cycle of employees: setting objectives in collaboration with the line, annual performance evaluation, identification of training needs, planning and distribution of work
  • Support, improvement and implementation of effective working processes, with an active contribution to optimizing the operational activities of the department
  • Collaboration with other internal departments to identify optimal solutions for our clients and continuously improve our processes

Your profile

  • Higher education in insurance and excellent technical skills in personal insurance, particularly in the field of sickness
  • Experience in teamwork, with the ability to motivate and develop collaborators
  • Ability to make clear decisions and maintain strategic direction, even in complex contexts
  • Excellent organisational and communication skills, as well as strong customer orientation
  • Mastery of French, both oral and written; good knowledge of German, English and/or Italian is an asset.
