Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Participation in cantonal IT projects

You actively contribute the needs of the IT service BVU, represent its interests and develop practical solutions for ongoing operations.

  • Support and management of specialist applications & IT services

You monitor, maintain and optimize selected applications and services to ensure their availability and performance.

  • Installation, configuration and maintenance of terminal equipment

You set up desktop computers, notebooks, mobile devices and peripheral hardware, adapt them to the respective requirements and ensure their long-term operation.

  • Identification and documentation of customer needs

You analyze work processes, derive the user’s requirements from them and document them clearly and comprehensibly.

  • Incident and Problem Management in the Service Desk

You receive fault reports, check and process incidents, initiate root cause analyses and ensure the consistent implementation of solution measures.

What you bring

  • Professional experience in incident, problem, change and release management
  • Very good knowledge of Windows 11, Microsoft 365, Microsoft Entra ID and Active Directory; corresponding Microsoft certifications are an advantage
  • Experience in the administrative environment and a good understanding of organizational processes and interfaces, ideally within a cantonal administration
  • enjoying contact with people and working with internal and external customers, suppliers and other IT organisations
  • You communicate clearly, openly and tackle challenges in a solution-oriented manner
  • An independent, committed and reliable working method and a high level of commitment; you take on responsibility, are actively involved and are ready to develop professionally and personally and take on new tasks

In the Department of Construction, Transport and Environment, we are actively developing the diverse habitat Aargau in the areas of space, energy, environment and mobility – today and for future generations. In the General Secretariat, we support the department head and the departments in the areas of human resources, finance and accounting, computer science, communication and general administrative tasks.
