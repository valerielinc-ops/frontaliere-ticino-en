Full description

The Social Centre for the Integration of Refugees (CSIR), attached to the Directorate of Integration and Solidarity (DIRIS), is responsible for providing social and financial support to refugees who have arrived in the canton of Vaud during the first years of their stay in Switzerland. In your capacity as Specialist File Manager, you monitor payments of financial benefits to refugees taking into account different personal situations.

  • Deliver the financial benefit in accordance with the FSAA, the DFA and their implementing provisions
  • Instructs applicants' entitlement to Income for Integration (RI).
  • Build and update the physical and computer records of the beneficiaries in MAORI software.
  • Meet and support beneficiaries in their rapid and sustainable integration and empowerment.
  • Participate in missions and/or projects led by CSIR. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Ms. Cosette Ikwanga, Multidisciplinary Team Leader, at the e-mail address: dgcs.recruitment@vd.ch
  • Trade Employee SWC or title deemed equivalent required.
  • Certificate of generalist in social insurance.
  • Experience of at least 3 years in administrative, accounting or financial and/or social services.
  • Experience as a trainer in the workplace (FPT) and/or great interest in training apprentices employed in commerce.
  • Knowledge of federal and cantonal social legislation and strong attractiveness for migration.
  • Very good knowledge of social insurance and social action networks.
  • Perfect mastery of common computer tools.
  • Aisance in communication, both in writing and oral.
  • Listen active and benevolent.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
