We are AS Elevators

As the second largest elevator company in Switzerland with 800 employees and more than 30 apprentices, we went to the individuality of an SME to the prospects and safety of a large company. AS offers an ideal start in working life. With us, you will receive a solid and varied training with professional support. After their apprenticeship, we offer talented graduates interesting opportunities for further training and career development.

In our company, we offer

Learning Polymechanicne CFC / Elevator assembler for 2027 in Le Mont-sur-Lausanne

What are your tasks?

  • A 2-year workshop training followed by 2 years of training in elevator assembly
  • Various and varied manual works
  • Accurate manufacturing of metal parts and components using different techniques
  • Mounting and commissioning of elevators from A to Z: from the empty lift duct to the elevator in running condition

What are the requirements?

  • Manual skill and no dizziness
  • A good spatial representation capacity
  • Pleasure in the assembly work on the yards
  • A sense of responsibility, autonomy and reliability
  • Good to very good school grades

Your benefits at home

  • Above average number of holiday days
  • A financial contribution for the driving licence
  • Your own business vehicle
  • A financial contribution for public transport subscriptions
  • Discounts for sport and fitness with selected partners
  • ... among other benefits that you can enjoy, and that you will find here
