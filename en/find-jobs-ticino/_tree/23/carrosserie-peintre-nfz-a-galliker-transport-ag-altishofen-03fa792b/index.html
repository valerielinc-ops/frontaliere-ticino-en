Full description

Carrosserie painter NFZ (a) As a leading transport company, we focus on quality, reliability and innovation in all our services. Our team of dedicated professionals plays a central role in our success. To keep our ever-growing fleet in top condition, we are looking for a truck painter to join our team. Task area

  • Preparation of trucks and other commercial vehicles for painting, including cleaning, grinding and covering.
  • Application of primers, varnishes and protective coatings according to manufacturer specifications and quality standards.
  • GRP repairs
  • Perform finishing and repair work to ensure high surface quality.
  • Maintenance of paint equipment and work area to ensure safe and efficient operation.
  • Collaborate with the maintenance and repair team to coordinate timing and work orders.

Requirement profile

  • trained carrosserie painter
  • trained eye for perfect surfaces
  • motivated for work and pleasure in the automobile
  • independent, reliable, capable of teamwork, resilient and flexible
  • Apprentices also welcome

Offer

  • More hours can be compensated
  • We maintain an uncomplicated interaction with each other and communicate at eye level.
  • With us you have the opportunity to contribute suggestions and to actively shape the future of the company Galliker.

We offer you a varied and interesting job in a super strong team in a top modern workshop. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application. Christoph Jeannerat, Human Resources, telephone: +41 62 748 82 70.
