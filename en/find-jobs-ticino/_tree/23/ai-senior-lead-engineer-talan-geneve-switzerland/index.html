Full description

We are looking for an AI Senior Lead Engineer to provide technical leadership and lead the design, development and deployment of large-scale Artificial Intelligence and Data solutions. You will be responsible for defining architecture, the AI roadmap and coordinating technical teams to ensure robust, scalable and industrialised solutions.

Your responsibilities

  • Define and pilot the IA roadmap.
  • Design and deploy Entity Extraction (ENR) and Document Classification solutions.
  • Develop and industrialize Data & AI architectures.
  • Design and implement institutional ontologies and Knowledge Graphs.
  • Deploy large-scale data processing pipelines.
  • Ensure quality, traceability and auditability of data.
  • Coordinate internal teams, partners and consultants.
  • Ensure technical monitoring and production of solutions.

Skills and experience required

  • Strong expertise in Data Engineering and Artificial Intelligence.
  • Excellent mastery of Python.
  • Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, Message Queues and Data Pipelines.
  • Control of databases, APIs and Model Serving (FastaPI or equivalent).
  • Experience of the Triplestores and SPARQL (GraphWise / OntoText).
  • Knowledge of property graphs: Neo4j, Cypher.
  • Experience of semantic and keyword search engines.
  • Experience in NER model production, multilingual document classification and processing.
  • Experience in processing millions of On-Premise environmental documents.
  • Agile / FASe Methodological Experience.
  • Ability to define Data & AI architectures and coordinate technical teams.

Complementary competences appreciated

  • Knowledge of archival standards: OAIS, RiC-O.
  • Experience in implementing professional ontologies.
  • Experience in Knowledge Graph.
  • Knowledge of RAG (Retrival Augmented Generation) architectures.
  • Experience in OCR and handwriting recognition.
  • Image annotation and metadata quality management.
  • Experience with Vector Databases.
  • Sensitivity to the principles of Security by Design.
