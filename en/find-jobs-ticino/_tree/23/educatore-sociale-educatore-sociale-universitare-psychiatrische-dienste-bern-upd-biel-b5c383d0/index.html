University clinic of psychiatry and psychotherapy of children and adolescents

At the foot of the Jura and in the middle of the country of the three lakes, is Biel - a bilingual city, open to the world and special charm. Bienne is not only known for her bilingualism and her many tour goals in the vicinity, but also fills an important gap in psychiatric care for children and adolescents in the Bernese Jura, Biel and Seeland. In your spare time, do you like to stroll around the old town with its many cafes and small shops? In the day clinic, we treat children aged 6 to 17. Psychic health, two words that say why it is worthwhile for collaborators to engage each day for our children and adolescents. With you? The team follows and supports seven children in a Francophone group in a semi-stationary setting in a beautiful environment in Biel.

Your tasks

  • You work with motivated colleagues in an interdisciplinary team, in which the fields of medicine, therapy, pedagogy and nursing are represented
  • You are committed to the well-being of children and interested in systemic work.
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation requires commitment, flexibility and teamwork
  • You take care to integrate children and adolescents as well as adults by taking care of your thoughts and actions
  • You attach importance to putting your ideas and actions into perspective and you want to broaden your knowledge

Your profile

  • You have HES training in social work, experience in the care of children of school age and good skills in German
  • You show enthusiasm, autonomy, resistance to stress and the willingness to communicate appropriately with our patients and their loved ones
  • You enjoy working in an interdisciplinary environment
  • You have good computer skills
  • You are native French (or German) with very good knowledge of the other language, oral and written

UPZ

The University Psychiatric Centre in Bern (UPZ) is the main centre of competence in psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region as a university clinic. It provides psychiatric care in the canton of Bern throughout the care chain, from early identification to outpatient treatment, day care and stationary treatment, to rehabilitation and forensic psychiatry. With more than 30 sites, UPZ offers a healthcare network covering the entire territory. The UPZ also makes an important contribution in the fields of education, training and research.
