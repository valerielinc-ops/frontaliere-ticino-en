The Opportunity

The PTB Senior Specialist role is part of the People Support Solutions team. The team focuses on payroll, benefits, absence management, customer service, and advisory support to employees, managers, and business partners. The specialist is responsible for executing HR system data maintenance and administrative activities accurately and timely for payroll, tax, social insurance, time, and various local and global benefits. The role contributes to continuous improvement and requires a broad understanding of local HR processes and tools, as well as downstream impacts on employee lifecycle management, finance, procurement, and IT.
