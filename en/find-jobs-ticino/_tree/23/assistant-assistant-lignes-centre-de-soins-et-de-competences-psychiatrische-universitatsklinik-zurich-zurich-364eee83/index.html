The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 90% The Management of Care, Therapies and Social Work (Directorate PTS) is looking for a service-oriented and committed personality for the management of the Care and Competence Center, from February 2027 or by agreement, at the Lenggstrasse location.
