Full description

As part of the medical service centers, anesthesia performs around 6,500 anesthesias annually in a technically broad range. With a 24-hour specialist presence system (shift operation), we ensure anesthesiological care in the operating room, in the intensive care unit, in the birth room, in the emergency department as well as in resuscitations.

Tasks

  • Very well-frequented pain consultation session in a team of two to three pain specialists: women with a high proportion of invasive pain therapy including implantation of neurostimulators
  • Anesthesiological spectrum of a medium-sized cantonal hospital including obstetrics with possibly part-time use in anesthesia including service system
  • Further training of our assistant doctor: inside
  • Assumption of professional responsibilities
  • In case of interest use as an emergency doctor:in SGNOR or equivalent
  • A manageable team that will gladly welcome you into its circle
  • Appreciative interdisciplinary exchange

Requirements

  • Specialist:in anesthesiology, recognized in Switzerland
  • Proven qualification in invasive pain therapy or advanced level of training in it
  • Interested in further developing the pain therapy well-established in the canton and at best in the near future
  • Understanding and interest in living quality in the areas of medicine, medical economics, medical processes, patient contact, stakeholder management and team
  • Willingness to constructively accompany hospitals Schaffhausen in the new hospital construction process

We offer

Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well.

The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
