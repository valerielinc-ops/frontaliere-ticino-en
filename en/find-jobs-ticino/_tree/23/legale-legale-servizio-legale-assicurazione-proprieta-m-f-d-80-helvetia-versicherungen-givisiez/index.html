What you bring

  • You have a completed Swiss law degree and a Swiss bar exam
  • You bring several years of practical professional experience, preferably in an insurance environment and/or in a law firm
  • You have sound knowledge of liability and insurance law
  • You impress with negotiation skills, assertiveness, and a solution-oriented way of working
  • You are characterized by a high willingness to learn and dedication
  • You have business-fluent French language skills in spoken and written form (mandatory) for supporting the Western Switzerland locations and communicate at a very high level in German (communication language in the corresponding team)
  • You are willing to travel to a certain extent (for negotiations throughout Western Switzerland as well as participation in team meetings in Basel)
