Full description

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population high quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and fairness. By joining the Lausanne Care Foundation, you are committed in a concrete way to promoting health and well-being in the canton of Vaud, within expert, motivated and enthusiastic teams, in a varied activity rich in human contacts. In the field, you intervene at the place of life or activity of the beneficiaries. You define with customers the solutions best suited to their situations and thus develop a fine knowledge and a privileged relationship of trust with them. As part of a team that is welded and supported, the exercise of these professions allows for a great autonomy but requires great responsibility and flexibility on the part of our employees. In order to complement our teams, the CMS of the Fondation Soins Lausanne is looking for or to agree on several:

Ergotherapist (M/F)

Participation rate between 60% and 100% Undetermined fixed-term contract

Your Mission

  • Assess the needs of the client and his/her environment, as well as his/her level of independence in carrying out day-to-day activities, and prepare an assessment of the functional capabilities, based on available resources
  • Restoring conditions that enable clients to carry out their daily activities independently, by providing ancillary means and facilities for the living environment and their occupational profile
  • Support clients in developing new physical, psychological and social skills. Actively contribute to the home care of clients through prevention and health promotion measures while taking into account individual needs with personalized care and promoting client autonomy
  • Participate in the development of the client therapeutic project within the interdisciplinary team
  • Participation in development projects and working groups on continuous improvement of benefits in the field of home care and care
  • Actively collaborate in the activities of interdisciplinary teams with a specific role allowing you to value your occupational therapist profession as a full-fledged part of the health system

Your profile and skills

  • Bachelor's degree in occupational therapy or equivalent (foreign diploma recognized by the CRS)
  • Authorization to practice or ready to initiate the proceedings - conditional on the undertaking
  • Experience in physical or psychiatric hospital service, rehabilitation service, home care or private practice an asset
  • Strong interest in community health
  • Ability to collaborate in networking and multidisciplinarity
  • A sense of responsibility, ability to organize independently and set priorities

We offer

  • A stimulating and warm work environment where the caregiver's autonomy is highlighted
  • A working environment that favours human contact and exchange
  • Exciting work in a pleasant environment for the well-being of clients
  • Fair working conditions and remuneration
  • Conditions of occupational security which extend beyond the minimum benefits provided by law
  • Flexibility of working hours and participation rates

The benefits of joining us

  • Plan of integration by occupation, offers of rich and varied continuing training, opportunities for vocational development
  • Gender pay equity ( Fair-ON-Pay certification)
  • Remuneration according to the Collective Labour Convention (CCT) of the public health sector of voodoo
  • Favourable offers with preferential rates in service companies around sport, health and welfare and miscellaneous leisure
  • Soft mobility policy and participation in public transport costs
  • Five weeks of vacation per year and six weeks from 50 years
