Full description

For our customers, we are the first bank. For us, the first bank means constantly inspiring our customers by focusing on their needs. We need you for that! Would you like to join our team at the Gams office? Then come to us!

This is what you will do.

  • You support the customer consultants in the administrative support of their customers and take over the administrative handling of financing, investment, pension and payment transactions in cooperation with the relevant specialist departments.
  • You support in the preparation and follow-up of customer meetings.
  • You will receive our customers in the customer hall and advise them on the basic services as well as our digital applications and bancomats.
  • If necessary, you take over representatives at the counter and provide competent and friendly support for our customers in their daily banking transactions.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed a completed banking apprenticeship or a banking internship.
  • The contact with customers gives you pleasure and you appear professional and sympathetic.
  • You are a communicative and team-oriented personality.
  • A reliable and exact working method as well as a high degree of initiative and independence distinguish you.
  • You actively participate and bring in ideas.

Information Stephan Wirth, Head of Gams Branch, is happy to answer your questions by telephone 081 750 31 59.

Application www.sgkb.ch/karriere, Sandra Schefer, Stv. For this position, no applications from external personnel service providers are considered.
