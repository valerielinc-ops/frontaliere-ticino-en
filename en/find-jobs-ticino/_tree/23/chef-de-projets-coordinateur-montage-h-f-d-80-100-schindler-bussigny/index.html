Project Manager / Coordinator Assembly (m/f/d) 80-100%

Your day - your plan! Organize your 40-hour week according to your needs. We attach great importance to your development and offer you exactly what you need. To learn more about us, check out this video

Your new mission

  • Responsibility for planning and processing orders in assembly.
  • Responsible for ordering from customers: proactive communication with customers regarding the start of assembly, possible delays and delivery of the elevator.
  • Planning the intervention of the assembly staff.
  • Planning of subcontractors and third party companies and delivery of equipment to the site.
  • Claims management and return of equipment to the assembly department.
  • Interlocutor for sale.

Your ideal qualities

  • Basic technical training in electrical engineering or mechanical engineering (knowledge of elevators is an advantage);
  • Continuing training in the commercial field.
  • Experience in order planning and processing is an asset.
  • Good knowledge of MS Office, SAP knowledge is an asset.
  • Good knowledge of the language of the region, other languages are an asset.

  • Healthy working environment: High standards of health and safety and health management at work internal to the company, offering social counselling and health promotion.
  • Business vehicle, which you can use privately.
  • Allowance for lunch.
  • Benefits for employees: Participation in public transport subscriptions and fitness subscriptions, discounts on the fleet, Reka cheques at reduced prices, premium insurance premiums and various benefits.
  • Cultural diversity and inclusion & diversity within the company.
