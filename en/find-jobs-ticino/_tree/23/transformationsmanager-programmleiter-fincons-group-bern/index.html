Full description

The Fincons Group is an IT consulting company that has been shaping the digital future of leading companies in international markets for 40 years. The Fincons Group has made a name for itself by predicting and successfully interpreting new business models and the rapid development of IT systems. She has built a complete range of services: from research and consulting to design and development, from system integration of leading software solutions to application management, supporting her customers step by step on their digital transformation journey. Fincons is a multinational company with more than 2,600 employees and several offices worldwide (in Italy, Switzerland, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Belgium and the United States), but above all a family business where the founders play a strategic role with commitment and passion, building the company on the same principles of a united and caring family. Tasks:

  • Overall responsibility for managing complex transformation and change programs.
  • Responsibility for programme governance, planning, budget, quality, risks and deadlines.
  • Coordination and control of several projects, streams and interdisciplinary teams.
  • Active stakeholder management at executive, management and operational levels.
  • Ensure a transparent decision-making, reporting and escalation structure.
  • Management of organizational changes as well as alignment of business, architecture, delivery and operational goals.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience in managing major transformation, digitalization or strategy programs.
  • Demonstrate success as a program manager, transformation manager, delivery lead or portfolio manager.
  • Experience in managing complex environments with numerous stakeholders and suppliers.
  • In-depth understanding of software development, system integration, enterprise architecture and organizational transformation.
  • Very good knowledge of budget, resource and risk management as well as strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
