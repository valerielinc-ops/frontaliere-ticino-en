The Position Your Department

As part of a global matrix organization, the "Pharma Operations Penzberg" division develops and produces biotechnological active ingredients for innovative drugs for the benefit of our patients with more than 1,000 employees. In order to ensure optimal and efficient production, we provide the active ingredient production with pure media (WFI, CI, pure steam, gases) and in the event of a major maintenance and maintenance activity around our production facilities and buildings. Our team is responsible for GMP compliance in this area.

We are a team of 7 employees and work on the whole range of GMP topics (document management, deviations, training, change control, quality risk management). After an intensive introduction to the basic GMP requirements we want to involve you in the topics of document management and quality risk management. You will work with a diverse and motivated team.

Your Tasks

As an intern, you support the employees of the GMP Service Facilities & Engineering and perform the following activities on your own, according to your instructions or specifications:

  • Creation of standard operating 0705s - Creation of quality risk documents - further quality assurance activities as required

The activity is also largely possible on request, mostly in the form of a remote computer. A laptop is provided. There is no claim to further equipment for mobile work.
