Full description

Portrait The Upper Level Center Biel-Madretsch is one of the three German-speaking upper level centers in the city of Biel. Our school is well organized, multicultural and diverse. We attach great importance to a successful coexistence of all students. Around 300 students attend the 7th to 9th grade in six secondary, nine real classes and two mixed-year real classes. They are taught by around 45 class and specialist teachers. We apply Model 3a (Manuel), in which the subjects German, French and mathematics are taught in level classes, the remaining subjects in the master classes.

Tasks

They teach technical design at a 9th real class. The group size is 9 young people and their lessons take place on Friday afternoon from 1:45-15:20.

The position is temporary and runs in the school year 2026-2027.

Requirements

For the demanding work as a TTG teacher, a PH study and several years of experience is desirable. Natural talents who are still in PH studies are also welcome

  • Willingness to cooperate in the college and participation in various school events as well as working groups are required.

We offer

  • Exciting field of work with high personal responsibility.
  • Various design options for teaching according to LP21.
  • Pleasant working atmosphere in a well-coordinated and very helpful college, which jointly assumes responsibility.
  • Support by the school management, the school secretariat, an IF teacher in real classes and a functioning domestic service.
  • New teachers receive a god/godti
  • Starting job a mentor
  • Modern infrastructure with beamer and visualizer in all classrooms, a PC room and laptop cart for rent in the classroom.
  • Central school location, reachable from Biel station on foot in 15 minutes or by bus with two stations. Parking is limited.
