Warehouse operator

As one of the leading laundry companies in Switzerland and a traditional family business, we have stood for reliability, discretion and personal support for decades. We specialize in the needs of exclusive homes and demanding hosts. Our services impress with the highest quality standards, uncompromising care and absolute reliability – even with the most sensitive textiles and in compliance with the strictest hygiene, safety and sustainability requirements.

Our customers include renowned companies from the luxury hotel industry, which depend on immaculate cleanliness, perfect presentation and smooth processes.

Our success is based on competence, commitment and innovation. That is why we are looking for people who not only collaborate, but also think – personalities who actively contribute their ideas and their energy and want to shape the future together with us.

Your tasks

  • Acceptance and recording of deliveries
  • Incoming goods inspection and warehousing
  • Position wheel ministration and data management
  • Organisation of the camp
  • Creating the annual inventory
  • Outgoing goods and order picking
  • Organisation of internal transport of goods
  • Your profile *
  • Training as a warehouse operator or logistician or at least 5 years of professional practice in logistics, warehouse or forwarding
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing, knowledge of foreign languages are welcome
  • IT\ User Knowledge (MS\ Office)
  • SUVA recognised forklift certificate or willingness to obtain it
  • Careful, independent and forward working methods with a high sense of responsibility
  • Good physical condition
  • Flexibility and serenity in relation to seasonal labour-intensive conditions and the ability to set priorities correctly

We offer

  • A varied activity with a lot of personal responsibility and scope for action
  • A collaborative and motivated team

Are you ready for a new challenge and do you want to become part of our success story? We look forward to your application!
