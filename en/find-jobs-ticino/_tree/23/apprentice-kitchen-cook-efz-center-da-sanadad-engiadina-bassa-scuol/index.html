Full description

We are offering tasks in the kitchen area in the Ospidal and in the Chasa Puntota as of August 1, 2027: Training and duration. During this time you will get to know the different areas of a modern kitchen in large-scale catering. You will gain insight into the different types of food, from gourmet menus to medical food. You will also work with the team at various caterings and banquets. The apprenticeship period lasts three years and ends with obtaining the federal certificate of proficiency “Köchin/Koch EFZ”. ​​​​​​​ Requirements: Your profile

  • You have a secondary or secondary school qualification
  • You are hard-working and sociable with our clients
  • You enjoy working in a team
  • You value flexible working hours with free time when others are working
  • You enjoy and have flair when handling food
  • You have robust health for predominantly standing work We offer: What we offer
  • Interesting and varied areas of responsibility
  • An attractive, motivated working environment
  • Modern infrastructure
  • Good Working atmosphere Under the umbrella of the “Center da sandà Engiadina Bassa” (CSEB) / “Lower Engadin Health Center”, important regional providers in the areas of health care and wellness share a common path. A wonderful environment with a wide range of recreational and sporting opportunities awaits you in the national park region. ​​​​​​​ If you would like further information, please do not hesitate to contact Charlie Maskos, CSEB kitchen training manager, telephone 081 861 10 00.
