Full description

07.09.2026 St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

As a middleware engineer, you design the interfaces that connect our applications and services. You work in a dynamic environment with technologically exciting tasks. For us, it is not only your professional skills that are at the forefront – it is above all important to us how we work together as a team, learn from each other and support each other. To strengthen our Middleware & Integration team in St. Gallen, we are looking for a communicative and solution-oriented personality as a middleware platform engineer (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Engineering, operation and further development of the middleware platform (Kafka, Message, Call and File)
  • Support for developers in the integration of applications
  • Collaboration with experts from network, storage, OS and databases
  • Automation of routine tasks

What do you bring?

  • Completed computer science training or comparable professional experience
  • At least 1 year of experience with Kafka, ideally with Red Hat streams on OpenShift
  • In-depth experience in dealing with APIs (design, integration and operation)
  • Knowledge of IBM MQ is desirable
  • Routine in dealing with enterprise operating systems such as AIX, Linux and Windows
  • Knowledge of Kotlin, Java and Argo CD in connection with helmet are advantageous
  • Readiness for piquettes
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least level C1)
