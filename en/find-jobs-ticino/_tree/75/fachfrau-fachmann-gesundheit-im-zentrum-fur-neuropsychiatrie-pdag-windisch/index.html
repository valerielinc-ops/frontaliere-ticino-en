Full description

Are you ready for a unique challenge? For the neuropsychiatric acute ward, our very well-rehearsed and humorous team is looking for you immediately or by appointment as tasks:

  • Inpatient acute care of people with an intellectual developmental disorder with various psychiatric illnesses in close cooperation with the highly qualified inter-disciplinary team
  • You share responsibility for the implementation of the therapeutic, nursing and educational measures
  • Thanks to early planning in shift work, you have the opportunity to plan your free time and work assignments in advance Requirements:
  • Completed training as a health specialist EFZ, an advantage with experience in psychiatry, acute geriatrics, acute medicine or with people with intellectual developmental disabilities
  • Willingness and interest to familiarize yourself with a highly specialized treatment concept and develop it further
  • Strong personality with strong social and self-competence, as well as enjoyment of complex challenges We offer: Our employees are the heart of our company. That's exactly why we offer you a lot: we strongly encourage further training. We also invest a lot in team cohesion and offer all of our teams access to the “Moodtalk” software, which strengthens team communication. We also offer attractive discounts on well-known brands such as Ikea, Zalando, Coop, Lidl and many more. Employees who commute to us by public transport receive an impressive eco-bonus at the end of the year. We also offer an attractive pension fund solution. We also promote work-life balance: We are always open to part-time work and offer the opportunity to buy a holiday.
