Employee in production & Stv. Production managerin

Do you like to take on responsibility? In this versatile function, you actively participate in production and at the same time support production management in planning, organization and administration. Become part of a dedicated team and actively shape our processes and daily operations.

What you move

  • You can be used flexibly in all production areas (cutting, equipment, freezers and picking)
  • You support purchasing and ordering
  • You consistently implement our internal quality, hygiene and safety guidelines
  • You support the production manager in administrative tasks and in production planning
  • You take over the deputy of the production manager in his absence
  • You actively coordinate and organize day-to-day business

What you bring

  • Education: Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ)
  • Specialty: meat specialist/\-woman
  • Professional experience: at least 2\-3 years of experience in meat processing
  • Several years of professional experience in a production plant
  • Initial leadership experience or willingness to assume leadership responsibility
  • High quality and sense of responsibility
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Secure handling of the common MS\Office\ applications
  • English (C1\)

What we offer you

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave / 8 weeks paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Social benefits: 730 days of sick leave with full net wages
  • Cumulus points: 2\-fold Cumulus points on food and 5\-fold on non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees
  • Holidays: 5 to 7 weeks holidays per year depending on and service
  • Club School Migros: refund of 70% of the course costs, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Sports and fitness offers: organization of various sporting events with discounts
  • Leisure and cultural offers: You benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events
  • You\-Culture: At Migros you smell \- because it is simply more relaxed
