Full description

Are you a leader and interested in the topics of living and care in old age? You want to go by 4. January 2027 or by agreement as Head of Digital Development & Projects for Health Centers for Old Age?

  • You are strategically and operationally responsible for the implementation and further development of the IT strategy in your area of responsibility and align resources and activities consistently with the corporate goals.
  • They bear the overall responsibility for the IT portfolio and demand management, ensure the holistic management of all IT change projects and ensure their alignment with strategic goals, benefits, profitability, risks and available resources.
  • They actively shape cooperation between business and IT, identify needs at an early stage and ensure coordinated planning, transparent communication and sustainable partnerships with internal and external stakeholders.
  • They are responsible for the further development of the IT and corporate architecture (Enterprise Architecture), ensure compliance with architecture principles and standards and ensure the quality and future viability of new IT solutions as well as their successful integration into the company.
  • You promote innovation, knowledge transfer and a learning-oriented team culture, develop your employees further and accompany organizational changes so that new solutions, technologies and ways of working are established in a sustainable manner.
  • Several years of management experience as well as comprehensive competence in the management of complex IT projects and digitization and transformation projects
  • Integrity and sovereign appearance, coupled with strong communication, cooperation and a good sense of people and change
  • Analytical and networked thinking, strategic understanding as well as a structured, solution-oriented and forward-looking working method with high goal and result orientation
  • Completed IT training with qualification at RF/FH level or equivalent, preferably complemented by specific knowledge in IT management, project, portfolio or process management
  • In-depth knowledge of IT portfolio and demand management as well as a good understanding of IT architectures and IT service management, combined with strong skills in stakeholder management and cross-divisional cooperation

An exciting task area in a professional and team-oriented working environment with many design possibilities. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The central services division is located in Zurich-Oerlikon and provides various services within the entire service department. These include computer science (technical IT), finance, HR, marketing, communication, facility management, construction development, central purchasing and quality management.
