Full description

Are you enthusiastic about AI and would like to use your expertise to advance innovative AI solutions in practice? Then you are right with us. What you do

  • You develop and optimize Suva’s central AI platform and ensure its reliable operation.
  • For development and product teams, you create the technical conditions to use AI services safely and efficiently.
  • Working closely with architecture, security and platform teams, you will promote the reuse of shared platform services and self-service offerings.
  • For AI agents, multi-agent systems and agent-based business processes, you define standards and best practices.
  • With your know-how, you actively shape the development and continuous development of Suva’s AI capabilities. What you bring
  • You have a university degree in computer science (ETH, university or university of applied sciences) or a comparable education.
  • As a software engineer, platform engineer, AI engineer or in a similar role, you have gained around five years of professional experience.
  • In setting up, operating or further developing cloud and platform solutions, you will bring practical experience.
  • With Agent Harnesses, Agentic AI Frameworks and Foundation and Frontier LLMs you have gained initial experience.
  • You are interested in modern AI technologies and their safe, productive use in the corporate environment.
