Full description

Portrait To strengthen our logo team, we are looking for a speech therapist for the municipalities of Reichenbach.

Tasks

With your inclusive pedagogical attitude, you are responsible for the clarification and assessment of children in the linguistic field. They treat with their appreciative nature disturbances of communication, spoken and written language. With your authentic nature, you advise and support guardians and teachers. They work interdisciplinary with the MR team (IBEM team), school management and specialist departments and actively participate in the exchange.

Requirements

Wanting to be a trained speech therapist / speech therapist You contribute your creative ideas and have a high communication, team and reflective ability. With pleasure and interest, you contribute to internal and interdisciplinary cooperation. You meet the everyday challenges with humor, flexibility and serenity.

We offer

We have our own MR office with a lot of teaching materials in Frutigen. The speech therapy takes place centrally in a well-equipped speech therapy room in the upper school building in Reichenbach.

The team consists of experienced, motivated and committed specialist teachers who would like to exchange ideas with other speech-only teachers. We attach great importance to interdisciplinary cooperation within the team and the logo team. Regular logo meetings and intervisions strengthen this cooperation. You have the opportunity to work with us indefinitely.

You can also apply for two. Passengers can be made flexible.
