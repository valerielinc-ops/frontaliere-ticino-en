Full description

Introduction

Do you want to take responsibility and ensure that our production lines run smoothly, efficiently and reliably? For our customer we are looking for you as a plant operator in production. In this role, you control, monitor and optimize the production facilities – with a focus on quality, processing speed and deadline compliance.

Tasks

  • Self-responsible operation and monitoring of production plants during the entire shift operation
  • Setting up, converting and starting the machines according to order and work plan
  • Ensuring product quality through in-process controls, approvals and compliance with specifications and process specifications
  • Detect and correct disturbances; carry out simple maintenance
  • Escalation after SOP as needed
  • Active participation in process improvements to increase efficiency and plant stability
  • Documentation of production data in the system (feedbacks, checklists, deviations) and reporting to team management

Profile

  • Completed training in production or manufacturing, e.g. as a polymechanic or equivalent
  • Professional experience in production, ideally in the control of production facilities
  • Strong technical understanding and safe handling of production machines
  • self-employed, structured and responsible working practices
  • Team-oriented and strong communication
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Other languages are beneficial

Advantages

  • Modern production facilities and well-equipped workplaces in a clean, organized environment
  • Opportunities for professional training and personal development
  • Collegial team and short decision paths
