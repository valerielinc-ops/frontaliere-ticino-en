Full description

You support project managers in the planning of ventilation, heating and sanitary facilities, from the idea to the delivery to the contractor.

Tasks

  • Support project leaders on building technology projects
  • Develop project, submission and implementation files
  • Making plans and diagrams using 2D and 3D CAD
  • Develop autonomously small ventilation, heating or sanitary facilities
  • Accompany the installations of the first idea until delivery to the contractor

Profile

  • Training as a building technology planner or equivalent
  • Good knowledge of CAD
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office
  • Open mind and taste for teamwork
  • Reliability and flexibility
  • Knowledge of foreign languages, French is an asset

Supply

  • Activity rate from 60 to 100%
  • Immediate entry or to be agreed
  • 3 to 6 months of acting, then fixed commitment
