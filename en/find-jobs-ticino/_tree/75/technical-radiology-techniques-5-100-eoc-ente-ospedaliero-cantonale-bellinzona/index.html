Technical / Radiology techniques - Ausschreibung L.EOC The Ticino multisite hospital is present with its institutes throughout the cantonal territory for a total of 1,000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring to the population a comprehensive and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required.

Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to human relationships, the ECC provides quality health care by taking care of over 41,000 patients a year and providing over 600,000 outpatient consultations.

The Institute of Integrated Diagnostics of the Iconic Switzerland (IDISI) brings together the clinics of radiology, nuclear medicine, pathology and laboratory medicine and, with its about 600 collaborators and almost 4 million examinations and analyses carried out each year, offers a complete and high quality diagnostic service, responding to the needs of patients resident throughout the cantonal territory.

By overcoming the traditional specialized barriers, IDISI adopts a multidisciplinary approach to favour more efficient and personalized diagnostic and therapeutic pathways, able to respond in a targeted way to the specific needs of each patient. Moreover, the Institute is oriented to university teaching and the promotion of biomedical research. The Radiologists Clinic carries out over 260,000 examinations per year, thanks to the effective integration of the skills of its 45 doctors and 150 radiology technicians and a. cutting-edge technological infrastructure.

To complete our team of the Radiology Clinic in the various locations of Mendrisio, Lugano, Bellinzona and Locarno, we look for Technicians / Radiology techniques (5-100) Profile: The people we are looking for are pro-positive, flexible, dynamic and action oriented. They maintain calm and self-control even in case of heavy workload and are able to manage properly the moments of pressure. They can make complex decisions through a careful analysis of problems in order to search for the best solutions.

They use their time efficiently, focusing their efforts on the most important priorities and are able to manage more activities at a time. They establish effective and constructive relationships with internal and external customers, gaining the trust of their interlocutors and managing to meet their expectations. They are open and able to take advantage of the advice of those with more experience. Technically able, they are comfortable with the new technologies and innovations in general that they can learn with ease.

Like us, they believe in the importance of putting the patient at the center of all our attentions and sharing the values and mission of the Cantonal Hospitaller, fully identifying himself in them.
