Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offers merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For the Senevita Westside in Bern we are looking for an internship Care 60-80 immediately or by appointment % The duration of the internship can be determined individually. Your tasks

  • In everyday life you take over the competent basic care according to individual needs.
  • You help with activating and supervising tasks (menu selection, daily structure, etc.). You report your observations to the responsible nurse.
  • You ensure order and cleanliness in the care area taking into account the company's internal regulations.

You bring that with you

  • You enjoy working with older people and respect their dignity and self-determination.
  • You are interested in nursing tasks and want to learn.
  • Working in a team is fun for you and you are a positive person.

We offer you

  • A versatile and interesting work and an insight into the comprehensive activities in long-term care
  • A consciously maintained, positive company culture with various employee events
  • Inexpensive lunch in the in-house gastronomy
  • Coffee, tea, water, fruits and bread are available for free

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
