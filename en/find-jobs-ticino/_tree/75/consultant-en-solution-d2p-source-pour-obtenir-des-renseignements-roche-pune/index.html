The Position Are you passionate about transforming procurement processes through technology and data? Join Roche and play a key role in shaping the future of our global ERP landscape.

As a Senior ERP Solution Consultant, you will partner with business and IT stakeholders to design and deliver innovative solutions across the Source-to-Procure (S2P) lifecycle. You will bridge business needs with technical capabilities, driving efficiency, data integrity, and digital transformation across procurement processes.

This is a highly visible role where you will contribute to global initiatives, influence solution design, and support the evolution of Roche’s procurement ecosystem.

Your Opportunity

In this role, you will

  • Lead end-to-end analysis and solution design across the Source-to-Procure lifecycle, including sourcing, contracting, purchasing, and invoicing
  • Translate complex business requirements into scalable technical solutions and user stories
  • Act as a functional expert for procurement platforms (e.g., SAP S/4HANA, Ariba, Coupa, GEP), ensuring system stability and continuous improvement
  • Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders across Procurement, Finance, and IT to deliver integrated solutions
  • Drive testing activities (SIT, UAT) and ensure high-quality, business-ready system deployments
  • Support product and roadmap discussions, balancing quick wins with long-term system sustainability
  • Contribute to process optimization, data quality, and user adoption through training and change management

Who you are

You bring a strong mix of functional expertise, problem-solving skills, and stakeholder management:

  • 6–8 years of experience in ERP solution consulting or business analysis within procurement or Source-to-Pay environments
  • Hands-on experience with platforms such as SAP S/4HANA, SAP Ariba, Coupa, GEP, or similar
  • Strong understanding of end-to-end Source-to-Procure / Procure-to-Pay processes
  • Experience translating business needs into technical requirements and implementation plans
  • Familiarity with system integrations, APIs, or middleware solutions is an advantage
  • Ability to work in agile environments and manage multiple priorities
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to influence and collaborate across teams
