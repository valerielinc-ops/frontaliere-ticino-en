Full description

Entry by arrangement · Bruderholz, OP Management · Job reference 12774 · Permanent employment

Tasks

In the operating room, you are the central contact person for safe and precise storage. You ensure that patients are comfortable and optimally positioned and that each procedure runs smoothly. You work closely with the surgical team and are deployed in the areas of spine, hip, shoulder, knee, trauma, visceral surgery and ENT.

  • When receiving the patients, you ensure that they feel safe and well accompanied
  • Before, during and after the operation Precise bearings and ensure that each position meets the requirements of the operation.
  • The installation of the bearings takes place individually according to the specifications of the surgeons.
  • Medical devices and materials ready You prepare professionally and serve them safely throughout the procedure.
  • According to modern standards, you pay attention to patient safety and the correct implementation of all storage guidelines.
  • After the procedure, you accompany the patients safely from the operating room and ensure a smooth conclusion.

Requirements

  • Completed vocational training as a professional health EFZ or other medical training
  • Specialist course for operations bearings of advantage or willingness to complete it
  • Ideally, professional experience in a comparable function and great interest in the operating environment
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • High dedication and willingness to perform weekend and night service
  • Precise, resilient, empathetic and team capable personality with a sense of exact work
