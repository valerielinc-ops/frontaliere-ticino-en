Location: Clinic Permanence Bern

Occupation by: by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73993

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

In our lively emergency area at the clinic Permanence, you play an important role in the care and monitoring of our patients. In doing so, you will work closely with our surrogate and hospital doctors and help ensure that our patients feel professional and well looked after at all times.

Your benefits to us

  • Family working environment
  • Staff restaurant with discounted conditions
  • Up to two additional weeks of holiday (Leisure Plus model)
  • Participation in public transport subscription
  • Discount on fitness subscription
  • Four additional free days per year at 100% allowance as compensation for changing time

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • Care and supervision of patients in an emergency under the guidance of the attending and hospital doctors
  • Independent implementation of all MPA activities such as laboratory, X-ray, ECG, infusions and plaster casts
  • Organization and implementation of consultation hours together with our attending physicians
  • Ensuring a smooth process in the varied emergency routine

Your profile

  • Completed training as medical practice assistant / medical practice assistant (MPA)
  • Emergency Experience Benefits
  • Readiness for early and late services
  • High load capacity, flexibility and strong teamwork
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work
