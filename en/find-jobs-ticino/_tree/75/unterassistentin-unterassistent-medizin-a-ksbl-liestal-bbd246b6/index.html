Tasks

  • You will learn how to deal with patients and their relatives at the acute department for general internal medicine
  • Elevation of medical history and status of internist patients

  • Intermediate results of examinations of medical studies (if available)
  • Copy of passport or identity card (and proof of health insurance if resident outside Switzerland).
