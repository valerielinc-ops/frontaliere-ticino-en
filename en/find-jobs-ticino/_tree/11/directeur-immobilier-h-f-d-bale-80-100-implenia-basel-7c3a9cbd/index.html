Full description

At our “Living” location in Basel, tenants not only feel at home, but are also managed optimally and efficiently by our new division of tasks in the team. This not only has advantages for tenants and ownership, but also for you. Through more specialization in the team, we work more closely together, and everyone can focus on their strengths.

If your heart beats for real estate, you are motivated, goal-oriented and also like to work in a team, then we look forward to your application as

Real estate manager (m/f/d)

Basel, 80 - 100 %

This is what moves you

  • You take responsibility for managing a portfolio of residential and mixed-use properties
  • This includes ensuring regular and extraordinary real estate maintenance, terminating and carrying out property visits and acceptances.
  • You are in contact with the ownership, tenant, house maintenance, craft businesses and offices
  • Negotiating and drawing up business rental contracts as well as the annual budget is also your responsibility.
  • In order to meet all these tasks, you can count on the support of both technical management and rental team members as well as the handling and home maintenance that you provide.
  • At the "Living" location, you will also take care of the personal support of the local customers together with your team.

This is why you succeed

  • You have a basic commercial education and a real estate management card (or are in training). In addition, you bring several years of professional experience in this area
  • Digital affinity and in-depth PC and software knowledge are skillfully used daily in working with different systems
  • An efficient and goal-oriented working method as well as good communication skills with negotiating skills qualify you in dealing with different and demanding target groups
  • A high service and customer orientation are a matter of course for you
  • Good manners, high load capacity as well as flexibility and endurance round off your profile

Here are some of our benefits

  • Working hours: We live mobile work with individual (year) working hours and/or part-time work. Up to 40% home office is possible. Work-life balance is a priority for us, because balanced and relaxed employees are important to us. The purchase of additional holidays is possible.
  • Mobility and food: You will receive a contribution for the mobile phone. For your food, we also make a contribution.
  • Education and training: Promoting employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support in internal and external education and training. We offer career opportunities in management, specialist and project functions
  • Salär: With us, you can expect market-based remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. The pension benefits are attractive and you can choose the right one for you from different models. The accident protection insurance covers the private hospital department.
