Full description

The police canton of Solothurn guarantees a high level of security for the people in the canton of Solothurn - competent day and night. For the command department we are looking for on 01.02.2027 or by agreement at the Solothurn location a Stv. service chief and specialist responsible for the integrated situation picture, 80-100%.

Your responsibility

  • As deputy service manager and specialist responsible for the integrated situational picture (ILB), you assume the personnel, organizational, qualitative, financial and performance-related management responsibility for the service in the absence of the service manager.
  • You independently manage projects or sub-projects in the topic area Situation, in particular with regard to the specialist application ILB.
  • The management of cantonal working groups and specialist groups for the development of situation-specific products as well as the participation in these committees are just as much part of your tasks as the co-determination, management and quality assurance of these products.
  • They manage the ILB department professionally and bear responsibility for the operation, content, up-to-date and quality of the data as well as for the user training of the application.
  • If necessary, you support leadership and create reports for managers and other relevant bodies. They integrate security-relevant information across departments and prepare it as the basis for sound analyses and recommendations for action.

Your profile

  • For this function, you will bring a completed police school or equivalent training and have mandatory expertise in the areas of police situation and ILB.
  • You preferably have several years of professional and managerial experience in the police environment, are a generalist with police tactical and technical expertise and have a good knowledge of the relevant laws and regulations as well as IT affinity. Further training in the field of project management is advantageous.
  • With your leadership, social and communication skills, your organizational ability and your pronounced assessment skills as well as negotiating skills, independence and flexibility, you convince in everyday work.
  • You enjoy preparing and analyzing data and can work on security-relevant topics and present them convincingly.
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as the willingness for extraordinary assignments and pikett services round off your profile. You are in possession of category B driving licence.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
