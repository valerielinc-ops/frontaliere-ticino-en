Full description

Do you like personal contact with customers? Active sales and advice give you joy? Together, we create links in Switzerland. With you, we connect with people.

Make a difference

  • Be part of Payerne's committed team and bring your sense of human contact with people coming to the counter and the sales area.
  • You sell all postal and non-post mark items, such as regional products or various telecommunications offerings.
  • You are also actively advising our clients on services from different sectors, such as health funds, insurance and banks.
  • Within your team, you have the opportunity to assume responsibilities in different roles and thus continue your personal development.
  • If you have never worked in a branch of the Post Office before, we will prepare for it! During our four-month "on the job" training, you prepare optimally for your new role as customer advisor or advisor. During this period, your occupancy rate is 80% and once you graduate, you work part-time as you wish. More information on professional conversion can be found in the Factsheet Reconversion à la Poste .

What you bring

  • Your innate sense of sales and your open and sociable character allow you to actively seek exchange with our customers.
  • You justify basic training in retailing or selling.
  • A nice and self-sustaining natural, you're comfortable in all sorts of situations.
  • You master digital technology and use common MS Office programs with ease.
  • In order to give competent advice, you must have excellent command of French. Knowledge in other languages is an asset.
