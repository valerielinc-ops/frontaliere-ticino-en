Full description

Urology Cabinet – Nyon To strengthen our team within our urology firm in Nyon, we are looking for a motivated, organized and conscientious medical secretary for a 40% position.

Your main responsibilities

  • Telephone call management and patient reception.
  • E-mail and correspondence management.
  • Strike, layout and follow-up of medical reports.
  • Administrative management of patient records.
  • Document filing and archiving.
  • Various administrative tasks related to the activity of the firm.

Your profile

  • •You have a medical secretary training or equivalent experience.
  • Experience in the medical field, ideally in urology, is an asset.
  • You have an excellent command of French, both oral and written.
  • You're comfortable with computer tools.
  • You are organized, autonomous, versatile and have a sense of responsibility.
  • You appreciate contact with patients and show empathy and discretion.
  • You strictly respect the confidentiality of medical information.

We offer you

  • A 40% position in a medical firm specializing in urology.
  • A professional and pleasant working environment.
  • A varied activity with diverse administrative responsibilities.

Interested? We invite you to send us your application (CV and cover letter) to Durrsim.ramadani@svmed-hin.ch We look forward to meeting you!
