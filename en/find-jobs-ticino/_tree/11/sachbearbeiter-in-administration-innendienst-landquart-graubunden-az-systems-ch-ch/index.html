Personnel/in Administration & Interior Service 100% Place of work: Landquart With over 15 years of experience in building automation, we stand for quality, reliability and practical solutions. Our projects range from control and regulation to the optimization of heating, ventilation, air conditioning, lighting and shading. We work project-oriented, with short decision-making paths and in close contact with our customers. As part of the Burkhalter Group, we also benefit from the support and network of a strong Swiss group of companies. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a reliable and versatile personality for in-house service. Your tasks

  • General administration and telephone exchange
  • Processing of mail and post
  • Preparation of offers, order confirmations and invoices
  • Management of debtors and creditors incl. Payment and dunning services
  • Participation in monthly and annual accounts and VAT accounts
  • Support for material procurement and management
  • Coordination of appointments, deliveries and service missions
  • Maintenance of customer, project and article data in the system
  • Administrative support of the team in internal processes, projects and organizational tasks

Your profile

  • Basic commercial training or equivalent qualification
  • Technical flair and interest in building automation
  • Very good knowledge of German, English is an advantage
  • Good MS Office knowledge, experience with ERP systems is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Organizational talent, team spirit and pleasure in customer contact

Your advantages

  • An established and motivated team
  • a versatile and varied area of responsibility
  • Modern workplace
  • Flexible working hours
  • 5+ weeks holidays
  • Opportunity for further training
  • Fruits, vegetables, coffee and tea for a healthy start to the day

Building automation is teamwork. While our technicians are out, you keep the strings together in the office and make sure everything runs smoothly. Sounds like you? Then we would like to meet you.
