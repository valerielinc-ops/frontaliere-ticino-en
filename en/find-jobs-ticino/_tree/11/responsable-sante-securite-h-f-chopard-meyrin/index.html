MISSION The mission of the post is to promote and coordinate actions related to the preservation of the health of employees, the prevention of risks and the development of a culture of health/safety at work. He advises the hierarchy for bringing the company into line with the relevant legal requirements, directives and orders and industry solutions. It shall keep itself informed of developments in the legislation and inform all concerned. It prepares, documents and updates files relating to all OHS measures undertaken by the company. It compiles and comments on the necessary statistics. Its tasks also include the training and facilitation of internal stakeholder teams (firefighters, first aid workers, rescue workers, OSH commission), as well as the maintenance of the means and equipment at their disposal.
