Army Digital Media (DMA) is the competence center for multimedia communication and training products within the Defense Group. We combine the latest technologies with challenging projects and provide an environment in which you can be creative and develop professionally. You bring your first professional experience in the digital sector and are eager to further develop your skills in a wide range of projects? Do you want to take responsibility and make a meaningful contribution to the Swiss army? Then we should get to know each other – we look forward to your application!

You can make this contribution

  • Lead complex multimedia projects in a heterogeneous, dynamic environment and ensure quality
  • Create concepts, scripts and production documents for e-learning, explanatory films and other product categories
  • Web-based training (WBTs) and explanatory films with authoring tools (e.g. Articulate Storyline 360, Articulate Rise, Vyond)
  • Coordinate and manage media production (speech ex, videos, illustrations, graphics, photos) for multimedia productions
  • If necessary, further develop in the subject areas VR/AR and other product categories

This makes them unique.

  • training in project management, vocational or secondary education with a methodological-didactic focus or comparable training or professional practice; high affinity for educational media, preferably also for communication media
  • Several years of experience in the production of multimedia, interactive educational and communication tools, experience with Articulate Storyline 360 and Adobe Creative Cloud
  • Personal responsibility and quality awareness, service orientation and pleasure in working with different stakeholders
  • High degree of willingness to learn and change: Fun with new technologies and joy to familiarize yourself with new tasks

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force.
