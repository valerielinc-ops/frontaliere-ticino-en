Full description

Do you love strong machines, modern technology and varied applications? Then you are exactly right at Liebherr. As a service technician, you ensure that our state-of-the-art construction machines on construction sites throughout German-speaking Switzerland provide top performance. No day is like the other – and that’s exactly what makes the job so exciting. These are your tasks

  • With maintenance, repair and inspection work, you ensure that our Liebherr earthmoving devices are ready for use at all times.
  • You get to the bottom of technical failures and quickly get the machines running again.
  • You carry out warranty work as well as technical retrofits and changes according to the current service information.
  • You make new, rental and special equipment fit for use – from provision to installation to commissioning.
  • You are on site with our customers, support them with your technical know-how and ensure first-class service.
  • You document your assignments in maintenance, inspection and service reports.
  • You inspire our customers with your professional and solution-oriented way of working and build long-term relationships. You bring that with you
  • Completed training as a construction machinery mechanic EFZ, agricultural machinery mechanic EFZ, automotive mechatronics specialist EFZ specialisation in commercial vehicles or engine equipment mechanic EFZ
  • Ideally, first professional experience in the field of construction machinery or related vehicle technology
  • An independent, reliable working method, team spirit and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Joy of direct customer contact and a secure appearance
  • Location in German-speaking Switzerland and very good knowledge of German
  • Category B driving licence (BE as well as truck, lifting platform or air conditioning test are a plus)
  • Readiness for travel, flexible assignments, piketting service and technical training You can look forward to
  • Exciting machines and technology that inspires
  • Flexibility through modern working hours and infrastructure
  • Own service vehicle and modern work equipment
  • A strong team in a successful group of companies
