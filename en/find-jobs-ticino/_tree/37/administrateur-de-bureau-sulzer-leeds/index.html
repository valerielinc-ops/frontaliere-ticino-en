Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

At Sulzer, we’re known globally for engineering excellence – but here in Leeds, we’re especially proud of the people who make it happen. With a team of around 350 highly skilled colleagues on site, we specialise in advanced engineering, precision manufacturing and high quality pump services that support critical sectors across the UK and worldwide. About the Role

We’re looking for a proactive, organised and people focused Administrator who loves variety and isn’t afraid to take the initiative. As the first point of contact for visitors, customers and colleagues, you’ll be a friendly, confident presence who enjoys building rapport and interacting with people at all levels of the business. This is a hands on, fast paced role for someone who naturally spots what needs doing and gets on with it. You’ll take ownership of tasks, solve problems as they arise and help keep everything running smoothly behind the scenes. Working as part of a supportive Administration team, you’ll collaborate closely with departments across the business, providing practical support, coordination and ideas to help teams work efficiently and effectively.

What You’ll Be Doing

  • Working as part of the administrative team to ensure smooth daily operations – providing flexible support to teams across the site when business needs require.
  • Greeting visitors and managing all front-of-house activity with professionalism and warmth
  • Handling incoming calls, customer queries and complaints, ensuring swift and effective resolution
  • Overseeing meeting room management
  • Managing business systems (SAP, Concur, Egencia, Softworks) and producing reports
  • Coordinating wellbeing initiatives and key site communications
  • Administering clock cards, SAP set-ups, post handling, parking permits and visitor logistics
  • Supporting emergency procedures and promoting a safe working environment
  • Ensuring excellent customer experience at every touchpoint – representing the Administration team’s commitment to collaborative, business-wide service.

What You’ll Bring

  • Strong interpersonal and customer service skills
  • Great organisational ability and a high level of attention to detail
  • Confidence working independently and taking ownership of tasks
  • Flexibility, problem-solving mindset and ability to adapt in a fast-paced environment
  • Experience in administration or assistant roles
  • Good working knowledge of Microsoft Office packages
