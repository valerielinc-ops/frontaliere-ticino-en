Full description

Thanks to its 800 employees, the Intercantonal Hospital of Broye (HIB) provides an acute care and rehabilitation mission. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative health institution striving for excellence for the benefit of the Broyard population. In order to strengthen its Care Pool team, the HIB care management is looking for a: Community care and health assistant Graduate on contract hours / requests on calls Your mission

  • Collaborate closely with the medical care team in the care of patients (reception – installation – mobilization – planning, etc.)
  • Ensure the execution of medical-technical procedures and the monitoring of care delegated by qualified staff
  • Provide care independently falling within its field of expertise in stable care situations
  • Carry out administrative and logistical work inherent to your activity, mainly linked to patient care
  • Ensure good information and the quality of reception for patients and their loved ones
  • Be a force for proposals and ensure initiatives to improve the efficiency of care
  • Apply and enforce the rules of hospital hygiene and safety in force in the institution Your assets
  • You benefit from a CFC Assistant in Community Care and Health
  • Excellent command of spoken and written French
  • You are a dynamic, involved, motivated person with a sense of responsibility
  • With a good sense of communication, you have a marked interest in multidisciplinary collaboration and demonstrate speed and precision in the execution of your tasks
  • Experience in acute care is an asset
  • Knowledge of the Soarian computerized patient file is an asset
  • Availability and autonomy We offer you Start of employment : immediately or to be agreed For any additional questions about this position, please contact Ms Françoise Roth, assistant to the care director and coordinator of the care pool on 026 662 86 79 or francoise.roth@hibroye.ch. In the event of technical difficulties, we invite you to contact our recruitment department on 026 662 81 91.

Requirements

  • Hold a CFC in Community Health Care Assistant

  • Excellent command of spoken and written French

  • Be a dynamic, involved, motivated person with a sense of responsibility

  • Experience in acute treatments is an asset

  • Knowledge of the computerized patient file Soarian is an asset

  • Availability and autonomy
