Become part of SBB and make an important contribution with your work for a reliable railway of today and tomorrow. This is exactly what you are doing together with your team. You can expect a versatile and varied working environment in the railway technology centre. You take responsibility, contribute your ideas and work together with your team to develop solutions for current challenges. You work at the interface between people, processes and modern logistics.   • Together with your teammates, you plan and manage the day-to-day business in the central warehouse and take over the professional management of four employees. You coordinate the logistics processes, support your team with technical questions and ensure optimal supply of production and delivery customers.
  • As the first point of contact, you support colleagues in internal troubleshooting, both on the system side and on topics relating to the automated warehouse infrastructure. For partners: Inside you ensure a high delivery performance and live a pronounced customer orientation.
  • You will gladly support the logistics team in their daily work and train employees with the help of process and training documentation.
  • You are responsible for master data quality in SAP and create evaluations and analyses. With this, you create the load and capacity preview, monitor the processes and prioritize the warehouse orders.
  • In various logistics projects, you drive the further development of supply chain processes and lead sub-projects in your specialist area.
  • Thanks to your commitment, ordered products in the right quantity and quality are delivered to the customer and to the right place in time.

You bring that with you.

  • With your organizational and coordination talent as well as your own initiative and expertise, you ensure smooth warehouse operations.
  • You work responsibly and reliably in the team and bring a high level of social skills.
  • With your communication skills, you work in partnership with internal and external contact persons and live customer and process orientation as well as a high level of safety and quality awareness.
  • When dealing with numbers, you feel comfortable, use in-depth user knowledge in ERP systems such as SAP EWM and work routinely with MS Office.
  • You bring a vocational apprenticeship as a logistics specialist: in EFZ and have completed a higher specialist training as a process or logistics specialist. You also have experience in the professional management of employees.
  • You bring German skills at level C1, French skills are an advantage. In addition, you have a valid forklift and the driver’s license of category B.
