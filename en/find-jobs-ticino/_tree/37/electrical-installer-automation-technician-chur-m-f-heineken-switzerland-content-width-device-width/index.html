Full description

For the Technical Service department at our site in Chur, we are looking for a motivated and team-oriented person starting from 1st March 2026 or upon agreement. You will work within the Technical Service in a young, dynamic, and collegial team. The focus will be on the maintenance of production and filling plants, as well as energy supply in our brewery. Tasks: Maintenance, troubleshooting, and maintenance work on production machines and building services. Development of solutions for control and automation issues. Implementation of improvements (processes, automations). Creation and application of technical documentation. Conducting root cause analyses. Collaboration in projects (commissioning of production machines in the electrical engineering sector). Programming of programmable controllers (PLC). Requirements: Completed apprenticeship as an electrical installer (NIV15 and above) or automation technician. 3-4 years of experience in maintenance. Willingness to work in shifts (three-shift operation with night shifts). Expertise in signal, control, and regulation areas. Good knowledge of MS-Office (SAP preferred). Experience with PLCs preferred (S7 and TIA). Very good German language skills and possibly English. Experience and willingness for continuous development. Are you interested in a versatile role as part of a dynamic team? Then Claudia Tschuor Magnin, HR Business Partner, looks forward to receiving your application. Respect, enjoyment, and quality from passion are our core values. As a beer producer with brewery sites in Chur and Lucerne, as well as our own beverage trade with 17 locations across Switzerland, we are a company of national significance. We strive for a mix of tradition, innovation, and customer proximity. With our beer brands, we contribute to the diverse Swiss beer culture. We brew the joy of true togetherness to inspire a better world. One day, one beer, one toast after another.
