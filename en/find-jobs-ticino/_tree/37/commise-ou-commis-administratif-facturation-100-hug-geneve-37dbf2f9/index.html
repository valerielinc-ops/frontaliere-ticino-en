Full description

The billing office is responsible for processing cases in four departments: the Department of Medicine (D-MED), the Department of Community Medicine and First Recourse (D-MCPR), the Department of Oncology (D-ONCO), and the Department of Aigue Medicine (D-MA). Within the Department of Medicine (CAF) Admission and Invoicing Centre team, you ensure the processing of outpatient claims invoices, different rejection lists, telephone response to different requests, patient reception for immediate invoices and any other missions related to your function. You are part of a continuity of service dynamic by taking over from your colleagues in case of absence and promoting the exchange of information within the team. You do not hesitate to improve your activities by seeking adequate help. More generally, you contribute to all activities aimed at improving the quality of services.
