Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate - that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company now employs over 3,000 people from more than 80 countries, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glarus. Against a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. The company's «Retail» department comprises over 250 chocolate shops in over 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. You will have the opportunity to work in one of our Swiss chocolate shops and complete a multi-year EFZ training for retail sales. There, you will learn how to advise customers competently, decorate shop windows and shelves, and organize various work processes. We will familiarize you with hygiene regulations and food laws and introduce you to the world of ordering. Do you have a desire to enter the world of work and learn something new? Then come to us. This is what you can expect. You will be supported and accompanied in the transition from school to the world of work by us. You will be individually and personally guided by our branch managers. You will receive a diverse, practice-oriented training with excellent prospects. This is what you bring with you. You love chocolate. You have a secondary school diploma (A) or a good secondary school diploma (B). You have a quick grasp and prefer a dynamic environment. You enjoy contact with people and are communicative. You appreciate a meticulous and reliable work style. Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh. Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Karin Morger, Branch Manager, at +41 31 312 02 34, is happy to answer any questions you may have.
