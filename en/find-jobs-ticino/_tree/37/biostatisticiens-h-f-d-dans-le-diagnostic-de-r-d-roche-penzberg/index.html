The Position As a biostatistician (m/f/d) you will work in a highly qualified team within the research and development of Roche Diagnostics. Our department supports the development of new systems, assays and digital solutions. Complex data management, design of experiments, the development of algorithms, as well as mathematical and statistical modeling are our core topics. The goal is to analyze and optimize the technical and clinical performance of our diagnostic products.

We are an interdisciplinary team that plays an important role in the research and development of Roche diagnostic products. We work closely with the teams in the assay development and systems integration departments.

This is what awaits you in the role

  • Support in the planning and evaluation of key experiments in assay and system development, using the corresponding methods of experimental planning and statistics.
  • You analyze existing processes, map them in mathematical models and improve them, among other things, through simulation and optimization.
  • Ensuring the availability and optimization of utilization as well as the configuration of our R servers.
  • You translate complex inquiries from our internal customers into tailor-made solutions and work closely with the responsible project managers.
  • You will continue to educate yourself professionally, product- and process-related and share your knowledge in the team.
  • supervising trainees and diploma students; also participates in the selection of topics and work plans.

Who you are

  • You have a successfully completed degree (Master) in mathematics, statistics, computer science or a natural science (e.g. biology, physics, chemistry), ideally with a doctorate.
  • Very good knowledge of biostatistics and applied mathematics, with practical experience in biometric methods in industry and very good programming knowledge (especially in R and web technologies).
  • Experience in methods and tools for data evaluation and data management of complex experiments.
  • Basic knowledge of biochemistry and medical terminology.
  • You have a solution-oriented, decision-making and team-capable way of working as well as a strong communication ability.
  • Fluent German and English skills in word and writing round off your profile.

This position is limited to two years.
