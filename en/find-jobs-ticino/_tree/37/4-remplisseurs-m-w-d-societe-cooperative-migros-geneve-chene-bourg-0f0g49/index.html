Full description

Join Migros Geneva and enjoy a dynamic job within a motivated team! We are looking for 4 candidates living within 15 minutes of our Chêne-Bourg store. In just 3 hours a day, from 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM, you will contribute to providing a pleasant experience for our customers. If this sounds like you, join us.

Duties

  • Department expert: You ensure the shelves are stocked and organized. Every product is in its place and handled with care.
  • Freshness master: Nothing stays out of the fridge and no expired product escapes you. With you, the cold chain is perfectly respected.
  • Hygiene champion: HACCP rules? Don't worry, you will be trained and become an expert on the subject.
  • Organization ace: Thanks to you, the store is always clean, tidy, and welcoming. Order becomes an art in your hands.
  • Indispensable teammate: With your team spirit, you contribute to quality service and a positive work environment.
  • Peerless advisor: Customers benefit from your warm welcome and expert advice for a 5-star experience.

Requirements

  • You communicate easily with colleagues and customers in French.
  • You are ready to commit long-term (at least 12 months), even to supplement another activity.
  • You are passionate about teamwork and always doing a good job.
  • You reside near our store (15 minutes).
  • For your application to be considered, you must provide a complete file.
  • Your personality, motivation, and skills are what matter most.
  • Initial or continuous training completed
  • French (Good command)

What we offer

  • Catering: Meal discount of 4 CHF (on purchases of 10 CHF) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus points: 2% and 5% discount depending on the assortment.
  • Migros Bank: Preferential interest rate and no fees for a savings account.
