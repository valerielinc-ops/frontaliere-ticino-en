Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Cantonal School Limmattal Housekeeper/in 100% The cantonal school Limmattal in Urdorf is a school of maturity for the regions Knonauer Amt and Limmattal. It comprises a long and short high school with all maturity profiles. The school currently has around 140 teachers, 35 administrative and operational staff and around 900 pupils; in the foreseeable future, the expansion to a number of students of around 1'100 is planned. As of April 1, 2027 or by arrangement, the position of a housekeeper must be filled. Tasks

  • Maintenance and maintenance of technical equipment according to schedule
  • General environmental work (garden)
  • General housekeeping duties
  • Services on occasions (even on weekends if necessary)
  • Association service at gymnasium operation
  • Pictet service in the event of technical failures
  • Winter service
  • They ensure that the cantonal infrastructure of the school is in perfect and safe condition at all times Profile
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as an expert/woman company maintenance, housekeeper with sed. Professional ID card is advantageous
  • Several years of professional practice in the field of home maintenance
  • They like to work in a team and enjoy dealing with young people
  • They act ecologically and responsibly in the areas of energy, materials and maintenance
  • You see yourself as a service provider who proactively supports everyday pedagogical life and provides an environment in which learning and teaching provide pleasure.
  • Varied activities and exciting tasks in a dynamic environment
  • Attractive employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • Motivated and open-minded team Application Further information about our school can be found at www.kslzh.ch. If you have any questions, please contact us at 044 736 14 (Secretariat) or by email at bewerbungen.ksl@edu.zh.ch. Please send the application documents to bewerbungen.ksl@edu.zh.ch by 28 November 2026. We look forward to your application.

Pensum: 100%
