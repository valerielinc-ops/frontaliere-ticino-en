Full description

You are as individual as the people you accompany. That is why we create an environment at eye level that offers you therapeutic freedom and a flexible working model – so that you can shape your professional and private life according to your needs. WePractice is a leading outpatient practice group with over 140 psychotherapists at 17 locations and part of the Medbase Group.

Luogo di lavoro: WePractice, Bern Seelandweg, Seelandweg 7, 3013 Bern

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Work in a heartfelt practice and benefit from the close and individual support of an experienced site management – case discussions and weekly supervision are a matter of course.
  • Accompany your patient: women in outpatient psychotherapy.
  • Determine who you work with. We help you build a patient tribe that fits your specialization.
  • Develop individual therapy plans, collect medical records and make diagnoses – our specially developed practice software gives More time for the essentials.

  • Master's degree (university, university): psychology
  • Psychologist / psychologist in further training for / as a recognised psychotherapist:
  • Completed clinical year in an institution of SIWF category A, B or C
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Joy of self-determined work
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Flat hierarchies: Be part of an appreciative community that supports you and is lived in cooperation at eye level.
  • Design and decision-making leeway: Choose your patients freely and design the therapy approach so that it fits pragmatically and meaningfully for care.
  • Our offices are warm and modern – a place where you and your patients feel comfortable.
  • Individual training: We accompany you with supervisions, case discussions, development discussions and training time / budget.
  • Flexible working hours: Design your working hours as they fit your everyday life and find your own rhythm.

Recruitment process

  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
