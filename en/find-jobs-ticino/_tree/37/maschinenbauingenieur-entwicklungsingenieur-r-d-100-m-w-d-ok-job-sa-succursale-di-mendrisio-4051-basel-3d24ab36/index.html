Mechanical Engineer / Development Engineer R&D 100% (m/f/d) Back to the list OK JOB SA is a Swiss HR and temporary employment company founded in 2012. Certified according to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001. For our customer in the Basel area we are looking for a mechanical engineer / development engineer R&D 100% (m/f/d). Job description

  • Management of development projects for new and further developments in mechanical engineering
  • Construction of components and assemblies
  • Production of technical drawings and documentation
  • Performance of calculations and simulations
  • Verification of calculation bases on experimental setups
  • Technical support of the sales team
  • Cooperation in the optimization of existing machines and systems

Profile

  • Completed studies as mechanical engineer/in ETH, Uni or FH
  • Experience in product or machine development
  • Very good knowledge of 3D CAD, ideally SolidWorks
  • Knowledge of conformity assessment procedures
  • Experience in finite element simulation, beneficial to ANSYS Mechanical
  • Knowledge of explosion protection and hygienic design is advantageous
  • Interest in interdisciplinary technical topics
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Independent and solution-oriented working method

This is what awaits you

  • Demanding and varied development projects
  • High personal responsibility and design possibilities
  • Modern infrastructure and development tools
  • Cooperation with different disciplines
  • Collegial and dynamic team
  • Attractive Preventive Solution
