Full description

Michel Gruppe AG comprises the psychiatric institution Privatklinik Meiringen, the Rehaklinik Hasliberg and the Foundation Helsenberg with three dormitories and various workplaces as well as the Haslital laundry and the Haslital daycare centre as well as services and services in other areas. It employs a total of around 850 people at various locations. For the Psychiatric Center for Young Adults (PZJE) of the Meiringen Private Clinic in Thun, we are looking for a committed, versatile personality who likes to tackle, take responsibility and strengthen our hotel industry team with their positive nature. Do you love cleanliness, tackle with enthusiasm and keep an overview even in lively moments? Then you are right with us!

Their tasks

  • Focus on cleaning: Independent, efficient and thorough cleaning of patient rooms as well as public and operational premises
  • Support in the catering sector: Assistance in the dishwashing room and in the necessary work around food
  • Be flexible: Support where helping hands are needed

Our expectations

  • Experience in professional cleaning, ideally in healthcare or hospitality
  • An efficient, thorough and quality-conscious way of working
  • Lots of energy, drive and physical resilience
  • Self-employment, reliability and a high sense of responsibility
  • Flexibility, team spirit and the willingness to participate outside of your own area of responsibility
  • Empathy, discretion and a respectful, professional approach to our young patients
  • Good knowledge of German

Our offer

  • A meaningful, varied activity with personal responsibility
  • A dedicated team and an appreciative cooperation
  • Attractive working hours compared to the classic hotel industry
  • A workload of 80-100% with openness to flexible working time models
  • Attractive employment conditions with paid breaks, discounted food, free birthday and other benefits
  • A centrally located workplace in Thun, easily accessible by public transport
