Polymecanic Team Leader Apply Division Automation Function Construction/Mechanics Related location Switzerland, Boudry As Team Leader of Polymechanics, you take the operational and human responsibility of a team of about 10 employees. Your role is to organize, mentor and develop your team while ensuring efficient, qualitative and safety-compliant production. Key responsibilities Management & Leadership (30%):

  • To supervise, support and develop the skills of employees (coaching, versatility, evaluations, training).
  • Ensure daily organization of the team according to planning priorities.
  • Monitor individual and collective performance (quality, timeliness, productivity).
  • Promote a positive, collaborative and solutions-oriented team climate.
  • Ensure compliance with internal rules, safety standards and Charter 5S. Production and Technology (70%):
  • Produce mechanical parts on CNC machines (freshing & turning).
  • Program parts on FAO software (Mastercam).
  • Ensure timeliness and quality.
  • Actively participate in continuous improvement of workshop processes. Key relational skills
  • Oriented solution
  • Ethical Compliance & Behaviour
  • Communication & feedback
  • Confidentiality and discretion
  • Performance Improvement Technical Skills
  • Technical knowledge
  • Sharing experience
  • Continuous improvement
  • Resource Management Profile
  • Polymechanical CFC (machining option) or equivalent: essential.
  • Confirmed experience in CNC machining: essential.
  • FAO programming skills (Knowledge of Mastercam software is an advantage).
  • Natural leadership, organizational direction and results orientation.
  • Ability to motivate, structure and develop a team.
  • Team management experience: an asset.
  • Access with computer tools (Windows)
  • Availability for 2x8 schedules (5h-1pm / 1pm-2pm) What We Offer
  • A key role in the workshop, with a real impact on the organisation and development of the team.
  • A varied activity combining management, technical and continuous improvement.
