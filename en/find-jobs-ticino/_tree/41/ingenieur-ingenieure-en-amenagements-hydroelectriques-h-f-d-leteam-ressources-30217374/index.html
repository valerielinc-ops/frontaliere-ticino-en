Full description

Company description

Our customer is a recognized Swiss engineering company specialized in the design and realization of sustainable solutions in the fields of water and energy.

A major player in the hydropower sector, the company supports its customers throughout the life cycle of their projects, from feasibility studies to commissioning, renovation and securing of the works.

As part of the development of its activities in French-speaking Switzerland, our client is looking for a hydroelectric engineer to strengthen his team based in Martigny or Sion.

Description of post

In this position, you are involved in the design, renovation and improvement of the performance and safety of hydroelectric infrastructures.

Your main responsibilities will be as follows

  • Design new hydroelectric developments in Switzerland, especially in Valais, from the first studies to the realization.
  • To carry out rehabilitation and upgrading studies of existing plants or developments.
  • Coordinate and carry out studies to evaluate hydraulic structures such as water intakes, plumbing, armoured wells, forced pipes and leak channels.
  • Perform load loss calculations and productivity simulations.
  • Estimate project costs and develop implementation budgets.
  • Prepare tender files, participate in bid evaluation and contract development.
  • Work closely with engineering, design and modelling teams.
  • Technical monitoring and site monitoring.
  • Participate in the development and maintenance of sustainable relationships with clients and partners.

Profile sought

  • A diploma of engineer EPF, Swiss HES or a training deemed equivalent.
  • At least five years of experience in hydropower projects.
  • A proven experience in the design and design of hydroelectric works.
  • Good general knowledge of electromechanical and hydromechanical equipment in hydroelectric installations.
  • Good control of applicable Swiss standards and guidelines.
  • An experiment in hydraulic calculations, load losses and productivity simulations.
  • Good capacity for analysis, coordination and project management.
  • An excellent oral and written mastery of French.
  • Mastery of German and/or English is an advantage.
  • An autonomous, rigorous, proactive personality enjoying teamwork.

We offer

  • A position of responsibility within a recognized engineering company.
  • The opportunity to participate in major and varied hydroelectric projects in Switzerland.
  • A stimulating, technical and multidisciplinary working environment.
  • Projects actively contributing to energy transition and sustainable infrastructure development.
  • Collaboration with experienced specialists in hydraulics, civil engineering and energy.
  • Attractive conditions of engagement and opportunities for professional development.
  • A workplace in Martigny or Sion.
