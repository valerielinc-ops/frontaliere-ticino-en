Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Education Office for Youth and Career Counselling Breitenstein Centre Intern, Addiction Prevention 40 - 60% The Office for Youth and Career Counselling of the Directorate of Education provides, commissions, manages and coordinates services in child and youth welfare and career counselling. The Breitenstein Centre is part of the Office for Youth and Career Counselling and is supported and co-financed by the municipalities of the Andelfingen district. The Breitenstein Centre provides social services in the areas of child and adult protection, personal assistance, counselling and prevention for the population of the Andelfingen district. The services are aimed at families, young people and children, as well as adults with special needs, social difficulties and/or addiction problems. The interdisciplinary Counselling and Prevention department of the Breitenstein Centre in Andelfingen is looking for an intern in the Addiction Prevention department from May 2027. Your tasks during the internship:

  • Contributing to the implementation of the diverse addiction prevention services
  • Conducting parent education events and supporting the training of multipliers
  • Supporting the provision of advice to organisations, authorities and key people in schools and municipalities
  • Assisting with the further development of prevention services
  • Collaborating with external specialist agencies and stakeholders in the education, health and social sectors
  • Taking on research tasks, documentation and quality assurance Your profile:
  • Student in «Health Promotion and Prevention»
  • Confident manner and skilled interaction with various stakeholder groups
  • Affinity for working with various digital media
  • Willingness to work flexible hours (evening shifts)
  • Independent working style and ability to work in a team What can you expect?
  • A workplace where you can actively contribute and develop and be part of an interdisciplinary team. The Breitenstein Centre is located directly by Andelfingen railway station, which can be reached from Winterthur in a few minutes. Curious? Then we look forward to receiving your online application by 8. November 2026. For additional information, Sabrina Hansen, addiction prevention specialist (Tel: 043 258 46 40) gladly available.

Workload: 40-60%
