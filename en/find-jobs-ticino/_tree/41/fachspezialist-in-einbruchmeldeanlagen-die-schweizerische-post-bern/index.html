Full description

With you, we develop our burglar alarm systems (EMA) in a future-oriented way. You take on the professional responsibility, set standards and use your know-how to advance projects and solutions. You network different stakeholders and ensure that safety and quality are the focus.

You can do that.

  • You assume the professional responsibility for intrusion alarm systems and ensure the implementation of basic principles, guidelines and tools throughout Switzerland.
  • For new or adapted products, you coordinate the projects, ensure the implementation and drive the further development.
  • You lead the specialist committee for intrusion alarm systems, support the decentralised project managers and are the central contact person for business units and other stakeholders.
  • You define and document relevant standards, guidelines and work instructions in a practical and comprehensible manner.
  • You ensure the professional flow of information and the transfer of knowledge and are responsible for quality management.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of experience in project management or product responsibility of intruder alarm systems.
  • A basic training in electronics, electrical engineering or building automation forms your professional basis; SES certification for intrusion and raid detection systems is an advantage.
  • You think analytically and networked, capture connections quickly and develop suitable solutions.
  • High personal responsibility, independence and initiative characterize your working method.
  • You combine clear technical specifications with pragmatic solutions and represent your position convincingly.
  • Very good oral German and French skills or the willingness to actively deepen your language skills round off your profile.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
