About Arxada

Arxada is a global leader in innovative solutions to protect our world. Our groundbreaking technologies, comprehensive regulatory expertise, and our manufacturing and process development expertise help our customers to efficiently protect food, health and infrastructure – through chemistry and biotechnology that promote sustainability.

We offer a broad portfolio of ingredients and services for various end markets, including health & nutrition, household and personal care, professional hygiene, paints & coatings, and wood protection. With customers in over 100 countries, the company generated sales of CHF 2 billion in 2024. Arxada's headquarters are located in Basel, Switzerland. The company employs 3,200 people worldwide at 24 production sites and 14 R&D centers, all of which are committed to the success of our customers.

To strengthen our team in Visp, Switzerland, we are currently looking for an operator for the day shift.

Business units

Arxada offers first-class solutions in two clearly defined areas:

M.C.C. (MCS) focuses on threats posed by microorganisms for humans and the environment. This division comprises five independent business areas: professional hygiene, household and personal care, wood protection, paints & coatings, and material protection.

The portfolio also includes vitamins and nutrient components, high-performance chemical intermediates and raw materials for a wide range of demanding applications. The portfolio also includes materials for the manufacture of composites in electronics, aerospace and other industries.
