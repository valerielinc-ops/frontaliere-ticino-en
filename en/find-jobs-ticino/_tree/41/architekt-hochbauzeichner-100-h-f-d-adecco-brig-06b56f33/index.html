Introduction

Would you like to help shape varied architectural projects in a modern and dynamic architectural office? Would you like to contribute your technical know-how and creativity to exciting construction projects?

Architect / Building Drawer 100% (M/F/D)

Tasks

  • Preparation of pre-project, project and implementation plans
  • Development of technical details and ongoing updating of project documentation
  • Preparation of building inputs and administrative documents
  • Close cooperation with construction managers, engineers and other project participants
  • Cooperation in technical coordination and further development of projects
  • Ensuring compliance with applicable building codes and quality standards
  • Development of technical and architectural solutions according to customer requirements

Profile

  • Graduate degree as architect FH/ETH or training as draftsman EFZ field of architecture
  • Several years of professional experience in a Swiss architecture office
  • Very good knowledge of ArchiCAD (or Revit) and MS Office
  • German as a mother tongue and good knowledge of French (level B1-B2)
  • Independent, structured and careful working methods
  • Team-capable personality with interest in varied architectural projects

Advantages

  • Secure permanent employment in a modern architecture office in central Valais
  • Exciting and versatile projects with a focus on Oberwallis
  • Collegial working environment with development opportunities and personal responsibility
  • Job opening: by agreement
