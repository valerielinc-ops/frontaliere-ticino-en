Chauffeur:euse Hoflogistik with dispatch activity handling and railway / shunting specialist:in

Working, where connections are created

combined transport from a single source. As a young and innovative transport company, we connect the whole of Switzerland with our own trains and trucks and transport goods from door to door. railCare AG is a subsidiary of Coop and employs around 600 people at 14 locations. You are chauffeur C/CE, have already gained initial experience in disposition or in the office and are looking for the next career step? With us, you combine driving (40%) with coordination and planning (60%) – and we train you in the field of railways!

Pensum

80\-100%

Contract

Perpetual

Job entry

immediately or by agreement

Tasks

Coordination (about 60%)

  • Professional management of the team and ensuring smooth day-to-day operations
  • Control of shunting manoeuvres, handling of trains and carrying out technical train inspections (including journeys according to FDV)
  • Central contact person for control centre, transport management, network line and locomotive personnel
  • Monitoring processes and equipment with fast, solution-oriented intervention in the event of disruptions
  • Active participation in new hub and shunting concepts as well as continuous process optimization

Operational day-to-day operations (about 40\)

  • Transhipment of swap containers with vehicles between lorries and railway wagons (horizontal transhipment)
  • Loading and unloading, provision and removal of swap containers
  • Timely pick-ups and deliveries according to customer specifications
  • Active assistance in general handling work on the site

Requirements

  • CH\ driver's license Cat. C or C/E as well as mandatory readiness for further training as a shunting A40 and wagon examiner
  • Flexibility for shift work (Monday to Saturday including public holidays) and good physical condition
  • Compellingly very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Strong organisational talent, networked thinking as well as high reliability and customer orientation
  • Use of modern means of communication and openness and willingness to learn for digital systems
