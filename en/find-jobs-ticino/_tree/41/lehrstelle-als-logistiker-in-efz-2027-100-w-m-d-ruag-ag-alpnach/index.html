Place of work

RUAG MRO Holding AG, Alpnach

Over the area

Drive forklifts, rock camps, strengthen Switzerland.

As a logistician or logistician EFZ at RUAG in Alpnach, you literally take off for high-altitude flights. Because we are the helicopter service provider for the Swiss Air Force. We also offer maintenance and overhaul services to external customers.

As a member of our team, you ensure that everything is in the right place, at the right time and in the right condition and control the entire process: from goods receipt to warehouse management to delivery.

What do you learn with us? A lot. You control deliveries, check quantities and quality, book material in the system and store everything properly. From day one, you are actively involved in day-to-day business, get to know storage and booking systems and become an expert in the world of logistics.

With us, personal responsibility is a priority, but of course you will always receive help from your experienced colleagues if you need it. We also attach great importance to practical training, so that you get clear professional prospects.

Sounds like your kind of teaching? Then apply and maybe you will soon be part of a team that not only moves packages – but also supports Switzerland’s security.
