Collaborator/maternal economy - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 5,000 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC provides quality health care to 380,000 patients per year. The following position is addressed to candidates who wish to apply spontaneously as a Contributor/trice domestic economy Profile The collaborator/la collaborator/trice of domestic economy must perform, in compliance with predefined programs and working procedures (types, modes, materials, equipment, etc.), all the operational activities of domestic economy to ensure both the compliance of hygiene and cleaning conditions provided both an accurate and quality service to the utilitarian functions. The activity takes place in turn (afternoon, evening, weekends and holidays).
