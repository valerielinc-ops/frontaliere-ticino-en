Full description

Do you like to get up early and enjoy being on the road independently? Then you are exactly right with us, because with you dreams arrive unharmed and on time.

You can do that.

  • Early in the morning (about 6 a.m.) you load the delivery vehicles together with your team and prepare your delivery tour.
  • Through your work, you shape the streetscape of Siebnen and embody the values of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the punctual and impeccable delivery of the parcels and letters in your area.
  • On your tour, you will answer questions from our customers and contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • You work regularly on Saturdays, with the services divided into the team.

You bring that with you

  • With your physical fitness, you can easily load and lift packages up to 30 kg.
  • You like to travel, have the driver’s license of category B and ideally A1 (motorcycle up to 125cc) or are ready to catch up before starting your job.
  • You appreciate the work in the team but you also like to travel independently and feel comfortable with vehicles in road traffic.
  • You are reliable and work fast.
  • Good oral German skills as well as an experienced use of digital devices round off your profile.
