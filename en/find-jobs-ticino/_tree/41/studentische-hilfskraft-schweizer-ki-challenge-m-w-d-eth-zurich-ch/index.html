Student assistant – Swiss AI Challenge (m/f/d)

10%-30%, Zurich, temporary

As a competence center for artificial intelligence at ETH Zurich, the ETH AI Center promotes excellent research, innovation in industry and AI entrepreneurship in order to develop trustworthy, accessible and inclusive AI systems for the benefit of society.

From January 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for committed, versatile and motivated student assistance to support the project management of the Swiss AI Challenge.

Project background

The AI Challenge (ki-challenge.ch) invites young people between 13 and 19 years old throughout Switzerland to discover the world of artificial intelligence in a creative and practical way. In a free online course, the participants acquire the necessary basic knowledge and then implement it in their own AI project, supported by doctoral students of the ETH AI Center. The aim is to strengthen future skills, initiate innovations and contribute to Switzerland’s long-term AI readiness.

Job description

  • Communication and social media management: planning, creating and publishing content on social media and in our newsletter
  • Event management: Planning and implementation of events and workshops
  • Community Building: Exchange with participants, schools and partner institutions
  • General project support: Creative participation in content creation and administrative tasks

Profile

  • You are currently enrolled at a Swiss university and will remain so for at least two semesters.
  • You speak fluent German and communicate safely in English (level C1)
  • You are interested in AI, education and the promotion of young people
  • Experience in social media, communication or event organization is an advantage
  • You bring creativity, initiative and a solution-oriented way of thinking
  • You enjoy actively shaping projects and taking on responsibility
  • You are capable of teamwork and work independently at the same time

We offer

  • A meaningful project with a social impact
  • Creative design room for your own ideas
  • Hourly wage – Staffnet | ETH Zurich according to the guidelines of ETH Zurich, max. 15h/week
  • Flexible working conditions in the hybrid working model
