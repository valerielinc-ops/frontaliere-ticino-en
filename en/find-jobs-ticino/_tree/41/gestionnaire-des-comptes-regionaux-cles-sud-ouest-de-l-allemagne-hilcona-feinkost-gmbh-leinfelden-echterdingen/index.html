Full description

Are you based in the Stuttgart, Freiburg, Ulm area, have a solid experience in the food industry, and have built a strong network in the foodservice over several years? Strategic and long-term customer management of regional major customers is part of your daily business? Then you're perfect for us. We look forward to receiving your application! Tasks: You are responsible for managing our defined merchant network in your region and consistently driving the expansion of Hilcona Foodservice product listings forward. Ensure that our products are sustainably established on the menus of relevant end-users in the community and gastronomy, especially in the urban areas of Stuttgart, Freiburg, and Ulm

  • strategically, creatively, and with market sense. Create sustainable growth for all Hilcona brands through the acquisition of new customers at large end-users. Manage and document trade and end-user projects and keep sales and revenue targets in mind at all times. Build a personal, reliable decision-maker network in your region and maintain it actively
  • from trade and end-users, from procurement, sales to product development, kitchen, and menu planning. Inspire multipliers through practical training and plan and implement trade fairs, industry events, and customer events with high external impact. Requirements: Food is your world: Regardless of education, study, or practice
  • you move confidently in the food and gastronomy industry and bring a real market understanding. Business is your language: You can interpret, analyze, and translate numbers, market mechanisms, and trends into concrete measures. Regional key accounts in focus: You have several years of experience in foodservice
  • ideally in managing regional major customers and trade, and are familiar with the urban areas of your region. Analytical & strategic: You recognize potential, develop suitable approaches, and consistently implement them in your region. Communicative with impact: You inspire people, negotiate confidently, and build relationships that count. Hands-on & team-oriented: You work independently, think solution-oriented, and appreciate team collaboration.
