General Services & Plant Security Coordinator — IBSA Institut Biochimique

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Mission of the Role

The resource operationally coordinates the General Services team and presides over the services to support corporate plants, ensuring operational efficiency, security and regulatory compliance.

It operates as a link between Real Estate & General Services, HSE and Technical Services, ensuring the proper functioning of general services, security systems and maintenance activities connected to safety and work environments.

Main Responsibility

General Services Coordination

  • Coordinate and supervise the General Services operating team.
  • Manage facility soft and hard services in collaboration with Technical Services, in particular:
  • cleaning and sanitation
  • waste disposal
  • security and access control
  • pest control
  • catering and vending services
  • management of workwear
  • Support the operational management of suppliers, monitoring performance, service quality and contractual compliance.

Business Plant Security – Supervisory, Access Control and Video Surveillance

  • Coordinate the physical security of the corporate plants (pharma production sites and warehouses), ensuring the protection of people, assets and production processes.
  • Operationally manage the supervisory and guardian services (armed and unarmed, portered), ensuring adequate coverage to the risk profile of the sites.
  • Oversee access control systems (badges, authorization profiles, visitor management and suppliers), in coordination with HSE, Technical Services, Quality and IT, ensuring:
  • segregation of sensitive areas
  • traceability of access
  • compliance with GxP requirements
  • Coordinate video surveillance and security systems, ensuring the proper functioning of the systems and compliance with current privacy and data protection regulations.
  • Collaboration with the management of critical events and emergency situations (intrusions, unauthorized accesses, security incidents), coordinating the actors involved.

Interface with HSE and Technical Services

  • Operate as an operating connection between General Services, HSE and Technical Services for:
  • integration of security measures with safety at work
  • coordination of ordinary and extraordinary maintenance activities
  • President of regulatory compliance on workplaces
  • Support HSE in management of non-compliance, audits and inspections.
  • Collaboration with the implementation of prevention and protection measures provided by current legislation.
