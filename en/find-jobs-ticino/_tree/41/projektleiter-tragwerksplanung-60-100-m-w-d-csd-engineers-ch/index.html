Full description

1,000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: The 3.5 Group is a dynamic, constantly growing engineering company. Inventive spirit, competence and multidisciplinarity characterize our teams, optimal and sustainable solutions for our customers with an added value for quality of life and the environment are our hallmark. They like to work in a professional and ailburgan environment and place great value on quality and efficiency. Be part of our success.

We are looking for an experienced civil engineer as project manager structural engineering 60–100% (m/f/d) for our committed and ambitious team in the field of structural engineering in Bern.

Your remit

  • Management of versatile projects in the construction engineering with a focus on new buildings and building maintenance in the building construction
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with related fields such as geotechnics, hydraulic engineering and landfill construction
  • Self-employed execution of static calculations using modern projecting methods (BIM, BIM2field)
  • Coordination of projects with building owners, architects and other technical planners
  • Responsibility for managing the projects in terms of deadlines, quality and costs
  • Coaching and professional support of our project engineers
  • Participation in the acquisition of new projects and competitions
  • Your profile
  • Completed civil engineering studies ETH/FH/TU
  • multi-year experience as well as experience in project management or a comparable function
  • Experience in the application of SIA standards or Eurocodes (EC)
  • Self-employed, structured working methods and joy in teamwork
  • Self-employed, solution-oriented working methods, openness to new ideas and joy in a collegial working environment
  • A strong interest in sustainability and willingness to implement these principles in projects
  • Good to very good German knowledge (minimum level B2, ideally C1 or higher); other languages are advantageous
  • Interest in professional development and openness to new methods and technologies
  • We offer
  • Development perspectives and scope for design
  • Exciting and versatile projects in the new building and in the maintenance of buildings
  • A dedicated and collegial team
  • Wide internal and external training opportunities, including through the Cf Academy and the Go1 platform
  • Flexible working models and home office options
  • Modern and centrally located workplace at Eigerplatz in Bern

We look forward to receiving your application!
