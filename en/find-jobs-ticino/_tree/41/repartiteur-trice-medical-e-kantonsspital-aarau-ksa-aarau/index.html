Full description

Central disposition

Job opening: From now on or by agreement, indefinitely

Tasks

  • Planning and coordination of elective operations as well as the anesthesia consultation including scheduling and coordination with the specialist areas.
  • Ensuring organizational requirements such as orders, appointment windows and completeness of the necessary documents.
  • Responsibility for bed management and early, late and weekend service in close cooperation with the stations.
  • Assuming front office tasks such as reception, patient registration and general administrative activities.
  • implementation of patient-related support activities in the context of technical training.

Requirements

  • Completed vocational training as MPA, FaGe or Dipl. Care professional with professional experience
  • Communicative personality, self-responsible, independent and competent work
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in the acute hospital/op environment, patient administration or disposition
  • Clear, friendly and solution-oriented communication
  • Independent, responsible work with a sense of priorities
  • Secure use of IT/planning tools; medical terminology is everyday for you

Welcome to the Cantonal Hospital Aarau (KSA). Whether in the Cantonal Hospital Aarau, at the locations KSA Bahnhof Aarau, KSA Lenzburg or KSA Frick – we are there for you around the clock. With our wide range of services, from basic care to highly specialized medicine, we are competent and reliable at all locations.
