Full description

For our team in the crèche Beluga we are looking for immediately or by appointment temporarily until 31. July 2027 (maternity representation) you as Specialist care (90%) As a crèche employee, you accompany and support children with and without impairment in our crèche Beluga. For example, you support them in body care, eating or playing. You take into account the abilities and possibilities of the children. You structure the daily routine and accompany the coexistence in the group. Your relationships with the children are professional. The independence and self-determination of the children are in the center. The Wagerenhof Foundation (affectionately called “Wagi”) in Uster has been a permanent home for around 250 people with multiple cognitive impairments for more than 100 years and offers over 50 employees in assisted jobs. We are a recognized, non-profit institution with a social mission. With a demand-oriented offer of living and care places as well as various day and leisure offers, we promote the quality of life, participation and self-determination of our residents. The special features of the Wagerenhof include size and Dörfli character, which allows space for encounters. For employees with different careers and professionalism, the Wagerenhof Foundation is an attractive employer with a wide range of development opportunities. For learners, Wagerenhof is a competent training partner. For this we give you the responsibility

  • Share responsibility for a children’s group
  • Design of the daily routine, together with other MA of the nursery
  • Collaborating with learners and people in need of support
  • Participation in team meetings, parenting events
  • Co-design of interiors
  • Taking on specialised tasks
  • Completed training as a specialist EFZ or comparable training
  • Balanced personality

This makes us the place for your work

  • The appreciative and human togetherness
  • The clearly strategic and professional organization
  • Professional support through an interdisciplinary and professionally acting environment
  • Good accessibility with public transport
