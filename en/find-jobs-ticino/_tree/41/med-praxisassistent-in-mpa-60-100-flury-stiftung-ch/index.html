Full description

With more than 600 employees in the Schiers hospital, the three retirement homes Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, Spitex, living with service, crèche, parental counselling and the Klosters medical center, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in the Prättigau between Grüsch and Klosters. The Medical Center Klosters (MZK) offers comprehensive family care for the population and guests of the region. We rely on close cooperation within the team, with the relevant specialists as well as the family doctors and hospitals in the region. To complement our team, we are looking for a Med on 1 December 2026/by agreement/ or for a winter season in the Bündner Bergwelt. Practice Assistant (MPA) 60 - 100%

Your tasks

  • Organization of everyday practice and appointment management with our software Axenita
  • Medical-technical tasks such as laboratory and X-ray
  • Carrying out medical examinations and treatments
  • Independent patient care

This is what you bring with you

  • MPA EFZ, advanced conventional intake (EKA) course of advantage
  • Independent, solution-oriented and reliable working method
  • Friendly, empathetic and communicative appearance
  • Good PC/Office skills
  • Good knowledge of German and English
  • Other languages are beneficial

This is what we offer

  • Du-culture
  • Varied and responsible MPA – activity
  • Modern infrastructure and a broad diagnostic spectrum
  • Working independently in a committed and appreciative team
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Professional and personal development opportunities
  • Working and living in an attractive region

For questions please contact Kristin Janowski, practice manager MZK on tel. +41 81 544 81 96 or kristin.janowski@flurystiftung.ch. Further information about the Flury Foundation can be found on the Internet: flurystiftung.ch We look forward to receiving your application documents by e-mail to hr@flurystiftung.ch!
