Full description

We act as a UNIQUE team for the success of the SSR. Together with the other areas, Finance is accountable for strategic leadership and allocation of objective oriented resources, and creates transparency for sustainable decisions.

Your Mission

Attached to the Head of Security – Western Region, you help protect people, infrastructure and activities from malicious acts. You develop and implement security measures adapted to the risks and specificities of the media environment, in compliance with the SSR's national standards.

Within the West Region Security Team, you have the following responsibilities:

  • Develop, implement and monitor safety concepts for West Region sites and buildings.
  • Conduct risk analyses and propose appropriate preventive and protective measures.
  • Monitor security systems (access control, video surveillance, alarms and intrusion detection).
  • Design and coordinate security arrangements related to external events, productions and activities.
  • Contribute to the protection of journalists and production teams during sensitive activities.
  • Collaborate with authorities, providers and internal partners to ensure a high level of safety, and contribute to cross-cutting projects and the sharing of good practices within the SSR, in support of the Head of Security – West Region.

Your profile

  • Recognized security training (CoSu, MaSu, federal patent or equivalent) or equivalent professional experience.
  • Confirmed experience of 3 to 5 years in site safety, event safety or personal protection.
  • Good control of technical security systems (access control, video surveillance, intrusion detection).
  • Excellent mastery of French and ability to evolve in a bilingual French-German working environment.
  • Spirit of analysis, sense of responsibility, ability to manage priorities and situations under pressure.
  • Excellent relational skills, discretion and availability for occasional irregular travel and schedules.

What we offer you

  • A motivating framework in a captivating environment.
  • A dynamic environment within a motivated and welded team.- An opportunity to discover a public service audiovisual media.

Activity rate : 80-100% Place of work: Ecuublens, frequent trips between the various RTS sites, in Geneva and in French-speaking Switzerland Entry into office: as soon as possible, ideally on 1 January 2027 Deadline for application: 22 November 2026
