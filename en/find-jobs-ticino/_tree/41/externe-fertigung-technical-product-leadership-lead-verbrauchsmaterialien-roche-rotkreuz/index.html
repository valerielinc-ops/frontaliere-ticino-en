The Position

The DOEA External Manufacturing Product & Project Leadership department is dedicated to driving operational excellence and shaping the future of our product lifecycle management. Our team operates at the critical intersection of product strategy, external manufacturing, and supply chain oversight, ensuring that our innovative products meet the highest standards of quality and efficiency. We foster a collaborative environment where cross-functional alignment and standardized governance lead to impactful organizational success and sustainable growth. As the External Manufacturing - Technical Product Leadership Lead - Consumables ( Technical Product Leadership Lead), you will act as the primary disciplinary and professional expert for our community of Product Leaders. In this critical leadership role, managing a team of approximately seven subject matter experts, you will guide portfolio alignment, spearhead organizational change initiatives, and drive innovation through new product management processes and technologies. You will ensure the continuous elevation of quality and efficiency standards across the entire product portfolio while cultivating strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders to support strategic investment decisions. The Opportunity

  • Drive Product Strategy & Governance (30%): Lead the harmonized, high-quality execution of product strategy by standardizing product lifecycle practices, defining portfolio-level KPIs, and rolling out the network strategy to ensure squad prioritization aligns with organizational objectives.
  • Lead Talent Development (30%): Build, mentor, and develop a high-performing team of Product Leaders by managing the full talent lifecycle—from recruitment and performance management to addressing skill gaps and fostering a highly engaging, psychologically safe work environment.
  • Optimize Resource Allocation (20%): Provide expert insights into Product Leader capacity and portfolio efficiency to ensure the right talent and investments are applied across associated squads and product initiatives.
  • Translate Strategic Goals: Convert high-level organizational requirements—such as CAPEX reductions, SMC cost reductions, and impairment reductions—into actionable objectives for multiple product squads and MSAT teams while managing network interfaces.
  • Oversee External Manufacturing (20%): Hold end-to-end accountability for the external manufacturing of assigned product areas (e.g., injection molding, automated assembly, reagent fill/finish) and manage the technical supplier evaluation and supply vulnerability analysis processes.
  • Manage Capacity & Performance: Set operational targets, monitor standardized KPIs (quality, delivery, cost, capacity, service), and collaborate with Supply Chain, Procurement, and Portfolio Management to oversee and adjust future capacity needs.
  • Ensure Technical Oversight & Escalation: Act as the primary escalation point for supplier-related product and manufacturing issues, maintaining robust technical oversight and escalation management in partnership with MSAT and Quality.
