Full description

29.10.2026 Montagny-near Yverdon

Be part of our team!

For the Bank Raiffeisen Mont-Aubert Orbe, we are looking for an organised and versatile person, in order to strengthen our team, to the post of Credit officer private customer (80%).

What are you waiting for?

  • Evaluate, approve or reject applications for credit within the jurisdiction assigned.
  • Support client advisors in the analysis, structuring and processing of funding applications.
  • Identify, assess and communicate risks associated with credit operations, while providing appropriate solutions.
  • Analyse applications for special or exceptional authorisations and coordinate risk transfers with Raiffeisen Switzerland if necessary.
  • Ensure periodic review of credit commitments and compliance with regulatory and internal requirements.
  • Contribute to the monitoring of the credit portfolio and the monitoring of compliance with agreed conditions.
  • Participate in continuous improvement of credit processes.
  • Represent and convey with conviction and enthusiasm the values of our slogan #PROCHEDEVOUS.

What are you bringing?

  • Commercial training (CFC), ideally branch Bank, or training deemed equivalent.
  • Experience in case management Recovery and/or real estate valuation skills is an asset.
  • Experience of at least two years in the analysis, validation or management of credit for private customers.
  • Rigor and sense of responsibility.
  • Spirit of analysis and critical mind.
  • Ability to argue and defend a position constructively.
  • Excellent relational skills and taste for teamwork.
  • Orientation solutions and direction of priorities.
  • A residence in the region would be a plus.

Our skills are expressed in a spirit of dialogue, proximity and entrepreneurship. We are looking for a Credit Officer who wants to combine expertise, responsibility and commitment with a team that places people and trust at the centre of its actions. Lionel Guignard, Head of Credit Office and Credit Administration Lionel Guignard Head of credit office and credit administration Why Raiffeisen?

We work in a friendly environment

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.

We live in diversity and equal opportunities

We encourage the diversity of perspectives, experiences and skills within our teams. This diversity makes us rich and makes a decisive contribution to our success.

We encourage personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is an integral part of our culture. We create a space for the professional and personal development of each individual and support our employees in a targeted manner, thanks to a wide range of in-house training and participation in external development costs.

We offer attractive social conditions and benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages over banking products, various discounts from third parties, as well as above average financial stability and old age insurance.
