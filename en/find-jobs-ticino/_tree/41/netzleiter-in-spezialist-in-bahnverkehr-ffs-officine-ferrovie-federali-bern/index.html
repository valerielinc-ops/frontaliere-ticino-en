Network manager:in / specialist:in rail traffic 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Network manager:in / Specialist:in rail transport Bern, 01.02.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You play a key role in the overall coordination and optimization of ongoing railway production in Switzerland. If terms such as disruption management, customer orientation, collaboration and decision-making competence are not foreign words for you, then you fit perfectly into our team! Do you also have a passion for tackling challenges and finding pragmatic solutions? Then we look forward to meeting you! You can move that.

  • As a network manager: guides and controls the overall operational situation of current rail traffic on the SBB network. In doing so, you analyze and assess the development of the situation in a forward-looking manner and proactively influence rail production.
  • You work closely with other control centres, railway companies and infrastructure operators at home and abroad.
  • For questions about traffic control and disruptions, you decide throughout Switzerland.
  • Within the SBB you inform verbally and in writing about current disturbances and events.
  • You also moderate briefings and agreements with various stakeholders.
  • In projects and interdisciplinary working groups, you represent production-specific interests.

You bring that with you.

  • Your communication strength, resilience and fast comprehension are complemented by your assertiveness.
  • A high customer orientation characterizes you, and you stick with your solution-oriented way of working.
  • You have several years of scheduling experience from an operations centre or control centre of a railway company and have very good knowledge of the regulations, processes and operational processes in railway production.
  • Working shifts and taking on irregular working hours at night and on weekends gives you pleasure.
  • You have German (minimum C1) and knowledge of French and Italian, or you are ready to acquire this knowledge up to level B1.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This position is in Requirement Level I, Regional Allowance Level 1.
