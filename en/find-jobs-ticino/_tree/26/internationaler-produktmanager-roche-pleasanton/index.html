The Opportunity

The International Product Manager, Sequencing Sample Preparation, is responsible for driving the global strategy for the sample prep solutions, specifically RNA-Seq portfolio, and for delivering on its execution.

Key Roles & Responsibilities

The key accountabilities for this position include, but are not limited to:

  • Acts as a facilitator and integrator across all stakeholders in order to ensure that the library prep solutions are developed and commercially available in scope, time and budget.
  • Working with the sequencing global business team, drives the systematic commercial development of the Roche RNA sample prep products from concept to launch, on-market support and obsolescence
  • Provides on-market product support and provides guidance to the affiliates
  • Collaborates with other commercial colleagues to execute on global commercialization strategy incl. clear positioning, messaging, value proposition, training, marketing materials, among other deliverables as product SME
  • Gathers, refines, analyses and prioritizes customer requirements from existing and potential customers across all markets and identifies portfolio gaps. Generates resulting product requirements and an aligned multi-year product roadmap.
  • Leads, manages and monitors product-specific competitor activities and development of actions to address them
  • Leads strong team spirit and motivates team members across the system team and related teams towards shared goals

Who you are

  • University Degree in Marketing, Science or related technical discipline
  • Preferably brings 8+ years of marketing/sales experience in the life sciences, diagnostics and/or pharmaceutical industry. Experience with sequencing products, specifically RNA-Seq library prep, will be a huge plus
  • Strong preference for candidates who have broad experience in NGS space and have undergone multiple cycles of product iteration or new product development
  • Brings excellent leadership & communication skills, ability to lead and influence cross-functional teams within a complex global matrix environment

Location: Remote – United States (Preferred) or South Africa. Relocation benefits are not available for this position.
