Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. As a customer assistant in charge of the frequency survey, you are in direct contact with travellers and seek to identify their travel habits. A part-time training (50%) varied and paid for four months awaits you, during which you will receive both theoretical and practical teaching. Our training sites are located in Olten and Zurich Altstetten. After completing the examination for your training, you will work on a team basis and on a part-time basis (50%) and will serve as a customer assistant for the frequency survey throughout Switzerland, starting and finishing your services each time at the same training site. The Customer Assistance section offers you interesting opportunities for development such as the training of customer assistants in charge of the control of transport documents, lasting four months, or the training of customer assistants, lasting eight months. As a customer assistant in charge of the frequency survey, you contribute to our central objective: enthusiasm for the clientele. You consider irregular schedules and teamwork as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. At home, working at night and on weekends entitles us to additional remuneration, for example CHF 16.– an hour for Sunday work. Time increases of 10 to 30% are granted for night work, which gives you more free time. To find out about social benefits, such as free GA and foreign travel at reduced prices (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits. Learn more about this profession in recording the information session. What you can move.

  • As a customer assistant in charge of the frequency survey, you are in direct contact with the customers you accompany throughout their journey. You ask passengers and passengers about their itinerary and travel documents in order to analyse their travel behaviour.
  • You enter the data collected on an electronic device.
  • You inform customers about their journey, ensure their comfort and direct the flow of people.
  • As part of a very varied professional daily, you move Switzerland with different colleagues every day.
  • With an extremely precise service plan, you complete your work on time, without having to perform any other tasks or process emails.

What you bring.

  • You have a sense of communication, be open-minded and distinguish yourself by your thoughtful attitude towards people and your marked customer orientation. Thanks to your excellent communication skills and your high ability to manage conflicts, you make a decisive contribution to a positive customer experience.
  • To carry out tasks that are sometimes physically challenging, we rely on collaboration and team spirit.
  • You have an affinity for the numbers, quickly assimilate the information and distinguish with a keen sense of responsibility. You work efficiently and accurately, master digital work tools and are willing to work on a team basis.
  • You have successfully completed a professional apprenticeship (minimum two years) or are mature. Your motivation matters more to us than the branch you come from.
  • You have very good knowledge in French (level C1) and you are already able to speak in German. You are also willing to obtain at least one language diploma (level A2) within two years in German.
  • During the application process, we will ask you for an extract from the criminal record as well as the current prosecution and bankruptcy register (less than three months old). This document may be ordered at the time of a personal interview.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
