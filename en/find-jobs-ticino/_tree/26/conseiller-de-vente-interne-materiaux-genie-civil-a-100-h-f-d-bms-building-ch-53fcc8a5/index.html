Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire construction customers and private customers with a great deal of commitment on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

To strengthen our team based in Bussigny Nord or Bulle, we are looking for a passionate and committed person from 1 January 2027

Missions

  • Process controls in the areas of materials (civil engineering, exterior design and building)
  • Ensure market and competition trends in these areas of activity
  • Advise our customers and provide optimal solutions to their requests
  • Monitoring orders in close collaboration with external sales
  • Maintain a strong and trusted partnership with suppliers
  • Work closely with suppliers
  • Ensuring versatility within the team
  • Timely participation in external and internal events
  • Various administrative work

Your Profile

  • Holder of an SWC in a profession related to the civil engineering, exterior development and building sectors, or
  • Holder of a trade/sales CFC
  • Ideally, experience in the field of construction merchants
  • Have predispositions to sale and administrative tasks
  • Strong customer orientation and able to manage multiple tasks at the same time thanks to excellent priority management
  • Mastering common IT tools
  • Specific, dynamic and motivated person, have ease in direct and telephone contacts
  • Know how to work independently while easily integrating into a team
  • Mastery of the German language is a plus

We offer

  • A stimulating, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where active collaboration is valued while encouraging autonomy.
  • A comprehensive integration programme; our new colleagues also benefit from in-house training on products and systems
  • Support for the professional and personal development of our employees
  • Collaborating discounts, various discounts such as fleet discounts, discounts in selected fitness centres and numerous offers to promote their mental and physical health
  • An attractive wage package combined with a strong pension fund, with flexible savings contributions and above average employer share
  • 5 weeks of holidays (6 weeks from 50 years) and the possibility to buy up to 10 additional days of holidays, as well as additional benefits such as a highly funded independent pension fund and discounts on our entire product range as well as external premium benefits
