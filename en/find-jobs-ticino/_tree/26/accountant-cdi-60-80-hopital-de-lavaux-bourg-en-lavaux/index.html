Full description

About the offer Lavaux Hospital is a private foundation recognized in public interest, member of the Vaudian Hospital Federation. Located in Cully (Bourg-en-Lavaux), in the heart of the vineyards and close to the public transport, the school and the Lavaux daycare. An expansion project is planned for 2027, with the relocation of care services to new facilities currently under construction at the hospital site. A modern and integrated regional hub, Lavaux Hospital is recognized for its excellence in community care, its specialization in geriatrics, rehabilitation and specialized palliative care, as well as its commitment to quality and innovation. In order to strengthen our Finance and Administration Department, we are looking for an Accountant (60-80%). Attached to the Financial Director, you will join the Hospital at a key point in its evolution and will support the Finance and Administration team in the projects related to the future institution. Your main responsibilities

  • Prepare and account for the hospital's general accounts
  • Establish and monitor VAT accounts
  • Maintain and record capital assets and monitor the calculation of depreciation;
  • Contribute to the drafting and updating of loop procedures and checklists
  • Participate in operational activities related to operating cost accounting (AAC), including monitoring and validation of imputations
  • Provide support to the Financial Director through the ITAR-K Institution (costs per case)
  • Coordinate the processing of external requests and requests from other internal services
  • Participate in working meetings with other services as part of continuous process improvement
  • Conduct ad hoc analyses and provide indicators as required

Your profile Technical expertise

  • You hold a trade employee's SWC or equivalent; A supplementary accounting certificate is highly appreciated.
  • You have several years of professional experience in an accounting department, ideally in the hospital or health sector.
  • You have a good knowledge of REKOLE hospital accounting standards.
  • Mastery of the Opale/BluePearl ERP is an asset. You are comfortable with the usual computer tools (Excel, Word, Access, Outlook).

Human skills

  • Rigor, honesty, method and organization.
  • Reliability, curiosity and willingness to update your knowledge regularly.
  • An analytical spirit, objectivity and a sense of prudence in the approach to his work.
  • Autonomy, precision, synthesis and responsiveness.
  • A sense of teamwork and adaptability.
  • Proposition strength and motivation to advance projects and new techniques.

We offer

  • A fixed position within a human-sized institution, recognised for the quality of its services.
  • A dynamic, collaborative and multidisciplinary working environment within a committed team.
  • Working conditions governed by the collective labour agreement for the public health sector (CCT San Vaud).
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development.
  • The possibility of benefiting from partial telework, according to the requirements of the position and the organization of the service.
  • A working environment located in the heart of Lavaux, easily accessible by public transport.
  • The opportunity to make a concrete contribution to the development of the Hospital and the projects related to the future establishment planned for 2027.

Effective date: November 2026
