Full description

Emergency room assistant doctors - Ausschreibung The EOC, the multi-site hospital in Ticino, is present with its institutes throughout the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1,000 beds. The organization allows us to effectively combine a local approach and an overall vision, guaranteeing the population a global and local hospital offer, regardless of the place where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 5,000 collaborators and their attention to human relationships, the EOC ensures quality healthcare for 380,000 patients per year. To complete our team of the Emergency Department of the Lugano Regional Hospital, we are looking for Emergency Room Assistant Doctors (100%) Profile:

  • Essential requirements: degree/diploma in medicine or qualification and relevant registration in MedReg before starting work. For doctors with foreign qualifications, recognition by the Federal Commission for Medical Professions MEBEKO and registration in MedReg before starting work are mandatory; proven knowledge of the Italian language (level required at least C1 or mother tongue) with relative registration with MedReg before starting work; minimum one year experience in Internal Medicine or Surgery, preferably in emergency settings; strong relational, communication and teamwork skills, spirit of initiative, flexibility and positive attitude
  • Knowledge of a second national language and previous experience in Emergency Medicine departments are an advantage. Entry date: Entry date: date to be agreed. Applications complete with curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, copies of study and work certificates, must be received by 30 June 2026 only in electronic format via the website www.eoc.ch. Applications sent by post or e-mail will not be taken into consideration, as will applications that do not meet the required requirements. Further information can be requested from the secretariat of the Emergency Service ( candidatureps.lugano@eoc.ch ).
