Full description

You take on a safe and competent care of patients on our monitoring station and in the recovery room. As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable, but for the region extremely important company. We maintain a collegial working atmosphere and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos. | For our monitoring station we search by agreement |

  • Care of interdisciplinary patients at the monitoring station and in the recovery room
  • Implementation of ambulatory pain therapies
  • Participation in preventive, diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • Assisting patients and their relatives in crisis situations
  • Careful documentation of all nursing activities
  • Professional and close interdisciplinary cooperation with different disciplines |
  • Completed training as a Dipl. Professional HF or equivalent recognised training
  • Post-diploma certificate Supervision care is advantageous
  • High sense of responsibility and a strong quality orientation
  • Precise and reliable operation
  • Empathetic, motivated and resilient personality
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of English, any other language beneficial
  • Willingness to work in all shifts (3 shift operation) |
  • Varied, responsible work
