Full description

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Specialization: Category Management / Procurement
  • Location: Interdiscount, Bernstrasse 90, 3303 Jegenstorf

At Interdiscount, we offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Take responsibility, be proactive with us and develop further.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for warranty and supplier returns from return to financial clarification and ensure that legitimate credits and claims against our suppliers are fully realized.
  • You organize international transports from Asia and Europe by sea, rail, air and road freight and together with our freight forwarding partners ensure reliable and timely processing.
  • You work closely with the head of import/export and take over the professional deputy after your training. Among other things, you coordinate orders, delivery dates, payments and cooperation with our procurement partners in Asia.
  • You coordinate import, export and customs clearance processes and work closely with the specialised departments of the Coop Group and our external partners.
  • You check and plausibility of transport and freight forwarding accounts with an annual volume in the millions and take over recurring operational accounting and control tasks reliably and carefully.
  • You work closely with categories, suppliers and logistics partners, clear deviations independently and actively contribute your ideas to the further development and digitalization of our goods flow processes.

Requirements

  • You have a commercial or logistical basic training and bring your first relevant professional experience.
  • You are interested in international flows of goods, logistics and import/export and want to continuously expand your expertise in these areas.
  • You like to take on responsibility, work independently and keep track of several parallel processes, appointments and financial topics.
  • You communicate openly and bindingly with suppliers and service providers, actively address open topics and stay tuned until a viable solution has been found.
  • Initial experience with international transport, import/export, SAP or cooperation with suppliers is an advantage – for us, the willingness to learn, initiative and a good commercial understanding are crucial.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and can communicate safely in English with our international partners.
