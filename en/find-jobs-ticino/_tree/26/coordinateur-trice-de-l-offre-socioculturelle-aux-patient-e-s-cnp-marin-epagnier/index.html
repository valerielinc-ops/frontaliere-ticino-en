Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research.

The Research Care Directorate, one

Coordinator of the sociocultural offer to patients

Under the responsibility of the Care Branch, you are helping to make hospitalization a springboard for recovery and social inclusion. You are looking for a cross-sectional function within the Neuchâtelois Psychiatry Centre, in conjunction with the care teams and external partners, in order to promote a coherent offer with therapeutic projects and institutional orientations. Your mission is to develop lasting partnerships with cultural, artistic, associative, sporting and social actors in the canton of Neuchâtel in order to offer meaningful activities to patients.

As part of your function, you will be required to

  • Identify and develop collaborations with the canton's cultural, artistic, sporting, associative and/or social institutions.
  • Build sustainable partnerships that allow patients to discover activities that already exist in the community during hospitalization.
  • Facilitate, where relevant, the continuation of these activities after leaving the hospital.
  • Develop, in collaboration with health care teams and external partners, an annual calendar of activities and events.
  • Define, with the care teams, guidance criteria for identifying patients for whom socio-cultural activity can support the therapeutic and recovery project, taking into account expressed needs, expectations, motivation and opportunities for continuity after exit.
  • Support teams in identifying needs and opportunities for partnerships.
  • Facilitate organizational and logistical aspects related to hosting external partners.
  • Ensure that the canton's cultural, social, sporting and citizen resources are monitored in order to continuously enrich the proposed offer.
  • Promote formats for meeting patients, citizens and local actors when compatible with the care environment.
  • Participate in the development of an image of a hospital open to its territory and fully enrolled in the community.
  • Regularly assess partnerships and activities developed to propose continuous improvements.

The main training and skills required are

  • Have higher education (HES or university) in social work, sociocultural animation, cultural mediation, community development or management of socio-cultural projects.
  • To benefit from proven experiences in partnering and networking projects, in the field of sociocultural animation and in an institutional context of health, ideally in mental health, is an asset.
  • Know the Neuchâtel community, cultural and institutional fabric or ability to develop it quickly.
  • Have a strong interest in issues of mental health recovery, social inclusion and citizen participation.
  • Know the methodological approach aimed at organizing from end to end the smooth running of a project.
  • Be able to interact with people with psychiatric disorders.
  • Expressing in a convincing and lively way to describe, explain, promote ideas, and reach out to its audience and interlocutors.

We offer you

  • The opportunity to contribute actively to the evolution of patient experience.
  • A working environment in close collaboration with health care teams.
  • The possibility of developing your professional skills within a recognised cantonal institution.
  • Working conditions governed by the CLC Santé 21 and attractive social benefits.
