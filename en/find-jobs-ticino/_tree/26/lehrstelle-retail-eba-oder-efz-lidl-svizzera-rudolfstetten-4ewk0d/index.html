Full description

Already have ideas for the time after you finish school? Start your future with us and take advantage of the development opportunities at Lidl Schweiz. Our stores are at the heart of what we do. As a retail apprentice (specializing in creating shopping experiences), whether EBA or EFZ, you contribute to the success of your Lidl store. Start/end EBA: August 1, 20XX - July 31, 20XX Start/end EFZ: August 1, 20XX - July 31, 20XX Your tasks

  • Operating cash registers and preparing cash settlements
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Stocking goods and presenting merchandise
  • Checking incoming goods in the warehouse and supporting your team with inventory counts
  • Supporting the planning and implementation of promotions and seasonal displays
  • Determining stock requirements and ordering goods in the 3rd year of training (EFZ)
  • Taking on your first shift-management duties in the 3rd year of training (EFZ)

Your profile

  • An average to very good secondary-school qualification
  • A1 in a foreign language for EBA and at least A2 for EFZ
  • Enjoying contact with people
  • Ability to work independently and a quick grasp
  • Ability to work in a team, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and commitment
  • Physical stamina
  • Very good command of German
