Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. The various missions that await you:

  • Contribute to the development of an existing client portfolio
  • Identify customer needs and submit tailored solutions
  • Work closely with employees of the General Agency Your profile:
  • CFC holder
  • Professional experience in insurance counselling
  • French or German mother tongue, mastery of the second language would be an asset
  • Holder of AFA certification would be an asset
  • Local anchoring on the Sarine or Lake District is desired You are entrepreneurial in nature, adaptable, you have a strong sense of negotiation, you are customer oriented, curious and well integrated into the social and economic environment of the region We offer you :
  • A healthy working environment in a mutualist company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • Multiple social benefits, including flexibility in work organisation
