The Position As a Data Scientist, you drive digital innovation and advanced analytics to take the development of diagnostic products to a new level. Become part of a collaborative, forward-thinking R&D environment where your expertise directly leads to creating secondary, life-changing solutions for patients worldwide. In this dynamic role, you build the bridge between biostatistics, data science and modern IT infrastructure. You will continuously develop our analytical skills in the areas of Core Lab & Near Patient Care.

The opportunity

  • Identify challenges and create innovative, global tools and automated solutions that sustainably increase efficiency and performance in biostatistics and data science.
  • Further develop existing data management systems, workflows and pipelines to seamlessly process patient, device and measurement data.
  • Independently apply advanced biostatistical methods and modern data science techniques to solve complex scientific issues in R&D projects.
  • Drive the use of innovative programming techniques and statistical standards and promote continuous learning and knowledge transfer in the team.
  • Act as an important technical interface and work closely with multidisciplinary teams from different departments to support product development and approval.

Who you are

  • You have successfully completed a university degree (Master’s or equivalent) in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, Data Management or a related quantitative subject area.
  • Very good knowledge in R and SQL combined with in-depth knowledge in technical data management, data transfer and data visualization.
  • 3 to 5 years of practical experience in statistical programming and validation in the industrial environment – including experience in dealing with patient, device or measurement data.
  • Solid foundation in practice-relevant biometric and statistical methods as well as modern data engineering practices.
  • Analytical thinking, pronounced problem-solving skills, very good English skills in word and writing as well as a team-oriented, appreciative working method.

This item is limited to two years.
