Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Job Summary The Finance Sr. Administrator is responsible for performing routine financial administrative tasks with minimal supervision. This role ensures accurate data entry, supports financial reporting, and maintains compliance with internal procedures. This job profile is intended for experienced professional-level staff that generally works autonomously and only requires closer supervision in non-routine situations. Join our team as a Finance Specialist and play a key role in ensuring smooth and accurate financial operations. In this position, you will manage accounts payable activities, support financial closing processes, collaborate with internal stakeholders and external service providers, and help maintain high standards of financial accuracy and compliance.

What You Will Do

  • Manage and oversee end-to-end accounts payable processes.
  • Review, reconcile, and resolve invoice and payment discrepancies.
  • Act as the main point of contact for accounts payable-related inquiries.
  • Coordinate with external service providers supporting PTP and RTR activities.
  • Execute weekly payment runs and process manual payments when required.
  • Support month-end and year-end closing activities.
  • Maintain and update finance master data records.
  • Collaborate with internal teams, including Accounting, FP&A, Treasury, Tax, and Procurement, as well as with Sulzer entities across the organization.

What You Need to Succeed

  • Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Business Administration, or a related field.
  • Experience in accounts payable, accounting, or finance operations.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with excellent attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively within a team.
  • Strong organizational and communication skills.
  • Customer-focused mindset with a proactive and solution-oriented approach.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Excel, including formulas, functions, and pivot tables.
  • Experience working with SAP FI/CO is an advantage.
  • Advanced English skills (B2/C1 level).
  • German language skills (B1 level) are a plus.

What Makes You Successful

  • You are highly reliable, discreet, and detail-oriented.
  • You enjoy working with numbers and financial data.
  • You are resilient, adaptable, and open to change.
  • You build strong relationships with stakeholders and communicate effectively across teams.
  • You take ownership of your work and continuously look for ways to improve processes.

What we offer you

  • A permanent contract.
  • A competitive salary as well as participation in the variable remuneration system based on personal and financial objectives.
  • Meal vouchers.
  • Access to a voluntary flexible compensation scheme allowing employees to optimize their salary package (e.g. health insurance, transport, childcare)
  • Be part of an experienced and international team.
  • A Good vibe working environment.
  • The opportunity to grow your career within a solid and well-established international company.
  • Be part of a dynamic organization undergoing continuous transformation and improvement.
  • Offices location: Nuevos Ministerios
  • Additional benefits may apply depending on the position and location.
