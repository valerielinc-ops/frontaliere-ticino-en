Full description

Dipl. Nursing Specialist Springerpool 20-100%

Department of Nursing & Therapeutic Services

Place of work St. Gallen

Job advertisement

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Holistic care and support for patients and their relatives
  • Responsibility for the implementation of professionally competent support and care, taking into account modern care and treatment concepts
  • active participation in the organization of the service plan
  • Flexible collaboration in different shifts and departments, depending on the springer pool model, also across locations

What you bring to this position

  • Completed training as a qualified nurse HF
  • Flexibility, openness and interest in different areas/locations and changing care situations
  • Willingness to be available at short notice, depending on the chosen jumper pool model
  • Structured and independent way of working as well as a high level of responsibility and resilience
  • Willingness to work 80% for at least 1 month during the introductory phase

Our offer to you

  • Working model Springerpool bonus in at least 2-3 specialist areas at the St.Gallen location with a monthly functional allowance of CHF 450.00 with 100% workload (pro-rata for part-time) and 4 free days per month, duty planning half by the employee and half by the Springerpool management with notification of assignments up to 4 hours before the assignment
  • Springerpool Plus working model at 2 HOCH Health Ostschweiz locations with a monthly functional allowance of CHF750.00 for 100% workload (pro-rata for part-time work) as well as an additional CHF 50 bonus per day for assignments outside the main location
  • At least 50% of the assignments are carried out at a defined main location, the remaining assignments take place at other HOCH Health Ostschweiz locations as required

Your application

  • Admission by appointment
  • You can find out more about this exciting position from Livia Amstutz, Head of Springerpool Care, telephone 41 71 494 25 98
  • If you have any questions about the application process, please contact Susanne Scharf, HR Business Partner, telephone 41 71 494 38 63
