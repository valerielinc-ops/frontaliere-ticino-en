Full description

Class and annual salary (including 13th monthly)

Special Contract

Tasks

  • performs, organizes and tests the individual therapeutic plan for the patient according to the indications received from the / her/ her/his/her direct superior referred to the diagnostic and pharmacotherapeutic framework, guaranteeing in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team, the taking into account the patient
  • performs and realizes medical-psychiatric or pedopsichiatric visits, where necessary in collaboration with psychologists/he, for patients, following the therapeutic plan and implementing it in compliance with its professional skills
  • actively collaborates with physicians of psychiatric and somatic hospitals and doctors with private practice, in accordance with the medical psychiatrist head of the OSC service, regarding the somatic care of the patients and according to the indications of the direct/upper
  • is required to form part of the specialty in psychiatry and psychotherapy or pedopsichiatry according to FMH provisions
  • realizes, in accordance with the direct/upper, relations with other public and private structures operating on the territory in order to create the ideal conditions for networking
  • assures the picket on the territory and/or the guards in Cantonal Psychiatric Clinic (CPC) as provided by the "regulation of OSC medical guards and spikes"
  • in collaboration with his/her superiors direct/and psychiatric medical expert to him/her entrusted and supervised
  • guarantees the correct editing of TARDOC and TARPSY performance

Requirements

  • Medical diploma (or equivalent title)
  • if in possession of a foreign diploma in medicine, present the recognition of the same issued by the Commission of medical professions Mebeko, Section "Training" and the certification of linguistic knowledge (Italian-B2)
  • candidates in specialist training in psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • candidates in specialized training in psychiatry and child psychotherapy and adolescence
  • preference is given to candidates who have already carried out the year of non-specific clinical training (somatic year) as provided by the Regulation for the professional improvement of the relative specialization of FMH (www.fmh.ch)
  • perfect mastery of the Italian language, knowledge of the English language and/or a second official language
  • aptitude for group work
  • knowledge of the territory and the social network of the Canton Ticino

Special remarks

  • Report of employment: assignment (Art. 15 GROSS)
  • full-time employment (100%) or lower employment (to be defined)
  • seat of service will vary depending on the places available. In its participation, the candidate is invited to indicate whether he is a candidate for his training in psychiatry and psychotherapy or child psychotherapy and adolescence.
  • availability by the candidate/to work on irregular shifts
  • start date: during 2026

  • curriculum vitae
  • Medical diploma (or equivalent title)

  • previous employers' certificates

  • the candidate(s) in possession of the diploma of foreign doctor in medicine and not yet in possession of the recognition issued by the Commission of medical professions Mebeko, Section "Training" and certificate of linguistic knowledge (Italian-B2) issued by Mebeko, must obtain both the requirements within the beginning as established during the selection for the specific vacancy. Failure to obtain the requirements for the task entails automatic decay
  • the beginning of the activity of the chosen candidate is subject to the release of the authorization to exercise by the Health Office of the Canton Ticino
