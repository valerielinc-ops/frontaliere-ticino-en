Full description

Introduction

For our Design department, we are looking for a Product Analyst (M/F) whose main mission will be to support the Product Manager in the analysis and synthesis of product, range and collection information, in order to help define the strategies for the introduction of the pieces, to monitor the life cycle of the collections and to prepare the product briefs and folders.

Responsibilities

  • Analyze and synthesize product, range and collection information
  • Support to the Product Manager in the development of the strategies for the introduction of parts and the positioning of product lines
  • Follow the evolution of existing collections and the product life cycle
  • Support the Product Leader in Product Brief Preparation
  • Collect and structure data in a clear and condensed way
  • Prepare files for various sessions and discussions
  • Participate in price and positioning analyses
  • Analyze product results on the market
  • Establish and maintain databases of information on watch products.

Profile

  • University, hotel or HEC training
  • Experience (3 years) in product management, product marketing or product management in watchmaking, luxury or within a multinational company
  • Sense of detail and constant concern for quality
  • Demonstrated analytical skills
  • Organized, rigorous and methodical
  • Dynamic, autonomous and versatile
  • Humble, respectful
  • Relationship, excellent communication and good synthesis
  • Ability to work with multiple teams
  • Advanced management of office automation software (Excel, PowerPoint) (e.g. launch of analyses to monitor product ranges)
  • Excellent mastery of French and English.

Conclusion

We offer a stimulating environment, attractive working conditions, flexibility schemes and top social benefits.

Please send a complete application file (CV, cover letter, diplomas, certificates).
