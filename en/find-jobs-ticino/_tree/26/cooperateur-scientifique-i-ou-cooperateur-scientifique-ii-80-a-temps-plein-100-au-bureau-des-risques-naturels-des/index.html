Scientific co-operator I or Scientific co-operator II (80%) – full-time (100%) at the Office for Natural Hazards, Fires and Projects, Bellinzona

Class and annual salary (including 13th monthly)

Scientific collaborator/author

10 88'633.-- / 141'990.--

or

Tasks

  • manages the processing and updating of studies on areas exposed to geological natural hazards (PZP) for areas of competence
  • participates in the management of emergency situations in relation to natural hazards
  • examines the practices of building nature (LE) and planning (PR) according to geological hazards and processes sectoral notice
  • guarantees the necessary technical-scientific support for monitoring unstable areas
  • provides geological advice for the activities of the Forestry Section
  • participates in intercantonal working groups and category associations

Requirements

  • complete academic training (bachelor + master or license) in geology or equivalent title
  • excellent knowledge of natural geological hazards
  • decision-making, flexibility and availability to work outside working hours, especially in case of emergency.
  • good ability to move on the ground
  • good knowledge of conventional computer programs, including GIS applications
  • teamwork attitude and sense of responsibility
  • good knowledge of German and French spoken and written

Special remarks

  • the function of scientific collaborator/scientist I (class 10) can be attributed only to candidates who have complete academic qualifications: university license, degree, Bachelor's + Master's degree or professional university (Bachelor + Master 270/300 ECTS credits) and who have gained at least 15 years of professional experience in the required scope
  • the function of scientific collaborator/scientist/II (class 9) will be attributed to candidates with less than 15 years of professional experience in the required field and in possession of complete academic qualifications: university license, degree, consecutive master's degree or professional university school (Bachelor + Master 270/300 ECTS credits)

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

3 November 2026
