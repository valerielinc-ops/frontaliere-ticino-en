Full description

A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a versatile activity in the family environment, the Thun Hospital also offers you the opportunity for flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region. Are you looking meaningful, but also fair? Then you're right here.

In the old-age psychiatric outpatient center, you work in a small nursing team, care for people with mental illnesses in their personal environment and support our medical service in the old-age and nursing homes of the region with the treatment. Your tasks also include talking to relatives and coaching the care teams in the homes. They participate in the interdisciplinary team, even make a blood sample or a depot injection.

The day clinic has a general psychiatric orientation with a treatment focus for patients: women with personality disorders. Treatment according to the latest and proven effective therapy methods, as well as an efficient interdisciplinary cooperation between nursing, psychology, art therapy, social work and medical service, are important to us. Her responsibilities include the management and co-management of milieutherapeutic and psychoeducational group services, as well as the co-care of patients in the day clinic from entry to discharge.
