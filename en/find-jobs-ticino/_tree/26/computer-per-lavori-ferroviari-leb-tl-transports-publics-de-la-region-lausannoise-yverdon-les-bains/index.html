Full description

Within movodis, the LEB links Lausanne to Gros-de-Vaud and supports the development of a growing region. By 2035, he plans to introduce a 10-minute pace between Lausanne and Echallens in order to offer ever more attractive and sustainable mobility.

This development is accompanied by many infrastructure projects and requires strict planning and coordination of railway and civil engineering works. To ensure the safety, reliability and sustainability of the network, movodis is seeking a motivated coordinator to organise and monitor the LEB's railway work.

Computers for railway works LEB

Your Mission

As part of the LEB's public transport service mission

  • Contribute to the safety and quality of transport delivery on the LEB network during work

carried out during the operation of the lines and in the vicinity of the tracks

  • Coordinate the implementation of the processes of modification of the holding by involving the

partners concerned internal and external

Your activities

Receipt of work requests

Ensures receipt, coordination and transmission of requests for work on the rail network:

  • By receiving and controlling internal and external work authorization (DAT) applications
  • By ensuring records of work carried out in compliance with laws, safety standards and aspects of Occupational Health and Safety (OHS)
  • By monitoring, maintaining and updating working procedures in line with the evolution of the legal security framework
  • By establishing the Electrical Manoeuvring Orders (OM)
  • Ensuring, at all times, levels of safety training for internal and external stakeholders

Coordination of work

Ensures coordination of agreed work in relation to relevant processes, internally and externally:

  • By regularly anticipating and informing the relevant processes about the disturbances caused by ongoing and/or future construction projects
  • By planning the work with a view to minimizing the disadvantages to the operation
  • By analysing the working methodology in order to define the appropriate safety measures and informing the applicant, in real time, of the constraints/adaptations resulting therefrom.
  • By alerting its hierarchy for any non-compliance or security risk
  • Providing LEB representation in coordination sessions with external partners
  • Ensuring the necessary monitoring and interface with municipal services
  • By organising checks on compliance with security measures and procedures during interventions
