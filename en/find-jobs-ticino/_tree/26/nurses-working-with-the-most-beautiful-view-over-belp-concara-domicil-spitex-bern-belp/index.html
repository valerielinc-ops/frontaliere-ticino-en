Full description

Tasks

For our Belp dormitory with 35 residents and our Centre of Competence Dementia Oberried with 80 residents, we are looking for dedicated care professionals to strengthen our teams in day service.

Nestled in a beautiful, rural environment, we offer you a workplace where coexistence counts, self-responsibility is promoted and the needs of our residents are the focus.

In the Belp dormitory, you can expect varied entrances and exits, which ensure a versatile and exciting everyday work. We look forward to professionals who, with their heart and expertise, ensure individual and need-oriented support.

At the Centre for Dementia Oberried, we offer you the opportunity to contribute and further develop your expertise in a specialized environment. You can expect a meaningful activity with a professional focus, in which the quality of life and well-being of our residents are at the center.

We are interested in you, your career, your skills and your goals. We are happy to tell you more about our houses and the open possibilities. Send us your application or contact us without obligation by phone or e-mail. We look forward to meeting you.

Requirements

  • tertiary education (FH, HF, DNII, ACP, PsyKP, KWS), or
  • Secondary education (FaGe, FaBe, DNI, FASRK, caregiver:in, home caregiver:in and others)
  • Joy of caring in a family atmosphere and fun together

We offer

  • Appreciative and collegial cooperation
  • Varied and exciting everyday work
  • Self-responsible working with creative scope

Domicil offers senior citizens stationary services in care and care. With more than 20 locations and around 1,600 employees, Domicil is the number 1 in long-term care in the canton of Bern. Under the umbrella of Concara, we are committed to an integrated all-round supply together with Spitex Bern.

Domicil dormitory Belp is located in the most beautiful location above the roofs of Belp. In a family environment, we care for and look after holistically and individually.
