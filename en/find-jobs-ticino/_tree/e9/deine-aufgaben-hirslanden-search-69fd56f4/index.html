Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Bern, Bern Occupation as of: August 1, 2027 Type of employment: Temporary Reference number: 69759 As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus. Hirslanden Bern AG includes the Beau-Site Clinic, the Salem Hospital and the Permanence Clinic with a total of around 350 beds. We are offering two apprenticeships as EFZ clerks when training begins on August 1, 2027. During your training, you will be deployed in the various administrative areas of our hospital group. This gives you a comprehensive insight into the healthcare system and allows you to benefit from different teams and skills at all of our locations. YOUR TASKS

  • You process outpatient and inpatient registrations
  • You order consumer goods and capital goods and manage the printed matter warehouse
  • You manage appointments, prepare meetings and answer emails and various correspondence
  • You record the services provided and create patient invoices
  • You answer inquiries from patients and insurance companies
  • You participate in ongoing marketing and communication projects and coordinate print and layout orders
  • You process Invoices, checks incoming payments and supports booking YOUR PROFILE
  • You have completed primary school (secondary school or very good secondary school qualification)
  • You enjoy a diverse range of tasks in the field of healthcare
  • You are interested in administrative work
  • You work reliably and independently
  • You are open and communicative
  • You have good manners
  • You are an upbeat, motivated and interested personality OUR OFFER

  • Annual learner excursion For further information, please contact Nefise Xhemaili, Head of Management Support and KV vocational trainer, at T and Sarah Jäggi, head of vocational training, is happy to help at T.
