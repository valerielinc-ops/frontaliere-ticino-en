Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population? Facility Management is a key partner for the smooth operation of the hospital and ambulatory health facilities. With the areas of technology, buildings & areas, security, catering, housekeeping and supply chain services, we ensure a reliable infrastructure and diverse services for high-quality patient care. With a planned new hospital building and various ongoing construction projects, we are actively shaping the future of our hospital. Already in the spring of 2027, the commissioning of an approximately 4,000 m2 area in a new building at the Biel station is scheduled.

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an

Head of Projects & Process Facility Management (80-100%)

You do.

As a staff member of the Facility Management Directorate, you play a multifaceted key role: You drive the further development of processes and digitalization, lead projects and coordinate the interests of Facility Management in construction projects – always with the aim of ensuring efficient operation.

  • Construction projects and commissioning: They coordinate the FM departments involved and ensure that operational requirements are taken into account at an early stage and that the conditions for a smooth start of operations are created.
  • New hospital building: Represented as sub-project manager/in Facility Management They take the operational interests of facility management in the planned new hospital building and actively contribute its requirements to the planning, implementation and commissioning.
  • Projects, processes and concepts: You lead cross-divisional projects, optimize processes and develop operational concepts together with the departments. They ensure the sustainable implementation and the integration of the results into operational operations.
  • Project Portfolio Management: You control and coordinate the Facility Management project portfolio in close coordination with the supervisor. You support the prioritization and coordination of projects and work on interdisciplinary hospital projects.
  • Digitalization and Applications: They drive the digitalization and further development of processes and systems in facility management. In particular, you are responsible for the further development of the CAFM solution and other relevant applications in cooperation with the responsible internal and external partners.
  • Professional representation and contract management: You represent the interests of facility management at internal and external interfaces, contribute your expertise to internal commissions, working groups and interdisciplinary projects and support the superior position in contract management.

That sets them apart.

  • Project and process experience: You have profound experience in managing complex projects, process optimization and controlling cross-divisional projects. They coordinate different interests, set priorities and carry out projects in a targeted manner from conception to sustainable implementation.
  • Construction project competence: You know the requirements of construction and commissioning projects. Experience in planning and construction-related facility management (pbFM) is particularly welcome.
  • Digitalization understanding: You are interested in the further development of digital processes and applications and ideally bring appropriate experience.
  • Social and service competence: You communicate convincingly, enjoy working with different professional groups and win stakeholders for joint solutions. You align your actions with the requirements of a reliable and economical hospital operation.
  • Personality and way of working: You think in a networked and entrepreneurial way, recognize potential for optimization and drive change forward on your own initiative, structured and solution-oriented. They have strong organisational skills, assertiveness and an independent and structured way of working.
  • Industry knowledge :

Responsibility with room for manoeuvre: You take on a versatile key position with close exchange with the management team of Facility Management and actively participate in the further development of projects, processes and digital solutions. Shaping the future of the hospital: They are involved in important construction and development projects and help ensure that new hospital spaces are optimally oriented to the operational requirements and are successfully put into operation.
