You like to be there for people and keep an overview in everyday life? As an innovative and regionally networked SPITEX, we focus on humanity, team spirit and a professional service. Employee customer service (m/f/d) Your tasks – versatile and close to people

  • Good MS-Office user knowledge
  • Experience with ERP systems is a plus
