Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2450 employees, including 245 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Finance and Information Technology Directorate provides its expertise in the financial management of the company, procurement and wealth as well as IT services. It is within this management, in direct connection with its Director, that you will lead the Group's digital strategy.

Mission

  • You define and guide the Group's digital strategy in line with its strategic directions and actively contribute to its digital transformation;
  • You manage the economic performance of IT, budgets, sourcing models and relationships with suppliers and strategic partners;
  • You position IT as a true partner of the trades, supporting the management in their digital transformation issues, Data and AI and ensuring the prioritization of needs;
  • You ensure the overall conduct of the IT Services Unit, define its priorities and ensure consistency between operation (y.c. critical infrastructures), architecture, platforms and transformation initiatives;
  • You guarantee efficient and sustainable IT governance, as well as the long-term coherence and evolution of the information system;
  • You drive and develop your teams in a culture of collaboration, autonomy and accountability, while ensuring the safety, risk management and resilience of digital services.

Profile

  • To benefit from a proven experience in the conduct of a complex IT organisation, with the ability to federate and accompany organisational developments, ideally acquired in a changing environment;
  • Mastering governance, IT portfolio, financial management and external partner management issues completes your journey;
  • At ease in interactions with trades and different levels of management, you can develop a true partnership relationship and challenge constructively;
  • A strong understanding of IT and digital issues, combined with a strategic vision of the topics of architecture, data, d
  • Leadership, pragmatism and arbitration enable you to combine strategic vision, operational performance and change support.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together for tomorrow's energy"
