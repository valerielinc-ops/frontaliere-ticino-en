The Position Internship in Business Enablement in Data, Analytics & Research (Location: Pune)

The Position Do you want to be part of a purpose-driven environment? Are you passionate about project and process management? Are you a team player and want to work in an international environment? Then join our Business Enablement Team in Data, Analytics & Research - for 6-12 months, based in Pune, India.

What we offer

  • Opportunity to gain experience and insights into Business Management and the future of Data, Analytics & Research in digital healthcare

Role Responsibilities

  • Provide transparent and timely reporting on assigned tasks as well as delegated assignments, such as monthly Data Fabric or Research Portfolio reporting collection and compilation
