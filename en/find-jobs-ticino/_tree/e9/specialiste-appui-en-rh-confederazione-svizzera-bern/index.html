Full description

Responsibility for the monitoring of strategic and conceptual HRM projects involving the various stakeholders as well as the development of basic principles and ensuring concrete HRM measures

  • Responsibility for HR process management and personal safety checks (according to ISG/BPG)
  • Professional support in the topics ICS and functional evaluation
  • Creation of data evaluations from the personnel information system
  • Provision of target group-oriented documents for the management (GL) of the BAZG and other committees
  • Support and representation in other HR subjects as required
  • (Fach-)Hochschulstudium or Eidg. Professional ID as HR specialist or comparable training in HR management
  • Several years of professional experience ideally within the federal administration
  • Experience with process management and function evaluations is beneficial
  • High affinity for figures and IT applications with very good SAP and Excel skills
  • Experience with other HR tools (e.g. Microsoft Power BI, SuccessFactors)
  • Strong analytical, organizational and conceptual skills
  • High initiative and the ability to work in a team and independently
  • Good active knowledge of a second official language
