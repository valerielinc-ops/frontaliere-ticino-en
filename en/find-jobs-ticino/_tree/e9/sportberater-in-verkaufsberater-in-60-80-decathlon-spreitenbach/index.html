Full description

Mission about us! We are Decathlon, one of the largest sporting goods manufacturers and distributors in the world! With businesses in 70 countries and a team of nearly 105,000 dedicated employees worldwide, we have been committed to our mission of moving people through the wonders of sport since 1976. Since our introduction in Switzerland in 2016, Decathlon Switzerland has grown considerably and now operates 42 stores throughout Switzerland with almost 1000 employees. Driven by our passion for accessibility, sustainability, affordability, quality and innovation, we are on a mission to make Decathlon Switzerland a destination for sports enthusiasts. Become part of our team and shape the future of Decathlon Switzerland! For our Spreitenbach branch, we are looking for motivated sports leaders (m/f/d) who can flexibly support our team in a workload of 50-70%. Your mission: Inspiring our customers and sharing your passion for bikes

  • Present the premium product range of Decathlon and draw attention to it for customers.
  • Receive and advise our customers on products, services and innovations.
  • Ensure availability and optimal presentation of products on the shelf.
  • Support the cash processes.
  • Actively contribute to the economic development of your area.
  • Take full responsibility for your sales profile
  • You bring experience in sports retailing.
  • Working hours MI-FR-SA
  • As a passionate cyclist you have an entrepreneurial mindset.
  • You are a sociable personality with strong communication skills and work in a structured and well organized way.
  • In addition, you are characterized by good networking skills and a pragmatic and efficient working style.
  • You’re ready to work on Saturdays – the most important day in retail.
