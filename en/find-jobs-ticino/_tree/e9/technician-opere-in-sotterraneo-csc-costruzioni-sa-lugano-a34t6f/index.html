Full description

CSC Buildings SA, part of the international group WeBuild, is a company based in Lugano that has been active for over 60 years in Switzerland in the realization of complex and sustainable works.

Thanks to the support and know-how of WeBuild, among the leading global players in the construction industry, CSC combines solidity, innovation and international expertise. With more than 300 employees and more than 20 projects in progress, we contribute to the development of modern and high quality infrastructure throughout Switzerland.

For our Technical Office Gare Lugano we are looking for a Technical Works in Underground

The resource will become part of the Technical Office Gare with the aim of supporting the preparation of technical-economic offers for projects of underground works, both for new achievements and for rehabilitation and modernization of tunnels.

The figure will work closely with technicians, estimators and industry specialists, contributing to project analysis and to the definition of the best technical and economic solutions.

Following the eventual award of the contract, the candidate may also be involved in the execution phase, ensuring technical continuity between the stage of the competition and the execution of the work in the yard.

Main responsibility

Race phase

  • Analyze the documentation of the race, including specifications, elaborate design, computi metrici e chronoprogrammi.
  • Evaluate the technical feasibility of the works and identify possible criticalities, opportunities and improvements.
  • Collaboration with the definition of the technical-economic strategy of the offer.
  • Prepare estimates of costs, times, resources and executive methodologies.
  • Create technical reports, elaborate graphics and documentation to support the offer.
  • Coordinate activities with external consultants, suppliers and specialists involved in the competition process.
  • Contribute to the enhancement of corporate references in tunneling and underground works.

Executive phase

  • Support the planning and organization of construction work.
  • Check the application of technical solutions developed during the race phase.
  • Provide technical and operational support to project management.
  • Care for technical documentation and progress monitoring.
  • Interface with Works, Committence and project stakeholders.
  • Collaboration with the resolution of emerging technical issues during the execution of the works.

Requirements

  • Technical diploma or degree in Civil Engineering, Construction Engineering or related disciplines.
  • Consolidated experience in underground works, matured in roles such as: Director of Shipyard, Construction Manager or equivalent positions.
  • Direct operational experience in traditional excavation projects, indispensable requirement. Scavo in TBM is a plus.
  • Excellent readability and analysis of capitulates, computations, design elaborates and technical documentation.
  • Ability to develop technical-economic evaluations and preliminary estimates.
  • Knowledge of the main processes of construction and rehabilitation of galleries.
  • Skills in drafting and managing offers, technical reports, computed and chronoprogrammes.
  • Ability to identify alternative technical solutions and value engineering activities.
  • Excellent knowledge of the Italian language.
  • Great knowledge of English and/or French.
  • German knowledge would be a plus.
