Full description

You like to work practically, think technically networked and keep track of complex plants? In this demanding function in 2nd-level support, you take on a central role in maintenance. You will analyze disruptions, develop sustainable solutions and actively contribute to the optimization of our peripheral, feed and conveying systems as well as our maintenance processes. Your Tasks:

  • Ensuring 2nd-level support for production facilities and technical systems
  • Analyzing, Diagnosing and Resolving Disorders in a Sustainable manner
  • Performing maintenance, inspection and maintenance work
  • Active participation in the continuous improvement of plants and processes (CIP)
  • Implementation and further development of standardizations in maintenance
  • Preparation, maintenance and updating of technical documentation and maintenance documents
  • Execution of mechatronic installation, conversion and adaptation work
  • Support for commissioning, testing and acceptance of plants and components
  • Close cooperation with internal specialist offices and external partners
  • Cooperation in spare parts management Your profile:
  • Basic technical training EFZ completed (e.g. polymechanic, production mechanic, plant and apparatus manufacturer, automotive mechatronics engineer or equivalent)
  • Several years of experience in mechanical engineering, assembly, maintenance or technical support
  • Very good mechanical understanding and basic knowledge of pneumatics
  • Safe in reading and understanding technical drawings and documentation
  • Experience in systematic troubleshooting and diagnosis in the industrial environment
  • Independent, structured and precise working method
  • High sense of responsibility, initiative and a solution-oriented way of thinking
  • Basic English skills for technical documentation and exchange with external partners We offer:
  • Six weeks of holidays as well as the possibility to buy additional holidays
  • Annual participation in success
  • Attractive sports offers and employee events
  • Staff restaurant with discounted conditions
  • Future-oriented company with a stable perspective
  • Varied, responsible area of responsibility
