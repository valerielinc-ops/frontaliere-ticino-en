Full description

In the role of Team Leader Training Logistics (m/f/d) in Neuchâtel am Rhein, you will lead the specialists of tomorrow – professionally, disciplinary and humanly. At the start you will accompany three to five apprentices in warehouse logistics, in the medium term we will grow to 10 to 15.

Mansioni

  • Professional and disciplinary management of our trainees in logistics
  • Responsibility for the complete, comprehensible and practical delivery of all training content
  • Development and further development of training concepts, rotation plans and quality assurance measures
  • Lateral management of practical trainees and specialist departments
  • Recruitment of trainees and practical trainees: You find the people who suit us and inspire them for logistics
  • Employer branding with substance – making education quality and social commitment visible at trade fairs, school cooperations and in everyday life at the location
  • Change and stakeholder management: reconciling vocational training and day-to-day operations
  • Control via key figures and reporting – so that training quality does not remain gut feeling, but becomes measurable

Requirements

  • 3: Training
  • Completed Vocational Apprenticeship (EBA): Logistics
  • Completed vocational training in the logistics environment, for example as a specialist for warehouse logistics, plus several years of practical experience in the corresponding professional field
  • Instructor suitability according to AEVO (AdA bill) – available or with the clear intention of acquiring it in a timely manner with us
  • Sound knowledge in didactics, methodology and competence-oriented assessment
  • Experience in developing clear goals, communicating and really giving up responsibility instead of just distributing
  • A role model that you don’t have to play because you live it
  • German and English negotiable in word and writing, French of advantage
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Thinking and acting with absolute respect for human dignity and taking into account the human consequences of decisions
  • Have the courage to try things without planning them down to the smallest detail and build trust through consistent and reliable actions
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (negotiable)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Flexible working time: You record your working time – and compensate for every single minute over your contractually agreed target time.
  • Flexible place of work / home office: You arrange your working hours and places individually tailored to you. You can also work from abroad for up to eight weeks a year.
  • Employee offers: As an employee you benefit from special prices (for your own needs) on our entire range
  • Individual training: 2,000 Edu Points per year for your self-study and garage projects
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: Our working environment is characterized by our values, an open feedback and a positive culture of error
  • Design and decision-making leeway: Via our innovation board, everyone has the opportunity to contribute their ideas directly to the management
  • Values instead of blabla: cooperative, innovative, pirate, self-responsible, ambitious – these five values we all demand and live.
  • Boldly think for yourself: We encourage everyone to think for themselves and actively. And love it when you think bravely, newly and differently.
  • Uncomplicated: We avoid the unnecessary and focus on the important. From mastering challenges to celebrating our successes.
  • Flexibility instead of bending (Office Jobs): In addition to home office possibilities, we allow you up to 8 weeks of workation. In addition, every position is also possible in the 80% amount.
  • Rapid growth: With us, you are constantly growing and developing. And get EDU points (2'000 CHF for employees in Switzerland, 1'600 EUR for German employees and 700 EUR for our Serbian employees) for your further development, so that you do not stand still.
  • Further Benefits Germany: Benefit from 28 days of holidays, exclusive employee prices in the shop and other benefits.
