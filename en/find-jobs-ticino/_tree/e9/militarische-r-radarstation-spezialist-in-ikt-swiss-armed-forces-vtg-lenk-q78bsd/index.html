Full description

Put to the Point

Do you want to use your professional skills in a unique working environment? Then support the Swiss Army in ensuring the operation of the Military Radar Station at around 3000 m above sea level and contribute your specialized know-how.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20705
  • Location: 3775 Steering

You can make this contribution

  • Responsible for the operation and maintenance of radar, information and communication systems
  • Provide the basic ICT infrastructure at the assigned main installation and its locations (installation, replacement and decommissioning of the hardware and network components)
  • Professional management of the technical staff and responsible for their technical education and training in the field of ICT
  • Participate in drills and missions of the company fire brigade, company medical, mountain and cable car rescue and help with snow clearance
  • Support the employees of the technical building equipment (mechanics, electricians, HLKKS specialists) in maintaining the plant infrastructure

This makes you unique

  • Higher vocational training or basic technical training (EFZ) as a network technician, mediamatist, electronics technician, automatic technician or equivalent
  • Experience in the operation and maintenance of radar, information and communication systems
  • Logical and analytical thinking skills, communicative team player, flexible and responsible (picking service)
  • Connected to nature, resilient, basic physical fitness provided
  • Knowledge of a second official language and knowledge of English

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Additional information
