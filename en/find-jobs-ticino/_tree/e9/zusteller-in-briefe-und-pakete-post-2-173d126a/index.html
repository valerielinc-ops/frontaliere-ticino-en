Full description

Do you like to get up early and enjoy being on the road independently? Then you've come to the right place, because with you, dreams arrive intact and on time.

You can do that

  • Early in the morning (around 6 a.m.) you and your team will load the delivery vehicles and prepare your delivery tour.
  • Through your work you shape the streetscape of Siebnen and embody the values ​​of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the punctual and flawless delivery of parcels and letters in your area.
  • On your tour you will answer questions from our customers and contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • You work regularly on Saturdays, with the duties being divided within the team.

This is what you bring with you

  • With your physical fitness, you can easily load and lift packages weighing up to 30 kg.
  • You enjoy traveling, have a category B driving license and ideally A1 (motorcycle up to 125ccm) or are willing to get this before starting the job.
  • You value working in a team but also enjoy traveling independently and feel comfortable driving vehicles in traffic.
  • You are reliable and work quickly.
  • Good oral German skills and an experienced use of digital devices round off your profile.
