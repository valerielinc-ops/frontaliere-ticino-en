Full description

Join Migros Geneva and benefit from a dynamic job within a motivated team! We are looking for candidates living less than 15 minutes away from our Servette store. In just 3 hours a day, from 6:30 AM to 9:30 AM, you contribute to providing a pleasant experience for customers. If this sounds like you, join us.

Tasks

  • Shelf Expert: You ensure the filling and organization of the shelves. Every product is in its place and handled with care.
  • Freshness Master: Nothing stays out of the fridge and no expired product escapes you. With you, the cold chain is perfectly respected.
  • Hygiene Champion: HACCP rules? Don't worry, you will be trained and become an expert on the subject.
  • Organization Ace: Thanks to you, the store is always clean, tidy, and welcoming. Order becomes an art in your hands.
  • Indispensable Teammate: With your team spirit, you contribute to quality service and a positive working atmosphere.
  • Outstanding Advisor: Customers benefit from your warm welcome and wise advice for a 5-star experience.

Requirements

  • You communicate easily with colleagues and customers in French.
  • You are ready to commit for the long term (at least 12 months), even to supplement another activity.
  • You are committed to working as a team and always doing well.
  • You live near our store (15 minutes).
  • For your application to be considered, you must provide a complete file.
  • Your personality, motivation, and skills are what matter most.
  • Completed initial or further training.
  • French (Good command).

What we offer

  • Catering: Meal discount of Fr. 4 (for purchases over 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus Points: 2% and 5% discount depending on the assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Preferential interest rate and no fees for a savings account.
