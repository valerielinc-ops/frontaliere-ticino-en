Full description

QA Engineer - AI-Powered • Geneva, CH • Full-time • Unlimited Your Role As QA Engineer in our Artificial Intelligence Department, you own the test strategy of a strategic legacy transformation - replacing a system with minimal automated test coverage, where your regression suite is the safety net for a module-by-module cutover. You use the bank's internal AI ecosystem to generate tests, analyze coverage and automate aggressively. You will join our newly established Artificial Intelligence Department and contribute to a strategic, multi-year legacy transformation program that modernizes core banking processes for our private banking business. You will be part of the AI team, leverage the bank's internal AI knowledge, tools and private-LLM ecosystem, and use AI to power your day-to-day work - while the focus of the role is on delivering the transformation, not on building AI products.

Your Responsibilities

  • Define and own the program-wide test strategy: automation, regression suite, environment management, and sign-off gates for every module go-live
  • Build the automation pipeline from the ground up during the foundation phase — the new platform must not inherit the legacy system's sparse test coverage
  • Own cross-module regression in the final phase: performance and security hardening, UAT defect burn-down, final data reconciliation
  • Coordinate UAT with the Product Owner and business SMEs, and validate integrations with surrounding core banking systems
  • Use AI-powered tooling from the bank's internal AI ecosystem for test generation, test-data creation, defect analysis and coverage reporting
  • Support data-migration rehearsals and user training in the later program phases, together with the Product Owner and UX/UI

Your Profile

  • University degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in software quality assurance, including test automation in complex, regulated environments
  • Strong hands-on skills in test automation frameworks (e.g. Playwright/Cypress, JUnit/RestAssured), CI/CD-integrated pipelines (Azure DevOps or similar) and API testing
  • Experience building regression suites for system replacement or migration programs is a strong advantage
  • Hands-on experience using AI tools (LLM assistants, AI test-generation tooling) in your daily workflow
  • Structured, risk-based mindset: you know what to test deeply and what to automate first
  • Fluency in English; other languages are an advantage
