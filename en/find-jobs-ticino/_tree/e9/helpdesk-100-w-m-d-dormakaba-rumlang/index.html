Full description

Become part of our team

As a global company that pursues very clear growth goals, we need employees who want to take themselves and the company forward. For our location in Rümlang we are looking for you immediately or by agreement as Helpdesk staff/100 % In this versatile function, you are the first point of contact for technical customer inquiries about our system solutions for access and time. You coordinate fault reports, support our customers and specialist partners and together with our specialists ensure reliable and customer-oriented processing.

Your tasks

  • You answer customer inquiries by phone and email and process customer orders.
  • You collect, document and process fault messages and technical requests. ,
  • You prioritize disruptions according to the agreed service levels (SLAs).
  • You pass on complex concerns to service technicians, software experts in the 2nd level or other responsible bodies.
  • You ensure the flow of information between customers, specialist partners and internal teams.
  • You plan and coordinate on-site operations of our service technicians.
  • You check the required material, drawings, configuration specifications and software for the applications.

Your profile

  • You have completed a technical education or a commercial education with a strong technical understanding.
  • Ideally, you will have around three years of experience in technical customer support, helpdesk or a similar environment.
  • You have good PC knowledge and technical understanding of hardware, software as well as electronics or electrical engineering.
  • You work in a structured, independent and solution-oriented way – even under high time pressure.
  • You convince with customer orientation, clear communication and a responsible appearance.
  • You are resilient, open and proactive and actively participate in technical challenges.
  • You communicate safely in German
  • Knowledge of French and English is an advantage.

What we offer

  • Best prospects in an internationally successful company with a family corporate culture
  • A motivated and competent team that looks forward to your support
  • Multifaceted tasks at the interface of customer service and technology
  • Self-responsible work and close exchange with technical specialists

Who we are For every place that matters. From automatic doors to cloud-based access management, as a global leader in the market for access solutions for schools, banks, airports, hospitals, hotels and many more, we enable safe and sustainable places where people can move seamlessly. With over 160 years of experience and a clear focus on excellence and innovation, we offer exciting opportunities to advance in an environment where you can truly make a difference. At dormakaba, you are encouraged to show initiative, develop your skills and shape your career together with 16,000 experts worldwide who set new standards in the industry every day.
