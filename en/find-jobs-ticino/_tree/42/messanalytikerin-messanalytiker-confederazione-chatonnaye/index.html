Put to the Point

If necessary, analyze, identify and correct faults for the Radio Monitoring section, which ensures quality assurance of the frequency spectrum for Switzerland and within the framework of international obligations.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20941
  • Location: Route de la Brévire 4, 1553 Châtonnaye

You can make this contribution

  • Analyse and assess disruptive situations
  • Carry out measurements, issue directives and take appropriate measures if technical requirements or conformity are not met
  • Taking into account the economic, technical, chronological and organizational requirements, handling incidents
  • Assist in the operation of the 24-hour port of call for security services (picquet service)
  • Support internal sites for measurements on complex information transfer systems

This makes them unique.

  • Completed technical training (TS / HF / Jun. Ing. FH / Bachelor)
  • Basic electrical, electrotechnical or computer science training and good knowledge of radio and measurement technology
  • Knowledge of RF and EMC measurement technology as well as good PC knowledge (Windows applications)
  • Independent, proactive, capable of teamwork and ready for special missions
  • Good oral and written knowledge of a second official language

With expertise into the future.

It is part of the Federal Department of Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications (UVEK).

As an employee of BAKOM, you will lead Switzerland into the future. They take responsibility in challenging projects with the aim of maintaining the high quality of life in our country.
