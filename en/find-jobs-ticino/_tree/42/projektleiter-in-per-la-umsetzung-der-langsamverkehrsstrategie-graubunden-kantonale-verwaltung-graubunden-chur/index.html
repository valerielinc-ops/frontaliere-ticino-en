Full description

The Civil Engineering Office is responsible for the planning, construction, maintenance and operation of cantonal roads as well as hydraulic engineering and non-motorized traffic in Graubünden. We are also responsible for the operational maintenance of the national roads in the canton. With the introduction of operational mobility management, the management of the cantonal vehicle fleet was also centralized in order to ensure more economical and environmentally friendly operation. As the specialist department for non-motorized traffic, we coordinate the planning and construction of non-motorized traffic facilities. This includes, in particular, walking, hiking, cycling and mountain biking as well as transportation with vehicle-like devices. As a Friendly Work Space certified office, the health and satisfaction of our approximately 480 employees is particularly important to us. For this reason, we are committed to systematic occupational health management (OHM). We are looking for a qualified specialist for the implementation of the slow traffic strategy 2037 (LV 2037 strategy) within the cantonal administration as of June 1, 2026 or by arrangement. ## Tasks

  • Implementation of the LV 2037 strategy ( Link )
  • Promoting and anchoring a LV-friendly planning culture in the canton of Graubünden
  • Designing processes and establishing a steering committee for the implementation of the LV strategy
  • Leading key projects related to the LV 2037 strategy
  • Coordinating work related to the LV 2037 strategy within the Civil Engineering Office and other cantonal offices and coordinating strategy, Preparation of reports on the implementation status of the LV strategy for the attention of the relevant office management and politicians
  • Procurement of external services for the processing of strategic topics in LV
  • Provision of data and bases for communication and for pilot projects in the area of LV ##
