Full description

Nidwalden Hospital is a modern acute hospital with 90 beds, around 600 dedicated employees and is responsible for extended basic care. As a company in the LUKS Group, we are part of a powerful network that is one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. Together, we ensure coordinated, digitally networked and accessible healthcare around the clock with passion and commitment. In order to further develop our orthopedics and trauma surgery clinic at the Nidwalden Hospital and thus within the LUKS Group, we are looking for someone from January 1st. January 2027 or by agreement a committed, integrative and entrepreneurial personality as tasks:

  • Medical, technical and organizational management of the orthopedic surgery and trauma surgery department at the Stans location
  • Operational, economic and personnel responsibility at the location, in close coordination with the cross-location clinic management of the orthopedics and trauma surgery clinic of the LUKS Group
  • Seat in the cross-location clinic management as well as active participation in the joint technical and strategic development of the clinic
  • As a member of Hospital management Shared responsibility for the development of the Nidwalden Hospital within the LUKS Group
  • Participation in the harmonization of treatment standards, processes and portfolios across the locations
  • Ensuring high-quality, economical and patient-oriented care
  • Leadership and further development of the medical team at the location
  • Promotion of interprofessional collaboration within the hospital as well as with referring doctors and other external partners
  • Further development of the range of services at the Stans location in line with the clinic's common strategy for orthopedics and trauma surgery of the LUKS Group
  • Participation in everyday clinical practice and background service Requirements:
  • Specialist title in orthopedic surgery and traumatology of the musculoskeletal system or specialist title in surgery with a focus on specialized traumatology (FMH or equivalent)
  • Proven specialization in a joint surgery focus (e.g. hip, knee, shoulder or foot/ankle) or in specialized traumatology
  • Broad and well-founded experience in general trauma surgery of the musculoskeletal system
  • Several years of management experience in a comparable function
  • Strong leadership, communication and social skills
  • Enjoy working together in a cross-location clinic structure and willingness to support and help shape common standards
  • Ability to accompany change processes, integrative personality
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and joy in the further development of structures and processes
  • High patient orientation as well as quality awareness
  • Networking in the region is an advantage We offer:

  • A committed and interprofessional team
  • Attractive employment conditions within the LUKS Group
  • Modern infrastructure and short decision-making processes
  • An appreciative corporate culture with a focus on collaboration and quality
