Full description

Are you an IT specialist and are you interested in the topics of living and care in old age? You want to go by 4. January 2027 or by agreement in this newly created position as specialist IT-Service Management Senior for the health centers for old age?

  • Together with the team, you set up a structured IT service management, establish practical processes and standards and support IT employees in their application in everyday work.
  • They develop our Atlassian solution into a practical working tool for the computer science of health centers for old age, optimize workflows, boards, automations and reporting and anchor their use in everyday operations.
  • You control change management and ensure that changes are planned, evaluated, documented and implemented in a structured manner.
  • They ensure transparency and commitment in operational IT service management, track service quality, risks, escalations and open measures and ensure traceable documentation and tracking.
  • They coordinate test and service continuity activities and manage operational cooperation with suppliers and internal partners to ensure agreed services, recoverability and improvement measures.
  • Several years of experience in IT service management as well as in-depth knowledge in change management, service continuity, test management, supplier management and service level control
  • Strong ability to structure ITSM processes and standards, to design them in a practical way and to anchor their application together with the team in everyday work
  • Good knowledge of ITSM tools, workflows, reporting and automation
  • Experience with Jira and Confluence is beneficial
  • Completed training at EFZ level with in-depth additional training as well as ITIL-4 certifications or comparable further training in IT service management
  • Strong communication, team and cooperation skills, high personal responsibility and a structured, solution-oriented and reliable working method

An exciting task area in a professional and team-oriented working environment with many design possibilities. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The central services division is located in Zurich-Oerlikon and provides various services within the entire service department. These include computer science (technical IT), finance, HR, marketing, communication, facility management, construction development, central purchasing and quality management.
