Full description

Do you want to help make quality cheese an exceptional specialty? In this function, you accompany the refinement of our Head of Monk with care, rigour and a true sense of quality. With your team, you ensure that we meet our high quality, hygiene and food safety requirements every day.

What you can do

  • You ensure the refining of our Moine Head according to the quality requirements defined.
  • You perform regular visual checks and quickly identify any quality differences.
  • You move and store the cheeses appropriately within the cellar.
  • You adapt the refining plan as needed and ensure optimal treatment of cheeses.
  • You use and monitor the rubbing machine and other facilities in the cellar.

What You Bring

  • You have received vocational training as a milk technologist or in another food-related field and you already have experience in the agri-food industry.
  • You work reliably, carefully and independently.
  • Quality, hygiene and food safety are your priorities.
  • You like to work as a team and willingly support where it is needed.
  • You are ideally licensed as a valid cartoonist.
  • You have good knowledge of French.

What We Offer

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy 5 weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your entitlement to leave gradually increases to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Provident Foundation Emmi pays your old age income beyond the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary, without deduction of coordination. Choose between a Standard or Plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Priority to your health: We offer corporate health management, preventive measures and free social counselling through our partner Proitera for professional and private matters.
  • Individual development planning: Through tailor-made development planning, we promote your individual skills in a targeted manner and encourage your long-term evolution – a central element of our sustainability strategy. Find out more.
  • Optional participation in results: Based on business results, you will receive voluntary participation in the results. We would like to reward your valuable contribution to the success of the company.
  • Attractive supplementary and supplementary benefits: You will receive a 20% premium starting at 8:00 p.m., pre-heating time paid, a credit for changing rooms and a fair allowance for picketing outside your working hours.
