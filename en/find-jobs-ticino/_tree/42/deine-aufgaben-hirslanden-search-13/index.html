Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna | Lucerne

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 54850

Working at St. Anna Clinic means more than just working. The cornerstone of our trusting and close cooperation is the family coexistence that we maintain together with our patients. Now we need your support in the above position.

YOUR TASKS

  • You will be instrumental in all interventions in our entire, broad range of operations
  • You carry out all preparatory and follow-up measures of the operations
  • You are responsible for the professional preparation and operation of the necessary equipment, instruments and sterile goods
  • You carefully document all nursing activities including performance recording
  • You help to ensure a smooth daily routine in the OR
  • You work professionally and closely with various interdisciplinary groups
  • After training, you have the opportunity to take on additional professional responsibilities or their deputies if you are interested.

Your profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Specialist surgical technology
  • Whether generalist or specialist, with us you will find what suits you
  • As a team player, open and respectful communication is just as important to you as it is to us.
  • Your own initiative and commitment are a matter of course for you
  • You are happy to be used at our two locations in Lucerne and Meggen
  • High motivation as well as readiness for shift and night work, piquet and weekend services
  • Returnees are also welcome!

Our offer

  • Since April 2025, we have been working in our state-of-the-art operating tract – become part of our team and shape the future!
  • In currently 11 operating rooms we operate in all specialist areas except heart surgery
  • Further development in a wide variety of specialist areas incl. Assumption of specialist responsibilities or their deputy with allowances up to CHF 350. -
  • Set your roster according to your private life – plan your night and weekend services yourself
  • Possibility of up to 7 weeks of holidays per year
  • Meal in our varied staff restaurant for CHF 5.- and free coffee, tea, mineral and seasonal fruit
  • Paid changing time in the form of up to 4 additional free days
