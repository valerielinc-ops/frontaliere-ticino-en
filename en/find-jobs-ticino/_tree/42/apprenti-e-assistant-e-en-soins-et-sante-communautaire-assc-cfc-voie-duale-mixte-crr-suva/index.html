Join our rehabilitation clinic in Sion and help patients regain their independence! Apprenticeship Community Health Care Assistant (CHS) - SWC, mixed dual route Why choose the RRC? At the Romance Rehabilitation Clinic (CRR), we help patients regain maximum autonomy after an accident or illness. You will discover the accompaniment of people with spinal cord injuries, amputations or neurological conditions, and you will be able to follow their progress during their rehabilitation. You will learn with care teams and other professionals, including physiotherapists and occupational therapists, in an interprofessional approach. To learn more about our clinic, visit our website www.crr-suva.ch/ Your mixed dual course: the first year takes place at the EPASC (theoretical and practical teaching). The 2nd and 3rd years take place in companies according to the dual model, with professional and inter-company courses. What you learn from us

  • You accompany patients in the activities of daily life and encourage them to develop their autonomy.
  • You gradually perform technical care, according to the skills acquired during your training and under the appropriate supervision.
  • You observe the evolution of patients, collaborate with the interprofessional team and contribute to the smooth functioning of the unit. What you bring to the team
  • Your interest in health and your desire to accompany patients.
  • Your curiosity, your benevolence, your sense of listening and your team spirit.
  • Your motivation to learn and gradually gain responsibilities. Your commitment to training We expect you to be involved in school as well as in the field, reliability, team spirit and desire to progress. A two-day discovery course is required before the start of your apprenticeship. What we offer
  • A variety of training, in contact with care and rehabilitation situations in different units of the clinic.
  • A structured accompaniment (days of supervision, referents) , adapted to your progress. Our benefits
  • Remuneration above market standards;
  • Low-cost meals;
  • Free access to sports facilities and swimming pool;
  • Time dedicated to your studies to suit your manager;
  • The ability to continue your career at the CRA after your SFA, depending on the positions available. Want to apply? If the world of health resonates in you and you want to join a team that will support you throughout your learning, apply to us!
