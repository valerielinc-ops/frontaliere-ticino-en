Full description

Your responsibilities

  • Establish timing standards (REFA and MTM)
  • Work directly on the shop floor to analyse assembly and machining processes
  • Set realistic target standard times
  • Make suggestions for improvements to reduce production cycle times
  • Carry out multi-point analyses to identify waste and propose concrete improvements
  • Map the value stream (VSM) in collaboration with shop floor teams
  • Train and support operators and managers in adopting the new standards and methods
  • Document processes to ensure operational continuity and knowledge transfer Your profile
  • You have a higher education qualification (Bachelor's or Master's degree from a university or HES) in mechanical engineering, industrial engineering or as a production engineer.
  • You have at least 2 years of experience in time studies (such as REFA methods) and implementing continuous improvement projects.
  • You are proficient in Lean Manufacturing principles and associated tools: 5S, VSM, Kaizen, Standard Work, DMAIC, etc.
  • Additional training in Lean Management, Six Sigma (Green/Black Belt), or an equivalent qualification is a significant advantage.
  • You are skilled in Microsoft Office tools, particularly Excel, and proficient with data analysis software (e.g., Power BI, Minitab).
  • You are fluent in French and have a very good knowledge of English (B2)
  • You are rigorous, methodical, and analytical, demonstrating autonomy as well as excellent team spirit.
  • You can work effectively in a cross-functional environment, interacting with production, methods, quality, and logistics departments.
