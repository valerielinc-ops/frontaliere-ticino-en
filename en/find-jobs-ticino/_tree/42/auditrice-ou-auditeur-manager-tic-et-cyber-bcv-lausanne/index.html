Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Put your managerial ambitions at the service of a healthy and sustainable performance by joining the canton of Vaud's first universal bank, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Internal Audit, we are looking for:

Announcement ICT & Cyber Manager Auditor Your main missions

  • planning, conducting and carrying out internal audit missions specific to ICT (information and communication technologies) & cyber risk management, business continuity and business resilience of the BCV Group
  • define, in collaboration with the Responsible Auditor, the analytical, evaluation and test approach applicable to the technical or operational aspects within the mission boundaries
  • coordinate the planning and execution of the work with ICT & cyber specialists, data analysts and other stakeholders involved in the missions in order to prioritize and adapt audit techniques to the risks to be covered
  • ensure that audit findings are supported by factual and verifiable evidence and that recommendations are concrete and achievable
  • ensuring the adequacy and effectiveness of the internal control system and ICT & cyber risk control within the Bank and its subsidiaries Your profile:
  • advanced computer and/or information security training EPFL/HES, CISSP certification(s), CCSP, CISA, CISM and/or AAIA an asset
  • confirmed experience in the field of ICT & cyber banking audit (internal and/or external) in Switzerland
  • excellent knowledge of banking applications and systems
  • ability to prioritize in a demanding environment
  • in the conduct, motivation and management of an audit team
  • rigor, analytical mind, capacity for synthesis
  • very good writing skills in French, good knowledge of English and/or German, an asset
  • residence in Switzerland.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
