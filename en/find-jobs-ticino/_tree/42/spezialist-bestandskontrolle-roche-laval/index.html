The Opportunity

In this position, you will play a key role in the inventory control group of the distribution centre. You will solve complex issues, provide technical support, and participate in a variety of RD Canada inventory control and diagnostic products activities. This is a full-time position on site.

  • Operational Support & Products: Support planned cyclical inventories, oversee the product control process with Quality Assurance and coordinate rotation (FIFO) and stock transfers with 3PL logistics providers.
  • Transaction Management & Materiel: Perform SAP transactions (adjustments, transfers, splits, reserve lots), support spare parts logistics and coordinate the disposal of obsolete or obsolete products.
  • Purchases & Requests Management: Manage the supply of critical packaging material (submissions, purchase orders), investigate customer complaints and resolve supplier billing issues in collaboration with accounts payable.
  • Projects & System Integration: Provide technical expertise for key distribution centre projects, document SAP issues via JIRA, perform validation tests in CAM and liaise with the IT department.
  • Data & Technical Support: Analyze large-scale data reports to optimize the efficiency of the distribution centre, provide technical support for RF scanners, and act as a replacement for MMC (Material Master Creation) and load sheets.
  • Collaboration & versatility: Participate in cross-training of the service by coaching your peers and respond in a shared manner to requests sent to the group's generic email address.

Who you are

  • Diploma of college studies completed in logistics or related field (or equivalent)
  • Between 3 and 5 years of relevant experience in the environment of a distribution centre.
  • This position of serving clients across Canada requires good command of English and French.
  • Strong experience and in-depth knowledge of desired SAP (or control of a similar ERP system covering multiple transactions associated with a distribution centre)
  • Mastery of Microsoft Office, Excel and Tableau; ease with AI.
  • Demonstrated team spirit, great communication capabilities, vivacity of mind, autonomy and orientation towards finding solutions.
