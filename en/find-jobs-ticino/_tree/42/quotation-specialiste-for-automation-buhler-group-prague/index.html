Join Our Dynamic Team - Bridging Automation and Customer Engagement!
Are you a people-person with a passion for automation and the drive to achieve results? If so, we've got an exciting opportunity for you! Aufgaben: • Craft precise price quotations for machine programming hours, electrical switchboards, and commissioning labor estimates. • Collaborate with the sales manager to seal the deal with customers from around the globe. • Work hand in hand with mechanical, automation engineers, and sales specialists on crafting competitive quotations. • Dive into Bühler's impressive portfolio of cutting-edge technologies, primarily in milling and feed production. • Analyze customer business terms & conditions, resolving any obstacles affecting pricing or project timelines. • You'll have the full support of an experienced colleague during your training process. Anforderungen: • Technical secondary education or a bachelor's degree in electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, or industrial automation. • Proficiency in English, both written and verbal - you'll communicate with colleagues worldwide on technical matters. • Experience in fields like process automation, machine commissioning, PLC work, or electrical installations is a valuable advantage. Two billion people eat food every day that was produced with Bühler equipment. One billion people drive vehicles whose parts were manufactured with our machines. Bühler aims to balance humanity, nature, and the economy in every decision as it develops solutions that unlock sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed, and mobility industries. We strive to create innovations for a better world, with a special focus on healthy, safe, and sustainable solutions. Therefore, we team up with customers, start-ups, multinationals, and academia to accelerate impact together.
