Full description

Specialist Building Technology (a) - Focus on Access and Monitoring Systems Our diverse logistics buildings and building technology facilities are monitored and controlled by a central department at the Altishofen site. For the support of the various systems as well as the processing of projects in this area, we are looking for you as a specialist building technology (a) with a focus on access and monitoring systems. Task area

  • Support and further development of access and authorization systems
  • Monitoring, optimisation and assurance of the operation of temperature and monitoring systems
  • Maintenance, management and control of various building technology systems
  • Performing troubleshooting and troubleshooting of technical building installations
  • Planning, coordination and implementation of projects and support for project management

Requirement profile

  • Completed training in building technology, ideally with focus on electrical or access control systems
  • Enthusiasm for building technology and technical connections
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Responsible, reliable and team capable personality
  • Good IT user knowledge is beneficial

Offer

  • Varied task in an innovative logistics environment with Switzerland-wide activity
  • Great own scope for action and autonomous working
  • Design room for technical projects and optimizations
  • Modern infrastructure and collegial working environment
  • Opportunity to develop into a project manager with the perception of the owner's representative for new and renovation projects

Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application. Christoph Jeannerat, HR Business Partner, telephone: +41 62 748 82 03.
