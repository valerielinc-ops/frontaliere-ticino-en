# Assistant with Bachelor or Master degree in Architecture

The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI), competes at the Department of Environment Construction and Design (DACD), for the

Bachelor in Architecture

, a position of

Assistant with Bachelor or Master

with a 100% employment degree. The main office is at the Mendrisio Campus. Starting from 1 February 2027.

Scope and purpose of position

In 3 years of activity at the Bachelor of Architecture, the assistant accompanies students and students in their project, construction and other fields of study. He collaborates and supports the teachers in their preparation, development and tutoring (revisions). It carries out administrative and management activities. Its contribution is central to assisting resources and managing disciplinary tools for basic training. A percentage of his activity is devoted to personal training.

Shipments

In the field of training

  • collaborate with the teachers in preparing documents for basic education activities, supporting studen-tesses and students during the execution of exercises and projects.

In the field of the activities of the degree program

  • collaborate with those responsible for administrative management in the planning of semi-annual activities and the organization of events such as the preparation of exhibitions and the preparation of conferences in the field of basic training.

Requirements

  • Bachelor or Master in Architecture SUP/ETH/USI (or business title) with less than 3 years of professional experience after the end of studies.
  • Improved knowledge of the context of SUPs.
  • Experience and interest in the themes related to the project, construction and sustainability of architecture.
  • Great knowledge of Office programs and proven experiences in the use of CAD programs and preferably also BIM (Revit, Archicad).
  • Group work attitude, with a strong focus on collaboration.
  • Ability to work independently respecting the established deadlines.
  • Availability and flexibility for achieving goals.
  • Good knowledge of Italian language. Knowledge of a second national language and English is a preferential title.

We offer

  • Maximum contract of 3 years.
  • Insertion in a dynamic and constantly changing working environment.
  • Possibility of vocational training.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
