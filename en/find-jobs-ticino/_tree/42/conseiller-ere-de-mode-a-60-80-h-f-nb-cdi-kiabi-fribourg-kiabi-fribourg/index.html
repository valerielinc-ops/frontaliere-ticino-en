Full description

As a Fashion Advisor, you are at the heart of the customer experience and the life of your department. Your main missions are:

  • Welcome, advise and loyalty our customers by offering them a warm and personalized shopping experience.
  • Ensure the daily restocking of goods in order to guarantee the availability of products and an always attractive radius for our customers.
  • Piloting your range in autonomy: ensuring its good standing, its organisation, the quality of merchandising and the presentation of collections in compliance with Kiabi standards.
  • Participate in the reception and processing of the goods, from unpacking to their installation on the surface of sale.
  • Contribute to the store's business performance through your business sense, product knowledge and involvement in business operations.
  • Demonstrate versatility by intervening in different areas of the store and adapting to business priorities.
  • Ensure a safe and pleasant environment, respecting company safety procedures and rules.

At Kiabi, we are looking first and foremost for a dynamic, autonomous, organized person with a true sense of customer service, able to take charge of his/her area while actively contributing to the team's collective success.

What we're looking for in your house

  • Customer commitment and passion for fashion: a high sense of service and active listening to meet needs.
  • Proactivity and adaptability: autonomy, responsiveness and versatility in the face of daily challenges.
  • Strong team spirit: active collaboration, sharing of expertise and collective involvement.
  • Operational mastery: knowledge of omnichannel sales techniques, merchandising, trade tools and ability to interpret trade indicators.
  • The mastery of several languages (French, German, Italian) is a major asset for success in this role.
