Researcher / Researcher in Sustainability Assessment Methods for Industrial Products and Processes The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI) competes in a position as Researcher / Researcher in Sustainability Assessment Methods for Industrial Products and Processes, at the ## Institute of Systems and Technologies for Sustainable Production (ISTePS) of the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI) with headquarters in Lugano. Employment rate of 100%. Entry into service 1 May 2026 or date to be agreed. ## Responsibility and activity

  • It carries out applied research on the issues of environmental and social sustainability of products, processes and production systems.
  • Analyze and model industrial products and processes using Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) methodologies and, where relevant, social evaluation of the life cycle.
  • It contributes to the development, adaptation and application of sustainability assessment methods in industrial contexts.
  • Integrates the results of sustainability analysis into decision-supporting tools and technical reports aimed at different stakeholders.
  • It participates in research activities carried out within national and European projects, contributing to methodological development, the production of results and their validation.
  • Collaborates with the editorial staff of technical deliverables, scientific reports and publications, as well as the presentation of results in national and international contexts.
  • It contributes to the writing of project proposals as a natural extension of its research activity, with particular reference to the definition of the scientific line, methodological approach and impact elements.
  • Supports technology transfer and innovation activities with local companies and organizations.
  • Contributes to training and supervision of bachelor and master students. ## Requirements
  • Master (MSc. or MSE) in Engineering with specialization in Business Engineering, Management Engineering or Industrial Engineering.
  • Solid knowledge of the main regulatory and methodological references in the field of sustainability assessment, such as ISO standards (ISO 14040/44), Greenhouse Gas Protocol (Corporate, Scope 1-2-3), Product Environmental Footprint (PEF), Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR) and related regulatory frameworks.
  • Advanced skills in Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) methodologies applied to industrial products and processes (preferably included the process of processing polymer materials, textiles and construction industry).
  • Documentable experience in the operational use of LCA software (such as openLCA and SimaPro or related), with particular reference to the construction, management and validation of models that use data from Ecoinvent, as well as their integration into structured workflows of analysis and reporting.
  • Ability to translate requirements for modeling and assessing sustainability into clear methodological specifications, facilitating the interaction between those who develop models and those who use the results in decision-making or application contexts.
  • Experience in the drafting of technical deliverables and research project reports in the field of industrial sustainability, with attention to methodological consistency, traceability of data and exposure clarity towards heterogeneous stakeholders.
  • Documented capacity to contribute to the drafting of proposals for research projects, and to the production of scientific and technical contributions in environmental and social sustainability.
  • Ability to coordinate small research groups within funded research projects, maintaining a scientific and collaborative approach.
  • Acts to operate independently within structured design contexts, contributing proactively to the development of research activities and the resolution of methodological issues.
  • Relations and cooperation skills in the national and international sphere.
  • Great knowledge of the English and Italian language; knowledge of a second national language is a preferential title. Preferential requirements
  • Experience in training activities, including educational support and training, supervision or development of training materials. ## What we offer
  • A fixed-time contract lasting up to 3 years, with possibility of renewal.
  • A wage in line with Swiss standards and with the experience of candidate or candidate.
  • A stimulating working environment within a young and dynamic research team, active in applied national and European research projects.
  • The possibility of contributing significantly to the development of research activities of the institute.
  • Professional and scientific growth opportunities in industrial sustainability. ## Applicant Only applications submitted by ##16 April 2026 will be considered through the application form, accompanied by:
  • Curriculum vitae
  • Letter of motivation that includes a brief description of the experience (1-2 pages)
  • Copy of Diplomas and certificates of study
  • Event work certificates
  • List of three references (e-mail name and address) For further information please contact the Prof. Marzio Sorlini. Incomplete applications will not be accepted, sent to different addresses or beyond the agreed deadline. In addition to the documents required for the online application, integration may be necessary with additional documentation, such as the judicial box or a copy of an identity document.
