Department: Emergency Centre

Location: Hirslanden Klinik Linde | Biel / Bienne

Occupation by: by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 57752

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments.

Clinic Linde is the leading private hospital in the Biel-Seeland-Jura area. With its high medical quality, its selected offers and the supra-regional reputation, it is the first choice for patients as well as for highly qualified medical doctors.

YOUR TASKS - EXCELLENCE IN FOCUS

As a senior physician in our interdisciplinary emergency center, you take on a responsible key position. You are part of a dynamic team that cares for more than 13,000 emergency patients every year – both outpatient and inpatient.

  • Independent implementation of emergency medical examinations and interventions, supported by an experienced evidence physician network
  • Active participation in internal training and clinical quality initiatives
  • Engagement in training: You specifically promote our assistant doctors, medical students and nurse practitioners – practical, collegiate and at eye level

Your profile – for a position with effect

You distinguish yourself through medical competence, leadership potential and enthusiasm for interdisciplinary emergency care.

  • Specialist doctor preferred in general internal medicine, surgery, orthopaedics, anesthesia or cardiology - other specialties are also welcome if appropriate
  • Swiss Federal Medical Diploma or MEBEKO-approved degree
  • Specialist title (CH or equivalent)
  • Valid ACLS and/or ATLS provider certificates (mandatory)
  • Additional designation in clinical or preclinical emergency medicine (SGNOR) and training in sonography beneficial
  • Strong teamwork, solution-oriented thinking and resilience in demanding situations
  • Pedagogical skill and joy of teaching responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German and/or French – ideally understanding in the other language

  • Wide range of AHA-certified courses (ACLS/BLS) as well as targeted support for external training (SGAIM, SGNOR)
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation in a modern hospital environment
  • Independent patient care with the support of an experienced medical team
