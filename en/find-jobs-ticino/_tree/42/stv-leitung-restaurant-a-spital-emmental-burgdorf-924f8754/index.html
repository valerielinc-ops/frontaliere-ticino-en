Task area

  • You support the management of the restaurant in the management of the guest company at the locations Burgdorf and Langnau and take over the deputy in their absence
  • You work actively in the restaurant as well as internal and external events and ensure a smooth process
  • You plan the assignments of employees and ensure a needs-based personnel assignment

  • You take guest feedback, derive appropriate measures and get involved in reports, meetings and projects
  • You ensure compliance with food hygiene and our internal quality requirements

Profile

  • You have completed training in gastronomy or hotel industry
  • You bring experience in restaurant operations; a leadership education or first leadership experience is advantageous
  • You are a guest-oriented and communicative personality and enjoy working with different people
  • You act reliably, keep track even in hectic situations and set priorities clearly
  • You convince through expertise, assertiveness and networked thinking
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • You are ready to take on assignments on schedule including weekend and holiday services
