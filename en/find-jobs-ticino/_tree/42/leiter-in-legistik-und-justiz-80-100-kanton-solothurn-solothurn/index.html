Full description

The State Chancellery is the staff office of the Government Council and supports it in the performance of its government and leadership tasks. It ensures the coordination of government business and provides a wide range of services for the Government Council, the Cantonal Council and the cantonal administration. The Department of Legal Affairs and Justice performs a central cross-cutting legal function within the cantonal administration and combines legislative tasks with a wide range of tasks in the judicial field. For the Department of Legislation and Justice, we are looking for a Head of Legislation and Justice, 80-100%, as of 01.06.2027 or by agreement at the Solothurn location.

Your responsibility

  • As head of the department, you lead the department in terms of personnel, expertise and organization and actively develop the department together with your team.
  • In the field of justice, you are responsible for and handle legislative transactions. In the other legislative projects of the departments, you take on the legal quality assurance and advice.
  • They work where legal, political and administrative issues come together. You write statements on political initiatives, co-reports and consultations.
  • They handle versatile and legally demanding transactions and procedures in the areas of justice, state liability, complaints and pardons.
  • Her varied responsibilities also include law and notarial services, legal internships, basic legal training and the right of submission. They represent the canton in specialist committees and promote professional exchange.

Your profile

  • Prerequisites for this function are a completed university degree in law and a lawyer's patent from a Swiss canton.
  • They have several years of legal professional experience, preferably in public administration. You also have in-depth knowledge and practical experience in legislation and legislation. Experience in the field of justice is an advantage.
  • They bring leadership skills, lead with pleasure and promote employees in their professional and personal development.
  • Analytical and conceptual thinking, very good editorial skills and a secure appearance in consulting and communication distinguish you.
  • An independent and solution-oriented working method, high integrity and very good knowledge of German round off your profile.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
