Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech, thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment and maintenance of installations.

As part of the strengthening of our team, we are looking for a CCV maintenance technician to ensure maintenance, troubleshooting and optimization of various technical installations. The position offers considerable autonomy in the organisation of interventions, in a climate of trust, in a team of solidarity where mutual assistance and collaboration are part of everyday life.

CCV Maintenance Technician 100%

Mission

  • Assume maintenance, maintenance, troubleshooting and installations for heating, ventilation and air conditioning.
  • Planning, organizing, implementing and monitoring work under mandates
  • Complete service orders, monitor them for quick and understandable billing by the customer
  • Ensure quality work and the smooth running of interventions, in accordance with existing standards and deadlines.
  • Identify client needs and advisor

Profile

  • Electrician mechanic CFC or equivalent training
  • Good knowledge in the fields of CCV and energy. ITTO 15, an asset
  • Experience confirmed in a similar position and/or electrical, mechanical, hydraulic (water) and pneumatic troubleshooting
  • Knowledge of basic computer tools (Suite Office)
  • French mother tongue. Knowledge in German and/or English is an advantage
  • Autonomy, proactivity, good stress management skills, sense of responsibility and organization, as well as priorities, strong team spirit

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate "Together, we are moving towards success"
