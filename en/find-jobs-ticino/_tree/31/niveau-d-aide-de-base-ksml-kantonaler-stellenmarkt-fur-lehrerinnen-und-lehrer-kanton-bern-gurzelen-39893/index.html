Full description

Portrait The school Gurzelen is a small, family school. The almost 80 children are taught in 4 classes. We have two basic levels and at the primary level two mixed classes (3rd/4th class and 5th/6th class). High school students visit the High School Center in Wattenwil or Seftigen.

Tasks

They work at the start of the school year on Wednesday from 8.20 a.m. to 9.50 a.m. and on Friday from 10.20 a.m. to 11.50 a.m. in the basic level. They supervise and support children in groups or individual work. They ensure supervision in different rooms. They help with everyday and not directly teaching-relevant actions.

Requirements

They are open to constructive cooperation and enjoy working with children.

We offer

  • 30 working hours as a class help
  • Pleasant working atmosphere in a small team
  • Manageable rural school
