Internship – AI Software Engineer - AI-Powered Non-Regression Test Case Generation

Join the Securities Settlement Technology team in Geneva and help redefine how critical financial systems are tested and validated.

Our team develops and maintains the applications that support securities settlement and transfer operations. These systems process business-critical transactions every day and must maintain the highest levels of reliability and accuracy as they evolve.

As application complexity grows, ensuring comprehensive regression coverage becomes increasingly challenging. This internship explores how Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Models (LLMs) can help automatically generate meaningful non-regression test scenarios by analyzing source code, business specifications, and application behavior.

This is a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of AI, software engineering, and financial market infrastructure, contributing to the reliability and evolution of mission-critical systems.

YOUR ROLE

As an AI Software Engineer Intern, you will help design and build intelligent capabilities that improve how settlement applications are tested and validated.

You will

  • Develop AI-powered solutions capable of analyzing application code and functional specifications to generate structured non-regression test scenarios.
  • Explore LLM-based approaches for code understanding, business-rule extraction, and automated test design.
  • Analyze Java and legacy applications supporting securities settlement activities.
  • Identify critical business workflows and ensure they are adequately covered by automated testing assets.
  • Build prototypes combining AI techniques with software engineering best practices.
  • Improve test coverage visibility through automated reporting and quality metrics.
  • Integrate generated outputs into existing testing frameworks and development workflows.
  • Collaborate with software engineers and architects to evaluate the effectiveness and scalability of AI-driven testing approaches.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Master's degree student in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
  • Strong foundations in software engineering, algorithms, and object-oriented design.
  • Good programming skills in Java or a comparable language.
  • Knowledge of LLMs, Generative AI, or AI development frameworks.
  • Understanding of distributed systems and backend application development.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Ability to navigate and understand large existing codebases.
  • Interest in financial systems and enterprise-scale applications.
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively within a team.
  • Curiosity, autonomy, and a passion for learning.
  • Residence in Switzerland or willingness to relocate

WHAT YOU WILL GAIN

  • Combination of AI and software engineering.
  • Exposure to enterprise testing ecosystems.
  • Opportunity to improve software quality at scale.
  • Collaboration with experienced developers and QA specialists.
