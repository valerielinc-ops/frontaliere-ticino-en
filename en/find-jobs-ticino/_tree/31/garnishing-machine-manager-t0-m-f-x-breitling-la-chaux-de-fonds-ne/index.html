Full description

We are a committed Squad whose mission is to redefine chronometrics, rooted in our legacy since 1884. Driven by innovation and precision, we create watches that combine a neo-retro design with casual, accessible and durable luxury — designed and designed by passionate teams around the world. Join our #Squad in La Chaux-de-Fonds as Manager Machines Garnishing T0 (m/f/x) and be part of this extraordinary mission! We are currently looking for our next colleague to complete our #Squad! You will be attached to the site of La Chaux-de-Fonds. It's more than just a Manager Machines position, it's a position where you can put all your knowledge to participate actively in improving and developing the business.

Your contribution: Production management

  • You drive and optimize production activity to achieve safety, quality, cost and timeliness goals.
  • You anticipate hazards, coordinate actions with support functions and ensure effective communication.
  • You follow the KPI of the workshop and implement the necessary action plans.
  • You animate the rituals and tools for managing performance. Quality & continuous improvement
  • You guarantee the application of quality standards and drive problem solving via the QRQC.
  • You identify and implement improvements to enhance the performance of processes and equipment.
  • You supervise the first level maintenance and ensure the reliability of the equipment.
  • You guarantee the application of working standards and lead the 5S approach in the workshop.
  • You are contributing to improvement projects and the introduction of new equipment and tools. Team management
  • You accompany, develop and unite your teams on a daily basis with leadership and exemplaryness.
  • You set goals, track performance and give regular and constructive feedback.
  • You conduct performance interviews and identify skills development needs.
  • You empower your employees and promote autonomy, cooperation and commitment.
  • You manage managerial situations with fairness and discernment. You think there's even more to find out about this job? You're right! Apply today to learn more! You will integrate perfectly with our team if:
  • You have 5 years of minimum experience in industrial environment, including 3 years in team management.
  • You are a graduate of engineering or equivalent training in the industrial field, with experience in project management.
  • You have mastered production management, associated steering tools and Lean Manufacturing principles.
  • You master the budget process as well as the common IT tools (MS Office, Teams).
  • You are comfortable communicating with internal and external interlocutors at different levels.
  • You are a pragmatic, results-oriented leader, able to federate and take initiatives.
  • You have excellent analytical, synthesis and anticipation capabilities and are a powerful proposition.
  • You can manage priorities, adapt to a demanding environment and collaborate effectively with multidisciplinary teams.
  • You are recognized for your reliability, commitment and collective sense, in accordance with our values.
  • You master French, both oral and written; knowledge of English is an asset. We invite you to apply even if you do not meet all of these criteria.
