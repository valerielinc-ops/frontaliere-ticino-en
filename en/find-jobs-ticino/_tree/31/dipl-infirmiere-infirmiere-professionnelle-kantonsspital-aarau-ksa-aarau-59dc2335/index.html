Full description

Ambulatorium angiology

Job: From 1 January 2027 or by agreement, for an indefinite period

Tasks

  • Carrying out angiological precautionary examinations, such as Doppler pressure measurements
  • Care of patients in the wound consultation session in cooperation with our wound experts
  • Co-responsibility for the coordination and organisation of the daily programme
  • Implementation of medically prescribed measures such as blood sampling, ECG, compression and infusion therapies
  • Assistance in interventional procedures, including endovenous laser ablations, miniphlebectomies and sclerotherapies
  • Close cooperation with the medical and nursing team of the vascular center

Requirements

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nursing professional DN II/HF
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in an acute hospital
  • Experience in wound care
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work
  • Resilience, flexibility and organisational talent
  • Motivation for professional development
  • Willingness to actively shape change processes
  • Good team and communication skills
  • Basic PC knowledge
  • Readiness for operations from Monday to Friday in shift operations between 07.00 and 17.30
