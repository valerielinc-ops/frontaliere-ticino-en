Full description

At Pfister, we share a vision: we want to move to every home in Switzerland with our products. To this end, we are looking for collaborators to actively support us. Help us bring the 40,000 products of our assortment into the home of our customers. Whether in one of our 18 branches or at the Suhr plant, a unique framework awaits, with attractive products, not to mention esteem and respect for your commitment. Today, nearly 1300 people work at Pfister: do you want to join them? For the start of learning in 2027 we are looking for one/one direction: furnishing -3- years of learning Your powers

  • During a 3 year training period, you acquire the necessary knowledge to advise our customers in a careful and competent way in the field of furnishing
  • In diverse departments, specialists will teach you in a targeted way the techniques, know-how and professional skills related to contact with our clients.
  • In addition, you acquire solid knowledge in the field of our assortment of furniture, this being the indispensable basis for an informed advice and guarantee of success

What you bring

  • You attend high school or borough school and get good grades

· In addition, you are an open and engaging person who communicates easily with others.

  • You like working as a team and are willing to benefit from the wise advice of your colleagues with extensive experience
  • With passion, you take advantage of your flair for shapes and colours that allows you to create spaces with evocative and expressive style
