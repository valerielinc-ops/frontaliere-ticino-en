Position Overview

Who we are Novelis is driven by the desire to shape together a sustainable world. As a world leader in innovative products and services and the world's largest aluminium recycler, we work in partnership with customers in the automotive, aeronautics, cans and specialty sectors to provide solutions that improve the benefits of light aluminum. We have a coordinated network of technically sophisticated rolling and recycling operations at 29 sites in 9 countries in North America, South America, Europe and Asia, with 12,750 employees worldwide. The county seat is Atlanta, United States. Our customers include some of the most important and well-known brands in the world, including Coca-Cola, BMW, Jaguar Land Rover, Airbus, Boeing, Nespresso, Samsung, and others. In addition, Novelis is leading the industry through its sustainability and recycling efforts. Participate in our mission to create a sustainable world and start working on our site in Sierre, Switzerland as soon as possible Maintenance engineer Pool E 2 MRA (m/f/d)

Responsibility & Qualifications

Your responsibilities

  • Participate in the improvement, commissioning and maintenance of our production tools and machines.
  • To provide troubleshooting services for machinery and plant installations.
  • Perform technical maintenance, assembly and quality control work on internal services installations and equipment.
  • Make mechanical parts and mechanically welded parts.
  • Identify problems and propose solutions, individually and in teams.
  • Provide documentation of maintenance activities.
  • Work with a turn of 4 teams.

How to convince us

  • You benefit from experience in industrial maintenance and/or similar industry.
  • You have a CFC of polymers or manufacturers of industrial appliances with good mechanical skills.
  • You have good knowledge of SAP.
  • You have a great sense of team spirit, are autonomous and oriented solution.
  • You speak French, another language is an asset.
  • You are comfortable in a complex environment and able to adapt to changes.
  • You are accurate, rigorous and meticulous.

Please apply until November 20, 2026.

Location Profile

Novelis的 Sierre is located in the Valais Switzerland employing around 550 people. It mainly focuses on production of aluminium sheet for the automotive market covering the entire process from casting to finishing. The plant also hosts a world-leaving Research & Innovation Center specialized in the development of new automotive sheet alloys surface treatment and joining technologies. In February 2022 together with research institutions and a local energy provider Novelis founded the Net Zero Lab Valais to develop carbon-neutral solutions for aluminum manufacturing. The lab focuses on identifying and implementing innovative solutions to neutralize the carbon footprint of Novelis The Sierre plant supports its community through direct sponsorship of many annual events promoting education sports and culture.
