Full description

Live and work in the Destination Davos Klosters? We look forward to reinforcement! ##STR1##

With your commercial knowledge, your language skills and your calm manner, you ensure a smooth process in the patient administration and a forward-looking admission of our emergency patients. In addition, you support our financial team with your figures and ensure that we can bill our services correctly and on time.

As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable company, but important for the region, we maintain a collegial working atmosphere and appreciate the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos.

By agreement we are looking for a:n

Personnel: in patient and performance management 50-100% ##

Your responsibilities

Patient management

  • Admission of planned patient and emergency entrances and coordination with the appropriate offices
  • Collection and maintenance of patient master data in the systems
  • Obtaining the cost-benefit languages, checking the insurance covers, monitoring the cost-benefit languages (including requesting custodial payments)
  • Treatment of insurance rejections and complaints
  • Preparation of evaluations and cooperation/support in quality management
  • Further administrative activities and insights about the patient journey at Davos Hospital

Performance management

  • Responsibility for the correct representation and application of ambulatory and stationary tariffs and services
  • Ensuring full power measurement
  • Preparation of ambulatory and inpatient patient bills

Your profile

  • Completed commercial training with experience in health care or a degree as a medical assistant:in
  • In-depth knowledge of the Swiss tariff system (Tarmed, TarDoc, DRG)
  • Careful, reliable and independent work and pleasure in patient contact
  • Experience in hospital or health insurance is a very big plus
  • Practical use of computer tools
  • Very good knowledge of German and English orally and in writing, every other language is a plus
  • Readiness for irregular weekend assignments
  • Willingness to familiarize oneself with a challenging subject area and to demonstrate willingness to persevere
  • Team player, engaging, helpful, responsible and performance-oriented

Our offer

  • Varied and responsible task in a collegial team
  • Attractive employment conditions and benefits; annual working hours
  • Hospital-owned nursery
  • Interesting internal training and further education opportunities
  • Plenty of sports and leisure activities in the highest city in Europe

For further information, Ms. Andrea Dönz (Head of Patient & Service Management) is at your disposal and our recruitment team is at your disposal by telephone or email.
