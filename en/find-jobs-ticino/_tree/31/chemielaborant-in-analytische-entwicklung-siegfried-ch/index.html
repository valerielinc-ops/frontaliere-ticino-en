Full description

"Expect to grow" , personally and professionally: At Siegfried you can expect exciting challenges and numerous opportunities to prove your talents. Your Role

In the chemical laboratory, you play a crucial role in the analytical development projects of our laboratory, which focus on drug active ingredients. They support the team in all development phases, from early development to process maturity.

Your Profiles

  • Responsibility for the analytical development part of customer projects in the area of Drugs Substance – from the early development phase to process maturity
  • Development, optimization and troubleshooting of chromatographic methods (HPLC, UPLC, GC, GC headspace)
  • Implementation, validation and transfer of chromatographic analysis methods
  • Analyzing the pharmaceutical active substances and intermediates, partially cGMP-compliant
  • Support for the maintenance and support of our modern equipment park

Technical strengths

  • Professional experience in a similar function, good HPLC/GC knowledge and interest in chromatographic separation techniques
  • Chemical laboratory training or equivalent training with a focus on analytics
  • Experience with tools like Chromeleon, LIMS, MS-Office, etc.
  • GMP experience is beneficial

Personal strengths

  • Teamwork is important to you in order to develop processes together
  • You are proactive and like to take responsibility
  • You have an exact working method to deliver high-quality results
  • Working independently and organizing project work makes you fun
  • Good English skills are important

Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has grown into a global network with 16 locations on three continents. With a team of over 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to industrial production and manufacture safe medicines for patients worldwide. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few companies that can now combine the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished dosage forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most trusted partner of the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operates manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
