Full description

Do you like to start your day early, stay in the open air and give a smile to the people you meet? Then a training in the delivery (specialization Distribution) is exactly what it does for you!

In the three years of basic training you will acquire the necessary tools to successfully enter the diverse world of logistics.

Your training

  • In the early morning you will split the letters and parcels assigned to you and charge your vehicle for the next round of distribution.
  • Afterwards you will move independently, deliver and take delivery of all kinds and take care of the demands of our customers.
  • You will end your working day by doing several subsequent jobs in your training site.
  • During the training you will receive the support of professionals who will introduce you to the practical part.
  • With the financial support of the Post you will also obtain the license to conduct for motorcycles of category A1 or for motor vehicles of category B and you will learn to drive a forklift.
  • You will complete your extensive logistics training with assignments in different internal functions, use in a warehouse service and individual promotion measures in line with your needs.

Your skills

We are looking for young people who are enthusiastic, positive and motivated to make a difference with their actions and meet the following requirements:

  • you like physical activity and outdoor work
  • you love working with others and you are a reliable, honest and discreet person
  • you are available, before the start of the apprenticeship, to obtain the licence for student/leader student for the licence to conduct A1 or B
  • you have concluded the compulsory school
  • at the beginning of the apprenticeship you have the language level B2 in Italian
