So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

What you do

  • You design and implement marketing and branding measures in the digital and print sector.
  • You participate in sponsorship activities and prepare advertising materials creatively.
  • You support photo shoots and sometimes carry them out independently – from preparation to post-processing.
  • You design and realize visual languages as well as visual worlds that fit the brand identity.
  • You design promotional products and other means of communication.
  • Start in November or by appointment (limited for max. 12 months

What you bring

  • You have completed studies or a comparable education in graphic design
  • You have good knowledge of graphic design software (Adobe Creative Suite)
  • You bring basic knowledge of photography and video, including using camera, light and simple image/video editing
  • You are saddle-proof in dealing with the Windows / PC environment, including secure handling of common office programs
  • You are a learning, communicative, reliable, resilient, service-oriented and humorous personality
