Full description

Portrait honest - committed - strong. The School Ittigen attaches importance to openness and maintains a close exchange between the regular teachers, special teachers, the school social worker and the school management. The magnificent old building with large classrooms in the Rain primary school is very easily accessible by public transport. Information about our school can be found at www.schule-ittigen.ch.

Tasks

  • 10 Lect. IF in cycle 1 (1./2. classes) and 5 bVSA int. (2nd clerk)
  • 2 Lect. IF in cycle 2 (5th/6th class) can be used
  • Healing education support for pupils in need of support
  • Teaching integrative in class, promotion in small groups or accompaniment of individual students.
  • Creation of individual support plans (education and support plans)
  • Close cooperation with teachers in the classroom and joint planning of learning environments.
  • They maintain a solution-oriented parental contact and support the class team.
  • Carrying out diagnostic tests, reporting and EB applications.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation within the framework of the professional mandate

Requirements

You have a Master in Healing Education

  • You are in training or have experience with students with special needs. You are a resource-oriented personality with a positive attitude and high social competence. They are used to independent and self-responsible work and enjoy working with children.

We offer

... a support team of several IF teachers in the same schoolhouse ... a good infrastructure and a workplace in our IF office ... good and fast accessibility by public transport (paper mill Ittigen) or car (parking available) ... the hour planning can be arranged with the class teachers .. they are part of a teacher college at the school location and an MR team for professional exchange
