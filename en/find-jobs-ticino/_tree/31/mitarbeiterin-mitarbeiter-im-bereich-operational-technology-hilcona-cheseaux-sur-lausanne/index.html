Full description

As an OT employee, you ensure the smooth operation and further development of our automated systems. They are deployed on-site, working with engineering, IT and production teams to ensure the performance, security and availability of our OT/IoT systems.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: French
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Chemin du Châtelard 5 1033 Cheseaux-sur-Lausanne

Tasks

  • Integration and maintenance of systems based on Siemens CPUs
  • Participation in the support and life cycle of existing and new facilities
  • Operation and support of on-site OT/IoT systems, including configuration, commissioning, maintenance, level 1/2 support (error correction, diagnosis, remote access), monitoring and tracking of assets, management of backups and contingency plans, and system change and update management.
  • Participation in the integration of automation systems into the structure of Bell Switzerland
  • Participation in automation projects and in the continuous improvement of plants
  • Ensuring the separation and processing of OT components in a dedicated network
  • Participation in the implementation of simplified OT/IoT security measures
  • Ensure the proper operation of labelling systems

Requirements

  • Technical training (EFZ, HF or equivalent) in the fields of automation, electrical engineering, industrial informatics or similar specialisations
  • Practical experience with PLC (ideally Siemens S7 / TIA Portal) and control systems
  • Basic knowledge of industrial networks or IT
  • Experience in the industrial environment (manufacturing, logistics or process industry)
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Ability to act efficiently on the ground and work with different contacts
  • Languages: fluent French and German, English beneficial
  • Reliable, flexible, proactive and stress-resistant personality
