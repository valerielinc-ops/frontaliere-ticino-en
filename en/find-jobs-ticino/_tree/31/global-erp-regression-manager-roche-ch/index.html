The Position As the Global ERP Regression Manager, you are the strategic architect and primary advocate for the safety of our production environment. You are responsible for the end-to-end regression strategy across the DIA and ASPIRE (S/4HANA) landscapes & all ERP in scope. Beyond defining the "law," you play a critical role in Stakeholder Education and Engagement. You will work directly with owners of global SAP templates and local live sites to ensure they understand the value of regression testing, help them identify new scope as the product evolves, and provide transparent, high-level reporting to ensure all stakeholders are aligned before any production release. You will be the guardian for the Implementation, Update and maintenance of the Regression test strategy inline with the overall test framework and strategy. You will be working with a team or internals or externals to help support the regression testing efforts for the entire scope. The aim will be to automate all possible scope of regression testing & hence also working closely with the Test Automation team.
