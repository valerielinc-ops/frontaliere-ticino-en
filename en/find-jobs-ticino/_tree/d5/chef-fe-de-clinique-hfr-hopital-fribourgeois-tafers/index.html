Full description

  • Outpatient care, covering a wide range of medical and surgical treatments and various trauma clinical tables
  • Close collaboration with family physicians in the region and internal and external HFR specialists to ensure optimal patient care
  • Guidance and supervision of medical assistants in training
  • Contribution to post-graduate and continuing training of assistant physicians
  • Contribution to the clinical training of students in human medicine of the University of Fribourg (block course and year to option)
  • A person motivated, engaged and with a good team spirit, who holds the title of FMH specialist in general internal medicine (or about to obtain it), or has equivalent post-graduate training recognised in Switzerland
  • Pedagogical talent coupled with interest in the transmission of knowledge and training of doctors
  • French-speaking and/or German-speaking (mastery of oral and written languages). Good knowledge of the other language (minimum B2 level) is an additional asset.
  • Experience in emergency medicine, as well as in the management of patients with simple trauma or in need of routine surgical care, is desirable
  • Interest in ultrasound diagnosis, ideally with basic (completed) training in abdominal ultrasound and emergency ultrasound
