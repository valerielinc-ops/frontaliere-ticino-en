Scientific Research Associate / Scientific Research Associate in the field of automation and collaborative robotics applied to human-centred production systems”

The University of Applied Sciences and Arts of Southern Switzerland (SUPSI) is inviting applications for a position as Scientific Collaborator / Scientific Collaborator in the field of automation and collaborative robotics applied to human-centred production systems” , at the

Institute of Systems and Technologies for Sustainable Production (ISTePS) of the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI), based in Lugano. Employment rate of 100%. Start date: 1 December 2026 or by agreement.

Responsibilities and activities

  • Devote a significant portion of the work (approximately 50%) to the development of the DemoStART demonstrator, an SPS laboratory platform dedicated to collaborative robotics and human-centred production systems, currently under development and also used in the Bachelor's degree programme in Management Engineering. This work includes designing, building, updating and maintaining the demonstrator, as well as demonstration activities for industrial, academic and institutional partners.
  • Carry out applied research, experimental development and demonstrator development activities in the field of collaborative robotics and automation for human-centred production systems, with particular reference to human-robot collaboration (HRC), human-robot interaction (HRI) and digital twins of production systems, including prototyping, conducting experiments, and collecting and analysing data, based on objectives defined with the team.
  • Contribute to the technical development of European and national research projects, particularly in software development and systems integration, in collaboration with team members and under their supervision.
  • Oversee the integration of sensors, actuators and communication systems between devices.
  • Develop software for the integration, programming and control of robotic and automated systems, particularly in Python and/or C/C++, also using programming and simulation environments such as RoboDK.
  • Contribute to experimentation with vision systems, data acquisition and sensing technologies, as well as the use of tools for the integration and orchestration of systems (e.g. Node-RED or equivalents) and middleware for data transmission (e.g. MQTT).
  • Explore the use of artificial intelligence and human-machine interfaces applied to collaborative robotics and human-centred production systems.
  • Support the dissemination of results through the preparation of scientific articles, conference presentations and technology transfer activities for local companies.
  • Occasionally support teaching activities and the supervision of students (Bachelor's/Master's theses), in line with the needs of the laboratory.

Requirements

  • University education (Master's degree or equivalent) in robotics, mechatronics, automation, electronics, computer science, mechanical engineering, management engineering or related disciplines.
  • Experience in research activities (also gained during a Master's thesis), preferably gained within a university research group or a research and development center.
  • Scientific publications and/or contributions to conferences in fields such as robotics, automation, mechatronics, production systems or related disciplines are considered a preferred qualification.
  • Good programming skills in Python and/or C/C++ and the ability to develop software for the integration and control of robotic and automated systems.
  • Experience or a strong interest in programming industrial and/or collaborative robots and their related programming and simulation environments, such as RoboDK or equivalent tools.
  • Familiarity with some of the technologies underlying the laboratory's activities is considered a preferred qualification: automation and system integration (sensors, actuators, communication between devices), vision and data acquisition systems, orchestration tools and middleware for data transmission (e.g. Node-RED, MQTT), digital twin (demonstrator and robotics activities); machine learning, deep learning, generative AI and agentic systems (applied research); Java, containerization (Docker), relational or NoSQL databases (software development and project activities).
  • Interest in research activities in the field of collaborative robotics, HRC, HRI, robot programming and human-centred production systems.
  • Strong autonomy in learning and using new technologies, including technologies not previously known, and the ability to independently complete the entire experimental cycle: from design and simulation to implementation, programming, integration and validation of a system.
  • Ability to work effectively in small teams and multicultural contexts, completing assigned tasks without the need for continuous supervision. Ability to develop experimental prototypes, with a willingness to use (also learning it quickly) CAD software for designing basic mechanical supports and interfaces, and 3D printing.
  • Excellent knowledge of English and proficiency in a national language. Knowledge of a second national language is considered a preferred qualification.

What we offer

  • A fixed-term contract lasting a maximum of 2 years, with the possibility of renewal.
  • A stimulating work environment in an applied research team active in national and European projects, with access to experimental infrastructure and industrial use cases.
  • The opportunity to contribute to the development of new lines of research and to technology transfer to local companies in the context of Industry 4.0/5.0 and Circular Economy.
  • Opportunities for professional growth through progressively increasing responsibility for project activities, publications and the acquisition of competitive funding.

Application Only applications submitted by

November 3, 2026 via the dedicated application form, accompanied by:

  • Curriculum vitae
  • A cover letter that also includes a brief description of your experience (1–2 pages).
  • Copy of diplomas and educational certificates
  • Any employment certificates
  • A list of three references (name and email address).

For more information, please contact Eng. Bettoni Andrea . Incomplete applications, those sent to different addresses, or those submitted after the deadline will not be accepted. In addition to the documents required for the online application, you may be asked to submit additional documentation, such as a criminal record check or a copy of an identification document.

  • Formazione universitaria (Master o equivalente) in ambito robotica, meccatronica, automazione, elettronica, informatica, ingegneria meccanica, ingegneria gestionale o discipline affini.

  • Esperienza in attività di ricerca (anche maturata durante la tesi di Master), preferibilmente maturata all’interno di un gruppo di ricerca universitario o di un centro di ricerca e sviluppo.

  • Pubblicazioni scientifiche e/o contributi a conferenze in ambiti quali robotica, automazione, meccatronica, sistemi di produzione o discipline affini costituiscono titolo preferenziale.

  • Buone competenze di programmazione in Python e/o C/C++ e capacità di sviluppare software per l'integrazione e il controllo di sistemi robotici e automatizzati.

  • Esperienza o forte interesse nella programmazione di robot industriali e/o collaborativi e dei relativi ambienti di programmazione e simulazione, quali RoboDK o strumenti equivalenti.

  • La familiarità con alcune delle tecnologie alla base delle attività del laboratorio costituisce titolo preferenziale: automazione e integrazione di sistemi (sensori, attuatori, comunicazione tra dispositivi), sistemi di visione e acquisizione dati, strumenti di orchestrazione e middleware per la trasmissione dei dati (ad es. Node-RED, MQTT), digital twin (dimostratore e le attività di robotica); machine learning, deep learning, IA generativa e sistemi agentici (ricerca applicata); Java, containerizzazione (Docker), database relazionali o NoSQL (sviluppo software e attività nei progetti).

  • Interesse per attività di ricerca nell’ambito della robotica collaborativa, HRC, HRI, robot programming e sistemi di produzione human-centred.

  • Spiccata autonomia nell'apprendimento e nell'utilizzo di nuove tecnologie, anche non conosciute in precedenza, e capacità di portare a termine in modo indipendente l'intero ciclo sperimentale: dalla progettazione e simulazione alla realizzazione, programmazione, integrazione e validazione di un sistema.

  • Capacità di lavorare efficacemente in team ristretti e in contesti multiculturali, portando a termine i compiti assegnati senza necessità di supervisione continua. Capacità di realizzare prototipi sperimentali, con disponibilità a utilizzare (anche apprendendolo rapidamente) un software CAD per la progettazione di supporti e interfacce meccaniche di base, e la stampa 3D.

  • Ottima conoscenza della lingua inglese e padronanza di una lingua nazionale. La conoscenza di una seconda lingua nazionale costituisce titolo preferenziale.
