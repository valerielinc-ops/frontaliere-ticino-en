Full description

Mission

Your future playing field

You will become part of our team at the Suhr store, where we get customers excited about sport every day. In a motivated team, we work together to ensure that sport remains accessible to everyone. Your Outdoor sports area is at the heart of our local strategy. Together with your team, you inspire athletes from the region and throughout Switzerland.

And now to your role

Are you looking for a dynamic job where no two days are the same? Do you want to share your passion for sport and offer all customers who enter our store an unforgettable shopping experience? Do you love action, active involvement on the sales floor and genuine team spirit? Then the position as a Sports Advisor in Sales is exactly right for you!

Why?

  • You are right at the heart of the action: Your everyday work takes place 100% on the sales floor. You are the face of Decathlon. Whether you welcome beginners or advise experts, you share your passion and build a connection.
  • You actively shape the retail of tomorrow: You are not content simply to sell a product on the shelf. You offer a sustainable omnichannel experience: digital orders, equipment rental, buyback of old products (Second Life)... You cater to everyone's needs.
  • You have autonomy and influence: You are the person responsible for your sport! You ensure that your department looks appealing, manage your inventory and organize your sales promotions. Your efficiency is directly reflected in your customers' satisfaction.

What you will do every day: Your tasks

  • Omnichannel advice and sales: You welcome your customers like guests. You advise them, close sales (in person or via our digital tools), offer our loyalty programme and handle payments at the checkout.
  • Commercial drive / Merchandising: You place products according to the seasons and merchandising guidelines, take care of the "Facing" so that your department always looks immaculate, and highlight the season's bestsellers and new arrivals.
  • Availability and logistics: You pitch in! From receiving deliveries to stocking the shelves, you ensure that your inventory is accurate and your products are quickly available. You also prepare web orders.
  • Circular economy and services: You are an ambassador for our new models: You manage the buyback of used goods and promote our rental solutions to extend the lifespan of our products.
  • Occasional assignments: Depending on your interests and development, you can take on tasks as a contact person for a specific topic, act as a trainer for your colleagues or even help with shift management at the store.

This job is NOT for you if...

  • You would rather sit quietly behind a screen all day.
  • You don't like approaching others spontaneously.
  • You think that unpacking pallets, stocking shelves and processing goods are "not your problem".
  • You are not prepared to see 10'000 steps on your watch at the end of the day.
  • You are looking for fixed, regular working hours: Here we live to the rhythm of sport and our schedules adapt to the activity.
  • You do not want to work on Saturdays.

Profile

What we expect from you

  • You have excellent interpersonal skills. Customer satisfaction is your compass.
  • You are brave, curious and always eager to learn.
  • You are organized, efficient and know how to set priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • You are a sports-loving and active person: That is the company's DNA!
  • You have a perfect command of German. Good English/French skills are a real plus.
  • You are flexible.

What we offer you

  • A flexible and secure hourly wage contract: As the name suggests, you will be paid by the hour. In other words: The more you work, the higher your salary! To give you security, we guarantee you a monthly minimum in any case (a workload agreed in the contract, varying between 20 % and 50 %). During peak periods, we may offer you additional hours. No need to panic about your planning: We will always let you know at least 48 hours in advance, and this only happens if your availability allows it!
  • Hourly wage: The wage is 25 CHF gross per hour. In addition, there are compensations for public holidays and vacation, as well as the 13. Monthly salary (if you have an open-ended hourly wage contract). On the 15. of the month, you will, based on the hours worked in the previous month (between the 16. of the previous month and the 15. of the current month) paid.
  • Employee shares: Once a year, you can become a shareholder in the company. This is an excellent investment, as the value of the share has never been negative since Decathlon was founded 50 years ago!
  • Passion for sport: We support your sporting activity! After 1 month of service, take advantage of a 25 % discount on your Decathlon purchases.
  • Training: You will take part in training courses to improve your technical knowledge of products, your sales techniques or your interpersonal communication.

Your career: With us, career progression is no myth

You start with us in the store? Are you starting as a salesperson? You can develop into a department manager, take on leadership as an Operations Manager and even become a Store Manager! We have dozens of stories like his.

The application process: How do we get to know each other?

  • Your application: CV + answers to the short questionnaire (no cover letter necessary!).
  • The assessment: A short AssessFirst test to discover your potential and help us better understand who you are "beyond your CV".
  • The interview: A concrete discussion with your future manager to see whether it "matches".
