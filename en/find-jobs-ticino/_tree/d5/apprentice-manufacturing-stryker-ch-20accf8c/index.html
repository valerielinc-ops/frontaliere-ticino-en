Full description

Work Flexibility: Onsite

You are sociable, like to take on responsibility and like independent work? We offer you a challenging training period with a versatile, practical and theoretical training in the workplace. You can expect varied and interesting activities. You will also gain insights into various areas of our company. As a compensation to vocational school and training company, we ensure a good exchange between our trainees with exciting activities.

This is what awaits you

  • In the training, you will learn skills for the manual and mechanical production of workpieces and components from different materials.
  • The focus of your training is the production of high-precision implants by turning, milling and drilling using computer-controlled machine tools (CNC).
  • In addition, in addition to the realization of the components, the operation, setup, programming and maintenance of machines are part of your training content.
  • You go through various production departments on the basis of a tailor-made through plan, where we offer you the opportunity for active participation.
  • In addition to training and the opportunity to participate in interdisciplinary project teams on small location projects, you will receive regular feedback to support your personal development.

This sets you apart

  • You have a good school diploma.
  • You enjoy the subjects of mathematics and physics.
  • A strong interest in technical issues as well as a high degree of care and organizational talent characterize you.
  • You like to work in a team and want to contribute to our success with your skills.
  • Craftsmanship and good spatial imagination round off your profile.

We offer

  • Helpful colleagues and a friendly working atmosphere
  • In-house training workshop with modern machines
  • Intensive exam preparation & varied throughput plan
