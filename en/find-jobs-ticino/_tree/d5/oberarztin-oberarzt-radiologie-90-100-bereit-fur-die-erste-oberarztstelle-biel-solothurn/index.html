Professionally, are you ready for more responsibility, only the appropriate senior medical office is still missing? In this position, you will be carefully introduced to your new role, gradually assume more responsibility and can develop a professional focus in the long term. The Center Hospital provides radiological care for a German- and French-speaking region. Every year, around 51’000 investigations are carried out at two locations. Accordingly, the clinical questions and the exchange with the various specialist areas are versatile. In everyday work, you regularly alternate between sectional image diagnostics, conventional X-ray, ultrasound, mammography, fluoroscopy and angiography. Among other things, three CT devices, a 3-Tesla and a 1.5-Tesla MRI, DSA and EOS are available. A subspecialization is welcome, but is not required. The position is also suitable for young radiologists who first want to deepen their clinical experience after the specialist title and only later set a specialist focus.

Their tasks

  • Independent implementation and diagnosis of the entire diagnostic examination spectrum
  • Assuming medical responsibility in clinical radiological everyday life
  • Discussion of the findings on reports and advice to the assigning departments
  • Participation in internal training and professional presentations
  • Participation in night and weekend services on call

Your Competences

  • Radiology, German or EU-approved
  • Support for recognition in Switzerland by Medimeo
  • Alternatively, you are in the final phase of your specialist training
  • Solid experience in diagnostic radiology and interest in a wide range of examinations
  • Very good knowledge of German and at least basic knowledge of French
  • Responsible and collegial personality with pleasure in clinical cooperation

Their advantages

  • Accompanied transition from the title of specialist to senior medical responsibility
  • Versatile spectrum of investigation without early determination on a subspecialization
  • Extensive technical equipment for diagnostics and angiographic methods
  • Opportunity to deepen existing expertise and build your own focus
  • Long-term development and promotion prospects within the hospital
  • Conditions of employment according to collective employment contract and reduced meals
  • Own-owned daycare centre
  • The region also offers a lot of quality of life outside the hospital: the lake, nature and urban life are only a few minutes apart.

With Medimeo at your side, you will receive personal and discreet support in the recognition of your specialist title, in the application process and in organizational questions relating to your move to Switzerland.
