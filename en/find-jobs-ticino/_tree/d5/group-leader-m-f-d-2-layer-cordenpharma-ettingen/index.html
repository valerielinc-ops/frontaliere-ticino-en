Full description

CordenPharma is one of the leading Contract Development and Manufacturing Organizations (CDMO) and develops and produces pharmaceutical active ingredients, drugs and related packaging services on behalf of its customers as a “full-service” service provider. The group employs around 3,500 people. Our network in Europe, Asia and the US offers flexible and specialized solutions for six technology platforms: Peptides, Lipids & Carbohydrates, Injectables, Highly Potent & Oncology, Small Molecules and Oligonucleotides. We strive for excellence in supporting this network and are committed to delivering the highest quality products for the benefit of patients. Our People Vision: We strive for excellence. We share our passion. Together, we make a difference in patients’ lives. Group Manager Production (m/f/d) 2-layer Ettingen, Basel-Land, Switzerland

  • Ettingen
  • from 1 January 2027
  • Shift work: 2 shifts from Monday to Friday
  • Perpetual

For our pharmaceutical manufacturing company, we are looking for an experienced leader with profound experience in industrial production.

  • Leadership and development of a 12 to 15-member team including administrative tasks (conduct of employee interviews, preparation of shift plan, holiday planning, presence and absence control)
  • Responsibility for the timely and qualitatively correct GMP-compliant execution of production orders
  • Ensuring and controlling compliance with safety, quality, OE and environmental standards
  • Close cooperation with neighbouring areas
  • Support in various projects as well as in the continuous development of production

To successfully master this role, we are looking for the following qualifications:

  • Industrial degree with appropriate training
  • At least 5 years of leadership experience (professional and disciplinary) in the pharmaceutical industry
  • Structured and solution-oriented working method with rapid comprehension
  • High commitment and a good understanding of stable production processes
  • High reliability and high quality awareness
  • Strong organisational skills and coordination skills
  • Strong team skills
  • Very good practical and theoretical understanding of production processes and production facilities
  • very good knowledge of German in word and writing (min. C1)
  • Knowledge of MS Office and SAP, TrackWise and PAS-X benefits

What we offer you Canteen KTG / NBU Premiums are 100% covered by us Above average pension fund services / 70% of contributions are covered by us Paid breaks free parking
