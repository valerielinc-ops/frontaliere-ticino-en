Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us.

The Cantonal Tax Administration is responsible for collecting direct taxes in the Canton of Graubünden insofar as the legislation does not provide for a competence of the Federation or the municipalities. The special tax department, section withholding tax, is responsible for collecting, monitoring and billing the withholding tax.

We are looking for the tax administration Graubünden, department special taxes, section withholding tax, per 1\. March 2027 or, by agreement, a withholding tax.

Your responsibilities

  • Collection, verification and correction of withholding tax statements
  • Clarification and cooperation with employers and municipalities
  • Independent processing of technical correspondence
  • Telephone information and advice to employers and employees of the assigned municipalities and other departments

That is what we want from you.

  • Commercial or equivalent training
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in tax or wage administration
  • Good flair for figures and interest in tax law
  • Willingness to quickly familiarize yourself with the subject of withholding tax
  • Independent, reliable and careful working methods
  • Responsible, discreet and resilient personality
  • Convincing appearance and assertiveness
  • Enjoying contact with employers and employees
  • Open and communicative personality
  • negotiation skills and stylish oral and written expression in German and Italian; very good knowledge of Italian is a prerequisite for the job
