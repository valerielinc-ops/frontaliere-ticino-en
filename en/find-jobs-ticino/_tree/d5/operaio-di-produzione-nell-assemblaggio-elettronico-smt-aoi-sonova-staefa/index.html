Produktionsmitarbeiter Elektronikfertigung (SMT / AOI) — Sonova

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

In this role you are an important part of our electronics and hearing aid manufacturing. With your technical understanding, your precision and your quality awareness you make a decisive contribution to the production of high-quality hearing solutions. In return, we offer you a stable production environment, modern technologies and a wide range of possibilities for professional further development.

!Please note that within the scope of our international recruiting setup, the responsible Recruiter from Germany or Poland can contact you. We ask you to take this into account in the application process when making incoming calls or emails.

Tasks

  • Operation of the systems Surface Mount Technology (SMT)
  • Operation and securing of the automated optical/animal inspection (AOI)
  • Visual control / testing of electronics under the microscope
  • Analysis of faults and first measures for repair in close cooperation with technical interfaces and executives
  • Ensuring a clear flow of information to managers
  • Documentation of orders and correct recording of work and order times
  • Active participation in shop-floor activities and continuous improvement processes
  • Support of other

Divisions if necessary and compliance with quality, safety and work requirements

Requirements

  • Completed school education (Sekundar level II), ideally with technical background;
  • Proven experience in SMT / AOI environment, for example certificate SMD assembly, certificate AOI operation
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in the production environment, preferably on SMT lines and in the AOI operation
  • Experience in electronics or medical technology advantage
  • Very good quality and safety awareness, certificate ICP advantage
  • Structured, reliable and flexible working methods
  • Team ability, willingness to learn and openness to change -

German knowledge at least at level B1 English knowledge of advantage - basic knowledge in the handling of PC systems, ideally experience with SAP - conventional soldering, painting and mounting of components of advantage

We offer As one of the world's leading companies in the field of hearing solutions with headquarters in Switzerland, we place great value on an inclusive and appreciative corporate culture.

We offer you

  • Flexible employment opportunities (80–100%)
  • Structured training and targeted internal and external training
  • Individual development and growth opportunities
  • A modern production environment with short decision-making methods
  • The possibility of taking responsibility and actively shaping processes
