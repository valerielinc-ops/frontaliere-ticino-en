Full description

Junior Project Manager Building Technology (a) Our diverse logistics buildings and building technology facilities are monitored and controlled by a central department at the Altishofen site. For the support of the various systems and the processing of projects in this area, we are looking for you as Junior Project Manager Building Technology (a) to strengthen the team. Task area

  • Planning, coordination and support of building technology projects
  • Representation of building owners for building technology in conversion and new construction projects
  • Maintaining and managing the various building technology systems
  • Determining, testing and implementing optimization potential in building technology systems
  • Supervision of the building technology facilities in close cooperation with the company’s own operating technology
  • Recording and maintenance of plant and building documentation
  • Planning and monitoring of maintenance processes and control and calibration work
  • Monitoring the operational efficiency, value preservation and fuel consumption indicators of the building technology installations

Requirement profile

  • Enthusiasm for building technology
  • Training in building technology (GA, electrical, heating or cooling)
  • Further training as an engineer, technician HF or similar desirable
  • good eye for process contexts, networked, analytical thinking
  • Very good IT user knowledge
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Team players

Offer

  • Professional infrastructure and working methods
  • Innovative and exciting projects
  • Flat hierarchies and collegiality
  • Individual development opportunities
  • Working in interdisciplinary teams
