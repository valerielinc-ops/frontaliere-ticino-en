Full description

Free sub-assistant positions in the field of internal medicine available from September 2026. The Cantonal Hospital Uri is highly committed to a qualified junior in the health care and non-medical professions. With around 80 apprenticeships, we are one of the largest apprenticeship companies in the region. Nestled in the beautiful mountains at the southern end of Lake Vierwaldstätter, the region offers numerous sporting (30 minutes to various skiing destinations) and cultural leisure opportunities as well as extremely good connections to the city regions of Zurich and Lucerne.

  • We offer one- to four-month training places in three different departments. The sub-assistants receive a wide range of training and competent support from the responsible doctors. We attach great importance to a good teaching and learning culture as well as a pleasant working atmosphere and a trusting approach. For the duration of the training, the sub-assistants are treated as equal in their training-related rights and duties towards the fully enrolled students of the Medical Faculty of Zurich. The practical training provided is identical in content to that provided to fully enrolled medical students at the University of Zurich. You appreciate the work in the regional hospital and want an excellent education? In addition, do you have good knowledge of German (level B2 or C1)? We look forward to your application with an application letter, CV, copy of the enrollment certificate and current grade mirror. If you have any questions, we are at your disposal on +41 41 875 51 58.
