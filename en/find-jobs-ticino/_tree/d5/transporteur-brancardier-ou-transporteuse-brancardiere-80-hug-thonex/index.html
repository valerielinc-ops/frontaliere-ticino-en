Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . With nearly 1,500 collaborators, the Department of Rehabilitation and Geriatrics (DRG) is a medical centre focused on the elderly and is spread over 5 sites: Trois-Chêne, Loëx, Bellerive, Beau-Séjour and Joli-Mont. It takes care of acute situations, thanks in particular to the emergencies of the Hospital of the Three Oaks, the specific problems related to age, medical rehabilitation and palliative medicine. Located in the heart of a park on the Belle-Idée estate, the Trois-Chêne Hospital welcomes people whose health requires hospitalization for geriatric care or rehabilitation treatments. The Hospital has a state-of-the-art radiology unit, intermediate care, and an emergency department that can accommodate patients aged 75 and over, out of a vital emergency.

Description of post

Your ease in human relations, empathy and diplomacy allow you to ensure and guarantee all patient transportation services within the Hospital of the Three Oaks, always ensuring the patient's comfort and scrupulously respecting the safety rules. You are a motivated person and you have a good physical condition, the position requiring daily transport of patients, beds and auxiliary means. You are also required or required to transport to the funeral chamber of the site. A keen sense of collaboration and a taste for teamwork are necessary to perform replacements within your team but also, in the near future, to the function of delegate or delegate to auxiliary means.
