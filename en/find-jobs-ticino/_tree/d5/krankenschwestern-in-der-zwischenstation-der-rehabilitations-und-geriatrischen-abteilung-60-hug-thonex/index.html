Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . With nearly 1,500 collaborators, the Department of Rehabilitation and Geriatrics (DRG) is a medical centre focused on the elderly and is spread over 5 sites: Trois-Chêne, Loëx, Bellerive, Beau-Séjour and Joli-Mont. It takes care of acute situations, thanks in particular to the emergencies of the Hospital of the Three Oaks, the specific problems related to age, medical rehabilitation and palliative medicine. Located in the heart of a park on the Belle-Idée estate, the Trois-Chêne Hospital welcomes people whose health requires hospitalization for acute geriatric care or rehabilitation treatments. The hospital has a state-of-the-art radiology unit as well as an emergency department that can accommodate patients 75 years of age and older, without a vital emergency.

Description of post

As a nurse or nurse in the intermediate care unit of the Trois-Chêne Hospital ("unit 13+"), you ensure, autonomously and/or under medical delegation, the overall and personalized care of elderly patients requiring continuous monitoring in view of their acute care situation or requiring intensive therapy (cardioversion). In particular, you take care of acute geriatric patients with specific equipment (arterial catheter, central venous pathway...) and exercise increased surveillance (management of non-invasive ventilation, amines). You associate with this technicality a multidimensional, relational, social and ethical management. You work in an interprofessional team and coordinate patient care. You are also responsible for coaching and teaching new employees and trainees. Finally, you contribute to the development of quality of care and conduct professional thinking based on the analysis of nursing practices.

Qualifications

You benefit from a Bachelor's degree in nursing or an equivalent nursing degree recognized by the Swiss Red Cross. Additional certification training in the care of the elderly is a real asset. Specialized training in intermediate care is required or will be required on-the-job. A two-year hospital experience in an acute care unit is essential. Experience in intermediate care and care of the elderly is strongly recommended. You have a high degree of rigour and a strong sense of organisation and management of priorities, excellent relational skills and a strong interest in the follow-up of elderly patients. You also have perfect coping and stress management capabilities in the face of acute care situations. You know how to be responsive and have or have excellent capacity to analyze clinical situations. Thanks to your team spirit and communication skills, you work in interprofessionality to unite all partners around a common vision to develop care projects for optimal care of patients and their loved ones. You appreciate collaboration and multi-disciplinary team work. An ease with computer tools is necessary to carry out this mission, as well as a very good knowledge of the French language.
