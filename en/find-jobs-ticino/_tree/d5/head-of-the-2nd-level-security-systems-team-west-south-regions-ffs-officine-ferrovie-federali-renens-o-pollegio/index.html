What we offer you.

Direct a team that guarantees second level technical support for internal and external railway safety systems, contributing directly to a safe and reliable rail traffic. Together with specialists and specialists, support the research and resolution of disturbances and maintenance, monitor the state of plants and consolidate technical skills through targeted training and knowledge sharing. In addition, connect projects with your technical skills and actively contribute to the further development of safety systems and related diagnosis and exercise systems. Find our benefits like free AG, discounted travel abroad – even for the family – on our website at the Benefit page. What you can move.

  • As team manager, assume the technical management and management of team 2nd Level Safety systems for the West and South regions. Support your employees and collaborators, develop their skills in a targeted manner and create a motivating work environment.
  • In everyday life, you are responsible for second-level operational support and ensure effective assistance to local teams in case of breakdowns or maintenance issues. In this regard, it attaches great importance to effective fault processing, systematic analysis of causes and lasting improvements.
  • Plans, coordinates and defines the priorities of human resources and operational activities, always taking into account the availability of plants, the safety of exercise and economics.
  • Promote knowledge development and professional exchange within the team and between regions. At the same time, you actively contribute to the development of the 2nd Level and commit yourself to harmonised processes, clearly defined responsibilities and effective working methods.
  • Strictly collaborate with the RMO team (Regione Centro/Est), teams on the territory and internal and external partners. Together with the person who plays the equivalent role in the RMO region, promote collaboration between regions and contribute to a coordinated 2nd Level at national level, based on uniform standards, clearly defined responsibilities and a shared understanding of their respective tasks.

What you bring with you.

  • You stand out for strong relational skills, excellent communication skills and an authentic interest in the management and development of collaborators and collaborators. You can also provide points of reference and establish a climate of trust in periods of change.
  • Thanks to your professional path, you have gained experience in running or have the potential and motivation necessary to assume a management function. Work in a structured and autonomous way, with entrepreneurial spirit.
  • Quickly understand the complex technical and organizational challenges, clearly define priorities and make decisions that are weighted and oriented to solutions, even in challenging situations.
  • You have gained several years of professional experience and have solid technical knowledge in the field of safety systems, in particular regarding the exercise, maintenance and troubleshooting of faults.
  • You have superior technical training (SSS, SUP, university or equivalent), preferably in electrical engineering or automation.
  • Master French and/or Italian and you have a good knowledge of German. You appreciate the collaboration between regions and different language communities and are willing to work regularly in different locations in Switzerland.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
