Full description

What awaits you with us

A creative start into the digital world: In the first year, you will learn the basics of IT at the Vocational Training Center Zurich Altstetten. From the second year onwards, you will be fully involved with us at the SMG in Zurich-Oerlikon and put your knowledge directly into practice - accompanied by trained young talent coaches.

Exciting tasks

  • Participation in projects or independent implementation of tasks
  • Design and create digital content for our platforms
  • Gain experience in the areas of graphics, design, marketing, communication and user experience
  • Creative work on designs and concepts that really make a difference

A supportive environment

You are not alone! You work closely with experienced teams in the areas of product design, marketing & branding and communication (also in the “hybrid work” setting). They support you, give you space for your ideas and help you reach your full potential.

Qualificates

This is what you bring with you

  • Interest in new media, design and communication
  • A flair for communication in German and English and the appropriate notes
  • Curiosity and courage to try something new
  • Have fun working in a team
  • Responsibility, reliability and motivation

About us

SMG Swiss Marketplace Group AG is a pioneering network of online marketplaces and an innovative digital company that simplifies people’s lives with future-oriented products.

SMG Swiss Marketplace Group AG offers customers the best tools to help them make important life decisions. The portfolio includes real estate (ImmoScout24, Homegate, Flatfox, CASASOFT, IAZI), automobiles (AutoScout24, MotoScout24), general marketplaces (Ricardo, tutti, anibis) and finance & insurance (FinanceScout24, moneyland.ch).
