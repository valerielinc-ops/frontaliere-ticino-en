Introduction

For our Commercial Division , we are looking for an SAV (H/F) Terminator whose main mission will be to accurately perform all the termination operations on the components within our modern SAV workshop located in Villaz-St-Pierre.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure the polishing and satining of the entire case as well as the bracelet of the watches returned in SAV, according to the original finish
  • Conduct rigorous self-checking and accurate inspections to ensure uncompromising quality according to defined criteria
  • Conduct washing and cleaning of the various components, thus preserving the brightness and durability of the materials
  • Actively contribute to the continuous improvement of our performance indicators

Profile

  • Terminator CFC in watch or equivalent clothing
  • Confirmed experience on a similar function ideally in a VAS workshop
  • Mastery of a computer system related to the trade
  • Manual skill, precision, thoroughness, finesse and dexterity when performing tasks
  • Good sense of analysis and ability to work independently
  • Customer orientation, listening, openness, rigour and organizational sense
  • Constance, attendance, autonomy, with a strong team spirit, versatility and flexibility

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.
