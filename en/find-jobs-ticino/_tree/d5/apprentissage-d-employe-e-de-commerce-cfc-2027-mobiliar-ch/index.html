Full description

What awaits

As a business apprentice, you will be part of the team of the Jura General Agency (on the Saignelegier site) from your first day. You will discover the insurance sector directly in the field and you will help us in the daily tasks.

What you can do with us

Your training as a business employee in the private insurance sector will be at the heart of your learning. You will go through different services, which will allow you to acquire a global vision of the insurance world and to use your curiosity and open mind to discover daily work, step by step. Of course, you will not be delivered to yourself: a trainer or trainer will accompany you during your training and will help you to familiarize yourself with the various facets of the daily life of an insurance company.

What you bring

  • Certificate of completion of compulsory schooling with good results
  • Reliable working method, willingness to learn
  • Open mind, curious and helpful nature
  • Interest on the insurance industry

Working with us

What is expected: three-year basic training in the insurance sector, with a varied training programme and personalized follow-up. The Jura General Agency is not only an ideal location, but also a motivated and dynamic team that is delighted to accompany you throughout your training. Your place of work is in Saignelegier and your apprenticeship begins on August 1, 2027.

What we offer

Social benefits

Our family allowances are based on the highest contributions from the cantons of Bern, Vaud and Zurich.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We contribute to continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer various discounts on cultural events and events as well as a staff restaurant with reduced prices.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Jura?

EU/EFTA residents living in the border zone of the country adjoining Canton Jura can apply for a G permit; the Swiss employer files it with that canton's migration office after the contract is signed. Border-zone rules and processing times vary by neighbouring country and canton, so confirm the specifics with Jura's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
