Full description

The Solution Team “Customer Relationship” develops and operates with its agile delivery teams the digital solutions for the CRM-PF and the CRM-related components such as the Customer Intelligence System (CIS) and the self-developed integration component (CLIMB) to the CRMPF. You take on the organizational, methodological and personnel responsibility and create optimal working conditions that support the teams in their value contribution. As a line manager of the solution team "Customer Relationship", you are also part of the management team of IT Client Channels & Analytics.

You can do that.

  • Together with the Solution Product Owner and the Solution Architecture Owner, you will form the professional leadership in the trio for the Solution customer relationship as part of our Enterprise Agile Delivery Organization
  • You are responsible for the delivery capability of the entire solution team and optimize throughput, quality and resource use together with the Delivery Team Leads, including BSI’s partner management as a product partner.
  • You solve obstacles within the solution team as well as in cross-divisional cooperation
  • You create optimal working conditions to make the organization more agile and innovative and to promote the leadership of increasingly self-organized teams
  • You live continuous learning and promote systematic development
  • You embody agile values and principles, live an Agile Growth Mindset and actively support Agile Leaders and the organization in the application of agile practices and the further development of the mindset

You bring that with you

  • Tertiary degree in computer science, business informatics and/or business administration
  • Several years of experience in a management position, for example as a line manager in an agile environment, as a Release Train Engineer or Agile Leader
  • Experience in the CRM environment and/or in the integration of standard product suites as an advantage
  • Extensive practical experience in the application and further development of agile practices and frameworks (e.g. SAFe, Scrum, Kanban, DevOps) as well as in agile product and software development
  • Open and profitable communication ability with strong competencies in moderation and conflict resolution
  • In-depth practical experience in team and personnel development as well as organizational development and change management
  • Complete knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in words and writing
