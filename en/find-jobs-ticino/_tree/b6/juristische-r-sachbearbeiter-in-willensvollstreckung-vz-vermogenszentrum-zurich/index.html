Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. With our services, we also set a benchmark in estate planning and settlement. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our estate at our headquarters near Paradeplatz in Zurich.

  • You look after heirs of less complex estates and accompany them until the full distribution of the inheritance, including all legal and administrative tasks.
  • You take over the correspondence and clarify concerns with participating offices, companies and authorities.
  • You coordinate liquidation measures such as valuations and sales.
  • You are a central contact person and are in daily contact with internal and external bodies.
  • Completed commercial training, in the field of notarial/law, trust and/or a BSc in Business Law/Applied Law, CAS/DAS Paralegal or comparable qualification
  • Ideally, several years of professional experience in a similar environment
  • Excellent MS Office knowledge
  • Professional, target group-oriented communication and secure appearance
  • Organizational talent, efficient working methods and high reliability
  • Engaged, service-oriented personality with strong empathy that contributes positively to the team spirit
  • A versatile and exciting field of work with a legal-administrative focus (property/inheritance law with interfaces to pension law, property law, tenancy law, etc.) in a collegial environment with largely independent mandate processing.
  • A thorough on-the-job training by experienced, motivated employees.
  • On our expansion course, we want to maintain our uncomplicated, open culture. If you get involved, this gives you the opportunity to shape and develop a unique company.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
