Full description

As Head of Administration at IWM AG, you ensure that our inspection activities in the area of transport facilities run smoothly administratively. You support the management, coordinate central processes and actively shape cooperation with different departments.

Your field of activity

Your tasks

  • You support the manager in administrative tasks, organize meetings and keep minutes.
  • You coordinate internal processes and support the field service with offers, test documents and reports.
  • You create invoices and maintain databases and quality management documents.
  • You coordinate administrative topics with various departments of BKW Engineering.
  • You translate personnel and technical documents between German and French.
  • You receive customers and support at events.

Your competence

  • You have completed a basic training in the bare-female area.
  • You work independently, reliably and accurately and approach tasks proactively.
  • You organize administrative processes in a structured manner and also keep track of different issues.
  • You communicate confidently, openly respond to different contacts and adapt flexibly to new situations.
  • You have solid IT skills, especially when dealing with office applications and administrative software.
  • You master German and French verbally and in writing at a very high level.
