The Company

Imerys is the world’s leading supplier of mineral-based specialty solutions for the industry with €3.4 billion in revenue and 12,300 employees in 40 countries in 2025. The Group offers high value-added and functional solutions to a wide range of industries and fast-growing markets such as solutions for the energy transition and sustainable construction, as well as natural solutions for consumer goods. Imerys draws on its understanding of applications, technological knowledge, and expertise in material science to deliver solutions which contribute properties essential to customers’ products and their performance. As part of its commitment to responsible development, Imerys promotes environmentally friendly products and processes in addition to supporting its customers in their decarbonization efforts. Imerys is listed on Euronext Paris (France) with the ticker symbol NK.PA.

Your role and responsibilities

Referring directly to Production Manager, you will be responsible for planning, management and optimization of all production activities of the Supply Department, ensuring the transformation of raw materials into high quality finished products. The position provides a working time a day, with a constant and solid presence on the field to lead the operational team, ensuring strict respect for production times and constantly promoting the culture of security, Lean Management and continuous improvement.

Specifically, you will take care of

  • Production Management and Lead Time: Plan and monitor activities on a daily and monthly basis to ensure compliance with established volumes and delivery times, maintaining high quality standards.
  • People Management & Leadership: Driving, motivating and increasing an operating team of about 40 employees (mostly personal shiftworkers), managing shift planning, annual performance assessment interviews and continuous development and training plans, and timely conflict prevention and management within the team to preserve a cohesive and collaborative working climate.
  • Health, Safety and Environment (EHS): Promote and strictly enforce corporate safety and environmental standards.
  • Lean Manufacturing and Continuous Improvement: Continuously apply Lean (5S, Kaizen, TPM, waste reduction) methodologies on cycles and plants, collaborating closely with process engineering for the industrialization of new solutions and operational optimization.
  • Maintenance and Logistics Operation: Coordinately coordinate with the Maintenance Manager for the planning and execution of periodic maintenance of continuous/supply process plants, and with the logistics for the supply and timely discharge of raw materials.

What are we looking for?

  • University education in Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Management Engineering, Mechanical Engineering
  • Solid and proven experience in management/supervision roles of production matured within structured industrial contexts, preferably characterized by continuous thermal processes, furnaces or chemical/material transformation.
  • In-depth practical knowledge of the tools and logic of Lean Management (Kaizen, 5S, TPM, reduction of waste / waste).
  • High sensitivity, rigour and firmness in the management of occupational safety and environmental protection (EHS).
  • Spicy field leadership, authority, relational skills, empathy and proven ability to mediate and effective management of interpersonal conflicts.
  • Strong orientation to the result, proactivity and ability to work effectively under pressure ensuring promptness in operational decisions.
  • Analytical approach and oriented to practical problem solving directly on line.
  • Excellent mastery of the Italian language (madrelingua or fluent) and good knowledge of the English language (speaking and written).

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
