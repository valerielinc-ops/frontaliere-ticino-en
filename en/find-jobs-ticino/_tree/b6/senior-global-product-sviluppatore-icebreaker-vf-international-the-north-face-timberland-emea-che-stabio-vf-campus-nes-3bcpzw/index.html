Full description

icebreaker is looking for an exceptional Senior Product Developer - icebreaker to join our team based in Stabio, Switzerland.

Driven by the belief that nature has the answers, we provide natural performance alternatives to synthetic-based apparel, to create a healthier and more sustainable future for our species and the planet. We're passionate about connecting people to natural alternatives.

Let’s Talk about the Role

As Senior Product Developer - icebreaker , you’ll take ownership of development execution for your assigned products through the go-to-market (GTM) process, from concept to finished goods. You will ensure alignment with the brand strategy, global business needs, sourcing capabilities and quality standards.

This role drives the tactical execution of industry leading product to design intent, collaborating cross functionally with a multi-season time horizon perspective.

As a key contributor to the product creation team, you will execute the end-to-end product development process against critical project timelines and support best practices and process improvements. You will act as a technical coach, sharing expertise and supporting colleagues in solving development challenges. Working collaboratively with cross-functional partners, you will turn product concepts into reality to drive business growth.

How You Will Make a Difference

End-to-End Product Excellence

Collaborate with Designers and Fit and Pattern Specialists to translate creative concepts into finished products. Prepare and review comprehensive product tech packs covering material requirements, sizing, construction details and schematics , incorporating specialist input and providing technical coaching to colleagues. Use performance data to drive product, process and manufacturing improvements across the Product Development function, with an emphasis on continually improving quality and finish.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Be a central communication hub between internal departments (Materials, Innovation, Design, Technical Design, Merchandising, Supply Planning) and external stakeholders (Sourcing, Vendor base & Quality Control). Establish strong working relationships, facilitate transparent communication and collaboration to ensure alignment on product strategies, timelines, and quality benchmarks.

Process & Critical Path Management

Develop and maintain detailed timelines for seasonal and non-seasonal product development. Identify and track critical -path milestones to align cross - functional partners and support the on-time delivery of prototype samples, salesman samples ( SMS ) and bulk production across the relevant categories. Use agile methodologies to coordinate development activities, identify and mitigate risks, and escalate issues that threaten delivery or quality.

Data Management & Reporting

Ensure relevant functional components of the Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) system are meticulously maintained and updated on-time according to the required seasonal inputs/outputs. Uphold standard operating procedures to ensure database integrity and data accuracy across all product lines facilitating seamless, on-time communication between the Product Development, Design, Merchandising, and Sourcing teams.

Stakeholder Management

Build strong sourcing and vendor relationships to ensure timely and accurate execution of sampling and production. Report directly to the Global Head of Product Development on deliverables, risks and improvements.

Skills for Success

Product judgement : Translate relevant market trends in sustainable outdoor performance apparel into practical development recommendations. Apply forward-thinking technical solutions that support icebreaker’s product direction and keep its products at the forefront of the industry.

Values: A commitment to sustainability, ethical production practices, together with a meticulous approach to ensuring high-quality execution in every aspect of product development.

Expertise : Apply in -depth knowledge of garment construction, technical specification creation and sustainable product development to translate creative ideas into scalable products and resolve development challenges. Prepare detailed tech packs , maintain accurate technical documentation and manage PLM data throughout the product lifecycle. Share this expertise through practical coaching and support for colleagues.

Collaboration : Foster a collaborative environment by building strong relationships across all functional teams. Your ability to effectively communicate and coordinate with Merchandisers, Designers, Fit and Pattern Specialists , and external partners will be key to success.

Communication : Excellent at facilitating effective collaboration and negotiation between internal and external stakeholders. Comfortable acting as a liaison between diverse groups, ensuring that project requirements and updates are clearly conveyed and that both technical and non-technical stakeholders are well-informed and aligned on project objectives.

Agility : Demonstrated ability to organise and prioritise, multi-phase product development projects with an understanding of critical path and process management. Flexibility to work and travel in an international context across regions and associated time zones, at times requiring meeting attendance early mornings or evenings.

Professionalism : Exceptional time management and strong interpersonal skills with an emphasis on building a cohesive and inclusive culture of excellence and respect within the team and across all stakeholder relationships. Manage conflict and work through challenges with an objective and calm approach.

Stakeholder Collaboration : Develop and maintain strong relationships with sourcing partners and external vendors. Share regular updates with relevant stakeholders on your progress against the critical path, process improvements, and cross-functional initiatives.

Education & Experience

High level of computer literacy is essential; proficient in Microsoft Office & Adobe CC with a deep understanding of PLM (Flex, Enovia) solutions.

5-7 years of experience in product development and technical roles within the apparel industry . Experience in the outdoor performance or sustainable apparel sector is highly preferred.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
