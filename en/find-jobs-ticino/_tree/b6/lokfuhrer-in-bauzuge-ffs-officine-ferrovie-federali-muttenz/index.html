Train driver: in construction trains 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Train driver:in construction trains Muttenz, 01.02.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

At SBB you have the chance to contribute your skills and at the same time acquire new skills that enrich you professionally and further. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid for, for example, an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits and more information about the job profile can be found here. You can move that.

  • As a train driver or train driver for construction trains, you make a big contribution to ensuring the renewal of the routes (steel, gravel, etc.).
  • You independently perform and perform various and complex maneuvering movements of trains up to a maximum of 100 km/h, form and disassemble them and prepare them for the journey.
  • In order to ensure regional logistics, you design, apply for and order driving regulations on time and are responsible for supplying construction sites with the necessary materials.
  • You have a high responsibility in the operational safety of the construction trains. Checking job security and creating reports round off the activities.
  • In addition, you will be used as a train driver on the construction site and your craftsmanship will be required.

You bring that with you.

  • You like both working in a team and doing tasks independently. You are reliable and characterized by the high flexibility in everyday work.
  • Irregular working hours, including night and weekend work, are part of your daily work. You are physically fit and enjoy working outdoors.
  • You have completed training as a train driver in VTE category B100 and have experience in track construction.
  • You are characterized by a strong awareness of security and keep calm and overview even in difficult and hectic situations.
  • Flowing knowledge of German (level C1) in word and writing is a prerequisite.
  • You have the driving licence category B.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This position is in requirement level F, Regional Allowance Level 1.
