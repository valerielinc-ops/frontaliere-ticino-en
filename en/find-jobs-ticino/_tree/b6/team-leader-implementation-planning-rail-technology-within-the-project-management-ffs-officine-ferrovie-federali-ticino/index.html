Team Leader Implementation Planning - Rail technology within the project management Apply » Date: 16 Nov 2026

Place

Zürich, CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. The Eastern Region Project Management is responsible for planning and implementing complex and multidisciplinary railway infrastructure projects, with a total volume of around 300 million francs per year. Put your talent at the service of a motivated and ambitious management team and contribute directly to the development of the transport infrastructure in Switzerland. As part of this exciting function, you lead a team of implementation project managers who lead complex and multidisciplinary railway infrastructure projects, in collaboration with different specialized fields and throughout the different phases of the projects. What you can move.

  • As a team leader, you lead and accompany a team of 10 to 12 project managers, mainly active in the field of railway technology as well as in the coordination of activities on construction sites. In this context, you rely on the values and principles of SBB conduct.
  • You like to organize and optimize working processes. You also encourage professional development, knowledge transfer, collaboration and a culture of performance within your team, as well as with your local counterparts.
  • With your team, you ensure the success of the projects entrusted to you. You take responsibility for all the project processes, up to completion and commissioning, in close collaboration with project managers, in-house railway specialists and construction companies.
  • You are a member of the Eastern Region Project Management Management Team and actively contribute to the achievement of the region's objectives. You encourage and support the development of processes, project management and digitization.
  • You take the lead in cross-cutting development themes and actively promote networking between different regions and specialized fields.

What you bring.

  • Thanks to your excellent social skills and communication skills, you motivate your employees to achieve the goals set and ensure their performance.
  • Your conceptual abilities and enthusiasm allow you to contribute constructively to the development of the region and the project management unit.
  • You have a real talent for driving and coaching employees in a demanding environment.
  • You have several years of experience as a project manager or specialist engineer in the field of railway technology and have ideally already acquired first experience in driving or followed corresponding training.
  • You have a university degree or a specialised high school with a specialisation in construction, economics or transport. You have also completed further training in business management or technology to ideally complete your profile.
  • You are fluent in oral and written German (minimum level C1) and preferably have good knowledge of French or Italian.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
