The Position The organization “Site Network Basel/Kaiseraugst”, part of the global group function “Corporate Strategy, Sustainability & Sites”, provides the infrastructure for the entire site and continuously develops it in an innovative way. We work with all stakeholders along the entire value chain, from early research to patient care. Within the organization “Site Network Basel/Kaiseraugst” we design reliable and sustainable solutions in the SHE Circle of Site Operations.

The possibility As a safety expert: in the laboratory and production environment Every day you help to make our location even safer. You ...

  • are independently and independently on the road in our buildings at night and on weekends / holidays and carry out control tours in the laboratory and production buildings.
  • Responsible for handling customer-specific orders in this environment.
  • In this role, you work closely with customers and other event organisations (e.g. alarm centre and fire brigade).
  • have an affinity for technical plants and can inspire you for the topic of digitalization.
  • You like to work in a small and dynamic team, which must be well coordinated.

Who you are

  • You have completed an apprenticeship (preferably to Chemikant:in, Laborator:in EFZ specialization Chemistry, Electro-Fitter:in or similar) with at least 3 years of professional experience in one of these professions.
  • You have the driver’s license Cat. B and can therefore also be used at the Kaiseraugst location.
  • You have proven professional experience in the pharmaceutical/chemical environment (production and laboratory).
  • You work in your field of work absolutely reliable, exact and conscientious.
  • You are used to being self-employed and alone at night, and shift work (mostly at night) and weekend work (day and night services also on holidays) do not matter to you.
  • You are flexible and ready for action, even short-term special missions do not present you with problems.
  • You have a good physical constitution, day trips of 15 km with many stairs at night are not a problem for you.
  • You can communicate and express yourself very well in the German language orally and in writing.
  • You have an impeccable reputation and a current criminal record without entries.
