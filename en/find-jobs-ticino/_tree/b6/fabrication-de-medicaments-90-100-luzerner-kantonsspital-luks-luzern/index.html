Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen sites is a company of the LUKS Group and is one of Switzerland's leading hospital groups. The more than 8500 employees are there with cordiality, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality. For our team we are looking for reinforcement by appointment or by appointment. Tasks:

  • In this position, you work in a GMP-regulated environment and in modern cleanrooms produce formula drugs for our patients as well as for partner hospitals. The spectrum ranges from non-sterile to sterile drug forms; in the production of cytostatics a production robot from Loccioni is also used
  • In addition, you work closely with laboratory technicians as well as pharmacists from the pharmaceutical manufacturing sector. Within the cytostatic production and the GMP production of small quantities, organize the daily business and take over plausibility checks as well as the release of regulations
  • In addition, your tasks include the interdisciplinary advice of doctors, doctors and nursing professionals in pharmaceutical matters, as well as the participation in ensuring the high quality standards of pharmacy.
  • Here you will create manufacturing regulations, work instructions and documents for qualifications and process validations and also participate in the piquetting service of the pharmacy Requirements:
  • For this position, you will bring a completed pharmacy study and ideally have expertise and experience in the production of pharmaceuticals in the GMP environment. We also offer dedicated professional starters a thorough introduction
  • Personally convince you with an independent, reliable and flexible way of working and appreciate interdisciplinary cooperation. Even in demanding situations, you remain resilient and enjoy taking responsibility
  • In addition, you are characterized by motivation, team skills and commitment and bring your communicative skills to the daily work
  • Good computer skills perfectly complete your profile We offer:
  • In this position, you work in newly acquired cleanrooms of cytostatic production, which are centrally located in the treatment plant of the new building oncology/haematology within the certified tumor center.
  • In addition, we are happy to support your further training in oncological pharmacy for pharmacists (DGOP); with the clinic information tool EPIC and the production software CATO®, you can use modern information tools for your everyday work.
  • In addition, you have the opportunity to contribute to the development of special recipes in new product requests
  • In addition, you can look forward to regulated working hours and an extensive internal training programme, benefited employee offers and attractive additional services
