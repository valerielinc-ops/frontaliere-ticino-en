Full description

The Vaudoise is one of the leading Swiss insurance companies and an attractive and future-oriented employer. In the spirit of its cooperative identity and values, the Vaudoise creates a healthy, inclusive and inspiring working environment in which you can develop further.

Tasks The various tasks that await you

  • Incoming post sorting and sorting
  • Self-employed and proactive handling of cases until completion, including: opening of claims files, checking the facts, requesting additional documents or supporting documents in case of incompleteness, checking insurance cover and calculation of the amount of the services due and finally granting of the payment order
  • answering customer requests (written and oral)
  • contact with our trusted veterinarian and veterinary practices or clinics

Requirements We are looking for an EFZ person as an veterinary and practical assistant and native German language knowledge. If you can also think independently, dynamically and analytically and feel comfortable with priorities and deadlines, then you are the right person for us!

We offer you

  • A pleasant working environment in a company that has been awarded the label "Best employer,
  • Various social benefits such as high contributions to the 2nd and 3rd pillar
  • Discounts on all Vaudoise insurances
  • 5 weeks holidays, 13 monthly wages as well as a bonus
  • Flexible working hours
  • The possibility to work in the home office. (max. 1-2 days per week)
  • A job in a young and dynamic team

Contact Do not hesitate any longer and apply! We look forward to receiving your complete digital application documents (with CV, motivation letters, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
