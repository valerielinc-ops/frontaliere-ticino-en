Automotive maintenance engineer CFC back to list To strengthen our teams, we are looking for an experienced CFC car maintenance engineer. Job description

Your mission

  • Perform maintenance, revision and repair work on light vehicles (mechanical, electrical, on-board electronics)
  • Diagnosing faults using electronic diagnostic tools and proposing suitable solutions
  • Perform technical checks (brakes, steering, suspension, exhaust, air conditioning, etc.)
  • Perform drains, tire changes, wear parts replacements
  • Conduct the expertise and prepare the vehicles for the official technical inspection
  • Document interventions in the workshop management system
  • Advise customers on vehicle condition and necessary repairs
  • Ensure compliance with safety standards, as well as maintenance of workshop equipment and tools

Profile

Profile desired

  • Motor Vehicle Maintenance Engineer CFC (or equivalent)
  • Several years of experience confirmed in a similar position
  • Excellent mastery of electronic diagnostics and modern automotive systems
  • Ability to mentor or transfer know-how to more junior profiles, an asset
  • Autonomy, rigor and sense of responsibility
  • Good communication capabilities for customer advice
  • Driver's licence category B essential
