Full description

Are you ready for a key role where you don't just manage numbers, but also shape the digital financial architecture of an entire group? At Hügli in Steinach, which is part of the bell Food Group, a task awaits you that combines strategic vision in Controlling with technical SAP expertise. As a Master Key User, you are the central pivot to take our financial processes to the next level across national borders. ##

  • Responsibilities
  • Process architecture: you design and standardize processes in SAP FI/CO modules across the group and ensure that our primary system is optimally aligned with the needs of local companies and departments
  • Standardization: developing global guidelines and best practices to ensure maximum transparency and efficiency across the group
  • Business partnering: as a central point of contact, you build the bridge between departments, IT, and local finance teams
  • Support & training: you enable local key users through targeted training (DE/EN) and act as a competent 1st level support for complex issues
  • Closing & planning: the system-based support of monthly closings and the mapping of budget processes in SAP are part of your area of responsibility
  • Project management: exciting rollouts and integration of non-SAP units in countries such as UK, Netherlands or Italy and the development of modern analysis options in SAP and BI (e.g. profitability analyses or CAPEX reporting) shape your agenda for the coming years ##
  • Requirements
  • Controlling expertise: you have years of experience in Controlling and see yourself as a true Business Partner who not only reads numbers but also derives recommendations for management
  • SAP expertise: sound knowledge in SAP FI/CO is your foundation to drive extensions and optimizations safely
  • Analytical sharpness: penetrating complex business processes and translating them into smart system solutions is what you enjoy
  • Communication strength: thanks to your winning style, it's easy for you to convey knowledge and reach stakeholders at various levels
  • International spirit: since we operate globally, negotiable English skills and joy in intercultural collaboration are self-evident for you
  • Hands-on mentality: you bring the necessary willingness to change to introduce new standards and anchor them sustainably in the company
  • Project experience: ideally, you have already accompanied SAP introductions in an international environment and have solid project management skills
