About us

MediPersonal is a modern, growing recruitment consulting company that provides new impetus on the Swiss market with innovative concepts and a future-oriented way of working. Our location in Wallisellen is characterized by a state-of-the-art working environment, first-class digital tools and flat hierarchies. We see ourselves as strategic partners of our customers and accompany them with tailor-made solutions in recruitment. To strengthen our team, we are looking for an experienced personnel consultant & recruiter with a focus on medicine.

Their tasks

  • Recruitment of medical professionals:

They take over the entire recruiting process from profile definition and direct approach to the successful placement of doctors, nurses and other medical specialists.

  • Advice and support of customers:

Through your market knowledge and your sense of the needs of your clients, you advise clinics, hospitals, health centers and practices professionally and develop individual solutions.

  • Building and maintaining a first-class candidate network:

You use your network, social media channels and job portals to find top candidates for challenging vacancies.

  • Market monitoring and innovation promotion:

They actively monitor industry trends, identify new recruitment channels and use modern tools to continuously develop our services.

  • Quality and customer orientation:

They maintain long-term, trusting relationships with customers and candidates, ensure a smooth mediation process and attach great importance to an excellent candidate experience.

Your profile

  • Experience in recruitment or placement:

Several years of professional experience in recruiting, ideally with a focus on medicine or healthcare.

  • Expertise & industry understanding:

A good sense of the peculiarities of the medical job market, including knowledge of relevant professional groups and qualification requirements.

  • Communicative Competence & Empathy:

Strong consulting, communication and negotiation skills as well as a professional appearance. They understand how to recognize and communicate the needs of both sides (customers & candidates).

  • High initiative & quality awareness:

Entrepreneurial thinking, self-employment and a structured way of working distinguish you.

  • Language skills:

German at native language level; further language skills, in particular English or French, are advantageous.
