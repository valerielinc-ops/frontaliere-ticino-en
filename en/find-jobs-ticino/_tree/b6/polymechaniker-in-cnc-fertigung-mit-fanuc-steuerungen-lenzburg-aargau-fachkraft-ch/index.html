Full description

The activity includes the independent programming and setup of CNC lathes. The company attaches great importance to the mass maintenance and quality of the manufactured workpieces. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • CNC lathes independently program and equip
  • produce workpieces according to process and quality specifications
  • monitor ongoing production and adjust settings
  • check dimensional accuracy and quality of the parts
  • Requirements
  • Teaching as a polymechanic or polymechanic or equivalent qualification
  • Practice on CNC long lathes in daily use
  • Know-how in programming with Fanuc controls
  • Self-employed, accurate and clearly structured working methods
  • High operational readiness and a strong sense of responsibility Offer
  • Performance-based remuneration
  • Daily assignments with flexible flextime
  • At least 5 to 6 weeks of holidays per employee
