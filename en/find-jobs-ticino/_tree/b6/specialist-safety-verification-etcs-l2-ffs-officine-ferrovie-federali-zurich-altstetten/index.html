  • Move Switzerland with us. Specialist:in safety verification ETCS L2 Zurich Altstetten / Work Smart, September 1st, 2026 or by appointment, 60-100% Apply now ## You can look forward to this. Become part of a competent, collegial and motivated team and get involved in the further development of digitalization and industrialization of project planning, testing and tracking in ETCS Level 2 system projects. Exciting and challenging projects all over Switzerland are waiting for you to work on. This makes an important contribution to providing a safe railway infrastructure. The ETCS Technology Industrialization (ETI) team is part of the Competence Center for Safety Systems in the Execution and Renewal Projects (AEP). ETI is responsible for providing evidence of the functionality and fulfillment of RAMS requirements as well as the integration of new technologies and components within ETCS L2 system projects and is actively involved in further developments in the areas of digitalization, tools as well as in the optimization of the project planning and testing process.   • As a specialist in safety verification, you will accompany ETCS L2 projects throughout Switzerland from the study to commissioning and support the project managers in making decisions in order to plan a compliant and safe ETCS L2 system.
  • As part of ETCS L2 system or change projects, you are the contact person for safety and verification in the project and in this role you are also responsible for the creation of safety evidence.
  • You assess the risks in the project with regard to compliance with the safety objectives and create risk analyzes or adapt the generic risk analyzes to the specific systems.
  • You are responsible for providing the system documentation on time.
  • You actively contribute suggestions for improvement and support the safety verification department in further development.
  • As part of the competence center, you are the contact person for colleagues from all regions and support them with technical questions. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are characterized by good analytical, conceptual and logical-abstract thinking skills.
  • You are proactive, enjoy working with different stakeholders and have good communication skills.
  • A strong awareness of safety and a careful way of working are essential for this task.
  • In order to be able to carry out this task competently, you must have completed a university degree (University/FH) in a technical subject such as electrical engineering or an equivalent qualification.
  • Ideally, you have already gained several years of experience in the railway industry and have already acquired knowledge of ETCS L2. Alternatively, experience in the areas of quality management, verification/validation or knowledge of standards makes it easier to enter the world of railway safety systems.
  • Since the specifications and regulations are formulated in German, you absolutely need very good knowledge of German (at least. C1) both oral and written.
