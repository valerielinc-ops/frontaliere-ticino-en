Put to the Point

They support the employees of the legal service EFD and deal with a wide variety of questions regarding state liability law and (financial market) criminal law.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027, by agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: 600-20615
  • Place of work: Bundesgasse 3, 3011 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Cooperation in administrative and administrative criminal proceedings, as well as treatment of other legal transactions in the area of responsibility of the EFD legal service
  • Collaboration in compensation and satisfaction proceedings against the Confederation, orders or statements in these proceedings edit
  • Participate in the instruction and preparation of decisions of the Federal Council and the department as well as draft opinions on complaints against departmental decisions
  • Dealing with questions concerning personnel law, public and procurement law as well as with donations to the Federal Government
  • Assist in investigations/investigations in administrative penal proceedings under financial market laws
  • Final minutes, penal decisions, non-execution/recruitment orders, etc. Preparation
  • Participate in political affairs such as official consultations or co-reporting procedures for the Head of Department

This makes them unique.

  • Above-average successful university degree in the legal direction (graduate) with the aim of obtaining the attorney’s patent
  • Interest in administrative law, civil liability law, financial market law or criminal law and willingness to familiarize yourself with complex issues
  • Initiativeal, persistent and team-oriented, sense of the essentials; good editorial skills
  • Basic accounting, accounting and financial products knowledge or willingness to acquire such knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of two official languages and knowledge of English

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Karin Schmid Head of Section General Legal Service +41 58 462 6217

The General Secretariat EFD (GS-EFD) is the central staff body of the department with the core areas of staff, communication, resources, legal service, digitalization/ICT and the delegates for multilingualism of the Confederation as well as the Digital Administration Switzerland (DVS).

Additional information

The term of office shall be one year. The degree may be awarded at the beginning of work max. 18 months ago. Please apply through our application management system. Click on “Apply now”. When filling this position, we do not consider personnel service providers.
