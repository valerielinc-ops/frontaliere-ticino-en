Full description

As of 1 March 2027 or by agreement, we are looking for a committed and competent personality who will strengthen our team.

  • They independently develop work instruments, process descriptions and processes and ensure the flow of information between the interfaces of the district school authority, schools, project teams and offices.
  • They advise and support management teams in the design, control and planning of school development projects, such as digitalization in the school living space.
  • You are responsible for records management and compliance in the school district of Schwamendingen.
  • They work in urban working and project groups, organize and actively participate in meetings of various committees of the district school authority and advise the members of the authorities in their supervisory function.
  • You lead projects and sub-projects within the framework of school development create conceptual and planning work for the implementation of these projects.
  • Completed training at tertiary level in the pedagogical field, preferably teaching diploma and very good knowledge of the urban and cantonal school system
  • Structured working methods, networked thinking and acting as well as a high level of personal responsibility
  • Active cooperation, joint solution finding and the willingness to support higher-level decisions
  • very good written expressive skills, in-depth IT knowledge as well as project management skills and experience in concept creation
  • Communication experience in the political context and enjoyment of solution-oriented cooperation with multi-professional teams in a responsible function

It awaits you a versatile and demanding task with high personal responsibility in a small, committed team in the administration of the district school authority Schwamendingen. They benefit from attractive employment conditions in the city of Zurich as well as an easily accessible workplace in the center of Schwamendingen.

The school district Schwamendingen is one of the seven school districts in the city of Zurich. About 3,400 students are taught in the 11 school units. More than 1,000 employees from the teaching, support and domestic service groups work for our schools.

The School Development Department supports schools and the district school authority in the implementation of city-wide school development projects. He advises schools on the conceptual and planning implementation of their projects and is responsible for quality development and assurance. In addition, the department coordinates internal initiatives of the school district as well as plans and controls future projects in the areas of promotion, support and teaching. Furthermore, the department advises and accompanies the authority in its supervisory activities.
