Full description

Do you have in-depth experience in production automation and would like to implement challenging automation projects in a regulated pharmaceutical environment?

In this position, you take over the holistic management of automation projects for highly automated production plants in the field of solid drug forms. You will be responsible for technical solutions, quality, deadlines and budgets and coordinate interdisciplinary teams, suppliers and internal and external stakeholders.

Hard facts

  • Start date: Per immediate, no later than 1. March 2027
  • Duration: 12 months
  • Extension: Probably
  • Pensum: 100 %
  • Location: Kaiseraugst
  • Home Office: 1 day per week
  • Work model: Contracting
  • Travel activity: Occasional travel possible as part of FAT activities
  • Working hours: standard working hours

Your tasks

  • Support for highly automated production plants that are networked with each other and with higher-level IT/OT systems
  • Self-responsible planning, control and implementation of automation projects across all project phases
  • Responsibility for technical solutions, quality, milestones, dates and the timely resolution of possible showstoppers
  • Preparation of business cases and support of CAPEX and project budget requests
  • Ongoing monitoring of project budgets in accordance with the applicable forecast and reporting processes
  • Need-based planning and coordination of the necessary resources
  • Coordination and leadership of interdisciplinary project teams from internal and external experts, partners and suppliers
  • Advising production areas on the definition and initiation of investment projects
  • Preparation of cost estimates and schedules and evaluation of new technologies
  • Early identification of project risks and development of appropriate risk mitigation measures
  • Ensure consistent application of relevant project management methods and standards
  • Regular reporting and progress monitoring based on clear key figures vis-à-vis stakeholders and steering committees
  • Technical support for ongoing production operations
  • Continuous analysis and optimization of existing processes to increase efficiency and plant performance
  • Active support for internal audits and official inspections in the GMP and SGU environment

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training, further education or studies in the field of automation, electrical engineering, technical informatics or an equivalent specialisation
  • At least five years of professional experience in production automation, preferably in the pharmaceutical sector
  • Very good practical user knowledge in Siemens S7, TIA Portal and WinCC
  • In-depth experience in the GMP and GAMP regulated environment
  • Experience with interfaces between automation systems and higher-level IT/OT systems
  • Experience in the independent management and implementation of automation projects over several project phases
  • Strong communication, coordination and stakeholder management capabilities
  • High social skills and enjoyment of working in interdisciplinary teams
  • Willingness to work in an agile, self-organized and role-based work environment
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • English and French skills are beneficial

Benefits

  • Enthusiasm and a self-responsible and customer-oriented working method
  • High degree of independence, initiative, integrity and reliability
  • Strong service orientation and a high sense of duty
  • Willingness to question conventional approaches and develop creative solutions
  • Ability to convey complex technical issues in an understandable and target group-oriented manner
  • Experience in the coordination of external engineering partners, plant suppliers or system integrators
  • Experience in supporting audits or inspections within a regulated production environment
