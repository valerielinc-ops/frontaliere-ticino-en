Place of work: St. Anna in the train station | Lucerne

Occupation by: by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74140

For our dedicated secretarial team of sports medicine & physiotherapy St. Anna in the train station we need reinforcement. Are you motivated, team-oriented and looking for a new challenge? Then we look forward to meeting you!

YOUR TASKS

  • As the first point of contact for our patients, you play a key role in ensuring a smooth routine in everyday practice.
  • With organizational talent and empathy, you respond individually to the needs of our patients – from scheduling to personal care before, during and after the examinations.
  • You actively support our sports doctors during consultations and diagnostic tests
  • You are an important contact person for our physiotherapists and support them in their daily routine
  • The independent conduct of various investigations – such as e.g. B. ECG or lung function tests – rounds off your varied everyday life
  • In addition, you will take on important administrative and digital tasks, such as maintaining patient data, organizing procedures in the practice management system and ensuring efficient digital documentation.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a Medical Practice Assistant (MPA)
  • A strong understanding of services as well as a high resilience distinguish you
  • Your working method is efficient, structured and characterized by organizational talent and a quick apprehension
  • Direct and telephone contact with patients brings you pleasure
  • You work very efficiently in the administrative area and have a secure handling of digital systems and practical software

Our offer

  • Do you like longer breaks? With us you have the option to take up to 7 weeks vacation per year
  • Do you like travelling with public transport or Velo? We contribute financially to your work journey
  • We work with work clothes, you are entitled to up to 4 additional free days of paid changing time
  • Does it all sound good? Further information on employment conditions and additional offers for employees can be found here

You have questions? Simply contact Julia Gasser, team leader Secretariat Physiotherapy & Sports Medicine St. Anna in the train station, under T or Merlin Abgottspon, HR recruitment partner, at merlin.abgottspon@hirslanden.ch.

For data protection reasons, we look forward to your online application.
