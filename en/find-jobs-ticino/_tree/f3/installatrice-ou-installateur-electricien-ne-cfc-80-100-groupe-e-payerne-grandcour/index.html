Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment and maintenance of installations.

Do you like technical challenges, good work and colleagues on whom to rely? Our Payerne branch is looking for new talent to strengthen its team! Whether you prefer to participate in large-scale projects, with projects ranging from CHF 500'000 to 1 million, or stand alone on small projects and outbuildings, we have opportunities to put your know-how to use. At Groupe E Payerne, you will join a team where mutual help, trust and a good atmosphere are part of everyday life. An ideal environment to value your experience, meet new challenges and continue to develop your skills.

Installer or installer CFC 80-100%

Mission

  • Perform various electrical installations, both on large-scale construction sites and on small-scale works, transformations or troubleshooting, depending on your experience and skills.
  • Install, connect and modify electrical equipment: tables, apparatus, communication systems, multimedia, security and control.
  • Diagnosing and solving electrical breakdowns, providing efficient solutions tailored to the needs of customers, as part of troubleshooting interventions.
  • Ensure reading and interpretation of electrical plans and diagrams, as well as the installation of pipes and the drawing of cables.
  • Participate in commissioning and ensure the quality, compliance and safety of facilities.
  • Establish the metres and governance reports required to monitor interventions.
  • Share your know-how and contribute to the training of our apprentices.

Profile

  • CFCs of electrical installers or mounting electricians.
  • Experience in general electrical installations, ideally acquired on construction sites of different sizes.
  • For troubleshooting and small work activities: proven experience, excellent practical sense and ability to diagnose and solve technical problems independently.
  • An organized and resourceful person, you show initiative, anticipation and a real sense of responsibility.
  • Team spirit and good communication: you enjoy working with your colleagues, sharing your knowledge and contributing to a positive working atmosphere.
  • Flexibility and openness, with the desire to meet various technical challenges.
  • Good command of French.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance, with four-and-a-half days of working time and free Friday afternoon
  • A function vehicle also suitable for private use
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate "Together, we are moving towards success"
