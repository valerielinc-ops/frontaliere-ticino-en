What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Retail Division, we are looking for one or more:

Announcement Advisor or remote advisor - International customer (guidance compliance) Your main missions:

  • ensure the execution of specific measures related to the treatment of retail customers domiciled abroad,
  • carrying out, as the first line of defence, the tasks of BA/FT clarification in relation, relationship review and transaction review,
  • ensure the processing of all requests for clarification from, inter alia, the Compliance Department,
  • to monitor the Bank's regulatory requirements, particularly with the help of the related workflows (y.c. EAR),
  • receive and serve international Retail customers by supporting their commercial, administrative and product support requests via the appropriate communication channel (telephone, mail, e-mail). Your profile:
  • successful experience in bank risk management,
  • results orientation, autonomy and organizational rigour,
  • Perfect mastery of French and English (level C1).

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
