Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Judicial Enforcement and Reintegration Probation and Enforcement Services Co-Stv. Head of Department and Head of Department Penal Imprisonment 80 - 100%

As part of the Directorate of Justice and the Interior of the Canton of Zurich, JuWe is responsible for the execution of criminal and measures and the implementation of the various types of detention. For our 1,400 employees from around 40 professions, the successful social reintegration of people who have become criminals and the prevention of relapses are of particular importance.

The probation and enforcement services (BVD) - one of the seven departments of JuWe - are responsible for the execution of imprisonment and judicially ordered measures as well as for probation assistance. They make an important contribution to the prevention of offences and the social integration of people who are in crime. The Department of Imprisonment regulates, coordinates and plans the execution of imprisonment in prisons. Further important decisions are taken on enforcement actions (placement, leave, conditional dismissal, repatriation, etc.).

In addition, applications for the release of imprisonment in the special forms of electronic monitoring and semi-captivity are examined and decided.
