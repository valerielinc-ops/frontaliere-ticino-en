Business Analyst (12 mois) — Pictet Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your team

The Pictet Group, founded in 1805, has its headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland. It is established worldwide (31 offices) and currently employs more than 5000 people.

Pictet Wealth Management (PWM) is based on both a Swiss bank legacy of over 200 years and international investment expertise. Group owned and led by associates, Pictet offers a full range of financial services for families and wealthy individuals, including discretionary management and investment consulting solutions as well as family office services.

Based in Paris, you will join the PWM France Market. You will work as close as possible to the private bankers and CEO of the Paris branch, at the heart of their daily activities. This role will allow you to discover the practical operation of an international private bank and to intervene on subjects of high added value.

Your role

Within the team, you will be led to

  • Analyze economic & financial data and produce summaries & presentations.
  • Assist bankers in daily prospecting, as well as sourcing and searching for new prospects.
  • Preparing investment proposals for clients and prospects by working with the Wealth Planning and Investment Specialists.
  • Support Investment Specialists in the implementation of investment ideas.
  • Contribute to the organization of customer events and cross-cutting projects of the team.

This position is a unique opportunity to plunge into the complex, competitive and ever-changing environment of private management. It will allow you to quickly develop solid technical skills while strengthening your relational skills in direct contact with teams.

Your profile

  • Young graduate of business school or master's degree in heritage management or finance.
  • Excellent mastery of MS Office computer applications: Word, Excel, PowerPoint.
  • Good knowledge of the main investment vehicles and asset classes; solid foundations in civil law and taxation of assets.
  • French mother tongue and bilingual English.
  • Accurate, conscientious, dynamic and fast, you have a curious mind and like to work in a group.
  • You are versatile and will be able to rely on your excellent analytical spirit. Your ability to find solutions and your proactive attitude will be major assets in your daily work.

Place : 5, rue de Beaujon 75008 Paris
