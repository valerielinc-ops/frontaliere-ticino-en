Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in vocational training, it gives an important place to the accompaniment and development of young talent, with more than 120 learners trained each year in its various departments. For its headquarters in Lausanne, Vaudoise Assurances wishes to recruit an apprentice computer operator CFC (m/f/x) from 1 August 2027. Are you passionate about computer science and new technologies? Do you like to understand how computer equipment works, solve problems and help others when they encounter technical difficulties? Want to learn a concrete and varied profession while developing your skills in a stimulating environment? Then this learning could be done for you! The missions that await you during these 3 years of training:

  • Dealing with incidents and requests for computer assistance;
  • Support and assist users in their IT needs;
  • Participate in user training;
  • Install and commission computer hardware, including computers and printers, as well as software;
  • Maintaining computer hardware and software;
  • Identify, analyse and resolve technical problems;
  • Document the procedures and solutions in place;
  • Connect devices and services to the network. Your profile:
  • You have completed or will soon complete your compulsory education;
  • You are curious, motivated and want to learn;
  • You are passionate about computer science and new technologies;
  • You like practical activities and have good technical skills;
  • You appreciate understanding, analyzing and solving problems;
  • You are logical and have a good mind of analysis;
  • You like contact with others, have a sense of service and enjoy teamwork;
  • You are organized, rigorous and ready to invest fully in your training. We offer you:
  • A healthy and stimulating work environment within a cooperative company certified as Great Place to Work;
  • Various and concrete vocational training in the field of informatics;
  • The ability to develop your technical skills while in contact with users;
  • The possibility of gaining practical experience in contact with IT professionals;
  • Support throughout your learning in order to gradually develop your skills and autonomy;
  • The possibility of discovering the computer environment of a large insurance company and gradually building your professional future;
  • Opportunities for engagement at the end of your training. Interested? So don't hesitate to send us your complete application package including:
  • your cover letter;
  • your CV;
  • your last three school reports;
  • your possible certificates of internship. We look forward to discovering your profile and receiving your application!
