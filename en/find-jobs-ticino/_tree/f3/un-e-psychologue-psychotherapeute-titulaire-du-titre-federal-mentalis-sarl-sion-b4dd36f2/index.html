Research: Psychologist-Psychotherapist with federal title

The Mentalis Centre, located at Avenue de la Gare 41 in Sion, is a human-sized psychological and psychotherapy centre. In a caring, modern and collaborative setting, we offer quality psychotherapeutic follow-up.

In order to strengthen our team and develop our clinical supervision, we are looking for: A psychologist-psychotherapist with the federal title

Your missions

Provide individual psychotherapeutic follow-up to adults.

Actively participate in the centre's life and team dynamics (clinical meetings, practice exchanges, intervisions).

Support and supervise psychotherapists in their clinical practice by promoting their skills development and professional autonomy.

Participate in clinical reflection on complex situations and contribute to the quality of care within the centre.

Write reports and ensure practice-related administrative follow-up.

Collaborate with the various partners of the network (doctors, institutions, insurance, etc.).

Contribute to the development and continuous improvement of clinical practices and care offerings at the centre.

Profile sought

Master in Clinical Psychology recognized in Switzerland.

Federal post-graduate title of psychotherapist completed and recognized in Switzerland.

Minimum 3 years of professional experience in psychotherapy after obtaining the title.

An important asset is experience in supervising, accompaniment or mentoring psychologists and psychotherapists in training.

Excellent relational skills and leadership qualities, with the ability to support, guide and develop training professionals.

A sense of responsibility, autonomy, rigour and capacity to take initiatives.

Motivation to invest sustainably in a collective project

Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written; knowledge of other languages is an asset.

We offer

An employee position within a dynamic, collaborative and caring team.

A responsible clinical role, with the possibility to participate actively in the supervision and development of psychotherapists in training.

The possibility of developing a supervisory and transmission role within a human-sized centre.

Bright, modern and warm rooms, ideally located in the centre of Sion, close to the train station.

Administrative support (secretariat) for patient record management and billing.

A motivating remuneration, adapted to experience and responsibilities, with the possibility of varying shares depending on the annual results of the centre.

13th salary.

An improved social plan (LPP and Insurance Accidents).

A flexible activity rate, to be agreed (between 60% and 100%).

25 days of vacation per year (for full time).

A gold team

Start: to be defined
