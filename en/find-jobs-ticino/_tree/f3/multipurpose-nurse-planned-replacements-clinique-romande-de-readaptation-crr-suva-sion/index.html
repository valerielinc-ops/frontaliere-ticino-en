Start-up: to be agreed Activity rate: 80% to 100% Join the French Rehabilitation Clinic (CRR) of the Suva in Sion, a reference centre specializing in the rehabilitation of injured persons, with locomotor disorders, neurological disorders or spinal cord injuries. If you are passionate about quality care and want to contribute actively to the well-being of patients, this position is for you! The RRC care department consists of four units, organized according to the following rehabilitation specialties: paraplegia, neuroreeducation, traumatology and mixed unit. In order to complement its teams, the care department is seeking, for a start to the activity to be agreed, a multi-purpose nurse (planned replacements) Are you motivated, flexible and looking for a variety of activities to develop skills in the different stationary units of our clinic? We have the perfect opportunity for you! To complete our team, we are looking for a graduate nurse who wants to work on all of our units as part of planned replacements. Placed under the direct responsibility of the care management, you will have a clear schedule, prepared and communicated in advance. Your main missions Ensure overall patient care in different units Actively contribute to exemplary quality of care Foster a positive and collaborative working environment by quickly integrating into each team Participate in the organization of care processes and take responsibility for your skills Enrich and diversify your professional skills through experience in the various clinical contexts Our expectations Diploma in nursing HES or ES (with recognition Swiss Red Cross if foreign) Hospital experience Ability to adapt quickly and taste for clinical diversity Interest in meeting team challenges and contributing to a common project Openness to innovation and flexibility Sensitization of responsibilities and ability to self-manage Excellent relational and communication skills Mastery of the French language, both oral and written (minimum B2 level) Why join us? If you are looking for an environment where every day is an opportunity to learn, grow and positively impact the lives of others, then the RRC care service is for you! Our strengths: Long-term patient support: Track patient progress and contribute directly to their return to independence. Enriching Environment: Collaborate with experts from various fields in an interdisciplinary approach. Continuing training: Access a wide range of training courses to strengthen your skills and develop your career. Dynamic team: Work in a caring environment where efforts are recognized and valued. Benefits: High social benefits, many benefits, free park space, and much more.
