Full description

Join our team and take on a hands-on role where you'll keep operations moving smoothly behind the scenes. In this position, you'll work with a supportive team to receive and organize incoming materials, accurately pick and prepare orders, and ensure products are packed and shipped with care. You'll also play an important role in maintaining an organized warehouse, making it easy to track and retrieve inventory. If you enjoy staying active, paying attention to detail, and being part of a team that values your contributions, this is a great opportunity to grow your skills in a team environment.

Aufgaben

  • Pull and prioritize order, restock inventory
  • Operate automated storage modules
  • Operates powered industrial trucks to load and unload materials from trucks and shelving
  • Performing daily equipment check such as recharging forklift batteries and general maintenance
  • Assist shipping and receiving unloading trucks and checking in merchandise
  • Moving goods packed on pallets or in crates around the storage facility
  • May assist in counting of physical inventory
  • Prepares orders by processing requests and supply orders: pulling materials and packaging orders securely
  • Examining products to verify conformance to quality standards
  • Process orders for shipment via UPS, FedEx, or Traffic to determine
  • Manages miscellaneous projects and completes various tasks as needed
  • Accepts and performs all other duties as assigned by immediate supervisor. To include any and all jobs or tasks that may not be directly related to this job description
  • Work shift is from 8:00am to 5pm (M – F)
  • Must arrive at work promptly and predictably

Anforderungen

  • Highschool diploma or equivalent
  • Experience with UPS & FedEx shipments
  • Experience with a forklift is a plus
  • Safe working habits
  • Good cooperation and communication skills
  • Maintenance of a neat and clean work area
  • High degree of mechanical aptitude
  • Good organizational skills
  • Good computer skills, ideally with SAP background
