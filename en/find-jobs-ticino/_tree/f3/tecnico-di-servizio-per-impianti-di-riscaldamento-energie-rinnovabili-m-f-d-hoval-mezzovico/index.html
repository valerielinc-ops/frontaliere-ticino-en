Full description

To strengthen our service team in Ticino, we are looking for you!

As a leading technology company, Hoval has been exciting its customers for decades with innovative system solutions in the heating and air conditioning sector. In order to offer our customers a wide range of service services, we need motivated people ready to exploit their skills. Do you have a good technical understanding, like working independently and want to further develop your professional skills? Then you're the right person for us.

Your main activities

  • Service of new plants
  • Search and troubleshooting on Hoval heat pumps
  • Maintenance work
  • Customer advice in the sale of maintenance contracts and spare parts
  • Availability to carry out the picket service
  • Interventions are carried out directly from home to our customers in the region

What we offer you

  • Diversified, stimulating and responsible activities
  • Sales and attractive social performance awards
  • A collegiate team available
  • Targeted training on our Hoval products and continuing training
  • Possibility of professional improvement as a specialist in thermal systems
  • Business vehicle, laptop, smartphone and more

Your profile

  • Technical training completed, for example, as a cooling technician or electrician, is preferential title
  • Autonomous, reliable and able to manage challenging situations
  • Good communication and commercial skills
  • Availability and pleasure to carry out a dynamic activity with movements within the region

Join our team! Take the opportunity to put into play your strengths and make a difference with us. In Hoval, challenging challenges await you, a hungry team and concrete opportunities for growth and professional development. Apply online now and join Hoval!
