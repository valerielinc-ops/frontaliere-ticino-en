Full description

Founded in Lausanne in 1843, the Asylum Foundation for the Blind includes the Jules-Gonin Ophthalmic Hospital, a university department of ophthalmology, a research centre, the Centre pédagogique pour épupils handicapés de la vue (CPHV), the social service, rehabilitation and low vision and the medical-social establishment Clair-Soleil.

More than 670 employees work daily in the visual health department to provide diagnostic, treatment and support services to people with vision.

To complement our team, we are looking for a

Nurse EMS Clair-Soleil

Your Mission

  • Ensuring integration and individualisation of the resident
  • Organize and participate in the work of the health care team
  • Provide and guarantee the quality of care and services to residents
  • Provide delegated medical care
  • Provide guidance to health workers, ASSCs and trainees.

Your assets

  • Bachelor of Science HES-SO in Nursing or equivalent
  • Case of a trainer practitioner, an advantage
  • Humanitude ® training, a real asset
  • Several years of practice in care, experience in EMS an asset
  • Ability to work in interdisciplinarity
  • Capacity for decision-making, analysis and synthesis, sense of observation and human relations
  • Good knowledge of common computer tools.

We offer you

An enriching activity within a dynamic environment where you can showcase your talents, specific training for our mission, salary conditions related to your skills. Activity rate: 80-100% Nature of contract: CDI Entry into office: to be agreed Place of work: EMS Clair-Soleil, Ecublens
