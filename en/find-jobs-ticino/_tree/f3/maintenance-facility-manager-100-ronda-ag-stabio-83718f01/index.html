Maintenance \& Facility Manager 100% (m/f/d)

Precision. Quality. Teamwork. As Maintenance \& Facility Manager, you will ensure the maintenance of plants and infrastructure, contributing to the safety and efficiency of the company.

What you can do

  • Enhancing ordinary maintenance* and extraordinary maintenance of production machinery, technical facilities and business infrastructure
  • Plan and coordinate preventive and corrective maintenance operations, repairs and calibration of equipment and measuring instruments
  • Coordinate external suppliers* and collaborate with internal departments and external partners to ensure proper operation of plants and infrastructure
  • Planning, managing and accompanying projects of automation and modernization of production processes, from analysis of needs to implementation and commissioning
  • Promote continuous improvement of production processes, optimizing plant efficiency, reducing costs and ensuring compliance with safety standards

What makes you stand out

  • Technical training* concluded in mechanical, electromechanical or electronic
  • Professional experience in the maintenance of machinery, technical facilities and infrastructure, with good knowledge of mechanics, electromechanical and plant engineering
  • Autonomous work method*, structured and reliable, with a strong sense of responsibility and good organizational skills
  • Spirit of team and good communication and relational skills, with attitude to collaboration with different interlocutors
  • Good knowledge of Italian and English; knowledge of other national languages is an important requirement

The charge we give you

  • A valued job: you carry out activities that generate a concrete value and increase your motivation
  • Growth opportunities: we support your professional and personal development through targeted initiatives
  • Attractive social benefits:* take advantage of an excellent pension and additional social benefits

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
