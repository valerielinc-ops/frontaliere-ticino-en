Full description

Quality standards, lived humanity and great respect for old age. Are you looking for a new challenge in an active, dynamic environment where you can get involved? Is the professional care just as important to you as a warm relationship with residents and colleagues?

Then complement our care team for our nursing living group at the Basel location as a dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH 80% | Basel location Your tasks

  • You accompany and look after our residents holistically, individually and resource-oriented.
  • You take on the responsibility for professional, person-centered care and promote the independence and quality of life of our residents.
  • You plan, coordinate, lead and evaluate nursing measures independently and according to current professional standards.
  • You recognize changes in your health status at an early stage, initiate appropriate measures and work closely with relatives and interdisciplinary specialists.
  • You document the maintenance process carefully and ensure a seamless transfer of information.
  • You will take on treatment-related tasks – including safe and responsible drug management and administration.
  • You actively contribute to an appreciative living and living atmosphere and shape the everyday life of our residents.
  • You work in a committed interdisciplinary team and bring your ideas to the continuous development of our care quality and processes. Your profile
  • Completed training for dipl. Nurse or equivalent education
  • Joy and enthusiasm at working with elderly people
  • Responsibility, team spirit and a dose of humor
  • Motivation, professionalism and an open heart
  • Interest in lean management and pleasure in critically questioning and actively optimizing processes This is what awaits you: Look forward to an appreciative environment, a dedicated team and a workplace where expertise and humanity go hand in hand. The Adullam Foundation is a leading institution for age medicine, therapy and care in northwestern Switzerland. In Basel and Riehen, it operates a hospital and an affiliated care centre. The approximately 800 employees offer elderly people a regionally unique all-round care, from acute medicine to rehabilitation to transitional and long-term care. The holistic care in Adullam includes a high quality standard, lived humanity and great respect for old age.
