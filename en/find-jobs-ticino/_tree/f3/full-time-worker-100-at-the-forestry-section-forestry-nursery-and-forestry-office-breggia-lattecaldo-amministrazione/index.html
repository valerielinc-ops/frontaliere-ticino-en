Full description

Tasks

  • ordinary and extraordinary maintenance of the nursery, gardens, flower beds, trees and green areas; execution of planting, pruning, collection and management of seeds, irrigation and care of plant essences
  • production and management of food materials
  • ordinary use and maintenance of machinery, equipment and service vehicles; management of composting square and plant waste collection areas
  • control of correct separation and conferment of compostable materials
  • monitoring of composting processes and maintenance of plant order and safety
  • assistance and information to the user; compilation of logs, activity reports and technical documentation
  • collaboration with the service manager and other municipal operators
  • training, accompaniment and supervision of gardeners
  • planning and monitoring of the training path in collaboration with the competent authorities and professional schools
  • periodic evaluation of professional skills of apprentices
  • promotion of compliance with safety standards and good professional practices during training

Requirements

  • Federal Certificate of Capacity (AFC) as Gardener/a or Sculpturer/trice
  • licence to conduct cat. B (the possession of the driver's license and the license to conduct cat. BE and C may be preferential title
  • proven experience in the work of nursery and plant waste management
  • experience with the use of plant protection products (with license use of plant protection products or to obtain)
  • to carry out manual maintenance work to the tools and machines supplied
  • experience in the management and training of apprentices. Enability as a trainer/trainer in the company or availability to achieve it within an agreed deadline
  • proven professionalism and irreproachable conduct
  • good physical constitution, flexibility, availability at high and irregular working rates
  • spirit of initiative, ability to adapt and good preparation for teamwork
  • willingness to assume responsibility roles
  • availability to attend training and professional updating
  • good knowledge of the Ticino cantonal reality

Special remarks

  • Workplace: Municipality of Breggia, Morbio Superiore, Lattecaldo

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

26 October 2026
