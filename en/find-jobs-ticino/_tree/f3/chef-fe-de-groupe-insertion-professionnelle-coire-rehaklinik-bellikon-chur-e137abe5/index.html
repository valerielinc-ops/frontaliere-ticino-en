Full description

Membership, meaningfulness and competence With an experienced team, we accompany patients through ups and downs and help them to find their way back to life or work after an accident or illness.

The Competence Center for Vocational Integration (KbE) at Bellikon Rehabilitation Clinic offers various measures with a professional focus. On behalf of IV positions and other clients, specialists accompany people on their way back to work after an accident or illness. Services at the Chur location include in particular integration measures, vocational assessments, career guidance and job coaching. The Chur site is home to an interdisciplinary team of around 11 employees and two civil service providers. The KbE Chur is part of the outpatient services of the Bellikon rehabilitation clinic with further KbE locations in Bellikon and St. Gallen. As of January 1, 2027, we are looking for an entrepreneurial and leadership-strong personality as a group leader / in vocational integration Chur.
