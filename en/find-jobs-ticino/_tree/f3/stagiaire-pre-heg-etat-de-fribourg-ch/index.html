Who are we?

The SPS Social Security Service supports and coordinates the implementation of policies for persons with disabilities and seniors. It plans the provision of benefits and is responsible for the financing and supervision of specialized agencies under its jurisdiction. It plans the need for places in medical-social institutions (EMS), the need for care and home support, and provides grants to people who cannot finance their stay in EMS. He is also responsible for financing the residual costs of care.
