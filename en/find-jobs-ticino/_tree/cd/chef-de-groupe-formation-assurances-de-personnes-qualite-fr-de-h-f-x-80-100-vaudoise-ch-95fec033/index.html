Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. In order to strengthen our Personal Insurance Services Division, we are looking for a Head/Group Leader Training Personal Insurance & Quality . At the head of a team of specialists, you play a key role in the development of skills, the quality of services and the harmonization of business practices within the division. The various missions that await you

  • Coach, support and develop a team of trainers and specialists Training & Quality
  • Organize team activities, optimal allocation of resources according to operational priorities and ensure administrative follow-up
  • Identify the training needs and business guidelines of different entities and coordinate actions with business managers and managers
  • Evolving initial and continuing training arrangements, including teaching materials, while ensuring the quality of integration pathways
  • Conduct projects to improve learning experience and efficiency of training pathways
  • Participate in the creation and development of trade directives and ensure harmonization of practices within the division
  • Support teams in the application of business instructions and ensure effective dissemination of regulatory and organisational developments
  • Lead quality control and monitoring activities and support managers in the analysis of results and the definition of improvement measures
  • Actively participate in continuous improvement of quality processes, services and standards
  • Contribute to the success of the Vaudoise thanks to your leadership, your professional expertise and your ability to unite teams around common goals What makes you unique
  • Higher education or equivalent experience in insurance, training or management
  • Experience confirmed in personal insurance, ideally in LAA benefits and/or loss of sickness earnings
  • Bilingual French German; minimum level C1 in the non-mother tongue (mandatory)
  • Successful experience in team leadership and ability to support change in a changing environment
  • High interest in knowledge transfer and skills development
  • Excellent analytical, organisational and communication capabilities
  • Quality sense, orientation solutions and ability to collaborate with various stakeholders
  • Natural leadership, ability to federate and build confidence
  • Aisance with digital tools and interest in digitalisation and continuous improvement issues If you are also committed, organized and service oriented, that you enjoy developing others' skills and evolving in a collaborative environment, then you are probably the person we are looking for! We offer you
  • A healthy working environment in a mutualist company certified "Great Place to Work"

  • Human values focused on the well-being of employees
  • Working in a dynamic and passionate team
  • A responsible position with high visibility and direct impact on the quality of services and skill development
