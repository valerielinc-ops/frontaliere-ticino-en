Automotive mechatronics specialist: in for train diagnostics 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Automotive mechatronics specialist:in for train diagnostics Basel, 01.10.2026 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You actively shape the reliability of our trains and help our customers arrive safely and on time. You can expect a collegial environment in which knowledge is shared and you can take on responsibility. At the same time, you have the opportunity to further develop your expertise and continuously expand your know-how in modern diagnostic systems. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid for, for example, an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits and more information about the profession can be found here. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • As a diagnostician: in passenger transport, you analyze disturbances on trains with modern diagnostic tools and thus ensure reliable and safe operation.
  • You create well-founded fault diagnoses during operation and during planned downtimes.
  • Rapid and sustainable troubleshooting are part of your job.
  • You support in various projects to increase vehicle availability and reduce disruption times.
  • With your commitment, you also support the team in other tasks at the location.

You bring that with you.

  • Team spirit, clear communication, an analytical mindset and the joy of independently solving complex problems set you apart.
  • Irregular working hours, including night and weekend work, are beneficial for flexible leisure time.
  • You have a category B driving licence.
  • Very good knowledge of German at level C1 in word and writing you bring with you. Knowledge of a second national language is an advantage.
  • A completed apprenticeship in technical or electrical direction, for example as an automotive mechatronics engineer:in, production mechanic:in, polymechanic:in or electrical installer:in, as well as corresponding professional practice.

Your reward and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. For this reason, further and detailed information on the wage system can be viewed transparently at SBB. This position is in requirement level F, Regional Allowance Level 1.
