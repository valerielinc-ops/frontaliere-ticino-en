Full description

Join the NPC as Medical Officer-Chief Emergency and Liaison. An exciting opportunity in a stimulating environment.

Tasks

  • Ensure quality of care in emergency and liaison units.
  • Develop continuous clinical practice with patients.
  • Supervise the activities of both medical units.

Competences

  • Post-graduate FMH in psychiatry and psychotherapy.
  • Experience in multidisciplinary team management required.
  • Interdisciplinary work and leadership skills.

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implements public policies to promote and prevent mental health

  • It acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research.

The Medical Directorate, within the Adult Department, Emergency Unit, Liaison, Hospital (ULH) and to lead the emergency and liaison units, its future

Medical Officer-Chief Emergency and Liaison 80-100%

The Psychiatric Emergency Centre (CUP) and the Liaison Psychiatry Unit are part of the ULH Service, which also includes three hospital units in Prefargier (25 EPT each). This package ensures continuity of care, from emergency to hospitalization, in a coordinated and integrated approach. The (CUP) ensures the assessment and management of acute crisis situations, in close connection with the Neuchâtel General Hospital and the care network. It offers a rapid and coordinated response, helping to stabilize situations, guide patients and ensure continuity of care. Composed of a multidisciplinary team of about 26 EFAs, the unit mobilizes medical, nursing and psychological skills in a logic of responsiveness and coordination. The liaison unit works with patients who are hospitalized in somatic care or are currently receiving and receiving treatment and who have psychiatric disorders. It provides care at the interface of the somatic and the psychic, in support of patients, their relatives and hospital teams. Composed of a multidisciplinary team of around 10 EFAs, it contributes to a comprehensive and integrated approach to care. You have a post-graduate FMH degree in psychiatry and psychotherapy or an equivalent qualification recognized by MEBEKO, you have several years of experience in a position of Assistant Physician, with a successful managerial experience, and want to integrate a recognized institution specialized in psychic health.

As part of your function you will be required to

o Ensure quality and consistency of care in both units, coordinate medical activity with colleagues and network partners o Develop direct and continuous clinical practice with patients o Seconding and replacing the Chief Medical Officer in Medical Services Management o Supervise and participate in all activities of both units o Contribute to the development of specific therapeutic approaches and develop the necessary plans and programs to achieve the objectives decided by the Medical Directorate and the Department o Organize and develop collaboration with partner institutions (general hospital, police/justice...) o Participate in updating and developing diagnostic and therapeutic methods o Contribute to the institution's research activity o Participate in ongoing training and supervision activities o Contribute to the evaluation and continuous improvement of clinical practices

The competencies required for this position are

o be in possession of an FMH post-graduate qualification as a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy or an equivalent title recognized by MEBEKO, and ideally a complementary title as a liaison psychiatry. o have a minimum experience of 3 years in a position of psychiatrist, head of clinic or equivalent, ideally with the public concerned o additionally, having had the opportunity to frame a multidisciplinary unit/team successfully for several years, to carry a vision and accompany change o have a spirit of interdisciplinary teamwork and networking o demonstrate leadership and a sense of planning, negotiation and flexibility o have a sense of listening and collegiality Why join the CNP? o A human and stimulating mission: Work at the heart of a public institution recognized for its model of integrated and community psychiatry. o An exceptional environment: Neuchâtel offers a unique quality of life, between lake and mountains, ideal for families and nature lovers. o Professional development: Opportunity to contribute to innovative psychiatry projects, with active support for research and continuing training. o LWOP provides financial and logistical support in the event of relocation to the Township (subject to conditions).
