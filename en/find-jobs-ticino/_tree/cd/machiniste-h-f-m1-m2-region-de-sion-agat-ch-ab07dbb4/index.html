Machinist (M/F) - M1 & M2 AGAT! Zion 5 Oct. 1 min reading On behalf of one of our customers located in the Sion region, we are looking for an experienced and versatile M1/M2 machinist. Main mission

  • Driving construction machinery
  • Earthworks, excavations and levelling
  • External development work
  • Installation of pipes and edges
  • Participation in various masonry works where necessary
  • Current maintenance of machinery used
  • Compliance with safety rules on construction sites

Profile

  • Confirmed experience in running construction machinery
  • M1 and M2 Swiss machinist licence compulsory
  • Good knowledge of masonry and civil engineering
  • Polyvalent and willing to work also on the ground
  • Autonomous, reliable and accurate in your work
  • Good team spirit
  • Good command of French
  • Driving licence

We offer you

  • One year post with possibility of fixed commitment
  • Salary conditions according to your experience and industry requirements
  • Various construction sites in the region
  • A stable working environment within a local company
  • A personalized follow-up by our agency
