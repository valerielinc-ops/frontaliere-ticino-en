Full description

Your heart is beating for the elderly? The caregiving profession is your vocation, which allows you not only to improve the quality of life of others, but also to give meaning to your life? You see opportunities to bring your skills and experience to us to have a positive impact on the lives of our clients? Then you're at the right address!

For our institutions Tertianum Delémont in the Jura region, we are looking for a

ASA between 60-90%

Your tasks

  • Guidance and support of assigned hosts
  • Support for body care, movement and other daily activities
  • Organization and proposal of daily activities for our guests with creative occupations, walks, etc.
  • Responsibility for tasks as reference person
  • Participation in care reports and transmission of important observations
  • Writing care notes in our CareCoach care documentation system (possible for mobility with the CareCoach smartphone)
  • Assistance and participation in the organization of logistical and household activities

Your profile

  • SWC care and support training completed
  • Professional experience in the care of several years is an asset
  • A warm attitude
  • Pleasure meeting various challenges
  • Good knowledge of French spoken and written

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Jura?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Jura's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Jura's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
