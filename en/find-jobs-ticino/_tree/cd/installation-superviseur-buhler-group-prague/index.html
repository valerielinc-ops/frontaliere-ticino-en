Full description

Help us bring food production facilities to life.

As an Installation Supervisor at Bühler, you'll be on-site where our projects take shape. You'll guide installation teams, coordinate contractors, solve technical issues, and ensure that our machines and production systems are installed safely, correctly, and on schedule.

This role involves up to 90% international travel, primarily across Europe, with occasional assignments in other parts of the world. Installation assignments typically last 3 to 8 weeks.

To support your onboarding and development, you'll be based in Prague during your first year. During this time, you'll follow a structured onboarding and training program while joining customer site installations alongside experienced colleagues. As you gain experience, you'll gradually take on more responsibility and build the skills needed to manage installation projects independently. Aufgaben: • Supervise the installation of Bühler machines and production solutions • Plan and coordinate daily installation activities on customer sites • Guide installation teams and contractors carrying out the work • Monitor quality, safety, schedules, and site readiness • Solve technical and organizational issues directly on-site • Keep customers, project managers, and internal teams informed • Prepare site reports and escalate risks or delays when needed The focus of this position is technical supervision and coordination. You'll guide the teams carrying out the installation rather than perform the installation work yourself. Anforderungen: We welcome both experienced installation professionals and candidates ready to progress from a practical technical role. You may already supervise installations or commissioning activities. Alternatively, you may have experience as a Service Technician, Site Engineer, or in another technical on-site role and be ready to take on more responsibility. What matters most is your technical understanding, sound judgement on-site, willingness to travel, and ability to work effectively with customers and contractors. You'll need: • Technical education or relevant practical technical experience • Good technical and problem-solving skills • Confidence communicating and coordinating with others • Communicative English (B2+) • Willingness to travel internationally • Driving licence New to installation management? We'll support you. Becoming a successful Installation Supervisor doesn't happen overnight. That's why we provide a structured onboarding program, technical training, and support from experienced colleagues. Whether you're already an experienced Installation Supervisor or stepping up from another hands-on technical role, we'll help you develop the skills and confidence needed to succeed in this position. Two billion people eat food every day that was produced with Bühler equipment. One billion people drive vehicles whose parts were manufactured with our machines. Bühler aims to balance humanity, nature, and the economy in every decision as it develops solutions that unlock sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed, and mobility industries. We strive to create innovations for a better world, with a special focus on healthy, safe, and sustainable solutions. Therefore, we team up with customers, start-ups, multinationals, and academia to accelerate impact together.
