Full description

Cross-entry Track construction

50\-100%

Anyone who works outside and wants to make a difference is right with us. Together we maintain tracks, switches and construction trains – for safe and punctual journeys. Do you have technical or craft vocational training? Then you are right with us. You are part of a team that tackles, takes responsibility and knows that without you there would be no track. With us, the next step succeeds – to training as a train driver:in for construction trains.

This is waiting for you:

  • Maintain, repair and modernise tracks, switches and rail installations so that rail transport runs safely and reliably
  • Maintaining and securing the surroundings along the route – for example, embankment work, keeping clear gauges and ensuring lines of sight
  • Detect and correct technical faults in the line area, even for operations outside the normal working hours
  • After completion of training, lead construction trains in maintenance and construction site operations and ensure safe journeys

You bring this with you:

  • Professional experience in construction, crafts, metal or in a technical environment
  • Joy of practical work outdoors – even in wind, and changing conditions
  • Technical understanding, careful working methods and responsibility
  • Readiness for \- and weekend assignments (around 40-50%)
  • Category B driving licence
  • Good vision and hearing for safe work in the track area

Your advantages – you can look forward to:

  • We promote the most flexible planning of your tours and shifts possible
  • Get a GA 2\. class, a GA 1\. Class or a half-tax subscription.
  • You have free time when others work. For pens in and on weekends there are surcharges.
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year.
  • Financial support for external further education and an exciting internal learning and course offer are at your disposal.
  • With seasonal events and courses we support your health

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What connects us? The one on what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
