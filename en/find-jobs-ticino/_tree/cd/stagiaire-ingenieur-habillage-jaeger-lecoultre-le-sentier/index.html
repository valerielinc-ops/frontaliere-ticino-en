YOUR MISSION

Combine innovation, project management and versatility! Dive into the heart of our industrialization service within the Dressing sector. Your main mission will be to design and deploy a powerful, intuitive and visual tool to plan, anticipate and manage the workload of our programming team in the face of new project launches. In parallel, you will expand your skills by actively participating in the monitoring of a major equipment investment and continuous improvement efforts.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

Main Mission: Programmer Load Pilot •

Analyze and map: Study in detail the current functioning of the programming team, map key processes and accurately identify planning needs.

Designing the steering tool: Drafting the functional specifications and developing a visual tool to help decision-making to anticipate and balance the team's workload in the face of industrialisation projects.

Leading change: Present the tool, train and support the programming team to ensure the rapid and sustainable adoption of this new system.

Measuring Efficiency: Define and implement relevant performance indicators (KPIs) to evaluate and optimize daily load control.

Secondary missions: Project management and continuous improvement

Follow an industrial investment: Participate actively in the operational monitoring of the acquisition and installation project of our Lavage Chain.

Solving problems in the field: Collaborate on continuous improvement projects and contribute to problem-solving approaches to optimize the overall performance of the Dressing sector.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE US?

Training: Student in general engineering school, industrial engineering, or master's equivalent.

Profile and Appetence: High sensitivity to technology, project management and manufacturing industry.

Critical and synthetic mind: Excellent capacity to take back our processes, challenge our current functioning and be a force for constructive proposition.

Personal qualities: You are recognized for your dynamism, proactivity and rigor. Your autonomy and relationship ease will allow you to collaborate effectively with multidisciplinary teams.

WHAT WE OFFER

A complete and responsible immersion in the industrialization of a demanding production sector.

A stimulating collaborative environment where your improvement proposals will be listened to and valued.

Community support by industrialization professionals.
