The Position P&C Integration Product Owner - RDT Group Functions

The People & Culture (P&C) Integration Product Owner will be a key member of the People & Culture Engineering team, part of Roche Digital Technology (RDT). Our primary business stakeholders are People & Culture at Roche. The P&C Engineering team operates as a foundational force across various P&C products, providing the essential enablers and technical capabilities required to meet strategic objectives.

As the Integration Architect/Product Owner, you will ensure that the "API-First" and "Reusable Asset" philosophy is applied consistently across all technical deliveries, providing hands-on architectural guidance to our developers and managed service partners.

The Opportunity In this role, you will apply your technical expertise and leadership to drive architectural excellence across our integration ecosystem . You will:

  • Translate high-level solution architecture designs into clear, actionable technical blueprints for implementation by the development squads, collaborating closely with system architects, integration leads, and business analysts
  • Oversee cross-squad technical alignment by identifying dependencies early in the design phase to proactively prevent architectural debt .
  • Mentor and provide deep-dive technical guidance to our Managed Service (MS) partners to ensure all deliveries strictly align with Roche's architectural standards .
  • Discover and identify common integration needs across teams that can be successfully converted into reusable MuleSoft services .
  • Own , manage, and prioritize the product backlog end-to-end for your specific component to maximize product value and support the squad in setting meaningful sprint goals .
  • Guide the development of whole-product solutions by identifying core problems and translating strategic value objectives into clear Minimum Viable Product (MVP) requirements .
  • Interpret and clarify complex or ambiguous backlog items for the squad, acting as a facilitator to remove roadblocks and keep delivery on track .
  • Validate and agree on story completion with the team, ensuring deliverables meet all acceptance criteria, contain appropriate testing, and fully comply with the Definition of Done (DoD) .

Who you are

We are looking for a collaborative, technically credible leader who brings a balance of architectural depth and product ownership mindset . To be successful in this role, you possess:

  • Experience: A minimum of 7 years of experience in integration architecture, combined with a strong, proven track record in Product Ownership roles
  • Education & Certifications: A university degree or equivalent formal education in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related technical field, ideally complemented by MuleSoft or Product Owner certifications .
  • MuleSoft Expertise: Advanced, hands-on knowledge of the MuleSoft Anypoint Platform (including Exchange, Runtime Manager, and API Manager) and a proven ability to design and enforce API-Led Connectivity across Experience, Process, and System API layers .
  • Data Modeling Skills: Proficiency in creating Conceptual & Logical Data Models, Taxonomies, Ontologies, and clear integration pathways for digital products .
  • Technical Credibility & Leadership: The technical capability to confidently review code, evaluate delivery quality from Managed Service partners, and mentor external or hybrid developer squads .
  • Trend & Vendor Assessment: Strong capability to drive external tech
