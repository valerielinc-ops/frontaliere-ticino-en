Full description

Pneumonteur / tyre practitioner (a) Galliker Service AG prepares the new cars from various car manufacturers for the Swiss market. In addition, it performs order-specific series, conversion and various workshop work on behalf of importers, corporate customers, rental car and leasing companies as well as dealers. For the car brands in question, Galliker Service AG is also responsible for delivering and taking back the rental cars and leasing vehicles and carries out all kinds of workshop, car body and paint work in the service of the customers. For year-round support of our workshop team, we are looking for you as a pneumonteur / tire practitioner. Task area

  • Tyre assembly and disassembly
  • Wheel balancing
  • Performing delivery service to customer vehicles
  • Miscellaneous simple workshop work
  • Vehicle displacement of customer vehicles on the site

Requirement profile

  • Completed apprenticeship as an automotive assistant or tire practitioner
  • Self-employed, reliable, capable of teamwork, dutiful and flexible
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Driving licence Cat. B

Offer

  • Air-conditioned workshops with the most modern infrastructure
  • Always on the pulse of the latest technology and digitalization
  • Personal development opportunities in a versatile company
  • More hours can be compensated
