Full description

80-100%, Lausanne / Smart Working

Shape your future with us!

As a member of the Distribution Regional Management Team, you are actively contributing to the development of our distribution channels (Foresight, All branches and partner agencies). You ensure that general agencies have the best processes, structures, know-how and resources to ensure their sustainable growth, productivity, profitability and quality. You put the regional leaders in the orchestration and pilotage of the sales area.

Join our Distribution sector. You'll be the point of contact with our clients. Every day, you will give the best to meet their needs, by offering human advice, oriented towards action and the future, in accordance with the motto "Customer first" of AXA.

What's waiting for you

  • Developing and supervising the management of three partner agencies, managing their performance, organisation and ensuring quality
  • Conduct regional strategic and operational studies and projects (e.g. business model, IA implementation) and ensure implementation of decisions
  • Support the transformation of working methods with the integration of AI in order to develop the efficiency of agencies
  • Ensure strict application of legal and internal standards (including LSA) and act as regional legal focal point
  • Working closely with general staff, sales officials and back office to ensure quality and efficiency
  • Conduct analyses, define measures and monitor them regularly in coordination with the various internal and external actors
  • Be sparring partner of general staff for the continuous development of their skills, particularly in terms of management and financial optimization

What you bring

  • University degree (Bachelor/Master) or continuing training in insurance, with a solid knowledge of the branch
  • Excellent organizational and (data-) analytical capabilities, experience in process management or business analysis
  • Ability to structure complex topics and implement them in a solution-oriented manner with various stakeholders
  • Good understanding of sales processes in insurance and in-depth experience in or with distribution
  • High communication and facilitation skills combined with high implementation capacity
  • Passion for transformation, innovation and continuous improvement. Regular use of AI to optimize work
  • Availability for high mobility within the sales area, with regular trips throughout Switzerland.
  • French mastered in writing and oral, very good knowledge of German (oral)
  • Mastery of all current tools of the office365 suite increased

What we offer you We are convinced that satisfied employees achieve better performance. That is why we propose salary supplements that bring tangible benefits beyond the ordinary.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model), annual working time, five weeks of holidays (six weeks from 50 years), two additional weeks of holidays offered every five years, possibility to buy up to ten additional days of holidays per year

Work and family life: family-friendly meeting hours, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, subsidies for day care, company family allowances, days of leave for caring for relatives or in difficult personal situations

Insurance and insurance: an attractive pension fund offering extended overcompulsory benefits and savings options, and up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible benefits: AXA Shareplan (staff participation), CHF 500 per year on the Swibeco benefits platform, lunch lunch or lunch card, Smart Working allowance of CHF 200 per year

Mobility: half-rate subscription refund, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free electric charging station, AXA bikes to borrow

The right balance at work: free health checkups, health weeks every year, varied physical activity offers (on-line and on-site), massage offers, rest rooms, Employee Care, online health portal, various seminars and events around health prevention and mental health

Development: three days for individual training, wide range of learning and development, free coaching, financial participation in continuing training, promotion of internal mobility, offers of talent development, seminars for the over 55 (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on different themes, participation in innovative projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), two days dedicated to volunteer missions (environment and social)
