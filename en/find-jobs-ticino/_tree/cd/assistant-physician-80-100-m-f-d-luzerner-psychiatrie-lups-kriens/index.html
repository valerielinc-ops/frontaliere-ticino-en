Full description

Lucerne Psychiatry AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and is represented with its offers at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professional groups and departments are humanely and competently committed to the well-being of patients. The offers are aimed at children, adolescents and adults up to old age. Assistant doctor 60-100% (m/f/d)

Their tasks

  • Psychiatric assessments and integrated psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatments of individuals, couples and families (all medical conditions)
  • Interesting mixture of interdisciplinary complex treatment of sufferers with different psychiatric diseases and psychiatric primary care
  • Psychotherapeutic Accompaniment of IV Measures and Work Rehabilitation
  • Participation in case meetings and supervision
  • If necessary, interdisciplinary cooperation with KJPD, somatics and family doctors
  • Upon request, conduct opinions

Your profile

  • Completed medical studies (MEBEKO recognition mandatory)
  • Mind. 2 years of psychiatric experience
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Joy of independent work in a team, appreciative communication

Benefits

  • Appreciative and benevolent working environment
  • Supportive and supportive leadership culture
  • Mind. 5 weeks holidays, sabbaticals and unpaid holidays possible
  • Regular working hours excluding weekend and night services
  • Attractive internal and external training and supervision
  • Varied psychiatric-psychotherapeutic work in general psychiatric treatment setting including addiction and age psychiatry
