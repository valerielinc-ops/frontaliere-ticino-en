Full description

In line with the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", the Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950.

  • We are an innovative pioneer in the fields of ventilation devices, automated pipetting and storage of samples, as well as the development of process sensors.
  • As part of the Shared Services (SHS) business unit of Hamilton Bonaduz AG, this department assumes central responsibilities for the Hamilton companies.
  • Teams in HR, ICT, Corporate Communication, Facilities and Finance ensure that processes run smoothly and employees worldwide are optimally supported. ## Responsibilities
  • Function as the central contact person for all health and safety issues in the company – in close exchange with internal departments and external partners, authorities and expert bodies
  • Independent implementation of EKAS guideline 6508 including continuous monitoring, further development and ensuring compliance
  • Independent advice to managers and employees on all matters of occupational safety and health protection – your technical expertise is actively requested and integrated into decision-making processes
  • Comprehensive planning, coordination and implementation of all health and safety-related activities – structured, proactive and on time
  • Independent analysis of accident and incident statistics, root cause analysis and development and implementation of effective preventive measures to sustainably strengthen the safety culture
  • Organization, execution and follow-up of internal audits, inspections and controls in the field of occupational safety – including derivation, prioritization and tracking of improvement measures
  • Preparation of sound risk and hazard analyses for workplaces, equipment and hazardous substances taking into account current legal, regulatory and company requirements
  • Continuous independent further development of the safety standard through systematic measures management with a clear focus on prevention and effectiveness
  • Maintenance, further development and quality assurance of safety-related documentation – structured, traceable and audit-proof
  • Monitoring the correct storage and handling of hazardous substances in accordance with legal requirements ## Requirements
  • Completed vocational training in an industrial environment and further education in the field of occupational safety and health protection (ASGS)
  • Professional examination as ASGS specialist with Swiss federal certificate or further education as EKAS safety engineer
  • Several years of professional experience as a safety officer (SiBe) in a complex industrial environment
  • Very independent, responsible and structured way of working with high personal initiative and decision-making ability
  • Broad and in-depth knowledge of occupational safety, health protection, prevention and relevant legal and regulatory requirements
  • Pronounced leadership qualities ## What we offer
  • The opportunity to assume central responsibilities for the Hamilton companies as part of the Shared Services (SHS) business unit of Hamilton Bonaduz AG
  • Comprehensive further education and development in the field of occupational safety and health protection
  • An independent and responsible way of working with high personal initiative and decision-making ability
  • A wide range of challenges and opportunities for development and improvement of the safety culture ## Contact
