SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. At SBB Cargo, you are on the pulse of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 185,000 tons a day, relieving the burden on the road by 15,000 trucks a day and the environment by 460,000 tons of CO2 a year. In the Production division, we ensure safe and high-quality transport services in wagon load, whole train and combined freight transport. You work in a team that assumes the growing corporate responsibility in an exciting and dynamic environment and thus makes an important contribution to the success of SBB Cargo. If you are looking for regulated working hours, this job is just right for you. You work mostly from Monday to Friday between 06.00 - 18.00 and have a clear structure for your everyday life. You can move that.

  • As a shift manager: in regions, you are the contact person for employees with operational questions and contribute significantly to the smooth running of production.
  • You are responsible for the operational processing in your assigned area. You always pay attention to economic criteria and ensure optimal use of human and operational resources.
  • Compliance with regulatory requirements, such as AZG/GAV/BAR in the short term, is also your responsibility.
  • In the event of a disruption, you redefine the operational processes and authorize the operational execution of the production in your assigned area.
  • You ensure the correct inclusion of the relevant operational and commercial data in the systems of SBB Cargo as well as the billing of additional and service services.
  • In case of staff shortages, you support the team operationally and represent the team leader: in case of absences.

You bring that with you.

  • You enjoy working with people and attach importance to a respectful approach to different personalities and cultures.
  • Self-employment, responsibility and flexibility are among your strengths and are particularly in demand in this position.
  • Your pronounced digital knowledge as well as your safe handling of the working time provisions of the collective employment contract (GAV) and the Working Time Act (AZG) round off your profile.
  • In exceptional cases, you bring the willingness to work on weekends.
  • You bring extensive experience in railway operations and have in-depth knowledge of the shunting processes.
  • In German you communicate in a negotiated manner (level C1).

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
