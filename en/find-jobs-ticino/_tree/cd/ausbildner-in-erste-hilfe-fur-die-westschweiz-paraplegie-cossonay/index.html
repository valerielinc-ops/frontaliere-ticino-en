Full description

The role of first responders is becoming increasingly important. It helps to react promptly and provides important time until the rescue workers are on site. The first aid section of SIRMED offers first aid courses in accordance with the IVR level system – both in the form of basic training and as a refresher.

Tasks: • Training our versatile customers – e.g. company paramedics, police officers or medical practice assistants – in the field of first aid • Development, planning and organisation of further education and training with a focus on layman training • Ongoing evaluation and derivation of necessary measures Requirements • Completed training as a dipl. Rescue paramedic HF or transport paramedic BP (Alternative: Experience in acute medicine as a dipl.) Nursing specialist HF/FH, ideally with postgraduate studies in anesthesia, intensive care or emergency care • Completed pedagogical qualifications, such as SVEB 1, or willingness to train in this regard • Open and team capable personality with pleasure in a varied educational work • High flexibility and load capacity • Enjoy working in the field We offer: You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits. SIRMED, the Swiss Institute for Rescue Medicine, is a subsidiary of the Swiss Paraplegic Foundation and the Swiss Rescue Air Force Rega. As a market leader in the training for first aid as well as emergency and rescue medicine, SIRMED offers practical courses in all national languages and English. These take place in Nottwil and at the clients on site. At the headquarters, SIRMED operates state-of-the-art simulation rooms. SIRMED is also a Higher School of Applied Sciences that trains HF and transport paramedics. The institute employs about 45 permanent employees and about 300 freelancers.
