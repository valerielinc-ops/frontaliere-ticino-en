University Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

The ambulatory crisis intervention (AKI) is attached to various outpatient clinics of the University Hospital for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (KJP) of the Universitarian Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ). For our offer at the locations Bern and Burgdorf, we are looking for a committed assistant psychologist. Our interdisciplinary teams support children and adolescents in mental crises with intensive, time-limited treatment in the outpatient setting. The offer may be preceded by further treatment in the outpatient, day clinic or inpatient setting. The aim is to improve the predicted treatment success, shorten treatment times and bridge possible waiting times until further treatment begins. As an assistant psychologist, you will have the opportunity to accompany children and adolescents as well as their families in phases of crisis escalation, to help shape individual treatment processes and to further develop your clinical skills in crisis intervention. You work closely with our nursing and pedagogical team: While you carry out outpatient psychological appointments, visiting contacts by employees of the nursing and pedagogical team complement the treatment in the patient’s living environment. You will get to know different mental illnesses and participate in the diagnostic assessment and psychotherapeutic treatment. You will actively contribute your psychological expertise and work closely with specialists from different professional groups as well as with internal and external cooperation partners. As a university clinic, we combine clinical practice, teaching and research. Clinicians and scientists work closely together to ensure evidence-based and high-quality treatment of our patients. Do you want to support children and adolescents in psychological crises, develop viable perspectives together with an interdisciplinary team and deepen your therapeutic skills in a dynamic clinical environment? Are you interested in intensive, closely coordinated treatment that is oriented towards the individual needs of patients? Become part of our team!

Your tasks

  • You work in an interdisciplinary treatment team and actively contribute your psychological expertise to the treatment
  • You accompany children and adolescents in crisis situations as part of a high-frequency, time-limited treatment in the outpatient setting and carry out psychotherapeutic interventions under professional supervision
  • You work closely with the different departments of our clinic
  • You will maintain cooperation with external institutions such as KESB, homes, schools and other specialist bodies
  • You recruit patients for scientific studies and gain insight into clinical-scientific questions
  • You take part in internal and external training, such as lectures, conferences, seminars, courses and training courses as well as subject-specific supervisions

Your profile

  • You have a master’s degree in psychology and are in a recognised psychotherapy training
  • You bring practical experience in the clinical-psychological field, experience in outpatient psychotherapy is beneficial
  • You are interested in working with children, adolescents and their families and enjoy a versatile clinical activity
  • Evidence-based work is important to you and you are interested in combining clinical activity with scientific questions
  • You work independently, responsibly and reflectively and at the same time appreciate the close cooperation in an interdisciplinary team
  • You are flexible and open to different clinical tasks and actively contribute to the further development of your professional skills
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
