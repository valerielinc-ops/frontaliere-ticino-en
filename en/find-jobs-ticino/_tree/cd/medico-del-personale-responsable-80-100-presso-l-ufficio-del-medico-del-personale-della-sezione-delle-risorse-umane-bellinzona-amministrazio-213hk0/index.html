This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • management of the Office of the Staff Doctor
  • assessment of the capacity/workability, functional limitations and resources of state employees (employees and teachers)
  • analysis of medical certificates of employees, management of cases of absence greater than 30 continuous days and special cases reported by the executive officer or by the attending physician, authorization of absences for physiotherapy/rehabilitative hearing/medical visits
  • assessment of medical suitability for the function of candidates for various public competitions
  • collaboration in the management of cases involving social insurance (injury insurance, military insurance and invalidity insurance) and the relevant advice
  • collaboration within the multidisciplinary team management of Human Resources absences within the Case management
  • member of the working group management absences of the Administrative Section of the Department of Education of Culture and Sports
  • advice on the management of health issues of employees
  • assessments in the field of work medicine
  • participation in health promotion projects at work and promoting a healthy working climate

Requirements

  • Federal diploma as doctor or equivalent qualification MEBEKO
  • authorization to exercise the medical profession
  • FMH title in general internal medicine, work medicine or psychiatry with multi-year clinical experience in hospitals or Swiss private studio
  • interest in medicine and insurance medicine
  • SVG/SSMC certificate of peritone SIM and/or medical confidence
  • good knowledge of the Swiss territory and health and social system
  • good synthesis and editorial skills in Italian and verbal and written knowledge of a second official language (German or French)

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

7 October 2026
