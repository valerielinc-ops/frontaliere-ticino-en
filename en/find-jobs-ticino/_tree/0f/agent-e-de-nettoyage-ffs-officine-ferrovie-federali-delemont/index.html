Cleaning agent 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cleaning agent Delémont, 01.02.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

The Facility Management Immobilier is the competent service of the SBB, active throughout Switzerland, with regard to cleanliness and the elimination of graffiti. With the maintenance of public facilities, we support the image and main activity of public transport. What you can move.

  • As a professional cleaning agent you contribute to the satisfaction of our customers by working independently to clean and dispose of waste at stations and other assigned objects.
  • You answer for cleanliness, order, and the elimination of vandalism so that our customers feel a sense of security.
  • You follow the procedures, working methods and cleaning protocols in performing the tasks.
  • You assume responsibility for the proper use and daily maintenance of cleaning and working machines and use them in accordance with the requirements.
  • In case of questions, you are here to advise SBB customers in and around the station.

What you bring.

  • A B/BE driving licence.
  • An AFP or SWC as a cleanliness officer or operating officer is ideally required for this position but not mandatory.
  • You stand out as an honest, trustworthy and reliable person. You demonstrate customer orientation, team spirit and manual skills.
  • You are dynamic and proactive in nature, in good physical condition and able to bear loads (carry and lift weights, work frequently standing, walking and outdoors). You show flexibility.
  • You have basic knowledge of digital work tools (smartphones) and ability to communicate with the applications used (Outlook, Teams, etc.).
  • You have at least a B1 level in French (certificate presentation of an official school in Switzerland to justify). You meet the safety requirements (RTE20100 Ssta exam) and medical fitness criteria

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency.
