Full description

Pensum: 100%

Are you interested in getting started in the changing energy industry? Together with Axpo, drive forward a climate-friendly future! ⚡

The trainee program allows you to immerse yourself in three different departments and build up your know-how. Take responsibility for new projects every six months and bring in innovative solutions.

The optimal start for your professional future! The traineeship starts on 1 April 2027 and is limited to 18 months with the option of continued employment.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • With your technical know-how, you contribute to secure electricity production and supply at Axpo. 🔌
  • You work on operational and/or strategic tasks in our teams.
  • You work independently on projects in the fields of electricity production, distribution and trade and take responsibility from day one.
  • Start your first assignment in the Mechanical Engineering department in the Hydroenergy & Biomass division, where you can ...
  • numerical models of power plant plants - consisting of driving water system, shut-off devices, hydraulic and electric machines - with the program "Simsen" of the EPFL and develop the theoretical foundations,
  • perform stationary calculations of the operating states and transient pressure surge calculations,
  • develop the basis for the implementation of the programme and the necessary libraries (modules),
  • ... and actively supports the mechanical engineering team in other topics!
  • You can choose the second and third assignment independently within Axpo Group (including our subsidiary CKW).

Profile & Skills

  • Master's degree 🎓 in engineering with a focus on mechanical engineering or comparable degree (degree within the last two years)
  • Knowledge of general mechanical engineering, fluid machines and fluid mechanics.
  • Initial experience with numerical calculation methods such as CFD, FEM or similar methods.
  • Possible first professional experiences e.g. as an intern:in or working student:in
  • Good analytical thinking, proactivity, independence and interest in interdisciplinary methods
  • Enjoyment in the application of numerical calculation programs and process technology
  • Negotiable in German and English

About the team

You will be part of the 21st generation of trainees. Meet for various events, such as lunches or visits to power plants. You work in an international, open and collaborative working atmosphere and make a valuable contribution to the future.

The innovative environment encourages us to contribute our own ideas, to further expand our knowledge of the energy industry and to achieve great things together. Happiness

You work in Baden and have the opportunity to work flexibly from home and benefit from attractive employee conditions. More details about the traineeship can be found [here](https://www.axpo.com/ch/en/men-and-career/student-and-university graduates.html).
