Are you interested in the fascinating world of management of sinisters and want to acquire a valuable practical experience during studies? Are you looking for an activity that allows you to collaborate on the management of concrete cases, taking responsibility immediately?

As a Medical Claims intern, you will join the Sinistri area of Zurich Switzerland, the Medical Claims team, and actively contribute to our common success. You will support the team in medical and hospital civil liability management, examine left notifications, prepare the necessary documentation for the evaluation of the practice and manage the dossiers with the support of our experts. You will also be in contact with customers and counterparts, as well as supporting the team in administrative activities and projects. A motivated team is waiting for you, oriented to collaboration and customers, which will offer you the opportunity to grow both professionally and personally.

What awaits you in this role

  • Support our team in the management of medical claims and contribute to efficient and correct settlement of requests.
  • Examine left incoming notifications and prepare the necessary documentation for subsequent management.
  • Clear open issues with customers and performance providers, ensuring smooth management of cases.
  • Collabori to the optimization of internal processes and proponi ideas to increase efficiency.
  • Do administrative tasks and support the team in current projects.

What makes you stand out

  • You are enrolled in a law degree program at a Swiss university for the entire duration of employment.
  • Nutritional interest in the management of sinisters; a first experience in a customer-oriented context is an advantage.
  • You communicate clearly and like to relate to people.
  • Work in a structured and accurate way, keeping a whole vision even in the most dynamic situations.
  • You have an excellent knowledge of Italian; knowledge of French and/or German is an advantage.
