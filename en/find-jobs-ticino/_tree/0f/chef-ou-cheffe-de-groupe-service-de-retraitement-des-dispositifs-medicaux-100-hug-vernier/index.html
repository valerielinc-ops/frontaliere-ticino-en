Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to discover the highlights of 2025 by clicking here . The post is attached to the Department of Operations. The mission of this department is to ensure the stewardship and logistics necessary for the proper functioning of all HUGs, in the restoration, cleaning and disinfection of premises and hygiene of linen. It also manages the medical devices reprocessing service, the biomedical service and equipment as well as Cressy-Santé. More than 1'300 professionals and professionals carry out their day-to-day missions in constant demand, with a sense of service and environmental responsibility. Within the medical device reprocessing department of approximately 90 people, under the responsibility of the management team, your mission is to ensure the reprocessing of medical devices as a medical device technologist.

Description of post

Under the responsibility of the chief of production, you are part of a team of group leaders, sharing the management of the 70 medical devices technologists in the department as soon as they take up their positions and on a daily basis. You arrange and ensure the reprocessing of medical devices for all services of the HUG (Operational Blocks, Care Services or any other in-house client) and external clients based on your customer-oriented attitude. You coordinate the management of medical devices according to good sterilization practices, priorities and emergencies for operating units and all clients. You know how to analyze situations, decide, work in autonomy and team to optimize the use of technical means in relation to priorities and needs. Sensitive to the patient's presence and hospital constraints, you supervise and enforce the procedures and protocols set out in the service quality assurance system and all good professional practices. Your sense of organization and planning ensures that you take care of medical devices according to the resources and needs of the various sectors while ensuring that the production needs are balanced with the well-being of employees. Of a positive, optimistic and listening nature, you motivate and accompany your collaborators by developing team spirit. You maintain a climate of partnership to proactively manage complex situations involving several professional actors and actresses. You are rigorous and you manage with respect and gratitude while being fair. You ensure that you provide regular feedback to develop your team's skills. You update the knowledge of your collaborators and your colleagues about the reprocessing of medical devices through continuous training and congresses.

Qualifications

You have the ideal profile if you are a medical device technologist CFC holder or in possession of the STE 2 with a 5-year experience in the reprocessing of medical devices including 1 year in the hospital field, supplemented by training in management with a minimum experience of 2 years in the supervision of a team. You have an exemplary attitude and embody institutional values (trust, respect, team spirit, recognition, equality & inclusion). You easily use Excel and Word desktop software and, ideally, you have a good experience with GPAO software. You have a very good command of the French language, both oral and written.

Further information

This position requires irregular schedules, the medical device reprocessing service being operational 24 hours a day and 365 days a year. The group leader activity will take place at the Cluse-Roseraie and Vernier sites throughout the service's deployment period, until 2027.
