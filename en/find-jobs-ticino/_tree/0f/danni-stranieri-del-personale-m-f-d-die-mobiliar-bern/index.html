Full description

This is what awaits you

XpertCenter AG, a company of the Mobiliar Group, supports and advises customers on damages in the areas of motor business, anti-abuse, regress management, case management and foreign damages.

This is what they do with us

They are responsible for the comprehensive processing of demanding claims (in particular material damage) of all liability and motor vehicle industries as well as for their final regulation in German and Italian. In doing so, you conduct intensive investigations and negotiations and are in telephone or written contact with our policyholders and/or partner companies, victims, their legal representatives and other claimants.

They also ensure the enforcement of private insurance recourses abroad and in Switzerland.

The support of the claims services of the management and general agencies in technical matters as well as the intensive contact with our partners abroad are also part of the versatile field of activity.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed basic training in the insurance industry, completed federal law. Professional ID card of private insurance is a plus
  • Professional experience in the claims department as well as good knowledge of liability law
  • Independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Team-oriented and flexible personality
  • Mother tongue German or Italian with very good knowledge of the other language in word and writing, other languages are advantageous

Working with us

A well-established and motivated team is waiting for you, divided between the locations of Bern, Nyon, Zurich-Oerlikon and Bellinzona and consisting of clerks, specialists and damage inspectors. Together they live a committed, solution-oriented and communicative way of working in a dynamic environment and at eye level.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

Our family allowances are based on the highest cantonal contributions of the cantons of Bern, Vaud and Zurich.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
