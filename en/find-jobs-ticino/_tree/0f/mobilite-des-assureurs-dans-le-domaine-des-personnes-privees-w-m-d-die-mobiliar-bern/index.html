Full description

That's what you expect: As an underwriter or underwriter mobility, you are part of a team that examines and assesses risks related to motor vehicle and travel insurance. This is what you do with us: Your main job is to support our general agencies in technical issues in underwriting for motor vehicle and travel insurance. You check, judge, decide independently and process the correspondence in these cases such as risk testing, discounting and individual cases.

  • In addition, you check and calculate special premium bills for private and large customer policies and transfer the inputs in the accounting system.
  • After your initial training, you have the opportunity to visit internal specialist trainings and the further development of processes and internal systems. That's what you bring:
  • Complete basic training in the insurance industry, preferably with certificate as an insurance specialist or business training
  • Multiannual professional experience in insurance, in particular in motor vehicle insurance
  • Controlling Microsoft Office tools and other IT tools
  • Strong negotiation skills and solution-oriented as well as analytical and conceptual skills
  • Team-oriented, committed and empathic personality
  • Bilingual or native French with very good German skills, Italian skills are a plus Working with us: A well-known team of underwriters awaits you. Together they maintain a dedicated, solution-oriented and communicative working environment. The department language is German.
