Working together for more safety in crafts

In Product Management at International, we design ranges that make the everyday work of craftsmen and women safer, easier and more efficient. As Product Manager (a) for Occupational Safety Products, you take responsibility for a diverse range and develop it together with international teams.

You combine market understanding, product competence and entrepreneurial thinking. They work independently, contribute their own ideas and move at the interface of procurement, quality, marketing and sales. Look forward to a varied task with creative leeway, short decision paths and numerous possibilities to develop professionally and personally.

Product Manager (a) for occupational safety products

Your duties:

  • They are responsible for and develop a range in the field of occupational safety – including products for hand, eyes, breath and face protection as well as for fall protection.
  • They observe the market, understand the needs of our customers and derive potential for existing and new products.
  • They accompany the development and introduction of new products – from the first idea to the market launch.
  • Together with internal and external partners, they ensure that our products meet the quality standards as well as the relevant legal and normative requirements.
  • They coordinate the necessary documentation while keeping an eye on quality, deadlines and cost-effectiveness.
  • Greater product launches Together with project teams from purchasing, marketing, sales and other specialist areas.
  • They support supplier negotiations, the creation of marketing and sales documents as well as product training.
  • You are available to our internal contacts as a competent point of contact for questions about your range.

This is what you bring with you:

  • They are interested in occupational safety and enjoy familiarizing themselves with technical, legal and application-related issues.
  • Ideally, they bring with them experience with occupational safety products – for example from the application, development, production, procurement or distribution. Another professional background with a high willingness to learn is also welcome.
  • Experience in product management, project management or in a comparable interface function is an advantage.
  • You enjoy working on the connection between procurement, product development and sales and have suitable training or training – or comparable practical experience.
  • They take responsibility, work in a structured manner and make decisions with a good eye for customer needs and economic contexts.
  • They like to tackle, actively promote topics and reliably complete tasks.
  • They enjoy working in a team and understand how to bring different interests together.
  • They communicate safely in German and English
  • Other language skills are a plus.
