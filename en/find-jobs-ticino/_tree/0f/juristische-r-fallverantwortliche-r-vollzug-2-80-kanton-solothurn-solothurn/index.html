Full description

The Prison Office (AJUV) is responsible for the practical and administrative execution of penalties and measures. Do you want to contribute to public security by planning and designing a resource- and risk-oriented sanctions enforcement? For the Department of Penal and Implementation of Measures, we are looking for a legal person responsible for cases 2, 80% at the Solothurn location as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • As a legal person responsible for the case, you plan, organize, coordinate and monitor enforcement in the area of serious sexual and violent delinquency (medium to very high risk of recidivism).
  • They develop and review the enforcement planning and ensure the implementation of necessary risk assessments as well as the ongoing legal prognostic evaluation of new incoming information.
  • The interdisciplinary exchange with law enforcement institutions, probation assistance, therapists and experts, among others, is ensured, conducts law enforcement meetings and is in telephone, written and personal contact with the convicted persons, legal representatives, authorities and, if necessary, victims.
  • They develop and take responsibility for enforcement decisions (e.g. implementation, easing of enforcement, transfers) and implement them.
  • Further information They hand over motions such as sanctions amendment, restitution, extension of measures as well as statements (e.g. in appeal proceedings) to the court and represent the department in subsequent proceedings in court.

Your profile

  • You have a law degree (at best with a lawyer's patent) and a forensic additional qualification or are ready to acquire it.
  • Experience in the prison system or in criminal law (execution authority, prison, criminal prosecution, court, etc.) is advantageous.
  • Shape the dynamic day-to-day implementation between the reading and analysis of court records, enforcement reports and expert opinions as well as telephone agreements and visits to enforcement institutions Thanks to your independent working method, you are self-responsible and flexible.
  • Your confident appearance and high social skills will benefit you both in direct contact with convicted persons and in interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Put your reliable, accurate and forward working method You specifically enter when writing enforcement orders, orders and submissions to the competent court.
