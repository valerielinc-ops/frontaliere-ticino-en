Full description

Rapelli has been a Tessiner Salumi tradition since 1929 and is known for high-quality products made from selected ingredients with a clear focus on quality, tradition and innovation. With the brands «Rapelli» and «Ticinella», the company is regarded as a culinary ambassador of Ticino in Switzerland. Rapelli is part of the ORIOR Group, an internationally active Swiss Food & Beverage company, which is listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange and focuses on high-quality specialities with strong regional anchoring. In a context of further development and strengthening within the group, Rapelli pursues a clear focus on performance, efficiency and enhanced cooperation at group level. In this context, we are looking for a personality as a personality within the framework of a follow-up regulation. Chief Financial Officer, 100% (m/w/d) They assume the strategic and operational responsibility for finance, controlling and administration and report directly to the CEO. In addition, you act as a central interface to the Group CFO at the headquarters in Zurich. The CFO supports the CEO in managing the Rapelli with the aim of achieving a steady increase in value. In particular, the CFO focuses on cost management and the improvement of efficiency along the entire P&L. Your main tasks

  • General responsibility for finance, controlling, accounting and administration

  • Creation and consolidation of monthly and annual accounts according to Swiss GAAP FER.
  • Analysis of costs, margins and production services.

  • Leadership and Promotion of Finance & Administration Team
