Full description

Portrait The Coordination Office for Special Support Köniz, KSK ensures that children and young people with special needs receive appropriate support in the regular schools of the municipality of Köniz. We network special education measures, bring together expertise and advocate for fair educational opportunities for all. At Wangental Juch and Niederwangen, we are looking for a dedicated speech therapist to replace the position holder during a maternity leave.

Responsibilities

  • – Substitute speech therapy. The whole substitution is 15 lessons, but full individual (half) days are also possible.
  • – You will carry out therapies with children who have language abnormalities, learning difficulties in the area of language or developmental delays.
  • – You will work directly on site in an interdisciplinary team with teachers, IF teachers, the school management and school social work.
  • – Duration: from 16. November to 24. December

Requirements

  • – A diploma in speech therapy (EDK-recognized or equivalent from German-speaking countries). Please no applications from non-speech therapists. A bridging solution is underway.
  • – Enjoyment of working with children and in-depth specialist knowledge to support individual linguistic development processes in a targeted manner – creatively, diagnostically sound and with a view to the whole.
  • – Desire for exchange, teamwork and a working environment in which your experience is valued.

We offer

  • – A warm, supportive staff and a co-school management on site who thinks along and supports.
  • – Professional support from the other speech therapists in the community as well as from the management of the coordination office for special support KSK.
  • – easily accessible by public transport
