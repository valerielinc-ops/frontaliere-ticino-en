Full description

Start by appointment as a nurse / nursing specialist Intermediate Care 40 - 100%

Our IMC at the Walenstadt site with six beds supervises interdisciplinary cases from internal medicine and surgery. The focus is on the monitoring and treatment of seriously ill and complex patients. The location Walenstadt, nestled between mountains and lake, impresses with a family atmosphere, a future-oriented workplace and very good connections to public transport.

Would you like to join our dedicated, established and warm team? Perfket, then you are exactly right with us!

These are your tasks

  • Competently supervise complex and unstable care situations, react quickly and professionally in acute situations and initiate immediate measures
  • You bear joint responsibility for the resuscitation service throughout the hospital and the interdisciplinary cooperation
  • You support patients and relatives in difficult situations

You bring that with you

  • A degree at tertiary level as a nurse / nurse specialist HF/FH or an equivalent diploma recognised by the SRK as well as several years of experience in the acute hospital
  • A post-diploma certificate of supervision care or willingness to acquire it
  • An independent, efficient and reliable working method with very good observation skills, a high sense of responsibility and pleasure in working together in a family team

Can you do something that others can’t? You make the difference? Then you belong to us!

  • Flexible working hours / Home Office / Part-time work: If the company allows it, working hours and expenses can be flexibly tailored to your current living situation.
  • Gastronomic offer: All four locations of the cantonal hospital Graubünden offer a wide range of catering options. With us you can eat varied, balanced and healthy.

  • Our values: The excellent quality of our services and the health and satisfaction of our employees are equally our focus.
