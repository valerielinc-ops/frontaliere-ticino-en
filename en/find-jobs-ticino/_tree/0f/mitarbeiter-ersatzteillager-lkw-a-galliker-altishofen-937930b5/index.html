Employee spare parts warehouse for trucks (a) From screwing to the office – your knowledge remains in demand! You don't just know trucks by sight, but also know what happens under the hood? Have you worked as a truck, agricultural machinery or car mechanic and would like to develop professionally? Or are you an experienced truck driver who knows the technology of his vehicles inside out and is up for a new challenge? Then your next step could be from the workshop or the street to the office. To complement our team, we are looking for you - with immediate effect or by appointment - to support our truck spare parts warehouse (a).

Responsibilities

  • Management of the spare parts warehouse
  • Storage and retrieval as well as the management of goods
  • Picking and processing of workshop orders
  • Posting of all movements on the service and repair orders
  • Supporting and advising workshop and paint shop employees

Requirements profile

  • Trained truck, agricultural machinery or car mechanic or
  • Experienced and tech-savvy truck driver
  • Thorough knowledge of vehicle technology trucks
  • Resilient and flexible
  • Team player
  • Very good written and spoken German skills

Offer

  • Brand diversity with varied activities
  • Always at the cutting edge of the latest technology and digitalization
  • Personal development opportunities in a versatile company
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Free employee parking
