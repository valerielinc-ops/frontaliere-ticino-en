Full description

Tasks

  • Ensuring an appropriate and professional as well as excellent nursing care as well as advice and instruction of our patients

Requirements

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nurse HF/FH in an acute hospital
  • Several years of professional experience are desirable, but young professionals are also welcome
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing

  • Joy in the profession and the willingness to maintain an empathetic relationship with the patients entrusted to you

We offer

  • Strong values and actively lived corporate culture
  • Characterisation and participation of the Claraspital by the employees

The Claraspital, the largest private hospital in Basel, is a university teaching and research hospital with a full service mandate for the highly specialized abdominal surgery and supra-regional broadcast of its centers "Bäu" and "Tumor" as well as its special fields. In the Claraspital, employees find jobs where they can develop both professionally and humanly. At Claraspital, we attach great importance to consciously nurturing and developing our corporate culture and actively living the cultural criteria developed together "together - competently - responsibly".
