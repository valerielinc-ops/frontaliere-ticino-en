Full description

The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital with more than 2,000 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care for patients, embodying the institution's values of authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation.

In order to complete our team of occupational therapists on the website of Monthey, for an indefinite period, we are looking for a:

Ergotherapist M/F at 80%, with experience in the field of geriatric and acute geriatric rehabilitation.

The main tasks of this post are

  • Ensuring the quality and continuity of stationary therapies in rehabilitation
  • To cooperate in the organization of work and in the smooth running of the service's activities
  • Participate in the supervision and evaluation of students and trainees
  • Contribute to an interdisciplinary approach to care and teamwork
  • Promote occupational therapy within and outside the institution

We ask

  • A Bachelor of Science HES-SO in Occupational Therapy or a title deemed equivalent
  • A full recognition by the Swiss Red Cross for foreign titles (cases without recognition or in the process of recognition will not be considered).
  • A willingness to acquire and develop new skills
  • An open and dynamic personality, wishing to integrate into the team already in place and to collaborate with the professionals of the various services

We offer

The HRC offers great job opportunities and career development in all its fields of activity: medical, nursing, medico-technical, hotel, administrative, technical, logistics. In a desire for continuous improvement, skills development is at the heart of the HRC's concerns. The institution offers its employees access to training both internally and externally.
