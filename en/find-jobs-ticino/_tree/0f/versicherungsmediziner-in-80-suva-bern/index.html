Full description

You like to move at the interface of medicine, law and insurance and want to combine your medical expertise with complex insurance questions? Then you are right with us. What you do

  • You create well-founded insurance medical assessments based on files or medical examinations.
  • You assess medical issues and answer questions about causality, working ability, reasonableness and integrity damage.
  • You advise the staff of the case management on insurance medical issues and support them in complex cases.
  • You work closely with insurance specialists: in different disciplines and bring your expertise across locations.
  • You will be involved in the further development of underwriting processes, methods and assessment practices and thus contribute to the quality and consistency of our assessments. What you bring
  • You have a specialist title, preferably in orthopaedics or trauma surgery, and have at least 5 years of clinical experience.
  • You are interested in insurance issues and are ready to further develop your medical expertise in this field.
  • You appreciate the professional exchange and actively contribute your expertise to interdisciplinary discussions.
  • You convince through your reliable, independent and careful working method.
  • You have knowledge of German at the native language level. Knowledge of French or Italian is an advantage.
