More about this search for cross-border applicants

Davos, nestled in the heart of the Swiss Alps, offers a unique job market for those seeking mountain-related employment. With its world-renowned ski resorts and breathtaking landscapes, Davos is a hub for tourism, hospitality, and outdoor recreation industries. The town's economy thrives on seasonal and year-round positions, catering to both locals and the influx of tourists. For cross-border workers, Davos presents opportunities in various sectors, with the added benefit of being close to the Italian border, facilitating commuting. The job market here is diverse, ranging from ski instructors and hotel staff to event organizers and retail workers, making it an attractive destination for job seekers looking to combine their love for the mountains with a fulfilling career.
