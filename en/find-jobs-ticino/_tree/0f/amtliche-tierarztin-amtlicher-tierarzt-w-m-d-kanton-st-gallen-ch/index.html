Full description

With its four offices and five services and its approximately 200 employees, the Department of Health provides for the protection, promotion and preservation of human and animal health. This includes ensuring a high level of basic medical care thanks to optimal hospital planning in the field of acute somatics and psychiatry, health prevention and consumer protection and veterinary services. The Office for Consumer Protection and Veterinary Affairs is responsible for the veterinary service and food and chemical control in the canton of St.Gallen.

What to expect

  • They are jointly responsible for meat control in the large slaughterhouses in the canton of St.Gallen; in particular in the Rinder-Grossschlachtbetrieb in St.Gallen. There are also operations in the pig slaughterhouse in Bazenheid and in the poultry slaughterhouse in Staad.
  • With the management on site and the team, you ensure the ante-mortem and meat inspection and supervise compliance with regulations in the areas of meat hygiene, animal welfare, animal diseases and disposal.
  • They order the necessary measures, ensure disease readiness and work closely with the food safety, animal welfare and animal health departments in the veterinary service. They are in close contact with those responsible for slaughterhouses.
  • Start of work outside normal office hours

What sets it apart

  • Completed study of veterinary medicine
  • Certificate of competence as Official Veterinarian or willingness to acquire it
  • Experience in livestock practice and in the field of meat control and food safety
  • Several years of professional experience in the veterinary sector
  • Solution-oriented working method
  • Good communication skills
  • Safe appearance and good enforcement
  • Readiness to start work early outside of usual office hours

What we offer

A demanding and responsible task in the focus of food safety, animal health and animal welfare. A dedicated team, regular training opportunities and attractive employment conditions.

Contact: For additional information, please contact Dr. Adrian Fäh, Head of the Department of Meat, Milk and Veterinary Medicine, Tel. 058 229 28 27. Further information can be found at www.avsv.sg.ch .
