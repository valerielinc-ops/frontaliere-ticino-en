Full description

Do you enjoy daily contact with people and like to provide advice and sell? Then a training in retail trade is exactly what it does for you! In the three years of basic training you will acquire the necessary tools to successfully enter the diverse world of retail at the Post. Your training site will be in Lugano or Chiasso.

Your training

  • You will be the Post Office business card and you will work daily in contact with our customers, to whom you will advise on the entire range of physical and digital products, the door and the sales surface.
  • You will perform varied and interesting tasks: acceptance of letters and packages, payments and payments, sale of products and services or provision of advice oriented to your needs.
  • The post is increasingly positioned as a service centre for partner companies such as insurance, banks and sick cases: in the course of your function you will have the opportunity to gain further knowledge of the various sectors.
  • The offer in the branches includes additional products and services related to issues such as home, gifts and experiences: you will be a key figure when supporting national and regional campaigns and creating diverse shopping experiences for customers.
  • You will have the opportunity to acquire and deepen your professional operational skills within a team and across different branches.
  • During internal and inter-company lessons you will relate the theoretical notions to the experiences acquired in practice.

Your skills

We are looking for young and enthusiastic people, positive and motivated to make a difference with their actions, who are able to become passionate about innovations and who meet the following requirements:

  • have concluded the average level of compulsory school with good results
  • you are flown for numbers and easily understand money management
  • you like contact with people and you are able to properly express yourself in written and oral form in different situations
  • a good organization and the sense of order are among the qualities that distinguish you
  • you are an open, reliable and discreet person
  • you like to convey your specialist knowledge
