Full description

In the Ambulatorium for Hematology and Oncology in Brugg, you accompany patients in medically demanding situations. They lead the MPA team, coordinate the practice operations and work themselves in medical and administrative tasks. Even if a lot is running at the same time, keep the overview. The position is available as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • They lead the MPA team professionally and in terms of personnel, are responsible for the personnel planning of the MPAs and promote good cooperation.
  • Together with the center coordination and the medical management of the Brugg site, they organize and coordinate the practice operations including the consultation hours and ensure smooth processes.
  • They receive and look after patients, answer telephone inquiries and perform administrative tasks.
  • They perform capillary blood sampling, operate the laboratory equipment and are responsible for quality controls.
  • They write ECGs, assist in bone marrow punctures and prepare the appropriate examinations.
  • They work closely with the medical profession and other professional groups and support the further development of the processes.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a medical assistant (MPA).
  • You have several years of professional experience as an MPA, ideally in an ambulatory or in oncology/hematology.
  • You already have experience in a senior position or would like to take the next step into team management.
  • They carry out blood sampling independently and safely and have experience in handling laboratory equipment.
  • They are familiar with digital practice and documentation systems.
  • You organize your day-to-day work independently, set priorities and keep an overview even with high work density.
  • They communicate clearly and appreciatively with employees, patients and the medical profession.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
