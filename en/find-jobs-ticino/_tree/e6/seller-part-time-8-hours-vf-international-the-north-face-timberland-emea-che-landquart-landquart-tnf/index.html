Apply now and take the chance to become part of a great team and take responsibility for providing our customers with an unforgettable shopping experience!

For our The North Face team in Landquart we are looking for an enthusiastic saleswoman part-time (8 hours).

As a salesperson, you inspire our customers, provide outstanding customer service and ensure that our sales room meets the highest standards.

The North Face belongs to the VF family. With our family of renowned brands with cult factor, the international clothing company VF connects people with the lifestyles, activities and experiences they love. At the heart of our work is our goal: We power movements of sustainable and active lifestyles for the betterment of people and our planet. That is our objective. That’s why we come to work every day. It unites us and motivates us to pursue our plans together. That is our calling.

The North Face is the world’s leading outdoor and adventure brand. We spread curiosity. We dare extraordinary things. We form communities. And we lead with integrity. We are a community of explorers and constantly looking for new ideas, places and people to connect with.

About the position

Our salespeople have a great opportunity to reach their full potential. With us, you support an international company and are affirmed to assume responsibility at branch level.

They make the difference

We expect our sellers to provide an unforgettable shopping experience by:

- Deliver first-class customer service and thus ensure respectful treatment of all customers

- Know exactly about the products offered in the store

- Maintain merchandising standards in accordance with brand guidelines

- Always up-to-date on the latest trends and current brand positioning within the market

- Compliance with all operational processes in the branch and in the storage room

What makes you the perfect salesperson?

- They love direct customer contact. With your hands-on mentality, enthusiasm and passion for creating a fantastic customer experience, you lead by example to the rest of the team.

- You have a positive, proactive attitude and take care of every situation in the store

- You have a passion for our brand, fashion and retail in general

- You have excellent written and oral communication skills in German and English

- They are flexible and would also work on weekends, public holidays and in the evening hours if necessary

- Experience is great, but not required. We make it important that you live and demonstrate our guiding principles: Live dutifully, act courageously and empathetically, be curious and remain steadfast.

This is what awaits you

With us you will receive the full service package.

Most companies advertise with competitive salary, a fantastic bonus, retirement savings and employee discounts (by the way: we have 50%!). We offer all of this. Just differently. In addition to all this, we offer you:

- Career responsibility that allows you to build your knowledge and experience across different brands

- A supportive, feedback-based work culture where respect and integrity are our guides

- Tailored training. From induction to ongoing training online and in presence, we strive to support you in your professional and personal development as best we can.

- The opportunity to network with other store managers across Europe and our headquarters

- Participation in the referral programme

- To support our employees, we offer a mental health program

Free development

Our ambition is to foster a culture of belonging based on respect, connection, openness and authenticity. We are committed to building and maintaining a workplace that celebrates the diversity of our employees so that they can be themselves during their work.

VF is an employer committed to equal opportunities, the promotion and support of disadvantaged groups by creating equal opportunities for both VF employees and applicants.
