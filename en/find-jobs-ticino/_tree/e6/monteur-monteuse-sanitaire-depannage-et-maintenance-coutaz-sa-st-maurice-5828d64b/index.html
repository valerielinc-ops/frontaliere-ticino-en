Full description

Do you like finding solutions, acting independently and being in direct contact with customers? For you, a successful troubleshooting involves as much the technical quality of the intervention as it does an impeccable customer service?

We are looking for a sanitary installer specialized in troubleshooting and maintenance, 100%, to strengthen our team.

Your Responsibilities

  • Outpatient care and health interventions with our clients
  • Search for faults and diagnostics of installations
  • Repair, maintenance and maintenance of sanitary facilities
  • Replacement of appliances, valves and sanitary equipment
  • Small transformation and renovation works
  • Self-organisation of mandated interventions
  • Information and advice to clients on the work to be performed
  • Transmission of information necessary for administrative and technical follow-up of interventions
  • Corporate representation with our customers

Profile sought

  • SWC installer or solid experience in the field
  • Experience in troubleshooting and maintenance highly appreciated
  • Very good ability to work independently
  • Ability to find faults and solve problems
  • Excellent customer contact, sense of service and good communication
  • Careful presentation and professional attitude
  • Trusted, organized, responsive and resourceful person
  • Essential driving licence

Why join Coutaz?

As a reference company in Valais since 1946, the Coutaz Group now has more than 90 employees active in building techniques and industrial piping.

For this particularly autonomous position, we are looking for a person who will become a true ambassador of Coutaz to our clients.

We offer you: autonomy, diversity of interventions, direct contact with clients, modern tools and stimulating and family work environment. Application
