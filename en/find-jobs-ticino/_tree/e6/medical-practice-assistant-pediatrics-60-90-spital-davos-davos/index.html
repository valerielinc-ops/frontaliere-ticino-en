Full description

Angiology and Cardiology work with us as a team. Together, we care for patients with complex cardiovascular diseases in an outpatient and inpatient setting. The Hochgebirgsklinik Davos is the only stationary angiological rehabilitation clinic in Switzerland. We treat patients with complex vascular diseases from all over Switzerland, often in situations where outpatient treatment is no longer sufficient. The Angiology will be further developed in an outpatient and inpatient setting in the coming years. For this, we are looking for a specialist in Angiology who wants to actively shape this development. ## Tasks

  • Care for complex vascular diseases: You treat patients with complex angiological diseases in an outpatient and inpatient setting.
  • Develop Angiology: You develop Angiology together with Cardiology in our cardiovascular team.
  • Work interdisciplinarily: You work closely with Cardiology, Pneumology, Dermatology, and other disciplines.
  • Shape structures: You bring your experience into the development of outpatient and inpatient offerings.
  • Take responsibility: As a chief physician, you take on clinical responsibility in the daily angiological routine. As a leading physician, you are additionally responsible for the Angiology department and shape its further development. ##
  • Requirements
  • Specialist qualification: You have the specialist title in Angiology or an equivalent qualification.
  • Broad angiological expertise: You enjoy complex vascular diseases and patients who are often not adequately cared for in an outpatient setting.
  • Interest in development: You want to shape structures and actively accompany the development of Angiology.
  • Team orientation: You enjoy working interdisciplinarily and appreciate collaboration with other disciplines.
