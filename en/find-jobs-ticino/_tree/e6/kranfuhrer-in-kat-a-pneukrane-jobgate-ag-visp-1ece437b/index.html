Full description

jobgate ag is a personnel services company based in Aarau and specializes in the placement and rental of specialist personnel in the areas of mechanics, technology, IT, commercial professions, logistics, timber construction, chemicals and the construction industry.

On behalf of our customer, we are looking for an experienced crane operator / In Kat immediately or by agreement. A.

Their tasks

  • Driving a pneumatic crane (category A)
  • Load impacts
  • Loading and unloading of trucks
  • External transverse transport of goods
  • Relocation work
  • Other logistics
  • Compliance with occupational safety – laws and regulations
  • Medium-term fixed work region

Your profile

  • Experienced and safe handling of pneu cranes (AC 40 and AC 45)
  • Crane driver’s license A and truck driver’s license including commercial driving licence
  • Experience and willingness to cooperate in the entire logistics sector
  • Good physical condition and pleasure in physical work (construction site)
  • Readiness for overtime and Saturday work in individual cases
  • Readiness for a high security culture
  • Professional appearance and reliable working methods
  • Cooperative, communicative and flexible personality

Our customer offers

  • Modern, collegial and dynamic working environment
  • Interesting small and large projects (CapEx) with various services in the pharmaceutical, medical and chemical sectors
  • Flat corporate hierarchy and structures and processes
  • High occupational safety through own SGU/EHS department
  • Fixed working hours in single shift operation
  • Expenses in project business (day trips, accommodation)
  • Internal training, toolboxes and instructions
