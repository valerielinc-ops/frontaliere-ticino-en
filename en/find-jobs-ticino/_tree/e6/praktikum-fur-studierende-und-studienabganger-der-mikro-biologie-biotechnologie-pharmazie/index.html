The Position At our location in Kaiseraugst we offer an exciting internship opportunity from February 2027. The Kaiseraugst production site is a cornerstone of Roche’s global production and logistics network. More than 1500 people are employed here and the site is home to Roche’s largest and most innovative packaging company in the world.

The possibility

As an intern in the field of QC microbiology at the Kaiseraugst production site, you support the team in a wide range of aspects.

In this internship, you will

  • Gain insights into the versatile project work of QC Microbiology via 4 different units and 2 different sites (Basel and Kaiseraugst)
  • Cooperation in laboratory activities (e.g. evaluation of agar plates)
  • Support our team in ongoing process improvement and documentation projects in our pharmaceutical laboratory
  • Identify opportunities for optimization in our workflows, contribute to compliance initiatives and support projects to increase efficiency and quality
  • Working independently on own projects (e.g. creating, presenting and implementing concepts and study plans)
  • Data collection and evaluation as a basis for process optimization
  • Create and edit documents such as reports, SOPs and presentations
  • Professional and organizational routine meetings accompany and coordinate

Who you are

You are matriculated or have Your studies at a university or university of applied sciences, preferably in the field of natural sciences ((micro)biology / biotechnology / pharmacy), have been completed within the last 12 months and are interested in using your knowledge in a 12-month internship in a modern working environment, as well as getting to know the pharmaceutical industry.

In addition, you round off your profile with the following competencies:

  • Independent and structured working method
  • High degree of initiative and quick understanding
  • Strong analytical and conceptual thinking skills
  • Interest in process optimization and pharmaceutical business activities
  • Enjoy the constructive cooperation in cross-divisional working groups and good communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of English and German
  • Good MS Office knowledge
  • Google Suite knowledge is an advantage

Additional information

  • The preferred start of the internship is in February 2027 or by appointment. Please enter your preferred start date in your application.
  • Non-EU/EFTA citizens (m/f/d) must accompany the application with a confirmation from the university that they must complete a mandatory internship as part of the training.
  • It is a full-time internship (100%) with a working week of 40 hours. The place of work is our location in Kaiseraugst.
  • This traineeship does not permit the writing of a thesis.
