Full description

In our rehabilitation clinic in Basel, we treat patients especially in the field of internistic and oncological rehabilitation through modern and patient-centered care. Your work thus remains versatile and varied. The team maintains an open and clear communication, the decision paths are short and your ideas and suggestions are welcome. So here you have the opportunity to get involved and actively shape the extension.

Your tasks

  • Patient-oriented care: You help our patients with internistic and oncological limitations to find their way back to an independent life
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: In close cooperation with the various disciplines, you promote holistic rehabilitation to achieve the best possible results
  • Diverse activities: In addition to the care and care of patients, dealing with modern medical technology is also part of your task, e.g. wound management, PEG probes and infusion management
  • Professional training: We promote your continuous development with regular internal and external training opportunities so that you always stay up-to-date

What you bring

  • Qualification: Completed vocational training as a qualified nurse or equivalent training with at least one positive SRC PreCheck
  • Experience: You have experience in dealing with complex care situations
  • Personality: You show commitment in interdisciplinary cooperation and enjoy participation and bring the necessary flexibility

What we offer you

  • Co-creation: Join a new modern work environment and get actively involved
  • Working time model: You choose between the different working time models to create a balanced work-life model for yourself
  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Flexibility bonus: Voluntary participation in our internal care pool for additional shifts, which are additionally attractively compensated.
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.

We look forward to your application via our online tool.
