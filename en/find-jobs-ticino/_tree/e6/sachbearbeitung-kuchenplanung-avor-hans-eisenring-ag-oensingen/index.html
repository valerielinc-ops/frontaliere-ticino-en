Companies … Job Portal Kitchen planning (AVOR) Kitchen planning (AVOR) As a clerk: in kitchen planning AVOR you plan and process versatile kitchen projects from the offer to the order. They draw kitchens with winners, create offer plans, perspectives, order confirmations, and execution and installation plans. You keep an eye on details, dates and order processes and ensure that every project is cleanly prepared. We are looking for a committed person with technical understanding, exact working methods and enjoyment of the kitchen industry. The most important in short Place of work Oensingen (SO) Pensum 80 - 100 %

Responsibilities

  • Professional order processing of versatile kitchen projects
  • Drawing/planning of ordinary to demanding kitchens with the Winner program
  • Create Winner Offers, Offer Plans and Perspectives with the Planning Program (System-Aided Price Calculation)
  • Creating order confirmations, execution and installation plans
  • Total order processing including follow-up orders

What you bring

  • Ideally completed vocational training as carpenter or draftsman
  • Professional experience in the kitchen industry or great interest in getting involved in this industry
  • Good PC user knowledge
  • Customer-oriented thinking and acting
  • Precise and reliable operation
  • Enthusiasm and commitment
