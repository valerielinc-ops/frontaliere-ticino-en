Full description

More powerful cars? More efficient aircraft? More durable textiles? Your career at Oerlikon makes these innovations possible – and many more!

Are you enthusiastic about technical innovations and challenging design tasks? Then strengthen our team as

Design engineer (gn)

Their tasks

  • Development and construction of handling components as well as parts clamping for thermal coating systems
  • Preparation of parts lists, drawings and production documents
  • Static design of mechanical components
  • Creation of layouts for coating plants as well as concepts for the optimal design of production processes
  • Preparation of project studies and concepts for customer orders incl. Cost estimation
  • Support in the commissioning of handling systems, in the acceptance of equipment and in the preparation of customer manuals
  • Further development of plant components and standards
  • Occasional support operations at customer facilities at home and abroad

Your profile

  • Successfully completed professional training as a constructor or Polymechanic (gn) with training at TS or FH level (mechanical engineering or equivalent)
  • Sound professional experience in construction
  • Structured, goal-oriented and self-responsible as well as proactive and initiative working method
  • Experience in the analysis and optimization of manufacturing processes is advantageous
  • Experienced handling of 3D CAD systems, ideally with Inventor
  • User knowledge in SAP
  • German in Word and Writing
  • English at language level B2

We offer

  • Attractive compensation package with good social benefits
  • At the beginning of 2027 Oerlikon Metco will move to Hausen (AG) – a modern production and technology centre for thermal coating is waiting for us
  • Varied work in an internationally oriented company in the high-tech sector
  • Open corporate culture and a friendly, open-minded team
  • Perspectives for personal and professional development
  • Working independently in an attractive working environment

Do you feel addressed? Then we are curious to meet you!

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in surface technologies and high-performance materials. With a unique portfolio that includes surface engineering, high performance materials, coating equipment and components, we improve products by increasing performance, efficiency and sustainability. Oerlikon serves a wide range of industries including aerospace, automotive, energy, medicine, luxury, semiconductors and tooling. The headquarters are located in Pfäffikon, Switzerland. Oerlikon operates in 38 countries, employs around 10,000 people and achieved sales of CHF 1.6 billion in 2025.

We offer a wide variety of activity profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and reach their full potential – guided by our core values of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.
