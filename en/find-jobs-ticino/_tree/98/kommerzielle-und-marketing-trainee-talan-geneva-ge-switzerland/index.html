Background

As part of the development of its activities in Suisse Romande, Talan strengthens its sales team and is looking for an intern in the last year to accompany the generation of pipelines and the deployment of marketing actions. This internship (6 months from September/October) is above all a gateway: if the adventure is successful on both sides, we like to follow up. Main missions

Within the sales team, you will intervene on three key axes

Database qualification

  • Identify and enrich contacts of decision makers (C-level, IT, Digital)
  • Structure and maintain data quality in CRM
  • Segment accounts and prioritize targets

Commercial prospecting

  • Contact prospects (telephone / email / LinkedIn)
  • Generate qualified appointments for commercial teams
  • Ensure rigorous monitoring of CRM interactions

Support for marketing actions

  • Participate in organizing events (webinars, breakfasts, customer events)
  • Contribute to marketing campaigns (invitations, reviews, follow-up)
  • Coordinate with marketing and marketing teams
  • Student in final year of business school / university
  • Strong interest in business development and B2B environments
  • Oral attendance, including by telephone
  • Structured, rigorous and results oriented spirit
  • Good mastery of French, professional English required

What You Will Learn

  • The fundamentals of B2B prospecting in a consulting / IT environment
  • Use of a CRM (Salesforce type)
  • Pipeline generation mechanics and marketing campaigns
  • The functioning of a structured business organisation
