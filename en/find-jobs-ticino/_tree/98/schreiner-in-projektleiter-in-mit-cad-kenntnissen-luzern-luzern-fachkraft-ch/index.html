Full description

The work includes the technical and commercial supervision of window projects. The company attaches great importance to short decision-making processes and an appreciative environment. We are looking for an activity for our client

  • technically and commercially supervising window projects
  • making CAD drawings and recording data in window construction software
  • coordinating coordination with architects, builders and general contractors
  • implementing measures on existing objects
  • assuming project management from incoming orders to billing

  • Strong entrepreneurial thinking and self-responsible action

  • High quality awareness and care in everyday work offer
  • Family-oriented company with short decision-making paths and appreciative working environment
