Together.

For what really counts Clinic for Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Focus: Diagnosis and treatment in adult psychiatry PBL

For the open-guided station F2 "Integrated Treatment", we are looking for a committed personality who appreciates a modern evidence-based work in a patient-centered clinic. Our new operating model enables VSAO-oriented service planning and a significant reduction in bureaucracy in clinical everyday life. We are guided by the FMH requirements for professional ethics and quality assurance.

Their tasks

  • Medical responsibility: You assume overall medical responsibility for diagnosis and treatment and lead a multi-professional treatment team.
  • Clinical expertise: You are responsible for the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic assessment, the indication as well as the planning, implementation and evaluation of individual treatments.
  • Integrated treatment: They design coordinated, multi-stage treatment and promote seamless transitions between inpatient, day-to-day, outpatient and outreach services.
  • Pass on knowledge: They accompany and promote assistant doctors and psychologists in their professional development.
  • Shaping the future: Develop together with your managers and the multi-professional team They advance treatment concepts, provide new impetus and ensure their implementation in clinical everyday life.

Your profile

  • Completed medical studies and MEBEKO-approved medical qualification
  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy or shortly before its acquisition
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation and understanding of different professional perspectives as enrichment
  • Interest in innovative, patient-centred care models and the further development of psychiatric treatment services
  • Independent, structured and responsible way of working, quick comprehension and the ability to keep an overview even in demanding situations
  • Clear, appreciative and addressable communication as well as very good knowledge of German

We look forward to seeing you! If you want to contribute your clinical experience, take responsibility and actively shape the psychiatric care of tomorrow, we look forward to meeting you. Become part of the psychiatry Baselland and design a modern, networked and patient-centered psychiatric care with us.

About us

The Psychiatry Baselland (PBL) is one of the leading psychiatric institutions in northwestern Switzerland. It offers comprehensive outpatient, daily clinical, home treatment and inpatient therapies for adults as well as children and adolescents. She supports people with mental and multiple disabilities in the areas of living, work and day-to-day planning (www.inclusioplus.ch). She also holds a stake in WorkMed AG, the Center for Work and Mental Health. At several locations in the Canton of Basel-Landschaft, PBL employs around 1,300 people and treats, supports and advises more than 15,000 patients per year (www.pbl.ch).
