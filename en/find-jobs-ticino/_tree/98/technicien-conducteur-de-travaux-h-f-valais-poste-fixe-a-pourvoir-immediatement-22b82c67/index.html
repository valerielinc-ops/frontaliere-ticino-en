Company description

The Valais built. And we're looking for someone who knows him.

You don't need to sell the canton to yourself if you're from here, you know what it means to work on Valais construction sites: demanding projects, solid teams, and a pride of the work that is well done and passed on from construction to construction.

We are an active placement agency throughout Switzerland (28 agencies), but it is here, in Valais, that we are looking for someone ancre. Someone who knows local businesses, their ways of working, and who doesn't need to tame the region before being effective.

In order to meet the needs of our faithful partner, we are looking for a Construction Operator (technician) for the Sion / Sierre region.

Description of post

You are piloting construction sites from A to Z — from the planning phase to the handing over of the keys. In concrete terms, this means:

  • Coordinate field teams, contractors and suppliers
  • Keeping deadlines and budgets without losing sight of quality
  • Ensure safety on site, without compromise
  • Be the trusted partner between management, clients and teams
  • Anticipate problems before they become blockages

Profile sought

  • Federal degree of construction driver
  • Federal certificate of team leader and/or foreman, a real plus
  • A solid experience on construction sites in Switzerland, ideally in Valais
  • The sense of the field: you probably started as a mason or foreman, and it shows in your way of working
  • Direct communication and natural leadership — teams follow you because they trust you

We offer

The companies we work with in Valais have a good reputation, and that is no coincidence. You integrate an environment where the profession is respected and your experience has value from day one.

  • Fixed post with salary up to your expertise
  • Real prospects for development and continuing training
  • A network of strong partner companies in the region
  • And of course you work where you live
