Full description

Your Mission

Within Digital IT, the Project Management Officer (PMO) Lead is responsible for structuring, facilitating and continuously improving the governance of projects, programmes and portfolios. A true partner of the Directorate, he/she ensures the consistency of steering practices, the quality of management data, the consolidation of portfolio visibility and the development of team maturity. At the crossroads of strategic, methodological and operational dimensions, you will play a key role in the success of transformation initiatives and in accompanying decision-making at the highest level of the organisation.

Your responsibilities

Governance & Methodological Framework

  • Define, deploy and evolve the governance framework for projects, programs and portfolios.
  • Ensure the harmonisation and consistency of project management practices throughout the Digital IT organisation.
  • Define rules for prioritization, arbitration and follow-up of initiatives, taking into account capacity, dependencies and strategic objectives.
  • Ensure compliance with internal requirements (audit, security, finance, architecture) as well as standards and good market practices.
  • Identify governance gaps and implement necessary corrective actions.
  • Provide methodological reference to Portfolio Managers, Project Managers and operational managers.
  • Support strategic initiatives by contributing to framing, defining governance mechanisms and securing conditions for success.
  • Facilitate the resolution of cross-dependencies and organizational barriers.

Portfolio Piloting & Executive Reporting

  • Strengthen all project and programme portfolios by ensuring data quality, reliability and consistency.
  • Provide a comprehensive and consolidated vision of progress, risks, dependencies, capabilities, budgets, investments and value creation.
  • Identify systemic risks, potential drifts and transverse impacts.
  • Prepare arbitrations and recommendations for governance bodies and steering committees.
  • Produce executive dashboards, strategic analysis and decision support.
  • Support and coach Project Managers and Portfolio Managers in the preparation of steering committees, steering committees and investment committees.
  • Ensure clear, synthetic and regular communication with Leadership Digital IT.

Continuous Improvement & Skills Development

  • Assess the maturity of project and portfolio practices within the organization.
  • Identify areas for improvement and define appropriate development plans.
  • Design and conduct training, methodological workshops, communities of practice and experience sharing sessions.
  • Accompany teams through mentoring and coaching actions.
  • Promote the adoption of a common project culture and strengthen collaboration between Digital IT teams and other departments.
  • Measure the impact of actions undertaken and adjust development arrangements to ensure continuous progress.

Reporting & Documentation

  • Ensure regular reporting to management.
  • Develop and maintain reliable and relevant performance indicators.
  • Produce and maintain documentation related to governance and pilotage activities.
  • Prepare and document governance bodies: agendas, materials, reports, decisions and action plans.
  • Ensure traceability and archiving of key deliverables.
  • You have more than 10 years of proven experience in managing projects, programs and portfolios, including several years in a senior PMO, portfolio governance or enterprise PMO-wide function.
  • You have strong expertise in project, program and portfolio management methodologies (PMI, PMBOK, Agile, SAFe, PRINCE2 or equivalent), as well as excellent control of associated governance mechanisms.
  • You master the issues of prioritization, arbitration, dependency management, capacity planning, as well as the financial and budgetary control of a portfolio of initiatives.
  • You have demonstrated your ability to define, implement and develop methodological frameworks, harmonize piloting practices, and support the maturity of project organisations.
  • You are recognized for your strong analytical, synthesis and communication capabilities, and know how to produce executive reporting, strategic dashboards and high-value recommendations for management.
  • You have a proven experience in accompanying project teams, managers and stakeholders, bringing structure, visibility and methodologies adapted to the challenges of the organisation.
  • You are easily evolving in complex, matrix and transformational environments, and are able to intervene on strategic initiatives with high stakes.
  • An experience within a Digital and/or IT department in an international context is a major asset.
  • You are fluent in English and French, both oral and written.

Behavioural skills

  • Collaborative leadership and ability to unite teams and stakeholders with diverse profiles.
  • Strong focus and focus.
  • Excellent ability to influence and communicate with interlocutors at all levels, including management committees.
  • Structured approach to problem solving and decision-making.
  • Ability to simplify complexity and turn data into actionable information.
  • A spirit of continuous improvement and willingness to advance practices and organizational maturity.
  • Autonomy, proactivity and organizational sense.
  • Excellent relational skills and ability to create a climate of trust and collaboration.
