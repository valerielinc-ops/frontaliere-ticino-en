Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. Strongly committed to its digital transformation, Vaudoise Assurances continues to invest in Artificial Intelligence. You will join us to develop the IA roadmap representative of Vaudoise Assurance to create value with IA tools. Within the Data & IA division, we are looking for a Data Scientist (h/f/x) 80-100% , which will work in close collaboration with the business services and the technical teams of digital transformation. The missions that await you:

  • Develop, implement, maintain and develop AI models and agents
  • Collaborate with representatives of the profession and identify opportunities feasible by AI-related technologies
  • Participate in acceptance and validation of deliverables and verify consistency between model/agent implementation and expected performance
  • Collaborate within the team with IA engineers, product owner and technical lead
  • Conduct feasibility studies and carry out prototypes
  • Listening to developments in AI domains including in the field of IA General
  • Your profile:
  • Master / Doctorate (or equivalent) in computer science, statistics or data science
  • Experience of at least 3 years in creating, evaluating and optimizing predictive models and agents using machine learning techniques. An experience in general AI is a plus
  • Excellent skills on Python and SQL
  • You have already used the machine learning framework (MLFlow, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Pytorch, Langchain) and have knowledge of applied statistics
  • Knowledge of Azure and Databricks is a plus as is knowledge of the world of insurance
  • Ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders, translating the results of analyses into strategic recommendations
  • Excellent level of French (C1) and English mastery (B2)
  • We offer you:
  • A healthy working environment within a mutual company certified Great Place to Work
  • Attractive conditions and competitive social benefits
  • Flexibility in work organisation
  • A stimulating framework for skills development and continuous improvement We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
