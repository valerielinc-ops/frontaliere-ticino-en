Your commitment to security and freedom

The Command of Operations (cdmt Op) is responsible for all operations and military commitments of the Swiss Army. The CDmt Op includes the Air Force, the Land Forces, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Skills Centre, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. About 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are employed in civilian occupations, from engineers to analysts to mechanics on aircraft.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 employees in four military professions and around 200 civilian professions, at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. It is also responsible for succession by training apprentices in more than 30 trades.
