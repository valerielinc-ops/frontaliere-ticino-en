Full description

You lead the training of learners and actively shape their learning progress. The company promotes your further development and relies on close cooperation in the experienced team. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Training of apprentices in plant and apparatus engineering
  • Recruiting new learners
  • Conduct metalworking courses for civil engineering mechanic learners
  • Creating training plans and learning documentation
  • Conducting locksmith work according to technical drawings

Requirements

  • Apprenticeship as plant and apparatus builder EFZ or metal builder EFZ
  • Vocational training completed
  • Professional practice of at least 2 years in the field
  • Negotiating knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of 2D/3D CAD would be desirable
