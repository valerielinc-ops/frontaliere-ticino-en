Network Manager: in 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Network Manager:in Bellinzona or Erstefeld, 01.02.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You independently take over the commissioning of projects in the area of a route and accompany them from the commissioning of the study, the resulting project to the successful completion. You make an important contribution to the further development and maintenance of the railway infrastructure. Look forward to working together in a diverse, open and helpful team that is characterized by strong cohesion and comprehensive expertise. In the NAT regions, we combine the competences of the client, lifecycle decision-making, network development and building portfolio planning and management, and together we take responsibility for all infrastructure facilities. Our benefits such as child allowances, free GA and discounted travel abroad - also for your family can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • You independently manage a geographical area (routes and nodes) as a network manager: in, client:in, client:in of SBB infrastructure projects
  • Taking into account railway operational and structural aspects, you bundle structural measures into meaningful projects that ensure the economic implementation of the construction work as well as the aspects of customer satisfaction.
  • You are responsible for the integral commissioning of the construction projects within the assigned area and thus make an important contribution to the planning and control of the entire construction portfolio plan of the SBB infrastructure.
  • In your role as an internal client for SBB Infrastructure, you are jointly responsible for achieving the goals (financing, costs, quantities, deadlines) in the projects. You lead the requirements and change management and control the projects from commissioning to acceptance.
  • In the ongoing projects, you represent the interests of the plant owners (Life Cycle Manager). In addition, you conduct contribution negotiations with third parties.

  • We require a university or university of applied sciences degree as an engineer:in or business economist:in or an equivalent education with corresponding experience. Alternatively, you have many years of professional experience in the railway industry.
  • German or Italian as a mother tongue, with very good knowledge of the other language, enable you to communicate and negotiate effectively with various partners.
