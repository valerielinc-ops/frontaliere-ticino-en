Full description

The Departmental Secretariat shall be the department and service centre of the Department of the Interior. It is divided into the departments of administration, legal service, controlling and digitization, communication, Swisslos funds and the upper offices. It supports and advises the head of the department and performs horizontal tasks for the five department offices. For the administration department, we are looking for a Stv. Head of Administration, 80-100% at the Solothurn location as of 01.03.2027 or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • They ensure the deputy of the head of the administration department with four employees and carry out general secretarial work (including telephone and counter service).
  • The coordination of the department’s governmental and cantonal council activities as well as the editorial and formal examination of applications to the government council are among your main tasks.
  • As an HR assistant, you support the department secretary in the management of the department secretariat with around 50 employees.
  • They manage the department’s electronic business administration and perform tasks within the creditor workflow.
  • Creating tutorials and instructions for electronic applications and processes and training employees in their application are part of your tasks.

Your profile

  • You have a commercial apprenticeship degree with additional training as a directorial assistant, a completed secondary education or an equivalent education as well as several years of professional experience and know the public administration.
  • As an HR assistant, you have the additional training as an HR assistant with a certificate Human Resources Swiss Exam (HRSE) or are ready to complete this additional training part-time.
  • You are a service-oriented person with quick comprehension, stylish in the writing of texts, have good communication skills, are resilient, flexible and work exactly.
  • Teamwork, loyalty, discretion and secrecy set you apart.
  • IT affinity and experience in dealing with electronic business management systems, such as CMI Axioma, round off your profile.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
