Full description

The production of sheet metal structures from different metals is the focus of this varied activity. The company offers you a modern working environment with diverse machines and a dedicated team. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Manufacture of self-contained sheet steel, chromium and aluminium
  • Production of custom-made products to size (exhaust hoods, rain hats)
  • Operation and programming of various machines (folding press)
  • Cutting, folding, punching and notching metal
  • Understanding and interpreting technical drawings
  • Welding work TIG/MIG/MAG
  • Compliance with deadlines and inspection of finished parts
  • Cleaning and maintenance of equipment

Requirements

  • Completed basic training as plant and apparatus builder / metal builder
  • Very good knowledge in the processing of sheet metal (0.5 - 3 mm)
  • Professionally competent and independent working method
  • High quality awareness, clean and accurate work according to plans, sketches and drawings
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Flexible, team-ready, motivated

Offer

  • Exciting, varied working area
  • Self-employment with high self-responsibility
  • Modern infrastructure and machinery park
  • Good public accessibility
  • Motivated and young team
