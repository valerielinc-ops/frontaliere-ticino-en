Full description

Is investment technology your world? In the Zurich-Bülligen Letter Centre, one of the most exciting technical locations in Switzerland awaits you – with state-of-the-art infrastructure and an environment in which expertise, personal responsibility and team spirit count. With you, we take our plant technology to the next level.

You can do that.

  • As part of the maintenance team, you ensure the smooth functionality of the entire investment park.
  • In close coordination with shift management, you coordinate your work efficiently.
  • You recognize potential for improvement in plant technology and in existing processes and are actively involved in their implementation.
  • You are involved in revision, maintenance and technical projects.
  • Together with your team, you keep an eye on the condition of the plants and ensure their high availability.
  • Through your participation in specialist teams, you actively shape the further development of strategies and processes.

You bring that with you

  • You have a degree EFZ as an Automatiker:in, Polymechaniker:in or a comparable technical education.
  • Experience in automation/control technology and technical maintenance you bring and use your know-how specifically.
  • You work flexibly and resiliently, set clear priorities and actively participate as a respectful team player.
  • Shift work in three-shift operation as well as weekend inserts can be easily reconciled with your everyday life.
  • Ideally, you have the installation permit according to NIV15 or are ready to complete it.
  • Very good knowledge of German and a category B driver’s license round off your profile.
