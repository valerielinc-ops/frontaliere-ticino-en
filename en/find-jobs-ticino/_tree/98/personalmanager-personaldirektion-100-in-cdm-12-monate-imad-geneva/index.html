Full description

The Human Resources Officer is attached to the Human Resources Directorate and will be responsible for part of the Community Health and Habitat Directorate (CHSD). Your missions and responsibilities

  • Partner and privileged advice of departments in charge of human resources management within the DSCH
  • Recruitment, selection, employee engagement: active contribution to the identification of staffing needs, the development of job profiles in accordance with current guidelines
  • Management, monitoring and development of employees: active contribution to the professional development of employees by providing advice and guidance, in line with institutional training policy
  • Reference person within its target audience for all matters related to existing laws and regulations
  • Participation in IMAD's organizational and development reflections and projects to assess possible impacts on collaborators and propose appropriate action plans to support change management
  • Monitoring and updating of dashboards, indicators and statistical data required for management
  • Direct line management of an HR assistant
  • Development, monitoring and monitoring of the target population and active participation in the budget building process.
  • Certified university and/or federal certificate in personnel management or equivalent training
  • Confirmed professional experience in human resources management
  • In-depth knowledge of Geneva laws, regulations and state processes
