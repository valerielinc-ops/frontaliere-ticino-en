Back to Overview Crane operator SUVA / gruista SUVA (m/f/d) Job opening Immediately or by agreement Employment level 100% Permanent Place of work/region VS-Wallis For our customer in Brig we are looking for a crane operator SUVA B You have the crane operator ID SUVA B and have already gained experience as a crane operator upper and lower turner.

Among your characteristics are

  • Reliability, teamwork and accuracy
  • You are mobile and versatile
  • You are ready to join the construction site

Your main tasks include

  • Driving of top and bottom handles (with or without remote control)
  • Maintenance and maintenance of machinery
  • At most, active assistance in construction works

You bring the following

  • Crane driver’s license recognised by SUVA
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Are resilient and ready for use
  • Have technical understanding and generally enjoy construction work
