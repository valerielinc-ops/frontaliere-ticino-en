Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us.

The Office for Primary Schools and Sports (AVS) is part of the Educational, Cultural and Environmental Protection Department of the Canton of Graubünden.

The Department of Services and Finance is responsible for the central administrative, legal and financial tasks of the Office of Elementary School and Sports. It supports the various departments of the Office in legal and organisational matters, processes official documents and transactions and ensures internal processes as well as cooperation with cantonal authorities and external partners.

We are looking for 1\. February 2027 or by agreement a legal employee for 60\-80%.

Your responsibilities

  • They bear the main responsibility for the legal interests of the Office and are the central contact person for legal issues internally and externally.
  • They advise management and employees on legal issues and support them in the legal assessment of projects and orders.
  • They assist the administration in general and specific staff tasks, as well as in the preparation of opinions and the handling of requests and assignments from the department.
  • They work independently on legislative projects and accompany them from conception to implementation.

That is what we want from you.

  • You have a completed law degree.
  • Ideally, you will have professional experience in administrative law and/or public administration.
  • They have good knowledge of the administrative law of the Canton of Graubünden or are prepared to appropriate it in a well-founded manner.
  • They are interested in political and social issues and understand how to assess legal contexts in a broader context.
  • They are characterized by analytical and networked thinking, a quick apprehension and a structured and independent working method.
  • They have a very good written and oral language and can convey complex legal issues in an understandable and addressable manner.
  • They work solution-oriented, reliable and responsible and convince through a secure and trustworthy appearance.
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is an advantage.
