Full description

Fixed Placement Agency and Temporary Work We recruit a building electrician to strengthen the teams of a general electricity company in Geneva. Autonomous and versatile, you intervene effectively on a variety of electrical installations: planning the installation, installing the systems and appliances, finalizing the electrical installations. Your missions

  • Read and interpret diagrams and plans
  • Prepare and complete installation, renovation and maintenance work
  • Lay pipes, pull and connect cables
  • Install appliances, equipment and lighting
  • Install electrical sockets, switches and panels
  • Putting facilities into operation, adjusting and testing
  • Prepare security controls and standards verification

Your profile

  • CFC electrical installer, assembly electrician or equivalent
  • At least 3 years of experience in a similar position
  • Excellent sense of organization and practice
  • Controlling NIBT and safety standards
  • Versatile, autonomous and rigorous with a good team spirit
  • Driving licence desirable

We offer you

  • Follow-up and personalized support
  • A dynamic working environment
  • Various projects combining technical and innovation
  • Development and continuing training prospects
  • Compensation according to industry and experience
