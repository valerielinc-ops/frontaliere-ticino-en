Full description

As the Plant Manager of Chocolat Alprose, you oversee and coordinate the daily operations of our production site in Caslano. With strong leadership skills and guided by our code of conduct, you act as a role model and motivate your team to achieve the production and quality targets of the company. You empower people to excel, ensuring that all safety measures are consistently followed. Your area of responsibility covers around 60 employees working in production, supply chain, quality assurance, technical services and product management. In a dynamic chocolate manufacturing environment, processes evolve constantly. You easily adapt to changes, maintain an open mindset and proactively look for opportunities to improve quality, efficiency and stability across production lines. You collaborate with other plant managers within the group and contribute to business development, supporting the evolution of the site’s core competencies, including special productions and product innovation.

Your responsibilities as Plant Manager at Alprose

  • Ensuring efficient and stable production through pragmatic daily solutions and forward‑looking strategic planning.
  • Defining clear processes and a transparent flow of information across departments.
  • Fostering a positive team atmosphere, effective communication and strong on‑site presence.
  • Initiating and managing the optimization of the site, production lines and processes with a hands‑on approach and technical knowledge.
  • Managing based on KPIs and developing the site economically in close cooperation with company management.

What you bring as Plant Manager at Alprose

  • Several years of experience managing a production plant or manufacturing unit, ideally in the food industry.
  • A hands‑on mentality, adaptive and open mindset combined with strategic thinking and technical expertise.
  • An empathetic and determined personality, with the courage to challenge and improve existing processes.
  • Excellent business and leadership skills.
  • Excellent command of English and Italian; additional languages are an advantage.

What we offer as Plant Manager at Alprose

  • A permanent employment contract.
  • A motivating work environment with challenging and varied tasks.
  • Opportunities to collaborate internationally within the group.
  • Creative freedom to explore new product ideas and solutions.
  • Professional development opportunities at group level.

Working at Alprose

At our Caslano facility, we produce a wide range of chocolate products every day from liquid chocolate to tablets and coated products. The site combines long‑standing expertise with reliable technologies, supported by a motivated team committed to quality. Eager to lead our production site and contribute to the future development of Alprose? Apply now via the form below!
