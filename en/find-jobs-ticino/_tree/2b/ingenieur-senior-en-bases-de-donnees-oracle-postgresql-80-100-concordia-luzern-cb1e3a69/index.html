Full description

Honest collaboration: 100 % healthy Tribschenstrasse 4 6005 Lucerne Become part of an experienced team that jointly designs and operates a modern, scalable database and infrastructure platform. With us, you take on responsibility, help shape architectural decisions and contribute your own ideas.

What you can expect

  • Together with the team, you are responsible for the stable operation, engineering and further development of our Oracle and PostgreSQL database platform, both in day-to-day business and in projects and releases.
  • You work on the further development of our database and data warehouse platforms, on the migration from Oracle to PostgreSQL and on expanding our database platforms in the Azure Cloud.
  • You support the team with automation, performance analysis, scripting, monitoring and logging.
  • Infrastructure as Code is an integral part of our way of working: You actively develop our automation and infrastructure standards further. Together, we drive topics such as declarative infrastructure, versioned changes and reproducible deployments forward—with tools such as Ansible, Terraform and CI/CD pipelines.
  • You support the further development of our data warehouse with GoldenGate and DataHub and help further automate manual processes.
  • Exchange and professional development are important to us: You regularly have the opportunity to attend user groups and vendor events.
  • You can expect a strong team: 3 other DBAs and 8 Linux Engineers work together with close technical exchange and short decision-making paths.
  • We speak Swiss German dialect & German and are in the Lucerne office at least 3 days per week.
  • You work in a self-organized environment with plenty of scope to shape things, flexibility, personal responsibility and trust.

What you bring

  • Several years of experience in operating and engineering Oracle or PostgreSQL databases.
  • Enthusiasm for modern platform and cloud technologies.
  • Experience in automation (e.g. Bash, Python, Ansible) and a desire to make lasting improvements.
  • A structured, independent way of working, a quick grasp and high quality standards.
  • Analytical thinking, strong conceptual skills and a solution-oriented approach.
  • Training in computer science or comparable professional experience.
  • Very good German language skills (company language) and the ability to understand English documentation with confidence.

Nice to have / Your development potential

  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD and declarative systems.
  • Automation with Ansible Automation Platform, GitHub Actions or Chef.
  • Infrastructure provisioning with Terraform (On-Prem & Azure).
