Full description

Join our production team in Ticino and assemble Delvitech's AI inspection machines, handling mechanical assembly and electrical wiring. Job Summary The General Assembler will be responsible for carrying out standard mechanical and electrical assembly activities within the production department. The role includes assembling kits and basic mechanical components, installing sheet metal parts, routing and connecting cables, and completing general machine assembly tasks according to drawings, instructions and production procedures. Key Responsibilities

  • General assembly: assemble standard kits, components and mechanical subassemblies.
  • Mechanical assembly: perform basic mechanical assembly activities using standard tools and equipment.
  • Sheet metal assembly: install covers, panels, brackets and other sheet metal parts.
  • Cable routing: route, position and secure cables according to assembly instructions.
  • Cable connection: connect electrical cables, sensors and standard components following defined procedures.
  • Documentation: follow assembly drawings, work instructions, checklists and production documentation.
  • Quality control: perform basic visual and functional checks during assembly.
  • Collaboration: work with production and technical teams to ensure correct assembly and material flow.
  • Continuous improvement: provide feedback and suggest improvements to assembly methods and work instructions. Requirements
  • Technical diploma or equivalent background.
  • Previous experience in mechanical or electrical assembly is preferred.
  • Basic ability to read mechanical drawings, assembly instructions and wiring documentation.
  • Familiarity with standard hand tools and assembly equipment.
  • Basic knowledge of cable routing and electrical connections.
  • Good manual skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to follow procedures and work instructions accurately.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Basic knowledge of English is a plus. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
