Full description

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population high quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and fairness. By joining the Lausanne Care Foundation, you are committed in a concrete way to promoting health and well-being in the canton of Vaud, within expert, motivated and enthusiastic teams, in a varied activity rich in human contacts. In the field, you intervene at the place of life or activity of the beneficiaries. You define with customers the solutions best suited to their situations and thus develop a fine knowledge and a privileged relationship of trust with them. As part of a team that is welded and supported, the exercise of these professions allows for a great autonomy but requires great responsibility and flexibility on the part of our employees. The mission of our services department is to ensure the implementation of home care and assistance services to ensure that each client receives a coordinated and integrated support in order to respect their home living project. It is within this structure that we seek immediately or to agree on:

ICLS - Specialized clinician nurse (M/F)

Participation rate at 80% Fixed-term contract

Your Mission

You are a clinical expert with clients and their relatives in the home life project. Your practice is based on evidence. Clinical practice and expert reasoning

  • Evaluate and manage complex situations with expert clinical reasoning
  • Adapt care to the individual's needs, values and context, in a population vision.
  • Contribute to health promotion and prevention.

Team support

  • Support teams in complex situations (consultation, guidance, coaching, supervision)
  • Disseminating good practices and strengthening the skills of caregivers
  • Supervising clinical documentation and integrating new collaborators

Innovation and change

  • Piloting continuous improvement and innovation projects
  • Guide the implementation of new practices and encourage team membership

Collaboration and coordination

  • Promote coordinated, intra and interprofessional teamwork
  • Strengthen collaboration with network partners
  • Strengthening the partnership with the client and his or her relatives

Evidence and quality

  • Ensure interventions based on up-to-date, home-specific data
  • Participate in protocols, adverse event analysis and indicator monitoring
  • Contribute to the development of home care nursing

Ethics

  • Promoting ethical reflection in clinical and institutional practices

Your profile and skills

  • Bachelor of Nursing (or equivalent)
  • Master's degree in compulsory nursing
  • Confirmed experience in medico-social, community health and clinical practice
  • Excellent clinical assessment and complex situation management capabilities
  • Demonstrated ability to work in interdisciplinary collaboration, with partners and networks
  • A sense of responsibility, initiative and ability to organize independently and set priorities
  • Ability to create a climate of trust and exchange within a team
  • Confirmed Project Management Capacity

We offer

  • A stimulating and warm work environment where the caregiver's autonomy is highlighted
  • A working environment that favours human contact and exchange
  • Exciting work in a pleasant environment for the well-being of clients
  • Fair working conditions and remuneration
  • Conditions of occupational security which extend beyond the minimum benefits provided by law

The benefits of joining us

  • Plan of integration by occupation, offers of rich and varied continuing training, opportunities for vocational development
  • Gender pay equity ( Fair-ON-Pay certification)
  • Remuneration according to the Collective Labour Convention (CCT) of the public health sector of voodoo
  • Favourable offers with preferential rates in service companies around sport, health and welfare and miscellaneous leisure
  • Soft mobility policy and participation in public transport costs
  • Five weeks of vacation per year and six weeks from 50 years
