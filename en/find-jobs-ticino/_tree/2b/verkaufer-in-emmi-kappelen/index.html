Full description

Baumann Käse AG, based in Zollikofen, specializes in the distribution of high-quality cheese specialties 🧀. At six locations, the company operates Chäsbueb cheese stores 🛍. Want to make your love for cheese a profession? 🤩🧀 Then we have the perfect opportunity for you! With us in the renowned cheese store "Chäsbueb" in Kappelen you can fully live your creativity and create unique shopping experiences for our customers 🎉

You can do that.

  • You play an active role in shaping everyday sales and ensure that our customers are happy to come back.
  • You inspire our customers with personal advice, expertise and good ideas.
  • You realize what there is to do, tackle independently and get things rolling.
  • You take responsibility in day-to-day business and support the shop management reliably.

You bring that with you

  • Passion for cheese and the joy of selling
  • You are energetic, resilient and happy to take part.
  • You work reliably and have a good sense of what is a priority in day-to-day business.
  • You can organize well and keep track even if a lot is going on.
  • Ideally, you will bring experience in the retail trade, in the food industry or in gastronomy.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Service Anniversary: Loyalty pays off: Every five years, we reward your loyalty with a premium, which you can also receive in the form of holidays or as a one-time contribution to the pension fund.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Voluntary child allowance: If you earn less than CHF 5,000, you will receive a voluntary allowance of CHF 100 per month for each child.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
