Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in vocational training, it gives an important place to the accompaniment and development of young talent, with more than 120 learners trained each year in its various departments. For its headquarters in Lausanne, the Vaudoise Assurances is looking for an apprentice who is motivated, engaged and curious, wishing to start his training on 1 August 2027. Your profile You meet the following criteria:

  • A good certificate of completion of high school (pregymnasial or general level, level 2)
  • Strong interest in insurance
  • Very good knowledge of French, both oral and written
  • A real motivation to discover the world of insurance and to invest fully in your learning
  • An enthusiastic, curious and committed personality
  • Good communication skills and good organizational sense We offer you
  • A healthy working environment within a mutual company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • A varied and exciting learning allowing you to discover the different facets of insurance
  • Support throughout your training to develop your skills and potential
  • Perspectives of commitment at the end of your learning Interested ? We look forward to discovering your application! Please send us your complete file by digital means, including:
