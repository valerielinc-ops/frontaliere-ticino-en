Full description

ELCO offers premium heating solutions with first-class service designed to meet the needs of our customers through the entire life cycle of products and heating systems. The focus is on excellent consulting as well as convincing products and heating systems. ELCO has been a leader in burner, condensing and solar technology for decades and has 1.7 million installed heating systems throughout Europe. Since 1928, ELCO has been a leading European brand offering intelligent solutions and technologies tailored to the needs of private and commercial projects, ensuring maximum energy savings.

In order to strengthen our service group in the field of gas, we are looking for a reliable and independent personality for the application area of the city of Bern and Agglomeration.

Service technician Gas (m/f)

With us there are numerous exciting tasks for you

  • Start-up of gas heating systems
  • Maintenance and management orders in the assigned service area
  • Resolving gas heating malfunctions and problems
  • Advising customers on the optimal settings of the supervised facilities
  • Collaboration with our contacts such as installers, sales indoor and outdoor service as well as employees of the entire service team

For our joint success story, bring with you

  • Completed training EFZ as a heating installer, sanitary installer or electrical installer, in the technical car industry or electrical engineering sector
  • Experience as a service technician is an advantage
  • Some years of professional experience and a high level of personal responsibility
  • Readiness for education and training on various energy sources
  • Structured, exact and effective working method
  • Correct and friendly treatment of internal and external customers
  • Willingness for Pikettdienst, great independence in work planning
  • Place of residence in theatre or adjacent

What makes us unique

  • Dynamic working environment with a family atmosphere and team spirit
  • Service vehicle with private use and equipment with modern technology such as smartphone and tablet
  • Modern and future-oriented products in the premium sector
  • Professional development opportunities
  • Start as a career at ELCO and develop personally and professionally
