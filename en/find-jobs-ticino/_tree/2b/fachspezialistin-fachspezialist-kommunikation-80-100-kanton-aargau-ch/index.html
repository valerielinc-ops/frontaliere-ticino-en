Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Communication Conductor: You plan, coordinate and orchestrate communication measures and coordinate them with project leaders, executives, the management ATB and the communication BVU
  • Perspective changer: You recognize the needs of different stakeholders in construction projects and ensure targeted internal and external communication
  • Word acrobat with sensitivity: You turn complex content into understandable messages and develop presentations, flyers, brochures, information boards and other means of communication from them
  • Organizational talent with performance: You coordinate communication projects, support events and public appearances and, if necessary, take over the word yourself
  • Digital navigator: You manage the digital channels of the civil engineering department and develop them further - from intranet and Internet via social media to other means of communication

What you bring

  • Bachelor or Master in Communication and Media or similar education
  • Additional training in the field of communication for non-profit organizations/public affairs
  • Experience in communication and project management within a larger company or administration
  • High IT affinity
  • Stylistic German as well as distinct writing and organizational talent
  • Customer- and team-oriented working methods and independent work

In the Department of Construction, Transport and Environment, we are actively developing the diverse habitat Aargau in the areas of space, energy, environment and mobility – today and for future generations. From green maintenance to winter service, from the redesign of local passages to innovative traffic management and the planning of new, safe and environmentally compatible transport facilities – in the civil engineering department we ensure the construction, maintenance and operation of the cantonal road network, which is over 1'150 km long, as well as the expansion of the more than 950 km velorout network.
