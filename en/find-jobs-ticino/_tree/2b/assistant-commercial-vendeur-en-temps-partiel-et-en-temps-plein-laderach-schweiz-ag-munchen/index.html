Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company now employs more than 2,500 people from over 80 countries, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glarus. Against a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates and develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The 'Retail' department comprises over 250 chocolate shops in over 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to be part of it? Then come to us. You can expect: You advise customers oriented and sell our diverse product range, for example when choosing gifts. You take care of the cash handling and ensure a precise and correct billing. You present the exquisite confectionery and chocolate assortment in a tasteful and inviting way. You adhere to our high quality standards with dedication and care. You ensure the observance of the hygiene and freshness concept. You bring: You have already gained experience in sales and bring joy in advising and selling. You place great value on high quality and have a well-developed sense for it. You work independently, efficiently and precisely. You communicate openly and master German and English with confidence. You are reliable and flexible, also for weekend assignments. You enjoy working in teams and love it when it's bustling. We offer you: An exciting work environment with motivated colleagues. A position with a lot of personal responsibility. The opportunity to introduce fresh ideas. An open and respectful feedback culture. Internal and external training. Individual career opportunities. Generous discounts on our products. Deutschlandticket travel allowance. Chocolate at the workplace. Good accessibility of the locations. Contact us. Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Your contact person is Mrs. Seda Öksüz and is available for any questions by email at Personal-de@laderach.com or by phone at 0221-99967501.
