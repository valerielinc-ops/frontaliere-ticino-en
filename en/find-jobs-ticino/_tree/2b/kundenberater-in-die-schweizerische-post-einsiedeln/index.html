Full description

Do you love personal customer contact? Do you enjoy active sales and advice? Together we network Switzerland. We are close to the people with you.

You can do that.

  • Be part of our dedicated team and bring your flair in dealing with people at the counter and on the sales floor.
  • You sell all postal and non-postal branded items. This includes, for example, regional products or various offers in the field of telecommunications.
  • You also actively advise our customers on services of various other industries such as health insurance, insurance and banks.
  • In the team, you have the opportunity to take on responsibility in various roles and develop yourself personally.
  • If you have never worked in a post office, we will make you fit! In our 4-month on-the-job training, you will be optimally prepared for your new role as a customer consultant:in. During this time, you work with a workload of 80%, after successful completion in the desired workload. You can find more information about the lateral entry in the factsheet entry sales and advice at the Post Office.

You bring that with you

  • Your natural sales flair and your open, sociable nature allow you to actively exchange ideas with our customers.
  • You have completed basic training in retail or sales.
  • As an open and responsible personality, you can easily cope in different situations.
  • You are digitally fit and have no trouble applying the common MS Office programs.
  • For competent advice you need a good knowledge of German, further language skills are an advantage.
