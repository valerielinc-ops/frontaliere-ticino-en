Full description

Assistant doctor, 80 - 100 %

Location: Riehen

From 01.10.2026 or by agreement

Founded in 1900, the Klinik Sonnenhalde in Riehen is now an established private clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland and southern Baden. We pursue a holistic, human-centered, milieutherapeutic psychiatry that combines excellent professional quality with a Christian value base. The clinic has 68 stationary beds, 4 day clinics and 4 ambulatories. As one of the first clinics in Switzerland, we are actively promoting digital therapy offerings, such as blended therapy and virtual reality and opened the first hybrid day clinic in Switzerland in February 2023.

To support our inpatient treatment team in Riehen, we are looking for a committed and motivated personality by agreement.

Your responsibilities

  • Integrated psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care of patients: inside in an openly managed department
  • Implementation of medical history, diagnosis, therapy and aftercare planning under the supervision of an experienced senior physician
  • Working in a multi-professional team
  • Participation in internal and external training events

Your profile

  • Federal State Examination or an equivalent education (MEBEKO recognition for foreign diplomas)
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (minor language level C1)
  • Engagement in the care of people with mental illness
  • Joy of working in a multi-professional team
  • Active interest in further developing professionally

Their possibilities

  • An interesting and broadly based psychiatric psychotherapeutic training as a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy at a further education institution certified for this purpose by the FMH
  • An appreciative corporate culture
  • Attractive employment conditions
