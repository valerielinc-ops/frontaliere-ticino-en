Quality Engineer 70-100% (m/f/d) — RUAG AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Support of production in quality-relevant issues and troubleshooting
  • Cooperation with Manufacturing Engineering, Configuration Management, Production, Supply Chain and the customer to ensure product quality
  • Establishment, testing and maintenance of quality plans, test plans, test instructions and evidence
  • Processing, evaluation and tracking of non-conformances (NCR) and deviations
  • Implementation and moderation of risk analyses (e.g. PFMEA) and derivation of suitable quality measures
  • Implementation of internal process audits as well as support for customer, authorities and certification audits

You bring that with you

  • Basic technical training in mechanical engineering/mechanics or electrical engineering, preferably in the aviation environment, subject to technical studies (HF, BSc or MSc)
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable function in industry (production or maintenance quality management of combat aircraft)
  • In-depth knowledge of industry-specific standards and regulations (EN 9100, EASA Part‐145/Part‐21) and secure handling of IT and SAP systems
  • Pronounced awareness of quality and safety, committed and responsible personality with high flexibility and get-the-job-done mentality
  • Team- and solution-oriented appearance with strong assertiveness and communication skills as well as negotiating-safe knowledge of German and English (at least level B2)
  • Experience in project management is an advantage

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.

Place of work

RUAG AG, Seetalstrasse 175, 6032 Emmen

Over the area

The RIGI project deals with the development of know-how in the area of F-35. In close cooperation with Lockheed Martin (LM), the F-35s will be assembled and equipped in Emmen. The knowledge built up will later be very valuable in maintenance. You will have the opportunity to actively participate in the creation of a highly complex aircraft and be part of a unique industrial project.
