Micromechanic Learning

CFC – 4 years For the beginning of August 2027

Who are we and what do we offer?

"Les Ateliers DB 1868" is part of the Richemont Group and is located in Breuleux (Jura). Our Manufacture develops and produces watchmaking clothing components (boxes, bracelets, clasps), as well as components for jewellery.

We offer an apprenticeship place for a young person aged 15 to 21 who has completed his compulsory school curriculum.

The CFC is a Federal Certificate of Capacity. The training takes place according to the dual system (school-enterprise) and is oriented towards the practical dimension of the trade.

Micromechanic learning is a 4-year course alternating practical courses within the DB 1868 Workshops and theoretical courses at the Technical Vocational School.

How to apply?

You are passionate about the world of precision and mechanics and your academic results indicate a good understanding of mathematics. You are motivated and thorough with a great capacity for concentration and excellent dexterity, this learning is done for you.

In order to assess your application, please send us

  • CV
  • Letter of motivation
  • 3 last school bulletins
  • Copy ID

If your profile meets the entry criteria for this training, you will be invited/invited to come to a practical immersion day during which you will meet the training manager and our HR Business Partner.
