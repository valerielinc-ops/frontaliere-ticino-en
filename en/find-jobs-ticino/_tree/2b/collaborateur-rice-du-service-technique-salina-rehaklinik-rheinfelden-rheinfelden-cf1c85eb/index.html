Reha Rheinfelden, Salina Rehaklinik and Park-Hotel am Rhein AG employ around 1,000 people. To achieve the best possible rehabilitation, interdisciplinary teams work together, with each individual’s expertise and experience contributing to success. Your team The technical service supervises buildings and state-of-the-art facilities in medical and building technology. Specialists from different professions work closely together at our locations of Reha Rheinfelden and Salina Medizin and ensure the implementation of maintenance work on technical facilities, furniture, facilities, devices, aids as well as in public rooms and patient rooms. The procurement and management of the required materials are also part of the task area. As a support for maintenance, our CAFM system serves us. Readiness for regular pikettdienst in the evening and on weekends is also required (within 45 minutes on site). Piketteineinsatz requires independent action, flexibility and technical understanding, even across disciplines.
