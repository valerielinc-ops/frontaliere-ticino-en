Integrate our clinic specializing in the advanced rehabilitation and reintegration of accident victims in Sion, and join our team at the technical foot orthopaedic workshop as: Apprenti·e Bottier/Ere-Orthopedist CFC What are we doing? Each of us at the RRC is committed to supporting patients to become actors in their lives again. We help those who have experienced an accident or have neurological disorders to regain their autonomy and, where possible, to return to work. We are there to meet specific rehabilitation needs: in para or tetraplegic people, amputated, burned or after a stroke or head injury. To learn more about our clinic, visit our website www.crr-suva.ch/ Your main missions

  • You adapt and repair standard shoes;
  • You make tailor-made soles and orthotics;
  • You make test shoes to test and adjust;
  • You welcome patients to understand their needs according to their medical prescription;
  • You mount final shoes: cutting, assembly, sewing, bonding;
  • You take prints from your feet and create a custom mold. What you bring to the team
  • Your attraction and skill for manual tasks;
  • Your investment and collaboration in helping patients;
  • Your sense of curiosity, relationship, your commitment and your benevolence;
  • Your desire to integrate the world of health and be part of our mission. What we expect from you We rely on you to be involved both in your training and in the field. Dynamism, sense of responsibility, precision and learning will be the key words of your journey, not to mention attendance and punctuality that define you fully! What we offer
  • Various missions combining manual know-how, technology and human contact;
  • A structured accompaniment throughout your learning. Our benefits
  • Remuneration above market standards;
  • Low-cost meals;
  • Free access to sports facilities and swimming pool;
  • Time dedicated to your studies to suit your manager.
  • The ability to continue your career at the CRA after your SFA, depending on the positions available. Want to apply? If the world of health resonates in you and you want to join a team that will support you throughout your internship, apply to us!
