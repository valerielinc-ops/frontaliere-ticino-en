Full description

Portrait Lengnau School is a living place of learning and personal development. Our school covers from kindergarten to 9. Around 650 students, accompanied by a dedicated team of 85 teachers.

The continuous development of our school is particularly important to us – both for our students and in the college. The municipality of Lengnau supports us with the necessary resources to provide optimal learning conditions for young people in our modern learning and working spaces.

We are also happy to introduce our school in more detail under the following link: https://youtu.be/9EKn3A0rN2Y?si=NtkdkoyVqmRoZMrf

Tasks

As a class teacher, you will assume responsibility for a 7th real class from 1 February 2026.

They shall teach the following subjects

German French Mathematics RZG ERG WAH Visual design In addition, you give French lessons at an 8th grade.

The ski camp from 8 to 13. February 2026 accompany you as part of the class teacher team.

The workload and timetable are fixed until the summer holidays of 2027. With the partial return of the job holder from August 2027, the workload can be adjusted. Applications for the entire duration of the job or exclusively until the summer holidays 2027 are equally welcome.

Requirements

  • They have a teaching diploma for upper secondary education or are in training.
  • They enjoy working in a team and appreciate the cooperation with colleagues, specialist departments and guardians.
  • They design a learning-promoting teaching, promote the students in their personal development and
  • You take responsibility for the educational, organizational and administrative leadership of your class.
  • You are a dedicated, resilient and humorous personality with pleasure in working with young people.
  • They maintain an appreciative exchange with the guardians.

We offer

A dedicated, open-minded and helpful college awaits you at an innovative school.

We offer

  • An appreciative and collegial cooperation
  • Support through reliable and practical school management
  • Modern infrastructure and contemporary teaching materials
  • School social work, day school and other support services
  • short decision-making paths and room for manoeuvre in class

Tolerance, respect and appreciation shape our school culture. We look forward to welcoming you to our team. Have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your application.

Application interviews are ongoing. The position can be filled as soon as we have found the right personality.
