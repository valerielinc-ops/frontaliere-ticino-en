Nursing Specialist HF, 50-100% permanent position The Ergolz Clinic is a small private hospital with a wide range of surgical patients as well as an outpatient cardiac clinic. To expand our team, we are looking for a motivated and committed qualified HF nurse immediately or by appointment.

Your profile

If you value collegiality, loyalty and team spirit and have training as a qualified nursing specialist (AKP/HF/FH/DNII), then you will fit perfectly into our team. Ideally, you have several years of professional experience in an acute hospital and are a trained vocational trainer or are interested in completing appropriate further training. You act calmly in demanding situations, are resilient, motivated and willing to work in all 3 shifts. Through your experience, friendliness, specialist knowledge and organizational skills, you contribute to the well-being of our patients and support the professional collaboration in our team. You are characterized by good communication skills. Responsible actions and a high level of attentiveness as well as an independent and reliable way of working round off your profile.
