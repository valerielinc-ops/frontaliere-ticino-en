Put to the Point

This body combines scientific methodological competence with the aim of making digital technologies effective in agricultural practice, not limited to a single technology, but with a view to real added value for farm management. We are looking for a personality who combines scientific expertise in digitalization and data science, understanding of agricultural issues as well as leadership and project experience. They are motivated, creative, like to solve problems and develop viable solutions together with others with an openness to new things.

Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal competence centre for research in the agricultural and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. The headquarters is Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is located at Eidg. Department of Economy, Education and Research WBF.

We offer this

We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population. Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring different perspectives. We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Additional information

The research group Digital Production uses information and communication technologies to solve various agricultural challenges. It develops new, data-based solutions to improve the efficiency and sustainability of production systems. In addition to understanding agricultural production processes, computer vision, sophisticated data evaluation and modelling are among our core competencies.
