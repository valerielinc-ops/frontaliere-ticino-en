Full description

Introduction

For our customers in the construction industry, we are looking for a responsible and experienced personality as a crane operator. In this position, you contribute significantly to the safe and efficient handling of the construction projects.

Tasks

  • Operation of tower rotary or mobile cranes according to operational planning
  • Safe lifting, displacement and positioning of building materials
  • Checking loads and monitoring safety rules
  • Perform simple maintenance and functional checks
  • Supporting the construction site team in various activities
  • Documentation of relevant processes and reporting of incidents

Profile

  • Completed training or recognised crane driver’s licence
  • Experience in cranes on construction sites
  • High attention and strong security awareness
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Independent, quiet and reliable working method
  • Team-oriented and resilient

Advantages

  • Varied applications and modern machines
  • Working in an experienced and helpful team
  • Current employment conditions
  • Professional workwear and safety equipment
  • Opportunity for further training
