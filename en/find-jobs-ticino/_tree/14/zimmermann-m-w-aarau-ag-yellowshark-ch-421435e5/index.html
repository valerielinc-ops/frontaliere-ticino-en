Are you ready for a new job as a carpenter!? Our clients from the canton of Aargau (AG) are looking for reliable and experienced carpenters for temporary or permanent employment, starting immediately or by agreement.

02 Your task

Working as a carpenter is varied and exciting.

  • Timber structures, structural erection and timber framing are just as much part of your main tasks as prefabricated construction in the workshop.
  • You enjoy working with machines – while observing all SUVA safety regulations.
  • You enjoy working on steep and flat roofs and are not afraid of heights.
  • Our clients value a collegial way of working and open communication.

04 Your profile

  • Vocational training as a carpenter in Switzerland or an EU country
  • Good German language skills
  • 20 to 50 years old
  • Not afraid of heights
  • Good physical condition
  • Independent and flexible
  • A Category B driving licence is an advantage
  • Knowledge of occupational safety

05 Useful information

Only electronic applications can be considered. Applications sent by post will not be returned.
