Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process.

You are an initiative teacher who

  • holds the Master SHP or MAS IF training, is in training for it or is a primary teacher and is interested in continuing education
  • Emphasis on high teaching quality and cooperation in the team
  • motivated and joyfully fulfills the entrusted tasks
  • gladly actively participates in our school and teaching development
  • Approach people with openness and appreciation and treat them with respect
  • sees itself as part of a team and is committed to our school

We offer

  • Current employment conditions according to cantonal specifications
  • A good working atmosphere
  • Support from colleagues and school management

More on the spot

Further information about our school can be found at www.schule.stadtluzern.ch Children are at the heart of our school. In the spirit of the New Authority, we create a framework characterized by presence, relationship, respect and mutual responsibility. Since this school year we are a day school. This allows us to accompany the children throughout their school day. Learning, mentoring and leisure interlock, creating multiple opportunities for relationship, participation and personal growth. In the student council and in the school garden, the children learn to take responsibility, to take account of each other and to actively shape the school community. This is how participation is lived and learned in everyday life. Wages Internships "Wage system of the city" Other advantages With around 1500 employees and around 6800 apprentices, the elementary school in the city of Lucerne is the largest elementary school in the canton. Whether in kindergarten or basic school, in primary and secondary school, in school support and in needs-based care services: The elementary school of the city of Lucerne offers exciting and versatile activities in the education sector.
