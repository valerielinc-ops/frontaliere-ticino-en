Full description

Portrait The Räbli Safnern school is located in an elevated position with a wonderful view of the Alps. Around 180 children are taught at the school from kindergarten to sixth grade. We see our school as a community in which you take yourself and others seriously, and in which everyone involved can feel comfortable. We are a small college where mutual respect and esteem are very important.

Responsibilities

  • For the school year 2027/28 we are looking for 1. August 2027 a committed and motivated teacher for our newly led, pure 2. Great.
  • Assumption of class responsibility for a pure 2. Class from the school year 2027/28 with accompaniment until the end of the 6. Great.
  • Planning and design of varied and competence-oriented lessons according to Curriculum 21.
  • Individual support and support of the students.
  • Appreciative cooperation with parents, staff and other professionals.
  • Active participation in class and school life.
  • Close cooperation in the class team in the event of a possible division of the workload.

Requirements

  • Teaching Diploma
  • Ability to work in a team
  • Flexibility
  • Pleasure to work in a great team.

We offer

  • A dedicated, helpful and open-minded team.
  • An appreciative and supportive school management.
  • The ability to remove a class from the 2. until the 6. Class and to help shape it in the long term.
  • Space for your own ideas, creativity and personal development
  • An open school culture with respectful and collegial cooperation.
  • A varied and responsible job.
  • Possibility to divide the 100% workload between two teachers.
