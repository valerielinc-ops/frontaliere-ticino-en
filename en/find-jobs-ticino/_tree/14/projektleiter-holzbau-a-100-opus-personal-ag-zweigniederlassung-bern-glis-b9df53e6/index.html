Full description

Are you working as a project manager in the wood sector and ready for the next career step? Then you can expect an exciting position with medium-term development opportunities.

Our mandate is looking for an experienced person from the wood industry who will take on responsibility as a project manager and want to implement further career steps in the medium term.

Their tasks

  • Management and support of demanding projects in the wood sector
  • Technical and economic project responsibility
  • Coordination of customers, planners and project partners
  • Leadership and coordination of project teams
  • Cooperation in strategic and entrepreneurial decisions
  • Active participation in corporate development

Your profile

  • Training as carpenter, carpenter or comparable training in the field of wood
  • Ideally, a further education as a timber construction technician, wood engineer, project manager or a comparable further education
  • Several years of experience in project management
  • Entrepreneurship and initiative
  • Leadership and communicative personality
  • Independent and solution-oriented working method

We offer you

  • Member of senior management
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Medium-term, exciting and clear development opportunity
