80-100%, Chur (GR) / Smart Working

Shape your future – together with us!

This is what awaits you

  • You handle complex claims in the area of ​​property insurance, including commercial and industrial areas
  • You visit damage sites on site - mainly Canton Graubünden, St. Gallen (Sarganserland - See-Gaster), Glarus. In addition, you will assess the facts using video expertise and work closely with experts
  • You are responsible for reviewing and assessing coverage and liability issues as well as the extent of damage
  • You initiate immediate measures, check and take recourse
  • You maintain personal, telephone and written contact with policyholders and their representatives, service providers, contract providers, tradesmen and also with internal departments
  • You have the opportunity to get involved in working groups, training courses and projects

This is what you bring with you

  • You have commercial training in the insurance industry
  • You have a Swiss federal qualification as an insurance specialist. Specialist certificate or diploma Insurance economist: in HF
  • You have very good knowledge and several years of claims experience in the property insurance sector
  • You have good knowledge of liability law and ideally have already gained experience in claims field service
  • You have a good understanding of technical relationships, especially in building technology
  • You are an organizational talent, flexible, have good negotiating skills and assertiveness
  • You are also enthusiastic, resilient and service-oriented
  • You round off your profile with good IT user knowledge
  • Your residence in the area of ​​activity is a plus

In the Customer Operations area, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of a claim or benefit. You are our focus and together we will find the right solution. This is how we ensure maximum customer satisfaction with our customer-first mentality.
