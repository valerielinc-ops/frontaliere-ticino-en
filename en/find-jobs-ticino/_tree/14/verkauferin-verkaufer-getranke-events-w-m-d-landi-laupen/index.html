The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their farms. Together with the brands Volg, TopShop and AGROLA, the LANDI cooperatives also provide the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy. Landi Genossenschaften is successfully active in the Region Bern. In order to strengthen our LANDI shop team in Laupen, we are looking for a sales representative in drinks and events by appointment.
