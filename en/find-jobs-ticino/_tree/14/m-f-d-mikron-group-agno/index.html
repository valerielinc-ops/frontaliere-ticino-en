Full description

Shaping efficiency together Mikron Machining is the leading operator in customized and highly productive manufacturing systems for the production of complex and high-precision metal components. For our after-sales service, we are looking for a 100% (m/f/d) Electrical & Software Engineer, to be inserted at our headquarters in Agno.

Main responsibilities

  • Quickly respond to customer needs, ensuring fast response times
  • Provide the necessary technical assistance remotely or on site so that the customer can restore the correct operation of the machine
  • Participate in development projects for Service Solutions products (upgrade, retrofit & digital service)
  • Collaboration in the preparation of offers and in the execution of projects
  • Promote the sale of Mikron Service products
  • Working with the technical area to solve and avoid future machine problems (supports product care)
  • Training courses for Mikron customers

Your profile

  • Degree of study in mechatronics or electronics
  • Logic knowledge of PLC programming
  • CNC Basic Knowledge
  • Knowledge and reading of electrical schematics
  • Experience in electrical wiring
  • Good knowledge of English
  • Travel availability (min 60% per year)

They will provide preferential title

  • Knowledge of Bosch platforms - Siemens - Fanuc – Mitshubishi
  • Previous experience in similar function
  • Programming HMIs & SCADA
  • German language knowledge

What we offer

  • Innovative and dynamic working environment
  • Highly technological product portfolio
  • Access to cutting-edge tools and technologies in the industry
  • Collaboration with a team of passionate and competent professionals
  • Continuing training on new technologies
