Full description

Butcher Meyer in Turtmann has stood for quality, tradition and innovation for generations.

To strengthen our great team, we are looking for committed specialists in the areas of:

Processing – Do you love traditional crafts and have experience in meat processing and product innovation?

Sale – You are customer-oriented, familiar with high-quality meat products and enjoy selling?

Management – Do you have business know-how and a sense of successful business management?

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for the respective departments (management, processing, sales)
  • Ensuring quality and hygiene rules
  • Customer advice and presentation of our regional specialties
  • Further development and optimization of our processes

Your profile

  • Completed training as a butcher, meat specialist or relevant experience in sales/management
  • Passion for regional products and high quality standards
  • Independent, reliable and team-oriented working method
  • In management: management experience of advantage, development opportunity

We offer you

  • A secure workplace in a traditional company
  • Attractive working hours without slaughter
  • Training opportunities and development prospects
  • A motivated team and a modern working environment
