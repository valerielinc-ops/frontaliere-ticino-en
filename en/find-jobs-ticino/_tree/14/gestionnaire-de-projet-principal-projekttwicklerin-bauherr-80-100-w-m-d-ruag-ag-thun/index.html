Full description

Responsabilita

  • Active management and design of strategic building projects in a unique real estate portfolio by taking over the entire project management and contractor representation in the SIA phases 11 to 31 (in area development and object planning)
  • Moderation of user needs, translation into a structured demand/user program, coordination of location and variant studies, development of realisation models, costs/benefits and risk balancing, as well as preparation of recommendations and decision bases for bodies
  • Linking objectives, scope, budget and dates with the ownership strategy and management of projects with integration of factory planning, operation (FM) and ability to successfully implement

Requisiti

  • Higher education in architecture, civil engineering or a comparable discipline
  • Ideally, an in-depth training at university level, e.g. in business, sustainable construction or project development
  • 5–8 years of experience with industrial or logistical construction projects on planner or owner side, including renovation/renovation in stock
  • Funded knowledge of the early stages of planning, in particular SIA subphases 11 (degree formulation), 21 (meability study) and 31 (preliminary project)
  • Impressed communication and moderation strength to act as an eloquent builder towards administration, public and internal stakeholders
  • negotiating skills and enforcement capacity in the management of planning offices, entrepreneurs and internal stakeholders
  • Detection of conflicts between production, architecture, FM, law and cost and development of pragmatic and easy to communicate solutions
  • Structured, independent operation, sattelfest in classic project management (cost/term/quality) and in handling popular PM tools
