Manager Market Management Southern Region — PostAuto AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The strategic development of public transport interests? Do you want to play a key role in market management and participate actively in the transition to electric mobility? As part of this demanding management function, you are responsible for the evolution of the market in an important region and you shape the future of CarPostal with various stakeholders. With you, the sustainable development of regional passenger transport is a success.

Make a difference

  • As a member of the CarPostal Market management team, you assume the overall responsibility for managing the market in the Southern Region (Central Switzerland and Ticino) and you maintain relations with internal and external stakeholders.
  • In addition, you manage the key process in regional passenger transport, negotiate offers and targets agreements and guarantee a correct, time-bound and grant-based statement of benefits, allowing you to make an important contribution to the company's success.
  • You are responsible for maintaining revenue in your region and you are primarily responsible for managing Key Account Management for the canton of Ticino in terms of staff and organization.
  • In addition, you develop and optimize network and schedule offers, alternative propulsion modes and on-demand solutions in close consultation with Product and Planning, and you use your expertise in a targeted way.
  • You coordinate collaboration with the various Business Units and actively participate in the transition to electric mobility and in improving competitiveness.
  • In addition, you are responsible for regional marketing communication and coordinate it with the main marketing strategy, using your strengths effectively.
  • You represent CarPostal in regional TP bodies, you develop market and sales plans for your region and you assume organizational responsibility for the mandate of the Arcobaleno Business Management.

What you bring

  • You have received advanced training in business economics, law or technology and ideally you have completed continuing training in public transport.
  • In addition, you have a long experience in managing and selling, as well as in-depth knowledge of public transport and the policy framework conditions in your market sector, all of which will bring the balance to your advantage.
  • You show entrepreneurship and synthesis, you act entrepreneurially and you convince by your ability to impose.
  • In addition, you express yourself concisely and convincingly and effectively use your excellent rhetorical abilities, allowing you to best value your skills.
  • You have extensive knowledge of business management and are able to leverage your experience in strategic management.
  • You have very good knowledge in Italian, oral and written, and you have good knowledge in German, which you still wish to perfect in order to be comfortable communicating in a national environment.
