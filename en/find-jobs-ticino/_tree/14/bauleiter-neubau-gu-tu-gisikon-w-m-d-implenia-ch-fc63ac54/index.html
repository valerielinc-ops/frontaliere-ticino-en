Full description

As a leading Swiss construction and real estate service provider, Implenia develops, realizes and manages living spaces, working environments and infrastructure for future generations in Switzerland and Germany. Implenia also offers tunnelling and related infrastructure projects in other markets. The company combines the know-how of highly qualified consulting, development, planning and execution units under one roof into an integrated, multinationally leading construction and real estate service provider. Headquartered in Opfikon near Zurich, Implenia employs more than 8,000 people (FTE) across Europe and achieved a turnover of CHF 3.5 billion in 2025. More information at implenia.com

We are looking for a construction manager: in execution GU/TU for our office in Gisikon. With us, you can expect exciting projects ranging from business and residential buildings to wooden high-rise buildings to industrial buildings and we offer you a modern working environment. In addition, lean principles as well as BIM are not only theory for us, but are implemented comprehensively and consistently in our projects. 20 years after its founding, Implenia is now optimally positioned and ready for a successful future.

You move that

  • As a construction manager: you lead, realize and coordinate new construction projects from the start of the project to the defect-free handover as a GU/TU
  • You actively target and lead the awarding process to subcontractors
  • You lead construction sites in a team taking into account the safety, timing, qualitative and quantitative specifications
  • You are the hub on site and the first point of contact for subcontractors, suppliers, planners, builders and authorities

This is why you succeed

  • Dipl. Construction manager:in, technician:in TS, building designator:in or equivalent education/training
  • Several years of construction management experience in projects in the field of new construction (GU/TU)
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • You are team-ready, a good organizer, responsible, results-oriented, flexible, resilient, proactive, efficient and have a healthy assertiveness

This is what we offer you

  • A multicultural working environment with the chance to shape a future worth living
  • State-of-the-art technologies and a contemporary workplace at a sustainable company
  • A company that promotes talent and the training of its employees
  • The security of a financially healthy, broad-based group with strong roots in Switzerland
  • An exciting selection of benefits:
  • 5 weeks holidays and 9 paid bridge days
  • an in-house pension fund with very good coverage and 3 contribution levels
  • numerous discounts on well-known suppliers of products and services
