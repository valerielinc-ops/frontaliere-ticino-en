The Position A patient’s path from initial diagnosis to treatment and follow-up is complex. Our mission at Roche is to improve this pathway in ophthalmology and create real value for patients, the healthcare system and our company. As Customer Partnering Manager (m/f/d), you are the key to the successful introduction of our next innovative product in ophthalmology. Your contribution is essential to bring our innovations where they are most needed.

This is what awaits you

  • Launch Readiness & Strategy: You will accompany selected surgical eye centers on the way to market our innovations in ophthalmology.
  • Customer contact & sales: You act as the primary interface for target customers in your area and use your communication and sales expertise to promote the appropriate use of our portfolio with suitable patients.
  • Partnering & network: You build strategic partnerships with relevant stakeholders that go beyond purely medical exchange.
  • Scientific exchange: You communicate current research results on a scientific level and answer complex inquiries objectively and transparently.
  • Cross-functional collaboration: You work closely with internal experts and bring valuable market insights to interdisciplinary teams.

You bring that with you We are looking for a committed personality with a strong customer focus who enjoys building networks and innovating in healthcare.

  • Legal qualification: Qualification as a pharmaceutical consultant: in accordance with § 75 AMG and a valid driving license.
  • In-depth training: completed university studies (medicine, natural sciences, business administration) or a comparable qualification.
  • Industry experience: Experience in a customer-facing role in the pharmaceutical industry as well as in-depth knowledge of the German healthcare system and its regulatory framework.
  • Strength of communication: Confident knowledge of German and a very good understanding of English literature and studies.
  • Digital competence: Secure application of modern IT tools (e.g. Google Suite, MS Office) as well as experience with digital and hybrid communication channels.
