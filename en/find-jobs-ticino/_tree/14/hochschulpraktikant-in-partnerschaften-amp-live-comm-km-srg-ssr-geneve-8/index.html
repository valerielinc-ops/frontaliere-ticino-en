Full description

The KM Channels & Production department is responsible for the strategic and operational management of the national communication channels (intranet, social media, newsletter, website, etc.), community management and national partnerships KM. It ensures the attractive, consistent and target group-oriented preparation and playout of communication content.

This is your contribution.

In the Channels & Production KM department of SRG SSR, you will be involved in the following topics from December 2026 (or by agreement):

  • Cooperation in the field of live communication, for example in the organization and implementation of internal and external events (guest management, support evenito, event organization, etc.)
  • Cooperation in the management of partnerships (communication, marketing and activation measures, ticket raffles, etc.)
  • Cooperation in the development and implementation of communication measures for publishing
  • Coordinative and administrative tasks

They inspire us.

  • Completed studies (uni/FH) in media or communication sciences or comparable training in the field of communication, sponsorship or live communication
  • Initial experience in the areas of sponsorship, live communication or corporate communication
  • Strong interest in the Swiss media landscape, audiovisual media and communication
  • Stylistic German or French with good oral and written knowledge of the other language
  • Experience with modern communication means and MS office instruments, experience with common CMS is an advantage

So we inspire you.

  • Insight into various communication areas of an electronic media house (newsroom) and into various partnerships and projects of live communication
  • Multilingual working environment
  • Exciting team with a good spirit and an open communication climate

The application period ends on 12 November. Thank you for your interest.

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion formation and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.
