Full description

With more than 100 employees at 12 locations in the Saaser, Matter and Visper Valley, the experience bank Raiffeisen Mischabel\-Matterhorn is committed to its customers with a lot of heart and passion.

Are you interested in a working environment that combines cohesion, exciting tasks and attractive working conditions? We look forward to your application!

Expert coach real estate (m/f/d)

What awaits you?

  • You enjoy accompanying builders in new construction, conversion and renovation projects.
  • As an experienced professional, you assess real estate in terms of renovation and modernization needs and support our customers in obtaining, assessing and comparing offers.
  • When coordinating construction projects, you always keep an eye on quality, costs and construction progress.
  • You are the contact person for technical questions of our employees and help them to develop further in your field.

What do you bring?

  • You have several years of experience in project management in the construction industry and are very familiar with all construction phases (according to SIA).
  • You have a strong understanding of quality, costs and deadlines as well as a structured and independent working method.
  • You enjoy advising customers and are a strong, solution-oriented and responsible personality.
  • You have good digital knowledge and experience in dealing with the common digital tools in construction and project management.
  • Ideally, you have experience or further education in the field of energy consulting.

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du\ culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We offer flexible working models

The work\-life\ balance of our employees is important to us. That is why we create the conditions for flexible working hours – with offers for mobile work, home office and part-time holidays.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
