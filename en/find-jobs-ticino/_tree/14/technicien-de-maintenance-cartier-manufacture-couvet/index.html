Our 10,000+ colleagues of more than 105 nationalities are united by a shared independent spirit and commitment to excellence, striking to continuously rich our Maison

WHAT YOUR IMPACT IS

  • You ensure the corrective maintenance with reactivity and effectively diagnoses the breakdowns.
  • You plan and perform preventive maintenance in order to limit stops and failures.
  • You analyze recurring breakdowns and implement improvement actions.
  • You coordinate external interventions with our partners and suppliers and ensure follow-up.
  • You install, connect and commission the new equipment.
  • You deploy continuous improvement on the existing machine fleet.
  • You help manage spare parts and secure critical equipment.
  • You are the guarantor of training in routine interviews and support for users on our equipment.
  • You ensure compliance with OSH rules and good practice during interventions.

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE

As a Maintenance Technician, you are distinguished by your organizational capacity, autonomy and analytical capacity. Controlling the numerically controlled machining machines, you can demonstrate an excellent team spirit and human qualities. For the benefit of an automatic CFC or equivalent training, you have a first 3-year work experience during which you have shown dynamism and autonomy. You master IT tools, ideally have SAP knowledge and are attracted to new digital tools.
