Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

Are you the kind and upstanding person who welcomes our patients with courtesy and respect and keeps the overview and calm in hectic situations? Would you like to work in an established, motivated and well-established team near the sea? We are looking for an immediately or by agreement

Medical secretary/in Clinic Anesthesiology 40-100 %

You do.

  • Personal reception and first contact of patients in German and French
  • Telephone service, appointments, provision of information and keeping patient dossiers in German and French
  • Management of our digital anesthesia consultation
  • Organization and implementation of consultation hours of our medical team
  • Service input in Ibicare and other administrative work
  • Close cooperation with medical staff as well as internal and external bodies
  • Ensuring holiday representation

That sets them apart.

  • Socially competent team-capable personality with reliable, efficient, exact and independent working methods, organizational flair with a pronounced service provider and coordination thinking for a responsible, exciting and varied activity in a lively environment
  • Medical secretary or medical assistant (MPA)
  • Ideally, work experience with knowledge of medical terminology
  • In-depth knowledge of PC users (Microsoft Office, electronic medical history)
  • Mother tongue German or French with good knowledge of the other language (word and writing)

They benefit from it.

The healthcare sector and thus also the Biel Hospital Center are in an exciting transformation phase, in which initiative and self-responsible action are required. You will have the opportunity to actively shape the future of the hospital center with a motivated team. Our personnel policy is family-friendly, with a company-owned KITA and contemporary employment conditions as well as good accessibility by public transport.
