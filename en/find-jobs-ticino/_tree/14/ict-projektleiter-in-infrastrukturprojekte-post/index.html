Full description

Ready to help shape the future of Post? In this role, you will lead challenging infrastructure projects from planning to successful implementation and ensure that our locations are technically optimally networked.

With you, we successfully implement infrastructure projects throughout Switzerland and create solutions that are noticeable for employees and customers on a daily basis.

You can do that.

  • You will lead infrastructure, rollout and migration projects from initialisation to successful completion.
  • The responsibility for deadlines, costs, quality, risks and sustainable project success lies in your area of responsibility.
  • You coordinate the cooperation between internal and external stakeholders as well as various specialist areas.
  • The successful implementation of infrastructure projects will be coordinated on topics such as WAN, WLAN, LAN, firewalls, access controls, time recording, video surveillance, building automation and other technical infrastructures.
  • For clients and management, you will develop project assignments, status reports and decision bases.
  • You identify risks at an early stage, initiate appropriate measures and ensure that projects are implemented in accordance with the applicable standards and methods.
  • Regular assignments throughout Switzerland enable you to accompany infrastructure projects directly on site and create sustainable results.

You bring that with you

  • You have a higher education or further education in computer science, technology, economics or project management.
  • Several years of experience in managing infrastructure projects, ideally related to IT, building or construction projects, distinguishes you.
  • You are very familiar with classical and agile project management methods
  • Knowledge of HERMES 5.x is an advantage.
  • Communication strength and a professional approach to different stakeholders set you apart.
  • Your working method is analytical, structured and solution-oriented.
  • High personal responsibility, assertiveness and decision-making are among your strengths.
  • You consider complex requirements holistically and develop pragmatic and customer-oriented solutions.
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of French round off your profile
  • English skills are a plus.
