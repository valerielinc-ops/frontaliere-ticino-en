Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. We create transparency in the banking and insurance market and advise our customers comprehensively and neutrally. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements for our pension foundations in our branch in a central location in Bern.

  • You handle varied business cases independently in the pension fund administration and work on projects. The most common business cases are entry and exit of insured, homeownership advance payments, purchases, retirements and various benefit cases.
  • You provide insured persons and other stakeholders with competent and independent support in all administrative matters. As the first point of contact, you ensure a reliable, solution-oriented and customer-oriented handling of all concerns in day-to-day business.
  • You are responsible for invoicing contributions and processing incoming payments. In addition, you support the accounting in the annual accounts.
  • Commercial training completed
  • Interest in the field of occupational pensions and an affinity for numbers
  • Rapid perception and motivation to learn new things
  • Secure communication in German, good oral knowledge in Italian and French beneficial
  • Enjoying a dynamic and service-oriented environment, reliable and exact working methods
  • After careful training, you work independently and gradually take on more responsibility.
  • Also for your further education you are at the right address with us. We support you individually and support you in the context of your job profile also in external further education.
  • Our surveys on satisfaction regularly confirm that our employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
