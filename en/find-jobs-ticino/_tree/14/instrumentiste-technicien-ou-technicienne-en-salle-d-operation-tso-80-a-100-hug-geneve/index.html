Description of post

As an instrumentist, technician or operating room technician (TSO) in the operating rooms, you ensure the patient's reception, installation and safety. You are responsible for preparing the necessary equipment for surgery and controlling its proper functioning. You assist the surgeon and you are in charge of the technical activities and the specific care of the operating unit in accordance with the rules of good practice, especially in the field of l'asepsy and hygiene rules. Your commitment and collaborative spirit allow you to actively contribute to interprofessional teams. You are actively involved in thinking about improving the quality and safety of care. You take part in the supervision of trainees, students and students within the operating rooms. You will have the opportunity to participate in highly specialized surgical procedures. Our institution also offers many opportunities for continuing education and career development.

Qualifications

You have a diploma as an ES technician. With excellent relational qualities, you love teamwork and easily integrate new professional environments. You have excellent abilities to manage unforeseen and stress. Your autonomy, rigor and ability to set priorities make you a valued professional.
