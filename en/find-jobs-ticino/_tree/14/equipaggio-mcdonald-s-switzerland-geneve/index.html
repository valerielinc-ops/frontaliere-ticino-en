Job description

As a crew member, you are at the heart of our restaurant. You work with great colleagues, serve our guests with a smile and provide unforgettable moments. Whether at the counter, in the kitchen or in the team – your energy and enthusiasm make the difference.

Main tasks

  • Friendly and efficient service for our guests
  • Preparation and distribution of delicious food and drinks
  • Collaboration in the team to achieve goals
  • Creating a clean and welcoming atmosphere
  • Flexibility and positive attitude even in hectic times

Requirements

  • Passion for making people happy
  • Team spirit and reliability
  • Willingness to learn and develop
  • Energy and enthusiasm for a dynamic environment
  • No previous knowledge required – motivation counts!

Benefits

  • Flexible working hours for a good work-life balance
  • Diverse tasks in service, kitchen and counter – boredom excluded
  • Supportive team and opportunities to learn for life and career
  • Remuneration and working conditions in accordance with the provisions of the collective employment contract (L-GAV)
  • 50% discount on our products in our restaurants in Switzerland and Liechtenstein
  • Sunrise subscriptions at a reduced price
  • Access to exclusive discounts via the "Corporate Benefits" platform
  • Additional holidays offered for over 50s
  • Free access to the largest online training library
