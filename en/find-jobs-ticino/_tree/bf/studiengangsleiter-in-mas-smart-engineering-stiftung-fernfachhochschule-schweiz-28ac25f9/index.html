Head of MAS Smart Engineering (40%)

The Distance University of Applied Sciences Switzerland (FFHS) offers part-time Bachelor's, Master's and further education courses according to the blended learning method. For our currently about 3100 students we employ 180 employees as well as over 550 (part-time) lecturers. Whether in teaching, research or administration, as a responsible employer, we count on committed employees who are willing to support our vision of time- and location-independent studying.

In order to reinforce the Department of Economics and Technology, we are looking for a person at our location in Zurich or Brig from now on or by agreement

These are your tasks

  • Strategic orientation and operational management of the degree programme
  • Content design and quality assurance of the modules and the curriculum taking into account current market requirements
  • Representation of the degree program internally and externally through active relationships with students, industry representatives and other stakeholders
  • Student acquisition and counselling as well as aptitude assessments of interested parties
  • Financial management of the degree programme (budgeting and cost control)
  • Lectures and presentations
  • Departmental management and teaching in the Department of Economics and Technology

This is what you bring with you

  • Open and team capable personality as well as high service orientation
  • Completed university degree (Master) in the relevant subject area, preferably with a doctorate
  • Didactic qualification and high affinity for e\-learning (blended\-learning) desirable, or willingness to develop further in this area
  • Affinity to teaching and research
  • Readiness for subject- and method-specific training
  • Network and understanding to leverage synergies with internal and external partners

You can expect that from us

  • Reconciliation of work, family and professional development
  • Motivated and experienced employees
  • Attractive employment conditions and good training opportunities
  • Modern working environment in the new campus in Brig or Zurich

For technical information, our Department Head of Economics and Technology (@, tel: \+ ) and our Head of HR (@, tel: \ \) are at your disposal.

We look forward to your application with the usual documents until 15\.11\.2026\.
