Full description

The actual location of this location is in Visp, Switzerland. Eligible applicants and their families will be assisted in moving if needed.

To strengthen our logistics team, we are looking for a committed, reliable and flexible personality as Logistics Specialist BioLOG (m/f/d).

In this versatile function, you support the operational logistics processes in central logistics. They take on cross-team tasks in goods receipt, warehouse management, production supply (picking & material provision) as well as in the outbound and shipping area. You ensure compliance with all relevant GMP, GDP, security and quality requirements.

Their tasks

  • Implementation of incoming goods processes including goods control, identification and storage of materials.
  • Ensure proper storage management in compliance with GMP/GDP requirements.
  • Processing of picking and order picking orders for timely supply of production.
  • Provision and transport of materials for production and operational processes.
  • Implementation of shipping activities and temperature-controlled transport for internal and external customers.
  • Complete documentation of all storage, transport and shipping processes in accordance with regulatory requirements.
  • Ensure compliance with GMP, GDP, IATA, ADR and other relevant guidelines.
  • Active participation in inventories, process optimizations and continuous improvement measures.
  • Flexible support for all logistics areas within central logistics according to operational requirements.

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training in the field of logistics, supply chain or a comparable field of expertise.
  • Experience in the areas of goods receipt, warehouse logistics, production supply or shipping is advantageous.
  • Ideally, knowledge of GMP-/GDP-regulated environments as well as in dealing with dangerous goods regulations.
  • High quality, safety and customer orientation.
  • Structured, independent and reliable working method.
  • Teamwork, flexibility and willingness to provide cross-cutting support.
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage.
  • Readiness for flexible working time model incl. Picetting performance
