Full description

The White Arena Group in LAAX is a pioneer in alpine tourism and an enthusiastic community for unique mountain adventures. With mountain railways, hotels, gastronomy, sports and rental shops and the LAAX School, we create unforgettable experiences for our guests - and attractive workplaces for our employees. Three houses, three different worlds, a common aspiration: special guest experiences with personality. In the signinahotel, the rocksresort and the Riders Hotel, alpine lifestyle, modern hotel experience and individual hospitality meet. Whether a stylish hotel, high-quality serviced apartments or an urban meeting point with a restaurant, yoga, gym and club – each house has its own character and offers our guests something special. As a Hospitality Services Circle, we connect reservation, distribution and systems and ensure that bookings flow efficiently, correctly and smoothly from first contact to check-in. | Revenue Management is for you more than adjusting prices – and distribution more than putting channels online? You understand how price, availability, systems and booking methods work together and enjoy when everything is bookable where it should be at the end. As Revenue & Distribution Coordinator, you are the operational interface between Revenue Management, Hospitality Services and our system and distribution landscape. You implement the revenue strategy in daily business, optimize prices and inventory in the short term and ensure that our hotels are booked correctly and optimally via all relevant channels. What You Move:

  • Steer Revenue: Observe pick-up and demand and optimize prices and inventory with focus on the last 7 days before arrival.
  • Optimize inventory: You control availability and room quotas in the PMS (protel).
  • Ensure bookability: You monitor prices, availability and offers across all channels.
  • Manage distribution: You maintain interfaces, channel managers and partner APIs.
  • Implement deals: You create and publish promotions, packages and partner offers from the acquisition
  • Solve problems: You analyze errors in rates, mappings and reservations and coordinate their correction.
  • Ensure quality: You check new offers and distribution channels for their operational and technical feasibility.
  • Develop further: You recognize optimization potential and improve revenue and distribution processes. What makes you:
  • Experience in revenue management, reservation or distribution in the hotel industry.
  • Good understanding of PMS, Channel Manager, booking platforms and interfaces.
  • Number affinity and understanding of price, demand, utilization and turnover.
  • Structured working methods and the ability to make pragmatic decisions in the short term.
  • Enjoy troubleshooting and sustainable solutions.
  • initiative and responsibility for your topics.
  • Enjoy working with Operations, Revenue, Reservation and external partners.
  • Secure communication in German and English.
  • Experience with Protel, Duetto, OTAs or hotel APIs is a plus. | An attractive workplace in the middle of an extraordinary mountain world is certain for you. You become part of a dynamic, bright team and can help shape the further development of LAAX. With us you will find an environment where professionalism, speed, pragmatism and humanity go together. Look forward to numerous employee benefits, such as free mountain rides, perks in our shops, hotels and restaurants, and benefits for your family. Start your professional adventure at the Weissen Arena Group with your complete online application.
