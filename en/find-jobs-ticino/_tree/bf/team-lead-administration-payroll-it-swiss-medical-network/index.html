Full description

  • Responsible for the HR Region Ticino team, with autonomous management of the administrative activities and salary processing of the assigned clinics, as well as support in the operational and organizational management of the team composed of three employees.
  • Responsibility for the proper administrative management of personnel during the entire life cycle of the collaborator (Employee Life Cycle), from hiring to ending the working relationship, including HR administration and wage management (excluding recruitment).
  • Support in the correct and timely processing of wages, in collaboration with internal and external partners.
  • Support in the management of all social insurance issues (AVS, LPP, LAINF, sickness-loss insurance, source tax, etc.).
  • Monitoring and updating of personal data, work contracts, work certificates and all relevant HR documentation.
  • Reference person for employees and managers on administrative and social insurance issues.
  • Participation and responsibility of sub-projects aimed at optimising HR processes, digitizing and introducing new HR IT tools.
  • Completed commercial training, supplemented by a professional improvement in human resources (at least federal professional certificate of Specialist in human resources or equivalent training).
  • Multiannual experience in the administration of the staff, preferably accompanied by a first experience in running a team or in a function of replacing the manager.
  • Great knowledge of the Swiss social insurance system, the processes of wage processing and the administration of staff.
  • Excellent mastery of MS Office tools and HR computer systems.
  • Structured, precise and reliable working method, accompanied by a strong sense of responsibility and high quality standards.
  • Spiccate communication and relational skills, service orientation and proactive approach in the search for solutions.
  • Ability to work effectively also under pressure, assertivity and excellent social skills.
  • Excellent mastery of German and Italian, both written and spoken (native level or equivalent). French knowledge is an important added value.
  • A diverse and responsible position, with development prospects in a modern HR environment.
  • The possibility to combine operational activities with strategic issues and actively contribute to the design and optimization of processes.
  • Interesting conditions of use, with flexible working hours and possibility of working in the home office.
  • Support for professional development through targeted continuing training offers.

Swiss Medical Network is one of the leading private groups of clinics and hospitals in Switzerland. With 21 clinics and hospitals and more than 60 outpatient clinics in 15 cantons, we offer high quality medical and hospital health services in all medical specialties and in all three language regions of the country. Our state-of-the-art infrastructure and highly motivated staff ensure medical care at the highest levels.

Applications in German language will only be considered.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
