Full description

Your field of activity

  • You are responsible for the professional development and governance of the BKW Group-wide transfer pricing framework
  • Transfer pricing models for Energy Production, Energy Markets, Infrastructure & Buildings and central service functions are further developed, implemented and monitored by you.
  • You ensure Group-wide transfer pricing compliance with OECD-compliant documentation, intercompany agreements and guidelines as well as value-added, functional and risk analyses.
  • For strategic business initiatives, reorganizations, acquisitions and international expansion projects, you will contribute your tax expertise in an advisory capacity.
  • You identify, evaluate and control transfer pricing risks and accompany tax and transfer pricing checks at home and abroad as well as APA and MAP procedures
  • Tax governance as well as the digitalization and automation of the transfer pricing processes you actively develop

Your expertise

  • Master’s degree in Business Economics or Business Administration as well as additional training in tax law, for example as a dipl. Tax expert:in, or a comparable degree
  • You have several years of experience in the transfer pricing of an international corporation or in a Big Four / tax consultancy
  • In-depth knowledge of the OECD transfer pricing guidelines as well as international tax and compliance requirements sets you apart
  • You combine your analytical and conceptual skills with a structured, independent working method
  • You communicate safely with finance teams, management and tax authorities and move safely in German and English
  • Your experience in managing cross-divisional projects as well as a strong understanding of consulting round off your profile
