Full description

Simplify together. Because life is full of possibilities. At the same time, it became complex. That is why we are looking for employees who work with us to promote simplicity.

They shall

  • as active support of the responsible GL member, implement the value proposition in the customer service and products segment
  • manage cross-channel market development for the retail segment and promote sustainable growth through targeted campaigns and sales processes
  • act as an interface between sales and internal partners (product management, marketing, IT) and manage projects independently
  • Ensure efficient controlling based on relevant KPIs and initiate appropriate measures in the event of deviations
  • further develop sales processes for the segment, define the consulting process and create tools for sales management
  • participate in the training and certification of our customer consultants in the area of plant and prevention

They bring

  • 5 to 7 years of professional experience in the digitally supported sales management of a financial service provider
  • ideally 3 to 5 years of professional experience in customer consulting
  • a completed higher technical school or a degree at university or university level
  • Strong analytical-conceptual skills and a strong goal and result orientation
  • a resilient, down-to-earth personality with high self-motivation and strong communication
  • Negotiation-proof and very good written knowledge of French (in-depth technical discussions and business correspondence possible)
  • Joy of working in interdisciplinary teams and the will to build segment-specific knowledge

About us Valiant is an independent Swiss financial services provider operating exclusively in Switzerland. It offers private customers and SMEs a comprehensive, easy-to-understand offer in all financial matters. Valiant is locally anchored in the following 15 cantons: Aargau, Basel Landscape, Basel City, Bern, Freiburg, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Vaud, Zug and Zurich. It is also present throughout Switzerland thanks to innovative, digital services. Valiant has a balance sheet total of 38.2 billion francs and employs around 1200 employees – of which more than 70 are apprentices.
