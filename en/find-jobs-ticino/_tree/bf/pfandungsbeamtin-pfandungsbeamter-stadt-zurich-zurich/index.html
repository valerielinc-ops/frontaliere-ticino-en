Full description

For our attachment department we are looking for 1. February 2027 or by agreement a competent and committed professional.

Support our four attachment areas in Springer function

  • Preparation, implementation and post-processing of seizures, retentions and arrests
  • Inclusion of official findings
  • Writing of qualified correspondence
  • Service of operating documents
  • Commercial education, several years of professional experience and experience at an enforcement office or in the debt recovery and bankruptcy law
  • independent, efficient work as well as a strong service thinking
  • Joy of direct contact with debtors* and other customer groups
  • High load capacity
  • Exact, committed working method
  • good IT user knowledge (MS Office, BEA.NET) and a high IT affinity
  • demanding, independent and varied work in a committed humorous team
  • Own Office
  • Reliable employer with progressive social benefits
  • Place of work at a central location in the district 4
  • Fully digitized office with its own health management

The management office in the multicultural district 4 not only operates people who have come into financial distress. In addition, it has a wide range of other tasks, such as municipal business or cooperation with various stakeholders. We take enough time for the parties involved in the process and maintain an appreciative friendly approach in the team. With our prudent way of working, we contribute significantly to maintaining social peace.
