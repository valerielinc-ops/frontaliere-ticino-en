Portrait Welcome to the Neumatt Primary School!

From 1. December 2026 we are looking for you as a class teacher at the 3rd grade (25 lessons)

The Belp elementary school has twelve decentralized kindergartens, attached to the school facilities Dorf, Mühlematt and Neumatt. In addition to additional supporting lessons (MR), students can receive targeted and individual support in an enrolment class, in an intensive German language development course. A day school, a well-developed elective subject and supplementary offer (AdS), the voluntary school sport and the task help expand the offer of the managed schools.
