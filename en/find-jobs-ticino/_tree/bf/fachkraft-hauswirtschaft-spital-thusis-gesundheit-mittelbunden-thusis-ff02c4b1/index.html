Our housekeeping team is looking for reinforcement!

Immediately or by agreement we are looking for one

Specialist Housekeeping 50-100 %

Task area

  • Maintenance and basic cleaning of all kinds in all premises of the hospital incl. OP
  • Cleaning of staff apartments in Sois
  • Cooperation in the company’s own laundry and coordination with external suppliers
  • Willingness to help out in all other hospitality departments (hotel, cafeteria and kitchen)

  • Shared responsibility for the implementation of the hygiene concept, cleanliness and value preservation

Requirement profile

  • Flexible and responsible personality

We offer
