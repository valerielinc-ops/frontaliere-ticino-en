Full description

Who are we?

The mission of the Food Safety and Veterinary Service SAAV is to ensure, through its inspections and analyses, that food and usual items do not endanger or deceive the health of consumers. It ensures the welfare and health of animals for themselves and the benefit of human beings.

Why join us?

Do you want to work for the entire population of the Fribourg region and join a committed team of diverse and complementary profiles? Join us to practice a meaningful profession, within a service of human size, while benefiting from the stability and resources offered by a large company that is the state of Fribourg.

In addition to this stimulating setting, we offer you attractive conditions: pleasant working environment, business restaurant, ergonomic workplaces, state-employer services and continuing training. Promotion of bilingualism through training in the partner language. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • As a deputy cantonal chemist and member of the SAAV's management, you support the cantonal chemist in the definition and implementation of the service's strategy for the control of foodstuffs and usual objects and you replace it in his absence.
  • As section chief, you lead the chemistry and biology laboratories of the service, which you verify and ensure the quality and compliance of the services as well as the analysis reports.
  • You oversee the implementation of the VAS quality management system and monitor laboratory indicators and objectives.

Profile desired

  • Master's or Ph.D. from a university, a FPS or a HES in chemistry, biochemistry, food sciences or natural sciences, supplemented by a federal degree in direction of official food control (DDCAL) or the desire to obtain it, in accordance with the federal order in force (RS 817.042).
  • Professional experience in an accredited analytical laboratory (direction and development), several years of training and/or management experience, ideally with experience in leading change.
  • Strong interest in the public service, good knowledge of its functioning as well as cantonal and federal structures.
  • Experience in communication, planning and organisation as well as in the management of computer systems (including LIMS).
  • Resistance to stress, ability to impose and ability to manage crises.
  • Rigourous, precise and organized.
  • French or German language with very good knowledge of the other language.
