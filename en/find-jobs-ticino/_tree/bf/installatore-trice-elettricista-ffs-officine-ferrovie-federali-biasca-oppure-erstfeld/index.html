Electricist installer 60% | FFS - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Electrical installation/machine Biasca or Erstfeld, 01.01.2027, 60% Apply here

What we offer.

A varied stimulant job awaits you and an innovative work environment where you can lead great changes and thus contribute to the digitization of FFS. Together with your team colleagues, you will develop solutions to current challenges and implement them as part of the team. You will have the opportunity to train and develop. For us are important working conditions interesting, even in case of irregular hours. Nocturnal work and weekend work is further paid by us, for example with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. Also, you will receive bonuses of time. This means you will have more free time than employees who do not work at night and on weekends. Our other benefits, such as children's allowances, free GA subscription in 2nd class and discounts for travel abroad - also for the family - are available on our website under the heading "Benefits". What you can move.

  • In this versatile role, you will perform new constructions, extensions, provide support and solve problems on electrical and telecommunications installations.
  • On the order of the service, you will carry out the service of picket, you will analyze and eliminate the disturbances on the plants in operation.
  • You will ensure the implementation of security measures (according to RTE 20100).
  • It is expected daily and night work in the week and weekend.
  • Residence in the region of Ticino or Erstfeld so that you can respect the indicated intervention times.

The one you bring with you.

  • You're self-employed and you know how to team up. You are able to work under pressure so as not to lose the overview even in hectic times.
  • You have completed a professional training as an AFC electrical installer (4 years), with relative experience.
  • Experience in the field of construction and maintenance of the telecommunications and electrical installations of the Base Galleries of Gottardo and Ceneri constitute preferential criteria.
  • Have you obtained or are willing to achieve Self-Protection Training Works (AP L), First Aid Training SBB Level 1 IAS CB and training «Working under tension».
  • You have IE power switching authorization depending on the available plants and a qualification for strong current systems.
  • You have excellent oral and written knowledge of the Italian language and, preferably, good knowledge of German and have the valid license to conduct category B vehicles.
