Full description

Welcome to Medbase Zurich HB Permanence

In our Medical Center in the middle of Zurich, directly in the main railway station, we look after patients without prior notification 365 days a year. Our team, consisting of almost 60 employees, consists of specialists in general internal medicine, laboratory employees and MPAs and covers the clinical, diagnostic and therapeutic spectrum of general and internal medicine. At Medbase Permanence Hauptbahnhof Zurich, we ensure a balanced work-life balance. Working part-time is possible in all functions, and working hours can be made flexible.

For this position, we are looking for a person who likes to work in the evening hours and appreciates the benefits of late service – more days off, less rush time and a quieter start to the day.

You can move that Do you want to work in a dynamic and innovative environment where you can fully develop your strengths, know-how and competencies? We create an inspiring environment where your ideas and contributions are appreciated and you have the chance to make a positive and meaningful impact on people’s well-being. You enjoy working late on duty, bring organizational talent, discretion and teamwork, like variety and challenges? Then you are exactly right with us!

These are your tasks

  • Reception, triage, care and farewell of our walk-in patients
  • Processing of patient requests by phone (little) and e-mail
  • Perform blood sampling and various laboratory tests
  • ECGs, Lufu`s and X-rays
  • Infusions and injections
  • Assistance in small surgical procedures and other emergencies
  • Management and electronic billing of patient records
  • Ensuring smooth practices

What you bring

  • Training as MPA EFZ
  • Further development in dose-intensive X-ray is advantageous
  • Organizational talent, communication strength, reliability and an independent and structured working method
  • Joy and pride in the meaningful activity as MPA
  • Empathy, professionalism and absolute discretion
  • Practical use of digital work equipment, good PC knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, other foreign languages advantageous
  • Joy and preference for late services (approximately 3 p.m. to 11.30 p.m.)
