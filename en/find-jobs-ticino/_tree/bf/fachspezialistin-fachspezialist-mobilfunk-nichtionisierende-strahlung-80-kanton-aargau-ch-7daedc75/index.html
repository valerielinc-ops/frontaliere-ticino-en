Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Radiation protector: As a member of our eleven-member team, you are responsible for the implementation of environmental protection legislation with regard to non-ionizing radiation and accompany projects to inform the population
  • Mobile phone tester: Together with your work colleague, you approve conversions and new builds of mobile phone systems, keep yourself up-to-date with the current state of technology and are therefore always on the pulse of mobile phone development
  • Digitalizer: You bring your digitalization flair into the team and lead our NIS implementation into the digital future: management and further development of our NIS database, development of a digitalization solution for our archive with over a thousand mobile systems
  • Multi-talent: You also bring know-how in air pollution control or noise protection? Excellent. Our section needs your support
  • Team player: Collaboration with other professionals, authorities, the population and mobile operators are part of our daily business

What you bring

  • University of Applied Sciences Department of Electrical and Information Technology, Physics or equivalent technical or scientific training
  • Professional experience in the field of mobile communications and / or enforcement of NISV or willingness to familiarize yourself with this topic
  • Strong communication personality with very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Strong negotiating skills with a goal- and result-oriented working method

In the Department of Construction, Transport and Environment, we are actively developing the diverse habitat Aargau in the areas of space, energy, environment and mobility – today and for future generations. The Department of Environment performs a wide range of tasks in various environmental disciplines for the long-term and sustainable protection and preservation of our natural resources.
