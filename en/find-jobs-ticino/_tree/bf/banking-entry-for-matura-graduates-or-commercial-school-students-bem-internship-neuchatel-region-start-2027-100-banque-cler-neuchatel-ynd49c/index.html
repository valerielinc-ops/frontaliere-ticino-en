Entrée dans le monde bancaire pour les maturant(e)s ou élèves de l'école de commerce: stage BEM - région de Neuchâtel (Rentrée 2027) 100%

Have you obtained your Matura and want to dive straight into the professional world? Then the BEM internship at Bank Cler is your ticket to hands-on experience: in 18 months, you will become a banking professional.

In fact, Aron, Jeremy, Antoine and other interns are looking for motivated reinforcement.

Here is what awaits you

During these 18 months of internship, you will benefit from numerous opportunities for your personal development, you will be right at the heart of the action, you will gain valuable experience through three varied assignments, and you will dive into the banking universe, for example:

  • Customer service at the counter or by phone
  • Account opening, payments and payment operations
  • In-depth advice on loans, investments and banking products for private and corporate clients
  • Exciting projects and insights into digital banking solutions
  • And obtain a Switzerland-wide recognized banking certificate (BEM).

And since you are stepping on the gas, other great things await you!

  • Exciting opportunities to progress: managing a branch later on? It might sound strange today, but if you invest yourself, you can evolve within the group and discover new exciting fields. Discover the portraits of our employees here.
  • Events that create connections: meet other interns and employees at numerous events organized throughout the group.
  • More for your wallet: for example with Reka checks, sports offers, and an SBB general abonnement.

Your learning locations

📍 Our bank branches in French-speaking Switzerland Your training location with us, where you will be supported and guided by experienced trainers.

📍 CYP (Challenge Your Potential) Here you will attend inter-company banking courses and deepen your banking knowledge through modern learning methods.

📍 Internal sales training and specialized courses We offer you various training courses that will prepare you for consulting, customer communication, and the sale of banking services.

You are a fit for us if...

  • you love communicating with people
  • you are interested in the world of economics and the banking sector
  • you will have completed your academic Matura, commercial professional Matura (commercial employee VET or commercial school) or specialized Matura by August 2027
  • you have achieved good to very good school grades in French, mathematics, economics, English and German
  • you are reliable, motivated and ready to learn new things
  • you like taking responsibility and working independently and in an organized manner

Does this sound like you?

Then apply today for your internship starting in August 2027 and send us the following documents:

  • CV
  • School reports from the last two years

And since we are looking for a motivated intern and not an advertising copywriter, you can skip the cover letter. Instead, we would appreciate it if you could answer three short questions in the application tool. Thank you!

Do you have questions?

Contact Maryline, Training Manager (maryline.bilello@bkb.ch / 021 310 34 61): she knows all the answers to your questions regarding the internship.

Or contact our intern Aron directly (aron.sadiku@cler.ch / 021 310 34 07) if you want to know what it is really like.
