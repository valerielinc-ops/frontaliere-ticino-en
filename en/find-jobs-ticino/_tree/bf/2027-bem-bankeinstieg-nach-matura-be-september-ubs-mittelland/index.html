Full description

Your role Do you want to go straight into practice after graduation, take on responsibility and experience the world of banking first hand? As part of our Junior Talents, you start your professional future at UBS. You are involved in day-to-day business from the beginning, rotate through different teams, support in the customer and product environment and analyze cases and prepare discussions.

This is what awaits you with us

  • An 18-month training course with exciting insights into our various areas and offices
  • During three six-month rotation assignments, you work in customer and product consulting (physical & digital) and learn a lot about topics such as money, loans, mortgages and payments.
  • In each assignment, a practical instructor who accompanies and promotes you
  • an ideal entry into banking with prospects for your further career

Your education Your education is practical, varied and focused on your development. In addition to your work as a team, at the CYP (cyp.ch), you will acquire the theoretical foundations of the banking subject together with students from other banks. In addition, you regularly attend internal specialist training courses. So you get to know our products and services in detail and can apply your knowledge directly in everyday work – optimally prepared for the final exam.

At the end of your training, you will receive the certificate “Bank entry to Matura”, issued by the Swiss Bankers Association (SBA). Many of our graduates then move directly to a permanent position with us or develop further in our two-year young talent development program (JUNA).

If you would like to know more about our BEM program and the required application documents, please click here.

Your team As a member of our UBS BEM trainee community, you will meet regularly for internal and external training, events and joint activities. This often creates friendships and networks that exist beyond the training period.

At UBS, we focus on modern technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) to continuously improve our services – including in your team. In this way, we make processes more efficient, use data more intelligently and make everyday work easier – for employees and customers.

Your skills and experience We are looking for curious, independent young people who like to learn, take on responsibility and appreciate the exchange with others – in the team as well as in contact with customers.

It is important that you bring one of the following degrees

  • High school maturity (preferably with a focus on economics)
  • Specialist Maturity
  • KV EFZ with professional maturity acquired outside the banking sector
  • KV EFZ with vocational maturity acquired outside the banking sector via commercial medium school

Do you recognize yourself again? Then you are exactly right with us!

You also convince by

  • Joy in dealing with people and a strong team spirit
  • Motivation and a positive basic attitude
  • High willingness to learn and openness to new things
  • Interest in the financial world, working with AI & modern digital tools

Perhaps you are already studying and realize that you lack practical relevance? Or have you gained initial experience in sales or customer-oriented activities? Think about how your past successes and experiences match the skills we are looking for.

The employment dates for our BEM trainees are in March and September. Please note that you can only apply for one start date and one region per year.
