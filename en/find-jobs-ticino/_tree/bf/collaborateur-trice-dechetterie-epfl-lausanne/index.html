Main tasks and responsibilities

Your duties and responsibilities as a waste disposal worker

Main tasks

  • Collection of AI/SV EcoPoints

  • Collection of specific containers and sorting cabinets

  • Strong sense of service, cross-cutting collaboration, ability to find solutions and manage multiple priorities under pressure with a range of stakeholders

  • Good mastery of French
  • English level B1, an asset
