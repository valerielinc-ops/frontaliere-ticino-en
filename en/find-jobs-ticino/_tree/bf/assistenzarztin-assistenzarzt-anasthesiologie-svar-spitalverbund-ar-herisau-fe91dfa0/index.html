Full description

Their tasks

  • Our goal is to accompany you step by step on the way to the specialist title of anesthesiology
  • After careful training, you take on tasks independently – always under the supervision of an experienced specialist.
  • They conduct preoperative educational interviews and support patients as competent and empathetic contact persons
  • With respect and responsibility, you accompany your patients through the anesthesia and the entire perioperative phase
  • Step by step develop and deepen your practical skills in general and regional anesthesia
  • Depending on your qualifications and progress, you will grow into more advanced tasks, e.g. in hall management, including efficient, economical and, above all, safe processes.

Your profile

  • Curiosity and enthusiasm for our specialist area with the aim of acquiring the specialist title of anesthesiology
  • Willingness to dare new things and accept challenges
  • Professional, reliable and appreciative appearance as well as a corresponding communication style
  • First experience as an assistant doctor – be it in anesthesia or another specialty with direct patient contact – is an advantage
  • They bring with them the ability for self-reflection, willingness to learn, team spirit and a responsible way of working
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing (native language or at least level C1)
  • You have completed a Swiss state examination or a recognised medical degree from an EU/EFTA country
