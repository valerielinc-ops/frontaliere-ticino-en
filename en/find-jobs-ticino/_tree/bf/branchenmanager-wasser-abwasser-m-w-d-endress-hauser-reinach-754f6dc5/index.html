Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress happens by working together. As the global leader in measurement instrumentation, our over 18,000 employees shape the future in the field of process automation. Whether developing and realizing new technologies as a team, collaborating to build innovative instrumentation, or strengthening vital relationships with global industries, we create trusted partnerships that help everyone thrive. As a family-owned company, we look beyond short-term goals and focus on creating long-term value for our people, our customers, and the world around us.

We are looking for an Industry Manager Water & Wastewater (m/f/d) to join our Product Center Flow team and drive the strategic growth of one of the world's most important industries. In this role, you will connect market needs, customer challenges, and innovation initiatives to help shape sustainable water and wastewater solutions for customers worldwide.

At Endress+Hauser Flow in Reinach, Switzerland, we develop and manufacture leading flow measurement technologies used across a wide range of industries. Together with colleagues around the globe, we support our customers in improving efficiency, sustainability, and operational excellence.

What is the role about?

Do you want to influence the future of the global water and wastewater industry? Are you passionate about translating market needs into innovative solutions and business strategies? Do you enjoy working with international sales teams, customers, and industry experts to create measurable impact?

In this global function, you will play a key role at the intersection of market development, portfolio strategy, innovation, and commercial execution. Working closely with global Industry Networks, the Strategic Industry Group Water & Wastewater, and cross-functional teams, you will help strengthen our position as a trusted partner in a rapidly evolving industry.

Which tasks will you perform?

You will

  • develop and implement the global Water & Wastewater industry strategy for our Product Center organization.
  • shape and continuously strengthen our portfolio and value proposition for the Water & Wastewater market.
  • act as the key interface between market requirements and innovation activities, ensuring customer and industry needs are reflected in future solutions.
  • represent our Flow Product Center within the Strategic Industry Group Water & Wastewater and regional Industry Networks.
  • enable and develop our global sales and representative network by strengthening industry, application, and technology expertise close to the customer.
  • represent Endress+Hauser at international conferences, exhibitions, industry committees, strategic customer accounts, and partner organizations.

What do we expect from you?

You

  • hold a degree in Engineering or a related discipline; an MBA is considered an advantage.
  • bring several years of international experience in process instrumentation, ideally within Water & Wastewater or Utilities.
  • have demonstrated success in developing and executing sales or market strategies and combine strategic thinking with a strong commercial mindset.
  • possess a solid understanding of flow measurement technologies and their industrial applications.
  • communicate effectively in international and cross-functional environments and enjoy building strong collaborative relationships.
  • speak business-fluent English, ideally complemented by German language skills, and are willing to travel internationally up to 30%.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-owned, highly committed company with a sustainable long-term vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer, and e-learning programs.
  • We support work-life balance through flexible working conditions and an environment built on trust and personal responsibility.
  • We offer an international and collaborative workplace where your ideas and expertise can make a visible impact.
  • We provide modern working environments, attractive social benefits, and access to innovative technologies.
  • We give you the opportunity to contribute to sustainable solutions that help customers manage one of the world's most valuable resources: water.
