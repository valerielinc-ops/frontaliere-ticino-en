[Zenhäusern Brothers SA]

Explore the taste of adventure with us!

In the heart of Valais and with its 40 years of history, our hybrid company, driven by the passion for flavours, is looking for committed and motivated personalities. From crispy bread to divine sweets, each creation celebrates authenticity.

Join a team of some 470 talents, where continuing training and career development flourish in a modern work environment. Reliability, Dynamism and Excellence \- our values form a corporate culture that places people at the centre.

Are you ready to actively build your future and become the artisan of your success?

Sheath worker (m/f/d) – 100%

Do you want to put your technical expertise at the service of quality products and gradually evolve towards more responsibility? This job is for you! We are looking for an experienced person in the manufacture of processed pasta and dry cake products, ready to fully invest and build its evolution with us.

Missions

  • You carefully produce the flakes, pastries and dry cakes according to our quality standards.
  • You control the various stages of manufacturing and ensure the regularity and quality of the products.
  • You are actively involved in the organisation and continuous improvement of the sector.
  • You bring your technical expertise to the team and contribute to the development of your colleagues' skills.
  • You take initiatives, identify potential problems and propose appropriate and effective solutions.
  • You contribute to the proper functioning of the sector by ensuring that quality, hygiene and safety standards are met.
  • You are close to the field and actively participate in the dynamics and performance of the team.

What you bring:

  • You have a solid experience in the field of toured pasta, flaking and/or dry cake, as well as very good knowledge of manufacturing processes.
  • You work independently, organized and rigorously, with a true sense of responsibility.
  • You stand out for your initiative, your ability to identify problems and your focus on finding solutions.
  • You have natural leadership and know how to unite, communicate effectively and advance a team.
  • You are oriented to quality and attach great importance to precision, regularity and continuous improvement.
  • You appreciate the fieldwork and want to put your know-how at the service of a dynamic and developing sector.

Your benefits:

  • 5 weeks of vacation
  • 13th salary
  • Increase pay 25% night work
  • Breakfasts offered
  • Loyalty premiums
  • 20% discount on our products
  • Discounts at different partners (fleet discount, fitness, etc.).
  • Free parking

Date of Entry: Immediately or to be agreed
