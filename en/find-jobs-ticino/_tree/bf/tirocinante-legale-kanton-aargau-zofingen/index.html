Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Assistance to the prosecutor in conducting criminal proceedings (inquiry and conclusion of preliminary proceedings)
  • Recordings
  • Accompaniment in the piketting service
  • Participation in court hearings (including making a plea under guidance)
  • Writing of complaint responses to the High Court
  • Redacting delegations and coercive measures, clarifying legal issues, drafting orders and correspondence

What you bring

  • Successfully completed Master's degree as a lawyer or lawyer
  • Interest in the activity of a prosecutor
  • Very good verbal and written expression
  • Precise and reliable working methods

The internship serves for the first professional experience and, among other things, also to prepare for a bar examination. The internship usually lasts at least 6 months.

Your working environment

We are responsible for criminal proceedings against adults in the canton of Aargau. We lead the preliminary proceedings and prosecute crimes and offenders in close cooperation with the police. We issue criminal orders, bring charges and represent the state criminal claim in court.
