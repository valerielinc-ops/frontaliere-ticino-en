Full description

In our snack bar-panettery in Via Buffi in Lugano (USI area) is part of the Fornaio and is open from Monday to Friday, from 7:00 to 17:30.

The source

  • is responsible for the reception and service to customers and the operational management of the snack bar-panetteria, both for the sale of bakery products and cafeteria
  • care for the planning, introduction and support of about nine employees/users
  • ensures compliance with quality standards, customer service, hygiene and food safety
  • collaborates in the realization of commercial activities with the other units of the Source
  • is responsible for the vocational training of employees/users, in particular the basic training of employees.

Requirements

  • federal certificate of capacity in hospitality or catering and Cantonal diploma of exercising
  • professional experience of at least 5 years in the field, strong customer orientation and established objectives
  • interest and attitude to the accompaniment of groups of collaborators with disabilities
  • sense of responsibility, dynamism, autonomy and ability to cooperate constructively with different professional figures within the foundation
  • interest in contributing to the further development of the offer to customers and professional skills of employees-users
  • will be given preference to residents

The first activity is scheduled for December 2026. The Management of the Source awaits with interest by 31 October 2026* the applications accompanied by a letter of motivation, curriculum vitae, certificates of training and work, references in pdf format to [recruiting@lafonte.ch] (mailto:recruiting@lafonte.ch)
