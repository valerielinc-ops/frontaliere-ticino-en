Your key missions

  • You receive and distribute meals according to established quality standards.
  • You prepare and set up salad buffets according to the production plan.
  • You guarantee a seamless, respectful and responsive service for beneficiaries.
  • You apply hygiene, food safety and HACCP procedures, while ensuring temperature control and traceability of foodstuffs.
  • You are involved in the maintenance of kitchens, equipment and workspaces, as well as the sorting of waste.
  • You accompany the beneficiaries involved in the cooking activities, pass on good hygiene practices to them and ensure the proper running of meals.
  • You ensure the storage of foodstuffs, the tracking of stocks and the information of service documents (temperatures, cleaning, presences).
  • You report any anomalies or malfunctions to your head or central kitchen.
