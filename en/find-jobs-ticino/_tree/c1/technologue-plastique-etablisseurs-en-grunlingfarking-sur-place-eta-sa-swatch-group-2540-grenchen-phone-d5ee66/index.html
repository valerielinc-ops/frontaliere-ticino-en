PLASTICS TECHNOLOGUE / ESTABLISHERS/IN GRÜNLINGFARKING, on site | Manufacture Horlogère Suisse | ETA SA

PLASTIC TECHNOLOGUE / ESTABLISHERS/IN GRÜNLINGSCHMARKING, on site Together with your teammates, you work as needed in day-to-day operations or shift operations and ensure the demanding qualitative and quantitative production standards. This includes the following activities:

  • Setting up and programming of injection molding machines and automation systems
  • Participation in the entire sampling process and optimization of existing production processes
  • Ensure production volumes and collect operational data (e.g. SPC, MDE)
  • Support of the group leader, especially in the training and training of employees
  • Detection and reporting of deviations and ensuring a stable production process
  • Co-responsibility for the maintenance and functioning of the facilities
  • In addition, you participate in projects and work closely with the areas of quality assurance, laboratory, tool construction and logistics.
  • Your skills and competenciesEducation as a plastics technologist EFZ or installer with many years of experience in injection molding
  • Enthusiasm for injection molding and MIM technology as well as precise work in the micro area
  • Readiness for 2-shift operation (including possibility for 3-shift operation)
  • Enjoying process optimization and actively shaping improvements
  • Good technical understanding and high quality awareness
  • Secure handling of common IT systems and applications
  • Independent working methods and teamwork in a cross-divisional environment
