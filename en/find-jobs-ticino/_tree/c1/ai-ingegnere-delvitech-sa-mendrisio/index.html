Full description

Job Summary Delvitech stands at the forefront of technological advancement, having pioneered innovative Automatic Optical Inspection (AOI) technology for assembled printed circuit boards (PCB) utilizing artificial intelligence. Our accomplished team is driven by a commitment to pushing technological boundaries and consistently delivering exceptional results. We are looking for an experienced AI Engineer to join our R&D team. This is a software engineering role, not a data science position: the ideal candidate builds production software that combines both classical computer vision and AI solutions, has a track record of delivering production-ready solutions, and can take technical ownership of complex projects. The role offers the opportunity to work on challenging problems in an industrial environment, driving the development of innovative solutions for automated optical inspection and advanced image analysis, while also providing technical guidance and mentorship to other members of the team. Education

  • Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Physics, Mathematics, or another relevant scientific discipline. Requirements (all three are needed together)
  • Software engineering: proven professional experience designing and implementing reliable production software; expert-level Python, object-oriented programming (preferably Java or a similar language), clean code and testing.
  • Computer vision: extensive, proven experience in image processing and computer vision, particularly using OpenCV (classification, object detection, segmentation, feature extraction, anomaly detection).
  • AI (not limited to LLM-based applications): hands-on experience with deep learning applied to images, including designing, training, optimizing and deploying models with at least one major framework, preferably PyTorch. Also Required
  • Experience integrating computer vision and AI algorithms into production systems, ideally in an industrial or product-development setting.
  • Experience working with real-world image datasets, including data preparation, augmentation and labeling. Nice to Have
  • Experience with 3D computer vision, 3D reconstruction.
  • Knowledge of C++. What We Offer You will join a young and highly technical R&D team, building production software that brings machine vision, computer vision and AI into industrial inspection systems. If you are a passionate software engineer looking for a challenging opportunity to develop innovative machine vision and AI solutions, we would love to hear from you. Delvitech is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
