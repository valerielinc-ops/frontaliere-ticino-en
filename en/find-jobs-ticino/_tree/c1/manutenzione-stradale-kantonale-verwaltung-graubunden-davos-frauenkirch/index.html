Full description

The civil engineering office is responsible for the design, construction, maintenance and operation of the cantonal roads as well as the hydraulic engineering and slow transport in Graubünden. We also take care of the operational maintenance of the national roads on the cantonal territory. The canton’s road infrastructure comprises over 1,450 artificial buildings, including bridges at airy heights of up to 90 meters and tunnels of up to 3 kilometers in length. The challenges that the nature of our mountain canton brings in summer and winter make our work demanding and versatile. As a Friendly Work Space certified office, the health and satisfaction of our approximately 480 employees are particularly important to us. For this reason, we are committed to systematic occupational health management (BGM). We are looking for the road maintenance department in the district 5 Davos per 1. September 2026 or by arrangement a roadkeeper foreman Tasks

  • Management and organisation of a maintenance group as well as active participation in the group
  • Responsibility for the implementation of operational maintenance on the assigned road section and control of the road condition and road installations
  • Organization and operational planning of the summer and winter service
  • Organization of clean-up and repair work after accidental or natural events
  • Performing pikettdienst (summer and winter) Requirements
  • Completed apprenticeship in the construction industry
  • Additional training for foremen or equivalent training
  • Craftsmanship and economic handling of equipment, machines and materials
  • Organizational talent, enjoyment of teamwork, personnel management and administrative tasks (creation of deployment and holiday plans)
  • Good IT user knowledge
  • Category C driving licence is advantageous
