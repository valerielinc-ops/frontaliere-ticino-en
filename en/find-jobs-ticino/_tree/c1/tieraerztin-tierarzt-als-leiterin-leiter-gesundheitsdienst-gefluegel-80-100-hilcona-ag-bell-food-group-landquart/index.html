Full description

The poultry health service at Bell Switzerland plays a central role in ensuring animal health along the entire value chain - from parent stock management to live poultry production and slaughter. In close collaboration with our contractual producers, internal departments and external partners, we implement high standards in hygiene, animal welfare and quality. Due to the upcoming retirement of the current position holder, we are looking for a motivated and experienced leader with a veterinary medicine background. In this key role, you will be responsible for the overall management of the poultry health service of Bell Switzerland AG. You will report directly to the Head of Animal Production and manage your department together with the Deputy Head of Health Service and will also be responsible for the laboratory together with the Head of Production Laboratory Poultry. Tasks: Leadership and development of employees according to leadership principles and personnel strategy. Responsibility for goal achievement, operational processes and veterinary medication storage. Creation of instructions and guidelines in consultation with the Head of Animal Production. Quality assurance in the area of responsibility. Development of concepts for live poultry production and animal health. Elaboration and implementation of regulations and hygiene guidelines. Veterinary care of live poultry in cooperation with the poultry advisory service. Conducting controls (TAM controls of poultry farms, hygiene controls in the hatchery) and vaccinations of parent stock. Participation in HACCP and Q teams as well as internal audits and projects. Maintenance of relationships with producers, customers, suppliers and authorities. Support for the further development of poultry farming within the framework of Bell integration. Ensuring information flows and documentation. Requirements: Completed studies in veterinary medicine with diploma and doctorate or PhD as well as authorization to practice in Switzerland. Experience in poultry medicine. Leadership experience and structured, thoughtful working style. Very good German language skills in writing and speaking, knowledge of English and French (possibly with B2) is an advantage. Willingness to work telephone pick-up service on weekends. Good IT skills (MS Office). Resilience, flexibility, loyalty and discretion. Goal-oriented, performance-oriented and service-oriented.
