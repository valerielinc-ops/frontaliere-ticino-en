What we offer.

As a traffic manager, trains keep track of the railway network assigned to you and your work station equipped with about eight screens, coordinates and monitors trains. Under normal operating conditions observe traffic, manage operations and ensure a smooth running of the service. In case of disturbances, operational management takes place: analyse the situation, initiate the necessary measures and solve the problem so that normality can be restored as quickly as possible. In this way you share responsibility for mobility in Switzerland. Without you, the network stops. The work is of systemic relevance and has an immediate impact: co-ordinates people and technology, make quick decisions and directly contribute to the safety and reliability of railway traffic. We offer you a second paid training as a traffic manager for trains lasting eight months. After successfully passing the final exam you will work in the Pollegio exercise facility and will help to ensure that every day 9000 passenger trains and 2000 freight trains reach their destination safely and on time. Learn more about the professional profile at the next event. Consider irregular working hours and shiftwork as an opportunity to enjoy leisure while others work. We receive night and Sunday work with an additional allowance: for example, 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For nighttime use, you also receive time supplements between 10 and 30%, which allow you to have more free time. Find benefits, such as free AG and travel abroad at discounted prices (also for the family), on our website at the Benefit page. What you can move.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
