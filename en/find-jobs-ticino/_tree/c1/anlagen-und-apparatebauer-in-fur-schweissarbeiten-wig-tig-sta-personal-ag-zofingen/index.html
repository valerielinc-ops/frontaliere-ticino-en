Full description

Finished metal structures and components according to technical drawings. The company attaches great importance to quality according to ISO 9001 and other standards. You will find versatile projects and short decision paths. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Producing welding structures according to technical drawings
  • Perform work according to QS specifications according to ISO 9001, EN 729 and EN 15085
  • Perform intermediate tests on manufactured products
  • Make final inspections of manufactured parts
  • Manufacture and assembly of metal structures and components

Requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship as a metalworker or comparable qualification
  • Welding experience and strong craftsmanship
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Solid technical understanding of metalworks
  • Flexible working in daily use

Offer

  • Multifaceted projects with structural steel, stainless steel and aluminium
  • Short decision paths in a flat organization
  • Opportunities for professional development
