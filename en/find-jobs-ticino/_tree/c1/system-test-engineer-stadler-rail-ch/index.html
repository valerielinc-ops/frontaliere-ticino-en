Full description

EXPERIENCE

  • Technical training at the level of HES, EPF, university or equivalent continuing training, supplemented by relevant professional experience in a technical environment
  • Experience in testing, analysis and validation of complex technical systems, ideally in railway, signalling, safety systems or automation
  • Good knowledge of electronic engagements (ESTW) and a strong understanding of system architectures, interfaces and communication protocols
  • Experience in the planning, execution and documentation of system testing, integration and reception
  • Analytical spirit, high quality and safety orientation and a structured and autonomous working method
  • Collaborative personality, with excellent communication skills and enjoying work with engineering, development, project and client teams
  • Excellent knowledge of French or German, with good knowledge of the other language
  • English is an asset
  • Availability for regular client site interventions as well as occasional night and weekend interventions
  • Driver's licence category B

Additional assets

  • Experience with testing and error management tools
  • Knowledge of relevant railway standards (e.g. CENELEC)
  • Experience in commissioning and automated testing procedures

LIABILITY

  • Plan, execute, analyze and document test activities in implementation projects
  • Perform manual and automated laboratory tests as well as on customer facilities
  • Analyze test, operational and diagnostic data, and perform fault analyses on existing facilities
  • Write and maintain test cases, test documentation and audit reports
  • Participate in the definition and optimization of test strategies, methods and tools
  • Support the configuration and setting of electronic activation systems (ESTW) in accordance with system and project requirements
  • Provide technical support to project teams during implementation, commissioning, receipt and certification phases
  • Ensure solutions that comply with standards, economically viable and customer-oriented, while respecting quality requirements
  • Participate in assembly, installation, commissioning and other project tasks
