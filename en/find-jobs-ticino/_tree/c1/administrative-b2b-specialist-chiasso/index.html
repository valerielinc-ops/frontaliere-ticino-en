The setup

  • Administrative B2B Specialist
  • Working model: Hybrid
  • Team: Partnership
  • .com, a dynamic global company of 1,200 people powering millions of holiday journeys. The Partnership team manages a growing portfolio of B2B partners, and we need a person who becomes their administrative point of reference: someone who understands how our partnership business works, with its flows and specificities, and the ability to translate business and Finance needs into functional requirements and use cases for Tech teams. Good project management skills, including dependency analysis and coordination across processes, systems and teams. Sitting between the Partnership and the Administration & Finance teams, you will make sure that our partners' sales are correctly reconciled, invoiced and covered, bringing the needs of the team and of our partners to Finance, and Finance's requirements back to the team. You will own your tasks end to end with autonomy, and you will use data tools and AI smartly to make our current processes faster and more reliable. If you are precise with numbers, curious about how a business works behind the scenes and keen to improve the way things are done, keep reading! ## Key Responsibilities Be the contact point of the Partnership team with the Administration team, and Administrative department of partners, for all administrative tasks, and the administrative reference for our partners.
  • Work closely with the Partnership team to learn the business flows and each partner's specificities, and represent the needs of the team and of the partners with Finance
  • Verify on a monthly basis, using Excel, the sales data communicated by the partners against the data in the company systems, investigating and aligning any discrepancies.
  • Provide the Administration team, on a monthly basis, with the data needed to issue accounting documents to the relevant partners, taking full end-to-end responsibility to ensure all internal and partner administrative steps are completed and numbers are properly reconciled.
  • Create and manage Purchase Orders to cover the team's payable invoices.
  • Manage the assigned tasks autonomously, from start to finish, meeting monthly deadlines.
  • Propose and implement improvements to the current flows, also leveraging AI tools and automation to optimize activities.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
