Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our approximately 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a Project Manager in Marketing Sales (m/f/d) to complement our team and strengthen our sales projects with technical know-how and strategic foresight.

What is this point about?

Do you enjoy solving technical challenges? Do you love working with different teams and international partners? Do you want to actively contribute to strategically positioning our production site?

Then you can expect a role where you can combine technical expertise with entrepreneurial thinking and make a real impact on our sales projects.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • edit sales and customer-related project questions in a small, motivated team.
  • clarify technical issues with internal specialist departments and external suppliers.
  • Develop pricing strategies for projects and accompany their implementation.
  • represent the interests of our production site within the organization.
  • Develop process improvements within and outside your area and ensure their implementation.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have completed technical or scientific studies or a comparable education.
  • bring at least three years of experience as a project manager (ideally with PMI or IPMA certification) or embedded engineer in the field of instrumentation.
  • have communication skills and the ability to understand complex issues.
  • can explain technical content in German and English – also directly to our international customers on site.
  • You will quickly familiarize yourself with new topics, share your knowledge and appreciate cooperation in an international environment.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through diverse training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support your work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport.
  • We live family-friendly policies, including childcare places and childcare services during the holidays.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.

Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding.
