Full description

Mission

Within the framework of this function, attached to our Development & Industrialization Service, your mission will be to carry out autonomous projects industrialization of products. You will organize and host technical meetings between Methods, Production and Quality, as required. In addition, you will actively participate in continuous improvement actions.

Responsibilities

  • Define the means to be used for the start-up of product production.
  • Participate in the definition of production processes, feasibility studies.
  • Develop production records while ensuring their standardization.
  • Manage industrialization from design to life-long transfer.
  • Coordinate the progress of manufacturing orders during the production phase.

Profile sought

  • Bachelor in mechanics or microtechnics.
  • At least 7 years of experience in a similar position.
  • Experience in the watchmaking field or in the field of the high-end dial.
  • Very good mastery of office tools (MS Office, including Excel).
  • Excellent relationship and diplomacy.
  • Team spirit and ease of cross-sectional collaboration.
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis, critical and methodical.
  • Strength of proposal, meaning of negotiation and persuasion.
