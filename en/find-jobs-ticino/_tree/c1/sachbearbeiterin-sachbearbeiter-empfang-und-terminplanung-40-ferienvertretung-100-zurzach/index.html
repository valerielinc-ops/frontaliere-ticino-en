Full description

In our rehabilitation clinic Basel in the Claraspital, we treat patients especially in the field of internistic and oncological rehabilitation through modern and patient-centered care. In order to ensure seamless service operations and the satisfaction of our guests and patients, our valued specialists provide high-quality and diverse services every day.

The regular workload is 40%. During the holiday representation a 100% work assignment is required.

Your tasks

  • Scheduling: Create patient therapy plans and take care of entry and exit management
  • Patient management: Record patient data in our clinic information system (KIS) and create patient dossiers in contact with assignment
  • Administration: Assist with general secretarial work such as mail processing and telephone control
  • Information: Be the information and contact point for patients, visitors and employees
  • Finance: You are responsible for the cash system incl. Monthly accounts
  • Organization: Support the further development and implementation of patient administration processes and structures

What you bring

  • Training: Commercial or paramedical training completed
  • Experience: Several years of experience in a similar function, preferably in healthcare
  • IT knowledge: Very good user knowledge (Word, Excel, planning systems, etc.)
  • Languages: Very good knowledge of German in Word and Scripture
  • Personality: You are characterized by a high service concept, an independent working method as well as a strong organizational and coordination talent and convince with teamwork, flexibility and reliability

Your benefits

  • Co-creation: Join a new modern work environment and get actively involved
  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts at various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers and pharmacies
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits in the thermal baths FORTYSEVEN and the thermal bath Bad Zurzach
