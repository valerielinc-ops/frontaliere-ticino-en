Full description

The Healing Education School Center (HPSZ): One school - four locations. We teach and support our students from kindergarten age to school leaving holistically and individually. Our diverse educational offer enables a good school, social and personal development. For the HPSZ Solothurn we are looking for a Stv. teacher / in special school, cycle 2, 34% as of 01.12.2026 or by appointment until 31.07.2027.

Your responsibility

  • As a class teacher, you promote and accompany students during teaching and break times. The percentages to be paid are set on Wednesday morning (4 lessons) and Friday (6 lessons).
  • Together with the multi-professional team, you are responsible for teaching, development planning and organizational and administrative work in connection with class management.
  • You work closely with parents, specialist positions, therapists, other institutions and school management.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a teacher with additional curative education or the willingness to acquire it.
  • Her strengths include high psychological resilience, consistent pedagogical action and genuine interest in relationship work. In challenging situations, you remain sovereign, capable of acting and solution-oriented.
  • Appreciate as a committed, creative and enthusiastic person They work in an interdisciplinary team and like to participate in the further development of the school.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
