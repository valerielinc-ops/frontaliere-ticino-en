Full description

We are looking for a reliable, flexible and committed personality for the function as chauffeur C/CE 100%.

In this versatile position, you play a key role in the smooth flow of goods in our transport network - either with a starting point in the Chur / Domat / Ems area or in the Lugano area - and benefit from targeted internal training.

Your main tasks as a chauffeur C/CE 100%

  • Professional management of the team and ensuring smooth day-to-day operations
  • Loading and unloading of swap containers
  • Supply of customers with trucks and trailers
  • Assignments in shunting operations after passing internal training
  • Active participation in new lifting and shunting concepts as well as continuous process optimization

Qualifications

Your qualifications as our future champion

  • CH driving licence category C/E mandatory
  • Readiness for shift work, assignments from Monday to Sunday and on public holidays
  • Reliable, punctual and responsible personality
  • High performance and operational readiness
  • Language skills: Good communication in German and/or Italian (Bilingualism German/Italian is a big plus).
  • Affinity to modern means of communication and dealing with the computer
  • Good physical condition
  • Experience in the cooling and/or removable bridge sector is advantageous
  • Ambitions for further development as a ranger

Your benefits

  • Option for further training as a shunt A40
  • Varied activity in combined transport
  • Innovative working environment in a dynamic transport company
  • Attractive employment conditions + top wage.
