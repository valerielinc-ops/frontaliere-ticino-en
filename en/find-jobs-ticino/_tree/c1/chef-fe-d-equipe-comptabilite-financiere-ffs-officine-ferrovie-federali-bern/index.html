You can expect a responsible management task with immediate effect on transparency, quality and reliability of the financial figures.   • You will lead the Financial Accounting team professionally and in terms of personnel and develop the team of 7 employees along clear goals and competencies.
  • You control monthly and annual financial statements on time and in high quality and ensure consistent processes and reliable figures.
  • Responsible for the internal control system (ICS) for the Shared Service Organization, you drive optimizations, ensure compliance with accounting standards, guidelines and guidelines.
  • You coordinate the cooperation with internal and external auditing, specialist departments and service units and act as a contact person for accounting issues.
  • With key figures, analyses and continuous improvement (operational excellence), you ensure stable processes and measurable progress.
  • You develop digital end-to-end processes in accounting and use standardization, automation and AI for greater efficiency.

You bring that with you.

  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills as well as a high implementation strength and self-initiative distinguish you.
  • With your secure appearance and your addressee-friendly communication, you convince different stakeholders of your ideas.
  • You will bring your first experience in results- and development-oriented personnel leadership in the financial environment for accounting topics.
  • You can prove a secure handling of ERP systems and financial processes, ideally experience with SAP S/4HANA and an affinity for digital tools such as AI tools.
  • You have a diploma as an auditor or an equivalent qualification as well as several years of professional experience in accounting, ideally in a digitized environment or in a service organization.
  • Negotiable oral and written knowledge of German at least at level C1 is required. Knowledge of other national languages is an advantage.
