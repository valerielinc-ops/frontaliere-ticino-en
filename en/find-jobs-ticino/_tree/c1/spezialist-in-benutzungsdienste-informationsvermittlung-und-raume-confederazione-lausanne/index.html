The Swiss Institute for Comparative Law (SIR) is an independent administrative unit of the Federation. We develop foreign and international law and thus make an important contribution to legal practice and science. We support court and administrative authorities, law firms, notaries and private individuals and conduct our own comparative legal research. Our research topics are oriented towards legal issues that are of particular importance to Switzerland, dealing with the legal challenges of complex societies or a globalised economy. Our excellent specialist library, with its unique diversity of foreign legal systems in Switzerland, is an invaluable resource. As part of the national library network Swisscovery, it is also broadly and internationally networked.
