Full description

Become part of our Design Engineering team as a highly experienced individual contributor. You solve challenging mechanical design tasks yourself, from concepts and CAD models to production drawings and prototypes to tested hardware, while supporting the extended team in informed technical decisions. As a Senior Design Engineer (m/f/x), you are a hands-on member of our Design Engineering Team and design the Climeworks CO2 Collectors, the heart of our direct air capture facility. How powerful these collectors are, how they are manufactured and how they scale is critical to the future success of DAC.

  • Mechanical Concept & Product Design: You independently transform new concepts into robust physical products: You develop mechanical concepts, create CAD models, design components and complete collector assemblies, create production drawings and accompany the hardware through production, assembly and testing.
  • Technical problem solving and risk reduction: Your experience will help you find the right solutions faster, identify problems early and reduce risks before they impact manufacturing or testing.
  • CO2 collector design: You develop mechanical systems from the requirements and initial concepts to detailed, production-oriented designs. You develop solutions, perform technical calculations and make the necessary decisions to develop robust industrial hardware.
  • Engineering based on basic principles: You use your experience and thinking based on basic technical principles to identify risks early on, quickly evaluate alternatives, and develop simple, robust solutions. You use calculations, tolerance analysis, design reviews and tests to reduce uncertainty.
  • Design from concept to commercialization: You work directly in SolidWorks to create and customize parts, assemblies and system layouts. You control complex interfaces, installation space restrictions and design changes so that the overall system works together optimally. You will create and maintain high-quality 2D manufacturing drawings, including appropriate GD&T specifications, tolerances, materials, surfaces and manufacturing requirements.
  • Prototype production, assembly & tests: You will remain closely involved even after completing the CAD work. You support the production, assembly and testing of prototypes, investigate any problems that arise and incorporate the knowledge gained back into the design.
  • Technical Leadership & Design Reviews: You act as an experienced technical contact for the team and support the management of the Design Engineering team in design reviews, engineering standards and the approval of critical designs. You will support the extended team with your technical judgment in complex mechanical design decisions, help maintain high engineering standards and share your experience through daily collaboration, mentoring and constructive questioning.
  • Interdisciplinary Engineering Collaboration: You work with manufacturing, testing, process engineering, procurement, suppliers and other engineering disciplines to ensure that mechanical designs work as part of the overall product. If necessary, integrate external design resources.

Requirements

What distinguishes you Your experience makes your engineering work more effective: you identify promising solutions early on, uncover weaknesses before they become expensive, and deliver designs that are clear, production-ready and ready for use in practice.

  • Mechanical Design Experience: You bring 15+ years of hands-on mechanical design experience, ideally in the development of complex industrial plants from conception to production and assembly to testing.
  • Sheet metal construction and production: You have profound practical experience in the construction of sheet metal parts, structures and assemblies for production in large quantities. You know bending, welding and joining processes, forming, tolerances and the choice of material and sheet thickness and know how design decisions affect tools, costs, assembly and quality.
  • CAD and drawings: You are a SolidWorks expert and continue to work in CAD yourself. You create high-quality production drawings personally and are confident in dealing with GD&T and tolerance chains.
  • System Design and Technical Judgment: You have experience in the design of components and mechanisms through subsystems and interfaces to complex assemblies. You understand how decisions at one level affect the entire collector and weigh structures, air guidance, motion, waterproofing, process interfaces, manufacturability, reliability and cost to arrive at robust solutions.
  • Manufacturing and DFMA: You have in-depth knowledge of machining parts, welding structures, materials and manufacturing processes, and apply DFMA to develop robust and cost-effective designs.
  • Collaboration and Technical Leadership: You work well with engineers, technicians, manufacturing partners and suppliers. You share your experience, question constructively and help others make better technical decisions.
  • Education: You have a bachelor's or master's degree in mechanical engineering or a comparable degree.
  • Language: You speak fluent English. This is a must-see criterion as our corporate language is English.

This job is not for you if...

  • You mainly want to lead people or projects.
  • You mainly run simulation, concept work or system engineering without your own CAD work.
  • you have no experience with sheet metal, welding constructions or production in large quantities.
