Head of intelligent measurement systems and grid connections (w/m, 80\-100%)

Our client is an established, future-oriented company in the energy and utilities industry with a strong regional anchorage and an excellent . In addition to a reliable power supply, it relies on innovative solutions in the fields of photovoltaics, electromobility and intelligent energy systems. The increasing digitalization and decentralization of energy supply brings exciting challenges and a wide range of possibilities.

Now take over the management of the department for intelligent measurement systems, grid connections and governmental tasks. In this versatile management function, you combine electrical engineering expertise with organizational responsibility and actively shape the development of the measurement and network infrastructure.

Your new task

  • You lead and develop your department in professional, human and economic terms.
  • They are responsible for the operation of intelligent measurement systems and ensure stable, reliable data communication.
  • They ensure the implementation of regulatory requirements, especially for new measurement and connection concepts such as EVG, ZEV, vZEV and LEG.
  • In addition, you drive the further development of the measurement and control infrastructure and implement exciting Smart Grid projects.
  • From technical assessment and contractual regulation to planning and implementation, you coordinate grid connections, even for large PV systems, battery storage and high-performance consumers.
  • They ensure reporting and control systems and coordinate external service providers in the areas of installation control, grid connections and intelligent measurement and control systems.

What distinguishes you

  • They have higher electrotechnical training, for example as electrical project manager, electrical safety consultant or as technician HF with control authorization according to NIV.
  • You have several years of professional experience in electrical engineering, ideally at an RU, in electrical installation, electrical safety or at a specialized service provider.
  • Furthermore, you have already led a team or, for example, would like to assume leadership responsibility for the first time as an experienced project manager.
  • They are familiar with the relevant electrical regulations and are interested in the regulatory framework, the energy transition and innovative technical solutions.
  • They work carefully, are IT\-affine and communicative and understand how to promote employees, coordinate external partners and further develop processes.

Our attractive offer

  • You benefit from an attractive salary including bonus, six to seven weeks holidays, flexible working hours and individual home office options.
  • In addition, you can look forward to an advantageous pension fund solution and an impressive package of additional benefits.
  • Your professional and personal development is actively promoted by the generous support for training and further education.
  • She expects a collaborative working environment characterized by an employee-oriented corporate culture, versatile tasks and exciting projects around the digitalization and transformation of energy supply.
