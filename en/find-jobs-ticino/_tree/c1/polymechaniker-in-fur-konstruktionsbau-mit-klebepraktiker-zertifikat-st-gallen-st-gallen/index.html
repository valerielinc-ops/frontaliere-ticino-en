Full description

The activity includes the precise machining of metal parts according to technical drawings. The company offers thorough training and good social benefits. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • machining metal parts according to technical plans
  • performing adhesive work according to specifications professionally
  • manufacturing assemblies in structural construction
  • Requirements
  • Completion in plant and apparatus construction, in polymechanics, production mechanics or similar
  • Certificate as an adhesive practitioner according to DIN EN 17460
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Practice in construction and efficient processing
  • Safe working exactly according to technical drawings Offer
  • Careful introduction to the field of work
  • Good social benefits
