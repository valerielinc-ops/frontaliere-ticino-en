Full description

09.11.2026 St. Gallen

Become part of our team!

We are looking for a committed, customer-oriented and sales-strong personality in our consulting team as individual personal consultant (m/f/d). In this versatile function, you will support employees of Raiffeisen Switzerland and their relatives holistically in the areas of financing, investing and pensions and are responsible for your own customer portfolio.

What awaits you?

  • You support and accompany employees of Raiffeisen Switzerland and their relatives holistically in all financial matters
  • You are responsible for your own customer portfolio, actively develop it and win new customers
  • You advise your customers needs-oriented in the areas of financing, investing and provisioning
  • You recognize customer needs at an early stage, exploit potential and successfully implement consulting and sales opportunities
  • You position yourself as a trusted contact person, work closely with our specialists and ensure a high quality of advice

What do you bring?

  • You have completed banking education and/or corresponding further education in finance
  • You bring several years of successful experience in private client consulting, especially in the areas of financing, investing and pensions
  • You have strong consulting, sales and acquisition skills and enjoy recognizing and successfully exploiting customer potential
  • You convince with your customer orientation, your professional appearance and your high social and communication skills
  • You work independently, structured and responsible and like to take on personal responsibility
  • You are a dynamic and compelling personality with an entrepreneurial mindset, team spirit and joy in building long-term customer relationships
  • You ideally have language skills in French and/or Italian

We focus on people, both on the customer and on the team side. You can expect a dedicated team with a lot of experience, passion for advice and a strong cohesion. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed.
