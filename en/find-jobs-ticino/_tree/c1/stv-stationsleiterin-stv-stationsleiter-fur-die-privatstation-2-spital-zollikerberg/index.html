Stv. Station Manager for Private Station 2 — Spital Zollikerberg, Zollikerberg (ZH).

Area: Nursing professions.

Welcome to your new place of work.

Our station manager of Privatstation 2, Maik Strutzberg offers you at 1. December or by appointment a job with a flexible workload between 80-100% as

Stv. Station Manager / Stv. Station manager for the private station 2

(Deputy station manager)

We are looking for a committed nurse with management experience, for our interdisciplinary private ward with a focus on acute medicine, internal medicine and surgery.

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Exercise of management and development tasks in the nursing team in close cooperation with the station management
  • Active co-creation of efficient organizational structures (e.g. personnel planning, day-to-day responsibility)
  • Support for the further development of the station – together in an interdisciplinary and interprofessional team
  • Active participation in the care team as a reference nurse: r as well as contributing your experience to a well-established team

Become part of the Department of Emergency and Acute Medicine at the Zollikerberg Hospital. In the Clinic for Internal Medicine with continuing education status A, we look after more than 2,500 inpatients, more than 6,000 emergencies and more than 450 patients in the interdisciplinary intensive care unit every year. On our interdisciplinary stations, you can expect a versatile field of activity with a focus on palliative care, internal medicine and surgery. Our stations are guided according to the reference care system.

In our 360° tour, get a first impression of your new place of work.

To make you feel comfortable with us, many benefits are waiting for you. Just to name a few:

  • Own pension fund with super-compulsory participation of the employer
  • Ecobonus for public transport users: inside
  • Exclusive discounts on telephony, Internet and TV at Salt and Sunrise
  • Transverse shifts to other areas, the development into a team leader or a specialization - even across several companies

All amenities for employees can be found here. If you also want to be the best version of yourself, you will find the possibilities with us.

This convinces us

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional / Dipl. Nursing professional (HF/FH, DN II or SRC recognition)
  • Several years of professional experience in the acute somatic hospital environment (experience in acute medicine, internal medicine or surgery of advantage)
  • First management experience of advantage (e.g. as shift manager or day manager:r)
  • Completion of leadership training (e.g. CAS) or willingness to undertake such training
  • Winning personality with high technical, social and communication skills as well as enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation

More information can be provided by Maik Strutzberg, station manager private station 2 under T 044 397 22 94.

Please apply via our online tool.

About the Zollikerberg hospital #LI-PZ1 We stand for a comprehensive range of services and strong cooperation partners. With a public service mandate and 173 beds, the Zollikerberg Hospital contributes to the optimal medical care of the greater Zurich area. Every year, more than 11,000 inpatients and 75,000 outpatients in all insurance classes can feel comfortable and in good hands. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
