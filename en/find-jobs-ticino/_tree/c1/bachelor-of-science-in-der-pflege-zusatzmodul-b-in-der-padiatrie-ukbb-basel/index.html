Full description

Have you completed your major studies at BSc. Pflege FH and would like to gain valuable practical experience as part of the additional module B? Then take the opportunity to get to know the varied everyday life in pediatric care! You work closely with experienced nurses, deepen your knowledge in paediatric nursing and develop your practical skills. The additional module B can be carried out on all stations:

  • General paediatrics
  • Psychosomatic Station
  • Surgery/Orthopaedics
  • Haematological oncology station
  • Paediatric intensive care unit
  • Neonatal intensive care unit
  • Interdisciplinary emergency department

Entry Date: By Agreement

Their tasks

  • Deepening your practical skills in pediatric care
  • Supporting the care team in the care and care of children and adolescents
  • Independent assumption of selected nursing activities under the guidance of experienced professionals
  • Accompaniment and interaction with pediatric patients and their caregivers
  • Get to know interdisciplinary processes and cooperation in the paediatric team

Your profile

  • Completed major study Bachelor of Science in Nursing
  • Interest in paediatrics and specialized care of children and adolescents
  • Enjoying the sensitive and professional treatment of pediatric patients and their caregivers
  • Commitment, responsibility and readiness for interdisciplinary cooperation

Our offer

  • Discover the diversity of pediatric care and get inspired
  • Practical guidance by experienced nurses
  • An appreciative and interprofessional working environment
  • The opportunity to gain valuable experience for your professional future
