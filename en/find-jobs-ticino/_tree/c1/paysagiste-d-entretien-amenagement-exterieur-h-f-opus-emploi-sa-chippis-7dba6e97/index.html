Full description

For one of our clients active in the field of green spaces and outdoor development, Opus Emploi SA is looking for an experienced maintenance landscaper for missions in central Valais.

Your tasks

General maintenance of green spaces

  • Tongue, size and routine maintenance work
  • Soil preparation and levelling
  • Installation of slabs and paving blocks
  • Small external development works
  • Preparation of surfaces before laying
  • Use of tools and machines for landscape work
  • Maintenance of the clean and organized site
  • Compliance with safety instructions and rules

Your profile

  • Experience in green space maintenance
  • Experience in slab laying and ground leveling
  • Good knowledge of outdoor development
  • Manual, autonomous and versatile person
  • Precise and careful work
  • Good physical condition
  • Serious, reliable and motivated person
  • Ability to work alone or in teams
