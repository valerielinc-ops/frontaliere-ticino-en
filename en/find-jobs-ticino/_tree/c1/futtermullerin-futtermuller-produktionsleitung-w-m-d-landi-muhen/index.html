The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their farms. Together with the brands Volg, TopShop and AGROLA, the LANDI cooperatives also provide the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy. LANDI Muhen is successfully active in the Suhrental region. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a food waste manager / food waste management immediately or by agreement.

These tasks inspire you

  • Planning and production of the compound feed according to the prescription
  • Organisation of extraditions by date

  • Implementation of the QSGF and HACCP in operation
  • Monitoring of cleaning and maintenance of infrastructure, machinery, equipment, equipment
