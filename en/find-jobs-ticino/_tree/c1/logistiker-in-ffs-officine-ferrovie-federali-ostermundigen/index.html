Logistician

Ostermundigen, 01.01.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

For the train to run, the right material must be in the right place at the right time. That's what you're doing. You help ensure that our warehouses and construction sites are reliably supplied with the required material and thus make an important contribution to a functioning railway infrastructure. You can expect a versatile and dynamic working environment with a lot of personal responsibility. Together with your teammates as well as internal and external partners, you will find pragmatic solutions even under demanding conditions. You react flexibly to short-term changes, keep track and ensure that the material arrives where it is needed. We support your further development and offer you a wide range of internal training opportunities for your personal career at SBB. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • You carry out logistics orders in the warehouse and on construction sites and represent us as a logistics service provider to our internal and external partners.
  • Independently, you take over the provision, dispatch and transport production of shipments and load them using the necessary tools such as forklifts, ants or crane.
  • As a “maker-type” you ensure the material supply of the construction sites: from the provision and delivery via the management to the return of the material.
  • In a versatile field of application, you are responsible for the acceptance of goods and the checking of goods receipts with booking in the SAP ERP system.
  • You ensure professional warehouse management and legally and environmentally compatible warehousing and contribute to the continuous improvement of our warehouses and processes.
  • You work with modern work systems such as laptop and mobile, SharePoint, teams and Outlook and thus stay in touch with our colleagues and partners.

You bring that with you.

  • Reliability sets you apart, you adhere to deadlines, guidelines and agreements consistently and work carefully and responsibly even in hectic situations.
  • You work independently, recognize the work involved and approach it on your own initiative. You do not always wait for a specific instruction, but actively participate and support the team where it needs you.
  • A completed vocational apprenticeship as a logistician: in EFZ or an equivalent education as well as at least two years of professional experience in warehouse and production logistics and the corresponding logistics modules.
  • You bring a valid truck pass as well as practical driving experience.
  • You have already gained experience in dealing with an ERP or warehouse management system as well as SAP knowledge.
  • You have knowledge of German at least level B2 in word and writing. In addition, you have good user knowledge of MS-Office applications.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This job is in Requirement Level D, Regional Allowance Level 1.
