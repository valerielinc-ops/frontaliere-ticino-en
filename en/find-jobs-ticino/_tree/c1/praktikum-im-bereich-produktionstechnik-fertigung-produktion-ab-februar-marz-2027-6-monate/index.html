Our offer to you

  • You will support our engineers in the Manufacturing Engineering department in optimizing manufacturing processes and gain comprehensive insights into our various divisions.
  • Furthermore, you support the manufacturing engineers of the production cell in terms of documentation, projects, problem solving, training, devices, etc.
  • You work closely with production, team leaders, quality engineers and other stakeholders and get the opportunity to actively participate in an international medical technology company with your practical solutions.

This sets you apart

  • You study engineering with the specialization Mechanical Engineering, Medical Engineering, Production Engineering, Process Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Mechatronics, Electrical Engineering or are enrolled in a similar degree program.
  • Your strong analytical skills as well as your structured, independent and precise way of working enable you to operate successfully in our dynamic team.
  • Your strong interest in manufacturing issues motivates you to deal with complex issues in the area of optimization processes.
  • Ideally, you could already gain practical experience in production technology or quality management.
  • Your secure use of MS Office and your very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing enable you to communicate easily in our international environment.
