Full description

For a strong future. In this diverse role, you will be responsible for all personnel-related issues in your assigned areas and locations. You will accompany leaders and employees throughout the entire employee lifecycle, actively contribute your HR expertise, and help shape the development of modern HR processes.

  • Your responsibilities Consultation and support: You will advise and support leaders and employees in all personnel-related issues and be the point of contact throughout the entire employee lifecycle. Area of responsibility: You will assume responsibility for the assigned locations and areas, lead the HR Assistant personally and professionally, and ensure professional and service-oriented HR processes. Recruitment: You will accompany recruitment processes from personnel planning to onboarding. Labor law and compliance: You will be proficient in labor law issues and handle cases in case of discrepancies. Case management: In case of long-term absences, you will assume responsibility and work closely with case management. HR projects: You will actively contribute to HR projects and cross-functional working groups.
  • Your profile Education: Successful HR specialist certificate or a completed degree (FH/HF) in work and organizational psychology, HR, or business administration. Experience: You have several years of experience in HR, ideally in the healthcare sector or a similar industry. Knowledge: You have solid knowledge of labor law and routine use of MS Office (Excel, Word, Outlook). Personality: As a team player, you remain professional and resilient even in challenging situations.
  • What we offer Involvement: Work in a modern working environment and actively contribute. Inspiring and collaborative working atmosphere: Work in a supportive, respectful, and humorous team that works together for the best possible patient care. Above-average social security contributions: Benefit from attractive social security contributions and comprehensive benefits. Training and further education: Generous co-financing of training and further education. Discounts: Use discounts with various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealerships, and pharmacies. Free entry: Enjoy free entry and other discounts in the wellness-therme FORTYSEVEN.
