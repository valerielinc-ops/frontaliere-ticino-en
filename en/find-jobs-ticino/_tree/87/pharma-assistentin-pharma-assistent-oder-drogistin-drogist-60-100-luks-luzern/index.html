Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement. Tasks: • The focus of your activity is the safe and careful direction of medicines for the inpatient and ambulatory area • In close cooperation with doctors and nursing staff, you contribute to a smooth medication process • In addition, you take on assignments in the public pharmacy and take care of administrative tasks such as warehouse management and health insurance accounts via Ofac Requirements • You have completed training as a pharmaceutical assistant EFZ, pharmaceutical assistant EFZ or drugist/drugist EFZ • Thanks to your rapid comprehension, you will quickly find your way into new processes and keep an overview even in a dynamic environment. • An exact, reliable and quality-conscious way of working distinguishes you – as well as your willingness to participate in hectic situations. • With your warm and team-oriented nature, you appreciate interprofessional cooperation and meet others with strong social skills. We offer: • Enjoy an exciting working environment at the interface between public pharmacy, hospital pharmacy and outpatient care • Modern systems such as PropharmaX and the clinic information system LUKiS help you to work efficiently and to have important information available quickly. • With us, you can expect a varied task with insights into different areas – including oncology and radio-oncology. • In addition, you have the opportunity to actively participate in the further development of our services and to gradually assume responsibility.
