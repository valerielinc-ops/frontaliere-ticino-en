Full description

If you want to make a difference and you have fun on missions abroad worldwide (about 60%). Then you are right with us!

Service technician (gn)

Your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for the worldwide installation and commissioning of thermal coating systems (use time approximately 4-6 weeks). You work very closely with our customer
  • You are responsible for internal acceptance of coating systems
  • You carry out service, calibration and maintenance work on thermal coating plants, as well as conversions and upgrades
  • You create simple rapports of your bets
  • You give training and advise the customers and operators on site
  • You support innovation projects

Your profile

  • You have completed training as an electrical engineer, mechatronics engineer or automatic engineer (gn), as well as experience in plant engineering with control/control technology and preferably initial robotics knowledge
  • You show interest in a challenge that requires a broad technical background, ingenuity and flexibility
  • The customer is your first priority and you do everything in your team to successfully implement projects
  • You feel Get comfortable in German and English to communicate with international customers

You can expect that from us

  • A very exciting, exciting, international working environment in the high-tech sector
  • You can develop personally and professionally
  • At the beginning of 2027 Oerlikon Metco will move to Hausen (AG) – a modern production and technology centre for thermal coating is waiting for us
  • We are open and honest with each other
  • We are a team that always supports you
  • We offer advanced employment conditions, an attractive compensation package, with good social benefits
  • Through the pension fund we offer you a well-developed pension provision

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in material science engineering. With the right combination of surface technologies, advanced materials, coating equipment, components and advanced manufacturing, we help customers improve the performance, efficiency, durability and sustainability of their products and processes. Our technologies are often invisible to end users, but critical to the business of the industries that make modern life possible: aerospace, energy, semiconductors, medical technology, luxury goods, tool industry, automotive industry and general industry. Oerlikon is headquartered in Pfäffikon, Switzerland, operates in 38 countries and employs around 10,000 people worldwide. In 2025, Oerlikon generated sales of CHF 1.6 billion.

We offer a wide variety of activity profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and reach their full potential, guided by our core values of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.
