We make habitats worth living.

E3 Zentralschweiz AG is an established company in heating, ventilation and refrigeration technology with around 45 motivated employees. We realize exciting projects with enthusiasm and high professional competence.

Your tasks

  • You will take over the management and further development of demanding construction projects in the area of building automation.
  • The independent planning and implementation – from concept development to detailed planning and successful implementation are also among your tasks.
  • As a project manager, you are the central contact for customers and the planning team, both in technical and economic matters.
  • You coordinate the cooperation with the various construction trades and communicate professionally with all project participants.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed technical training as well as further education (HF or FH) in the field of building technology or building automation.
  • Ideally, you have already participated in or led GA projects.
  • In addition, you have experience as a project manager, as well as an interest in digitalization topics and their further development.
