Full description

Are you an IT specialist and are you interested in the topics of living and care in old age? You want to go by 4. January 2027 or by agreement in this newly created position as responsible for IT governance and controlling for the health centers for old age?

  • They are responsible for the development and development of IT governance and IT compliance management and ensure compliance with relevant guidelines, standards and regulatory requirements.
  • They coordinate the overall governance in the areas of information security and data protection (ISDS), IT risk management, internal control system (ICS) and IT audit and ensure the systematic follow-up of agreed measures.
  • You control IT controlling, monitor budgets and costs and create meaningful key figures and decision bases for IT management.
  • They ensure the up-to-date and consistency of the service catalogue as well as compliance with the service level agreements, analyze processes and key figures, derive improvement measures and promote the continuous development of IT quality and efficiency.
  • They monitor the performance of external IT suppliers, coordinate service reviews and initiate appropriate measures in the event of deviations.
  • Integrity and sovereign appearance, coupled with open communication, enjoyment of cooperation and a strong service orientation
  • Analytical and networked thinking, strategic action as well as clear prioritization and a consistent goal and result orientation
  • Completed university of applied sciences, preferably in business informatics, as well as a demonstrable further education in IT governance, IT controlling, compliance management or information security and several years of professional experience in these areas
  • Certifications in ITIL 4 as well as further training in process, risk or quality management are advantageous
  • Good knowledge of information security, data protection (ISDS), IT risk management, IKS and IT service management as well as experience with reporting, KPIs and ITSM tools is an advantage

An exciting task area in a professional and team-oriented working environment with many design possibilities. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The central services division is located in Zurich-Oerlikon and provides various services within the entire service department. These include computer science (technical IT), finance, HR, marketing, communication, facility management, construction development, central purchasing and quality management.
