Full description

Do you not only want to accompany technical projects, but also design them from the first idea to successful implementation? Then bring your know-how to us and, together with internal and external partners, develop our production facilities efficiently, sustainably and future-oriented.

What to expect

  • Plant Engineering Project Management: Planning, control and successful implementation of projects for optimization and modernization of mechanical production plants
  • CO2 reduction & sustainability: Development and implementation of technical measures to increase efficiency and reduce energy consumption
  • Overall responsibility: responsibility for technical interpretation, budgeting, deadline compliance and quality of allocated projects
  • Maintenance & Modernization: Coordination of conversion, maintenance and renewal measures on mechanical components in ongoing operation
  • Interface management: Close exchange and cooperation with internal specialist departments as well as external partners, engineering offices and equipment providers
  • Presence on site: Active support and monitoring of the assembly and commissioning phases directly at the production facilities

What you bring

  • Completed studies or technical further education in mechanical engineering, process engineering, plant engineering or a comparable field
  • Several years of professional experience in mechanical project management, ideally in the industrial environment
  • In-depth knowledge in technical planning, budgeting and project management
  • Secure handling of common project management tools
  • Analytical, solution-oriented working method with high safety and quality awareness
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing

Our offer

  • Onboarding & getting started: Look forward to a team-oriented, open and future-oriented working environment. We accompany you from the beginning with a careful familiarization, practical and structured, so that you can start successfully.
  • Career & Development: We create opportunities. With us, you are at the center as a person with your individual strengths and potential. Anyone who wants to is supported by us and can achieve a lot.
  • Appreciation & Team Culture: With us, you are part of a strong and inclusive team that holds together, shares ideas and masters challenges together. A trusting leadership culture ensures that your commitment is seen and appreciated.
  • Flexibility & Work-Life Balance: We offer flexible working hours, 41 hours, part-time opportunities, 25-30 days holidays, holiday purchase, extended maternity and paternity leave
  • Attractive compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional benefits and a very good pension fund
  • Employee benefits: From Reka checks to shares at a preferential price to the Swibeco advantage world - we make sure that you feel comfortable and benefit from attractive additional services
