Full description

Reference code: JR134301

Who are we?

A High Jewelry House with a strong heritage who promote exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allow our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to attract talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that when diversity and inclusion are fully embraced and empowered, creativity and knowledge emerge to deliver excellence.

Do you match the profile?

You justify a proven experience in managing complex development projects, ideally in jewellery, watchmaking or precision industry. You are a good communicater, teacher and have a great listening capacity. You demonstrate real proactivity, a spirit of synthesis and a facilitator approach to problem solving. You have a rigorous scientific methodology and a strong understanding of strategic and operational issues. You know how to manage demanding situations with serenity and have a strong appeal for major projects.

What do we want from you?

Attached to the Head of Groupe R&D Émail, within the Directorate of Trades of Art, you ensure the overall management of R&D projects related to the development of jewellery parts in volume mail.

As part of your missions

  • You are piloting projects to develop new e-mail jewellery in volume, from the design phase to the production of launch quantities, using the development workshop and the pilot lines.
  • You ensure compliance with the development process, its milestones and deliverables.
  • You prepare and follow the planning, budgets and resources of each project.
  • You guarantee compliance with the aesthetic, quality, cost and time criteria set out in the specifications.
  • You coordinate the production of prototypes, preseries and launch quantities, in partnership with the Production Manager.
  • You run the project instances and set the delivery schedule.
  • You federate and coordinate the various project team members internally, as well as external partners. You promote collaboration and effective communication between all key players.
  • You build and update monitoring tools, communicate key performance indicators (KPIs) and prepare regular reports to ensure effective project monitoring.

More than a job... we recruit for a career

Integrating Van Cleef & Arpels is above all a team where the development of employees is at the heart of our ambition.

From your integration, you will be immersed in the world of the House thanks to trainings on our history, know-how and culture.

The recruitment process

Apply directly online.

If your profile is selected, you are contacted by the HR team for an interview.

You will meet the Head of Group R&D Email, then the Head of Business Development of Art, as well as the Head of Human Resources.
