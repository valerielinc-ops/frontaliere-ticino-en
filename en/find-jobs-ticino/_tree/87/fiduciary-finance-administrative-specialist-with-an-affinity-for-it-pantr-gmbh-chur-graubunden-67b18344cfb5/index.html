Full description

For a leading, Switzerland-wide networked trust and consulting group, we are looking for a numerical and IT-enthusiastic personality as a case manager: in Treuhand & Accounting at the location in Chur. In this versatile role, you combine classic fiduciary tasks with modern digital processes in a small team on site. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Finance & Wages: Independent management of financial accounting, preparation of VAT accounts as well as competent handling of wage accounting.
  • Financial statements & Taxes: Active support in the preparation of financial statements and assistance in the preparation of tax returns.
  • Digitalization & IT: Proactive support of the team on IT topics, implementation of smaller IT projects and optimization of digital processes (DMS, DeepBox, Ebics).
  • All-round support: Processing of business register mutations and taking on further, varied escrow tasks.

This is what you bring with you

  • Experience: In-depth work experience in the escrow sector, ideally collected in a similar interface function.
  • IT affinity: Very good MS Office knowledge and experience with Abacus, M-Files or similar systems
  • Excitement for modern software solutions and digital tools.
  • Working method: A pronounced organizational talent, a structured working method, high deadlines and a strong service orientation.
  • Languages: German at native language level is mandatory, knowledge of French or English is a welcome bonus.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Conditions & Benefits: Attractive remuneration, fair compensation of overtime and a monthly catering allowance of CHF 200.
  • Flexibility & Holidays: Flexible working hours, home office options as well as 25 to 27 days of holiday with the possibility to take unpaid holidays.
  • Culture & Development: An appreciative atmosphere, regular employee events, professional exchange in a large network of experts as well as time and financial support for your further education.
