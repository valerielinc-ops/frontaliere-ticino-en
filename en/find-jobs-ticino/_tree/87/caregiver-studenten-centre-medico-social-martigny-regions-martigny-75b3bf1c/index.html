Caregiver students

Are you passionate about care and have the ability to make the daily lives of those who need help shine? Looking for an opportunity that will allow you to make significant improvements in the lives of others? Do not look further, this exceptional opportunity is within your reach.

For our sites in Saxon and Martigny, we are looking for dedicated students in the care field.

Join our teams and contribute to our mission of caring for our customers on a daily basis.

About us: You will not just be a care professional, you will become a craftsman of well-being, actively contributing to improving the quality of life of our valuable clients. Your mission will be to provide quality care and assistance directly at home. By working closely with a multidisciplinary team, you will play an essential role in maintaining the well-being of our customers.

What we are looking for:

  • An EHS/ES or Basic Care (CRS) SWC or equivalent;
  • Experience in the area of home care and assistance would be an asset;
  • The ability to work within a multidisciplinary team and independently;
  • Knowledge of the canton's health, social and voluntary network;
  • Reflexivity and adaptability;
  • The mastery of common computer tools;
  • Available on evenings and/or weekends;
  • No one's being transported.

What we offer

  • A varied activity in a growing sector;
  • A dynamic working framework in collaboration with the network and families;
  • Mobile work tools that facilitate customer tracking;
  • Developing skills and opportunities for continuing training;
  • First-rate social benefits;
  • Conditions in accordance with the SLD CTC.

Don't hesitate! Join us as soon as possible to make your positive contribution and make a difference in the well-being of our customers.

Entry into office: as soon as possible or date to be agreed.

For the sake of inclusiveness, all the male forms used extend to the female as well.
