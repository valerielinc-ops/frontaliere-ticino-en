Full description

For a neurological private ward with a total of 21 beds. It consists of single and twin rooms.

The team currently consists of 15 dedicated employees. What is particularly nice about our work is that our patients usually have longer stays with us. This enables us to experience their progress first hand: Many patients enter us as full-care cases and leave the ward independently, a particularly fulfilling moment.

Above all, we as a team are humorous, empathetic and helpful – values that shape our everyday work.

Your tasks • Care and care: You help our patients to find their way back to an independent life. Your work has a direct impact on the healing and treatment process • Hygiene service: You can carry out any domestic work according to our hygiene standards independently • Support: You take on further activities within the framework of the competence regulation for assistant health and social affairs

Your profile • Training: Completed training on Assistant/Health and Social Affairs (AGS) • Language: Good knowledge of German in words and writing • Personality: You show a high sense of responsibility, pleasure in daily work and a strong team player attitude. In addition, you bring a friendly and safe attitude in dealing with patients as well as a reliable and independent working method.

What we offer

• Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate • Inspiring and collegial working environment: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care • Flexibility bonus: Voluntary participation in our internal care pool for additional shifts, which are also rewarded attractively. • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other perks at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
