Full description

As Health & Safety Lead Region Mittelland, you ensure the effective implementation of Health & Safety at the locations Suhr, Dagmersellen, Kaltbach. You support site managers and executives in anchoring safety and health as an integral part of their daily leadership work. You are more than just a professional contact person: As a sparring partner, coach and driving force, you recognise risks at an early stage, create transparency and ensure sustainable improvements together with the responsible managers.

You can do that.

  • You identify and evaluate hazards, risks as well as uncertain conditions and actions and derive effective prevention measures from them.
  • You implement central health and safety standards at the supervised locations, make local deviations visible and support the harmonization between the plants.
  • You advise, coach and empower site managers and managers so that they effectively assume their responsibility for safety and health in everyday work.
  • You analyze accident events, near miss and recurring patterns, derive appropriate measures and check their effectiveness.
  • You assess work-related health and stress factors, such as ergonomics, physical stress and influences of the work environment.
  • You will conduct H&S inspections, audits and reviews, support risk assessments and root cause analysis, and plan training, instructions and prevention actions.
  • You introduce health and safety requirements at the interfaces to HR/BGM, engineering, facility management, sustainability and production and ensure the consistent follow-up of agreed measures.

You bring that with you

  • You have experience in industrial production and understand the challenges of an operational environment, ideally also in shift operations.
  • You bring a recognized qualification as a specialist: in occupational safety according to the ASA directive, at least as a safety specialist or specialist: in ASGS. Alternatively, you are ready and suitable to acquire this qualification within 24 months of assuming office.
  • You convince people, create commitment and move sustainable changes even without direct authority.
  • You act demandingly and supportively towards managers at the same time and strengthen Health & Safety as a leadership task.
  • You think analytically, identify risks, causes, patterns and trends and consistently keep an eye on the implementation and effectiveness of the derived measures.
  • You have a broad understanding of occupational safety and health protection, such as hazardous substances, fire, gas and explosion protection, emergency management, legal compliance and work-related burdens.
  • You communicate in German in a negotiable and addressable manner, build up sustainable networks and clearly address security-critical topics.

This is what we offer you

  • 6 weeks vacation: Enjoy six weeks vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to seven weeks.
  • Sabbatical & Holiday Shopping: Give yourself unpaid holidays or extend your holiday entitlement by up to two additional weeks.
  • Service Anniversary: Loyalty pays off: Every five years, we reward your loyalty with a premium, which you can also receive in the form of holidays or as a one-time contribution to the pension fund.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Individual development plan: With tailor-made development plans, we specifically promote your individual skills and support your long-term growth – a central component of our sustainability strategy. Learn more .
