Full description

15.09.2026 Vernier Vernier

Be part of our team!

Join an ambitious wealth management team and contribute, through your rigor, service and team spirit, to provide an irreproachable experience for a demanding clientele.

What are you waiting for?

  • Ensure the administrative and operational support of wealth managers in the daily monitoring of customers.
  • Manage customer requests, correspondence and appointment preparation, while ensuring the quality and customization of the service.
  • Prepare, monitor and update contractual, regulatory and administrative documentation related to banking relations (opening, amending and closing accounts).
  • Ensure quality and compliance of client files, including through periodic reviews, KYC/TMS controls and enforcement of regulatory requirements.
  • Monitoring and execution of banking and financial operations (securities, exchange, transfers, maturities, investment solutions, etc.).
  • Effectively coordinate interactions with different internal partners to ensure smooth and rigorous processing of applications.
  • Contribute to the identification of operational risks and compliance with internal and regulatory procedures.

What are you bringing?

  • Bank training completed or equivalent. Higher education in banking, finance or wealth management is an asset;
  • Minimum 5 years experience in a similar function, ideally in private banking or wealth management;
  • Good knowledge of HNWI or Affluent customers and the banking and regulatory environment;
  • Customer orientation, sense of service, rigour, autonomy and team spirit;
  • Perfect mastery of French and good professional level in English.

Frédéric Delaloye Head of customer advice placement

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
