Description of the area Your immediate focus will be within the Medical Affairs Domain. Global Medical Affairs serves as the bridge between clinical development and the commercialization of medicines. The domain is increasingly focused on External Stakeholder Engagement, aiming to derive deep insights into unmet medical needs, treatment barriers, and patient perceptions of data. By synthesizing these insights, Medical Affairs shapes the organization’s overall strategic direction. As a Delivery Manager in this space, you will support the digital transformation of how scientific evidence is generated, managed, and communicated to the global medical community.
