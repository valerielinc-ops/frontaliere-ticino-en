Full description

Thanks to its 800 employees, the Intercantonal Hospital of the Broye (HIB) provides a mission of acute care and rehabilitation. His ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges ahead, HIB is looking for a:

Head of Internal Medicine Clinic from 80% to 100%

Your Mission

  • Ensuring high quality medical services to patients, in accordance with professional standards and institutional values,
  • Participate in the management and coordination of the medical activities of the service, including acute geriatrics, and rehabilitation.
  • Contribute to project management and development, as well as monitoring of performance indicators,
  • To supervise, train and supervise medical assistants, trainees and students;
  • Promoting the coherence and quality of interdisciplinary work,
  • Actively participate in symposia, sessions and training.

Your assets

To benefit from a federal diploma in medicine and an FMH degree in internal medicine or foreign title recognized by MEBEKO. You have clinical experience in the specialty. A strong interest in the training of doctors. You are recognized for your sense of responsibility, your spirit of collaboration and your ability to work in an interdisciplinary environment. You have perfect command of French, both oral and written (Level B2). Entry into office: to be agreed
