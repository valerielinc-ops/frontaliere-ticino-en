Since 1833, drive by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressed its inventiveness through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication.

YOUR MISSION

Attached to the Purchasing Department, you will play a pivotal role between our purchasing project managers, our buyers, our After Sale Service and our suppliers. During this 6-month internship, you will work on two major technical projects, including the strategic transition to lead-free materials, while providing indispensable operational support for VAS reindustrialization projects and the continuous improvement of our processes.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

Follow the deployment of Sans-Plomb materials: Migrating and structuring technical data in our Product Life Cycle Management tool, ensuring the technical relationship with our suppliers and orchestrating the operational logistics deployment.

Implement technical optimizations: Ensure the technical follow-up of a family of targeted components in order to deploy a major and lasting technical improvement.

Supporting VAS Reindustrialization: Providing direct support to the VAS component procurement team. Defining reference pieces, piloting the process of industrialization among suppliers and ensuring technical follow-up until commissioning.

Contributing to continuous improvement: To be a power of proposal to change workflows, optimize the management of technical data and fluidize exchanges between Purchases, Technique and VAS.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE US?

Training: Student in engineering school, HES or University with a specialization in Mechanical Engineering, Micromechanical Engineering, Materials or Industrial Engineering.

Technical skills: Excellent mastery of the Office Pack (Excel, PowerPoint). A first sensitivity to PLM or ERP tools is a plus.

Know-how: A real field person, you are recognized for your autonomy, rigor and proactivity. Entrepreneur, you like to question existing methods to optimize them.

Relationship relationship: Excellent communication skills, comfortable to exchange with both internal technical experts and external industrial interlocutors.

WHAT WE OFFER

A comprehensive mission combining micromechanical expertise, project management suppliers and logical purchasing.

A concrete assumption of responsibility on a key environmental issue.

A stimulating immersion at the heart of the operations of an exceptional Manufacture.

A proximity framework that values initiative and field pragmatism.
