Full description

Welcome to Medbase Neuchâtel La Côte

In our dynamic and versatile medical center located in Corcelles-Cormondrèche, just a few kilometres from Neuchâtel, we provide on a surface of more than 900 m2 the interdisciplinary management of our patients, always oriented towards quality. Some 80 collaborators and collaborators in general internal medicine, dermatology and allergology, cardiology, physiotherapy, psychotherapy and dental medicine work in close interprofessional collaboration. We also keep a permanent for outpatient emergencies.

Medbase Neuchâtel The Coast favours a good balance between professional and private life. It is possible to work part-time in all functions and the working hours can be arranged in a flexible way. For doctors, collaboration is possible under the status of employee or independent, to be agreed upon during the interview.

Your tasks

  • You will provide some of the outpatient emergency consultations as well as the medical and traumatological clinic, by organizing the follow-up in collaboration with the physician treating patients.
  • You will supervise the medical assistants in their clinical activity, in collaboration with the medical officers.
  • An activity in a medical office is also possible, according to interest, and at the Federal Asylum Centre.
  • You will evolve in a caring and human-sized environment.
  • You will be actively involved in internal and external training.
  • Hours: Monday to Friday from 8 a.m. to 7 p.m.
  • No night guards, weekends and public holidays.
  • Type of contract, duration: 6 months or 1 year CDD.

Your profile

  • You are in possession of the federal diploma of doctor or equivalent recognized with MEBE-KO.
  • With good communication skills with patients and integration into team work, you are passionate, committed and dynamic. You like to coach a team and teach.
  • This post is intended for doctors who are advanced in their training or who have completed it.

Ready for the future? Apply today.

#weAreMedbase

To achieve great things, you need to have a vision for the future. Twenty years ago, Medbase was founded with the idea of creating a health care network for all of Switzerland. From a medical firm with three collaborators in 2001, we have now become a 360 degree health provider, with about 4,000 employees spread over 170 sites, operating in four areas of activity: medical and dental centres, Montpellier, drug distribution and digital health platform.

Take the opportunity to continue this success story with us, as Medbase continues to develop and improve the future of health care in Switzerland every day.
