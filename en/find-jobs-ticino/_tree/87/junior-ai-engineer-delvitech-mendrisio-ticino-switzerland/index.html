Full description

Job Summary Delvitech stands at the forefront of technological advancement, having pioneered innovative Automatic Optical Inspection (AOI) technology for assembled printed circuit boards (PCB) utilizing artificial intelligence. Our accomplished team is driven by a commitment to pushing technological boundaries and consistently delivering exceptional results. We are looking for a Junior AI Engineer to join our R&D team. The ideal candidate has a good academic or practical background in AI (mostly applied to computer vision applications), image processing, and a genuine interest in software engineering. The candidate will be responsible for developing end-to-end applications, from data collection and model training through to deployment. Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and integrate computer vision or AI components into our production software.
  • Write clean, maintainable and tested code, working closely with senior engineers on design and code reviews.
  • Contribute to the implementation, testing and evaluation of image processing and AI solutions for defect detection and classification. Education
  • Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Physics, Mathematics, or another relevant scientific discipline. Requirements
  • Software engineering fundamentals: Python, object-oriented programming (preferably Java or a similar language), clean code, version control (Git).
  • University, thesis or personal projects in at least one of these areas: computer vision (e.g. OpenCV, image processing), or AI (e.g. deep learning with PyTorch). Nice to Have
  • Experience with 3D modeling and 3D reconstruction. What We Offer You will join a young and highly technical R&D team, building production software that brings machine vision, computer vision and AI into industrial inspection systems. If you are a passionate software engineer looking for a challenging opportunity to develop innovative machine vision and AI solutions, we would love to hear from you. Delvitech is an equal opportunity employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
