Full description

Bell Schweiz AG, a leading food brand in Switzerland, offers you the perfect start to your professional life with a WMS internship. During the one-year internship, you will get a versatile insight into different areas of our daily work. In addition to organizational and administrative tasks, there is the opportunity to actively participate in communication, marketing and event topics and increasingly take responsibility for own tasks and smaller projects. With our support, you will become part of the Bell Learning community and start your career in an innovative environment.

  • Contract type: traineeship
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: Flexible time
  • Job opening: 01.08.2027
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Alsace Road 174 4056 Basel

Tasks

  • Receiving, responding to and processing sponsorship requests
  • Upload and prepare YouTube shorts
  • Support for digital communication and campaign projects
  • Preparation of tickets and documents for events such as Basel Tattoo, Swiss Indoors
  • Participation in the project planning of Play & Win
  • Coordinate dates and procedures
  • Support for the organisation and implementation of events
  • Participating in communication and negotiations with cooperation partners

Requirements

  • Communication capability
  • Joy of working with other people
  • Self-employment
  • Responsibility
  • Reliability
  • Good knowledge of German

This is what we offer

  • Work-life balance: With us you benefit from 35 days of vacation, so that you have enough relaxation time and are then fit for everyday work and school.
  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: freshly prepared food in our staff restaurant, a lunch menu incl. Soup and salad, water & tea and free fruits as well as high-quality products at greatly reduced prices await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from our contribution to environmental mobility, convenient connection to public transport and free SBB general subscription (GA)!
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management and a free subscription at Update Fitness - for health and leisure fun!

  • Market-friendly wages and social benefits: With a good academic and operational performance, you can look forward to your performance premium. Twice a year!
