Technical Service Support Electrical BSG - Bobst Italia Apply now Bobst Italia Spa, based in Piacenza, is looking for an Electrical Technical Service Support. Our office in Piacenza specialises in the sale and field service of Bobst machines for the printing of corrugated and taut cardboard. The technician, remotely, provides continuous electrical support to customers and colleagues in the field, identifying the best solution to the problem as soon as possible. Main tasks: Technical support to customers and technicians in the field. Solving electrical/electronic problems. Management of the action plan, interventions and reporting of problems. Coordination with managers, colleagues, field technicians and technical offices in Lausanne and Lyon. We require experience of at least 5 years in field service activities and/or remote electrical technical assistance within the production of cardboard machines or mechanical processing machines (CNC). Ability to diagnose faults and make recommendations and solutions. Technical knowledge of Bobst or similar systems will be considered a plus. Analytical skills and attention to detail.
