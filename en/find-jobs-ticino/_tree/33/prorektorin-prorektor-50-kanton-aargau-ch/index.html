Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Collaboration in the school management as Vice Rector / Vice Rector together with the Rector, the Vice Rectors, the Vice Rector and the Head of Services. The school management is responsible for the educational, administrative and financial management of the school and its further development.
  • Supervision of several departments of the high school
  • PICT and Intranet divisions and other tasks

What you bring

  • Completed university studies and a diploma for the Higher Magisterium in a subject granted at the Gymnasium
  • Great pedagogical experience at high school level
  • Leadership and organisational talent
  • Resilience, curiosity and joy in dealing with young people, teachers and employees
  • Teamwork

The Old Cantonal School Aarau is located in a historic park next to Aarau Train Station, five minutes from the city centre. As an equally traditional and innovative school, we run a business school (WMS) and a computer science school (IMS) in addition to the secondary school. Our approximately 1400 students are taught and supervised by around 180 motivated teachers.
