Who are we?

A High Jewelry House with a strong heritage who promote exceptional know-how in an enchanting and creative environment.

Our Maison is looking for talented individuals who contribute to developing and transmitting their expertise with care and no compromise. At Van Cleef & Arpels you will be surrounded by passionate experts and will contribute to many new projects which allow our Maison to regularly reinvent itself.

At Van Cleef & Arpels, we are proud to attract talents from many different backgrounds and experiences. We believe that when diversity and inclusion are fully embraced and empowered, creativity and knowledge emerge to deliver excellence.

Do you match the profile?

You are recognized for your artistic abilities and your sense of colour.

You have a strong dexterity, thoroughness and precision. You show rigor, patience, commitment and love working as a team.

If so, do not hesitate to apply!

What do we expect from you?

Within the Atelier Métiers d'Arts, you will create the enamelling of watch dials according to the aesthetic and technical criteria of the House.

As part of your duties you

  • Be in charge of preparing supports and enamels Identify the necessary parts and tools for future repairs
  • Make enamelling according to the necessary techniques (muffled, partitioned, up-to-date fold, gray...)
  • Manage cooking and stoning
  • Perform self-control on aesthetic and dimensional criteria
  • Participate in process development for new projects.

The Recruitment Process

Apply directly online.

If your profile is selected, you are contacted by the HR team for an interview. Otherwise, you will receive an email informing you of the refusal of your application.

You then meet the Head of Atelier Métiers d'Arts and the Director Métiers d'Arts.
