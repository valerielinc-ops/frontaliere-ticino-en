Full description

Overview Microsoft Cloud Operations and Innovation (CO+I) is the team behind the cloud. Within CO+I, the Integrated Planning & Execution (IPE) - Infrastructure Utilization Effectiveness (IUE) team Responsible for global planning to maximize efficiency of our capacity while optimizing safety, security, and availability across our global datacenter portfolio. As a Senior Technical Program Manager focused on Infrastructure Utilization Efficiency, you will be responsible for leading and managing dependent stake holder OKRs across the EMEA Region, partner team projects, analytics, and growing key business partnerships aimed at optimizing power management infrastructure across diverse geographical locations and multi-disciplinary teams. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to develop and implement strategies for maximizing efficiency, increasing energy consumption, and ensuring sustainable practices in our cloud infrastructure. In alignment with our Microsoft values, we are committed to cultivating an inclusive work environment for all employees to positively impact our culture every day. We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated Senior Technical Program Manager to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will have a proven track record in managing enterprise level programs in a complex cloud infrastructure environment, ensuring seamless integration of cloud services, and optimizing performance across the organization. As an IUE Lead, you will play a key role in driving a successful multi-year execution, implementation of strategies and ongoing management of our cloud infrastructure initiatives across MSFT.

Responsibilities

Program Leadership and Strategy

  • Execute a comprehensive multi-year program strategy to optimize infrastructure utilization across Microsoft's global datacenters.
  • Collaborate with senior leadership to align program goals with organizational objectives and business priorities.
  • Lead cross-functional teams, including engineering, operations, and finance, to ensure program success.

Technical Expertise and Management

  • Utilize deep technical knowledge of datacenter IT and electrical infrastructure and tile power consumption, to drive program initiatives.
  • Oversee the design, implementation, and optimization of power distribution systems within datacenters to improve efficiency and reduce costs.
  • Analyze and interpret data related to power consumption to identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities for improvement.

Project Planning and Execution

  • Develop detailed project plans, timelines, and budgets for program initiatives.
  • Monitor project progress, identify potential risks, and implement mitigation strategies to ensure timely and successful delivery.
  • Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders to ensure seamless execution of program activities.

Stakeholder Engagement and Communication

  • Serve as the primary point of contact for EMEA Region, providing regular updates to senior leadership and stakeholders.
  • Foster strong relationships with key stakeholders, including datacenter operators, engineers, and external vendors.
  • Prepare and present comprehensive reports, presentations, and dashboards to communicate program status, achievements, and challenges.

Continuous Improvement and Innovation

  • Drive a culture of continuous improvement by identifying and implementing best practices, innovative solutions, and process enhancements.
  • Stay current with industry trends, emerging technologies, and regulatory requirements related to datacenter infrastructure and power utilization.
  • Lead efforts to pilot and evaluate new technologies and methodologies to enhance program outcomes.
