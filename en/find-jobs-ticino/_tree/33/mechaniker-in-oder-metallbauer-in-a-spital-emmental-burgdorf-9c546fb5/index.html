Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Would you like to be part of our technical team? Then a varied working environment awaits you, in which you are challenged daily and actively contribute to the smooth operation of our plants. Shape your future with us – and strengthen our team in Burgdorf from now on or by agreement as

Your task profile

  • You ensure the proper operation of the facilities
  • You control and assess the property and building infrastructure
  • You will independently carry out repair and maintenance work on buildings, technical facilities and medical equipment
  • You accompany and monitor suppliers and craftsmen
  • You are responsible for the operation and further development of the mechanical equipment
  • You document the facilities with the appropriate software

Your profile

  • You have completed basic technical training as a maintenance mechanic, polymechanic, plant and apparatus manufacturer, industrial mechanic, metalworker or equivalent training
  • You are a dedicated and service-oriented personality with an exact and conscientious way of working
  • You enjoy teamwork and are ready to support each other’s colleagues
  • You are flexible in terms of time and location with regard to your assignment times (picquet bets)
  • You will benefit from experience in the medical technology sector
  • You have very good knowledge in the maintenance and maintenance of building technology systems
  • You have a category B ticket
