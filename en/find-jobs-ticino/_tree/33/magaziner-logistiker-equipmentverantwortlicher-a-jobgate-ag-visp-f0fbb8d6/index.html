Full description

jobgate ag is a personnel services company based in Aarau and specializes in the placement and rental of specialist personnel in the areas of mechanics, technology, IT, commercial professions, logistics, timber construction, chemicals and the construction industry.

On behalf of our customer, we are looking for a magazine / equipment manager (a) immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for all operational equipment
  • Organization and management of the tool warehouse in Visp and the external warehouse in Olten
  • Issuance and withdrawal of tools, machinery and equipment
  • Implementation of visual condition checks at issue and withdrawal
  • Ensuring operational readiness and availability of equipment
  • Organisation and coordination of maintenance, repairs and security controls
  • Monitoring of test intervals and maintenance cycles
  • Labelling and management of new equipment
  • Maintenance and mutation of equipment data in IT systems
  • Collaboration with purchasing, logistics, project management and operational teams
  • Ensuring order, cleanliness and structure in the magazine

Your profile

  • Completed apprenticeship in a technical or craft profession
  • Several years of professional experience as a magazine, material warden, tool manager or in a comparable function
  • Very good technical understanding
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Very good computer skills (Office 365, Excel, ERP systems)
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Forklift identification category R1 and R4
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • High initiative and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Organizational skills and solution-oriented thinking
  • Flexibility in terms of place of work and working time

Our client offers

  • Responsible and diverse work
  • Modern work equipment and digital systems
  • Short decision-making paths and high personal responsibility
  • Collegial working environment
  • Possibility to actively shape the equipment organization
  • Long-term perspective in a successful company

We are happy to provide you with detailed information by telephone.
