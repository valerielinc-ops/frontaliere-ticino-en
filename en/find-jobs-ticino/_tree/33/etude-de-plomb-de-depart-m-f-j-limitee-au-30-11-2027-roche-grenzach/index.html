Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters. The Position Your new team As a member of the PDG Country Study Start-Up Team, your main focus is to ensure a smooth and highly efficient trial start to support the delivery of clinical trials to patients. You innovate clinical trials: You meet the specific needs in the start-up phase across different therapeutic areas and guarantee a smooth process for patients: inside and testing centers. In this key role, you will network intensively with local and global teams as well as our CRO partners (contract research institutes). Responsibility | This is what awaits you In this role, you will focus on the efficient and legally compliant initiation of Roche-sponsored clinical trials. You develop effective start-up strategies, control operational processes and drive process improvements at country and center level – always in compliance with all regulatory requirements. Your main tasks include:

  • Strategic management: You lead teams in the strategic planning of the operational implementation of the start of the study, with responsibility for risk management, regulatory compliance and partnership with external stakeholders.
  • Operational excellence: You manage the start-up activities and ensure timely activation of the test centres and regulatory compliance. You are responsible for and control the entire budget and contract process – from budget creation to contract negotiation and insurance documents to system-side data maintenance to ensure financial and legal compliance and precise planning.
  • Global Alignment: You work with the global study team to ensure that schedules are met and manage changes throughout the study cycle.
  • Innovation & Standardization: Identify opportunities for process optimization and automation and work with regional and global partners to harmonize systems and optimize schedules.
  • Authority communications: You interact with authorities and institutions (e.g. ethics committees and health authorities) to coordinate policies and practices in clinical trials. Qualifications | This is what you bring For the role as Study Start Up Lead (m/f/d) you bring a successfully completed study (MD, PhD, Master or Bachelor) in the Life Sciences or a comparable qualification. A further university degree (Postgraduate/Master) is very desirable. The following points round off your profile:
  • Professional experience: You have profound experience in the start-up of clinical trials, in regulatory submission processes and in cross-functional cooperation.
  • Expertise: You have an in-depth understanding of the ICH-GCP guidelines, the EU-CTR and the local regulatory framework and have already gained experience in managing service providers and CROs.
  • Methods & IT: You are confident in handling clinical systems (e.g. Veeva Vault, CTIS, RIM) and have strong organizational, analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Mindset: You bring a collaborative and adaptive mindset. You also manage to keep track of complex, matrix-organized and global teams
