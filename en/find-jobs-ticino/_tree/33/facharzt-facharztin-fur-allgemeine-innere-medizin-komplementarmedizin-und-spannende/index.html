Would you like to combine conventional medicine and complementary medicine in your family practice? Then an interdisciplinary centre awaits you, where you can work hand in hand with experienced specialists in naturopathy and build up a key role in the long term. This practice in the heart of Bern has dedicated itself to complementary medicine and specifically combines conventional medical approaches with naturopathy. A large, experienced team of therapists awaits you, which is characterized by open communication and mutual appreciation. You can also look forward to new premises and a modern, well-equipped infrastructure. Have you become curious? I look forward to your contact by phone or WhatsApp: +41 31 310 98 79

Their tasks

  • Maintenance of the general medical consultation with an urban patient base
  • Close cooperation with the therapist: inside and orientation of treatment plans on a naturopathic basis
  • Joint development of the family medical offer with the team and long-term assumption of a senior position in the medical team
  • Active participation and further development of a holistic treatment and care concept

Your Competences

  • As a specialist in general internal medicine, you have experience in family medicine
  • Complementary medical treatment methods interest you, and you want to build a concept that combines conventional and complementary medicine
  • A structured way of working, communication strength as well as openness and motivation for new things distinguish you
  • You are happy to take on responsibility, and the versatility of a senior medical function excites you

Their advantages

  • A strong, experienced team that has been working together for years and is ready to further develop the offer
  • Modern, new premises in the middle of Bern
  • Design room for further thinking of the combination of conventional and complementary medicine and to shape the offer decisively
  • Varied consultation hours with patients: insiders of all ages
