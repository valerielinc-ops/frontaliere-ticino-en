The Position

Job Summary职位概述

The Medical Network Expert/Lead is a key role that brings the Medical Scientific Affairs (MSA) strategy to life for a given disease area by serving as the interface between China Medical Affairs teams, China cross-functional strategy teams, Regional, and Global teams to deliver an integrated medical strategy plan that incorporates short-, mid- and long-term perspectives. The Medical Strategy Partner works hand in hand with the Medical Affairs team members to understand and address customer needs, leverages external stakeholder generated insights, and collaborate with the Global teams to provide China-specific insights into products in development.

医疗网络专家/经理是一个关键角色，通过作为中国医疗事务团队、中国跨职能战略团队、区域和全球团队之间的桥梁，为特定疾病领域带来医学科学事务（MSA）战略，以提供包含短期、中期和长期视角的综合医学战略计划。医学网络专家/经理与医学事务团队成员携手合作，了解并满足客户需求，利用外部利益相关者提出的见解，并与全球团队合作，为正在开发的产品提供中国市场特有的洞见
