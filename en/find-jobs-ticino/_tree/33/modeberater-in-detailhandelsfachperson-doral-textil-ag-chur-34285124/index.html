Full description

Do you love fashion, enjoy selling and like to approach people?

Doral Textil AG runs several Berg Bijou boutiques at central locations in Lenzerheide, Chur and Wildhaus. Our versatile range includes fashion for women, men and children as well as shoes, accessories and home articles.

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a reliable, fashion-loving personality for our branch in Chur:

Your tasks

  • Personal and active advice to our customers
  • Sale of our fashion, shoe and accessory collections
  • Checkout and processing of payments
  • Appealing presentation of the goods and maintenance of sales areas
  • Assistance in receipt of goods, management of goods and warehouses
  • Ensuring order and a well-kept appearance of the boutique
  • Support in the care and support of our webshop
  • Assistance in the organisation and implementation of events

You bring with you

  • Practical experience in sales, ideally in the fashion, textile or shoe sector
  • Joy in fashion and a good sense of style and trends
  • Joy of personal customer advice and active sales
  • Friendly and well-groomed appearance
  • Reliable, independent and responsible working methods
  • Teamwork and flexibility
  • Readiness for Saturday and Seasonal Operations
  • Training in the retail trade is an advantage, but not a requirement. Above all, your sales experience, your personality and your pleasure in fashion and customer advice are important to us.

We offer you

  • Attractive employee discounts on our range
  • A family and appreciative working environment
  • Varied tasks with space for own initiative
  • Cooperation in events, promotions and special customer events
  • Possibility to actively participate in the presentation of goods, webshop and design
  • A versatile working environment with various boutiques and assortments
  • Occupational Health Management (BGM) with various measures to promote health and well-being in the workplace
  • Regular exchange and joint team activities
  • Fair employment conditions
  • Dog friendly company
