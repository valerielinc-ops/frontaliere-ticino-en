The CMS Martigny \&R\égions has more than 450 employees at Saxon, Martigny and Entremont sites.

In order to meet the needs of a diverse population and to pursue the achievement of our long-term objectives, consistent with our vision, we wish to strengthen our HR Administrative Service through a contract of up to 18 months.

You want to join a dynamic environment, where collaboration and cohesion are essential values? We put the position of:

HR Assistant 60%

Your Mission

As an HR assistant, you contribute to the administrative management of personnel and the proper functioning of the HR service. You track files, including entries, departures and contractual changes. You are also involved in writing letters, checking hours and monitoring statistics and training.

You collaborate with the various internal contacts and provide administrative support to the sites of Saxon, Martigny and Entremont, according to the needs of the service.

Your profile

Training and experience

  • Holder of the HR Manager certificate, you have good knowledge of the Swiss legal framework and justify at least three years of experience in a similar function.

Technical expertise

  • Your ease of writing and excellent command of French allow you to produce quality professional documents.
  • You are comfortable with the extraction of statistics, data control and document layout.
  • You master Microsoft Office (Teams, One Note, Outlook, Excel and Word) and knowledge of Canva would be an asset.

Personal competence

  • Organized and rigorous, you know how to prioritize your activities and meet deadlines.
  • Autonomous and endowed with a good analytical spirit, you know how to identify errors and propose improvements.
  • Listening, caring and assertive, you encourage constructive collaboration with your interlocutors.
  • Discreet and respectful, you attach particular importance to the confidentiality of information.
  • Versatile and flexible, you can cope with unforeseen circumstances and meet the needs of the service.

Prerequisite

  • You have a Class B driver's licence and a personal vehicle.

What we offer you

  • A varied activity in a growing sector;
  • A dynamic and collaborative framework;
  • Mobile work tools;
  • Valuing your skills and opportunities for continuing training;
  • First-rate social benefits and benefits;
  • Flexible hours from Monday to Friday, with the possibility of teleworking;
  • Conditions of engagement in accordance with the SLD CTC of the Valais Group of Medical and Social Centres.

Practical information

  • Entry into service: Effective January 1, 2027 or to be agreed upon;
  • Deadline: November 12, 2026\.
