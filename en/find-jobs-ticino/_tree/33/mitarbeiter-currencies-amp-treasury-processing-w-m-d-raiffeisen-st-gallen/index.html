Become part of our team!

As part of the Currencies & Treasury Processing team within Banking Operations, we offer challenging and interesting tasks across the full range of products from currency to money market, precious metals, banknotes and interest. In order to strengthen our team based in St. Gallen and the opportunity to work in the home office, we are looking for a communicative and committed personality as an employee Currencies & Treasury Processing (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • Ensure timely and correct processing of payment transactions and cash settlements
  • Contact with trading, mid-office and counterparties
  • Technical support to the commercial departments
  • Detection of faults and initiation of measures
  • Further development and optimization of existing processes and service quality

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training or shortly completed further education in the field of banking & finance
  • Professional experience in trading and cash settlements
  • Rapid comprehension, strong communication skills and confident appearance
  • Avaloq knowledge is beneficial
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, French and Italian are advantageous

In this challenging role, you will immerse yourself in a dynamic and challenging area of responsibility. Roger Knöpfel Group Leader Currencies & Treasury Processing Why Raiffeisen?
