Who are we?

The Information Technology and Telecommunications Service (SITel) ensures the availability of the information and telecommunications resources necessary for the proper functioning of the cantonal administration. The security and evolution of the information systems of the State and all its partners is also its responsibility.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

Attached to the Head of Sector "Health, Social Affairs, Territory, Environment and Economy", the main mission will be to manage various application projects, in collaboration with beneficiaries and external partners. At the head of a portfolio composed of several projects to be carried out in parallel, you guarantee the attainment of objectives, the coherence of solutions, the control of risks and the alignment with the computer strategy of the state. In a demanding, complex and highly exposed environment, you play a central role as a leader, facilitator and trusted partner with different stakeholders. The "Territory, Environment, Economy, Health, Social Affairs" sector brings together several projects, involving multidisciplinary teams made up of project managers, architects, application managers, developers, beneficiary representatives and external partners.

Activities

Project management

  • Qualifying new project applications in terms of feasibility, cost-effectiveness and success factors and financial estimate
  • Conduct projects, budgets, objectives, boundaries, planning and resources
  • Validate project mandates and document deliverables using HERMES methodology
  • Implement the actions leading to the effective success of the projects, respectively to implement them
  • Prepare and lead the negotiations necessary for the success of the projects
  • Anticipating and managing expectations, differences of interest and sensitive situations
  • Supporting the planning and implementation of business changes, major as minors
  • Ensure communication and maintain trust relations between cantonal administration services, suppliers and institutional partners
  • Ensure quality, receipt and validation of project results
