Full description

Reference code: JR133565

Found in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the oldest watch Manufacture in continuous production for more than 270 years, actually perpetuating a proud heritage of watchmaking excellence and stylistic sophistication through generations of master craftsmen.

At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timespieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches.

YOUR REASON TO BE IN THE HOUSE

As a privileged technical support, you will support all our quality teams and ensure the Quality Assurance of our product developments.

Come and actively contribute to a culture of continuous improvement and the achievement of our most ambitious quality objectives. You will be a key actor in the birth of every new caliber.

YOUR ROLE TO PLAY

By joining our team, you will be an essential pillar of our quality approach, with varied and challenging responsibilities.

Piloting Strategic Quality Projects

You coordinate cross-cutting quality projects to ensure the reliability of our products and processes.

You ensure the full management of the deployment and follow-up of these projects (respecting the planning, facilitation of the bimonthly monitoring committees with the management, support and restart of the teams).

You initiated and contributed to the continuous improvement process focused on dimensional and aesthetic issues.

You are actively contributing to the House's innovation projects.

Operational Support and Continuous Improvement

You provide daily technical support to the entire Quality Department.

You are involved in the definition, deployment and operation of Multi-service Quality tools within the company.

You ensure the backup of the role of quality technician, including the piloting of QRQC

You initiate and follow the resolution of waste-related problems, guaranteeing the rigour of the procedures (trees of causes, respect of steps, etc.).

Development and Monitoring of Performance Indicators

You are actively involved in the definition, implementation and maintenance of KPI for all Quality Departments.

You contribute to the creation of relevant Quality indicators by using data from SAP, PowerBI, SharePoint and Excel.

You work closely with different trades to harmonize practices and define new common rules.

QUALIFICATIONS FOR EXCELLING

Success in this role depends on

You have a ES, CFC or equivalent technical training in the field of micromechanical, general mechanics or watchmaking.

Further training in quality control, metrology or quality assurance would be a real asset.

A significant experience as a quality project manager, ideally in a watchmaking factory, or in an industrial environment require linking production lines, project management and continuous improvement.

Your mastery of the technical aspects of industrial manufacturing and your excellent knowledge of measuring instruments.

Your expertise on continuous improvement and problem solving (5 Why, 8D, PDCA, AMDEC...).

Your mastery of SAP and office tools (text processing, advanced spreadsheet with dynamic graphs and cross tables, presentation tools).

Your excellent sense of listening and communication, you master the art of piloting and conducting work sessions effectively.

Your spirit of analysis and synthesis is recognized. You have methods and relevance.

Your ability to anticipate, demonstrate responsiveness, adaptability and ability to challenge you.

Your pedagogy, you appreciate teamwork and have a keen sense of service.

Vacheron Constantin celebrated 270 years as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755. Based in Geneva, Switzerland, our House operates a worldwide network of nearly 200 outlets in 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional community – watchmakers, artisans and professionals – shares the same passion for our legacy and ambition to build an even brighter future.
