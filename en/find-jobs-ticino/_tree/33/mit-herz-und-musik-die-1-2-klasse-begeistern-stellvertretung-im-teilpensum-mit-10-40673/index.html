Full description

Portrait Herbligen and Brenzikofen. These are two small, rural villages adjacent to the Aaretal, but at the same time they each have their own school. Around 100 students are taught in both schools. The schools of these two villages realized early on that they can only continue to exist if they network well. Thus, the two schools are run as an operational school community as one school. The HeBr schools are also well networked regionally. At school management level, there is a regular exchange and also in relation to MR and SSA as a regional cooperation.

Our focus is on being on the road together. We regularly implement cross-class and school-house projects and deal intensively with the further development of teaching and school in educational conferences. Our existing concepts – as well as our mission statement – are dynamic and are continuously reflected and further developed.

Tasks

You take over a deputy during maternity leave and accompany the children of 1./2. Class with heart, expertise and appreciation. You create a varied lesson and strengthen their independence. You create a learning atmosphere in which they feel comfortable and trust something. With the parents and the class team you maintain an open and trusting exchange.

  • You will teach a total of 10 lessons on Monday from 7.30 a.m. to 11.50 a.m. and on Tuesday from 10.20 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.
  • These include a lesson MGS in half class, a lesson elective Ukulele, and a lesson sports on the breakground.
  • You will also teach German, nature, people, society (NMG) as well as textiles and technical design (TTG). The TTG lessons take place in team teaching.

Requirements

  • Completed training as a primary teacher or a teaching diploma/bachelor for the preschool level.
  • Musical enthusiasm and joy in winning children to sing and play music.
  • Enjoy the contribution of own ideas as well as the exchange and cooperation in a competent and committed team.

We offer

  • A manageable, family-run schoolhouse with a pleasant working atmosphere
  • A school in which age-mixed learning and integration are central guiding principles
  • You will receive valuable support from the healing teacher and the class team.
  • Space for your own ideas, co-creation.
  • The possibility of a prior school visit.
