Civil engineering project leader Apply » Date: 15 Nov 2026

Place

Renens, Renens (VD), CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. You are helping to design safe and reliable rail infrastructure with a direct impact on customers. You will evolve in a varied environment within the engineering team of the Romandie works, where collaboration and knowledge transfer take place on a daily basis. During the first two years, you will benefit from in-depth train training and you will develop continuously. The post we propose is located in Renens and mainly concerns the Valais line, including the Haut-Valais. More information about this position can be found here: Project leader in civil engineering, SBB, or other information about projects underway in French-speaking Switzerland: French-speaking Switzerland and Valais: Railway projects and regional construction programmes, SBB To find out about social benefits, such as free AG and foreign travel at reduced prices (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits . What you can move.

  • As a project leader, you manage a portfolio of multidisciplinary projects and conduct engineering works from the preliminary project to commissioning.
  • You are responsible for the overall direction of projects and ensure that costs, deadlines, quality and safety are respected.
  • You coordinate internal specialists as well as external agents and various stakeholders, and ensure an effective interface with railway operations.
  • You control and validate designs, dimension the supporting structures and conduct internal expertise if necessary.
  • Depending on the file, you take overall responsibility for the execution of the work and represent the projects in bodies or outside bodies.

What you bring.

  • Through your proactive and committed work, you lead interdisciplinary teams and convince various stakeholders.
  • In demanding project situations, you resolve problems in a structured manner and communicate clearly and appropriately to the interlocutors.
  • You bring several years of professional experience, e.g. engineering, construction or project management; an initial experience in managing railway projects is an advantage.
  • You have solid technical knowledge in the calculation, design and execution of carrier structures, for example with experience in reinforced concrete and metal construction.
  • You have a degree in civil engineering (ETH, HES/HEI or equivalent qualification) and use French at level C1 and German at level B2 in the professional setting.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
