Full description

Introduction

For our Purchasing, Support and Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a robotics engineer-regulator whose main mission will be to ensure the availability of logistics and fleet equipment by performing first-level maintenance, prioritizing and coordinating interventions with maintenance, and proposing improvements in reliability to ensure continuity of production.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct first-level maintenance operations in the logistics perimeter
  • Ensuring equipment performance
  • Document and ensure traceability of all interventions
  • Ensuring the accuracy of machine states (Flexnet)
  • Analyze and formalize recurring malfunctions
  • Propose and follow improvements
  • Maintenance support during re-service
  • Inform managers without delay of difficulties encountered on equipment
  • Communicate with different interfaces for higher level interventions and preventive maintenance
  • Team hours 3X8

Profile

  • CFC Automatician or equivalent
  • Computer (SAP, Flexnet, BI, Office Tools)
  • Logistics equipment interfaces
  • Good knowledge of industrial computing and automatic equipment
  • Good knowledge of MS Office software
  • Knowledge in driving CNC machines
  • Team spirit, listening, easy communication
  • Autonomous, decision-making ability, discernment
  • Organized, proactive, good stress resistance, rigorous
  • Priority management, adaptability, responsiveness, customer orientation
  • Good match to corporate values

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.
