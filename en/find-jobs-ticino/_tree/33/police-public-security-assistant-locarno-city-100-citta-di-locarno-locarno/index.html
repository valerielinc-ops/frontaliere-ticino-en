Full description

Bastian Lerch BL/st 6 November 2026 ADVISO DI CONCORSO The MUNICIPIO DI LOCARNO makes known that the contest for the recruitment of a public security assistant of the Police City of Locarno (100%) The Police City of Locarno acts as police Polo for the Region 6 and guarantees an operating service 24 hours on the territory of competence, which includes 14 municipalities agreed. The Territorial Section, among other things, deals with the management of administrative procedures in the field of authorization, to ensure compliance with the regulations relating to public exercises, to trade and to the taxi service and to the preparation and implementation of devices for the management of traffic, as well as operational controls on fiscal subjection and secondary residences. Tasks

  • carry out delegated tasks on road traffic in accordance with law and

relative orders;

  • apply the regulations in force in the areas of competence of the municipal police, by virtue of delegates

or specific tasks;

  • respect and enforce municipal regulations and ordinances;
  • take special tasks delegated from superiors or established by internal, municipal or

cantonal;

  • collaborate with police patrols in viary management in case of events;
  • to notify executive acts and precepts;
  • take care of the assigned neighborhood and maintain regular contacts with its associations;
  • to ensure, in case of need, the coverage of the operating power station on 24 hours.

Requirements

  • certificate of Public Safety Assistant ISP or availability to attend the School for

public security assistants;

  • excellent knowledge of the Italian language, spoken and written; knowledge of other national languages

is a preferential title;

  • strong capacity for persuasion and cooperation and good skills in managing

conflicts and search for consent;

  • strong ability to communicate clearly, effective and authoritatively in addition to a good

listening skills;

  • the person must have Swiss citizenship;
  • licence to conduct categories A and B; in the absence of category A, availability to achieve it

by the end of the test year. Physical and psychological requirements

  • good general state of health, in particular absence of pathologies that can compromise

the regular conduct of police work and the performance of shiftwork, even on 24 hours;

  • good psychophysical resistance to stress;
  • absence of dependencies incompatible with the function of public security assistant (with

possibility to be subject to medical examinations);

  • self-control skills and attitude to group work.

The expected salary, paid in 13 monthly, is established by the Organic Regulation of the collaborators and collaborators of the Municipality of Locarno (ROC-LO) and the Regulation concerning the unification of the ranks and salary conditions of the cantonal and municipal police bodies (RUGraS), Assistant II class 3 (minimum CHF 52’756.- / maximum CHF 81’560.-), to which are added the children’ allowances The service is scheduled as soon as possible. This notice of competition is published on the website of the City at the address The applications must be submitted exclusively through the online portal by Friday 4th December 2026 at 23:59 The City Hall cannot, in any case, consider applications and attachments transmitted through other channels (paper, email, fax, etc.) or incomplete applications.

The application must include the following documents

  • curriculum vitae and motivation letter;
  • medical certificate or self-certification downloadable on www.locarno.ch - section desk

virtual - different forms;

  • diplomas or certificates of study and work;
  • certificate of Public Safety Assistant ISP, if already obtained;
  • extract of the judicial box;
  • copy of the identity document;
  • Excerpt office execution and failures;
  • passport format photo.

Any information can be requested directly at the Police Command of the City of Locarno (tel. 091 756 33 29). THE MUNICIPLE

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
