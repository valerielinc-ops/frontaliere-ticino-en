Full description

16.10.2026 Aarberg

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Seeland, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Aarberg, Biel, Büren, Lyss, Ins and Studen, we are looking for a motivated personality as a manager for our office in Aarberg.

What awaits you?

  • Management and further development of 5 employees of the Aarberg office. Silvia, Neo and other Kolleg:innen look forward to you
  • You are responsible for the success of the Aarberg office and ensure team success by promoting a positive performance culture
  • As a professional contact person, you accompany and support employees with complex customer inquiries
  • Individual and needs-based advice to customers in all financial matters in the areas of financing, investing and provisioning
  • Identifying and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous processing of the existing customer portfolio
  • You represent the Raiffeisenbank in Zealand in the Aarberg region with your personality and develop your network further

What do you bring?

  • You have completed banking education as well as higher education in banking or finance
  • You are a competent leader with the talent and passion to accompany, promote and successfully develop employees
  • You have in-depth knowledge in the areas of investing, pension and financing
  • You convince through your strong customer orientation, high consulting skills and entrepreneurial thinking
  • You have a safe and profitable appearance and are ideally familiar with the local market

Do you not only want to run a business office, but also develop people, take responsibility and shape the future of our bank in Aarberg? Then I look forward to meeting you. Sven Hohl Head of Market Area South Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
