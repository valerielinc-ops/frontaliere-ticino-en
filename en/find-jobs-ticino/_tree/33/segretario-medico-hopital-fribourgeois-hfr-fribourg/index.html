Full description

In order to complete its team, the medical department is looking for the secretariat of the office and the operating centres of the HFR Fribourg – Cantonal Hospital, one/one

  • planning the medical appointments of the department and interdisciplinary
  • planning service interventions according to internal service directives
  • central relay all requests for endoscopy service and operating centres
  • communicating and transmitting information in a clear and accurate manner by adapting to specific and/or problematic situations
  • ensure coordination with other services
  • ensure the delivery of the service independently and flexibly, in close cooperation with the Chief Medical Officer, the Assistant to the Chief Medical Officer or the Secretary responsible, while respecting the multisite organisation, standards and directives of the service
  • planning, monitoring and implementing an operating programme by knowing the constraints of different services while focusing priorities on quality management of the patient
  • to receive patients in the secretariat of endoscopy
  • type medical reports
  • carry out various administrative work inherent in the service of endoscopy and the operating centres
  • SWC business employee or equivalent, medical secretary degree - an asset
  • knowledge of the medical environment with good knowledge of medical terminology an asset
  • mastery of the French language with good knowledge of German (minimum B2)
  • excellent computer tools (MS Office)
  • knowledge of institutional applications (Winscribe, Polypoint, MedFolio), an asset
  • excellent stress management, adaptability, sense of organization, flexibility, ease of communication and reliability, relational ease
