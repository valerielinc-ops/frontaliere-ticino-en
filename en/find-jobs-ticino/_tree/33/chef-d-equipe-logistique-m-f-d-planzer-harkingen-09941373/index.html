Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

Tasks

  • Together with your employees, you manage day-to-day operations and hold all the strings in your hands.
  • The responsibility for the day-ready work execution and the return processing lies with you
  • You ensure that the goods land safely and quickly in the right storage space and monitor the provision of the goods for delivery to our customers
  • Optimizing processes in logistics gives you pleasure and you actively participate in the implementation of CIP processes
  • The department management can count on your support in various general tasks and you are also happy to take part in bottlenecks in day-to-day business
  • You convert secure and efficient communication and cooperation with internal areas for smooth production logistics
  • Order, cleanliness and work safety are important to you and you monitor their compliance

Profile

  • You bring an education in the logistics sector and thanks to your already accumulated professional experience you know how the rabbit runs
  • With a structured and independent way of working, you keep track even in hectic situations and set the right priorities
  • A solution-oriented mindset and a flexible and positive mindset are among your strengths
  • You enjoy working on the PC and feel as comfortable in administrative tasks as in the operational logistics environment
  • Oral and written you can safely express yourself in German
  • A good understanding of numbers distinguishes you and you can realistically estimate the work volume and the associated performance expenditure
  • You communicate clearly, make well-founded decisions and ideally have already gained initial leadership experience
  • Knowledge of AS400 is a plus

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Benefit in our internal staff shop from discounted prices

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
