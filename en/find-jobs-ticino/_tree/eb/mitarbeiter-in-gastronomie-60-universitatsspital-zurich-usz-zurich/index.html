You love contact with people and like to make our guests feel comfortable? Our restaurant offers a diverse gastronomic environment where hospitality and personal service are the focus. Become part of our dedicated gastronomy team and bring your joy of hosting to us.

Your main tasks

  • You serve our guests customer-oriented and professionally.
  • The operation of the cash registers and execution of the billings are also part of your tasks.
  • When accepting goods, you take over the entry controls and the storage.
  • You are jointly responsible for the independent installation and dismantling of the goods and the professional preparation of the food.
  • Cleaning work and working in the office are also part of your job.
  • The hygiene standards according to HACCP are consistently observed.

Your profile

  • You have training as a restoration specialist EFZ, hotel specialist EFZ or a corresponding training in the food industry.
  • In dealing with money and cash systems, you already have experience.
  • A friendly, courteous and well-groomed appearance distinguishes you.
  • You work independently and responsibly and convince through punctuality, reliability and flexibility.
  • You bring basic PC knowledge and openness to new technologies.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing.
  • Work assignments from Monday to Friday between 06.30 and 15.30 are well compatible for you.
  • For occasional assignments on the weekend you are also ready.

Our offer

We offer you a versatile task in an advanced working environment characterized by high quality orientation, independence and a high sense of responsibility. A motivated and committed team supports you in all respects. As a modern employer, we apply exemplary and respectful management principles.
