Full description

Tasks: You accompany our patients through their rehab routine and thus relieve the burden of care.

  • Accompany: You accompany patients according to the appointment list on their paths in the house
  • Support: You help in everyday life, such as wearing shoes or filling out questionnaires
  • Working together: You work closely with the care and regularly exchange with her
  • Collect: You collect patient data in our rehabilitation software KISIM
  • Keep order: You clean the coffee machines and ensure order in the rooms on the floors | Requirements: You bring that – and what you can not yet, you learn with us
  • Joy to people: You like to approach people and bring a good mood
  • Fitness: You are good on foot and like to walk many steps a day
  • Language: You talk well in German [level B1/B2]
  • PC: You do simple administrative work on the computer
