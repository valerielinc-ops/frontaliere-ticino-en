Full description

An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Located on the Bürgenberg, a 500-meter-high rocky outcrop in central Switzerland, the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne stands out for its bold design, groundbreaking sustainability, breathtaking landscapes and state-of-the-art leisure activities. Around 700 employees from 54 nations provide excellent hospitality and provide the perfect setting for moments beyond imagination. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two accommodation options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Tavern 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence; at the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. For the third consecutive year, Bürgenstock Resort has been officially certified as a “Great Place to Work” in Switzerland – an award that fills the entire team with pride. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an

Reservation & Guest Service Agent 80% (f/m/i)

You are primarily responsible for

  • the acceptance and booking of reservations made by telephone, in writing or by e-mail
  • Retrieving and processing reservations via the various reservation systems
  • the active sale and advice of guests on the phone
  • Participation in mystery calls and individual coaching afterwards
  • Contact person for hotel guests via telephone, acceptance of room service orders and SPA requests
  • the sale of hotel vouchers
  • Responsibility for guest correspondence
  • Processing daily checklists and requests
  • active action and prevention of guest complaints, internal training courses available

They have completed vocational training in the administrative field. Ideally, you already have experience in tourism or in the hotel industry. German, English and preferably French are fluent in words and writing. Shift and weekend work is a matter of course for you. They show a high level of performance, are very flexible and resilient and work calmly, accurately and efficiently even under time and work pressure.
