Full description

Graphic designer / in 80% - Marketing & Communication You think visually, are saddle-proof in Adobe and know how an idea becomes a convincing means of communication? Do you move just as safely between InDesign, printing and corporate design as between video, photography and new AI tools? Then you will fit us. With us, you actively shape visual communication and ensure that our appearance remains professional and recognizable across all channels. This is what awaits you

  • You design communication tools for print and digital channels.
  • Together with the marketing team, you implement on- and offline campaigns.
  • You prepare print data professionally and coordinate production with our printers.
  • You pay attention to the consistent implementation of our corporate design.
  • You use Adobe Creative Cloud and AI-enabled applications for your daily design work.
  • You realize internal video and photo recordings and edit the resulting image material.
  • You keep an eye on our printed materials and make sure that the required materials are always available and up-to-date.

You bring that with you

  • An in-depth training in graphics, polygraphy or visual communication
  • Very good knowledge and experience in Adobe InDesign, Photoshop and Illustrator
  • Experience with prepress and production processes
  • Openness for the professional use of AI
  • A secure eye for design, typography and visual language
  • Independent and careful work as well as organizational talent
  • An uncomplicated, team-oriented working style

We offer our more than 400 employees

  • An attractive working environment in a family atmosphere with a du-culture and many employee events
  • Staff breakfast and discounted food in our hotel restaurants
  • 6 weeks holidays as well as the possibility of unpaid holidays
  • A versatile offer for health promotion

For further information, Sarah Ochmann, Head of Marketing, T 061 836 24 38, is at your disposal.
