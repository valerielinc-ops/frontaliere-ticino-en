HUGO BOSS is one of the leading fashion and lifestyle companies in the premium segment with more than 19,000 employees worldwide. As versatile as we are, we share a common goal: We love fashion, we change fashion!

Become a brand ambassador and part of a team that works with passion, ambition and know-how to create excellent customer experiences. Be among the first to bring our collections from the Runway to the customers! Become part of our team and discover your customized career opportunities.

From now on, we are looking for a dedicated part-time salesperson for our team at the BOSS Store in Ascona who will inspire customers with passion and inspiration for our BOSS brand. The position will be filled as a seasonal contract: from 01.04. to 30.10., the amount is 60%, from 01.11. to 31.03. 20%. We look forward to receiving your application and welcoming you to HUGO BOSS.

This is what awaits you with us

  • With the presentation of our products you ensure a first-class and unforgettable shopping experience

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
