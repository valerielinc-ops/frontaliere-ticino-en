The Position We are a dynamic, production-accompanying compliance team in Kaiseraugst! As a compliance group for our prefabricated syringe line (PFS), we are responsible for the quality and regulatory standards for the aseptic filling and processing of syringes under the strictest GMP specifications. In this role as GMP Compliance Specialist (internal: Asset Excellence Manager), you ensure the continuous GMP compliance and stability of our processes. Its main focus is on operational compliance – from timely testing of batch documentation (batch record review) to self-responsible management of deviations (deviations), CAPAs and changes (changes). As a central interface, you work closely with production, quality assurance (QA) and other support functions to ensure the highest standards in a highly regulated environment.

The possibility

  • GMP compliance: Professional responsibility for compliance with national and international GMP rules in the field of sterile filling of prefabricated syringes.
  • Batch Record Review: Timely implementation of the check for completeness and correctness of the batch records as well as preparation of the company-side batch closure for transfer to the QA.
  • Deviation & CAPA management: self-responsible handling of events, deviations and complaints as owner/SME, carrying out root cause analyses (RCA) and defining and tracking effective CAPAs.
  • Change Management: Sustainable management and processing of change processes as a change owner or support as a specialist (SME).
  • Audit & inspection responsibility: Representation of the department as SME in internal and external audits as well as official inspections.
  • Training & knowledge management: Creation of training concepts, implementation of training and further development of the team members.
  • Operational Excellence & Digitalization: Co-creation of digital processes (MES/SAP) and use of KPI and LPS tools to increase performance.

Who you are

  • Completed pharmaceutical, scientific or technical studies or vocational training in combination with 2-3 years of professional experience.
  • Good knowledge or professional experience in pharmaceutical production, development, quality assurance and control
  • Very good communication skills in word and writing in German
  • Knowledge of English according to the activities carried out
  • High commitment to the job and the employer
  • Openness towards new
  • Resilience and flexibility
  • Constant performance with changing requirements.
