Full description

The Klinik Wysshölzli is a specialized psychiatric and psychotherapeutic private clinic in the Oberaargau (Help bushing, BE). Job: Psychologist Psychotherapeutin 07.2026–60% 80 04/2026. The clinic Wysshölzli is a leading psychiatric and psychotherapeutic specialist clinic for women with dependency diseases and eating disorders. Our main location in the Oberaargau, canton of Bern, in a quiet location convinces with beautiful nature and the proximity to Bern and Olten. The clinic offers 37 stationary treatment sites and two outpatients. Instructions are made from all cantons of German-speaking Switzerland, and patients are planned. We are looking for a psychologic / psychotherapist 60 - 80%

  • Your tasks
  • With your psychotherapeutic work in individual and group settings, you make a valuable contribution and offer our patients a safe space to develop their strengths.
  • You take over the therapy planning and the case management. Our patients are often in stationary treatment for several months.
  • They are in intensive exchange with the psychologist team. The interdisciplinary cooperation and the regular inclusion of the relatives are of course for us.
  • Our versatile and exciting work is complemented by psychotherapy with outpatients and the conduct of pre-conferences for indication testing.
  • You document illnesses and write reports.
  • Their profile
  • they bring great pleasure in deepening psychotherapeutic work with mostly young women and their relatives.
  • They have completed or advanced psychotherapy training.
  • You have several years of clinical experience, preferably with eating disorders or dependence diseases.
  • With your connected thinking and ideas, you contribute to the continuous development of our therapy program.
  • With your open, uncomplicated and collegial way, you fit into our lively team.
  • They are resilient and enjoy an independent and interdisciplinary work. Your chance
  • With a lot of self-employment you take on an interesting, responsible and versatile activity.
  • In our modern, manageable operation, collegial, open and uncomplicated culture and interdisciplinary cooperation are highly appreciated.
  • There are internal and external supervision as well as training opportunities.
  • We offer affordable meals and free parking. We look forward to your application.
