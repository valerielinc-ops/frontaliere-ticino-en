Full description

Carrosserie painter PW (a) Galliker Service AG prepares the new cars from various car manufacturers for the Swiss market. In addition, on behalf of importers, corporate customers, rental car, leasing companies and dealers, it performs order-specific series, conversion and various workshop work. For the car brands in question, Galliker Service AG is also responsible for delivering and taking back the rental cars and leasing vehicles and carries out all kinds of workshop, car bodywork and painting work in the service of the customers. Task area

  • Carrying out painting work on customer vehicles
  • Grinding of carrosserie parts
  • Carrying out preparatory work
  • Polish and Finish

Requirement profile

  • Learned carrosserie painter
  • Trained eye for perfect surfaces
  • Motivated to work and enjoy the automobile
  • Independent, reliable, capable of teamwork, resilient and flexible
  • Driving licence Cat. B

Offer

  • Always on the pulse of the latest technology and digitalization
  • Personal development opportunities in a versatile company
  • More hours can be compensated
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
