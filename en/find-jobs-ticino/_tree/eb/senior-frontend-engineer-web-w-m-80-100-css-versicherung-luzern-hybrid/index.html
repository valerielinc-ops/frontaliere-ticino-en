This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: You continue to develop the frontend of our website and work with a broad technology stack from standard technologies such as CSS/SCSS, HTML, TypeScript and Vue.js to Storybook. Everything is integrated into our content management system AEM as a cloud service.
  • In the UI/UX area, you work with Figma, keep the designs up-to-date and develop them continuously.
  • You connect interfaces to the frontend, ensure unit and UI testing, manage pipelines and actively participate in our cloud infrastructure on Azure.
  • In the team, you develop architecture-compliant solutions and implement them. In addition, you set up automated tests and carry out code reviews.
  • You build it, you run it! As a DevOps and Scrum team member, you are responsible for the software, from conception to operation.
  • You are part of an Agile Release Train that works according to the Lean Agile Mindset of SAFe and lives a culture of collaboration and continuous improvement. You bring that with you
  • First things first: Your heart beats for frontend development, DevOps and you can get excited about UI/UX. You have at least five years of experience in agile software development with current frontend technologies and enjoy complex issues.
  • A computer science education or a study in the field of software development – whether classic or detours, we are open for your individual career path.
  • You are familiar with technologies such as CSS/SCSS, HTML, Typescript, Figma, and ideally also have experience in using AI tools.
  • Knowledge of Azure Infrastructure with Terraform, Github Actions Pipeline (CI/CD) and NodeJs are not a must, but a plus.
  • High software quality is a matter of course for you: your code is thoughtful, clean and sustainable.
  • You keep your knowledge technologically and methodologically up-to-date, network and share your knowledge in the team.
