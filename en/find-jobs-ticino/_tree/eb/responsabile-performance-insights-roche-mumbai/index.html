The Opportunity

Are you ready to be a key driver in transforming our procurement function into a data-driven organization? As our Performance & Insight Manager, based in Mumbai you will leverage complex data to generate actionable insights that optimize strategies, improve supplier performance, mitigate risks, and enhance overall procurement efficiency.

  • Reporting Line: The position directly reports the Head of Procurement Center of Excellence (DOP).
  • Wider team and stakeholders: Works closely and cross-culturally with procurement team members, category managers, finance, IT, and other global stakeholders.
  • Role Type: A highly analytical and strategic role focused on data integration, insights generation, and the continuous enhancement of procurement intelligence solutions.
  • Impact: You will translate complex data into clear, impactful recommendations. Specifically, you are accountable for:
  • Insights Generation & Analysis: Proactively identifying areas where data can optimize decision-making. Conducting in-depth analysis to spot trends and opportunities, and performing scenario planning and sensitivity analysis for different procurement strategies.
  • Reporting & Visualization: Designing and developing insightful dashboards, reports, and visualizations to track key procurement metrics (e.g., spend analysis, savings, supplier performance, risk, and compliance).
  • Data Integration: Identifying, collecting, cleansing, and integrating relevant data from various internal and external sources, including ERP systems and market intelligence platforms.
  • Program & Rollout Support: Supporting the deployment of procurement tools and processes by defining reporting requirements, developing tracking mechanisms, and ensuring data integrity during implementation.
  • Technology Evaluation: Evaluating, recommending, and driving the implementation of appropriate data analytics tools and platforms in close collaboration with IT.
  • Stakeholder Communication: Preparing and presenting findings effectively, translating complex data insights into clear, understandable formats for both technical and non-technical audiences, including leadership.

Who you are

We look for professionals with a strong analytical mindset, a can-do mentality, and a high willingness to take personal responsibility to drive our data evolution.

  • Education: A Bachelor's or Master's degree in a quantitative field such as Data Science, Business Analytics, Statistics, Economics, Supply Chain Management, or a related discipline is required.
  • Experience: 5+ years of proven experience in data analysis and insights generation, specifically focused on procurement or supply chain. Experience working cross-culturally in a global position is essential.
  • Procurement Knowledge: A deep understanding of procurement processes, methodologies, and best practices.
  • Technical Skills: Strong proficiency in SQ
