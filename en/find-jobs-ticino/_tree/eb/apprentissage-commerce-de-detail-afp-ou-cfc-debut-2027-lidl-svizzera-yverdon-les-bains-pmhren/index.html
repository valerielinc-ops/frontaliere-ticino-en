Full description

Do you already have an idea of what you will do after completing your school-leaving qualification? Set out on your future with us and take advantage of the development opportunities at Lidl Suisse. Stores are at the heart of our business. As a retail apprentice (with a focus on creating shopping experiences), AFP or CFC, you will contribute to the success of your Lidl store. Start/End AFP: August 1, 2027 – July 31, 2029 Start/End CFC: August 1, 2027 – July 31, 2030

Your responsibilities

  • Cash register management and preparation of cash statements
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Stocking and presenting goods
  • Checking the receipt of goods in the stockroom and assisting your team during stocktakes
  • Assisting with the planning and implementation of promotions and seasonal displays
  • Determining merchandise requirements and ordering merchandise in the 3rd year of apprenticeship (CFC)
  • First experience in leading a team in the 3rd year of apprenticeship (CFC)

Your profile

  • Average to very good grades in the lower secondary school-leaving certificate
  • AFP: A1 level in a foreign language and CFC: at least A2
  • Enjoy working with people
  • Independent working style and quick thinking
  • Team spirit, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and availability
  • Physical stamina
  • Good knowledge of French

Our offer An apprenticeship that opens up many paths for you. After successfully completing your apprenticeship, and if you have good personal skills, we will help you progress to a management position in the store. In addition, you will benefit from the Lidl collective labour agreement and numerous attractive benefits
