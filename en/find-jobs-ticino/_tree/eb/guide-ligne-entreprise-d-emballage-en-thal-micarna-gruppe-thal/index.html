Full description

Micarna SA, a company of the Migros Group, stands for high-quality food, modern production and the highest standards of quality, hygiene and safety. At the Thal location, you can expect a dynamic environment, a committed team and a responsible task with room for manoeuvre.

Mansioni

  • You ensure a smooth flow on the assigned production line.
  • You operate and monitor machines and plants professionally and ensure safe operation.
  • You coordinate the work processes on the line and divide the employees appropriately.
  • You ensure that quality standards and hygiene and safety guidelines are consistently adhered to.
  • You perform quality controls during production and document deviations or faults.
  • You implement the production plans together with the team leader and contribute to the timely completion of the orders.

Requirements

  • You have experience in food production, ideally in a comparable function: You convince with leadership skills, assertiveness and a high sense of responsibility.
  • No training required: You bring knowledge of production processes, machine operation and quality assurance.
  • You work reliably, team-oriented and communicate clearly and addressable.
  • You consistently pay attention to safety, hygiene and quality requirements.
  • You have language skills in German at least level B1.
  • German (flowing)
