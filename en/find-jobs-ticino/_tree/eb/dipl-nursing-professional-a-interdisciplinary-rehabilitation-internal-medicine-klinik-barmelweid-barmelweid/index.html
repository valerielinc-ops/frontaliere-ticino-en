Become part of our rehab station – a varied and responsible task awaits you! Employment Level: 60–100% Entry by Agreement

You don’t have to know everything with us. But you can ask anything. You can try out ideas and take responsibility. For us, lean means: giving responsibility, trusting and becoming better together. We create experiences together and cultivate an open, warm and honest culture. We achieve success together in a team. You move that with us

  • You ensure the entire care process and independently organize the daily routine.
  • You recognize changes in the state of health and crisis situations in good time and act competently.
  • You promote interdisciplinary cooperation and coordinate the services offered.

  • with your completed training as a Dipl. Care specialist HF / Dipl. Nursing professional HF.
  • with your ability to act calmly and objectively in complex and challenging situations.
  • with your friendly, helpful and service-oriented approach to patients, relatives and in the team.
  • with your reliable and independent working method.
  • with your willingness to work shifts (day and night services, including weekends and public holidays). Barmelweid is the leading specialist and rehabilitation clinic in northwestern Switzerland and the largest psychosomatic clinic in Switzerland. In 285 beds, the clinic provides inpatient care for patients. The clinic specializes in geriatric, internistic, cardiovascular and pulmonary rehabilitation, long covid, psychosomatic medicine and psychotherapy, psychocardiology and sleep medicine. At Barmelweid, the focus is on lean thinking and methods: all efforts are geared towards patients. This includes standardized safety rounds, the patient board and the leveling of the load on the stations by means of flows. The care in the department of Psychosomatic Medicine and Psychotherapy is completed with the method of reference care, the care in the department of Internal Medicine with the care managers. As a rule, patients stay on the Barmelweid for 2 to 6 weeks. This gives our nurses time to engage with the patients in depth. You can expect a lively company with interprofessional work that retrieves all the skills of a nurse. Barmelweid employs more than 750 people. It is located at an altitude of around 800 meters on a clearing in the Aargau Jura and is embedded in a unique environment with a nature park.
