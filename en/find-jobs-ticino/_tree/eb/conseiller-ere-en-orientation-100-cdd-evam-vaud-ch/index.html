What you bring to our mission

Within the area of Support for Migrants in Apartment (AMA) Pôle Orientation et Emploi, you promote the socio-professional integration and empowerment of the beneficiaries by providing advice and guidance through individualised support. You help coordinate integration services and develop collaborations with the internal and external network.

Your key missions

Socio-professional support for beneficiaries

  • Conduct stocktaking and monitoring interviews to assess their resources and skills
  • Build a social and professional integration project with the beneficiary
  • Conduct collective information sessions
  • Orient and support beneficiaries in active, internal and external measures, according to their needs, resources and skills
  • Collaborate with the recipient support network
  • Make necessary funding requests and ensure administrative follow-up

Coordination and development of benefits

  • Coordinate the participation of beneficiaries in internal and external integration measures.
  • Participate in developing partnerships with the voluntary, private and voluntary network
  • To contribute to the development of the Pôle's partnerships and services, as well as to projects where necessary, the Pôle or the Institution.

Your profile

  • Master in Social Sciences or other higher education related to the function
  • Minimum 3 years experience in the field of socio-occupational integration, particularly with a vulnerable audience
  • Knowledge of the cantonal network active in the field of integration an asset
  • Interest in migration
  • Maintenance skills and strong relational skills
  • Ability to manage complex situations and collaborate with multiple actors
  • Sense of planning and analysis
  • Knowledge of English as an asset

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
