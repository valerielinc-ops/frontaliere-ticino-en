Spital Langenthal Printing place Post as PDF We are looking for a supplement to our team from February 2027 or by agreement

They do it with us.

  • Treatment (therapy, diagnosis, counselling) of patients in inpatient and outpatient setting in all areas of clinical speech therapy
  • Where possible, use in the interprofessional stationary departments of acute care and palliative care
  • Active co-creation of the stationary and ambulatory logopaedic offer
  • Opportunity to expand outpatient therapy in the field of pediatrics, depending on individual experience and personal interest
  • Collaboration in or independent implementation of team-internal and interprofessional projects for the further development and establishment of speech therapy in everyday hospital life as well as for the continuous improvement of quality

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed training in speech therapy with a diploma recognised in Switzerland
  • Interest in clinical work with adult patients
  • Independent and reliable working methods as well as competence in interprofessional cooperation
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Enjoyment of working in a collegial and committed team
  • Engaged and empathetic personality

Medical personnel service

Operating committee

SRO AG has an operating committee, which forms the link between the employees and the management. As a staff representative, it is available to the employees as a contact person.

Health promotion services

Within the framework of occupational health management, SRO AG offers ongoing offers in prevention in occupational safety and case management.

Fleet rebate

Our employees benefit from fleet discounts at various brands.

Allowances and bonuses

For assignments in the evening, at night and on weekends, an inconvenience allowance of CHF 9 per hour is paid out. For evening and night assignments, an additional time credit of 20% is credited. We also reward short-term intervention or the successful placement of a new team member with a premium.

Pension Fund

Our employees are insured by the pension fund SRO. SRO AG covers up to 60% of the contributions (instead of the usual 50%) and depending on the individual situation can be chosen between three savings plans.

Family and childcare

Employees who are legally entitled to family allowances also receive a monthly care allowance of CHF 180.– to CHF 250.–. Continued pay during 16 weeks of maternity leave and 15 days of paternity leave shall be 100%.
