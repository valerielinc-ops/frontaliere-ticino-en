Full description

Become part of our team! We are looking for a social pedagogue 40-45% (on 2.5 days per week) with case responsibility for the care of a kindergarten child with special education status (ISR type C) by 1 January 2027 or by arrangement.

  • Care of a kindergarten child with special education status (ISR type C) in lessons on 2.5 days with case responsibility
  • Guidance for the school assistance involved in the setting
  • Specialist exchange with class teacher
  • Cooperation with parents
  • Degree as a social pedagogue FH or HFS; manager
  • Infant child educator or childhood educator HF. For applications with a foreign diploma / certificate, a Swiss diploma recognition must be attached
  • humorous, flexible and team capable personality
  • Supportive in cooperation
  • Open to the basic attitude of “New Authority”
  • Curious, open to nursing tasks
  • living an inclusive attitude
  • The opportunity to contribute your own ideas for integration
  • A generous and modern infrastructure
  • Structuring and thoughtful processes
  • a varied everyday life in the middle of the city of Zurich

Zmitzt in the heart of Züri - there(s) are me! Every day at the pulse of education and where our future is growing and learning.
