Real time resource planner Apply » Date: 14 Nov 2026

Place

Renens (VD), CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. At the Traffic Control Center, you are at the heart of the operations.You manage in real time the engagement of passenger rolling stock on the western network of Switzerland. In a dynamic and demanding environment, you make decisive operational decisions to ensure the continuity, safety and quality of railway operations. In normal situations as in the event of disruption, you quickly analyze situations, define priorities and coordinate with key players to maintain - or restore - a reliable service. What you can move.

  • You orchestrate in real time the allocation of rolling stock and ensure the continuity of operation.
  • You manage incidents and disturbances by making quick, structured and assumed decisions.
  • Even in a constrained situation, you restore exploitation by optimizing available resources.
  • You coordinate key players (trains, driver/manoeuvre personnel, and maintenance centre) to ensure an effective response.
  • In each of your decisions, you integrate technical, operational and economic constraints.
  • You simultaneously manage multiple and unexpected demands while maintaining a global vision and contributing to continuous improvement.

What you bring.

  • You have a strong, reliable and solutions-oriented personality with strong communication skills.
  • You are able to quickly analyze complex situations and make reliable decisions under pressure.
  • You have confirmed experience in railway operations or in a comparable operating environment.
  • You have a recognised vocational training (CFC or equivalent).
  • You combine resilience, spirit of inter-service collaboration, flexibility to work in irregular hours (24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year) and a mastery of French or German with knowledge of the other language (B1 desired).
  • You master the simultaneous use of several computer tools and systems.
