Full description

In order to strengthen our team in Geneva, we are looking for a 100% commercial cold project leader.

Your tasks

  • You will be responsible for the profitability of your business, monitoring and financial management;
  • You will be in charge of the study and costing of the estimates on your construction sites and on our maintenance sites;
  • You will guarantee compliance with contractual and budgetary commitments;
  • You will supervise the organisation of the interventions of your assemblers, technicians and manage the equipment;
  • You also pilot subcontracting;
  • You are a powerful proposition and offer suggestions for improvement in terms of performance, operation and safety.
  • You have to travel all over the Swiss Romande as part of your projects.

What Characterizes You

  • You have advanced technical training in cold (ES technician, engineer, IFI, HES, BF, DF,...);
  • You have at least two years of experience in managing refrigeration projects;
  • You have very good technical knowledge in the field of cold, especially CO2. Advanced knowledge in the field of electricity and regulation is a plus;
  • You know how to use the usual computer tools;
  • You have an operational level in technical English. Knowledge of another language is an asset;
  • You are good communicating, you are organized and have a good spirit of analysis and synthesis.

This is what we offer you.

  • We offer good social benefits;
  • Various and stimulating projects;
  • A dynamic and collaborative team;
  • Prospects for training and development in the group.
