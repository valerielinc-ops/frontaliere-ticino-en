Pensum: 80-100 %

You can design it.

  • You lead the project planning and the project support professionally, strengthen the cooperation in the team and develop employees further.
  • You provide orientation in everyday work, clarify priorities and responsibilities and promote an open exchange of knowledge.
  • You ensure an efficient project planning process, identify bottlenecks at an early stage and improve handovers and processes together with the participating areas.
  • You keep an eye on project progress, workload, quality and deadlines and initiate appropriate measures in the event of deviations.
  • You ensure the professional quality of project planning at network levels 5–7 and combine technical requirements with economic solutions.
  • You coordinate network construction projects with all stakeholders and support them reliably until completion.

That makes you

  • You have an education as a master grid electrician or an equivalent qualification and know the relevant technical regulations.
  • You have experience in the design and execution of distribution networks and can assess technical solutions technically and economically.
  • You have experience in leading employees professionally, formulating expectations in a comprehensible way and providing constructive feedback.
  • You analyse processes, implement improvements effectively together with others and think beyond areas.
  • You work responsibly, set clear priorities and remain focused and solution-oriented even in complex tasks.
  • Ideally, you know lean management or continuous improvement processes and are open to new professional and digital solutions.
