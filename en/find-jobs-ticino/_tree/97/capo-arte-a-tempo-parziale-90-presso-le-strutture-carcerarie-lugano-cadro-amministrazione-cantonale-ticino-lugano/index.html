Full description

Tasks

  • Share responsibility for managing the Central Kitchen laboratory
  • Coordinate the work of assigned detainees and actively participate in detainee training activities (apprenticeships)
  • Independently execute production cycles according to daily planning
  • Ensure hygiene and food cleanliness standards according to HACCP company levels and complete required training to acquire relevant safety skills
  • Participate in organizing internal events and in menu preparation and planning
  • Guarantee the proper functioning of the laboratory from an economic perspective and be willing to attend professional development courses
  • Maintain order and safety in the laboratory, collaborating with security personnel
  • Contribute to the overall smooth operation of the prison facilities and undertake additional tasks as directed by management ## Requirements
  • General requirements:
  • Swiss citizenship
  • Minimum age: 35 years; candidates born in 1991 or earlier are eligible
  • Federal Certificate of Competence (AFC) as a cook
  • Additional certifications or diplomas in relevant fields and dietetics may be considered a plus
  • Several years of proven professional experience
  • Trainer/mentor certification for apprentices (training master), or willingness to obtain it
  • Experience in training apprentices is a plus
  • Possession of category B driving license (manual transmission)
  • Strong motivation for the work
  • Physical and psychological requirements:
  • Good overall health, particularly absence of conditions incompatible with the role of Head Chef
  • Good physical and stress resilience
  • Psychological suitability for the role
  • Flexibility to work split shifts, including weekends and holidays
  • Skills:
  • Proficiency in spoken and written Italian and good knowledge of at least one other official language
  • Adequate computer skills and proficiency in Microsoft Office
  • Leadership qualities, ability to work independently and in teams
  • Sense of responsibility, systematic approach, initiative, and strong organizational skills
  • Interpersonal skills
  • Reliability, accuracy, impartiality, and discretion
  • Ability to handle conflict and stressful situations; problem-solving skills
  • Conduct and background:
  • Police background check confirming no elements or past convictions incompatible with the Head Chef role, including severity or nature of offenses and recidivism
  • Good reputation; no conduct incompatible with the role such as dishonorable behavior, violation of societal ethical values, or conflict-prone traits
  • No ongoing legal proceedings or financial insolvency certificates ## Special Notes
  • Service location: Lugano-Cadro
  • Include a copy of your driving license (front/back) with your application ## Application Submission Conditions
  • Applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application will not be accepted.
  • Along with the online application (cover letter, motivation, CV, diplomas, certificates, and work attestations), additional documents may be requested later (criminal record with self-certification and health status self-declaration) ## Employment Conditions
  • Appointment or assignment: as per the Law on the Organization of State Employees and Teachers of March 15, 1995 (LORD) and related regulations. Any legal modifications regarding salary and organization are explicitly reserved.
  • Working hours: 42 hours per week (for full-time employment), flexible or according to service needs ## Deadline April 27, 2026 ## Contact Further information can be obtained from Mr. Enrico Ghilardi, Prison Facilities Management Officer, tel. 091 815 00 11
