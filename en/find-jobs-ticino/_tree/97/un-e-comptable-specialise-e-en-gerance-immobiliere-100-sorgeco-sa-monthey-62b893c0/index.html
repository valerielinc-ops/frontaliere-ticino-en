Full description

Your missions

  • Maintenance of the accounts of buildings in management (rentals and EPP) until closure
  • Preparation of heating and ancillary costs counts and PPE expense counts
  • Preparation of budgets and annual accounts of condominiums, and presentation at meetings if necessary
  • Monitoring of rent receipts, reminders and litigation management in collaboration with managers
  • Control and payment of supplier invoices and works
  • Reporting to owners and bank reconciliations
  • Interlocutor for managers, owners, PPE administrators and trustees
  • Your profile
  • SWC or equivalent training, supplemented by a federal certificate of specialist in finance and accounting or ongoing training
  • Confirmed experience in real estate accounting within a governance or stewardship (indispensable)
  • Good knowledge of the law of the lease and the functioning of EPPs
  • Mastery of management software (Quorum) and Excel
  • Rigor, autonomy, sense of priorities and relational ease
  • French mother tongue
  • German is an asset
  • You are offered
  • A stable and varied position in a human-sized team

Only applications corresponding to the profile sought will be processed.
