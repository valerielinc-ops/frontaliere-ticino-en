Full description

Are you a negotiation talent, trend scout, and organizational talent all in one? Then take on a key role in our procurement of raw and auxiliary materials for the food sector. With your feel for markets, your strategic foresight, and your confident demeanor, you will shape processes, optimize conditions, and build strong supplier relationships. Become part of a motivated team that values quality, innovation, and efficiency and bring your ideas directly to the table! Sounds exciting? We look forward to getting to know you. ## Responsibilities

  • Purchasing raw and auxiliary materials in the food sector in compliance with the procurement strategy
  • Creating budgets and annual planning as well as ensuring target achievement
  • Conducting continuous market monitoring of trends, innovations, prices, and supplier offers
  • Obtaining and comparing quotes, conducting price negotiations, and creating framework agreements in coordination with disposition
  • Performing supplier management including selection, support, evaluation, specifications, and compliance certificates
  • Maintaining material master data and ensuring correct purchase prices
  • Close collaboration with development, quality management, and other internal interfaces ## Requirements
  • Completed basic or advanced training or several years of professional experience in procurement/SCM, ideally in the food industry or industrial sector
  • High reliability, accuracy, and independent working style
  • Strong strategic thinking and action skills
  • Polished manners and confident demeanor in supplier and colleague contacts, even in stressful situations
  • High self-initiative, solution orientation, and flexibility as well as willingness to learn in the face of changes
  • Experience in handling SAP, SharePoint, Teams, and very good MS Office skills
  • Fluent German language skills in speaking and writing, English and French skills are an advantage
