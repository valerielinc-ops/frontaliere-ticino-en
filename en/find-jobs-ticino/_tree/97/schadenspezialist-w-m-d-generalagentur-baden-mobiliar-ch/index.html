Full description

This is what awaits you

Are you full of passion for your job and appreciate the open exchange in the team? As a damage specialist: you accompany our customers through challenging damage cases – proactively and uncomplicated. In this way, you strengthen confidence in the furniture as the most personal insurance company in Switzerland.

This is what you do with us

The core of your job is the comprehensive support of our customers in the event of damage. You handle them from the first claim notification to the successful completion and ensure a smooth process throughout the entire claim processing.

You work closely with your team and everyone involved to find suitable solutions. With your willingness to provide services and your empathy, you provide our customers with security even in challenging situations.

In addition, you carry out damage inspections on site. This allows you to assess situations directly, personally accompany our customers and develop individual solutions.

This is what you bring with you

  • Ideally, several years of experience in claims processing (mainly property, liability and motor vehicle damage)
  • Enjoy contact and support of our customers in challenging situations
  • High motivation to learn new things in a varied field of activity and to take on responsibility
  • Proactive and independent working methods
  • Responsible, empathetic and open personality
  • Secure handling of office products and digital affinity
  • fluent knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Other languages are beneficial.

Working with us

A motivated and dynamic team awaits you at the Generalagentur Baden. Together you live an open, collegial and service-oriented working atmosphere.

Your workload is negotiable from 80% and the job starts immediately or by agreement. Your place of work is bathing, home office is also possible.

Look forward to good working conditions, further education opportunities and a modern working environment.
