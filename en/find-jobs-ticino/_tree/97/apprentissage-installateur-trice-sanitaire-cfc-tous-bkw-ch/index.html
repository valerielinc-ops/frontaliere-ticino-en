Who are we?

Michel Rime SA, founded in 1898, is the company specializing in sanitary facilities, photovoltaics, heating systems and pipe laying in the Gros-de-Vaud region. Whether it's for new construction, transformation or renovation, we put our skills at the service of our customers, from consulting and planning to maintenance to realization. As an innovative and dynamic family-owned company, we guarantee our customers top-notch consulting and installation skills, as well as the implementation of modern and environmentally friendly technologies. Within the BKW Group, we are cultivating an inclusive welcome culture, open to all.
