Full description

Mission Join a transversal, Group-level initiative to actively participate in, and drive for Group Risk Management as 2nd line of defense, the implementation of a Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) tool sponsored by the Group Head of Risk Management. As a member of the business-side Project Management team under PMO’s leadership, you will act as a key actor of the deployment phase, in close partnership with the Business Programme Management Office, Group Risk Management, local Risk Representatives, Business stakeholders, and IT Departments. You will support end-to-end delivery — from preparation and rollout to post-implementation — ensuring data readiness, user adoption, quality, relationship with the provider and support tool’s governance, reporting to Group Risk Management and you will be located in Geneva.

Main responsibilities As a member of the project team, your responsibilities will include:

  • Act as the central point of contact for data mapping, data loading, and data quality reviews, coordinating with IT, Data Owners, and project stakeholders.
  • Contribute to pre-requisites including specifications, access management definition, implementation, and deployment.
  • Accompany deployment activities and support GRC local champions and end-users across locations.
  • Contribute to the design, preparation, and delivery of training sessions for all end-user groups; maintain training materials and learning paths.
  • Collect, centralize, and prioritize user feedback; propose and contribute to response plans and continuous improvements, and provide reporting on the evolution of the deployment phases.
  • Prepare and maintain project documentation and operating procedures in line with Group’s standards.
  • Monitor the quality of the end-user support via AI Assistant, in coordination with the provider or suggest mitigation measures.
  • Contribute to the governance model for GRC tool maintenance in partnership with the provider and IT, aligning with Bank’s methodology.
  • Define adoption and usage KPIs; design and deploy controls to ensure proper and compliant use of the tool.
  • Execute post-implementation actions and monitor remediation progress.
  • Build and animate a GRC Tool Community of Practice with 1st and 2nd lines Representatives across legal entities and divisions.
  • Prepare recurring project and post-implementation reports; present updates to stakeholders and to project’s governance.

Your Profile

  • Practical experience implementing GRC tools or a tool with a similar control and reporting feature, with emphasis on Internal Control System functionalities; experience on the business side as Project Manager or project contributor.
  • Background in Risk Management, Internal Control, Audit, IT, and/or Compliance is a strong asset.
  • 5+ years of relevant experience.
  • Strong analytical skills and meticulous attention to detail.
  • Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage deadlines and multiple workstreams.
  • Clear, adaptable communication skills; able to work across diverse environments and cultures.
  • Solid understanding of business processes and financial sector regulations
  • Private Banking experience preferred.
  • Solution-oriented mindset; comfortable driving proof-of-concepts and iterating quickly.
  • Ability to work independently while fostering strong team cohesion and communication.
  • Demonstrated change management capability and stakeholder engagement.
  • Excellent command of French and English ; knowledge of German would be a plus.

Others Swiss Residence
