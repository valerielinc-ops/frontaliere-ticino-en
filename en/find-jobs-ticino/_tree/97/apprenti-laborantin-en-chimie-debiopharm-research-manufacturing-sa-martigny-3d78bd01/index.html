[Debiopharm Research & Manufacturing SA]( )

Debiopharm is an independent biopharmaceutical company based in Switzerland. Debiopharm is primarily active in oncology and infectious diseases and aims to improve patient outcomes and quality of life. Debiopharm Research \& Manufacturing SA, based in Martigny, is a pharmaceutical research, development and production company with GMP compliant facilities and certified by major regulatory authorities. As the group's centre of expertise in the field of therapeutic chemistry, the company is world leader in injectable products based on polylactic acid\-coglycolic (PLGA).

The chemistry field is interested? You finished your compulsory education? You're motivated by this learning and you have a strong desire to learn?

Then join Debiopharm Research \& Manufacturing \à Martigny as:

Apprentice Laborantin in Chemistry

We offer

  • Comprehensive and practical training in different laboratories, including analytical chemistry, synthesis and bioanalysis.
  • The chance to work on a variety of projects, ranging from innovative research to GMP laboratory experiments.
  • The opportunity to handle chemical and biological samples, to learn how to analyze data and interpret results, while following training on essential hygiene and safety rules in the laboratory.
  • An environment where you can learn with passionate specialists and participate in concrete projects alongside motivated teams.

What we are looking for

  • You completed your compulsory education with good results.
  • You like science and mathematics.
  • You have an appeal for laboratory work.
  • You are rigorous and thorough and have a good sense of organization, discipline and rigour.
  • You like working with a team.
  • You are curious and motivated to discover new techniques and work with molecules used in medicines.

Information Additional

  • Duration: 3 years in collaboration with the Intercantonal School of Chemistry (EPIC) in Monthey.
  • Semesters 1 to 3: you will be full time at EPIC, with 2 days of theory and 3 days of practice.
  • Semesters 4 to 6: You will work in company and take 1 day of lessons a week at the EPIC.

Have we awakened your interest?

We look forward to receiving your application!

  • Entry into office: 1 August 2027
  • A cover letter with your Curriculum Vitae with a copy of the school bulletins of the last years of study.
  • Certificates of internship, diplomas or certificates if you have one.
  • Deadline for application: 15 November 2026\.
  • A course will be organized for successful candidates.
  • The practical and theoretical tests organised by EPIC will have to be carried out.
