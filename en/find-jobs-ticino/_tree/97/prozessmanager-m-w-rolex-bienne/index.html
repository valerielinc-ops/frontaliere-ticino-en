Full description

Introduction

For our industry Industrialization & Production Drafts, we are looking for a Process Manager (M/F) whose main mission will be to intervene from the moment of product design to ensure compliance with the recommendations, define and update the Product-Process standards, pilot the life cycle of the products up to their industrial qualification, process the requests for modification (DMPP), and carry the continuous improvement of the Processes in connection with Production and Industrialization.

Responsibilities

  • Intervene from the product definition phase during the co-design to ensure compliance with recommendations and identify potential deviations and associated impacts
  • Define and update Product-Process recommendations to ensure a homogeneous product between different sizes
  • Managing the whole life cycle of products, from the development of new products to the end of their life cycle, notably by validating the series and industrial qualification
  • Analysing, validating, prioritizing and piloting the follow-up of Product Modification Requests (PDMD)
  • Propose and pilot actions for continuous improvement of the Production Process, in collaboration with Production and Industrialization

Profile

  • Diploma of engineer HES, or equivalent in mechanics or microtechnics
  • Knowledge of industrialization, project management and associated tools
  • Control of production processes (watchmaker)
  • Technical knowledge of watchmaking
  • Risk management
  • Mastery of common office tools
  • Human contact and communication
  • Rigourous, organized and autonomous
  • Spirit of analysis, synthesis and solution orientation
  • Ability to federate the resources involved
  • Customer orientation

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
