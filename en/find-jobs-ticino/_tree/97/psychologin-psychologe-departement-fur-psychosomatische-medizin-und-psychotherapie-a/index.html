  • You are interested in interdisciplinary exchange and are actively involved in further education and training.

  • your very good knowledge of German in word and writing. Barmelweid is the leading specialist and rehabilitation clinic in northwestern Switzerland and the largest psychosomatic-psychotherapeutic clinic in Switzerland. The Department of Psychosomatic Medicine and Psychotherapy has 118 beds. Two psychotherapeutic day clinics and two ambulatories in Aarau and Frick are also on offer. The department defines itself in its self-image as a psychotherapeutically based and somatically integrating clinic institution. Close cooperation with somatic disciplines of the Clinic Barmelweid is established and thus makes it possible to treat people with multimorbid somatic diseases as well as psychopathological disorders. Barmelweid offers over 750 employees an attractive, interdisciplinary working environment in a family atmosphere. This includes advanced employment conditions, advantageous social benefits, a wide range of leisure activities and an in-house day care centre.
