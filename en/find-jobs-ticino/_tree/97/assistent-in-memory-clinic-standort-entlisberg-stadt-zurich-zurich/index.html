Full description

Are you interested in administratively supporting investigations and consultations for people with dementia in our specialized clinic? You want to start from the 1. January 2027 or by agreement with our team in the Memory Clinic to use for the health centers for old age?

  • You manage the administrative part of our Memory Clinic independently and ensure a smooth process.
  • They take over all assistance tasks, process the triage of incoming requests, order patients and carry out the billing in the Tardoc system.
  • They maintain correspondence with various contacts.
  • As a first point of contact, they are the “good soul” of our Memory Clinic and are sensitive to the concerns of patients and their relatives.
  • You have a basic commercial education
  • Experience or training in the medical field, for example as an MPA, physician secretary or nurse, is an advantage
  • They ideally have several years of experience with people with dementia and their relatives and are interested in supporting people with cognitive limitations.
  • You have a structured way of working and have organizational talent

A family-friendly position with flexible working hours in a competent, collegial and helpful team.

They experience a high level of expertise from long-term employees in the Memory Clinic team. Look forward to an exciting area of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits. Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between CHF 77’272.– and CHF 103’252.– (100%).

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The Memory Clinic, as a specialized clinic for age medicine, provides clarification and advice for people with a memory disorder or suspected dementia.
