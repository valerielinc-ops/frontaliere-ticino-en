Full description

What awaits

In this position, you are part of the Private Planning contract management team in Nyon, which is responsible for both the process management and the processing of insurance contracts, for our clients throughout Switzerland.

What you can do with us

Your main mission is to assess, process and coordinate clients' requests for 3rd pillar life insurance contracts, in French and German, in collaboration with various interlocutors, such as our general agencies. You also support managers in processing complex files.

In addition, you identify and implement process optimizations within the team or service, working with other specialists. You ensure the quality of the services provided by the team and participate in the training of your colleagues. You contribute to the achievement of service objectives through working groups or inter-service projects.

Thanks to your open and solutions oriented personality, you play a key role in strengthening our image "Dear Mobilier", placing the customer at the centre of your concerns.

What you bring

  • Federal patent for private, professional or social insurance specialists or equivalent training
  • Some years of experience in the field of private insurance, occupational insurance or social insurance
  • Project experience is an asset
  • Knowledge in the field of Compliance is an asset
  • Interest and curiosity for topics related to foresight as well as for continuous optimization
  • Dynamic, autonomous and customer-oriented personality with an excellent sense of collaboration
  • French and/or German mother tongue with excellent knowledge in the other language (min. C1)

Working with us

The Contract Management department of the Operations Department consists of three teams including motivated managers and specialists who look forward to your participation. They work together in a pleasant atmosphere and with a strong team spirit.

What we offer

Balance of working life - privacy

We apply flexible work models and allow 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. We also offer above-average family allowances and a sustainable pension fund.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of in-house courses and allow you to participate in possible programs abroad. We contribute generously to external continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, half-rate subscription, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.
