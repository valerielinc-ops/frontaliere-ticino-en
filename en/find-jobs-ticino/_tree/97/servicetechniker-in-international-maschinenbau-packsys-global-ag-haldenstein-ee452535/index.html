Service technician in International \- Mechanical engineering

  • Your future is our future – as part of PackSys Global

As a Swiss company, PackSys Global is a global leader in the technology for the production of innovative packaging solutions and acts as a business unit of the Group. With unmistakable “Swissness”, we manufacture unique machines that set the highest standards worldwide.

Working for us means being involved in a working environment where growth and responsibility are encouraged, innovation is a daily pursuit and team spirit is a matter of course – whether from our locations in Rüti, Burgdorf or Haldenstein, Switzerland, or on site around the world.

Your tasks

  • At our plant in Haldenstein, you will take over the electromechanical structure and the precise electrical wiring of our high-tech systems.
  • You install and maintain our machines professionally with technical know-how and an eye for detail
  • In close cooperation with our customers, you will accompany test runs and factory acceptances
  • Variety guaranteed: You are on the road worldwide, put our facilities into operation at the customer's premises and accompany them until final acceptance on site
  • So that everything runs smoothly, you train the customer staff and make them fit in handling our facilities
  • Travel is part of this for you – you are working a total of around 50% of your working time abroad, each in assignments of 1 to a maximum of 5 weeks at a time.

Your profile

  • You have completed mechanical training – e.g. as a polymechanic – or a comparable qualification
  • Ideally, you will bring electrotechnical knowledge and thus complement your technical profile
  • You think analytically, act goal-oriented and find suitable solutions for complex tasks
  • Your very good English skills in words and writing enable you to communicate securely in an international environment – other languages are a welcome plus
  • You distinguish yourself through customer orientation, sense of responsibility and organizational skill
  • You see international travel as a welcome change and exciting challenge

Our offer to you

  • International travel activities (about 50%) to our customers worldwide
  • High scope for action and design with attractive employment conditions
  • Responsible and varied tasks in a small, dynamic team

Your contact person

Azghandi is looking forward to your application!

As part of a strong, globally active group of companies in machine and plant engineering for plastics processing, we offer our employees the opportunity to develop in a diverse and supportive environment and to jointly pursue innovative paths.

The group brings together people from different cultural backgrounds, skills and experiences. We cultivate a corporate culture that is characterized by integrity, tolerance and mutual respect and are happy if you also bring your individual perspective in the future.
