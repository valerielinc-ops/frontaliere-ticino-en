Full description

As part of our Risk & Regulatory Community, you will immerse yourself in the world of regulatory audits and advisory mandates with financial institutions after your studies. You rotate between our teams – Financial Risk Management, Compliance & Regulation, Risk Consulting and Cyber. The rotation is planned individually, so that you can get to know the entire range of audits and advice and set specific priorities. The program is designed for about 3 to 4 years: At the beginning, the focus is deliberately primarily on regulatory testing to strengthen your foundation and expand your skill set. To do this, you start training to/to dipl. Accountant:in which we support. ## Mansioni

  • Regulatory Audits with Impact: Part of a high-performing specialist team and examine what really matters for our customers. The focus is on regulatory audits – primarily for financial institutions (in particular banks) – in areas such as money laundering and terrorism, suitability, own resources, liquidity, operational and cyber risks, as well as other issues of financial market law. On site with our customers: you can get a clear picture and set the basis for sound decisions.
  • End-to-end audit responsibility: Planning regulatory audits. Conducting interviews and process talkthroughs and assessing the internal documentation of our customers. Identified gaps or potential for improvement are summarized in reports and discuss them with management. From the start, you are a full member of the test team and in direct exchange with decision-makers.
  • Assessment of frameworks: Assessment of governance, risk and compliance frameworks in the light of regulatory requirements, assesses control design and effectiveness and demonstrates concrete action needs. This allows you to ensure that financial institutions are robust, consistent and sustainable.
  • In-depth analyses: You analyze, among other things, KYC processes, risk classifications, risk measurement and monitoring, transaction monitoring mechanisms and organisational risk mitigation measures – including issues at the interface to cyber and operational risks. Your analyses form the basis for concrete recommendations and optimizations.
  • From the audit to the consulting: depending on your development and performance, you will gradually be involved in other projects where you use your built-in knowledge in consulting-related mandates – e.g. in the further development of frameworks, the implementation of regulatory requirements or the optimization of processes and controls. ## Requisiti
  • Strong interest in Risk & Regulatory: Joy in understanding complex issues in the financial sector, recognizing relationships and developing smart, practical solutions.
  • High Performance & Commitment: You're counting on the high-performance graduates: in internships, secondary activities or projects, you've shown what's in you. You are ready to bring a high commitment to your professional and personal development.
  • Analytical & technical background: Adapt to numbers, models and complex information. You think structured and meet sound decisions. You have a high school degree (usually on a Master's level) in economics or legal sciences, finance, an STEM/STEM field or a comparable, analytically shaped study course.
  • Teampirit: If you like to share your knowledge, learn from your team and contribute to an open, trusting cooperation – also in international and interdisciplinary constellations.
  • Flexibility & Drive: Feel comfortable in a dynamic environment, go actively to customers, take responsibility and stay focused in intensive phases.
  • Care & Quality Consciousness: You have an eye for detail, work reliably and structured and place value on high quality – in analyses, documentation and communication.
  • Languages: German language skills are required. You also have very good English skills
  • Other languages are a plus. ## Cosa offriamo
  • Training funding: We support you financially and temporally in the training to/from the dipl. Auditor.
  • Practical learning: Collect valuable insights into the audit and build your network at PwC step by step.
  • Mentoring and support: Experienced colleagues will help you with advice and action and help you develop.
  • Diversity and culture: We live diversity, appreciate different perspectives and promote your personal growth. ## Contatto Make the next step! Come to one of our exclusive Recruiting Days on June 4 or 5 at our office in Zurich. On these days, you will expect:
  • Presentations of our Risk & Regulatory Partner/Director with exciting insights into current topics.
  • Practice reports from our associates who share their experiences from the Risk & Regulatory world with you.
  • Case study where you can actively cooperate and get to know your future work area.
  • HR interview.
  • Networking with team members on different career levels – experience the spirit and culture of our teams close!
