23.10.2026 Wattwil

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Mittleres Toggenburg is looking for a strong personality for SMEs and corporate client consulting. You actively participate, make decisions in short ways and work closely with the bank management.

What awaits you?

  • Support and targeted further development of an established corporate client portfolio
  • Comprehensive advice to your customers on financing issues, succession planning and on investing and paying
  • Active market development and representation of the bank in the region
  • Strategic and operational development of your department
  • Close cooperation with the bank management and active participation in the corporate client strategy
  • Acting responsibly within the framework of your competences with short decision-making paths

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking education with further training in banking or finance
  • Sound and practical know-how in the lending business
  • Several years of experience in corporate banking with focus on financing
  • Safe, entrepreneurial thinking and joy of responsibility
  • Delight in daily customer contact and a convincing appearance
  • Motivation to make a difference and actively develop your own area

“Short decision-making paths, great freedom of design: You bring your ideas and implement them directly.” That's what makes the difference. Urban Koller Chairman of the Bank Management Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We live diversity and equal opportunities

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.
