Full description

Within the HFR Tafers, a community hospital based in its region, you operate in a human-sized environment, fostering collaboration and proximity with patients. The Directorate of Research Care, for the internal medicine department of the HFR Tafers, consists of two units of 24 and 30 beds for a German-speaking and French-speaking patientel

  • providing quality care in partnership with the patient and the patient's environment, in accordance with the current care philosophy and with a view to continuous improvement
  • promote and actively participate in interdisciplinary collaboration, both at team and hospital level
  • evaluate your services and adapt them with a view to continuous quality improvement
  • contribute to the development of the service, while ensuring hours of 8h24
  • mentor, support and advise new employees and learners
  • degree in general care, ES or HES level, or equivalent training
  • professional experience in acute care is an asset
  • Mastery of German (current, min. B2) or willingness to learn, knowledge in French is an advantage
  • mastery of common computer tools
