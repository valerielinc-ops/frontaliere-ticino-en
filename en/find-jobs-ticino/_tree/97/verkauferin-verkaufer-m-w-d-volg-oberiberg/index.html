About us

Volg is the specialist for village shops and small-format stores in German-speaking and French-speaking Switzerland. We stand for being close to our customers – both as a convenient local shopping option and through personal contact. Employees are the heart of the store, and we attach great importance to individuality and personality. That is why our motto is "fresh and friendly".
