The Position Application Specialist Customer Support – São Paulo, Brazil We are looking for an experienced Application Specialist to provide high-level, cross-border technical support to our customer laboratories across Latin America. This position is for 100% on-site work in São Paulo (SP).

About the opportunity

  • Handling of customer and staff inquiries by phone and in written form (70%): Provide first-level technical support to Latin America affiliate customers (inquiries and complaints), resolving concerns efficiently. Who you are

  • Education & Professional Experience: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Biology, Microbiology, Health Sciences, or equivalent Applications degree. Consolidated experience as an Application Specialist with solid expertise in Serum Work Area (SWA) within the medical/diagnostic market.
