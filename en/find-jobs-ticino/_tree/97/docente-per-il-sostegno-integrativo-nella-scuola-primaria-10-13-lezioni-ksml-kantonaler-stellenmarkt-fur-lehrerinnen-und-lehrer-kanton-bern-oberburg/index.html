Full description

Portrait At the Oberburg School, around 350 children and adolescents are taught in 17 classes: 3 kindergarten classes, 10 primary classes and 5 classes of Sek 1. There are three parallel classes with two years: 1./2. cl.; 3./4. cl., 5./6. cl., 7./8. cl. The two 9th classes are grades. At our school, all children and young people are taught integratively. The lessons of the MR pool are distributed among all classes and the teachers work closely together.

  • Good cooperation forms the basis for teaching and learning.
  • The Oberburg School is an innovative school where traditions are maintained and new ideas are always welcome.
  • Our values are: community, trust, respect, responsibility, transparency, tradition and further development.

Tasks

In close cooperation with the standard teachers, you promote the SuS of the 3rd / 4th classes needs-oriented. You will take on six lessons for a bVSA student and support further SuS in both classes.

Requirements

Healing education or training, level diploma, patent primary teacher.

We offer

  • Dedicated team that supports each other and works closely together.
  • Integrative work in class with the possibility to use group rooms for group or individual settings.
  • Workplace in the IF office.
  • Long-standing tradition in integration.
  • Diverse and extensive materials.

Employment limited until July 2027, continuation possible.
