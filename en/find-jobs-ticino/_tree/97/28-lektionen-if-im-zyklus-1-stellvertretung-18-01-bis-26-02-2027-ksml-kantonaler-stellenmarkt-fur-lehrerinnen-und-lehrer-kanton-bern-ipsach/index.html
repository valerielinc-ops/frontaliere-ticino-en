Full description

Portrait The school Ipsach includes kindergarten, primary level and day school in one location. We look after and teach around 300 children and work together in a team of 40 teachers. Our municipality Ipsach has 4'000 inhabitants, has a high quality of living and has a beautiful local recreation area on the lake.

Tasks

You accompany students with special needs in the four classes in the kindergarten and in the three classes in the lower school. You support and advise the teachers and parents. With the class teachers you are in close exchange and offer individual and group support. Together with the curative pedagogue in cycle 2, you will perform the break kiosk. Are you looking for a professional curative education team at an integrative school? Then you are exactly right with us and we look forward to your application.

Requirements

Healing education or teacher training

We offer

  • Competent and supportive team consisting of two trained curative pedagogues, two curative pedagogues in training and a DaZ teacher
  • Top equipped IF room and IF office
  • Committed and helpful college
  • Principal with a curative background
