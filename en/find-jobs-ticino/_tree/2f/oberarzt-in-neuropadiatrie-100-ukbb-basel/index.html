Full description

The University Neuropediatrics, Developmental Pediatrics and Neurorehabilitation Northwest Switzerland is a cross-location university center of the University Children's Hospital of Basel (UKBB) and the Children's Hospital Aarau (KSA). We look after children and adolescents with diseases of the central and peripheral nervous system as well as developmental disorders at the highest professional level. In addition to a broad general neuropaediatric care, we offer numerous special consultation hours as well as interdisciplinary and highly specialized care services. In order to strengthen our team at the Basel location, we are looking for a committed and team-oriented personality as a senior physician/superior neuropediatrician.

Entry date: as of January 1, 2027 or by arrangement

Their tasks

  • Outpatient and inpatient care of children and adolescents with neurological and developmental neurological diseases
  • Performing general neuropediatric consultation hours
  • Participation in specialized neuropaediatric consultation hours according to individual interests and focuses
  • Consultative activities within the UKBB and in cooperation with other disciplines
  • Participation in the further education and supervision of assistant doctors
  • Active participation in student teaching as well as in further and further training
  • Participation in the UKBB neuropaediatric background service and paediatric services

Your profile

  • Specialist title Paediatrics and Adolescent Medicine with focus title Neuropaediatrics or shortly before its acquisition
  • Joy in caring for children, adolescents and their families
  • Teamwork and interest in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Self-employed, responsible and structured working methods
  • Engagement in teaching and further education
  • Interest in scientific activity and research beneficial, but not a requirement

Our offer

  • A varied clinical activity in a university environment
  • Collaboration in a dynamic and committed team with a broad range of expertise
  • Opportunity to co-create and further develop specialized clinical offerings
  • Attractive opportunities for academic development, teaching and research
  • Structured promotion of individual professional development
  • Modern infrastructure and attractive employment conditions of a university children's hospital
  • Close cooperation with Aarau Children’s Hospital, Basel University Hospitals and national and international partners
