Event Manager TCC Regulation Apply » Date: 13 Nov 2026

Place

Renens, CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. At Traffic Control Center (TCC), you are at the heart of rail operations. You ensure the operational conduct of the regulation of resources and events for passenger traffic on the western network of Switzerland. In a dynamic and demanding environment, you make decisive decisions to ensure continuity and quality of operation. In normal situations as in the event of disruption, you quickly analyze events, define priorities and coordinate the actors involved in order to maintain or restore reliable operation and quality customer information. What you can move.

  • You answer on behalf of the Travellers Division, operational operations in your work area, and you take responsibility for regulating resources, managing disruptions and customer information in case of disruption.
  • You ensure compliance with the quality guidelines (punctuality, timely decisions and client requirements, information in case of disruption) in your work area.
  • You ensure the operational conduct of passenger regulation in its area of responsibility and coordinate the collaboration in case of disruption with SBB Infrastructure within the regulation of the Voyageurs division.
  • You manage the disruption management of SBB Voyageurs and replace the SBB Voyageurs control room chief during his absence.

What you bring.

  • You have a recognized vocational training (CFC or equivalent) .
  • You have very good knowledge of the operational processes of railway production, very good knowledge of the schedule structure and the public transport network.
  • You are able to quickly analyze complex situations and make reliable decisions under pressure.
  • You have a driving experience or ongoing training in the conduct of staff.
  • You combine resilience, spirit of inter-service collaboration, flexibility to work in irregular hours (24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year) and a mastery of French or German with knowledge of the other language (minimum level B1).

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
