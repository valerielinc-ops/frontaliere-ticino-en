# Logistician:in EFZ Lager, Bellinzona #

In your profession as a logistician: you take goods into stock, manage them and send them on the journey to the recipient. You always keep an overview of the stock.

Important ##

Minimum age at start of training: 15 years

Duration

3 years

Summer 2027

Language

Place of teaching

Bellinzona

What makes a:e logistics operator:in EFZ Lager? ##STR1##

Accepting goods ###

  • You register and control goods and goods that arrive at the warehouse
  • Storage of goods with lift truck
  • Recording products in the computer

Observing material stocks ###

  • Maintain statistics on material stocks
  • If necessary, make orders

Warehouses & Equipment Operation ###

  • Make sure the goods are stored correctly.
  • You have an overview of the stocks and can therefore plan well ahead.
  • You also operate the truck.

Shipping of goods ###

  • Careful preparation when shipping goods
  • From packaging to delivery papers to addressing, everything goes over your table
