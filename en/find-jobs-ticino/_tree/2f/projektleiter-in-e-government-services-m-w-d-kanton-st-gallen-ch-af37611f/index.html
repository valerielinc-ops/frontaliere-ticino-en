This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We lead St.Gallen into the digital future. A digital administration is only really digital if it works across organizations. With our STREBAS platform, we are building the basis for citizens to understand their concerns once and for administrative units to work together across municipal, city and cantonal levels. For this purpose, we develop strategic e-government basic services and combine specialist processes, registers and identities into end-to-end services.

What to expect

  • You lead challenging IT projects according to HERMES over all project phases – from initialization to successful completion
  • You control deadlines, costs, quality and risks and ensure targeted project implementation
  • You lead interdisciplinary project teams and coordinate internal and external stakeholders
  • You develop specialist and implementation concepts and bring architecture, operation and security aspects together
  • You are responsible for public procurement and cooperation with external service providers
  • You ensure the quality of the project results and accompany tests, acceptances and the transfer to the company
  • You develop project management standards and promote knowledge transfer within the organization

What distinguishes you

  • University degree (master or equivalent) in computer science, business informatics, systems engineering or a related field
  • Several years of experience managing complex IT projects or programs, ideally in a public or regulated environment
  • In-depth knowledge of classical, agile or hybrid project management methods as well as governance and controlling
  • Experience in conducting tenders and ideally in public procurement (BöB / IVöB)
  • Strong organisational and analytical skills as well as a structured and solution-oriented working style
  • High competence in stakeholder management as well as a secure appearance towards specialist bodies, committees and external partners
  • Convincing communication, decision-making and leadership skills as well as enjoying actively shaping change

What we offer

  • Exciting and socially relevant digitalization projects with great impact for the population, economy and administration
  • A committed, interdisciplinary team with high personal responsibility and short decision-making paths
  • Flexible forms of work with attractive opportunities for home office and modern workplaces
  • Individual education and training opportunities as well as space for professional and personal development
  • Contemporary employment conditions and the opportunity to actively shape the digital administration of the canton
