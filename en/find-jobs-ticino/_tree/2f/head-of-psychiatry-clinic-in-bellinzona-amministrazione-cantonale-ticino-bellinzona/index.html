Full description

The Medical Assessment Service (SAM) is a specialized center for the execution of multi-disciplinary, bi- and mono-disciplinary medical assessments, working in accordance with the best medical knowledge and insurance medicine, and responsible for the assessment of complex clinical profiles on mandate from the Invalidity Insurance and various insurance and/or legal institutions (courts and tribunals). The psychiatrist will make available to the Medical Assessment Service (SAM) the specific specialist competences that guarantee the elaboration of the part of their competence of the assessments requested by the Invalidity Insurance, as well as by various insurance and legal institutions (courts and tribunals), for the assessment of complex clinical profiles. Consultations will be carried out in accordance with the principles related to the quality of the assessment. Collaborates actively with other SAM specialists and any partners involved in the territory. Ensures the picket on the territory as provided for in the "Regulation of guards and medical pickets OSC". ## Requirements

  • Medical degree (or equivalent title)
  • Specialist title FMH in psychiatry and psychotherapy (or equivalent title)
  • SIM (Swiss Insurance Medicine) expert title
  • Authorization to practice medicine in the Canton of Ticino, issued by the Health Office
  • Ability to work in a team
  • Knowledge of the territory and the sociopsychiatric assistance network of the Canton of Ticino
  • Perfect command of the Italian language and knowledge of another official language ## Special Observations
  • Employment relationship: appointment (art. 15 and 16 LORD), temporarily authorized until December 31, 2027
  • Part-time employment (60%)
  • Place of service: Bellinzona
  • Availability of the candidate to work irregular shifts
  • Start date: to be agreed
  • The candidate who has not yet obtained the authorization to practice medicine in the Canton of Ticino, the language knowledge certificate (Italian-B2) and, in the case of foreign titles, the recognition issued by the Mebeko Commission of Medical Professions, must obtain all the requirements by the start of activity as established during the selection process
  • Failure to obtain the requirements for the appointment will result in automatic termination
