Full description

  • In procurement projects, take responsibility for the timely logistical introduction and correct handling of BODLUV and aviation systems in the army logistics centers

  • Participate in international/national meetings with the contracting party/OEM and carry out domestic missions to the army logistics centres
  • Completed basic technical training (EFZ) with a further higher education (engineer/in FH, technician/in HF) in the technical field with practical professional experience
  • Experience in supply chain management and ILS as well as technical product support of BODLUV / anti-aircraft / aviation systems or similar complex systems
  • Ability to recognize complex relationships, to develop and implement solutions, structured and team-oriented working methods and high self- and social competence
  • In-depth knowledge of SAP and in dealing with MS Office applications.
  • Willingness to travel both nationally and internationally
  • Good oral and written knowledge of a second official language as well as knowledge of English (min. B2)
