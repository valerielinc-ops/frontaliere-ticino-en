Full description

COMPETITION TO RECRUIT AN ARCHITECT OR ENGINEER FOR THE MUNICIPAL TECHNICAL OFFICE (PRIVATE BUILDING SECTOR) The Municipality of Minusio opens the competition for the permanent hiring of an architect or engineer with an ETH/EPF or SUP diploma for the municipal technical office (part-time, between 50% and 80%) for the private building sector, under the conditions of Organic regulation of employees (ROD) approved on 11.05.2026 by the City Council and whose entry into force is scheduled for 01.01.2027.

  • Basic requirements > Diploma (Bachelor and/or Master) as an architect or engineer ETH/EPF or SUP or equivalent qualification > Swiss citizenship or a country signatory to a bilateral agreement, with "C" permit (domicile) > Healthy physical constitution > Clean conduct > Ability to carry out work suited to the function > Work experience in the construction or public technical/administrative field and on issues related to territorial planning > Ability to manage paperwork construction and complete knowledge of architecture and project evaluation > Knowledge of the theme of sustainability in the built environment and of the theme of the "Sponge City" > Ability to operate independently, sense of responsibility and strong sense of initiative > Strong organizational and coordination skills > Proactive and proactive person, with a predisposition for group work oriented towards solving problems > Availability for continuous updating > Availability for services outside normal working hours > Ease of contact with professionals and with users > Good knowledge of IT tools for technical office activities (Word, Excel, Autocad, etc.) > Good editorial skills (minutes, reports, technical reports, etc.) > Good knowledge of national languages, in particular the German language
  • Preferential requirements > SUPSI municipal technician diploma or willingness to obtain it within the deadlines set by the Municipality > Proven experience in the public sector > Proven work, organisational, managerial experience > Certificates / diplomas of post-training courses > Knowledge of the municipal territory > Municipality domicile or availability to transfer within terms to be defined > Type B driving license Hierarchical position The architect / engineer reports to the manager of the private construction sector.

Description of the function The description of the function can be downloaded from the website www.minusio.ch / Register / Competitions or can be requested from the municipal chancellery (cancelleria@minusio.ch / 091 735 81 81) Entry into service To be agreed as soon as possible; the first year of employment is considered a probationary period. Salary (gross annual, including the 13th month) The initial salary will be established on the basis of the new ROD and the new Municipal Ordinance on the classification of functions (under discussion with the Personnel Commission and the Unions, but which is expected to come into force on 01.01.2027) taking into account the requirements, suitability and professional experience: Grade Name of function Title Class RO II Technical collaborator I Bachelor 8 + any allowances for children The classes they refer to the salary scale of State employees (valid from 01.01.2026). Any changes to the law regarding the function, salary and organization, respectively any evaluation within the scope of the new municipal ordinance on the classification of functions, remain explicitly reserved. Considering that the new employee organic regulations and the new municipal ordinance on the classification of functions have not yet formally entered into force, it is deemed appropriate to also publish the salary scales of the employee organic regulations currently in force, and better: > Senior official 2 Class 15-16 (minimum CHF 79'154.72 / maximum CHF 106'108.86) Class 16-17 (minimum CHF 81'291.90 / maximum CHF 108'973.80) + any allowances for children Information Further information can be obtained from the secretariat of the municipal technical office (ediliziaprivata@minusio.ch / 091 735 81 41).

Submission of applications / Competition deadline Applications must be submitted online via the link https://concorsi.minusio.ch/ by 11:00 am on Monday 9 November 2026 accompanied by the following documents: > cover letter indicating any proven experience in a similar position and in leading a team at medium/large sized public and/or private institutions > curriculum vitae with passport-sized photograph > criminal record extract¹ > extract UEF certifying the absence of executive procedures in progress¹ > self-certification on the state of health (downloadable at the link https://concorsi.minusio.ch/)² > diplomas and certificates of study, training and professional development > work certificates, references > copy of driving license ¹Documents issued less than 6 months ago ²The appointment may be subject to the outcome of a visit by a doctor of the Municipality who certifies the psychophysical suitability of the candidate to carry out the function for which they are competing. The municipal employees in office are exempted from submitting the required documents but must in any case forward a cover letter, indicating any proven experience in a similar position and in leading a team at medium/large sized public and/or private institutions. The Municipality reserves the right to request further documents during the examination of applications and/or at the time of hiring, as well as to ascertain suitability and preparation through an interview and/or aptitude test. The Municipality reserves the right not to proceed with the hiring if there is no candidate deemed suitable

  • Late applications will not be taken into consideration.

Documents sent in paper form, via fax or email will not be considered valid. Minusio, 16 October 2026 The Town Hall
