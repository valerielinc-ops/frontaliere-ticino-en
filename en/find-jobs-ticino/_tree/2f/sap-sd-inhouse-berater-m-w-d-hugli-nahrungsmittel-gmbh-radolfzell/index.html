Full description

We are an innovative and growing company that relies on modern SAP solutions to make our business processes optimal. To strengthen our IT team, we are looking for a committed SAP in-house consultant (SD) as well as strong knowledge in customs and foreign trade at the earliest possible time. Tasks:

  • Support, further development and optimization of our SAP SD processes with focus on sales, shipping and billing Conception and implementation of solutions in the area of customs and foreign trade (customs processing, preferences, export control)
  • Analysis and optimization of business processes in close cooperation with the departments Implementation of customization adjustments in SAP SD and adjacent modules
  • Support for FI-relevant topics, in particular at the interfaces to SD (e.g. revenue accounts, sales tax, accounts receivable)
  • 2nd and 3rd level support for SAP SD users in the company
  • Implementation of user training and preparation of documentation
  • Project work on SAP rollouts, upgrades and the introduction of new functionalities
  • Cooperation with external service providers and SAP partners Participation in the harmonization and standardization of SAP processes Requirements:
  • Completed study of (economic) informatics, business administration or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience as SAP Consultant in the module SD
  • In-depth knowledge of SAP SD processes (sale, shipping, invoicing, pricing, conditions)
  • Very good knowledge of customs and foreign trade, ideally with AEB
  • Basic knowledge of the SAP FI module, especially at the interfaces to SD
  • Experience in customizing and process optimization
  • Analytical thinking and structured working methods
  • Communication strength and teamwork
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing and good knowledge of English
  • Travel readiness for occasional assignments in other locations is advantageous:
  • SAP certification in the area of SD
  • Knowledge of SAP S/4HANA
  • Experience with ABAP or debugging is beneficial
  • Know-how in logistics processes
  • Project experience in international roll-out projects
