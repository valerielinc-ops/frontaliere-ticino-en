Become part of something bigger. Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life. On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you.

Tasks: • Professional and personnel management of a team of about 8 employees • Co-responsibility for cleaning and internal disposal transports • Assuming evening responsibility within the housekeeping an mind. four days/week from 13.20/16.00 to 22.30 • Professional contact person during evening operations • Readiness for special missions and weekend missions • Active cooperation at the front as well as taking over holiday representatives within the team Together with the head of housekeeping responsible for: • Implementation of quality controls and training • Introduction and training of new employees • Coordination of event and internal transport logistics and disposal • Cooperation in projects and further developments Requirements • You have completed training in housekeeping or in building cleaning and several years of management experience in cleaning. • The special requirements of hospital cleaning and the applicable quality and hygiene standards are very familiar to you. • You take your leadership role actively, responsibly and with joy. • When dealing with the PC and the common Microsoft 365 applications, you are safe and well-versed. • You have very good knowledge of German in words and writing. • Thanks to your independent, reliable and solution-oriented way of working, you set priorities correctly and maintain an overview even in hectic situations. • You are flexible in terms of time and willing to work regularly on evening work as well as on weekends if necessary. We offer: You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits. The Swiss Paraplegic Centre in Nottwil is a private, nationally and internationally recognized specialist clinic for cross-sectional, back and respiratory medicine. As an acute and rehabilitation clinic, the centre offers its patients comprehensive care and supports them throughout their lives. The clinic is part of the Swiss Paraplegic Group, which provides a globally unique service network for the holistic rehabilitation of people with paraplegia.
