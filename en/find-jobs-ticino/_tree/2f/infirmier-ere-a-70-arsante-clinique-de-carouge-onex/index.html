Full description

We are looking for a qualified and motivated nurse to join our emergency department team. The ideal candidate is dynamic, able to react quickly in a demanding environment and knows how to offer quality care with compassion and professionalism. Main missions:

  • Provide care, assessment and care for patients in emergency situations
  • Administer first aid and stabilize patients awaiting treatment or intervention
  • Working with physicians and other caregivers to provide timely and appropriate care
  • Managing priorities in a tense flow environment by applying existing protocols and procedures
  • Participate in ongoing patient monitoring, anticipate and respond to immediate needs
  • Ensuring clear and reassuring communication with patients and their families
  • Inform and maintain medical records accurately and confidentially Competencies required:
  • State diploma of nurse or recognized equivalent
  • Right of practice in the Canton of Geneva
  • Experience in emergency service or other acute care environment (a major asset)
  • Mastery of the Swiss sorting scale.
  • Mastery of immobilization techniques (including Woodcast)
  • Mastery of emergency technical gestures and medical surveillance tools
  • Ability to manage stressful situations and work in an intense environment
  • Excellent observation and rapid decision-making capacity
  • Organizational sense, rigour, and ability to manage priorities
  • Good relational skills and ability to work in a multidisciplinary team Conditions:
  • Start: as soon as possible
  • Activity rate: 70%
  • Schedules on rotation, including weekends, and holidays
  • Stimulating working environment with a supportive and dynamic team
  • 5 weeks of vacation
