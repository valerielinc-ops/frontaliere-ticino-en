Full description

In winter 300 kilometres of slopes where others go skiing and in summer 700 km of top bike trail. Do you want this right on your doorstep? Then become part of our team in Davos.

Your new employer

With around 70 employees, Caviezel AG and its Elektro Christoffel branch are the leading provider of electrical installations, telematics and building technology in the Davos area.

Your tasks

Your tasks in our telematics department include independent building wiring as well as service and maintenance work on telecommunications and low-power installations for our customers. You actively help build new customer relationships and deepen existing ones.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training as an electrical installer or comparable independent, committed and solution-oriented work
  • Reliable, responsible and customer-oriented personality
  • Readiness for Picetting
  • Category B driving licence

This is what we offer you

  • In a dreamlike environment, we offer a responsible position in an industry with a future
  • Good pay and social benefits
  • Extensive training and education opportunities
  • Miscellaneous benefits
  • Individual working time models
