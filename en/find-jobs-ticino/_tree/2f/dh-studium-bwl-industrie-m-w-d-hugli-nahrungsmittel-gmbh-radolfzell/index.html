Full description

Are you interested in completing a Bachelor's degree and being able to apply business theory directly into practice? Then the DH-Studium BWL Industrie is exactly what you're looking for! Tasks: During your Bachelor's degree, you will be involved in our procurement management of raw materials and packaging materials, get to know our sales divisions, including their marketing departments, visit our administrative departments, finance and accounting, and personnel departments, pass through the various areas of our group-wide organized IT, support our development and quality departments, gain insights into our order planning and production procedures, gain insights into our production and logistics procedures, deepen your practical knowledge in theoretical phases at the Dual Vocational School and find further information on the course of study here. The course of study starts on October 1, 2026 - before the start of the course of study, you will complete a one-month paid internship at Hügli and get a taste of the Hügli atmosphere and understand the organization of the course of study through a structured training plan. The course of study alternates every 3 months with theoretical phases at the Dual Vocational School and practical phases in our company. The responsible vocational school is the Dual Vocational School Baden-Württemberg / Villingen-Schwenningen. The course of study lasts 3 years and ends with the title of "Bachelor of Arts" (B.A.). Requirements: You have achieved general or subject-specific higher education entrance qualifications with good grades in the core subjects. You are interested in business connections. You enjoy mathematics and have solid English knowledge. You have a high level of commitment, initiative, endurance, self-discipline, and self-regulation. You are able to manage your workload well and be flexible. You have a friendly demeanor.
