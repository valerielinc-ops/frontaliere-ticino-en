The Position

People Support Operations Specialist

At Roche, we believe that every employee’s journey matters. As a People Support Specialist within our People Support Solutions team, you will be the face of People & Culture (P&C), serving as the primary point of contact for employees and managers through our People Portal.

You won't just be answering questions; you will be a strategist of the employee experience, managing the Employee Lifecycle and ensuring that our P&C services are delivered with the highest quality and impact.

The opportunity

  • Provide insight and support on P&C policies and HR systems, specifically Workday.
  • Deliver consistent service across multiple channels, including chat, ticketing, and phone.
  • Maintain system data and execute administrative tasks related to core HR processes.
  • Resolve P&C inquiries and manage employee requests efficiently.
  • Gather process feedback and identify knowledge gaps to enhance service delivery.
  • Maintain up-to-date documentation by staying current on internal processes and best practices.
  • Participate in strategic projects as required by the business.
  • Embed customer care principles into all daily interactions and activities.
  • Ensure strict compliance with data privacy regulations and internal security standards.
  • Drive process improvements to ensure high-quality service and operational excellence.

Who you are

  • Experience in a Shared Service Center (SSC) or a dedicated HR services environment.
  • Broad HR Generalist knowledge with a proven track record of succeeding in a matrix organization and strong knowledge of the Polish Labour Law.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities effectively within a fast-paced, dynamic setting.
  • Cross-border experience, showing comfort and competence in a multicultural environment.
  • Technically proficient in navigating complex HR data structures and IT applications.
  • Strong customer service orientation with a focus on proactive problem-solving and continuous improvement.
  • Tech-savvy, with proficiency in Google Suite and a solid understanding of Workday HR processes.
  • Fluent in English and Polish, both written and spoken.

What you get

  • Contract of employment (Umowa o pracę).
  • Annual bonus payment based on your performance.
  • Hybrid work model (2 days per week in our Warsaw office).
  • Dedicated training budget (training, certifications, conferences, diversified career paths etc.).
  • Recharge Fridays (2 Fridays off per quarter available).
  • Take time Program (up to 3 months of leave to use for any purpose).
  • Flex Location (possibility to perform our work from different places in the world for a certain period of time).
  • Take Time for Charity (additional paid leave of maximum 2 weeks to engage in the charity action of your choice).
  • Private healthcare ( LuxMed packages) and group life insurance (UNUM).
  • Stock share purchase additions.
  • Yearly sales of company laptops and cars and many more!
