Full description

La Tour Hospital is a high-level, private, independent and human-scale acute care facility. Anchored in its mission to restore the best possible quality of life for its patients, La Tour Hospital has placed continuous improvement and the development of medical excellence at the heart of its priorities.

Orthopaedic surgery treats diseases and accidents affecting the motor system, including bones, joints, muscles, tendons and nerves.

We recruit one

UCO Nurse - HOM 4 months at 80% (ref: 2036)

Your mission

You will need to accommodate the patient and prepare him physically and psychologically for surgery.

As such you are in charge of;

  • Install the patient and present the administrative organization information
  • Provide pre- and post-operative care, surveillance and technical care in orthopaedic surgery
  • Prevent the occurrence of nosocomial infections by promoting a healthy environment around the patient, by respecting hygiene and aseptic rules
  • Perform care on medical prescription and in accordance with unit and institutional procedures
  • Maintaining the Informatized Patient Record (Carefolio)
  • Attend the necessary training to maintain and develop the skills expected for his position
  • Adapting to emergency situations; know how to alert and contact the right person and ensure emergency actions
  • Actively participate in the organization of the unit

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • Diploma of Nurse
  • In possession of Red Cross Recognition (for foreign degrees)
  • 2-3 years of hospital experience
  • Meet the requirements of all our patients (semi-private, private and VIP)
  • Languages: French, English (ideally)
  • Experience in orthopedic surgery
  • Meaning of contact and service in a private establishment
  • Autonomous, benevolence, versatility (surgery pole), adaptability, organization and priority sense are essential qualities
  • Demonstrate flexibility in planning
  • Positive attitude within a team and are able to generate a climate of confidence
  • Good knowledge of computer tools (Opale, Carefolio)

We offer you

Type of Contract: Contract with Duration Determined Maximum of 4 months Percentage: 80% Start date: As soon as possible.
