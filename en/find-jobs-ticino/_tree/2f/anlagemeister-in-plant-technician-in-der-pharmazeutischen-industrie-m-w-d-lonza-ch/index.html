Full description

As a plant foreman (m/f/d), you will be responsible for the technical and logistical handling of production campaigns in a production company for the production of bioconjugates. You will be a member of an interdisciplinary team and work in project teams that take care of investments and optimisation of processes, activities and systems. ## Mansioni

  • Involvement in the design and engineering phase of projects to comply with technical specifications for the plants and ensure a successful production campaign
  • Organizational and operational support, as well as monitoring of production campaigns, to ensure production and safety even outside normal working hours
  • Preparation of cGMP-compliant documentation in cooperation with the plant team leader, R&D and the Plant biotechnologists
  • Plant engineering instruction and production-specific training of operators during production
  • Control of complete compliance with operating regulations and production protocols, as well as intervention in the event of deviations
  • Support of the exchange of information and communication within the operating group
  • Continuous improvement of plants, procedures and processes (6S, Lean Six Sigma, KAIZEN) in compliance with safety, hygiene, environmental and Quality requirements ## Requisiti
  • Completed technical training or further education (apprenticeship, higher professional examination, bachelor's, master's degree)
  • Production experience in the manufacture of chemical or biotechnological products, or in the food industry would be advantageous
  • Ideally, initial experience in the ISO and/or GMP production environment
  • You have basic knowledge of MS Office (Excel, Word) and have an affinity for electronic systems and applications
  • Very good Knowledge of written and spoken German, as well as English skills
  • Willingness to perform on-call duty must be present ## Cosa offriamo
