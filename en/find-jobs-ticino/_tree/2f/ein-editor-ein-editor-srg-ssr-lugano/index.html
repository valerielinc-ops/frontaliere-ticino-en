Full description

Radio, TV, multimedia: public service takes shape thanks to men and women who transform ideas into programs and content and shape the company strategy with creativity and balance. With the added value of their personality. The information world never stops: 365 days a year must be fed with services, interviews, insights, tabs, reports and thematic series.

Shipments

In this role you will be called

  • Contribute to the production and development of information content in a stimulating and creative environment, but also stressful and demanding.
  • Ensure management and development of team managers, promoting professional resource growth.
  • Ensure proper workload planning, in collaboration with the Resource Manager and Skills.
  • Participate in strategic projects and contribute to the evolution of the information offer.

Requirements

  • Consolidated journalistic experience and passion for topicality, writing, images and new technologies.
  • In-depth knowledge of the mechanisms of information and Swiss, Swiss-Italian and international realities.
  • Curiosity, inventiveness, spirit of initiative and propensity to modern languages of multimedia journalism.
  • Co-operative capabilities, solidity and reliability.
  • Excellent capacity for stress management, emergencies and high workloads.
  • Good knowledge of national and English languages, flexibility and availability to work on shifts and with variable hours.

What we offer

  • A stimulating activity in a constantly changing environment, in the context of news and informational study.
  • Professional and personal growth opportunities.
  • The possibility of contributing concretely to the corporate image and the constant consolidation of the role of public service.
  • Continuous training in journalistic and technological fields.

Our promise

What are the ingredients to reach the inhabitants of Switzerland with quality format? Know-how, enthusiasm and an environment rich in stimuli, in which everyone can grow. To give the best it takes a source of inspiration: Welcome to the world of RSI!

  • Produce content: Quality journalism is our main activity and our passion. We enjoy editorial freedom, promote creativity and give ample space to the production of new and high-level content.
  • Give shape to digital: Quality journalism is also digital. We explore new possibilities and experience innovative forms: digital, interactive and crossmedia. A look at tomorrow for our spectators today.
  • Valorize the person: We cultivate solidarity relations, work independently and contribute together with a good working climate. Our success is also based on the personal and professional qualities of all employees.
  • Make sense: With our programs we reach and thrill Switzerland. We act with respect and sense of independence; we promote the free formation of opinions and strengthen pluralism. We are proud of the contribution we make to Switzerland.
