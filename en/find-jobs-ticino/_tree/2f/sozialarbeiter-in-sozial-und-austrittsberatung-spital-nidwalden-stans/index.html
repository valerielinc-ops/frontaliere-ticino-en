Together we provide around the clock with passion and commitment for coordinated, digitally connected and accessible healthcare for all. We are looking for an immediately or by agreement

Your field of activity

  • They advise and accompany patients: women of all ages and life situations as well as their relatives with expertise and empathy.
  • The term of office shall run until April 2027. From spring 2027, there is the option to continue the activity in a permanent position with a workload of 80 to 100%.
