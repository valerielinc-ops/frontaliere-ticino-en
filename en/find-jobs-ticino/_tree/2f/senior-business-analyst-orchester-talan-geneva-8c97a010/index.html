Full description

Background As part of the transformation of its energy trading information system, our customer is looking for a Senior Business Analyst to support its business and IT teams in modernizing its applications. A strategic project to migrate to a new ETRM platform is underway to replace the historical system with a modern and scalable solution. You will intervene at the interface between business teams, developers and publishers to collect needs, formalize functional requirements and support their implementation.

Responsibilities

Business analysis and functional design

  • Collect, analyse and formalize business needs.
  • Write functional specifications, features and User Stories.
  • Understand trading processes and propose solutions tailored to operational needs.
  • Participate in the ETRM platform settings: configurations, workflows, templates and pricing.
  • Collaborate with technical teams and publishers to ensure the correct implementation of solutions.

Tests and migration

  • Prepare and execute functional test plans.
  • Verify the compliance of developments with user specifications and requirements.
  • Participate in User Recipe Phases (UAT).
  • Contribute to the migration project to the new ETRM platform.
  • Collaborate with other Business Analysts and IT teams on migration activities.

Project management and support

  • Participate in the management of application evolutions and backlog monitoring.
  • Identify opportunities for improving business processes.
  • Support users in the management of solutions.
  • Provide functional support and help resolve incidents.
  • Document processes and share knowledge with teams.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in the energy sector or the Commodity Trading.
  • Experience on ETRM platforms ( Orchestra, Calypso, Murex, Endur...).
  • Good understanding of energy markets and transaction life cycle.
  • Knowledge of Front-to-Risk flows, physical and financial instruments and P&L calculations.
  • Mastery of needs collection, functional specification writing and User Stories.
  • Experience in functional testing, UAT and ETRM migration projects.
  • Knowledge of Agile and DevOps methodologies.
  • Knowledge of the Back-Office and Finance processes is a plus.
  • Advanced computer or engineering training.
  • Common English and French.
