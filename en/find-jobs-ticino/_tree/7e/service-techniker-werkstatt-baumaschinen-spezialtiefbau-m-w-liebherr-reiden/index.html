Full description

Do you love large machines, sophisticated technology and varied applications? Then you are exactly right at Liebherr. As a service technician, you ensure that our high-performance special foundation equipment is reliably in use throughout Switzerland. Whether in our modern workshop in Reiden or directly with our customers on construction sites throughout Switzerland – you can expect a versatile working day with exciting challenges. These are your tasks

  • With maintenance, repair and inspection work, you ensure that our Liebherr special underground construction equipment is ready for use at any time.
  • You work in our workshop in Reiden or support our customers directly on construction sites throughout Switzerland.
  • Technical faults in the areas of electrics, electronics and hydraulics you analyze independently and repair them professionally.
  • You carry out warranty work as well as technical adjustments and retrofits on our devices.
  • You put new and used machines into operation directly at the customer and perform them professionally.
  • Special equipment is assembled, checked and prepared for use.
  • You create inspection and service reports and document the work performed reliably.
  • With our customers, you are available as a competent contact person and support them with your technical know-how. You bring that with you
  • Completed training as construction machinery mechanic EFZ, agricultural machinery mechanic EFZ, construction locksmith EFZ or equivalent technical training
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Category B and BE driving licence
  • Readiness for flexible deployments throughout Switzerland
