Head / Head of Therapy Center Spital Zollikerberg

If you too want to make a difference in people’s lives, these are your tasks:

  • Strategy and concept development Responsibility for the strategic, conceptual and organizational development of the therapy center in accordance with the hospital strategy and the needs of the patients.
  • Operational management and cost-effectiveness Shared responsibility with the department managers for budget, personnel deployment, capacity planning and economic resource deployment as well as ensuring efficient and needs-based operational processes
  • Personnel management and development Personnel and organisational management of divisional management and promotion of their professional and personal development. Joint development and establishment of participatory management and decision-making structures involving employees and the various therapeutic occupational groups.
  • Quality and specialist development Ensuring and continuous development of high-quality, evidence-based therapeutic care in cooperation with the professionally responsible persons.
  • Interprofessional cooperation and networking Promotion and further development of cooperation with clinical departments, nursing expertise, medical service and other professional groups. Participation in the development of integrated treatment pathways and care models
  • Communication and representation of interests Central contact person for the entire therapy center as well as representation of his concerns in internal and external committees. Ensure transparent communication and promote a common identity.

Become part of the Department of Care Expertise, Therapies and Counselling at the Zollikerberg Hospital. At the Spital Zollikerberg Therapy Center, we bring together various therapeutic disciplines and a wide range of services under one roof. Our broadly based primary care includes internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics with neonatology, general surgery, an interdisciplinary emergency center and orthopedics with specialized spinal surgery.
