Full description

At the Outpatient Competence Center Neurology at KSB, you play a central role in everyday practice. They receive patients, coordinate the consultation hours and provide medical and administrative support. Your organizational talent and communication strength ensure that the process in neurology remains efficient and human. Their tasks:

  • They coordinate the neurological consultation hours incl. telephone service.
  • They process inquiries from patients, doctors and internal and external bodies – by telephone and in writing.
  • They take care of the reception and take blood samples.
  • They perform individual electrophysiological examinations (e.g. SudoScan).
  • They edit medical reports.
  • They perform general secretarial tasks and take care of material management. Your profile:
  • You have completed training as MPA EFZ, medical secretary or equivalent.
  • Ideally, they bring professional experience in the field.
  • They master medical terminology and communicate safely in German – other languages are an advantage.
  • They have very good administrative skills and know MS Office, ideally also KISIM and Navision.
  • They like to work with people and stay friendly and focused under pressure. Their advantages: The KSB offers more than an exciting workplace with good working conditions.
