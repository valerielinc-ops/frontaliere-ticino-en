Full description

Would you like to put your installation-electrical CFC or installation electrician at the service of troubleshooting? This opportunity concerns the building department.

Responsibilities

  • Provide troubleshooting interventions to strengthen the building department of one of our customers, with an installation-electrical CFC or assembly electrician

Your profile

  • You have an electrical installer CFC or installation electrician. You justify a proven experience in electrical installations and telecommunications. You have very good knowledge of re-compliance according to ITTO requirements. You are able to draw up small quotes and make metres, an asset. You have a real interest in new technologies and communication systems and enjoy sharing your knowledge. Versatile, motivated and dynamic, you have a keen sense of responsibility. You work autonomously and organizedly and can be flexible. You attach particular importance to compliance with safety directives and rules. You have a valid driving licence. You have an excellent presentation and are comfortable in customer contacts.

We offer

  • A long-term mission with real prospects for fixed engagement. A varied and autonomous position, with diverse interventions and responsibilities. Some flexibility in the organization of working time and/or activity rate, depending on the job needs and candidate profile. The opportunity to use and develop your technical skills in a professional and dynamic environment. Attractive working conditions, in line with your skills and experience. Personalized support and attentive follow-up by your advisor throughout your mission.

We support you in your professional development through ongoing training in relation to your profession, fully supported by the Temptraining Training Fund.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed.
