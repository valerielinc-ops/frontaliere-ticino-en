Full description

Your Field of Work

  • As an Electrician: EFZ, you are responsible for the classic electrical installations.
  • In private households and in the trade, you install connections for electricity, telephone, internet, television, and radio in a professional manner.
  • You create and control the connections in buildings from the main connection to the sockets.
  • In construction sites, you carefully lay the pipes for all electrical lines and install safety and switching cabinets.
  • After a structured introduction, you will carry out your work independently.
  • In case of complex installations, an electric installer will be at your side.
