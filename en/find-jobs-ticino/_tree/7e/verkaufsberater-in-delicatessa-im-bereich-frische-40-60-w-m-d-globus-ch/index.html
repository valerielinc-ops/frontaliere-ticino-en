Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the “place we love to be” to life together with us. Here you make the difference

  • As a host, you ensure that our customers feel welcome and welcomed as guests.
  • Thanks to your excellent service and individual advice, you can ensure unique shopping experiences.
  • You are jointly responsible for the freshness and quality of our high-quality products.
  • You ensure strict compliance with legal and operational hygiene laws and regulations.
  • With your positive charisma and your passion for our *delicatessa you contribute decisively to the company’s success.

That draws you out

  • You are a passionate host: a sales person with passion and high-quality food fascinates you.
  • You bring several years of professional experience in the food sector, a completed apprenticeship in the retail trade is an advantage, but not a must.
  • You have perfect manners and a stylish appearance.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German and like one or the other foreign language.
  • Working on Saturdays and having time off on another weekday is completely okay for you.

BENEFITS FOR YOU Together with around 1,700 other lovers of detail, you are an important part of our fascinating premium and luxury world. You experience real appreciation, work in a great team and we trust you so that you can use space and make a difference with your skills. If you understand your craft and bring a large portion of passion, then many career doors are open to you. We are happy to support you with internal but also external training and further education.

Also, look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment with flat hierarchies in the heart of St. Gallen.
  • Benefit-based premiums and attractive social benefits.
  • Flexible working time models and at least 5 weeks holidays (PS: You get more free time thanks to the option "unpaid holiday" - from 7 to 180 days).
  • up to 50% discount on products from the globe world for you and your family.
  • Much more...

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in St. Gallen?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with St. Gallen's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
