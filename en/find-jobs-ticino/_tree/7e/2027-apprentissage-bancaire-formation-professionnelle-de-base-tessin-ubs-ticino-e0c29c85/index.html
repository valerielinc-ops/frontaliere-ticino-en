Full description

Do you like contact with people? Are you curious and open to news? Do you want to make a difference and face new challenges? Then join our team and help shape the future with us!

We offer you complete business training with a lot of practice in the banking sector. From us you will learn everything you need for a successful career and your professional future in UBS.

Your training During the three years of apprenticeship, you will work in several teams of the bank and discover the many interesting areas of UBS. Our motivated and competent Practical Advisors will always be at your side.

In addition to the professional school, you will receive practical training at CYP in your region. There you will meet other apprentices of different banks and learn the theoretical basis of the banking sector. The CYP will prepare you perfectly for the final exam of banking training. In UBS you will deepen your knowledge about our products and services and know our UBS consulting approach. In addition, we attach great importance to the development of your social skills, such as behavior in sales and communication.

From us, your apprenticeship will not simply end with the Federal Certificate of “Impiegata/or di commercio” with integrated professional maturity. Many of our former apprentices remain with us and begin their careers with a fixed employment or deepen their knowledge in our biennial training program (JUNA).

Your team You will be part of a large group of UBS business apprentices. You will meet regularly during internal and external training, events and much more. Often friendships arise that go beyond work and training.

UBS relies on modern technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) in order to constantly improve its services, even within our team. In this way, we make processes more efficient, we use data more intelligently and simplify everyday work, both for employees and customers.

Your knowledge and experiences You have completed the highest compulsory school level with good grades in mathematics, German, English and Italian.

For us these aspects are particularly important

  • You like to work with people and have a good team spirit.
  • You are motivated and always demonstrate a positive approach.
  • You like to learn new things and you are open to changes.
  • You're interested in the world of finance.
