PROFILE

  • Completed vocational training as a polymechanic:in or adequate professional experience in CNC machine operation (preferably in profile processing)
  • Sound ISO code knowledge
  • Reading and understanding NC codes and technical drawings
  • Readiness to work in 2-shift operation in Steinach
