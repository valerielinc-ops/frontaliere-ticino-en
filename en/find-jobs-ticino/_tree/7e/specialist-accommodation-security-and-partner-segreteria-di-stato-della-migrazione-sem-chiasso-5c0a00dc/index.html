The migration concerns us

The Secretariat of State of Migration (SEM) deals with all issues related to foreign law and asylum law in Switzerland. The SEM asylum sector implements the asylum and refugee policy defined by Parliament and the Federal Council. It is also responsible for the accommodation and assistance of asylum seekers and for the completion of asylum procedures.
