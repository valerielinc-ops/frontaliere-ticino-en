Company description

Are you looking for a new professional challenge? With 39 years of experience in the field of advice and personnel management, Leteam is now positioning itself as a key player in recruitment and service in fixed and temporary placement throughout Switzerland and Liechtenstein.

Through our 29 agencies, we offer fast and tailor-made solutions for both temporary and permanent missions. Our core activity? Connecting talent to companies in various sectors with a clear objective: to be always efficient.

Description of post

We are looking for a staff placement specialist available in January 2027, with excellent relational skills. After a comprehensive training course on our services, you will be responsible for prospecting new partners, including the electrical and building sectors in the Valais region.

  • Develop client portfolio through prospecting (telephone, physics, etc.) and maintain existing partnerships
  • Recruit profiles tailored to client needs and expand the candidate network
  • Analyze client and candidate expectations to ensure the best collaborations in line with each other's expectations
  • Strengthen your visibility, that of leteam, and the links with our partners through various events
  • Optimize relationships and network to increase revenue
  • Managing relationships with partners and stakeholders

Profile sought

  • Being animated by the challenge
  • You live near Martigny and you have a good network
  • Be a result of technical, commercial or equivalent training
  • Have at least 2 to 3 years of experience in a similar role (recruitment, personnel management, or business development)
  • Have a strong interest in negotiation and outcome (have a good knowledge of sales and prospecting techniques)
  • Bring its added value thanks to its personality, authenticity
  • Being autonomous, while knowing how to surround the right people to achieve their goals
  • Proven perseverance and resilience to challenges
  • Excellent relational skills: a sense of communication and ability to build and maintain relationships of trust with clients and candidates
  • Perfect mastery of French both orally and in writing (a second language is a plus)

We offer

What we love about leteam is shared success. It is important for us that every internal staff member can have the tools and support necessary for the successful implementation of his or her mission.

Therefore

  • You will benefit from in-house continuing training (individual and collective) as well as workshops to develop your skills and confidence in this profession
  • We offer you an attractive base salary with a non-ceiling provisioning system
  • You will be surrounded by a family, young and jovial working climate, with a horizontal hierarchy
  • We provide you with modern infrastructure and state-of-the-art tools, as well as an agency vehicle for your visits
