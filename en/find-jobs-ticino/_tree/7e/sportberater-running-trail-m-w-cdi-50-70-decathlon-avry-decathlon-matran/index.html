Our expansion continues, especially in German-speaking Switzerland, offering career opportunities throughout the country. Why is this job for you? Are you looking for a moving profession, where you share your passion for sports from morning to night? You want to be proud to advise the right equipment and see your client go away with a smile? Do you like action, terrain and teamwork? Your 3 great benefits to us:
