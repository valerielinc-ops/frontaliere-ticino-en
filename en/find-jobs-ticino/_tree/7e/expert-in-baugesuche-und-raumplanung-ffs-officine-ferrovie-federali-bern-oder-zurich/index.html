  • Move Switzerland with us. Expert:in Construction Application and Spatial Planning Bern or Zurich / Work Smart, 01.10.2026 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now ## You can look forward to that. The Department of Land Stock and Potentials ensures the long-term development and preservation of the value of SBB’s land. You work in a technically strong team that examines several thousand building applications and spatial planning templates from all over Switzerland every year and thus make a tangible contribution to the sustainable development of our areas. In this specialist leadership role, you actively shape the further development of our processes and represent the interests of SBB vis-à-vis authorities, municipalities and other stakeholders. You can move that.
  • As a Switzerland-wide expert guide for building applications and spatial planning consultations, you ensure a uniform application of the technical specifications and continuously develop methods, standards and processes.
  • You will analyse spatial planning developments and identify opportunities for securing and increasing the value of SBB’s land portfolio at an early stage.
  • Complex third-party construction projects and in-house group projects will be assessed thoroughly and you will represent the interests of SBB with authorities and other stakeholders.
  • In close cooperation with internal interfaces, you support the assessment of consultations and provide the necessary information.
  • You maintain an active network with authorities, specialist bodies and partners and represent the concerns of SBB professionally and convincingly. You bring that with you.
  • With a strong consulting, negotiation and communication strength, you bring together different interests and develop viable solutions.
  • You work in a structured and self-responsible manner and keep an overview even in demanding situations.
  • You have completed university or technical college studies at master level or equivalent in spatial planning, real estate management or in a related field of expertise.
  • You bring several years of practice in spatial planning, public building law, real estate management or in a comparable specialist function.
  • In real estate law as well as in spatial planning and building law you have in-depth knowledge.
  • You are confident in negotiating German at level C2 and ideally have a good knowledge of a second national language at level B1. ## Your reward and benefits.
