Full description

Do you already have an idea of what you will do after completing your school-leaving diploma? Get started on your future with us and take advantage of the development opportunities at Lidl Switzerland. Our stores are at the heart of our business. As an AFP or CFC retail apprentice (with a focus on creating shopping experiences), you contribute to the success of your Lidl store. AFP start/end: 1 August 2027 – 31 July 2029 CFC start/end: 1 August 2027 – 31 July 2030

Your responsibilities

  • Managing cash registers and preparing cash reports
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Stocking and presenting merchandise
  • Checking the receipt of goods in the stockroom and assisting your team during inventories
  • Helping plan and implement promotions and seasonal displays
  • Determining merchandise requirements and ordering merchandise in the third year of apprenticeship (CFC)
  • First experience in leading a team in the third year of apprenticeship (CFC)

Your profile

  • Average to very good grades in the lower secondary school-leaving qualification
  • AFP: A1 level in a foreign language and CFC: at least A2
  • Enjoy working with people
  • Independent working style and quick thinking
  • Team spirit, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and availability
  • Physical stamina
  • Good command of French

Our offer An apprenticeship that opens many doors for you. After successfully completing your apprenticeship, and if you have good personal skills, we will help you progress to a management position in the store. In addition, you will benefit from Lidl's collective employment agreement and numerous attractive benefits
