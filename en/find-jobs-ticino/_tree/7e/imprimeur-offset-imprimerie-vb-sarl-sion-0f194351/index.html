Full description

Your missions

  • Within the print shop, you are responsible for the conduct of our Heidelberg four-colour offset presses, in 35 x 50 cm and 50 x 70 cm formats. As such, you:
  • Prepare and set the machine for each work
  • Check the conformity of printing throughout the print run: colours, tracking, printing quality
  • Validate the drawer and ensure compliance throughout the draw
  • Monitor the production and proper execution of worksheets
  • Perform press maintenance and cleaning
  • Respect the safety and hygiene rules in the workshop

Your profile

  • Degree in printing or equivalent training
  • Experience in offset printing, especially on Heidelberg presses
  • Autonomy, rigor and sense of responsibility
  • Good eye for color and attention to detail
  • Ability to work as a team and communicate
