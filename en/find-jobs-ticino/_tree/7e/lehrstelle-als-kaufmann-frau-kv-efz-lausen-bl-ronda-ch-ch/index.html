Full description

Training merchants EFZ – optional with Berufsmatur In the summer of 2023, the new commercial basic education «Kauffrau/Kaufmann EFZ» comes into force. It makes learners fit for the challenges of the future. Tomorrow’s merchants act in agile forms of work and organization, interact in a networked working environment and work with new technologies. This requires technical skills and a digital mindset, social and self-skills as well as critical thinking and creativity. The new commercial basic education is consistently focused on action competences. It empowers learners to deal with changes in the economy and society as well as lifelong learning. What we expect from you

  • Completion of secondary school level E or P
  • Good grades in German, English, French and math
  • Enjoying foreign languages
  • Keyboard writing in the ten finger system
  • Check S2 or the willingness to complete the free vocational check on yousty.ch Practical training Already at the beginning of the apprenticeship you will be involved in daily business, gradually familiarized with new tasks and you will get your own small projects to work on. In the departments, motivated and competent professionals accompany you and you have contact with the trainees of other apprenticeships at Ronda at events such as Future Day, Parents Day or Apprenticeship Day. In accordance with the objectives of the performance target catalogue "Services & Administration", you work over the course of the three years in the following areas:
  • Purchasing
  • Accounting & Invoicing
  • Telephone & Reception
  • Marketing
  • Interior Service Apprenticeship application & trial apprenticeship You are convinced? Then your next step is a trial lesson. It gives you an insight into the real working day and also serves as a review of your professional desire. But beware: Since Ronda trains only one apprentice per year, trial apprentices are offered exclusively for apprenticeship applicants and for free apprenticeship places.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Basel?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
