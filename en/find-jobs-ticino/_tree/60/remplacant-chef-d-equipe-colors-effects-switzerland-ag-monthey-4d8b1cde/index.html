Replacement Team Leader

DEVELOPING HEAD OF EQUIPMENT – FINISHING BATITMENT (m/f/d)

About us

We give color to the world! A member of the Chione Group, Sun Chemical is a world leader in printing inks and pigments. Present in 63 countries, more than 22,000 employees work on 250 sites every day to serve many industries, from cosmetics and packaging to agriculture and automobiles. Together, we have an annual turnover of approximately US$8.5 billion and are developing innovative and sustainable solutions with our customers.

For the Colors \&Effects website in Monthey, we are looking for a replacement team leader in the finishing building.

Your main missions

  • You train, apply and enforce all the procedures and directives in force to ensure that the tasks are carried out safely and environmentally.
  • You perform production tasks such as conducting processes on manual or automatic installations, loadings and discharges of products, issuing fire bonds and/or work permits, drafting procedures.
  • You are autonomous in managing tasks of medium complexity and ensure the operation of the buildings and annexes that are entrusted to you.
  • You are responsible for monitoring and reporting production.
  • You are involved in sampling and labelling, simple analytical checks, TPM and follow the projects or major revisions.
  • You are looking for faults and causes of deviations from the process and facilities to correct them.
  • You replace the team leader on an ad hoc basis, providing staff management and administrative tasks (including SAP).

PROFILE RESEARCH

  • You have a CFC operator of automated machines, a technologist in chemical and pharmaceutical production or an equivalent training.
  • You benefit from a solid experience in the industrial environment and have good technical and mechanical knowledge of manufacturing facilities.
  • You strictly apply the EHS guidelines and actively contribute to the promotion of an exemplary safety culture.
  • You are professional and accurate in the application and monitoring of manufacturing procedures.
  • You have excellent communication skills and enjoy teamwork.

Sun Chemical lives in diversity and, with all employees, we are actively committed to equal opportunities. Therefore, we follow the principle that candidates are selected and engaged solely on the basis of their individual performance and competence in relation to the position to be filled.

If you are interested in this position, please send your complete file (motivation letter, curriculum vitae, copy of your diplomas, work certificates and certificates of continuing training).
