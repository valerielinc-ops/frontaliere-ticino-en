What you do

  • With us you will experience a change of perspective: Accidents, which you otherwise treat in a practice or clinic, you now see from an insurance medical perspective.
  • You can expect a versatile job with the opportunity to participate in an interdisciplinary team.
  • You investigate and advise people who are injured and learn how to prepare insurance medical assessments.
  • Our specialists supervise you for mono- and interdisciplinary assessments.
  • The assistant medical position is recognized for the specialist training "Prevention and Public Health" and is limited to one year. What you bring
  • In your career so far, you have been able to complete at least three years of basic clinical training in traumatology, orthopaedics, surgery or neurology.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing in German on a native language level.
  • When dealing with work on the computer, you are well-versed and open to further developments in the digital environment.
  • You enjoy cross-disciplinary communication and the ability to understand complex issues.
