Full description

EOC – Ente Ospedaliero Cantonale is a multisite hospital in Ticino with its institutes present throughout the cantonal territory for a total of 1,000 beds. The organization allows for the effective combination of local approach and overall vision, ensuring a global and proximity hospital offer to the population, regardless of the location where services are requested. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to human relationships, EOC ensures quality healthcare, taking care of over 41,000 inpatients per year and providing over 600,000 outpatient consultations. To complete our team in the Human Resources Area of the General Management, we are looking for an Organizational Specialist and HR Business Analyst (100%). Scope of the function: Support the Human Resources Area transversally, ensuring correct management of the company structure and supporting the continuous improvement of internal processes through the collection, analysis, and interpretation of HR data. Support the implementation of HR process digitalization projects, supporting the Human Resources Area in the adoption of change management tools. Main tasks and responsibilities: Analysis, management, and maintenance of the company structure in SAP (HCM / SuccessFactors) with related updates of organizational units, positions, roles, and reporting lines

  • Guarantee the quality, coherence, and integrity of HR data
  • Collection, analysis, and interpretation of personnel data to support the implementation of strategic decisions to improve performance, well-being, and organization
  • Collaboration with the Digital Evolution and Technology Area and other stakeholders on HR processes and integrations
  • Management and/or support of different phases of HR automation and digitalization projects to ensure that proposed solutions are aligned with identified needs
  • Coordination and management of the process of creating new functions and their related job descriptions at a transversal level
  • Management and promotion of continuous updating, organization, and archiving of documentation (Instructions, forms, directives) in the dedicated document management system for the Human Resources Area
  • Management of ad hoc proposals and initiatives related to the Human Resources Area. The candidate: Identifies with the values and mission of EOC; Establishes effective and constructive relationships with internal and external clients and colleagues, obtaining the trust of their interlocutors and committing to meet their needs
  • Works towards objectives and is result-oriented
  • Interacts with heterogeneous professional families aiming to reconcile interdisciplinarity and interprofessionalism. Required requirements: University education in economic or humanistic disciplines or information technology
  • At least 3 years of experience in HR or organizational development
  • Proficiency in SAP (HCM / SuccessFactors) and/or other HR systems
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work in a team and establish effective relationships with stakeholders
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and adapt to changing priorities
  • Ability to maintain confidentiality and handle sensitive information
  • Ability to work in a multicultural environment and adapt to different work styles
  • Fluency in English and/or other languages (German, French, Italian, etc.).
