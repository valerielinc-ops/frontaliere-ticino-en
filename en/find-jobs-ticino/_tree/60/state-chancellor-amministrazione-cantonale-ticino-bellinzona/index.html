Full description

Tasks

  • prepare the new edition of estage for 2027, coordinating a communication campaign on multiple channels
  • conduct guided tours to Palazzo delle Orsoline, transmitting notions of civics to students of all ages
  • participate in the production of information content dedicated to the cantonal administration for the various institutional communication channels (website, intranet, instagram, LinkedIn, Youtube, WhatsApp, Argomenti, etc.) with the possibility to propose themes and contents
  • collaborate with the editorial staff of the daily review
  • participate in other communication projects within the SIC activities, including “OltreconfiniTi”, the multimedia platform dedicated to the Ticino emigration in the world, and the communication within the cantonal administration

Requirements

  • academia in a humanistic faculty or in relation to the proposed tasks
  • excellent editorial skills
  • good understanding of languages (Italian, German, French, English)
  • good experience in creating and managing multimedia content on social media (for example through Canva, Edits, CapCut or other dedicated apps)
  • enterprise, flexibility and flexibility
  • ability to work both team and independently

Special remarks

  • duration of the internship: from 1 January 2027 or from date to be established for a period of 6 months, with employment to 80%
  • knowledge related to video production (also in mobile), graphics, typography or web design are preferential title
  • the possibility to actively collaborate in the development and management of initiatives to improve institutional communication, the opportunity to propose and realize original projects. In this regard, we recommend preparing for the interview by exploring what has already been done in these areas by the SIC

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
