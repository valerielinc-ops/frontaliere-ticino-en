Sales Manager MICE 100% (w/m/i) - Bürgenstock Resort

Description An icon among Swiss hotels: In 1873 the history of the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne began – a story that still impresses and touches today. Up on the Bürgenberg mountain is an oasis of relaxation and a place of first-class hospitality. The Bürgenstock Resort and its approximately 700 employees from 54 nations offers the perfect setting for dazzling moments that remain in memory.

Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two overnight accommodations The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the taverne 1879 (3*) are at guests' disposal. Each of these houses has a unique atmosphere. Ten restaurants, bars & lounges are the showcase of culinary excellence; the award-winning Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa provide the ultimate in relaxation.

The Bürgenstock Resort is not only a dream destination for travellers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. The resort ranks fourth among Switzerland's best employers in 2023 in the category gastronomy, tourism, accommodation, entertainment and leisure, and received the official certification as "Great Place to Work" of Switzerland 2025. Two awards that the entire team is proud to meet. To strengthen our team we are looking for a sales manager MICE 100% (w/m/i) from July 2026 or by appointment.

  • Implementation of professional hotel presentations and site-visions for potential customers and partners
  • Participation in the creation of revenue budgets and

Activity plans - Responsibility for achieving defined sales targets and relevant key figures - Ensure efficient coordination and coordination of all sales activities with internal stakeholders and sister hotels
