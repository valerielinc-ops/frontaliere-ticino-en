State-of-the-art robotic therapy for musculoskeletal and geriatric rehabilitation, surrounded by beautiful parkland. The first gerontotraumatological rehabilitation department in Switzerland. The environment at the Dussnang rehabilitation clinic also benefits the approximately 330 employees. Here, the makers mentality and open exchange lead to technologishcer innovation in therapy and long-term satisfaction. That is how we move forward together. Here, the family culture and space facilitate progress. Develop state-of-the-art therapies as flexibly as your job and career.

You can look forward to this

  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: treatment of orthopaedic and geriatric patients in close cooperation with doctors, nurses and other therapists.
  • Team spirit and experience: Working in a diverse and experienced physiotherapy team with an excellent team spirit and strong cohesion.
  • Career opportunities: Exciting development opportunities, such as the training of students, participation in rehak coordination or a specialist specialization with the option of a specialist responsibility.
  • Unique work environment: Opportunity to work in rehabilitation that covers the entire treatment pathway – from inpatient to outpatient follow-up care.

  • Completed training in Physiotherapy: A recognised diploma in Physiotherapy (foreign diplomas with SRC recognition are welcome).

  • Empathy: The ability to empathize with the patient’s needs and feelings and support them in the treatment process.
  • Motivation and support: Joy in motivating patients and helping them achieve their therapeutic goals.
  • Language skills: Good knowledge of German in words and writing (at least level B2).
