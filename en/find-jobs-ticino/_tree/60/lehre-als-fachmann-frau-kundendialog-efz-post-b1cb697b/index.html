Full description

Are you enthusiastic about daily contact with people, communicative and helpful? Then training at Contact Center Post is exactly right for you! During the three-year initial vocational training, you will acquire the skills needed for a successful entry into the world of customer communication.

Information about the application process: For this position, in addition to the application dossier, a brief self-assessment of personality and working style is collected. After submitting your application, we first check whether the basic requirements for the position are met and all required information is available. If these requirements are met, you will receive a link to the self-assessment by email. The dossier and your self-assessment will be taken into account jointly for the assessment.

Your training

  • You are the first point of contact for all telephone and written enquiries from our customers.
  • At Contact Center Post, you advise customers, help them solve their problems and competently answer their questions.
  • You work in a varied and flexible way across various information and communication channels.
  • You gain insight into the various areas of Post and thus get to know the work processes, services and products.
  • After the introductory phase, you will soon also be able to provide competent information in another national language.

What you bring

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference. You should meet the following requirements:

  • You have completed the upper or middle level of primary school with good to very good results.
  • You enjoy interacting with people.
  • You want to further develop your language skills.
  • Your quick comprehension and motivation to learn new things set you apart.
  • You are communicative, reliable, and discreet.
