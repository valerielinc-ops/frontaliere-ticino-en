Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2450 employees, including 245 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

The Energy Solutions Directorate develops global offers combining consulting, design, execution, maintenance and operation. It is also the single entry point for major projects, energy efficiency and automation, electrical engineering, facility management and innovation.

Mission

  • Planning, monitoring and ensuring the establishment of facilities for connection of various buildings to remote heating for renewable energy
  • Performing indoor facility surveys, dimensioning and monitoring work
  • Prepare plans and diagrams of technical installations
  • Manage calls for tenders and requests for building authorization
  • Verify the adequacy of the projects with the specifications, budget and deadlines for implementation
  • Assist and collaborate with engineers in the thermal projects of heating plants, cold and hot industrial installations.

Profile

  • SWC of projector in building technology, installer in heating or equivalent training, e.g. CVCS, or a diploma in ES Technician;
  • First experience in project implementation and monitoring;
  • Knowledge of energy systems and construction actors;
  • Excellent sense of communication and comfortable in the negotiations.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success."
