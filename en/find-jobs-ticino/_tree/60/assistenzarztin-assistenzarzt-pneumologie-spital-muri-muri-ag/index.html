Full description

The Pneumology of the Hospital Muri is newly recognized by the SIWF as a training center for Pneumology category C (1 year). For our first training position we are looking for 1. January 2027 a colleague who wants to learn ambulatory pulmonology in its entirety – personally supervised in a one-to-one ratio. Our department serves more than 1,600 patients every year, with its own lung function laboratory, outpatient sleep diagnostics, endoscopy and consultancy services.

Their tasks

  • Own pneumological consultation hour
  • Implementation and diagnosis of extended lung function with body plethysmography, diffusion capacity, FeNO and methacholine provocation, spiroergometry, 6-minute walking test and allergological prick testing
  • Detection of cardiorespiratory polygraphs and initiation and follow-up of CPAP and NIV therapies, long-term oxygen therapy and home ventilation
  • Flexible bronchoscopy with bronchoalveolar lavage and biopsy as well as radiosonographically controlled transbronchial biopsy (rEBUS) – initially assistive, with growing experience in own interventions
  • Thoracic sonography, pleural puncture and thoracic drainage
  • Pulmonary consultants in hospitalized patients
  • Assistance in emergency practice with ten services, excluding night services
  • Cooperation in SGP-certified ambulatory pulmonary rehabilitation

Your profile

  • Swiss or MEBEKO recognised medical diploma
  • Several years of training in general internal medicine, ideally with completed basic training or specialist title
  • Objective Specialist title Pneumology or pneumology as a module of your postgraduate training in General Internal Medicine, in the curriculum GP/GP or hospital internist/spital internist
  • Enjoyment of outpatient medicine, independent work and interprofessional cooperation
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Your opportunity Look forward to a structured and practical further education in a committed, collegial team. You will be personally supervised by the head of the training centre, carefully incorporated in the tandem and supported in your professional development by regular location meetings and at least four workplace-based assessments per year. Four hours of structured training per week, the daily X-ray meeting with radiology, the pneumological case colloquium as well as the Journal Club, the ILD colloquium and the interdisciplinary thoracic oncology tumor board together with the pneumology of the Canton Hospital Baden offer you a wide range of opportunities to deepen your knowledge and contribute your own cases.

Their advantages

  • Holidays: You benefit from holidays between 25 and 30 working days (depending on age) and 13 paid holidays. For this you have the possibility to buy 5 holiday days.
  • Working hours: The company’s normal working hours for a full-time workload are 42 hours per week (excluding doctors). The annual working time applies, which ensures the highest possible flexibility in the organization of working time. Service requests are implemented where possible and part-time employment is possible in many functions – also in management positions.
  • Education and further education: Your personal and professional development is important to us. We support participation in internal and external further education and training through generous participation in the form of working hours and/or course costs.
  • Food: In our staff restaurant – nominated with the “Best of Swiss Gastro” award – you can enjoy menus from the market-fresh cuisine at very attractive conditions.
  • Arrival/parking: Employees of Spital Muri benefit from cheap parking. The car park is located on the hospital area. If you come to work by public transport, bike, motorcycle or on foot, we will reward you with a monthly eco-bonus.
  • Social insurance: We attach importance to adequate social benefits. For night, weekend and holiday work, we pay you fair allowances. We insure your salary at the pension fund Profond without coordination deduction (excluding doctors). The wages of doctors are insured by the VSAO preventive foundation.

Pensum: 100%
