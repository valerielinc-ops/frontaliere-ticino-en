Clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy

For our day clinic we are looking for you immediately or by arrangement as The Psychiatric Services Aargau AG (PDAG) is responsible for the high-quality medical, therapeutic and nursing treatment and care of mentally ill people in the Canton of Aargau. They employ around 1,600 people in over 50 professions. Clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy 5210 Windy Workload: 60 % 60 %

Over the area

In the clinic for child and adolescent psychiatry, psychosomatics and psychotherapy (Clinic Director Dr. med.) Angelo Bernardon) are treated children and adolescents up to the age of 18. The integrated treatment offer of the clinic includes an outpatient area with a central ambulatory as well as four ambulatory facilities distributed across the canton, a consultancy and liaison service and an inpatient area with an acute care unit, two psychotherapy units, a day clinic and a clinic school. In the teams, clinical specialists from the fields of medicine, psychology, nursing and social pedagogy work and in the clinic school teachers and curative school teachers. The Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry, Psychosomatics and Psychotherapy is a teaching hospital of the University of Zurich and a certified continuing education institution category A.

Tasks

  • They offer individual and group therapies for children and adolescents with mental illness
  • They implement ergotherapeutic treatment (focusing on children and adolescents) based on evidence and adapt their interventions according to scientific developments.
  • You are part of the interprofessional treatment team and actively participate in the implementation of the therapeutic goals within the framework of the overall treatment
  • They implement the ergotherapeutic treatment within the treatment concept competently and support the further development with their specialist knowledge
  • You participate in interprofessional cooperation, including participation in patient-related case meetings, rapports, team meetings and supervision
  • You create a technically sound documentation

Profile

  • Dipl. Occupational therapist (BSc, FH, HF) or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland Experience in ergotherapeutic work with mentally ill children and adolescents
  • Strong social skills in dealing with mentally ill children and adolescents
  • Ability to think and act in a networked, conceptual and innovative way
  • They work independently, structured and characterized by flexibility, teamwork and resilience
