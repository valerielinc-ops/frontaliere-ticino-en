Full description

Administration / Assistance / Substantive processing Busy: 50% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site The global Specialty Lines CH & International business (SpL CH & Int.) is an essential part of our strategic orientation. In our role as a global specialist, we are consistently expanding our strong market position nationally and internationally, taking advantage of selected growth opportunities and shaping our offering in a sustainable and profit-oriented manner. You are an organizational talent, like to communicate and keep an overview even when different topics come together at the same time? In this versatile 50% position, you work at the interface of internal communication, executive support and internal events. You ensure that information is processed professionally, events work smoothly and important organizational topics progress reliably.

What you do

  • You create and publish intranet news and internal messages and support other internal communication topics.
  • You accompany the communication around new functions and organizational changes and work closely with Communication & Branding.
  • You plan and coordinate town halls, offsites and other internal events and ensure professional preparation and follow-up.
  • You support in the executive environment in the organization of meetings and appointments as well as in the preparation of relevant documents.
  • You create meeting minutes, keep an eye on agreed measures and pendencies and ensure a reliable follow-up.
  • You work closely with the assistant, take on mutual deputies and coordinate organizational issues efficiently.
  • You support CHRO in selected organizational, administrative and HR-related tasks and are flexible in ad hoc topics.

What you bring

  • You bring experience in assistance, corporate services, communication or a comparable coordination function and want to use your strengths in many ways.
  • Organizing is your strength: You bring people, topics and dates together and keep track of changing priorities.
  • You like to formulate and confident in style and have a feeling for preparing information clearly, appealingly and appropriately for the target group.
  • You work independently, structured and reliable, with an eye for detail and the aspiration to actively advance topics.
  • You value trusting cooperation, support others with pleasure and deal discreetly and professionally with confidential information and executive topics.
  • You are happy to use digital work and communication tools to make organization and cooperation efficient.
  • With your very good German and English skills in words and writing, you bring people and topics together and actively shape internal communication.
