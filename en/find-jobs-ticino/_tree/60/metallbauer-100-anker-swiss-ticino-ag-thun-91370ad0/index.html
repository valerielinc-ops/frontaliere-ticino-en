Metalworkers 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people – now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by agreement we are looking for a temporary or permanent employment of a metalworker EFZ

Your challenge

  • Manufacture and assembly of steel and metal structures
  • Welding and manufacturing of assemblies
  • Independent work based on workshop plans
  • Quality control and preparation of components for assembly
  • Participation in the optimization of processes and processes

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training as a metalworker EFZ
  • Enjoyment of challenging metal construction work

  • Modern infrastructure and high-quality work equipment
  • Short decision paths and a family environment

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
