What awaits you.

Join our team and leave your mark on a new major and strategic railway line (> CHF 1 billion in investment), in the heart of a developing region. At the SBB, we continuously transform and modernize the infrastructure for fluid connections, point trains and welcoming stations. Following the completion of the preliminary project, we are strengthening our team to achieve a key step: the preparation of the investigation file. We are looking for a project manager in charge of the transversal domain. Your mission: Pilot the transversal subjects of the project such as rolling stock, conditions of access to the network, security to road/rail interfaces and technical equipment of tunnels. Why would you like this job?

  • A concrete impact: you contribute to reliable, safe and sustainable mobility for an entire region, with an impact on national traffic.
  • The scope and the challenge: you take up the stakes of a flagship project, technically demanding (a speed of 15 minutes on a 5% rack-free ramp, ETCS Level 2, etc.).
  • A stimulating framework: you are evolving in a multidisciplinary team, with cutting-edge railway technologies and real development opportunities.

What you can move.

  • As a project leader, you define the functional and technical requirements of rolling stock for the new line and then ensure their consistency (including ramp performance, braking, sound level and compatibility with ETCS L2). You translate them into specifications, ensure traceability and coordinate their evolution with stakeholders.
  • You define and negotiate the conditions for access to the network: expected performance, speeds, paths and capacity, compatibility with the signaling and control system (ETCS), as well as operating and maintenance constraints.
  • In collaboration with internal services and emergency services, you help to develop response scenarios and organize tunnel exercises. You also prepare the records necessary for acceptance.
  • You coordinate the interfaces between the various fields, including infrastructure, signalling, energy, operation, rolling stock, civil engineering and telecommunications.
  • You develop and maintain relationships of trust with partners and stakeholders, both internally and externally.
  • You control objects placed under your responsibility by ensuring that costs, deadlines, quality and safety are respected. You develop and follow study and execution plans, while limiting the impact on the daily lives of clients and residents.

What you bring.

  • Thanks to your experience, you have developed excellent communication skills, a proactive mindset oriented solutions and natural leadership that promotes team collaboration.
  • You easily lead and coordinate various partners within multidisciplinary groups and you can unite around your project.
  • To achieve your mission, you have mastered the studies and conduct of work, including AIS phases, planning, and budgeting and management. You also have strong technical knowledge related to rolling stock (performance, braking), tunnel technical equipment and road/rail interface safety.
  • You bring at least 5 years of experience in railway projects, ideally with transverse pilotage.
  • You benefit from a graduate degree in engineering or architect EPF/HES/University, accompanied by a proven experience in managing complex projects in the field of construction.
  • You master French (minimum level C1), a good knowledge of German is an asset.
