Full description

Clinic Wysshölzli is a leading psychiatric and psychotherapeutic specialist clinic for women with addictions and eating disorders. Our main location in the Oberaargau, Canton of Bern, in a quiet location convinces with wonderful nature and proximity to Bern and Olten. Our ambulatorium in the city of Bern is centrally located near the train station. The clinic offers 37 inpatient and numerous outpatient treatment places. Allocations are made from all cantons of German-speaking Switzerland.

We are looking for our ambulatorium in Bern immediately or by appointment:

Psychologist / psychotherapist 60 - 100% of your tasks

  • psychotherapeutic treatment of outpatients: in individual and group setting, with

Broad diagnostic spectrum and focus on treatment of eating disorders

  • Conducting networking, couple and family talks
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with established specialists and external institutions
  • Reliable and timely documentation including reporting
  • Conduct of preliminary discussions on indication

Your profile

  • completed or already advanced psychotherapy training
  • Enjoy independent psychotherapeutic work and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Clinical experience in the ambulatory sector
  • open, resilient, initiative and team capable personality

Your opportunity

  • an interesting, responsible and versatile activity in a modern and

Manageable operation

  • independent work in a motivated and interprofessional team
  • Supervision and training opportunities

We look forward to your application. Dr. phil. Liliane Wenger, Head of Treatment or Barbara Ammann, Director will be happy to provide you with more information on tel. 062 510 58 00. Please apply via the online portal on our website or send your application to personal@wysshoelzli.ch.
