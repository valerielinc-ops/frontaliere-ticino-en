Put to the Point

Do you want to use your expertise to make a decisive contribution to the security and stability of complex IT infrastructures? With us, you take responsibility for central directory and certificate services and actively shape their further development.

  • Date of entry: 01.05.2027
  • By agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 609-20854
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3014 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Operate and develop Microsoft Active Directory and certificate services
  • Configure, maintain and monitor Windows server systems
  • Ensure safe and stable operation of the responsible systems
  • Plan, implement and document system migrations
  • Automate recurring operational tasks through scripts
  • Implement technical safety requirements and check their compliance

This makes you unique

  • Completed training in computer science (university, higher vocational training or equivalent qualification) with relevant professional experience
  • Several years of experience in systems engineering, administration and operation of Microsoft-based IT infrastructures
  • In-depth knowledge of Microsoft Active Directory, ideally complemented by experience with certificate services and managing privileged user accounts
  • Experience in analyzing and correcting technical failures as well as knowledge of automation with PowerShell or comparable scripting languages
  • Structured and solution-oriented working methods and enjoyment of working together in interdisciplinary teams
  • Knowledge of agile working methods and IT service management is an advantage
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and good knowledge of English

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

How much (B)IT is in you?

We – the Federal Office for Informatics and Telecommunications (BIT) – are the future-oriented and agile office. Here, curious minds, ambitious makers and innovative thought leaders develop a wide range of digital solutions for Switzerland. We support and promote mobile and location-independent work. In this way, our team players can design their work flexibly and individually where possible - for the compatibility of family and work. In addition, we maintain an open you culture at BIT and promote you and your future with a broad range of training and further education.
