Full description

Portrait We are a medium-sized, innovative institution that accompanies the care and accompaniment of young people and children of 4-25 years. Among various offers, we run a special elementary school with the promotion focus on behavioral abnormalities. We are implementing attachment pedagogy and trauma pedagogy. Load-bearing down-to-earth stability is necessary to perform the work.

Tasks

  • Individual support and support of a child with special care needs in everyday school life
  • Building a trusting, appreciative and sustainable relationship
  • Support for emotional and social development and self-regulation
  • Assistance and de-escalation in challenging situations and conflicts
  • Promotion of self-employment, ownership and social skills
  • Support for participation in teaching and school activities
  • Providing security, orientation and clear daily structures
  • Close cooperation and regular exchange with teachers, school curative education and other specialists
  • Observation and documentation of developments and specific occurrences

Requirements

  • Enjoyment of the individual accompaniment and promotion of children
  • Strong social skills, empathy and an appreciative attitude
  • High psychological resilience, patience and assertiveness
  • Ability to act calmly, calmly and solution-oriented even in difficult and conflict-ridden situations
  • Security in setting clear boundaries and consistent and respectful action
  • Knowledge of de-escalation and dealing with crisis situations is an advantage
  • Self-employed, responsible and reliable working methods
  • Flexibility and willingness to adapt to changing situations
  • Good communication and team skills as well as willingness to work closely with teachers and specialists
  • Reflection and openness for professional exchange

(- Completed education in the pedagogical, socio-pedagogical or supervising area of advantage) (- Experience in dealing with children with challenging behavior and special needs beneficial)

We offer

Temporary employment until 31.07.2026 | Possibility of extension likely

With us, you can expect varied, meaningful and responsible work in an attractive working environment with fair employment conditions. They have the opportunity to accompany, promote and sustainably strengthen a child individually in their development.

As part of a dedicated, interdisciplinary team, you will actively participate in the further development and implementation of our attachment and trauma pedagogical concept. We also offer you the opportunity to complete a basic course in trauma education.

Regular team and fallup visions with our consultant psychiatrist as well as pedagogical coaching support you in your professional and personal development and offer valuable support in the demanding everyday school life.

We attach great importance to an appreciative, respectful and collegial cooperation, mutual support and an open professional exchange.
