Full description

Missions

  • You organize the team to meet the goals of quantity, quality and time
  • You coordinate the activities of your sector with other departments to ensure the smooth running of production (logistics, quality,...)
  • You distribute employees to positions and partially manage the planning (short term absence management, distribution of positions)
  • You take into account the instructions of the workshop and forward the information at the end of the post.
  • You organize the replacement of employees in case of emergencies, including in other areas.
  • You replace the team leader in case of absence and animate the shopfloors
  • You take an active part in the training of employees and share your knowledge with them.
  • You check the conformity of products manufactured using checklists at control and assembly stations and rework the products if necessary
  • You manage anomalies (processing of non-compliant parts, quality warnings, problems with means of production, etc.) and inform the team leader
  • You perform pre-diagnostics in case of malfunction using the available documentation and participate in the maintenance of the means of production
  • You ensure that workstations are clean and orderly according to 5S principles.
  • You replace employees in case of absence or emergency and support them in case of technical problems
  • You assign the employees to the different teams (management of absences, planning) and support the team leader in managing the employees (conflicts, identification of strengths and weaknesses) Profile
  • You have an CFC for technician, mechanic, mechanic or equivalent training
  • You benefit from a minimum experience of 5 years in production
  • You have a good knowledge of reading manufacturing plans
  • You have ideally received leadership training and experience in team coordination
  • You are fluent in French and have knowledge of German or English
  • You have good knowledge of MES (Manufacturing Executive System) and InfoLN systems as well as Microsoft Office software
  • Your personality is characterized by your versatility, your professional awareness, your autonomy, your ability to work as a team as well as your punctuality, your flexibility, your sense of communication and organisation Our offer Can we motivate you to take on a key role in a family, dynamic and independent business?
