# SUPSI Open Rank Professor (Full or Associate Professor) at the Institute of Systems and Applied Electronics

The University of Applied Sciences and Arts of Southern Switzerland (SUPSI) invites applications for a position as

SUPSI Open Rank Professor (Full or Associate Professor)

at the

Institute of Systems and Applied Electronics (ISEA)

within the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI), based in Lugano. The position carries an employment rate between 80% and 100%, with a starting date to be agreed upon.

Context and Objectives of the Position

The Institute of Systems and Applied Electronics (ISEA) of the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI) at SUPSI conducts applied research, technology transfer, and education in the field of electronics, ranging from electronic design to the development of advanced embedded and cyber-physical systems. Through close collaboration with companies, public institutions, and national and international academic partners, the Institute develops technological solutions with a high application impact and contributes to innovation within the industrial sector. As part of its strategic development, ISEA intends to strengthen and expand its scientific expertise through the appointment of a Professor capable of developing an original, distinctive, and sustainable research line, while contributing to the scientific and educational growth of the Institute. The position is intended for candidates with solid scientific experience, a strong aptitude for collaboration, and a vocation for establishing strategic partnerships.

Areas of particular interest include, but are not limited to

  • Advanced control systems, including approaches based on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven methodologies;
  • Electronics, sensing, and control for robotics and autonomous systems;
  • Power electronics, drives, and electrified systems;
  • Intelligent energy systems and technologies supporting the energy transition;
  • Edge AI and machine learning for embedded systems and resource-constrained platforms.

Applications in other emerging areas of electronics, automation, and cyber-physical systems, as well as interdisciplinary profiles capable of creating new synergies with the expertise available within ISEA, DTI, and SUPSI, will also be given due consideration. The ability to propose and develop activities of strategic relevance to the Institute will constitute an important element of the evaluation.

Activities and Responsibilities

  • Develop and consolidate a scientific area of strategic relevance for ISEA;
  • build and coordinate a research group, contributing to the scientific and professional development of its team members;
  • conceive, acquire, and lead applied research and development projects funded through competitive programmes and collaborations with industrial partners;
  • develop high-quality scientific and technological outputs and promote their valorisation and transfer;
  • establish and maintain collaborations with companies, public institutions, and national and international academic partners;
  • contribute to the strategic development of the Institute and to synergies with other areas of DTI and SUPSI;
  • carry out teaching activities at Bachelor's, Master's and/or continuing education level and supervise theses and projects;
  • represent SUPSI in networks, partnerships, and scientific, industrial, and institutional contexts.

Applications must include

  • A cover letter in Italian or English outlining the candidate’s motivation (maximum two pages);
  • A complete curriculum vitae in Italian or English, including scientific and technological outputs, acquired research and development projects (indicating the role held and the corresponding funding volume), university-level teaching and supervision activities, as well as any honours, awards, and scientific or professional recognitions;

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
