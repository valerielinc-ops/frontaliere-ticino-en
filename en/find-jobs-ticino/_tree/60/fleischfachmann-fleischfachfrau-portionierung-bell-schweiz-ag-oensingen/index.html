Full description

Do you want to work for a company that stands for quality, sustainability, tradition and enjoyment? Are you a responsible and reliable personality and enjoy working as a team player: in a large company? Then we are looking for you as a meat specialist for our portioning department for the production of products for the catering and self-service counter. In this role, you are jointly responsible for the entire process in the portioning and gastro department at our location in Oensingen.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Job: from now on
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Dünnernstrasse 31 4702 Oensingen

Tasks

  • Professional cutting and portioning of our products according to cut specifications
  • Manufacture of products for the catering sector (Charbonnade, Plätzli, etc.)
  • Quality check of raw materials and ingredients used
  • Assistance in ensuring full traceability
  • Compliance with process, quality and hygiene regulations

Requirements

  • Completed basic training as a meat specialist EFZ/EBA
  • Good physical condition as well as an independent and forward working method
  • Flexibility in terms of working hours and used to working in cool temperatures
  • High quality and hygiene awareness
  • Technical understanding of plant operation
  • Good knowledge of German

This is what we offer

  • Work-life balance: A balanced work-life balance is important to us. You benefit from at least 25 holiday days and a transparent, electronic time recording. So you can flexibly compensate for any overtime with additional free days.
  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: freshly prepared food in our staff restaurant, a lunch menu incl. Soup and salad, water & tea and free fruits as well as high-quality products at greatly reduced prices await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport, attractive subsidies for tickets and sufficient parking in the car park.
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive further education offers, high-quality training in the Coop Education Center and a wide range of personal development opportunities.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management, attendance bonus, discounted Reka checks, exclusive employee discounts and subsidized fitness subscriptions – for health and leisure!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments at our departmental event and strengthen the team spirit during the company run – celebrate, move and make contacts together!
  • Market-friendly wages and benefits: Secure your future with our above-average retirement savings, receive anniversary bonuses, Christmas bonuses and attractive employee referral bonuses. In addition, you receive the personal discount card from Coop and we cover two thirds of your pension fund contributions!
  • Family-friendly employer: Make your working hours flexible with part-time models, work in professional work clothes and experience an inclusive work environment where diversity is valued and encouraged!
