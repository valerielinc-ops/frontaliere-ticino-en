Full description

For our customer from the Federal Administration we are looking for a:n experienced:n project manager:in IT. In this versatile role, you take over the operational control and coordination of demanding IT projects and ensure an efficient implementation of the defined project goals.

Your tasks

  • Support and competent deputy of the internal project manager within the defined tasks, competences and responsibilities
  • Planning, control and coordination of sub-projects across all project phases
  • Monitoring project progress, milestones, resources and costs and ensuring compliance with defined project objectives
  • Identification, evaluation and tracking of project risks, dependencies and open measures as well as early implementation of appropriate countermeasures
  • Ensuring structured task planning, prioritization and consistent tracking of project activities in Jira
  • Coordination and coordination with internal and external stakeholders as well as ensuring a transparent and timely flow of information
  • Application and further development of the predefined agile working methods and project management processes
  • Creation, maintenance and assurance of a complete and comprehensible project documentation in Confluence and other planned systems
  • Preparation and documentation of relevant project information, delivery results, decisions and status reports
  • Support for the continuous optimisation of project processes and ensuring efficient cooperation between the stakeholders involved

Your profile

  • Higher vocational training (HF) or university of applied sciences degree in computer science, business informatics, business administration or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in IT project management as well as in the independent control and coordination of subprojects
  • In-depth knowledge of classical and agile project management methods as well as experience in the application of structured project management processes
  • Very good knowledge in dealing with Jira and Confluence for project planning, task management and documentation
  • Experience in project controlling, especially in monitoring resources, costs, deadlines, milestones and risks
  • Strong organisational and coordinative skills as well as experience in dealing with different stakeholders
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method with high personal responsibility, assertiveness and analytical thinking skills
  • Very good communication skills as well as the ability to prepare and communicate complex issues appropriate to the addressee
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, good English is advantageous
  • Clean Leumund is mandatory
  • Experience in public administration projects or in a security-relevant environment is beneficial
  • Certifications in HERMES, IPMA, PRINCE2 or agile project management methods are advantageous
