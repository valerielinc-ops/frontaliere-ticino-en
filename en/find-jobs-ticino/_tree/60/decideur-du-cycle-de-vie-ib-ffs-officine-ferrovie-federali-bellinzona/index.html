What we offer you.

Give shape to the optimal management of our plants for our customers! As a Life Cycle Decision Maker Genio Civile, you help define the strategies for renewal, extension and maintenance of civil engineering works throughout the asset life cycle. With an integrated and long-term vision, ensure the preservation of the value of the railway infrastructure and respect for the highest standards of safety, availability and efficiency for our customers. On our website you will also find our benefits, including child checks, free AG and advantageous conditions for travelling abroad, also for your family on our website at the Benefit page . What you can move.

  • You are responsible in the region of maintaining the substance (including planning requirements, defining measures priorities) for the works of the civil genius.
  • Incarichi, accompany and follow preliminary technical studies contributing to the definition of the best variant after involving experts.
  • In collaboration with the I-VU-UEW "Organization Units Maintenance Industry Surveillance", prepare the planning of requirements (including the definition of priorities for IR investments and ER maintenance).
  • In close coordination with network management and rayon-manager, define the necessary measures to be implemented at the optimal time through a global vision of routes/nodes.
  • Please increase I-VU-UEW for the monitoring of plants within the framework of OLA and provide the necessary resources and framework conditions.
  • Take care of regional risk management for the type of plants you have.

What you bring with you.

  • You have excellent technical knowledge in civil engineering and, ideally, a professional experience in the entire railway system.
  • You have a strong propensity to make decisions.
  • As a generalist, it is easy to question the evaluations and recommendations of specialists in an objective and credible manner.
  • You are in possession of a university degree (University, ETH, FH), in the construction sector, or you have an equivalent traineeship in the rail field with an affinity for technical topics.
  • Excellent communication skills, assertivity and organizational talent are among your strengths.
  • Italian or German language and excellent knowledge of the other language at least B2 level.
