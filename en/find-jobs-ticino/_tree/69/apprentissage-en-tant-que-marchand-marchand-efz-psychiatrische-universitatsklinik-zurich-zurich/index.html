Full description

The Psychiatric University Hospital Zurich (PUK) is one of the leading psychiatric clinics in Switzerland. With more than 2,500 employees, we are committed to the holistic treatment and care of people with mental illnesses every day. As a university clinic, we combine clinical expertise with research and teaching – in a working environment characterized by interdisciplinary cooperation, trust and personal responsibility. 100% The Directorate of Care, Therapies and Social Work brings together various professional groups that accompany and support our patients on their way to mental health. The care provides professional care in everyday life, the therapies promote individual skills and resources, and the social work supports personal, family and social challenges. In order for these diverse tasks to go hand in hand, reliable administration in the background is required. For the three-year apprenticeship we are looking for 2nd in our Management Care, Therapies and Social Work in Zurich. August 2027 motivated learners. Their tasks

  • Complete a wide range of administrative and organizational tasks in various departments, also outside the management of care, therapies and social work
  • Meeting preparation and organisation of events and internal events
  • Preparation of presentations, statistics and other documents
  • Recording, processing and storing data and documents in compliance with data protection requirements
  • Support for appointment coordination

Your profile

  • Joy of contact with people and cooperation in a team
  • Reliable, careful and independent work
  • Readiness to learn, initiative and openness to new things
  • Good verbal and written expression
