Full description

Clinic for Psychosis and Dependence

At the University Psychiatric Center (UPZ) in Bern, you will accompany people with psychotic and affective diseases through acute crisis situations at the open psychosis station in Münsingen (OPM). Especially at night, your presence, your peace of mind and your professional actions are particularly important: You provide security, recognize changes at an early stage and are a reliable contact person for our patients. You work independently and at the same time closely with your team – with an attitude that is characterized by appreciation, empathy and encounter at eye level.

Your tasks

  • You actively participate in the night on the ward and provide professional nursing care for patients
  • You observe the mental and physical state and recognize relevant changes or developments early on
  • In acute crises and challenging behavior, you intervene appropriately and use your psychiatric expertise in a targeted manner.
  • You carry out prescribed medications and nursing-medical measures professionally and ensure the necessary care during the night
  • You document your observations, interventions and relevant events in a comprehensible way and ensure the flow of information in the treatment team with a careful handover
  • You work closely with the responsible medical and nursing specialists and actively contribute your observations and assessments to the further treatment
  • You participate in the further development of the station concept and contribute your experiences and ideas on the quality of psychiatric care

Your profile

  • You have a diploma as a nurse HF/FH or an equivalent foreign diploma recognised in Switzerland
  • You have experience in psychiatric care or bring experience from somatics and would like to develop professionally in psychiatry
  • You work independently, responsibly and calmly and deliberately even in demanding situations during the night
  • You are interested in complex psychiatric issues and meet people with different clinical pictures openly and unbiased
  • You communicate clearly and appreciatively and can also create a professional relationship in demanding situations
  • You work reliably and carefully and appreciate working with an interprofessional team

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
