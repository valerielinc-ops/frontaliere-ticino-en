Full description

Tecnico/a test PCBA*

Your responsibilities

  • Perform the SMD/THT assembly of electronic cards (PCBA) according to technical instructions and IPC standards.
  • Run automatic and functional tests with Flying Probe, ICT, functional simulators and cabinet testing.
  • Check the compliance of the cards to technical specifications and document test results and non-compliance.
  • Apply fault search on cards and electronic subgroups and support corrective actions.
  • Collaborate with quality and with the line manager to the continuous improvement of production processes.
  • Your profile *
  • Technical diploma in electronics or electronic AFC.
  • 3 years experience in a similar role in the production environment and testing of electronic components.
  • Operational experience on automatic testing systems, functional simulators and cabinet testing.
  • Readability of electrical schematics and use of basic measuring instruments (multimeter, oscilloscope). IPC-A-610 certification is a plus.
  • Precise, methodical and able to work in semi-autonomy on diagnosis and testing activities; good attitude to collaboration.
  • Italian native language
  • English is a plus.

About us

AGIE CHARMILLES, inventor of EDM technology (electrohesion), is recognized as a brand of excellence and leader in innovation in the field of wire, dip and drilling electroerosion. Its automation solutions are used in the construction of moulds and in the production of high-end components, ensuring the precision, reliability and performance worthy of the Swiss tradition UNITED MACHINING is one of the world's leading suppliers of complete solutions for manufacturers of precision components and tools and for the mold construction industry. The portfolio includes milling, electro-erosion, laser texturization, laser micro-processing and additive manufacturing. In addition, the division offers spindles, automation, tools and digitalized solutions, supported by a customer service and unparalleled assistance.

UNITED MACHINING includes AGIE CHARMILLES, CHARMILLES, MIKRON MILL, LIECHTI, STEP TEC and SYSTEM 3R.

UNITED MACHINING is based in Biel, Switzerland, and currently has over 3,000 employees in more than 50 locations worldwide.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
