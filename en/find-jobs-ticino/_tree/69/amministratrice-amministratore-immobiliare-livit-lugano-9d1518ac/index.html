Full description

Real estate management Lugano | Degree of employment: 100%

Are you an open and ambitious person? Are you interested in working in a modern context in the heart of the real estate industry? With an exemplary customer orientation, from Livit you will take care of the needs of owners and landlords, contributing significantly to establish a lasting relationship with customers based on trust and to promote a positive image of the company. Do you have the right motivation to face the challenges of today and tomorrow and to make a difference in the real estate world? Then you're in the right place at Livit's. Your personal contribution is valuable to us.

His duties

  • Responsibility for the complete management of a portfolio of properties owned mainly residential
  • Preparation and control of the annual budget, including the forecasts and control and accounting of creditors
  • Management and education of its employees (including evaluations, definition of annual objectives and monitoring of their achievement)
  • Support and assistance to tenants, owners and facility management
  • Responsibility for inspection and maintenance of property as well as periodic assessments of state and reporting conditions
  • Management of adjustments to lease contracts, modification of legions and adjustments to indexed contracts
  • Supervision of the current plague policy and advice to owners on the structure of market legions
  • Control and approval of credit bills, management accounts and additional charges

Your profile

  • Business apprentice with CFC or equivalent training
  • Federal real estate manager (obtained or in progress) and several years of professional experience (3-5 years) in the real estate sector
  • Good knowledge of the right to lease (CO, CCS and LEF) and basic accounting knowledge
  • Italian and/or German speakers; good knowledge of their other national languages is of advantage
  • Digital affinity and ability to actively participate in changes
  • Competence, customer orientation, partnership and results orientation
  • Mentality and entrepreneurial spirit
