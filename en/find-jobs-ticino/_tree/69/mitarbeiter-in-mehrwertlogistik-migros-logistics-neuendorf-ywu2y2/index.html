Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics unites Migros Verteilbetrieb AG and Migros Logistique Romandie SA and provides daily supply to Switzerland. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient. For the value-added logistics department, we are looking for a motivated and responsible personality after agreement.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (Neuendorf), Neustrasse 49, 4623 Neuendorf

Important information

Working hours: Monday to Friday from 6.00 a.m. to 3.30 p.m., optionally until 4.15 p.m. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Cooperation in the multiple packaging plants
  • Independent processing and marking of packaging contracts
  • Installation and assembly of displays and sales assistants
  • Quality control of products (packaging, shelf life, etc.)
  • General storage and cleaning work
  • Support of the plant management and optional takeover of Stv. Plant management in absence
  • Vacation forklift activities

Requirements

  • 2-3 years of advantage: Logistics
  • Completed basic or further education: advantageous in the logistical sector
  • Recognised forklift (transverse seat and push mast)
  • Independent and flexible personality with good organizational talent, who enjoys learning new things
  • Good physical condition and resilience
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Central location: Our locations are directly connected to the motorway or public transport
  • Occupational pension provision: Migros Pension Fund benefits are clearly above the law. All are retired at age 65
  • Fair wages: We pay a fair and fair wage. The basis is our personnel management and compensation system
  • Holidays: With us all have at least 5 weeks holidays (depending on age or service even 6 or 7 weeks) and can buy holidays.
  • Cumulus Points: MVB AG employees can collect additional Cumulus Points for purchases in Migro’s stores and stores

Contatto

Application and contact Ramona Kobel

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.

Grado di occupazione: 80-100 %
