Together we define new standards and build the future: from us you will not limit yourself to performing tasks, you will work with people who take responsibility, keep high standards and have the courage to walk new ways.   • More years of professional experience as a director
  • German-Italian bilingual, with excellent oral and written knowledge
  • High social skills and strong communication skills

  • Affinity with LFA themes
  • Percentage of use: 60% We believe in peer collaboration, mutual trust and people willing to take responsibility. The different perspectives help us develop better solutions, both for our customers and for us as a team.
