Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Department of Psychiatry, with more than 1,300 employees, is responsible for a three-pronged mission, dealing with the treatment of mental disorders, training and research. Thus, the department's mission is to effectively detect and treat increasingly frequent and polymorphic pathologies in the respect of patients and their relatives. Organised in 6 services, the department has a very wide and diverse range of care, including hospital care, multidisciplinary outpatient care in different ways and intensities, organised according to the principles of community psychiatry, as well as specialized programmes and units. Quality integrated clinical and structured education is provided by the department for doctors in training, raising their awareness of the psychological, biological and social dimensions. The department also provides structured theoretical training in the 3 main models of psychotherapy as well as individual and group supervision included in the training curriculum. The department also offers active support for research courses at a high level of quality. Finally, the department seeks to understand the origins of mental disorders using cognitive and affective neurosciences, the true flagship of the Geneva site. To this end, he conducts academic and clinical research oriented to a high level of quality.

Description of post

In view of your course of training in psychiatry and psychotherapy, you integrate a department unit of care and ensure, within a multidisciplinary team, the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic hospital or outpatient follow-up of patients in your unit. In addition to your activity, you benefit from a clinical post-graduate training and structured quality integrated according to the requirements of the FMH for the qualification of specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy. You can also participate in ongoing research activities at the service level. You can also receive an accompaniment to complete a doctoral thesis. An integrated quality training that meets the requirements of the Federation of Swiss Physicians (FMH) which includes both practical training through clinical and theoretical activity in all the fields of psychiatry as well as in the three main axes of psychotherapy. As part of your training, you ensure a tournus within the various departments of the department according to your choice of training courses. This practical and theoretical training is complemented by participation in psychotherapy supervisions and congresses. The department supports all training.

Qualifications

You have a federal degree in medicine or have training deemed equivalent by the Medical Professions Commission of the Federal Office of Public Health (MEBEKO). Experience in the field of psychiatry and/or somatic medicine is an asset. For non-French-speaking candidates, the level of French C1 on the spoken and written test is required. When applying, please attach a certificate certifying your language level.
