Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of structural and interior construction materials. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

For our location in Visp we are looking for 1. January 2027 a service-oriented personality as

Task area

  • Your main tasks include the creation and processing of offers and order releases
  • You are responsible for all telephone requests of our customers (mainly commercial customers from the construction industry)
  • Derived from this, you provide information, make clarifications and, if necessary, take and process customer complaints
  • Further administrative tasks such as taking orders, obtaining offers from suppliers round off the field of activity

Profile

If your heart beats faster in the area of responsibility described above and you have the following competencies, you are our desired candidate:

  • Completed training in the field of plates / parquet / interior construction with commercial further education (advantageous) and / or commercial training with technical background resp. Great affinity towards the construction industry
  • Good IT knowledge as well as an experienced handling of the common MS-Office programs
  • Strong communication skills, customer orientation and a high quality awareness
  • Detailed and team-oriented working method
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, French : good basic knowledge

We offer

  • A challenging, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where the emphasis is on active cooperation and at the same time self-employment
  • Intensive induction is a program, whereby our new colleagues can benefit from product and system training within the company
  • Support of our employees in their professional and personal development
  • Basic wage gross x 13 plus variable wage share
  • Home office possible according to regulations and agreement
  • 5 weeks holiday (6 weeks from 50 years of age) and holiday shopping up to max. 10 days, additional services such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and discounts on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits, free parking

We look forward to your complete application documents in electronic form.
