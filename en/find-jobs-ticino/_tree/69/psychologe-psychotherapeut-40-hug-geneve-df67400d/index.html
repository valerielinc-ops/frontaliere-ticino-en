Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is under the Department of Rehabilitation and Geriatrics and the Department of Clinical Neurosciences. With nearly 1,500 collaborators, the Department of Rehabilitation and Geriatrics (DRG) is a medical centre focused on the elderly and is spread over 5 sites: Trois-Chêne, Loëx, Bellerive, Beau-Séjour and Joli-Mont. It takes care of acute situations, thanks in particular to the emergencies of the Hospital of the Three Oaks, the specific problems related to age, medical rehabilitation and palliative medicine. The Department of Clinical Neurosciences provides care to patients of all ages, children and adults, with neurosurgical, neurological, ophthalmological and oto-rhino-laryngological conditions. Care is tailored to the needs and difficulties of the patient and patient, from the most acute sectors (intermediate care units) to the rehabilitation sectors (neuroreeducation). The Neuroreeducation Service of the University Hospitals of Geneva specializes in the rehabilitation of adult patients with the consequences of acquired brain damage (vascular, traumatic or other). The aim is to provide psychotherapeutic care for patients who have cerebralised in the context of their hospitalization. Interact with the Liaison and Crisis Response Psychiatry Service. Intervene with patient families and care teams to inform and coordinate the psychotherapeutic approach.

Description of post

As part of this position, you ensure psychotherapeutic care for patients who are brainbroiled during hospitalisation, taking into account their psychological needs and their clinical situation. You work closely with the Liaison and Crisis Response Psychiatry Service to ensure interdisciplinary and coordinated management. You also intervene with the families of the cared for, as well as with the care teams, with a view to informing, supporting and coordinating the psychotherapeutic approach.

Qualifications

You have a university degree in psychology. You have post-graduate training in psychotherapy, in progress or completed, ideally cognitive-behavioural and/or systemic orientation. You have knowledge of the supervision of groups of caregivers. Knowledge of neuropsychological diagnosis is an asset. You have a solid experience in the diagnosis, interpretation and management of psychiatric disorders, including mood disorders, anxiety, addictions and behavioural disorders. You also have professional experience with cerebralised adult patients with various cognitive disorders. Autonomous and organized or organized, you know how to manage stressful situations calmly and efficiently. Available and flexible, you can manage priorities and adapt to different situations. You enjoy working in a multidisciplinary team and you can integrate into an interprofessional dynamic. You have a good capacity for synthesis and know how to transmit information in a clear and appropriate way to the patient as well as to the various health professionals and professionals.
