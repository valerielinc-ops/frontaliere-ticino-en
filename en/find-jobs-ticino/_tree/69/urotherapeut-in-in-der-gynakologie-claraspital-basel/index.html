Full description

Dynamics. Dream Team. discipline.

Their tasks

  • Expert advice and urotherapy of predominantly outpatients and their relatives.
  • Implementation of functional diagnostics and conservative forms of therapy for bladder, urinary and pelvic floor complaints.
  • Close interdisciplinary exchange with specialists in urogynecology, nursing and physiotherapy.
  • Advice and management of urological aids and discharge systems.
  • Training and further training of in-house nursing staff on urotherapeutic topics.
  • Participation in working groups to continuously improve the quality of treatment processes.

Your profile

  • Professional qualification as a qualified nurse (HF/FH)
  • Completed training for urotherapist (or willingness to complete it).
  • Experience in urology, urogynecology or neuro-urology is beneficial.
  • Structured working methods as well as a strong organizational and communication strength in dealing with those affected.
  • Team spirit and enjoyment of close cooperation in a forward-looking, interprofessional environment.

About the Claraspital

The Claraspital, the largest private hospital in Basel, is a university teaching and research hospital with a full service mandate for highly specialized abdominal surgery and supra-regional broadcasting of its centers “Bäu” and “Tumor” as well as its special fields. In the Claraspital, employees find jobs where they can develop both professionally and humanly. At Claraspital, we attach great importance to consciously nurturing and further developing our corporate culture and actively living the cultural criteria developed together “jointly – competently – responsibly”.
