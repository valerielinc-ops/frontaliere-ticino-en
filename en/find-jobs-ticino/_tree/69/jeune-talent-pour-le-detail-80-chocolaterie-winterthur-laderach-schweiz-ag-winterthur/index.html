Full description

Our Swiss chocolate manufacturer, Läderach, is known for its high-quality and fresh chocolate. Founded in 1962, our family-owned group today employs over 3000 people from more than 80 countries, with a growing trend. Our headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. We are situated in a picturesque Alpine backdrop and produce our fresh chocolates and develop new innovative ideas in agile teams. Our "Retail" department comprises over 250 chocolate shops in more than 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite our customers to experience the freshness of chocolate again and again. Do you also want to be part of it? Then come to us. This position offers you the opportunity to develop further in a future-oriented company and grow within the organization. We will support you with targeted training and a clear development plan to prepare you for a store management position and give you the opportunity to work in other stores in Eastern Switzerland or the Greater Zurich Area. What awaits you. You will support the store management in the operational management of the store. You will take on leadership and decision-making tasks. You will ensure excellent customer service and optimal store organization. You will be involved in creating sales strategies and implementing company goals. You will take on responsibility for the induction and development of employees. You will be involved in improving store processes and procedures. What you bring with you. You have initial experiences in store management, retail or similar leadership positions. You bring a high motivation for taking on responsibility and leadership. You will show flexibility and the willingness to grow continuously. You have excellent communication skills and a team orientation. You are ready to work in other stores in the Eastern Switzerland and Zurich region. You will work with organizational skills and an independent and solution-oriented working style. What we offer. An engaging work environment with motivated colleagues. A position with a lot of personal responsibility. The opportunity to introduce fresh ideas. An open and respectful feedback culture. Internal and external training. Individual career opportunities. Generous discounts on our products. Chocolate at work. Good accessibility of the sites. Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh. Sounds interesting? Then apply with us. Nora Rechsteiner, Store Manager, +41 52 203 34 86, is at your disposal for any questions. We ask for understanding that we will not consider external recruitment agencies for filling this position.
