Full description

For electricity to flow, good civil engineers are needed

Project engineer in construction in civil engineering · 80-100% · BadenYou don’t just build substations and cable lines. You create the infrastructure that makes power supply possible – and accompany your projects from the first idea to the finished construction.Axpo plans buildings and civil engineering around our high-voltage network – for Axpo and third-party customers. You are not only in demand at the desk: you represent Axpo as the client, bring the participants together and develop solutions that convince technically and prove themselves on the construction site.

You plan conversions and new constructions of substations and cable lines. You prepare tenders, coordinate internal and external partners and keep an eye on quality, deadlines and costs. In doing so, you accompany your projects from the preliminary project to construction acceptance – and take responsibility for solutions that work in everyday life.

You have a completed degree in civil engineering or a comparable qualification and professional experience in civil engineering and/or civil engineering. You are familiar with statics and construction administration programs, work in a structured way and communicate very well in German. And you want to take responsibility for your own projects.

Sounds like a construction project with meaning? Then bring your expertise where it works. We look forward to your application. For questions please contact Marcel Oesen, Talent Acquisition Manager, +41 56 200 35 47 or marcel.oesen@axpo.com
