Office for Hunting and Fisheries Graubünden / DIEM \| 75% \| Region Rueun\-Waltensburg\-Andiast

Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us.

The Office for Hunting and Fishing is responsible for the game stocks, fish stocks and their habitats in the Canton of Graubünden. Many species such as caper chicken, birkhuhn, stone chicken, Wiedehopf and river runners have distribution focuses in Graubünden. In recent years, large predators such as the and the lynx or temporarily the have migrated back to Graubünden.

As a gamekeeper, you work under the guidance of the gamekeeper district chief and exercise hegerian, hunting planning, fishing as well as hunting and fishing police functions. They know the intended area of operation and its waters well.

Your responsibilities

  • Monitoring of fish stocks and their habitats on the municipality of Breil/Brigels (Waltensburg, Andiast) and Ilanz (Rueun, Siat)
  • Monitoring of different wildlife species
  • Support for administrative work in the district

That is what we want from you.

  • Completed apprenticeship
  • Physical fitness
  • Practical experience in hunting and fishing in the Canton of Graubünden
  • Knowledge of wildlife and fish species and their habitats
  • Shooting skills
  • Independent and responsible work
  • Good communication skills
  • Willingness to provide piquetting services
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Fulfillment of the 20\. Year of life
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is advantageous
  • Good computer user knowledge
