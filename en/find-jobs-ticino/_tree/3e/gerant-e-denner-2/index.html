Full description

Workplace: Denner SA, Rue de l'Avenir, 1950 Sion

At home, you're not just running a store, but an essential link in our business. You take responsibility, but we always cross the finish line together. You deserve our esteem and support for your mindset. And friendly relationships within a winning team. We're Denner.

Luogo di lavoro: Denner SA, Rue de l'Avenir, 1950 Sion

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned. For this position, a letter or motivational video is desired.

Mansioni

  • Train, support and promote team members - and be their role model
  • You pay particular attention to the freshness of fruits, vegetables and bread, and you fight against food waste by making ingenious orders.
  • Order the goods at the right time and in good quantity
  • Skillfully plan the work as part of the weekly plan and thus ensure efficient processes in your store
  • Every effort is made to ensure that the figures are balanced - in terms of turnover, staff costs and stock of goods

Required

  • Three or more years of professional and leadership experience: as head of service, sector or retail department
  • Completed Professional Learning (AFP): in the retail trade (food) or related profession is an advantage
  • Love fresh produce and ideally have experience in this field
  • Ability to plan and organize tasks and a strong sense of key figures interpretation
  • In good physical shape, some packages weigh up to 10 kilos
  • Good computer, tablet and smartphone knowledge, rapid adaptation to other digital tools (e.g. for goods management)
  • French (Very good knowledge)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: A strong brand, well rooted in the retail trade within the Migros Group.
  • Working in good health: High standards in occupational safety, ergonomics and hygiene.
  • Maternity/paternity leave: 5 additional weeks of maternity leave (19 weeks in total) with a salary of 100% and 4 weeks of paternity leave.
  • Fair pay: 13 salaries per year and increases for everyone instead of a bonus for a minority.
  • Mobile subscription: The embarrassment of choice: subscription Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise from 10 francs.
  • Migros Bank: Better interest rates and less charges on your private, investment or mortgage accounts.
  • Offers for employees: 10 percent discount on the next big purchase (twice a year).
  • Holidays: 6 weeks of vacation for everyone and extra days for anniversary service.
  • Leisure and culture: Discounts with car rental companies, hotels, events and in many other areas.
  • Individual continuing training: In-house training and financial support for useful external training.

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Personal interview video or telephone: In the case of a large number of candidates, personal video or telephone interviews are optional. This allows us to get to know more candidates in a short time so as to invite the people concerned for the next round. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Professional interview: During the professional interview, the manager and/or other specialized person, or even the team, asks specific questions about the knowledge to accomplish the work. If possible, then there is more time to get to know the workstation and team in a broken stick discussion. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Test work: A day or half-day on the test allows you to become familiar with the future working environment and team members. Conversely, we have the opportunity to see how compatible the candidates are with the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Interview with HR and the department: The interview is conducted in distanciel or face-to-face with the HR person responsible and/or the specialized department. It serves primarily to learn more about personal and professional skills and allows both parties to assess cultural compatibility with the company and the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and establishment of contract: The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by post. Return time: up to 3 weeks
