About the job As a Principal Engineer for AI and Infrastructure Research and Development, you will bring deep systems accuracy to agent scale, connecting cutting-edge model research to large-scale production platforms. In this role, you will define the technical strategy bridging systems infrastructure and agent architectures, such as model inference acceleration, simulation-driven infrastructure optimization, and end-to-end platform efficiency. You will establish the engineering foundations and shared frameworks that enable internal organizations to deploy autonomous agents safely, robustly, and at hyperscale. Additionally, you will serve as an executive technical advisor and external thought leader, engaging C-suite customers on advanced architecture, delivering keynote perspectives, and shaping industry and governance standards while driving strategic alignment across DeepMind, Core, and Google Cloud. The AI and Infrastructure team is redefining what’s possible. We empower Google customers with breakthrough capabilities and insights by delivering AI and Infrastructure at unparalleled scale, efficiency, reliability and velocity. Our customers include Googlers, Google Cloud customers, and billions of Google users worldwide. We're the driving force behind Google's groundbreaking innovations, empowering the development of our cutting-edge AI models, delivering unparalleled computing power to global services, and providing the essential platforms that enable developers to build the future. From software to hardware our teams are shaping the future of world-leading hyperscale computing, with key teams working on the development of our TPUs, Vertex AI for Google Cloud, Google Global Networking, Data Center operations, systems research, and much more.
