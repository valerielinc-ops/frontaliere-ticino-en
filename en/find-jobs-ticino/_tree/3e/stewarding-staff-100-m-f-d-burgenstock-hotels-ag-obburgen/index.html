Full description

An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Located on the Bürgenberg, a 500-meter-high rocky outcrop in central Switzerland, the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne stands out for its bold design, groundbreaking sustainability, breathtaking landscapes and state-of-the-art leisure activities. Around 700 employees from 54 nations ensure excellent hospitality and provide the perfect setting for moments beyond imagination. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two accommodation options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Tavern 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence; at the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. For the third consecutive year, Bürgenstock Resort has been officially certified as a "Great Place to Work" in Switzerland - an award that fills the entire team with pride. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement:

Sommelier 100% (w/m/i)

In this hospitable position, you are responsible for

  • Personal, stylish advice for our guests on wine selection in all restaurants and bars
  • Further development and maintenance of the wine lists in close coordination with the F&B team
  • Active care of the wine storage
  • Conduct of training and tasting sessions for employees
  • Planning and implementation of wine tastings, theme evenings and events
  • Ensuring optimal wine distribution and proper storage
  • Close cooperation with service teams to implement coherent pairings

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed training in catering or hotel industry as well as sommelier certification (WSET, ASI, etc.)
  • Several years of experience in the upscale restaurant or hotel environment
  • Passion for wine, in-depth knowledge of international growing areas and current market trends
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, every other language is advantageous
  • A well-kept, sovereign appearance, sales competence and enjoyment of guest contact
  • Independent working methods, organisational talent and teamwork

It awaits you In addition to a modern workplace, you benefit from numerous attractive employee offers and perks. The staff house has its own gym, a TV lounge, a pool room and a library. In addition, you will enjoy discounts in our restaurants and preferential conditions for you, your family and friends for overnight stays in our hotels. Other benefits include perks for tennis, golf, sporting goods, insurance, group courses and REKA checks. We also support your mobility: Public transport is financially supported and parking is available at attractive conditions. We invest in your future: With access to the Beekeeper employee app, a wide range of further education offers, internal development opportunities and first-class career prospects, we create the framework for your personal and professional development. She expects the chance to be part of a world-class resort, to actively contribute to the joint success and to continuously develop in an inspiring environment. We look forward to your complete application documents with letters of motivation, employment certificates and photo.
