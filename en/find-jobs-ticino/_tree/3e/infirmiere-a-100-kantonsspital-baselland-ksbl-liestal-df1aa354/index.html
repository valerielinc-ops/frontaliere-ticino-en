Start date by agreement · Bruderholz, Liestal, Orthopaedics & Traumatology · Job reference 13197 · Fixed-term

Responsibilities

  • Contributing to the planning of clinical studies, study protocols and other submission documents for the ethics committee, as well as supporting our medical and student staff in preparing these documents
  • Close interdisciplinary collaboration and communication with external partners/sponsors for study activation and ensuring correct study conduct and study documentation, including reporting relevant events
  • Enrolment and care of study patients in collaboration with the investigators
  • Coordination, conduct and documentation of patient visits

  • Further development of quality assurance
  • Conducting staff training
  • The position is fixed-term for 2 years with the prospect of extension

Requirements

  • Qualification as a registered nurse (HF/FH or equivalent) or physiotherapist with additional training and/or many years of professional experience in the field of clinical studies and/or data management
  • Committed personality with a high level of dedication, team spirit and social skills, including when dealing with sick people
  • Organizational skills and the ability to approach complex situations in a solution-oriented manner, as well as a structured way of working, are among your strengths
  • Proficient in MS Office applications, knowledgeable about database structures, ideally with knowledge of REDCap
  • Good German and English skills
  • Regular working hours from Monday to Friday. Flexibility regarding a workplace with two locations in Liestal and Bruderholz, as well as a willingness to also work after 17:00.
