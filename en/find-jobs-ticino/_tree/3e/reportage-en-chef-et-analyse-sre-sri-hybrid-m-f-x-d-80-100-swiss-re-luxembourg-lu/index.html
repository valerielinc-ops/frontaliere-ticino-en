Full description

About the Role As Head Reporting & Analytics SRE/SRI, you will take ownership of the end-to-end reporting and analytics function for Swiss Re Europe (SRE) and Swiss Re International (SRI), leading a talented team of approx. 6-7 colleagues dedicated to statutory, regulatory, and management reporting excellence. Reporting to the CFO SRE/SRI, this is a pivotal leadership role that combines deep technical expertise with strategic vision — you will shape the future of Legal Entity reporting, champion data-driven decision-making, and drive the finance transformation agenda across Luxembourg entities and their branches. You will be a trusted partner to senior Finance stakeholders, regulators, and cross-functional teams, ensuring that reporting is not only compliant and accurate, but also insightful, scalable, and future-ready. Key Responsibilities

  • Lead and develop a high-performing reporting and analytics team, fostering a culture of ownership, continuous learning, collaboration, and operational excellence
  • Own and oversee all local reporting activities for Luxembourg entities and their branches, including Lux GAAP statutory reporting, Solvency II and Solvency UK reporting — ensuring timely, accurate, and fully compliant submissions. Ensure high-quality and concise internal management reporting to the SRE/SRI Board of Directors, Management Committee, the General Manager Committee, and other committees and meetings as appropriate.
  • Drive the strategic reporting and analytics agenda for SRE/SRI, aligning team priorities with GFO&T transformation goals and Swiss Re's broader finance strategy
  • Champion data-driven insights by overseeing the analysis of financial and operational data to identify trends, risks, and opportunities — translating complex datasets into clear, actionable narratives for senior leadership and regulatory decision-making
  • Lead the development and rollout of the Legal Entity Dashboard, steering design, testing, go-live, and continuous improvement phases while driving adoption across stakeholder groups
  • Identify and implement process improvements and automation opportunities across carrier finance and reporting workflows, building more efficient, standardised, and scalable solutions for local "last-mile reporting".
  • Represent the team in senior management forums, regulatory interactions, and cross-functional governance bodies, preparing and presenting high-quality reporting packs and strategic materials
  • Strengthen regulatory reporting governance by setting and maintaining robust controls, documentation standards, and data quality frameworks
  • Support and lead regional and global Carrier Management activities, including facilitation of senior meetings, delivery of knowledge-sharing sessions, and coordination of training programmes
  • Contribute to GFO&T strategic initiatives, providing leadership on quantitative and qualitative analysis, implementation activities, and milestone tracking

About the Team GFO&T (Global Finance Operations & Transformation) is the division responsible for the accounting and reporting of accurate and consistent financial information for Swiss Re globally — supporting both external reporting and internal analysis to facilitate strategic decision-making. This spans IFRS reporting as well as statutory financial information for all Swiss Re legal entities, including SST, Solvency II, Solvency UK, and a broad range of local regulatory reporting requirements. GFO&T is at the forefront of transforming Global Finance — streamlining it towards a simpler, more effective, and forward-looking organisation through increased use of automation, analytics, and digital capabilities. The team doesn't just keep pace with change — it leads it. Joining GFO&T means becoming part of a team where your ideas matter, your expertise is valued, and your work has a direct and visible impact on Swiss Re's financial integrity and strategic direction.

About You

You are an inspiring and credible finance leader who combines strategic thinking with hands-on analytical expertise. You bring gravitas and clarity to complex regulatory and financial topics, and you have a proven track record of building and motivating teams to deliver excellence. You are energised by transformation, have strong analytical and data leadership skills, are comfortable navigating ambiguity, and skilled at building trusted relationships with senior stakeholders across cultures and functions. You lead by example — taking ownership, communicating with confidence, and always looking for smarter, more impactful ways to work.

We are looking for candidates who meet these requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related quantitative field
  • 7+ years of progressive experience in finance, regulatory reporting, finance operations, or consulting within the insurance or financial services industry, including demonstrated people leadership experience
  • Deep knowledge of regulatory reporting frameworks, including statutory reporting, Solvency II, and/or Solvency UK requirements, with a strong understanding of financial controls and data governance in a regulated environment
  • Strong analytical and data leadership skills, with the ability to oversee the interpretation of large, complex datasets and translate findings into strategic insights; familiarity with data analytics and visualisation tools (e.g. Power BI, Tableau, Alteryx, SQL, Python, or similar)
  • Mindset of curiosity and mental agility for continuing enhancement and problem solving around AI driven initiatives and project work
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, with a proven ability to engage, influence, and present to senior leadership and regulatory bodies with confidence and clarity
  • Fluency in English is required (working language of the team); French is desirable given the Luxembourg-based environment and regular interaction with local stakeholders and regulatory bodies

These are additional nice to haves

  • Experience in re/insurance finance environments and familiarity with Swiss Re's legal entity structure or similar multinational carrier setups
  • Knowledge of IFRS accounting standards and Group financial close/reporting processes
  • Proven track record of leading finance transformation, automation, or digital innovation initiatives within reporting or finance operations functions
  • Experience with enterprise data platforms, regulatory reporting tools, or finance systems implementation
  • Exposure to AI, machine learning, or advanced analytics applications in a finance or reporting context
  • Experience working in a matrix organisation and collaborating across geographies and functions
