Full description

You keep track of things even in demanding after-sales cases and ensure satisfied customers with your solution-oriented way of working. The place of work can be chosen between Dintikon AG and Zurich.

Tasks

  • Recording and monitoring after-sales cases and advocating for individual solutions
  • Responsible for checking invoices and credit notes as well as booking them correctly
  • Daily contact with external parties such as service partners, forwarders, and end customers
  • Making a significant contribution to the efficient closure of our warranty cases by proactively obtaining credit notes from our service partners
  • Right in the middle instead of just being there - you actively ensure process optimization and challenge the status quo
  • Competent and solution-oriented handling of all inquiries to achieve the highest customer satisfaction and that certain "WOW" effect

Requirements

  • 2 years of experience: Administration / Back Office
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ): Commercial sector
  • Customer contact and very good PC user skills (MS Office, ERP systems) are necessary
  • You are a customer- and service-oriented person and go the extra mile for a perfect customer experience
  • You bring strong teamwork skills, but also enjoy working independently, have a quick comprehension, and are resilient
  • You are highly motivated to learn new things and like to face new challenges
  • Align actions and decisions with your personal convictions and values
  • Use creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • German (fluent)
  • English (an advantage)
  • Italian (an advantage)
  • French (an advantage)

What we offer

  • Values instead of blah-blah: Cooperative, innovative, piratical, self-responsible, ambitious – we all demand and live these five values.
  • Think courageously for yourself: We encourage everyone to think actively and independently. And we love it when you also think courageously, newly, and differently.
  • Uncomplicated: We avoid the unnecessary and focus on what is important. From mastering challenges to celebrating our successes.
  • Flexibility instead of bending (office jobs): In addition to home office options, we allow up to 8 weeks of workation. Furthermore, every position is also possible at an 80% workload.
  • Fast growth: With us, you grow and develop constantly. And you receive EDU points (CHF 2,000 for employees in Switzerland, EUR 1,600 for German employees, and EUR 700 for our Serbian employees) for your further development so that you don't stand still.
  • Other benefits Switzerland: Benefit from 25 days of vacation, exclusive employee prices in the shop, mobile, internet, and TV subscriptions at corporate rates, and other advantages.
