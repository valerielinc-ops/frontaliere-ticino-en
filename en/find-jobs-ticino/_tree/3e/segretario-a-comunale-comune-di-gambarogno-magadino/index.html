AVVISO DI CONCORSO FOR PERSONAL ASSESSMENT II Gambarogno City Hall opens the competition for the recruitment of a 100% municipal secretary under the conditions of the organic Regulation of municipal employees: The objective of the function The Secretary/Communist assures the management, coordination and good functioning of the Municipal Administration, ensuring the legal, administrative and organizational support to the Town Hall and the correct implementation of its decisions. It promotes efficient, modern and quality-oriented management of services, ensuring the coordination of different administrative sectors, the enhancement of human resources and the continuous improvement of working processes. In view of the importance and responsibility of the function, the applications that meet the requirements and competences required by this notice and its duties will be taken into consideration. General requirements

  • Swiss citizenship, possession and exercise of civil rights and unexceptionable conduct;
  • healthy physical constitution suitable for function and ability to stress resistance;
  • flexibility and availability to perform tasks even outside normal working hours;
  • commitment to continuing training on the function;
  • licence to conduct cat. B;
  • Italian mother tongue with good knowledge of German.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
