Full description

Entry as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement · Bruderholz, Liestal, Central Patient Management · Job reference 13496 · Permanent employment

Tasks

Do you like to lead people, enjoy developing processes and keep an overview even in demanding situations? Do you want to help ensure that our patients are cared for at the right place at the right time and that cooperation between the different areas works smoothly? Then you are exactly right with us. Central Patient Management (ZPM) is part of Integral Capacity Management (IKM) and plays an important role in the coordination of our patient flows. We work closely with the medical profession, the care and various internal and external partners. As head of the ZPM, you will assume personnel and technical responsibility for a team of around 22 employees at the Liestal and Bruderholz sites. Together with your deputy management, you ensure a well-organized everyday work, promote your employees and continuously develop our processes and quality standards. Your tasks

  • You lead and develop our cross-location team together with the deputy management and promote an open, appreciative and binding cooperation
  • You ensure an efficient and needs-based process in entry, exit and transfer management and support your team with challenging issues
  • You review existing processes, identify potential for improvement and develop uniform and practical processes for both locations together with your team.
  • You are responsible for quality assurance in patient management and social service and ensure compliance with and further development of our standards
  • You maintain a close and constructive cooperation with the medical profession, the care and our external partners and represent the interests of the ZPM in cross-divisional issues
  • You participate in projects to optimize patient flows and actively shape the further development of Central Patient Management

Requirements

  • You have completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH
  • You bring further training in leadership or personnel management as well as in case management or are ready to complete it
  • You enjoy the leadership of employees, can motivate and promote people and are happy to take on responsibility
  • You have a pronounced organizational and coordination talent, think in a networked way and keep track of different requirements
  • You communicate clearly, appreciatively and bindingly, can bring different interests together and find constructive solutions even in demanding situations.
  • You question existing processes, recognize potential for improvement and enjoy implementing changes together with your team
  • You know the Swiss healthcare system and understand the needs of our patients as well as the operational and economic connections
  • You work independently, decisively and solution-oriented and appreciate cooperation with different professional groups

Benefits

Being fit & staying fit: Attractive offers around sports, relaxation & nutrition
