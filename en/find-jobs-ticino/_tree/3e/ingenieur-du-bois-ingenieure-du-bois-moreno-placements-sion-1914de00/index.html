Full description

Do you want to design and monitor wood construction projects? This engineering function covers technical solutions, calculations and implementation supervision.

We are looking for a wood engineer / wood engineer for one of our clients.

Responsibilities

  • Design and develop various projects in wood construction (structure, building, mountain construction, historical monument,...) involvement in all phases of projects
  • Technical support for special carpentry and fire protection
  • Collaborate with architects, contractors and internal teams to provide suitable technical solutions
  • Completing the static calculations, dimensioning and engineering plans necessary for carrying out the projects
  • Oversee the implementation of projects by ensuring compliance with quality standards and standards
  • Participate in continuous improvement of business processes and innovations

Your profile

  • Degree in wood engineering or equivalent training
  • Confirmed experience in wood construction
  • Mastery of design tools (Cadwork, AutoCAD, etc.) and 2D/3D structure computing software (DLUBAL RFEM or equivalent)
  • Knowledge of wood/metal calculation rules (SIA260/261/263/265 or Eurocodes 0/1/3/5)
  • Knowledge of Swiss fire protection standards an asset
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Team spirit

We offer

  • Dynamic and innovative company, carrying out extremely varied projects and numerous public procurement, with technical challenges
  • A modern working environment that values know-how and collaboration

We support you in your professional development through ongoing training in relation to your profession, fully supported by the Temptraining Training Fund.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
