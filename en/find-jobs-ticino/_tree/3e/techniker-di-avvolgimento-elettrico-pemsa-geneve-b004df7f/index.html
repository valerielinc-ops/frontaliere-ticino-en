Your assignment

Perform rewinding in repair Perform disassembly, diagnosis and rewinding of AC/CC electric motors. Perform manual or semi-automatic rewinding according to technical specifications. Replace insulation materials, paint and impregnate wrappings. Check winding compliance and perform electrical tests. Ensure the reassembly and operating tests of the equipment. Read and interpret electrical patterns and technical drawings. Respect quality, safety and procedures in force. And now we're talking about you? Diploma CFC as an automatist, electrician, mechanical-electricist or training technician considered equivalent. Experienced in the winding of electric motors or in a similar task. Good mastery in reading drawings and schematics. Electrotechnical knowledge and industrial maintenance. Autonomy, precision and sense of responsibility. Team spirit and ability to adapt.
