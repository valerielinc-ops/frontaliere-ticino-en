Get to know us. This is how we tick. We work with heart, respect, and a strong sense of togetherness. Quality, reliability, and appreciative interaction characterize our everyday life. The self-service restaurant Barmelguet with sun terrace and kiosk is open 7 days a week all year round and is a central meeting point for internal and external guests. In addition to daily operations, the team is also responsible for aperitifs, banquets, and seminars for 12 to 200 guests, actively shaping the guest and employee experience. Duties

  • You ensure a warm and professional service for our guests in the restaurant, at banquets, and during seminars, and take care of the preparation, organization, and execution of banquets.
  • With your attentive and service-oriented manner, you create positive guest experiences.
  • You carry out all work efficiently, in a structured manner, and with a focus on quality according to the daily schedule.
  • You prepare the various food distribution areas (Free Flow), monitor them, and ensure smooth operations.
  • You ensure cleanliness and hygiene in your area and consistently comply with applicable regulations.
  • Requirements
  • Completed training in service or several years of professional experience in service, along with a strong service mentality and positive attitude.
  • Quick grasp of new tasks, clear and open communication style, and very good German language skills.
  • Well-groomed appearance and excellent manners.
  • Team spirit as well as an independent, structured, and efficient way of working.
  • Mobility (own car) and good MS Office skills, which you use confidently for administrative tasks.
