Full description

Come and join and strengthen our team in French-speaking Switzerland. We manage, develop, design and plan infrastructure projects of all types in the field of civil engineering and construction. We support these projects from study to completion.

Come and join and strengthen our team and take on responsibilities as: DEPUTY DIRECTOR OF THE WESTERN REGION, CIVIL BUSINESS UNIT 80-100% (m/f/d) LAUSANNE / FRIBOURG / DELÉMONT

YOUR TASKS

  • Team management: Supervise and lead the project and construction management team, ensure a cooperative and stimulating work environment
  • Project Management: Develop and oversee detailed project planning, taking into account resources, timelines, and technical specifications. Establish accurate budgets for each project with the finance department and monitor expenditures throughout the process
  • Customer and partner relations: Ensure smooth communication with customers, architects, engineers, and other stakeholders to ensure a clear understanding of needs and expectations

YOUR PROFILE

  • Engineering degree (Uni/ETH/HES/HF) or equivalent
  • Proven experience in leading civil engineering projects, leading project portfolios and managing acquisitions
  • Team leadership: promoting a culture of collaboration and cohesion. Supporting, inspiring and motivating a high-performing team, providing clear direction and fostering professional development
  • Excellent knowledge of French and good knowledge of German
  • Flexibility, communication and entrepreneurial spirit are your assets. Committed to the development of the region, you are ready to travel. Your network participates
