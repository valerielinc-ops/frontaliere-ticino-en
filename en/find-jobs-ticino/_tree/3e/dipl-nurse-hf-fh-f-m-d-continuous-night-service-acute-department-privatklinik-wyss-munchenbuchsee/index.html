Full description

Privatklinik Wyss AG is a leading specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy. Its particular areas of expertise include the treatment of depression, including burnout, as well as anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders. The clinic provides outpatient, day-clinic and inpatient services in the Bern, Münchenbuchsee and Biel region and employs around 340 people across all three locations.

Registered Nurse HF/FH (all genders) - Acute Psychiatry Unit

Workload 70 - 100 % Permanent position Location Privatklinik Wyss AG Start date 1 January 2027 or by agreement

#Psychiatry, #Responsibility, #Teamwork, #Development

A meaningful role offering room to shape things and partnership-based collaboration in an appreciative environment is waiting for you!

The essentials at a glance

With us, there is more to it than routine! We work across professions, holistically and according to the Recovery approach—together with our peer colleagues. Professional de-escalation management (ProDeMa) is standard for us—restraints are a thing of the past. Are you empathetic, professional and able to keep an overview even in complex situations? Then we are looking for you.

What your working day with us looks like

  • You support people in acute mental health crises with empathy and professionalism
  • You actively help shape the care process within the primary nursing system
  • You work using milieu therapy and closely with doctors, therapists and peers
  • You independently lead groups and co-moderate them—for example, psychoeducation, recovery groups or individual offerings
  • You work all shifts

What you bring

  • You are a diploma-qualified nurse with an HF or FH qualification
  • You enjoy a dynamic environment in which change is seen as an opportunity
  • You are self-reflective, calm and empathetic in your interactions with our patients
  • You are hands-on, think proactively and enjoy contributing your own ideas
  • You contribute to an open, appreciative team culture

What you can expect from us

  • A structured induction and a team that welcomes you
  • A pleasant, supportive team atmosphere
  • A mindset that supports a good work-life balance, with the option of fixed working days or days off, as well as unpaid leave
  • An attractive bonus for taking over shifts at short notice
  • Regular supervision, peer consultation and case discussions
  • Opportunities for progression through talent development
  • Modern infrastructure in a natural setting

Interested?

If you would like to rethink acute psychiatry and become part of a committed team, we look forward to receiving your application!
