Full description

The private clinic Wyss AG is a leading specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy. Among the special areas of expertise are the treatment of depression including burnout as well as anxiety and obsessive disorders. The clinic offers its outpatient, day-to-day clinical and inpatient services in the Bern, Munichbuchsee and Biel region and employs around 34 employees at all three locations.
