University of Zurich, Implementation Science in Nursing Science

Job opening 01.05.2027, temporary

The SNSF-funded FICUScale study examines the implementation of the FICUS® family-focused intervention in ten intensive care units in Switzerland by means of a randomised study comparing two different approaches. An implementation team accompanies the local teams in the intensive care units with the introduction and sustainable anchoring of FICUS®. For the implementation support of the participating intensive care units in German-speaking Switzerland, we are looking for a committed personality with experience in the implementation of complex interventions in the healthcare sector.

Tasks

  • They accompany local implementation teams in intensive care units in German-speaking Switzerland in the implementation of the family-focused intervention FICUS® according to the study protocol.
  • They support the teams in the analysis of local framework conditions, the development of implementation plans and the contextual adaptation and further development of implementation.
  • They conduct regular, proactive exchanges and consultations with the local teams and support them with challenges in the implementation process.
  • They plan and accompany on-site visits.
  • They participate in the organisation and support of a learning community as well as in project-specific training and support activities.
  • You coordinate your activities with the implementation team and the study management and document the implementation activities according to the study specifications.

Profile

  • have a master’s degree in nursing science or in a comparable health science subject
  • Alternatively, you are a nurse with relevant training (e.g. implementation in the healthcare sector).
  • You have experience in acute care, intensive care medicine or family-focused interventions.
  • You have experience implementing complex interventions in healthcare.
  • You can advise and accompany local teams in a structured manner in context analysis, implementation planning and adaptation of interventions.
  • They communicate securely and appropriately with clinical and scientific stakeholders and have strong advisory skills.
  • They work independently and reliably and are ready to visit participating intensive care units on site.
  • You have a good knowledge of German
  • English and French skills are beneficial.
  • They are motivated to familiarize themselves with project-specific content, processes and the study protocol.
  • You are ready to flexibly adapt your working time to the project progression and the support needs of the participating intensive care units. In this case, phases with higher workload can be compensated by quieter phases.
