Who We Are The Global Data, Digital & AI (GDDA) organization plays a central role in Roche Pharma Commercial’s digital and AI ecosystem. GDDA sets the enterprise digital strategy, translates commercial objectives into a coordinated portfolio of investments, and defines the standards and operating model that enable scalable execution across programs and products. By connecting strategy, portfolio, standards, programs, and products, GDDA ensures that data, digital, and AI capabilities are delivered as scalable, enterprise solutions that drive adoption and improve outcomes for patients, healthcare providers, and the broader healthcare ecosystem. The Opportunity The Digital Program Lead - Pharma Commercial (PACE) plays a critical role within GDDA's Digital Global Programs and Products function. PACE (Powering & Accelerating Commercialization Excellence) is a global program to drive patient and business impact through a unified commercialization engine. The role ensures that PACE digital program needs are translated into actionable requirements across digital product suites. Reporting to the Head of Global Digital Programs & Products, this role operates at the intersection of program management, product coordination, and enterprise orchestration — connecting the dots across multiple product suites, shared (Roche Digital Technologies) RDT & delivery team capacity, and the PACE commercialization excellence agenda. This role ensures that PACE-driven digital priorities are integrated into GDDA's product planning and delivery rhythm. As GDDA’s portfolio evolves, the role will expand to support orchestration across additional cross-cutting programs and initiatives within Pharma Commercial.
