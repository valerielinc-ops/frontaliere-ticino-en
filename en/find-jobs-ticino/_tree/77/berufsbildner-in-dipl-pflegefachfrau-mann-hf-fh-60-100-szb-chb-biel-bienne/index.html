Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

The training of future care professionals excites you? Would you like to pass on your expertise with pleasure, accompany learners and students on their way and support them in realizing their potential? As a vocational trainer, you make an important contribution to the development of the next generation of care professionals. You promote professional and personal skills, support learners and students in challenging situations and actively contribute to a high-quality education. For the accompaniment of our learners and students, we are looking for a committed, motivated and pedagogically experienced personality as:

Vocational trainer/in Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH 60-100 %

You do

  • Accompaniment of learners and students during their internship
  • Conduct of site, course and assessment discussions
  • Participation in practical qualification procedures as well as taking on experts Expert activities
  • Active participation and further development of training quality at the Biel Hospital Centre
  • Care of patients as a qualified nurse
  • Close cooperation with team leadership, nursing team and education team

This sets you apart

  • Diploma as nursing specialist HF/FH
  • Pedagogical training (SVEB I) or the willingness to complete it
  • Enjoyment in accompanying and promoting learners and students of different levels of education
  • Strong communication, coaching and reflection skills
  • High social competence and the ability to accompany people in an appreciative way in their development
  • Interest in continuous professional and personal development
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as basic knowledge of French

You will benefit from this

  • Attractive working conditions: a modern infrastructure, family-friendly personnel policy and fair compensation
  • Meaningful work for high patient satisfaction on the pulse of the regional population
  • Bilinguism: Collaboration with bilingual colleagues from many different professions in a dynamic environment. Support in the development of your language skills (German/French)
  • Career prospects : Support for further education and individual development opportunities Professional development in the field of education
