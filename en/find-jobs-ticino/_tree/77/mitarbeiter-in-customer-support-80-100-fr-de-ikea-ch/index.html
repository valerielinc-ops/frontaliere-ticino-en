Full description

Who you are

Do you enjoy contact with customers and find suitable solutions for challenging issues? As an employee of Customer Support in the resolution area, you handle complaints and customer concerns and accompany our customers from the first contact to the solution. With clear communication, empathy and a solution-oriented way of working, you actively contribute to a positive customer experience.

  • You have experience in contact with customers and ideally already have experience in sales, customer service or call center.
  • You have very good communication skills and can also communicate professionally and solution-oriented in demanding situations.
  • You work in a structured, independent and reliable way and find suitable solutions for different customer concerns.
  • You have strong competences in conflict management and can successfully solve and de-escalate conflict situations.
  • You have good IT knowledge and work safely with different systems.
  • You enjoy working in a team and with various internal and external interfaces.
  • fluent knowledge of German and fluent knowledge of French in words and writing (at least C1); English is an advantage.

You are responsible for

  • Your goal is to find suitable and customer-oriented solutions for our customers. You will process complaints and customer concerns by telephone and in writing.
  • You respond specifically to the concerns of our customers and find a suitable solution together with them.
  • You also communicate professionally, clearly and solution-oriented in the case of demanding complaints.
  • You support customers with questions and problems before, during and after the purchase and ensure a professional handling of their concerns.
  • You take responsibility for your cases and ensure consistent follow-up until the final solution.
  • You work closely with stores, internal departments and other interfaces to efficiently solve customer concerns.
  • You carefully review and document customer information and solutions in our systems.
  • You support the team in various tasks and contribute to an efficient and customer-oriented service.
  • When dealing with customer concerns, you take customer expectations into account as well as our internal processes and service goals.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Aargau?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Aargau's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
