The opportunity As a Technical Support Partner for Lab-IT, you are responsible for carrying out field service functions for Roche Diagnostics Digital Products. Your primary duties include digital product troubleshooting, installation, commissioning, and performing preventive maintenance procedures. Reporting to the Technical Support Lead for Lab-IT, you will respond promptly to all service calls and handle various after-sales service issues to ensure customer satisfaction and optimal instrument performance.

The ideal candidate is a detail-oriented, proactive, service-minded with strong communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively across functions. You will be responsible for

  • Perform digital product installation, commissioning, and troubleshooting for all Roche Diagnostics digital Products.
  • Respond promptly and effectively to all service calls from customers.
  • Conduct preventive maintenance according to the established schedule to ensure instrument & digital product reliability.
  • Manage and resolve other issues related to digital solution after-sales service to maintain high levels of customer support.
