Company Description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals of Geneva are a benchmark establishment at the national and international level. To find out more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here. The Crans-Montana Clinic, nestled in the heart of the Valais Alps, is a HUG hospital site. Its hospital missions consist of caring for patients for rehabilitation in general internal medicine and psychosomatics, multi-purpose post-operative or post-hospitalization rehabilitation in an acute environment. The Crans Montana Clinic holds FMH recognition for general internal medicine and trains internal doctors. She develops academic research projects in conjunction with the Faculty of Medicine of the University of Geneva.

Job Description

As part of your role, you assess the psychological functioning of patients and manage their difficulties. You develop appropriate care plans, develop intervention and prevention activities and thus contribute to proactive care. In your activities, you work interdisciplinary with medical-care teams, medical-therapeutic staff and actively participate in interdisciplinary rehabilitation conferences. In collaboration with your psychologist colleagues, you organize the therapies and the distribution of units to be covered. You supervise psychologists in postgraduate psychotherapy training, initiate and collaborate in teaching and research activities linked to the interests of the Clinic.

Qualifications

You have a Master's degree in psychology, a federal psychotherapist title, a right to practice from the canton of Valais (or failing that, be at the end of psychotherapy training and have at least 1 year of practical experience in a recognized ISFM psychiatric and psychotherapeutic institution). With 6 years of practical experience, you have an interest in psychosomatic hospital rehabilitation and wish to contribute to the development of the Psychology Department. You are able to work independently and in a multidisciplinary team. Thanks to your solid experience in leading groups, you are able to design relevant interventions and effectively share your knowledge and skills with other psychologists and professional partners.
