The position

As a Communications & Public Affairs department, we work together to develop exciting stories from the diverse content of our company and to implement them communicatively with creative ideas and formats for our employees, media representatives and our environment. We support the strategic orientation of the company and make the identity of Roche experienceable. As a volunteer:in you get insights into the content and organizational processes of internal and external corporate communication and get to know our communicative day-to-day business.

Tasks

  • Communication with concept: You start to create communication concepts and learn how to organize and implement projects. You independently manage communication projects under our guidance.
  • Texts that arrive: You write texts of all kinds
  • from the press release to Intranet news to articles for internal and external media.
  • Good content, many forms: You implement topics for different channels and in different formats: from podcast to daily news app.

You take different perspectives into account, plan your own target group and are creative in the preparation. - Event with planning: You support the organization and communicative support of external and internal events or press discussions. - Communication in all facets: You get insights into the different areas of responsibility of the communication department such as product and disease communication, internal communication, executive communication, change communication, media work or event communication

Requirements

  • You have already completed your degree (Bachelor or Master's degree) and the subject area is not decisive. More important to us is that you are enthusiastic about the communication subject, are open to the variety of topics and are able to prepare them attractively and target group-oriented. - You enjoy texting, conceptualizing, graphic design and organizing. - You bring knowledge about the application of digital media in communication and have first experience in the production and publication processes.
