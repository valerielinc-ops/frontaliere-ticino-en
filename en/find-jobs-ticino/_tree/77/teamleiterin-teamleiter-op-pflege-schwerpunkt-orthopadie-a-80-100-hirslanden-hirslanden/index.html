Do you want to take on a central leadership role in a highly specialized and innovative surgical area?

In our modern surgery, you will find a challenging and dynamic environment with a focus on orthopaedics, traumatology, sports surgery, hand and foot surgery. Together with interdisciplinary teams, you will work at the highest medical level and accompany complex interventions with the latest technologies and innovative systems such as the LOOP-X as well as high-precision navigation in cranial operations.

With us, you actively shape the future of cutting-edge medicine and work in an environment that combines innovation, quality and teamwork.

YOUR TASKS

  • Management of a specialist group of surgical care with focus on orthopaedics, traumatology, sports surgery, hand and foot surgery
  • Ensuring and further developing quality standards in surgical care
  • Coordination and monitoring of processes in the area of responsibility
  • Professional and organisational further development of specialised areas
  • Active participation in routine operations during complex interventions

Your profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Specialist surgical technique HF or equivalent training with SRC recognition
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in the field of orthopaedics
  • Leadership experience or readiness for management training
  • High social skills and strong communication skills
  • Resilient, responsible and team-oriented personality

You can expect a state-of-the-art and innovative working environment with the latest technologies such as the Hybrid OP and the DaVinci 5.

You work on exciting and highly complex surgical procedures in interdisciplinary cooperation at the highest level. You are part of a motivated, committed and professionally strong team that lives an appreciative corporate culture. In addition, you benefit from attractive further education and development opportunities and have the chance to actively shape the area.
