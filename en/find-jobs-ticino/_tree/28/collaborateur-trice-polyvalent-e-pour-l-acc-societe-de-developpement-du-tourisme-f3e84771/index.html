Full description

What if your next professional challenge was in the mountains? Do you like mountains, outdoor activities and winter sports? You enjoy contact with visitors as much as field work, and have an interest in animation and communication? Torgon Tourism is looking for its future multi-purpose guest worker, 80–100%!

Your Mission

At the heart of the destination, you help offer a quality welcome to our visitors and make Torgon shine throughout the year.

Your main responsibilities

• To welcome, advise and inform visitors at the Tourist Office; • Manage requests by phone and e-mail; • Make bookings and sales related to activities and events; • Update the content of the website and the communication media of the destination; • Promote the activities and experiences proposed in the station. Depending on the seasons, needs and your affinities, you can also: • Take part in administrative tasks, such as monitoring residence taxes, accounting records and processing incoming and outgoing invoices; • Contribute to the organization of events and support certain animations, such as the organization of musical animations in restaurants; • Create content for social networks and newsletters; • Participate in the setting up and maintenance of Torgon's tourist products, such as the archery trail, the escape game or Torgonland. Your profile • You have a real interest in tourism and mountains; • You benefit from training in tourism, hotels, commerce or an equivalent title; • You speak English very well

  • German is a major asset;

• You have good editorial skills; • You are comfortable with social networks, Canva and Microsoft Office suite; • You are smiling, welcoming, creative and enjoy human contact; • You know how to work autonomously, with a practical and solutions oriented spirit; • You are flexible and available on weekends, public holidays and occasionally in the evenings; • You have a driving licence and a vehicle. Who are we? Torgon Tourism is an independent structure active in services and tourist management. Attentive to the needs of its partners and customers, it develops the positioning and reputation of the destination, while contributing to the dynamism and added value of the regional economy.

What we offer

• A varied position in the heart of a tourist area integrated with Portes du Soleil; • A human-sized working environment within a young and dynamic team; • Various missions that allow you to combine reception, communication, tourism and field activities. Want to join the adventure? We look forward to receiving your complete file including a cover letter, CV, certificates, photo, wage claims and desired activity rate.
