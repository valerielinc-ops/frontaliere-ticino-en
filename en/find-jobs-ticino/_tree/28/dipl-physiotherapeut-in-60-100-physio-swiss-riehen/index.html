Here we are

Physical therapy means more to us than treatment plans and timing – it thrives on time, attention and real encounter. That is why we consciously work without mass processing and focus on individual, appreciative therapy for each individual person. Our team meets openly, honestly and at eye level. We support each other, communicate transparently and create a working environment in which you can feel comfortable both professionally and humanly. Our new, bright and modern treatment rooms have perfect transport connections, directly at the Niederholz station in Riehen. In the building is the Migros Fitness, which is always available for your therapy. We are a team of 5 physiotherapists and two front office managers who take over all your administrative tasks.

  • Individual treatment of patients (e.g. orthopaedics / surgery / neurology)
  • Self-responsible working with creative scope in therapy
  • Flexible working hours through an own, bright and spacious treatment room
  • Paid company leave without own leave
  • Collegial, supportive team
  • Modern practice rooms and good equipment
  • Training support and development opportunities
  • Attractive remuneration

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training or studies as a physiotherapist
  • Empathetic and friendly appearance
  • Independent and reliable working methods
  • Teamwork and joy in dealing with people
