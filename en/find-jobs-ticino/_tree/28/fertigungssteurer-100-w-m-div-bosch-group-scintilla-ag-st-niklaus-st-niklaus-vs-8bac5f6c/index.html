Business description

This is what Bosch is all about: about you, our business, our environment.

At Bosch, we shape the future with high-quality technologies and services that inspire enthusiasm and improve people’s lives. Our promise to you is solid: We grow together, enjoy our work and inspire each other.

With its accessories business unit, Bosch Power Tools is the world’s leading supplier of accessories for home and craft customers as well as industrial customers. At the subsidiary Scintilla AG in St. Niklaus in the canton of Valais, a workforce of around 800 employees worldwide develops, manufactures and distributes accessories for power tools.

Job description

As a production manager (f/m/div.), you are responsible for production control in the production department. You are the link between logistics, purchasing and production.

Your tasks essentially include

  • The processing of appointment requests as well as the weekly capacity planning fall within your area of responsibility
  • You guarantee material and product availability in production
  • You work closely with production, purchasing and logistics to ensure smooth operations
  • You analyze production key figures and derive optimization measures from them
  • The examination of the wrong material and the replacement planning round off your area of responsibility

Qualifications

  • Training: Basic technical training (polymechanic, automatic technician (f/m/div.)) ideally with motivation for further training as a process specialist (f/m/div.)
  • Personality and way of working: Communicative, team capable and resilient personality with a structured way of working
  • Experience and know-how: Experience in production technology of advantage, secure application of the MS Office pallets as well as good SAP knowledge desirable
  • Languages: Good communication and expressiveness in German
