Full description

Do you want to work with people and make a difference? Then start your training with us! With us you will become a care professional: with your care and support you will enable people to be cared for where they feel most comfortable - at home. Study of qualified nursing specialist HF in company employment (focus P/K) Your training

  • The holistic care and support of our patients with a wide variety of medical illnesses
  • Nursing tasks - to be taken on step by step
  • Teamwork – as well as working independently

This is what you bring with you

  • Career changers with an EFZ vocational qualification or equivalent schooling - complete the three-year course.
  • Health specialists EFZ - complete the shortened two-year training course.
  • Interest in health, care and medicine – and enjoy contact with people
  • Have fun being on the move – with an e-bike, e-scooter or your own car

Your advantages

  • A strong team - supports you right from the start
  • Careful training - in your areas of operation
  • Responsible and varied work - in a professional environment
