Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • You plan and coordinate the use of all available means of transport
  • With your friendly and competent manner, you support and advise our customers about transport
  • You create individual transport offers for our customers
  • The guidance and support of our chauffeurs and contract drivers are also part of your area of responsibility.
  • Customer complaints and damage cases you approach solution-oriented and professionally
  • Our transport orders are recorded, mutated and billed independently
  • Also in administrative tasks you are reliably involved

Why do you succeed?

  • Very good knowledge of Swiss geography – you know where to go
  • You think economically and find the right solution even in challenging situations
  • Working shifts (between 04:00 and 22:00 in the week change) matches your life rhythm
  • Flexibility, resilience and reliability set you apart
  • You have completed an apprenticeship – ideally in the transport industry
  • With the common EDP applications you know yourself
  • A driving licence Cat. C or C/E as well as experience as a chauffeur are advantageous, but not a must
  • You communicate safely in German – oral as well as written and French skills are a plus
  • As an honest, team-capable and trustworthy personality, you are a perfect fit for us

Your Benefits

  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop yourself - we promote and challenge talents
  • Family culture: You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Own staff restaurant: Enjoy delicious menus at fair prices

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
