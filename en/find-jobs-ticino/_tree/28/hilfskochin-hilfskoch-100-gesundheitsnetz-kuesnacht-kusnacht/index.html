The Gesundheitsnetz Küsnacht AG (GNK), with the Tägerhalde and Wangensbach senior care and health centres housing around 130 residents, as well as a day-care centre, Spitex, senior living and advisory services, and around 250 employees in total, is a non-profit joint-stock company wholly owned by the municipality of Küsnacht.

We are looking for a committed individual to provide hands-on support to our kitchen team on a fixed-term basis to help bridge a staffing shortage.

  • Shared responsibility for safety and hygiene in the kitchen and catering areas
  • Several years of professional experience as a kitchen assistant, preferably in a care home or hospital setting
