Product Manager (m/f/d) Platform and Technologies 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Product Manager (m/f/d) Platform and Technologies Bern / Work Smart, 01.01.2027, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You design a platform on which SBB infrastructure applications critical to railway operations run reliably and make a direct contribution to safety and punctuality in rail transport. Together with an experienced platform team, you will bring modern cloud principles together with existing technologies in your own data center and develop into product management, architecture and collaboration with internal partners. Benefits: Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • You will be responsible for the further development of the DSO TRS internal platform and service strategy and ensure stable and secure operation for our mission-critical applications.
  • From a common roadmap, you derive features and stories and control the backlog via several agile teams.
  • You anchor DevOp’s principles and joint operational responsibility in the everyday life of the teams and strengthen customer orientation towards internal clients.
  • Together with the system architect, you will examine architectural decisions and combine technical requirements with management decisions.
  • You control stakeholders and suppliers and conduct tenders and contract renewals, such as for AWS Outposts, Solace PubSub Plus and database services.
  • In the management of the Small Release Train, you work closely with the RTE (Release Train Engineer), Scrum Master and the system architecture, represent the interests of management and partner teams and promote continuous development.

You bring that with you.

  • You enjoy working in a team, leading with clarity and cooperation and developing a lived DevOps culture together.
  • With strong communication and problem-solving skills, you translate strategy, technical dependencies and operational needs into clear priorities.
  • Several years of experience in professional leadership in agile environments as well as in the responsibility for platforms with high availability distinguish you.
  • In-depth understanding of platform, cloud, messaging and monitoring technologies as well as practice with AWS Outposts, Solace PubSub Plus, monitoring solutions and Tibco Rendez Vous.
  • In German you communicate at level C1 and in English at level B2.
  • A university degree in computer science, business informatics or a comparable education or equivalent practice will qualify you for this role.
