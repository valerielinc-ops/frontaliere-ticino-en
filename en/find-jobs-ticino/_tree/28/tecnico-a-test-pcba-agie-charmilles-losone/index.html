Full description

Technician/a PCBA test Employment: 100% Location: Los Angeles

Your responsibilities

  • Perform the SMD/THT assembly of electronic cards (PCBA) according to technical instructions and IPC standards.
  • Run automatic and functional tests with Flying Probe, ICT, functional simulators and cabinet testing.
  • Check the compliance of the cards to technical specifications and document the test results and do not

compliance.

  • Apply fault search on cards and electronic subgroups and support corrective actions.
  • Collaborate with quality and with the line manager to the continuous improvement of production processes.

Your profile

  • Technical diploma in electronics or electronic AFC.
  • 3 years experience in a similar role in the production environment and testing of electronic components.
  • Operational experience on automatic testing systems, functional simulators and cabinet testing.
  • Readability of electrical schematics and use of basic measuring instruments (multimeter, oscilloscope).

IPC-A-610 certification is a plus.

  • Precise, methodical and able to work in semi-autonomy on diagnosis and testing activities; good attitude to

collaboration.

  • Italian native language
  • English is a plus.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
