Full description

Our rehabilitation occupational therapy service develops its expertise in the fields of neurology and orthopaedics as well as in the detection and treatment of swallowing disorders.

Ergotherapist at 90-100%

Your missions

  • Ensure adequate and high quality care in neurology, orthopaedics
  • Participate in the interdisciplinary therapeutic process in the context of a rehabilitation hospital
  • Actively participate in occupational therapy service activities

Your profile

  • Bachelor of Science HES-SO in occupational therapy or equivalent recognized by the Swiss Red Cross
  • High interest in rehabilitation and interdisciplinary work
  • Experience in one or more of the following disciplines: neurology, geriatrics and/or orthopaedics and general medicine
  • Experience/willingness to train in the management of dysphagic patients, more
  • Experience in outpatient care, more
  • Good mastery of common computer tools

Your skills

  • Responsibility and autonomy
  • Relationship and communication
  • Capacity for analysis, synthesis, anticipation and reflexivity
  • Organizational direction and priorities
  • Ability to adapt / demonstrate flexibility
  • Stress resistance

Further information

  • Working place: Val-de-Ruz site
  • Entry into office: from 1 January 2027 or to be agreed on an indeterminate contract
  • Inquire: Mrs Métrailler Céline, Ergotherapist Head of Unit, +41 79 559 46 96

Any questions concerning the postulation procedure can be addressed by telephone to the Engaging department at +41 32 713 30 13.

The Neuchâtel hospital network (RHNe) includes the sites of Pourtalès (Neuchâtel), La Chaux-de-Fonds, Val-de-Travers, Val-de-Ruz, Le Locle and La Chrysalide. More than 2,600 collaborators provide acute care, rehabilitation and palliative care for the Neuchâtel population.
