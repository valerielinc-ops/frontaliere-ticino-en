Mandated by one of our clients, a company recognized in the field of carpentry and layout in Valais, we are looking for a

Technician ES wood

for a fixed commitment within the company.

You want to evolve in a human-sized company where autonomy, trust and responsibility are part of everyday life? This opportunity will allow you to integrate an evolving dynamic structure and participate in various projects in the field of carpentry and layout.

Your missions and responsibilities

  • Review client requests and analyze their needs.
  • To carry out metres, establish offers and monitor them.
  • Design projects and develop technical plans.
  • Plan production and organize construction sites.
  • Ensure technical, budgetary and logistical monitoring of business.
  • Pilot laying teams and coordinate subcontractors.
  • Ensure relationships with clients, architects, contractors and suppliers.
  • Participate in commercial development and customer loyalty.
  • Work closely with management and contribute to the company's strategic thinking.

Competences

We are waiting

  • Training of ES Wood Technician or equivalent.
  • Minimum experience of 3 years in a similar function.
  • Experience confirmed in managing carpentry and layout projects.
  • Very good knowledge of metres, figures and establishment of offers.
  • Mastery of site planning and monitoring.
  • Excellent coordination capabilities with the various stakeholders.
  • Mastery of technical drawing software.
  • The knowledge of SketchUp is an asset.
  • Person autonomous, organized, rigorous and oriented solutions.
  • Initiative and responsibility.

We offer

  • A function with responsibilities with great autonomy.
  • Enriching and varied projects within a company recognized in its sector.
  • Direct collaboration with the management and various departments of the company.
  • A stimulating, dynamic and human-sized working environment.
  • A corporate culture that promotes exchanges, trust and initiative.
  • Opportunities for professional development and development.
  • A friendly working atmosphere with a strong team spirit.
  • Participation in challenging and diverse technical projects.
  • An absolute discretion in the processing of your application.
  • Immediate entry date or to be agreed upon.
