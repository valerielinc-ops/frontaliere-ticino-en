Full description

For Spitex we are looking for a responsible personality as a Dipl after agreement. Psychiatry specialist HF with case management.

  • As a case-leading care specialist, you control and control all steps in the care process.
  • You are responsible for the careful and complete care documentation.
  • They provide a resource- and goal-oriented care of our customers with mental illnesses.
  • They support our customers in the design and implementation of a self-determined everyday life.
  • Diploma as nurse HF/FH, at least 2 years of professional experience in psychiatric setting
  • Joy and interest in the psychosocial care of older people
  • Self-employed and responsible working practices
  • Knowledge of InterRai CMH and Perigon
  • Driving licence Cat. B

Freedom in the design of your work as well as a motivated care team with a fixed customer base. Our employees also benefit from attractive employment conditions in accordance with Staff Law of the City of Zurich as well as targeted training and further education. In addition to at least four weeks holidays, you are entitled to 6 company holidays.

The Foundation for Old-Age Apartments of the City of Zurich (SAW) operates around 40 urban settlements with more than 2000 affordable apartments for people aged 60 and over. It supports its tenants with everyday, social and nursing services and thus enables them a high quality of life into old age. Its diverse socio-cultural offer supports the development of neighbourly contacts and an active settlement life.
