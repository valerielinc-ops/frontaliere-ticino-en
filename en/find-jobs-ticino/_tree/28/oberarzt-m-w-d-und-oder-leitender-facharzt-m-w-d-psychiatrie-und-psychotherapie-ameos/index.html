Full description

The AMEOS Seeklinikum Brunnen is located on the beautiful Vierwaldstättersee and at the foot of the myth in Switzerland. As a clinic, we offer you an attractive place to work, a college of integrity and an interesting learning and teaching environment.

As a training institution of category B stationary anyway category B ambulant we are established as a committed partner in the further education to psychiatric specialists (m/f/d) and psychological psychotherapists (m/f/d).

For our inpatient treatment offer at the AMEOS Seeklinikum Brunnen, we are looking for an initiative and committed personality who would like to participate in a small and committed team.

Senior physician (m/f/d) and/or senior specialist (m/f/d) psychiatry and psychotherapy — 80-100 %, inpatient and/or partly outpatient

Are you looking for a new challenge? Our clinic specializes in inpatient setting on stress secondary diseases (depression, burnout, anxiety diseases, non-organic insomnias) and has a high level of competence in this area.

Their tasks

  • As a senior physician (m/f/d), you are responsible for the specialist therapeutic management in close cooperation with the senior psychologists (m/f/d) and the nursing station management.
  • You understand the management task to mean that discussions are conducted and moderated along the respective professional competence. In doing so, you strive to ensure that knowledge increases through these disputes and that the best possible approach emerges as a jointly supported path.
  • As a specialist (m/f/d) or advanced in the mediation in further education You are happy to provide the specialist content to our colleagues (m/f/d) in further training as a specialist (m/f/d) psychiatry and psychotherapy. You enjoy promoting and supporting the decision-making competence of your employees.
  • They are given sufficient time for further training.
  • At the same time, you will also have the opportunity to participate in training courses with active contributions up to presentations at accredited training events for medical and psychotherapeutic colleagues (m/f/d).
  • Through the professional discussion, but also by dealing with business-ethical topics across all hierarchical levels, you gain new insights.
  • We attach great importance to the fact that quiet working determines everyday life, as well as a low service load.

Your profile

  • You contribute to projects, inspire the collegial environment with your openness and initiative and gain diversity through new insights.
  • Loyalty and the constructive handling of mistakes are among their core values.
  • They have very good oral and written knowledge of German (at least level C2 or equivalent) and appreciate open and respectful communication.
  • They have an accreditable medical diploma and, if necessary, an accreditable continuing education title, as well as the confirmation of the Medical Occupational Commission (MEBEKO).
