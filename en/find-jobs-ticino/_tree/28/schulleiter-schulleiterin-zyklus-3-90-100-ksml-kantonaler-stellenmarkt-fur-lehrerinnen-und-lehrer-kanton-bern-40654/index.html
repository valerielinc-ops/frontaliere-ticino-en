Full description

Portrait Portrait of the School/Institution Close to the center is Ittigen east of the city of Bern. The municipality of Ittigen has three school locations. The primary schools Rain and Altikofen consist of a total of 44 classes from kindergarten / basic level up to the 6th school year. In the upper-level center we lead 17 classes from the 5th to the 9th school year. Our approx. 1250 students are taught by 150 teachers and curative educators. At the Ittigen school, both students from educational families and from socio-economically burdened backgrounds attend classes. Other-speaking children enrich the classes to a large extent. The school Ittigen is run by 6 school management.

Approximately 320 pupils attend the Upper Level Center in 2 intermediate, 14 upper level classes and one class for special promotion 7-9. They are taught by 35 teachers and 5 curative educators. The municipality of Ittigen runs the upper-level center in model 3a with level lessons in German, French and mathematics in the three levels Real, Sek. and spezSek. The upper school centre is pedagogically, personnel-wise, financially and administratively run by a school management. The area MR (measures regular school) and the two middle school classes are not part of the tasks of the school management.

Tasks

  • Educational, human, financial and administrative management of the upper-level centre
  • Further development of school and teaching quality
  • Design and support of school development processes
  • Promoting a cooperative and appreciative team culture
  • Ensuring the smooth running of schools
  • Cooperation with authorities, parents and external specialists
  • Networking with Cycle 2 and secondary schools
  • Participation in the school management conference and in the education commission

As a school management, together with the school management team and the education department management, you shape the further development of our school. They lead with clarity and empathy, create orientation and promote trusting cooperation.

Requirements

  • Teaching patent for elementary school
  • Several years of teaching experience, preferably in cycle 3
  • Completed school management training or willingness to address it
  • Leadership experience in school and/or comparable context
  • Educational understanding and joy in school development
  • Conceptual action for a school
  • Ability to communicate openly and clearly
  • Teamwork and good empathy
  • Leadership and organisational talent
  • High social skills and the ability to connect people
  • Resilience, negotiating skills and enthusiasm

We offer

  • Temporary employment as deputy from October 2027
  • Permanent employment from February 2028
  • School Secretariat 40 %
  • A dedicated, supportive team with motivated and dedicated teachers
  • Engaged school management conference
