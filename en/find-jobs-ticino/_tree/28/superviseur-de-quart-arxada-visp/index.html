About Arxada Arxada is a global leader in innovative solutions to protect our planet. Our cutting-edge technologies, extensive regulatory expertise, and experience in production and process development support our customers in efficiently protecting nutrition, health, and infrastructure through chemistry and biotechnology that promote sustainability. We offer a wide range of ingredients and services for various end markets, including health and nutrition, home and personal care, professional hygiene, paints and coatings, and wood protection. With customers in over 100 countries, the company achieved a turnover of 2 billion CHF in 2024. Arxada’s headquarters are located in Basel, Switzerland. Globally, the company employs 3,200 people across 24 production sites and 14 research and development centers, all committed to the success of our customers. Are you ready for more responsibility? To strengthen our operational department MPP3 Monoanlagen at our site in Visp, Switzerland, we are looking for a committed, leadership-oriented person with a strong sense of responsibility for the role of Shift Supervisor. In this key position, you will take technical and personal responsibility for your shift team and ensure safe, efficient, and high-quality operation of the plant. You will be the central point of contact between production, technical, and management and actively contribute to the continuous improvement of our processes. ## Main tasks and responsibilities:

  • Personnel responsibility for the assigned shift team and active support for the operations management team in team development
  • Structured responsibility for the training and professional and personal development of employees
  • Ensuring smooth shift operations, including organization and coordination of all incoming work orders and ensuring proper shift handover
  • Management and planning of the team’s personnel, considering orders, priorities, break management, and absences
  • Ensuring contact and coordination with external parties at the plant in case of incidents (e.g., gatekeeper, on-call duty, fire brigade)
  • Ensuring the correct execution of all work in the assigned work area, in compliance with current safety, environmental, and quality regulations ## Qualifications and experience:
  • Strong commitment to actively promoting and strengthening the safety culture
  • At least 5–10 years of experience as a shift supervisor, deputy shift supervisor, or plant specialist in chemical or manufacturing service companies, with solid knowledge of process control systems
  • Ideally completed CPT or CPP training (former certified operator) or willingness to complete it
  • Motivation and willingness to work in shifts (including night shifts and weekends)
  • Openness to new technologies and continuous professional and personal development
  • Committed, flexible, and independent work style with a high level of reliability
  • Strong social and teamwork skills ## What we offer Arxada offers a world-class offering in two distinct areas:
  • Microbial Control Solutions (MCS) focuses on the threat posed to humans and the environment by microorganisms in five business areas: professional hygiene, home and personal care, wood protection, paints and coatings, and material protection.
  • Nutrition, Care & Environmental (NCE) provides materials for the production of composites for electronics, aerospace, and other markets, as well as vitamins and food supplements, high-performance chemicals and intermediates, and raw materials for a wide range of high-performance applications.
