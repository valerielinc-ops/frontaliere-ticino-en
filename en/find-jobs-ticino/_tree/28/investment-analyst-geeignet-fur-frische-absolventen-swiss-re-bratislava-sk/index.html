Full description

Does work with financial products and investments excite you? Then AM operations is the best way how to launch your career! About the Role This is an opportunity for you to join our Asset Management Operations team whose purpose is to support the trade life cycle processing of the products managed by the various desks, such as rates and credit products, securities lending, repos, derivative payments, collateral payments, real estate investments, acquisitions and many more. As an Investment Analyst, you will be responsible for execution of daily cash and position reconciliation of Swiss Re Investment accounts. This role will allow you to see all events related to investment lifecycle and you will play a key role in ensuring all these events are executed on market as expected.

Key responsibilities

  • Execution of daily cash and position reconciliation of Swiss Re investment accounts
  • Monitoring, active investigation, proactive resolution and reporting of any reconciliation outstanding items
  • Reporting of investment activity to Finance
  • Active participation in initiatives and projects
  • Communication with internal and external stakeholders
  • Providing support to internal / external audit
  • Challenging Status Quo – Suggesting improvements to enhance processes and tasks

About You

  • Ability to learn, openness and flexibility for new tasks and challenges
  • Detail orientation and good analytical skills
  • Basic investments knowledge
  • Ability to set priorities and meet deadlines
  • Excellent command of written and spoken English
  • Strong teamwork and "can do" attitude
  • University degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics or another related degree
  • Advanced knowledge of MS Excel
