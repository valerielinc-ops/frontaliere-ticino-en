Full description

The GBS St.Gallen is an important vocational training institution in Eastern Switzerland. We train around 4,500 vocational learners in over 40 professions in basic industrial and commercial education. In the Bridge Offerings department with around 450 apprentices, young people between the ages of 15 and 21 are prepared for entering the professional world. We work team- and performance-oriented and with a focus on modern forms of teaching and learning.

What to expect

  • German, English, French, subject lessons, elective courses
  • In the basic lessons, you accompany the learners as part of self-organized learning (SOL) in our learning environments
  • You take on tasks as a learning coach and at the same time teach as a teacher in the classic teaching setting
  • Fulfillment of the professional mandate for teachers at vocational colleges
  • Active participation in the department, department and school

What sets it apart

  • Communicative personality with strong social skills
  • Initiative and robust
  • Flexible and open to new developments
  • High-level teacher training or curative/healing teacher training
  • Secure and sound acting as a learning coach and teacher as well as teaching experience at upper school
  • Self-organized learning (SOL) experience or willingness to complete a corresponding further education
  • Exemplary appearance and joy in dealing with learners and teaching

What we offer

We offer you an interesting and challenging job in a motivated environment. The recruitment takes place under the Supplementary Regulation on the employment of teachers at vocational and training centres. Are you interested and motivated? We look forward to your complete application documents. Please submit your digital dossier until 5. November 2026 via the link below
