Full description

Do you want to take on an entrepreneurial responsibility in tandem with the site manager, develop the performance of the Geneva Mail Processing Logistics Centre (CLT) and support the teams in future transformations? As head of CLT Geneva, you contribute directly to the quality of our services, to the satisfaction of our clients and to the development of the Post Office of tomorrow. Thanks to the complementarity of your skills and shared leadership, you are advancing the sorting sector in a sustainable, efficient and close to the field. With you, we shape efficient, humane and forward-looking mail logistics.

Make a difference

  • As a member of a management team, you are responsible for the Geneva mail processing logistics centre and contribute to its operational, human and strategic objectives.
  • With your driving team, you guarantee reliable, efficient and customer-oriented service delivery in daily operations.
  • You drive, develop and coach those responsible attached to you. You encourage a culture of collaboration, responsibility and dialogue.
  • You analyse the results, define clear priorities and implement the measures necessary to achieve the agreed objectives, while identifying synergies and potential for optimisation. You also ensure efficient, safe and quality logistics processes throughout the industry.
  • You lead organizational transformations and actively accompany teams in changes in processes, tools and working methods.
  • You inities, direct or support projects and working groups related to culture, organization, health, safety, sustainability and benefit development.
  • You are part of the West Service Region driving team, contribute to the definition and implementation of its priorities and, as an interlocutor or local contact for Logistics Services, maintain a strong network with internal and external partners while representing the Post Office in your area.

What you bring

  • You have several years of experience driving a large operational unit and can lead through several levels of responsibility.
  • You have completed advanced vocational training or graduate studies, ideally in the areas of leadership, logistics, process management or business economy.
  • With your proven experience in leading change and managing projects, you act with an entrepreneurial vision, translate goals into concrete priorities and assume responsibility for their implementation.
  • You see change as an opportunity, act with courage and open-mindedness in a complex environment and contribute to collective success by developing the skills of your teams while embodying the values and behaviours expected of one or another.
  • You communicate in a clear, convincing and appropriate way to your various interlocutors, including in demanding situations.
  • You master Microsoft 365 tools and use data in a targeted way to drive activity and make decisions.
  • You have very good knowledge of French, oral and written, and you also have good knowledge of German.
