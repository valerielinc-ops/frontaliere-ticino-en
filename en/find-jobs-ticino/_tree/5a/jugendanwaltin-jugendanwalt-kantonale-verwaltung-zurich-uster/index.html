Full description

The Juvenile Advocacy Office See/Oberland, with its approximately 18 employees from the fields of law, social work and administration, is responsible for the districts of Hinwil, Meilen, Pfäffikon and Uster and leads the criminal investigations and enforcement of criminal sanctions against minors. To complement our team of the See/Oberland Youth Advocacy Office, we are looking for an engaged and open-minded personality immediately or by agreement. These tasks are waiting for you:

  • They conduct and carry out criminal investigations against juveniles and conclude them with a criminal order, recruitment order, non-execution order or with charge to the juvenile court. You also represent charges before the juvenile court.
  • They exercise individual judicial functions within the framework of criminal proceedings (issuing of criminal orders).
  • In cooperation with social workers, you clarify the need for youth measures and order and implement protection measures and penalties under juvenile law.
  • They are willing to provide regular pikettdienst in the competent district and cantonal pikettdienst several times a year (including night and weekend). This is what distinguishes you:
  • You have a completed master’s degree in law and have advantageously acquired the attorney’s patent.
  • You have many years of experience in criminal justice as well as in-depth knowledge in the field of juvenile criminal law.
  • Your interest in pedagogy and psychology as well as your broad life experience enable you to deal with young people in a sovereign and professional manner.
  • Your strengths include your resilience and decision-making ability, your assertiveness as well as a basic attitude towards people.
  • They have strong communication skills, are capable of teamwork and appreciate interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • They are characterized by flexibility, are willing to learn, resilient and digitally affinity. Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete online application.
