Full description

Sales consultant in the store Lucerne 50% (M/F) Kuhn Rikon is an independent family business with headquarters and production location in Rikon in the Tösstal. We design, develop, produce and distribute well thought-out and high-quality products that protect the environment, make life easier and bring joy to the kitchen. To complement our team in the Store Lucerne, we are looking for a cooking-enthusiastic, active and flexible sales person for this function.

Talk to you...

  • Working in a small sales team?
  • to sell actively at the POS and to advise our customers competently?
  • Responsibility for optimal goods management and taking over the cash?
  • Working on different days of the week, even on Saturdays?

From our new employee we wish...

  • completed training in the retail trade (industry knowledge is an advantage),
  • experience in the sale of high-quality consumer goods,
  • customer-oriented thinking and acting,
  • social skills, resilience, flexibility, independence and teamwork,
  • the ability to communicate positively, openly, honestly and winning,
  • good user knowledge of the common MS-Office programs,
  • very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing.

Our team includes a sales consultant who...

  • enjoys selling and advising, can tackle and wants to make a difference,
  • enjoys cooking and fulfills demanding customer wishes,
  • Takes responsibility, appreciates honest and open communication,
  • would like to work in a team and achieve goals together.

You can also look forward to these “ingredients”...

  • an innovative family business at the traditional production site in Rikon,
  • an individual training and an exciting task in a motivated team,
  • a wide variety of shopping incentives,
  • a 40 hour week and between 25 and 30 days holiday according to GAV,
  • Health promotion for employees: vital@kuhnrikon,
  • And much more...
