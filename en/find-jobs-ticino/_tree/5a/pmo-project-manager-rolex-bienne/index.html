Full description

Introduction

For our Procurement, Support & Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a PMO Project Manager (H/F) whose main mission will be to monitor and support a portfolio of projects.

Responsibilities

  • Follow the methodology and use the project management techniques defined by the OMP
  • Ensure before implementation, that all aspects necessary for the conduct of the project have been put in place, and in particular that the Sponsor has agreed to implement the project plans
  • Contributing to achieving objectives
  • Set and monitor project indicators (objective, cost, time, risk, communication, resources, etc.) and communication
  • Manage consolidated planning, define and track critical path and key milestones
  • Ensure that the required deliverables have an identified lead
  • Involving the various departments/lines and other relevant stakeholders from the start of the project
  • Coordinate and inform project team members
  • Coordinate project reporting and communication activities;
  • Alert COPIL and CODIR of non-tolerance deviations and present alternative solutions proposed by the project manager
  • Convene and prepare CODIR and COPILs; co-animate COPILs
  • Complete and record the project's balance sheet
  • Support, advise and coach project manager on project management issues
  • Ensuring that the right level of resources and the smooth running of projects are respected
  • Ensuring the motivation and commitment of teams
  • Ensuring sound project documentation

Profile

  • Desired university training
  • Confirmed and successful experience over 5 years in inter-departmental project management
  • Project Management Certification (PMP, PSM I and PRINCE2 Practitioner or equivalent)
  • Perfect mastery of advanced practices and tools in project management
  • Competence in organization and planning
  • Mastering computer tools for reporting, including PowerPoint and Excel
  • Excellent written and oral communication skills
  • Intergent, assertiveness and flexibility in the relationship with all stakeholders

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
