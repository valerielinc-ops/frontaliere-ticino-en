Full description

The North Face EMEA is looking for an Associate, Marketplace Marketing to join our Marketing team based in Stabio, Switzerland for a temporary contract (1 year).

The North Face is the premier exploration company in the world. We spark curiosity. We dare to disrupt. We create communities. And we lead with integrity. We are a community of explorers and are constantly looking for new ideas, places and people to connect with.

Let’s Talk About the Role

As an Associate, Marketplace Marketing reporting to the Director, Marketplace Marketing TNF EMEA, you will support the coordination and execution of brand initiatives across The North Face’s retail and wholesale partners. The role focuses on ensuring consistency between channels, optimizing implementation, consolidating operational reports, and improving the efficiency of communication between internal teams and stakeholders. You will play a key role in supporting daily marketplace marketing activities within the EMEA region, helping to elevate the brand presence and visibility across all marketplaces.

What You Will Do

  • Coordinate brand initiatives across retail and wholesale channels, ensuring alignment under a common marketing framework.
  • Support the marketing needs of various EMEA locations and sales channels, maintaining consistency and quality of execution.
  • Oversee the operational implementation of creative briefs and ensure timely delivery of campaign materials.
  • Consolidate reports related to campaign execution, marketing investments, and overall initiative summaries.
  • Develop templates and frameworks for management consolidation and reporting to senior leadership.
  • Consolidate data to inform the brand map by channel across EMEA.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with internal stakeholders to ensure effective alignment and clear deliverables.
  • Support continuous improvements in marketing process efficiency and operational workflows.
  • Contribute to maintaining brand integrity and consistency across all consumer touchpoints.

Skills for Success

What You’ll Bring

  • Proven organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong analytical mindset with experience in consolidating and interpreting marketing data.
  • Proficiency in Microsoft PowerPoint and Excel; able to present insights and reports clearly.
  • Understanding of marketing principles and channel activation initiatives (retail and wholesale).
  • Attention to detail and commitment to delivering high-quality work.
  • Excellent collaboration skills and the ability to coordinate across departments and teams.
  • A self-starter approach with curiosity and initiative to learn and contribute proactively.
  • Prior experience within marketing, brands or retail/dtc environments preferred.
  • Fluent in English; additional European languages are a plus.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
