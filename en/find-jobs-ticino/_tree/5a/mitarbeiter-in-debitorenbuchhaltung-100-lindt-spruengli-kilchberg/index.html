About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics centre, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland, we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Transport House in Lucerne. More than 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

What you do with us

  • Daily bank and postal payments incl. Capture and edit account reconciliations
  • Complete account adjustments and the dunning and operation system independently
  • Monitor and maintain credit limits and issue order clearances
  • Check and release invoices and prepare and book manual invoices, credit notes and internal rebookings
  • Maintain debtor master data of our national and international customers and as Master Data Expert support the debtor process
  • Create evaluations and statistics for monthly and annual accounts as well as participate in goods inventories of our Lindt Chocolate Shops
  • Monitor work processes in debtor accounting and continuously review process and ICS documentation and update them as needed

When you fit in with us

  • Completed basic commercial training and several years of professional experience in a comparable position
  • Advantageous training as an accounting clerk (or equivalent)
  • Good knowledge of SAP and MS Excel
  • Team capable, proactive and humorous person who thinks analytically and communicates step by step
  • Independent and reliable working methods with high quality standards
  • Hands-on mentality, committed, open and eager to develop
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing as well as good knowledge of English for cooperation in our international customer environment
