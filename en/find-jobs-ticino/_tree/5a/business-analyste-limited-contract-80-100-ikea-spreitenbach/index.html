Interest in understanding the business and its main drivers.

Your responsibilities

Support the Business Navigation & Operations Manager in securing performance, profitability, and the financial frame of the Remote Customer Meeting Point.

Clear understanding of business goals, and priorities to follow through on commitments to achieve results in line with the IKEA vision and values.

Identify problems and build a good stakeholder relationship to establish a problem-solving culture.

Perform data analysis to identify trends, opportunities, and risks.

Provide relevant, user-friendly business information, and promote standardized reporting.

Challenge existing processes and ways of working.

Be an ambassador for global business information solutions and common tools and provide ad hoc reports when standard solutions are not available.

About this work area

The Business Analyst/ Business Navigation role in the Remote Customer Meeting Point has a wide area of work. You will be collaborating with different Stakeholders and developing a deep understanding of the business and its operational processes. You will be involved in Finance, Operations, Customer Insights, Staff Planning, and other areas.

Does this sound like your next challenge? IKEA offers an exciting and empowering work environment in a global workplace. As the world’s leader in life at home, you have exceptional opportunities to grow and develop together with us.

We see ourselves as influencers – we steer the IKEA business by finding the truth behind the figures. Together we secure compliance with local and international laws and reporting standards; we assure that accounting processes are efficient and that tax compliance meets business needs. We’re a diverse group of people driven by providing reliable financial data to a wide variety of stakeholders. At the end of the day, that’s one of the foundations to make IKEA even better!
