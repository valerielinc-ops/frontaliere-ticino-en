Metalconstructor 100% We gather what belongs to the whole! The Anker Swiss Group, born in April 2021, is growing and is now also present in ICTINO. Do you want to be part of a future-oriented society? On behalf of our esteemed customer in the sheet metal processing industry, we are looking for the following professional figure immediately: LASER PROGRAMME / DEVELOPMENT 100% Working area: Ashtrays Main handling

  • Programming and management of CNC laser cutting systems for sheet metal processing.
  • Development of sheet metal parts starting from the technical drawings provided by the customer.
  • Realization and modification of 2D and 3D drawings for the preparation of production programs.
  • nesting and optimization of material use.
  • Analysis of the technical feasibility of the components to be realized.
  • Collaboration with workshop and production to ensure process quality and efficiency.
  • Programming laser machines and, preferably, CNC punching machines.
  • Verification of quotas and control of the conformity of technical drawings.
  • Production support for any changes and improvements in existing programs.
  • Management of technical documentation relating to orders.

Skills required

  • Experience in programming CNC laser systems for sheet metal processing.
  • Ability to develop sheet metal details from technical drawings received by customers.
  • Excellent knowledge of the mechanical technical design.
  • Experience in reading and interpreting constructive tables and executive drawings.
  • 2D and/or 3D CAD software knowledge (Autocad, SolidWorks, Creo or equivalents).
  • Ability to optimize production cycles and material consumption.
  • Precision, operational autonomy and quality orientation.

Preferential requirements

  • Experience in programming CNC punching machines.
  • From light carpentry workshops or sheet metal processing.
  • Experience gained in the technical development of bent sheet metal components.
  • Knowledge of bending, cutting and assembly processes of sheet metal.
  • Experience gained in the Swiss market.
  • Driving license category B and half.
  • Immediate or short-term availability.

We offer

  • Insertion into a firm and structured company.
  • Vocational and technologically advanced working environment.
  • Possibility of professional growth and long term employment.
  • Interesting wage conditions tailored to the experience gained.
  • Constant support from the Anker Swiss team.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
