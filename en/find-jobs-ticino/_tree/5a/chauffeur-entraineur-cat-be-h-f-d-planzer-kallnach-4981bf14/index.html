Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Marti.

As a firmly rooted company in the Seeland region, we offer comprehensive transport and storage solutions and ensure cleanliness in over 30 communities with our waste transports. Our success is based on motivated employees and modern infrastructure. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

Tasks

  • You distribute general cargo shipments throughout Switzerland in accordance with scheduled tour planning and ensure reliable and timely delivery
  • As a die-hard chauffeur, you enjoy passing on your expertise to apprentices, lateral entrants and new drivers.
  • With pleasure you accompany our newcomers on their first trips with motor cars and trailers and stand by them with advice and deeds
  • You teach practical everything about cargo securing, trailer, vehicle body, lifting platform, as well as a safe and efficient driving style
  • Together with you, your vehicle is the most important and always visible business card of our company – so you take care of its care and maintenance

Profile

  • You have mastered driving with trailers of the BE category and are safely on the road
  • Thanks to your patient and appreciative nature, you take time to understand processes.
  • You bring a high degree of flexibility and are ready to adjust your working hours to the daily requirements and transport volume
  • VZV training is beneficial, but not a prerequisite
  • Do you have an ADR card? Perfect!
  • Your criminal record is impeccable, there is no complaint with you (please send along)
  • Orally you can safely express yourself in German or French

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Quality is important to us and rewarded
  • You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
