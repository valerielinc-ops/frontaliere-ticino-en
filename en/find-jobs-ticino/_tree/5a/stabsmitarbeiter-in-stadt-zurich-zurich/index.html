Full description

For the maternity representation of the current job holder, we are looking for a committed, administratively experienced and communication-strong personality.

The position is limited in time until the end of December 2027. The job starts as soon as possible or by agreement.

  • You ensure the uniform high quality of all audit reports by taking responsibility for editorial editing, final inspection and digital workflow.
  • They maintain the exchange with politics, media and external supervisory authorities, process inquiries and prepare the reporting documents for the commissions.
  • They write demanding correspondence and structure the agenda for the management, coordinate questions from Parliament and departments and are responsible for internal communication.
  • They coordinate procurement, carry out protocols, manage the site and ensure quality management for management processes as well as general support.
  • They organize meetings, workshops, training sessions and events and take over the entire administrative support.
  • They have completed vocational training and have several years of practical experience in public administration, which is an indispensable condition. Ideal are experiences from a comparable staff or administration office. Tertiary education or training is desirable, but practical aptitude and familiarity with administrative procedures are paramount.
  • They are characterized by an exceptionally professional, stylish and target group-oriented expressive ability in words and writing, with experience in dealing with political actors, media and demanding correspondence.
  • They convince through a careful, methodical and reliable working method, networked thinking and can set priorities efficiently in complex environments.
  • Absolute trustworthiness, confidentiality and high social competence are a matter of course for you.

We offer you an appreciative climate, very good employment conditions and targeted further training. Work in a motivated team at the central Zurich-Stauffacher location and benefit from the opportunity to work flexibly from home one day a week.

The Financial Control of the City of Zurich is an independent, ISO-certified specialist body of the municipal financial supervision. With the expertise of our 25 employees, we support the municipal council and the city council in exercising their supervision of the administration. The staff accompanies the management strategically, operationally, administratively and communicatively.
