Full description

A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a wide range of activities in the family environment, you can also expect the possibility of flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region. Are you looking for meaningfulness, but also fairness? Then you are right here.

As a result of the retirement of the current position holder, we are looking for a hospital specialist to work in our modern and very well-equipped intensive care unit with a wide range of intensive care medical conditions. The expandable station is SGI certified for the operation of 8 beds and training facility category B for assistant doctors.

This is waiting for you

  • A varied and responsible executive position in a collegial team with flat hierarchies
  • A future-oriented intensive care unit that you can further develop with the other cadre doctors
  • Long-term cooperation with personal development opportunities
  • Various opportunities for your own further education

Become part of our team

  • Federal Specialist Title Intensive Medicine
  • If possible, also specialist title General Internal Medicine or Anesthesiology
  • Several years of professional experience in intensive care
  • Enjoyment of the education and training of assistant doctors
