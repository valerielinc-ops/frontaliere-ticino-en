Responsibility As a production employee / laboratory worker (m/f/d), you play a decisive role in our team.

Your activities include

  • Careful implementation of manufacturing processes and analytical protocols according to standard operating procedures (SOPs), taking into account the highest quality standards.
  • Preparation of buffers and solutions
  • Purification of proteins by the following methods: cell disruption by high-pressure or ultrasound disruption, precipitation, chromatography, ultrafiltration and diafiltration, dialysis.
  • Independent operation and control of automated laboratory facilities and systems (process control system (PLS), PCS7 Simatic Batch, Unicorn).
  • Conduct and evaluate analytical tests to ensure the quality and efficiency of our processes.
  • Active participation in the implementation of manufacturing processes on existing and new plants and the commissioning of automated plants (e.g. phase tests, recipe).
  • Identification and resolution of technical and process-side challenges (troubleshooting).
  • Support for validation and qualification measures of our processes and plants.

Your Profile | Qualifications

  • Completed vocational training as a technician or Bachelor in a relevant field
  • Or alternatively completed training as a chemical laboratory, biology laboratory, chemical or similar qualification (e.g. CTA, BTA) with several years of professional experience
  • Experience in production and analysis (ideally of enzymes), combined with the ability to work precisely and efficiently.
  • Competence in creating recipes for controlling automated large-scale production plants
  • High accuracy and responsibility in compliance with quality and safety standards (e.g. GMP).
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing to fully meet documentation and communication requirements.
  • Technical skill (high interest in IT and process engineering) and team spirit, coupled with the willingness to face new challenges proactively.

The position is limited to 2 years.
