Material Planner (m/f/d) 80-100%

Your main responsibilities

  • Determine the required materials and generating purchase orders ensuring that the material is sourced according to the defined lead time and the inbound cost is under control.
  • Balance the trade-off between inventory levels, inbound costs and customer service, working to provide the maximum service for the internal production.
  • Ensure the consistent and adequate supply of materials necessary for internal production.
  • Manage inventory issues, schedule changes, and cancellations.
  • Liaise with customer support, suppliers, and distributors, maintaining good relationship with all of them
  • Manage master data and analyze SAP data to ensures that they are always correct and up to date and extract business relevant insights.

• Learn the basics of the inbound flows and their cost drivers to be able to act as deputy of the team Inbound Specialist

What you bring

  • Bachelor degree in Engineering, Economy o Math.
  • At least 2 years experience in multinational & structured companies in procurement and/or material planning functions.
  • Advanced knowledge of SAP MM and Excel.
  • Fluent in Italian and English (German is a plus).
