Full description

Training station Dipl. Emergency paramedics* in HF from 1. July 2028 You want a profession where no day is exactly like the other? During your training at Surselva, you will learn to take responsibility in emergency medical situations, make decisions and be there for patients together in the team.

The Surselva rescue is IVR recognized and part of the Regionalspital Surselva AG, which is responsible for the extended basic and emergency care in the tourism region of Bündner Oberland. Join our team and gain the knowledge and skills needed to help people in emergency medical situations during your training.

Paramedics lead and organize emergency responses. They are responsible for the preclinical emergency care of patients of all age groups and transport them to a hospital for further treatment. You perform these tasks independently or in cooperation with other health and rescue professionals. You will receive the necessary authority from the medical management of the emergency service.

This is what you bring with you

  • You have a 3-year basic education with a federal certificate of ability or a specialised school degree or Matura
  • At the beginning of your training you have a minimum age of 23 years
  • You have had a driving licence for category B (121 or 122) for two years and will acquire category C1 and D1 before starting your training
  • Your competence analysis health HF (Multicheck health /HF paramedic and personality analysis) must not be older than three years at the time of application

This is what awaits you

  • Training in a demanding rural area
  • You will learn to organize emergency missions and perform them professionally.
  • You work closely with health and rescue professionals
  • You take care of patients of all age groups in medical emergency situations and accompany them until handover in the hospital
  • External internships give you additional insights into the urban rescue service
  • High personal responsibility
  • Internal and cantonal learning days complement your practical training
  • A dedicated training team accompanies you on your way to the diploma

Are you ready for the next step? We look forward to your application!

Please send us the following proof

Competence analysis Health HF / Multicheck · Professional health HF rescue incl. Personality analysis. Copy of driving licence· Criminal records · ADMAS extract · Proof of the passed admission day at Emergency Training Centre AG until the end of January 2027
