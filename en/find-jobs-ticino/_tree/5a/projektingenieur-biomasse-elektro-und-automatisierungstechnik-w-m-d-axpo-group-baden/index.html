Full description

Pensum: 80% or more | Bathing

Shape the energy future with us! Do you want to move ambitious infrastructure projects? Then you are exactly right at Axpo. As a project engineer with a focus on electrical and automation technology, you are responsible for the electrical planning and implementation of our biomass and biogas projects – from the first idea to commissioning.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You are responsible for your electrical and automation technology department in projects of all sizes – from planning and building input to procurement and implementation. You keep an eye on costs, deadlines and quality and regularly coordinate with the overall project manager about the project progress.
  • You plan electrical systems as well as control and control systems, define technical concepts and interfaces and coordinate the implementation of building permit requirements.
  • You will produce technical reports, documents and specifications, develop cost calculations and budgets and support the project teams as an electrotechnical specialist.
  • You carry out tenders, create the necessary documents professionally and on time and with the most supporting award and procurement.
  • You coordinate external planning and programming services as well as the creation of plans, schemes and technical documentation. In addition, you monitor the assembly, accompany the commissioning and ensure a structured transfer to the company.
  • You represent Axpo as a client in your area of expertise and coordinate building permit procedures and technical clarifications with authorities, specialist planners and entrepreneurs.
  • You bring in ideas, shape processes and continuously develop our working methods. Depending on the interest and development, further development towards overall project management is possible.

Profile & Requirements

  • You have completed basic electrotechnical training, for example as an electrical installer, electrical planner, automatic engineer or a comparable qualification.
  • You have a further qualification in electrical engineering, for example as a technician HF or engineer FH. An equivalent qualification due to many years of successful professional experience also fits.
  • You have experience in planning, project planning, tendering and realization of electrical systems as well as control and control systems.
  • You know the relevant Swiss standards, regulations and safety regulations and apply them safely. Knowledge of PLC and SCADA systems, measurement technology, drives and plant commissioning are advantageous.
  • Experience with biogas plants as well as energy and process plants is a plus.
  • You work in a structured, independent and solution-oriented way, quickly address new topics and keep track of several parallel projects.
  • You have very good knowledge of German in words and writing.

Is this new challenge electrifying you? Roger Hofer is looking forward to your application.

We are working on projects that promote a sustainable energy supply in Switzerland. You can expect an experienced team in a professional and varied environment. If you like working in the office as well as on our facilities and tackle topics independently, you are a good fit for us. Become part of our growing engineering team!
