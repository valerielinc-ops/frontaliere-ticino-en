Build your future with us!

Are you ready for a new challenge? We are looking for a person with a strong attitude to sales, a solid experience in the Ticino insurance market, and an independent and flexible indole. Our Broker Consultant is the first point of reference for all brokers with AXA and their customers, ready to guarantee a punctual and quality service.

Become part of our Distribution. Our industry is the window on our customers. We are committed daily to responding to their needs with proactive, progressive and personal consulting solutions – perfectly in line with “Customer first”, our most important business value.

Here's what's waiting for you

  • Provide advice and assistance to partner brokers, as well as training on insurance products
  • Care and consolidate relationships with brokers
  • Working on all Ticino territory
  • Actively manage offers, assignments and cancellations
  • Organize annual meetings of brokers and representation at AXA events
  • Create and implement annual and action planning aligned with broker goals and segmentation
  • Coordinate activities and ensure information with other services and their specialists
  • Working closely with the Back Office team

That's what we're looking for.

  • Strong customer and sales orientation
  • Professional experience in insurance within the Ticino market
  • Knowledge and passion for insurance in general
  • Good knowledge of the Non-Life sector (diploma is preferential title)
  • Entertainment, high commitment and ability to work under pressure
  • Excellent knowledge of Italian and good knowledge of German spoken and written

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status. Note: this listing allows remote work — teleworking above 25 % of your time can shift your social-security base to Italy, so confirm the agreed remote share with HR before signing.
