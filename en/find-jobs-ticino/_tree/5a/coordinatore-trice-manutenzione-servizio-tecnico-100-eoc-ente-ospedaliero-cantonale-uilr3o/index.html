Technical Service - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite Ticino hospital is present with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our technical service team at the EOC Rehabilitation Clinic (CREOC), headquarters of Novaggio we look for a(a) Coordinator/maintenance Technical Service (100%) Profile: For this place we look for a dynamic person with strong manual and organizational skills as well as a strong predisposition to teamwork. It is flexible and has a good spirit of initiative. He can keep calm under stress even in difficult situations finding, with his own knowledge and technical skills, effective solutions.
