Full description

Support Engadin St. Moritz AG provides high-quality specialist services in the areas of finance/accounting, employee management and computer science. In doing so, it supports important service providers in the Upper Engadin in achieving their entrepreneurial goals. The Support Engadin team is looking for an employee in Human Resources 80-100% as of February 1, 2027 or by appointment.

Your impact and design space

  • Support of several companies in various areas of human resources
  • Administrative work in wage processing, social security, day allowance management, employee recruitment and much more
  • Contact person for employees, supervisors and third parties
  • Topic management or project management tasks depending on personal background

You have

  • Affinity for employees and pleasure in their administration
  • Ideally, training and/or experience in Human Resources & Abacus
  • Enjoying direct contact with employees in a lively environment
  • Self-employed and reliable working methods
  • Style-confident written expression in German as well as knowledge in Italian and English are advantageous

What we offer you

  • A corporate culture that leaves space and encourages creativity
  • Exciting mandates from tourism, hospitality/gastronomy, health, education and events
  • Attractive working conditions and an annual working time model
  • Close cooperation in an established and committed team
