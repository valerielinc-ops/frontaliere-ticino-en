Full description

You are a real all-rounder with hands-on mentality, think along and take responsibility? You keep an overview even in hectic situations and appreciate a varied working day? For the Club School St. Gallen we are looking for a versatile personality who supports the school with technical understanding, organizational talent and service orientation.

Luogo di lavoro: Club School Migros, Club School St. Gallen, Bahnhofplatz 2, 9000 St. Gallen

Mansioni

  • Responsible for the timely provision of school and course facilities incl. Infrastructure
  • Ensure proper operation and maintenance of technical equipment (HLKS, fire detection, sprinkler and safety systems)
  • Carrying out maintenance, maintenance and minor repair work and ensuring the operational readiness of classrooms and function rooms
  • Detection, correction and coordination of technical failures including alarm management
  • Supporting teachers, employees and guests with technical concerns, IT questions and questions about teaching and presentation technology
  • Coordination and cooperation with the cleaning partner, as well as with internal and external partners
  • Participation in the implementation of energy saving measures

Requirements

  • Professional experience: beneficial in technical building maintenance, facility management or home service, ideally in a school or educational establishment
  • Initial or continuing education: in the technical or craft sector (e.g. electrical, heating, sanitary, building services, maintenance or similar)
  • Good IT user knowledge and interest in digital tools
  • Place of residence St. Gallen or agglomeration
  • Pragmatic approach to technical and digital challenges
  • Strong service and service orientation as well as enjoyment of contact with different stakeholders
  • High level of independence, initiative and a sense of responsibility
  • Good physical strength is a prerequisite and pleasure in practical work
  • Readiness for occasional assignments outside regular working hours
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • L-GAV: Attractive Land collective employment contract
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in self-selected courses
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
