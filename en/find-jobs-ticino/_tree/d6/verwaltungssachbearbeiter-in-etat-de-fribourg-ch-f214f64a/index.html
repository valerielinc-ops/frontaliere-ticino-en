Appointing authority / department / institution

The Office of Public Health (GesA) is tasked with ensuring health promotion and maintaining and restoring population health. Health must be accessible and affordable for everyone. The GesA is the competence center for all questions on major public health topics. It determines the health needs of the population, ensures adequate provision and monitors the quality of services. Furthermore, together with its cantonal and federal partners, it actively participates in considerations regarding the development of the healthcare system in Switzerland.

Your tasks

In the Licensing, Supervision and Legal Affairs department, you will become part of a dynamic team and contribute to the processing and follow-up of the approval dossiers of health professionals and health facilities.

In this context, you will take on the following tasks in particular:

  • Instruct applications for professional licenses for health professionals, operating licenses for health facilities and authorizations to work at the expense of compulsory health insurance
  • Ensure the independent processing and follow-up of the dossiers in compliance with the applicable legal principles and procedures
  • Ensure that the various processing steps run smoothly and identify any problems or discrepancies
  • Carry out administrative tasks related to the activities of the department
  • Inform and advise citizens and health professionals in the field of public health

Desired profile

  • Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ) as a businessman or equivalent training
  • 5 years of professional experience in a comparable position, particularly in administrative processing and dossier management
  • Careful, structured and precise way of working when processing dossiers and data
  • Ability to reliably apply procedures and ensure high quality administrative processing
  • Ability to work independently and enjoy working in a team
  • Good knowledge of common IT applications as well as interest in new digital tools and processes
  • Excellent knowledge of French and good knowledge of German (level B1–B2)
