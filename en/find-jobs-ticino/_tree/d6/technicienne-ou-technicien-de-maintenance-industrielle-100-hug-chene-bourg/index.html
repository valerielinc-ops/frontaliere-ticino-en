Full description

As an industrial maintenance technician or technician, you independently perform all preventive and corrective maintenance tasks on production lines for washing, drying, folding and transporting hospital linens as well as installation work requested and organized by the supervisors. You monitor and monitor production machines by ensuring compliance with the applicable time limits, standards and guidelines, including hygiene and safety. You regularly report your work to your hierarchy and through the computer tools and databases available. You report any malfunctions, damage or technical anomalies to the appropriate hierarchy or persons or by means made available. You can control mechanical interventions, such as the replacement of mechanical transmission chains, bearings or conveyor conveyor belts. You have knowledge of pneumatics and hydraulics. Ideally holder of the ITTO authorization art13, you will be asked to intervene on electrotechnical equipment (frequency converter, electric control cabinets BT, detection cells of various technologies, etc.). You are comfortable with HMI-type computer interfaces for programming one-line processing equipment (folding robot, industrial dryers, etc.) and have notions in industrial automation and computer science.

  • You update the technical documentation and perform the maintenance and cleaning of the tools, premises and machines entrusted.
  • In addition, you are involved, as required, in stock management and the preparation of materiel requests.
  • You are interested in the evolution of techniques and pass on your knowledge and know-how.
