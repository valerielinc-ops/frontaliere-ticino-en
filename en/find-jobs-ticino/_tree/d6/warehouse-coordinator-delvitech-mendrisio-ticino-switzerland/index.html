Full description

Lead Delvitech's warehouse team: coordinate stock, inventory and material flows for the production of our high-precision inspection machines. Job Summary The Warehouse Coordinator will lead and organize the daily activities of the warehouse team supporting the production of Delvitech's AI-based optical inspection machines. The role ensures that goods receipt, storage, picking, packing and shipping are carried out efficiently and safely, and that high-value precision mechanical, optical and electronic components are handled, stored and traced with the utmost care. The Warehouse Coordinator is responsible for stock accuracy, inventory control and the correct registration of stock movements in the company ERP system, working closely with Production, Purchasing and Logistics to guarantee material availability. Key Responsibilities

  • Team coordination: plan, organize and supervise the daily work of the warehouse team.
  • Goods flow: oversee the receipt, storage, picking, packing and shipment of materials, components and finished machines.
  • Handling of precision components: ensure that precision mechanical, optical and electronic components are inspected at receipt, stored and handled according to the required standards (e.g. ESD protection, protective packaging, controlled storage).
  • Inventory control: organize regular stock counts and ensure inventory accuracy.
  • ERP management: ensure that goods receipts, shipments and stock movements are correctly registered in the ERP system.
  • Traceability: define and maintain storage locations, labeling and traceability of all materials, including serial numbers and component revisions.
  • Material availability: coordinate with Production and Purchasing to guarantee a smooth flow of materials and support the management of shortages.
  • KPIs and continuous improvement: monitor warehouse performance indicators and propose improvements to layout, processes and safety. Requirements
  • 3 to 5 years of experience in warehouse operations, including the coordination of a team.
  • Experience in a manufacturing environment for precision mechanics, machinery, automation, electronics or similar high-tech products is required.
  • Experience in handling high-value and sensitive components (precision mechanical, optical or electronic parts).
  • Good knowledge of ERP systems for stock management.
  • Solid understanding of inventory control, traceability and warehouse organization.
  • Leadership, organizational and communication skills.
  • Attention to detail and ability to manage priorities. Nice to Have
  • Knowledge of Progel.
  • Experience with warehouse KPIs and layout optimization.
  • Knowledge of ESD handling rules.
  • Knowledge of English. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
