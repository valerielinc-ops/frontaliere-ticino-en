Full description

Spitex Regio ZO is a modern and future-oriented non-profit organization. On behalf of the municipalities of Fehraltorf, Russikon and Weisslingen, we accompany people of all ages with professional care, care and advice. With expertise, empathy and a resource-oriented attitude, we promote the independence and quality of life of our clients and enable them to live independently in their familiar home.

We search immediately or by agreement

Dipl. Nurse HF Psychiatry 50%-60% Meaningful work, real encounters and the opportunity to make a difference: If you want to practice your profession with heart and expertise, you are exactly right with us. So you can bring your expertise and personality to us

  • You accompany and support our clients with psychiatric diseases and psychosocial stress in their familiar home environment
  • You ensure a needs-based, professionally sound care and care and actively contribute to a high quality of care
  • You support our clients in building and strengthening their resilience as well as in developing and maintaining individual coping strategies
  • You take responsibility for the entire care process, from needs assessment with inter-RAI CMH to planning and evaluation of care measures
  • You maintain a professional, comprehensible maintenance documentation and ensure correct electronic performance recording
  • You coordinate the cooperation with family doctors, relatives, hospitals and other participating specialists
  • You ensure a transparent and timely exchange of information within the team as well as with relevant internal and external bodies
  • You advise and support employees in the somatic field on psychiatric issues within the framework of interdisciplinary client support
  • You are engaged You in the further development of our quality standards as well as in measures for occupational safety and health protection
