Full description

Are you ready to be at the center of equity trading operations in Australia & New Zealand? Do you thrive working closely with the trading desk, taking ownership of risk outcomes, and improving how things are done? We’re looking for a proactive Middle Office professional seeking genuine front office exposure, ownership of key controls, and the opportunity to drive meaningful process improvement and automation—primarily for New Zealand, with additional responsibility supporting Australia. In this role, you’ll sit at the heart of the Australia & New Zealand Equities business, partnering closely with Trading, Business Management, clients, and global support teams. You’ll own critical front to back controls, ensure trade integrity, and play a key role in managing operational and risk outcomes on T0 and beyond. You’ll be trusted to act decisively, think critically, and continuously improve how we support the business.

  • Own and operate a suite of front to back controls, identifying and capturing risk events as part of the first line of defense
  • Ensure T0 trade data integrity, with a strong focus on completeness, accuracy, and timeliness
  • Produce and facilitate sign off for T0 risk reporting and T+1 reconciliations
  • Act as a key point of contact for Australia & New Zealand Equities Trading desks and Business Managers, providing time critical support
  • Liaise with clients and external brokers to resolve trade booking and economic discrepancies
  • Partner with the business and local support functions on new initiatives, change, and process improvement to enhance efficiency and control
  • Drive automation and continuous improvement, leveraging data and tooling to reduce risk and manual effort
