Full description

Start now. Design tomorrow. BFuture.


Colors are your thing and you want to see how technology and design come together? Then the apprenticeship as an industrial painter: in EFZ at Bühler is exactly the right thing for you!


Apply now with your complete application documents (motivation letter, CV, certificates) and start your future with us! Tasks: • You clean and cover components professionally for further processing • You base and paint surfaces precisely according to specifications • You perform powder coatings • You control and process surfaces and ensure impeccable quality • You mount control signs and provide materials for delivery Requirements • Awareness of Colors • Craftsmanship and practical understanding • Precise operation • Reliability and responsibility • Teamwork We offer: Attractive holiday entitlement Sufficient time for recreation and personal interests - we offer from 25 to 30 days of holidays per year depending on the year of life. Attractive mobility concept We offer an OSTWIND ZVV / Z-Pass + Half-Tax for 365 CHF + 400CHF public transport credit as well as an attractive first-class subscription or free parking. Staff restaurant VIVA We offer our employees a daily fitness menu from 9.00 CHF. Mobile subscriptions We offer special conditions for mobile subscriptions via Mobilezone at Sunrise and Swisscom. Health port We are committed to health prevention through laboratory analyses, massages, nutritional advice, physiotherapy, group courses and a company doctor. Sports activities We promote sport through self-organized circuit training and running groups as well as vouchers for update and active fitness. Two billion people eat food produced at Bühler plants every day. One billion people travel in vehicles whose parts were produced using Bühler technologies. With every decision, Bühler pursues the goal of reconciling the needs of the economy, people and nature. In this way, we develop solutions that open up sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed and mobility industries. We want to innovate for a better world, with a particular focus on healthy, safe and sustainable solutions. This is why we work with customers, start-ups, multinational corporations and scientists to achieve a faster impact together.
