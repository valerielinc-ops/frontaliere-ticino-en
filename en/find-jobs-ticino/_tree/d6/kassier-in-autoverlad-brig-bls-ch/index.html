Full description

Escape everyday life and immerse yourself in another world through the Simplon – thanks to you, the anticipation of our numerous customers begins when loading their vehicle in Brig, because you are the friendly face of car loading.

Here’s what’s waiting for you

  • Sell tickets to customers, advise and inform them
  • Collect missing customer data
  • Ensure proper handling of money and payment cards
  • Operate / monitor height limit and act correctly during alarm
  • Manage storage space in Brig

Requirements

  • Completed professional training
  • Experience in sales and customer service and a high level of customer orientation
  • Good PC user skills
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, good oral language skills in Italian, English and French are an advantage
  • Irregular working hours (evening, weekend)

Your benefits – you can look forward to them

  • We promote the most flexible possible planning of your tours and shifts
  • Get a GA 2. Class, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare pass.
  • You have free time when others are working. There are surcharges for work at night and on weekends.
  • It is possible to purchase an additional five to ten vacation days per year.
  • Financial support for external further education and an exciting internal range of learning and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

About the BLS

With almost 4,000 employees, BLS is the second largest railway company in Switzerland. Moreover, we are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? The pride of what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And lots of exciting jobs.

Requirements

  • Abgeschlossene Berufsausbildung

  • Erfahrung im Verkauf und in der Kundenbetreuung und eine hohe Kundenorientierung

  • Gute PC-Anwenderkenntnisse

  • Sehr gute Deutschkenntnisse in Word und Schrift, gute mündliche Sprachkenntnisse in Italienisch, Englisch sowie Französisch von Vorteil

  • Unregelmässige Arbeitszeiten (Abend, Wochenende)
