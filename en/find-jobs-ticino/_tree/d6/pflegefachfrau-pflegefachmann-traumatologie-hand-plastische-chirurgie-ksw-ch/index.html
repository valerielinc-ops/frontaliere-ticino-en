Full description

For the bed station at the clinic for traumatology and hand / plastic surgery with integrated day clinic we are looking for 1. February 2027 or by arrangement a motivated colleague to complement our team.

Tasks

  • Constructive cooperation in a stable, young and collegial team
  • Care and care of patients and their relatives in a holistic high quality
  • Responsibility for the implementation of professional care and care taking into account modern care and treatment concepts as well as care standards
  • Varied work content with inpatient and outpatient patients
  • Promotion and accompaniment of trainees as day-care providers
  • Creating an active and positive team and clinic culture

Profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH, preferably with experience in the field of surgery
  • Joy in the nursing profession, motivated, committed and resilient personality
  • High quality awareness and interest in professional and personal development
  • Joy and commitment in the field of care as well as in interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
