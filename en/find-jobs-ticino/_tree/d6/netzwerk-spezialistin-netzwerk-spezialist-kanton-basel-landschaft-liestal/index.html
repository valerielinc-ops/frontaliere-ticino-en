Full description

Focus on WLAN / NAC / Security Financial and Church Directorate Central computer science Liestal immediately or by agreement

Your responsibility: self-responsible, versatile and in partnership

  • Maintenance and expansion of the cantonal network infrastructure
  • Configuration and operation of all active network components such as switches, routers, firewalls, proxy servers, load balancers, WLAN and VPN devices
  • Monitoring of systems via network management systems in terms of function and performance
  • Operation and maintenance of DNS, DHCP, IP address management and radius server infrastructure
  • Second-level support in the area of Cantonal Network and Remote Access
  • Cooperation in projects as a specialist
  • Implementation of maintenance work at marginal times and cooperation in the piketting service

Your profile: experienced, analytical and communicative

  • You have completed a bachelor’s degree in computer science or a comparable education and have several years of experience in computer science and at least two years of experience as a network specialist
  • You have completed additional training in the field of Cisco networks (ideally with CCNA, CCNP or comparable certification)
  • Your very good analytical skills and sense of actionable and beneficial solutions will help you master business challenges.
  • You are an open, team-oriented, communicative and responsible personality with an exact and independent working method
