Full description

Bräcker AG, the world’s leading supplier of rings and travelers for ring spinning systems, is a subsidiary of the Rieter Group. Bräcker, based in Pfäffikon ZH (Switzerland), creates customer value through system expertise, innovative solutions, after-sales excellence, and global presence. The company manufactures its main products – rings and travelers for ring spinning machines – in Pfäffikon and Grignasco (Italy). In addition, Bräcker offers grinding machines used for maintenance of cots.

Committed and satisfied employees are of particular importance to our company's success. For this reason, we are looking for an active personality as

Are you a proactive, service-oriented personality with a passion to tackle your tasks? In this challenging and very diversified function, you take responsibility for Controlling and Administration and ensure the following activities.

Your Main Tasks and Responsibilities

  • Act as a trusted business partner to the Managing Director and leadership team by providing actionable financial insights, constructively challenging assumptions, and supporting strategic decision-making.
  • Lead and develop the Controlling and Administration functions with both functional and disciplinary responsibility.
  • Oversee the financial and administrative management of the two legal entities in Switzerland and Italy.
  • Ensure the effectiveness and continuous development of the Internal Control System (ICS).
  • Drive business performance reviews and profitability analyses, identifying opportunities to enhance profitability, productivity, and cash generation.
  • Monitor working capital, cash flow, and liquidity development, and recommend measures to improve financial performance.
  • Lead the budgeting, forecasting, and mid-term planning processes.
  • Support investment decisions through business case development, financial modelling, and post-investment reviews.
  • Manage and monitor budgets, investments, and cost-reduction initiatives.
  • Serve as the key interface between the business and the centralized finance organization.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement and digitalization of finance processes, systems, and reporting tools.

Your Profile

  • Degree in Finance, Controlling, Business Administration, or a related field (University of Applied Sciences or University degree).
  • 7-10 years of progressive experience in finance, controlling, or business partnering roles.
  • Several years of experience in a similar role within an industrial or manufacturing environment.
  • Proven leadership experience with the ability to develop and motivate teams.
  • Solid knowledge of SAP S/4HANA, BOFC, and related financial systems.
  • Good understanding of IFRS reporting requirements.
  • Strong analytical, business partnering, and stakeholder management skills.
  • Fluent in German and English
  • Italian is a strong advantage.
