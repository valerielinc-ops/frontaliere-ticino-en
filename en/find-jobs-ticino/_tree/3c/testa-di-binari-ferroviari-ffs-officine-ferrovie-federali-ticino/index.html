Head of team railway tracks Apply » Date: 5 Nov 2026

Place

Neuchâtel, CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. Join an open, committed and action-oriented team. You will have the opportunity to develop while contributing to the daily railway operation in the heart of the western region. Together, you will imagine and implement innovative solutions to meet the challenges of the network. In return, we offer a stimulating work environment and many attractive benefits. What you can move.

  • Support a team of about 25 people: support them in their professional and technical development and succession management of the SBB infrastructure.
  • Adopt motivating and professional leadership: you build confidence and foster collective performance.
  • Effectively pilot track maintenance and sub-projects in accordance with SBB safety, economic and quality requirements.
  • Ensure the availability of the human and material resources necessary for the construction, maintenance and resolution of incidents on our railway infrastructure.
  • Monitor and analyze financial and operational indicators and be responsible for your team's performance.
  • Detect and conduct optimizations: you propose improvements, implement them and mobilize your team around new ideas.

What you bring.

  • You are comfortable with people of diverse cultures and contribute to an inclusive environment.
  • You have a global vision of the company and strong relational and technical skills: you can inspire, coach and guide your team towards the goals set.
  • You promote transparency on a daily basis: you communicate with insurance and presence.
  • You have a CFC or proven experience in a railway technical field. Personnel management training (e.g., recognized leadership specialist at the federal, technical commercial, public transportation) is an asset — or you are prepared to do so within the next two years.
  • You have good computer skills (Office 365, SAP) and an indispensable category B driver's license.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
