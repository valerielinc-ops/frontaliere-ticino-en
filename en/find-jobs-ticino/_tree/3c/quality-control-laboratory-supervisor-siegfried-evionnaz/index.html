Full description

"Expect to grow", personally and professionally: At Siegfried, we offer exciting challenges and many opportunities to showcase your talents. As a trusted and respected partner in the pharmaceutical industry, we meet the highest standards of safety, quality and sustainability, while cultivating a culture that allows you to thrive. Join a growing global company where diverse perspectives and experiences come together in a collaborative environment. Here you will find a workplace that values collective success as much as your individual contributions—offering long-term opportunities to evolve and have an impact.

Your Role

As a laboratory, you are involved in the conduct of instrumental analyses (GC, HPLC, GC-HS). You control the analyses in Empower and track the results in LIMS. In addition to analytical management and document control, you participate in actions related to OSO, analytical deviations, recovery forms and CAPA. You perform off-stream analyses (analytical transfer, stability analyses, reference substances). You are actively involved in the management and maintenance of aircraft/columns and in activities related to MPGs.

Your Profile

  • You have a chemical laboratory (CFC) training after several years in chromatography
  • You master traditional computer tools such as Word, Excel, Labware and Outlook
  • You are able to provide rigorous and methodical work
  • You are comfortable working with colleagues on a team

  • Plan to purchase generous and customizable shares
  • Child allowance: Siegfried AG increases the cantonal legal allowance by 10% and pays in addition CHF 100 per month
  • Individual continuing training opportunities
  • Reka Cheques: Siegfried AG grants its employees a 20% discount
  • Attractive enterprise pension fund
  • On-site sports courses and numerous corporate events
  • Access to the Swibeco employee benefits platform
  • 5 weeks of vacation and possibility of redemption up to 2 weeks

Founded in 1873 in Zofingue, Switzerland, Siegfried has become a global network of 16 sites spread over three continents. With a team of more than 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to the industrial scale and manufacture safe medicines for patients around the world. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few organizations able today to ensure the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished galenic forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most reliable partner in the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operators manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
