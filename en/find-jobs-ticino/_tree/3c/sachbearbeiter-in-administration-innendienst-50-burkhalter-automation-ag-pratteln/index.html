Full description

With over 20 years of experience in building and industrial automation, we stand for quality, reliability and practical solutions. Our projects range from control and regulation to the optimization of heating, ventilation, air conditioning, industrial systems, lighting and shading. We work project-oriented, with short decision-making paths and in close contact with our customers. As part of the Burkhalter Group, we also benefit from the support and network of a strong Swiss group of companies.

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a reliable and versatile personality for in-house service.

Your tasks

  • General administration and telephone exchange
  • Processing of mail and post
  • Preparation of order confirmations and invoices
  • Management of debtors and creditors incl. Payment and dunning services
  • Participation in monthly and annual accounts
  • Support for material procurement and management
  • Maintenance of customer and personnel data in the system
  • Administrative support of the team in internal processes and organizational tasks

Your profile

  • Basic commercial training or equivalent qualification
  • Very good knowledge of German, French is advantageous
  • Good MS Office knowledge, experience with ERP systems is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Organizational talent, team spirit and pleasure in customer contact

Your advantages

  • An established and motivated team
  • a versatile and varied area of responsibility
  • Flexible working hours
  • 5+ weeks holidays
  • Opportunity for further training

Automation is teamwork. While our technicians are out, you hold the strings together in the office and make sure everything runs smoothly.

Sounds like you? Then we would like to meet you. Send us your application to: d.sig@buat.ch
