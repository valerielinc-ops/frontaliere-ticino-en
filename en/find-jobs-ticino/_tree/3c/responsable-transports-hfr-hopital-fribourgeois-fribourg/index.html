Full description

  • ensure the human, organisational and operational conduct of the service, ensuring the quality, safety and continuity of patient and freight transport
  • framework, federate and develop the team: define clear objectives, support employees, evaluate results and ensure compliance with the framework
  • changing practices and strengthening a culture of cooperation, accountability and patient guidance by valuing the skills and initiatives of employees
  • pilot transport and logistics projects, from needs analysis to implementation, monitoring time, resources and results
  • ensure the link between the different services of the hospital and the transport team
  • develop collaboration with healthcare, medical, technical and logistical services in order to improve coordination and fluidity of transport
  • analyse activity from relevant field observations and indicators, define improvement actions and measure their effects
  • control and monitor and maintain transport equipment
  • knowledge and enforcement of waste management, patient transport and safety rules
  • participate and stimulate interdisciplinary work accompanying cultural change in the team
  • collaborate in the development and management of the Procurement and Supply Department in accordance with the HFR Charter and Values
  • vocational training in the field of logistics, transport, health or in a relevant field supplemented by management training, or route deemed equivalent
  • certificate of transport of patients intramural or equivalent (possibility to acquire in-service)
  • successful experience of at least 5 years in supervising an operational team; a hospital experience is an asset
  • concrete experience in carrying out projects and implementing organizational changes, with demonstrable results
  • unifying leadership and ability to set a clear framework, make decisions and ensure its application with fairness and consistency
  • mastery of the French language with very good knowledge of the German language
  • confirmed skills in communication, negotiation and conflict management; experience of dialogue with staff representatives or social partners is an asset
  • ability to structure activities, manage priorities and use indicators to improve service quality and efficiency
