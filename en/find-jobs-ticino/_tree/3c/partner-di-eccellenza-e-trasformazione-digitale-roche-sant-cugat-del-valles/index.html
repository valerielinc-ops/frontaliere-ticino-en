The Position

As a Customer Excellence & Digital Transformation Partner, you will play a key role within our Supply Chain organization, combining operational Customer Service responsibilities with a strong focus on digital transformation and process optimization. This is a hybrid position designed for someone who enjoys both hands-on operational work and contributing to a more automated, efficient, and customer-centric future. You will manage daily Order-to-Cash activities while also helping identify opportunities to simplify processes, improve customer interactions, and support the transition from manual operations to digital-first solutions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage customer end to end order flow: order management, invoicing, pricing discrepancies, operational incidents and collecting activities within SAP and Order-to-Cash workflows
  • Provide customer support through phone and email while ensuring high-quality service and timely issue resolution
  • Coordinate with internal stakeholders across Supply Chain, Finance, Logistics, and Commercial teams to ensure smooth operations
  • Identify repetitive manual activities and contribute ideas to improve efficiency through automation, digital tools, and AI solutions
  • Support digital transformation initiatives by helping customers adopt online ordering, automated workflows, and more agile ways of working

Who You Are

  • Experience in Customer Service, Supply Chain, Billing, Order Management, or related operational environments
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills with a customer-oriented mindset
  • Analytical and proactive profile with interest in process improvement and operational excellence
  • Curious about digital transformation, automation, AI, and new technologies applied to business processes
  • Comfortable working in dynamic, fast-paced environments with an agile and collaborative mindset

Technical & Digital Skills

  • SAP or ERP systems experience is highly valued
  • Familiarity with CRM tools and Order-to-Cash processes
  • Advanced Excel and strong data accuracy mindset
  • Interest or experience in automation, digital workflows, or AI-driven solutions
  • Understanding of Agile methodologies and continuous improvement practices

Languages

  • Fluent Spanish
  • Professional English
  • Catalan is a plus
