Full description

Your role Attached to the Financial Directorate, you are the preferred contact point for trades and sectors on the follow-up perimeters that will be entrusted to you. You intervene both in the reporting of the Swiss entity and in the Group consolidated reporting. 1. Improved profitability of the Group and the Swiss entity You help provide a clear, reliable and relevant vision of financial performance:

  • To raise in the MIS the financial indicators necessary to produce reporting and to monitor the performance of the Private Bank Front Office,
  • Produce and analyse monthly and quarterly trade indicators for the Comex Group, the Group's Financial Directorate, as well as trades and sectors,
  • Participate in the monthly analysis of staff, personnel and line costs and contribute to cost optimization,
  • Participate in the preparation, consolidation and monitoring of the budget,
  • Work on preparing files for the Group's governance bodies (administrative board, audit committee). 2. Transverse Projects & Role Beyond the production and analysis of recurring reporting, you play an active role in the evolution of the Finance function:
  • Contribute to the development and improvement of reporting tools (MIS) and methodologies for monitoring the Group's activities,
  • Intervening in the management of finance projects (evolution of tools, process redesign, new indicators...). 3. Efficiency of the Finance sector You act as a business partner within the industry and with your interlocutors:
  • Participate in the facilitation of the network of management control correspondents within different geographies, promote the sharing of good practices and the harmonisation of methods,
  • Promote collaboration and increase exchanges in the Finance sector and with other sectors, including Information & Technology and Human Resources,
  • Identify ways to improve the quality and relevance of financial processes and reporting tools (MIS and Group reporting) Your profile
  • Higher education in finance at Master level or equivalent,
  • 2 to 4 years of experience in external audit or management control & MIS, ideally in the banking, asset management or financial services sectors.
  • Strong skills in banking accounting, financial analysis, modelling and interpretation of performance indicators,
  • Aisance with reporting and data management tools (Advanced Excel, BI restitution tools and SQL-type IT tools) and skills for improving information systems,
  • High interest in business issues related to reporting activity and trade performance,
  • Ability to interact with various stakeholders (financial management, trades, sectors, international geography) and simplify your analysis to make them accessible
  • Rigor, reliability, synthesis and timeliness,
  • Initiative spirit, taste for transversal projects and transformation,
  • Language level: French and professional English required
  • Any other language is a plus. Without news from us within a month, you may consider that your application has not been accepted.
