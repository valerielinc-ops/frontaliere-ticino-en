Full description

You combine sound IFRS expertise with regulatory reporting. At the BKW Group, you contribute to shaping Group financial statements and annual reports, developing solutions for complex accounting issues and advancing the EU taxonomy.

Your field of activity

  • You contribute centrally to the IFRS consolidated financial statements and the annual report of the BKW Group
  • You will analyze complex IFRS questions from the business areas and develop practical solutions
  • In the EU taxonomy you support the determination and reporting of the key figures as well as their further development
  • Acquisitions are accompanied by you from an IFRS perspective, especially with purchase price allocations according to IFRS 3
  • You assess new IFRS standards, analyse their impact and accompany the implementation in the Group
  • You advise finance departments and Group companies, work with external auditors and get involved in financial projects

You bring that with you

  • Studies in Accounting, Finance or Business Administration or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of in-depth IFRS experience in the corporate environment, auditing or accounting advisory
  • Very good knowledge of IFRS and experience with consolidated financial statements
  • Knowledge of EU taxonomy and interest in regulatory developments
  • Analytical and conceptual strength as well as an independent, structured working method
  • Enjoyment of cross-divisional cooperation and stylish German and English

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
