Mission

  • Operate safely and with competence to guide category B SUVA certified cranes.
  • Manage loading, unloading and handling of materials in building or industrial sites, complying with the safety standards in Switzerland.
  • Ensure regular maintenance and functional control of the crane to ensure optimum operation.
  • Collaboration with the construction team to ensure an effective and coordinated workflow.

Profile

  • License Gru SUVA Category B mandatory, according to the current Swiss legislation, indispensable for the management of the specified lifting systems.
  • Experience proven in safe and autonomous management of cranes in construction or industry contexts, preferably in the Swiss market.
  • In-depth knowledge of safety standards on construction sites and preventive maintenance of cranes.
  • Effective communication skills in the team
  • Italian knowledge at B2 level is considered a significant advantage for coordination and safety at the site.
  • Proactive attitude to work and rigour in compliance with operational and safety procedures.
