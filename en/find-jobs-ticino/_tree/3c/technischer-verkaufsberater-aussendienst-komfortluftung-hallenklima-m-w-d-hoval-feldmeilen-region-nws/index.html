Full description

To strengthen our sales team for the Northwest sales area, we are looking for YOU! As a leading technology company, Hoval AG has been inspiring for decades with groundbreaking system solutions for heating and air conditioning technology. The distinctive family culture inspires not only our own employees, but also our customers. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed and sales-strong personality as a technical sales consultant in the field service.

Do you enjoy direct customer contact and successful sales? As a tech-savvy and service-oriented personality, you will inspire our customers with innovative products and tailor-made solutions in the field of heating and air conditioning technology.

Your tasks

  • Active sales and competent advice of our products and services in the assigned sales area Northwest
  • Independent management of sales negotiations – from initial consultation to successful conclusion
  • Maintaining and expanding existing customer relationships as well as targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Periodic participation in reporting, training and product days
  • Responsibility for budget control and target achievement
  • Successful market launch of new products
  • Continuous market and competition monitoring in your region
  • Deputy sales colleagues in absence

We offer you

  • A responsible and versatile task in a sustainably growing company
  • Great room for manoeuvre and great personal responsibility
  • Collegial cooperation in a family environment
  • Attractive employment conditions in a defined benefit company

Your profile

  • Basic technical training in ventilation, building technology or HVAC
  • At least 3 years of experience in the technical field service as well as in-depth knowledge of the ventilation and air conditioning industry
  • Convincing, professional and safe appearance
  • Strong customer and service orientation
  • Resilience, initiative and pleasant manners
  • Enjoyment of travel and independent work

You are a passionate sales representative and appreciate independent work as well as personal customer contact? Then you are exactly right at Hoval AG.
