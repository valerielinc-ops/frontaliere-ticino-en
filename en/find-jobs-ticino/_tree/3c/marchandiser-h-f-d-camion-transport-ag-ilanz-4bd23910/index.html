CAMION TRANSPORT Value Added Services | Pensum/Assignments: approximately 2.5 hours per week, each on Friday ###

Start on October 1, 2026 or by appointment | Jumbo Ilanz

Buying decisions are often made at the point of sale. An optimal product presentation is the key to success. You work on site with our customers - from shelf management to conversions and new facilities.

Your tasks ##

You work independently with our customer and manage the assortments as specified. Here you fill the warehouses according to the agreed specifications with the delivered consumables.

  • Mobile with your own car
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Impeccable repute (criminal records are required for employment)
