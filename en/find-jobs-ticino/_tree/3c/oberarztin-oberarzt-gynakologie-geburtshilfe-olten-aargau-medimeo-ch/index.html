What if your next career step not only advances you professionally, but also leads you into a new healthcare system? This position combines the entry into Switzerland with a broad spectrum of gynecological and obstetric care and good opportunities to develop operationally and professionally. Our client is an established acute care and center hospital in German-speaking Switzerland with a wide-ranging women’s clinic. More than 2,000 births are cared for each year. The spectrum includes high-risk pregnancies, multiple and premature births and complex obstetric situations. Prematurely born from the 24. Pregnancy week together with neonatology. Gynecology also offers a wide range of conservative and operational procedures with minimally invasive procedures and specialized areas.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of gynecological and obstetric patients
  • Working in operational gynecology and targeted further development of your operational skills
  • Care of regular births, high-risk pregnancies and complex obstetric situations
  • Taking responsibility in clinical everyday life and in the further education of younger colleagues
  • Participation in the medical service

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Gynecology and Obstetrics
  • Experience in a broad gynecological-obstetric environment
  • Initial operational experience and interest in further expanding it
  • Safety in dealing with more challenging obstetric situations
  • In the case of a foreign specialist title: Requirements for recognition in Switzerland

Their advantages

  • Targeted professional development within a broad gynecological-obstetric spectrum
  • Operational development opportunities and perspectives in various gynecological focuses
  • Demanding obstetrics with more than 2,000 births per year and care for premature babies from the age of 24. SSW
  • Supported training and opportunities to take on additional responsibility
  • Comprehensive support when moving to Switzerland: We advise you on suitable career paths, different hospital sizes and professional orientations, the Swiss health system and the next professional development steps.
  • Assistance in the recognition process: Also for questions about the recognition of your foreign specialist title, we accompany you in the process.
  • Part-time opportunities as well as attractive employee benefits and family-friendly offers
