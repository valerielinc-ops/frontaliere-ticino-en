Leadership Support Head of Communication (m/f/d) 50-60% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Leadership Support Head of Communication (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, limited to 31.12.2027, 50-60 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

A lively and exciting working environment awaits you. As part of the communication team of SBB, you are on the pulse of events and get insight into many topics. You will work closely with the Head of Communication and the Communications Management Board and get to know strategic, operational and political topics of corporate communication up close. You take responsibility in a central hub function and contribute your ideas for modern digital working methods and AI-supported tools. You can move that.

  • In this important hub function, you comprehensively support the Head of Communication at SBB in day-to-day business, keep his agenda, edit the inbox and prepare all relevant documents for the future.
  • You prepare communication meetings of the management, keep minutes and always keep the overview of all committee meetings.
  • The preparation of management documents is just as much a part of your job as the preparation of reports and presentations.
  • Independently and professionally, you lead the secretariat, organize smaller events and workshops, coordinate appointments and support the team in all administrative matters.

You bring that with you.

  • A pronounced organizational talent as well as a structured, service-oriented and reliable way of working enable you to keep an overview even in hectic situations.
  • Addressable, clear and sovereign communication with different stakeholders is one of your strengths.
  • You actively take on change, you recognize the need for action at an early stage, work independently, think ahead and implement solutions independently and purposefully.
  • A basic commercial training is required, ideally supplemented by additional training in office management or higher education. Several years of experience in a comparable function make you the ideal occupation of the role.
  • You master German at least at level C1 and French at least at level B1.
  • Thanks to your IT affinity and your safe use of modern tools (M365), you are easy to get started and work efficiently. In addition, you are interested in new digital working methods and AI-supported tools and use them sensibly.

Your rewards and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and are actively committed to equal pay. Therefore, further and detailed information on the wage system is transparently visible at SBB. This position is in Requirement Level I, Regional Allowance Level 1.
