Full description

Go ahead courageously, make your own decisions and enjoy the trust of the employer. Surely that is possible! In the field of human resources, you contribute to the fact that this motto can be lived and experienced with us. Together with the managers, you create the framework conditions so that employees can fully develop their skills and successfully fulfill their tasks.

Tasks

  • Establishment, maintenance and further development of organisational structures
  • Mapping of organizational changes in HR systems
  • Preparation and maintenance of organizational charts
  • Support and implementation of reorganisations
  • Management of posts (installation, mutation and closure of positions)
  • Ensuring correct allocations (cost centers, functions, reporting lines)
  • Support for governance and job release processes
  • Ensuring high data quality and consistent organizational logics
  • Collaboration in the further development of HR processes and workflows
  • Coordination between HR, IT and Finance for organizational changes
  • Advising HR business partners and executives on organizational issues

Requirements

  • Commercial or business education, HR training beneficial
  • Several years of experience in organizational or post management (HR operations)
  • Good knowledge of HR systems (e.g. SuccessFactors, HR Access); Ingentis is beneficial
  • Strong understanding of organizational structures and governance processes
  • Experience in supporting reorganizations and transformation projects
  • High data and system affinity
  • Structured, precise and reliable working method
  • Analytical thinking and pronounced detail
  • Understanding the interfaces between HR, IT and finance
  • Independent, service-oriented personality with a high level of communication
  • Flowing knowledge of German, other languages beneficial
