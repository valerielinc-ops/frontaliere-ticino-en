Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for an agreement

Task area

  • Specialist care and treatment of outpatients and inpatients in general and visceral surgery
  • Independent implementation and assistance of elective and emergency interventions with focus on modern, gentle and minimally invasive procedures
  • Collaboration in our broad range of services, including colorectal, upper GI, liver & bile, hernia, endocrino, tumor and venous surgery as well as robotic surgery
  • Leading a surgical consultation as well as cooperation in visits, training and reports
  • Participation in the further education and guidance of assistant doctors and under-assistants
  • Participation in surgical emergency and service operations as well as in the further development of quality, processes and services

Profile

  • You have the specialist title FMH Surgery or are about to graduate. Alternatively, bring a foreign title recognised as equivalent
  • Broad clinical and operational experience in general and visceral surgery
  • In-depth knowledge in one area of our range of services is an advantage
  • Enjoyment of modern, minimally invasive and robotic surgery, interdisciplinary cooperation and medical training
  • Independent, careful and patient-oriented work as well as strong social skills and team spirit
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing
