Full description

Mission

As part of this function, attached to our Clothing Division, you will be in charge of carrying out the work of crimping baguette and invisible according to the Patek Philippe quality criteria.

Responsibilities

  • Perform mainly of the rod and "invisible" crimping of different types of stones and on all types of material (gold, platinum, steel).
  • Master the different techniques of crimping: traditional, snow, enclosed, mechanical.
  • Ensure repair work.
  • Guarantee the quality, precision and finish of the parts performed.

Profile sought

  • SWC or BMA of Jewellery orientation crimping or training deemed equivalent
  • 3 years d, minimum experience on traditional crimping
  • 1 year d, minimum experience on baguette and invisible crimping
  • Mastering of techniques of crimping baguette, invisible and snow
  • Knowledge of claws and down techniques represent a plus
  • Excellent visual acuity and very good manual skill
  • You work in a careful, precise, rigorous and autonomous way
  • You are patient, calm and you have excellent ability to concentrate
  • Strong quality orientation
  • Spirit
