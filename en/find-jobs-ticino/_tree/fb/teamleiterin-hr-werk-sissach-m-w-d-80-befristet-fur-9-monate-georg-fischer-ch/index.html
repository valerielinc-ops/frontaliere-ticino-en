Full description

As part of a maternity representation, you will take over the management of our HR team at the Sissach location for nine months. In this versatile management function, you and your team ensure a professional HR service for around 300 employees and advise the site management team and the managers on all personnel-relevant issues. She expects a varied role with high visibility, short decision-making paths and the opportunity to actively accompany and support the organization and managers.

Their tasks

  • Professional and personnel management of the local HR team
  • Advising and supporting the site management team and the managers in all personnel-relevant issues
  • Ensuring professional HR service throughout the Employee Lifecycle
  • Support and implementation of HR, organizational development and change initiatives
  • Support and coaching of leaders in topics such as leadership, talent development, performance management and employee retention
  • Ensuring compliance with labour law requirements and company-internal HR guidelines
  • Active participation in local and cross-location HR projects as well as the opportunity to contribute your own ideas and impulses
  • Trustworthy and solution-oriented cooperation with internal stakeholders

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience in HR, ideally as an HR Business Partner, HR-Lead or in a comparable role with management responsibility
  • Leadership experience or proven experience in the professional management of teams
  • Experience in organizational development, change management and the support of managers
  • Hands-on mentality and the ability to manage operational and strategic issues equally
  • Strong communication and advisory skills as well as high social skills
  • Good knowledge of Swiss labour law
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English

No applications from recruitment agencies shall be considered for this call.
