Full description

Bad Zurzach Rehabilitation Clinic offers rehabilitation in all facets at a high medical level. The rehabilitation offer ranges from neurology to musculoskeletal/rheumatology, cardiology to sleep medicine (Clinic for Sleep Medicine). The Pain Center Bad Zurzach offers pain treatments within special programs that include pain diagnosis, counseling, and therapy.
