Become part of our team!

We are looking for a committed and sales-oriented personality in our consulting team as a customer consultant (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • You support and advise our customers personally, competently and solution-oriented
  • You recognize customer potential through active and continuous support of the customer portfolio and develop it further
  • You advise our customers in the areas of investing, provisioning, figures & savings and communicate further topics to our specialists.
  • You will conduct comprehensive and individual consultations, supported by modern consulting tools
  • You actively participate in market development measures and contact campaigns and thus make an important contribution to sales success
  • You take on a responsible consulting function with a lot of customer contact, attractive development opportunities and a modern workplace in the heart of St.Gallen

What do you bring?

  • You have completed banking training and have already gained several years of professional experience in customer consulting
  • You distinguish yourself through a pronounced service and customer orientation and consistently focus on the needs of our customers
  • You enjoy the active development of customer relationships, approach people proactively, show initiative and feel comfortable in a dynamic environment
  • You convince with a professional and sovereign appearance
  • You enjoy working in a team, are resilient and keep track of changing tasks

Also desirable

  • You have knowledge of French or Italian or are willing to learn one of these languages and use it in customer contact
  • You have a SAQ certification or are ready to purchase it in a timely manner
