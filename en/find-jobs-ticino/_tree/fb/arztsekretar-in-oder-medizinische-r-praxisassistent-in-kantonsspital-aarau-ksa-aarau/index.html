Full description

Children's Hospital Aarau

Job: 1 January 2027, indefinitely

Tasks

  • Planning, organizing and coordinating complex office hours across multiple medical disciplines, including ensuring efficient and smooth operations.
  • Professional telephone communication with patients and their relatives, assignment offices, family doctors, external services and insurance companies.
  • Formatting and sending office hours reports, including careful review and administrative post-processing.
  • Care of patient registration and responsible back office activities that require a high level of service orientation, accuracy and organizational strength.
  • Support the medical team in a wide range of organizational and administrative tasks in order to ensure a smooth running of daily business.

Requirements

  • Completed training as medical secretary or medical assistant (MPA)
  • Sound knowledge of medical terminology as well as stylish German in word and writing
  • Good IT user knowledge and secure handling of common programs and clinic information systems
  • Self-employed, efficient and reliable working methods
  • Enjoying a varied job and working together in a team

Welcome to the Cantonal Hospital Aarau (KSA). Whether in the Cantonal Hospital Aarau, at the locations KSA Bahnhof Aarau, KSA Lenzburg or KSA Frick – we are there for you around the clock. With our wide range of services, from basic care to highly specialized medicine, we are competent and reliable at all locations.
