Full description

During your commercial training at the Canton of Solothurn, you will get an insight into the versatile working environments that the Canton of Solothurn has to offer. With us at the Solothurn location, you have the opportunity from 01.08.2027 to get involved in your varied training as a merchant EFZ.

Your education

  • During your training, you benefit from an attractive and varied rotation system.
  • You will find versatile and demanding tasks in different working environments such as Finance & Tax, IT & Digital Business.
  • During your apprenticeship, you can count on competent learning support from specialists from our working environments.
  • You have the opportunity to design your own individual portfolio, which is designed for your interests and strengths.
  • We participate in language stays and support measures that enable you to successfully teach.

Your profile

  • As a motivated, reliable and flexible person, it gives you pleasure to help people who approach you with a cause.
  • You like to go to school and convince by very good performance in the sec E or P or a comparable degree.
  • Concentration in challenging tasks, participation in projects but also independent, dutiful work, you bring under a balance.
  • You can be trusted. You work on business tasks in the service of society, in teams and working groups.
  • You have good analysis and reflection skills and great interest in digital working methods and IT applications.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity for mobile work
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
