Since 1833, drive by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressed its own inventive spirit through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication.

YOUR MISSION

You ensure the delivery of the tools on time, with the quality expected and with respect to costs. You optimize processes, resources and load/capacity management while maintaining sufficient agility to respond to production emergencies. You report live to the Manager of Methods & Industrialization.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

Your role will be structured around the following missions

Team management

> Encourage, motivate and mentor a team of about 20 employees including 3 leaders.

> Promote skills development and versatility.

> Create a positive working environment based on communication and teamwork.

> Define individual and collective goals and develop team skills.

> Manage relationship with external suppliers and manage subcontracted tool orders.

Production management

> Ensure the delivery of tools on time and according to quality standards.

> Plan and optimize service load/capacity matching.

> Communicate proactively on risks, drifts and action plans.

Strategy and development

> To be the driving force for the improvement of the department's tool design, manufacturing processes, equipment and organization.

> Study and define the integration of new production equipment and technologies by ensuring their relevance and cost-effectiveness.

> Ensure technological monitoring to anticipate developments in the sector.

> Promote a culture of performance, accountability and continuous improvement.

Performance indicators

> Monitoring the service rate.

> Load/capacity monitoring.

> Follow-up of return rate and reasons.

> Monitoring of costs (subcontracting, consumables and tooling costs).

Technical expertise

Knowledge in automatic and progressive stamping would be a valued asset.

Computer / Languages

Knowledge

You are recognized for your leadership and good communication. Agile, entrepreneurial, methodical and rigorous, you are oriented quality and customer, with an excellent relational sense. You know how to manage priorities and emergencies and are focused on results.
