Full description

Portrait In the agglomeration community of Münchenbuchsee with just over 10,000 inhabitants - 10 km from Bern - around 1,150 children and adolescents are taught in 11 kindergarten, 33 primary school, 14 upper school classes, two classes with special support (KbF) and a 6-class comprehensive school (Diemerswil) at six different locations. In the atmospheric and listed school house Paul Klee, 8 classes of the 1st-4th class are taught in double rows. The educational offer of the school is complemented by a growing day school. Münchenbuchsee is an important residential and commercial location with very good transport connections, various well-known educational institutions (Pedagogical Center for Listening and Language Munichbuchsee (HSM), special school home Mätteli, Gymnasium Hofwil), diverse shopping opportunities and cultural offers (Restaurant Bären with 80 concerts per year).

Tasks

  • They teach 60% to 80% (17.88 to 22.44 lessons). The definitive amount is defined at the interview.
  • They teach on three or four days (Mo/Di morning and afternoon, Mi and ev. Do).
  • The job partner teaches the gymnastics on Friday (and possibly Thursday).
  • It is a temporary employment (15.02.-04.07.2027).
  • The location discussions are conducted until the sports holidays.

Requirements

  • The interplay of teaching and learning between teacher and student is a matter of course for you.
  • Your pleasure in your profession is reflected in your committed, empathetic and creative way of working.
  • A cooperative and professional cooperation between you, the teammates, the students and parents is a central concern for you.
  • They bring a pinch of humor and a positive attitude to life into the schoolroom.
  • You have a diploma as a teacher, are in training and/or bring several years of professional experience into your working environment.

We offer

  • Temporary employment according to cantonal guidelines
  • a beautiful, spacious, bright kindergarten with contemporary infrastructure
  • Small but fine college
  • excellent public transport connection to Bern and Biel (7 min). Walking distance from/to the railway station
