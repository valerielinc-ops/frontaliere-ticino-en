Full description

The Security and Justice Department of the Canton of St.Gallen is committed to the security of the population, a functioning rule of law and social coexistence. The Office for Prisoners is part of the Department of Security and Justice and employs around 260 people in various professional fields. Together, we open up new perspectives for people to live independently outside of crime and thus make an important contribution to a safe society. The Platanenhof Youth Home is anchored in the Office of Prison Office as the main department. With around 85 employees, it supports young people and young adults admitted to civil and criminal proceedings in accordance with socio-pedagogical principles and concepts. In a closed and open framework, new social, educational and professional development perspectives are developed with the young people.

What to expect

  • They bear overall responsibility for the personnel, organizational, professional and economic management of the Platanenhof
  • You lead and develop your employees and promote professional, appreciative and binding cooperation
  • They shape the strategic and operational development of the Platanenhof and ensure contemporary structures, processes and work concepts
  • They ensure a high quality of the offers and services and develop them further, taking into account new technical knowledge and the needs of the instructing authorities
  • You are responsible for budget planning and compliance and ensure an economical and sustainable operation
  • They represent the Platanenhof vis-à-vis authorities, specialist bodies, partner institutions and other stakeholders and maintain a sustainable network.
  • They work closely with the office management, participate in relevant specialist committees and represent the Platanenhof also to the public and media.

What sets it apart

  • You have a master’s degree or comparable higher education, preferably in social education, social work, psychology, pedagogy or a related field
  • They bring extensive, in-depth professional experience as well as several years of leadership experience, ideally in a complex institutional environment in the social, judicial, educational or health sectors.
  • They have proven leadership skills and experience in the leadership and development of employees and managers; a corresponding leadership formation is advantageous
  • You think strategically and conceptually, recognize higher-level relationships and develop organization, offers and structures forward-looking
  • You are used to assessing complex and interdisciplinary issues, developing viable solutions and using your scope for action responsibly
  • They combine a high professional quality with organizational and economic thinking and make clear and comprehensible decisions even when dealing with challenging issues.
  • They convince as an integrity, resilient and communication-strong leader and act safely and bindingly towards employees, authorities, specialist bodies and other stakeholders.

What we offer

  • A versatile and responsible leadership function with high social relevance
  • The opportunity to actively shape the future of the Platanenhof and its professional and organizational development
  • A demanding and varied field of activity with high personal responsibility and short decision-making paths
  • Close cooperation with the administration and a broad network of authorities and specialist departments as well as insight into the versatility of the Department of Security and Justice
  • Attractive employment conditions and contemporary framework conditions of the Canton of St.Gallen
