The Position Reporting to the Access & Corporate Affairs Head, the Policy Shaping & Public Affairs Value Partner is responsible for developing and implementing external affairs and communication strategies that support medium- to long-term policy shaping.

This role contributes to public policy efforts with government institutions, professional societies, and internal stakeholders, while strengthening Roche Romania’s visibility and reputation as a responsible leader in healthcare. The position also oversees media relations and collaboration with external agencies, including responsibility for execution and budget management.

Key Responsibilities & Accountabilities

Public Affairs

  • Expand the company’s involvement in public policy development and ensure readiness for future healthcare needs
  • Develop and implement external affairs strategies and strategic communication plans to support policy shaping, including value argumentation and health economic analyses
  • Strengthen Roche’s visibility and reputation among key stakeholders (government, industry associations, civil society)
  • Represent the company in industry associations and meetings with local and international authorities
  • Provide guidance and leadership on public policy shaping initiatives and best practices
  • Collaborate with professionals and policymakers to develop industry positions
  • Monitor healthcare reform trends and communicate insights internally
  • Build and maintain relationships with key external stakeholders and professional bodies

Communications

  • Lead strategic media activities (press releases, briefings, interviews, events) and prepare media statements
  • Develop clear, engaging materials translating complex data into accessible content
  • Collaborate with regional and global teams to implement corporate communications
  • Support market-shaping initiatives and product communications in collaboration with cross-functional teams

Who You Are

  • University degree in Medicine, Pharmacy, Life Sciences, Communications, Journalism, Public Relations, or related field (Master’s degree is a plus)
  • Proven experience in public affairs and communications (5+ years), ideally in pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or related industries
  • Strong knowledge of the Romanian healthcare system and global industry landscape
  • Experience in healthcare policy is highly preferred
  • Proven track record in policy-shaping strategies and multi-stakeholder communication
  • Solid understanding of pharmaceutical legal and regulatory frameworks
  • Local and English language proficiency (written and spoken)

Competencies and Approach we look for

  • Proactive, reliable, and results-oriented mindset
  • Strong relationship-building skills with internal and external stakeholders
  • Ability to translate complex information into clear, compelling communication
  • Trusted advisor mindset with senior leadership exposure
  • Excellent organizational and time management skills
  • Strong written, verbal, and presentation abilities
  • Strategic thinking with a focus on innovation and continuous improvement
