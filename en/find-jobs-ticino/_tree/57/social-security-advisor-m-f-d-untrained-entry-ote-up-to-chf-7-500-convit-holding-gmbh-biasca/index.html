(m/f/d) – experienceless entry, hybrid with Homeoffice

Would you like to guide your professional path towards social security? In Convit you find a solid context to learn the profession step by step.

Main information Employer: Convit International GmbH Zone: Porza and surroundings · hybrid work, Homeoffice possible Training and team days: Via al Mulino 22a, 6814 Cadempino, Switzerland Contract: Indefinite recruitment; degree of employment according to the approved position Retribution (OTE): CHF 1'500–7'500 per month (fix + variable part, depending on the degree of employment) Language: Italian B2 or higher

Activities

  • To deepen the basic notions and prepare for consulting
  • Support the organization and documentation of social security practices
  • Conduct advice independently after qualification and internal qualification

Your profile

Pre-existing VBV qualification is not required to start the route. The availability of training and the commitment to acquire the necessary qualifications are essential. Counselling and intermediation activities begin only after fulfilling the applicable requirements and internal authorization.

  • Spiccata propensity to listen and good communication skills
  • Accurate, reliable and targeted working method
  • Motivation to acquire new skills in the social security sector

What we offer

  • Structured insertion and comparison with experienced colleagues
  • Digital tools and clear procedures for preparation and documentation
  • Clear Growth Path: junior → senior team leader
  • Hybrid working model: Homeoffice possible, appointments with customers in your area, training and team days in our Cadempino headquarters.

Training and qualification

The VBV admission exam preparation course is free for you: Convit supports course costs. The qualification requires overcoming the relevant examination and compliance with the applicable requirements.

Right to work

Applications of persons resident in Switzerland are welcome. For this campaign, those who reside in Italy must already have a valid G permit. The individual situation is verified before hiring.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status.
