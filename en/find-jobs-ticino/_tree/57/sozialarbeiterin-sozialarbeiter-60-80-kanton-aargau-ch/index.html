Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Advice and support for men detained in the prison within the framework of the risk-oriented penal system and taking into account the operational and legal framework (case management)
  • Assumption of client-related tasks: initial interviews, preparation of implementation planning and review of the achievement of goals, clarification of costs, conduct of fact preparation interviews, organisation of holidays, support in administrative matters, exit planning
  • Interface with internal and external bodies
  • Documentation of case management and diverse reporting

What you bring

  • University of applied sciences degree in social work and several years of experience in social work, preferably with clients in a forced context
  • High resilience, teamwork and goal-oriented working methods
  • Conscious approach to closeness and distance
  • Stylish written and oral expression skills in German, good knowledge in at least one other language and good IT application skills (Microsoft Office)
  • Good repute (excerpts from the penal and enforcement register without entries) and minimum age 30 years

Your working environment

The Lenzburg Prison provides care for persons who have been sentenced to imprisonment or to a custodial measure. The offer includes 380 prison places for closed prison, pre-trial detention and short sentences.

There are 199 detention centres for men serving longer sentences, detained early or staying in the SITRAK I (High Security) special prison department.

Security, peace and order are provided by the guards in the care and security service and specific specialist services, including the prison social service.
