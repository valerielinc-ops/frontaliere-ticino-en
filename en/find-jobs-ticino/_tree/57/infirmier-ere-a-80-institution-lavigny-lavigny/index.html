Full description

Nurse at 80%, 80% Integrated into the heart of a large recognized institution, the Hospital of the Institution of Lavigny is one of the reference Romanesque centers for patients with brain damage requiring neurological rehabilitation, or with difficult-to-treat epilepsy. Open since 1 July 2024, the University Neurorehabilitation Service (SUN) is an innovative structure linking two institutions, CHUV and Hôpital de Lavigny. With 75 beds and nearly 200 collaborators, the SUN provides highly specialized and interdisciplinary care in academic neurorehabilitation, for more than 500 in-patients and nearly 2000 outpatients per year. It is in this context that the hospital department develops, while keeping alive the values dear to it: Benevolence, fairness, respect and responsibility in a collaborative managerial approach We offer you the opportunity to discover an exciting work in neuro-reeducation, at the heart of interdisciplinarity, in a partnership with patients and their loved ones. Within the care team of the Hospitaller Department of Lavigny, we are looking for a:

Nurse at 80%

Your missions

  • Organize and deliver nursing in accordance with the patient's clinical route in neurological rehabilitation, in collaboration with the entire interdisciplinary team, and in partnership with the hospitalized patient and his or her relatives
  • Integrate patient rehabilitation objectives into daily care
  • Perform medical procedures, usually delegated to medically prescribed staff
  • Inform, guide, inform the patient and his surroundings
  • Initiate and participate in health education and promotion in collaboration with the interdisciplinary team.
  • Participate in the training and development of colleagues, nursing students, trainees, apprentices and auxiliary staff.

Your profile

  • Training in nursing, general care or equivalent
  • Skills to work in interdisciplinarity
  • An experience in neurology and rehabilitation or an interest in training in this field
  • The training of trainers and trainers in enterprises is an asset.
  • Good adaptability and responsibility.

We offer

  • Schedules in 12h
  • A stimulating and exciting work within an institution belonging to the Vaudian neuro-rehabilitation sector, recognised as regards the quality and diversity of its services, which despite its large-scale mission remains human-sized
  • The possibility of being able to highlight its professional skills in an interdisciplinary way where each competence is valued and taken into account
  • Membership in a committed and motivated care team and being able to contribute to projects to improve the quality of care in an environment or human being still has its place
  • The opportunity to meet many exciting challenges in a context of expansion of the structure, in close collaboration with an involved, committed and caring coaching team
  • A magnificent working environment in new premises where you can take care of your hip therapy, on a climbing wall or in a modern therapeutic pool, all with an implemented robotic park
  • Working conditions according to CTC standards in the public health sector in Vaud
  • Social benefits of a large institution, cafeteria with preferential prices, free parking, insured public transport (internal shuttle and MBC bus) from Morges station.
