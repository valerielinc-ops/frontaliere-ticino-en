Full description

Be the voice of Delvitech's customers in quality: analyse field issues, improve escalation processes and drive lasting fixes. Job Summary We are looking for a Customer / Field Quality Engineer to create a structured connection between issues observed at customer sites and the continuous improvement of our products, processes and Quality system. The role works closely with Application Engineering, HPT, R&D, Production and Quality, analyzing escalations, failures and complaints to identify trends and systemic causes. It does not replace operational support: it ensures that field information becomes evidence, corrective actions, product improvements, requirements, tests and reusable knowledge, and it develops the end-to-end customer issue and escalation process. Key Responsibilities

  • Analyze customer complaints, escalations, field failures and recurring issues.
  • Design and continuously improve an end-to-end ticketing and escalation system, from the customer through support levels L1, L2 and L3 to Quality and engineering.
  • Define the information, evidence, ownership and traceability required at each stage, linking issues to serial number, configuration, software release and components.
  • Identify trends and systemic issues and distinguish isolated events from issues requiring structural action.
  • Coordinate Root Cause Analysis and Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA), verifying their effectiveness.
  • Define and monitor Field Quality KPIs and contribute to Quality reporting for management.
  • Ensure significant field issues are translated into requirements, test cases or regression tests together with R&D and validation teams.
  • Turn validated solutions into knowledge articles and troubleshooting guides; use AI and data tools to classify tickets and identify patterns. Requirements
  • Experience in Customer Quality, Field Quality, Application Engineering, Technical Support, Field Service or equivalent roles.
  • Experience with Root Cause Analysis, corrective actions and structured problem solving.
  • Strong analytical skills and ability to work with data, tickets and failure histories from multiple sources.
  • Ability to design and improve ticketing, escalation and issue-management processes.
  • Strong communication skills with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Familiarity with ticketing systems and traceability tools.
  • Professional working proficiency in English. Nice to Have
  • Experience with AOI, machine vision, inspection systems or complex industrial equipment.
  • Familiarity with 8D, CAPA, FMEA or Six Sigma.
  • Experience with Jira, CRM or service management platforms.
  • Experience analyzing field reliability, complaints or warranty data. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
