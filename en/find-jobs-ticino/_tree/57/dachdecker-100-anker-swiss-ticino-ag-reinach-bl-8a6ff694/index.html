Full description

Roofing 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people – now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by agreement we are looking for a temporary or permanent employment

Your challenge

  • Technical administration for our complete offering in the areas of flat and steep roof, incl. Spraying work
  • Calculation of conversions and new buildings in building and civil engineering, elaboration of documents of submissions (technical reports, etc.) Participating in the acquisition and support of AVOR
  • Negotiating with clients
  • Construction site designs
  • Recording of dimensions / calculations according to plan documents
  • Capture addenda / additional work of construction sites
  • Development of economically and technically meaningful solutions
  • Support and exchange of experience in implementation
  • Advising clients / architects
  • Customer care

This is what you bring with you

  • Training as a dipl. Spenglers, flat or steep roof builders
  • Calculation experience in the fields of flat and steep roof construction as well as sparging work
  • Experience in handling and using EDP (Sorba) is an advantage
  • Cost-based thinking in personnel and material use
  • Compliance with work processes on construction sites according to SIA and SUVA regulations
  • VKF diploma for executing lightning protection systems
  • Category B driving licence

What we offer you

  • A dynamic working environment in which you can fully develop and there is room for your own ideas
  • With us you will find lean structures, short decision paths, contemporary work equipment and a modern workshop
