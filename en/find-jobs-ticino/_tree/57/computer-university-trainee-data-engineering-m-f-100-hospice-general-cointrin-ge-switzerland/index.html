Full description

Your Mission

The objective of the course is to contribute to the construction and industrialization of the General Hospital Data Platform, a fully open source Lakehouse infrastructure deployed on Kubernetes, ensuring that each data product brings concrete added value to our beneficiaries and our collaborators. Your mission will be to design and build end-to-end data pipelines, challenge the platform's technological choices and test them on specific and measurable business cases. Within the Information Systems and Digital Branch, you will work with the Product Data Intelligence team to design, build and build these data products in their social context. Your main activities

  • You will actively participate in the construction of the platform and the delivery of first data products with high added value.
  • You will contribute to the implementation and evolution of the components of the Data Platform (ingestion with Apache NiFi and RedPanda, Apache Iceberg storage on S3, Nessie / Polaris catalog, Trino request, ...).
  • You will design and test end-to-end data pipelines, from Bronze and Silver layers to the Gold layer, on specific business cases.
  • You will develop dbt transformations and associated quality tests (fresher, data contracts compliance, reference integrity, business rules, non-regression).
  • You will write and evolve Data Contracts in connection with data producers and consumers.
  • You will contribute to the compliance of the platform (prometheus metric, Loki logs, Grafana dashboards and alerts) and to the monitoring of pipeline ALSs.
  • You will compare and challenge available technological solutions and make reasoned recommendations.
  • You will be able to propose to make data a lever of value for our beneficiaries and collaborators, and to promote its adoption (board tables, documentation and user support).

Methods used

  • Use of agentic AI (development agents, tool orchestration, MCP protocol, ...) to speed up and reliability data work: dbt / SQL code generation and review, writing of Data Contracts and tests, documentation, incident analysis, respecting data confidentiality.
  • Three-layer Lakehouse architecture (Bronze, Silver, Gold), based exclusively on open source technologies.
  • Ingestion of batch data and streaming with Apache NiFi and RedPanda from the original source systems (ERP, SI loom, files).
  • S3-compatible object storage and open table format Apache Iceberg, Nessie / Polaris catalog allowing Git-like connection of data.
  • Transformation with dbt and SQL, Data Contracts (YAML) approach and 5-level quality testing pyramid.
  • Request and restitution with Trino and Metabase.
  • Development with Python and SQL for integration, automation and testing.
  • Deployment on Kubernetes via Helm charts and GitOps (CD Flux), CI/CD with Azure DevOps and JFrog Artifactory, isolated DEV, QUAL and PROD environments.
  • Observability from end to end with Prometheus, Loki and Grafana.
  • Security from design: authentication, RBAC, encryption, secret management and integration with SIEM/SOC.
  • Governance and protection of personal data in a context of social action.

Your profile During Master level training in a computer, data engineering, data science or related field (HES, university or EPFL)

Skills sought

  • Strong knowledge of SQL, data modeling and Phyton programming.
  • Interest in modern data architectures: Lakehouse, open table formats (Iceberg, Delta Lake), transformation tools (dbt).
  • Ingestion and processing of batch and streaming data (ETL / ELT, Kafka, NiFi, ...).
  • Git practice and sensitivity to IC/CD and DevOps / GitOps approaches.
  • Containerization (Docker).
  • Sensitivity to data quality (tests, contracts, documentation).
  • Understanding data security and confidentiality issues.
  • Excellent ability to collaborate and communicate.
  • Proposition strength and search for innovative solutions.
  • Ability to popularize complex technical concepts.
