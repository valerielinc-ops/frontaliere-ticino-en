E2E Test Engineer

ABOUT DELVITECH

Delvitech stands at the forefront of technological advancement, having pioneered innovative Automatic Optical Inspection (AOI) technology for assembled printed circuit boards (PCB) utilizing artificial intelligence. Our accomplished team is driven by a commitment to exceed technological boundaries and consistently deliver exceptional results. We are actively seeking individuals who are eager to embrace challenges, contribute proactively to achieving our set objectives, and play a pivotal role in propelling the company’s growth. Presently, an opportunity exists for a proficient and motivated E2E Test Engineer

JOB DESCRIPTION

We are looking for a highly motivated E2E Test Engineer to design, implement, and maintain automated test cases for our industrial inspection software. Working closely with the development and DevOps teams, you’ll ensure the reliability and performance of our microservices-based platform through robust and scalable end-to-end testing, primarily using Playwright. This role is based at our headquarters in Mendrisio. The ideal candidate will bring a patient, organized, and problem-solving approach to their work and must have prior experience in a similar field.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Design and implement end-to-end automated test cases using Playwright (TypeScript or Python).
  • Maintain and extend the current testing framework to support new features and system changes.
  • Collaborate with developers and product owners to understand features and define test coverage and acceptance criteria.
  • Execute automated and manual tests as needed, analyze results, and report bugs with clear reproduction steps.
  • Ensure the reliability of test pipelines and integrate E2E tests into the CI/CD process.
  • Contribute to the development of test strategies and best practices to ensure software quality and maintainability.
  • Participate in code reviews related to test scripts and provide feedback for improvement.
  • Maintain test documentation and collaborate with QA, DevOps, and R&D teams for continuous improvements.

REQUIREMENTS

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • Hands-on experience with Playwright (preferably in Python or TypeScript).
  • Strong understanding of test automation principles, especially for end-to-end scenarios.
  • Experience with CI/CD tools (e.g., TeamCity).
  • Familiarity with microservices architecture and RESTful APIs.
  • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills.
  • Good communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively in a cross-functional team.
  • Knowledge of Docker / Docker Compose.
  • Experience testing industrial software, hardware-integrated systems, or machine vision solutions.
  • Familiarity with test case management tools and bug tracking systems.

If this position matches your skills and aspirations, please send your complete application, including CV and references, to career@delvi.tech with your name and “E2E Test Engineer” in the subject line. Only short list candidates will be contacted.
