Become part of our team in BRIG. We lead, develop, design and plan infrastructure projects of all kinds. We accompany them from planning to implementation.

Take on responsibility with us as

Project Engineer Road and Railway Construction 80-100% - Brig

YOUR TASKS

  • Management of complex construction and rehabilitation projects in the field of road and railway construction: from project planning to commissioning
  • Project management in compliance with cost, quality and deadline specifications
  • Technical leadership and technical support of the project teams

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Exciting regional and national projects for renowned clients
  • Collaboration in interdisciplinary and multilingual teams and projects
  • Broad training opportunities at AFRY
  • Flexible working time model with the possibility of working from home

YOUR PROFILE

  • Degree as a civil engineer (ETH/EPFL or FH)
  • Several years of professional experience and in-depth specialist knowledge in road or railway construction, ideally also in industrial plants
  • Experience in project management and employee management
  • Experience in construction management is an advantage
  • Communicative, independent, cost-conscious and flexible personality
  • Good knowledge of German and French, both written and spoken (both at least level B2) and willingness to deepen it further
