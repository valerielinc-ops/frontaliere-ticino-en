Spenglermeister/in / Spenglerpolier/in 80 until 100% Do you know us? We are a traditional Swiss company with competences in the areas of flat roof, steep roof, lightning protection and spenglerei. With our location in Ebikon and 24 employees, we are active throughout the Canton of Lucerne and in adjacent areas. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the leading provider of cross-trade building technology in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. Their tasks

  • Management and organisation of spengler work on construction sites and in the workshop (no office work)
  • Planning, production and assembly of sheet and roof structures
  • Quality control and compliance with safety and safety requirements
  • Customer advice and technical implementation of construction projects

Your profile

  • Completed basic training as a spengler and further training as a spengler polisher or master spengler (or in training)
  • Vertigo-free and safe working on steep and flat roofs
  • Independent working methods & enjoyment of working in a team
  • Driving licence Cat B, trailer test
  • Motivation to learn new things and take responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Our offer

  • Varied and regional activity in a well-established team
  • Collegial and appreciative work environment
  • Contemporary employment conditions (40h/week)
  • Motivated, experienced and committed team
  • Individual training opportunities
  • Very modern machine park with digital connection
  • Personal company vehicle also for the commute
