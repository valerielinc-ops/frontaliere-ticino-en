Clinic for Aging and Neuropsychiatry

At the Clinic for Aging and Neuropsychiatry of the University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ), you will accompany people with cognitive impairments in challenging life situations. At our Crisis Intervention Station, you will support our patients in overcoming crises, regaining stability and improving their quality of life. Together with a committed interprofessional team, you will design a person-centered care and actively develop our station concept.

Your tasks

  • You accompany and support our patients as a caregiver and maintain contact with their relatives
  • You design the care process from entry to exit independently and orient yourself to the individual needs of the patients
  • You take on tasks in the day-to-day business of our crisis intervention station and bring your expertise and professional experience to bear.
  • You actively implement our station concept, develop it together with the team and contribute to living common attitudes in the station everyday life
  • You guarantee comprehensive, person-centered care and ensure compliance with our high quality standards
  • You work closely with the interprofessional treatment team to sustainably promote the stabilization of our patients and their quality of life

Your profile

  • You have a degree as a qualified nurse HF or a qualified nurse HF, an equivalent degree or a foreign diploma recognised in Switzerland
  • You have at least two years of professional experience, ideally accompanying people with cognitive impairments
  • You are a flexible, motivated and committed personality who likes to take on challenges and actively shape change
  • You enjoy working with people in acute crisis situations and meet them with empathy and an appreciative attitude
  • You enjoy working in the interprofessional team, take on responsibility and actively participate in the further development of the care and the station concept

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
