The Position

Internship in Project Portfolio Management with focus on IT Architecture & AI Applications (m/w/d)

Would you like to help shape the future of project portfolio management and develop innovative AI solutions? Then you are exactly right for us!

Who we are

The second largest production site within Roche Global Operations is the company's second largest production site, and raw material supplier for the entire Division of the Company. As a leading manufacturer of biological and chemical agents, DOZ is an important hub in the global Roche network.

We are looking for a dedicated and motivated intern who strengthens our project portfolio management (PPM) team. In this exciting role you work at the interface between IT architecture and the implementation of artificial intelligence to optimize and make our project management processes sustainable. If you are enthusiastic about project management and managing a large project portfolio, then we should get to know each other!

Your tasks

  • Support in the development of a future-oriented IT architecture for project portfolio management: They help us design a scalable and efficient architecture that meets the requirements of our growing project landscape. - Identification and evaluation of potential for the use of AI applications in the PPM: They research and evaluate innovative AI solutions that can be used to improve forecasting, resource planning, risk management and decision-making in the project portfolio.
  • Work with the design and implementation of PLAN-of-concepts (PoCs) for selected AI applications: They are actively involved in the implementation of first prototypes to demonstrate the feasibility and added value of AI-based solutions. - Analysis of project management data for derivation of optimization potential: They support us in data analysis in order to identify bottlenecks and improvements in our existing PPM processes.
  • Documentation of processes, architectures and best-practices: They contribute to the creation and maintenance of relevant documentation to ensure knowledge transfer and sustainability. - Support of the team in the daily business of Project Portfolio Management: They gain insights into the daily processes and support in administrative and organizational tasks.

Your profile

  • Student enrolled in (economic) computer science, engineering, business administration or a comparable degree program.
  • High motivation, willingness to learn and passion for process optimization.
  • Enthusiasm for project management or first touch points with project or portfolio management.
  • Interest in IT architecture and fascination for AI as well as willingness to actively accept new technologies
  • Analytic, problem solution-oriented thinking and the desire to face challenges.
  • Self-employed working methods, good communication and team spirit.
  • Flowing English and German skills.
