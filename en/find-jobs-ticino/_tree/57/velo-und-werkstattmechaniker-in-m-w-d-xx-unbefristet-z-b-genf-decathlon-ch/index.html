Mission Bicycle & Workshop Technician (m/f/d) - 80%

Location: Zürich City Center

Your future playing field

You will become part of our store in the heart of Zurich, which opened in 2019. Your area is at the center of our local strategy: In the middle of the city, we ensure that our customers get around quickly, competently and safely. In our 6-person workshop team, we work together, keep the pace high and value a dynamic, sporty environment.

Become part of an adventure that continues to grow

Decathlon in Switzerland is a success story that began in 2017 and continues to grow. We offer you an environment with flat hierarchies, a great deal of trust and genuine development opportunities.

What you can expect from us

Are you looking for a job where you do not just stand silently behind the workbench, but actively think things through, make decisions and engage directly with athletes?

  • Responsibility from day 1: You not only carry out repairs, but also keep an eye on utilization, spare-parts inventory and your key figures.
  • Interface between technology & people: Your technical expertise is important – at least equally important is your ability to advise customers competently, honestly and in a solution-oriented manner.
  • Trust & freedom to develop: If you have ideas for making the workshop more efficient, try them out. Initiative is encouraged and rewarded here.
  • Good conditions: A motivated, young and sporty team, fair compensation, varied tasks and plenty of room for your personal development.

What you bring

  • Experience or a quick grasp: You already have solid workshop experience or learn extremely quickly and have a strong technical understanding.
  • Resilience & pace: Even during extremely hectic periods and with a heavy workload, you keep an overview and work quickly and precisely.
  • Customer focus: You enjoy interacting with people and provide advice confidently, authentically and competently.
  • Passion for sport: You are enthusiastic about cycling and actively participate in at least one other sport.

This job is NOT for you if...

  • You prefer to avoid stressful situations or a high work pace.
  • You are interested exclusively in tools and do not want contact with customers.
  • You need strict instructions ("Top-down") and do not like making decisions independently.
  • You are not willing to work on Saturdays.

Your tasks

  1. Diagnosis & customer consultation
  • Welcoming customers, precise needs analysis and preparation of accurate diagnoses.
  • Transparent preparation of cost estimates and reliability with appointments.
  • Expert advice on repair, maintenance and circular-economy services (e.g. Second Life).
  • Handing over materials including useful care and usage tips.
  1. Technical & operational services
  • Professional execution of service and repair work with a focus on bicycles , complemented by ski service, racket stringing and scooter service .
  • Processing of product personalizations (textile printing, engravings etc.).
  • Managing the spare-parts inventory and reordering required components.
  • Compliance with all safety and quality standards and ensuring a clean, well-organized workshop.
