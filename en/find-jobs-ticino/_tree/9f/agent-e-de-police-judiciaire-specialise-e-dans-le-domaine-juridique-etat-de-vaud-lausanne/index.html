Full description

Following a political and strategic will to strengthen the fight against economic and organised crime, the Financial Brigade of the Vaudian Security Police, in collaboration with the Public Prosecutor's Office, set up a criminal seizure group. The task of this group will be to intensify the confiscation of assets acquired fraudulently by conducting its own investigations and supporting the other brigades of the Vaudian Security Police and the Public Prosecutors' Office (MP). As a specialized criminal law/procedure judicial police officer (APJS), you will participate in investigations and operations to combat economic crime, prepare reports and participate in hearings. You will legally support investigators and magistrates, propose and implement solutions for seizure and confiscation of criminal assets. You will participate in the training of investigators and magistrates in your field of competence. By joining the CPV, you contribute to a public service committed to serving its population, its security and the maintenance of social cohesion.

  • Analyze and return investigative information in a relevant form for judicial purposes.
  • Administratively and legally support investigators and magistrates in the fight against economic crime and the seizure of criminal assets.
  • In his capacity as delegated PJSA, prepare and sign reports attached to the judicial file, conduct hearings and searches, participate in police operations and letters rogatory.
  • Identify, develop and propose concepts for the development of new ways of investigating economic crime and seizure of criminal assets.
  • Legally support police officers and work closely with the MP in complex cases.
  • Contribute to close relations and permanent liaison with the School of Criminal Sciences and the UNIL Faculty of Law, EPFL, HEIG-VD and/or other reference schools/institutions in the legal and economic field.
  • Contribute to the legal watch on finance law, banking law, tax law, economic criminal law and economic analysis of law.
  • Participate, in collaboration with the financial brigade and MP management, in the establishment of a group specializing in the seizure of criminal assets and actively work on it once established.
  • Provide training related to the specialisation of the criminal chain.
  • Provide permanency services during the week, weekends and holidays.
  • Is equipped with personal safety equipment, except firearms. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact BIERI, head of the financial brigade at 021 338 04 15. Further information on the activities of the Cantonal Police can also be found at www.police.ch
  • Master level training in criminal law and/or criminal procedure
  • CAS, DAS, MAS useful for the function (criminal prosecution, judicial administration) desired.
  • Diploma in economic crime or title deemed equivalent desired.
  • Experience in legal and/or criminal matters.
  • Excellent knowledge of criminal procedure.
  • Good knowledge of economic, financial and commercial matters.
  • Excellent writing convenience and good knowledge of computer programs.
  • Public service responsibilities and ethics, adaptability and flexibility.
  • A spirit of mutual help and collaboration.
  • Capacity for analysis and synthesis.
  • Stress resistance and discretion.
