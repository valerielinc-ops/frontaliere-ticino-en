Full description

What awaits you

You are at work with heart and appreciate the open exchange within the team? You enjoy personal customer contact and do not lose humor even in hectic situations?

Become part of a motivated team, which is working daily for its customers and insured persons and does not let them down in the event of damage. You take responsibility and contribute to a strong trust in Mobiliar as the most personal insurance company in Switzerland.

This is what they do with us

The focus of your job is the uncomplicated support of our customers in the event of an accident or illness – from registration to completion.

You bring your expertise and commitment to personal injury. You provide our customers with security and reliability during a damage event.

You are in close contact with your team as well as with all parties involved in the event of damage.

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic commercial training
  • Experience in damage processing (personal injury)
  • Joy of contact and accompaniment of customers: inside in case of damage
  • Independent work, high motivation in a varied field of activity to learn new things
  • Responsible, empathetic and open personality
  • Fluent knowledge of German in words and writing, knowledge of other languages is a plus

Working with us

At the Generalagentur Basel you will be welcomed by a motivated and dynamic team. Excellent working conditions, great training opportunities and a modern working environment awaits you.

The Insurance Supervision Act requires for the execution of this function the conclusion of insurance intermediary /-in VBV, profile non-life. If this is not available, we are happy to support with tailor-made internal training.

Job entry takes place immediately or by agreement.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Basel?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
