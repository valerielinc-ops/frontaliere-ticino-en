The Position Job Summary The Production Technology Engineer is a key position responsible for the development, implementation, and continuous optimization of production equipment and processes. This role drives significant impact by optimizing time to market, enhancing the reliability and quality of products, and improving overall cost efficiency and operational standards. The scope of impact is broad, extending across the organization including internal manufacturing and involving critical external stakeholders, including tier 2 suppliers. The engineer also provides technical support to the GEM organization. The position balances strategic analysis with operational execution, involving technical risk management, while simultaneously identifying, evaluating, and implementing cutting-edge production technologies and driving innovation. Planning is typically multi-year to ensure long-term process capability and quality.
