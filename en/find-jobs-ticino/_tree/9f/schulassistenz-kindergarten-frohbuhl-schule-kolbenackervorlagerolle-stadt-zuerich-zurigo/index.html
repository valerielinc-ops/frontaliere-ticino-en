Full description

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a school assistant in class at the school Kolbenacker, in the kindergarten Frohbühl 2.

  • Accompaniment and simpler guidance of classes and/or individual students in everyday school life
  • Co-creation of positive social and learning-enhancing relationships with and between students
  • Simple administrative and organizational work at class level (e.g. absence lists)
  • Maintenance, cleaning up and provision of materials (e.g. teaching materials, etc.)
  • They pay the amount of 43% on 5 days per week as follows: Monday - Friday, 08:00 - 12:00. No assignments are planned during school holidays
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least level C1) and basic PC user knowledge (e.g. Word, Outlook)
  • Experience in dealing with children and young people, patience and resilience
  • Good communication skills, openness to new and surprising situations, safe appearance
  • Good social skills, teamwork and flexibility
  • Confidentiality and discretion towards the outside as well as willingness to further develop in pedagogical topics
  • A varied, versatile and independent activity
  • A multicultural, exciting environment
  • Competent support from school management
  • Progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich

We are a large school community in Zurich-Nord with 18 schools. We derive our pedagogical attitude from the concept of New Authority and thus meet the daily challenges in everyday school life together and effectively.

In the school Kolbenacker we have 28 classes, of which 8 kindergartens.
