Full description

80% - 100% | IT / Business Engineering | Zurich | Experienced professionals We are modernizing. Is Client Engineering and Microsoft Windows your passion and do you want to help shape the transformation? For our team Winfox, we are looking for a committed, energetic and visionary personality who, as a solution architect, will rethink the basis of the digital workplace. With us, you will work in a wide range of tasks with flat organizational structures, attractive development opportunities and great freedom of design. In an agile team, you design the digital workplace for our employees and ensure that communication and security work together optimally. You combine architectural and technical know-how with a strong understanding of service and contribute to the fact that we are reliable for our customers every day as a nearby bank. Your tasks

  • You will continue to develop the existing hardware and software solutions and consolidate them.
  • You develop and implement new, sustainable solutions and concepts in the areas of client engineering, including hardware, operating system, software distribution and packaging, virtual clients and security solutions.
  • You examine relevant architectural concepts with regard to integration capability and consistency.
  • You define and develop high safety and operational requirements in the workplace area.
  • You plan and implement challenging enterprise projects in close cooperation with System Architects, Engineers and IT Security.
  • You design the journey to the cloud, in particular the connection of our clients to various cloud services, such as zero-trust clients, client monitoring and device control.
  • You identify and design automation potentials along the client and workplace processes.

Your profile

  • You have completed university studies (FH or university) in computer science or an equivalent education.
  • You bring several years of experience as a solution architect in the workplace or client environment.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of classic, on-premises client management, such as SCCM, group policies, scripting/PowerShell and remote access solutions.
  • You have practical experience with modern, cloud-based client management solutions in the Microsoft 365 environment, such as Intune / Endpoint Manager, Azure AD and Windows Defender.
  • You convince with your team-oriented, communicative and learning-ready way and are open to new things.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very safely in German, good knowledge of English is an advantage.

Our offer

  • Mutual support and team spirit are very important to us.
  • You can expect an exciting and diverse range of tasks with many network possibilities.
  • You benefit from great freedom for autonomous decisions and participation.
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support and accompany you on your way.
  • You work in a central and modern workplace.
  • Flexible working hours, mobile working and the compatibility of work and private life are a matter of course for us.
  • You can expect a comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and a variable remuneration offers many benefits and perks – from A such as retirement savings to Z such as ZVV bonus pass
