Full description

Are you passionate about cyber security and complex IT infrastructures? Do you want to use your know-how to help protect systems and digital services sustainably? In this role, you take responsibility for modern security solutions in hybrid infrastructure and cloud environments and work closely with various IT departments. With you, we strengthen the security of our systems and further develop our cyber security landscape.

You can do that.

  • You develop, operate and optimize security solutions for on-premises infrastructure as well as for AWS and Azure cloud environments.
  • In this function, you ensure smooth processes in various areas in close cooperation with operational IT security and IT specialists.
  • You define, maintain and automate interfaces using your networking and scripting expertise.
  • Advising and supporting application managers and project managers on firewall and security issues are part of your area of responsibility.
  • You handle 2nd- and 3rd-level support cases in firewall operation and take care of pikett services.
  • In firewall operation, you quickly detect, analyze and correct anomalies and regularly prepare statistics and evaluations.
  • Configure, test and operate physical hardware for existing and new requirements.

You bring that with you

  • You have an IT-related engineering degree or a completed computer science education with experience in the field of IT security.
  • Several years of experience in complex network and firewall technologies, especially with Check Point and Tufin, set you apart.
  • You work safely with Linux, scripting languages and automations and use your know-how successfully in practice.
  • You think future-oriented, like to learn new things and bring the necessary assertiveness to successfully complete special orders and sub-projects.
  • In addition, you stand out with a high level of quality and safety awareness, and work as carefully on logical configurations as on physical hardware, bringing your strengths to the full.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English, which rounds off your profile.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
