Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Obwalden stands for customer advice at a high level. Do you inspire entrepreneurs with competent advice, identify opportunities and develop sustainable financing solutions? With your experience in the corporate banking business, your network and your strength, you are actively shaping the success of our customers and our bank.

What do you expect?

  • Comprehensive needs- and solution-oriented advice for corporate and real estate customers
  • Takeover and targeted expansion of an existing customer portfolio
  • Development of solution-oriented financing concepts for corporate and real estate customers
  • Active mediation of business opportunities in other areas of our bank
  • Performing representative tasks and active network maintenance
  • Personal responsibility in a committed and collegial team

What do you bring?

  • Customer-oriented personality with a secure, winning appearance and pleasure in proactive customer contact
  • Completed banking education with further training in banking or finance (business administration, corporate finance, trust, etc.)
  • In-depth expertise in financing, both in operational financing and real estate financing (returnables and commercial properties)
  • Experience in the support and acquisition of corporate clients
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, negotiation skills and final strength
  • Commitment, initiative and team spirit
  • Network and anchoring in the canton of Obwalden are beneficial
