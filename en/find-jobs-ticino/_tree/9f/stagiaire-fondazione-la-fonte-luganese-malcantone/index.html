Full description

You can perform an internship at a housing facility (Source 3 in Neggio or Fonte 8 in Lugano) or at a protected laboratory (La Fattoria in Vaglio, Il Fornaio in Lugano, Lo Spazio Officina in Agno).

Conditions

  • 100% working percentage (42 hours per week)

  • pay of CHF 800.- monthly net (according to CCL Social Institutions)

Interested persons are required to submit their application by indicating the field of interest, the possible start and duration of the internship, together with a presentation letter, a curriculum vitae and the copy of the certificates of study by email to [recruiting@lafonte.ch](mailto:recruiting@lafonte.ch)
