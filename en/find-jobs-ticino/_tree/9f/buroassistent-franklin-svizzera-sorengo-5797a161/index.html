Executive Assistant, President's Office (signed commission)

Mansions

  • management of agendas, meetings and appointments
  • coordinate national and international travel, including itineraries, accommodation, visas where required, refunds and related logistics
  • support special projects and carry out other joint tasks with the responsibilities of the President's Office and with the mission of the institution
  • draft, review, format and distribute correspondence, reports, presentations and other executive documents in English and Italian
  • support the Presidency and Advancement events by coordinating invitations, recordings, venues, catering, travel, audiovisual needs and event materials

  • collaborate in the collection and organization of institutional data from academic and administrative units
  • welcome visitors, respond and direct telephone calls from the President's Office
  • monitor projects, requests, deadlines and actions to be carried out on behalf of the President's Office, ensuring timely follow-up with the offices concerned

Requirements

  • person with solid administrative skills, good judgment, discretion and a service-oriented approach
  • Trade apprenticeship (AFC/EFZ), higher vocational training, degree or vocational training equivalent
  • at least three years of administrative experience or executive support with progressively increasing responsibility, preferably in support of senior leadership
  • excellent organizational skills, planning and coordination of projects and processes, with proven ability to manage multiple projects, deadlines and interlocutors in a dynamic environment
  • excellent written and oral communication skills both in English and in Italian
  • ability to exercise good judgment, maintain maximum confidentiality and manage sensitive information with discretion
  • solid interpersonal skills and professional and service-oriented approach in relationships with colleagues, trustees, visitors and external interlocutors
  • excellent knowledge of Microsoft 365 and common office technologies
  • ability to work independently, take initiative, solve problems and adapt to changing priorities

Preferential experiences

  • experience in university, nonprofit or international organizations
  • experience supporting executive leadership and/or governance bodies
  • experience in coordinating institutional events or donors
  • familiarity with CRM, survey systems or institutional information systems (e.g. Slate, Qualtrics or similar platforms), along with a good command of Excel and other reporting tools
  • experience supporting accreditation initiatives, assessment or institutional effectiveness
