Full description

Your tasks

  • You manage and coordinate construction sites in high-voltage systems of the BKW network as well as for third-party customers and ensure a smooth project flow
  • Compliance with occupational safety requirements on construction sites is your responsibility and contributes to a safe working environment
  • You commission primary and secondary plants in substations and power plants and ensure their reliable function
  • Maintenance and service work on protective systems as well as on the secondary technology you carry out independently and with great care
  • Together with internal specialist departments and external stakeholders, you coordinate projects and contribute your expertise to construction and coordination meetings
  • Testing and commissioning documentation create, maintain and check carefully

You bring that with you

  • You have an education in electrical engineering (HF/FH or comparable)
  • Sound knowledge and practical experience in secondary technology in the high-voltage environment or in the commissioning of automation/industrial plants
  • You work independently, structured and with high quality standards

Willingness to travel within Switzerland as well as the driving licence category B are a matter of course for you

  • Very good knowledge of German round off your profile
  • French is a plus

We look forward to your online application.

About us

BKW offers its customers internationally complete solutions in the fields of energy, buildings and infrastructure. We employ around 11,500 people. Together we build networks, produce energy, plan and realise energy-efficient buildings and design sustainable living spaces of tomorrow. For this we need you: people with ideas and commitment. Make a valuable contribution to society and our environment with us!
