Full description

Integrated directly with the Movements and Squelettage team and attached to the Chef d'Atelier, your main mission will be to achieve Anglage hands on our skeleton movements.

In this context, your main responsibilities will be

  • Be comfortable lifting and finishing corners
  • Perform different surface finishes on our high added value parts
  • Organize your production also according to the quality, quantity and time limits previously defined
  • You justify at least one year's experience in a similar function
  • Set and open, you have a good team spirit
  • You are autonomous, rigorous and passionate about your work
  • You are recognized for your excellence and your concern for quality

What we offer

  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual holiday weeks and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The ability to work remotely depending on function
  • A business restaurant
  • An advantageous PBL
