fenaco is the largest agricultural cooperative in Switzerland. With more than 80 subsidiaries, we operate in Switzerland and Europe. Together, we ensure that the valuable Swiss food from the earth reaches the table – de la terre à la table. Halag Chemie AG, UFAG Laboratorien AG, ufamed AG and SwissVets AG together form the Strategic Business Unit (SBU) Food Safety within the fenaco Group and make an important contribution to food safety in Switzerland – from hygiene to analytics to animal health.
