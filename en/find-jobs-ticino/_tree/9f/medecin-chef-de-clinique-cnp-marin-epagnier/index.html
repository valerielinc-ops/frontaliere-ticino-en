Full description

The NPC is looking for a Medical Officer of Clinic for its outpatient service. A rewarding career opportunity within a mental health institution.

Tasks

  • Develop and implement multidisciplinary clinical guidelines.
  • Provide expertise in front-line medicine and home care.
  • Participate in ongoing training and development of treatment methods.

Competences

  • Recognized diploma in psychiatry, experience of at least 2 years in hospital.
  • Leadership and Planning.
  • Ability to work as an interdisciplinary team.

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implements public policies to promote and prevent mental health

  • It acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research.

The Adult Department is seeking, for its outpatient service, within the day hospital of the CPCM (La Chaux de Fond) and the consultation of the CPCL (Prefargier), a

Clinical head doctor 50 to 100 %

You have a Swiss diploma or are recognised by MEBEKO. Have you obtained the FMH title in psychiatry-psychotherapy (or at least the first part of the title being obtained), and would like to join a recognized institution specialized in mental health? We welcome your application and treat it confidentially.

As part of your function you will be required to

o You develop and implement the guidelines and programs necessary for the objectives of the department, and ensure the smooth functioning of clinical activities in a spirit of multidisciplinary collaboration. o You have to collaborate in a multidisciplinary approach and in a mobile team. o You provide expertise and assistance to front-line medicine, home care teams, and in collaboration with somatic care. o You are able to provide specialized care and adequate support outside the psychiatric hospital setting. o You participate in the continuous training of teams to ensure a high level of skills, and contribute to the development and improvement of diagnostic methods of processing.

The competencies required for this position are

o have a minimum of 2 years of hospital and/or outpatient psychiatry activity o have knowledge of the Neuchâtelois or Switzerland health network o have a spirit of interdisciplinary teamwork and networking o demonstrate leadership and planning o be able to make decisions Why join the CNP? o Clinical activity specialized in complex and varied situations o Participation in research and development projects in psychiatry o An environment conducive to continuing training and supervision o A responsible position to contribute actively to the development of the unit An exceptional environment: Neuchâtel offers a unique quality of life, between lake and mountains, ideal for families and nature lovers. o LWOP provides financial and logistical support in the event of relocation to the Township (subject to conditions).
