What tasks await you?

  • Assembly and commissioning: You assemble complex mechatronic systems for laboratory automation from mechanical, electrical and electronic assemblies and commission them professionally.
  • Programming and control technology: You will learn how to install system software as well as the programming and configuration of mechatronic control systems (e.g. PLC controls).
  • Troubleshooting and maintenance: You're looking for clues! You use measuring and testing devices to analyze faults in our diagnostic systems and replace defective components.
  • Working with technical documentation: You will learn to read, understand and create technical drawings, circuit diagrams and construction documents confidently.
  • Quality assurance: Since our products can save lives in the medical sector, you strictly test the finished components and systems for function, safety and compliance with our high quality standards.
