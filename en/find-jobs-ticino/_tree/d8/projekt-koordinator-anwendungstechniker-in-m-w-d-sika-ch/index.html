Full description

About the role We are looking for reinforcement for our team in Widen on 1 February 2027 or by agreement a project coordinator / application technician full-time. The following is an extract from the activities and responsibilities:

  • Coordination of customer orders & material tests in the field of facades, windows & insulating glass (order preparation, processing and reporting)
  • Performance of application-related adhesive tests according to Sika standards or according to specific specifications
  • Logging and analysis of test results including reporting and maintenance of report filing
  • Effective operation and maintenance of laboratory equipment of the test laboratory
  • Assistance and support in the preparation and implementation of training courses, meetings or workshops
  • Responsible for the smooth running of customer orders

Your skills and experience

  • Practical and technical training or further education, for example as a laboratory assistant
  • Experience in relevant areas and in an application laboratory
  • Structured and quality-oriented working method with excellent organizational talent
  • Good communication skills
  • fluent knowledge of German
  • English skills are mandatory at level B
  • Excellent computer skills and security in dealing with the common programs

Why you should come to us A 40-hour week, a staff restaurant, 5 weeks holidays, good social benefits and training opportunities. Sika is a great employer

  • Despite our size, we have managed to maintain a family feeling in our culture – something we call Sika Spirit. This results from our commitment to open cooperation, flat hierarchies and positive cooperation.

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
