Full description

More than 1,000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: the CSD Group is a dynamic and growing company. Thanks to the ingenuity, skill and multidisciplinarity that characterize our teams, CSD offers innovative and sustainable solutions for our customers with a "plus" for quality of life and the environment. We are looking for a Senior Energy Project Manager in Lausanne (f/h/d) 80-100% for our Environnement Vaud Branch.

  • Your missions
  • Lead / Coordinate complex energy projects, including:
  • remote heating
  • industrial thermal
  • renewable heating (including heat pumps)
  • storage of thermal/electric energy (incl. network coupling)
  • production of renewable gases (H2/biogas, etc.)
  • Participate in the acquisition of new projects and clients
  • Write offers and reports
  • Support and coordination of other project managers and collaborators
  • To direct the works and follow the works, a minimum, to have a good knowledge of the problem of an installation site
  • Your profile
  • EPF/HES energy or thermal engineer or equivalent training
  • At least 15 years of experience
  • Knowledge of Swiss legal bases and standards, SIA
  • Perfect mastery of French, both oral and written. Mastering German is an asset
  • Autonomous profile, driver's licence holder
  • versatility, synthesis and innovation
