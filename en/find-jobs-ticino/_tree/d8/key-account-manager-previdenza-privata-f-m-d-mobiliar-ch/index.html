What awaits you: In this function, you will be responsible for supporting general social security agencies. In particular, for general agencies in the region of Ticino and, ideally, also for some in the region of Argovia and Lucerne with a travel activity of 40%. Your contribution: The focus of this position is to strengthen and support our general agencies in social security insurance for individuals and SMEs. You will be responsible for training new insurance consultants with centralized courses and will be responsible for continuing training of general agencies. With your experience and specialist knowledge, you will support social security specialists and pension planners of general agencies in fulfilling their tasks and will provide them with experience exchange and continuous development platforms. In complex social security cases you will also participate in customer talks. Its skills:

  • Federal diploma insurance specialist as a preferred qualification or financial advisor or equivalent training
  • Professional experience of a few years in the social security and sales sector
  • Experience in adult education
  • Experience in technical sales management, with particular attention to coaching, motivation and sustainable performance development, as well as the accompaniment and training of business partners
  • Prior experience in consulting or managing an SME is an advantage
  • Independent and objective-oriented working method and strong ambition
