Full description

As a hands-on tech lead, you not only want to develop good software, but also shape the technical direction of a central system? Then you are exactly right with us. Together with our agile team, you will develop a solution that securely archives 50 million documents every year. At the same time, you are shaping their future: from the possible replacement of today’s system to the expansion towards Enterprise Content Management (ECM). Ruth, Silvan, Aleksandar, Fabian, Luca, Björn and Saskia are looking forward to taking the DMS with you to the next level. You can expect a lot of creative leeway, a supportive team, innovation days as well as flexible working hours and home office.

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: You are responsible for the architecture and technical implementation of the CSS archive solutions – from conception to development to operation in the DevOps environment. This includes our own core system and the SaaS archive for non-customer documents.
  • In this context, you make architecture decisions, represent them and coordinate solutions with the System Architect and the tech leads of other teams.
  • You shape the future: Together with the product owner, you develop the technical basis for the further development or replacement of the archive solution. In addition, you will contribute your expertise in the expansion towards ECM including information architecture.
  • As a sparring partner and coach, you give the team orientation in technical questions, pick up the quiet voices and promote self-organization and continuous improvement.
  • You build it, you run it: You actively develop new features, automated tests and code reviews.
  • On the pulse of time: You use Clean Code, TDD, DDD and AI-supported development.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You have at least five years of experience in software development with Java as well as initial experience in technical guidance or software architecture.
  • You are familiar with several of these technologies: Java/Jakarta EE, event- and workflow-driven architectures, Docker, Kubernetes and REST interfaces.
  • You think in terms of information, not just code: metadata, classification and the life cycle of documents are familiar to you or you acquire this knowledge quickly. Experience with archive, DMS or ECM solutions is a plus.
  • You go ahead and listen: You are happy to take on technical responsibility, make decisions in the product in consultation with your team and stakeholders.
  • High software quality is important to you: You know design patterns and best practices and keep your knowledge up-to-date.
  • A computer science education or a study in the field of software development - whether classic or detours, we are open for your individual career path.
  • Work flexibly: Your team often works in the home office or on the road. Thanks to laptop with integrated SIM card and other technical possibilities no problem.
  • Stay up to date thanks to our Software Engineering Academy, the Innovation Days and the visit of external conferences.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Choose your holiday model: 5 or 6 weeks holiday? Your decision. Accordingly, you work 41 or 42 hours a week.
  • Take a break: house renovation, world travel or learning languages - what is your dream? Celebrate your 10th anniversary with a two-month sabbatical.
  • Enjoy your family life: Make time for your offspring: 3 weeks for fathers, for mothers up to 20 weeks. There are also generous child and education allowances.
