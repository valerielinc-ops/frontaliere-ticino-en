Full description

Do you want to actively shape the technical availability of modern production facilities? At Delica, you play a key role in maintenance, analyzing disruptions, optimizing maintenance strategies and working with internal and external partners. Become part of one of the best employers in Switzerland and design intelligent solutions for Swiss quality products.

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG Birsfelden, Hafenstrasse 120, 4127 Birsfelden

Mansioni

  • You plan, coordinate and monitor preventive and condition-oriented maintenance measures for our production and infrastructure facilities.
  • You analyze disruptions, failures and maintenance indicators and derive sustainable improvement measures from them.
  • You create, optimize and maintain maintenance plans, technical master data and maintenance orders in SAP PM.
  • You coordinate internal departments, team leaders and external service providers and ensure efficient resource planning.
  • You will continuously develop the maintenance strategy and make an important contribution to plant availability, efficiency and operational reliability.

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: as a maintenance planner or in a comparable function within an industrial production environment.
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): preferably in a mechanical environment; further training in the field of maintenance, technology or work planning is advantageous.
  • Analytical thinking as well as the ability to systematically assess technical relationships, plant criticalities and maintenance data and derive effective measures from them.
  • High personal responsibility, strong communication and enjoyment of working with different internal and external stakeholders.
  • Good knowledge of SAP PM as well as experience in maintenance planning, order processing, master data maintenance and dealing with MS Office.
  • in German (negotiation certain)
