Full description

BLS relies on SAP—and therefore entirely on you! As Head of SAP Technology, you will have an impact on the entire company through your team of 13 employees. Whether in the trains, the workshops or the business processes. Thanks to your commitment, day after day we manage to get our passengers to their destination safely and on time. Your work is part of everything—what a great feeling!

What awaits you

  • You lead and develop an experienced team of experts in SAP Basis, authorizations and development, and create the conditions for self-directed and effective work
  • You are responsible for the technological architecture and further development of our SAP landscape and ensure that on-premises, cloud and integration solutions work together over the long term
  • Together with your team, you take on project management tasks in the technology environment, ensure stable, secure and high-performance SAP operations, and continuously develop the operating models, platforms and technical standards
  • You are responsible for technological solution concepts, architectural decisions, operating concepts, and security and compliance requirements, and represent them to business units, IT and external partners
  • As a member of the management team of the SAP Competence Center, you actively help shape SAP's technological direction and contribute to strategic initiatives and cross-functional decisions

What you bring

  • Advanced training in computer science and/or business informatics at a university of applied sciences, with an affinity for technology
  • Several years of experience leading SAP technology teams (Basis, authorizations and development)
  • A sound understanding of modern SAP architectures and the ability to assess technological dependencies holistically
  • An aptitude for structuring complex technological issues, reaching architecture and solution decisions, and presenting them clearly and convincingly to different stakeholders
  • A solution-oriented approach, along with the ability to maintain an overview of the overall system even when interests differ and complexity is high

Your benefits – this is what you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a Half-Fare travelcard
  • Work wherever suits you best. Whether at home or in the office
  • Organize your working hours flexibly. We support part-time work, top sharing and job sharing
  • You have the option of purchasing an additional five to ten vacation days per year and taking a 40-working-day sabbatical every seven years.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.
  • Financial support for external continuing education, a leadership development program and an exciting internal range of learning opportunities and courses are available to you.

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second-largest railway company. What's more: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.

Requirements

  • Weiterführende Weiterbildung auf Stufe FH Informatik und/oder Wirtschafsinformatik mit Affinität zur Technologie

  • Mehrjährige Erfahrung in der Leitung von SAP-Technologie-Teams (Basis, Berechtigungen und Entwicklung)

  • Fundiertes Verständnis moderner SAP-Architekturen und die Fähigkeit, technologische Abhängigkeiten gesamtheitlich zu beurteilen

  • Flair, komplexe technologische Fragestellungen zu strukturieren, Architektur- und Lösungsentscheide herbeizuführen und diese gegenüber unterschiedlichen Stakeholdern verständlich und überzeugend zu vertreten

  • Lösungsorientiertes Handeln ergänzt mit der Fähigkeit, auch bei unterschiedlichen Interessen und hoher Komplexität den Blick für das Gesamtsystem zu wahren
