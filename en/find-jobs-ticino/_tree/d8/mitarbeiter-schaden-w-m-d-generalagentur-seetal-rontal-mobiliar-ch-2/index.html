Full description

This is what awaits you

You are at work with heart and enthusiasm and appreciate the open exchange within the team? As an employee: you accompany and support our customers proactively and easily in the event of damage. In this way, you contribute to the trust in the furniture as the most personal insurance in Switzerland.

This is what you do with us

You accompany our customers in the event of damage – from their notification to the conclusion and provide security towards our customers with regard to the processing of damages.

You maintain a close exchange with your team and all parties involved in the event of damage and inspire our customers through your high willingness to provide services. Optionally, you take over the practical training of the learners, carry out damage inspections on site and take responsibility for the commissioning, coordination and control of the craftsmen.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training in the insurance sector
  • Enjoying contact and accompanying customers in the event of damage
  • High motivation to learn new things in a varied field of activity
  • Self-assured occurrence
  • Proactive and independent working methods
  • Responsible, empathetic and open personality
  • Security in dealing with the Microsoft Office range and digital affinity
  • Fluent knowledge of German in words and writing, knowledge of other languages is a plus

Working with us

At the Seetal-Rontal General Agency, you will be welcomed by a motivated, dynamic and experienced team. Excellent working conditions, great training opportunities and a modern working environment await you.

Your workload is 100% or by arrangement a high part-time workload. Your place of work is Hochdorf and the job starts immediately or by agreement.

If we are successful, our employees also benefit. Each year, the distribution of a successful share and its amount is decided.

Training and development
