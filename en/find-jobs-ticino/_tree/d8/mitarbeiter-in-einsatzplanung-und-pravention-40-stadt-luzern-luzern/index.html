Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. You don’t see red in the blue light organization. With your administrative talent you support goal-oriented at the headquarters of the fire brigade city of Lucerne.

Your tasks

  • You support the mission planning & prevention team in the areas of mission planning and fire police in all administrative tasks and provide telephone information.
  • You help keep the operational documents up-to-date and complete so that the relevant information is available in the event of an incident.
  • You coordinate fire safety training and evacuation exercises, helping to ensure that employees and participants are prepared for an emergency.

Your profile

  • You have a completed technical or commercial vocational apprenticeship, ideally supplemented with a further education at the level of professional card.
  • You bring several years of practical experience with the fire brigade and ideally have already exercised a squad function.
  • You are familiar with plans or have experience in handling plan documents.
  • You formulate safely and stylishly in German and use the applications of MS 365 routinely and efficiently.
  • You work independently, accurately and reliably. Even in a varied working environment, you remain flexible and keep an overview.

More on the spot
