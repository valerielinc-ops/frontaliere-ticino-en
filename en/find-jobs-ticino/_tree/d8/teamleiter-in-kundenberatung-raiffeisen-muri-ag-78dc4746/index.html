Full description

22.10.2026 Muri AG Sins

Become part of our team!

For our offices in Muri and Sins, Raiffeisenbank Oberfreiamt is looking for a committed personality who, together with its team, ensures a convincing customer experience. You take on responsibility, continue to develop employees and at the same time stay close to our customers.

What awaits you?

  • You lead and develop a team of 4 to 6 employees (including learners)
  • You plan the human resources and ensure a smooth operation
  • You ensure a high quality of service and consulting
  • You advise customers in the areas of numbers, savings, investing and pensions
  • You recognize customer potential, strengthen existing relationships and win new customers
  • You represent our bank at customer events and actively maintain your network in the region
  • You bring in ideas and actively shape the further development of the area

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training or similar training in the financial services environment
  • Experience in customer service
  • First leadership experience or motivation for the next career step
  • High customer and service orientation as well as pleasure in dealing with people
  • Independent, responsible and solution-oriented working method
  • SAQ certification is beneficial

Ready for the next step? Then we look forward to meeting you. Together we shape the future of customer consulting at Raiffeisenbank Oberfreiamt. Marco Pürro Head of Private Client Services Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
