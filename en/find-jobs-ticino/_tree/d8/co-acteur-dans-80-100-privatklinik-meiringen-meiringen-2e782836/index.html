Full description

At the Meiringen Private Clinic, people with all forms of mental illness are admitted, treated and cared for. The third-largest psychiatric clinic in the Canton of Bern is open to patients from all over Switzerland and abroad and is responsible for inpatient psychiatric primary care in the eastern Bernese Oberland. Care and support are provided according to the current state of medical-psychiatric and nursing knowledge. As a member of the "Swiss Leading Hospitals", the Meiringen Private Hospital is one of the leading private hospitals in Switzerland.

The teams with a focus on acute admission open and protected care for people with illnesses from the entire spectrum of psychiatry who require extensive psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment. The protected focus has a 24-hour emergency admission obligation. The performance mandate is based on the latest scientific findings in an individually defined treatment and care process. Psychosocial reintegration is considered a primary treatment goal.
