Shaping the future with innovation Become part of our success and shape the future with us! Their mission

  • Design, construction and testing of prototypes (robots, camera systems, measuring systems)
  • Commissioning and documentation of industrial plants
  • Maintenance and debugging at the end customer
  • Support for deep learning projects (labeling, data acquisition, databases, process pipeline, test & validation)
  • Performance of measurement series
  • Configuring, ordering and commissioning of systems (industrial vision, sensors, high-performance PCs)
  • Support for mechanical constructions (CAD & Hands On)

Their tasks

  • Execution of tasks in accordance with the requirements and instructions of engineers using a wide variety of procedures and techniques
  • Performance of measurements and tests and analysis and communication of results
  • Maintenance of equipment and machinery and management of material and product orders
  • Participation in interdisciplinary projects and activities
  • Maintenance and continuous improvement of processes
  • Participation in ensuring and developing quality, including related documentation
  • Responsibility for infrastructure or the laboratory:

Your profile Technical competences

  • Completed basic training as an automatic engineer EFZ or electrical engineer EFZ with corresponding further training in the field of data analytics or computer science or mechatronics or robotics

Other requirements

  • Knowledge of Python, C# or C++ before advantage
  • Hands on work (electronics, mechanics, software)
  • Embedded systems (e.g. Raspberry Pi, ESP32, Arduino)
  • Windows and Linux
  • Databases (SQL, NoSQL, Time-Series)
  • Basic Data Analytics
  • Basic knowledge of control and control technology
  • Knowledge of additive manufacturing processes (3D-printing)
  • Self-employed and efficient working methods
  • MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • GIT version control system
  • Broadband technical understanding
  • Workshop/mechanics and CAD basic knowledge
  • Independent and methodically structured working style, ready to take responsibility
  • German fluent (C1+), English (B1+)

Personal competences

  • Readiness for interdisciplinary cooperation with colleagues from other fields
  • They like to work in several interdisciplinary projects at the same time and can distribute the priorities as needed.
  • Willingness to continue to train and become familiar with new fields of application
  • Flexibility, networked thinking and acting, resilience and an open, team-oriented and communicative nature
  • Development projects are familiar to you and you enjoy solving interdisciplinary tasks in teamwork. Thanks to your improvisational talent, you can also tackle challenging challenges in an uncomplicated way. Your process engineering skills, your enjoyment of teamwork and your creativity can be put to full use in a technology-oriented environment.
