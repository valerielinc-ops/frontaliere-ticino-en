Full description

Professional profile

As a polymechanic, you handle a wide range of orders from our production and are responsible for perfect execution on the agreed date. Ronda supports projects in which you solve tasks independently as well as in teamwork with other learners or professionals.

What we expect from them

  • Successful school leaving at secondary level E or P
  • Good mathematical skills
  • Interest in STEM subjects
  • spatial imagination
  • Joy of craftsmanship

Practical training

Basic education

We ensure that you receive an excellent basic education in the first two years of training. So you learn the basics of manual and mechanical production and produce for example

  • a grain that you will use in day-to-day operations, or
  • a movement model of turning and milling parts, in which, after assembly, you understand how a pointer drive works

Priority training

From the third year onwards, you will start your main training and work on challenging parts on computer-controlled milling and lathes; for example, clockwork boards, machine parts, test equipment, etc.

Internal internships

During your polymechanics training at Ronda, you will complete exciting internships in various departments:

  • In toolmaking, you will help to produce and test new punching tools
  • In order to understand what is “gold-plated” at Ronda, you will get to know the coating process for surface finishing.
  • How the smallest gears of a movement are interlocked, you see in the wheel fabrication
  • Making a movement board with a Ø 1.00 mm milling cutter is not everyday. With these tiny tools, you work in the milling department.
  • In the laboratory, you perform measurements on a clockwork movement and decide whether the required readings are met

Applying for an apprenticeship and trial apprenticeship

Are you convinced? Then your next step is a trial lesson. It gives you an insight into the real working day and also serves as a review of your professional desire. But beware: The trial apprentices are offered exclusively for apprenticeship applicants and for free apprenticeship places.
