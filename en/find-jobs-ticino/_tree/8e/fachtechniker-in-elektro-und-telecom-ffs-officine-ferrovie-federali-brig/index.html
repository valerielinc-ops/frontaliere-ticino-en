Your day-to-day work is particularly diverse: from railway and business communication to watch systems, medium-voltage, building automation and card terminal systems. With us you work not only on a plant, but on a variety of different technologies. With 25 electrical and 35 telecommunications platforms, you can expect an exceptionally broad and varied technical environment. You not only keep existing plants running, but also actively shape their further development. You can expect varied technical challenges and the opportunity to take on responsibility and develop yourself professionally and personally.
