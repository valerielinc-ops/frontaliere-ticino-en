Full description

Are you ready to help develop the digital transformation? As an IT service and competence center of the cantonal administration of Graubünden, we support our internal customers with a secure basic infrastructure and advanced applications. Together with you, we are advancing the digital transformation of the canton of Graubünden.

We as the Office for Computer Science are looking for an ICT specialist in Digital Workplace & Collaboration.

Tasks

  • Ensure the operation and further development of the collaboration and automation tools based on the M365 platform
  • You plan and prioritize projects together with the stakeholders and accompany them throughout the entire life cycle of the solution
  • You support our customers in the digitization and automation of collaboration and business processes
  • You work in 2nd and 3rd level support
  • You create and maintain technical documentation and specifications

Requirements

  • Education or training in computer science or a comparable environment
  • Experience with Microsoft 365 (Teams, SharePoint Online, Power Platform, Admin Center)
  • Experience in the operation and in the further development of collaboration and workplace solutions
  • Networked, analytical thinking and a structured and team-oriented way of working
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is advantageous
