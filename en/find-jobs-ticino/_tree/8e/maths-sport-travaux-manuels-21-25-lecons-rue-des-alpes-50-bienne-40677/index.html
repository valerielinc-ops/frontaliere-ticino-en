Full description

Portrait La Filière Bilinguale Secondary is a bilingual secondary school (German and French) located in Biel, which welcomes students from 9am to 11am. At the FiBiS, both languages and cultures are practiced daily. This means that students speaking other languages are also welcome to the FiBiS. The FiBiS thus reflects the cultural diversity of the city of Biel.

Tasks

For the second semester of the school year 2026/2027, we are looking for a teacher of mathematics, sport and manual work to replace a departure on training leave. Do you like working as a team, conducting innovative projects and have a great sense of organization?

Profile required In order to carry out this successful secondary teaching profession, a HEP diploma and a few years of experience are desired. Natural talents who are still in HEP training are also welcome. The willingness to collaborate within the teaching profession and to participate in various school events and working groups is required. The J+S Ski and/or Basis Pool diploma are also desired.

We offer

  • An exciting field of activity with a high degree of personal autonomy.
  • Many possibilities for organizing courses according to LP21 and RIP.
  • A pleasant working atmosphere within a well-functioning and very helpful team, which assumes its responsibilities collectively.
  • Support from school management, secretariat, support teachers and an effective maintenance service.
  • A bilingual and multicultural environment
  • A modern infrastructure with video projector and visualizer in all classrooms, a computer room and laptop carts to borrow in the classroom.
  • A nice location, accessible on foot in 15 minutes from Biel station or by bus (two stops). Parking spaces are limited.
