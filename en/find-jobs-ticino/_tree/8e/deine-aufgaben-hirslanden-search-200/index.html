Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Permanence | Bern Occupation by: by appointment

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Do you want to be part of a motivated Hirslanden Group team in the fields of orthopaedics, sports medicine and medicine? We are looking for a dipl. Nurse as support for our team. We look forward to seeing you!

Your Advantages in Us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in fitness subscription

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • You are responsible for professional, interdisciplinary and evidence-based patient care.
  • Your main tasks include participating in preventive, diagnostic and therapeutic measures and implementing medical prescriptions
  • With us, you take on a lot of personal responsibility and independence in clinical decision-making

Your profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Nurse / Nurse specialist HF
  • Several years of professional experience as a Dipl. Nurse, preferably in acute hospital
  • In-depth competences in patient education, discussion and clinical decision-making
  • Good knowledge of German and ease of use with the computer
  • Your established personality and your appreciative attitude towards patients, relatives and employees round off your profile.

For additional information, Moira Straubhaar, Head of Care Service, is available at T. We look forward to your contact.
