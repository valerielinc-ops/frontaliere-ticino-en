Full description

Building and technical designer

Your main responsibilities

Develop and update architectural and technical plans

  • To carry out architectural, technical and principle plans according to graphic charter and SIA standards.
  • Conduct on-site measurements and ensure that plans such as constructed are updated.

Pilot the procedure for drawing up plans

  • Update the graphic charter.
  • Ensure the application of standards (internal and external) and good practices.

Managing development plans (Workplace Management)

  • Design and update workspace plans according to OLT3, EHS and other legal or internal standards.

Manage fire safety plans

  • Produce and maintain fire safety plans and evacuation posters related to the campus security team.

Project: CAMF/GMAO Tool

  • Integrate the plans and generate the reports necessary to pilot the service.
  • Participate in the development of the tool.

Your profile

Training

  • Building drawing CFC or equivalent training (required) .
  • Additional specialization in a building-related field (attraction).

Experience

  • Minimum 5 years experience in building design or building technique (industrial offices and halls) (required) .

Technical knowledge

  • SIA standards, cantonal energy and fire safety requirements.
  • Reading electrical diagrams, hot/cold, ventilation and sanitary.
  • Knowledge OLT3.

Computer tools

  • REVIS (Required)
  • AutoCAD (Required)
  • CAFM (important asset)
  • MS Office (required)
  • SAP (Attraction)

Languages

  • French (current).
  • English (an asset).

Personal suitability

  • Autonomy, rigor, sense of responsibility.
  • Practical spirit, oriented solutions and service.
  • Organization, anticipation and management of priorities.
  • Facilities in on-site measurements and technical inventory.
  • Team spirit and ability to collaborate in a changing environment.
