About us

The Institute of Social and Preventive Medicine (ISPM) at the University of Bern is active in research, service and the teaching of public health, clinical epidemiology, biostatistics and international environmental health. www.ispm.unibe.ch In Switzerland, an estimated half a million people live with a rare disease. Nevertheless, nationally available data on diagnoses, disease courses, therapies and the needs of those affected are still lacking. But it is precisely this information that is crucial for improving care and advancing research in a targeted manner. This is where the Swiss Register for Rare Diseases comes in. Under the direction of Prof. Dr. med. Claudia Kuehni works together with hospitals, patient organisations, Orphanet Switzerland and kosek to build this data base. The register creates important foundations for care and research. In the future, it should enable studies and make it easier for those affected to participate in research projects.
