Place of work: Hirslanden Salem Hospital | Bern

Occupation by: From now or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74078

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Our team at the day clinic is looking for a new team member.

Your benefits to us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • No late, night and Sunday services
  • 4 extra free days thanks to paid changing time
  • Participation in public transport costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in fitness subscription

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • You take care of the transport of patients to the operating room
  • You also accompany patients during the entry process
  • You perform logistical activities, such as managing and replenishing materials
  • In case of interest and suitability, you can take on other similar activities

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a nursing assistant (SRK, AGS) or have experience in a day clinic
  • Your empathy in contact with our patients distinguishes you and with you our team gains a flexible, team-capable and open personality
  • You are also known for your efficient way of working and always keep an overview in a dynamic working environment
  • General PC skills as well as good German skills are required
  • Your working days are from Monday to Friday (start of work from 06:15 am), occasionally you are also ready to work on a Saturday between October and April

For more information, Corinne Liechti, Head of Day Clinic/Admission Clinic, is available at T. We look forward to your contact.
