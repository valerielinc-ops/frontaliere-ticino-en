Full description

In the new “shop-in-shop” concept at the Post Office, you are right on the pulse of our customers. You actively address the customers, recognize their needs and inspire with competent advice and tailor-made solutions. With your strong sales mentality, you take advantage of opportunities, strengthen sustainable customer relationships and help position PostFinance as the first choice in financial matters.

You can do that.

  • You advise customers: inside expertly and sell the products Creation, provision, pay and saving in a post office
  • You actively address current customers in the post office and recognize potential
  • You develop solutions that are tailored to the individual wishes, goals and life situations of our customers and successfully lead them to the conclusion
  • You identify further potential in the area of financing and pension provision and forward it specifically to our internal specialists: insiders for in-depth consultations

You bring that with you

  • Good knowledge of banking or finance through education, training or professional experience
  • Enjoyment in sales and closing as well as a sense of customer needs as a basis for sustainable advice
  • Open, communicative personality with a secure appearance and high self-motivation
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good user knowledge in MS-Office range, further language skills are advantageous
  • Your working hours range from around 9 a.m. to around 6:30 p.m.
