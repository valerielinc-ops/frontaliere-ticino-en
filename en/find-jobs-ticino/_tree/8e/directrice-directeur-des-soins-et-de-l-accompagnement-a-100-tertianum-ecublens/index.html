Director / Director of 100% Care and Support

Your tasks

  • Responsible for quality and implementation of delivery mandate and care process
  • Development of care and support for our guests
  • Quality and productivity control (cot occupancy, entry of services in CareCoach)
  • Implementation of budget and achievement of sector objectives
  • Organisation and coordination of different interest groups (closer ones, family doctors, other organisations and institutions and insurance)
  • Caregiver management
  • Employee recruitment and development process
  • Close collaboration with management

Your profile

  • Infirmary/nurse ES graduate training or equivalent training recognized by CRS
  • Professional and leadership experience in the care of several years with people with dementia is an advantage
  • At least 1-2 years of experience as a care and support manager or at least 3-4 years of professional experience as a team leader
  • Dynamic framework with a certificate in management
  • French mother tongue, knowledge of German is an advantage
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Great empathy and special ability to work as a team
