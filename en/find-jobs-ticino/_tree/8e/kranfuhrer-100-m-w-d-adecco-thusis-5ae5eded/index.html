Full description

Introduction

For a customer with an industrial operating environment, we are looking for a crane operator (m/f/d) to support ongoing operations. The position includes the safe operation of crane systems within the framework of clearly defined workflows and compliance with all applicable safety regulations. We are looking for a reliable person who works precisely and has a responsible handling of heavy loads. The activity is versatile, since different lifting, transport and loading tasks can occur depending on the area of use. The workspace is organized in a practical manner and requires attention, care and a secure understanding of technical processes. Long-term cooperation is envisaged as the contract is offered for an indefinite period.

Tasks

  • They operate crane systems safely and responsibly in the ongoing operation.
  • They transport and move loads as specified and in compliance with the safety rules.
  • They shall check the correct condition of the equipment used before work begins.
  • They work closely with colleagues and other stakeholders for smooth implementation.
  • They comply with all operational requirements as well as the applicable accident prevention regulations.

Profile

  • They have practical experience or in-depth knowledge in the operation of crane systems.
  • They work reliably, carefully and keep an overview even during load movements.
  • They bring a strong security awareness in dealing with technology and loads.
  • They are ready to take on different and changing tasks in the company.
  • They communicate clearly and enjoy working in a team with other participants.

Advantages

  • The remuneration is predominantly above the collective agreement and is regulated transparently.
  • You can expect varied activities in the daily workflow.
  • A permanent employment contract is offered.
