As a leader or leader, you lead the area. They shall establish relations with the external implementing bodies and service providers of the IV scheme. You are the contact person for the IV positions and the RAD regarding the pension and the procedure within the disability insurance.

  • Date of entry: 01.03.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20698
  • Location: Effingerstrasse 20, 3008 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Managing the area in terms of personnel, expertise and organisation
  • Track the administrative investigation procedure and its relevant development in the pension investigation procedure and the invalidity assessment
  • Participating in legislative work, drawing up instructions and responding to parliamentary initiatives
  • Review first- and second-instance case law in the field of procedures and invalidity assessment and prepare the legal documents
  • Ensure uniform implementation in the field of procedure and pension in IV
  • Ensure a uniform fight against insurance abuse in IV and develop measures against unjustified benefits

This makes them unique.

  • Readiness to work, independent working methods and mastery of an official language and negotiable in a second official language
  • Very good knowledge of social security, especially IV
  • Political sensitivity and negotiating skills
  • High social skills, assertiveness, will to shape and flexibility
