Full description

USZ Campus

by January 2027 or by agreement

Each irradiation counts: With your expertise and sensitivity, you contribute every day to ensuring that patients are treated safely and optimally. You work with the latest radiation technology and personally accompany people through an important part of their therapy. In this way, you combine highly specialized technology with an activity that makes a noticeable difference for patients.

Your main tasks

  • Professional and independent execution of irradiation with the linear accelerator (Siemens) and the integrated surface guidance (VisionRT) as well as the IGRT systems
  • Independent and professional application of brachytherapy (Varian) as well as radiation with MR-Linac
  • Performing planning MRI examinations in irradiation storage at 1.5T and 0.55T (Siemens), planning computer tomography and 4D computer tomography
  • Careful care and guidance for patients
  • Digital collection and management of data in the clinic information system (Aria and Kisim)

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a radiology specialist / radiology specialist HF/FH and ideally professional experience
  • You are a motivated, tech-savvy and communication-strong personality and have high social competence, empathy and resilience
  • Patient orientation is not a foreign word for you and you have a friendly attitude
  • You have good knowledge of German. English skills are beneficial
  • Good teamwork is important to you. You are reliable, self-initiative and resilient

Our offer

We offer you an interesting workplace in the university environment, equipped with the latest technology. You will get the chance to bring your acquired knowledge to a strong interdisciplinary, international team in an exciting and challenging environment. You benefit from attractive employment conditions and from various further education opportunities. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered. Thank you for your understanding.
