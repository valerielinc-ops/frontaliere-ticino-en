Personnel Developer — Kantonale Verwaltung Basel-Landschaft

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • Further development of management-internal personnel development by improving processes and structures in close cooperation with the managers of the various departments in order to skillfully link effective learning and everyday work
  • Designing, coordinating and accompanying internal and external further education and ensuring that learning content has a lasting impact for employees and for the employer
  • Active participation and support of managers in the determination of key positions and in the identification of employees with development potential
  • Support of managers in development and succession issues as well as active support of employees in their professional and personal development
  • Development and support of digital and user-oriented learning formats for management and departments as well as organisation and moderation of workshops
  • Taking over partly administrative tasks

Requirements

  • University degree in psychology or business administration as well as specific training in personnel development and pronounced professional and project experience
  • Interested in working in public administration with proximity to political issues and a diversity of people from a wide range of fields
  • digital affinity, creativity and good conceptual skills
  • Strong presentation and moderation capabilities
  • independent, networked, structured and solution-oriented working method

The cantonal administration of Basel-Landschaft is the largest employer in the canton. It offers interesting jobs and a variety of training opportunities. In a wide range of professions and specialist areas, more than 6,000 employees provide services for the population and the economy on a daily basis. In doing so, they contribute to a high standard of living and promote the attractiveness of the canton. Actively shape the future with us!
