Interaction with different disaster services and communication with stakeholders and partners motivates you? Of a high performance nature, do you like to use your expertise in the field of automotive claims to offer the best possible service to our customers?

In this role, you are part of the sector of activity Sinister motor vehicles, in the team of Automotive Experts, in Zurich Switzerland. As an automotive expert you assist our customers and injured persons in repairing damage in a fast and non-bureaucratic way. Whether with a private car or a company car: the one on the way can count on Zurich.

If you live in the Chablais region and have a high sense of responsibility associated with an ability to impose yourself and a great flexibility in dealing with the different stakeholders complete your profile, then we look forward to your postulation.

Your missions

  • Performing motor vehicle expertise.
  • Determine methods of repair and calculate the amount of damage.
  • Assess insurance coverage and process liability claims.
  • Verify repair invoices, loss of value claims and loss of use compensation, in collaboration with other Help Points and Claims Centres.
  • Conduct negotiations with policyholders, injured persons and repair companies.

Your profile

  • Federal patent of motor vehicle mechatronician, carmaker or car painter OR training deemed equivalent.
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in the repair sector or in the reception of vehicles damaged in an accident.
  • Great customer orientation and fun at work in contact with different stakeholders.
  • Very good knowledge of the MS-Office palette as well as systems related to repair.
  • French mother tongue; knowledge in German is an asset.

Professional travel: up to 50% per year
