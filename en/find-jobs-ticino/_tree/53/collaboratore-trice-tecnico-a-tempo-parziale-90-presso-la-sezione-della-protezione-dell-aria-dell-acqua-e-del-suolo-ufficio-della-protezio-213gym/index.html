Full description

Tasks

  • applies the provisions of the Ordinance on the protection of water and technical regulations applicable to the transport sector (garage, workshops, etc.) and the building industry
  • examines and approves construction applications for the construction or rehabilitation of water treatment plants
  • releases authorizations to channel waste water of artisan origin
  • performs testing of water disposal systems and plants, drafting its testing reports
  • takes additional tasks in the area of hazardous liquid storage
  • cooperates in national working groups in developing industry-specific environmental directives
  • performs special tasks, at the request of superiors, related to professional skills and knowledge
  • is available to join SPAAS's Nucleo Operativo Incidenti (NOI) picket service

Requirements

  • university education or university education (bachelor) in chemical engineering or environmental sciences, or equivalent title
  • experience in the field of waste water treatment plants
  • good knowledge of conventional computer tools, including GIS applications
  • spirit of initiative and attitude to independent work as well as group
  • ease of contact with user
  • availability to follow study days, training courses and conferences outside Canton
  • excellent knowledge (conversation and editing) of Italian and good knowledge of German and French spoken and written

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

5 November 2026
