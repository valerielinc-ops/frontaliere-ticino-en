Do you want to combine leadership responsibility in psychiatry with plannable free time? In a specialized stationary unit in the Bern area, you can expect a family team, a broad professional activity with further development possibilities and a manageable cadre service system in a natural environment. Our client is an established specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the Berne area with inpatient, daily clinical and outpatient services. They take responsibility for a psychotherapeutically oriented unit with around 25-26 therapy places. The focus is on depression and burnout as well as modern psychotherapeutic and pharmacotherapeutic treatment concepts. The team consists of 22 employees. This includes three assistant doctors, nurses and staff from psychology, psychotherapy and peer work. The management takes place together with nursing and psychology. The cooperation is characterized by a lived du-culture, short decision-making paths, flat hierarchies and a family working atmosphere in which the interprofessional exchange takes place at eye level.

Their tasks

  • Medical-therapeutic, organizational and conceptual co-management of the inpatient psychotherapy unit together with nursing management and senior psychologist
  • Clinical responsibility with reports, visits as well as entry and exit management and close connection to patient care
  • Personnel management on the medical side as well as supervision, teaching and further training of assistant doctors
  • Further development of evidence-based psychotherapeutic and pharmacotherapeutic treatment concepts
  • Participation in quality assurance, clinic-wide projects and – depending on the professional profile – further therapeutic tasks

Your Competences

  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy or corresponding recognised or recognised qualification from EU/EFTA
  • In-depth knowledge of psychotherapy and pharmacotherapy as well as interest in the treatment of depression and burnout
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Reflected and empathetic leader with decision-making ability and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation
  • In the case of a foreign degree, we provide you with advisory and financial support in the mediation process for MEBEKO recognition

Their advantages

  • Plannable medical routine: 42-hour week, approximately 1–2 weekend services per quarter
  • Time for rest: 25 days of holiday up to 44 years, 27 days from 45 and 32 days from 55; in addition, service compensation for a further 10 days
  • Natural and yet well connected: green clinic area with park and animal facilities as well as good accessibility with public transport and car; the Bern region offers forests, rivers, lakes and a wide range of leisure opportunities
  • Professional development remains part of the position: internal and external training, SIWF-certified training centre and, if qualified, perspective on a deputy chief medical function
  • Transparent salary level: fixed salary according to experience and responsibility; for additional project or clinic tasks, a higher overall package is possible
