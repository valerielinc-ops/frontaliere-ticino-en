Project Manager (H/F/X) – CDM (1 year) Attached to the Project Manager Process, you are a member of the Project Process department of the Technical Directorate. HOW DO YOU HAVE AN IMPACT? You will be responsible for deploying two automatic lubrication platforms on our assembly lines:

  • Integrate customer needs into the manufacturer's technical standards and identify project risks
  • Perform a benchmark of possible solutions, validate these solutions on our test platform and write test reports
  • Formalize the specifications, resulting from the conclusions of your test plans
  • Preparing the demand for investment from these platforms
  • Monitor investment with development and construction planning with the help of suppliers and internal teams
  • Pilot your project indicators (quality, cost and time)
  • Ensure communication, reporting and project animation
  • Be responsible for receiving the various milestones and meeting the project budget
  • Develop platforms with our partner
  • Draft operating standards and train production staff in the piloting of platforms (operators and regulators).
