Full description

Are you passionate about shaping the future of risk governance? Do you thrive at the intersection of policy design, stakeholder collaboration, and operational risk oversight? If so, this is your opportunity to make a meaningful impact at one of the world's leading reinsurance companies. About the Role As an Operational Risk Policy Governance Analyst at Swiss Re, you will play a pivotal role in building and shaping a central second line of defence (2LoD) policy governance capability for selected operational risks, including emerging and evolving areas such as AI, Data Management, and Records Management. This is a unique opportunity to contribute to the design of clear, consistent, and effective policy frameworks that drive sustainable governance across the organisation. You will work closely with first-line teams and subject matter experts, bringing structure, rigour, and insight to the full policy lifecycle—from architecture and guardrails through to approvals, periodic review, and continuous improvement.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain policy architecture — design and evolve the structural framework for selected operational risk policies, ensuring clarity, consistency, and alignment with Swiss Re's governance standards
  • Define policy requirements and guardrails — draft clear, concise, and practical policy requirements that provide effective second line oversight and guidance to first line teams
  • Coordinate approvals and periodic reviews — manage governance processes end-to-end, ensuring timely approvals, structured review cycles, and accurate documentation
  • Support end-to-end policy lifecycle management and reporting — oversee the full lifecycle of policies from inception through to retirement, maintaining high-quality records and producing meaningful governance reporting
  • Provide trusted second line oversight and constructive challenge — act as a credible and collaborative partner to first line teams and subject matter experts, offering sound judgement and independent perspective on governance matters
  • Engage and manage stakeholders across functions and seniority levels — build and maintain strong professional networks, communicating effectively with both technical and non-technical audiences
  • Contribute to continuous improvement — proactively identify opportunities to enhance policy governance processes, frameworks, and tools in a dynamic and evolving risk environment

About the Team The ORM Policy Governance team sits within Group Functions Risk Management (GFRM) at Swiss Re. The team is responsible for building and maintaining a central 2LoD policy governance capability for selected operational risks, working across the organisation to ensure that policy requirements are clear, robust, and fit for purpose. The team collaborates closely with first line teams and subject matter experts to promote consistent, sustainable governance across a broad range of evolving operational risk topics — from AI and Data Management to Records Management and beyond.
