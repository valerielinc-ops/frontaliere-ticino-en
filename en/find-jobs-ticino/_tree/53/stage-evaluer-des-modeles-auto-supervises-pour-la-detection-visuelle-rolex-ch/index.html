Introduction

For our Quality & Sustainability department, we are looking for a Trainee whose main mission will be to carry out a comparative study of self-supervised models for the detection of visual defects, in order to select and generalize good practices.

Responsibilities

  • Explore the state of art of self-supervised models, both internally and in the literature
  • Compare the performance of different models on data sets from several internal cases
  • Integrate data enhancement opportunities into the training strategy
  • Providing models and strategies developed in a shared library

Profile

  • Currently in Master in Data Science, Robotics or deemed equivalent
  • Available for a period of 6 months at 100%, from September 2026 or to be agreed
  • Knowledge of languages (e.g. Python)
  • With analytical capabilities
  • Service oriented, proactive and autonomous
