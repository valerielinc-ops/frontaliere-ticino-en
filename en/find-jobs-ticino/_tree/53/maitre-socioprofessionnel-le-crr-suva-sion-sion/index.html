Online since 14.09.2026 Share on URL copied in the clipboard! Entry into office: 1 January 2027 Activity rate: 80% Want to put your skills at the service of a mission that makes sense? Join the Roman Catholic Rehabilitation Clinic of the Suva in Sion and help to accompany injured people towards more autonomy and a return to the professional world. Our Vocational Rehabilitation Skills Centre (CCRP) has a space of 720 m2 allowing patients to compare their abilities with the concrete requirements of different professions: administration, mechanics, welding, wood, building and fine dexterity activities. In order to complete our team, we are looking for a socio-professional master from 1 January or to agree on it (80 % activity rate) Your main missions

  • Provide guidance to insured patients for the assessment of professional skills.
  • Develop integrated professional assessments according to internal standards.
  • Write the final report on the care of insured patients, in collaboration with counselling psychologists.
  • Manage its schedule for the planning of patient-friendly activities, including external internships.
  • Establish contacts with Rehabilitation Advisors of the AI Office and case managers Suva.
  • Manage materials and supplies necessary for the proper operation of workshops and apply hygiene and safety procedures.
  • To ensure a permanence within the CPRC during the patient-insured reception hours (8h-17h), in liaison with colleagues. Our expectations
  • Diploma of socio-professional master/ ES
  • Training in industry or mechanics
  • Ideally, experience (3 years) in a similar function
  • Organizational and Responsibilities
  • Flexibility and adaptability
  • Great relational ease and excellent interpersonal skills
  • Mastery of current computer tools and French language ( minimum B2 level ) Why join us?
  • Culture and values: We value collaboration, innovation and respect. You will be part of a team committed to providing the best possible services.
  • Personal development: We offer many opportunities for further training and professional development. Your career progress is important to us.
  • Benefits: High social benefits, many social benefits, free park space and much more. Modalities
  • Date of entry into office: From 1 January 2027 or to be agreed
  • Deadline for receipt of applications: 27 September 2026
  • Complete dossiers (letter of motivation, CV, copies of diplomas and work certificates) should be sent via the form below.
