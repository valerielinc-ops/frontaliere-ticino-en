At PhysioMobil Doris Schwiede, you can expect a varied job outside of the classic day-to-day practice. You treat your patient in their own home as well as in retirement and nursing homes in Glarnerland. You can concentrate on the therapeutic work – the organization and administration takes over our back office. Top remuneration from CHF 100,000 to CHF 130,000 Good work deserves exceptionally good remuneration. With a 100% fee, we offer you an annual compensation package of CHF 100,000 to CHF 130,000. The package consists of your fixed salary, a performance-related bonus and the agreed expenses. Those who take responsibility, work reliably and ensure a high quality of treatment should also benefit financially. Quality and patient satisfaction always come first. The bonus is therefore not only based on your performance, but also requires that our patients are professionally competent and personally convincingly cared for. 🗓️ 100% work – 4-day week possible You want to work a full time, but not necessarily five days a week? With appropriate assignment planning, you can spread your 100% expense over four working days. Even with a workload between 40 and 100%, we coordinate working days and assignment times together with you. This creates a working model that fits both our patient and your life. 📋 An existing treatment plan – with room for your wishes Your patient is already there. You do not have to acquire or build your own workload first. You adopt an existing treatment plan that allows you a structured and plannable start. At the same time, this is not carved in stone: working days, operating times and tours can be adapted to your wishes after joint agreement. Our back office supports you with appointment organization, coordination and administrative tasks. You are on the road independently, but have reliable support in the background at all times. 🚗 Mobile on the go – well organized and fairly compensated Since your work is mobile, we also provide a suitable mobility solution. Depending on the arrangement, you will receive a business vehicle with a fuel card or a generous expense refund. In addition, we provide you with workwear and support suitable professional training financially. Geriatrics is our physiotherapy in its entire range Geriatric physiotherapy does not mean treating the same thing every day. Our patients bring diseases from the entire physiotherapeutic spectrum.

These include in particular

  • Acute surgical-orthopaedic post-treatments for reduced mobility
  • Orthopaedic complaints and postoperative rehabilitation
  • Neurological and internistic pathologies
  • Manual lymphatic drainage
  • Walking and balance training
  • Mobilisation, strengthening and fall prevention
  • Oncological and palliative physiotherapy
  • Complex and multimorbid treatment courses
  • Promotion and maintenance of independence in everyday life

You treat your patient where your support is directly needed: at their home or in retirement and nursing homes. As a result, you not only get to know a clinical picture, but also the actual life situation of the person. This enables individual therapy goals, varied treatments and long-term therapeutic relationships. What you get with us

  • Top compensation of CHF 100,000 to CHF 130,000 for a 100% fee
  • Performance-related bonus that rewards your personal commitment
  • 4-day week also possible at 100%
  • Pensum between 40 and 100 %
  • Existing treatment plan and existing patients

  • Administrative and organizational relief through our back office

  • Personal structures and direct decision-making processes
  • Time for individual and high-quality treatment

What you will not find with us

  • A hectic everyday practice in 20-minute intervals
  • Rigid standard programs
  • Same procedure every day
  • Unnecessarily rigid working hours
  • Acquisition of patients on their own
  • An anonymous large company

You bring that with you

  • Training as a physiotherapist FH/HF or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • Ideally, first or several years of professional experience
  • Manual lymphatic drainage training or readiness to complete it
  • Interest in geriatric, lymphological, oncological or palliative treatments
  • Independent and reliable working method
  • Empathy, responsibility and joy in dealing with people
  • Good organization and careful treatment documentation
  • Category B driving licence
