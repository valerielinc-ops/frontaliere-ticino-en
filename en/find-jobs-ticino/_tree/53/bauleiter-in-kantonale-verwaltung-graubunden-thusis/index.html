Site Manager — Kantonale Verwaltung Graubünden

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The civil engineering office is responsible for the design, construction, maintenance and operation of the cantonal roads as well as the hydraulic engineering and slow transport in Graubünden. We also take care of the operational maintenance of the national roads on the cantonal territory. The canton’s road infrastructure comprises over 1,450 artificial buildings, including bridges at airy heights of up to 90 meters and tunnels of up to 3 kilometers in length. The challenges that the nature of our mountain canton brings in summer and winter make our work demanding and versatile. As a Friendly Work Space certified office, the health and satisfaction of our approximately 480 employees are particularly important to us. For this reason, we are committed to systematic occupational health management (BGM).

Tasks

  • Management and control of the allocated construction sites as a client representative
  • Termination and coordination of work
  • Ensuring the quality, extent and execution of the entire contract work
  • Preparation of estimates, tender documents and accounts
  • Budget monitoring
  • Completion of technical road maintenance tasks
  • Performing of Picettdienst as operational manager/in

Requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship in construction
  • Additional training as construction manager, polisher, construction manager or equivalent training
  • Several years of professional experience in road and civil engineering
  • Practical experience in devising
  • Personality with assertiveness, organizational and negotiation skills as well as team skills
  • Romance skills are beneficial
