Location: Lausanne, Switzerland Work model: Hybrid / Full time Contract type / duration: Permanent

Shape the future through scientific breakthroughs and innovation.

As a Technician in Lipids & Metabolic Analytics department at Nestlé Research, your mission is to provide analytical support in lipids analytics and deliver timely, high-quality data that supports research, innovation, and operational needs. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with scientists, experts, peers across analytical groups, and external partners. This is your opportunity to turn ideas into sustainable and innovative solutions for people, pets, and the planet.

How you’ll make an impact as a Technician in Lipids Analytics

  • Delivering timely, high-quality lipid data that advances research projects and supports operational priorities.
  • Executing lipid extraction and chromatography experiments with accuracy, and rigor.
  • Implementing analytical methods and technical solutions that improve lipid analysis capabilities.
  • Advancing innovation by exploring and applying new analytical technologies that expand what is possible in lipids analytics.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing knowledge in lipid classes, lipid extraction, and the analysis of lipophilic compounds with a similar position in industry or in academia.
  • Applying knowledge of gas chromatography and liquid chromatography methods to deliver precise analytical results.
  • Collaborating with a quality-focused, proactive and curiosity mindset while working confidently across diverse teams and stakeholders.
  • Communicating fluently in French and English is a plus.
  • Bringing collaboration, fresh thinking, respect, and a positive, wellbeing-oriented mindset.
