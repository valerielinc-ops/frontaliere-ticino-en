More than Care Quality of Life Starts with You

Dipl. Physical therapist: from 60% across locations from January 2027 or by agreement We offer adult people in need of support a perspective in an environment where they feel secure and where they are cared for individually and professionally. This can only be achieved together. We have our own night team. So you only work from early to late. The nights take over others. With our two pool models, we create flexibility in operational planning and relieve permanent employees. So that free days really remain free. We trust you. You can actively contribute your knowledge and take on daily responsibility. For questions, a professional contact person in the house will help. Discover all the benefits

Your place of work

400 residents live at three locations and around 700 employees work for Solina. More about the Foundation

How you work

  • You plan and carry out the therapy process (e.g. individual therapy, MTT) independently at the Spiez and Steffisburg locations
  • You take over administrative activities (service and patient documentation)
  • You work purposefully in an interdisciplinary team and maintain a close exchange with other specialist areas for a holistic therapy.
  • You support us in the design and expansion of the therapy offer with your ideas

What makes you

  • You have completed training as a physiotherapist (foreign diplomas only in the SRC recognition procedure)
  • Experience in long-term care and care of neurological patients: females of advantage
  • In your work, you attach importance to “evidence based therapy”
  • An empathetic approach to people and a high level of social competence are a matter of course for you
  • Our values appeal to you and you are motivated to actively live them in everyday work

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
