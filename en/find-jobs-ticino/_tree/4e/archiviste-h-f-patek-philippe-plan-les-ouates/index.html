Full description

Mission

As part of this function, attached to our Commercial and Marketing Division, you participate in the organizational and operational support activities in the management of the Patek Philippe Historical Archives.

Responsibilities

  • Current Management and Conservation of Historical Archives.
  • Management of payments and research on applications.
  • Sorting and repackaging archives.
  • Migration and maintenance of archival databases.
  • Coordination of digitization.
  • Processing requests for Archives Extracts and Relative Correspondence.
  • Processing of flight announcements.

Profile sought

  • Bachelor of Science HES in documentary information, or equivalent experience.
  • Minimum 3 years experience in a public, private, or equivalent archive environment.
  • Language skills in French and English, other languages an asset.
  • Excellent writing skills in French and English.
  • Mastery of office tools (MS-Office).
  • Knowledge of a digital archive management tool.
  • Experience in the preservation of historical documents and images.
  • Aisance in a digital environment.
  • Rigor and precision, analytical capability and organizational sense.
  • Autonomy and good team spirit.
