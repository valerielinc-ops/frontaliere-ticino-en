About the Solothurner Spitäler AG

Solothurn Spitäler AG (soH) runs three somatic hospitals (the Cantonal Hospital Olten, the Bürgerspital Solothurn and the Hospital Dornach) and the psychiatric services Solothurn. It also offers decentralised services in the canton.

In addition to the task of providing extended basic medical care for the population, the hospitals also have different focus areas. The credo applies: medically top, humanly close.
