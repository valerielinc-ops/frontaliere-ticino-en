Who are we?

The Friburg detention facility brings together prison structures in the canton of Fribourg. It has a status under public law and has legal personality. It is administratively attached to the Directorate of Security, Justice and Sport, under the high supervision of the Council of State. The institution shall ensure the custody, accommodation, care and treatment of prisoners entrusted to it and shall contribute to the achievement of the objectives assigned to the execution of custodial sentences and measures.
