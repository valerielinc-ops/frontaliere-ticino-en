Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock. Care with heart, mind and flexibility – welcome to the Emmental Hospital. What makes us special? As a versatile peripheral hospital with heart and mind, we live a trusting coexistence and a working environment that is characterized by openness, respect and appreciation. Working modernly for us means: acting humanely, with freedom of design and an open ear for each other. Our employees are in the middle of us instead of just there – both professionally and personally. Family-friendly conditions and jointly designed service planning are part of everyday life for us

For our interdisciplinary emergency department in Burgdorf and Langnau we search immediately or by agreement:e

Your tasks – responsible and versatile

  • You support our patients and their relatives in acute situations competently, sensitively and professionally
  • You take over the triage as well as the holistic care in the emergency department
  • Together with the medical team, you will design the initial care of our acute patients
  • You coordinate the further treatment in the interdisciplinary team
  • You work in 24/7 shift operation – Flexibility is a matter of course for you

Your profile

  • You have a diploma in care HF / FH (or equivalent foreign diploma with recognition in Switzerland)
  • You bring experience in acute care (especially for caregivers without NDS in emergency care absolutely necessary)
  • You are already recognized dipl. Expert/dipl. Expert Emergency Care or, if interested, have the opportunity to complete the postgraduate course in Emergency Care
  • You meet people with respect and empathy, because the well-being of patients comes first for you
  • You enjoy working in a team, are resilient and responsible
