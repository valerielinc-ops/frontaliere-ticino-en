Appointment authority / department / institution

The Office for Vocational Counselling and Adult Education (BEA) helps young people and adults to choose their careers and to plan their careers. It informs them about the existing offers and advises them in their choice.

Become part of our team!

The BEA is at the disposal of the population of the canton for all questions related to career choice and professional development. It operates in a dynamic professional environment that is constantly evolving.

Their tasks

  • Post-compulsory education and career
  • They accompany and support persons after completion of compulsory school, including learners in secondary and tertiary education, as well as adults seeking advice on their professional projects, training projects and careers;
  • They inform and advise these target groups in their decisions
  • They analyze and evaluate the employability of individuals
  • They conduct workshops and information events for different target groups

Desired profile

  • Master in Psychology of Career Counseling and Human Resources Management or another recognised degree in the field of professional and career counselling or the willingness to complete this training part-time (not funded by the Office)
  • Sociability and ability to speak in front of an audience
  • Self-employment and good organisational capacity
  • Ability to deal with stress and change
  • Mastery of German with knowledge of French
