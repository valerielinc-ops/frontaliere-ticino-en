As part of our department Sigma Temporaire Industrie \& Technique, we are pleased to collaborate with several recognized companies in Valais, active in the fields of industry, production, energy and technical installations. To meet their needs, we are actively looking for a:

Industrial Electrician CFC (M/F)

Your responsibilities

  • Reading and interpreting electrical diagrams, wiring plans and technical folders
  • Construction of industrial electrical installations on machinery and production equipment
  • Installation and wiring of electrical cabinets, control panels and industrial equipment
  • Drawing and connection of power cables, control and instrumentation
  • Installation commissioning, testing and control
  • Diagnosis of electrical breakdowns and troubleshooting
  • Preventive and corrective maintenance of production equipment
  • Participation in continuous improvement and optimization of facilities
  • Collaboration with maintenance, production and technical services teams
  • Strict compliance with existing safety standards, quality procedures and requirements

Your profile:

  • CFC of Industrial Electrician, Automatician, Installation Electrician or equivalent
  • Experience in industry
  • Very good control of reading electrical diagrams
  • Experience in industrial wiring and troubleshooting
  • Knowledge of automated production equipment, an asset
  • nacelle permit desired
  • Good analytical and diagnostic capacity
  • Independent, rigorous and conscientious person
  • Team spirit and sense of responsibility
  • Driver's licence and vehicle, an asset

What we offer

Active collaboration with a dynamic team with strong technical know-how and recognized skills.

Absolute discretion guaranteed, with immediate or suitable start-up.

Attractive remuneration, in line with your skills and experience.

Possibilities for continuing training, in order to develop your knowledge and develop professionally.

Personalized support throughout your mission with a dedicated contact.

Access to a network of recognized companies offering varied opportunities in industry, energy and technology.

Perspectives of evolution, with real possibilities of fixed engagement in our partner clients.

Simplified recruitment process, allowing rapid and effective integration.
