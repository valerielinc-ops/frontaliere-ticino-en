Business description

dasteam ag is one of the nationally leading job placements with a total of 28 branches in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. For more than 35 years, we have specialized in the placement and rental of personnel in various industries, mainly in the areas of main and secondary construction, crafts, technology, industry.

Our customer is a modern and innovative construction company from the Upper Valais region, which stands for quality, exciting construction projects and a strong team.

As a long-standing partner of dasteam ag Visp, the company relies on reliable cooperation, mutual respect and a working environment in which experience, skill and commitment are valued. Here, professionals work who tackle, take responsibility and make a difference together.

Employees benefit from long-term assignments, very good working conditions and varied projects – from classical building construction to demanding specialised work.

For crane operators (cat. B) and other construction specialists, the company offers versatile missions, a motivated team and the opportunity to actively participate in future-oriented construction projects.

Those who work here become part of a dynamic team that builds with passion, sticks together and works every day to make strong projects a reality.
