Customer Manager / Customer Manager Self\-Checkout 20\-40 %

Introduction

You enjoy direct customer contact and want to help shape modern technologies in the retail trade? Do you want to do your best every day to offer our customers the best possible shopping experience? Do you want to be part of a team that is needed every day? Then you are exactly right with us. Exciting and versatile career opportunities await you in the dynamic retail environment.

Your tasks

  • Welcome and advice of customers in the cash register sector
  • Address and motivation of customers to use the Self\-Service offers
  • Support during the scanning and payment process at the self-checkouts
  • Assistance with technical or operating issues
  • Ensuring a proper and promotional appearance in the self\-checkout\ area
  • Performance of quality controls
  • Cash activity at regular cash registers

Your profile

  • Experience in sales or customer support is beneficial
  • Flexibility regarding the operating times (MO\-SA between 05:00\-22:00 ; \- and/or late shift)
  • Enjoyment of direct customer contact and advice
  • Basic technical understanding for POS systems
  • Patience and sensitivity
  • Strong communication and problem-solving skills
  • Resilience and flexibility, even with high customer volumes
  • Good knowledge of German, other languages beneficial

We offer

We prepare you for your varied and interesting tasks with an intensive training. If desired and good personal suitability, internal further developments are possible. In addition, we offer you the security of a permanent job, a great team, the advantages of the Lidl\-GAV, an excellent occupational provision and other attractive benefits.
