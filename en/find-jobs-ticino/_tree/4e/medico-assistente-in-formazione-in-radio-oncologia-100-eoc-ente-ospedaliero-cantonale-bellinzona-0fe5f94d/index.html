Assistant Doctor in Radio Oncology - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of more than 41,000 patients per year and providing more than 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team at the Oncology Radio Clinic of the Oncological Institute of Italian Switzerland, we look for an assistant doctor in formation in Radio Oncology (100%) Profile: The person we are looking for wishes to begin/ pursue medical training in radio-oncological discipline, has motivation, own initiative and spirit of collaboration. He believes, like us, in the importance of putting the patient and his family at the center of all our attentions.

Comprehensive application of presentation letter, curriculum vitae, photocopies of certificates of study and work and photography, must reach us by 30.06.2026.
