Assembly employees Commercial vehicles (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to an extensive bus model program, HESS projects, builds and sells commercial vehicle bodies and conversions of all kinds.

To complement our team, we are looking for an independent, skilled assembly employee in the production of commercial vehicles at the Subingen site. Are you fascinated by vehicles and would you like to use your know-how in the production of commercial vehicles? With us, you have the opportunity to work in vehicle construction and find the end product of your work later on the road. Their tasks

  • Self-propelled operation of a wide variety of structures, conversions and superstructures on commercial vehicles of all kinds
  • Manufacture of sub-assemblies and components by constructional drawing
  • Execution of varied assembly and locksmith work

Our requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship as a vehicle locksmith (metal builder, plant or apparatus builder) or many years of experience in assembly
  • Self-employed work
  • Flexibility
  • Teamwork

We offer them

  • Varied and interesting permanent employment
  • Moving working time
  • Performance-based conditions of employment
