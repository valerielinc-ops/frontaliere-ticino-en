About us

We are a central service laboratory for researchers in the field of life sciences at the University of Bern. We process all possible samples containing proteins and analyze them using nanoLC-MS/MS technology on state-of-the-art mass spectrometry devices. Our laboratory is located in a new building – centrally located between the Inselspital and Länggasse as well as in the immediate vicinity of the main railway station in Bern.
