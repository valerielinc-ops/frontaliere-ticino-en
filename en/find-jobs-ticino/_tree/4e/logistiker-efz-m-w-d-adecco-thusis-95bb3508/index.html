Full description

Introduction

For a customer from the logistics sector, we are looking for a logistics operator (m/f/d) at the earliest possible time. In this position, you support the operational processes along the movement of goods, take on organizational tasks in the storage and shipping environment and ensure a smooth flow of materials. It is used by a company that works with clear processes, structured cooperation and reliable processes. Through the personnel service provider, you have an experienced contact person from the HR area at your disposal, who accompanies you during the entire application and assignment process. In addition, there is a takeover option by the customer, provided that the cooperation is mutually convincing. The position is aimed at people who want to work responsibly, carefully and practice-oriented in the field of logistics.

Tasks

  • They shall take over the acceptance, control and proper placing in storage of goods in the storage area.
  • They assist in picking, packing and making shipments available for dispatch.
  • They ensure a proper flow of goods between warehouse, production and shipping.
  • They check stocks, document movements and work carefully with the storage documents.
  • They maintain order and cleanliness in the work area and observe the operational requirements.

Profile

  • Ideally, they have experience in logistics, warehouse or shipping.
  • They work reliably, structured and keep an overview even for several tasks.
  • They bring physical resilience as well as a careful and accurate work behavior.
  • They are capable of teamwork and communicate clearly and objectively in everyday work.
  • They are willing to familiarize themselves with operational processes and take on responsibility.

Advantages

  • Takeover option by the customer with appropriate cooperation and positive development.
  • Assistance by an experienced HR service expert throughout the assignment.
