Full description

Is technology your passion? With us you do not expect a rigid everyday life, but a varied task around our modern logistics and production facilities. If you want to contribute your technical know-how to a collegial team and actively shape the future of our company, we should get to know each other. We look forward to your application or call!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, Gossau headquarters, Industriestrasse 47, 9200 Gossau

Mansioni

  • Keep systems fit: You take over the maintenance and troubleshooting of our modern logistics and meat processing machines so that everything runs smoothly.
  • Solution finders are: If disturbances occur, keep a cool head, find the cause and quickly fix the problem.
  • Improve processes: You immediately see where something could run smoother? Perfect – you recognize optimization potential and implement technical improvements directly.
  • Co-create new things: When commissioning new plants, you are right from the start.
  • Organize: You keep the documentation clean and keep a close eye on our spare parts warehouse.

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in the maintenance of technical equipment
  • Completed initial or continuing education: as a mechanic, electrician or in a related field
  • You are interested in technical tasks, such as the construction of self-designs or the individual production of spare parts.
  • You like to use the opportunity to continuously develop and learn new skills.
  • You appreciate the fact that your assignment is mostly in day shifts and are also flexible (approximately every 6 - 8 weeks piquet or assignment on Saturday morning).
  • You love technical challenges and like to build your own constructions or produce spare parts individually.
  • You communicate easily in German and like to get involved and engaged.
  • German (good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Fair wages: Attractive employment conditions and certified equal pay
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Individual further education: Broad spectrum of professional and personal education and training opportunities
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Sports and fitness offers: discount on subscriptions to leisure and fitness facilities
