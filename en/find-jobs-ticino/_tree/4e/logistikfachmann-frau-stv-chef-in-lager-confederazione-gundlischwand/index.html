Full description

In a nutshell

Are you passionate about logistics in a unique environment? The Swiss Armed Forces Logistics Base offers you the opportunity through this role to ensure the smooth operation of the receipt, storage, dispatch and delivery, as well as the handover, of military material at the Oberland warehouse. An exciting challenge awaits you, along with the opportunity to contribute and further develop your expertise.

  • Start date: by agreement
  • Employment type: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-19892
  • Work location: Hinter der Egg 123c, 3815 Gündlischwand

This is how you can contribute

  • Ensure that the Head of the Oberland Warehouse is represented in all matters during her absence
  • Plan and coordinate schedules and work processes in materials management
  • Independently handle diverse assignments in the area of replenishment (a large part of the work is carried out underground)
  • Ensure compliant warehouse management on site, as well as the planning and execution of inventories in all warehouses
  • Ensure the quality of stored goods

What makes you unique

  • Completed vocational training as a logistics specialist, with further training as a logistics professional
  • Several years of experience in logistics and management experience desirable
  • Good organizational skills and an efficient, conscientious way of working
  • Category B driving licence and SUVA-recognized forklift training required; a category C driving licence is an advantage and must be obtained if required
  • Very good MS Office and SAP skills
  • English at B1 level is an advantage
  • Willingness to undergo training in coercive measures and weapons is required

What we offer

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Diversity in practice Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and contribute different perspectives.
  • Health in the workplace We support and advise our employees on matters of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Logistics Base of the Swiss Armed Forces (LBA) is there for the troops. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss Armed Forces and, with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12'000 militia personnel, manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss Armed Forces. Whether in the workshop or in the office, indoors or outdoors: the LBA offers a wide range of jobs throughout Switzerland. There is something for everyone here.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ over 9'000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions—and at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. There are also opportunities for young people: the Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in over 30 professions.

Additional information

Employment with the Swiss Armed Forces is meaningful and comes with many benefits. Find out for yourself at armee.ch/vorteile.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. We therefore particularly welcome your applications.
