Customer consulting – Sales Workload: 100% Location: Experience level: Entry-level FlexOffice: On-site Would you like to launch your career in sales after completing your vocational training or degree? Over 18 months, the Sales Excellence Program will specifically prepare you for your future role as a customer advisor. You will get to know the insurance industry from the ground up, develop your consulting and sales skills, and be supported by experienced mentors. Step by step, you will take on more responsibility, build your own customer base, and lay the foundation for a successful career in sales.

What you achieve

  • During the Sales Excellence Program, you learn step by step everything you need for a successful career as a customer advisor.
  • You advise customers by phone and in person and gain valuable practical experience in sales.
  • You acquire new customers and gradually build up your own customer base.
  • You support market development in your region and schedule consultation appointments.
  • You work together with experienced colleagues and benefit from their knowledge and experience.
  • You will be involved in sales and marketing activities and get to know different sales channels.
  • You will complete the comprehensive basic training, including the VBV certification financed by us, and continuously expand your specialist knowledge as well as your consulting and sales skills.

What you bring to the table

  • Completed training (apprenticeship, baccalaureate or university)
  • Enjoyment of dealing with people and enthusiasm for advice and sales
  • Strong communication skills, empathy and a confident appearance
  • Initiative, willingness to learn and the desire to develop yourself personally and professionally
  • Goal-oriented and independent way of working
  • Stylistically confident written and spoken German skills
  • Category B driving licence
  • Residence in the region or the willingness to build a strong network there
