You support various internal bodies, coordinate administrative processes and ensure that everything runs smoothly in day-to-day business. Your tasks

  • Offering: support for the calculation, recording and processing of offers, mottos and orders as well as tracking of open offers
  • Construction administration: preparation of work contracts, preparation of order confirmations, construction guarantees and final invoices including enclosures
  • Accounts receivable: collection, control and accounting of invoices and occasional control of receipts of payments and dunning support
  • Project administration: Opening and maintenance of projects and works contracts in the ERP system
  • Submissions: Processing tenders and requests via platforms such as Olmero, SIMAP and other acquisition channels
  • Administration: Maintenance of master data, reference lists and order overviews as well as responsibility for filing and archiving
  • Secretariat & Reception: Support of the telephone center and support in the versatile commercial day-to-day business

What you bring

  • Completed basic commercial training and several years of professional experience in a comparable function
  • Ideally, experience in construction, main or secondary construction or in a company close to construction
  • Practical experience in offering, order processing and/or debtor accounting
  • Understanding of commercial processes around construction projects, work contracts and invoicing
  • Very good knowledge of German and a safe handling of MS Office
  • Good IT user knowledge, ideally experience with ABACUS and digital tendering platforms
  • An exact, structured and independent working method with a good sense of priorities
  • You are communicative, service-oriented, resilient and like to tackle where support is needed

Particularly welcome: Personalities who have already gained experience in offering or construction administration and consciously want a versatile all-rounder function. What to expect

  • A varied and responsible position with a wide range of commercial responsibilities

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Aargau?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Aargau's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
