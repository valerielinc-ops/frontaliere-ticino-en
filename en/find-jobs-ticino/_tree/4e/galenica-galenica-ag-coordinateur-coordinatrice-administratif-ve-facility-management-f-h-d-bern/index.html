Full description

Workplace: Galenica AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

Do you like keeping an overview and making progress when our pharmacies need technical support? A real point of coordination, you ensure the link between our internal specialists, our external providers and our pharmacies. You contribute to the smooth running of administrative interventions and processes in a dynamic and bilingual environment.

Your field of action

  • You process the technical requests of our pharmacies and follow them up until they are resolved.
  • You plan and coordinate the interventions of our technical Facility Management specialists to ensure efficient use of resources.
  • You coordinate the interventions of external providers and ensure that they are carried out on time and as required.
  • You ask for offers from suppliers, prepare investment requests and check invoices as part of administrative processing.
  • You provide administrative management of maintenance contracts and coordinate with our external partners the cleaning and waste management of our pharmacies.
  • You keep up to date the documentation of the technical facilities as well as the lists and documents necessary to monitor the activities.

Your assets

  • You have completed training as a pharmacy assistant or as a commercial employee.
  • You have a very good command of German in writing and oral (C1/C2) and good knowledge of French in oral and written (B2/C1).
  • You easily use Word, Excel and Outlook and are comfortable with administrative processes.
  • You can organize several tasks in parallel, define priorities and keep an overview, even when the pace accelerates.
  • You communicate in a service-oriented way and appreciate the collaboration with various internal and external interlocutors.
  • You work autonomously and in a structured manner, assume your responsibilities and ensure reliable follow-up of your files.

We offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation, with the possibility of buying up to 10 additional days of vacation or taking leave without pay
  • Pension fund with advantageous distribution – we cover almost 2/3 of the premiums
  • Miscellaneous discounts in our pharmacies and other major brands
  • Opportunity to expand its skills through encouragement of training and development measures and opportunities for internal change
  • Taking into account the needs of families, with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of salary) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of salary), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Nominal actions at preferential prices – not only engage professionally for Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly by department/position

About us

The strongest network in the Swiss health market. Galenica is the leading provider of health care in our country. The Galenica network offers workplaces for a wide variety of future-oriented occupations that have one thing in common: employees who are committed to the health and well-being of people in Switzerland. We operate Switzerland's largest pharmacy network and offer well-known own brands as well as exclusive brands and products from business partners. In addition, we are the leading wholesaler in the health market and offer suppliers quality logistics with the corresponding services. The headquarters is in Bern, where all central functions are grouped. This is where we provide valuable services for the entire network, such as HR, IT, marketing, communication and finance.
