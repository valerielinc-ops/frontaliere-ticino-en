What awaits you.

In collaboration with your team colleagues, you develop and implement solutions to meet existing challenges. Your contribution allows teams to focus on their goals while ensuring the quality, rigour and fluidity of processes. You are evolving in a collaborative environment that values mutual support, information sharing and continuous improvement with concrete learning and development opportunities. Social benefits: To find out about benefits, such as free AG and reduced-cost overseas travel (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits What you can move.

  • Performs maintenance and renewal of contact lines and removals of disturbance.
  • Perform work triggering/engaging on contact line installations.
  • With proper training, drive construction trains, nacelle and cranes.
  • Take a picket service.
  • Have to work according to irregular schedules, especially at night and sometimes at weekends.

What you bring.

  • Learning with CFC is in an electrical field with experience, if possible, in the construction of contact lines, and/or on power grids with strong current. Either in a mechanical branch (precision mechanics, automobiles, locks...).
  • Experiences and knowledge of maintenance processes and operating conditions in the field of the contact line or high voltage are an asset.
  • High sense of responsibility, high sensitivity to security issues and readiness to attend in-house training.
  • Easy use of IT tools Good health and optimal hearing and visual abilities.
  • Home near the workplace (possibility of 30-45 minutes).
  • Driving licence B compulsory.
