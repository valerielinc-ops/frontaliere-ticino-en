Full description

The Civil Engineering Office is responsible for the planning, construction, maintenance and operation of the cantonal roads as well as the water construction and the slow traffic in Graubünden. We also take care of the operational maintenance of national roads in the cantonal area. The canton's road infrastructure comprises over 1'450 art buildings, including bridges in airy heights up to 90 meters and tunnels with a length of up to 3 kilometres. The challenges that the nature of our mountain canton brings with it in summer and winter make our work challenging and versatile. As a Friendly Work Space certified office, we are particularly interested in the health and satisfaction of our around 480 employees. For this reason, we are committed to a systematic occupational health management (BGM).

  • Technical responsibility for new construction, development and repair projects on the main road network in the entire canton
  • responsibility for the expert, on-time and cost-oriented project processing as well as the monitoring of compliance with the quality requirements
  • superstructure management for all projects on the main roads of the canton
  • independent management of demanding road and civil engineering projects
