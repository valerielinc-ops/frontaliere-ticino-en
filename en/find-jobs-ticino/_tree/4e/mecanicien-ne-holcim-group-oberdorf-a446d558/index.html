Full description

You are tech-savvy, find a suitable solution for every problem and like to tackle yourself? Then become part of our team and ensure that our plants and machines run reliably and safely.

What to expect

  • Repair, maintenance, inspection and repair of our production facilities, machinery and equipment
  • Carrying out periodic checks at installations, handling stations and worksites
  • Production and assembly of spare components and procurement of required spare parts
  • Implementation of welding and cutting firing work
  • Support for standstills and revisions in the gravel and concrete works in the region
  • Cooperation in the continuous optimization of plants, machines and workflows
  • Documentation of the maintenance work carried out and support in the planning of annual audits
  • Independent implementation of small to medium-sized projects as well as support for general plant maintenance work

What you bring

  • Completed technical vocational training in mechanical engineering
  • Professional experience in repair, maintenance, steel or plant construction
  • In-depth knowledge of mechanics and welding technology as well as basic knowledge of electrics and pneumatics
  • Technical understanding and knowledge in structural engineering and rapport
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method with a high sense of safety and responsibility
  • Solution-oriented thinking, initiative and willingness to take responsibility
  • Team spirit, helpfulness and good communication skills
  • Good IT user knowledge and openness to new tasks and technologies
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, basic knowledge of English is advantageous

  • Attractive compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional benefits and a very good pension fund
