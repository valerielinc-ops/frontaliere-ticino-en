Full description

The EMIL EGGER Group is the Swiss leader in heavy goods logistics. It employs more than 230 employees in the areas of gruting, special transport, industrial relocations and leasing of lift platforms. Thanks to its extensive specialized expertise, it develops customized and innovative solutions for its customers every day. For our Avenches site, we are looking for a flexible and team-minded person to strengthen the exceptional transport support team. You like to be on the road, you know how to take responsibility and you don't step back from any challenge? Then join us! Your missions

  • You accompany exceptional transports throughout Switzerland: as a replacement for the police, you are a guarantee of serenity on the road
  • When necessary, use your driver/driver skills in the field of heavy goods logistics
  • Each intervention brings new challenges

You bring

  • Several years of experience as a driver/driver in specialized transport
  • Category C/E driving licence
  • ATB training or motivation to take this training
  • German with knowledge in French or French with knowledge in German -> essential
  • Autonomous, reliable and responsible

We propose

  • State-of-the-art vehicles and equipment that make driving enjoyable
  • Fair conditions, attractive social benefits and free parking

  • Flexible entry date – to be agreed
