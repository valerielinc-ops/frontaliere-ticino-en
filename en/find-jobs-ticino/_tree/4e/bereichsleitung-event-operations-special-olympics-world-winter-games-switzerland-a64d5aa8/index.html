Area management (a) Event operations (infrastructure, technology, logistics)

We are looking for you!

Is the human being at the center for you? Are you an experienced leader looking for something different? Do you have event blood in your veins? Then you are right with us.

In 2029, Switzerland will host the Special Olympics World Games. These take place every four years and, with around 2,500 participants, are the second largest multisport winter event worldwide. In nine sports, people with cognitive impairment compete for 9 days at three locations in Graubünden.

For the World Games Switzerland 2029, a non-profit association based in Chur was founded, which is responsible for the implementation of the National Games 2028 and the World Games 2029. For success, we are currently looking for the right person for the following job:

Area management (a) Event Operations 80\-100 % Guest services \- Transport, accommodation, food (01\.02\.2027 or by agreement limited to 30\.06\.2029\)

Location: Chur / Zurich / Hybrid

Your tasks

+ Overall responsibility for infrastructure, technology and logistics at all game and event locations of the Games. + Responsibility for the professional implementation and the standard in all places, together with the Venue\ managers. + Responsibility for construction, electricity, screens, broadcasting and material – planning, tendering, implementation. + Control of the involved trades and service providers. + Budget responsibility (budget lead) for the department over all locations in coordination with the Domain Guardian. + Interfaces and orders for other domains, reporting to Domain Guardian and controlling.

Your profile

+ Completed subject-relevant training (study, HF/FH) or comparable professional experience. + Several years of proven senior management experience in infrastructure, technology or event logistics at major events (must). + Experience with high budget responsibility and complex procurement. + Existing network in the area of event infrastructure (infrastructure, technology/IT, construction, materials). + Broad technical understanding of several trades (construction, power, broadcasting, material). + Experience in planning and implementation at several locations simultaneously. + Secure handling of budgets, tenders and service provider management. + High assertiveness coupled with strong social skills and team skills. + High commitment and a clear commitment to inclusion and diversity. + High load capacity and hands\-on\ mentality in the implementation phase. + very good knowledge of German (must), good knowledge of English

  • Other languages are beneficial.
