Full description

The LEB (Lausanne–Echallens–Bercher) is a key player in the mobility of Gros-de-Vaud, which links the countryside and the city in a sustainable way and is evolving at the pace of a developing region.

At the dawn of a new stage with integration in Movodis as early as January 2027, new perspectives opened up: ambitious projects, a changing environment and many opportunities for change. We are looking for talents ready to engage in this adventure, within a human, demanding and moving enterprise.

Want to move the lines with us?

Become our future

Multidisciplinary project leader - LEB

Your Mission

  • You are piloting multidisciplinary projects to ensure the availability and development of rail infrastructure and LEB buildings, within the deadlines, budgets and quality requirements. In this context, your projects concern both existing infrastructure and new achievements.
  • You conduct projects under your responsibility, from preliminary study to commissioning.
  • You supervise the technical studies and the operational monitoring of the yards, while coordinating the internal and external partners involved.

Your profile

  • You have a degree in engineering in a technical field, ideally in civil engineering, electrical engineering or equivalent.
  • You justify more than five years of experience in managing transversal projects related to building, railway infrastructure or electrical safety installations.
  • A good knowledge of the technical characteristics of railway construction is an asset.
  • Your listening ability and clear communication enable you to work effectively with all stakeholders.
  • You appreciate teamwork while being able to carry out your missions independently and reliably.
  • Your analytical and synthesis capabilities allow you to set priorities, meet deadlines, and be a powerful proposition.
