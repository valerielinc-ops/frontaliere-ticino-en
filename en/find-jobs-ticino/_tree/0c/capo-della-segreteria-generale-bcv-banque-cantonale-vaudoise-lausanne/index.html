Announcement The BCV General Secretariat provides operational and organizational support to the Branch. It facilitates coordination between the various internal entities and the Directorate, ensuring rigorous, confidential and effective management of the Branch's information and decisions. Following the retirement announcement of the current incumbent, we are looking for one or one: Head of the General Secretariat of the BCV Your main tasks

  • Steering and coordinating weekly meetings of the Directorate General: preparation of meetings, organization, verbatim reporting, transmission of decisions, retention of documents
  • Ensuring a fluid and structured relationship between the Directorate-General and the Bank's departments and with subsidiaries
  • To be the internal entry point of FINMA, the Ombudsman and the band's umbrella organizations; coordinating the professional relationship
  • Oversee the Branch secretariat team: human resources, organization, communication and data security, among others
  • Assist the CEO in ad hoc missions, follow up on specific files
  • Acting as an alternate or alternate to the Secretary of the Board of Directors, in his absence Your profile
  • University degree level Master, in Economics, Law or any other field related to the function
  • Professional experience in the banking or financial sector in Switzerland, in a similar role and/or as Project Manager, Project Management Officer (PMO) or Administrative Manager
  • Analytical spirit, high administrative rigour
  • Relationship, diplomacy and discretion
  • Mastery of French (spoken and written), excellent editorial skills
  • Very good knowledge in German and English desired, Italian an asset

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
