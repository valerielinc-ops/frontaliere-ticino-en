Full description

Motivated by an apprenticeship as a production engineer? Ready to become a reference in your profession through quality training in a leading company? Join us!

The production mechanic manufactures watch movement parts using traditional and digitally controlled machines. He/she interprets technical drawings, prepares tools and materials necessary for the manufacture of parts. In addition, this person is responsible for the adjustment and control of the machines and for the quality of the parts produced.

This apprenticeship is suitable for young people who are interested in metalworking, industrial machinery and who love precision.

Profile

  • A common sense of space representation
  • A logical and methodical mind
  • Manual ability and precision

To apply

If you are motivated to meet the challenge, do not hesitate to apply. Your folder must contain:

  • a curriculum vitae
  • a cover letter
  • your school reports for the last three years.

Boxed by experienced trainers, you can evolve in an extraordinary working environment. This training will also provide you with excellent prospects. Rolex is committed to offering you more than just learning: a real opportunity for personal and professional development.
