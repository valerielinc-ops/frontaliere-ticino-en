Electrical assembler 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Electrical assembler Le Châtelard (Valais), 01.01.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

For the Châtelard hydropower plant in Valais: an innovative working environment awaits you. In collaboration with your team colleagues, you develop and implement solutions to meet existing challenges. This position also includes picket services. Social benefits: To find out about benefits, such as free AG and reduced-cost overseas travel (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits What you can move.

  • As an electrician, you are responsible for maintenance and technical maintenance in our internal and external installations, according to the prescribed standards.
  • In this way, you guarantee a low-perturbation operation in collaboration with the control plant in Zollikofen.
  • After the training period in the installation technique you will get authorizations for electrical maneuvers as well as grounding and you will work independently.

What you bring.

  • You have a CFC of electrician assembler (or equivalent training) and benefit from good professional experience, ideally in the maintenance of energy or industrial fields.
  • Driving licence B.
  • You speak fluently in French or German and have good knowledge of the other language.
  • You are willing to do picket service and sometimes work on shifts (irregular schedules).
  • Ability to work independently or as a team, initiative and responsibility
  • Basic computer skills (MS Office).

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the E level and is eligible for a level 0 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits .
