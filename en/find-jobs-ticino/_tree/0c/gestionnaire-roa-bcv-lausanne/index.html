Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV IT? Put your skills and experience at the service of one of the few banks that develops, maintains and ensures the security of its own Information System. The endless and varied professional opportunities within the 3 IT departments offer you the opportunity to carry out strategic projects, which will contribute to the success of the entire bank. For our IT Departments, we are looking for one or more:

Announcement Manager ROA Your main mission – Application Managers Management:

  • be the point of contact for all activities and questions related to the Operational Managers of Application (ROA) and Technical Managers of Application (RTA),
  • monitor exchanges concerning the management of enforcement officials,
  • carry out updates of the application managers in the architecture repository and in the organisation of the Bank, in accordance with established rules (approval, assignment of function),
  • document and update missions related to the role of enforcement officials,
  • developing the process of welcoming a new enforcement officer to his or her post, following his or her appointment, and their training in general,
  • organise and manage the annual plenary session of enforcement officials. Support for the Architecture group:
  • Follow up on the architecture exchanges – monitoring and disaggregating the messages to the relevant interlocutors according to the topics.
  • Assisting architects in managing the architecture repository:
  • Targeted creation and updating of applications;
  • Support for the management of applications developed outside central IT (Shadow IT). Follow-up to the Federal Data Protection Act (DPA) Registry:
  • entering data categories, sensitive categories, data transfers, and categories of data subjects in the Privacy Management module of the architecture repository,
  • monitor the progress of the perimeter covered by the Privacy Management module with the Legal Service,
  • be able to train other users in the use of the above-mentioned module. Your profile:
  • meticulous and able to be persistent in following up your tasks, you know how to remain a diplomat in the face of interlocutors of different hierarchical levels,
  • previous banking experience, a plus,
  • Knowledge of the Privacy Management module of the Hopex platform, a plus.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let us write together three stories, one of your career, one of your bank, one of our region.
