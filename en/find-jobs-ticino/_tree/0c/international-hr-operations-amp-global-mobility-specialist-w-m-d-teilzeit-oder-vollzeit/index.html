Full description

Account management Pensum: 50% - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Create international careers and facilitate global cooperation Do you want to accompany people in cross-border career steps and make a visible contribution to an internationally networked company? Then you can expect an exciting role at the interface of HR operations, global mobility and international cooperation. As part of our Group HR team, you ensure that international employment, transfers and secondments are implemented professionally, efficiently and compliantly. You work with employees, managers and specialized partners on several continents and actively shape the international employee experience.

What you do

  • You will enable employees and managers to make successful international assignments and accompany them with transfers, secondments and cross-border employment.
  • You will be responsible for central international HR operations at our locations in Miami, Liechtenstein, Singapore and the UK, providing a seamless employee experience.
  • You advise executives and internal stakeholders as a trusted advisor on issues relating to global mobility, payroll, immigration and international employment models.
  • You control the cooperation with internal departments as well as external experts from Payroll, Tax, Immigration and Relocation and successfully bring different interests together.
  • You create transparency through meaningful reports and analyses and support informed decisions in an international environment.
  • You actively shape the further development of our international HR operations and mobility processes and drive forward modern, future-oriented solutions.

What you bring

  • Several years of experience in the international HR environment with a focus on HR Operations, Global Mobility or International Employment.
  • Passion for international cooperation and the joy of accompanying people at important career and life stages.
  • Strong consulting and stakeholder management competence as well as the ability to convey complex topics in an understandable and solution-oriented manner.
  • A proactive, structured personality with high quality standards, a sense of responsibility and discretion.
  • Interested in international HR topics such as payroll, immigration, compliance and cross-border employment.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English as well as digital affinity and pleasure in actively developing processes.
