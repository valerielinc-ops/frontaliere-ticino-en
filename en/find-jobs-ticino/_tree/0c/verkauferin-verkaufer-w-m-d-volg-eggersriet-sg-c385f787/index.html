About us

Volg is the specialist for village shops and small retail outlets in German-speaking and western Switzerland. We foster proximity to our customers – both as a nearby shopping opportunity and through personal contact. Employees are the heart of the shop, and we attach great importance to individuality and personality. That is why our motto is "frisch und fründlich".
