Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with around 1100 employees:innen. As engineers, planners and consultants, we work on all aspects of the technology of civil engineering and civil engineering over the entire life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and sustainable. These are your tasks

  • Independent management, development and support of the electric team
  • Technical and organisational management of subordinates
  • Planning of the employee(s): Inside assignment in the team (resource planning)
  • Independent processing of demanding projects from concept to implementation
  • Conceptual project development, testing of technical feasibility and implementation of innovative solutions
  • Advising customers on economic efficiency over the entire life cycle of a property
  • Close cooperation with the other departments of Amstein + Walthert
  • Participation in project competitions and in contract acquisition
  • Shared responsibility and active participation in the acquisition
  • Further development in management with the aim of divisional management
  • Co-creation of the services of A+W Basel and active team development
  • Higher level of technical training Specialisation Electrical and/or several years of experience as Head Electrical
  • At least 5 years of professional experience and experience leading interdisciplinary project teams and staff
  • Proven experience in the planning and implementation of technically demanding projects
  • In-depth experience in electrical planning
  • High social competence, secure appearance and pronounced customer orientation
  • Enthusiasm and pleasure in demanding work
  • Self-employed, reliable and dutiful character with purposeful working methods
  • Good knowledge of MS Office and planning tools (NPK, Steiger, etc.)
  • Experience and/or interest in CAD (3D planning in Revit)
  • German skills in word and writing
