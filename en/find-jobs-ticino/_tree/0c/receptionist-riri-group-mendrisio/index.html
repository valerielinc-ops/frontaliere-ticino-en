Receptionist

Oerlikon Riri, part of the international group Oerlikon and reference point in the luxury sector for the production of high quality metal accessories, search for a Receptionist for the headquarters of Mendrisio.

The main activities will be

• Management of the telephone station for the headquarters of Mendrisio and support to other locations; • Customer welcome; • Registration of inputs and outputs; • Organization of reception and meeting rooms; management and sorting of incoming and outgoing mail; • Search and book flights, taxis and hotels; • Wi-Fi access creation for guests; • Activation and distribution of badges; • Preparation and distribution of the daily plan of workers' absences and the weekly plan of executive attendance; • Read and sort business emails; • Screening of CVs received in the shared mail box dedicated to applications; • Collection of employee absences and daily update of the absence report; • HR support in the use of the Zucchetti program and in the management of the absence archive; • Logistics support: verification of the invoices of the couriers in the AS400 system.

The ideal resource has

  • High school diploma or equivalent qualification;
  • Comprehensive work experience as Receptionist, Attachment to Front Office or similar roles;
  • Excellent knowledge of the Microsoft Office package;
  • Ability to manage priorities and time management;
  • Excellent knowledge of English and French language, both written and spoken;
  • Preferential title is the knowledge of the German language;
  • Accuracy, reliability, flexibility and teamwork skills complete the profile, along with good relational and communication skills.

We offer

Indefinitely contracted PT to 60%; the RAL is expected to be between CHF 25,000 and CHF 27,000.

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in surface technologies and advanced materials. With a unique portfolio spanning surface engineering, high-performance materials, coating equipment and components, we make products better by enhancing performance, efficiency and sustainability. Oerlikon Serves a wide range of industries, including aerospace, automotive, energy, medical, luxury, semiconductors and tooling. Headquartered in Pfäffikon, Switzerland, Oerlikon operates in 38 countries with approximately 10,000 employees, achieving sales of CHF 1.6 in 2025.

We offer a wide variety of job profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and reach their full potential - guided by our core values of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.

About Oerlikon Luxury

Oerlikon Luxury specializes in producing accessories for the high-end fashion industry. It includes two brands: Oerlikon Riri, which produces branded Riri and Cobrax zippers and buttons respectively; and Oerlikon Fineparts, which offers metal components, fashion jewellery and surface treatments.

Oerlikon Luxury is headquartered in Mendrisio, Switzerland, counts 1100 employees at 11 sites located in Italy, Switzerland, France and Portugal as well as sales offices in New York, Paris and Shanghai.
