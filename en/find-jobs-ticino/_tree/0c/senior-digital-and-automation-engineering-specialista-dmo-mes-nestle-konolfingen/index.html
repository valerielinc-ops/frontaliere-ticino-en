Location: Konolfingen, Switzerland Work model: Hybrid / Full time Contract type / duration: Permanent

Be a force for good and help nurture a healthier generation

As a Senior Digital and Automation Engineering Specialist (DMO/MES) at Nestlé Nutrition & Health, your mission is to identify, develop and scale digital manufacturing and automation solutions that strengthen performance across the Nutrition & Health manufacturing network. At our R&D center in Konolfingen, Switzerland, we develop science-proven nutrition products, processes and packaging, while providing technical support to Nestlé Nutrition & Health factories worldwide. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with factory, engineering, quality, operations, research and development, digital and automation teams, technology partners and strategic suppliers. This is your opportunity to grow your expertise and deliver meaningful results that help improve lives through nutrition innovation worldwide.

How you’ll make an impact as a Senior Digital and Automation Engineering Specialist (DMO/ MES)

  • Leading the development, technical support and global deployment of digital manufacturing and automation solutions that improve reliability, efficiency and operational performance across factories.
  • Driving the standardization and continuous improvement of manufacturing execution systems, operational technology and automation solutions across Nutrition and Health sites.
  • Building strong partnerships with factories, project teams and technology suppliers to turn emerging digital technologies into scalable business value.
  • Delivering expert guidance for investment projects, from design and commissioning to qualification, validation and handover to operations.
  • Enabling secure, reliable and actionable manufacturing data through data integration and engineering initiatives that support digital transformation.
  • Coaching teams and sharing best practices that strengthen digital and automation capabilities across a global manufacturing community.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing strong expertise in digital technologies applied to manufacturing, automation, industrial software and operational technology, supported by a Master’s degree in Engineering with a background in digital technologies.
  • Applying strong experience in digital manufacturing solutions, AI and robotics, manufacturing execution systems (DMO/MES), automation and digital engineering within manufacturing or food industry environments.
  • Using solid knowledge of process mastery applications, control system platforms such as Rockwell, Wonderware or AVEVA, and process simulation tools to strengthen operational performance.
  • Collaborating across multidisciplinary teams to deliver engineering projects, commissioning, start-up and continuous improvement in manufacturing facilities, ideally within dairy processes.
  • Communicating with full professional proficiency in English and bringing the willingness and ability to travel internationally up to 30% of the time.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
