Full description

With your local knowledge, you skilfully maneuver the letter and parcel shipments across the streets and make our customers happy with the punctual delivery. With you, the post of tomorrow stays in motion!

You can do that -

  • Early in the morning (about 6 a.m.) you load the delivery vehicles together with your team and prepare your delivery tour.
  • Through your work you shape the streetscape of the (region/city) and embody the values of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the punctual and impeccable delivery of the parcels and letters in your area. --
  • On your tour you will answer questions from our customers and contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • You also work on Saturdays, with services split up in the team. --

You bring that with you

  • With your physical fitness, you can easily load and pick up packages up to 30 kg.
  • You like to travel, have the driver’s license of category B.
  • You appreciate the work in the team but you also like to travel independently and feel comfortable with large vehicles in road traffic. --
  • You are reliable and you work fast.
  • Good oral German skills as well as an experienced use of digital devices round off your profile.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed.
