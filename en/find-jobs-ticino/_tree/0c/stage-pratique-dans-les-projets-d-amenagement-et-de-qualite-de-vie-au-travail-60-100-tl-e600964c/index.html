Full description

We are committed to supporting you throughout your training to develop the knowledge necessary for your professional success.

Are you looking for an internship where your work will really make a difference?

Join a dynamic team that will give you the means to apply your skills while allowing you to bring your ideas. As an intern, you will contribute directly to the improvement of the quality of life at work of our more than 2,000 employees, while supporting the "collaborative service" team in its various projects: spatial planning, restoration, satisfaction survey and improvement of the quality of life at work. Your missions

Support for staff services

  • Ensure administrative follow-up and resolution of requests (tickets) related to workspaces.
  • Organize, monitor and optimize the allocation of lockers and locker rooms in the company.
  • Provide concrete solutions and recommendations to improve our services.

Small development projects

  • Participate in the improvement of the work and breaks available to employees.
  • Actively participate in various projects, with true freedom of initiative.
  • Work with our internal and external partners.
  • Manage administrative aspects and support project leaders in budget monitoring and planning.

Supporting projects that improve the quality of life at work

  • Perform quantitative and qualitative analyses: excel, dashboards, graphs to objectiveise the problems encountered.
  • Prepare estimates and summaries for the working groups and COPIL.
  • Contribute to the identification of action levers based on the analyses performed.

Your profile

  • Young graduate or student in a field related to business management, project management or innovation (HEG or similar).
  • Curious, enterprising, and with an excellent sense of service.
  • Ability to learn quickly, communicate easily and work as a team.
  • An affinity for spatial planning projects (offices, meeting rooms).
  • An affinity for concrete projects that improve the quality of life at work.
