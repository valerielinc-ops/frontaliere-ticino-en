Located in the heart of Lausanne and open to all the population, the Montchoisi Medical Centre has a team of multidisciplinary experts. An integral part of the Montchoisi Clinic, it benefits from first-rate facilities and medical know-how of the highest quality. The philosophy of the Montchoisi Medical Centre is to establish a multidisciplinary competence centre of excellence for the inhabitants of the Lausanne region. The Montchoisi Medical Centre also houses the Lausanne branch of the Neurocentre GSMN, Centre for Brain and Nervous System Diseases.
