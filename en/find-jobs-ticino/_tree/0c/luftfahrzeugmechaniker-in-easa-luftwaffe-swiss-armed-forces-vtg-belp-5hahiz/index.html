Put to the Point

As a military airfield of the Swiss Air Force, the Meiringen Aerodrome Command is responsible for the permanent operational readiness of the Luftwaffe from the military airfield of Meiringen and from the federal base of Bern-Belp. You can expect the latest technology, demanding tasks and an environment in which every handle counts. With precision, quality and commitment, you make a direct contribution to the operational readiness of the Luftwaffe – where it matters.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20913
  • Location: Aerodrome Road 5, 3123 Belp

You can make this contribution

  • Repair aircraft at Belp and, if necessary, support repair work at other aerodromes
  • Ensure daily flight operations for the benefit of the Luftwaffe (LW) and the Federal Office for Civil Aviation (FOCA) and provide piquetting services
  • Accompanying passenger and material transports as loadmasters
  • Maintain the air force’s infrastructure at Belp (Federal Base) and ensure proper maintenance

This makes them unique.

  • Technical apprenticeship and trained as certified aircraft mechanic EASA Part 66, category B1 and/or B2; or readiness to complete training in accordance with Part 66
  • Several years of professional experience in aviation
  • Driving licence Cat. C (lorry) with SDR/ADR identification
  • Readiness for missions abroad
  • Oral and written knowledge of a second official language and good knowledge of English

Your commitment to security and freedom The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are civil employees – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
