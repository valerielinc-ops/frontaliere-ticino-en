Full description

In order to strengthen the administrative team of the Ecole Supérieure Santé Valais-Wallis, ES Nursing in Monthey, the Haute Ecole de Santé is contesting the position of:

Administrative/administrative collaborator (60%)

Your Mission

  • Provide quality reception service
  • To take part in administrative tasks under the secretariat of the Ecole Supérieure Santé Valais-Wallis, section ES Nursing in Monthey
  • Actively participate in the organisation and functioning of the sector, according to the needs defined by the manager of the sector
  • Collaborate with the secretariat of the High School of Health and with central departments to ensure effective coordination of activities
  • Provide logistical and material support to teachers by facilitating the preparation and smooth conduct of educational activities

Your profile

  • Diploma or SWC of trade employee
  • Experience in written and oral communication and in contacts with staff and clients
  • Ability to work independently and in teams
  • Flexibility - initiative - sense of organization
  • MS-Office
  • French mother tongue with oral and written German knowledge

Activity rate: 60%

Working days : Wednesday-Thursday-Friday

Workplace : Monthey

Entry into office: 01.01.2027 or to be agreed

For further information, please contact Ms Jeanne-Emmanuelle Jollien-Héritier, responsible for the sector, tel. 058 606 88 17, jeanne-emmanuelle.jollien-heritier@hevs.ch.

We invite you to submit your complete application file (CV, cover letter, diplomas and work certificates) by clicking on the "Post" button until November 26, 2026. Only applications registered online will be taken into account.
