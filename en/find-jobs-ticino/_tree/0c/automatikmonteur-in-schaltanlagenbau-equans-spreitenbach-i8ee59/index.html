Full description

For our location in Spreitenbach we are looking for an automatic installer: in switchgear construction in 80-100% time.

You can move that with us.

  • Assembly and wiring of switchgear as well as controls according to diagram and given documents
  • Tasks in all areas of low-voltage distribution
  • Mechanical structures and equipment for building services
  • Supply and assembly of switchgear to customers
  • Processing of PVC covers as contact protection
  • Compliance with applicable standards and work regulations
  • Compliance with safety policies
  • Implementation of changes and additions to existing installations

That's what makes you.

  • You have successfully completed basic training as an automatic installer:in, automatic engineer:in EFZ or electrical installer:in EFZ
  • You have experience in switchgear construction
  • You are familiar with the Swiss Work Regulations
  • You are a reliable, team-capable and independent person with an exact working method
  • Your knowledge of German is impeccable and you have a driving license Cat. B

That's what we offer you.

  • Versatile field of activity with a lot of personal responsibility
  • Well-established and established team
  • Lively and challenging environment
  • Attractive social benefits
  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • A family working atmosphere, state-of-the-art work equipment and career opportunities within a large corporation
