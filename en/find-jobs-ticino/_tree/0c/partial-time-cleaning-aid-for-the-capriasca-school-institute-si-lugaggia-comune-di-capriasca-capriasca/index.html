Full description

Published at the albi from 18 November to 17 December 2026 Notice of contest for the recruitment of an auxiliary / part-time cleaning service for the school institute Capriasca, SI di Lugaggia The Town Hall of Capriasca opens the contest for the partial recruitment of an auxiliary / cleaning fee for the school institute Capriascalato (SI section of Lugaggia, 700 hours annual, equal to the regulations of the school. Intake conditions Appointment indefinitely in part time with a daily charge, a weekly one, to which the monthly and annual cleaning is added, according to the need indicated in the specifications. As regards cleaning at the SI headquarters in Lugaggia, the work takes place outside school hours. In the event of need, having heard the opinion of the manager, the City Hall has the ability to perform internal shifts between the various schools. During the summer, the auxiliary may be called/to make cleaning shifts at the facilities of the Sports Arena (pool, tennis court, football field, etc.).

Conditions of salary

The conditions of the ROCol, salary class 1 (minimum hours fr. 20.92 / maximum hours fr. 32.00) of the cantonal class, plus possible allowances for children. In accordance with the ROCol, the newly appointed employee perceives the minimum of the class. The Town Hall may derogate from this principle if the candidate has a valid professional experience gained in similar function. The assignment will take place according to the provisions of ROCol. Entry into service on 1 March 2027. For this contest, the application must be submitted by 12.00 on Thursday 17 December 2026, only online on the website of the Municipality on the page “Albo – competitions”. Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
