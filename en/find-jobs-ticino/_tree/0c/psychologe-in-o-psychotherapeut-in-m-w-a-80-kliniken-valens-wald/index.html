Full description

A vision for rehabilitation and my career – that is the feeling of life and work at the Valens clinic group. Exchange, progress, interdisciplinary teamwork and good prospects are being made here.

As a supplement to our team we are looking for a new one or two on 01.09.2026 or by agreement

Tasks: Your area of responsibility includes the psychological care and treatment of cardiovascular, pulmonary and oncological as well as neurological and musculoskeletal patients during their rehabilitation stay. This includes in particular the clarification and short-term psychotherapy of anxiety states, postoperative processing disorders and depression as well as crisis intervention. Another focus is the cooperation in the interdisciplinary smoking and stress management programs as well as their further development.

Requirements: You have a university or university degree in psychology and are in the last stages of psychotherapy training (clinical psychiatry years already completed) or have already completed it. You have great interest or, at best, practical experience in dealing with somatic diseases and/or rehabilitation medicine and you are characterized by interest in the best possible patient treatment, high social skills and independent work.

Teamwork and safe handling in administrative activities are required.
