Full description

Are you looking for secondary employment as a flexible help on Saturday? Come to our branch MMM Limmatplatz! We are looking for dedicated help for different tasks such as shelf filling, customer service, and cash register. If you're reliable and motivated, get involved and support our team!

Mansioni

  • You actively support us at the cashier and contribute to shaping a positive shopping experience for our customers
  • You serve our modern scanning cash system and ensure a smooth running at the cash register
  • You are responsible for the management of Subito stations
  • You inspire our customers through friendly and competent advice
  • If required, you can also support in other store areas

Requisiti

  • Experience in retailing: preferably in the food sector
  • You enter customer-oriented and friendly
  • You like to work independently and reliably
  • You are flexible and willing to work outside the usual working hours? Enjoy your free time when others work!
  • No training required
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa offriamo

  • Healthy work: Healthy offers such as massages and health checks
  • Mobility: With the unique leisure bonus per year of max. CHF 500.– we participate in your ÖV subscription
  • Mobile phone subscription: You can look forward to special conditions for the mobile phone subscription
  • Occupational pension: Excellent pension funds with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: Fair social benefits
  • Cumulus points: You get additional cumulus points when shopping
  • Migros Bank: preferential interest in private and investment account, free account management, discounted mortgages and more
  • Employee offers: Up to 1,000 CHF per year (depending on your pensum) for your recovery and further development – usable for fitness subscriptions, courses, health offers or a grant for the ÖV subscription.
  • Sports and fitness offers: You can enjoy a leisure bonus of max. CHF 1000.– e.g. for an annual subscription for the M-Fitnesscenter
  • education and training: support for vocational training and training
  • Contatto
