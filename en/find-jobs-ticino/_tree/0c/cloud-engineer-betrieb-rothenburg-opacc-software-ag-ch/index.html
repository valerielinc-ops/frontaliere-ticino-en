Homepage Cloud Engineer (operation) Rothenburg, Switzerland Who are you working with? As part of our Opacc Cloud Service Team, you automate and standardize our delivery of services in the Cloud Center. You work with our project managers, developers and system engineers. Your everyday life After a comprehensive familiarization at the campus in Rothenburg, you support the team in the following areas:

  • Support of our customer systems in the Opacc Cloud Center
  • Install and update our systems/applications in the Opacc Cloud Center
  • Monitoring of systems
  • Processing of incidents
  • Help develop our Opacc Cloud Center

Your background

To complete this task successfully, you bring the following

  • Sound technical training and practical experience in the IT environment
  • Excellent knowledge of Windows and Linux
  • Good knowledge of server infrastructure and network technologies
  • Enjoy contact with our Opacc Cloud customers
  • Enjoyment of working in a team and high problem-solving competence
  • Readiness for evening missions to keep our customer systems up-to-date
  • Collaboration in our 7x24 Pikett organization
  • Motivation to learn new and take responsibility

And of course we offer you

  • Flexible working hours, annual working hours
  • A top modern workplace.
  • Valuable training offers
