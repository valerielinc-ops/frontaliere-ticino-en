Here we are

The Limmatklinik is a modern clinic located in the emerging Zurich West with private sponsorship and admission to the Zurich hospital list. The range of operations includes plastic, ophthalmic and oral surgery, ORL, orthopaedics, general and vascular surgery. Do you want to work in a modern environment where teamwork, appreciation and a high quality of care are the focus? Then we look forward to meeting you! To strengthen our Limmat Care team, we will be looking for one from January 2027 or by appointment

Dipl. Nurse HF / Dipl. Care professional HF 60-90 %

Your mission with us

  • Comprehensive pre- and postoperative care and care of patients (adults and children from 3 years)
  • Implementation of the care process from entry to exit, taking into account patient resources
  • Collection, documentation and billing of care services
  • Responsibility for the professional and timely execution of medical prescriptions
  • Identifying crisis situations and taking appropriate action
  • Taking over the shift management of the station
  • Correct and economical use of care materials and equipment
  • Assistance in the training of new employees and support for learners, students and trainees

This is what you bring with you

  • Diploma as Dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH
  • Experience in the care and care of children as well as in cooperation within the documentary doctor model desirable
  • Empathetic personality with pedagogical and didactic skill
  • High reliability, sense of responsibility and resilience
  • Maintained appearance as well as very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Flexible commitment and team spirit

This is what we offer you

  • Working hours from Monday to Saturday as well as occasional Sunday services (about 7 per year) in 3-shift operation with company holidays over Christmas/New Year
  • Versatile and varied activity in a dynamic environment
  • Modern workplace with very good connection to public transport
  • An appreciative, committed team with real collaboration at eye level
