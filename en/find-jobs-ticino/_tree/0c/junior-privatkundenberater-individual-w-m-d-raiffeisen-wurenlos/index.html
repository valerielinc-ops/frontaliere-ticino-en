Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Würenlos serves customers between Baden and Zurich, in the Limmat Valley and the Furt Valley. Every day, our employees with empathy and professionalism find tailor-made solutions for different financial issues.

What awaits you?

  • You will accompany our customers with their financing requests regarding living and advise them holistically in the areas of assets, provision and security
  • You work closely with experienced consultants and continuously deepen your expertise
  • You develop tailor-made solutions that are tailored to the individual needs and life situations of our customers
  • You actively and sustainably manage your own customer base and recognize new potential
  • You gain new customers and further expand existing relationships
  • You support sales and marketing activities and actively contribute to their success
  • You represent our bank outside and maintain a strong local network

What do you bring?

  • You have completed your banking education and have gained at least three years of banking experience, ideally two years in customer service.
  • You have trained in banking or finance or would like to further develop in this area
  • You enjoy consulting, recognize customer needs and act sales-oriented
  • You work independently, reliably and accurately and appreciate cooperation in the team
  • You are characterized by commitment, flexibility and a high service orientation

Our values of sustainability, quality, passion, trust, respect, appreciation and clarity form the basis for our cooperation – in the team as well as with our customers. «You have successfully completed your banking education, already gained initial consulting experience and now want to take the next step in your career? » Then this position offers you the opportunity to take on responsibility, further expand your expertise and actively shape the future of our customers together with us. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed.
