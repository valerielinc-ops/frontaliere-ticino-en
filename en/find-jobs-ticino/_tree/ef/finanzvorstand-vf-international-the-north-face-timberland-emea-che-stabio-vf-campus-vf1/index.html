Full description

VF Corporation is looking for an exceptional Senior Financial Analyst to join our The North Face FP&A team, based in Stabio, Switzerland.

Let’s talk about the role!

As Senior FP&A Analyst, you will support The North Face FP&A activities across P&L Planning and Forecasting, with a primary focus on sales and margin, helping the team to assess channel performance and trends to maximize revenue and profitability.

You will gather Budget, Forecasts and Ops & Risks data, to load into our reporting tool (BPC), while delivering ad hoc reports, as requested by Senior Management.

To achieve these objectives, you will work closely with your FP&A colleagues, as well as with other functions, including Planning, Sales and DTC, while reporting directly to the FP&A Director.

Primary Accountabilities

You will be expected to deliver strong results across the following FP&A responsibilities:

  • Manage financial planning and forecasting tools, ensuring consistency and accuracy of figures submitted to Corporate
  • Ensure actuals and forecasts are accurately loaded into the system
  • Drive forecasting accuracy excellence, by proactively analysing and reporting critical trends and dynamics, mainly within WHS channels and major accounts
  • Maintain alignment between financial and commercial expectations, to ensure sales, margin and inventory targets are achieved
  • Support the SG&A COE to deliver accurate profit analysis
  • Ensure that all requests are delivered accurately and on time, including BPC loading, store roll, key account details, revenue walk, margin bridge, category overviews, etc.
  • Support the Budget and five-year rolling financial plan, partnering with Planning team, Merch and Operations

Skills for Success

You will bring proven experience in controlling and financial planning, along with a strong analytical mindset:

  • Master’s degree in finance or a related field
  • 3+ years of relevant work experience with a proven track record in FP&A roles
  • Strong financial acumen and understanding of the outdoor industry
  • Excellent organizational and time management skills, with a goal-oriented approach
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to interact with non-finance stakeholders
  • Advanced software skills, including Excel, PowerPoint, SAP, COGNOS and BPC (experience with AI tools is a plus)
  • Fluent in English and preferably one additional EU language
