Full description

They develop and supervise electrical test infrastructures for low-voltage three-phase systems in the field of research and development. You take responsibility for electrical design, firmware development and laboratory validation.

Tasks

  • Development and maintenance of electrical test infrastructures for low-voltage three-phase systems
  • Development and validation of embedded firmware and control solutions in C
  • Programming of PLC applications for laboratory and test environments
  • Planning and conducting functional, performance and security tests on hardware prototypes
  • Collaboration with interdisciplinary R&D teams to define requirements and solve problems
  • Preparation and maintenance of technical documentation during the development process

Profile

  • Experience in embedded firmware development with C for microcontroller or microprocessor systems
  • Knowledge of low voltage three-phase systems, power distribution or power conversion
  • Experience in electrical design, testing, validation or commissioning of electrical systems
  • Familiarity with PLC programming and industrial automation
  • Good communication and team skills in interdisciplinary environments
  • Good English skills, other languages beneficial
