The Position As a Power Platform Administrator, you will ensure the security, scalability, and health of our program and be the key technical expert on its technical setup and configuration. You will manage environments, implement Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies and other detailed configuration, and oversee governance frameworks to empower citizen developers and application teams across Roche. You will monitor platform capacity and other platform health metrics, manage user licensing, and perform other administration tasks. As appropriate, you will implement automations for administration and provide technical leadership and supervision to others who may also be acting as administrators. By bridging the gap between IT governance and developers, you will ensure our organization leverages the full potential of the Power Platform in a secure, high-performing, and efficient manner.
