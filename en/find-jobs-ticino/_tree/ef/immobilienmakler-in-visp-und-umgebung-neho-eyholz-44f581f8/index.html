Real estate agents: in Visp and the surrounding area

Are you a sales talent with enthusiasm for the real estate industry? Then become a real estate agent: at Neho and join the team in Valais!

Neho is one of the market leading brokerage companies in Switzerland. We stand for innovative real estate services that combine human expertise with powerful digital solutions. This enables a faster, transparent and high-quality real estate sale. A special feature: We offer our service at a fixed price instead of a commission, because we consider it fair and timely.

You are bold, flexible and ready to tackle new challenges? Then you are right with us. At Neho, the pace is high and the learning curve steep – the perfect opportunity to learn a lot quickly, whether with or without experience in the real estate industry. With your contribution, you can not only make a difference at Neho, but in the entire real estate sector. Because that is our goal: not only to be the market leader, but to reinvent our industry. *

Workload: 80 \- 100 %

Your tasks *

  • Meet with owners in your region (Oberwallis \& Mittelwallis), evaluate their sales plans and conduct negotiations in German and German
  • Create property estimates using banking software and your market expertise
  • Develop a customer-specific sales strategy and complete the mandate
  • Manage the requests of those interested in buying, negotiate the offers and accompany the sale until the notarial phase \- with operational support from an experienced in-house service team
  • Build a trusting relationship with your customers, advise and accompany them throughout the sales process
  • Become a reference person in your area, expand your network and increase your visibility as a real estate expert
  • Collaborate with our real estate financing specialists to offer buyers suitable financing solutions
  • Get actively involved in the further development of the Neho\ strategy. With your growing experience, you will become a driving force in our team

What we offer

  • An accelerated training: learning the profession of real estate agent and a very fast implementation in practice on site
  • Compensation that is one of the most attractive on the market and can reach over CHF 200,000 per year for the best performing in the long term
  • High autonomy, flexible time and work division as well as the possibility to work from home every day
  • A fast-growing company, flat hierarchies and an inspiring working atmosphere
  • An experienced back office team that takes on administrative tasks and supports you in day-to-day business
  • Continuous training and exchange of experience and proven processes with your colleagues
  • A dynamic, entrepreneurial and stimulating working environment with innovative tools and tools
  • Unique opportunity to help shape the Swiss real estate market
  • 5 weeks of holidays \+ additional days by seniority, fully paid maternity / paternity leave, regular team events and much more!

What you bring

  • Good knowledge of Upper and Middle Valais as well as a well established professional and/or personal network in the region
  • Fluent German and ösisch skills in words and writing are a prerequisite for being able to serve customers in both languages
  • Proven experience in daily customer contact and customer acquisition, preferably in the B2C area
  • Knowledge of the real estate sector is a big plus (e.g. marketing, management, or similar)
  • A sales-oriented, customer-centric attitude and an open ear
  • Sociability and excellent appearance
  • A sense of responsibility and a high degree of independence
  • Affinity for technologies and a secure use of new IT\ tools
  • Possession of a driving licence and own vehicle

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
