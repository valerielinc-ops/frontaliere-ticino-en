Full description

Job Summary

With limited guidance, perform finance-related activities and oversee core accounting operations and support financial strategy within our insurance business. This role will play a key part in ensuring accurate financial reporting, strengthening internal controls, and driving process improvements and automation initiatives.

Job Qualifications

Education & Experience

  • Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.
  • Minimum 5–7 years of experience in accounting/finance, preferably within the insurance or financial services industry.
  • Prior experience in a supervisory or team lead role is highly preferred.

Technical Skills

  • Strong knowledge of accounting standards (IFRS / PSAK) and financial reporting requirements.
  • Solid experience in general ledger management, reconciliations, and month-end/year-end closing processes.
  • Strong understanding of AR/AP processes and internal control frameworks (ICFR/SOX).
  • Experience in budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis.
  • Proficiency in ERP systems such as SAP, Oracle, or similar platforms.
  • Advanced Excel skills and familiarity with financial modeling techniques.

Job Accountabilities - Key Accountabilities

  • Perform general ledger activities including accruals, provisions, reconciliations, and journal postings.
  • Prepare periodic financial and ledger reports, analyze variances, and explain key results to stakeholders.
  • Manage Accounts Receivable (AR) and Accounts Payable (AP) processes, ensuring timely and accurate transactions.
  • Conduct basic accounting and regulatory research relevant to insurance and finance operations.
  • Collaborate with internal teams and external stakeholders (e.g., auditors, vendors, regulators) to ensure compliance with accounting standards and procedures.
  • Support business decision-making through accurate financial analysis, data insights, and reporting.
  • Contribute to annual budgeting and periodic forecasting processes in partnership with cross-functional teams.
  • Develop, maintain, and review moderately complex financial models to support planning and analysis.
  • Act as a subject matter expert (SME) in accounting for internal projects and initiatives.
  • Monitor and improve accounting processes by identifying opportunities for automation and efficiency using appropriate technologies.
  • Perform and maintain Internal Control over Financial Reporting (ICFR) activities in accordance with company policies and regulatory requirements.
