back to the overview Machine operator folding press (a) 100 % Job opening Immediately or by agreement Employment level 100% Place of work/region BE-Bern For our customer in the Berne region, we are looking for an experienced specialist for the sheet metal department. The focus is on sophisticated metal construction solutions made of steel, aluminium and chrome-nickel steel.

Their tasks

  • Programming and operation of the bending press
  • Production of sheet metal parts according to plans and specifications
  • Control of the quality of the manufactured parts
  • Cooperation in the current production operation

Your profile

  • Basic vocational training in metal construction or related crafts
  • Several years of practice on folding and punching machines
  • Safe handling of technical drawings and production plans
  • Independent, reliable working method with an eye for practical solutions
  • Adaptability and collaboration in the team
  • Good knowledge of German

We offer you

  • Interesting orders with bending and punching in high quality
