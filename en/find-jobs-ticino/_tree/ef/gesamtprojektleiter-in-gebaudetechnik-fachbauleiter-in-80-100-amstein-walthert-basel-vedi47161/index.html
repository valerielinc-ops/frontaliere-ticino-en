Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with around 1,100 employees. As engineers, planners and consultants, we work on all aspects of the technology of civil engineering and civil engineering over the entire life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and sustainable. These are your tasks

  • Overall responsibility for the planning, coordination and implementation of demanding projects in the field of building technology (HLKSE & GA)
  • Leadership and control of interdisciplinary planning teams
  • Taking over the specialist construction management on construction sites as well as ensuring quality, costs and deadlines
  • Coordination of building masters, architects, specialist planners, entrepreneurs and authorities
  • Ensuring smooth information and project management across all project phases
  • Close cooperation with other specialist areas of A+W
  • Preparation of surrender documents
  • Support in the acquisition and development of customer relationships You bring that with you
  • Completed education or study in Switzerland in the field of building technology, electrical engineering, HVAC, energy or building technology
  • Several years of experience as project manager, overall project manager or specialist construction manager
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss standards, guidelines and construction processes
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and high customer orientation
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong communication and leadership skills
  • Interest in the developments in the industry
  • Enthusiasm and pleasure in demanding work
  • German in Word and Writing
  • Other national languages or English are advantageous
  • Further education as a project manager, overall project manager or specialist construction manager
  • Experience in complex refurbishment, new construction or infrastructure projects
  • Knowledge of BIM methodology and digital project management with Revit
  • Experience with technical and spatial coordination
  • Existing network in the Swiss construction and real estate industry
