Marketing Assistant (m/f/d) area of personal care and skin protection, 80-100%

Coop Cooperative — Basel, BS, Svizzera

  • Job opening: by agreement
  • Specialization: Category Management / Procurement
  • Location: Coop, Thiersteinerallee 14, 4053 Basel
  • Pensum: 80 - 100%

Tasks

  • Administrative and operational support in purchasing and product management cosmetics for all Coop channels
  • Obtaining, analysing and evaluating supplier offers as well as handling promotional offers
  • Examination of invoices, control of print releases and processing and billing of advertising contributions
  • Maintenance of master data in SAP R/3 and management of product samples
  • Proactive support of Purchasing Managers in day-to-day business as well as coordination and communication with internal and external contacts:
  • Creation of the shelf layouts for the assortment change

Requirements

  • Completed basic commercial training, ideally with professional experience in purchasing or product management
  • Strong interest and affinity for the cosmetics industry
  • Quick comprehension, high commitment, initiative and independent, customer-oriented working methods
  • Engaged, communicative and team-oriented personality with a convincing appearance and enthusiasm
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, ideally knowledge of English and French as well as good knowledge of MS Office, SAP knowledge of advantages
  • Perks: 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket, up to 20% discount on various insurance services, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop Group, participation in the subscription, perks for cultural events, perks for leisure activities
  • Conditions of employment: Pension fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, collective employment contract (GAV), 18 weeks paid maternity leave and 4 weeks paid paternity leave
  • Working hours: 5 weeks holidays
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription
