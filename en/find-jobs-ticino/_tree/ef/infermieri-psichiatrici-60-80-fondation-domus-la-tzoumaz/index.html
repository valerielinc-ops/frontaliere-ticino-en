Full description

The Domus Foundation, a Valaisan institution for psychosocial rehabilitation, supports adults facing significant mental health issues. Around 150 colleagues are engaged with conviction and professionalism. Our vision: self-determination for each, a solution-centered approach, and professional practices rooted in kindness and responsibility. We are looking for:

  • Nurses in Psychiatry (60% and 80%)
  • full-time or part-time We offer you:
  • to provide daily nursing care for the individuals we support, at a preventive, curative, medical-educational, and palliative level, as needed;
  • to manage all medical consultations and the continuity of care;
  • to support the beneficiaries within the La Tzoumaz or Ardon residence in their daily activities within a psychosocial rehabilitation perspective based on self-determination;
  • to support residents in their individual projects and goals;
  • to develop a privileged relationship with the network of supported individuals;
  • to collaborate in an interdisciplinary manner with social workers, ASE, ASSC, day centers, and workshops. We offer you:
  • to join a foundation guided by participative management, a positive dynamic, and a strong spirit of openness;
  • to be part of an institution certified by Promotion Santé Suisse, concerned about the well-being of each and the teams where listening, collaboration, mutual aid, and conviviality are cultivated;
  • to find your place in a structure that regularly develops numerous projects to which each can contribute;
  • to benefit from annually planned working hours and collaboration within a multidisciplinary team;
  • to benefit from a pleasant working environment and good social and salary conditions;
  • to participate in continuous training every year through our training catalog «Agir Ensemble». Your profile:
  • you hold a Bachelor's degree in Nursing or an equivalent title, CAS in psychiatry is an asset;
  • you have at least 2 years of professional experience. Knowledge of the RAI (Resident Assessment Instrument) software is an asset;
  • you are organized, manage priorities, enjoy working in a team but also autonomously, appreciate versatility;
  • you master common computer tools (MS Office);
  • you hold a driver's license and have a private vehicle. Interested? Further information can be obtained at 027 205 75 00. Please send your complete dossier with CV, photo, cover letter, diplomas, and work certificates by April 10, 2026. Dossiers will be treated in complete confidentiality.
