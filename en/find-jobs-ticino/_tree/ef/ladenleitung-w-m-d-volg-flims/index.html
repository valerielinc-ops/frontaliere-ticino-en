Who we are

Volg is the specialist for village shops and small areas in German and French-speaking Switzerland. We live close to the customer – as a close shopping opportunity as well as in personal contact. The employees are the heart of the shop and we attach great importance to individuality and personality. That's why our motto is "fresh and cheerful".
