Full description

Introduction

For our customer, an established company in building construction, we are looking for you as construction manager (m/f/d). In this role, you take responsibility for operational management and ensure quality, costs and deadlines across all project phases.

Tasks

  • Overall responsibility on the construction site: organisation, coordination and management of the entrepreneurs and service providers involved
  • Cost, schedule and quality control according to project objectives
  • Reports, construction journals, meetings and minutes
  • Tendering and award in cooperation with project management/planning, auditing and supplementary management
  • Interface management for building management, planning, authorities, neighbours and internal bodies
  • Quality assurance and occupational safety (SUVA specifications), carrying out controls, acceptance and defect management until handover
  • Construction administration and scheduling

Profile

  • Completed construction-related training (e.g. building drawing artist/in EFZ, Zimmermann/Zimmerin EFZ, mason/in EFZ) with further training as construction manager, technician/in HF construction planning or equivalent
  • Several years of experience in building management
  • In-depth knowledge of SIA standards, construction processes and construction administration; safe occurrence on the construction site
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strong communication and negotiation skills
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Other languages are beneficial

Advantages

  • Current employment conditions
  • Modern work equipment
  • Further education opportunities and clear development prospects
