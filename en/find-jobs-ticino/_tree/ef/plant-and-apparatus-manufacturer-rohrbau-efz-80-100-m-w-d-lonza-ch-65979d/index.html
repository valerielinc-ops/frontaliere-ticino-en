Full description

Relocation support is available to eligible candidates and their families as needed.

Join our workshop team as a plant and apparatus fabricator!

In this varied role, you will take on demanding work in the field of piping as well as the reactive, proactive and preventive maintenance of our production facilities. With your practical skills and technical expertise, you will ensure the safe and reliable operation of our piping systems. In doing so, you will make an important contribution to high plant availability, product quality and compliance with our quality, safety and compliance standards at the Visp site.

What we offer you

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous benefits in the areas of lifestyle, family and leisure. You can also find the complete list of our global benefits at: https://bit.ly/3wjkoFi

Your responsibilities

  • Accurate and cost-effective measuring, fabrication and installation of demanding pipelines and support systems in piping construction
  • Creating isometric drawings and piping segments in accordance with technical specifications and project requirements
  • Planning and organizing workflows based on P&ID flow diagrams for project work and plant modifications
  • Applying PED guidelines and working in accordance with internal pipe classes, company standards and quality assurance requirements

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as a plant and apparatus fabricator EFZ specializing in piping, or as a pipefitter, heating installer or sanitary installer
  • Several years of professional experience as a pipefitter or pipe fabricator is an advantage
  • Willingness to pursue continuous further training
  • Enjoyment of cross-departmental work as well as the ability to work independently and flexibly in a team
  • Reliability, resilience, entrepreneurial thinking and action, as well as open, direct communication
  • German language skills, both spoken and written
  • English skills are an advantage

About Lonza

At Lonza, our employees are our greatest strength. At more than 30 sites across five continents, our globally connected teams work together every day to make the medicines of tomorrow. Our core values—collaboration, accountability, excellence, passion and integrity—reflect who we are and how we work together.
