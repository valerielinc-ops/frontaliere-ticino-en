Full description

In this role, together with a colleague (70 % penalty), you are responsible for the reception and the internal sales shop. In addition, you take on deputy tasks of the assistant head of production fresh meat in their absence.

  • Contract type: Perpetual
  • Pensum: 70%
  • Job opening: 01.02.2027
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Dünnernstrasse 31 4702 Oensingen

Tasks

  • organisation and coordination of reception
  • Visitor management, access control
  • Receiving and forwarding incoming telephone calls
  • Support of the internal sales shop
  • Boardroom and pool vehicle management services
  • Processing of mail input and output
  • General correspondence and administrative work
  • Assistant duties in deputy of the assistant head of production fresh meat
  • Record keeping
  • Deputy teammate (holiday, illness)

Requirements

  • Commercial vocational training completed
  • Professional experience in receiving and handling administrative tasks
  • Very good MS-Office and Outlook knowledge
  • Stylish-confident German in word and writing, oral French skills advantageous
  • Careful appearance, good manners and pleasure in dealing with people
  • Strong teamwork, open and communicative personality
  • Willingness for flexible working days and 100% representation for holidays or illness

This is what we offer

  • Work-life balance: A balanced work-life balance is important to us. You benefit from at least 25 holiday days and a transparent, electronic time recording. So you can flexibly compensate for any overtime with additional free days.
  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: freshly prepared food in our staff restaurant, a lunch menu incl. Soup and salad, water & tea and free fruits as well as high-quality products at greatly reduced prices await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport, attractive subsidies for tickets and sufficient parking in the car park.
  • Professional development opportunities: Expand your knowledge through extensive further education offers, high-quality training in the Coop Education Center and various personal development opportunities.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management, attendance bonus, discounted Reka checks, exclusive employee discounts and subsidized fitness subscriptions – for health and leisure fun!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments at our departmental event and strengthen the team spirit during the company run – celebrate, move and make contacts together!
  • Market-friendly wages and benefits: Secure your future with our above-average retirement savings, receive anniversary bonuses, Christmas bonuses and attractive employee referral bonuses. In addition, you receive the personal discount card from Coop and we cover two thirds of your pension fund contributions!
  • Family-friendly employer: Make your working time flexible with part-time models and experience an inclusive working environment where diversity is valued and encouraged!
