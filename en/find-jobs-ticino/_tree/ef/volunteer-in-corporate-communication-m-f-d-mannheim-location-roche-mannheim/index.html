The Position

Who we are

Roche employs more than 100,000 people worldwide, pioneering diagnostics and pharmaceuticals to create a healthier future for everyone. The company has grown to be one of the world's largest biotech companies, as well as a leading provider of in vitro diagnostics and a global provider of innovative solutions for large therapeutic areas. In Germany, around 18,600 employees work daily for the benefit of patients.

Our success is based on innovation, curiosity and diversity – and on our view of our differences as an advantage. To drive the health sector through innovation, we want to continue to learn and grow. That's why we are looking for new colleagues who are pursuing the same ambitious goals as we are.

Group Communications

We are part of Group Communications as Roche's central corporate communications division. The division is responsible for creating a sustainably positive Roche image and representing the company's voice at Group level. This means that we communicate the Roche strategy and the implementation of our goal, Doing now what Roha tec next, to Roche's colleagues:inside and diverse external target groups. We conduct integrated and global campaigns and provide platforms through which our business partners and subsidiaries can communicate with the target groups.

We help build trust in our company, maintain Roche's brand and reputation, and create a favourable environment for our business. We also strive to be innovative, forward-looking, creative and courageous, and to represent a strong, integrated corporate brand and a positive audience experience. We work in an environment where people can unfold, learn new skills, create great things and have fun together.

Our communication vision is ambitious and simple: we inspire the world to contribute to the health of people.

Site Network Communications Germany

The German communication team at the United States' Sites is responsible for implementing communication strategies and measures that contribute to the positive perception of Roche and our locations, both in our current and future employees and in the Communities we operate in. With our local expertise and network, we promote integrated communication within the Group's communications and build bridges between local needs and global priorities to maximise the impact of our communication work.

As a communication department of the "Silver Network Germany", we work together to develop exciting stories from the diverse content of our company and to implement them communicatively with creative ideas and formats for our employees, media representatives and our environment. We support the strategic orientation of the company and make the identity of Roche tangible.

As a volunteer, you will gain insights into the content and organizational processes of corporate communication and learn about our daily communicative business. You will help to understand the transformation of business and the stories of our locations Mannheim, Penzberg and Ludwigsburg and to put people at the centre of the project. In the team, you can contribute your ideas and actively contribute to the further development of our communication strategies.
