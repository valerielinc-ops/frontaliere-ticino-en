Full description

Who are we ?

The Social Action Service SASoc, the competent authority in the canton of Friborg in particular for compensation for LAVI victims, the recovery and advances of maintenance contributions and the coordination of social assistance, offers people at the end of their internship as a lawyer and in parallel with their preparation for the bar exam, a position as a trainee lawyer at 50-60% for a duration of min. 6 months.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages work-life balance and offers an attractive working environment and conditions to its employees. To find out more, follow this link.

Missions

  • Drafting of administrative decisions regarding LAVI compensation / recovery and advances of maintenance contributions
  • Representation of the interests of the State before the judicial authorities and the prefectures of the canton, within the framework of civil and criminal proceedings
  • Legal issues in the processing of social assistance files

Profile sought

  • Master's degree in law, lawyer internship completed, preparation in progress for exams to obtain the lawyer's certificate
  • Mastery of the French or German language with very good knowledge of the other language
