Full description

BISON is one of the leading Swiss IT companies and has various locations in Switzerland and Germany and employs around 550 people. Bison offers complete IT solutions for various industries, from software to all infrastructure support on site or in one of the company's own data centers.The BISON Group develops and implements innovative retail ERP and software solutions and has been successfully supporting SMEs and large companies in the trade, agricultural and energy sectors for more than 40 years with a focus on the DACH region. BISON is a company of the fenaco-LANDI Group and acts as its central IT service provider.As of August 2027, we offer an apprenticeship as an ICT specialist EFZ to an established and motivated school leaver.

You do that.

  • You set up IT jobs and keep them running.
  • You support our employees in IT support.
  • You install, configure and maintain hardware and software.
  • You work with networks, servers and cloud services.
  • You help with IT projects and bring your own ideas.
  • You learn new things every day around the world of computer science.

Requirements, tasks and general information are very good under ICT vocational training ICT specialist / ICT specialist EFZ described. There you will also find a short video that explains the tasks of an ICT specialist - so, nothing like going and looking! The apprenticeship as an ICT specialist lasts 3 years. The versatile and interesting vocational training is complemented by the visit to the vocational school and by intercompany courses in which knowledge on subject-specific topics is imparted. If suitable, the Bison Group also supports the visit to the BMS (BM 1 - teaching-accompanying model).

You bring that to me.

A school degree with good grades is a condition to complete an apprenticeship as an ICT specialist. Interest in mathematical subjects, logical/abstract thinking skills and good linguistic expression, as well as the willingness to work for a successful course of teaching are further prerequisites. Since we also serve customers in Western Switzerland, good knowledge of French is an advantage. Applications must also include CV and Multicheck. Also welcome signalling test, if available.
