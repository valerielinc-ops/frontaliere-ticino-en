Full description

The Moncucco Hospitaller Group, a recognized private care institution of public interest and which manages the Moncucco Clinic and the Santa Chiara Clinic, seeks

First-aid doctors interested in completing training in emergency medicine and intraospedal emergency

Required features

  • Specialization in general internal medicine or in general surgery or anesthesia

Or

  • Doctors in training for internal medicine, general surgery or anesthesia, with at least three years of experience

What distinguishes us

  • solid corporate values
  • rapidly changing hospital structure
  • excellent inclusion in the region
  • excellent opportunities for continuing training
  • contributions to mobility and closeness to public transport
  • excellent social benefits

What we offer

  • 12-month training contract in a category A first aid, recognized by the SGNOR as category 2 training center for emergency medicine and intraospedal emergency
  • possibility of collaboration with the green cross Lugano for extra-hospital emergency medicine

Percentage of use: 80-100%

Date of entry into service: to be agreed

Interested persons are requested to submit their application to the Human Resources Service of the Moncucco Hospitaller Group (applications@moncucco.ch).

Information can be requested by Dr. Pietro Greco, doctor responsible for PS ( pietro.greco@moncucco.ch )

The answer will be given only to candidates who correspond to the required characteristics.
