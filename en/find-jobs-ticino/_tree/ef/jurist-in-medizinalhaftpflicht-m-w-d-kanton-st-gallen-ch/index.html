Full description

The Department of Insurance and Risk Management is integrated into the General Secretariat of the Department of Finance and manages the risks of the state administration and those of public institutions. In this area of responsibility, the regulation of "medical liability cases" - self-regulated without insurance - on behalf of the Hospital Association HOCH and the Psychiatry St.Gallen is also covered.

What to expect

  • Comprehensive processing of medical liability cases of cantonal hospitals and psychiatries
  • Independent negotiations with patients, legal representatives and insurers
  • Legal advice and representation of cantonal hospitals and psychiatries
  • Implementation of prevention activities
  • Representation of the head of medical liability in their absence

What distinguishes you

  • Legal studies, preferably patent as a lawyer
  • CAS Liability and insurance law or willingness to complete it
  • Several years of experience in the field of civil liability law and/or social security law
  • Interest in medical law issues as well as damage case processing
  • Affinity and experience with the application "Leonardo"
  • Social skills, teamwork, negotiating skills and pleasant dealings
  • Initiative, self-confident personality with conceptual and communicative skills

What we offer

  • Demanding, interesting and self-responsible field of activity
  • Collegial and appreciative cooperation in a small team
  • Good employment conditions with flexible working hours
  • Attractive workplace in the city of St.Gallen with modern infrastructure
  • An open YOU culture where your opinion counts and trust is lived
