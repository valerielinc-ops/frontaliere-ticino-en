In the contract logistics team at Kühne+Nagel, you are responsible for warehousing operations, such as picking goods. With your work, for example, you make it possible to store and deliver flowers and fresh ingredients for a nice ambiance and delicious food in your favorite restaurant. Logistics at Kühne+Nagel is more than you can imagine.

At our modern logistics location in Möhlin, we are responsible for warehouse logistics for renowned customers in the pharmaceutical and healthcare sector. As Operations Manager Logistics (m/f/d), you are responsible for the smooth running of all warehouse processes and lead a team of up to 40 employees. In close cooperation with our site management, you ensure the processes and drive efficiency through KPI-controlled process optimizations. You actively shape our customer business at the location and develop structures, processes and employees sustainably. Become part of our team and shape the long-term development of the location.
