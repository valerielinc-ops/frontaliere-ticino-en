We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Your world is green, you love plants, trees and working outdoors. At Stadtgrün Luczern you can contribute your passion and shape the city of Lucerne!

Your tasks

  • You work in a motivated team and design the most well-kept, attractive green spaces.
  • You plant and maintain discounts with a lot of care.
  • You bring shape hedges into shape properly.
  • You take care of the care and maintenance of lawns and meadows.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a gardener* in EFZ.

  • You enjoy plants and their professional care.
  • You have a category B driving licence.
