Full description

Family medicine thrives on continuity and on knowing and accompanying patients for years. If you value this long-term relationship, you can expect the right environment in a family practice in Liestal, Canton BL. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for a specialist for general internal medicine in the amount of 50 – 100%. In outpatient primary care, you take care of prevention, diagnosis and therapy, work with clear structures and digitally supported processes and benefit from regulated working hours without night and emergency services. This is what awaits you

  • Independent and holistic care of your patients in modern family medicine
  • Use of digital practical solutions and structured processes to support your medical work
  • Collaboration with an interprofessional team at eye level
  • Flexible workload and room for manoeuvre within the practice
  • Opportunity for professional development and participation in quality circles

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine or General medicine with MEBEKO recognition
  • Very good knowledge of German (at least C1)
  • Experience in the Swiss healthcare system is an advantage
  • Structured, goal-oriented working method and pleasure in patient-centered care

That sets them apart.

  • Empathy, respect and appreciation in dealing with patients and team members
  • Responsibility and commitment to high medical treatment quality
  • Openness for modern, digital practical concepts
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and collegial exchange

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Attractive and fair employment conditions with a fixed employment contract (no documentary doctor system)
  • Above-average social benefits (1/3, 2/3 pension fund scheme, 100 % takeover of the KTG by the employer)
  • Regulated working hours in day-to-day operations, excluding night or emergency services
  • No evening services; for rare Saturday assignments 25% wage surcharge
  • Active promotion of medical development and training
  • Modern, bright practice space in a central location

In addition for EU citizens

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition
  • Accompaniment until successful professional practice in Switzerland
  • Upon request, provision of initial accommodation through our internal rental department

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Basel?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
