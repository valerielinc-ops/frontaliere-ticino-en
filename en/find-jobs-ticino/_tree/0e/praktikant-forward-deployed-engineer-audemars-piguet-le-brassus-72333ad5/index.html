Full description

For our Digital Factory team in the IT department, we are looking for our future trainee for a period of 6 months, starting in February 2027. During your internship, your main mission will be to design and develop custom web and mobile applications.

In this context, you will be responsible for

  • Work closely with our experienced developers to design, develop and maintain web and mobile applications that meet the needs of the company and its customers
  • Manage your own projects independently, while exchanging regularly with the team to share your progress and questions
  • Participate in code reviews to ensure development standards, code quality, and knowledge sharing
  • You are currently a Bachelor or Master in Computer Science or in a similar field
  • You have a strong appetite for frontend as well as backend and ideally have a GitHub profile to share
  • You have good knowledge of JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML and CSS, as well as an IU library and you are fluent in English
  • You have good analytical and organizational skills, and be recognized by your excellent critical mind
