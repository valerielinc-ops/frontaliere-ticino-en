Full description

Shaping the future with innovation Become part of our success and shape the future with us! CSEM’s Additive Manufacturing and Component Reliability group in Neuchâtel is looking for an experienced LPBF specialist to reinforce the existing team. The appointment shall preferably take place as soon as possible, depending on the availability of the selected person and by mutual agreement. Their mission Its mission is to strengthen CSEM’s LPBF team and further develop additive manufacturing of components made of one or more metals. They take responsibility for the LPBF printing activities and play a key role in the successful implementation of additive manufacturing projects. With your expertise in mechanical engineering, you will help solve complex industrial challenges and develop innovative manufacturing processes. Their tasks The position covers the full range of LPBF activities. The focus is on the practical operation of three complementary LPBF systems, which requires a high level of technical expertise.

Other tasks include

  • Scientific participation in LPBF activities and projects.
  • Preparation of technical documentation and reports on project progress for industrial customers.
  • Regular presence on site, implementation of laboratory tours and supervision of students within the framework of semester work and internships.

Your profile Technical competences

  • Proven practical experience in operating LPBF systems.
  • sound scientific background, demonstrated by any of the following qualifications:

– Doctorate in a relevant field with a background in mechanical engineering or – Master’s degree in a relevant field, combined with several years of professional experience as well as background or practical experience in mechanical engineering.

  • Good communication skills in German, French and English.

Personal competences

  • Curious and creative, with a strong spirit of innovation across disciplines and the ability to develop unconventional technical solutions.
  • Practical, proactive and results-oriented.
  • Ability to work both independently and in multidisciplinary teams.
  • Strong organisational skills.

Application deadline is 30. October 2026. Mission and Values of CSEM We believe in a future conceived, shared and shaped together. At CSEM, we don’t just innovate – we design technologies that shape the future. Since 1984, our goal has remained unchanged: to use science and technology for the benefit of society. Today, more than 650 people work at different locations in Switzerland for our mission: to develop and transfer world-class solutions in the fields of digital technologies, precision manufacturing and sustainable energy systems. This enables Swiss industry to remain innovative and competitive. Our strength lies in the excellence of our employees. We are convinced that strong values promote both the successful development of our organization and the harmonious and balanced development of all employees. We are

  • A unique place at the interface between research and industry and at the forefront of new technologies
  • An innovative, non-profit-oriented and employee-driven company
  • A dynamic, multidisciplinary and multicultural environment
  • A dedicated team that enables solutions to the energy challenges of a sustainable world

Working at CSEM means

  • Being part of an engaged community.
  • Flexibility, attractive working conditions and development opportunities.
  • Benefiting from a leadership style based on trust and feedback and promoting work-life balance.
