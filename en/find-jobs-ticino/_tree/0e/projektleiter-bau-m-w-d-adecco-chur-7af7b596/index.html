Full description

Introduction

For our customer, a solid and established company in the construction industry, we are looking for a responsible and solution-oriented personality as a project manager: in construction. In this role, you take over the overall management of construction projects and ensure quality, deadlines and costs across all phases.

Tasks

  • Project responsibility from preparation/tendering to execution until acceptance and guarantee phase
  • Planning, organisation and coordination of all project participants
  • Schedule and cost management incl. Risk/change management, contracts/additions and claim management
  • Ensuring quality according to SIA standards and SUVA/HSE specifications
  • chair meetings, hold minutes, report to management/builder
  • Transparent reporting of KPIs
  • Create tenders/devis to NPK, examine tenders and prepare awards
  • Create and control schedules (e.g. MS Project/Primavera); Construction site and resource planning in close coordination with Bauführung/Polier
  • Process and cost optimisation
  • Active participation in digitalization/BIM and continuous improvement

Profile

  • Completed training as a civil engineer:in FH/ETH, architect:in FH, technician:in HF Bauführung or dipl. Construction manager:in
  • Several years of experience in project management
  • Very good knowledge of SIA standards, NPK/Devis, construction processes and construction administration
  • Security in terms/cost management, contracting and supplementary management
  • Communicative, assertive, structured - with high quality standards and entrepreneurial thinking
  • Very good German in word and writing
  • Cat-B driving licence

Advantages

  • Contemporary employment conditions with clear competences and scope for design
  • Annual working hours, flexible classification and mobile work by arrangement
  • Further education and individual development pathways
  • Modern work equipment
