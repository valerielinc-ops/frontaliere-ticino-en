Two formulas, at your choice

  • Employees, full-time or part-time, on an indeterminate contract.
  • Independent, with your own RCC number: you work on your account at our premises, taking advantage of our equipment, our assistant and our secretariat. An ideal solution to settle without investing in a firm or starting from scratch.

What We Offer

  • A complete vestibular tray: Frramir Multitest Balance platform, rotary chair, videonystagmography (VNG).
  • Rehabilitation devices (Giger MD, MOTOmed), with an assistant trained to install and monitor patients.
  • A secretariat that manages reception and appointments.
  • An existing patientel and faithful prescribers.
  • Full training in our equipment.

Your role

  • Assess and treat patients with internal ear and balance disorders (vertigo, tinnitus).
  • One more: maxillofacial physiotherapy (somatosensorial tinnitus).
  • To be the physio responsible for device sessions, present on site during their run.

Profile

  • Diploma of physiotherapist, with specialization or experience in vestibular.
  • For the employee formula: recognition of the Swiss Red Cross (SRC) obtained, or ongoing procedure. If you only did the PreCheck, count about 12 months of procedure.
  • For the independent formula: diploma recognized in Switzerland, and number RCC or conditions to obtain it in Geneva.
  • Autonomy, sense of teamwork, and good communication with patients.
