Full description

Tasks

  • Care and information for our guests at the Thermal Spa (Helenabad), Family Spa and in summer at the garden pool
  • Monitoring of the entire bathing and sauna area including implementation of safety and first aid services
  • Water measurements as well as e.g. Performance of sauna infusions
  • Implementation of cleaning activities, disinfection of walking surfaces and easier repair work (safety, order and cleanliness throughout the area incl. Wardrobes, toilets, showers and loungers
  • Filling of fresh linen and guest care products and disposal of dirty linen
  • Support of other departments as needed

Requirements

  • First Aid Course CPR/SRLG Brevet Pool Plus Required
  • Professional experience in a comparable position is beneficial
  • Further education for / to the Med. Bathing or sauna master/in an advantage
  • Good knowledge of German, other foreign languages (English, Italian) advantageous
  • Readiness for evening and weekend assignments
  • Good manners, teamwork, flexibility, resilience and honesty

We offer

  • Taster evenings and perks at Thermal Spa
  • Free Tickets Tamina Gorge
  • Test sleep after passing the test period
  • Up to 50% discount in restaurants
  • Up to 50% discount in the Fitness Center and Tamina Therme

The Grand Resort Bad Ragaz has its origins in the healing thermal water of the Tamina spring. Since 1242 this place stands for regeneration, new energy and quality of life. On this basis, we continue to develop the resort consistently – with the aim of becoming the leading Wellbeing & Healthy Living Resort in Europe and creating a place of strength for generations.

Around 800 employees shape the experience of our guests every day. Different talents, cultures and personalities work together on one goal: to create unique experiences. What unites us is the common aspiration to make this special place a bit better every day – One Resort – One Team.
