Full description

Regionalspital Surselva AG provides basic and emergency care for around 22,000 residents as well as seasonally additional holiday guests in the tourist region. Our employees treat approximately 2,400 inpatient and 16,000 outpatient patients annually in the areas of surgery/orthopedics, internal medicine, gynecology/obstetrics, pediatrics and anesthesia. Modern infrastructure and cooperation with Chur Center Hospital enable an extended range of services.
