The Position Custom Software Developer - Salesforce Center of Excellence Description of the Area The Customer Engagement Platforms and Services function acts as Salesforce Center of Excellence (CoE) within Roche Digital Technology . These platforms and services are consumed by others to create business facing platforms, products and services . The goal is to empower product teams to develop business capabilities efficiently and compliantly, while reducing their cognitive load and allowing them to focus on value generation . Although development and operations remain decentralized, the Salesforce CoE can offer additional services, especially to smaller organizations that may lack certain specialized skills . The team is mandated to significantly reduce unintended customization and technical diversity by partnering with the business functions using the systems and driving re-use, simplification and standardization .

The Opportunity

  • Support Salesforce Engineering: Support the design, crafting, configuration, deployment, and sustaining of Salesforce software solutions (including LWC, Apex, and Flow) tailored to business requirements and objectives .
  • Contribute to Global Projects: Serve as an active project contributor within global product teams, operating primarily at the project level within an internationally distributed team .
  • Implement Delivery Processes: Implement the entire software delivery process, managing transitions from sandbox development to production deployment using CI/CD tools and SFDX .
  • Optimize Software Quality: Improve existing software quality management and metrics through robust execution of Apex Unit Testing and detailed code reviews .
  • Manage Stakeholder Communications: Identify and communicate with stakeholders in a straightforward manner, maintaining a clear view of how components fit into larger systems .
  • Assess Technical Feasibility: Support product decisions by providing reliable technical feasibility assessments and actively contributing to the refinement of requirements .
  • Support Cross-Platform Integration: Apply basic knowledge of cloud platforms (such as AWS, Azure, or GCP) to support seamless cross-platform integrations when necessary .
  • Engage in Knowledge Sharing: Participate actively in organizational knowledge-sharing initiatives, including tech universities, conferences, and Communities of Practice (CoPs) .

Who you are

  • Educated Professional: You hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related technical domain .
  • Experienced Salesforce Developer: You bring 3+ years of progressive work experience as an active project contributor in software engineering, focusing on configuration and deployment within the Salesforce ecosystem .
  • Full-Stack Technical Expert: You possess proven expertise using a combination of frontend, backend, and full-stack development tools, specifically LWC, Apex, and SOQL .
  • Architectural Knowledge: You have a solid understanding of Salesforce architecture (including sharing models and security settings) and know how Sales, Service, and Community Clouds interact .
  • Collaborative Problem Solver: You are highly adept at providing and seeking constructive feedback, communicating technical risks, and evaluating alternative ways to solve problems .
  • Growth-Oriented Learner: You independently execute personal development plans, stay updated on current technological trends, and actively participate in professional communities .
