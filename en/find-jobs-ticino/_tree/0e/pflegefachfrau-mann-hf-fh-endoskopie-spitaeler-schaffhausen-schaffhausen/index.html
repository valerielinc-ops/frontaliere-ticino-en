Full description

In our department, approximately 2,800 endoscopic examinations are carried out annually. These include all common diagnostic and interventional methods of gastroscopy, colonoscopy, ERCP, endosonography and bronchoscopy/EBUS. Another focus is on abdominal sonography including punctures and drainage inserts.

This is what awaits you

  • You supervise and monitor inpatient and outpatient patients before, during and after endoscopic examinations in the fields of gastroenterology and pneumology.
  • You assist the doctors in diagnostic and interventional procedures and work closely with them.
  • You prepare examinations and instruments and ensure the proper handling of the endoscopes and technical devices used.
  • You also keep an overview in demanding investigation situations and contribute to a safe and efficient process with your forward-looking working method.
  • Day services from Monday to Friday, cooking services on weekends

This will take us further.

  • You have completed training as a dipl. Nursing professional / dipl. Nursing professional HF/FH or equivalent training with SRC recognition.
  • You have technical skills and interest in medical devices and sophisticated examination procedures.
  • You work independently, quickly and accurately and also act responsibly and concentrated in dynamic situations.
  • You meet our patients empathetically and professionally and understand how to provide them with security even in stressful situations.
  • You appreciate the close interprofessional cooperation and are committed to the team.
  • Professional training in endoscopy or several years of professional experience in this field is advantageous, but not a prerequisite.

Precautionary benefit Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well.
