Full description

The Klinik Schloss Mammern, embedded in an impressive landscape, is a leading private clinic for every phase of rehabilitation and has been run since 1889 as a family business in the meantime 4th generation. With around 440 dedicated employees, we offer first-class medical care and therapy in four disciplines: cardiological, internistic-oncological, musculoskeletal and pneumological. Our patients enjoy an upscale hotel industry at five-star level in a stylish atmosphere and with the latest infrastructure. The personal and heartfelt treatment of patients is important to us and we attach great importance to a long-term and mutually enriching cooperation. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an employee on a temporary basis for 6-9 months with a permanent employment option.

Responsibility

  • Support in the processing of wages of around 450 employees
  • Processing of personnel mutations (entry and exit)
  • Management of personnel master data
  • Guaranteeing all social security matters (AHV, BVG, daily allowances, family allowances)
  • Representation in the collection and control of illness and accident reports incl. Entry of daily allowances and exemptions
  • Calculation and recording of holiday reductions due to absences
  • Billing of contributions to the staff of the clinic-internal child care center
  • Support for the introduction of the new wage system Abacus on 01.01.2027
  • Help with end-of-year work
  • Preparation of monthly reporting and statistics
  • Participation in HR projects

This is what you bring with you

  • You have completed basic commercial education and experience in wage administration/CH social insurance.
  • Further training as a payroll assistant or comparable qualifications is an advantage.
  • They have very good MS Office knowledge, especially Excel and payroll accounting software (e.g. Abacus).
  • They have a pronounced flair for numbers and know how to deal with confidential data and information.
  • You are a team player and like to work in the team for the team and at eye level.
  • They work autonomously, accurately and reliably, quickly and have a strong hands-on mentality.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing.

This is what we have to offer you

  • Experience a meaningful activity with relevance and responsibility in an inspiring environment and a collegial team.
  • Take advantage of a wide range of internal and external training opportunities with time and financial support.
  • Take advantage of our sports offer with training equipment and indoor pool, which is available free of charge at marginal times.
  • Increase your holiday quota of five weeks by purchasing an additional five days.
  • We reward our long-term employees with seniority gifts in the form of additional holidays or anniversary payments.
  • With us you will receive a free examination of the vaccination status and on request an individual vaccination refresher. We offer the flu vaccination free of charge.
  • Take advantage of access to exclusive product and leisure offers at unbeatable prices.
  • Take advantage of our free parking spaces and reach us comfortably by public transport.
  • Trust our professional childcare (12 weeks to 12 years) in the company’s childcare centre at discounted rates.
  • Enjoy first-class food and comfortable living options.
  • Join our team events, Christmas parties and summer parties.
  • Relax on our private beach on the beautiful Untersee.
