Tasks You accompany and teach as deputy/deputy a second class at the Schulhaus Oberdorf in Wimmis. Your lessons take place from Monday to Friday in the morning and on Tuesday afternoon. You teach German, NMG, mathematics, music, MGS, sports, BG and are class teachers. In 2 lessons MGS the lessons take place in the semiclass.

Requirements You have a teaching diploma for the school and are a dubious, communicative and resilient personality. You enjoy your job and can respond to children and motivate them.

We offer

  • A pleasant working environment in an established and innovative team.
  • Support from the team and the school management.
  • Good cooperation with DAZ, Logopedics, IF, Psychomotorik and school social work on site.
  • Good public transport connection and free parking.

Requirements

  • Teaching diploma for school

  • Conscientious, communicative, and resilient personality

  • Enjoyment of the profession

  • Ability to connect with and motivate children

  • Teaching German, NMG, Mathematics, Music, MGS, Sport, and BG

  • Availability Monday to Friday mornings and Tuesday afternoons

  • Ability to teach in half-classes (2 lessons of MGS)

  • Teamwork skills
