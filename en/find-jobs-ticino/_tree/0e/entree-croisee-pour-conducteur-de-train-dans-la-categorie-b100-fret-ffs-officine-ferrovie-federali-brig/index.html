Cross-entry train driver: in category B100 Cargo 100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cross-entry train driver: in category B100 Cargo Brig, 01.09.2027, 100% Apply now

You can look forward to that.

A varied, paid 14-month full-time education awaits you. You will complete the theoretical training in Olten or Zurich Altstetten and gain your first practical experience in a regionally assigned marshalling station. After training, it is possible to work part-time. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid for, for example, an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits and more information about the profession can be found here. You can move that.

  • As a train driver: in category B100 Cargo, you travel regionally at a speed of up to 100 km/h and deliver freight wagons to customers and pick them up. This makes you an important economic contribution.
  • You take over the head of the train and the responsibility for the safety and punctuality of freight trains in the busy rail network.
  • Your high level of independence helps you to supply the construction sites with the necessary materials on time.
  • You manage complex shunting movements, act independently, collect commercial and operational data in digital systems and ensure customer contact.

You bring that with you.

  • For this demanding work you have to work conscientiously, reliably, in a team and ready in 24-hour shifts.
  • You are physically and psychologically resilient and can act consciously and correctly with your own responsible work.
  • You have at least two years of recognized professional apprenticeship incl. Certificate of ability, ideally a basic craftsmanship, or completed the Matura.
  • In the language of the training location, you are sure to negotiate (level C2) both verbally and in writing.
  • You have a perfect color sense (no red / green weakness).
  • In the application process, we will ask you for a current criminal record (not older than 3 months). The document can be ordered at the invitation for personal acquaintance.

Your rewards and benefits.

Classification during secondary education shall be based on function and age. More about the concrete reward for this role can be found under Quereinstieg.
