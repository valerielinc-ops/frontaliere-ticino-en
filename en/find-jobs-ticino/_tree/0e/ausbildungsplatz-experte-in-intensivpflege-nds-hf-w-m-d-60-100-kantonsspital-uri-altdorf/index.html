Full description

Dive into the exciting world of intensive care and shape your professional future with us. In the Cantonal Hospital Uri, one of the largest employers in the region, a dynamic and future-oriented team awaits you. With our comprehensive range of services, we secure outpatient and inpatient extended basic care for the canton of Uri and attach the highest importance to quality patient care. Start of studies: in February and September each time, with employment 3 to 4 months before the start of studies. Duration of studies: Two years at a 100% time. Become part of our team and actively contribute to securing health care in the Uri region at the highest level. YOUR TASKS

  • Providing high quality safe care in a recognised intensive care unit
  • Use of research-based, care-related, medical and pharmacological expertise
  • Independent and responsible action, implementation of interventions in delegation and cooperation with specialists
  • Monitoring and competent care of patients in highly complex disease states and in different age groups
  • Assistance in intensive medical interventions
  • Granting of intensive monitoring - before or after operational interventions
  • Carrying out pain therapies
  • Initiation and implementation of emergency and resuscitation measures
  • Monitoring of the use and control of medical devices
  • Care and care of ventilated patients OUR REQUIREMENTS
  • Degree in care: You have a degree as a dipl. Care specialist HF / dipl. Nursing professional HF, a Bachelor of Science in Nursing FH or a foreign diploma in nursing recognised as equivalent by the competent authority
  • Professional experience: You can have a professional experience of at least 6 months (with 100% employment) in the acute care area of a hospital
  • Professional and personal competences: Resilience, flexibility and a high level of professional, personal and social competence distinguish you
  • Quick comprehension: You have the ability to quickly familiarize yourself with new situations and show understanding for people in extraordinary situations.
  • Communication Strength: Professional and empathetic communication is a matter of course for you your personalities
  • Individual support: Our dedicated vocational trainers accompany you competently and individually through your entire training period.
  • Diverse practical experience: You become part of a comprehensive range of services on our interdisciplinary station and gain valuable practical experience.
  • Broad range of learning opportunities: We organize a varied range of learning opportunities with insights into various subject areas.
  • Achieving your goals: We offer you a working environment in which you can learn and achieve all qualification goals.
  • 1:1 learning support: Your development is the focus – we plan targeted 1:1 learning support in order to promote you optimally.
  • Further education opportunities: Benefit from internal training and transfer lessons to deepen your knowledge and apply it in practice. Personal and professional development: We support you in developing yourself not only professionally, but also personally.
  • External internships at larger, renowned hospitals: You get the opportunity to get to know the advanced competencies of a center hospital, apply your learned knowledge in an extended setting and consolidate your professional skills. Our training manager NDS Intensivpflege, Janine Lischer, is happy to provide you with further information at janine.lischer@ksuri.ch. We look forward to your application.
