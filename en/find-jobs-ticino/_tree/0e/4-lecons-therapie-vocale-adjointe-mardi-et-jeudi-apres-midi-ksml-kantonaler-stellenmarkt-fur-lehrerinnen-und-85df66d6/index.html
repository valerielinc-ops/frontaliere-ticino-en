Portrait We are a district school in the district of Bümpliz in Bern. Our vision is to make school a safe place – a vision we have been pursuing consistently for five years. As a college, we have dealt with topics such as social and emotional learning, bullying, dealing with cross-border situations and the peacemaker approach. During the school year, we assess grade-free and attach great importance to support-oriented feedback. Through structured cooperation vessels and regular college events, we also strengthen cohesion at our school
