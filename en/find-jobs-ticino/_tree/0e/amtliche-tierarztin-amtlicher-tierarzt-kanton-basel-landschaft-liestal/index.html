Full description

Animal health and food safety Directorate of Economics and Health Food Safety and Veterinary Office Liestal 1 January 2027 or by agreement

Your responsibility

  • In this function, you actively contribute to maintaining the health status of the Basel bidder animal population.
  • They independently handle the implementation of the monitoring programmes and help with the handling of animal diseases as well as imports and exports of animals.
  • They carry out controls and independently record control results, orders and criminal complaints and are responsible for the dossier management of the cases assigned to you.
  • They communicate with farmers, animal owners, veterinarians, key stakeholders as well as other cantonal authorities and federal agencies.
  • You contribute your experience in emergency planning in the field of animal diseases.
  • In a small way, our team supports you with the slaughter animal and meat control in the Basel bidder slaughterhouses.

Your background

  • Completed study of veterinary medicine with a federal or recognised diploma
  • Certificate of competence as official veterinarian or willingness to acquire it
  • Veterinary enforcement experience and good knowledge of animal diseases, control and prevention
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and working with a high practical relevance
  • Safe appearance and strong communication ability in dealing with animal owners, professionals and authorities
  • Responsible, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Category B driving licence
