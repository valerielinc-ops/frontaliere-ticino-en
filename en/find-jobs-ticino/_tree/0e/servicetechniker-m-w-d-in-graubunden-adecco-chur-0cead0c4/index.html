Full description

Introduction

Our customer is one of the world’s leading manufacturers of automatic doors for passenger passage and has extensive technological and commercial know-how. The company is part of an international group and has its headquarters in Switzerland and specializes in the development, production, distribution, installation and maintenance of automatic doors. For the region of Graubünden, we are looking for a personality who wants to develop as a service technician or has already gained initial experience. Interested?, then apply today!

Tasks

  • You plan and carry out service missions independently.
  • Timely maintenance and maintenance of door systems are in your hands.
  • Fix faults and carry out repairs? You can do that easily.
  • You know the end users expertly about the use and ensure a smooth operation.
  • You take care of piketting services and are available to our customers in urgent cases.
  • You support our installers in the installation of new equipment.

Profile

  • Completed training as an electrical installer, electrical installer, assembly electrician, electromechanic, automotive mechanic, electronics technician or a similar qualification, but also craftsman-skilled lateral entrants are welcome.
  • In-depth knowledge of electrics and mechanics - this is your area of expertise.
  • Ideally, you have already gained professional experience in your learned profession or as a service technician
  • Residence in the Graubünden region or ready to move
  • An independent, flexible and resilient personality with good organizational ability.
  • Driving licence Cat. B

Advantages

  • A varied and exciting activity.
  • Attractive social benefits.
  • A company vehicle for your missions.
  • Support for your professional development.
  • A modern, pleasant working environment.
