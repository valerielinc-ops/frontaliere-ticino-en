Full description

Your drive The heart of BVZ Holding is the railway company Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn and the international top brands Gornergrat Bahn and Glacier Express. Passionate and qualified employees form the basis of our success. Every day, around 700 employees are committed to offering our guests a first-class experience. We live diversity and have one thing in common: Emotions are our drive. What is your drive? Your field of activity

  • With your technical know-how, you ensure that our train and trailer vehicles are on the road reliably
  • Maintenance, service and repair services of modern rolling stock
  • Analysis, diagnosis and correction of electrical, pneumatic and mechanical faults
  • Cooperation in technical improvements and optimizations

Your profile

  • Completed apprenticeship as an automatic technician, electrician, electronics engineer, polymechanic or in a related technical profession
  • Enjoyment of technology as well as interest in modern vehicle and railway technology
  • Knowledge of electrical engineering, mechanics or pneumatics – Train knowledge is a plus, but not a must
  • Motivation to learn new things and develop professionally
  • Readiness for early and late shifts as well as for cooperation in the picket and track team
  • Ticket Cat. B
  • Independent, reliable and team-oriented working method

Your advantages

  • Internal and external training as well as development opportunities towards specialist or team responsibility
  • Opportunity to develop yourself into a vocational trainer for automatists, actively accompany learners and shape the next generation
  • Exciting technical environment with modern railway technology in a motivated, collegial and family working environment
  • Attractive benefits such as GA for you and perks for your family as well as 5 weeks holidays

Requirements

  • Abgeschlossene Berufslehre als Automatiker, Elektriker, Elektroniker, Polymechaniker oder in einem verwandten technischen Beruf

  • Freude an Technik sowie Interesse an moderner Fahrzeug- und Bahntechnik

  • Kenntnisse in Elektrotechnik, Mechanik oder Pneumatik – Bahnkenntnisse sind ein Plus, aber kein Muss

  • Motivation, Neues zu lernen und dich fachlich weiterzuentwickeln

  • Bereitschaft für Früh- und Spätschichten sowie zur Mitarbeit im Pikett- und Aufgleisteam

  • Fahrausweis Kat. B

  • Selbständige, zuverlässige und teamorientierte Arbeitsweise
