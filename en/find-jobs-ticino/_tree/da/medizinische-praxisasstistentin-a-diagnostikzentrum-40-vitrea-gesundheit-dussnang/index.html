Medical practitioner (a) Diagnostic Center 40 % Place of work: Dussnang rehabilitation clinic Where your job is doing rehab.

State-of-the-art robotic therapy for musculoskeletal and geriatric rehabilitation, surrounded by beautiful parkland. The first gerontotraumatological rehabilitation department in Switzerland. The environment at the Dussnang rehabilitation clinic also benefits the approximately 270 employees. Here you will be personally appreciated and get more professional skills. Take on a key role in the team and in the in-depth accompaniment to therapy success.

You can look forward to this

  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
