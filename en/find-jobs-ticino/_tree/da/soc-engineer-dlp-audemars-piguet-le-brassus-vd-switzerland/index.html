Full description

Within Cyber Security, you will be directly linked to Cyber Security Operations Manager. You will play a key role in protecting the company's information systems and data.

In this context, your activities are

  • Pilot the life cycle of security incidents (detection, response and recovery).
  • Ensure governance of Security Operations Center (SOC) tools, processes and procedures.
  • Define, deploy and improve detection rules and security alerts.
  • Supervise and support SOC Level 1 and 2 activities carried out by our service provider.
  • Identify root causes of incidents and manage corrective actions.
  • Participate in a level 3 SOC periodicity service and provide support for critical escalations.

Specialization Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

  • Provide level 3 DLP incident analysis and management.
  • Deploy, maintain and optimize rules, procedures, playbooks, runbooks and DLP usage cases.
  • Work with security engineering teams to integrate and improve DLP controls.
  • Automate detection, investigation and response processes.
  • Coordinate escalation and management of major data leak incidents.
  • Contribute to data protection projects and insider risk management.

Additional experience appreciated

  • Security Orchestration, Automation and Response (SOAR).
  • Artificial intelligence applied to security operations.
  • More than 6 years of experience in managing security incidents and responding to critical incidents.
  • Mastery of SIEM, XDR and DLP solutions, with the ability to define detection rules and alerts.
  • Confirmed experience in threat detection, investigation and continuous improvement of SOC capabilities.
  • Strong knowledge of cyber security response procedures.
  • Data Loss Prevention (DLP) experience, including major incident management and collaboration with relevant stakeholders.
  • Knowledge of automation of security operations and/or secure use of artificial intelligence.
  • Excellent communication capabilities in English and French in an international environment.

What we offer

  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual holiday weeks and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The ability to work remotely depending on function
  • A business restaurant
  • An advantageous PBL
