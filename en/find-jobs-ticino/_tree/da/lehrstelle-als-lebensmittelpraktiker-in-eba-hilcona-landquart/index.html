Full description

Tackle, think and produce high-quality food! You like to work practically, are reliable and want to experience how food is produced? As a food practitioner you are an important part of our production team. You will support with various work steps and learn how our products are produced safely and carefully. During your two-year training, you will gain valuable practical experience. You will be introduced step by step into your tasks and accompanied professionally.

  • Contract type: apprenticeship
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Working time model: Flexible time
  • Job opening: 01.08.2027
  • Language: German
  • Location: Hilcona AG, Riedlöserstrasse 7 7302 Landquart

Tasks

  • You support in the preparation and processing of raw materials
  • You operate production and packaging plants according to instructions
  • You control products and packaging for completeness and quality
  • You perform simple work steps in food production
  • You help pack, label and provide the products
  • You keep your workplace clean and tidy
  • You observe hygiene, safety and quality regulations
  • You work in a team and support your colleagues in day-to-day business
  • You learn to increasingly carry out workflows independently

Requirements

  • You like to work practically and move
  • You are reliable, motivated and willing to learn
  • You enjoy food and varied tasks
  • You work carefully and adhere to guidelines
  • You are physically resilient and like to participate
  • You like working in a team
  • You treat other people respectfully
  • You want to develop personally and professionally

This is what we offer

  • Work-life balance: With us you benefit from mind. 5 weeks holidays as well as 15 paid holidays and optional up to 6 bonus days. We have no business holidays throughout Hilcona. You share your days off with your supervisor and apprentice responsible.
  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: special discounts for our trainees at your lunch in our staff restaurant, free fruits and promotions such as free maroni, ice cream or smoothies. In our outlet, high-quality products at greatly reduced prices await you.
  • Mobility: Benefit from convenient connections to public transport (bus stop directly in front of the Hilcona), attractive subsidies for tickets, sufficient parking for cars, e-bikes, scooters and a tool pillar for bicycles, should there be a breakdown – sustainable and worry-free on the road! Your travel costs to the vocational school will also be covered by us.
  • Professional development opportunities: Your school costs are covered by the company Hilcona. For your own laptop you get a grant of CHF 300. After your successful education, great career opportunities and development opportunities await you at Hilcona. By the way, if you reach the grade 5.0 or higher on your final exam, you will receive an additional bonus at the Schaan location.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management, our company doctor, rewards for reliability, exclusive employee discounts in Liechtenstein, Switzerland and Vorarlberg as well as promoted fitness subscriptions – for health and leisure fun!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments at our learning events such as the meeting days with all learners at the beginning of your training, our three days of experience in autumn or various workshops during your training – celebrate together and make contacts!
