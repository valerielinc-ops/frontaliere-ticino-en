Full description

Portrait Portrait of Our School

At Hasliberg we accompany children from kindergarten until the end of primary school. Our manageable school offers personal encounters, short distances and a mountain world on the doorstep that invites you to discover and learn.

We want a school where children like to learn, trust themselves and take responsibility for themselves and others. This includes hero training. We practice trusting together, strengthen social skills and learn to tackle conflicts together. Our principles accompany us through everyday school life:

We trust each other. We help each other. We work together. We're having fun.

Our previous class teacher Isabelle Berchtold is also headmaster. From the summer of 2027, she wants to strengthen her

  • Devote tasks to school management. That is why she is looking for a suitable personality to whom she can entrust her class with a good feeling. As headmaster, she remains at the school and accompanies the handover.

Tasks

You take on the class responsibility for our age-mixed 1st / 2nd class and accompany the children through their first years of school.

You will design a lively, carefully constructed lesson according to curriculum 21 and strengthen the basics of reading, writing and mathematics.

You recognize different learning requirements, promote the children specifically and make their progress visible.

You lead your class with warmth, clarity and reliable structures.

You implement hero training in everyday life and empower the children to learn and take responsibility together.

You maintain an open cooperation with parents, specialist and IF teachers as well as the school management and help shape our school life.

Requirements

This is what you bring

You have a recognised teaching diploma for the appropriate level and enjoy teaching a 1st / 2nd class.

We want a personality that...

Take children seriously: You listen, recognize strengths and stay turned even in challenging moments.

Clarity: You formulate understandable expectations and act calmly and consistently.

Enthusiasm arouses: You bring ideas and enjoy small advances as well as big breakthroughs.

Brings humor: With you can be laughed, tried out and even restarted.

Takes responsibility: You work independently, organize yourself reliably and get support when you need it.

Collaboration lives: You talk about things openly, think along and look for solutions together.

You are ready to engage in hero training and support our common pedagogical attitude.

We offer

This is what we offer you

A personal school where you can participate and contribute your own ideas.

Collaboration with a dedicated team and the professionals involved.

A careful handover by the previous class teacher.

A school manager who knows the class and the everyday lesson from his own experience.

A shared school program with hero training.

A natural environment with many opportunities for teaching and shared experiences.

Conditions of employment according to the cantonal requirements of the Canton of Bern.

Let's get to know each other!

Do you want to read with children, count, wonder, laugh and grow beyond yourself? Then we look forward to your application and to getting to know you.
