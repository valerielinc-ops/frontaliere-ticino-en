Full description

At the heart of the forest division of the Directorate-General for the Environment (DGE-Forêt), you play a key role in the sustainable management of protective forests, which now represent one fifth of the canton's forest areas. You are actively contributing to the protection of the population and property, providing one of the main functions in the management and development of the Vaudian forest. You are working with the newly created "Natural Hazards and Protected Forests" team to implement integrated risk management for natural hazards under forest law and in the area of forest protection. Deputy Head of Section, you support his mission, particularly in the areas of protective forests and forest protection. In addition, you ensure professional support for boroughs and triage, notably through advice, training, expertise and the development of good practice in the field. Finally, you participate in an interdisciplinary way in the daily work of the DGE-Forêt and ensure internal and external coordination with the topics dealt with by the division. For further information, the specifications are available here . For more technical details you can contact Mr Sébastien Lévy, Head of the Natural Hazards section at the following telephone number: 021 316 61 59

  • Ensure the sustainable management of the canton's protective forests and its integration into relevant policies, legal bases, directives and activities, particularly in the face of climate change and forest-giber issues.
  • Pilot the partial programme "Protective forests" of the Forest Programme Convention, ensuring the achievement of objectives, monitoring of resources and reporting
  • Pilot the coordination and cantonal representation in the field of protective forests.
  • Oversee forest protection strategies against biotic and abiotic damage and the implementation of related measures.
  • Training in forest engineering ETH or a Master EPF/UNI with specialization in forest management or natural hazards, or title deemed equivalent.
  • Professional experience of 10 years or more in a study office or administration, with financial and project management responsibilities. Knowledge of the Vaudois and Switzerland forest context is an asset.
  • Integration and collaboration skills, team spirit and oriented posture seek solutions.
  • Very good writing and communication skills, perfect mastery of French, oral and written. Good knowledge of German, spoken and written, is an asset.
  • Self-control and ability to maintain calm in the face of crisis situations generated by natural events.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
