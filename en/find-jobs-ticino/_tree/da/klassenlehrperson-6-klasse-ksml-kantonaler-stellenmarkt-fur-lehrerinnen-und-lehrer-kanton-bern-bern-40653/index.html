Full description

Portrait The school Manuel is a managed quarter school (Schulkreis Kirchenfeld-Schosshalde) of the city of Bern. From the summer of 2026, the school will lead 46 classes from kindergarten to 9. School year with about 900 children and adolescents and 130 employees. The school location Manuel includes the school complex Manuel, the Wittigkofen School House, the Elfenau Quarter School House, the Kirchenfeld full-day base level and day care.

Tasks

As a class teacher in a 6th grade, you teach 20 lessons per week in the subjects NMG, Mathematics, German and Visual Design and bear the overall educational responsibility for the class. The teaching days will be Mondays from 7.30 a.m. to 11.50 a.m. and 1.45 p.m. to 3.20 p.m., Wednesday, 7.30 a.m. to 11.50 a.m., Thursday from 9.10 a.m. to 11.50 a.m. and Friday from 8.20 a.m. to 11.50 a.m. and 1.45 p.m. to 3.20 p.m.

With pleasure, high level of expertise and tangible commitment, you design a varied, motivating lesson. They accompany the students attentively in their personal and school development and create learning spaces in which curiosity, trust and learning pleasure can grow. With your clear attitude, you create a safe learning atmosphere.

The close, appreciative cooperation with the class team as well as with the parents is a matter of course for you and is experienced by you as an enrichment. With openness and creativity, you are actively involved in school life, participate in projects and events and contribute actively to the further development of our school.

Requirements

You have a seminary primary teaching patent or a bachelor's degree for the primary level and ideally bring experience in cycle 2.

They are enthusiastic about the teaching profession, have pedagogical skills and work with joy and commitment with children.

As a committed, communicative and resilient personality, you consistently put students at the center of your actions.

They value teamwork and professional exchange and actively and constructively contribute to the College.

They also show willingness to actively participate in school life, especially at the obligatory conferences on Monday from 16.45 to 18.00.

We offer

We offer an attractive amount of 20 lessons, in addition, the class teaching function is paid at 5%. The office is until 31.07. 2027, but an extension would be possible.

She expects an appreciative, collegial working atmosphere.

Our idyllic schoolhouse is located in a lively Bernese quarter.

In addition, you benefit from very good connections to public transport as well as parking facilities in the immediate vicinity.
