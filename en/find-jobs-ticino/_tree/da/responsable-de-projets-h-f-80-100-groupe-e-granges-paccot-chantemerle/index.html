Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2,250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Thanks to efficient and innovative solutions, the Celsius Directorate specialises in the production, supply and distribution of renewable heat and natural gas, the company now operates some 50 remote heating networks.

Mission

  • Lead strategic and cross-cutting projects, from scoping to implementation, ensuring that objectives, timelines, resources and budgets are met
  • Supporting organizational transformations and leading change, coordinating different stakeholders
  • Assessing and implementing growth and development opportunities, including through analysis and business plans to support decision-making
  • Piloting the deployment and optimization of management and steering tools, including the ERP and dashboards, by accompanying users
  • Identify and implement opportunities for innovation and continuous improvement to optimize processes, performance and value creation

Profile

  • Higher education (HEG, HES or equivalent), ideally supplemented by training or certification in project management (PMI or equivalent)
  • Confirmed experience in strategic, cross-cutting or transformational project management and multi-stakeholder coordination
  • Good knowledge in economics and business management, with experience in business development, process improvement or project portfolio management
  • Mastery of the driving tools and of Excel, as well as good knowledge of ERP environments, ideally Microsoft Dynamics 365
  • Excellent mastery of French and good knowledge of German
  • English is an asset

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • Social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together for tomorrow's energy"
