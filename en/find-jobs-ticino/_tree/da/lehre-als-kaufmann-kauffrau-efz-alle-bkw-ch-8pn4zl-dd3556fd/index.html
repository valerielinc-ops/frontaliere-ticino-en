Your field of activity

Hinni offers an apprenticeship with the two compulsory elective areas second foreign language (with or without vocational training) or individual project work. In the 3rd year you have the opportunity to deepen different competences (communication in German or French, finance, technologies). As a merchant or merchant, you independently perform a wide range of administrative and organizational tasks. You will gain insight into various departments such as back office, purchasing, financial accounting and marketing. The variety of these areas of activity guarantees you an exciting and challenging teaching, during which motivated and professional practical and vocational trainees accompany you. Together with your team, you take care of the concerns of our employees, applicants, customers and suppliers. So no day is like the other and you are always challenged.

Hinni AG is a successful service and production company. We have been dealing with fire hydrants and the water pipe network since its foundation.
