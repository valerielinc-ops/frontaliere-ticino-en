Full description

As part of our recruitment activities, we are looking for a qualified, motivated and experienced (M/F) sanitary assembler for one of our clients.

Your missions

  • Installation and assembly of sanitary equipment.
  • Installation and connection of hot water, cold water and drainage pipes.
  • Installation of toilets, washbasins, showers and bathtubs.
  • Reading and interpreting technical plans.
  • New renovation and construction work.
  • Troubleshooting, maintenance and repair of sanitary facilities.
  • Sealing and compliance with safety standards.

Your profile

  • Medical installer CFC or equivalent training.
  • Professional experience in health.
  • Good control of assembly and connection techniques.
  • Ability to work independently and as a team.
  • Serious, organized, ad hoc and motivated person.
  • Driver's licence category B: an asset.

We offer you

  • Various missions to our partner companies.
  • A professional working environment.
  • Conditions of engagement depending on position and experience.
  • Personalized support throughout the recruitment process.
