Full description

Bracco is an international Group active in the healthcare sector and a leader in diagnostic imaging. The Group’s main products are contrast agents sold in over 100 countries, both directly and indirectly through branches, joint ventures and license and distribution agreements.

About the Role

We are looking for a Medical Device Specialist to support the development of our ACIST Medical devices (Injector, IVUS and FFR) business across all of Switzerland. In this field-based role, you will combine commercial, clinical, and technical expertise to promote innovative medical devices and support healthcare professionals in optimizing their use. You will work closely with Cardiologists, nurses, biomedical engineers, and hospital decision-makers, providing product demonstrations, application support, training, and commercial guidance. Key Activities Sales & Business Development

  • Promote Bracco Diagnostic Imaging systems and related medical devices
  • Develop and maintain strong relationships with hospitals, clinics, and healthcare professionals
  • Identify customer needs and propose tailored solutions
  • Negotiate and support equipment sales opportunities
  • Monitor market trends and competitor activities within the assigned territory
  • Develop and execute territory action plans aligned with company objectives.

Clinical & Application Support

  • Provide product demonstrations and application training to healthcare professionals
  • Explain product features, benefits, and clinical applications
  • Support customers during installations and implementation phases
  • Ensure optimal utilization of Bracco Medical Devices
  • Deliver scientific, technical, and product-related information to customers.

Territory & Customer Management

  • Maintain accurate customer records and activity reports using Salesforce.com
  • Ensure regular follow-up of the installed equipment base
  • Collaborate closely with Marketing, Sales, and Technical Service teams
  • Participate in scientific meetings, congresses, and customer events
  • Manage territory activities in compliance with company policies and regulatory requirements.

Education and Prior Experience

  • Degree in Biomedical Engineering, Radiology Technology, Cardiology (Cath Lab), Life Sciences, or equivalent experience
  • Experience in medical device sales, applications, or clinical support preferred
  • Knowledge of Cardiology workflows and diagnostic imaging environments is an advantage
  • Strong communication and presentation skills
  • Commercial mindset combined with customer-focused approach
  • Ability to work independently and manage territory effectively
  • Good computer skills, including MS Office and CRM systems.

Languages

  • German: fluent (mandatory)
  • English: required
  • French: an advantage

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
