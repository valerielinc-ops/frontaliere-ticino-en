Full description

GP medicine is at the heart of an efficient healthcare system.

Especially in rural areas, it means closeness to people, trust and lasting relationships.

The Centro Medico Valposchiavo (CMVP), a practice community of three family doctors,

in close cooperation with the Centro Sanitario Valposchiavo – Hospital, Spitex

and nursing home – offers a modern, innovative and sustainable model of primary care.

The doctors of the CMVP provide outpatient care, are responsible for the

Treatment of patients who are admitted to hospital in San Sisto and provide

emergency care in the valley. This attractive model combines a high degree of

Responsibility with comprehensive professional autonomy.

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement:

Specialist for internal medicine (FMH)

who is committed and wants to contribute together with us concretely to the further development of general medicine in a rural region. Knowledge of Italian is required.

In Valposchiavo you can expect a stimulating working environment, a warm community and a high quality of life in the middle of a picturesque southern Alpine landscape.

The valley also offers a lively cultural landscape combining Swiss tradition and Mediterranean lifestyle.
