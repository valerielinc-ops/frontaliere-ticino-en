More about this search for cross-border applicants

Bern hosts a compact but growing product management ecosystem, with opportunities concentrated in fintech, health tech, and federal digital services. The city offers a lower cost of living than Zurich and Geneva, though salaries for product roles typically run 10-15% below those markets. English is widely spoken in tech teams, but German proficiency accelerates advancement. The labor market favors candidates with B2B SaaS experience and regulatory domain knowledge. Commuting from Italy is impractical given the distance and Alpine geography; this market primarily serves local residents and those relocating within Switzerland.
