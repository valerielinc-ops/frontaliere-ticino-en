Put to the Point

As an experienced leader, you play a key role in the Swiss Army’s cyber defense. You will lead a highly specialized team, be responsible for handling complex cyber incidents and actively shape the further development of CSIRT and cyberdefence.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20833
  • Location: Papiermühlestrasse 20, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Lead a competent team of cyber security experts in organisational, personnel and professional terms
  • Ensure the operational planning of the analysts for the permanent service, as well as for missions of the army
  • Planning and further development of the resources used as well as the permanent realignment of employees to the current threats ensure
  • Ensure full representation of the head of the Cyber Fusion Center during his/her absence
  • Responsible for, planning and coordinating threat hunting on the ICT infrastructure of the VBS to identify anomalies or unrecognized threats in the network
  • Using Armasuisse’s Cyber Defence Campus and the Cyber and Electromagnetic Actions (CEA) Department to research prototypes to develop new detection methods and identify new directions in research

This makes you unique

  • Graduate degree in computer science, information security or engineering
  • Additional training in cybersecurity (CAS, MAS) is beneficial
  • Several years of leadership experience of interdisciplinary teams in an agile environment as well as profound experience in project and process management and in agile methods
  • Officer training and multi-year military leadership experience are a plus, but not a condition
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and passive knowledge of a third official language

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization.
