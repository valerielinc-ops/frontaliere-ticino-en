Full description

Klinik Im Hasel AG is a psychiatric-psychotherapeutic specialist clinic with a cantonal service mandate for the treatment of addiction and trauma disorders. As a health insurance-approved institution and part of the cantonal hospital list, we offer high-quality, evidence-based and human-oriented treatment for our patients. With around 170 dedicated employees at three locations – our inpatient clinic in Gontenschwil, the ambulatorium and the day clinic in Lenzburg and the trauma-specific ambulatorium in Baden – we combine professional competence with an appreciative and interdisciplinary cooperation.

Assistant doctor:in Baden (m/f/d), 80 - 100%

At our location in Baden, we are building up a daily clinical, trauma-specific treatment offer. For this we are looking for an assistant doctor as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement.

Your tasks

  • Psychiatric-psychotherapeutic care of patients:women who are in daily clinical and outpatient trauma-specific treatment
  • Indication and periodic examination of psychological psychotherapies in the arrangement model
  • Regular case meetings with psychotherapists:
  • Participation in the implementation of advanced innovative treatment concepts
  • Further development and implementation of the integrative therapy concept

Your profile

  • MEBEKO recognition is a mandatory requirement
  • Multi-year medical and psychiatric experience
  • Experience in the field of trauma follow-up disorders is advantageous
  • Team capable, open, resilient and creative personality
  • Good organisational skills
  • We expect a basic attitude that is characterized by appreciation and respect in dealing with our patients.

Our offer

  • We are a recognized training institution for the focus title Psychiatry and Psychotherapy of Dependency Diseases Cat. A (2 years, eligible for the title of specialist psychiatry and psychotherapy)
  • Regulated working hours as well as a 42-hour week with a 100% fee
  • Self-employed and responsible activities
  • Dedicated and established team
  • Further education opportunities and employee benefits
  • Well-developed occupational health management
