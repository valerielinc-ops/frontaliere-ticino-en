Full description

Careers that change lives start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life. Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact. A Day in the Life At Medtronic, you can expect a versatile area of responsibility in an internationally successful medical technology group with long-term training and development opportunities.

We offer you a position in which you are in a growing environment in the Business Unit Neurovascular as Sales Repsentative.

This position includes the Bern Geneva and Lausanne region and is therefore a remote position. Remote means that your home address is considered the primary place of work (home office). Meetings may occasionally require your presence in the Medtronic Office. Responsibilities include:

  • Build, maintain and strengthen long-term relationships with diverse stakeholders such as medical and non-medical professionals
  • Responsibility for sales in the sales region, identification of growth opportunities and development of commercial solutions
  • Advising, training and supporting medical professionals regarding our products and solutions for everyday clinical use
  • Conduct of negotiations with medical staff, clinic administration and purchasing department
  • Active participation in fairs and congresses

Required knowledge and experience

  • Professional experience in the medical sector alternatively a bachelor/master degree in medical technology, medicine, business administration with particular interest in sales
  • Residence within the assisted area and readiness for regular business trips (with overnight stay if applicable)
  • Enjoying intensive exchange with diverse groups of people, strong customer and service orientation as well as strong communication skills and empathy
  • Self-initiative, high motivation and a can-do mentality
  • Entrepreneurial mindset and results orientation
  • Creativity and openness to change
  • Very good knowledge of German, French and English, both oral and written. Knowledge of Italian is desirable and beneficial.
  • Ideally, knowledge in interventional neuroradiology or in the distribution of medical devices
