Bewirtschaftung Geneva

You are open-minded, you have ambition and use a forward-looking and leading-edge method of work in the real estate sector is important to you? At Livit, you meet the expectations of owners and tenants with exemplary customer orientation. You contribute to a lasting relationship of trust with customers and to a positive image of the company. Ready to meet the challenges of today and tomorrow and move things around in the real estate world? You're in the right place at Livit's. We look forward to your personal contribution.

Aufgaben deine

  • Active and comprehensive management of a portfolio of buildings
  • Status of places of entry and exit when developing and changing tenants
  • Conduct and continuing training of subordinate assistant(s) (including setting of annual targets and monitoring of their achievement)
  • Personal and telephone supervision of tenants
  • Maintain the building in accordance with the management contract
  • Periodic inspection of land with corresponding status reports
  • Make lease changes and adjust rents and indexed rents
  • Supervision of the current rent policy and advice to the owner in establishing market rents
  • Control and visa of invoices payable, accounts of stewardship and incidental costs

Dein Profile

  • Business learning with CFC or equivalent training
  • Federal building manager (obtained or in progress) or continuing training in real estate
  • Several years of professional experience (3-5 years) in real estate management
  • Good knowledge of accounting and law (lease law, CO, CC, LP)
  • Mastery of the French language in oral and writing with good editorial qualities
  • Digital affinity and ability to participate actively in change
  • Strong customer orientation and partnership relationship management
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and action with skill
