Location: Corporate Office | Home Office

Occupation by: by agreement

Hirslanden stands for first-class, responsible and individual health care that puts people first. From birth to old age as well as from prevention to healing – at any time, physically and digitally – the Hirslanden Group is concentrating on the further development of the “Continuum of Care” together with private and public partners.

Our focus is not only on the well-being of our patients, but also on that of our employees.

YOUR TASKS

  • Ensuring first-class and personal VIP IT support for the Corporate Management, Directors and other Hirslanden Group executives
  • Central contact person for all ICT concerns of our VIP users as well as independent processing of requests and disruptions
  • Consulting, configuration and support of Windows and Mac clients, Microsoft 365, Citrix, VPN and other modern workplace services
  • Rapid, personal and solution-oriented support for technical challenges, especially important meetings, events and business-critical concerns
  • Coordination and management of escalations as well as close cooperation with internal ICT specialists until successful problem solving
  • Personal support and advice for our VIP users on the introduction of new devices, applications and ICT services as well as on arrivals and departures
  • Professional support, support and promotion of our ICT learners (specialist ICT specialist) as well as ensuring practice-oriented training

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training in the field of computer science and ideally further training or certification in the ICT environment
  • Several years of professional experience in VIP IT support or in a comparable function with direct support of managers and demanding stakeholders
  • In-depth knowledge of Microsoft 365, Windows 11, iOS and Modern Workplace environments
  • Pronounced service orientation and a high level of quality awareness with the aspiration to offer first-class service even for complex concerns
  • Excellent communication skills as well as a sovereign, confident and professional appearance
  • High degree of discretion, sensitivity and diplomatic skill in dealing with confidential information and different stakeholders
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in words and writing, knowledge of French is advantageous
