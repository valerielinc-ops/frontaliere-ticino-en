Full description

Brislach effective immediately (or by agreement) until 31 July 2027

Your responsibilities

  • You are open, motivated, creative and cheerful.
  • You value close interdisciplinary collaboration within the pedagogical team.
  • You teach in a student-centred and internally differentiated manner.
  • You maintain an appreciative relationship with parents and guardians.
  • You work together with the pedagogical team.
  • You can envisage the following workload: 4 lessons of German as a second language (DaZ) (in two kindergartens) as well as 3 lessons of German as a second language (DaZ) in a primary school class (preferably grade 4).
  • You can identify with our vision "teaching happily together".

Your background

  • You preferably hold the CAS in Diversity-Oriented Language Support in Multilingual Schools (DaZ) or already have teaching experience.
  • You hold a teaching diploma for primary school or for the cycle 1. Or you are studying at least in the 5. Semester of the PH.
  • You preferably already have teaching experience.
  • You value cooperation within the class team and the pedagogical team, as well as the further development of your own teaching.
  • You are an open, creative and motivated teacher who enjoys contributing to a small, close-knit team.
  • You have knowledge of media and computer science or are willing to acquire it.

Your benefits with us

Employees at the centre

Appreciation, respect and reliability shape the way we work together. Because our employees are important to us, we protect and promote their health and ensure safety in the workplace.

Shaping the future for the general public

The annualised working hours model allows you to coordinate your professional and non-work activities according to your personal needs. Flexible working hours and part-time work support individual lifestyles. There are also interesting options for early or partial retirement.

Mobile working

Some work can be carried out from home or another location. This increases the flexibility of work organization and your work-life balance.

Generous vacation and leave

All employees are entitled to at least five weeks of vacation. In addition, there is the option of converting the 13th month's salary into additional leave or taking unpaid leave.

Numerous professions in an exciting environment

We are the largest employer in Basel-Landschaft and have employed specialists in a wide variety of professions since 1833. We keep pace with the times and stay on top of digital developments.

Job and social security

The canton offers secure jobs and takes responsibility for its employees. It is also distinguished by solid social benefits.

Centrally located and well connected

The workplace is located in the heart of Basel-Landschaft and has convenient public transport connections. It also offers a wide range of dining options, as well as great leisure and cultural activities for unwinding after work.

Fair and transparent salaries

The canton fulfills its role as a role model and clearly commits to a fair pay policy. We use a transparent and comprehensible pay system. Good performance and dedicated work have a positive impact on individual salary progression.

Fully compensated work

Career start

The high-quality vocational apprenticeships in many different fields provide sound knowledge and open up a wide range of options for a future career. During training to become a professional, we support language stays and participation in elective courses. The annual apprentice camp is a special highlight.

Advancing thanks to a long-term perspective

Our personnel planning is sustainable and designed for the long term. This includes, in particular, promoting the personal and professional development of employees, specialists and managers. Learn more about our employment conditions The Brislach primary level comprises seven primary classes and a two-class kindergarten with around 150 pupils in total, who are taught by 19 committed teachers. We offer you a multifaceted and varied position in a collegial and committed team. Appreciative collaboration is actively practiced at joint school events and in school development. As a teacher of German as a second language, you guide and support several children in their language development. You will be supported by experienced pedagogical teaching teams that empower you in your work and, together with you, promote the children's linguistic and academic progress.
