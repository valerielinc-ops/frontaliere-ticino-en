Full description

Do you have an excellent pen, a keen sense of organization and priorities and show proactivity? You particularly appreciate the rich environments by the diversity of their missions and occupations? Join us at the Directorate-General for Agriculture, Viticulture and Veterinary Affairs (DGAV)! You are the secretariat of the Director General and the General Staff of the DGAV, the organisation's real steering centre. You are the focal point, you facilitate the flow of information, monitor files and ensure the quality of documents and processes. You are also a privileged interlocutor for many internal and external partners. A cross-cutting role, at the heart of exchanges and decisions, where each day brings its set of challenges and where your contribution really makes a difference.

  • To act as the secretariat of the Director General and the General Staff, including the management of the agenda, mail and signatures;
  • Ensure a smooth and targeted flow of information within the DGAV and to the relevant interlocutors;
  • Rereading and quality control of the documents of the Directorate-General and the General Staff, ensuring their accuracy, consistency and editorial quality;
  • Supervise and coordinate the management of the archives of the DGAV, including their payment and disposal in accordance with the procedures in force;
  • To provide the administrative interface with the General Secretariat of the Department, including monitoring of files in SIEL and timeliness;
  • Organize and provide administrative follow-up to Executive Committee meetings and activities (CoDir), draft minutes and follow-up decisions;
  • To provide for the replacement of the Executive Secretary of the DAGRI (Directorate for Agriculture, Viticulture and Structural Improvements). Further information can be found in the specifications by clicking here. Mr Luc Belloy, Head of the Major State, is at your disposal for any questions at 021 316 39 14. We invite you to submit your complete application file (CV, cover letter, diplomas, work certificates) via the Vaud State platform.
  • Holder of an SWC of a trade employee or an equivalent training;
  • To benefit from a confirmed experience of at least 5 years in a similar function;
  • The Federal Certificate of Management Assistant or equivalent training is an asset;
  • Mastering common computer and office tools;
  • Excellent level of French (C2), both oral and written, and an impeccable spelling as well as a knowledge in German (B2), both oral and written.
  • Rigor, accuracy, reliability and good priority management;
  • At ease in relations with interlocutors at all levels in a spirit of collaboration;
  • Autonomy, flexibility and proactivity, as well as the strength of proposals in improving administrative practices;
  • Take a step back from situations and exercise discretion and a strong sense of confidentiality.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
