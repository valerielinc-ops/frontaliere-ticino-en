As a strong operational leader, you bring together a team of 10 people at the service of a demanding building operation and drive projects forward efficiently and pragmatically. Independent, responsive and solution-oriented you turn the unexpected into opportunities to act. Your care, your sense of service and your commitment make you a reliable contact who combines operational efficiency and team spirit. Do you feel addressed? We look forward to your application!

You can make this contribution

  • Lead multidisciplinary teams, plan and organize the service, share resources, set priorities and monitor their correct implementation (Reporting, KPI)
  • Manage the operation and ensure the availability of the infrastructure (renovation, maintenance, maintenance priorities)
  • Guarantor for the safety of persons and property
  • Develop and maintain relationships with internal and external customers
  • Actively submit proposals for cross-cutting projects
  • Deputy Head of Real Estate and Facility Management
  • Participate in the squad piquetting service as part of facility management (evenings, weekends, public holidays)

This makes them unique.

  • Higher education (Federal certificate, HF diploma or equivalent) in building technology, facility management or real estate + very good knowledge of building technology (HVACSE), MCR control systems, building automation (KNX)
  • Recognized training in occupational safety (EKAS) and health protection (EGASA) as well as a certification in fire safety recognized in Geneva
  • At least 5 years of experience leading multidisciplinary teams
  • Inspiring leadership skills, constructive handling of tension or conflict situations, ability to unite the team and carry a strategic vision
  • Service and solution orientation, organizational talent, careful planning, efficient problem solving
  • Very good knowledge of two official languages
  • Knowledge of German at level B2 is desirable. Further language skills are advantageous.
