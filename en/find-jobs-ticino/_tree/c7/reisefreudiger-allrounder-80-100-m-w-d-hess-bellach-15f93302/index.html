Traveling Allrounder (m/f/d | 80 - 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 500 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company.

To complement our global bus LIFE-CYCLE-MANAGEMENT team, we are looking for a skilled and flexible all-rounder.

Are you looking for a new challenge? With us you will find an interesting activity with many different tasks. With your craftsmanship, you ensure that the electric buses of our customers in Switzerland (partly in neighbouring countries) do not stand still and that the passengers arrive at their destination as planned.

Their tasks

  • Performing simple repair work on electric buses at the customer
  • Perform retrofitting and conversion of various systems
  • Accompaniment of suppliers during customer visits
  • Supervision and coordination of series conversions

Our requirements

  • Basic craft training
  • Good language skills in D (F/I beneficial)
  • High organizational ability even in hectic situations
  • Flexibility is expected and offered
  • Self-employed work
  • Willingness to travel at home and abroad (about 20-30% in neighboring countries)
  • Good communication skills

We offer them

  • Interesting and varied permanent position
  • Moving working time
  • Opportunity to work part-time
  • Working in a collegial team with very good cohesion
  • Modern electric vehicle as a business car
