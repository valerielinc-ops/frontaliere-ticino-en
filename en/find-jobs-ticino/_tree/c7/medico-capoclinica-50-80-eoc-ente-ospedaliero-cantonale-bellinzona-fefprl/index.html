Full description

Medico capoclinica - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team at the Mendrisio Regional Hospital (RAMI) we look for a(a) Head doctor (50-80%) Profile: The person we seek works independently and at an interdisciplinary level, promoting the skills required by the function of Capoclinic Doctor.

  • Requirements: FMH Title in Internal Medicine or equivalent title. For doctors with foreign titles, the Federal Commission’s recognition for medical professions MEBEKO is mandatory and its registration in MedReg before the start of the activity. Knowledge of the Italian language (for which we require at least the attainment of level B2) with relative registration to MedReg before the start of the activity. Spirit of initiative; strong relational and communication skills. Organizational capacity and preparation for interdisciplinary collaboration with a view to continuous improvement of department development. Flexibility and positive attitude, pleasure to work in a team and to collaborate transversally. Date of entry: from 1 December 2026 or date to be agreed. Applications and information: Applications, complete with curriculum vitae, motivation letter, copies of certificates of study and work must be submitted by 30 September 2026 only in electronic format through the appropriate online platform (https://www.eoc.ch/Work.html). Applications sent by mail or e-mail, as well as those that do not comply with the requirements, will not be considered. Further information can be requested from the Secretariat of Geriatrics at 091 811 32 36.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
