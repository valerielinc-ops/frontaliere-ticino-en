Full description

What lies ahead

As a Product Owner, you are part of an interdisciplinary team that supports the Mobilière in its development in the market of Vision through ambitious projects and agile methods.

What you can do with us

As a Product Owner, you link business needs, user expectations and implementation teams.

In collaboration with the professional experts and the Product Manager, you help define the vision of the solutions and prioritize the backlog according to the value and capabilities of the team. You analyze the needs, develop the solution scenarios and ensure their translation into clear requirements for Scrum teams.

Ensuring consistency between business objectives and the developments achieved, you lead priorities, participate in the planning of Program Increments and sprints, and ensure that the objectives are achieved. You also support the production of solutions, the validation of deliverables and the conduct of change to maximize the value delivered to users.

What you bring

  • University training in business economy, management information technology or training deemed equivalent
  • Experience in Business Analysis and/or Product Ownership, including application management and prioritization
  • Excellent ability to analyze needs, structure requirements and translate them into solutions of high business value
  • Strong customer orientation, synthesis, autonomy and solution orientation, with a capacity to balance business challenges, technical feasibility and added value
  • High interest in new technologies and digital transformation as well as excellent relational capabilities and ease in collaboration with trades, IT and stakeholders
  • Mastery of French and/or German, with very good knowledge of English

Working at home

The Business Transformation Private Planning department is responsible for the conduct and implementation of projects of the Planning. It is composed of dynamic, diverse teams from Business Analysts and project managers working together in a collegial atmosphere.

Your occupancy rate is negotiable from 80% with access to flexible working time models. Below you will learn more about our commitment as an employer.

What we offer

Balance of working life - privacy

We apply flexible work models and allow 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. We also offer above-average family allowances and a sustainable pension fund.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of in-house courses and allow you to participate in possible programs abroad. We contribute generously to external continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, half-rate subscription, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.
