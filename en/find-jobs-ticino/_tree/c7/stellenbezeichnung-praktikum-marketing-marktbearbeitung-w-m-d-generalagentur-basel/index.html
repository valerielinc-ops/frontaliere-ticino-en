This is what awaits you

In this internship, you will be part of the team from your first day, which is responsible for the conception and implementation of the marketing and market development measures of the General Agency Basel.

This is what you do with us

The focus of the position is on supporting the Marketing & Market Development team. You support the team in the organization and administration.

In the implementation of exciting projects and in the administration of our market development measures you actively help.

You are creative, work with numbers, have your own areas of responsibility and support in the areas of sponsorship, advertising and events.

This is what you bring with you

  • Certificate of secondary education or business secondary education
  • Reliable working methods and willingness to learn new things
  • Open-minded, curious and helpful personality
  • Interest in the Insurance World

Working with us

Not only is there a varied field of work waiting for you, but also a diverse and well-established team that looks forward to accompanying you during your one-year internship.

Your workplace is located at our modern location near the SBB train station and in our City Lounge in the heart of Basel.

Your assignment starts on 01.08.2027 and the workload is 80-100%.
