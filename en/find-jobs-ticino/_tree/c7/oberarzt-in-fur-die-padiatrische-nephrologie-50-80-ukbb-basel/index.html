Full description

Pediatric nephrology at the UKBB is part of a Basel-Bern-Ticino network. It offers the full range of outpatient and inpatient treatments for pediatric nephrology.

Entry date: by immediate or by agreement

Their tasks

  • They care for children and adolescents with kidney diseases in the ambulatory and in the inpatient area
  • They perform acute and chronic peritoneal dialysis and care for children and adolescents before and after kidney transplantation
  • They maintain a close cooperation with the urology, the intensive care unit and the other pediatric focus areas in the house
  • They maintain intensive contact with colleagues in nephrology and transplantation immunology and genetics at the USB, as well as colleagues in paediatric epirology at the Inselspital Bern and in Bellinzona (Ticino).

Your profile

  • You have a medical and specialist diploma in paediatric medicine and preferably a major title in paediatric nephrology (or equivalent)
  • You have proven expertise and experience in the whole field of paediatric nephrology
  • Applications from «Fellows» in further education with a focus on paediatric nephrology, training centre category B, are considered in the function as Stv. Oberarztzt*in.

Our offer

  • A varied activity in which you can actively participate
  • Regular professional exchange
  • Excellent infrastructure for individual and interdisciplinary care of our patients
  • Attractive employment conditions and flexible working time models for optimal working conditions and the best possible professional and human patient care
