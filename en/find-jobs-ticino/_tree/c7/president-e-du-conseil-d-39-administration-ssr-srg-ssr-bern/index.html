Full description

The Central Secretariat is the coordinating centre of the SSR Association and the secretariat of the Board of Directors.

Your Mission

SSR moves Switzerland.

As Switzerland's largest media company, SSR guarantees independent journalism, cultural diversity and public service in all four linguistic regions. In a changing media environment, it shapes the digital future of the public audiovisual service and strengthens the social cohesion of our country. For the next administrative period, we are looking for an integrity personality, with a strategic spirit and great persuasion, to fill the post of

Chair: SSR Board of Directors (50%)

In this demanding function, you take over the leadership of the Board of Directors and represent the SSR to political and economic circles, civil society and the public. In collaboration with the Board of Directors and the Executive Committee, you define the strategic development of the SSR and support its transformation in a changing media environment.

  • Management of the SSR Board of Directors and ensuring effective and forward-looking corporate governance
  • Piloting the strategic development of the SSR and ensuring the sustainable fulfilment of its public service mandate in the digital age
  • Support for the successful transformation of the company and the culture and development of "UNE SSR" taking into account the cultural and linguistic diversity of the country
  • Close exchanges with the Director General; sparring partner role
  • Representation of the SSR to the authorities, policy makers and other stakeholders; encouraging dialogue between business, institution, politics and the public
  • Strengthening the anchoring of public service in all linguistic regions; representation of SSR in political, societal and media circles

Your profile

  • Several years' experience in management and governance at the highest level, coupled with proven experience in strategic support of complex transformation and change processes
  • Excellent knowledge of the political functioning and decision-making processes of Switzerland with a wide network at the crossroads of Swiss society, economy and media
  • Affinity for technology; clear vision of how the public media service should position itself in the democratic and societal context in the face of the growing dominance of artificial intelligence
  • In-depth knowledge of modern corporate governance principles; experience in managing a complex set of stakeholders
  • Strong adherence to the public service mandate and its importance for Switzerland
  • Talent of communicator, persuasiveness and assurance against politics, the media and the public
  • Very good active knowledge of German and French; willingness to acquire knowledge of Italian and to fully experience the plurilingualism of Switzerland

What we offer you

  • A decisively key role at the heart of the public media service with the possibility of actively contributing to shaping the future of the public audiovisual service in Switzerland
  • An exceptional leadership function, of major importance to society and with national visibility
  • Close collaboration with committed members of the Board of Directors, with an experienced Management Committee and various stakeholders from all language regions
  • An environment characterized by diversity, plurilingualism and the strength of innovation

Interested persons are kindly requested to e-mail SRG.SSR@egonzehnder.com . The deadline for applications is November 15, 2026.

Our promise

Reaching the entire Swiss population with high quality formats requires expertise, enthusiasm and a stimulating and creative environment in which everyone can invest and evolve. For the best to give the best of themselves, they need to have a source of inspiration: Welcome to the world of SSR!

  • Create Content: Quality journalism is the core of our activity and passion. We enjoy complete freedom of writing, encourage creativity and grant autonomy for innovation and quality.
  • Living digital: In our country, quality journalism can also take a digital form. This is why we are exploring new opportunities and experimenting with new formats and practices: digital, interactive and cross-media. Our content is part of the air of time and responds to public concerns.
  • Human being: We maintain informal relations, work independently and contribute to a good working atmosphere. Our success is based on the strengths of everyone and on the technical skills of all our employees.
  • Make sense: With our program, we reach all of Switzerland. We act independently and respectfully to encourage the formation of opinion and strengthen cultural diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Requirements

  • Expérience de plusieurs années dans le domaine du management et de la gouvernance au plus haut niveau doublée d'une expérience avérée dans l'accompagnement stratégique de processus complexes de transformation et de changement

  • Excellentes connaissances du fonctionnement politique et des processus décisionnels de la Suisse assorties d'un vaste réseau à la croisée de la société, de l'économie et des médias suisses

  • Affinité marquée pour les technologies; vision claire de la manière dont le service public média doit se positionner dans le contexte démocratique et sociétal face à la domination croissante de l'intelligence artificielle

  • Connaissance approfondie des principes modernes de gouvernance d'entreprise; expérience dans la gestion d'un ensemble complexe de parties prenantes

  • Forte adhésion au mandat de service public et à son importance pour la Suisse

  • Talent de communicateur.trice, force de persuasion et assurance face au monde politique, aux médias et au public

  • Très bonnes connaissances actives de l'allemand et du français; disposition à acquérir des connaissances de l'italien et à vivre pleinement le plurilinguisme de la Suisse
