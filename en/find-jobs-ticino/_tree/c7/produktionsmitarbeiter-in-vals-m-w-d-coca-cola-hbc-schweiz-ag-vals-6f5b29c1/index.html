Production staff:in \- Vals (GR) m/f/d

Made by mountains: Our natural VALSER mineral water flows through 1000 meters deep rock for at least 25 years depending on the source and is bottled in its most original form crystal clear, pure and highly mineralized in Vals. Our unique product portfolio includes «VALSER Still», «VALSER Calcium \+ Magnesium», «VALSER Prickling» and the fruity aromas «VALSER Viva». With VALSER, we inspire everyone to find their way through the barriers of everyday life and feel limitless – just like VALSER mineral water itself. VALSER \#FeelLimitless.

VALSER is part of Coca Cola HBC Schweiz AG, one of the largest companies in the non-alcoholic beverages segment in Switzerland.

At our production site in Vals, we are looking for you as a reliable and committed reinforcement.

Your main tasks:

  • You operate and convert plant parts independently.
  • You are repairing machine malfunctions.
  • You carry out cleaning and maintenance work on the production facilities.
  • You support the maintenance team with repairs and revisions.
  • You consistently implement the requirements for occupational safety and food quality.

You bring this with you:

  • You are committed, flexible and capable of teamwork.
  • You work carefully, conscientiously and accurately.
  • You bring technical understanding and ideally PC\ knowledge.
  • Ideally, you have completed training as a food technologist.
  • Ideally, you have professional experience with production facilities, preferably in the food industry.
  • You ideally have MS\Office knowledge.
  • You have good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage.
  • You are ready to work in 2\- to 3\-shift operation.

We offer you:

  • A comprehensive introductory programme and a wide range of training opportunities.
  • A secure job with the same pay and social benefits as at our other Coca Cola HBC locations in Switzerland.
  • The opportunity to work in a global corporation and at the same time enjoy life in the mountain village.

Sounds exciting? Then we look forward to your complete application documents including application letters and testimonials. Please submit all documents as a single PDF file via our online tool.

This means that our environment offers equal opportunities for everyone, regardless of race, colour, religion, disability, sexual orientation or gender identity. Join us and help foster a culture where everyone feels part and contributes to shared success.
