Area management (a) Event Operations Guest Services (transportation, accommodation, food)

We are looking for you!

Is the human being at the center for you? Are you an experienced leader looking for something different? Do you have event blood in your veins? Then you are right with us.

In 2029, Switzerland will host the Special Olympics World Games. These take place every four years and, with around 2,500 participants, are the second largest multisport winter event worldwide. In nine sports, people with cognitive impairment compete for 9 days at three locations in Graubünden.

For the World Games Switzerland 2029, a non-profit association based in Chur was founded, which with its role-based organizational form is responsible for the implementation of the National Games 2028 and the World Games 2029. For success, we are currently looking for the right person for the following job:

Department Management (a) Event Operations 80\-100 % Guest services \- Transport, accommodation, food (01\.02\.2027 or by agreement limited to 30\.06\.2029\)

Location: Chur / Zurich / Hybrid

Your tasks

  • Overall responsibility for transport, accommodation and food across all locations.
  • Responsibility for the operational implementation and smooth operation of the assigned areas, on site together with the managers of the venue.
  • Control of relevant partners and service providers from planning to implementation.
  • Budget and result responsibility (budget lead) for the department over all locations in coordination with the Domain Guardian.
  • Establishment and management of the department including recruitment and development of your own team
  • Interfaces, needs clarification and professional commissioning for other domains, reporting to Domain Guardian and Controlling.

Your profile

  • Completed subject-relevant training (study, HF/FH) or comparable professional experience.
  • Several years of proven senior management experience in transport, hospitality, accommodation or event catering (must).
  • Experience with high budget responsibility and the management of large service providers and complex quotas.
  • Existing network in the area of transport, hospitality and F\&B is advantageous.
  • Strong planning and coordination talent at high volumes over several locations.
  • High assertiveness coupled with strong social skills and team skills.
  • High commitment and a clear commitment to inclusion and diversity.
  • Resilience and service orientation in the implementation phase.
  • very good knowledge of German (must), good knowledge of English
  • Other languages are beneficial.

Why us?

  • The unique opportunity to actively shape a major international event from planning to implementation
  • You work in a committed team with short decision-making paths and get the opportunity to take responsibility and shape the organization sustainably
  • The opportunity to work together with a wide range of specialists across different sectors
  • A varied and dynamic working environment with diverse tasks
  • A meaningful activity with a visible effect
  • A lot of personal responsibility as well as room for ideas and improvements
  • Flexible forms of work and contemporary employment conditions
  • A motivated and committed team
