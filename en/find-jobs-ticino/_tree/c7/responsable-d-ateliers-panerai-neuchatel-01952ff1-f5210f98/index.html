Full description

Reference code: JR128887 Found in Florence in 1860, Panerai began as a workshop dedicated to precision instruments, shaped early on by a decisive partnership with the Royal Italian Navy. Designing instruments and tool watches for extreme conditions forged a mindset rooted in performance, innovation, and purpose. After joining the Richemont Group in 1997, Panerai earned international recognition for its distinctive DNA of Italian design and Swiss watchmaking expertise. Today, the Maison continues to create timespieces that push boundaries, translating its technical heritage into contemporary watchmaking for Modern Heroes—individuals driving by curiosity, courage, and the will to go further. We empower these who embrace challenges, value authenticity, and seek to leave a mark, together with a Maison that has never stopped moving. In order to strengthen our Watchmaking department, we are looking for a Head of Workshops YOUR MISSION Within the Watchmaking department, you ensure the day-to-day management of your sectors to ensure the achievement of quality, cost, delay and safety objectives by managing your team and the means of production. HOW WILL YOU MAKE DIFFERENCE? In your role as Workshop Manager, you will be led to: Be responsible for achieving objectives (Security, Quality, Time, Cost, Management) Define, respect and manage indicators and budgets (efficiency, absenteeism, internal non-quality, operating costs, etc.). Follow the course and schedule definition in collaboration with the schedule. Pilot the continuous improvement of its working environment (operational excellence, problem solving) through action plans, KAIZEN yard, DMAIC projects. Oversee supply and stocks of consumables/tools. Guarantee processes and standards Ensure repeatability, standardization and evolution of assembly processes. Ensure completeness, maintenance and compliance with standards. Contribute to the definition of processes and certify that the industrialization of new products is in line with needs. Conduct product/process audits of its sector in partnership with the quality/industrialization team. Anticipate the renewal of resources and investment needs. Ensure the safety of employees and the conformity of workplaces. Ensure cleanliness and maintenance of work tools (posts, machines, common spaces). Be a transverse facilitator and facilitator Animate morning routines (workshop and transverse level). Ensure good collaboration and information sharing with other sectors/businesses. Manage and contribute to the resolution of cross-cutting issues. Ensure the management of its team Be a relay of communication with its teams and promote the values of the House. To ensure compliance with the rules, guidelines and procedures of the trade mark and group. Manage the need for resources, training and skill adequacy. Create positive team dynamics, good cohesion and a culture of continuous improvement within teams. Address and address difficulties in collaboration with HR Department. WHAT ARE THE CLES OF YOUR SURVEY IN OUR HOUSE? Diploma of engineer or diploma of higher technician or equivalent. Management of production or technical teams. Watchmaking knowledge (assembly movements and embossing). Lean, operational excellence. Knowledge ERP & MES. Good knowledge of computer tools (pack office). languages: French and English. 5 to 10 years of experience in a similar position in industry. Adaptability to contexts, people and change. Question and open mind. Good communicator and vulgarizer. Will to learn and culture of continuous improvement. Sense of organization, rigour, a spirit of analysis and synthesis Transverse coordination. WHAT IS SINGLE? You will be part of a dynamic House where your skills and talents will be recognized. You will share with us the same values such as community enrichment, benevolent attitude, continuous improvement and individual responsibility. You will work on a multicultural team and develop an international experience. You will navigate with us on the path of sustainable development. You will develop and advance your career through opportunities within Maison and the Richemont Group.
