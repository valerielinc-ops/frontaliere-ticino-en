Full description

The Office for Imprisonment (AJV) Graubünden is a department of the Department of Justice, Security and Health of the Canton of Graubünden. The AJV is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention. In Graubünden, the execution of penalties and measures is thus regulated as a uniform topic and holistic task, in which different professions work interdisciplinary with people who have become delinquent in order to prevent further delinquency. The prison (JVA) Cazis Tignez employs about 110 people and has 150 detention places in the closed prison. Of these, 100 are in normal prison, ten are in the entrance department, 28 are in special prison and twelve are in custody. We are looking for a division manager in Social Affairs & Health Tasks

  • You take over the professional, personnel and organizational management of the Social & Health department
  • lead, promote and develop the employees in your area (two teams - social service and health service - with a total of 13 people) and support them in their daily tasks
  • provide psychosocial counselling and preventive social work for admitted persons
  • Ensure high-quality basic support in the sense of professional case management
  • coordinate education in the prison system (BiSt) as well as pastoral care.
  • Member of the institution’s management and provide six to eight financially remunerated piketting services on weekends every year Requirements
  • a university degree preferably in social work with several years of professional and managerial experience
  • Ideally, knowledge in the prison or forensic field
  • High social skills, empathy and flexibility
  • Strength of assertion and ability to act in a solution-oriented and reflective manner and to make decisions safely and responsibly
  • Addressable communication skills, very good organizational skills and a high resilience
  • very good written and oral expression in German; additional knowledge of cantonal and foreign languages is an advantage
  • an impeccable reputation is required (no entries in the penal and enforcement register)
