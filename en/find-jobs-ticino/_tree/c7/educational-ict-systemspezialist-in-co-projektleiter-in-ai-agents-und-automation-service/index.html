Build on the Future of Education

The digitalization of university teaching is facing a profound change. For a key strategic project in the field of digital testing, we are looking for a personality who brings together technologies, people and processes and actively promotes the use of AI agents.

As an Educational IT system specialist: in and (co-)project manager: in AI agents and automation of the service digital exams (m/f/d) temporarily for 1 year, renewable to 3 years, the group Digital Education Services in the section EduIT.

Project background

The Educational IT Services (EduIT) section of ETH-Informatikdienste supports contemporary, learning-effective and innovative teaching at ETH Zurich with a coordinated portfolio of tools, services and support. The portfolio supports lecturers and students along the entire high-school didactic practice, starting from the learning goals through the design of learning activities, teaching, exams and evaluation. EduIT works closely with the other sections of the ETH Information Services and within the ETH Rectorate with the Department for Teaching and Learning (UTL) and the Academic Services (AkD).

EduIT is responsible for the digital testing service. With flexibly customizable digital exam environments for authentic, resource-rich and professional exams, as well as scaling to currently more than 500 exams per year, EduIT is an international leader in this field. EduIT develops open source learning technologies such as the Safe Exam Browser (SEB) and Moodle plugins or the ETH EduApp. EduIT is also involved in building the ETH AI platform. Many of the technologies developed in EduIT are used in universities worldwide.

Job description

In this role, you are working on a project with great appeal for the entire organization: As part of the project AI Agents and Automation of the service digital exams, the service digital exams will be comprehensively automated. They develop and implement modern automation solutions as well as AI agent systems for digital testing. In doing so, you design and implement interfaces, integrations and APIs between a variety of platforms and services and create the basis for user-friendly, powerful and thus scalable solutions. Together with the departments, you will analyze existing processes, define automation workflows and shape their implementation along complex technical, professional and organizational requirements.

From conception through implementation to piloting and successful service transition, you accompany the entire life cycle of the solutions. They continuously develop the automations together with the relevant stakeholders and ensure their sustainable operation. In addition, you assume responsibility in (co-)project management, coordinate different stakeholders and make an important contribution to the successful implementation of a strategically important project.

You will also contribute your experience to the further development of the university-wide AI platform under construction. Here you work closely with a second person employed for this project, formulate requirements from practice and operation and participate in the design of future-proof solutions. Through the active development of processes and services, you contribute to making the potential of AI and automation sustainable in the university environment.
