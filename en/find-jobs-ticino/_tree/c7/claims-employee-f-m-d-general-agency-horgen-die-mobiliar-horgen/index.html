Full description

This is what awaits you: Our damage service is the central service area to fulfill our service promise to our customers. In this position, you will accompany our customers proactively and uncomplicatedly in case of damage.

This is what you do with us: At the centre of your work is the optimal support of our customers in case of damage - from their notification to their conclusion.

You can bring your expertise and your strong service orientation into the company's supervision of the entire process and give customers the confidence they need to handle the damage. You are in close contact with your team and in case of damage with all parties involved. If you wish, you can also visit the damage directly on site and influence it in a decision-making process.

You bring this with you

  • Eidg. commercial training
  • Well-founded professional experience in damage management
  • Insurance intermediary VBV or insurance specialist card
  • High service orientation and joy in customer contact
  • Solution-oriented action and networked thinking
  • Self-employed, responsible and initiative personality
  • Knowledge of German in word and writing.

Working with us: A diverse and dynamic team awaits you in the general agency Olten. With 41 employees, 4 of them learners and the own damage department on site, we live close to the local area and are a reliable partner for our customers. You profit from a top modern workplace in central location and attractive working conditions with actively supported health culture in the company.

Your work location is in Olten, the possibility for partly working in the home office is possible upon consultation.

The Insurance Supervision Act requires that insurance intermediaries, profile non-life, be completed in order to perform this function.

We offer this: Participation in success

If we are successful, our employees will also benefit. Every year, the distribution of a share in the success and its amount is decided.
