Dicastery general administration Human resources In Piazza 10 6528 Camorino AVVISO DI CONCORSO FOR PERSONAL ANSUNTION

The Municipality of Bellinzona opens a public competition for the appointment, with 80% employment degree, of:

an Employee/administrative (personnel training officer) at the Human Resources Sector, Staff Service.

Main tasks

  • ensure the operational application of internal provisions and procedures and act as a reference point

on training;

  • co-ordinate the survey of needs, and elaborate the general training plan;
  • conceive, coordinate and organize courses and internal and external projects of training, updating or and

improvement or professional and, in compliance with the available financial resources;

  • examine the training proposals by verifying consistency with the functions, skills required and

provisions in force;

  • to monitor the performance and effectiveness of the activities forming tives, prepare reports and statistics and propose

improvement measures;

  • ensure and that all or all staff carry out the compulsory and/or periodic courses provided;
  • identify and document specific training requirements for each professional function within

of the organic;

  • take care of the administrative activities connected with the management of training, including inscriptions, convocations,

the relative documentation and updating of personal files;

  • manage the incoming and outgoing correspondence of the Area of activity (letters, e-mail, telephone, etc.);
  • participate in specific projects of the Sector and working groups;
  • collaborate in the development of new procedures by supporting the needs of the Service and in the activities of

continuous improvement for existing operational processes;

  • maintain regular contacts with public institutions and professional associations related to the Sector

Human resources and, more generally, the training landscape;

  • collaborate with other Municipal Administration Services;
  • service in case of need outside time, on weekends (Saturday and Sunday), on public holidays and

during voting or elections (presence at the seat, discards, etc.);

  • other services according to the needs.

Formal requirements

  • Federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as employee of trade.

Requirements in terms of professional experience

  • Consolidated professional experience of at least 3 years in the training sector.

Preferential requirements

  • knowledge and experience in applying applicable laws, ordinances, regulations;
  • experience in interpersonal relationships and with staff at all levels;
  • certified as an apprentice trainer.

Aptitude requirements

  • excellent organizational, planning and priority management skills;
  • irreproachable professional conduct;
  • excellent editorial and communication skills;
  • strong relational qualities;
  • orientation to service and continuous improvement;
  • sense of responsibility and ability to operate independently within the competences assigned;
  • ability to work under pressure;
  • reliability, flexibility, confidentiality and discretion;
  • collaboration and team spirit;
  • proactivity and problem solving skills.

Working conditions

  • employment degree: 80%;
  • 40 hours working week (to 100%);
  • workplace: Camorino (possibility of smart working).

Documents to be produced

  1. letter accompanies tory; 2. curriculum vitae; 3. copy of the identity document; 4. copy of permission, if foreign; 5. jpg format photography; 6. copy of study certificates (examination and diploma notes) and work; 7. self-certification relating to the judicial box; 8. self-certification relating to the certificate of solvency (UEF); 9. self-certification * relating to health.
  • Self-certification is available on www.bellinzona.ch.

Entry into service

  • date from with e.

Special conditions

  • hiring refers to the organic regulations of employees of the City of Bellinzona (ROD);
  • applications supported by incomplete documentation will not be considered;
  • will not be considered valid offers and documents transmitted in paper, by fax or by e-mail.

Wages conditions

  • ROD conditions apply, in particular the following classes and annual salary (including 13th monthly):

administrative fee 3: 5-6-7 / CHF 54’400.00 – CHF 69’950.00 or Administrative fee 2: 7 -8-9 / CHF 5 9’200.00 – CHF 7 7’000.00 or Administrative employee 1: 9 -10-11 / CHF 65’200.00 – CHF 85’650.00

Deadline

  • job offers accompanied by the required documents must be sent through the online application form

by Friday 20 November 2026, at 23:59. This notice of competition is also visible on www.bellinzona.ch/asks and can be found at the multifunctional offices of the districts. For further information please contact the Head of Staff Service, Mr. Alessandro Metrico (tel. 058 203 11 10).
