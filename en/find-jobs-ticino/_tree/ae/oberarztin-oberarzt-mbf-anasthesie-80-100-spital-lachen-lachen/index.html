Full description

Would you like to contribute your experience in anesthesiology to a versatile, regional acute care hospital and actively shape perioperative patient care? For our anesthesia team, we are looking for a committed specialist who combines clinical competence with the joy of interdisciplinary cooperation. Admission: From now on or by appointment.

Your tasks

  • Independent specialist care of our patients in the field of anesthesiology
  • Shared responsibility for high-quality and safe patient care
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with the various departments
  • Participation in the professional development of the area
  • Depending on qualifications and interests, participation in pain therapy, emergency or intensive care medicine
  • Support in the area of further education and training and promotion of assistant doctors

Your profile

  • Specialist title in anaesthesiology or an equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland (MEBEKO recognition)
  • Several years of clinical experience and enjoyment of responsible work
  • Interest and knowledge in the field of regional anesthesia
  • Additional qualification in interventional pain therapy (SSSIPM), emergency medicine (SGNOR) or intensive care medicine (SGI) is an advantage
  • Independent, structured and patient-oriented way of working
  • Strong ability to work in a team, commitment and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • High social, methodological and professional competence
  • Very good written and spoken German language skills (at least level C1)

Your benefits

  • Holidays to 49. Age: 25 working days 50. – 59. Age year: 30 working days from 60. Age: 35 working days
  • Seniority gift from 5 years in the form of holidays or wages
  • Extensive training and further education opportunities in the form of working hours and/or cost sharing
  • Employee benefits Possibility of a free room upgrade at Lachen Hospital Permanent discounts on products and services from well-known providers Discounted meals in the staff restaurant Massages and fitness courses at our physiotherapy
  • Accident insurance Worldwide accident cover in the private department at no additional cost
  • Parking Variety of parking spaces at very fair prices
