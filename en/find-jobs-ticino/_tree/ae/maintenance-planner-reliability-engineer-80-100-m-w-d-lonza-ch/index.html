For our location in Visp, we are currently looking for a Maintenance Planner & Reliability Engineer 80-100% (m/f/d). In this role, you are responsible for ensuring high plant availability and reliability through effective planning, coordination and continuous optimization of maintenance activities. The focus is on preventive, corrective and reliability-based maintenance strategies in full compliance with GMP, safety and quality requirements. By analyzing plant performance and disruptions, the function drives sustainable improvements and supports stable, efficient production operations. The role acts as a central interface between engineering, maintenance, production and quality. What we offer you:

  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance

  • Planning and scheduling of preventive and corrective maintenance measures to ensure high plant availability taking into account production, resource and GMP requirements.
  • Creation, maintenance and optimization of maintenance plans, work orders, task lists and spare parts strategies in SAP PM.
  • Analysis of plant failures, failures and performance losses using reliability and root cause analysis methods as well as implementation of sustainable improvement measures.
  • Definition and continuous development of maintenance and reliability strategies (preventive, condition-based and risk-based) for the assigned assets.
  • Supporting production operations by ensuring stable and reliable processes as well as the role as a technical interface between maintenance, engineering, production and QA.
  • Preparation, coordination and support of maintenance windows, shutdowns and major revisions, including coordination of external service providers as needed.
  • Ensuring GMP-compliant documentation and rule-compliant change management, including deviations, CAPAs and lifecycle documentation in the area of responsibility.
