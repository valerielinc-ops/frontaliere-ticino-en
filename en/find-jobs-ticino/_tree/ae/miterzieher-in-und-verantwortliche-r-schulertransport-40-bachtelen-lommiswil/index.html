Full description

Bachtelen is involved in the canton of Solothurn with a wide range of offers for the promotion, support and integration of young people. This includes special schools, various housing offers, individually coordinated therapy offers as well as consultations and daycare centers. Bachtelen employs around 550 people. In Grenchen, Bachtelen runs four daycare centers, the Kita Villa Kunterbunt, the Kita Märlihus and the Kita Teddybär as well as in Lommiswil a nursery and the Kita Lommiswil. These daycare centers offer the children a diverse learning and living world. For the Kita Lommiswil we search immediately:e Educator:in and responsible:r Schülertransport 40 % In the hourly wage. A business car is available for student transport.

Your work includes

  • You pick up and pick up our Hort children by car in Oberdorf or accompany our kindergarten children on the Kindergsi path
  • During the lunch table you will look after our hoard children with an age-appropriate play offer
  • Occasionally you support the kindergarten team in the care of the kindergarten children

This is what you bring with you

  • Joy of accompanying Hort children in their everyday life
  • A category B driving licence with the addition of the BPT 122 (or the openness to take the exam)
  • Creativity, flexibility and high commitment characterizes your personality

This is what we offer you

  • An attractive workplace with plenty of room for initiative and participation
  • Flat hierarchies, short decision paths and an open, appreciative cooperation
  • Various employee events
  • Contemporary employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • A meaningful work environment where your work makes a real difference
  • Generous support (money and/or time) for further training

You have questions? Olivia Steiner, Head of Kitas Bachtelen, is happy to provide you with information on 032 531 08 01 or olivia.steiner@bachtelen.ch. We look forward to your application!
