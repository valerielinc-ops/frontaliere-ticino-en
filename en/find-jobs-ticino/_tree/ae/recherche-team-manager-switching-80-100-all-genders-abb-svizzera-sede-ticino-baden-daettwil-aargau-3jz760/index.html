Your role and responsibilities


In this role reporting to the Research Department Manager, you will supervise research towards development of new switchgear solutions for the broad ABB product portfolio in a dedicated research team. The group covers multiple aspects of switching, from plasma physics to new current interruption technologies) and has deep expertise in experimental methods, physical modelling, and simulations.

Additional to identification and development of innovative switching solutions, the team supports technology implementation in ABB factories worldwide. The goal is to ensure technology leadership of ABB products, while strongly focusing on sustainable and cost-efficient solutions without compromises on performance.


The work model for the role is: hybrid


You will be mainly accountable for

  • Managing a research group with 8-10 employees and contributing to a motivating and innovative working climate as well as coaching project leaders for scientific excellence and professional execution of R&D projects. You will also manage the group’s laboratory infrastructure and control of workplace safety.
  • Planning and ensuring an optimal project portfolio for the team, focusing on a balanced long-term pipeline of low and high TRL activities
  • Recommending, developing, and improving processes and procedures to enhance local operations in addition to carrying out own research projects
  • Overseeing and assisting with transfer of technology and knowledge between internal partners and closely collaborating with Business Area Research Managers while maintaining and extending network to relevant ABB Divisions as well as Product and Technology Management



• Qualifications for the role


  • MSc/PhD in physics or engineering with a scientific background
  • Several years of relevant experience in industrial R&D and research project management
  • Communication skills and interest in lively interaction within an interdisciplinary team
  • Interest in new fields of research and technology, and in people management
  • Fluency in English and willingness to learn German



More about us

The ABB Research Center located in Baden-Dättwil, Switzerland, is where we investigate new technologies which support and drive the development of ABB’s next-generation products and solutions. Our international research teams focus on Power Electronics, Energy Storage, Intelligent Systems, and Switching.
