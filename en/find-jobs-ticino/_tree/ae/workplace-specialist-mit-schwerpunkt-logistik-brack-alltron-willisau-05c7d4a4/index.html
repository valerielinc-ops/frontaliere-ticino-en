Full description

Tasks

IT Support and Workplaces

  • You process IT support tickets and fix technical problems
  • You install, configure and maintain IT workstations (logistics and office)
  • You take over the staging of new jobs
  • You can help package software for Microsoft Intune
  • You contribute to the rollout of software and hardware
  • Manage users in the Microsoft 365 environment

Team coordination and logistics

  • You plan and organize logistics projects and coordinate suppliers and external companies
  • You coordinate closely with other IT and logistics departments and thus ensure smooth processes
  • Ensure compliance with safety regulations and operational requirements

Requirements

  • Practical experience with Active Directory
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft 365 administration, including Exchange Admin Center
  • Experience managing Microsoft Teams
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Intune
  • Experience with telephony systems such as Mitel MiCollab and Ascom phones, ev. Telephony teams
  • Experience in printer management
  • Willingness to complete the annual medical examination for work in storage areas with oxygen deficiency
  • Strong problem-solving ability and a customer-friendly, service-oriented attitude
  • Organizational skill and joy in taking on responsibility in the team
  • Completed apprenticeship as a computer scientist or many years of experience in IT support
  • Experience in a logistics operation is advantageous

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.
