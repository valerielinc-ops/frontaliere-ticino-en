Manager (m/f/d)

Visp, Switzerland

Shape the future of biopharmaceutical production at one of the world’s leading life science sites. In this key role, you ensure the smooth operation of state-of-the-art production facilities and directly contribute to the production of life-changing medicines.

This is a position with full duty of presence at the site. Close cooperation on the ground enables direct, efficient coordination in real time and ensures the technical precision required to produce medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What to expect

  • A versatile and responsible position in an innovative production environment
  • Attractive development and training opportunities within a global company
  • An open, collegial and appreciative corporate culture
  • Performance-based remuneration and attractive additional services
  • The opportunity to participate in the production of vital therapies

The full overview of our benefits can be found here: https://www.lonza.com/careers/benefits

Their tasks

  • Ensuring the technical and organisational support of piloting and production campaigns, including branding responsibility, to ensure safe and trouble-free plant operation
  • Preparation and maintenance of GMP-compliant documentation such as SOPs, work instructions and checklists in close cooperation with production and technical teams of experts
  • Support engineering and investment projects by early implementation of operational and production requirements
  • Coordination of technical maintenance, troubleshooting and optimisation measures together with engineering, workshop and technical service
  • Monitoring ongoing production campaigns and analysing and correcting deviations to ensure quality, safety and productivity
  • Training and technical support of employees in handling production facilities and operational processes
  • Continuous improvement of equipment, operations and processes using Lean, Six Sigma and 6S methods

What we are looking for

  • Training as a chemical or pharmaceutical technologist, chemistry or biology laboratory technician or study (bachelor/master) in the field of biotechnology, pharmaceutical sciences or a similar discipline
  • Several years of professional experience in a regulated production environment, preferably in the pharmaceutical, biotech or chemical industry
  • Experience in the GMP environment and a strong quality and safety awareness
  • Good knowledge of process control systems, ideally DeltaV
  • Sound technical understanding and pleasure in solving technical challenges
  • Basic English skills for working in an international environment
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method with strong teamwork skills

About Lonza

At Lonza, our employees are our greatest strength. With more than 30 locations on five continents, our globally networked teams work every day to manufacture the medicines of tomorrow. Our values of collaboration, accountability, excellence, passion and integrity shape our daily activities. Innovation comes from different perspectives. This is why we promote diversity and create an inclusive working environment in which all employees can develop their potential. If you want to help turn breakthrough ideas from our customers into effective therapies, we look forward to meeting you.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed.
