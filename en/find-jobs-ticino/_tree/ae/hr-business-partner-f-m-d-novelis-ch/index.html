Position Overview

Who we are Novelis is driven by its purpose of Shaping a Sustainable World Together.

As a global leader in innovative products and services and the world's largest recycler of aluminum, we partner with customers in the automotive, beverage can, aerospace and specialties industries to deliver solutions that improve the benefits of balanced lightweight aluminum.

We have a coordinated network of technically sophisticated rolling and recycling plants as well as foundries across 29 facilities in 9 countries throughout North America, South America, Europe, and Asia with 12,750 employees worldwide. The company is headquartered in Atlanta, USA.

Our customers include some of the largest and best-known brands in the world, including Coca-Cola, BMW, Jaguar Land Rover, Airbus, Boeing, Nespresso, Samsung, and others. In addition, Novelis is leading the industry with its sustainability and recycling efforts.

What awaits you

In this key role of HRBP , you will act as a trusted partner to managers and employees, providing both strategic and operational HR support in a dynamic industrial environment. You will combine strong business understanding with hands-on HR expertise and contribute actively to our people, organizational and transformation priorities. The position offers a broad scope and close interaction with operational teams, giving you the opportunity to make a tangible impact on both our people and our business.

As HR Business Partner, you will

  • Partner closely with managers on people, organization and workforce topics, translating business needs into effective HR solutions.
  • Coach and advise managers on leadership, employee relations, performance management and people development.
  • Manage complex and sensitive employee relations cases and provide pragmatic, legally compliant HR guidance.
  • Ensure the appropriate application of Swiss employment law, internal policies and HR processes.
  • Support organizational changes and transformation initiatives, helping managers and employees navigate change effectively.
  • Contribute to talent management and succession planning, with particular attention to critical roles and capabilities.
  • Support recruitment, onboarding and retention activities in partnership with Talent Acquisition and the business.
  • Drive and support the annual HR processes, including performance management, talent reviews and development planning.
  • Act as a visible and accessible HR partner, maintaining strong proximity to employees and teams across the site.
  • Contribute to HR projects and continuous improvement initiatives aimed at strengthening HR effectiveness, employee experience and operational excellence.

How you convince us

We are looking for someone who combines strong HR expertise with practical business understanding.

Ideally, you have

  • A university degree or equivalent qualification in Human Resources, Business Administration or a related field.
  • Solid professional experience as an HR Business Partner with significant business partnering responsibilities.
  • Strong knowledge and practical experience of Swiss employment law and Swiss HR practices.
  • Experience working in a fast-paced operational or industrial environment would be a strong advantage.
  • Demonstrated experience in employee relations, manager coaching, performance management and organizational change.
  • The ability to handle complex and sensitive situations with sound judgement, confidentiality and pragmatism.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to build trust at all levels of the organization.
  • A hands-on, solution-oriented approach combined with the ability to step back and understand the broader business context.
  • The ability to work autonomously while collaborating effectively within a local and international HR organization.
  • Fluency in French and English. German is a plus.

Location Profile

Novelis’ Sierre is located in the Valais Switzerland employing around 550 people. It mainly focuses on production of aluminium sheet for the automotive market covering the entire process from casting to finishing. The plant also hosts a world-leading Research & Innovation Center specialized in the development of new automotive sheet alloys surface treatment and joining technologies. In Feburary 2022 together with research institutions and a local energy provider Novelis founded the Net Zero Lab Valais to develop carbon-neutral solutions for aluminium manufacturing. The lab focuses on identifying and implementing innovative solutions to neutralize the carbon footprint of Novelis’ manufacturing operations and neighboring communities starting in Sierre. The Sierre plant supports its community through direct sponsorship of many annual events promoting education sports and culture.
