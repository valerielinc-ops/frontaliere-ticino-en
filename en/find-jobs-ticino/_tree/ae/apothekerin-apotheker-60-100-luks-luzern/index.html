Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement. Tasks: • Take over in our public pharmacy You take responsibility for the withdrawal regulations of our patients • The focus of your professional assessments and consultations is the Rx area of acute and highly specialized medicine. • They validate the levies and can view the patient file at any time and consult directly with the medical profession and care • In this versatile function, you combine official pharmacy with hospital pharmaceutical activities Requirements • For this position, you will bring a completed pharmacy degree and ideally have expertise and experience in the hospital environment. • Very good pharmacological knowledge as well as familiarity with the processes of a public pharmacy distinguish you • Personally convince you through an independent, reliable and flexible working method and appreciate the interdisciplinary cooperation • Good IT user knowledge rounds off your profile We offer: • Through the organizational integration of our public pharmacy into pharmacy, you benefit from the active exchange with colleagues from all areas of hospital pharmacy. • They are relieved of tasks such as direct purchasing, dealing with pharmaceutical representatives, cosmetic consulting and manufacturing as well as other activities • Regulated working hours in two-shift operation from Monday to Friday between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m. at the latest offer you a reliable framework • Bringing in a modern, innovative and positive corporate culture You incorporate your ideas, shape the future-oriented further development of the company in various projects and have the opportunity to obtain the specialist pharmacy title in officin pharmacy
