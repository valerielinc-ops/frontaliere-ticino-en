Your drive The heart of BVZ Holding is the railway company Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn and the international top brands Gornergrat Bahn and Glacier Express. Passionate and qualified employees form the basis of our success. Every day, around 700 employees are committed to offering our guests a first-class experience. We live diversity and have one thing in common: emotions are our drive. What is your drive? Your field of activity

  • Leadership of the team Revisions & Conversions in the area of rolling stock & traction at Polywerkstatt Brig. Collaboration in the management team of the Polywerkstatt Brig department
  • Leading the technical and timely handling of the audit work, conversions and modification orders for thermal and electric traction and towed vehicles
  • Ensuring the quality and economic processing of orders combined with the continuous optimization of processes

  • Implementation strength through exemplary function and assertiveness
