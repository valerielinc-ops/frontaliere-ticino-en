Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the “place we love to be” to life together with us. Here you make the difference

  • As a host, you ensure that our customers feel welcome and welcomed as guests.
  • Thanks to your excellent service and individual advice, you can ensure unique shopping experiences.
  • You support visual merchandising (including promotions) and tasting, which inspires our guests to purchase our exclusive products.
  • You ensure compliance with legal and operational hygiene guidelines and regulations.
  • With your positive charisma and your passion for our wine products, you contribute decisively to the company’s success.

That draws you out

  • Your DNA consists of a deep passion for first-class food and the fascinating world of wine
  • You love and live the role as a host:in and have a pronounced customer orientation.
  • You have successfully completed a vocational apprenticeship in the retail trade or gastronomy and already have professional experience in the field of wine and spirits.
  • You have a good relationship and a good appearance.
  • You are very reliable, flexible (assignments possible from Monday to Saturday) and act on your own initiative.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German
  • Other languages are not a must, but of great advantage for our international guests.

BENEFITS FOR YOU Together with around 1,700 other lovers of detail, you are an important part of our fascinating premium and luxury world. You experience real appreciation, work in a great team and we trust you so that you can use space and make a difference with your skills. If you understand your craft and bring a large portion of passion, then many career doors are open to you. We are happy to support you with internal but also external training and further education.

Also, look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment with flat hierarchies in the heart of Bern.
  • Benefit-based premiums and attractive social benefits.
  • Flexible working time models and at least 5 weeks holidays (PS: You get more free time thanks to the option "unpaid holiday" - from 7 to 365 days).
  • up to 50% discount on products from the globe world for you and your family.
  • Much more...
