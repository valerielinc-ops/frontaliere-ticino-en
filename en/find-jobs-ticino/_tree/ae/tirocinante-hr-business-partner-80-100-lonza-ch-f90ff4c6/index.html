Full description

With the HR Business Partner Internship at Lonza, you will have the opportunity to start your career in the field of Human Resource Management in a highly dynamic and international environment. During your internship, you will support the HR Business Partners in both operational and strategic tasks, gaining an immediate insight into the varied responsibilities of an HR Business Partner and the role of HR as a strategic partner in a globally operating company. Additionally, you will gain experience in implementing various HR projects and support the personnel marketing initiatives at the Visp site. We count on your support in all topics that we encounter in our daily work. For our Visp site, we are looking for an HR Business Partner Intern (m/w/d) for a period of 6-12 months. What to expect: An agile career and a dynamic work culture. An inclusive and ethical work environment. A variety of benefits, depending on function and site. Your responsibilities: Operational support of the various business partners in their daily business Independent creation and interpretation of HR reports and generation and graphical presentation of presentations Support of managers in various questions (regarding our HR system, processes, etc.) Active participation in HR projects and their implementation Participation in the organization and implementation of internal trainings and workshops Implementation of IDB (Inclusion, Diversity & Belonging) activities at the Visp site Optimization of collaboration with our external partners Participation and implementation of personnel marketing initiatives What we are looking for: BSc/MSc in Human Resources, Economics, Psychology, Law or similar studies or training Proactive, independent and customer-oriented way of working Communication and integration skills Enjoyment of learning new things Agility in a very dynamic environment Affinity for planning and organization, prioritization and handling of IT tools (Workday, MS Office) Discretion in handling confidential data Good German and English skills in writing and speaking About Lonza At Lonza, our employees are our greatest strength. Across over 30 sites on five continents, our globally connected teams work together every day to produce the medicines of tomorrow. Our core values: collaboration, responsibility, excellence, passion and integrity, reflect who we are and how we work together. The ideas of each individual, big or small, have the potential to improve millions of lives, and that is exactly what we want you to be part of. Innovation thrives when people of different backgrounds bring their unique perspectives. At Lonza, we appreciate diversity and strive for an inclusive environment for all employees. If you are ready to turn our customers' groundbreaking ideas into practical therapies, we would like to welcome you aboard. Ready to shape the future of Life Sciences?
