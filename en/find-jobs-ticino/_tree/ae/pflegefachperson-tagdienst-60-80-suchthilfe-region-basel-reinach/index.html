Full description

The Addiction Aid Region Basel is one of the largest private sponsorships in the Basel area. Our services include prevention, counselling, addiction treatment and harm reduction in addiction care. The ESTA – Clinic for Addiction Treatment in Reinach accompanies people with addiction diseases on their way to an addiction-free life. We offer a versatile range of treatments in withdrawal and therapy setting and attach great importance to professional, appreciative and individual care. As part of our reorientation, we are further developing our treatment concept in a targeted manner and will increasingly focus our services on adolescents and young adults over the age of 16. You have the opportunity to actively shape the further development of our clinic, to contribute new ideas and to implement innovative treatment approaches together with a committed team.
