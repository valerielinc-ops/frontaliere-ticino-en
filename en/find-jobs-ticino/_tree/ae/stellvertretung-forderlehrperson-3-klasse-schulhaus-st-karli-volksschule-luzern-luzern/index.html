Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process.

You are fit for us if you

  • with your professional competence and your pleasure in teaching, accompany the children in their development attentively and committed.
  • have training as a primary or IF teacher.
  • Understand teaching quality as a common task and enjoy working in a team.
  • Want to accompany children in their individual learning progress
  • openly, respectfully and respectfully approach children, parents, colleagues and other employees.
  • Take responsibility, show your own initiative and take on your tasks with commitment and joy.
  • understand you as part of our school community and are ready to help shape it.

More on the spot

You can find out more about our school and our surroundings on www.schule.stadtluzern.ch or on our Instagram channel Welcome to the integrative day school St. Karli. At our integrative day school in St. Karli, around 300 students learn, discover and grow in 12 rule classes and 4 kindergartens. They are accompanied by a dedicated team of around 40 teachers who contribute their different strengths and perspectives. We are a school that shapes together and continuously develops. This school year, we place particular emphasis on the integrative promotion of gifted and gifted people as well as on learning process support with learning coaching. It is important to us to take up new ideas, learn from each other and jointly develop our teaching and school quality. Would you like to become part of a dedicated team and actively shape the school? Then we look forward to meeting you!
