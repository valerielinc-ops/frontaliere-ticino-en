Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Salem Hospital | Bern

Composition by: 01. January 2027 or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 75091

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

We attach importance to a family-oriented and professional care of pregnant women, childbearers and their caregivers. The focus of our work is the individual and self-determined birth experience, including midwife-guided births within a wide range of services. For our birth department with around 830 births per year, we are looking for you to support our midwife team.

Your Advantages in Us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in the fitness subscription & entrance to the indoor pool

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • You take over the holistic care and accompaniment of pregnant women, childbearers and their caregivers
  • You ensure the quality and continuity in the care sheet of pregnancy, birth and postpartum and support the midwife team in complex situations
  • The review and further development of guidelines, instructions and professional standards in cooperation with our midwife expert is just as much a task as the introduction of new employees.
  • The planning and implementation of department-internal specialist and case meetings are just as much a part of your tasks as the close cooperation with the postpartum department.
  • You are committed to a good interdisciplinary cooperation with our doctors and other specialist areas

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a midwife HF or FH and ideally already have professional experience as a responsible midwife
  • The continuous development of our quality of care and the care process is important to you
  • As a midwife in the birthroom in the document doctor system, you have a high level of independence as well as a reliable and responsible working method
  • Initiative, flexibility and resilience distinguish you and you bring the willingness to take on all layers
  • We address an empathetic, communication-strong team member with a strong social competence

For additional information, Annette Kunz, Head of Gebärzimmer, is available under T. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by e-mail for data protection reasons. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
