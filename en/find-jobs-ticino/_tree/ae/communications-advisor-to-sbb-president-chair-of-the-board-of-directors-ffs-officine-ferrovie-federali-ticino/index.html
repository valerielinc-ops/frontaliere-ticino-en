SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. In this key role, you work directly on the side of the Executive Board’s Bureau. You will help shape effective leadership communication, develop strategic messages and accompany performances with high visibility. The cooperation with corporate communications, public affairs and the business areas is close and trusting. Responsibility at the top management level, diverse topics with social relevance and an environment in which professionalism, discretion and impact count. You can move that.

  • As a communication consultant, you advise the Chairman of the Board of Directors (VRP) on positioning, core messages and communicative opportunities and risks.
  • You control and coordinate the VRP’s internal and external communications business and ensure clear priorities, responsibilities and deadlines.
  • You create and finalize briefings, presentations, speech blocks, core messages and background documents addressable and in high editorial quality.
  • You prepare internal and external appearances as well as stakeholder meetings communicatively, accompany them if necessary and ensure follow-up.
  • In close cooperation with the Head of Communication, CEO/Group Communication, Public Affairs, the departments and the supporting offices of the Board of Directors, you ensure coordinated processes and involve the responsible offices in strategic, sensitive or reputation-relevant topics at an early stage.
  • In addition to your work as a communication consultant: in VRP you are responsible for further communication dossiers of SBB.

You bring that with you.

  • You think conceptually, write down stylish and effective content for the respective audience.
  • You understand corporate governance, politics, media and stakeholder relationships and consider these perspectives in your work.
  • You work in a structured, binding and discreet way, keep track of things under pressure and set priorities reliably.
  • With your confident appearance and constructive nature, you create trust among decision-makers and specialists.
  • A university degree (Uni/FH) in communication, corporate communications or a related field as well as several years of experience in corporate communication, public affairs, leadership support or a comparable environment. You also have knowledge of the mobility industry and SBB with their strategic topics.
  • In German you express yourself verbally and in writing. Knowledge of French or Italian is an advantage.
