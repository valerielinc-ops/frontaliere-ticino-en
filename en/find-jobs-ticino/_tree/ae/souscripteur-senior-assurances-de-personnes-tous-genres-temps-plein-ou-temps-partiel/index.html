Underwriting / product management Occupancy rate: 50% - 100% Workplace: Experience level: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Together, let us redefine standards and shape the future: your role in us is not limited to dealing with tasks. You work with people who take on responsibilities, are driven by great ambitions, and have the courage to explore new ways. You will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand. Here we rely on you to bring your ideas, take initiatives and actively contribute. This gives you the opportunity to really move things for our clientele, your team and your personal development.

What you bring

  • Risk assessment, pricing and premium calculations, risk subscription for the daily sickness benefit, LAA and LAA-C branches
  • Establishment of offers and insurance policies
  • Analysis of profitability, branch risks and portfolio data
  • Conduct of medical and technical risk examinations
  • Definition of acceptance guidelines, coverages and conditions
  • Processing of call for tenders / broker requests
  • Definition of premiums and contractual conditions
  • Assistance for complex customer solutions
  • Contractual changes and transfers
  • Implementation of renewal and remediation measures and implementation
  • Processing of wage and premium declarations
  • Technical support to brokers
  • Participation in interviews with clients
  • Advice on products and subscription directives
  • Negotiation of contractual conditions
  • Participation in product and process improvement
  • Place of work : Lausanne or Lugano

Your profile

  • Higher education, preferential insurance
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of subscription of personal insurance (health/accidents) for the brokers channel
  • Good knowledge of social and personal insurance
  • Strong analytical and associative thinking capacity, high quality awareness
  • You have a good sense of negotiation, you're customer-oriented and broker-oriented and you know how to handle pressure and make decisions.
  • You're a team player and you're willing to work as a team
  • You take into account interpersonal dynamics and contribute to constructive and objective-oriented collaboration
  • You know how to organize yourself, you act proactively and show confidence and anticipation in your actions and determine the right priorities
  • Excellent mastery of computer tools (MS-Office, including Excel, etc.), knowledge of the software "Syrius" is a serious asset
  • Excellent mastery of the French language, as well as ease of writing; good knowledge in German desired
