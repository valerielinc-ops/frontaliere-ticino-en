Building Technology Specialist (a) - Focus on Access and Monitoring Systems Our diverse logistics buildings and building technology systems are monitored and controlled by a central department at the Altishofen site. To support the various systems and to work on projects in this area, we are looking for you to strengthen the team as a building technology specialist (a) with a focus on access and monitoring systems.

Responsibilities

  • Support and further development of access and authorization systems
  • Monitoring, optimization and ensuring the operation of temperature and monitoring systems
  • Maintenance, management and control of various building technology systems
  • Troubleshooting and rectification of faults in technical building systems
  • Planning, coordination and implementation of projects as well as support of the project management

Requirements profile

  • Completed training in building technology, ideally with a focus on electrical or access control systems
  • Enthusiasm for building technology and technical contexts
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working
  • Responsible, reliable and team-oriented personality
  • Good IT user knowledge is an advantage

Offer

  • Varied task in an innovative logistics environment with activities throughout Switzerland
  • Great freedom of action and autonomous work
  • Creative freedom in technical projects and optimizations
  • Modern infrastructure and collegial working environment
  • Opportunity to develop into a project manager with client representation in new construction and renovation projects
