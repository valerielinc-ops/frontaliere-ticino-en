Full description

Introduction

For our customer we are looking for an expert person as agricultural machinery mechanic EFZ to strengthen the technical team. It is used in an environment that focuses on the maintenance, maintenance and repair of agricultural machinery and equipment. We are looking for a reliable professional who can safely assess technical connections and carry out their work carefully and responsibly. The position offers varied tasks around diagnosis, repair and functional control of various agricultural machinery. Our customer attaches importance to a structured working method, technical precision and practical experience in dealing with mechanical, hydraulic and electrical systems. Through us you get access to a professional recruitment consultancy with experienced HR support and a versatile activity in the technical environment.

Tasks

  • They service, repair and test agricultural machinery, equipment and technical components according to operational specifications.
  • They systematically and carefully diagnose mechanical, hydraulic and electrical errors on agricultural machinery.
  • They carry out assembly, disassembly and adjustment work on components and assemblies in a professional manner.
  • They document work carried out, identified defects and recommended measures in a comprehensible manner.
  • They support maintenance and ensure the functionality of the machines in use.

Profile

  • They have completed training as agricultural machinery mechanic EFZ or an equivalent technical qualification.
  • They bring practical experience in the maintenance, repair and maintenance of agricultural machinery.
  • They have a technical understanding of mechanical, hydraulic and electrical systems.
  • You work independently, reliably and pay attention to the precise execution of your tasks.
  • They bring resilience, teamwork and a careful working method for everyday technical life.

Advantages

  • professional support throughout the application process.
  • Permanent employment contract with a long-term perspective in the company.
