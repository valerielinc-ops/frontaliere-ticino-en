Full description

System detective as a traction engineer (m/f/d | 80-100%) HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 500 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company. To complement our global life cycle management team in the Bus & Trolley division, we are looking for a qualified and dynamic system detective as a traction engineer. Do you love to delve deep into systems, analyze connections and solve complex errors sustainably? Then an exciting challenge awaits you, in which you can use and further develop your technical know-how and your analytical skills. As a fallback level in the event of complex and connected faults, you contribute significantly to the stability and further development of our products and actively shape future-proof solutions. Their tasks

  • Acceptance of customer concerns in the area of traction and mechatronics of battery and trolley buses
  • Error diagnosis in complex and cross-system error images
  • Support of service technicians (remote and on site)
  • Independent exchange of electronic components at the customer
  • Support for ET management and preparation of technical documentation
  • Assistance in customer training and in-house training
  • Preparation of project- and assembly-specific technical evaluations
  • Answer the request via ticket system Our requirements
  • Basic technical training as an automotive mechatronics engineer or in the electrical sector
  • Further education (Electrotechnician TS/HF, Automotive Diagnostician FA, BSc in Automotive Engineering or similar)
  • Knowledge of high-voltage systems
  • Basic knowledge of control engineering and programming
  • Good language skills (compulsory German, second national languages advantageous)
  • Working independently
  • Good communication skills
  • Willingness to travel at home and neighbouring countries (travel activity of about 20-40%) We offer them
  • Exciting, varied activity with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Permanent office
  • Moving working time
  • Opportunity to work part-time
  • Working in a collegial team with very good cohesion
  • Modern electric vehicle as a business car If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
