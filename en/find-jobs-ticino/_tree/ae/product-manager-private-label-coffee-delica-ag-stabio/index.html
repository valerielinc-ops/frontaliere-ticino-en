Full description

Do you want to help bring private label coffee projects to life from pre-sales to execution and make a real impact in an international B2B environment? Join a team that creates daily moments of enjoyment with passion, innovation and know-how — and celebrates success together, because great coffee and great teamwork simply belong together.

Mansioni

  • You support Sales in pre-sales and acquisition phases with product documentation and presentations
  • You coordinate private label coffee projects end-to-end using corporate project management tools
  • You plan, prioritise, document and monitor development activities and ongoing projects

Requisiti

  • 1 year of experience in a similar Product Management role: ideally in FMCG; B2B experience is a plus
  • Master's degree (university / university of applied science): in Business Administration or a qualification in Product Management / Project Management
  • You are organised, reliable, proactive and solution-oriented, with the ability to manage multiple topics and build strong relationships across functions and levels.
  • You are a fast learner, feel comfortable working with stakeholders across different cultures, have good communication skills.
  • English (Business fluent)
  • Italian (Business fluent)
  • German (Fluent)
