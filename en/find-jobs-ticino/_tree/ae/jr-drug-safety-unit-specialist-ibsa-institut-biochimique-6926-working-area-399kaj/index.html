Jr Drug Safety Unit Specialist — IBSA Institut Biochimique

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The resource will be inserted within the Drug Safety Unit (DSU) and will support pharmacovigilance activities related to IBSA products both in post-marketing and clinical development.

Main responsibilities

  • Management of pharmacovigilance cases resulting from post-marketing experience, including:
  • Collection, registration and management of adverse events/reactions (AE/ADR) EU and extra- EU from any source
  • Download and manage Individual Case Safety Reports (ICSR) from EMA (EVWEB and MLM) and other national databases
  • Notification to the Competitive Authorities (local EMA and NCA), where applicable
  • Case follow-up activities
  • Reconciliation activities with Business Partners, Service Providers and IBSA departments
  • Preparation of Periodic Benefit-Risk Evaluation Reports (PBRER) and ACO for IBSA products
  • Support for the management of pharmacovigilance activities in clinical development, including:
  • Revision of clinical trial documentation
  • Continuous safety monitoring
  • Management of Serious Adverse Events (SAE) from clinical trials
  • Preparation and submission of the Development Safety Update Report (DSUR) for IBSA experimental medicinal products
  • Drafting, updating and maintaining SOP and Working Instructions on pharmacovigilance activities
  • Preparation and management of Pharmacovigilance Agreements (PVA) for the exchange of security information within the IBSA Group and with third parties
  • Provision of pharmacovigilance training to DSU and other departments of the IBSA Group

Requirements

  • Bachelor of Science (e.g. CTF, Pharmacy, Biology, Biotechnology or related)
  • Experience of at least 2 years in Pharmacovigilance
  • Good knowledge of English, written and spoken
  • Knowledge of the legislation and guidelines ICH, EMA, UK, Swissmedic, applicable to Pharmacovigilance.
  • Mastery of computer tools
