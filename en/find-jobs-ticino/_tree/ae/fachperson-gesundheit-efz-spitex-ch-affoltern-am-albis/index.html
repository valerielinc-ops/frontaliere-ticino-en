🌞 Our team Dementia Care is looking for you as a health professional EFZ 🌞 Health professional EFZ Dementia care means relationship care. Enabling people with dementia to live a dignified life in their familiar home – that is our heartfelt concern. Would you like to have time for people and real encounters again? Then you are exactly right for our specialized, dedicated specialist team! 🌞

We are looking for YOU! for our team Dementia Care with empathy, competence and team spirit:

↑ This is what your new everyday life looks like

  • You take over the basic and treatment care with responsibility and expertise
  • You care for our customers with dementia with a lot of empathy and patience
  • You adapt your care individually and are a communicative link in the team
  • In the interdisciplinary team, you are actively involved in regular exchanges

🙌🏽 What to expect from us

  • A lot of passion and design freedom in the co-creation of a specialized dementia team
  • An interprofessional team with real cohesion
  • Promoting your professional and personal development
  • A working environment where humor, exchange and mindfulness count

👑 You bring that with you

  • Completed training as FaGe EFZ
  • Ideally experience and genuine interest in dementia care
  • Readiness for further training in the field of dementia
  • Joy of person-centered care, co-creation and teamwork
  • High empathy, patience and an appreciative attitude

Portrait Spitex Knonaueramt employs around 160 employees who are committed to the needs of their approximately 500 customers on a daily basis in a competent, open, personal and flexible manner. The catchment area comprises around 54,000 inhabitants. We offer individual care, care, relief and counselling at home for sick and recovering people of all ages: acute, chronic and mentally ill, people with disabilities, age-related limitations, convalescence, overwork, accident and maternity. We also care for people with serious and/or incurable diseases, accompany and relieve the relatives. The Spitex Knonaueramt is a private-law association that provides help and care at home on behalf of 13 municipalities in the district of Affoltern.
