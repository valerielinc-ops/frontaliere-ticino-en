Full description

Location: Stäfa, Switzerland Fulltime, 4-6 Months

At Sonova, our Research & Development (R&D) department delivers innovative hearing solutions and services for people with hearing loss and hearing care professionals worldwide. Ensuring high audio quality in every life situation and providing the best hearing performance experience to our customers is the primary goal of this team.

To further improve the development and testing of audio quality and hearing performance in realistic scenarios, particularly during phone calls, we are looking for a motivated intern to join our team in Stäfa for up to six months.

Your tasks

  • Become familiar with existing hardware and software setups used for assessing audio quality during phone calls
  • Configure and operate Sonova hearing instruments, smartphones, and laboratory equipment for audio quality during hands-free phone calls
  • Create, modify, and maintain measurement objects and test configurations for audio quality assessments
  • Conduct measurements and tests to evaluate phone call audio processing performance with selected smartphone and hearing device models
  • Analyze measurement results using existing software tools and support customization through basic Python scripting
  • Document test procedures, results, and recommendations clearly for team members and stakeholders

Your profile • BSc or MSc in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Acoustic Engineering, Signal Processing, or a related field

  • Interest in audio technologies, signal processing, telecommunications, acoustics, or hearing systems
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to work independently
  • Basic Python programming skills are an advantage
  • Experience working in an acoustic laboratory or with audio hardware is a plus
  • Fluent English

What We Offer

As one of the world’s leading hearing care providers headquartered in Switzerland, we’re committed to building an inclusive culture. We want to create an environment where you can balance a successful career with your commitments and interests outside of work, through our flexible hybrid working model.

We can offer you a new challenge, with interesting tasks and much more – including an open corporate culture, flat hierarchies, support for further training and development, opportunities to take on responsibility, and an excellent range of foods, sports and cultural facilities.
