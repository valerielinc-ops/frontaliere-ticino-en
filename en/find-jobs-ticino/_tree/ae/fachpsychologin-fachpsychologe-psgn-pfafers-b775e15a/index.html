Full description

Your tasks

  • As a member of our interdisciplinary treatment team, you serve patients in the ambulatory.
  • After a thorough familiarization, you will take over the psychotherapeutic clarification, treatment and advice of patients and their relatives in individual and group setting.
  • Your varied day-to-day work covers the entire spectrum of psychiatric illnesses, in which you conduct individual and multi-person discussions.
  • You coordinate treatments independently, plan therapies and carry out test psychological assessments.
  • You actively participate in rapports, case discussions and training and are in close contact with other practitioners.
  • With the support of our secretariat, you will handle case-related documentation, write reports and record services in the clinic information system. Your profile
  • You have successfully completed your psychology degree and have already gained experience in outpatient or inpatient adult psychiatry.
  • Your psychotherapy training is recognised in Switzerland and either completed or advanced.
  • You already have the psychotherapy vocational training permit of the Canton of St.Gallen or are ready to acquire it before starting your job.
  • Interest in outpatient, interdisciplinary treatment distinguishes you, always taking into account the individual needs of patients and their relatives.
  • You are motivated to actively contribute your expertise, develop your skills and help shape the future of psychiatry in St.Gallen.
  • You bring your own initiative, sense of responsibility and very good knowledge of German (compulsory C1). With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. We are on the road digitally and are committed to sustainability, so we look forward to your online application via our homepage. As soon as your application has been received by us online, you will receive a confirmation of receipt by e-mail. We are happy to get to know you based on your dossier and check whether your qualifications, skills and you as a person fit the advertised position and the psychiatry St.Gallen. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also get the opportunity to get to know your future team. You will receive a telephone confirmation from us if we decide for you after the second interview and ask you if you are still convinced of us. If we agree, we will draw up your contract. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. So you can get a differentiated picture of the company and already prepare for the job. On the first day you can expect an onboarding in your new team and with other colleagues. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also receive your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
