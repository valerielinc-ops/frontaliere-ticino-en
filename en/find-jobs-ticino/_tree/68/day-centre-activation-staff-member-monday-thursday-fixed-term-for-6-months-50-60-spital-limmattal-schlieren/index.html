By agreement We look after our residents and their relatives with expertise and a lot of human commitment in often complex situations. The treatment, care and care in high quality therefore require specific knowledge in the field of geriatrics as well as individually adapted support in health, social or mental matters. This is why our multi-professional team works closely together. It consists of doctors:women, nurses, a social worker, activation specialists, therapist:women and pastoral workers. Direct applications are preferred, external recruitment offices are not considered. Here they engage

s'LIMMI: Your partner for life As the largest care center in western Zurich, we offer a stationary home for 126 people in need of care.
