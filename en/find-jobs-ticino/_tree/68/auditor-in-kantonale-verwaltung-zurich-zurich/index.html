Full description

Canton of Zurich Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs Juvenile Criminal Justice Auditor/in 100% The five regional youth prosecutors conduct criminal investigations against juveniles between the ages of ten and eighteen, file charges with the juvenile court and execute juvenile criminal decisions. You have recently completed your Master’s degree in Law and would like to gain initial practical experience? Are you interested in juvenile criminal law and would like to apply your legal knowledge in an exciting and socially relevant field of activity? Then apply as an auditor (100%) at one of the two youth advocacy offices of the Canton of Zurich. For the Jugendanwaltschaft See/Oberland we are looking for an auditor with employment as of 1 January 2027. For the youth advocacy office Limmattal/Albis we are looking for an auditor with employment as of 1 March 2027. An auditorium provides a diverse and well-founded insight into the activities at a youth advocacy office and enables you to enter into practice in the juvenile criminal justice system. As an auditor, you support youth lawyers in conducting and completing criminal investigations against young people by, among other things, preparing criminal orders, recruitments, hearings and indictments. They also support those responsible in

  • Tasks relating to the execution of penal and measures under juvenile law. In addition to a completed master’s degree in law, interest in criminal law and criminal procedure law as well as very good knowledge of German is required. An auditorium lasts at least twelve months and is credited 100% to the legal internship. If you are interested in an auditor position, we ask you to apply via our online tool. For questions, the HR specialist Angela Ryser is available at tel. +41 43 258 41 78 is available.

Pensum: 100%
