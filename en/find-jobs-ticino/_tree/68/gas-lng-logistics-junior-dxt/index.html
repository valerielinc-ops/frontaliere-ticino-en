Full description

GAS & LNG LOGISTICS JUNIOR We are looking for a highly motivated candidate to join our Natural Gas Logistics team, a fundamental component of the gas trading desk. The successful candidate will gain on-the-job knowledge of European gas market processes and operating principles and an in-depth understanding of gas transportation, storage, balancing and LNG procedures. Responsibilities:

  • Daily execution of the nomination activity on gas market hubs, TSOs (Transmission System Operators), storage plants and LNG terminals
  • Managing the internal IT data system process
  • Work based on a shift schedule that includes evening and weekend shifts Candidate profile:
  • Degree (B.Sc. or M.Sc.) in Engineering or Finance / Economics or equivalent relevant professional experience in similar roles of 1-3 years
  • Diligent and reliable
  • Fluency in English is essential (spoken and written); other European languages would be an advantage. Good knowledge of Italian is also required.
  • Previous work experience in GAS or LNG Logistics will be considered a plus
  • Ability to work in a team as well as individually
  • Proficiency with MS Office (especially Excel) is required, VBA a plus
  • Adaptability, flexibility and the ability to work to tight deadlines Offer:
  • Competitive salary and benefits package including help with relocation if necessary
  • The opportunity to be involved in a challenging international working environment
  • Access to the Duferco Academy for personal and professional growth Please send your current CV to: hr.gas.lng.logistic@dxt.com We will only contact candidates whose profiles match the required criteria. DXT Commodities S.A. is an energy and commodity trading company headquartered in Lugano, Switzerland, part of the Duferco Group. The company operates globally with offices in London, Singapore, and Stamford (USA).
