Who are we?

Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is an area of innovation and an important driver for the economic and cultural life of the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Establishment and coordination of a training programme for young researchers (postdocs)
  • Organization of events and training programmes for post-docs
  • Implementation of a training program for teachers and post-doc instructors, in close collaboration with faculties
  • Creating a website for postdocs
  • Establishment of best practices
  • Participation in a national post-doc network
  • Regular evaluation and development of measures and activities

Profile desired

  • Doctorate
  • Ideally, postdoctoral research experience
  • Knowledge of the Swiss academic landscape
  • Ideally, experience in managing and editing websites
  • High personal motivation and flexibility
  • Social skills, customer orientation and teamwork
  • Mastery of the French, German or English language, with good knowledge of the other two languages
