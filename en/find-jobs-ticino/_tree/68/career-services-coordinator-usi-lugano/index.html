Career and Alumni Service Coordinator at the Career and Alumni Service (100%) The University of Italian Switzerland (USI) is a young and agile entity in continuous development, driven by its three core values: quality, openness, and responsibility. Active in various fields of study and research, USI offers its students a proactive, engaging, and interdisciplinary education, and provides its researchers and staff with a privileged space of autonomy and initiative. The Career and Alumni Service fosters privileged relationships and connections between USI students and graduates with major professional entities, facilitating the qualified integration of graduates into the workforce. The service also maintains relationships with USI alumni by establishing and consolidating contact networks among USI graduates, promoting exchange among graduates, companies, and institutions, and supporting professional development and networking within the alumni community. To complete the team of the Career and Alumni Service, USI is opening a position for a Career Services Coordinator (100%). Position
