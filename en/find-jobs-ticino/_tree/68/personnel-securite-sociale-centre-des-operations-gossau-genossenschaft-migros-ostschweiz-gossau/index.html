Full description

Bring your expertise to Migros! You can expect a supportive team, exciting challenges and the opportunity to make a difference. Together we live "Tämä Erfolgrich": You support employees and managers, take on responsibility in the social insurance sector, carry out training courses and develop processes further. Become part of the Migros world and apply now!

Mansioni

  • As the first point of contact for employees and managers, you provided advice, support and training in the area of the time management tool MAPS
  • You independently supervise and coordinate social insurance-relevant topics in the areas of accident, illness, family allowances and compensation and are the central point of contact for internal and external stakeholders
  • You plan and conduct training and courses for groups of up to 40 participants and accompany them professionally and practically
  • You optimize existing HR processes and develop them continuously

Requirements

  • Professional experience: social security
  • Primary or continuing education: in the field of social security
  • You are a reliable, committed and inquisitive personality with an independent and structured way of working
  • Interconnected thinking, a strong quality awareness and the ability to keep track even in hectic situations are among your strengths
  • You perform safely in front of groups and understand how to convey content in an understandable, target-group-oriented and practical manner
  • Customer and service orientation are a matter of course for you: you recognize needs, provide competent advice and find pragmatic solutions
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Employee offers: Professional home at one of the most successful and financially sound Swiss companies
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks and promotion of personal responsibility
  • Social benefits: Very good social benefits in the areas of child allowances, sickness allowance and accident insurance
  • Sports and fitness offers: discount on subscriptions to leisure and fitness facilities
