Full description

In this task, you produce precision components and small series according to technical specifications. The company offers you a modern working environment that allows a lot of personal responsibility. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • to produce prototypes and small series based on technical plans
  • to carry out microplasma welding processes independently
  • to apply TIG/TIG welding techniques
  • to process production orders on time and with high quality
  • to carry out pre- and post-processing such as folding, cutting and deburring
  • Requirements
  • Completed vocational training as a plant and apparatus manufacturer
  • Well-founded and comprehensive professional experience in the field
  • Self-employed and goal-oriented working method
  • Motivated and committed personality
  • Teamwork and cooperative cooperation Offer
  • Varied task with high personal responsibility in an experienced team
  • Modern and technically contemporary working environment
  • Attractive employment conditions with a long-term perspective
