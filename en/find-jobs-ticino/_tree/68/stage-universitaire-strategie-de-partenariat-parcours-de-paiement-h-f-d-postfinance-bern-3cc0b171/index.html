Full description

Duration of university internship: 1. February 2027 - 31. July 2027

34 colleagues at Payment Journeys are shaping the future of payment transactions at PostFinance. Two teams are responsible for national and international payment transaction solutions. Our eleven-member Shared Services team ensures optimal conditions with enabling and cross-sectional services. Your area of work is the exciting world of software partners. With their solutions, they enable our customers to automatically integrate PostFinance products into business processes.

What you can do

  • You will analyze, structure and evaluate the existing software partner landscape.
  • You will take up the needs and expectations of our software partners and classify them.
  • You will develop a strategic vision for the future partner landscape, taking into account overarching requirements and perspectives of other teams, such as sales and service partnerships outside of payment transactions.
  • You will redesign the website, including the product data sheets of the software partners, and derive requirements for the supplier from this.
  • You will be accompanied by an experienced partner manager who will support you with information, documents and advice and action.

What you bring to the table

  • You have a bachelor's degree in business administration, business informatics or a comparable field that was not more than two years ago at the start of the program or are at least in the 4th semester of your bachelor's degree
  • Interest in payment transactions, digital business models and partner ecosystems
  • Strategic, conceptual and analytical mindset
  • Ability to document complex content in an understandable way and present it convincingly
  • High willingness to learn, curiosity, initiative and a winning appearance
  • Very good knowledge of German; other languages are an advantage
