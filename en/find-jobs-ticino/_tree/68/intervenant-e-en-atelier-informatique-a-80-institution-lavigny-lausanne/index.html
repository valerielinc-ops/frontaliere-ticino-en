Full description

80% computer workshop speaker, 80% The Département Plein Soleil de l'Institution de Lavigny, located in Lausanne, is dedicated to people with progressive neurological diseases or brain damage, and includes a 70-bed EMS, a 10-seat CAT, 12 sheltered accommodation and an outpatient therapy centre. It is a unique place of life and care in the Canton of Vaud, which offers personalized accompaniment, with care services and therapies, educational and socio-cultural activities, workshops for personal development and well-being, spiritual support and a service dedicated to the loved ones. Do you want to put your creativity and skills at the service of projects that make sense? Do you appreciate the field work, the meeting, the group dynamics and want to contribute actively to the quality of life and the social participation of the accompanied people? We are looking for a workshop team for our sociocultural sector:

80% computer workshop speaker

In particular, you will be responsible for

  • Accompany residents to the computer workshop, offering activities tailored to their abilities, desires and projects.
  • Actively participate in the evolution and modernization of the IT workshop, developing new activities and new materials.
  • Imagine and lead projects around digital, computer, graphic, office, photo, video, multimedia and social networks.
  • Develop individual or collective projects that promote self-reliance, creativity, social participation and the development of each individual's skills.
  • Be a force for proposal and contribute to the current, creative and innovative dynamics of the workshop.
  • Adapt tools, software, peripherals and workstations to the needs and capabilities of residents.
  • Explore new digital tools and uses that could enrich the proposed activities.
  • Work with socio-educational, health-care and therapeutic teams to integrate workshop activities into individual projects of residents.

Your profile

  • You are ideally at the benefit of a socio-professional teacher's training in ES, IT, media or have relevant experience in accompaniment and/or the digital field.
  • You are comfortable with IT and various digital tools and want to put your skills at the service of residents.
  • You are a motivated, creative, curious and willing person to invest in the development and modernization of the workshop.
  • You attach particular importance to the quality of support and know how to adapt your proposals to the capacities, needs and pace of each resident.
  • You have a real interest in accompanying people with neurological diseases or brain damage.
  • You are interested in new technologies and the possibilities they can offer to people with physical or cognitive limitations.

We offer you

  • Internal in-service training adapted to the function;
  • The possibility of a variety of meaningful work within an institution recognised for the quality and diversity of its services;
  • A working environment conducive to exchange, proximity, creativity and teamwork;
  • The possibility of actively participating in the evolution of the computer workshop and developing new projects;
  • Working conditions according to the CLC of the parapublic health sector Vaudois;
  • The social benefits of a large institution: parking, restaurant, public transport on the site, etc.

• Entry into office: immediately or to be agreed Information: Mr. Thibault Decreuse, Social Sector Manager, at 021,651 28 34. If you are interested and correspond to the profile you are looking for, we invite you to send us your complete file — cover letter, CV, diplomas and certificates of employment — directly and only via Jobup.
