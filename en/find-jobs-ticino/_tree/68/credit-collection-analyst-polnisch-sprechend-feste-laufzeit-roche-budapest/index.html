The Position

This is a fixed-term opportunity until the 13th of November, 2028

The Record to Report (R2R) Department is a global organization of over 650 experienced finance professionals working together to deliver financial services and solutions for Roche across the EMEA region. Our services cover financial management, period-end closing, capital and lease accounting, credit and receivables, manufacturing finance, and divisional reporting, ensuring compliance for 263 reporting entities in 96 countries with accounting standards (IFRS, GAAP) and providing assurance of financial integrity.

The Opportunity

As a Credit & Collection Analyst for Poland , you are an important link between the business and the customers across Roche Pharma divisions. You manage the customer queries, help the customers for dispute cases and reduce the volume of overdue invoices while continuously improving the performance of the RSS and conduct an effective credit management strategy to reduce Roche exposure risk to doubtful accounts.

  • Managing and applying the Collection Strategy for the Customers Portfolio of the Roche Affiliate
  • Contacting and doing follow-ups with customers on overdue invoices, manage Customer Complaint (dispute management) and dunning process
  • Preparing and analyzing Customer Ageing/Open item Report (as defined in the Local Credit Management Policy and in alignment with FGAR)
  • Evaluating Customer Credit based on financial strength and ability to pay on time, classify risk categories
  • Managing blocked Sales Orders as defined by the Local Credit Management Policy
  • Collaborating with other teams/ team members, stakeholder, daily based conversation with the supported Affiliate

Who you are

  • 1-3 years of related experience in Collection and/or Credit Management is desirable and experience in Shared Service Center environment is preferred
  • University or College Education, concentration on Finance or Accounting is a plus
  • SAP knowledge, Intermediate Excel skills and understanding of the Order to Cash process & analytical skills is an advantage
  • Self-driven, Team-player attitude with independent decision making skills is required
  • Proficiency in English and Polish
