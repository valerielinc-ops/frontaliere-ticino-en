Full description

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and fairness. By joining the Lausanne Care Foundation, you are committed in a concrete way to promoting health and well-being in the canton of Vaud, within expert, motivated and enthusiastic teams, in a varied activity rich in human contacts. "Learning by playing, small:pas" is a play and learning program to promote the social integration of families with preschool children (2-4 years). In order to meet the needs of the system and thus respect the working orientation of peers, we are looking for a person with personal educational experience in the field of early childhood from 1 February 2027 for a position of

Speaker at home at 35% (M/F)

Participation rate at 35% annualised according to school calendar Fixed-term contract

Your Mission

  • Prepare and conduct weekly home visits according to schedule and document according to program and coordinator directive
  • Conduct interactive play activities between parents and children at home to support development and strengthen family ties
  • Attend weekly team meetings with coordinator
  • Managing the agenda of home visits with families
  • Motivate families to participate in community group activities (in and out)
  • Participate and contribute to the facilitation of group meetings
  • Participate in ongoing training that addresses topics relevant to the mission

Your profile and skills

  • Personal or/and professional experience with early childhood children
  • Love to share games activities with families
  • Have a common sense of listening and show creativity
  • Ability to analyse practice in a reflexive way
  • Building relationships of trust
  • Love to work independently and flexibly in an intercultural context
  • Mastery of French in oral and written (B2) as well as basic computer tools (World, Excel, Outlook)
  • Driver's licence and necessary private vehicle
  • Home in the Lausanne region
  • Mastery of several languages an asset
  • Good knowledge of the local socio-associative network an asset
