Your role in our House

You develop the activity of Bucherer Fine Jewellery with a demanding Swiss and international clientele and accompany in the acquisition of exceptional jewellery creations thanks to your expertise.

You are actively exploring new customers with high potential and contributing to the sustainable development of your customer portfolio.

You retain a high-end customer base by offering it a personalized, exclusive and perfectly suited to its expectations.

You identify market opportunities and generate new sales by developing long-term trust relationships.

You organize and participate in customer events, networking actions and relationship initiatives to enhance the visibility and visibility of the House.

You are actively contributing to the store's business objectives by demonstrating a strong entrepreneurial spirit.

You represent the values, history and excellence of Bucherer Fine Jewellery and act as the true ambassador/rice of the House, both inside and outside the shop.

You are developing brand awareness among local and international customers, as well as with key partners and prescribers in the region.

You embody the highest standards of service, elegance and professionalism and create lasting relationships with your customers.

Your profile

You benefit from several years of experience in the world of luxury and have a strong expertise in selling high-end products.

You have excellent knowledge in jewellery and gemmology

  • An experience in the world of high jewellery is a major asset.

You know the Geneva market as well as the regional economic fabric and are comfortable in international environments.

You have a private and professional network already established in the German region, which you can develop and maintain with ease.

You have demonstrated your ability to acquire, develop and retain a demanding high-end customer base.

You are endowed with a strong commercial sense and especially enjoy relationship selling and active networking.

You display an impeccable presentation, excellent know-how and a natural sense of exceptional service.

You are recognized for your discretion, your sense of confidentiality and your ability to build a lasting relationship of trust with your clients.

You work independently while easily integrating into a team and fostering constructive collaboration with your colleagues.

You have excellent relational and communication skills and a positive and proactive attitude.

Your mother tongue is French and you are fluent in English; any other language is an asset.

You stand out for your sense of responsibility, professional rigour and outstanding customer orientation.
