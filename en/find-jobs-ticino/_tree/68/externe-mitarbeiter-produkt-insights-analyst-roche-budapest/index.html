The Position Workforce Optimization (WFO) provides expertise at global level with the ambition of elevating the way leaders shape Roche’s workforce. In partnership with P&C and cross-functional experts, they further evolve a framework on how to partner with the external workforce, provide insights regarding total workforce (workforce including external partners), and data-driven, skill-based workforce solutions. These frameworks/solutions enable us to partner with the business in defining integrated workforce strategies and to unlock the strategic potential of our total workforce (TW). Shaping new practices/products to navigate the evolving business landscape and to proactively shape Roche’s workforce to be future ready, working with new technologies and cross-functional partners offers great learning opportunities.

The Opportunity

As an External Workforce (EW) Product & Insights Analyst, you support Roche’s cross-functional EW Framework with regards to efficient & compliant processes, and enable TW insights by data quality, data analysis and regular reporting at a global level.

  • Contribute to the data accuracy of EW data e.g. by supporting month-end closing procedures, by regular operational data audits, as well as driving data enhancement processes
  • Prepare workforce reports reconciling data from our Finance & P&C systems in order to provide transparency of our EW across Roche and gather relevant insights
  • Support regular reporting on EW for Senior Leadership and/or global audit data requests
  • Support initiatives - in the field of TW Insights - to enrich EW across Roche, drive automation and efficient WFO operations and support EW data and insights requests
  • Partner closely with EW Framework & Enablement team as well as Product & Insights Partners to ensure a good understanding of the data (& processes) requirements and translate them into EW data products
  • Support the regular communications to PSS regarding EW initiatives/updates of relevance to Roche Responsible Persons (=RRPs, persons responsible for managing external partners) - in close collaboration with the EW team
  • Keep track on feedback, challenges and requests of RRPs (via PSS) regarding data entry process & beyond, and loop this back into EW related product evolution
  • Support the design and offering of training for RRPs, regarding their responsibilities, the external partner lifecycle and usage of EW infrastructure

Who you are

  • 3-4 years of work experience in matrixed, global and data related settings, preferably within People & Culture
  • Communication skills, incl. know how to communicate data and how to use technology, ideally experience in designing learning journeys
  • Fluency in English
  • Data-driven mind with expertise in data analysis, analytics and interpretation, with strong skills in Excel/Google Sheets; and know how to apply AI, algorithms/libraries
  • Analytical & technical skills, leveraging data & translating them into action and can address complex problems via data-driven solutions
  • Flexibility to adapt to different needs and navigate ambiguity in complex environments
