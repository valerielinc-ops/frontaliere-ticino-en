All-rounder vehicle treatments (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company.

To complement our services team, we are looking for a motivated all-rounder for our underfloor treatments. Your work makes the difference: With underfloor treatments on our customers’ buses, you ensure that the vehicles are still on the road reliably for a long time. Get involved and become part of a dedicated team! Their tasks

  • Independent execution of underbody and cavity protection treatments on buses in the repair center
  • Rusting and corrosion protection work on buses
  • Carrosseries, flooring and underfloor cleaning services on buses (removal of dirt, salt, oil and brake backup)
  • Protective measures (covering) on the vehicle for treatment
  • Minor repairs to the chassis and chassis components
  • Checking the underbody and the carrosserie for rust and various damages
  • Material management in the repair centre

Our requirements

  • Experience in the automotive industry is an advantage
  • Craftsmanship
  • High physical strength
  • Transport and independent working methods
  • Vehicle ID Cat B of Advantage

We offer them

  • Varied and interesting permanent employment
  • Moving working time
  • Conditions of performance
