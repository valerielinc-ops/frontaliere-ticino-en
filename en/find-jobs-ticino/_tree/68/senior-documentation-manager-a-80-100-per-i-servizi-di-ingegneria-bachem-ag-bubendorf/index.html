Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in Engineering Services, we are looking for a dedicated Documentation Manager (a) (80 -100%)

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for the structured management, storage and archiving of technical documentation in electronic and physical form
  • Ensuring compliance with GMP, HSE and internal documentation standards as well as audit-proof document storage
  • Professional leadership of document management teams in engineering and CAPEX projects
  • Creation and implementation of document management concepts, testing processes and training concepts
  • Coordination of document deliveries and control of review, approval and change processes
  • Creation and maintenance of documentation plans, document registers and reporting dashboards
  • Creation, revision and implementation of SOPs, policies and standards in document management
  • Advising project teams as well as support for audits, inspections and requests from authorities
  • Central contact for documentation topics and interface to engineering, HSE, quality assurance, CQV, maintenance and operations

Your profile

  • Technical or scientific training or relevant professional background
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in the chemical, pharmaceutical or life sciences industry
  • Good knowledge of GMP and HSE requirements and relevant technical regulations and standards
  • Experience with technical and GMP-compliant documentation as well as document and filing structures
  • Strong communication, analysis and problem-solving skills as well as a secure approach to different stakeholders
  • Safe use of Microsoft Office
  • Experience with document management systems and SAP is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of German and English in word and writing

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development and further education opportunities
  • Flexible working hours with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Company-internal KITA on the Bachem Campus Bubendorf
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to advantage platforms with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
