Institute of Public Health (IPH) PhD student position at the Institute of Public Health, Faculty of Biomedical Sciences at USI Università della Svizzera italiana Università della Svizzera italiana (USI) is a young and lively university, a hub of opportunity open to the world where students are offered a quality interdisciplinary education in which they can be fully engaged and take center stage, and where our researchers can count on having the space to freely pursue their initiative. Established in 1996, USI is in constant evolution, always taking on new challenges while remaining true to its three guiding principles: quality, openness and responsibility. The Institute of Public Health (IPH) is an interfaculty institute dedicated to teaching, research, and service activities in public health sciences and related disciplines, acting as a central hub for public health within USI. It brings together expertise from fields such as epidemiology, psychology, economics, and communication sciences to advance health promotion, disease prevention, and evidence-based public health practice. The Institute invites applications for a research and teaching PhD position (100%, 3 years). The PhD candidate will develop his or her dissertation project within the field of public health. The project Within the SNSF-funded project Education, Work and Cognitive Stimulation across the Life Course: Evidence from Natural Policy Experiments, the PhD candidate will contribute to advancing knowledge on the causal effects of education, work and retirement policies on cognitive ageing and dementia risk. The project examines how education policies (compulsory schooling, tracking, free-education reforms) and retirement policies shape exposure to cognitively stimulating environments across the life course, cognitive function and dementia risk in later life. It relies on causal inference methods and on harmonised international ageing cohorts (SHARE, ELSA, HRS, etc.) linked to policy data from the Gateway to Global Aging (https://g2aging.org/). The project is led by Prof. Mauricio Avendano (Unisanté/University of Lausanne), in collaboration with Prof. Emiliano Albanese (Università della Svizzera italiana, Lugano) and international partners (University of Southern California, University of Luxembourg). The project aims to generate robust, policy-relevant evidence through high-quality scientific publications and synthesis outputs, informing national and international strategies for brain health and dementia prevention. The PhD Position The doctoral student will be enrolled in the PhD programme at the Faculty of Biomedical Sciences. The doctoral student will work under the scientific supervision of Prof. Emiliano Albanese (http://usi.to/r8p). The successful candidate will be offered the possibility to work in a dynamic research team and in a multidisciplinary and international scientific environment. pagina The PhD candidate will collaborate on the development of the institute research agenda. He or she will have the task of assemble, harmonise and analyse data from international ageing cohorts and policy databases for his or her dissertation, while at the same time participating in a variety of tasks related to the research streams in which he/she is involved. On the teaching side, he/she will work as teaching assistant in courses at either bachelor or master level, helping in the preparation of teaching materials and tutoring students. The PhD candidate is also asked to present papers at scientific conferences and produce publications for scientific journals. Candidates’ profile
