Full description

You lead technical customer projects for the development of plastic solutions for industrial and consumer goods applications and advise on material selection and injection molding processing.

Tasks

  • Managing projects for the development of plastic solutions with customers from industry and consumer goods or mechanical engineering
  • Technical advice on material selection, component and material analysis and injection moulding processing
  • Implementation of tests and inspections in the injection molding plant and at customers
  • Development of technical basics, feasibility analyses and cost accounting
  • Collaboration in cross-functional teams with research, production, materials analysis and sales
  • Market monitoring to identify new trends and technologies
  • Lectures and internal training

Profile

  • Completed studies in plastics engineering, materials engineering or mechanical engineering
  • Professional experience in injection molding technology and design of technical plastic components
  • Decision-making, entrepreneurially thinking personality with goal orientation
  • Willingness to work and learn as well as initiative and flexibility
  • Good English skills in Word and Writing

Offer

  • Modern infrastructure for process tests, analyses and developments
  • Great room for manoeuvre and personal responsibility
  • 3–6 months interim, then permanent employment
