Full description

Responsible work at modern production facilities awaits you. The company attaches importance to quality and a good working environment with attractive social benefits. We are looking for an activity for our client

  • Ensuring smooth operation at the allocated production facilities
  • Managing the respective machine group in compliance with the specified goals
  • Responsible for compliance with production management and quality specifications
  • Carrying out maintenance, service, repair work including preventive maintenance work
  • Assistance in projects in the machine room as well as in the development of solutions for improving machine performance
  • Co-responsible for training and support of employees at the plants
  • Requirements
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a polymechanic or similar training
  • Professional experience in the field of production technology and/or maintenance
  • Experience in the field of process control and automation, hydraulics and pneumatics skills are advantageous
  • Good German skills in word and writing, oral English skills are advantageous
  • Willingness to work in shift operation is required (rotating early shift from 05.30 a.m. to 2 p.m., late shift from 2 p.m. to 2 p.m. and also night shift from 22.30 a.m. to 5.30 a.m.) Offer
  • 40-hour week, at least 5 weeks of holidays
  • Canteen
  • Super Benefits and social services
  • Fitness room and sports courses
  • Very good remuneration
