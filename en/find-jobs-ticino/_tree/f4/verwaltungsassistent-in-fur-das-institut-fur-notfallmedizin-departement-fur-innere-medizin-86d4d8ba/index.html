Full description

USZ Campus

by arrangement, limited to 1 year with option of extension

The Institute of Emergency Medicine, located in the Department of Internal Medicine at the USZ, is the first point of contact for patients around the clock. For everything to run smoothly here, a strong organisation is also needed in the secretariat. This is where you come into play: With your commitment, you ensure that reports, services and inquiries are processed efficiently and that the interfaces to care, doctors, insurers and relatives function optimally. We award this position by agreement, for a limited period of one year with an option for renewal.

Your contribution makes the difference

  • You take over the reporting and control medical services
  • In the transition from TARMED to TARDOC you are actively involved
  • Classic secretarial tasks such as cost languages and evaluations are also part of your everyday life
  • You are the first contact person for care, doctors: inside, insurance, patient: inside and other partners, by phone as in writing
  • You process inquiries from our internal legal service and take care of complaint management
  • This also includes the administration of office supplies and absentee representation in the admin team of the Department of Internal Medicine.

What distinguishes you

  • Completed commercial training with EFZ, MPA or further training as medical secretary:in
  • Very good knowledge of medical terminology or the motivation to acquire this knowledge
  • Safe to use MS Office
  • Knowledge of CIS is a plus
  • High social skills and brings a hands-on mentality
  • Joy of contact with patients: inside, relatives and other interfaces
  • Very good knowledge of German, English and other languages are advantageous

What we offer you

In addition to the advanced working conditions in a university environment, we offer you a versatile, demanding and varied activity. We offer you the opportunity to design your workflows in a dynamic environment in a goal-oriented and independent manner. If you want to be part of our interdisciplinary and interprofessional team of the Institute of Emergency Medicine in the Department of Internal Medicine, where you are challenged and promoted, we look forward to your application.
