Full description

With its comprehensive range of services, our new hospital with state-of-the-art infrastructure secures the outpatient and stationary extended basic care for the canton of Uri. Our SGI-approved intensive care unit is a modern, interdisciplinary department with six beds, including two ventilation areas. The competent and sensitive support of our patients – as well as their relatives – in challenging situations is for us an expression of lived quality and professionalism. Our dedicated team of around 25 employees is looking forward to welcoming a new team member immediately or by appointment. YOUR TASKS

  • Monitoring and care of our intensive care patients
  • Patient-oriented care of all intensive care patients
  • Cooperation and participation in the further development of organizational and administrative processes
  • Accompaniment and support of students NDS HF in intensive care YOUR PROFIL
  • You are a professional with NDS intensive care and have found your passion in intensive care
  • Responsible, committed personality who contributes her experience effectively and likes to pass on knowledge
  • Team-oriented, communicative and open to the interdisciplinary exchange of your personalities
  • An experienced, committed team of nursing and medical staff
  • Flat hierarchies, a family environment and a lived DU culture
  • Attractive employment conditions with extensive social benefits
  • Possibility of a trial day to get to know our field of activity and team personally Our head of intensive care, Evelyne Simon, will be happy to provide you with further information on 041 875 55 00. We look forward to your application!
