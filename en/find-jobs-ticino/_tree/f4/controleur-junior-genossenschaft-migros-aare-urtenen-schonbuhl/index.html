Junior Controller*in — Genossenschaft Migros Aare

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

As a Junior Controller you get a broad insight into the controlling of Migros Aare. You will be carefully trained, gradually take on more responsibility and develop professionally in the direction of controllers or business partner controlling. You will be supported by an experienced controlling team that shares knowledge and works closely together.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operations headquarters Schönbühl, Industriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You support our business partners in reporting, forecasting, budgeting and multiannual planning
  • You create recurring evaluations, analyses and decision bases for management
  • You contribute to monthly financial statements and monthly division calculations and ensure high data quality
  • You support you in economic calculations as well as in cross-divisional projects and ad hoc analyses
  • You develop evaluations and reports in Excel, use Power BI for analysis and help to standardize and automate controlling processes
  • You gradually take on your own controlling topics

Requirements

  • First practical experience: in the finance, accounting or controlling environment, for example through a permanent position or an internship
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): or shortly before graduation or a comparable further education in business administration, finance or controlling
  • You work analytically, structured and carefully and enjoy dealing with numbers
  • You have very good knowledge of Excel, experience with SAP and Power BI are an advantage
  • You communicate comprehensibly, enjoy working with different specialist areas and appear appropriate to the addressee
  • You are willing to learn, flexible and motivated to gradually take on more responsibility
  • in German (negotiation confident)

Cosa of friamo

  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Migros Bank: Free account management, preferential interest on capital or a discount on interest rates on newly concluded fixed mortgages
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
  • Sports and fitness offers: cost-sharing of subscriptions to over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, aquaparks
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with high self-responsibility
