Full description

The logistics centre of the cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland includes transport logistics, intralogistics, production, technology, vehicle park and logistics service. Using the latest technologies, professional services are provided here that set new standards.

Mansioni

  • Independent operation and monitoring of the packaging line
  • Professional packaging and loading of meat and pan products for self-service sales
  • compacting the meat packs into packs according to specifications
  • Ensure correct marking and picking of self-propelled packages in compliance with quality standards
  • Operation of Multivac packaging systems and correction of simple failures
  • Assumption of tasks in work preparation (AVOR)

Requirements

  • specific professional experience: in the food industry
  • Basic or continuing education completed: beneficial
  • Team-oriented and flexible personality with high quality standards
  • Self-employed and efficient work, even in labour-intensive phases
  • Willingness to work flexible hours in accordance with seasonal requirements
  • Physically resilient and in good condition; used to lifting loads up to 15 kg and working in cooled environment
  • Safe handling of PC applications as well as experience with technical systems
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Company parking: Free parking directly at the workplace
