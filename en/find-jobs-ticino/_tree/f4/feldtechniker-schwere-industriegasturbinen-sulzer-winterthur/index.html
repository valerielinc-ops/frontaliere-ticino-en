Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. About the role Join our Sulzer Turbo Services team, part of Sulzer Services, which is one of the world's most advanced independent providers of repair, overhaul, and maintenance within the turbine industry. We provide our customers worldwide with solid, customer-oriented, and flexible support for their Gas Turbines. Come join our proud and highly skilled international crew as a Field Service Engineer for Heavy Industrial Gas Turbines The role can be based in any European country with a Sulzer presence. In this role you will be accountable for:

  • Monitoring or managing the technical aspects and quality of the overhauls and service activities based upon the technical specifications
  • Discussing the repairs and methods to be carried out with engineers and field service team. Stating the critical factors and establishing the scope and risks
  • Providing mechanical off site and on-site technical direction and supervision during maintenance events (inspections, revisions, commissioning) of customer owned Power Generation Facilities utilizing Gas Turbines manufactured by Siemens, Ansaldo, Alstom, General Electric and MHI
  • Provide mechanical off site and on-site technical direction and supervision during maintenance events (inspections) of customer owned Facilities utilizing rotating equipment
  • Ensure accurate installation/trouble shooting/maintenance & diagnostics of the rotating equipment heavy industrial gas turbines according to Sulzer Specifications, engineering instructions, drawings & process instructions
  • Manage site activities like planning/integration/monitoring of resources and technical assistance based on agreed contractual work scope
  • Supervise contract employees, manpower by providing directions, instructions, monitor quality and progress during Gas Turbine overhauls
  • The support of field service activities in a back-office role
  • The developments on rotating equipment related services and products To succeed in this role, you will need:
  • A bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering
  • 5 years’ experience in rotating equipment, Gas Turbines engineering and hands-on maintenance
  • Experience to understand and implement mechanical drawings (section, construction, assembly dwg, P&ID, alignment specification, flushing/blowing procedure, piping specification, etc.)
  • Very good English language skills
  • A proactive, flexible, and problem-solving attitude
  • Excellent interpersonal and communication skills What we offer you
  • Chance to make a significant impact and drive the global strategy across the marketing structure.
  • Collaborative and supportive work environment with a focus on innovation and excellence.
  • Exciting opportunities for professional growth and advancement in a dynamic and evolving industry.
  • Significant opportunities for global travel. Sulzer is an equal opportunity employer.
