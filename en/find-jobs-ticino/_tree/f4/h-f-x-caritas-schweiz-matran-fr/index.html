Full description

The Maison de Formation et d'Integration (MFI), composed of a multidisciplinary team of 20 people, is responsible for welcoming and housing persons who have obtained refugee status, whether minors, young adults or families. The MFI accompanies the residents for their first steps towards integration in the canton of Fribourg by offering them a professional and caring socio-educational follow-up. Access to occupation programs in the areas of concierge, cooking and stewardship. Pre-school classes that prepare children until they enter public school and a day care centre for the youngest. Unaccompanied minors benefit from an accompanying programme specifically adapted to their needs. In addition to the various integration activities, the MFI offers beneficiaries a safe and warm place to live in community, respecting each other's identity, journey and culture. Mansioni

  • To act as the reference person for the socio-educational accompaniment of the beneficiaries, including MNA
  • Welcome, support and support beneficiaries in their integration into the canton of Fribourg

  • Be a guarantor of supervision, compliance with regulations, rights and duties within the MFI and outside
  • Work actively within the educational team and with the various partners to provide quality services
