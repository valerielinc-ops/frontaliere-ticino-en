Full description

02.11.2026 Muotathal

Become part of our team!

To strengthen our consulting team, we are looking for a personality as a private client consultant who thinks, takes responsibility and feels comfortable in a manageable, family environment.

What awaits you?

As a private customer consultant, you are a central face at the counter and actively help ensure that our customers are well looked after. Your tasks are versatile and can be shaped – depending on the workload and strengths:

  • You create positive customer experiences and are the first point of contact at the counter
  • You accompany our customers in different life situations such as numbers, savings, investing and provisions
  • You are jointly responsible for cash management
  • You are open to project work and other side tasks such as foreign customer advice or help with marketing

What do you bring?

  • Ideally, you will bring a completed commercial (banking) education. Important for us is your interest in banking.
  • You have the opportunity to acquire the basics of banking business via a lateral entry program
  • Your pleasure in customer contact and your good communication skills distinguish you
  • You are a down-to-earth, reliable and team-capable personality
  • You ideally live in the Muotathal or in the immediate vicinity

We are one of the smallest Raiffeisen banks in Switzerland – and we are proud of that. With 16 employees, short distances and firm roots in the village, we stand for closeness, trust and personal relationships – with our customers as well as in the team. Bruno Betschart Chairman of the Bank Management Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.
