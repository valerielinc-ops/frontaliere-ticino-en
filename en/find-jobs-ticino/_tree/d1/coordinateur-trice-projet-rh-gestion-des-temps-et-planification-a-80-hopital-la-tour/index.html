Full description

The La Tour Hospital is a high-level, private, independent and human-scale acute care facility. Founded in its mission to restore the best possible quality of life for its patients, the Hospital de La Tour places continuous improvement, medical excellence and the accompaniment of its collaborators at the heart of its priorities. The institution employs more than 1,200 employees, representing more than 60 professions, and collaborates with more than 550 registered doctors.

We are recruiting

Coordinator HR Project – 80% HOM Time Management and Planning (ref: 2080)

As part of a time management and planning change project, the Human Resources Directorate of La Tour Hospital is strengthening its team.

A privileged interlocutor for the publisher's project manager, you make the operational link between the publisher, the Time Management/Pay team, the business-side project leader and other internal stakeholders. You help translate business needs into the tool, ensure consistency of management rules and prepare for deployment. A transversal and stimulating mission, ideal for a person who likes to reconcile regulatory rigour, project vision and proximity with teams.

Your mission

You ensure, on the establishment side, the operational coordination of the project to implement the new time management and planning tool, in order to ensure its regulatory compliance, its relevance to business needs and the quality of its deployment within the institution.

Coordinate the project and the relationship with the publisher

  • Ensure the daily link between business teams, time management/pay experts, business manager and publisher;
  • Prepare, organize and participate in workshops;
  • Collect, challenge and formalize business needs and functional requirements;
  • Follow actions, decisions, dependencies, risks and open points;
  • Contribute to operational governance and the preparation of project bodies;
  • Ensure schedule compliance and alert about required deviations or arbitrations.

Bring your time management expertise

  • Participate in the analysis, formalisation and harmonization of the rules on working time management;
  • Verify compliance of the rules and parameters with the Labour Act (LTr) and its Orders (OLT 1 and OLT 2), the applicable collective agreement and internal regulations, and with good practice;
  • Take into account the specificities of a hospital environment operating 24/7;
  • Integrate the diversity of hourly profiles, cycles, absences, meters, bonuses and allowances related to working time;
  • Identify organizational impacts and propose solutions to simplify or secure practices.

Pilot settings and functional validation

  • Participate in design and setting reviews with the editor;
  • Control the consistency of calculation rules, interfaces and functional processes;
  • Contribute to the definition of test scenarios and cases, including complex or exceptional situations;
  • Organize and coordinate the functional recipe with business users;
  • Document and track anomalies until resolved and validated.

Supporting change and structuring processes

  • Contribute to defining the target processes and clarifying roles and responsibilities;
  • Participate in the drafting of procedures, procedures, user guides and training materials;
  • Contribute to the support plan for Time/Pay Management teams, managers and employees;
  • Establish and maintain a network of key users by service, and contribute to their training;
  • Prepare and conduct information, awareness and training workshops;
  • Participate in communication actions related to the project.

Preparing Go Live and Stabilization

  • Contribute to the preparation of the transition to production and to the rocking plan;
  • Coordinate the recovery, control and reliability of data in the trade;
  • Follow with the publisher and the DSIO the implementation and recipe of interfaces (pay, planning, absence management);
  • Participate in the preparation of the proximity support device at startup;
  • Monitor incidents, corrections and adjustments during the stabilization phase;
  • Contribute to the assessment of deployment and formalisation of continuous improvement actions.

In particular, you will work with: the Time/Pay Management Team, the Business Project Manager, the Project Manager and the Editor's Consultants, the Human Resources Directorate, the Information Systems Directorate, as well as managers and representatives from operational sectors.

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

Training and experience

  • Advanced training in Human Resources, HR management or equivalent; specialization in labour law, time management, HRSI or organization is an asset;
  • Confirmed experience in an HR function with a strong time management component;
  • Confirmed practice of one or more time management and/or planning tools;
  • Experience of complex environments, ideally hospitalized or comparable: 24/7 activity, atypical hours, various cycles, diverse populations and multiple bonuses and allowances.

Technical expertise

  • Very good control of time management processes and their interactions with pay;
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss labour law in terms of working time (LTr, OLT 1 and OLT 2): working time, rest, absences, hours, night work, Sunday and public holidays, increases;
  • Ability to analyze and challenge management rules and parameterization proposals;
  • Controlling functional recipe methods and monitoring anomalies;
  • Good mastery of Excel and general ease with computer tools;
  • Ability to clearly formalize functional processes, rules and requirements.

Your qualities

  • Strong coordination and organizational capacity;
  • Rigor, reliability and a sense of detail;
  • Spirit of analysis, synthesis and orientation solutions;
  • Excellent relational skills and ability to unite diverse interlocutors;
  • Autonomy, proactivity and ability to alert appropriately;
  • Pragmatism and ability to reconcile regulatory requirements, operational needs and technical constraints;
  • Experience in a context of transformation and ability to accompany change.

We offer you

The opportunity to lead a strategic project for the institution, at the heart of the transformation of time management and planning practices, through direct contact with HR, Pay, DSIO, managers and the publisher. You will join a committed team in a dynamic environment where collaboration, continuous improvement and a sense of service are essential.

Type of contract: maximum term contract (MDC), entirely dedicated to the project

Activity rate: 80%

Duration: until production and stabilization of the new tool (approximately 12 months)

Start date : Last quarter 2027

Linkage: Human Resources Directorate

Workplace: La Tour Hospital, Meyrin, with regular interactions with the publisher and project stakeholders
