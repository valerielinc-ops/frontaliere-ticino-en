Full description

26.10.2026 Kaisten Laufenburg Sulz

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Regio Laufenburg, a successful and locally oriented bank with advisory points in Kaisten, Laufenburg and Sulz, we are looking for a motivated personality as a financial advisor who shapes and shapes our financial advice with enthusiasm and commitment.

What awaits you?

The Raiffeisenbank Regio Laufenburg stands for personal customer proximity, entrepreneurial action and a family working environment. As an independent bank, we focus on short paths, personal responsibility and employees who actively shape our future with their ideas and commitment. As a financial advisor, you take on a central role in advising and supporting our customers:

  • You consult with foresight. In the areas of pensions, insurance, retirement and taxes, you accompany our customers holistically and develop solutions that fit their individual life situation.
  • You develop investment solutions with added value. Based on the needs, goals and risk profile of your customers, you will develop suitable investment proposals and accompany their implementation.
  • You recognize potential and use opportunities. By actively and sustainably supporting your customer portfolio, you can expand existing customer relationships and create long-term added value.
  • You gain trust and new customers. With your sense for people and your winning nature, you inspire new customers for our bank.
  • You help shape our market success. In market development measures, customer events and representative tasks, you are actively involved and strengthen our presence in the region.
  • Your commitment counts. You are not a number with us. Your ideas, commitment and initiative are appreciated and make a real difference.

What do you bring?

  • You bring in-depth banking expertise. A completed banking education forms your basis. Ideally, you have supplemented your knowledge with further training in banking or finance.
  • You create trust through competence. Your many years of experience in the asset and investment business enables you to support our customers competently and individually and to develop sustainable solutions.
  • You recognize opportunities and act customer-oriented. Customer needs are the focus for you. At the same time, you bring strengths in sales, sales and acquisition.
  • You take responsibility and enjoy working in a team. Your own initiative, reliability and an appreciative cooperation are a matter of course for you.
  • You want to make a difference. As a flexible and performance-oriented personality, you actively engage and tackle challenges with commitment.

We advise our customers with passion and commitment. Are you interested in the financial markets, active in customer contact and enjoy working in a strong team? Then you are exactly right with us. Pascal Stäuble’ Head of Financial Advising and Member of Bank Management Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We give space for personal initiative and participation

We invite our employees to actively participate in their work and to take on responsibility. This creates varied tasks that create real added value – for everyone who wants to make a difference.

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.
