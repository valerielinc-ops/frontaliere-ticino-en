Full description

Install, commission and support Delvitech's AI optical inspection systems at customer sites, solving technical issues on the production line. Job Summary Our Service and Application team is looking for an Application Engineer (Post-Sales) who will be responsible for the installation, commissioning and technical support of our optical inspection systems used on SMT electronics assembly lines. After an initial training and shadowing period, you will manage service interventions from preparation to final verification, keeping customers informed on the progress of activities and taking on increasingly complex assignments with the support of our internal team. Key Responsibilities

  • Installation and commissioning: install and commission our systems at customer sites and verify their correct operation.
  • Configuration: set up and optimize inspection programs according to the customer's products and processes.
  • Troubleshooting: diagnose and resolve technical, software and application issues, both on site and remotely.
  • Training: train operators and technical staff on the use of the system.
  • Service: carry out the maintenance, upgrade and verification activities required throughout the system's life cycle.
  • Documentation and escalation: document all activities performed and involve the internal team when an issue requires further analysis or technical escalation. Requirements
  • Technical diploma in electronics or a related field is preferred; candidates who have gained equivalent skills through experience in the installation, commissioning or servicing of industrial equipment will also be considered. A university degree is not required.
  • Ability to diagnose faults, use technical instruments and explain technical issues and solutions clearly to customers.
  • Willingness to travel internationally approximately 30 to 40% of the time, according to installations and service needs.
  • Customer orientation, strong communication skills and a well-organized, independent working style.
  • Good knowledge of English. Nice to Have
  • Experience with SMT processes or industrial inspection systems. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
