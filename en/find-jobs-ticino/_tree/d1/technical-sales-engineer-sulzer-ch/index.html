Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

Technical Sales Engineer is responsible for providing technical expertise and support throughout the sales process, from initial customer engagement to post-sale implementation. This role involves analyzing customer requirements, preparing technical proposals, and delivering product demonstrations. The Sr. Sales Engineer ensures that solutions are technically sound and aligned with customer needs. Location: Almaty, Kazakhstan We are looking for a technically strong Sales Engineer for industrial pumps and rotating equipment. This role is not a pure sales position. The successful candidate should be able to understand customer processes, read technical documentation, discuss operating conditions, prepare technically correct offers, and support customers during project execution. At the same time, the role requires commercial initiative: regular customer visits, development of new opportunities, follow-up of installed base, preparation of technical presentations/seminars, and active contribution to order intake growth in Kazakhstan and Central Asia. The role is approximately 60–70% technical/application engineering and 30–40% commercial sales development.

Main responsibilities/Technical/Application sales

  • Analyze customer requests, technical specifications, data sheets, operating conditions, and tender requirements.
  • Prepare technically and commercially correct offers for industrial pumps, rotating equipment, and spare parts.
  • Clarify technical requirements with customers, Sulzer factories, tendering teams, engineering teams, and service teams.
  • Support the technical documentation approval process between customers and Sulzer factories.
  • Review customer comments to technical/commercial documents and coordinate required changes.
  • Participate in technical discussions with customers, design institutes, EPCs, and end users.

Customer and business development

  • Develop regional sales in Kazakhstan and Central Asia with focus on mining, metallurgy, chemical industry, mineral fertilizers, and general industry.
  • Maintain and develop relationships with existing customers.
  • Search for new customers, applications, projects, and installed-base opportunities.
  • Plan customer visits, technical meetings, seminars, webinars, and product presentations.
  • Build opportunity pipeline and contribute to order intake growth.

Tendering, order execution, and coordination

  • Prepare tender documentation, including work with electronic tender platforms.
  • Review commercial conditions and support contract approval with customers.
  • Register orders in SAP and coordinate order execution.
  • Monitor delivery timelines and communicate with customers, factories, logistics, accounting, and tendering teams.
  • Report activities, maintain forecasts, and provide regular updates on sales opportunities.

Requirements

  • Higher technical education: mechanical engineering, hydraulic engineering, process engineering, materials science, or similar.
  • Minimum 3 years of experience with industrial equipment, preferably pumps, compressors, rotating equipment, process equipment, or engineered products.
  • Ability to read and understand technical specifications, pump data sheets, drawings, and customer RFQs.
  • Understanding of centrifugal pump principles: flow, head, efficiency, NPSH, pump curves, operating point, cavitation risk.
  • Basic understanding of industrial materials: cast iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, duplex/superduplex, elastomers, coatings.
  • Experience with industrial customers in mining, metallurgy, chemical industry, oil & gas, power, water/wastewater, or general industry.
  • Working professional English and fluent Russian. Kazakh is an advantage.
