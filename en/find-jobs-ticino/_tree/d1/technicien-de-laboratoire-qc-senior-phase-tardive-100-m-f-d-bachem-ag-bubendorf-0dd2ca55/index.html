Full description

To drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified specialists. For our QC Late Phase department, we are currently looking for a dedicated Senior Lab Technician QC with a focus on HPLC (w/m/d) for independent and precise analytical work in a specialized team. Currently, we have an unlimited vacancy that provides exciting insights into the late phase of quality control.

Tasks

  • Analytic activities: Self-employed implementation of HPLC-/ U(H)PLC analyses in the context of release, stability and development projects. Professional preparation, operation and maintenance of the devices, including possibly troubleshooting, to ensure reliable results. - Documentation & evaluation: Careful documentation of all work steps and evaluation of raw data in compliance with relevant quality standards (GMP). - Quality assurance & compliance: Ensure that all laboratory activities comply with quality specifications and safety guidelines.

In case of quality events (OOX cases) Support in processing and documentation. - Teamwork: Close cooperation with QA, production and other internal areas for continuous improvement and quality assurance in the laboratory. Good communication and precise working methods to support efficient and smooth work in the team.

  • A well-defined seniority in the QC laboratory: expert knowledge in HPLC methods, high problem solving skills and the ability to identify methodological risks early, address them in a targeted manner and guide the team in a safe and ro-taxed execution.

Requirements

  • Completed vocational training as a chemical laboratory, preferably with a focus on analytics
  • Well-founded knowledge of HPLC/U(H)PLC and experience in independent analytical methods; LC-MS knowledge of advantage
  • professional experience in the pharmaceutical industry in the GMP environment
  • knowledge in the field of peptides
  • problem solving competence
  • well-defined organizational and fiancian skills
  • safe handling of computer-aided systems; proven experience with and A of efficiency to LIMS and Chromeleon
  • good German and English knowledge in word and writing

We offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities - Staff support through numerous internal and external training opportunities
