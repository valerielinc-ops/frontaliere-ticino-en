Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for cutting-edge medicine in the personal environment – true to our clinic goal “First-class medicine with personal treatment, as we wish for our loved ones”.

YOUR TASKS

  • You receive our patients warmly and accompany them from the first call to the following appointment
  • You organize consultation hours and appointments and are the interface to the medical profession, specialist areas and external partners
  • You keep the patient dossiers, record services and prepare the billing
  • You support our doctors with blood sampling, ECG, dressing changes and minor procedures
  • You provide ready-to-use treatment rooms and equipment and order the material
  • You get involved in quality topics, projects and events and help to further develop our processes

Your profile

  • You have completed an MPA education and have ideally advanced
  • You bring experience from an acute hospital or medical practice, preferably also in patient reception and practice administration
  • You keep an overview even in hectic moments and think solution-oriented
  • You work safely with Windows and Office and master German verbally and in writing perfectly
  • You act friendly, approach people empathetically and treat sensitive information discreetly
  • You work independently and reliably and are also ready for assignments outside the opening hours, for example at sports events or workshops

Maybe we have just the right thing for you! With us you will find a working environment that is characterized by appreciation, team spirit and humor. We offer you a varied job, interesting career and development opportunities, a family environment, at least 25 days of vacation, four additional paid days off as compensation for the changing time and many other employment highlights.
