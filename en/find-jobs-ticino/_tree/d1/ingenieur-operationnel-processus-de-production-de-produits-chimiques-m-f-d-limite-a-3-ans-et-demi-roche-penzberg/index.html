Responsibility | This is what awaits you Taken over in the role You are responsible for ensuring the functionality and technical availability of process and building facilities. In addition, you will take on the following exciting tasks:

  • Plant availability: Through efficient maintenance measures and continuous optimization, you ensure smooth operation and the cost-effectiveness of the technical plants.
  • Strategic Asset Management: You develop modern service concepts and maintenance strategies based on reliability studies and plant monitoring and maintain them in the SAP system.
  • Project management: You plan and control technical measures and projects in the field of process technology independently – from commissioning to timely implementation.
  • Budget responsibility: You support cost center planning for technical services, monitor the cost development of your measures and proactively initiate corrections in the event of deviations.
  • Lifecycle Management: You are responsible for seamless technical documentation and control data maintenance throughout the entire lifecycle of buildings and facilities.
  • Compliance & Safety: As a plant manager, you monitor compliance with legal requirements and quality standards (GMP/GEP) and ensure operational and traffic safety at all times.

Qualifications | This is what you bring

  • You have successfully completed a degree in mechanical engineering, process engineering, supply engineering or a comparable qualification.
  • You have several years of professional experience in plant construction, in the support of technical plants or in professional building operations.
  • You will bring in-depth specialist knowledge in the maintenance of technical facilities as well as in dealing with GMP and GEP guidelines.
  • You have experience in the application of IT systems (especially SAP) and have a strong understanding of chemical-process engineering relationships.
  • You speak negotiable English and generally have excellent communication skills. As a team player, you are able to work effectively with different stakeholders and communicate complex relationships in an understandable way.
