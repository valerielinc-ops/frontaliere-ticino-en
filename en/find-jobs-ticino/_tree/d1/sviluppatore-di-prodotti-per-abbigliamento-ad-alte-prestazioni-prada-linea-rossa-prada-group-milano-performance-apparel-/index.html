Full description

Founded in 1913 in Italy, the Prada Group was built on a tradition of excellence and with a vision of innovation. The Group, a world leader in the luxury sector, operates in over 45 countries with the brands PRADA, Miu Miu, Versace, Church's, Car Shoe and Luna Rossa and employs over 100 nationalities. ## Mansioni The Performance Apparel Product Developer/Development Assistant is part of the Prada Linea Rossa Product Development team and plays a central role in the development of technical clothing and performance, helping to transform creative vision into concrete, innovative and industrialized products. This figure combines technical skills, product sensitivity and organizational skills to ensure that each garment meets the highest quality standards, performance and aesthetic consistency of the brand. The Apparel Product Developer works in close synergy with Design, Modelling, Production and external suppliers, accompanying the product throughout the development cycle: from the initial concept to the final validation of the product. The role includes a strong focus on performance materials (waterproof, breathable, thermal, stretch) and innovative construction solutions. ## Responsibility

  • Collaboration with the Senior Product Developer and Design team to translate creative concepts into detailed technical specifications, ensuring feasibility, stylistic consistency and industrial sustainability of the product.
  • Manage and oversee the entire product development process, from the launch of prototypes to the validation of final products, ensuring compliance with timing, costs and quality standards.
  • Coordinate the development of high-performance technical garments, with particular attention to innovative materials (waterproof, breathable, thermal, stretch) and advanced construction solutions.
  • Interface with models, suppliers and productive partners, both in Italy and abroad, for the sharing of technical specifications, the collection of feedback and the management of product reviews.
  • Drive fitting and revision of prototypes, identifying and implementing technical and constructive improvements to optimize performance, fit and quality.
  • Insert and update technical data, separate base (BOM), material specifications and documentation produced within PLM systems, ensuring accuracy and traceability of information.
  • Support the introduction of new materials, finishes and technological solutions in collaboration with Design and Materials body, in line with market trends and contemporary consumer needs.
  • Coordinate internal and external stakeholders during the different phases of the project, facilitating communication and ensuring an efficient and structured workflow.
  • Organize and maintain updated archives of prototypes and samples, contributing to the construction of a reference technical historian for future collections.
  • Ensure that each developed product meets the quality, technical and aesthetic standards of the brand, contributing to the overall excellence of the collection. ## Requirements
  • Diploma and/or Degree in Fashion, Industrial Design or equivalent
  • 3/4 years of experience in Development Product sportswear/technical high performance and innovation
  • Excellent familiarity with PLM systems and Office package (in particular Excel)
  • Organizational skills, precision and detail orientation
  • Great knowledge of the English and Italian language
  • Excited propensity to innovation, with interest in new technologies, advanced materials and solutions The engine of our success is the importance and value we give to the talent and passion of our people, encouraging their professional growth.
