Description

  • ensures social intervention in its therapeutic and rehabilitative intent
  • intervenes in situations of its competence that affect the well-being of users
  • accompanies the psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care
  • coordinates its activity with that of operating institutions and solicits the contribution of private or public services in case of need
  • prepares reports for various authorities
  • contributes to the training of trainees ## Requirements
  • Swiss university education or SUP diploma as a social worker (option: social assistant) or equivalent title
  • ability to work both autonomously and in teams and ease in interpersonal relationships
  • flexibility and ability to adapt
  • previous experience in the exercise of the profession will be a preferred title
  • knowledge of the territory, social services and the Ticino canton's health network
  • perfect knowledge of the Italian language and knowledge of another official language spoken and written
  • driving license ## Observations
  • employment rate: 100%
  • place of work: Bellinzona
  • candidate's availability to work irregular hours
  • start date: to be agreed ## Application Submission Conditions
