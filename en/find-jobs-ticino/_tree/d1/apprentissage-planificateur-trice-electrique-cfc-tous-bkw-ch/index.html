Full description

We make spaces where it is good to live.

Learning began in 2027

As an electrical planner, you know where the current flows! In learning, you discover how electrical installations can be created and planned, so that each building is continuously fed in the future.

Your area of activity

During your four years of learning, you learn how to plan electrical projects, make plans, and monitor their implementation on construction sites. You prepare the project documents on computer and accompany the work on site.

  • You make plans for various electrical systems, including lighting, communication, safety and alarm systems, electrical mobility and photovoltaic installations.
  • You calculate and scale installations, such as lighting systems, power socket circuits and electrical connections.
  • Using modern design and planning software, you create detailed plans for mounting, wiring and connecting electrical installations.
  • You ask for offers from electrical installation companies, prepare estimates and check invoices.
  • You follow the progress of work and control the installation of equipment at the various stages of the project.

Your profile

  • Interest in technology
  • Good perception of colours
  • Communicative and teamwork
  • Good spatial imagination
