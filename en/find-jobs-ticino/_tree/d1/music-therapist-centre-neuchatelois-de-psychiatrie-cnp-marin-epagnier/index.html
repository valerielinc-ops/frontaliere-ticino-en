Full description

Join the CNP as an assistant physician, a dynamic institution.

Tasks

  • Conduct psychiatric examinations and medical diagnoses.
  • Coordinate care with the multidisciplinary team.
  • Participate in various projects and write medical records.

Competences

  • Diploma recognized by MEBEKO and training in psychiatry required.
  • Good knowledge of French, minimum B2 level.
  • Ability to work in a multidisciplinary team.

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients suffering from mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model; implements public policies to promote and prevent mental health

  • It acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research.

Assistant physician

You have a Swiss diploma or are recognised by MEBEKO. You have started your training in order to obtain the FMH title in psychiatry-psychotherapy and wish to integrate a recognized institution specialized in psychic health. You have a good knowledge of the French language (level B2).

We offer you

  • Several opportunities in various fields, in the outpatient sector, throughout the canton of Neuchâtel (Switzerland)
  • Access to a wide range of institutional, internal and external training
  • Attractive working conditions within dynamic multidisciplinary teams
  • A spirit of collegiality between departments, conducive to a coherent training plan
  • Opportunities for future development within our organization

Function-related tasks are motivating and varied

  • Management of psychiatric examinations of patients/residents
  • Participation in medical diagnosis and treatment implementation
  • Coordination of care with the multidisciplinary team
  • Preparation and monitoring of medical records, reports, exit letters
  • Participation in many projects
