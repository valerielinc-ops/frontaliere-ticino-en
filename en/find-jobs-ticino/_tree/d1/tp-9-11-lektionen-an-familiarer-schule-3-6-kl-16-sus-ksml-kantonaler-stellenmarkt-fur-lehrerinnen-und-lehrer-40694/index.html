Full description

Portrait The Matzwil School is something special: a charming schoolhouse surrounded by nature, which despite its hundred years of history is innovative and full of ideas. We are two school classes – a basic level and a 3rd to 6th grade – as well as a great team.

For more information click at https://www.schulematzwil.ch

We warmly welcome you!

Tasks

  • You teach nine lessons at our middle school (3rd–6th grade) with 16 children:
  • 3 Lessons from Franz 3./4. Kl.
  • 2 lessons TTG 3.-6. cl. (half class)
  • 2 lessons NMG 3.-6. Category
  • 2 lessons Music 3.-6. Category
  • Lessons Wednesday (5L) and Thursday (morning 2L, afternoon 2L)

Optionally, Monday afternoon could be taken with NMG / Sport (change after a quarter).

Requirements

You are an established, positive personality and share our passion for school and teaching development. You enjoy working with a small, family team and appreciate the exchange in the class team.

You have a teaching diploma or are about to acquire it. Ideally, you have experience with multi-level classes, alternatively, you are curious and open to age-mixed learning.

We offer

We offer you a school location where you can get involved and develop. We are an innovative school that at the same time maintains its traditions. We live the age-mixed learning and the cross-stage togetherness. Our school is special. We would be happy if you want to be part of it and we get to know you.

Continuation possible from August 2027.
