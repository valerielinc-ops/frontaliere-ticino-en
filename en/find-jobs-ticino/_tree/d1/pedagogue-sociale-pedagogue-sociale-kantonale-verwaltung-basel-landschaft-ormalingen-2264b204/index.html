Tasks

  • With 8-12 hours per week, you take on the responsibility for socio-educational support in lower-level classes (1st/2nd grade).
  • They are part of the educational class team. The cooperation with all participants should create the best possible development conditions for all students.

Requirements

  • For this position, you bring a diploma in social education or are about to acquire it.
  • They are used to maintaining open, appreciative and trusting communication with children and adults.

At the Ormalingen School, around 220 children are taught in 12 classes. We are an interdisciplinary team of about 40 people. We currently have three classes at each level: kindergarten, 1st/2nd grade, 3rd/4th grade, 5th/6th grade. At the Ormalingen School, motivated people work together and for each other!
