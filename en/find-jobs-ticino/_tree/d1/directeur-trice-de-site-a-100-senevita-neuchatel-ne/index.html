Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services to the elderly and has about 4000 employees. Ambulatory and stationary offers merge perfectly. While Senevita sites are synonymous with autonomous shelter and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading private service provider of help and home care throughout Switzerland.

For our site Senevita Casa in Neuchâtel, we look for a 100% site manager as soon as possible. Your tasks

  • Strategic management and site development: contribute to the definition and implementation of the strategy, increase the clientele and strengthen the presence of Senevita Casa, identify opportunities
  • Economic leadership and performance: monitoring and analysing economic indicators, monitoring results and implementing measures to achieve objectives
  • Operational management and process optimization: ensuring the proper functioning of the site, optimizing internal processes and ensuring compliance with company procedures, quality requirements and standards
  • Administrative and financial management: overseeing the monitoring of benefits, ensuring the reliability of data required for invoicing and monitoring of salary and financial elements
  • Leadership and human resources management: lead and mobilize teams, set objectives, develop skills and ensure efficient organisation and clear division of responsibilities
  • Quality of services and customer satisfaction: ensuring the quality and continuity of services, supervising complex situations and ensuring the satisfaction of clients and their relatives
  • Interdisciplinary coordination and collaboration: ensuring effective collaboration between care, planning and administration, and ensuring smooth communication between teams and different stakeholders

Your profile

Management and personal skills

  • Responsibility: Ability to assume overall responsibility for the economic and operational performance of the site, to analyse the results and take the necessary steps to achieve the objectives set
  • Leadership and management: ability to federate, mobilize and develop teams, support change and build a culture of responsibility, collaboration and commitment
  • Customer orientation: ability to reconcile quality of services, satisfaction of clients and their relatives, development of activity and control of resources, with a view to sustainable growth
  • Entrepreneurial spirit: a visionary, dynamic and pragmatic personality capable of identifying opportunities, carrying innovative initiatives and developing sustainable partnerships
  • Team spirit and sense of service: relational ease, ability to work in networks and to unite the different interlocutors around common projects

Training and work experience

  • Advanced training in business, business management or equivalent, complemented by several years of experience in operational pilotage and team management
  • Experience in business development, active exploration and partnership building, ideally in the health or service sector
  • A career path in the care sector is an asset
  • Good knowledge of medical and operational issues related to home care

Language and computer skills

  • Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written; knowledge of German is an advantage
  • Aisance with IT tools and digital solutions

  • A great autonomy in the exercise of your responsibilities, with the support of an experienced team
  • A function car and attractive conditions and benefits
  • The opportunity to actively contribute to the development of Senevita Casa and to meet entrepreneurial challenges
