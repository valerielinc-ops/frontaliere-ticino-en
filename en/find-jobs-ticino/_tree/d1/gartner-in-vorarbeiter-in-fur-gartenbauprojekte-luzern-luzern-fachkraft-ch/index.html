Full description

The independent management of construction sites and projects is the focus of this demanding position. You take over the professional leadership of a gardening team and coordinate missions efficiently. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • horticulture projects and construction sites independently lead
  • garden team professionally lead and coordinate assignments
  • implement new plants and changes in horticulture
  • create natural stone works, paving, walls and stairs
  • monitor work quality, efficiency and safety on construction sites
  • Requirements

  • Several years of practice in gardening and landscaping
  • Experience in team management and construction site organisation
  • Good oral knowledge of German and customer-oriented approach
  • Driver's card category B would be desirable Offer
