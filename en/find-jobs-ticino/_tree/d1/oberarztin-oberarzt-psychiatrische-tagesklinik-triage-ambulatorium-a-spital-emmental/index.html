Full description

The psychiatry of the Emmental Hospital offers demanding jobs for doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers and administrative staff with the opportunity for personal development and takes into account their own resources. In our manageable company, the personality of each individual employee shapes the way we deal with each other and our cooperation. We regard individuality as enrichment. We offer jobs at the two locations Burgdorf and Langnau. We are a SIWF-approved training institution.

Good psychiatry comes together. Are you a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy – or are you about to graduate? You want to take responsibility, contribute your ideas and actively shape a psychiatric offer instead of simply managing existing processes? Then we should get to know each other! The psychiatry at Emmental Hospital stands for short decision-making paths, close interprofessional cooperation and an environment in which different perspectives are expressly desired. In our day clinic in Burgdorf, we accompany people with mental illnesses in a semi-stationary setting – committed, evidence-based and with an eye for what people really need. We are looking for a personality who thinks professionally, convinces humanly and has the desire to make a difference together with us. Curiosity, team spirit and the willingness to find good solutions together are at least as important to us. 40 to 100% – you get involved, we find the right model together. For a smaller workload, your focus is in the day clinic. For a higher workload, you can also participate in the triage. Outpatient psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatments are also possible. Do you already have your own practice? All the better: We are open to part-time models that can be meaningfully combined with your practical work. What to expect from us – and what you can do

  • Medicine with responsibility: You take over the psychiatric diagnosis, treatment planning and therapeutic support of our patients. You have the necessary professional design room and a team at your side.
  • Teamwork instead of lone fighting: You work closely with different professional groups, coordinate treatment processes and bring your perspective into joint decisions. Because good ideas do not have a certain professional or hierarchical status.
  • Variety instead of routine: You conduct psychiatric-psychotherapeutic one-on-one meetings, assess complex situations and accompany people even in crises. Professional clarity and empathy are required.
  • Participating, thinking, shaping: You bring your ideas to the further development of the day clinic and can – depending on the workload – also shape the triage and the ambulatory offer. We welcome people who ask questions, question existing ones and make constructive suggestions.
  • Learn from each other: You participate in case discussions, share your knowledge and benefit from the professional exchange in the team. Nobody needs to know everything – but we want to be better together.
  • Working in a networked way: You maintain contact with the assignment offices, relatives and treatment partners and contribute to a good, continuous care.

What you bring

  • A specialist title in psychiatry and psychotherapy or you are about to graduate.
  • experience in psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment
  • Experience in semi-stationary setting is welcome, but not a must.
  • Enjoy complex clinical questions and the courage to think differentiated and reflect on decisions together, even in the event of uncertainty.
  • An independent, structured way of working and a patient-oriented attitude.
  • Team spirit, openness and desire for interprofessional cooperation at eye level.
  • Curiosity, initiative and joy in not only using an offer, but also developing it further.
  • And yes, humor helps. Not every treatment goes by textbook and not every day by plan. All the more important is a team that can hold together and laugh together even in demanding situations.
