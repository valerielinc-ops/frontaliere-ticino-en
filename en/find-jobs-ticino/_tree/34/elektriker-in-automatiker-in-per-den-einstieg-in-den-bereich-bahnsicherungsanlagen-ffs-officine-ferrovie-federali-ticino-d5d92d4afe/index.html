SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. As a safety system technician, you can locate and repair disturbances at our complex systems such as signal boxes and level crossings. You are the one who brings our customers back on track in an instant! Weekly you take over the pikettdienst – and of course there is a fair compensation. In addition, you are responsible for the maintenance, construction and of signalling and control systems. With a well-established team, check not only plants, but the smooth running of Switzerland’s largest transport company. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even for irregular working hours. For this, you can enjoy the free swimming pools and fitness centers during the week and make your free time more flexible. Night and weekend work is also paid for. You will also receive time allowances. This means you have more free time than others without night and weekend assignments. Our other benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under " Benefits". Further insights into the profession as a security system technician: inside you can get at the professional worlds of SBB or directly here: Technician: inside security systems | SBB You can move that.

  • You take care of the construction, maintenance and maintenance of infrastructure facilities and thus make an important contribution to smooth rail traffic.
  • A varied and diverse working day awaits you at various SBB railway stations in the Lucerne region.
  • You ensure the shutdown and commissioning of various security systems: From electronic signal boxes to relay signal boxes.
  • Through your systematic approach, you recognize and report the irregularities and defects and initiate their correction and further development.
  • You will undergo a two-year internal training as a technician for safety systems / safety systems. Here you will be trained to locate and repair disturbances on the interior systems independently.
  • With your newly learned expertise and experience, you can locate and repair disturbances at the security systems independently in the Pikettdienst.
