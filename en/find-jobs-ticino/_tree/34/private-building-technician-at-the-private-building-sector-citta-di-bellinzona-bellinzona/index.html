Full description

In Piazza 10 6528 Camorino AVVISO DI CONCORSO per L’ASSUNZIONE DI PERSONALE The Town Hall of Bellinzona opens a public competition for the appointment, with an occupational degree of 100%, of: a /a T ecnic or/a private building in the Private Building Sector.

Main tasks

  • advice to users, in particular in building procedures;
  • examines applications for construction and technical checks;
  • evasion of planning requests;
  • management of administrative practices of various kinds;
  • execution of yard checks and works carried out;
  • collaboration with other municipal and cantonal administrative services.

Formal and preferential requirements

  • diploma of civil engineer/architect, civil engineer/edile technician or federal certificate of capacity

(AFC) as a designer of civil/edile genius (or equivalent titles);

  • cantonal diploma of municipal technician or availability to attain within 3 years of recruitment;
  • are preferential requirements for the proven knowledge of public building law and

the experience gained in the field in public or private.

Aptitude requirements

  • precision, flexibility, reliability, personal responsibility, confidentiality and discretion;
  • aptitude for both independent and group work, good editorial and organizational skills;
  • strong relational and communicative qualities, mediation ability, propensity to contact with the user;
  • good knowledge of the municipal territory;
  • ability to use Office computer programs and design-based knowledge with CAD.

Documents to be produced

  1. accompanying letter; 2. curriculum vitae; 3. copy of the identity document; 4. copy of permission, if foreign; 5. jpg format photography; 6. copy of study certificates (examination and diploma notes) and work; 7. self-certificationconcerning the judicial box; 8. self-certification relating to the certificate of solvency (UEF); 9. self-certificationrelating to health. Self-certifications are available on www.bellinzona.ch/asks.

Entry into service

  • date from convent.

Special conditions

  • hiring refers to the Organic Regulation of employees of the City of Bellinzona (ROD);
  • applications supported by incomplete hearing will not be considered;
  • will not be considered valid offers and documents transmitted in paper, by fax or via e -

mail.

  • ROD conditions apply, in particular the following classes and annual salary (including 13a
