Full description

Located in the charming municipality of Lavigny, in the heart of the canton of Vaud, the Pietro Sarto Medical Centre aims to improve the care and follow-up of children with neurological, developmental and behavioural disorders. By working in synergy with various health professionals, we provide comprehensive and personalized care.

We are looking for our Pietro Sarto Centre

Medical Secretary (M/F) - Pietro Sarto Centre at 60%-80%

Entry into office to be agreed.

WE OFFER YOU

  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of redeeming an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time leave, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • Sports activities (Yoga, Pilates, walking races, discounts at various centres such as several fitness centres or the Bains de Lavey), events for the children of collaborators, a company drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Internal transfer facilitated according to your desire for development.
  • Many in-house or certifying training courses are available to you, as well as a training grant.

YOU MAY

  • A medical secretary's training, or title deemed equivalent.
  • A prior experience of at least two years in a medical secretariat in Switzerland, an asset, coupled with an excellent knowledge of medical terminology.
  • Recognized skills in the use of computer tools and excellent spelling.
  • Flexibility and versatility in order to provide doctors with quality support in the execution of all the activities of a medical secretariat.
  • A strong team spirit, autonomy and the desire to evolve in an environment conducive to collegiality.
  • Rigor, detail care and good management of priorities allowing you to cope with an ever-changing activity.
  • A relational ease allowing harmonious collaboration with the various medical-care workers.

OBJECTIVES

  • Ensure patient reception and orientation
  • Strike, reread and transmit medical reports and test results to internal and external physicians in a timely manner
  • Manage invoicing of medical procedures.
  • Follow up on all day-to-day administrative tasks involved in the proper functioning of the medical centre, such as managing emails and planning.
  • To guarantee the preparation, monitoring and archiving of patients' physical medical records

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation facilities, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal transformation according to the aspirations and development projects of the collaborators.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
