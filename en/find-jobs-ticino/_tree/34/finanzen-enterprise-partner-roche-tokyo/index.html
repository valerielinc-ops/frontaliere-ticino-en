The Opportunity

  • Lead Business Planning Processes: Direct and execute key local affiliate planning cycles, including the strategic business plan, annual budget, and monthly financial outlooks .
  • Drive Strategic Financial Modeling: Deliver comprehensive analytics, innovative insights, resource allocation models, and financial tools for the full affiliate P&L and balance sheet .

  • Execute Strategic Ad-Hoc Projects: Lead special financial projects and ad-hoc strategic assignments to support broader affiliate and enterprise objectives .

Who you are
