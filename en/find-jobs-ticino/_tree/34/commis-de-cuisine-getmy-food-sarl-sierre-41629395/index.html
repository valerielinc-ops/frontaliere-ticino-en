Full description

For the Christmas season 2026, we are looking for a kitchen clerk to join the team of our short-lived restaurant, L'Igloo de Noël, in the heart of the Christmas Village of Sierre. YOUR MISSIONS

  • Prepare and send the dishes on our menu: Valais specialities, fondues and traditional seasonal cuisine.
  • Ensure the establishment and smooth running of services.
  • Ensure the quality, regularity and presentation of the dishes.
  • Respect standards of hygiene and food safety.
  • Work effectively with the team, especially during periods of high influx.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Cooking training or equivalent work experience.
  • Ability to work effectively and manage shots.
  • Autonomy, organization, speed and team spirit.
  • Reliable, timely and dynamic person.
  • Availability in December, including evenings and weekends.

WE OFFER

  • A seasonal contract for the Christmas Village period.
  • Pay according to profile and experience, in accordance with the CCTT.
  • An original working environment, at the heart of an essential event in Sierre.
  • A professional team and a friendly working atmosphere.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed.
