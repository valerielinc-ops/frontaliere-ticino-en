Full description

Logistics (a) As a national and international transport and logistics company, Galliker Transport AG focuses on its core areas of Car, Cargo and Food Logistics. The Altishofen LU logistics park is the hub of the national and international Galliker network. We offer comprehensive storage, picking and assembly services for our customers. To complement our storage team, we are looking for a logistics provider (m/f/d).

  • carrying out warehousing work in goods entry and exit
  • picking orders
  • packing and provision of shipments
  • loading of consignments in trucks,s
  • logging in and out of warehousing movements in the computer system
  • completed professional apprenticeship logistics company EFZ / logistics-assistent / apprenticeship or good professional experience in the warehouse area
  • very good German knowledge (word / font)
  • forklift pass SUVA recognized
  • experienced computer users (MS Office, warehouse management programs)
  • reliable, flexible, independent, team-capable, freight-performing, quality conscious offer
  • top modern infrastructure
  • process-oriented working time flex models
  • modern break/canteen areas
  • internal and external training opportunities
