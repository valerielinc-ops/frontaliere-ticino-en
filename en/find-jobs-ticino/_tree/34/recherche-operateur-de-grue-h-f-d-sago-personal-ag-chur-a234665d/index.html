Full description

Their tasks

  • Independent operation and operation of construction cranes on civil engineering sites
  • Operation of top leno and bottom leno cranes
  • Perform safe and precise crane work as directed
  • Lifting, transporting and positioning of building materials and components
  • Loading and unloading of trucks with various construction materials
  • Setting of formwork elements, reinforcements and precast concrete parts
  • Transport of concrete troughs, pallets and other construction site materials
  • Ensuring a smooth material flow on the construction site
  • Cooperation with polishers, foremen and construction site personnel
  • Communication with attackers and instructors by radio or hand signals
  • Perform daily visual and functional checks on the crane
  • Checking of load handling equipment and impact materials
  • Detection and reporting of technical failures and safety deficiencies
  • Compliance with the permissible payloads and safety distances
  • Monitoring wind and weather conditions during crane operation
  • Support for general construction work, if necessary
  • Compliance with all safety and quality regulations on the construction site

Your profile

  • Mandatory crane driver’s certificate of category B (turret cranes) valid and recognised in Switzerland
  • At least 3 years of professional experience as a crane operator is advantageous
  • Very good knowledge in the operation of upper leno and lower leno cranes
  • Experience in civil engineering works
  • Safe handling of different types of crane and lifting equipment
  • Experience in precise lifting and positioning of heavy loads
  • Good knowledge of the applicable safety regulations in crane operation
  • Experience with radios and hand signals
  • Knowledge of striking and securing loads
  • Technical understanding and a strong sense of responsibility
  • Experience in carrying out daily crane and safety checks
  • Independent and reliable working method
  • High safety and quality awareness
  • Sufficient knowledge of German for a secure understanding on the construction site
  • Driving licence category B for passenger cars is advantageous
  • Own car not required
  • Vertigo-free and physically resilient
  • Concentrative, reliable and weatherproof
  • Accurate, independent and team-oriented working method
