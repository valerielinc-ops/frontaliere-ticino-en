Full description

Your flair for technology illuminates the most complex production plants? Then come to us in the Emmi and make sure that every day 1 million liters of milk flow through the right lines.

You can do that.

  • You will supervise, maintain and optimize the controls of our production facilities including Adaptations to PLC programs as well as the exchange and extension of peripherals.
  • Maintenance work – be it preventive or corrective – is routine for you, as is finding and fixing disruptions to production and building facilities.
  • You actively shape improvement processes and support investments to strengthen our technological leadership position.
  • The opportunity to lead projects and take responsibility is open to you.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as an automatist:in, an electrical installer:in or in a related profession with EFZ.
  • Further training in automation technology or a similar field is desirable.
  • You bring your first experience with programming in Siemens Step7 Classic or TIA Portal.
  • You have team spirit and the ability to work effectively in a dynamic environment.
  • Good knowledge of German is very important for this position.
  • You are ready to do a few weeks of spare time per year.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Sabbatical & Holiday Shopping: Give yourself unpaid holidays or extend your holiday entitlement by up to two additional weeks.
  • Further education and development opportunities: At Emmi, the focus is on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
