The Position About the team

We at Global Diagnostics Procurement (DOP) are a team of about 200 enthusiastic professionals, committed to driving value with our suppliers worldwide. From locations around the world and with a diverse and multicultural team, we source, manage our supplier base, develop & execute category strategies and constantly develop & digitize processes that enable us to best support our business to grow sustainably. If you aspire to make an impact and want to be part of a high performing and world-class Procurement team, this is for you. We are DOP. At Roche, we do now what patients need next.

The Opportunity

Are you ready to orchestrate the supply networks that make life-saving diagnostics possible? As our Senior Sourcing Manager in Sant Cugat, Spain, you will be a key part of our global sourcing operations. You will lead end-to-end sourcing projects within our diagnostic portfolios, mentor a rising team of Sourcing Managers, and serve as a key advisor to executive leadership. If you thrive on complexity and want to feel a direct impact on the delivery of life-saving diagnostics to patients worldwide, this is where you belong. To support our rapid growth, we are hiring a cohort of Senior Sourcing Managers who will be aligned to one of two specialized technology teams: Near Patient Care (NPC), Sensors & Cartridges, and External Manufacturing (EM) or Active Diagnostic Ingredients (ADI), Reagents, and Instruments.

  • Reporting Line: The position directly reports to the Sourcing Lead, and is part of the Procurement Center of Excellence (CoE).
  • Wider team and stakeholders: You will be part of a regional team of Senior Sourcing Managers and Sourcing Managers, managing relationships with other DOP stakeholders (Category, Delivery) as well as external suppliers and contract manufacturers.
  • Role Type: An Individual Contributor with responsibilities to mentor junior team members.
  • Impact: You drive global sourcing & contracting excellence, financial value, and compliance across the network footprint. Specifically, you are accountable for:
  • Performance & Savings: Driving key metrics—including PPV savings, preferred supplier share, and shortened cycle times—to deliver bottom-line value while tracking stakeholder satisfaction.
  • Sourcing & Analysis: Executing global sourcing activities, standard processes (RfIs, RfQs, RfPs), and NPI benchmarking in close collaboration with Category and New Product Introductions (NPI) managers.
  • Contract & Document Management: Setting up and reviewing high-complexity contracts (e.g., MSAs) and purchasing documentation, while accurately managing their lifecycle repository and renewals.
  • Operational Excellence & SME Leadership: Acting as a Subject Matter Expert (SME) to simplify workflows, analyze site user requirements for best-value methods, and ensure strict compliance with global policies.
  • Bottleneck Resolution & CSR: Partnering with support functions like Accounts Payable (AP) to clear supplier-related administrative bottlenecks, while championing supply base CSR initiatives (diversity, sustainability, ethical practices).
  • Conflict & Exposure: Managing high-stakes conflicts regarding competing project priorities or supplier selections, and delivering high-frequency monthly/quarterly updates to the CoE LT and DPLT.
