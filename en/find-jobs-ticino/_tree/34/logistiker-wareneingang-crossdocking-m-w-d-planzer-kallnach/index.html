Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Marti.

As a firmly rooted company in the Seeland region, we offer comprehensive transport and storage solutions and ensure cleanliness in over 30 communities with our waste transports. Our success is based on motivated employees and modern infrastructure. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

Tasks

  • You check the delivered goods carefully and make sure that everything is correct
  • You book the goods in the system and carefully record the expiration date and LOT according to the different customer instructions
  • Crossdocking processes are carried out efficiently and ensure that goods are correctly forwarded without detours
  • With various industrial trucks you move the goods safely and purposefully
  • Quality controls are part of your daily tasks so that only impeccable goods are further processed
  • You always keep the warehouse tidy and support your team flexibly with additional tasks to ensure a smooth process

Profile

  • You have a completed vocational training as a logistician EFZ and bring professional experience in logistics in the field of small parts picking
  • You are already a proud owner of a forklift card
  • Oral and written you can safely express yourself in German
  • Reliability, accuracy and rapid adaptability to new challenges set you apart
  • Even if it gets stressful, you stay cool and keep track.

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks of holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Fit and healthy at work? Health promotion is part of our BGM

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
