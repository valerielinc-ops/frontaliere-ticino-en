Full description

Become part of our team As a global company with ambitious growth goals, we are looking for people who want to make a difference. For our headquarters in Rümlang, we are seeking a Manager Media Relations & Executive Communications (m/f/d). In this high-visibility role, you will strengthen dormakaba’s corporate reputation as a trusted media relations professional and support our CEO with engaging, impactful executive communications. Your primary focus will be corporate and financial media relations (60–70%), complemented by executive communications (30–40%).

Your responsibilities

  • Manage corporate and financial media enquiries and act as a reliable corporate press-office contact.
  • Build and maintain trusted relationships with Swiss business and financial journalists and relevant international media.
  • Draft press releases, media statements, Q&As, briefings and interview materials; prepare executives for media interactions.
  • Support issues management, sensitive announcements and crisis communications with sound judgement and composure.
  • Identify proactive media opportunities and strengthen the company’s external positioning.
  • Partner closely with the CEO as a trusted communications advisor and constructive sparring partner.
  • Write speeches, talking points, video scripts and social media posts in the CEO’s authentic voice.
  • Moderate Town Halls and Leadership Events, and Conduct Lively, Professional Video Interviews with Executives.
  • Advise the CEO and Executive Committee on messaging, media impact and public positioning.

Your profile

  • Several years of proven corporate media relations or press-office experience, ideally in a listed company, consultancy or business journalism.
  • A strong network among Swiss business and financial journalists and an excellent understanding of corporate and financial topics.
  • Outstanding writing and editorial skills, with the ability to translate complex issues into compelling messages.
  • A confident, engaging and natural presence on stage and on camera, combined with excellent moderation skills.
  • The credibility, discretion and interpersonal skills to build a trusted relationship with the CEO and senior leaders.
  • Quick thinking, composure, energy and good humour, even in spontaneous or demanding situations.
  • Fluent English
  • German is a strong advantage.
  • Willingness to be regularly present at our Rümlang headquarters.

What we offer

  • A visible role with direct exposure to the CEO and Executive Committee.
  • The opportunity to shape media relations and executive communications at a global company.
  • An international environment, meaningful responsibilities and room for your ideas.

Who we are For every place that matters. dormakaba is a leading global provider of access solutions, helping people move seamlessly through safe and sustainable places. Join an international team committed to innovation and making a difference.
