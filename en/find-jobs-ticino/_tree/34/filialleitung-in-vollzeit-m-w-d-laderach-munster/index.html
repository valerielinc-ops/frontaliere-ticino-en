Full description

Branch Manager Münster Full-time (m/f/d) Fresh, craftsmanship, Switzerland – that's what the Läderach chocolate manufactory stands for. Founded in 1962, our family-owned company today employs over 2500 people from around 80 countries, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters is located in the canton of Glarus. Against a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here. The «Retail» business unit comprises over 200 chocolateries in 21 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you want to be a part of it? Then come to us. ## Responsibilities

  • Overall responsibility for the results of the sales outlet while adhering to the specified budget
  • Leadership, management, and motivation of the branch and branch employees
  • Ensuring quality standards
  • Ensuring and adhering to internal branch regulations
  • Communication with the company headquarters
  • Active participation in the branch and sales process ## Requirements
  • Leadership competence: team leadership and motivation: You should be able to lead a team, motivate it, and, if necessary, coach it to achieve goals together.
  • Communication skills: open communication: You should be able to communicate clearly and openly, both with your team and with customers and the company headquarters.
  • Organizational talent: time management: You must be able to handle various tasks simultaneously, such as personnel planning, goods management, and sales strategies, while always keeping an overview.
  • Sales talent: sales strategies: You should have a good sense of sales promotion and know how to present products attractively to increase sales.
  • Experience in retail
  • Industry experience: experience in retail, ideally in a leading position, helps you to better understand and apply the different aspects of business operations. ## What we offer
  • A stimulating work environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring forward fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate at the workplace
  • Good accessibility of the locations
  • Subsidy for the Germany ticket ## Contact Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Your contact person is Ms. Seda Öksüz and she will be happy to answer your questions by email at Personal-de@laderach.com or by phone at 0221-99967501.
