Description of department/team The position of Lab Support ensures smooth and efficient laboratory operations in the areas of Gene Therapy Cell Culture Development and Bio IV. The main tasks include comprehensive material and warehouse management, stringent compliance with laboratory hygiene, washing and laundry handling as well as basic equipment support (e.g. daily pH calibration). The reliable execution of these organisational, logistical and preparatory activities ensures a safe and highly efficient working environment for scientific research teams and laboratory staff.

Your responsibilities/position

  • Cleaning laboratory dishes in the kitchen using automatic dishwashers and cleaning the laboratory area in compliance with the hygiene plan
  • Sorting of clean dishes, placing in cabinets and drawers
  • Disposal of industrial waste, glass waste and paper
  • Filling of laboratory consumables
  • Implementation of orders and messengers
  • QS work (device responsibility and calibration of laboratory small devices in the GMP environment, e.g. thermomixers, refrigerators, scales and pH meters)

Who are you

  • You have a secondary school degree or comparable school education
  • We are also happy to welcome lateral entrants with a technical and chemical interest in laboratory work.
  • You have a structured, prudent, detail-oriented and quality-conscious way of working and organizational skills
  • Ideally, you already have experience in the laboratory environment
  • You have a strong sense of responsibility and deadlines
  • You have experience in handling the PC and knowledge of MS-Office products
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing

​ This position is limited to 2 years.
