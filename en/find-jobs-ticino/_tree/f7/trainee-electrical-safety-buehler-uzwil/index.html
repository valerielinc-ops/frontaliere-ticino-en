Full description

As part of the local EHS team, you will work in a global environment to further develop a sustainable structure for electrical safety. You coordinate with internal and external specialist offices as well as stakeholders on the entire campus – including those from production, customer service and development. This gives you insight into different business areas and helps to anchor electrical safety across all areas and in the long term.

Tasks

  • You are responsible for creating a sustainable electrical safety concept
  • You develop risk-based action plans, define responsibilities and document the test cycles
  • You take over the recording of the equipment inventory in a digital software solution
  • You create an implementation plan with priorities, evidence and the transfer to the line organization
  • You enjoy working with internal and external specialists as well as stakeholders from different business areas

Requirements

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering or completed training in Electrical Engineering or Electrical Installation
  • Independent working methods and a good understanding of complex relationships
  • Teamwork as well as an open, self-confident appearance and enjoyment of active exchange with different stakeholders
  • Knowledge of the application of MS Office
  • Negotiating knowledge of German and English
