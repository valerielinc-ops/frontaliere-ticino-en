We create motion. The FAULHABER transmission systems are masterpieces of precision and electromechanical mechanics, at the limit of what is technically possible. Take us with your enthusiasm and become part of our team.

An internship, many areas to discover

We welcome spontaneous applications for internships in the different areas of our company. The announcement is not limited to a specific field or course of studies: tell us what activities you are interested in and where you would like to experience. Each internship proposal is evaluated according to the training course, the area of interest and the possibility of reception of the department during the period required. To offer a useful experience and a proper accompaniment, the activation of an internship also depends on the availability of people who can follow you. For this reason, while appreciating every event of interest, we cannot always follow the application or propose an internship in the desired period.

Tell us what you want

Send your application a certain advance from the desired start, attaching your CV and a short presentation. To help us evaluate your request and direct it to the most suitable department, it indicates:

  • the department or area that represents your main interest and any activities you would like to deepen;
  • the course of studies or training you are following and the goals of your internship;
  • the desired period and the expected duration;
  • any requirements required by the school or educational institution.

We will be pleased to know your motivation and understand if we can build together an experience in line with your path.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
