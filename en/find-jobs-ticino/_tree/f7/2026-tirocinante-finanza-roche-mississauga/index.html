The Position Internship in Inclusion & Belonging International

The Inclusion & Belonging International (I&B) Office is at the heart of our People & Culture strategy. This internship offers you the unique opportunity to gain practical experience, help shape strategies and shape a truly inclusive environment. Located in Mannheim or Penzberg (Municipality of Munich), the position offers a flexible start date and opportunities for hybrid work to support your work-life balance.

Your possibilities In this role, you not only watch, but actively drive sustainable cultural change. You work closely with the international I&B team and network with executives, project managers and local impact networks (employee resource groups). You will become part of the vibrant, multicultural community of the Roche Intern Network and receive mentoring from experienced I&B experts – rounded off by attractive benefits.

Your main tasks

  • Country Impact Network & Community Enablement: You act as the central contact for Roche’s German Impact Networks (Business/Employee Resource Groups). You support them in the distribution of resources, the preparation of action days for cultural awareness and communication work.
  • Project Management & Operations: You support solving complex tasks and the core deliverables of the team. In addition, you actively promote day-to-day business using AI systems and innovative methods.
  • Engagement & Communication: You support in the preparation of network actions as well as internal and external events. In addition, you will carry out follow-ups, success measurements and social media activities.

You bring that with you You want to actively shape your professional future and are looking for a company in which your job title is only the beginning. As a passionate advocate of inclusion and belonging, you bring an empathetic, curious and open-hearted attitude towards different cultures. You are ready to promote creative ideas and use innovative solutions (including AI).

Required Qualifications & Skills

  • Education: You are currently enrolled in a Master’s degree program (ideally related to Inclusion & Belonging, e.g. Psychology, Business Administration, Sociology, HR, Global Studies)
  • Analytical skills: You’re adept at handling data, identifying trends, and translating numbers into clear, engaging PowerPoint or Google Slides presentations.
  • Organization: Structured way of working and the ability to coordinate multiple parallel tasks in a dynamic matrix environment.
  • Language skills: Negotiable knowledge of German and English in word and writing. Other languages are a plus.

Your benefits and framework conditions

  • Location: Mannheim or Penzberg (Municipality of Munich) / Hybrid work possible
  • Period: by agreement for a period of 6 months
  • Compensation: € 2,268 / month for a full-time internship (> 3 months)
  • Working time: Flexible time allocation
  • Food: 50% discount in the employee canteen
  • Health: Own fitness center on the Roche campus
  • Network: Strong exchange via the Roche Intern Network
