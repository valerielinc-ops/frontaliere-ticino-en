Team Leader Strategic Facilities Management Access to mobility 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team Leader Strategic Facilities Management Access to mobility Bern / Work Smart, immediate start or suitable, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

As a team leader, Strategic facility management for the "Access to mobility" division, you and your team are shaping the future of public installations and low voltage installations, and making an important contribution to customer-oriented, efficient and sustainable rail infrastructure. In doing so, you will guide your team during the next development phase from an organizational and specialized perspective. As a member of the "Strategic Facilities Management" management team, you are involved in the development of the strategic facilities management and ensure effective coordination of the strategy, funding and maintenance of the quality of the network. To find out what benefits we offer, such as children's allowances, free AG 1 class or foreign travel at a reduced price (also for your family), visit our website under Social Benefits. What you can move.

  • Within the new organizational unit, you lead and develop the team "Strategic Facilities Management Access to Mobility" by strengthening intersectoral collaboration.
  • In this role, you respond to the strategic development of facility management for all reception facilities and low-voltage consumers of SBB. You ensure that our customers have an efficient, safe and sustainable infrastructure when they train.
  • You ensure the long-term financial viability of maintenance and renewal. Your team is responsible for the technical development of the offer relating to the maintenance of the substance "Access to mobility" for the benefit agreement with the OFT.
  • You are moving forward from operational management to more strategic management and fostering a common vision within the team and its partner organizations.
  • With your team, you ensure the implementation of the facility strategy by coordinating strategic direction with facility managers in the four regions (West, South, Central and East) and working closely with project portfolio management.
  • You ensure that the facility strategy, performance, customer utility and profitability are in a sustainable way.

What you bring.

  • You have several years of driving experience and have successfully developed teams, ideally also in situations of change or integration.
  • You quickly analyze complex technical, organizational and financial issues and draw from them pragmatic and strategic decisions.
  • Thanks to your strong management of stakeholders, you are convincing, you negotiate equally and you manage to have different interests converge to achieve a common vision. It is easy for you to pass on your knowledge and experiences.
  • You have knowledge of the strategic management of facilities in the infrastructure environment. Experience in public transport or the railway environment is an asset.
  • You have a graduate degree (HES/EPF/university) in a technical or economic field, ideally complemented by continuing training in the field of driving, management or asset management.
  • You have very good knowledge of German, both oral and written. Knowledge in a second national language (French/Italian) is an asset. You can use your digital skills (informatics, MS Office and tools) daily.

Your salary and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This commitment is in accordance with the Code of Obligations, taking into account a comparison with the team, market and industry.
