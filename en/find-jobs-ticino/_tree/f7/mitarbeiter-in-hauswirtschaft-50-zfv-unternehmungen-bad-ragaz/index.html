Full description

The Sorell Hotels are the largest self-owned Swiss hotel group with 15 individual city and holiday hotels in the 3 and 4 star range in Zurich, Dübendorf, Winterthur, Bern, Rapperswil, St. Gallen, Schaffhausen and Bad Ragaz. The 4-star Sorell Hotel Tamina with its 50 rooms as well as seminar and banquet rooms for up to 150 people is located in the center of Bad Ragaz. The restaurant in the park, the garden terrace and the Tamina Lounge are a popular meeting place not only for hotel guests, but also for guests from the region. The breathtaking Sarganserland mountain landscape with the impressive Tamina Gorge, the Tamina Therme thermal bath right next door and the golf course make this personal 4-star house in Heidiland the perfect holiday resort. Are you our new team member who will support us as an employee in housekeeping (50%) immediately or by appointment? We are looking for 2 people each with a 50% workload. Working hours are spread between Monday and Sunday from 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. or from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Your task menu:

  • Cleaning and maintenance of the restaurant and department rooms
  • Creation of the mise en place and maintenance of the floor office
  • Implementation of the couverture service
  • Collaboration in the basic cleaning of all areas
  • Cleaning and maintenance of all areas in the hotel
  • Completion of porting tasks
  • Compliance with and implementation of hygiene and occupational safety guidelines Your recipe for success:
  • Professional experience in cleaning hotel rooms desirable
  • Experience in the cleaning industry
  • Basic oral and written knowledge of the German language
  • You like to lend a hand and keep track of things even when there is a high workload
  • You are a team player, helpful and open to learning new things What we offer you: We create teams that support each other and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that is good for you. Among other things, we attach great importance to pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, we offer an annual salary of around CHF 52,000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
