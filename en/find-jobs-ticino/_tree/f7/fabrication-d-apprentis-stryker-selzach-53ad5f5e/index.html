Full description

Work Flexibility: Onsite

Are you sociable, like to take on responsibility and like working independently? We offer you a demanding training period with versatile, practical and theoretical training on the job. Varied and interesting activities await you. You will also gain insights into various specialist areas of our company. As a balance to the vocational school and training company, we ensure a good exchange between our trainees with exciting activities.

This is what awaits you

  • In the 4-year apprenticeship as a polymechanic, you will learn skills for the manual and mechanical production of workpieces and components made of different materials.
  • The focus of your training will be on the production of high-precision implants by turning, milling and drilling using computer-controlled machine tools (CNC).
  • In addition to the realization of components, your training also includes operating, setting up, programming and maintaining machines.
  • You will go through various production departments based on a tailor-made flow plan, whereby we offer you the opportunity to actively participate.
  • In addition to training and the opportunity to participate in interdisciplinary project teams on small site projects, you will receive regular feedback to support your personal development.

This is what sets you apart

  • You have a good school leaving certificate.
  • You enjoy mathematics and physics.
  • You have a strong interest in technical issues as well as a high degree of diligence and organizational talent.
  • You enjoy working in a team and would like to contribute to our success with your skills.
  • Craftsmanship and good spatial imagination round off your profile.

We offer

  • Helpful colleagues and a friendly working atmosphere
  • In-house training workshop with modern machines
  • Intensive exam preparation & varied schedule
