Put to the Point

Actively shape the future of our buildings. In this versatile key position, you assume professional responsibility for building automation and shape its further development over the entire life cycle of our properties. They combine technical know-how with strategic thinking and bring together different specialist areas, project partners and specialists for intelligent, connected and high-quality buildings.

  • Date of entry: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 620-20586
  • Place of work: Fellerstrasse 21, 3027 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Further develop GEBA guidelines and integral automation concepts and ensure their implementation in new construction and renovation projects
  • External GEBA specialist planners and system integrators control and accompany as well as ensure the quality of concepts, specifications and functional descriptions
  • Accompanying projects from the strategy and concept phase through planning and tendering to commissioning, integral testing and final acceptance
  • Act as a central technical interface between project management, real estate and property management, facility management, IT and external partners
  • Provide expertise in strategic topics, specialist committees and working groups, develop standards and guidelines and advise internal offices on challenging GEBA issues

This makes them unique.

  • Higher technical education or a degree in building automation, building technology, electrical engineering or a comparable discipline
  • Several years of professional experience in building automation, building technology or in the planning and implementation of integral building technology projects
  • In-depth knowledge of building automation, system integration, commissioning and the relevant norms and standards
  • Experience in the management or technical control of external specialist planners and project partners as well as strong communication and negotiation skills
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented personality with high quality standards and strategic understanding
  • Active knowledge of a second official language
