Full description

A job with prospects: In addition to the state-of-the-art infrastructure and a versatile activity in the family environment, the Thun Hospital also offers you the opportunity for flexible leisure in the surrounding natural and lake region. Are you looking for meaningful, but also fair? Then you're right here.

The area of responsibility in digital radiology includes conventional diagnostics, computed tomography and MRI. You plan the patient appointments independently and complete the patient administration, including billing. For this purpose, our cross-location Ris and Pacs system is available. This is characterized by the complete integration into the entire company and guarantees a paper and filmless operation. Other modern devices such as DR, DSI and CT systems are also available. You are used in all services (24-hour emergency operation).

Admission immediately or by arrangement.
