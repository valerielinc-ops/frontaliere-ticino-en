Your assignment

Restructuring projects of electrical installations from A to Z You will take part in renovations of apartments, villas and residential buildings, taking care of the modernization, standardization and improvement of existing electrical systems. Read and interpret electrical schematics You will analyze the projects, schemes and technical documents in order to prepare and implement the works accurately, in compliance with the requirements of the project and the Swiss standards in force. Perform preparation and wiring work Execute the groove work with the help of a groover, sheathing and laying of cables, necessary for the realization of new plants or the modification of existing networks. Install and connect electrical equipment It guarantees the installation, connection and commissioning of equipment, electrical panels, sockets, switches, lighting fixtures and other equipment, in compliance with NIBT norms and company quality standards. Intervention on CFO/CFA plants Perform installation, modification and commissioning of strong current and weak current equipment (CFO/CFA) ensuring reliability, safety and proper operation. Ensure fault repair and adjustments You are able to diagnose faults and malfunctions, perform the necessary interventions and adapt existing systems according to the needs of the customer and the constraints of the building. Working independently and representing the company Autonomous in your yards, you are regularly in direct contact with customers.Represent the company with professionalism and make sure you offer a quality service, from the preparation of the works to their completion. And now we're talking about you? A recognised technical training You have an AFC as an electrical installer or an equivalent training recognized in Switzerland. You have the technical skills necessary to take autonomous action on projects to restructure electrical installations. A proven experience in restructuring You have gained several years of experience in the renovation of villas, apartments and residential buildings. Master the work of transformation, modernization and adaptation to the norms of existing electrical systems. A real autonomy in the field Used to manage your yards independently, you can organize your work, anticipate your needs and take initiatives .You are able to conduct your interventions effectively, from preparation to completion of the work. An excellent sense of service Professional, reliable and equipped with a curated presentation, you feel comfortable in dealing with customers and the various subjects involved. Your sense of service contributes to conveying a positive and professional image of the company. Solid technical knowledge Master the main techniques for the renovation and transformation of electrical installations . A good understanding of the current NIBT standards is an important advantage in ensuring consistent and safe interventions. Mobility and availability In possession of the driver's license, you are mobile and at ease with everyday travel between the different yards. Quick availability is highly appreciated to be part of our teams as soon as possible. A reliable and committed professional Rigorous, conscientious and motivated, you have the taste for well done work and attach particular importance to quality, safety and customer satisfaction. Your commitment directly contributes to the success of your projects.
