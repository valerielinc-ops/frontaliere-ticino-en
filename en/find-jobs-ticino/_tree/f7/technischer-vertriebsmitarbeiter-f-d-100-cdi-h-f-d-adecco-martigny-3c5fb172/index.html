Introduction

Are you passionate about building technology and customer relationships? Would you like to develop in a dynamic environment and offer comprehensive solutions and concepts in the field of fire protection? Then apply now!

Technical sales representative F/D 100% CDI (M/F/D)

Tasks

  • Establishment and maintenance of customer relationships (offers, invoicing, tracking)
  • Establishment of timetables and organisation of work tracking
  • Coordination and support of teams on construction sites
  • Technical and administrative follow-up of construction sites Management of material orders and implementation of tenders
  • Presentation of fire protection solutions and concepts for customers and interested parties
  • Participation in management meetings and ensuring the sharing of internal information

Profile

  • Basic training in construction
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in construction with a focus on building construction
  • Sound knowledge of building standards, ideally in the field of fire protection
  • Secure appearance in project management and execution monitoring
  • Strong customer and sales orientation
  • Bilingual French/German or Swiss German
  • Very good knowledge of the Valais market
  • Organized, independent and solution-oriented personality

Advantages

  • Diverse position that combines technology, management and business development
  • Stable working environment in a specialized company
  • Attractive employment conditions according to your skills and experience
  • Start of work: immediately or by agreement
