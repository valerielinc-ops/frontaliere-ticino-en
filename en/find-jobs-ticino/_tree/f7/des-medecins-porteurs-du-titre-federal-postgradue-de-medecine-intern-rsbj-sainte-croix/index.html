Full description

Type of Contract: CDI Rate: 100% Date of entry: immediately or to be agreed

The Balcony of Jura.vd Health Network (RSBJ) is a recognized association of public interest that provides integrated care through close collaboration between its hospital structure, a medical-social institution, medical-social support structures, two group medical offices and a health prevention and promotion centre. It works with all regional health partners, including home care, NBSR and HNVs. In order to complete the team of doctors installed in its group medical offices in Baulmes and Sainte-Croix, the RSBJ is looking for:

Doctors with the Postgraduate Federal Title of General Internal Medicine (FMH) Flexible work rate between 50 and 100% Possible mixed post (if desired) with hospital activity You...

  • Benefit from a PhD in medicine and a post-graduate degree (FMH) in general internal medicine. A few years' experience would be a plus
  • Have good communication skills
  • Experience and interest in interdisciplinary work
  • Master the French language (oral and written), minimum level C1
  • Have interpersonal skills and flexibility

Your mission...

  • Provide a first-recourse general family medicine activity
  • Provide medical consultations and follow-up to patients and ensure the proper conduct of the administrative process
  • Completing a team of two doctors in a group practice
  • Care

interdisciplinary patient collaboration with network partners and ensure continuity of care

  • Join a health network that highlights integrated care, prevention and the work of city/hospital teams (see Strategic plan available on our website www.rsbj.ch )

We offer...

  • The opportunity for an independent physician to enter a medical practice, the organization and operation of which is ensured by the RSBJ (infrastructure, administrative staff, support of shared services of the RSBJ, etc.).
  • A collaboration with the Medical Directorate of the RSBJ which provides support and facilitates access to specialized hospital services, the technical platform of the RSBJ including radiology, as well as the support of senior doctors of the hospital centre if necessary
  • Excellent working conditions
  • An activity within an institution promoting participatory management, the possibility to participate actively in various institutional projects
  • Partnership work in a regional medical college

Entry into office: immediately or to be agreed
