Full description

You coordinate our electrical projects on site – your work turns our customers into fans.

Responsibilities

  • Lead electrical installation teams and manage a variety of projects in the commercial, industrial, and service sectors
  • Coordinate work with site management, suppliers, and skilled tradespeople from other disciplines
  • Carry out new installations, maintenance work, and repairs
  • Pass on your technical expertise in a targeted way to employees and apprentices

Requirements

  • Completed basic training as an electrician EFZ, installation electrician EFZ, or an equivalent qualification recognized in Switzerland
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally also in a leadership role
  • Good German skills (spoken and written)
  • Category B driver's licence

About us

ETAVIS is the optimal partner for innovative electrical engineering, communication, and automation solutions in Switzerland and Liechtenstein. We provide tailor-made, high-quality solutions for business customers of all sectors and sizes, as well as customised services for private customers. As part of the VINCI Group, we benefit from global innovative strength and are at the same time locally rooted with our around 60 branch offices. As the largest small business, we operate with a more personal and family-like approach – here, every single person counts. With over 2,400 employees and several hundred apprentices, we work every day towards a more sustainable, better-connected world.
