Full description

Privatklinik Wyss AG is a leading specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy. Special areas of expertise include the treatment of depression including burnout as well as anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders. The clinic offers its outpatient, daily clinical and inpatient services in the region of Bern, Münchenbuchsee and Biel and employs around 340 employees at all three locations. Senior doctor / Senior physician (m/f/d) employment level 80% - 100% employment relationship permanent location Privatklinik Wyss AG Start of work as of 1. October 2026 or by agreement No documentation from recruitment agencies will be considered for this position. #Psychiatry, #responsibility, #teamwork, #development A meaningful task with design scope and clinical depth in an appreciative environment awaits you! To strengthen the specialist cadre, we are looking for 1. October 2026 or by appointment a specialist FMH for psychiatry and psychotherapy. The position is suitable for flexible and independent colleagues with completed specialist training in psychiatry and psychotherapy (or shortly before completing the training curriculum). Do you enjoy varied clinical work with a focus on inpatient psychotherapy, taking over case management and appreciate the regular exchange with an interprofessional team? We look forward to your application. You can also get involved in the station management and, if appropriate, train as a leading doctor. This is what your professional day looks like with us in this position Together with the chief physician, you become involved in the supervision and teaching of case-leading psychologists and assistant doctors, take over case management yourself and coordinate psychiatric diagnosis and treatments. You are responsible for the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic patient treatment in the individual setting e.g. on the ward for supplementary insured persons and work closely with an interprofessional team. In the absence of the chief physician, you represent the professional management of the private department (25 beds). In addition, You will carry out organizational tasks such as triage, correspondence and cost languages, support your team in further education and training and participate in interdisciplinary cooperation, internal training and working groups. Background services as well as networking and public relations work round off your versatile field of activity. You have a completed state examination in medicine (CH) or an equivalent qualification from the EU/EFTA as well as a specialist qualification in psychiatry and psychotherapy. Well-founded knowledge of psychotherapy as well as very good knowledge of German in words and writing are essential for this position. Your commitment, commitment and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation characterize you. In addition, you attach great importance to a respectful treatment of patients, relatives and colleagues. We offer you a structured and efficient introduction in the workplace as well as a pleasant working atmosphere and a unique company culture in a committed interprofessional team. You will receive professional and leadership-related support from your manager and benefit from a wide range of development opportunities. In addition, if you are interested, you have the opportunity to gain leadership experience, develop yourself in the long term and get to know various treatment settings through rotations – in a recognized FMH training centre for psychiatry and psychotherapy. You can expect a varied and professionally independent work in a future-oriented company. In addition, we offer attractive employment conditions, a modern infrastructure and a wide range of internal and external training opportunities.
