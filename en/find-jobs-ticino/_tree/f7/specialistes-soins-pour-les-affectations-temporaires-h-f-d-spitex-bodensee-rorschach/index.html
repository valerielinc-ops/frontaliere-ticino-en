Spitex Bodensee – Care at home in the Goldach / Rorschach region Spitex Bodensee is a regionally anchored non-profit organization and provides ambulatory care and support in the communities of Goldach, Mörschwil, Rorschach, Rorschacherberg, Tübach and Untereggen. Care takes more than routine. With us, you work in clear structures, with reliable processes and a team that holds together. If you want to practice your profession actively and independently, you are right with us. We provide basic and treatment care services in accordance with KLV and enable our clients to provide professional care in their own home. For the temporary reinforcement of our team, we are looking for dedicated and reliable specialists for outpatient care. Specialists Care for temporary assignments (m/f/d) Do you want to make a difference for a certain period of time? We have the right job for you! Not every professional decision has to be long-term. Sometimes a temporary employment fits exactly the current life situation – be it for bridging, for a professional reorientation, during a further education or simply because you want to contribute your expertise for a certain period of time. At Spitex Bodensee you can expect a meaningful task with a lot of personal responsibility, personal encounters and a team that holds together. Your area of application

  • You nurture and look after our clients in their familiar home.
  • You carry out basic and treatment care according to your qualifications and competences.
  • You promote the independence and quality of life of the cared for people.
  • You observe changes, assess care situations and document your work professionally.
  • You work closely with relatives, nurses and other specialists.

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a specialist health EFZ, qualified nurse HF/FH or a corresponding qualification in the nursing assistant area.
  • Delight in ambulatory care and direct contact with people.
  • Self-employment, a sense of responsibility and a reliable working method.
  • Flexibility and openness for a temporary deployment.
  • Category B driving licence.

You can look forward to that.

  • Flexible Pensen: We take into account your availability and living situation as far as possible.
  • No night services: Your assignments take place between 7 a.m. and 10 p.m.
  • Schedulable working hours: Service planning with optional.
  • Self-employment: Self-responsible work with clear structures and processes.
  • Team support: You are also a full part of our team during a temporary assignment.
  • Good work equipment: Modern digital documentation as well as company vehicles and e-bikes.
  • Perspective: In the case of mutual interest and corresponding need, we are happy to consider an extension or a further employment.
