Contact Line Technician, 100% CFF - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Contact Line Technician, Chief Editor Neuchâtel, 01.01.2027 or suitable, 100% Apply here

What awaits you.

In collaboration with your team colleagues, you develop and implement solutions to meet existing challenges. Your contribution allows teams to focus on their goals while ensuring the quality, rigour and smoothness of processes. You are evolving in a collaborative environment that values mutual support, information sharing and continuous improvement with concrete learning and development opportunities. Social benefits: To find out about benefits, such as free AG and reduced-cost overseas travel (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits What you can move.

  • You work professionally in the construction and maintenance of contact line facilities to ensure the availability, reliability and safety of the facilities in a cost-effective manner.
  • You manage construction, rehabilitation and/or maintenance by following the applicable construction and/or maintenance guidelines.
  • You drive the employees on the construction sites, monitor the correct execution of the works and ensure, as the head of safety, that the safety measures are respected in accordance with the ad hoc arrangements
  • Preparation of the work preparation for the work, management of the assignments on the worksites, ensuring the specialized management of the employees and monitoring the correct execution of the work as well as compliance with safety instructions

What you bring.

  • CFC holder in an electric field with several years of experience in the construction of contact line (catenary). Experiences and knowledge of maintenance processes and operating conditions in the field of the contact line are essential.
  • High sense of responsibility and sensitivity to safety issues, good resistance to stress. Experience in team management in the field required.
  • Head of security TEN 20100 or ready for training. Directorate of security TEN 20100 or ready to take training.
  • Easy use of computer tools, good health and optimal hearing and visual abilities.
  • Home close to the workplace (possibility to visit within 30-45 minutes).
  • Driver's licence B compulsory.
