St. Moritz is one of the most famous resorts in the world. Its name is protected as a quality brand and stands for style, elegance and class worldwide. It offers locals and guests the highest quality of life. In the OVAVERVA indoor pool, spa & sports centre, your source of power fun and well-being, you can draw on the full sporting and health! For the OVAVERVA Bistro we are looking for immediately or by agreement:

Their tasks The OVAVERVA Bistro is a self-service restaurant with attractive service hours. The bistro is open daily from 09:00 to 21:00. With coolness you can cope with the hectic pace of everyday life:
