Full description

Social Assistants General competition valid for the year 2026 Pro Senectute Ticino and Moesano, the largest cantonal organization of services and services for the maintenance of elderly people at home, for over a hundred years present in a widespread way throughout the territory thanks to an important number of regional offices and structures, opens the following general competition: Social Assistants Location description: – Creating, maintaining and restoring the user’s autonomy, trying to strengthen his resources and foster his participation in social life; – Ensure the material existence of the user who lives in a precarious situation; – Facilitate the lives of people belonging to vulnerable groups; – Possibility to assume responsibility tasks.

  • Requirements essential to the position: – Bachelor of Science SUP in social work, social assistant option (or equivalent); – Driving license and availability of own cars; – familiarity with the use of the usual computer tools; – Good relational, communication and listening skills; – Spirit of initiative, autonomy and decision-making skills; – Confidentiality and discretion.
  • We offer: A new challenge, a varied, interesting and stimulating occupation to be carried out in a professional and pleasant working environment within the Social Counseling and Information Service. Compulsory application documentation: – Curriculum vitae updated; – Letter of presentation; – Diplomas and certificates (the equivalence certificates are mandatory for foreign titles); – Certificates of work and references. Further documentation may be requested during the selection process: the interested parties are invited to submit their application by e-mail to concorsi@prosenectute.org. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid. This competition shall also be open in the absence of an actual requirement of person. As an annual competition, with the possibility of being recruited during 2026, candidates will not be given a positive or negative answer. Application data will be deleted at the end of the competition.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
