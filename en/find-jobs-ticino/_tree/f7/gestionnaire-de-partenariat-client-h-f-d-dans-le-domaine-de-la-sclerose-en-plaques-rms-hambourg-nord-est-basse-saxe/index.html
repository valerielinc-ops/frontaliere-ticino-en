The Position Customer Partnering Manager (CPM) (m/f/d) takes over You have a holistic, customer-oriented key role in an ever-changing market environment. You actively shape healthcare throughout the patient journey by representing our therapeutic portfolio, communicating scientific findings and working closely with interdisciplinary teams. In doing so, you will analyze regional care structures in order to reduce hurdles in the system, ensure the targeted positioning and use of our products for suitable patients and establish sustainable partnerships in the healthcare sector.

This is what awaits you

  • Customer contact & sales: You act as the primary interface for target customers in your area and use your communication and sales expertise to promote the appropriate use of our portfolio with suitable patients.
  • Portfolio & Positioning: You represent our therapy portfolio through high-impact customer engagement and scientifically sound advice in the approved area.
  • Scientific exchange: You communicate current research results on a scientific level and answer complex inquiries objectively and transparently.
  • Project & event management: You plan, organise and evaluate medical training, product training and regional projects independently.
  • Cross-functional cooperation: You work closely with internal experts and bring valuable market insights to interdisciplinary teams

You bring that with you We are looking for a committed person with a strong customer focus who enjoys building networks and innovating in healthcare.

  • Formal requirement: Qualification as a pharmaceutical consultant: in accordance with § 75 AMG and a valid driving license.
  • In-depth training: completed university studies (medicine, natural sciences, business administration) or a comparable qualification.
  • Industry experience: Experience in a customer-facing role in the pharmaceutical industry as well as in-depth knowledge of the German healthcare system and its regulatory framework.
  • Strength of communication: Confident knowledge of German and a very good understanding of English literature and studies.
  • Digital competence: Secure application of modern IT tools (e.g. Google Suite, MS Office, AI tools) as well as experience with digital and hybrid communication channels.
