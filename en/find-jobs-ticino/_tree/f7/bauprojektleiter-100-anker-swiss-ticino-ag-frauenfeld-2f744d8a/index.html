Full description

Construction Project Manager 100% Your next career step starts here Design construction projects with ambition and effect. In a modern working environment with short decision-making paths and a lot of personal responsibility, you take on a central role in the successful implementation of our projects. From now on or by agreement we are looking for a construction project manager EFZ (m/f).

Your challenge

  • Management of challenging construction projects over all project phases
  • Coordination of building owners, authorities, specialist planners and entrepreneurs
  • Responsibility for deadlines, costs and quality
  • Preparation of tenders and awards
  • Management and logging of project and planning meetings
  • Monitoring of approval and implementation processes
  • Monitoring of project objectives from planning to handover
  • Ensuring efficient and economical project implementation

This is what you bring with you

  • Training as architect FH/ETH, technician HF building construction or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in project management of building construction projects
  • In-depth knowledge of Swiss construction processes and standards
  • Experience in the coordination of specialist planners and entrepreneurs
  • Safe handling of CAD and MS Office applications
  • Strong cost, quality and deadline awareness
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

What we offer you

We are a family-owned architecture firm with a focus on high-quality residential construction projects in timber and solid construction. Thanks to state-of-the-art digital planning processes, short decision-making paths and a well-established team, we realize individual new and replacement buildings with the highest quality standards.
