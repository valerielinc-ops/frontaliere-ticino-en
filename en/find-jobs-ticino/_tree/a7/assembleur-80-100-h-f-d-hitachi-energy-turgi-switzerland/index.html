Full description

About the position As an assembler, you are responsible for the professional assembly and wiring of modules and systems. You will work with design drawings, schemes and detailed work instructions to ensure that our products meet high quality and safety requirements. Depending on the production order, you work in small teams, in the workshop or in line assembly. In addition, you contribute your experience, support new employees with their training and contribute to an efficient and smooth production process.

You can do that.

  • Professional assembly and wiring of modules and systems according to design drawings, diagrams and detailed work instructions
  • Execution of production orders in compliance with specified quality and safety standards
  • Collaboration in teams of 2 and 3 in joint production orders as well as in workshops, line assembly or individual workstations
  • Ensuring careful and high-quality execution of assembly and wiring operations
  • Support for new and temporary employees through knowledge transfer and practical guidance
  • Active participation in an efficient production process and reliable implementation of the tasks involved

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as an automatic technician/EFZ or several years of professional experience as a professional in the field of electrical cabinet construction, preferably in industry or on construction sites
  • Technical understanding and ability to safely read and implement design drawings, schemes and work instructions
  • Independent and reliable working methods as well as a strong quality and safety awareness
  • Teamwork, willingness and joy of working together in small production teams
  • Good computer skills are beneficial
  • Fluent knowledge of German and willingness to work overtime if necessary

More about us Hitachi Energy is shaping the energy future with innovative technologies for a sustainable and reliable energy supply. With us, you can expect a modern working environment, a strong team and the opportunity to actively participate in future-oriented solutions.
