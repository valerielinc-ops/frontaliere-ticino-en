Would you like to move to outpatient pediatrics after your specialist training and benefit from working with an experienced pediatrician? In this modern group practice, you can expect an already well-established patient base and a versatile range of pediatric activities. The modern and generously equipped group practice in the Canton of Aargau combines pediatric and adolescent medicine, general medicine and cardiology under one roof. Due to the high demand for paediatric care, the paediatric area is to be further strengthened. Together with an experienced and already very well utilized pediatrician, you look after children and adolescents from birth to the transition to adulthood. Especially for doctors who want to take the step into outpatient work after the specialist title, the practice offers an environment with an existing patient base, professional exchange and modern infrastructure.

Their tasks

  • Comprehensive outpatient care for children and adolescents in acute and chronic diseases
  • Carrying out preventive, developmental and vaccination studies
  • Diagnostic examinations with, inter alia, ECG, lung function, auditory, visual and allergy tests
  • Care for patients with asthma, obesity or psychosomatic issues
  • Long-term support for children, adolescents and their families
  • Close cooperation with the experienced pediatrician and the interdisciplinary medical team

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in paediatric medicine or shortly before completion of further education
  • Interest in broad-based outpatient pediatrics
  • Joy of long-term care for children, adolescents and families
  • Self-employed and responsible work
  • Openness for professional exchange and collegial cooperation
  • Interested in developing further in an established practice environment

Their advantages

  • Direct entry with an already very good patient base
  • Collaboration and professional exchange with an experienced pediatrician
  • Flexible selectable workload – also 80% very well possible
  • Starting salary of CHF 170,000 gross with 100% plus revenue share
  • 5 weeks holidays, support for further education and attractive KTG and BVG services
  • For foreign specialists: Support for MEBEKO recognition and entry into the Swiss healthcare system
