Full description

Portrait We are a cantonal vocational training center in the Bernese Oberland with around 300 employees and 3,500 learners. At several locations, we offer training and further education in the fields of industry, service and fashion design (IDM).

Is your heart beating for the educational world? Then you are exactly right with us. We are looking for you as

Tasks

Are you ready?

  • Teaching in a class of pre-teaching with a focus on BTN (construction, engineering, nature) and AVL (administration, sales, logistics)
  • Teaching and working in the fields of: profession and society, language and communication, mathematics, sports, ICT, free subjects, SOL teaching, coaching
  • Working days: Mo and Di
  • Promotion of educational, personal and professional competences
  • Support for learners in preparation for a follow-up solution or in vocational orientation, support for learners for work in the pre-teaching company
  • Cooperation and networking with partner organisations, specialist bodies, parents/guardians and companies
  • Teaching in teamteaching incl. Coaching and mentoring of learners
  • Engagement within the framework of the vocational mission for a school that is developing

Requirements

You bring that to me.

  • Teaching diploma at secondary level I
  • Competences and experience in teaching profession and society, language and communication, mathematics, sport
  • Innovative and open attitude
  • Joy of working with young people and young adults
  • Enthusiasm and team skills
  • Active and binding cooperation with partner organisations etc.
  • Digital Affinity
  • An independent and responsible personality

We offer

This is what we offer you.

  • A meaningful working environment with the opportunity to accompany young people on their career path
  • A culture of trust and appreciation with a focus on development, innovation, sustainability, health protection and strong cooperation
  • Commitment and joy in working according to the guiding principle: “We do what we do because we like to do it.”
  • Internal courses and support for external education and training
  • A requirement-based, market- and performance-related salary policy in accordance with the law on the employment of teachers of the Canton of Bern
