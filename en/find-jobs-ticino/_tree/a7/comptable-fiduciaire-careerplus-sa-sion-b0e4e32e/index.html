Accountant

Do you like the trustee for its diversity, but especially look for an environment where you can really develop your autonomy and skills? This opportunity is for you!

On behalf of a Swiss trustee firmly established in French-speaking Switzerland, we are currently looking for a fiduciary accountant. Recognized for the diversity of its activities and the quality of its support to clients including SMEs and entrepreneurs, this structure offers comprehensive services in accounting, taxation, auditing and consulting.

Your duties with your new employer

  • You ensure complete accounting for a client portfolio
  • You establish periodic and annual closures
  • You prepare VAT accounts
  • You participate in the preparation of tax returns of natural and/or legal persons
  • You take on various tasks related to the administration of wages and social insurance
  • You are gradually accompanying your clients in their various accounting and administrative issues
  • Depending on your level of experience, you manage your mandates more and more independently

You offer to your new employer

  • You benefit from training in accounting, finance or fiduciary
  • You ideally have a first successful fiduciary experience in Switzerland
  • You know the principles of Swiss accounting and VAT
  • You are comfortable with computer tools and accounting software
  • You are a rigorous, organized and autonomous person
  • You appreciate contact with clients and work in a team
  • You have perfect command of French

You can also enjoy the following benefits

  • Beautiful premises ideally located
  • An environment conducive to continuing training
  • A parking space available
  • Flexible hours and telework opportunities

We have awakened your interest? So apply today!
