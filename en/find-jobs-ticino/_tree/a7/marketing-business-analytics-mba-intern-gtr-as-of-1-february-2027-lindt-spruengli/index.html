Full description

Lindt & Sprüngli Portrait

The Marketing Business Analytics Intern has a key role in the GTR Category & Revenue Growth Management (RGM) team, located at the headquarter in Kilchberg. In our global team structure, the MBA Intern is responsible for the efficient and effective execution of central, operational tasks covering several regions and is the owner of the global sell-out database and processes.

The MBA Intern is a hands-on person, who has a strong background in business & market analytics, brings good analytical thinking skills and is adapting quickly to changing environments.

In this internship, you are closely working with the CatMan Manager, RGM Manager and Head of Marketing in Kilchberg, and in addition will support the Regional Sales Managers around the globe in their responsibilities. You will gain insights into external performance analysis, reporting, data management and global pricing projects.

Key Duties and Responsibilities

Market & Business Analysis

  • Support in generating and analyzing the sell-out performance on a monthly basis on global, regional and brand level
  • P&M and Novelty tracker and scorecard development and monthly update
  • Update of distribution overviews (weighted & numerical)
  • Formulation of recommendations, insights generation
  • Support in the preparation and active participation in Quarterly Performance Calls with the different geographical regions
  • Ownership of Monthly Competitor Report – qualitative data collection from different sources & sharing with wider GTR community

Data Management

  • Own data collection process and harmonization on a global basis
  • Main Point of contact and coordinate with external data agency
  • Build databases and maintain them
  • Ensure high data quality throughout all processes

Pricing & Promotion

  • Monitor market pricing across airports, identify relevant price movements of own and competitor products, and translate findings into actionable internal updates. Assess promotional efficiency and effectiveness , identify optimization opportunities, and provide recommendations on promotional mechanics and POS execution.
  • Project support for improvement of reporting and data collection as well as ad-hoc analysis on different topics (own retail reporting, category developments, airport development, sell-out, pricing, promotion).

Minimum Experience Requirements: Strong interest in Analysis, Pricing, Business & Sales management

Preferred Experience Requirements
