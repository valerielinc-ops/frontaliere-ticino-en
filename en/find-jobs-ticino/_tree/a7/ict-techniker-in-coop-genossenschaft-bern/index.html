ICT technician:in

Coop Cooperative — Bern, BE, Svizzera

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Computer Science
  • Location: Coop, Riedbachstrasse 165, 3027 Bern
  • Pensum: 100%

Tasks

  • Planning, ordering, commissioning and maintenance of the entire IT infrastructure in the area of WAN, LAN, WLAN, VoIP, UKV, EDV rack, UPS, mobile, PC, printer as well as POS-HW (checkout, EFT, scales, etc.) in administration, logistics, production and in sales outlets
  • Responsibility, planning and realisation in the computer science subdivision for new and conversion projects
  • Analysis, prioritization and follow-up of the received IT malfunction reports as well as on-site support and repair of IT problems, as well as forwarding of non-solvable problems by means of tickets to the competent specialist offices
  • Complete collection and maintenance of the entire IT inventory, including management of mobile subscriptions
  • Cooperation in migration and rollout projects in administration, logistics, production and sales outlets

Requirements

  • Building IT specialist:in EFZ, telematician:in EFZ or electro-installer:in EFZ with a strong IT affinity
  • In-depth knowledge in current HW, Windows 11 and M365 as well as experience in the areas of LAN, WAN, UKV, VoIP, Mobile etc.
  • Stylistic German, as well as good oral and written knowledge in a second national language
  • Readiness for pikettdienst (7x24 hours - about 6 weeks per year) as well as work on marginal times
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Discount on the travel offer ITS Coop Travel, 5 times the number of super points in the supermarket, up to 20% discount on various insurance services, up to 20% discount in all formats of the Coop Group
  • Conditions of Employment: Pension Fund: 2/3 is paid by Coop, Attractive Conditions of Employment and Certified Equal Pay, Total Employment Contract (GAV)
  • Working time: 5 weeks holidays, flexible working time models
  • Training: Over 600 internal training courses, support for external education and training
  • Sustainability: Commitment to people and animals and nature with over 400 actions
  • Communication: internal app for employees
  • Health: Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription, free participation in various company runs
