Full description

You want to actively design our freshness range (fruit & vegetables, flowers & plants)? You prove negotiating strength and ensure the best price / performance ratio on the market? Welcome to shopping at Lidl Switzerland! In the dynamic environment of retail, you can expect a job where you can take responsibility and contribute directly to the company’s success.

Your tasks

  • Planning of demand / production quantities for the freshness range and action weeks to ensure the availability of goods in the stores
  • Preparation and conduct of purchasing negotiations
  • Co-design of the purchasing strategy in the Freshness range
  • Conduct of assortment analyses, product group reconciliation, tasting and decommissioning
  • Recognizing trends and changes in customer purchasing behavior
  • Further development of the quality of the freshness range
  • Ensuring optimal sales packaging to protect products

Your profile

  • Completed studies (uni/FH), preferably with a business focus or comparable further education
  • Professional experience in the Swiss retail trade or in the fresh sector (fruit & vegetables, flowers & plants)
  • Organizational and coordination skills as well as strong analytical and communication skills
  • Teamplayer mentality
  • Enforcement, independence and diligence
  • Willingness to work occasionally on Saturdays and public holidays
  • Possession of a valid category B driving licence
  • Knowledge of German min. Level C1, language skills in French and English are beneficial
