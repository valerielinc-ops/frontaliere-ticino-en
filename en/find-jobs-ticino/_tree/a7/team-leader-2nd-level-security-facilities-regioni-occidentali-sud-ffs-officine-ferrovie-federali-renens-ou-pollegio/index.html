Team Leader 2nd Level Security Facilities - West/South regions 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team Leader 2nd Level Security Facilities - West/South regions Renens or Pollegio, 01.02.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

You lead a team that provides second-level technical support for the internal and external installations of railway safety technology. You contribute directly to the safety and reliability of rail traffic. With specialists, you are involved in troubleshooting and maintenance, monitoring the condition of the facilities and strengthening technical skills through targeted training and knowledge sharing. You also support projects by providing your technical expertise and contribute actively to the evolution of the security facilities and associated diagnostic and operating systems. To find out about employee benefits, such as free GA and reduced-cost overseas travel (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits. What you can move.

  • As Team Leader, you ensure the technical leadership and management of the 2nd Level Team Security Facilities for the West and South Regions. You encourage your employees, develop their skills in a targeted way and create a motivating work environment.
  • On a daily basis, you are responsible for second-level operational support and ensure that you provide effective support to field teams in case of disruption or maintenance issues. You attach great importance to the effective treatment of disturbances, the systematic analysis of their causes and the implementation of sustainable improvements.
  • You plan, coordinate and define priorities for human resources and operational tasks. You keep in mind the availability of the facilities, the safety of the operation and the profitability.
  • You promote the development of knowledge and professional exchanges within the team, as well as between regions. You are actively contributing to the development of the 2nd Level and are committed to harmonized processes, clear responsibilities and effective working methods.
  • You work closely with the RMO team (Central/East Region), field teams and internal and external partners. Together with your RMO counterpart, you encourage collaboration between regions and help establish a nationally coordinated 2nd Level, based on uniform standards, clear responsibilities and a common understanding of your missions.

What you bring.

  • You are distinguished by strong relational skills, an excellent sense of communication and a real interest in the conduct and development of collaborators. You also know how to set benchmarks and build confidence in times of change.
  • Thanks to your experience, you have gained the driving experience or the potential and motivation to assume a leadership role. You work in a structured, autonomous and entrepreneurial manner.
  • You quickly understand complex technical and organizational issues, clearly define priorities and make thoughtful and solution-oriented decisions, including in demanding situations.
  • You have several years of professional experience and solid technical knowledge in the field of safety installations, including in the fields of operation, maintenance and troubleshooting.
  • In terms of training, you benefit from higher technical training (ES, HES, University or equivalent), preferably in electrotechnical or automation.
  • You master French and/or Italian, and have a good level in German. You appreciate the collaboration between regions and different linguistic communities, and are willing to work regularly on different sites in Switzerland.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
