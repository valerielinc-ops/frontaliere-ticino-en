Welcome to Medbase Zweisimmen

Our modern practice, which is over 400m2 and has its own pharmacy, is located in the village centre of Zweisimmen. The homely village in the Bernese Oberland with direct connection to the hiking and winter sports region Gstaad offers a high recreational and recreational value. Our interdisciplinary team takes care of the basic medical care of the local population as well as the first care of tourists. Our specialists from the fields of general internal medicine and paediatrics offer home-patient consultation hours and special medical examinations.

The MPA team ensures an excellent practice organization. Medbase Zweisimmen is actively involved in a new and future-oriented supply concept in the region in cooperation with Spital STS AG.
