Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. You will assume a leadership role with responsibilities that will have a direct impact on the transparency, quality and reliability of financial figures. With a committed team, you will develop modern accounting processes, advance the digitization of financial accounting and ensure the efficiency of operations, from day-to-day operations until the accounts are closed. You will have room for improvement, develop the professional and methodological skills of your team and collaborate with the control, tax, audit and IT services. To find out about the benefits, such as the free AG and foreign travel at reduced prices (for family as well), visit our website under "Social Benefits". What you can move.

  • You lead the Financial Accounting team in technical and hierarchical terms and develop the team according to clear objectives and skills.
  • You manage monthly and annual closures on time and with a high level of quality, and ensure consistency of processes and reliability of numbers.
  • As head of the Shared Services Internal Control System (IOS), you are advancing optimizations and ensuring compliance with applicable accounting standards, directives and requirements.
  • With internal and external audit, specialized units and service units, you coordinate collaboration and are the reference person for accounting matters.
  • Through indicators, analysis and continuous improvement (Operational Excellence), you ensure the stability of measurable processes and progress.
  • You develop digital processes from end to end in accounting and rely on standardization, automation and artificial intelligence to increase efficiency.

What you bring.

  • Strong analytical and conceptual capabilities distinguish you, and you effectively and proactively implement your decisions.
  • A clear and public-friendly communication allows you to convince various stakeholders.
  • You have first experience in staff management, focused on results and development, in the financial field and on accounting matters.
  • You control ERP systems and financial processes. You can ideally showcase an experience of SAP S/4HANA as well as ease with digital tools, including artificial intelligence tools.
  • An accountant's degree or equivalent qualification is acquired and you have several years of accounting experience, ideally in a digital environment or a service organization.
  • You master German, at least at level C1. Knowledge of other national languages is an asset.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
