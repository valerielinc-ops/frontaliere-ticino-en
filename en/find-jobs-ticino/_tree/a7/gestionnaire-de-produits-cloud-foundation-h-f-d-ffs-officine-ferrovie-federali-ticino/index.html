The solution "IT Base Infrastructure" provides cloud and platform services, data and messaging infrastructure, API management and developer experience for the entire SBB. Our Agile Release Train (ART) “Cloud Foundation” provides computing services for applications and technical services as well as the necessary network and application security and thus provides the basis infrastructure for SBB’s IT services and IT solutions. As a product manager for the ART "Cloud Foundation" you are technically well-founded on the road and take the wheel for the product portfolio in hand. You define the strategy, set clear priorities and orchestrate the implementation together with product owners, technical teams, system architect and release train engineer (RTE). As ART Trio, we are driving cross-ART initiatives, actively participating in the service lead and transforming platform services into true enablers for product teams. As ART, we design the basis on which the SBB applications run. If you’re motivated to defuse technical complexity, think big and build services that make an impact – then shape the future with us. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a product manager for the Cloud Foundation, you control the product portfolio, align the strategy and thus create the technological basis for stable and secure SBB applications.
  • Together with the management of the solution and the product owner, you develop the product vision and the roadmap and translate them into feasible projects.
  • With professional leadership and coaching, you strengthen the product owners in the Agile Release Train and prioritize and coordinate their workflows.
  • Along the entire product lifecycle, you identify the needs of customers and coordinate with relevant stakeholders so that the solutions create tangible benefits.
  • You ensure availability, quality and business relevance and are responsible for the effect based on clear key figures and target agreements.
  • You plan and coordinate releases with internal and external partners, ensure transparency in dialogue with stakeholders and work closely with law, compliance, operations, development, support and finance.

You bring that with you.

  • With clear communication, you reach different stakeholders and communicate complex issues in an understandable way.
  • In agile working methods, you have profound experience and bring several years of practice as a product manager or as an experienced product owner, ideally in scaled environments with an Agile Release Train.
  • Broad technical knowledge brings you to public cloud, private cloud, sovereign cloud, data center infrastructure, network and application security.
  • You set priorities and coordinate workflows across multiple teams while consistently developing your strategy and roadmap.
  • With your development-oriented and result-oriented leadership, you strengthen teams, promote trust and enable responsible cooperation.
  • You master German at least at level C1 and English at level B2.
