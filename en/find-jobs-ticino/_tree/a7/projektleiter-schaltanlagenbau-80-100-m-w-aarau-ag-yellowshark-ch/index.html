01 Future employer

For an established company in the field of electrical engineering and automation, we are looking for a committed professional who enjoys managing switchgear projects holistically and leading a team both technically and on a personnel level. You can expect a varied role with a high degree of autonomy, direct customer contact and exciting technical challenges.

02 Your responsibilities

  • You independently manage several switchgear projects from technical and commercial preparation through to successful handover.
  • In doing so, you coordinate material procurement, resources and staff deployment and ensure smooth project execution.
  • You lead the employees assigned to you and support your team with technical and organizational matters.
  • You keep costs, schedules and quality under control at all times and initiate appropriate measures at an early stage in the event of deviations.
  • You maintain professional contact with customers and actively develop existing business relationships.
  • Thanks to your technical understanding, you recognize additional customer needs and thereby contribute to winning follow-up and additional orders.
  • You work closely with internal departments and other project stakeholders and ensure efficient collaboration.

04 Your profile

  • A completed vocational qualification as an automation technician EFZ, switchgear fitter or in a comparable electrical engineering profession.
  • Further training in switchgear construction, project management or electrical engineering is an advantage.
  • Professional experience in the planning, design or implementation of switchgear and control systems.
  • Initial or extensive experience in managing employees, as well as enjoying taking responsibility for a team.
  • Good knowledge of working with CAD applications and a strong affinity for digital tools.
  • An independent, solution-oriented and structured way of working.
  • Strong organizational skills as well as a good technical and commercial understanding.
  • A communicative, proactive and team-oriented personality who enjoys driving things forward.
