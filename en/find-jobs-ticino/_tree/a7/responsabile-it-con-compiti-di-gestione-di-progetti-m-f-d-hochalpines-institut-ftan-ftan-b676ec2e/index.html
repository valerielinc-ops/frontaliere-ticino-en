Full description

Your responsibility

  • You ensure the reliable, secure and efficient operation of our IT infrastructure.
  • You will oversee and coordinate central IT systems, including network components, firewalls, printer and telephony systems, end devices and other location-relevant solutions.
  • You carry out regular maintenance and monitoring and develop the infrastructure forward-looking.
  • You handle IT concerns in 1st and 2nd level support as a competent contact person for our users.
  • You analyze and repair hardware, software and network disturbances sustainably.
  • You edit incidents and service requests structured via the ticket system.
  • You are responsible for user and authorization management.
  • You set up software and hardware user-friendly and document them in a comprehensible manner.
  • You coordinate cooperation with external service partners as well as with our headquarters in Singapore.
  • You clarify technical and organizational issues with the specialists at the headquarters and accompany technical adjustments.
  • You ensure compliance with applicable security policies and standards.
  • You recognize optimization potential, standardize processes and participate in the introduction and further development of digital solutions.
  • You plan, coordinate and realize smaller IT projects independently – in close coordination with internal and external stakeholders.

You bring that with you

  • Completed training in computer science, ICT or a comparable technical background.
  • In-depth knowledge of Windows Server and Windows as well as hardware, software and networks.
  • Ability to quickly understand complex technical relationships and implement them in a solution-oriented manner.
  • Careful, structured and independent working method – also for several parallel concerns.
  • Strong service orientation, professional appearance and clear communication.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English in word and writing
  • Secure communication in English is essential for regular cooperation with the headquarters in Singapore.
  • Enjoy taking on responsibility and advising users in an understandable and addressable manner.
  • Ability to work constructively and firmly with different stakeholders.
  • Experience in coordinating or managing smaller IT\ projects is an advantage.

This awaits you with us

  • A varied task with a great deal of creative scope.
  • An international, innovative educational environment with flat hierarchies and short decision-making paths.
  • The opportunity to contribute your own ideas and actively shape digital solutions.
  • Professional development in a lively educational and living community.
  • Collaboration with a sympathetic student body and a motivated, future-oriented team.
  • A unique place of work in the mountain backdrop of the Lower Engadin.
