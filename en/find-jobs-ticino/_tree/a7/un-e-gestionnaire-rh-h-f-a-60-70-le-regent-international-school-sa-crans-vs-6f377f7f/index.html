Full description

The Regent International School, located in Crans-Montana, is looking for a HR Manager (M/F) at 60–70% to strengthen its administrative team as soon as possible.

Located in the heart of the Swiss Alps, Le Régent offers an international school environment where every student is encouraged to flourish, develop his talents and become a responsible and open citizen of the world. Our school is distinguished by its educational approach combining academic excellence and personal development in an exceptional natural setting. In full development, Le Régent is constantly evolving to meet the challenges of an international school environment and to support its growth.

RECOGNIZED PROFILE

  • Under the responsibility of the Head of HR, you ensure the administrative management of human resources and accompany employees throughout their journey within the institution.
  • Rigoury, autonomous and endowed with excellent relational qualities, you listen to and appreciate teamwork in an international environment.

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Ensure administrative management of staff, from start-up to departure of employees.
  • Participate in the recruitment process: publication of announcements, follow-up of applications and organization of interviews.
  • Prepare, draft and monitor employment contracts, amendments, certificates and other HR documents.
  • Ensure the preparation and follow-up of applications for residence and work permits for international collaborators, in conjunction with the competent authorities.
  • Maintain staff records and ensure the reliability of HR data.
  • Monitor and transmit variable salary items to the payer.
  • Follow-up of absences, leave, accidents and medical certificates.
  • To be a local HR contact for employees and managers, providing answers and support in current HR issues.
  • Participate in the implementation of the new HRIS and contribute to the optimization and digitization of HR processes.
  • Ensure compliance with Swiss labour legislation and internal procedures.

EXPERIENCE AND PERSONAL QUALITIES

Diplomas / Certificates

  • SWC business employee or training deemed equivalent.
  • HR HR HRSE Certificate or title deemed equivalent required.

Experience

  • Prior experience in an HR administrative function or in personnel management.
  • Good knowledge of HR practices and Swiss labour legislation.
  • Knowledge of Swiss procedures for residence and work permits is an asset.

Technical expertise

  • Good computer skills and ease in the use of HR management systems.
  • Very good command of French and English, both oral and written.

Personal suitability

  • Rigor, autonomy and organizational sense.
  • Discretion, confidentiality and sense of responsibility.
  • Team spirit, excellent relational qualities and adaptability.

CONDITIONS AND BENEFITS

  • The Regent offers you:
  • A stimulating and constantly evolving working environment.
  • An international environment, friendly and dynamic, human-sized and at the heart of school life.
  • A part-time job, with an activity rate of 60% to 70%

Applications will be reviewed as they are received. The Regent reserves the right to close recruitment once a profile corresponding to the requirements of the position has been identified.
