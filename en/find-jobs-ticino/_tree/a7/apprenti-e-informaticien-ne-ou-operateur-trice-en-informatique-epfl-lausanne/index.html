Full description

Mission

With over 100 apprentices in training in a dozen professions, EPFL is a fully-fledged training company. Regardless of the apprenticeship chosen at EPFL, apprentices carry out their vocational training in an extraordinary environment composed of research laboratories in fields as varied as engineering, exact science and architecture. They benefit from all the dynamism and diversity of a university campus that offers them many opportunities, advantages and opportunities for personal development.

At the École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), you have the opportunity to embark on an exciting apprenticeship in the field of computer science, whether as computer science or computer science, operating and infrastructure orientation or in the direction of developing applications or as an operator or operator in computer science. Computer science plays a central role in apprenticeship at EPFL, with 15 to 20 apprentices spread over 3 or 4 years.

Whatever the orientation chosen, you will spend all or part of your first year of training at EPFL's Computer School Laboratory dedicated to computer apprentices, where you will have the opportunity to learn together, to acquire practical knowledge with the apprentices on the same flight, to work on time on joint projects with other flights, other professions, other units.

Main tasks and responsibilities

As an IT apprentice, you will be involved in a variety of activities:

  • design and maintenance of computer systems and applications,
  • support for users
  • configuration of hardware, devices.

You will have to work with

  • various components of EPFL's information system (network, storage, LDAP directory, AD,...)
  • databases
  • Web services.

You can also connect remotely on different machines (customers or servers).

As you train, depending on your skills and aspirations, you can contribute to more ambitious projects, such as network systems or software, or participate in application developments.

The four-year SWC computer training provides two guidelines: application development and operation and infrastructure.

Application Development Activities

  • needs analysis and application design (web, mobile or desktop),
  • code programming, testing and documentation,
  • database design and management,
  • production and maintenance of applications.

Operational activities and infrastructure

  • installation, configuration and maintenance of workstations, servers and peripherals,
  • setting up and monitoring networks and services (stocking, directory, courier),
  • IT security and backups,
  • support for users.

As an IT operator you will be a versatile professional who will be responsible for the proper operation of computer hardware and software in an organization. Every year, you will change positions within EPFL, which will allow you to gain experience and work on different aspects of computer technology, from installation to troubleshooting and support to users. It is a dynamic and essential profession for the proper functioning of any modern enterprise.

Learning places are offered in various services, notably within the support teams of the faculties or units of the central services of EPFL. You will participate in training courses in specialized services, for example at the help desk or in the field of the network.

In any case, you will benefit from coaching experienced trainers.
