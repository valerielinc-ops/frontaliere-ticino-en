Full description

Attached to the Assistant Director Maintenance, you play a key role in the deployment, administration and continuous improvement of our maintenance management tools. A true interface between operational teams, users and suppliers, you help optimize our processes and develop our business tools.

At home, you can move things.

  • Participate in the management of new contracts and support their integration into our systems.
  • Deploy the contracts sold by implementing the piloting, tracking and reporting tools within our GMAO solution.
  • Train and support operational teams in the use of tools.
  • Provide technical and functional support to users.
  • Install and track fixes and developments of the GMAO tool in collaboration with the publisher.
  • Ensure operational reporting and compliance with internal procedures.
  • Participate in the modernization and continuous improvement of management and piloting tools.
  • Develop and optimize dashboards and reporting tools, especially under Power BI.
  • Contribute to the improvement of business tools such as encryption, activity tracking and sales tools.

What characterises you.

  • Advanced training of the type Engineer, Bachelor or equivalent in a technical or computer field.
  • Significant experience in setting up, deploying or administering a GMAO solution.
  • Good database mastery and excellent knowledge of Excel.
  • Experience in creating and improving Power BI dashboards.
  • Experience with IT tools and information systems.
