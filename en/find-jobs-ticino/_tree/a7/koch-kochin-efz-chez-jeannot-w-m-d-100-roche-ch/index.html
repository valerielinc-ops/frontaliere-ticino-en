The Position Foodlicious is our daily business

Taverne Roche has grown into a successful catering company in the field of community gastronomy over 30 years. Tavero AG is a 100% subsidiary of F. Hoffmann-La Roche. At our locations in Basel and Kaiseraugst, we employ around 350 people from over 37 nations. Our offer includes both staff restaurants that live diversity and fine dining restaurants with upscale cuisine and excellent service. In addition, we offer restaurants with delicacies from all over the world, coffee in all variants, charmed by our baristi, as well as the highest lounges in Switzerland – fantastic afterwork spots above the rooftops of the city. Grab'n'Go offerings from sushi to burgers and the first and only club in Roche's history complement our offering. In addition, we offer our guests meeting room and event catering for up to 1,500 people. The public restaurants, the Schöpfli in the park and the Chez Jeannot in the Museum Tinguely, round off our offer.

We are looking for a chef for the Museums Restaurant, chez Jeannot as of January 2027.

Your tasks

  • Production & Mise en place: professional and timely preparation and preparation of all food components for the daily offer and the ongoing service.
  • Frontcooking & Guest Interaction: Attractive presentation and fresh preparation of dishes in the frontcooking area with noticeable hosting pleasure.
  • Quality & product philosophy: High appreciation of fresh ingredients, precise cooking according to recipe including regular tasting and active introduction into the continuous improvement process (CIP).
  • Goods management & warehousing: Professional and proper storage of all food products in the dry, refrigerated and frozen area including daily inventory control and triggering of internal and external orders.
  • Inventory & cost awareness: Daily maintenance of inventories, active participation in the monthly inventory as well as a resource-saving, economical handling of food and materials.
  • Hygiene & Standards: Consistent compliance and implementation of the applicable HACCP and Quality Guidelines (TQM) for maximum cleanliness and food safety in the workplace.
  • Working hours: Attractive daily working hours, usually between 09:00 and 17:30 (Tuesday to Sunday, assignments by arrangement and roster)

Your profile

  • Professional qualification: Completed vocational apprenticeship as a chef (EFZ) or equivalent, recognised training.
  • Professional experience: At least 5 years of solid practical experience after successful teaching, ideally supplemented by experience in frontcooking.
  • Working methods & leadership: High organisational talent, a strong sense of responsibility and a structured, independent and assertive working method.
  • Personality: Team-oriented, reliable personality with real pleasure in hosting and high commitment.
  • Professional & legal knowledge: Sound and proven knowledge of Swiss food legislation as well as the current HACCP guidelines.
  • Language competence: Very good German and good English skills in word and writing for courteous, competent care of our guests.
  • Digital Affinity: Secure and routine handling of common MS Office applications and openness to digital work processes.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed.
