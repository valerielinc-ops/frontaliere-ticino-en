Job Description

As DBA Team Leader, you will lead EFG’s database administration function and be accountable for the availability, security, performance, resilience, and lifecycle management of the bank’s database platforms. This role combines hands-on technical leadership with people management, service ownership, operational governance, capacity planning, automation, and continuous improvement.

You will oversee a heterogeneous database landscape (including Oracle, SQL Server, MySQL, PostgreSQL and Azure database services), ensuring robust support for business‑critical banking applications, regulatory requirements, and strategic transformation projects. You will also drive database optimisation and innovation, contributing to the evolution of EFG’s database strategy across on‑premises, hybrid, and cloud environments.

The position is based in Geneva and may require participation in an on‑call rotation and occasional work during weekends to support planned activities and critical incidents.

Key

  • Responsibilities

· Lead, coach, and develop the DBA team, setting priorities, allocating work, and ensuring appropriate skills coverage, mentoring, and knowledge transfer. · Own the operational delivery and lifecycle management of the bank’s Oracle, SQL Server, MySQL, PostgreSQL and Azure database services, ensuring stable and efficient platforms for business‑critical systems. · Define, implement, and enforce standards for database architecture, engineering, security, backup and recovery, monitoring, patching, and performance, in line with regulatory, risk, and audit expectations. · Ensure database availability, resilience, capacity, and recoverability in accordance with agreed service levels and business continuity requirements, including high‑availability and disaster‑recovery architectures (e.g. Oracle Exadata, Oracle Data Guard). · Lead incident, problem, and change management activities for database services, including root‑cause analysis, preventive actions, and clear communication data strategy. · Maintain effective controls for privileged access, vulnerability remediation, data protection, audit evidence, and compliance, in close collaboration with Information Security, Risk, and Compliance functions. · Partner with Application Development, Infrastructure, Cloud, Architecture, Security, and Project Management teams to design and deliver secure, scalable, and supportable database solutions for new and existing applications. · Manage relationships with external service providers and vendors, including licences, maintenance contracts, and database‑related budgets, within the agreed governance framework. · Organise and participate in the on‑call rotation, ensuring appropriate operational coverage and readiness, and provide senior escalation support for complex database issues and business‑critical projects.
