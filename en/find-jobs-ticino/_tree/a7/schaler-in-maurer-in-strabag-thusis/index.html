Schaler:in / Maurer:in Your contribution with us

  • You perform formwork, concrete and masonry work professionally
  • You create and assemble formwork for different components
  • You concrete walls, ceilings and other components
  • You perform general masonry work as well as minor repair work
  • You implement plans and specifications precisely on the construction site
  • You work with modern machines, equipment and formwork systems
  • You work closely with the Polisher and your construction team
  • You pay attention to a safe, clean and high-quality execution

What matters to us

  • Completed training as a mason: in EFZ or equivalent professional experience
  • Experience in formwork, concrete and masonry construction
  • Safe handling of various formwork systems
  • Craftsmanship and good technical understanding
  • Independent, reliable and careful working methods
  • Physical Resilience and Joy at Working Outdoors
  • Teamwork and willingness to take part in the construction site
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Category B driving licence is advantageous

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Graubünden's migration office after the contract is signed.
