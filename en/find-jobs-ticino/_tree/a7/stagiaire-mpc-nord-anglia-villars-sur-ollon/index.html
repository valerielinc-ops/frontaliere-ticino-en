Full description

From 1 August 2027

Founded in 1910, the Collège Beau Soleil is one of Switzerland's most renowned private schools. As a prestigious international institution, we welcome students aged 11-18 from over 60 different nationalities and offer them holistic boarding education. Our College joined the Nord Anglia Education group in 2011, the world leader in high-end schools, bringing together 90 international schools around the globe.

Our employees benefit from a dynamic, family-friendly work environment on a modern campus. They also take advantage of the opportunities arising from our membership in a global organization dedicated to education and professional development.

YOUR TIME

  • Attend receptionist and ensure replacement as required
  • Answering telephones and welcoming visitors
  • Process arrival of mail and parcels

General administration

  • Assist in organizing events
  • Participate in sending mails and other mails
  • Manage various files for data administration
  • Maintain the Intern's guide

Transport

  • Assist the person responsible in planning and organizing student travel

School shop

  • Attend the manager when ordering uniforms, school supplies, etc...
  • Assistance for fitting days

Facilities

  • Help the manager organize events (room layout, catering management)
  • Assist in monitoring maintenance tasks

Marketing

  • Assist the marketing department with administrative tasks

Admissions, HR and Finance internship opportunities based on interest.

YOUR KNOWLEDGE

  • Good knowledge of English
  • Mastery of the Microsoft universe (Word, Excel,...)
  • Very good oral and written communication capacity
  • Very good presentation, smiling, punctual, flexible, rigorous, motivated and enthusiastic
