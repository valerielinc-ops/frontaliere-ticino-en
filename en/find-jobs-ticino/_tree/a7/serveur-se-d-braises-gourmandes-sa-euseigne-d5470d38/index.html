As part of the opening of La Pinta, a village café-bar in Euseigne (Val d

OUR ESTABLISHMENT La Pinta will be a warm and friendly meeting place, open to a varied clientele – inhabitants, families and tourists – throughout the day. From morning coffee to an aperitif, to some darts with friends, our café bar will live at the pace of the village.

YOUR MAIN MISSIONS

  • Welcome customers with professionalism and friendliness.
  • Provide service at the bar and in the room, as well as cash.
  • Prepare and serve drinks and small food
  • Participate in the setting up, storage and maintenance of work spaces.
  • Ensure hygiene, safety and quality standards (HACCP) are met.

YOUR PROFILE

  • You have experience in catering service; A coffee bar experience is an asset.
  • You know good hygiene practices (HACCP) and apply them rigorously.
  • You are punctual, organized, smiling and professional in all circumstances.
  • You know how to work independently as a team.
  • You are available on an irregular schedule, including weekends, evenings and public holidays.
  • You are being transported with limited public transport schedules.
  • You have mastered French
  • Any other language is an asset.
  • You are a Swiss national or eligible for a work permit

WE OFFER

  • An indeterminate position, with an activity rate of 30-80% to be defined together.
  • Working conditions and wages in accordance with the hotel and restaurant CCTT.
