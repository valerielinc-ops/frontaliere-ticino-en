Supply Chain Manager in operational purchasing 80 \- 100% (m/f/d)

For more than 40 years, we have been shaping the future of mechanical ventilation. Medical develops intelligent ventilation solutions that support medical professionals at crucial moments.

Our ventilators are used in intensive care units, in emergency transport and in the MRI environment. They help medical teams worldwide to care for adult, pediatric and neonatal patients.

Your work has a real impact here. Every improvement you make will help to further improve the respiratory care of patients around the world.

You can do that *

  • Responsibility for operational procurement for one’s own purchasing area
  • Ensuring material availability
  • Maintaining existing supplier relationships
  • Implementation of supply chain optimizations
  • Interface between production, quality department and suppliers
  • Negotiation of framework contracts
  • Improvement of the procurement process and the ERP system (Microsoft Dynamics, in future SAP)

You bring that with you

  • Technical knowledge of electronics, electromechanics, pneumatics or plastics/metal processing
  • Several years of experience in procurement in the industrial environment
  • Independent working methods, sense of duty and team orientation
  • Solution-oriented personality
  • Negotiation strength and enforcement
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English (word and writing)
  • Experience with ERP systems
