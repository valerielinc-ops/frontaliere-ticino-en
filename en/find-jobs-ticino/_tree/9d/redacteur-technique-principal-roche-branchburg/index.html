The Position Join our dynamic cross-functional team as a Senior Technical Writer, where you will drive product launch success and operational excellence across complex biochemical, engineering, and manufacturing processes. In this role, you will author high-quality technical documentation and ensure the integrity of global labeling from product design through distribution. We offer a highly collaborative environment where your expertise directly shapes compliance, systems integration, and product delivery.

The Opportunity

  • Lead the creation, review, and release of customer facing labeling, SOPs, work instructions, and supporting documentation for complex manufacturing and engineering products and processes.
  • Utilize desktop publishing tools and enterprise content management systems efficiently to manage labeling content, graphics, translation workflows, and version control.
  • Coordinate labeling activities for product launches and claim extensions across all product lines, partnering with Project Managers, Product Managers, Design & Development, Quality teams, and more.
  • Maintain full compliance with Quality System Regulations during internal and external audits, establishing performance metrics to continuously improve practices.
  • Evaluate change impacts across business areas, lead labeling obsolescence initiatives, and safeguard data accuracy according to corporate standards.
  • Serve as an extended core team member for product launches, providing guidance to technical teams and acting as deputy site project manager as needed.

Who You Are

  • You hold a Bachelor’s degree in Technical Writing, Medical Technology, Communications, Health Science, or an equivalent discipline.
  • You bring 5+ years of prior technical writing, product labeling, and technical publication experience.

Preferred

  • You possess working knowledge of FDA regulations, quality system guidelines, ISO standards, and operating in a highly regulated, matrixed environment.
  • You demonstrate excellent writing, editing, organizational, and project management skills, with proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
  • You have the ability to work independently and creatively, as well as learning technical details of the Roche product lines and document structure.
  • You have prior experience with content management systems.
