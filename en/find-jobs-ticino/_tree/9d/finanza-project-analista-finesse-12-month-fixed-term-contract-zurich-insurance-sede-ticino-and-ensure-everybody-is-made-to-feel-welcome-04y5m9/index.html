Location: Douglas, Isle of Man

Closing date for applications: 30th July 2026

The opportunity

We have an exciting opportunity for a Finance Project Analyst to join our Finance Improvement Project Team on a 12-month fixed term contract.

In this role, you’ll work closely with colleagues across ZIL and build strong relationships with stakeholders across the wider Zurich Group. You’ll play a key part in improving finance processes, helping to simplify, automate, and strengthen the way we work.

If you enjoy using analysis to shape decisions and communicating insights clearly and confidently, this could be a great next step in your career.

This is an excellent opportunity to develop your skills, gain exposure to senior leaders, and contribute to meaningful business change.

What you’ll do

  • Review existing manual finance processes to understand current ways of working and identify opportunities to simplify, automate, and improve efficiency.
  • Contribute to the review and assessment of reconciliation remediation actions.
  • Work collaboratively with finance and non-finance teams to deliver process improvements.
  • Assist Workstream Leads and Project Managers with testing, implementation, and training activities.
  • Build strong relationships and communicate effectively with stakeholders at all levels.

What we’re looking for

  • Previous experience working in a finance team, in roles such as business analyst or accountant.
  • A proactive, self-starting mindset with the confidence to challenge the status quo.
  • Ability to work both collaboratively and independently.
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders at all levels.
  • Experience in process improvement and control design within finance (e.g. strengthening controls, standardising processes, supporting automation).
  • Strong analytical skills and a good understanding of finance processes and systems.
  • Proven experience completing financial reconciliations.
  • Experience in a change-focused environment would be beneficial.
  • Insurance industry knowledge would be an advantage.
