The Opportunity

As an Intern in our team your main tasks will include

Option 1 - Oligo synthesis

  • Discovery of new techniques for the synthesis of complex oligonucleotides
  • Purification and analysis of reactions involving oligonucleotides with state of the art chromatographic techniques.
  • Scale up your findings into processes for the preparation of multi-gram quantities of desired target materials.

Option 2 - Oligo Analytical Chemistry

  • State-of-the-art analysis of complex synthetic oligonucleotides by LC-MS.
  • Development and implementation of innovative analytical methods to support synthesis, development and production.
  • Conducting critical in-solution stability and hold-time studies.

Who you are

  • An enthusiastic, motivated and scientifically interested person who enjoys teamwork and has a strong willingness to learn new techniques.
  • A BSc or MSc student, or a recent graduate (within the last 12 months prior to the start date), in Chemistry or Biochemistry . Prior hands-on laboratory experience is required .
  • Prior experience with oligonucleotide chemistry (solid phase synthesis, conjugation, purification and analysis
  • Option 1) or LC-MS analytics (Option 2) is desirable.
  • Excellent organizational skills and proactive knowledge sharing.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
