Full description

As part of a mission, the Digital and Information Systems Branch of Vaud State is looking for an Application Manager for an 18-month term. At the end of this period, the post will be open on CDI. Do you want to meet the challenges of managing and evolving applications in a constantly changing technological environment? Do you like to coordinate technical and functional activities, support users and guarantee the quality, safety and performance of information systems? Join the Department of Digital and Information Systems (DGNSI) of Vaud State and contribute to the evolution and smooth functioning of HR, Health and Social applications.

  • Manage the life cycle of products and applications under your responsibility to ensure their quality, safety, performance and efficiency, in response to the needs of recipient services and the objectives of the NSB.
  • Assess, prioritize and manage maintenance and safety needs, impacts of technological developments, and business demands.
  • Plan and coordinate corrective and scalable maintenance activities and application technical roadmaps.
  • Participate in projects of implementation and evolution of products and applications, ensuring compliance with safety and architecture standards.
  • Contribute to the design, implementation and deployment of new versions of applications, including participation in impact assessments, testing and operational revenue.
  • Supervise the operation of applications, provide support to users and coordinate incident resolution to ensure continuity and quality of services.
  • Ensure the documentation of applications, the monitoring of service objectives and the implementation of monitoring and monitoring systems.
  • Work closely with technical teams, information systems managers and business representatives to ensure effective application management and continuous improvement. CV in French and letter of application in PDF format. Please also attach the work certificates and diplomas. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact the Head of the HR, Health and Social Unit, Delphine Jet François (delphine.jet-francois@vd.ch ) who is at your disposal.

Profile sought

  • Bachelor or Master's degree in computer science, information systems, IT management or equivalent.
  • Experience confirmed in conducting work on information systems: management of applications in production, management of release applications, projects and support.
  • Certification in project management, such as IPMA or PMI, is an asset.
  • Knowledge of relational database management systems, including Oracle, and SQL language.
  • Knowledge of ETL software and Datawarehouses.
  • Knowledge of application servers
  • Knowledge of project management methodologies, such as HERMES and Agile, as well as test management methodologies and tools.
  • Ability to drive and coordinate application releases, analyze the impacts of functional and technical developments, and manage incidents and problems.
  • Ability to communicate, negotiate and build trust with technical teams, business partners and users.
  • Rigor, autonomy, sense of responsibility, analytical spirit and ability to make decisions.
  • Good knowledge of English, both oral and written. Special constraints:
  • You may have to intervene outside the usual working hours, especially in case of incident, night, Saturday, Sunday or public holidays, and participate in a picket service.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
