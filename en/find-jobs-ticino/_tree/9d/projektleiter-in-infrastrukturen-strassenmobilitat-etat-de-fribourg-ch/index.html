Full description

Appointment authority / department / institution

The Civil Engineering Office (TBA) ensures the sustainable development of roads by providing transport participants with a safe and needs-based road infrastructure, managing and managing the public space of roads and performing the administrative tasks to achieve these goals.

Become part of our team!

We focus on a wide range of skills and perspectives because we know that this is one of our greatest strengths and that different careers and profiles enrich our team. We also promote work-life balance and offer flexible working conditions. You can find out more about this on the following page.

Their tasks

Its tasks include studies on road transport infrastructures and the realisation of these infrastructures. As a project manager, you are the client or project manager. The client is responsible for the entire process according to SIA 103 and 112 of the mobility infrastructure projects, as well as for ensuring that the results meet the objectives set by the Office and that the requirements regarding quality, deadlines and costs are met. They also supervise the cantonal mobility infrastructures for the state.

Desired profile

  • Master (Uni, ETH or FH) in Civil Engineering or equivalent education and experience
  • Training and professional experience in the management and planning of mobility infrastructure projects
  • Experience with studies and in monitoring the implementation of mobility infrastructures
  • Very good analytical skills and networked thinking
  • Sense of organization and planning
  • Creativity for solving technical problems related to civil engineering
  • Knowledge of public procurement
  • Experience in Quality Management
  • Ability to write texts
  • Mastery of the German or French language with good knowledge of the partner language
