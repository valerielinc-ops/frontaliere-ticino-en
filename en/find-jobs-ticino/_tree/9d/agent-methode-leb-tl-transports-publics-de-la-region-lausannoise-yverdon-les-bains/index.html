Full description

Within movodis, the LEB links Lausanne to Gros-de-Vaud and supports the development of a growing region. By 2035, he plans to introduce a 10-minute pace between Lausanne and Echallens in order to offer ever more attractive and sustainable mobility.

This evolution is accompanied by many infrastructure projects and requires clear, reliable and tailored working methods to the needs of the network. To support movodis and LEB teams and to contribute to the safety, reliability and sustainability of the installations, movodis is looking for a motivated method agent to structure and improve maintenance processes and working tools.

LEB method agent

Your Mission

As part of the LEB's public transport service mission

  • Planning work for maintenance activities
  • Participate in continuous improvement of maintenance processes
  • Support and support entities in the development and use of professional maintenance tools and procedures
  • Assist the maintenance areas in their missions by organizing and managing the monitoring of vehicles and infrastructure in the dedicated information system

Your activities

Preparation of work

Ensures the preparation of work, material and documentation for its entity:

  • By creating and following the technical posts and equipment in the ERP (following versioning, location, etc.)
  • By creating and following periodic maintenance plans
  • By providing the entity with supplier maintenance documentation (manuals, parts catalogues, schematics, software, prospectus)
  • By creating the various maintenance documents (procedures, operating ranges) required for maintenance activities
  • By participating in the validation of maintenance documentation and structuring it in the dedicated system
  • Following the standards of spare parts (in relation to manufacturer catalogues)
  • Ensuring data quality (indicators, budgets, analyses, planning, advice, OT)
  • By participating in the creation of operational ad hoc maintenance indicators
  • By participating in investment projects

Management, support and monitoring

  • By participating in the management, support and monitoring of maintenance activities
  • By updating in the dedicated system the maintenance documentation (operational schedules, management, work and safety procedures)
  • By integrating new equipment, production tools, vehicles and railway infrastructure and updating data in SAP
  • By planning periodic activities and revisions with strict adherence to the maintenance intervals specified by suppliers
  • By ensuring traceability of maintenance step changes by drawing up the necessary change sheets with the internal engineering office.
  • Following the configuration of installed equipment
  • Monitoring the costs of revisions
  • Invoicing third-party benefits
  • Being the interface with subcontractors on the operational management of maintenance contracts
  • By ensuring the maintenance and conformity of common tools (mobile equipment, safety equipment, etc.), as well as service vehicles
  • By administratively supporting management and technicians
  • By participating in transversal CAP approaches and using standards deployed in maintenance
  • By monitoring, developing, monitoring and supporting the first level of IT tools used in maintenance

Your profile

Training

  • Technical CFCs or equivalent
  • Higher vocational training (ES, EPD, Patent, CAS or equivalent) in the technical field, ideally a federal process agent patent.

Professional experience

  • The occupation requires between 3 and 5 years of professional experience, in a similar field related to maintenance processes and/or technical maintenance

Authorizations, legal requirements and security

  • Compliance with company IT rules and data confidentiality.
  • Knowledge of safe maintenance standards
  • Category B permit (light vehicles)

Practical information

  • Quality working conditions and social benefits
  • Place of work : Echallens
  • Contract: 100% indefinite
  • Selection process: 1st interview with manager and recruitment specialist to get to know each other
