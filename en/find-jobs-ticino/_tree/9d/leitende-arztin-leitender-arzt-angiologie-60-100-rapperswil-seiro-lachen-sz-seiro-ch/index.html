Full description

For a modern practice for vascular medicine in the Rapperswil area, we are looking for a committed senior physician with high professional competence, pleasure in interventional vascular medicine and interest in interprofessional cooperation. You can look forward to a versatile outpatient field of activity, modern infrastructure and attractive development opportunities within a renowned medical competence center. The job is available immediately or by agreement in a charge of 60 to 100%. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • Care of outpatients with diseases of arterial, venous and lymphatic vessels
  • Conducting a broad range of invasive and non-invasive angiological examinations and therapies
  • Independent consultation hours within the practice of vascular medicine
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with angiology, vascular surgery, interventional radiology and wound experts as well as wound experts
  • Cooperation in the further development of modern vascular treatment concepts
  • Participation in the professional support and further training of assistant doctors
  • Working within an established vascular center with high medical quality

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed specialist training in angiology and or specialist training in internal medicine with further training in angiology
  • Broad general-internal expertise
  • Ideally experience in interventional therapy
  • Self-employed, responsible and quality-oriented working methods
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation and outpatient vascular medicine
  • High communication skills as well as commitment and initiative

That sets them apart.

  • Professionally accomplished and humanly convincing personality
  • High social competence and professional treatment of patients
  • Interest in the further development of modern vascular structures
  • Teamwork as well as interprofessional thinking and acting
  • Organizational skills and forward-looking working methods

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Versatile activity within a modern interdisciplinary vascular center
  • Close cooperation with specialist areas at a high medical level
  • Attractive internal and external training opportunities
  • Support for education and training through working time and cost sharing
  • At least 25 days of holiday with additional holiday entitlement from 50. Age year
  • Gifts of seniority in the form of leave or wages
  • Attractive employee benefits at well-known providers
  • Discounted food in the staff restaurant
  • Offers such as fitness classes and massages within physical therapy
  • Worldwide private accident insurance without additional costs
  • Fair parking facilities directly at the workplace
  • Working in an established medical competence center with regional and supraregional broadcast

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition
  • Assistance in organizing travel to Switzerland
  • Support in the search for housing by the internal rental department
