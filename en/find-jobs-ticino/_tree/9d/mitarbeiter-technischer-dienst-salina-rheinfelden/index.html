Reha Rheinfelden

Technical service staff 100% per 1 December 2026 or by agreement

  • Permanent employment
  • Rheinfelden

Reha Rheinfelden, Salina Rehaklinik and Park-Hotel am Rhein AG employ around 1,000 people. To achieve the best possible rehabilitation, interdisciplinary teams work together, with each individual’s expertise and experience contributing to success. Your team The technical service supervises buildings and state-of-the-art facilities in medical and building technology. Professionals from different professions work closely together at our two locations and ensure the implementation of maintenance work on technical equipment, furniture, facilities, equipment, aids as well as in public rooms and patient rooms. The procurement and management of the required materials are also part of the task area. As a support for maintenance, our CAFM system serves us. The readiness for regular pikettdienst in the evening and on weekends (within 45 minutes on site) is also required. Piketteineinsatz requires independent action, flexibility and technical understanding, even across disciplines.
