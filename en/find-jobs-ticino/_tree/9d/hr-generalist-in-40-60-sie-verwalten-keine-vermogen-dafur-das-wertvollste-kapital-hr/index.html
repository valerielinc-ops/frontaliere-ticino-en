ID: Category: HRM Region: Basel Reference: HR generalist 40 – 60% in the banking industry Our client, an established family-owned private bank based in Basel, specializes in asset management as well as related finance and consulting services. The focus of their actions is personal, sustainable and comprehensive support as well as long-term customer relationships based on trust and discretion. In the course of a realignment of the HR department, this exciting position was re-created. For this versatile function, we are looking for a professionally experienced, team-capable and integrity personality as HR generalist 40 – 60% – You do not manage assets. The most valuable capital for this.

Their tasks

  • Responsibility for the entire staff administration from entry to exit
  • Preparation of employment contracts, employment certificates and other HR documents
  • Processing of all registrations and deregistrations with social security funds and public authorities
  • Support and advice for employees and managers in all personnel-relevant issues
  • Recruitment of qualified specialists in close cooperation with line managers and management
  • Support and participation in the further development of HR processes as well as participation in HR projects
  • Deputy Payroll Manager including support in the processing of wages and the associated administrative tasks

Your profile

  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable, generalist HR function
  • Further training as an HR specialist or a comparable qualification
  • Good knowledge of wage processing and associated administrative processes
  • German as a mother tongue
  • Strong service and customer orientation
  • Very good communication skills as well as a safe and winning appearance
  • Independent, careful and structured working method
  • High initiative, organizational talent and hands on mentality
  • Unconditional discretion and a perfect reputation

This is what awaits you

You can expect a varied and responsible job in a traditional private bank with a family culture and short decision-making paths. They take on a wide range of tasks with a lot of personal responsibility and actively shape the further development of the newly created function. The job can be started immediately or by agreement, at the latest in February or March 2027.
