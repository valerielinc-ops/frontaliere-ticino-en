Welcome to Medbase Aigle Chablais

Our future multi-professional medical centre will occupy a central location in Aigle, two minutes walk from the train station, in the heart of Chablais. This modern, bright and well equipped site of 515 m2 will bring together family medicine, paediatrics, sports medicine and neurosurgery. It will include ten consultation rooms, four treatment rooms and a children's area.

The centre will benefit from direct synergies with radiology, physiotherapy and pharmacy located in the same building, as well as with the Medbase Vevey centre. This collaboration will promote interprofessional and coordinated care. Driven by strong regional demand and a project well integrated with the development of Chablais, Medbase Aigle Chablais represents a great opportunity for doctors wishing to invest in a practice of proximity, centered on the quality and well-being of patients.

Medbase attaches great importance to work-life balance. It is possible to work part-time in all functions and the work schedule can be flexibly arranged. For doctors, collaboration under employee or independent status is possible, to be agreed upon during the interview. Your tasks

  • You welcome and accompany patients and prepare examinations
  • You help the doctors during the consultation.
  • You perform laboratory tests, perform X-rays, perform administrative tasks on the computer and request additional information by phone
  • You perform various tasks autonomously and assume responsibility for them

Your profile

  • You report good performance in all subjects at section A/E high school after orientation
  • You're interested in medicine and health
  • You have a good sensitivity, you are reliable and able to work as a team, you treat your appearance and show courtesy.
  • You have a sense of human contact and great communication qualities

#WeAreMedbase

To achieve great things, one must have a vision of the future. Twenty years ago, Medbase was founded with the idea of creating a health care network for all of Switzerland. From a medical practice with three collaborators in 2001, we have now become a 360-degree health provider, with approximately 4,000 employees located in 170 sites, operating in four fields of activity: medical and dental centres, pharmacies, drug distribution and digital health platform. Take the opportunity to continue this success story with us, as Medbase continues to develop and improve every day the future of health care in Switzerland.
