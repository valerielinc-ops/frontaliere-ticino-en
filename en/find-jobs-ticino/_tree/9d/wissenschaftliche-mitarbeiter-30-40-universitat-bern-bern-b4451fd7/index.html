Full description

The University of Bern is interconnected. With us, you are part of an international community and benefit from interdisciplinary collaborations.

Missions

  • Analysis and modelling of agricultural production systems and sustainable production potential and identification of existing and potential marketing channels in the region.
  • Data collection and analysis
  • Drafting of texts and reports and participation in scientific and other publications
  • Support for the organization, implementation and documentation of workshops and conferences
  • Administrative support to project management

Requirements

  • University degree (master, degree) with specialization in agronomy/agriculture/food systems, agricultural economy, environmental sciences, sustainability sciences
  • Mother tongue: French; level C2 in German (ideally bilingual, understanding dialect being an asset), the project takes place in two languages
  • Knowledge and experience in sustainable food systems, agriculture, regional parks, and interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary research experience
  • Knowledge of quantitative and qualitative data collection and analysis
  • Experience in analysing statistical data (AGIS / AGOV, Agristat, AgriGis) would be an asset
  • Experience in project and program evaluation
  • Very good analytical skills as well as editorial skills and good expression skills
  • Self-employed, responsible and reliable method of work; co-operative personality, able to work as a team, communicating and with a sense of organization
  • Very good mastery of current Office software, computer-assisted data analysis and administration experience

Collaboration with Swiss food system stakeholders from the private sector, administration and regional parks Application and contact Date of taking up office: from 1 March 2027 or to be agreed Fixed-term contract: until 30 April 2029. An increase in working time could be envisaged at a later stage Working place: CDE, University of Bern, Mittelstrasse 43, Bern, Switzerland; www.cde.unibe.ch Deadline for applications: 30 November 2026 The CRC attaches great importance to diversity and equal opportunities. We therefore encourage applications from qualified individuals who meet the required criteria. At home, you create value. For yourself and for society.

Project

The research project "Bienne Food System – Intercantonal Model Region for Sustainable Food" (duration: 2026-2029) aims to make the region extending from Jura to Biel, via Seeland and parts of Mittelland, a sustainable regional food system model. This bilingual project has a transdisciplinary orientation and, as partners, involves actors from the municipal and cantonal administrations, natural parks and stakeholders throughout the food value chain. It is possible to participate in the additional evaluation of a programme. In this case, the workload could be slightly increased.
