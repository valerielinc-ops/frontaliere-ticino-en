Adventitious Personnel Competition Summer 2026 Description The OSCAM is an acute care hospital based in Castelrotto that serves the Malcantone region. The Giuseppe Rossi Foundation manages departments of internal medicine, surgery, psychiatry, obstetrics-gynecology and palliative care, as well as an emergency room and outpatient services for the population of the Lugano-Malcantone district. The competition is published as an official notice. The full details (requirements, professional profile, application procedure, deadline for submission, contact details of the reference staff) are available in the attached PDF document. Application Methods Applications are made by post or e-mail to the administrative management of OSCAM according to the instructions of the call.
