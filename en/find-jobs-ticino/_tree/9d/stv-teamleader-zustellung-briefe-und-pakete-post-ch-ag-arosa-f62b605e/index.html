Full description

Are you looking for an opportunity for personal development and want to expand your leadership skills? Then apply now. Because with you we create a motivating working environment and strengthen cooperation!

You work as part of the management trio of the logistics team at Chur Delivery Centre in Arosa and monitor and coordinate day-to-day business in this office.

*You can do it *

  • Together with the team management you lead a team of about 10 employees in the letter and parcel delivery.
  • As a leadership duo, they work hand in hand and promote team development.
  • You promote the exchange and cooperation in the team.
  • With your team, you are responsible for high-quality delivery.
  • You take on leadership roles (such as leading team meetings) and represent team leadership during absences.
  • You integrate new employees into the team and support them in the introductory phase.
  • You set a good example and support your team through your active involvement in day-to-day business.

You bring that with you

  • You bring experience in delivery and want to develop further in the field of leadership.
  • In the role as a role model, you feel comfortable and you have a talent for planning and organization.
  • Your appreciative approach and your willingness to help make you a reliable support for your team.
  • You are physically fit and have a driving license of categories B and A1.
  • Your safe handling of MS-Office sets you apart.
  • Very good oral German skills round off your profile.

With you, flexibility becomes everyday life. We promote progressive teamwork and value a balanced working life. This position can also be taken over by two persons in "co-management". You can apply directly as a duo or we support you in the search for a suitable person.
