Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The University of Freiburg is an educational institution, research centre, employer and event organiser in one. Its innovative power makes it an important engine for the economic and cultural life of the region. As an attraction for students and researchers from all over Switzerland and from all parts of the world, the university has shaped the life of the bilingual city of Freiburg since its foundation in 1889.

Become part of our team!

The employer promotes the compatibility of work and private life and offers its employees a modern working environment and attractive working conditions. You can find out more about this on the following page.

Their tasks

  • Establishment and coordination of a programme for the training of young researchers (postdocs)
  • Organisation of events and training programmes for early-stage researchers
  • Establishment of a further education program for professors and supervisors of young researchers, in close cooperation with the faculties
  • Building a website for postdocs
  • Collection of best practices
  • Participation in a national postdoc network
  • Regular evaluation and further development of measures and activities

Desired profile

  • Doctorate
  • Ideally, research experience after the doctorate
  • Knowledge of the Swiss university landscape
  • Ideally experience in editing websites
  • High self-motivation and flexibility
  • Social skills, customer orientation and teamwork
  • Mastery of the French, German or English language and good knowledge of the other two languages
