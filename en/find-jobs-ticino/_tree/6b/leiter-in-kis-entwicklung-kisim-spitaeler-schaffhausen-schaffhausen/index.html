Full description

Shape the digital future of hospitals Schaffhausen with us! Digitalization in the healthcare sector offers exciting opportunities to simplify processes, strengthen cooperation and sustainably improve everyday hospital life. This is where you come in! Two years ago, we introduced the clinic information system KISIM from Cistec. Now we want to further develop the system in a targeted manner, promote innovative solutions and further optimize its use in clinical everyday life. For this we are looking for a committed personality who brings ideas, inspires people and actively shapes change.

This is what awaits you

  • You design and lead the further expansion of our clinic information system KISIM, from the first clarification of needs to the successful introduction in everyday life
  • You develop requirements together with the medical profession, nursing, therapies and administration and develop practical solutions from them
  • You create concepts, coordinate their implementation and ensure that agreed goals are achieved
  • You will evaluate new features in terms of their benefits and impact and accompany their introduction with targeted training and a strong key user network
  • You inspire our employees for digital innovations, support them as a coach and ensure that KISIM is used optimally in everyday work
  • You are the central contact person for our system supplier Cistec, coordinate the cooperation, conduct negotiations and ensure compliance with agreed services
  • You follow the product development and new releases of Cistec, recognize opportunities at an early stage and derive concrete further development opportunities for the hospitals Schaffhausen

This will take us further.

  • You have an apprenticeship in computer science, business or medical informatics (HF/FH) or a basic education in the healthcare sector with in-depth training in computer science or project management
  • You have experience with clinic information systems, ideally KISIM, as well as in the management and implementation of projects.
  • You understand clinical and nursing processes and develop practical solutions
  • You work independently, pragmatically and goal-oriented and actively promote change
  • You communicate convincingly and stylishly in German, inspire different professional groups and win them for new solutions
  • You see yourself as the driving force of digitalization and accompany employees with commitment and enthusiasm through change
