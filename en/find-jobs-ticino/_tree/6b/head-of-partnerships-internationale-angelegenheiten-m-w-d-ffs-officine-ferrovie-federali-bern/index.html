Move Switzerland with us. Head of Partnerships - International Affairs (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, 01.01.2027 or suitable, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

As Head of Partnerships, you are actively contributing to shaping the collaboration between SBB Infrastructure and our main infrastructure partners in France and Italy. Together, we create the necessary conditions for efficient, reliable and attractive cross-border rail traffic. You link people, organisations and different interests, advance joint initiatives and thus contribute directly to sustainable mobility in Europe. You will have the opportunity to exercise a function offering great freedom of action, a concrete and visible impact, as well as an international network. This position will also allow you to develop your leadership and functional driving skills. What you can move.

  • As Head of Partnerships, you develop bilateral partnerships with France (SNCF Réseau) and Italy (RFI), in accordance with agreed objectives, and identify opportunities for joint projects in collaboration with the specialized units.
  • You initiate, coordinate and lead joint projects and activities, foster dialogue and ensure effective and results-oriented collaboration and communication.
  • You build strong and lasting relationships with our partners' interlocutors and decision makers, organize corporate exchange platforms, host meetings and facilitate the search for solutions on complex issues.
  • You regularly prepare reports and follow-up on the implementation of activities agreed with partners. In case of deviations from objectives, you will identify, in collaboration with the specialized units responsible, the appropriate corrective measures.
  • Ideally, you also take on the role of alternate or alternate national contact point (National Contact Point) for the TSI Telematics system task within the International Affairs and Technical Standards team.

What you bring.

  • You have strong skills in developing and maintaining strategic partnerships and know how to build trust with different stakeholders, including at the level of the Directorate-General.
  • You demonstrate strategic and cross-cutting thinking, identify common interests as well as development potentials and put them into practice through your strong focus on implementation and results.
  • You have excellent relational ease and a great ability to convince. Thanks to your sense of diplomacy, you can bring together parties with differing interests, including in complex or controversial situations.
  • You have a degree in economics, international relations, engineering, or equivalent tertiary level.
  • Ideally, you already have knowledge in the field of railway infrastructure as well as experience in implementing IT projects.
  • You must have mastered French and Italian at level C1. You also have a C1 level in English and a minimum B2 level in German, both oral and written.
