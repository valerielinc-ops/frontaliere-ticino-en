Full description

Are you familiar with textiles, household goods and paperwork? The varied work in sales appeals to you and you want to accompany our customers when shopping? Then you are exactly right with us. In the role of specialist manager, you serve and inspire our customers with an extensive range and are responsible for the professional leadership of the team.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, MM Worb, Richigenstr. 1, 3076 Worb

Mansioni
