Full description

The role of the first aid worker is becoming increasingly important. It helps to respond quickly and optimize time until emergency services arrive. SIRMED's first aid department offers first aid courses using the IAS system – both in the form of basic training and refresher training.</p>

Aufgaben

  • Train our diverse clientele – for example, business first aid workers, police officers or medical assistants – in the field of first aid
  • Development, planning and organization of continuing and complementary training for non-professionals
  • Ongoing assessment and implementation of the necessary measures

Anforderungen

  • Completed training in ES ambulance or BF ambulance technician or experience in emergency medicine as an ES/HES graduate nurse, ideally with post-graduate training in anaesthesia, intensive care or emergency care
  • Educational qualification acquired, e.g. ESFA 1, or willingness to complete further training in this field
  • Open personality and able to work as a team, enjoying varied pedagogical work
  • High flexibility and stress resistance
  • Taste for field work

More info about parplegie.ch/vorteile.

The Swiss Institute of Emergency Medicine SIRMED is a subsidiary of the Swiss Paraplegic Foundation and the Swiss Rescue Air Guard Rega. A leader in first aid, emergency medicine and rescue training, SIRMED offers practical courses in all national languages and in English. The courses take place in Nottwil or in the offices of the principals. At its headquarters, SIRMED has the most modern simulation rooms. In addition, SIRMED is a specialized higher school and thus trains both paramedics and paramedics. ES. The Institute employs about 45 people and employs about 300 freelancers.
