Full description

MAIN OBJECTIVE OF THE POST

Preschool class assistants are an integral part of the educational community and actively contribute to the proper functioning of the section. They accompany teachers in the implementation of educational and educational activities, promote the development and well-being of children and ensure their safety throughout the school day.

They thus contribute to creating a welcoming, caring, stimulating and secure environment conducive to the development and learning of each child.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure hygiene, health and safety of all students
  • Contribute to the daily organisation of the class and administrative tasks related to the operation of the preschool section (bus lists, day care, naps, attendance monitoring, etc.)
  • Work professionally with educational teams, families and various school services
  • Assist teachers to provide quality education
  • Support teachers in the preparation and facilitation of educational and educational activities
  • Participate in maintaining a positive, structured and learning-friendly classroom environment
  • Accompany during educational outings if necessary
  • Replace teachers in case of short absence by proposing activities designed and prepared by them
  • Replace assistants if necessary
  • Active monitoring and supervision during recreation and meal times
  • Accompany students on arrivals and departures by bus, ensuring their safety and well-being
  • Care for equipment
  • Take an active role in School initiatives, if necessary
  • Actively promote school values, including RISE, Golden Rules and Positive Discipline principles.

These responsibilities are not exhaustive and may evolve according to the needs of the institution.

APTITUDES AND SKILLS

To succeed in this position, you demonstrate

  • Positive, caring and professional attitude
  • Accountability
  • Organizational sense
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously
  • High interest in the development and well-being of young children
  • Initiative
  • Team spirit
  • Flexibility
  • Experience in communicating with children and adults
  • Interest in a multicultural environment
  • Resistance to job requirements
  • French or English mother tongue or equivalent and very good knowledge of the second language.

ESSENTIAL INFORMATION It is important that the incumbent of the position is always aware of the child protection guidelines and that he/she observe a professional distance with all students, while developing effective and caring working relationships. It understands and accepts the importance of diversity and integration and opposes any form of discrimination. Background surveys and reference checks for the past 10 years will be conducted during the selection process.

WHO ARE US

The Collège du Léman is one of Switzerland's most prestigious international boarding schools and externates, located on Lake Geneva, overlooking the Swiss Alps. We welcome children aged 2 to 18 who are part of an international community of more than 120 different nationalities.

Our vision is to shape generations that value differences and give our students the power to change the world for the better.

We cultivate individual strengths through our excellent educators and the wide variety of opportunities we offer in our program and activities.

The Collège du Léman is part of the Nord Anglia Education family, which has over 80 premium quality schools around the world.

Applications will be reviewed in order of arrival. We look forward to reading you.
