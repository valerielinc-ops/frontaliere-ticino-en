Full description

They understand hospital processes and combine technical know-how with a good sense of the needs of the users. As an Application Manager, you support core medical applications, analyze requirements and accompany solutions from the initial idea to stable operation. You will work closely with specialist departments and external suppliers and help ensure that medical applications function reliably and are continuously developed. The position is available immediately or by agreement.

Their tasks

  • They ensure the smooth operation of core medical applications and are an important interface between users and suppliers.
  • They analyse requirements from different specialist areas, coordinate them and advise internal customers on the applications and cross-departmental processes.
  • They accompany the conception, implementation and introduction of solutions and ensure their quality and reliable operation.
  • You take over the 2nd and 3rd level support, analyze any problems that arise and coordinate their resolution. They regularly provide pikett readiness and deployment
  • They develop solutions for new requirements and coordinate these with the participating specialist areas and suppliers.
  • They plan and carry out system tests, document the results and accompany software updates of the supervised systems.
  • They work on the further development of the medical application landscape and take into account the requirements of the various specialist areas.

Your profile

  • They have a higher education in medical informatics, computer science or business informatics (HF, FH or university), a completed vocational apprenticeship in computer science or relevant professional experience.
  • You have experience in supporting medical applications and business analysis.
  • They know the processes in the healthcare sector and how they are depicted in a medical application landscape.
  • You are familiar with terms such as archives, RIS, PACS, DICOM and HL7 interfaces and ITIL.
  • They have a strong customer and service orientation and communicate clearly and appropriately in German.
  • They think analytically, have good technical skills and quickly capture new connections.
  • You have advantageous knowledge of SQL as well as Windows Server and Linux.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
