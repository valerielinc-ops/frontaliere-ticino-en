Put to the Point

Actively participate in flood protection and the revitalization of waters in Switzerland.

  • Date of entry: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20318
  • Place of work: Mühlestrasse 2, 3063 Ittigen

You can make this contribution

  • Direct contact person of the cantonal specialist offices and the planners responsible for flood protection and revitalization work
  • Flood protection and restoration projects, accompanying and financially and technically controlling (for example, Ticino)
  • Opinions on dossiers affecting hydraulic engineering
  • Participate in the development of the hydraulic engineering strategy and the corresponding guides

This makes them unique.

  • Completed training as civil or environmental engineer/in ETH/FH or geographer/in
  • Several years of practical experience in the planning and realization of flood protection structures and revitalizations
  • Experience in project management and management of working and specialist groups
  • Good communication skills, negotiating skills creativity and flexibility
  • Knowledge of German at least level C2. Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and, if possible, passive knowledge of a third official language. Good to very good knowledge of English

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Quality of life for today and tomorrow.

The Federal Office for the Environment (FOEN) is responsible for the sustainable use of natural resources such as soil, water, air and forests, for protection against natural hazards and for the preservation of biodiversity. It is part of the Federal Department of Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications (UVEK).

We keep an eye on Switzerland today and tomorrow and assume responsibility for the successful development of our country in challenging projects. The FOEN offers the opportunity to make a significant contribution to a sustainable Switzerland in a modern working environment.

Additional information

Entry date: 01.01.2027 or by arrangement.

Persons from the French and Italian linguistic communities are sub-represented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
