Put to the Point

Do you love working exactly and do you like working in a team? Then become part of our HR Service Center of the Swiss Army and together with the teams HR Administration and HR Infoline (about 40 employees) ensure the wage and personnel administration as well as the advice of the approximately 6'500 civilian and approximately 3'500 military employees throughout Switzerland. Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to welcoming you as a new team member soon.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: permanent
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20701

You can make this contribution

  • Complete Deputy of Team Leader HR Admin 2
  • Competently advise employees, supervisors and HR managers in the HR Service Center and resolve inquiries in first and second level support
  • Maintain personnel, wage and organizational data in the HR systems and process wage, expense and social security processes reliably and on time
  • Perform mutations independently in SuccessFactors and SAP HCM (IPDM and PSD)
  • Processing complex special cases independently and carefully controlling financially relevant data and HR processes
  • actively accompany IT developments, projects and system tests, as well as create evaluations and analyses
  • Create work instructions and training documents and share knowledge in the team

  • Several years of experience in personnel and wage administration
  • Secure handling of SAP or comparable HR systems as well as enjoyment of digital processes

  • Interested in taking on responsibility and developing professionally

Your commitment to security and freedom The Army Staff (A Staff) supports the Chief of the Army in the leadership of the Departmental Department of Defence and is responsible for the overall military planning and development of the Swiss Army. Here, in accordance with the political guidelines, the strategic foundations, the armed forces development and the army and armaments planning are developed and tasks in the international security context are performed. The command, control and coordination of all cross-cutting areas (personnel, finance, real estate) also fall under the responsibility of the Army Staff.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
