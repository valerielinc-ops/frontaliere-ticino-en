Specialist in kitchens (m/f/d)

80% - 100%

Alephant

By agreement

Share

At pfister, we pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today – will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Competent advice and enthusiasm of our customers in our modern kitchen studios
  • Inspiration for our customers with our attractive and diverse range
  • Planning and selling dream kitchens to our customers with the help of the planning tool Carat
  • Responsible for the preparation and processing of the offer up to the purchase contract
  • Ongoing exchange with the teams in kitchen logistics and after-sales to ensure smooth order processing
  • Main contact for the customer up to the complete processing of the order, even if ordering, measuring and assembly of the kitchen are not part of the task area
  • Assistance in the implementation of an attractive product presentation in the kitchen studio
  • Committed sales talent and initiative on the sales floor
  • Joy and experience in consulting-intensive dealing with customers
  • Team player, open-minded and customer- and solution-oriented
  • Negotiating knowledge of German
  • Completed training in the sales or craft sector
  • Experience with digital planning systems (furniture, kitchens, spatial planning, etc.)
  • Oral French skills
