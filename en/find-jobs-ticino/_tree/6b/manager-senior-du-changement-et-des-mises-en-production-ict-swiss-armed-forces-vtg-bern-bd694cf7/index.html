Put to the Point

Become part of the Cyber Command and actively shape the digitalization of the Swiss Army. Get involved in this unique task – building the new digital platform – in a dynamic working environment.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21094
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Assuming responsibility for the required implementation of changes and releases in the operational operation of the army’s digitalization platform (NDP) as well as for process management in the area of service operations
  • Define methods and standards in configuration and change management, support engineers in changes and releases, ensure their scheduled and timely implementation and, if necessary, create management reports
  • Configuration, change and release management processes, coordinate processes and intervene in the event of discrepancies to ensure trouble-free NDP operation
  • Support internal and external audits in the area of change and release reviews if necessary

This makes you unique

  • University of Applied Sciences (FH) / University / ETH in the field of engineering or computer science or equivalent education
  • Several years of professional experience in an application manager, ICT support or comparable function in the ICT sector
  • Leadership experience is beneficial, with high IT and service management affinities, for the development of sustainable, compliance-compliant change and release management solutions
  • Experiences in the military, professional or militia, or in the public sector facilitate familiarization in a challenging phase and are therefore advantageous
  • Good active knowledge of an official language and passive knowledge of a second official language

  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
