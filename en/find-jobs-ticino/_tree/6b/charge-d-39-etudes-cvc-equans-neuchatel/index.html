Full description

CCV study supervisor 100%

Placed under the direct authority of the Head of Service and based in Neuchâtel, we are looking for a 100% study.

Your missions

  • You are working on the technical development of a "project" of HVAC works or installations,
  • You propose a global technical solution,
  • You realize the schematics of principle and the dimensioning of equipment and networks,
  • You prepare applications for authorisation and technical specifications,
  • You set the cost price of the preliminary draft's estimated budget for the bid book,
  • You follow up on the offer and additional requests from the customer,
  • You manage the interfaces between services and potential co-contractors to complete your study,
  • You are responsible for organizing your study and meeting the deadlines for submitting documents or submitting offers,
  • You are the client's preferred contact during the study and encryption phase.
  • You ensure the transfer to the Chargé d'affaires for execution.
  • You make live quotes on request from the customer, ranging from a small installation modification to the complete proposal of a new concept,
  • You intervene mainly in the industrial sector,
  • You perform private or public bid encryption and are able to offer alternative technical solutions,
  • You ensure the transfer of business records to business managers,
  • You work on multiple topics simultaneously,
  • You know and respect the standards and regulations of the profession and intervene in support of operations.

What Characterizes You

  • A graduate in climate engineering or equivalent, you have a minimum of 5 years' experience in the field of CVC studies in Switzerland.
  • Your spirit of analysis and synthesis, as well as your rigor, allow you to precisely dimension and encrypt.
  • You can manage multiple projects simultaneously.
  • You like to go on site to understand the operation of the installations and propose modifications.
  • You stand out for your team spirit, sense of initiative and customer orientation.
  • Finally, you master computer tools perfectly.
