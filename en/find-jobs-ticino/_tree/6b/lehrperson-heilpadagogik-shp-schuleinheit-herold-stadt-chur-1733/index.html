Full description

Teacher Healing Education SHP, School Unit Herold With 41,000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees.

The Chur City School is the largest public school in the canton of Graubünden with numerous attractive offers from kindergarten to secondary school.

The Herold School House is one of 12 primary schools. In the corresponding 5 kindergartens and 12 primary classes, a total of 310 children from 40 dedicated teachers are currently taught. With a total of nine pavilions, the building creates an atmosphere like a residential quarter. The idea behind this is that it favors a family mood. With the butterfly roofs and the clever parceling of the outdoor squares, the School House Herold is an eye-catcher and architecturally of cantonal importance.

To complement our team, we are looking for a

Teacher Healing Education SHP, 6 lessons

Cycle 1 / Primary level / for a 2nd class

Their tasks • Teaching with passion: You teach in a 2nd grade primary level as a teacher SHP. Working together with class and specialist teachers within the framework of integrative promotion in order to optimally address the heterogeneity of the class and to individually promote students is the basis of your work. Other experienced SHP teachers work at the Herold School House. This ensures a successful and exciting cooperation. • Teamwork and exchange: You work closely with a helpful and committed team. Joint projects and cross-class events are important to us.

Further fields of activity are described in the professional assignment for teachers of the city of Chur in the fields of teaching, learners, school and teacher. Your profile You have qualified training as a SHP teacher. Together with all participating teachers, they design a lesson that enables learning success for all children. You are the contact person for the parents of your students. Your SHP work is carried out integratively. Their active participation in joint school events completes the overall picture. Our offer Become part of our team! We offer you:

  • Common stance: we learn together, we care for each other and we take responsibility. These principles shape our school day.
