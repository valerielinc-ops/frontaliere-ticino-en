Full description

Are you ready for a job where you can make a meaningful difference for people and nature? Our garden monument maintenance department supervises the municipal inventory, accompanies construction projects, supports the maintenance of historical facilities and carries out public relations work. As of January 1, 2027 or by appointment, the Garden Monument Care offers a one-year internship.

Tasks

  • They participate in the advice and assessment of building applications in the areas of garden monument maintenance and associated qualitative procedures
  • Support for archival research
  • You work in professional and technical projects (e.g. inventory sheets, care plans)
  • You support the team in administrative and operational tasks

Profile

  • Study of landscape architecture, architecture or art history, ideally with a focus on garden monument maintenance
  • Conceptual thinking, structured working methods and good self-organization
  • Knowledge of Vectorworks or a comparable drawing program
  • Secure language and text understanding as well as stylish written and creative expression

We offer

An internship at the City of Zurich provides insights into everyday work, offers the opportunity to get a concrete picture of a profession, gain initial professional experience and opens up prospects for further professional career.
