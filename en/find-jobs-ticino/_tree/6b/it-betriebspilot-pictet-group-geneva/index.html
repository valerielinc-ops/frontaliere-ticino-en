IT Operations Pilot — Pictet Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your team

The Pictet Tech division ensures the design and integration of a wide range of IT solutions, from portfolio management systems to trading platforms, banking applications and business management tools. At the origin of some of the Group's most important innovations, the division is also responsible for the proper functioning of operations and the development of depository banking and fund administration services.

Within the Infrastructure & Production department, the Production Monitoring & Control (PMC) team is responsible for the ongoing monitoring of all IT resources and their operational management, within the required service levels.

The evolution of the visions and practices of Computer Production is at the centre of our concerns and is taken into account in all our interventions. As IT Operations Shift Leader, you will be part of a team that plays a key role in ensuring the proper functioning of the infrastructure, monitoring and availability of the information system (IS), while providing an innovative vision based on new technologies.

Your role

  • Ensure production by guaranteeing the level and commitment of service, monitoring of alerts, unlocking, monitoring of production plan and opening of incidents;
  • Analyze messages received at the pilotage unit and perform all routine operations necessary for the start-up and proper functioning of the IT infrastructure, according to the procedures defined;
  • Propose and implement methodological and technical solutions to optimize computer production;
  • Power the base of incidents and ensure maintenance of pilotage documentation;
  • Provide service support tailored to applications under our responsibility, working effectively with the various teams involved;
  • Identify opportunities for improvement related to new technologies in order to adapt the tools/processes used;
  • Ensure the maintenance of the general conditions of production and follow our plan on schedule 3x8, 7j/7.

Your profile

  • CFC or ES computer technician degree, or equivalent;
  • Experience in flying activity (one plus);
  • Knowledge of Windows, Unix and Linux environments, Control-M, Kibana, Jira and Grafana (one plus);
  • Very good analytical and communication skills;
  • Mastery of spoken and written French; good knowledge of English;
  • Home in Switzerland required.

With a good listening capacity, available and flexible, you want to join a dynamic team where you can showcase your spirit of collaboration and appreciate your efficiency. Your sense of analysis, your pragmatism and your strong stress resistance will be as valuable assets that will enable you to carry out the missions you will be responsible for.
