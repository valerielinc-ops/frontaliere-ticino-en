Full description

This is what you can expect

In this role, you will be responsible for supporting general agencies in the area of pensions. This applies in particular to the general agencies in northwestern Switzerland, the Swiss Plateau and Central Switzerland. Travel activity therefore accounts for around 40%.

This is what you can do with us

The focus of this position is on enabling and supporting our external organisation in terms of pension provision for private individuals and SMEs.

You will train the new insurance advisors in central courses and offer further training in the general agencies. With your experience and expertise, you will support the pension specialists and pension planners of the general agencies in the performance of their tasks and offer them platforms for the exchange of experience and further development.

In the case of complex preventive care cases, you will also take part in the customer meeting.

What you bring to the table

  • Completed specialist certificate in the field of insurance or financial planning or equivalence
  • Several years of experience in the sale of pension solutions as well as in adult education
  • Experience in coaching and developing employees and distributors
  • Knowledge of advising or managing an SME is a plus
  • Sense of innovation and ability to motivate people
  • Outgoing, willing to compromise as well as goal- and solution-oriented personality
  • Great independence and commitment
  • In-depth MS Office knowledge including Copilot
  • Mother tongue Swiss German, French is a plus

Working with us

A team of various specialists is waiting for you. Together you will live a communicative, solution-oriented and independent working atmosphere. You can find out more about our commitment as an employer below.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. We participate generously in job-relevant, external training courses.

Perks

Among other things, we offer you up to a 25% premium discount, a Half-Fare travelcard, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with a flat rate and discounts on cultural events.
