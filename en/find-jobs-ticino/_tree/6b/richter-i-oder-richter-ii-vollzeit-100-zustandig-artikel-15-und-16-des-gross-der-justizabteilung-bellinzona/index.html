Giurista I oppure Giurista II a tempo pieno (100%), incaricato/a (art. 15 e 16 LORD) presso la Divisione della giustizia, Bellinzona — Amministrazione Cantonale Ticino

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • examines and ensures the management of legal practices relating to the matters covered by the Division of Justice and the administrative units to it subordinated
  • prepares draft messages, bills, regulations and directives in matters covered by the Division of Justice
  • makes decisions on complaint and appeal of the Division of Justice
  • prepares proposals for response to parliamentary acts, federal and cantonal consultation procedures and various correspondence
  • deals with practices in civil liability of the State
  • provides legal advice on issues of jurisdiction of the Division of Justice
  • carries out special tasks at the request of the Directorate of the Division of Justice

Requirements

  • academic training (bachelor + master or license) in Swiss law with patent lawyer
  • good knowledge of the main Microsoft computer tools and propensity to digitization
  • excellent linguistic knowledge of Italian (conversation and editing), good knowledge (conversation and editing) of French and German
  • positive and positive attitude, critical sense, precision, reliability and confidentiality
  • decision-making, analysis and synthesis
  • guidance on achieving objectives
  • aptitude for independent and group work, flexibility, ability to manage workload
  • availability and strong communication skills
  • excellent conduct, no previous
  • Swiss citizenship

Special remarks

  • employment report: Article 15 and 16 of the EC Treaty
  • the function of scientific collaborator/scientist I (class 10) can be attributed only to candidates who have complete academic qualifications: university license, degree, Bachelor's + Master's degree or professional university (Bachelor + Master 270/300 ECTS credits) and who have gained at least 15 years of professional experience in the required scope
  • the function of scientific collaborator/scientist/II (class 9) will be attributed to candidates with less than 15 years of professional experience in the required field and in possession of complete academic qualifications: university license, degree, consecutive university master or professional university school (Bachelor + Master 270/300 ECTS credits)

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

11 August 2026
