Become part of our team!

For the Raiffeisenbank Appenzeller Hinterland in Herisau, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Urnäsch and Waldstatt, we are looking for a personality as a customer consultant.

What awaits you?

  • Competent and comprehensive advice to private customers in the basic products Numbers, saving, investing and provision
  • Actively contributes to the positioning of Raiffeisen as a competent partner in the field of finances (especially investments and pensions)
  • Responsible for the strategically compliant maintenance and support of a team customer portfolio of the Private Customer Base segment
  • Actively addresses customers and non-customers, recognizes individual customer needs and transfers them to the relevant specialist areas
  • Performing the reception and hosting role in the customer zone

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training
  • Strong customer and sales-oriented thinking
  • They have hosting qualities.
  • They are empathetic, communicative and love contact with people
