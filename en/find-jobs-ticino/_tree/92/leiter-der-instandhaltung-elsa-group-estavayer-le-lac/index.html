Full description

Elsa Group is one of the leading Swiss dairy industry groups and is part of the Migros Group (Migros Industry). Elsa Group is an expert in the manufacture and packaging of dairy products as well as in the production, refining and processing of cheeses and the production of plant-based products.

Mansioni

  • Organize, plan and perform preventive and corrective maintenance on facilities (production machines and/or logistics), to ensure optimal operation
  • Identify and implement continuous improvement actions to optimize equipment performance
  • Analyze breakdowns through root cause methods and implement sustainable corrective actions
  • Monitoring and implementing an action plan on performance indicators (availability, efficiency, costs) to ensure reliability of facilities
  • Work actively with operational departments to prioritize interventions and coordinate internal and external resources
  • Participate in the management of spare parts in collaboration with the technical store, in order to guarantee their availability and minimize unforeseen stopping times
  • Coaching, developing and supporting the maintenance team, promoting skill development and versatility
  • Ensure continuous operation of equipment by planning and participating in the picket service

Required

  • Experience confirmed: in industrial maintenance and in the conduct of personnel
  • Initial or continuing training completed: in the technical field (automatician, polymerician, electrician mechanic or equivalent), supplemented by a federal patent for maintenance agent
  • Ability to work independently, with an analytical mindset and oriented solutions
  • Adaptable leadership, with a good team spirit and flexibility
  • Knowledge of IT maintenance management tools such as SAP PM or other GMAO, as well as MS Office
  • Home in the area due to picket service (max. 45 minutes drive)
  • French (Very good knowledge)
  • German (Basic terms)
  • English (Basic terms)
