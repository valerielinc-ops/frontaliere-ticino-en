Full description

The Community Health and Habitat Directorate (DSCH) includes teams from the 29 IEPAs operated by IMAD. The managers of the IEPA team are responsible for the tenants of these buildings whose owners are foundations of public or private law or communal law. Each IEPA team leader is responsible for up to 60 employees. Your missions and responsibilities

  • Direct and coordinate the decentralized activities of the IEPA service, on delegation from management, ensuring a regular presence at the sites and constant alignment with institutional policies and values
  • Managing teams in compliance with HR policy by ensuring daily functioning, cohesion and operational performance
  • Ensure adequate response to tenants' support, safety and supervision needs, ensuring quality and relevance of services
  • Promote customer orientation across all actions, promoting a tenant-centred approach and strengthening interdisciplinary practices and inter-service collaboration
  • Organize, animate and monitor teams on different buildings by structuring efficient and results-oriented communication spaces
  • Develop, develop and enforce directives, procedures and operating standards in heterogeneous environments
  • Piloting the planning and implementation of Community actions within IEPAs under responsibility
  • Ensure budget management and monitoring in collaboration with the administrative officer, in accordance with current procedures
  • Conduct, lead and participate in institutional projects and cross-cutting working groups within the IEPA Directorate
  • Collaborate with the owner foundations, their principals and the cantonal and communal partners, and ensure continuity of activities by replacing the other team leaders.
  • Bachelor in social work HES or university deemed equivalent accepted or Bachelor HES-SO in nursing
  • University training in management would be an asset
  • Minimum 5 years of practice in community health or social work
  • Demonstrated managerial experience and ability to manage in a multidisciplinary manner some 60 decentralized collaborators
  • Interest in mobility in the canton and managing various teams
  • There is an interest in managing several decentralised activities simultaneously
  • Very good organization to monitor long-term activities
  • Excellent sense of communication and negotiation, good situational and relational intelligence
  • Excellent knowledge of the Geneva health and social network.
  • Posting time: 8.11.2026
  • Entry into office: to be agreed
  • Please attach to your file:
  • an updated curriculum vitae,
  • a cover letter,
  • a copy of your diplomas and work certificates.
