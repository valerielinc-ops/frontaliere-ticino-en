Homepage Team Leader Test and Release Management (TURM) Rothenburg, Switzerland Who are you working with? Together with your team, you are responsible for the quality and release capability of our software, from test strategy to safe and efficient delivery. Your everyday life With us, you can expect multifaceted tasks after a systematic introduction:

  • Head of Testing
  • End-to-end responsibility for test strategy and quality assurance
  • Organisation and implementation of individual and release tests of development results
  • Integration of automated tests and quality gates as well as continuous development of continuous testing
  • Creating the specifications, realizing and maintaining the test environments in cooperation with the system technology
  • Bug and performance analysis
  • Head of Release Management
  • Creation of deliverable development results (updates, subsequent deliveries, etc.) for tests and approvals
  • Coordination of the provision, validation and release of software releases
  • Creation of documentation (release notes, update description, technical notes)
  • Evaluation and promotion of the use of modern tools, such as AI, to improve testing and release management processes
  • Staff management
  • Work and resource planning, technical support, promotion and further development of employees

Your background So that you can successfully start or start with us, you have:

  • Extensive professional experience as a software tester, ISTQB Certified Tester - Foundation Level (or equivalent training)
  • Leadership experience
  • Strong quality awareness
  • Good analytical skills, systematic and structured working methods
  • Affinity and understanding of business processes (ERP)
  • Process and application knowledge in the field of finance (necessary for testing interfaces)
  • Strong communication (written and oral) team spirit, healthy assertiveness, flexibility, independence, creative solution finding and initiative
  • Advantages: ISTQB Certified Tester – Advanced Level (Test Manager or Test Analyst)
  • Advantage: Good knowledge in the field of systems engineering (operating systems, network, hardware)

Our Benefits

Among other things, we offer you

  • Flexible working hours, annual working hours and sabbaticals
  • A top modern workplace
  • Valuable and attractive training offers
