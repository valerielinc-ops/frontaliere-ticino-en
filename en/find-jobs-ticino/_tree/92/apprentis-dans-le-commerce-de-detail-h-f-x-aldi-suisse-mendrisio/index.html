About ALDI SUISSE AG

ALDI SUISSE AG is a Swiss company based in Schwarzenbach (SG) and is part of the company ALDI SÜD, a global company operating in retail trade. Simplicity, responsibility and reliability are the fundamental values that guide business. This means that, in addition to offering quality products at an always affordable price, it also strives to maintain sustainable and environmentally friendly production and animals. A substantial part of the turnover obtained from the approximately 1800 products of the standard assortment of SUISSE ALDI comes from articles of Swiss origin. With over 4000 employees and 247 subsidiaries, ALDI SUISSE has been positioned since 2005 as one of the largest employers in the Swiss retail sector.
