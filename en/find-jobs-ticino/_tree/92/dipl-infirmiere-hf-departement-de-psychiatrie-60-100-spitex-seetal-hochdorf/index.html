Full description

Spitex Seetal is an innovative and future-oriented non-profit organization. Competent and flexible – Our team is working around the clock for people who need support at home. Whether care, physiotherapy, psychiatry or housekeeping - Around 80 employees and 17 apprentices/students provide high-quality and needs-based care for the Seetal population with a lot of personal commitment, pleasure and competence. To strengthen our team, we are looking for you immediately or by agreement, as Dipl. Nurse HF, Department of Psychiatry 60 - 100% Your tasks

  • You look after our clients with all medical conditions in the different and complex situations of the outpatient psychiatric area professionally and responsibly.
  • As a case-leading care specialist, you assume responsibility for planning, controlling and controlling all steps in the care process of the clients.
  • You work closely with the services involved such as psychiatric clinics, doctors, social authorities, etc.
  • You will help to further develop the Department of Psychiatry within our Spitex organization as well as to build networking within the regional non-profit Spitex organizations in the Department of Psychiatry.

Your profile

  • You have a completed education as a dipl. Nurse HF, Department of Psychiatry.
  • You have at least 2 years of professional experience in outpatient or inpatient psychiatric care and meet the legal requirements to independently carry out needs assessments.
  • You are a reflected, open-minded and flexible personality and have a high level of expertise.
  • You have a forward-looking and structured way of working and keep an overview even in challenging situations.
  • You have the driving licence category B.
