Full description

In order to complete its team at the Adult Psychiatry Hospital Unit (UH Jaman), the Nant Foundation is putting into the competition a position of

100% Nurse Jaman THE MISSION The nurses of the Jaman Hospital Unit are responsible for providing the care required for each patient in close collaboration with an interdisciplinary team. Jaman is an open hospital unit operating on the model of the therapeutic community.

WE OFFER

The Nant Foundation is a private institution recognized in the public interest, mandated by the State to organize and provide psychiatric care in the eastern Vaud region. Working in Jaman is working with an interdisciplinary team offering short- to medium-term stays to support and consolidate the recovery process of patients with severe mental disorders causing social and relational difficulties. WE SEEK A nurse with experience in psychiatry, holder of a Bachelor of Science HES-SO in nursing with Red Cross recognition (or equivalent). Furthermore, having the ability to interact with patients in both group and individual dynamics is important. We want to hire a person motivated by the Foundation's public health mission and loving the work as an interdisciplinary team.
