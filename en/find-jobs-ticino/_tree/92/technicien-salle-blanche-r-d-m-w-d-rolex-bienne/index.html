Full description

Introduction

For our Research and Development department, we are looking for a (M/F) R&D Technician whose main mission will be to ensure the proper functioning of the clean room and the continuous improvement of practices and processes.

Responsibilities

  • Perform clean room operations in the field of microfabrication
  • Ensure compliance with safety, quality, cleanliness and traceability requirements
  • Conduct process equipment according to instructions and revenues defined
  • Prepare and handle semiconductor components, consumables and tools for testing
  • Perform microfabrication steps such as cleaning, deposits, photolithography, engraving or other processes according to available equipment
  • Inform follow-up documents to ensure traceability of operations and enter data into IT tools
  • Ensure first level maintenance and ensure cleanliness standards are maintained in the clean room

Profile

  • Laboratory CFCs, Polymechanic, Automatician, Electronician, Technologist or equivalent training
  • Diploma of ES technician in microtechnology, industrial systems, processes or related field, an asset
  • HES training in microtechnics, materials, chemistry, applied physics or close discipline, welcome for some technical profiles
  • Experience in clean room, micro-fabrication, semiconductor environment or high-tech industrial setting is an advantage
  • Knowledge of clean room working rules, contamination requirements, security and documentary discipline
  • Experience or interest in microfabrication processes and silicon substrate handling
  • Concept of thin layer processes, photolithography, engraving or metrology is a plus
  • Interest in demanding technological environments and R&D activities

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
