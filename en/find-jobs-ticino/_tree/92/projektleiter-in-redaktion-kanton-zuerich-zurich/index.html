Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Teaching Equipment publishing Zurich Project manager/in editorial staff 80 - 100% The Lehrmittelverlag Zürich is the leading provider of learning media in the elementary school. As a cantonal publisher, he develops, produces and distributes teaching tools for the whole of German-speaking Switzerland with around 75 employees. To complement our sympathetic and highly motivated editorial team, we are looking for a strong personality as a project manager at the earliest possible time with a humanities degree, a weakness for language and publishing experience.

  • As a project manager/editor, you are responsible for the development of new teaching tools from the idea to realisation. They initiate, plan and manage projects across all development phases, from conception to proofreading to market launch. You work closely with the internal specialist departments as well as with external authors and experts. In addition, you will supervise the further development and updating of existing teaching materials and take care of their ongoing maintenance and corrections.
  • You have good knowledge of the school field and relevant experience in publishing work as well as project management and have already worked as an editor, ideally in a teaching material publisher. Strong communication and organizational skills complement your professional qualifications. Your German is stylish, knowledge of English and/or French is required. A secure appearance, negotiating skills and skill in dealing with people are among your strengths, as well as resilience, assertiveness and teamwork. The good command of the MS-Office range and collaboration platforms is a matter of course for you.
  • We offer this diverse and challenging activity is integrated into a team of competent, experienced and warm colleagues. You can expect a modernly furnished workplace and contemporary employment conditions as well as the possibility of home office.
