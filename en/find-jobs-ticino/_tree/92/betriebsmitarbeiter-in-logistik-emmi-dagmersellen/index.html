Full description

You feel at home in logistics, like to take part and keep an overview even if a lot is going on? Then you are exactly right with us! Together with your team, you ensure that our goods are reliably in the right place at the right time.

You can do that.

  • The control, booking and professional storage of goods from production, raw materials, packaging materials and other deliveries is in your hands.
  • You pick our products reliably, prepare them for loading and provide the required goods for production.
  • All movements and orders are carefully processed and recorded in our ERP systems.
  • For replacements in the areas of HRL, cooler and control room, you are flexible and support in general forwarding and logistics tasks.
  • With clean documentation and compliance with our quality, safety, environmental and hygiene requirements, you ensure reliable processes.
  • Regular cleaning work according to the cleaning plan is also part of your versatile working day.

You bring that with you

  • Training in logistics is an advantage. Equally welcome is corresponding practical professional experience.
  • Reliability, care and an independent working method distinguish you.
  • Even if it gets hectic, you keep track and set the right priorities.
  • Dealing with digital systems and ERP applications is easy for you or you are motivated to familiarize yourself with them.
  • Quality, safety, hygiene and a responsible approach to our environment are a matter of course for you.
  • As a flexible and helpful personality, you appreciate teamwork and get involved where support is needed.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
