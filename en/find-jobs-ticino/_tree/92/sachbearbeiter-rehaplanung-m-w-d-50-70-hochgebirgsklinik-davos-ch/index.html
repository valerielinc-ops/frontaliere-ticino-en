Full description

During rehabilitation planning, you ensure that our patients are at the right time with the right doctor or in the right therapy. You are the hub between the medical profession and therapies. Schedule doctor's appointments: You plan the doctor's appointments of our patients. Coordinate therapies: You coordinate the appointments in the therapeutic areas. Working digitally: You communicate electronically and edit documents in the system. |

  • Requirements: We are not looking for an ideal cast on paper, but people who work well-founded and in the mood for something new. Education: You have completed a commercial education or an education as an MPA. Healthcare experience is a plus. IT: You work safely with MS Office
  • Knowledge of KISIM is a plus. Language: You speak and write German very well [level C1]. Working method: You keep track of many appointments and stay friendly in contact with patients and teams.
