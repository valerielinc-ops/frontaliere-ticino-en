Full description

Portrait The standard school MR Spiez-Aeschi-Krattigen supports children with special needs in 12 school locations with more than 1,350 pupils. We are looking for 26.04.2027

Tasks

You are responsible for the curative care of 4 primary classes

Integrative support according to the guideline MR

-Advising and supporting teachers in teaching planning and implementation as well as in dealing with a specific challenge of a child -Cooperative teaching with the rule instructor -Promotion of students in small groups

Requirements

They have completed training in curative curative education, are in training for it or have other educational experience. They quickly find a good connection to children and can work together with various teachers. They help to implement integrative teaching and curative education in school. At the same time, you can respond to the opportunities and needs of local teachers. Pensum can be divided into 3 to 4 half-days

We offer

  • temporary employment in cantonal LAG. Continuation of employment within schools Spiez possible from summer 2027
  • an efficiently organized school with a supportive MR team on site
  • Integration into a competent, well-connected and helpful MR-Kollegium Spiez-Aeschi-Krattigen
  • Good connection to public transport
  • Work where others go on holiday
