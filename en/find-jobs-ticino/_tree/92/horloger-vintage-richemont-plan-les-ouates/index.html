Full description

Reference code: JR135077

Found in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the oldest watch Manufacture in continuous production for more than 270 years, actually perpetuating a proud heritage of watchmaking excellence and stylistic sophistication through generations of master craftsmen.

At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timespieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches.

FOR THOSE AND THOSE WHO RESEARCH EXCELLENCE

Join one of the iconic houses of high watchmaking, where excellence is a constant requirement. Take part in our collective success and the creation of our future heritage.

YOUR REASON TO BE IN THE HOUSE

We are looking for a Vintage (M/F) Watchmaker.

Connected to Operations, the Customer Service department is dedicated to ensuring relationship excellence and improving customer experience. To do this, we rely on solid expertise, innovative projects and data-driven operational performance, in order to rigorously meet the demands of Luxury.

YOUR ROLE TO PLAY

You will be responsible for

· Ensure the maintenance and restoration of timepieces from the 1930s to 1980s within the framework of the Customer Service Workshop, in order to guarantee a restitution in accordance with the quality criteria of Vacheron Constantin and the deadlines set · Establish the diagnosis of parts and the preparation and monitoring of subcontracting services · Conduct strict dismantling and cleaning of parts · Replace defective components · Crossing of plates and bridges · Run the blocked flat polished steels · Replace balance axes and balance · Ensure complete assembly, adjustment and housing of timepieces. · Work in self-control while actively contributing to the continuous improvement of the working environment and workshop performance.

QUALITY FOR EXCELLING

Success in this role depends on

· Your proven experience as Horloger Restoration, ideally started with an apprenticeship and complemented by several years of experience in the field · Your mastery of restoration of ancient pieces and your desire to express your talent on emblematic period mechanisms of the House (1930-1980) · Your motivation to work on beautiful pieces while challenging yourself daily on achieving goals and participating in the right team dynamics · Your ease in using IT tools · Your excellent mastery of French, ideally supported by a good level of English · Your acute sense of organization, combining autonomy, proactivity and efficiency · Your natural team spirit, as well as fluid and transparent communication

Vacheron Constantin celebrated his 271th anniversary as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755. Based in Geneva, Switzerland, our House operates a global network of nearly 200 outlets in 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional community – watchmakers, artisans and professionals – shares the same passion for our legacy and ambition to build an even brighter future.

Ready to be part of our story? We will be delighted to discover your application.
