Full description

Education Directorate Vocational School Uster Deputy Teacher for Physics (temporary until July 2027), 3 lessons, 12% From the spring semester 2027 we are looking for a committed and initiative teacher. You accompany and promote our learners in apprenticeship teaching and our students in basic vocational training as well as in vocational maturity in the Beriech Technik. With us, you have the opportunity to develop new training concepts in an innovative and multifunctional learning and working environment and to implement your ideas creatively. Take a look at our website and get an impression. Your tasks

  • You impart knowledge in the field of physics to our learners and accompany them on their way in basic vocational training and vocational maturity.
  • In our modern learning environment, you develop and implement creative and future-oriented training concepts.
  • You promote the social development of our learners and support them in their professional and personal development.
  • With your enthusiasm for the subject, you inspire the learners and motivate them to realize their full potential. Your profile
  • You have completed a physics degree (major) and the associated teaching diploma or are still in the middle of training as a teacher.
  • It gives you pleasure to inspire young people and inspire them with new ideas.
  • You’ll be creative and enjoy developing and applying innovative teaching methods and have a high technological affinity to effectively integrate modern tools and digital media into the classroom.
  • Your social competence helps you to promote the personal development of the learners and to accompany them individually.
  • Your own initiative, team spirit and sense of responsibility characterize you. Our offer
  • A state-of-the-art infrastructure with diverse learning and work opportunities.
  • A motivated, collaborative and creative team working together to further develop the educational landscape.
  • The opportunity to contribute your ideas in a dynamic environment and to actively shape the future of vocational training. Your application We look forward to meeting you. If you have any questions, Debora Matthey (personal@bzu.ch ), Head of Human Resources, is happy to be there for you.
