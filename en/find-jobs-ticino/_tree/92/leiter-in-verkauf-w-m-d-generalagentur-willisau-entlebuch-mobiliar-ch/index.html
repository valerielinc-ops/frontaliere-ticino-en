Full description

Do you want to lead, shape and make a difference together? : Then this task could be just right for you.

For our general agency Willisau-Entlebuch we are looking for a committed and entrepreneurial leader who enjoys people, sales and the further development of a successful organization.

As a manager: in sales, you take on a central key role with us. Together with the management team, you shape the future of our general agency, further develop our sales organization and create the conditions for our employees to be successful.

Two things are particularly important to us: We want to be successful and achieve ambitious goals. At the same time, we want to create a working environment in which people enjoy working, supporting each other and celebrating success together.

This is what you do with us

You shape a positive, trusting and at the same time performance-oriented leadership culture. You are close to your employees, give orientation, lead as an example and manage to inspire people for common goals.

You know our customers and our market, recognize opportunities and derive the right sales activities from them. Together with your team, you ensure active and ambitious market development and that we achieve our goals.

Despite leadership responsibility, you never lose sight of our customers. You maintain your network, represent our general agency in the region and also manage an existing customer portfolio yourself.

This is what you bring with you

  • Delight in leading, nurturing and making people successful
  • Leadership experience and conviction through your strong social and communication skills
  • Courage to initiate change, use new technological possibilities and consistently implement decisions
  • Customer and market orientation and pleasure in sales
  • Knowledge of the insurance industry and corresponding professional and ideal leadership experience
  • Self-employment and you keep track of challenging tasks
  • A dipl. HF degree, a federal professional card as an insurance specialist or a dipl. Financial planner is a plus

What awaits you with us? A really good team. A lot of room for manoeuvre. Short distances. And the opportunity to really make a difference.

The general agency Willisau-Entlebuch employs 46 people, including five learners. Our own claims department is also directly on site. We are strongly anchored in our region and want to be a personal, reliable partner for our customers. As a manager: in sales, you are not simply part of our organization. You are a central person for our joint success and for the further development of our general agency. With us, you get a lot of trust, responsibility and design room.

In order to perform this function, the Insurance Supervision Act requires the conclusion of an insurance intermediary/in VBV, Profil Allbranche. If this is not available, we are happy to support with tailor-made internal training.

Job entry takes place immediately or by agreement.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
