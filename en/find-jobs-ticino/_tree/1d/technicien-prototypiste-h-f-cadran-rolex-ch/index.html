Full description

Introduction

For our Research & Development division, we are looking for a (M/F) Protot Spherical Technician whose main mission will be to make complete prototypes of dials.

Responsibilities

  • Machine dial prototypes
  • Think about design in collaboration with manufacturers and project managers
  • To achieve the ranges linked to the trade skills of the team (bottom-up, decal, assembly, and use of the means of machining available in the workshop)
  • Program under Creo ProE (or other FAO software)
  • Processing in digital control on 3/5 axis milling and turning
  • Design and implement specific tools
  • Order tools, in direct contact with suppliers
  • Communicate new products to industrial and production partners on methods of machining used

Technology watch comprising

  • Follow developments in machining techniques
  • Contact suppliers (tools, tools, machines)
  • Study new techniques of assembly and/or design of dials.

Profile

  • Polymechanical CFC or mechanical technician
  • Mastering of CNC machining and programming (fee 3/5 axes, turning: an asset)
  • Knowledge of conventional machining techniques
  • FAO's good knowledge, Master's degree in ProE is an asset
  • Basic knowledge of the dial and manufacturing methods: an asset
  • Dynamic, autonomous and open person, with a team spirit and good communication
  • Proactivity and postivity are also expected to encourage innovation.

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top-class social benefits.

We thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificate and diplomas).
