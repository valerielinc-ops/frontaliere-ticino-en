Full description

In the endoscopy center at USZ Airport “The Circle” we perform gastro-, recto- and colonoscopies and are a center for interventional functional diagnostics. As part of the preventive colonoscopy, we remove polyps, take tissue samples and perform minor procedures.

In our interdisciplinary ambulatory, we look after patients from the fields of gynecology, gastroenterology and radiooncology as well as the Prevention Center. This is rounded off by a modern, future-oriented infrastructure and a unique view of the airport.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.
