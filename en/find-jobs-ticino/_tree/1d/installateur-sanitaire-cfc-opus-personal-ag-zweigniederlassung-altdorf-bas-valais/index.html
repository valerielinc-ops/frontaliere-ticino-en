  • Cut tubes, attach fittings, elbows, assemble, screw, paste, solder
  • Lay, adjust and attach pipes
  • Conduct leak testing, put the installation in operation, control and adjust pressure
  • Isolate pipes against noise, condensation, fire
  • Install all kinds of sanitary appliances (washers, showers, bathtubs, toilets) and machines (water heater, water softener, washing machine, etc.), place the taps and accessories (glass door, shower rod, etc.) and connect
  • Install solar thermal and photovoltaic installations, toilet ventilation systems
  • Final inspection and construction reports
  • Find a breakdown, then troubleshoot
