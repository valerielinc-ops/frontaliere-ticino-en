The Position Manager, Customer Engagement Events - Indianapolis, IN

The Opportunity

As the Manager, Customer Engagement Events , you will serve as the operational leader for Roche Diagnostics’ largest customer engagement portfolio, based out of our Indianapolis, IN headquarters. In this high-impact role, you will provide centralized oversight of the people, facilities, fleet operations, technology, budgets, vendors, and logistics required to successfully plan and execute approximately 400 customer engagement events annually .

You will lead a team of 8 event planners, drive strategic alignment across business areas, optimize program workflows, and deliver best-in-class experiences that amplify company exposure, drive sales leads, and heighten customer loyalty.

Key Roles & Responsibilities

  • Enterprise Program Leadership: Directed end-to-end operational strategy, execution, and continuous optimization across 6 core enterprise programs, including Webinars, Roche on the Road fleet operations, 360[view] Experience, Lab Tours, Specialty Events, and Peer Exchanges.
  • Multi-Campus Operations: Oversee event planner allocation, facility readiness, speaker preparation, and standardized execution across five Roche campuses (Indianapolis, Tucson, Pleasanton, Santa Clara, and Wilmington) to deliver an exceptional customer experience.
  • Showroom & Facility Management: Lead the daily operations, ongoing maintenance, and continuous process improvements for the Indianapolis and Pleasanton 360 Lab showrooms.
  • Fleet Management & Routing: Direct the Roche on the Road fleet operations, managing scheduling, logistics, routing, preventive maintenance, and contingency planning to maximize asset utilization.
  • People Leadership & Coaching: Manage, coach, and mentor a team of 8 event planners, optimizing workflows, resolving complex escalations, and driving professional development.
  • Vendor & Agency Oversight: Direct third-party organizations and agency partnerships, negotiating terms and continuously evaluating vendor performance to ensure high-quality service delivery.
  • Strategic Innovation & ROI Tracking: Launch innovation initiatives by establishing clear KPIs, managing project budgets, and developing ROI models to guarantee measurable business impact and profitability.
  • Cross-Functional Governance & Compliance: Manage departmental operating budgets and event technology platforms, while driving cross-functional alignment across Business Areas in strict compliance with Ethics & Compliance (E&C) standards.

Who You Are

You are a strategic, highly organized operational leader with a passion for delivering exceptional customer experiences and empowering teams within a matrix organization.
