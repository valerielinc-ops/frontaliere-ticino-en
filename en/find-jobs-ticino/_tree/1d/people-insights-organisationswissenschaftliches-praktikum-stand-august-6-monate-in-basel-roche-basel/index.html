The Position Within People & Culture (P&C), People Insights & Technology (PI&T) unleashes exponential people and business impact through data-driven decision-making, actionable insights, and embracing people-centric technology. We are looking for a motivated and curious intern to join our People Insights Area of Practice for a 6 months internship based in Basel, Switzerland, starting August. In this role, you will collaborate closely with Organisational Scientists on applied research and people analytics initiatives. In this role, you will support the team by conducting literature reviews, collecting and analyzing employee data, uncovering actionable insights, and supporting evidence-based decisions that improve employee experience and organizational effectiveness.
