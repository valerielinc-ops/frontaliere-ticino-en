Welcome to Medbase Zweisimmen

Our modern, over 400m2 practice with its own pharmacy is located in the village center of Zweisimmen. The homely village in the Bernese Oberland with direct connection to the hiking and winter sports region Gstaad offers a high leisure and recreational value. Our interdisciplinary team takes care of the basic medical care of the local population as well as the initial care of tourists. Our specialists from the fields of general internal medicine and pediatrics offer family consultation hours and special medical examinations. The MPA team ensures excellent practice organisation. In cooperation with Spital STS AG, Medbase Zweisimmen is actively committed to an integral and forward-looking care concept in the region.
