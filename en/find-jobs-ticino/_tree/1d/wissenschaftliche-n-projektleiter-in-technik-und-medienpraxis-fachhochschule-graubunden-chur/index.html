Full description

As a university, FH Graubünden focuses on dynamic thinking and proactive action. Our more than 2400 students in education and training develop into responsible personalities. In our interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary research, we focus on applied future technologies, development in the Alpine region and entrepreneurial activity. As an employer, we promote equal opportunities and diversity. For our Institute for Multimedia Production (IMP), which trains communications and media specialists and analyzes the converging media world and actively shapes its future, we are looking for an In this role, you will lead and design the module Studio 1B "Community and Society in Digital Transition" in the joint master program "Digital Communication and Creative Media Production" of the University of Applied Sciences Graubünden and the University of Fribourg. They design and teach practice-oriented courses and develop innovative learning formats. You are responsible for the design, conceptual and media technology perspective in close coordination with the social and media science content of the University of Fribourg. They work together with practice partners, accompany students in application-oriented media projects and coordinate internal and external lecturers. In addition, you are responsible for performance certificates, coordinate with other modules and supervise study and master theses. In applied research, you independently develop and lead research projects in the field of media production and also take over service and cooperation projects. They contribute their own research ideas, gain partners and acquire third-party funding. You will preferably work together with the Institute for Digital Communication and Media Innovation (IDCMI) at the University of Fribourg. A regular presence in Chur and teaching activities at other locations are required. | Your profile
