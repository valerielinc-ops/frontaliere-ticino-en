Full description

MISSION The Laboratory Supervisor figure has the technical and organizational responsibility of the daily activities of the laboratory, ensuring the proper conduct of analysis in compliance with the timing, business procedures and GMP standards, with particular attention to the management of microbiological control activities in support of sterile productions. Coordinates and supports the analyst team, ensuring effective resource management and contributing to the maintenance and improvement of quality standards and laboratory performance.

ACTIVITIES AND MAIN RESPONSIBILITY

  • Plan and coordinate the daily activities of the laboratory, assigning priorities and supporting analysts in managing operational issues.
  • Ensure availability and proper management of instruments, reagents and standards, ensuring compliance with their qualifications and deadlines.
  • Oversee compliance with analytical methods, GMP procedures and the correct compilation of laboratory documentation.
  • Coordinate the qualification and validation of analytical methods, as well as analytical transfers and process verification activities (APS), with particular reference to microbiological controls to support sterile manufacturing processes.
  • Collaborate with the Coordinator in the management of deviations, OOS, CAPA and Change Control.
  • Ensure an adequate level of training and staff update on laboratory procedures and methods, including specific requirements related to product management and sterile environments.
  • Promote the continuous improvement of activities and processes, proposing solutions and initiatives aimed at increasing quality and efficiency.
  • Collaborate with other business functions and support the Coordinator in team management, replacing it in case of absence.

TRAINING AND EXPERIENCE REQUEST

  • Degree in scientific disciplines (preferably Biology, Biotechnology, Pharmacy, Chemistry or related).
  • Consolidated experience in microbiological quality control laboratories in pharmaceuticals, matured in contexts characterized by sterile and injectable productions.
  • Experience in the management and coordination of microbiological control activities related to sterile production processes, including controls in classified environments and in aseptic conditions.
  • Good knowledge of GMPs and the requirements ofAnnex 1 for the production of sterile medicines.
  • Experience in the main microbiological analytical techniques, including sterility tests (in white and insulator chamber), bacterial endotoxins, environmental monitoring, microbiological controls on non-sterile products, PCR, immunoenzymatic analysis and sub-visible particle determination.
  • Experience in coordinating technical activities and in managing laboratory resources.

PERSONAL COMPETITION AND FEATURES

  • Coordination, planning and organization of work.
  • Leadership and attitude to support and growth of people.
  • Explain problem solving skills and result orientation.
  • Proactive approach and orientation to continuous improvement.
  • Accuracy, reliability and attention to quality.
  • Good communication and relational skills, with attitude to interfunctional collaboration.
  • Moving between two workplaces: Lamone and Collina D'Oro.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
