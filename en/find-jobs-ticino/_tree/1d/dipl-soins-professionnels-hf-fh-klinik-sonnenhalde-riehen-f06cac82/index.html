Dipl. Care professional HF / FH, 70%

Location: Riehen

From 01.12.2026 or by agreement

Founded in 1900, the Klinik Sonnenhalde in Riehen is now an established private clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland and the South Baden region. We pursue a holistic, human-centered, milieutherapeutic psychiatry that combines excellent professional quality with a Christian value base. The clinic has 68 stationary beds, 4 day clinics and 4 ambulatories. As one of the first clinics in Switzerland, we are actively promoting digital therapy offerings such as blended therapy and virtual reality and opened the first hybrid day clinic in Switzerland in February 2023.

For the team of the open department Chrischonahaus we are looking for 01. December 2026 or by agreement a committed and warm personality.
