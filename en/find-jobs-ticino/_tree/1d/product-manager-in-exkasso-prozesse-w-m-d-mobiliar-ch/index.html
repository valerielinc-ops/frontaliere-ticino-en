Full description

This is what awaits you

As a Product Manager, you are part of the “Contract Management Process” department and the troika of an ART. You are actively involved in the modernization and standardization of the insured/exclusive processes by switching to SAP FS-CD S/4 HANA and focus on the needs of our customers and users.

This is what you do with us

As a product manager, you take on your tasks as part of the Epic Flow and accompany and lead the topics on the levels of portfolio, capability and features. You review portfolio requirements, develop requirements for capabilities and features and are responsible for their refinement process. You will lead the product owner of the agile team FS-CD professionally and accompany the implementation and validation of the features.

As a member of the Troika, you also ensure that the organizational framework for the feature teams is ensured and further developed.

You count on your openness in dealing with people, your sense of optimization potential and your curiosity to break new ground. As a service-oriented person, you operate an active customer and stakeholder management and develop high-quality solutions with a customer focus in cooperation with IT architects, product owners and the ART Troika.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed studies or comparable further education in business administration, business informatics, insurance, product management or a related field
  • Several years of experience in scaled agile product development (e.g. SAFe) as product manager, product owner or in a comparable role
  • Experience in an insurance environment, preferably in-/exclusive collection, dunning and operation processes
  • Leadership experience, ideally also in interdisciplinary team constellations
  • Knowledge of the SAP module FS-CD or another SAP contract account current
  • Solution-oriented, independent and targeted working methods with the ability to maintain an overview in a complex environment with different stakeholder groups and to prioritize requirements in a value-oriented manner
  • Communication strength with strong team and customer orientation as well as strong assertion and decision-making ability
  • Structured, reliable and constructive cooperation with agile teams, stakeholders and management bodies
  • Flowing German and good English skills. French is beneficial.

Working with us

The dedicated and motivated department «Contract Management Process» and an ART Troika in the SAFe environment are waiting for you. We maintain an open, trusting, communicative cooperation, work independently and solution-oriented and meet at eye level.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
