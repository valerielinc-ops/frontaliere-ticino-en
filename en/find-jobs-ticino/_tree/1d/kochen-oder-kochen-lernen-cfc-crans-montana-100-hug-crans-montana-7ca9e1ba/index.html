Company Description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals of Geneva are a benchmark establishment at the national and international level. The Crans-Montana Clinic, nestled in the heart of the Valais Alps, is a HUG hospital site. Its hospital missions consist of caring for patients for rehabilitation in general internal medicine and psychosomatics, multi-purpose post-operative or post-hospitalization rehabilitation in an acute environment.

Job Description

At the Crans-Montana Clinic, the kitchen team prepares around 180 meals every day for patients as well as the institution's staff. It also offers snacks in the cafeteria. Its mission is to guarantee a balanced, varied and tasty diet, meeting Swiss nutritional recommendations and developed in compliance with hygiene and food safety standards. Labeled “Cuisinons notre region” and “Fourchette verte”, the service favors fresh and local products in order to highlight the flavors of the region. As an apprentice cook, you participate in the preparation and creation of dishes intended for a varied clientele, while taking into account the specific diets linked to the hospital environment. The diversity of services and the requirements specific to our healthcare environment allow you to benefit from rich and comprehensive training. You discover all the positions in the kitchen and gradually develop your technical and organizational skills. During your training, you learn food preparation and cooking techniques, hygiene and food safety rules, as well as the basics of organizing and managing a professional kitchen, in accordance with the objectives of your course.

Qualifications

You have successfully completed your compulsory education. You enjoy teamwork and maintain good relationships with others. You are motivated by the field of cooking and want to acquire real professional know-how. Curious and creative, you like to discover new flavors, products and culinary techniques. You demonstrate rigor, organizational skills and a good ability to learn. You have an interest in the hospital environment and are ready to invest in training combining practical work in a company and academic teaching. You are committed to contributing to the well-being of patients by participating in the preparation of quality meals. If you recognize yourself in these qualifications, don’t hesitate to join us!
