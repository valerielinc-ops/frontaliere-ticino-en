Full description

The Cantonal Tax Office is responsible for implementing the provisions of federal/cantonal and municipal taxes. It ensures a complete registration of all taxpayers, professional and customer-oriented tax assessment and a uniform tax reference. The General Department of Natural Persons is responsible for the uniform assessment of income and property taxes and the examination of the refund of the withholding tax of around 330,000 natural persons as well as for the valuation of around 22,000 non-listed companies based in the Canton of St.Gallen.

What to expect

  • You carry out the re-taxation of self-reported or discovered by the tax authority income and assets (without criminal tax proceedings)
  • You make the tax assessment of the previously undeclared values
  • You correspond and negotiate with taxpayers and/or their representatives
  • You are handling objections and continuing appeals to the Administrative Recourse Commission
  • You answer general questions about after-tax procedures

What distinguishes you

  • You have professional experience in tax or escrow
  • Ideally, you have completed a further education in the financial sector or are in training for it (e.g. trustee with federal law). Professional card, SSK course IIA, community school JFS or higher business education
  • You are decisive, resilient and capable of teamwork
  • An independent and careful working method distinguishes you
  • You have a stylish expression in language and writing
  • You have a safe presence

What we offer

  • You can expect a versatile and demanding job with high personal responsibility in a motivating, collegial environment with DU culture.
  • You get excellent opportunities for professional and personal development
  • You benefit from contemporary working models with flexible working hours and the possibility of home office
  • An attractive workplace in the center of the city of St.Gallen awaits you
