Full description

Classical skin medicine, skin cancer prevention and aesthetic dermatology – combined with the room for manoeuvre to help build a specialist area from scratch. In Bern, we are looking for a specialist in dermatology and venerology in the range of 40 – 60% or by agreement. In an established practice clinic for plastic and aesthetic surgery, the targeted expansion of dermatology and hair transplantation awaits you in close interdisciplinary cooperation. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They cover classic skin medicine and skin cancer prevention in their own consultation hours
  • They treat patients in the field of aesthetic dermatology
  • You are expanding the field of dermatology and hair transplantation together with the clinic management
  • You work interdisciplinary with the Head of Plastic and Aesthetic Surgery
  • They cooperate closely with the medical cosmetics of the clinic

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed specialist training in dermatology and venerology, as well as all requirements for vocational training and OKP approval in the Canton of Bern
  • Alternatively for applicants from Germany or Austria: completed specialist training in dermatology and venerology and at least three years of work at a recognised Swiss training institution
  • Flowing knowledge of German in words and writing, other languages beneficial
  • Experience in classical and ideally in aesthetic dermatology

That sets them apart.

  • Professionally competent, innovative personality with pleasure in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Teamwork and an appreciative, patient-centered approach
  • Interested in actively shaping and developing a subject area

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Design leeway in setting up dermatology and hair transplantation in an established practice clinic
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation with plastic and aesthetic surgery
  • Cooperation with practice-owned medical cosmetics
  • Flexible part-time model with individually compatible workload

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
