HPC Systems Software Engineer – OpenCHAMI for Lugano or Zürich

80%-100%, Lugano, fixed-term

The Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS) develops and operates a high-performance computing and data research infrastructure that supports world-class science in Switzerland. Its user laboratory is available to domestic and international researchers in academia, industry, and the business sector. The centre is operated by ETH Zurich and has offices at its data centre in Lugano and in Zurich.

Project background

Project Overview

OpenCHAMI stands for Open Composable Heterogeneous Adaptable Management Infrastructure . It is an open source project that provides modern system management and provisioning tools for High Performance Computing (HPC) clusters and supercomputers. The project with limited project financing and therefore limited contract duration aims to bring cloud-like deployment, automation, and security capabilities to HPC environments.

This project with work location either Lugano or Zürich focuses on the development and advancement of OpenCHAMI for CSCS’s next-generation supercomputing infrastructure. The work will contribute to building an open, scalable, and modern approach to cluster management for large-scale High Performance Computing (HPC) systems.

The main objective is to design, develop, integrate, and deploy OpenCHAMI capabilities tailored to the operational requirements of CSCS supercomputers.

The project will address the challenges of managing increasingly complex HPC and AI systems and contribute to the evolution of next-generation open-source cluster management technologies.

The Opportunity

We offer an initially two-year contract providing the opportunity to work at the forefront of HPC and AI infrastructure at CSCS, contributing directly to the development of technologies that will support future supercomputing systems.

You will have the opportunity to contribute to OpenCHAMI and related open-source initiatives, collaborating with the broader HPC community and contributing to high-impact programs at both the national and international levels, including HPSF, OpenCHAMI, and related initiatives.

This is an opportunity to help shape how future CSCS supercomputers are managed, monitored, and operated, while contributing to open-source technologies with an impact well beyond a single system or institution.
