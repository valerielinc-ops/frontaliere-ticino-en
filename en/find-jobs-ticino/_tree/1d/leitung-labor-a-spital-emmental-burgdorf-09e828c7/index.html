Head laboratory (a) — Spital Emmental

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

For the professional, personnel and organizational management of our committed laboratory team, we are looking for agreement in the context of succession planning following retirement.

Tasks

  • Professional, personnel and organizational leadership of the laboratory team (biomedical analysts: inside, intern: in) at the Burgdorf location, together with your deputy – with an appreciative, communicative and authentically shaped leadership understanding
  • Ensuring smooth and efficient laboratory operation around the clock (24/7) in a polyvalent working environment in the following areas: hematology, clinical chemistry, hemostasis, immunohaematology, microbiology and POCT
  • Guarantee plausibility of all analytical results and evaluation and release of laboratory values
  • Ensure the functioning of the analysers at all times or ensure equivalent processing of samples.
  • Monitoring quality management and promoting positive error culture through prevention-oriented error management
  • Responsible for resource-saving service and deployment planning as well as participation in night, weekend and pikett service as required
  • Organisation of induction, promotion and further training of new employees:inside and intern:inside
  • Further development and optimization of processes, methods and laboratory informatics as well as future-oriented management of equipment
  • Creating an environment in which employees can work motivated, safe and inspired. The successes are then celebrated together.
  • Maintenance of interdisciplinary cooperation with clinics, family practice and assignees

Profile

  • Diploma as dipl. biomedical:r analyst:in HF (BMA HF) or equivalent qualification (for foreign diploma with SRC recognition)
  • Completed higher specialist examination for a federally qualified expert in biomedical analysis and laboratory management or equivalent diploma of leadership and management training
  • Several years of professional experience in a polyvalent hospital laboratory as well as proven management experience, ideally in a 24/7 laboratory operation
  • Readiness for continuous training
  • In-depth expertise in hematology, clinical chemistry, hemostasis and immunohaematology, technical skills in handling modern analytical devices and well-versed IT knowledge (laboratory information system)
  • Independent, initiative and interested person with careful, exact and responsible work
  • Transparent communication style and shows empathy in dealing with employees, patients and interdisciplinary partners
  • Appreciative, motivating and authentic leader who empowers, promotes and gives orientation even in demanding situations, strengthens independence
  • Resilience, networked thinking and loyalty, teamwork and flexibility live
  • Willingness to participate in night, weekend and piketting
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
