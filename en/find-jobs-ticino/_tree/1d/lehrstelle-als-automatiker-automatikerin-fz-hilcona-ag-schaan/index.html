Full description

Technology, machines and clever solutions – your future starts at Hilcona! Are you interested in technology, electronics and automated systems? You want to know how modern production facilities work and how they themselves help to run reliably? Then the apprenticeship as an automatic engineer / in FZ at Hilcona is exactly the right thing for you. During your four-year training, you will learn to build, program, assemble and maintain electrical and electronic equipment. You combine craftsmanship with technical understanding and work on exciting solutions for modern food production. Tasks:

  • You mount, wire and test electrical controls and systems
  • You learn to read electrical schematics and technical drawings
  • You program and test controls and automation systems
  • You perform measurements and functional controls
  • Support for maintenance and repair work on production facilities
  • You search for errors systematically and repair faults as independently as possible
  • You pay attention to safety, quality and hygiene regulations
  • You work with specialists from technology, production and maintenance Requirements:
  • You are interested in technology, electronics and machines
  • You have a good logical and technical understanding
  • You work accurately, focused and carefully
  • You have craftsmanship.
  • You are motivated to learn new things and take responsibility
  • You enjoy working in a team and stay tuned for challenges
  • You enjoy mathematics and science topics
  • You have a good degree from the FL-Realschule / CH-Secondary School
