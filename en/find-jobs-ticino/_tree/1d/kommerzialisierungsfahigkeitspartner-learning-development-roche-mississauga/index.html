The Position

A healthier future drives us to innovate, continuously advance science, and ensure everyone has access to the healthcare they need today and for generations to come. We want to build a world where we all have more time with the people we love. That’s what makes us Roche.

At Roche, we believe it is critically important to ensure our employees have the necessary knowledge and ability to confidently navigate their continuous learning pathways, supporting our priority brands and disease areas.

As a Commercialization Capability Partner, you will use your knowledge of innovative learning solutions and adult learning principles to understand needs of the business, determine and design the appropriate learning solutions and ensure they are delivered in an effective manner so that Roche employees are consistently meeting their objectives.

In your role, you will coach team members, manage complex projects, challenge the status quo, and drive efficiencies. With strong influencing and communication skills, you will develop and implement strategy, leveraging cultural differences as assets, while maintaining integrity and compliance. Also, you will oversee the Product and Disease Knowledge (PDK) framework, ensuring employees receive necessary training, and act as a point of contact for capability development across various functions.

Join Roche and play a crucial role in fostering continuous learning pathways for our employees and shaping a healthier future for everyone.

The Opportunity

  • Ensure the learning journey maps and performance solutions continue to meet identified gaps and challenges.
