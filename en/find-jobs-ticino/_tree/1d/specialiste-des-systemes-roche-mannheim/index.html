The Position

As System Scientist (m/f/d), you coordinate the central aspects of the system core in our development projects in the Design Evaluation and Prototyping department. In this way, you make a direct contribution to the implementation of our vision: Improving the quality of life of patients worldwide. You combine technical requirements analysis with innovative manufacturing processes and ensure that our electrochemical sensor systems meet the highest performance and quality standards. Through your work, you help to develop groundbreaking diagnostics and lay the foundation for a healthier future – together.

Your new team

You will become part of our dedicated team in the field of Design Evaluation and Prototyping. We work at the interface of different disciplines and develop complex diagnostic solutions. As a system scientist, you control multidisciplinary interfaces and work closely with experts from R&D, medical and operations. Trust, open exchange and the will to pursue innovative paths together form the basis of our cooperation.

Your responsibilities

Your mission is to oversee the requirements for the system core, to exploit optimization potential and to advance prototype production on schedule. You will take on the following exciting tasks:

  • You coordinate the design and manufacturing processes of different configurations of electrochemical sensor system modules closely with the specialist areas in order to optimally achieve the project goals.
  • You control the interfaces of all participating departments to balance influences and ensure maximized system performance.
  • You coordinate the timely production of development and prototype samples of electrochemical sensor systems.
  • You are responsible for the GMP-compliant documentation and arrange for the creation of all necessary development-related documents in the regulated environment.
  • You bring Your expertise is actively involved in the development of technically challenging systems and keeps your knowledge up-to-date in a dynamic process environment.

This is what you bring with you

We are looking for a solution-oriented and communication-strong personality who enjoys interdisciplinary cooperation and complex system projects. You will bring the following qualifications and skills:

  • Successfully completed Master's degree (M.Sc.) with reference to medical devices.
  • Sound professional experience in the development of medical devices in a regulated environment.
  • Very good knowledge of design, electrical engineering and programming.
  • Knowledge in electrochemistry as well as in the production of electrochemical sensors, complemented by solid knowledge of biochemistry, are advantageous.
  • Proven experience in configuration management as well as in production and development documentation (especially for clinical studies).
  • Experience in the development of optimization strategies as well as in cooperation at interfaces to R&D, medical, operations and external partners.
  • Fluent English skills in words and writing for secure exchange in international project contexts.
  • Strong social skills, communication skills, negotiating skills and the ability to motivate others.
