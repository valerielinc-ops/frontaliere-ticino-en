Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our approximately 18,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices, or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusting relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general.

We are looking for a technical employee to strengthen our team in production and contribute with craftsmanship and technical understanding to the production of our high-quality flow meters.

What is the role about?

Do you like working with your hands and enjoying technical tasks? Do you value precision and quality? Would you like to produce demanding flowmeters in a dedicated team?

Then you can expect a varied activity in our production, in which you make an important contribution to the reliable production of our products with your craftsmanship and your quality awareness.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • Welding and assembling sophisticated flow meters, ensuring the highest precision and quality.
  • Pick individual parts and ensure an efficient and smooth production process during completion.
  • Maintain and maintain equipment to ensure optimum plant availability.
  • live the lean philosophy and drive the continuous improvement of our processes with your commitment.
  • work flexibly in 2-shift operation and contribute significantly to team performance with your reliability.

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have completed basic training and bring craftsmanship and technical understanding.
  • have ideally gained initial experience in the industrial environment and work precisely and carefully.
  • communicate safely in German, both verbally and in writing.
  • are flexible, like to get involved and appreciate the cooperation in a committed team.
  • act quality-consciously, assume responsibility and keep an overview even with high work cycle.

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We support the work-life balance with flexible working hours.
  • We offer support in sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport.
  • We support family-friendly offers, including childcare places and childcare services during the holidays.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications sent to us by e-mail can unfortunately not be considered for data protection reasons.

Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding.
