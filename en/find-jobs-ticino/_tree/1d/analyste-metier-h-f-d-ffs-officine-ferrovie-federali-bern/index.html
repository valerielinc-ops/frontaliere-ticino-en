Business Analyst (m/f/d) 60-100% | SBB

Move Switzerland with us. Business Analyst (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, immediately or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. You work in an environment that bundles data relating to rail traffic and prepares it for analysis so that punctuality and production processes can be specifically improved. At the interface between the department and IT, you help shape requirements, translate data into usable insights and accompany solutions from the idea to operation. You can expect autonomous teams, close collaboration with various stakeholders and the opportunity to further develop yourself professionally and methodically. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • You carefully model data requirements and participate in the design of suitable data models so that analyzes receive reliable foundations.
  • You define and describe features and user stories so that implementation teams can clearly prioritize and deliver.
  • You provide different target groups with precise documentation and keep content up-to-date and understandable.
  • During implementation, you work closely with the teams involved and support them in realizing the stories.
  • You support functions throughout the entire life cycle from conception to implementation and testing to commissioning.
  • You validate data quality, reporting logic and test results and carry out acceptance tests, and you also support product responsibility with necessary clarifications. This is what you bring with you.
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to clearly present complex issues for business and IT are among your strengths.
  • You use analytical thinking and a structured way of working effectively, even in a dynamic environment.
  • You are interested in technical topics and technical details and you interpret data confidently.
  • You will use basic knowledge of SQL and data modeling in a targeted manner to support requirements and results.
  • You have experience with agile approaches such as Scrum Kanban and SAFe or you are willing to learn this methodology quickly.
  • You have completed a degree in business informatics or computer science or an equivalent qualification and you work independently and as a team player in self-organized teams.
