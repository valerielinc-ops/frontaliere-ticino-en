Medical Secretary/-in Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld

80-100%

We offer a lot of personal responsibility and a wide range of tasks with a focus on teamwork. A modern infrastructure awaits you in a future-oriented and successful service company. On request with parking, very good public transport connection, company-owned daycare and good staff restaurant.

They take over

  • Preparation of office hours, emergency, surgical, exit and insurance reports
  • Preparation of electronic health records
  • Organization and assistance in the surgical consultation
  • General administrative and organisational secretarial tasks
  • Settlement in Tarmed
  • In addition to the surgery secretariat, you also work in the interdisciplinary ambulatorium (reception work) in cooperation with orthopaedics, hand and plastic surgery and anesthesia

They bring with them

  • Several years of professional experience as medical secretary
  • Stylistic German
  • Mastering medical terminology
  • Very good PC knowledge and can deal with various programs such as MC-Office, Kisim, SAP and IBICare and learning more in-house software will not cause you any problems.
  • Reliable, flexible and resilient personality
  • Teamwork, sociability, flexibility and resilience with and have pleasant manners
  • Freight forward and exact work are for you of course us You have a quick understanding.

Spital Thurgau AG introduces itself

The successful company Spital Thurgau AG includes the cantonal hospitals in Frauenfeld and Münsterlingen, the psychiatric services Thurgau in Münsterlingen and Weinfelden and the Klink St. Katharinental for rehabilitation and long-term care in Diessenhofen. We also operate central medical and administrative services and institutes. We offer a comprehensive range of services and constantly invest in high-quality infrastructure. We offer our approximately 4,100 employees attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
