Full description

Do you want to work with customers at eye level and convince them with your expertise? Then you are right with us. What you do:

  • You carry out payroll revisions in all company sizes – from small companies to large companies.
  • You advise our customers competently and comprehensibly about the insured group of people – solution-oriented and at eye level.
  • For social security issues, you are the first point of contact and convince through your reliability and expertise.
  • You become part of a motivated team that attaches great importance to cohesion.
  • You benefit from a structured introduction and a well-founded familiarization that optimally prepares you for your tasks. What you bring:
  • You have completed basic commercial training as well as in-depth experience in the areas of payroll, payroll accounting audits and/or trust – a social security card is a plus.
  • You master German stylishly in word and writing, supplemented by good knowledge of French, and are confident in using modern digital tools as well as MS Office, especially Excel.
  • With your high level of social skills, empathy and communication skills, you enjoy working with people and find your way flexibly in different working environments.
  • Your way of working is independent, structured and responsible. You think analytically, recognize connections quickly and never lose sight of the essentials.
  • You have a category B driving licence.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
