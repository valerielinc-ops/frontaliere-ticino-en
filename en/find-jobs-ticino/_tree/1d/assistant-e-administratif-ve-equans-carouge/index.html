Full description

To strengthen our team in Carouge, we are looking for a motivated, service-oriented personality with an excellent sense of relationship.

In our house, you can move things.

As an administrative assistant, you contribute to the smooth administrative and operational functioning of several sites in French-speaking Switzerland. You are a key interlocutor for internal teams, clients and external partners.

Your missions

  • Provide daily administrative management of several sites
  • Coordinate the exchanges with the various internal and external interlocutors (providers, tenants, clients and operational teams)
  • Follow-up of applications, interventions and benefits
  • Participate in planning and coordinating operational activities
  • Ensure quality and timeliness of delivery
  • Respond to customer requests with professionalism and responsiveness
  • Contributing to continuous improvement of administrative processes

Which is what you do.

  • Business employee CFC or equivalent business training completed
  • Experience confirmed in a corporate administrative support position, ideally in the area of Facility Management
  • Good understanding of the operation of a business and its processes
  • Excellent relational skills and strong customer service
  • Self-reliant, reliable and able to set the right priorities
  • Working method organized, rigorous and oriented solutions
  • Initiative, proactivity, flexibility and dynamism
  • Co-ordination with various stakeholders
  • Good stress resistance and ability to manage periods of high activity
  • Excellent oral and written communication in French (other languages an asset)
  • Want to invest sustainably in a stable and stimulating environment

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
