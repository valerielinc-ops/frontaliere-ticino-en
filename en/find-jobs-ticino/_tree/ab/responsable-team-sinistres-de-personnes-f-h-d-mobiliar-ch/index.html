Full description

What awaits you: In this leadership role, you process claims files and lead a team of managers for our Court Affairs Directorate. What you can achieve with us: Your main task is to independently resolve loss of earnings and accident claims and coach a team through participatory management focused on taking responsibility and autonomy. In collaboration with your team, you manage all claims files in person insurance for brokers in the French-speaking Switzerland. You work very closely with the disaster department of our headquarters in Bern as well as the colleagues of the Court Affairs Department in Zürich. In addition, your interpersonal skills and customer orientation allow you to develop and maintain personal relationships with our brokers and to ensure harmonious collaboration between different departments. What you bring:

  • Training in insurance, as well as the Federal Patent in Social Insurance and/or Private Insurance
  • Confirmed experience in dealing with personal insurance claims
  • Experience in team leadership, or will for driving
  • Personality with a sense of contact, communication and excellent customer orientation
  • French mother tongue with good knowledge of German Working with us: A team of motivated managers awaits you. They work together in a dynamic atmosphere. Your occupancy rate is negotiable from 80% with access to flexible working time models and home office. Below you will learn more about our commitment as an employer. What we offer: Balance of working life - privacy We apply flexible work models and allow 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. We also offer above-average family allowances and a sustainable pension fund. Continuing training and development We offer a wide range of in-house courses and allow you to participate in possible programs abroad. We contribute generously to external continuing training related to work. Benefits We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, half-rate subscription, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.
