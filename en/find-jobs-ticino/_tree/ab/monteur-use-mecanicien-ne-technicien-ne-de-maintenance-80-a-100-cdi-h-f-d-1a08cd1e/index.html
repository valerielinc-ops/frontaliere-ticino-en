Full description

Company description

For one of our customers, a company based in the region of Martigny / Vernayaz specializing in the sale, installation and SAV of garage equipment, we are looking for an assembler/mechanic/ne - technician/maintenance for an immediate entry or to be arranged quickly. The post is proposed on a temporary basis with the possibility of a fixed appointment.

Description of post

  • Delivery, assembly and installation of garage machinery and equipment
  • Putting facilities into service at the customer level
  • Mounting of 2 and 4 column lift bridges, scissor bridges and hydraulic equipment
  • Installation and maintenance of pull-off, balancers and other workshop equipment
  • Diagnosis of breakdowns, troubleshooting and VAS
  • Client interventions throughout the French-speaking Switzerland
  • Preparation and assembly of workshop machines
  • Mechanical, hydraulic and electrical works
  • Control of the proper functioning of installations

Profile sought

  • Training or experience in mechanics, mechatronics, maintenance or mechanical assembly
  • Profile from automotive mechanics, heavy goods vehicles, agricultural machinery or construction machinery welcome
  • Very good mechanical skills and machine assembly
  • Hydraulic knowledge
  • Knowledge of electricity and low voltage 24 V control systems
  • Good technical logic and ability to diagnose problems
  • Independent, resourceful, dynamic and careful person
  • Good physical condition
  • Driving licence B compulsory
  • BE/trailer licence: serious asset
  • Good command of French
  • German, Italian or English: a plus

We offer

  • A varied position between workshop work and client interventions
  • A technical activity with a wide variety of machines
  • Progressive autonomy in interventions
  • A temporary mission with real possibility of fixed commitment
  • A dynamic working environment within a specialised company
  • Immediate entry into office or to be agreed quickly
