Full description

When plants are stationary, are you there - with know-how instead of hectic? ####

With a leading company in the building materials industry, you keep PLCs, control systems and networks running and, with expertise in Allen Bradley and Siemens S7, ensure that complex systems work reliably.

The main tasks ####

  • Ensuring the operational readiness of technical systems
  • Installation troubleshooting and fault diagnosis
  • Commissioning of demanding plants and installations
  • Calibration and maintenance of measuring equipment
  • Maintenance and development of the technical network
  • Regular cooking (day and night)

The requirement profile ####

  • Basic electrical engineering training completed
  • Experience in measurement and control technology
  • Good knowledge of PLC programming and drive technology
  • Expertise in control systems of different manufacturers
  • High personal responsibility and problem-solving ability
