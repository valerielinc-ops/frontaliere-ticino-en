Full description

Lonza is now a world leader in life sciences, operating on three continents. We work in science, but there is no magic formula for how we approach work. Our most important scientific solution is talented employees who collaborate and develop ideas that help other companies to help people. In return, our employees steer their career independently. Because their ideas, big and small, improve the world. And that's the kind of work we want to be part of.

At the Visp site, we are looking for an apprentice laboratory technician in EFZ Chemie. As a laboratory technician EFZ Chemie, you work in laboratories in various fields such as research and development, analytics, synthesis and partly also in biology. Your task as a laboratory technician EFZ Chemie is to carry out laboratory tests. In these trials, you make new products and analyze them. Your tasks also include quality control / analysis of products from the companies. You create a work plan, get the materials and chemicals you need, and decide which appliances are best for work.

Your education The teaching lasts three years. At the beginning of the apprenticeship, you will complete an introductory course in the modern Lonza training laboratory for 5 months. Then you go on rotation in the various laboratories of Lonza. In four internships, each lasting five to seven months, you will work in the various departments such as Research & Development, Synthesis, Analytics and Biology. Vocational education is attended for one and a half days per week.
