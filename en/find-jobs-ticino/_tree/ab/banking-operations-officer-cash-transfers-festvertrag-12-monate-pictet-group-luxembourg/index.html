Full description

Your team

The Pictet Group is one of the world’s leading independent wealth and asset managers. Founded in 1805 and headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland, the Group is represented at 30 offices in financial centres worldwide, and currently employs over 5200 people. Pictet has been present since 1989 in Luxembourg, where it employs over 700 people.

Operations provide secure and efficient processing of Pictet’s banking transactions and investment funds. Our teams ensure all activities follow regulations and manage risks. By developing and maintaining a modern, robust infrastructure, we guarantee reliable, high-quality services for Pictet wealth management, asset management and real estate clients.

We are seeking a dedicated and detail-oriented Transaction Officer - Cash to join our Banking Operations team . The primary responsibility of this role is to process IN/OUT payment transactions efficiently and accurately, ensuring seamless operations across various payment systems and customer categories.

Your role

  • Process, invoice, and validate payment orders across systems such as TARGET, SEPA (SCT/SDD), and cash correspondent networks
  • Handle transactions for all customer categories, including PWM, PAS FRONT, FPS, and PAM PAI, in all currencies and for all BPAG entities (7 Business Units)
  • Manage incoming SWIFT MX and SEPA messages (Pacs.00x & Camt.0xx)
  • Reconcile suspense items and monitor investigation files
  • Ensure accurate accounting of incoming funds via SWIFT, SEPA, TARGET, and BACS for our customers
  • Analyze and process reversals, cheque cashing, and issue requests
  • Perform first-level processing of Fircosoft hits
  • Manage standing orders, including creation, validation, and closure
  • Process SWIFT FreeText eT Broadcast messages
  • Enter, correct, verify, and authorize SWIFT messages in the AMH system
  • Ensure that risk management is controlled at every stage of operations processing, including during the updating of procedures
  • Analyze existing processes to identify productivity gains and apply Agile and Lean working methods

Your profile

Requested experience

  • Minimum Bac +2 or equivalent in Economics
  • At least 3 years of experience in a "Custodian Bank / Custody" department
  • Proficiency in back-office transfer processes
  • Familiarity with IT tools such as Avaloq, AMH, Swift, Fircosoft, Jira, Lotus (suspense tracking), Idoc, AvaloqOrderList Loader, and Luxis
  • Strong knowledge of MS Office software
  • Understanding of SEPA, TARGET, and BACS payment systems
  • Strong interest and ease in using new technologies as well as IT tools (Artificial Intelligence, VBA, Python, Power Query, etc.)

Soft skills

  • Strong analytical skills and a willingness to learn
  • Ability to meet deadlines and work under pressure
  • Team-oriented mindset with empathy and excellent communication skills
  • High level of discretion and confidentiality
  • Spirit of innovation, curiosity, and a taste for process improvement

Language skills

  • Fluent in French
  • Good knowledge of English
