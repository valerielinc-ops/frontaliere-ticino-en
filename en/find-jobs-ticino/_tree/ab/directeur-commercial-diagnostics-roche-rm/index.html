As the Commercial Manager for Roche Diagnostics in Chile, you will serve as a pivotal sales leader, serving as a member of the Affiliate Leadership Team (DLT) and driving the overall commercial strategy across the territory. Reporting to the Country Manager, you will lead a high-performing sales team, maintain key customer relationships and distributor partnerships, define commercial policies, and drive cross-functional synergies with Strategy & Business Innovation (S&BI), Medical, Access, and Customer Experience teams. In this role, you will inspire senior commercial talent, foster a collaborative culture, and achieve long-term sales revenue, expenditure, and market-share targets.

About the opportunity

  • Lead, coordinate, and oversee all sales activities for the Diagnostics division in Chile, ensuring the achievement of top-line revenue targets, sales forecasts, and expenditure budgets.
  • Serve as the primary commercial voice on the Affiliate Leadership Team (DLT), collaborating closely with S&BI, Finance, Medical, Access, and Customer Experience to execute business strategies.
  • Establish strategic, long-term partnerships and operational frameworks with customers and distributors to maximize product penetration and network expansion.
  • Lead and empower a team of senior sales professionals—driving talent management, high-potential development, performance management, and recruitment in alignment with corporate values.
  • Define and execute targeted commercial strategies based on deep insights into current and prospective client needs within the Chilean healthcare ecosystem.
  • Champion digital sales transformation by leading the effective rollout, utilization, and value generation of the corporate CRM tool.
  • Ensure full adherence to corporate policies, local regulations, Good Clinical Practices, and compliance standards across all commercial activities.

Qualification

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Engineering, Healthcare Sciences, or a related field.
  • Proven track record as a seasoned commercial leader in large multinational companies within Diagnostics (highly preferred), Medical Devices, Equipment, or Pharmaceuticals.
  • Strong hands-on field experience with a proven history of managing complex commercial territories, customer and distributor networks, preferably n Chile.
  • Exceptional people leadership skills with demonstrated success in inspiring, developing, and guiding sales teams and managing high-potential talent.
  • Results-driven, highly collaborative mindset with strong strategic vision and business acumen.
  • Full professional fluency in both English and Spanish (mandatory, non-negotiable).

Location & Mobility: Santiago, Chile (On-site / Hybrid model with local travel as required).

Relocation benefits are not available for this position.
