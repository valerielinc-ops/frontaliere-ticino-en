Put to the Point

As an inspector at the Swiss Federal Plant Protection Service (EPSD), you play an important role in preventing the introduction and spread of plant diseases and pests. In this way, you make an important contribution to the protection of agriculture and the environment.

  • Date of entry: 01.02.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21031

You can make this contribution

  • Implementation of phytosanitary controls at import (entry point/consignee) and action in case of violations of the provisions of the phytosanitary legislation
  • Ensuring the obligation to declare and control goods from third countries subject to phytosanitary legislation
  • Answering inquiries from private individuals and advising importers, customs authorities and forwarders on the implementation of plant health legislation
  • Co-responsibility in export certification
  • Implementation of export certification controls and sampling of phytosanitary risks at the border and inland
  • Collaboration in plant health projects (e.g. communication, adaptation of leaflets, etc.)

This makes them unique.

  • Bachelor FH in the field of agronomy, environmental technology or similar
  • Training/phytosanitary and phytosanitary skills
  • Team capable, communicative, with good assertiveness
  • independent working principle
  • Knowledge of two official languages and English

Agriculture and food: help shape!

The Federal Office for Agriculture (BLW) is the federal competence centre for the agricultural sector. Its task is to implement agricultural policy and to prepare for its further development. The BLW includes the agricultural research institute Agroscope, which conducts research in the interest of a productive and sustainable agriculture and food industry.
