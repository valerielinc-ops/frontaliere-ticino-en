Place of work: Hirslanden Salem-Spital | Bern Occupation by: Immediately or by appointment

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Shape the future of our hospital infrastructure.

Your benefits to us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in fitness subscription
  • A responsible and varied activity with a high level of independence
  • Exciting projects in a dynamic hospital environment
  • Modern employment conditions and attractive social benefits

  • Involvement in a committed team

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • Management of construction, conversion, renovation and infrastructure projects across all project phases
  • Representation of the building owner towards planners, entrepreneurs and authorities
  • Planning, coordination and monitoring of costs, deadlines and quality
  • Development of concepts for technical installations and building technology
  • Ensuring trouble-free hospital operation during construction and maintenance measures
  • Coordination of internal stakeholders from medicine, care, technology and administration
  • Cooperation in the conduct of tenders and procurement procedures
  • Support for investment, budget and multiannual planning
  • Participation in energy, sustainability and security projects
  • Documentation and transfer of completed projects to operation

Your profile

  • Basic training in construction main or construction subsidiary with further training as building drawing, construction manager, technician HF or comparable qualification
  • Experience in project management of construction and infrastructure projects
  • Knowledge of building technology (HLKS, electrical, building automation) is an advantage
  • Experience in healthcare or in complex operational environments desirable
  • Strong organisational and coordination skills
  • Negotiation skills and a secure attitude towards internal and external partners
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Good knowledge of common office applications and ideally of project management tools
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

For additional information, Hiltbrunner Werner, Head of Technology and FM, is available at T. We look forward to your contact.
