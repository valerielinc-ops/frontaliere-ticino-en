Operator / Senior Operator 80%-100% (m/f/d)

Location: Visp, Switzerland

This is a fully site-based role.

This is an exciting time to join our team, whether you’re a seasoned professional or just starting your career. We’re seeking motivated and resilient individuals to play a key role in vaccine production—both during the project phase and as we transition to routine operations.

Join us as an Operator – Opportunities for All Experience Levels!

This is a fully site-based role. Working together in person supports close, real-time collaboration and the technical precision needed to manufacture medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What You Will Get

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace.
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance.
  • A variety of benefits dependent on role and location.

The full list of our global benefits can be found here: https://www.lonza.com/careers/benefits

What You Will Do

  • Support project teams in the implementation and startup of innovative vaccine manufacturing processes.
  • Contribute to the qualification, commissioning, and validation of new production areas, systems, and equipment.
  • Operate manufacturing equipment independently, including preparation, execution, monitoring, and cleaning activities in accordance with approved procedures.
  • Perform environmental monitoring, in-process controls (IPC), and sampling within GMP-regulated manufacturing environments.
  • Monitor production processes, identify potential issues, and support troubleshooting and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Ensure GMP-compliant and timely documentation of manufacturing activities using electronic systems and logbooks.
  • Handle, inspect, clean, and store production equipment while maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and compliance.

What We Are Looking For

  • We welcome candidates from all experience levels, including experienced manufacturing professionals and career changers with relevant qualifications.
  • Completed apprenticeship in a pharmaceutical, chemical, biotechnology, or related technical field, or a university degree in a relevant discipline.
  • Previous experience in pharmaceutical or biopharmaceutical manufacturing and GMP-regulated environments is advantageous.
  • Strong safety awareness and commitment to quality and compliance.
  • Ability to work effectively in a team while demonstrating ownership and accountability.
  • Fluent German and/or English communication skills.
  • Flexibility and willingness to work in a 4-shift production environment.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
