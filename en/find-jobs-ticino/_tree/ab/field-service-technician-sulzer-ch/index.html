Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

This is a hands-on role to liaise and support customers with onsite repairs and service, together with the Supervision of field services personnel, ensuring the day-to-day safe and efficient running of the assigned area.

  • Dismantle, Inspection, machining, repair and rebuilding of both Sulzer and non-Sulzer rotating equipment
  • Field service repairs and/or overhauls in both workshop and client locations
  • Supervision of subcontractors, from time to time
  • Liaising with customers to compile job reports and represent Sulzer in all aspects of rotating equipment services.
  • Assist in the production and maintenance of the OSH, Quality and Environment policies and procedures
  • Assist in implementing and maintaining ISO accreditation
  • Interpreting engineering drawings
  • Balancing of rotating Equipment, and Alignment of pumps, motors etc.
  • Machining of components

Education requirements

  • Trade Qualification in Electrical / Mechanical Engineering or related discipline.

Key skills / Experience / Role Requirement

  • 5+ years’ experience with Electrical / Mechanical products.
  • Field Service Experience
  • Strong communication skills.
  • High level skills in time management.
  • Ability to engage and work with others.
  • Travel ~ >50% of time.

Language skills

  • Fluent in written and spoken English is a must.
