Full description

For our customers in the region, we are looking for motivated and reliable roofers for temporary assignments with the possibility of permanent employment.

Your tasks

  • Performing steep and flat roof work
  • Floating of roofs with bricks, slate and other materials
  • Repair, refurbishment and maintenance work
  • Installation of thermal insulation and sub-roofing
  • Waterproofing work on flat roofs
  • Assembly of roof windows and further roof elements
  • General spengler and follow-up work by experience
  • Compliance with safety rules on the construction site
  • Independent work according to plans and specifications

Your profile

  • Completed roofing training EFZ/EBA or several years of professional experience
  • Experience in steep and/or flat roof
  • Craftsmanship and technical understanding
  • Physically resilient and vertigo-free
  • Self-employed, reliable and clean work
  • Good knowledge of German is advantageous
  • Advantageous driving licence Cat B
  • Own vehicle is advantageous

We offer

  • Varied assignments with different customers
  • Contemporary and fair employment conditions
  • Attractive remuneration according to qualification and experience
  • Personal support throughout the operation
  • Long-term application possibilities
  • Possibility of permanent employment
  • Fast and uncomplicated application process
