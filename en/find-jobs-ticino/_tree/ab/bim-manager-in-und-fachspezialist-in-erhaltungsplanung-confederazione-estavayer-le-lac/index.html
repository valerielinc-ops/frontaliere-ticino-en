Full description

Put to the Point

You participate in the development, application and optimization of BIM-based processes in the Estavayer-le-Lac branch by accompanying or leading projects related to conservation planning. They make an important contribution to increasing efficiency in favour of a future-oriented national road infrastructure and to further digitally empower your colleagues.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027 or by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20482
  • Place of work: Place de la Gare 7, 1470 Estavayer-le-Lac

You can make this contribution

  • Support all branch departments involved as BIM experts in the concrete application of data-driven processes
  • Shaping digitalization through the development, establishment and monitoring of digital processes as well as active participation in specialist and working groups
  • Identify, structure and integrate BIM use cases to ensure their long-term anchoring and make the best use of data across the entire lifecycle of construction works
  • Develop a strategy for the long-term safeguarding of data in the area of conservation planning
  • Accompany and support the various project participants with a view to data-driven execution

This makes them unique.

  • university degree (ETH/FH) in the areas of civil engineering or electrical engineering or in a related field of expertise
  • Additional qualifications in the field of construction digitalization are beneficial
  • Proven expertise and computer science in the areas of digitalization, software configuration, openBIM quality testing and data management
  • Team and communication skills
  • Analytical, conceptual and networked thinking as well as the ability to independently open up, organize and coordinate new topics
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and, if possible, passive knowledge of a third official language

Move the Switzerland.

The Federal Office for Roads (ASTRA) is responsible for the construction, maintenance and operation of the national road network and is committed to sustainable and safe mobility on the road. It is part of the Federal Department of Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications (UVEK).

As an employee of ASTRA, you move Switzerland. They take responsibility in challenging projects that advance and connect all regions. ASTRA offers you the opportunity to make a significant contribution to the successful development of Switzerland in a modern working environment.
