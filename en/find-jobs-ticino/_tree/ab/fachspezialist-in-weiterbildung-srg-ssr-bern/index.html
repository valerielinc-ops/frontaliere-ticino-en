They contribute to the further development of our further education offers and contribute to the fact that education and further education in the SRG is effectively, contemporary and qualitatively sound.

  • You will analyse training needs and develop suitable learning and training solutions together with the relevant areas
  • They participate in the conceptual further development of the SRG’s training landscape and develop basic principles and concepts for this purpose.
  • You contribute your expertise to contemporary learning, learning formats, methods and didactic approaches and develop them further
  • They coordinate further education offers and accompany their mapping and administration in our Learning Management System (LMS)

They inspire us.

  • Experience in the conception and implementation of further education offers as well as affinity for digital learning formats
  • Experience in dealing with a Learning Management System (LMS)

  • Very good knowledge of German (mother tongue) and good knowledge of French
