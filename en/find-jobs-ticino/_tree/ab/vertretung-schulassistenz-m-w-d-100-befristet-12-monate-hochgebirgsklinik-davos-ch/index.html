Full description

Tasks: Admission: 1. January 2027 Contract: limited until 31. December 2027 At our cantonally recognised clinic school, children and young people continue to learn about the teaching content of their regular schools during their rehabilitation stay. You help them to keep up with their school, in a small team between education and health.

  • Accompanying lessons: You support the teachers in the classroom and accompany students with very different needs
  • Structuring learning: You help the children and young people to organize their everyday school life and to learn increasingly independently
  • Secure connection: You support the return to regular school and help not to miss the school material
  • Helping to shape offers: You will contribute your own strengths, whether professional, creative or practical, and accompany activities in and outside the clinic
  • Organising: You will support the school management with administrative tasks such as accounting, evaluation and documentation | Requirements:
  • Education: You have completed vocational training; you do not need pedagogical training. Professional experience is a plus
  • Dealing with children: You meet children and young people of all ages with joy, patience and appreciation
  • Way of working: You work in a structured way and adapt to changing health situations of your students
  • Language and IT: You speak good German [level B2/C1] and work confidently with common digital tools
