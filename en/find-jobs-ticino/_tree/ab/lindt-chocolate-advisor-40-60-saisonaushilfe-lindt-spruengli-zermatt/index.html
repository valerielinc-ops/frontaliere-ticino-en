About us

We enchant the world with chocolate! Lindt & Sprüngli (Switzerland) AG is a subsidiary of the global Lindt & Sprüngli Group with headquarters and production facilities in Kilchberg on Lake Zurich. In Altendorf we operate our own logistics centre, while the Lindt Cocoa Center in Olten produces cocoa mass for our own use as well as for other European production facilities. Throughout Switzerland, we proudly operate 15 Lindt shops, including iconic locations such as the Jungfraujoch and the Swiss Transport House in Lucerne. More than 1000 employees from more than 50 nations contribute to our success.

For our Lindt Chocolate Shop in beautiful Zermatt we are looking for a vendor with high flexibility for the winter season from December 2026 to March 2027. Your assignment takes place as required within the framework of a regulated hourly assignment.

What you do with us

  • First-class sales advice for our international customers
  • Merchandising of sales shelves (including freshness checks)
  • Cash register operation
  • Assistance in warehouse management
  • Preparation of accounts

When you fit in with us

  • Completed retail training and/or experience in similar, mainly service-oriented branches, such as catering and hospitality
  • Convincing salesperson with a high level of customer orientation
  • Flowing German and good English skills, other languages beneficial
  • Team-capable, committed and open personality
  • Passion for our premium chocolate and for the attention to detail
  • High flexibility and willingness to take over also on weekends and public holidays

We are all unique!

Health Health is important to us. With a sports bonus, ergonomics advice, healthy nutrition and other health initiatives, we support you to stay fit.

Family-friendly environment - properly insured Our social insurance, as well as maternity leave, goes far beyond the mandatory - for your own protection and for your loved ones.

Flexibility Depending on the function, flexible working hours, home office and part-time work are possible. We also offer you the option to increase the holiday days.

Advantage Numerous perks such as a top staff restaurant, discounts in the Lindt shops or great leisure offers are at your disposal.
