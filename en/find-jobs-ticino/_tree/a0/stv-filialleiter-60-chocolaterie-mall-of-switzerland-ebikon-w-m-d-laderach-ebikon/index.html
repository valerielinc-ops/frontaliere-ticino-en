Full description

The company is based in the canton of Glarus. Founded in 1962, the family-run company employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries, and is now increasingly active in the group. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glarus. In front of the picturesque alpine scenery, we produce our fresh chocolates and develop new, innovative ideas in the teams of the company. The "Retail" division comprises over 250 chocolateries in over 30 countries. Here we offer our chocolate specialities and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Now we write a new chapter: With the reopening of our first outlet in Switzerland we create another place where chocolate lovers can discover the world of Läderach. Do you want to participate in it?
