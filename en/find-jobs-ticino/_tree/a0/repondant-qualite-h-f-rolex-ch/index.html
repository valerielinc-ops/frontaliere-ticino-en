Full description

Introduction

For our Draft Sector, we are looking for a (M/F) Quality Reply whose main mission will be to pilot and coordinate quality problem solving, while implementing a continuous improvement approach aimed at zero faults.

Responsibilities

  • Inform and monitor quality control indicators
  • Be the reference person to perform the experiment plans
  • Train employees in tools and good quality reflexes and convey quality culture
  • Address variances and manage non-compliances
  • Analysis of non-quality costs
  • Contribute to risk analyses, products and processes
  • Take part in defining quality objectives and intermediate validation milestones during the industrialization of a new product
  • Participate in the development of functional and organizational standards

Profile

  • Holder of a diploma in ES technician, HES engineer or equivalent
  • In-depth knowledge of standard quality methods (QQOQCCP, 5 Why, 5M, etc.) and statistical approach (MSP piloting, sampling, etc.)
  • Expertise in problem resolution methodologies (QRQC, 8D) and experience plans
  • Mastery of common office tools
  • Human contact and communication
  • Rigourous, impartial, organized and autonomous
  • Customer orientation
  • Ability to evolve into multidisciplinary and cross-cutting teams
  • Knowledge of feedback culture
  • Focus on quality and sustainability

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
