General Care Nurse - Job Advertisement EOC, the multi-site hospital of Ticino, has facilities throughout the canton, with a total of 1,000 beds. The organisation enables an effective combination of a local approach and an overall vision, ensuring that the population has access to comprehensive hospital services close to home, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of its more than 6,500 employees and their focus on human relationships, the EOC ensures high-quality healthcare by caring for over 41,000 inpatients per year and providing more than 600,000 outpatient consultations. The following position is aimed at candidates who wish to submit an unsolicited application as a General Care Nurse Profile The nursing service at the Cardiocentro Ticino Institute employs around 130 staff members, divided between the cardiology and cardiac and thoracic surgery departments, in a care environment based on interpersonal relationships.

  • Essential requirements Nursing diploma (SUPSI Bachelor’s degree) or
  • Nursing diploma (SSSCI) or
  • Nursing diploma obtained in other Swiss cantons or
  • Degree in nursing (Italy or other countries) recognised by the Swiss Red Cross
  • Aptitude for providing high-quality, patient-oriented care, for interdisciplinary collaboration within the team and for transparent communication
  • Excellent knowledge of spoken and written Italian and, if applicable, a second national language
  • Knowledge of the main IT tools (Office suite) is essential. Notes Applications, including a Curriculum Vitae, cover letter and copies of academic and employment certificates, must be submitted in electronic format only, via the website www.eoc.ch (Work at EOC). Applications from candidates who do not meet the above requirements will not be considered under any circumstances and will therefore be removed from our database. Applications will only be assessed if there is a need for staff. As this is a position for unsolicited applications, no information will be provided by telephone. EOC – our hospital. Interested?
