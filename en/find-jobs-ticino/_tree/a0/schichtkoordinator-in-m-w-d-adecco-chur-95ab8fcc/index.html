Full description

Introduction

For a company in Chur we are looking for a shift coordinator or a shift coordinator for structured operation in the detection and coding centre (ECZ). In this function, you coordinate shift operations, ensure a smooth process and ensure close coordination between the ECZ and the letter centres. The focus is on the efficient organization of volume flows, the targeted use of resources and the support of operational processes in day-to-day business. The working environment is clearly process-oriented and requires a reliable, independent and team-oriented way of working. With your structured approach, you help ensure that the operation in the shifts is stable, transparent and efficient.

Tasks

  • You coordinate and organize the entire operation during your shifts so that all processes remain structured and efficient.
  • You monitor the flow rates and continuously coordinate the use of resources to ensure optimal utilization.
  • You ensure the flow of information in the team through daily briefings and promote a common understanding of the situation.
  • You report operational and personnel characteristics to the team management so that measures can be initiated in a timely manner.
  • You are actively involved in data collection and coding and support further logistical processes in day-to-day business.
  • You coordinate the coordination between the ECZ and the letter centres so that operational interfaces function reliably.

Profile

  • You have very good PC user knowledge and work safely with digital systems in the operational environment.
  • You master keyboard writing in the ten-finger system safely and use it efficiently in everyday work.
  • You bring experience and profound knowledge in the areas of letter and parcel mail as well as in logistics processes.
  • You are motivated to quickly acquire the knowledge about the processes in the ECZ and apply it practically.
  • You have strong organizational skills as well as a structured and independent working method.
  • You communicate clearly, address-oriented and solution-oriented and enjoy working closely together in a team.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German.

Advantages

  • Permanent employment contract with a long-term perspective.
  • Varied activity with different operational tasks in shift operations.
