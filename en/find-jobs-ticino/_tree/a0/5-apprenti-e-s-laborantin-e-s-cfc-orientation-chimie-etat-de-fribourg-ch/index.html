Who are we?

The University of Engineering and Architecture Fribourg (HEIA-FR) is a member of the University of Applied Sciences of Western Switzerland (HES-SO) and offers bilingual training at the Bachelor and Master level.

Why join us?

The HES-SO//FR adopts a recruitment policy for equal opportunities. We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

The three-year training course, offered at HEIA-FR, covers all 2 areas of guidance in chemistry (organic and analytical). It takes place according to the dual mode (work in company and professional courses at EPAI Fribourg). We offer apprentices the opportunity to follow the integrated technical professional maturity and evolve in a framework conducive to vocational training. You will be accompanied and supported by experienced trainers, trade specialists, who will allow you to develop knowledge and skills in line with the needs of the job market or to start a higher education.

Profile desired

  • Compulsory schooling completed (pregymnasial or general class) with very good results in mathematics and languages. The graduation diploma is required to start learning.
  • Interest in chemistry and science
  • Meaning of organization, observation and precision
  • Taste of experimentation and manual skill
  • Mastery of the French or German language with good knowledge of the other language
