About us

We give color to the world! A member of the Chione Group, Sun Chemical is a world leader in printing inks and pigments. Present in 63 countries, more than 22,000 employees work on 250 sites every day to serve many industries, from cosmetics and packaging to agriculture and automobiles. Together, we have an annual turnover of approximately US$8.5 billion and are developing innovative and sustainable solutions with our customers.

For Colors \& Effects \in Monthey, we are currently looking for a Technologist in Chemical and Pharmaceutical Production (m/f/d).

Technologist in Chemical and Pharmaceutical Production (m/f/d)

Your primary responsibility

  • You train, apply and enforce all the existing EHS procedures and directives enabling the safe and environmentally friendly performance of tasks.
  • In a team, you computer-driven complex production processes by applying manufacturing procedures.
  • You detect and analyze possible deviations, then implement corrective actions decided during interdisciplinary team work.
  • You perform tasks such as handling containers, loading and unloading raw materials or presses.
  • You participate in the production support activities: sampling, labelling, cleaning, storage and simple analytical checks.
  • You are contributing to the TPM (Total Productive Maintenance) and continuous process improvement.
  • You work in teams (5 or 7 days a week, 24 hours a day) within the Operation sector.

PROFILE RESEARCH

  • You have a CFC from Technologist in Chemical and Pharmaceutical Production and ideally benefit from a successful experience in the chemical industry.
  • You comply with the EHS guidelines and attach particular importance to safety, health and environmental issues.
  • You are rigorous and professional in the application and monitoring of manufacturing procedures.
  • You appreciate teamwork and actively contribute to constructive collaboration.
  • You have flexibility, motivation and a good team spirit.

Sun Chemical lives in diversity and, with all employees, we are actively committed to equal opportunities. Therefore, we follow the principle that candidates are selected and engaged solely on the basis of their individual performance and competence in relation to the position to be filled.

If you are interested in this position, please send your complete dossier (motivation letter, curriculum vitae, copy of your diplomas, work certificates and certificates of continuing training).
