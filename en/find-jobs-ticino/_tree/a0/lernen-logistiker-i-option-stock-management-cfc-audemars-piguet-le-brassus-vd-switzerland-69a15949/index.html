Full description

Have you ever considered moving in the watch industry and especially in the field of logistics? We offer you the opportunity to integrate an apprenticeship of Logisticien CFC, starting at the beginning of the school year 2027, for a duration of 3 years. In a pleasant and dynamic working environment, you will benefit from:

  • A complete training course allowing you to discover all the activities of the Logistics Department of a renowned Horlogère Manufacture
  • D a practical experience in the reception, control and management of goods
  • Support in learning the processes of storage, distribution and computer management of flows
  • Dto raise awareness of the areas of procurement, security, finance and planning in order to acquire a global vision of the logistics environment
  • Successfully completed secondary school, you have very good academic results and have been able to carry out discovery courses in the field of Logistics
  • You are recognized for your motivation and curiosity and are ready to engage in an exciting and demanding CFC
  • You have perfect command of French and good English skills that you are ready to develop
  • Quiet and patient, you demonstrate a great capacity for concentration and autonomy
  • Finally, you have good visual acuity, excellent dexterity and are careful

What we offer

  • Personalized support throughout your training related to the prescription requirements
  • Opportunities for internships in different departments to broaden your vision of the profession
  • A safe and caring environment conducive to learning
  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • Annual participation in your travels
  • 7 annual weeks of vacation in the first year, then 6 weeks from the second year. To this is also added 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
