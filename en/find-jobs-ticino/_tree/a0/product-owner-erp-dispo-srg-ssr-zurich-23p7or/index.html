Full description

The Operations Department plays a central role in the technological orientation and management of SSR production. The ERP Available team ensures the reliable operation and continuous development of central planning and disposal systems, including VimBiz. As a Product Owner ERP Available, you define and prioritize product developments with the Product Manager. You link the needs of specialized departments to strategic objectives, while ensuring effective and reliable operation with the team.

Your Mission

Within the Operations department, you are engaged as Product Owner in the ERP Available product team on the following topics:

  • Align VimBiz's evolution and operation with the product strategy, business requirements and needs of users
  • Clarify requirements with specialized departments and other stakeholders, then translate them into feasible and prioritized Product Backlog elements
  • Define together and communicate clearly the product vision, objectives and roadmap, in close collaboration with the Product Manager
  • Pilot implementation functionally and coordinate with team and external partners iteration planning, validations, releases and deployments
  • Ensure with the team the effective processing of service requests, incidents, problems and changes, as well as compliance with agreed service levels
  • Coordinate interfaces and dependencies with related systems and teams and continuously improve product and operating processes

Your profile

  • In-depth professional experience in ERP or layout systems and good understanding of resource disposition and planning processes; the knowledge of VimBiz is an asset
  • Experience in prioritizing and piloting products or services and managing Product Backlog
  • Knowledge in managing service requests, incidents, problems and changes, and in coordinating releases and deployments
  • Excellent communication and animation skills and collaborative experience with specialized departments, engineers and external partners
  • Structured and customer-oriented method of work, understanding governance and quality requirements and desire for continuous improvement throughout the product life cycle
  • Very good command of a national language as well as German or French at the B1 level at least; level B1 is an asset

What we offer you

  • Flexibility to contribute to the development of VimBiz and the SSR's central disposal systems
  • A varied function at the interface between computing, layout and production
  • Agile and focused collaboration with specialized departments, Product Manager and interdisciplinary team
  • The possibility of actively contributing to effective disposal processes and the technological development of SSR
