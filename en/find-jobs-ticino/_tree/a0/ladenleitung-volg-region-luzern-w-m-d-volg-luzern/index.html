About us

Volg is the specialist for village shops and small spaces in German-speaking and French-speaking Switzerland. We live and breathe customer proximity – as a close shopping opportunity as well as in personal contact. The employees are the heart of the store and we attach great importance to individuality and personality. That's why our motto is "fresh and friendly".
