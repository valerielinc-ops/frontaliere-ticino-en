Full description

Competitions for the appointment or engagement of officials at the State Administration 2026 April 9 ## Cantonal Administration

  • 58/26 ## Spontaneous applications for summer work at the Cantonal Administration for students residing in the Canton of Ticino or Italian-speaking Grisons The Cantonal Administration offers the possibility of being employed for simple office work during the summer months, as compatible with the availability of the services. This opportunity is aimed at students residing in the Canton of Ticino and Italian-speaking Grisons who:
  • will complete their 4th year of secondary education in June 2026
  • are attending upper secondary schools
  • are attending universities In the application, the applicant will need to specify the chosen summer month (July or August). We inform you that it will not be possible to be employed for periods shorter than one month or spanning both months. The allocation of positions will be in the order of receipt of applications and based on the availability of the services; employment in a service located in the district of residence is not guaranteed. The candidates will be informed about the possibility of doing or not doing the internship by the end of May. ## Expected Salary
  • CHF 30.-- per day for students who have completed secondary education
  • CHF 50.-- per day for upper secondary school students
  • CHF 60.-- per day for university students ## Conditions for presenting applications
  • Applications must be submitted online on the website www.ti.ch/concorsi.
  • Other forms of application will not be considered valid ## Deadline April 24, 2026 ## Further information Can be obtained at the Administrative Management Area, tel. 091 814 46 70 or e-mail dfe-sru.concorsi@ti.ch.
