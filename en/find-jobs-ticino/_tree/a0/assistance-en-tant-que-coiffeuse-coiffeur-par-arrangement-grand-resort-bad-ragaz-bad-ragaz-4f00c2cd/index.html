Full description

Tasks

As a hair stylist, you ensure the professional implementation of the services taking into account the individual guest wishes. This also includes:

  • Support of regular and regular customers (hotel guests) along the entire service process; from consulting to implementation in the areas of cut, color and styling
  • Advising and selling exclusive hair care and styling products
  • Careful cash management and settlement in daily business
  • Support in the reception area and organization and maintenance of scheduling
  • Sovereign and solution-oriented handling of demanding guest feedback

Requirements

You have completed training as a Coiffeuse / Coiffeur EFZ or an equivalent training, in addition you have

  • Experience gained in a demanding, luxurious salon environment
  • Very good knowledge of German as well as good knowledge of English, other languages are advantageous
  • Safe handling of common PC applications and reservation systems
  • Strong passion for high-quality products, excellent styling and a keen sense of aesthetics and trends
  • Independent, structured and organized working methods with high quality standards
  • Respectful and discreet treatment of guests as well as a solution-oriented attitude

We offer

  • Taster evenings and perks at Thermal Spa
  • Free Tickets Tamina Gorge
  • Test sleep after passing the test period
  • Up to 50% discount in restaurants
  • Up to 50% discount in the Fitness Center and Tamina Therme

The Grand Resort Bad Ragaz has its origins in the healing thermal water of the Tamina spring. Since 1242 this place stands for regeneration, new energy and quality of life. On this basis, we continue to develop the resort consistently – with the aim of becoming the leading Wellbeing & Healthy Living Resort in Europe and creating a place of strength for generations.

Around 800 employees shape the experience of our guests every day. Different talents, cultures and personalities work together on one goal: to create unique experiences. What unites us is the common aspiration to make this special place a bit better every day – One Resort – One Team.
