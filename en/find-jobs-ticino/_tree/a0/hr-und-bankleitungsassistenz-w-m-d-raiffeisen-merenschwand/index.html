Full description

05.11.2026 Merenwand

Become part of our team!

We are looking for – probably exactly you – as an HR and bank management assistant. You would become part of a 43-member team, which is divided between the four offices in Auw, Beinwil (Freiamt), Merenschwand and Obfelden. In the advertised position, you work closely with the bank management and assume a central hub function between employees, managers and bank management. Working for us means doing a meaningful job and being able to count on the support of our teammates. We are convinced that together we will achieve more than everyone alone. Therefore, we maintain a good cooperation and also meet outside working hours for a joint lunch or a team trip.

What awaits you?

  • HR administration (payroll, absentee management, personnel insurance as well as drawing up employment contracts and HR documents)
  • Participation in HR processes from personnel planning and recruitment to entry and exit management and employee development
  • Support and advice of managers in personnel matters
  • Support for the bank management in organisational, administrative and coordination tasks
  • Organisation, implementation and pre- and post-processing of meetings, workshops and staff events
  • Settlement of staff bank orders

What do you bring?

  • Commercial apprenticeship completed
  • Banking experience is beneficial
  • Very good computer science user knowledge (MS Office) as well as experience with collaboration tools and document management systems
  • Independent, responsible, discreet and team-oriented personality
  • Strong organisational competence and a structured and reliable working method
  • Quick comprehension and exact working methods
  • Stylistic German in Word and Writing

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We live diversity and equal opportunities

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

What do we offer you?

  • A motivated team with an appreciative and collegial corporate culture
  • Support for your professional and personal development including generous cost sharing
  • Flexible working hours and the possibility for home office
  • Attractive ancillary services such as reduced personnel conditions, Reka and other benefits
  • Free underground parking
  • Additional leave or remuneration for service anniversaries
  • Small attentions with great effect: daily free Znünibrötli and an additional holiday day for your round birthday

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed.
