Full description

Health Centre Fricktal AG | online since : 28.10.2026

Place of work: GZF Rheinfelden, Villa Robersten Rheinfelden

Six operating rooms, two locations, a strong team. Spital Rheinfelden is at the heart of our work: this is where the processes of central sterilization come together and where our surgical organization is vibrant. Work is carried out in a two-shift operation from Monday to Friday (08:00-20:00). On Sundays, we also offer committed employees attractive deployment opportunities. Sounds exciting? Then join the team where precision meets team spirit!

  • You prepare medical devices professionally by cleaning, disinfecting and sterilizing instruments and medical devices – always to the highest quality and hygiene standards
  • You keep an overview, compile operating screens and individual instruments as needed and ensure their timely provision
  • You coordinate orders and repairs, have the stock under control and ensure a smooth process
  • You carry out process controls, document them carefully and maintain the reporting system reliably
  • You ensure compliance with all legal and normative requirements and are actively involved in the continuous quality improvement
  • You have completed vocational training as well as vocational training as a technical:r sterilization assistant:in (level I, ideally level II). First experience in the processing of medical devices You are ideally already with
  • You master the German language safely in word and writing and are open, friendly and communicative
  • You work flexibly, responsibly and with a strong understanding of quality and hygiene. Care and reliability are a matter of course for you
  • You enjoy working in a team and contribute to smooth processes with your structured, reliable working method
