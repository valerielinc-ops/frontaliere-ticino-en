Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • You pick goods without proof using a modern MDE device and thus contribute to a smooth and efficient operation in the warehouse
  • The day-to-day processing of customer orders is your responsibility, whereby you always work reliably and on time.
  • Ensure compliance with all regulations regarding packaging, shipping and labelling to ensure that the goods arrive safely and correctly at the customer
  • Entry and removal activities are also part of your tasks to ensure the order and structure in the warehouse.
  • You are flexible in different departments and support the team where help is needed

Why do you succeed?

  • Ideally, you have completed an apprenticeship as a logistician or can look back on experience in picking
  • You have an SUVA-approved forklift and thus have the necessary qualifications for safe handling of forklift trucks
  • Flexibility, accuracy and a forward-looking way of working are among your strengths and distinguish you in your everyday work
  • You can communicate well in German and communicate safely in the team and with other departments
  • As a team-capable, resilient and physically robust personality, you approach your tasks with commitment and are reliable even in stressful situations.
  • Your assignments take place on Mondays and Tuesdays from about 6:00 to 18:00 – so you have the rest of the week for other plans!

Your Benefits

  • Generous holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop yourself - we promote and challenge talents
  • Central introductory training: You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Free parking: Get ready for parking - with us you can park for free

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
