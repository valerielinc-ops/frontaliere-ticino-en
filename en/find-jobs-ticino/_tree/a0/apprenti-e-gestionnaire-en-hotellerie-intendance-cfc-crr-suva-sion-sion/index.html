Entry into office: from 1 August 2027 In order to complete our teams dedicated to the advanced rehabilitation and vocational reintegration of accident victims in French-speaking Switzerland, the Romance Rehabilitation Clinic (Suva) in Sion is looking for an Apprenticeship Manager in hospitality and stewardship CFC Our expertise We are specialists in the state-of-the-art rehabilitation and professional reintegration of injured people in French-speaking Switzerland. We also offer specific services for the rehabilitation of bone marrow trauma (para and tetraplegia), amputees and patients who have been burned. Your main missions

  • Welcome, advise and respond to guests, clients and patients;
  • Prepare rooms for seminars and serve snacks at events;
  • Know, prepare and serve simple drinks and dishes;
  • In the field of cleaning: knowledge of materials, products, use and maintenance of equipment and machines according to HACCP hygiene standards;
  • Organize and perform laundry tasks;
  • Receive, control and store goods (food, maintenance products, etc.) while keeping an inventory;
  • Carry out administrative work. Profile sought The position is particularly suitable for a dynamic, responsible person who wishes to train in an institution recognized for the quality of his or her services. Our offer Looking for an enriching and well-structured learning space? Join our establishment! In a caring environment, a dedicated trainer will guide you throughout your journey. Every day, you will discover a variety of tasks to develop your skills. In addition to professional experience, take advantage of our sports facilities, swimming pool and subsidized restaurant. Ready to start an exciting career? Apply now! Our benefits
  • Remuneration above market standards;
  • Low-cost meals;
  • Free access to sports facilities and swimming pool;
  • Time dedicated to your studies to suit your manager.
