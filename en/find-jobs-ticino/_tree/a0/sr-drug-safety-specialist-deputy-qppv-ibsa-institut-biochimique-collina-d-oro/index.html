Full description

MISSION

The Deputy QPPV supports the QPPV in the supervision and maintenance of the pharmacovigilance system of IBSA Group companies, ensuring that all pharmacovigilance activities are carried out in accordance with the applicable regulatory requirements, Good Pharmacovigilance Practices (GVP), business procedures and global requirements. In the absence of the QPPV, Deputy QPPV assumes the delegated responsibilities, ensuring the continuity of pharmacovigilance activities, in compliance with current regulations.

ACTIVITIES AND MAIN RESPONSIBILITY

Deputy QPPV role and PV system supervision

  • Supports the QPPV in the maintenance and continuous improvement of the IBSA Group Pharmacovigilance System and in the supervision of pharmacovigilance personnel.
  • It ensures that the pharmacovigilance system complies with European law and applicable local requirements.
  • Collaborates with the preparation, revision and updating of the Pharmacovigilance System Master File (PSMF).
  • Supports in the management of pharmacovigilance agreements.
  • When necessary, carry out QPPV activities to ensure the h24 coverage of the pharmacovigilance system
  • Act as the only contact point for the Competent Authorities, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, in the absence of the QPPV
  • Presidiare quality and performance of the PV system: MedDRA, security database, ICSR and SUSAR notification on Eudravigilance and MHRA portal, XEVMPD.
  • Monitor database validation status and corrective actions

Security, risk management and regulatory activities

  • Continuously monitor safety profiles and new product safety issues in which the Group holds the AIC
  • To preside over safety signs, benefit/risk assessment, risk minimization measures and conditions or obligations associated with AICs
  • Implement and manage Risk Management Plan (RMP) and follow post-authority security studies (PASS)
  • Evaluate and advise actions to take (e.g. product retreats) on safety and quality issues
  • Contribute to regulatory actions in response to new security issues (variations, urgent restrictions, communication to patients and healthcare providers)
  • Ensure complete and timely responses to the demands of the Competing Authorities
  • Working with R&D and Regulatory to review SPC, CCSI and Investigator's Brochure

Quality, training and compliance

  • Ensure scientific validity and compliance of PV documents (e.g. PSUR, RMP) and quality and completeness of data sent to the Authorities
  • Advising the continuing training of internal staff and sales force on PV themes
  • Revision of contractual data exchange agreements with third parties
  • Create and maintain SOP and operating instructions for PV activities
  • It ensures the monitoring and follow-up of CAPA resulting from audits, inspections and compliance activities.
  • Supports in the preparation and management of audits and pharmacovigilance inspections.
  • Participate in quality management activities, including deviation management, CAPA and change control.

TRAINING AND EXPERIENCE REQUEST

  • Degree in scientific disciplines (Farmacia, CTF, Biology, Medicine or equivalent).
  • Specialist training in Pharmacovigilance and European legislation of medicinal products
  • At least 5 years of experience in pharmacovigilance
  • Experience in managing compliance and quality management activities in pharmacovigilance
  • Experience in preparing audits and regulatory inspections
  • Knowledge of case signal processing, management, risk management and aggregate reporting
  • Experience with safety database systems

PERSONAL COMPETITION AND FEATURES

  • Solid knowledge of pharmacovigilance legislation (GVP, EMA requirements and Competent Authorities) and signal and risk management processes
  • Family with MedDRA, security database, Eudravigilance and XEVMPD
  • Experience in the preparation and revision of PV documents (PSUR, RMP, PSMF, Pharmacovigilance Agreements, audit and inspection, signal management)
  • Ability to assess the benefit/risk profile and decision support on safety and quality issues
  • Accuracy, attention to detail and strong compliance orientation
  • Reliability, sense of responsibility and ability to manage urgent situations, even outside standard time, to ensure h24 coverage
  • Excellent communication and collaboration with internal functions (R&D, Regulatory, Quality), third-party partners and Authorities
  • Organizational capacity and coordination of PV staff
  • Italian: fluent.
  • English: fluent (written and spoken)
