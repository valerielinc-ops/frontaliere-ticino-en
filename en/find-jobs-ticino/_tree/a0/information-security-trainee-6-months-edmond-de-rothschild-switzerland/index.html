Full description

Within the Edmond de Rothschild Group, you join the Information Security Team for a two-phase training course. You start with a discovery phase of cybersecurity and IT risk management, where you become familiar with the Group's information system and the basic principles of security. Accompanied by the RSSI team, you are gradually taking on operational tasks: processing and qualification of security deficiencies, validation of applications to open application or web feeds, and exchanges with the first line of defense and IT teams as part of the evolution of the information system. In a second phase, you are trained in developing security concepts for new applications or functional reorganizations, thus contributing to the Group's Information Systems Security governance. You are also conducting a study dedicated to risks and opportunities related to emerging technologies (IA, new AD attack techniques, post-quantum cryptography, etc.), defined with the RSSI team. Throughout the course, you produce monthly activity reports and an internship report showing your achievements and academic interest. MISSIONS

  • Discover the information system of the Edmond de Rothschild Group and identify missing mapping elements in the field of cyber security (inventory, network diagrams, elements of the cyber defence device).
  • Become familiar with the IT risk management elements (identification, analysis, processing) and their application within the framework of the Group.
  • Support the management of computer security anomalies, mainly in the field of data leakage by learning about the use of our DLP (Data Leakage Prevention) tool.
  • Take an active part in the processing of requests addressed to the IRSS team (rights of access, exchange of information to outside or intra-group) with improvements.
  • Perform simple data protection tasks taking into account existing regulations, internal rules and good practices in Information Security.
  • Be able, at the end of the mission, to supervise a member of the team; to produce deliverables expected from a cyber security engineer such as: Analysis of a security questionnaire
  • Participation in a risk analysis
  • Writing a risk note
  • Participation in drafting a directive
  • Participate in technology watch by proposing a model of documentation of identified threats and actions to be undertaken in case of proven threat. PROFILE
  • You are currently in the final year of training at a university/high school in the field of informatics or telecommunications and are looking for an internship to validate your curriculum (Master)
  • Good knowledge of computer networks
  • Good understanding of OSI layers
  • Solid foundations in computer and operating systems administration
  • Understanding access rights management principles is an asset
  • Knowledge of concepts related to cryptography, information systems architecture and artificial intelligence is a more
  • Autonomy, rigor, proactivity, sense of detail and good organization
  • Relationship and team spirit
  • Master's in English and French (written and oral)
  • Excellent editorial capacity
