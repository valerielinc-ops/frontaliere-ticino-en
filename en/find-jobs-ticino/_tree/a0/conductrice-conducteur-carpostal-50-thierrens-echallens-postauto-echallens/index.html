Full description

Do you like personal contact with customers and look for an activity that allows you to travel the roads independently? Do you have the qualifications to drive a bus? So you're the person we're looking for, because with your mastery of driving, every bus ride is becoming a real experience.

Make a difference

  • On our various and varied regional lines, you drive the travellers to their destination every day from Monday to Friday.
  • You welcome customers aboard our postal buses and advise them on any questions regarding prices and schedules.
  • The contact with the people you carry enriches your daily life with exciting encounters and you strive to create positive experiences for our clientele.
  • As a driver, you do much more than move your vehicle on the road. In direct contact with customers, each day brings its share of new and varied situations.

What you bring

  • You have a driving experience in passenger transport and appreciate the flexibility of half-day interventions.
  • For this position, you must have a Class D driver's licence and an up-to-date driver's qualification card.
  • You have good knowledge of the region and your journey from home to the workplace does not exceed 40 minutes.
  • One of your strengths is the reliability and pleasure of being in daily contact with customers.
  • You have good knowledge in French and you're comfortable with digital devices.

This position is proposed under a 50% part-time contract spread over all days of the week, Monday to Friday, either in the morning (05:00 - 10:00) or in the evening (15:00 - 20:00). By submitting your application, you confirm that you have become aware of this specificity.

Please attach your driver's licence (category D) as well as your driver's qualification card (OACP). Only full applications may be considered.
