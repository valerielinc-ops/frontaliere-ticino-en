Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Do you want to bring your optimistic and open way of working with people in special situations? And helping to shape it?

Your tasks

  • You provide assistance in adult protection on behalf of the child and adult protection authority.
  • You advise, support or represent the persons concerned in legal, administrative, caring and financial matters, including income and asset management.
  • You work with internal and external specialists.
  • You produce reports, statements and motions.

Your profile

  • You have completed a degree in social work and have received further professional training, for example with the CAS in mandate management in child and adult protection or are ready to acquire this further education.
  • You have a CAS mandate in child and adult protection or you are ready to acquire this further education.
  • You have professional experience in legal social work and mandate management.
  • You are an empathetic and well-organized personality with a great interest in legal, social and socio-political issues.
  • Good knowledge of social security law as well as administrative and financial issues distinguish you.
