Full description

Introduction

Do you not only want to test quality, but also actively shape it? For our customer, a modern industrial company, we are looking for you as a quality specialist for production. In this role, you ensure that processes run stably, specifications are adhered to and our customers consistently receive top quality - from incoming goods to series release.

Tasks

  • In-process quality assurance in production: implement test plans, carry out random checks and approvals, detect deviations
  • Measuring and testing technology: measuring with slide valve, bracket measuring screw, altimeter
  • Documentation & evaluation: test reports, first sample tests
  • Audit & Standards: Participation in internal audits, maintenance of specifications and work instructions according to ISO 9001
  • Training & Support: Instruct employees in testing methods, strengthen quality awareness in teams
  • CIP/Lean: Sustainable reduction of quality, scrap, rework and downtime together with production

Profile

  • Completed technical training (e.g. polymechanic, production mechanic) or further training in the field of quality/measuring technology
  • Several years of experience in quality assurance in the production environment
  • Safely read in drawings (ISO-GPS) and in dealing with common measuring instruments
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Self-employed and structured working methods

Advantages

  • Design freedom in a role with direct impact on process and product quality
  • Modern measuring and production environment, short decision paths and appreciative culture
  • Opportunities for professional development
  • Pleasant working atmosphere in a motivated team
