Full description

The Wattwil Street District Inspectorate is responsible for the construction and operational maintenance of 132 km of cantonal roads.

What to expect

  • Focus on marking, signalling, operation and construction
  • Maintenance and construction work of roads and smaller structures and drainage
  • Operational maintenance services such as road markings, green maintenance, cleaning, etc.
  • Snow clearance and black ice control
  • Winter service and accidents

What sets it apart

  • Completed apprenticeship as a specialist in business maintenance – field of work service or a completed vocational vocational training in the main building industry or corresponding professional experience in a comparable job
  • Independent, physically resilient personality with an efficient way of working
  • Team-ready, collegial and helpful
  • Cat B, BE (ideally Cat C or CE or the willingness to obtain it)
  • Accessibility of the workshop within 25 minutes
  • Readiness for assignments outside regular working hours

What we offer

  • Attractive employment conditions and good training opportunities
  • A modernly equipped maintenance company
  • Demanding activities in a small, collegial working group
  • Careful familiarisation with the task area
