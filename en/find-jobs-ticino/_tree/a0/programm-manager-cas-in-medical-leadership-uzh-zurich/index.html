Executive Education of the Faculty of Economics

Job opening 01.05.2027 or by arrangement

The Executive Education of the Faculty of Economics of the University of Zurich offers a diverse continuing education portfolio for national and international specialists and executives. It includes certificate programs, customized company programs and executive MBA programs in the areas of business administration, finance, real estate and computer science. We combine scientific foundation with practical relevance, reflection and high quality. With us, you work in an entrepreneurial environment with a lot of room for manoeuvre and personal responsibility. You can expect a varied job, modern and flexible working conditions and the opportunity to participate in innovative projects, contribute your own ideas and actively make a difference. Become part of our dynamic team and shape the future of further education with us!

Tasks

  • Conceptual development: Take responsibility for the continuing education program CAS in Medical Leadership. Develop existing curricula in terms of content and didactics and design new modules and programs together with Professor.
  • Acquisition and support: Actively promote your program and gain new training participants. During the implementation, you accompany and look after the participants professionally.
  • Budget and Resource Planning: Prepare the programme budget and accounting and represent the programmes to various bodies

Profile

  • Completed Bachelor's or Master's degree, preferably in the field of economics.
  • At least five years of professional experience and experience in dealing with customers:inside.
  • Interest and curiosity in academic issues and work in an academic environment, especially with respect to medicine.
  • You enjoy working on new topics and developing creative solutions that you implement independently and in a team.
  • You are flexible, show your own initiative and take responsibility.
  • Very good knowledge of German (C2) in word and writing.
  • You enjoy working in a team and actively engage in collaboration with various stakeholders in order to advance projects together.
  • You perform competently and gainfully, have a solid understanding of the university environment and work precisely and structured.
  • Knowledge of the Swiss training landscape is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of MS Office, AI applications and CRM programs
  • Readiness for about 10 missions per year on Saturday
