Full description

Machinist or manufacturing specialist (m/f) 100% About PIANORM Innenbau AG We are a renowned carpentry and family business with over 50 years of experience in the field of interior design. Our modernly equipped production facility relies on the latest technology to turn your individual wishes into reality. From single pieces of furniture to the complete interior design of your apartment or house in Illnau, Zurich or Winterthur - there are no limits to your ideas.

Your profile

You have completed a vocational apprenticeship as Schreiner EFZ

  • You bring experience, on machine work on the CNC, blank and edge glue
  • You are between 25 and 45 years old
  • You are curious and like to learn
  • You are looking for the challenge
  • You are not afraid of the PC or smartphone

Your tasks

You produce on the horizontal blank, edge glue and on the CNC machine

  • You can independently program, set up and operate modern CNC machining centers
  • You strengthen our team in the production of kitchens, cabinets, bathroom furniture, doors
  • You are responsible for the maintenance and maintenance of the plants (Homag machines)

You can expect the following

  • Personal development and no standstill
  • Responsibility and competences
  • A top infrastructure and fair employment conditions
  • Support for further education in the carpentry industry
  • Working hours, our workweek has 41.5 hours
  • Benefits such as coffee and Friday afternoon free are included
