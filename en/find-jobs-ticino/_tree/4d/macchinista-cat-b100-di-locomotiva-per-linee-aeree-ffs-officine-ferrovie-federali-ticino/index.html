SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. As a train driver: in construction trains in the area of infrastructure, you directly contribute to ensuring that construction and maintenance projects progress safely and that the power supply of the trains remains reliable. You work in an experienced team of catenary, logistics and construction, which supports each other and finds practical solutions. Your everyday life is varied between locomotive guidance, maneuvering and assignments on the construction site. In internal education and training, you develop further and take on additional responsibility step by step. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid for, for example, an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits and more information about the job profile can be found here. You can move that.

  • In this exciting function, you operate the catenary tractor (FZM) to carry out maintenance and renewal work on catenary systems.
  • You transfer the catenary tractor between the operating sites and, if necessary, support construction site traffic with further transport services by rail.
  • You are responsible for challenging train movements up to 100 km/h, preparing the composition for the journey and providing the necessary driving regulations.
  • Working independently is just as much a part of your everyday life as working closely together in a team of two – teamwork and clear agreements are crucial.
  • In the driver's cab as well as on the construction site you bring your craftsmanship and use various tools safely and professionally - Safety is a top priority at all times.

You bring that with you.

  • You like both working in a team and doing tasks independently. You are reliable and characterized by the high flexibility in everyday work.
  • Irregular working hours, including night and weekend work, are part of your daily work. You are physically fit and enjoy working outdoors.
  • You have completed training as a train driver: in VTE category B100 or the Cat. A and have gained practical experience as a train driver:in construction trains.
  • You are characterized by a strong awareness of security and keep calm and overview even in difficult and hectic situations.
  • Flowing knowledge of German (level C1) in word and writing is a prerequisite.
  • You have the driving licence category B.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
