SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. Design the optimal management of our facilities for our customers! Thanks to integral planning and control of lifecycle management in the NAT regions of the SBB infrastructure, optimal and targeted measures over all time horizons (expansion and substance preservation) and for all plant types are determined and commissioned. As a Life Cycle Decision Maker, you ensure the long-term value preservation of the infrastructure as well as compliance with the specifications of civil engineering with regard to safety and availability. Our benefits such as child allowances, free GA and discounted travel abroad - also for your family can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • You are responsible for preserving the substance (including demand planning, prioritizing the measures) of the civil engineering in the region.
  • You order, accompany and track SE preliminary studies and decide on the best variant after involving the technical experts.
  • In cooperation with I-VU-UEW, organizational unit monitoring the maintenance area, you set up the demand planning (including prioritization IR / ER).
  • In close coordination with the network and rayon management, you define the necessary measures, which must be carried out with an overall route/node view at the optimal time.
  • You manage asset-specific risk management, which lists regional asset risks.

You bring that with you.

  • You have good technical knowledge in civil engineering and ideally professional experience in the overall railway system.
  • You have a strong willingness to make decisions.
  • As a generalist, it is easy for you to challenge assessments and recommendations from specialists objectively and credibly.
  • You have a completed university education (uni, ETH, FH), preferably in the construction direction, or have an equivalent railway training with an affinity for technical topics.
  • Good communication skills, assertiveness and organizational talent are also among your strengths.
  • Mother tongue Italian or German and very good knowledge of the other language at least level B2.
