Full description

Fresh high-quality chocolate - that's what Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company today employs over 2500 people from over 80 countries in the entire group, and the trend is upwards. The company's headquarters is located in the canton of Glaris. It's here, in the picturesque setting of the Alps, that we produce our fresh chocolates and continuously develop new and innovative ideas within agile teams. The company's «Retail» division comprises over 250 chocolate shops distributed across 30 countries. It's there that we offer our chocolate specialties and invite our customers to continually rediscover fresh chocolate. Want to take advantage of this too? Then join us.
