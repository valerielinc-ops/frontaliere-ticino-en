Description of employment

The Valais Hospital is looking for a

Contribute to professional skills development through simulation.

Within the Training Centre, you play a key role in the preparation, implementation and smooth running of simulation activities. A true partner of trainers, you put your technical, organisational and logistical skills at the service of a safe and efficient learning environment.

Requirements

Your mission

  • Coordinate and prepare rooms, equipment and infrastructure for simulation activities;
  • programming, setting and piloting simulators, dummies and audiovisual equipment;
  • ensure the proper functioning, maintenance and first-level maintenance of technical, computer and audiovisual equipment;
  • manage the equipment, stocks and consumables required for service activities;
  • support trainers and users in the technical implementation of simulation scenarios;
  • ensure the technical and logistical coordination of services and the reception of participants;
  • participate in continuous improvement, development projects and integration of new simulation technologies.

Your profile:

  • Tertiary training recognized in the field of health, technology or in an area deemed equivalent;
  • professional experience in the field of health;
  • simulation training
  • The DU Santé route Specialist in simulation techniques and teaching is an asset or willingness to follow it;
  • good knowledge of computer, audiovisual and digital tools;
  • ease in the use and learning of various technologies;
  • sense of organization, autonomy and ability to manage multiple activities simultaneously;
  • spirit of initiative, orientation solutions and interest in innovation;
  • excellent relational skills and pleasure in collaborating with professionals from different backgrounds;
  • manual skill, creativity and ability to solve technical problems and develop suitable practical solutions.
