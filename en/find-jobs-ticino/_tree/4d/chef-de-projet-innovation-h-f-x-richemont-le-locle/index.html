WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

Mission

Located in the Vie-Série and Innovation department within the Development Directorate, your main mission will be to contribute actively to the management and coordination of key projects in our roadmap. You will be a central player in our transformation plan, especially on the manufacturing performance initiatives and product security. In close collaboration with internal and external stakeholders, you will coordinate with multiple actors in the advancement of projects.

Your Key Responsibilities

Coordinating and piloting the Innovation projects of the Swiss Jewellery Centre (hereinafter "PJS") – 80% •

  • Piloting affected projects: integration of new production processes...
  • Develop and ensure the smooth running and management of these projects at interfaces.
  • Ensure technology transfer to end users (production, maintenance, development...).
  • Lead the project management process: milestones, deliverables, resources, interfaces, etc.
  • Support, if necessary, strategic partners in the adoption of new technology/process bricks related to our partner cells.
  • Coordinate all actors around projects: production, quality, logistics...

Report and communicate about the projects carried out - 20% •

  • Weekly reporting of completed projects.
  • To lead a multidisciplinary team.
  • Adopt and communicate a broad vision around the project: interfaces with support services, interlocking projects with all stakeholders...
  • Develop projects within the different sharing bodies.
  • Help strengthen the image of the PJS as a pioneer, agile and industrially successful player, both in the House and in industry.

WHAT ARE THE CLES OF YOUR SURVEY IN OUR HOUSE?

With engineering training or an equivalent course, you ideally have a proven experience in managing transversal, technical, R&D or Methods projects, acquired in a mature industrial environment with significant stakes. This experience has enabled you to develop strong leadership and a strong expertise from PMO.

You master project management methodologies and have excellent knowledge of product development processes. You are also comfortable with the office tools (Pack Office) and SAP. Your experience has ideally taught you how to also handle CAD software (CREO) and brought you IoT knowledge.

With a keen sense of organization, rigour and autonomy, you have excellent written and oral communication skills, as well as a sense of diplomacy. Your ability to work in project mode and to unite different actors, combined with your proactivity, your proposal strength and your results orientation, is an essential asset. Finally, your team spirit and relationship ease will allow you to integrate harmoniously and collaborate effectively with all actors, both internal and external.
