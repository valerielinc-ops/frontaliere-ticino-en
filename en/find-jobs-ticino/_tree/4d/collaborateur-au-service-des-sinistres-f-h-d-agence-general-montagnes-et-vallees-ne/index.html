Full description

What awaits you

As a disaster worker, you offer our customers a quick and easy service in case of a disaster.

What you can achieve with us

You support our clients in the processing of claims and will provide them with personalized follow-up throughout the process.

You receive claims for all insurance branches on all channels and are responsible for their processing.

You take immediate action as soon as possible and remain in close contact with all parties concerned.

What you bring

  • Training in the insurance industry
  • Strong professional experience in the area of disaster management
  • Motivated by the idea of providing professional follow-up to our disaster-stricken clients and playing an active role in disaster management
  • Independent in nature with a sense of responsibility and customer orientation
  • Very good knowledge of German, any other language is an asset

Working with us

At the General Agency of Mountains and Valleys NE, you will be welcomed by a motivated and dynamic team. Excellent working conditions, continuing training opportunities and a modern working environment await you. Your activity rate is negotiable from 60% with the possibility of teleworking.

To perform this function, the Insurance Supervision Act provides for the training of AFA insurance intermediary, a non-life profile. If this certificate is not available, we will gladly help you by organizing tailored in-house training.

What we offer

Participation in the outcome

Our success also benefits our employees. Every year we decide on the payment of a contribution to the result and its amount.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We contribute to continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and various discounts on cultural events.

Interested?: Send us your application quickly and easily by email. We look forward to meeting you!

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status.
