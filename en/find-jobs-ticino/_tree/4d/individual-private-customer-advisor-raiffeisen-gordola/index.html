27.05.2026 Gordola

Join our team!

For the Banca Raiffeisen Piano di Magadino, a successful bank oriented to the local clientele, we look for a person with experience, dynamic and motivated, as a consultant to the private individual senior customers. This function will independently manage a customer portfolio of the mortgage industry, will offer tailored financial solutions with guidance to the quality of the service and will be able to establish solid relationships of trust and collaboration.

What's up?

  • Responsibility of management in accordance with the strategy and development of a customer portfolio of the individual private customer segment
  • Personalised and oriented advice to the needs of individual private customers in financing, pension and asset planning
  • Recognition and development of the potential of customers through continuous active advice and expansion of existing customers through targeted acquisition of new customers of the individual private customer segment
  • Professional management of mortgage practices
  • Processing of solutions tailored to the needs of the individual customer
  • Interesting working conditions

What do you offer?

  • Federal diploma of employee/trade branch bank and professional maturity or equivalent specialized training
  • SAQ certification “Consulting to individual customers”
  • Complete and multiannual specialist banking experience in the mortgage industry
  • Experienced, dynamic, good communication skills and strong organizational skills
  • Proactive attitude to quality counselling, flexibility, spirit of initiative and preparation for team work
  • Market knowledge and integration in the Bank's area of activity are preferential title
  • Good knowledge of national languages, particularly spoken and written German
