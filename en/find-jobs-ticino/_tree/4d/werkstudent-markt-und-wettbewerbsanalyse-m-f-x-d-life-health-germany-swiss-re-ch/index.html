Full description

Your entry into the world of life & health reinsurance – with real impact! Do you want to gain insights into an international insurance environment during your studies and actively participate in exciting projects? Then this working student position is exactly the right opportunity for you: With us you not only get to know the life & health business up close, but also contribute directly with your ideas and analyses to deliver real added value to our customers and Swiss Re. About your tasks To strengthen our German market team in the Life & Health sector, we are looking for a working student at the Munich location (m/f/d):

  • Support in the preparation of competitive analyses & market comparisons
  • Cooperation in carrying out and preparing quantitative market studies
  • Creating analyses and presentations for internal and external stakeholders

This is an excellent opportunity to delve deep into the L&H insurance business and gain direct insight into how your contribution supports the work of our customers and our company. About your team You will work in a highly motivated, cross-functional team. Our aim is to work very results-oriented. The positive relationship with our customers and our strong brand are the basis for our success. With our approach to continuous improvement, we aim to provide our customers and internal colleagues with fast, easy and effective ways to conduct business in an environment where risks are understood through an evidence-based approach to work. So that you can get to know your team well and work actively with us, we want a regular presence in the Munich office.

About you

  • You are enrolled in economics or a comparable degree program with a relevant focus. Ideally, first experience in life insurance.
  • You are interested in getting to know the world of insurance and reinsurance.
  • You enjoy working in multidisciplinary teams, do not shy away from challenges and actively contribute your own ideas.
  • You are motivated, willing to learn and work independently, structured and reliable.
  • Analytical thinking and dealing with numbers are among your strengths.
  • You speak reliable German and fluent English.
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office (Excel and PowerPoint) is a prerequisite.
  • Ideally, first knowledge in R or the willingness to build it.

Duration : at least 6 months with the option of renewal, start timely Working hours: 20 hours / week (as a working student) If desired, the activity can be started with an upstream internship (38 hours/week). About Swiss Re The Swiss Re Group is one of the world’s leading providers of reinsurance, insurance and other insurance-based forms of risk transfer with the aim of making the world more resilient. It anticipates and manages risks – from natural disasters to climate change, from ageing populations to cybercrime. The aim of the Swiss Re Group is to help society succeed and make progress by developing new opportunities and solutions for its customers. The Swiss Re Group has its headquarters in Zurich, where it was founded in 1863, and operates through a network of around 80 offices worldwide.
