Full description

Who are we?

Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is a space for innovation and an important driver for economic and cultural life in the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Administration of the department in all areas (providing courses, student files, various examinations, calendar, website, verbatim reporting, personnel files, operating credit and third-party means, etc.)
  • Coordination and organization of the activities of the Faculty and Rectorate in the Department
  • Administrative collaboration with the Institute of Plurilingualism (information, activities etc.)
  • Internal communication (students, department team, other departments, Faculty of Letters and Eduform Faculty, central services)
  • Communication external to the University (future student, institutions, media etc.)

Profile desired

  • SWC or equivalent
  • Experience in administrative and organizational work
  • Autonomy and structured working method
  • Very good planning and organizational capacity
  • Responsibility, flexibility and service orientation
  • Very good mastery of German and French, solid knowledge in English
