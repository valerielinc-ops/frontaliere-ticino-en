Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

An exciting task in a modern clinic with a broad rheumatological-immunological spectrum of diseases. In our clinic for Rheumatology & Immunology at the Biel Hospital Center, we offer comprehensive outpatient care with modern, evidence-based diagnosis and therapy. General rheumatology is a central part of our activities. In addition, we have a wide range of specialized consultation hours, including a PMR and vasculitis fast track clinic (incl. vascular sonography), as well as consultation hours for Raynaud's phenomenon (incl. capillary microscopy), collagenosis and myositides as well as for rare immune diseases. Our clinic is a SIWF-approved category B training centre for rheumatology. In order to strengthen our team, we will search for one or more as of 1.7.2027 or by agreement:

Senior physician / Senior physician rheumatology 60-100 %

You do.

  • Independent outpatient care for patients with the entire spectrum of rheumatological diseases
  • Perform ultrasound examinations
  • Performing diagnostic and therapeutic punctures and infiltrations on the musculoskeletal system
  • Implementation of capillary microscopy (knowledge is provided if not already available)
  • Support of the rheumatological-immunological day clinic
  • Conduct of consulates in emergency and bed stations
  • Supervision and further training of assistant doctors
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with the various disciplines of the hospital center as well as with external assignees
  • Participation in telephone rheumatological-immunological background service

This draws you

  • Specialist title Rheumatology FMH (or shortly before acquisition) or an equivalent foreign specialist qualification with MEBEKO recognition
  • Musculoskeletal sonography (SGUM) - or willingness to acquire it
  • Enjoyment of general rheumatology and deep clinical experience
  • interest in the care of inflammatory systemic diseases; appropriate special knowledge is welcome, but no requirement
  • Very good knowledge of German or French as well as good knowledge of the other language.
  • Engaged, independent and team-oriented personality with initiative and pleasure in shaping our department
  • Motivation to actively participate in the further education and supervision of younger colleagues

They benefit from it.

  • Varied activity in a modern, dynamic clinic
  • Very broad rheumatological and immunological spectrum of diseases, including rare and complex inflammatory systemic diseases
  • Possibility of introducing your own technical priorities and developing them further
  • Modern ultrasound device per office room
  • If interested, it is possible to learn specialized ultrasound diagnostics in rheumatology and immunology (vessels, salivary glands, etc.)
  • A collegial, motivated and experienced team with flat hierarchies and short decision paths
  • Regulated working hours in an outpatient clinic with good compatibility of family and work (fixed working days are possible)
  • Attractive place to work in a modern new building (Maison de la Santé) directly at the train station in Biel with excellent access to public transport
  • Advanced employment conditions according to GAV Bern hospitals, company-owned daycare center
