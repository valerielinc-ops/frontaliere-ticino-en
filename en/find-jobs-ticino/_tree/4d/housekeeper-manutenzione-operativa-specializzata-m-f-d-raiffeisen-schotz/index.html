Become part of our team!

Our Raiffeisenbank Luzerner Landschaft Nordwest is a successful and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Schötz and bank offices in Sursee, Triengen, Dagmersellen, Reiden, Pfaffnau and Altbüron. We have 75 employees, of which 6 are in training. In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an independent, committed and service-oriented personality as a housekeeper / specialist in operating maintenance (m/f/d).

What awaits you?

  • An experienced, collegial and dynamic team
  • Maintenance, cleaning and maintenance work at our banking locations
  • Support of technical installations and building technology
  • Environmental work and winter service
  • Repair and minor renovations

What do you bring?

  • Technical or craft training completed
  • Further education as a housekeeper with eidg. FA or graduation professional / -woman maintenance EFZ beneficial
  • technical understanding and skill
  • independent and careful working methods
  • Teamwork and flexibility
