Move Switzerland with us. Finance Business Partner Group Divisions (m/f/d) Bern / Work Smart, by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. In this exciting and responsible position, as Finance Business Partner (m/f/d), you will actively shape the financial management of several corporate divisions. You will gain insight into the diverse group services and experience daily collaboration with various stakeholders. As a business co-pilot, you work closely with those responsible for business and make a significant contribution to the strategic orientation, financial management and successful implementation of projects. Look forward to an exciting working environment in which your skills will be developed and you will contribute to SBB's sustainable performance. Become part of the Group Division Finance team and move Switzerland with us. You can move that.

  • As a Finance Business Partner (m/f/d), you are the driving force behind the financial management of several divisions in close cooperation with the responsible business managers regarding business issues and decisions. This includes shared responsibility with the business and active participation in the roadmap, projects and risk management.
  • You shape the financial future of the corporate divisions by developing consistent and resilient financial plans for the latest estimate, the budget and the medium-term corporate planning.
  • You play a key role in achieving financial goals by creating a well-founded decision-making basis, performance evaluations and proactive suggestions for measures for management.
  • You will exemplify a sustainable performance management culture by conducting constructive, critical dialogues at eye level with those responsible for the business and optimizing the necessary financial management instruments. This is what you bring with you.
  • You have a confident demeanor in order to be able to introduce and implement finance interests diplomatically but clearly and have good communication skills at various hierarchical levels up to management.
  • Your high solution and ambitious implementation orientation is combined with great flexibility and resilience.
  • You have at least 5 years of controlling/finance or consulting experience, ideally in performance management. Initial management experience and knowledge of SAP-FI/CO are an advantage.
  • We require a university degree in economics with a focus on finance & controlling, business administration, strategy or an equivalent further education.
  • You communicate fluently in German, both verbally and in writing (level C2), a good knowledge of English and another national language are an advantage.

Your salary and your benefits.
