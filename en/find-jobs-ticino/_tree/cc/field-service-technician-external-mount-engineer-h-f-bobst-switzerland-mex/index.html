Field Service & Commissioning Engineer – Automation & Motion Control

Join Our Web-Fed Solutions Team

Within the Services & Performance Business Unit, we offer you the opportunity to join our Web-Fed Solutions External Field Service Pool as a Field Service & Commissioning Engineer – Automation & Motion Control.

This is a full-time permanent position (100%) based at our Mex (VD) site, which serves as our corporate headquarters and production center.

This role is intended for a professional who is passionate about complex industrial environments and capable of working autonomously on highly sophisticated automated production lines. You will be required to travel internationally for 50% to 60% of your working time, often on assignments lasting several consecutive weeks.

  • Commission automated production lines in Switzerland and internationally.
  • Perform retrofit, modernization, and upgrade projects on existing installations.
  • Independently plan and organize on-site commissioning activities.
  • Diagnose and resolve complex technical issues related to automation, motion control, electrical systems, and industrial IT.
  • Program, modify, and optimize automation systems using Siemens TIA Portal and Siemens S7.
  • Commission and configure drive systems and synchronized motion axes.
  • Perform troubleshooting interventions both on customer sites and remotely.
  • Coordinate technical activities with customer teams.
  • Validate equipment performance and contribute to final acceptance testing.
  • Train customer maintenance and production teams.
  • Provide structured reporting to Project Managers and Technical Support teams.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of our products through field feedback and lessons learned.
  • HES Engineering degree, ES Technician diploma, or equivalent qualification in Automation, Electrical Engineering, or Mechatronics.
  • Strong expertise in Siemens TIA Portal and Siemens S7 environments.
  • Good knowledge of drive systems, variable frequency drives, and synchronized motion control.
  • Proven experience in reading electrical schematics, industrial troubleshooting, and commissioning electrical control cabinets.
  • Proficiency with Windows environments and virtualization solutions.
  • Good command of English (minimum B2 level) for technical exchanges and customer training.
  • Strong analytical and root-cause problem-solving skills.
  • Autonomous, organized, and results-oriented.
  • Ability to work on customer sites for several consecutive weeks in an international environment.
  • Ability to effectively engage internal support teams whenever required.
  • Valid Category B driving license.
  • Siemens PLCs (TIA Portal, S7)
  • Motion Control Solutions: Bosch Rexroth, Siemens
  • Drives and Inverters: Siemens Sinamics, Baumüller, Nidec, Schneider Electric, and other suppliers
  • Siemens Industrial PCs
  • Automated production lines typically integrating 30 to 100 synchronized axes
  • The opportunity to join an experienced and highly respected team.
  • Comprehensive training on our products and technologies.
  • Exciting international projects.
  • A highly diverse technical environment combining commissioning, retrofits, troubleshooting, and technical support.
  • A high level of autonomy and meaningful responsibilities.
  • Professional growth and career development opportunities.
  • The chance to work with customers and cultures from around the world.
