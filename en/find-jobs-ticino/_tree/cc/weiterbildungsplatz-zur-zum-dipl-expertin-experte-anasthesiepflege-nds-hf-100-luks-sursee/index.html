Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 August 2027. Tasks: • During their further education You are directly involved in the entire anesthesia process and carry out perioperative care interventions and comprehensive care independently and in the delegation of a specialist anesthesiology. • They look after our patients in a versatile spectrum – from general surgery to orthopaedics and traumatology to gynecology/obstetrics, urology and ENT • Your day-to-day work is characterized by close cooperation with specialists in anesthesiology, which allows you to continuously deepen your specialist knowledge. • Together as a team, you make an important contribution to patient care. With your in-depth expertise, you contribute significantly to safety and quality in anesthesia care Requirements • You have successfully completed your training as a nurse HF (or an SRK-approved diploma) and have already gained at least six months of professional experience in a Swiss acute care hospital • Challenges inspire you: You are ready to complete the extra-occupational post-graduate studies and sustainably expand your professional competence • Your reliability, precision and technical flair combine with a quick comprehension – this makes you the ideal addition to our team • In addition to team spirit, resilience and a quick comprehension, you bring empathy and understanding for people in exceptional situations. We offer: • We offer you a two-year part-time training in anesthesia care: practical in the anesthesia Sursee, combined with well-founded theory at XUND Lucerne • Our competent and motivated vocational trainers and specialists in anesthesiology support you with individual and practical learning support. • You will have the chance to complete insightful internships in various specialist areas, which are in close cooperation with anesthesia care • LUKS covers the majority of the study costs and offers you attractive benefits as the largest employer in Central Switzerland. A state-of-the-art hospital information system that maps the entire patient process digitally and comprehensively supports you around the clock.
