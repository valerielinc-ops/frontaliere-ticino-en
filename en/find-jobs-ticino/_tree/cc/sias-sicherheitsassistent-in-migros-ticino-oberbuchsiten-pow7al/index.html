Full description

At L+S, you take on a versatile, responsible task in the security environment and become part of a committed company. You protect people, goods and infrastructure, recognize risks at an early stage and shape the safety culture. If you take responsibility, act forward-looking and keep a cool head even in demanding situations, you are at the right place with us.

Luogo di lavoro: Lüchinger+Schmid, Halmackerstrasse 351, 4625 Oberbuchsiten

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned. For this position, an application letter or video is desired.

Mansioni

  • You consistently implement applicable laws, regulations and internal security policies and ensure their compliance.
  • You recognize risks, vulnerabilities and unusual situations at an early stage and initiate appropriate measures.
  • You analyse accidents and participate in the development and implementation of prevention and improvement measures.
  • You organize and coordinate the SHE area in your area of responsibility according to the given guidelines.
  • You work closely with internal teams such as maintenance, production, logistics and cleaning, as well as with authorities and external partners.
  • You make an important contribution to the safety of people and property values and support the development of a lived safety culture.

Requirements

  • Ideally, you have training as a fire protection officer: You have experience in the field of safety, ideally in the food industry or in a logistical environment.
  • Higher School of Applied Sciences: You have completed further training as a process specialist with a federal certificate, as a technician HF or a comparable technical or process-oriented training.
  • You communicate safely with employees, visitors, authorities and external partners and have stylish knowledge of German in word and writing (C1).
  • You know operational processes from practice, recognize connections quickly and want to contribute your knowledge specifically to occupational safety and health protection – You do not need previous knowledge in the ASGS area for this.
  • You bring a high sense of responsibility, assertiveness and a solution-oriented mindset.
  • You like to approach people, can explain things in an understandable way and appear safely at all hierarchical levels.
  • Ideally, you will have in-depth knowledge of safety standards, risk prevention and emergency procedures. These are advantageous, but not absolutely necessary.
  • You also act calmly, professionally and decisively in challenging or stressful situations.
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)
  • English (advantageous)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We continuously review applications received, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Discussion with case presentation: Specific expertise is examined on the basis of a case or other assessment instruments. After processing, the result of the leader is presented and then discussed together. Usually, the team is also represented at the presentation. As a rule, the conversations are conducted by the superior, without a representative from the HR. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Reference information: When obtaining references, we ask for impressions, strengths and weaknesses again and have them verified by former supervisors or employees. References can be obtained after the first or second round and are only made with consent. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Final discussion before the contract is issued: Before the contract is issued, we hold a final discussion in order to clarify open questions of both sides and to regulate contractual details. If all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
