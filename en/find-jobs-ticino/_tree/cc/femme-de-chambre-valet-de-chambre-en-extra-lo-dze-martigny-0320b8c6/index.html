Full description

The Quartier LôDzé is looking for an extra Room Woman / Room Valet to strengthen its accommodation team. We look for a conscientious, organized and versatile person, committed to the cleanliness, hygiene and comfort of our customers.

Your missions

  • Cleaning and refurbishing of rooms and sanitary facilities according to hotel standards.
  • Preparation of rooms for new arrivals.
  • Maintenance of common areas: corridors, stairs, lobby, windows, courtyard, cafeteria, public toilets and staff spaces.
  • Cleaning and maintenance of public bath areas, including surfaces, changing rooms and sanitary facilities.
  • Resupply of service trucks and inventory management of laundry and maintenance products.
  • Maintenance of work equipment and reporting of damage or anomalies to the governess.
  • Management of dirty laundry and waste.
  • Compliance with hygiene, disinfection and safety protocols and instructions for use of cleaning products.
  • Response to customer requests and transmission of information to the governess.
  • Application of establishment quality standards and collaboration with other teams.

Profile sought

  • An experience in hotel maintenance is an asset.
  • Organizational sense, rigour and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work independently and as a team.
  • Clean and neat presentation, discretion and respect for the privacy of clients.
  • Versatility, reliability, and positive attitude.
  • Flexibility and availability for various schedules, including weekends and public holidays.

We offer you

  • Reduction for employees in the various outlets
  • 5 weeks of holidays, 6 holidays
  • Salary according to applicable CLC and 13th months.
  • A dynamic and varied working environment.

We will only examine the complete files (CV, cover letter, work certificates, etc.) of the candidates corresponding to the profile sought.
