Full description

With your medical and administrative knowledge, you make an important contribution to pediatric care in the Davos region. You take care of the well-being of our youngest patients and their families. As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home, and Spitex, we are responsible for the basic medical care of the Davos population and guests. Just as important to us is the well-being of our employees. Working with us, you will be part of a small but extremely important operation for the region. We foster a collegial work climate and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities in Davos. ## Tasks

  • Activities in the clinical as well as the administrative area including appointment management
  • Telephone counseling and triangulation in case of medical questions
  • Active participation in the documentation in our patient system
  • Processing and forwarding of internal as well as external mail ## Requirements
  • Medical practice assistant EFZ or administrative training with basic medical knowledge and professional experience of great advantage
  • Patient-oriented thinking with pronounced team spirit
  • Pronounced communication skills as well as flexibility and resilience
  • Very good German and good English skills, further foreign languages of advantage ## What we offer
  • Interesting and responsible task in a small, motivated team
  • Attractive employment conditions in a popular destination
  • Exciting environment with interesting further and continuing education opportunities
  • A high leisure value in the highest city in Europe ## Contact
