Pemsa is a leading national recruitment agency, specialized in building and industrial sectors. Our expertise ensures efficient and qualified support in recruitment and placement of specialized personnel that meets the needs of the Swiss market.

Mansions

  • Autonomous execution of plastering and plastering works in new constructions and renovations, both in indoor and outdoor environments.
  • Installation of partition walls, ceiling suspensions, substructures and plastering of walls and ceilings.
  • Pose of insulating materials and fire systems in accordance with current regulations.
  • Preparation and post-work management as a rule of art of the yards, including the ordering of materials and the management of the warehouse.
  • Collaboration with leaders, architects and other craftsmen for the precise realization of the projects.
  • Respect for safety and quality standards and Swiss building standards.
  • Documentation and control of the progress of the works for the purpose of effective management of the yard.

Profile required

  • Training completed as an AFC plasterer and boarder, solid knowledge in the field of new constructions and renovations.
  • Multiannual professional experience in Switzerland as a plasterer and wall mount in plasterboard, ideally with certification of completed projects.
  • German mother tongue or equivalent language skills to ensure smooth communication on site.
  • Valid driving license of category B and availability to ensure mobility on different yards.
  • Good insight into the application of rational working methods and modern dry construction technologies.
  • Ability to work on team, reliability and an approach to self-employed and precise work.
  • Good physical resistance and flexibility regarding work assignments, including shipyards throughout Switzerland.
