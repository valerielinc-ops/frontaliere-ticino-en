Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in vocational training, it promotes the development of young talent, with every year more than 120 learners trained in our departments. For its General Agency of the Broye, Vaudoise is looking for an apprentice engaged and involved as of 1 August 2027.

Are you looking for an employer that offers you the benefits of a large company while offering you a rewarding and motivating work environment in which you can actively participate in the development of the company?

Your profile meets the following criteria

  • A good certificate of completion of secondary education (pregymnasial or general level 2 route)
  • Very good knowledge of French (oral and written)
  • Motivation and determination to know the insurance field and to succeed in your apprenticeship
  • Enthusiastic and curious personality
  • Ability to communicate and sense of the organization We offer you:
  • A healthy working environment in a certified mutualist company "Give a place to work"
  • A varied and exciting professional apprenticeship in the field of insurance
  • Opportunities for commitment at the end of your training Interested?

If so, please send us your complete package including your cover letter, CV, last three school reports and any certificates of internship. We look forward to receiving your application.
