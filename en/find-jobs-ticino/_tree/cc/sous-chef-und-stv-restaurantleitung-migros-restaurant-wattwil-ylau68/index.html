Full description

The gastronomy of the cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland is diverse, modern and dynamic. Whether Migros restaurant, Migros take away, shared gastronomy or catering services – we offer every customer a tasteful experience.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Restaurant, Migros-Restaurant Wattwil, Bahnhofstrasse 19, 9630 Wattwil

Important information

Flat hierarchies, great team spirit and a free parking space await you with us.

Mansioni

  • You take responsibility for the smooth running of the kitchen and provide an environment in which our guests and the team feel equally appreciated
  • During the holidays and absence hours of the restaurant management you bear the overall responsibility
  • You are actively involved and responsible for the presentation and staging of meals in the buffets
  • You actively serve and advise guests on food choices in the buffet area and at the checkout to ensure perfect service.
  • You support the business management of the company, optimize operational processes and consistently implement the hygiene guidelines according to food safety
  • You take on general administrative tasks such as ordering, personnel planning, HACCP responsibility

Requirements

  • 2 to 3 years of professional experience: in gastronomy
  • Completed basic or further education: in gastronomy (cook*cook)
  • You love being in touch with people and enjoy getting our guests excited
  • Fresh foods are just your thing – you know how to put them perfectly on stage
  • You are a team player, flexible and tackle everywhere. It gives you pleasure in being able to contribute to your success in a multicultural team
  • Independent and accurate work is a matter of course for you
  • You want to develop and learn new things
  • First leadership experience is beneficial
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Company parking: Free parking directly at the workplace
  • Working hours without room hour: 41h week and regular working hours without room hour
  • L-GAV: Attractive Land collective employment contract
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers and events

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed.
