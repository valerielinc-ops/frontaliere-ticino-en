Full description

We are a Spitex organization with 24 employees. Our catchment area comprises 6 municipalities with around 14,000 inhabitants. To strengthen our team, we are looking for an agreement FAGE, 20 - 80% m/f/d They care for and support sick, disabled and elderly people and enable them to remain in their usual environment.
