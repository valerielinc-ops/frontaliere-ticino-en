Full description

Forst Goms is a dedicated, modern forestry company with a wide range of tasks and services. In addition to forest conservation, we produce wood pellets on behalf of the company Valais Pellets and distribute them throughout Valais.

In your new challenge:

  • you are our contact point for written and telephone customer inquiries and orders
  • are you responsible for order management and project accounting
  • design our various information channels
  • Do general administrative and organizational work

You are distinguished by:

  • Commercial education
  • Secure communication in German and French
  • an open-minded, team-capable and sociable personality
  • A flexible, independent work execution
  • Safe use of digital aids

Further education opportunities are standard with us. Home office is also partially possible.

Do you feel addressed?

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed.
