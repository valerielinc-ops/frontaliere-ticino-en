Full description

Are you a heavy-duty vehicle professional and ready to take responsibility? As a truck mechatronics specialist and deputy manager of the company garage, you carry out repairs and maintenance yourself, coordinate the work of the mechanic team and ensure their deputy in the absence of the management of the company garage. Show us your skills – apply now and get started!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operating garage Schönbühl, Induestriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Mansioni

  • Carry out repairs on heavy duty vehicles
  • Deputy manager of the company garage in absence
  • Coordination of vehicle repairs with external service providers
  • Instruction and control of accident prevention according to SUVA regulations
  • Creating workshop and MFC planning
  • Organisation and training of learners

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in mechanics, vehicle electrics, pneumatics and hydraulics
  • Completed basic or advanced training: as a truck mechanic, ideally with advanced training as a diagnostician
  • Several years of experience in the fields of mechanics, vehicle electrical, pneumatic and hydraulics
  • Readiness for administrative and managerial tasks
  • Resilient, independent, reliable and proactive
  • Knowledge of the application of MS Office programs
  • German (flowing)
