Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The Finance and Operations Directorate is looking for its technical service: 2 Maintenance Agents, Profile 1: Locker and Profile 2: Electricity The mission of the Technical Infrastructure Department is to manage the maintenance and maintenance of the company's complex technical facilities.
