We at pfister pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today - will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Ensuring an attractive product range appearance at the POS through an inspiring product presentation
  • Competent and service-oriented customer advice
  • Proper processing of customer orders
  • Assisting in arranging, decorating and marking products
  • Goods management and filling
  • Presentation of our range taking into account the needs of our customers
  • If necessary, support at the cash register
  • If necessary, support and assignments in our branch in Basel
  • Experience in sales and customer advice
  • Enjoy regular customer contact and professional and comprehensive advice
  • Flair for design and beautiful living
  • Communicative, convincing and friendly personality
  • Completed vocational training as a seamstress, seamstress or in the field of retail trade
  • Experience in industries with a strong customer orientation
