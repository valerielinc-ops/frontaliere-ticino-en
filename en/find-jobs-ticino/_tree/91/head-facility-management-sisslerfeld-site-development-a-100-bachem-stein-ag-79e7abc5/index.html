Full description

The Bachem Group is investing in a new planned site in Sisslerfeld in Northwestern Switzerland. This step is part of the long-term strategy to expand production capacity by the end of the decade. The new production site will play a central role in achieving these goals. The design phase of the project offers you unique opportunities to shape the future organization.

To drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team we are looking for a dedicated Lead Facility Management – Sisslerfeld Site Development (a) 100%

Your tasks

  • Define and implement a scalable FM operating model, including organization, governance, responsibilities, sourcing strategy, standards and service delivery model
  • Represent FM during design, construction, commissioning and handover, ensuring maintainability, operability, accessibility, cleanability and lifecycle requirements are embedded in the facility design
  • Establish the required FM processes and systems, including CMMS, asset structures, maintenance workflows, service management, KPIs and reporting
  • Establish and manage hard and soft FM services and external service level agreements, including building maintenance, cleaning, gowning, pest control, security, reception, workplace services, catering and grounds maintenance
  • Develop the sourcing strategy and manage tendering, contracts, SLAs, service providers, OPEX planning and performance improvement
  • Build and develop the FM organization in line with site ramp-up, including recruitment, capabilities, training and effective interfaces with CAPEX Engineering, Site Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality and EHS

Your profile

  • Master Degree in Engineering, Facility Management, Building Services Engineering or a comparable technical discipline
  • Strong experience in Facility Management, Site Services, Engineering or Operations, preferably in pharmaceutical, chemical or another regulated industry
  • Sound knowledge of Hard & Soft FM, CMMS/asset management, maintenance concepts and supplier management
  • Experience with outsourced services, tendering, contracts, SLAs/KPIs and OPEX management
  • Strong leadership skills with experience in building or developing teams and organizations
  • Fluent English
  • German is an advantage
  • Strong stakeholder and interface management skills in a complex project and operational environment
  • Good understanding of GMP, EHS and compliance requirements

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing work environment with internal opportunities with internal development opportunities and continuing education
  • Importance on a healthy work-life balance. That’s why we offer flexible work schedules with the option to work from home and the opportunity to take additional vacation days in exchange for a reduced workload
  • Additional days off (bridge days), which are determined each fall by Bachem
  • On-site daycare center and staff restaurant at the Bachem Campus in Bubendorf
  • 60% coverage of pension fund contributions by Bachem, as well as option for extra-mandatory retirement savings through our optional Pension Plan Plus
  • Access to benefits platforms offering discounts from external partners
  • A wide range of free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
