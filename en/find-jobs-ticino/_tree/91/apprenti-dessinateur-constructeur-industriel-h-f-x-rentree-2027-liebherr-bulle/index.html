Full description

Do you want to find out how an engine works? The world of machines awakens your curiosity? Do you want to integrate a dynamic team of apprentices into a modern and state-of-the-art environment? Liebherr Machines Bulle SA has trained apprentices since 1981 and every year about 20 young people get their CFC after 3 or 4 years of apprenticeship in our company. Duration of apprenticeship: 4 years – from 1 August 2027 to 31 July 2031 Your missions Behind every manufactured piece is first an idea, then a drawing. During your four years of learning:

  • You study and design mechanical systems of various tools and machines
  • You establish and model in 2D/3D, on computer, technical documentation, such as parts manufacturing drawings, assembly and maintenance drawings and their parts lists
  • You work on projects within the various technical offices (Hydraulics, Diesel Engines and Gabarits), in collaboration with the managers of the production workshops
  • You have direct contacts with production and manufacturing and carry out different courses Your profile
  • You have completed your compulsory education and you have good skills in mathematics, computer science and technical drawing.
  • You're interested in mechanics, technique and design.
  • You enjoy working on computer and want to learn how to master design and technical drawing software
  • You have a good spatial representation capacity.
  • You show ease of contact and a good spirit of collaboration
  • You work with care, accuracy and accuracy
  • You have a logical, methodical and curious mind Our offer At Liebherr Machines Bulle, you will benefit from a complete and varied training, which combines technical design and discovery of the terrain. Thanks to our system of internships in different sectors, you will discover several activities and professions while gradually developing your skills. Your qualified trainers will accompany you throughout your career to help master the design tools, develop your technical understanding, gain autonomy and gradually gain confidence in your profession. For the re-entry of 2027, we are looking for several apprentices and it is with pleasure that we await your online application. So that we can take into account your application, think about attaching your CV, your cover letter, copies of your academic results and other degrees. In order to ensure a fair application procedure, we do not accept applications by mail or e-mail. Have we awoken your interest? There we look forward to receiving your online application. If you have any questions, please contact Lisa SCHNEIDER. One Passion. Many Opportunities. The company Liebherr Machines Bulle SA is the competence centre for combustion engines (diesel and gas) and hydraulic components. The company develops and manufactures diversified products used in civil engineering, mining, agriculture, decentralized energy, mechanical and industrial engineering and aviation.
