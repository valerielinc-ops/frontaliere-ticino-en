We are committed to health – because health is everything. We are committed to this, also as an employer. We are convinced: Only in a healthy working world do the best talents feel comfortable and move forward: professionally and personally. For that we give everything. This is how you shape health

  • You check controlling lists, identify inconsistencies and take appropriate measures to ensure high quality.

You handle cancellations, complex customer requests, and outstanding balances and refunds.

  • You ensure the correct booking of incoming payments of the service providers and support in the processing of reminders and open items.
  • You complete and check documents and invoices in the Syrius system and are responsible for completing and indexing electronic documents.
  • You support work preparation (AVOR) and handle triage and routing of business cases within the E-claim workflow.

  • You work in release and test management and support employees in system engineering issues.
  • You maintain the telephone exchange with social and private insurance institutions, service providers and authorities.

  • You have good expertise in insurance products and services.
  • You convince with your pronounced customer orientation and understand how to recognize individual needs and offer suitable solutions.
  • You communicate safely, understandably and empathetically, even in complex situations.
  • You keep an overview even with a high volume of work, set the right priorities and organize yourself independently.
  • You bring good and experienced MS Office skills as well as a strong technical affinity.
  • You enjoy analysing complex questions, finding solutions and actively developing processes.
  • You convince through your own initiative, commitment and a high willingness to learn.
  • You speak fluent English

Why SWICA

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed.
