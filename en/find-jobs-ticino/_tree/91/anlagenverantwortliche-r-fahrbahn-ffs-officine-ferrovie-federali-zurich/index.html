Plant responsible:r Roadway 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Plant responsible:r Roadway Zurich / Work Smart, from now on or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you can drive major changes and thus contribute to the digitalization of SBB. The Infrastructure Availability & Maintenance department is responsible for ensuring that the track systems are in impeccable condition. For our team in mobile testing, you develop solutions for current challenges and implement them. Our benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for your family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • In your role, you are responsible for the plant safety and the optimal availability of the road systems and, as the plant manager, ensure the ownership interests vis-à-vis internal and external bodies of the road systems assigned to you.
  • You will make detailed analyses, assessments and evaluations of the results from the diagnostic vehicle data and the local monitoring and measurements, taking into account the risks.
  • As plant manager: r you define the necessary maintenance measures and are responsible for the detailed commissioning to the service providers.
  • You trigger immediate safety and quality measures and provide on-call service.
  • In addition, you ensure the defect-free transfer of new plants into your own plant portfolio and the correct tracking of the plant data.
  • With in-depth root cause analyses after faults, you provide the plant managers with the necessary information for continuous improvement of quality (processes, instruction, technology).

You bring that with you.

  • Your strengths include independence, reliability and commitment.
  • With your sense of responsibility, your organizational skills and your social skills, you convince your stakeholders.
  • Ideally, you will bring several years of experience in the field of track construction, as foreman:in or polisher:in. With modern work equipment and the common IT programs such as MS Office product range and SAP etc. you are adept.
  • You will bring a completed further education in the craft sector as well as potential and willingness to train yourself as a foreman/polisher.
  • In order to be able to carry out the activity as an investment manager:r, you need a valid driving license category B.
