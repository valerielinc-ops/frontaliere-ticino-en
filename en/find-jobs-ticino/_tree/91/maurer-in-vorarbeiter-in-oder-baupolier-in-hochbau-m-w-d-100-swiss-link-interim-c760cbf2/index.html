Full description

Our customer is a construction company with around 20 employees and a very personal, family team spirit. Mainly smaller new buildings such as single-family houses and conversions are realized – all works incl. Civil engineering is carried out with its own staff, without accordants.

Their tasks

  • Independent management of building construction sites (new constructions and conversions)
  • Leadership, classification and guidance of a team of 3 to 4 employees
  • Active participation in all masonry and civil engineering work
  • Coordination with construction management and other stakeholders
  • Management of working papers and reports

Your profile

  • Completed training as a bricklayer or construction practitioner/EBA
  • Advantageous training for the foreman or building polisher
  • Construction site and management experience in Switzerland
  • Good physical condition and resilience for work on the construction site
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing (working papers)
  • Cat B driving licence mandatory

We offer

  • Varied projects from A to Z without accordants
  • Lots of responsibility and independence in site management
  • Very personal and family working environment
  • Possibility of permanent employment after 3 months of temporary employment
