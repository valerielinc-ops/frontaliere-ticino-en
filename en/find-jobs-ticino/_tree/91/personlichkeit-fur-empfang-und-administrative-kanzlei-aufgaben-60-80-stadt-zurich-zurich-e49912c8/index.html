Full description

For our main location in Zurich Wiedikon we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by arrangement temporarily until the end of 2027 with option of continued employment a dedicated and service-oriented personality for the reception and the law firm administration.

  • They welcome our external visitors warmly and are the first contact person professionally and competently at their side.
  • They receive incoming telephone and written inquiries, answer them independently or forward them to the competent body.
  • You take on a wide range of administrative tasks, work with the Swiss Post team and are responsible for office materials management for SRZ.
  • They actively support the establishment of the new service center SRZ.
  • You supervise a learning person in the first year of apprenticeship according to the new KV-BiVO.
  • Completed basic commercial education EFZ or a comparable training (e.g. retail specialist EFZ, specialisation in postal or paperwork)
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable function
  • Excellent manners, a well-kept and sympathetic appearance as well as a high degree of reliability and discretion
  • Vocational trainer card or the willingness to acquire it if necessary, as well as great pleasure in accompanying learners and in topics of vocational training
  • Sound MS Office skills, oral foreign language skills and strong organizational skills
  • Responsible and versatile work with exciting insights into Switzerland’s largest civilian rescue organization
  • Motivated, collaborative team and an appreciative working environment
  • Attractive employment conditions, a healthy pension fund and a centrally located workplace directly at Zurich Wiedikon station

Protection & Rescue Zurich (SRZ), as a service department of the Department of Security, unites the professional and militia fire brigades, the medical department and the operational control center (ELZ) for the emergency calls 144 and 118. In addition, civil protection, fire police and a training center with an affiliated Higher School for Rescue Occupations (HFRB) belong to the largest civil rescue organization in Switzerland. Intervention and prevention units protect and save people, animals, property and the environment around the clock in the city of Zurich, at Zurich Airport and in agglomeration.
