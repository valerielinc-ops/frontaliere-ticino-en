To expand our dedicated team, we are looking for a motivated physiotherapist by agreement.

  • Completed training as a dipl. Physical therapist (B.Sc. or equivalent, foreign degree with SRC recognition)

  • Manual therapy

  • Trigger point therapy
  • Dry needling
  • Sports physiotherapy

  • Care for fitness and MTT customers
  • Close cooperation with professionals from other medical disciplines
