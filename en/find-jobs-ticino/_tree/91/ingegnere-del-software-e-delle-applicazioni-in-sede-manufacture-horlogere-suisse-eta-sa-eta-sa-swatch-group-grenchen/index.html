SOFTWARE AND APPLICATION ENGINEER ON SITE

SOFTWARE AND APPLICATION ENGINEER ON SITE Your future missionsYou design and develop internal web applications in C#/.NET and React/TypeScript. Your business focuses primarily on solutions to support and digitize our production processes.

  • You analyze applications in collaboration with the various specialized departments and translate them into thoughtful, user-friendly and easy-to-maintain software solutions.
  • You are changing existing applications and modernizing our application portfolio for production as well as various internal services and functions.
  • You create REST APIs and interfaces allowing the integration of our applications within the existing IT environment.
  • You guarantee software quality with a clean, structured and sustainable code, as well as automated tests and code reviews.

You work in an agile development team, implement the work lots independently and actively contribute to the design of technical solutions and to the improvement of development processes. You support our Service Desk as part of the 3rd level support, participating in the analysis of technical incidents and the implementation of durable solutions for their resolution. Your skills and skillsYou have completed IT/NEC training with application development guidance, an IT degree at the ES, HES or university level, or have an equivalent qualification.

  • You justify at least three years of relevant professional experience in the development of web applications.
  • You have good practical knowledge in C#/.NET and ASP.NET Core, as well as in React and TypeScript.
  • You have experience in developing and integrating REST API, as well as basic knowledge in SQL and relational databases.

You use Git with ease for version management and collaboration within the development team. You also have experience with IC/CD processes as well as Azure DevOps or GitHub platforms.

  • You are familiar with agile working methods and actively participate in planning, implementation and continuous improvement within the team.
  • An asset is the knowledge of the Entity Framework, as well as experience in automated testing and code reviews.
  • An experience in using ServiceNow is also an asset.
  • You demonstrate an analytical mindset, understand the professional context in its entirety and develop pragmatic, sustainable and responsive solutions.
  • You work in a structured and autonomous way, willingly share your knowledge and cultivate open, constructive and team-based collaboration.
