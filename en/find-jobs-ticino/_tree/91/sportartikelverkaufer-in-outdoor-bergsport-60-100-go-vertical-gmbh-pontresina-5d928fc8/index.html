Full description

Sportswear vendor/in Outdoor & Mountain Sports at the Pontresina Shop Go Vertical is a mountain sports company in the Bundner Bergen with shops in Pontresina, St. Moritz, Davos, Chur and Tiefencastel. We are a young, dynamic company and are constantly developing. Alpinists, freeriders, ski touring enthusiasts, mountain bikers and other mountaineers feel comfortable in our shops and on govertical.ch

For our shop in Pontresina we are looking for a committed and independent personality with experience in mountain sports or outdoors for the winter season and with a view to permanent employment. Mountain sports is your passion, in sales you inspire your customers with your experience and expertise about high-quality products? Then you are the perfect addition to our team!

With us, you benefit from attractive employee benefits and exclusive promotions. You can live out your passion for mountain sports right on your doorstep, the mountains are your workplace and your leisure paradise at the same time. And if you need the right equipment, we will gladly support you in starting your next adventure with the best equipment.

  • About us

Together with the Pontresina Mountaineering School, Go Vertical has been one of the leading drivers in alpine tourism in the Canton of Graubünden for years. Graubündenweit we offer tours, courses and training with our mountain guides. In our shops we advise and sell alpine equipment and clothing.

In addition to the three shops in the Engadin (Pontresina, St. Moritz, Diavolezza) are also stationed in Chur and Tiefencastel. Most recently, we took over Fullmoons mountain sports in Davos in the spring of 2022 and thus gained another important location.

In addition to the services offered by alpine leisure experiences and the sale in our shops, Go Vertical also includes a high ropes garden, the operation of a climbing hall in the Engadin and a restaurant. More information can be found at govertical.ch

Our sense and purpose

We see ourselves as part of the mountain sports community. Go Vertical is our contribution to this community to make access to the best products, best experiences and best experiences as easy as possible.

Our vision

We want to be an inspiring example of an authentic, local, comprehensive and sustainable mountain sport offer. As an employer, we offer an attractive perspective for living and working in the mountains.
