Put to the Point

In the technical workshop in Monteceneri, you will use your mechanical skills for varied orders and accompany them from execution to final inspection. With your work, you help ensure that the troops have safe and functional material at their disposal.

  • Date of entry: 01.02.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21026
  • Location: Army Logistics Center Monte Ceneri, 6802 Rivera

You can make this contribution

  • Carry out maintenance and repair work on Army machinery, equipment and systems and meet delivery deadlines
  • Advise and support military and civilian customers on the delivery and return of materials and check the condition of the returned materials
  • Train troop craftsmen in Italian and German and guide them in practical work, including in courses
  • Inspect, maintain and repair weapons of the army and their accessories in accordance with technical instructions, as well as inspect and repair optical materials
  • Obligation to complete the intended training for the use of force and weapons and to carry the service weapon in the performance of the function

This makes them unique.

  • Completed apprenticeship as a polymechanic or weapons mechanic
  • Professional experience in the mechanical field and good knowledge in metalworking and maintenance
  • Independent and responsible working methods and the ability to analyze technical problems and find solutions
  • Service orientation and economic use of resources
  • Experience in using digital tools to process data and information
  • Oral and written knowledge of a second official language required for the function

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
