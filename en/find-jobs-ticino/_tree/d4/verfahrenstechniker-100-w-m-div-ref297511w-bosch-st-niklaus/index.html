Full description

Your wich

As a process engineer (m/w/div.) in the field of accessories for the oscillator multi-tool with a focus on manufacturing processes, you take responsibility for the support and the reliability of a wide variety of systems and processes. As a deputy of the work preparation provider you support in various topics and projects. You are the link and contact person for technical concerns of the plants and processes in the production unit (MSE 3).

As a process engineer (w/m/div.) you are responsible for the technical support of the production plants and carry out manufacturing tests. The development of improvement potentials belong to your area of responsibility.

Your tasks include

  • The standardization and further development of processes are subject to your responsibility
  • You support new starts
  • As a problem solver you develop processes and procedures further
  • The daily support of the manufacturing team is one of your responsibilities
  • You are responsible for the compliance with the 5S standards as well as training of team leders, operators and other mechanics
  • The help with projects for cost reduction (Lohnratio, process times, auxiliary materials) complete your field of responsibility

Your profile

  • Training: Basic technical training (e.g. polymechanic / automatic / automechanic (m/w/div.)) ideally with a further training to become technician HF / FH / Uni
  • Personality and working method: Solution-oriented, communicative and able to work with a team with an independent and structured working method and a flair for improvement potential
  • Experience and know-how: Experience in production technology of advantage
  • Languages: Good communication and expressiveness in German, English skills are a plus
