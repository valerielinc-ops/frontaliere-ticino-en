Full description

Portrait Worb is rural, attractive and easily accessible by public transport. In the Worb primary school, more than 500 children learn, accompanied by around 70 dedicated teachers, who understand school as a living learning and living space. Sir works in a centrally located schoolhouse with spacious room conditions in a small team.

Tasks

  • They teach and accompany a child with bVSa int. Status in a basic level class.
  • They are an open personality with knowledge and experience in dealing with children with special needs. They work independently and resources oriented.

Requirements

  • You are a trained curative school teacher and/or have experience and knowledge in special education.
  • You are familiar with the processes for accompanying a child with bVSa int. Status or are ready to acquire it.

We offer

  • 6L as SHP
  • Handover by current holder or Class team
  • Supportive Environment: Class Team, Special Teachers, SSA, SL
  • Centrally located school house with few classes (3 classes)
