WhO YOu Are

As an IKEA Food Co-worker, you will strive to make people smile while also providing irresistible food options in a welcoming atmosphere that gives our customers a good reason to come back.

  • You have experience in a high-volume, customer-oriented environment, ideally in hospitality or sales.
  • You are a friendly person, you enjoy working directly with customers and you always strive to provide them with excellent service.
  • You are physically resilient and able to stand for an extended period of time, even without customers being present.
  • You are flexible and able to work during the store’s opening hours.
  • You are a strong team player, but you also like to take responsibility for working individually.
  • You are comfortable in a highly dynamic and stimulating environment and work well under pressure.
  • You have a good knowledge of IT systems, particularly MS Office.
  • You speak Italian fluently and have a good level of English. Knowledge of another national language is an advantage.

Your Responsibilities

  • Serving and meeting customers with an empathetic and caring attitude based on IKEA values.
  • Ensure that the food, drinks and products in your area of responsibility are of the highest quality and freshness and are presented in the most appealing way.
  • Always keep your area of responsibility clean, tidy and welcoming.
  • Seeing the sales area through the eyes of our customers in order to continuously improve and meet their needs.
  • Receiving payments and handling cash transactions at the till.
  • Finding more effective ways of working to reduce costs, including controlling waste, adjusting portion sizes, controlling production levels and choosing the most suitable methods for preparing dishes.
  • Respect and follow internal rules, policies and daily operating routines with regard to issues such as quality, food safety and stock levels.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
