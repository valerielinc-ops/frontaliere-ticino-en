Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Diagnostic Department, consisting of eight medical services, is responsible for providing diagnostics, therapeutic products, therapeutic procedures, and specialized, high-quality medical clinical consultations, as soon as possible and at an acceptable cost, to guide the management of patients and contribute to therapeutic decisions and actions. The primary mission of the Nuclear Medicine and Molecular Imaging Service is to provide clinical diagnostic services using molecular imaging technologies, particularly those using radioactive sources administered to patients. In addition to the services provided by the other departments of the HUG, it uses radioisotopes for therapeutic purposes and for certain laboratory tests. It proposes scintigraphy, monophotonic emission tomography (TEMP) or positon emission tomography (TEP or PET scan). The service also develops new tracers and biomarkers for molecular imaging and provides, through specific devices (X-rays, ultrasound), services for the diagnosis of osteoporosis and the assessment of body composition.

Description of post

You ensure, under the supervision of the medical hierarchy, the practical activities, clinical and therapeutic interventions entrusted to you and you engage in post-graduate training in nuclear medicine and molecular imaging according to the ISFM program. You are contributing to the training of intern doctors. You progress in your training course according to the defined plan and/or FMH requirements for the specialization in nuclear medicine and molecular imaging.

Qualifications

You have a federal doctor's degree or equivalent training. If your diploma comes from a foreign country, you have obtained recognition by the Medical Professions Commission of the Federal Office of Public Health (MEBEKO) and your diploma is registered in the Medical Professions Register (MedReg). An experiment in nuclear medicine would be an asset. For non-French candidates, the French level C1 for the spoken and written test is required. With excellent communication skills in a multidisciplinary team and patient, you are a dynamic, flexible and committed person. In addition, you have demonstrated strong adaptability.
