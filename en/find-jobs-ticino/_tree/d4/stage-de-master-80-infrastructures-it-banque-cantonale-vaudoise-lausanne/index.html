Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Put your managerial ambitions at the service of sound and sustainable performance by joining the canton of Vaud's first universal bank, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Strategy and Organization department, we are looking for one or more:

Master Trainee Your BCV project: Systemic modelling and criticality analysis of technical infrastructures (Comfort & Data Centers)

  • Develop a Work Breakdown Standardised structure for all technical installations (CVC, Electricity, ADB, etc.), allowing a clear classification of equipment.
  • Create Block Flow Diagrams and Process Flow Diagrams illustrating the interdependencies between the different subsystems, with a particular focus on redundancy and data center distribution.
  • Develop a facility criticality matrix (risk and impact assessment in case of failure) to align maintenance priorities with banking business continuity requirements. Your profile:
  • 2nd year Master at EPFL in Energy Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering,
  • great interest in carrying out your Master's work in banking,
  • relational ease, dynamism, organization, proactivity and adaptability.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let us write together three stories, one of your career, one of your bank, one of our region.
