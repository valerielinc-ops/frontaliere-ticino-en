Are you based in the Berlin, Halle, Leipzig, Dresden area, have a solid experience in the food industry and have built a strong network in the Foodservice over several years?   • from trade and end-users, from purchasing, sales to product development, kitchen and menu planning. Business is your language: you can order, analyze and translate concrete measures into numbers, market mechanisms and trends. Regional key accounts are in focus: you have several years of experience in Foodservice
  • ideally in managing regional key customers and trade and know the urban areas of your region well. Analytical and strategic: you recognize potential, develop suitable approaches and apply them consistently in your region. Communicative with effect: you inspire people, negotiate securely and build relationships that count. Hands-on and team-oriented: you work independently, think solution-oriented and appreciate collaboration in the team.
