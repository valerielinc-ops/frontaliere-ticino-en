Team Lead Electrical Engineering

Their tasks

  • Professional and personnel management as well as further development of a dedicated electroengineering team
  • Responsibility for the further development of existing products and evaluation of new components and technologies
  • Close cooperation with product management, production, sales and other specialist areas
  • Ensuring and continuously improving engineering standards, processes and quality policies
  • Planning and coordination of resources, projects and engineering activities
  • Initiation and implementation of CIP improvement measures

Your profile

  • Solid basic technical training and further education at HF/FH level (electrical, automation or system engineering)
  • Several years of professional experience in electrical engineering as well as experience in the management and development of teams
  • In-depth expertise of relevant standards, safety standards in the field of electrical engineering in mechanical and plant engineering
  • Profound knowledge in electrical CAD systems (EPLAN P8, COMOS or comparable systems)
  • Understanding automation and robotics solutions, knowledge of ABB or FANUC systems are a plus
  • Very good communication skills in German and English in dealing with customers, as well as internal and external contacts (gn)
  • Independent, motivated and team-oriented personality

We offer

  • Exciting projects in an internationally oriented company in the high-tech sector
  • Open corporate culture and a friendly, open-minded team
  • A modern production and technology centre for thermal coating from 2027 in Hausen (AG)
  • Perspectives for personal and professional development
  • Working independently in an attractive working environment
  • Attractive employment conditions and progressive social benefits

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in material science engineering. With the right combination of surface technologies, advanced materials, coating equipment, components and advanced manufacturing, we help customers improve the performance, efficiency, durability and sustainability of their products and processes. Our technologies are often invisible to end users, but critical to the business of the industries that make modern life possible: aerospace, energy, semiconductors, medical technology, luxury goods, tool industry, automotive industry and general industry. Oerlikon is headquartered in Pfäffikon, Switzerland, operates in 38 countries and employs around 10,000 people worldwide. In 2025, Oerlikon generated sales of CHF 1.6 billion.

We offer a wide variety of activity profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and reach their full potential, guided by our core values of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.
