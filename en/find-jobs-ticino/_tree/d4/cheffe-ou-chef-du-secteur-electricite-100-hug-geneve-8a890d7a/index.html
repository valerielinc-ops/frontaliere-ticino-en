Description of post

Within the Infrastructure Maintenance and Optimization Service (SMOI), you take responsibility for the Electricity sector whose installations are critical for the safety of our patient and the functioning of the institution. As such, you manage all the maintenance, operation and development activities of these high sensitivity technical facilities. You define the operational objectives of your sector in line with the service's strategy and ensure its deployment to your team. You manage a team of technicians by empowering them and providing them with the support and supervision necessary for the proper functioning of the sector. You train and support employees operating electrical installations. You manage activities and human, material and financial resources, ensuring that standards, timelines and budgets are met. You ensure the regulatory compliance of the facilities under your responsibility, and ensure a high level of safety and reliability of the equipment. You set up and follow performance indicators to drive the effectiveness of preventive and curative maintenance, in order to continuously optimize your industry practices. You conduct technical projects (compliance, renewal of facilities, continuous improvement), in collaboration with internal services and external partners. You ensure a technological and regulatory watch in order to anticipate the necessary changes in facilities in relation to medical requirements and safety standards. Manager of specialized teams, you ensure the development of skills, the transmission of know-how and the commitment of your employees. You promote a culture of demand, collaboration and continuous improvement. As a technical manager, you have the general authorisation to install within the meaning of Article 9 of ITTO. You are also the "installation operator" within the meaning of the FCIO and the ESTI 100 Directive. You are also a key contact person for users, advising them on appropriate technical solutions and prioritizing interventions based on safety and continuity issues.

Qualifications

You have a federal degree in electrical installation and safety (Federal Master's degree) enabling you to obtain a general authorization to install within the meaning of ITTO Article 9 and to make it available to the HUGs, supplemented by management training (CAS or equivalent). You have a solid experience (minimum 8 to 10 years) in managing complex technical facilities, ideally in critical environments (hospital, industry, public infrastructure). You have excellent control of standards and regulations, as well as technical expertise related to your sector's facilities. Your managerial experience allows you to drive technical teams with demand, fairness and commitment. You are recognized or recognized for your leadership, sense of responsibility, and ability to unite. At ease in demanding environments, you know how to make decisions, prioritize and manage emergency situations in cold blood. You master GMAO tools and common office tools. A good command of French is essential. Your sense of service, your rigor and your ability to collaborate with various interlocutors make you a key player in the proper functioning of hospital infrastructure.
