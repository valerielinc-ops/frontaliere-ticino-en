Back to Overview International service technicians - Focus Switzerland (a) 100% Job opening Immediately or by agreement Employment level 100% Place of work/region BE-Bern For an internationally leading industrial company in the Port region, we are looking for a tech-savvy personality as an international service technician (a).

Their tasks

  • Install cleaning facilities at the customer and put them into operation
  • Qualify facilities and train operators
  • Carry out maintenance, repairs and calibrations
  • Support customers with technical problems by telephone and remotely
  • Implement customer-specific upgrades and optimizations
  • Accompany factory acceptances with customers and project management
  • Area of application: approximately 80% Switzerland and 20% abroad

Requirements

  • Training as a mechatronics technician, automatic technician or equivalent
  • Knowledge of mechanics, pneumatics and electrical engineering
  • Experience with control components is advantageous
  • Enjoyment of technology and complex systems
  • Willingness to learn new things
  • Good MS-Office knowledge
  • Stylistic German and French
  • Good English skills
  • Readiness for national and international missions

We offer you

  • Modern workplace in an international industrial company
  • Flexible working hours
  • No cooking service
  • Own company vehicle
