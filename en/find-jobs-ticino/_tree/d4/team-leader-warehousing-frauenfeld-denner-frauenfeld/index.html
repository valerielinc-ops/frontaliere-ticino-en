Full description

Are you behind the scenes ensuring our store runs smoothly? Welcome to our winning team. Without logistics, nothing works for us. It’s the bottleneck, efficiency booster, and heartbeat all in one. Here, we need absolute professionals like you. Denner. This is our store. ## Tasks

  • Lead a team of 22 employees both personnel-wise and professionally
  • Promote and develop cross-departmental collaboration within the distribution center
  • Support employee development and conduct performance reviews and annual employee qualifications
  • Ensure smooth processes and actively shape the continuous improvement process (KVP) in operational activities
  • Actively support daily operations, such as storage and replenishment
  • Participate in location-based projects ## Requirements
  • At least 4 years of professional experience in a comparable position
  • Completed vocational training (EFZ) in logistics, supplemented with a federal diploma in logistics
  • Proven leadership experience in larger teams
  • Process-oriented and cross-departmental thinking and acting
  • Sensitivity and empathy when dealing with others
  • Experience with MS-Office and various logistics tools, preferably with LFS
  • German (business fluent) ## What We Offer
  • Mobile subscription: The choice between Galaxus Mobile, Wingo, or Sunrise, starting at CHF 10.-, keeping you connected
  • Employee discounts: 10% off your next big purchase (twice a year)
  • Responsibility & freedom: Plenty of space for your ideas and to move forward with us
  • Creative and decision-making freedom: Opportunity to shape Denner’s success story and implement your own ideas
  • Vacation: 6 weeks of holiday for everyone, plus additional days for service anniversaries ## Contact ## Application & Contact Fabienne Tschantré ## Recruitment Process An interview with HR and the specialist department The interview is conducted virtually or on-site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. Response time: up to three weeks Personality/Performance Test Under certain conditions, we use an additional personality test (BIP, Scales test, or similar) to support the selection process. The test results may be discussed personally and form the basis for further selection. Response time: up to one week Trial Work A trial day or half-day provides the opportunity to better get to know the work environment and team members. Conversely, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Response time: up to three weeks Offer & Contract Signing After our verbal job offer, the main contractual points are verified again. Upon agreement, the contract is drafted and sent by mail.
