Full description

With a new real-time data platform, we are creating the foundation for significantly more precise forecasts and data-driven decisions. If you want to use modern streaming technologies and help shape a strategically important platform from the outset, you are in the right place.

Your area of responsibility

  • You develop core components for a modern real-time data platform and accompany them from conception through to productive operation
  • Together with experts from different disciplines, you shape architectural and design decisions for scalable data and platform solutions
  • You implement streaming solutions with Apache Kafka and Apache Flink and ensure their stability and quality
  • The further development of deployment, automation and operations processes is part of your area of responsibility
  • You analyze complex technical issues in a structured manner and derive pragmatic solutions for sustainable platforms from them
  • With your expertise, you help build a new data platform that significantly advances BKW's forecasting capabilities

Your strengths

  • You have several years of experience in software engineering, data engineering or platform engineering and are comfortable working in production-like environments
  • Sound knowledge of Apache Kafka and Apache Flink enables you to successfully implement distributed systems and streaming architectures
  • You use German and English confidently in your everyday work and communicate appropriately with different audiences and at different levels
  • You are characterized by an independent and proactive way of working and recognize tasks before someone has to assign them
  • Openness and constructive interaction with different stakeholder groups help you develop viable solutions together
  • Knowledge of Java or Python as well as experience with Kubernetes, Azure Cloud or modern lakehouse technologies such as Apache Iceberg, Apache Paimon, Databricks or Snowflake ideally round out your profile
