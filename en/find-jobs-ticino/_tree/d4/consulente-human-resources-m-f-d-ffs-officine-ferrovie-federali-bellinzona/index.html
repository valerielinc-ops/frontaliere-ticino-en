What we offer.

Contribute to moving Switzerland with us and provide advice to SBB executives on various and challenging HR issues. A collegial team is waiting for you, where individual collaboration and responsibility play a key role. What you can move.

  • Provide advice to our executives, in person, in digital and telephone format, on all matters relating to conditions and employment relationships within our collective agreement (CCL).
  • Manage multiple complex issues within HR processes and constantly apply the Outside-In principle, orienting your work to customer needs.
  • As an interlocutor and partner of comparison of the executives, it supports them in implementing measures of labour law and in implementing directives.
  • Working with executives and HR Business Partners in the implementation of reorganization.
  • You specialize in specific HR themes, collaborate with specialist teams and actively share your knowledge with colleagues. You also contribute to the continuous improvement of our services.
  • Provide advice and support to the line and HR Business Partners in the operational implementation of reorganisations.

The one you bring with you.

  • Among your strengths are a strong orientation to solutions, ability to manage conflicts and pragmatism.
  • Ideally you have several years of experience in consulting.
  • It is preferential to have a good preparation for the right of work.
  • We require a degree in business or psychology, or training as a specialist in human resources with complementary training.
  • Good language skills are indispensable in Italian, at level C1. Excellent knowledge of German or French (level B2) are welcome.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
