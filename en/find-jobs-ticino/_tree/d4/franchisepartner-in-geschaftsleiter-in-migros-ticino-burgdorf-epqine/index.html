Full description

For our VOI Migros partner in Burgdorf-Steinhof we are looking for a success-oriented, ambitious and motivated personality who is ready to operate a customer-oriented, modern offer with us on February 2027. As a franchise partner with equity, you and your qualified employees independently take over and manage a future-oriented VOI sales office.

Luogo di lavoro: VOI, VOI Burgdorf-Steinhof, Bernstrasse 67, 3400 Burgdorf

Important information

More information about our franchising concept: www.voi-migropartner.ch/de-ch/franchising We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Independent management of a future-oriented and modern VOI Migros partner sales office
  • Recruitment, management, planning and development of employees
  • Assortment management with high-quality Migros products, supplemented with local specialties
  • Management of turnover and personnel costs
  • Active care of customer relationships

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in food retailing or related industries
  • Completed basic or further training: ideally in the food sector and in the area of management
  • Enthusiasm and skillful interaction with people
  • Responsibility and flexibility
  • Success-oriented, ambitious and motivated personality (ideally from 30 years)
  • Experience in management as well as entrepreneurial thinking & acting
  • German (very good knowledge)
