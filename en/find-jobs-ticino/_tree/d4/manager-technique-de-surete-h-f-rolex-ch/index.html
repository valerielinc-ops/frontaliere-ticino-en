Full description

Introduction

For our Purchasing, Support & Infrastructure Division, we are looking for a Community Manager (M/F) for the Security Techniques team, within the Piloting Projects department. Your main mission will be to pilot a team of specialists responsible for the operation, maintenance and evolution of technical safety systems at our sites.

Responsibilities

  • Communicate the department's guidelines, set objectives and assist employees in achieving them
  • Organize the team's activity by ensuring the match between load, capacity, priorities and skills
  • Develop the skills, autonomy and commitment of employees, and ensure knowledge sharing
  • Create a framework of trust, fostering communication, cohesion and collective performance, with demanding and caring community management
  • Ensure availability, reliability and performance of technical safety systems in accordance with expected service levels
  • Supervise preventive, corrective and scalable maintenance activities, updates, release climbs and obsolescence management
  • Pilot internal and external contractors, monitor maintenance contracts and contribute to the control of operating budgets
  • Ensure consistency of technical architectures, their interfaces with information systems and their compliance with cyber security requirements
  • Organize expert support to internal operators and users, and monitor activity using indicators of availability, quality of service, incidents and costs
  • Coordinate the processing of works and interventions that may impact security facilities, with Building & Infrastructure teams, information systems, project managers, operators and implementing companies
  • Oversee systems development, renewal and deployment projects, from need expression to commissioning
  • Ensure the quality of technical concepts, documentation, testing, receptions and transfer to operation
  • Deploy and maintain the standards, processes and tools necessary for operation and maintenance, with a logic of continuous improvement
  • Develop trust relationships with partner services and ensure compliance with security, confidentiality, compliance, cybersecurity and business continuity requirements

Profile

  • Successful experience in team management
  • Experience in contracting, managing maintenance contracts and conducting technical projects
  • Confirmed experience in the operation, maintenance and evolution of technical safety systems
  • Advanced technical or university training: HES/EPF engineer, Bachelor or Master in Engineering, or equivalent
  • Good knowledge of access control, identity management and physical access solutions, video protection, intercom, visitor management, parking or security hypervision
  • Excellent understanding of IT infrastructures, networks, servers, databases, application interfaces and cybersecurity issues
  • Project management training or certification: an asset
  • Good knowledge of common office automation and reporting tools
  • Excellent mastery of oral and written French; desired technical English and German is an asset

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
