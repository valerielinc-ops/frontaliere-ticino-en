Full description

We are looking for our cosmetics specialist school immediately or by agreement : Cosmetics teacher EFZ, part-time

Tasks

  • Training of course participants in theory & practice
  • Preparation and follow-up of your own lessons
  • Conduct of practical and theoretical tests

Requirements

  • Language skills: German & English (must-requirements) and advantageously available in other languages (especially in French, Spanish and Portuguese)
  • Training as a beautician EFZ and at least 3 years of professional experience
  • In the case of a foreign education, we require proof of equivalence, i.e. the recognition of foreign professional qualifications and professional cards in Switzerland
  • Furthermore, similar training such as EFZ Apotheker or HF Gesundheit are also conceivable.
  • At least 2 years of experience in training apprentices or adults
  • Natural Authority and Patience
  • Optional: further training in the field of cosmetics (e.g. master examination)

We offer

  • Strong team with a pleasant working atmosphere
  • Interesting Conditions of Employment
  • Potential for further development and assumption of greater responsibility
  • Part-time workload of approximately 20 – 60% (depending on language skills and number of classes)

Do you live in Switzerland or the EU and have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete application with photo by email for the position as cosmetics teacher. Swiss Education Company, Dübendorfstrasse 2, 8051 Zurich, email: hr@sbacademy.ch
