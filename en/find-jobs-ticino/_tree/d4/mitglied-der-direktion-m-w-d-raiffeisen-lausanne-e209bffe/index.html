Full description

18.09.2026 Lausanne

Be part of our team!

Would you like to put your expertise and leadership at the service of a cooperative bank firmly anchored in its region? With around 50 collaborators, four branches (Lausanne, Epilinges, Mézières and Oron-la-Ville) and a balance sheet of CHF 1.4 billion, Banque Raiffeisen Lausanne-Haute-Broye-Jorat is a leading banking player in its region. The Bank also has a dedicated wealth management team located in our Grand-Chêne premises. In order to strengthen our Directorate, we are looking for an inspiring personality to take responsibility for our Market department including all customer-related activities. Directly linked to the President of the Directorate, you lead a strategic sector involving private customers, wealth management and corporate clients. You are actively contributing to the Bank's strategic directions, to the development of our distribution network, and to the strengthening of our position in our market. A recognized leader, you support industry leaders in their development, foster a culture of performance and proximity, and create the conditions for your teams to reach their full potential. Thanks to your excellent knowledge of the regional economic fabric, you represent our Bank with clients, partners and key regional players.

What are you waiting for?

  • you are actively involved in the Bank's strategic thinking and development as a member of the Board;
  • you manage and develop the different customer sectors of the universal bank;
  • you support the development of private customers, you speed up the development of wealth management, you contribute to the implementation of corporate customers;
  • you accompany and make grow the leaders of the 4 sectors by a local management, demanding and mobilising;
  • you define the distribution actions (Sales) and ensure that they are implemented throughout the network;
  • you enhance collaboration, operational efficiency and digitalization;
  • you represent the Bank with the economic and institutional players in the region and develop a strong and sustainable network;
  • you are actively contributing to the evolution of the organization and the accompaniment of change.

What are you bringing?

  • higher education in banking economy or finance with a confirmed experience (8-10 years) in customer counselling, including a solid managerial practice within a financial institute;
  • ability to support the increase in skills of its collaborators, delegating with demand and in a climate of trust;
  • Strategic vision, unifying leadership and negotiation;
  • a strong understanding of the issues of distribution and market development;
  • understanding the needs of different segments, openness to digital and ability to drive change;
  • a high level of responsibility, ethics and risk.

"We are looking for a personality that embodies our values of trust, audacity, respect, commitment and community spirit, and that wishes to contribute actively to the sustainable development of our bank and its region. Collaborative spirit and leadership are key in the relationships that drive us." Stéphane Mathey President of the Directorate

Why Raiffeisen?

We leave room for personal initiative and make sense of our work We invite our employees to actively shape their work and assume responsibilities. The result is a variety of tasks that create real added value – for all people who want to move things.

We encourage personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is an integral part of our culture. We create a space for the professional and personal development of each individual and support our employees in a targeted manner, through a wide range of in-house training and participation in external development costs.

We offer attractive social conditions and benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages over banking products, various discounts from third parties, as well as above average financial stability and old age insurance.
