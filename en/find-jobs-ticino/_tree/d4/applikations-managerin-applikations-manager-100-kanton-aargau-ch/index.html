Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Technical overall responsibility for the operation and life cycle of business applications - from planning to releases to ongoing operation according to SLA
  • Management of stakeholders (experts, customers, management) in cooperation with the service manager and active participation in the application roadmap
  • Coordinating and implementing changes and releases, processing incidents and requests according to ITIL practices - including participation in escalation management in the event of major disruptions
  • Maintenance and further development of application documentation (operation manual, configuration management) as well as participation in security documentation (information security, data protection)
  • Drive continuous service improvements and automation in the operation and support process
  • Operational supplier management and participation in tenders - including budget and SLA monitoring

What you bring

  • Education in the field of business informatics, computer science or digital business (Bachelor FH/HSW, HF Informatik, Swiss Federal Diploma) or equivalent professional experience
  • Several years of experience in the operational operation of applications in the corporate environment with appropriate knowledge in release and test management
  • Practical proven IT Service Management (ITSM/ITIL) Knowledge, ITIL Foundation Certification
  • Knowledge in Requirement Engineering, Business Analysis and Process Modelling as well as experience in CMDB maintenance, Configuration Management and Application Inventory Administration
  • Experience in the preparation of operational documentation, technical test cases, information security concepts, data protection analyses and operational supplier management
  • Strong service orientation, strong communication skills and analytical thinking

The Department of Finance and Resources, with around 650 employees, is responsible as a cross-sectional department for ensuring that the canton has the necessary resources to fulfil its tasks.

Computer science solutions make daily work easier and more efficient. The employees of the Computer Science Department Aargau contribute to increasing the efficiency of the cantonal administration. You are responsible for the strategy and operation of central computer science. They accompany large-scale cantonal projects and provide efficient and modern IT solutions that enable the public to have uncomplicated contact and exchange with the canton’s administrative offices.
