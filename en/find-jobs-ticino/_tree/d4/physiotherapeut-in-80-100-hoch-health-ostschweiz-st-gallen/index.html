Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Targeted and client-centered treatment of inpatient and outpatient patients in the fields of internal medicine and surgery including intensive care units
  • Treatment of outpatients in medical training therapy and in outpatient medical rehabilitation programs
  • Close cooperation in the interdisciplinary treatment team
  • Participation in practical training of physiotherapy students

  • Intensive cooperation with specialists from various disciplines
  • Attractive employment and training opportunities
