Service staff half board (m/f/d)

The Hotel Hotel Edelweiss in Davos will be looking for a dedicated service force (m/f/d) for the half-board area from the end of November 2026 or by arrangement, who will actively support our team.

Your duties

  • Care of our half board guests during breakfast and evening service
  • Proper food and drink service
  • Preparation and construction of the breakfast buffet
  • Ensuring a smooth service process and a high level of guest satisfaction
  • Close cooperation with the kitchen and other departments
  • Care and cleaning of the work area and compliance with hygiene standards

Your profile

  • Professional experience in service, ideally in the hotel industry
  • Friendly and well-groomed appearance
  • Enjoyment in dealing with guests and a strong service orientation
  • Flexibility and willingness to work even on weekends and holidays
  • Good knowledge of German, further language skills beneficial

We offer

  • A varied job in a family and friendly working environment
  • Fair pay
  • Possibility of accommodation and food by arrangement
  • Working in one of the most beautiful regions of Switzerland with numerous leisure opportunities

Aroused interest? Then we look forward to your full application (curriculum vitae, certificates, cover letter) by e-mail to or by post.
