Senior physician / senior physician day clinic Hybrid, 50-70 %

Location: Basel

From 01.01.2027 or by agreement

Founded in 1900, the Klinik Sonnenhalde in Riehen is now an established private clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland and southern Baden. We pursue a holistic, human-centered, milieutherapeutic psychiatry that combines excellent professional quality with a Christian value base. The clinic has 68 stationary beds, 4 day clinics and 4 ambulatories. As one of the first clinics in Switzerland, we are actively promoting digital therapy offerings, such as blended therapy and virtual reality and opened the first hybrid day clinic in Switzerland in February 2023.

Take on a responsible specialist work in psychiatry and psychotherapy, shape innovative treatment concepts and contribute your professional and personal skills to the interdisciplinary cooperation and further development of the clinic.

Your responsibilities

  • You have the leadership responsibility for an interdisciplinary team that can consist of assistant doctors:innen, psychologist:innen, nurses, social workers:innen and specialist therapists:innen
  • You have the overall medical responsibility for the treatment of all patients: inside the day clinic and for the clarification of the indication for the day clinic Hybrid. In addition, you are responsible for the supervision, further education and professional promotion of the assistant doctor:
  • They promote employees through target agreements and treat them prudently, appreciatively and act result- and solution-oriented. In the professional management of professional groups that do not correspond to their own, you will be supported by experts:
  • You are responsible for occupying the day clinic, implementing the strategic objectives of the clinic and further developing the therapeutic offer with a focus on blended care and virtual reality (VR).

Your profile

  • You are a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy with an interest in innovative psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment concepts
  • You are a dynamic, innovative and communicative personality with pronounced self-reflection and willingness to take on specialist and managerial responsibility
  • You are motivated to develop professionally and appreciate the interdisciplinary cooperation in a care-oriented clinic
  • They are open and interested in the Christian values of the clinic, support its mission statement and advocate a holistic approach.

Their possibilities

  • Varied psychiatric-psychotherapeutic activity with management tasks in a competent, multi-professional team
  • Great design freedom and versatile portfolio of tasks in your own area of activity
  • Appreciative corporate culture and pleasant working environment in a manageable clinic
  • Attractive employment conditions
  • Good further education and training opportunities for professional and personal development
