Employee / Employee Administration 40-80 %

Their tasks

  • Daily guest administration/guest advice
  • Contact person for internal guests and external visitors
  • Operation of the telephone exchange and support for reception
  • Independent implementation of monthly invoicing including control and timely processing
  • Administrative and operational support of the management
  • Preparation and management of contract documents
  • Accounting tasks such as creditor accounting
  • Management and ordering of office supplies and printed matter
  • Assistance and support in the implementation and organisation of marketing measures and events

Your profile

  • Completed training for the EFZ businesswoman and several years of professional experience in a comparable position
  • In-depth experience in invoicing with SAP as well as the ability to perform it independently, reliably and on schedule
  • Very good computer science knowledge (MS Office, CRM, CMS, SAP)
  • Precise operation
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying multiple challenges
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Employment level and periods of use
