Full description

In the middle of the beautiful Emmental – Your new challenge is waiting for you! With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Care with heart and responsibility – become part of our team! What makes us special? As a versatile peripheral hospital with heart and mind, we live a trusting coexistence and a working environment that is characterized by openness, respect and appreciation. Working modernly for us means: acting humanely, with freedom of design and an open ear for each other. Our employees are in the middle of us instead of just there – both professionally and personally. Family-friendly conditions and jointly designed service planning are part of everyday life for us. For our medical department at the Burgdorf site, which has 33 beds and includes about 50 employees as well as students and learners, we are looking for an immediate or by appointment.

Create care, strengthen people – your field of action with us

  • With self-responsibility and according to the latest nursing findings, you look after and care for our patients
  • You take over the management of a care group and work closely with health care professionals, nursing assistants, students and learners
  • You control the entire care process – supported by our clinic information system Clinical – and ensure seamless documentation
  • Together as a team, you continuously optimize the care processes and contribute your ideas
  • As a mentor: you accompany and promote students and learners on their way to the nursing profession

You need this to be successful

  • You are dipl. Nursing professional (HF/FH) or shortly before graduation and have ideally already gained experience in acute care
  • The wide medical range of a family peripheral hospital you find exciting and enriching
  • You meet people with respect and appreciation, because the best possible care of our patients comes first for you
  • You work carefully, conscientiously and like to take responsibility
  • Further development is a matter of course for you and you want to constantly expand your skills
  • You master the German language stylishly (B2 proof for foreign-language applicants is the minimum requirement)
