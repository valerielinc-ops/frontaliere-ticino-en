Mission At Decathlon, sport is at the heart of our daily lives, and every member of the team can invest and grow. Here, team spirit is essential: it is by playing together that we achieve our goals together. Join a company where it is good to live, and integrate a motivated and united team. Come and express your initiative, take responsibility and help us make sport accessible to all. Your playground: The Federal Workshop is responsible for repairing and repairing Decathlon customers' products for all Decathlon subsidiaries in Switzerland (e-commerce site, warehouse and 40th store). Created in 2022, the Federal Workshop consists of a team of 20 people working daily to develop the country's circular hub strategy. We are looking for a 100% personalization technician. Your missions: Pre-printing & Graphic Design: Verification and vectorization of logos, preparation of files and creation of BAT for validation. Production & Flocage: Adjusting machines, cutting, stowing and placing on hot press (temperature/pressure adjustment depending on the material). Quality & Control: Check the final rendering (centering, colors, marking holding) before shipment. Maintenance & Stock: First-level maintenance of equipment (cleaning, settings) and monitoring of consumable stock. Customer order management: Timeliness monitoring and compliance with Load Taking (PEC). Profile Your profile:

  • You are passionate about the sport, the company's DNA!
