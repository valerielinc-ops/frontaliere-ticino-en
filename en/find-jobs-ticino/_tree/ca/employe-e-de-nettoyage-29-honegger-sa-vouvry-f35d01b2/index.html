Full description

Honegger SA is a company run by its owner that offers comprehensive services for buildings in the fields of cleaning and Facility Management throughout Switzerland.

The traditional family company currently employs nearly 6,500 employees in 24 branches in the four language regions of Switzerland and Liechtenstein. We guarantee Swiss values: Reliability, openness to the world and, in particular, cleanliness. We manage our employees in a respectful, fair and consistent manner.
