SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. The solution "IT Base Infrastructure" provides cloud and platform services, data and messaging infrastructure, API management and developer experience for the entire SBB. Our Agile Release Train Application Platform provides the basic infrastructure to enable business applications to be implemented and operated quickly, securely and scalably. You play a key role at the interface of platform services such as databases, containers, IaaS, monitoring, API management and messaging. These are services on which hundreds of applications run and which form the backbone of the digital SBB. You keep the operation running, drive the further development and keep the economy under control. At the same time, you have the ear on the market and at the digital solution organizations to recognize early on where new technologies or services are in demand. Validate, try, turn off when you fail, massively scale when you succeed - with built-in quality - this is your playing field. A role with great effect and a lot of design space. You can move that.

  • As Product Manager (m/f/d) Application Platform, you will be responsible for platforms that form the digital cornerstone of SBB and thus form the basis for Swiss rail operations.
  • You design a challenging and very broad field of activity in an IT that must be designed for maximum availability, standardization and scaling and in which your decisions are directly noticeable.
  • You will work closely with ARTs such as Identity & Access Management, Development Experience and Cloud Foundation to establish even better, scaled and economical platforms and practices.
  • You make sure that technology does not remain an end in itself, but reliably brings people from A to B every day.
  • In addition, the ART leadership actively participates in the further development of the ART and initiates and drives cross-ART initiatives.
  • In close interaction with product owners, development, operations and SaaS providers, you balance requirements and ensure sustainable decisions in conjunction with security, architecture and business IT.

You bring that with you.

  • Strong leadership skills to proactively promote topics, further develop teams and employees, promote self-responsibility and act effectively in the shared leadership model.
  • Winning appearance, curiosity about technology trends, entrepreneurial thinking and the tenacity to get topics flying across many stakeholders.
  • Sovereign stakeholder management and target group-oriented communication in a complex field of tension between operations, development, business IT, security and architecture. Strong storytelling capabilities and executive presence to convey complex platform topics.
  • In-depth understanding of infrastructure and platform technologies in order to work with architects and developers at eye level and to contribute to technology decisions.
  • Several years of experience in the product management of platform services in a scaled, agile environment with many customers.
  • Validation-driven workflow with the courage to try out new things, evaluate them with customers and scale them as needed.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
