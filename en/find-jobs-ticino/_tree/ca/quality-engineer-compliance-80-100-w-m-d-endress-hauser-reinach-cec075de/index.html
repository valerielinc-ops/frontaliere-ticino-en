Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through cooperation. As the world’s leading provider of measurement technology with over 17,000 employees, we are shaping the future of process automation. Whether we develop and implement new technologies as a team, jointly manufacture measuring instruments or strengthen important relationships with countless industries worldwide – we work to create trusting partnerships that help everyone involved to succeed in the long term. As a family-run company, we think beyond short-term decisions and pursue a future-oriented vision – for our employees, our customers and for the world of tomorrow.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a Quality Engineer Compliance (m/f/d) who will play a central role in strengthening quality and regulatory compliance in our production environment. As part of Flowtec, one of our global production centers for flow measurement technology, you will work closely with production and development, ensuring that our products meet international standards and are valued by customers worldwide.

Do you like to work close to production and always focus on quality and compliance? Does it motivate you to translate complex norms and regulations into practical, value-adding solutions? Are you happy to share your expertise and appreciate collaboration across departments and locations?

In this function, you combine technical know-how with a practice-oriented way of working. They ensure that standards are implemented effectively, audits are carried out professionally and continuous improvement is firmly anchored in our production processes.

What are your responsibilities?

They

  • plan, carry out and support internal and external audits in the production environment.
  • analyse deviations, define appropriate corrective measures and ensure their effective implementation.
  • apply your in-depth knowledge of relevant norms and standards in a practical and production-oriented manner.
  • actively share your know-how in the global network and support colleagues on regulatory and compliance issues.
  • work closely with production, R&D, engineering and quality.
  • travel occasionally as part of your activity (about 10%).

What do we expect from you?

  • have a completed university or FH degree, e.g. in a field such as engineering or in quality assurance, compliance or a comparable field of expertise.
  • have a strong technical background and several years of experience in quality management in an industrial environment, ideally in production and auditing.
  • have very good knowledge of relevant standards and regulations, in particular pressure equipment directives, hygienic applications, ex-regulations and ideally approvals in the field of seals and marine.
  • have experience in implementing norms and standards in existing production processes as well as in carrying out assessments and audits.
  • work process-oriented, pragmatic and with a hands-on mentality and appreciate cross-divisional cooperation.
  • have proven experience in building, developing and closing quality and compliance gaps.

What can you expect from us?

  • A family-run, committed company with a sustainable vision
  • Promoting lifelong learning through multiple training and development opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e‐learning
  • Supporting a healthy work-life balance through flexible working hours
  • Promoting sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport
  • Family-friendly services, including childcare and childcare during school holidays
