50-80% | Place of work: Brig | Start of work: immediately or by agreement The FernUni Switzerland stands for completely online university courses and is accredited according to the HFKG. It combines flexible, location-independent distance learning part-time with an active, interdisciplinary research culture at Campus Brig. In five faculties – Law, Psychology, Economics, History and Mathematics & Computer Science – we have been shaping modern teaching, learning and research for more than 30 years and count over 2,700 students and around 550 employees.

To strengthen our team, we seek a dedicated personality as

Assistant or postdoctoral researcher in private law, 50–80 % Place of work: Brig Start of work: immediately or by agreement Their tasks

  • support in teaching in the field of private law, in particular in the fields of bond law, new technologies,
  • Civil Procedure Law/SchKG and interdisciplinary issues.
  • Support for the submission of research projects (SNSF, Innosuisse, cantonal projects, etc.) in the field of private law.
  • participation in publications. Cooperation in the correction of student work.
  • Writing a doctoral thesis or continuation of a postdoctoral project.

Your profile

  • Interest in research and teaching in the field of private law, in particular contract law, civil procedure law and debt enforcement law, law of new technologies (data law, blockchain) and interest in interdisciplinary issues (especially legal psychology).
  • For assistants: Requires a master’s degree in law and interest in the doctorate.
  • For postdoctoral students: Requires a doctorate in law, preferably with a specialization in private law.
  • Experience in teaching and/or academic publishing (or the desire to pursue an academic career in one of the areas sought).
  • Very good knowledge of German (level mother tongue), good knowledge of French and English is advantageous.
  • For international applicants: availability to spend a fixed working day per week in Brig and willingness to move to Switzerland.

We offer

  • Research-strong academic environment: You work in a dynamic university context with proven scientific expertise and interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Attractive employment conditions: You benefit from contemporary working conditions, generous social benefits and a wide range of training opportunities.
  • Dedicated team: You become part of a qualified team that actively lives innovation, quality and collaboration.
  • Inspiring working environment: Our campus in Brig is located in close proximity to the old town, the SBB railway station and attractive natural and mountain regions. It is a place of academic exchange with a high quality of life.

We look forward to your complete application by e-mail to the following address: jobs@fernuni.ch . For more information please contact Prof. Dr. Bruno Pasquier: bruno.pasquier@unidistance.ch . Applications from people who are resident in Switzerland or are considering this in the future will be considered. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
