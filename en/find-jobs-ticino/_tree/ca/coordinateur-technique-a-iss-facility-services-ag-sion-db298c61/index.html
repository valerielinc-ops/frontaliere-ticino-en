Technical coordinator (a)

Place of work : Sion + canton du Valais Activity rate: 100% Start date: To be agreed

At ISS, we all call ourselves "Placemakers". Our goal is to provide our clients with the best possible environment and make them feel good in their workplace. Our nearly 13,000 employees have a high level of service knowledge to meet the different needs of our clients. We achieve them according to the highest quality requirements.

We are looking for an experienced and proactive person to join our team as Technical Manager. You will be in charge of the technical management of several sites, including the maintenance of buildings, as well as the follow-up of maintenance requests. If you are self-sustaining, responsive, have excellent organizational capacity and a strong sense of customer service, this position is for you. You will play a key role in the continuous improvement of the sites and will be responsible for the management of the technical works, while guaranteeing a quality service.

Your tasks:

  • Organize technical activities (CACSE maintenance)
  • Monitoring and monitoring of operational activity
  • Support and monitoring in the monthly maintenance activity
  • To be the contact point for customer managers in the area of Banking
  • Planning the work of concierges and technicians
  • Preparation and availability of internal and audit documents
  • Initiate and monitor small projects
  • Rotational participation in a picket service
  • Ensure administrative monitoring, including reporting of activities carried out on behalf of the client (KPI)

Your profile:

  • CFC in building technology (heating, sanitary, electricity) or equivalent
  • Good knowledge of building technology and project administration
  • Experience in team management
  • Knowledge in German, an asset
  • Very good mastery of common IT tools (MS Office)
  • At ease with administrative tasks
  • Strong customer orientation, strong team spirit
  • Flexible at travel level
  • Meaning of communication
  • Compulsory driving licence, blank criminal record

Ready for us? We are for you.

Every day at ISS Switzerland, we ensure that offices, laboratories, airports, production sites, railway stations, schools and hospitals are well-functioning and comfortable. Our more than 14,000 collaborators create living spaces throughout Switzerland in which more than a million people feel good and can thrive on a daily basis.

ISS stands for innovation, quality of service and proximity to customers. As a leader in our market, we offer stable jobs, a variety of development opportunities and a corporate culture based on esteem, safety at work and responsibility -- translated into five dedicated programmes, more than 200 trainings and improvements, as well as the systematic use of sustainable products and solutions.
