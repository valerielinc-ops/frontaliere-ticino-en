Full description

Bethesda Spital AG | online since : 13.05.2026

As a successful Basel clinic with a private character, the Bethesda Spital combines top medicine with first-class service and a unique feel-good atmosphere. With the five focus topics of women’s medicine, pregnancy & childbirth, musculoskeletal system and rehabilitation as well as palliative care, our 6,000 inpatients annually benefit from state-of-the-art treatment centers under one roof – in the middle of one of the most beautiful hospital parks in northwestern Switzerland.

In order to strengthen the medical team, we are looking for a senior physician emergency medicine 60-80 by agreement %

Their tasks

  • Self-responsible triage, medical care and treatment of emergency patients in a senior function with a focus on musculoskeletal diseases
  • Targeted diagnosis and pain therapy with independent sonography
  • Close interprofessional cooperation with nurses, medical staff and the various in-house specialist areas
  • Care, further education and supervision of assistant doctors
  • Participation in the 2-shift system

They bring with them

  • Specialist title FMH or equivalent foreign title in anesthesia, surgery, orthopaedics and traumatology or general internal medicine
  • Advanced knowledge of emergency sonography of advantage or motivation to acquire the FA POCUS
  • Regional anesthesia knowledge beneficial
  • Enjoyment of modern clinical emergency medicine, as well as motivation and initiative for significant cooperation for the further development of our emergency department
  • Fluency in German and English, French is advantageous

We offer

  • An exciting and varied job in a committed, collegial team that values open communication and mutual support
  • Structured training in emergency sonography with targeted teaching by experienced instructors
  • Possibilities for obtaining the POCUS Component 1 (basic emergency sonography); Component 2 (focused sonography on the musculoskeletal system); with specialist title Anesthesia Component 9 (Regional Aesthesia and Vascular Points) according to SGUM
  • Modern equipment of the emergency department
  • Options for co-designing the roster for reconciling family and work
  • No night services Here you come to the employment conditions at Bethesda

Are you a team player and interested in this exciting challenge? We look forward to your online application. Our recruitment process has been defined according to environmental and economic aspects and does not provide for applications by post or email. Postal and e-mail applications will not be considered. For further questions and more information, please contact Mr. Eckehart Schöll, Medical Director of Emergency Department at 061 315 2585.
