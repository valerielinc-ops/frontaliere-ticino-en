Full description

Post "Legal intern, FNPG Marsens" au RFSM - Réseau fribourgeois santé mentale, Marsens (FR). Le RFSM est le pôle de santé mentale du canton de Fribourg : psychiatrie adulte, enfants/adolescents, personnes âgées, addictions.

Appointing authority / service / institution

The Freiburg Network for Mental Health (FNPG) is administratively assigned to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs. The FNPG is a medical, nursing and psychosocial competence center specializing in psychiatry and psychotherapy and, in a broader sense, mental health.
