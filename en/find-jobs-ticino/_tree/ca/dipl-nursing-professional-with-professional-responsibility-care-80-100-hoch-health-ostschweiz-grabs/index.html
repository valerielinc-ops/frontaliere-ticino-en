Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Holistic care and care of patients of the Stroke Unit
  • Responsibility for the implementation of professional care and care taking into account modern care and treatment concepts as well as care standards
  • Support of care professionals in demanding care situations and in the implementation of overarching concepts and standards on the station
  • Active participation in the cooperation in the MBF triangle (management – education – specialist development) and transfer of expertise and experience
  • Responsible for quality work on the station and ensuring high quality of service through use in all three shifts

What you bring for this job

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing professional FH/HF with min. 1 year of professional experience
  • Personal motivation and pleasure in further developing patient-oriented specialist care on the ward
  • High social skills and commitment to constructive and appreciative cooperation, strong communication skills
  • Rapid comprehension, networked thinking and responsibility
