Full description

EOC – Ente Ospedaliero Cantonale is the hospital network of Ticino, present with its institutes throughout the cantonal territory for a total of 1,000 beds. The organization allows for the combination of local approach and overall vision, ensuring a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of the location where services are requested. Thanks to the commitment and competence of over 6,500 employees and their attention to human relationships, EOC ensures quality healthcare, taking care of over 41,000 inpatients per year and providing over 600,000 outpatient consultations. To complete our team of the Occupational Medicine Service, we are looking for a Chief Medical Officer Occupational Medicine (100%).

  • Profile: The person we are looking for has professional experience and autonomy in the field of general internal medicine and ideally occupational medicine. They also have a strong motivation, initiative, and spirit of collaboration and believe, like us, in the importance of protecting the health of EOC's employees. They are oriented towards identifying and managing risks related to clinical, industrial, and general work activities.
  • We offer a modern and dynamic work environment and the opportunity to work with highly motivated and prepared colleagues. Required qualifications:
  • Federal specialist title in General Internal Medicine in accordance with Articles 20 and 21 of the Federal Law on University Medical Professions (LPMed). For foreign titles, recognition by MEBEKO is required before starting activity
  • Alternatively (or preferably in addition): Federal specialist title in Occupational Medicine in accordance with Articles 20 and 21 of the Federal Law on University Medical Professions (LPMed). For foreign titles, recognition by MEBEKO is required before starting activity
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss healthcare system and social insurance system
  • Skills in performing workplace risk analysis, occupational health visits, and preventive exams in accordance with the relevant laws or willingness to acquire them
  • Ability to ensure compliance with legal requirements and safety and health standards at work
  • Good interpersonal and teamwork skills, as well as a propensity for collaboration with other health and safety services present in the company
  • Mastery of the Italian language (required level at least B2) with registration to MedReg before starting activity. Good knowledge of another national language (French, German) and English are also desirable. Start date: to be agreed. Applications, in electronic format, complete with curriculum vitae, motivation letter, and copies of study certificates are invited.
