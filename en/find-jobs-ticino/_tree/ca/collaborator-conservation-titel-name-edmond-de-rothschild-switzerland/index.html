Full description

MISSION Attached to the Conservation Department within the Operations Department, you will be responsible for the proper management of the stock of precious metals and physical securities held within the Bank. LIABILITY

  • Management of physical inputs and outputs of precious metals and value papers, notification of transactions to clients, collection of necessary documents from managers and delivery of physical certificates to custodians.
  • Follow-up of requests from clients/managers on the transfer, dematerialization or issuance of physical securities in collaboration with the various correspondents. Claims management.
  • Realignment of physical metal positions in the system (Avaloq).
  • Management of Swiss registered shares and reconciliation of registry positions with positions recorded in Avaloq.
  • Request to open separate accounts with our depositories and openings of bank or ex-Custody accounts in Avaloq, on request.
  • Analysis and analytical accounting of the invoices of our depositaries and correspondents.
  • Follow-up to the depositary and corresponding budgets.
  • Establishment of certificates of possession of precious metals.
  • Ensure back-up for caisse activity. PROFILE
  • Federal Certificate of Banking Capacity (FCC) or equivalent.
  • Minimum experience in a back office titles.
  • Interest in economic and financial news.
  • Essential IT skills: MS Office suite, (Excel, World, Outlook and Powerpoint).
  • Organizational know-how to manage simultaneously the required versatility of the position.
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis.
  • Very good written and oral knowledge of French and English.
