SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. As a partner manager, you actively shape the cooperation between SBB Infrastruktur and our most important infrastructure partners in France and Italy. Together we create the conditions for efficient, reliable and attractive cross-border rail transport. You connect people, organisations and interests, promote joint projects and thus make a direct contribution to sustainable mobility in Europe. You can expect a task with a lot of creative freedom, visible impact, an international network and the opportunity to further develop your professional leadership skills. You can move that.

  • As a partner manager, you will develop the bilateral partnerships with France (SNCF Réseau) and Italy (RFI) in accordance with the agreed goals and identify opportunities for joint projects together with the specialist areas.
  • You initiate, coordinate and design joint projects and activities, promote dialogue and ensure efficient and effective cooperation and communication.
  • You build sustainable relationships with contact persons and decision-makers in the partners, design platforms for exchange at the top management level, moderate common exchange formats and communicate complex questions.
  • You prepare regular reports and reports on the implementation of the agreed partner activities and identify suitable measures in the event of deviations together with the responsible specialist areas.
  • Ideally, you will take over the deputy in the International Affairs and Technical Standards team as a National Contact Point for the system task TSI Telematics.

You bring that with you.

  • You have strong skills in building and maintaining strategic partnerships and understand how to build trusting relationships with different stakeholders up to the top management level.
  • You think in a networked and strategic way, recognize common interests and development potential and translate these into successful results with your high implementation orientation.
  • You have a high level of performance skills, communicate convincingly and use diplomatic skills to bring different interests together, even in complex or controversial situations.
  • A university degree at FH/uni level in business, international relations, engineering or a comparable further education at tertiary level.
  • Ideally, you already have knowledge of the railway infrastructure and experience in the implementation of IT projects.
  • We presuppose French and Italian at level C2, you also have English at level C1 and bring German at least at level B2 in word and writing.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
