Full description

Doing the right thing. In Switzerland and in around 20 countries on four continents. Engage with us for the vision of a solidarity society, as Caritas Switzerland has been running asylum centres for families, individuals and unaccompanied minors (MNA) at five locations in the Canton of Zurich since March 2024 on behalf of the Cantonal Social Office (KSA). The Volunteer and Leisure department coordinates volunteer assignments and further develops volunteer work in the asylum centres and various departments. He also coordinates leisure activities and holiday activities for residents in the asylum centres. Their tasks

  • Cross-location and cross-departmental coordination and further development of volunteer work in coordination with the responsible department heads and the volunteer work group
  • Organisation of exchange meetings and promotion of knowledge transfer in volunteer management
  • Development of standards and processes for volunteering as well as evaluation of offers and assurance of quality
  • Clarification of the need for intervention with the locations and recruitment, selection and placement of volunteers
  • Organisation of the mission administration and advice and support of volunteers and contact persons at the locations
  • Organisation of training, exchanges and measures for the recognition of voluntary commitment
  • Coordination of internal and external leisure activities and holiday activities for residents (focus on children and young people) in the asylum centres and development of new projects
  • Cooperation and networking with internal and external partner organisations as well as participation in fundraising

Our requirements

  • University degree (at least Bachelor) in social work, socio-cultural animation, social education or a related discipline
  • Knowledge of the asylum area is beneficial
  • Experience in volunteer coordination and/or further training in volunteer management or volunteer coordination
  • Own commitment as a volunteer*r is beneficial
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German as well as good oral knowledge of another national language and/or English
  • Good user knowledge in Microsoft Office
  • Communication strength, independent and forward-looking working methods and strong organisational capacity

The place of work is Zurich. Start of work: immediately or by agreement. Caritas offers attractive employment conditions and an open working atmosphere at the pulse of social issues. For the transformation towards role-based work, we count on employees with a digital and agile mindset who like to participate in change processes and take responsibility.
