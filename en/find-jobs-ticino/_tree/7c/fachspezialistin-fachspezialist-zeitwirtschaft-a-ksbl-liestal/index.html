Full description

Entry by arrangement · Bruderholz, Human Resources · Job reference 13448 · Permanent employment

Tasks

  • Co-responsibility for the stable, safe and efficient operation as well as the continuous development of Polypoint PEP
  • First point of contact and technical support for complex issues around Polypoint PEP
  • Control of changes, releases and system updates for smooth deployments
  • Coordination of cooperation with external manufacturers, suppliers and internal departments
  • Precise mapping and implementation of labour law, GAV and internal requirements in the system landscape
  • Predictive analysis of legal, regulatory and operational changes and derivation of necessary system adjustments
  • Analysis of technical and technical challenges as well as development of sustainable optimization solutions
  • Competent advice to internal stakeholders on system functions and their compliance with the rules

Requirements

  • Several years of profound experience in the administration, configuration, maintenance and further development of Polypoint PEP
  • Practical proven knowledge in the control of changes, releases and tests as well as in cooperation with internal and external partners
  • Great enthusiasm for acting as central specialist and contact person for Polypoint PEP
  • Strong analytical thinking combined with a structured, pragmatic and solution-oriented way of working
  • High analysis competence in complex problems as well as the ability to develop and implement sustainable solution concepts
  • Confident knowledge of German in words and writing as well as a precise and addressee-oriented communication

Benefits

On and off: mind. 5 weeks holidays, loyalty holidays, 11 paid holidays and bridge days

Area: Human Resources

Level of employment: 60-80 %
