Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

The Peace Court is primarily the child and adult protection authority. It shall act on request or on its own initiative. Upon completion of the procedure, a decision shall be taken as to whether to impose a measure. The peacekeeper also has competences in inheritance law as well as in the area of judicial prohibitions of entry.
