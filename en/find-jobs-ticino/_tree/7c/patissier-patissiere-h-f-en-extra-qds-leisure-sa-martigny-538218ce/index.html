Full description

The Quartier LôDzé is looking for an extra Pastry / Pastry (M/F) to strengthen its teams. We are looking for a motivated, creative and rigorous person, passionate about pastry and concerned about the quality, freshness and presentation of the products.

Your missions

  • Prepare and make various pastries, desserts and other sweet creations under the supervision of the pastry chef.
  • Make the basic preparations: pasta, creams, biscuits, fillings and other baking components.
  • Provide dressing, finishing and presentation of pastries according to the establishment's standards.
  • Participate in the establishment and organization of production as required.
  • Ensure freshness, quality and good product conservation.
  • Apply revenue, procedures and instructions from the pastry chef.
  • Participate in raw material management and stock monitoring.
  • Work closely with the pastry chef and other team members.
  • Strictly apply food hygiene and safety rules, including HACCP standards.
  • Ensure the cleaning and maintenance of equipment and workspace.

Profile sought

  • Pastry training or equivalent work experience.
  • An experience in artisanal pastry, catering or hotels is an asset.
  • Mastery of basic baking techniques.
  • Creativity, precision and attention to detail.
  • Organizational sense, autonomy and reliability.
  • Ability to work as a team and follow the direction of the pastry chef.
  • Sensitivity to product quality, presentation and freshness.
  • Strict compliance with HACCP hygiene and food safety rules.
  • Flexibility and availability according to the needs of the establishment, including weekends and holidays.

We offer you

  • Reduction for employees in the various outlets
  • 5 weeks of holidays, 6 holidays
  • Salary according to applicable CLC and 13th months.
  • A dynamic and varied working environment.

We will only examine the complete files (CV, cover letter, work certificates, etc.) of the candidates corresponding to the profile sought.
