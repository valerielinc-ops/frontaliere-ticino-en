Full description

During your apprenticeship, you will actively participate in the care of patients in our care services. Accompanied by experienced professionals, you will gradually develop the skills necessary to carry out your future profession.

Your main activities will include

  • Participate in basic care and patient support in daily life activities.
  • Observe patient health and provide relevant information to the health care team.
  • Participate in the conduct of medical and technical care and procedures according to your level of training.
  • Collaborate with health, medical and therapeutic teams.
  • Contribute to patient well-being and comfort.
  • Respect the rules of hygiene, safety, aseptics and professional ethics.
  • You have a strong interest in health care.
  • You show empathy, kindness and respect for others.
  • You enjoy human contact and have good listening and communication skills.
  • You are interested in care, observation of patient health and medical-technical aspects.
  • You are reliable, adaptable and have a common sense of responsibility.
  • You know how to manage stress and evolve in a dynamic environment.
  • You are motivated, curious and eager to learn.
  • You want to get an SWC from ASSC and contribute actively to the quality of care.
  • You're comfortable with computer tools.

During your apprenticeship, you will be able to discover different fields, develop your autonomy and gradually build your professional experience. We offer you:

  • A variety of training in institutions recognized and close to their patients.
  • The opportunity to discover the professional world in a stimulating and constantly evolving environment.
  • Personalised support throughout your learning by committed and experienced trainers.
  • Conditions for learning, progressive autonomy and skills development.
  • The possibility of making a concrete contribution to the quality of care provided to the population of the region.
