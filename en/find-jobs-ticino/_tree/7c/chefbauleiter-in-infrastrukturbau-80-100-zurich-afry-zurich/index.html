Become part of our civil engineering team. We manage, develop, design and plan infrastructure projects of all kinds in railway and tunnel construction. YOUR TASKS

  • Chief construction management or local construction management of major infrastructure projects in German-speaking Switzerland
  • Coordination and control of the work on the construction site and management of the construction management team
  • Responsibility for deadline and contract management as well as for acceptance processes and warranty work
  • Support in project planning or project management
  • Construction management using state-of-the-art working methods / BIM2Field

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Exciting large-scale infrastructure construction projects in Switzerland
  • Independent and self-reliant way of working

  • Familiarity with the common MS Office programs, knowledge of Messerli Bauad is an advantage
  • Customer-oriented thinking, reliable and independent way of working
  • Team-oriented and communicative personality
  • Very good written and spoken German, other language skills are a plus
