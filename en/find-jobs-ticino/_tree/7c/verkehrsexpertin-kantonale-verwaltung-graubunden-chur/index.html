Full description

The Road Traffic Office is responsible for the authorization of vehicles, drivers, and ships, the periodic inspection of vehicles and ships, the issuance of administrative measures against faulty vehicle drivers, and the preparation and collection of traffic taxes. We are looking for the Road Traffic Office / Department of Justice, Security, and Health for a Traffic Expert for Chur, starting on 1st September 2026 or by agreement. Tasks: As an expert for vehicle and driver tests, you will reinforce our technical team. You will conduct theoretical and practical driver tests. You will inspect vehicles for their technical fitness. Requirements: You have a completed training as an Automobile Mechatronics Technician, Agricultural or Construction Machine Mechanic. You are at least 25 years old and have some years of professional experience. This position requires a high level of responsibility, resilience, and empathy. You are communicative, decisive, and appreciate direct customer contact. You are interested in modern traffic topics and are willing to further your professional training. Good manners and a customer-oriented attitude are a plus. Good German language skills - Italian language skills are an advantage. You have held a Swiss driver's license of category B for at least five years.
