International plays a central role within the Group. As a global player, we supply the whole world and make from Chur the central purchase of the goods of all companies outside Germany. As an organizational talent with strong planning skills, you are an important link in the supply chain. We are looking for a dynamic personality who is accustomed to working independently as soon as possible or by agreement, with a quick apprehension to strengthen our five-member disposition team at our Landquart location.

Person in charge of goods disposition (a)

What moves you

  • You are responsible for your own supplier and article portfolio and ensure optimal product availability.
  • You plan requirements, forecasts and promotions and control the stocks with the help of modern planning systems.
  • They monitor open orders, delivery dates and stocks and initiate the necessary measures in case of bottlenecks or deviations.
  • They are in close contact with suppliers as well as internal specialist areas such as purchasing, product management, customer service and logistics.
  • They analyze key figures, identify optimization potential and actively participate in process improvements and digitalization projects.

What you bring

  • Commercial or technical training, ideally in the field of trade, purchasing, logistics or supply chain management
  • At least three years of professional experience, preferably in purchasing / materials management or goods management
  • SAP/ERP knowledge is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of the MS Office\-Palette
  • Fluent German and English skills (C1 level)
  • Customer-oriented, networked and solution-oriented thinking, analytical thinking skills, data-based decision-making, process-oriented working methods
  • assertiveness, strong communication and team skills
