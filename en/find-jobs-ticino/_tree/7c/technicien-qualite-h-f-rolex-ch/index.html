Full description

Introduction

As part of the development of our activities in the canton of Fribourg, we are a pool of talents to accompany the deployment of our new industrial site in Bulle. To this end, we will soon be looking for (M/F) Quality Technician profiles to strengthen our teams.

Responsibilities

  • Lead quality in a manufacturing sector, in close collaboration with operating teams
  • Monitor quality indicators and ensure quality incident handling
  • Managing quality gaps and contributing to the implementation of corrective actions
  • Participate in the qualification of new products and processes
  • Ensure quality relationship with internal clients and suppliers
  • Contributing to continuous improvement

Profile

  • Higher mechanical training, HES type or ES technician
  • Experience in a quality function in the industrial environment
  • Confirmed practice of quality tools, including gap resolution, corrective actions and problem solving
  • Analytical capacity and knowledge of machining, finishing and/or assembly
  • Communication skills and ability to lead team work in close proximity to operation
  • Reactive, curious and rigorous personality, complemented by a strong learning and integration capacity
  • Availability for initial training in Geneva before continuing the mission in the canton of Fribourg

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
