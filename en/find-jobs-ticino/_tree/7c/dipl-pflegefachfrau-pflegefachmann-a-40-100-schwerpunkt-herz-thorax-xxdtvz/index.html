As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

For our bed stations in the clinic Beau-Site (specialist areas of cardiac/thoracic/visceral surgery) we are looking for support!

YOUR TASKS

  • Individually oriented and competent care and care of our patients according to our nursing mission statement
  • Together with our family teams you ensure the best possible quality of care
  • Through our document doctor system, you carry a lot of responsibility and act very independently in clinical decision-making and the measures to be initiated
  • Accompaniment of learners and students as well as nursing documentation are also part of your tasks
  • With us you mainly provide day and late shifts, as we have our own night service team

Your profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing specialist (HF / FH), for foreign diploma with SRK recognition
  • Experience in Acute Hospital
  • Friendly and communicative mindset
  • Good knowledge of German (min. B2)

Your loves for us

  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in the Fitness Abo & Entrance to the indoor pool
