The Position Global Product Planner

The Global HARP planner (Holistic Aligned Replenishment Planning) is part of the Global Planning team and is accountable for ensuring affiliate product availability supplied in the most efficient way. This includes generating robust replenishment plans and aligning requirements with GPP’s to ensure timely hub availability.

Key responsibilities include actively driving replenishment strategies, managing affiliate master data, and governing the process to ensure automated, demand-driven replenishment and optimized stock levels across our global and local network. Furthermore, the GPP serves as the central orchestration link in the Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP) processes to guarantee seamless supply continuity across all regional and central warehouse locations. Through proactive and close collaboration with affiliates and GPP’s, the HARP planner optimizes the replenishment process, monitors affiliate orders and shipments (focus on achieving high ocean shipment ratio).

The Opportunity Hub to affiliate replenishment planning (50%)

  • Supply & inventory optimization: Accountable for creating accurate, robust affiliate supply plans based on demand inputs with efficient MOT processing. Defines product-based inventory strategies (including safety stock parameters) to balance customer service levels, business continuity, and cost efficiency.
  • S&OP governance & performance monitoring: Tracks plan adherence and initiate necessary corrective actions and clarifications within the Supply Review framework of the Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP) process. Prepares for and hosts affiliate planning alignment meetings which are an integrated part of our S&OP process. Continues communication with all stakeholders and regular updates to affiliates.
  • Proactive conflict management: Daily/weekly review of affiliate inventory projections, open orders at the hub, in transit shipments and forecast performance to proactively avoid service or cost issues.
  • Lifecycle & launch management: Oversee the affiliate product lifecycle process to avoid service impact, hub financial impact and costly air shipments

Harmonized Aligned Replenishment Planning (HARP) Governance (20%)

  • HARP strategy & replenishment execution: Drives and manages the globally managed inventory process for assigned product portfolios and countries, ensuring automated, demand-driven replenishment and optimized stock levels across global distribution centers and affiliates.
  • Collaboration & service level assurance: Acts as the primary interface between global supply networks and affiliate partners, continuously monitoring inventory health to maximize product availability while preventing overstock situations.
  • System optimization & analytics: Oversees required system parameters and performance metrics, proactively identifying trends, tracking deviations, and implementing corrective actions to streamline the global replenishment cycle.
