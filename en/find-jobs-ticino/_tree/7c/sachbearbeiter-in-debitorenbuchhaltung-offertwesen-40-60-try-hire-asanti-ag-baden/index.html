Full description

J e t t t b e w e r b e n For our customer in the district of Baden, we are looking for a committed and reliable personality as Accounts Receivable & Quotation Clerk (40-60%) / try&hire

Responsibilities

Accounts Receivable & Administration

  • Recording, checking and posting invoices
  • Preparation of final invoices including enclosures, warranty certificates and service contracts
  • Opening and adapting projects and construction contracts in the ERP system
  • Monitoring incoming payments and supporting the dunning process
  • Creation of construction guarantees
  • Maintaining the address master data in ABACUS
  • Responsible for archiving and filing

Quotation & order processing

  • Supporting the calculator in the preparation of quotations
  • Editing tender and acquisition platforms such as Olmero and SiMap
  • Entering, opening and sending offers
  • Processing quotes, foreign exchange and orders
  • Preparing work contracts and creating order confirmations
  • Obtaining material prices and creating price requests
  • Processing cancellations and active follow-up for open offers
  • Maintaining incoming orders and the order book
  • Maintaining the reference list
  • Obtaining credit reports and checking customer terms and conditions

Administration and reception

  • Supervising the telephone exchange and first point of contact for internal and external contacts
  • Administrative and organisational tasks

Your Insight

  • Commercial training or a comparable qualification
  • Ideally, experience in the construction industry, main construction or ancillary construction industry or in a construction-related company
  • Practical experience in quotation, order processing and/or accounts receivable
  • Understanding of commercial processes relating to construction projects, work contracts and invoicing
  • Safe use of MS Office; knowledge of ABACUS and ERP systems is a plus
  • Independent, structured and precise working methods
  • Organisational talent with a pronounced flair for numbers
  • Communication skills, teamwork skills and a friendly demeanour
  • Pleasure in taking responsibility and coordinating different areas of responsibility

What to expect

  • A varied and responsible position with a wide range of commercial tasks
  • A collegial, well-positioned team in a down-to-earth construction company
  • Versatile interfaces and a lively working day
  • Infrastructure
  • Attractive employment conditions and good social benefits
  • Entry into the Try & Hire model with the possibility of a subsequent permanent position with mutual fit

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Aargau?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Aargau's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Aargau's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
