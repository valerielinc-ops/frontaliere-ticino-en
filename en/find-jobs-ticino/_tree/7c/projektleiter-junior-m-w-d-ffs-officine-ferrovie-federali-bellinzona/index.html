What we offer.

An innovative working environment in which you have a central role in defining and implementing the necessary changes to meet the needs of our customers in the name of the corporate purpose of Production Travelers "Your journey

  • our pride" and its winning spirit "Quality and safety

A work, stimulating, varied and responsible at the SBB plant in Bellinzona; a centre of competence for the modernization, renewal and maintenance of electrons and their components.

  • As Head of Project you are responsible for defining activities, planning tasks to achieve ultimate goal for the benefit and service of our customers in safety, quality, time and cost.
  • Facilitate transversal work between departments in a matrial organization, between sectors and divisions both locally and in all regions of Switzerland.
  • This function is sought in the context of the definition of industrialization of projects for the modernization and revision of rolling stock and covers a fixed-term contract of 3 years.

What you can move.

  • Lead complex projects in the maintenance and modernization of our fleets by defining implementation activities, operational and economic objectives.
  • Define processes, prepare documentation and forge the employees involved.
  • You are responsible for the definition and creation of the Supply Chain, with particular attention to Standardization, process optimization, keeping the time and production costs defined, following the company strategy and with an end-to-end vision.
  • The solutions, methods identified and implemented must be sustainable in all areas using FFS Best Practices; applying waste elimination using Lean techniques including 5S.
  • You are the reference person in all business areas until the end of the industrialization phase, oversights, monitors and undertakes the necessary measures to maintain the costs, quality and time defined to complete customer satisfaction and setting up the necessary reports for stakeholders.
  • Finally, you manage the risks, anticipating the problems with the appropriate measures.

What you bring with you.

  • You are a dynamic, organized, precise, positive person with good communication skills, collaboration and negotiation, responsible and with Leadership skills.
  • Ability in Lean Standardization and Problem Solving techniques, capable of solving and anticipating problems, with strong skills in identifying the real causes of problems and identifying opportunities.
  • You have work experience of 2-7 years in a field similar to the railway sector.
  • You have experienced a proven project management experience and experience in Supply Chain.
  • You have completed an ETH/EPFL technical training - SUP/FH or equivalent training with adequate experience.
  • You have a good knowledge of Italian and good knowledge of German spoken and written (B2) and English (B2).
