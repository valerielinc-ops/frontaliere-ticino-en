Full description

La Tour Hospital is a high-level, private, independent and human-scale acute care facility. He employs more than 1'200 employees and works with more than 550 registered doctors.

Our Endoscopy department welcomes outpatients and hospitalized patients for digestive and pulmonary endoscopic examinations. The team intervenes within the department, the operating unit and the Intensive Care. It works in close collaboration with doctors, in a climate of trust and with a high level of safety and hygiene requirements.

In order to complete our team as part of a maternity leave replacement, we recruit one of the following:

We recruit one

Nurse (-E) in Endoscopy – CDM 10 months at 70% (ref: 2041)

Your mission

You ensure the reception, safety and care of patients before, during and after their endoscopic examination. As such, you are responsible for:

  • Assist the physician in the endoscopic actions and administration of sedation, ensuring the conformity of the medical devices used;
  • Monitor the patient after the examination and provide the physician with all relevant information;
  • To achieve the care of your own role and on prescription, in accordance with the hygiene and aseptic rules, adapting them to the needs of each patient;
  • Ensure targeted, written and oral transmissions and proper maintenance of the care record;
  • Coordinate and organize the daily programme, in conjunction with doctors and hospital workers;
  • To contribute to the proper functioning of the service (orders, lapses, storage) and the seizure of services;
  • Participate in the seminars and projects of the service and institution.

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • Diplôme d'infirmier (-ère) HES or training deemed equivalent (DE France);
  • Right of practice in the canton of Geneva (recognition of the Red Cross for foreign diplomas);
  • Professional experience in endoscopy, an asset;
  • Case "Practice of endoscopy", a real asset;
  • Rigor, organizational sense, adaptability and team spirit;
  • Meaning of contact and service in a private establishment.

We offer you

  • Type of contract: contract with a maximum duration of 10 months (replacement of maternity leave)
  • Part-time: 70%
  • Effective date: January 1, 2027
  • Workplace: La Tour Hospital, Meyrin
  • Hours : between 8 and 10am / day (Monday to Friday)

Do you want to give new impetus to your professional career?

Join La Tour Hospital and take part in a team that places quality of care, patient safety and excellence at the heart of its priorities.
