Contractors & Audits Specialist — IBSA Institut Biochimique

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The resource will be introduced within the Contractors & Audits group and will collaborate with the Quality Compliance Team of the Quality department.

Main responsibilities

  • Qualification of third parties such as CMO, Customers/Distributors, logistic service providers, laboratories etc.: planning, execution audits, reports, CAPAs and related follow up
  • Management of Expertise: planning, internal audit execution, reports, action plans and follow up
  • Support to the Team during the preparation and follow-up of Inspections/Audit by Authority/Clients
  • Team support for the implementation and monitoring of quality systems related to branches, trading, branches, etc.
  • Support for the Team for the implementation, maintenance and monitoring of the Company Quality System according to cGMP/GDP, ISO and current regulations. In particular: document management (revision and approval of Procedures, Analytical Methods, Reports, Validations, etc.); management of quality flows such as Deviations, Change control, OOS, CAPA, Reclaims etc.

Profile required

  • Bachelor of Science (preferably in Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Pharmaceutical Techniques)
  • At least 5 years of experience in the role played within pharmaceutical companies (previous experiences with sterile productions are considered a plus).
  • In-depth knowledge of cGxP, reference regulations (Eudralex Vol. 4, CFR 21 Part 11, 210, 211) and guidelines for Medical Devices (ISO 13484 etc)
  • Excellent knowledge of English written and spoken
  • Excellent knowledge of terms, tools and methodologies Quality
  • Good knowledge of basic computer systems
  • Excellent relational and communication skills
  • Guidance on problem solving and propensity to collaboration
  • Proactivity, dynamism and flexibility
  • Ability to work independently
  • Availability to travel
