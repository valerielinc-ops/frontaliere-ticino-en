Full description

designs. Organizing. Get started Take responsibility for our workshop!

As a result of the retirement of our long-term employee, the municipality of Täsch is looking for a committed and responsible personality for the newly created function as manager/workshop. With this position, we want to further develop our workshop and building maintenance organizationally. We are looking for a practice-oriented leader who keeps an overview, structures processes and at the same time actively participates in daily operations.

Manager/workshop 100% With management tasks in the fire brigade

Your tasks

  • You lead our workshop and building maintenance team
  • You take on management tasks in our fire department
  • You are responsible for compliance with occupational safety and the maintenance of our vehicles and machines
  • You work as a workshop employee actively in day-to-day business

If you have the following qualifications, the better

  • Completed craft or technical vocational training (automotive or metal construction industry, mason, carpenter, or other craft training)
  • Firefighting training/experience in the rank of officer
  • Teamwork, communication skills and friendly treatment of the population
  • Readiness for flexible work assignments, in particular in the context of winter service
  • Practical professional and managerial experience

We offer you

  • A varied and responsible task
  • The possibility of organizing and developing the workshop and building maintenance
  • A modern infrastructure and flexible working hours with a good work-life balance
  • Generous holiday regime

Job opening

As of February 1, 2027 or by arrangement.

Application

Applications with all usual documents are addressed to the municipality of Täsch, to send until October 20, 2026.

For further questions, our head of department Jan Bergamin is at your disposal.
