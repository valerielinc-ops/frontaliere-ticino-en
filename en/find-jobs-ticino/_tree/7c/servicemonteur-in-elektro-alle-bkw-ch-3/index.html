Full description

We make habitats worth living.

With around 440 employees spread over 10 locations, swisspro AG works with the highest level of performance and constant innovation. Do you want to do independent service work with the latest technologies? Together we ensure stable and secure connections for our customers!

Your tasks

  • The renovation, conversion and new installation of low- and high-voltage power plants are among your main tasks.
  • You are also responsible for the support and advice of private customers and SMEs.
  • You carry out small orders and service work independently.
  • Maintenance work on telephone, computer and power plants completes your responsibility profile.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed basic training as an electrical installer: in EFZ.
  • Ideally, you will bring at least two years of professional experience, preferably in the service area.
  • You enjoy working precisely and cleanly with high quality standards.
  • Flexibility and resilience as well as independence and high customer orientation characterize your nature.
  • Your appearance is well maintained and you have good manners.
  • You have the driving license Cat. B and good PC knowledge.
