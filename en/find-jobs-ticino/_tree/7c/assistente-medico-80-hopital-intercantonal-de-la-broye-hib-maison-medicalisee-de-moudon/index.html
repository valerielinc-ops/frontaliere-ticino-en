Full description

Thanks to its 800 employees, the Intercantonal Hospital of Broye (HIB) provides an acute care and rehabilitation mission. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative health institution striving for excellence for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges that await it, the HIB on the Moudon site, the Policlinique is looking for a: Medical assistant at 80% Start of work: immediately or to be agreed. We thank you for sending us your complete application file by applying online. Mansioni

  • Ensure the organization and operation of medical and paramedical consultations within the HIB Policlinic in Moudon:
  • Welcome, direct and announce the arrival of patients to the various doctors
  • Organize all administrative aspects linked to the Policlinic (preparation of the patient file, management of insurance reports, scan the various medical documents)
  • Organize the doctors' diaries
  • Write consultation reports and invoicing
  • Take charge patients (technical assistance and care for consultations, ECGs, samples and injections and basic care)
  • Carry out laboratory analyzes and development of digital x-rays Required
  • You have a federal certificate of capacity (CFC) as a medical assistant, as well as solid experience in a medical office.
  • Equipped with a sense of listening and contact, your organizational and relational skills allow you to offer a quality welcome and to adapt to the demands of the field of an outpatient clinic.
  • A very good command of French (spoken and written), as well as the use of usual IT tools is essential. (Institutional software: Opale, Soarian, Ultragenda and Winscribe.
