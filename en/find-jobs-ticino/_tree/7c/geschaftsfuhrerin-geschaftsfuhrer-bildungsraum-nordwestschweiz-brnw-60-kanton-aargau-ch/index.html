Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • They manage the office of the Education Area Northwest Switzerland and coordinate between the different levels of education as well as between the participating cantons Aargau, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt and Solothurn
  • They help to shape and further develop the education system
  • They advise and support the Government Committee and the Leadership Conference Elementary School, lead the Management Committee and develop the basis for decision-making
  • They are responsible for business planning and finance and coordinate national education businesses
  • You maintain the coordination of educational space topics and internal and external communication by writing session invitations and minutes, organizing events, providing media documents or maintaining the website

What you bring

  • You have a university degree and are interested in educational topics
  • They enjoy working in networks and at the interface between different bodies
  • Your strengths include independent work, written expression and organizational talent

Specific

  • It awaits you a challenging activity in favour of the education system of the four cantons
  • The position is for the time being until 31. December 2028, but there is the possibility of a subsequent employment

The elementary school department in the Department of Education, Culture and Sports is responsible for education at the elementary school level in the Canton of Aargau. Kindergarten, primary school, district, secondary and secondary school as well as special forms of primary school belong to the department's area of responsibility. Important tasks include the development and implementation of educational policy measures as well as the resourcing, supervision and support of the Aargau schools. www.ag.ch/volksschule
