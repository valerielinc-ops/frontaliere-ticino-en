Full description

Holcim (Switzerland) SA is one of Switzerland's leading suppliers of innovative and sustainable construction solutions in the fields of structural engineering, civil engineering and infrastructure. With around 1200 employees in more than 60 locations across Switzerland, we produce concrete, gravel and cement and recycle demolition materials into resource-efficient products.

Operations Manager Concrete - Valaisan region

Location : Sion, Valais

With us, you can expect a wide range of demanding tasks, in which we always pay attention to health, safety and environmental awareness. We attach great importance to the fact that you can reconcile professional and private life. An atmosphere of collegial work, fair conditions of employment and the promotion of your personal development are obvious to us.

Description

In order to ensure a safe, efficient and customer-oriented operation within our concrete plants in Valais, we are looking for a person who can organise the activities of the site, accompany a team in their daily lives and maintain smooth operation, in accordance with our HSE requirements and quality.

What awaits you

  • Supervise concrete production, quality, safety and operation of sites
  • Plan, accompany and coordinate the team, fleet and maintenance
  • Ensure deliveries, pumping and monitoring of quality controls with the laboratory
  • Optimize the processes, reception and attractiveness of outlets
  • Collaborate with AGG/RMX managers, business teams and clients
  • Participate in site visits and propose suitable solutions
  • Contribute to financial targets (costs, volumes, margins, EBITDA)

What you bring

  • Technical training (construction/building), ideally supplemented by concrete formation
  • Experience in concrete production or on construction sites; knowledge of aggregates is an asset
  • Knowledge of cement/concrete/granulat products and local market
  • Relationship and customer orientation
  • Ability to mentor, train and support a team in its activities
  • Responsibility, autonomy and decision-making capacity
  • Mastery of IT tools
  • French C1
  • German/English = an asset
