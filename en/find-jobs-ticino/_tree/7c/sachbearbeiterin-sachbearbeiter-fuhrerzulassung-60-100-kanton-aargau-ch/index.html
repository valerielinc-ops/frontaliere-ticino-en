Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Issuing of identity documents (driving licences, training licences, etc.)
  • Advising customers at the counter and on the telephone
  • Disposition of theory and leadership examinations
  • General administrative activities in the back office
  • Control and archiving of documents

What you bring

  • Retail trade specialist with trade school or similar
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in direct customer contact
  • Advantage at 60%
  • Training with 100%
  • High load capacity and exact working method
  • Strong customer orientation

Your work environment

With its ten departments and more than 2,400 employees, the Department of Economics and Home Affairs performs important tasks in the police, law enforcement, penal system, migration and integration, community supervision and economics. The Secretariat-General provides the central services for the department and is the interface with the general administration.

With your work, you make an important contribution to road safety in the Canton of Aargau. You can expect a versatile and responsible job in a committed, dynamic team.

In the driver’s license, you accompany customers on their way to the learning and driving license. You check and process applications, issue identity documents, process foreign driving licenses and create parking permits. The disposition of leadership and theory exams is also one of your tasks.

Whether at the counter, on the phone or by e-mail – you advise our customers competently and friendly and ensure that their concerns are reliably dealt with.
