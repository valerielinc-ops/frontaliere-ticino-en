Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organizations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Operation and monitoring of complex systems as a plant operator (e.g. plant for the thermal disposal of explosives and ammunition components, water jet cutting plant)
  • Dismantling of ammunition components
  • Responsible for cleaning, servicing and servicing the assigned facilities
  • Ensuring internal logistics (e.g. transport of materials with forklifts, packaging, labelling and palletizing of materials)
  • Ensuring quality in your own area of responsibility (e.g. carrying out number of pieces checks and documentation of production volumes)
  • Professional management of the EOD specialists assigned to the facilities or in their own work process

You bring that with you

  • Craft background and technical understanding
  • Experience in dealing with explosives and ammunition components (e.g. several years of experience as an EOD Specialist in a comparable function, training as an ammunition specialist or weapon specialist)
  • Flair for modern IT infrastructure (e.g. plant control and plant monitoring)
  • Explosive certificate and forklift certificate
  • Readiness for shift operation
  • Category B driving licence required
  • Proper reputation (is a prerequisite for security inspection)

Over the area

The Altdorf site specializes in the dismantling of ammunition and the thermal disposal of ammunition and explosives and represents an industrial core capability of RUAG. Both fully automated and semi-automated processes are used, supplemented by manual dismantling and sorting processes to ensure safe and efficient processing.

A dynamic, highly qualified team ensures the professional handling and processing of ammunition. The work at the site is highly technically oriented and is aimed specifically at the special requirements of ammunition disposal.
