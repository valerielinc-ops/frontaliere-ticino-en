Full description

ETAVIS Grossenbacher AG is one of the leading companies in the Swiss electrical installation and telecommunications market in Eastern Switzerland. To maintain our high standard in the future as well, we are looking for a customer-oriented, flexible, and committed person like you!

Your scope of impact

  • Lead electrical installation teams as well as versatile projects in the commercial, industrial, and service sectors
  • Coordinate work with construction management, suppliers, and tradespeople from other industries
  • Carry out new installations, maintenance work, and repairs
  • Pass on your in-depth technical expertise to our apprentices

What you bring

  • Completed vocational training as an electrician EFZ or equivalent qualification recognized in Switzerland
  • Several years of professional experience
  • Strong quality awareness, a representative demeanor, and an independent way of working

What you can expect

  • You matter to us - we offer you an open, appreciative, and family-like environment.
  • We are successful - with us you have a long-term and secure perspective.
  • We share our success with you - through employee shares and bonuses.
  • Competent managers ensure exciting projects and smooth workflows.
  • With us you have diverse further training opportunities - we are happy to support you.

The branch in Valbella specializes in service and maintenance as well as high-voltage and low-voltage systems.

Who we are

ETAVIS is the optimal partner for innovative electrical engineering, communications, and automation solutions in Switzerland and Liechtenstein. We offer customized, high-quality solutions for business customers of all industries and sizes, as well as individually tailored services for private customers. As part of the VINCI Group, we benefit from global innovative power and are at the same time locally anchored with our around 60 branches. As the largest small company, we operate more in a human and family-like way - here every individual counts. With over 2,400 employees and several hundred apprentices, we work every day towards a more sustainable, better-connected world.
