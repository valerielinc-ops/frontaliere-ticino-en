Full description

Class and annual salary (including 13th monthly)

2 47'348.-- / 72'822.--

Tasks

  • supervises the work of employees/workers in ordinary management and possible conflicts
  • coordinates the group's activities by defining work plans and managing attendance and absences. In collaboration with the catering service head prepares and updates the annual work plan (holidays) and, in case of new recruitment, select the staff of the service
  • It plans and imparts training to newly recruited staff and, in collaboration with the Head of Service, takes care of the continuing training of its members and cooperates in the training of apprentices. In addition, it conducts annual and periodic evaluation interviews of staff
  • co-operates with the Head of Chef, with the responsible chefs and the Head of staff to the general services to ensure the smooth running and successful service
  • cleanliness and disinfections according to the provisions and the program established, in compliance with institutional rules, and controls the correct execution of the work done by the collaborators of the general services entrusted to him/her. It also checks the correct use of products, equipment and machines, ensuring compliance with the provisions relating to individual protection devices (DPIs) and ensuring safety at work
  • manages the equipment and the material supplied in the spaces of its competence (orders, inventories, etc.) and rediges and forwards through the service routes the requests for repairs and anomalies found in its area of competence
  • collaborates with the Head of Service in the preparation of documentation for the quality system
  • involves and controls the users of CARL in the execution of simple reorganization and cleaning activities, in collaboration with the caregiver

Requirements

  • Federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as a technical/cleaning operator or (AFC) as a catering worker or (AFC) of similar professions in the catering sector
  • multiannual experience in the catering sector
  • proven experience in conducting and coordinating a working group, with the ability to organize ordinary and extraordinary activities, distribute tasks and oversee their performance
  • flexibility, organizational and relational skills
  • good relations and communication skills and attitude to collaboration
  • availability to work in contact with people in the sociopsychiatric field
  • knowledge of Ticino
  • good knowledge of the Italian language (conversation and editing)

Special remarks

  • degree of employment: 90%
  • Workplace: Mendrisio
  • availability by the candidate to work at irregular times and to work on shifts (weekends and holidays included)
  • start date: to be agreed

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required additional documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements
