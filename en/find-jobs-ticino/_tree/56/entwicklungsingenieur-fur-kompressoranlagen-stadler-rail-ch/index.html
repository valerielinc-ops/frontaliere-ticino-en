Full description

PROFILE

You have a completed degree in mechanical engineering, vehicle engineering or a comparable technical discipline.

You bring several years of professional experience in machine, plant or system construction.

Ideally, you have knowledge of compressed air generation, compressed air processing or comparable technical systems.

You have experience working with 3D CAD systems, preferably CATIA V5.

You work in a structured, self-responsible manner and tackle technical challenges in a solution-oriented manner.

You communicate safely with internal and external stakeholders and feel comfortable in an international project environment.

TASKS

You are responsible for the evaluation, design and integration of compressor systems for our rail vehicles.

You will accompany technical solutions along the entire project cycle from the offer phase to commissioning and construction support.

Together with system suppliers, you will develop innovative and standardized solutions for our compressor systems and drive their continuous development.

You create, check and take responsibility for technical documentation, specifications, CAD data and drawings.

You work closely with suppliers, supervise their qualification and carry out technical approvals at key suppliers.

You evaluate customer requirements, estimate technical efforts and present solution concepts to customers and internal stakeholders.
