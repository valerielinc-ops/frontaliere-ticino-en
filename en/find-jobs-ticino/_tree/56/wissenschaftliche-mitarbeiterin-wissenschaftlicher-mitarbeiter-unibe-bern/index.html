Full description

UniBE is renowned. With us, you benefit from the good reputation of a long-established institution worldwide.

Responsibilities

  • Contribution to teaching and research, primarily in the fields of business law

(corporate law, financial market law, corporate group law) and animal law

  • Contribution to publications, presentations, and public statements
  • Preparation for and participation in teaching sessions
  • Preparation and marking of written examinations and minute-taking for oral examinations
  • Supervision of seminar papers and Master's theses, including preliminary marking

Requirements

  • Completed university degree in law (MLaw or bar examination certificate)
  • Committed and team-oriented personality
  • Independent and meticulous working style
  • Very good German language skills

Opportunity to write a doctoral dissertation within the regulatory framework Application and contact With us, you create value. For yourself and society.

Who are we At the Institute of Business Law (IWR), we conduct research, teach, and publish in the fields of Swiss, European, and international business law. The position of a research assistant at the chair of Prof. Dr. Peter V. Kunz is to be filled as of January 1, 2027, or by agreement.
