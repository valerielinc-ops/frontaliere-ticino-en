Full description

For our Research & Development team, we are looking for our future intern for a period of 6 months, starting in May 2027. During your internship, your main mission will be to contribute to the continuous improvement of business processes, in an environment where excellence, accuracy and customer satisfaction are at the heart of the concerns.

In this context, you will be responsible for

  • Participate in the analysis of departmental processes and interfaces (process maps, VSM, SIPOC, ISHIKAWA, data collection)
  • Identify areas for improvement in terms of quality, time and costs
  • Propose and implement concrete solutions (Lean, 5S, Kaizen), as well as specifications for the implementation of dashboards and indicators
  • Develop a tool to help raise ideas for improvement and operational excellence AP models to enrich the toolbox
  • Finally, you will have to collaborate with different multidisciplinary teams
  • You hold or are in the process of obtaining a university degree in continuous improvement, quality or industrial management
  • You know the Lean, Six Sigma or equivalent tools, as well as the Office pack
  • Rigoury and concerned about detail, you have good relational and communication skills
  • You have a perfect command of French both orally and in writing, English is a plus

  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The ability to work remotely depending on the function
  • A business restaurant
  • An advantageous PBL
