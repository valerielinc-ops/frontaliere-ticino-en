Coop Genossenschaft — Schafisheim, AG, Svizzera

  • Job opening: by agreement
  • Field of expertise: Production / Technology
  • Place of work: Coop, Rupperswilerstrasse, 5503 Schafisheim

Tasks

  • You take care of the maintenance of our buildings, facilities, machines and equipment and carry out minor repairs yourself.
  • You keep our outdoor facilities in order and also take part in gardening work.
  • Are external craftsmen: inside us, accompany and coordinate their work on site.
  • You will assist with events and ensure that our meeting rooms are ready when needed.
  • You take care of the distribution of office supplies and keep track of the warehouse management of our external cleaning companies.
  • You walk with open eyes through the area, recognize the need for action and take care of it.

Requirements

  • You have completed an apprenticeship as a specialist / specialist in company maintenance and already some years of professional experience in domestic service.
  • You are skilled in craftsmanship and like to tackle yourself.
  • You work independently, reliably and solution-oriented.
  • You like to be in contact with people and act friendly and safe towards different stakeholders.
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing.
  • You can use Word, Excel and Outlook safely.
  • You have the driving license of category B. Category BE is a plus.
