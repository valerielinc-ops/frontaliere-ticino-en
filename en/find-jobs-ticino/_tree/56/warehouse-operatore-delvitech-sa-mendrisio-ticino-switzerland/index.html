Full description

Join Delvitech's warehouse in Ticino: handle goods receipt, packing, forklift operations and stock movements for our production. Job Summary The Warehouse Operator will be responsible for the daily handling of goods, including loading and unloading materials, unpacking incoming items and packing goods for internal or external shipment. The role includes managing warehouse locations, performing inventory control and registering stock movements in the company ERP system. The operator will collaborate with production, purchasing and logistics teams to ensure material availability and efficient warehouse operations. Key Responsibilities

  • Goods handling: load and unload incoming and outgoing goods using forklifts or other equipment.
  • Unpacking and packing: unpack incoming materials, check for damage and sort goods by type and destination; pack finished goods or components safely for internal use or shipping.
  • Warehouse management: organize storage locations and maintain a clean and safe warehouse environment.
  • Inventory control: conduct regular stock counts and monitor inventory accuracy.
  • ERP usage: register goods receipts, shipments and stock movements in the ERP system.
  • Material identification: ensure proper labeling and traceability of all stored materials.
  • Coordination: collaborate with production and purchasing to ensure a smooth material flow.
  • Continuous improvement: propose improvements to warehouse layout, inventory accuracy and packing efficiency. Requirements
  • High school diploma or equivalent technical background.
  • Proven experience in warehouse operations, including packing, unpacking and material handling.
  • Familiarity with ERP systems for stock management (knowledge of Progel is a plus).
  • Understanding of inventory principles and warehouse organization.
  • Attention to detail, good organizational skills and ability to work autonomously.
  • Team player with effective communication skills.
  • Good computer skills.
  • Basic knowledge of English. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
