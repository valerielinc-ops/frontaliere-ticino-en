Full description

Klinik Gut is an established private clinic for orthopedics and trauma surgery. We look after our national and international customers at the clinic locations in St. Moritz and Fläsch, as well as in the practice locations in Chur and Buchs, Ascona and Zurich. We are looking for someone for our Fläsch location as of January 1, 2027 or by appointment Employee Technical Service North Bünden 80-100% Your main tasks are In this versatile role, you and the team will ensure that everything runs smoothly in everyday technical and operational life. Your area of ​​responsibility is broad and should be helped to shape it.

  • Maintenance, servicing and rectification of technical defects in buildings, machines, devices and facilities
  • Execution of general repair, maintenance and carpentry work
  • Participation in day-to-day technical business and taking on various all-round tasks
  • Coordination and support of external specialist agencies and craft businesses
  • Collaboration on access, key and parking issues
  • Internal postal service and professional disposal
  • Administrative work and documentation in the relevant systems

You bring with you

  • Completed craft or technical training, for example as a business maintenance specialist, plumber, carpenter or electrician, or a comparable qualification. Alternatively, you have several years of professional experience in the technical field.
  • Enjoyment of a varied everyday work routine and a strong flair for practical all-round activities
  • Independent, reliable and solution-oriented way of working
  • Sense of responsibility, flexibility and organizational talent
  • Friendly and service-oriented demeanor
  • Good user knowledge in dealing with digital tools
  • Driving license category B

You can look forward to that You can expect a varied job with a lot of personal responsibility and creative freedom in a small, committed team. With us you can't expect a rigid distribution of tasks: We want to use your experience and strengths in a targeted manner and are open to structuring responsibilities within the team accordingly. Interested? Would you like to find out more about the position? Pascal Hirt, Head of Technical Services North Bünden, will be happy to give you information on 081 595 55 71. We look forward to receiving your complete application by email to y.rueesch@klinik-gut.ch
