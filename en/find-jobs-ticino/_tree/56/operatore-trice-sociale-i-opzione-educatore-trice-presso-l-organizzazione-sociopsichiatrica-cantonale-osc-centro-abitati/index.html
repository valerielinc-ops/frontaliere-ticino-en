Full description

Tasks

  • performs the educational care interventions and tasks established and agreed upon within the framework of team work intended to promote and maintain the state of health, stimulating the resocialization and personal autonomy of the resident(s)
  • participates, depending on the objectives envisaged in the educational project, in the rehabilitative activities and social and professional integration of the resident(s)
  • collaborates and maintains regular contact with both the other health and social workers caring for the resident(s), as well as with the family or guardian
  • performs and records on the appropriate documents the entry evaluations, the educational work and the development of the resident, as well as participates in the summary and supervision meetings scheduled in the Housing Unit or Inclusive Professional Areas (API)
  • collaborates with the Social and Health Schools in the training of the student(s) in compliance with the internship objectives
  • manages and takes care of the order and cleanliness of the sanitary material, the equipment provided, the medicines in the infirmary of the residences and API
  • carries out the medical prescriptions and therapeutic interventions authorized by his/her specific training, or by special instruction received and verified by the direct superiors; complies with all rules of hygiene and ensures quality logistical conditions within the sheltered housing or API ##
  • Requirements
  • diploma of social worker/ social worker SUP (option: educator/educator) or equivalent qualification
  • if in possession of foreign qualification recognition by SEFRI (State Secretariat for Education, Research and Innovation)
  • previous experience in intermediate socio-educational and rehabilitative facilities
  • ability to work in a team, critical thinking skills, creativity, proactive attitude, flexibility empathy and interpersonal skills
  • ability to enter and stay in relationships even in crisis situations
  • ability to elaborate and support rehabilitation projects in compliance with educational-pedagogical principles
  • knowledge of the territory, institutions and the socio-medical network of Canton Ticino
  • perfect command of the Italian language and knowledge of another official language ## Special remarks
  • employment relationship: full-time or part-time, in their application the candidate is asked to indicate the desired percentage
  • place of employment: CARL, Mendrisio
  • willingness on the part of the candidate to work irregular hours and shifts (holidays, night)
  • start date: to be determined ## Conditions of application
  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application will not be considered valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (cover letter and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and work certificates), additional documentation may be requested at a later date (criminal record with corresponding self-certification and self-certification on health status) ## Conditions of employment
  • appointment or assignment: are those provided for in the Law on the Ordering of State Employees and Teachers of March 15, 1995 (LORD) and its Regulations. Any changes in the law concerning salary and organization remain explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to the needs of the service ## Expiration date May 19, 2026 Further information can be obtained from Mr. Pellandini Laurent, tel. 091 816 58 01.
