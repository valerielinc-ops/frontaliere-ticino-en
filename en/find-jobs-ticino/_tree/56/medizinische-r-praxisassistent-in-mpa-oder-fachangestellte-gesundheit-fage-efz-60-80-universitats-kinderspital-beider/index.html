Full description

The University Children's Hospital of Basel (UKBB) is the university centre of excellence for pediatric and adolescent medicine as well as for teaching and research in northwestern Switzerland. You will find us at Spitalstrasse 33 in Basel. Do you want to use your medical expertise in a versatile pediatric environment and appreciate direct contact with children, adolescents and their families? To extend various consultation hours in our pediatric polyclinic, we are looking for 1. January 2027 a committed, open-minded and responsible personality. You can expect a varied working day in which you can contribute your experience, work independently and contribute together with an interdisciplinary team to a professional and sensitive care of our patients.

Entry date: 01.01.2027

Their tasks

  • Ensuring a smooth process in the polyclinic
  • Assistance in the office hour/speak hour assistance
  • Independent conduct of investigations
  • Performing various tests
  • Measurement and documentation of vital parameters
  • Implementation of vaccination
  • Venous and capillary blood draws in patients of all ages
  • Accompaniment and guidance of trainees

Your profile

  • Completed training as MPA / FaGe
  • Experience in pediatrics is beneficial
  • efficient, independent and flexible working methods
  • High sense of responsibility and resilience
  • Strong professional and social skills
  • Very good communication skills
  • Great interest in interdisciplinary cooperation
  • good knowledge in dealing with KISIM as well as the common PC programs
  • very good knowledge in German language in word and writing, other foreign languages (French, English, Italian) advantageous

Our offer

  • Individual training
  • Possibility of hospitation
  • Working hours Monday to Friday
  • Varied and challenging activity
  • Participation in change
  • Opportunity to participate in internal and external training
