Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate - that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company now employs more than 2,500 people from over 80 countries, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glarus. Against a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates and develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The "Retail" department comprises over 250 chocolate shops in over 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to be part of it? Then come to us. You can expect office organization: coordinating appointments, handling correspondence, managing documents, and preparing meetings. Communication: conducting phone calls, processing emails, and managing customers and business partners. Commercial tasks: creating and checking invoices, triggering orders, and comparing offers. Administration & Support: supporting projects, planning trips, and organizing events. Data & Software Management: working with office programs, recording data, and maintaining systems. You bring with your school diploma: at least a high school diploma or a real school diploma. Organizational skills: structured, careful, and reliable work. Communication strength: good verbal and written expression skills, as well as a friendly demeanor. Basic computer knowledge: first experiences with MS Office and interest in commercial processes. Motivation: learning ability, commitment, and interest in office and administrative tasks. We offer you an exciting work environment with motivated colleagues A position with a lot of personal responsibility An open and respectful feedback culture Generous discounts on our products Chocolate at the workplace Good accessibility of the locations If you're interested, please apply to us. Name, Position, Phone Number, are at your disposal for any questions.
