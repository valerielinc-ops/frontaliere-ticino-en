Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss delivery and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals and retailers).

Tasks

  • You collect telephone customer orders in the ERP system
  • You handle quality complaints and provide transparent customer information
  • You support our demanding customers on the phone or by written medium
  • You support the webshop customers and advise them on the self-service possibilities of Transgourmet
  • You support internal customers according to service catalogue and processes
  • You recognize customer needs, capture them and forward them

Requirements

  • You have completed a commercial education, an education in customer dialogue or an equivalent education
  • You bring your first professional experience in a comparable function – ideally in the food and/or catering sector
  • German is your mother tongue and you have very good knowledge of Italian and/or French (minor level B2)
  • You are well versed in dealing with IT tools as well as digital products and systems (e.g. webshop and apps)
  • You have a high level of social skills as well as a pleasant and respectful communication ability
  • Your way of working is careful and exact
  • You have healthy assertiveness and a strong resilience
  • You are flexible. Our accessibility is from Monday to Friday from 07:00 to 17:30 and on Saturday from 07:00 to 12:00

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
