Full description

USZ Campus

per 1 March 2027 or by agreement

For the further development and strategic coordination of our diverse research activities, this position offers a unique combination of research management and independent scientific activity. In equal parts, you contribute to the organizational and strategic success of our research and at the same time pursue your own scientific projects in the field of ORL.

Their tasks

Research management (50%)

  • Strategic and organizational support of the Clinic Directorate in research issues
  • Support and coordination of eight research groups without direct professional leadership responsibility
  • Professional and personnel management of a research administration team
  • Budget planning, third-party management and resource management
  • Support for clinical trials, ethics procedures and research management issues
  • Coordination of scientific cooperation and promotion of young scientists
  • Maintenance and further development of the ISO 9001-certified quality management system in the field of research as well as coordination and support of internal and external audits
  • Quality assurance, reporting and further development of administrative research processes

Own research (50%)

  • Independent development and implementation of scientific projects
  • Establishment and further development of an independent research focus
  • Acquisition of third-party funding, scientific publications and supervision of early-stage researchers

Your profile

  • Completed university studies with dissertation (Dr. med., PhD or equivalent)
  • Proven scientific experience and publication activity
  • Organizational talent, initiative and strong communication and team skills
  • Good German and very good English skills
  • Experience and knowledge in research management, quality management, third-party funding recruitment and management and human resources management

We offer

A varied position in an internationally networked university research environment with modern research infrastructures, diverse collaborations and 50% protected time for own scientific projects and research. In addition, there is the possibility to participate in university teaching as well as supervision of master theses and dissertations. For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered.
