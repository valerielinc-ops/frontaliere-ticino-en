Full description

Own the technical documentation of Delvitech's AI optical inspection products and turn complex engineering into clear, useful content. Job Summary You will own the technical documentation of Delvitech's AOI (Automatic Optical Inspection) products. Working closely with our hardware and software developers, you will turn complex technical information into clear, accurate documents and use them to train our Application and Pre-Sales (HPT) Engineers. The role is the company reference for structured product knowledge. Location: Mendrisio, Switzerland, 100% on-site. Reports to the Product Team Coordinator (R&D). Key Responsibilities

  • Write and maintain user guides, release notes, application notes and machine operating procedures.
  • Collect information from hardware and software developers through interviews, reviews and product testing.
  • Keep documentation aligned with every software and hardware release (versioning and change control).
  • Prepare training materials and run training sessions for Application and HPT Engineers, owning the internal product training curriculum.
  • Create diagrams, screenshots and short how-to content.
  • Define and improve documentation standards, templates and structure.
  • Manage the product knowledge base and standard procedures.
  • Support documentation for installation, validation and troubleshooting.
  • Collaborate with R&D, Production, Service, Quality and Application teams so that documentation reflects actual product behavior and best practices. Requirements
  • At least 2 years of experience writing technical documentation for hardware and/or software products.
  • Engineering or technical background, or equivalent experience.
  • Excellent written English.
  • Ability to understand technical topics quickly and explain them simply. Nice to Have
  • Documentation for industrial machines, machine vision, AOI or SMT/electronics manufacturing.
  • Software release management and product life cycle documentation.
  • Docs-as-code tools (Markdown, Git) and Confluence.
  • Experience creating training materials or e-learning.
  • Italian or German. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
