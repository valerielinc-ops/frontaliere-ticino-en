Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

For our financial accounting we are looking from 1. December 2026 or by agreement one or more

Senior Accountant, 60-70 %

You do.

  • Management and supervision of the accounts of several subsidiaries of the group
  • Preparation of certain accounts receivable and monitoring of open positions
  • Monitoring and control of creditors
  • Reconciliation and control of accounts
  • Cooperation in financial statements
  • Preparation of accounts and supporting documents
  • Active participation in the consolidation process of consolidated financial statements
  • Preparation, examination and reconciliation of data required for consolidation
  • Coordination and monitoring of intra-group balances
  • Application of the accounting systems Abacus and SAP
  • Cooperation with various internal contacts and the finance department

That sets them apart.

  • Accounting/financial training or equivalent training
  • Demonstrable experience in accounting, ideally in a group-wide environment
  • Experience with financial statements
  • Experience consolidating consolidated financial statements is beneficial
  • Good understanding of consolidation processes and intra-group transactions
  • Good knowledge of Abacus and/or SAP and Excel
  • Independent, precise and structured work
  • mother tongue German or French, with good knowledge of the other language.

They benefit from it.

The healthcare sector and thus also the Biel Hospital Center are in an exciting transformation phase, in which initiative and self-responsible action are required. You will have the opportunity to actively shape the future of the hospital with a motivated team. The Biel Hospital Center offers you attractive employment conditions according to a collective employment contract and has its own restaurant with advantageous prices for the staff as well as a day care center. In in-house language courses you can refresh your French or German skills.
