Full description

Introduction

For our Supplier Quality Assurance sector, we are looking for a (M/F) Group Manager whose main mission will be to manage a control methods service integrating basic data, control methods and associated means, as well as the control of new products.

Responsibilities

  • Support and unite a team of 20 people (basic data agents, project managers, methods respondents and quality controllers)
  • Define, manage and prioritize the team's activities and projects according to the needs and missions of the Supplier Quality Assurance sector
  • Coordinate closely with the different internal sectors as well as the outsourcing group throughout the process of introducing new products, until their qualification
  • Build with the objective of external certification, the provision of continuous support: ranges of controls, installations, programs, adequate training, etc.
  • Actively participate in continuous improvement projects linked to internal and external activities, particularly with our suppliers
  • Provide expert support to external quality management in the event of major non-conformities and associated diagnostics
  • Ensure monitoring of innovative control means

Profile

  • Higher technical training
  • Proven experience in management and management of cross-functional projects
  • Very good knowledge of the field of control methods
  • Strong capacity for analysis, synthesis and strength of proposal
  • Creative spirit and oriented towards performance and innovation
  • Autonomy, flexibility and active collaboration in teamwork
  • Ease of communication, listening and empathy
  • Natural leadership, ability to unite teams

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and first-rate social benefits.

We thank you for sending a complete application file (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
