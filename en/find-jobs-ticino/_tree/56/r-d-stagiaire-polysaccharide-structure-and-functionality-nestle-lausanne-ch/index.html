As an R&D Intern – Polysaccharide Structure and Functionality at Nestlé Research, your mission is to explore how selective enzymatic treatments influence polysaccharide structure and functionality in model food systems. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with scientists and experts across the Nestlé Institute of Food Sciences and wider Nestlé Research community. This is your opportunity to make an impact while gaining hands on experience and mentorship to kick-start your career.

How you’ll make an impact as an R&D Intern – Polysaccharide Structure and Functionality

  • Investigating how selective enzymatic treatments shape polysaccharide structure and functionality in model food systems.
  • Designing and carrying out laboratory experiments to identify optimal reaction conditions and prototype approaches.
  • Characterizing polysaccharides using techniques such as size exclusion chromatography, monosaccharide composition analysis, rheology, dynamic light scattering and microscopy.
  • Consolidating and interpreting experimental results to build meaningful insights for food science innovation.
  • Presenting your findings clearly to the project team and contributing to collaborative scientific discussions.

What will make you successful

  • Current student or fresh graduate in physico-chemistry, food science or related scientific fields
  • Having a first exposure to laboratory environment, planning experiments and documenting results with care.
  • Having a proactive, result-oriented mindset while contributing positively to a collaborative team environment.
  • Communicating confidently in English, both orally and in writing.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning.
