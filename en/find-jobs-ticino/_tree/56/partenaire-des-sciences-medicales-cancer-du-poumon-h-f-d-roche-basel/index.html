At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. The Position At Roche Pharma Switzerland, the principle “Doing now what patients need next” drives us to be innovative. We are advancing science so that everyone gets the healthcare they need and creating a world where people can spend more time with their loved ones. In our team, you can expect an exciting and international working environment characterized by mutual respect, discussion and cooperation.

We are currently looking for a:n Medical Science Partner (MSP) for Lung Cancer. In this medical, non-promotional field service function, you act at a high scientific level as an important link between medical experts in hospitals as well as practices and Roche Pharma Switzerland. The position is associated with a national and international travel activity of about 60%.

The possibility

  • Medical expertise: External and internal medical-scientific expert for the portfolio over the entire product life cycle. Medical input and review of marketing authorisation applications, HTAs and other scientific documents.
  • Strategy design: Significant co-design of the indication strategy and development of relevant medical tactics to achieve common business goals.
  • Customer engagement: Building effective peer-to-peer relationships with external stakeholders and identifying/developing scientifically based collaborations with therapy experts (TAEs). Interaction with doctors to identify needs and potential areas for scientific cooperation.
  • Medical Information & Training : Presentation of medical and scientific data and provision of training for external audiences and Roche employees.
  • Insights Generation & Strategic Consulting: Implementation of landscaping and profiling for new indications. Introducing insights from the field and classifying strategically implementable recommendations for the indication team.
  • Clinical trials: Primary contact person for TAEs and medical professionals for scientific exchange and clinical Roche-sponsored or Investigator Initiated (IIS) trials. Strategic prioritization of studies for the Swiss market within the framework of the overall strategy and positioning at clinical centers.

Who you are

  • University degree in medicine, pharmacy or natural sciences (preferably PhD or MD).
  • Experience as a Medical Science Liaison or Medical Manager
  • In-depth knowledge of Lung Cancer/Oncology
  • Existing network in the field of oncology in the Swiss market is advantageous.
  • Ability to interpret and present clinical data and excellent communication skills.
  • Strong initiative, strategic thinking and action
  • Customer-centred thinking and the ability to flexibly align priorities in a dynamic environment.
  • Confident in German and English
  • Knowledge of French is an added advantage.
