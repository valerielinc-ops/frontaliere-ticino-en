Full description

Our company is a leader in electrical installations and building technology. We develop and implement advanced solutions in the areas of telephony, telematics, building automation and security. We perform maintenance and repair work on existing electrical installations and systems of all sizes.

To strengthen our team in Vevey, Attalens and Lausanne, we are looking for a start-up date to be agreed on an electrical installer convenience with CFC or an installer-electrician troubleshooter with CFC.

You are involved in services, small works and troubleshooting, but you are also at ease on major construction sites.

You have an electric installer CFC and a driver's licence valid in Switzerland. You benefit from experience in the management of electrical troubleshooting and you are organized, rigorous and autonomous.

We offer you a pleasant working environment in a young and dynamic team and a long-term commitment. In addition, we offer continuous training to stay at the cutting edge of technology and good wage and social benefits.
