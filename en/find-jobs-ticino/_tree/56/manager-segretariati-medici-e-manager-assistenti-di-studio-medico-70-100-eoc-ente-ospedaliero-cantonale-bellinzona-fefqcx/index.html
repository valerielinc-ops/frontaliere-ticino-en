Full description

Responsible for medical secretariats and head of medical assistants - Ausschreibung L’EOC the Ticino multisite hospital is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of more than 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing more than 600,000 outpatients. As part of the reorganization of the medical secretariats of the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona and Valli, Istituto Oncologico della Svizzera Italiana and Istituto Pediatrico della Svizzera Italiana, we are looking for the Head of Medical Secretariats and Head of Medical Study Assistants (70%-100%)

  • Profile: The people we are looking for have a training (Switzerland Federal Certificate) related to the function; further diplomas achieved within the framework of leadership or communication constitute preferential title as well as the experience gained in the management and management of a team of medium-large dimensions. They know, in addition to the Italian language, at least another national language and English as well as have good skills in the use of the main computer applications. It requires flexibility to go to all other EOC locations. As part of its role, the managers will be engaged both in conducting activities and in operational activities. As part of the management, they must be able to independently program, coordinate and supervise all activities using their own organizational skills; they demonstrate flexibility, availability to interdisciplinary and group collaboration and know how to communicate and interact effectively with both internal and external users. They show particular attention and sensitivity to the continuous improvement of working processes. They have a strong personal motivation and the ability to lead a team for which good leadership and development skills are needed. They believe, like us, in the importance of putting the patient and his whole family at the centre of our attention. Date of entry: to be agreed For further information, Mr Dila Zanetti, head of administration and patient services, is available at 091 811 90 10. Applications must reach us by 15 November 2026 . Paper applications or e-mail applications will not be accepted.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
