What tasks await you?

  • Manufacture biotechnological and chemical products to detect, prevent and treat diseases
  • Controlling and monitoring processes on large-scale systems using process control systems
  • Responsible for safe, environmentally friendly production that meets quality criteria
  • Active teamwork in interdisciplinary exchange with laboratory and technology
  • Digital recording and evaluation of process data and test series

As the training progresses, internships lasting several weeks take place in the technical training center on content such as: B. Production and process engineering, systems engineering, biotechnology, microbiology, measurement and control technology, thermal and mechanical separation methods, etc.

Your advantages with us

  • Finances: Attractive remuneration as well as holiday and Christmas bonuses.

  • Development: Intensive exam preparation, technical college entrance qualification (optional) and a wide range of further training.
  • Extras: International working environment, assignments abroad and discounted canteen meals.

What requirements do you have to meet? At least intermediate level of education

How long does your training last? 3.5 years, with good performance 3 years
