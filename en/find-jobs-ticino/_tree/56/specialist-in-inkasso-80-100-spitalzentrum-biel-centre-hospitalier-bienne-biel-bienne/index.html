The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

Specialist in debt collection, 80-100 %

You do.

  • Ensuring and further developing efficient debt collection processes
  • Implementation and monitoring of reminder runs
  • Maintenance of debtor lists and preparation of evaluations
  • Implementation and monitoring of debt collection measures
  • Accompanying debt collection dossiers from request for enforcement to prosecution
  • Preparation of payment agreements as well as their monitoring and dunning
  • Correspondence with operating offices

That sets them apart.

  • Basic commercial training completed
  • Advantageous further training in debt collection, escrow or law
  • Several years of professional experience in debt collection or receivables management as well as sound knowledge in the SchKG
  • High initiative and a structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Stylish-confident German as well as good French skills in word and writing

They benefit from it.

The healthcare sector and thus also the Biel Hospital Center are in an exciting transformation phase, in which initiative and self-responsible action are required. You will have the opportunity to actively shape the future of the hospital with a motivated team. The Biel Hospital Center offers you attractive employment conditions according to a collective employment contract and has its own restaurant with advantageous prices for the staff as well as a day care center. In in-house language courses you can refresh your French or German skills.
