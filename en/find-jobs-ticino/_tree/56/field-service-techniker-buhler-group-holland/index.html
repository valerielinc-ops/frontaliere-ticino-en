Field Service Technician — Bühler Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Assembles, tests, repairs, rebuilds, retrofits and installs Buhler products while performing a variety of manufacturing tasks at the customer site, according to established procedures, while conforming to all safe work practices.

Aufgaben

  • Performs manufacturing tasks including wiring, pipe bending, fabricating and installing of hydraulic and electrical components following blueprints
  • Inspects components to insure proper installation
  • Prepares written reports to provide history and follow up requirements
  • Resolves customer complaints or refers issue to qualified individual
  • Follows individual training plan under the supervision of Team Leader

Anforderungen

  • Degree appropriate for position or 3 or more years of related experience
  • Exposure to Microsoft Office Suite
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills
  • Ability to maintain positive composure in stressful situations
  • Demonstrated knowledge of industrial controls, electronic, hydraulic and mechanical systems
  • Success working in a team environment
