Full description

An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Located on the Bürgenberg, a 500-meter-high rocky outcrop in central Switzerland, the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne stands out for its bold design, groundbreaking sustainability, breathtaking landscapes and state-of-the-art leisure activities. Around 700 employees from 54 nations provide excellent hospitality and provide the perfect setting for moments beyond imagination. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two accommodation options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Tavern 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence; at the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. For the third consecutive year, Bürgenstock Resort has been officially certified as a “Great Place to Work” in Switzerland – an award that fills the entire team with pride.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for an agreement

Housekeeping Attendant 100% (w/m/i)

Their tasks include

  • the preparation and cleaning of guest rooms & suites
  • cleaning the public areas and in the back of house
  • the distribution of laundry, water and various housekeeping items on the floors
  • fulfilling guest wishes - from the toothbrush to the extra bed
  • Supporting floor supervisors through targeted work
  • Guest and hotel linen handling
  • the control & cleaning of offices, auxiliary rooms and public areas
  • Minibar inspection and orders for minibar items

Preferably, you bring experience from similar positions in the 5* hotel industry. We presuppose reliability, physical resilience and teamwork. They work precisely, cleanly and well-structured, act calmly and efficiently even in hectic situations. We expect a well-groomed, friendly and courteous attitude. Foreign languages are an additional advantage in dealing with our employees and guests.
