Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of a fixed employment? For 30 years, we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, offering them the best talents. We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the (M/F/D) Radio Protection Technique location

Your assignment

  • Ensure radiological monitoring of plants and interventions
  • Perform contamination, irradiation and absorbed dose controls
  • Leaving teams during interventions in restricted areas
  • Define the conditions of intervention and the appropriate means of protection
  • Ensure compliance with radioprotection and safety standards
  • Inform, advise and sensitize staff involved on radiological risks
  • Make measurements, sampling and analysis using dedicated equipment
  • Check and ensure monitoring of radioactive waste
  • Participating in risk prevention and continuous improvement of radioprotection practices

And now we're talking about you?

  • Training completed with positive outcome in OBBLIGATORIA radiation protection: PNR (INSTN), BTS in Radioprotection, BTS in Nuclear Environment, Degree in Radioprotection or equivalent
  • Experience of at least 3 years in operational radioprotection in a nuclear environment
  • Master of radiological control techniques and measuring instruments
  • High sensitivity to safety and risk prevention issues
  • Team spirit and good relational skills in the field
  • Ability to work independently in strict procedures
  • Strict, responsiveness and sense of responsibility
  • Stipendium based on profile and experience
