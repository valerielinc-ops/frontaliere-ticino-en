Full description

The UniBE is versatile. With us, you can expect a wide variety of topics and a lot of design freedom.

Their tasks

  • Leadership and development of the Service Center team and coordination of tasks and resources
  • Ensuring efficient and correct financial accounting in accordance with Swiss GAAP FER and compliance with university and legal requirements
  • Responsibility for the operational financial processes of the organizational units in the central area and their continuous optimization
  • Participation in monthly, quarterly and annual accounts and ensuring timely completion of the final work
  • Support of the department management in financial accounting issues as well as in budgeting and personnel planning
  • Preparation and preparation of financial information and evaluations for internal and external reports, settlements and funders
  • Management of creditor and debtor processes and audit and accounting of invoices and expenses
  • Cooperation in internal projects and responsibility for tasks in the area of internal control system (ICS)

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed commercial training and further training in accounting (e.g. professional card) or a comparable qualification
  • Ideally, further training in the field of leadership as well as the willingness to take responsibility for a team and promote employees in their development
  • Good user knowledge in MS Office; SAP knowledge is an advantage
  • Very good oral and written expressive skills in German and good knowledge of English
  • Strong social and advisory skills as well as the ability to assert themselves in a situational manner
  • Excellent planning and organisational skills as well as an independent, efficient and structured working method
  • High awareness of quality and responsibility as well as a strong service orientation
  • Openness to change and the willingness to continuously develop
