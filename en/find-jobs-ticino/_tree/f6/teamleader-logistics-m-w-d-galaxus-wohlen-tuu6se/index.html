Full description

We are looking for dedicated team leaders for our Packing, Picking, Skypod, Receiving and Pick & Pack divisions. In this role you are in the middle of it instead of just there. You lead your team with heart and mind, take part in everyday life and actively shape how we make our logistics a little better every day.

Mansioni

  • Responsibility for the smooth running of day-to-day business and take action yourself
  • Professional and personnel management of a team of around 10-20 employees including responsibility for their safety and health protection
  • Continuous development of the team as well as independent implementation of employee, health and annual meetings
  • Active participation in projects and contributing team and logistics know-how to the continuous improvement of work processes

Requirements

  • first to several years: leadership experience
  • Completed initial or further education: further education in logistics or leadership
  • Solid expertise in logistics through practical experience, specific training (e.g. logistics specialist) or further training in leadership
  • First to several years of management experience in logistics or production
  • Very good IT skills in MS Office as well as experience with warehouse management systems
  • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Mobility and readiness for our two-shift operation (Mo–Fr 05:00–23:00 / Sa 05:00–18:00)
  • Show the courage to take risks and act beliefs under uncertainty
  • Reconcile actions and decisions with your personal beliefs and values
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Boldly think for yourself: We encourage everyone to think for themselves and actively. And love it when you think bravely, newly and differently.

  • Rapid growth: With us, you are constantly growing and developing. And get EDU points (2'000 CHF for employees in Switzerland, 1'600 EUR for German employees and 700 EUR for our Serbian employees) for your further development, so that you do not stand still.
  • Further benefits Switzerland: Benefit from 25 days of holidays, exclusive employee prices in the shop, mobile, Internet and TV subscriptions at the company rate and other benefits.
