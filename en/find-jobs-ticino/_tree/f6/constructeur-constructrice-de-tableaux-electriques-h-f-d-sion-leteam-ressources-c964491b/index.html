On behalf of our customer, a company active in the field of electrical installations, we are looking for a manufacturer of electrical panels to strengthen his team.

leteam sa is a temporary and fixed placement agency renowned in Switzerland, operating with pride for nearly 40 years and owning 28 agencies across the country. Specializing in the placement and rental of staff, she is distinguished in the fields of building, crafts, industry, technology, pharmaceuticals and health.
