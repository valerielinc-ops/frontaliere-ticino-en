The Position The Opportunity The Admin and SHE Officer plays a dual role in ensuring operational excellence and organizational resilience for Roche in Cameroon. You will be responsible for the seamless day-to-day administration of the office while serving as the strategic lead for Safety, Health, and Environment (SHE). Your mission is to protect our people, secure our assets, and drive a high-performance culture through digital innovation and rigorous compliance.

Main Responsibilities Administrative Support

  • Documentation & Financial Flow: Manage the full lifecycle of physical and digital records, including contracts and correspondence, while ensuring all invoices are accurately uploaded for processing.
  • Operational Readiness & Compliance: Assist in documenting core processes like Site Management and Business Continuity Planning (BCP) to ensure the team is fully prepared for upcoming SHE audits.
  • Logistics & Team Coordination: Oversee complex schedules, travel arrangements, and office procurement, while organizing team-building activities to foster a high-performance culture.
  • Digital Innovation: Proactively identify and implement digital tools and solutions to streamline workflows and improve the overall effectiveness of the team.

SHE Support

  • Strategic Leadership & Advisory: Lead the "One SHE" team in Cameroon and act as a key advisor to Senior Management, integrating safety, health, and environmental strategies into the broader business planning process.
  • Operational Security & Resilience: Implement site security protocols and maintain a robust Business Continuity Plan (BCP) to ensure organizational resilience and rapid recovery during operational disruptions.
  • Compliance & Standards: Ensure all activities align with local regulations and Roche Global policies, managing the documentation of SHE KPIs, reporting, and maintaining critical liaisons with law enforcement and professional bodies.
  • Performance & Culture: Foster a workplace culture that prioritizes well-being while driving continuous improvement by benchmarking local practices against global industry standards.
