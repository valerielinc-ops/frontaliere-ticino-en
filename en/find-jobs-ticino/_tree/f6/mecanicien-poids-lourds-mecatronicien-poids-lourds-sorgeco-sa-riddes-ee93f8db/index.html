Description of position

  • Perform diagnostics, maintenance and repairs on heavy-duty vehicles.
  • Perform mechanical, electrical, hydraulic and pneumatic work.
  • Identify faults using diagnostic tools.
  • Provide preventive and corrective maintenance.
  • Perform safety checks and ensure vehicle compliance.
  • Inform intervention reports and follow up work.
  • Meet existing safety, quality and procedures.

Position Requirement

  • Heavy-duty mechanic CFC, heavy-duty mechatronician or equivalent training.
  • Experience confirmed in the maintenance and repair of heavy-duty vehicles.
  • Good knowledge of mechanics, electricity, electronics, hydraulics and pneumatics.
  • Mastery of diagnostic tools.
  • Team spirit, autonomy and sense of responsibility.
  • Mastery of French, both oral and written.
