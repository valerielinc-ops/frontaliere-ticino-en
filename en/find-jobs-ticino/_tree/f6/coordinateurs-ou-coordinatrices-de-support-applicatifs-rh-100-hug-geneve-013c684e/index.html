Job Description

You are joining the Time Management team for the ePop project as the HR Applied Support Coordinator. You play an essential role in assisting users, managing incidents and continuously improving tools. The ePop project aims to develop a unique institutional tool integrated into the HUG information system for planning. It takes into account the complexity of the planning and the legal and institutional rules. In your role, you train and accompany users to ensure the adoption and optimal use of the new planning tool. You provide them with effective support tailored to their needs. Thanks to your expertise in time management and planning, you advise your interlocutors on the application of the rules in force. With your overall vision, you analyze, diagnose and resolve tool-related incidents, identify any malfunctions and create Jira tasks by performing macro analysis. You distribute tickets to dedicated teams. In addition, when you identify a tool malfunction, you provide detailed information to development teams for tool correction. As part of incident management, you ensure the tracking and traceability of incidents via the internal tool. You guarantee regular communication to officials on the status of pending applications. You also participate in the testing and validation of new features, while identifying and proposing improvements. You ensure a quality relationship with users, ensuring optimal response times, clear and courteous communication, and strict confidentiality of information. You also manage access rights in connection with time management. You check, assign and remove accesses while ensuring compliance. You develop and update the manuals for using the new tool, organize and deliver the training to the right holders and follow the validations of the e-learning modules. Finally, your mission includes strong collaboration and sharing of expertise. You participate in user and user working groups and committees to improve existing processes and tools. You also provide support to new collaborators, students and trainees in application support.

Qualifications

You have a Bachelor's degree or an ES degree in computer science, information systems or equivalent. You justify a proven experience of at least three years in a technical support role within a complex environment. Knowledge of human resources related to time management and its legal framework is required. You have strong knowledge in using ticket management tools such as Jira or ServiceNow. A familiarity with Java is an additional asset. You have a global vision that allows you to prioritize tasks, optimize ticket allocation and understand the tool as a whole. You work closely with the project team, the technical functions of the ISD and the planning staff. You have excellent communication capabilities, you are able to explain and popularise technical concepts clearly to a non-technical audience. You are responsive and rigorous in the resolution of incidents, quickly identifying the causes of the problems and proposing appropriate solutions. Your patience and listening skills allow you to identify and understand the needs of users in order to respond effectively with strong customer orientation. You appreciate teamwork and work easily with different departments, including development and infrastructure teams. Finally, your curiosity and interest in technological intelligence allow you to evolve in a constantly changing environment. You have the strength to propose and adapt to new tools and organizational changes.
