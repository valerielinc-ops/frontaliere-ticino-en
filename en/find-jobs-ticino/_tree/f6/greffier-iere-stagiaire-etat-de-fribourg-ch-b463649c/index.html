Who are we

The Justice of the Peace is the authority for the protection of children and adults. As such, it acts upon request or ex officio. At the end of the investigation, it is decided whether or not to introduce a protective measure. The Justice of the Peace also has jurisdiction in matters of inheritance, investments for assistance purposes and in the area of ​​banishment.
