The Position Executive Assistant

The Opportunity

Provide high-level, strategic administrative and operational support to the Molecular and Sequencing Sales Leaders and their teams . This role serves as a trusted partner, enabling the commercial leadership and their teams to operate efficiently and effectively by managing priorities, optimizing workflows, and driving coordination across complex, cross-functional initiatives.

The Executive Assistant exercises sound judgment, discretion, and autonomy in handling sensitive and confidential matters. This role requires a proactive mindset, a solutions-oriented approach, strong business acumen, and the ability to anticipate needs, solve problems, and influence outcomes in a fast-paced, matrixed environment.

Key Roles & Responsibilities

This role requires broad and comprehensive experience, skill, and knowledge of organizational policies, practices, and procedures. Works independently on assignments related to Marketing & Sales and exercises considerable discretion and independent judgment.

  • Manages the preparation processes for internal and external meetings, including the logistics, presentations, and content. Ensures meetings have structure and time is well spent.
  • Strategically manages a leader’s time, looking for opportunities to reprioritize and maximize the leader’s effectiveness.
  • Provides as needed support to the overall senior leadership team.
  • Leads/coordinates special projects and initiatives as assigned.
  • Builds and maintains strategic relationships across the business to effectively learn and leverage company resources to drive business effectiveness within the department.
  • Proposes and executes continuous improvement solutions within the department to drive business effectiveness.
  • Makes data-driven recommendations to streamline operations.
  • Prepares and initiates routine and non-routine internal/external correspondence and memoranda.
  • Responsible for the release of a variety of complex communications and information that affect a unit of the organization.
  • Stewards candidate interview calendars and new hire onboarding activities within the department.
  • Schedules appointments, meetings, and travel itineraries and coordinates related arrangements. Attends meetings, records, and distributes meeting minutes.
