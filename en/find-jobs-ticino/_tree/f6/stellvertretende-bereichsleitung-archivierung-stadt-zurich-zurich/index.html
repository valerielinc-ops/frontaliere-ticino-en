Full description

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for a scientific employee at the Stadtarchiv Zürich by appointment or immediately. They support the departmental management of archiving in the development, conservation and conceptual issues and represent them in holiday absences.

  • They access analogue and digital files in the archive database, plan and process deliveries and post-developments and control developed stocks based on internal specifications.
  • They look after employees in the development and recruit new employees.
  • They develop and update internal principles (guidelines, instructions, concepts) and take on conservation tasks in the context of the opening of analogue files.
  • Together with the specialist data management, you model and implement data-based workflows, actively accompany the transition to records in contexts (RiC) and work in projects, especially in the replacement of the archive information system.
  • They support and represent the divisional management technically and operationally. For their holiday absences, you are also the contact person for the team and for inquiries from day-to-day business.
  • University/specialist university degree or comparable education and training, preferably in archival and information science, as well as further education in project management and in the use of AI in the archival environment
  • At least 7 years of relevant professional experience, broad practical experience in the development of analogue and digital files and very good knowledge of processes and processes in an archive
  • Knowledge of the development standards (ISAD(G), RiC), the conservation of analogue and digital archives as well as good knowledge of archive law and data protection
  • Solid project experience in data management and AI, knowledge of common AI and data management tools in the archive environment as well as coding knowledge
  • Ability for professional and personnel leadership as well as pleasure in deputising for the divisional management
  • Reliable, structured and independent working methods, strong communication and team skills as well as very good knowledge of German and English. (Knowledge of French is advantageous)

Increasing digitalization brings new and exciting challenges for archives. You can therefore expect a versatile and forward-looking activity with a lively corporate culture, a committed team and a workplace in the heart of the city of Zurich. Flexible and progressive working conditions with excellent social benefits, home office opportunities and targeted education and training are a matter of course for us.

The Zurich City Archive is an information and services centre for the administration and the public. It documents the history and development of the city of Zurich and makes the actions of the administration traceable. It thus makes a decisive contribution to the rule of law and legal security and offers rich source material for historical research. Equality, diversity and inclusion are important to us.
