Full description

ZURZACH Care Clinic for Sleep Medicine is the leading address for the prevention, diagnosis and therapy of sleep disorders of all kinds. As an accredited sleep center SGSSC with sleep ambulatory and sleep laboratory, we look after patients around the clock. Our location in Baden is located in the charming Bäderquartier, where tradition, health and recreation meet.

What is special about this?

In this responsible role, you take over the professional and organizational management of the psychological area and actively shape its further development. You work closely with the various disciplines of the clinic and bring your psychological and psychotherapeutic expertise to a highly specialized, interdisciplinary environment. In addition to the management, he is still clinically active.

The position offers you an exciting combination of leadership, clinical activity, psychotherapy and sleep medicine.

Your tasks

  • Professional and organisational management of psychology
  • Leadership, support and further development of the psychological team
  • Ensuring a high quality of psychological and psychotherapeutic care
  • Further development of treatment concepts and psychological offers in the area of sleep medicine
  • Diagnosis and therapy of non-organic sleep disorders as well as psychotherapies for various psychiatric disorders
  • Caring for patients with organic sleep disorders
  • Evaluation and interpretation of computer-assisted sleep medical examination procedures
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with doctors and other specialist areas
  • Participation and active participation in interdisciplinary meetings, case meetings and public events
  • Participation in the further development of the clinic as well as in internal and external training and further education offers
  • Lectures and workshops in prevention and occupational health promotion

You bring with you

  • University degree in clinical psychology as well as a completed and recognized further training in psychotherapy in Switzerland
  • Several years of professional experience in clinical psychology and psychotherapy
  • You are in possession of a Professional Permit (BAB) or ready to apply for it
  • Ideally, experience in a management or leadership role or a strong interest and willingness to assume leadership responsibility
  • Experience or strong interest in sleep medicine
  • Previous sleep medical knowledge is an advantage
  • Enjoying the combination of clinical activity, leadership and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Constructive, solution-oriented and entrepreneurial thinking and working methods
  • Strong social skills, communication skills and pleasure in the leadership and development of employees
  • Teamwork, flexibility, resilience and independent working methods
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Further language skills are advantageous

We offer you

  • A responsible leadership function with room for manoeuvre and the opportunity to actively develop the field of psychology
  • A varied and demanding job in a future-oriented company
  • A highly specialized, interdisciplinary working environment with a close exchange between psychology, medicine and other disciplines
  • A well-founded familiarization with the sleep medicine specialist area
  • Regular internal and external training
  • Regular supervision and intervision
  • High personal responsibility and diverse opportunities for professional and conceptual development
  • Very good working conditions and attractive social security benefits
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
