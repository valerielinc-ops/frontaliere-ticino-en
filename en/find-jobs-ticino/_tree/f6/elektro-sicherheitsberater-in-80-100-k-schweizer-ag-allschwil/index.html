Electrical safety consultant 80 until 100% Do you know our yellow vehicles? They are always in use where we, K. Schweizer AG – Your reliable electrical partner on site – just performing a demanding electrical installation or fixing a fault. In order to strengthen our team in our branch in Riehen, we are looking for a service-oriented and committed personality as an electrical safety consultant. Their tasks

  • Independent processing of final inspections and periodic inspections of small, medium and large projects
  • Preparation of safety demonstrations and control reports
  • Machinery and equipment testing, electrothermography
  • Network analysis
  • Customer advice, concepts and instructions
  • Compliance and monitoring of the legal bases

Your profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship with further training as an electrical safety consultant / inspector / electrical safety consultant / inspector with federal law. Specialist card
  • A further development as a lightning protection specialist is advantageous
  • You are in possession of a driving license Cat. B
  • Entrepreneurial, independent personality
  • They work reliably, are capable of teamwork as well as flexible and organize skillfully
  • You have stylish knowledge of German
