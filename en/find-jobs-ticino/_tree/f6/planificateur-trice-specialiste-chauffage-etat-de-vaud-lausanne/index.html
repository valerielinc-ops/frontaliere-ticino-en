Full description

The Engineering and Sustainability Directorate (DID) provides technical and strategic expertise to the Buildings and Heritage Branch in energy and environmental matters. As a specialist heating planner, you contribute to the operation and energy quality of the heating facilities of the Vaud State housing stock and ensure the technical link between the engineering and operation of the buildings.

  • Independently conduct technical visits, audits and site records of the heating facilities of the cantonal housing stock, analyse their operation and make recommendations for optimization
  • Perform basic dimensioning calculations (powers, flows, temperatures), develop estimates, detailed costing and techno-economic variants, and contribute to bid preparation and bid analysis
  • Read, analyze, develop and update facility principle diagrams from land surveys using OAD tools
  • Technical monitoring of field installations, monitoring of compliance and support to engineers and operating teams during commissioning and receptions
  • Analyze malfunctions using monitoring supervision tools, propose corrective measures and contribute to the monitoring of technical anomalies. For further information, please consult the specifications here or contact Mr. A. Moutinho Mucha, Engineering Manager, at 021 316 37 27.
  • Projector CFC in building technology, heating orientation, or training deemed equivalent. ES diploma and further training in renewable energy or energy optimisation, an asset
  • Professional experience of at least 10 years in the field of heating (design, costing and monitoring), including at least 5 years in the replacement or optimisation of heat production, in particular with the integration of renewable energies
  • Mastering of basic calculations for the dimensioning of heating installations (powers, flows, temperatures) and ability to produce estimates, detailed figures and techno-economic variants
  • Mastery of reading, analysis and development of schematics, technical surveys and DAO tools
  • Autonomy, good analytical skills, sense of responsibility, ability to collaborate with different actors.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
