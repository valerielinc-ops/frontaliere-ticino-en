Assistant Sales & Marketing — Pictet Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your team

Founded in Geneva in 1805, the Pictet Group is now one of the main independent players in wealth management and asset management in Europe, with funds under management and in deposits exceeding EUR 878 billion at the end of June 2026. It is run by seven partners, who are both owners and managers.

Pictet Asset Management (Pictet AM) is dedicated to managing assets on behalf of third parties through the management of mandates and investment funds. Present in 18 financial centres around the world and employing more than 1100 employees, Pictet AM has been working to deliver an excellent service to a leading professional and institutional clientele since 1980.

This course will allow you to acquire an overall vision of the ecosystem of asset management and develop a good understanding of the different investment strategies and economic and financial news. You will also develop your business skills, synthesis and marketing creativity, while working daily on a dynamic and international team.

Your role

Within Pictet AM's commercial and marketing team, based in Paris, you will assist the team as follows:

Trade missions (60%)

  • Support of the commercial team in the maintenance of the customer relationship (institutional, wholesale, and retail clients) in the process of selling Pictet funds (emerging, thematic, bond, alternative and ETF funds).
  • Translation and adaptation of management points and investment content Pictet.
  • Preparation and updating of commercial media (PowerPoint, e-mails, newsletters, product presentations).
  • Response to client questionnaires or requests (telephone or e-mail).
  • Organization and logistics at customer events.
  • Writing of summaries in French of technical content received from management teams.

Marketing missions (40%)

  • Creation and programming of emailing campaigns.
  • Dissemination and programming of communications on the investment funds and the macroeconomic views of Pictet AM.
  • Online and updated content on our websites and those of our partners.
  • Creation and updating of marketing media (banners, brochures, e-mails, etc.).
  • Organization and logistics of customer events.
  • Participation in the creation of audio and video content: monitoring, preparation, recording and editing.
  • Drafting and adapting content on funds according to current market developments.
  • Maintenance and maintenance of the client database.

Your daily life will be varied, interesting and punctuated by projects at different times as well as some recurring missions, requiring an excellent sense of organisation and management of priorities.

Your profile

We are looking for a person interested in financial markets and asset management, with very good relational qualities, bilingual French/English, adaptable, flexible, autonomous and curious. You have a good spirit of synthesis, know how to manage multiple files simultaneously and meet deadlines. A first experience with a CRM tool, especially Salesforce, would be a plus.

REF: ASM/SGV/MH

Start date: January 1, 2027
