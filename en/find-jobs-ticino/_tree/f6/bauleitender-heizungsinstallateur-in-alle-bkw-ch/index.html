We make habitats worth living.

Become part of a motivated team in a dynamic company with tradition and family touch.

Your tasks

  • You lead a small team with social competence prudently and goal-oriented and communicate clearly and step-wise.
  • You handle projects in residential, commercial and industrial construction on schedule and cost-consciously.
  • You will develop optimal technical solutions thanks to your knowledge of other building technology trades.
  • You participate in construction and coordination sessions.
  • You procure the necessary materials and monitor additional and reduced costs.
  • Together with the project management, you prepare account and final invoices.

Your expertise

  • You have a successfully completed apprenticeship as a heating installer: in EFZ

  • You have a pronounced service and customer awareness, are an organizational talent and convince with a friendly and well-kept appearance

About us

Guggisberg Kurz AG, based in Ostermundigen and Urtenen-Schönbühl, has stood for the highest quality and reliability since 1926. As a traditional company, we offer home technology solutions in the areas of heating, sanitation, ventilation, spenglerei and thermal solar systems. Our customers benefit from comprehensive consulting and the use of the most modern and energy-efficient techniques and solutions – because environmental awareness and energy saving are particularly important to us.
