About us

Guggisberg Kurz AG, based in Ostermundigen and Urtenen-Schönbühl, has stood for the highest quality and reliability since 1926. As a traditional company, we offer home technology solutions in the areas of heating, sanitation, ventilation, spenglerei and thermal solar systems.
