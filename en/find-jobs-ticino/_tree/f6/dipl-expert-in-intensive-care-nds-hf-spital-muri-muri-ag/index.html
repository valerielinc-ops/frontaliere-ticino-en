Full description

As a health centre in the Freiamt region, Spital Muri offers high-quality basic care for all phases of life. 940 employees provide comprehensive care for our patients in a family atmosphere. Every year, we carry out around 78,000 outpatient and 7,400 inpatient treatments. Our hospital has six operating rooms, four bear rooms, around 100 beds in the stationary area and six certified intensive care facilities. We are a family-friendly and attractive employer with progressive working conditions.

Their tasks Together with your colleagues, you are responsible for the patient-oriented, professional care and care of intensive care patients from all departments of the hospital. Embedded in a multi-professional team, they make a significant contribution to the therapy and care process of our intensive care patients. They look after relatives in crisis situations and participate in or initiate preventive, diagnostic and therapeutic measures. It is possible to actively participate with innovative ideas in working groups.

Your profile You have completed training as a nurse HF/FH and a specialist training in intensive care. They are characterized by a professional understanding of care with a high level of technical and social competence and bring a pronounced sense of quality and responsibility. With your team-capable, resilient and flexible personality and your interdisciplinary thinking and acting, you are actively involved in the treatment and team process.

Your opportunity It awaits you immediately or by agreement a responsible and varied activity in a modern and future-oriented hospital with a wide range of services. The cooperation within the different areas, team spirit and a cross-divisional feeling of togetherness are consciously lived with us.

Their advantages

  • Holidays: You benefit from holidays between 25 and 30 working days (depending on age) and 13 paid holidays. For this you have the possibility to buy 5 holiday days.
  • Working hours: The company’s normal working hours for a full-time workload are 42 hours per week (excluding doctors). The annual working time applies, which ensures the highest possible flexibility in the organisation of working time. Service requests are implemented where possible and part-time employment is possible in many functions – also in management positions.
  • Education and further education: Your personal and professional development is important to us. We support participation in internal and external further education and training through generous participation in the form of working hours and/or course costs.
  • Food: In our staff restaurant – nominated with the “Best of Swiss Gastro” award – you can enjoy menus from the market-fresh cuisine at very attractive conditions.
  • Arrival/parking: Employees of Spital Muri benefit from cheap parking. The car park is located on the hospital area. If you come to work by public transport, bike, motorcycle or on foot, we will reward you with a monthly eco-bonus.
  • Social insurance: We attach importance to adequate social benefits. For night, weekend and holiday work, we pay you fair allowances. We insure your salary at the pension fund Profond without coordination deduction (excluding doctors). The wages of doctors are insured by the VSAO preventive foundation.

Pensum: 40-100 %
