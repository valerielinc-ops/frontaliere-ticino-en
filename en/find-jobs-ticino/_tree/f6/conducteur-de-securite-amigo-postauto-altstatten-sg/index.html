Safety Driver AmiGo — PostAuto AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you want to be part of an innovation project that is unique in Europe and write the history of mobility and actively participate in the future of autonomous driving? At AmiGo, you accompany the latest vehicle technology in real-life test operation and take on a central safety role. With you, autonomous driving becomes safe, tangible and ready for the mobility of tomorrow.

You can do that.

  • In this temporary position, you accompany our autonomous 4-wheeled vehicles in test operation and monitor all safety-relevant processes in the vehicle at all times.
  • Potential hazards are detected at an early stage and, if necessary, intervene preventively or actively in the vehicle control system.
  • You carry out ongoing safety assessments and take control of the vehicle immediately in an emergency.
  • All incidents, anomalies and observations are documented promptly and electronically for continuous optimization of the overall system.
  • You ensure the safety of passengers, other traffic participants, pedestrians and animals at all times.
  • You are available to our customers as a competent and professional contact person directly in the vehicle.

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed vocational apprenticeship, a completed study or several years of relevant professional experience.
  • For at least three years you have had a valid driving licence of category B or higher and bring at least three years of driving experience in Switzerland. Preferably, you also have the permit for professional passenger transport (training BPT 121), or are ready to complete it.
  • You have a strong security awareness and have an impeccable reputation.
  • You are flexible for operations in the morning, afternoon, evening and on weekends.
  • It gives you pleasure to pioneer in an innovative, learning and highly motivated environment.
  • You have a quick comprehension, high responsiveness as well as analytical-technical skills and convince at the same time through customer orientation, responsibility, teamwork and a professional appearance.
  • You communicate verbally and in writing very well in German and bring good English skills.

The post is limited in time until 28.02.2027.
