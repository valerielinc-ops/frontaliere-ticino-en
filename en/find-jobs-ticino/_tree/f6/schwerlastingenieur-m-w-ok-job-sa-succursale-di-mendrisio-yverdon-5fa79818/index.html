Heavy Duty Engineer (M/F) back to list To strengthen our teams, we are looking for a (M/F) heavy-duty mechanic Job description

Your mission

  • Perform maintenance, maintenance and repair work on trucks and commercial vehicles
  • Perform diagnostics and identify mechanical, electrical and electronic failures
  • Perform general mechanical work: brakes, suspension, transmission, clutch, engine, etc.
  • Carry out inspections and interventions in accordance with the manufacturer's requirements, including preventive measures to ensure the reliability and safety of vehicles
  • Repair and replace defective parts
  • Use diagnostic devices and domain-specific tools
  • Ensure accurate documentation of the work carried out and collaborate with the various interlocutors of the workshop

Profile

Profile desired

  • Holder of a mechanic/ne car maintenance CFC, ideally utility vehicle option, mechatronician/ne car, utility vehicle option or equivalent technical training
  • Professional experience in the maintenance and repair of heavy goods vehicles, commercial vehicles or similar equipment
  • Experience in using diagnostic tools and devices
  • Sense of precision, quality and work well done
  • Logical spirit and good analysis and problem solving capabilities
  • Autonomy, ability to work as a team, reliability and sense of responsibility
  • Driver's licence (category C/EC): an asset
  • Motivation to train in new technologies and developments in the sector
