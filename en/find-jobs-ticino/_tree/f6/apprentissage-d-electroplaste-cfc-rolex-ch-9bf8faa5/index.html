Full description

Motivated by electroplastic learning? Ready to become a reference in your profession through quality training in a leading company? Join us!

The electroplast deposits with chemical baths, usually electrochemical, various coatings on metal objects, sometimes even synthetic ones, in order to modify their properties (aspect, resistance or functionality). He/she monitors, maintains and corrects the chemical and physical parameters of these baths by means of analyses, with the aim of making them as resistant and as beautiful as possible.

This learning is suitable for young people who love technological environments as well as accurate and methodical work.

Profile

  • Interest and aptitudes in chemistry and physics
  • Taste for physical and manual activities
  • Logical and methodical spirit
  • Rigor and accuracy
