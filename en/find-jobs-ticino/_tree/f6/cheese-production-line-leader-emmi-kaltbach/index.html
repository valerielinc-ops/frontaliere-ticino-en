Full description

  • In the cheese production units, you produce our unique Kaltbach cheese varieties in 2-shift operation and also prepare the corresponding in-house cultures.
  • Variety is guaranteed and you also take on work in milk reception and standardization, as well as in the cheese salting plants.
  • You are familiar with hygiene, safety, production and process standards and, to ensure that everything is correct, you prepare all necessary proof documents.
  • You enjoy working with your team and, as a coach, you train employees and apprentices on the plants according to the training program.
  • You also ensure the availability of materials on the plants with your foresight, check and carry out cleaning and maintenance work according to guidelines and make suggestions for optimizing the plant, production process and interfaces.
  • Your training as a dairy technologist or dairy technologist EFZ, food technologist or food technologist EFZ is perfect for this role, preferably with a few years of work experience in cheese production.
  • You appreciate a family atmosphere, a great team and our unmistakable cheese enjoyment :-)
  • You are familiar with common Office programs and have no problems with German.
  • If you are also open to new things, willing to learn, able to think in a networked way and are reliable, then you are the right person for our team!
