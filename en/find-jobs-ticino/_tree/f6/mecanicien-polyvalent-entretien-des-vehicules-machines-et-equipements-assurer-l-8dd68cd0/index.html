Multipurpose Mechanic – Vehicle, Machinery and Equipment Maintenance

Mission Provides maintenance, maintenance and first-level repairs to the company's commercial vehicles, machinery and equipment to ensure their smooth operation, reliability and availability.

Tasks

  • 1. Maintenance of commercial vehicles
  • Conduct periodic services and interviews.
  • Control levels, brakes, tyres, lighting and safety equipment.
  • Perform small mechanical repairs.
  • To monitor the general condition of vehicles.
  • Prepare vehicles for technical inspections.
  • 2. Machinery and equipment maintenance
  • Provide routine maintenance of machinery and professional equipment.
  • Perform the necessary operational controls and adjustments.
  • Diagnose simple failures and perform appropriate repairs.
  • Replace wear parts and defective components.
  • Ensure the cleanliness and condition of the equipment.
  • 3. Lockwork and welding
  • Perform small welding work (steel, various repairs).
  • Perform simple repairs and modifications on metal structures.
  • Make or adapt small parts and supports.
  • 4. General maintenance and other work
  • Perform DIY and general maintenance work.
  • Maintain the equipment and facilities of the workshop.
  • Participate in the organization and storage of the workshop.
  • Manage consumables and report spare parts requirements.
  • Provide technical support to teams as required.

Profile sought

  • Training or experience in automotive, agricultural, industrial or similar mechanics.
  • Good knowledge in general mechanics.
  • Welding skills and metal work.
  • Ability to work independently and find practical solutions.
  • Versatility, initiative and responsibility.
  • A careful, organized and conscientious person.
  • Driving license adapted to the function.

Personal suitability

  • Debrouillardise and practical sense.
  • Autonomy and flexibility.
  • Team spirit and availability.
  • Reliability and compliance with safety instructions.
