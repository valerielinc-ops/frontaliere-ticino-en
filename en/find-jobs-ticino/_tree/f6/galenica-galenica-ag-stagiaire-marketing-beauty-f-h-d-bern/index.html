Full description

Workplace: Verfora AG, Untermattweg 8, 3001 Bern

Are you passionate about marketing with a strong interest in the world of dermocosmetic / skincare? Then join our dynamic team where your talent and creativity will make a difference! Attached to the Beauty Marketing Division, you will be involved in the exciting missions of a Brand Manager and will be able to actively contribute to the realization of the marketing plan of several renowned brands of dermocosmetic.

Your field of action

  • You coordinate and contribute to the implementation of activities according to the marketing plan, such as product launches, promotions and animations in pharmacies and drugstores.
  • You develop attractive promotional tools for points of sale (glass windows, displays, leaflets...) as well as for BtoC digital platforms.
  • You set up training and motivation tools for pharmacy/drug teams, while managing the digital BtoB training platform.
  • You follow and contribute to the development of digital communication (social networks, influencer collaborations).
  • You create effective tools for presentations in pharmacies/drugstores (salesfolders, etc.).
  • You analyze consumer sales and monitor the market and competition.

Your assets

  • You are in training or graduate in marketing or management.
  • You have an analytical mind and a great capacity for synthesis, a true passion for beauty, as well as an overflowing creativity and a keen sense of detail.
  • Reliable and always ready to help, you are a pillar the team can rely on!
  • You are fluent in German or French and communicate with ease in the other language (minimum level B2). English is a valuable asset for sharing with our partners.

We offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation, with the possibility of buying up to 10 additional days of vacation or taking leave without pay
  • Pension fund with advantageous distribution – we cover almost 2/3 of the premiums
  • Miscellaneous discounts in our pharmacies and other major brands
  • Opportunity to expand its skills through encouragement of training and development measures and opportunities for internal change
  • Taking into account the needs of families, with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of salary) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of salary), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Nominal actions at preferential prices – not only engage professionally for Galenica, but also invest
  • Mobile and flexible work in consultation with the team

Benefits may vary slightly by department/position

About us

A portfolio of products with brands of excellence Verfora is a Galenica network company that sells premium health and beauty products whose renowned brands and products are found in almost every Swiss household: Perskindol®, Anti-Brumm®, Merfen®, Algifor® – to name but a few of the leading products. We have a strong position in the areas of pain, cough, cold and respiratory diseases. Our marketing and distribution activities are primarily aimed at all pharmacies and drugstores. At Verfora, you will participate in a mission: "to help people feel good, to be and to stay healthy". In parallel, you are helping to continuously develop our product portfolio and gain new market segments.
