Job information

For the daily supply of customers in the extensive valleys of the canton of Graubünden we have an efficient delivery platform in Landquart. With modern vehicle park, we deliver the chilled and frozen food and normal goods professionally and on time.

To strengthen our proven driver team, we are looking for a chauffeur, a chauffeuse C / CE in all areas immediately or by agreement.

Task area

  • Distribution of general cargo / Food / Healthcare / Fresh goods / Frigo shipments with solo vehicle

Requirement profile

  • Reliable, independent and dutiful
  • Good knowledge of the German language in word and writing, Italian knowledge of advantages
  • Professional appearance and pleasure in customer contact
