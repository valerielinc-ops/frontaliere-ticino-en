Your Purpose

We are seeking a highly analytical and detail-oriented Compensation Intern to join our Compensation & Benefits team for a 6-month on-site internship. This role offers an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in compensation within a global organization.

Your Impact

Compensation Review Support

  • Assist the Compensation team during compensation review cycles, including merit increases, promotions, and off-cycle salary adjustments.
  • Support the preparation and validation of compensation review files and manager recommendations.
  • Review compensation data for completeness, accuracy, and consistency.
  • Help monitor and track compensation-related approvals and process milestones.

Data Collection and Analysis

  • Gather, consolidate, and validate employee compensation data from multiple sources.
  • Prepare and maintain compensation databases and reporting files.
  • Analyze compensation trends and support the development of recommendations.
  • Conduct data quality checks and identify discrepancies for review.

Market Benchmarking

  • Support salary benchmarking activities using external market survey data.
  • Compile market data and assist in preparing competitive pay analyses.
  • Assist with job matching and survey submissions where required.

Reporting and Analytics

  • Create Excel-based reports, dashboards, and data summaries.
  • Develop pivot tables, lookups, and other analytical tools to support compensation decision-making.
  • Support ad hoc compensation analyses and reporting requests from HR and business stakeholders.

Project and Administrative Support

  • Assist with compensation and total rewards projects.
  • Support maintenance of compensation guidelines and governance documentation.
  • Help prepare presentations and communication materials for stakeholders.

Your Journey so far

Education

  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor's or Master's degree in:
  • Human Resources
  • Business Administration
  • Finance
  • Economics
  • Statistics or Related field

Preferred Qualifications

  • Interest in Compensation & Benefits, HR Analytics, or Total Rewards.
  • Familiarity with HR systems (Workday, SAP SuccessFactors etc.) is an advantage.
  • Experience with Power BI or data visualization tools is a plus.

Your Essentials

  • Strong analytical and quantitative skills.
  • Advanced Excel skills are required, including Pivot Tables, Vlookup/Xlookup, Data Validation, Basic formulas and reporting, as well as Large data set management.
  • High attention to detail and accuracy.
  • Strong organizational and time management skills.
  • Ability to handle sensitive and confidential information.
  • Proficient in Microsoft PowerPoint and other Microsoft Office applications.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team environment.

What You'll Gain

  • Hands-on experience supporting annual and off-cycle compensation reviews.
  • Exposure to salary benchmarking and compensation analysis.
  • Practical experience working with large HR datasets and advanced Excel applications.
  • Understanding of compensation governance, pay practices, and total rewards programs within a global organization.
  • Opportunity to work closely with experienced Compensation professionals and HR leaders.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
