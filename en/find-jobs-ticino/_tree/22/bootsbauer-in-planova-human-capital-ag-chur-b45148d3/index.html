Our goal is to become even better every day. Better for our clients, for employees and of course for you. For the region of Graubünden we are looking for a committed boat builder in temporary employment from now on or by agreement.

What do you have to do?

As a boat builder, you are dealing with very different materials and working techniques. They saw, mill and hobgle wood, bend and rivet steel and aluminum sheets. They laminate and grind the plastic. At your disposal is our machine park, which you provide for the maintenance of. When building a new boat, you first make the hull based on the plans. Then grind, paint and paint the surface to make it weather resistant.

CONDITIONS ####

What do we expect from you?

  • You act well and gladly as a motivator, whereby you also gladly participate in the "front" action yourself.
  • In planning, you work in a structured and transparent manner and understand how to consistently translate your ideas into results
  • Your good comprehension and flexibility allow you to constantly adapt to complex processes and situations.
  • We address a goal-oriented personality from the construction industry with several years of experience

A professional team with a lot of expertise forms a solid basis for a successful future.
