Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team in quality assurance, we are looking for a dedicated release manager (a) 100%.

Their tasks

  • Release of pharmaceutical active substances and intermediates on behalf of the responsible person (FvP)
  • Verification and approval of manufacturing and testing requirements and product specifications
  • Assessment and approval of deviations and OOS. Establishment of necessary CAPA measures
  • As one of the "QA Persons in Plant", you are regularly on site in areas such as production in order to clarify QA questions directly.

Your profile

  • Master or Ph.D. in Chemistry, Biology or Pharmacy
  • at least 2 years of professional experience in pharmaceutical quality assurance, preferably in active ingredient production
  • High understanding of quality assurance aspects and in-depth cGMP knowledge
  • Ability to understand complex events and contexts and communicate internally and externally
  • Resilient, service-oriented, communicative and flexible personality
  • Very good oral and written expression in German and English

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
  • Fresh, healthy and diverse food in our staff restaurant
  • Various free sports activities on the Bachem Campus
