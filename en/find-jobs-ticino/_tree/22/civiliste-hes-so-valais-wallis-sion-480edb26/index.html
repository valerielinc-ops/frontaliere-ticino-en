As part of a project with the state of Valais, the Energy and Environment Institute of the High School of Engineering is looking for one or more:

Your mission

The state of Valais manages a fleet of about 400 buildings equipped with technical facilities, followed by a computer-assisted maintenance management (GMAO) to centralize the equipment. The post is part of the energy management and decarbonisation strategy of the state of Valais, through the development of planned maintenance to reduce operating costs, energy consumption and CO2 emissions.

In this context, your main tasks are

  • Field coordination
  1. Take charge of the tool developed for checking the database in the field (tool made by the student body)
  1. Plan visits to buildings and supervise students in charge of verification via QR codes during the month of August
  1. Process, verify and structure the collected data and import it into the GMAO
  • Development and structuring of GMAO
  1. Taking charge of the existing GMAO tool
  1. Create and structure maintenance ranges
  1. Set up a maintenance schedule related to defined ranges
  1. Collaborate with the editor of the GMAO for its improvement and deployment

The objectives of the position are as follows

  • Optimize energy performance of buildings
  • Reliability of technical data
  • Structure and improve GMAO
  • Implement effective and planned preventive maintenance
