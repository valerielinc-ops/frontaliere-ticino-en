Full description

Want to move to a clinical management function? At Ardentis, we believe in the potential of people and we are committed to building talent. The successful development of the group creates opportunities for evolution for a position of Director or Director of Clinic and we wish to appeal to potential candidates to allow everyone to manifest themselves. Being Director or Director of Clinic at Ardentis is to have the opportunity to make a difference every day, to support the teams in their development, to ensure the medical quality of the care and to contribute actively to the collective success. It also embodies our values and brings our culture to life. In the end, it is to diversify one's skills and offer an alternative path to that for which one predestines himself as a doctor-dentist. We are looking for people with a Doctor-Dentist degree who like to federate, take initiatives and accompany change around common goals. Are you planning on this evolution? We would be delighted to know more about you in particular about:

  • Your motivations
  • Your geographical mobility
