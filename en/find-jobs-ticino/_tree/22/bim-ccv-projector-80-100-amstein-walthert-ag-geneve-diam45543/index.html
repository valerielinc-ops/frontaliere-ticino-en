Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with approximately 1100 employees. As engineers, planners and consultants, we deal with all aspects of building technology and infrastructure throughout their life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and durable. Your missions:

  • Participation with the CCV project manager in the analysis of input data and the analysis of missing data to carry out design studies
  • Preparation of dimensioning notes and selection of equipment for Heating, Ventilation, Air-conditioning, Smoke-free
  • Production of digital HVAC models under REVIT and participation in multidisciplinary coordination
  • Implementation of plans and diagrams of projects in the field of HVAC
  • Implementation of drilling plans and coordination plans HVACSER
  • Mastering the level of detail to be produced according to the AIS phases
  • Technical support to the HVAC project leader, with participation in proxy sessions if necessary
  • Planning and management of technical records according to the ISO quality manual
  • Establishment of Metres and Quantitative Bids Your skills and qualifications: Minimum requirements:
  • SWC of projector in building technology or title deemed equivalent
  • 3 years of experience in an engineering office or company
  • Mastery of HVAC technology and Swiss standards
  • Perfect knowledge of REVIT and BIM environment desired
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft environments - Windows & Office
  • Rigor and attendance at work
  • A sense of responsibility, organization and teamwork
  • Relationship strength
  • Ability and motivation to fully invest in a team vision The plus:
  • Experience in the monitoring of execution and participation in the worksite sessions
  • Ability to develop specific MEP components under REVIT
  • Mastery of Autocad
  • Motivation to gradually develop the skills necessary to access project manager responsibilities
