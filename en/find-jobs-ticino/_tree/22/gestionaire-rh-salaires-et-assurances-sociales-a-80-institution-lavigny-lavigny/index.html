HR manager - salaries and social insurance at 80%, 80% Deployed at several sites, the Lavigny Institution consists of a neurological hospital with outpatient consultations at two sites, two departments for adults with disabilities, with an intellectual disability with a physical problem, a specialized school and a workshop and vocational training sector respectively. To strengthen our HR team, we are looking for a

HR manager - 80% salaries and social insurance

Your missions You are the person in charge of all personnel administration (administrative and salary management) for a defined population.

  • Administrative management of staff, from entry to exit: contracts, amendments, LPP, certificates and work certificates.
  • Maintain staff records and monitor work permits and contractual obligations.
  • Inform and inform employees and supervisors on any issues related to the CTC and HR administrative activity.
  • Make announcements to social insurance and ensure the administrative follow-up of sickness, accident and maternity records.
  • Integrate and control variable wage elements related to social insurance and absences.
  • Ensuring proper application of tax at source
  • Participate in the preparation and control of salaries of monthly and hourly paid staff and ensure the reliability of payroll data.
  • Contribute to day-to-day administrative tasks, hosting and improving HR processes.
  • Participate in annual processes: annuities, indexations, promotions, etc.
  • Participate in annual closures, regulatory returns and salary certificates.

Your profile

  • SWC or training deemed equivalent.
  • Experience confirmed in a similar position.
  • Certificate of HR Manager and/or Social Insurance.
  • Mastery of Swiss social insurance, payroll processes and applicable legal provisions.
  • Mastery of common computer tools, including Excel. The knowledge of Opale bluePearl and Polypoint is an asset.
  • Independent, rigorous and organized person able to manage priorities and ensure a high level of quality.
  • Interest in numbers and excellent analytical spirit.
  • Relationship strength, positive attitude and taste for teamwork.
  • Interest in the socio-health field.

Do you enjoy collaborating in a professional, caring and welded team, where good humor and humour also have their place? Then we'd be happy to meet you!

We offer you

  • Pleasant and human work environment.
  • A varied activity within a motivated and collaborative team.
  • Opportunities for continuing training
  • The social benefits of a large institution (staff restaurant, cafeteria and employee prices, free parking, participation in public transport costs, etc.).
  • Flexible working hours, wages and social benefits in line with the function and standards of the CLC in the Vaudian public health sector.
