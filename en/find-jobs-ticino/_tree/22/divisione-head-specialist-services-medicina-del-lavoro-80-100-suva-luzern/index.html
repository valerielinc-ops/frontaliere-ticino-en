Full description

Do you want to take on responsibility, combine medical expertise with strategic design and shape the future of occupational medicine together with a strong interdisciplinary team? What you do

  • You lead four teams with around 30 specialists in occupational medicine and assume the technical and personnel overall responsibility.
  • You shape the medical development of the assessment of occupational diseases and are responsible for the quality assurance and optimization of work processes.
  • You contribute your expertise on occupational diseases to strategic internal and external projects as well as specialist committees and contribute to the implementation of our prevention mandate.
  • You ensure the further training of assistant doctors as a specialist for occupational medicine.
  • You work closely with various areas of Suva and maintain the network of medical stakeholders in clinics and professional societies.
  • The vacancy shall be filled as a result of retirement. What you bring
  • You are a specialist in occupational medicine and have experience in the assessment of occupational diseases and their prevention.
  • In your career so far, you have ideally already gained initial experience in leadership as well as in quality and project management.
  • You are interested in scientific issues and engage in public relations work.
  • You like to work interdisciplinary, promote professional exchange and lead meetings and workshops to success.
  • You think networked, recognize development opportunities and implement new ideas effectively.
  • You have stylish knowledge of German. Good knowledge of French and English is a great advantage.
