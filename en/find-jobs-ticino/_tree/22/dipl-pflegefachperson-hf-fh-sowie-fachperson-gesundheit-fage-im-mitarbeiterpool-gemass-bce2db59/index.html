Fringe Benefits

Employees benefit from additional services that support health and well-being – including company health management, fitness offers, medical care, as well as hotel comfort during hospitalization. In addition, they enjoy a variety of benefits in pharmacies, sports goods, consumer electronics and drinks.

Food

The employees enjoy varied meals in all our restaurants at all locations at reduced prices.

Medical personnel service

The personnel medical service is happy to advise employees on health issues. As soon as winter time approaches, we offer a free flu shot.

Operating committee

SRO AG has an operating committee, which forms the link between the employees and the management. As a staff representative, it is available to the employees as a contact person.

Health promotion services

Within the framework of occupational health management, SRO AG offers ongoing offers in prevention in occupational safety and case management.

Fleet rebate

Our employees benefit from fleet discounts at various brands.

Allowances and bonuses

For assignments in the evening, at night and on weekends, an inconvenience allowance of CHF 9 per hour is paid out. For evening and night assignments, an additional time credit of 20% is credited. We also reward short-term intervention or the successful placement of a new team member with a premium.

Pension Fund

Our employees are insured by the pension fund SRO. SRO AG covers up to 60% of the contributions (instead of the usual 50%) and depending on the individual situation can be chosen between three savings plans.

Family and childcare

Employees legally entitled to family allowances also receive a monthly care allowance of CHF 180.– to CHF 250.–. Continued pay during 16 weeks of maternity leave and 15 days of paternity leave is 100%.
