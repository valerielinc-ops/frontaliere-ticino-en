Full description

The primary task of the assistant physician at the UHPA (Psychiatric Hospitalization Unit for Adolescents) will be to participate actively in the assessment, monitoring and care of mentally retarded adolescents, in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team. He or she will contribute to ensuring the quality of care in hospital settings, within a therapeutic framework adapted to the specificities of the adolescent population.

The missions are as follows

  • Conduct pedopsychiatric patient assessments
  • Develop and implement treatment plans in collaboration with the child psychiatrist team
  • Clinical follow-up of hospitalized patients, monitoring their progress and readjustment of treatment plans according to changes in health status
  • Prescribing and managing psychiatric drugs
  • Care for adolescents in situations of psychiatric crisis (acute crimes, suicide attempts, behavioural disorders)
  • Intervening in case of acute psychiatric crisis and providing emergency care for in-patient adolescents.
  • Attend multidisciplinary team meetings to discuss patient care
  • Maintain accurate and detailed electronic medical records
  • Provide family support in addition to individual patient care
  • Participate in the development and implementation of child-psychiatric protocols and procedures in the stationary sector
  • Collaborate with other health care providers to ensure comprehensive patient care
  • Participate in child psychiatric emergencies external to the unit
  • A medical degree or equivalent recognized by the Federal Medical Professions Commission (MEBEKO) is compulsory.
  • An experience of at least 1 year of attendance in adult psychiatry or paediatrics is required
  • An experience of at least 1 year in psychiatry and psychotherapy for children and adolescents would be ideal.
  • A proven ability to work effectively within a multidisciplinary team is essential.
  • A strong commitment to medical training and knowledge dissemination is required.
  • Mastery of the French language at a minimum level B2 is necessary.

We offer a varied and stimulating activity within a dynamic and constantly evolving establishment. The teams benefit from high-quality professional guidance and from real career development prospects and very good pay conditions. Thanks to a highly qualified multidisciplinary team and a high-performance technical platform, we guarantee a working environment conducive to excellence. Continuing training is of great importance in order to enable everyone to develop their skills and develop professionally. Community-based and multi-disciplinary institutions serving patients, the Arc Network offers high-value activities in the somatic and mental health fields. With close to 1,300 employees, the hospitals and Medicents of the Arc Network cover the medical needs of a population of more than 50,000 inhabitants and are characterised by their dynamism, excellent infrastructure and constant concern to develop services of proximity and quality. We are looking for an assistant doctor to join our team of stationary child psychiatrists of the Arc Network in Switzerland. In this full-time role, you will work with child psychiatrists and other health professionals (psychologists, nurses, medical secretary,) to provide high-quality child psychiatric care to our patients in a hospital setting. The Mental Health Department of the RCAF Network is an ISFM training institution (in recognition for category B) for hospital and outpatient activity.
