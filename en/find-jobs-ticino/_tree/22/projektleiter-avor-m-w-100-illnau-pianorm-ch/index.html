Full description

Project Manager / Avor (m/f) 100% About PIANORM Innenbau AG We are a renowned carpentry and family business with over 50 years of experience in the field of interior design. Our modernly equipped production facility relies on the latest technology to turn your individual wishes into reality. From single pieces of furniture to the complete interior design of your apartment or house in Illnau, Zurich or Winterthur - there are no limits to your ideas.

Your profile

  • Training: Completed basic craftsmanship training as carpenter EFZ
  • Further education: Successful further education in the field of AVOR / Project Manager.
  • Working method: You work independently, in a structured manner and have a high motivation for challenging tasks.
  • Know-how: skilled handling of CAD and industry software as well as technical understanding for modern CNC production

Your tasks

  • Work preparation & planning: You take over the independent and precise AVOR for varied projects in upscale interior design and kitchen construction.
  • CAD design: You draw detailed CAD work plans and create all necessary order papers and material lists.
  • Procurement: You are responsible for order-related material purchase and ensure material availability.

You can expect the following

  • Personal development and no standstill
  • Responsibility and competences
  • A top infrastructure and fair employment conditions
  • Support for further education in the carpentry industry
  • Working hours, our workweek has 41.5 hours
  • Benefits such as coffee and Friday afternoon free are included
