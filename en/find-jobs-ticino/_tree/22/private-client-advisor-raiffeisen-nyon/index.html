03.02.2026 Sois

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Mittelbünden, a successful and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Cazis and six other locations, we are looking for a personality as an individual client advisor.

What awaits you?

  • Recognizing and exploiting customer potential through active and continuous support of your own customer portfolio
  • Guaranteeing well-founded advice on figures, investment, provision and financing
  • Development of needs-based solutions taking into account the customer’s living situation as well as within the segment-specific / customer-specific product range
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures, in particular in contact campaigns

What do you bring?

  • Several years of experience in the commercial sector
  • Interest in the banking and financial services industry
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method with high customer orientation
  • Regional anchoring or willingness to actively network in the region of Central Alliances and in particular in the region of Albula-Surses
  • Enjoyment of personal customer support on site as well as flexibility for assignments and appointments in the region

We are looking for a committed personality who enjoys customer contact and wants to shape our region. What counts here is not only professional competence, but above all humanity, team spirit and the will to accompany our customers honestly and solution-oriented. I look forward to motivated colleagues who want to make a difference together with us. Rico Räth Team Leader Private Client Services Why Raiffeisen?
