We are looking for one

Specialist educator CDI-Activity rates to be agreed

At the Institution of Lavigny, we accompany adults with disabilities to their daily lives in a variety of places: polyhandicap, intellectual disability, aging.

Your mission

  • Accompany residents and organize daily life at the place of life, share and animate their daily lives with a focus on trust and respect.
  • Ensure physical and mental well-being, socialization of residents and value their social skills and roles.
  • Observe the health of the resident on a daily basis and act appropriately according to needs (intervention, call to the care centre and doctor, information to colleagues and follow-up as appropriate).
  • Implement personal projects according to the general goals and objectives of the place of life set at the interdisciplinary project meeting.
  • Work closely with the day shift, night shift and external stakeholders in a spirit of interdisciplinarity.
  • Promote and develop partnership in relations with families and legal representatives.
  • Participate in the development and implementation of sector and living area projects.
  • To contribute to the development of the quality of benefits to residents and the conditions under which they are delivered.

Your profile

  • Diploma ASE / ASSC / HES / ES or recognized equivalence.
  • Experience in accompanying people with disabilities.
  • Driver's license.

We offer

  • Working conditions agreed by the Comité du secteur social parapublic Vaudois
  • A salary in line with the function and in line with the scales of the CLCs in the Vaudian parapublic sector, with mobility-related benefits (parking, participation in public transport costs)
  • In-service training and specialized supervision (including TSA and challenge behaviour), internal mobility, possible funding of diplomas and certifications.
  • A business restaurant offering varied meals at preferential rates, in a pleasant business environment encouraging exchanges.
  • A wide choice of sports and relaxation activities (yoga, fitness, aquagym, pilates etc.) as well as massages, while enjoying regular business life events
  • Flexible and continuous schedules across sectors, with the possibility of part-time work for optimal work-life balance
