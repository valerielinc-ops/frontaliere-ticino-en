Yverdon-les-Bains, CH

Company

SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. At Yverdon Workshops, we are responsible for the heavy maintenance of all the rolling stock of SBB Voyageurs, certain subsidiaries and third party customers. Our services are fully customer-oriented and optimized according to Kaizen principles. Our main objective is to ensure the availability of the fleet according to the constraints of the schedule, by revising and modernizing all rolling stock. What you can move.

  • As a replacement for the Production Team Leader, you will replace the Team Leader.
  • You are involved in the development of the resources needed for the activity, both human and technical (recruitment, definition of objectives, individual monitoring and evaluation, training plan). You guarantee the level of skills and versatility of the collaborators necessary to carry out the production missions.
  • To help achieve the objectives, you monitor quality, timeliness, productivity and costs.
  • Based on Kaizen philosophy, you apply and promote the principles of continuous improvement on a daily basis.
  • You ensure compliance with safety and environmental regulations, as well as with various internal and external regulations.
  • You contribute to the operational conduct of the team and assist the staff in carrying out their missions.

What you bring.

  • You have a CFC in the electrical field and justified experience in this field with several years of experience in maintenance.
  • You hold the Intermediate Leadership Certificate and ideally completed by

Federal Superior Diploma in Industrial Production

  • Your journey has provided you with an initial experience in team management and an excellent sense of leadership.
  • You have good knowledge of MS Office and SAP tools.
  • Good communicator, you master French; basic knowledge of German would be an asset
  • You have a sense of responsibility, are dynamic and appreciate teamwork. You are also ambitious and want to move towards a future role as team leader.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
