Accounting manager

The Groupe Comptoir Immobilier is a leader in real estate services dating back to 1825\. With a culture of a family and human nature, we now have more than 380 employees located between the headquarters in Geneva and the various agencies located in the cantons of Vaud and Valais.

Based on our experience and know-how, we offer a full range of services such as stewardship, PPE administration, the sale and estimation of all types of goods, the management of promotions and architectural projects, renovations, as well as commercial and investment real estate.

Professionalism, performance, sense of service and quality of advice are the foundation of our values, combined with our commitment to sustainable development and our ethics based on relationships of respect and loyalty, a guarantee of our success. Recognised as a formative company, Comptoir Immobilier is also committed to offering its employees a stimulating and collaborative working environment by valuing the skills of everyone.

If you identify with our group, don't wait any longer: join us!

For our agency in Monthey, we are looking for one today: Accounting manager

Your main responsibilities:

  • Managing independently the accounting of a portfolio of buildings in management, in accordance with internal procedures and deadlines;
  • Processing and tracking of invoices, mortgage interest and current accounting transactions;
  • Produce management accounts, separate cash accounts, heating and ancillary charges;
  • Conduct checks and analyses of tenant accounts and ensure their accuracy;
  • Ensure the management of proprietary cash and make payments according to availability and maturity;
  • Take over the operations related to the takeover and loss of mandates, both administratively and accountingly;
  • Actively participate in quarterly and annual closures and ensure the reliability of the financial statements of managed buildings.

Your profile:

  • Successful experience of at least 4\-5 years in a similar position;
  • Advanced training in accounting, patent an asset;
  • Team spirit, rigor and precision;
  • Good understanding, adaptation and analysis;
  • Knowledge Quorum, an asset.

We offer

  • The opportunity to join a developing family group;
  • A dynamic environment affecting the various real estate professions;
  • Attractive remuneration corresponding to your skills and performance;
  • 5 weeks of vacation;
  • Flexible hours.

Entry: to be agreed

Loyalty, performance, dynamism and elegance are the qualities of mind that animate all our employees. If you also share these values, we will be happy to discover your complete application package (CV, cover letter, work certificates, diplomas) via JobUp only.
