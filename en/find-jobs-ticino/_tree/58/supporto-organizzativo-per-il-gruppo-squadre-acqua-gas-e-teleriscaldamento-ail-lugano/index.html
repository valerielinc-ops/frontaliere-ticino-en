Who we are

Aliantis is born from the evolution of the commercial activities of AIL and the desire to gather them in a dedicated structure, able to value the existing skills and to respond with greater flexibility to the transformations of the energy market. In this context, we look for an organized, reliable and customer-oriented person for the role of Employee / or Administrative – Secretariat and customer management B2B

What you will do concretely

You will be a reference point for secretariat activities and daily administrative management of B2B customers, ensuring punctual support to the commercial team and professional customer service. You will contribute to the proper management of requests, documents and internal processes, promoting coordination, quality and operational continuity.

In particular, you will take care of

  • manage secretariat activities, correspondence, appointments and organizational coordination

team;

  • provide organizational and administrative support to the Directorate, including preparing documents, the

coordination of appointments and monitoring of activities and deadlines;

  • ensure the grip and evasion of B2B customer requests, acting as an interface with units

competent interiors;

  • manage the administrative cycle of B2B contracts, supporting its preparation, control,

subscription, archiving and monitoring deadlines, as well as offerings, confirmations and other commercial and administrative documentation;

  • update customer records and information in business systems, ensuring completeness

and data quality;

  • collaborate with the sales team in monitoring deadlines, open activities and steps

operations related to customer management;

  • contribute to the continuous improvement of administrative processes and service to B2B customers.
