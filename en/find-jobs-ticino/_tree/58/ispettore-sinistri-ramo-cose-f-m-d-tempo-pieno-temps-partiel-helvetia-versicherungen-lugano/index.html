Full description

Together we define new standards and build the future: you will not just do tasks with us, you will work with people who take responsibility, maintain high standards and have the courage to walk new ways. We will provide you with an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand. Here you count your ideas, your initiative and your contribution. This will give you the opportunity to make a real difference for our customers, your team and your personal growth.

Tasks

  • Manage the complex claims files within the branch
  • You will act as expert claims, carrying out regular surveys in Italian Switzerland to assess the damage, follow its evolution and provide a thorough technical analysis.
  • You will analyze and determine the appropriate insurance cover, as well as the responsibilities involved.
  • You will develop collaboration with partners, craftsmen with the aim of promoting proactive management of practices and reducing the impact of costs.
  • At the heart of the dialogue with your counterparts, you will conduct negotiations and process correspondence with insureds, lawyers, experts and partners, enhancing your negotiating and relational skills.

Requirements

  • You have a solid knowledge of insurance products and experience in the management of claims, ideally in a position similar to that we seek.
  • Or you have a solid training as an engineer or technical construction, or as a director of the works, with significant experience in the construction sector.
  • Sei can manage a high amount of work independently; you have a capacity for resolving conflicts and negotiating skills.
  • Ottima knowledge of German and, ideally, a good level of French, as well as excellent editorial skills.

Our values We believe in peer cooperation, mutual trust and people willing to take responsibility. The different perspectives help us to develop better solutions, both for our customers and for us as a team.
