Full description

Rail travel is one of the safest forms of transport in the world, statistics show. But behind the figures are people – people like you! 60 million passengers a year rely on your expertise and ambition to keep the vehicles reliably maintained and thereby ensure their safety. That makes you proud as a skilled professional.

This is what awaits you

  • Conduct fault analyses followed by challenging repair work on vehicles and components
  • Perform a wide range of maintenance work on multiple units in the passenger vehicle fleet
  • Carry out challenging repairs on multiple units in the passenger vehicle fleet and their systems
  • Accurately record maintenance and fault data
  • Schedule, process and finalize vehicle stays

This is what you bring

  • Vocational training as an automotive mechatronics technician, automation technician, automation fitter, agricultural machinery mechanic or truck mechanic at EFZ level
  • IT user skills
  • Very good command of German
  • Working night and weekend shifts

Your benefits – this is what you can look forward to

  • We support the most flexible planning possible for your routes and shifts
  • Receive a GA 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare travelcard.
  • You have free time when others are working. There are additional allowances for night and weekend work.
  • You have the option of purchasing an additional five to ten vacation days per year.
  • Financial support for external further training and an exciting internal range of learning and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second-largest railway company. What's more: We are also a shipping company, a real estate firm, an international rail freight company, a bus company, and we transport cars safely through Lötschberg and Simplon. What connects us in all this? Pride in what we do every day. For us, mobility has many facets. And many exciting jobs.

Requirements

  • EDV Anwenderkenntnisse

  • Sehr gute Deutschkenntnisse

  • Leisten von Nacht- und Wochenendarbeit
