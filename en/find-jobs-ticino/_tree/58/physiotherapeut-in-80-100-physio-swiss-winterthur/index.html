  • You carry out physiotherapeutic treatments with a neurological focus and plan their content and processes independently.
  • You already have experience in the physiotherapeutic treatment of adults with a cognitive impairment and/or could Imagine working in this area.
  • You support the care of aids and ensure the greatest possible independence in everyday life and also advise the patients and their relatives.
  • You work with internal and external patients.

Why you?

  • Because you are trained in physiotherapy and have SRK recognition.

  • Because you like to work independently and at the same time contribute to the interdisciplinary team.
  • Because you have good administrative and organizational skills.

Why do we?

  • We offer regulated working hours.
  • We offer varied and responsible work in a dynamic and motivated team.
  • We offer a lively working atmosphere and an environment in which human values and trust are lived.

You get

  • 6 weeks vacation on a 42 hour. Week and above-average benefits

  • Own Kita directly in the house
  • Attractive internal and external training opportunities
  • Regular interdisciplinary exchange and supervision
