Reference code: JR135579

Found in 1755, Vacheron Constantin is the oldest watch Manufacture in continuous production for more than 270 years, actually perpetuating a proud heritage of watchmaking excellence and stylistic sophistication through generations of master craftsmen.

At the pinnacle of Haute Horlogerie and understated elegance, the Maison creates timespieces with unique technical and aesthetic signatures, and an extremely high level of finishing touches.

LEARNING OF CFC PRODUCTION

YOUR REASON TO BE IN THE HOUSE

Learning Horloger-e en Production takes place in 3 years at the Training Centre of the Vacheron Constantin factory of Plan-les-Ouates. The apprentice learns the essential skills of the production watchmaking industry by participating in assembly, adjustment and compliance of mechanical movements.

YOUR ROLE TO PLAY

  • You are responsible for the assembly of components (manual, automatic and quartz movements) in a sequence
  • You also make the installation of dials and needles
  • You carry out packing and finishing operations
  • You perform adjustment, control and compliance of the watch
  • You ensure strict adherence to procedures and quality standards
  • You make simple micromechanical tooling

QUALITY FOR EXCELLING

Success and eligibility in this role is based on

  • Compulsory education completed
  • A pass on your admission exams and are aged 15-22
  • Effort on manual work
  • A beautiful manual dexterity, as well as precise, methodical and careful work
  • Basic IT Skills
  • The mastery of French, written/oral, English is a plus
  • Your calm, rigor, patience and team spirit
  • Your example of reliability and procedural compliance

Vacheron Constantin celebrates over 270 years as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755.
