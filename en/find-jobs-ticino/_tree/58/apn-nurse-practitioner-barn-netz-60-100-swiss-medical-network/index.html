Healthcare is facing major challenges. Social, demographic and health policy changes require well-coordinated services to ensure the quality of medical care in the future. The medical center Ostermundigen is part of the Bärn network, an integrated care region in the greater Bern area. In the Bärn network, family practices, specialists, hospitals, therapeutic services, care and Spitex services work more closely together to better accompany patients along their health path. The goal is a coordinated, residential and high-quality care, with strong basic care as a central anchor. VIVA creates an alternative basic insurance model within this network with a focus on prevention, health coordination, chronic care management and personal support. The health coordination supports patients in everyday life, creates orientation in the care system and helps to avoid unnecessary duplications, emergency consultations and hospitalizations. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated APN Nurse Practitioner. You work closely with doctors, therapists, the care team and an already integrated APN and actively develop the APN role in primary care and for the new basic insurance model VIVA.
