Full description

Does your heart beat for older people? Is the nursing profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to meaningfully contribute your skills and experience to us in order to have a positive influence on the lives of our guests? Then you are a perfect fit for us!

We are looking for someone for the Tertianum Schibistei in Heimenschwand immediately or by appointment

Health specialist EFZ 60-80% on night duty

Your tasks

  • Holistic care and support for guests in accordance with our nursing principles and the nursing process
  • Carrying out treatment-related activities
  • Supporting our guests in personal hygiene and other everyday life activities
  • Regular tours
  • Observation and evaluation of nursing and care situations
  • Electronic care documentation using CareCoach (mobile possible with CareCoach smartphone)
  • Taking on and taking responsibility for assigned technical tasks at night

Your profile

  • Completed training as a health specialist EFZ
  • Experience in long-term care is an advantage
  • RAI knowledge desired or willingness to attend these courses (paid)
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy diverse challenges
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German
