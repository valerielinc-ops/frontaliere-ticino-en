Hardware Verification Specialist — Sonova

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

At Sonova, we create innovative hearing care solutions that improve the quality of life for millions of people worldwide. As a Hardware Verification Specialist, you will help ensure the quality, reliability, and performance of our hearing aids and accessories before they reach customers.

Working in a collaborative R&D environment, you will combine hands-on technical work with problem-solving and testing, contributing directly to products that make a meaningful difference in people's lives.

More about the role

In this role, you will

  • Execute hardware verification tests on hearing aids, accessories, and assemblies
  • Perform acoustic, electronic, mechanical, and chemical measurements using specialized laboratory equipment
  • Analyze test results and document findings in technical reports
  • Support the development and improvement of test methods, verification processes, and laboratory structures
  • Investigate unusual test behaviors and collaborate with engineers to identify root causes
  • Maintain laboratory equipment and support day-to-day lab operations
  • Contribute to R&D activities, team collaboration, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement initiatives

More about you

You are a hands-on and detail-oriented professional who enjoys understanding how electronic products work and finding reliable ways to verify their performance. You combine technical curiosity with a structured and quality-focused approach to your work.

Must-Have

  • Technical education in Electronics, Mechatronics, Electrical Engineering, or a related field
  • Hands-on experience with manufacturing, repair, servicing, or troubleshooting electronic devices
  • Practical experience with soldering and working with small electronic or electromechanical components
  • Strong knowledge of electronics and measurement technologies
  • Good English and German communication skills (written and spoken)
  • Proactive, quality-focused, and eager to learn

Nice to have

  • Experience in hardware verification, testing, or validation
  • Experience in medical devices or other regulated industries
  • Knowledge of acoustic measurements
  • Experience in results documentation
  • Familiarity with Confluence and MS Teams

More about what we offer

As one of the world’s leading hearing care providers headquartered in Switzerland, we’re committed to building an inclusive culture. We want to create an environment where you can balance a successful career with your commitments and interests outside of work, through our flexible hybrid working model. We offer a wide range of training opportunities for both your professional and personal development, and there are exceptional growth opportunities with individual development plans.

Magdalena Suszek, Talent Acquisition Partner, is looking forward to receiving your application (cover letter, CV, references and certificates) via our online job application platform.

For this vacancy only direct applications will be considered.
