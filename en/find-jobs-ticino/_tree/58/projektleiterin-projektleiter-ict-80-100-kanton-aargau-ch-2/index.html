Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Assuming overall project management, partial project management or project support in computer science and digitization projects over the entire project life cycle
  • Support and advise the departments with competence in technology, processes and project management in the design and implementation of effective computer and digitization projects
  • Accompanying our in-house customers in process design, business analysis, quality assurance and implementation of solutions
  • Coordinating and connecting collaboration between clients, departments, internal IT teams and external suppliers
  • Further develop and contribute to the project management section through innovative, trusting, effective and appreciative cooperation

What you bring

  • Several years of experience in managing computer science and digitalization projects, ideally in a complex organizational environment
  • University or university degree in computer science, business informatics or equivalent education
  • Further education in project and process management
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working methods as well as a good sense of priorities, risks and different interests
  • Communicative and open personality with pleasure in a trusting and appreciative cooperation with customers, specialist areas and suppliers
  • Delight in helping shape and implement innovative, viable and effective solutions for cantonal administration

The Department of Finance and Resources, with around 650 employees, is responsible as a cross-sectional department for ensuring that the canton has the necessary resources to fulfil its tasks.

Computer science solutions make daily work easier and more efficient. The employees of the Computer Science Department Aargau contribute to increasing the efficiency of the cantonal administration. You are responsible for the strategy and operation of central computer science. They accompany major cantonal projects and provide efficient and modern IT solutions that enable the public to have uncomplicated contact and exchange with the canton’s administrative offices.
