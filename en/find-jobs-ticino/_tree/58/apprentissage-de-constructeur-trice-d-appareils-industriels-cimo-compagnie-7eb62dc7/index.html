Full description

Industrial equipment manufacturer's apprenticeship CIMO Compagnie industrielle de Monthey SA is a Swiss company founded in 1997 with 450 employees in 80 specialized trades. As part of its strategy for sustainable development and innovation, CIMO is active, inter alia, in the fields of energy production and distribution, waste treatment, technical maintenance, engineering, automation, hygiene and safety, environment, laboratory and occupational medicine.

  • We are looking for candidates for one: Industrial Appliance Builder Learning
  • Your profile
  • Good spatial representation
  • Manual skill
  • Accuracy and thoroughness
  • Physical resistance
  • Ability to work as a team
  • Methodical spirit
  • Technical sense
  • Training plan
  • The learning takes place over 4 years and leads to the Federal Certificate of SWC Capacity.
  • Theoretical training:
  • Courses are given at the Ecole Professionnel Artisanat Service Communautaire (EPASC) in Martigny for one or two days a week.
  • Practical training:
  • In-company practical training takes place at CIMO, under the responsibility of a trainer.
  • The inter-company courses (CIE) take place at the Vocational Technical and Trades School (EPTM) in Sion.
  • The trade
  • As part of your apprenticeship, you will be asked to discover four options in the profession: piping, cauldron, locks and plastics.

In these 4 areas, you will make measurements, you will make unique parts according to 2D and 3D models, you will use advanced machines (CNC, robot, etc.), you will make welding through different processes, you will manufacture supports and will carry out the laying of these on the various construction sites.

  • The main task is to plan and implement complex industrial installations, parts and appliances.
  • Future prospects
  • After getting your SWC, you can consider the following enhancements:
  • Diploma of welding specialist
  • Federal patent
  • Federal diploma
  • Bachelor of Science HES
  • Etc.
