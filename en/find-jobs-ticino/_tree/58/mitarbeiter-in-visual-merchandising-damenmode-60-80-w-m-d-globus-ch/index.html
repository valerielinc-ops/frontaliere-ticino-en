Full description

With great attention to detail, our employees make GLOBUS the leading premium and luxury address in Switzerland. It is people like you who make the difference and bring the “place we love to be” to life together with us. Here you make the difference

  • You are responsible for brand and target group-oriented visual merchandising and styling in the field of women’s fashion.
  • You plan, design and visualize the promotions and detailed action plans according to the design guidelines and guidelines according to corporate design and corporate identity.
  • Rayon care and control are part of your daily task.
  • You develop product presentation concepts and take over the communication & communication of the concepts to the sales staff in the department store.
  • You implement the central globe promotion concepts professionally and on time.
  • You independently bring creative and innovative ideas for the presentation of goods and themed worlds.
  • You actively support changes and conversions in the department store.
  • You work closely with purchasing and central visual merchandising to present the new product range topics at the POS in a competent and attractive manner.
  • You train the sales staff at the POS in terms of visual merchandising.

The draws you out

  • You have completed an apprenticeship EFZ as a polydesigner: in 3D, of advantage in the field of fashion.
  • You are a communicative and team capable personality with high passion and affinity for women’s fashion.
  • You have a good relationship and a good appearance.
  • You are very reliable, organized, flexible and act on your own initiative.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German
  • Other languages are not a must, but of great advantage for our international guests
