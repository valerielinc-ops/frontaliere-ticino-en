Full description

At La Tour Hospital, we are committed to bringing together professionals who are passionate, committed and motivated by a common goal: to provide each patient with quality care in an environment where collaboration, innovation and continuous improvement are central. Do you want to evolve within a dynamic operating unit, participate actively in the smooth running of interventions and put your skills at the service of an experienced multidisciplinary team? Join us and contribute every day to the excellence of our care!

In order to complete our Operating Block we recruit an(-e)

Technical assistant specialised in the operating room (ATSSO) 100% (ref: 2030)

Your role at the heart of the operating room

In close cooperation with instrumental nurses, anaesthetist nurses, room assistants and surgeons, you play an essential role in the proper functioning of the procedures and the safety of patients.

Every day you will be asked to

  • Accept and install patients in the operating room, ensuring their comfort and safety.
  • Accompany the surgical team throughout the operation and meet its equipment and logistics needs.
  • Prepare and restore the operating rooms after each operation, ensuring the availability and good condition of the equipment.
  • Strictly apply the hygiene, care and safety protocols adapted to the different procedures.
  • Contribute to the quality and efficiency of the service, thanks to your organizational sense, responsiveness and teamwork.

Your profile: competence, rigour and commitment

Do you hold the technical assistant certificate specialized in the operating room (ATSSO) and have a confirmed experience in the operating room?

We are looking for a person who is distinguished by

  • A recognized ATSSO certificate.
  • At least 3 years of experience in the operating room — an indispensable condition.
  • A great versatility and a strong sense of rigour.
  • Excellent stress management and ability to react calmly and effectively.
  • A true team spirit and constructive communication with different professionals.
  • Ongoing commitment to the quality, safety and well-being of patients.

What we offer you

  • A 100% CDI within a reference institution.
  • A stimulating working environment, with a multidisciplinary team.
  • A varied position that values your expertise and autonomy.
  • The ability to put your skills at the service of demanding and quality management.
  • A team that focuses on collaboration, respect and professional commitment.

Do you want to give new impetus to your professional career?

Join La Tour Hospital and take part in a team that places quality of care, patient safety and excellence at the heart of its priorities.

We look forward to discovering your application!
