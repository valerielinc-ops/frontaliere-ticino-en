Migration moves

The State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) is responsible for all issues relating to the right of aliens and asylum in Switzerland. The Directorate International Affairs (DB INT) of the State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) is responsible for implementing Swiss foreign migration policy. It focuses in particular on strengthening protection and reception structures in the countries of origin and transit, preventing irregular migration, returning rejected asylum seekers and ensuring Switzerland’s internal and external security in line with national migration policy.
