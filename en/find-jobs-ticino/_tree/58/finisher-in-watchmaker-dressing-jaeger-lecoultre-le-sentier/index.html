Reference code: JR127117 Since 1833, driven by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressed its inventiveness through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication. YOUR MISSION: Within our Manufacturing Directorate and attached to the Boxes Department, you will be in charge of driving, coordinating and controlling the Boxes Complications workshop in order to comply with the programs and their implementation under the conditions of quantity, quality, cost, delay and safety. HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT? Finishing and Polishing of Watch Boxes: Perform all finishing operations (polished, satin, brushing, etc.) on watch boxes and components, using specific machines and ensuring compliance with technical and aesthetic specifications. Preparation and Assembly: Prepare the boxes for subsequent steps (galvanoplasty, crimping) and assemble their various elements (carriage, bezel, bottom, ice, joints) according to tolerances and sealing procedures, and then check their fit and operation. Competences Jewellery Specifics: Prepare boxes for crimping, finish jewellery parts (including adjustments or small repairs) and master the specifics of precious metals (gold, platinum). Quality Control: Perform rigorous visual and dimensional controls at each stage, identify and report non-compliance, and ensure traceability of parts and operations. THE KEY OF YOUR SUCCESS? YOUR SKILLS! Training and Experience: CFC Terminator Dress Watchmaker, ideally complemented by a diploma or significant experience. Minimum 3 years experience in watchmaking termination (high-end products) and jewellery (preparation for crimping, finishing of jewellery pieces). Technical skills: Mastery of watch finishing techniques (polishing, satining, brushing) and associated machines. Excellent dexterity, visual acuity, knowledge of metals (steel, gold, platinum, titanium) and ability to read technical drawings. Personal qualities: Minutia, precision, rigour, sense of organization, autonomy and team spirit. Ability to manage pressure and meet deadlines, with a passion for high quality watchmaking and jewellery.
