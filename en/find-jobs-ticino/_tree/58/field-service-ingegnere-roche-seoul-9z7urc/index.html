The Position Field Service Engineer Location: Mansfield and surrounding areas (Central East UK) Job Function: Technical Customer Support & Service Engineering

The Opportunity Are you looking for an exciting challenge in a key support role within a team of experts across high-profile customer accounts where the work you do truly matters? We are seeking a Field Service Engineer to join our solution-focused team of Applications, Information Solutions, and Engineering Specialists covering the Central East region of the UK (Mansfield and surrounding areas) .

In this role, you will provide comprehensive, whole-solution support for a diverse range of high-value medical diagnostic equipment. From installation, fault diagnosis, and preventative maintenance to customer training and technical troubleshooting, you will play a vital role in shaping the future of healthcare delivery across NHS, University, and private laboratory environments. Key Responsibilities

  • Empower Yourself: Take charge of providing technical support for complex electrical and mechanical medical diagnostic equipment, ensuring optimal performance, safety, and reliability.
  • Deliver Impact: Perform fault diagnosis, routine repairs, preventative maintenance, and system installations, both on-site and via remote diagnostic support.
  • Build Connections: Represent Roche Diagnostics with professionalism, empathy, and integrity, building trust-based relationships with key laboratory personnel and stakeholders.
  • Make a Difference: Collaborate closely with multi-disciplinary teams in hospital and laboratory environments to enhance operational efficiency and improve patient care outcomes.
  • Drive Proactive Solutions: Work as part of a close-knit, multi-disciplinary team offering proactive implementation, technical systems support, and analytical reviews alongside reactive troubleshooting.

Who You Are

You thrive under pressure in a fast-paced, time-sensitive environment with shifting priorities. Resilient, tenacious, and analytical, you possess a passion for insights and feel comfortable working with technical, scientific, and numeric data.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Proven Experience: Qualified Engineer with a proven track record servicing high-value capital equipment.
  • Education: Equivalent qualification to HNC/HND level or above in Electrical, Mechanical, or Systems Engineering is highly desirable.
  • Customer Focus: A confident, customer-centric mindset with the ability to navigate complex matrix environments and build productive relationships.
  • Collaboration: Strong capability to work effectively within a multi-disciplinary team (Applications, IT, and Engineering) to deliver a seamless service solution.
  • Flexibility: Willingness and capability to work within a 7-day support model to meet customer and patient needs across the region.

What We Offer

  • Unlock Your Potential: A highly competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package, coupled with clear opportunities for career progression.
  • Invest in Yourself: Access to Roche’s world-renowned product training programs, continuous professional development, and expert mentoring.
  • Join the Journey: Be part of a pioneering global organization dedicated to delivering innovative diagnostic solutions that make a tangible impact on human health.
