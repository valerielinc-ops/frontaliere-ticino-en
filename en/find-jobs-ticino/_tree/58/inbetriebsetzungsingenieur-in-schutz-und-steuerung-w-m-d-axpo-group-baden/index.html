Pensum: 80% - 100% | Baden

Are you looking for a new, responsible challenge in an exciting environment? Then bring your knowledge and commitment. For our operations in northeast Switzerland, we are looking for a dedicated personality for commissioning in Axpo’s distribution network and customers’ plants. We provide an environment for independent work with comprehensive technical equipment, including a company vehicle, which can optionally be used privately. Tasks & Responsibilities:Commissioning of protective equipment and control systems in the substations and power plants on site

Control and conduct of periodic maintenance tests of protective systems

Setting/parameterizing protection and control systems

Support for error analysis and troubleshooting in the context of protective technology

Participation in KVP and Q-Circle Measures in the field of engineering and commissioningProfile & requirements:You have successfully completed your studies or a comparable qualification in the field of electrical engineering

You have experience in the parameterization, testing and commissioning of protection and control systems in the environment of medium and high-voltage systems

You have an independent, efficient and responsible way of working and have a high demand on your work quality

You have a good knowledge of German (English/French is an advantage)About the team:We offer you a team-oriented environment with a collegial working atmosphere, by being able to actively contribute your strengths and thus contribute to the success of Axpo. An experienced, motivated and open team of specialists awaits you. In addition, you have the opportunity to acquire special knowledge, take on professional responsibility and develop yourself personally as well as professionally. You will be comprehensively introduced to your responsible and versatile tasks. #Grid
