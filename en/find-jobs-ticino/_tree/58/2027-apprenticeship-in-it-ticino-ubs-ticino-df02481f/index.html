Full description

Do you have a passion for new technologies that simplify life? You want to turn it into your profession? Then join our new levers and plan with us the future of the banking sector.

We offer you

  • four-year training with insights into our different IT teams
  • in every rotation, a Practical Advisor that supports you and accompanies you
  • a practice-oriented IT training that prepares you for the digital future

In the “apps development” address, make sure that our programs and applications function smoothly and safely, developing creative and high-quality software solutions, correcting errors and introducing continuous improvements. You will immerse yourself in the world of programming languages like Java, Python and C#, you will test programs and applications, collaborate closely with other developers and developers.

Your training What makes apprenticeship special at UBS? We offer the perfect combination of practice and theory. In the UBS training center, during the first year you will learn everything you need to become a real professional in the field of technology. This includes not only the practical experience, but also the frequency at the professional school 1-2 days a week to deepen your knowledge. In the next three years you will be working in international teams on exciting IT projects for UBS and therefore for our customers. In addition to a great team spirit, you will live firsthand as your projects will come to life and produce concrete results.

In the end you will get your federal capacity certificate (AFC) and you will be perfectly prepared for the future! Finally your journey with us will not end here. Many apprentices will start a promising career as IT specialists or deepen their skills in our biennial youth promotion program.

Your team You will be part of a group of IT apprentices. You will meet both during internal and external training. From these experiences often come friendships that go far beyond the job.

In your team, you will actively welcome the future. Together, we integrate artificial intelligence (AI) into our digital solutions and processes to drive contemporary innovations, work more efficiently and simplify our daily lives. You will discover how artificial intelligence makes our digital products smarter and how you can contribute to further development. Join our team and contribute to the change you want to see.

Your knowledge and experiences You have completed the highest compulsory school level and have good grades in mathematics, German and English.

What do you want?

  • You have a passion for new trends and technologies and appreciate teamwork
  • Are you motivated to constantly learn new things and make your contribution
  • You like to think out of patterns and find solutions for each challenge
  • Quickly assimilate new information and imagine innovative solutions
