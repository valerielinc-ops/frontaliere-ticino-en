Full description

We ensure that warranty cases run smoothly – with quality, transparency and efficiency. This creates a customer experience that convinces even when something goes wrong. We are looking for people who want to think, tackle and further develop the department with us. Honest communication and shared responsibility are not posters on the wall, but everyday life.

Mansioni

  • Handling warranty and complaint cases independently from acceptance to conclusion
  • Check, document and import returned items for repair, replacement or recovery
  • Record repair orders in the ERP, track them and keep an eye on deadlines
  • Move, repackage and safely provide heavy items for onward transport
  • Identify stumbling blocks in our processes and initiate improvements

Requirements

  • Advantage: Experience from warehouse logistics or in logistics processes
  • First touch points with an ERP system and nimble fingers on the PC
  • Dealing with heavy articles is part of your everyday work – physical resilience is therefore a prerequisite
  • Great motivation, personal responsibility and commitment to take on tasks reliably and do a good job in day-to-day business
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • The ability to adapt to continuous changes in the working environment
  • No education/training required
  • German (flowing)
