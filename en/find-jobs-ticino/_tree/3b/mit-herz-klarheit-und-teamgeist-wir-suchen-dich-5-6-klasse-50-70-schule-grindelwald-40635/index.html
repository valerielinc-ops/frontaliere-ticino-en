Full description

Portrait For our 5/6. With immediate effect or by appointment, we are looking for a committed and cordial class teacher who enjoys accompanying children on their learning path and designing schools together with a motivated team.

Tasks

  • As a class teacher, you provide a good classroom management for a positive learning atmosphere, clear processes and an appreciative cooperation.
  • You plan and design varied, differentiated and learning-promoting learning opportunities.
  • You promote the independence and personal responsibility of the students.
  • You work closely with the teachers and organize the partly departmental lessons together with them.
  • You maintain an open and constructive cooperation with parents and other professionals.
  • You contribute your ideas and actively participate in the teaching and school development.
  • You promote the independence and personal responsibility of the students.

Requirements

  • A teaching diploma for primary level, ideally with experience in cycle 2.
  • Structure and orientation: You create a reliable framework in which the children feel safe and can learn successfully.
  • Heartiness and humor: You meet the children with warmth, patience and a positive attitude.
  • Team spirit: You like to work closely with others, bring your own ideas and work together with the team.
  • Openness: You enjoy developing lessons and trying new things.

We offer

  • A school that would rather ask “How could we make this possible?” instead of “Why can’t we?”
  • A warm, helpful team that holds together and a working environment in which laughter, personal exchange and common aperitifs also have their place.
  • Spaces, materials and structures that make it easier for you to accompany
  • Children who challenge you – in the best sense – and show you why you love this profession
  • A lot of room for manoeuvre and even more appreciation

And a place where others go on holiday!
