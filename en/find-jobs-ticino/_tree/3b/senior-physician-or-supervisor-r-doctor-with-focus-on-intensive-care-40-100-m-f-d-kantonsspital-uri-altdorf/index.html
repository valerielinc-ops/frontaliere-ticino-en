Full description

With its comprehensive range of services, our hospital provides an ambulatory and the stationary extended basic care for the canton of Uri safer. In order to strengthen our medical team, we are looking for a senior physician for intensive care medicine with complementary work in internal medicine or anesthesia. YOUR TASKS

  • Specialist co-responsibility for high-quality care of intensive care patients
  • Co-design and further development of the intensive care unit (6 beds) – together with the head of intensive care and the head of intensive care
  • Participation in day-to-day business and service operations according to your specialist training
  • Supervision and further training of assistant doctors
  • Co-design of in-house training OUR REQUIREMENTS
  • Specialist title FMH or Mebeko for intensive care medicine, general internal medicine or anesthesiology
  • Practical experience as a specialist in intensive care medicine
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • Team-capable, committed and emphatic personality with a good knowledge of German
  • Your personal development is just as important to us as the development of our hospital in general and intensive care medicine in particular
  • Support from a motivated and highly qualified team
  • Attractive employment conditions and personal development and co-creation opportunities as well as other benefits shape our corporate culture and make us an attractive employer Our specialist head of intensive care medicine, Florian Mayer, will be happy to provide you with further information. We look forward to your application!

Requirements
