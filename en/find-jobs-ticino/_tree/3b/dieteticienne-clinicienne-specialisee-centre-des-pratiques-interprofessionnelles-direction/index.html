Full description

Within the Centre for Interprofessional Practices (CPI), the specialized clinician dietician brings her expertise in complex nutritional situations. She advises and assists IMAD professionals and helps improve nutrition and diet practices in the context of home and community care. The specialized clinician dietician is a resource for teams in complex situations and contributes to the development of nutrition and diet practices through clinical expertise, leadership, implementation and continuous improvement. Your missions and responsibilities

Expertise in Human Nutrition and Metabolism

  • Provide clinical expertise specialized in nutrition and dietetics in the analysis and management of complex nutritional situations at home.
  • Support dietitians in nutritional assessment, development of nutritional diagnosis and development of patient-friendly intervention plans (PNPs).
  • Contribute to the identification of nutritional risk factors and the identification of responses tailored to the home and community context.
  • Develop, update and promote evidence-based clinical recommendations, tools and resources.

Advanced Practices and Leadership in Nutrition and Diet

  • Assume a second-line reference role for clinical situations requiring advanced clinical expertise in nutrition and diet.
  • Assist professionals in the analysis of complex situations and support clinical decision-making.
  • To promote the development of the skills of dieticians through practice analysis, counselling and training.
  • Contribute to the development of clinical leadership and recognition of nutrition and diet expertise within IMAD.
  • To contribute to the evolution of professional practices and the development of innovative responses adapted to the challenges of home care.
  • Participate in the design, implementation and evaluation of clinical projects to improve the quality and safety of nutrition and diet services.
  • Develop and update evidence-based recommendations, clinical processes, etc.
  • Contribute to the training and integration of scientific knowledge into professional practices.

Nutrition epidemiology and implementation in clinical nutrition:

  • Ensure scientific and professional monitoring in nutrition and dietetics to ensure the updating of practices in accordance with the latest knowledge.
  • Mobilize scientific and nutritional epidemiology data to support clinical decision-making.
  • Identify emerging issues that may be subject to evaluation, implementation or applied research projects.
  • Participate in institutional, evaluation or innovation projects related to client needs and home care issues.
  • Contribute to the analysis, valuation and dissemination of the results of projects and work carried out to support the development of benefits.

Interprofessional collaboration

  • Working with all IMAD professionals,
  • Promote collaborative interprofessional practices in managing complex situations.
  • Maintain collaborations with the health-social network and relevant partners.
  • HES-SO Bachelor in Nutrition and Dietetics, completed with a Master of Science in Health Sciences (MScSa), obtained or in progress.
  • Significant clinical experience in nutrition and dietetics,
  • Experience in accompanying complex situations, training or developing practices.
  • Experience in project management and/or quality improvement.
  • Excellent analytical, synthesis, writing and scientific outreach capabilities.
  • Clinical leadership, autonomy, proactivity and excellent relational and pedagogical abilities.
  • Ability to work in interprofessionality and to be part of institutional orientation.
  • Good knowledge of the context of home care and the Geneva health and social network: an asset.
  • Knowledge of English (read and spoken): desired.
