Damage / Services Busy: 50-100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Vehicle damage is evolving and you can actively shape this change. In this versatile role, you work closely with the leadership and leadership team, bring your ideas and take responsibility for different topics. You can expect an exciting combination of organization, coordination and conceptual work with space for initiative.

What you do

  • You support the management of vehicle damage and the management team in strategic, operational and organizational issues and prepare the most decision-making basis
  • You coordinate projects and cross-cutting initiatives, keep an overview and ensure that agreed measures are implemented
  • You accompany the further development of the vehicle damage area and contribute your ideas to the design of new processes and working methods
  • You prepare management meetings and presentations and provide valuable impulses for the management of the area with sound analysis and meaningful reporting
  • You organize workshops, events and leadership events and promote efficient cooperation between the participants

What you bring

  • A higher education (HF, FH, university or comparable) as well as experience in a management assistant, staff function or comparable role
  • A good understanding of management and organizational processes as well as enjoyment of versatile and demanding tasks
  • Organizational talent, analytical thinking skills and an independent, reliable and forward-looking working method
  • A good sense of urgency and the ability to keep track of tasks and set priorities correctly
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft 365, especially PowerPoint, Excel, Teams and SharePoint
  • Initiative and communication strength as well as the ability to drive forward topics and shape change
