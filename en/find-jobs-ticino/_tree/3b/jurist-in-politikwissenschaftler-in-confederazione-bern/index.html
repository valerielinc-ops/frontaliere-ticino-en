Put to the Point

Do you like the interface between administration and Parliament? Do you like to use your legal or political science know-how for socially relevant topics such as safe food, healthy and species-appropriate animals or for the contemporary approval of plant protection products? Then a motivated and committed team looks forward to your application.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20883
  • Location: Schwarzenburgstrasse 155, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Editing responses to parliamentary initiatives
  • Write reports on parliamentary initiatives and prepare speaker notes for the head of the department
  • Ensure timely and correct planning, management, processing and control of transactions and dossiers
  • Review submissions on official consultations of other offices and co-reporting procedures of other departments
  • Support in legal clarifications and deliberations and participate in legislative projects
  • Working in internal, interdisciplinary working groups

This makes them unique.

  • Master in Law or Political Sciences (experience in Federal Administration of Advantage)
  • Excellent analytical skills, high self-motivation and rapid comprehension, openness and networked thinking
  • Solution-oriented, addressee-oriented and very exact working method, skillfulness in written and oral expression, high social competence and team skills
  • Strong political sense and interest in administrative processes
  • German, French or Italian mother tongue with very good knowledge of a second official language and English

For the welfare of humans and animals

The Federal Food Safety and Veterinary Office (BLV) is the federal competence centre for food safety, nutrition, animal health, animal welfare and species protection in international trade. With respect, competence, trust, commitment, creativity and openness, we make a great contribution to the common good. As an employee of the BLV, you actively promote the health and well-being of humans and animals.

The reconciliation of work and family as well as the professional development opportunities are highly appreciated by the employees and managers of the BLV.

The offices of the Federal Department of the Interior (EDI) are systematically committed to good working conditions and the health of their employees and are awarded the label Friendly Work Space.
