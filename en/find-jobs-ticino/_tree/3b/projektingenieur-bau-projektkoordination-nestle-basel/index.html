Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet

As a Project Engineer Construction & Project Coordination at Nestlé Suisse, your mission is to ensure the planning, coordination and realization of construction projects of varying complexity for our THOMY factory in Basel. The range of tasks ranges from smaller maintenance and renovation measures to new and conversion projects. You will make a difference by working closely with internal clients, authorities, planners, architects, engineers and entrepreneurs. This is your opportunity to take on responsibility, develop your expertise and actively shape the future of our production site.

As a project engineer, you will make a difference in construction and project coordination.

  • Independent management and support of construction projects from need definition to commissioning.
  • Planning, tendering, awarding and monitoring of construction and maintenance measures as well as drawing up project, cost and schedules and ensuring compliance with them.
  • Leadership and coordination of external planners, architects, engineers and entrepreneurs.
  • Implementation of condition analyses and development of renovation and maintenance concepts as well as organization and support of conversions, renovations and smaller new construction projects.
  • Supervision of construction, construction site visits and control of offers, invoices and supplements.
  • Ensure compliance with legal and Nestlé-internal regulations on occupational safety, internal requirements, quality standards and compliance guidelines for the construction sector as well as contact person for internal customers, authorities, planners and entrepreneurs.

This makes them successful.

  • Background as a civil engineer, architect, technician of construction planning/construction management or comparable qualification with experience in project management and construction.
  • Experience in planning, coordinating and realizing construction and conversion projects.
  • Knowledge of construction management, tendering and procurement procedures as well as a good understanding of structural, economic and organisational connections.
  • Experience in dealing with authorities, planners and entrepreneurs as well as an independent, structured and solution-oriented working method in close coordination with the other departments of the factory.
  • Strong communication, organizational and enforcement capabilities and secure use of MS Office
  • Knowledge of CAD or project management tools is an advantage.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage.
  • Courage, team orientation, agility and continuous willingness to learn.
