Full description

Do you like working with people? You would like to work for the health center for the Old Bullinger-Hardau on January 1, 2027 or by agreement with our care team?

Tasks

  • They are responsible for the care and care process.
  • They are responsible for planning, implementing, monitoring and adapting the care process as well as delegating tasks to employees of other levels of competence.
  • They advise and accompany residents and relatives during illness and crises.
  • They participate in the development of departmental goals to ensure quality and cooperation.
  • You take on day-to-day responsibility in the department.

Profile

  • Empathetic personality with a strong human orientation
  • Several years of professional experience with complex nursing situations as well as psychiatric experience are beneficial
  • Completed training as a nurse (HF/FH/DNII) with SRC recognition for diplomas from abroad
  • Excellent knowledge of German in words and writing as well as excellent communication skills
  • Strong sense of responsibility, the ability to think in a network and high resilience

Enjoy an exciting and varied area of responsibility in a professional and team-oriented working environment with du culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life.

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The Health Center for the Old Bullinger-Hardau is part of the distinctive Hardau settlement in District 4. We offer you the best of both worlds: peace and relaxation in your own apartment with balcony and the lively city center right outside your door. Here you work together with 70 colleagues.
