Full description

Strengthening employees: 100% healthy Bundesplatz 15, 6002 Lucerne Our employees are satisfied because CONCORDIA is like a second home for them. Maybe yours too soon? We are strengthening our field of performance technology and are looking for initiative and open personalities like you.

This is what awaits you

  • Together with your team, you will edit OCR-processed invoice data (Optical Character Recognition) from digital and physical input channels.
  • You plan and coordinate human resources and support your employees in their professional and personal development.
  • You create the conditions for efficient cooperation and ensure the high quality standards.
  • If necessary, you support your team in day-to-day operations and ensure a smooth process in the areas of QA and OCR accounting.
  • In close cooperation with the Product Owner (PO), you identify optimization potentials, formulate requirements and accompany their successful implementation.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of leadership experience in dealing with different personalities, generations and cultures.
  • As a leader, you convince with your clear, empathetic and reliable leadership style.
  • You have completed a commercial education and trained in the business sector.
  • You like to take part in day-to-day business and confidently deal with the repetitive nature of your tasks in your leadership role.
  • You enjoy working with data and creating evaluations - especially with Excel - you master perfectly.
