Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate - that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company now employs over 3,000 people from more than 80 countries, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters is located in the Canton of Glarus. Against a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates and develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The "Retail" department comprises over 250 Chocolateries in over 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite our customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again. Do you also want to be part of it? Then come to us. That's what you can expect. You consult customers in a customer-oriented manner and sell our diverse product range, for example when selecting gifts. You take care of the cash handling and ensure precise and correct billing. You present our select confectionery and chocolate assortment in an inviting and tasteful way. You adhere to our high quality standards with dedication and care. You ensure the observance of the hygiene and freshness concept. What you bring. You bring valuable professional experience in retail and have a genuine passion for sales. You love to offer our customers a unique and special shopping experience. In turbulent times, you always put quality and customer orientation first. You take on responsibility, are a true team player and work hand in hand with your colleagues. You appreciate open and respectful communication and are proficient in German and English; knowledge of French and other languages is an additional advantage. You are not afraid of flexible working hours and are also available on weekends, Saturdays, Sundays or holidays. What we offer. An exciting work environment with motivated colleagues. A position with a lot of autonomy. The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas. An open and respectful feedback culture. Internal and external training. Individual career opportunities. Generous discounts on our products. Chocolate at work. Good accessibility of the locations. Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh. Sounds interesting? Then apply with us. Tanja Munz, Store Manager, +41 52 203 34 87, is happy to answer any questions you may have. We apologize for not considering external recruitment agencies in the selection of this position.
