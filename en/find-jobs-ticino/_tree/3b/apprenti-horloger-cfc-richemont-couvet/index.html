WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

Missions

From the beginning of August 2027, you will be trained for 3 years to obtain your Federal Certificate of Capacity in Clockmaking. During your apprenticeship, you will develop your technical skills and discover different areas of watchmaking.

During this training, you will develop your skills by working on a variety of watch models, while acquiring essential know-how in terms of efficiency and productivity.

Responsibilities

  • Mastering the assembly of various watch movements: mechanical, automatic, quartz and chronograph
  • Initiate the basic techniques of micromechanics, including limage, precision turning (on turn 70), metrology, milling, drilling, as well as thermal treatments (tremp and income)
  • Acquire the skills in dressing: placement of the dial and needles, coupling, adjustment of the bracelet and mounting of the boxes
  • Training in specific pivot, tuning and tuning operations
  • Develop pendulum concepts
  • Participate in various practical internships in our workshops and with partner companies

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE

  • Excellent management of learning outcomes and proven learning capacity are required to complete vocational training
  • Excellent mathematical skills and interest in logical and technical reasoning
  • Interest and curiosity and motivation for watchmaking products and precision mechanisms
  • Manual dexterity, autonomy and rigour
  • Faculty of concentration, patience and logical reasoning
  • Team spirit and relationship ease
