12.11.2026 St. Gallen Product Manager Bank Packages & Cards (m/f/d)80 %

Become part of our team!

In our Basic Banking department, we are shaping the comprehensive basic offering for private customers and continuously developing it. Bank packages and card products are among Raiffeisen’s most important customer offers and make a significant contribution to an attractive and contemporary customer experience. Together with internal and external partners, we develop customer-oriented solutions and shape our offer for the future. To strengthen our team based in St. Gallen and the opportunity to work partly in the home office, we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement a committed and independent person with experience in basic banking.

What awaits you?

  • Responsibility for the further development of Raiffeisen bank packages and co-design of card products
  • Analysis of customer needs, market trends and key figures as well as derivation of optimization measures
  • Develop and prioritize professional requirements in close cooperation with IT, Operations, Marketing and Sales
  • Cooperation in strategic and operational projects in the field of basic banking and cards
  • Advising and supporting the Raiffeisen banks
  • Representation of Raiffeisen in internal and external expert bodies

What do you bring?

  • Higher education or further education (uni/FH/HF) in business administration, banking or a similar discipline
  • Experience in product management or in the further development of products and services
  • Banking background, ideally in the area of basic banking or card products
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills
  • High affinity for IT topics and digital solutions
  • Communication strength and enjoyment of working in interdisciplinary teams
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of French and English is an advantage

As part of our team, you will design the central basic offer for Raiffeisen customers throughout Switzerland. You combine customer needs, analytical thinking and digital solutions and continuously develop our basic banking offering. Philipp Flammer Group Leader Packages & Cards Why Raiffeisen?

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in St. Gallen?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed.
