Director General The Balcony of Jura Health Network (RSBJ) is a major player in health in the Vaudian North. Located in Sainte-Croix, it brings together nearly 320 collaborators in a community hospital, an EMS, a medical-social support structure (CAT and LADA) and 2 medical offices. Our mission is clear: to provide people with access to quality, human and coordinated care while developing innovative models to meet the challenges of ageing, chronic diseases and the evolution of the health system.

As part of its development, the RSBJ seeks its

Director General Member of the Executive Committee, along with the Medical Director and the Director of Care, you carry the strategic vision of the institution and ensure its overall management. You drive the transformations necessary for the evolution of the RSBJ, in a demanding environment where care excellence, economic balance, innovation and proximity combine. You develop relationships of trust with cantonal authorities, health network partners and teams, in a spirit of dialogue, responsibility and cooperation.

The challenges ahead

  • Deploy the institutional strategy and support organizational transformations
  • Work to improve economic efficiency while preserving the quality of services
  • Strengthening collaborations with Vaudian health system partners
  • Develop integrated care and health pathways to meet the needs of the population
  • Supporting a managerial culture based on trust, responsibility, innovation and continuous improvement
  • Contribute to the outreach of an establishment recognized for its services to the population of the Balcony of Jura and surrounding areas.

Your profile

You have strong leadership experience in the health sector and know how to combine strategic vision, leadership and delivery capacity.

You are to the benefit

  • higher education (HES, University or Polytechnic) in the fields of health, management or business management
  • of a recognized title in hospital management (Patent, Diploma of Expert, MAS or equivalent recognized by the Cantonal Medical Service)
  • a confirmed experience of at least five years in an executive or executive function of a health facility
  • an excellent knowledge of the Swiss health care system, the Vaudois legal framework and hospital financing mechanisms
