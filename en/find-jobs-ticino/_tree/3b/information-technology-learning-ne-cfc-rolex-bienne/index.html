Full description

Motivated by an IT apprenticeship? Ready to become a reference in your profession through quality training in a leading company? Join us!

The IT company develops, integrates, installs, secures and maintains IT systems and applications. In its support function, it provides support to users and handles infrastructure, software and personal computers connected to the company's network.

This learning is suitable for young people who are passionate about the field and want to embark on a constantly evolving profession.

Rolex offers training corresponding to the two existing guidelines for obtaining the CFC (application development or operation and infrastructure).

Profile

  • Interest and skills in mathematics and technical fields
  • Capacity for concentration and adaptation to technological change
  • Logical, methodical and analytical spirit

To apply

  • your school reports for the last three years.
