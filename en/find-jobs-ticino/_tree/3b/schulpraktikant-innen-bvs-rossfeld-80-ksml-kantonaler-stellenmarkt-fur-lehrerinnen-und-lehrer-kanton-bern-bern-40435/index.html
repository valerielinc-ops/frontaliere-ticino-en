Full description

Portrait Are you still looking for a job as a school intern: in August 2027? We look forward to your application!

The Rossfeld Foundation offers schools, residential groups and therapy for children and adolescents with a physical disability who are capable of normal education. In the Canton of Bern, we are the only competence centre for children and young people with physical disabilities in the regular school sector.

All interns take part in our internal internship training and receive personal support from our teachers.

Tasks

You will

  • in one of ten classes (kindergarten to upper school)
  • Supporting teachers in curative education
  • have minor work assignments on residential groups and in other classes
  • the students: accompanying the students to water therapy
  • Working with warehouse and project weeks

Requirements

You bring with you

  • at least 18 years old
  • preferably secondary education or training with EFZ
  • Interest in education in the pedagogical, curative or similar field
  • Natural skill in dealing with children and young people
  • Flexibility and simplicity
  • Joy of teamwork

We offer

You will find a challenging, exciting and versatile internship with children and young people. Your main area of application is in the school area, but you will also make smaller assignments in therapy and on the residential groups. You work five days a week. We offer versatile and interesting teamwork with regular working hours as well as internal training. The school holidays correspond to the holiday plan of the city of Bern.
