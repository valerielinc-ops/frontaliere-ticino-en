Full description

This is what awaits you

As an employee cleaning service, you are responsible for the maintenance cleaning at the management locations in Bern.

This is what you do with us

You work in the group cleaning service and support them in general cleaning work as well as in special cleaning.

You monitor and supervise the external service providers as part of the assigned cleaning work.

In addition, you are responsible for material management and invoice control.

This is what you bring with you

  • Eidg. Certificate of competence as an expert / professional cleaning technology EFZ
  • At least 5 years of professional cleaning experience
  • Sound practical experience in operational, maintenance and specialised cleaning
  • Safe handling of modern cleaning machines
  • Strong quality awareness and careful, efficient working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German

Working with us

There is an established and motivated team waiting for you. Together we live a solution-oriented and collegial way of working.

Your workload is 100%. The place of work is Bern. More about our commitment as an employer can be found below.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
