Your Responsibilities

  • You assemble test engines, prototypes and new components according to technical requirements
  • You perform repair, transformation, dismantling and analysis of engines
  • You monitor engine compliance, perform the necessary measurements and prepare components for metrology operations
  • You participate in pre-start tests and make sure that the parts are commissioned before they are assembled.
  • You contribute to the continuous improvement of products, assembly methods and tools by offering innovative solutions
  • You identify, analyze and communicate the technical problems encountered by proposing suitable resolution paths
  • You write technical documentation, intervention reports and capitalize on feedback
  • You apply the 5S standards and collaborate on the activities of the test benches to ensure an efficient and flexible working environment Your profile
  • You have a mechatronician training in automobiles, heavy goods vehicles, agricultural machinery or equivalent training
  • You justify a 3 to 5 year experience in workshop environment or workshop coordination, ideally in the field of agricultural machinery engines or heavy goods vehicles
  • You have strong knowledge of thermal motors, mechanics and IT tools
  • You have mastered MS Office, knowledge of an ERP and tools such as Jira, GDE or Albatros is an asset
  • You have perfect command of French, a solid foundation in English or German is an advantage
  • You are flexible to intervene on different types of engines, technologies and operational situations
