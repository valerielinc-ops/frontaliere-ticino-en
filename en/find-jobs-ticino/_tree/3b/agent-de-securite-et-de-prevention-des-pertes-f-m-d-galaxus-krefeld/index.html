Full description

You want to prevent goods from disappearing from our stockpiles and have great fun working with people, security equipment and developing our security measures? In this role, you are actively committed to protecting our corporate values and strengthening the safety culture at our location.

Mansioni

  • Recognize and monitor new abuses and dangers regarding goods safety
