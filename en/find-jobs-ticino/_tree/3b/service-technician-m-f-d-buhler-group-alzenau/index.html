We look forward to a dedicated team member (m/w/d) who actively supports our service team in the customer service of the business area Grinding & Dispersing through initiative, flexibility and customer orientation. In your role as a service technician (m/w/d) you convince with commitment and communication strength and do not shy away from new challenges. Take your chance and become part of the Bühler world

Let us make a difference at Bühler! Two billion people eat food produced on Bühler plants every day. One billion people travel in vehicles whose parts have been produced by Bühler technology. Tasks

  • You carry out commissioning and service work such as repairs, modifications, inspections, repairs and maintenance of individual machines and systems in Germany and abroad
  • You take over technical support or support of our global customer base by telephone or on site and maintain customer contacts
  • You analyze and fix errors/problems with the relevant departments
  • You train customers' employees during the service period in the field of machine operation
  • You are working on updating the machine software

Requirements

  • You have completed vocational training as a mechatronics engineer (m/w/d), industrial mechanics (m/w/d), electrician (m/w/d) or comparable, likes to continue with further training as a master/technician (m/w/d) or a degree in electrical engineering
  • You have professional experience in machine and plant engineering, ideally in the field of dispersion and wet grinding technology
  • You have knowledge of PLC control (Siemens)
  • You communicate very well in English
  • A self-employed and customer-oriented approach and a worldwide willingness to travel round off your profile
