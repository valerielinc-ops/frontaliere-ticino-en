Full description

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population high-quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and equity. By joining the Lausanne Care Foundation, you are committed in a concrete way to promoting health and well-being in the canton of Vaud, within expert, motivated and enthusiastic teams, in a varied activity rich in human contacts. In order to complete our teams, the CMS of the Riponne is looking for or to agree several:

Reception and Reception Manager (M/F)

Activity rates between 40% and 80%
