Full description

Who are we?

Justice of the Peace is the Authority for the Protection of Children and Adults. As such, it acts on request or ex officio. At the end of the investigation, it is decided whether or not to introduce a protective measure. The Judge of the Peace shall also have jurisdiction in matters of inheritance and in the area of layoffs.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Draft court decisions in the following areas: law on the protection of adults and children, successions and denials
  • Attend Justice of the Peace hearings with advisory voice

Profile desired

  • License/Master in Law and/or Lawyer Patent
  • Interest in civil law and in the protection of children and adults in particular
  • Stress resistance and editorial convenience
  • Mastery of the Tribuna programme, an asset
  • Knowledge of court organization, an asset
  • Excellent command of the French language
