  • Care and treatment of acute and chronic complaints of patients, mainly in the fields of rheumatology, orthopaedics/surgery and rehabilitation after accident or surgery
  • Instruction and support in Medical Training Therapy (MTT) in our training room
  • Care of athletes in ambulatory rehabilitation
  • Domicile treatment and care of age and nursing residences

Your profile

  • Further training in the field of manual therapy and/or training therapy/sport physiotherapy as well as lymphatic drainage would be advantageous
  • Independent and solution-oriented working method, pronounced customer orientation and high quality awareness as well as flexibility and resilience
  • Dedication, high social skills and teamwork

The variety of our treatments includes in addition to orthopaedic rehabilitation after accidents / operations also the treatment of chronic / acute diseases of the postural and musculoskeletal system, as well as medical training therapy, manual lymphatic drainage, manual therapy, shock wave therapy, sports physiotherapy and much more.
