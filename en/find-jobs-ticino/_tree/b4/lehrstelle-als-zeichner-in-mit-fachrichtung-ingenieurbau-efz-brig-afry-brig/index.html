Full description

With a draughtsman apprenticeship at AFRY Schweiz AG, you will set the course for your professional future. We offer you a varied and challenging apprenticeship in the exciting world of an engineering company. Over a period of four years, you will gain interesting insights into various specialist areas of the construction industry as well as into the new digital working methods and complete your apprenticeship with a federal certificate of competence. As of August 2027, we have a vacant apprenticeship position at our branch in Brig: DRAUGHTSMAN SPECIALIZING IN CIVIL ENGINEERING EFZ - BRIG During the apprenticeship, you will learn the following activities, among others:

  • Preparation of plans in 2D and 3D in civil engineering (e.g. public buildings, residential buildings, earthquake reinforcements, industrial buildings)
  • Creation of plans in 2D and 3D in civil engineering (railway construction, road construction, bridge construction, infrastructure construction)
  • Cooperation with civil engineers and assistance in construction inspections
  • Construction site internship of at least 2 weeks, at the earliest in the 4th semester

For the apprenticeship, you preferably have the following profile:

  • Completed orientation school (level I or very good level II)
  • Interests / strengths in geometry, mathematics and physics
  • Enjoyment of technical drawing and precise work
  • Good spatial awareness
  • Motivation for construction and working on a laptop

We offer an exciting and varied apprenticeship in the centre of Brig (5 minutes walk from the train station) with modern employment conditions in a collegial and helpful team. We look forward to receiving your online application (preferably with Multicheck)!
