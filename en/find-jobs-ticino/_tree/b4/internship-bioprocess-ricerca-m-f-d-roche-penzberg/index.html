The Position

Have you ever wondered how therapeutic proteins are developed? And did you know that Roche covers the entire value chain of such medicines, from discovery to manufacturing, at Europe's largest biotech site in Penzberg?

Our department of Bioprocess Research is part of Large Molecule Research (LMR) and pharma Research and Early Development (pRED) unit. This is where the technical development of promising drug candidates begins. Specifically, at Bioprocess Research we develop production processes for monoclonal antibodies and next generation biologics like multispecific antibodies and recombinant adeno-associated viral vectors (rAAV). We are responsible for the development of the upstream (cell culture) and downstream (purification) processes and work very closely with cell line development and protein/rAAV analytical teams.

Your internship

During your internship you will be working hands-on with our scientists, engineers and lab associates and will learn and apply cutting-edge technologies for process development, cell line development as well as the corresponding analytical methods. You will have the opportunity to test new workflows or improve existing methods.

You might also be contributing to the development and implementation of new technologies inspired by synthetic biology approaches or use state-of-the-art data analysis tools to learn from the results and present your findings in a team. You will participate in regular meetings to track the progress of your project and will have the opportunity to present to colleagues from Roche sites other than Penzberg.
