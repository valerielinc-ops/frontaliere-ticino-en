Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Put your managerial ambitions at the service of a healthy and sustainable performance by joining the canton of Vaud's first universal bank, one of the world's strongest banks, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our IT Departments, we are looking for one or more: Announcement Head of IT Distribution Marketing Platforms - Head of Department Your main tasks:

  • support the bank's Digitale transformation on Marketing and Distribution themes,
  • managing the portfolio of projects in the field, budgets and reporting,
  • lead a team of about 15 employees and external consultants,
  • ensure the execution of IT delivery processes and take corrective action if necessary,
  • contribute to the definition and implementation of the strategy for the evolution of application platforms, in collaboration with the business representatives (Core Banking System Thirds - Account, Entry into Relations, CRM). Your profile:
  • Master EPF or equivalent,
  • experience of at least 5 years in the construction, development and conduct of a similar entity,
  • mastering development methodologies (including agile) in large-scale projects;
  • excellent knowledge of the distribution themes of a universal bank (Retail Banking, Corporate Banking, Private Banking and Asset Management) on the aspects of Marketing, Sales force management, Service and customer relationship in a multi-channel context of digitalization of Front to Back processes,
  • very good knowledge of the market CRM platforms (e.g. MS Dynamics 365) and several key applications (contact entry, Campaign Management Platform, Distribution Channel Management Platform, Advisor Post),
  • organized, dynamic, very good communicator, able to frame transversal projects involving the various trades Bank Front. If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Deadline for application: 15.08.2026 Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
