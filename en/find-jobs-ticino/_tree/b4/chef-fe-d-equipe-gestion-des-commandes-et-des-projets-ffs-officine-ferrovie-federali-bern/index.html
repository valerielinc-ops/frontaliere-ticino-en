Team Leader Order and Project Management 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team leader contract and project management Bern / Work Smart, 01.09.2026 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

Become part of SBB’s Controlling Service Center and assume responsibility for a team that reliably manages orders and projects and thus enables the implementation of strategic projects. In an environment with high visibility, you actively shape processes and working methods and ensure transparency, quality and adherence to deadlines. This team management position is very suitable as an introduction to leadership and at the same time offers space to deepen existing leadership experience. An appreciative team, short decision paths and the opportunity to lead and develop effectively are waiting for you. You can move that.

  • You lead the team in order and project management professionally and personnel and ensure service quality, adherence to deadlines and transparency so that projects are implemented effectively.
  • As a clock: you control the service delivery process of our organization and coordinate interfaces with relevant stakeholders.
  • With conceptual strength, you develop standards, processes and templates and accompany committee runs until the decision.
  • You take on project mandates or management tasks in sub-projects in order to advance our organization both technically and in terms of content.
  • With coaching, feedback and clear goals, you develop your team and promote a culture of collaboration and learning.

You bring that with you.

  • With joy in people and leadership, you build trust, strengthen cooperation and promote performance and development.
  • You capture complex issues quickly, think conceptually and structure topics so that they are ready for decision-making.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder competence helps you to steer committees and prepare decisions. Ideally, you have already gained initial leadership experience.
  • You bring experience in project management and you know governance and committee processes from practice.
  • A tertiary level degree in business administration with a focus on finance or an equivalent qualification will round off your profile.
  • Knowledge of German at level C2 is a prerequisite, every other national language a plus.

Your reward and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This employment takes place in accordance with bond law, taking into account a comparison with the team, the market and the industry.
