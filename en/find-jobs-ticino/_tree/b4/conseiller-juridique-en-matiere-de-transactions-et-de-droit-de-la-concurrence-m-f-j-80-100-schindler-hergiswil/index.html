We Elevate... Your Responsibilities

For our Global Legal & Compliance team, we are looking for an experienced attorney to support Schindler’s global growth agenda. In this role, you will work on M&A transactions, advise on anti-trust and competition law, and help translate complex legal requirements into practical guidance for the business.

What makes this ideal role attractive to strong candidates: The role combines transaction work, competition law, international exposure, and close collaboration with business stakeholders in a global industrial company. The position is well suited for someone who enjoys intellectually demanding legal work, pragmatic business advice, and a team culture built on professionalism, trust, and integrity.

Your Responsibilities

  • Support M&A transactions from early structuring through due diligence, contract documentation, negotiations, merger control and post-closing integration
  • Advise on antitrust and competition law across Schindler's business, including new installations, existing installations, modernization, repairs, sourcing/procurement, supply chain, and digital activities
  • Work with internal stakeholders, external counsel, and authorities worldwide on merger control, investigations, and other regulatory or corporate law matters
  • Provide practical legal input on external communication, competitive positioning in transactions and beyond, and other business-critical initiatives
  • Monitor legal and regulatory developments and assess their implications for transactions, business operations, and strategy
  • Develop targeted, user-friendly trainings, guidelines and best practices that help business teams apply legal and compliance requirements effectively

We Elevate... Your Best Self

  • Law degree, preferably from a Swiss university, and admission as attorney at law; LL.M., doctorate, or international experience an asset
  • 3–5 years' experience in corporate law, M&A, and/or competition law (law firm and/or in-house)
  • Ability to develop expertise quickly and deliver structured, pragmatic solutions in a fast-paced business environment
  • Entrepreneurial mindset with a strong focus on practical, customer-oriented legal and compliance solutions
  • Excellent legal English and German (C2 – Proficient), additional languages are a plus
  • Strong digital proficiency (Microsoft 365) and interest in AI-enabled legal technologies
