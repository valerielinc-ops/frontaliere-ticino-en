The Opportunity

Are you ready to optimize the global Demand-to-Pay landscape and drive the tactical execution of digital procurement innovations? As our Business Process Expert (BPE) located in Sant Cugat, Spain, you will take tactical and strategic ownership over complex business processes in the Demand-to-Pay space for DOP. In this role, you will analyze, design, and implement innovative solutions - including adjoining IT ecosystems like SAP and myBuy - to increase stability, reduce errors, and drive seamless process adoption across the global network.

  • Reporting Line: Reports directly to the Digital & Process Management Lead and is part of the Procurement Center of Excellence (CoE).
  • Wider team and stakeholders: You will manage cross-functional relationships across the DOP Leadership Team (DPLT), CoE LT, DOP Category, DOP Delivery, DOP Sourcing Teams, RSS Demand to Pay, Global Procurement, and external service providers.
  • Role Type: An Individual Contributor role blending tactical process execution with strategic design elements.
  • Impact: You drive departmental efficiency, process compliance, and effective change management. Specifically, you are accountable for:
  • Process Optimization & System Integration: Managing and further developing complex business processes within the Demand-to-Pay landscape and designing efficient interfaces to other business processes.
  • Innovation & Automation: Proactively identifying opportunities for process improvements, automation, and digitalization in close collaboration with Delivery, Category, Digital & Process Management, and RSS Demand to Pay.
  • Project Leadership: Leading partial and overall projects independently, contributing to large local and global cross-functional teams, and independently designing workshops and training courses.
  • Innovative Solution Design: Developing and analyzing innovative approaches to complex problems and system optimizations, verifying them at a global level, and guiding their local implementation.
  • Process Optimization: Analyzing and implementing area-specific solutions to reduce critical errors, increase stability and productivity, and integrate Best Practices for optimal usage.
  • Strategic Process Development & Knowledge Transfer: Driving continuous improvement through simple, fast, and agile business processes while acting as a Subject Matter Expert (SME) to ensure robust know-how transfer across the organization.
  • Conflict & Exposure: Resolving matrix conflicts regarding competing project priorities or process expectations, while delivering high-frequency (monthly/quarterly) KPI updates - such as cycle times and compliance - to the CoE LT and DPLT.
