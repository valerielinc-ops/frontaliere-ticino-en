Full description

You support our kitchen team reliably in all areas of cleaning and organization. Independent and dutiful work as well as teamwork (6-10 people) are prerequisites.

Tasks

  • Washing dishes of blackware, pots and pans
  • Kitchen and storage cleaning services
  • Sliding of snow, terrace
  • Help with desserts and ice creams
  • Breakfast preparation
  • Driving hotel bus (valid driving license required)

Requirements

  • German language skills at least A2
  • Valid driving licence Cat. B
  • Self-employed, clean and reliable work
  • Team-oriented and resilient

We offer

  • 5-day week, 43.5 hours
