Drafter

We are looking for one of our clients based in central Valais, one/one:

Aluminum designer at 80% \- 100%

Join a company recognized in Switzerland for the quality of its achievements and technical requirements. You work at the heart of projects, with a structured team and rigorous working methods. They value the autonomy, accuracy and continuous improvement of the essential qualities to produce reliable, compliant and ready-to-carry plans.

Your responsibilities

  • Prepare and update technical plans and diagrams based on data and instructions from the design office.
  • Design details, cuts, views and nomenclatures according to standards and internal documentation.
  • Prepare production files and ensure consistency of information between different versions.
  • Check the conformity of plans (sizes, tolerances, annotations, legends) before dissemination.
  • Work with engineers and project teams to clarify requirements and resolve technical issues.
  • Monitor changes (revisions) and ensure document traceability.
  • Contribute to document optimization to improve readability, standardization and efficiency.
  • Participate in technical exchanges and trace the risks or inconsistencies detected in the documentation.

Skills required

  • Technical training (CFC or equivalent) in industrial design, construction, mechanics, building or related fields.
  • Mastery of computer-assisted design and drawing tools (CAO/DAO) to produce quality plans.
  • Good understanding of drawing standards and representation rules (views, cuts, ratings, annotations).
  • Ability to read and interpret general plans and technical documents to extract the necessary information.
  • Rigor and detail to ensure plan compliance and accuracy.
  • Organization and method for managing multiple versions, revisions and priorities in a project environment.
  • Mastery of French in oral and written language to communicate effectively with teams and document the work.
  • Prior experience in the design office or in the production of plans (even on partial projects) appreciated.
  • Good knowledge of the programming of several CNCs in the workshop.

What you will gain

  • A structured framework, oriented to quality and standards.
  • Concrete projects where your precision makes a difference.
  • Direct collaboration with technical profiles and an environment conducive to progress.

If you have recognized yourself in this profile, we look forward with great pleasure to your complete file.

It will be answered only to the corresponding files with the prerequisites of the announcement.
