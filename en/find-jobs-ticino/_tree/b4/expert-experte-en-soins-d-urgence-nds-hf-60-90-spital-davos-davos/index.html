Full description

Descrizione experts Emergency care NDS HF can handle quickly changing situations and complex issues. They enjoy the independent and interdisciplinary nursing care of people of all ages with different socio-cultural origins and different degrees of illness and injury. As a regional hospital/acoustic hospital with rescue service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for the basic medical care of the Davos population and guests. We also care about the well-being of our employees. With us you work in a manageable but extremely important operation for the region. We maintain a collegial working environment and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities of Davos. ## Mansioni

  • Detection of Clinical Patient Situation and Initiation of Immediate Measures
  • Self-Employed Triage and Priorization of Patients

  • Interdisciplinary Cooperation in Team
  • Self-Employment of and Support in Therapeutic and Diagnostic Measures incl. POCT and gypsum
  • Competent transfer of patients to downstream units
  • Compensated handling of nursing and medical standards
  • If necessary, take parts of patient administration such as reception and dismissal ## Requisiti
  • Dipl. Expert/Expert Emergency Care NDS or Dipl. Career HF with emergency experience
  • Interest in a seasonal, regenerative and varied operation
  • Professional experience of at least 12 months in emergency intake
  • Loadability, team capacity, equivalence and joy in rapidly changing situations
  • High sense of responsibility, careful working, pleasure in self-employment
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of English, any further language of advantage
  • readiness to work in all layers.
