Dipl. Nursing professional 80-100 %

Emergency Centre

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Admission and care of emergency patients of all disciplines in the focus and center hospital
  • Service provision according to the latest findings of emergency medicine and emergency care
  • Instruction and promotion of the apprentice HF as a day care provider
  • Documentation and performance recording with the computer science programs available for this purpose
  • Co-design and further development of modern deadline-look structures as well as triage processes in an emergency

What you bring for this job

  • Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH or a foreign education with SRK recognition, with professional experience at the bed station in the acute hospital
  • Interest and willingness to develop professionally within the framework of the postgraduate degree in emergency care (NDS HF)
  • Joy to work in a lively environment with unpredictable situations
  • Team-oriented, flexible and resilient personality

In our leading emergency centre, you can expect a working environment where team spirit and expertise are paramount.
