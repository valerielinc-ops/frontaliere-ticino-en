Full description

To strengthen its team, the Medical Directorate is looking for a

Deputy physician responsible for respiratory rehabilitation 70-80 %

Your mission

  • be responsible, within the Pulmonology Unit, for respiratory rehabilitation (based at HFR Riaz)
  • participate in the outpatient and inpatient care of patients with pulmonary diseases, including at the HFR Fribourg site as required by the unit
  • perform bronchoscopies at the HFR Fribourg site
  • participate in the on-call duties of the Pulmonology Unit
  • supervise resident physicians and rotating clinical fellows and participate in their training
  • participate in teaching students at the University of Fribourg
  • specialist qualifications (ISFM) in pulmonology and general internal medicine
  • solid clinical experience in inpatient and outpatient pulmonology, including as a clinical fellow or senior physician
  • experience in pulmonary rehabilitation is a definite advantage
  • experience as a clinical fellow in hospital-based internal medicine is an advantage
  • proficiency in the diagnosis and treatment of sleep-related breathing disorders, including non-invasive ventilation
  • dynamic, open and collegial personality

Your profile

  • interest in organizing undergraduate, postgraduate and continuing education for physicians and nursing staff
  • proficiency in French or German, with good knowledge of the other language (ability to conduct a medical interview)

Start date: March 1, 2027 or by agreement

Further information: Prof. Julien Vaucher, Head of Department, T 41 26 306 23 59

Application deadline: October 31, 2026
