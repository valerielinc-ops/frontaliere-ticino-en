Are you on helicopters? Then just screw it yourself!

You don’t want to sit in the office and click PowerPoint, but get your hands dirty? Then grab your overalls and become a polymechanic with us in Alpnach.

As part of our helicopter team, you ensure that the machines of the Swiss Army and other nations remain ready for flight. Whether gearboxes, engines or other upgrades – your hands ensure that the rotors rotate again.

With us you are from day 1 member of the team. You learn the important basics in machining and assembly technology and then deepen your knowledge in specialist training. In the end, you master everything: CNC voltage, helicopter maintenance and helicopter component maintenance.
