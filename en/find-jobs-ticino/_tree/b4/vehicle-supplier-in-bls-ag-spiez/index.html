Full description

After a demanding workday, MUTZ can be treated by our maintenance professionals today. The team is already waiting at the track, and logistics is bringing the replacement material. And now it is your big moment. You set the points to the track leading into the large hall and safely guide the vehicle in for its wellness treatment.

This is what awaits you

  • Moving trainsets and locomotives to and from maintenance
  • Carrying out shunting operations from the workshop area as well as the shunting limit (station)
  • Operating disposal and supply facilities
  • Carrying out exterior cleaning of trainsets and locomotives as well as cleaning and maintaining the assigned through-cleaning facility
  • Coordinating all shunting movements on the workshop premises

What you bring

  • A valid license as a train driver, category A40, is an advantage, or willingness to obtain one
  • Enjoyment of irregular working hours, including nights and weekends
  • Very good command of German
  • Basic IT skills

Your benefits – what you can look forward to

  • We support the most flexible planning possible of your routes and shifts
  • Receive a GA 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare travelcard.
  • You have time off when others are working. There are supplements for shifts at night and on weekends.
  • You have the option of purchasing an additional five to ten vacation days per year.
  • Financial support for external continuing education and an exciting range of internal learning and courses are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses

About BLS

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland's second-largest railway company. What's more: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and the Simplon. What unites us? Pride in what we do every day. Mobility has many facets for us. And many exciting jobs.
