Full description

Become part of our team! We look forward to a dedicated professional who enriches our team with your ideas, your expertise and the joy of working with young people. As of 1 December 2026 or by agreement, we are looking for an expert support EFZ.

  • Planning and implementation of attractive leisure and care services for young people
  • Promotion and support of students in cooperation with the school’s specialists
  • Completion of administrative, operational and domestic tasks
  • EFZ training as a specialist
  • Motivated, open and humorous personality
  • Joy of working with young people
  • Committed and responsible working methods
  • Interest in the further development of living space school

An exciting environment awaits you and a great, competent team at a secondary school. Your working days and working hours are determined by agreement. During school holidays you have free time. They also receive timely employment conditions and a salary in accordance with the guidelines of the School and Sports Department of the City of Zurich.

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
