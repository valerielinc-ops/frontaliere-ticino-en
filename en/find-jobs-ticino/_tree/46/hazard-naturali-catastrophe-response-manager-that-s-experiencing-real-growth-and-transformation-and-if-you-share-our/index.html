Let’s create a brighter future together, let’s make a difference

It’s important to know, what you do matters. At Zurich, we don’t just cover, we care.

This newly created opportunity has been established to support a new strategic partnership and growing portfolio of new business, providing you with the chance to make a meaningful impact and help shape the future of the team.

As our Natural Hazards and CAT Response Manager, you will play a critical role in shaping how we respond to some of Australia’s most impactful events. Sitting within our GI Claims team, this role is focused on designing and continuously improving our catastrophe response approach, ensuring we are ready to support our customers when it matters most.

You’ll lead the way in preparing for and responding to natural hazard events such as flood, bushfire, cyclone and storm, bringing together people, processes and partners to deliver strong outcomes for our customers, communities and business. Your work will directly influence how we build resilience, respond at pace, and care for customers during challenging times.

Backed by supportive leadership and clear pathways for growth, this is an opportunity to build a rewarding career while developing future-ready skills. We invest in your growth through access to global learning, technical pathways, and opportunities to build digital and AI capability. You’ll also join a values‑led, inclusive team that celebrates great work through a vibrant peer‑driven recognition programme — because care in every claim should be recognised and shared.

You will also be responsible for the following

  • Design and maintain end to end catastrophe response plans covering preparation, response and recovery
  • Integrate natural hazard scenarios into business continuity and surge planning for claims operations
  • Lead coordination of claims operations during catastrophe events, guiding decisions and response activities
  • Strengthen partnerships with internal teams and external vendors to ensure effective response capability
  • Monitor operational performance during events and drive improvements to customer outcomes
  • Represent Zurich in industry and community forums focused on resilience and catastrophe response

Important to your success – let’s grow together

You are someone who brings a strong customer-first mindset, with the ability to balance empathy and sound judgement. You enjoy working collaboratively, are open to learning, and take pride in making a genuine difference in the moments that matter.

To be successful, you will have the following

  • Extensive experience in general insurance claims, including exposure to catastrophe or surge events
  • Demonstrated ability to plan and coordinate complex, multi stakeholder operations or programs
  • Strong understanding of natural hazards and their impact on customers and insurance portfolios
  • Experience working with industry bodies, government agencies or community organisations
  • Solid knowledge of claims operations and customer journeys in high volume environments
  • Ability to interpret operational data and translate insights into clear actions
