Full description

Do you enjoy putting new ideas into practice and actively accompanying the development of innovative meat products? In this role, you are part of a dedicated team that creates new articles, optimizes processes and works closely with different partners. With your know-how and experience, you help drive quality and innovation.

Mansioni

  • Processing of approved product orders for new articles and their implementation from the idea to the first delivery
  • Cooperation and management of sub-projects, such as machine introductions and testing of new raw materials (VT projects)
  • Clarification and procurement of raw materials and packaging materials (film, cardboard, labels)
  • Accompanying the development phase incl. HACCP clarification, QA clearance and production support during the first three production runs
  • SAP recording and termination of the article opening in cooperation with internal and external interfaces (sale, QA, procurement, etc.)

Requirements

  • 3-5 years of experience: Meat industry
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): meat specialist or cook
  • Further education as a meat technician, meat technologist or studying meat/food
  • Very good knowledge of SAP and Office 365 programs
  • Strong teamwork and openness to change
  • Experience in the management and cooperation of projects and processes
  • Ability to consistently comply with legal and specific guidelines and occupational safety requirements
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (advantageous)
