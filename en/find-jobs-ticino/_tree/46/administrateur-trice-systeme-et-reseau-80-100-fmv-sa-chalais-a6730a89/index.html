[FMV SA]( )

We are the Valais company that will become the Swiss leader in hydroelectric production. With more than 140 highly qualified collaborators, we are actively contributing to the energy transition and the security of supply of 100% renewable, decarbonated and indigenous electricity serving Valais and Switzerland.

To strengthen its "Networks" department, FMV is looking for a

System administrator and network 8-100%

In this function, you work in the Network Systems sector of FMV.

Your mission

Your main task is to install, configure, manage and maintain the company's OT IT infrastructures, including networks and servers, in order to ensure their smooth operation and availability. You are working with the RSSI OT to define the concepts used in the OT IT infrastructure. You also play a crucial role in protecting the organization's data and systems by putting in place appropriate security measures, monitoring potential threats and responding to security incidents.

You evaluate and integrate new technologies to improve the efficiency and performance of existing infrastructures.

Your profile

  • HES diploma or federal patent in computer science, or diploma of equivalent training
  • 3-5 years experience in similar roles, such as system administrator or network administrator
  • Knowledge of IT/OT architectures and industrial environment information systems (SCADA).
  • Good experience in Windows system administration \& Linux
  • Experience in network administration \&firewall (CISCO \– PaloAlto)
  • Class B licence holder
  • Rigor and accuracy, analytical capability
  • Autonomy, initiative and flexibility
  • Ability to work independently and as a team
  • French or German mother tongue with good knowledge of the other language (B1\) and good knowledge of English (B2\).

Why join us?

A multidisciplinary, open and dynamic team is looking forward to working with you. Your occupancy rate is 80 to 100%. Your place of work is Chalais.

We also offer

  • Flexibility and balance: annualised working time and telework opportunity.
  • Attractive conditions: 5 weeks of vacation, 14 holidays and a variable share linked to collective goals.
  • Social benefits: preferential pension contributions and excellent social insurance conditions.
  • Professional development: active support for continuing training
