Full description

The Quartier LôDzé is looking for an additional receptionist (M/F) to strengthen its team at the Hotel Borsari and the Bains Publics. We are looking for a welcoming, versatile and autonomous person with an excellent sense of service and customer relationship.

Your missions

  • Ensure check-in and check-out of guests of Hotel Borsari.
  • Welcome customers with professionalism and friendliness.
  • Manage reservations for District LôDzé, follow up and process special requests.
  • Respond to customer requests by telephone and email.
  • Inform and promote the hotel services as well as the various F&B outlets.
  • Ensure the closing and updating of accounts receivable in the PMS system, as well as the collection of entries at the Public Baths.
  • Perform administrative tasks related to reception.
  • Ensure compliance with quality, hygiene and public bath regulations.
  • Effectively manage potential problems and complaints from clients.
  • Inform customers about activities, attractions and events in the Valais region.
  • Work closely with other team members to ensure quality customer experience.

Profile sought

  • Experience in a reception post, ideally in hotels.
  • Excellent sense of hospitality, communication and customer service.
  • Good control of IT tools and ease with hotel management systems (PMS).
  • Organization, autonomy, versatility and responsibility.
  • Ability to manage claims and claims calmly and effectively.
  • Good knowledge of Valais and its tourist activities, an asset.
  • Careful presentation, team spirit and attention to detail.
  • Flexibility and availability for various schedules, including weekends and public holidays.

We offer you

  • Reduction for employees in the various outlets
  • 5 weeks of holidays, 6 holidays
  • Salary according to applicable CLC and 13th months.
  • A dynamic and varied working environment.

We will only examine the complete files (CV, cover letter, work certificates, etc.) of the candidates corresponding to the profile sought.
