Full description

Reference code: JR134348

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

MISSION

Strengthen metallurgical control and numerical simulation of a thermomechanical process to optimize the manufacture of a series of watch components.

LIABILITY

In direct collaboration with the Materials Innovation team, under the responsibility of your trainee master, your missions will be as follows:

  • Develop the state of the art of the subject;
  • Experimentally identify the metallurgical behaviour of an innovative metal alloy, in parallel with others already employed in production;
  • Develop a simulation model with the finite elements of the process, to be calibrated using online measurements;
  • Optimize the thermomechanical parameters of the process on the basis of previous results;
  • Capitalize your work in the form of technical notes and regular presentations.

PROFILE

  • You are a master's student or equivalent and you have solid knowledge in the field of metallurgy and associated transformation processes.
  • You master digital modeling with finite elements and have experience with simulation tools, ideally with Forge software.
  • Rigourous.se, methodical, you are endowed with a good critical mind and you are the force of proposal. Your sense of responsibility and self-reliance will be assets in carrying out this mission.

WHY IS THIS STAGE FOR YOU?

  • A unique experience within a prestigious international group.
  • A stimulating and collaborative working environment.
  • The opportunity to develop your skills and invest in concrete projects.
  • A springboard for your career in the watch industry.

Based mainly in Glovelier, you will also have the opportunity to meet colleagues from other research sectors within our hub located in Microcity, Neuchâtel.

Interested? Don't wait any longer, send us your application and join the Richemont adventure!

Internship of 6 months from February 2027
