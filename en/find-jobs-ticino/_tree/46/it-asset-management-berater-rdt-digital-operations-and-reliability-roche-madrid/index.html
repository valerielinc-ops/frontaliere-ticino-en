The Position As a Senior Business Analyst you will fill the gap between the Software Asset Management (SAM) team and the technology delivery. You will act as the vital link between SAM functional teams and software engineering. You will translate complex software licensing frameworks, entitlement logic, compliance needs, and asset lifecycle workflows into robust technical specifications, data models, and actionable user stories. In this role, you will lead end-to-end technical business analysis workstreams, evaluating SAM tool capabilities, analyzing platform options, and driving the implementation, configuration, and integration of SAM platforms. You will ensure the technology solutions and SAM tools built or deployed directly automate SAM processes, reduce compliance risk, and optimize software spend, while serving as a trusted advisor to both functional leads and engineering teams.

Job Responsibilities

  • Scope / Content Leadership: Independently manages end-to-end technical BA workstreams, translating SAM operational needs into structured engineering backlogs, functional specifications, and solution workflows without requiring guidance. Proactively shapes agile team processes and technical BA standards.
  • Accountability / Problem Solving: Leads technical root-cause analysis for SAM tooling and data pipelines. Evaluates gaps in discovery data, CMDB integrations, and license calculation logic, taking full accountability for delivering clear technical requirements and integration mappings that resolve business bottlenecks.
  • SAM Tool Analysis & Implementation: Conducts in-depth tool capability assessments, fit-gap analyses, and platform evaluations. Defines functional requirements, configuration logic, and integration specs to drive the successful implementation and enterprise rollout of SAM tools.

  • Impact / Strategy: Ensures implemented SAM tools and custom technical solutions deliver tangible business outcomes—such as automated license reconciliation, audit readiness, and software cost reduction. Translates long-term SAM functional roadmaps into scalable, phased technical implementation plans.
  • Complexity / Product Size: Operates across multi-product environments and complex technical ecosystems involving CMDBs, cloud management platforms, discovery tools, procurement engines, and enterprise SAM platforms. Navigates ambiguous requirements across intricate software licensing models (e.g., SaaS, core-based, concurrent user) and legacy tool migrations.
  • Business / Technical Ability: Demonstrates deep domain expertise in Software Asset Management concepts (ISO 19770, license entitlement modeling, cloud cost management) coupled with hands-on technical proficiency in SAM tool architectures, API integrations, data flow mapping, and query logic (SQL) to guide platform development and system integration.
