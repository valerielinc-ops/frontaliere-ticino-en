Full description

Portrait To strengthen our team, we are looking for a speech therapist. We are a diverse, decentralized primary school with 7 kindergartens, 20 regular classes and 2 classes for special support (KbF). Around 540 students are taught by 65 teachers in a total of 29 classes.

Tasks

As a speech therapist, you work at the Schönau school location.

Your tasks include

  • Clarification of the need for support or therapy (recording and diagnostics)
  • Speech therapy support (individual therapy and small group work)
  • Advice for parents and teachers
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration within the framework of the professional mandate
  • regular exchange of expertise between speech therapists in the city of Thun

You can count on the support and help of your colleagues. The maternity replacement is expected to start mid-November 26 and will transition to a permanent position after the maternity leave.

Requirements

You have a recognized diploma or bachelor's degree in speech therapy or are training to become a speech therapist. In addition, you will work with children from cycles 1 and 2.

We offer

We offer permanent employment in accordance with cantonal requirements. You have access to a very well-equipped therapy room as well as modern infrastructure. Two experienced, motivated speech therapists are looking forward to expanding the team. Of the 18 lessons, you will also accompany some children with special school status in individual lessons.
