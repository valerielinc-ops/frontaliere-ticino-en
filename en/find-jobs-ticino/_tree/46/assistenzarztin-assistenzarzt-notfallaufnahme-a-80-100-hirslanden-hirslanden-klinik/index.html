Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Stephanshorn | St. Gallen

Occupation by 1 May 2027

Type of employment: Temporary

Reference number: 68315

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus. We are looking for an agreement:

YOUR TASKS

  • Outpatient consultation and inpatient admission of medical and surgical emergency patients under specialist guidance in a 24/7 operation
  • Learning targeted diagnostics with independent sonography
  • Regulation and assessment of further diagnostics according to training status
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with the clinic’s partner doctors
  • Active participation in domestic and external training programmes

Your profile

  • Federal or recognised foreign medical diploma (MEBEKO)
  • One year of clinical experience in patient care
  • Interest in interdisciplinary general and emergency medical primary care
  • Readiness for shift service with good feasibility of part-time wishes
  • Innovative, communicative and team-capable personality
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Do you want to work in a dynamic and innovative environment where you can fully develop your strengths, know-how and competencies? We create an inspiring environment where your ideas and contributions are appreciated and you have the chance to make a positive impact on people’s well-being.

Our offer

  • Varied activities in a dynamic and competent environment
  • Regionally firmly anchored medical hospital with a public service mission
  • Modern infrastructure, advanced working conditions and training opportunities
  • Category 2 training centre for “Clinical Emergency Medicine SGNOR”
  • Category IV Training Centre for "General Internal Medicine"
  • Sonography training under the guidance of SGUM-approved trainers

For additional information, Bodo Giannone, Chief Medical Officer for Emergency Care, is available at bodo.giannone@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.
