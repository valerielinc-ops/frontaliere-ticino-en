Experienced technical calculator (M/F) back to list To strengthen our teams, we are looking for an experienced (M/F) technical calculator. Job description

Your mission

  • Analyse tender documents, plans, specifications and technical specifications
  • Accomplish the metres and identify technical constraints related to the projects
  • Develop comprehensive cost studies with optimized technical and cost-effective solutions
  • Consult, negotiate and analyse supplier and subcontractor offers
  • Establish competitive bids while ensuring cost-effectiveness of projects
  • Participate in technical sessions with contractors, architects, engineers and partners
  • Provide technical support to project managers and contribute to the continuous improvement of costing methods
  • Ensure continuous monitoring of materials, construction techniques and market developments

Profile

Profile desired

  • Advanced technical training in building, construction or building technology
  • Experience confirmed for several years in a similar function in Switzerland
  • Excellent mastery of reading plans and analyzing complex technical files
  • Strong knowledge of the standards and practices of the Swiss construction market
  • Experience in bidding and calculating large projects
  • Mastery of common computer tools as well as computing and submission software, experience on Messerli, WinBau, Sorba or any other similar business software is an asset
  • A strong sense of analysis, entrepreneurial spirit and excellent priority management
  • Ability to defend technical and financial choices with different stakeholders
