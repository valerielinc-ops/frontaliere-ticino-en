Full description

We are looking for apprentices for our apprenticeships as health specialists i.A. In this three-year training, you will take care of our patients so that they recover faster. This also includes so-called medical technical activities. You will learn things like taking blood, dispensing medication, applying bandages – and much more. In addition, you will also carry out administrative and logistical work. Your advantages: KSB offers more than just an exciting job with good working conditions. Get to know our benefits HERE. If you have any technical questions regarding the submission of your application, please contact HR Vocational Training on +41 56 486 29 53. For professional information, please contact Andrea Martina Inderwildi on +41 56 486 19 02.
