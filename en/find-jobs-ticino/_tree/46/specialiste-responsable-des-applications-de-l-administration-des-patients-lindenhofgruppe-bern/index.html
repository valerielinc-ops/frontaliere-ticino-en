Full description

What you do

You will be responsible for ensuring the technical processing of all patients in the ERP system Business Central (module Padmin) as well as the decentralized service recording in the clinic/practice information systems. Your main tasks are:

  • Responsibility for master data, tariffs, regulations, contracts and service spectrums including quality assurance and traceable documentation.
  • Technical support as well as accompaniment of changes, tests, releases and disruptions
  • Analysis and optimization of existing processes in patient administration and development of new, efficient process structures.
  • Identification of forward-looking technologies and methods and their integration into administrative processes.
  • Collaboration in projects and cross-departmental coordination.

What sets you apart

  • You have a degree at HF, FH or university level, ideally in the field of business informatics, business administration, process management or a related field.
  • You have very good IT user skills, especially in dealing with MS365, ERP systems (e.g. Navision/BusinessCentral, KISIM, CuraMed) and digital tools; affinity for digital solutions and data analysis.
  • A high affinity for IT, digitalization, system applications and evaluations are among your strengths.
  • You have a strong analytical understanding and recognize connections between tariffs, processes, systems and data quality.
  • In addition, you have good communication skills and enjoy working with different internal and external stakeholders.
