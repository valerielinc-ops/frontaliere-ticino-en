Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process. Are you looking for a meaningful task in which you can accompany and support children in everyday school life? In our supplementary school supervision of the Sali Steinhof Primary School, you can expect a varied environment, a committed team and the chance to contribute your pedagogical skills with pleasure.

Your tasks

  • You actively participate in the care team and design a professional, child-friendly care and promotion.
  • You are a reliable caregiver for children and parents and lead parent talks.
  • You will maintain contact with teachers, home maintenance and other specialists at the school.
  • You take on the day-to-day responsibility of the children’s group and the day-to-day organisation.
  • You accompany the children in the children's restaurant and oversee various offers over lunch and in the afternoon
  • You actively participate in the weekly team meetings.
  • You work mostly during the school weeks, 11.30 – 14.00 or until 18.00

Your profile

  • You bring a completed socio-educational, socio-cultural or educational education.
  • You have experience and joy in accompanying children from 10 to 13 years old, supporting them and supporting them in their everyday life.
  • You empathize with the children’s world and deal with culturally and age-mixed groups.
  • You convince through your empathetic, appreciative and team-oriented way and actively contribute to our school life.
  • You bring experience in the administrative and planning area of responsibility.
  • You work independently, reliably and communicate openly, clearly and transparently.
