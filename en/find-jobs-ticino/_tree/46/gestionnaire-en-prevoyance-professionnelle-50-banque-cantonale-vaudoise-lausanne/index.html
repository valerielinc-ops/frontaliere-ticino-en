Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Asset Management and Trading division, we are looking for one or one:

Announcement Professional Provident Manager - 50% Your main tasks:

  • independently manage a portfolio of member companies within AVENA - BCV Foundation 2nd pillar, according to the regulatory and legal provisions,
  • inform clients (insured, insured and employers) about all aspects of the provision,
  • take on all tasks related to the occupational pension of insured persons, from their affiliation to their exit (incentives to own housing, divorce in occupational pension, redemption, retirement, etc.),
  • dealing with work incapacity in coordination with the Foundation's reinsurer,
  • carry out annual winding-up work, including reconciliations between technical management and accounting,
  • monitor the collection of employer fees and manage the litigation process. Your profile:
  • commercial training with proven experience in occupational insurance (Federal Patent of Specialist in Provident Management or Social Insurance is an asset),
  • rigor, organization, relational ease and sense of service,
  • perfect mastery of French, good knowledge of English and/or German an asset.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
