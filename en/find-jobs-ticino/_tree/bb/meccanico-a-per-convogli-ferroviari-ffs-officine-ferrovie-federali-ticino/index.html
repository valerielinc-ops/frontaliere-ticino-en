SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. Would you like to use your craftsmanship skills on different types of vehicles and technical systems? In the area of vehicles at the Olten plant, you review, assemble and test components that many travelers rely on every day. Whether doors, air conditioning or pneumatics. The variety of systems always offers you the opportunity to learn new things. You can expect a modern working environment in which you independently take responsibility and tackle it together with your team. With internal specialist training, you deepen your knowledge of individual components and systems. Smooth working hours give you leeway in shaping your day-to-day work. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a specialist technician: in industry you carry out revisions and component changes on different trainsets. Your tasks include, for example, doors, air conditioning systems and pneumatic components.
  • You independently implement maintenance work according to defined specifications and document your work in a comprehensible manner.
  • You set built-in components according to specifications and check their function. With your careful work, you help ensure that our vehicles are safe and reliable on the road.
  • You coordinate your work with your colleagues and support them with your knowledge and craftsmanship.
  • As part of KAIZEN, you will recognize opportunities for improvement in your everyday work and develop your own processes further. This is how you shape how we do our work more easily and efficiently.

You bring that with you.

  • You enjoy the craft and want to understand how different components and vehicle systems work.
  • You show initiative, take responsibility for your tasks and contribute ideas to improve your workflows.
  • Working together in a team, coordinating with each other and supporting your colleagues in joint tasks is part of your daily tasks.
  • You work carefully according to maintenance requirements and are ready to expand your expertise on an ongoing basis. Safety and quality are crucial for you in every job.
  • A completed vocational apprenticeship EFZ in the craft sector, for example as a polymechanic:in, production mechanic:in, agricultural machinery mechanic:in or car mechanic:in, and several years of experience in an industrial environment are mandatory for this position.
  • In addition, you have good oral and written knowledge of German (min. B1).

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
