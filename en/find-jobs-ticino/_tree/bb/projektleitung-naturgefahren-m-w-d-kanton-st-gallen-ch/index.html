Full description

The Office for Water and Energy (AWE) is committed to safe and intact waters as well as protection against floods and natural hazards. The Natural Hazards Section focuses on the protection of persons and property values as well as on the safety of dams. There will be exciting new tasks in the coming years. The development of protective structures management and the overall revision of hazard maps will present interesting challenges.

What to expect

  • Project management in the tracking of natural hazard maps, in municipal operational planning as well as specific issues in natural events
  • Advising the population, municipalities and cantonal authorities on the implementation of hazard maps
  • Revision of basic principles such as topic-specific guidelines, work aids and natural hazard analysis guidance
  • Supervision of the safety of dams, including by accompanying inspections and preparing an annual report for the benefit of the Federal Office for Energy

What distinguishes you

  • Higher or higher education degree in environmental, engineering or a similar specialisation
  • Professional experience and knowledge of natural hazards or dams
  • Experience in project management
  • Motivation, initiative and team spirit
  • Self-employment, social skills and a secure appearance

What we offer

  • Responsible and versatile work in a highly topical topic
  • Plenty of room for initiative and the professional participation in a future-relevant topic area
  • Exciting, varied and interdisciplinary projects with different stakeholders
  • A motivated, collegial team as well as modern and flexible working conditions

Contact: Hubert Meusburger, Head of Section Natural Hazards, telephone 058 229 21 42
