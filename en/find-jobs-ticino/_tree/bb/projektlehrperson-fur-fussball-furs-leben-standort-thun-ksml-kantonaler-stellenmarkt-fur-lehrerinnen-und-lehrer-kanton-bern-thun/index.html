Full description

Portrait About the project "Football for Life" “Football for Life” is an integrative sport and education project that sustainably improves the chances of children in the 3rd-6th grade (cycle 2) – especially girls. Through a combination of weekly language- and values-oriented football training and semi-annual cultural excursions, the project strengthens the movement behavior, the joy and self-confidence in speaking and the personal development of the participating children. The enthusiasm for football serves as a vehicle to promote language and personality playfully. The weekly sessions, which take place at the participating schools, are led by an interdisciplinary team of prospective teachers and trainers: local associations.

Tasks

For our project “Football for Life” we are looking for a dedicated project teacher for the current school year 2026/2027 who enjoys strengthening children in their linguistic and personal development through football and wants to get involved in a meaningful sport and education project at the project location Thun.

Your responsibilities

  • Joint planning and implementation of the language- and values-oriented football units in an interdisciplinary team during the school year 26/27: once a week for 2 hours Wednesdays, 13:00-15:00
  • Accompanying the project group on cultural excursions (once per half-year)
  • Supervision of the project group at the annual football final tournament

Requirements

Your profile

  • ideally students of the Bachelor's degree or teachers of the primary level
  • Interest in implementation and, where applicable, Further development of moving learning sequences and language support integrated into football training
  • First lesson and/or training experience in football
  • J+S diploma is an advantage
  • Interest in participating in an integrative sport and education project
  • Good access to children of 3-6. Class level

We offer

You can look forward to this

  • Fee of approximately CHF 35 per hour
  • Valuable practical experience in dealing with diverse groups
  • Getting to know innovative approaches to language and personality promotion through sports and games
  • Exciting insights into professional football by accompanying cultural events with professional football clubs
  • Receiving an employment certificate for your commitment
  • The opportunity to provide children with better sports and educational opportunities
