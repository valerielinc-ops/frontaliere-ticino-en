Full description

Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The aim of the pharmacy, which is attached to the Medical Directorate and the quality of the HUGs, is to provide reliable and responsive medicines, from pharmacy to healthcare units, by anticipating market risks, to manufacture tailor-made drugs in compliance with quality standards to improve safety of use, to offer expertise to health professionals and professionals and patients, to optimize drug management from prescription to administration and to be a reference and competence centre in training, teaching and research, integrating innovative methods.

Description of post

You participate, together with pharmaceutical preparers, logisticians and pharmacy assistants, in the activity of pharmacist or pharmacist routine of the Pharmaceutical Logistics Unit of the pharmacy of the HUG. You ensure, under the supervision of pharmacists, project managers and assistants, the proper functioning of the supply of medicines and their availability to patients. In collaboration with two other pharmacists, you are the reference or reference for the various requests of doctors and care units in connection with obtaining medicines, you supervise the management of ruptures, you manage the stocks of illicit narcotics. You monitor the prescriptions of critical drugs. You manage the batch control and removal process with the Quality Assurance Manager. You guarantee the implementation of the drug policy and quality and safety procedures. You contribute to the continuous improvement of the organisation, in close collaboration with the other pharmacists of the unit, including by participating in cross-sectional sessions with other units or services (clinical pharmacy, production). You are involved in the training and supervision of pharmacist employees including learners (MAS, trainees).

Qualifications

You have the federal diploma of pharmacist or pharmacist or title deemed equivalent. You benefit from a notable experience in the organization and coordination of activities related to the drug circuit. You have excellent skills in using IT tools (office automation and logistics management). You have the sense of organization and priorities, are autonomous and bring great rigour to your work. You are recognized for your ability to work as a team, your relationship ease, your spirit of collaboration, your common sense of communication and excellent stress resistance. You have perfect command of French, if possible of German or English and an ease of writing. A successful experience of at least 2 years in hospital, a completed specialization in hospital pharmacy (MAS, FPH, DES) are substantial assets.
