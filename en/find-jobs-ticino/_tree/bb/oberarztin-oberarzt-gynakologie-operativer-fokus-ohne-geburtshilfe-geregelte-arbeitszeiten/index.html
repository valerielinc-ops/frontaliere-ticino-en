Do you want to focus entirely on gynecology – without obstetrics and without services? Are you looking for an operational activity with regular working hours and your own office hours? A regionally anchored acute care hospital in German-speaking Switzerland offers an independent gynecological department with a clearly structured service mandate – deliberately without inpatient obstetrics. The department covers a wide range of surgical and conservative gynecology, including classical procedures as well as specialized procedures such as endoscopic surgery and urogynecological treatments. The offer is supplemented by a versatile outpatient care with own emergency consultation hours, colposcopy, anti-conception consulting and specialized consultation hours. As a recognized training institution with involvement in a training association and cooperation with a center hospital, the clinic offers stable framework conditions, clear processes and targeted development opportunities – especially for doctors who want to work without the burden of obstetrics.

Their tasks

  • Independent care of gynecological patients in the inpatient and ambulatory area
  • Performing gynecological operations
  • Leading your own office hours
  • Participation in regulated day-to-day operations (no services)
  • Participation in the further training of assistant doctors: inside
  • Possibility for specialisation within the framework of the continuing education network

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Gynecology & Obstetrics (or equivalent recognized)
  • Experience in operational gynecology
  • Interested in working without obstetrics
  • Independent, structured working method
  • Team-oriented personality with high reliability

Their advantages

  • Clear focus on gynecology without obstetrics
  • No services and regulated working hours
  • Combination of operational activity and own office hours
  • Structured, manageable organization with stable processes
  • Recognised training centre with development opportunities
  • Attractive additional services and long-term perspective
