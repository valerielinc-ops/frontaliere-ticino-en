Full description

To strengthen our Business & Project Management team in the Business & Technology Solutions department, we are looking for a resilient, flexible and goal-oriented personality as Business & Project Manager. In this role, you assume responsibility for strategic and operational projects in the area of transaction banking and actively drive the further development of our platform, process and service landscape. What would your activities be?

  • Management and successful implementation of business, transformation and regulatory initiatives (scope, budget, quality, timeline).
  • Ensure structured project governance including risk, dependency and escalation management.
  • Management of cross-divisional projects between business, operations and IT.
  • Responsibility for the successful implementation of complex projects and initiatives.
  • Analysis, further development and optimization of processes, operating models and service structures.
  • Ensure regulatory requirements in projects.
  • Active participation in the transaction banking strategy (scaling, standardization, automation).
  • Support management in strategic issues and initiatives.
  • Creation of decision-making bases and management reports.
  • Stakeholder management and coordination of interdisciplinary teams. What are we looking for?
  • University or technical college degree in business administration, business informatics or comparable education.
  • Several years of experience in business and project management in the financial services environment.
  • Experience in managing and managing complex business and IT projects.
  • In-depth understanding of transaction banking, banking operations and relevant regulatory requirements.
  • Experience in managing complex business and IT projects incl. End-to-end ownership.
  • Very good understanding of core banking systems (e.g. Avaloq) and their integration (interfaces, data flows, platform architectures).
  • Experience in dealing with external providers, vendors and platform solutions.
  • High IT affinity as well as strong analytical and conceptual skills.
  • Ability to communicate and prepare for decisions at senior management level.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method with high self-responsibility.
  • Resilient, flexible and communicative personality with a safe appearance.
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English.
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the willingness to use AI-based tools and ways of working. What do we offer? (Copied under your section requirements)
  • A responsible role with direct impact in central platform and transformation projects.
  • Plenty of creative leeway as well as flat hierarchies and short decision paths.
  • Modern workplace in Zurich (near Paradeplatz) and flexible, hybrid working model.
  • Attractive training and development opportunities
  • Open, collaborative team culture (“One Vontobel”).
  • Attractive employment conditions including bonuses and benefits and free lunch.
  • Additional benefits such as fruits & coffee as well as possibility for additional unpaid holidays. We are looking for talented people, not just a CV that meets all the criteria. Even if you do not meet all requirements, we look forward to your application. Why Vontobel? At Vontobel, you are building a better future for our customers and society – and for yourself. By 2030, we want to be known as one of the leading and most trusted global investment firms. We employ more than 2,000 people at 28 locations worldwide and have our headquarters in Zurich. Our diverse team values different perspectives, and we are looking for people to make a difference in a dynamic and changing world.
