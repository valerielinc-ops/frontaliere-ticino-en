Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of structural and interior construction materials. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at around 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

Do you love consulting customers and presenting innovative solutions? Perfect! We are looking for a personality with heart and mind as of January 1, 2027 or by appointment, who will take over our customer advice in the field service in the area of plates. Your area of application is the Obersee region, based at our location at Eichwiesstrasse 2 in Jona.

Look forward to a solid customer base, exciting projects and the opportunity to shape your own career path. We put high-quality products, excellent service and satisfied customers first – and you can be part of it.

Does that sound like your next challenge? Then let us be successful together!

Task area

  • In your role, you take responsibility for the sales region Obersee (with a workplace in the region and the opportunity to make home office)
  • As the main task, you maintain the existing customer base and convince new customers of the company Richner (our customer groups include recorders, architects, investors, etc.)
  • You are responsible for revenue and margin targets in your assigned area
  • As a link to our customers, you are the first contact person for specialist questions
  • You implement sales promotions such as promotions and promotions in your sales area
  • Positioning of the Richner brand in the market

Profile

If your heart beats faster in the area of responsibility described above and you have the following experiences and competencies, you are our desired candidate:

  • You have a high enthusiasm and passion for our products and interior design
  • You have a sales flair, are strong and ideally already have experience in the field
  • Independent planning and prioritization of the daily tasks both effective time management and self-organization are up to you
  • In addition, you ideally have a further education as a sales specialist with a Federal professional certificate. OR
  • With a winning and positive way you convince our customers
  • Disc knowledge is an advantage
  • Your nature is characterized by curiosity
  • You have stylish knowledge of German in word and writing

We offer

  • a business car that can also be used in a private setting
  • Remote Work from one of our locations in the sales region as well as Home - Office
  • a challenging, versatile and interesting job in a good working environment in which active cooperation and self-employment are emphasised
  • intensive training is a program, whereby our new colleagues can benefit from product and system training within the company
  • Support of our employees in their professional and personal development
  • a well-established network of craft centres and close cooperation with sales management
  • 5 weeks holiday (6 weeks from 50 years of age) and holiday shopping up to max. 10 days, additional benefits such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and discounts on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits
