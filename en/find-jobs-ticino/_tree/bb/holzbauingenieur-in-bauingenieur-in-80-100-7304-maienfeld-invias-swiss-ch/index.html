Full description

EUR:IN / BAUINGEUR:IN 80 – 100% In order to continue and expand our success story, we are looking for an experienced personality as Timber Engineer:in / Civil Engineer:in 80 – 100% immediately or by arrangement at one of our locations in Maienfeld or Davos.

Your tasks include

  • Planning of timber construction projects from multi-family houses to hall buildings to temporary event buildings
  • Development of practical solutions together with our engineers and technicians
  • Active support of the team in various projects
  • Support for graduates by experienced engineers

What we offer you

  • INVIAS offers you a modern, innovative working environment in the midst of an inspiring mountain world. Probably the most varied canton in Switzerland is our home where we work and live.
  • Benefit from numerous fringe benefits, attractive social benefits, training opportunities and flexible working models.
  • 5 weeks holidays and 3 weeks winter bridge
  • Enthusiasm, dynamism, sustainability, connection and trust are the values that shape our corporate culture.

What you bring

  • Completed degree as a timber construction engineer:in (BSc or FH) or civil engineer:in (BSc or FH) with an interest in timber construction
  • Knowledge of modern wood system construction
  • Basic craftsmanship training in construction or timber construction is advantageous
  • Professional experience as a wooden engineer:in or civil engineer:in an advantage
  • Structured working methods and teamwork
  • Safe handling of relevant software programs (e.g. CAD, static software)
  • Interested university graduates are also welcome
