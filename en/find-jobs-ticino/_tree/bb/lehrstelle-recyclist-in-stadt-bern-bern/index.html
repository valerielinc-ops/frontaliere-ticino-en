Full description

Recycling instead of throwing away - sustainability is the trump card.If you are interested in the recycling cycle, you like to handle tools and machines, you are wind and weather resistant and you like to have customer contact. Then come to us as a recyclist.

Tasks

  • Get to know how to handle a wide variety of materials (metal, aluminum, glass, etc.)
  • At our waste disposal yards receive, sort, store and process recyclables
  • Operating various handling equipment such as forklifts or sorting excavators

Requirements

  • Completed real or secondary school
  • Craftsmanship and interest in a practical and varied activity
  • Enjoying customer contact and handling machines
  • Robust Health and Joy at Working Outdoors

We offer

Diverse and fair More than 20 exciting apprenticeships await you with us – from crafts to administrative. As a versatile apprenticeship company, we value the different role models and pay all apprenticeships the same wage – and learners from 25 even a little more.

The city of Bern is committed to sustainable vocational training and offers you a wide range of opportunities for your career. Around 200 apprentices are currently completing their training in over 20 different professions – accompanied by more than 300 experienced employees who pass on their knowledge and enthusiasm. With us you not only learn your dream job, but also actively shape life in our city. Become part of a team that works with joy, responsibility and heart for Bern.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
