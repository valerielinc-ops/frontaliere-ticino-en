Full description

Tasks

  • co-operates in the Educational, Culture and Sport Department; analyzes the needs of users and services, formalizes requirements and contributes to defining technical and functional solutions
  • designs, develops, implements, integrates and maintains web and educational applications, portals and management solutions; manages and develops LMS, especially Moodle, and WordPress/Multisite, including customizations, plugins and specific features
  • participates in the management and evolution of data banks, integrations and migrations, guaranteeing quality, consistency and continuity of information; manages and maintains web environments, hosting, domains, DNS, email and management tools of educational laboratories
  • creates, applies and maintains standards, procedures and technical documentation, functional and legal, promoting quality and standardization of industry and office processes
  • co-ordinates and/or participates in cantonal, intercantonal and federal digital and school projects, curating planning, priorities, coordination, verification and testing; contributes to the common objectives and transversal processes of the CERDD strategy
  • provides technical and application support, analyzes and solves problems of platforms, applications and digital services; collaborates with the training and accompaniment of users and collaborators in the use of digital solutions

Requirements

  • diploma of higher specialized schools (SSS) of computer science/management or equivalent title
  • excellent knowledge of the Ticino school system
  • multi-year experience certified in the development and management of web applications, digital platforms and/or computer systems, with proven experience in Moodle, plugin development, specific features and in WordPress portals, even in multisite environment with experience in using visual page builders, in particular Elementor and Divi
  • proven full-stack web development knowledge with PHP, HTML/CSS, JavaScript and MySQL
  • knowledge in the design, management and maintenance of databases and data migration and integration
  • knowledge of tools and development environments such as Git/GitHub, Docker, JSON/XML and Bash terminal
  • ability to analyze requirements, design solutions, plan tasks, define priorities and autonomously follow IT projects from analysis to testing
  • ability to draft and maintain technical and functional documentation and to define standards and operational procedures
  • excellent communication, listening and relationship with users and collaborators, even in situations of technical support and training
  • ability to observe, evaluate and analyze technical and application problems, proposing and launching appropriate measures
  • high autonomy, sense of responsibility and ability to work both autonomously and in collaboration with colleagues, superiors, users and external partners
  • excellent knowledge (conversation and editing) of the Italian language, good knowledge (speaking and written) of the English language and basic knowledge (speaking and written) of at least another official language (French or German)
  • availability to work out of time and availability to provide picket service on weekends and out of time
  • are preferential title:
  • knowledge of authentication and integration systems and methods, such as SAML, LDAP and OAuth
  • experience in managing digital projects in educational, school or cantonal administration
  • knowledge of other LMS, as well as Moodle
  • teaching or working experience at Ticino schools
  • experience and/or training in mobile solutions
  • Certifications relevant to tasks such as ITIL, Cisco, Microsoft, ...

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required further documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

4 November 2026
