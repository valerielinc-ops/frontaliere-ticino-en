Full description

We offer you As a trial apprentice at IKEA Switzerland, you will get an insight into your desired apprenticeship and experience how interesting and varied it is to work and develop in an international company. You can find more information about apprenticeships and about IKEA as a training company on our website: www.ikea.ch/berufsbildung Who you are To apply for a trial placement, please send us your CV and a short text explaining why you are interested in this apprenticeship. The trial days take place in calendar weeks 25–26. Unfortunately, applications by email or post cannot be considered. You can find more information about the application process as well as our most important application tips on our website: www.ikea.ch/berufsbildung Oh yes, did you know that there are nothing but advantages as an apprentice at IKEA Switzerland?!

  • IKEA covers all mandatory costs incurred during your apprenticeship
  • The learner community with over 100 learners throughout Switzerland offers you a large network that supports you and which we strengthen with national events.
  • If you get good grades at school, you will be rewarded with a semester gift.
  • The weekly learning hours in the teaching company help you to complete the most important teaching tasks so that you still have enough free time.
  • During your teaching, you will change departments in your department so that your teaching is varied and instructive.
  • The well-trained specialist (your vocational trainer) is always at your side and supports you in your development.
  • Are you up for a little adventure? If you want, you can work for a short exchange in another furniture store during your apprenticeship. After your apprenticeship, all international opportunities are of course open to you.
