Full description

Portrait Do you like to teach at a small, family school? Then you are right with us! At the Burgfeld Quarter School, around 90 pupils in 6 age-mixed classes from kindergarten to 6. Class taught and supported in their learning. Together with the school houses Bitzius and Matte we belong to the school location Altstadt-Schosshalde Bern. These are a total of 30 classes with around 600 children. Further information can be found on our homepage: www./kirchenfeld-schosshalde.ch/altstadt schosshalde

Tasks

At the Burgfeld Quarter School you work during the maternity protection of the job holder in a kindergarten, a 1 / 2 class and a 3 / 4 class. The deputy is expected to start at the beginning of March 2027 and will last until the autumn holidays of 2027.

Requirements

  • School curative/educational or SHP training
  • Experience in special education
  • Interested, open, communicative and resilient personality
  • Willingness to work in a team and to engage with students with a vision
  • Experience in dealing with children with different needs
  • Experience in parental cooperation

We offer

  • An interesting place to work in the Burgfeld Quarter School
  • Collaboration with experienced teachers and support in the MR team
  • A pleasant working atmosphere in a well-coordinated and ready-to-meet small school team

The interviews are ongoing.
