Full description

Are your sense of responsibility and self-initiative on your strengths? Would you like to be a member of a leading and successful team? Then fit us perfectly. As a Lindenhof group, we are only so successful because more than 800 nursing professionals, about 100 hospital doctors, 300 documentaries and doctors, as well as 1300 other employees work in hand – and can always rely on each other. The focus of high-quality medicine is always a strong team. And to strengthen this team we are looking for you! Tasks: For our certified oncology center, we are looking for a dedicated and service-oriented specialist to support tumor documentation and medical data collection.

  • Collection and maintenance of oncological case data and aftercare data in tumor documentation software
  • Working with the weekly tumor boards including presentation of cases and support in protocol management
  • Control and plausibility of key figures with the controllers and the creation of relevant documentation for certifications and audits
  • Participation in internal and external audits
  • Collection and collection of documented data of highly specialized medicine (HSM) and medical registers from patient dossiers and medical reports
  • Support for annual accounts and data processing Requirements:
  • Complete training and experience in the medical field, ideally in nursing or surgical engineering
  • Professional experience in oncology
  • Very good IT user knowledge and a good understanding of data management and database systems
  • Structured, careful and quality-conscious operation
  • Team-oriented, flexible and solution-oriented personality with positive basic attitude
  • Motivation to make an important contribution to the continuous improvement of treatment quality We offer: Do you want to use your medical expertise in a new, sensory environment? With us, you take on a responsible task and make an important contribution to the quality of the care of our predominantly oncological patients. The tumour documentation and the collection of medical data form an important basis for the certification of our oncology centre and compliance with legal requirements. With your careful work, you actively support quality assurance and further development of our treatment standards. There is a collegial team, a modern working environment and ergonomically equipped jobs.
