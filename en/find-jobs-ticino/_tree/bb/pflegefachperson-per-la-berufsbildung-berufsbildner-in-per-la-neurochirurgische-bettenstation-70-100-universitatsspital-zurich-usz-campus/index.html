Full description

Become part of our neurosurgical unit with 32 beds and a team of over 40 employees. For the support and promotion of our students and learners, we are looking for a motivated nurse as a vocational trainer:in.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Comprehensive and person-centered care and care of neurosurgical patients • Independent planning, organization and execution of nursing interventions as well as active involvement of learners/students in the day-to-day care • Design and development of the care process in the reference care system • Accompanying, supporting and assessing learners/students (maintenance HF/FH, FaGe) by holding apprenticeship interviews and assessing with different types of qualifications in close cooperation with the vocational trainee/educational manager of the department • Share responsibility for a motivating, appreciative and learning-promoting training environment Requirements • You are a nursing specialist HF/FH with several years of professional experience in an acute hospital, ideally in the field of neurosurgery • Education experience or interest in developing further in education • Additional training SVEB 1 or willingness to complete it • Joy of supporting young adults in professional and personal development • Interested in the care of neurosurgical patients: women with commitment and quality awareness for patient care • As a socially competent, team-capable, flexible and resilient personality, you can move in a complex and dynamic work environment. We offer: Look forward to an exciting and varied working environment with many opportunities for personal and professional development – especially in the education sector. Our strength lies in interprofessional cooperation, characterized by mutual appreciation, respect and a high quality standard of care. A structured and careful introduction to your area of responsibility is a matter of course for us. You will be warmly supported by a committed, motivated team.
