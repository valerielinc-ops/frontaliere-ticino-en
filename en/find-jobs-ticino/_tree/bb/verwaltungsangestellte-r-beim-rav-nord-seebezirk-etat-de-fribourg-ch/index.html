Appointment authority / department / institution

The Office for the Labour Market (AMA) is the cantonal authority dealing with the labour market in the canton of Freiburg. Its task is to contribute to a market that is as balanced and prosperous as possible.

Become part of our team!

The employer promotes the compatibility of work and private life and offers its employees a modern working environment and attractive working conditions. You can find out more about this on the following page.

Their tasks

Administration, secretariat, reception and administrative support in the allocation to labour market measures (AMM).

Desired profile

  • Eidg. Certificate of Competence as Purchase. Employee, supplemented by several years of professional experience in administration
  • Editorial skills (protocols and correspondence)
  • Excellent knowledge of common computer programs (Word, Excel, etc.)
  • Mobility and flexibility
  • Interest in labour market issues and recruitment of job seekers
  • Knowledge of the economic structure
  • Mastering the German or French language with very good knowledge of the other language
