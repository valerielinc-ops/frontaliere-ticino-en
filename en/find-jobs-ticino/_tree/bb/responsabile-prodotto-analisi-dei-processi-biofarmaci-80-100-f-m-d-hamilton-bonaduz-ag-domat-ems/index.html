Descrizione True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", the Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the health sector since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in the fields of ventilators, automated pipetting, sample management and in the development of process sensors. The Business Unit Process Analytics develops, produces, and distributes state-of-the-art process measurement technology for customers in the biopharmaceutical, chemical and brewing industry.
