Head of Finance \&HR \- 8ю-100%

Recognized and essential for more than 40 years, Multi Personnel is an agency specialized in the provision of administrative staff and managers, temporary and fixed, in many economic sectors, such as banking, financial companies, multinationals, NGOs, SMEs and local institutions.

We would like to recruit a Head of Finance \&HR – 8ю-100%

Mission:

  • Independently manage accounting.
  • Prepare and complete the monthly and annual loops in full.
  • Manage debtors, creditors, liquidity and payment transactions.
  • Develop budgets, forecasts and various financial documents.
  • Ensure full management of wages and social insurance.
  • Manages personnel administration and monitoring of HR files.
  • Monitor insurance, contracts and various administrative procedures.
  • Work closely with management, external partners and the trustee.

Profile

  • Holder of a federal patent, Bachelor's degree or higher education deemed equivalent (essential requirement).
  • Professional experience confirmed in a similar function, ideally within an SME.
  • Excellent mastery of general and analytical accounting, looping and salary management.
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss labour law and social insurance.
  • Good mastery of Microsoft 365 as well as accounting software.
  • Self-contained, organized and rigorous personality, with discretion and a great sense of responsibility.
  • Knowledge of A3 software and the basics in German are assets.
