Full description

Put to the Point

As a university intern in the field of political business, you support the research assistant political business and the head of the Cyber Command in the processing of political business, the development of written products as well as presentations and research on relevant topics. You will get in-depth insights into current topics in the Cyber Command, various areas and various projects. You will learn to compile information and prepare it in accordance with the addressees and get in contact with people from different departments as well as other stakeholders in the federal administration. An open, efficient and team-oriented approach is particularly important. We offer you the opportunity to participate in real work fields and to take responsibility and work together with experienced professionals.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20519
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Support the Chief Cyber Officer and the research assistant in day-to-day political business (controlling, statements, file and speech notes, preparation of operational principles and guidelines)
  • Pre- and post-processing of political inquiries, actively working on interpellations, motions and official consultations, as well as developing and visually preparing presentations
  • Follow current thematic developments, conduct research and participate in the creation of products
  • Mastering versatile administrative activities independently (including organising correspondence, meetings and events and writing minutes)
  • In their absence, the representative of the scientific staff ensure political business
  • Working in the deployment and coordination of Command Cyber

This makes you unique

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in International Relations or Political Science at a technical college or university
  • Professional and methodological competence in the political and scientific environment
  • Interest in commercial work, very good IT knowledge (Microsoft Office)
  • Motivated, team-capable, communicative and reliable personality
  • Independent working methods, flexibility and very good organizational ability
  • Enjoy learning and dealing with people as well as good knowledge of a second official language

Your commitment to security and freedom

The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, the latest technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
