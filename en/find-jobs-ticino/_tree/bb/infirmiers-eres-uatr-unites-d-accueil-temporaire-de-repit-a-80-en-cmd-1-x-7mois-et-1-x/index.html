Full description

To allow family caregivers a period of rest, the UATRs offer AVS-age clients short stays and provide services in continuity with those provided at home. Client care and support adapt to their physical and mental health. Since the UATR is a 24-hour service throughout the year, working hours are in 3x8 hours. The nurse works in interdisciplinarity at a main assignment site:

  • Right side: Mervelet, Petit-Saconnex (for the 10-month HOM),
  • Left Bank: Villereuse, Geneva 1207 (for the 7-month HOM),

The nurse may be required to intervene on an ad hoc basis on one of the other 3 sites according to the needs of the service (third site: Twins, Thônex). (Please contact your mail carefully: your availability date and for which building(s) you are applying). Your missions and responsibilities

  • Deliver comprehensive, preventive, curative, educational and palliative personalized nursing services to AVS-age clients independently and/or on medical delegation
  • Exercise a role of situational referent, on a daily basis, to adjust the care of the client according to his/her needs and acting in accordance with the principles of the professional code of ethics nurse
  • Organize medico-social approaches, coordination and collaboration with all stakeholders in a given client situation by mobilizing all available resources (both primary and professional)
  • Work closely with ASSCs and caregivers by delegating care from their area of expertise
  • Provide written and oral transmissions of accompanying customer information to enable follow-up and continuity in client care
  • Participate in training and research activities within the objectives defined by the Professional Practices Directorate.
  • Bachelor of nursing or equivalent training, authorization of practice in the canton
  • Minimum two years of professional experience in the geriatric community
  • Knowledge of the Geneva care network
  • Desired psycho-geriatric experience
  • Ability to work independently and as an interdisciplinary team
  • Excellent adaptability and delegation
  • Responsibility and direction solution.
  • Start: as soon as possible
  • Postulatio delay: 8.11.2026
  • Please attach to your file:
  • an updated curriculum vitae
  • a cover letter
  • a copy of your diplomas and work certificates.

We only consider full applications. We guarantee complete confidentiality in the processing of your file.
