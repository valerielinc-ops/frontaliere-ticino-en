Secondary School Liestal

The Liestal secondary school is the largest secondary school in the canton of Baselland and consists of the two Burg and Frenke school buildings. We currently teach 47 school classes from the A, E and P traits, with over 850 pupils from eight municipalities. Our interprofessional team consists of 145 teachers, curative school teachers and social teachers. We cultivate collegiality, an open approach and humor is not lacking - according to our motto: "tame lehre, tame grow, tame si". The school management accompanies and supports you at the beginning.
