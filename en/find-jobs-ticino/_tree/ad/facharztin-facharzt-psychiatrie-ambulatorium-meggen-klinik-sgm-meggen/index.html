We treat people with respect and appreciation. As a holistic person, we take you seriously.

As a recognized specialist clinic for psychiatry, psychotherapy and psychosomatics with a cantonal service mandate, we set standards in the treatment and care of our patients. With around 175 employees, we offer inpatient and outpatient treatments according to the bio-psycho-socio-spiritual model at various locations in German-speaking Switzerland and integrate Christian values and perspectives into our therapies on request. The synergy of expertise, personal support and holistic support makes us a valued address for people seeking support in challenging life situations.

Their tasks

  • Specialist responsibility and supervision of medical and psychological-psychotherapeutic treatments
  • Active participation in the interdisciplinary team of therapists as part of a holistic treatment concept
  • Self-responsible psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of outpatients
  • Networking with local and regional partner institutions
  • Discussions and consultations

Your profile

  • Completed or advanced specialist training in psychiatry and psychotherapy (or equivalent) with several years of professional experience
  • Motivation to work with a holistic treatment concept
  • Strong relationship strengths in team and patient work
  • High service orientation and high quality awareness
  • Process-oriented, entrepreneurial and result-oriented thinking and acting
  • Willingness to actively engage with Christian faith in the working context
