Full description

For our customer, a major Swiss distributor in the food and non-food sector, we are looking for several, experienced and experienced chauffeurs / chauffeuses Kat. B

Tasks

  • Timely and correct loading of the 3.5 To. delivery vehicle according to the loading list (fresh and frozen products, non-food and general goods)
  • Ensuring quality aspects of hygiene and cold chain
  • Timely and correct delivery of customer orders
  • Payment transactions with the customer (cash payment, debit and credit card debits and credit notes)
  • Handling of returns and receipt of empty goods
  • Billing of tours and operation of the cash center
  • Vehicle maintenance (care, cleaning and refuelling of the vehicle)
  • Ensuring an economical driving style and careful handling of the equipment
  • Scheduled working hours: Monday to Saturday from 10.00 to 20.45

  • Good physical condition
  • Valid CH driver certificate
  • Clean criminal record extract
