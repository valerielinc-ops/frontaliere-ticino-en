Full description

Tasks

  • A collaborative, cross-functional product team with a team lead, a product manager and several T-shaped full-stack engineers who share responsibility
  • Depending on what suits the team, you work with Scrum or Kanban. Engineers take part in the refinement and actively shape architecture and product decisions, instead of just implementing them
  • Plenty of space to shape high code quality in your own team and across team boundaries. Enough legacy, complexity and grown structures to make things noticeably better step by step

Requirements

We match when you

Java, Python and Backend Depth

  • In-depth practical experience with Java 21+ and Spring Boot 4
  • have practical experience with reactive or asynchronous programming models, understand the non-blocking programming model and know when it makes sense.
  • safe in modern Python, use it specifically and know when Python is the right tool and when Java
  • have built and operated distributed backend systems on a large scale, ideally in e-commerce or comparable high-throughput domains
  • move with event-driven communication patterns (Kafka, RabbitMQ) and know the trade-offs between synchronous and asynchronous approaches

Engineering practices

  • modern engineering practices in everyday life: CI/CD, automated tests, decoupling, code reviews and observability
  • Seeing concepts like DDD, Clean Architecture, Event-Driven Architecture, SOLID, Feature Flags and Dependency Injection as practical tools rather than buzzwords, and using them pragmatically and with judgment
  • Convinced behind "You build it, you own it" and have experience in environments that release several times a day
  • Lead the team on code quality and meaningful testing practices, with coverage not an end in itself – tests that find real bugs count for you

AI / Agentic Coding: a core expectation, not a nice-to-have

  • AI coding tools such as Claude Code or GitHub Copilot of course used in everyday work, for code generation, tests, refactoring and documentation
  • Go beyond autocomplete: You actively try out agent workflows, such as automated PR reviews, scaffolding, and multi-level code generation, and critically review their output before committing
  • Understand the risks: You know that AI-generated code can accelerate technical debt and undermine understanding if not carefully reviewed, and you enforce appropriate review standards
  • helps shape the introduction of AI tools in the team and shares what works, what does not and where the limits are

Collaboration and Product Mindset

  • Enjoy sharing knowledge about pair programming, whiteboard sessions and code reviews, and actively help raise the engineering level in the team
  • likes to work closely with product managers and really understand the problem to be solved. You help shape requirements and think from the point of view of users and business
  • Values true agility: short iterations, fast learning, active feedback loops and data-driven decision-making
  • Communicate warmly, appreciate an open learning culture, build personal relationships in the team and not take yourself too seriously

We probably don’t fit together that well if you

  • Prefer frameworks such as SAFe or projects that start with a fixed specification
  • has worked mainly in highly regulated environments, with no experience in a fast, product-oriented environment with distributed systems
  • feel most comfortable in models where infrastructure, backend, frontend, testing and product thinking are strictly separated
  • Requirements are better formulated, instead of shaping them together with product management
  • Seeing AI tools as a threat or gimmick, not a real productivity lever worth mastering
  • See training primarily as an employer’s task and not as your own intrinsic motivation
  • You are more likely to be frustrated by imperfect systems than encouraged. Small Spoiler: We are not yet at our destination
  • Work and humor strictly separated from each other

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Super-compulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business appointments.
