Full description

A solid and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in professional training, it fosters the development of young talent, with more than 120 apprentices trained each year across our departments. For the HR Digital Transformation team within our Human Resources Department, we are looking for an intern to start immediately or by agreement. As a university/HES intern, you will actively participate in various HR digital transformation projects, making an active contribution to the digitalization of HR processes through projects with high strategic and organizational impact. You will provide genuine support in driving digital HR projects and in optimizing our systems and data.

Responsibilities

  • Project coordination: Active support for the planning, monitoring, and management of HR digital transformation projects (including tool implementation and process optimization).
  • Data analysis: Collect, analyze, and interpret data to inform strategic decisions (Power BI, advanced Excel).
  • Reporting: Prepare regular reports on project progress and achieved results.
  • Team support: Provide ongoing operational support for existing HR systems and processes.
  • Documentation and continuous improvement: Draft and keep project documentation up to date.
  • Communication and change management: Facilitate communication among stakeholders by preparing materials (slides, FAQs, internal news, etc.) and ensure transparency of information.

Profile

  • You are currently pursuing an academic curriculum in a field closely related to Human Resources, Information Systems, Project Management, Business Analysis, or equivalent.
  • You have a good knowledge of data analysis tools (Excel, Power BI).
  • You are interested in the world of HR, innovation, and digital tools.
  • You have an analytical, structured, and solution-oriented mindset.
  • You have good communication skills in French and ideally in English.

Contact The HR team looks forward to receiving your complete application package (CV, cover letter, and last 3 grade transcripts).
