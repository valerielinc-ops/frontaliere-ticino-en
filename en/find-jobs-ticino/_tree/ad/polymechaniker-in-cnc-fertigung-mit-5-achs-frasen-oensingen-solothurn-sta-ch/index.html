Full description

The activity mainly takes place on modernly equipped 5-axis milling plants. You work independently on prototypes and series up to 50 pieces and improve the milling programs. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Set up 5-axis milling plants with Heidenhain independently
  • Operating CNC machines safely in continuous operation
