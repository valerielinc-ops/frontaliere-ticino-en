Specialist in Urology - Ausschreibung

L.EOC The multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes throughout the cantonal territory for a total of 1,000 beds.

The organisation allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring that the population has a comprehensive and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required.

Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to human relationships, the EOC provides a quality health care care by taking care of over 41,000 patients per year and providing over 600,000 outpatient consultations.

To complete our team of the Regional Hospital of Locarno "La Carità" we look for a medical specialist in Urology (8-100%),

Profile

The person we are looking for will be part of the Urology department of the Regional Hospital of Locarno.

We intend to expand our team in anticipation of the imminent acquisition of a new laser instrumentation for the treatment of calculosis, urinary pathway malignancies and prostate hypertrophy.

Our unit is part of the EOC Cross-Servizio di Urologia, and collaborates with the offices of Bellinzona and Lugano for the treatment of oncology and with robotics.

Requirements

  • Degree in medicine and specialization in urology already concluded.
  • For doctors with foreign qualifications, recognition by the Federal Commission for the medical professions MEBEKO and registration with MedReg before the start of the activity is required.
  • Get the Libero Exercise authorization issued by the DSS Canton Ticino.
  • Proven knowledge of Italian (level required at least B2) with relative registration to MedReg before the start of the activity.
  • The Commission is also concerned about the need for a European Community policy on the environment.

Information

  • Date of entry: to be agreed.

Candidate

  • Full candidacies from the "Lunch Vitae" letter of motivation, copies of the qualifications and work, must be sent in electronic format via the EOC web portal in the section "Work Offers - Medical Staff."
  • Applications sent by ordinary mail or e-mail will not be taken into account.
