Full description

We require real professionals – not only on the road, but also in logistics and our own workshops. Our state-of-the-art fleet of around 1,800 vehicles deserves regular maintenance and precise repairs. Are you joining us?

Tasks

  • You carry out repair and maintenance work on industrial equipment throughout Switzerland at our logistics sites and in our workshop
  • By professionally replacing wear parts and components, you ensure reliable operation
  • You document your outdoor missions carefully and ensure clear communication with the workshop
  • You support customer service with relevant information about required spare parts

Profile

  • You have completed vocational training in vehicle technology and have solid technical know-how
  • Ideally, you will bring experience as a truck mechanic: with you and know you well in the areas of hydraulics and electronics
  • You work independently, accurately and reliably, are team-oriented and resilient even in demanding situations
  • Very good knowledge of German distinguishes you
  • Knowledge of French is a plus, as is the category B driving licence

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • You become part of our family culture - respect, trust and decency are important to us
  • We keep up with the times - you have modern and safe work equipment at your disposal
  • Early Bird or Night Owl? Full-time or part-time? We accept your wishes.

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
