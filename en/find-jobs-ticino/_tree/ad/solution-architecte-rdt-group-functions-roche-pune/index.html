The Position At Roche, we believe every employee can make a mark. As a Senior Solution Architect, you will join our Legal, Compliance and Group Communication (LCG) team to lead the architectural evolution of our Product Data, Analytics, and AI portfolio. Reporting to the Head of Product Data, Analytics and AI, you will act as a visionary change agent, challenging business requirements and proposing AI-driven efficiencies to design complex, global compliance solutions. The Opportunity In this role, you will have the chance to influence the strategic direction of our data landscape through the following priorities:

  • Lead the strategic migration of legacy SAP BW systems to SAP Datasphere, ensuring high performance and data integrity.
  • Drive the Roche corporate Data strategy by aligning architectural transitions with global enterprise standards.
  • Act as a high-level consultant to senior business stakeholders, proactively challenging requirements to ensure genuine business value.
  • Define the architectural roadmap for data modeling, analytics, and integration patterns (APIs, ETL) within the LCG domain.
  • Implement cutting-edge AI efficiencies using Generative AI, Machine Learning, and Agentic AI frameworks to automate compliance.
  • Ensure robust information architecture and provide expert guidance across the broader Data & AI product suite.
  • Govern architecture standards to maintain a scalable and secure data fabric/mesh environment for global operations.
  • Collaborate with geographically dispersed teams to lead virtual architecture organizations and foster a culture of technical excellence.

Who you are

We are looking for a proactive negotiator and technical expert who meets the following qualifications:

  • You possess 15+ years of professional experience, with at least 10 years specifically in Architecture leading complex migrations.
  • You hold a Master’s or Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related technical field.
  • You are an expert in the SAP Ecosystem, specifically with deep, hands-on knowledge of SAP BW/4HANA and SAP Datasphere.
  • You demonstrate AI/ML mastery through a proven track record of implementing GenAI and Agentic AI within an enterprise context.
  • You have extensive Data Engineering experience with Big Data systems, modern data fabric, and cloud stacks like Snowflake and SAP BTP.
  • You bring a proactive mindset, with the ability to anticipate business needs and "look around corners" before requirements are articulated.
  • You are a preferred expert in compliance reporting, such as the Sunshine Act or similar global regulatory data standards.
