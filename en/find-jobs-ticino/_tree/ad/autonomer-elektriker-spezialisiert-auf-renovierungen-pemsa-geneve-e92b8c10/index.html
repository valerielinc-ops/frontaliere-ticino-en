Your mission

Making complete projects for the renovation of electrical installations You will take part in renovations of apartments, villas and residential buildings, ensuring the modernization, adaptation to the norms and reliability of electrical installations. Read and interpret technical documentation You will analyze electrical designs and schematics to ensure precise execution, complying with project requirements and current standards. Perform preparation and wiring work You will perform the groove work with the help of a channel, the sheathing and laying of the cables necessary for the realization of the electrical systems. Install and connect electrical equipment Provide installation, connection and commissioning of equipment, electrical panels, lighting fixtures and other equipment, in compliance with quality standards and standards. Intervention on strong current and weak current systems You will participate in the installation, modification and commissioning of strong current and weak current systems, ensuring reliability, performance and safety. Ensure fault repair and technical adjustments You will diagnose malfunctions, perform repair operations and perform the necessary changes on existing systems. Working independently and representing the company In direct contact with customers on the field, you will give proof of professionalism, rigour and an excellent sense of service to ensure their satisfaction during all interventions And now we're talking about you? Solid electrical training You have a Federal Qualification Certificate (CFC) as an electrical installer or an equivalent recognised training. Proposals of the technical skills necessary to intervene independently on electrical restructuring projects. A proven experience in restructuring You have gained several years of experience in the renovation of villas, apartments and residential buildings. Master the work of transformation, modernization and adaptation to the norms of electrical installations. Real autonomy in the field Used to manage your yards independently, you can organize your work, take initiatives and ensure a rigorous monitoring of the interventions entrusted to you. Excellent relational skills Thanks to your sense of service, your professionalism and your care in appearance, you represent the company in a reliable and serious way towards customers and the various subjects involved in the yards. Appreciated technical skills The mastery of NIBT standards is a real strength and allows you to operate in compliance with the safety and quality requirements in force. Mobility and availability In possession of the driver's license, you feel at ease in everyday travel between different yards. Quick availability is very appreciated to reach the teams as soon as possible. A reliable and committed profile Rigorous, conscientious and motivated, attach great importance to well done work and fully dedicate yourself to the success of projects entrusted to you
