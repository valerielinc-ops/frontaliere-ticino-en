Kummler+Matter EVT AG. Together with the future. We specialize in electrical installations for infrastructure projects in energy, transport and telecommunications sectors and contribute to a reliable flow of energy, mobility and data. We design and manage electrical and mechanical systems: from traffic control and driving systems to parking, energy plants and network infrastructures oriented to the future. As a company in Equans Switzerland, a leader in providing complete solutions for buildings and energy in Switzerland, we also promote the transformation and digitization of our customers and work together with the energy transition.   • Professional experience in telecommunications and optical fibres
  • High sense of responsibility and strong safety orientation
  • Suitable for operational activities in the field and in the railway environment
  • Good knowledge of German language
  • Driving licence category B
