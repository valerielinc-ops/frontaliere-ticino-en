Entry into office: to be agreed Activity rate 80% - 100% The Clinique romande de réadaptation (CRR) in Sion is a leading player in the field of specialized rehabilitation in Switzerland. A true pillar of the integrated care chain, it works closely with university hospitals and specialized centres to provide coordinated, innovative and high-quality care, focusing on functional recovery and the sustainable reintegration of patients. In order to complete our healthcare team in our neurological rehabilitation sector, we are looking for: Nurse HES or ES Activity rate: 80–100% Workplace: Sion (VS) Contract type: CDI Entry into office: immediately or to be agreed Mission As a HES-ES nurse, you intervene in patients with complex neurological conditions: stroke, spinal cord damage, head and brain trauma, multiple sclerosis, neurodegenerative diseases or other diseases of the central and peripheral nervous system. You: Develop and implement individualized care plans oriented towards autonomy and quality of life. Ensure technical and relational care adapted to complex situations. Observe, analyze and anticipate the clinical evolution of patients. Coordinate and oversee the care provided by ASSCs, ensuring safety and excellence. Participate in interdisciplinary clinical decisions. Work closely with the medical profession, therapists and the entire interdisciplinary team. Manage and train learners and contribute to the integration of new employees.

  • Your profile Diploma ES or Bachelor in Nursing, or recognized equivalent (recognition Swiss Red Cross for foreign degrees) . Interest in neurological rehabilitation and accompaniment of patients with motor, cognitive or behavioural deficits. Initiative, high sense of responsibility and reliable professional posture. Strong psychosocial skills, with a proven ability to establish a fair and professional therapeutic relationship with patients in situations of high vulnerability. Stress resistance, professional stamina and receding ability, necessary to exercise in a demanding care environment. Openness to intervene on an ad hoc basis in other units to promote continuity of care and mutual assistance between teams. Motivation to engage with patients who require intensive and long-term support. Master's degree in written and oral French (minimum B2). Our Long-Term Patient Support Offer: Track patient progress and contribute directly to their return to independence. Enriching Environment: Collaborate with experts from various fields in an interdisciplinary approach. Continuing training: Access a wide range of training courses to strengthen your skills and develop your career. Dynamic team: Work in a caring environment where efforts are recognized and valued. Benefits: High social benefits, many benefits, free park space, and much more.
