Your tasks

  • In this role, you take over the personnel, organizational and professional leadership in a dual leadership model of the openly run psychotherapy station 2 with 20 beds.
  • You develop the care and treatment quality continuously in accordance with the economic requirements and together in a team.
  • Team development and employee promotion on the station are also part of your area of responsibility.
  • You work on cross-departmental projects and actively shape the therapeutic environment and the processes on the station.
  • The networking and coordination of care within the framework of the reference person system and cooperation in the interdisciplinary and interprofessional treatment team is important to us. Your profile
  • The training as a nurse HF / FH you have completed (SRC recognition required with a foreign diploma), ideally with a focus on psychiatry. Experience in the field of psychiatry or interest in a deepening in this specialty are beneficial.
  • You have several years of professional experience and have already gained initial leadership experience and have already deepened it in a leadership education or are ready for it.
  • You recognize problems, solve them with a view to the future and enjoy working in an interdisciplinary and sustainable manner.
  • You have a high willingness to deal with the development of modern psychiatric treatment.
  • As a resilient and open-minded personality, you convince with a high level of social and communication skills and have very good organizational skills. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also get the opportunity to get to know your future team. You will receive a telephone confirmation from us if we decide for you after the second interview and ask you if you are still convinced of us. If we agree, we will draw up your contract. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information.
