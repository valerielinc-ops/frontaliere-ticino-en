Lateral entry train driver: in shunting Lucerne 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Lateral entry as a train driver: in shunting in Lucerne Lucerne, by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. Around 14,000 passenger transport employees ensure that the more than 1.4 million SBB customers we transport every day have easy access to public transport and travel from A to B both safely and on time. As a central professional group, the shunting personnel make a key contribution to this. An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you will move SBB together with your teammates, as well as varied and paid training. You also have various internal training opportunities. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even with irregular working hours. We pay additional wages for night and weekend work - e.g. 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night shifts you will receive additional time surcharges of between 10 and 30%. This means you have more free time than employees without night and weekend shifts. Possible start dates in 2027:

  • March 1, 2027
  • May 1, 2027

You can move that.

  • You prepare passenger trains for their journey and move them to the correct starting point in and around the station (up to a maximum of 40 km/h).
  • You put trains together, separate individual cars and make sure that everything is in the right place.
  • You get the train ready to go, check the most important functions and carry out brake checks.
  • During your work, you maintain an overview and ensure that all processes run safely and smoothly.
  • You can repair minor problems yourself or report them to the responsible office. You can find out more about the job profile here. This is what you bring with you.
  • You are attentive, concentrated and physically resilient in order to successfully complete this demanding work in 24-hour shifts. You also enjoy working outside in any weather.
  • Since all instructions and documentation are electronic, you will quickly find your way around using digital tools.
  • You have completed basic education (at least EBA) and already have practical experience
  • ideally from a craft or logistics environment.
  • You have very good spoken and written knowledge of German (level B2).
  • You have a perfect sense of color (no red/green weakness).
