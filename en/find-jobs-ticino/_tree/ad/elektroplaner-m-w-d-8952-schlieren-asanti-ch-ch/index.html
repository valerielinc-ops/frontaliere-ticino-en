Full description

J e t z t b e w e r b e n Asanti – Your reliable partner for all matters concerning commercial, technical and industrial professions. We support job seekers and companies in the cantons of Zurich and Aargau. For our customer, a successful company in the Limmat Valley, we are looking for a motivated electric planner (m/f/d) to strengthen his team. Admission: immediately or by agreement Your tasks

  • Creation and adaptation of electrical schemes for network and industrial plants – from the concept phase to execution planning
  • Processing of technical documents and support in the standardization of planning processes
  • Participation in tenders: development of specifications, cost estimates and technical specifications
  • Support of the project managers: in the planning and implementation of network construction projects – you ensure that technical requirements, standards and safety requirements are met
  • Coordination with internal and external bodies such as energy suppliers and assembly partners
  • Preparation of audit documents and support for project documentation

Your profile

  • Completed training as an electrical planner or equivalent qualification
  • Good application knowledge in common CAD programs (e.g. EPLAN, AutoCAD or similar)
  • Knowledge of common systems and tools in the field of planning, calculation and documentation (e.g. Steiger, NPK, MS Office)
  • Enjoying a varied activity between technical planning and practical relevance
  • Team-ready, committed and structured
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Category B driving licence

What to expect Our customer asks you for a challenging and versatile job in a growing company, where you can use your expertise in a targeted way and further develop it. Flat hierarchies, short decision-making processes and a collegial working environment characterize our corporate culture. We promote education and training, offer modern work equipment and an attractive premium model – and give you the opportunity to actively participate in the design of our projects.
