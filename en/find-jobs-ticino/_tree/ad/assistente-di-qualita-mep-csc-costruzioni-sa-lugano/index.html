Full description

CSC Costruzioni SA , part of the international Webuild Group , has been operating in Switzerland for over 60 years, delivering complex and sustainable construction projects.

With more than 300 employees and 20+ active projects , we combine local expertise with the strength and know-how of one of the world's leading construction groups.

For our major project in Geneva , we are looking for a: Quality Assistant - MEP

The position supports the Quality Manager in implementing and monitoring project quality requirements, with a specific focus on MEP (Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing) systems and related construction site activities.

Key Responsibilities

  • Support the Quality Manager in implementing and monitoring project quality requirements, with a specific focus on MEP systems .
  • Perform regular inspections and quality control checks on works, products, and processes.
  • Verify compliance with project specifications, technical drawings, method statements, and applicable regulations.
  • Record, manage, and analyze quality control data and inspection results.
  • Ensure that quality records and documentation are accurately maintained, updated, and readily available.
  • Identify, document, and report non-conformities, defects, and deviations to the Quality Manager.
  • Support the implementation and follow-up of corrective and preventive actions.
  • Assist in the preparation and updating of quality documentation for internal and external audits.
  • Collaborate with site teams, subcontractors, and supervisors to ensure quality requirements are properly understood and consistently implemented.
  • Monitor adherence to project quality standards across construction activities.
  • Ensure that assigned quality-related activities are completed within the deadlines established by the Quality Manager and in accordance with the project schedule.

Requirements

  • Degree in Engineering or a related discipline. Specialization in MEP is considered a plus.
  • 1-5 years of professional experience in a similar role.
  • Fluent proficiency in at least two of the following languages: Italian, English, and French.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office applications.
