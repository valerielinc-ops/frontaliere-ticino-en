Full description

With your flair for numbers, you'll support our finance team and ensure that our services are correctly and timely billed. As a regional hospital/acute hospital with an ambulance service, nursing home, and Spitex, we are responsible for the basic medical care of the Davos population and guests. However, the well-being of our employees is just as important to us. Working with us, you'll be part of a manageable, yet regionally important operation, we maintain a collegial work environment, and we appreciate the proximity to nature and the numerous sports and leisure opportunities in Davos. ## Responsibilities

  • Responsibility for the correct representation and application of the ambulatory and in-patient tariffs and services
  • Ensuring complete service recording
  • Creating ambulatory and in-patient patient bills to insurers and the public sector ## Requirements
  • Completed commercial or medical education
  • In-depth knowledge of the Swiss tariff system (TarDoc, DRG..)
  • Careful, reliable, and independent working style
  • Experience in the hospital or insurance sector is a great plus
  • Skilled use of computer tools
  • At least good oral English skills
  • Willingness to immerse oneself in a challenging subject area and perseverance ## What we offer
  • Varied and responsible task in a collegial team
  • Attractive employment conditions and social benefits; hospital-owned kindergarten
  • Interesting internal training and further education opportunities
  • Diverse sports and leisure offer in the highest city in Europe ## Contact
