Company description

leteam is a leading temporary work company with 27 agencies and over 39 years of experience. Our areas of competence are construction, crafts, industry and technology. We are looking for people who like to move, meet challenges and work in a rhythmic environment. Our partner, active in logistics and delivery, is looking for several experienced drivers/deliverers to ensure the distribution of parcels to individuals in the Upper Valais, including mountain areas.

Description of post

Your missions

  • Daily delivery in Upper Valais.
  • Independent organization of your tour.
  • Direct contact with customers.

What you need to know

  • Opening hours: start at 8 a.m., with a stamp card that calculates your working time from deposit to return.
  • Break: 1h adjustable automatically deducted.
  • Workload: important, with a steady pace and stress to manage.
  • Overtime: frequent due to high activity.

We are looking for an experienced person, able to manage a significant workload while maintaining quality contact with customers. You will have to exercise autonomy in organizing your tour, while respecting delivery times. Knowledge of the region between Sierre and Brig is essential to optimize your journey and ensure fast and efficient service. As a driver/distributor, you will be the face of our company to customers, so it is crucial to maintain a professional and courteous attitude in all circumstances. The ability to work under pressure and to manage stress is also paramount in this role, as overtime is frequent due to the high level of activity in the logistics and delivery sector. In short, this position is for you if you like movement, challenges and a dynamic working environment.

Profile sought

  • OBLIGATORY: 2 years experience as a delivery driver
  • OBLIGATORY: live at max 30 minutes from Gamsen
  • Valid driver's license.
  • Good physical condition.
  • Stress resistance.
  • Experience confirmed in delivery.
  • Good knowledge of the region between Sierre and Brig.
  • Basic skills in German.

We offer

  • A dynamic mission in a recognized company.
  • A stimulating environment.
  • Personalized coaching by Pablo Muino.
