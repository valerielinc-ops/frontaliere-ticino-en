Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement. Tasks: • You support the management of anesthesia care competently in administrative and organizational matters, coordinate meetings and assume a central hub function within the care area anesthesia • They accompany the personnel processes from entry to exit, take over the administration of the NDS students and reliably coordinate the administrative concerns of the employees • They are responsible for the monthly PEP degree, coordinate the weekly schedule of anesthesia care and provide competent support with questions about working hours, time management and short-term personnel assignments. • They supervise the care-specific SharePoint and intranet pages, support in authorization and role management (IAM), organize training and team events and together with the team ensure a reliable representation within the secretariat. Requirements • They have completed basic commercial training EFZ or a comparable qualification. Ideally, you already have experience in healthcare, personnel administration or a similar coordination function. • They have good IT user knowledge, are well-versed in dealing with digital applications and systems and appreciate working with different stakeholders • They work in a structured, independent and reliable manner and also keep track of different tasks and priorities in a dynamic working environment. • They convince with communication skills, discretion and sense of responsibility and maintain an overview even in demanding situations, act solution-oriented and perform professionally We offer: • You take on a central and versatile role in anesthesia care and work closely with the care management as well as various specialist areas of a dynamic clinic in a center hospital. • She expects a versatile and responsible area of responsibility with a lot of independence and exciting insights into the personnel, planning, management and organizational processes of a modern acute care hospital. • You benefit from an appreciative and collegial working environment and work closely with experienced specialists and leaders from different professional groups • You will gain versatile insights into the healthcare sector and thus create a valuable basis for your long-term professional development in the hospital environment
