Full description

You are an expert in financial analysis at the Investment, Planning and Projects division of the Finance Directorate. You play a strategic role and create the conditions for professional and optimized management of the financing of the institution's major projects. This item comprises about ten institutional projects, each representing an investment of 20 to 60 million francs, mainly related to the real estate estate estate with a fleet of more than 2 billion francs. In this context, you support the financial management of these major projects from initialisation to implementation, ensuring the completeness and accuracy of the financial information submitted to the Management Committee, while respecting the standards in force. Thus, you manage and guide in an integrated way the process of production and monitoring of business plans specific to these projects. You provide your expertise and methodological support for the development of these project plans and budgets. More specifically, you advise and provide models for calculating project profitability, including the calculation of return on investment. With your analytical expertise and in-depth knowledge of the hospital environment, you question and challenge the relevance of the assumptions made in the business plan. In addition, you offer an overall view of the institution's short-, medium- and long-term investments. At the same time, you are actively involved in the development and automation of global reporting and monitoring of investments, thus ensuring greater efficiency in the financial management of the institution. Upon delegation, you represent the Finance Directorate on major project steering committees, in particular on real estate projects.
