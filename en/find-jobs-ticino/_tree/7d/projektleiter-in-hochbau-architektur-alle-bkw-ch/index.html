Full description

Exciting engineering projects | scope for design | The advantages of a strong company

You accompany building construction projects in the engineering environment from the first idea to realization. You take responsibility for planning and coordination, develop well-thought-out solutions together with specialists and ensure that concepts become successful construction projects.

Your design space

  • At Grid & Hydro Engineering at BKW in Bern, you will take over the planning and project management of demanding building construction projects in the field of energy infrastructure such as substation construction and hydropower plants from the first project idea to the realization of the project.
  • As a building construction specialist, you bring your architectural and technical expertise to interdisciplinary engineering projects and develop solutions that convince both functionally and technically.
  • The project planning and coordination of buildings in conjunction with electrical, structural and other technical requirements are part of your versatile task area
  • Together with engineers and specialist planners from different disciplines, you will develop viable concepts, technical project foundations and solutions for complex structural issues.
  • You coordinate the disciplines involved, coordinate with clients and external partners and ensure compliance with quality, costs and deadlines
  • From feasibility testing to project planning and implementation, you bring your experience to bear and ensure that technical requirements result in thoughtful and feasible construction projects.

Your strengths

  • You have completed your studies as an architect: in FH or a comparable education at university level
  • In the management of building construction projects you have already gained experience and are familiar with the challenges of interdisciplinary planning and coordination
  • An independent, structured and solution-oriented working method as well as a clear communication with different project participants are among your strengths
  • You quickly recognize technical and structural connections, know the relevant legal framework and keep an overview even for complex projects.
  • Joy of cooperation, professional curiosity and the aspiration to develop convincing solutions together
