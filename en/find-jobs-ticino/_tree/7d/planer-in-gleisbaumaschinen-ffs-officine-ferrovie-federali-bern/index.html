Planner: in track construction machines 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Planner:in track construction machines Bern / Work Smart, 01.02.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

In this role, you will make a significant contribution to the timely and cost-effective provision of track construction machinery (GBM) for infrastructure maintenance and renewal. You will work closely with colleagues from planning, logistics and operations and shape processes that strengthen the reliability of the railway network. You can expect a helpful team with short decision paths and the opportunity to develop professionally and digitally. Our benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for your family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • For the four regions, you organize the logistics chain of heavy track construction machines (stamping machines, rail grinding trains, excavating machines, cranes) for maintenance and road renewals under economic and availability criteria.
  • In the internal planning tool Construction site assistant BSA you plan assignments for SBB, BLS, TPF and private construction sites and ensure efficient utilization of the machines.
  • In your role, you coordinate appointments and resources with regional coordinators, negotiate with external GBM service providers and monitor the provision of services in accordance with contracts.
  • In addition, you will take on partial technical management tasks for the GBM team by ensuring that the technical guidance is up-to-date, developing and carrying out training courses and supporting the professional development of employees.
  • As a power user, you are responsible for the setup and further development of the BSA, train users, coordinate permissions, correct errors and ensure the connection to SAP S4. In addition, you will develop automated processes and reporting to optimize planning and control.

You bring that with you.

  • You communicate convincingly and step-by-step with internal and external contacts: inside, convince through assertiveness and initiative and implement tasks responsibly, solution-oriented and targeted.
  • You bring several years of experience in the road area.
  • Secure handling of SAP and the common office applications as well as IT affinity and the willingness to familiarize yourself with area-specific applications is required.
  • A completed commercial, technical or railway basic training EFZ as well as in-depth knowledge in construction and logistics processes.
  • You master German very well (min. C1) and have basic knowledge of French (min. A2).
