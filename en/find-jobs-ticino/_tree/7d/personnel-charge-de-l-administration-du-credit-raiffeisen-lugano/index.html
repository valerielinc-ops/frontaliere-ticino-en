06.10.2026 Lugano

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Lugano, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Lugano and Pregassona, we are looking for a service-oriented personality as an employee / in credit processing.

What awaits you?

Their tasks

  • Processing of credit applications: collecting and controlling the data entry, checking the completeness of the documents, drawing up the contract documents, opening the necessary accounts, disbursement and takeover on time, archiving the fully processed credit dossiers and closing and returning credit positions.
  • Management of credit exposures: preparation of periodic reassessments, data collection for balance sheet analyses, processing of construction loans, maintenance of master data of mortgages and collateral, generation of interest and amortisation charges and monitoring and implementation of the dunning system for open positions.
  • Supporting credit counselling in the preparation of loan applications and data collection for further processing.
  • Detailed investigations with credit advisors or third parties (e.g. authorities, pension funds, insurance companies) in connection with credit processing.

What do you bring?

Your profile

  • Completed banking training
  • Good knowledge and experience in credit administration
  • Good computer science knowledge and openness to digital developments
  • mother tongue Italian
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Residence in Ticino
