Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • They advise and accompany the 10 cantonal schools as well as internal bodies in procurement projects over the entire procurement process.
  • They plan and conduct procurements and tenders under public procurement law and draw up the necessary documents and contracts in close coordination with the required agencies.
  • They help to establish a lean, digitally supported procurement management with clear specifications, standards, templates and role models.
  • They further develop contract, supplier and reporting and create more transparency on expenditure, contracts and savings potential.
  • They identify bundling, standardization and optimization potentials and implement them together with the participants.

What you bring

  • You have completed training or studies in business administration, procurement, supply chain management, law, administration or a comparable field.
  • They bring several years of practical experience in procurement, tendering, contract management or supplier management, ideally in a public environment.
  • they are familiar with public procurement, in particular the requirements for legally compliant procedures
  • Knowledge of the IVöB is an advantage.
  • They work in a structured, self-responsible and service-oriented manner and move safely in an environment with different interests.
  • They convince with communication, negotiating skills, analytical thinking and a good sense for pragmatic, sustainable solutions.

The Department of Vocational Education and Training and Secondary School combines diverse and attractive activities: it is responsible for the planning, control, supervision and financing of educational offers at the secondary level II, higher vocational training and vocational preparation and counselling.
