[Neuroth Hearing Centre SA]

Audioprosthetist (m/f/x)

Together for more quality of life

Do you want to help people hear better and participate fully in everyday life? Then join Neuroth and help us build a better future.

Groupe Neuroth is a successful family business that has been providing its expertise for over 115 years. Present in Switzerland since 2001 with more than 85 hearing centres, we accompany our clients every day towards a better quality of life through innovative hearing solutions. What unites us: our passion for hearing, innovation and proximity to our clients.

For our auditory center MONTHEY we are looking for a person engaged as

Occupancy rate: 80 \- 100%

You accompany hearing impaired people on their way to a better hearing experience. Thanks to your skills, your listening skills and your enthusiasm for modern hearing solutions, you can offer customized solutions that improve the daily lives of our clients.

Your missions:

  • Professionally advise and support our clients, customers and interested persons on our hearing solutions.
  • Adapt, sell and monitor the most modern hearing systems and associated accessories.
  • Perform hearing tests, earprinting or ear scans.
  • Develop and maintain lasting relationships of trust with our clients.
  • Manage and update customer data in our systems.
  • Actively participate in continuing training to constantly develop your professional knowledge.

Your profile

  • Completed audio-prothesist training or equivalent qualification.
  • Passion for your job and pleasure working with people.
  • Customer-oriented personality, empathic and with a strong sense of service.
  • Current professional knowledge and interest in new hearing technologies.
  • Experience with digital tools and good administrative and business knowledge.
  • Independent, reliable and rigorous method of work.

What we offer you:

  • A modern, caring and collaborative working environment.
  • Hearing centres equipped with professional and modern infrastructure.
  • Attractive working hours and 5 weeks of vacation.
  • Contemporary conditions of engagement as well as attractive social benefits.
  • Many training and development opportunities, including e-learning offers.

Ready for next step?

Do you want to put your skills, your passion for audioprosthesis and your personality at the service of a company that places the human at the heart of its concerns?

So we look forward to receiving your application and building with you the future of the hearing.
