Full description

The surgical bed ward at Campus Clara takes care of a total of 28 beds for patients before and after surgical procedures (mainly urology and gynecology) as well as for various surgical diseases.
The treatment includes pre- and postoperative care, pain therapy, wound care and mobilization. Tasks: • Ensuring professional and responsible nursing care as well as advice and instruction for our patients • Ensuring the care process • autonomous organisation of its own area of responsibility • Active support in achieving the educational goals of our students HF/FH and learners Requirements • Completed training as a dipl. Nurse HF/FH in an acute hospital • Several years of professional experience are desirable, but young professionals are also welcome • Good knowledge of German in word and writing • high level of technical and social competence and a strong sense of responsibility • Joy in the profession and the willingness to maintain an empathetic relationship with the patients entrusted to you We offer: • 28-33 paid leave days per year • Extensive opportunities for further education and training, e.g. own seminar program with a wide range of educational opportunities • Occupational health management with preventive measures such as ergonomic advice in the workplace • Part-time work and possibility of job sharing also in management functions • Bright, friendly rooms with modern infrastructure • Strong values and actively lived corporate culture • Characterisation and participation of the Claraspital by the employees The Claraspital, the largest private hospital in Basel, is a university teaching and research hospital with a full service mandate for highly specialized abdominal surgery and a supra-regional broadcast of its centers "Bäu" and "Tumor" as well as its special fields. In the Claraspital, employees find jobs where they can develop both professionally and humanly. At Claraspital, we attach great importance to consciously nurturing and developing our corporate culture and actively living the cultural criteria developed together "together - competently - responsibly".
