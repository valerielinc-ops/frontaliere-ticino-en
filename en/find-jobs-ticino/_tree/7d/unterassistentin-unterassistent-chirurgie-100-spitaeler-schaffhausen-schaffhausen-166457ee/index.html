Full description

The hospitals Schaffhausen operate an interdisciplinary emergency centre at the Cantonal Hospital. Together with the emergency practice led by family doctors, we offer comprehensive and high-quality medical care. With approximately 15,000 patients treated in a 24-hour operation (emergency centre) and additionally approx. 7,500 patients in the emergency practice (18 – 22 o’clock) are treated and cared for more than 20,000 patients per year.

This is what awaits you

  • Together with the emergency team, you support the surgical patients at the emergency center (wound care)
  • Entrance examinations/anamnesis survey in elective patients
  • If necessary, you provide assistance in various operations
  • Participation in departmental work such as visits, patient care and KG registrations
  • Integration into emergency/standby service (piket)
  • free accommodation in the nearby staff house

This will take us further.

  • Commitment and dedication in a clinically interdisciplinary oriented activity
  • Very good knowledge of German in language and writing (minimum requirement level C1)
  • Social competence in dealing with patients and relatives
  • Swiss or EU citizens
  • Student in Human Medicine
