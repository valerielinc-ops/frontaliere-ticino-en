Full description

To innovate, it is above all to seek concrete answers to the needs of our trades and customers. Within the Strategy & Innovation entity, we explore issues, explore solutions and confront them with the reality of the field to inform reflection and guide decisions.

After a year devoted to the evolution of our strategy and the implementation of new approaches, we continue the explorations undertaken, with particular attention to the evaluation of solutions and user tests.

To support these activities, we are looking for a curious, organized and pragmatic person who likes to understand the needs of users, go to the field and contribute to various exploration approaches.

Intern Innovation (60-80%)

Your Mission

Participate in exploration and innovation processes

  • Participate in ongoing exploration, including research and assessment of solutions.
  • Help explore new issues according to needs and opportunities.
  • Conduct research, collect and analyze information useful for exploration.
  • Document activities and summarize key lessons learned.

Prepare and perform user tests

  • Participate in the preparation of tests (frame definition, script development, planning).
  • Conduct field tests.
  • Contribute to the analysis and interpretation of results.

Support the operational functioning of the team

  • Participate in the organization and monitoring of certain activities.
  • Help coordinate various operational topics.
  • Prepare and update materials, monitoring documents and contact lists.
  • Provide administrative and logistical support according to current needs and priorities.

Contribute to other ad hoc activities

  • Participate in the organization of the presence of tl at the Innovation Crunch Time of HEIG-VD, in March.
  • Depending on the needs and opportunities, contribute to other topics or small missions with support.

Your profile

A student or young graduate of a university or college, you are interested in innovation, service design or user research. Curious, organized and proactive, you like to explore problems, go to the field and chat with users. You have a good analytical mind, can synthesize your observations and adapt your priorities according to needs.

Why is this internship a unique opportunity?

  • You will contribute to innovative approaches rooted in the concrete needs of trades and tl customers.
  • You will discover the different stages of an exploration approach, from understanding needs to assessing solutions.
  • You will have the opportunity to go to the field, meet users and participate in concrete tests.
  • You will benefit from proximity support, while having the opportunity to gain autonomy and take initiatives.
