Full description

Are you sensitive to the challenges of psychomotorism and want to contribute to the well-being and development of children and young people in difficulty in the Vaudian training system? Join our team in Carrouge!

  • Prevent, diagnose and treat functional and/or organic psychomotor disorders. Conduct a specific intervention with children and young people in schools affecting the body in its expressive, relational and functional aspects in order to promote their development and social, educational and vocational integration.
  • Support the school's training mission by providing specific skills and knowledge within the school.
  • Working with parents, school stakeholders and other stakeholders involved For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Ms Joëlle Bourgeois, team leader, who is happy to be at your disposal by email at joelle.bourgeois@vd.ch. Possible disclosure of criminal history is required for this function. Please attach a complete file (CV, diplomas and work certificates) in PDF only.
  • Master in psychomotority or title deemed equivalent.
  • Professional experience, interest in multidisciplinary and group work.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
