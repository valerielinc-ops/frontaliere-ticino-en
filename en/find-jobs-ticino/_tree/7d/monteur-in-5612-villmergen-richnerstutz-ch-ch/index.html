Full description

Assembly + Logistics – (100%)

  • Installer*in
  • In order to strengthen our assembly team, we are looking for a reliable and flexible fitter with a craftsman’s background.
  • This is what awaits you

As a member of a competent and committed team, you are responsible for the professional assembly of our projects in the areas of outdoor advertising, temporary buildings, event & exhibition construction, space & architecture and POS & Retail. This includes the installation of large format prints, cladding of scaffolding or the installation of light advertising throughout Switzerland. A large part of the assembly work also takes place in the interior, where exhibition stands or event locations are set up or premises are rebuilt. Your profile For this challenging and exciting work you bring several years of experience in dealing with metal and preferably electrics or Blachen montages and have learned as a further plus a craftsmanship profession. Fear of heights you do not know and you have a valid driver's license. Do you enjoy climbing or are even in possession of a certificate as a certified high-altitude worker – even better. Irregular working hours (even night shifts or weekend assignments) and assignments in the Swiss mountains, with sometimes several days at a time, are no problem for you. It is easy for you to communicate verbally and in writing in German. A high degree of flexibility, communicative and social competence as well as loyalty and a holistic quality thinking round off your profile. This is what we offer you We offer you a varied and challenging job in an exciting environment with a lot of personal responsibility, where you can contribute your personality and your skills every day. A generous expense regulations and a contemporary salary round off the package. You are in close contact with motivated project managers and are on the road throughout Switzerland. The collegial working environment and the sympathetic DNA of our company are other reasons why you should work with us. Further information
