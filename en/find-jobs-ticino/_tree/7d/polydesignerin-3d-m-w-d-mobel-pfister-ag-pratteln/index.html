Description

At pfister, we pursue a vision: We want our products to find their way into every Swiss home. To achieve this, we are looking for dedicated support. Help us bring the approximately 100'000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: you can expect a unique environment, work with appealing products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your efforts. Around 1'400 people already work at pfister - will you be joining them soon?

Your new responsibilities

  • Assistance with implementing local and national activities such as image campaigns, seasonal refits, promotions and sales promotion measures
  • Active assistance with positioning the furniture range and merchandise displays
  • Using suitable materials, create sources of inspiration for an inviting and beautiful home
  • Create focal points in the display by applying the specified visual merchandising principles

Requirements

  • Completed training as a Polydesigner 3D EFZ
  • A strong sense of style and understanding of interior design
  • You enjoy pitching in, have manual aptitude and physical stamina
  • Creative flair, spatial imagination and a talent for commercial presentation
  • Reliable when it comes to working hours
  • Professional experience as a Polydesigner 3D in the furniture and decoration industry
