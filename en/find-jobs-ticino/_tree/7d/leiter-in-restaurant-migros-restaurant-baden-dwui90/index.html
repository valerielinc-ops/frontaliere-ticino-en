Full description

You run a lively catering business with heart and entrepreneurial thinking. Together with your team, you provide enthusiastic guests, a smooth process and high quality standards. You take on responsibility for employees, key figures and operational operations and take on where you are needed.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Restaurant, Migros Restaurant Baden City, Bahnhofstrasse 42, 5400 Baden

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You take over the operational management of the company and ensure a smooth process and a special guest experience in all areas
  • You lead, motivate and develop your team, create rosters, organize training, and are responsible for employee development and assessment.
  • You support the implementation of the annual goals and keep an eye on sales, budget, personnel and goods costs at all times to ensure an economical operation
  • You are responsible for the presentation of goods, ensure availability and take care of orders and inventories
  • You actively advise and supervise guests, are the first point of contact for guests and employees and ensure high customer satisfaction
  • You ensure compliance with our quality, hygiene and safety standards and ensure the organisation and maintenance of the company
  • You work closely with the team and get involved in day-to-day operations where support is needed

Requirements

  • At least 3 years: Leadership experience in a similar function
  • Completed basic or further education: as a chef EFZ and completed hotel school or business education advantageous
  • You understand business contexts and deal safely with key figures, costs, budgets and processes
  • You inspire as a warm host personality, take action yourself, motivate your team and bring joy and passion for gastronomy and cooking
  • You work independently, keep track even in demanding situations and think networked
  • You lead clearly, openly and appreciatively and create an environment where cooperation and trust are lived.
  • You promote and develop employees, strengthen the team spirit and celebrate joint successes
  • You attach great importance to service and quality standards and are happy to take responsibility for the company, your team and enthusiastic guests
  • in German (negotiation confident)

Cosa of friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks maternity leave and 4 weeks paternity leave
  • Occupational pension fund guarantees the financial security of Migros employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points in all Migros branches and various subsidiaries
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation in courses of Migros Club School
  • Sports and fitness offers: Discount on fitness subscriptions
