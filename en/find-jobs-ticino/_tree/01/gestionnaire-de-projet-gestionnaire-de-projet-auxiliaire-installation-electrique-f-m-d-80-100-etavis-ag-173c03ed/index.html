Full description

Are you looking for a new professional challenge where you can act independently and responsibly? Do you have a great passion for managing electrical projects? Do you enjoy coordinating a young team of professionals and achieving ambitious goals? If so, this could be the job you are looking for.

What you do

  • You plan and accompany projects in the high- and low-voltage sector from conception to acceptance.
  • In direct contact with customers, you will develop solutions that meet their needs and develop customer-specific offers.
  • You plan and optimize the use of resources, lead project teams and coordinate services with all project participants.
  • By expanding the customer base, you generate follow-up or additional orders.

This will make it easier for you to get started

  • After completing your basic training as an electrician EFZ, you will have gained several years of professional experience and ideally continue your education (eidg. dipl. Elektrofitateur:in, Elektro-Projektleiter:in mit eidg. FA or similar).
  • You have already led teams and fit effortlessly into an existing team.
  • Open and honest communication is important to you, and you express yourself confidently in German, both orally and in writing.
  • For construction site visits, you are in possession of a category B driver's license.

What to expect

  • We are successful – you can make a big difference with us.
  • We share in our success – with employee shares and bonuses.
  • We combine the possibilities of a large global company with the advantages of an SME.
  • Competent managers support you in achieving your goals and ensure professional conditions.
  • You have career opportunities with us – we are happy to support you.
  • You are important to us - we offer you an open, appreciative and familiar environment.
