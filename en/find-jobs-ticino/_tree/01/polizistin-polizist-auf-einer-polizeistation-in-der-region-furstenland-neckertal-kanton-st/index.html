Full description

The cantonal police of St.Gallen ensures peace, security and order in the canton of St.Gallen around the clock. It investigates crimes and monitors compliance with applicable laws. When working at a police station, you receive facts (ads) and make varied and exciting assignments and stand by the citizen with advice.

What to expect

  • As an employee of a police station, you pursue a varied, exciting and responsible work
  • They provide peace, order and security and prevent crimes through visible presence
  • They maintain contacts with citizens, offices and partner organisations as well as with the local administration
  • Their tasks include punishing violations of the law, independently recording, processing and detecting crimes and searches for perpetrators.
  • Comprehensive written reporting rounds off this activity

What sets it apart

  • You have the Federal Professional Card Police Officer
  • For this area of activity, we are looking for motivated, flexible and resilient employees who work independently and are responsible and capable of teamwork.
  • They are open and friendly to other cultures and people
  • In difficult situations, keep a cool head and have expertise in general policing
  • They provide night, weekend and special services within the scope of service operations.

What we offer

  • An exciting and challenging job in a dynamic team
  • Training opportunities
  • State-of-the-art work devices such as laptop and mobile phone are provided to you
  • Mobile work (work anywhere)
  • Flexible working models with 5-10 additional Fridays and flexible working hours
