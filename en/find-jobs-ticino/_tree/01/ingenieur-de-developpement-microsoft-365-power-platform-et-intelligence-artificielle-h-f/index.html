Your Mission

As part of the missions of the Directorate of Information and Digital Systems (DIRSIN), participate in the design, development and evolution of digital solutions based on the Microsoft 365 ecosystem. Contribute to the digitization and automation of business processes through Power Platform, Microsoft 365, Azure and artificial intelligence technologies, while respecting the standards of security, governance and quality in the General Hospice. Your main activities

  • Design, develop and maintain business solutions based on Microsoft 365, Power Platform and Azure;
  • Develop applications with Power Apps (Canvas Apps and Model-Driven Apps);
  • Automate business processes with Power Automation;
  • Design and integrate conversational agents and digital assistants with Microsoft Copilot Studio;
  • Develop solutions that enhance the capabilities of artificial intelligence in line with the organisation's governance framework;
  • Develop integrations with Microsoft Graph, REST APIs and third-party systems;
  • Develop and maintain SharePoint Framework (SPFx) components;
  • Design and operate Dataverse data models;
  • Produce dashboards and indicators with Power BI;
  • Participate in the implementation of documentary and collaborative management solutions under Microsoft 365;
  • Provide corrective and scalable maintenance of existing solutions;
  • Participate in the definition of technical standards, good practices and governance of Microsoft platforms;
  • Provide expert technical support and contribute to the transfer of knowledge to internal teams.
