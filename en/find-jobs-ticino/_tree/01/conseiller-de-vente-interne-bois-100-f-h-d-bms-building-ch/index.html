Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire with great commitment construction customers and private customers on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

To strengthen our Aigle-based team, we are looking for a wood professional, who already has an experience in selling ideally or who wants to develop in this field from 1 January 2027 as

Missions

  • Reference person for wood products internally and with our customers
  • Advise clients and provide irreproachable customer service
  • Establish offers and orders in the field of wood
  • Monitoring offers related to suppliers and customers
  • Relaunch and systematically track orders, offers and business
  • Negotiate prices and margins with our suppliers
  • Systematically monitor sales prices and conditions, correct them if necessary (e.g. special prices)
  • Ensuring versatility within the team
  • Timely participation in internal and external events
  • Work closely with representatives and various outlets
  • Participate in inventories

Your Profile

  • Holder of an SWC of a profession related to the Wood sector with predispositions to sale and in administrative tasks or
  • Holder of a trade/sales CFC and possess good knowledge in the field of wood (necessary)
  • Strong customer orientation and able to manage multiple tasks at the same time thanks to excellent priority management
  • German mastery would be appreciated
  • Mastering common IT tools (ERP one plus)
  • Dynamic and motivated person, have ease in contacts

We offer

  • A challenge within a changing company
  • A comprehensive integration programme; our new colleagues also benefit from in-house training on products and systems
  • Support for the professional and personal development of our employees
  • Collaborating discounts, various discounts such as fleet discounts, discounts in selected fitness centres and numerous offers to promote their mental and physical health
  • An attractive wage package combined with a solid pension fund, with flexible savings contributions and above average employer share
  • 5 weeks of holidays (6 weeks from 50 years) and the possibility of buying up to 10 additional days of vacation, as well as additional benefits such as a highly funded autonomous pension fund and discounts on our entire product range as well as external premium benefits

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
