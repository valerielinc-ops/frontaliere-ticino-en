Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. Various activities are bundled in our Corporate Functions division. In addition to our finance and IT services, this area also includes global marketing and sales management as well as human resources. Every day fresh ideas are created here, which together we lead to success. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You support the development of retail materials and tools that enable an optimal product presentation in the store – for example displays, product stands, sampling tools, counter elements or seasonal VM elements. You will accompany the entire process from needs analysis to the creation of briefings and close cooperation with internal stakeholders and external agencies.
  • You will accompany seasonal campaigns end-to-end: You will support with demand planning, coordinate purchasing and delivery to the various markets and prepare the input for the campaign manual, the seasonal Instore Guidelines
  • You analyze past orders and consumption data, derive insights from them and help to continuously improve demand planning and avoid overstock and bottlenecks
  • You maintain and manage item data in our system, implement uniform naming conventions and ensure that our stores worldwide can order the right non-food items and tools at any time.
  • You document processes in the form of clear instructions and handy templates and thus contribute to ensuring that processes are traceable, scalable and easy to use for everyone involved

You bring that with you

  • You have a degree in marketing, business administration or a comparable field and are enthusiastic about product management. A parallel Master's degree does not stop you from applying
  • You have already gained your first professional experience in marketing, retail or project management and have a good feeling for brand-appropriate and commercially effective implementations
  • You distinguish yourself through strong organizational and analytical skills as well as a high level of detail orientation and control several topics in a structured and reliable manner in parallel.
  • You have fluent German and English skills in words and writing and enjoy working in an international environment
  • You bring your own initiative, reliability and a hands-on mentality and have the motivation to actively shape and consistently implement things

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Free parking
  • Discounts in Läderach Café and staff restaurant

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Fabio Ferramosca, Talent Acquisition Partner, is at your disposal for any questions.
