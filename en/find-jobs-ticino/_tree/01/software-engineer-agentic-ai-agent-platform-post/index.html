Full description

Do you think of AI agents in terms of identities, permissions and telemetry rather than demos? Do you want to build the platform that enables Swiss Post developers to work with AI agents safely, traceably and at a reasonable price? Then you might just be the right person for us. Swiss Post IT is changing the way software is developed: AI agents take on a growing part of the work while people set the goals, check the results and control the processes. Together with you, we build an enterprise-grade platform for AI agents and scale it from pilot teams to our DevOps organization.

You can do that.

  • You build the platform on which our AI agents run, from the agent runtime environment and AI and MCP gateways to identity and access management for people and agents. Where Post already has suitable building blocks, you integrate them instead of developing parallel solutions.
  • In addition, you implement guidelines, guidelines and governance rules in code and enforce them centrally – from data protection to agents’ access rights.
  • You set up cost controls, quotas and alerts so that use remains transparent and budgets are adhered to.
  • You also define and automate metrics, baselines and telemetry, so we can show what changes when agents deliver, not just how often they are used.
  • You provide proven Large Language Models and connect the platform to our existing SDLC tools.
  • In addition, you ensure that the platform works reliably and verifiably, so that we can understand which agent has done what on whose behalf.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of practical experience in software or platform development, for example with Go, Python, Java or TypeScript, and have solid experience with Infrastructure as Code and CI/CD pipelines such as GitHub Actions.
  • You also have experience with APIs, identity and access management (OAuth/OIDC) and container or cloud platforms such as Kubernetes or AWS.
  • You have a strong understanding of security for platforms running AI agents, including secret management, permissions, network access, input and output controls, and privacy.
  • In addition, you understand how AI agents work technically – from model APIs to tool calls to MCP – and therefore know what requirements a corresponding platform must meet.
  • You have experience in the pragmatic use of GenAI tools in practical software development. Knowledge in areas such as AI and MCP gateways, prompt firewalls, Policy as Code, FinOps, OpenTelemetry, Backstage and DORA metrics are beneficial, but can also be acquired from us.
  • You have very good oral and written English skills. Knowledge of a national language is an advantage.

You don’t have to meet every single criterion. If you’ve built and operated platforms that others rely on and are really interested in AI agents, we’d love to hear from you. It is more important to us how you think and how you learn than for your profile to match perfectly with all keywords in the job description.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
