Full description

Quality begins in the laboratory. With your expertise and attention to detail, you ensure that our dairy products meet the highest quality and safety standards. Together with your team, you make an important contribution so that our consumers can rely on Emmi every day.

You can do that.

  • Together in our team, you are responsible for carrying out microbiological, chemical and physical analyses of raw materials, semi-finished products and finished products such as milk, whey, cream cheese and milk powder.
  • Carrying out PCR analyses is also part of your job.
  • You support in the functional testing of the laboratory equipment and participate in ring tests.
  • You can process the daily sample input independently, controlling the sample status and capturing the samples in the system.
  • All this is done in compliance with work instructions, laboratory specifications and hygiene and safety regulations.
  • Your thoughtful, forward-thinking and analytical nature allows you to participate continuously in the improvement process of the laboratory.

Why do you succeed?

  • You have a completed vocational apprenticeship as a biology or chemical laboratory expert, at best with practical expertise in dairy laboratory analysis
  • You bring a safe handling of MS Office and at best SAP.
  • You are characterized by a very high sense of responsibility and quality with an independent and careful working method.
  • In this position, you will also be helped by an analytical and structured way of thinking, organizational and communication skills.
  • With your high willingness and flexibility, you support us with weekend and holiday assignments.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Sabbatical & Holiday Shopping: Give yourself unpaid holidays or extend your holiday entitlement by up to two additional weeks.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Individual development plan: With tailor-made development plans, we specifically promote your individual skills and support your long-term growth – a central component of our sustainability strategy. Learn more .
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed.
