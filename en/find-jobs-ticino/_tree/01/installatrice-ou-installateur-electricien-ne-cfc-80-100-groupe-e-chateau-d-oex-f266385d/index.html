Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our clients realize their projects by providing global, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether for the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. An exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2,250 employees, including 200 apprentices, as well as the balance between private and professional life.

A local player, Groupe E Tech, thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of installations, electrical panels and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as the maintenance of installations.

Mission

  • Carry out work related to multi-technical electrical installations, you master reading plans and electrical installation diagrams
  • Install, connect and modify electrical panels, various electrical devices, communication, multimedia, security, control systems, etc.
  • Participate in the various commissionings
  • Also carry out the laying of channels, the pulling of wires and cables
  • Establish footage and management vouchers
  • Participate in the training of our apprentices

Profile

  • CFC installer-electrician
  • Proven experience in the field of general electrical installations
  • Organized and autonomous, you have a sense of anticipation and responsibility
  • You are recognized as an open-minded, flexible person
  • You enjoy working in a team with a strong team spirit
  • You master French

What we offer you

  • An evolving environment favorable to balance between professional and private life.
  • The social benefits of a modern company, fair treatment, professional development opportunities.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: “Together, we move towards success”
