Lonza is today a world leader in life sciences, operating on three continents. We work in science, but there is no magic formula for how we approach work. Our most important scientific solution is talented employees who collaborate and develop ideas that help other companies to help people. In return, our employees steer their career independently. Because their ideas, big and small, improve the world. And that's the kind of work we want to be part of.

For the Visp site, we are looking for a QC monitoring sampler for routine monitoring.

What we offer you

  • An Agile Career and Dynamic Work Culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • In addition to competitive salaries, you will find a wide range of lifestyle, family and leisure benefits. Depending on location, these benefits may include family allowances, childcare assistance, discounts for local businesses and attractions, travel allowances, and subsidized meals.

Their tasks

  • Implementation of quality monitoring in clean rooms (gowning required) as well as monitoring of ultra-clean media systems such as WFI (water for injection) and process gases.
  • Responsible supervision of the assigned production facilities and independent organization of the sampling in your area.
  • Transfer of samples to quality control (QC).
  • Alternating allocation to different production facilities at a fixed rhythm, ensuring variety and diverse insights.
  • Close coordination and cooperation in the team, as responsibilities change regularly.
  • Working in a day job.

Your requirement profile

  • Completed professional training.
  • Safe handling of PC and standard software.
  • Reliable and conscientious working methods as well as a high willingness to learn and a sense of responsibility.
  • Solution-oriented, structured and independent working method.
  • Flowing knowledge of German in words and writing.

Success for us means morally acceptable progress. Lonza is joined by people who take on challenges and use their creativity to develop new ideas for complex problems. Together we make a contribution that can improve the lives of many people around the world. This offers them the certainty and satisfaction of really making a difference with their own work.
