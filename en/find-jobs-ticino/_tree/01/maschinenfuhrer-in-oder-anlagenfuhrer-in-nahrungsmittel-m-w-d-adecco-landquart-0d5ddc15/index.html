Full description

Introduction

For an established company in the food industry, we are looking for a reliable and motivated personality as a machine operator or plant operator. You take over the operation and monitoring of modern production facilities and together with the team ensure a smooth production process and compliance with the highest quality standards.

Tasks

  • Operating, setting up and monitoring production and packaging facilities
  • Ensuring a trouble-free production process
  • Resolve minor failures and perform simple maintenance
  • Control of product quality according to internal specifications
  • Compliance with hygiene, safety and quality guidelines
  • Documentation of production data and workflows
  • Cooperation in cleaning and conversion work on the plants

Profile

  • Completed training as a plant operator, machine operator or comparable technical professional experience
  • Experience in food production is beneficial
  • Technical understanding and pleasure in dealing with machines
  • Self-employed, responsible and careful work
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Team capable and resilient personality

Advantages

  • Long-term employment option with permanent employment option
  • Modern production environment and structured familiarisation
  • Varied activity with personal responsibility
  • Collegial working environment and short decision paths
  • Current employment conditions
