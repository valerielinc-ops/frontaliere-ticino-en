Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges ahead, HIB is looking for:

80% cooking aid

Your Mission

  • Ensure cleaning, maintenance and storage of dishes, equipment and premises.
  • Participate in the food distribution chain.
  • Occasionally and according to needs, participate in the setting (vegetations, fruits, salads, desserts, snacks...).
  • Support and collaborate with the brigade.

Your assets

A first experience in cooking is an asset. You are motivated to engage in community cooking and have excellent physical condition. Working independently and flexibly, you have an excellent team spirit. Dynamic and helpful, you perform your tasks with care and speed. You collaborate and communicate without difficulty in French within a brigade and work in accordance with hygiene standards.

Entry into office: 1 September 2026 or to be agreed
