Full description

Your team

The Operations Division covers the processing and monitoring of all types of financial instruments and administers investment funds. The division supports the Group's other businesses and provides customers with a high quality of service, building on modern and robust infrastructure. You will join the Reconciliation department within the team specializing in Cash Reconciliation, in charge of monitoring and monitoring cash operations carried out by all our business lines and entities, both in Switzerland and internationally.

Your role

  • Perform daily reconciliation of non-automated cash transactions, ensuring the reliability and accuracy of reconciliations.
  • Identify, analyse and assign the suspense to the units concerned, and follow up until complete resolution.
  • Address anomalies, deviations and incidents, ensuring their timely and sustainable resolution.
  • Develop, update and maintain documentation on procedures and internal controls to ensure compliance and traceability of operations.
  • Serve as a point of contact with business lines, internal correspondents and entities abroad, promoting smooth and efficient communication.

Your profile

  • SWC, professional maturity or higher education, with 3 to 5 years of experience in banking.
  • Interest in figures and data analysis.
  • Very good mastery of computer tools, including MS Office (Excel, Word, Outlook), as well as ease in handling databases.
  • Excellent analytical capabilities, rigor, organization and attention to detail.
  • Team spirit, sense of service, flexibility and ability to evolve in a demanding environment.
  • Excellent mastery of French and very good knowledge of English, both oral and written.
  • Residence in Switzerland required.
