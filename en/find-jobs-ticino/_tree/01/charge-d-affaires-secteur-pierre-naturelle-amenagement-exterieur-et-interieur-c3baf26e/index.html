Responsible for business sector \- natural \- exterior and interior development \- Geneva / Vaud M/F

The Swiss company Imporphyre SA of the CAPRI group, active in the trade of natural stone, marble, and high-end surfaces for interior and exterior design.

For 60 years, we have been accompanying architects, decorators, general contractors and contractors on exceptional projects.

Our know-how is expressed through a careful work of marble, quartzite in open book or continuous vein.

Want to know more?:

As part of our development and growth, we recruit a sector business officer for the sector Geneva / Vaud

You take responsibility for the commercial development of a strategic sector in the Canton of Geneva and in the Canton of Vaud.

Your role is clear

  • Developing turnover
  • Conquer new customers
  • Bring CAPRI to the field

Your missions

Development \&conquest

  • Leading the development of an existing client portfolio while opening new accounts
  • Actively prospect for new professional building and fit-up clients
  • Identify projects upstream: construction projects, future programmes, local opportunities

Relation \&value added

  • Create a relationship of trust with your customers
  • Highlight our materials, stocks and field expertise
  • Position CAPRI as a reliable and committed partner, not just as a supplier
  • Follow your customers until the realization

Organization \&pilot

  • Define your sector business strategy
  • Organize your tours and activities independently
  • Follow your commercial pipeline via CRM
  • Upgrading a precise field vision to feed trade orientations
  • You own your sector, your dynamics and your performance.

What we expect

You are made for this position if

  • Minimum 3–5 years experience as an external commercial in the high-end building, layout, carpentry design, stone or surfaces
  • You like to build your business and see your results progress
  • You are resilient and able to keep energy despite refusals
  • You are autonomous, organized, rigorous and field oriented
  • Knowledge of sales cycles related to construction and development projects
  • Compulsory driving licence

We offer

  • Real autonomy in the sector
  • Single product expertise on the market
  • An existing client portfolio with high potential
  • A CDI with an attractive remuneration package (fixed monthly variable deflated \- Function vehicle)
  • Integration path and in-service training
  • 5 weeks holiday with free weekends
