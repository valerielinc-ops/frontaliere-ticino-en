Xundheitszentrum is a competent and reliable partner for your health and is part of a network of family practices established throughout Switzerland under the auspices of Swiss Medical Network AG. In Grindelwald, in the middle of the beautiful Bernese Oberland, we offer comprehensive medical care in our Xundheitzentrum for patients of all ages and from all over the world.

Our motivated and experienced team ensures basic medical care, participates in the regional emergency service and also focuses on the support of chronically ill and elderly people at home and in the retirement home. To supplement the medical team, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement: Specialist in General Internal Medicine 80-100 % The job profile combines family practice with a versatile area of responsibility in emergency medicine. On request, employment can also be temporary for the winter season
