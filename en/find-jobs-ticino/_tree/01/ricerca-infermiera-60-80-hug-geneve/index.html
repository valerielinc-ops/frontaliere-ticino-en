Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Geneva Translational Research in Infections and Vaccination (GeTRIV) is a joint project of the Faculty of Medicine and HUG, led by the Centre for Emerging Viral Diseases (CMVE) and Vaccinationology and the HIV Research Unit. Its objective is to promote translational science in the field of immunovirology and vaccineology, by providing advanced laboratories, biobank facilities and shared offices and meeting spaces for clinicians and fundamental researchers to link basic research to clinical applications. GETRIV recruits a clinical research nurse to work with its team on all projects within GETRIV.

Description of post

You are involved in studies at the GETRIV. You work mainly in the Tulip Building (64, avenue de la Roseraie). Home visits or removals from other hospitals in the canton of Geneva are also possible. As part of the studies, you participate in identifying eligible participants and check the inclusion criteria in collaboration with the study investigators. You apply existing protocols, participate in meetings, use follow-up tools and contribute to the training of the various stakeholders involved in the course of participants. You welcome, support and accompany the participants in the various studies. You ensure that they understand the information transmitted and participate in completing it if necessary. You organize, plan and carry out the various visits planned by the study, including inclusion, follow-up and completion visits. You perform the paramedics required by the protocol, and coordinate the transfer of samples with the laboratories to the GETRIV and the HUG. You also guarantee the traceability of the different stages in accordance with the requirements of the protocol. Finally, you enter the data into the study databases, verify its accuracy and consistency, and ensure the management of the documentation and treatment related to the study, in collaboration with the GETRIV team. You work mainly in regular weekdays. Work at weekends or evenings/nights can be considered on an ad hoc basis, according to the arrangements to be agreed, with a recovery during the week, according to the needs of the different studies.

Qualifications

You have a Bachelor's degree in nursing or an equivalent qualification recognised and registered by the Swiss Red Cross, and you have mastered computer tools as well as computerised patient records (DPI). You have a very good sense of organization and are comfortable in an environment that requires cross-sectional work and collaboration with different professionals. Theoretical and practical knowledge in the field of clinical research is an asset. A good command of English, both oral and written, is also a plus.
