Full description

You get data to flow and keep track of complex data structures? As a data engineer, you will continue to develop our data platform, connect systems and ensure stable and reliable data flows. This lays the technical foundation so that Emmi can fully exploit the potential of his data.

You can do that.

  • 🚀 You will continue to develop our data platform and build reliable ETL pipelines and interfaces with dbt, Fabric and Azure DevOps.
  • 🧩 You bring structure to our data and develop data warehouse models that create a strong basis for analytics and data-based decisions.
  • Happiness You understand the requirements of our departments and turn them into clever and technically sustainable data solutions.
  • 🔎 You keep an eye on our data analytics platform, analyze errors and work with the team to ensure that our data flows are stable.
  • ✅ With tests and clean documentation, you ensure the quality of our interfaces, integrations and data processing.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
