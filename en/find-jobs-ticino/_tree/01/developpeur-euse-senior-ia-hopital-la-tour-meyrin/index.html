Full description

BUILD CONCRETE SOLUTIONS FOR TOUR HOSPITAL

Do you want to design useful, secure and directly integrated AI agents and applications for healthcare and administrative teams?

Located in Meyrin, La Tour Hospital is a private and independent acute care facility with 24-hour emergency facilities covering most medical and surgical specialties.

Within the Directorate of Digital and Organizational Transformation, AI is a central lever for transformation. The aim is not to multiply the proof of concept, but to develop and produce reliable, useful and fully integrated solutions to hospital practices.

In this context, we are recruiting

Developer Senior IA (ref: 2071)

able to design custom-made business agents and applications, integrating d

Your mission

At the interface between technology, trades and the hospital environment, you take charge of the development, industrialization and evolution of high value-added AI solutions. You transform business needs into secure, sustainable and deployable applications across the institution.

Development of agents and applications

  • Design and develop AI agents and services in Python, including with Microsoft Agent Framework or LangGraph.
  • Develop custom-made business applications, web and API, integrating d
  • Exhibit services via REST or MCP APIs and integrate them into the hospital ecosystem: ERP, patient file, HL7/FHIR feed, business applications, Power Platform and Azure.
  • Participate in workshops with project leaders and business experts to identify use cases and translate needs into technical specifications.
  • Write technical and functional documentation and contribute to testing, validation and deployment phases.

Industrialization and exploitation

  • Contain and deploy solutions with Docker, Kubernetes on-premise or AKS, using CI/CD Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions pipelines.
  • Implement Infrastructure as Code with Bicep or Terraform.
  • Organize continuous evaluation of agents: testing sets, quality metrics, regression monitoring and hallucination detection.
  • Ensure compliance with solutions by tracking traces, logs, costs and performance.
  • Ensure support, maintenance and evolution of production solutions.

Architecture, security and governance

  • Contribute to the choice of architecture and models, cloud or open source, according to criteria of quality, cost, performance and confidentiality.
  • Apply the development, security and governance standards of agents: safeguards, human validation, access management and protection against quick injection.
  • Ensure health data protection and accommodation requirements in Switzerland.

Why join this project?

  • Develop direct integrated AI solutions for daily hospital use.
  • Intervention throughout the life cycle, from professional framing to production.
  • Work with healthcare, administrative and digital teams in a stimulating and concrete environment.
  • Contribute to the institution's technological choices, architecture and governance IA.
  • Join a private, independent, human-scale institution able to decide and experiment quickly.

We are looking for a profile that meets the following criteria:

  • University degree or HES, Bachelor or Master, in computer science, engineering or related field.
  • Mastery of Python and TypeScript, knowledge of C#/.NET is an asset
  • At least five years of experience in software development, including experience in the production of a generic AI solution.
  • Mastering of the development of agent: tool calls, multi-agent orchestration via workflows, creation and exploitation of skills.
  • Experience in developing full-stack web applications integrating AI and advanced practice of AI-assisted development tools.
  • Experience of containers, IC/CD, Infrastructure as Code and good knowledge of Azure, including Azure AI Foundry, Azure OpenAI, AKS and Functions.
  • Knowledge of Power Platform, Dataverse, HL7 or FHIR is an asset.
  • Context management and optimization of tokens, with an impact on response quality, latency and cost is an asset
  • Experience in RAG architectures and vector research: indexing, document cutting is an asset.
  • Sensitivity to application security, confidentiality and data protection.
  • Ability to popularize complex topics, to dialogue with non-technical interlocutors and to accompany business teams.
  • Current French and English level B2 minimum.
