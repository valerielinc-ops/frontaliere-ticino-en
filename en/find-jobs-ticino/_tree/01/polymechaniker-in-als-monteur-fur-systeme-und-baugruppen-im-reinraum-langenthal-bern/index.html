Full description

You put technical systems into operation independently and carefully adjust measuring systems. The operation allows you to actively participate in product optimization. To strengthen our customer, we are looking for one or more activities

  • assemble series products assemblies and prototypes independently
  • actively participate in the optimization of assembled products
  • adjust measuring systems and sensors and document measurements
  • put series products and drive systems into operation
  • repair mechanical and simple electrotechnical faults
  • Requirements
  • Teaching as a polymechanic EFZ or automatic machine EFZ completed
  • Multi-year practice in the assembly and commissioning of customer-specific systems
  • Very good technical understanding
  • Very good computer knowledge
  • Very good oral and written German language skills Offer
  • 40 hours week with annual working time model
  • 25 to 30 days of holiday by age
