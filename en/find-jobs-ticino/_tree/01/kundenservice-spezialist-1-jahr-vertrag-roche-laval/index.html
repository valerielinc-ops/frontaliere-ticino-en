Opportunity

As a customer service expert, you will develop strong trust relationships based on your understanding of your accounts and your ability to provide a positive procurement experience. By proactively identifying the needs of clients and introducing references to appropriate team members, you will help improve and customize the overall customer experience offered in the organization. Your strong leadership and influence skills will allow you to challenge the status quo and seek effectiveness by providing recommendations for reliable and consistent service.

  • Manage reserved batch solutions for all clients across Canada in accordance with Roche's policies and procedures, while working closely with the supply chain planning team to ensure data accuracy and effective control flow.
  • Provide analysis and direct communication with clients for their reserved batches, and communicate their requirements to the planning team.
  • Manage all specialty accounts requiring special attention and with logistical exceptions, while maintaining and managing "Automatic Orders" for all our accounts and territories to ensure a high level of accuracy.
  • Process appeals, provide assistance, understand logistics complaints and provide satisfactory solutions.
  • Collaborate with regional brigades on key accounts to provide an unparalleled supply experience.
  • Identify and propose improvements or optimizations of manual processes and tasks without added value of customer service.
  • Make adjustments to invoices (credits/debits), manage return requests, analyze daily reports, and proactively communicate delays or changes to clients.
