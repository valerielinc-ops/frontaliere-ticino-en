Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society.

About the role

We are responsible for installing, configuring, calibrating and repairing products and technical systems for our customers. This operation carries out a variety of handling tasks and technical support and can provide training locally to provide customer support. This cargo profile is intended for heavy duty work that follows the procedures established and processed under close supervision. Decisões menores/mais faceis são tomadas de forma autônoma. Main Responsibilities:

  • Identify the plants on the Sulzer equipment and inform them of the locations of the plants and the Sulzer-responsible stores;
  • Installation and removal of rotary equipment in offshore camps and platforms;
  • Start-up installation of selagem systems;
  • Alinhamento between motor and bomb;
  • Installation and disassembly of fittings for thermal expansion, fittings for hydraulic expansion;
  • Adjusts the gap between the machine and the machine, shown by gaskets;
  • Assembly/disassembly of mechanical parts, patented metal radiators and bearings, axles and blade adjustments;
  • Centralization of rotary conjunto;
  • Adjusts and mounts conical pins;
  • Basic operation of flushing machines with lubrication units, valves and small door actuators among other relevant cargo activities.

Necessary Skills

Ténico Mecânico Completo

  • Practical and technical understandings of rolling bearings, mechanical adjustment by folga and interference
  • Basic operation of operating machines, metrology and use of medical instruments such as meter, external and internal micrometer, tubular micrometer, tube, comparator and measuring instrument;
  • Conhecimento em controle dimensional com emissão de relatório;
  • Leitura and interpretation of mechanical engineering design;
  • Basic information in office packages for emissary of reports;
  • Understanding of docking, lifting and transporting of cargo;
  • Basic bomb instructions.
