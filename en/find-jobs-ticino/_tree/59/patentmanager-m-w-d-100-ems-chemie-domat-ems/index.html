Full description

What to expect

  • Implementation, defence and enforcement of intellectual property
  • Defense of claims from foreign intellectual property
  • Contact person within the EMS Group for all patent- and trademark-related matters
  • Performing patent and literature searches, in particular for determining the state of the art in the case of new applications or oppositions, regular monitoring of third-party rights and freedom-to-operate investigations
  • Examining and drawing up contracts in cooperation with the Legal Service

What do you bring with you

  • University studies University/Technical College/Technical College of chemistry, polymer chemistry, materials science, chemical engineering, plastics engineering
  • Good knowledge in the field of polymer chemistry
  • Experience in the field of patents is advantageous, also patent attorney candidates in training or patent attorneys
  • Performance-oriented, innovative, communicative, structured, thorough and flexible
  • Good knowledge of German and English

You can look forward to that.

  • Working in the middle of beautiful natural and recreational areas
  • Exciting tasks in an international environment with short decision paths
  • Lots of personal responsibility and attractive management positions
  • Market-oriented remuneration, progressive social benefits and own pension fund with good benefits
  • Staff restaurant, free parking

About us

EMS has 25 production sites in 16 countries, employs around 3,000 people worldwide and, with around 140 apprentices, is the largest vocational trainer in the canton and winner of the National Education Award.

EMS-GRIVORY is part of the EMS Group’s high-performance polymers business unit and employs more than 1000 people worldwide. The largest development and production centre is located in Domat/Ems in Switzerland. Further production facilities are located in Germany, China, Taiwan and the USA.
