Laboratory Engineer Robotics and Simulation (m/f/d)

100%, Zurich, temporary

Connect high-performance simulation with real robots – for the production of tomorrow!

In the Advanced Manufacturing Lab (am|z) of ETH Zurich, we are exploring the production of the future – at the interface of robotics, physical AI, high-performance computing and manufacturing technology. Our open, interdisciplinary team of about 25 colleagues combines pioneering spirit with the most modern infrastructure. As of January 1, 2027, we are looking for you as a laboratory engineer: in (100%): a technology-enthusiastic personality who builds, operates and further develops our HPC/GPU-based simulation and robotics workflows – thus bridging the gap between numerical process simulation and real robotic systems.

The employment is initially on a temporary basis, with the possibility of a transfer to a permanent employment relationship.

Create the bridge between high-performance simulation and real robotics with us – we look forward to your application!

Job description

In this key role, you will be responsible for the technical basis of our research – from our own simulation software to the HPC/GPU infrastructure to robot-assisted production tests in the laboratory.

Simulation & HPC

  • Development, maintenance and performance optimization of own numerical simulation software (SPH, MPM) for complex manufacturing processes – including large deformations, contact, material flow and non-linear material behavior as well as validation and benchmarking
  • Establishment, operation and further development of the HPC/GPU infrastructure: parallelization with CUDA, MPI and/or OpenMP, profiling and automation of large simulation campaigns on clusters (job scheduling, data management)
  • Transfer of real process, machine and material properties into numerical models, planning of measurement and validation strategies and participation in the construction of digital twins

Robotics & Physical AI

  • Integration of high-resolution process simulations in robotics and physical AI environments such as MuJoCo or Genesis as well as development of stable interfaces to robot models, sensors, control and learning algorithms
  • Construction and technical support of robot-assisted production tests: integration of robots, end effectors and force/moment sensors, implementation of trajectories and control concepts as well as coupling of simulation and experiment
  • Support for Physical AI workflows (e.g. Reinforcement Learning, Imitation Learning, Sim-to-Real) through efficient simulation and training pipelines

Support & laboratory infrastructure

  • Technical advice, training and support for researchers, doctoral candidates and students in simulation, HPC, robotics and experimental setups
  • Ensuring sustainable scientific software development (version management, testing, CI/CD, container, documentation, reproducibility)
  • Participation in the selection, procurement, commissioning and maintenance of computer, robotics, sensor and laboratory components

Profile

  • University degree (MSc or equivalent) in Mechanical Engineering, Computational Engineering, Robotics, Mechatronics, Computational Science or a related field
  • Several years of practical experience in numerical simulation and scientific software development (C++, Python)
  • In-depth experience with SPH and/or MPM and in Computational Mechanics
  • Very good knowledge of HPC/GPU computing (CUDA, MPI, OpenMP, SLURM)
  • Safe handling of Git, Docker and CI/CD workflows
  • Experience in robotics (kinematics and dynamics), control technology and trajectory planning as well as with sensors and robot hardware
  • Practical experience with ROS 2
  • Experience with manufacturing processes and their modeling
  • Experience with Physics Engines such as MuJoCo or Genesis as well as with Physical AI methods (e.g. Reinforcement Learning, Sim-to-Real)
  • Strong analytical and systematic thinking as well as high problem-solving competence
  • Independent, responsible and careful working with a focus on reproducible research and sustainable technical solutions
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation, knowledge exchange and technical support for researchers and students
  • Very good knowledge of English; our working languages are German and English
