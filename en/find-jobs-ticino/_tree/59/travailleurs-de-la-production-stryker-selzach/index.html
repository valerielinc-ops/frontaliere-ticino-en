Full description

Work Flexibility: Onsite

Do you want to work in a production environment where quality and precision are crucial? In this position, you will support our team in the reliable processing and testing of our products and help to achieve our quality and volume goals.

This is what awaits you

  • Carry out defined production processes such as washing and laser marking processes independently according to specified process and quality requirements.
  • Prepare machines, tools and workstations for the respective work steps and ensure professional use and cleaning.
  • Test products, materials and components using drawings, test reports and specified test equipment.
  • Use measuring equipment such as measuring projectors and measuring microscopes and evaluate test results according to the defined specifications.
  • Completely document production and test results and perform documentary checks according to applicable quality specifications.
  • Detect minor errors and deviations on machines, materials or products, perform simple error analyses and lock defective products according to specifications.
  • Support continuous improvement and lean initiatives by identifying problems and bringing forward concrete suggestions for improvement.
  • Help colleagues familiarize themselves with the activities of your task area and work according to the given priorities and team goals.

You bring that with you

Requirements

  • Completed three years of vocational training or Teaching or comparable qualification or professional experience in the relevant field of activity.
  • Professional experience in a comparable production, manufacturing or testing area.
  • Knowledge of German at least at B1 level.
  • Basic computer skills.
  • Experience in dealing with standard tools in the relevant work area.
  • Physical ability to work standing for long periods of time depending on the activity and to lift loads of up to 15 kg.

Benefits

  • Experience in the quality testing of products, materials or components on the basis of technical drawings and test reports.
  • Experience in using measuring projectors or measuring microscopes.
