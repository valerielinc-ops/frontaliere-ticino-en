Full description

Motivated by an apprenticeship in Production Mechanic? Ready to become a reference in your profession through quality training in a leading company? Join us!

The production mechanic manufactures parts of watch movement or dressing using traditional and digitally controlled machines. He/she interprets technical drawings, prepares tools and materials necessary for the manufacture of parts. He/she is responsible for setting and checking machines and is responsible for the quality of the parts he/she/it produces.

This learning is suitable for young people who are interested in metalworking, industrial machinery and who like precision.

Profile

  • Good spatial representation
  • Logical and methodical spirit
  • Manual skill and accuracy
