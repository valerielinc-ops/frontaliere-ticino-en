Tasks

You want more than classic office hours organization? In our interdisciplinary ambulatorium Pneumologie & Angiologie you can expect a versatile everyday work with direct patient contact, diagnostic examinations and organizational responsibility. With us, you combine medical competence with teamwork and actively contribute to high-quality patient care.

  • Organisation and coordination of pneumological and angiological consultation hours
  • Care of patients as well as scheduling and patient management
  • Conducting diagnostic examinations such as body plethysmography and oscillodoppler examinations
  • Support for specialists in consultation hours, examinations and interventions
  • Implementation of blood sampling and administration and organisational tasks
  • Varied activity with a combination of medical-practical and organizational tasks
  • Possibility to take responsibility and actively shape processes
  • Working in a team that lives an open team culture and supports each other

Requirements

  • Completed training as medical practice assistant / medical practice assistant EFZ or health specialist
  • Ideally, experience in the ambulatory environment
  • Structured and reliable working methods and organizational skills
  • High patient and service orientation
  • Open and appreciative communication
  • Rest and overview, even in a dynamic working day
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation

You don't bring everything? Apply anyway – personality, motivation and team fit are just as important to us as experience.
