You can look forward to that.

When it comes to SBB’s corporate safety, you make an important contribution to protecting people, plants and operations. In the Security division, you combine expertise with data and process analysis in the 2nd Line of Defence and develop effective measures with a focus on suicide prevention together with internal and external partners. You work across specialist and organizational boundaries, think strategically and accompany concrete changes from the idea to implementation. You can expect a responsible task with creative leeway in a committed environment in which you can develop professionally and your impact becomes visible. You can move that.

  • As an expert, you will take over the technical management of suicide prevention within SBB, be the contact person for a wide range of internal and external stakeholders and committees and coordinate concepts and measures until their implementation.
  • You analyze security-relevant data, identify developments and derive the need for action, from which you create a sound basis for decision-making.
  • With a sustainable network, you maintain cooperation with internal and external specialist departments, coordinate joint activities and represent your specialist topics effectively.
  • You lead projects and sub-projects in your area of expertise and participate in various Group security projects.
  • You develop processes, standards and key figures further, check the effectiveness of measures and derive improvements.
  • Professionals and managers advise, design and convey training content and promote knowledge transfer.

You bring that with you.

  • You are a solid, resilient and winning personality and tackle challenging strategic issues with the necessary sensitivity, evaluating people and situations quickly and differentiated.
  • With your strong negotiating skills, you win different stakeholder groups for joint solutions and you are happy to network within and outside the organization.
  • Structured, reliable and self-responsible, you work efficiently with modern IT tools, use AI skilfully and carry out tasks in a targeted manner and are happy to participate in different projects and security topics.
  • With in-depth expertise in suicide prevention and data analysis, you have and bring several years of professional experience in this field, knowledge of railway operations and legal knowledge from a police background are beneficial.
  • You have a university degree at FH or university level or an equivalent education and you have experience in conceptual work and implementation, professional and strategic questions lie with you.
  • You master German or French at level C2 and the other language at least at level B2, Italian and English are advantageous.
