Department of Education, Environment and Culture \| 50% \| Chur

Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the cantonal administration Graubünden together with us.

The Department of Education, Environment and Culture (DBUK) comprises five offices with a wide range of tasks. As a staff unit, the General Secretariat supports the management of the department and accompanies, among other things, legal and political affairs.

Clarification in legal matters! Do you not only want to analyze legal issues, but also process them into well-founded decisions? In the legal service of the DBUK, you will process a wide range of administrative cases and draft corresponding decisions in the department’s area of responsibility.

You expect varied legal tasks, space for independent work and a collegial team that supports each other and appreciates the professional exchange.

We search by 1\. February 2027 or by agreement a lawyer for the legal service.

Your responsibilities

  • Independent processing and instruction of administrative appeal proceedings in different legal fields until the drafting of the corresponding decisions
  • Advising and assisting departmental offices on legal matters
  • Handling administrative and criminal matters, especially in the areas of education, environment and culture
  • Participation in consultations on federal legislative procedures

That is what we want from you.

  • Completed law studies
  • Preferably work experience in our areas of law and enjoy working in our areas of law
  • Independent, structured and careful working method with a flair for practical solutions
  • Stylish written and oral expression in the German language
  • Team spirit, communication strength and pleasure in working with different contacts

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to help shape the future of the canton

General Secretariat DBUK Quaderstrasse 17 7001 Chur
