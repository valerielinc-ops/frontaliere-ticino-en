Bellinzona 80\-100% (m/f/d)

DB Cargo AG is the company of Deutsche Bahn AG that deals with freight transport. Leader in Europe in the rail transport of goods, aims decisively to increase its network internationally and today operates across the continent through subsidiaries. These include DB Cargo Schweiz, based in Glattbrugg, which contributes to the development of the network, both for service quality and for transported volumes.

Major tasks

  • Manage and support a team of about 20 engineers at Arbedo headquarters
  • Provide technical support to external personnel and ensure proper operation in railway stations and plants
  • Supporting employees in acquiring and developing the necessary knowledge and qualifications and achieving the objectives required
  • Specify periodic interviews and, if necessary, targeted interviews with the employees; carry out control courses
  • Ensure the suitability of engineers for their profiles
  • Keep staff skills updated in the competence management system

Secondary tasks

  • Contribute to the training of staff, practical instructions and controls, including safety of the railway
  • Select new drivers and take care of their integration in collaboration with Human Resources
  • To participate in the preparation of operational requirements and educational materials
  • Manage interventions during the picket service

Requirements

  • B category driver with driving licence in Switzerland and Italy and several years of experience in the railway sector
  • Proven experience in staff management and coordination
  • Propensity to train employees and be their reference point
  • Availability to assume responsibility and new assignments
  • Operational and decision-making autonomy, structured approach and strong organizational skills
  • Teamwork attitude with other industry managers
  • Italian mother tongue or equivalent knowledge (minimum level C1\)
  • Good knowledge of German written and spoken (minimum level B1\) or readiness to learn it

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
