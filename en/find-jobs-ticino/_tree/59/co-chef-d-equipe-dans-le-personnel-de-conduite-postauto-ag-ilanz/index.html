Full description

Do you want to actively contribute to the further development of PostBus and assume leadership responsibility in a modern co-management model? As co-team leader: in driving personnel at the Ilanz location, you and your co-leader shape the leadership and development of the driving personnel. You share the responsibility for a stable and high-quality driving service and share the management tasks in partnership and on an equal footing.

You can do that.

  • Together with your co-direction, you lead, motivate and develop the driving staff and ensure a positive and appreciative working environment.
  • Together with your co-management, you ensure that the personnel resources for the driving service are provided on time and in high quality.
  • You accompany employees throughout the entire HR lifecycle – from recruitment and introduction to individual development.
  • You keep an eye on relevant key figures such as absences, time balance and fluctuation and initiate optimization measures if necessary.
  • You deal professionally with complaints, irregularities and special operational situations.
  • You support the functioning of the operation on site and work closely with control center, personnel management, operational planning and fleet management.
  • In addition to your leadership role 50%, you take on driving services yourself, whether it is planned or to bridge bottlenecks.

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed vocational training (EFZ) and have a further education as a manager or manager with federal law. Completed a professional certificate or a comparable qualification – or you are ready to complete it in the near future.
  • Initial leadership experience is beneficial, but not a prerequisite – more important is your motivation to take responsibility and develop.
  • You enjoy working in a team and appreciate the exchange and cooperative partnership in the co-management model.
  • With your communication skills, you bring people together, engage in dialogue at eye level and find viable solutions even in difficult situations.
  • You are capable of conflict and always act professionally and sovereignly in negotiations and discussions with employees, superiors, representatives of PEKO and/or social partners.
  • You are an organizational talent and have the ability to keep an overview even in hectic situations.
  • You have a category D driving licence or are ready to obtain it.
  • You communicate very well orally and in writing in German and have good (oral) knowledge in a second national language.
