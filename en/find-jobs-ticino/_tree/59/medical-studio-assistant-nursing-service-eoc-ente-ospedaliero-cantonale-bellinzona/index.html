Medical Practice Assistant - Job Advertisement

EOC – Ente Ospedaliero Cantonale, Ticino’s multi-site hospital, is present with its institutes throughout the entire canton, for a total of 1,000 beds. The organization makes it possible to combine a local approach and an overarching perspective effectively, guaranteeing the population a comprehensive and local hospital offering, regardless of where the services are requested. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of its more than 6,500 employees and their focus on human relationships, EOC ensures high-quality healthcare by caring for more than 41,000 inpatients each year and providing more than 600,000 outpatient consultations. To complete our team at the Outpatient Clinic of the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona and Valli, Bellinzona site, we are looking for a Medical Practice Assistant (100%).

Profile

  • The person we are looking for holds a Federal Certificate of Proficiency as a medical practice assistant and has at least two years of experience in the healthcare sector;
  • has good computer skills and knowledge of the national languages.
  • The candidate will work closely with the doctors and, together with the interdisciplinary team, will handle the practical and administrative management of various disciplines;
  • has a natural aptitude for teamwork, excellent organizational and interpersonal skills, and considerable sensitivity when trying to understand the needs of service users.
  • Knows how to manage their time effectively and efficiently, focusing their efforts according to priorities.
  • Handles heavy workloads while remaining calm in stressful situations and does not shy away from personal responsibilities.
  • Believes, as we do, in the importance of placing the patient at the center of all our attention.
