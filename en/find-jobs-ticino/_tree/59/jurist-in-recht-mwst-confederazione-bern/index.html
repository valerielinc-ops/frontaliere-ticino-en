The Swiss Federal Tax Administration (ESTV) is the federal competence centre for tax issues. Thanks to VAT, direct federal tax, withholding tax and stamp duties, it is responsible for most of the federal revenue. Its services include the proper application and enforcement of tax law, the further development of taxation and participation in international tax issues.

The reconciliation of work and family life as well as the professional development opportunities are highly appreciated by the employees of ESTV.
