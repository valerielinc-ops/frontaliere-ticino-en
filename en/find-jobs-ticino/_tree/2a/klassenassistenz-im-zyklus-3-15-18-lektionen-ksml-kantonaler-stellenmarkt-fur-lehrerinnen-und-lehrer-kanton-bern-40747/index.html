Portrait School in Balance and Movement Independence, sense of community, trust, tolerance, respect and nonviolence, words from our mission statement that are important to us and make up our school.

Tasks

You support the teachers in teaching activities. This can be, for example, the accompaniment and promotion of the students in their learning process under the guidance of the teachers. The responsibility for the class and the teaching activity is always with the teacher.

Requirements

  • You are flexible in your schedule design and can cover a total of 15-18 hours per week.
  • You are patient, reliable and enjoy working with young people and adults.

  • You are discreet and comply with agreements and confidentiality.
