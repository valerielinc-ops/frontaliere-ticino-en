Company description ##

For our customers in the Wallis region, we are constantly looking for ambitious carpenters. If you are a carpenter and are looking for a new Heruas challenge then contact us. We would love to have you on our side.

Job description ##

  • Production and assembly of high-quality wooden furniture according to customer requirements
  • Carrying out repairs and restoration work on existing furniture
  • Machinery and manual processing of wood and wood-based materials
  • Collaboration with other craftsmen and construction teams on extensive projects
  • Ensuring high quality standards and compliance with agreed deadlines
  • Compliance with safety rules and environmentally friendly working practices

We are expecting ##

  • Completed training as carpenter or a comparable qualification
  • In-depth knowledge in woodworking and handling various tools and machines
  • Experience in assembling furniture and dealing with building plans
  • Creativity and skill in the implementation of individual customer requirements
  • Teamwork and communication strength for collaboration with colleagues and customers
  • Reliability and flexibility to adapt to changing project requirements
  • Knowledge of German at native level or fluent language skills

We offer ##

  • Varied and demanding tasks in an established craft business
  • Space for creativity and the opportunity to contribute your own ideas
  • Appropriate remuneration and transparent working conditions
  • Training opportunities and further development prospects
  • A motivated team and a pleasant working environment

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed.
