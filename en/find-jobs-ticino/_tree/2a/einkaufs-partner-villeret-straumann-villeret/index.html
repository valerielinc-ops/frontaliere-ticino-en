Introduction

As Procurement Lead for our Villeret manufacturing site, you will play a pivotal role in ensuring the reliable, compliant, and cost-effective supply of goods and services that support our operations. Acting as the primary procurement business partner to the site leadership team, you will connect global procurement strategies with local execution, helping drive operational excellence, product innovation, and regulatory compliance.

This is a highly visible role within the Villeret Site Leadership Team, offering the opportunity to collaborate across Operations, Quality, Engineering, Finance, and Global Procurement while making a direct impact on manufacturing performance and business success.

Your Role

As Procurement Lead, you will

Business Partnering & Operational Support

  • Serve as the primary procurement partner to the Villeret Site Leadership Team.
  • Support production priorities by ensuring a secure and reliable supply of materials and services.
  • Identify and mitigate supply risks to maintain operational continuity.
  • Contribute to site performance objectives and key operational metrics.

Supplier Management & Performance

  • Manage strategic supplier relationships and performance.
  • Monitor and improve supplier delivery, quality, and cost performance.
  • Lead supplier issue resolution and recovery planning when required.
  • Foster collaborative partnerships that support long-term business goals.

Quality & Regulatory Compliance

  • Ensure procurement activities align with quality management system requirements and applicable regulations.
  • Support supplier audits, corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), and change control processes.
  • Maintain procurement documentation and audit readiness.

Product Lifecycle & Engineering Collaboration

  • Partner with Engineering and cross-functional teams to support new product introductions and product changes.
  • Contribute to design-to-cost initiatives and sourcing strategies.
  • Ensure supplier readiness for industrialization and production scaling.

Cost Management & Procurement Excellence

  • Deliver sustainable cost savings and value-creation initiatives.
  • Execute global procurement strategies at site level.
  • Provide transparent reporting and tracking of procurement performance.
  • Support procurement transformation initiatives and continuous improvement efforts.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Build strong partnerships with Operations, Quality, Engineering, Finance, and Global Procurement teams.
  • Act as a key link between site requirements and global procurement priorities.
