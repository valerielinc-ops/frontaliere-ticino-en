Material handlers / warehouse employees

Summary of function

As a material handler, you are responsible for the management of the Clinical Label Services warehouse. The preparation of the print orders, such as the booking in and out of labels, booklets and consumables are also part of your task.

Their tasks

  • Register labels, booklets and consumables in CMAP, GPM and/or Coda
  • Ensuring on-time and quality delivery of the goods to be printed
  • Cleaning and maintenance of storage areas
  • GMP-compliant documentation of the tasks performed
  • Active participation in process improvement
  • Work in the 2-shift model (early and late shift)

Your profile

  • At least 2 years of professional experience in storage, logistics or as a material handler
  • Experience in the pharmaceutical or food industry is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing (English knowledge of advantage)
  • Experience in dealing with computers / Basic knowledge of common MS-Office applications
  • Personal responsibility, independent work, reliability and willingness to work in 2 shifts
  • Initiative, flexible and capable of teamwork
