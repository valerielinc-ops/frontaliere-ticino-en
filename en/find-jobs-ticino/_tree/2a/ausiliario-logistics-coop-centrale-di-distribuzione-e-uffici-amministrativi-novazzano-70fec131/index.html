Full description

The employer is looking for one

Fruit and vegetable quality controller (marked)

Mansions

  • you will be responsible for quality control of fresh fruit and vegetables (F\V) by carrying out checks on the basis of legal requirements and company directives
  • register your evaluations in the goods management system, take care of the registration of the goods and the management of the ramps, transmitting your findings to the managers
  • carry out the daily control of the goods in stock, inform the purchasing office on the evolution of the items present in stock and ensure communication with the stakeholders of the logistics and with the suppliers
  • verify the development of the goods, documenting the results of the samples taken and regularly informing the purchasers
  • you are the reference person regarding all quality issues related to F\V

Requirements

  • you have proven knowledge of the fruit and vegetable products and have already gained experience in the food sector
  • you have developed professional knowledge in the field of logistics, in the context of quality control of F\V or food production
  • know the systems of management of the goods and you have good computer skills
  • you have a personality oriented to teamwork and you can always keep calm, even in hectic situations
  • speak fluently Italian (the knowledge of German is preferential title)
  • licence to conduct cat. B
