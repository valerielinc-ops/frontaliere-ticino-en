As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital Zurich, we are committed to showing commitment with our professional diversity in the top medical environment and always keeping up with the times. We do not achieve success alone, but together! Each individual counts and together we bring ourselves to the highest level of care professionally.

Want to be part of our team? You can make a difference with us!

  • You are a specialist in health / health care, have been trained for at least 6 months, have experience in the acute field / IMC and are looking for an exciting, educational and interesting working environment? Are you flexible, motivated, have a quick apprehension and enjoy working in changing teams? Then we can offer you just that!
  • Your assignments provide you with insights into various specialized areas and ensure a varied everyday work
  • Your service life is flexible and depends on your current needs and availability
  • You work in different teams and can use and develop your expertise in a variety of ways
  • You will be employed in the hourly wage and benefit from an attractive, transparent wage. The hourly wage is CHF 43, in addition you will receive corresponding shift allowances

So how exactly does that work? You yourself tell us via an app when you are available and can be booked. Of course, you will receive the usual supplements for Saturday, Sunday and night work for the mentioned salary. The advantages we offer you over temporary offices are obvious: You always work at the same hospital and no longer have to travel across the country for your assignments. With us you can get to know new specialist areas and expand your expertise due to the top medical environment. The salary paid by us corresponds to what you usually receive from employee rental companies, but we pay more into your pension fund. With this model, we stand for flexibility and individual solutions. Whether you want fixed days and fixed services or want to have several months off in the summer, everything is possible. You can specify your availability up to one year in advance or reschedule on the same day. Both the deployment and the holiday planning are according to your needs – after all, you state your availability yourself and do not have to consult with anyone if you want to have several months off in the summer. You benefit not only from maximum flexibility, but also from personal support: Lea Kaltenbach, head of department IMC Pool, CPU and interdisciplinary day clinic, is always at your disposal as a contact person.
