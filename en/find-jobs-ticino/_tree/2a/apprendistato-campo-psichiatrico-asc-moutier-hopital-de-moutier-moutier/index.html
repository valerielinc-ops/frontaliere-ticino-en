Full description

  • Participate in basic care and support for psychiatric patients
  • Observe and contribute to the assessment of the physical and mental state of patients
  • Support daily activities to maintain patient autonomy
  • Collaborate with health, medical, social and therapeutic teams
  • Respecting the rules of hygiene, safety and professional ethics
  • A strong interest in psychiatry and mental health
  • Empathy, a caring and respectful attitude
  • A listening and communication capacity adapted to each situation
  • Good stress resistance and a desire to learn in a dynamic environment
  • The desire to obtain a CFC from ASSC and contribute to the well-being of patients
  • Use of IT tools

During your apprenticeship, you will be able to discover different fields, develop your autonomy and gradually build your professional experience. We offer you:

  • A variety of training in institutions recognized and close to their patients.
  • The opportunity to discover the professional world in a stimulating and constantly evolving environment.
  • Personalised support throughout your learning by committed and experienced trainers.
  • Conditions for learning, progressive autonomy and skills development.
  • The possibility of making a concrete contribution to the quality of care provided to the population of the region.
