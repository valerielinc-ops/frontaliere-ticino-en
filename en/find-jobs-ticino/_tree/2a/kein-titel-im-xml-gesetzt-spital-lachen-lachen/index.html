Full description

At the bedside departments of the hospital, you will accompany patients competently and personally during their inpatient stay. In a versatile acute hospital, we combine professional care with individual, holistic care. They work closely with different professional groups and actively shape the treatment process – with the aim of promoting recovery and enabling our patients to enjoy safety and quality of life.

We offer you

  • You want to accompany older people in challenging situations and work in a strong team? Then you are exactly right in the Akutgeriatrie.
  • We expect an exciting and varied everyday life with complex geriatric patient situations, interdisciplinary cooperation and holistic care.
  • We live team spirit: mutual support, a family relationship, a great sense of humour and short decision-making paths are of course part of this.
  • You will find a place where you will be appreciated for your part in thinking, initiative and a holistic view of the people.
  • Together we create an atmosphere in which humanity, expertise and genuine cohesion are perceptible every day.

What do you bring with you?

  • You are a trained health specialist EFZ and want to work in an acute hospital.
  • You accompany our patients with empathy and help them maintain and promote their independence in the best possible way.
  • You take on nursing and medical tasks competently and responsibly within your competence area.
  • You work closely with qualified nursing specialists as well as the interprof-essional team and actively contribute to a high quality of care.
  • You bring in your own initiative, take responsibility and value a collegial relationship. You also keep track of the changes in a varied daily work routine and make changes with flexibility.
