Installer for cable construction (80\-100%)

The company offers interesting opportunities for training, growth and further development. Our customers have high quality standards that motivate us to perform at the highest levels every day.   • Installation and cabling of traffic signalling systems
  • New construction and maintenance of floodlights on sports fields and public lighting
