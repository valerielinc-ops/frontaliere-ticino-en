The post is attached to the Department of Women, Children and Adolescents, whose mission is to provide quality care tailored to the needs of women at every stage of their lives and to the health problems facing newborns, children and adolescents from birth to age 18. For many years, the Department of Women, Children and Adolescents has been continuing to develop state-of-the-art services and expertise in several medical specialties. The Paediatric Specialty Service provides in-patient and out-patient care for the following specialties: paediatric allergy, paediatric cardiology, paediatric gastroenterology, paediatric nephrology and metabolism, paediatric pneumonology, neuropediatry, exercise and obesity medicine. He works on the development of state-of-the-art services and specialist expertise. The Cardio-pediatric Unit supports children from the fetal period (in collaboration with obstetrics) to adulthood. The Paediatric Cardiology Unit and Congenital Cardiac Surgery received the unrestricted qualification of Highly Specialized Medicine (HSM). Performing more than 2,000 consultations a year, specialists are able to carry out all the advanced examinations, such as electrocardiograms (ECGs), Holters, transthoracic, transoesophageal and vascular echocardiograms, stress tests, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and cardiac catheterism. More than 200 cardiac procedures are performed each year at the Children's Hospital. Paediatric cardiologists supervise their care, since admission, during the stay in intensive care, and then on the paediatric floor in collaboration with doctors of other services.

Description of post

As an assistant physician or assistant to the Cardio-Pediatric Unit, you contribute to the supervision of the activity, the supervision of in-house physicians and the chiefs of clinics. You collaborate in clinical activities with head and head doctors and assistant and assistant physicians. You ensure the coordination of practical activities, clinical, therapeutic or surgical interventions of the patients entrusted to you. You're part of the guard and picket system. Demonstrating a confirmed interest in teaching, you participate in pre- and post-graduate instruction and continue in the unit. Dynamic, flexible and committed, you develop interdisciplinary clinical projects. You participate, deploy and search the unit. You are a person recognized for your leadership and use your excellent organizational, relational and communication skills, as a multidisciplinary team and with caregivers and their families.

Qualifications

You have a federal degree in medicine, a diploma as a specialist in paediatrics and a thorough training in paediatric cardiology or have training deemed equivalent by the Medical Professions Commission of the Federal Office of Public Health (MEBEKO). You hold a doctoral thesis and are in possession of the right of practice allowing you to practice in the canton of Geneva or are able to obtain it before you take up your post. You have experience in pediatric cardiology and have already worked as head or head of advanced clinic or senior physician in a hospital. You have a strong or strong experience as a clinical leader in a pediatric cardiology unit as well as in team management and management. You have experience in pre- and post-graduate teaching and clinical research. For non-French speakers, the French level C1 on the spoken and written test is required.

Further information

You can work both in daytime, nighttime, weekend and public holidays. You also participate in guards and pickets.
