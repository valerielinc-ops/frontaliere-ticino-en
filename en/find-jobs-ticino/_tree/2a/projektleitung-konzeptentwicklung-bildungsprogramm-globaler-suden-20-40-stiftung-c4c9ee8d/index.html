Full description

Would you like to contribute your educational experience and develop an educational concept together with specialists in Uganda? For a temporary phase of concept development, we are looking for a specialist with experience in education, concept development and international cooperation.

This is what you do with us

  • For the Gott hilft Foundation, you are developing an educational program for teachers and carers in the Global South. You will develop a well-founded concept that includes further education modules, evaluated initial trials as well as financing and implementation planning.
  • Together with the partners in Uganda, you clarify the need and develop goals and modules from it. The starting point is the own pre- and primary school in Lira.
  • You coordinate the testing of selected modules in Lira and equip them with the local team.
  • You bring together the results in the concept, with quality criteria, budget and requirements for a later transfer to other locations.

This will convince us

  • You bring pedagogical expertise and experience in the development of educational offers, for example from school development, adult education or social pedagogy. Appropriate training or comparable professional experience is welcome.
  • You have experience in international cooperation. Decisive is your willingness to learn and develop together with local experts.
  • You can design development processes, incorporate experiences from different contexts and write clear concepts.
  • You write stylishly in German and can communicate safely in English in a professional context.
  • You respectfully carry our Christian image of humanity and our values.

This is how we imagine it

A self-responsible and time-limited project task with design possibilities and flexible working conditions. The position can be organised alongside another professional activity.

  • From March 2027 or by arrangement, limited to 4 to 8 months
  • Temporary employment or mandate with the possibility of cooperation in implementation
  • Readiness required for one to two project trips to Uganda, a total of three to four weeks.
  • Home office and flexible working hours
  • Connection to the Foundation Gott hilft in Zizers
  • Possibility of a combination with the separately advertised fundraising position 40 to 60%

Interested?

We look forward to meeting you. For a first exchange, your CV and a few sentences about your experience in educational development and international cooperation are sufficient.

We are happy to clarify with you how the task fits your profile and your time availability.

Daniel Wartenweiler, Head of Foundation God Helps
