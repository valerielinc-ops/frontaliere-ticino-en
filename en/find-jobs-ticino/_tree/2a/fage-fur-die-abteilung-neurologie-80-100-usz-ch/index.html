Full description

USZ Campus immediately or by agreement

We are especially looking forward to you if you

  • are a health professional and already have experience in the acute field or feel like switching there
  • Comes from the age and long-term care or the rehabilitation area and wants to take the step into acute setting
  • Starting fresh from training or having many years of professional experience, we are open to both
  • Are motivated to learn new things and engage in a dynamic, neurospecialized environment

What awaits you?

  • In tandem with a dipl. You are a nurse at one of our two neurological bed stations
  • Your main task is the care of patients: women with stroke, Parkinson’s, multiple sclerosis and neuromuscular diseases
  • We also take on patients: insiders from our vertigo center as well as from the Stroke Center
  • You work closely with an experienced interdisciplinary team (doctor: inside, therapist: inside as well as specialist: inside different disciplines and focus areas)

What can we offer you?

With us you will experience the entire, exciting breadth of neurology. Much of it will seem familiar to you and we will gladly show you how many touch points you already bring. In the areas that are still new to you, you learn quickly and practically with us. Our two neurological bed stations at USZ work closely together and support each other. Interdisciplinary cooperation is also central: Our experts, from the medical service to the therapist, are working with you to ensure the best possible care for our patients. Since we regularly coordinate with the two IMCs of the Stroke Center (Stroke Unit and Post Stroke Unit), you also benefit from station and cross-professional training, which is prepared both by nursing and doctors.
