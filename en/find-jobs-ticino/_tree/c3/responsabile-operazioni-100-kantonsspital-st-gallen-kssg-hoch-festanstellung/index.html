Group leader/in surgery care 100%

Orthopedics and Traumatology / Plastic Surgery and Hand Surgery

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Leadership, promotion and coaching of employees in the field
  • Participation in surgical coordination in close consultation with the professional groups involved
  • Participation in the strategic and operational development of entrepreneurial and sector-specific projects
  • Active activity in the operating room

What you bring for this job

  • Training as a Dipl. surgical care specialist or as a dipl. Specialist surgical technology HF with professional experience in the field
  • Leadership experience is beneficial, willingness to complete internal leadership training
  • Open, innovative, assertive and resilient personality
  • High level of social competence, dialogue and motivational ability, as well as ability for interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • High patient orientation and high quality awareness

Our offer to you

  • Employment at one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland in the immediate vicinity of Lake Constance and Alpine Mountains
  • Constant innovations and state-of-the-art operating methods
  • Use of innovative technologies such as Velys robotics in knee surgery or augmented reality in shoulder surgery, navigated spinal surgery, lobe surgery in the field of plastic surgery
  • Advanced electronic OR maintenance documentation with integrated material management
  • Engaged collaboration in a highly specialized interdisciplinary environment
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
