Full description

For our open and collegial management team we are looking for 1. January 2027 or by agreement

a committed and organizational personality who supports us administratively and actively shapes everyday school life.

School management assistant regular school (SAR), 70% (yearly working hours)

Your working days are: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday and Friday

  • Administrative support for school management and management teams
  • Central start-up and turntable point for children, parents, employees and administration
  • Telephone, information and triage of requests
  • Management of global credit and general secretarial tasks
  • Log management and data and file management
  • Assistance/presence at events and training events
  • Participation and presence in school events and further education
  • Commercial education (EFZ) or equivalent qualification, e.g. commercial diploma VSH
  • Experience in a school environment or in a public administration is beneficial
  • Joy of contact with people of all stakeholders and cultures
  • Style-confident knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Organizational talent with independent and reliable working methods
  • Initiative, flexibility and humor
  • Very good user knowledge in information and communication technologies and in data management
  • Motivated, helpful team with humor
  • Multifaceted activity with variety
  • Attractive urban employment conditions

The Day School Mattenhof is an age-mixed school in Zurich-Schwamendingen. Thanks to the immediate proximity to the Stettbach station, our location is easily accessible by train, tram and bus.

In three kindergarten classes and ten mixed-age primary classes, children of different ages learn and live together. In our two care facilities we accompany and care for up to 230 children every day.

Since 2020, the school Mattenhof is run as a day school of the city of Zurich. We combine teaching and supervision and continuously develop their cooperation. A lively school life and a good cooperation shape our school day.
