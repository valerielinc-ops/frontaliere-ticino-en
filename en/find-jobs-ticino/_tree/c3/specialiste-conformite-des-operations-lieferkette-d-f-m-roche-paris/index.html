The Position As a Compliance Specialist in Supply Chain Operations (d/f/m) you will be the guarantor of the quality, compliance and continuous improvement of the supply chain unit. You will be referred to for the preparation, maintenance and monitoring of audits, as well as for the documentation updates and support of the deviation unit. As a Quality Correspondent for Supply Unit documentation and Coremap reference for the Unit, you will coordinate and document initial and ongoing training and empowerment.

Your Responsibilities

Training and empowerment of Supply Chain members

  • Refers to our Business Partners and the initial and continuing unit training documentation;
  • Management of the individual training course (Cornerstone tool): Unit employees and providers;
  • Initial training: management of the course of integration of new employees (definition and validation of the course of training and empowerment, monitoring of the progress of the training programme and collection of participation documents);
  • Ongoing training: coordination of the continued empowerment of Unit staff and providers;
  • Updating of function cards;
  • Propose the adjustment of training pathways in a continuous improvement approach.

Documentary Quality

  • To be a reference for the Quality Department: follow-up of the documentation updates, ensuring compliance with the update schedule;
  • Coordination of the Unit's documentary update: monitoring of updates, drafting and validation of documents, initiation of signature cycles and coordination with the providers' reference staff;
  • Centralize optimisation proposals in a continuous improvement approach;
  • Write the change controls of the Unit and accompany the collaborators in writing deviations;
  • Coordinate audits and inspections with the reference staff.

Documentary system

  • Organization and archiving of documentation in Coremap tool;
  • Unit support on archiving issues.

Compliance of logistics operations

  • To guarantee the compliance of our internal logistics providers and supply chain process with the Roche (subsidiary and global) repositories;
  • Provides the missions of LSO (Logistic Security Officer) and LECO (Local Export Compliance Officer).

Your profile

  • Mastery of pharmaceutical standards (GMP, BPD);
  • Experience in pharmaceutical distribution and knowledge of Supply Chain operations;
  • Very good knowledge of document management and tools (Veeva would be a plus);
  • Knowledge of SAP-S4 Hana;
  • English: good command (written and spoken);
  • You are rigorous, organized, methodical;
  • Spirit of continuous improvement, force of proposal;
  • Editorial, relational and transversal experience with the pedagogy.
