What we offer you.

An innovative and challenging environment awaits you in the SBB passenger transport maintenance sector. A fantastic and motivated team is happy to welcome you as a new partner. In the first months you will be inserted into the service structure and work on vehicles, passing through practical and theoretical modules. We support you in training in a varied and interesting business environment, offering opportunities for internal growth as product manager, shift or as a technical specialist. We are an attractive employer and offer numerous benefits such as additional allowances for children, general subscription of 2nd class free and discounted trips abroad also for the family. Find all information on our website under the Benefits section. What you can move.

  • Autonomously perform all maintenance, regulation, measurement and inspection work in accordance with technical directives and protocol them to art
  • Replace wear parts and adjust the parts according to maintenance instructions
  • Perform self-restoration of medium complexity (in accordance with system/vehicle documentation, on the basis of professional knowledge of electronics/mechanics/informatics and a clear control of operation) on the basis of system/vehicle documentation and communicate them according to the directives
  • Detecting abnormalities and potential improvement in the process (PMC, MOM, RNC+ )
  • Diagnose abnormalities and perform repairs on vehicles

What you bring with you.

  • Working schedule 7/7
  • AFC professional internship in one of the following addresses: automation operator, automotive mechatronics or an electrical address of 4 years (e.g. electrical installer) or multi-year experience in the maintenance of rolling stock
  • Additional SSST training or car diagnostic APF constitute a preferential title
  • Good computer knowledge and related media (e-mail, SAP, Intranet).
  • Knowledge of the German language is a preferential title
  • Analytical thinking, solution-oriented approach, independence, sense of responsibility and attitude to educate employees.
