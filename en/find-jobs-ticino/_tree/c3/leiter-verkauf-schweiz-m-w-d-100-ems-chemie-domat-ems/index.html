Full description

What to expect

  • They are responsible for marketing and selling high-performance polyamides in Switzerland.
  • In particular, they look after the market segments of industry and consumer goods.
  • Recognize new market opportunities and develop concrete customer projects from them.
  • They build long-term customer relationships and continuously develop them.
  • You lead and motivate a team of key account managers.
  • Together with your team, you ensure the achievement of volume and sales targets.
  • They develop and implement sales and marketing strategies.
  • They advise customers on products in need of technical and explanation.

What do you bring with you

  • Ideally, they have many years of experience in plastics technology and/or mechanical engineering.
  • They bring in-depth business knowledge and leadership experience.
  • They have experience in sales and marketing of technical B2B products.
  • They are characterized by a pronounced customer orientation and high commitment.
  • They are communicative, enthusiastic and results-oriented.
  • They convince through negotiation skills and a pronounced entrepreneurial thinking.
  • They have reliable knowledge of German and English.
  • Further language skills are advantageous.
  • They bring a high willingness to travel of around 30-40%.

You can look forward to that.

  • Working in the middle of beautiful natural and recreational areas
  • Exciting tasks in an international environment with short decision paths
  • Lots of personal responsibility and attractive management positions
  • Market-oriented remuneration, progressive social benefits and own pension fund with good benefits
  • Staff restaurant, free parking

About us

EMS has 25 production sites in 16 countries, employs around 3,000 people worldwide and, with around 140 apprentices, is the largest vocational trainer in the canton and winner of the National Education Award.

EMS-GRIVORY is part of the EMS Group’s high-performance polymers business unit and employs more than 1000 people worldwide. The largest development and production centre is located in Domat/Ems in Switzerland. Further production facilities are located in Germany, China, Taiwan and the USA.
