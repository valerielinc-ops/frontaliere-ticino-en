Full description

The blue-silver of our freight trains belongs to the East Switzerland streetscape. And yet we drive piece goods and complete loads across the whole of Switzerland and even beyond the borders – on the road, day and night, with heart and brain. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

What you move

  • Based on the planning of the scheduling, you carry out the transport orders in national traffic and deliver the goods on time and professionally to our customers
  • Loading and unloading? No problem for you - you get involved and make sure that everything is safely stored
  • You make sure the cargo is perfectly secured – Nothing is left to chance
  • Together with you, your vehicle is the most important and always visible business card of our company – so you take care of its care and maintenance

Why do you succeed?

  • You have the driver's license Kat. CE in the bag and can drive trailer train like a professional
  • Advantageously, you already have experience in general cargo transport and have the truck ID and the ADR ID.
  • Your driver permits are all up to date
  • Your willingness to spend the night in the driver’s cab 2–3 times a week makes you a flexible part of our team
  • In Switzerland you know yourself well and feel at home on the street
  • Oral and written you can certainly express yourself in German
  • You are reliable, flexible and, thanks to your resilience, ready to go full speed even in stressful moments

Your Benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Quality bonus: Quality is written in our capital and rewarded
  • Central introductory training: You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Generous expense regulations: With our expense regulations you are well taken care of

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
