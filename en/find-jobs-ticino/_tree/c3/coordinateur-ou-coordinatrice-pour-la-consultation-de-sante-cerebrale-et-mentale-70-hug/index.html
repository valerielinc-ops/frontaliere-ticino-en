Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . Operational since 1 November 2024, the objective of the HUG Brain and Mental Health Consultations at Biotech Campus is to provide care to patients receiving neurological and psychiatric follow-up, in order to better understand the disorders in these areas and to develop new treatment strategies. The patient will have access to clinical follow-up thanks to neuropsychologists and dedicated doctors (neurochirurgiens and neurosurgeons, psychiatrists or neurologists), without having to return to hospital. Clinical paths will be established by the respective teams of neurology, psychiatry and neurosurgery for various pathologies, making the best use of the technical infrastructure of the Biotech Campus. Mobile patients will benefit from the unique CBT framework and will be seen by specialized clinical teams. In terms of research, several teams will also be available to propose participation in translational research projects, in order to advance the understanding of disorders such as fatigue and apathy, schizophrenia or motivational deficit, and pave the way for new patient-friendly treatment strategies. An additional asset will be the creation of a register to better document and understand the targeted pathologies and the treatments applied, with standardized and quality data. This initiative will help to customize patient care and will strongly promote bilateral interaction and fruitful cooperation between clinicians, clinicians and researchers, researchers to provide high-quality care and leading-edge translational research.

Description of post

In this position, you manage interactions between clinicians and researchers from different departments and institutions. You organize and coordinate periodic meetings to maintain interaction among members, present new projects and share project progress. You coordinate the effective allocation of resources for clinical and research activities (staff and equipment). You manage the assignment of patients to appropriate research projects and researchers (including the coordination of junior research staff in charge of recruitment). You are involved in the regular updating of databases on research projects. You help establish procedures and processes to ensure accurate testing and integrity of clinical and research protocols. You monitor the progress and status of projects in order to make regular reports. You guarantee the proper functioning of logistical and administrative activities (creation badges, allocation of access, ...).

Qualifications

You have a master's degree or equivalent and a certification in project management (a considerable asset). You have a successful experience in project management, as well as a minimum of 5 years experience in a similar function. You have great abstraction and conceptualization capabilities and are able to solve complex problems. While you have a good sense of organization and priorities, you demonstrate responsiveness, autonomy, rigour and precision in the tasks entrusted to you. Empathic, you like human contacts and tactful in sensitive situations. You are able to work as a multidisciplinary team. Finally, you have a keen sense of confidentiality.
