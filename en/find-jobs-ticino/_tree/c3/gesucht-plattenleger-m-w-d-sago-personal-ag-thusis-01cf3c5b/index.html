Their tasks

  • Execution of general plate laying work in new, renovation and renovation construction
  • Installation of ceramic, porcelain and natural stone slabs
  • Installation of wall and floor panels in bathrooms, kitchens and living quarters
  • Preparation, cleaning and balancing of substrates
  • Cutting and adapting plates to different masses and shapes
  • Waterproofing work in wet rooms
  • Combining and siliconizing wall and floor coverings
  • Creation of gradients in showers, terraces and other areas
  • Laying of large-format plates and mosaics
  • Carrying out repair and renovation work
  • Removal and replacement of damaged or old plate coverings

  • Experience in providing and executing silicone joints
  • Safe handling of plate cutting machines and various tools
  • Experience in laying large plates is advantageous
  • Knowledge of gradient and waterproofing in wet spaces
  • Experience in the new, renovation and renovation sector
  • Independent and professional work according to building plans

  • German skills are advantageous
