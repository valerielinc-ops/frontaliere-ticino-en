Logistics / 100%

Department of Real Estate & Operation, Supply Chain Management

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Checks on receipt and exit of goods
  • Timely order picking of storage orders according to specifications
  • Booking via SAP MM and WM module along the supply chain
  • Processing of all transport orders according to route planning and individual orders
  • Legally compliant handling and disposal of all goods in the area of transport and disposal
  • Readiness for occasional shift work and weekend assignments

What you bring for this job

  • Completed vocational training as logistician/EFZ
  • Practical experience in warehousing and distribution required
  • Category B driving licence
  • SUVA approved forklift certificate
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Good PC user knowledge
  • Reliable, independent and physically resilient personality

  • Family cooperation in an interdisciplinary environment
