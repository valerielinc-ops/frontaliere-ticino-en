The Position About the Role At Roche Digital Technology (RDT), we are advancing the boundaries of Applied AI. As part of our strategic initiatives, we enable high-value AI solutions (with a strong focus on Generative AI and agentic systems) through fit-for-purpose platforms, services, and applications. The Applied AI Engineering Team is tasked with building innovative AI applications, GenAI agents, and agentic foundations. In the 2026 tech landscape, the lines between traditional disciplines have blurred. We operate in small agile teams (typically ~9 members) powered by advanced coding agents (like Claude Code) to develop and ship solutions faster than ever before (Agentic SDLC). To support our rapid-turnaround, high-velocity engineering teams, we are seeking a highly skilled, deeply experienced IT Business Analyst. Operating in a highly regulated pharmaceutical and clinical environment (GxP), you will serve as the strategic interface between complex business priorities, regulatory compliance frameworks, and cutting-edge engineering realities. You are not a traditional analyst who merely documents requests; you are a passionate, technically literate individual contributor equipped with advanced AI skills (including prompt engineering & modern agentic workflows) and the business acumen to collaborate closely with product leaders. Furthermore, you possess strong Product Owner (PO) skills and are fully prepared to confidently act as a Proxy PO for the squad if necessary—managing the product backlog, defining sprint goals, and steering delivery timelines to align with Roche’s strategic objectives. Core Tech Stack & Scope This role requires a deep understanding of three core domains: AI systems, the life sciences and pharmaceutical business context, and the complexities of operating within a highly regulated environment. The IT Business Analyst serves as the strategic bridge between complex business requirements and cutting-edge technical execution. You will take ownership of the end-to-end requirements lifecycle for AI initiatives, ensuring that all architectural designs and engineering deliverables are scalable, high-performing, and strictly compliant with global regulatory standards. By aligning technical roadmaps with strategic priorities, you will drive the delivery of robust AI solutions that provide measurable business value and maintain the highest levels of data integrity and quality assurance.
