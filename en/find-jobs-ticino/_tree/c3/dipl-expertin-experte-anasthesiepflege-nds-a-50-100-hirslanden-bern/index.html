Reference number: 71954

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

In the clinic Permanence you can expect a responsible task in the fields of orthopedics, neurosurgery, visceral surgery and ENT, in which you can contribute your expertise and further develop. You can expect an activity in day-to-day operations supplemented with pikett assignments on weekends.

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in the fitness subscription & entrance to the indoor pool
  • Flexible service plan model
  • Possibility to obtain up to two additional weeks of holiday with the Leisure Plus model

YOUR TASKS

  • A careful monitoring and preparation of patients for regional and general anesthesia is a matter of course for you
  • You are responsible for the preparation and control of all materials, medicines and equipment within your jurisdiction
  • You can look forward to a partnership with anesthesiologist: inside, surgeon: inside and the entire surgical staff
  • You support in emergency measures and may be involved in missions in radiology and in the resuscitation team (area of application without pediatrics and obstetrics)
  • Opportunity to participate in vocational training and take on specialist responsibilities
