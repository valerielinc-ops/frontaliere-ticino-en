Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • rules on the execution of ambulatory and inpatient measures and imprisonment
  • Implementation of easing of penalties including conditional dismissals and ordering probation assistance
  • drafting of enforcement decisions; preparing written case summaries; assessment by means of risk assessment tools
  • Introduction of in-depth internal and external clarifications (e.g. expert assignments)
  • accompaniment of offenders during enforcement and optimum preparation for freedom
  • Conducting hearings and location discussions with clients and representatives of institutions
  • Close cooperation with various law enforcement institutions (judicial institutions, intervention centres, clinics); Interdisciplinary cooperation with the specialists of probation assistance

What you bring

  • Successfully completed legal studies (lic. iur. or MAS)
  • Specialised additional training (e.g. CAS Dissociality) desirable or willing to complete it
  • Several years of professional experience and knowledge of criminal law
  • High performance and resilience

Your working environment

The Office for Prisoners brings together institutions and services that deal with the execution of penalties and measures and thus contribute to social security.

This also includes the Section of Implementation Services and Probation Assistance, in which court-ordered prison sentences and criminal measures are executed. Employees take into account the specific offences, the risk potential and the development needs of the clientele and keep the most important goals of recidivism reduction and social integration in mind.
