Full description

Portrait The school Bettenhausen-Ochlenberg-Thörigen runs a total of five basic and six primary classes at the three locations Bettenhausen, Thörigen and Neuhaus.

The individual school buildings are manageable in their size, partly rural and places where our students can feel comfortable and develop. We actively maintain an open, appreciative and constructive cooperation both within the college and with the students and parents.

For more information, visit our homepage: www.schule-bot.ch.

Tasks

In cooperation with the co-class teacher, you will lead a base level class with 16 SuS and 16 lessons of team teaching. This ensures a supportive environment.

Requirements

You have a teaching diploma or are in primary education. Teamwork, openness, flexibility and reliability are required. We want a committed and creative teacher who knows how to inspire students and promote them in their development. A willingness to cooperate and an open, appreciative attitude in dealing with students, colleagues, parents and guardians are part of this for us.

We offer

  • Temporary employment according to the cantonal employment conditions (LAG).
  • Good school house infrastructure at a medium-sized school in Thörigen

(with three base level classes and two 3./4. classes).

  • Working together in a helpful, experienced and appreciative team.
  • Modern IT infrastructure for teaching

(interactive wall panel, laptops for students and teachers, teacher office, KLAPP).

  • Free parking on site
