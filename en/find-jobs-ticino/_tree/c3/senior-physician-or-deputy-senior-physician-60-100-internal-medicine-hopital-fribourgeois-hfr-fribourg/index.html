The Department of General Internal Medicine and specialist areas includes a reference center for general internal medicine at the HFR Freiburg - Cantonal Hospital with almost 130 beds. It is also present at 3 other locations: HFR Meyriez-Murten, HFR Riaz and HFR Tafers. Patients are cared for on an outpatient and inpatient basis. General internal medicine offers comprehensive patient care, including for complex diagnoses, including in close collaboration with the department's specialist departments. To complement its team, the Medical Directorate for General Internal Medicine at the HFR Freiburg - Cantonal Hospital is looking for a senior physician or deputy senior physician (60-100%) Tasks

  • You teach as part of the diploma and post-diploma courses as well as in further medical training.
  • You participate in the development of departmental and research projects.
  • You take part in the on-call service. Profile
  • Federal medical diploma or equivalent training
  • FMH specialist title in general internal medicine or in the process of acquiring it, or equivalent training
  • Several years of experience as an assistant doctor in internal medicine in a hospital
  • German or French with a good knowledge of the other language
