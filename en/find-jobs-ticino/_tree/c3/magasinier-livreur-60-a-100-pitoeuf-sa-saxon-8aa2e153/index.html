Shopper\-Booker (M/F) 60 to 100% (permit C1 or C)

Since 1947, Piteuf SA has been supplying restaurants, hoteliers and food professionals. based in Saxon, our Valais company relies on a modern logistics center and concrete commitments: reliability, quality and traceability.

To strengthen our team, we look for a person who is ad hoc, organized and attached to the work well done. Both on deposit and on delivery, you contribute directly to the quality of service offered to our customers.

Your missions

  • Receive, control and store goods in refrigerated spaces and freezers.
  • Prepare the controls accurately and load your vehicle.
  • Ensure deliveries to our professional customers according to the tour plan.
  • Respect the cold chain and hygiene and safety rules.
  • Represent Piteuf SA to customers through reliable service and courteous contact.

Your profile

  • You have a valid C1 or C driver's licence and driving experience.
  • You master French to understand instructions, process delivery documents and exchange with customers.
  • You are comfortable with handling and working in a refrigerated environment.
  • You show punctuality, rigour and organizational sense.
  • You know how to manage priorities and maintain your effectiveness during sustained periods.
  • You enjoy teamwork as well as autonomy on the road.

What we propose

  • A varied activity combining logistics, driving and customer contact.
  • A modern working environment within a Valais company established since 1947\.
  • An indeterminate contract with an activity rate of 60 to 100%, to be agreed.

Your application

Do you recognize yourself in this profile? Send us your file, including your CV and your work certificates. Please specify your desired activity rate and your availability date.
