Full description

Looking for a student or temporary job in the retail trade? Actively support our team on the sales floor and serve our customers at the cash desk.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Lucerne, registered office Dierikon, Industriestrasse 2, 6036 Dierikon

Important information

Minimum age 15 years. In the next step, you can specify your desired region. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Disposal, presentation and maintenance of goods
  • Quality, data and freshness control
  • Customer service in compliance with the cash regulations and providing customer information
  • Settlement of monetary transactions with the branch office

Requirements

  • First professional experience is advantageous: in the retail trade
  • No education/further education required: Job suitable for students
  • The joy of dealing with people
  • Self-employment and flexibility
  • Very good physical strength
  • Reliable and accurate working methods
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Part of the Migros Group: As part of the Migros Group, you benefit from stable structures, diverse career opportunities and attractive benefits
  • Modern infrastructure: Our facilities are modernly equipped to ensure optimal working conditions
  • Flexible working hours: Our flexible working hours support a balanced work-life balance
  • Du-Culture: With Du-Culture we promote closeness and team spirit and create an open working atmosphere
  • Personal responsibility & freedom: Personal responsibility and freedom promote your personal development, initiative and job satisfaction

Contatto

Application and contact Vivienne Schegg’

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision to advance or withdraw from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And conversely, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
