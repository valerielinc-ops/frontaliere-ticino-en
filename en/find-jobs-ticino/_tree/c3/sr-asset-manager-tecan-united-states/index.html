Senior Asset Manager

Full-Time Morgan Hill, CA | Onsite

Make an Impact With Our Team

Are you a strategic supply chain professional who thrives on solving complex material challenges, building strong customer relationships, and driving operational excellence? We are seeking a Senior Asset Manager to join our Supply Chain team and play a critical role in ensuring material availability, supporting customer programs, and optimizing inventory performance.

In this role, you'll manage material planning, customer-owned inventory, excess inventory resolution, procurement coordination, and commodity strategies while partnering closely with Purchasing, Planning, Program Management, and customers. You'll proactively identify supply risks, develop mitigation plans, and help ensure products are delivered on time to support business objectives.

If you're analytical, customer-focused, and passionate about supply chain strategy and continuous improvement, we'd love to hear from you.

How Will You Make an Impact?

  • Material Planning & Inventory Management
  • Analyze material requirements for production kits over a rolling 6–8-week planning horizon.
  • Plan and commit material for assigned customer programs to support production schedules.
  • Identify material shortages or supply gaps that could impact kit readiness and customer deliveries.
  • Monitor material availability and proactively mitigate supply risks that may impact revenue.
  • Perform Bill of Materials (BOM) profile analyses to identify sole-source components, long lead-time items, risk buys, and obsolete inventory.
  • Conduct Engineering Change Order (ECO) material impact analysis and disposition planning.
  • Customer Asset & Inventory Management
  • Manage customer-owned inventory purchases and coordinate material alignment with Program Management.
  • Provide customers with weekly excess inventory approval requests and drive timely resolution.
  • Coordinate excess inventory sales, BOM updates, and material shipments back to customers.
  • Partner with customers to process purchase order revisions resulting from inventory adjustments or final count variances.
  • Prepare and analyze monthly excess inventory reports, validating inventory status and recommending corrective actions.
  • Ensure component cancellations and material returns are completed for assigned customer accounts.

Supply Chain Coordination

  • Partner with Commodity Buyers to monitor pull-in activities and resolve material shortages.
  • Run "What-If" analyses to support customer pull-in and push-out requests.
  • Develop and implement commodity and material management strategies that improve supply continuity and inventory performance.
  • Collaborate with Purchasing, Planning, Program Management, Manufacturing, and Customer Service to resolve material issues and support operational goals.

Continuous Improvement

  • Maintain accurate reporting and documentation to support inventory planning and customer communications.
  • Identify opportunities to improve planning processes, inventory management, and operational efficiency.
  • Support quarterly material risk mitigation initiatives and continuous improvement programs.
  • Build strong relationships with internal stakeholders and customers while delivering exceptional service.

What You Bring

  • Strong analytical, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills.
  • Customer-focused mindset with a commitment to delivering exceptional service.
  • Ability to prioritize multiple projects in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.
  • Strong organizational skills with exceptional attention to detail.
  • Proven ability to make sound decisions and follow through on commitments.
  • Collaborative approach with the ability to work cross-functionally across Supply Chain, Purchasing, Planning, Manufacturing, and Customer teams.
  • High level of initiative, accountability, and professionalism.
  • Advanced Microsoft Excel skills for reporting, analysis, and data management.

Your Background

  • Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain Management, Business, Operations, or a related field.
  • 5–7 years of experience in materials management, supply chain, or inventory management.
  • 3–5 years of experience in planning, procurement, or purchasing.
  • Experience working within ERP/MRP systems.
  • Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications.
  • Experience performing material planning, inventory analysis, and supply risk management.
  • Experience supporting customer-owned inventory and excess inventory resolution.

Nice to Haves

  • APICS (CPIM) or NAPM certification.
  • MBA or Master's degree in Business Administration or a related field.
  • Experience with ManEx and Spotlight software.
  • Experience developing commodity strategies and supplier management initiatives.
  • Knowledge of electronic manufacturing services (EMS) or contract manufacturing environments.
  • Experience leading cross-functional supply chain improvement initiatives.

What We Offer

  • Competitive compensation and comprehensive benefits package.
  • Medical, Dental, and Vision insurance.
  • 401(k) with company match.
  • Paid time off and company holidays.
  • Professional development and career advancement opportunities.
  • Collaborative and team-oriented work environment.
  • Opportunity to make a meaningful impact on customer satisfaction and operational excellence.

Salary Range: 100K-120K (Compensation is based on education, experience, skills, internal equity, and market data.)
