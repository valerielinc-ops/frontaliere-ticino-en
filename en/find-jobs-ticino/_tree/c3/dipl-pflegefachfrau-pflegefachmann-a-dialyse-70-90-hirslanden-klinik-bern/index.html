Full description

Location: Salem Hospital - Bern

Occupation by: Immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 75319

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards at Hirslanden with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

We are expanding the range and capacities of our dialysis department in the Salem Hospital. That is why we are looking for a Dipl to complement the team. Nursing professional (a).

Your Advantages for Us at DIALYSIS

  • Several weeks in advance planned work assignments
  • No night and Sunday services (Monday – Saturday; 06:30 – 19:00)
  • No piquetting services
  • Treatment rooms with daylight and views of the old town of Bern
  • A helpful and warm team atmosphere
  • Leisure Plus model (buy up to two additional weeks of holiday)
  • Depending on the level of employment, up to four additional free days (compensation changing time)

An overview of our employment conditions and benefits can be found under: Team diagnosis - What we lack is you.

YOUR TASKS

  • You independently carry out hemodialysis treatments in the dialysis department as well as in the intensive care unit
  • Furthermore, you are responsible for carrying out peritoneal dialysis in the center
  • You keep the care documentation TDMS/TSS carefully, seamlessly and comprehensibly and take responsibility for the precise execution of medical prescriptions
  • In addition, you ensure the quality of care and work interdisciplinary with all professional groups to ensure optimal patient care.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a qualified nurse (for foreign degrees: including valid SRC recognition)
  • You bring experience in the acute hospital or ideally in a nephrological department / dialysis
  • As a team-capable and time-flexible personality, you are ready for shift work
  • Your empathy with patients and your high level of personal responsibility distinguishes you
  • You have very good knowledge of German, every other foreign language is an advantage

For additional information, Jolanta Pawlik, Head of Dialysis, is available at T, and Raphael Brand, Head of Recruitment, at recruiting.bern@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact and application.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by e-mail for data protection reasons. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
