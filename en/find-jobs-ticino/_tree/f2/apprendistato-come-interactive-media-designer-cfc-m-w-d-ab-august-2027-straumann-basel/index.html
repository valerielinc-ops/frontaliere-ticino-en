Full description

The Straumann Group has been one of the most innovative companies in Switzerland for more than 70 years. Our employees work every day to further develop dentistry. We develop, manufacture and supply dental implants, CAD/CAM prosthetics and biomaterials that help people to live better around the world. In more than 100 countries, we work together on new, forward-looking solutions, combining technology, innovation and teamwork. At our headquarters in the Jacob-Burckhardt building directly next to the SBB station in Basel, we currently train 11 apprentices in 6 different professions. You start your apprenticeship at our location in Basel. After opening our new Technology and Innovation Center in Arlesheim, you will continue your education in a state-of-the-art work environment. The change of location offers you the opportunity to become part of an innovative environment and to continue your professional development in a modern infrastructure. During your four-year training, you will gain exciting insights into the diverse world of visual design and can actively contribute your creativity. You get to know the entire range of design and work on varied projects – from corporate identity to web design and text design to audio and video productions as well as 2D and 3D applications. With us, you take on responsibility early on and can implement your own smaller projects independently. Your ideas and your work are not only appreciated in the team, but can also be presented and exhibited within the company or externally. Your profile

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed.
