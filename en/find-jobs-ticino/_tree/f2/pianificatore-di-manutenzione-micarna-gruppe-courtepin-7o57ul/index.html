Full description

As a maintenance planner, you are responsible for planning and monitoring the preventive or extraordinary maintenance work on the production facilities. Is this the challenge you are looking for? Then join Micarna, a member of the Migros industry and Swiss market leader in the production of meat, poultry, eggs and fish.

Mansioni

  • Planning of preventive or extraordinary maintenance measures in cooperation with the production areas and the various maintenance departments
  • Planning and coordination of required external resources
  • Coordination of operations, management of resources and monitoring of plant maintenance. Ensuring the availability of spare parts
  • Reduction of major plant downtimes by optimizing preventive maintenance
  • Participation in the implementation and improvement of preventive maintenance plans in cooperation with maintenance managers and technicians
  • Cooperation with production to integrate preventive and specific maintenance into the planning of their activities
  • Support for those responsible by creating key performance indicators, participating in the creation of procedures and technical standards and managing documentation
  • Participation in continuous improvement initiatives aimed at increasing the performance and availability of production facilities
  • Assistance in managing the telephone calls of our hotline to coordinate the needs for internal/external resources in case of unplanned downtimes

Requirements

  • 1-3 years: Experience in a similar position
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): EFZ as a polymechanic, automatic technician, electrician, mechanic or equivalent training. Professional certificate as a maintenance specialist is an advantage
  • German (flowing) French (good knowledge): German as a mother tongue with good knowledge in French (min. B1)
  • Good knowledge of industrial maintenance
  • Mastery of SAP PM and Microsoft Office
  • Procedure, organizational talent and sense of responsibility. Flexibility, accuracy and independence
  • Good management of stress, ability to handle several tasks simultaneously. Good communication and negotiating skills and willingness to compromise

Cosa of friamo

  • Maternity leave / paternity leave: 18 weeks paid maternity leave / 8 weeks paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Occupational pension: Comprehensively secured in the Migros pension fund – with first-class and attractive services
  • Meals: Balanced lunch menu/buffet in the staff restaurant for max. CHF 7.-
  • Cumulus points: 2 times Cumulus points on food and 5 times on non-food items
  • Migros Bank: Migros grants higher interest rates on the private and investment account for its employees
  • Club School Migros: refund of 70% of the course costs up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and cultural offers: You can benefit from discounted tickets at selected cultural events
  • Sports and fitness offers: organisation of various sporting events with discounts
