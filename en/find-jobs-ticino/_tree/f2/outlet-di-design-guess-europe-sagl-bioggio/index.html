Full description

The Designer will be responsible for researching and interpreting emerging fashion and consumer trends, translating them into commercial products that reflect the Factory Outlet brand identity and meet customer expectations. By identifying new product opportunities and developing commercially relevant collections, the designer will play a key role in driving the evolution of the assortment while collaborating closely with Merchandising and Buying, and Product Development teams. According to their level of expertise, the designer will independently manage advanced design activities and contribute to strategic product decisions.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
