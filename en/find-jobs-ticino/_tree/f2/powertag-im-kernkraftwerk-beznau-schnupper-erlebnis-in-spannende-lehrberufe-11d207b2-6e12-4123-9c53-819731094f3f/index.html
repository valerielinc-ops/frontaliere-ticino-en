Powertag for students

Experience apprenticeships live at the Beznau nuclear power plant!

Data (on Wednesdays each): 18.11.2026 | 20.01.2027 | 03.03.2027 | 24.03.2027 Location: Nuclear power plant Beznau (KKB)

The Beznau nuclear power plant can be easily reached by public transport.

Are you in 1st or 2nd high school and would like to find out whether a technical or craft profession suits you?

During a varied day, you will get insights into three technical teaching professions at Axpo:

  • Automatic / in EFZ
  • Polymechanics/EFZ
  • Insulator/in EFZ

Together with our apprentices and vocational trainees, you will immerse yourself in practice, learn about typical work and tools and discover how versatile and fascinating technical professions are in a nuclear power plant.

In addition, short career inputs, application tips and a joint lunch break with our learners await you. An ideal opportunity to openly ask questions and learn directly from the everyday life of the learners.

Note

Please note that Powertag is not intended for students who are interested in apprenticeships starting in the summer of 2027. This day is especially aimed at students who are currently in the vocational exploration phase and would like to get a first insight into the teaching professions.
