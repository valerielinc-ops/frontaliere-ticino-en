Full description

The NPC is looking for a Psychologist responsible for outpatient services. Opportunity for leadership in a stimulating environment.

Tasks

  • Organize and develop the psychological care of the unit.
  • Provide supervision and training for professional teams.
  • Participate in defining clinical and organizational objectives.

Competences

  • Master in psychology and experience in psychotherapy required.
  • Essential relational and pedagogical skills.
  • Collaborative leadership and sense of planning required.

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The Department of Childhood and Adolescence is looking for an outpatient service at the Neuchâtel site:

Psychologist in charge of outpatient services in Littoral 60%

As a responsible psychologist, alongside the physician in charge of outpatient service, you play a central role in the quality, coherence and development of psychological and psychotherapeutic care within your unit, sector or sector. Both experienced clinician, supervisor and leader, you are actively contributing to the evolution of practice, professional training and clinical pilotage, in close cooperation with medical supervision.

As part of your function, you will be required to

Organization, leadership and development

  • Participate in the definition, implementation and monitoring of clinical and organizational objectives defined by the management of the NPC and the department.
  • Under medical delegation, conduct, organize and manage the service.
  • Plan activities to ensure quality of care, continuity of care and working conditions favourable to teams.
  • Carry out specific mandates and pilot projects.

Clinical activity

  • Develop and maintain direct and continuous clinical practice with patients.
  • Conduct clinical assessments, psychological reviews and therapeutic indications.
  • Conduct integrated psychotherapeutic treatments and counselling.
  • Work closely with the internal and external interdisciplinary network.
  • Participate in clinical reflection times, interdisciplinary meetings and departmental orientations.

Training, supervision and outreach

  • Actively participate in the post-graduate and continuing training of psychologists, doctors and other professionals in care.
  • Provide oversight and knowledge transfer roles.
  • To contribute, according to your interests, to the influence of the institution (publications, conferences, participation in professional companies or commissions).

The core competencies required are

  • Hold a master's degree in psychology or equivalent recognized at the federal level.
  • Post-graduate title in psychotherapy of a recognized orientation (for at least 3 years).
  • Experience confirmed for several years as a psychologist-psychotherapist.
  • Prior experience in supervision and training.
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss and cantonal health care system.
  • Use with the usual computer tools.
  • Collaborative leadership and ability to delegate.
  • Strategic vision and sense of planning.
  • Excellent relational, pedagogical and communication skills.
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis, creativity and adaptability.
  • A strong sense of ethics, discretion and teamwork.

We offer you

  • A role with high clinical, managerial and institutional added value.
  • A stimulating interdisciplinary environment at the heart of a recognised public institution.
  • The possibility of actively contributing to the development of mental health care, training and practices.
  • Working conditions governed by the applicable CTC, with attractive social benefits.
  • A privileged working environment in the canton of Neuchâtel, between lake and mountains.
