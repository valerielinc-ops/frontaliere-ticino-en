We are looking for you as Sales Advisor for the canton of Vaud.

Your role In this position, you advise our customers on the modernization and replacement of elevators.

Your concrete contribution

  • Preparation of bids and responses to bids
  • Responsible for pricing using approved tendering tools, and for optimizing prices and discounts within its authority
  • Negotiation and conclusion of contracts in accordance with KONE commercial policy
  • Responsible for achieving price and discount sales targets
  • Responsible for reporting in CRM tool and maintaining complete information on opportunities, tasks and visits
  • Transfer of projects to the project manager in accordance with internal processes
  • Guaranteeing customer satisfaction and resolution of complaints with the rest of the KONE team
  • Responsible for collecting relevant market and competition information on the ground
  • Development of its network through active communication on professional social networks

Value added

  • Basic training in commercial and/or technical fields
  • Successful experience of at least 5 years in the field of sales of technical solutions, ideally in the sale of elevators, or in the building/real estate sector
  • Important network of contacts in Romandie
  • Negotiating capacity and accountability
  • Faculty to achieve goals and stress resistance
  • Excellent presentation and entrepreneurial spirit
  • French mother tongue. Knowledge of English and/or German would be an asset
  • Good knowledge of computer tools and CRM tools type Salesforce
  • A good knowledge of digital and online networking tools
  • Valid driving licence required
  • Residence in the canton of Vaud compulsory

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
