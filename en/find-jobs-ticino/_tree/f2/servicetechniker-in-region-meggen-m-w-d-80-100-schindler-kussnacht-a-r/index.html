We are AS Elevators

AS Elevators is a Swiss-wide partner for the assembly, modernization and maintenance of elevators. The diversity and individualization of our products characterizes us. Be as unique as we are!

Experience the AS Spirit as

Service technician* in Meggen region (m/f/d) 80-100 %

Through your start at AS Elevators you benefit from an appreciative and family environment with a lot of team spirit. Thanks to your own business car, which you can also use privately, you are flexible and design your day according to your needs. Find out more about us here: Video

What to expect

  • Independent planning and execution of maintenance work according to specified cycles
  • Remediation of incidents
  • Execution of minor repairs
  • Interesting piquettes
  • Customer communications

What you bring

  • Completed apprenticeship in the mechanical, electrical or hydraulic sector (e.g. polymechanic, car mechanic, plant and apparatus manufacturer or electrical installer)
  • Some years of professional experience, ideally with customer contact
  • Impeccable manners and high willingness to learn
  • Good knowledge of the local language, other languages beneficial
  • Valid driver’s license – you can’t do without it

Your many advantages

  • 2-month training at full wage in our lift camp for your optimal preparation for your new job.
  • Own company vehicle, which you can also use privately.
  • Compensation for your lunchtime meal, fair on-call allowance for pikett and the cleaning of your work clothes, which of course will be provided by us free of charge.
  • Excellent development opportunities: Staff development is lived with us. We provide our employees with various national and international talent programs and support them temporarily and financially with training and further education.
  • Work-life balance: 40h week, 25 to 30 days of vacation, flexible working hours, part-time work and unpaid leave.
  • Healthy working environment: High health & safety standards and company health management including social counselling and health promotion.
  • Employee benefits: generous participation in the public transport and fitness subscription, fleet discounts, reduced purchase of Reka money, reduced insurance premiums and various benefits.
