We are looking for dedicated talents for training as a nursing specialist HF

You want to learn a profession with responsibility, perspective and real added value for society? Then the training for the nursing specialist HF is just the right step.

As a nursing specialist, you support patients in dealing with illness and crises. You accompany, advise and shape everyday life together with them. A resource-oriented approach is the focus.

Close cooperation in an interprof-essional team is a central part of your work. Quality care is based on the continuous reflection of your own actions and evidence-based practice. This means that current scientific findings are combined with clinical experience and personal skills.

Your Tasks

  • Implementation of the entire care process according to the level of training
  • Support for people with mental illness and inclusion of their social environment
  • Guidance and management of patient groups according to your level of education
  • Active participation in the interprofessionellen team

  • Experienced and qualified professional trainers
  • Individual learning support and support

  • Attractive development perspectives through a talent promotion model

You bring this with you
