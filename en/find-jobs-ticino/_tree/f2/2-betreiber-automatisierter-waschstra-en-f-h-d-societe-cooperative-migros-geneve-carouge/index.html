Full description

Every day, you play a key role in the proper functioning of our central kitchen. Thanks to your energy, your sense of organization and your team spirit, our food boxes arrive at the right time in production. You like to move, work in the field and see the concrete results of your work? Join us and become an essential link in an activity that is in full swing.

Mansioni

  • You pilot the automated washing line by ensuring its start, monitoring and shutdown. You control the quality of cleaning, detect anomalies and guarantee smooth operation throughout the day.
  • You arrange the order to wash the boxes according to the needs of the central kitchen. You adjust priorities, anticipate the volumes to be processed and ensure a continuous flow to avoid any supply disruption.
  • You feed the line and evacuate the clean pallets using handling equipment. You coordinate movements with storekeepers and storekeepers to maintain a steady and secure pace.
  • You check the condition of the boxes after washing and immediately isolate those with a defect. You ensure that only containers that meet quality requirements are released.
  • You perform the daily controls of the installation and perform the first level maintenance. You check the levels, filters and cleaning products to ensure optimal machine performance.
  • You work closely with production, logistics and quality teams. You exchange priorities, share useful information and help resolve unforeseen events quickly.

Required

  • You control logistics or industrial environments and use handling equipment with ease. You ideally have a recognized Carist A license or are ready to get it quickly.
  • You keep going when the pace accelerates. You can manage priorities, follow specific procedures and stay focused in a dynamic environment where every minute counts.
  • You appreciate teamwork and direct communication. You easily interact with different people, demonstrate reliability and help create effective collaboration on a daily basis.
  • 1 year in a similar environment
  • No initial/continuous training required
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.
  • Cumulus points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on assortments.
  • Food: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
