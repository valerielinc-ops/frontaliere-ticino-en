Full description

Join our team to help make every day unforgettable. You will play a key role in providing an exceptional shopping experience. Your passion for customer service and versatility will be your best assets to succeed in this challenging position. Get out of here!

Mansioni

  • You welcome, you listen and you advise customers.
  • You handle cash transactions and respond effectively and professionally to customer requests.
  • You manage the flow of the Subito zone so that the experiment is fluid and you perform random checks according to internal procedures.
  • You contribute to the right organization and keep the cash area clean like the sour zone.
  • You perform tasks related to the management of a caisse and the Subito zone with vigilance while being available to support other shelves in the store.
  • You work with the team to ensure seamless and transparent communication, ensuring an exceptional customer experience.

Required

  • 2-3 years of experience: in the field of retail with customer contact
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Retail Manager
  • Smile, versatility, patience and rigour
  • Excellent communication skills and natural ease in customer contacts
  • Customer orientation and solutions, great ability to keep calm, comfortable in a dynamic environment, stress resistance
  • French (Good Mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Food: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and without charge for a savings account.
