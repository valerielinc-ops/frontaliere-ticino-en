Full description

In this newly created function, you contribute significantly to the establishment of estate planning. You advise our customers competently and empathetically, optimize processes and actively contribute your expertise. We are looking for you: a committed personality who, with expertise and enthusiasm, develops sustainable solutions tailored to the needs of our customers.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Bank, Zurich branch - Seidengasse, Seidengasse 12, 8001 Zurich

Mansioni

  • You contribute to the further development and development of the estate planning and bring your ideas into offers, processes and cooperation with the customer consulting units
  • You will work in a newly created Swiss-wide estate team, share your expertise and support colleagues in technical issues
  • You support the planning and implementation of the goals in the estate business and provide suggestions on how to increase growth and efficiency
  • You advise and supervise discerning private clients on estate issues, create wills, marriage and inheritance contracts and, if necessary, lead will-execution mandates
  • As an important contact person for estate issues, you maintain contacts with internal and external partners such as lawyers, tax consultants and notaries
  • You support in the conception and implementation of professional training and further education for asset advisors and act as a speaker if necessary: in customer events

Requirements

  • min. 5 years: estate, inheritance planning
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Law
  • Several years of professional experience in estate planning, ideally with a financial service provider or in a comparable consulting function
  • Strong interest in the further development of the field and willingness to take responsibility in projects
  • Good conceptual skills in cooperation in the design of services and processes as well as a structured, solution-oriented and networked way of thinking
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation, high customer orientation and consulting flair as well as a high degree of integrity and discretion
  • Readiness to travel
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (advantageous)
  • Italian (advantageous)
