Full description

In Bad Zurzach, we treat patients with neurological diseases and musculoskeletal/rheumatological complaints as well as oncological or geriatric diseases. The aim of the interprofessional teams is to enable patients in the event of acute illness, chronic suffering or after an accident to return to everyday life and independence. This requires experience and expertise as well as commitment and empathy as well as great interest in interprofessional cooperation. We are looking forward to a music therapist who would like to participate with us.

Your tasks

  • Self-responsible and independent execution of music therapies, mainly with neurological patients, in individual and group setting
  • Implementation of music therapy in an outpatient setting (EMR recognition)
  • Participation in interdisciplinary reports and internal training

You bring with you

  • Completed training as an art therapist: in music therapy with industry certificate and HFP in prospect or MAS in clinical music therapy
  • Member of the Swiss Association for Music Therapy (SFMT)
  • Experience in the treatment of neurological patients, chronic pain patients and patients with psychosomatic disorders
  • High interest in rehabilitation setting and pleasure in interprofessional cooperation
  • Constructive, creative, pragmatic and solution-oriented approach
  • Teamwork, at the same time independence, flexibility and resilience
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German, further language skills beneficial

We offer you

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial working environment: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other perks at the FORTYSEVEN spa.

We look forward to your online application.
