Full description

Portrait Welcome to Meiringen. In the beautiful Haslital are located in Meiringen three primary school houses. At the Pfrundmatte location, located in the middle of the village near the train station, we each have 3 parallel multi-year classes (3x 1./2. Kl, 3x 3./4. Kl, 3x 5./6. Kl). The Meiringen School also includes 5 kindergartens, a day school and the upper secondary school. Further information can be found at www.schule-meiringen.ch We are warm, committed, lively and open - the team is looking forward to them!

Tasks

Are you looking for a deputy in the fourth quarter? Are you interested in a great class to work with a dedicated class team? We are looking for a substitute during maternity leave. At Schulhaus Pfrundmatte you will teach the 1st / 2nd class on Monday and Tuesday and work closely with the job partner (Mi-Fr) and the curative teacher. The existing step team of the Meiringen School will make it easier for you to get started and enable an enriching cooperation. You will also be well embedded in the College.

Requirements

You have a recognised level diploma for the primary level or are on the way to it. They are a dutiful, communicative and resilient personality and are accustomed to dealing with children and parents. Other important aspects: clear and open communication, humor, resilience, structured yet flexible working methods.

We offer

  • A good school infrastructure with enough space
  • a well-established school house team where you can actively participate
  • School management and colleagues who are helpful
  • A community that supports and supports the school
  • Good connection to public transport
