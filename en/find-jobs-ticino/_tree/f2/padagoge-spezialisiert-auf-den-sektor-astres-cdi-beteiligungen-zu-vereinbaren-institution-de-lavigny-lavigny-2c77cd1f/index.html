We are looking for one

Educator specialized for the sector "Astres" CDI - Participation rates to be agreed

At the Lavigny Institution, on the Astres Sector, we accompany adults with autism spectrum disorders (ASD) associated with complex behavioural disorders on a daily basis. The Astres sector consists of 3 places of life. Their reality is to accompany men and women between the ages of 18 and 80 years living with intellectual disabilities and predominant behavioural disorders.

Your mission

  • Work in human-sized places of life.
  • Decode behaviour from complex profiles to better understand needs.
  • Create secure benchmarks and use appropriate communication tools (PECS, CAA).
  • Accurately accompany, between structuring and benevolent settings.
  • Show patience, creativity and consistency in dealing with situations that are sometimes intense.
  • Live days combining support in the acts of daily life with supervised activities, thought for each and conducted over the long term.
  • To ensure the physical and psychological well-being of the residents, to pay attention to their state of health and to intervene quickly if necessary (nurse, general practitioner and psychiatrist present in the Institution).
  • To be a trusted reference, able to understand the emotions behind the acts.
  • Build and adapt customized individual projects.
  • Work closely with an experienced multidisciplinary team.
  • Encourage and maintain good relations with families and legal representatives.

Your profile

  • Training of specialist teachers at the ES or HES level in the field of special education or social services.
  • ESA training or equivalent.
  • Analytical skills developed for fine-tuning complex situations and adapting intervention strategies
  • Experience or strong skills to accompany complex profiles.
  • Trained or sensitized to approaches: ABA, TEACCH, PECS, CAA (an asset).
  • Ready to learn, share and evolve with the team and welcomed people.

We offer

  • Working conditions agreed by the Commission for the para-public social sector
  • A salary in line with the function and in line with the rates of the Vaudian parapublic sector's CLCs, with mobility-related benefits (parking, participation in public transport costs)
  • In-service training and specialized supervision (including TSA and challenge behaviour), internal mobility, possible funding of diplomas and certifications.
  • A business restaurant offering varied meals at preferential rates, in a pleasant business environment encouraging exchanges.
  • A wide choice of sports and relaxation activities (yoga, fitness, aquagym, pilates etc.) as well as massages, while enjoying regular business life events
  • Flexible and continuous schedules across sectors, with the possibility of part-time work for optimal work-life balance
