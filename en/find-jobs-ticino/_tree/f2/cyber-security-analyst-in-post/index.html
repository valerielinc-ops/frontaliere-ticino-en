Full description

Are you interested in cyber security and would like to actively contribute to the protection of one of the largest IT landscapes in Switzerland? Are you enthusiastic about the analysis of vulnerabilities and the further development of modern security processes? Swiss Post operates a national critical infrastructure and one of the largest computer networks in Switzerland. With you, we make our systems even safer and further develop our vulnerability management.

You can do that.

  • You will analyze and evaluate weaknesses in our IT systems and coordinate the necessary measures.
  • In this role, you will continue to develop our vulnerability management and automate recurring tasks, making the most of your expertise.
  • You investigate security events and get to the bottom of any abnormalities and potential attacks.
  • In addition, you move safely on different platforms, use the command line and work with tools such as Splunk and Python, which brings your technical know-how specifically.
  • Working with technical teams and other stakeholders is part of your everyday life, communicating security topics in an understandable and practical manner.
  • You actively contribute your ideas and help us to continuously improve Swiss Post’s cyber security, which makes an important contribution.

You bring that with you

  • You have an education or training in computer science and are enthusiastic about cyber security.
  • Several years of experience in operational IT security or vulnerability management distinguish you.
  • You think analytically, work structured and know relevant security standards and best practices.
  • In addition, you bring experience with Python and Splunk
  • Security certifications such as GIAC are a plus, which further strengthens your profile.
  • You keep an overview even in challenging situations and communicate safely with technical and non-technical contacts: inside.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English, which rounds off your profile.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
