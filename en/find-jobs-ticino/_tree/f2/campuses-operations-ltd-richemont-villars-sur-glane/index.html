Reference code: JR132789

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

Your Main Mission

As Campus Operations Supervisor, you will play a key role in optimizing and operating the daily operations of our Villars-sur-Glâne site. You will actively contribute to the creation of inter-entity synergies, the continuous improvement of processes and the implementation of decision support tools based on relevant performance indicators. You will also ensure the rigorous application of procurement compliance policies and provide essential administrative support to the department, including data management and compliance.

Your Key Responsibilities

Operational Support and Decision Support

Provide critical operational support to ensure the smooth functioning and smooth running of campus day-to-day operations.

Reduce the workload of existing teams by implementing streamlined processes.

Collect and analyze various data to ensure effective management of the department.

Propose and implement monitoring and data collection tools (e.g. security, real estate, energy, cleaning) for the Villars-sur-Glâne site.

Create and manage Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) relevant to assess performance, identify bottlenecks and justify strategic adjustments.

Provide fundamental administrative support to the department, ensuring proper performance of routine tasks and document management.

Actively assist in the design and implementation of scalable and sustainable solutions for data management and IT compliance, including contribution to the data system architecture and the definition of collection and analysis processes.

Organize the distribution of mail on the site, propose and implement optimization solutions to reconcile quality of service, capitalization and traceability.

Compliance Procurement Compliance

Implement and enforce the Procurement Compliance Policy.

Define clear rules, integrate them into systems and train relevant teams.

Actively monitor procurement processes, correct gaps and engage stakeholders.

Ensure a systematic process of tendering and comparison of contract prices for all contracts and purchases in the department (BOS - Building Operations Services), covering a wide range of goods and services (e.g. cleaning contracts, civil engineering, miscellaneous supplies).
