  • Move Switzerland with us. Shift manager: in Olten paint shop, May 1st, 2026, 60-100% Apply now ## You can look forward to this. The Olten plant is the SBB's largest maintenance location.   • As a shift manager, you are responsible for the technical management of around 12 employees and coordinate with your colleagues the activities for the employees' shift (no night work, but late shift). You ensure adherence to deadlines, quality and safety.
  • In day-to-day business you will work operationally with the team. You sand, blast, fill, prime and paint our rolling stock and work with various 2K water- and solvent-based paint systems using AirMix and conventional painting processes.
  • As a shift manager, you are the first point of contact for questions about execution, quality and safety for employees on the shift.
  • In addition, you promote and support the further development of the team through consultation with the supervisor. This requires an open feedback culture.
  • In addition to your role as shift leader, you enjoy working on projects and supporting the team leader in recruiting new employees. This is what you bring with you.
  • As a team player, you find it easy to deal with people and you can both lead and inspire others. You think in a networked manner and keep an eye on the big picture. With your confident appearance, you also convince critical voices and persistently look for common solutions, which you then implement.
  • Due to your high level of flexibility, you are used to moving in an agile working environment and adapting to changing conditions.
  • Your holistic entrepreneurial thinking coupled with your flair for interpersonal relationships makes it easier for you to coach your shift employees and adhere to deadlines, quality and safety.
  • You have completed a 4-year apprenticeship as a car painter EFZ, industrial painter EFZ or car body painter EFZ. Initial management experience in an industrial environment is an advantage.
  • You are fluent in the German language (at least C1) and are used to working with common Microsoft Office applications on a daily basis. ## Your salary and your benefits.
