Real Estate Accountant \- Monthey

On behalf of our client, a recognized real estate player in Valais, we are looking for a real estate accountant to strengthen his team based in Monthey. Take over as soon as possible.

Your responsibilities

  • \- Independent management of the accounting of a stewardship portfolio of buildings.
  • \- Processing of current invoices, payments and accounting operations.
  • \- Preparation of accounts for management, heating and ancillary costs.

  • Participation in quarterly and annual closures.
  • \- Accounting management of takeovers and mandate purposes.

Your profile

  • \- Accounting training, federal patent an asset.

  • \- Rigorous, organized personality with a good analytical spirit.
  • \- Self-reliance and sense of teamwork.

  • \- A dynamic and collaborative professional environment.
