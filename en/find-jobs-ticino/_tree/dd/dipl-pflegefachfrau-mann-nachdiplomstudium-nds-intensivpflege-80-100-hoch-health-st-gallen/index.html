Full description

Dipl. Nursing specialist after diploma (NDS) Intensive care 80-100 %

Clinic for Intensive Care Medicine

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Comprehensive care, monitoring and care of our patients within the framework of the competencies acquired during the NDS
  • Participation in diagnostic and therapeutic measures within and on intra-clinical transport outside the department
  • Working in an interprofessional team with medical and therapeutic services
  • Informing and caring for relatives and caregivers
  • Work in three shifts

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF / FH
  • Willingness and commitment to continuously develop and work independently in an interdisciplinary team
  • Empathic attitude, resilience, flexibility, team spirit and humor
  • Enthusiasm for working with people

Our offer to you

  • Shift compensation and self-study days for relief in shift work and during study
  • Individual support during the extra-occupational post-graduate course for intensive care
  • Exciting and broad field of activity in intensive care in an A-clinic
  • Working on a new, modern intensive care unit in the new building House 7a
  • Various offers for employees in the field of health promotion, job and family as well as mobility

Your application

  • Entry as of 1 January 2027 or by agreement
  • Learn more about this exciting job from Ursula Betschart, Head of Care Intensive Care, telephone +41 71 494 26 58
  • For questions about the application process, please contact Lorena Cicia, HR Business Partner, telephone +41 71 494 38 50
