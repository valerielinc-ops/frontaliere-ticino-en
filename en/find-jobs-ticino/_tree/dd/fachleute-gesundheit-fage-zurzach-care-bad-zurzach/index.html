Full description

The team consists of 13 employees who supervise 24 beds in the disciplines MSK and INT/Angio. Thanks to the area care, we guarantee both individual support and holistic and needs-based care for our patients. As a team, we meet at eye level. Everyone takes responsibility for their own tasks, and mutual support is a matter of course for us. We are a team that likes to laugh together, communicate openly and respectfully and attach great importance to a positive working environment.

Your tasks

  • Patient-oriented care: You accompany our patients with your expertise and support them in their recovery
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: In our interprofessional team, you work closely with other nurses, therapists and doctors. Your expertise is highly appreciated!
  • Diversity: You enjoy exciting challenges and continuous learning opportunities. Through the variety of clinical pictures you gain valuable experience and can constantly expand your knowledge

Your profile

  • Training: Completed vocational training as a specialist / health professional (FaGe) or equivalent training with at least one pos. SRK-PreCheck
  • Personality: You are an enthusiastic and communicative personality with a sense of humor. You convince through a sense of responsibility and high-quality work and can set priorities effectively

What we offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial working environment: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Flexibility bonus: Voluntary participation in our internal care pool for additional shifts, which are also rewarded attractively.
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other perks at the FORTYSEVEN spa.

We look forward to your online application.
