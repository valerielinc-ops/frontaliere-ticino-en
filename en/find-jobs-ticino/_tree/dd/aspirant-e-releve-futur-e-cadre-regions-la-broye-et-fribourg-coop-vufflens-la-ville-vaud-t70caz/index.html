Mission

  • Provide customers with the best possible shopping experience through optimal management of the availability, presentation and promotion of goods.
  • Direct and motivate employees by ensuring that key key figures such as net proceeds, liquidations, staff costs, waste and inventory differences are kept for the assigned area.
  • In addition, follow the guidelines for quality and presentation at the POS: freshness, quality, hygiene, temperatures, expiry dates.
  • Also provide direction to the point of sale in the absence of the manager.

Qualifications

  • Experience in a comparable function with driving tasks

  • Flexibility in terms of working hours
  • Independent and rigorous work, ease of handling computer tools
  • Perfect mastery of written and spoken French

  • Sustainable development: A commitment to man and animals and nature with more than 400 acts
  • Communication: Internal app dedicated to staff
