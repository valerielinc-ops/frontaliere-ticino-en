Full description

The Tarchini Group seeks a new resource to strengthen its staff and support the Group's growth. The Tarchini Group operates in Ticino in the design, construction and management of industrial, commercial, logistics and residential buildings and operates FoxTown Factory Stores Mendrisio.

THE SEARCH POSITION IS

ADMINISTRATIVE/ CONTACT

The person selected will mainly deal with the active cycle of billing and administrative management of lease contracts.

Main handling • Registration and administrative management of lease contracts, with the provision of the relevant adjustments • Management of active billing, with periodic issue of invoices and payment policies for tenants • Control of cash and open positions, with payment reminders management • Management of relations with tenants on administrative and billing aspects • Secretariat and support for general administrative activities

The candidate will join a dynamic and motivated team of people and will respond directly to the Administrative Manager.

Requirements

• Age: 25–35 years • At least 5 years of experience in a similar role with companies based in Switzerland; preferable in the real estate sector, in particular in the administrative management of lease contracts • Training in accounting • Good knowledge of Swiss administrative, accounting and tax system • Good knowledge of written and spoken English • Good knowledge of the common IT applications for administrative, accounting and billing activities • Good communication skills, precision, reliability and professionalism • Ability to work in teams and manage administrative and billing relationships with tenants

It offers • Young, dynamic and diversified working environment • Remuneration for role, experience and skills • Possibility of professional growth within the Group

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
