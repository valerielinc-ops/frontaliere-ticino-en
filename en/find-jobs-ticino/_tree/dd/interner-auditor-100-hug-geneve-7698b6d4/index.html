Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 trades, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Internal Audit Directorate (IAD) performs two categories of tasks: assurance missions (audit missions) and advisory missions. In the course of its assurance tasks, the Internal Audit Directorate shall provide the Audit Committee with an audit report including an objective evaluation of an entity, process, system or other area for the purpose of formulating an opinion or conclusion. It brings added value to the Institution by taking into account strategy, objectives and risks, proposes ways to improve governance, risk management and control processes, and objectively provides relevant assurance. The extent and nature of the work shall be determined by this Directorate. The advisory missions aim to improve the functioning of the HUGs. They concern institutional governance, risk management and internal control processes. When carrying out an advisory mission, the internal auditor shall be objective and shall not assume any managerial responsibility for the activity concerned or interfere with its prerogatives. As Chair of the Audit Committee, the Internal Audit Directorate is responsible for the development of internal audit policies, definition and overall content of its audit and advisory activities. It shall carry out the annual planning of the audit missions, which shall require the approval of the Audit Committee, their implementation and the coordination of the work relating to those missions and the specific missions decided by or with the endorsement of the Audit Committee. It ensures that processes are independently and systematically audited to assess their effectiveness against economic objectives and risks.

It shall regularly inform the Audit Committee of the progress of the current audits in accordance with the time limits laid down. The Internal Audit Directorate shall prepare the annual activity report which shall be submitted to the Audit Committee and, after review and approval, communicated to the Executive Board. It coordinates the risk management activity and the internal control system, the implementation of which is the responsibility of the focal points and focal points of departments and directorates.

Description of post

The IAD is looking for an internal auditor to complete its team of 6 internal auditors. In this context, your main tasks are to plan, conduct and document audit missions according to the defined methodology. You identify and analyze risks and evaluate the internal control system put in place by the HUGs to limit them. You prepare relevant, clear and succinct audit reports with recommendations that add value and then follow up on recommendations made. You regularly inform the Director of the IAD about the course of your activities and the important facts found. You clearly and succinctly present the reports and findings to the Director of the IAD and to those responsible for the areas audited. In addition, you are an internal reference person for any questions, information, documentation related to institutional procedures and guidelines and to the cantonal or federal legal framework. Finally, you help simplify internal processes by promoting collective intelligence. You have an interest in hospital management in a university hospital and want to participate directly in the continuous improvement of patient care and quality of care.

Qualifications

You have a higher professional and/or academic background, such as a Master's degree in Economics, an accounting degree, or an equivalent recognized diploma. You benefit from a minimum of 5 years' experience in the field of financial risk audit or in a related analysis and control function (e.g., procurement, suppliers, accounting reviews, billing, bidding, logistics or property management) during which you conducted audit missions independently. The CIA (Certified Internal Auditor) and/or CISA (Certified Information Systems Auditor) certification are required. Competencies in the areas of cybersecurity, information system governance and data protection, as well as experience in auditing IT projects, digital transformations and continuity of critical systems and operational resilience are assets. You demonstrate a perfect mastery of general accounting; experience in cost accounting is an asset. Holding the ECDL Advanced Certificate on Word, Excel (dynamic cross tables, macros, automated calculations, planning, graphics, databases...) and Powerpoint is an asset. You have a great ability to manage stress and meet tight deadlines, while ensuring quality services. Your excellent ability to work as a team allows you to work closely with auditors and health professionals. A dynamic and methodical person, you demonstrate a spirit of analysis and synthesis to deal with complex and constantly evolving situations. Organization, rigour and a sense of detail are essential skills to succeed in the audit missions and other activities entrusted to you. Your relational ease and persuasiveness facilitate the interactions you have with the various services of the institution. You are able to manage multiple tasks simultaneously (priority management, prioritization of requests, flexibility). Very good French communication skills, both oral and written, are required, as well as knowledge of German or English. Your teamwork and ease in integrating a new environment are essential to joining the IAD institution and team.
