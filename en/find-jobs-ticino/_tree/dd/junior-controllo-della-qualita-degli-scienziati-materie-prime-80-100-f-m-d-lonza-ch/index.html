We are looking for a committed person for the position of (Junior) Scientist (f/m/d) Quality Control, Raw Materials . In this role, you will be instrumental in ensuring the quality of raw materials. They support the development and validation of analytical methods and carry out comprehensive quality controls.

What you will do

  • Perform quality controls by certificates on raw materials according to established SOPs and specifications
  • Collaboration in troubleshooting activities
  • Collaboration in the processing of deviations, OOS results and CAPAs
  • Ensuring compliance with GMP and GLP guidelines as well as internal quality standards
  • Active participation in the continuous improvement of processes and procedures in the team

What we are looking for

  • Academic degree in Biochemistry/ Biology/ Chemistry/ Pharmacy or a comparable scientific discipline
  • Initial professional experience in the field of quality control, preferably in the pharmaceutical or biotechnology industry (for the junior position - a corresponding academic experience is also sufficient)
  • Sound knowledge of common analytical methods (e.g. HPLC, GC, spectroscopy, ELISA, SDS-PAGE)
  • Familiarity with GMP and GLP regulations is an advantage
  • Fluent English is required, good German language skills are preferred
  • Strong ability to work in a team, initiative and careful way of working
