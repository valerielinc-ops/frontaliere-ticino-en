Security Assisting Security Lodge & Control Service 30–50 %

For assignments in the Canton of Aargau or in the Birr region, we are looking for service-oriented, communicative and team-capable employees to strengthen our team.

You can move that with us.

In this function, you ensure security and order in various areas: Area Security Lodge:

  • Access control for persons and vehicles
  • Professional intervention in emergencies
  • Offering of different intervention groups in the event of disturbance or occurrence
  • Monitoring via CCTV system
  • Visitors and parking management
  • Processing and control of suppliers

Control service area

  • Control round throughout the area
  • Closure and opening of buildings
  • Checking accesses, interior doors, windows, lighting, appliances, machines, heaters, refrigerators, vehicle parks, etc.
  • Detection, correction or reporting of fire hazards, sources of fire, liquid or gas leakage
  • Pay attention to unusual, suspicious noises (e.g. water noise, running motors, etc.)

That's what makes you.

  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German (mandatory)
  • Good oral and written English skills (mandatory)
  • Good physical and mental condition (mandatory)
  • Willingness to work irregularly during shifts and for night and weekend assignments
  • Flexible, sporty active and reliable personality with a high degree of safety thinking
  • Confidentiality
  • Advantage: First aid training with Defi.
  • Advantage: Security experience and training
  • Advantage: Cat B driving license and own vehicle
  • Correct reputation (criminal record and eviction not older than 3 months must be attached to the application)
  • Swiss Citizen or Authorization B/C
  • Good PC/MS Office user knowledge (mandatory)

That's what we offer you.

  • Workload between 30% and 50% possible
  • Timely and marketable salary
  • Serious introduction / instruction to your tasks
  • Paid education and training
  • Workwear
