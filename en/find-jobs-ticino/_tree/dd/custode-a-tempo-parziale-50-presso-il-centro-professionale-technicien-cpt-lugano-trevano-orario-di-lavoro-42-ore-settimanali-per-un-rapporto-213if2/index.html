Full description

The following is a list of tasks and responsibilities of the position:

  • managing auditorium bookings
  • overseeing event management (audio, lights, projections, etc.)
  • setting up the stage according to requests
  • performing minor maintenance tasks
  • availability for site visits
  • managing extraordinary and urgent maintenance interventions, also on other buildings in the sector, in collaboration with the logistics service
  • collaborating, when necessary, with the CPT Trevano custodians, taking into account their availability and in the absence of assignments at their location
  • the function involves an activity carried out mainly in autonomy; however, a constant comparison and coordination with the CPT colleagues is required, to whom the person will have to refer and report on the activities carried out ## Requirements
  • Federal Certificate of Competence (AFC) as a Building and Infrastructure Operator, or Federal Certificate of Competence (AFC) as a Multimedia Technician, or Federal Certificate of Competence (AFC) as an Electrician
  • ability to work independently
  • high sense of responsibility, flexibility, attitude to independent and team work
  • basic knowledge in the field of maintenance and administration to manage relationships with the Logistics Section, external companies, etc.
  • knowledge and use of computer tools and main applications
  • driving license (category B) ## Particular Observations
