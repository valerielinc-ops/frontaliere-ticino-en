Numbers under control, people in focus - does that sound like your next challenge? ####

At an innovative technology company for demanding industrial projects, you will assume overall responsibility for finance, controlling, HR and administration as part of the management team.

The main tasks ####

  • Overall responsibility for finance, controlling, HR and administration
  • Co-shaping of strategic and operational corporate development
  • Direct management of accounting, controlling and liquidity planning
  • Professional and disciplinary management of several teams
  • Driver role in digitalization and AI deployment

The requirement profile ####

  • Completed business studies or eidg. Specialist card
  • Several years of management experience in the commercial sector of an SME
  • In-depth knowledge in accounting (OR, Swiss GAAP FER), controlling and liquidity planning as well as experience in personnel administration
  • Stylistic German, English is an advantage
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, operational involvement and communication strength

This is what awaits you ####

Exciting projects, a lot of creative freedom and a collegial team await you. With modern work equipment and flexible working conditions, you have the opportunity to actively develop finance, controlling, HR and administration and to shape the company strategically.
