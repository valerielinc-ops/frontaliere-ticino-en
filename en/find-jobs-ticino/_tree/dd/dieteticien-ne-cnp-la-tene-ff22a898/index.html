Full description

Mission

  • The CNP is responsible for the implementation of the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions.
  • The CNP aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated and community psychiatry model.
  • It implements public policies for the promotion and prevention of mental health.
  • It acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research.
