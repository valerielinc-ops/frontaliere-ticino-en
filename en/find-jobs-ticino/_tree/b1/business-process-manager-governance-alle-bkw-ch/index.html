Full description

From process management to governance to organizational development: At the growing BKW AEK Contracting AG you bring different topics and people together. You create clarity, develop processes further and make changes in everyday life effective.

Your field of activity

  • You develop and control the Integrated Management System (IMS) and ensure that processes, guidelines and standards work in everyday work
  • Cross-divisional end-to-end processes you analyze and improve together with the departments and create clarity about responsibilities and interfaces
  • In cooperation with the BKW specialist offices, you coordinate compliance, regulatory and data protection requirements and develop practical solutions
  • In risk, control and audit processes, you keep an overview and ensure that agreed measures are consistently implemented.
  • The management supports you in strategy implementation, cross-divisional initiatives and the management of measures and key figures
  • You accompany changes from planning to implementation and ensure that new working methods are anchored in the specialist areas in the long term.

You bring that with you

  • You have a higher education in business administration, business informatics, organizational development or a comparable field of expertise
  • Several years of experience in process management and in the cross-divisional improvement of processes you already bring with you
  • Ideally, you will be familiar with governance and compliance requirements and will be able to translate them into workable solutions.
  • Thanks to your experience in change or project management, you will succeed in bringing together different interests and driving change
  • You quickly recognize complex relationships, structure them in a comprehensible way and communicate clearly and addressable
  • With your own initiative, persuasion and assertiveness, you ensure that agreed standards and improvements are actually implemented.
