Full description

We bring together what belongs together!

The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people -- now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company?

From now on or by agreement we are looking for a permanent job with 1 - 3 years of experience as a construction machinery mechanic

Your challenge

  • Adaptations: conversion of machinery, manufacture and assembly of spare parts or special components
  • Maintenance and Diagnostics: Regular inspection of motors, hydraulic systems, electrics and electronics with modern diagnostic equipment.
  • Repairs: repair of defects directly in the workshop or on site at the construction site using service buses

This is what you bring with you

  • 1 - 3 years of experience as a construction machine mechanic
  • High performance and flexibility
  • Punctuality and reliability
  • Independent, reliable and exact working method

What we offer you
