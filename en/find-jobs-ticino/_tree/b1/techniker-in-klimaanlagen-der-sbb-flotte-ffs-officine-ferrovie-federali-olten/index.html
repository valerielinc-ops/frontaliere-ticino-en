  • As a technician: in air conditioning systems of the SBB fleet, you are actively involved in the repair, revision and testing of air conditioning systems for our fleets. This ensures that our customers can travel with the highest possible comfort. You also contribute to a good climate, especially on the train.
  • Independently, you operate the climate testers to carry out input and output tests as well as diagnoses on the air conditioners.
  • You are responsible for the refrigeration procedures of the air conditioners and perform them with high efficiency and quality.
  • With your extensive know-how, it gives you pleasure to support the team.

You bring that with you.

  • In our industry, teamwork is the key to success. You are an absolute team player with the ability to work interdisciplinary.

  • Very good technical understanding and experience in processing in an industrial production plant are advantageous.
  • You have basic training in the mechanical or electromechanical field with EFZ degree. Training such as refrigeration system installation: in EFZ or the like are of great advantage.
