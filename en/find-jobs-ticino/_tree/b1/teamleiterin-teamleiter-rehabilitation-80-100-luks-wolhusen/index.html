Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for 01. November 2026 or by agreement reinforcement. Tasks: • The management of rehabilitation will be handed over to the next generation. As a team leader, you take on the professional and organizational co-responsibility in your field of rehabilitation. They support the department management in the management of about 20 employees. Working together with the department management and the entire nursing management team In accordance with the service mandate and targets, taking into account the care models and care diagnoses applied in the LUKS • They are jointly responsible for the implementation of efficient, effective and appropriate care taking into account the framework conditions. Through your far-sighted and reflective personality, you actively participate in various projects and are involved in their implementation. • They are responsible for competent and patient-oriented care of our patients, provide assignments in direct care in all shifts and support employees in their professional development. • A state-of-the-art clinic information system, which maps the entire patient process digitally and comprehensively and lists all patient data at any time and up-to-date, supports you around the clock. Requirements • They have completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH and have several years of professional experience, preferably in the rehabilitative field. In addition, at best, you already have leadership experience at the level of team leadership or in another function. • They are characterized by a committed, reliable and exact working method, gladly take on the responsibility and are an organizational talent. You are a communicative and open personality and enjoy working in a team. • You meet your fellow human beings with appreciation and empathy and keep a calm head even in hectic situations, in dealing with patients and relatives you prove a high level of social and professional competence. We offer: • You can expect a responsible and varied job with diverse tasks in an innovative working environment as well as a great cooperation in a committed, dynamic and interprofessional team. • We offer you the opportunity to work independently and to lead directly subordinate employees in cooperation with the department management. A modern understanding of leadership, the du-culture as well as a close interdisciplinary cooperation are a matter of course for us. • A progressive hospital awaits you, which will take a big step towards the future with its strategic orientation towards Lean Hospital and the new hospital building, while you can expect a varied and challenging activity. • In addition to the beautiful place of work in nature, you benefit from very good social benefits as well as an active promotion of your personal training with a targeted and individual competence development.
