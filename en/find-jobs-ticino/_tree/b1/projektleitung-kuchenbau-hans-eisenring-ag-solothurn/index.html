Companies … Job Portal Project manager kitchen construction Project manager kitchen construction In this function, you take over the overall management of kitchen projects and look after business and private customers. They organize the construction site including measurement and construction progress checks and are responsible for deadlines and budget. In addition, you monitor the assembly quality and take the kitchens together with the customer, including defect management and complaints. They hold meetings with the project participants and are present at customer events. You are a good fit for this task if you have training as a carpenter or carpenter EFZ or a comparable technical training and have experience in the kitchen or furniture industry as well as on construction sites. You have already managed projects or construction sites and work in a structured and cost-conscious manner. The most important in short Place of work Regions Aargau, Zurich, Basel, Solothurn Pensum 100 %

Responsibilities

  • Overall management of holistic kitchen projects
  • Communication and support of business and private customers
  • Responsibility for meeting deadlines and budget
  • Management of the construction site organization incl. Mass recordings and construction progress checks
  • Monitoring of assembly quality
  • Acceptance of kitchens with customers as well as management of defect management and handling of complaints
  • Holding meetings with project participants
  • Participation in customer events

What you bring

  • Completed training as carpenter EFZ or comparable technical training, ideally with business training
  • Experience in the kitchen or furniture industry as well as proven construction site experience
  • Project management knowledge and proven experience in managing projects/construction sites
  • Strong assertive maker personality, reflected, down-to-earth, networked thinking
  • Strong analysis, communication and team skills as well as sales talent
  • Structured, goal-oriented working method and a mere, cost-conscious action
  • Good IT affinity
