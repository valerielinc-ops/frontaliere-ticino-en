[Chubb Sicli SA]( )

Technician (100%) French\-German

Chubb Sicli, a subsidiary of the APi Group, has been operating in Switzerland since 1923, in the sale, installation and maintenance of high quality products in the fields of fire protection, firefighting, evacuation and electronic security.

We are looking for a motivated and reliable person for our Romandie East agency:

Contract Type: Permanent Contract

Onward: to be agreed

Intervention sector: Region of Sion and Upper Valais

Are you looking for a new challenge?

We offer you a great opportunity with an interesting and varied activity in a group structure. A professional introduction will be provided by qualified colleagues.

The role of technician is to install fire extinguishers, signage or other small fire protection equipment, as well as to maintain the annual fire stations with our various customers and the sale of our various products during the interventions.

Very autonomously, you are responsible for the following tasks:

  • Verification and maintenance of fire extinguishers and fire stations.
  • Replacement and sale of fire extinguishers.
  • Verification and maintenance of safety lights.
  • Sale of various small security equipment.
  • Make appointments clients.

Qualifications and experience required

  • SWC in a technical trade or training deemed equivalent.
  • Experience in a technical function is an asset.
  • Very good command of French, both oral and written (minimum level C1) and

mastery of German compulsory level B2 minimum.

  • Driver's licence B required.

Your profile

  • Comfortable with manual work, assembly and use of tools
  • Autonomous, dynamic and field oriented.
  • Reliable, rigorous and conscientious.
  • Organized, with a common sense of priorities.
  • Team spirit and relationship ease.
  • At ease with travel within a large work area

What does Chubb SICLI offer?

  • Quality training provided by qualified professionals.
  • An opportunity to take leave without pay in addition to vacation.
  • An advantageous contingency plan.
  • A pleasant working climate based on trust.
  • A corporate culture that places people and the development of employees at the heart of its priorities.

Please submit your full file.

Our culture and what we offer you

Our five corporate values are the foundation of our corporate culture: we protect people by placing their safety at the centre of our concerns. We anticipate and actively shape the future. We identify with our work and take responsibility for our actions. We meet the challenges with motivation and commitment. These values guide our conduct, foster innovation and collaboration, and help us move forward continuously.

From the beginning, we support you with targeted training and personalized integration to allow you to get started in your new functions. With us, many professional and personal development opportunities await you throughout your career.

Do you want to make the world safer by our side? Then join our team!
