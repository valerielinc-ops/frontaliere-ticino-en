Full description

We are looking for a committed, analytical and design-oriented personality who will actively develop the future of cross-border management together with us. If you would like to take on responsibility, structure complex issues and achieve real impact in an international environment, you are in the right place! What are your tasks:

  • Ensuring and continuously developing a holistic, effective cross-border framework (including methods, processes and reporting, using targeted automation and AI technologies)
  • Responsibility for planning, coordinating and implementing challenging cross-border projects with high visibility
  • Central office for cross-border issues: consulting, coordination and close cooperation with Relationship Manager, sparring partner for market heads and senior management
  • Analysis, development and further development of cross-border relevant guidelines, decision-making bases and reports involving AI-supported analysis and reporting tools
  • Active co-design and further development of cross-border governance, process and operating models
  • Cooperation and content support in internal audits and regulatory audits What do we expect?
  • Completed university or university of applied sciences (economics, law, finance or similar)
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of cross-border in the financial industry, mandatory in the "First Line of Defense" in the private client environment, consulting experience is a plus
  • In-depth knowledge of project and stakeholder management combined with excellent communication skills in German and English
  • Very good knowledge of the (front) core processes of a bank as well as relevant regulatory national and international topics (e.g. CRD VI, MiFID II, FIDLEG, GWG)
  • Specific expertise in operational risks, key risk indicators, risk & control self-assessment as well as testing and documentation of controls
  • Proactive, resilient, self-confident personality with an analytical, pragmatic and result-oriented mindset as well as a pronounced change and development mentality What we offer:
  • A technically challenging and future-oriented working environment with complex issues, modern technology and a strong focus on innovation
  • A dynamic, international environment with room for manoeuvre that requires flexibility while pursuing clear long-term goals
  • The opportunity to gain end-to-end perspectives on client management and actively shape processes and governance
  • High personal responsibility, visibility and numerous opportunities for professional and personal development, We are looking for talented people and not just a CV that meets all the criteria. Even if you don’t bring everything we ask of you, you should apply. At Vontobel, you are working on a better future for our customers, society and yourself.
