Full description

We are building a Digital Eye Clinic (DEC) at the Eye Clinic – with the aim of combining clinical care, research and artificial intelligence. The focus is on the development and clinical validation of AI-based applications for ophthalmological diseases, the use of multimodal imaging and clinical data and the development of new digital biomarkers. The DEC is intended to transfer innovative technologies from research into everyday clinical practice and to create a scalable platform for digital ophthalmology in the long term. For this construction we are looking for a committed personality for a limited period of 1 year with an option for extension.

  • Overall coordination and operational development of the Digital Eye Clinic
  • Control of interdisciplinary projects at the interface of medicine, artificial intelligence, clinical research, IT and digitalization
  • Accompaniment of AI-based projects from idea and conception to development and validation to clinical implementation
  • Coordination of cooperation with doctors, data scientists, AI/software developers, IT, data protection, legal service and external partners
  • Development and implementation of clinically relevant AI and digital health projects
  • Support in the clinical evaluation and validation of new AI-based applications
  • Identification of new scientific and technological developments and transfer into concrete projects
  • Collaboration in scientific publications and preparation of research, grant and third-party applications
  • Establishment and maintenance of cooperation with academic, clinical and technological partners
  • Supporting the development of DEC into a sustainable clinical and scientific platform

Professional qualification

  • Medical background or education in healthcare, ideally with experience in clinical research
  • Good knowledge and strong interest in artificial intelligence, machine learning and digital health
  • Experience in project management, ideally in complex interdisciplinary projects
  • Experience in scientific work and publishing
  • Experience in the preparation of research and funding applications and ideally in the acquisition of third-party funds
  • Understanding clinical processes and the challenges of implementing digital technologies in healthcare

Personal competences

  • Strong communication and coordination capability
  • Enjoy working with different professional groups and disciplines
  • Ability to connect complex medical, technical and organizational issues
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Negotiation skills and sovereign dealings with different stakeholders
  • Entrepreneurship and interest in innovation
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Good English skills

A versatile and responsible position at the interface of medicine, research and artificial intelligence. You have the opportunity to actively shape the development of the Digital Eye Clinic and to accompany innovative AI-based applications from the scientific idea to the clinical application. We offer an interdisciplinary environment with close exchange between ophthalmology, clinical research, AI, computer science and other partners as well as the opportunity to actively develop scientific projects, publications, third-party grant applications and innovative digital care concepts. In addition, you benefit from attractive employment conditions and excellent social benefits from the city of Zurich.

With over 36'000 inpatients and around 455,000 outpatients per year, the Stadtspital Zürich ranks among the top 10 Swiss hospitals. As a modern center hospital at several locations in the city of Zurich, we offer the entire range of basic medical care, including 24-hour emergency and intensive care. Our 4,500 employees are committed and committed to providing excellent patient care every day.
