Full description

The Swiss police corps and the federal agencies responsible for police tasks have established a public-law corporation «Polizeitechnik und -informatik (PTI) Schweiz» to jointly procure IT solutions and technical products as well as gradually harmonize existing solutions. In order to strengthen our office PTI (14 persons), which is affiliated with the Cantonal Police of Zurich in terms of personnel law, we are looking for a professionally proven personality who, based on your police and management experience, is suitable for the following newly created task: Team leader / in the office (PTI) 80 - 100% tasks

  • Establishment and management of a new team, which will in future be responsible for the management of mission- and business-critical, centralized applications and products within the PTI office. The product management includes above all the release and lifecycle management and the organizational, professional and financial management of the solutions in cooperation with the corresponding PTI committees. An essential task is also the monitoring of externally provided operational services (performance, availability, security).
  • Coordination of external project managers and employees who, in addition to internal resources, support the fulfillment of challenging tasks.
  • Ensure the legal and contractual foundations, the PTI methodology as well as the budget and architecture specifications in the individual projects and products.
  • Management and coordination of heterogeneous, multilingual and interdisciplinary operational and project committees. Responsibility for Swiss-wide product communication and product marketing.
  • If suitable, there is a chance to become part of the successor solution of the existing PTI management, which is due to retirement in 4-5 years. Profile
  • Completed studies (uni, ETH, FH) technical direction with business administration training (or vice versa)
  • Many years of police and management experience in the logistics, IT and technology environment
  • Good methodological competence in IT operational and project management (ITIL, Hermes, Scrum) as well as ideally in the area of service management and business analysis
  • Resilient personality, accustomed to deadline pressure with a certain willingness to travel (support of all Swiss police corps)
  • Negotiating, initiative and solution-oriented personality with pronounced analytical and conceptual skills, which can be structured systematically and goal-oriented even in a complex environment
  • native language German or French as well as good knowledge of the other language in word and writing (at least B2)
  • Impeccable reputation and job entry: by agreement Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your complete online application.
