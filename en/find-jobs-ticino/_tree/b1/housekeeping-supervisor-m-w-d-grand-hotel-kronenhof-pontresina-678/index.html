Executive Housekeeping Manager (m/w/d) — Grand Hotel Kronenhof, Pontresina (Engadin, Graubünden). Workload: 100%. Contract: Unbefristet / Permanent. Start: 2026-11-01.

Where historic charm meets modern luxury – Grand Hotel Kronenhof in Pontresina is one of the most renowned 5-star superior hotels in Switzerland. Overlooking the fascinating Engadin mountain scenery, we celebrate hospitality at the highest level. To perfectly present this magical place for our guests, we are looking for a personality who has cleanliness, elegance and leadership in their blood.

This is what you move with us

As Executive Housekeeper, you are the driving force behind the impeccable appearance of our hotel. You report directly to the Rooms Division Manager and lead an international team of approximately 24 employees.

  • Quality assurance at 5-star level: You ensure impeccable cleanliness and living quality in all guest rooms and public areas – always in line with the demanding standards of Swiss Deluxe Hotels, Forbes and Leading Quality Assurance (LQA). You are also responsible for our staff accommodation.
  • Inspiring leadership: You are a coach and role model for your team. You are responsible for staff scheduling, conduct training sessions (equipment and cleaning procedures), carry out performance reviews and are actively involved in the recruitment process of new talents.
  • Efficient management & logistics: You manage inventory, oversee the purchasing of hotel linen and uniforms, and coordinate the smooth laundry process in close cooperation with our sister hotel, Kulm.
  • Interface professional: You are in daily contact with the Executive Management, Engineering, Floristry and Front Office to ensure smooth housekeeping operations.

This is you

  • Your foundation: Completed vocational training in the hotel industry (Hotel Management Specialist) or a professional qualification in Housekeeping Management, ideally complemented by specific further training.
  • Your experience: Several years of management experience in Housekeeping, preferably in the 5-star hotel industry. The position is suitable for both experienced managers and committed junior managers who are ready to take the next step in their career.
  • Your language skills: Must-have: Fluent German or English. Advantageous: Knowledge of Italian and/or Portuguese.
  • Your personality: You are characterised by a keen eye for detail, strong business acumen, excellent organisational skills and absolute discretion.
  • Start date: Flexible, by agreement.

What we offer

  • Affordable staff accommodation in Pontresina or Samedan.
  • A workplace in one of the most beautiful locations in the Swiss Alps.
  • Room for personal responsibility and extensive decision-making authority within your area.
  • A motivated, interdisciplinary team and an excellent working environment in one of the top Swiss Deluxe Hotels.

Ready for the next step in your career? If you are ready for this challenge and would like to keep our Housekeeping team on the path to success, we look forward to receiving your comprehensive application.

Benefits

At Kulm Group, our employees are the heart of our success. We offer attractive development opportunities, comfortable accommodations, affordable meals in the staff restaurant, and numerous perks.
