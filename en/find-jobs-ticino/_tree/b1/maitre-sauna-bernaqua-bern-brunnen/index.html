This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

As a sauna master at Bernaqua, you transform every visit into a unique wellness experience. With your skills and passion you promote the well-being of our guests. Are you ready to become part of our team and inspire our guests?

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for supervision in the bathing and sauna areas
  • You perform sauna infusions and design sound bowl ceremonies
  • You are responsible for first aid in medical emergencies and accidents
  • Your role includes selling and consulting as well as taking care of bath and wellness guests
  • Ensuring smooth processes is your responsibility
  • You carry out the maintenance of the facilities

Requirements

  • Ideally professional experience: as a sauna master*
  • Initial or continuing education: first responder, emergency responder course and Samaritan course beneficial
  • You are in good physical condition
  • Working in the evening, on weekends and on holidays is no problem for you and you are resilient
  • Advantageously, you are already in possession of the SLRG-Brevet and CPR-ID, as well as emergency helper and Samaritan course
  • Good knowledge of German, foreign language skills in French or English
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • French (good knowledge)
  • English (advantageous)

Cosa of friamo

  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment terms with very good social benefits
  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation on self-selected courses
  • Fair wages: functional and market-oriented remuneration
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers, language stays, Migros holidays, interhome and events
