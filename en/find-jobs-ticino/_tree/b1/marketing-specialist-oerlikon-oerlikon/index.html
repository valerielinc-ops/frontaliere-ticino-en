Marketing Specialist

Oerlikon Riri, part of the international group Oerlikon and reference point in the luxury sector for the production of high-quality metallic accessories, seeks a/a Marketing Specialist for the headquarters of Mendrisio.

The main activities will be

• Organization and management of participation in trade fairs and events, including related communication activities. • Periodic update and development of new content for business websites. • Management of corporate social media channels. • Support for communication activities related to the launch of new products, both towards the market and within the organization. • Production and updating of product catalogues and promotional materials for the sales network and customers. • Management of relations with press, schools, local institutions and other external stakeholders. • Preparation of commercial presentations and marketing tools to support sales activities. • Organization of internal events and corporate engagement initiatives. • Support in market analysis and competitor monitoring activities.

The ideal resource has

• Degree in Marketing, Communication or related disciplines. • Experience, also through internships or internships, in marketing and/or communication. • Excellent knowledge of English written and spoken. • Good mastery of the Microsoft Office package. • It will provide preferential knowledge of the French language.

  • Accuracy, reliability, flexibility and teamwork skills complete the profile, along with good relational and communication skills.

We offer

100% fixed-time contract for maternity replacement; the RAL is between CHF 45,000 and CHF 50,000.

About Oerlikon

Oerlikon is a global leader in material science engineering. Through the right combination of surface technologies, advanced materials, coating equipment, engineered components and advanced manufacturing, we help customers improve the performance, efficiency, durability and sustainability of their products and processes. Our technologies are often invisible to the end user, but mission-critical to the industries that power modern life: aerospace, energy, semiconductors, medical, luxury, tooling, automotive and general industries. Headquartered in Pfaeffikon, Switzerland, Oerlikon operates in 38 countries with a global workforce of around 10,000 people. In 2025, Oerlikon achieved sales of CHF 1.6 billion.

We offer a wide variety of job profiles in a dynamic, supportive environment where people are empowered to grow and reach their full potential - guided by our core values of integrity, team spirit, excellence and innovation.

About Oerlikon Luxury

Oerlikon Luxury specializes in producing accessories for the high-end fashion industry. It includes two brands: Oerlikon Riri, which produces branded Riri and Cobrax zippers and buttons respectively; and Oerlikon Fineparts, which offers metal components, fashion jewellery and surface treatments.

Oerlikon Luxury is headquartered in Mendrisio, Switzerland, counts 1100 employees at 11 sites located in Italy, Switzerland, France and Portugal as well as sales offices in New York, Paris and Shanghai.
