Full description

At Visp site, an intern in the field of quality control raw material laboratory is sought. Specifically, semi-automatic dimension meters for primary packaging are constructed, qualified and a process is defined for routine operation. This body is intended to support the team in these important activities and also to develop and implement certain steps in the training process autonomously. ## What awaits you:

  • An agile career and a dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical working environment.
  • A variety of benefits, depending on function and location. ## Your Tasks:
  • Learning and Applying the Device Functions of a Dimensional Meter
  • Understanding and Editing the Data Integrity according to the currently valid guidelines incl. Explanations with QA / IT
  • Writing and Validating Methods
  • Specify and Optimize routine process ## What we are looking for:
  • You can network thinking and have a basic understanding in analytical chemistry
  • You are in a bio/chemistry study in laboratory analyst training or similar scientific, technical studies or you have recently completed such a training.
  • You have a potential for technical applications
  • you want to learn more about the GMP environment and device qualification
  • you are independent, responsible, communication and team capable and enjoy interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • You have good communication skills in German and English. At more than 30 locations on five continents, our globally networked teams work together daily to produce the drugs of tomorrow.
