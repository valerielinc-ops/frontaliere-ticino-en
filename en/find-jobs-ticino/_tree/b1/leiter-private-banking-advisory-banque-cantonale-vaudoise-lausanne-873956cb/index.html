Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Put your managerial ambitions at the service of a healthy and sustainable performance by joining the canton of Vaud's first universal bank, one of the world's strongest banks, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Private Banking division, we are looking for a: Sector Manager Advice Private Banking Your main missions:

  • developing the Active Advisory and Consulting sector as part of an established growth strategy to make it a reference in its field of activity,
  • strengthen the advice offer in terms of generation process ideas and support to advisors in accordance with the value propositions for all private banking segments;
  • lead a team to support and deliver the wealth management consultancy offer and adapt it to the needs of clients and advisors,
  • ensuring the quality of active investment advice to private clients and the day-to-day management and conduct of the team,
  • ensure close collaboration with the Investment Policy and Asset Management teams within the Bank,
  • identify opportunities, generate investment proposals and communicate them on different internal media,
  • propose the arguments for investment proposals and develop communication media for advisors and clients. Your profile:
  • university or higher education (HES or equivalent),
  • similar experience with institutions active in wealth management for private customers,
  • proven experience in portfolio management and expertise in financial markets and wealth management products,
  • leadership, teamwork, service and communication
  • excellent analytical and organizational skills, critical mindset, result orientation in order to achieve business and business transformation challenges,
  • very good writing skills in French and good knowledge of English and German. If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
