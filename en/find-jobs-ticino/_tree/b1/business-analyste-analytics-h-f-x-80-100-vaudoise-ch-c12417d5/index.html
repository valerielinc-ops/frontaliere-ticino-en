Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. At the heart of our data recovery strategy, we are looking for a Business Analyst Analytics - 80-100%. Your main mission will be to translate business needs into detailed technical specifications within a team of Data Engineers. The missions that await you:

  • Work closely with business teams to identify, analyse and formalize KPI and data analysis needs, and then write accurate and usable user stories
  • Actively contribute to the testing phase by validating that the developments achieved meet functional requirements and business expectations
  • Documenting semantic models and KPIs available to the trade
  • Using insurance expertise to challenge business demands, propose relevant key indicators (KPIs) and ensure added value of BI solutions
  • Define acceptance criteria, test plans and scenarios, then coordinate UATs
  • Use BA standards and tools (Jira, Confluence)
  • Manage the full life cycle of requirements (traceability, updates, evolution) Your profile:
  • An experience of at least 3 years in the world of Business Intelligence / Analytics as a developer or business analyst
  • A first experience as a Business Analyst that demonstrates your ability to formalize complex needs for detailed documentation
  • Knowledge of the insurance sector, including Property & Casualty, would be an important asset
  • Familiating Scrum/Agile environments, you know how to structure your deliverables to speed up the work of data engineers
  • You master SQL to explore databases in autonomy and validate the relevance of the product indicators
  • Experience in communication and collaboration with a variety of stakeholders, both technical and functional
  • Initiative and autonomy to effectively manage priorities and propose innovative solutions Proactive, open and curious, you like to work as a team in a multidisciplinary framework. Of an enthusiastic and client-oriented nature, you have ease of communication. You will play a key role in inspiring and empowering our team to embrace change, develop new skills and thrive in a changing technological landscape. We offer you:
  • A healthy working environment in a shared enterprise labeled "Best Employer"
  • Multiple social benefits, including flexibility in work organisation
  • Working in a dynamic and passionate team
  • Human values focused on the well-being of employees We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
