Full description

CSC Costruzioni SA, a member of the international group WeBuild, is a Lugano-based company active in Switzerland for over 60 years in the construction of complex and sustainable projects. With the support and expertise of WeBuild, a leading global player in the construction sector, CSC combines strength, innovation and international expertise. With more than 300 employees and over 20 ongoing projects, we contribute to the development of modern and high-quality infrastructure throughout Switzerland.

Position : Business Developer

Linkage: Reporting to the Director General of CSC and functionally attached to Webuild's Business Directorate

Location: Lausanne

Mission

Business Developer's mission is to develop and consolidate CSC's presence in the markets of French-speaking and German-speaking Switzerland by identifying and securing new business opportunities.

It actively contributes to the growth of the project portfolio by ensuring a strategic positioning upstream of tenders.

Key responsibilities

Market development and relations

  • Develop and maintain strategic relationships with institutional and private clients, designers, partners and key local stakeholders
  • Act as a local reference point for CCS to ensure access to market information
  • Promote the image and positioning of CSC on Swiss territory
  • Contribute to the development of partnership strategies and potential joint ventures
  • Support the Director General in the preliminary negotiation of agreements with partners
  • Liaise with business development activities in Ticino, ensuring an effective interface with the Regional Business Developer, the Managing Director and the Webuild Sales Directorate

Identification of market opportunities

  • Ensure market watch and identify new development opportunities
  • Contribute to the selection and qualification of business initiatives (Long List) in coordination with the Executive Director
  • Ensuring timely access to relevant project information
  • Monitor competitive and regulatory market dynamics
  • Analyse the main local players and market developments
  • Provide structured feedback to the Directorate on trends and opportunities

Tender support

  • Providing strategic local contributions to pre-qualification and tender activities
  • Coordinate with internal teams (engineering, biding, procurement and contract) when preparing offers
  • Participate, as appropriate, in meetings with clients and partners

Profile required

Essential requirements

  • Well-established profile in French-speaking Switzerland, with a well-structured local network in the construction sector (infrastructure, civil engineering, engineering)
  • Experience in infrastructure construction in Switzerland
  • Excellent knowledge of territory, actors and local market dynamics
  • Current French, knowledge of at least one other national language and English
  • Excellent relational and empathic skills, with ability to develop business relationships even without specific experience in the role
  • Knowledge of Swiss public procurement laws

Complementary assets

  • Civil engineering training or similar disciplines
  • Experience in international environments or in structured groups
  • Knowledge of public works and civil engineering regulations

Complete profile

  • Team spirit and excellent communication and relationship skills
  • Organizational and decision-making capacities
  • Results orientation
  • Ability to work with intensity and flexibility
  • Availability for frequent domestic travel
