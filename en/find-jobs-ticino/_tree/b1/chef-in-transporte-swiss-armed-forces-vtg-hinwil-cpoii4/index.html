Put to the Point

In this role, you lead the Transportation department. Together with your employees, you ensure reliable transport handling and efficient freight handling, ensure spare parts supply and thus make an important contribution to the operational readiness of the army.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20876
  • Place of work: Überlandstrasse 17, 8340 Hinwil

You can make this contribution

  • Employees of the spare parts warehouse, freight handling platform/forwarding and replenishment tank systems in personnel and organisational terms
  • Responsible for transport management in cooperation with other specialist bodies and/or external service providers
  • Ensure transport of general cargo and wagon loads in accordance with business principles
  • As a member of the management team replenishment shape the further development
  • Ensure and optimize internal logistics in cooperation with stakeholders
  • Exercise training responsibility for road transport professionals

This makes you unique

  • Completed technical or commercial apprenticeship with further education and completion of a higher technical school
  • Management experience and professional experience in at least one subordinate area such as spare parts warehouse, freight handling platform/forwarding or filling stations
  • Military knowledge in traffic and transport beneficial or willingness to acquire the knowledge
  • Flexibility, solution-oriented and networked thinking
  • Oral and written knowledge of a second official language

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
