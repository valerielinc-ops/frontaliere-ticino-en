Full description

Swiss Education Group (SEG) is the largest private education group in Switzerland. We manage four of the best hotel schools in the world and welcome more than 6,000 students to our programs.

With nearly 40 years of experience teaching hotels, management and culinary arts, our group is deeply rooted in the great tradition of Swiss hospitality.

Our mission is to give our students the leadership and entrepreneurial skills they need to succeed in the hospitality industry and beyond. We offer a wide range of courses, from Bachelors and Masters to short professional courses, so that each student can choose a program tailored to his or her professional needs and objectives.

For our campuses at Culinary Arts Academy Switzerland in Bouveret and César Ritz Colleges Switzerland in Brig, we are looking for a:

Part-time Reading – English (M/F/X) Contract paid per hour

You will join a dynamic and international academic team committed to offering high quality programs, and will teach in a multicultural environment.

Your main missions:

  • To teach French and ensure the preparation, delivery and evaluation of courses, in accordance with current procedures and policies
  • Contributing to a stimulating and motivating learning environment alongside school management
  • Participate in the development of school and SEG academic standards
  • Actively and constructively collaborate with the academic team

Your profile:

  • French mother tongue, with a very good level of English
  • Relevant business or hotel experience
  • A first teaching experience is an important asset
  • Autonomy, organizational sense and timeliness
  • Curiosity and openness to new ideas
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Taste for work in a multicultural environment
  • Flexibility to teach on one or both campuses (Le Bouveret and Brig)

We offer you:

  • An international and stimulating working environment within a reference group in hotel education
  • Part-time engagement, with an hourly volume defined according to academic planning

Acting:

  • Le Bouveret (Culinary Arts Academy Switzerland): January – February 2027
  • Brig (César Ritz Colleges Switzerland): March – April 2027
