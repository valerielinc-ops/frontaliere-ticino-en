Full description

We search immediately or by agreement 1-2 Car Chairs as follows:

  • Full-time (100%)
  • Part-time (20-90%)
  • Assistance (on call)

As a car chauffeur, you will guide passengers safely and comfortably throughout Europe in modern vehicles. With your high level of social competence and friendly attitude, you make the trip a very special experience for our customers.

The requirement profile

  • Joy of working with people
  • High willingness to provide services and social skills
  • Above-average quality thinking
  • Category D driving licence, including CZV courses (or intent to acquire it) (*)
  • Readiness for irregular working hours / flexible readiness to work
  • German mother tongue

(*) If only Cat. C/CE is available, at best a retraining would also be possible, since we have a travel car with double pedal. We have also already carried out retraining in cooperation with RAV.

This is what we offer you

  • Know-how Transfer and personal development in the travel industry
  • Varied journeys at home and abroad
  • Modern fleet
  • Fair, punctual remuneration
  • Family, motivated team that lives for the car industry
