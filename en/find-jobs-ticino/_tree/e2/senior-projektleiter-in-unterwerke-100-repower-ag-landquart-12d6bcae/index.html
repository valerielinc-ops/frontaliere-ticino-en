Senior Project Manager: in Unterwerke 100%

We are looking for an immediately or by agreement

Repower is one of the leading energy companies in Switzerland. The 120-year history of Repower begins in the very south of Graubünden in Valposchiavo. In addition to the headquarters in Poschiavo, we have further locations in Landquart, Zurich, Ilanz, Küblis, Disentis, Grono and Bever. As an employer, Repower relies on initiative and trust to promote the creativity of its approximately 730 employees. Because the energy industry of tomorrow needs fresh ideas. We offer you an inspiring working world in which you and your team develop future-oriented solutions for a sustainable energy future.

The "Electrical Engineering" planning department consists of 15 employees and is responsible for the technical project planning of new plants, conversions and expansions as well as renewals in the field of electrical engineering. The employees work at various locations: Poschiavo, Ilanz, Bever, Landquart and Küblis.

You will do this *

  • Overall responsibility of projects for sub-works, from planning to successful implementation
  • Development of technical specifications and concepts
  • Monitoring and controlling deadlines, costs and interfaces to ensure a smooth project flow
  • Coordination and leadership of the project team and suppliers
  • Management of the authorisation procedure with relevant authorities

You bring that with you

  • Studies in Electrical Engineering (University/FH) or comparable qualification
  • Many years of professional experience in substation engineering, several years of project management experience in the substation sector
  • In-depth electrical engineering knowledge in the field of high-voltage systems as well as knowledge in control and protection technology
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of English and/or Italian
  • Able to use project management tools
  • Networked thinking, teamwork and high commitment
