Full description

Operator department Raffineria — MKS PAMP SA, Castel San Pietro (TI).

MIXI

The resource will be inserted within the refinery production department, will handle the management and control of the process of refining precious metals.

MAIN ACTIVITIES AND RESPONSIBILITY

  • Conduction of galvanic treatment plants;
  • Management of treatment baths and displacement of anodes, therefore weight lifting;
  • Daily maintenance of the plant;
  • Effective collaboration with colleagues and department managers;
  • Application of the directives given by the Directorates-General;
  • Compliance with health and safety regulations and procedures at work.

TRAINING AND EXPERIENCE REQUEST

  • Possess of the diploma of Chemical Perito;
  • Preferable experience in a similar or metal-mechanical sector.

QUALIFIES AND COMPETENCES

  • Sense of responsibility and spirit of initiative;
  • Ability to work independently;
  • Resistance to stress and pressure of time, with ability to manage periods of maximum activity;
  • Excellent interpersonal skills;
  • Agile and flexible mentality, with responsive approach to operational demands;
  • Attention to details;
  • Ability to interact with colleagues from different cultures;
  • Availability to work on three shifts;
  • Swiss or EU citizen or holder of a valid Swiss working permit.
