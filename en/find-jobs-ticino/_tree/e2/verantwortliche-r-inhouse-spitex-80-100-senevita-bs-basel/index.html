Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For the Senevita Erlenmatt in Basel, we are looking for a motivated personality as soon as possible. Specialist in Inhouse-Spitex Dipl. HF 80 - 100% Your tasks

  • You conduct clarification interviews and carry out the needs clarifications
  • You evaluate the nursing planning and determine suitable measures together with the nursing service
  • Together you strive for the best possible care quality with your well-established team and thus ensure the satisfaction of our residents
  • You lead by example every day, are motivated and committed and bring your expertise to the team
  • Based on the care concept, you coordinate and organize daily care

You bring that with you

  • An education as a Dipl. Nurse HF or equivalent training
  • Experience in long-term care or in the Spitex area
  • You have a flair in dealing with older people
  • You are interested in the development of care and help to improve the quality of care
  • You are an open and loyal personality who likes to get involved

We offer you

  • A team awaits you who will always strengthen your back and accompany you on your way.
  • A structured workplace with an open feedback culture
  • You work in the morning - as well as late service, twice a month in the weekend service
  • Extensive training offer of the Senevita Academy, can be visited free of charge
  • Discounted food in the in-house gastronomy
  • Various perks (fuels, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 or from 60 years 7 weeks)
