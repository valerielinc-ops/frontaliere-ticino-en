Full description

EOC – Ente Ospedaliero Cantonale, the multi-site hospital in Ticino, is present throughout the cantonal territory with its institutes for a total of 1,000 beds. The organization allows for the effective combination of local approach and overall vision, ensuring a global and proximity hospital offer to the population, regardless of the location where services are requested. Thanks to the commitment and competence of over 6,500 collaborators and their attention to human relationships, EOC ensures quality healthcare, taking care of over 41,000 inpatients per year and providing over 600,000 outpatient consultations. To complete our team of the Regional Hospital of Bellinzona and Valleys, located in Bellinzona, we are looking for a Receptionist/Operator (50%).

  • Profile: The receptionist is the face and voice of the hospital, managing the front office and switchboard, ensuring orientation, clarity, and security for patients, visitors, and staff.
  • We offer a structured and service-oriented professional environment, integration into a dynamic and collaborative team in a modern healthcare context, where reception is an integral part of care. The person we are looking for has a federal certificate of capacity as a hotel communication employee (AFC) or a federal certificate of capacity as a sales employee (AFC) with proven experience in the field of reception/acceptance. They have a perfect command of the Italian language, good knowledge of spoken and written German, French, and English (preferred title) as well as solid knowledge of the main IT applications (Office package and management systems). The future collaborator has strong relational and communication skills to ensure the best possible reception for patients and their families. They have marked skills in stress management and priorities as well as excellent organizational skills with a team-oriented and service-oriented attitude. They are a precise, reliable person with a strong sense of responsibility, flexible, and available to work shifts according to service needs (day, evening, and holiday shifts) in a variable schedule between 07:00 and 20:00. They believe, like us, in the importance of putting the patient and their family at the center of all our attention. Start date: to be agreed. Further information can be requested from Mrs. Fiorella Leonardi at 091 811 91 11. Applications must be submitted to us by no later than March 31, 2026. No paper or email applications will be accepted.
