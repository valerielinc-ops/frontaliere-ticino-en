Full description

The RSI Finance Department is competing for the RSI Regional Offer Business Controller. As a Business Controller, you ensure a transparent, effective and responsive management of the SRG SSR strategy.

Shipments

Within the team, you will be responsible for the following activities:

  • You will perform operational tasks such as providing employee cost analysis, preparing reports or creating and checking databases.
  • You will line up in assigned areas for all business management issues. You will be financial experts on the planning of the regional offer costs. With a holistic view of the company, you will act as financial representatives of the line and also convey the business perspective within finances.
  • You will support work and project groups by providing technical skills and preparing the basis for decision-making. You will ensure the follow-up of the implementation of the decided savings plans and will proactively contribute to the search for financial solutions for the transformation of the company.
  • You will analyze financial flows, identify opportunities and risks and prepare the necessary elements for the management of the activity you will be responsible for. You will identify medium-term financial risks and proactively propose corrective measures.

Requirements

  • Solid analytical skills and experience in management control.
  • University education in economics, diploma of expert in finance and controlling (EPS), certificate of specialist in finance and accounting (APF) or equivalent.
  • Good strategic and transversal vision, business orientation and team spirit.
  • Constructive work approach and solutions oriented.
  • Mastery in SAP CO/FI & BI and Excel (preferred Power BI).
  • Knowledge of public service media is an advantage.
  • Great knowledge of German, knowledge of French is an advantage.

What we offer

  • A professional team, highly qualified, in an open and motivating working environment.
  • Stimulating tasks in a transformation organization where you can actively make a difference.
  • Modern working environment with flexible hours and the possibility of home office.

Our promise

What are the ingredients to reach the inhabitants of Switzerland with quality format? Know-how, enthusiasm and an environment rich in stimuli, in which everyone can grow. To give the best it takes a source of inspiration: Welcome to the world of RSI!

  • Produce content: Quality journalism is our main activity and our passion. We enjoy editorial freedom, promote creativity and give ample space to the production of new and high-level content.
  • Give shape to digital: Quality journalism is also digital. We explore new possibilities and experience innovative forms: digital, interactive and crossmedia. A look at tomorrow for our spectators today.
