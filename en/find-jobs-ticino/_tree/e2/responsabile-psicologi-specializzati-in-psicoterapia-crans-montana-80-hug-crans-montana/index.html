Full description

Attached or attached to the unit physician, you are part of the team of managers of the service and work closely with all professionals and professionals, as well as with the institutional respondent in the psychologists' field. As an expert psychologist, you put your expertise at the service of the team and ensure the transmission of knowledge. You supervise clinical assessments, psychotherapeutic activities and ongoing training of psychologists. As such, you organize their planning, ensure the quality of the follow-up of files, the compliance of billing procedures, and the relevance and accuracy of oral and written communications. You also take institutional responsibility for the supervision of pre-graded trainee psychologists and post-graduate psychologists. You are actively contributing to the development and improvement of care programmes, as well as to the establishment of evaluation mechanisms within the service. In collaboration with professionals and professionals in the service, you initiate, coordinate and participate in pre- and post-graduate education activities. You conduct research projects related to the service's axes and accompany, if necessary, the collaborators mandated for public or private research. Clinically, you evaluate the patient's psychological functioning, take care of their difficulties and develop appropriate intervention plans. You develop therapeutic and preventive actions that promote proactive management. Your activity is part of a strong interdisciplinary dynamic: you work closely with medical and therapeutic teams and actively participate in interdisciplinary rehabilitation seminars. Together with your psychologist colleagues, you organize the distribution of the therapies and the coverage of the different units. Finally, you initiate and support teaching and research activities consistent with the objectives and interests of the Clinic.
