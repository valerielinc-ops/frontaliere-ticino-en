Full description

For our modern laboratory in the Sois Hospital, without night and piketting services, we are looking for the addition of our team at 1. January 2027 or by agreement one or more

Task area

• Implementation of analyses (chemistry, haematology, clotting, microbiology and immunohaematology)

  • Assessment of the analysis results according to professional / technical criteria taking into account plausibility and the QM
  • Logging of analysis results and recording of services
  • Storage, reception and placing on the market of blood products, reagents and control materials
  • Implementation of external and internal quality controls in accordance with applicable rules
  • Maintenance and supervision of analytical systems and working conditions

Requirement profile

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Biomedical analyst(s)
  • Independent and precise working methods, teamwork
