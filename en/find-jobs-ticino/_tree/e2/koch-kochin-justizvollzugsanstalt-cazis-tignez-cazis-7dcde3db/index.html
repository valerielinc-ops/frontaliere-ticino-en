Full description

Prison Office | 100% | JVA Cazis Tignez, Cazis

The Office for Prisoners (AJV) Graubünden is a department of the Department of Justice, Security and Health of the Canton of Graubünden. The AJV is responsible for the execution of penalties and measures as well as the implementation of the various types of detention. In Graubünden, the execution of penalties and measures is thus regulated as a uniform topic and holistic task, in which different professions work interdisciplinary with people who have become delinquent in order to prevent further delinquency.

The prison (JVA) Cazis Tignez employs about 110 people and has 150 detention places in the closed prison. Of these, 100 are in the normal prison, ten in the entrance department, 28 in the special prison and twelve in custody.

We are looking for a cook / cook immediately or by agreement

Your responsibilities

  • Leadership, supervision and guidance of assigned assignees in accordance with the principles of labour practice
  • Support of the kitchen team in all aspects of the corresponding task area depending on service between 06.00 – 18.00 in 7-day operation
  • Co-responsibility for a rational, varied and healthy preparation of food for the instructed persons and the employees
  • Compliance and implementation of hygiene rules
  • Supervision of the staff and assistance with events and meals of visitors and groups
  • Promotion of persons assigned to their professional and social development

That is what we want from you.

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as chef with EFZ and several years of professional experience
  • They are motivated to guide and look after people in their day-to-day life in accordance with work-agogic principles
  • Balanced, team-capable, resilient and flexible personality with high social competence
  • Good knowledge of German (at least C1); additional knowledge of cantonal and foreign languages is an advantage
  • An impeccable reputation (no entries in the criminal record and the enforcement record)
