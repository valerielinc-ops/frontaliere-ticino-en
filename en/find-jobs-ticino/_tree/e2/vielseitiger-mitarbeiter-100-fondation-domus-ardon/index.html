The Fondation Domus, a Valais-based psychosocial rehabilitation institution, supports adults facing significant mental health challenges. Nearly 150 employees are committed to this mission with conviction and professionalism. Our vision: self-determination for everyone, a solution-focused approach, and professional practices rooted in kindness and responsibility.
