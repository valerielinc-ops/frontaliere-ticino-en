Introduction

For our customer, we are looking for a committed and precise personality as a constructor / EFZ. In this varied position, you are responsible for the development and construction of technical solutions and accompany projects from idea to implementation.

Tasks

  • Creating 3D models and technical drawings with CAD programs
  • Development and construction of components, assemblies and technical systems
  • Preparation of production documents and parts lists
  • Cooperation in new and further developments of products
  • Technical clarifications with customers, suppliers and internal departments
  • Support for prototypes, tests and optimisations

Profile

  • Completed training as constructor/EFZ
  • Safe handling of CAD software
  • Technical understanding and spatial imagination
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Teamwork and enjoyment of technical challenges
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Advantages

  • Exciting and versatile projects
  • Modern work equipment and contemporary infrastructure
  • Collegial and appreciative working environment
  • Opportunities for professional and personal development
  • Attractive employment conditions
