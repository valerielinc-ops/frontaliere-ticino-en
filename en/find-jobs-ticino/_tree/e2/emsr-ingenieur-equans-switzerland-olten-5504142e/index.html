Industry 4.0 is your thing? You want technology that excites – and projects that move? Then come to us!

You can move that with us.

  • You plan and lead EMSR projects – from idea to implementation (control and control technology projects)
  • You create electrical diagrams, control cabinet layouts and network concepts
  • You work closely with the software team and ensure smooth integration
  • You accompany the commissioning directly at the customer on site
  • You are the central contact person for customers and suppliers during the project
  • You bring Your ideas – innovation is expressly desired with us

That's what makes you.

  • A successful HF/FH degree in electrical / system engineering
  • You have experience in electrical planning or automation projects
  • Experience in the CAE tool ePlan P8 is an advantage
  • You are communicative and enjoy working in a team
  • You have a solution- and practice-oriented way of thinking and acting

That's what we offer you.

  • An education on our tools & introduction to processes by our Senior Software Engineers
  • External training at our system partners such as Siemens
  • Opportunity to work at home or part-time
  • A top infrastructure and good social benefits
  • An independent and diverse activity
  • A collegial working atmosphere with DU culture at eye level
  • Attractive training and development opportunities
