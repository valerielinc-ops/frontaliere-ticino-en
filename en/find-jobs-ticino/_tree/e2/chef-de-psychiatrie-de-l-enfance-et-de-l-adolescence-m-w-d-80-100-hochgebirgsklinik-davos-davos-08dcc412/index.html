Full description

210 beds, 5 disciplines, over 120 years of history – and 1560 m above sea level. The Hochgebirgsklinik Davos is more than a workplace: We are an interdisciplinary rehabilitation center with university ties, embedded in the spectacular nature of the Bündner Alps. Our department «Parents–Children and Adolescents» is unique in Switzerland: We accompany children, adolescents, and their families holistically – medically, psychologically, pedagogically, and humanely. Do you want to not just treat, but really accompany? Do you want to work interdisciplinarily and actively shape the rehabilitation daily life? Welcome to us. ## Responsibilities

  • Live and work with quality – in the middle of nature, with good infrastructure. As a head of child and adolescent psychiatry, you take responsibility in the multiprofessional team and actively shape the professional and organizational further development of our department.
  • Psychiatric diagnosis and therapy planning: You accompany children and adolescents with psychosomatic complaints during their rehabilitation and create individual treatment plans.
  • Therapeutic guidance on an equal footing: You conduct individual and family conversations and accompany young people with foresight, empathy, and professional competence.
  • Strengthen interdisciplinary collaboration: You work closely with professionals from psychology, pediatrics, nursing, and pedagogy – together you create an effective and sustainable treatment.
  • Take on professional responsibility: You accompany younger colleagues, shape case conferences, and contribute to quality assurance.
  • Develop the department: You get involved in conceptual questions and have the perspective to take on a leading function in the medium term. ## Requirements
  • We are looking for people who work professionally, are human, and have a desire for the new – not an «ideal fit» on paper.
  • A recognized specialist title in child and adolescent psychiatry and -psychotherapy (FMH or equivalent).
  • Several years of clinical experience in working with children, adolescents, and their families – ideally in a psychosomatic or rehabilitative setting.
  • Willingness to take on responsibility – e.g., in case conferences, supervision, or in guiding younger colleagues.
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary collaboration, innovation, and openness to new therapy forms.
  • Initiative and strategic thinking – you want to develop our department and are interested in a future leadership function.
  • Interest in a living environment with quality of life – in the middle of nature, with good infrastructure for families. ## What we offer
  • Shape instead of follow Your ideas count – professionally and organizationally.
  • Well-balanced work-life Planifiable working hours, family-friendly, and reliable.
  • Unique parent-child rehab We aim for sustainable effectiveness and pull together on a single thread.
  • Leisure time quality including mountains, movement, nature, good schools – a dream environment for you and your loved ones.
  • We support you upon arrival – also in the job search for your partner.
  • And a place where you can grow – professionally and privately.
