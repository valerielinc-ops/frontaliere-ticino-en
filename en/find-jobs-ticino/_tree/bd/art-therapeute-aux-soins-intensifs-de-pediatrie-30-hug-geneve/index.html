Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The post is attached to the Department of Women, Children and Adolescents, whose mission is to provide quality care tailored to the needs of women at every stage of their lives and to the health problems faced by newborns, children and adolescents from birth to age 16. He works on the development of advanced services and specialized expertise. The Intensive Paediatric and Neonatological Care Department is responsible for any newborn, infant or child whose vital functions are threatened and who requires intensive medical supervision or treatment.

Description of post

Within the paediatric intensive care unit, you accompany in-patient children and their relatives through artistic mediations adapted to their clinical condition and specific needs. In collaboration with the interdisciplinary team, you contribute to the overall care of the child, particularly in the management of anxiety and pain, emotional expression, the prevention of medical trauma, and the accompaniment of end-of-life situations. Individual care takes place in the patient's bed or isolation room, requiring constant adaptation to the constraints of the intensive care environment (hygiene, asepsia and infection prevention). Based on your clinical assessment, you identify the needs of the child and his/her family, define individualized therapeutic objectives and ensure the traceability of interventions in the patient file. Through various means such as drawing, modelling, storytelling, music, puppets or collage, you promote emotional expression, the development of the child's resources and the support of his or her experience of hospitalization. You accompany patients, from infants to adolescents, with anxiety, pain, invasive care or the consequences of medical trauma, including when communication skills are limited or absent. With parents and siblings, you provide them with appropriate support throughout the care journey, especially in the context of palliative care, end of life or mourning. As part of interdisciplinary symposia and clinical transmissions, you share your observations and contribute to the development of coordinated therapeutic responses. You are involved in the development of service projects related to the well-being of the hospitalized child, developmental care and paediatric palliative care. Materiel management and monitoring of the budget allocated to the activity are also part of your responsibilities. With healthcare teams, you value the contribution of art therapy, share your expertise and contribute to the development of this practice within the institution. You are involved in evaluation and research projects aimed at enriching knowledge and practices in hospital art therapy.

Qualifications

You benefit from a Bachelor HES of art therapy or title deemed equivalent recognized in Switzerland. You also hold the Federal Diploma in Art and Therapist or equivalent recognized in Switzerland, which is an asset. You have a minimum of five years of clinical practice in hospitals, ideally in pediatrics or acute care, with children, adolescents and their families. You show autonomy, creativity and adaptability in the face of unforeseen events. In accordance with professional secrecy, you know how to keep the information entrusted to you confidential. You have very good emotional stability, flexibility in your organization and effective stress management.
