Mandated by one of our well-known customers in its field of activity, a well-known iron-coverage company in Valais and Western Switzerland, we are looking for a (M/F)-confirmed iron-covering machine for a long-term commitment.

Your missions and responsibilities

  • Construction of ironworks on new construction sites and renovations
  • Installation of gutters, chéneaux, water descents and metal cladding
  • Manufacture, shaping and assembly of iron and steel fittings
  • Reading plans and taking action
  • Making connections, welding and finishing work
  • Guarantee of watertightness and quality of works
  • Ability to perform accurate and cared work
  • Self-employment on site and collaboration with existing teams

Competences

  • Ferblantier CFC or equivalent
  • Minimum 3 years experience in the field
  • Excellent mastery of traditional ironworks
  • Good reading of plans
  • Self-reliant, thorough and motivated
  • A sense of responsibility and good work
  • Driving licence
  • Vehicle (an asset)

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed.
