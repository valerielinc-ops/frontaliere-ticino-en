Full description

Location: Hirslanden Klinik Linde | Biel / Bienne

Occupation by 1 October 2026 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 72745

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments.

Clinic Linde is the leading private hospital in the Biel-Seeland-Jura area. With its high medical quality, its selected offers and the supra-regional reputation, it is the first choice for patients as well as for highly qualified medical doctors.

YOUR TASKS

  • As a leader, or Head of Facility Management, you will lead the department in terms of personnel, technical, organizational and economics and develop it continuously together with your team.
  • You ensure the safe, reliable and legally compliant operation of the technical facilities and infrastructures and ensure structured operational management, seamless technical documentation and compliance with the relevant safety, quality and operator obligations.
  • You plan and coordinate maintenance, maintenance and repair work, controlling external specialist planners, suppliers and service partners.
  • You are responsible for the budget of your department, keep an eye on costs and deadlines and develop decision-making bases for upcoming investments, at the same time you advise the management team on the strategic development of the clinic and contribute to the medium- and long-term investment planning.
  • Together with the medical, nursing and operational departments, you identify requirements and develop viable solutions for conversions, conversions and operational optimizations.
  • Smaller construction and infrastructure projects you manage independently
  • For larger projects, you represent the operational interests of the clinic vis-à-vis internal and external project partners.
  • The optimization of processes and digital work instruments is just as much a part of your tasks as support in day-to-day operations and participation in technical piketting services.

Your profile

  • You have a basic technical training, ideally in building technology, electrical engineering, mechanics or a comparable area, supplemented by a higher education in facility management, building technology, project or process management or a comparable qualification.
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience in technical facility management or in the operation of complex building infrastructures. Experience in healthcare or in a comparable 24-hour operation is an advantage.
  • In-depth knowledge in the maintenance of technical facilities as well as experience in managing construction, conversion or infrastructure projects round off your professional profile.
  • Your way of working is structured, forward-looking and solution-oriented. Even in the event of disruptions or parallel projects, you keep an overview and combine entrepreneurial thinking with a pronounced quality, safety and cost awareness.
  • As a leader, you communicate clearly and bindingly, act assertively and maintain an appreciative and cooperative leadership style. A pronounced understanding of customers and services as well as enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation distinguish you.
  • You are familiar with digital FM and CAFM systems
  • Knowledge in WaveWare is an advantage.
  • You have very good knowledge of German or French and can communicate in the other language in everyday work.
  • You are ready to participate in the technical piketting service.

Why does it work for us?

  • We work appreciatively and respectfully at eye level with each other
  • Possibility of working with flex and leisure plus models
  • Up to 4 additional free days per year as compensation for changing time (pension-dependent)
  • Above-average night and weekend allowance of CHF 10.- per hour
  • Many discounts and perks in the fitness center, over 100 service providers, leisure events and mobile subscriptions
