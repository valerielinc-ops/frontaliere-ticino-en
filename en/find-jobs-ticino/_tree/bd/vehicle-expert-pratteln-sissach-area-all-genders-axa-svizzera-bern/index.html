Shape your future – together with us!

Would you like to actively shape the future with us? And develop innovative solutions together? If yes - then you have come to the right place! We offer a working environment in which thinking is required, teamwork is highly valued and flexibility is encouraged. Support us on the way to a successful future - committed, courageous and open to new things. We look forward to you and your participation and are convinced: “Together we make the difference.”

In the Customer Operations area, we design the individual experiences of our customers in the event of a claim or benefit. You are our focus and together we will find the right solution. This is how we ensure maximum customer satisfaction with our customer-first mentality.

This is what awaits you

  • In this position, you will assess damage to motor vehicles both on screen and on site and prepare comprehensive technical damage reports
  • You conduct negotiations with customers and repair technicians in order to achieve optimal solutions
  • You analyze coverage issues from a technical perspective and make informed decisions
  • You will also determine the current values ​​of cars, trucks, motorcycles, agricultural vehicles and equipment as well as classic and classic cars
  • By checking repaired vehicles and checking invoices, you ensure the quality and performance of our services
  • You actively expand your internal and external network and maintain it through regular exchange and collaboration
  • With your proactive attitude, you contribute to the continuous improvement and development of our business processes

This is what you bring with you

  • You have training as an automotive mechatronics engineer or bodywork plumber in EFZ as well as further training as an automotive diagnostician or workshop coordinator
  • You have several years of practical professional experience in customer service, as a workshop manager or operations manager
  • You are fluent in the German language, both spoken and written; additional language skills are an advantage
  • Your strengths lie in a strong sense of service and excellent communication skills
  • You have in-depth knowledge of common IT tools and use them efficiently
  • You are characterized by flexibility, independence and good organizational skills
  • You are a committed and motivated personality with a strong entrepreneurial mindset
  • You are based in the main area of ​​activity
