Going to work with pleasure: a more for your health Place Bel-Air 3, 1400 Yverdon-les-Bains Our diversity is our asset: CONCORDIA brings together people from diverse cultures, professions and fields of activity. Will you come soon to enrich our team? For our agency located in Yverdon, we are looking for a versatile and open-minded personality like you.

Your responsibilities

  • You acquire new customers and sell health insurance and insurance solutions tailored to their needs.
  • You independently manage existing insured persons and maintain daily contact with customers.
  • You make offers, perform administrative tasks and make appointments proactively.
  • You take part in fairs and stand actions and represent CONCORDIA on the spot.

Your profile

  • You've received professional training in a service or sales environment.
  • Customer contact, sales and advice are your passion, and administrative skills complement your profile.
  • You have very good knowledge of French
  • Any other language is an asset.
  • You have taken AFA training or you are ready to take it - we are accompanying you in this process.
