Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of permanent employment? For 30 years, we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, offering them the best talents. We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the location of (M/F/D) Watches

Your assignment

  • Design and develop components for watches and finishes in compliance with aesthetic, technical and industrial requirements.
  • Making 3D models, technical drawings and related technical documentation.
  • Ensure the technical feasibility of the projects and accompany its industrialization.
  • Develop and follow prototypes, models and presets.
  • Strictly collaborate with the departments Products, Quality, Purchases, Production and Post-Sales.
  • Participate in technical exchanges with suppliers and subcontractors.
  • Analyze production processes and propose adequate technical improvements.
  • Ensure compliance with costs, deadlines and quality requirements throughout the entire development cycle.
  • To contribute to the continuous improvement of products, working methods and development processes.
  • Ensure technical data management and monitoring within CAD and PLM tools.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Training as an ES technician, HES engineer in microtechnical or training considered equivalent.
  • Experienced in the construction of watches (10 years) and in the development of products in a Swiss watchmaking context.
  • Excellent knowledge of the aesthetic components of watchmaking (cases, lunettes, quadrants, straps, closures or related parts).
  • Mastery of production processes such as mechanical processing, turning, injection molding, molding, surface treatments or assembly.
  • Excellent mastery of functional quotation principles, quota chains and tolerances.
  • Good knowledge of 3D CAD software (CREO, SolidWorks, Catia or equivalent).
  • Experience with a PLM environment and technical data management.
  • Ease of dealing with suppliers and industrial partners.
  • Good mastery of French and ability to understand technical documentation in English.
  • Strict, autonomous and solution oriented person.
  • Collaborative spirit and strong propensity to teamwork.
