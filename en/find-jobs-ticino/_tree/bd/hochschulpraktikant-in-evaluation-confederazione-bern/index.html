Put to the Point

During a 1-year internship, you will help with the implementation of complex projects in the area of evaluations with the aim of improving the execution, effects and profitability of state measures. You are responsible for assigned subprojects in the department, carry out research and support the mentor and the team in all respects. A varied, wide-ranging job in an exciting environment with demanding conversation partners awaits you – do not hesitate to apply!

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: 611-20963
  • Location: Monbijoustrasse 45, 3011 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Participate in the development of evaluation concepts (impact models, evaluation questions and indicators)
  • Collect and analyze data using established methods (e.g. data analysis or surveys)
  • Participation in the creation of clear and precise reports and presentations in a well-kept language
  • Perform research tasks in various specialist topics
  • Prepare, record and evaluate surveys

This makes you unique

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Economics, Political Sciences or Social Sciences with knowledge in various research methods (interviews, document and data analyses)
  • Strong curiosity and joy in opening up new topics and questions
  • Interest in evaluations as well as political, social and economic issues
  • Precise written expression with the ability to present complex issues in an understandable way, a high willingness to learn, initiative, as well as a healthy mix of independence and team spirit
  • Good active knowledge of two official languages

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.
