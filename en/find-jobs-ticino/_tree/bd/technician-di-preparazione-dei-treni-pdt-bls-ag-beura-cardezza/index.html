Full description

Without you no train can leave. As a member of the dedicated team of BLS Cargo Italia, make sure everything works perfectly. Rail transport is passionate about you and in a blink of an eye, so that our trains can circulate. Thanks to you, thousands of tons of goods can cross the Alps safely.

Your job

  • Train training: detection of characteristic data and preparation of train escort documents
  • Technical verification of vehicles and cargo.
  • Verification of suitability for circulation.
  • Ensure compliance with safety standards.
  • Railway manoeuvre activities: Union and detachment of railway vehicles.
  • Composition and decomposition of convoys.
  • Support for the operations necessary for the preparation of the train.

Your skills

  • Comprehensive PDT Abilitation: Train preparation and proven experience in the role of PDT Italia
  • Good computer knowledge
  • High School Diploma
  • Excellent knowledge of the Italian language
  • Even if not mandatory, will constitute preferential title the possession of railway permits of TKC / ZV Switzerland
  • Knowledge of English and German languages

Who are we?

We can't move the mountains, it's clear. But it doesn't matter. Because, as experienced mountaineers in freight transport on rail, we see the mountains as a challenge, not as an obstacle. We transport goods of tons from the North Sea, through the Alps, to the Mediterranean, and this is a complex enterprise. We team up with our approximately 450 employees in Switzerland, Belgium, Germany and Italy. Our customers are international, our local roots. For this reason, working for BLS Cargo is particularly challenging.
