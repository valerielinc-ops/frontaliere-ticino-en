Full description

Are maintenance, maintenance and inspection your passion? Then you are exactly right with us! As a specialist in maintenance & infrastructure, you ensure that our technical facilities run reliably and are constantly optimized. You work independently and contribute your know-how in many ways. Apply now and become part of our team!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, operations headquarters Schönbühl, Industriestrasse 20, 3321 Urtenen-Schönbühl

Important information

This position includes Sunday, night and piquetting services. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Planning and execution of maintenance, repair and cleaning work on mechanical and electrical equipment
  • Implementation of extensions and conversions
  • Ensure the flow of information in the event of critical incidents and emergencies as well as detection of malfunctions in the maintenance software
  • Compliance and implementation of the rules and values of the Migros Aare Cooperative

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: in the maintenance of logistics equipment or conveying equipment
  • Completed basic or further training: polymechanic, electrical installer, automatic technician or similar
  • Readiness for shift and weekend services and pikett
  • Very good technical, mechanical and electronic knowledge and safe handling of equipment, instruments, tools and machines
  • Independent work, flexibility and the ability to keep a cool head even in hectic situations
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Healthy work: Occupational health management incl. Friendly Workspace Label
  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life
  • Social benefits: Attractive employment conditions with very good social benefits
  • Food: Extensive culinary offer at discounted prices
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Sports and fitness offers: Cost sharing in subscriptions of over 50 Migros fitness, wellness, aquaparks
