Responsibility | This is what awaits you In this role, you are the engine for our production processes and ensure that innovation and precision go hand in hand. Your tasks include:

  • You take over the professional management in the day-to-day business of the production area. You accompany projects professionally and ensure the smooth flow.
  • You supervise production approaches as well as operational tests and control modern systems and software for the production of our products.
  • You implement safety- and quality-relevant processes, perform batch record reviews and evaluate production deviations in close coordination with the interfaces.
  • You will be responsible for the transfer of new methods and processes from development to pilot scale and optimize existing facilities in cooperation with the field of Manufacturing Science and Technology.
  • You perform analytical tests, execute them and create precise manufacturing guidelines and calculations according to the applicable specifications. Qualifications | This is what you bring You are a proactive personality who likes to take responsibility and solve complex chemical problems in a structured way.
  • You have successfully completed your studies in chemistry (master or diploma) with a doctorate in organic synthesis chemistry.
  • You have in-depth practical knowledge of preparative synthesis. You have experience in the regulated production environment of organic-chemical production and in the scale-up (laboratory to pilot plant)
  • You enjoy familiarizing yourself with new technical facilities and always act responsibly with regard to work safety and quality assurance.
  • You are a team player who interacts confidently with various interfaces and convinces through assertiveness and a conscientious way of working.
  • You communicate safely in German and routinely deal with common EDP applications. Your application We ask you for the following documents:
