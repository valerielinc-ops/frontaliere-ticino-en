Support Coordinator Field Service (m/f/d) Where do people love what they do and are great at it? At Swisslog, of course! Our teams are at the heart of a world-leading data- and robot-driven company that harnesses the power of technology to shape the future of intralogistics. The result? We deliver performance and efficiency to our customers around the world, giving them the decisive advantage in their markets. Job description Here you do something

  • Personnel planning and coordination of maintenance operations
  • Operational preparation with the customer, the field service technician and ensure that the right tools and materials are available
  • Coordination and ensuring on-time processing of support tickets
  • Coordination of missions with the control and support colleagues
  • Examination of technical work report and maintenance reports and identification of supply needs
  • Calculation and preparation of offers for spare parts and assembly services
  • Follow-up of offers until successful completion
  • Coordination of interfaces to Project Management, Spare Parts Management, Account Management and Automation
  • Close coordination with field service technicians and plant managers regarding scheduling and assembly preparation
  • Independent order processing including supplier orders, cost control and customer billing
  • Identification and planning of training needs
  • Cooperation in administrative tasks and continuous support of the team

Applicant profile This is what you bring with you

  • Basic technical or commercial training, ideally with technical understanding
  • Experience in order processing, service coordination or technical customer support is an advantage
  • Experience in operational planning
  • Experience with CRM (Salesforce 4 Service) and ERP (SAP), adept at handling Microsoft Office products
  • Secure handling of commercial processes such as calculation, quotation and cost control
  • Strong analytical thinking skills and the ability to prioritize customer- and business-oriented
  • High customer orientation and an independent and structured working method
  • Team players with strong communication skills and high flexibility
  • Willingness to continuously familiarize yourself with existing and new technologies
  • Secure written and oral communication and German, English and French
  • Documents the work and processes safely and completely
