Full description

The Welle7 Workspace is the popular meeting and event location in the heart of the city of Bern. The Workspace is a brand of Miduca AG, which belongs to the Migros Group. Your passion belongs to the hospitality sector and you love to inspire people with your hospitality? Then we look forward to getting to know each other!

Luogo di lavoro: Welle7 Workspace, Schanzenstrasse 5, 3008 Bern

Important information

Working hours in shift service from Monday to Saturday between 6.00 a.m. and 22.30 a.m. No partial service. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Contact person for all guests of the workspace
  • Provision of rooms including technology, drinks & snacks
  • Correct implementation and compliance with the various standards
  • Close communication with all departments of the Workspace and Welle7
  • Acceptance of deliveries

Requirements

  • First professional experience: in a comparable position
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the catering or hotel industry
  • Experience in the hospitality industry/gastronomy or a completed professional training in the hospitality industry
  • Strong hosting, good manners and a safe presence
  • Independent, customer- and service-oriented personality
  • Careful working methods and high quality awareness, PC knowledge as well as basic knowledge in conference technology are advantageous
  • German (flowing)
  • English (good knowledge)
  • French (advantageous)
  • Italian (advantageous)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Central locations with excellent public transport connections
  • Insurance: Cheapest insurance for various private and health insurances
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Migros Bank: Private and investment account with preferential interest and expense-free account management
  • Sports and fitness offers: Cost sharing in subscriptions to various leisure and fitness facilities of the Migros Group
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with a high level of self-responsibility

Contatto

Application and contact Jean-Philippe Weiss Talent Acquisition Coordinator

Recruitment process

  • Submit application documents: For a complete application, we need your CV, certificates and diplomas. A photo in the CV and a letter of motivation are not necessary. We live equal opportunities for all applicants. We have a standardized recruitment process. If this does not work for you, mention it in your application dossier.
  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Personal video or telephone conversation: For a large number of applicants, we optionally conduct a personal telephone or video conversation. This gives us the opportunity to get to know more candidates within a short time in order to invite suitable people for the next round. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week

Note for personnel service providers For this position, we only consider direct applications.
