Full description

The Ambulatories of the Clinic Barmelweid have the recognition as a training institution Cat. C (2 years) and consiliar and liasison service Cat. D2-CL (2 years). Highly qualified training with close supervision by the cadre doctors, a generous compensation for further education costs and sufficient free days to complete psychotherapeutic training are a matter of course for us. Employment Level: 50–100% Entry by Agreement

You don't have to know everything with us. But you can ask anything. You can try out ideas and take responsibility. For us, lean means: giving responsibility, trusting and becoming better together. We develop goals together, are all per-you and communicate at eye level. Commitment and appreciation are our focus and joy and humor are not neglected. Do you want to work flexibly – without weekend and night services? This is the right place for you. You move that with us

  • Under supervision, you treat outpatients with predominantly affective disorders and personality disorders. In addition, you will be trained in the treatment of all psychosomatic symptoms, which you treat interdisciplinary, especially in cooperation with the specialist areas of rheumatology, pain medicine, gynecology and oncology
  • You perform consiles at the Hirslanden clinic. With advanced specialist training, you have the opportunity to acquire part of the focus on consultancy and liaison psychiatry
  • It is possible to take over the management of topic-specific and interactional group psychotherapies. You inspire us...
  • with your medical degree and foreign diplomas direct or indirect recognition of the medical diploma by the Medical Commission (MEBEKO)
  • with your interest in biopsychosocial and psychosomatic connections and your perennial psychiatric experience
  • with your planned specialist training in psychiatry and psychotherapy or as recognition as a foreign year in the field of child and adolescent psychiatry
  • with your very good knowledge of German in word and writing (German mother tongue or language level according to the European Reference Framework at least C1) Our outpatient laboratories offer consultation hours for psychosomatic medicine, so psychotherapeutic treatments are in the foreground. We treat mainly affective disorders, personality disorders and somatoform disorders. Particular focus is on eating disorders, trauma follow-up disorders, pain disorders, psycho-oncology (breast center) and disorders related to pregnancy and childbirth. Psychotherapies are carried out in individual and group therapies. Social advice is provided by our social service. There is a close cooperation between the clinic Barmelweid and its outpatients, so that an ambulatory, semi-inpatient or inpatient treatment can be carried out as required. In the clinic, psychosomatic medicine and psychotherapy work closely with internal medicine, making it possible to treat people with multimorbid somatic diseases and psychopathological disorders.
