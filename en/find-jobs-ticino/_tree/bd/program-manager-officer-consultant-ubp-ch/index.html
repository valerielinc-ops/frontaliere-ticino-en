Full description

Mission As part of the Project Team, the role will be responsible to support the programme management team of a major transformation initiative. The projects scopes will be cross-functional, with a focus on Trading, Treasury and WM activities.

Main responsibilities

Programme management support

  • Coordinate, prepare and facilitate project meetings.
  • Prepare and publish project reporting and presentations for senior management and Executive Committees.
  • Monitor project interdependencies and assess their potential impact.
  • Maintain action, risk, issue and decision logs, and follow up on agreed actions.
  • Ensure stakeholder and sponsor engagement throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Proactively identify risks, issues and delays, and escalate them when appropriate.
  • Support governance, planning and delivery tracking across the programme

Capacity and budget planning and monitoring

  • Coordinate capacity planning and reviews, role changes, onboarding and vacation planning.
  • Monitor capacity and budget consumption
  • Ensure that project budgets are regularly updated and tracked, with exceptions escalated in a timely manner.

Your Profile Education/ Background

  • 5–10 years of experience in project or programme management, PMO or a related role.
  • Experience in Private Banking is a strong advantage.
  • Experience in people management is a plus.
  • University degree.
  • Professional certification such as PMP or PRINCE2 is a plus

Specific Competencies

  • Excellent organizational skills
  • Strong Analytical skills
  • Drive, leadership and “go-getter” attitude
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills
  • Excellent PowerPoint skills

Oher Skills/Abilities

  • Good team player, resourceful and independent
  • Ability to convince
  • Experience working with cross-functional teams
  • Stress resistant
  • Fluent in English, other language is a plus
  • Very proficient with MS Office suite
  • Swiss work permit (L, B or C permit)

Core Competencies : Adherence to the company’s values: Dedication, Conviction, Agility and Responsibility - Compliance with regulations and internal directives
