Full description

The location of this location is in Stein, AG, Switzerland. Eligible applicants and their families will be assisted in moving if needed.

Do you value clear processes, high precision and enjoy working in a structured environment with fixed processes? In this role, you perform the manual visual inspection of vials and ready-to-use syringes in the hygiene zone and make an important contribution to product quality and patient safety with your trained eye. Your Career in Drug Product Services at Lonza

This body requires full presence on the ground. Personal collaboration enables close, timely collaboration and the technical precision required to produce pharmaceuticals to the highest quality and safety standards.

Their tasks

  • Perform manual visual inspection of vials and ready-to-use syringes including defect classification
  • Cooperation in the creation, qualification and requalification of test sets
  • Support for deviation and cause analysis
  • GMP-compliant documentation of activities in MES systems and logbooks
  • Ensuring smooth operations and compliance with SOPs, hygiene and safety regulations

What we are looking for

  • Completed apprenticeship
  • Fluent German skills as well as basic English skills are a must
  • Experience in the visual inspection of vials and syringes in the GMP environment, as well as experience with SAP and MES knowledge are advantageous
  • Structured, precise and independent working methods combined with team spirit, reliability and a high level of quality awareness
  • You feel comfortable in a routine working environment, enjoy working in the hygiene zone (zone CNC), consistently comply with GMP and hygiene guidelines and estimate scheduled processes with fixed working hours from 07:00 in the morning.
