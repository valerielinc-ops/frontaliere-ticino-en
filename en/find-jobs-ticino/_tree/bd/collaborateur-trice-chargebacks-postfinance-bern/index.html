Full description

As an employee:in Chargebacks you check and process complaints of card bookings. You make sure that receivables are handled correctly and in time, making an important contribution to protecting our customers in payment transactions. You can do that

  • You process complaints of card bookings and execute the batchback process according to the requirements of the card organizations (e.g. Mastercard)
  • You will check inquiries and complaints from cardholders: both internal and external partners and answer them in writing or by phone
  • You carefully analyze the individual cases, decide on possible recovery and assemble the necessary documents for further processing
  • You keep the cases on card organisations or partner banks and make sure that all deadlines are met
  • You document the individual steps in the system and ensure a clean follow-up of cases
  • You make sure that our customers are informed about the status of their complaint
  • In case of suspicious transactions, you work closely with the Fraud Prevention team and take the necessary steps That's what you bring
  • Completed commercial or comparable vocational training
  • Experience in batchback, card service or payment
  • Stylish German as well as good English skills
  • French and Italian of advantage
  • Secure handling of MS Office and digital tools
  • A structured and accurate way of working as well as the pleasure of analyzing complex cases and finding solutions
  • A service-oriented mindset in dealing with customers: internal and external partners
