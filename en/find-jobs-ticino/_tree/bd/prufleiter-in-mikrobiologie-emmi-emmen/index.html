Full description

At our location in Emmen, popular Emmi products are produced every day – from fresh dairy products to innovative specialties. To ensure that our high quality standards are guaranteed at all times, our microbiological laboratory plays a central role. As a test manager, you take on responsibility in an accredited laboratory environment, contribute your expertise and develop our laboratory services together with your team.

You can do that.

  • Among other exciting tasks, you will take over the organization of the accredited microbiological laboratory.
  • You will accompany internal audits, test equipment monitoring and ring tests.
  • You will perform microbiological analyses using classical approach techniques and various biochemical tests as well as microscopy.
  • You supervise the employees of the microbiological laboratory and ensure that the analyses are carried out in conformity.
  • You work closely with other departments and support the optimization of laboratory services.

You bring that with you

  • Degree as laboratory assistant:in EFZ field of biology or dairy farmer/laborant:in
  • Several years of experience in microbiological analysis, ideally in the food sector.
  • High interest regarding new trens in your area of responsibility.
  • Good knowledge of German in oral and written form.
  • A secure handling of MS Office and at best SAP.

This is what we offer you

  • Sabbatical & Holiday Shopping: Give yourself unpaid holidays or extend your holiday entitlement by up to two additional weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Attractive surcharges & additional services: You receive 20% time allowance from 8 pm, paid warm-up times, a time credit for changing times and a fair compensation for piquetting outside your working hours.
  • Further education and development opportunities: Emmi focuses on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
