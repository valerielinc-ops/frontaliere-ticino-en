Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Salem-Spital | Bern Occupation per: By appointment Type of employment: Indefinite reference number: 69851

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus. We attach importance to a family-oriented and professional care of pregnant women, childbearers and their caregivers. The individual and self-determined birth experience with a wide range of offers is the focus of our work. For our birth department with a small neonatology area, 19 beds and around 800 births per year, we are looking for you to support our team.

Your benefits to us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in the fitness subscription

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • You take over the holistic care and accompaniment of pregnant women, childbearers and their caregivers
  • You ensure the quality and continuity in the care sheet of pregnancy, birth and postpartum
  • Advising families and providing guidance on breastfeeding in accordance with UNICEF guidelines are just as much a part of your tasks as working closely with the Postpartum Department
  • In addition, you are committed to a good interdisciplinary cooperation with our doctors and other specialist areas
  • If you are interested, you will also take on the function in the area of vocational training and will be involved in accompanying and supervising the interns, learners and students in the department.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a midwife HF or FH and ideally already have professional experience
  • As a midwife in the birthroom in the document doctor system, you have a high level of independence as well as a reliable and responsible working method
  • Initiative, flexibility and resilience set you apart
  • We address an empathetic, communication-strong team member

For more information, Anette, Conductor Gebärzimmer, is at your disposal under T. If you have any questions about the application process, you can contact recruiting.bern@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by e-mail for data protection reasons. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
