The Position Senior Data Center Network Engineer (d/f/m) Introduction At Roche, we believe that every voice matters, and we invite you to bring your unique perspective to our team. As a Senior Data Center Network Engineer, you will join a culture of technical excellence, architecting resilient, high-performance infrastructures that empower our global mission. You will act as a technical expert, leveraging advanced automation to solve critical business challenges and drive our digital evolution.

The Opportunity

  • Architect Scalable Networks: Lead the end-to-end design and deployment of "first-time-right" network solutions, specifically focusing on Spine-Leaf topologies and L2/L3 fabrics.
  • Drive Technical Leadership: Act as a Solution Architect for large initiatives, managing resource estimation, dependencies, and risk mitigation across technical teams.
  • Implement Network as Code: Modernize operations by developing and maintaining Arista AVD, Ansible, and CI/CD pipelines to ensure scalable, repeatable deployments.
  • Master Complex Problem Solving: Collaborate with cross-functional teams to diagnose complex network incidents, performing deep root-cause analysis to prevent recurrence.
  • Maximize System Reliability: Define and maintain Service Level Objectives (SLOs) through advanced telemetry and streaming analytics to ensure "five-nines" availability.
  • Influence Strategic Roadmaps: Translate technical roadmaps into business value for stakeholders and steer vendor product evaluations to align with long-term strategy.
  • Lead Seamless Migrations: Oversee high-stakes brownfield-to-greenfield migrations with a focus on hitless cutovers and zero-downtime execution.
  • Foster Innovation and Growth: Proactively refine fabric performance and mentor junior engineers, contributing to a workplace culture of continuous learning.

Who you are

  • Experienced Architect: You bring deep mastery of Arista EOS and EVPN-VXLAN architectures, with a proven track record of designing large-scale fabrics.
  • Educated Professional: You hold a Bachelor’s degree in a technical field and bring years of relevant industry experience; CCIE-level expertise is highly desirable.
  • Automation Expert: You are proficient in Infrastructure as Code (IaC), specifically using Arista AVD, Ansible, and CI/CD pipelines for network automation.
  • Strategic Thinker: You possess the ability to translate complex system concepts into clear, actionable insights for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • Resilient Problem Solver: You demonstrate expert-level troubleshooting skills and remain decisive in high-pressure, complex technical scenarios.
  • Project Leader: You have significant experience leading technical teams through complex project dependencies and risk management.
  • Visionary Engineer: You are a proactive learner who stays ahead of industry trends to maintain architectural excellence in modern fabric environments.
