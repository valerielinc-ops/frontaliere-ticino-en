Go higher?! Schindler Professional Training. Training space of Polymechanic-ne CFC / Elevator assembler for 2027 in Sion

Welcome to the world of urban mobility! The Schindler Group, founded in 1874, is one of the world's leading elevators and escalators. Thanks to its mobility solutions, Schindler transports more than 2 billion people worldwide every day. The company owes this success to its approximately 70,000 employees operating in more than 100 countries.

Schindler vocational training

Schindler Vocational Training is a leading training centre with many years of experience and modern infrastructure. Throughout Switzerland, we offer practical training for nearly 300 apprentices in more than 10 different occupations. With the Schindler Next Generation program, we offer opportunities for part-time students and trainees. To our professionals of tomorrow, we offer attractive career opportunities and thus make a valuable contribution so that the world continues to be mobile also in the future.
