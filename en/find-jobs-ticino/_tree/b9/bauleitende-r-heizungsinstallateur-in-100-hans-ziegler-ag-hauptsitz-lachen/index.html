Construction manager/heating installer 100% Do you know us? We are a Swiss company with expertise in sanitary, heating/cooling and ventilation/air conditioning technology. With our locations in Lachen and Uster and around 110 employees, we operate in the cantons of Schwyz, Glarus, St. Gallen and Zurich. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the leading provider of cross-trade building technology in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. You keep an overview on the construction site and like to lead projects from assembly to handover? Then you are exactly right with us!
