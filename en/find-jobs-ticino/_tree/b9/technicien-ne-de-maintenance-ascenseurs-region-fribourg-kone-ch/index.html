Together for more than 115 years and together towards the future KONE is a world leader in the manufacture and maintenance of elevators, stairways and automatic doors, employing more than 60,000 people. We are known for our innovative and sustainable solutions. We also offer an open, Finnish and Nordic work culture that values teamwork and diversity.

KONE (Switzerland) SA is represented by 5 branches and nearly 400 employees. Our corporate culture is based on #CARE , #COLLABORATION , #CUSTOMER and #COURAGE - the values we live in every day in order to create an inclusive, sustainable and innovative working environment.

We are looking for you as a Maintenance Technician Elevators for the Fribourg region.

Your mission

After in-depth training, you intervene independently on a varied elevator park (residential, tertiary, public) and you represent KONE with our customers in the region.

Your concrete contribution

  • Conduct maintenance, service interventions and repairs within defined quality and safety standards
  • Ensure a safe working environment that complies with all legal requirements, directives and internal procedures
  • Ensure professional and constructive communication with clients, interlocutors and end users
  • Take responsibility for the safety and proper functioning of the allocated facilities
  • Ensure proper and orderly management of the site's environment during interventions
  • Identify and report additional repair needs, in coordination with on-site clients
  • Prepare accurate, complete and timely response reports
  • Participate in periodic picket service and failure response (helpline)

Value added

  • Recognized technical training in electrical or mechanical fields
  • An experience in the elevator area would be a real plus.
  • Great manual know-how and ability to work independently
  • Very good command of French (minimum level C1)
  • Taste for customer contact and willingness to represent KONE professionally outside
  • Valid category B driving licence
  • Court case blank
  • Home in Switzerland (Swiss or foreign nationality with a valid residence permit)
  • Your home is 30 minutes from Fribourg. This makes it possible to reconcile work and private life and facilitates collaboration within the team.

Our offer for your future

  • Work in a global enterprise with a large network and international collaboration.
  • An innovative, professional and user-friendly working environment that emphasizes the well-being of employees.
  • Many opportunities for further training (e.g.: KONE Academy in Hanover) and active support for your professional development
  • Remuneration according to the market, a service car with 100% authorized private use (Fr 150.-/month), a meal allowance of Fr 25.–/day worked (without leaving), at least 25 days off, free life insurance as well as other benefits such as a high performance LPP and participation in exciting business events.
  • Fair Pay Certified: Equal pay for women and men – transparent and verified
  • Commitment to sustainable development: carbon neutrality target by 2030
