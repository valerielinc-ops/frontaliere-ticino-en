Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, get involved and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • You reliably distribute our shipments within German-speaking Switzerland and adhere to the schedule of dispatch
  • Timely and friendly you serve our customers
  • You handle transport documents carefully and ensure correct handling
  • The control of the exchange devices is also part of your responsibility
  • When loading and unloading the goods you pack and secure the load properly
  • You keep your vehicle clean and in good condition – you take care and maintenance carefully

Why do you succeed?

  • You have the driving license of category C or CE and like to be on the axle
  • Experience in general cargo transport is an advantage and helps you to find your way quickly
  • Good knowledge of Swiss geography supports you in safe navigation
  • Resilience, flexibility and teamwork characterize you – even in hectic situations
  • You work fast and clean
  • Careful handling of vehicles and work equipment is a matter of course for you
  • You can communicate well in German – both verbally and in writing

Your benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Quality bonus: Quality is written in our capital and rewarded
  • Central introductory training: You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Generous expense regulations: With our expense regulations you are well taken care of

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
