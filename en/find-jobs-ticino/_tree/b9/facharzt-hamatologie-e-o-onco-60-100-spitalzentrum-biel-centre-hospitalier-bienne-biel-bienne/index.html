Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population? Oncology and hematology are innovative specialist areas and key elements of medical care in our network, we work in modern spaces in the center of Biel. The Clinic for Oncology and Hematology at the Biel Hospital Center provides committed and interdisciplinary care for our patients. She expects a responsible task, a versatile job in a motivated team and an attractive environment. The Biel Hospital Center has 3 certified cancer centers (intestinal center, prostate center, breast center). We work closely with the University Hospital for Hematology and Oncology at Inselspital Bern and are a centre of the Swiss Association for Clinical Cancer Research (SAKK).

To supplement our team, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement

Principal Physician/Chief Physician Hematology 60-100 %

You do.

  • Independent support of hematological consultation hours and therapies in the modern facilities of the hospital center in MEDIN Biel
  • Participation and moderation / management of interdisciplinary tumor board
  • Cooperation and maintaining good relations with the assigning doctors
  • Participation in the further training of assistant doctors and nursing staff
  • Commitment in the sense of increasing optimization of cooperation and processes
  • Consultative care of inpatients

That sets them apart.

  • Specialist title Hematology or equivalent with MEBEKO recognition
  • Ability to independently conduct a hematological consultation
  • Respectful, solution-oriented, integrative personality with an interest in interdisciplinary cooperation and good communication skills
  • Quality awareness with a focus on safe, efficient and resource-efficient processes
  • Engagement in the further and further training of the hematological junior (doctors and nurses)
  • Language competence German or French at the level of mother tongue with knowledge of the other language

They benefit from it.

  • We offer you attractive employment conditions, modernly designed premises in a central location and a workplace in the immediate vicinity of the station.
  • We live diversity: multilingual colleagues from different professions in the multicultural city of Biel/Bienne
  • We work together: a large interdisciplinary cooperation and a lot of mutual support. We continue to evolve: further education opportunities and the opportunity to actively shape the future of the hospital center
  • We offer you various discounts (pharmacy, public transport or leisure) as well as an internal crèche
