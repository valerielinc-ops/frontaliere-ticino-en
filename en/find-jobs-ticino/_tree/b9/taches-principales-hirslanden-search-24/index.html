Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Clinique Cecel Permanent

Reference Number: 68964

The Hirslanden Group is Switzerland's largest medical network. We offer all individuals, regardless of their situation, high-level individual medical care to improve their quality of life - from birth to old age and from prevention to cure. As part of our Corporate Together We Care strategy, our ultimate goal is to improve the quality of life of people who trust us.

MAIN TASKS

For one of our doctor specialized in visceral surgery at Cecil, you will perform the following tasks:

  • Provide advanced clinical practice including full assessment of health status, prescribing and interpreting examinations
  • Adapt medication according to convention
  • Care for patients and referral to other occupational health processes
  • Accompany patients on their journey, inform relatives and ensure quality and safety of care
  • Possibility of conducting an ad hoc clinical research activity

YOUR PROFILE

  • University Master of Science in Nursing Practice (MScIPS)
  • Graduated nurse, for a foreign diploma, Red Cross recognition is necessary
  • Dynamic, communicative and positive, you like working as a multidisciplinary team
  • Flexible and invested in your function you like to evolve in a demanding and motivating environment that requires autonomy and anticipation

WE OFFER YOU

In addition to an attractive workstation in the heart of Lausanne, you will integrate a dynamic team that looks forward to welcoming you into a human-sized structure.

You will benefit from attractive conditions of employment with, among other things, opportunities for continuing training and development throughout your career with us, as well as social benefits and other interesting benefits.

For an optimal balance between work and private life, we offer our employees various contractual models, including part-time and FLEXible contract. There is also the possibility to enjoy up to 7 weeks of holiday thanks to our offer "Congé-Plus".

In addition, employees working in uniform shall be granted 4 days of additional annual leave in proportion to the time of attendance.

See all the conditions of employment and employee benefits by clicking Benefits at the Cecil Clinic

YOUR CANDIDATURE

Valérie Kolecek, Head of HR, is pleased to receive your full application (motivation letter, curriculum vitae, copies of your diplomas and certificates).

For further information, Ms Fabienne Porzadny, fabienne.porzadny@hirslanden.ch, is at your disposal.

Please note that for data protection reasons, we cannot consider paper files (which will not be returned) or applications by e-mail. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
