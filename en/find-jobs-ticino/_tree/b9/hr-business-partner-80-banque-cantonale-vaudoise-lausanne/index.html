Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Human Resources department, we are looking for a:

HR Business Partner Announcement - 80% Your main tasks

  • act as a strategic partner for management in the management and development of their teams,
  • recruiting senior managers,
  • ensure the implementation of processes (integration, development and skills development, performance and remuneration, succession and appointment), ensuring compliance with HR policies and assuming the operational part;
  • be the point of contact of collaborators for any type of questions and / or reflections and intervene as mediator in case of particular situations,
  • actively participate in projects. Your profile:
  • university or higher HR training,
  • work experience of at least 5 years in a similar position within a bank/financial structure an asset,
  • ability to structure and conduct projects,
  • good knowledge of Swiss human resources law,
  • Relationship ease, leadership, autonomy and proactivity.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let us write together three stories, one of your career, one of your bank, one of our region.
