You burn for the care and training of learners - become part of our team employment level: 60-100% admission by agreement

You don't have to know everything with us. But you can ask anything. You can try out ideas and take responsibility. For us, lean means: giving responsibility, trusting and becoming better together. Become part of our rehab station - a varied and responsible task and a motivated team is waiting for you! We create experiences together and cultivate an open, warm and honest culture. We achieve success together in a team. You move that with us

  • In the interdisciplinary rehabilitation in the field of internal medicine (with an internistic and geriatric focus), you carry out the delegated nursing measures professionally and ensure the entire nursing process.
  • You care for and accompany medium to severely needy patients.
  • You support the interdisciplinary cooperation and coordination of the offered services. You inspire us...
  • With your completed training as a specialist health EFZ.
  • With your ability to act calmly and objectively in complex and delicate situations.
  • With your readiness for shift work (day and late duty) Barmelweid is the leading specialist and rehabilitation clinic in northwestern Switzerland and the largest psychosomatic clinic in Switzerland. In 285 beds, the clinic takes care of patients in hospital. The clinic specializes in geriatric, internistic, cardiovascular and pulmonary rehabilitation, long covid, psychosomatic medicine and psychotherapy, psychocardiology and sleep medicine. At Barmelweid, the focus is on lean thinking and methods: all efforts are geared towards patients. This includes standardized safety rounds, the patient board and the leveling of the load on the stations by means of flows. The care in the department of Psychosomatic Medicine and Psychotherapy is completed with the method of reference care, the care in the department of Internal Medicine with the care managers. As a rule, patients stay on the Barmelweid for 2 to 6 weeks. This gives our nurses time to engage with the patients in depth. You can expect a lively company with interprofessional work that retrieves all the skills of a nurse. Barmelweid employs more than 750 people. It is located at an altitude of about 800 meters on a clearing in the Aargau Jura and is embedded in a unique environment with a nature park.
