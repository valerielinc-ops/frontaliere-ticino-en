Full description

In order to drive our continuous organic growth, we are constantly looking for highly qualified professionals. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a dedicated specialist cleaning validation (a) 100%

In our team "Cleaning Validation" you develop and carry out cleaning validations for new or existing plants or for new products. They create cleaning validation plans and reports and are primarily responsible for coordinating the entire cleaning validation process. In the event of deviations or anomalies, clarify them and develop solutions. You work in close cooperation with the operator of the production facilities and the quality assurance and quality control departments.

Do you like to actively participate and enjoy good documentation practice and the development of cleaning validations? Their tasks include:

Their tasks

  • Cooperation in the development of cleaning validation strategies
  • Planning and implementation of cleaning validations in cooperation with other departments
  • Preparation of technical documentation (plans and reports) for cleaning validation according to good documentation practice
  • Coordination of the entire cleaning validation process in the GMP environment
  • Supporting production in solving specific/difficult problems during cleaning validation
  • Support for periodic revalidations of validated cleanings
  • Assistance in processing change requests for cleaning methods (change control procedures) in the GMP environment

Your profile

  • Proven several years of professional experience in cleaning validation
  • Practical experience in the GMP environment
  • Master or doctorate in chemistry or a related field of advantage
  • Professional and project-related leadership skills
  • Strong communication and organisational skills
  • Analytical, systematic and solution-oriented working method
  • High flexibility, independence and teamwork
  • Excellent knowledge of German
  • Excellent MS-Office knowledge
  • Knowledge of peptide chemistry is a plus

Our offer

  • A dynamic and rapidly growing working environment with internal development opportunities
  • Flexible working time arrangements with the option of home office and the option to purchase additional holidays with reduced workload
  • Employee support through numerous internal and external training offers
  • 60% takeover of pension fund contributions by Bachem AG as well as possibility for over-compulsory provision with our elective savings plan
  • Access to the advantage platform Swibeco with discounts from external partners
