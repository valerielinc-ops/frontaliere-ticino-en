Full description

Do you have several years of experience in the field of security services and would like to support the cooperative Migros Basel, in which you, as an employee, provide security services for more security for our customers and employees? As a shop detective, you further support us in reducing possible thefts.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Basel, BZM GMBS, Ruchfeldstrasse 15, 4142 Munichstein

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You monitor and control the sales premises to prevent theft, as well as to maintain peace and order
  • Ensure visible presence with increased gang crime
  • You edit and report thefts.
  • You provide assistance for all adverse events (fire, evacuation, crime) and medical emergencies

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience: security service
  • Completed initial or continuing education: in the field of security
  • You have several years of experience in the field of security services
  • You are an honest and reliable person and stand out through absolute discretion
  • You are emotionally and physically resilient and can handle challenging situations
  • You are also ready to work on Saturday and Sunday
  • German (flowing)
