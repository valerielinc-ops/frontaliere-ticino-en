Put to the Point

You are looking for a varied challenge in a versatile service environment. In addition to the development and implementation of specifications, it gives you pleasure to develop individual solutions for different needs in the areas of infrastructure, mobility and security and to implement them in an agile day-to-day operation.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: permanent
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20688
  • Location: Guisanplatz 1b, 3014 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Operationally implement and optimize access management
  • Operationally implement and optimize alarm management
  • Perform function as object safety officer at the Berne site and lead the emergency organisation
  • Continuously optimize fleet management and take responsibility for mobility strategy and ensure procurement, operation and liquidation of armasuisse’s fleet
  • Represent the areas of logistics, safety and fleet management as sub-project manager in administrative and inter-administrative projects
  • Represent the team management of real estate logistics, operational tenant representation and property security in personnel, professional and strategic terms

This makes you unique

  • Basic commercial/technical training with education and training in the field of object safety FA, HF or equivalent
  • Professional experience in the field of emergency management and object safety, management experience desired
  • Strong innovative and analytical capabilities for the development of integral solutions and their independent and reliable implementation
  • High level of social competence and strong teamwork to jointly develop, understandably communicate and credibly enforce guidelines
  • Enjoy professional leadership in a matrix organization and working with different stakeholders
  • Very good oral and written expression in German. Basic knowledge in French, Italian or English (verbal) desired
