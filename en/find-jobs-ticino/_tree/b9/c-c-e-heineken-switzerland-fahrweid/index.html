Full description

For our subsidiary Stardrinks AG, we are looking for an independent and motivated person at our Fahrweid location to work as a Category C/E driver

You are responsible for the punctual and needs-based delivery of all products in our range to our customers – primarily in the catering, hotel and retail sectors.

In doing so, you ensure that the vehicle is roadworthy in accordance with the requirements of the Road Traffic Act and that the statutory provisions on loading (load securing, weight, weight distribution) are complied with.

This varied and dynamic role includes, among other things, the following

Tasks

  • Transport of beverages to catering businesses and events
  • Collection of empty containers
  • Management of customer warehouses
  • Transport of refrigerated trailers and dispensing vehicles
  • Correct documentation using GreenMile
  • Support in the warehouse as needed

Requirements

To successfully carry out this responsible role, you bring the following:

  • A valid Category C driving licence (mandatory), ideally supplemented by Category E
  • Professional experience as a driver
  • Impeccable manners, reliability and dependability
  • Independent and structured way of working
  • Good German language skills, both spoken and written
  • Even in hectic times, you keep an overview and strike the right tone
  • Physical fitness and resilience
  • Ideally, residence in the Fahrweid region
