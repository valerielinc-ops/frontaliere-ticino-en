Full description

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and fairness. By joining the Lausanne Care Foundation, you are committed in a concrete way to promoting health and well-being in the canton of Vaud, within expert, motivated and enthusiastic teams, in a varied activity rich in human contacts. In the field, you intervene at the place of life or activity of the beneficiaries. You define with customers the solutions best suited to their situations and thus develop a fine knowledge and a privileged relationship of trust with them. Being part of a team that is strong and supportive, the exercise of these professions allows for a great autonomy but requires great responsibility and flexibility on the part of our employees. In order to complete our teams, La Fondation Soins Lausanne is looking for a suite or to agree on several:

Mental Health Nurse (M/F)

Participation rate between 60% and 100% Fixed contract with undetermined duration

Your Mission

  • Accompany clients with mental disorders or psychiatric illnesses in their home living project. Working from a partnership perspective by giving meaning and coherence while working closely with the people around them and their loved ones to develop bonds of trust
  • Develop the therapeutic project taking into account the complexity of care and develop it according to evolution
  • Pilot the overall and integrative care of clients while relying on in-house stakeholders and interdisciplinary teams and in good coordination with the institutions involved in the care network
  • Actively contribute to the sustainable home care of clients through prevention and health promotion measures while taking into account individual needs with personalized care and promoting client autonomy
  • Acting in support and guidance of interdisciplinary teams in the care of clients in general care or mental health
  • Participate in development projects and working groups on continuous improvement of mental health care services in the area of home care and support
  • Actively collaborate on the activities of interdisciplinary teams with a unique role allowing you to value your nursing expertise as an integral part of the health system

Your profile and skills

  • Bachelor's degree in nursing or equivalent (foreign diploma recognized by the CRS)
  • Minimum experience of 2 years in the psychiatric and/or psychogeriatric field in Switzerland
  • Experience in the medical and social field a strong asset and interest in community health
  • Ability to collaborate in networking and multidisciplinarity
  • Responsibility and professional ethics

We offer

  • A stimulating and warm work environment where the caregiver's autonomy is highlighted
  • A working environment that favours human contact and exchange
  • Exciting work in a pleasant environment for the well-being of clients
  • Fair working conditions and remuneration
  • Conditions of occupational security which extend beyond the minimum benefits provided by law

Benefits of joining us

  • Integration plan by occupation, offer of rich and varied continuing training, opportunities for vocational development
  • Gender pay equity ( Fair-ON-Pay certification)
  • Remuneration according to the Collective Labour Convention (CCT) of the public health sector of voodoo
  • Favourable offers with preferential rates in service companies around sport, health and welfare and leisure
  • Soft mobility policy and participation in public transport costs
  • Five weeks of vacation per year and six weeks from 50 years
