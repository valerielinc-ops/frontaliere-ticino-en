Description of post

Are you currently in human resources training and want to develop concrete experience in a dynamic and stimulating environment? We propose that you join the Department of Medicine's human resources team as part of a six-month intracursus contract. The HR team of the Department of Medicine is composed of six HR professionals and professionals accompanying 1'500 employees and their line managers. You will have the opportunity to discover a local HR function, at the heart of the human and organizational challenges of a large university hospital. Along with the team, you participate in the administrative follow-up of employees throughout their professional career. You contribute to the preparation of HR documents, the monitoring of administrative files, data management and various support activities essential to the smooth functioning of the team. You also support the department's internal communication by participating in the preparation of presentations, the updating of information materials and the dissemination of communications for employees. With direct contact with managers, employees and internal partners, you develop a concrete understanding of HR activities in the field. You are required or required to respond to various requests, to direct the interlocutors to the appropriate resources and to contribute to the quality of service provided by the HR team.

Qualifications

This internship is part of a Bachelor/Master degree or HES course and requires an intracursus internship agreement. You are currently undergoing training in human resources, management, administration or related fields of interest to HR activities. Organised or organised and rigorous or rigorous, you are able to manage several administrative tasks simultaneously while respecting the expected deadlines and quality standards. You have perfect command of French in both writing and speaking and excellent editorial skills. You have a good spirit of analysis and synthesis, as well as a great attention to detail. You appreciate the administrative work and attach particular importance to the quality and reliability of the information processed. You master the usual office tools and are comfortable with computer tools in general. Experience with HR information systems is an asset. Evolved in a dynamic and constantly changing environment, you show flexibility, responsiveness and a good ability to adapt to changing priorities. Curious or curious and proactive or proactive, you do not hesitate to seek the necessary information from your interlocutors or in the documentation available in order to provide adequate answers to the requests entrusted to you. You have an excellent sense of service and enjoy contact with various interlocutors. Your discretion, your sense of confidentiality and your professionalism allow you to evolve in an environment that processes sensitive data on a daily basis. Finally, you love working as a team and easily develop trusting relationships with your interlocutors through your listening, diplomacy, availability and positive attitude. Your curiosity and desire to learn allow you to integrate quickly and actively contribute to the HR team's activities.
