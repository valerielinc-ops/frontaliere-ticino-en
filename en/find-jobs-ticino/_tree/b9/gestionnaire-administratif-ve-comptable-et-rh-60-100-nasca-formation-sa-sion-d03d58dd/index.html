Full description

Want to join a team where every day is an opportunity to meet new challenges and an opportunity to progress?

At Nasca Formation, we are looking for a

Administrative Manager

(60-100%)

and ready to contribute actively to the life and development of our organization, in a stimulating and constantly changing environment.

We offer you a varied position in the heart of the Valais capital, in a committed and energetic team. Here, team spirit and skill sharing are part of our DNA.

Your main missions

(in collaboration with our finance and HR experts)

  • Routine administrative management and case tracking.
  • Actively participate in the establishment, monitoring and monitoring of billing.
  • Ensure the seizure, control and monitoring of accounting records until they are completed.
  • Participate in salary management and monitoring, in coordination with internal stakeholders.
  • Administrative monitoring of social insurance (AVS, LPP, APG, etc.).
  • Manage the different stages of the staff life cycle (entry, transfer, exit).
  • Ensure that administrative and HR records are maintained and updated.
  • Contribute to the improvement and smooth functioning of administrative, accounting and HR processes.

Your profile

  • Commercial training (CFC of trade employee or equivalent), ideally supplemented by training in accounting and/or human resources
  • Professional experience confirmed for at least 3 years in a multifunctional administrative function, including accounting and HR tasks.
  • Good knowledge of Swiss accounting and billing processes.
  • Practical knowledge of Swiss salary management and social insurance.
  • Good mastery of common computer tools (MS Office) and ease with accounting and salary management software.
  • Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written.
  • Organizational sense, rigour and ability to manage multiple files simultaneously.
  • Autonomy, versatility, initiative and sense of responsibility.
  • Discretion and respect for confidentiality, particularly in the processing of salary data and staff.
  • Team spirit, flexibility and approach oriented solutions.

Why join us?

  • A meaningful mission where every day you make a difference
  • A passionate team and a stimulating working environment
  • A framework of autonomy and creativity to develop your ideas
