Full description

PROFILE

The systems engineer Safety Systems is responsible for the technical integration, verification, validation and licensing support of safety-related railway systems such as ETCS, national train protection systems, train radio systems and driving data registration systems. It shall ensure conformity with the requirements of the CCS TSI, EIRENE FRS/SRS TSI, ERA subsets and national licensing requirements.

As a key technical function, he coordinates the collaboration between engineering, suppliers, customers, authorities, reviewers and commissioning teams across the entire project lifecycle.

Competences

  • Completed degree in electrical engineering, systems engineering, mechatronics or comparable
  • Experience in Systems Engineering
  • Experience in technical specifications, testing and commissioning

• Experience in requirements management (e.g. Polarion, DOORS or similar tools)

  • Knowledge of ETCS, PZB, LZB or other systems

• Knowledge of train radio and driving data registration is advantageous • Willingness to travel up to 20%

  • Analytical and structured working method
  • High personal responsibility and strong communication and coordination skills
  • Good knowledge of German and English
  • Enjoy working in interdisciplinary teams
  • Interest in rolling stock

TASKS

  • Technical support for bid projects
  • Evaluation of supplier solutions
  • Analysis of customer requirements regarding ATP and security systems
  • Technical coordination with customers and suppliers
  • Participation in system and integration concepts

Requirements Engineering

  • Analysis and evaluation of customer requirements
  • Establishment and specification of requirements for driving data registration

• Ensure traceability and consistency of requirements over the entire V cycle

  • Requirements management over the entire development process

System integration

  • Integration of ETCS, Class B systems, train radio systems and train data registration

• Definition, coordination and tracking of electrical, mechanical and software interfaces to vehicles and subsystems

  • Review of schematics and software requirements
  • Coordination with internal and external stakeholders on system integration

Verification and validation • Planning and coordination of FAT, SAT, integration and type tests in cooperation with suppliers, customers and test centres

  • Preparation of test plans
  • Review and evaluation of test results

• Establishment and review of technical evidence for TSI CCS conformity and vehicle approval

Entry into service and approval • Technical support for commissioning, type tests and system approval

  • Accompanying authorities, customer and expert tests

• Technical support of homologation and licensing procedures in cooperation with customers, experts and authorities
