Mission To develop our Digital Development department, we are looking for an intern to support our strategic initiatives of continuous improvement and digital continuity in product development. You will be an integral part of a young and dynamic team, where you can develop your talents and quickly gain skills on topics of high industrial importance. Your mission will be to lead a technological transition project by evolving our methodologies for managing technical product data. This project aims to optimize our product development process, improve operational performance, and strengthen collaboration across departments, from the design office to quality methods and production. Responsibilities Define and structure new 3D methodological standards, in collaboration with CAD experts and departments (design, metrology, functional analysis, methods, quality) Ensure digital continuity of product data, promoting collaboration between teams and eliminating information gaps and re-entries Deploy technological solutions around 3D to optimize access, reading, and utilization of models (inspection ranges, correction matrices, machine interactions) Contribute to integrating 3D data into quality processes by analyzing technical and methodological gaps and proposing suitable solutions Lead and support change management by monitoring performance indicators (KPIs), communicating project progress, and highlighting gains to the Executive Committee WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR As part of your engineering research & innovation, mechanical, or industrial engineering studies, you are seeking an end-of-studies internship, a Bachelor’s work, or equivalent. You are proactive and proficient with 3D design and modeling tools, with a strong interest in industrial process digitalization and technical data management.
