Full description

The Tarchini Group operates in Ticino in different sectors, from design to construction and management of industrial, commercial, logistics and residential buildings, as well as managing FoxTown Factory Stores Mendrisio. As part of the strengthening of its staff and to support the growth of the Group,

RESEARCH A

TECHNIQUE OF CANTIERE AND COORDINATOR FOR WORKING AND RESTRUCTURE

The selected person will take care of independently, from the beginning to the end, construction and small maintenance operations at the Group's stables, including renovations, standardized, renewal of common spaces and repairs. It will take about 70% of the activity at the stables and in the yards; the remaining time will be dedicated to the preparation of estimates, to the comparison of the offers of suppliers and contractors, to the orders and to the documentation. The selected person will respond to the Technical Manager and coordinate internal workers and external companies.

Requirements

• Diploma in technical (edile and/or plant) • Training as head of team, head of office or technician/SSS in conducting construction work, or equivalent experience • At least 5 years of experience in the yard with role of management of teams and firms • Experience in the maintenance and restructuring of stable occupants, in particular commercial, residential and used as offices • Proven experience in setting quotes and ordering materials • Read plans and familiarize yourself with using AutoCAD • Operational knowledge of all construction and safety standards at work • Good electrical knowledge, RVCS and maintenance facilities • AICAA/VKF fire requirements • License to conduct cat. B.

We offer

• Interesting and dynamic work • Retribution to capacity • Full-time use • Place of work Mendrisio and at the Group's premises in Ticino • Starting immediate activity or to be convented.
