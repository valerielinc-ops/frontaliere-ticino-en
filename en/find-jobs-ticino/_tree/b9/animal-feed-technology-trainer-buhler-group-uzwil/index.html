Full description

Would you like to pass on your expertise in feed technology and train people from all over the world in a practical manner? At our School of Feed Technology (SFT), you will combine sound process knowledge with pedagogical skills. They design theoretical and practical training courses, continuously develop training content and accompany customers and internal employees directly at our facilities or on site at the customer. Look forward to a versatile task in an international environment with plenty of room for manoeuvre and the opportunity to actively shape the future of education and training.

Tasks

  • You carry out theoretical and practical training in all areas of feed technology and impart your expertise to customers and internal employees in a practical manner.
  • They continuously develop training materials, teaching materials and training formats and adapt them specifically to the needs of the participants
  • You independently carry out tests with raw materials and training in our application center, analyze the product quality and derive optimization potential from this
  • They accompany international course participants during their training and ensure professional, competent and hospitable support.
  • They support our customers with mill inspections, technological issues and training on site and actively shape the professional and organizational development of our School for Feed Technology

Requirements

  • Completed apprenticeship as a miller: in the field of animal nutrition or alternatively in the food sector, as well as ideally several years of professional experience in a feed mill
  • In-depth knowledge in the field of feed waste disposal, in particular in the processes of grinding, mixing, compacting and cooling, as well as good knowledge in machine science and process engineering
  • Educational training and/or experience as a trainer:in, lecturer:in or Trainers: an advantage, combined with the pleasure of communicating complex content in an understandable and practical manner
  • Convincing, communicative and motivating personality with pleasure in working with people from different cultures as well as an independent and structured working method
  • Very good knowledge of German and English, knowledge of another foreign language is a plus
  • Readiness for worldwide travel of 20%

Two billion people eat food produced on Bühler plants every day. One billion people travel in vehicles whose parts were produced using Bühler technologies. With every decision, Bühler pursues the goal of reconciling the needs of the economy, people and nature. In this way, we develop solutions that open up sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed and mobility industries. We want to innovate for a better world, with a particular focus on healthy, safe and sustainable solutions. This is why we work with customers, start-ups, multinational corporations and scientists to achieve a faster impact together.
