Put to the Point

SECO’s economic cooperation and development service area is the federal competence centre for economic development cooperation. It supports partner countries in creating a sustainable framework for sustainable and inclusive economic development and in strengthening the private sector. In order for companies to invest, grow and remain competitive, they need reliable framework conditions in the partner countries. This includes access to capital, an attractive investment and business environment, and responsible corporate governance. The Department for Private Sector Promotion (WEIF) strengthens these framework conditions for companies – for Switzerland and its partners. It also promotes a sustainable financial sector, supports impact investing initiatives and is responsible for investment instruments, including guarantees.

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
