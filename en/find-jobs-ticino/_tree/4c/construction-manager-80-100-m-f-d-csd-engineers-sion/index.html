More than 1,000 employees spread across 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: the CSD group is a dynamic and constantly growing company. We are looking for a Construction Manager 80-100% (m/f/d) for our Environment branch in Sion (VS). ##

  • Develop network planning projects in a built and operational environment (roads)
  • Coordinate drawing work for tender preparation and project execution
  • Prepare tender documents
  • Monitor construction work and coordinate with involved partners (MO, municipal and cantonal services, networks, private entities, etc.) ##
  • Your profile
  • Technician in civil engineering, draftsman, or construction professional with CFC and experience in construction management (a diploma would be an advantage)
  • Experience in the field of road infrastructure and networks
  • Proficiency in standard drawing and management software (AutoCAD, Messerli, etc.)
  • Strong writing skills and experience in the implementation phase (tender preparation and construction management)
  • Willingness to grow and interest in company development
  • Versatility and ability to synthesize
  • Dynamic and autonomous personality with initiative and appreciation for teamwork
  • Driver's license ##

  • A dynamic work environment in a climate of mutual respect
  • Attractive conditions allowing great flexibility in work time management ## Placement agency offers will not be considered as part of this recruitment process. Therefore, CSD will not enter into any negotiations regarding payment of claims.
