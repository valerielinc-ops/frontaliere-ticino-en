Full description

The Pallas Clinics are specialized in ophthalmology and aesthetics. With over 350 employees and 17 locations, we are one of the leading providers in Switzerland. We offer a highly professional environment with attractive employment conditions and a motivated and dynamic team. If you have high commitment, interest in regular training and enjoyment of teamwork, excellent cooperation is already inevitable. Our modern infrastructure in all disciplines is continuously expanded to optimally support your career as an ophthalmologist.

To complement our existing team in our clinic group, we are looking for you as an initiative and independent personality.

Assistant physician Ophthalmology 80-100%, Region Aargau

Responsibility

You have already gained at least 3 years of experience in ophthalmology and are looking for a job that values your advanced skills, specifically promotes you and at the same time offers you a high quality of life? With us, you work in a state-of-the-art infrastructure, relieved of administrative bureaucracy and embedded in a strong medical network.

  • Consultation hour competence: Independent management of the general ophthalmological consultation hour including initial emergency assessments.
  • Diagnosis & therapy : Performation of ophthalmological basic diagnostics as well as common laser treatments (e.g. YAG, ALK).
  • Interdisciplinary exchange: Close cooperation with our specialists (e.g. Retina, Glaucoma, Refraktiv) in the cross-location network.
  • Knowledge Management: Active participation in internal training and joint case discussions.

Requirements

  • Education: Swiss or MEBEKO-approved Master's degree in medicine.
  • Experience: At least 3 years of clinical experience in Swiss ophthalmology (within the FMH curriculum).
  • Working method: Joy of independent, outpatient work with a high level of quality and service understanding.
  • Language Competence: Flowing knowledge of German (C1/C2) for clear, empathetic and address-oriented patient communication.

Benefits

  • Guaranteed continuing education status: Our location is recognized by the FMH/SIWF as a category B continuing education institution
  • We guarantee you a structured education according to the curriculum.
  • Safety through supervision: Even at your own location, you will not be left alone – an experienced specialist or senior physician is always physically on site.
  • Focus on medicine instead of bureaucracy: An experienced team of optometrists, orthoptists and administrative MPAs keeps your back free and relieves you of administrative tasks to the maximum.
  • Real work-life balance: regulated working hours during the week, no classic night shifts on site and a fair, plannable pikett system for more time for family and leisure. In addition, you can benefit from our broad-based medical team. All locations are very centrally located and our regulated working hours allow you time for family and leisure. The assistant doctor training is led by very experienced internal trainers.

Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to meeting you personally!
