Full description

Bethesda Spital AG | online since : 13.11.2025

As a successful Basel clinic with a private character, the Bethesda Spital combines top medicine with first-class service and a unique feel-good atmosphere. With the five focus topics of women’s medicine, pregnancy & childbirth, musculoskeletal system and rehabilitation as well as palliative care, our 6,000 inpatients annually benefit from state-of-the-art treatment centers under one roof – in the middle of one of the most beautiful hospital parks in northwestern Switzerland.

In our clinic for women’s medicine, approximately 1,800 births and 2,200 gynecological procedures are carried out annually by doctors and observing physicians employed at Bethesda Hospital. The Clinic for Women's Medicine is open 24 hours a day and seven days a week. From now on or by agreement, we are looking for a senior physician to strengthen our team (60 - 100%).

Their tasks

  • Self-employment of office hours in the field of obstetrics and gynecology
  • Independent conduct of special consultation hours, in particular urogynecology
  • Management of births, especially risk births from the age of 35. Week of pregnancy
  • Perform operations throughout the spectrum of standard gynecological and obstetric surgery, as well as emergency operations
  • Pre- and post-operative care of patients
  • Training of assistant doctors
  • Night, weekend and emergency reception services

They bring with them

  • Completed medical studies including MEBEKO recognition
  • Specialist training completed or advanced
  • Knowledge of German at least. Cat C1
  • High social skills and team skills
  • Preparedness

We offer

  • A versatile, responsible and interesting activity
  • An innovative field of work focusing on the continuous development of the quality of medical care
  • Organizational structures that promote interprofessional cooperation
  • Promoting personal and professional development through an attractive training programme
  • Access to free sports facilities such as Pilates, Yoga and CrossFit as well as the use of our gym for your health and well-being

Are you a team player and interested in this exciting challenge? We look forward to your online application. Our recruitment process has been defined according to environmental and economic aspects and does not provide for applications by post or email. Postal and e-mail applications will not be considered.
