Courts of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft

The courts of the Canton of Basel-Landschaft are responsible for the jurisdiction of the canton. At the level of the first instance, they consist of two civil district courts, the criminal court, juvenile court and coercive measures court as well as the tax and expropriation court. The second instance is the cantonal court with four departments. The court administration is attached to the cantonal court.

Civil District Court Basel Landscape West

The Civil District Court of Basel-Landschaft West in Arlesheim is competent as first instance court for the handling of disputed civil cases in the field of application of the Civil Code (e.g. family law, neighbouring law, inheritance law) and the Code of Obligations (e.g. employment contract law, tenancy law) as well as judicial matters in debt collection and bankruptcy law.
