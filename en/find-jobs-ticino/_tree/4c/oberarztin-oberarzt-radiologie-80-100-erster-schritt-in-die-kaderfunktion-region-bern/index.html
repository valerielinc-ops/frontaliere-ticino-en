You have recently completed your specialist title radiology or are about to – and are wondering if you are already ready for a senior doctor function? In this position, you don’t have to be able to do everything from day one. An experienced team accompanies you as you enter the new role, while you continue to work extensively in CT, MRI, ultrasound, conventional radiology and mammadiagnostics. Radiology is part of a regional acute care hospital and serves inpatient and outpatient patients as well as several external practice locations. The medical team consists of the chief physician, several senior physicians, around eight senior physicians and a few assistant physicians. This structure enables a close professional exchange, short decision paths and individual familiarization. The institute covers the entire spectrum of diagnostic radiology, from conventional radiology to CT, MRI and ultrasound to mammadiagnostics. The offer is complemented by interventional procedures and close cooperation with diagnostic neuroradiology.

Their tasks

  • Independent diagnosis of CT, MRI, X-ray and ultrasound examinations
  • Implementation of interventional basic procedures according to your previous experience
  • Care of outpatients and inpatients
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with the clinical departments as well as presentation of findings on reports and tumor boards
  • Gradual assumption of senior responsibility in clinical everyday life
  • Supervision, professional support and further training of assistant doctors

Your Competences

  • Radiology, German or EU-approved
  • Support for recognition in Switzerland by Medimeo
  • Alternatively, you are in the final phase of your specialist training and are expected to earn the title later this year.
  • Interest in a broad diagnostic activity and motivation to develop professionally
  • Independent, structured and quality-conscious working method
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation and the further education of assistant doctors
  • High social competence, team spirit and an appreciative treatment of patients and colleagues
  • Very good knowledge of German

Their advantages

  • Structured entry into the first obstetric responsibility
  • Direct professional support by several experienced cadre doctors
  • Broad activity including ultrasound, Doppler and vascular diagnostics
  • Possibility to gradually build a personal focus
  • No night services and scheduled late service until 10 pm
  • Late service approximately once a week with a later start on the following day

With Medimeo at your side, you will receive personal and discreet support in the recognition of your specialist title, in the application process and in organizational questions about a possible change to Switzerland.
