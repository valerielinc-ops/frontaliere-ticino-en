Full description

Luzerner Psychiatrie AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and provides its services at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professions and specialist fields work with humanity and expertise for the well-being of patients. The services are aimed at children, adolescents and adults into advanced age.

Assistant Physician 50-100% (m/f/d)

Your responsibilities

  • Psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment of children and adolescents
  • Conducting diagnostic and therapeutic interviews with children, adolescents and their parents/families
  • Working as a physician in tandem with psychologists
  • Collaborating with internal and external specialists and institutions
  • Participating in specialist consultations
  • On-call duty for the entire Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service (KJPD) as part of a large on-call pool

Your profile

  • Completed medical degree
  • Ideally, psychotherapy training already started or at an advanced stage
  • Preferably, experience in child and adolescent psychiatry or another specialty
  • Flexible, resilient, good-humoured and team-oriented personality
  • Native (Swiss) German speaker or very good German skills (C1)

Benefits

  • Appreciative and supportive working environment
  • Appropriate assumption of responsibility (deputy senior physician position if suitable)
  • Highly collegial and supportive interdisciplinary team
  • Progressive working conditions with attractive social benefits (coverage of the KTG premium and two thirds of the pension fund premium)
  • Individual planning of your postgraduate training
  • Opportunity to pursue a doctorate and/or undertake scientific work (UniLu)
  • Significant expansion of our services/specialist consultations
