Foreman: Road Construction and Civil Engineering Your Contribution with Us

  • Operational management of construction sites in road construction and surfacing
  • Leadership and coordination of site personnel as well as subcontractors
  • Deployment planning of personnel, machinery and materials
  • Monitoring of deadlines and execution quality
  • Ensuring occupational safety on the construction site
  • Preparation of daily reports and measurements

What Counts for Us

  • Completed basic training in construction
  • Further training as a certified foreman
  • Solid professional experience in road construction and team leadership
  • Structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Good German skills, spoken and written
  • Driving licence category B

Our Added Value for You With us, you will find a down-to-earth team that sticks together and works together, right in the Bern region. As a foreman, you lead your team directly on site, coordinate the processes and ensure that quality and deadlines are met. Versatile projects in road and civil engineering, a modern fleet of machinery and short distances to site management make your working day exciting. We offer you fair employment conditions, a culture of appreciation and further training that will help you move forward.
