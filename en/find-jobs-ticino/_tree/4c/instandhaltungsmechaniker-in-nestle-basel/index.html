Short overview position

Fabrik Basel is a high-performance production company of Nestlé Suisse S.A. and produces culinary products of the THOMY brand for Germany and abroad.

For our production department we are looking for a committed personality as a maintenance mechanic immediately or by agreement.

A day in the life of a maintenance mechanic

  • Maintenance and servicing work on bottling plants
  • Analysis and resolution of disruptions in day-to-day business
  • Production of spare parts or procurement from the manufacturer
  • Identification of optimization possibilities and implementation of technical improvements
  • Accompanying line staff for format and product changes
  • Technical competence training of line staff
  • Ensuring compliance with safety and quality standards

This makes them successful.

  • Completed apprenticeship as (poly)mechanic, industrial mechanic, mechatronics or related professions
  • Experience in maintenance, preferably in an industrial food business
  • In-depth knowledge of turning, welding, milling and making technical drawings
  • Experience in preventive maintenance, continuous improvement processes and 5S beneficial
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Openly and transparently communicating team player
  • Very good oral and written German skills
  • Readiness for flexible work assignments in a mainly 2-shift operation from Monday to Friday and readiness for Picettdienst
