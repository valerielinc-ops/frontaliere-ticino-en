Full description

Portrait The school Schwabgut is a lively and multicultural school in the west of Bern. The focus is on people, their abilities, their development and their well-being.

Tasks

Together with a class team, you will become part of a dedicated team. This team has the important and at the same time challenging task of individually supporting the children and accompanying them in their development into self-confident and independent personalities. A special emphasis will be placed on the promotion of self- and social skills in order to create a strong basis for further learning and coexistence.

Fr 08.20-12.00, 4 lessons Rule lessons Monday/Tuesday/Wednesday, 4-6 lessons MR (accompaniment bVSAi and integrative promotion), time and distribution of lessons may be more or less desired

In almost all lessons a second person is involved (healing teacher, class help, coteaching, speech therapy or DaZ)

Requirements

  • Open to new
  • Joy in dealing with children
  • Seeing our multicultural environment as an opportunity
  • We prefer to fill the position with a trained person. (teaching diploma or curative education)

We offer

  • A helpful, warm and open college
  • Support from the class team - here everyone helps everyone
  • All lessons are in the same 1st grade

Discussions on staffing shall be ongoing.
