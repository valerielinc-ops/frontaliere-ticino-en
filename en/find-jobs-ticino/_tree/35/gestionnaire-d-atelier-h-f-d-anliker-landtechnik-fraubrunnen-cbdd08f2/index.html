Anliker Landtechnik, part of Serco Retail AG, is a regional sales and service centre for the global brand CLAAS as well as other premium brands of agricultural and special machinery. Anliker Landtechnik offers a unique and complete range of products for professional agriculture. At the Fraubrunnen site, we are looking for a workshop manager to strengthen our team with immediate effect or by appointment.

These tasks inspire you

  • Leadership, motivation and further development of the workshop team including our apprentices
  • Planning and control of workshop utilization as well as service and repair orders
  • Responsibility for quality, deadlines and profitability
  • Advising our customers on all aspects of service, maintenance and repairs
  • Preparation and support of cost estimates and repair orders
  • Close cooperation with sales and spare parts service
  • Communication with customers, suppliers and sales partners
  • Organization of the on-call service during the harvest season
  • Active further development of our procedures and processes

  • Several years of professional experience in a similar function, ideally in agricultural technology, as well as management experience is an advantage
  • Fluency in German and French is an advantage
  • Communicative and down-to-earth personality who is willing to lend a hand
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented way of working
  • High quality and customer orientation
  • Entrepreneurial thinking and cost awareness
  • Category B driving licence
