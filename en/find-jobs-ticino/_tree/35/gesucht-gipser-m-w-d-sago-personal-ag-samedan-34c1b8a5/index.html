Full description

Their tasks

  • Execution of general plaster work in new, renovation and renovation construction
  • Interior and exterior plastering of walls, ceilings and facades
  • Application and processing of base plaster, white plaster and abrasion
  • Drywall construction and suspended ceilings
  • Mounting of plasterboard and lightweight structures
  • Fitting, smoothing and repair work
  • Preparation of facade insulations and external thermal insulation
  • Installation and processing of insulating materials
  • Performing decorative plaster work and surface designs
  • Preparation, cleaning and priming of various substrates
  • Carrying out repair and refurbishment work on existing buildings
  • Performing white cleaning work on walls and ceilings
  • Creation of edge protection profiles and clean closures
  • Operation of plaster machines and plastering equipment
  • Professional handling of plaster tools, machines and materials
  • Independent work according to building plans and specifications of the foreman
  • Compliance with safety and quality regulations on the construction site

Your profile

  • Completed training as a plaster dryer EFZ is advantageous
  • At least 3 years of professional experience as a plasterer (mandatory)
  • Very good knowledge of general plastering and plastering work
  • Experience in interior and exterior as well as in facade work
  • Safe handling of various types of plaster and plaster materials
  • Good knowledge of drywall and assembly of plasterboard
  • Experience in applying ground plaster, white plaster and abrasion
  • Knowledge of facade insulation and thermal insulation is advantageous
  • Experience in the new, renovation and renovation sector
  • Good knowledge of troweling, smoothing and surface work
  • Safe handling of common plastering machines and tools
  • Experience in reading and implementing building plans
  • Independent and professional working methods
  • German skills are advantageous
  • Category B driving licence is advantageous
  • Own car not required
  • Physically resilient, reliable and vertigo-free
  • Precise, clean and team-oriented work
