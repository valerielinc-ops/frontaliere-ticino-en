Full description

The State Chancellery is the staff office of the Cantonal Council and Government. The Department of IT Law and Data Protection manages and accompanies legislative projects in the context of the digital transformation of the administration of cantons and political communities and is the central legal advisory centre in the field of digitization and data protection issues.

What to expect

  • You lead complex legislative projects in the context of the digitalization of the Canton of St.Gallen and its municipalities, e.g. for electronic administrative procedures or in the field of artificial intelligence.
  • You lead the Department of IT Law and Data Protection (consisting of you and a lawyer in IT Law and Data Protection).
  • You participate in strategic project committees at the cantonal and federal level, including in the areas of artificial intelligence and data management.
  • You will develop opinions on political business in the field of IT law and data protection.
  • You advise and support eGovernment St.Gallen digitally, the departments and the State Chancellery on legal issues in digitization projects (including legal basis analyses, protection needs analyses, data protection impact assessments, contracting, instructions and ISDS concepts).
  • You bear the professional responsibility for revisions of the Cantonal Data Protection Act.
  • You control the further development of IT law and data protection and network with the relevant stakeholders of the Confederation, cantons and municipalities.

What distinguishes you

  • Completed legal training (master), attorney's or doctorate of advantage
  • Several years of professional experience with focus on digitalization issues in state and administrative law and/or data protection law
  • Legislation experience desired
  • Very structured, precise and independent working method
  • Very good digital skills and affinity for technical issues
  • Style-confident German, French skills of advantage
  • Sovereign treatment of different actors and stakeholders as well as interest in political processes and processes

What we offer

  • A versatile and responsible activity and the opportunity to actively participate in the digital transformation of the public sector
  • Collaboration in a committed team with broad stakeholder groups at cantonal, local and national level
  • Modern working time models and attractive training opportunities
  • Workplace in the government building in the old town of St.Gallen

Contact: Law and Legislation | sg.ch
