Credit Officer (m/f) 80-100 %

We are very much on Za(c)k. As a young subsidiary of the Basler Kantonalbank, we have launched the first Swiss smartphone bank, Zak. This makes us a tick faster, smarter and perhaps even more cheeky than other banks. We also deal openly with the topic of money, in general we address topics that are important to us directly. More women power, for example. That is why we are particularly pleased about applications from women. Men? Of course, they are still welcome. Want to know more about who we are and how we tick? Very happy.

Sandra, Angelo, Nadja and 5 other colleagues from the Team Credit Office in Basel are looking for reinforcements. In other words, you!

Your new job

  • Assess and decide on mortgage and real estate financing for private customers, real estate customers and housing cooperatives
  • Analyze creditworthiness, sustainability, lending and real estate values and continuously monitor credit risks
  • In the case of mortgages and commercial real estate, take into account project, property, market and rental risks
  • Support customer advisors in complex financing and explain credit decisions in an understandable way
  • Contribute expertise in the team and actively shape credit processes and training on specialist topics

We benefit from this

  • Banking or commercial education with financial reference, supplemented by further education or studies in Finance/Credit
  • Several years of experience in the Swiss mortgage and real estate financing business, focusing on residential, yield and mixed properties
  • Very good understanding of property valuation, credit risks and regulatory requirements in the mortgage sector
  • Strong analytical thinking, high risk awareness and precise, structured working methods with clear decisions
  • Very good communication skills in German; good knowledge of French and basic knowledge of Italian are a plus

This is how you benefit

  • An open approach to the topic of money, also with regard to equal pay – that really exists with us
  • Challenge with extremely exciting and varied cases
  • Flexible time work, opportunities for home office
  • An unexcited and uncomplicated team spirit
  • Do you expect an all-round good employment package from us? You are right, here are the details and many other advantages
