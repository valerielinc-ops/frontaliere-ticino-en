Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement as of 1 October 2026 or by agreement. Tasks: • They are responsible for the care process within the framework of the reference person concept, taking into account the care models and care diagnoses applied in the LUKS • Participation in improving the quality and effectiveness of care is also part of your area of responsibility • They take over the coordination of care in interprofessional cooperation. In doing so, you promote interdisciplinary exchange and thus contribute significantly to the holistic care of patients. • Your commitment to the guidance, accompaniment and assessment of trainees is of great importance. They promote their professional and personal development through targeted support, constructive feedback and an appreciative learning atmosphere Requirements • You have completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH. They bring the necessary knowledge and practical skills to act professionally even in demanding situations • In a dynamic working environment, you also keep an overview under pressure. Their open and solution-oriented nature promotes a positive working environment and supports efficient cooperation in the team • They have strong social skills in dealing with patients and their relatives and meet them with empathy, appreciation and empathy. We offer: • We offer you a varied, demanding and diverse area of responsibility. On our bed station, you have the opportunity to fully express your passion for care in an appreciative environment. • Thanks to our wide variety of internal and external neurorehabilitative educational offers, future prospects are always opening up for you. • A state-of-the-art hospital information system that digitally and comprehensively maps the entire patient process and lists all patient data at any time and up-to-date supports you around the clock. • As part of our working time model, we offer you the opportunity to shape the roster according to your needs, e.g. two-shift system, fixed work and Fridays, unpaid leave
