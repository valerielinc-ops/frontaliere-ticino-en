Who are we?

Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is an area of innovation and an important driver for the economic and cultural life of the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889.

Having signed the CoARA declaration, the University of Fribourg emphasizes qualitative assessment of academic achievement.

Your missions

The successful candidate will develop an internationally recognized competitive research program investigating the impact of environmental stressors on the prevention, pathogenesis, and clinical outcomes of female-predominant diseases. They will collaborate with the Freiburg Hospital (HFR), the University Hospital Bern (Inselspital), the University Hospital Lausanne (CHUV) and the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL).

Profile desired

  • Qualified applicants must have an outstanding track record of successful independent experimental, epidemiological and/or clinical biomedical research, preferably in disease areas that predominantly affect women.
  • Meaningful teaching experience of human medicine at the university level is mandate. Racing are taught in French and/or German.
  • The University of Fribourg is bilingual (French/German). Knowledge of the second language (level B1 oral) is expected. If this is not the case, it must be acquired within two years of taking up the position.
