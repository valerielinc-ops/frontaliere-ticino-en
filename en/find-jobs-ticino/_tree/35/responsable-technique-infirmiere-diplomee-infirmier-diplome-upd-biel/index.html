Psychiatry Bienne UPZ Münsingen

Your tasks

  • You can use your expertise and experience in a targeted way and actively support us in improving the processes of the care unit
  • You help ensure that collaboration within the team takes place on an equal footing, constructively and interprofessionally
  • You are helping to implement our concept of care unit and you are constantly developing it with us.
  • In collaboration with the interprofessional care team, you help stabilize patients and thus improve their quality of life.
  • In close collaboration with the management of the care unit, you guarantee patient-centred care, evidence and outcomes, especially in difficult situations
  • You are responsible for guaranteeing and promoting the quality of care in the unit
  • You provide specialized advice and coaching to nurses in the care process
  • You lead clinical case discussions within the team
  • You are actively involved in the implementation and development of the concept of care unit

Your profile

  • You completed an ES/HES graduate nurse training
  • You can justify at least 2 years of professional experience in psychiatry, preferably in a therapeutic center or similar field
  • You think in a way focused on goals and people
  • You resist stress well and like to meet the challenges encountered in an acute psychiatric unit

UPZ

The University Psychiatric Centre in Bern (UPZ) is the main centre of competence in psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region as a university clinic. It provides psychiatric care in the canton of Bern throughout the care chain, from early identification to outpatient treatment, day care and stationary treatment, to rehabilitation and forensic psychiatry. With more than 30 sites, UPZ offers a healthcare network covering the entire territory. The UPZ also makes an important contribution in the fields of education, training and research.
