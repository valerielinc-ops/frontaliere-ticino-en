Full description

Want to discover an inside bank and experience a unique learning experience in Switzerland?

By joining us as an apprentice, you are embarking on a 3-year trip to the various teams that make Swissquote, the leader of the online bank in Switzerland. Come and explore our world by following a comprehensive and varied training course. Guided by a learning master, you will spend several sessions from 3 to 6 months in different teams (Accounting, Marketing, Sales Support, Logistics, etc.) to learn their main jobs and carry out concrete missions. After these three years with us, you will be able to orientate towards the job you loved most. In addition to our practical training, you will also be able to take the specialized and general culture courses of the commercial vocational school.

And that's not all! We also offer great advantages, such as the half-price paid or preferential offers for sport (fitness, climbing, football...).

By joining Swisssquote, you integrate more than one company but a young working environment (35 years of average age), various (70 different nationalities) and friendly (no dress code required). Then come and grow up with us!
