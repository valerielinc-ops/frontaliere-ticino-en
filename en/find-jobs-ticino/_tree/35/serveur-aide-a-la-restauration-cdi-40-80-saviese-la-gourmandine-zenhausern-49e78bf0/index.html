[Zenhäusern Brothers SA]

Explore the taste of adventure with us!

In the heart of Valais and with its 40 years of history, our hybrid company, driven by the passion for flavours, is looking for committed and motivated personalities. From crispy bread to divine sweets, each creation celebrates authenticity.

Join a team of some 470 talents, where continuing training and career development flourish in a modern work environment. Reliability, Dynamism and Excellence \- our values form a corporate culture that places people at the centre.

Are you ready to actively build your future and become the artisan of your success?

Server/Aide à la restauration(m/f/d) \- CDI \- 4ю-80% \- Saviese La Gourmandine

Missions

  • You guarantee a daily welcome, advice and service to the customers without charge
  • You are attentive to customer demands and well-being
  • You promote specialties and new products
  • You create a welcoming and warm atmosphere
  • You ensure the setting up of tables and the opening and closing of the restaurant

What you bring:

  • Your experience of several years in catering allows you to value the services and menus offered.
  • You pay particular attention to customer satisfaction and know how to retain customers from your service.
  • You have very good knowledge in French (oral and written)
  • You make ice cream, pancakes, and drinks.
  • Ensure optimal presentation of the goods, including salad buffet, drinks, and ice cream.
  • You effectively manage the stocks, the cellar, the service
  • Flexible and stress-resistant, you know how to work autonomously and Sunday and Weekend work is not a brake for you.
  • In addition, you stand out for your sense of organization, your dynamism, your team spirit and a positive attitude at all times.
  • You are available for the following hours: Monday to Friday between 5:30am and 7pm, week\-end 5:30am and 6pm (variable hours 4 weeks in advance).

Your benefits:

  • 5 weeks of vacation
  • 13th salary
  • Wage increase 15% Sundays and public holidays (sales and catering sector)
  • Breakfast offered
  • Loyalty premiums
  • Continuing training
  • 20% discount on our products
  • Discounts at different partners (fleet discount, fitness, etc.)

Date of entry: immediately
