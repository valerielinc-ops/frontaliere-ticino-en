Full description

Reference code: JR133802

Since 1833, drive by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressed its inventiveness through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication.

YOUR MISSION

You ensure the delivery of the tools on time, with the expected quality and cost-effective. You optimize processes, resources and load/capacity management while maintaining sufficient agility to respond to production emergencies. You report live to the Manager of Methods & Industrialization.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

Your role will focus on the following missions

Team management

> Encourage, motivate and mentor a team of about 20 employees including 3 leaders.

> Promote skills development and versatility.

> Create a positive working environment, based on communication and teamwork.

> Define individual and collective goals and develop team skills.

> Manage relationship with external suppliers and manage subcontracted tool orders.

Production management

> Ensure the delivery of tools on time and according to quality standards.

> Plan and optimize service load/capacity matching.

> Communicate proactively on risks, deviations and action plans.

Strategy and development

> To be the driving force for the improvement of the department's tool design, manufacturing processes, equipment and organization.

> Study and define the integration of new production equipment and technologies, ensuring their relevance and cost-effectiveness.

> Ensure technological monitoring to anticipate developments in the sector.

> Promote a culture of performance, accountability and continuous improvement.

Performance indicators

> Monitoring the rate of service.

> Load/capacity monitoring.

> Follow-up of return rate and reasons.

> Monitoring of costs (subcontracting, consumables and tooling costs).

HOW ARE YOU TO BE US?

You have the following skills

Technical expertise

You have excellent knowledge

> In tool design: 3D modeling, 2D design and definition of precision tools.

> In manufacturing processes: conventional machining and CNC and assembly.

Knowledge in automatic and progressive stamping would be a valued asset.

Computer / Languages

> Knowledge of computer tools and Office suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams).

> Windchill, CREO, etc. necessary for the digitalised working environment.

Knowledge

You are recognized for your leadership and good communication. Agile, entrepreneurial, methodical and rigorous, you are oriented quality and customer, with an excellent relational sense. You know how to manage priorities and emergencies and are focused on results.
