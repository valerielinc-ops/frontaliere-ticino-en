Spital Langenthal Printing place Post as PDF The following position is to be filled in the management staff of SRO AG as of 01.01.2027:

They do it with us.

  • You will continue to build our compliance management system and an integrated, company-wide risk management system and continuously develop it further
  • They ensure compliance with the revised Data Protection Act and cantonal data protection law and implement these requirements in a practical manner in everyday hospital life.
  • They keep the list of processing activities and accompany data protection impact assessments in new systems and projects, for example in clinical information systems or AI applications.
  • You advise specialist departments, IT and project teams on privacy by design and examine contracts with external service providers
  • You process data subject requests and control the management of data protection incidents, including reports to the competent authorities
  • They maintain the hospital’s risk map, coordinate the annual risk inventory and link compliance, data protection and clinical risks to a holistic overall picture.
  • They plan and carry out internal audits and accompany external audits and regulatory controls
  • They develop guidelines and instructions, train employees and managers and promote a lived compliance, data protection and risk culture.
  • They report regularly and independently to the hospital management and the hospital council and thus contribute to the fulfilment of our public service mission.

This is what they bring with them.

  • A degree (university or technical college) in law, business, health management, risk management or a comparable qualification
  • Professional experience in compliance, data protection or risk management, preferably in healthcare
  • In-depth knowledge of the DSG and cantonal health and data protection law as well as relevant health legislation
  • Knowledge of common risk management standards a basic understanding of information security in the hospital environment
  • Experience in dealing with supervisory authorities and accompanying audits
  • An analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method with an eye for the whole
  • Integrity, independence and assertiveness, combined with comprehensible interests in exchange with the medical profession, care, IT, administration and authorities
  • The ability to communicate complex legal issues in an understandable way
