Full description

The Vocational and Further Education Centre for Health and Social Professions St.Gallen – BZGS – trains around 3,000 learners and students in various basic and further education professions in the fields of health, social affairs and home economics. The BZGS is a dynamic and innovative competence centre.

What to expect

  • Fulfilling the vocational mandate for teachers at vocational colleges in the FABE department
  • Professional-specific teaching according to the school curriculum
  • Collaborative work in the college and in the further development of the faculty
  • Further activities according to cantonal professional mandate

What sets it apart

  • Completed Social Education / Social Work / Socio-cultural animation HF/FH or comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of people with disabilities
  • Diploma Vocational School teacher or willingness to complete an appropriate degree programme
  • Motivation to accompany young people in their professional and personal development
  • Very good IT skills and openness to digital forms of learning
  • Team-oriented, flexible and resilient personality with high social competence
  • Willingness to actively participate in an innovation-oriented environment

What we offer

  • A responsible, varied and independent activity in a dynamic environment
  • Attractive, modern employment conditions
  • A collaborative, motivated team and an appreciative and supportive leadership
  • An innovative educational and competence centre with space for ideas and further development

Contact: Further information can be found on our website www.bzgs.ch
