Full description

Tasks

  • After careful training, you will take on independent activities in the Andrology laboratory and, depending on previous experience and suitability, in the IVF laboratory.
  • These include the following activities: analysis and processing of sperm samples and TESE material as well as their cryopreservation, including associated logging and documentation.
  • Depending on previous experience and suitability, you will also support us in the IVF laboratory, such as egg searching or the preparation and processing of zygotes and embryos, including documentation.
  • You work closely with our medical profession and the nursing team

Requirements

  • A completed training as a dipl. Biomedical analyst in HF or an embryology or biology study
  • Experience in the IVF and Andrology laboratory is beneficial
  • You are used to working and documenting very precisely, carefully, (self-)critically and responsibly
  • You bring a manual skill and have a good technical understanding
  • You are used to working strictly according to the requirements of quality management
  • With your sensitive nature and safe appearance, you accompany our patients and ensure a pleasant process.
  • You bring German skills and can also communicate in English and French
  • As a team-capable, resilient and open-minded person, you are the ideal complement for our team.
