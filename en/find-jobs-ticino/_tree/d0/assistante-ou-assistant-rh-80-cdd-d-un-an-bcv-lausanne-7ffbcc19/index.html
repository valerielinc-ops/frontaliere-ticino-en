Announcement HR Assistant - 80% (One-year contract) Your main missions:

  • Aisance with numbers and computer tools (the knowledge of SAP is a serious asset),
  • Rigor, sense of organization, discretion and versatility,
  • Team spirit and excellent service orientation.
