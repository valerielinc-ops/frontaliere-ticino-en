Full description

Luzerner Psychiatrie AG is a teaching hospital of the University of Lucerne and, through its services, has a presence at various locations in the Canton of Lucerne and in Sarnen, Canton of Obwalden. More than 1,600 employees from a wide range of professions and specialist fields work with compassion and expertise for the well-being of patients. The services are aimed at children, adolescents and adults through to advanced age.

Lead Physician (m/f/d) 80 - 100%

Your responsibilities

  • Clinical, medical and personnel leadership of three wards (depression and stress-related disorders, rehabilitation, residential psychiatry)
  • Conducting assessments as well as integrated psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment of patients
  • Monitoring and ensuring performance targets in your area of responsibility
  • Helping shape and implement professional and institutional development
  • Proactively contributing to projects and driving them forward
  • Leading, coaching and supporting the development of employees reporting to you

Your profile

  • Specialist qualification in psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in a senior physician leadership role or initial experience as a lead physician
  • A highly capable leader with strong interpersonal skills and a positive leadership mindset
  • A passion for interdisciplinary work, somatic medicine/psychiatry and further developing the treatment offering
  • Very good German language skills
  • Mebeko recognition available

Benefits

  • Appreciative, supportive working atmosphere with an open feedback culture
  • Internal leadership training
  • Internal and external supervision and professional development
  • Trust-based working hours with 35 days of holiday per year
  • Very generous social insurance benefits (coverage of the KTG premium and two-thirds of the PK premium)
