Description of post

As a cleaning employee, you contribute directly to the comfort and well-being of our guests and the team. You ensure clean rooms, toilets and internal spaces. Thanks to you, every visit takes place in a pleasant and secure environment.

Key responsibilities

  • Keep the room clean and welcoming
  • Clean and disinfect tables, chairs, floors and sorting stations
  • Ensure impeccable and hygienic sanitation
  • Keep internal areas clean and organized
  • Strictly comply with hygiene and safety rules
  • Report any special repair or need to management
  • Guarantee a clean and comfortable setting for guests

Qualifications required
