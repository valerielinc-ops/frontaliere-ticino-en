Full description

For our tax team in Opfikon Zurich we are looking for a committed working student (m/f/d) in the area of tax compliance 60% as of January 1, 2027. Do you want to gain valuable practical experience during your studies and gain insights into exciting tax issues? In our dynamic team, you take on responsibility early on, work closely with experienced specialists and actively support us in day-to-day business as well as in varied projects.

Processing the workflow

  • Examination of assessments and tax invoices incl. Conditioning

  • Support for correspondence with tax offices and internal contacts

Tax returns and annual accounts

  • Cooperation / preparation of tax returns (for profit and capital tax, property profit tax, etc.)
  • Support for tax work within the framework of the annual accounts

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
