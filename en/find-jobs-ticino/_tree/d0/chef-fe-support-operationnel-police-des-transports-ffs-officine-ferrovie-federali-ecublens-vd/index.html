Head of Operational Support - Transport police 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head of Operational Support - Transport police Ecublens (VD), 01.02.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

Do you want to put your police experience at the service of a function combining operational expertise, coordination and project management? As Chief·fe Operational Support, you play a key role in the proper functioning and development of the Western Transport Police. In contact with many internal and external partners, you are actively contributing to the anticipation of security issues and the implementation of concrete solutions. A varied and demanding function that will allow you to value your experience while addressing new professional challenges. What you can move.

  • Ensure an interface role between public security, different SBB entities and police partners, including cantonal and judicial police.
  • Represent public safety in the West Region and develop effective collaboration with internal and external partners.
  • Ensure the operational capacity of the region for guidelines, equipment and training needs.
  • Pilot operational and strategic projects, while actively participating in various working groups and cross-cutting projects.
  • Analyze security developments, identify risks and propose appropriate preventive and operational measures.
  • Provide expertise and support to regional officials in the preparation and management of specific operational situations.

What you bring.

  • You are the holder of the Federal Police Certificate and have a solid operational experience in the police field.
  • Several years of experience in team and/or project management, combined with very good knowledge of police tactics and techniques (driving course II).
  • A confirmed experience in conducting interventions in major events or special situations.
  • With an excellent analytical spirit, you can anticipate security issues, take initiatives and propose pragmatic solutions.
  • Your relationship ease, your ability to convince and your sense of collaboration allow you to interact effectively with different partners.
  • Good knowledge of German (minimum B1 level).

Your salary and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This commitment is in accordance with the Code of Obligations, taking into account a comparison with the team, market and industry.

Here's a good way to start.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the greatest motivation for us. We therefore waive our request for a cover letter for this position. If you wish, you can write to us in our application tool.
