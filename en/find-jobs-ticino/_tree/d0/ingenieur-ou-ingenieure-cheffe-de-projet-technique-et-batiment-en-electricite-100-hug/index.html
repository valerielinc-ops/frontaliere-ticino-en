Full description

As a project leader engineer, you are strengthening the construction engineering and technology sector (SITB). The team, composed of about fifteen people specialized in the various technical fields of the building: Heating-Ventilation-Climbing (CVC), sanitary, medical gas and electricity, manages about 60 projects in parallel. You thus evolve in an environment that is both demanding and innovative. In this role, you lead construction or renovation projects, from design to implementation, while highlighting your expertise in electricity. This includes projects including strong and low current distribution, transformation and upgrading of TGBT and BT cabs, installation of solar photovoltaic panels, charging terminals or lighting replacement. You conduct pre-project studies by analyzing feasibility, identifying needs, estimating costs and defining planning. You write detailed specifications and propose technical solutions that allow informed decision-making. You ensure a rigorous framing of the projects, thus ensuring precise validation of the objectives and controlled progress. You evaluate technical solutions by selecting the most suitable equipment and developing detailed plans. You are also preparing the bids needed to launch public tenders. You ensure active supervision by ensuring compliance with the laws, standards and regulations in force at each stage. You oversee the execution of projects by coordinating the various stages, following budget and planning, and ensuring optimal collaboration with project teams. You participate in the Steering Committees (COPIL), where you present progress, propose solutions to potential challenges and contribute to strategic decision-making. From a cross-cutting perspective, you are actively involved in continuous improvement by contributing to the evolution of the reference standards and technical guidelines. You update the documentation, perform a technology watch in your areas of expertise and propose relevant improvements.
