You don’t have to know everything with us. But you can ask anything. You can try out ideas and take responsibility. For us, lean means: giving responsibility, trusting and becoming better together. We are a cool kitchen team that cooks and keeps together with passion. With us you learn step by step everything about cooking – and help to conjure up fine dishes on your plate every day. You move that with us

  • You learn how to prepare food correctly – whether steaming, stewing, sautéing or gratinating, you try a lot and develop your skills continuously.
  • You take on the goods acceptance, check the deliveries and learn how to store food correctly.
  • You test the quality and freshness of food and develop a good sense of what really convinces.
  • You prepare fine and attractively presented food and implement work instructions in English.

Barmelweid offers you an attractive working environment in a family atmosphere in which cooperation and mutual support are given priority. We work closely with various disciplines and support ourselves in everyday life. During your training as a cook, you will get exciting insights into different areas of the kitchen – from cold and warm cuisine to patisserie. In addition to work, the cooperation is not too short: In our internal "gym" you can train as a learner / learner against a one-time contribution in the marginal lessons. In addition, sauna and indoor pool are available free of charge. During the annual apprentice excursion, you have the opportunity to meet other apprentices and exchange ideas with the experienced “learning hare”.
