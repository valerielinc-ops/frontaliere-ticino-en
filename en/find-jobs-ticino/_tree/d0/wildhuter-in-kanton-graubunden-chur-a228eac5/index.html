The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the Cantonal Administration Graubünden together with us. The Office for Hunting and Fishing is responsible for the game stocks, fish stocks and their habitats in the Canton of Graubünden. Many species such as caper chicken, birkhuhn, stone chicken, Wiedehopf and river runners have distribution focuses in Graubünden. In recent years, large predators such as the wolf and the lynx or temporarily the bear have migrated back to Graubünden. As a gamekeeper you work under the guidance of the gamekeeper district chief and exercise hegerish, hunting planning, fishing as well as hunting and fishing police functions. They know the intended area of operation and its waters well.
