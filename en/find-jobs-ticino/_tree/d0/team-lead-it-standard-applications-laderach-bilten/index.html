Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. Various activities are bundled in our Corporate Functions division. In addition to our finance and IT services, this area also includes global marketing and sales management as well as human resources. Every day fresh ideas are created here, which together we lead to success. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You will lead the IT Standard Applications team in Bilten, Ennenda and Porto in terms of expertise and personnel and develop your employees in a targeted manner.
  • You ensure a stable, secure and economical operation of business applications
  • You will continuously develop application and service management with clear responsibilities, standards and KPIs
  • You control and prioritize application roadmaps, upgrades and lifecycle measures while promoting standardization and reducing unnecessary special solutions.
  • You work closely with product owners, business stakeholders, IT architecture, security, infrastructure and other IT teams
  • You control external partners and are responsible for licenses, contracts and budgets in your area
  • You support international projects, rollouts and transformations and promote a performance- and service-oriented team culture

You bring that with you

  • You have a degree or higher education in computer science, business informatics or a comparable qualification
  • You bring several years of experience in business applications, application management or enterprise applications
  • You have leadership experience and enjoy developing employees and teams
  • You have experience with business-critical standard solutions, ideally in areas such as ERP, e-commerce, HR, P2P or Data & Analytics
  • You have a good understanding of application, service and lifecycle management and ideally bring ITIL knowledge
  • You bring experience in stakeholder, supplier and budget management and convince through entrepreneurial thinking, high service orientation and implementation strength
  • You understand how to combine operational stability with strategic development and have very good German and English skills

This is what we offer you

  • An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues
  • A position with a lot of personal responsibility
  • The opportunity to bring in fresh ideas
  • An open and respectful feedback culture
  • Internal and external training
  • Individual career opportunities
  • Generous discounts on our products
  • Chocolate in the workplace
  • Free parking
  • Discounts in Läderach Café and staff restaurant

Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh.

Sounds interesting? Then apply to us. Fabio Ferramosca, Talent Acquisition Partner, is at your disposal for any questions.
