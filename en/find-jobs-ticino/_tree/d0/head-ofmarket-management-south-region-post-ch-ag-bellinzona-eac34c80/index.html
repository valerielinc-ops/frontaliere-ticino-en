Full description

Are you interested in the strategic development of public transport? Do you want to play a key role in market management and actively shape the transformation towards electromobility? In this challenging leadership role, you will be responsible for market development in an important region and shape the future of PostBus together with a wide range of stakeholders. With you, the sustainable further development of regional passenger transport succeeds.

*You can do it *

  • As a member of the management team Markt PostBus, you assume overall responsibility for market management in the South region (Central Switzerland and Ticino) and maintain relationships with internal and external stakeholders.
  • In addition, you lead the core process in regional passenger transport, negotiate offer and target agreements and ensure correct, timely and subsidy-compliant service billing, which makes an important contribution to the company’s success.
  • You are responsible for securing earnings in your region and primarily manage key account management for the Canton of Ticino in terms of personnel and organization.
  • In addition, in close coordination with the product and planning, you develop and optimize network and timetable offers, alternative drives and on-demand solutions and contribute your expertise in a targeted manner.
  • You coordinate cooperation with the various business units and actively contribute to the transition to electromobility and to increasing competitiveness.
  • You will also be responsible for regional marketing communications and align them with the overarching marketing strategy, leveraging your strengths effectively.
  • You represent PostBus in regional public transport bodies, create market and sales plans for your region and take on the organizational responsibility for the mandate of the management Arcobaleno.

You bring that with you

  • You have a business, legal or technical education at university level and ideally a further education in public transport.
  • In addition, you bring many years of management and sales experience as well as in-depth knowledge of public transport and the political framework in your market area, which convinces us.
  • You think entrepreneurially and networked, act proactively and convince through your assertiveness.
  • In addition, you express yourself succinctly and convincingly and use your pronounced rhetorical skills effectively, making the most of your skills.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of corporate management and experience in strategic management.
  • You communicate very well verbally and in writing in Italian and have a good knowledge of German with the willingness to improve yourself further in order to be able to communicate safely in a national environment.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
