Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

In the middle of life – close to the people. What makes us special? As a versatile peripheral hospital with heart and mind, we live a trusting coexistence and a working environment that is characterized by openness, respect and appreciation. Working modernly for us means: acting humanely, with freedom of design and an open ear for each other. Our employees are in the middle of us instead of just there – both professionally and personally. Family-friendly conditions and jointly designed service planning are part of everyday life for us. Your tasks

  • You care for patients together with the qualified nurses and align your actions with their individual needs.
  • Nursing and medical measures are carried out independently and safely within your area of competence. You recognize changes early and direct your observations specifically
  • You document your work comprehensibly and record care services and material completely in the clinic information system
  • You take on logistical, administrative and domestic tasks and ensure that the processes around patient care work.
  • You question existing processes, submit suggestions for improvement and contribute to the further development of care quality

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training as a specialist in health EFZ and bring experience in acute care
  • You work attentively and in a structured way, set priorities even when changing requirements and know the limits of your competence area
  • You communicate clearly, coordinate reliably with the team and address uncertainties early on
  • You meet patients and their relatives respectfully and deal with their respective situation
  • You want to further develop your professional skills and implement new things in everyday care
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing. In the case of a foreign-language qualification, we require a language certificate at level B2
