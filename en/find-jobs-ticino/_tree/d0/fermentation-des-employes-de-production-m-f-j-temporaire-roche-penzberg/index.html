The Position Your department Diagnostics Operations Penzberg is part of the global production network of Roche’s Diagnostics Division and as such the most important producer of high-quality materials for diagnostic tests. We produce monoclonal antibodies, antigens, enzymes and nucleic acids from mammalian cell cultures and microorganisms at a fermentation scale of 0.1 – 2,000 L. The products purified therefrom are used in a wide variety of diagnostic tests, for example for the detection of infectious diseases.

Responsibility As a team, we are particularly proud of the good mix of long-term experienced and young employees who have just completed their training.
