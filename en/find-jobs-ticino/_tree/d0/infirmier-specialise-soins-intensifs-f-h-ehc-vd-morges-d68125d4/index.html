Full description

Policlinic, emergency, outpatient care, hospitalizations: from birth to old age, the Morges Hospital offers a wide range of care services. The establishment has 240 acute care beds, one maternity, with about 1,500 births per year, and nearly 30,000 emergency consultations. In all, more than 40 medical disciplines have been practiced in its walls for more than 150 years. Surrounded by vineyards, with an incredible view of the mountains and the lake, the Morges Hospital welcomes you in a warm and dynamic setting.

Nurse specializing in intensive care (M/F) at 60-100%

WE OFFER YOU

  • The opportunity to join a team, warm and competent, committed to a resolutely humane and quality care of patients in critical situations.
  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of redeeming an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (child care, unpaid leave, part-time, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • A mobility plan including participation in your cycling or basketball trip or in your public transport subscription, with a view to the environment following an institutional sustainability road map.
  • Sports activities (Pilates, walking races, discounts at various centres such as several fitness centres or Bains de Lavey), events for the children of collaborators, a company drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Hot drinks offered in the morning and meals at CHF 8.50 in our cafeterias.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Internal transfer facilitated according to your desire for development.
  • Many in-house or certifying training courses available to you.
  • A programme dedicated to managers to help them acquire the skills needed to manage their teams.

YOU MAY

  • A recognized CRS or equivalent nursing degree
  • EPD-ES Intensive Care Expert Diploma
  • Confirmed ICU Experience
  • A great autonomy, while knowing how to work in interdisciplinarity
  • Good stress resistance
  • Knowledge of basic computer tools

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Provide ongoing monitoring of the patient's evolving clinical condition in a critical situation and systematically assess its complex needs
  • Effective response to critical cases requiring prompt and accurate response
  • Collaborate effectively with internal stakeholders (caregiver, physician, student) in accordance with each other's specifications to develop and implement an optimal care plan
  • Provide clear information and practical advice to patients and their families to best manage recovery and home care.
  • Actively participate in projects to improve the quality of care and implement new practices

WE ARE

  • An institution comprising acute care and rehabilitation facilities, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences in the region of La Côte as well as the first clinical school of hypnosis dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company with approximately 70 apprentices in 13 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
