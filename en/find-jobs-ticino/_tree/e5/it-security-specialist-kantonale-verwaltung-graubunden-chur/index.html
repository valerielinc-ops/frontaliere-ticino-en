Are you ready to contribute to the digital transformation? As the IT service and competence center of the cantonal administration of Graubünden, we support our departments with a secure base infrastructure and advanced applications. Together with you, we drive the digital transformation of the Canton of Graubünden. IT security is exactly your thing? We, the Office for Informatics, are looking for an IT Security Specialist to start immediately or after agreement. ## Tasks

  • You advise our customers regarding protection needs and IT security requirements
  • You support with appropriate solutions for projects in our data centers as well as in the cloud
  • You accompany and check projects regarding information security
  • You actively bring in ideas to further develop guidelines, standards, and recommendations
  • You work closely with our IT Security Governance Team and IT service providers ## Requirements
  • A degree or further training in computer science and several years of experience in the IT security field
  • Practical knowledge with ISDS concepts, IT security architectures, and solutions
  • Good customer orientation, solid project management experience, independent working, and a confident appearance
  • Good German language skills in speech and writing
