Full description

In Piazza 10 6528 Camorino AVVISO DI CONCORSO per L’ASSUNZIONE DI PERSONALE The Municipality of Bellinzona opens a public competition for the appointment, with an employment degree from 80% to 100%, of: a Governor at the Autonomous Institute BellinzonaAnziani, headquarters Casa Anziani Comunale, Sementina, Greina, Residenza Pedemonte and Centro.

Main tasks

  • manage the Home Economy Hotel Service, coordinating its activities and ensuring a

appropriate use of human, financial and material resources as well as infrastructure and inventories;

  • plan, organize and supervise the work of collaborators and collaborators of the service,

taking care of the inclusion, training and motivation, promoting a working climate based on respect, mutual trust and collaboration;

  • manage the service budget according to the directions of the Direction, planning and coordinating the

purchases of materials, products and equipment of expertise, ensuring proper and peaceful use;

  • organize and supervise the management of linen and clothing, in collaboration with

Nursing service as well as laundry, ironing and tailoring services;

  • manage and supervise the cleaning activities of spaces and infrastructures, ensuring respect

of the hygiene rules and procedures applicable;

  • ensure proper management of the dining room during meals;
  • to cooperate with the other professional figures and the services of the Institutes, promoting the coordination of

activity and a multidisciplinary approach;

  • monitor the quality of performance and processes of competence, manage reports and

complaints and proposals for improvement, in compliance with the provisions on safety and health at work and sustainability;

  • carry out additional tasks consistent with the function and with the needs of the service.

Formal requirements

  • maturity or other equivalent/superior studies consistent with the competition function;
  • Federal professional certificate (APF) as Head of the hotel sector - economy

domestic or other equivalent/superior degrees.

Preferential requirements

  • Federal diploma (DF) as head of the hotel sector - domestic economy or equivalent title;
  • at least 2 years of professional experience with role of coordination, management and leadership at

medium-sized public or private organizations;

  • excellent knowledge of hygiene standards and ability to ensure their application;
  • good computer knowledge, in particular Office applications and research tools in

network;

  • knowledge of national languages i.

Aptitude requirements

  • strong relational, communication and listening skills, combined with confidentiality and discretion;
  • ability to motivate, value and develop the skills of employees and collaborators;
  • ability to foster dialogue and identify constructive solutions in complex situations;
  • strong organizational skills and planning of the assigned assets and resources;
  • analysis, troubleshooting and priority management skills;
  • guidance on objectives, results and continuous improvement of performance quality;
  • ability to collaborate effectively in a multidisciplinary context;
  • reliability, sense of responsibility and personal balance;
  • adaptability, flexibility and ability to work even under pressure in a challenging environment.

We offer

  • design, innovation and excellence oriented structure;
  • will set up a dynamic, motivated and ready-to-understand multidisciplinary work team

future challenges with a proactive and collaborative attitude;

  • centrality of the elderly person and care -giver in the construction of an individualized path based

on the evidence and in full respect of the will of the elderly person himself.

Documents to be produced

  1. accompanying letter; 2. curriculum vitae; 3. copy of the identity document; 4. copy of permission, if foreign; 5. jpg format photography; 6. copy of study certificates (with exam notes) and work; 7. self-certification relating to the judicial box; 8. self-certification relating to the certificate of solvency (UEF); 9. self-certification relating to health. Self-certifications are available on www.bellinzona.ch/asks.

Entry into service

  • date from convent.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
