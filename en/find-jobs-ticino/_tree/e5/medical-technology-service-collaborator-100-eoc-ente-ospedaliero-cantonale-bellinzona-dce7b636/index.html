Full description

Collaborator/professional medical technology service - Ausschreibung L’EOC the Ticino multisite hospital is present with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team of the Medical Technology Service we look for a(a) Medical Technology Service Collaborator (100%) Profile: The person we seek is proactive, flexible and dynamic. Prepared to work in teams and collaborate constructively with colleagues, has a strong ability to identify and solve problems effectively and promptly. He is able to respond to requests with flexibility and competence, demonstrating excellent organizational and management skills.

  • Requirements: Technical training with capacity certificate (Medizintechniker, electronic or similar), with experience in the maintenance of sensitive equipment. Good knowledge of the national languages, particularly mastery of German. Good knowledge of the main information applications. Good knowledge of both LAN and WiFi network facilities. Availability for mobility Date of entry: to be agreed Applications, in electronic format, complete with curriculum vitae, letter of motivation, copies of certificates of study and work, must be deposited on the appropriate platform by 19 July 2026. Applications that will come beyond this date will not be considered, as well as those received in paper form or by email. Further information may be requested by Mr Sassi Flavio, Head of Technical Maintenance, at +41 91 811 13 20.
