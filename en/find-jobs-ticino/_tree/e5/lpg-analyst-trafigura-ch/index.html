Full description

As an LPG Analyst to support our global oil trading team, providing in-depth market analysis, building and updating supply and demand models, providing value-add market insights, and analyzing high-frequency indicators of market dynamics. The role is based in Geneva, Switzerland.

Key Responsibilities

  • Collating, analysing and interpreting data to produce commodity market insights
  • Conduct comprehensive research and analysis on fuel oil markets, including supply/demand dynamics, pricing trends, arbitrage opportunities, and regulatory developments across major global trading hubs
  • Build and maintaining supply and demand models, and develop detailed market reports, presentations, and trade recommendations to inform trading strategies
  • Provide trade flow analysis and other ad-hoc analysis to support decision-making by senior traders and management
  • Collaborate closely with traders, operations teams, and other analysts to identify trading opportunities, monitor positions, and manage risk exposure
  • Build and maintain proprietary models and tools for market analysis, forecasting, and scenario planning.
  • Stay up-to-date on industry news, market intelligence, and competitor activities to provide a competitive edge
  • Establish strong relationships with key market participants, including refiners, suppliers, and customers, to gather market intelligence.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Economics, Finance, Engineering, or a related quantitative field;
  • Minimum of 4 years of experience in commodity analysis or a related analytical role, preferably with exposure to fuel oil/refined products markets
  • Prior experience in commodity research, specifically in LPG analytics
  • Strong quantitative and analytical skills with the ability to synthesize complex data sets and extract meaningful insights
  • Experience using trade data, data visualization tools and statistical data modeling software.
  • Proficiency in data analysis tools (e.g., Python, R, SQL) and Microsoft Office suite.
  • Knowledge of commodity trading fundamentals, risk management principles, and financial modeling
  • Fluency in English

Preferred Qualifications

  • Advanced educational degree
  • Fluency in another foreign language
  • Programming experience and a solid understanding of database concepts (Python and SQL preferred; R, MATLAB or VBA beneficial).
  • Direct knowledge of LPG markets

Attributes for success

  • Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal, with the ability to present complex information clearly and concisely
  • Strong intrinsic interest in financial markets, geopolitical developments and a sound understanding of economic theory and cycles
  • Self-motivated, detail-oriented, and able to work effectively in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
  • Ability to work independently and proactively to deliver timely results.
  • Analytical mindset and critical thinking skills, with an emphasis on providing proactive insights that have tradeable value.
