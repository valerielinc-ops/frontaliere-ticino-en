Full description

On behalf of our customer, we fill a management position in a mountain restaurant in the Bündner Bergwelt. We are looking for a service-oriented personality who assumes operational responsibility during the winter season and together with the team ensures a first-class guest experience.

Your tasks

  • Operational management of the service area in day-to-day business
  • Leadership, motivation and support of a small service team
  • Active cooperation at the front and care of guests
  • Ensuring a smooth service flow in restaurant operations
  • Responsibility for Mise en Place, quality of service and guest satisfaction
  • Coordination of processes between kitchen and service
  • Participation in operational planning and organisation of day-to-day business
  • Ensuring compliance with quality and hygiene standards
  • Support for the training of new employees

Your profile

  • Completed training in the catering or hotel industry
  • First leadership experience in the service sector is beneficial
  • Self-employed and responsible working practices
  • Enjoyment of guest care and high service orientation
  • Teamwork, flexibility and resilience
  • Very good knowledge of German, further language skills are advantageous
  • Safe and well-kept appearance
  • Willingness to work in a mountain operation during the winter season

Your benefits

  • Seasonal employment from December to early April
  • Attractive working hours from about 08.00 to 16.30
  • Workplace in a unique mountain landscape
  • Accommodation option if required
  • Free parking
  • Employee benefits within the catering and leisure businesses
  • Free annual ticket for the ski area
  • Benefits for family members
  • Additional free day tickets for friends and acquaintances
  • Loyalty bonus for recurring seasonal bets
  • Contemporary employment conditions and development opportunities
