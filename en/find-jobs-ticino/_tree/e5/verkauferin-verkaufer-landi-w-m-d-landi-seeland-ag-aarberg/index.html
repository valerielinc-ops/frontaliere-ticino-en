Together with the Volg, TopShop and AGROLA brands, the LANDI cooperatives also supply the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy. Discover your benefits

  • Training and continuing education: We invest in the development of our employees. A broad range of internal educational opportunities is open to everyone, and we support external continuing education.
