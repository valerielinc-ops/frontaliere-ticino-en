Full description

Connect managers into the daily activities of human resources, support them in managing complex situations related to staff and help find lasting solutions. Analyzing key indicators for human resources, individuals improvement measures and strengthens positive collaboration within the company.

Workplace: Fresh Food & Beverage Group, Regional bakery S.Antonino, Via Serrai 9, 6592 San Antonino

Important information

Every application we receive makes us very pleased, but we can only consider those online. Mail applications will be deleted for data protection purposes and will not be returned.

Shipments

  • Provide top-level advice on human resources to managers and support them in applying human resource processes and guidelines
  • Help managers manage the most common individual situations
  • Participating in difficult talks, conflict resolution procedures and long-term absence monitoring together with stakeholders
  • Monitoring human resource indicators (assenteism, turnover, involvement, overtime) and helping to define improvement measures
  • Contribute to maintaining a positive social climate and promoting good practices in human resources among those responsible

Requirements

  • 3 - 5 years: in a general role in the field of human resources

  • Open and empathic personality, which loves to interact with different people
  • Integrity, authenticity and sense of responsibility.
  • Excellent computer skills (Microsoft 365, SAP SuccessFactors)
  • Italian (Good Level)
  • German (Very good knowledge)
