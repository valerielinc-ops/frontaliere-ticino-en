Full description

Founded in 1894, Universal Genève has been a pioneer in the art of watchmaking, celebrated for its legendary models and in-house movements. Revered by collectors and enthusiasts alike, the brand’s timeless designs and technical innovations earned it the title “ Le Couturier de la Montre ”. Universal Genève invites a seasoned Senior Editor to join our Marketing team , bringing expertise, elegance, and a passion for exceptional storytelling. If you have a meticulous eye for detail, an ability to weave history into modern narratives, and a dedication to exceptional storytelling, we would love to hear from you. As a Senior Editor , you will steward Universal Genève’s written communications across diverse platforms, lending a cohesive and distinctive voice to articles, blogs, press releases, books, and more. Your work will embody the precision, heritage, and innovation for which Universal Genève is renowned. We seek a professional with extensive experience in journalism or corporate communications, who understands the nuances of deadline-driven environments and can deliver polished, compelling content. The ideal candidate is a native English speaker with a rich portfolio, demonstrating an aptitude for producing content that informs and resonates. A passion for uncovering stories and connecting deeply with heritage, couture, and craftsmanship is essential. Your future team You will start your new professional journey by joining our “Le Couturier de la Montre” team in Geneva .

Your contribution will include writing, commissioning, and editing:

  • Articles, blogs and newsletters
  • Press releases
  • Print publications
  • Scripts, speeches, and presentations
  • Exhibition interpretive material
  • Digital content pieces, e-newsletters and website
  • Other materials throughout Universal Genève’s communications You will perfectly fit into our Team if:
  • You have 5+ years in journalism or corporate communications
  • You are a native English speaker with business-level proficiency in French
  • You are able to craft engaging, polished content
  • You have proven interviewing expertise and exemplary research abilities, reflecting curiosity and precision
  • You are familiar with luxury-brand communications
  • You are able to manage multiple editorial projects while maintaining high standards
  • You are proficient in Microsoft Office suite (SharePoint, Teams, Outlook, Word, PowerPoint) Please apply with CV, cover letter, and relevant portfolio that demonstrates your exceptional writing skills.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
