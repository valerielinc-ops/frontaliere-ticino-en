Full description

Once you complete your 3 year basic training, this one-year internship will allow you to deepen your theoretical knowledge and obtain your Federal Certificate of Capacity (FCC). Our professional trainers will support you throughout, and you will have the opportunity to perform different tasks in autonomy, while taking responsibility gradually.

We are part of the main health insurance, accidents and things in Switzerland, with more than 2,700 employees. Every year we train over 100 apprentices and trainees in various professions.

Your mission

  • You manage the overall prosecution procedure for your assigned files
  • You track files, including tasks arising from deadlines
  • You write correspondence in relation to managed files and written correspondence with clients
  • You open and process incoming mail
  • You establish payment arrangements, blockages and manage exclusions from LCA
  • You are participating in vocational training projects

What you bring

  • You have obtained a professional maturity issued by a business or management school (system 3+1)
  • You have good grades in languages and mathematics, a taste for accounting, an attraction for numbers, and a good spelling.
  • You work with precision and think in an interconnected way
  • You are motivated, with a strong desire to learn and surpass yourself
  • You show a sense of communication and organization
  • You like to work as a team and you are customer-oriented
