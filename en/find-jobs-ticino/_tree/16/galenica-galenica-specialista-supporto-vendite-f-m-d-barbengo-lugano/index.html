Full description

Workplace: Pharmaceutical Union Distribution SA, Via Figino 6, 6917 Barbengo-Lugano

Do you like to stay in touch with people and contribute every day to a quality service? As a sales support specialist, you are a reference point for customers and partners. You will join a committed team, who collaborates with a spirit of trust and mutual support to achieve common goals. Thanks to the many telephone contacts and the variety of activities, every day is different and requires flexibility, initiative and orientation to solutions.

Your contribution

  • You are the reference point for our customers: provide advice and professional support on products, availability, deliveries and services, contributing to high customer satisfaction
  • Find fast and effective solutions to customer requests and collaborate actively with suppliers and internal departments to ensure a quality service
  • Manage special orders, urgent deliveries and special requests, monitoring the entire process until delivery
  • Processes and monitors orders, complaints, returns and non-compliance, identifying the most appropriate measures to ensure the best customer experience
  • Take proper administrative management, including data inputs, billing, corrections and reporting activities
  • Strict contact with different teams and contribute to the continuous improvement of processes, keeping your knowledge on the products and services offered

Your skills

  • You have a completed training as a Pharmacy Assistant or Study Assistant
  • Have you gained experience in pharmacy or, ideally, in the medical sector or in the telephone customer service
  • You like contact with people and communicate professionally, clear and friendly
  • Works organized and keeps calm even in the most intense moments
  • Securely utilises IT tools (MS Office); knowledge of AS400 is an advantage
  • You communicate safely in Italian and have a good knowledge of French or German (level B2 or higher)

What we offer

  • 5 weeks of holiday, with the possibility to acquire up to 10 days of additional holiday or to take an unpaid leave
  • Pension fund with advantageous allocation of the premium – approx. 2/3 at our expense
  • Several discounts in our pharmacies and other quality brands
  • Enlargement of skills through advanced and supported training courses and opportunities for internal development
  • Family-friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of the wage) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of the wage) and participation in the fund for children
  • Nomination at preferential prices – commitment not only professional for Galenica but also investment

Benefits may vary slightly depending on department / location

Who are we?

The complete assortment for Ticino Unione Farmaceutica Distribution (UFD), founded in 1931, is the most important and the only healthcare logistics company with a complete assortment in Ticino and is part of the Galenica network. We are very present at regional level. With our Lugano distribution centre, we offer pharmacies, medical dispensaries, nursing homes and hospitals a wide range of services and products. From us the warehouse logistics occupies the first place. In the UFD team you are committed to offering customers excellent services and adapted to market prices – in Ticino, in the Mesolcina Grigionese and in Val Calanca.
