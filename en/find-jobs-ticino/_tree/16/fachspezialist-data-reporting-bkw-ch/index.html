Full description

We make habitats worth living.

In this versatile function, you ensure a high quality and consistency of our HR data. You prepare meaningful reports and analyses, accompany central HR processes and support executives and HR business partners in the sound use of data.

Your tasks

  • Data management and data quality: You ensure high data quality in the relevant HR systems Abacus and SFEC, maintain and validate master data, carry out data cleansing, plausibility checks and quality controls.
  • Data flows: You are responsible for correct and consistent data flows between HR, finance, controlling and IT.
  • Reporting and analysis: You create and update HR reports, prepare key figures and analyses and, if necessary, develop ad hoc evaluations and analysis packages.
  • Pay Round, Bonus and Provisions: You create and maintain the appropriate lists, ensure data consistency and versioning, support executives and HR business partners in the use of data.
  • Collaboration and Support: You work closely with HR Services, HR Business Partners, IT, Finance and Controlling, take over the first-level support and proactively act as the topic owner in other HR topics.

Your expertise

  • You have a commercial basic education (EFZ) as well as a further education in HR or a further education in data and analytics.
  • You bring experience in HR data management, reporting or HR operations as well as in-depth knowledge in data preparation and analysis.
  • You know Abacus well, are ideally familiar with SFEC and have very good MS Office skills, especially in Excel.
  • You handle sensitive data responsibly and discreetly, know the requirements for data protection and compliance and communicate fluently in German and advantageously in French.
  • You work accurately, structured and quality-consciously, think analytically, prepare complex data understandably and convince through a team-oriented, proactive and solution-oriented way of working.
