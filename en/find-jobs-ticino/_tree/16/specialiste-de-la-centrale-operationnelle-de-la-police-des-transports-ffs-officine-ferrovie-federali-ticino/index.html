SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. As a specialist: in the operational control center of the transport police you are the first point of contact for our customers and the train staff. With your commitment, you contribute significantly to safety in trains and train stations. Every day thousands of travellers and employees trust you – you are a central factor for peace, order and a safe travel experience. You can move that.

  • As the first point of contact in the operational control center, you receive emergency calls and messages from customers and train personnel, assess the situation and initiate the necessary steps quickly and reliably.
  • You support our colleagues at the front by professionally accompanying and coordinating the missions throughout Switzerland and keeping an overview of the situation at all times.
  • Together with more than 190 colleagues in train stations and public transport, you make your contribution to security, peace and order.
  • In close cooperation with our partner organisations, you take on responsibility in the security association and help ensure that our customers feel well protected and safe at all times.

You bring that with you.

  • Thanks to your service-oriented, open and winning nature, you will always meet our customers and the train staff in a friendly and professional manner.
  • Teamwork is important to you – you enjoy working with others and actively participate in the team.
  • You use your good IT and administration skills in day-to-day operations.
  • Ideally, you have the Swiss passport Police Officer: in I and have in-depth police tactical and technical knowledge, ideally supplemented by experience in an operational centre.
  • You communicate clearly and confidently in German or French (level C1); have good knowledge of the other language (level B2) and other languages such as Italian or English are a plus.
