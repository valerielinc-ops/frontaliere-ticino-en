Application dossier

Applications must be submitted in writing with an application letter, a short CV and a recently issued (not older than one year) extract from the criminal and debt collection register.

Do not forget to submit the official application form mentioned on the Judicial Council website.

Desired profile

  • Licensed to practice law, licentiate or master of law and sufficient practical knowledge to carry out the office
  • Experience in the area of ​​the justice of the peace in accordance with Art. 58 JG is an advantage
  • Voting rights in cantonal matters. Foreign nationals must have a settlement permit and have been resident in the canton for at least five years. There must be no loss certificates against the persons. You must not have been convicted of criminal offenses for actions that are incompatible with judicial office
  • Mastery of the German language with a good knowledge of the French language
