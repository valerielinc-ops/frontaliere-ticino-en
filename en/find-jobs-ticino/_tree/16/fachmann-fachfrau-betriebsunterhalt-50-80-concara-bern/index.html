Full description

Tasks

You like to tackle, keep an overview and use your craftsmanship to ensure a smooth process in the background? In this versatile function, you are responsible for the maintenance of our public areas and their surroundings. You carry out maintenance and control work on technical systems (heating, sanitary and electrical systems), maintain the outdoor systems and carry out simple repair work or coordinate these with external specialists. The professional and efficient use of machines, materials and cleaning products is also part of your everyday life. In addition, you ensure the correct disposal of the waste and regularly take over pikett services via several locations as part of a network.

Requirements

  • Completed training as a specialist in operation maintenance EFZ
  • Maintenance specialist or completed training in an artisanal profession
  • Enjoying versatile challenges and high willingness to provide services, teamwork, resilience and flexibility
  • Competent and professional handling of our elderly residents

We offer

  • 5 weeks holiday, from 45 years 6 weeks, from 55 years 7 weeks
  • Competent, committed and collaborative team
  • Appreciative corporate culture and value-oriented leadership culture

In the Bern Nord habitat, exciting applications await you at the Domicil Wyler and Domicil Spitalackerpark locations. While the Domicil Spitalackerpark convinces with its green location in the popular Breitenrain quarter and a warm, hospitable atmosphere, the Domicil Wyler as a modern new building offers an innovative working environment in which the quality of life of the inhabitants and the appreciation of the employees are at the center.
