Full description

“Facts instead of moral preaching, laboratory instead of gut feeling and self-responsibility instead of paternalism” You advise recreational users in drug checking, people with substance use in the counselling center and help us design DIBS as a competence center for recreational drug use right from the start. We are looking for a social worker at the Drug Checking and Counselling Centre (50%) as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement – with a view to increasing the workload from 2028 The Counselling Centre is one of several, professional and closely networked offers of the Foundation Addiction Help Region Basel. The counselling centre is responsible for advising and accompaniing people at risk of addiction and addiction-dependent persons from young to old and their environment. DIBS (Drogeninfo Basel) is the outpatient drug checking service of the Addiction Assistance Region Basel. Since 2019, as part of the counselling centre, it has made it possible to dispense psychoactive substances in conjunction with a personal consultation. The offer is complemented by Safer Dance Basel as a nightlife and damage reduction offer for recreational consumers with outreach work in nightlife, clubs and festivals as well as mobile drug checking on site. The clients are the cantons Basel-Stadt and Basel-Landschaft. Together, the offers provide factual and well-founded information on the effects, risks and possible consequences of the use of psychoactive substances. The focus is on an accepting attitude, the promotion of risk competence and the support of self-determined decisions. In the transitional year 2027, the still separate offerings DIBS and Safer Dance Basel will be merged and from 2028 onwards will continue as a “center of excellence for recreational drug consumption”. You work in close exchange with the two managements of the offers and are part of the team of the counselling center of the Addiction Aid Region Basel.
