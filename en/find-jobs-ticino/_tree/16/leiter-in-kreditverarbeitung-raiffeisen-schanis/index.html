Become part of our team!

We – Raiffeisenbank Schänis-Amden – are a locally anchored consulting bank and employ more than 30 employees at our two locations in Schänis and Amden, including three apprentices. As a modern employer, we continuously promote and challenge our employees. Their further development is just as important to us as a balanced work-life balance. To strengthen our credit processing team, we are looking for a competent leader who enjoys managing employees, developing processes and actively shaping the future of our bank.

What awaits you?

  • Professional and operational management of the credit processing team
  • Auditing, processing and management of new and existing credit positions in accordance with regulatory requirements
  • Analysis, assessment and approval of credit transactions in the private and corporate customer segment within the jurisdictional order
  • Identification, assessment and active management of credit risks incl. Monitoring of existing commitments
  • Share responsibility for data quality and reporting to bank management and board of directors
  • Active contribution to projects and process optimizations and participation in the further development of our credit processing
  • Cooperation with offices, insurance companies and other external bodies

What do you bring?

  • Banking education with higher professional training in finance or banking
  • Several years of profound experience in the lending business (private and corporate customers), Raiffeisen experience of advantage
  • Strong competences in the analysis, assessment and decision-making of credit risks
  • Experience in estimation and ideally knowledge of tools such as Wüest Dimensions
  • High reliability, sense of responsibility and teamwork
  • Analytical flair, rapid comprehension and high quality awareness
  • Independent, careful and structured working methods

If you want to take responsibility, actively shape change and develop a motivated team, you are at the right place. You can expect a varied environment in which your ideas are appreciated and you shape the future of our bank. Roland Oberkalmsteiner Member of the bank management Why Raiffeisen?

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We promote personal and professional development

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in St. Gallen?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with St. Gallen's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
