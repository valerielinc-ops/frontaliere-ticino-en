Full description

To strengthen our team at AMEOS Seeklinikum Brunnen we are looking for

Swiss-approved psychotherapist (m/f/d) and/or head of psychology (m/f/d) — full-time or part-time, different models are conceivable

Their advantages From now on or by appointment, we offer you the opportunity to become part of our family team. You can expect a versatile, attractive working environment in which you take care of patients on one of our stations. Furthermore, you can expect a structured training and supervision program. Our clinic is recognized by the SIWF of the FMH as a B-Spital, which allows you to acquire eligible time for the arrangement model. Our work location Brunnen is located in an environment with a high quality of life as well as many leisure activities and is very easily accessible from Lucerne, Zug or Zurich by all means of transport, including by ship.

Their tasks They primarily look after our inpatient patients in individual and group therapies. They ensure individual, interdisciplinary therapy planning and support with specialist departments and nursing team. Furthermore, you participate in interdisciplinary reports and take over the administrative case management in the stationary stay. Other

  • Tasks are outpatient preliminary interviews and service on working days at working hours.

and/or Operational, operational, professional and personnel management as senior psychologist Responsibility for the professional training of assistant psychologists and supervision of assistant doctors and psychology practitioners Ensuring and coordinating the interdisciplinary care of patients in the area of responsibility

Your profile They have completed further psychotherapy training and a Swiss Federal Recognition in Psychotherapy. You wish to take on a leadership task or to develop such competencies. They are open to interdisciplinary teamwork, with patients at the centre. Experience in sleep medicine is welcome.
