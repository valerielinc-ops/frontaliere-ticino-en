Full description

The UniBE is versatile. With us, you can expect a wide variety of topics and a lot of design freedom.

Their tasks

As a clerk / in purchasing and logistics, you take on operational tasks in purchasing, procurement, warehouse management and logistics. You also assume application responsibility for the purchasing and goods management system "Selectline". They play an important hub function within the institute and monitor orders and delivery dates.

The priorities shall include

  • Operational & Strategic Procurement: You are responsible for the entire procurement process of laboratory materials, reagents and capital goods
  • Supplier management: You monitor delivery deadlines and evaluate the performance of our partners at home and abroad
  • Warehouse & inventory management: You optimize warehousing taking into account expiration dates (batch management) and ensuring just-in-time supply of the various laboratory areas
  • Interface function: You work closely with the various laboratory departments and laboratory management to plan needs at an early stage and to identify potential cost savings and risks
  • Master data maintenance: You manage article data in the ERP system and support the Head of Finance in budget planning

Your profile

  • Commercial or technical basic education (BMA / laboratory assistant). Advantageous training in purchasing or equivalent training
  • Many years of professional experience in purchasing and / or logistics, ideally in the field of life sciences, medical technology or healthcare
  • In-depth knowledge of data evaluation and data interpretation. Understanding the specific requirements of an accredited medical laboratory
  • Knowledge of ERP systems (SAP) and Selectline
  • Strong service and solution orientation
  • Reliability and a very precise working method
  • Very good knowledge of German (level C1) and English (level B2)
  • Category B driving licence required

About us

The Institute of Infectious Diseases (IFIK) performs tasks in teaching and research, as well as in diagnostic microbiology in the fields of virology, bacteriology, mycology, parasitology and serological infection detection. As the only university institute in Switzerland, it brings together all specialised microbiological disciplines in clinical microbiology under one roof. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
