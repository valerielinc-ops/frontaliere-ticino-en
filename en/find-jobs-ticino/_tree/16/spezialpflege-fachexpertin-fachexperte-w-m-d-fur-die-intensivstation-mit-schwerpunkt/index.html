By 01.02.2027 or by agreement

As a specialist care provider at the University Hospital Zurich, we are committed to showing commitment with our professional diversity in the top medical environment and always keeping up with the times.

Want to be part of our team? With us you can make a difference

  • With your expertise, you ensure professional and patient-oriented care and actively participate in all three shifts.
  • Together with the care expert or To the care expert, you develop the quality of care and support the team in complex care situations as well as in the treatment process.
  • You pass on your expertise with enthusiasm. You empower and promote the nursing team and carry out further education and training.
  • You are actively involved in the introduction and implementation of concepts and guidelines as well as in our shared governance team.
  • With your ideas and commitment, you get projects rolling, inspire others for change and ensure that good ideas become practice.
  • You will bring a completed training as a qualified expert NDS HF intensive care provider. You have already completed or are ready to complete a CAS or equivalent training with a professional focus.
  • A structured way of working, the ability to prioritize and a responsible attitude set you apart. Even at work peaks, you keep an overview and are a professional role model for your team.

Our offer

Cutting-edge medicine requires a high standard of care quality, which we offer and further develop together with your expertise for patients and relatives. We offer you an extended, varied and responsible field of work in a demanding, highly complex and multidisciplinary environment. You benefit from a broad range of further education in the university setting and the connection to the Center for Clinical Nursing Science. Our career model supports you in your career planning and opens up long-term prospects in the professional field of care. Get to know us! For this post, dossiers from recruiters cannot be considered.
