Full description

  • be responsible within the Pneumology Unit for respiratory rehabilitation (based on HFR Riaz)
  • participate in the outpatient and stationary management of patients with lung pathologies, including at the site of the HFR Fribourg according to the needs of the unit
  • perform bronchoscopy on the site of the HFR Fribourg
  • participate in Pneumonic Unit pickets
  • supervise and participate in the training of physicians-assistant-e-s and chief-fe-s of rotating clinics
  • participate in the teaching of students of the University of Fribourg
  • Professional qualifications (ISFM) in pneumology and general internal medicine
  • strong clinical experience in hospital and outpatient pneumonology, including as head of clinic or executive physician
  • an experience in pulmonary rehabilitation is a certain advantage
  • an experience as head of clinic in hospital medicine is a plus
  • control of the diagnosis and treatment of respiratory sleep disorders, including non-invasive ventilation
  • dynamic, open and collegial personality
  • interest in the organisation of pre- and post-graduate and in-service training of doctors and carers
  • mastery of the French or German language with good knowledge of the other language (ability to conduct a medical interview)
