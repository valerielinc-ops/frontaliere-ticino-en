At STRABAG, around 89,000 people at more than 2,400 locations worldwide are building progress. Uniqueness and individual strengths characterise not only our projects but also each and every one of us. Whether in building and civil engineering, road and civil engineering, bridge and tunnel construction, in building materials production, project development or building management - we think construction further to become the most innovative and sustainable construction technology group in Europe. Together we implement projects successfully and in partnership and grow with new tasks. Together we create great things. Let's progress!

Technical Group Leader Civil Engineering and Road Construction Your Contribution with Us

  • Responsible for managing the construction business in the region in cooperation with all relevant stakeholders
  • Developing a shared vision and strategy and living it within the team
  • Responsibility for the performance and results of the branch
  • Leadership and development of employees in the branch
  • Acquisition of construction contracts and systematic customer care in agreement with the division management
  • Leadership and control of project and construction management for optimised project execution
  • Conclusion of subcontractor contracts in coordination with the division management
  • Negotiation with the client on essential issues of construction execution, interpretation, modification and extension of the construction contract as well as approval of amendments in agreement with the division management
  • Control of construction progress and site results
  • Implementation and co-responsibility for work preparation and site organisation
  • Participation and co-design of innovation topics in construction (e.g. BIM)
  • Responsibility for cost, schedule and quality issues

What Counts for Us

  • Several years of experience as a construction foreman in civil engineering and road construction is an advantage

  • Very good MS Office skills and experience with RIB iTWO is an advantage
  • High commitment and reliability
  • Assertiveness, entrepreneurial thinking and acting as well as a strong goal orientation
  • Teamwork and leadership experience, negotiation skills, flexibility as well as an independent and responsible working style complete your profile
