Appointing authority / administrative unit / institution

Freiburg University of Social Work (HSA-FR) is a member of the University of Applied Sciences in Western Switzerland (HES-SO). It counts more than 400 students for the Bachelor in Social Work and participates in the offer of the Master in Social Work of the HES-SO. It is also active in research and innovation, training and services.

Their tasks

Practical training (PA) is a professionalizing situation for students that contributes to the development of the skills expected at the end of the training in the sense of an integrative variety. Task areas:

  • Lessons in various modules of the Bachelor's degree program
  • Supervision, support and evaluation of students during practical training as PA managers
  • Management of the PA module as module manager
  • Participation in the management of practical training
  • Conducting practical analyses
  • Supervision of bachelor theses
  • Participation in training and services

Desired profile

  • Master in Social Work, Social Sciences or equivalent degree with proven experience in social education
  • Multiannual comprehensive professional experience
  • Advantageous training as a practice instructor or in adult education
  • excellent knowledge of the Western Swiss network (BE-JU-NE-FRI)
  • Experience with practical analysis
  • Mastery of IT tools (Moodle, MS Office, IS-Academia, etc.)
  • Teamwork and solution-oriented working methods
  • Good organisational and planning skills, initiative and flexibility
  • Mastery of the German or French language with good knowledge of the other language, bilingualism advantageous
