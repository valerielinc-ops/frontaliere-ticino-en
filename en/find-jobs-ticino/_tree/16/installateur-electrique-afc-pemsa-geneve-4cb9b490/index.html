Your assignment

Making quality electrical installations You will participate in the construction of plants in strong current and weak current (CFO/CFA) , ensuring that the projects, Swiss standards in force and safety requirements are respected. Read and interpret technical documents You will analyze projects, schematics and technical documents in order to realize reliable, efficient and comply with the requirements of the project. Install and connect equipment You are responsible for laying and connecting cables, installing electrical equipment and commissioning, in compliance with NIBT standards and company quality standards. Making wiring infrastructure You will install the channels, supports and infrastructures necessary for the realization of the electrical networks, ensuring its solidity, safety and durability. Contribute to the success of the yards In collaboration with the various parties involved, you will actively participate in the smooth running of the work, promote effective coordination and ensure compliance with deadlines, quality requirements and objectives. Your contribution Thanks to your technical know-how, your rigour and team spirit, you directly contribute to the realization of reliable, safe and customer-friendly electrical systems. And now we're talking about you? Solid technical training You have an AFC as an installer-electricist, editor-electricist or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland. You have the skills necessary to operate with autonomy, rigor and professionalism in different types of yards. Experience in the electrical industry Master the strong current and weak current plants (CFO/CFA) and you are familiar with reading and interpreting electrical plans and schematics. Good mastery of Swiss standards Ideally, you have an updated NIBT certificate and know the Swiss electrical standards as well as the safety requirements applicable in the yards. Recognized practical skills The passage of cables, the laying of equipment, the connections and the installation of channels are among your skills. Perform your interventions with precision, method and constant attention to quality. Experience in the Swiss market Ideally, you have at least 2 years of professional experience in Switzerland, which allows you to be quickly operational and integrate effectively into teams and different projects. A reliable and involved professional Autonomous, rigorous and conscientious, you appreciate teamwork and you can adapt to demanding environments. Your sense of responsibility, commitment and professionalism contribute directly to the success of the projects entrusted to you. What will make the difference Your technical mastery, your knowledge of Swiss standards, your field experience and your professional commitment will make you a true partner of trust, able to contribute permanently to the quality and success of our achievements.
