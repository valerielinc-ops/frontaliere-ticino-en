Location: Riehen

From 01.10.2027 or by agreement

Do you want to link theory with practice? Are you interested in innovation and administrative activities in the health sector? Do you want a comprehensive training in business administration, adapted to the areas of health care? Would you like to put what you have learned into practice in everyday hospital life? Then apply for the dual study at 01.10.2027 with us! Further information on the course of study can be found here: Study contents & profile Founded in 1900, the Klinik Sonnenhalde in Riehen is now an established private clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy in Switzerland and southern Baden. We pursue a holistic, human-centered, milieutherapeutic psychiatry that combines excellent professional quality with a Christian value base. The clinic has 68 stationary beds, 4 day clinics and 4 ambulatories. As one of the first clinics in Switzerland, we are actively promoting digital therapy offerings, such as blended therapy and virtual reality and opened the first hybrid day clinic in Switzerland in February 2023.

The study takes place in cooperation with the Duale Hochschule Baden-Württemberg in Lörrach (D).
