Full description

We are looking for designers who will be part of our MySolution Division, which mainly deals in developing medical devices based on the specific patient’s anatomy that support surgeons to implant correctly the prosthesis.

The candidate will be responsible to execute the following activities:

  • Quality Control of CT/MRI scans
  • 3D Segmentation of patient’s anatomy
  • Simulation of prosthesis implantation (surgery plan)
  • Design of custom instruments for each specific patient

Hard Skills

  • Technical Diploma or Scientific Degree, preferably Biomedical and Mechanical;
  • Max 3 years of experience;
  • knowledge of 3D CAD, SolidWorks (nice to have);
  • Fluency in English.

Soft Skills

  • Ability to work in a very dynamic environment;
  • Excellent interpersonal skills;
  • Seriousness and precision;
  • Availability to work 2 shifts;
  • Strong sense of organization and good aptitude for team work.

What we offer

  • permanent contract;
  • carpooling organized by Medacta;
  • incentives for mobility by public transport;
  • advantageous conventions;
  • growth opportunity, working in a continuously improving and expanding environment;
  • annual salary review linked to performance and professional development;
  • continuous training.
