Shape your future with us!

Are you ready to make a difference? Within our team, you will have the opportunity to accompany our clients in crucial moments by effectively responding to their needs during disasters. By working closely with them, you will help understand their expectations and offer them the most suitable solutions. Together with our resolutely customer-oriented approach, we will strive for excellence in customer satisfaction!

In the Customer Operations sector, you are involved in shaping the customer experience that has suffered a loss and receive benefits. Our clients are at the centre of our concerns and we are looking with them for a solution tailored to their needs. By systematically applying the motto "Customer first", we guarantee very high customer satisfaction.

What's waiting for you

  • As the primary interlocutor for our automotive claims clients, you consistently place their satisfaction at the heart of your concerns by proactively responding to their needs and keeping them informed of decisions made about their cases.
  • You manage motor vehicle insurance claims (civil liability, casco) independently and targeted, clarifying facts and assessing coverage and liability issues accurately
  • You verify and rule all claims fairly and effectively, ensuring fair treatment for each case
  • Thanks to your excellent communication skills, you communicate by phone and in writing with insured persons, injured persons, lawyers and other insurance companies, always focusing on quality customer service

What you bring

  • You have commercial or equivalent training (ideally in the insurance sector)
  • Experience in processing claims or subscription to car insurance would be a valuable asset
  • You have good expression and negotiation skills and, ideally, you are comfortable with exchanges in several languages.
  • You appreciate customer contact and are determined to find the best possible solutions
  • You see change as an opportunity for evolution
  • You demonstrate an ability to work efficiently in dynamic environments and keep your calm in high load situations

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed.
