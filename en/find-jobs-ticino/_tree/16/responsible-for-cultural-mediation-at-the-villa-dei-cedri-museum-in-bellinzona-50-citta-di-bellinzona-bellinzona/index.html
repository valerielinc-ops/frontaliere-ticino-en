Competition for the indefinite recruitment of a cultural mediator at the Villa dei Cedri Museum in Bellinzona (50 %)

The Bellinzona Musei Autonomous Body opens the competition for the indefinite recruitment of a part-time cultural mediator (50 %) at the Villa dei Cedri Museum in Bellinzona (for detailed information on the museum see Profile and objectives The responsible for the cultural mediation of museum conceives, realizes, coordinates, follows and evaluates the activities and creation of supports for the current and potential public of the Museum. The aim is to propose educational and cultural projects that promote the largest number of links between the Museum and the population. It collaborates with the various services of the Museum and with the partners of the pedagogical, cultural and social network. Tasks

  • participates, under the responsibility of the direction of the Museum, to the definition of

mediation action programmes;

  • defines, coordinates and evaluates the mediation activities and the necessary supports; in

special defines and realizes the program of specific days, involving adults, children and families;

  • creates and curates relations with external bodies at the Museum that act as intermediaries

for the various public of the Museum (communication, promotion);

  • defines together with the direction of the Museum the necessary supports for the promotion of

cultural mediation activities and the promotion of these texts (for the press, website, newsletters);

  • updates the website regarding cultural mediation activities;
  • recruit, shape and direct employees participating in mediation projects

cultural (guides, animators/cars, accompanying staff);

  • contributes, together with the direction of the Museum, to the formation of staff

welcome and surveillance for its specific sector;

  • is responsible, under supervision and in accordance with the direction of the Museum, the

budget of cultural mediation activities;
