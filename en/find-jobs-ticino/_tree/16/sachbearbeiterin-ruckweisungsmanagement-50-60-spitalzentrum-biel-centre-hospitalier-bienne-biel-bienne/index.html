Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

They are the central hub in the secretariat of medical coding. With your organizational talent, your accuracy and your understanding of medical and insurance-related processes, you ensure that administrative processes are handled efficiently, correctly and on time. We are looking for you immediately or by agreement

Personnel in rejection management, 50 - 60 %

You do.

  • Examination and handling of incoming insurance enquiries
  • Correspondence with insurers and other service providers
  • Close cooperation with medical coding
  • Clarification of insurance-relevant questions with the treating doctors
  • Administrative and professional support of medical coding

That sets them apart.

  • Commercial education, preferably in insurance or health care, or a comparable qualification
  • Knowledge of medical terminology
  • Secure handling of administrative processes and enjoyment of handling complex issues
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method with a high level of service orientation
  • Enforcement and communication skills in exchange with insurance companies and internal specialist areas
  • Enjoyment of team-internal and interdisciplinary cooperation

They benefit from it.

You have the opportunity to work in a highly motivated team and to position yourself as an internal service provider. The Spitalzentrum Biel offers you attractive employment conditions with flexible working hours, as well as the possibility of a proportional home office activity according to a collective employment contract and has its own restaurant with advantageous prices for the staff as well as a day care center.
