Full description

Join an adventure that continues to grow. Decathlon in Switzerland is a success story that began in 2017 in Neuchâtel. Since then, we have opened around ten stores per year to reach 50 points of sale today. And we don't plan to stop there! Our expansion continues, particularly in German-speaking Switzerland, offering career opportunities throughout the country. Why is this job for you? Are you looking for a job that moves, where you share your passion for sport from morning to evening? Do you want to be proud to recommend the right equipment and see your customer leave with a smile? Do you like action, the field and teamwork? Your 3 big benefits with us:

  • You are at the heart of the action. Your daily life is 100% on the ground. You are the face of Decathlon. Whether it's welcoming a beginner or advising an expert, you share your passion and create a bond.
  • You invent the business of tomorrow: You don't just "sell". You offer sustainable solutions: equipment rental, repurchase of second-hand products (second life) or digital orders. You act for sport and for the planet.
  • You are autonomous and responsible: This is your area! You manage your stocks, you showcase your new products and you organize your commercial operations. Your impact is immediately visible on the satisfaction of your customers. What you will do on a daily basis: your missions
  • Advice above all: You welcome, you listen and you guide athletes towards the perfect product, whether on the shelves or via our digital tools. You also offer our loyalty program and manage collection.
  • An impeccable department: You install the products, you manage the "facing" and you make your sales space attractive, dynamic and adapted according to the seasons.
  • Inventory control: From receiving pallets to putting them on the shelves, you guarantee that the customer always finds what they are looking for. You also prepare web orders.
  • The circular economy: You are the ambassador of our new models, rental and second-hand repurchase to offer a second life to equipment, for example.
  • One-off missions: Depending on your wishes, you can become a referent on a subject, a trainer for your colleagues or even help with the overall management of the store. This position IS NOT for you if...
  • You prefer to sit behind a screen all day in peace.
  • You don't like to approach others spontaneously.
  • You think that unpacking pallets, tidying up a shelf and processing merchandise is “not your problem”.
  • You don't want to work on Saturdays.
  • You are looking for fixed and routine schedules: here, we live to the rhythm of sport and our schedules adapt to the activity (even if we communicate them to you 6 weeks in advance so that you can organize yourself!).
