Full description

Customer Service – Sales Workload: 100% Place of work: Level of experience: Career entry FlexOffice: on-site Would you like to get started in sales after your apprenticeship or studies? In the Sales Excellence Program, we prepare you specifically for your future role as a customer advisor over a period of 18 months. You will get to know the insurance industry from the ground up, develop your consulting and sales skills and be accompanied by experienced mentors. Step by step, you will take on more responsibility, build up your own customer base and lay the foundation for a successful career in sales.

What you do

  • During the Sales Excellence Program, you will learn everything you need for a successful career as a customer advisor step by step.
  • You advise customers by phone and in person and gain valuable practical experience in sales.
  • You will win new customers and gradually build up your own customer base.
  • You will support market development in your region and arrange consultation appointments.
  • You will work with experienced colleagues and benefit from their knowledge and experience.
  • You will be involved in sales and marketing activities and get to know different sales channels.
  • You will complete the comprehensive basic training, including the VBV certification financed by us, and continuously expand your specialist knowledge as well as your consulting and sales skills.

What you bring to the table

  • Completed training (apprenticeship, baccalaureate or university)
  • Enjoyment of dealing with people and enthusiasm for advice and sales
  • Strong communication skills, empathy and a confident appearance
  • Initiative, willingness to learn and the desire to develop yourself personally and professionally
  • Goal-oriented and independent way of working
  • Stylistically confident written and spoken German skills
  • Category B driving licence
  • Residence in the region or the willingness to build a strong network there
