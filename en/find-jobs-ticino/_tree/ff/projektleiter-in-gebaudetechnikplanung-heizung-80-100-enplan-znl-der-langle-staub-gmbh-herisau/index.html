Full description

Do you know us? Enplan, ZNL of Längle & Staub GmbH is a planning and engineering office for building technology with five employees in Herisau. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the leading provider of cross-trade building technology in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein.

Are you ready to shape the future with us? For our motivated team we are looking for a project manager in building technology planning heating, 80-100%.

Your tasks

  • You lead projects independently and supervise planning tasks in the field of building technology from the design to the specialist construction management.
  • You plan modern heating systems for new buildings, conversions and renovations with different uses.
  • You design plants for efficient operation and control them during commissioning.

Your profile

Have you completed an apprenticeship as a building technology planner or a comparable further education? Do you feel comfortable with the daily use of the MS-Office package and CAD, like to work independently and precisely and are an open and service-oriented personality? If you are also a communicative person who likes to be part of the team and has fun responding flexibly to customer requirements, then you are at the right place.

Our offer

In our offices you will meet a young and open-minded team. Together we are actively contributing to the energy transition. Advanced employment conditions with a 4.5 day week, state-of-the-art infrastructure and training opportunities round off our offer.

Your contact

Do you feel addressed? Olivier Wetli is happy to answer your questions on +41 71 354 89 70. Send your application documents by email to olivier.wetli@enplan-herisau.ch.
