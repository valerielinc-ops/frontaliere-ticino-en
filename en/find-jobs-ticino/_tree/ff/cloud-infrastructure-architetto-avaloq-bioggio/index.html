Full description

Founded and headquartered in Switzerland, Avaloq is continuously expanding its global footprint with around 2,500 colleagues in 12 countries, and more than 170 clients in 35 countries. We are an industry-leading provider of wealth management technology and services for financial institutions around the world, including private banks and wealth managers, investment managers, as well as retail and neo banks. Our research led approach and continual innovation is powered by the passion and creativity of our colleagues.

We are always looking for talented people to join us on our mission to orchestrate the financial ecosystem and democratize access to wealth management. Avaloq offers the opportunity to work closely with some of the world’s leading financial institutions as we jointly develop and shape careers. Championing a collaborative, supportive and flexible work environment empowers our colleagues to reach their full potential.

Join our Cloud & Infrastructure Delivery Architecture team and play a key role in shaping the future of our modular, cloud-native banking platform. You will be contribute to the design, development, and deployment of our Avaloq Banking Solution SaaS offering on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), ensuring scalability, reliability, and automation. The team acts as the primary architectural interface between clients, delivery execution, and central architecture functions, ensuring alignment between customer needs, execution feasibility, and architecture standards.

Your key tasks

  • Develop end-to-end infrastructure and cloud solution designs for Avaloq SaaS on OCI
  • Support Cloud Engineers and Developers ensuring solutions are deployable, scalable, and operationally sustainable
  • Collaborate with internal teams and clients to deliver tailored cloud solutions aligned with strategic goals
  • Provide expert-level support and guidance on cloud architecture and DevOps practices
  • Contribute to cost optimization and implementation approaches
  • Troubleshoot complex issues and drive continuous improvement in system performance and reliability

Qualifiche

  • Proven experience as a Cloud Architect or in a similar role
  • Strong expertise in DevOps methodologies and Git-based project workflows
  • Solid understanding of cloud-native architectures, including Oracle Kubernetes Engine (OKE), Oracle Exadata, Oracle RAC, container orchestration, and enterprise deployment strategies
  • Proficiency with automation tools such as Terraform and Ansible is strongly preferred
  • Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and abstraction skills
  • Strong communication and collaboration abilities
  • A proactive, team-oriented mindset with a passion for innovation

It would be a real bonus if you have

  • Hands-on experience with Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) is essential; experience with other cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP) is a plus
  • OCI Certified Solution Architect preferred
  • Knowledge of cloud governance, landing zones, multi-region architectures, business continuity, and cloud security best practices
  • Additional certifications in AWS, Azure, or GCP are advantageous
  • Experience with enterprise observability and monitoring solutions such as Grafana, Prometheus, ELK/OpenSearch, or Splunk

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
