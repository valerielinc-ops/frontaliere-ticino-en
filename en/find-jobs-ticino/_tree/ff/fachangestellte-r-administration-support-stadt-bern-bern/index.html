Full description

The Support Section is the hub of the Asylum Service: Together with the social workers of the Integration 1 and 2 sections, we ensure that our clients receive their insurance, benefits and requirements correctly and reliably. We clarify questions about AHV, family allowances, health insurance and scholarships, are the first point of contact at the counter and on the phone and take care of post, new registrations and accounting in the case management system. We are in exchange with clients, authorities, medical practices and insurance companies.

We work in specialized fields, represent each other, consider strengths and preferences and share responsibility. With us, a flat hierarchy and a collegial approach awaits you.

Due to a change in the law for persons with S status and temporarily admitted (VA), new social assistance rights arise. We are therefore facing various adjustments, such as the change from collective insurance to individual insurance as well as new entitlements to family allowances and scholarships. In order to cope with this additional effort, we are looking for several new employees in the Support section.

Application deadline until 23.11.2026 Tasks: • Collaboration with main focus in the field of research scholarships, health insurance or AHV • Additional fixed cooperation in at least one other field of work • Processing of orders of social workers and general administrative tasks (data management in the case management system NFAM, counter, telephone service, postal processing) Requirements • Commercial or comparable training • Ideally, work experience in the asylum and refugee sector or in public welfare • Joy of dealing with people from different cultures • Planning and organisational skills • Careful work and persistence for routine work and tricky business cases We offer: For sense and city life With us you change something. You help shape city life, carry responsibility and make Bern more liveable. In the city of Bern, more than 4,500 people work together across five directorates and more than 80 job profiles. We are committed with passion and conviction to the people who live here, who work here. Together we shape the community and the future of this city – visible, tangible, sustainable. for our city. For Bern.
