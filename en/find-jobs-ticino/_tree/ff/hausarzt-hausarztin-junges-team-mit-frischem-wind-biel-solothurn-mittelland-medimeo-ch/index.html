Full description

Their language skills make the difference: for a medicine that is approachable and accessible to all. In this family practice, you look after many patients from the Balkan states and Hungary, for whom a conversation in the mother tongue is particularly valuable. In this way, you create trust and closeness, facilitate the exchange of health concerns and contribute to an understandable, individual and patient-oriented care. This family practice was taken over by a committed young team in early 2026 and has an established, large patient base. The modern and spacious practice facilities are located in an easily accessible village in the canton of Solothurn – next to the train station. Due to the current office hours, a workload of a maximum of 80% is possible. The practice is closed on Thursdays. I am happy to tell you more about this exciting position in a personal conversation. I look forward to your contact on +41 31 310 98 79

Their tasks

  • Independent family care of an established patient base
  • Carrying out preventive examinations, diagnosis and treatment of acute and chronic diseases
  • Counselling and support of patients: women in all health issues
  • Close cooperation with the practice team and, if necessary, with specialists: inside and other healthcare providers

Your Competences

  • Specialist title General Internal Medicine or title Practical Doctor (or equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland)
  • Enjoying an empathetic and patient-oriented care
  • Language skills in an Eastern European language or Hungarian are beneficial
  • Self-employed, responsible and team-oriented working methods

Their advantages

  • Modern, spacious practice facilities directly next to the station and thus optimally accessible
