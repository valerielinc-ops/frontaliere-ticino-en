Start-up: as soon as possible Activity rate from 80 to 100% The Romance Rehabilitation Clinic (RRC) is part of the Suva clinics and is an essential pillar of the integrated care chain. It works closely with university hospitals and specialized centres to ensure coordinated, high-quality and sustainable patient reintegration. In order to temporarily strengthen our healthcare teams, we are looking for several : INFIRMIER/EER HES / ES Specialized unit paraplegia / tetraplegia

  • Your Mission As a HES-ES nurse, you accompany patients with paraplegia or tetraplegia in their life rehabilitation and reconstruction journey. Within a specialized unit, you play a central role in the overall care of patients who require complex, technical care and long-term specialist care. Develop and implement individualized care plans that promote patient autonomy and quality of life. Ensure specific technical care related to spinal cord injury, prevention of secondary complications and follow-up of paraplegic and tetraplegic patients. Assist patients in the various stages of their rehabilitation, taking into account their physical, psychological and social needs. Participate actively in interdisciplinary symposia and contribute to patient-centred therapeutic goals. Coordinate and oversee the care provided by the ASSCs, ensuring the safety, continuity and quality of care. Work closely with the medical profession, the therapists and the various professionals involved to ensure consistent and individualized care. Coach learners and participate in the integration of new collaborators.
  • Your profile Bachelor in nursing or recognized equivalent (Swiss Red Cross recognition for foreign degrees) Interest in accompanying patients with paraplegia or tetraplegia. A sense of responsibility, autonomy and reliable professional posture. Excellent relational skills and ability to create an appropriate therapeutic relationship with patients facing major changes in their autonomy and lifestyle. Ability to evolve in a demanding environment that requires rigour, interdisciplinary collaboration and backward capacity. Motivation to invest sustainably with patients requiring intensive and long-term support. Flexibility and collaborative spirit to support continuity of care and interdisciplinary mutual assistance. Master's degree in written and oral French (minimum B2). Our Long-Term Patient Care Offer: Track patient progress and contribute directly to their return to independence. Enriching Environment: Collaborate with experts from various fields in an interdisciplinary approach. Continuing training: Access a wide range of training courses to strengthen your skills and develop your career. Dynamic team: Work in a caring environment where efforts are recognized and valued. Benefits: High social benefits, many benefits, free park space, and much more.
