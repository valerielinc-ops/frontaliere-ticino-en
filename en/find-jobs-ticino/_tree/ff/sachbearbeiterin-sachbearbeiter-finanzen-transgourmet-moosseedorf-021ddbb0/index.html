Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services.

Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You process goods invoices and take care of the creditor workflow (KWF) in SAP
  • You create payment runs and carry them out on schedule
  • You take over the processing of the car bank as well as QR payments and clarify open questions.
  • You make ledger bookings and vote on various accounts
  • You are handling incoming creditor reminders
  • You are the contact person for assigned Prodega markets and an important support when registering subsidiaries
  • You conduct correspondence with customers via email and telephone

Requirements

  • You have a completed basic commercial training
  • Ideally, you already have a further education as a finance and accounting clerk.
  • French and Italian skills are beneficial
  • You are well-versed in the MS Office programs
  • You bring advantage SAP knowledge
  • You have a flair for numbers and pleasure in customer contact
