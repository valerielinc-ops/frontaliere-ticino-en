Full description

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation and technology advisory group that helps companies thrive in a dynamic digital world. With first-class experts from business and technology, we empower our customers to set new standards for sustainable transformations – from strategy to implementation. Whether in the design of sustainable organizations, the development of new potential through data and AI or the protection of companies through holistic cybersecurity: We deliver results pragmatically and purposefully. Our team of around 1,300 dedicated employees is represented in offices in Switzerland, Germany, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Denmark, Sweden, Austria, the UK, Singapore and the USA and combines global perspectives with strong local roots. Around 450 employees work at the Swiss locations in Zurich, Bern and Lausanne.

This is the job

  • Creation of offers, presentations and customer-specific documents including preparation of analyses, business cases and decision templates for management
  • Support of project managers in the development and implementation of IT solutions in customer projects in the field of digital manufacturing
  • Independent collection, documentation and prioritization of technical requirements from the manufacturing departments as well as their translation into clear specifications for IT and digitalization projects
  • Participation in workshops including preparation and follow-up of the content, documentation of the results and derivation of concrete recommendations for action together with your team
  • Participation in knowledge transfer initiatives, e.g. contributions to publications or internal projects

This job is for you if

  • You have a successfully completed university degree, e.g. in information technology, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, industrial engineering or a comparable degree program, preferably at master level.
  • You have gained first professional experience or internships at manufacturing companies or in management consulting.
  • You have experience or interest in using AI in transformation projects for measurable customer impact.
  • You speak German and English at at least C1 level and have strong communication and presentation skills in the customer context.
  • You have a team- and solution-oriented way of working.

As an i-top you bring with you

  • Initial experience in the manufacturing environment, especially in current topics such as MES, Industry 4.0, Smart Factory and Digital Manufacturing.

What is important to us With Eraneos, what counts is what you want to move. Even if you do not fully meet every requirement, we look forward to your application. We are interested in your mindset, your ideas and the impact you want to create. Diversity is a source of growth and inspiration for us. We look forward to every application.

Annalena Raich is your contact person and looks forward to receiving your direct application via our website (CV, diplomas and employment certificates as PDF).

Applications by e-mail or through personnel service providers are not considered.
