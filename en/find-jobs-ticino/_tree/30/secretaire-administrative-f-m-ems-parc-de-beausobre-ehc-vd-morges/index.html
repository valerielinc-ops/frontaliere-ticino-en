Full description

The park of Beausobre is nestled in the park of the same name in the city of Morges, and offers a personalized welcome for 82 residents. Designed to foster intergenerational ties, the EMS is surrounded by a sheltered housing residence, a Child Living Centre and two colleges. The park of Beausobre also has a restaurant and cafeteria, open every 7 days/7, a wellness area for residents (jacuzzi and sensory baths) and an aromatic garden with a fully secure perimeter.

We are looking for EMS Parc de Beausobre

Administrative Secretary (M/F) - 50% (annualised rate)

The activity rate is 50% annualized. You will therefore be required to complete 100% replacements during the vacations of your two colleagues in office, with recovery of the working time over the rest of the year.

The position is proposed on an open-ended contract (ILC), with an entry into office or to be agreed upon.

WE OFFER YOU

  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of redeeming an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time leave, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • A mobility plan including participation in your cycling, basketball or public transport subscription, with an environmental focus on an institutional sustainability plan.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience, as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Free pilates courses, financial participation in sporting events such as the 20km of Lausanne or Bike to Work, a catalogue of advantages offering discounts in the fields of sport, well-being, leisure, mobility, insurance and finance.
  • Hot drinks offered in the morning and meals at CHF 8.50 in our cafeterias.
  • A "Pâquis Plage" business drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Opportunities for internal change, according to your desire for development.
  • Many accessible in-house or certifying training.
  • A programme dedicated to managers to support them in acquiring the skills necessary to manage their teams.

YOU MAY

  • An EFA or equivalent.
  • Strictness, flexibility, a very good team spirit and availability to perform replacements during the holidays and absences of the team.
  • A confirmed professional experience of at least 3 years in a secretarial position, ideally acquired in EMS or hospital.
  • An excellent mastery of French, both oral and written (writing ease and spelling).
  • A very good mastery of the usual computer tools
  • Knowledge of Carefolio and Opale software is an asset.
  • The ability to work in a structured environment requiring administrative procedures.
  • Excellent organization and ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously in a dynamic environment.
  • An excellent sense of hospitality and service to residents, families and visitors.
  • An ability to adapt to an annualised activity rate, which may involve working periods up to 100%, as well as one-off trips on the network's EMS to Morges and Aubonne.

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Ensure all administrative management (resident movements, admissions, transfers and exits).
  • Manage the EMS fund as well as those of residents according to the procedures in force.
  • Provide mail, billing, management of various administrative documents and treatment of care evaluations.
  • Prepare animation plans and write minutes.
  • Schedule sessions, organize various calendars via Outlook and process email boxes.
  • Effectively alternate between administrative tasks, reception at reception and telephone, ensuring continuity of service.
  • Help maintain a professional, warm and caring welcome for residents and their loved ones.
  • Ensure the replacement of team secretaries during their vacations and absences, which may require up to 100% activity in certain weeks.

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
