Full description

Class teacher 3rd class, school unit Masans With 41,000 inhabitants, Chur is the urban center of Graubünden. As an attractive and versatile city, it provides customer-oriented and modern services with its 1,400 employees.

The Chur City School is the largest public school in the canton of Graubünden with numerous attractive offers from kindergarten to secondary school.

Masans School House is one of twelve primary schools of the Chur City School. Due to the location directly next to the church and the roots with the district of Masan, it has always retained the character of a quarter school house. The school unit also includes the two kindergartens Masans 1 and 2 and the kindergarten Weisstorkel.

As a maternity representative we are looking for approx. From early March to mid-June 2027,

Class teacher 3rd grade, (28 lessons)

(optional permanent employment from school year 2027/28 as a specialist teacher possible)

Their tasks

  • Enable learning: As a class teacher, you teach the 3rd grade and together with all participating teachers you design a lesson that enables learning success for all children.
  • Teamwork: You work closely with a helpful team. Joint projects and cross-class events are always part of our culture.
  • Cooperation: They advise and accompany guardians and children in the sense of the new authority.

Further fields of activity are described in the professional assignment for teachers of the city of Chur in the fields of teaching, learners, school and teacher. Your profile

  • Education: You have a completed teaching diploma for the primary level.
  • Character: You cultivate a communicative, open and critical approach in the team and actively shape projects and processes.
  • Skills: You have a good self-organization and can convince didactic-methodically.

Our offer

  • Place of work: We offer you an interesting and creative job with a large creative scope.
  • Team: You can expect a motivated and committed team that is committed to positive school development and is mindful of its resources.
  • Chur City School: With the City School as an employer, we are embedded in an innovative and professional school with attractive offers. https://stadtschule.chur.ch/
