Full description

Appointment authority / department / institution

Today more than ever, KUB Freiburg guarantees access to reliable and high-quality information. In the heart of the city and the canton, it offers a place of learning, discovery and remembrance.

Both online and in its new building, KUB provides extensive information resources. It also preserves the canton’s cultural heritage and organises a diverse cultural programme.

Become part of our team!

We provide a dynamic environment related to the creation of a new library in the city centre, which provides for the development of modern services. KUB is a founding member of SLSP (national network) and participates in the development of the services of the Swisscovery network.

We focus on the complementarity of competences and the diversity of perspectives, because it is the wide range of careers and profiles that makes up the strength of the state of Freiburg. We promote the reconciliation of professional and private life as well as flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link.

Their tasks

  • Together with a team of eight employees You will attend the various stages of the acquisition process for printed and electronic documents and support the participants at the KUB headquarters and in the documentation centers of the university
  • Working in collaboration with other departments In particular, you participate in the further development of the digital offer, in the management of freely accessible holdings and magazine holdings, in the management of financial resources and the collection of invoices, and in the coordination of the Freiburg institutional zone (IZ 8) and the library system.
  • You set the acquisition policy for the general collections and select for the KUB headquarters documents acquired by purchase, subscription or donation
  • You participate in the strategic leadership of the institution, especially in your field of expertise

Desired profile

  • You have a university degree with additional training in information science or a master’s degree in information and documentation
  • they have professional experience in information and documentation
  • Experience in team management is beneficial
  • You are familiar with organizational and financial issues and move safely in this environment
  • They have a broad general education
  • You speak French or German and have a good knowledge of the other language and English

Competences

Analytical and networked thinking Team leadership Flexibility Strategic thinking
