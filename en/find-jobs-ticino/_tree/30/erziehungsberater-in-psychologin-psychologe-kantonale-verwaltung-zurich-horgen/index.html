Full description

Canton of Zurich Education Directorate Office for Youth and Career Counselling Education consultant (psychologist/psychologist) 60% Good for life – that is our motto. As part of the Education Directorate of the Canton of Zurich, the Office for Youth and Vocational Advice is responsible for the offers in the field of child and youth welfare as well as in career guidance. Our work focuses on children, young people and families. We work for them every day with passion and commitment. Create perspectives for children, young people and families with us! Would you like to accompany parents in challenging parenting and development issues and work out new perspectives together with them? Are you interested in working in a committed, interdisciplinary team? Then we look forward to meeting you! For our children and youth welfare center (kjz) Horgen we are looking for a parenting consultant by 1 February 2027 or by appointment. Their tasks:

  • They accompany and advise families on challenging parenting and development issues
  • You work interdisciplinary with different specialists and jointly develop viable solutions
  • They advise specialists from the early childhood and school sector on technical issues
  • They participate on behalf of KESB in clarifications and instructions
  • They design and conduct parenting lectures and parenting courses
  • You bring your professional competencies into the further development and expansion of our consulting offer your profile:
  • Completed psychology degree at master level
  • In-depth knowledge of developmental psychology or clinical psychology
  • Sound experience in advising parents
  • Resource and system-oriented working methods
  • Empathetic and appreciative attitude towards clients
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary cooperation as well as strong communication and teamwork skills What we offer you:
  • A versatile and demanding activity in a varied psychosocial field of work
  • A dedicated team and the opportunity for professional exchange
  • Design leeway and the opportunity to actively shape the further development of our service
  • An attractive workplace in the immediate vicinity of the train station and the lake
  • Progressive employment conditions in accordance with cantonal guidelines For further information, Helen Burri, head of kjz Horgen, can be contacted at tel. 043 259 92 47 is available. On www.ajb.zh.ch you will find additional information. Please submit your complete application documents via the application link.

Pensum: 60%
