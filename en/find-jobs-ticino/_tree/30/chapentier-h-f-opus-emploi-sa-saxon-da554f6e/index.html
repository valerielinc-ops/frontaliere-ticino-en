Full description

Carpenter M/F

As part of its development, our client is looking for a Carpenter, motivated and autonomous, able to intervene on different types of new construction sites as well as in renovation.

Your Mission

You are involved in the manufacture, preparation and installation of wooden frame elements. You work on site or in a workshop as required, ensuring compliance with plans, technical instructions, deadlines and safety standards.

Your Responsibilities

  • Cut, assemble and assemble wooden frame elements
  • Prepare and install supporting structures, wood frames, roofs and roofing elements
  • Read and interpret technical plans
  • Conduct lifting, assembly and attachment work on site
  • Participate in renovation, transformation and new construction
  • Ensure necessary finishes and adjustments
  • Monitoring the quality of the work performed
  • Observe existing safety instructions and standards

Your profile

  • Confirmed experience in the field of structural engineering
  • Good control of plan reading
  • Ability to work independently as a team
  • Serious, accurate, manual and organized person
  • Good physical condition
  • Driving licence
