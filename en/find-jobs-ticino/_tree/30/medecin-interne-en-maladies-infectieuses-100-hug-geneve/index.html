Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here. The Department of Medicine (DMED) provides comprehensive and specialized care for patients in hospitals and outpatients. Driven by a holistic, human approach based on best medical practices, it offers reference care in many specialties (cardiology, gastroenterology, pneumology, angiology and nephrology), with 24/7 availability. Engaged in clinical excellence, teaching and research, DMED promotes innovation, continuous skill development and patient-centred care, while ensuring the quality of life at work and the efficiency of benefits. Within this department, the purpose of the Infectious Diseases Service is to reconcile a clinical activity that meets the needs of a university hospital with an academic research and teaching activity with the Faculty of Medicine. It provides diagnostic and therapeutic care for patients with infectious diseases, including HIV/AIDS. It relies on a medical team specialized in infectious and microbiology and develops its activities in close association with many hospital partners in order to ensure multidisciplinary and high quality management. This position is newly created and is part of the ALTER deployment, which aims to ensure that work organisation complies with the Labour Act (LTr).

Description of post

As an in-house doctor, you have started your training course in internal medicine and now want to move towards becoming an infectious specialist. In this context, you join the Infectious Diseases Service and participate in the activities of the various specialized consultations, including general infectious diseases, with immunocompromised people, the elderly, in the field of septic orthopaedics and HIV/AIDS, through rotations. Under the supervision of the executive doctors, you ensure the care of the patients who are entrusted to you, both in outpatient and intrahospital care, within the whole of the institution. You are gradually developing your clinical expertise by being confronted and confronted with various and sometimes complex situations, which strengthen your analytical capabilities, your clinical judgment and your ability to adapt quickly to new medical contexts. Thanks to your sense of organization and rigour, you ensure the clinical, diagnostic and therapeutic activities necessary to manage patients. You ensure compliance with internal rules and procedures, with a constant concern for the quality of medical services provided. Given the variety of requests for consultation and the complexity of certain clinical situations, you are showing composure, discernment and good stress management. Your intellectual curiosity encourages you to deepen your knowledge and understand the challenges of the situations you face. You also welcome constructive feedback with openness and use it as a lever of progress in achieving your training objectives. Evolved in a challenging and demanding university environment, within a multidisciplinary team, you also participate in the training of trainees. You work closely with all the medical teams in the institution, in a proactive approach that focuses on the needs of patients. You ensure that you maintain clear, respectful and appropriate communication with both your colleagues and with the cared for and their relatives. You also have the opportunity to participate in ongoing research projects within the department. As part of your training course, you develop your skills in accordance with the training plan and the requirements of the FMH for three years within the service. It offers an environment recognized for the quality of its teaching, a wide range of infectious activities and development opportunities for obtaining the FMH title. You also benefit from personalized support through coaching sessions, targeted training and regular supervision by senior doctors as well as clinical leaders.

Qualifications

You have a federal doctor's degree or a diploma recognised as equivalent by the Medical Professions Commission (MEBEKO). You have at least three years of experience in general internal medicine or already holds an FMH in general internal medicine. An initial experience in clinical infection is an asset. You are aiming to qualify as an FMH specialist in infectious disease and want to complete all or part of your training within the department. You have a perfect command of the French language (level C1 required) and a very good written and oral capacity. With excellent communication skills in a multidisciplinary team and patient, you are a dynamic, flexible and committed person. In addition, you have demonstrated strong adaptability.
