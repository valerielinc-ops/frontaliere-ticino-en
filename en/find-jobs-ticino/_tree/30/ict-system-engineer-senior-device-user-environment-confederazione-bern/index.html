Full description

In a nutshell

Become part of Cyber Command as a Senior Lead Engineer, help shape an open and interoperable end-device ecosystem for mobile security communications, and create the prerequisites for different user organizations to use their end devices securely and reliably with the shared MSK services.

  • Start date: by agreement
  • Employment type: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-20754
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

This is what you can contribute

  • Work in the team according to agile DevSecOps methods and actively participate in ceremonies at the Agile Team and Agile Release Train levels
  • Work closely with the Product Owner to understand users' requirements and develop complex solution proposals
  • Configure mobile end devices and associated solutions in the area of Mobile Device Management and Unified Endpoint Management
  • Analyze incidents affecting large ICT platforms or applications and actively contribute to troubleshooting (including on-call duty)
  • Maintain technical system documentation

This is what makes you unique

  • Higher vocational education in computer science, mobile computing, systems engineering or a comparable field, as well as several years of senior-level experience in the Device / Enterprise Mobility environment
  • In-depth knowledge of Mobile Device Management and Unified Endpoint Management, as well as Android Enterprise, iOS/iPadOS, Secure Workspace and mobile application environments
  • Several years of experience with device security, identities, certificates, eSIM and the integration of heterogeneous end-device ecosystems
  • Very good understanding of the end-to-end interrelationships between end devices, radio access, applications, security, core and platform
  • In-depth experience in the technical evaluation of end devices and in conducting integration, compatibility and Proof-of-Concept tests
  • Good active knowledge of a second official language

This is what we offer

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and contribute different perspectives.
  • Healthy at the workplace We support and advise our employees in the area of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for expertise in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine our enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Armed Forces and our partners in the Swiss security network. This enables the Kdo Cy to give the Swiss Armed Forces the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a secure Switzerland.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ over 9'000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in more than 30 professions.

Additional information

A job with the Swiss Armed Forces makes sense and comes with many benefits. See for yourself at armee.ch/vorteile. Offers from recruitment agencies will not be considered.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Their applications are therefore particularly welcome.
