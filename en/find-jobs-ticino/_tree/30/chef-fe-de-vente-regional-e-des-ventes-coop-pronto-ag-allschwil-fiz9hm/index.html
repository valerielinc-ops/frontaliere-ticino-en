Regional Sales Manager Coop Pronto

Coop Pronto AG — Allschwil, GE, Svizzera

  • Entry into office: immediately or by appointment
  • Area of activity: Other areas
  • Workplace: Coop Pronto AG, Hegenheimermattweg 65, 4123 Allschwil

Coop Pronto SA is a company of the Coop Group. As a leading Swiss company in the fields of convenience, service stations and energy, we operate a network of over 330 Coop Pronto shops, with or without service stations, in Switzerland and Liechtenstein.We continually develop our network of Coop Pronto shops and Coop service stations.You support and develop the franchise partners of your region and guarantee the economic, legal and professional support of Coop Pronto shops. You follow key indicators, availability for sale and market trends, and encourage targeted sales, training and succession in your area.

Mission

  • Support and development of franchise partners
  • Guarantee of operational, legal and economic monitoring of Coop Pronto shops (approx. 20-25 sites)
  • Monitoring of measures put in place to ensure compliance with turnover, sales volume, quality assurance, labour law, profitability and sales promotion objectives
  • Overall responsibility for sales readiness in the sales area
  • Development of budgetary targets for key sales indicators in the area of sale
  • Market surveillance and support for fuel pricing
  • Support for initial and continuing training and the promotion of the succession of franchise partners

Qualifications

  • Learning in sale/retail, preferably from the food sector
  • Several years of professional experience in the retail trade, including at least 3 to 5 years in a managerial position, and good performance in managing turnover and costs
  • Continuing training in the Swiss retail trade or in business management / management / marketing
  • Excellent consulting and communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of MS Office and guaranteed mastery of digital tools
  • High organizational capacity and strong personal motivation, as well as high time flexibility and good stress resistance
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of French and good oral knowledge of German are required.
  • Home in the " Lausanne/Geneva region" (if not moved)
  • A cover letter in German is desired
