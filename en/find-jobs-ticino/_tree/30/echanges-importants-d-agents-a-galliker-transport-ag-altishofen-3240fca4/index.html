Substantial agent swaps (a) The central customer service in Altishofen coordinates and addresses the concerns and needs of our customers in the areas of Cargo, Food, Frigo and Healthcare Logistics. In order to be able to further optimize our services in terms of perfection and quality, we are looking for you as an agent in the exchange business (a). Task area

  • Recording, processing and monitoring of exchanger orders in the system
  • Checking and ensuring complete and timely order processing
  • Close cooperation with planning for optimal route and resource planning
  • Coordination of processes between chauffeurs, logistics and administration
  • Telephone support and advice for our customers with questions and concerns about exchange equipment
  • Organisation and commissioning of empties and general collections
  • Monitoring and management of empty stocks to ensure availability

Requirement profile

  • Basic commercial training completed
  • A well-groomed and convincing appearance
  • Enduring, diplomatic personality with strong communication skills
  • Negotiation skills and high adaptability
  • Good oral English and French skills

Offer

  • Personal development opportunity in a versatile company
  • Flat hierarchies and fast decision-making processes
  • Working in a dynamic family business with a clear, direct corporate culture
  • Generous, modern workplace (good public transport and free parking)
