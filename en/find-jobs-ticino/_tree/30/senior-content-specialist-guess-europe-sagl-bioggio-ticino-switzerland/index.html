Full description

The Senior Content & Art Direction Specialist will join the Content & Advertising Production team, contributing to the creative development and hands-on art direction of fashion content across multiple brand touchpoints.

The role will translate brand positioning, creative guidelines and marketing priorities into compelling visual concepts and high-quality content executions across advertising campaigns, lookbooks, e-commerce, video, social media and digital platforms.

Combining strong fashion sensibility with a solid understanding of visual storytelling and commercial objectives, the position will play an active role throughout the creative process, from concept development and moodboards to hands-on art direction during production and final asset review.

The role will ensure a cohesive creative vision across different formats and channels, adapting visual narratives to specific communication and product needs while maintaining brand consistency and quality standards.

CORE JOB RESPONSIBILITIES

The primary responsibilities will be

Creative Concept & Visual Development

Art Direction & On-Set Execution

E-commerce Visual Development & Quality Improvement

Content Integration & Cross-Channel Alignment

Creative Collaboration & Production Coordination

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
