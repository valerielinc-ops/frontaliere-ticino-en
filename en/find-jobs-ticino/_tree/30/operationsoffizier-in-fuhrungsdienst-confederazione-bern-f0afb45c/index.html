Put to the Point

Actively shape the operational leadership of the Swiss Army: In this versatile function, you combine military staff work with modern leadership processes and digital leadership instruments. Together with internal and external partners, you will lay the foundations for successful operations, missions and support services.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20537
  • Place of work: Papiermühlestrasse 20, 3014 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Support the planning, situational tracking and follow-up of operations, missions and support services of the army as well as work on assigned sub-areas independently
  • Create, consolidate and further develop management products along the staff control process as well as operate and keep up to date the management and information systems used
  • Develop, coordinate and continuously develop the Staff Regulations for the respective Joint Operational Planning Group
  • Written contributions for management reports, briefings, commands and decision-making principles and coordinated with internal and external partners
  • Support projects for the further development of leadership instruments, processes and systems and represent the interests of the army’s leadership service in working and specialist groups

This makes them unique.

  • University or technical university degree at bachelor level or higher technical or business education with several years of relevant professional experience
  • In-depth experience in military staff work and as a staff officer; military degree major or higher
  • Knowledge of project management as well as experience in the cooperation or management of demanding projects
  • Strong analytical, conceptual and organizational skills as well as the ability to prepare complex content accurately and appropriately
  • High digital affinity and very good knowledge of MS Office and military command and information systems; good knowledge of a second official language and English language skills at level B2

Your commitment to security and freedom

The Command Operations (Kdo Op) is responsible for all military operations and operations of the Swiss Army. The Kdo Op includes the Luftwaffe, the Army, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT Competence Center, the Special Forces Command and the Military Police. Around 40% of the approximately 2,500 employees are employed by civilians – from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
