Full description

HORIEN Salt Battery Solutions, FZSoNick's new business brand, is a world leader in the production and marketing of sodium nickel-chloride storage systems. It offers safe and completely sustainable energy storage and backup solutions: from raw materials to maximum recyclability of components, our products aim to foster the decarbonization process towards a more sustainable world.

HORIEN is an industrial group with manufacturing plants and commercial branches in Italy, Switzerland, United States, Mexico, Brazil, China, France and Germany. The group is active in the energy and automotive components sectors and is known for its commitment to supporting the transition to new developments and innovations.

Location For the strengthening of our team at the Stabio headquarters, we look for an Electromechanical Keeper who will take care of the following tasks:

  • Fault diagnosis and resolution on electrical installations and components;
  • Preventive and corrective maintenance on semiautomatic machines and industrial plants;
  • Support in PLC analysis, inverters, sensors and control systems;
  • Reading and interpretation of electrical schematics and technical documentation;
  • Collaboration with the team for the continuous improvement of the performance of the plants.

Requirements

  • Technical diploma (electrical, electronic, mechatronic or affine);
  • 4-5 year experience in industrial maintenance;
  • Good readability of electrical schematics;
  • Basic knowledge of industrial automation;
  • Interest in extending skills also in mechanical, pneumatic and hydraulic sectors;
  • Activity to problem solving and teamwork;
  • Accuracy and attention to safety regulations.

Other information

  • Availability is required for work both day and shift, including night shift;
  • The activity involves contact with nickel-containing materials: the absence of allergy to this material is therefore necessary
