Who are we?

The Service du médecin cantonal SMC is the centre of reference for all kinds of public health issues and contributes to the maintenance and improvement of the health of the Fribourgeois population. To this end, it cooperates closely with other federal, cantonal and communal public services and coordinates its activities to provide the necessary information and advice to the Council of State and the Department of Health and Social Affairs DSAS. The Centre fribourgeois de santé sexique (CFSS) is a sector of the MSC that actively contributes to the prevention and promotion of the sexual health of the Fribourgeois population by providing services tailored to their needs.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Ensure the evaluation, management, coordination and monitoring of situations related to sexual acts, such as problematic sexual behaviour in children, suspicions or revelations of sexual violence or abuse, in collaboration with the partners of the Fribourg network
  • Conduct interviews related to these situations with children, young people, families and in collaboration with the partners of the Fribourg network
  • Train and sensitize partners and future professionals on issues related to sexual health, the use of digital tools, the prevention of sexual violence, discrimination and the management of situations
  • Contribute and improve the quality of the sector's services

Profile desired

  • Master's degree in psychology, criminology, social work or equivalent training
  • Further training in sexual health or in a relevant field
  • Knowledge of the cantonal and federal legal framework on sexual health, protection of persons and prevention
  • Experience in assessing and accompanying complex sexual health situations
  • Bilingual French - German or mastery of the French language with very good knowledge of German
