\\Based in Vouvry (Chablais Valaisan)\\ Want to put your expertise in safety at the service of a pharmaceutical and biotechnological environment? For our laboratory in Vouvry, we are looking for 100% security charge, for an indefinite period. As an international Swiss pharmaceutical company active in more than 60 countries, Chemedica has been a major player in the production and distribution of reference therapies for the treatment of ophthalmological, rheumatological and neurological disorders for 40 years. At a human scale, our company places autonomy, trust and creativity at the heart of its culture to foster professional development. Our organization in project mode allows to develop cross-cutting skills and encourages versatility as well as internal promotion. Specializing in the production of rheumatological injectables and a neurological active substance, our Vouvry site meets the highest requirements, notably through its GMP and ISO 13485\\. \\
