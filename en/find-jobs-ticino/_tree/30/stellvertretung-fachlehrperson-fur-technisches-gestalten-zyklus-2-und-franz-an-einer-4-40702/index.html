Full description

Portrait The Rubigen Primary School is a school in the agglomeration of Bern with modern infrastructure and very good public transport connection. The kindergartens, the basic level classes and the primary classes from 1 to 6. School year are located on a spacious school area in the immediate vicinity of nature. We also run a day school directly in the schoolhouse. Further information can be found at www.schule-rubigen.ch

Tasks

We are looking for a deputy specialist instructor for technical design at 3 classes in cycle 2.

The teacher also teaches 3 French lessons in a 4th grade. The working days are Tuesday and Thursday.

The other subjects: NMG, Franz, Sport, BG will be taught on Monday and Wednesday in a 4th / 5th class. The position can also be divided.
