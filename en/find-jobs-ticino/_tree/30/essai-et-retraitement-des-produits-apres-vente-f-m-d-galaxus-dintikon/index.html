Full description

Our after sales service in Dintikon handles returns and in particular warranty cases in cooperation with our suppliers. In the Refurbishing Team, you give returned goods a second life. You check, clean and reset devices so that they go back on sale technically flawless and clean. Sounds like you? Then apply now!

Mansioni

  • You control returned products for functionality and completeness
  • Missing accessories you complement so that the product is complete again
  • Technically and visually you prepare the products – including data deletion on electronic devices
  • You ensure that all products and product descriptions meet our high quality standards
  • You document the condition and properties independently in the ERP system so that the products are back for sale

Requirements

  • Advantage: warehouse, production, technical area or preparation of goods
  • Consumer electronics is your thing – you bring real interest and expertise
  • You are resilient, have physical endurance and a careful, reliable and independent work method
  • On the computer you work safely and use sovereign ERP systems
  • You adapt to continuous changes in the working environment while remaining flexible
  • Your actions and decisions are aligned with your personal beliefs and values.
  • No education/training required
  • German (flowing)
