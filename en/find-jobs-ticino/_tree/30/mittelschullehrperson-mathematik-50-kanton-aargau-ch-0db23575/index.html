Exciting tasks await

  • They teach mathematics at the high school and at the secondary school and design a technically sound and contemporary teaching.

What you bring

  • Good knowledge of English and willingness or Experience teaching mathematics immersively in English is an advantage.

The Cantonal School Zofingen is an innovative, ambitious middle school with a high school and specialised secondary school. A dedicated and motivated college teaches more than 600 students who benefit from a wide range of educational opportunities.
