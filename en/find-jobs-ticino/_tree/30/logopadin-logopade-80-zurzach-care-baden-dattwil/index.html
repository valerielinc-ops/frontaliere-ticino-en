Full description

ZURZACH Care is the leading group of companies for inpatient and outpatient rehabilitation, sleep medicine, prevention and reintegration. At 16 locations in German-speaking Switzerland, we offer medical and therapeutic expertise for a healthy life. In this way, we support and facilitate the re-entry into everyday life after inpatient rehabilitation.

In our clinic in Baden-Dättwil, we offer surveillance rehabilitation and neurological rehabilitation at a high medical level. This is why you are looking for

Speech therapist / Speech therapist 80%

What makes the job special

  • Patient-oriented therapy: You treat our patients over several weeks and accompany them on their way back to an independent life. You build a trusting relationship and experience firsthand how they progress and achieve their goals.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Therapy, care and medical service work hand in hand. Your expertise flows actively into the exchange and contributes significantly to the successful rehabilitation
  • Various medical histories: Each patient brings an individual medical history. This diversity makes your day-to-day work varied and exciting as you learn about different diagnoses and treatment approaches.
  • Speech therapy: You diagnose and treat inpatients with speech, speech, voice and/or swallowing disorders in individual or group therapies. This advises and motivates the patients to self-training and is the contact person for the relatives.

You bring that with you

  • Training : You are a qualified speech therapist / qualified speech therapist with EDK recognition (if not available, we support you in obtaining it)
  • Experience: Have an advantage You already have experience in rehabilitative therapy and dysphagia or tracheal cannula management
  • Personality: You have a high level of empathy

We offer this

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern work environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial working environment: Work in a supportive, appreciative and humorous team that works together to provide the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits and comprehensive benefits
  • Education and training : generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts at various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers and pharmacies
  • Free admission : Enjoy free admission and other benefits in the thermal baths FORTYSEVEN and the thermal bath Bad Zurzach
