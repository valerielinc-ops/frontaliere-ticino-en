Full description

Actively shape the strategic development of BKW Power Grid. You develop strategic options, accompany their implementation and make progress transparent. With well-founded analyses and recommendations, you create the basis for decision-making for management.

Your field of activity

  • You will further develop the strategy of Power Grid and derive strategic options and recommendations for action from business and business analysis
  • Demanding strategic projects you manage and coordinate independently and accompany their implementation together with the specialist areas
  • Complex questions and new initiatives you structure and analyze independently, bring creative ideas and develop practical solutions
  • For management, you develop well-founded and meaningful decision bases
  • With key figures, monitoring and reporting, you make strategic progress transparent and recognize the need for action at an early stage.
  • You moderate strategy workshops, advise the management and promote the anchoring of the strategy in the business area with targeted communication measures

You bring that with you

  • Completed university studies in economics, technology or natural sciences or a comparable education
  • Several years of experience in strategy and project management as well as profound knowledge in strategic management, preferably in large companies
  • In-depth knowledge and practical experience in developing and implementing strategies as well as managing complex, interdisciplinary projects, ideally in the energy environment
  • Strong analytical and conceptual skills as well as experience in market and financial analysis and the development of strategic options for action
  • Strategic thinking, networked action and an independent, structured working method with a high level of initiative, consulting competence and quality standards
  • Very good German and French skills
