Director / Director Consorzio Depurazione Acque del Verbano

Consorzio Depurazione Acque del Verbano (CDV)* population, the territory and contributes to the environmental quality of one of the most important water basins of Switzerland: the Major, at the center of a region of great tourist importance. With a team of 20 people, we manage the treatment of waste water of more than 120\,000 inhabitants in 22 Municipalities through two large purification plants, over 120 km of network, 43 stations pumping and 23 clarification basins. We invest in human resources, reliability infrastructure, biogas enhancement and future-oriented solutions for management water and energy and to address new climate challenges.
