Full description

Within movodis SA, the LEB links Lausanne to Gros-de-Vaud and supports the development of a growing region. By 2035, he plans to introduce a 10-minute pace between Lausanne and Echallens in order to offer ever more attractive and sustainable mobility.

This evolution is accompanied by many infrastructure projects and requires reliable and rigorously maintained safety (IS) and electrical installations. To ensure the safety and availability of the network, movodis is seeking a motivated team leader to supervise and coordinate the maintenance of these facilities and to put his technical expertise at the service of the LEB.

IS team leader and LEB electric

Your Mission

As part of the LEB's public transport service mission

  • Coordinate, supervise and monitor daily maintenance operations on safety and electrical installations in accordance with the expected cost, time and quality
  • Daily monitoring and management of entity employees in the field
  • Conduct preventive and corrective maintenance of safety and electrical installations

Your activities

Field monitoring of the team: Ensures the operational conduct of its team:

  • By relaying the instructions of the electric maintenance manager
  • By taking a position of influence, participatory leader against the team
  • By relaying issues or proposals from the team to the hierarchy
  • By supervising the work and following the proper execution of the work
  • Providing expertise as a technical respondent

Management of the team: Assumes the responsibilities inherent in the conduct of staff vis-à-vis employees directly attached to it, i.e.:

  • Ensuring the adequacy of the tools and placing the necessary controls
  • Developing weekly schedules
  • Developing the annual picket schedule
  • Recruitment and Integration
  • Management of team results
  • Skills development
  • Operational monitoring

Maintenance: Runs maintenance operations for safety and electrical installations:

  • By providing preventive maintenance
  • By providing corrective maintenance
  • Controlling the operation following interventions
  • Providing picket service and related interventions
  • Respecting quality and defined costs
  • By carrying out some revision work on equipment

Compliance with safety and quality standards: Meets and enforces the required safety standards within the company:

  • By applying the safety procedures and ensuring that they are respected by the staff of the team
  • Ensuring traceability of interventions carried out

Your profile

Training

  • CFCs in the technical field, ideally as automatician (electrician), electronician or equivalent
  • In-service specialised training (internal and external) in railway clearances and necessary for the practice of the trade

Professional experience

  • The trade requires between 3 and 5 years of professional experience, in a similar field as a railway technician
  • First successful experience of coaching

Authorizations, legal requirements and security

  • Compliance with company IT rules and data confidentiality
  • Driver's licence category B (light vehicles)
  • Chief safety officer according to R TEN 20100 (periodic training every 3 years)

Practical information

  • Quality working conditions and social benefits
  • Place of work : Echallens
  • Contract: 100% indefinite
  • Selection process: 1st interview with manager and recruitment specialist to get to know each other

At LEB, talent does not have a gender: we accept female and male candidates with the same interest.
