Full description

The Office for Forest and Natural Hazards (AWN) is the cantonal specialist for forestry and protection against natural hazards. The employees of the AWN are committed to the needs of society in the forest and ensure that the forest is handled sustainably.

You make sure that the Bundner Wald

  • can protect people, animals and infrastructure,
  • habitat for a variety of animal and plant species,
  • is a raw material supplier of wood, water and energy,
  • can continue to be a recreational and leisure space for people.

The Office for Forest and Natural Hazards offers a professional internship (80-100%) in the region of Domination/Prettigau/Davos (place of work Landquart).

You can look forward to that.

  • Individual design of the internship according to your/our preferences
  • Get a comprehensive insight into mountain forest management
  • A meaningful task with direct reference to integral risk management in natural hazards, nature and technology
  • Working independently in a committed and collegial team
  • Modern working conditions

Tasks

  • Diverse insight into the work of the cantonal forest service Graubünden
  • Participation in mountain forest strike drawings in the protective forest
  • Accompanying visits related to natural hazards
  • Insight into the planning and implementation of protective structures and developments
  • Development of forest ecology concepts
  • Support for public relations work
  • Support for forest law issues

Requirements

  • Environmental scientist with a deepened forest or landscape (minor Bsc) or forestry engineer or similar training according to the tasks
  • Internship duration 4-6 months (preferably in the months March to November) or by agreement
  • Engaged, team-oriented and reliable personality
  • General working language is German
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is an advantage
