Put to the Point

SECO’s Labour Directorate is the federal competence centre for labour market policy. The labour market/unemployment insurance benefit area is responsible for unemployment insurance and public employment services. The Basics Group accompanies current political affairs in the field of labour market policy and coordinates these cross-departmentally with education, social and migration policy in particular, deals with economic, labour market and social policy issues in connection with unemployment and accompanies external studies and evaluations.

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
