The Position Job Description: Launch Partner

Position Overview Are you looking for an agile, high-intensity opportunity to shape a high-stakes launch at Roche Thailand? We are seeking a dynamic, business-minded talent to step into the role of Launch Partner. This is not a traditional back-office role. It is a unique hybrid archetype combining the data-driven strategic ownership of a Product Manager with the frontline execution capability of a Key Account Partner.

Working alongside our Strategic Launch Lead and an ecosystem of enabling partners (Local ICX, Medical Enablers, and the Alliance Field Force), you will completely own the end-to-end execution engine for an assigned portfolio of priority hospital accounts. Your main objective is to eliminate "last-mile" bottlenecks: accelerating local hospital listings, standardizing field messaging, and driving rapid prescription adoption among top Nephrologists and Rheumatologists.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Brand Strategy & Campaign Execution
  • Translate high-level brand plans into localized marketing programs utilizing local segmentation data and analytics.
  • Design, test, and rapidly adapt promotional tools tailored specifically to the nuances of the Nephrology and Rheumatology therapeutic areas.
  • Break out of single-domain silos by managing both the high-level data translation and the practical, frontline application of marketing materials.
  1. Sales Team Enablement & Field Excellence
  • Run continuous scientific messaging workshops to upskill the contract sales force.
  • Provide direct, in-field support to ensure the sales team delivers a standardized, high-impact scientific message to clinicians.
  • Monitor field execution performance against frontline KPIs, adjusting coaching and tools responsively.
  1. Frontline Account Ownership & Hospital Access Acceleration
  • Act as the strategic commercial engine for a dedicated portfolio of target hospital accounts, ensuring zero territory or portfolio overlap.
  • Serve as a trusted advisor to core Nephrologists and Rheumatologists to build strong product confidence and bypass hospital formulary barriers.
  • Own the "last-mile" listing process by navigating complex institutional hospital frameworks to accelerate local formulary approvals.
  1. Cross-Functional Synergy & APAC Network Integration
  • Act as the central operational hub connecting local field insights back to our APAC Network tools, local Medical Enablers, and the ICX Partner's digital campaign network.
  • Collaborate closely with the Head of Business Enablement and ICX Enablers to leverage commercial excellence analytics and data visualization tools.

Who You Are (Qualifications & Skills)

  • The Hybrid Mindset: A strong, proven blend of strategic thinking (data translation, campaign localization, segmentation) and frontline commercial drive (KOL relationship development, presentation mastery, account navigation).
  • Agile Collaborator: Proven ability to manage complex interdependencies. You excel at working cross-functionally and acting as a unifying hub between external vendors, strategic leads, and contract sales teams.
  • Communication & Influence: Excellent scientific and commercial communication skills, with the credibility required to engage high-level TAEs)
  • Resilience & Agility: Thrives in ambiguous launch settings, values quick testing and adaptation of promotional tools, and is deeply motivated by hitting frontline KPIs.
  • Language: Be able to communicate in Thai with TAEs and related STHs
