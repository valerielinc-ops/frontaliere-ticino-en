Civil service offers an alternative to Swiss military service which cannot reconcile this service with their conscience. Civil servant positions are reserved exclusively for persons admitted to civilian service and have made all the necessary requests to the Federal Civil Service Office (CIVI).

IMAD, recognized as a priority assignment facility, offers the opportunity to fulfill your civil service obligation as: Assistant Advisor to Home Security Clients Missions and responsibilities 50% Client response Respond to requests and questions from the clients of the Autonomy Technology Service. These are mainly elderly people with a home safety system, as well as a small proportion of hearing impaired people with light signals. 25 per cent Security check In a framed way, perform simple checks of the tele alarm system among the beneficiaries. Check the security system in place at our customers. Contact clients to ensure that the security system is always appropriate, that the list of respondents in case of a call for help is relevant, and that administrative data is up to date. 25 per cent Heatwave Identify clients most vulnerable to risks during the summer to prepare the next summer's heat wave.

  • Experience with common IT tools, especially Excel
  • Customer service and relationship ease, especially in communication with the elderly
  • Interest in new technologies.
