Full description

Are you passionate about continuous improvement and always keep the big picture in mind? Do you want to increase efficiency in a dynamic environment and actively help shape the future of our production? Then this position could be just right for you. At our site in Oensingen, we are looking for a committed and experienced person to fill the role of Deputy Head of Process Engineering & Lean Management (80%). In this role, you will have the opportunity to further develop our improvement management and sustainably optimize processes. ## Main tasks

  • Development and maintenance of our TOP X system as a central element of continuous improvement
  • Analysis of relevant KPIs to identify key areas and deviations
  • Independent management of optimization projects and introduction of innovative ideas to increase efficiency and sustainability
  • Support and motivation of employees to fully utilize their potential and actively contribute to process improvement
  • Coordination and monitoring of measures along the entire supply chain at the site
  • Conducting audits and system reviews, as well as deriving, developing, and accompanying improvement measures
  • Development and documentation of strategic goals for the further development of the production system in Switzerland
  • Management and parallel coordination of several small projects ## Requirements
  • Completed vocational training, preferably in the food industry (butcher, food technologist, plant operator)
  • Further training in the field of Lean Management and Total Productive Maintenance (TPM)
  • Several years of experience in implementing Lean Management in a manufacturing industry
  • Experience in project management, small to medium-sized projects
  • Experience in the food industry, pharmaceutical industry, or a similar sector
  • High affinity for IT, good knowledge of MS Office programs (especially Excel) and experience with ERP programs (SAP, CSB, etc.)
  • Experience in a regulated industry and handling GMP
  • Knowledge of maintenance strategies and systems is an advantage
  • Very good German skills, French and English skills are an advantage
