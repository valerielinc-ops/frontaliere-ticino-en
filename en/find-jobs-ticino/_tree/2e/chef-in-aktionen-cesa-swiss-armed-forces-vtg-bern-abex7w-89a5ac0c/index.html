Put to the Point

Become part of the Cyber Command and actively shape the Cyber and Electromagnetic Security and Defense (CESA) of the Swiss Army. You are responsible for threat analysis, action planning and tracking of cyber and electromagnetic self-protection and business management of the CESA department. We are looking for an expert who is already well-versed in the cyber world and has profound experience in staff management. Are you passionate about protecting the Swiss army from modern threats? We look forward to your application.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization.
