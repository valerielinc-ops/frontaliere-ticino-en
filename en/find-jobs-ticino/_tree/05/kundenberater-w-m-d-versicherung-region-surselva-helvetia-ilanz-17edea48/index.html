Full description

Setting new standards together and shaping the future - for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

What you do ##

  • Advice & support with foresight: You accompany private and corporate customers in all insurance issues with real commitment and ensure tailor-made solutions for every individual need. As a customer advisor: you are there when it matters.
  • Build long-term relationships: Trust is at the heart of your work - you maintain and develop sustainable customer relationships based on your excellent service.
  • Active growth: You have a good network and bring your energy to the acquisition of new customers. Your commitment ensures that our customer base is constantly growing. How can we support you in this? By getting your own customer base with private and corporate customers.
  • Marketing & Sales Support: With fresh ideas you support our marketing campaigns, visit trade fairs and events to strengthen our network.
  • Always on the pulse of the times: Thanks to our basic training and continuous further education, you stay up-to-date and offer the highest consulting competence.

What you bring with you ##

  • Experience & expertise: You bring experience in customer advice or sales - ideally in the financial or insurance industry. Also ambitious lateral entrants: women who are ready to start quickly are welcome.
  • Communication talent: People inspire and win - that's in your blood. You shine through communication and negotiating strength. As a customer consultant, you can argue convincingly and have stylish knowledge of German.
  • Service is your strength: The field service is your passion and your high customer orientation and willingness to service distinguish you.
  • Commitment & initiative: With above-average motivation and entrepreneurial spirit, you are ready to achieve great things and exceed goals.
  • Regional anchorage: Your place of residence is in the Surselva region where you serve your customers. You have a strong network in this region and appreciate building and maintaining new contacts.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Graubünden's migration office after the contract is signed.
