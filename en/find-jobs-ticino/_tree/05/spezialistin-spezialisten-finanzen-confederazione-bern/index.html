Put to the Point

SECO’s Labour Directorate is part of the federal competence centre for all core questions of labour market policy and unemployment insurance. The labour market/unemployment insurance (TC) service area manages and develops one of the most important social security systems in Switzerland. As part of our transformation program TC-LevelUp, we modernize our services, drive digitalization and shape the organization of tomorrow. In order to strengthen our FCFB department, we are looking for an experienced and committed personality who wants to put their financial expertise at the service of an important public task and actively participate in the transformation of SECO-TC.

  • Entry date: by immediate or by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20453
  • Location: Friedheimweg 14, 3007 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Maintaining the financial accounting (principal account, accounts payable, accounts payable, including workflow payable and investments) of the unemployment insurance compensation office (ALV). Responsible for the regularity and legality of this accounting
  • Preparation of the monthly and annual accounts of the unemployment compensation body
  • Cooperation in the preparation of the periodic consolidated financial statements of the ALV Fund and the preparation of reports
  • Advise and support implementing agencies on financial and accounting issues, training of learners
  • Participation in modernization, digitalization and transformation projects of unemployment insurance
  • Assistance in planning, managing and managing the financial resources of the compensation fund

This makes them unique.

  • Commercial training with further training in finance and accounting (skilled accounting officer, specialist in finance and accounting or equivalent training)
  • Several years of professional experience (minimum 7 years) in finance and accounting
  • Strong understanding of financial processes and high process orientation
  • Analytical thinking and rapid understanding
  • Independent, careful and result-oriented working method, high willingness to perform as well as team-ready and flexible
  • Excellent IT skills in MS-Office and SAP ERP; Experience with SAP S/4HANA is beneficial
  • Good active knowledge of two official languages and good knowledge of English

We offer this

  • Working for Switzerland: We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Living diversity: Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work: We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
