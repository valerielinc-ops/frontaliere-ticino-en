Full description

University Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

At the foot of the Jura and in the middle of the three-lake country, lies Biel – the bilingual, cosmopolitan city with a special flair. Biel is not only known for its bilinque charm and its many excursion destinations in the vicinity, but also closes an important gap with regard to child and youth psychiatric care in the Bernese Jura, Biel and Zealand. During your free time do you like to stroll through the old town with its numerous cafés and petits magasins? In the day clinic we treat children between 6-17 years. Mental health, two words say why it is worthwhile for employees to work for our children every day. You too? Do you also want to meet people at eye level and work in an inspiring, multicultural environment? Here at the Psychotherapeutic-Pedagogical Center Biel-Bienne at the University Hospital for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy (KJP), the focus is on the individual. Here you have the opportunity to become active in an appreciative and highly competent interdisciplinary team and to contribute your expertise. In this position, you work in a varied and exciting environment that offers you a lot of scope for action. You benefit from excellent team dynamics and develop innovative treatment approaches.

Your tasks

  • You take over the day clinical, child and adolescent psychiatric treatment
  • You plan and conduct patient-centered treatments in an interdisciplinary team
  • You check the effect of the measures taken and have relieving, orientation-giving discussions with patients, their family systems and other helper systems
  • You document the care and patient data and organize your work tasks independently
  • Actively participate in team meetings and case conferences to ensure the exchange of expertise and continuous improvement of treatment strategies

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a social pedagogue FH /HF or as a dipl. Nursing professional
  • You ideally have experience working with children, adolescents and their family systems
  • You distinguish yourself through high social and professional competence and work constructively in an interdisciplinary team
  • You integrate children and guardians fully into your actions and stay calm and reliable even in difficult situations
  • You feel comfortable in the clinical environment and have good IT knowledge
  • You have very good knowledge of German and French in word and writing
  • You are active, independent and resilient as well as ready for an intensive discussion with our patients

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
