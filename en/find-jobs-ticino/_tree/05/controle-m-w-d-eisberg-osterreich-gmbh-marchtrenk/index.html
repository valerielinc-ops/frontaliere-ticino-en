Full description

Good decisions need good numbers. And that's where you come in. You analyze key figures, question developments and make complex relationships understandable. With your reports, forecasts and analyses, you support the company in important decisions. This position is full-time. Tasks:

  • Timely preparation of monthly reports as well as deviation analyses including their commentary
  • Budgeting and forecasting
  • Support in the preparation of the UGB annual accounts
  • Cooperation in the establishment and expansion of professional controlling structures
  • Further development and creation of Power BI reports in coordination with the central BI team
  • Responding to ad hoc requests and creating analyses
  • Supporting the management of finance requirements:
  • Completion of a higher business education (e.g. university or university of applied sciences)
  • Several years of professional experience (minimum 3 years) in controlling, preferably in the manufacturing industry
  • Analytical thinking and high number affinity
  • Experience in the analysis of key figures and deviations
  • Structured, independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Ability to understand complex relationships
  • Process and cross-sector thinking
  • Excel skills are excellent
  • Experience with Power BI and Power Query desirable
