Full description

Portrait We are a lively elementary school in which good work is done every day with passion and commitment.

In the school facility Staffel and in three decentralized kindergartens, around 450 students are taught. The school has been run by a co-management since 2013 and sees itself as a comprehensive school across all levels.

Tasks

You teach 4 lessons in the 1/2 mixed class on Wednesday morning for 5 weeks

Requirements

  • You are a motivated, reliable teacher who is interested and committed to their students.
  • You like working in a team.
  • You will work with the curative teacher as well as with the class teams.
  • You prepare the lessons conscientiously and carry them out according to curriculum 21.

We offer

  • Temporary employment as a deputy.
  • You come into a helpful and committed team.
  • We have a modern infrastructure.
  • An exciting field of work with a high level of personal responsibility and a wide range of options for teaching.
  • Close cooperation with school social work and day school.
  • A school that is there for you.
  • The school Moosseedorf is easily accessible by public transport, but also has enough parking.
