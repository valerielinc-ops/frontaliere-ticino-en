Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. In our production, we produce fresh couvertures from high-quality raw materials. Afterwards, we process and refine the liquid chocolates in our chocolate factory to form FrischSchoggi, pralinés, truffes, figures and much more. Do you also want to participate? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You take on various tasks – from filling up the ingredients to packaging the finished fresh shoggi tables
  • You clean and maintain our modern production facilities

  • You work shifts from 03:30 to 13:00
