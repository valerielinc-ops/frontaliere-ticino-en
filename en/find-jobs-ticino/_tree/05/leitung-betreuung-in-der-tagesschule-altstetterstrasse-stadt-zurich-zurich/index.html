Full description

The day school Altstetterstrasse in Zurich-Altstetten is a diverse and growing school with four kindergartens, ten primary school classes and four care locations. As a QUIMS school, equal opportunities, respect and the participation of all children are particularly important to us. Our school is characterized by a strong team culture in teaching and supervision as well as an attractive school complex with a school garden and a “green classroom”. As of February 1, 2027, we are looking for a manager who can reliably manage his department in terms of expertise, personnel and operations and at the same time develop our day school as a shared educational and living space. She builds bridges between supervision and teaching and shapes the further development of our school in the management team and together with the employees.

  • Personnel management, promotion and development of employees in support at four support locations
  • Educational, human and operational management of care across four care locations, including resources and budget
  • School development in cooperation with school management
  • Active participation in the day school and the cooperation of teaching and support as a common educational and living space
  • Binding cooperation at central interfaces within and outside the school, in particular with other management functions, parents and specialists
  • A degree at tertiary level in the field of pedagogy, social pedagogy or a comparable subject
  • Completed leadership training or willingness to complete it in a timely manner as well as proven leadership experience
  • An in-depth pedagogical understanding of care as an educational and living space and the ability to develop pedagogical quality together with employees
  • The ability to work in an interdisciplinary manner while incorporating different perspectives
  • A responsible leadership function with a wide range of creative scope in a growing day school
  • A dedicated team with a strong culture of cooperation, mutual support and joint development
  • Attractive employment conditions according to the guidelines of the City of Zurich

The Zurich-Letzi school district comprises Stadtkreis 9 with the districts Albisrieden, Altstetten and Grünau. In the 16 schools of our rapidly growing school circle, more than 5,000 primary school students are taught and supervised. More than 1,200 employees work for them in our schools in the areas of teaching, care, home service and technology as well as administration. They all work daily for the interests of the students and a smooth school day.
