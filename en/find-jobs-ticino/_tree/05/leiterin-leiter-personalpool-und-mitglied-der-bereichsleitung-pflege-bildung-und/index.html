Full description

Care, education and social services

Do you want to actively shape the future of flexible personnel solutions in the healthcare sector? You combine strategic thinking with business understanding, strong leadership skills and enjoyment of the further development of innovative personnel models? Then you take responsibility for the established internal staff pool Flex 26 with around 300 employees and shape its further development. As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement we are looking for you as The Psychiatric Services Aargau AG (PDAG) is responsible for the high-quality medical, therapeutic and nursing treatment and care of mentally ill people in the Canton of Aargau. They employ around 1,600 people in over 50 professions. Care, education and social services 5210 Windy Workload: 80 % 80 %

Over the area

The cross-sectional area of care, education and social service is composed of the following: Competence center education, staff pool flex26, care development, de-escalation management, social service and central registration. The Competence Center for Education supports the staff with and during initial, further and further training and thus makes an important contribution to ensuring a professional and high quality of care. Care quality is also a central concern of care development, which together with the specialists manages cross-station projects based on evidence-based knowledge. Thanks to our own staff pool flex26, we are able to quickly and professionally compensate for short-term vacancies / failures and to compensate with PDAG-internal nursing staff. The de-escalation management supports the treatment team in the competence to de-escalate milieu design, advises and supports employees before and after threat situations. It develops interdisciplinary training concepts and conducts training on this topic. The social service is an important part of the interprofessional treatment offer. It supports and supports patients in social and professional integration with the aim of regaining their independence in everyday life. The central registration is the first point of contact for the external assignees, helps with questions about our offers, accepts assignments and tries them.
