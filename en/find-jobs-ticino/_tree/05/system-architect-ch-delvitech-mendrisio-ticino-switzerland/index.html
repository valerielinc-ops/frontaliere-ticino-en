Full description

Shape the architecture of Delvitech's AI optical inspection systems, connecting hardware, software and AI into one reliable platform. Department: R&D Location: Mendrisio, Switzerland, 100% on-site Reports to: Head of R&D The Opportunity Delvitech develops advanced AOI (Automatic Optical Inspection) systems for electronics manufacturing. We are looking for a System Architect to strengthen the architecture and analysis of our next-generation software platform, working alongside our senior software architects. This is an individual-contributor role (no people management). Main Responsibilities

  • Analyze requirements and translate them into a clear system architecture: modules, service boundaries, interfaces and data models.
  • Contribute to the design of Delvitech's future software platform, from concept to production.
  • Make pragmatic technology choices and stay close to the implementation (prototypes, proofs of concept, code reviews).
  • Lead design and architecture reviews; raise engineering quality (testing strategy, CI/CD, performance, reliability, security).
  • Work closely with hardware, machine-vision and software teams on hardware-software integration.
  • Document architecture decisions and technical specifications. Requirements (Must Have)
  • Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or a related technical field.
  • 6+ years of software engineering experience, with solid hands-on experience in system architecture and design.
  • Strong skills in at least one of: C++, Java/Spring, Python.
  • Experience with distributed systems / microservices, REST/OpenAPI and Linux.
  • Track record of bringing complex software systems into production.
  • Professional fluency in English. Nice to Have
  • Experience with industrial, embedded or hardware-software systems.
  • Knowledge of machine vision, AOI or SMT/electronics manufacturing.
  • Rust, TypeScript/React.
  • Docker/Kubernetes, observability tools (Grafana, Prometheus).
  • Experience integrating AI components into production software.
  • Italian or German. Soft Skills Structured and analytical thinking, pragmatism, clear communication with technical and non-technical stakeholders, mentoring attitude, ownership, kind, humble, friendly.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
