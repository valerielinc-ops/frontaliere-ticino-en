Full description

Key responsibilities In this exciting, sales- and conclusion-oriented advisory function, you are responsible for processing credit requests and can leverage your commercial talent for our premium product lineup. This includes the seizure and verification of written and telephone credit applications, as well as the development of client-specific solutions. As a customer advisor, you offer top-notch service to our clients and work closely with internal interfaces.

We are looking for a new colleague who will take over the following main tasks:

  • Processing and verification of incoming credit applications
  • Verification of the accuracy and authenticity of customer information and documents
  • Communication of credit decisions to the customer
  • Telephone advice for customers around the "BANK-now offer"
  • Systematic exploitation of potential (repurchase of external credits, realization of the potential for up-selling, sale of complex products and cross-selling)

The team In BANK-now, you will find a Swiss SME culture, integrated into the UBS group. We are proud of our agile and collegial environment, where (almost) everyone knows each other. Here, every collaborator and collaborator is part of our success story. We are proud of our horizontal hierarchies and short decision-making circuits.

You will evolve in a collegial environment, within a young and dynamic team based at Horgen headquarters. We attach great importance to active collaboration and mutual support. Servability and strong personal motivation of team members form the basis for achieving our common goals.

Your skills and experience

  • Several years of professional experience in a consulting and sales environment
  • Excellent mastery of French, as well as ease of negotiation in English, German or Italian. Any other language is an asset.
  • Pleasure and openness in acquiring new customers
  • High flexibility and a clear sense of customer and service
  • Commitment and entrepreneurship
  • Service-oriented personality with excellent communication skills
  • Structured, results-oriented and very precise working method
