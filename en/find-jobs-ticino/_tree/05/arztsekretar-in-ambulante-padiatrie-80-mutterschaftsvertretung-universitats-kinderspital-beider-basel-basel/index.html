Full description

The Secretariat of Outpatient Pediatrics at UKBB ensures a professional and efficient administrative process. In close cooperation with the medical departments, the team contributes to the optimal care of patients and their families on a daily basis. For a temporary maternity representation until 31.12.2027, we are looking for a committed personality with a strong organizational talent.

Entry date: as of 01.02.2027 or by arrangement

Their tasks

  • Management and maintenance of electronic medical histories (KG)
  • Processing and coordination of reporting including report editing
  • Processing of insurance and IV inquiries
  • Independent management of general secretarial work (correspondence, telephone and mail, postal processing, lists and statistics)
  • Support for the optimization of workflows and processes
  • Close cooperation with the medical profession, nursing and other internal and external bodies
  • Organisation of congresses and symposia

Your profile

  • Completed commercial training, ideally supplemented by further training in the medical field
  • Several years of professional experience as medical secretary, preferably in an outpatient environment
  • Experience in pediatrics and knowledge of common hospital and administration systems
  • Strong organisational talent as well as a structured, independent and networked working method
  • Open to change, with initiative and pleasure in actively shaping improvements
  • Communicative, resilient and team-oriented personality
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of French and/or English is an advantage
  • High service orientation and a reliable and efficient working method, even in demanding situations

Our offer

  • A varied and responsible activity with scope for design
  • A dedicated, warm and collegial team in a professional working atmosphere
  • The opportunity to contribute your own ideas and actively shape processes
  • Attractive employment conditions and modern work equipment
