SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. Shape the digital future of Swiss rail transport and play a key role in one of the most important transformation programmes. In the ERTMS@Vehicles SBB program, you will create the basis for the migration from voice and data radio GSM-R to the Future Railway Mobile Communication System (FRMCS) and for future forms of operation with the European Train Control System (ETCS) and Automatic Train Operation (ATO). As a programme manager, you have a direct impact on the performance and interoperability of our rail vehicles. You work closely with interdisciplinary teams, national and international committees, authorities and technology partners and get a high degree of design freedom. With us you will find a modern working environment and the opportunity to develop professionally and personally. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As program manager, you take over the overall responsibility for the on-board migration program from GSM R to FRMCS and thus strengthen the digital performance of the rail system.
  • You represent the program to corporate management, authorities, vehicle manufacturers, suppliers and other partners and ensure sustainable decisions.
  • Throughout the entire lifecycle, you build and develop the program organization and take responsibility for deadlines, costs, quality and risks at the program level.
  • You manage the program organization, lead the program team professionally and ensure effective control of the clients and committees.
  • With a view to the future, you will further develop the vehicle-side migration strategy and integrate requirements from ATO into the program.
  • In close coordination with the track-side migration program and other ERTMS initiatives, you coordinate dependencies and reduce risks across program boundaries.

You bring that with you.

  • With your strong ability to structure complex relationships, you make clear decisions and bring together stakeholders at management level and at the expert level.
  • You work cooperatively in a team, communicate appropriately and win people for common goals.
  • Several years of experience in managing complex programs or major projects with high technical and organizational complexity, ideally in the area of railways, rail vehicles, control and safety technology or infrastructure.
  • Knowledge of ERTMS, ETCS or telecommunications systems in the railway environment is an advantage, and you have experience working with authorities, industry partners and external service providers.
  • You have a university degree in engineering, electrical engineering, transport systems or in a comparable discipline.
  • In addition, you have very good knowledge of German and English (at least C1) and ideally French at level A2.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
