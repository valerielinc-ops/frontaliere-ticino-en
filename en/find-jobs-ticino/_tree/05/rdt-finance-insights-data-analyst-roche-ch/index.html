The Position

RDT Finance Insights & Data Analyst

A healthier future. It’s what drives us to innovate. To continuously advance science and ensure everyone has access to the healthcare they need today and for generations to come. Creating a world where we all have more time with the people we love. That’s what makes us Roche.

At Roche, our Finance Centers of Excellence have led the way in breaking down silos, removing hierarchies, and working in new ways. We’ve learned from our successes and from the things that haven’t gone so well. We’re better equipped to look at interfaces and synergies and to understand the risks of creating new silos. Now is the time to build on those learnings, shifting from a divisional/functional approach to an enterprise approach with an Enterprise Center of Excellence (eCoE).

The Opportunity

The eCoE focuses on three areas delivering a high level of financial expertise, innovative insights, and an entrepreneurial mindset that enables agility and flexibility for stakeholders. The eCoE is a talent engine for Finance through capability building and network building.

There are three focus areas within eCoE

  • Financial Backbone
  • Digital Innovation
  • Analytics and Insights

While there are three focus areas and clusters, we are not seeking to create silos. We are looking for individuals who display the Finance Mindset and behaviors and will support a living organism that will leverage the business knowledge and financial expertise of the network to deliver exceptional results for Finance and Roche. Team members will partner with Roche Services & Solutions, Finance Enterprise Partners, and the Business to deliver many of Finance's core processes and deliverables while also driving process scaling/simplification/harmonization and progressing our digital ambition. Ensuring business continuity is a must.

The key deliverable of the role include

  • Support monthly actual reporting events (incl. variance analysis), annual budgeting and monthly forecasting processes, ensuring compliance of the Roche Digital Technology (RDT) financials with the overall Group Finance processes.
  • Support the analysis and production of reports to facilitate in-depth reviews and deep dives on financial and business performance.
  • Support the maintenance of high quality master data for RDT to ensure data accuracy and integrity and to guarantee consistent financial planning and reporting for the guidance and decision-making support.
  • Collaborate with the RDT teams and FEPs, and senior leaders, to gather, consolidate, and analyze financial data to provide clear and actionable insights in alignment with strategic objectives and financial goals.
  • Assist in the preparation of financial materials for leadership team meetings and other communication, and conduct ad-hoc financial analysis and scenario planning to support new business initiatives and key decisions (e.g. resource allocation).
  • Drive process improvements and automation initiatives, based on Digital and AI notably, to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of financial planning and reporting activities.
  • Support the lifecycle management of the team's digital solutions, representing requirements throughout the development process and assisting with key design decisions.
  • Enable / support the successful implementation of organizational changes and bring financial advisory to business and finance transformations and support the development and implementation of new sales and cost reporting models.
