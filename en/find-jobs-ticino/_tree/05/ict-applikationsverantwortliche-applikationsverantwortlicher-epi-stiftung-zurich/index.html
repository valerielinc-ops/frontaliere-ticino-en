Full description

The Multifaceted Day

  • As the third member of the application team, they ensure the reliable operation and lifecycle management of specialist applications and digital services.
  • You will analyze requirements and business processes, identify potential for improvement and develop practical solutions together with our specialist areas.
  • They simplify and automate processes with existing tools and suitable technologies such as the Microsoft Power Platform, workflow and integration solutions, always with a view to economic benefits and sustainability.
  • They look after and optimize interfaces between our business applications and help build a new integration platform and solution architecture.
  • You are responsible for the operational tasks in Identity & Access Management and develop role, authorization and user processes together with the specialist departments involved.
  • They coordinate internal customers, ICT teams and external partners, clarify priorities and create a sustainable basis for decision-making. Even with different expectations and limited resources, keep the goal in mind.
  • They accompany projects from the requirements to the implementation to the introduction and ensure with documentation, training and knowledge transfer that the solutions exist in everyday life.

Their diverse talents

  • They have completed computer science training (EFZ or equivalent) or higher specialist training (HF/FH) in computer science, business informatics or related qualifications.
  • You bring several years of relevant ICT professional experience – ideally in application management, system integration or the digitization of business processes.
  • You are familiar with Microsoft 365 and workflow solutions and bring practice with the Microsoft Power Platform, especially Power Automate, as well as IAM or integration solutions. Experience with UiPath is a plus.
  • They understand business processes and can develop realistic, understandable and feasible solutions from requirements.
  • They can win people over for change and remain constructive and persistent even in the case of resistance.
  • They work in a structured and responsible manner – especially when dealing with authorizations, sensitive data and mission-critical applications in a regulated environment.
  • They are familiar with common project methods and ITSM tools and communicate stylishly in German (C1) and securely in English (B2).
  • A task with substance: They combine operational responsibility with the possibility of developing applications and processes in the long term.
  • A reliable environment: You work in an established organization with diverse tasks, long-term perspectives and a socially important task.
  • Design options with ground adhesion: Good ideas are welcome. Crucial are comprehensible benefits, careful implementation and viable solutions for operations.
  • A collaborative team: You work with dedicated ICT specialists, different specialist areas and external specialists.
  • Professional development: We support your personal and professional development through targeted further education and training.
  • Fair employment conditions: Transparent salary based on the cantonal pay scale, five weeks of holidays (six weeks from the age of 50) and an own pension fund with an employer share of 60%.
  • Further advantages: staff apartments, parking spaces, various discounts and an attractive EPI Park Restaurant with a wonderful view of Lake Zurich.

EPI Foundation

The Swiss Epilepsy Foundation (EPI) is a multi-sector company. It includes the EPI WohnWerk, the Lengg High School, the donation Dapples, a seminar center with restaurant and the EPI Central Services. In addition, the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation together with another foundation forms the sponsorship of Klinik Lengg AG, each with a clinic for epileptology and neurorehabilitation. Around 1000 employees are committed to these social tasks. The area is located on the outskirts of Zurich, with a fantastic view of the nearby Lake Zurich and the Alps.
