Job posting Technical Office Specialist Who are we? Centiel SA is a Swiss technology company that designs, manufactures and provides leading solutions in the industry protection of food for critical infrastructure. Our modular and high efficiency UPS systems, developed by pioneers of the first UPS without transformer, guarantee maximum reliability and availability. Today we operate in over 50 countries, with more than 80 partners and 8 branches worldwide. Who we are looking for To support our growth and strengthen the technical team, we are looking for an office specialist technical with a solid technical base, operational accuracy and ability to manage product data, distinct base, co-divisions, technical changes and information to support business processes. The figure will be inserted inside the technical office and will report to the technical office, collaborating with design, production, quality, purchases, service and other internal functions.

  • Place of work: Cadro (Lugano), Switzerland
  • Report: Technical Office Manager

Purpose of the role The selected person will contribute to the management, updating and optimization of technical aspects related to products, components and articles managed by Centiel. The role supports the correct flow of technical information within the company, ensuring quality, consistency and traceability of data necessary for production, quality, document and operational processes.
