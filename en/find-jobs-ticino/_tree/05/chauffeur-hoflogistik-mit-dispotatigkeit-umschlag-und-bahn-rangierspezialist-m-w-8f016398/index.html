Full description

You are an organizational talent, like to take part and keep an overview in a dynamic environment? For our customer in Brig, we are looking for a reliable personality who combines operational farm logistics with coordination tasks and wants to develop further in the area of shunting service and rail.

TASKS

Professional management of the team and ensuring smooth day-to-day operations

Coordination and control of shunting manoeuvres, train handling and technical train inspections

Contact person for control centre, transport management, network line and locomotive personnel

Monitoring of operational processes and equipment as well as rapid resolution of disruptions

Cooperation in the further development of lifting and maneuvering concepts as well as in process optimization

Handling of swap containers between lorries and railway wagons

Loading, unloading, provisioning and bridging of swap containers

Ensuring timely collection and delivery

Active participation in general handling work on the site

Requirements

Cat C or C/E driving licence

Compulsory readiness for further training as shunting A40 and wagon examiner

Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Willingness to shift work from Monday to Saturday, including public holidays

Good physical condition and pleasure in practical work

Strong organisational talent, networked thinking and high reliability

Customer-oriented working methods and a strong sense of responsibility

Secure use of modern means of communication and openness to digital systems

We provide

Employment with a workload of 80-100%

Responsible and varied activity at the interface between farm logistics, scheduling and railway

Possibility for further technical development in shunting and wagon testing

Working independently with short decision-making paths
