Location: Hirslanden Klinik Aarau | Aarau

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73077

Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for cutting-edge medicine in a manageable, personal environment - true to our clinic goal "First-class medicine with personal treatment, as we wish for our loved ones".

We are looking for a Technical Sterilization Assistant 70-100% by agreement:

YOUR TASKS

  • You are responsible for the professional preparation, cleaning, disinfection and sterilization of all medical products from the surgical area
  • You are also responsible for the careful, exact packaging of all instruments of all disciplines
  • Your tasks also include the correct documentation with the Instacount Plus system
  • You have the willingness to work in all shifts (day, late, piquet and weekend shifts)

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a medical device technologist
  • Alternatively, you have completed an apprenticeship as a technical sterilization assistant with specialist knowledge 1 or 2 and bring several years of professional experience in the AEMP
  • You are motivated, team-oriented and have a responsible, reliable way of working
  • In your area of responsibility, you act independently and conscientiously
  • Good PC knowledge as well as fluent knowledge of German are mandatory

We offer you independent work in a motivated and experienced team. A very familiar environment awaits you, interesting career and development opportunities, up to 29 days of holidays and many other employment highlights.

For additional information, Natalie Bolliger, co-director of operating rooms and intervention rooms, is available under T.

We look forward to your complete online application.
