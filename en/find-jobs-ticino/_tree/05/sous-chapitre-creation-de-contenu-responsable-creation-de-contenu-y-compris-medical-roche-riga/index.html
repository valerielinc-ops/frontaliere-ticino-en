The Opportunity The Content Creation Subchapter Lead is a pivotal people leader who inspires and engages subchapter members to collaborate across the network, significantly contributing to Roche’s Ten Year Ambitions. This role is accountable for driving the integration of the new operating model, exemplifying agile collaboration. The content creation chapter team consists of medical writers, content editors, project delivery coordinators, and medical copy quality assurance specialists.

As the Content Creation Subchapter Lead , you will be responsible for supporting the strategic vision and direction of the chapter, continuously seeking opportunities for innovation and value creation.
