  • The big step into the exciting world of Bosch

As an international company of the Bosch Group and one of the world’s largest manufacturers of power tools and accessories, we focus on single-minded and inquisitive learners. With modern learning methods and intensive support from experienced specialists, we support you until the successful completion of your training. Even after the apprenticeship, we offer you a wide range of attractive career opportunities in various areas. In the Upper Valais region, we are also one of the largest industrial and training companies and can draw on years of experience in the training of learners. Job description Together with clients, engineers and other experts, you develop, model and change concepts, individual parts and assemblies for devices, machines or plants as well as tools and devices for production

  • When developing solutions, you work closely with specifications with the most important information about function, production, quality and costs
  • On the computer you create all documents required for the production such as detailed drawings, parts lists and assembly drawings as well as documentation
  • You take part in meetings, consultations and are part of the project from start to finish!
