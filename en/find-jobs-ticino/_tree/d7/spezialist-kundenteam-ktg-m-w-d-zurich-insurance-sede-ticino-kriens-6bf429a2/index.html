Full description

137860 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich | Hybrid

Do you want to carefully clarify services in the event of illness and help ensure that insured persons are reliably supported? Do you enjoy working with different parties and handling damage cases in a solution-oriented manner?

As a customer team specialist KTG (m/f/d), you will strengthen Zurich Switzerland’s personal insurance services business area. You are part of the customer teams KTG Deutschschweiz, in the team services PV Zurich KTG 2. You regulate benefits in the event of illness in accordance with the legal and contractual provisions, clarify medical matters and coordinate with insured persons, employers and other participating bodies. In this way, you contribute to ensuring that claims are handled competently and services are handled reliably.

This is what awaits you

  • Regulation of sickness benefits in accordance with the legal and contractual provisions.
  • Correspondence and discussions with the parties involved in claims settlement, including insured persons, employers, the medical service, other insurers and law firms.
  • Clarification of medical matters and execution of payments in accordance with the legal requirements and insurance contracts.
  • Management of recourse claims against third parties and other insurers.

This sets you apart

  • Commercial basic education in the insurance sector or a diploma in the social insurance sector.
  • Experience in the processing of sickness allowance cases.
  • Medical experience.
  • Excellent communication and negotiation skills.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing; further language skills are beneficial.
