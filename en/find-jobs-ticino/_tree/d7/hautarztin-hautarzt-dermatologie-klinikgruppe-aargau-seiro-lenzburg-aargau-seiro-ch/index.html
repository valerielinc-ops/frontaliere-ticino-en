Full description

Dermatology in one of the leading private hospital groups in Switzerland, with around 700 patients every day and real room for manoeuvre. In Lenzburg, Canton Aargau, we are looking for a specialist in dermatology in the range of 60 – 100% by appointment. In an established clinic group for dermatology as well as aesthetic and plastic surgery, you can expect a dynamic medical team, modern infrastructure and a broad spectrum of dermatology in the outpatient setting. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They take care of dermatological patients independently in the outpatient setting
  • They cover the entire spectrum of classical dermatology and also treat aesthetically if interested
  • They compose the patient documentation and maintain correspondence with assigning doctors
  • You work in collegial exchange with a medical team of around 30 specialists
  • They actively shape the professional development of dermatology

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed specialist training in dermatology and venerology, as well as all prerequisites for professional license and OKP approval in the canton of Aargau
  • Alternatively for applicants from Germany or Austria: completed training as a specialist in dermatology and venerology and at least three years of work at a recognised Swiss training institution
  • Openness to innovative tools and applications that reduce bureaucracy and support medical quality
  • Flowing knowledge of German in words and writing, knowledge of French, Italian or English is advantageous

That sets them apart.

  • Self-employed, responsible work with high professional quality standards
  • Strong social and communication skills in dealing with patients, referees and colleagues
  • Enjoying interdisciplinary collaboration in a growing team

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Works in one of the leading private hospital groups for dermatology and aesthetic and plastic surgery in Switzerland
  • Collegial exchange in a medical team of around 30 specialists and around 110 employees
  • Modern infrastructure and broad range of treatment with around 700 patients every day
  • Possibility for use at several locations in German-speaking Switzerland
  • Appreciative corporate culture with the aspiration to grow together
