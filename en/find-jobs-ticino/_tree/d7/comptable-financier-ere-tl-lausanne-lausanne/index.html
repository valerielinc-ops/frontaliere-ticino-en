Full description

As a reference player in public transport, they transport each customer by making the heart of the Lausanne region beat by a commitment to simple, innovative and sustainable mobility.

To strengthen our teams, and also contribute to this experience, join us.

Become our future

Financial Accountant

Your Mission

Attached to the Head of Accounting, within the Finance and Procurement Unit, you contribute to the maintenance and reliability of the financial accounting of the company.

In particular, you are responsible for the accounting of capital assets, while participating in the various general accounting activities.

As such, you will have the following tasks

  • ensure the accounting management of fixed assets, from activation to exit
  • ensuring correct accounting and compliance of fixed assets
  • monitor assets in collaboration with relevant officials and carry out the necessary checks
  • participate in the accounting monitoring of investments and contribute to the work related to investment control
  • contribute to general accounting activities, including accounting records, reconciliations and audits
  • actively participate in monthly, quarterly and annual loops
  • prepare the necessary elements for the revision work and respond to the requests of the auditors
  • contribute to continuous improvement of accounting processes and controls

Your profile

  • You benefit from the Federal Patent of Specialist in Finance and Accounting, or from training deemed equivalent.
  • You have several years of experience in financial accounting, ideally in a large company.
  • You have a good knowledge of accounting for capital assets and investments.
  • You are familiar with general accounting principles and have good knowledge of VAT.
  • You are comfortable with computer and ERP tools; a good mastery of SAP FI/CO is a real asset.
  • Rigourous and organized, you are able to work independently while enjoying teamwork.
  • Your spirit of analysis and your ability to work with different interlocutors allow you to ensure reliable follow-up of the files entrusted to you.
