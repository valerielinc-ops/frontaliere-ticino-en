Full description

Are you enthusiastic about real estate, are you advising customers on an equal footing and would like to actively accompany the sale of apartments and residential buildings? Then you can become part of our fast-growing team.

  • You accompany owners in the sale of their residential properties from the first consultation to the conclusion of the contract.
  • You evaluate real estate, develop marketing strategies, coordinate visits and negotiate with interested parties.
  • You work closely together with our specialists from the fields of pension, financial and real estate consulting.
  • You represent our company professionally and contribute to the growth of our business.
  • You start in the marketing team in Zurich and work your way there for several months.
  • Ideally, you have the real estate marketing card and bring experience in marketing or selling real estate.
  • You know the regional real estate market and maintain a valuable network.
  • You convince in the advice and sales with competence and safe appearance.
  • You work independently, structured and with a lot of initiative.
  • You communicate stylishly in German; for the Lausanne region additionally in French.
  • A business model with great growth potential that opens up many opportunities.
  • A fixed annual wage without being dependent on commissions
  • A dynamic and established [SM1] team to count on.
  • An uncomplicated and open culture in which you can actively participate.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
