Who are we?

The main task of the Public Prosecutor ' s Office is to prosecute, in collaboration with the cantonal police, all criminal acts committed by adults. The Prosecutors shall also decide by order the criminal proceedings before them, subject to cases of removal or disqualification. Prosecutors are still carrying out the prosecution tasks before the law enforcement authority. The public prosecutor's office in the canton of Fribourg currently comprises 16 prosecutors and about 70 employees.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Managing the Prosecutor's agenda
  • Reception of telephone calls and contacts at the counter
  • Monitoring of criminal cases in the Tribuna V3 computer database
  • Management of criminal cases (preparation, establishment and maintenance of judicial records)
  • Establishment of contacts with the cantonal police and other authorities or procedural stakeholders
  • Mail management and typing on dictated or autonomous correspondence
  • Participation in hearings with the direct seizure of minutes
  • Preparation of applications and extracts of judgement for criminal record (VOSTRA system)

Profile desired

  • Business employee CFCs or training deemed equivalent
  • Experience in using MS environment IT tools (Word, Outlook, Teams, etc.), mastering the Tribuna V3 business application is an asset
  • Stress resistance and ability to cope with large workloads
  • Professional experience useful in the position, ideally with knowledge of the criminal field
  • Flexibility, responsibility and teamwork
  • Mastery of the French language (written and oral) with good knowledge of German
