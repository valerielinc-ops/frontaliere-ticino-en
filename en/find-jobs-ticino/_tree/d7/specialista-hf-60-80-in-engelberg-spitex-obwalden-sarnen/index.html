Nurse HF 60-80% in Engelberg On behalf of the municipalities, Spitex Obwalden provides comprehensive Spitex services throughout the Canton of Obwalden. We offer professional support in the areas of care, psychiatric care, housekeeping and maternal and paternal counselling – competent, reliable and with heart. Spitex Obwalden is also an attractive training company and invests in the specialists of tomorrow. Do you like working independently and enjoying outpatient care? Then you are exactly right with us. Your tasks

  • Taking on nursing and administrative tasks
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with employees, specialist offices and doctors
  • Project work and assumption of additional tasks Your profile
  • Training as a nurse HF
  • Outpatient professional experience is beneficial
  • Appreciative communication culture and mastery of the German language in words and writing
  • interRai HC course or willingness to complete it We offer
