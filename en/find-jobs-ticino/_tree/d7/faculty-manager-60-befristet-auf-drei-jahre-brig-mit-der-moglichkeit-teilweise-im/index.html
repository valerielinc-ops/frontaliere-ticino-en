It combines flexible, location-independent distance learning part-time with an active, interdisciplinary research culture at Campus Brig. In five faculties – Law, Psychology, Economics, History and Mathematics & Computer Science – we have been shaping modern teaching, learning and research for more than 30 years and count over 2,700 students and around 550 employees. To strengthen our service Faculty Services, we are looking for a dedicated personality as: Faculty Manager (60%), limited to three years Place of work: Brig, with the possibility to work partially in the home office Start of work : 04.01.2027 The Faculty Services are part of the Academic Services of the FernUni Switzerland. They help faculties achieve excellence in distance learning and research. The person hired as Faculty Manager will belong to the Faculty Services and work closely with the Dean's Office of the Faculty of Law as well as with the other faculties in cross-faculty tasks. Their tasks

  • Interface function between the Dean of the Faculty of Law, the Vice-Deans, the Head of the Bachelor of Law (DE and FR) and the Master of Law (DE and FR), the internal professors, the lecturers, the Faculty Services as well as the other academic and central administrative services and ensure smooth cooperation.
  • Participation in the further development of the Faculty of Law and its degree programmes Bachelor of Law (DE and FR) and Master of Law (DE and FR).
  • Accompanying the teaching teams.
  • Support of the Dean and the Law Faculty in academic issues as well as responsibility for the implementation of the decisions taken.
  • Participation in the communication strategy of the Faculty of Law.
  • Supervision of the websites of the faculties of the FernUni Switzerland and their social media channels as well as creation of content.
  • Support in the planning and organization of events of the faculties of the FernUni Switzerland.
  • Participation in the implementation of the internationalization strategy of the Faculty of Law.
  • Active participation in internal and cross-faculty projects.

Your profile

  • Master's degree in law or a related discipline.
  • Experience in an academic service of a university or college of applied sciences is beneficial.
  • Experience in science communication.
  • Very good user knowledge of common IT applications.
  • Engaged, organizationally strong and service-oriented personality with high flexibility and openness to other disciplines.
  • Strong social skills and teamwork.
  • German or French mother tongue with the ability to work safely and skillfully in the other language.
  • Very good knowledge of English.

We offer

  • Attractive employment conditions: You benefit from contemporary working conditions, generous social benefits and a wide range of training opportunities.
  • Dedicated team: You become part of a qualified team that actively lives innovation, quality and collaboration.
  • Inspiring working environment: Our campus in Brig is located in close proximity to the old town, the SBB railway station and attractive natural and mountain regions. It is a place of academic exchange with high quality of work.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
