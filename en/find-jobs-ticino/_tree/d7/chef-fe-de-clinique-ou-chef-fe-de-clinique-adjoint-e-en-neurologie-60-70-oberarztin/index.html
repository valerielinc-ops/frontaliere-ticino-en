Full description

In order to ensure the quality of outpatient and inpatient care and to ensure participation in undergraduate and postgraduate training in neurology (category C clinic), the Medical Directorate of the hôpital fribourgeois is seeking, for the Department of Neurology of HFR Fribourg – Hôpital cantonal, a

Senior physician or deputy senior physician in neurology 60 - 70 %

Your responsibilities

  • contribute to high-quality care for patients in the stroke unit and inpatient units
  • participate in outpatient consultations
  • support, where possible, the neurophysiological activities (in particular Doppler and/or ENMG) in inpatient and outpatient consultations
  • participate in on-call duty
  • supervise assistant physicians and participate in their training
  • participate in multicentre or locally developed clinical studies
  • participate in teaching students at the University of Fribourg

Your profile

  • holder of an FMH specialist qualification in neurology (or equivalent) or in the final year of training as a neurologist
  • at least 3 years of experience in neurology in a category A clinic
  • ideally, an SSNC certificate or equivalent for Doppler, ENMG, and/or EEG
  • proficiency in French (written and spoken) and German (spoken)
  • dynamic, open and collegial personality
  • interest in general neurology, teaching and clinical research

Start date: 1st November 2026

Further information: PD Dr A. Humm, Chief Physician, Department of Neurology, HFR Fribourg – Hôpital cantonal, Tel. +41 026 306 22 35, PD Dr F. Medlin, Deputy Physician, Department of Neurology, HFR Fribourg – Hôpital cantonal, Tel. +41 026 306 22 37
