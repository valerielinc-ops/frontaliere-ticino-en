Full description

As one of six Migros Partner branches in the area of the cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, we provide everything for the daily needs of our customers. Have we aroused your interest? Then send us your complete application documents by post or e-mail. Applications from Germany please only via e-mail. Further information can be found at www.rupfag.ch under the heading Jobs.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland, Migros Partner Ramsen, Petersburg 416, 8262 Ramsen

Important information

The position includes regular assignments on weekends and public holidays.

Mansioni

  • Customer service and advice
  • Presentation and disposition of goods
  • Filling and management of the Café Mojito area
  • Preparation of cold and warm cuisine etc.
  • Data and freshness control
  • Ordering and incoming goods control
  • Responsibility for order and cleanliness in the work area
  • Support of the post office and customer service, customer bills, telephone service, customer complaints

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in the field of gastronomy or sales advantageous
  • Completed basic or further education: in the field of gastronomy or sales of advantage
  • Self-employed, forward-looking and precise working methods
  • High flexibility, teamwork, reliability and loyalty
  • Willingness to intervene in the event of holiday absences or illness-related failures also at short notice
  • Customer-oriented and friendly appearance
  • Clean and well-groomed appearance
  • German (very good knowledge)
