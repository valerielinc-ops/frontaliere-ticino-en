Full description

Are you excited about daily contact with people, are you a communicative and helpful person? Then a training in the Contact Center of the Post is exactly what it does for you! In the three years of basic training you will acquire the necessary tools to successfully enter the world of communication.

Your training

  • You will be the first contact point for all the written and telephone questions of our customers.
  • In the Contact Center of the Post Office you will advise customers, you will help them solve problems and you will respond competently to their questions.
  • You will work in a versatile and flexible way on different channels of information and communication.
  • You will gain a general view of the various Post Office units and learn about the work processes, products and services.
  • After the introduction phase, you will soon be able to provide accurate information in another national language (work announcement in Schaffhausen: after the introduction phase, you will soon be able to provide accurate information in English).

Your skills

We are looking for young people who are enthusiastic, positive and motivated to make a difference with their actions and meet the following requirements:

  • have concluded the upper or middle level of the compulsory school with a good or very good performance
  • you like contact with people
  • wish to perfect your language knowledge
  • the speed of understanding and motivation to learn new things distinguish you
  • you are a communicative, reliable and discreet person

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
