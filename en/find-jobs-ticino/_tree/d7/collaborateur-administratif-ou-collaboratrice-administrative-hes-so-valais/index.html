Full description

To strengthen its team, the Energy and Environment Institute is putting the following position in the competition:

Administrative contributor (50% - 60%)

Your Mission

  • Support the head of institute in administrative and organisational tasks related to the Institute's missions
  • Participate in the general tasks of the Institute secretariat
  • Participate in organizing Institute events
  • Collaborate and provide administrative support to project managers
  • Participate in updating the Institute's website
  • Manage logistics (office equipment, reservation and room preparation, equipment order, etc.)

Your profile

  • Commercial professional maturity or equivalent
  • Positive, dynamic, empathic personality with strong customer orientation
  • Ability to work independently and as a team with a strong sense of organization, rigour and discretion
  • Very good editorial skills
  • Very good environmental control MS-Office
  • Proven professional experience in an academic environment would be an asset
  • French or German mother tongue with excellent knowledge of the other language and English (spoken and written)

Activity rate 50% - 60%

Place of work Sion

Entry into office 1 December 2026 or to be agreed
