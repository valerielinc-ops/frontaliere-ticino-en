Full description

Portrait

  • We are one of four semi-autonomous quarter schools in Ostermundigen.
  • At our school, around 70 teachers teach about 350 children and young people from kindergarten to 6th grade.

The school is centrally located in Ostermundigen on Bernstrasse 60. Our school has different class types: for example, we run a KbF, an intensive German course as well as classes of the special elementary school. We also have a day school. We attach great importance to integration. Sorry!

Tasks

  • You will take over from 01.03.2027 to 08.04.2027 the deputy in a 6th class.
  • You teach 23 lessons and take on the role of class teacher.
  • Subjects: German (3L), French (3L), Mathematics (3L), Sport (3L), Technical Design (3L), NMG (5L), Music (2L), MI (1L)
  • During 5 lessons you will be supported by an experienced healing teacher.
  • Working days: Monday - Thursday

You guide and accompany the students in their learning and development. You maintain the exchange in the class team.

Requirements

  • You have a cycle 2 teaching qualification or are in training.
  • You have joy and interest in school children from different cultures.
  • You are creative, patient and resilient.

We offer

  • We offer a good introduction in advance.
  • We are a competent, committed and helpful team.
  • We maintain good cooperation within the stage.
  • We have an attractive learning and working place with good infrastructure.
  • We can do well with public transport.
