Full description

138014 | 80 - 100% | Oerlikon, Zurich

Are you fascinated by the connection between self-employed sales, well-founded clarifications and contact with different people? Would you like to use your experience in property and liability insurance to find fair and sustainable solutions for our customers even in demanding situations? Then we should get to know each other.

As a claims inspector at Zurich Switzerland, you strengthen the Claims business unit in the area of property and detention, team inspectors, and handle complex claims cases in property and general liability insurance in the Zurich and Aargau regions. You accompany the cases from the first clarification to the conclusion, assess coverage, liability and damages and negotiate with the parties involved. You are regularly on site, make decisions independently and ensure careful, efficient and customer-oriented claims processing.

This is what awaits you

  • You handle complex claims in property and general liability insurance independently – from claims notification to conclusion.
  • You clarify matters with insurance companies, injured parties, experts, legal representatives and insurance companies and visit damage locations in the Zurich and Aargau region.
  • You assess insurance coverage, liability and damages and make decisions within your competences.
  • You negotiate with the parties involved, coordinate the necessary measures and initiate damage payments.
  • You will examine opportunities for recourse and work closely with internal specialist offices and external partners.

This sets you apart

  • insurance training; a professional card insurance or a degree from a high school insurance is an advantage.
  • At least five years of professional experience in the processing of claims, ideally in property and/or general liability insurance.
  • In-depth knowledge of coverage and liability assessment as well as experience in dealing with complex claims cases.
  • Secure negotiating skills, convincing behavior even in demanding situations and a pragmatic and customer-oriented solution finding.
  • Independent, structured and reliable working methods, very good knowledge of German and good MS-Office skills
  • Further language skills are advantageous.
