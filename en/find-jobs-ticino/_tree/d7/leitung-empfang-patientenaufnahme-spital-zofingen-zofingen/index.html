Full description

Their tasks

  • Personnel, organizational and professional leadership as well as further development of a team of around 15 employees
  • First point of contact for patients, relatives, visitors and external stakeholders
  • Ensuring a professional and patient-oriented reception as well as the administrative entry of inpatient and outpatient patients including checking the guarantors
  • Ensuring professional telephone information on the main telephone number
  • Clarification of the insurance coverage of patients
  • Ensuring correct and courteous communication with both internal and external stakeholders and promoting interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Responsibility for personnel planning and resource management as well as ensuring efficient shift operations
  • Active participation in day-to-day business and projects taking into account operational requirements in 24/7 operation
  • Optimization and further development of processes, processes and interfaces with clinical and administrative areas
  • Analysis and further development of relevant key figures as well as derivation of measures to improve service quality and process efficiency
  • Responsibility for the preparation, compliance and further development of internal and external guidelines, instructions and guidelines

Your profile

  • Commercial, medical or comparable basic training with appropriate professional experience in health care or in a service-oriented environment
  • Several years of leadership experience, ideally in patient administration, reception or in a comparable field
  • Integrative leader with high team orientation, social competence and strong communication skills
  • Experience in the management and organization of employees in shift operations as well as in the management of complex operational processes is an advantage
  • Entrepreneurial and analytical thinking as well as acting and an independent, structured as well as exact working method
  • Enjoying the further development of processes, changes and cooperation with different professional groups
  • Strong decision-making, conflict-solving and problem-solving skills as well as the ability to maintain an overview and set priorities even in demanding situations
  • Knowledge of systems such as Opale, KISIM and the PEP human resource planning tool is beneficial
  • Willingness to familiarize yourself with new systems and processes
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, ideally supplemented by further knowledge of foreign languages
  • Flexible, resilient as well as solution-oriented personality with a pronounced customer and service orientation and a high sense of responsibility

Applications from recruiters may not be considered for this post. Personal, Family, Competent In the regional and supra-regional health network, the Zofingen Hospital plays an important role. With more than 750 employees and various partner organizations, we provide medical care for the population and have also specialized in high-quality old-age medicine. Livia Lampert, Head of Services and Facility Management
