Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our more than 18,000 employees are shaping the future of process automation. Whether in developing and implementing new technologies, collaborating on innovative devices or fostering important relationships with global industries, our work is based on trust and shared success. As a family-run company, we think long-term and create prospects for our employees, our customers and society.

We are looking for a Data Engineer (m/f/d) to strengthen our team in Reinach and help shape the reliable, future-proof provision and use of data at Endress+Hauser Flow. At our site, we develop and produce innovative flow measurement technology for demanding applications in the process industry.

What is this point about?

Are you excited about modern data architectures? Do you want to make data from different sources reliably available and usable? Do you enjoy working with different departments and a global data community? In this role, you actively shape our data landscape and create a reliable basis for data-based decisions within our organization.

What tasks will you take on?

They shall

  • planning, designing and implementing the provision of data
  • Monitor, optimize and reliably ensure the operation of our data warehouse
  • Integrating new data sources into the existing data landscape
  • Coordinate requirements with data controllers and specialist areas and develop sustainable data and integration solutions
  • Collaborate with the group-wide data community and promote exchange on standards and new solutions

What do we expect from you?

They

  • have a degree or training in data engineering, data warehousing or in a comparable field of study
  • brings in-depth experience in the analysis and modeling of complex data structures, in particular with Data Vault, as well as with cloud data infrastructures
  • have practical experience with several of the following technologies: Snowflake, dbt, SQL, Git, Apache Kafka, Azure DevOps and Power BI
  • communicate in German and English at least at level B2, ideally at level C1
  • work independently and pragmatically and bring your ideas openly into the cooperation with different stakeholders
  • are enthusiastic about data topics and want to actively shape modern data solutions

What can you expect from us?

  • We are a family-run company with great commitment and a sustainable vision.
  • We promote lifelong learning through a wide range of education and training opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning.
  • We offer you a versatile area of responsibility in which you can take responsibility and actively shape our data landscape.
  • We create a collegial working environment in which knowledge is shared and cooperation is promoted across disciplines and national borders.
  • We support a balanced work-life balance through flexible working conditions.
  • We offer you a modern working environment at our location in Reinach.

How do you apply?

To apply, we ask you to upload your documents to our career page. Applications that reach us by e-mail cannot be considered for data protection reasons.

Agencies, personnel service providers or similar companies are asked to refrain from the unsolicited transmission of profiles and requests for possible cooperation. We do not currently work with external service providers for this position. All unsolicited applications from service providers are not considered and deleted without further examination. Thank you for your understanding.
