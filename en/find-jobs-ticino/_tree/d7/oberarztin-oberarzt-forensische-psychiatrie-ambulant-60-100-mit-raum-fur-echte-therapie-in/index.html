You are interested in the person behind the crime and in the question of what really helps him? At the same time, should your private life not be neglected? Here you work as a senior physician from 60%, on an outpatient basis and without a closed station. This leaves time for your patients and for yourself. The forensic psychiatry at this university psychiatric center is its own university hospital and covers the entire spectrum, from the acute area to the penal system to the assessment. At the center of this position is a large forensic ambulatorium in the city of Bern, supplemented by a forensic dormitory. Anyone who feels like variety can also rotate into other areas of the clinic.

Their tasks

  • Medical responsibility in the forensic ambulatory and dormitory
  • Treatment of patients with court-ordered outpatient measures, comparable to the enforcement of measures in Germany, as well as of voluntary patients with forensically relevant disorders
  • Independent interventional therapies accompanied by forensic supervision
  • Criminal and civil reports, only if you want
  • Primary psychiatric care in prison facilities, as a small part of the workload and alternately with the team
  • Participation in general psychiatric background service

Your Competences

  • Specialist title in Psychiatry and Psychotherapy
  • Is your title from Germany? The MEBEKO usually recognizes EU title, and Medimeo accompanies You through the procedure
  • Interested in forensic psychiatry. Experience, for example from the implementation of the dimensional rules, is a plus, but not a condition
  • Structured functioning and security in exchange with the judiciary, authorities and specialist bodies
  • Very good knowledge of German (C1)

Their advantages

  • University environment with teaching, research and support for further education and training
  • 16 weeks maternity leave and 15 days paternity leave, both paid
  • Living in Bern: a capital with old town, Aare and the Alps in sight

Your change with Medimeo
