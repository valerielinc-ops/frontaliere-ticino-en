Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. For the Senevita Pilatusblick in Ebikon we are looking for an immediately or by agreement Professional / Health EFZ (m/f/i) on night duty 50-100 % Your important contribution
