Full description

For the station of geriatric rehabilitation and transitional care with 27 beds, we are looking for a qualified nurse HF.

Tasks

  • Care and care of our patients and their environment according to defined quality criteria / guidelines / standards
  • Planning, control and evaluation of all partial steps in the care process from entry to exit
  • Responsibility for professional communication with patients and interprofessional services
  • Co-responsibility for the training "Fachmann/frau Gesundheit EFZ" and "Farmer/frau HF"
  • Leading a sector with an assigned patient group

Requirements

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nurse HF/FH or a foreign diploma with SRK recognition
  • Do you have experience in rehabilitation? Perfect – this competence is of great value to us
  • A few years of professional experience in Switzerland, regarding the care process, are beneficial
  • A strong sense of responsibility
  • Flexibility and resilience

We offer

Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well.

The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
