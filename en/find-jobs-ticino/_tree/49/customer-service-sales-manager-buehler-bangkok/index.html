Customer Service Sales Manager — Bühler Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Sales-driven Customer Service professional to grow Bühler's after-sales business in Thailand for Human and Animal Nutrition. This role will drive service sales, develop customer relationships, and convert opportunities into orders, working closely with the sales and service teams.

Aufgaben

  • Achieve sales targets (orders, revenue, margin, pipeline).
  • Identify, develop, and close Customer Service opportunities.
  • Grow service business across spare parts, service agreements, retrofits, upgrades, and field services.
  • Build strong relationships with customers and understand their operational needs.
  • Translate customer challenges (e.g., downtime, efficiency, quality, cost) into solutions.
  • Manage sales pipeline, quotations, and forecasts using CRM tools.
  • Drive recurring revenue through service contracts and long-term agreements.
  • Collaborate with internal teams to deliver competitive proposals.
  • Provide market insights on customer needs, competitors, and pricing.

Anforderungen

  • Degree/Diploma in Engineering, Business, or related field.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in service, aftermarket, or technical sales.
  • Proven track record in sales and customer relationship management.
  • Experience in machinery, spare parts, or industrial solutions preferred.
  • Fluent in Thai and English.
  • Familiarity with CRM and digital/AI sales tools is a plus.
  • Knowledge of food/feed processing industries is an advantage.

Two billion people eat food every day that was produced with Bühler equipment. One billion people drive vehicles whose parts were manufactured with our machines. Bühler aims to balance humanity, nature, and the economy in every decision as it develops solutions that unlock sustainable business opportunities in the global food, feed, and mobility industries. We strive to create innovations for a better world, with a special focus on healthy, safe, and sustainable solutions. Therefore, we team up with customers, start-ups, multinationals, and academia to accelerate impact together.
