Full description

Ready to reveal your potential? At Straumann Group, we are experiencing an exciting adventure, driven by growth, innovation and our desire to have a positive impact. Our mission is to improve oral health and transform the lives of millions of people around the world. United by this ambition, we give every day the best of ourselves, in a culture of high performance that encourages collaboration, curiosity and the desire to learn. With us, you have the opportunity to take charge of your career, value your skills and continue to evolve through your commitment and desire to progress. Together, we not only help create new smiles: we reveal the potential of everyone, including yours. Your next opportunity Do you want to combine proximity management, operational excellence and continuous improvement in a demanding industrial environment? Join our site at Straumann Villeret as the Leader Production and contribute directly to the manufacture of medical devices that make the difference. Attached to the UAP Manager, you take responsibility for a production team and ensure that you achieve safety, quality, delivery and performance objectives.

  • Your mission: create the conditions for your team to succeed, develop the skills of your employees and make lasting progress in our production processes. Your role On a daily basis, you will be asked to:
  • Pilot your production team by organising activities, setting priorities and ensuring optimal use of available resources.
  • Ensure a safe working environment by promoting risk prevention and compliance with health, safety and environmental standards.
  • Ensure compliance of medical devices manufactured in accordance with internal procedures and applicable regulatory requirements, including ISO 13485 and FDA.
  • To achieve production objectives by reconciling quality, timeliness, expected volumes and cost control.
  • Support the development of your employees, identifying their skills, training needs and opportunities for progress.
  • Promote your team's performance through relevant indicators, management routines and adapted action plans.
  • Deploy a culture of continuous improvement, encouraging field initiatives, Lean methods and collective problem solving.
  • Anticipate operational risks and collaborate with partner teams, including quality assurance, to deliver sustainable solutions.
  • Your profile Essential Skills for Success:
  • You have an CFC or equivalent qualification, supplemented by training in industrial team management, or have an equivalent professional background.
  • You have experience in coaching and coaching production teams.
  • You master problem solving methods and know how to use data to make decisions.
  • You are comfortable with computer tools and management systems used in an industrial environment.
  • You know how to organize priorities, coordinate resources and monitor performance indicators. Additional assets:
  • Knowledge of the quality and regulatory requirements for the manufacture of medical devices.
  • Experience with Lean approaches and continuous improvement tools. All qualified practitioners will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, or disability
