Full description

Far-sightedness for rehab and my career – that is the lifestyle and working feeling at the clinic group Valens. Exchange, progress, interdisciplinary teamwork and good prospects are important here.

Our new Zurich Triemli rehabilitation clinic will open in spring 2027. At the beginning we will operate a station with about 32 beds, rented in the city hospital Zurich Triemli. Our new building is already under construction and will open at the end of 2029 and have about 140 beds. For this exciting operational setup, we are looking for an agreement to strengthen our team. Tasks: • You plan, lead and evaluate movement and sports therapeutic interventions as part of outpatient cardiac rehabilitation independently • The professional design and management of heart groups taking into account the individual resilience as well as the cardiovascular risk profile of our patients, you take over independently • Endurance, strength and coordination trainings you perform, monitor and control individually based on clinical and performance physiological parameters • The control of stress-relevant parameters as well as the early detection of stress limits and warning symptoms are part of your everyday life and ensure a high level of patient safety • With your motivating and health-promoting advice, you support our patients in integrating an active lifestyle into their everyday lives. • Constructive and situational cooperation with the medical service, physiotherapy and other participating specialist areas is ensured • The professional documentation of the therapeutic measures you carry out conscientiously and actively participate in quality assurance and development processes • With your commitment, you actively contribute to the conceptual development of our outpatient cardiac rehabilitation service. • The preparation and implementation of specialist lectures, patient trainings and educational group offers are also part of your versatile field of work. Requirements • You have successfully completed studies in sports and movement sciences, sports therapy or a comparable qualification • The training as cardiac and vascular therapist, i.e. You have successfully completed cardiac and vascular therapist SCPRS and thus meet a mandatory requirement for this position • Ideally, you could already gain experience in carrying out outpatient cardiac rehabilitation programs • A patient-centered and evidence-based working method is a matter of course for you • With your strong communication skills and your team orientation, you contribute to a successful interprofessional cooperation. • Experience in spiroergometry is an advantage and rounds off your professional profile • You have very good knowledge of German in word and writing; English skills are beneficial • Commitment, a sense of responsibility and a high level of social competence distinguish you and enable you to treat our patients in an appreciative way. We offer: • Prospective and easy to reach workplace – in a collegial environment with clear structures, competent managers and appreciative communication • Real benefits instead of empty words – with paid changing time, 28 days of holidays as well as numerous internal and external discounts and offers. Convince yourself • Further education that advances you – internal and external training, including through our Rehab Academy, the group-wide training center • Diversity as strength – We rely on thinking, self-confident and critical employees with different experiences, qualifications and perspectives, because we are convinced that this will bring us all forward. With 13 locations and nine specialist areas, the Valens Clinical Group is the largest rehabilitation provider in Switzerland. As carriers of the labels "Friendly Work Space" and "Best Employers Switzerland", we support our 2300 employees with their work-life balance and cultivate a culture of cooperation.
