Full description

Newly created, the SSR staff field has been responsible since 1 April 2026 for the uniform management of all SSR staff tasks, in collaboration with the various fields and regions of the company. This includes SSR conduct processes, business development, process optimization, legal issues, and management of SSR partners.

Your Mission

As a member of the Chancellery Department (Central Services Team), you have the following responsibilities:

  • Strategic implementation and coordination of the management of relations with authorities, including follow-up on important regulatory files (e.g.: new SSR concession)
  • Advice and support to the Chiefs of Staff of the members of the Directorate on matters of content, design, coordination and form (including replacement in case of absence)
  • Participation in planning and organizing Executive Committee meetings, including document verification
  • Developing challenging concepts and mandates for cross-cutting themes and projects
  • Project management and execution of ad hoc tasks under the mandate of the General Staff
  • Active participation through capacity to anticipate, early identification of needs for action and proactive support for staff work

Your profile

  • A university degree (e.g. master's degree) is an asset; an equivalent qualification acquired through a solid work experience of several years in a position similar to that of a staff or in a strategic function is also welcome.
  • Great vivacity of mind, conceptual spirit, high degree of self-organization and sense of service in performing tasks
  • Structured, time-sensitive and solutions-oriented work method
  • Demonstrated ability to perform tasks in an effective and targeted manner, even in complex situations (hand-on)
  • Very good knowledge of French or German with good knowledge of the other language; knowledge of Italian is an asset
  • Desired experience in a function similar to that of a Staff
  • Expertise in relations with authorities and in regulatory and policy issues is an asset

What we offer you

  • An interesting key role within the SSR Staff and close to the Group's Directorate
  • Collaboration with SSR's various areas of activity and an overview of key strategic issues
  • Many opportunities to participate and get involved in a newly created team of highly experienced specialists.
  • A hybrid work model with flexible schedules and the possibility of teleworking

Please submit your application exclusively via the SSR Job Opportunities Portal. The deadline for applications is 18 November 2026.

Our promise

Reaching the entire Swiss population with high quality formats requires expertise, enthusiasm and a stimulating and creative environment, in which everyone can invest and evolve. For the best to give the best of themselves, they need to have a source of inspiration: Welcome to the world of SSR!

  • Create Content: Quality journalism is the heart of our activity and passion. We enjoy complete freedom of writing, encourage creativity and grant autonomy that promotes novelty and quality.
  • Living digital: In our society, quality journalism can also take a digital form. This is why we are exploring new opportunities and experimenting with new formats and practices: digital, interactive and cross-media. Our content fits into the air of time and responds to public concerns.
  • Human being: We maintain informal relations, work independently and contribute to a good working atmosphere. Our success is based on the strengths of everyone and on the technical skills of all our employees.
  • Make sense: With our program, we reach all of Switzerland. We act independently and respectfully to encourage the formation of opinion and strengthen cultural diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
