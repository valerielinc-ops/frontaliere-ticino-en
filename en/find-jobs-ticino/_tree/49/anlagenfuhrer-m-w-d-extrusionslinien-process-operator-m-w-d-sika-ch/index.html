About the role After successful training (approximately 1.5 years), the plant manager extrusion lines 100% (m/f/d) is responsible for:

  • Installation, operation and monitoring of extrusion plants
  • Technical management and support of assigned staff within the shift
  • Independent troubleshooting and immediate action
  • Timely and quality-compliant implementation of order planning
  • Active cooperation in production trials
  • Careful documentation of product quality and feedback of process orders (SAP)
  • Maintenance and cleaning work
  • Ensuring order and cleanliness in the plant area
  • Training and instruction of new employees

Your skills and experience

  • Readiness for use in 3 to 4 shift operation
  • Very good social skills and ability to guide employees professionally
  • Strong safety and environmental awareness
  • Motivation to learn new
  • Very good technical understanding and a flair for numbers
  • High reliability, flexibility and load capacity
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (min. B2)
  • Professional experience in a craft environment

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 103 countries, produces in more than 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025. At Sika Manufacturing AG in Sarnen, we produce high-quality plastic membranes for roof, tunnel and structural sealing. The plant operator (M/F) has a key function in the production process and together with his team is responsible for ensuring a high quality and performance standard.
