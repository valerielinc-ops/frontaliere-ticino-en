The position yesAre you looking for a diverse and future-oriented course of study? Then reinvent the future of healthcare together with us. At Roche, we develop innovative diagnostics and medicines to enable people to live longer, better lives. Become part of this mission and make a crucial contribution to patients worldwide right from the start.

What tasks await you?

  • You form the direct bridge between the departments and IT and coordinate exciting joint projects.
  • You develop smart IT solutions from the initial idea through programming to implementation using agile methods such as Scrum.
  • You actively optimize business processes and control processes – be it in logistics, development, production or controlling.
  • You will actively participate in exciting data and IT projects and contribute to digital transformation by using innovative approaches.
  • You gain practical experience in one of the various company areas, such as finance & controlling, development, production, human resources or site management.

You will spend your lecture-free time and the practical semester at Roche Diagnostics GmbH in Penzberg. You will also do your bachelor’s thesis in our company. The lectures take place at the Technical University in Rosenheim.

Your advantages with us

  • Finances: Attractive compensation.
  • Work-life balance: proportional vacation during the practical phases, company sports and comprehensive health care.
  • Extras: International working environment, discounted canteen meals.

What requirements do you have to meet?

  • General university entrance qualification
  • Subject-specific university entrance qualification
  • English level B2

How long does your training last? A total of 3.5 years (or 7 semesters). This is preceded by an approximately one-month, company-specific onboarding internship so that you get off to the perfect start.
