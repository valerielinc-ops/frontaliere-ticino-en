Full description

The role involves fabricating and installing piping systems in the workshop and on construction sites. The company offers modern infrastructure and diverse continuing education opportunities in welding. You bring practical experience and take on varied

  • Tasks. For our client, we are looking for a/an activities
  • Manufacture and pre-assemble pipework in the workshop
  • Install piping systems on construction sites and at customers' premises
  • Perform welding work such as TIG or MIG/MAG
  • Putting technical drawings and plans into practice
  • Maintain, repair and service pipeline systems
  • Requirements

  • Experience in pipeline construction and assembly
  • Solid knowledge of welding
  • Willingness to undertake assembly work involving possible travel

  • Modern infrastructure
