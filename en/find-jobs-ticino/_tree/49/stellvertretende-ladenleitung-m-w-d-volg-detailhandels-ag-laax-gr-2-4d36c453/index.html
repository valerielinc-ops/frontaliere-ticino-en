Full description

The branch in 7032 Laax Signina GR is looking for a responsible and motivated personality by immediately or by agreement.

Deputy charging line (m/f/d)

You are looking forward to these tasks

  • Friendly and courteous advice to our customers
  • Management of shop staff in the absence of shop management
  • Ensuring a conceptual and attractive presentation of goods
  • Ensuring assortment orders and availability of goods
  • Professional handling of fresh products (fruits, vegetables, etc.)
  • Cash register operation
  • Completion of various administrative activities

We are looking forward to this profile

  • Completed retail training with EGZ or equivalent
  • Several years of professional experience in sales or a similar environment (preferably food retailing)
  • Enjoyment of active customer contact
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Readiness for flexible deployment times and occasional assistance at other points of sale
  • Independent and reliable working method
  • High level of social and professional competence
