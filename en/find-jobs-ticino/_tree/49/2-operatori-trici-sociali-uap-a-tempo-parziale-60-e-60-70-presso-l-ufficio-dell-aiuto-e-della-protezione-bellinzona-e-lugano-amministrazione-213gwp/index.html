Full description

Tasks

  • provide advice to families, third persons, entities, proposing direct intervention or directing to external services, to ensure an answer to all requests
  • examine the needs of minors and their families to define the project of intervention
  • to provide social assistance, by activating, where appropriate, the resources necessary to ensure adequate support for minors and their families
  • perform the socio-family evaluations required by the protection and judicial authorities, to deepen situations of potential risk or danger for minors
  • take protection warrants pursuant to article 307 cpv. 3 of the CCS, to monitor uncomfortable situations, prevent the emergence of more important issues and provide support to families
  • to prepare, execute and verify the custody of minors with entrusting families and educational centers, to ensure their protection, examining the possibility of reconstructing the conditions of welcome of their families
  • collaborate with the other sectors of the Office to offer coordinated and synergistic interventions

Requirements

  • bachelor SUP in social work or equivalent title, or, if combined with experience in individual social taking, university title in related disciplines such as sociology, psychology, education science, pedagogy
  • proven experience gained in the protection of minors
  • availability, reliability, cooperative and welcoming attitude, communication and mediation skills
  • knowledge of services and authorities in Ticino
  • organizational capacity, work for objectives and networking
  • good mastery of the Italian language (conversation and editing) and good knowledge (conversation) of a second official language; knowledge (conversation) of the third official language is preferential title
  • practical knowledge of the main computer applications (office package)
  • licence to conduct cat. b

Special remarks

  • entry into service:
  • seat of Bellinzona 1 January 2027 or to be agreed
  • seat of Lugano 1 February 2027 or to be agreed
  • availability to work outside office hours

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), further documentation may be requested at a later stage (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
