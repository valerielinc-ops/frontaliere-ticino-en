Full description

Introduction

For our Finance division, we are looking for a (M/F) Cost Price Manager whose main mission will be to ensure the accounting valuation of the items.

Responsibilities

  • Valuing new items, in particular by calculating their cost price and precious material
  • Ensure the management and reliability of basic accounting data relating to our products
  • Recalculate annually the valuation data of the items and ensure their updating
  • Use extraction and control tools to analyse deviations
  • Participate in calculations and monitoring of new product development projects
  • Collaborate closely with the partners of the various production sites

Profile

  • Training in finance, accounting, management control or equivalent
  • An industrial experience would be appreciated
  • Good Mastery of Excel
  • Knowledge of SAP and Tableau, an asset
  • Good analysis
  • Ability to communicate, work in teams and cross-cutting
  • Rigoury, organized and versatile
  • Curiosity, openness and adaptability

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.
