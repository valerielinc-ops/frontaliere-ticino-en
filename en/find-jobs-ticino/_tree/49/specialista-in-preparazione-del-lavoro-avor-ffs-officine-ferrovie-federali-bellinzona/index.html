What we offer you.

You will find an innovative working environment where you will play a top role in major changes. A stimulating and varied work in the SBB Workshops in Bellinzona and in the future at the new railway factory in Castione, the centre of competence for the modernization, renewal and maintenance of rolling stock. We will help you familiarize yourself with a varied and interesting business. Discover also the many FFS benefits: family allowances, free AG, advantageous conditions for international travel and many other opportunities for your family. What you can move.

  • With your work you will contribute to the maintenance of the terms and quality of the work done, as well as the availability of the FFS fleet.
  • In the role of specialist in the preparation of work you will carry out the activities of preparation of work, definition/surveillance of prices and management of the working processes.
  • You will be responsible for the proper implementation of time and methods to ensure optimal production in terms of quality, time and costs.
  • Maintain effective, robust and current processes of your competence, implementing targeted and continuous improvements.
  • You'll interface with all the bodies you need to ensure the effective exchange of information.
  • You will then perform an exemplary function by constantly perfecting. You will be putting your colleagues in small and large multidisciplinary work groups.

The one you bring with you.

  • You are a dynamic person, open to new challenges, responsible, proactive and with good communication skills.
  • You are sensitive to safety and safety issues.
  • You have completed training at the SSST or equivalent level and basic training in the mechanical or electrotechnical field (AFC).
  • You have experience in the AVOR sector (Work preparation) and SAP knowledge.
  • You have Italian or German as a native language and good/good knowledge of the other language (level B2)
