Aufgaben

  • DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT - Creating and managing configurators for our machines, both locally and globally.
  • INNOVATION - Always aiming to design state-of-the-art configurators for our new machines, as well as keeping our existing configurators up-to-date and efficient.

Anforderungen

  • Understanding of manufacturing principles.
  • Fluent English (B2+), knowledge of Czech and German is a plus
  • Comfortable juggling multiple projects and meeting deadlines.
  • Good interpersonal, communication, and teamwork skills
  • Customer-focused with good judgment and decision-making abilities.
  • Bonus points for experience as a configurator builder, and knowledge of SAP/PLC, Windchill
