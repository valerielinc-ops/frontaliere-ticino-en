Qualifications | This is what you bring

  • Professional background: Successfully completed economic studies (or comparable) as well as a successfully completed tax consultant exam.
  • Many years of expertise: at least 8–10 years of in-depth work experience in tax law with a focus on sales and payroll tax, acquired in a tax consultancy, financial administration or industrial company.
  • Leadership competence: Demonstrable experience in the management of employees as well as in cooperation within a dynamic matrix organization.
  • Digital Affinity: Very good user knowledge in SAP (ERP/S4HANA) coupled with enthusiasm for digital control processes and IT tools.
