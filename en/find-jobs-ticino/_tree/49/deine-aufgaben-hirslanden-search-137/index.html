Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Klinik Beau-Site | Bern Occupation by: by immediate or by appointment

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Your Advantages in Us

  • A workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Remunerated changing time
  • Participation in mobility costs
  • Discount in the Salem Pharmacy
  • Participation in fitness subscription

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • You work in direct care in an emergency and are responsible for the professional, interdisciplinary and evidence-based care of patients.
  • Advising and coaching team members on care issues are among your core tasks, as well as promoting interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • You actively participate in higher-level nursing-specific working groups and projects and are responsible for monitoring and participating in the implementation of changes
  • In addition, you are involved in the introduction, training, implementation and evaluation of care guidelines, instructions and concepts.

Your profile

  • Dipl. Nursing professional (HF / FH) or NDS emergency care
  • Care-specific training at level CAS or DAS (or willingness to undertake such training)
  • Several years of professional experience as a Dipl. Nurse, preferably in acute hospital. Experience in emergency setting is beneficial.
  • In-depth competences in patient education, discussion and clinical decision-making
  • Quality-conscious, critical and reflected thinking as well as self-responsible and reliable action
  • The joy of taking responsibility

For more information, please contact Angela Mathys, Head of Emergency, at T, or Amina Prada, HR recruitment partner, at amina.prada@hirslanden.ch.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
