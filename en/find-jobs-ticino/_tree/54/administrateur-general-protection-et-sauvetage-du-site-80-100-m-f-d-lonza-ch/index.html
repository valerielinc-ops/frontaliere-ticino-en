Full description

Where necessary, eligible candidates and their families will be assisted in moving.

As Deputy Head Site Protection & Rescue, you take on a central management and control function within the Site Protection & Rescue organization. You are responsible for the operational management of the fire brigade, the training and further education of the emergency forces as well as the organization of the Picettdienst. In the event and emergency, you will lead missions as the overall mission manager and ensure safe, professional and efficient mission management at the site.

What we offer you

  • An Agile Career and Dynamic Work Culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous benefits in the areas of lifestyle, family and leisure. The full list of our global services can also be found at: https://bit.ly/3wjkoFi

Your tasks

  • Planning, organisation and continuous development of the training and further education of the fire brigade as well as training of employees in the areas of firefighting, initial intervention, evacuation and self- and external protection.
  • Ensure compliance with legal, regulatory and internal training standards and complete documentation of qualifications and evidence of formal qualifications.
  • Planning and management of exercises, scenario training and major exercises as well as continuous development of training concepts.
  • conduct operations as Commander-in-Chief or in any other management capacity, and coordinate cooperation with internal and external operational organisations and authorities.
  • Participation in crisis and event organisation, implementation of mission analyses and derivation and implementation of improvement measures.
  • Responsibility for planning and securing the Picett service, coordinating resources and optimizing alerting and deployment processes.
  • Professional and disciplinary leadership of emergency responders and promotion of a performance-oriented security and leadership culture.
  • Support of the Head Site Protection & Rescue in personnel, organizational and strategic management tasks.
  • Responsibility for operational documentation, accounting, compensation of operational forces as well as support for budget planning, cost control and reporting.

Your profile

  • Completed professional training with a technical or chemical background.
  • Completed training as Fire Instructor/FKS.
  • Several years of professional experience in the chemical industry.
  • At least five years of experience as an officer/works or base fire brigade.
  • In-depth knowledge of operational, emergency and crisis management.
