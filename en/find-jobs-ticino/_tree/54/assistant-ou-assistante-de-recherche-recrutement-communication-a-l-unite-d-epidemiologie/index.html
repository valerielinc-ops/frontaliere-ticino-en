Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The position is attached to the Department of Community Medicine and First Recourse (DMCPR). This department combines a societal mission whose aim is to allow access to care for all and an academic mission whose aim is to make pre- and post-graduate education and research in first resort medicine. The first-recourse medical service provides outpatient care for adult patients in primary care. This service also provides outpatient emergency medical services to the population. As part of the HUG's general interest missions, it provides access to care for people who are disadvantaged by their age, their social, economic, legal status (asylum applicants, victims of non-entry, clandestines, prisoners), or their lifestyles

  • If necessary, the first resort medical service also ensures the continuity of their care.

Within the Department of Community Medicine and First Recourse (DMCPR), Primary Medicine Service (SMPR), the Population Epidemiology Unit (PIU) conducts large-scale epidemiological studies, including the Bus Santé study and the Specchio study ( www.specchio-hub.ch ), which aim to collect health indicators from the Geneva population. As part of several innovative research projects within the Population Epidemiology Unit, we are looking for a 100% research assistant to support recruitment, communication and coordination activities with participants.

Description of post

Within the Population Epidemiology Unit, you play a central role in the operational implementation of studies, with particular emphasis on the recruitment, support and follow-up of participants. You contribute to the design, implementation and monitoring of recruitment strategies. As such, you are regularly in direct contact with participants and the public, including via e-mail, telephone and field activities. You present the current studies in a clear and engaging manner, answer questions and accompany interested people in the different stages of their participation. You are actively involved in creating a positive and engaging experience for participants, from first contact to follow-up during the study. You help identify the most effective ways to improve the communication, recruitment and retention of participants, and propose adjustments where necessary. You are also involved in the design, writing and dissemination of communications materials related to unit projects: information materials, content for participants, recruitment materials, news or other communication tools. You ensure that you produce clear, accessible, attractive and tailored content for different audiences. In close collaboration with the scientific and operational team, you support the logistics organization of field activities: preparation of recruitment sessions, management and follow-up of contacts, coordination with participants and updating of monitoring tools. You also contribute to the continuous improvement of recruitment procedures and processes. Finally, you are involved in the writing and updating of research project documents, such as information documents, procedures, recruitment materials and activity reports. This position is thus at the interface between research, public health, recruitment and communication, and involves regular interactions with various audiences.

Qualifications

You have a Bachelor's degree or equivalent in social sciences, health, public health, communication or related fields. You have a strong interest in human contact and the recruitment of participants. You are comfortable getting in touch with people by phone, e-mail or face-to-face, presenting research studies and engaging them in a professional and respectful way. You have excellent relational qualities, a common sense of listening and a clear and appropriate communication for different interlocutors and interlocutors. You are an organized, rigorous and able person to monitor several activities and contacts in parallel. You have good writing skills in French and are comfortable writing content for the public, including studies, projects, publications and unit news. A good command of English is an asset. Initial experience in research, health, communication, recruitment, project coordination or in an activity involving regular contact with the public is desired. An advantage is an interest or experience in public health, epidemiology or clinical or population research. You are autonomous, proactive or proactive, dynamic and oriented or oriented solutions, while enjoying teamwork and collaboration in a multidisciplinary environment.
