ABOUT ZEGNA The global leader in luxury menswear, ZEGNA was founded in the Italian Alps in 1910. Part of the Ermenegildo Zegna Group, the brand remains true to the values of its visionary Founder, Ermenegildo Zegna, who believed in creating world-class fabrics in harmony with both nature and local communities - a philosophy embodied by Oasi Zegna, the home of our values. The inspiration behind the brand’s values, Oasi Zegna, continues to guide everything we do at ZEGNA - from supporting our people to fostering a culture of growth, where craftsmanship and innovation are core elements of our learning processes and long-term growth strategy. YOUR MISSION IN THIS ROLE The Zegna Brand HQ Controlling Intern will support the Zegna Brand HQ Controlling team in budgeting, forecasting, reporting, and cost monitoring . The role will contribute to the financial planning process for HQ Functions, ensuring accurate cost tracking, data consolidation, and analysis to support business decision-making. You’ll work closely with your Team to drive meaningful results and shape the way we work and grow. More specifically you will: Budget and cost control Support the budgeting / forecasting and actual processes for HQ Functions. Assist in the planning and monitoring of HQ function expenditures. Monitor budget performance and analyze variances against forecasts. Support month-end closing activities and cost reconciliations. Collaborate with Finance teams to ensure data accuracy and consistency. Verify the correct allocation of costs by cost center, project/WBS and account Data management Support the day-to-day activities. Prepare, update, and maintain Excel files and reporting templates. Upload, extract, and process data from company systems. Prepare ad-hoc financial reports and analytical schedules. Maintain well-organized supporting documentation and working files. WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR Person with strong analytical and numerical skills. Good English knowledge in writing and speaking Advanced Excel skills: management of large datasets and the proficiency use of pivot tables and advanced filtering and sorting Bachelor's or Master's degree (or currently pursuing) in Economics, Finance, Business Administration, or a related field. Previous internship experience in Finance, Accounting, or Controlling is preferred Strong interest in Finance, Controlling, and Financial Analysis. Basic knowledge of accounting principles, including: Cost accounts, Accrual and provision accounts, Cost allocation and financial reporting concepts Knowledge of SAP, Oracle, Power BI will be considered as a plus Excellent attention to detail for reconciliations and cross-checks Strong organizational skills and ability to meet deadlines JOINING ZEGNA Joining us means stepping into a world of excellence, where the uniqueness of our authenticity is rooted in the talents of our people, who together contribute to weaving a story of innovation and craftsmanship. You will become part of an ongoing journey of development, uncovering your talents while contributing to a proud legacy and a vibrant tomorrow.
