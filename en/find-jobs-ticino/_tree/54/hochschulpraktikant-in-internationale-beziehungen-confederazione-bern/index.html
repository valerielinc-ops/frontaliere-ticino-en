fedpol in action for Switzerland – anytime and anywhere

Fedpol controls national and international police cooperation. The International Police Cooperation Directorate (IPC) of fedpol ensures cooperation between the Confederation and cantons as well as with foreign partner authorities, operates the national contact points for the international search and information exchange (e.g. via INTERPOL, Europol and Schengen) and ensures police coordination in cross-border and international situations.
