Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Physiotherapeutic treatment and care of our inpatient and outpatient patients
  • Support of the student physiotherapists during their internship
  • Documentation management services
  • Active participation in the further development and quality assurance of the department

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Physiotherapist/RF/FH/BSc
  • In-depth expertise in orthopaedics, internal medicine, neurology, acute geriatrics, visceral and traumatological surgery and gynecology
  • Experience or Further training in the field of acute neurology and palliative medicine or geriatrics is advantageous
  • Organisational capacity, resilience and flexibility

Our offer to you

  • Future-oriented and innovative company
  • Interesting and challenging area of responsibility in which you can apply and expand your expertise
  • Active promotion of education and training
  • Employment according to cantonal guidelines
