Full description

Employee Spare Parts Warehouse Truck (a) From screws to the office – your knowledge remains in demand! You know trucks not only from seeing, but also know what happens under the hood? You have worked as a truck, agricultural machinery or car mechanic and would like to develop professionally? Or are you an experienced truck driver who knows the technology of his vehicles from the Effeff and is in the mood for a new challenge? Then your next step could lead from the workshop or the street to the office. To complement our team, we are looking for you - immediately or by agreement - as support for our truck spare parts warehouse (a). Task area

  • Management of the spare parts warehouse
  • Entry into and removal from storage as well as the management of the goods
  • Order picking and processing of workshop orders
  • Booking of all movements on service and repair orders
  • Supporting and advising workshop and paint shop staff

Requirement profile

  • trained lorry, agricultural machinery or car mechanic; or
  • Experienced and technology-savvy truck driver
  • In-depth knowledge of vehicle technology trucks
  • Resilience and flexibility
  • Teamplayer
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Offer

  • Brand diversity with varied activity
  • Always on the pulse of the latest technology and digitalization
  • Personal development opportunities in a versatile company
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Free employee parking

We offer you a varied and interesting job in a future-oriented team. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application. Christoph Jeannerat, Human Resources, telephone: +41 62 748 80 80.
