Full description

Workload: 100%

Location: Baden & Rathausen

Ready to keep Axpo's people up and running? Join our Group IT team and become the go-to person for fast, reliable IT support - we welcome your unique perspective and experience.

What you will do

  • Act as a point of contact for on-site and remote IT support, handling incidents, requests, and escalations
  • Provide hands-on support for IT hardware (laptops, peripherals, meeting room equipment) and software, ensuring fast and effective resolution
  • Classify, prioritize, and route tickets accurately in Jira, following defined processes and SLA targets
  • Troubleshoot and resolve advanced technical issues, escalating to L2/L3 teams when needed and documenting follow-up
  • Identify recurring problems and propose long-term solutions, including knowledge base updates and process improvements
  • Support onboarding of new colleagues by preparing and delivering IT equipment and access according to defined standards

What you bring & who you are

  • Completed vocational training or degree in IT, Computer Science, or equivalent experience, with at least 2 years in IT end-user support and hands-on troubleshooting
  • Experience working with ticketing systems such as Jira Service Management or ServiceNow
  • Technical knowledge of IT hardware and standard software troubleshooting (Windows, Microsoft 365)
  • Strong communicator who can diagnose and resolve technical issues independently
  • Ideally, experience with endpoint management tools (e.g., Microsoft Intune) and familiarity with ITIL processes
  • German language skills at C1 level
  • Willingness to undergo a PSP (Personensicherheitsprüfung)
  • Car and driving licence

About the team: Our Group IT Support team keeps colleagues across Axpo productive every day. We work closely together, share knowledge openly, and value every voice - bringing diverse backgrounds and experience together to keep improving how we support our organization
