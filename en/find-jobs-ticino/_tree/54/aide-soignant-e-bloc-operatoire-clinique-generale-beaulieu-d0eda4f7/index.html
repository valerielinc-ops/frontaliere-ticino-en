Full description

  • Prepare and verify the equipment needed to install the operation (table – mattress – supports), specific request of the surgeon or anaesthetist.
  • Plan and verify the medical-technical equipment, in agreement with the instrumentist, specific to each intervention.
  • Take charge of the operation as soon as it arrives at the block and accompany it to the programmed room, with its complete file.
  • Transmit, if necessary, recommendations or wishes made at the time of the nurse's visit.
  • Attend the anaesthetist in the induction room and install the basic elements (pressure sleeve, ECG electrodes, arm support, stative, pulsoxymeter) and assist the physician when taking the venous route, general anaesthesia (intubation) or loco-regional.
  • Introduce the operation into the room with the anaesthetist doctor and continue the final installation.
  • Control the installation (support points, withers, leg pads) under the control of the instrumentist, surgeon and anesthetist.
  • Respond to requests for equipment from the surgeon, anaesthetist and instrumentalist during the duration of the surgery.
  • Install the instrumentists when preparing their instrumentation table.
  • Respect and apply the various protocols put in place (hygiene, decontamination, washing, traceability).
  • Apply Work Instructions (IT)
  • Complete the developed forms (FO) as part of the ISO 9001 Quality Manual
  • Follow the checklists (CL)
  • Accurately record medico-technical services on operating fees and complete the references accurately.
  • Check the sterility deadlines of Medical Devices before opening on the table.
  • Respect the equipment and report any incidents that occurred during its use to the block's technical manager (report on its notebook).
  • Gather the elements of the operator's folder before exiting the block (labels – radiological folder).
  • Accompany the anaesthetist physician for patient transfer upon waking up after disconnecting the various devices used.
  • Evacuate biological waste, cloth contaminated according to the protocols established and implemented by the nurse who is responsible for hygiene in the operating room.
  • Remove and store in their respective place medical-technical devices that will no longer be used.
  • Clean the operating room according to the protocols in effect.
  • Make the operating room clean and prepare it again for the program suite.
  • END OF PROGRAMME
  • Cleaning the anesthesia room according to established protocols.
  • Rehabilitation of the anesthesia room.
  • "Control" and "resupply" of all equipment necessary for an operating day.
  • Cleaning the tables and their accessories (to remove traces of sticky paper, scotch) and preparing tables for the next day's program.
  • ON PONCTUAL REQUEST by the superiors
  • Large complete cleaning of anesthesia closets, tables, medico-technical appliances during periods of decrease of activity.
  • Control of medical devices of anesthesia drawers, expiry dates (intubation probes – needles ...)
  • Participate in year-end inventories.
  • Caregiver diploma
  • Diploma ATSSO
  • One-year experience in an operating room
  • French mother tongue (or excellent knowledge)
  • Good knowledge of English a plus
  • An activity in an exciting and diverse field, within an internationally renowned clinic, at the cutting edge of technological development.
  • A pleasant working environment in a human-sized structure
  • Wage and motivating social conditions
  • Full file requested (CV, cover letter) - a work certificate is desired
