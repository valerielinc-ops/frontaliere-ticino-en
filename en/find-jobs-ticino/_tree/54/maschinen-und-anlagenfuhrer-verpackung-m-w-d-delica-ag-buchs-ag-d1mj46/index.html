Full description

You take responsibility and actively contribute to the quality and efficiency of our production. As a plant operator, you are the engine of our packaging line! As a team, we celebrate successes, promote each other and create sustainable moments of enjoyment for our customers. Come to us, move more than you can imagine – and yes, we also celebrate with cake!

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG Buchs, Bresteneggstrasse 4, 5033 Buchs AG

Important information

Letter of motivation? No need – complete employment certificates and diplomas already. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You operate and independently monitor our packaging and wrapping systems and ensure the highest quality.
  • You carry out quality controls (IPK) and ensure that our products meet the highest standards.
  • You repair simple disturbances independently, support the technology during maintenance work and document everything carefully.
  • You make correct bookings in SAP and ensure a smooth flow of materials.
  • You are responsible for small teams of temporary employees, train new plant employees and ensure a seamless shift transfer.
  • You support the implementation of changes in the production environment.

Requirements

  • Min. 2 years of experience: in the field of plant management, packaging technology or production
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): EFZ/EBA or readiness for adult education by type. 32 desired
  • English (very good knowledge): min. B1
  • Experience with SAP or ERP systems as well as good knowledge of MS Office
  • Knowledge of hygiene and safety regulations and technical understanding of mechanical adaptations
  • Flexibility and resilience – you are ready for 2- to 4-shift work and tackle with a structured, reliable way of working
  • You are a team player, proactive and find independent solutions

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Talks with HR & department: The interview is conducted virtually or on site with the responsible person from HR and/or the specialist department. It is primarily about learning more about the personnel and specialist skills and gives both sides the opportunity to assess the cultural fit into the company and team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Reference information: When obtaining references, we ask for impressions, strengths and weaknesses again and have them verified by former supervisors or employees. References can be obtained after the first or second round and are only made with consent. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Test work: A trial day or half day gives the opportunity to get to know the future working environment and the team members better. And vice versa, it gives us a chance to assess how well someone fits into the team. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Final discussion before issuing the contract: Before the contract is issued, we hold a final discussion in order to clarify open questions of both sides and to regulate contractual details. If all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition (Digital): After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent for digital signature by e-mail. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
