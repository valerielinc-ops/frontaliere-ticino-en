Portrait The schools of Péry-La Heutte and Sonceboz are looking for their classes of 3H un-e

Tasks

  • Replacement of maternity leave from 11.01.2027 to 23.05.2027 (approximately)
  • 3H class education (support for pupils following an introductory programme and co-education)
  • Collaboration with women teachers can take several forms (class sections, small group teaching, co-education...)

Profile required

  • Holder of a Master's degree in special education or/and good experience with young students who need time to enter 3H apprenticeships.
  • You are dynamic, motivated to find new strategies, willing to embark on a new challenge of collaboration with 3H teachers.
  • You are ready to invest in two schools with the goal of supporting students and teachers.

We offer

  • Replacement of 15 lessons in two medium-sized schools (Pery-La Heutte and Sonceboz).
  • Working environments that are welcoming and offer multiple educational opportunities.
  • The licensee's current schedule is spread over 3 days (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday) but may be modified.

The interviews will take place on Tuesday 24 November at 4 p.m.
