Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden BernOccupation by: Immediately or by appointment Type of employment: Indefinite reference number: 74247

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

Hirslanden Bern’s diabetes consultancy offers in-patient and outpatient consultations at the three locations Klinik Beau-Site, Salem-Spital and Klinik Permanence.

In order to strengthen and further expand our existing offer, we are looking for an additional specialist. In this way, we strengthen cross-location care, increase the availability of diabetes counselling and ensure continuous and high-quality care for our patients.

Together we continue to develop the diabetes consultancy of Hirslanden Bern.

YOUR ADVISORY ADVISORY ADVISORY ADVISORY

  • A collaborative and appreciative team: You work in a committed, helpful team with short communication channels and a family working atmosphere
  • Individual introduction: We accompany you in a structured and personal way in your new task and design the familiarization to suit your experiences and needs
  • Versatile and varied working environment: Working at three locations and caring for inpatient and outpatient patients, you can expect a varied working day with a wide range of professional issues
  • Co-creation and development: As part of the expansion of our diabetes consultancy, you have the opportunity to contribute your expertise, help shape processes and actively accompany the further development of the offer.
  • Interprofessional cooperation: You work closely with doctors, nurses, nutrition consultants and external partners and are therefore an important part of holistic patient care
  • Attractive benefits: You can expect an extensive range of benefits, including PubliBike, public transport coupons, discounts for fitness subscriptions and other attractive offers
  • Discounted food: In the restaurants of our clinics you benefit from discounted food options

YOUR TASKS

  • You support patients with diabetes in the inpatient and outpatient area and carry out diabetes therapies at different locations, including in malnutrition, nephrology and visceral surgery with a focus on pancreas
  • You will conduct individual training, consultations and support at T1DM, T2DM, GDM and specific forms of diabetes
  • You organize and coordinate the diabetology and endocrinology office hours
  • You ensure a close interprofessional cooperation with the medical service, nursing, nutritional advice and external partners such as Spitex and nursing homes
  • You are actively involved in health promotion and prevention
  • You are involved in the quality assurance and further development of diabetes care
  • You instruct and accompany patients in dealing with continuous glucose measurement (CGM) and insulin pumps
  • You organize and conduct training and further education for nurses and assistant doctors

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a nurse or nurse HF/FH as well as further training as a diabetes specialist consultant HöFa1/DAS/HFP (for foreign diploma including SRK recognition)
  • Professional experience in the field is advantageous
  • You bring with you the willingness to develop personally and professionally and to actively shape the further development of an ever-changing field of expertise
  • You work independently, independently and reliably
  • You have very good oral and written knowledge of German (minor level C1), English and French skills are an advantage
