Full description

True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors.

Hamilton Storage develops, manufactures and distributes robots for storing up to ten million chemical substances for pharmaceutical companies. We also produce robots for storing biological samples at -80 degrees Celsius, which are used in biobanks for clinical research purposes.

You can do that

At Hamilton Storage, you don’t work on classic refrigeration systems. You are responsible for highly complex automated storage systems in which sensitive samples are stably stored in the temperature range from -80 °C to +30 °C.

In the field, you are the central specialist for all refrigeration technology topics and ensure that our systems run reliably – even under demanding conditions and with international customers.

This role is aimed at trained refrigeration technicians with a solid professional basis. A senior level is not required – it is crucial that you master your craft and want to develop further.

  • Repair and maintenance at our international customers
  • Operational planning of third-party companies at our customers
  • Monitor and evaluate the work of third-party companies
  • Renewing contracts with third parties
  • Monitor and maintain our refrigerant storage facility
  • Creating and maintaining the status overview of refrigeration systems
  • Maintenance according to local regulations
  • Processing of customer complaints
  • Feedback to engineering to improve systems

This is what you bring

  • Completed training as a refrigeration system technician:in EFZ (mandatory)
  • Certification in the handling of refrigerants (mandatory)
  • First to several years of experience in refrigeration technology
  • Self-employed and structured working methods
  • Enjoyment of customer contact
  • German stylish confident, English advantageous
  • Category B driving licence

Framework conditions

  • Operations are plannable and structured
  • No piketting service
