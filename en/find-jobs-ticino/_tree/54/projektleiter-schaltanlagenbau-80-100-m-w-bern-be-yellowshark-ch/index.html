01 Future employer

For an established company in the field of electrical engineering and automation, we are looking for a committed individual who enjoys managing switchgear projects holistically and leading a team both technically and personally. You can expect a varied role with a high degree of personal responsibility, direct customer contact and exciting technical challenges.

02 Your role

  • You will independently manage several switchgear projects from technical and commercial preparation through to successful handover.
  • In doing so, you will coordinate material procurement, resources and personnel deployment and ensure a smooth project process.
  • You will lead the employees assigned to you and support your team with technical and organizational matters.
  • You will always keep costs, deadlines and quality under control and initiate appropriate measures at an early stage in the event of deviations.
  • You will maintain professional contact with customers and actively develop existing business relationships further.
  • Through your technical understanding, you will identify additional customer needs and thereby contribute to winning follow-up and additional orders.
  • You will work closely with internal departments and other project participants and ensure efficient collaboration.

04 Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as an automation technician EFZ, switchgear assembler or in a comparable electrical engineering occupation.
  • Further training in switchgear construction, project management or electrical engineering is an advantage.
  • Professional experience in the planning, engineering or implementation of switchgear and control systems.
  • Initial or extensive experience in managing employees, together with a desire to take responsibility for a team.
  • Good knowledge of CAD applications and a strong affinity for digital tools.
  • An independent, solution-oriented and structured way of working.
  • Strong organizational skills as well as a good technical and commercial understanding.
  • A communicative, proactive and team-oriented personality who enjoys driving things forward.

05 Useful information
