Osteopathy at PULSE AND BALANCE – helping to shape it right from the start. You do not just want to get into an existing system, but to help build and shape a specialist area? You value professional exchange and do not want to live osteopathy in isolation, but as part of an interdisciplinary treatment concept? Then you could be a good fit for us.

YOUR SCOPE

You take over the osteopathic care of your patients independently and at the same time build up a new department together with us. How this develops, we do not want to specify to the last detail – your experience, your ideas and your professional perspective should play a central role.

YOUR TASKS

  • Independent osteopathic care and treatment of our patients
  • Findings and development of individual treatment strategies
  • Care of a wide range of patients – from people with everyday complaints to athletes and active people
  • Close cooperation with our specialists, physiotherapists, chiropractors and specialists from athletic and performance diagnostics
  • Regular professional exchange within our interdisciplinary team
  • Structure and technical development of osteopathy
  • Co-design of processes, offers and interfaces to the other specialist areas
  • If you are interested in participating in interdisciplinary offers, check-ups and prevention and health formats

What you bring

  • Master of Science FH in osteopathy, intercantonal GDK diploma in osteopathy or an equivalent foreign degree recognised in Switzerland
  • Professional Permit for the Canton of St. Gallen or Compliance with the conditions for granting them
  • Professional experience in the independent treatment of patients
  • Enjoy thinking about osteopathy interdisciplinary and exchange ideas with other disciplines
  • High quality standards and an independent, structured working method
  • Empathetic and professional appearance
  • Interest in sports, exercise and holistic health
  • Initiative and joy in shaping a new department not only technically, but also organizationally

PULSE AND BALANCE is an interdisciplinary Medical & Performance Center in St. Gallen. On approximately 1’200 m2, we combine orthopaedics, physiotherapy, chiropractic, performance diagnostics and athletics under one roof. What we still lack today: osteopathy. Our roots lie in top sport – but our claim applies to everyone. We want to offer people regardless of age, level of performance or complaint a high-quality, holistic and interdisciplinary care.For the development of our field osteopathy, we are therefore looking for a personality who not only wants to treat, but also think, shape and build something of his own.

What awaits you with us

  • Co-create: You build the field of osteopathy from the beginning and bring your own ideas
  • Interdisciplinarity: Direct exchange with orthopaedics, physiotherapy, chiropractic, athletic and performance diagnostics
  • Inspiring setup: An active environment and at the same time a broad spectrum of patients
  • Self-responsibility: Short decision paths, direct communication and plenty of room for manoeuvre
  • Development potential: Modern infrastructure and a broad network of medicine, sports and health

Our philosophy is simple: You don’t have to be a professional athlete to be looked after like one.

We transfer the way of thinking and quality from top sport to the care of our patients and bring together different medical and therapeutic expertise in one place.You want to shape osteopathy at PULSE AND BALANCE right from the start?
