Full description

Become part of our team in BRIG. We lead, develop, design and plan infrastructure projects of all kinds. We accompany them from planning to implementation. We are looking for two new team members for the branch in Brig: PROJECT ENGINEER CONSTRUCTION 80-100% - BRIG PROJECT MANAGER CONSTRUCTION WITH POTENTIAL Deputy BRANCH MANAGER 80-100% - BRIG

YOUR TASKS

Project Engineer

  • You will support in the detailed processing of road, railway construction, as well as industrial and energy projects, earthquake reinforcements
  • Independent management and processing of projects throughout all construction phases
  • Dimensions and structural planning
  • Coordination with AFRY specialists

Project manager with potential Deputy branch manager

  • Project management of versatile and demanding new construction and repair projects in the areas of civil engineering, road and railway construction as well as energy plants, from project planning to commissioning
  • Leadership and technical support of project teams
  • Support of the branch manager in the acquisition of new projects
  • Support of the branch manager in maintaining existing and establishing new customer contacts

YOUR OPPORTUNITIES

  • Exciting regional and national projects for well-known builders
  • Collaboration in interdisciplinary, multilingual (D/F/E) teams and projects
  • Further development opportunities at AFRY
  • Flexible working time models with the possibility of working from home

YOUR PROFILE

  • Degree in civil engineering from ETH or FH or equivalent
  • Several years of professional experience and in-depth expertise in the field of civil engineering, road or railway construction, possibly civil engineering and industrial plants or building construction
  • Experience in successful project management and management of employees
  • Construction management experience is an advantage
  • Communicative, independent, cost-conscious and flexible personality
  • Native speaker German and good written and oral French skills
