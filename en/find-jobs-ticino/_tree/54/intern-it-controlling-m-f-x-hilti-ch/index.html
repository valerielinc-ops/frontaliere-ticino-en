Full description

Operations Simplified. Platforms Standardized.

We are looking for an IT Controlling Intern to support our Global IT Controlling team. In this role, you will contribute to process improvement, automation initiatives, and monthly operational controlling tasks across our IT portfolio. You will collaborate with Global IT stakeholders to enhance reporting efficiency, strengthen internal processes, and support day‑to‑day controlling activities.

This internship is ideal for someone who enjoys problem‑solving, improving workflows, working with data, and supporting financial transparency within a global IT environment.

This is a full‑time internship position. Start date is flexible. Duration for at least 6 months. No travel is foreseen for this position.

What You’ll Bring

Become a valuable member of our highly professional and international team and meet the challenges of a global multinational company using the latest technologies. You will have the freedom to act in your area of responsibility while you can count on the support of your colleagues to master the challenges ahead.

What you need is

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program in a relevant field (e.g. Finance, Accounting, Business, Engineering or Information Systems) throughout the full internship duration
  • Analytical mindset with an interest in process improvement
  • Proficient in Microsoft Office
  • Interest in automation and digital solutions
  • Self-driven working style
  • Strong communication skills in English

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in St. Gallen?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton St. Gallen's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with St. Gallen's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
