Full description

Surgery, Urology Ambulatorium

Job: By arrangement, indefinitely

Tasks

  • Independent care, advice and support of patients with urological diseases.
  • Assistance in urological examinations and outpatient procedures such as cystoscopies, biopsies and catheter changes.
  • Implementation of diagnostic and therapeutic measures according to medical prescription.
  • Professional preparation and follow-up of examinations as well as ensuring hygiene and quality standards.
  • Coordination of outpatient consultation hours and interprofessional cooperation with the medical service and other specialist areas.
  • Ensuring seamless care documentation as well as guidance and advice for patients in dealing with their disease.

Requirements

  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nurse HF/FH or an equivalent education recognised in Switzerland.
  • Professional experience in ambulatory or urology is beneficial.
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods as well as strong organisational talent.
  • High patient orientation, empathy and joy in dealing with people.
  • Team capable, flexible and resilient personality with good communication skills.
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation and willingness to develop professionally and personally.
