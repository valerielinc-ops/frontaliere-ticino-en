The Position The Patient Value Partner is responsible for the engagement of all activities related to the brand assigned through different levels of their life cycle, covering all specific steps associated to patient’s journeys.

Your Responsibilities

  • Provides input to the Chapter Head for strategy definition, but also its periodic adaptation, depending on the changes that have occurred in the external environment or whenever it is needed.
  • Takes the proper actions to implement the brand strategy in order to maximize the use of the brand, for relevant patient population, achieving the Company objectives.
  • Collaborates in the Chapter to ensure the relevance of his/her brand strategies and the achievement of overall goals.
  • Working cross-functional with other Chapters.
  • Initiates the budget proposals and participates in the pre-budget discussions.
  • Responsible for the resource allocation within the product promotion activities for assigned brands.
  • Identifies and recommends new channels for product promotion.
  • Develops and maintains high standard of product knowledge and updated information refers to all matters related to the product promotion and commercialization.
  • Actively supports the field force on product information, key messages and company’s strategies regarding the assigned brands.
  • Actively participation in allocated Squad, with higher expertise and knowledge on the brand/disease area, but also with overall perspective on all activities driven inside the squad.
  • Proactive market research and analysis covering trends, competitiveness, innovative tools, specific to customers promotion or/and from other agile markets.
  • KOL’s engagement with solid product knowledge, consultancy over the product space and interactions.
  • Active presence and contribution to products comities internal and external.

Who You Are

  • You hold a University degree in natural sciences, Medical, Pharmacy, Chemistry, or Biology. Business related degree would be an advantage
  • You built more than 5 years of work experience in marketing (product management)/or similar in the Pharmaceutical industry
  • You built in-depth knowledge of competitive activity in the pharmaceutical/ biotechnology industry
  • You built excellent product and marketing knowledge within the hematology and hemophilia therapy areas
  • Proven track record of effective decision-making, and proven financial analysis, planning and reporting capabilities. Moreover, advanced knowledge of Microsoft Office suite: Excel, Word, and PowerPoint
  • You are strategic thinker who brings analytical and problem-solving skills, scenarios and risks/opportunities mind set to growth products perspective
  • Advanced organisational and time management skills, and ability
  • You are a self-motivated team player who excels in cross-functional collaboration
  • You have excellent communication skills and high sensitiveness to local conditions
  • You are creative, curious, and flexible at adopting new methodologies and exploring new business areas
  • You are fluent in English and Romanian languages, both written and spoken
