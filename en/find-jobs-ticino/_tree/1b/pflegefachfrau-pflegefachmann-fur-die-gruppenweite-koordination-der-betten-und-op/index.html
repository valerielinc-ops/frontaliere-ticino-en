Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

For our team we are looking for reinforcement immediately or by agreement. Tasks: • As a patient dispatcher, they are responsible for the smooth planning of operations, interventions and entrances as well as for the scheduling of elective and emergency patient entrances. • You coordinate the planning for surgery, medicine, women’s clinic, children’s hospital and for the hospital locations Sursee, Wolhusen and Nidwalden • The coordination of the administrative entry process of our patients is within their versatile and demanding area of responsibility. • Your active participation in change processes and the interdisciplinary cooperation with employees at all locations and in various functions enriches your everyday work Requirements • As dipl. Nursing professional HF or as a specialist health EFZ, they ideally have several years of professional experience in the acute area of a hospital • You are a personality with strong communication skills, high organizational skills and networked thinking • They like to work in a team and practice their profession with joy and commitment • High personal responsibility, high understanding of services and quality awareness round off their profile We offer: • Look forward to a varied field of work at one of the largest employers in Central Switzerland in a lively, collegial environment • You have the opportunity to make a valuable contribution in a dynamic operation with great growth potential • For a coordinated work-life balance, we offer working conditions that allow mothers and fathers alike to combine work, family responsibilities and leisure activities. • They expect attractive employment conditions and interesting employee offers. Thanks to our wide variety of internal educational offers, they also benefit from targeted and individual competence development and promotion.
