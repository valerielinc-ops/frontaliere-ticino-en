Full description

The LEB links Lausanne to Gros-de-Vaud and supports the development of a growing region. By 2035, he plans to introduce a 10-minute pace between Lausanne and Echhallens in order to offer ever more attractive and sustainable mobility.

This development is accompanied by many infrastructure projects and requires rigorous maintenance of railway lines and civil engineering works. In order to ensure the safety, reliability and sustainability of our network, we are looking for a motivated person to put his technical skills and know-how at the service of our railway infrastructure.

Track Maintenance Technician / GC LEB

Your Mission

As part of the LEB's public transport service mission

• Carry out preventive and corrective maintenance of the tracks, as well as their infrastructure, in accordance with the costs, deadlines and quality expected • Ensuring safe operating conditions

Your activities

Track maintenance

Runs track maintenance operations

  • By ensuring preventive maintenance
  • By performing corrective maintenance operations
  • By conducting specific work trains

Infrastructure maintenance

Participates in infrastructure maintenance operations

  • By carrying out preventive and corrective maintenance on infrastructure
  • By carrying out the operations of curing and washing infrastructure
  • By carrying out small civil engineering and green space maintenance

Compliance with safety and quality standards

Meets and enforces the safety standards required in the workshop:

  • By applying safe, work management procedures and updates
  • By ensuring traceability of interventions carried out
  • Controlling the operation following interventions
  • Ensuring quality and defined costs

Your profile

Training • Basic vocational training with certification; ideally CFC as a railway manufacturer or mason CFC • Continuous training (internal and external) in railway clearances

Professional experience

  • The trade requires between 1 and 3 years of professional experience, ideally in the railway field

Authorizations, legal requirements and security • Compliance with company IT rules and data confidentiality • Ability to work at night

  • Licence B100 or ability to attend training

• Driver's licence category B (light vehicles)
