Full description

Description of the position The position is inserted within the Information Technology & Security Quality Assurance department at the Head Quarter of the company IBSA Institut Biochimique SA located in Lugano south (CH). The company is geographically distributed across various production plants and various commercial countries worldwide. As Jr IT QA Specialist, the person will follow both qualification and compliance maintenance activities of the company IT infrastructure. Within the following skills:

  • carry out the qualification activities of the Corporate and branch IT infrastructure
  • carry out the qualification activities of the Computerized Systems in charge of the ET&T department
  • issue the procedures and work instructions of the IT department
  • draw up the qualification documentation of the systems in charge of the IT department
  • carry out and/or support the qualification tests of the systems in charge of the IT department
  • review the validation documentation and department procedures
  • apply the IT and IS procedures in accordance with company policies ## Required profile
  • Minimum 1-2 years of experience in similar positions
  • Preference Degree in scientific disciplines
  • Excellent knowledge of basic IT systems
  • Excellent knowledge of spoken and written English
  • Knowledge of the regulatory framework in the GMP environment such as ICH, PICs, FDA, EUGMP
  • Knowledge of the regulatory framework relating to the qualification and maintenance of IT Infrastructures in the Life Science sector
  • Knowledge of the regulatory framework relating to the integrity of data and computerized systems
  • Basic knowledge of network infrastructures and related components
