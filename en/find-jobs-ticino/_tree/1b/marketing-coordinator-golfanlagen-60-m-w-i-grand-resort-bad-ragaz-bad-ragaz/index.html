Full description

Tasks

  • Coordination and implementation of marketing activities for the golf facilities Bad Ragaz and Heidiland
  • Co-creation of a growth-oriented marketing strategy and marketing planning in the area of golf and resort across the board
  • Support in the design and implementation of sales, image and new customer acquisition campaigns including success monitoring
  • Content and functional development of the website as well as support for social media channels
  • Planning, organisation and implementation of photo and video productions
  • Support in the areas of public relations and cooperation
  • Creating marketing communication tools such as newsletters, advertisements and brochures (member communication)
  • Writing target group-oriented and convincing texts for various communication channels

Requirements

  • Basic commercial training with further training in marketing, communication and/or social media as well as corresponding professional experience
  • Proven expertise in Digital & Content Marketing (SEO/SEA, Web, Web Analytics, Email and Social Media Marketing, Storytelling, Controlling)
  • Safe handling of MS Office, InDesign and Photoshop
  • Strong coordination and organizational skills as well as a high implementation orientation
  • Independent, initiative and responsible personality
  • Stylistic German as well as very good English skills in word and writing
  • Affinity for golf is beneficial
  • Readiness for occasional weekend assignments

We offer

  • Tasting evenings and perks at Thermal Spa
  • Test sleep after passing the test period
  • Up to 50% discount in restaurants
  • Up to 50% discount in the Fitness Center and Tamina Therme

Grand Resort Bad Ragaz is the only resort in Switzerland with two golf courses. The 18-hole Championship Course of Golf Club Bad Ragaz is one of the best courses in the country and is one of the "Leading Golf Courses". The restaurant gladys, awarded several times as "Best Swiss Golf Restaurant", spoils you culinary. In addition, the clubhouse offers an extensive golf shop for golf and leisure fashion.

The Golf Club Heidiland offers with its 9-hole Executive Course and an exercise center with 20 covered and 50 lawn teeing areas optimal training opportunities and has established itself as a beginner club of the region. A bistro and a golf shop complete the wide offer.
