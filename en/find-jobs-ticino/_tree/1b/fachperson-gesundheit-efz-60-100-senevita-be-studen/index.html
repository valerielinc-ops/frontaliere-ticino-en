Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,300 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland. For the Senevita Wydenpark in Studen BE we are looking for a / Health professional EFZ 60 - 100% Your tasks

  • You ensure the professional, individual, and holistic care and care of the residents
  • You actively support the residents in maintaining and promoting their existing abilities and involve them in their care decisions
  • You recognize early changes in the health status of the residents, document them precisely and initiate appropriate measures
  • You maintain an empathetic and transparent exchange with relatives and caregivers and are available as a competent contact person
  • You actively participate in the further development of our care quality and contribute your ideas to the optimization of processes
  • Optionally, you can engage as a vocational trainer

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a specialist/health EFZ
  • Ideally, you have already gained professional experience in long-term care
  • You have a big heart for elderly people and respect for their life story
  • You value empathetic, appreciative and resident-centred care
  • Teamwork is important to you. You contribute with humor and helpfulness to the positive work climate
  • Good knowledge of German is essential for communication with our residents and in the team

We offer you

  • The opportunity to work independently and exchange ideas with an experienced team
  • Interesting training courses of the Senevita Academy, which support your professional development
  • Discounted food in the in-house gastronomy
  • Attractive Senevita benefits (SBB half-ax, fueling, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Regular team events and shared experiences that strengthen cooperation

Generous holiday regime

  • Our statement "closer to people" counts for our employees as much as for our residents

Interested? A warm and open team is looking forward to you! Please send us your online application.
