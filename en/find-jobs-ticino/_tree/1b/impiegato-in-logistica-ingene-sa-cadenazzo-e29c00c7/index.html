Full description

The employer is looking for one

Employee in logistics (marked)

Mansions

  • warehouse management: manage the daily activities of the warehouse, guaranteeing order, cleanliness and correct organization of the spaces.
  • receive and control the goods incoming, verifying the correspondence with orders and transport documents.
  • verify the integrity and quality of the materials received and report any differences, damages or non-compliance.
  • correctly to manage the movements of entry, exit and return of the goods.
  • arrange storage of materials according to order criteria, accessibility and security.
  • monitor the stocks and promptly report any shortcomings or need to resupply.
  • participate in periodic inventories and verify the correspondence between physical and managerial fields.
  • preparation and distribution of the material to the yards: prepare the necessary material for the yards on the basis of the requests received from the managers and the team leaders.
  • verify the completeness and correctness of the material before delivery to the teams.
  • arrange and arrange supplies so that they are available in the expected time.
  • manage additional material requests from the yards.
  • control the material returned from the yards, checking quantity, state and integrity.
  • manage the return, accommodation and registration of the unused material.
  • report any missing materials, damaged or not returned.
  • management of equipment and means: manage the distribution and return of company equipment for teams.
  • verify the status of the equipment and report any failures, damages or maintenance needs.
  • ensure that the equipment is correctly identified, kept and kept in order.
  • collaborate with the operational management of the company van park, monitoring any maintenance needs and reporting anomalies.
  • verify the availability of the equipment necessary for the performance of the operational activities.
  • management of purchases and suppliers
  • collect and manage purchase requests from different departments.
  • check availability of the materials in stock before proceeding with new supply requests.
  • prepare order requests and purchase orders according to business delegates and procedures.
  • monitor order confirmations and delivery dates of suppliers.
  • request suppliers in case of delays or lack of deliveries, informing the superior when necessary.
  • verify the correspondence between ordered and received material.
  • cooperate with the control of invoices suppliers in relation to goods received and orders made.
  • report to the Logistics Manager and Purchase any issues related to prices, quality, availability or reliability of suppliers.
  • transport and shipment management: operationally arrange shipments and deliveries of material.
  • prepare the necessary documentation for shipments and transport.
  • monitor delivery times and report any delays.
  • manage the documentation relating to the movement of goods, ensuring proper storage.
  • to cooperate in the management of transport and customs clearance practices, according to the indications of the superior.
  • coordination and internal communication: maintain a constant contact with the site manager, team leaders and other business departments to ensure the availability of the necessary materials.
  • promptly communicate any lack of material, delays in deliveries or logistic problems.
  • ensure a clear and timely passage of information between warehouse, purchases and operational departments.
  • support employees in managing logistic requests and operational needs.
  • collaborate with the Logistics and Purchasing Manager in the optimization of processes and in the organization of activities.
  • company security and procedures: respect and enforce, within the scope of its activities, the company security provisions.
  • correctly use the handling equipment, in compliance with the required skills.
  • maintain order and cleanliness in working spaces.
  • promptly report any risk or non-compliance situations.
  • work in compliance with business procedures and internal provisions.

Requirements

  • previous experience in the field of at least 3 years
  • ability to work in team
  • good knowledge of Italian
  • basic knowledge of English
  • licence to conduct cat. B
  • self-employed person

Details of use

  • 100% employment
  • use immediately, indefinitely
  • wages according to CCL of the sector
