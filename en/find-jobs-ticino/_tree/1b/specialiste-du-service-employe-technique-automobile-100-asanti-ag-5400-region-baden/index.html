J e t z t b e w e r b e n Service specialist / Technical employee Automotive 100%. You are tech-savvy, enjoy customer contact and find a suitable solution for every problem? You do not want to sit at your desk all day, but also put your technical know-how from the automotive industry to practical use? Then this job could be exactly your next step! For our customers, we are looking for a versatile personality that combines technical advice, sales and service. Your tasks – versatile instead of monotonous Customer Advice & Sales

  • You advise customers by phone, email and via the webshop on products, applications and technical solutions.
  • You process orders, create offers and stay active in your offers.
  • You support the sales team with your technical expertise.
  • You maintain customer relationships and make sure that customers feel well taken care of.
  • You keep customer and order data up-to-date in the corresponding systems.

Workshop, Returns & Repairs

  • You check returned products and assess repairability, warranty claims and possible replacement deliveries.
  • You perform error analysis, maintenance and repairs.
  • You take care of a clean documentation and a smooth handling of returns.
  • You make sure that repaired products work properly and meet quality requirements.
  • You recognize recurring product problems and bring in suggestions for improvement.

You bring that with you

  • A completed technical vocational training in the automotive industry as well as a commercial further education.
  • Experience in customer service, technical sales or in a comparable function.
  • Enjoy solving technical questions and competently advising customers.
  • Good knowledge of MS Office and ideally experience with CRM and ERP systems.
  • A structured, independent and reliable way of working.
  • Communication skills, organizational talent and team spirit.
  • The willingness to constantly familiarize yourself with new products and technologies.

This is what awaits you

  • A varied activity with a balanced mix of customer advice and workshop work.
  • A versatile working day in which you can combine your technical and commercial knowledge.
  • Direct customer contact and the opportunity to independently develop solutions.
  • Technical training and product training.
  • A collaborative team that supports each other.
  • Regulated working hours from Monday to Friday at a 100% rate.
