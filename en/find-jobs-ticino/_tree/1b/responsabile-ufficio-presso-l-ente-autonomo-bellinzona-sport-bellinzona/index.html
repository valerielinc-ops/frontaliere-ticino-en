Bellinzona Sport Casella Postal 1219 6501 Bellinzona WARNING FOR PERSONAL ASSESSMENT The Executive Board of the Bellinzona Sport Autonomous Body opens a public competition for the appointment, with an occupational degree of 100%, of: one/person office at the Bellinzona Sport Autonomous Body, Promotion Office and Public Bath.

Main tasks

  • support the Directorate in the definition and implementation of strategies, projects and initiatives

promote the Bellinzona sport as part of the valorization of the sports companies of the territory, as well as the management of sports events and training fields;

  • promotes the Bellinzona sport through the channels provided by the Belli+ platform;
  • co-ordinates the management of the Belli+ platform;
  • is responsible for the management and promotion of the pool sector, as well as staff management

attributed (bagnines/e, cashiers/e);

  • I would like to thank the rapporteur for his work.
  • care for operational relations with the media, drafting press releases;
  • collaborate with the Management in the evasion of administrative practices, including the preparation of Messages

municipal, answers to questions / questions, answers on institutional issues and the preparation of estimates and conjunctives of the Sports Board;

  • set up estimates and statistics, draw up conjunctives and reports relating to its area of competence;
  • service out of time, on weekends (Saturday and Sunday), on holidays and according to need

in occasion and sporting events;

  • carry out other tasks entrusted by his or her superiors according to the needs.

Formal requirements

  • maturity.

Preferential requirements

  • higher or university education;
  • proven experience in the management/organization of events, in the administration of infrastructure or

in the journalistic/publishing sector;

  • proven knowledge of the city sports environment, municipal administration and active bodies

on the territory, as well as experience in the professional sports sector;

  • experience in writing articles (printed/web card), in the interview technique (written and video) and

in the management of publishing projects (Content Management);

  • experience in operating and logistics management of sports facilities, big events, complex projects or

public/private structures with user flows management;

  • excellent capabilities in the use of computer programs;
  • excellent knowledge of the Italian language and knowledge of the national languages;
  • license to conduct category B.

Aptitude requirements

  • sense of responsibility, fairness and reliability;
  • guidance on objectives and results, with the ability to define priorities;
  • strong coordination skills, collaborative leadership and project management;
  • strong communication and relational skills;
  • sensitivity and capacity for relations in the institutional sphere;
  • trading capacity;
  • management, collaboration and teamwork, maintaining operational effectiveness even during periods

of greater activity;

  • flexibility and availability at work outside time and on public holidays, according to the needs of service.

Working conditions

  • Occupational grade: 100%;
  • 40 hours working week (to 100%);
  • workplace: Bellinzona (possibility of smart working).

Documents to be produced

  1. accompanying letter; 2. curriculum vitae; 3. copy of the identity document; 4. copy of permission, if foreign; 5. jpg format photography; 6. copy of study certificates (with exam notes) and work; 7. self-certification relating to the judicial box; 8. self-certification relating to the certificate of solvency (UEF); 9. self-certification relating to health. Self-certification is available on www.bellinzona.ch.
