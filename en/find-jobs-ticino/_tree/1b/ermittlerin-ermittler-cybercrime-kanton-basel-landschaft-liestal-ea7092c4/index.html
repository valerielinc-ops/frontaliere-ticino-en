Basel Police Landscape The Cyber Investigation Team Liestal 1 January 2027 or by agreement

Your responsibility

  • Conduct of police preliminary investigations, investigation procedures and investigations by the public prosecutor’s office in the field of cybercrime and digitized crime
  • Define and initiate immediate measures for currently occurring cyber crimes
  • Acquisition, analysis and documentation of information from publicly available digital sources (OSINT)

The Cyber Investigation Team

The Cyber Investigation team specializes in solving complex cases of cybercrime and technically sophisticated digital crime and conducts appropriate investigation procedures. The tasks are varied and exciting: They range from malware analyses and reverse engineering to the tracing of cryptocurrencies to investigations in the darknet, information gathering in the digital space and preventive activities to strengthen cybersecurity. The team works closely with the cybercrime department of the public prosecutor’s office. In addition, the experts support other investigative services and the uniform police in classical crimes with digital reference. Prevention work in the field of cybercrime and digitised crime is also part of the team’s remit. This demanding activity requires highly qualified specialists. The team includes both civilian IT specialists and police officers with appropriate training. Together they form a competent and committed unit in the fight against cybercrime.
