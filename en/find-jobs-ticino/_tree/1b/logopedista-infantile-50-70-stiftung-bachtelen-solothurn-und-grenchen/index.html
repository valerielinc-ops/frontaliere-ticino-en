Full description

Bachtelen is engaged in the canton of Solothurn with a wide range of offers for the promotion and integration of children, young people and young adults.

At the HPD Bachtelen (Healing Education Service), we are responsible for early logopedia and early curative education in the Solothurn/Grenchen area and are pleased to perform this cantonal task with high professionalism.

Are you passionate about giving young children the best possible language development opportunities through your work?

Early logopede / Early logopede (50 – 70 %)

Job: by appointment

Your interesting field of activity includes

  • Speech pedagogical examinations in children aged 0 – 4 years
  • Speech and group therapy with preschool-age children
  • Advice for parents in connection with logopaedic issues
  • Cooperation in an interdisciplinary team
  • Networking with external specialists

This sets you apart

  • EDK-approved training in speech therapy
  • Creativity for speech therapy with young children and commitment to parental counselling
  • Willingness to build specific early-stage expertise
  • Self-reflection and further education are an integral part of your professional practice
  • High communication and social skills
  • A human-friendly attitude, which is characterized by acceptance
  • Confidence and curiosity

You can expect a versatile field of activity with high personal responsibility in a committed team. Your careful training through professional mentoring is important to us. Fallsupervision, Intervision and further education are an integral part of our professional activities. The current employment conditions correspond to the cantonal guidelines.

The management of HPD Bachtelen, Claudia Althaus, is happy to answer your questions and accept your written application. We look forward to meeting you!
