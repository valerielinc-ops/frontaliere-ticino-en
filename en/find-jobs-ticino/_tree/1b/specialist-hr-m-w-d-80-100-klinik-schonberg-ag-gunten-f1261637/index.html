Full description

A good year ago, we began to realign our HR department. Many HR processes, standards and instruments are still under construction or are being rethought. We are therefore looking for an HR specialist who likes to be in the middle of the action: advises managers, creates orientation and binding HR standards, drives projects forward – and just as naturally takes part in the operational and administrative day-to-day business. If you like to roll up your sleeves, take responsibility and stay on with perseverance when changes take time, you will find a task with room for manoeuvre.

Your tasks

  • Responsibility for operational HR support throughout the entire employee lifecycle, including recruitment
  • Advising managers and employees on all HR issues
  • Support for demanding personnel situations (case management, conflicts, separations)
  • Participation in the new and further development of HR processes, standards, instruments and basics
  • Participation in HR projects as well as in the gradual digitalisation of HR
  • Close cooperation with Payroll and HR administration as well as active participation in day-to-day operations

Your profile

  • Commercial basic education with in-depth further education in the field of HR (HR specialist with federal technical certificate or comparable)
  • Several years of experience in a generalist HR role
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing (level C1)
  • Comprehensive knowledge of employment law and security in advising managers
  • Good understanding of payroll and HR administration processes as well as enjoyment of operational tasks
  • Enjoy working with pragmatic solutions while building new, digital HR solutions

Our offer

  • Design room for manoeuvre in the development and further development of basic HR work
  • A responsible task in the middle of the action and close to people and company (regular home office is not planned)
  • Flexible working hours that leave you space to use our special location on Lake Thun for a walk, a sporting round or a break in between
  • Opportunities for personal and professional development
  • Relaxing rest (fitness classes, free wellness offers in our partner companies and discounts on fitness subscriptions)
  • Contemporary employment conditions with progressive non-wage benefits

Interested? Then we look forward to your online application. Klinik Schönberg AG, Estelle Fuchs, Human Resources, Schönbergstrasse 40, 3654 Gunten
