University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

At the University Psychiatric Center in Bern, we look after 18 people with complex psychiatric diseases and acute stress crises. Our team specializes in the treatment of psychiatric movement disorders such as catatonia and therapy-resistant depression and has in-depth expertise in both somatic and psychiatric care. Thanks to our proximity to somatic medicine, we provide our patients with comprehensive and holistic care. As a night watch, you take on a responsible task and ensure the professional and safe care of our patients during the night. With your calm, attentive and empathetic nature, you create an atmosphere of trust and recognize changes in the psychological and somatic state at an early stage. Would you like to become part of our dedicated team and, with your expertise and empathy, also contribute to the recovery of our patients during the night?

Your tasks

  • You provide individual, professional care during the night and accompany our patients through complex recovery processes
  • In acute crisis situations, you act confidently and with empathy – your calm and professional competence make the difference
  • During the night you take responsibility for the care process and contribute significantly to the continuity in therapy
  • You bring your ideas and expertise to the further development of our care concepts – your voice counts!
  • In the interdisciplinary team, you work closely with specialists from different disciplines to achieve the best possible result for our patients.

Your profile

  • You have a diploma as a nurse HF / FH and ideally bring professional experience in the somatic and/or psychiatric care of patients or would like to continue your education in this area
  • Your empathy and high level of expertise make you a valuable support for our patients and their relatives.
  • Even in challenging situations, you remain calm and calm, show initiative and take responsibility
  • You value team collaboration and actively contribute your ideas – joint success motivates you
  • A careful, independent way of working is a matter of course for you, and you enjoy mastering everyday life with a healthy level of humor.

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
