Street warden/\-in in the tunnel cleaning team

Civil engineering office \| 80\-100% \| Sois

Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the cantonal administration Graubünden together with us.

The civil engineering office is responsible for the design, construction, maintenance and operation of the cantonal roads as well as the hydraulic engineering and slow transport in Graubünden. We also take care of the operational maintenance of the national roads on the cantonal territory. The canton’s road infrastructure comprises over 1,450 artificial buildings, including bridges at airy heights of up to 90 meters and tunnels of up to 3 kilometers in length. The challenges that the nature of our mountain canton brings in and with it makes our work demanding and versatile. As a Friendly Work Space certified office, the health and satisfaction of our approximately 480 employees are particularly important to us. For this reason, we are committed to systematic occupational health management (BGM).

We are looking for the road maintenance department in district 7 Thusis per 1\. March 2027 or by arrangement a/\-n street warden/\-in

Your responsibilities

  • Cleaning of the cantonal and national road tunnels throughout the canton (eight months of summer service)
  • Execution of road maintenance work in a small maintenance team
  • Cleaning up and maintenance after accident or natural events
  • Control of road conditions and road installations
  • Performing night service (about 8 months a year)
  • Winter piket service (about 4 months a year)

That is what we want from you.

  • Completed training in construction or as a mechanic
  • Craftsmanship and technical and mechanical knowledge
  • Readiness to perform night work and piquetting
  • Physical and psychological resilience
  • Joy of teamwork in a small group
  • Category C driving licence or willingness to acquire it

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to shape the future of the canton

Place of work

civil engineering office Graubünden, district 7 Thusis Palace Race 5 7430 Sois
