Full description

Portrait The standard school Spiez-Aeschi-Krattigen supports children with special needs in 12 school locations with about 1,350 children. We search by 16.08.2027

Tasks

Tasks according to the cantonal guide MR

  • Advising guardians and teachers on language acquisition
  • Subject-specific assessment and reporting to teachers, school management and specialist bodies.
  • Planning, implementation and reflection of needs-based, targeted special teaching for students with disorders of spoken and

written language, communication and oral motor skills

  • Speech pedagogy support and promotion of language development and communication skills by releasing blockages in language development,
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Collaboration in the College.

Requirements

Completed training in speech therapy or in studies on it

We offer

  • a temporary employment within the framework of 90 to 100 percent, the workload is split up to 2 people
  • a well-equipped and centrally located speech pediatric room
  • Cooperation with the job partner
  • a well-established and well-functioning team with two psychomotor therapists and another speech therapist on site
  • well-established organizational processes
  • Membership of the MR Spiez-Aeschi-Krattigen
