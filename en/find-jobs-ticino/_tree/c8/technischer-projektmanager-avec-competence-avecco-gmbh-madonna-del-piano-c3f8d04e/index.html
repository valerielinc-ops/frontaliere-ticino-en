Full description

You lead the execution of medium-size projects related to low- and medium-voltage transformers and reactors, ensuring compliance with quality, health, safety and environmental requirements.

Tasks

  • Develop project schedules and determine resource requirements
  • Monitor and report on project status including cost, timing and staffing
  • Ensure adherence to internal and external quality standards
  • Manage after-sales projects by fulfilling customer needs
  • Identify and resolve obstacles to completing projects on time and on budget
  • Manage multiple interrelated projects, including business transformation projects
  • Integrate vendor tasks into the project plan and track vendor deliverables

Profile

  • Electrical engineering or mechanical engineering background
  • 2-3 years of proven experience in project management with focus on transformers and reactors
  • Experience collaborating with a diverse number of stakeholders
  • Very good knowledge of English and Italian
  • Strong communication skills, self-driven and open-minded
  • Structured and organized, able to work under time pressure
  • Team player able to build and motivate a project team

Offer
