Exciting tasks await

  • Creative implementation of GIS projects focusing on nature conservation, sustainable forest management and hunting and fisheries management
  • Active co-design and further development of innovative GIS applications together with our team of experts

What you bring

  • Completed specialist or university degree in geography, geoinformatics or related fields
  • GIS experience with ESRI products is beneficial
  • Python or R knowledge is advantageous
  • Independent and exact working method with stylish expression in word and writing
  • Pleasure to be part of a small team

In the Department of Construction, Transport and Environment, we are actively developing the diverse habitat Aargau in the areas of space, energy, environment and mobility – today and for future generations. The Aargau is one of the forest-richest cantons in Switzerland. We in the Forest Department are committed to ensuring that the protection and use of the forest and wild and aquatic animals are in harmony.
