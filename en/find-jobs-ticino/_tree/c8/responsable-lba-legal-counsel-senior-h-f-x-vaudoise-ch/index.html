Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. In order to strengthen our General Secretariat Department which offers our internal units high-level legal and compliance services, we are looking for a Senior LBA & Legal Counsel Manager, between 80 and 100%. The missions that await you:

  • Pilot and develop our company's LBA system, ensuring compliance with legal and regulatory requirements (OAR-ASA), updating directives, procedures, internal controls and training programmes, while advising the units concerned and accompanying managers in implementing regulatory changes and good practices.
  • Monitor legal and regulatory developments in the fight against money laundering, assess their impact on our activities and guide their integration into LBA processes, tools and activities, as well as represent our company in the ad hoc committees of the ASA.
  • Conduct the necessary analysis and measures to combat money-laundering, particularly in sensitive or complex situations, including the preparation of cases which require it for validation by senior management and the submission of communications to competent authorities (MROS), where legal conditions are met.
  • Chapter audits and controls related to LBA themes, ensure interactions with internal and external oversight bodies, oversee implementation of recommendations and ensure follow-up of corrective actions.
  • Provide legal advice and support to business lines, in particular in the development of new foresight solutions, in the drafting and negotiation of contracts with partners and suppliers in such projects, as well as on an ad hoc basis in legal matters related to the insurance activities of non-life persons.
  • Ensure regular reporting to the LBA Audit and Risk Directorate and Committee, presenting risks, regulatory developments, compliance indicators, reporting on the operations of the period under review, and topics requiring special attention from a governance perspective. Your profile:
  • Master in Swiss law, post-graduate training in LBA and/or insurance compliance an asset
  • Professional experience of at least 5 years in managing LBA devices, preferably in a Swiss insurance company
  • Evidence of knowledge and experience in Swiss regulation against money laundering (OAR-ASA) in insurance with savings component (Life Insurance and Provident Solutions) and in essential mortgage loans
  • French or German mother tongue with excellent second language proficiency (min. level C1)
  • Confirmed knowledge of non-life insurance in addition, an asset
  • Ability to work and deliver independently, while demonstrating team spirit You are also pragmatic, solutions oriented and comfortable in your contacts with your colleagues and external stakeholders We offer you :
  • A healthy working environment in a mutualist company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • Multiple social benefits, including flexibility in work organisation
  • Support in development and training
  • Human values focused on the well-being of employees We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
