You have your specialist title in your pocket and feel that it is time for the next step, but not at the expense of your nights? And you want to live radiology in its entirety, instead of committing to a single organ system early on? Then this senior medical office might suit you exactly. In a regional acute care hospital in the Swiss Mittelland you have found the entire spectrum from CT and MRI to ultrasound and are in direct contact with the clinics. In the evening you go home, because at night the teleradiology of a university hospital takes over. An experienced squad will give you support, especially if it is your first senior doctor position. Have you become curious? Just write me via WhatsApp on +41 31 310 98 74. The acute care hospital has around 130 beds and offers comprehensive medical care in the Swiss Mittelland. Radiology is closely involved in clinical care and works directly with the departments of the house as well as with external referees. The institute is modernly equipped with two 1.5 Tesla MRI systems including Deep Resolve Boost, two CT devices, including dual energy technology, as well as conventional radiology, ultrasound, mammography and fluoroscopy. In addition, AI-based findings support is used.

Their tasks

  • Independent assessment of radiological examinations in MRI, CT, ultrasound, mammography, fluoroscopy and conventional radiology
  • Clinically oriented radiological assessment of outpatient and inpatient patients
  • Participation in interdisciplinary reports, case discussions and clinical decision-making processes
  • Direct professional exchange with clinical departments and external assignees
  • Supervision and professional support of assistant doctors in day-to-day training
  • Participation in internal training as well as in selected specialist and public lectures

Your Competences

  • Radiology, German or EU-recognized (we support you in recognition in Switzerland)
  • Delight in diagnostic radiology with a broad clinical reference
  • Interest in interdisciplinary cooperation and patient-related assessment
  • Willingness to accompany and further develop assistant doctors
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Team-oriented personality who likes to actively participate in the day-to-day work of the institute
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing

Their advantages

  • Broad radiological spectrum with MRI, CT, ultrasound, mammography, fluoroscopy and conventional diagnostics
  • Modern infrastructure with two 1.5 Tesla MRI systems and CT diagnostics including dual energy technology
  • Recognized training institution of category B with established training concept
  • Collegial and committed team with pleasure in teaching and professional exchange
  • Support for congresses and radiological events
  • Well-organized service model with nocturnal teleradiological support by a university center
  • Manageable hospital environment with short distances and direct clinical cooperation
  • Central location in the Swiss Mittelland with easy accessibility towards Bern, Basel, Zurich and Lucerne
