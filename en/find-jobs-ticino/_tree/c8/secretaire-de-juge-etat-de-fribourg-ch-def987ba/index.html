Full description

Who are we?

Justice of the Peace is the Authority for the Protection of Children and Adults. As such, it acts on request or ex officio. At the end of the investigation, it is decided whether or not to introduce a protective measure. The Judge of the Peace shall also have jurisdiction in matters of inheritance and in the area of layoffs.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Maintenance and monitoring of the Judge's agenda, contacts with Justice of the Peace partners, including information and appointments, support to the Registry
  • Taking audition VP on the fly and under dictation
  • General correspondence, citations, opening of files and maintenance of digital and physical records
  • Notification of court decisions
  • Telephone and counter answering, general information to third parties
  • File archiving

Profile desired

  • SWC business employee or experience deemed equivalent
  • Several years of professional experience
  • Flexibility, responsibility and discretion
  • Excellent mastery of IT tools (MS-Office) and office automation
  • Ease of contact and teamwork
  • Ability to work under pressure
  • Mastery of the French or German language with excellent knowledge of the other language (min. B2)
