MUNICIPALITY OF TERRE DI PEDEMONTE TEGNA - V RSC O - CAV OLIANO COMPETITION FOR THE HIRING OF A MUNICIPAL WORKER WITH 100% EMPLOYMENT The Municipality of Terre di Pedemonte opens the competition for the appointment of a municipal worker (general or qualified), with a 100% employment rate. Workplace: the entire municipal area (fractions of Tegna, Verscio and Cavigliano)

Duties and tasks

. execution of the tasks assigned within the external service activity of the municipal technical office; . maintenance and care of greenery; . various seasonal jobs; . small maintenance of work equipment and machines; . care and maintenance of municipal infrastructure and buildings; . winter service; . availability to carry out activities even outside normal working hours and on holidays if necessary; . everything not contained in these duties, but which will be deemed appropriate by the Municipality.

Requirements

Those provided for by the Employees' Organic Regulations (hereinafter called ROD) and in particular:

a) impeccable moral conduct; b) healthy physical constitution and predisposition to carry out outdoor work; c) if general worker: demonstrated experience in various sectors; if qualified worker: federal certificate of professional capacity; d) driving license, cat. B; e) aptitude for independent and team work, organizational skills, ease and friendliness in contacts with citizens, sense of responsibility, availability and flexibility on a personal level and in relation to working hours; f) availability to follow refresher and specialization courses; g) Swiss citizenship, subject to the application of international agreements; h) domicile preferably in the Municipality of Terre di Pedemonte

For newly hired candidates with little professional experience, the Municipality may establish a 5% reduction in salary compared to that expected for the function for a period of two years maximum.

Trial period and cancellation

The first year of employment is considered a probationary period. During this period the relationship can be terminated by either party at the end of each month with thirty days' notice. The Municipality has the right to extend the probationary period to a maximum of two years.

Entry into service

As soon as possible, or on a date to be agreed.

Required documents

Each application must be accompanied by the following documents:

. application letter. CV with recent photograph; diplomas and study and work certificates, references; . extract from the criminal record; . self-certification on the state of health (form downloadable from the website www.pedemonte.ch). photocopy of the driving license est. B

Competition methods

The offers, in a sealed envelope with the external wording "Municipal worker competition" and accompanied by the required documents must reach the Municipal Registry of the Municipality of Terre di Pedemonte, PiazzaDon Gottardo Zurini 2, 6652 Tegna, by 5.00 pm on Thursday 5 November 2026. The Municipality reserves the right to request further documents or information from the candidates during the examination of the applications and/or at the time of hiring, as well as call them to interviews or subject them to a test. Offers that reach the Registry after the date and time indicated will not be considered and we reserve the right not to take a decision if there is no candidate deemed suitable. The municipal secretary is available for any further information (091 785 60 05).
