Technical maintenance employees – logistics facilities (a) As a nationally and internationally oriented transport and logistics company, Galliker Transport AG concentrates on its core areas of car, cargo and food logistics. The Altishofen LU Logistics Park is the hub of the national and international Galliker network. We offer comprehensive storage, picking and packaging services for our customers. In various logistics centers are located logistics facilities such as pallet lifts, automatic high-bay warehouses, conveying techniques, etc. ... To complement the technical team responsible for the maintenance, maintenance and support of the logistics facilities, we are looking for an employee for the technical maintenance of the logistics facilities immediately or by agreement. Task area

  • assist in ensuring and ensuring optimum functionality of conveyor systems, lifting or door systems of automatic bearings
  • Carrying out inspection and repair work
  • Carrying out maintenance work on storage and conveying equipment
  • Resolving malfunctions, breakdowns, operational interruptions
  • Participate in the regular pikettdienst (every 4-8 weeks)
  • Maintain/operate the associated systems (warehouse management, material flow calculator, visualizations, etc.)

Requirement profile

  • Basic training or in-depth experience in the field of electrical / electromechanics
  • Good IT user knowledge (office programs, possibly already knowledge with material flow and warehouse management systems)
  • Knowledge of conveying technology and material flow systems is advantageous
  • Good technical understanding
  • Approval Art. 15 NIV or higher, or the willingness to complete it
  • independent, capable of teamwork, balanced, helpful and flexible

Offer

  • top modern infrastructure
  • a diverse, varied and interesting area of responsibility
  • Modern break/canteen areas
  • internal and external training opportunities

The area of responsibility includes activities in the electrical, mechanical and system engineering sectors. Did we get your interest? We look forward to your application. Elisha Affentranger, Human Resources, telephone: +41 62 748 80 70.
