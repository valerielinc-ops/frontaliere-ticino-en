Full description

Are you looking for a challenge in the field of politics and administration, with an employer with topics as diverse as life? Here in the General Secretariat of the Directorate of Justice and Home Affairs, the strings converge. We support the directorate and the offices with a wide range of services, process legal remedies and advise other directorates on draft laws. We are looking for a lawyer in the legal service for twelve months. After six months as an auditor, there is the possibility of working as a legal secretary until the end of the employment if there is a good performance. Tasks

  • Processing of appeals against orders of our administrative units (in particular in prison, name, civil rights and civil status) as well as supervisory complaints
  • Treatment of political advances from the Cantonal Council
  • Participation in the specialist areas of the legal service (including political rights, remedies and supervision) as well as in the legislative service (legislative examination of decrees)
  • Participation in projects (e.g. in the areas of data protection, municipalities 2030 or discrimination protection) Profile
  • Completed Law Studies (MLaw)
  • First professional experience after graduation (e.g. a court or lawyer internship)
  • Ability to quickly capture complex questions and work independently
  • Enjoyment of state and administrative law as well as interest in political contexts
  • Communicative personality as well as committed, careful and structured working methods
  • Interested in a career in cantonal administration What awaits you with us
  • We offer you a versatile and challenging job in a motivated team with creative leeway at the pulse of society.
  • We give you a good picture of the societal function of law, legislative procedure, administrative recourse procedure, administration and politics.
  • You will gain practical experience in the field of state, administrative and public procedural law as well as in selected areas of civil and criminal law, which will be useful for a further career in cantonal administration and also for the bar examination.
  • We give you a suitable introduction to the cantonal administration: First you will be introduced to the tasks and have the opportunity to take on more responsibility after a six-month internship.
  • We attach importance to flat hierarchies and flexible home office regulations.
