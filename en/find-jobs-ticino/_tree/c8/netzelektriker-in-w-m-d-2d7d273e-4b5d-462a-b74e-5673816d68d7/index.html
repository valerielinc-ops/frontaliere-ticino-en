Full description

Pensum: 80-100 %

You can design it.

  • Together with your team, you expand the distribution network.
  • You independently carry out maintenance work on your own and third-party plants and thus ensure a secure power supply.
  • You reliably repair network interruptions as part of the Picetting and Disruption Service and keep a cool head.
  • You actively introduce improvements and implement measures to increase the efficiency and security of the network facilities.

This is what makes you

  • You are a trained grid electrician or grid electrician EFZ with experience in line construction.
  • You have knowledge in network construction and can work independently as well as in a team.
  • You are communicative and a real team player who feels just as comfortable in the exchange with the team as in dealing with the customer.
  • Ideally, you have a category B vehicle ID so that you can be flexible on the road with our business vehicles.

07.00 – Knowledge exchange 08.30 – Distribution network expand 13.20 – Disruption service 15.00 – Maintaining distribution network

  • 07:00 – Exchange of knowledge: Your working day starts together with the team. In the morning meeting, you will discuss the upcoming tasks, plan the distribution of the work and clarify important details. Then you equip the necessary material and make your way to the construction site.
  • 8:30 a.m. – Expand distribution network: Together with your team, you expand the existing distribution network on site, install cable lines and commission a new transformer station to secure the power supply for a new residential quarter.
  • 1:20 p.m. – Disruption service: A call reaches you: In a nearby community, an excavator has damaged a cable. You drive right to the site, analyze the situation and take measures to repair damage and ensure the stability of the power supply.
  • 3 p.m. – Maintain distribution network: On the construction site, the work is done and you still carry out the maintenance of the distribution boxes. Back in the workshop, the material is unloaded, cleaned and old cables are disposed of. On the last day of the week, you and the team will end the day in a relaxed round.
