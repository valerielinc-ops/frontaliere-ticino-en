Full description

Forel Klinik is one of the leading addiction clinics in Switzerland and specializes in the treatment of people with alcohol or drug dependence. At two locations, more than 180 employees provide support for those affected on their personal path to a better quality of life and self-determination. We offer individually tailored therapy concepts in the inpatient, daily clinical and outpatient setting. Our addiction treatment combines evidence-based methods with a value-oriented attitude – with the aim of enabling sustainable and effective change. As an addiction specialist clinic, it is anchored in our DNA to open up prospects and contribute to the development of people. This principle also shapes our dealings with employees. We are looking for you by November 2026 or by appointment for our inpatient clinic in Ellikon a.d. Thur. Psychotherapist/-in 80% (m/f/d) Create with us paths that work. With us, you work in an environment that combines therapeutic diversity, professional reputation and the space for careful relationship design. As part of an interdisciplinary team, you contribute to ensuring that our treatment concepts have a lasting effect and are constantly evolving – for patients, their environment and society.

Their tasks

  • Case-leading psychotherapeutic treatment of inpatients
  • Development of therapy goals and plans, implementation and evaluation of psychotherapeutic interventions in individual, couple or family discussions
  • Management of inpatient group therapies
  • Conduct of consultations and reporting
  • Site discussions with employers and, if necessary, with other third parties or institutions involved
  • Data collection for diagnosis and medical history management
  • Participation in concept groups

Your profile

Prerequisite is a completed psychology study incl. Evidence of clinical psychology with advanced or completed psychotherapy training. Experience in the treatment of addiction diseases is advantageous.

Our offer

With this position, we offer you responsible tasks in a motivated, modern and well-connected environment. Your professional and personal development is a concern for us, which we support generously and specifically with structured further education and training offers.

The working conditions are contemporary and appealing

Reliable service planning in a stable environment

  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • 60 % employer contribution to the pension fund
  • 100 % employer contribution to KTG insurance

Your place of work: Our stationary clinic in Ellikon an der Thur is idyllic in the countryside and at the same time offers good accessibility from the Zurich, Winterthur and Eastern Switzerland region and the German border area. Further advantages of the place of work are the in-house gastronomy and sufficient free parking. Our offer is aimed at people of all genders and identities. Further information can be provided by Andreas Müller, Chief Physician, Tel. +41 52 369 11 31. We look forward to your application directly on our homepage.
