Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

As a versatile peripheral hospital, we are characterised by a low-threshold and family working atmosphere. Our employees also report very good development opportunities and a good skill & grade mix in the departments. Lean management is not a foreign word for us. Family-friendly conditions round off our profile. We attach particular importance to autonomous service planning, in which the team creates the service plan together. For our interdisciplinary private department with integrated specialised inpatient palliative care at the Burgdorf location, which has 24 beds and comprises approximately 46 employees as well as students and learners, we are looking for the first time. December 2026 or by agreement

Your tasks – versatile and responsible

  • You care for and look after our patients independently, professionally and according to current nursing knowledge
  • As head of a care group, you work closely with health care professionals, nursing assistants and students and learners.
  • You control the care process, including the performance of materials and care, securely, supported by our clinic information system, and are committed to the ongoing optimization of care processes
  • Accompanied by students and learners, you pass on your knowledge and promote young professionals

Your profile – professionally experienced and humanly convincing

  • You have a completed education as a dipl. Nurse (AKP, DN II, HF, FH), ideally with professional experience in acute care
  • The wide medical spectrum of a family peripheral hospital is exciting and enriching
  • The well-being of patients is at the heart of your work and you meet people with respect and appreciation
  • You work reliably and conscientiously and like to take responsibility
  • Your professional and personal development is important to you
  • A stylish German rounds off your profile (for foreign-language diploma level B2 is required)
