Full description

  • With her projects and offers, Caritas Bern is committed to fighting poverty and social exclusion, promoting integration and standing up for people. To this end, it cooperates with different partners and fulfils public mandates. Engage with us for a solidarity and respectful coexistence as

Intercultural interpreters

For interpreting on site, the interpreting service Comprendi immediately or by agreement for the language SOMALI and UKRAINI They form a language bridge with the necessary cultural and social background in communication between migrants and public authorities in the health, social and educational sectors in the Canton of Bern.

Their tasks

As an intercultural interpreter, you are responsible for ensuring that correct and complete interpretation is possible between interlocutors from different backgrounds. You pay attention to the cultural, ethical and social backgrounds, with the aim of facilitating communication and equal access to services.

Our requirements

  • Your mother tongue is Somali or Ukrainian
  • You are at least 25 years old
  • You have very good knowledge of German (level C1, at least level B2 of the European reference system)
  • have completed vocational training and good general education;
  • Ideally, you already have experience in oral interpretation
  • You have the certificate INTERPRET or are interested in acquiring it
  • You live in the canton of Bern and have access to Internet / e-mail and have a mobile phone and PC / laptop
  • Your strengths include responsibility, reliability, punctuality, flexibility, discretion and good administrative skills

We offer

  • Issuance of an employment contract (assignments on request, no entitlement to assignments)
  • Flexible application options on request for intercultural interpretation on site and on the phone
  • Contemporary remuneration, social benefits and expenses
  • Information and exchange meetings, internal training and supervision

Further information about Comprendi can be found at the interpreting service Comprendi Caritas Bern. If you have any questions, Mrs. L. Zbinden is at your disposal (031 378 60 52).

We thank you for your interest and look forward to receiving your application via the online portal www.caritas-bern.ch or at the following email address: l.zbinden@caritas-bern.ch
