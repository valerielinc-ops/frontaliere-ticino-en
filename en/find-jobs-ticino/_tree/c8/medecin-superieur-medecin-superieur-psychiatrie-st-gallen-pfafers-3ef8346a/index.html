  • The focus of your work is stationary care, where you take over the professional management of the interdisciplinary team in partnership dual management together with the nursing station management.

  • The assigned assistant physicians, assistant psychologists and assistant psychologists accompany and promote you with structured further education and regular supervision.
  • Together with the senior medical team and the nursing management, you will continuously develop the concepts and the orientation of the station. Your profile
  • As a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy or shortly before completing further education, you will bring the professional basis for this position.
  • A valid professional training permit from the Canton of St. Gallen is required for your work or you are ready to acquire it before starting your job.
  • You appreciate the inpatient work with mentally ill patients as well as the close cooperation in an interdisciplinary team at eye level and live a recovery-oriented attitude.
  • With your independent and responsible way of working, you make safe decisions and at the same time convince through flexibility, team spirit and communication strength.
  • Your readiness for continuous development and very good knowledge of German at at least C1 level round off your profile. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. We are on the road digitally and are committed to sustainability, so we look forward to your online application via our homepage. As soon as your application has been received by us online, you will receive a confirmation of receipt by e-mail. We are happy to get to know you based on your dossier and check whether your qualifications, skills and you as a person fit the advertised position and the psychiatry St.Gallen. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also get the opportunity to get to know your future team. You will receive a telephone confirmation from us if we decide for you after the second interview and ask you if you are still convinced of us. If we agree, we will draw up your contract. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. So you can get a differentiated picture of the company and already prepare for the job. On the first day you can expect an onboarding in your new team and with other colleagues. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also get your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
