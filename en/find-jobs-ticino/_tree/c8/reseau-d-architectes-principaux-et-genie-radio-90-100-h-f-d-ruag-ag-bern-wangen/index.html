Full description

Responsibility

  • Architecture lead over the entire project life cycle of several Switzerland-wide broadband and radio stations
  • Design, Implementation and Documentation of New Communications Network for Army, Confederation and Blue Light Organizations
  • Creation of new network concepts and further development of existing network concepts with focus on stability, security and long-term operation
  • Configuration, commissioning and testing of network components in projects and operating environments
  • Maintenance and upgrades of existing customer networks
  • Analysis and resolution of complex network and security issues in the 3rd-level environment
  • Technical support for project managers in pre-sales, architectural issues, cost estimation and tender preparation

Requirements

  • Completed tertiary level technical training (HF, BSc, MSc or MAS) in engineering or equivalent qualification
  • In-depth experience with TCP/IP, routing and communications security technologies
  • Practice with network solutions from manufacturers such as Cisco, Juniper or Checkpoint
  • Further training in the field of IP communications is advantageous, for example Cisco certifications
  • Experience in directional radio or interest in getting involved in this field
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, good knowledge of English in word and writing
  • Motivated to learn new things
