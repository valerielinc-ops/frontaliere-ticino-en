Outpatient psychiatry with room for manoeuvre: You are responsible for the diagnosis and treatment of adults into old age, shape the psychiatric offer and pass on your knowledge to the next generation. If you wish, you will acquire the main title of age psychiatry entirely on site – at an academic clinic near Basel. Her employer is a large public psychiatric institution in northwestern Switzerland. She treats adults as well as children and adolescents on an outpatient, daily clinical, inpatient and home treatment. Adult psychiatry has several areas of expertise, including age psychiatry, crisis intervention, addiction disorders and psychotherapy and psychosocial therapies. The institution is a certified continuing education institution of the highest category. The ambulatory works in social psychiatry and general psychiatry, with an emphasis in age psychiatry. It has the continuing education recognition of the highest category A for the focus title of age psychiatry and psychotherapy. Her place of work is a modern campus in the countryside, in a small town in the agglomeration of Basel. With the S-Bahn you are in Basel in about a quarter of an hour. A car park for employees is directly on campus.

Their tasks

  • They carry the specialist responsibility for diagnosis and treatment in the ambulatory setting, general psychiatric and age psychiatric
  • They assess psychiatric, somatic and psychosocial issues holistically and control individual treatment concepts with the aim of maintaining independence and quality of life as long as possible.
  • They work closely with relatives, family doctors, institutions and authorities
  • They actively develop modern psychiatric and psychiatric treatment offers and promote interprofessional cooperation.
  • They train assistant doctors, assistant psychologists and medical students

Your Competences

  • Specialist title Psychiatry and psychotherapy or in the last year of training
  • Enjoying the outpatient treatment of people from 18 years to old age and networked cooperation
  • Independent, structured working method, high social competence as well as managerial experience or interest in professional development
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Foreign diploma? Recognition via MEBEKO is a prerequisite. You will be supported organizationally and financially by us

Their advantages

  • Additional title on site: If you are interested, you can acquire the focus title of old-age psychiatry and psychotherapy completely at your own workplace, at a category A training institution.
  • Academic environment: Teaching at the University of Basel and further training of young medical and psychological researchers
  • Transparent salary: comprehensible wage system with defined wage bands, paid out in 13 monthly wages, with regular examination of equal pay
  • Family and professional life: 16 weeks of full pay maternity leave, 10 days of paternity leave, up to 12 months of unpaid parental leave, discounted day-care places and an education allowance in addition to the child allowance
  • Strong protection: 100% wage in the event of illness or accident up to 730 days, own pension fund in a cantonal pension fund
  • Rest: 25 days of leave (from 50 years of age 27 days), after the third year of service two additional days off, plus eleven holidays
  • Location: modern campus in the green, very easy to reach with public transport, Basel in about 15 minutes
