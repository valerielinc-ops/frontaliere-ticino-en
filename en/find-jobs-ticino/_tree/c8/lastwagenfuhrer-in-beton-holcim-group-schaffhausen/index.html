Full description

Sustainability drives us. You too?

If you want to make a difference, you are right with us.

“Hey you!” We as truck drivers: inside have top conditions at Holcim: the wage is good and here is really looking at my safety! At Holcim I feel valued and through training I can constantly develop. Another plus is our high-quality vehicles, which enable efficient, safe and sustainable driving. Here I not only ride, but represent the Holcim to our customers: inside and on the roads. - Christian, Chauffeur at Holcim (Switzerland) AG

What to expect

  • You ensure reliable delivery in the company’s own, well-kept vehicles
  • You represent your new employer with customers: inside and on the street
  • You take care of a professional, environmentally friendly and prudent operation of our vehicles
  • You are responsible for checking the delivery notes after loading
  • Also for smaller maintenance and maintenance work your participation is required (vehicle & plant)

What you bring

  • You have completed vocational training and a valid driving licence of category C
  • Through your experience as a truck driver: you bring a high level of reliability and stress resistance
  • Safety is your top priority and new challenges spur you
  • You know the region well and bring technical interest
  • You work independently and like to take on responsibility

Our offer

  • Onboarding & getting started: Look forward to a team-oriented, open and future-oriented working environment. We accompany you from the beginning with a careful training, practical and structured, so that you can start successfully.
  • Career & Development: We create opportunities. With us, you are at the center as a person with your individual strengths and potential. Anyone who wants to is supported by us and can achieve a lot.
  • Appreciation & Team Culture: With us, you are part of a strong and inclusive team that holds together, shares ideas and masters challenges together. A trusting leadership culture ensures that your commitment is seen and appreciated.
  • Flexibility & Work-Life-Balance: We offer flexible working hours, 41 hours, part-time opportunities, 25-30 days holidays, holiday purchase, extended maternity and paternity leave
  • Attractive compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional benefits and a very good pension fund
  • Employee benefits: From Reka checks to shares at a preferential price to the Swibeco advantage world - we make sure that you feel comfortable and benefit from attractive additional services
