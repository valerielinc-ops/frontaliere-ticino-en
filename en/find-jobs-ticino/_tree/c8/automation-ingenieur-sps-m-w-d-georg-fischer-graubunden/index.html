Full description

Our automation team in our modern plant in the German town of Seewis continues to grow and we are looking for a committed reinforcement immediately. This automation position, which focuses on PLC programming, is ideal for a person who values a complex, technological environment and has the ambition to make good things even better. It is an extremely varied activity with a high level of personal responsibility and the opportunity to contribute significantly to the success of our work.

Their tasks

  • Programming of PLC-controlled test and assembly systems as well as robot controllers – in compliance with the applicable SEV and SUVA safety regulations
  • Construction and commissioning of new and converted facilities
  • Troubleshooting and inspection, maintenance and repair of automated equipment and robotic systems
  • Careful documentation of work steps and activities
  • Identification and implementation of PLC programming optimizations
  • Participation in automation projects

Your profile

  • Completed apprenticeship as an automatist, further education, e.g. HF of advantage
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in a comparable function
  • In-depth knowledge of PLC programming, ideally complemented by robotics experience
  • Independent, reliable and quality-conscious working method
  • Persuasiveness and ability to communicate efficiently (with production, logistics, external suppliers, etc.)
  • Safe handling of MS Office

Candidate dossiers of deliberations are not considered. Interested people will give to apply directly, thanks!
