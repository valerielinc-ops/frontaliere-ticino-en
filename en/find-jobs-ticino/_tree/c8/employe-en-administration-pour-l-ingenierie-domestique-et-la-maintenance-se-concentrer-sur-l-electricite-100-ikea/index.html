Full description

Who you are

As a facility employee, you are part of the facility management team, which offers employees, visitors and customers a sustainable, safe, clean and functional environment. The facility services include all general building maintenance and operational services, mostly administrative effort and daily troubleshooting.

  • You have advantageously completed the technical merchant and bring great interest for administrative activities.
  • You have good written and oral communication skills in the German language and have basic knowledge of English.
  • You work independently and goal-oriented, alone or in a team.
  • You are able to deal with stressful situations and can preventively detect critical events.
  • You have professional experience in a similar position and are familiar with electrical systems and installations, ideally already meet the approval requirements according to NIV Art. 13 or Art. 15.
  • You don’t mind working in the Picettdienst and you have a driver’s license.

You are responsible for

  • Responsibility for general administrative activities (orders, invoices, offers & cost comparison, monitoring of projects).
  • The daily execution of the house technology routines on site (inspection, inspection and maintenance, repairs and improvements)
  • The plants life cycle management
  • Obtaining and checking offers, order processing, cost control
  • Communication, coordination and control of external service providers
  • Recording and reporting of findings, risks or damage
  • The independent execution of repairs to facilities and buildings
  • The support and control of construction and technical projects as well as general maintenance work.
