Full description

Dipl. Nursing professional 40-100 %

Clinic of Pneumology / Sleep Medicine

Place of work St. Gallen

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Holistic, professional care and care of patients with acute and chronic pneumological diseases in close cooperation with the non-invasive ventilation team
  • Implementation and consideration of modern, evidence-based treatment concepts of clinics as well as the reference care concept
  • Promotion, guidance and accompaniment of apprentices in the company’s various training programmes, in the function of day-care provider
  • Active participation in shaping an appreciative, positive team and clinic culture as well as constructive cooperation in an interprofessional environment
  • Interesting, versatile and challenging field of activity in the pneumological department with a 24-bed station

What you bring for this job

  • Completed training as a dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH or a diploma recognised in Switzerland
  • Sound nursing knowledge and interest in professional and personal development
  • High quality and responsibility awareness
  • Strong social skills and commitment to constructive, interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • Flexible, initiative personality with good organizational ability and clear priorities

Our offer to you

  • A dedicated and enduring team where collaboration and appreciation are lived
  • Comprehensive and structured introduction concept for an optimal start
  • Close interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation in a highly specialized environment
  • Wide range of professional and personal training opportunities
  • Various employee offers in the areas of health promotion, job and family, mobility and attractive benefits
