Your responsibilities

  • Perform the final testing of EDM machines before shipment, with geometric, mechanical, electrical and functional checks according to test protocols.
  • Do machining tests to verify machine performance and compliance with customer specifications and requirements.
  • Locate and analyze non-compliance, perform adjustments and small corrective interventions and document results in testing reports.
  • Working with Assembly, Quality and R\&D to solve technical problems and contribute to the continuous improvement of the product and processes.
  • Support acceptance testing in the presence of customers and prepare shipping machines.

Your profile

  • Federal capacity certificate (AFC) as polymechanic, mechatronic, electronic or equivalent technical training.
  • Experience in testing, assembly or maintenance of machine tools; the knowledge of electro-erosion is a plus.
  • Ability to read technical drawings and electrical schematics and use measuring instruments.
  • Precise, reliable and quality oriented person, with team spirit.
  • Italian fluent; knowledge of English is an advantage.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
