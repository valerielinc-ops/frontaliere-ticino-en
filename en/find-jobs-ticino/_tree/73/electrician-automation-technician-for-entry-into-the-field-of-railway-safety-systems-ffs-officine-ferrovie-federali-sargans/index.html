Electrician:in, Automatic:in for entry into the field of railway safety systems 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Electrician:in, Automatic:in for entry into the field of railway safety systems Sargans, as of now or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You are an electrical installer with body and soul: in or automatic operator: in and want to take responsibility in the area of safety systems. In an innovative environment, you and your team will advance the digital development of the infrastructure and make a visible contribution to the safety and punctuality of trains. Together, you develop practical solutions for current challenges and implement them in a team. We support your professional development with internal training and further education, including the two-year training as a safety system technician:in. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. Night and weekend work is also paid for, for example, an hourly wage supplement of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night assignments, you get time surcharges of 10 to 30%, which allows you more free time. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. Interested in learning more about our professions? We look forward to seeing you from 22 to 24. 2026 in our SBB Berufswelten Café at Zurich Central Station. All information can be found at sbb.ch/berufsweltencafe. You can move that.

  • You supervise the construction, maintenance and maintenance of security systems and thus ensure safe and punctual rail traffic.
  • You ensure the removal and commissioning of electronic signal boxes and relay signal boxes.
  • With your systematic approach, you identify irregularities and deficiencies, report them and drive the correction and improvements in the team.
  • In the Sargans area, you will be working at various indoor and outdoor locations and will experience a varied daily work experience.
  • You will complete an internal two-year training as a safety system technician and learn to independently locate and correct disturbances on indoor systems.
  • In the Pikettdienst you locate and repair disturbances to security systems independently.

You bring that with you.

  • You enjoy working in a team, taking on responsibility and communicating clearly and solution-oriented.
  • Security is your priority and you approach tasks systematically to solve problems in a structured way.
  • You have completed basic training with EFZ in an electrotechnical profession such as automatics, electromechanics or electrical assembly or a comparable qualification.
  • For use in shift operation, you bring the necessary endurance. You are attentive, focused and physically resilient to successfully handle this demanding work in 24-hour shift operations. You also like to work outside in any weather.
  • In order to be able to perform piquetting service, you have a category B driver’s license. You bring your German language skills to level B2 in word and writing.
