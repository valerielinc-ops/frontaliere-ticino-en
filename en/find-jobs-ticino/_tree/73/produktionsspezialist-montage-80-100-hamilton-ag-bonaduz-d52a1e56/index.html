Full description

Production specialist Assembly 80 \- 100% (m/f/d) True to the vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative pioneer in terms of ventilators, automated pipetting and storage of samples and the development of process sensors. The Operations business unit produces parts for all Hamilton companies from injection moulded parts and pipette tips for liquid handling to metal and plastic parts. Our goal is to produce as many parts as possible in-house. In this way, we remain agile, independent and efficient.

You can do this

  • Analysis and correction of disruptions in the production environment

  • Setting up and supporting production lines
  • Cooperation in improvement projects to increase quality, safety and efficiency
  • Participation in the introduction of new products into production
  • Support for the preparation and updating of work instructions and technical documentation

  • Support for Lean and CIP activities
