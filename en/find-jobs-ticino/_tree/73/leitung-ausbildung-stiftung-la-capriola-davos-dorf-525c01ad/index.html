Head training (60 \- 100%)

To complete our team, from February 2027 or by arrangement we will search for one for our location in Davos.

Director training (60 \- 100%) (for basic vocational training in hospitality and catering)

What to expect from us

  • Unique training concept with individual support in the first job market
  • Teamwork with active participation in exciting projects and project weeks
  • Responsible varied task where your ideas are in demand
  • Cooperation and promotion of young people with special requirements
  • regular training and training opportunities
  • the allocation of percentages shall be determined jointly at the time of employment

As part of your responsibility:

  • the promotion of learners in the workplace (hotel) in all hospitality sectors, with affinity to the hospitality industry
  • Practical and theoretical support
  • the overall planning of training;
  • Administrative tasks and coordination with hotel staff
  • Cooperation with the socio-educational living area

You bring with you

  • a correspondingly completed training with a wide range of professional experiences
  • have further expertise in other areas of gastronomy/hotelry
  • with your patient and responsible and your experience can challenge, consistently promote and advance the learners
  • ideally have a completed hotel school
