Full description

With its comprehensive range of services, our hospital provides the ambulatory and inpatient extended basic care for the canton of Uri more securely and is also one of the four largest employers in the region. The hospital employs around 655 people and trains around 80 learners and students. Wouldn't that be something for you? Your education is important to us

  • That is why you have the opportunity to start training four times a year.
  • Dedicated professional trainers who accompany you individually and competently through the training.
  • You can expect a comprehensive range of services at our interdisciplinary stations.
  • We organize a wide range of learning opportunities for you with insights into various subject areas.
  • We offer you a field of work in which you can learn and achieve all qualification goals.
  • We plan 1:1 learning accompaniments in which you are the focus.
  • You can participate in internal training and transfer lessons. Requirements
  • As a specialist in health (FAGE) EFZ you can start with the two-year, shortened training.
  • With a completed education at secondary level 2 or a vocational training EFZ you choose the three-year education.
  • Further professional requirements and information relevant for training can be found on the XUND homepage www.xund.ch The education center XUND, our training partner is located in Lucerne where you visit the modular school blocks. You will be invited by us to a one-day selection process or receive a reasoned rejection.We would very much welcome the addition of a multicheck for nurse HF or if necessary also request it. If questions arise about the training, the practice teacher Pflege Ursula Kruse is at your disposal. We look forward to your online application.

Requirements

  • Requirements - As a health specialist (FAGE) EFZ, you can start with the two-year, shortened training.

  • Option to start with the shortened two-year training if holding a FAGE EFZ qualification

  • Three-year training program with completed secondary level 2 education or EFZ vocational training

  • Specific requirements and relevant information available at www.xund.ch

  • The XUND education center is located in Lucerne

  • Attendance of modular school blocks at XUND in Lucerne
