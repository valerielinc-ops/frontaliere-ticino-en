The Position As an automatic / electrician (m/f), you work in the area of "Solids & Antibiotics" at the Kaiseraugst location.

The Electrician Group Solida is responsible for innovative, cost-effective and GMP-compliant planning and realization of investment projects at the Kaiseraugst site. In addition, the division makes the building and operations engineering, the technical quality management and is responsible for reliable troubleshooting and maintenance, including the operation of the necessary infrastructure. We support our customers in biotechnological, chemical and galenical production plants, laboratory buildings and administration buildings.

The department is responsible for ensuring the operation of buildings and providing services in the maintenance area of pharmaceutical, chemical, research and infrastructure facilities.

As a competent automatist / electrician (m/f) in the electrical & automation workshop, you are part of a dedicated team that supports plant operators in the maintenance and operation of galenic production plants and robotics. The extensive area of responsibility requires broad expertise for production facilities in the GMP environment.

Their use is intended for tableting, filling and packaging lines and production buildings. Maintenance work on these plants is also part of the work area. The planned maintenance is intended to ensure the most uninterrupted possible operation. Troubleshooting must be performed throughout the electrical and automation sector. Readiness for education and training is a prerequisite for understanding and supporting new technologies. The team is an interdisciplinary group consisting of automation and electrical specialists.

Its tasks include the following activities

  • Maintenance and troubleshooting of filling, packaging and tableting plants in the galenic production buildings.
  • In normal operation as well as in the event of a malfunction, you support operators in complex tasks or issues in the electrical & automation sector and make a significant contribution to ensuring that the systems reach a high quality standard and a high availability.
  • They are in direct contact with the operators and engineers and develop improvements together.
  • Furthermore, the parameterization and commissioning of EMSR devices such as sensors and actuators on new or existing plants are among your tasks.
  • They are willing to work in a two-shift operation and provide piquetting service.

Who are you/your qualifications

As a communicative, competent automatic / electrician (m/f), you will bring the following training and experience:

  • Successfully completed vocational training as an electrician, automatic technician, electrical installer, assembly electrician, grid electrician, telematician or equivalent with professional recognition in Switzerland.
  • Several years of professional experience in the field of automation and conventional electrical and control technology in the GMP environment.
  • Several years of professional experience in the area of maintenance of automated systems in the GMP environment.
  • Experience with PLC/PLS systems, such as Siemens.
  • Knowledge of safety technology, conventional controls and pneumatics.
  • Experience with industrial PCs and camera systems (Supply Chain Control) is an advantage.
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of English is an advantage.
  • Procedural knowledge and SAP knowledge are desirable.
