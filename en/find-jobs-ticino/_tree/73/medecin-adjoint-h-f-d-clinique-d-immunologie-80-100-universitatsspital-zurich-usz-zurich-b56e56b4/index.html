Full description

Work at the interface of modern immunology, clinical practice and research in a leading university environment. Enjoy a wide range of activities, targeted further education and a collegial cooperation at the University Hospital Zurich.
The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Clinical care of outpatient immunological patients: inside as well as consultancy work, each under supervision by a senior physician of the Clinic of Immunology • Cooperation in special consultation hours (e.g. sarcoidosis, lupus) and interdisciplinary consultation hours (ORL) • Active participation in interdisciplinary team meetings and case meetings • Medical reporting and implementation of administrative activities • Interdisciplinary cooperation with other disciplines at the USZ • Active participation in clinical and scientific training Requirements • Two years of experience in general internal medicine at a category A training centre • High level of expertise, teamwork and commitment • Respectful treatment of patients: your employees are a matter of course • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing We offer: We offer you varied and responsible work in a university-driven, innovative environment where research, teaching and clinical practice are closely linked. We support your professional development through structured further education in accordance with the SIWF programme as well as through participation in external courses and international congresses. A dedicated, interdisciplinary team accompanies you in everyday work and ensures a collegial environment. Benefit from attractive employment conditions, a modern infrastructure and a centrally located workplace on the campus of the University Hospital Zurich.
