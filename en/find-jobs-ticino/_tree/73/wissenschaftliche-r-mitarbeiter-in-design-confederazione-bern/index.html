HR BAK NB

Live the culture!

The Federal Office for Culture (BAK) is the specialist authority for fundamental cultural policy issues, for the promotion of culture and for the preservation and mediation of cultural values.

The Directorate for Cultural Creation and Cultural Participation develops the cultural policy prerequisites for strengthening artistic creation, cultural expressions and cultural diversity in Switzerland. It improves the framework conditions for professional and non-professional cultural professionals, increases the visibility of cultural achievements and promotes public access to culture as a basis for social participation and understanding.

The Cultural Creatures and Prizes section is responsible for awarding and awarding Swiss cultural prizes in the fields of art, design, performing arts, literature, music and book design. With the awards, the Federation recognises the excellence of cultural creators and their works and contributes to their mediation at home and abroad. The section also supports the creation of favourable framework conditions for professional cultural organisations.

The offices of the Federal Department of the Interior (EDI) are systematically committed to good working conditions and the health of their employees and are awarded the label Friendly Work Space.
