Full description

100%, place of work: Agence Générale Prévoyance & Heritage, Lausanne

Shape your future with us!

In the field of health, which is in the long term an absolute priority of the AXA Group and AXA Switzerland, we rely exclusively on our own distribution and therefore on you. Quality is a top priority for us!

In order to strengthen our team of professionals in the health insurance sector, we are looking for a motivated and highly focused person with good knowledge of the health insurance environment and first professional experience in this sector!

What's waiting for you

  • Personalized counselling in the field of health insurance
  • Proposal for complementary insurance solutions
  • Highlighting the potential savings in basic insurance and tracking the customer
  • Transmission of your specific health knowledge to colleagues
  • Placement of other AXA products (non-life, life)
  • Acquisition of new customers and development of external networks
  • Performing administrative tasks related to your position, with the support of the entire AXA structure and modern monitoring tools

What you bring

  • To the benefit of a Federal Certificate of Capacity (FCC) and AFA and you have already held a position in the health insurance sector
  • You want to put consulting and sales activities with integrity and excellence at the heart of your business
  • Your home must be located in the area of activity concerned, you have a very good knowledge of the local economic fabric and a vast network in this region
  • Your personality is service oriented and you have ease in contacts with others, you seek solutions for your clients and you know how to adapt to any situation
  • Your method of work is autonomous and oriented towards the objectives
  • You can speak English and French both orally and in writing.
  • You have ease with common computer systems as well as learning new digital tools

What we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you the opportunity to fully develop and exploit your potential. As a Swiss insurance leader, we offer ambitious people interesting career opportunities with great freedom of action.

  • Up to 30% discount on products from AXA
  • Five weeks of vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-tariff subscription/participation in general subscription costs
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Freedom of action and self-employment
  • Innovative structures, processes and tools
  • Strong and professional training and development opportunities
