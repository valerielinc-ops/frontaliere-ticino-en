Full description

Your role As a travelling lifestyle agent, you are a flexible talent of our agency. Depending on your profile and assignment, you advise and sell, represent brands, look after guests or provide professional, creative, artistic or entertaining services. You represent EBL and our partners professionally and implement your orders reliably and according to the briefing.

Working model

  • Employed on an hourly basis, exclusively as fake work on demand
  • No guaranteed fixed load and no guaranteed order volume
  • We offer you individual orders. You decide freely whether to accept it or reject it.
  • With your acceptance, the order, the agreed order days and the confirmed times become binding.
  • Changing locations in the agreed area

Your tasks

  • Advice and sales, brand representation, promotion and guest support or professional, creative, artistic or entertaining services, depending on the order
  • Professional, authentic and order-oriented appearance for EBL, our partners and clients
  • Implementation of applicable brand, service and quality standards as well as participation in agreed training
  • Preparation for your order days based on the briefings and careful, professional implementation on site
  • Adherence to the agreed times and procedures as well as a proper work or appearance area
  • Ongoing information to the Agency contact person. You report customer feedback, special incidents and suggestions for improvement promptly.
  • Complete and timely recording of working hours and order reports
  • Immediate notification of preventions and changes to the Agency
  • Careful handling of products, materials and confidential information. You comply with the requirements for data protection, hygiene and security.
  • Working independently within the briefing. Prices, special conditions, order changes or additional services are discussed with the agency.

Your profile

  • Interested in beauty, lifestyle, events, brands or creative and artistic tasks
  • The experience, training or practical expertise required for your assignments (e.g. in sales, promotion, cosmetics, makeup, styling, art or entertainment)
  • Clear, friendly communication and a professional, order-oriented appearance
  • Reliable, punctual and self-responsible work
  • Willingness to learn and constructive handling of feedback
  • The language skills required for the respective contract
  • Mobility for changing order locations in the agreed area

What matters to us The quality of your work is reflected in a professional appearance, a professional execution, the achievement of the agreed goals and a reliable cooperation with the agency, our partners and clients.

We offer

  • Varied assignments for well-known brands and clients
  • Free choice of which orders you accept
  • Training and briefings to prepare for your assignments
  • Hourly wage: 27-28CHF/h

Application Apply with CV, photo and indication of your area (Beauty, Sales, Promotion, Artist, Creative Arts or Entertainment) on our website.
