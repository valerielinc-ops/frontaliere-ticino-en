Internship Assistant Institutional Architecture (M/F)

Start of internship: January 2027

Location: Geneva, Switzerland

Duration: 6 months – full time

Department: Within the Design Innovation & Institutional Architecture division

Do you match the profile?

Are you a student in design or architecture school?

Are you dynamic, curious and attracted to innovations?

Enjoying teamwork, you have a good relationship and a sense of service?

Are you rigorous and methodical in managing your projects? You master English and the Adobe Suite?

Do you master 2D software as well as 3D software (Sketchup)?

What do we expect from you?

The Design Innovation division is responsible for developing objects and environments offering a customer experience (exhibitions, events, shops, showcases, etc.).

This department is also responsible for monitoring and carrying out architectural projects, off-shop, such as the Customer journeys in our workshops.

-You follow and provide support on different planning, organization of sending and receiving materials, followed by prototyping, project guidelines.

  • You communicate regularly with our suppliers and our internal teams. You ensure good relations with these.

-You report on ongoing projects and record keeping.

-You perform a monthly competitive watch and present research and development related projects.

-You accompany the team on 3D design and modelling.

-Finally, you work independently on the follow-up of creative projects.
