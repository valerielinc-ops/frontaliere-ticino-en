Full description

Every year, the canton of Friborg advertises more than forty apprenticeships for businesspeople with EBA and EFZ. The Office for Personnel and Organization is responsible for accepting applications and forwarding them to all administrative units and departments that employ personnel in these professions.

Why do an apprenticeship with the Canton of Friborg?

Completing an apprenticeship with the state of Freiburg doesn’t just mean learning a profession. It also means becoming part of the largest employer in the canton of Friborg and discovering the world of work in a dynamic, varied environment focused on serving citizens.

We offer

  • You will be accompanied by dedicated trainers who will take the time to explain the tasks to you and guide you in a benevolent manner.
  • You will get to know departments with a wide range of tasks (human resources, justice, finance, education, economics, environment, spatial planning, etc.).
  • You will get to know and understand how the public service works and the meaning of the public mandate.
  • You have the opportunity to complete your training with an integrated commercial vocational baccalaureate.
  • You will become part of committed teams and actively participate in the life and tasks of the units and departments.
  • You receive 5 weeks of vacation per school year and a salary that corresponds to the cantonal recommendations.
  • You have access to the benefits granted to public servants (e.g. corporate discounts on mobile phone contracts, etc.).

Application

To apply, please send us the following documents

  • Your resume (CV).
  • A cover letter.
  • A copy of your school reports from the last two years.
  • Optionally, you can also provide us with additional documents (internship certificate, employment reference, etc.).

Notes

We reserve the right not to consider incomplete or intentionally incomplete application documents.

Your personal data will be processed in accordance with the law of October 12, 2023 on data protection (DSchG; SGF 17.1).

Contact

Rebecca Zannin, responsible for vocational training at the state, spo-dpo@fr.ch

Requirements

  • You have the opportunity to complete your training with an integrated commercial vocational baccalaureate.
