Full description

With 1,650 employees, the Société Coopérative Migros Neuchâtel-Fribourg offers professional opportunities to those who wish to grow and share their ideas and motivation. To strengthen our Facility Management section, we are looking for a Facility Management Manager for the Fribourg region.

Tasks

  • Monitoring and execution of repairs and site maintenance
  • Ensuring the operation of technical installations
  • Applying methods, specifications, and work processes
  • Controlling the quality of the execution of ad-hoc tasks performed by third parties
  • Requesting offers, negotiating, ordering, checking services, and managing costs
  • Contributing to building security
  • Management and supervision of a team of employees

Requirements

  • Proven experience in building technology
  • Completed professional apprenticeship (CFC) in an HVAC field
  • Recognized experience in management
  • Versatility and independence
  • Proficiency in standard IT tools
  • Ability to work irregular hours and on-call duty
  • Written and oral proficiency in French
  • Article 13 can be an advantage
  • Maintenance agent certificate would be a plus
  • Bilingual French-German would be a plus
  • French (Very good knowledge)
  • German (Preferable)
