Full description

Portrait The district of Mett is a diverse and multilingual quarter in the lively city of Biel/Bienne. The school unit Mett comprises three school houses. Every day around 160 children from different backgrounds come together in the Sahligut School House and learn with around 25 teachers in German-speaking classes, from kindergarten to 6th grade. This in a variety of forms, usually integrative and aligned with the Churer model. The school unit offers a day school structure.

Tasks

We are looking for a teacher for Wednesday and Friday morning who wants to lead a diverse class in standard lessons, as well as in team teaching, and who can specifically promote students who need more support. On Wednesday there are 4 lessons Rule lessons German and NMG or an overarching topic. You will be supported by a class help. On Friday morning there are 5 lessons Teamteaching with the class teacher for the targeted promotion and support in the class association and small groups, also in the field of German and NMG.

Requirements

You are a dedicated healing teacher:in, that is, you like the educational challenge, you have an eye for the small steps forward. Grateful students: inside and a successful cooperation in the class team give you meaning and momentum.

If you feel addressed, but a criterion does not fit directly, please contact us.
