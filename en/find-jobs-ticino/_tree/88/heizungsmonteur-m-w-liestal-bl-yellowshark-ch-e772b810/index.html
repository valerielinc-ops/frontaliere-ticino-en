01 Future employer

Get started now! We are looking for experienced heating installers for our clients in the canton of Basel (BL) for temporary employment, preferably starting immediately or by agreement.

02 Your responsibilities

  • The work as a heating installer is varied and diverse.
  • You are responsible for installing heating systems, thermal technology and cooling-water pipes.
  • Your assignments will take place in new buildings, renovations and industrial buildings.
  • In addition, service and repair work are regularly part of your responsibilities.
  • Close and effective teamwork contributes to your success and is appreciated by our clients.

04 Your profile

  • Professional experience as a heating installer in Switzerland or EU countries
  • Sound technical knowledge in heating and thermal technology
  • Clean and efficient way of working
  • Good command of German
  • Experience with underfloor heating / floor distribution systems
  • Team-oriented and communicative
  • Physically fit and resilient
  • Knowledge of occupational safety
