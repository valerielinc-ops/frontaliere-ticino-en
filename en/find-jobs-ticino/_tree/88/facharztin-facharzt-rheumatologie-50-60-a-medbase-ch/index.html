Welcome to Medbase Baden

Our modern medical center is centrally located in the historic city of Baden, directly at the station. We provide interdisciplinary and quality-oriented care for our patients. Our team, consisting of around 20 employees, consists of specialists from the fields of General Internal Medicine, Gynaecology and Obstetrics, ORL and Rheumatology as well as MPAs. At Medbase Baden we pay attention to a balanced work-life balance. Working in the part-time pool is possible in all functions and the working hours can be flexibly designed.
