Who are we?

The University of Management (HEG-FR) in Freiburg is a member of the University of Applied Sciences in Western Switzerland (HES-SO) and offers bilingual and trilingual training at the Bachelor and Master level.

Why join us?

The HES-SO//FR adopts a recruitment policy for equal opportunities. We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

Ensure the maintenance and development of HEG-FR's AACSB international accreditation through continuous improvement, standard compliance and institutional coordination.

  • Managing and monitoring the accreditation and re-accreditation process AACSB
  • Coordinate the preparation of reports, indicators and documents required for the AACSB evaluations
  • Supervise the monitoring of requirements for faculty qualifications (Faculty Qualification) and teaching coverage (Faculty Sufficiency)
  • Managing Insurance of Learning (AoL) and ensuring the implementation of continuous improvement measures
  • Animating and coordinating the contributions of the various internal actors involved in accreditation
  • Ensure the eve of the AACSB standards, make recommendations to the Directorate and lead the necessary improvement actions
  • Represent HEG-FR at AACSB International and develop relations with professional networks related to international accreditations

Profile desired

  • Master in management, quality, administration or training deemed equivalent
  • Confirmed experience in the autonomous management of complex projects involving multiple stakeholders
  • Experience in AACSB and/or Business Schools accreditation is a major asset
  • High autonomy and ability to lead a strategic project independently
  • Natural leadership, ability to unite teams and gain the support of the various institutional actors
  • Strong sense of organization, planning and timeliness
  • Spirit of analysis and ability to identify risks, priorities and opportunities for improvement
  • Excellent knowledge of MS-Office programs, ideally d-Academ, and interest in training on new computer systems
  • Mastery of the French and English language with good knowledge of German
