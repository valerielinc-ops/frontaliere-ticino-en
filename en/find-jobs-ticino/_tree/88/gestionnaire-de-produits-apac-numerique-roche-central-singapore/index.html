The Position APAC Product Manager (Digital)

The Opportunity

  • Leading the marketing, commercial strategy, and lifecycle management of the Digital Infrastructure portfolio across the APAC region, driving sustainable business growth, digital enablement, and customer value.

As the APAC Product Manager (Digital), you will be responsible for:

  1. Strategic Marketing & Portfolio Management
  • Drive strategic marketing and product lifecycle management for the Digital Infrastructure portfolio, including customer engagement, regional workshops, congresses, and capability building for affiliates.

  • Drive and support launch excellence across APAC using the Launch Powerhouse (LPH) framework.
  • Support or lead regional squad teams (including affiliate launch leads and cross-functional teams) to ensure strong commercial readiness, affiliate engagement, and successful local market adoption.

  • Drive business performance through portfolio analytics, KPI sales tracking, opportunity pipeline reviews, and strategic business initiatives across APAC.

  1. Lifecycle & Stakeholder Management
  • Serve as the Single Point of Contact (SPOC) and Subject Matter Expert (SME) for all discussions regarding the assigned digital portfolio.
