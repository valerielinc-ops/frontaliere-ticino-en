Full description

There is much more to a host than you can see at first glance. Among other things, there is a need for openness, interest in people, tact and a good sense of the opposite. Does your heart beat for our versatile tasks? Then apply!

Daily services 06 50 – 15.35 and 11.00 – 20.00 / 365 days a year The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees are committed to the welfare of our patients every day.

Tasks

  • Warm reception of our patients at the department and during an entrance interview
  • Personal advice on menu and beverage selection and ordering via iPad
  • Hospitable and professional service for meals, drinks and other needs
  • Ordering food and station laundry
  • Various hotel services in the patient rooms (make beds, fill up laundry etc.)
  • Close cooperation with the care, kitchen, transport and other interfaces
  • Responsible for compliance with the specified quality and hygiene standards
  • Various administrative tasks

Requirements

  • Completed training (EFZ) and/or multi-year professional experience in the fields of hospitality or gastronomy
  • Flexible working hours fit for you
  • Very good oral and written German skills (Level C1)
  • Dealing with MS-Office is unproblematic for you
  • You like to work independently, love a dynamic environment and are flexible
  • Standardized processes are no problem for you
  • Your heart beats for a courteous service

We offer: We create a well-being for patients and patients together and maintain an open, warm and helpful approach. We achieve success together in the team and we work with enthusiasm.

With the patient hotel industry, a colourful and multicultural team awaits you. We are happy to meet you.
