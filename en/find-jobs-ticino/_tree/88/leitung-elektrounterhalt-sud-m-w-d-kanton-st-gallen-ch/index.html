Full description

The National Roads Division VI of the Civil Engineering Office of the Canton of St.Gallen makes a significant contribution to the safety of the national roads in the cantons of Glarus, Thurgau and St.Gallen. For this purpose, the territorial unit has four workshops in Thal, Mels, Biäsche and Oberbüren as well as two bases in St.Gallen and Müllheim.

What to expect

  • Leadership of a team of 7 employees as well as responsibility for the economic, technical and administrative operation of electrical plants in the area of national and cantonal roads
  • Responsibility for the operation, maintenance and troubleshooting of operational electrical installations
  • Preparation and examination of offers and calculations including ongoing monitoring of results
  • Responsibility for billing and budgeting of services to third parties
  • Technical support of project management BSA and coordination of the required resources
  • Coordination and close cooperation with internal departments and external offices

What sets it apart

  • Completed training as an electrical installer with further training as a federal dipl. Electrical installer or equivalent qualification
  • Several years of professional and managerial experience, ideally in a comparable technical environment
  • Independent, initiative and solution-oriented working method
  • Communication strength and negotiating skills in dealing with suppliers and various stakeholders
  • High flexibility and load capacity
  • Good user knowledge in MS Office

What we offer

  • Responsible, varied and very independent tasks
  • Diverse contact with internal and external stakeholders
  • Exciting and interesting activities in a motivated team
  • Attractive employment conditions and good training opportunities
