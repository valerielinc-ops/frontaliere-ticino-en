Full description

Location: Hirslanden Klinik Linde | Biel / Bienne

Occupation by: immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74371

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments.

Clinic Linde is the leading private hospital in the Biel-Seeland-Jura area. With its high medical quality, its selected offers and the supra-regional reputation, it is the first choice for patients as well as for highly qualified medical doctors.

Are you looking for a versatile role with interface work, variety, exciting challenges and want to work more than just in the operating room? In our lively operation with 5 operating rooms, we attach particular importance to appreciation, open communication and team spirit. As a surgical coordinator, technical surgical specialist or in anesthesia care, you combine operational expertise with administrative organization and the design of smooth, patient-oriented care across all departments.

If you enjoy controlling interfaces, planning resources and making a direct contribution to quality management and patient experience through your work, you are at the right place.

YOUR TASKS

  • Physical presence in the operating room and telephone accessibility from 06.45-16.00, for daily coordination of quick cuts, transport services, inquiries practices and internal inquiries etc.
  • Contact person for medical doctors and practices in case of follow-up and emergencies
  • OP coordination is the single point-of-contact for enquiries regarding the operating day program (48h time horizon, today for today, today for tomorrow)
  • Daily monitoring, integration of emergencies and follow-up notifications and implementation of the surgical program in interdisciplinary coordination with the day-responsible anesthesia doctor and the surgical and anesthesia care
  • Managing the daily operating meeting at 2 p.m.
  • Participation in the 10:45 Performance Briefing to coordinate the current day and for the following days
  • Member of the OP Steering Group

Your profile

  • Dipl. Specialist surgical technique HF, TOA or dipl. Expert/Expert Anesthesia Care NDS HF
  • Experience in OP coordination or OP disposition is beneficial
  • Organizational talent, communication strength and teamwork
  • Enjoyment of administrative tasks and coordination
  • Flexibility, responsibility and a structured way of working
  • Your calm thinking and acting in hectic situations and emergencies sets you apart
  • Well-founded IT knowledge that you bring to your daily work
  • Good knowledge of German and/or French as well as the ability to communicate in the other language

Why does it work for us?

  • We work appreciatively and respectfully at eye level with each other
  • Possibility of working with flex and leisure plus models
  • Up to 4 additional free days per year as compensation for changing time (pension-dependent)
  • Above-average night and weekend allowance of CHF 10.- per hour
  • Many discounts and perks in the fitness center, over 100 service providers, leisure events and mobile subscriptions

Benefit as a Hirslanden employee: in many more benefits!

For additional information, Andrea Stegemann, Head of OP & Intervention Rooms, is available at andrea.stegemann@hirslanden.ch or Jennifer Perez at jennifer.perez@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by email. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via the tool on our homepage.
