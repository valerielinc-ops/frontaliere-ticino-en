Full description

La Source Clinic is a unique, multidisciplinary acute care facility. It belongs to the La Source Foundation, an independent, private, not-for-profit institution that also oversees the La Source Institute and High School of Health. The independence conferred on him by this status allows him to invest continuously in human and technological terms in order to guarantee his patients the best possible care.

We are looking for a 100% Human Resources Trainee

Key Position Information

Reference : CLS/RH/2026.792 / Type of contract : Contract with Duration Determined 100% / Date of entry : 1 January 2027 / Location : in the heart of Lausanne (M2 stop « Riponne » or Bus 2, 3 or 21 stop « Beaulieu-Jomini »)

Why do we need you?

This internship will allow you to move to the heart of an HR team and contribute to various activities, in support of several key functions of the department.

What will you do?

  • Contribute to the deployment of a digitalised tool for the drawing up of work certificates (definition of content, testing and implementation with the employees concerned).
  • Calculate the wages at commitment on the basis of our salary schedules and provide the necessary information to the HR administration for the establishment of contracts.
  • Conduct the exit interviews and analyse the information gathered in order to identify the main reasons for departure and ensure reporting.
  • Participate in the start-up process, in particular by drawing up work certificates and monitoring the various administrative steps.
  • Provide support to staff and the HR team on the various topics related to the work cycle.
  • Participate in continuous improvement of HR processes and tools, as well as their digitization where necessary.
  • Participate in different HR projects, depending on the needs and priorities of the team.

Who are you going to work with?

Our team is composed of 7 professionals with recognized and complementary HR skills. It is important for us to collaborate in confidence and share moments of conviviality.

In this position, you will join the team of Charlotte Lang, Head of Human Resources, for whom autonomy, trust and accountability are the keys to a successful team. Together, we meet the needs of the collaborators and the challenges of La Clinique.

What do we expect from you?

The indispensables

  • Be a Bachelor or Master, ideally with a specialization in human resources or management
  • Mastering common IT tools, including Microsoft Office
  • Demonstrate autonomy, organization and rigour in the management of its activities
  • Have excellent editorial and analytical skills
  • Appreciation of teamwork and exchanges with various interlocutors

Your assets

  • Demonstrate curiosity and a real desire to learn, with an ability to take initiatives
  • Be proactive and comfortable in an environment where activities are varied and priorities may change
  • Be endowed with a strong customer orientation and be attentive to the needs of the employees and the various HR interlocutors
  • Know the care environment or have first experience in a hospital or clinical environment

Why choose The Source Clinic?

  • Join the largest private clinic in the canton of Vaud and evolve in a dynamic environment, recognized for its expertise and know-how
  • Benefit from a human-sized work environment, while benefiting from the wealth and diversity of activities of a future-oriented health institution
  • Benefit from attractive terms of engagement and advantageous social benefits (reception and integration, LaMal participation, advantageous LPP, discount, etc.)
  • Reconciling work and private life through teleworking opportunities, part-time work, up to more than 7 weeks of holidays, unpaid leave, compensated holidays and exchange time valuation
  • Sharing similar values: a Unique, Respectful, Innovative and Responsible Clinic
