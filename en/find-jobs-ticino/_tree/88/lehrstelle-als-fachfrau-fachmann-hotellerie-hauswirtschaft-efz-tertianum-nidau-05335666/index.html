Full description

Start a varied and meaningful training with us! Your future is important to us. Our experienced vocational trainers will accompany and support you throughout your entire training.

We are looking for you for the house Tertianum Villa Sutter as of August 1, 2026

Apprenticeship as a specialist in hotel housekeeping EFZ or housekeeping practitioner

You'll learn that with us

  • General cleaning work
  • Linen supply and linen ordering
  • Look after guests and provide service
  • Record and process feedback and information from guests
  • Support the kitchen team
  • Implementation of operational procedures and compliance with work quality requirements
  • Decorating rooms and premises

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed school education (secondary school A or B) with good academic performance
  • Great willingness to learn and sense of responsibility
  • Empathy and patience as well as interest in dealing with our guests
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Well-groomed appearance, good manners as well as awareness of hygiene and a sense of order
  • Creativity and an eye for detail
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoy diverse challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written German

Your advantages

We offer a wide range of different benefits, e.g.:

  • SBB half-fare subscription
  • Discounts at Sunrise
  • Alpamare discounts
  • Fitness perks
  • Insurance discounts
  • Regular team events
  • Lunch for CHF 5
  • At least 60 minutes of learning time in the learning room or learning workshop once a week

Your training starts with an exciting introductory day. In the second year of training, our learners take part in the Tertianum Summer Camp. We celebrate the completion of the training with a fitting graduation ceremony. After your apprenticeship, there are numerous opportunities to work within our company in different companies and language regions.

You can find more benefits and insights on our website.

Interested in training at Tertianum?

Once we have received your complete application documents, we will check them and invite selected candidates to an initial interview. Afterwards you have the opportunity to get to know everyday working life at Tertianum better on one or more trial days.

Your complete application

  • CV including application photo
  • Letter of motivation
  • School reports
  • Results of the signal box test and, if available, the multicheck

Our team looks forward to well-positioned and interested learners. Do you recognize yourself? Then we look forward to receiving your complete application via our online application tool.

If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact Markus Baumann, Head of Kitchen, or Iris Majana L'Heureux, Head of Service and Housekeeping on 032 332 81 00.
