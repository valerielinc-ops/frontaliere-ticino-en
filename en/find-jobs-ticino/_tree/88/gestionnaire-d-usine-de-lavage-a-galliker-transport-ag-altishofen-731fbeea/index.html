Full description

Washing plant manager (a) Our trucks are one of the most important trademarks of Galliker Transport AG. Maintenance and repairs are therefore of great importance. Most tasks are carried out in-house in our modern workshops. You want to take responsibility, move a team and at the same time tackle yourself? Then you are exactly right with us! As the Washing System Manager (a), you ensure that our laundry line runs smoothly, lead your team with clarity and appreciation and ensure that our vehicles are available in the best condition every day. At the same time, you support Car Logistics with a wide range of operational tasks and bring your hands-on mentality where it is needed. You can expect a varied function with responsibility, teamwork and a lot of movement in everyday work. Task area

  • Professional responsibility of washing plant employees
  • Monitoring of careful vehicle cleaning to ensure cleanliness and hygiene standards
  • Carrying out damage checks on cleaned vehicles
  • Contact person to internal customers
  • Support for Car Logistics in operational activities

Requirement profile

  • Holder of the driving licence Cat. C
  • Joy of leading a team professionally
  • Professional responsibility in a previous employment of advantage
  • Good physical strength
  • Craftsmanship
  • Good knowledge of German

Offer

  • Collegial and dedicated team
  • Occupational workwear
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • More hours can be compensated
  • Free parking for employees
