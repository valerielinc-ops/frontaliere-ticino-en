Full description

Pensum: 100% | Location: Beznau

Do you want to contribute directly to the safety of our nuclear power plant? Then we will look for you! With us, you can expect varied tasks, a committed team and contemporary employment conditions. We look forward to meeting you.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Advising the power plant management and the specialist departments in the field of physical security
  • Permanent, situational review and control of the security concept
  • Conceptual support of the safety guidance system
  • Management of work teams and support for physical security transport
  • Preparation of physical security reports and regulations
  • Contact person for authorities, including for background checks (personal security check, PSP)Profile & requirements:
  • You have a completed apprenticeship
  • You bring police or military training in security/object protection (mil officer grade is advantageous)
  • You are familiar with the staff work of military or civilian organizations
  • You can express yourself well orally and in writing in English
  • You are characterized by good communication skills and a strong self-management
  • You have very good problem-solving skills, also across departments and in projects
  • Ideally, you will bring basic technical knowledge

Even if you do not meet all points, we look forward to your application.

About the team

You will become part of the plant protection department – a small, experienced team of specialists who perform central cross-sectional functions in the KKB. We attach great importance to cooperation, mutual support and team spirit.

In our company, we strive for a culture of continuous learning, personal development and commitment to the community at the national level. We care about giving our employees the tools and resources they need to succeed, and we’re sure you’ll love to be part of our team!

  • Public transport With a free half-tax (max. CHF 190) you can get cheaply from A to B. The KKW Beznau is easily accessible by public transport.
