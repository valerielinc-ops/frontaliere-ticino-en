Forestry internship at the Office for Forestry and Natural Hazards, Mittelbünden/Moesano Region

Office for Forests and Natural Hazards \| 80\-100% \| Deep Castel

Together for Graubünden The only trilingual canton in Switzerland is economically, culturally and politically diverse. Graubünden is the largest canton in Switzerland with 7106 km2 and offers a high quality of life with 150 valleys, 615 lakes, 140 resorts and countless sunny days per year. The cantonal administration of Graubünden is just as versatile as our landscape. Bring your unique skills and shape the future of the cantonal administration Graubünden together with us.

The Office for Forest and Natural Hazards (AWN) is the cantonal specialist for forestry and protection against natural hazards. The employees of the AWN are committed to the needs of society in the forest and ensure that the forest is handled sustainably.

You make sure that the Bundner Wald

  • can protect people, animals and infrastructure,
  • habitat for diverse animal and plant species,
  • raw material supplier of wood and energy,
  • can continue to be a recreational and leisure space for people.

The Office for Forests and Natural Hazards offers a professional internship (80\-100%) in the region of Mittelbünden/Moesano (working place Tiefencastel).

You can look forward to it

  • Individual design of the internship according to your/our preferences
  • Get a comprehensive insight into mountain forest management
  • A meaningful task with direct reference to integral risk management in natural hazards, nature and technology
  • Working independently in a committed and collegial team
  • Modern working conditions

Your responsibilities

  • Diverse insight into the work of the cantonal forest service Graubünden
  • Participation in mountain forest\-stroke drawings in the protective forest
  • Accompanying visits related to natural hazards
  • Insight into the planning and implementation of protective structures and developments
  • Development of forest ecology concepts
  • Support for public relations work
  • Support for forest law issues

That is what we want from you.

  • Environmental scientist with a deepened forest or landscape (minor Bsc) or forestry engineer or similar training according to the tasks
  • Internship duration 4\-6 months (preferably in the months March to November) or by arrangement
  • Engaged, team-oriented and reliable personality
  • General working language is German
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is an advantage

Our advantages

Flexible working time models and where possible incl. Home office and mobile work\-life\-balance with mind. 5 weeks holidays and service leave Special social allowance in addition to child allowance and support for third-party childcare Attractive wage and social benefits Various training and further education opportunities to shape the future of the canton

Place of work

Forest and Natural Hazards Office Veia Dalmeras 13 7450 Deep castle
