Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Implement and develop CIS-compliant security hardening and baselines on Windows and Linux servers
  • Define operating models, runbooks, monitoring and change/release processes and ensure operational excellence
  • Analyze security alerts and incidents, take immediate action and coordinate sustainable resolution
  • Technical implementation of security and compliance requirements (including verification) and support for compliance with legal requirements and instructions (creating ISDS concepts)
  • Maintain close cooperation with the System Engineering team, the Information Security department and other stakeholders

What you bring

  • Vocational apprenticeship EFZ in computer science (or equivalent training) as well as certifications in Linux (LPIC-2), Microsoft (AZ-800/802) and Security (Security+ / GSEC) or the motivation to acquire these qualifications on-the-job
  • Experience in system engineering with (Windows / Linux), in particular practical knowledge in the use of Powershell, processing of group policies in the Windows area and knowledge of tools such as OpenScap, Ansible in the Linux area
  • Center of Internet Security Workbench Software (CIS)
  • Very good understanding of security and compliance principles (e.g. data protection, access concepts, classification)
  • Structured, solution-oriented working method as well as the willingness to assume responsibility and continuously develop professionally
  • Strength of communication in dealing with different stakeholder groups and confident action in the political environment of a public administration

The Department of Finance and Resources, with around 650 employees, is responsible as a cross-sectional department for ensuring that the canton has the necessary resources to fulfil its tasks.

Computer science solutions make daily work easier and more efficient. The employees of the Computer Science Department Aargau contribute to increasing the efficiency of the cantonal administration. You are responsible for the strategy and operation of central computer science. They accompany large-scale cantonal projects and provide efficient and modern IT solutions that enable the public to have uncomplicated contact and exchange with the canton’s administrative offices.
