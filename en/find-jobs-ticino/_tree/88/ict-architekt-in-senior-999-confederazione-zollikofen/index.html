Put to the Point

As a system architect, you are responsible for the target architecture for authorization, identity management and delegated management. You work closely with the architecture of the entire business unit to build an API and event-driven platform that enables federated identities, self-service and secure access control.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • By agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: 609-16854
  • Location: Eichenweg 3, 3052 Zollikofen

You can make this contribution

  • Taking responsibility for the end-to-end target architecture of identity management
  • Drive the establishment of an API and event driven integration style
  • Ensuring that identity-related processes can be implemented in government
  • Act customer-oriented and focus on self-service solutions
  • Creating the architectural basis in ART

This makes you unique

  • More than 10 years of experience in software/data architecture; of which more than 3 years experience in managing large-scale modernization projects in regulated environments
  • In-depth knowledge in event-driven and ETL-based deployment, in IAM, in particular in authorization management in EIAM (Employer-IAM) and IGA
  • Experience in APIs, API contracts and related standards as well as CI/CD and containerization
  • management competence at technical level with experience in project and contract management
  • Assuming responsibility, controlling topics sovereignly and communicating clearly and solution-oriented
  • Active knowledge of a second official language and enjoyment of working in an interdisciplinary environment
