Over the area

"The Center for Dependency Diseases is part of the Clinic for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy of the PDAG (Clinical Director Prof. Dr. med. Marc Walter) and is dual of PD Dr. med. Eva-Maria Pichler, Chief Physician, and Michael Geissberger, Head of Care. It treats people with dependency disorders, including mental and concomitant disorders, in particular dual diagnoses, and covers the spectrum from harm reduction to abstinence-oriented treatment. At the main location in Windisch, three specialist stations with different treatment focuses and daytime stations are available. Also in Windisch, the center provides substitution-assisted treatment as well as heroin-assisted treatment for people with opioid addiction. At the Aarau, Baden and Windisch locations, it offers outpatient addiction medical examinations, treatments and special consultation hours, including for behavioral dependencies. Participation in ongoing clinical research projects is possible and supported. The center is a SIWF-approved training center for the focus psychiatry and psychotherapy of addiction diseases, category A, inpatient and outpatient (2 years for the focus, 1 additional year credited to the specialist title).

Tasks

  • They accompany mentally ill people aged 18 to 64 years with multiple dependencies. As part of the inpatient qualified withdrawal treatment, you will design ergotherapeutic individual and group therapies
  • They align the ergotherapeutic treatment in an evidence-based and needs-oriented manner and document the therapeutic course in a technically sound manner.
  • You contribute your ideas and actively shape the conceptual development of occupational therapy at the focus station
  • They work closely together in the ergotherapeutic and interprofessional team and together implement the therapeutic goals of the overall treatment
  • You bring your ergotherapeutic perspective to interprofessional rapports, team meetings and super or intervisions

Profile

  • You have completed a degree programme recognised in Switzerland or a corresponding training in occupational therapy (BSc, FH, HF)
  • You have professional experience in the same or comparable environment and ideally have clinical experience in psychiatry
  • They preferably already have experience with therapeutic interventions for multiple addictions. Even if you would like to familiarize yourself with this focus and further training out of special interest, we look forward to you
  • We would like to have in-depth knowledge of ergotherapeutic models and the associated assessments
  • You incorporate different perspectives and connections into your work, act independently and are happy to contribute new ideas
