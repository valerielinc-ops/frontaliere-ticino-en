Full description

About us Learning to live again and again. The REHAB Basel, Clinic for Neurorehabilitation and Paraplegiology, treats and rehabilitates patients with brain injury, paraplegia and related clinical pictures after an accident or illness. We offer a wide range of therapy in order to successfully help the people entrusted to us to achieve the greatest possible independence and an optimal quality of life. The clinic has 110 beds and an outpatient operation. Around 660 employees implement the visions and values of REHAB Basel in their everyday work. You work in a unique building in the attractive tri-border area. To strengthen our team, we will be looking for a physical therapy therapist 60% from January 2027 or by appointment.

Job title Therapist in physical therapy 60% m/f/d

Your responsibilities

  • Treatment of patients in the inpatient area on all stations from early rehabilitation to late rehabilitation
  • Written documentation of treatment units

Your profile

  • Completed training Med. massage therapist EFA or SRK recognised massage diploma or completed training as a physical therapist
  • Professional experience in neurology is beneficial
  • Very good communication skills and high empathy ability
  • Good segregation in clinical setting with severe pathologies
  • Have fun with classic massage
  • Interest in different treatment methods in the spectrum of physical therapies
  • Enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation with an understanding of a patient-centered way of working
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German (minor level B2 with proof)

You will find with us

  • Working hours Mo-Fr until 5.15 pm/no pikett, night or weekend service
  • A demanding and diverse field of activity
  • Openness to innovation in physical therapy
  • A targeted introduction to the field of neurorehabilitation
  • An appreciative working atmosphere in a committed and open-minded team

In addition, you will find a wide range of team-promoting and employee-friendly offers such as staff outings, free sports for employees and much more.

Our team is looking forward to you.

Position to be filled January 2027 or by agreement
