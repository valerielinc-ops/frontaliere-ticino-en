Full description

Bell is one of the leading food brands in Switzerland. Do you like to get involved and are interested in the food industry? At our location in Zell LU, you work in a modern logistics sector and ensure that our products reach their destination safely, reliably and on time. You will get to know the handling of chilled food and gain valuable experience in a versatile working environment. If you decide to apprentice as a logistician / logistician EFZ at Bell, you will You are a good choice. Always supported by us, you take your first steps into your professional life and become part of the community of Bell learners.

  • Contract type: apprenticeship
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Job opening: 01.08.2027
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Zelgmatte 1 6144 Zell

Tasks

  • Acceptance, control and proper storage of goods
  • Pick and provide food for dispatch
  • Work in refrigerated and frozen areas
  • Compliance with hygiene, quality and safety regulations
  • Checking stocks and documenting movements of goods
  • Use of industrial trucks and modern storage equipment
  • Collaboration with various departments and support in day-to-day logistics operations

Requirements

  • Completed elementary school
  • Self-employment
  • Responsibility
  • Reliability
  • Good sense of order

This is what we offer

  • Work-life balance: With us you benefit from 35 days of vacation, so that you have enough relaxation time and are then fit for everyday work and school.
  • Food: Cuisine is very important to us: freshly prepared food in our staff restaurant, a lunch menu incl. Soup and salad, water & tea and free fruits as well as high-quality products at greatly reduced prices await you in our outlet.
  • Mobility: Benefit from our contribution to environmental mobility, convenient connection to public transport and free SBB general subscription (GA)!
  • Professional development opportunities: We take over the school materials and participate in the amount of CHF 700.- in a notebook of your choice. After your training, we also offer you exciting prospects in an international environment and you can develop further through training in the Coop Education Center.
  • Health: Your well-being is important to us: Benefit from our health management and a free subscription at Update Fitness - for health and leisure fun!
  • Company events: Experience unforgettable moments with our learning community and every two years at our learning event with over 150 learners from all over Bell Food Group!
  • Market-friendly wages and social benefits: With a good academic and operational performance, you can look forward to your performance premium. Twice a year!
  • Family-friendly employer: Work in professional workwear and experience an inclusive work environment where diversity is valued and encouraged!
