Full description

Our heart beats for the Swiss gastronomic sector. We are constantly committed to our clients. In our 31 self-service markets, through external service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a varied assortment, attractive own brands, specialized and professional advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Suisse SA is part of the international group Transgourmet. The company is a leader in the delivery and self-service business in Switzerland and has around 2'100 employees, as well as 100'000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals, as well as retail).

Mission

  • Proactive sale and advice
  • Recognize, capture and communicate client needs to the Head of Department, who is the superior
  • Ensure a good presentation of the goods, ensure freshness and seasonality (shelves, 2nd point of sale, promotional location)
  • Ensure labelling complies with labelling concept
  • Inventory according to specifications
  • Take note of the claims and transmit them accordingly
  • Active participation in the ongoing improvement process (PAP)
  • Effective collaboration with internal and external suppliers and clients
  • Provide regular information and knowledge to employees
  • On the Job training for employees and apprentices

Qualifications and qualifications

  • Completed training in the food sector, with strong knowledge of fruits and vegetables
  • Experience in a comparable function and good commodity control
  • First successful experience as a team leader
  • Taste of customer contact
  • Training in handling the popular forklift and/or drawbar (SUVA standard)
  • Availability to work on Saturdays and take your job from 6 am
  • Good Mastery of MS Office
  • Excellent mastery of French
