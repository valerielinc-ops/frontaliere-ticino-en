Here, you will find a workplace that values both collective success and your individual contributions—offering long-term opportunities to evolve and make an impact. ## Role In the context of strengthening our mechanical workshop in Switzerland, we are looking for a rigorous and quality-oriented mechanical technician, capable of evolving in a demanding environment respecting GMP standards and applicable regulatory prescriptions. ## Responsibilities Within the Change-over service, you:

  • Perform repair and preventive maintenance operations on equipment
  • Monitor compliance with directives and prescriptions using technical means
  • Fill out documents related to each task performed ## Requirements

  • Sense of responsibility, rigor, team spirit, and initiative
