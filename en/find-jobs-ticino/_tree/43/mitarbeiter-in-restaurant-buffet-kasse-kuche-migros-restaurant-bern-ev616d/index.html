Full description

Do you want to become part of our innovative and lively restaurant business? With your commitment, you ensure that our customers feel at home and their versatile culinary wishes are fulfilled. If quality, freshness and hospitality are a matter of course for you, then we should definitely get to know each other!

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Restaurant, Migros Restaurant Bern Breitenrain, Breitenrainstrasse 11, 3013 Bern

Important information

Start of work from 06:30. Employment on an hourly basis. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for setting up and presenting the offer depending on the time of day and season
  • At the buffet and at the checkout, the customers are actively served and advised by you
  • You support in the kitchen in the production of cold dishes
  • Compliance with and enforcement of the HACCP concept falls within your remit
  • You help in the dishwashing room

Requirements

  • Ideally, a few years of professional experience: in the field of (system) gastronomy / retail trade
  • You show a high sense of quality and responsibility
  • Selling and consulting are your passions and customer satisfaction your motivation
  • You convince with your resilience, flexibility and high willingness to perform
  • PC skills and high digital fitness set you apart
  • No education/training required
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • Fair wages: functional and market-oriented remuneration
  • Self-responsibility & freedom: Varied tasks with high self-responsibility

Contatto
