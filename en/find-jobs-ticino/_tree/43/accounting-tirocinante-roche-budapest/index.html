The Position

What we do

The Record to Report Department, often abbreviated as R2R department provides financial services for more than 130 affiliates in the EMEA region. Our main areas covered are: Finance and Accounting (General Ledger Accounting, Management Accounting) and Supply Chain Finance. The department is constantly expanding since our main goal is to perform end-to-end accounting activities in the near future.

Your day to day responsibilities are

As an Accounting Intern you will execute the day-to-day activities according to the agreed service level.

As a qualified expert, you

  • Process General Ledger bookings (and any other local request)
  • Clear customer and GL accounts
  • Contribute to month-end closing tasks
  • Provide help of other team members in need of assistance due to workload
  • Assist in investigating account discrepancies involving inventory, variances, and allowances
  • Gain practical, real-world exposure to enterprise-level financial systems within a shared services environment

What can you bring to our team

We are looking for someone who is self motivated, really passionate about his or her job and understands that providing a high-quality service is crucial for the organization. As an ideal team member you are open-minded, dedicated to make a difference and open to constant development. Furthermore, you have:

  • Ongoing university studies in the field of Economics - specialized in Accounting
  • Excellent communication and analytical skills
  • Available for 25-30 hours per week
  • Customer focused mindset
  • Proactive mindset
  • Proficiency in English
  • A solid academic foundation in accounting and finance principles (GAAP or IFRS)
  • Proficient with spreadsheet applications and standard office tools (Excel, PowerPoint, Word, G-Suite)
  • Bonus Points: Prior exposure to ERP systems (such as SAP modules like FI, CO/PA, MM, PP) or financial close software
