Full description

With different coatings, fastening systems meet their specific requirements for securing, sealing and insulation. In our own surface technology, we coat the products on specialized plants – precisely tailored to the respective application areas. Your Tasks:

  • Personnel and technical management of the coating team
  • Planning and control of production orders
  • Plant and process optimizations
  • Creating offers and new parts development
  • Co-responsible for the training and further education of your profile employees:
  • Completed basic education from the metal industry such as polymechanics or similar
