Polydesigner in 3D (m/f/d)

80%

Emmen

2027 1 1

We at pfister pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today - will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Assistance in the implementation of local and national activities such as image campaigns, seasonal conversions, promotions and sales promotions
  • Active assistance in the placement of the furniture range and goods carrier
  • Using suitable materials to create inspiration points for an atmospheric and beautiful living
  • Using the given visual merchandising principles, set accents in the exhibition
  • Completed training as a polydesigner in 3D EFZ
  • Style-safe understanding of room design
  • You like to take part, craftsmanship and physical resilience
  • Creative flair, spatial imagination and a knack for commercial presentation
  • Reliable regarding working time
  • Professional experience as a poly designer in 3D in the furniture and decoration industry
