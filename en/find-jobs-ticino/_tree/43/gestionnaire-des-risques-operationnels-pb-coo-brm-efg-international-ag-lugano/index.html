Job Description

Motivated, passionate, hard worker and positive individual who continuously aims to make outstanding contributions and effect a difference from day 1.

Main Responsibilities

  • Execute all BRM controls on Private Banking area on a monthly respectively quarterly basis and ensures precise and proper documentation of results
  • Preparation of the CRO RSC and Quarterly Control report
  • Ensure that the identified control non in compliance and related risk mitigating actions are timely remediated
  • Contribution on preparation of the annual risk and control self-assessment (RSCA) as well as other ad hoc assessment & reviews.
  • Work with the Regional and Global PB BRM to achieve a robust First Line of Defence in the Area
  • Report on the internal control environment and potentially arising risks or issues.
  • Collaborate with local Compliance teams, Ops Risk and auditors for any ad-hoc reviews

Skills and experience

  • Economics Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in the related field
  • Experience in the Financial & Audit Industry, Risk Control domain, Compliance and Risk Management functions
  • Excellent data analysis and Program Languages (Python, SQL, R), Bloomberg Terminal
  • Languages required: English, German, French and Italian
  • Pro-active and clear communicator with strong organizational skills
  • Detailed oriented, precise and ability to work under pressure and tight deadlines
  • Willing to go the extra mile, efficient and resourceful

Our Values

  • Accountability: Taking ownership for tasks and challenges, as well as seeking continuous improvement
  • Hands-on: Being proactive to rapidly deliver high-quality results
  • Passionate: Being committed and striving for excellence
  • Solution-driven: Focusing on client outcomes and treating clients fairly with a risk-aware mindset
  • Partnership-oriented: Promoting collaboration and teamwork. Working together with an entrepreneurial spirit.
