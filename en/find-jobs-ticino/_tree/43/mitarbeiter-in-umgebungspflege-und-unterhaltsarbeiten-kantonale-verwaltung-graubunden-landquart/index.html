Full description

The Plantahof is a department in the Department of Economics and Social Affairs and the competence center for agricultural education and advice in the Canton of Graubünden. In addition to the agricultural vocational school, the Plantahof offers various further courses, further education courses and conferences and is responsible for comprehensive advice and the provision of specialist knowledge for the benefit of Grisons and Glarner agricultural and alpine farms and other circles involved in agricultural topics. For these purposes, he also runs his own experimental and estate operation at four different locations together with two farm shops. Finally, it has a conference centre with boarding school, lecture hall and a catering facility.

We are looking for 1. January 2027 or by appointment an employee in environmental care and maintenance work

Tasks

  • Maintenance and maintenance of green areas, lawns, hedges and flower discounts
  • Cleaning and maintenance of places, paths and other interior and exterior areas incl. Winter service
  • Care, cleaning and inspection of pool vehicles and ensuring their operational readiness
  • Support of the Chur Waldhaus/Plantahof branch office
  • Assistance in the preparation of seminar rooms, guest care and special events

Requirements

  • Apprenticeship or professional experience in horticulture, green care or maintenance or equivalent qualification
  • Independent and reliable working methods as well as the ability to organize work according to care plan and operational specifications
  • Category B driving licence
  • Readiness for weekend assignments, especially in winter service and on special occasions
  • The general working language is German, knowledge of another cantonal language is an advantage
