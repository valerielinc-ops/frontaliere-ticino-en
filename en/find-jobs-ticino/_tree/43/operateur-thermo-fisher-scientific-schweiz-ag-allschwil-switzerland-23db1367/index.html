Job Description

Operator

Summary of the function

Efficient, timely and GMP-compliant preparation, execution and completion of manufacturing and packaging orders including implementation of in-process controls (IPCs), as well as the cleaning of equipment and machine parts.

Your tasks

  • Efficient, timely and GMP-compliant preparation, execution and completion of manufacturing and packaging orders incl.

Implementation of in-process controls (IPCs)

  • Efficient and GMP-compliant conduct of cleaning of equipment and machinery parts and of production premises
  • GMP-compliant documentation of the work carried out
  • Compliance with the specific requirements of the production and packaging of clinical trial samples, in particular the GMP rules, hygiene and safety regulations and the relevant standard operating instructions (SOPs) and order instructions
  • Ensure active, timely and appropriate communication of progress and difficulties in

colleagues and supervisors - active cooperation in identifying, communicating and solving problems and inefficiency as well as in improving possibilities - working in cooling zones

Your profile

  • Preferably completed teaching in an area relevant to the position
  • experience in the pharmaceutical or food industry
  • GMP experience or training of advantage
  • mastery of the German language in word and writing
  • competence for appropriate, good communication with colleagues and superiors in German language (word and writing)
  • ability to comply with time limits
  • competence for independent work even under stress and in a multicultural environment
  • IT skills (Microsoft Office) of advantage
  • flexibility for shiftwork
