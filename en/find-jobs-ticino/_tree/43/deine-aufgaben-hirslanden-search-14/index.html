Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna | Lucerne

Occupation by: immediately or by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 12115

Working at St. Anna Clinic means more than just working. The cornerstone of our trusting and close cooperation is the family coexistence that we maintain together with our patients. For our certified IPS/IMCU with 6 IPS and 6 IMC beds, including 3-4 ventilators, we need your support.

YOUR TASKS

  • As an expert, you take on patient-oriented, professional and professional care and care of our intensive care patients in highly complex situations.
  • Your area of responsibility includes the challenging disciplines of our clinic, including thoracic, neuro, visceral and vascular surgery, urology, gynecology and cardiology incl. Catheter interventions
  • As a shift manager, you take responsibility for the day organization
  • The operation of various medical devices is just as much a part of your tasks as the performance recording and patient documentation
  • You are also responsible for caring for and accompanying relatives

Your profile

  • Further training as an expert/in intensive care or equivalent specialist training in intensive care
  • Returnees are also welcome!
  • Reliable, flexible and competent personality with high social competence and self-initiative, who keeps her smile even in hectic times
  • Interdisciplinary thinking and openness towards the new
  • Conscious, careful and professional working methods

Our offer

  • A family and supportive working environment with flat hierarchies and an open du-culture
  • Our IPS/IMC stations are in our modern new building at the same location
  • A structured familiarization so that you can quickly find your way around and get started
  • Up to 7 weeks holidays due to the possibility to buy additional holidays or take unpaid holidays
  • Discounted meals for only CHF 5.- and numerous other employee benefits
  • Further employment conditions and employee offers can be found here

For additional information, please contact Chantal Britschgi, Head of Care IPS/IMC, at T or Merlin Abgottspon, HR recruitment partner, at merlin.abgottspon@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

For data protection reasons, we look forward to your online application.
