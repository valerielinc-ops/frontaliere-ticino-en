Your role and responsibilities

As a Mechanical Engineer in Traction, you are responsible for the mechanical design of converters, energy storage systems and cooling units for railway and heavy on or off-road applications. The products are used worldwide in rail vehicles, e-buses and e-trucks or construction machines. To strengthen this motivated team, we are looking for a committed and experienced mechanical design engineer with interest in power electronics.


The work model for the role is: hybrid

This role is contributing to the Rail Systems Business Line in Untersiggenthal.

You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Mechanical engineering and design of complete converters and cooling units

  • Create, maintain, and manage product data within PLM systems (Product Lifecycle Management)

  • Plan and perform validation tests, particularly for cooling performance and structural strength

  • Support production during the introduction of new products and contribute to product maintenance and continuous improvement.

  • Create technical specifications and clarify technical requirements with suppliers and customers

  • Support the sales organization during the concept development and quotation phase


You will join a dynamic and high performing team, where you will be able to thrive.


• Qualifications for the role:

  • Trained and experienced Mechanical Design Engineer with further technical school (TS), or bachelor’s /master’s degree in mechanical engineering.

  • Experience with CAD and ERP systems, ideally Creo and PTC Windchill

  • Solid English skills, German is preferred

  • Open, collaborative team player with a hands-on and approachable working style

  • Applications from early-career professionals and motivated graduates are also welcome.

  • Preferably experienced in the design of power electronic equipment for rolling stock, automotive, or other vehicle applications
