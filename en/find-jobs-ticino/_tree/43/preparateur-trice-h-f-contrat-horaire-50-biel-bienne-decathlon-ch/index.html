Mission Your future playground You will join the Biel store, opened in 2019, made up of a team of 22 collaborators. Your perimeter: THE MAGASIN to satisfy our omnichannel customers. And now, place in the profession: You are looking for a dynamic profession You are methodical in nature You have a goal of 10,000 steps a day ;) So the prep position is made for you Why?

  • You're at the heart of the action. Your daily life is 100% in the field. You are the guarantor of the satisfaction of our customers who ordered online.
  • You have autonomy and impact. You guarantee the speed and accuracy of order preparation! Our customers' satisfaction goes beyond the walls of our store. What You Will Do Every Day: Your Missions
  • Taking orders from the web: throughout the day you are responsible for taking orders from our customers
  • Packing and dispatch of packages: You will pack and ship orders prepared the same day
  • Availability and logistics: Put your hand to the dough! From receipt of the delivery to dispatch, you guarantee the accuracy of your stocks and the rapid availability of your products. This job isn't for you if...
  • You'd rather sit behind a screen all day in peace.
  • You don't like to go spontaneously to others.
  • You think unpacking pallets, tidying a radius and processing merchandise "that's not your problem."
  • You're not ready to see 10,000 steps on your watch every night.
  • You are looking for fixed and routine schedules: here we live in the rhythm of sport and our schedules adapt to the activity
  • You don't want to work on Saturdays. Profile What we expect from you:
  • You have an excellent relationship. Customer satisfaction is your compass.
  • You're bold, curious, and you always want to learn.
  • You're organized, efficient, and you know how to manage your priorities in a fast moving environment.
  • You're passionate and practicing sports: company DNA!
  • You know French and German What we offer:
  • A flexible and secure hourly contract: As its name indicates, you are paid on time. In other words, the more you work, the higher your salary! To bring you security, we guarantee you no matter what happens a minimum per month (rate agreed in the contract and which varies between 20% and 50%). During peaks of activity, we can offer you extra hours. Don't panic about your organization: you will always be notified at least 48 hours in advance, and it will only be done if your availability allows you!
  • Hourly earnings: The salary is CHF 2 5 gross per hour. In addition to this are the allowances for holidays, holidays and the 13th (if you are on CDI hourly contract). Every 15th of the month, you will be paid according to the hours worked the previous month (between the 16th of the previous month and the 15th of the current month).
  • Shareholding: Once a year, you can become a shareholder of the company. This is an excellent investment since the value of the action has never been negative since the creation of Decathlon 50 years ago! (CDI only)
  • Passion sport : We support your practice! Benefit from 25% discount on your Decathlon purchases after 1 month of seniority
  • Continuing training: You take part in training to boost your technical knowledge about products, sales techniques or interpersonal communication.
