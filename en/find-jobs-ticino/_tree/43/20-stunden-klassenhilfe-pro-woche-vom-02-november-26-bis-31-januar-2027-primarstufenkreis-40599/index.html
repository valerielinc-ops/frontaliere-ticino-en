Full description

Portrait Creating a School Together

Worb is rural, attractive and easily accessible by public transport. In the Worb primary school, more than 500 children learn, accompanied by around 70 dedicated teachers, who understand school as a living learning and living space.

At Wyden1 we introduce three 1./2. and four 3./4. age-mixed classes with plenty of room for individuality, cooperation and educational innovation.

Tasks

Employment as a class aid to relieve teachers, with a particular focus on accompanying and supporting a child.

Requirements

  • Joy and skill in dealing with children of primary school age
  • Ability to give a child guidance, safety and clear boundaries
  • Reliability, responsibility and confidentiality
  • Educational experience is beneficial, but not a requirement

We offer

  • A warm, committed team with strong cohesion
  • A supportive, present and appreciative school management that gives confidence and promotes development
  • Open doors, short distances and an appreciative communication culture
  • Plenty of pedagogical design room and space for your strengths
  • Good and modern infrastructure
  • Good accessibility with public transport
