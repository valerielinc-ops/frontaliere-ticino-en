  • You take care of the construction, maintenance and maintenance of infrastructure facilities and thus make an important contribution to smooth rail traffic.

  • You ensure the shutdown and commissioning of various security systems: From electronic signal boxes to relay signal boxes.
  • Through your systematic approach, you recognize and report the irregularities and defects and initiate their correction and further development.
  • You will undergo a two-year internal training as a technician for safety systems / safety systems. Here you will be trained to locate and repair disturbances on the interior systems independently.
