Finance & Accounting Integration Specialist - Determined Time

Company: Damiani Group Workplace: Headquarters of Mendrisio (Switzerland) Contract type: Time determined

Who are we?

Damiani Group is one of the leading international luxury groups in the jewellery and watchmaking industry, recognized for artisan excellence, Made in Italy design and a portfolio of prestigious brands. As part of the Group's growth and integration of a new brand acquired, we are looking for a Finance & Accounting Integration Specialist to be included in our Headquarters in Mendrisio.

Objective of the position

The resource will join the Finance & Accounting Corporate team with the responsibility to preside over the accounting and administrative activities related to the integration of a luxury brand recently acquired by the Group.

The role provides for control, reconciliation and monitoring of accounting and treasury flows, ensuring accuracy, completeness and adequate documentary support for the monthly closing processes, both for the Swiss company and for the foreign companies involved.

Main responsibility

  • Monitor and support the acquired brand's monthly operations, ensuring the correct transposition into the Group's local company balance sheets.
  • Ensure the accuracy and completeness of the main accounting posts, with particular reference to revenues, purchases and valorization of the warehouse remains.
  • Check the correct management of the active and passive cycle, ensuring completeness of the billing process and its accounting.
  • Perform audits and accounting reconciliations to support the monthly closures.
  • Coordinate the information flow with the local administrations of the Group companies, favouring an effective alignment of accounting information.
  • Manage the onboarding of new suppliers, following the approved routes provided by business projects.
  • Register the suppliers invoices and monitor the relative payment flows for the purpose of the correct monthly accounting closure.
  • Collaboration with Finance teams and stakeholders involved in the integration process of the new brand, contributing to the harmonization of administrative and accounting processes.

Requirements

  • Degree in Economics, Administration, Finance or related disciplines.
  • Experience of at least 2-5 years in administrative-accountable, preferably matured in structured or multinational companies.
  • Solid knowledge of general accounting.
  • Good knowledge of stock accounting.
  • Solid experience in managing customer accounts and suppliers.
  • Knowledge of OIC accounting principles.
  • Experience using SAP ERP.
  • Familiarity with Business Intelligence tools and data analysis.
  • Excellent mastery of Microsoft Excel.

Personal skills

  • Precision and high attention to detail.
  • Excellent analytical capacity and data reconciliation.
  • Organizational capacity and priority management.
  • Problem solving and proactive approach.
  • Excellent relational skills and preparation for work in international teams.
  • Flexibility and ability to operate effectively in dynamic contexts and during business integration processes.

Languages

  • Italian: fluent.
  • English: level B2 or higher.

What we offer

  • The opportunity to join one of the leading international luxury groups.
  • A strategic role within an international integration project with high exposure to the different companies of the Group.
  • A dynamic, international and collaborative work environment.
  • 6-month fixed-term placement at the Group's Headquarters in Mendrisio.
