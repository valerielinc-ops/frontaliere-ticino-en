The Position The possibility Quality Control (QC) is responsible for the analysis of all products and incoming materials manufactured at Roche. QC assumes a wide range of tasks at the Kaiseraugst and Basel locations. The task of our team as a department of the QC is to check analytical data for accuracy and completeness in accordance with current regulations and guidelines of good manufacturing practice (cGMP). We play a central role in the coordination of laboratory analyses and the associated communication with site-internal and global interfaces in order to enable timely provision of the analysis results. In addition, the contact persons for product-specific inquiries and activities (single point of contact) are localized in our team.

These activities also include the processing of documents and the preparation of deviation reports and corrective measures. Due to our highly networked role, we are also involved in various cross-divisional projects for digitalization and process optimization.

Due to the wide working environment, there are numerous opportunities for you to make contacts in Roche Basel and Kaiseraugst.

As an intern in the QC You undertake these or similar tasks

  • Priority support of project management in the QC-wide project “Holistic Lab Planning”
  • Supporting the department and QC management team in administrative tasks and projects
  • Collaboration in the introduction of a tool for laboratory planning and forecasting in the QC
  • Collection and evaluation of key performance indicators

Furthermore, insights into the routine activities of the team are also possible. This includes, among other things, the processing of GMP documents, the support of the QC managers in the preparation and completion of the analyses as well as the cooperation in deviations or other records of the QMS (e.g. Changes, CAPAs).
