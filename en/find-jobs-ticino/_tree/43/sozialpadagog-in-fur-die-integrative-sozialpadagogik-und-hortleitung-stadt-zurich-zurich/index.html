Full description

The workload includes 43% ISR settings, of which 19% is limited to 31. July 2027.

Do you have a strong demeanor and big heart, enjoy working in a team and have a strong flair for behavioral young people? We are looking for you!

Since August 2026, a new school building with a day school at the secondary level has been in operation on Zurichberg. Around 200 students will find space for learning, growing and living together. In the first school year, the school is run with 5 classes, from summer 2027 with 8-9 classes.

As a young day school, we shape our school day with pioneering spirit, optimism and fresh ideas. Community and relationship strength shape our cooperation, while close cooperation forms the basis for a holistic and sustainable learning of our students.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for 1. 2027 or by agreement a social pedagogue who meets young people with empathy and clarity, builds sustainable relationships even in challenging situations and strengthens them in their personal development.

  • You are responsible for the planning, organization and coordination of lunchtime offers and carry out activities alone or together with the care team.
  • You build sustainable and trusting relationships with young people in ISR setting.
  • You support young people in the ISR setting in the interdisciplinary area and promote their social skills.
  • You work closely with the SHP and actively involve the environment of the young people.
  • one of the following CH-recognised diplomas: Social Work FH/HFS; Social education FH/HFS or an equivalent education. Kindergarten or teaching diploma of elementary school, university degree with major in social education, pedagogy or educational science
  • Professional experience in educational work and joy as well as ability to lead a large group of young people with benevolent determination
  • Appreciative treatment and competent communication with different contacts: inside
  • Patience, endurance and sensitivity as well as solution-oriented action in challenging situations
  • Ability to endure resistance and ambivalent without taking it personally

A challenging task in an open-minded and helpful team, in which cross-functional and close cooperation takes place. We attach importance to a solution- and goal-oriented cooperation as well as the development and development opportunities of the individual employees. We achieve this through a committed and very experienced school management, a competent management supervision and a well-organized school secretariat as well as an established cooperation with the school authority. In addition, you can look forward to progressive salary and social benefits according to the guidelines of the School and Sports Department.

Your assignment days are expected: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday (working hours by appointment). You work as part of an annual working period. During the semester, your workload is more than your contractual workload

  • You compensate for the additional working hours during school holidays. On Tuesday afternoon, after completing classes, you will participate in the school team’s sessions and other session vessels.

The Zurichberg School District comprises districts 1, 7 and 8 with the districts Altstadt, Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Riesbach and Witikon. The district school authority manages 18 school houses. These are connected to 43 kindergartens and over 40 care facilities of different sizes. Of the 14 organizational units, 11 are day schools.
