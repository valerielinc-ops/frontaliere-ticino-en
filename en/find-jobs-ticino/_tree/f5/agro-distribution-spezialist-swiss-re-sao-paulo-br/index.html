Full description

Make the farmers more resilient through the insurance solutions is our objective and we need people with this sense of construction.

About the Role

You would be responsible for expanding the Agro Insurance Business, seeking new solutions and developing Distribution Channels!

Some of these activities are

  • Define and drive the business expansion strategy for the Livestock/Bloodstock and Property Rural Lines as well as balance the Crop portfolio;
  • Develop the commercial strategy align with the underwriting leaders;
  • Define, prospect and monitor strategic distribution channels;
  • Support the Corso commercial structure in Agro Products

About the Team

We are a team of experts in Agro insurance, combining a good background and spirit of innovation to contribute with the development of this segment in the market.

About You

Must have

  • More than 2 years of experience in this Agro Insurance market, expertise in Crop Insurance, Property Rural (machinery/equipment) and Livestock;
  • Good knowledge in Brazilian Agribusiness and strong relationships with brokers and distribution channels;
  • Bachelor's Degree required , preferably in the following areas: Agronomy, Zootecnics , Agricultural Engineering or similar courses in rural science

Nice to have

  • Fluent in English and Spanish.
  • Rural risk underwriting experience
