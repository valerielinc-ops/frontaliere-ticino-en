Statistics count. Also for you.

The Federal Statistical Office (BFS) is the national centre of excellence for public statistics and a key player in the Swiss data ecosystem. It produces statistical information, works with the national data management for harmonised data and develops the knowledge gained from data using data science methods. The BFS thus contributes to the formation of public opinion and makes an important contribution to modern democratic governance.

Additional information

The Department of Vital Statistics and Epidemiology provides Switzerland-wide and regional statistics on deaths and causes of death, on reproduction and on the occurrence and treatment of cancer diseases – for the best possible information of the public, health, research, administration and politics. The statistics on causes of death provide important information on the diseases of the Swiss population. To code the causes of death, we are looking for a specialized employee.
