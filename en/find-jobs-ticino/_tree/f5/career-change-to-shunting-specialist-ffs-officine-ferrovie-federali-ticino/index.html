SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. At SBB Cargo, you are on the pulse of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 185,000 tons a day, relieving road traffic of 15,000 trucks per day and the environment of 460,000 tons of CO2 per year. An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you move the SBB Cargo together with your teammates. In addition, you will receive a varied and paid training in the marshalling station. In addition, various further training opportunities are open to you, such as training as a shunting engine driver. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even for irregular working hours. Night and weekend work is also paid for with us - for example with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night assignments, you also get time surcharges between 10 and 30%. This means that you have more free time than employees without night and weekend assignments. You can move that.

  • As a shunting specialist, you help ensure that our customers are served reliably and on time by preparing freight trains for their journey.
  • You take over the cars, carry out security checks and prepare the train through complex shunting movements for the next journey.
  • You collect the necessary data for the train with personal, modern IT devices (e.g. tablet).
  • Due to the timely delivery and parking of the cars with the customer, you have direct customer contact every day. You receive their feedback and forward it to the responsible body if necessary.

Here you will learn more about the job profile. You bring that with you.

  • You are attentive, focused and physically resilient to successfully handle this demanding work in 24-hour shift operations. You also like to work outside in any weather.
  • Ideally, you have completed a basic education or already have professional experience in the crafts, logistical or comparable field.
  • Since all instructions and documentation are electronic, you will quickly find your way around with digital tools.
  • In German (level C1) until you are fluent in word and writing.
  • You have a perfect color sense (no red-green weakness) and have no entries in the penal and enforcement register.
