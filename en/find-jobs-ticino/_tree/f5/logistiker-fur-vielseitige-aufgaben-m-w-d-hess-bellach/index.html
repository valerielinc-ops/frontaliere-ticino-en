Logistics specialist for versatile tasks (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company.

To complement our team in the Commercial Vehicles department, we are looking for a motivated logistics specialist for the production site in Subingen (SO). They are an established and flexible personality and love the dynamic environment in logistics. In our warehouse in the branch in Subingen we have the ideal job for you! In a motivated team you take on versatile and independent warehouse all-rounder tasks - with us you will never get bored! Their tasks

  • Reception, control and recording of receipts of goods
  • Proper warehousing and storage management
  • Commissioning and provision of workshop orders
  • Booking in the ERP system and maintenance of stockpiles
  • Carrying out inventories and inventory checks
  • Carrying out general storage activities and transport services (parent company, post office, etc.)

Our requirements

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as logistician EFZ
  • Several years of experience in storage logistics
  • Flexibility and interest to take on diverse tasks
  • High initiative and organisational talent
  • Reliability and teamwork
  • Good PC knowledge
  • Valid driving licence Cat B and stacker licence
  • German in Word and Writing

We offer them

  • Permanent employment in a successful traditional company
  • Varied and independent activity
  • Performance-based employment conditions, contemporary remuneration
  • Flexible working hours
  • Long-term perspectives

If you feel addressed, send us your application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
