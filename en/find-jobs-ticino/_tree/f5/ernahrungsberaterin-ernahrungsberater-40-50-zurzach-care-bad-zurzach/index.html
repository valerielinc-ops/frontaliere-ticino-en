Full description

What makes us special? Our cooperation.

As a small team, we are closely connected with the diet kitchen, speech therapy – especially in the area of dysphagia – care, the medical profession and the hotel industry. This lively exchange shapes our everyday life and makes our work versatile, exciting and personal.

Individuality is our focus: Together we are committed to providing patients with the best possible and needs-oriented support.

Own ideas are expressly welcome – you actively shape our nutrition concepts and bring new impulses into the further development of our therapy offers.

Tasks

  • Patient-oriented care: You carry out nutritional consultations in the areas of diabetes, obesity, malnutrition, intolerances, cardiological and oncological diseases, etc.
  • Further education and training : You design further education, training and lectures for patients, specialists and stakeholders and carry them out
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: You work closely with the areas of care, therapy, medicine, hospitality and diet kitchen

Conditions

  • Training : Completed professional training BSc in Nutrition and Dietetics / Dipl. Nutritionist/in HF/FH
  • Experience: You bring your first professional experience in nutrition consulting
  • Personality: You are a service-oriented, dedicated personality with strong communication skills, teamwork, flexibility and a great pleasure in interprofessional collaboration
  • IT knowledge : You bring good IT knowledge

We offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collaborative work climate: Work in a supportive and appreciative team that works together for the best possible patient care
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, pharmacies and our membership of Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
