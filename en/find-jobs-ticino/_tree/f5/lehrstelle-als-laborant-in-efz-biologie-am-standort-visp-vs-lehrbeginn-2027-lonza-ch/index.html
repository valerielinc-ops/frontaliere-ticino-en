Full description

Lonza is today a world leader in life sciences, operating on three continents. We work in science, but there is no magic formula for how we approach work. Our most important scientific solution is talented employees who collaborate and develop ideas that help other companies to help people. In return, our employees steer their career independently. Because their ideas, big and small, improve the world. And that's the kind of work we want to be part of.

At the Visp site, an apprentice laboratory technician is sought. As laboratories, we work in a team under the responsibility of biologists or chemists in the areas of research, development, quality control and small quantities of production. Tasks such as the development of biotechnological processes, monitoring cells on a small scale, cleaning and controlling products with specialized devices are also included. For professionals in a pharmaceutical company, the constant compliance with the given safety and labor regulations is commonplace. The exact documentation of the work steps and evaluation of the results on the computer is of great importance. They work with researchers from different countries and therefore often communicate in English.

Your education The teaching lasts three years. The first 6 months of the training, all laboratory specialists complete biology together in the Lonza training laboratory. Afterwards, you will go on a rotation of 5-6 months each in various laboratory areas of Lonza, such as research, development, quality control and small quantities of production. You attend vocational school lessons on one and a half days a week during the entire apprenticeship period.

Your profile

  • Interest in research and experimentation
  • Good observation skills and networked thinking
  • Fine motor skills
  • Clean and accurate work
  • High reliability
  • Interest in dealing with electronic devices and complex equipment
  • Very good knowledge of German (minimum B2 level)
