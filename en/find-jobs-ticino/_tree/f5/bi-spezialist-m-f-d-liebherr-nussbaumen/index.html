Tasks

  • Support and develop group-wide reporting solutions using Microsoft Business Intelligence technologies, including SQL Server and Power BI in close collaboration with business departments
  • Understand business stakeholders’ objectives and metrics and translate them into effective reports
  • Apply best practices in managing data sets and creating data visualizations
  • Ensure the applications’ continuous availability and upgrades
  • Maintain the systems documentation
  • Responsible for data security and data access performance
  • In charge of users-oriented training on report usage and interpretation
  • Contact person for questions and support-requests from end-users
  • Liaise with the Liebherr BI community
  • Identify and action opportunities to improve processes Qualifications:
  • Several years of relevant experience in BI reports development
  • Proven ability to facilitate requirements gathering to help customers define their needs
  • Ability to foster and maintain positive working relationships with teammates when addressing issues and other needs
  • University degree in computer science or business administration with adequate work experience as a BI specialist
  • Experience in the development and technical support of Microsoft Business Intelligence solutions, with a strong focus on Power BI and SQL Server, including DAX and Power Query
  • Strong understanding of data modeling for Business Intelligence, including relational and dimensional modelling, Star Schema, and Tabular models. Experience with SSRS and other reporting tools is an advantage
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving and troubleshooting skills, with the ability to independently investigate issues, identify root causes and develop effective solutions
  • Experience in Corporate Performance Management Reporting is a strong advantage.
  • Knowledge of CPM software CCH Tagetik or similar tools is a big plus
  • Fluent spoken and written English, German is a plus and French skills are an advantage
