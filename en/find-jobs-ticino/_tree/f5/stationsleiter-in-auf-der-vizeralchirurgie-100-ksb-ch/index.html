Full description

Your leadership makes the difference – for your team and our patients.

At our visceral surgery at the KSB, you can expect a versatile management task with a lot of room for manoeuvre. You contribute your experience and ideas, strengthen cooperation and together with your team create the basis for high-quality and human care. Do you enjoy accompanying people, promoting potential and actively shaping change? Then we look forward to meeting you.

Their tasks

  • They lead, organize and coordinate the team at Vizeral with empathy and competence.
  • They are responsible for the technical, organizational and personnel management as well as targeted promotion of employees.
  • Planning, organization and coordination of work processes according to principles of Lean Management.
  • They are actively involved in the further development of care as well as in future-oriented organizational, quality and care projects.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH with several years of professional experience in the acute hospital - preferably supplemented with management training or the willingness to complete it.
  • You are a responsible, team-capable, entrepreneurial and networked, motivated personality with a situational leadership style.
  • Other personality traits that make you stand out are good communication skills, negotiation skills and a large portion of empathy.
  • Multi-professional cooperation, a professional understanding of care and high customer orientation are a matter of course for you.

About us

The KSB provides safe and close to home healthcare for more than 300,000 residents in the eastern part of the canton of Argovia. All our clients benefit from high-tech medicine at the highest level. However, at the KSB the most modern technology never replaces direct contact and conversation with our affected clients and patients. We listen to their wishes and concerns.

More than 2000 employees in seven medical and nursing departments, as well as three administrative departments, care every day for the well-being of our patients. Specialists from various disciplines work closely together in eight interdisciplinary centers. The KSB is not only represented in Baden (capitol of canton Argovia), but also present at several external locations. We keep distances short and communication simple: We are close to our patients.
