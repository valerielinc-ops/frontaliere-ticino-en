A Day in the Life The Digital Health Delivery Lead – DACH & France is responsible for driving the adoption, deployment, and commercial growth of Digital Health solutions across Germany, Austria, Switzerland, and France. Reporting to the Digital Health Leader Western Europe, this role leads regional market development, coordinates cross-functional teams, and drives execution of digital health strategies within clinical pathways and healthcare systems. The position combines business development, stakeholder engagement, and operational leadership to ensure successful implementation and scaling of digital health solutions across the region.
