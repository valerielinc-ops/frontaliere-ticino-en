Full description

Pediatric cardiology is a department of pediatrics at the University Children's Hospital of Basel (UKBB). The range of services covers all areas of non-invasive pediatric cardiology and is continuously developed together in the team. In order to actively promote the interdisciplinarity of our specialist area, special consultation hours exist in cooperation with prenatal practitioners of the Frauenspital, ACHD cardiologists for the transition of cardiological patients and electrophysiologists of the department of cardiology. Cardiac section imaging using state-of-the-art technology is carried out in close cooperation with the radiologists at the UKBB and USB. There are also numerous clinical and scientific collaborations with all paediatric and surgical departments at the UKBB. As part of the Kinderherznetzwerk Bern-Basel, catheter intervention and cardiosurgical procedures are carried out at the Inselspital Bern. Regular interdisciplinary heart conferences and joint case discussions promote close professional exchange and enable care at the highest level.

Entry date: by agreement

Your profile

  • The position is suitable for already recognized child cardiologists as well as for colleagues in advanced training (at least two years). Structured training for one year in the field of non-invasive pediatric cardiology for the acquisition of the major title can be completed at the UKBB.
  • High motivation and strong empathy in the care of children and adolescents with heart disease and their families
  • Great willingness to develop and qualify in the field of predominantly outpatient and non-invasive pediatric cardiology
  • Participation in on-call services
  • Engagement in training of students and doctors in specialist training Pediatrics
  • Integrative, committed personality with pleasure in interdisciplinary cooperation

Our offer

  • A varied and demanding clinical and scientific activity in a dedicated team in which your commitment is appreciated and promoted
  • A modern infrastructure for the individual, interdisciplinary care of our patients
  • If interested, participation in scientific studies is supported
  • Diverse opportunities for continuous further education and training
  • Flexible working time models with a burden, which can be agreed individually
