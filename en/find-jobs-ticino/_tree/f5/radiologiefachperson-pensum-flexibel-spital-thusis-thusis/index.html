Full description

To complement our radiology team at the hospital Thusis we are looking for YOU immediately or by agreement as

Radiology specialist

Task area

  • You work on all modalities of the department (CT, MRI, X-ray).

• You will ensure high-quality and customer-oriented patient care.

  • Like the teammates, you take care of piquetting services with 31 minutes of intervention time on weekdays (Monday to Friday), weekends and public holidays (24 - 7 / 365 days).
  • You will also assist our doctors and radiologists with infiltrations and interventions.
  • In addition, you will do administrative work within the department.

Requirement profile

• Completed vocational training as a Dipl. Radiology specialist HF (CH) / Med. Techn. Radiology Assistant (MTRA) or equivalent training • Professional experience in a comparable capacity • Pronounced service orientation and high social skills • motivated, independent and team-oriented personality

We offer

• A flexible workload adapted to you. • a modern infrastructure on a new digital X-ray machine and computed tomography from Siemens and an MRI 1.5T from Philips. • an exciting, responsible and varied activity with daily new challenges due to emergencies in our tourism region. • A small, family motivated team. • Provision of a spare room and overnight accommodation near the hospital. • Contemporary and flexible employment and working conditions as well as min. 25 days of vacation. • professional training opportunities as well as numerous offers and advantages in the leisure sector. • a high leisure value in the touristic very attractive environment at any time of the year.

Good to know

This position will be rewarded with a bonus in the case of a direct application (i.e. without recruitment) after the first year of employment.
