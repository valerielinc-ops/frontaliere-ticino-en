Full description

Founded and headquartered in Switzerland, Avaloq is continuously expanding its global footprint with around 2,500 colleagues in 11 countries, and more than 170 clients in 35 countries. We are an industry-leading provider of wealth management technology and services for financial institutions around the world, including private banks and wealth managers, investment managers, as well as retail and neo banks. Our research led approach and continual innovation is powered by the passion and creativity of our colleagues. We are always looking for talented people to join us on our mission to orchestrate the financial ecosystem and democratize access to wealth management. Avaloq offers the opportunity to work closely with some of the world’s leading financial institutions as we jointly develop and shape careers. Championing a collaborative, supportive and flexible work environment empowers our colleagues to reach their full potential.

Join our Avaloq Cloud Solution Architecture team and play a key role in shaping the future of our modular, cloud-native banking platform. You will contribute to the design, development, and deployment of our Avaloq Banking Solution SaaS offering on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), ensuring scalability, reliability, and automation.

Your key tasks

  • Design and develop cloud-native architecture for Avaloq SaaS on OCI
  • Support Cloud Engineers and Developers to build solid automated infrastructure provisioning and configuration
  • Collaborate with internal teams and clients to deliver tailored cloud solutions aligned with strategic goals
  • Provide expert-level support and guidance on cloud architecture and DevOps practices
  • Troubleshoot complex issues and drive continuous improvement in system performance and reliability

Qualifiche

  • Proven experience as a Cloud Architect or in a similar role
  • Strong expertise in DevOps methodologies and Git-based project workflows
  • Solid understanding of Kubernetes architecture and deployment strategies
  • Proficiency with automation tools such as Terraform and Ansible is strongly preferred
  • Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and abstractions skills
  • Strong communication and collaboration abilities
  • A proactive, team-oriented mindset with a passion for innovation

It would be a real bonus if you have

  • Hands-on experience with Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) is essential; experience with other cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP) is a plus
  • OCI Certified Solution Architect preferred
  • Additional certifications in AWS, Azure, or GCP are advantageous

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status. Note: this listing allows remote work — teleworking above 25 % of your time can shift your social-security base to Italy, so confirm the agreed remote share with HR before signing.
