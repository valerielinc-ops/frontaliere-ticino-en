Full description

Are you enthusiastic about automation, find the right solution for every disruption and want to actively shape the digitalization of a modern industrial plant? Then bring your know-how to us and ensure that our production runs reliably.

What to expect

  • Ensuring the operational readiness of our automation, control and electrical systems
  • Analysis and correction of faults and performance of maintenance and maintenance work
  • Optimization of existing plants and active participation in digitalization projects
  • Support of emission measurements and calibration and maintenance of measuring equipment
  • Cooperation with production, maintenance and quality management as well as support for technical networks
  • Participation in the Pikettdienst and taking on further technical tasks as required

What you bring

  • Completed technical vocational training in the electrical sector (e.g. automatic technician:in or electrical installer:in)
  • Several years of professional experience in automation and control technology as well as good knowledge in PLC, measuring, control and drive technology
  • Experience in troubleshooting and troubleshooting as well as a high IT affinity
  • Analytical, independent and structured working method with a high sense of responsibility
  • Team spirit, organisational skills and interest in new technologies
  • Very good German, as well as good English skills in word and writing
