We are a family-run, modern physiotherapy practice with a spacious MTT/training room and a broad range of treatments. Due to the retirement of a team member, we are looking for a committed and professionally competent physiotherapist (60–100 %).

Your responsibilities

  • – Independent physiotherapy treatment of patients
  • – Preparation of individual treatment plans with a focus on active rehabilitation
  • – Treatment of orthopedic, surgical and, where applicable, neurological patients
  • – Careful progress documentation as well as appointment and therapy planning in day-to-day practice
  • – Helping shape practice processes and quality development
  • Your profile
  • – Completed training as a qualified physiotherapist HF/FH or equivalent
  • – SRK recognition or an ongoing recognition process
  • – Professional experience is an advantage, but motivated career starters are also welcome
  • – Independent, structured and responsible approach to work
  • – Teamwork skills, empathy and strong social skills
  • – Very good command of German (additional languages are an advantage)
  • We offer
  • – Family-run practice with modern equipment and an MTT area
  • – Collaborative, appreciative working environment
  • – Flexible employment levels (60–100 %)
  • – 5 weeks of vacation per year
  • – 5 paid days per year for continuing education and training
  • – Support with continuing education and training
  • – Long-term prospects at an established practice
  • International applicants

We support international applicants through the SRK recognition process and help with all steps possible on our part to
