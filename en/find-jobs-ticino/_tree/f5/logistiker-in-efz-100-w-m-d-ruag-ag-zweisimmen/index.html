Full description

Every day, around 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland. They ensure that the Swiss Army and other operational and security organisations can perform their tasks comprehensively at any time.

You can move that

  • Taking of incoming material in the assigned area of responsibility
  • Provision of material on the basis of setup lists
  • Completion of set-up orders, partly with preparation of the goods
  • Unpacking of incoming goods & packing of goods to be shipped
  • Preparation of goods for incoming goods inspection
  • Logistical support in current projects on site
  • Prepare material for the goods exit
  • Booking of receipts of goods in the ERP
  • Loading and unloading of trucks

You bring that with you

  • min. Completed training as a logistician EFZ
  • approx. 5 years of professional experience in inventory, warehousing or logistics
  • Organizational talent with high personal responsibility and independence
  • High sense of responsibility and a precise and careful working method
  • Strong service and team orientation
  • Flexibility and willingness to work, if necessary also outside regular working hours
  • Very good IT knowledge : SAP S/4HANA and Excel, as well as experienced handling of digital warehouse management
  • Very good German skills in words and writing, English skills are advantageous
  • Category B driving licence, forklift licence and crane driving licence must be of advantage

Don't you meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated people who want to enrich our team. We attach great importance to diversity and equal opportunities and our goal is to build a diverse team in which everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. We particularly encourage all individuals, regardless of gender, who may have concerns about accepting a job in an army-related environment to apply.
