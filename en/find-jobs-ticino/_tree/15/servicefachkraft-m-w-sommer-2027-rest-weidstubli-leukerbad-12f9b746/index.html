Full description

From the summer season 2027 onwards, we are looking for our renowned mountain restaurant above Leukerbad, directly at the valley descent of the ski slope and in the heart of the hiking trails, a dedicated service specialist (m/f) for our à la carte service.

  • We offer you the opportunity to work in a unique mountain environment and look forward to a long-term cooperation.

You help us, through your friendly and attentive service, that our guests feel welcome and comfortable with us. They have experience in the same position and pleasure in the profession. They are friendly, flexible, physically and mentally fit. They are capable of teamwork, work independently, fast and reliable and keep a cool head even in hectic times. They can quickly adapt to operational processes and bring a high level of operational readiness. Very good knowledge of German is required.

We offer a professional workplace in a lively daily operation with a very good working climate in a beautiful area at 1500 meters in the middle of the Valais mountains - close to heaven.

You can look at our company at weidstübli.ch to get a little insight.

We look forward to your complete application documents with photo by email.

Application documents, which are transmitted by post, we unfortunately cannot return.
