Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly.   • Compliance with hygiene regulations and assumption of domestic activities
  • Independent management of care documentation

You bring that with you

  • Teamwork, flexibility, resilience and responsibility
  • Good written and oral German skills

  • Interesting employee conditions such as free SBB half-ax and discounts for refueling, fitness, pharmacy, etc.
