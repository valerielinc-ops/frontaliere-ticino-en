Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Enterprise Division, we are looking for one or one:

Cash Manager announcement for business customers Your main tasks:

  • selling cash products in collaboration with business advisors,
  • advise clients on the various ERP or ERP software available,
  • providing technical support to clients for connecting their accounting and management systems to the BCV system, including their multi-bank software,
  • supporting enterprises in innovation, technological change and updates, in relation to developments in the payments and receipts market,
  • training clients in the use of tools (BCV-Net, bexio,...),
  • independent processing of external and internal cash management support requests
  • Bring its expertise to the migration and product development projects (technical specifications, opportunity study...)
  • promote BCV digital products,
  • ensure that innovations are launched on the market in order to maintain the knowledge of the sales force. Your profile:
  • 3 to 5 years experience in the technical consulting of integrated management software or ERP available on the market,
  • excellent knowledge of payment/cash standards and solutions, as well as multi-bank management (PAIN, SEPA, CAMT, etc.),
  • excellent understanding of the business market,
  • strong customer orientation and demonstrated interest in new technologies,
  • autonomy and entrepreneurial spirit,
  • mastery of French, knowledge of English an asset.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
