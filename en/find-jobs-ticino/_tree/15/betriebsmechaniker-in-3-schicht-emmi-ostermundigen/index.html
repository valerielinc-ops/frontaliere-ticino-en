Full description

Freshness in focus, technology under control - At the traditional location Ostermundigen, we passionately produce popular classics such as Emmi Caffè Latte, the finest yogurt and refreshing glace. In a dynamic environment that stands for freshness, speed and the highest quality, we are looking for people who want to think, shape and grow with us.

You can do that.

  • You are the first point of contact at the site if there is a malfunction on the machines or control systems.
  • You are responsible for troubleshooting and troubleshooting the failed machine or system with the aim of generating as few fault times as possible.
  • You exchange spare parts and take responsibility for conversions where necessary.
  • You make custom-made products so that our machines are brought back to work as quickly as possible.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed vocational training as a car mechanic: in, agricultural machinery mechanic: in or in a similar professional field and ideally already have experience in a similar function.
  • Bonus points are earned through in-depth knowledge of electrical engineering.
  • You are flexible and ready to work in 3-shift operation
  • You speak fluent German.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
