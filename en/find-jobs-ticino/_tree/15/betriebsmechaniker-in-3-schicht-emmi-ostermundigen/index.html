Full description

Freshness in focus, technology under control - At the traditional location Ostermundigen, we passionately produce popular classics such as Emmi Caffè Latte, the finest yogurt and refreshing glace. In a dynamic environment that stands for freshness, speed and the highest quality, we are looking for people who want to think, shape and grow with us.

You can do that.

  • You are the first point of contact at the site if there is a malfunction on the machines or control systems.
  • You are responsible for troubleshooting and troubleshooting the failed machine or system with the aim of generating as few fault times as possible.
  • You exchange spare parts and take responsibility for conversions where necessary.
  • You make custom-made products so that our machines are brought back to work as quickly as possible.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed vocational training as a car mechanic: in, agricultural machinery mechanic: in or in a similar professional field and ideally already have experience in a similar function.
  • Bonus points are earned through in-depth knowledge of electrical engineering.
  • You are flexible and ready to work in 3-shift operation
  • You speak fluent German.

This is what we offer you

  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Further education and development opportunities: At Emmi, the focus is on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
  • Service Anniversary: Loyalty pays off: Every five years, we reward your loyalty with a premium, which you can also receive in the form of holidays or as a one-time contribution to the pension fund.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
