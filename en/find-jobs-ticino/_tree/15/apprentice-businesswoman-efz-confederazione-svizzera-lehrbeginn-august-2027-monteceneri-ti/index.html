Put to the Point

You want to learn, work in a team, have direct customer contact and manage data via modern IT and communication media? Then the versatile and practical commercial training in the service and administration sector is just right for you. As a merchant you are the administrative part of the army logistics center. During your 3-year apprenticeship, you will get to know the different areas of responsibility in the company and thus become an all-round talent in the office and lay an optimal foundation for your professional future.

  • Date of entry: 01.08.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20539
  • Location: Army Logistics Center Monte Ceneri, 6802 Rivera

You can make this contribution

  • Take on various administrative and organizational tasks and work in the various areas of the Army Logistics Center
  • Organise and coordinate meetings, courses, events and other operational activities
  • Participate in internal and external communication and prepare and publish information and content through various communication channels, including digital media and social media
  • Take over accounting tasks and participate in the corresponding administrative processes
  • Create and manage emails, letters, logs, documents and statistics

This makes you unique

  • Compulsory education with good performance
  • enjoyment of languages, especially German and French
  • English skills are beneficial
  • Good communication skills, pleasure in dealing with people and interest in creating content
  • Reliability, accuracy, sense of responsibility and the ability to work increasingly independently
  • Teamwork and enjoyment of working with different contacts

We offer this

  • We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Diversity, dynamism, perspectives – teaching professions of the Swiss Army The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3,500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
