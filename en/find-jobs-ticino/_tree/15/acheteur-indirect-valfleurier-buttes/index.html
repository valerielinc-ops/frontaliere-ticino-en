Full description

Reference code: JR135037

Richemont owns some of the world, leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strength in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison representatives a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seems to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and design new products which are in keeping with our Maisons, through a process of continuous creativity.

  • Position to be filled as soon as possible -

Attached to the Procurement & Procurement Manager, your main mission will be to strategically optimize CAPEX investments (machine tools) and indirect purchases. Your expertise, in close collaboration and upstream with technical teams, will be essential to ensure the performance of our production tools. You will select the best solutions and negotiate global packages (service, long-term relationship), online with our standards of excellence, innovation and CSR commitments.

HOW DO YOU HAVE A IMPACT?

CAPEX Procurement Management (Machinery Tools & Production Equipment):

  • Strategic Partnership & Technical Integration: Collaborate closely and upstream with the Technical Engineering teams, acting as project manager and buyer integrated into tenders.
  • Value & Sustainability: Conduct strategic negotiations focused on co-construction, service and a comprehensive package (beyond the price), integrating technical, financial, legal, timelines, and CSR criteria, to secure supply and establish long-term commitments, adapting the approach to focus on relationship and service when the strategic issue requires it.
  • Responsible Technology Watch & Innovation: Evaluate and select emerging technologies (industrial IoT, robotics, predictive maintenance, digital twins) considering their environmental and social impact. Ensure constant monitoring of innovations, including sustainable and eco-responsible solutions.
  • Performance Management Suppliers: Develop sustainable partnerships, stimulate innovation and integrate CSR objectives in the monitoring and continuous improvement of CAPEX suppliers' performance.

Management of Indirect Procurement (Services, MRO, IT, etc.):

  • Strategic Portfolio Management: Manage a diversified portfolio of indirect purchases (maintenance, consumables, services, etc.) for manufacturing.
  • Rigorous & demanding negotiation: Negotiate framework contracts and commercial conditions, with particular attention to services and maintenance, to optimize costs, guarantee quality and define clear standards, including CSR requirements.
  • Challenge and panel development Suppliers: Identify, qualify and actively develop new suppliers. Implement multi-sourcing and systematic competition strategies to optimize performance, strengthen panel resilience and integrate sustainability, ethics and social compliance criteria.

Cross-cutting & Strategic Management

  • Optimization and alignment: Pilot cost optimization (TCO) and continuous improvement of procurement processes, in close collaboration with the Procurement Manager and the relevant department directorates for strategic alignment.
  • Risk Management and CSR: Proactively manage supplier risks (financial, technical, procurement, CSR) and actively integrate social and environmental responsibility principles into all procurement processes, ensuring compliance and fingerprint reduction.
  • Operational rigor: Ensure rigorous administrative management of orders, warranties and sourcing tools, ensuring compliance and traceability.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE USED WITH US?

  • You have 5 years of proven professional experience on a similar perimeter.
  • Your technical expertise:
  • Mastery - or strong appreciation of advanced production concepts and technologies (IoT, Big Data, IA applied to production, predictive maintenance, additive manufacturing).
  • Knowledge of machine tools and automation and robotics systems is a plus.
  • Your key skills:
  • Excellent negotiating skills, combining strategic firmness and diplomatic finesse, with an ability to defend the interests of the company while building lasting relationships.
  • Ability to step back and adopt a strategic vision to focus on long-term relationship and service where required.
  • Excellent analytical and synthesis capabilities.
  • You are invested and engaged, with the ability to go to the end of your subjects. You know how to ask for support when necessary to allow you to move forward and know how to have the right level of synchronization with your hierarchy.
  • Your excellent adaptability allows you to integrate into a complex environment, having transverse interactions and evolve into a multi-sector team.
  • You are autonomous, rigorous. Your sharp sense of priority management allows you to achieve complex projects within the deadlines set for you.
  • You have strong interpersonal skills: your relationship ease, your active listening and your sense of diplomacy allow you to engage your interlocutors in your issues.
  • You speak French. An intermediate level in English and German would be a plus.

WHAT DISTINGUES OUR GROUP?

Our true strength lies not in our similarities, but in the rich diversity of our arts, cultures and human skills, as well as our specific ability to encourage untapped potentials.
