Medical Assistant

Dianalabs Valais is a Valaisan medical analysis laboratory, a company of the Sonic Healthcare International Group.

For our laboratory in Sion, we are looking for

Medical assistant 8-100%

Your role:

  • Perform blood samples and participate in preanalytical
  • Telephone response to patients and physicians: common information about laboratory analyses, timelines, sampling requirements.
  • Record analysis and verification files.
  • Printing and sending analysis reports.
  • Carry out certain administrative tasks related to the organisation and day-to-day management of the laboratory
  • Reception and orientation of visitors.

Your profile:

  • You have a medical assistant CFC or equivalent.
  • You are versatile and comfortable on the phone.
  • You have already worked in a laboratory or a medical office: an asset.
  • You master computer tools and office work.

If you are, moreover, a reliable and autonomous person, with a strong sense of responsibility, confidentiality and quality, you are the personality we seek.

Only applications corresponding to the profile requested will be considered.
