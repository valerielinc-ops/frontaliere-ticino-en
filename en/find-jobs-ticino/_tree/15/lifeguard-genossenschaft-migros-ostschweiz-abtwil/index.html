Full description

The leisure facilities of the cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland allow many people variety, relaxation and sporting activities. With movement and relaxation offers, they provide a balance to everyday life and time with quality that the body and mind remember.

Luogo di lavoro: Leisure facility Säntispark, Wiesenbachstrasse 7, 9030 Abtwil

Mansioni

  • Supervising bathing operations and ensuring the safety of our guests
  • Implementation of sauna infusions and care of the sauna area
  • Performance of water gymnastics
  • Support and monitoring of the slide tower
  • Ensuring order, cleanliness and hygiene in bathing and sauna areas
  • Friendly advice and care of our guests
  • Compliance with safety and operational policies

Requirements

  • Professional experience: leisure
  • Valid Brevet (BIP) or SLRG Brevet Pro Pool and completed BLS-AED course
  • Reliable, responsible and self-employed work
  • Enjoyment in dealing with bathers and a pronounced service orientation
  • Teamwork, flexibility and a high level of security awareness
  • Initial or continuing education completed
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • L-GAV: Attractive Land collective employment contract
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Social benefits: very good social benefits in the areas of child allowances, sickness allowance and accident insurance
  • Mobile subscription: Highly discounted conditions for private mobile subscription also for family and friends
  • Offers in the Säntispark: Free access to bath and sauna landscape, div. Benefits in Säntispark
