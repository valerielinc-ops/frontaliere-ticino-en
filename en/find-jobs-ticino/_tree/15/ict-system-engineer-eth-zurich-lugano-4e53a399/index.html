Il datore di lavoro è alla ricerca di un/a

Systems Engineering / Storage Engineer (professione segnalata)

Mansioni

  • implementazione e la gestione di servizi di storage HPC all'interno di un'infrastruttura multi-tenant.
  • in qualità di Storage Engineer, contribuirai direttamente alla progettazione, all'implementazione, alla manutenzione e alla documentazione di servizi di storage destinati all'HPC.
  • il tuo contributo avrà un impatto diretto sullo sviluppo e sul potenziamento dei sistemi HPC, migliorandone la funzionalità e l'efficienza complessiva.

Requisiti

  • titolo di studio (laurea triennale o superiore) in Ingegneria Informatica, Informatica o un ambito tecnico affine, oppure un'equivalente esperienza pratica.
  • solida competenza in amministrazione di sistemi Linux.
  • aspetti e funzionalità dei file system (dati, metadati, dimensione dei blocchi, ecc.).
  • tecnologie di ridondanza dello storage (RAID, D-RAID, EC, ecc.).
  • soluzioni e concetti di backup (DMF, TSM, Veeam).
  • concetti di Object Storage e utilizzo/implementazione di servizi S3.
  • strumenti e framework di automazione, inclusi processi ed ecosistemi CI/CD (ad es. GitLab CI, HashiCorp Vault).
  • ottima conoscenza dell'inglese e buona conoscenza dell'italiano

È preferibile, sebbene non indispensabile – essendo previste ampie opportunità di apprendimento e acquisizione di esperienza sul campo – possedere competenze in:

  • file system paralleli (GPFS, Lustre).
  • cluster di storage Ceph.
  • sistemi di versionamento e flussi di lavoro CI/CD (ad es. ArgoCD).
  • sistemi di installazione automatizzata per server fisici (ad es. OpenCHAMI, MAAS o Foreman).
  • sviluppo di configurazioni Ansible.
  • sviluppo di servizi basati su Kubernetes.

Einleitungstext The Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS) develops and operates cutting-edge, High-Performance Computing (HPC) systems as an essential service facility for science. The centre enables world-class research through its scientific user lab, which is available to domestic and international researchers in academia, industry, and the business sector. The centre is operated by ETH Zurich and has offices in Lugano (headquarter) and Zurich.

Projekthintergrund With the convergence of HPC and cloud technology, supercomputing service providers like CSCS strive to improve our service portfolios that focus on large, complex, and diverse scientific and engineering applications. Effectively providing performant and capable storage systems supporting the needs of HPC platforms running on a multi-tenant infrastructure is complex and challenging engineering work.

To support these efforts, CSCS is seeking a Storage Engineer (Systems Engineering) to join our team in Lugano. The position is initially offered on a two-year contract, with the possibility of extension. This position is funded by a project that will enable scientists to benefit from large scale climate and earth observation data, and will involve collaboration with partners such as the ETHZ Geolab and C2SM, as well as international partners such as ECMWF, NASA and NOAA.

Aufgaben-/Stellenbeschreibung The main goal of this position is to implement and operate HPC storage services in a multi-tenant infrastructure. As a Storage Engineer you will directly contribute to the designing, implementation, maintenance, and documentation of storage services for HPC.

Your contributions will directly impact the development and enhancement of HPC systems, thereby enhancing overall system functionality and efficiency.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
