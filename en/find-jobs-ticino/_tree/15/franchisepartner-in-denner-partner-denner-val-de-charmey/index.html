Location: Denner Partner Companies, Rte du Centre 11, 1637 Val-de-Charmey

You want to become self-employed and lead a Denner with a very personal touch in your region? Let’s turn your entrepreneurial flair, your keen sense of the local market and our know-how as a leading discounter into something great: Your store.

Luogo di lavoro: Denner Partner Companies, Rte du Centre 11, 1637 Val-de-Charmey

Important information

Please submit CV, certificates, certificates in full.

Mansioni

  • As a franchise partner, run your shop independently as a Denner Partner or Denner Express
  • The Five and s’Weggli: Benefit from the know-how of the most successful food discounter and be independent at the same time
  • On the broad and cheap Denner range as well as on attractive weekly promotions fall back
  • Complement Denner’s range with regional products
  • Kantönli- or even Dörfligeist? We think it is a good thing! Your store brings life to the neighborhood or to the countryside

Requirements

  • Industry and leadership experience: in the retail trade (food) would be ideal
  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade, preferably in the food sector
  • Entrepreneurial and business thinking
  • Great commitment and bite to succeed with your store
  • Feel for the needs of customers at your location
  • An impeccable reputation and a starting capital of CHF 100,000
  • German (very good knowledge)

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of applications: We check applications received on an ongoing basis, create a pre-selection and release them to the manager for viewing. The decision on progress or withdrawal from the recruitment process will be communicated by telephone or email. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Expert Discussion: In a specialist discussion, specific knowledge relevant for the execution of the job is requested by the manager and/or another specialist and, if necessary, the team. If possible, there is time to get to know the workplace and team better in a free conversation. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
  • Getting to know the team: Whether someone fits into the team is an important factor for employees and employers when deciding for or against employment. In a casual framework, we therefore give the opportunity for everyone to get to know each other.
  • Final discussion before issuing the contract: Before the contract is issued, we hold a final discussion in order to clarify open questions of both sides and to regulate contractual details. If all points are clarified, the written offer follows. Duration to feedback: Up to one week
  • Offer & Contract Exhibition: After our oral job offer, the most important contract points are verified again. After agreement, the contract is commissioned and sent by post. Duration to feedback: Up to three weeks
