Full description

Entrance by 1. May 2027 or by appointment · Bruderholz, Liestal, Intensive Care · Job reference 13371 · Permanent employment

Tasks

  • As a vocational trainer you combine clinical expertise, educational know-how and leadership
  • You work as a dipl. Expert Intensive Care and get involved in the further education of our students according to the Swiss curriculum
  • You create an appreciative and motivating learning environment in which students can take on responsibility and develop professionally and personally
  • You plan and conduct clinical lessons, learning sequences, introductions, location and qualification interviews as well as examination preparations and exams
  • You are responsible for the planning, documentation and evaluation of the learning processes and ensure targeted training support
  • You work closely with vocational trainees, specialists and other interprofessional partners
  • You actively participate in innovations and optimization measures in the care and in the interprofessional context
  • As a member of the station management committee in the sense of shared governance, you bring your perspective and shape the further development of the intensive care unit together with you we ensure a professional, evidence-based intensive care at the highest level in the future.

Requirements

  • Diploma as nursing specialist (HF, FH or equivalent) and completed NDS in intensive care
  • At least two years of professional experience as a Dipl. Expert/intensive care
  • Completed pedagogical training (SVEB 1) or willingness to complete it
  • Joy of accompanying, promoting and enthusiastic about intensive care
  • Strong communication and social skills as well as reflective ability and team orientation
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method
  • Flexibility, resilience and enjoyment of organization and collaboration Sounds like you?

If you want to support our patients, your team and the KSBL with expertise, commitment and passion, we look forward to meeting you.

Benefits

Being fit & staying fit: Attractive offers around sports, relaxation & nutrition
