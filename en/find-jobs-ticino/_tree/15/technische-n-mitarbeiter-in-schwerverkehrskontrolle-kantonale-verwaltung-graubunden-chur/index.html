Full description

The heavy traffic control centers Unterrealta/San Vittore along the N13 make an important contribution to traffic safety on this central north-south axis. On behalf of the Federal Roads Office (ASTRA), the Graubünden Cantonal Police carries out technical inspections of heavy traffic and implements the relevant provisions. ## Mansioni

  • Independent performance of technical inspections on heavy motor vehicles (traffic safety, legal regulations, technical manipulations)
  • Inspection of load securing, weights, exhaust gas and AdBlue systems and other safety-relevant elements
  • Enforcement of the provisions of the Working and Rest Time Ordinance (ARV1/ARV2) and implementation of ARV operational controls
  • Sanctioning of established violations and preparation of legally usable documentation
  • Specialist support for the regional police and participation in training and further education ##
  • Requisiti
  • Completed vocational training in the field of vehicle technology (e.g. automotive diagnostician/heavy commercial vehicles, automotive mechanic/mechanic EFZ for commercial vehicles or agricultural machinery mechanic EFZ) or equivalent technical training
  • Practice in technical error analysis (handling of diagnostic tools POBD/TEXA), or the willingness to train in these diagnostic technologies
  • Knowledge of Swiss road traffic law (ARV1/ARV2) or readiness for training
  • Driver's license C/E, reliability, high
