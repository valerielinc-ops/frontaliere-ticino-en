Full description

Reference code: JR133472

Since 1833, drive by an unquenchable third for innovation and creativity, and inspired by the peaceful natural surrounds of its home in the Vallée de Joux, Jaeger-LeCoultre has been distinguished by its mastery of complications and the precision of its mechanisms. Known as the Watchmaker of Watchmakers, the Manufacture has expressed its inventiveness through the creation of more than 1,400 different calibres and the award of more than 430 patents. Harnessing 190 years of accumulated expertise, La Grande Maison With 180 skills bought together under one roof, the Manufacture creates fine timespieces that combines technical ingenuity with aesthetic beauty and a distinctively understated sophistication.

YOUR MISSION

Integrated into our Technical Innovation Unit, you will put your scientific and academic knowledge at the service of high precision watchmaking. Your main mission will be to analyze and significantly improve the performance and reliability of the wristwatch using advanced digital simulation. Through the construction of virtual models of complex systems, you will actively participate in the development of the mechanical movements of tomorrow.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT? •

Modeling watch systems: Build complete and realistic numerical simulation models representing the behavior of complex mechanical watch systems.

Correlate theory with reality: In-depth analysis of the results of the calculations and rigorous comparison with the actual physical measurements from our testing laboratory.

Conduct a methodological benchmark: Carry out a technological monitoring and comparative study of software tools and market simulation methodologies to identify the best performing solutions.

Optimize virtual models: Refine, calibrate and continuously optimize the digital simulation model to increase its accuracy and predictive power.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE US?

Training: Student in Master's or final year of engineering (mechanical engineering, microtechnical or applied physics), or PhD profile.

Software skills: Mastery of a 3D design tool (ideally Creo) and a digital simulation software (ideally Ansys).

Know-how: You have a rigorous analytical and synthesis spirit, a great intellectual autonomy and a natural passion for high-precision technical innovation.

Language: Current mastery of French.

WHAT WE OFFER

A unique immersion in an R&D team of excellence (Technical Innovation Cell) exploring future watchmaking technologies.

A subject of applied research with high scientific value, combining theoretical physics and high-end micromechanical.

Access to leading-edge software tools and laboratory testing facilities.
