Health Centre Fricktal AG | online since : 14.10.2026

Place of work: GZF Rheinfelden

Do you care for critically ill patients or those after major interventions with a lot of empathy and heart blood? Interdisciplinary cooperation is important to you and gives you pleasure? Then you are exactly right on our modern IMC – our care team is looking forward to your reinforcement.

  • You take on all the tasks of a qualified nurse and ensure a high quality of care.
  • You provide care and care for patients in complex situations and work with increased responsibility.
  • You plan, execute, monitor, document and evaluate the care independently, accurately and reliably.
  • You actively participate in the interdisciplinary visits as well as the daily rapports.
  • You are a member of the internal REA team and make an important contribution in emergency situations.
  • Completed training as a Dipl. Nursing professional HF or equivalent (DN II / Health and Nursing etc.)
  • Ideally, you will have NDK supervision care or are ready to complete it in a timely manner.
  • A reliable, independent and precise working method as well as a high sense of responsibility
  • Flexibility, resilience and excellent manners
  • Willingness to work shifts including weekend services
