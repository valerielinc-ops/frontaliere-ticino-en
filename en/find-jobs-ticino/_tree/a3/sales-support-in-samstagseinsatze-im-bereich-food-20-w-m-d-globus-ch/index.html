Full description

At Globus, our employees are the key to our success, especially those who leave the final impression on our customers. You are the final, radiant smile of our brand and ensure that every customer leaves our store with a positive feeling and wants to come back. For our Lucerne store, we are looking for exactly you—an engaged personality who, with passion and commitment, offers our customers a first-class experience.

THIS IS WHERE YOU MAKE THE DIFFERENCE

  • As a host, you ensure that our customers feel welcome and well looked after as guests.
  • You share responsibility for the freshness and quality of our high-quality products.
  • You ensure strict compliance with statutory and company hygiene guidelines and regulations.
  • With your positive charisma and your passion for our *delicatessa, you make a decisive contribution to the company's success.

THIS IS WHAT SETS YOU APART

  • You are passionate about hosting, have a talent for sales, and are fascinated by high-quality food.
  • You have impeccable manners and a stylish appearance.
  • You have excellent German skills and, ideally, one or more additional foreign languages.
  • Working Saturdays is absolutely fine for you.

BENEFITS FOR YOU

Together with around 1,700 other detail-oriented people, you are an important part of our fascinating premium and luxury world. You experience genuine appreciation, work in a great team, and we give you the trust you need to make use of your freedom and make a difference with your skills. If you know your craft and bring a great deal of passion, many career doors are open to you. We are happy to support you with internal as well as external training and continuing education.

You can also look forward to

  • an exceptional working environment with flat hierarchies in the heart of Lucerne.
  • performance-based bonuses and attractive social benefits.
  • up to 50% off products from the Globus world for you and your family.
  • much more...
