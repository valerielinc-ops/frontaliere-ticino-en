The Opportunity

  • Lead and manage strategic and operational projects for customers in alignment with Roche’s goals
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to optimize business processes and enhance customer experiences

  • Support sales and marketing teams with tender opportunities and contribute to customer sales development
  • Foster collaboration with internal teams and external partners to strengthen expertise and strategy

Who you are

  • You live in lubelskie voivodeship
  • Valid driver’s license and readiness to travel extensively within Poland
  • You have minimum 5 years of experience in project management, preferably in healthcare or laboratory settings
  • Proficient in modern planning and office software with proven experience in planning tools
  • University degree
  • Fluent in Polish and English
  • Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills
  • Excellent interpersonal and communication abilities, with a customer-centric focus
  • Demonstrated success in managing complex projects and collaborating across teams
  • Knowledge of hospital and laboratory organizational structures is a plus
  • A proactive team player with commercial acumen
