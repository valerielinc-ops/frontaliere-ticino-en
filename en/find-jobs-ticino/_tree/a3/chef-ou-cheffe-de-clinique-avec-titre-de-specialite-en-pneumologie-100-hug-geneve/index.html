Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to consult our 2025 retrospective by clicking here. The Department of Medicine (DMED) provides comprehensive and specialized care for patients in hospitals and outpatients. Driven by a holistic, human approach based on best medical practices, it offers reference care in many specialties (cardiology, gastroenterology, pneumology, angiology and nephrology), with 24/7 availability. Engaged in clinical excellence, teaching and research, DMED promotes innovation, continuous skill development and patient-centred care, while ensuring the quality of life at work and the efficiency of benefits. Within this department, the Pneumonology Department provides care for adults with acute or chronic respiratory diseases, including diagnosis, treatment and follow-up, in outpatient and hospital settings. He also specializes in the screening and management of lung cancer, interventional pneumonology, pre- and post-pulmonary follow-up, as well as the prevention and treatment of tuberculosis and pulmonary hypertension. This position is newly created and is part of the ALTER deployment, which aims to ensure that work organization complies with the Labour Act (LTr).

Description of post

As a head or head of clinic who holds a Pneumonology degree, you ensure all the clinical activities of the service and evolve in a university environment, at the interface of care, teaching and research. Thanks to a quarterly tournus within the various specialized units, you develop a diverse expertise covering both hospital practice and ambulatory with versatility. You are involved in the management of interstitial pneumonitis, pulmonary complications in immunocompromised patients or patients, pulmonary transplantation, interventional pneumonology and sleep respiratory disorders, in close collaboration with the Sleep Medicine Centre. You join a team of 9 chiefs and chiefs of clinics, 4 assistant doctors and 5 internal doctors, under the responsibility of the professor head of department. You work closely with a multidisciplinary team of specialized nurses, respiratory physiology technicians and technicians, administrative staff and the various medical centres and departments of the institution. You ensure the diagnostic and therapeutic management of acute and chronic respiratory pathologies. You are involved in the evaluation and monitoring of complex situations, including dyspnoea, tuberculosis, respiratory insufficiency and the various medical-technical fields of the specialty. You demonstrate resilience, clinical sense and a high analytical capacity in dealing with sometimes complex situations. Thanks to your sense of organization and your ability to prioritize, you effectively ensure your clinical activities while responding to multiple demands (patients, telephones, correspondence, meetings). You ensure that you maintain a clear, caring and appropriate communication with patients and patients, their loved ones and all the professionals involved in their care, adapting your speech to their understanding. Building on your team spirit, you show solidarity with the unexpected. As head or head of clinic, you are actively involved in the supervision and training of internal doctors in the medical department in the field of pneumology, by adopting a mentoring posture and sharing your expertise.

Qualifications

You have a federal doctor's degree or training deemed equivalent by the Medical Professions Commission of the Federal Office of Public Health (MEBEKO).

You benefit from or are close to obtaining an FMH specialisation in pneumology or you have an equivalent title recognized by MEBEKO and are in possession of the cantonal right of practice or are able to obtain it before you enter office with the cantonal doctor of Geneva. You demonstrate a strong interest in hospital and outpatient activity in both primary and tertiary facilities. Good clinical experience and knowledge of the hospital world are required. For non-French speakers, the French level C1 on the spoken and written test is required.

You have excellent communication skills in a multidisciplinary team and with the patient, you are a dynamic, flexible, committed and discreet person. In addition, you have demonstrated strong adaptability and team spirit and solidarity with team members.

Further information

You work in daytime, participating in the shift tour for nighttimes and weekends.
