Company description ##

dasteam ag looks back on more than 35 years of experience in the field of personnel services in Switzerland and is today one of the leading companies in the industry. With a total of 27 branches in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein, we are one of the leading national employment agencies. Our customer is looking for dedicated employees for sorting packages for his parcel centre in Untervaz.

Job description ##

In this exciting position, you are responsible for loading and unloading packages that can weigh up to 30 kg. Their main task is to sort the packages efficiently while maintaining the high quality standards of our company. They work in the evening shift. Working hours Monday - Friday from 18.00h - about 03.00h. This position requires physical fitness and the ability to move loads effortlessly. We are looking for motivated employees who are willing to work in a dynamic environment and make a valuable contribution to our team.

We are expecting ##

We expect you to be physically fit and in good health. You should be able to easily move loads up to 30 kg. Knowledge of German or Italian is required, as well as a quick understanding. You should be used to working in high quality, fast and independently. Great commitment and high motivation are essential for this position. In addition, a category B driving licence or another way to reach the place of work is necessary. A perfect reputation (criminal record extract) is also required.
