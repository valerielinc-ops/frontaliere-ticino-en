Full description

Drawer: in the field of architecture About the profession Drawers: In the field of architecture are technical employees in the team of an architecture team. After ideas, sketches and information from architects, they create project, work and detailed plans for all those involved in the construction – builders, craftsmen, entrepreneurs, authorities – and sometimes also for competitions. In many cases, they also create simple models of the planned buildings. On the site, they support the construction management. Invias Activities Architecture

  • Create project, execution and detailed plans of buildings of all kinds, for example residential buildings, shopping centres, office buildings and industrial buildings
  • Smaller projects and conversions independently design

Technical drawings and design documents

  • Making plans, sketches and models for construction projects
  • Check project drafts for feasibility
  • Create building input plans and forms for the authorities and take into account the various regulations (disabled building, fire protection, etc.)
  • For the sketches and plans both make technical drawings by hand and use computer-aided software, so-called CAD programs
  • If necessary, carry out on-site measurements to obtain accurate data
  • Create scale representations and 3-D views of construction projects

Project work

  • Develop solutions for certain parts of the project, making mathematical calculations and taking into account scientific laws and building regulations
  • Participate in the selection of materials
  • Communicating and presenting developed solutions when necessary
  • Apply expertise in the chemical and physical properties of building materials and materials, i.e. plan the right materials (e.g. insulation) for execution
  • Environmentally sound design
  • Execution of the planned work on the construction site and verification of the timely delivery of materials
  • Perform administrative tasks, such as creating offers and writing reports
  • Estimate the costs of the construction project and make estimates

Write project descriptions and create timetables You bring that with you For the profession of draftsman: in the interest in technical drawing and a good spatial imagination is centrally important. Accordingly, mathematics and geometry knowledge are fundamental. Drawers : inside can not simply design buildings or parks to your heart's content. They work in a team headed by architects and must follow their wishes. They are responsible for the feasibility of the designs and must propose detailed solutions. With the help of computer systems and corresponding programs, they primarily draw design and execution plans. 3-D models are nowadays part of the profession.
