The Position Are you looking for an exciting job that will give you valuable insights into the working world of Roche Diagnostics?

As part of our Customer Care Team, you support our customer:innen Training Center (KTC) in Vienna. Practical customer:inner training takes place on our devices. The Customer Care Team is the central point of contact for laboratory employees throughout Austria. This position offers you the opportunity to gain valuable practical experience and to optimally combine study and profession through flexible part-time (10 hours per week).

What to expect

  • Interface function: You coordinate the cross-departmental processes and activities in the Training Center.
  • Hands-on course support: Practical preparation and follow-up of the courses on site (e.g. boot up equipment, orders, goodie bags)
  • Administration & organization: appointment coordination and arrangement of trainings, travel organization for customers (hotel, taxi, etc.)
  • Data management: Course system, certificate creation and feedback evaluation in our training system (Roche Diagnostics Academy)
  • Process automation & AI: You identify potential for automation in our daily processes and implement it independently (e.g. with the help of AI tools).

Who you are

  • You are a student: in the field of natural sciences
  • You are characterized by a structured, independent and very reliable way of working and keep an overview of many parallel tasks.
  • You like to take part (hands-on mentality) and are a communicative, team-oriented personality.
  • Fluent German and good English skills in word and writing.
  • Organizational talent in the administrative sector, an experienced handling of IT systems, digitally versed
  • Flexibility

The position is limited for 12 months.

You can expect an annual salary of 37,800€ gross (on a full-time basis), as well as attractive benefits. Flexible working time models, company restaurant, company health promotion and a wide range of sports offers are just some of them. We look forward to your application.
