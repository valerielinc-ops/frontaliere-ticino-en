The Position The trainee acts as an operational facilitator to ensure the effectiveness of the procurement process, from the selection of the supplier to the final payment.

Key responsibilities Compliance Support and Risk Management

  • Due Diligence Suppliers: Verify that the records contain all required supporting documents (tax documents, bank documents, etc.) prior to SEO.
  • Risk Monitoring: Assist in the financial health assessment of partners (Due Diligence) to minimize the risk of failure.
  • Ensure strict compliance with the group's compliance processes and rules within the subsidiary.

Operations Facilitator Procurement (Transactional Support)

  • Entry Point Users: Serve as a first contact for internal customers on procurement processes and tools (SAP, Smartsheet).
  • Order and Litigation Management: Manage requests for indirect purchases and collaborate with accounting to resolve invoice disputes.

Contractual Management and Databases

  • Contract life cycle: Follow the signature circuit of the framework agreements and ensure an alert on the renewal of contracts coming to maturity.
  • Master Data: Pilot the actual SEO and vendor update in SAP and the subsidiary's IT tools.

Support for the purchase process

  • Conduct comparative analyses of financial tenders (RFI/RFQ) during calls for tender.
  • Help users follow the purchase procedure and guide them in their current purchases.
  • Update of G-site purchase for internal users.

Who are you? Required training

  • Recently graduated with a specialization in Purchasing, Supply Chain, Management or International Management.
  • An initial experience (stage or associative project) in the corporate environment is a plus, but not mandatory.
  • Current Mastery of French and English

Competences Required

  • High attention to detail and analytical spirit
  • Excellent verbal and written communication
  • Management of Priorities and Organization
  • Appetence for Computer Tools. A familiarity or willingness to learn management tools (SAP, Smartsheet or Google Sites) is essential.
  • Team Spirit and Proactivity
