The Position This is a fixed-term opportunity until the 31th of December, 2029

The Record to Report (R2R) Department is a global organization of over 650 experienced finance professionals working together to deliver financial services and solutions for Roche across the EMEA region. Our services cover financial management, period-end closing, capital and lease accounting, credit and receivables, manufacturing finance, and divisional reporting, ensuring compliance for 263 reporting entities in 96 countries with accounting standards (IFRS, GAAP) and providing assurance of financial integrity.

The Opportunity

As a Global Financial Reporting Specialist , you bring strong expertise in Month-End Closing (MEC), consolidation, reporting, data reconciliation, variance analysis, financial planning, and management reporting across functional, divisional, and global levels. As a Specialist in this field, you will play a key part in mentoring team members to build collective expertise while driving initiatives in simplification, automation, and standardization. Furthermore, you will influence future organizational directions by actively contributing to scoping and assessment workshops within the transition framework.

  • Serving as a Data Steward for various tools, implementing policies and procedures to ensure data integrity, quality, and end-to-end operational data access
  • Performing data validation checks and conducting extensive variance analysis to ensure accuracy and reliability
  • Acting as the business owner for key reporting applications in the P74 system (e.g., COPA, Logistics, GtN, and FinCo, and financial planning tool) and partnering with HQ Finance teams to provide insights and support
  • Representing the reporting portfolio and applications, sharing updates and insights in cross-functional stakeholder meetings (e.g., FGAR experts meetings, Aspire SME meetings, and co-creation sprints)
  • Providing first-level support for planning tool within Diagnostic division, resolving user issues and queries, and preparing training materials to enhance user proficiency
  • Coordinating Aspire transitions for related applications, involving central finance and IT teams, and driving the redesign of the reporting environment with innovative improvement ideas
  • Testing Aspire mapping to P74, collaborating with affiliates post-go-live, and providing feedback on reporting performance

  • Skilled in organizing diverse data into actionable insights
  • Demonstrating familiarity with SAP ERP Systems and financial reporting tools

  • Fluent in English with good communication skills
