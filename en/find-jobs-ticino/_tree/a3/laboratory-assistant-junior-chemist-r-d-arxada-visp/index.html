Full description

Arxada is a global leader in innovative solutions to protect our world. Our breakthrough technologies, deep regulatory expertise, and manufacturing and process development expertise help our customers efficiently protect food, health, and infrastructure through chemistry and biotechnology that promote sustainability.

We offer a broad portfolio of ingredients and services for various end markets, including Health & Nutrition, Home & Personal Care, Professional Hygiene, Paints & Coatings and Wood Protection. With customers in over 100 countries, the company generated sales of CHF 2 billion in 2024. Arxada is headquartered in Basel, Switzerland. Globally, the company employs 3,200 people at 24 production sites and 14 R&D centers, all committed to the success of our customers.

To strengthen our team in Visp, Switzerland, we are looking for a Lab Assistant. As a member of our Composite Material Laboratory at Arxada in Visp, Switzerland, you will plan and carry out laboratory experiments and application tests in the field of polymers and their composites in close cooperation with the project teams.

Main Responsibilities

  • Planning and execution of laboratory experiments in cooperation with project teams
  • Manufacturing and further development of product formulations
  • Laboratory syntheses on a gram to kilogram scale
  • Analysis and characterization of raw materials and end products
  • Execution of application tests on polymer and composite materials
  • Ensuring high safety and quality standards in the laboratory
  • Careful documentation of results and active knowledge exchange within the team

Qualifications & Experience

  • Completed training as a laboratory assistant EFZ, Bachelor's, Master's or comparable qualification
  • Several years of experience in chemical laboratory synthesis and formulation development
  • Strong security awareness and active contribution to the safety culture
  • Independent, structured way of working as well as enjoyment of teamwork and project work
  • Very good knowledge of German; knowledge of English is an advantage
  • Good MS Office skills (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) as well as affinity for computer-aided laboratory systems

Arxada offers best-in-class solutions in two clearly defined areas:

Microbial Control Solutions (MCS) focuses on threats posed by microorganisms to humans and the environment. This area comprises five independent business areas: Professional Hygiene, Home and Personal Care, Wood Protection, Paints & Coatings and Material Protection.

Nutrition, Care & Environmental (NCE) supplies materials for the production of composites in electronics, aerospace and other industries. In addition, the portfolio includes vitamins and nutrient components, high-performance chemical intermediates, and raw materials for a wide range of demanding applications.
