Raiffeisen is the second banking group in Switzerland and is a leader in the retail sector. A unique banking reality, where values and ambitions merge.

What are you waiting for?

  • Referent of Raiffeisen Banks of Italian Switzerland for investment issues, including services, solutions and consultancy tools, as well as for all development initiatives promoted by the Group
  • Provides competent and specialized advice and supports Raiffeisen Banks in the development of private customers on the theme “Investiments”
  • Provides competent and timely support for innovations regarding Raiffeisen Group's solutions and investment processes and general information on financial markets
  • Implement the Group's strategy in collaboration with Raiffeisen Banks' investment managers, actively promoting the Group's “Filosophy and Investment Policy”
  • Organizes, animates and carries out meetings for exchanges of experiences with investment consultants of Raiffeisen Banks, prepares and conducts training courses on the theme "Investiments" for the consultants of Raiffeisen Banks and makes available for demonstrations or roadshows aimed at customers

What are you offering?

  • University level education (university school or professional university, economic or scientific address) or federal diploma in business economy, with specialization in the financial market.
  • Multiannual and consolidated experience as investment consultant SAQ Wealth Management Advisor
  • Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously
  • communicative personality with a strong ability to synthesis and decision-making, organizational and autonomy
  • Teamwork availability and strong customer orientation and spirit of initiative
  • Flexibility and availability for frequent trips to the Ticino and Moesano region for the treatment of the market and in German Switzerland at the San Gallo and Zurich
  • Italian language (level C1/C2), with an excellent editorial and orthographic level and excellent knowledge of the German language

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status. Note: this listing allows remote work — teleworking above 25 % of your time can shift your social-security base to Italy, so confirm the agreed remote share with HR before signing.
