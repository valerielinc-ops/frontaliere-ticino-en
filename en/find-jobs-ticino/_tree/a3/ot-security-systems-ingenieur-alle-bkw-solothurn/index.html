Full description

OT and IT are growing together - and you are actively helping to ensure that our systems are stable, secure and future-oriented. If automation, cyber security and smart solutions inspire you, you are in the right place.

Your field of activity

  • You implement company-wide security requirements, standards (ISO 27001, IEC 62443) and regulatory requirements and monitor their compliance
  • OT Security Risks and Vulnerabilities Identify, Assess and Document and Track Security Measures
  • Security Incidents supports analysis, escalation and coordination with IT Security and SOC
  • You advise OT projects on security by design and secure system architectures and strengthen security awareness through reporting and key figures
  • The OT data center infrastructure with Fortinet FortiGate and Proxmox VE runs, maintains and manages virtual machines and infrastructure resources
  • You coordinate technical changes with internal and external partners, carry out functional tests, accompany acceptances and maintain the technical documentation

You bring that with you

  • You have completed an apprenticeship in electronics, automation or computer science or bring as a lateral beginner: in several years of relevant experience
  • Thanks to your experience with ICT or automation systems, you will be familiar with networks, OT security, risk management and standards such as IEC 62443 and ISO 27001.
  • With Fortinet FortiGate you are familiar and have practical experience with firewalls, network segmentation, VLANs, routing and VPN
  • You know Proxmox VE from practice and bring experience with virtualization, storage, clustering and backup as well as knowledge of TCP/IP, Linux and server infrastructures
  • Your own initiative, sense of responsibility and reliability distinguish you and you enjoy working in a team
  • You communicate very well in German and well in English
