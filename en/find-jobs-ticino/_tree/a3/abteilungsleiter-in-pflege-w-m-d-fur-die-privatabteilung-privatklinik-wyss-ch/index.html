Full description

Privatklinik Wyss AG is a leading specialist clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy. Special areas of expertise include the treatment of depression including burnout as well as anxiety and obsessive-compulsive disorders. The clinic offers its outpatient, daily clinical and inpatient services in the region of Bern, Münchenbuchsee and Biel and employs around 340 employees at all three locations. Department Head of Care (m/f/d) for the Private Department Employment Level 80 - 100% Employment Permanent location Private Clinic Wyss AG Start of work 1. March 2027 or by agreement #psychiatry, #responsibility, #teamwork, #development This position is suitable for flexible and independent colleagues with professional experience in psychiatry care with pleasure in an in-depth psychotherapeutic treatment of our additional insured patients. Whether as a beginner in leadership or a nurse with several years of professional experience in psychiatric care - we accompany you on your career path into leadership responsibility. In this position, together with the medical-psychological management, you will take over the medical-therapeutic, organizational and conceptual management of the private department. Your responsibilities

  • You lead the nursing team of the private department in terms of personnel and expertise: from induction to employee interviews to targeted support in everyday life
  • You regularly work in the nursing service yourself, look after patients directly and are therefore present and approachable for your team in everyday life.
  • Together with the medical-psychological management, you will lead the department and develop treatment concepts, processes and services interprofessionally.
  • You ensure that our patients are the focus: with individual, psychotherapeutically oriented care and a high standard of hospitality
  • You ensure a needs-based personnel and assignment planning and focus on participation: your team actively and independently shapes the service planning
  • You develop care quality and processes continuously, in the sense of shared governance with nursing and education expertise as well as with lean instruments such as the Kaizen board
  • You live an open dialogue and error culture where mistakes are considered learning opportunities and team well-being counts your profile
  • You are a qualified nurse AKP/DN II/HF/FH with a focus on psychiatry and several years of professional experience
  • You have a leadership training (CAS-Leadership or similar). Ideally, you already have leadership experience
  • The implementation of the care process and the constant quality development in care inspires you as well as a good work-life balance
  • You are an integrative leader, have a proven organizational competence, business know-how and a high affinity for hospitality We offer
  • With us you will find a working environment that is characterized by appreciation, team spirit and humor. A versatile job awaits you with exciting career and development opportunities
  • The development of attractive future prospects through a talent promotion model
  • An optimal work-life balance through coordinated assignment planning and opportunities for unpaid holidays
