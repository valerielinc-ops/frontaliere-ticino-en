Full description

Arxada is a global leader in innovative solutions to protect our world. Our groundbreaking technologies, comprehensive regulatory expertise, and our manufacturing and process development expertise help our customers to efficiently protect food, health and infrastructure – through chemistry and biotechnology that promote sustainability.

We offer a broad portfolio of ingredients and services for various end markets, including health & nutrition, household and personal care, professional hygiene, paints & coatings, and wood protection. With customers in over 100 countries, the company generated sales of CHF 2 billion in 2024. Arxada's headquarters are located in Basel, Switzerland. The company employs 3,200 people worldwide at 24 production sites and 14 R&D centers, all of which are committed to the success of our customers.

To strengthen our team in Visp, Switzerland we are currently looking for an operator.

As an operator, you support our production plants as a valuable member of a dedicated shift team. With your commitment, you ensure that our production plants run reliably, safely and efficiently. You actively contribute to strengthening our safety culture, work flexibly in various areas of production and shape how we master challenges and continuously grow beyond us.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Alternating activity in the measuring station and in the production facilities of a multi-stage operating department.
  • Monitoring, control and analysis of process parameters in the measuring station as well as detection and repair of faults.
  • Safe entry and exit of production facilities according to specifications.
  • Flexible use in the assigned facilities on site, including support for conversions and maintenance work.
  • Self-employed execution and complete documentation of work processes and tours according to specifications.
  • Working with Outlook, Excel, Word, SAP and various process control systems.

Qualifications and experience

  • High standards of a lived safety culture.
  • Advantage: Experience as an operator in chemical production or service companies as well as in the handling of process control systems – cross-entry is expressly welcome.
  • willingness to work in the 4 shift system (early, late, night and weekend services) and flexibility within shift teams.
  • Very good German language skills in word and writing for the safe application of work instructions, safety regulations and internal systems.
  • willingness to learn new technologies and to continue to train.
  • Self-employed, reliable and committed working methods, team orientation and resilience.High awareness of safety and quality standards.

Arxada offers first-class solutions in two clearly defined areas:

M.C.C. (MCS) focuses on threats posed by microorganisms for humans and the environment. This division comprises five separate business areas: professional hygiene, household and personal care, wood protection, paints & coatings, and material protection.

The portfolio also includes vitamins and nutrient components, high-performance chemical intermediates and raw materials for a wide range of demanding applications. The portfolio also includes materials for the manufacture of composites in electronics, aerospace and other industries.
