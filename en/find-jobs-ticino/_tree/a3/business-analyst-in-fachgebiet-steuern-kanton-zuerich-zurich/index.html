Full description

From now on or after agreement, we are looking for a motivated business analyst. In this challenging position, you will play an important role in the project and portfolio management team at the Cantonal Tax Office. The function is characterized by exciting tasks and complex challenges with different stakeholders. Your tasks

  • You are flexible in all areas of the tax office and support the departments in the methodical and strategic development of their areas of expertise.
  • You take requirements, identify weaknesses and develop suggestions for improvement. You accompany the stakeholders through the process of needs assessment, ensure the business case, define acceptance criteria for change requests and projects, carry out tests and organize user training if necessary.
  • In addition, you advise the clients regarding organizational optimizations and economic efficiency. In the coming years, the application landscape will be completely renewed, in this project you can also contribute your experience in business analysis and actively contribute to the design of future-proof solutions.
  • In individual cases, you lead projects and/or sub-projects. Your profile
  • You have proven experience as a business analyst and ideally have tax expertise. In addition, you bring a higher education in business analysis, business administration, engineering. You can critically review and classify requirements from different stakeholders and use them for the optimal alignment of processes, IT support and structures.
  • Certifications in HERMES and/or IPMA certifications are beneficial.
  • You can structure tasks, approach them systematically and complete them on schedule.
  • You are empathetic, have a strong communication ability and feel comfortable being the contact person for the relevant stakeholders. If you want to promote digitalization in the cantonal tax office, you can expect an exciting task with a high degree of independence and personal responsibility. A comprehensive familiarization is a matter of course for us. With us you will find much more than just a job - we create an inspiring working environment for our employees: Your work-life balance is important to us, so we support you with home office opportunities and flexible working hours. In addition, we attach great importance to social security, a collegial working environment and a modern workplace just a few minutes walk from Zurich Altstetten station. The Financial Directorate employs around 1200 people at four locations in Zurich. We supervise the dossiers finance, taxes, personnel, procurement and computer science.
