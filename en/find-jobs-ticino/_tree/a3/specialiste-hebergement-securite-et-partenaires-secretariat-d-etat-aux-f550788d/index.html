Full description

Chiasso (and work at home)

What you can contribute to

  • Ensure the proper functioning of Federal Refugee Centres (FACs) and other MSS sites, coordinate operational activities and contribute to the development of solutions tailored to the needs of the service
  • Coordinate and supervise service providers by ensuring the quality of services provided and compliance with existing provisions and agreements
  • Manage the occupancy and hosting capacity of structures and contribute to effective and flexible planning of available resources
  • To act as an interlocutor for authorities, institutional partners and service providers, by promoting dialogue, collaboration and the search for concerted solutions
  • Identify and assess rapidly situations of particular vulnerability, social fragility or complexity, identify the specific needs of the persons concerned and contribute to the coordination of appropriate support and support measures

What makes you unique

  • University degree or equivalent training, preferably in social work, social sciences, psychology, social pedagogy or related fields
  • Professional experience in managing complex situations and working with professional and institutional networks
  • Excellent organisational and coordination capacities, combined with a strong capacity for operational management and priority setting, as well as a readiness to ensure regular presence in the different CFAs of the procedural region, in particular between Ticino and Central Switzerland
  • Excellent relational and communication skills, with great ease in building trust relationships and working with diverse interlocutors
  • Ability to develop pragmatic and sustainable solutions while maintaining an overall vision in a dynamic environment
  • Good active knowledge of a second official language and good knowledge of English

In a few words

In this function, you will contribute to the proper functioning of the Federal Refugee Centres by coordinating many partners and interlocutors in a dynamic operational environment. With your organizational and relational skills, you will promote effective collaboration and develop pragmatic solutions for the benefit of all stakeholders.

  • Occupational health We assist and advise our collaborators on all matters related to physical or mental health.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
