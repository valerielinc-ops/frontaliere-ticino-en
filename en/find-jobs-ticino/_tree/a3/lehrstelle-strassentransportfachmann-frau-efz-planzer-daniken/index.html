Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who think ahead, pitch in and want to grow with us. Become part of our family and help shape the future of transport and warehousing logistics with us.

What you do

  • You transport our customers' goods safely and on time using trucks, truck-and-trailer combinations or tractor-trailers
  • Before every trip, you inspect your vehicle, load it correctly and unload it at the destination
  • You comply with national transport regulations at all times, including when handling dangerous goods
  • You travel within Switzerland, observe traffic rules and drive in an environmentally friendly manner
  • You keep your vehicle in good working order, maintain it and ensure it is ready for use
  • In your daily contact with customers, you are always friendly and helpful
  • During your apprenticeship, you will also gain insight into a truck workshop

Why you succeed

  • Technology fascinates you, and you also have manual dexterity
  • Independence, reliability and calm under time pressure are among your strengths
  • With physical fitness and the ability to cope with stress, you handle your working day on the road
  • Openness, sociability and friendliness characterize the way you interact with others

Your benefits

  • Family-oriented company culture: With us, respect, trust and fair treatment matter – we are on a first-name basis and support you personally on your journey.
  • Holidays: In 1. Apprenticeship year you have 8, in 2. 7 and 6 weeks of vacation in the following years.
  • Events: During your apprenticeship with us, you take part in various events such as, WelcomeDay, in-depth seminar, apprentice camp, apprenticeship graduation ceremony etc.
  • Financial support: With us, it's not just the pay that's right: You receive a fair apprentice wage plus a 13th-month salary. If you give your all at school, in inter-company courses and at the company, you can even secure a performance bonus. We cover the cost of your school materials and give you a financial contribution towards a laptop. On top of that, you receive a Half Fare Travelcard for your entire apprenticeship.
  • Talent development: We offer you good opportunities for advancement and development.
  • Driving licences for road transport: In road transport, you have to obtain driving licences B/BE and/or C/CE. We support you and cover the costs of the training.

Who we are For us as a company, supportive collaboration and a positive working atmosphere are central. That is why we particularly value qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness, and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty, and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
