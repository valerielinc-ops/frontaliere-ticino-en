Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate

  • that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family-owned company today employs over 2500 people from more than 80 countries, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters is located in the canton of Glarus. Against a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates and constantly develop new, innovative ideas in agile teams. The «Retail» business unit includes over 250 chocolateries in over 30 countries. Here, we offer our chocolate specialties and invite customers to experience fresh chocolate again and again.
