Full description

The Solothurn Health Office ensures easy access to safe and high-quality health services in the canton. The Department of Old-Age Care and Addiction Assistance examines and grants authorisations to work as a person or institution in the long-term and Spitex sectors, is responsible for the implementation of the care initiative and is responsible for the cantonal Coordination Office for Addiction Assistance. For the Department of Age, Care & Addiction Assistance, we are looking for a specialist expert at the Solothurn location on 01.01.2027 or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • The approval and supervision of social institutions in the areas of age, care and addiction is your responsibility, you check the service quality and ensure compliance with the legal requirements in accordance with the social and health law.
  • They advise older people with care and care needs as well as their relatives and support sponsorships and home management in technical issues.
  • Complaints are examined and assessed in terms of content and, in cooperation with the Legal Service, they develop the relevant provisions.
  • They are involved in subject-specific projects, participate in the development, elaboration and updating of concepts and documents and contribute to the continuous improvement of workflows and processes.
  • You represent the department in committees and working groups and participate in conferences and further education at federal, cantonal and municipal level.

Your profile

  • You have a Bachelor or Master of Science in Nursing as well as several years of professional experience in the field of long-term care.
  • You are familiar with the various professional groups and institutions in the healthcare sector and you have experience in working on projects together with interdisciplinary partners.
  • They have a strong interest in social, economic and health issues.
  • A high level of social competence, a secure and convincing appearance, strong communicative skills and analytical and scientific way of thinking distinguish you.
  • They act in a solution-oriented and flexible manner, show their own initiative and are ready to get involved in different areas of work.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
