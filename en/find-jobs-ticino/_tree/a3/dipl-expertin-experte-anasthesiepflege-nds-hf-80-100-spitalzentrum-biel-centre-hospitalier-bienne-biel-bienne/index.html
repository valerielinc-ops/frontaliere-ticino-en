Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

Would you like to contribute your expertise in anesthesia care in a versatile environment and professionally accompany both adult patients and children? With us, you can expect a responsible job, a committed interprofessional team and the opportunity to actively shape the further development of our anesthesia care. We are looking for anaesthesia / recovery room – children and adults by appointment

Dipl. Expert / Expert Anesthesia Care NDS HF, 80–100 %

You do.

In this versatile function, you play a central role in the professional and safe care of our patients around anesthesia:

  • They look after and care for patients during regional and general anesthesias under the responsibility of the competent specialist.
  • They are active in both adult and pediatric anesthesia.
  • In the operating room, you ensure competent, safe and patient-oriented care.
  • In life-threatening situations, you recognize changes at an early stage and initiate the necessary measures.
  • They support the proper storage of patients.
  • Depending on requirements, you will take on further interdisciplinary tasks and contribute to a constructive cooperation in the treatment team.

That sets them apart.

You are a committed specialist who keeps an overview even in challenging situations and attaches importance to a professional, appreciative cooperation.

  • Completed specialist training in anesthesia care with Swiss or equivalent certificate of competence
  • Experience in pediatric anesthesia is beneficial, but not a requirement
  • High sense of responsibility and an independent and reliable working method
  • Flexibility, commitment and pleasure in professional development
  • Open, constructive and team-oriented communication
  • Good general IT user knowledge
  • Very good knowledge of German or French as well as knowledge of the other language

They benefit from it.

The healthcare sector and thus the Biel Hospital Center are in an exciting transformation phase. This requires initiative, expertise and the willingness to actively shape change. With us, you will have the opportunity, together with a motivated management and care team, to shape the future of the Biel Hospital Center. She expects a varied working environment with high professional responsibility and interprofessional cooperation. At the same time, we attach importance to attractive framework conditions and the compatibility of work and private life. This includes a family-friendly personnel policy, a company-owned KITA, contemporary employment conditions and good accessibility with public transport.
