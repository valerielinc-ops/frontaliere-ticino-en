CHAUFFEUR KAT. C/E (m/f/d) | Soldier / Springer | Full-time or part-time You love driving, like variety and still want to be home every night? Then you are exactly right with us! For our Härkingen location, we are looking for flexible and reliable chauffeurs in the general cargo sector who like to jump in where they are needed. Whether full-time, part-time or as a re-entry into the chauffeur profession – various working models are possible with us. Your job – versatile instead of monotonous

  • You take over as a detacher different day tours
  • You are travelling with different vehicle combinations – from solo vehicles to trailer trains and semi-trailers to city liners
  • You jump in flexibly and ensure that our transports roll reliably
  • You have contact with our customers and represent our company on the go

You bring that with you

  • Cat C/E driving licence and general cargo experience
  • Enjoy driving and dealing with different vehicles
  • Flexibility, independence and a reliable working method
  • Readiness for entire and sometimes longer working days
  • An uncomplicated way and pleasure in a varied work day

This is what awaits you with us

  • Full-time or part-time: Together we find a suitable employment model. Important! It must be whole days
  • Ideal for re-entry: Do you want to return to the wheel after a family break? We look forward to seeing you!
  • Every night at home: No nights away
  • Variety guaranteed: different tours, vehicles and customers
  • Modern vehicle fleet: Well-equipped vehicles for your everyday work
  • Strong employer: A family-driven Swiss logistics company with progressive employment conditions and good social benefits

Want to get back on track? Whether you want to go full speed or consciously work part-time: If you like to drive trucks, are flexible and appreciate variety, we want to get to know you.
