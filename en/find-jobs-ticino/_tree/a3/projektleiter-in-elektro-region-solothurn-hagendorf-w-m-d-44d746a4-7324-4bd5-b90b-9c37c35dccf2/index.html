Full description

Pensum: 80% - 100%

You can do this

  • You plan and realize electrical projects in the Solothurn region independently and responsibly.
  • You create submission and offer documents efficiently, structured and cost-consciously.
  • You will conduct project meetings and work closely with architectural offices, building owners and specialist planners.
  • You develop plan and schematic documents with the help of CAD programs.
  • You accompany the CKW offices of the Solothurn region and ensure the smooth implementation of their customer projects through close cooperation.
  • You deepen your installation expertise in future-oriented areas such as e-mobility, photovoltaics and heat pumps through internal training.

That makes you stand out

  • You have completed a higher education in the field of electrical at the level of project management or master or are in appropriate training.
  • You are very familiar with the relevant norms and guidelines (NPK, Steiger, NIN).
  • You are strong in communication and like to engage in customer acquisition.
  • You have a valid driver’s license.
