City of Locarno — public competition in Locarno (TI), Switzerland.

Bastian Lerch BL/cz 20 July 2026 ADVISO DI CONCORSO IL MUNICIPIO DI LOCARNO makes it known that the competition for the indefinite assignment of one/one is open: auxiliary/school institutes at our school canteen for students and students of the elementary school of Solduno (local canteen at the Istituto San Carlo). The function of auxiliaries of school institutions deals with the management of the kitchen adjacent to the cafeteria SE located at the Institute for the elderly San Carlo. In particular, it includes washing of the dishes used by children and girls enrolled in the canteen and the reorganization and cleaning of the kitchen and the canteen room, at the end of the meals. The commitment is focused on four working days (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday) in the time slot between 12.30 and 14:30 and coincides with the school calendar. Required Requirements

  • Suitable for practical/manual work (washing, reorganizing, cleaning, displacement of dishes and light materials);
  • Attention to hygiene and cleaning;
  • Italian language knowledge;
  • Ability to work independently in the assigned tasks, as well as to collaborate with the staff of the canteen and the school. The entry into service is scheduled from Monday 31 August 2026. Remuneration The position is framed in the role of auxiliary/school institutes class 2 (according to the Organic Regulation of Collaborators and Locarno Collaborators). The expected salary fork, for 100% use, starts from CHF 45'405.- up to a maximum of 68'108.-, to which allowances for children must be added (CHF 2'580.- /CHF 3'216.-). This notice of competition is also visible on the website of the City at the following address www.locarno.ch/it/albo- comune/assunzioni-personale. The City Hall cannot, in any case, consider applications and attachments transmitted through other channels (paper, email, fax, etc.) Applications must be submitted exclusively through the online portal by Monday, August 3, 2026 at 23:59 accompanied by the following documents:
  • curriculum vitae and motivation letter;
  • certificates of study and work;
  • medical certificate or self-certification downloadable on www.locarno.ch - virtual door section - different forms;
  • copy identity document; 2
  • copy work permit for persons of foreign nationality;
  • photo passport size. The extract of the judicial box and the certificate of the Office of executions and bankruptcies will be required when convening the selection interview. Any further information may be requested directly to the deputy director of municipal schools, Mrs Tamara De Albuquerque at 091 756 34 27 or by e-mail at dealbuquerque.tamara@locarno.ch.

Sector:* Public Administration
