Full description

We like lakes, we like nature, we like our guests – and we like people who want to contribute together with us to the successful future of BLS shipping. We offer our employees a unique, varied and stable environment. With us, new horizons can be discovered. With us, you will have the opportunity to supplement your craftsmanship with a nautical training for sailors, machinists or even skippers.

This is waiting for you

  • Our national and international guests provide convincing and competent advice and care
  • Checks and sales of tickets
  • Preparation and follow-up of ships
  • Collaboration in nautical service (we train you)
  • Maintenance and renewal of our fleet in winter

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed training metalworkers: in EFZ and some years of professional experience
  • Category B driving licence
  • Enjoyment of dealing with guests from different cultures
  • Language skills: Very good German, oral English & French skills (or willingness to learn them)
  • Strong sense of security and responsibility
  • Readiness for irregular work assignments (weekend / evening)

Your advantages – you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2. Class or a half-tax subscription, as well as attractive family benefits and free international free tickets.
  • You have free time when others work. You can work part-time, and there are surcharges for pens at night and on weekends.
  • It is possible to buy an additional five to ten holiday days per year.
  • We contribute 60 percent of the pension fund contributions.
  • Generous gifts of seniority from five years of service.
  • A dreamlike place of work awaits you in the heart of the Bernese Oberland.

About BLS shipping

White ships. Blue water. Fresh air. The mountains of the Bernese Oberland. What almost sounds like cliché is nothing more than the fascination of shipping. For around 120 employees on and on Lake Brienzer and Thunersee, this is part of daily reality.

  • Sprachkenntnisse: Sehr gut Deutsch, mündliche Englisch- & Französischkenntnisse (oder Bereitschaft diese zu erlernen)

  • Bereitschaft für unregelmässige Arbeitseinsätze (Wochenende / Abend)

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
