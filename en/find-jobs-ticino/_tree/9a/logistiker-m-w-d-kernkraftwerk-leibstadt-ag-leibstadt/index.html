Full description

Your tasks

  • Reception and control of deliveries (delivery notes, purchase order, quantity, identification numbers, visual inspection)
  • Recording in the ERP / storage system and professional storage incl. Marking and documentation
  • Commissioning, packaging, provision and transfer of material for maintenance, projects and audits
  • Disposal of storage items (order proposals, appointments, priorities)
  • Accounting of all material movements as well as participation in inventories incl. Stock corrections
  • Monitoring of minimum distances and reporting of material management/purchasing needs
  • Dealing with quality-relevant components (securing traceability, compliance with approval processes)
  • Storage of hazardous materials according to legal and operational requirements
  • Cooperation with purchasing, materials management, maintenance, radiation protection, quality assurance and forwarding
  • Cooperation in import and export processing (checking documents, securing customs-relevant documents)
  • Representation of the camp area in internal bodies and working groups
  • Use in shift or piketting during revisions

Your profile

  • Training as a logistician/warehouse operator or comparable professional experience in the industrial environment
  • Practice in dealing with ERP systems (preferably SAP) and technical documentation
  • Category BE driving licence
  • High safety and quality awareness as well as careful, independent and reliable working methods
  • Team-capable, communicative, resilient and flexible (readiness for overtime, shift/picquet service, weekend work in revisions)
  • Physically resilient; ID as forklift driver and crane operator or willingness to purchase it
  • Experience in a regulated environment (e.g. energy, pharmaceutical, chemical, aerospace, railway) is beneficial
  • Knowledge of dangerous goods/hazardous materials as well as import/export processing is advantageous
