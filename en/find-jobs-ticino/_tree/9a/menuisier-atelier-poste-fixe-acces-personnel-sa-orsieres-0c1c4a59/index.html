Full description

Personal Acès recognizes all talents

As part of the development of new projects and the growth of its business, our customer wants to strengthen its production team on a sustainable basis. We are looking for an established carpenter who is passionate about woodworking, concerned about the quality of the finishes and motivated to participate in high-level tailor-made projects.

This opportunity will allow you to integrate a recognized company in the region of Entremont, where know-how, quality and team spirit are at the heart of the concerns.

Your missions

  • Manufacture of custom furniture and fixtures in the workshop
  • Assembly and assembly of wood elements according to project plans and requirements
  • Finishing work with a high level of precision
  • Reading and interpreting technical plans
  • Preparation and organization of works before manufacture
  • Use of conventional machinery and workshop equipment
  • Specific work of old wood and development of noble materials
  • Participation in special projects and high-end achievements

Your profile

  • Essential requirements
  • Carpenter CFC or equivalent
  • Minimum 3 years experience in the field
  • Excellent knowledge of old wood work
  • Confirmed experience in manufacturing and assembly in workshop
  • Sense of detail and quality finishes
  • Ability to work independently and rigorously

Your assets

  • Team spirit and sense of responsibility
  • Passion for work well done
  • Flexibility and flexibility
  • Interest in tailor-made and high-end achievements

We offer our mission delegates

  • · Enriching missions: Work on varied and stimulating projects within renowned companies.
  • · Personalized support: Get regular follow-up and advice to optimize your mission experience.
  • · Professional development opportunities: Access continuing training and develop your skills.
  • · Attractive pay: Benefit from competitive pay and various benefits.
  • · A working environment: Stimulating and collaborative
  • · A flexible working environment: Adapt your work schedules and workplaces according to mission needs and your preferences.
  • · Wide professional network: Expand your network by working with professionals from various sectors.
  • · Active collaboration: with a dynamic team with technical know-how and skilled business skills
  • · Absolute discretion: guarantee, immediate entry date or to be agreed

You will integrate a recognized company in the region, enjoying an excellent reputation with its customers. You will evolve in a modern workshop equipped with powerful tools, within a human-sized structure where everyone contributes directly to the success of the projects. Management focuses on proximity, trust and autonomy, while promoting skills sharing and quality work.

Do you want to put your skills at the service of a dynamic company and participate in quality projects? Don't wait and seize this opportunity!
