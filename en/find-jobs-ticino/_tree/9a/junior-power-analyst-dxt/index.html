Junior Accountant — DXT Commodities S.A.

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

As part of our continued growth, a new opening has arisen in our Accounting team at our headquarters in Lugano. This position is open to junior professionals with 1 – 3 years of experience in accounting, audit, or similar roles.The nature of the role will require you to work both as part of a closely knit team and individually. We offer a full-time permanent position with a competitive salary. You will also be eligible for our benefits and wellness programs. Full on-the-job training will be given by your mentor and colleagues in a fast-moving professional environment. Individual growth and development support is provided by the Duferco Academy.

General tasks include

  • Record, review and reconcile accounting transactions in compliance with company policies and IFRS accounting standards.
  • Prepare and coordinate daily payment instructions with banking counterparties, monitor cash movements, and perform monthly bank reconciliations.
  • Support quarterly closing activities.
  • Assist in the preparation of IFRS and Local GAAP statutory financial statements.
  • Liaise with external auditors and support audit processes by providing documentation, analyses, and explanations as required.
  • Respond to ad hoc requests from management, auditors, and internal stakeholders.
  • Contribute to the enhancement, standardization, and automation of accounting processes, internal controls, and reporting procedures.

Requirements

  • Master’s degree in Business Administration, Economics or Management.
  • 1 to 3 years of experience in accounting or audit.
  • High degree of accuracy, discretion and confidentiality and excellent teamwork skills.
  • English and Italian proficiency.
  • Very good knowledge of Microsoft Office.

If you feel this opportunity represents the right step for you and you are confident of bringing value to the team, please send your updated CV together with a cover letter to: hr.junior.accountant@dxt.com
