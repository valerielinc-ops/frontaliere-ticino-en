This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Tasks

  • performs the different works of preparation, maintenance and repair of vehicles, machines and equipment owned by the State
  • supports managers in internal training and state-based apprentices
  • cooperates with the various departments present within the State Office (OfS)
  • cooperates proactively in promoting the guidance and guidelines of the Office for Automobiles and Machines of the State (UAMS)

Requirements

  • Federal capacity certificate (AFC) such as mechatronics or mechanical maintenance or equivalent title in the car industry
  • experience in the management and training of apprentices
  • multiannual experience in similar workshop activities
  • spirit of initiative, prepared to work in teams and good organizational skills
  • proven professionalism and irreproachable conduct
  • flexibility and availability at high and irregular working times
  • good knowledge of Ticino's cantonal reality
  • is in possession or is willing to obtain licences to conduct categories C1 and BE within 12 months of recruitment

Special remarks

  • service office: Camorino
  • entry into service: date to be agreed

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), in a second moment may be required additional documentation (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the state of health)

Conditions of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the order of employees of the State and of teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulation. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

Deadline

8 June 2026

Further information can be obtained from Mr Manolo Gaggetta, Head of the Office for Vehicles and Machines of the State, Tel. 091 814 94 44.
