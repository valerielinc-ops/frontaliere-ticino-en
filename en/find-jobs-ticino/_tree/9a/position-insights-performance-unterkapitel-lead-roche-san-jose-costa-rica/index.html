Position Summary: As a Subchapter Lead in the Global Digital Hub within Roche Services & Solutions, you ensure delivery of the Hub’s long-term outcomes, strategy, and priorities; inspire subchapter members to collaborate across the network and contribute to Roche’s Ten-Year Ambitions; drive embedding of the new operating model and role model agile collaboration; and help shape the Hub’s future by developing cutting-edge digital capabilities and supporting colleague development across Social Media Management, Channels & Publishing, Creative Design, Digital Campaign Management, Marketing Automation, and Digital Operations.

The Opportunity

  • Owning a complete business service end-to-end, including managing both the digital work and the people who deliver it, acting as the primary point of contact and leader to global partners and stakeholders.
  • Leading, mentoring, and coaching team members by setting goals, managing performance, onboarding, and growing careers to maintain high effectiveness across the subchapter.
  • Leveraging domain depth in digital operations to set technical and quality standards for the service, establish process governance principles, and coach team members in their craft.
  • Attracting, developing, and retaining top talent aligned with evolving business needs, advocating for diversity and fostering an inclusive team culture reflecting the world around us.
  • Building collaborative partner relationships across Subchapters, Chapters, and RSS organizations to align strategic priorities, accelerate decision-making, and embed the One Roche mindset.
  • Driving continuous service improvement by monitoring resource utilization and customer satisfaction, making recommendations to enhance responsiveness, and ensuring compliance with local or global labor regulations and policies.

Who you are

  • You have a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree with 7+ years of experience working in digital operations for large organizations, agencies, or startups, alongside 3+ years of experience in people leadership, coaching, and talent development.
  • You possess strong domain credibility and business leadership skills, capable of setting quality bars, driving strategic planning, and owning end-to-end partner relationships.
  • You are skilled in people leadership, adept at handling conflict constructively, giving and seeking open feedback, and modeling a growth mindset.
  • You have proven experience operating and influencing effectively in matrixed, cross-functional global environments using agile ways of working.
  • You have strong communication skills in English (written and spoken) with the ability to engage confidently with senior leaders and cross-functional partners.
  • You are a proactive, innovative problem solver with strong project and change management skills, end-to-end accountability, and a focus on sustainable, scalable solutions.
