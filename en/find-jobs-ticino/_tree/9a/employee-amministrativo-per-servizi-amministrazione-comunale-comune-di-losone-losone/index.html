Full description

MUNICIPALITY OF LOSONE COMPETITION FOR THE RECRUITMENT OF ADMINISTRATIVE EMPLOYEE The Municipality of Losone opens the competition for the full-time hiring of an administrative employee for the services of the municipal administration under the conditions established by the Organic Regulations for municipal employees (ROD). An internal appointment is envisaged. Purpose of the function The execution of all administrative work relating to the new General Help Desk, essentially including Chancellery tasks, Resident Control, initial general reception of users for all services (Secretariat, Finance and Social Help Desk, Municipal Police). The detailed list of duties (specifications) and further information can be obtained from the Municipal Registry (tel. 091/785.76.00) or downloaded from the website www.losone.ch. Minimum requirements required ▪ Integrity moral conduct and psychophysical state compatible with the function ▪ AFC commercial employee or equivalent training Other preferential qualifications ▪ Professional training and experience in the public sector ▪ Mother tongue Italian, German spoken and written correctly ▪ Good knowledge of the GECOTI relational database and predisposition in the field of IT. Personal characteristics ▪ Spirit of initiative and innovation, problem-solving ability ▪ Interpersonal communication skills and aptitude for mediation in relationships with authorities, citizens and users ▪ Availability and flexibility to work out of hours ▪ Dynamic and proactive attitude ▪ Organizational and coordination skills ▪ Sense of hospitality ▪ Aptitude for working in a team and in contact with users ▪ Ability to independently draft texts such as correspondence, reports to the Municipality, etc. ▪ Availability and interest in training and professional development ▪ Discretion, confidentiality and impartiality complete the ideal profile. Required documents ▪ Handwritten letter indicating the deadline for availability ▪ Curriculum vitae ▪ Study certificates (with notes) and work certificates ▪ Individual certificate of civil status or family deed ▪ Certificate of good conduct ▪ Extract from the criminal record ▪ Health questionnaire ▪ Passport-sized photograph

  • dispensation for those domiciled in the Municipality.

Municipal employees are exempt from submitting the required documents. The individual certificate of civil status or family certificate, good conduct and the extract from the criminal record must be issued less than 6 months ago. Salary The annual salary, including the 13th month, is that established by the salary scale for municipal employees: class 4, min. Fr. 59'404, max. Fr. 92'315.--. In addition to the salary, child allowances are paid to those entitled to it. Entry into service Date to be agreed. Deadline of the competition Applications for employment, accompanied by the documents mentioned above, must reach the Municipality of Losone, in a sealed envelope and with the external wording "Administrative employee competition", by 4.00 pm on Thursday 12 November 2026. For anything not covered in this competition notice, the Organic Regulations of the employees of the Municipality of Losone apply. The hiring will be decided at the sole discretion of the Municipality, which reserves the right to cancel the competition if the applications do not meet the needs. Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
