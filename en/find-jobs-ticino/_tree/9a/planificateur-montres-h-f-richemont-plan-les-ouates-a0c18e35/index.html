Reference code: JR130898 PLANNING AMOUNTS (H/F) Plan-les-Ouates, GE, CH Fixed-term contract 6 to 8 months FOR THOSE AND THOSE WHO SEARCH FOR EXCELLENCE Join one of the iconic houses of high watchmaking, where excellence is a constant requirement. Take part in our collective success and the creation of our future heritage. YOUR REASON TO BE IN THE HOUSE Are you attracted to logistics, and benefit from several years' experience in this field? You have a great team spirit, you know how to evolve in a multinational environment and work with a wide variety of stakeholders? You are known for your agility, flexibility and like to work cross-sectionally? If so, we would like to offer you the opportunity to join our team based in Plan-les-Ouates as a Watch Planner. YOUR ROLE TO PLAY You will be in charge of Manage short-term planning on the watches perimeter: Refine the planning guaranteeing the smoothing of load assembly shows and establish the load/capacity plan. Validate the feasibility of the production plan in collaboration with other departments (Production, Quality, Supply Chain and Supply Chain). Identify and track the risks of component supply disruptions (internal production and procurement) and then adjust the planning according to hazards. To guarantee the monitoring of production. Pilot the monitoring of new production plans in collaboration with Product Development and Production. Participate in improving planning processes and working standards. QUALITIES FOR EXCELLING Success in this role depends on: Your engineering training (or title deemed equivalent) in industrial or logistics production management. Your confirmed 3 year minimum planning experience Your great ease with SAP and office automation tools, including Excel Your organization, rigor and proactivity, with a good team spirit and customer/service orientation. Your very good analytical and synthesis skills, your great flexibility and your positive attitude. True team player, you have excellent communication skills and don't hesitate to challenge the status quo. Vacheron Constantin celebrates 270 years as the oldest watchmaking factory in the world, in continuous operation since 1755. Based in Geneva, Switzerland, our House operates a worldwide network of nearly 200 outlets in 36 countries. Every member of our exceptional community – watchmakers, artisans and professionals – shares the same passion for our heritage and ambition to build an even brighter future. Ready to be part of our story? We will be delighted to discover your application.
