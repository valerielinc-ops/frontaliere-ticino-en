Without SBB, the country stands still. And without you the SBB. You make a valuable contribution to the supply of Switzerland. You will complete an apprenticeship as a signalling employee:r with final exam. Practical and theoretical training takes approx. 2 months with full wage payment You can move that.

  • As a signal box employee: r you serve the signal box in the former Olten marshalling station. You change switches, create shunting lanes/ways, serve relevant signals and grant approvals for shunting trips.
  • You monitor, schedule and drive the shunting traffic.
  • The shunting trips are coordinated with the partners involved.
  • You guarantee operational security in compliance with security regulations and, if necessary, using checklists.
  • In the 24-hour operation of SBB, you are in shift operation. Irregular working hours and assignments on weekends are the rule for us. For this, you enjoy free days during the week and can thus enjoy your leisure activities flexibly and outside the big hustle and bustle.

You bring that with you.

  • As a switchgear employee:r you bring several years of operational experience and are not afraid to operate simple PC systems.
  • Security-conscious thinking and acting, teamwork, independence and reliability are your strengths.
  • Communication with various partners brings them pleasure.
  • You have a good knowledge of the German language (minor level B2).
  • You like working shifts 24/7.
