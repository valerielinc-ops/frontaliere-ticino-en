We at pfister pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today - will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Ensure courteous customer support during the delivery of goods
  • Ensure smooth flow of our goods inlet and outlet at the branch
  • Care of our warehouse
  • If necessary, help in other departments (e.g. our decorating team in shop construction and conversions in the exhibition)
  • Happy, accurate, helpful, reliable and forward-thinking personality
  • Craftsmanship and a good sense of activities
  • Good physical performance and great flexibility
  • Experience in storage and affinity for furnishings
  • Experience with goods entry and exit bookings in SAP
  • Enjoy assembly of furniture
