Full description

It is with great passion and love of detail that our employees make GLOBUS the first address of the Swiss Luxury and Premium. They are people like you who make the difference and who, with us, give meaning to "the place we love to be".

IT'S WHAT YOU DO DIFFERENCE

  • Coaching, motivation and team management in order to maintain and develop our high-end customers
  • To be the guarantor of a unique customer experience by ensuring an inspiring atmosphere, a high quality implementation and an irreproachable reception
  • Welcome, advice and sales to customers by applying our principles of excellence and know-how
  • Acting as a trainer for new employees and apprentices
  • Implementation of the goods in close cooperation with our decoration department and its visual merchandising concepts
  • Setting up events and animations on the sales surface
  • Acting as the sales manager's support for organisational and coordination tasks
  • Support and replacement of the sales manager during absences
  • Management of administrative processes in support of the Head of Sales

WHAT DISTINCE YOU

  • You have a CFC Retail Manager, with at least 5 years of experience in perfumery
  • Knowledge of accessories, leather and textiles would be an advantage.
  • You justify a successful experience in leading a team, with minimum 3 years of experience in this field
  • Your personal and social skills allow you to assist the person in the conduct of the staff
  • Your ease in stimulating others and your team spirit have already proved their worth
  • You are oriented to solutions and taking a step back and the sense of the organization allows you to effectively manage the radius
  • With an impeccable presentation, you are committed to offering high quality service to our customers
  • At ease with IT tools, you master Office tools
  • The mastery of German or English would be an additional asset.

BENEFITS FOR YOU With more than 1,700 other retail enthusiasts, you are an integral part of our fascinating world of high-end and luxury. You are recognized and valued, you work with an exciting and motivated team and we give you our trust so that you can enjoy free time and make a difference with your know-how. If you are an expert in your profession and you put your heart into it, many doors will open to develop your career. We will gladly support you in your internal and external trainings.

You can also enjoy the following benefits

  • an exceptional working environment with a horizontal hierarchy in the city center.
  • performance-related premiums and attractive social benefits .
  • flexible work models and at least 5 weeks of vacation (PS: You can benefit from more free time thanks to the option "unpaid leave" - from 7 to 180 days).
  • up to 50% discount on Globus universe products for you and your family.
  • and much more...

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
