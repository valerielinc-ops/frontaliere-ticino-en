Full description

VZ is the leading independent financial services provider in Switzerland. With our Mortgage Center, we are setting new standards in the financing of real estate thanks to our many years of real estate expertise. As we are growing strongly, we are looking for reinforcements at our headquarters in a central location in Zurich.

  • You create self-employed loan applications and resubmissions for our mortgage customers from all over Switzerland.
  • You take care of the renewals of expired mortgage artranes.
  • You will complete restructuring of mortgages including property valuation (e.g. increase in mortgage, mortgage mutations and layoffs, purchase/sale of property, gift, divorce, death)
  • You advise our customers by phone and email about our services.
  • As a specialist, you are the contact point for the customer advisors of the VZ VermögensZentrum for questions about real estate and mortgages and are in close contact with the specialist departments within the HypothekenZentrum.
  • Administrative work rounds off this exciting job profile.
  • Commercial training at a Swiss bank
  • Several years of experience in the Swiss mortgage business for private customers
  • In-depth expertise in the Swiss mortgage business, which you also like to share in the team.
  • Great pleasure in telephone customer contact, where you put the customer concern in the center.
  • Impeccable communication in German in word and writing, very good Swiss German skills are an advantage.
  • Motivation to work in a young, dynamic and service-oriented team.
  • Careful introduction to your work.
  • A collegial working environment with flat hierarchies.
  • Individual support through internal training and support for external training.
  • Independent work and the opportunity to contribute and implement your own ideas.
  • Surveys regularly show that employees classify the VZ as an above-average positive environment in which they can fully develop. Become part of it!
