Full description

For our school Nordstrasse we are looking from 01. August 2027 a committed and resilient personality.

  • Together with your job partner, you lead the school Nordstrasse in personnel, pedagogical and operational matters. You will use your strengths in particular in operational management and financial management.
  • They ensure efficient operational processes, clear responsibilities and a forward-looking organisation of everyday school life.
  • They take over the financial responsibility of the school, plan and control the budget, keep an eye on the expenses and use the available resources responsibly and according to needs.
  • Together with your job partner, you lead and develop the team of teachers, school management assistants and other specialists in support and home service. They promote employees in their professional development.
  • They contribute to the continuous development of the school and contribute to the safeguarding and further development of school quality.
  • Together, you represent the school to the outside and maintain cooperation with parents, institutions and specialist bodies.
  • It is possible to supplement the school board with lessons.
  • Educational training or an equivalent university degree, ideally supplemented by completed school management training or the willingness to complete it.
  • An open-minded pedagogical attitude with teaching qualification for the elementary school in Switzerland.
  • Strong communication skills, high social skills and enjoyment of working together.
  • Strong operational strength, organisational talent and experience in the efficient design of operational processes combined with good IT knowledge.
  • Experience in financial and resource management, a good understanding of numbers and the ability to plan and manage budgets.
  • Design will, enthusiasm and assertiveness as well as resilience, sovereignty and flexibility.
  • General interest in school and social issues.

We offer you a responsible and versatile leadership role in a motivated and collegial school team. It expects an open, open and helpful environment that sees diversity as an enrichment and cultivates an appreciative, inclusive community. We work together in a spirit of trust, taking responsibility for each other and promoting a culture of shared learning and development. They benefit from the support of a professional school secretariat, a modern infrastructure in an attractive school facility and employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines. You also have the opportunity to actively participate in school development and use your ideas and leadership skills in a mindful, healthy working environment.

The school district Waidberg comprises 17 school units with over 6200 students and around 1,700 employees in the districts Wipkingen, Höngg, Unterstrass and Oberstrass. The Waidberg stands for a contemporary pedagogy and current guidelines on "democracy and participation" for the overall school development.

The day school Nordstrasse is located in Wipkingen and currently comprises three kindergarten and 4 multi-year classes at both lower and intermediate levels. Around 230 children visit our school, which can also visit our morning table as well as the lunch and evening hoard.

We see diversity as enrichment, community as strength and learning as a common path. We create space for development, participation and trust - for children like Erwach-sene. Open cooperation, mutual appreciation and careful interaction shape our culture and make our school a lively learning place.
