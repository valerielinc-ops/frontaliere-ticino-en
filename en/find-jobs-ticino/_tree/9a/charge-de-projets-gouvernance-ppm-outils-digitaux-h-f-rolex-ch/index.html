Full description

Introduction

For our Industrialization & Production Support sector, we are looking for a PPM Governance & Digital Tools Project Manager (M/F) whose main mission will be to structure, deploy and drive the governance of the Industrialization & Production portfolio of projects, in order to make the steering of the roadmaps reliable, harmonize practices and provide management with a consolidated vision that is useful for decision-making.

Responsibilities

  • Structure and evolve the I&P portfolio in the PPM tool: data, status, milestones, flows and management rules
  • Ensure the quality and consistency of project data, in conjunction with project managers and activity managers
  • Preparing and facilitating PPM governance bodies: consolidation of information, monitoring of actions, risks, decisions and arbitrations
  • Design and evolve portfolio reporting for management
  • Support the use and harmonization of associated monitoring and collaboration tools: PPM, Monday, SharePoint or equivalent
  • Collaborate with internal and intersite stakeholders to align governance processes, standards, reporting and practices
  • Contributing on an ad hoc basis to transversal digitalisation, process improvement or data recovery projects

Profile

  • Advanced training in project management, industrial engineering, information systems, organization, data/BI or equivalent
  • Experience confirmed in project coordination, PMO, portfolio governance or digital transformation, ideally in industrial environment
  • Good control of project management methods: framing, planning, progress monitoring, risk management, preparation of forums and reporting
  • The experience of a PPM tool, Monday, SharePoint, Excel and/or Power BI/Appreciated Table
  • Excellent analytical, synthesis and communication capabilities; ability to bring together diverse interlocutors, structure complex subjects and advance actions without direct hierarchical authority

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
