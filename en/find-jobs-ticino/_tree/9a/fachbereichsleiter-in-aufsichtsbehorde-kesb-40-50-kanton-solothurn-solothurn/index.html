Full description

The Office for Society and Social Affairs supports the people in the canton of Solothurn in various situations. The Social Services department includes the faculties Family Supplementary Benefits, Asylum, Social Assistance and Supervisory Authority KESB. For the Social Services department, we are looking for a department manager KESB, 40-50% at the Solothurn location as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement.

Your responsibility

  • They supervise the three child and adult protection authorities (KESB) in the canton of Solothurn through supervisory visits, controlling and reporting and lead harmonization and further development projects in cooperation with the KESB.
  • The controlling and reporting of the supervisory authority is ensured by you.
  • They lead strategic-political and operational working groups with systemic partners such as representatives of the social regions and the Association of Resident Communities.
  • You lead your department professionally and personnel.
  • They work intensively with the legal service and the social assistance department.

Your profile

  • They have several years of in-depth experience in child and adult protection.
  • Thanks to your suitable university degree, e.g. in a legal or social science discipline, you have an analytical-abstract thinking ability, a holistic understanding of problem-solving as well as at best knowledge of political processes and an affinity for municipal structures and concerns.
  • You preferably have leadership experience as well as expertise in social assistance.
  • You are a team-capable, communicative and performant person with an independent and structured way of working. They keep an overview of numerous, parallel tasks and enjoy working on complex topics.
  • For this, you bring flexibility and commitment as well as perseverance and innovative spirit: you want to design instead of simply managing.
  • Promoting personal work-life balance through an annual working time model
  • Part-time work, also in cadre
  • Opportunity to work mobile
  • Progressive employment conditions based on a GAV
  • Equal pay between men and women
  • Loyalty rewards
  • 16 weeks of maternity leave on full pay
  • 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Financial contributions to childcare
  • Comprehensive offer of internal and external training
