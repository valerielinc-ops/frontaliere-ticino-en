Put to the Point

The Swiss Accreditation Body (SAS) is the national accreditation authority of Switzerland. From product safety tests to energy management systems to material analyses in the laboratory - when it is checked whether products, systems or services comply with certain standards and legal requirements, SAS also takes a close look. In this way, we create trust in the services of others and help to make the everyday life of many people safer and to reduce technical barriers to trade. The Knowledge and Information Management department, consisting of six established employees, ensures contemporary knowledge and information management within SAS with the aim that SAS can perform its core tasks in the best possible way.

The State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO) is the federal competence centre for all core questions of economic and labour market policy and belongs to the Federal Department for Economic Affairs, Education and Research (WBF). It promotes international market opening in goods, services and investment. To reduce poverty, SECO is committed to economic development cooperation. It plans the economic and trade policy measures for developing countries, the Eastern European and Central Asian countries and the new member states of the European Union (EU).
