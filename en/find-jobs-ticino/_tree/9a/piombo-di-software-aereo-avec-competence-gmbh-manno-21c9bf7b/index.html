Full description

This role leads and grows an airborne software engineering team, overseeing software development and verification activities for certified aerospace systems.

Tasks

  • Establish and grow the airborne software team, fostering a collaborative engineering culture
  • Act as the primary software focal point, aligning with the software organization at headquarters
  • Plan, coordinate, and lead software development and verification activities across the software lifecycle
  • Define tasks and mentor software engineers
  • Ensure compliance with aviation certification standards, processes, quality requirements, budgets and schedules
  • Collaborate with systems engineering, program management, and external partners and suppliers
  • Build relationships with certification authorities, vendors and cross-functional teams

Profile

  • Degree in engineering (Bachelor's level or higher)
  • Extensive experience in software development within a regulated environment
  • Demonstrated success leading multicultural and multidisciplinary engineering teams
  • Strong expertise in C programming, software architecture, software testing and model-based development (SCADE)
  • Experience applying DO-178C and related standards in certified aerospace projects
  • Understanding of modern software development processes and tools, including Git, Jira and DOORS
  • Fluent English (C1 or higher); Italian language knowledge highly desired

Offer

  • 3–6 months interim, then a permanent position
  • Flexible working hours and individual pension solutions
  • Support for training and professional development

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
