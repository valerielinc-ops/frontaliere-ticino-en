Full description

The EMS Nelty de Beausobre, opened in 1989, bears the name of the last descendant of the Beausobre family. It offers 32 single rooms as well as 9 double rooms. Close to the train station and the pedestrian area of ​​the city center, the EMS is ideally located and allows you to take advantage of the nearby shops and cafes or go to the shores of Lake Geneva.

We are looking for two EMS Nelty de Beausobre

Healthcare auxiliaries (M/F) at 70-80%

In order to strengthen our Geriatrics services, position to be agreed.

WE OFFER YOU

  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of buying back an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 free public holidays.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time work, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • A mobility plan including participation in your bike ride, basketball ride or your public transport subscription, with a concern for the environment following a sustainability plan at institutional level.
  • Remuneration based on the value of your years of previous professional experience, as well as bonuses linked to seniority within the EHC.
  • Free pilates classes, financial participation in sporting events such as the Lausanne 20km or Bike to Work, a catalog of benefits offering discounts in the areas of sport, well-being, leisure, mobility, insurance and finance.
  • Free hot drinks in the morning and meals for CHF 8.50 in our cafeterias.
  • A “Pâquis Plage” company refreshment bar to share convivial moments on sunny days.
  • Possibilities for internal transfers, according to your development desires.
  • Numerous internal or certified training courses available.
  • A program dedicated to executives to support them in acquiring the skills necessary to manage their teams.

YOU OWN

  • A healthcare assistant or nursing assistant diploma recognized by the Swiss Red Cross.
  • The Gineste-Marescotti (GM) and Advanced Age Psychiatry (FAP) certificate, an asset.
  • Successful experience in geriatrics or psychiatry of advanced age, an asset.
  • Knowledge of the health system and EMS in Switzerland, an asset.
  • Ability to work day and night, continuous and split hours.
  • Gentleness, empathy and excellent relationships with elderly people.
  • A team spirit and solidarity allowing the maintenance of good interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Dynamism, commitment and a good ability to adapt.

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Support residents in activities of daily living.
  • Ensure the physical care and comfort of residents.
  • Participate in the adaptation and maintenance of their environment with their collaboration.
  • Participate in an interdisciplinary care process for our residents.

WE ARE

  • An institution comprising acute care and rehabilitation establishments, a private clinic, EMS, medical centers and hotlines spread across the La Côte region as well as the 1st school of clinical hypnosis dedicated to the healthcare sector in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A healthcare network allowing numerous opportunities for internal transfer according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees caring for patients and residents of the region, sharing values ​​of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment favoring the completion of projects.
  • A training company committed to training the next generation, with around 80 apprentices in 16 different professions, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
