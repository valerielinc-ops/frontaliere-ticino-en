The Position Role Overview We are seeking a motivated and organized Working Student to join our Marketing department. In this part-time role, you will support our team across various marketing initiatives, from administrative tasks to digital media campaigns and creative coordination. This is an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a dynamic diagnostics environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Administrative Support:
  • Purchase Order (PO) creation and new vendor setup.
  • General administrative support.
  • Marketing Coordination:
  • Coordinating product-related updates and communications across the Product Management team.
  • Coordinating Marketing Team Meetings and corresponding tasks.
  • Digital & Social Media:
  • Creating content for social media channels and influencer campaigns.
  • Facilitating communication with agencies and external partners.
  • Marketing Materials Creation:
  • Executing simple graphic design tasks.
  • Performing translation checks.

Requirements

  • Currently enrolled in a university degree program (e.g., Marketing, Business Administration, Communications, or a related field).
  • Interested in healthcare and diagnostics.
  • Fluent in English.
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Ability to balance administrative tasks with creative projects.
  • Excellent communication skills and a proactive approach to problem-solving.
