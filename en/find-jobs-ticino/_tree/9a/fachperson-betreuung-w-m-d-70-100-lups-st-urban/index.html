Full description

Over 90 employees in the areas of care and social pedagogy accompany and support people with mental and multiple disabilities. The Sonnegarte dormitory in St. Urban is an organizational unit of Lucerne Psychiatry AG. Professional support (m/f/d) 70-100 %

Their tasks

  • Accompaniment and care of adults with cognitive or multiple impairments, in accordance with their personal needs and resources
  • Participation in the planning and evaluation of the daily routine on the residential group and support in the implementation
  • Participation in everyday and leisure activities
  • Assumption of ministry and reference work

Your profile

  • Completed FaBe or similar social or health education
  • Experience or interest in accompanying and caring for people with disabilities and challenging behaviour
  • Solution-oriented working style
  • Engaged, resilient and humorous personality
