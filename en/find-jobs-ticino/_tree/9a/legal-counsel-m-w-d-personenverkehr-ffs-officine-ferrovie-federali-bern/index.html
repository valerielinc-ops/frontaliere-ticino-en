Move Switzerland with us. Legal Counsel (m/f/d) Passenger Traffic Bern / Work Smart, 01.03.2026 or by agreement, 70% Apply now

You can look forward to that.

We bring more than 1.4 million travelers to our destination every day and stand for punctuality, reliability and quality. When it comes to law and compliance passenger transport, you support a dedicated team and work in shops and projects with high visibility. You work across all areas with the market and production of passenger transport, assume responsibility from legal assessment to pragmatic implementation and thus contribute to sustainable solutions for customers and companies. You can move that.

  • Business units in the market and production passenger transport divisions advise and supervise you in legal and compliance topics, in particular in contract law, procurement law, company law and general public law.
  • The advice and support of project teams and executives in legal issues is taken over from project start to implementation.
  • You identify legal opportunities and risks at an early stage, show scope for action and safeguard the interests of SBB and its Group companies.
  • You provide appropriate legal bases and contribute to the legal education and further education of employees.

You bring that with you.

  • Thanks to your analytical and networked thinking, you can also keep track of complex questions.
  • With your flexible and empathetic nature, you win different stakeholders for common goals.
  • As a personality, you are resilient, assertive, solution-oriented and appreciate working in a team.
  • On your professional path, you have gained practical experience in civil law as well as in public law, ideally in procurement law.
  • We presuppose a university degree in law at a Swiss university and the attorney’s patent.
  • You are a native German language and have very good knowledge of French and English.

Your reward and benefits.

We are actively committed to equal pay. This employment takes place in accordance with bond law, taking into account a comparison with the team, the market and the industry.
