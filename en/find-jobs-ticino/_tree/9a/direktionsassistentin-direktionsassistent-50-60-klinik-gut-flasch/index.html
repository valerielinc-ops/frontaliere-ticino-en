Full description

Klinik Gut is an established private clinic for orthopaedics and accident surgery. We serve our national and international customers at the clinic locations in St. Moritz and Fläsch, as well as at the practice locations in Chur, Buchs and Zurich. For our location in Fläsch, we are looking for a committed and organizational personality immediately or by agreement, ideally with a working time spread over five mornings per week, as Directorate Assistant / Directorate Assistant (50-60%) In this versatile function, you are an important contact person for our CEO and the clinic management. You ensure smooth organizational processes, keep an overview and contribute to a well-coordinated management day with your forward-looking work.

Your tasks

  • Planning and coordinating the CEO’s agenda and organizing appointments and meetings
  • Central contact and coordination office between CEO, clinic management, internal departments and external contact persons
  • Preparation and follow-up of meetings, including protocol management and follow-up of pendencies
  • Processing of correspondence and support in the preparation of presentations, documents and meeting documents
  • Assuming further administrative tasks and participating in the organization of internal projects and events in cooperation with other departments

Your profile

  • Open, communicative and service-oriented personality with pleasure in cooperation
  • Strong organisational talent and the ability to maintain an overview even in a dynamic environment
  • Independent, structured and forward-looking working method with a high sense of responsibility
  • Self-initiative, flexibility and a solution-oriented way of thinking
  • Discretion, loyalty and a good feeling for different people and situations
  • Completed commercial training, ideally with experience in a comparable function
  • Very good knowledge of German and safe handling of MS Office

You can look forward to this

  • Varied and responsible work
  • A lot of personal responsibility and space for your own ideas
  • Appreciative corporate culture with short decision paths and an open du culture
  • Collegial working environment and a committed team
  • Attractive employment conditions and modern infrastructure

Interested? Want to know more about the job? Noemi Bossi, Head of HRM, is happy to provide you with information on 081 836 34 71. We look forward to your complete application documents by e-mail to n.bossi@klinik-gut.ch
