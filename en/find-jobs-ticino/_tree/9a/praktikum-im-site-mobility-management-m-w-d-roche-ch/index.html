The Position

Who we are

Roche Pharma AG in Grenzach is one of the leading pharmaceutical companies and is looking for a motivated intern (m/w/d) to support our Site Services team in the areas of fleet & mobility. In this internship we offer you the opportunity to gain valuable experience in the conversion of our service car fleet to electric mobility as well as in the development and implementation of mobility concepts for our location and our employees.

Your main tasks

  • Support for the implementation of a fleet management company (FMC), efficient coordination and documentation of regular coordination dates with suppliers to ensure a smooth flow of information - cooperation in the development of mobility concepts for the site, taking into account sustainability, efficiency and cost optimization (e.g.

Charging stations, job bike, vehicle UVV, driving licence testing, etc.)

  • Analysis of mobility data and development of solutions for improving employee mobility
  • Active participation in projects to improve the fleet, mobility & travel processes
  • Administrative support of the team as well as co-design and development of a sustainable storage structure for consistent knowledge management

Who you are

  • Advanced studies in business administration, project management, business information technology, mobility management or a related field
  • interest in electromobility and sustainable mobility
  • Analytic thinking and the ability to understand complex relationships and to develop solutions
  • Good communication skills and team spirit
  • Self-initiative, self-employment and a structured working method
  • Good knowledge of MS Office and Google applications
  • Good German and English skills in word and writing

We offer you the opportunity to work in a dynamic and innovative environment where you can bring your ideas to life and implement them. You will be supported by experienced experts and gain insights into the latest developments in the field of electric mobility and sustainable mobility solutions.

What we offer you

  • Flexible time-sharing - 2268 € monthly remuneration for a full-time internship - Reduced prices (-5%) in our canteen

Your application

Please upload only your CV online.

Remember that this internship is exclusively aimed at students (m/f/d) with ongoing enrolment throughout the entire period of the internship or alternatively at persons in a maximum one-year Gap-Year (between Bachelor and Master's degree), where it can be demonstrated in writing at which university/university and exactly when (month/year) the studies are continued within one year.

Period: from 01 July 2026 for a period of 6 months.

Please note that recommendations for all student activities (e.g. internships, theses, etc.) are excluded.
