Metalworkers 100% We bring together what belongs together! The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people – now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company? From now on or by agreement, we are looking for a temporary or permanent employment with 1 – 3 years of experience as a metalworker

Your challenge

  • Project management in metal and glass construction
  • Coordination of planning, production and assembly in cooperation with our employees and suppliers
  • Responsible for communication and coordination with building manager, architect and construction management
  • Co-design of internal projects with space for own initiative

This is what you bring with you

  • Basic training in construction and further education as project manager, metal builder, technician or construction manager
  • Independent and decision-making
  • Team-capable, quality-conscious and resilient
  • They have a sense of people, material and implementation.

What we offer you

  • Flexible working part-time or full-time
  • Annual working hours, 41 hours per week
  • Training opportunities and personal development
  • Independent division of your work
  • Very good social benefits
  • Annual company events
  • A motivated team and a pleasant working atmosphere
  • Direct connection to the public transport with connections every 1/2 hour from Burgdorf and Thun or free parking
  • 23-30 days holidays
