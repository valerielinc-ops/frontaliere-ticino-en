Full description

In pediatric radiology at UKBB, we use state-of-the-art technology to carry out all imaging tests such as sonography, fluoroscopy, X-rays, EOS, MRI and CT directly on site. Our age spectrum ranges from prenatal imaging (fetal MRI) to adolescents. We are a dedicated team of 4 senior doctors and 5 assistant doctors, who live a high level of personal flexibility and collegial cooperation. Technically, we cover the entire spectrum of pediatric radiology, with a focus on pediatric neuroradiology and musculoskeletal radiology. As a senior doctor, you take on background services after your daily work – during the week and on weekends from the comfort of your home.

Entry date: by agreement

Their tasks

  • In your role, you assume a central function in radiological care and actively shape the further development of our department
  • They independently perform radiological examinations – from premature babies to adults – including sophisticated diagnostics such as CNS and fetal MRI
  • They work interdisciplinary with various specialist departments as well as with surrounding hospitals and thus contribute to networked and high-quality patient care.
  • They are involved in the supervision and training of assistant doctors and promote their professional development
  • They do not take on classical piketting services, but participate in telephone call and provide second-level support for urgent emergencies if necessary.
  • They conduct daily reports

Your profile

  • You bring professional expertise and personal skills that make you a valuable addition to our team
  • You have a specialist degree in radiology or an equivalent education
  • You have profound experience in pediatric radiology – ideally with a focus on pediatric radiology
  • You are a committed, flexible and team-oriented personality who acts independently and actively contributes
  • They are characterized by high social and clinical competence and maintain a collaborative cooperation – both in a team and with other disciplines.
  • You are interested in university teaching, further education and training and would like to pass on and deepen your knowledge

Our offer

  • With us you will find an environment that convinces both professionally and humanly:
  • They work with state-of-the-art imaging techniques, including a 3-Tesla MRI and a 64-line CT, enabling the highest diagnostic quality
  • You benefit from a modern and excellently developed infrastructure that supports efficient and pleasant work
  • You will become part of a motivated, friendly and open-minded team that lives a professional and appreciative working atmosphere
  • You take on a varied job in which you can actively contribute your ideas and commitment
  • You have many opportunities for scientific work and can participate in research projects
  • We actively support you in your professional development – by participating in internal and external training events. In addition, we offer you the opportunity to take on a special field as part of daily diagnostics and to deepen it in a targeted manner.
  • You will receive attractive employment conditions, which you can see in more detail under the "Benefits" section
