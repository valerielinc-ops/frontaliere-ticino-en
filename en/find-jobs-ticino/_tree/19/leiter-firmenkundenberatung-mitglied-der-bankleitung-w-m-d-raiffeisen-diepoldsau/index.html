25.08.2026 Diepoldsau

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Diepoldsau-Schmitter, a successful and locally oriented bank, we are looking for an entrepreneurial personality with leadership skills for management consulting and a member of the bank management immediately or by agreement.

What awaits you?

  • As a member of the bank management, you are jointly responsible for the strategic, entrepreneurial and business management of the bank.
  • Professional, sales and personnel management as well as further development of corporate customer business
  • Support of an own corporate client portfolio
  • Need- and solution-oriented advice for corporate clients on financial topics
  • Maintaining and expanding existing customer relationships within the framework of cross-selling
  • Self-employment thanks to entrepreneurial freedom
  • Targeted acquisition of new customers in the business circle
  • Performing representative tasks and actively maintaining networks

What do you bring?

  • Higher banking or business education with corresponding further education including proven practice as well as management experience
  • Many years of professional experience in corporate banking
  • Team-capable and entrepreneurial personality with a pronounced customer orientation, negotiating skills and a secure appearance
  • Solution-oriented, independent and networked thinking
  • Will to move forward together – according to the motto: “mìttanand – föaranand”
  • Regional anchorage

Are you looking for an exciting task in which you can take on corporate responsibility and actively shape the future of Raiffeisenbank Diepoldsau-Schmitter? Do you have in-depth competencies in the corporate customer business and take on your leadership responsibility? Then you are exactly right with us. I look forward to your application.
