Public Green The Public Green of the City of Lugano Urban Space Division is responsible for the management, care and enhancement of the city's green heritage. Its activities include the maintenance of parks and gardens, the production of plants and flowers, the management of trees, forests and leisure areas. Among the most important activities are the design, design and construction of parks, gardens, leisure areas and playgrounds, as well as wild and agricultural projects. The public green also contributes in synergy with other communal services to the design, design and construction of public and tree-lined spaces. Through its activities, it promotes the quality of public spaces, the protection of natural heritage and the well-being of citizenship. 2 Mansions On the basis of work programmes, service needs, guidance and priorities established by the managers, it deals with forestry and gardening work using the necessary tools and equipment.
