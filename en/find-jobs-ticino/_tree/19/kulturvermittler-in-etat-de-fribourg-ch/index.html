Full description

Appointment authority / department / institution

Today more than ever, KUB Freiburg guarantees access to reliable and high-quality information. In the heart of the city and the canton, it offers a place of learning, discovery and remembrance.

Both online and in its new building, KUB provides extensive information resources. It also preserves the canton’s cultural heritage and organises a diverse cultural programme.

Become part of our team!

We provide a dynamic environment related to the creation of a new library in the city centre, which provides for the development of modern services. KUB is a founding member of SLSP (national network) and participates in the development of the services of the Swisscovery network.

We focus on the complementarity of competences and the diversity of perspectives, because it is the wide range of careers and profiles that makes up the strength of the state of Freiburg. We promote the reconciliation of professional and private life as well as flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link.

Their tasks

As a cultural mediator, you are part of the mediation and communication department of KUB, which is responsible in particular for organising cultural events as well as for the continuing education and leadership programme. The department also distinguishes itself as a centre of excellence dedicated to the challenges of the information society and the teaching of information competence.

  • Participate in the organisation of the semi-annual programme for cultural and scientific education of the KUB, especially in the German-speaking area
  • Contribute to the design and implementation of the exhibitions entrusted to the Department of Mediation and Communication
  • Develop and participate in the training and leadership programme
  • Write the texts produced by the department in German or translate them into German

Desired profile

  • Bachelor’s degree in cultural education or equivalent education
  • Several years of profound experience in cultural education for adults
  • Ability to fit into a small team, initiative, independence and organizational talent
  • Secure appearance at public events and skillfulness in the use of common IT applications (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, social media, if applicable InDesign or Resolve)
  • Perfect command of the German language with good knowledge of the French language (B1-B2)
