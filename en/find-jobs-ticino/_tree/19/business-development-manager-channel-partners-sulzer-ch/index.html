Full description

Sulzer is a leading engineering company with a proud heritage of innovation. Join our global team to grow your expertise and develop innovative solutions that enable a prosperous and more sustainable society. About the role Your main tasks and responsibilities:

  • Driving sales of Sulzer Submersible Pumps, Mixers & Grinders to new and existing business partners.
  • Planning, supporting and following-up of sales activities in-line with the sales strategy and defined processes.
  • Developing and maintaining a product training for existing and new Flow Business partners.
  • Monitor the performance for key business partners and take remedial action where necessary to ensure that targets are met.
  • Liaise and work closely together with the Sales Manager - Pacific and the Service teams with customers.
  • Establishing new, and maintaining existing, long-term relationships with customers.
  • Supporting marketing activities by attending trade shows, conferences and other marketing events.
  • Ensure orders and projects are launched in a timely and professional manner.
  • Develop and maintain strong relationships with customers and suppliers in order to reduce commercial risk and to maximize future business opportunities.
  • Maintain and monitor up to date knowledge of competitor products and activities.
  • Identify new markets and business opportunities, maximizing the potential of Sulzer's complete product offering in the targeted sub-segments. To succeed in this role, you will need:
  • 5+ years experience in Pump product sales
  • A detailed knowledge and experience in the selection and selling of Submersible Pumps, Mixers & Grinders.
  • Relevant tertiary/trade and/or pump sales experience highly regarded.
  • Strong communication skills.
  • High level negotiation skills. What we offer you
