Apprenticeship as restaurant specialist EFZ

Training centres

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • You will complete your training in the field of "Jung-Chef de Rang", "Jung-Chef Barista" or "Jung Sommelier"
  • You set tables and provide a beautiful ambience
  • You will advise our guests on the selection of food and drinks in the numerous restaurants
  • Receiving guests as well as serving and helping with banquets and events is also part of your daily work routine.
  • You order goods, create invoices and participate in the goods management
  • You watch the guests attentively and recognize their needs
  • You learn how to prepare simple dishes

What you bring for this job

  • You have successfully completed the compulsory schooling until the start of your apprenticeship and can communicate in English and French
  • You are in direct contact with our guests, therefore a well-groomed appearance, a situational communication and good manners are a must
  • You have a feeling for people and it is important to you to advise the guests competently and friendly
  • You like to be on your feet and moving, because you often work while standing
  • Hectic situations are not a problem for you, because you are resilient and like to organize
  • Operations in the evening or on weekends are no problem for you

Our offer to you

  • It is also possible to complete training at EBA level
  • During your apprenticeship you are allowed to work in various restaurants at HOCH Health Ostschweiz (at the Cantonal Hospital St.Gallen)
  • You work in an advanced environment and benefit from a well-trained, qualified and experienced team
  • You complete your training in a company where almost all learners successfully complete
  • For the cost of your school supplies you will receive financial support
  • You are allowed to attend internal training courses and information events

Your application

  • Entrance by 06. August 2027. You can find out more about this exciting apprenticeship on our website or by Jasmin Steingruber, telephone +41 71 494 23 54
  • Take your chance and complete your education at HOCH Health Eastern Switzerland

About us

Healthcare is our profession and our calling. As the largest employer in Eastern Switzerland, we are committed to offering our employees a versatile working environment. We promote individual development and thus make an active contribution to the common future. We focus on team spirit, interdisciplinary cooperation and innovative solutions.
