Full description

Key responsibilities

  • Co-lead, with the GDPO, the privacy governance framework within Risk Management, aligned with Group policies.
  • Advisor to a privacy by design/default approach for IT/data projects (cloud, data analytics, IA), including risk assessments.
  • Conduct and challenge DPIA/PIA and security impact assessments, with IT/Security and Data Governance.
  • Maintain ROPA (processing record) and data mapping; supervise updates via OneTrust.
  • Define and maintain information records, coordinate with Digital/Marketing.
  • Manage data contracts (DPA/processing agreements), SCCs etc.
  • Coordinate response to incidents and data baches with IT Security and Legal; conduct analyses, notification and remediation.
  • Supervise the management of Vendor Risk (due diligence questionnaires, audits, contractual measures) via OneTrust/third parties.
  • Supervise rights management (DSAR) and relationship with clients/collaborators in connection with HR/Front Office.
  • Ensure monitoring and impact: GDPR, nLPD (Switzerland), ePrivacy etc.
  • Train/sensitize teams (IT, trades, control).
  • Contribute to internal/external audits and regulatory reviews; prepare documentation and evidence of compliance.
  • Administer and optimize OneTrust (ROPA, DPIA, Vendor Risk, Data Mapping, Consent, DSAR depending on relevance).
  • Prepare Risk reports on the various incidents for the Board.

Your Profile

  • 5–10 years experience in data protection/privacy ideally private bank/ fortune management.
  • Legal training with strong operational advisory capacity within a Risk Management framework.
  • Mastery of GDPR, nLPD, ePrivacy executives; practice of international transfers (SCCs, TIAs, Schrems II).
  • Strong IT culture: cloud architectures, data pipelines, analytics, IA/ML; understanding security controls (IAM, encryption, DLP).
  • Experience with DPIA/PIA, ROPA, records/cookies, DPA/SCCs, incidents, sales risk and data governance.
  • Confirmed use of OneTrust.
  • Common professional English; clear communication, business oriented and risk reduction.

Qualifications & Certifications

  • Degree in Law (Master 2/LL.B/LL.M) or equivalent
  • Bar a plus.
  • Certifications: CIPP/E, CIPM, CIPT (highly desired).
  • Any further training in information security or risk management valued.

Interfunctional collaboration & Reporting

  • Direct reporting to the GDPR; contribution to Risk Management committees and control reviews.
  • Daily partnerships with IT/IT Security, Data Governance, HR, Compliance, Internal Audit, Front/Back Office.
  • Coordination with Legal/Contracts for DPA/SCCs; with Digital/Marketing for ePrivacy/cookies/consent.
  • Interface with CH/EU/UK/HK/SGP and local relays; animation of the private community.

Core Competencies : Adherence to the company - Compliance with regulations and internal guidelines
