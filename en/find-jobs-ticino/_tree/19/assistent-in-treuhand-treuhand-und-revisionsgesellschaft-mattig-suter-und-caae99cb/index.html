Assistant/\-in trust (60\-80%) (a)

You have a flair for numbers, enjoy working independently and would like to contribute your skills in finance and accounting? Then you are exactly right with us in Brig!

Place of work: Brig

Actively bring about change in the economy, society and state in cooperation with our customers!

We are an independent company and with more than 100 employees at six of our own headquarters in Switzerland, we are one of the most renowned central Swiss trust and audit firms.

Our employees are our strength. We attach great importance to a supportive cooperation and promote professional competence. Our corporate culture is to accompany every single person on their individual professional path.

YOUR ROLL

  • You are the first contact person for our customers at the Brig office: you receive our customers, manage our telephone exchange and process the mail.
  • You support our specialist team in escrow and ensure a smooth process in day-to-day business.
  • You carry out financial and payroll accounting as well as debtors and accounts payable for our customers.
  • You support the creation of interim and annual financial statements and actively contribute your figures.
  • You prepare tax declarations for natural and legal persons and ensure reliable processing.

Your profile

  • You have completed a commercial education and ideally already have experience in the escrow sector.
  • You communicate stylishly in German and have good knowledge of English.
  • You are characterized by an independent, structured and careful working method.
  • You are a team player and convince with your open, friendly and winning personality.
  • You are confident in using MS Office and quickly find your way around new programs.
  • You have a pronounced number flair, organizational talent and keep an overview even in hectic situations.

You should expect that.

  • The job offers you a varied activity in a lively environment in which you are challenged and promoted.
  • You work together with a team of more than 80 professionals. Regularly take place team and company events for the exchange.
  • We offer you all the advantages of a dynamic team, state-of-the-art office infrastructure, market-oriented remuneration, contemporary social benefits as well as generous support for training.
