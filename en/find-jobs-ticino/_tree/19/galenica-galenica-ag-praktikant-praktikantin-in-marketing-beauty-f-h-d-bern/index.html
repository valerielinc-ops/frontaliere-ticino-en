About us

A product portfolio with top brands Verfora is a company in the Galenica network and markets high-quality health and beauty products with well-known products and brands found in almost every Swiss household: Perskindol®, Anti-Brumm®, Merfen®, Algifor® – just to name a few of the top products. We have a strong position in the areas of pain, cough, cold and respiratory diseases. Our work in marketing and sales is primarily aimed at all pharmacies and drugstores. At Verfora, you are part of a mission: “Help people feel good, get and stay healthy.” At the same time, you help to continuously expand our product portfolio and open up new market segments.
