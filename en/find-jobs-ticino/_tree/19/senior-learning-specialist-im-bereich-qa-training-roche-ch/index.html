The Position In the QA training team within Quality Compliance, Systems & Training at Basel Drug Substance, we combine regulatory requirements with effective education and training. We design effective learning, empower people and safely implement GMP, because good training shows that employees understand their tasks and can carry out them independently and in accordance with GMP in everyday work. In this way, we are continuously developing our organization into a proactive and future-oriented learning partner. For the position as a Senior Learning Specialist, we are looking for an experienced personality who combines learning expertise, understanding of production processes and pronounced implementation power. In this role, you take responsibility, develop practical solutions and apply them together with stakeholders. They combine operational proximity to production with professional scope for design and actively shape the further development of our learning unit as an effective partner in production, without bearing disciplinary personnel responsibility.

The possibility As a Learning Partner, you play a central role in the design, operational implementation and optimization of our training strategy for the production areas.

  • Independent support of production areas as well as advice for managers and employees on learning topics.
  • Development and implementation of effective, practical training and qualification concepts for complex GMP and process requirements.
  • Further development of the Site Learning Roadmap, introduction of competence-based learning approaches and continuous review of training effectiveness.
  • Operational control of the learning processes, support of training plans and use of the corresponding IT systems.
  • Management of training-related deviations (CAPAs) and active support during audits and inspections.
  • Digitalization of learning solutions and simplification of workflows through standardization and automation.
  • Professional qualification of the team and representation of site interests in cross-site, global networks.

Who you are

They have a passion for adult education and convince through a structured, solution-oriented and communicative way of working.

  • Completed studies or comparable qualification with several years of experience in the learning/training area as well as in-depth knowledge in adult education and didactics.
  • Practical experience in the GMP-regulated environment, ideally directly in pharmaceutical production or manufacturing.
  • Strong communication and consulting skills to convey complex content in a comprehensible way and to work constructively with different stakeholders.
  • Proactive, independent and implementation-oriented way of working coupled with the joy of sharing knowledge and empowering others.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English as well as a sense of excellence in dealing with learning management systems (e.g. Cornerstone) and digital work platforms.
