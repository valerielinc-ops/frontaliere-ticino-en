Would you like to contribute to an innovative project in the field of first-aid care and evolve into an environment conducive to collaboration between health professionals?

The CMS Martigny \&R\gions is developing a new model of care in Valais, combining a Martigny medical practice with home care. This project aims to integrate the role of IPS to meet the needs of the population and to strengthen continuity of care.

An employee of the CMS Martigny Region, you share your work between the medical office and the client's home, in close cooperation with partner doctors and CMS teams.

You also collaborate over several years with a research team that accompanies the implementation and evaluation of this model of care.

The post is open to newly graduated IPS as well as to professionals who already have experience in this function.

We put the position of

Specialised Practitioner Nurse (SPI) at 8-100%

Your main activities:

As part of your field of expertise and practice, and in accordance with the arrangements for collaboration with partner physicians, you are required to:

  • Conduct an advanced clinical assessment and make diagnoses;
  • Develop, implement and monitor care and treatment plans in partnership with clients and their relatives;
  • Prescribing examinations and treatments and performing certain diagnostic and therapeutic interventions;
  • Monitor chronic and stable situations, including complex ones, in the firm and at home;
  • Coordinate care and direct clients to the professionals and resources of the network, promoting continuity of their journey;
  • Develop prevention, health promotion and therapeutic education activities for clients and their relatives;
  • Work closely with partner doctors, CMS teams and other professionals involved;
  • Participate in training, knowledge sharing and continuous improvement of practices;
  • Contribute with clinical partners and the research team to the implementation and evaluation of the care model.

Your profile:

  • You have a Master's degree in Specialised Nursery Practice (IPS) or an equivalent qualification;
  • You have a strong interest in first resort care, home care and support for people living with chronic diseases or complex health situations;
  • You have clinical assessment and reasoning skills;
  • You exercise autonomy, discernment and a sense of responsibility;
  • You appreciate interprofessional collaboration and have good communication skills with clients, their relatives and teams;
  • You want to participate in the development of the role of IPS in Valais and invest in a project combining clinical practice and research.

We offer you:

  • A varied clinical activity, shared between a medical office in Martigny and home care;
  • The possibility of contributing to the development of an innovative first-aid model in Valais;
  • Close collaboration with partner doctors and CMS teams;
  • Accompaniment to taking up office and clinical supervision adapted to your experience;
  • A multi-year collaboration with a research team to contribute to the production of knowledge on the integration of the role of IPS in first resort care;
  • An environment that promotes skill sharing and professional development.
  • Attractive social benefits and benefits;
  • Working conditions consistent with the LTC SLD.
