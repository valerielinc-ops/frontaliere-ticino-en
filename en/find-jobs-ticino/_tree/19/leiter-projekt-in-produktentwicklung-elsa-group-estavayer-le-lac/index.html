Full description

Elsa Group is one of the leading Swiss dairy industry groups and is part of the Migros Group (Migros Industry). Elsa Group is an expert in the manufacture and packaging of dairy products as well as in the production, refining and processing of cheeses and the production of plant-based products.

Mansioni

  • Co-lead with marketing the projects of product development and renovation, from the creation of the first prototypes to the validation of the industrial phase
  • Identify and evaluate new ingredients, technologies and suppliers to enrich the innovation portfolio in collaboration with Purchasing
  • Design and optimize complex formulations in the beverage, dessert, fermented products and vegetable alternatives categories
  • Provide scientific expertise on the functionality of proteins (solubility, viscosity, gelling, blooming, emulsification, stability and interactions with other ingredients)
  • Conduct, organize and analyse laboratory, pilot and industrial tests
  • Ensuring the achievement of quality, cost, technical performance and timeliness objectives

Required

  • Minimum 5 years experience: in the development of food products
  • Graduate School: Food Science, Food Technology, Biotechnology or Process Engineering
  • Confirmed experience in dairy products and/or plant alternatives
  • Experience in managing transversal projects in an industrial environment
  • Strong knowledge of functional ingredients and particularly dairy proteins (caseins, serum proteins, concentrates and protein isolates)
  • Understanding industrial processes such as pasteurization, UHT, homogenization, fermentation and thermal treatments
  • Knowledge of SAP tools in particular EH&S and PLM
  • Industrial curiosity and technology application
  • Cross-cutting leadership and ability to unite multidisciplinary teams
  • Reactivity, autonomy and direction results
  • French (Good Mastery)
  • English (Good knowledge)
  • German (preferably)
