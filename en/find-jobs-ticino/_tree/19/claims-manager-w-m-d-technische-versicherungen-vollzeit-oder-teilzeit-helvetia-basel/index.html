Full description

Damage / Services Pensum: 50% - 100% Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Understand technology. Resolve damages professionally. Give customers security. As Claims Manager for Technical Insurance Specialty Lines, you combine technical know-how with insurance expertise and customer orientation. You take responsibility for demanding claims of our industrial customers and design convincing solutions together with internal and external experts.

What you do

  • Do you want to combine technical expertise with insurance know-how and support demanding claims cases on your own responsibility? In this role, you are the central contact person for our industrial customers and partners and ensure that claims are handled professionally, solution-oriented and customer-oriented.
  • You are responsible for processing and managing claims in the area of technical insurance for our industrial customers, for example in the area of recycling or key accounts.
  • You analyze claims reports and technical documents, assess the insurance coverage and determine the amount of damage or damages. compensation.
  • You accompany our customers and partners professionally through the entire claims process and are in direct contact with insured persons, brokers, repair companies and other parties involved.
  • With your technical understanding and your view for the whole, you contribute to finding pragmatic, fair and sustainable solutions for our customers.

What you bring

  • We are not looking for the perfect CV, but people who combine technical understanding, analytical thinking and the joy of working with internal and external partners.
  • You primarily have technical training and commercial understanding or you have an underwriting background with a strong interest in technical topics.
  • You are very welcome if you come from a technical environment and want to take the next step towards claims or already have experience in insurance damage management and want to live out your interest in technical matters.
  • You work analytically, structured and solution-oriented and feel comfortable taking responsibility for more complex issues.
  • You have a good feeling for people and different interests and manage to communicate clearly, constructively and at eye level, even in demanding situations.
  • You build trusting relationships with internal and external partners and combine customer orientation with a sound professional assessment.
  • In German you communicate stylishly and convincingly, both in writing and orally. Good English skills are necessary, French and/or Italian are a plus.
