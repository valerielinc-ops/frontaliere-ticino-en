Introduction of society

OMEGA is a prestigious watch brand worldwide known for its quality, precision and innovation requirements for over 170 years. Our watches are among the most beautiful in the world and are characterized by their state-of-the-art technology and timeless style.

Over the course of our long history, we have broken records of precision, redefined sporting timing and maintained our position at the cutting edge of Haute Horlogerie.

As an international brand, we are always interested in meeting future colleagues and apprentices wishing to contribute to the work of our teams on almost every continent with their talents, ambitions and inspirations.
