Full description

We are looking for our multicultural school immediately or by agreement: Intern, agent Sales & Administration

Tasks

  • Advice & Sales for Beauty Services, Products and Courses
  • Care of the running customers, telephone, email, cashier incl. End of day
  • Collection and maintenance of the customer base
  • Implementation of new customer acquisition measures
  • In-house or place orders with the supplier
  • Simple secretarial tasks
  • Inventory support

Requirements

  • Sales or equivalent training, also possible as a lateral entrant
  • Good written and oral language skills in German and advantageous in other languages (especially in English, French, Spanish and Portuguese) available
  • Basic knowledge of Windows and Mircosoft Office programs (Email, Word, Excel, ...)
  • Communicative personality with a strong sales flair
  • Organizational talent who likes to work independently
  • Accurate and reliable operation

We offer

  • Traineeship of 12 months
  • Gaining practical professional experience for a successful entry into professional life
  • Established team with a pleasant working atmosphere and application of foreign language skills

If suitable, it is possible to get a permanent job after completion of the internship. The job is also suitable for people who want to work part-time. Do you live in Switzerland or the EU and have we aroused your interest? We look forward to your full application. Swiss Education Company, Dübendorfstrasse 2, 8051 Zurich, email: hr@sbacademy.ch
