Full description

Fresh and high-quality chocolate – that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach represents. Founded in 1962, this family-owned business now employs over 2500 people from more than 80 countries, with a growing trend. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glaris. It's in this picturesque Alpine setting that we produce our fresh chocolate and continuously develop, in agile teams, new creative ideas. The "Retail" division comprises over 230 chocolate shops in more than 25 countries. We offer our chocolate specialties and invite our customers to rediscover the pleasure of fresh chocolate again and again. You have the opportunity to complete a several-year EFZ training in retail trade, in one of our chocolate shops in Switzerland. There, you will learn to advise customers professionally, to decorate shop windows and shelves, and to organize various work processes. We will teach you the hygiene rules, food legislation, and the order system. Want to discover the professional world and learn new things? Then join us.
