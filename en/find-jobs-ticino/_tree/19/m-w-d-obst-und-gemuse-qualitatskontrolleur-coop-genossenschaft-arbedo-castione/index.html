(m/f/d) fruit and vegetable quality controller

Coop Genossenschaft — Arbedo-Castione, TI, Switzerland

  • Entry into service: 01.02.2027 or to agree
  • Specialization: Logistics / Transport
  • Place of work: Coop, Via Industria 116, 6532 Arbedo-Castione
  • Your workplace: Castion
  • Working quantity: 100%

Shipments

  • You will be responsible for quality control of fresh fruit and vegetables (F&V) by carrying out checks on the basis of legal requirements and company directives
  • Register your ratings in the freight management system, take care of the registration of the goods and the management of the ramps, transmitting your findings to the managers
  • Perform the daily control of the goods in stock, inform the purchasing office on the evolution of the items present in stock and ensure communication with the logistics stakeholders and with the suppliers
  • Check the development of the goods, documenting the results of the samples taken and regularly informing the purchasers
  • You are the reference person for all quality issues related to F&V

Requirements

  • You have proven knowledge of fruit and vegetable products and have already gained experience in the food sector
  • You have developed professional knowledge in the logistics sector, as part of F&V quality control or food production
  • Know the goods management systems and have good IT skills
