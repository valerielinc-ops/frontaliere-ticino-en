Full description

MediPed is a child health centre offering general paediatric consultations and longitudinal follow-up including multidisciplinary and specialized collaborations. MediPed provides an effective and complementary solution for paediatric services and offers cross-cutting care including a comprehensive approach. To accompany the opening of the MediPed Centre in Porrentruy from April 2027 we are looking for a:

  • Position Responsibilities
  • · Conduct paediatric consultations and monitor the health of infants, children and adolescents, ensuring their growth and development.
  • · Establish the diagnosis and ensure the management of common and complex pathologies in paediatrics, taking a professional and innovative approach.
  • · Define prevention and vaccination actions according to current recommendations and participate in public health awareness campaigns.

· Collaborate at the MediPed centre with other specialists (allergicology, pneumonology, endocrinology, obesity, developmental pediatrics, etc.) as well as with the pediatrics department of the hospital to ensure quality multidisciplinary care. · Provide guidance and transfer of your expertise to young doctors and nursing teams in an ongoing training approach.

Qualifications

  • · Diploma of Doctor with specialization in Pediatrics, recognized in Switzerland or eligible for equivalence. MEBEKO recognition in Switzerland or during the proceedings
  • · Experience in general paediatrics (hospital care and consultations) and ability to manage a variety of childhood pathologies.
  • · Ability to work as a team and collaborate with different actors in the care path.
  • · Interest in social or medical-psychosocial paediatrics.

Benefits

MediPed management encourages a practice focused on general paediatrics with a team ready to invest in a regional context close to patients. The possibility of exercising a varied activity including a coupled format of care (consultations in practice and hospital practice). A framework for training, individual development and sharing experience within a state-of-the-art technical platform. Very good social conditions and a structured remuneration model.
