Full description

Basic builder:in EFZ About the profession As a basic builder: you are used before the actual construction work begins. You ensure that the underground on which the buildings or the traffic routes are later built is sustainable and safe. You solidify the ground by, for example, drilling or ramming piles of concrete or steel into the ground. Very deep excavations you ensure against collapse risk. This is especially important on slopes or close to existing buildings. If a pit reaches into the groundwater, you lower the groundwater level. Activities

  • Calibration and erection
  • Setting up the construction site. Precisely measure drill holes. Transport and position devices.
  • Probing and determining
  • Explore ground conditions with small drilling equipment. Drill samples and record results.
  • Drilling and driving
  • Spraying cement mixture in loose substrate or voids. Drill or ram piles into the ground.
  • Securing and anchoring
  • Secure deep excavations with special walls. Welding and concrete reinforcement. Securing slopes with rock anchors.
  • Lowering and grasping
  • Lower groundwater for deep construction sites. Groundwater for drinking water supply.
  • You bring that with you

Basic builders: inside have to do with impressive machines and vehicles, they work in different places and are on the road all over the country. As a basic builder: in you are physically active and outside in any weather.

  • In this profession, you take on responsibility early on and your chances on the job market are excellent. As well as your further education and promotion opportunities.
  • Vocational apprenticeship at Ribbert AG
  • Ribbert AG is a subsidiary of Zindel United and trains basic builders in EFZ. The teaching lasts 3 years.

If you need a little more time to learn but are practically gifted, you can complete the 2-year vocational basic training basic construction practitioner: in EBA. If you have sufficient skills, you will then have the opportunity to graduate as a basic builder: in EFZ in another 2 years. Apply now Back to Overview
