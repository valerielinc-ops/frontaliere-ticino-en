Full description

About the role In order to strengthen our Test Center team in Widen (near Zurich), we offer a temporary position as a working student about 50% (m/f/d) in the laboratory for 6 months. In this role, you will assume the following tasks:

  • Provision of materials and test pieces in international projects
  • Production of bonded test specimens with different adhesive systems and different materials
  • Performance of tests on sealants and adhesives in accordance with standard specifications
  • Evaluation and documentation of results in English
  • Interface to various departments and functions

Your skills and experience

  • Student (uni or FH) in the field of natural sciences, materials and engineering or engineering
  • Ideally first experience in testing adhesives and substrates
  • Craftsmanship and technical understanding
  • Careful, reliable and structured workflow
  • Initiative and strong communication skills
  • Very good knowledge of English (negotiable) and good knowledge of German

About Sika Sika is a specialty chemical company, global leader in the development and production of systems and products for gluing, sealing, steaming, reinforcing and protecting in construction and industry. Sika has a global presence with subsidiaries in 102 countries, produces in over 400 factories, develops innovative technologies for customers around the world and thus contributes significantly to the sustainable transformation in construction and transport. The more than 33,000 employees generated a turnover of CHF 11.20 billion in 2025.
