SBB CFF FFS What awaits you. At SBB Cargo, you are at the heart of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 185 000 tonnes per day, thus relieving the road by 15,000 journeys per truck per day and the environment by 460,000 tonnes of CO2 per year. An innovative working environment, in which you move the CFF freight with your teammates. At SBB Cargo, you are at the heart of the Swiss economy. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even in the case of irregular working hours. The night and weekend work are also paid at home - for example with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night missions, you also benefit from time supplements of between 10 and 30%. This means you have more free time than employees who do not work at night or on weekends. What you can move.

  • As a category B100 Cargo locomotive pilot, you drive up to 100 km/h in your area and deliver and pick up freight cars from customers. You circulate goods to ensure the quality of life in Switzerland.
  • You make sure along the railway lines of Switzerland that our customers' goods arrive at their destination in a safe and timely manner.
  • You take the lead in the convoy and respond to the safety and punctuality of freight trains on a busy rail network.
  • You take on the role of specialist in safe, appropriate (articulate) and ecological driving of different freight trains.
  • You manage complex manoeuvring movements, act autonomously, enter business and operating data into digital systems, and ensure the customer relationship within this framework.

What you bring.

  • Team spirit and willingness to work according to irregular schedules and weekends.Physical and psychic resistance; independent and responsible working methods; ability to act correctly and deliberately.
  • In possession of the Cat. B100 locomotive pilot licence.
  • Knowledge of the requirements specific to freight traffic is an asset, you are willing to acquire the skills necessary for the full execution of the transport mandate (radio remote control, VOT, EWC).
  • Oral and written understanding of the language of the place (mother tongue level); knowledge of a second national language (certified level A1).

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
