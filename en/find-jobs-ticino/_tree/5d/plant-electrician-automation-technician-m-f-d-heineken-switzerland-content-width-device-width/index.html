Tradition, technology and passion – that's what our brewery stands for. To ensure secure, smooth and efficient production processes in the future, we are looking for an engaged and responsible person at our site in Lucerne. Tasks: Working on electrical equipment, machines and installations Maintenance and troubleshooting in production and energy installations Program changes and adjustments to SPS controls (Siemens is an advantage) Coordination of external companies and internal work in close collaboration with production Support and interface to alarm and notification systems (e.g. fire alarm systems, PSA) Creating, maintaining and applying technical documentation Order processing and storage of spare parts Participation in technical projects Implementation of improvements (processes, automations, TPM) Carrying out cause analyses according to TPM methodology Compliance with all applicable safety regulations Requirements: Completed apprenticeship as an electrician or automation technician (NIV 15 13 is an advantage) Several years of professional experience in maintenance of industrial plants is an advantage; motivated job beginners are also welcome Experience with SPS controls (Siemens) is an advantage Technical knowledge in measurement and control technology Good user knowledge of MS Office (E-Plan is an advantage) Very good German language skills in speech and writing Lean knowledge (TPM, Six Sigma etc.) is an advantage Willingness for on-call duty and for further training We offer a job without shift work in a small, well-coordinated team, attractive employment conditions, interesting benefits, a collegial working environment in a centrally located brewery. If our job interests you, please send us your application documents. Respect, enjoyment and quality from passion are our core values. As a beer producer with brewery sites in Chur and Lucerne and our own beverage trade with 17 locations throughout Switzerland, we are a company of national importance. We rely on a mix of tradition, innovation and customer proximity. With our beer brands, we contribute to the varied Swiss beer culture. We brew the joy of true togetherness to inspire a better world. One day, one beer, one toast after the other.
