Full description

KOMPETENT UND HERZLICH With more than 600 employees in the Schiers hospital, the three retirement homes Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, the Spitex, living with service, the crèche and parental counselling, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in the Prättigau between Grüsch and Klosters. As of 1 August 2027, we will award an apprenticeship specialist hotel industry - housekeeping EFZ During your training you will learn:

  • Creating a pleasant atmosphere for patients as well as for residents and our guests
  • Organisation of cleaning of rooms and facilities
  • Use and maintenance of professional cleaning machines
  • Working in the laundry circuit. Collection, sorting, washing, preparation and repair of laundry items
  • Organising, planning and implementing events
  • Receiving, advising and serving guests in the service
  • Responsibility for the entire course of the meal service
  • Making and cooking simple dishes
  • Learning and implementing regulations such as regulations of hygiene, health protection, occupational safety and environmental protection in the Flury Foundation

This is what you bring with you

  • Completion of compulsory schooling
  • Enjoyment in dealing with and contacting our patients and residents
  • Joy in domestic activities and sense of order and cleanliness

We offer you

  • A varied apprenticeship
  • A competent and warm team that accompanies and supports you during your training
  • Modern infrastructure combined with very good employment conditions
  • Monthly, practical learning days with the trainer
  • 6 weeks of vacation First questions will be answered by Mrs. Gabriela Sprenger or Mrs. Evelyne Willi Tel. +41 81 308 08 25 More information about the Flury Foundation can be found on the Internet: flurystiftung.ch Please send your application by email to: hr@flurystiftung.ch
