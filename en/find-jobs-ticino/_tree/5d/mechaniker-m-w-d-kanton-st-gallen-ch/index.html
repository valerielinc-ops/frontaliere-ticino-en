Full description

The National Roads Division VI of the Civil Engineering Office of the Canton of St.Gallen makes a significant contribution to the safety of the national roads in the cantons of Glarus, Thurgau and St.Gallen.

What to expect

  • Maintenance of vehicles and equipment and technical installations
  • Procurement and management of material and spare parts
  • Locksmith work on vehicles, signalling, game protection fences, etc.
  • Repair of sanitary facilities (WC, washing boxes, etc.) on rest areas
  • Vehicle roadside breakdown service also outside normal working hours, depending on urgency
  • Road maintenance and winter service equipment
  • Signalling work on national roads
  • Miscellaneous cleaning work and maintenance of the green areas on the workshop
  • Increase in mileage LSVA

What sets it apart

  • Completed apprenticeship as a truck or agricultural machinery mechanic or as a locksmith
  • C/CE driving licence or willingness to acquire it
  • Careful and responsible handling of machinery and equipment
  • High commitment and a resilient and flexible personality
  • Team spirit and joy of cooperation
  • Good PC knowledge and safe handling of digital aids

What we offer

  • A versatile and exciting activity with varied challenges
  • Working with a modern and well-equipped fleet
  • Attractive employment conditions and contemporary working conditions
  • Careful training
  • Diverse training and development opportunities
  • A motivated team and a collegial working environment

Contact: Andreas Koller, Head of Workshop, Tel. 058 229 85 33
