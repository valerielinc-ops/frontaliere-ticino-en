Full description

Description of work EMIL EGGER SA is one of the leading Swiss logistics companies and offers its customers a full range of services in the fields of general freight, heavy transport and warehouse logistics. About 800 employees currently work in 11 locations for renowned customers in industry, crafts and commerce. Our special works department includes gruting services, special transport, industrial relocations and the rental of lifting platforms. As part of our business development, we are looking for a Logistics & Maintenance Operator – Automated Store to ensure the smooth operation of our automated installation and to participate actively in logistics operations.

Tasks

  • Ensure the various logistical activities related to the loading and unloading of the automated store.
  • Analyze faults and perform first-level repairs.
  • Perform the necessary controls and interventions for the proper functioning of the equipment.
  • Ensure compliance with safety procedures, work instructions and quality standards.
  • Participate in the continuous improvement of facilities and processes.

Your Profile

  • Holder of an Automated Machine Operator CFC or equivalent training.
  • Successful experience in a similar position, ideally in an industrial or automated environment.
  • Good knowledge of automation, electricity and IT, as well as good ability to read and interpret technical drawings and plans.
  • Practical mind and ability to diagnose and solve technical problems.
  • Autonomous, rigorous and organized, you also appreciate teamwork.
  • You are comfortable in an environment where responsiveness and versatility are essential.

Assets

  • Working hours during the day.
  • Interesting and forward-looking activities in a dynamic company
  • Different tasks in an exciting environment
  • state-of-the-art equipment
  • A Family Business Culture
  • Pay in line with responsibilities
