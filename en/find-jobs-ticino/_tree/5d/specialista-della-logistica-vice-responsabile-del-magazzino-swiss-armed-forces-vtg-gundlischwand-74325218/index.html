Full description

To the point

Are you passionate about logistics in a unique environment? With this role, the Logistics Base of the Swiss Armed Forces gives you the opportunity to ensure the smooth operation of the receipt, storage, dispatch, delivery and handover of military equipment at the Oberland warehouse. An exciting challenge awaits you, as well as the opportunity to apply and further develop your expertise.

  • Start date: by agreement
  • Employment type: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-19892
  • Work location: Hinter der Egg 123c, 3815 Gündlischwand

This is what you can contribute

  • Provide cover for the head of the Oberland warehouse in all matters during her absence
  • Plan and coordinate schedules and work processes in materials management
  • Independently handle a wide range of assignments in the area of resupply (a large part of the work is carried out underground)
  • Ensure compliant warehouse management on site, as well as the planning and execution of inventories at all warehouses
  • Ensure the quality of the goods in storage

This is what makes you unique

  • Completed vocational training as a logistics specialist with further training as a logistics professional
  • Several years of experience in logistics and leadership experience are desirable
  • Good organizational skills and an efficient, conscientious working style
  • A Category B driving licence and SUVA-recognized forklift training are required; a Category C driving licence is an advantage and must be obtained if required
  • Very good MS-Office and SAP skills
  • English at B1 level is an advantage
  • Willingness to undergo training in the use of coercive measures and weapons is required

What we offer

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to Switzerland's successful model and work for the well-being of the population.
  • Diversity in practice Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring different perspectives to the table.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees on physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Logistics Base of the Swiss Armed Forces (LBA) is there for the troops. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss Armed Forces and manages the materiel, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss Armed Forces with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12'000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, indoors or outdoors: the LBA offers a broad range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. There is something for everyone here.

The Swiss Armed Forces employ over 9'000 people in four military and around 200 civilian professions—and this at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: the Swiss Armed Forces train apprentices in over 30 professions.
