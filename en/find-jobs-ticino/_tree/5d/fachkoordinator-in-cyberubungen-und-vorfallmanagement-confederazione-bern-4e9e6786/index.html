Full description

  • Design, create and lead realistic exercise scenarios involving various relevant knowledge providers and experts
  • Lead and take responsibility for the strategic involvement of the BACS in cyber exercises and coordinate the participation of the BACS in cyber exercises
  • Participate in cyber exercises as a representative of the BACS and, if necessary, take over the coordination of cyber exercises across organisations
  • Prepare and present conclusions and results drawn from cyber exercises (review of the applied processes, effectiveness of the cyber exercise, etc.) in a step-wise manner and propose improvement measures
  • Support the Cyber Commander in handling cyber incidents of high relevance and represent them in their absence
  • Track the global cyber threat landscape and technical developments in the cybersecurity environment to incorporate new trends, patterns and impacts on critical infrastructures into the above-mentioned services
  • university degree or equivalent in computer science or information security or equivalent professional experience
  • Knowledge of the current cyber threat landscape, common attack vectors and the tactics, techniques and procedures (TTPs) used by cyber actors
  • Good knowledge of best practices and processes for managing cyber incidents at all levels (technical, organizational, legal, etc.)
  • Experience in controlling and coordinating complex coordination processes
  • Strong communication and cooperation skills and strategic thinking and action
  • Good knowledge of two official languages and in English
