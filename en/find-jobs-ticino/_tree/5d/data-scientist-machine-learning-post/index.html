Data Scientist (Machine Learning) — Die Schweizerische Post

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

At Swiss Post, data plays a central role in improving processes, making more informed decisions and developing data-based solutions for our business partners. Do you want to help shape this development and not only design machine learning solutions, but also implement them robustly, comprehensibly and close to production? With you, we successfully put machine learning solutions into practice!

You can do that.

  • You will develop data- and model-based solutions for concrete business challenges in the context of our mobility services.
  • You work with diverse data sets, modern cloud technologies and a team that combines data science, data engineering and business understanding.
  • You will independently carry out data analyses and develop machine learning models for specific technical issues – from exploratory analysis to productive use.
  • Together with an interdisciplinary team, you drive the data-driven further development of our products and services.
  • You build reproducible training, evaluation and deployment pipelines, moving from individual analyses and prototypes to sustainable ML solutions.
  • You work closely with data engineers and business stakeholders, assess the technical feasibility and model quality and communicate the results clearly to different stakeholders.

You bring that with you

  • You bring several years of experience in data science and machine learning and have not only developed, but also evaluated and used models productively.
  • A university degree (university or university of applied sciences) in data science, computer science, mathematics, statistics or a comparable discipline forms the basis for this role.
  • You have in-depth knowledge of ML engineering and MLOps practices, such as experiment tracking, model versioning, CI/CD for ML, reproducible pipelines and automated testing.
  • Cloud technologies in the AWS environment such as SageMaker, S3, Lambda or Bedrock as well as machine learning frameworks and tools such as Python, scikit-learn, TensorFlow or PyTorch you use securely and purposefully.
  • You value clean, traceable and maintainable code and bring the basics of software engineering (versioning, testing, code reviews).
  • Stylistic German and good English skills for working with your teammates in Lisbon round off your profile.

Due to the team composition, we are particularly pleased about applications from women.
