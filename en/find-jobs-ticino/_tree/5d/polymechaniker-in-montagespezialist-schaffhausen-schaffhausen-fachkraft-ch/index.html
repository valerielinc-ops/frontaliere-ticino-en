Full description

Install and machine electrically, hydraulically and pneumatically controlled components with precision. The company offers you a versatile working environment with modern equipment. For our client we are looking for an activity

  • fitting electrical, hydraulic and pneumatic parts professionally
  • complete assembly and disassembly of injection moulds
