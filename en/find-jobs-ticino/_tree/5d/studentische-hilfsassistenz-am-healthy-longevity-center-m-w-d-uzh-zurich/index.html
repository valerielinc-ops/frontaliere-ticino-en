Healthy Longevity Center

Job start January 2027, temporary

The Healthy Longevity Center (HLC) of the University of Zurich (https://www.hlc.uzh.ch/en.html) pursues research and innovation in the field of healthy longevity with the overarching goal of understanding and promoting the development and maintenance of functional ability and quality of life in old age. With the LIFEstreams data ecosystem, we at the HLC are developing an infrastructure to support research and innovation and are looking for a student assistant with an interest in engaging in the integrative, sustainable use of data for ageing research. Applications are now possible. The position shall be filled and limited in time from January 2027.

Tasks

The position to be filled is located in the project "Ethical, Legal and Social Implications (ELSI)" and is associated with a versatile range of tasks, such as: Research activities  Organizational and substantive participation in the activities of the ELSI project Assistance in organizing events Simple data analysis and visualization of data and analysis results

Profile

 Bachelor degree in a field of study that has interface knowledge to technology, society, law and ethics, e.g. psychology, sociology, data ethics, law, data science, life sciences as well as interdisciplinary study programs in the field of digital society, digital humanities or science & technology studies Interest in Healthy Ageing and Sustainable, Integrative Use of Data Independent, reliable, careful and self-initiative working style  Very good knowledge of German (C1), good knowledge of English (B2)  High communication skills  Secure handling of common MS Office applications (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)  Basic statistical knowledge
