Full description

Lead the SAP Expert team within BU TR Hub EUC which supports the SAP users as internal business partners to train, improve processes and harmonize the way of working within SAP templates across 17 factories.

Drive digitalization and global program implementation within the HUB by ensuring adherence to standard processes and effective use of core systems across the full value chain - from quotation and engineering to project management, manufacturing, and shipment. Identify, analyze and enhance business processes to enable continuous improvement, innovation, and sustainable growth, while harmonizing operations and resolving user issues. Partner with cross‑functional stakeholders and Digital Transformation Organization (DTO) to enhance organizational efficiency and promote operational excellence.

  • § Coach less experienced staff and provide training to develop new talent.

  • § Develop risk mitigation strategies.
  • § Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

Requirements

  • § Minimum of 5 years of experience in operational roles related to business process management or digital program implementation in the manufacturing industry.

  • § Strong understanding of change management methodologies and best practices.
