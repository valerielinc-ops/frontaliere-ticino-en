Your Mission

  • Apply personalized physiotherapy treatments for internal and external patients.
  • Care for patients with various pathologies requiring rehabilitation or rehabilitation (orthopaedics, neurological, post-surgical, respiratory...).
  • Collaborate with the in-house health and medical team and hospital services for diagnostic, preventive and therapeutic measures.
  • Take part in various activities such as gymnastics classes, yoga in partnership with the animation service.
  • Actively participate in the medical staff and in the interdisciplinary conference.

Your profile

  • Diploma in physiotherapist (recognized in Switzerland or ready to take the necessary steps).
  • Professional experience, particularly in rehabilitation.
  • Interest in geriatrics.
  • Team spirit, sense of initiative and innovation.
  • Ability to work interdisciplinaryly.

Our strengths

  • Modern technical tray with 3 therapy rooms, a movement space, a wellness area and specialized equipment.
