# Polymechanic EFZ 80-100% - Connecting precision and technology #

Employment: 80-100 %

Location: Klosters

Industry:Industry diverse

Release date: 06.11.2026

Our client is a company based in the canton of Graubünden. Thanks to its central location in the Alps, it has developed over decades into a special supplier of valves for hydropower plants. Today, customers worldwide draw on the company’s expertise to deploy reliable safety organs in hydropower plants. For more than 50 years, it has specialized in the development and production of valves for hydropower plants.

Your tasks

  • Production of prototypes, individual parts and small series
  • Operation and programming of CNC milling and/or lathes
  • Independent manufacture of mechanical components according to technical drawings

What to expect

  • Varied activity in the field of renewable energy
  • International environment and technologically advanced products
  • Self-employed and self-responsible work
  • Flexible working hours and attractive employment conditions
  • Long-term employment with training options
  • Strong and highly motivated team

Your profile

  • Completed apprenticeship as a polymechanic, production mechanic or equivalent training (apprentices are welcome)
  • Good knowledge of the operation of CNC controls on milling and/or lathes
  • Quality-conscious, precise and independent working method
  • Team-capable, open-minded and resilient personality
