To support the project leadership in the establishment and implementation of the hospital-wide process and document management, we are looking for a person for the 1st of July 2026 or upon agreement for 6 to 9 months.   • Taking on tasks in quality management and in collaborations

  • Open, communicative, and engaged personality with initiative

  • Affordable parking with Parkingpay With us, you will experience a versatile internship in a dynamic hospital environment, get direct insights into corporate development, and can bring in your ideas. You will work closely with the corporate development leadership, take on responsibility, and develop skills that will take you further in the long run.
