Full description

Municipal clerk: Take on responsibility, make an impact – for a modern, service-oriented municipality.

Our client - the municipality of St. Moritz - shapes the sustainable development of the town, creates reliable conditions and provides a wide range of services for residents, the business community, political institutions and other stakeholders. The municipal administration plays a central role in this and operates in a demanding and dynamic environment. As part of a succession process, we have been commissioned to approach you as a professionally skilled and personable Head of Chancellery / Municipal Clerk.

Responsibilities

  • Reporting directly to the municipal president, you lead the municipal chancellery, which encompasses chancellery services, residents' services, social services, libraries and legal services, with a total of 18 employees.
  • You plan and support the affairs of the municipal executive, the municipal council and the municipal assembly.
  • This includes preparing for and following up on meetings, taking minutes, and editing official communications, applications and resolutions.
  • Together with the legal services team, you ensure that matters are processed in compliance with the law.
  • You are also responsible for elections and votes, official publications, the municipal archive, and various permitting and service processes.
  • You further develop the chancellery, optimize processes, drive digitalization forward and contribute to cross-departmental projects.

Requirements

  • For this varied role, you have a sound education and further training in public administration, municipal secretarial services, Public Management, law or business administration.
  • You have several years of leadership experience in a municipal administration, a municipal secretariat, an executive office, a legal department or a comparable role.
  • We expect a good understanding of administrative and municipal law, confidence in dealing with political authorities, and a polished command of German.

Contact
