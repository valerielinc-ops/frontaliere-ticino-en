Full description

The Team Partnerships & Live Comm KM is responsible for the national corporate partnerships in the area of communication and designs relevant and diverse encounters for stakeholders, audiences and employees. The team is part of the KM Channels & Production department, which is responsible, among other things, for the target group-specific creation and distribution of content on SRG’s national communication channels.

This is your contribution.

In the Team Partnerships & Live Comm KM you are committed to the following topics:

  • Conduit, i.e. Participation in national projects of strategic importance
  • Negotiation and management of partnerships in line with the SRG corporate strategy
  • Design, planning and implementation of live communication projects
  • Planning of communication activities in consultation with planners: within the Corporate Newsroom
  • Optional: management of the Partnerships & Live Comm KM team as part of the management team of the Channels & Production department within the Communications & Public Affairs department

They inspire us.

  • Completed studies (uni/FH) in the field of marketing, sponsorship, live communication or communication sciences
  • Several years of professional experience in the areas of sponsorship and live communication or in-depth experience in project management
  • Independent and structured working methods as well as strategic and holistic thinking
  • Negotiation and communication strength as well as ability to communicate convincingly, securely and at eye level in interdisciplinary teams
  • Strong interest in the Swiss media landscape, audiovisual media and communication
  • Stylistic German with good oral and written knowledge of French

So we inspire you.

  • Exciting and versatile function in an agile and dynamic service public environment
  • Broad range of tasks as well as a lot of personal responsibility and freedom in the design of your work
  • Multilingual working environment
  • Experienced team with an open communication climate
  • Flexible working hours with the option to work partially in the home office

Application deadline: 27. November 2026.

We thank you in advance to submit your application exclusively via the SRG job portal.

Our promise

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expertise, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRG!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion formation and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Geneva?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Geneva's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Geneva's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
