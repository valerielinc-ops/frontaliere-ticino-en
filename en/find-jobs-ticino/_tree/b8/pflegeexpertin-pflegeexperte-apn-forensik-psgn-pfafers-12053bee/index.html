Your tasks

  • As a committed and experienced nursing expert, you will strengthen our two teams in forensics and bring your special interest in psychiatry with specialised forensics.
  • With the opening of the second forensic station in October 2027, you accompany their professional development and develop the nursing care with your knowledge and experience.
  • You advise and support the interdisciplinary teams in highly complex patient situations with a focus on forensics and develop and implement practical nursing concepts and solutions.
  • In practical day-to-day work, you work directly with the teams together with the station management and the nursing management forensics in complex situations and work closely with other specialist areas for holistic patient care.
  • You introduce new employees professionally and support them in a safe entry into the forensic psychiatric work day. Your profile
  • You have completed the Master of Science in Nursing and would like to register as a nursing expert APN CH or are still in the master’s degree and would like to deepen your expertise in forensics.
  • With your empathetic personality, strong communication skills and a high level of teamwork, you can keep a cool head even in challenging situations.
  • You bring a high level of consulting competence and want to contribute and further develop your specialist leadership skills.
  • You see your work as a connection between stationary work and cooperation with the Department of Care.
  • As an initiative personality, you act goal-oriented and solution-oriented and enjoy the multi-professional cooperation. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. We are on the road digitally and are committed to sustainability, so we look forward to your online application via our homepage. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also get the opportunity to get to know your future team. You will receive a telephone confirmation from us if we decide for you after the second interview and ask you if you are still convinced of us. If we agree, we will draw up your contract. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also get your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
