Full description

At our “Living” location in Basel, tenants not only feel at home, but are also managed optimally and efficiently by our new division of tasks in the team. This not only has advantages for tenants and ownership, but also for you. Through more specialization in the team, we work more closely together, and everyone can focus on their strengths.

If your heart beats for real estate, you are motivated, goal-oriented and also like to work in a team, then we look forward to your application as

Rental specialist real estate (m/f/d)

Basel, 50 %

This is what moves you

  • You are responsible for processing the digital re-letting process with a focus on secondary objects
  • Creating high-quality and error-free online advertisements as well as commissioning further marketing measures such as photos and 360° tours are among your tasks.
  • You carefully examine applications and select suitable tenants to subsequently create rental contracts for residential and ancillary properties
  • The implementation of apartment visits with tenants as well as independent acceptances and handovers are also your responsibility
  • First contact person: in the office you take over the customer support on site and initiate measures to monitor and prioritize rentals and vacancies

This is why you succeed

  • You have completed vocational training (e.g. KV, Detailhandel, Handelsschule or Ä.)
  • You also have several years of professional experience in customer contact
  • First real estate experiences are advantageous, lateral entrants: insiders are also welcome
  • You act solution- and customer-oriented and enjoy the personal interaction with people
  • An independent and reliable working method as well as good communication skills and negotiating skills distinguish you
  • Your readiness for flexible deployments in the evening and Saturday round off your profile
  • You communicate verbally and in writing fluently in German, English of advantage

Here are some of our benefits

  • Working hours: We live mobile work with individual (year) working hours and/or part-time work. Up to 40% home office is possible. Work-life balance is a top priority for us, because balanced and relaxed employees are important to us. The purchase of additional holidays is possible.
  • Mobility and food: You will receive a contribution for the mobile phone. For your food, we also make a contribution.
  • Education and training: Promoting employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support in internal and external education and training. We offer career opportunities in management, specialist and project functions
  • Salär: With us you can expect a market-based remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. The pension benefits are attractive and you can choose the right one for you from different models. The accident protection insurance covers the private hospital department.

More information about our benefits can be found on our career page.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Basel's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Basel's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
