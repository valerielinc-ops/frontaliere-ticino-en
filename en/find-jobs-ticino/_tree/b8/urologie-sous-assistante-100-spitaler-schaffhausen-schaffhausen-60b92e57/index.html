Full description

In the elective year (2nd academic year Master in Human Medicine), students can gain in-depth insights into the work of a urologist as sub-assistants in our clinic for urology. They actively participate in clinical everyday life.

Tasks

  • Together with the medical team, you will look after the urological patients in the ambulatory, inpatient area and in the operating room.
  • Entrance examinations/anamnesis survey in elective patients
  • If necessary, you provide assistance in various operations
  • Participation in departmental work such as visits, patient care and KG registrations
  • Various department-internal training opportunities
  • Free accommodation in the nearby staff house

Requirements

  • Commitment and commitment in a clinically interdisciplinary oriented activity
  • Very good knowledge of German in language and writing (minimum requirement level C1)
  • Social competence in dealing with patients and relatives
  • Swiss or EU citizens
  • Student in Human Medicine

We offer

Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well.

The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
