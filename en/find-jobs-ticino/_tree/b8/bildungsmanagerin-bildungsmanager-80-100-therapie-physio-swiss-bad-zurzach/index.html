As a leading training center for medical-therapeutic specialists, we develop practical educational offers for professional and personal development. At our locations in Bad Zurzach and Winterthur, we accompany numerous specialists every year on their learning path. Now we take the next step: Instead of purchasing existing courses, we develop our own educational formats and structured learning paths. For this we are looking for a committed personality who actively shapes and develops our educational portfolio. Your tasks Developing educational services

  • You design new training offers and develop existing courses
  • You define learning goals, structure, methodology and didactics and provide a compelling learning experience
  • Together with experts, you will develop innovative and practical educational formats

Designing learning pathways

  • You develop structured learning paths with coordinated basic, advanced and certification courses
  • You create attractive training opportunities that promote sustainable learning

Managing the Educational Portfolio

  • You analyze market needs and define the appropriate offer mix.
  • You assess demand, utilization and participant feedback and derive optimizations from this
  • Together with the area of finance, you develop economically successful offers

Cooperation with lecturers

  • You gain qualified lecturers and accompany them in the development of their courses
  • You ensure a high quality of our educational offers through clear didactic guidelines

Planning and marketing

  • You are responsible for the rolling course planning and coordinate appointments and capacities
  • You work closely with marketing and provide target group knowledge and content for effective communication

What you bring

  • Education: You have training in physiotherapy. Further training in adult education (e.g. SVEB certificate, CAS adult education or a comparable qualification) is an advantage
  • Experience: You bring experience in the development of educational offers or course formats
  • Working method: You work analytically, structured and independently. You think entrepreneurially and keep track of several projects
  • Personality: You are strong in communication, convince through your safe appearance and enjoy actively promoting topics

What we offer

  • Co-creation: Work in a modern working environment and actively participate
  • Inspiring and collegial working atmosphere: Working in a supportive, appreciative and humorous team
  • Above-average social security benefits: Benefit from attractive social security benefits
  • Education and training: generous share of costs in education and training
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers, pharmacies and our membership at Benefit@work
  • Free admission: Enjoy free admission and other benefits at the FORTYSEVEN spa.
