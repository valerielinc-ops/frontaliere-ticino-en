Full description

The Bergbahnen Brigels AG offer in the middle of a magnificent mountain world with railway companies, restaurants and snow bars all the advantages of a family-friendly holiday destination. With the 40 km best groomed slopes, of which 20 km snowed, we guarantee from 1100 to 2418 m above sea level with the four chairlifts and the two ski lifts fascinating snow sports with wide slopes and numerous downhills without waiting times for young and old.

For the coming winter season we are looking for reinforcement as (m/f/d):

Train workers

We offer: A motivated team with a good working atmosphere and a great view. Seasonal employment in full / part-time or as a temporary from December to the end of March. A free annual pass for the snow sports area Brigels Waltensburg Andiast as well as a discounted annual pass for your children (children up to 16 years old receive a free subscription), your partner. Three free day tickets of our mountain railways for handover to your friends / acquaintances. Cash loyalty bonus on a season repeat. Free parking in Brigels and Waltensburg. If desired with accommodation in the staff house. During your employment, you will benefit, among other things, from an employee discount in your own catering establishments as well as from an assortment and service discount in the Pradas sports shop.

Your profile: You are enthusiastic about winter sports and can ski or snowboard. You have good German skills, Romance and English skills are an advantage but not a must. Willingness for weekend service round off your profile. You are sociable, responsible, friendly and happy to take care of the well-being of our small and large guests.

Are you having fun spending the winter season in the idyllic mountains of the Surselva? Then you are exactly right with us!
