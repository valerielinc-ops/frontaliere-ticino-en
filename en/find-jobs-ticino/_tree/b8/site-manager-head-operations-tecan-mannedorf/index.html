Site Manager & Head Operations (80-100%)

Männedorf, Zürich

Your contribution

In this challenging leadership position, you are responsible for the professional and personnel management of the Operations area. You ensure high-quality, timely and cost-efficient production and delivery of our equipment and equipment. As a Site Manager, you take over the overall responsibility for occupational safety, health and environmental protection as well as the facility management at our locations in Männedorf and Nänikon. You promote a culture of continuous improvement and create a work-friendly environment.

Defining and controlling targets for production, purchasing, planning and shipping

Construction and leadership of a “best in the industry” Operations Team

Introduction and further development of continuous improvement processes (CDP)

Ensuring compliance with laws, standards and internal requirements (ISO 9001, ISO 13485, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, MDSAP)

Responsibility for the Budget and Resource Planning in Operations and Facility Management

Your profile

We are not only looking for expertise: we are looking for people who translate their passion and talent with us.

Higher technical basic training (FH or comparable), ideally with engineering background

Business and/or production/logistics further education

Multiannual industry and leadership experience

Knowledge of production, material management, purchasing and logistics

Very good knowledge of German and English, other languages of advantage

Entrepreneurial thinking, high leadership qualities and team spirit

Load capacity and enforcement capacity
