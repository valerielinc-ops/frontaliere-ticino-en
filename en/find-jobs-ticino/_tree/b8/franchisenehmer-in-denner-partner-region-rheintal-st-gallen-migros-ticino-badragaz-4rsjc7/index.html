Full description

Do you want to become self-employed and run a Denner store in your region with your own personal touch? Let’s combine your entrepreneurial flair, your keen sense for the local market, and our know-how as a leading discounter to create something great: your store.

Responsibilities

  • Independently manage your store as a Denner Partner or Denner Express franchise partner
  • The best of both worlds: benefit from the know-how of the most successful food discounter while being your own boss
  • Access Denner’s wide and affordable assortment as well as attractive weekly promotions
  • Supplement the Denner offering with targeted regional products
  • Local or village spirit? We love it! Your store brings life to the neighborhood or countryside

Requirements

  • Industry and management experience: ideally in retail (food)
  • Completed basic or further training: preferably in retail, ideally in food
  • Entrepreneurial and business thinking
  • High commitment and drive to make your store a success
  • Sensitivity to the needs of your local customers
  • Good reputation and starting capital of at least CHF 50,000
  • German (very good skills)

What we offer

  • Individual training: targeted development through Denner AG’s internal hubs.
  • Responsibility & freedom: plenty of room for your ideas and to make a difference together with us.
  • Creative and decision-making freedom: opportunity to help shape Denner’s success story and implement your own ideas.
  • Successful franchising system: simple and promising franchising system with an attractive financing model.
  • Proven services: we relieve you of many tasks so you can focus on your customers, employees, and store.
  • Management of multiple locations: particularly successful partners can also manage several locations (multi-site operations).
  • Reliable partnership: competent and collaborative advice based on over 50 years of experience in building successful partnerships.
  • Reduced risk: the proven franchising system offers manageable risk for your self-employment.
  • Introduction: professional onboarding with an experienced Denner partner who has already successfully taken this path.
