YOUR TASKS

  • Control and follow-up of patient and case data
  • Obtaining cost-benefit languages and information from service providers
  • Preparation of cost estimates for national and international patients: inside for upgrades and depots
  • Handling and transmission of guarantors’ letters
  • Recording and monitoring of incoming cost good languages
  • Ensuring timely information to patients: inside, guarantors, clinics and medical profession
  • Providing information to internal bodies and doctors as well as various administrative tasks (written and by telephone)
  • Close coordination and cooperation with relevant interfaces such as emergency disposition, patient disposition, check-in / guest services and Hirslanden International

Your profile

  • Teamwork, social skills and responsibility
  • High customer orientation and joy at the tel. Customer contact
  • High strength and assertiveness
  • Style-confident German and English in word and writing, every other language is advantageous
