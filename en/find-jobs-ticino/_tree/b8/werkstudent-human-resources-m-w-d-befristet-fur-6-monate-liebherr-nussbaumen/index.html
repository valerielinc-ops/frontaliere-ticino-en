Full description

As a working student, you will support our HR team in day-to-day operations and gain exciting insights into a wide range of HR topics. Tasks:

  • Support for administrative HR processes from entry to exit
  • Creation of employment certificates, confirmations and other HR documents
  • Assistance in the maintenance and management of personal data
  • Support in applicant management and recruitment
  • Support for the organisation of training and onboarding measures
  • Participation in HR projects and support for process optimization
  • Preparation of evaluations, presentations and statistics Qualification:
  • Ongoing studies with specialisation in human resources, psychology or comparable fields of study
  • Interest in human resources and administrative processes
  • Independent, structured and careful working methods
  • High discretion and trustworthiness in handling sensitive data
  • Good MS Office skills, especially Excel and PowerPoint
  • Very good knowledge of German, good knowledge of English and French Our offer: Can we inspire you to take on a responsible task in a dynamic and independent family business? We offer you freedom for ideas, a secure workplace, progressive and family-friendly employment conditions such as flexible working hours, home office and further education opportunities. Have we aroused your interest? Then we look forward to your online application. For any questions, please contact Gina Aeberhard. Please note that applications via e-mail are not considered.
