Seller with shift responsibility (m/f/d) 60\-80 %

Introduction

Selling is your passion? Do you want to do your best every day to offer our customers the best possible shopping experience? Do you want to be part of a team that is needed every day? Then you are exactly right with us. Exciting and versatile career opportunities await you in the dynamic retail environment.

Your tasks

  • Shift responsibility in the absence of branch management and deputy
  • Implementation of customer orientation and sales standards in our branches
  • Support in personnel planning, personnel management and branch organisation
  • Goods disposition and presentation
  • Cash activity

Your profile

  • Professional experience in sales or a similar environment
  • Ideally completed vocational training in the retail trade
  • Flexibility regarding the operating times (MO\-SA between 05:00\-22:00 )
  • Communicative skills and pleasure in dealing with people
  • High motivation, commitment and resilience
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

We offer

At Lidl Switzerland, there is room for ambition and innovation. Actively participate. Grow daily on a professional and personal level. We prepare you for your varied and interesting tasks with an intensive training. If desired and with good personal suitability, we will develop you into the deputy branch management (m/f/d). In addition, we offer you the security of a permanent job, a great team, the advantages of the Lidl\-GAV, an excellent occupational provision and other attractive benefits. Take the chance & apply online now!
