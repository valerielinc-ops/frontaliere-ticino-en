Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. With a lot of commitment, we are there for our customers. In our 31 pick-up markets, by field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a versatile range, attractive own brands, professional specialist advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet Group. The company is a leader in the Swiss supply and pick-up wholesale trade and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers).

Tasks

  • You coordinate and implement maintenance work and smaller construction projects at our locations throughout Switzerland
  • You realize approved investment projects
  • You create annual budgets together with the location managers
  • You lead projects independently and coordinate external teams
  • You make sure that costs, deadlines and quality are met
  • You drive the further development and use of new technologies
  • You actively participate in the implementation of our sustainability topics

Requirements

  • You have technical and/or craft training in the construction sector and have experience in project management
  • You are familiar with facility management and retail business
  • You have experience in the realization of building technology and construction projects
  • You have in-depth PC knowledge, especially in MS Office
  • Ideally, you already have professional experience
  • You have knowledge of French
  • You are team-ready, open, flexible and loyal
