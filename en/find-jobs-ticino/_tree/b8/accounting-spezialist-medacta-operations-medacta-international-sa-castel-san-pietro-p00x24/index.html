Accounting Specialist - Medacta Operations — Medacta International SA

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The person we are looking for has matured an experience of 6/7 years in the general accounting and will take care of the management of the accounting of the new logistic pole located in Cermenate.

His duties will be

  • registration first note of cash and banking movements;
  • general accounting records (Suppliers, Customers, Setting Writings, etc.);
  • registration and control Cespiti;
  • supervise the fiscal and customs aspects related to logistics and production activities, ensuring compliance with VAT, customs and import/export regulations;
  • analysis and determination of monthly appropriations and processing and monitoring;
  • accounting closures and support for the editorial staff
  • preparation Payments to suppliers (Manuals and DTA);
  • Payments for tax payments and contributions;
  • analysis of economic accounts and assets;
  • reporting for management;
  • reconciliation Intercompany relations;
  • insertion and verification of data in Tagetik;
  • preparing documents for auditing companies.

Hard Skills

  • AFC trade diploma, accounting or equivalent title;
  • 6-7 years of experience in a similar position in industrial companies;
  • English language knowledge;
  • knowledge of the ERP Business Central system;
  • excellent knowledge of Office and especially Excel;

Soft Skills

  • autonomy at the workplace;
  • seriousness and precision in carrying out their activities;
  • strong organizational sense and ability to work in teams;
  • ability to work in a dynamic environment;
  • aptitude to “problem solving”.

What we offer

  • contract indefinitely;
  • flexible working hours;
  • carpooling organized by Medacta;
  • incentives for mobility with public transport;
  • advantageous business conventions;
  • growth opportunities, working in an environment in continuous improvement and expansion;
  • Annual review of the salary related to performance and professional development;
  • continuing training.
