Full description

As one of the world’s leading clinics, we develop an individualized treatment program with the aim of enabling our patients to have the highest possible degree of independence and freedom of life, thereby optimizing their quality of life. We address both patients from the Swiss health insurance system and international patients. cereneo offers highly specialized rehabilitation programs for people with neurological diseases and injuries. These include stroke, brain injury, neurodegenerative diseases such as Parkinson's, multiple sclerosis and many other neurological disorders. To strengthen our team, we are looking for an experienced and independent person in Hertenstein.

Your role

You play a crucial role in providing quality and appreciative care to our patients.

  • You are actively involved in the development and development of our new Intermediate Care (IMC), which will open in 2027.
  • You ensure individual, evidence-based and patient-centered care at a high professional level.
  • You work closely with doctors, therapists and other specialists to continuously develop the treatment quality and patient outcomes.
  • You are actively involved in the design and optimization of care processes and make an important contribution to the further development of the IMC.
  • You promote appreciative cooperation and contribute to a positive team culture with your open communication.

Your profile

We are looking for a resilient, communication-strong and proactive nurse who feels comfortable in an interprofessional environment. Ideally, you bring the following:

  • Completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH or equivalent training recognised by the Swiss Red Cross (SRC).
  • expertise and experience in monitoring care
  • Professional experience in neurorehabilitation is beneficial.
  • In-depth knowledge of quality standards in care and modern care methods.
  • A positive attitude, a high degree of patient, organizational skill and intercultural competence.
  • You take responsibility, think solution-oriented and bring your own initiative.
  • Good knowledge of German and English
  • Further language skills are advantageous.

Look forward to

  • Modern infrastructure: Perfectly located working environment with a view of Lake Vierwaldstättersee, Pilatus and Rigi.
  • Development: Personal training and development opportunities.
  • Social benefits: Good social benefits (NBU, employer share pension fund 60%).
  • Parking & public transport: Free parking and access to e-charging stations, subsidy for public transport.

Our corporate culture

  • EXCELLENCE: Using the latest research findings and first-class service
  • CHARACTERISTICS: We are always there with all our heart. Of course, this also includes the sincere treatment of our patients: inside, but also between our staff
  • MOTIVATION: Joint goal achievement and support of patients: women to improve their quality of life
  • TEAM WORK: Reliability and mutual support in the team
  • INNOVATION: Research and testing of new therapeutic approaches and devices
  • If you are an open, committed person who would like to work in an interdisciplinary team and continuously develop, we look forward to your application. Become part of our team and actively shape the future of neurological rehabilitation.

Ready to make a difference? If you are passionate about improving the lives of people with neurological diseases and want to develop in a supportive environment, we look forward to your application. Become part of the cereneo family and shape the future of rehabilitation with us. Apply now!
