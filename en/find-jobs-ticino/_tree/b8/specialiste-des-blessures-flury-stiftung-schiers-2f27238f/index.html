WUNDFACHPERSON | FROM 1 JANUARY 2027 OR AFTER AGREEMENT Do you want to accompany people with acute and chronic wounds professionally and actively shape wound care? Then bring your knowledge and ideas to our wound ambulatorium at the Schiers Hospital. With around 600 employees, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in the city between Grüsch and Klosters. These include the Hospital Schiers, the retirement homes Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, the Spitex, living with service, the crèche, the parental counselling and the Medical Center Klosters. Spital Schiers is a modern acute care hospital in the canton of Graubünden. With the clinics surgery/orthopedics, medicine, gynecology and obstetrics as well as a wide range of documents, it enables an extended basic care. The modern infrastructure includes three operating rooms, a certified IMC, a day clinic and a bed station with only single rooms. ITS TASK – MULTIPLE AND MEANING

  • They assess, treat and document acute and chronic wounds professionally.
  • They advise and accompany patients and their relatives around wound care.
  • They support and advise nurses and other professional groups in complex wound situations.
  • They ensure professional, evidence-based wound care according to current standards.
