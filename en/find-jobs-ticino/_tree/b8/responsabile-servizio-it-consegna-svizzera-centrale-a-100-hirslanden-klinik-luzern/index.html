Full description

Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna Lucerne | Home Office

Occupation by 1 May 2027 or by arrangement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 75175

Hirslanden stands for high-quality, responsible and integrated healthcare. In its network, Klinik St. Anna is the most important private acute care clinic in Central Switzerland. Classified by the federal government as a center hospital and equipped with a public service mandate, the clinic forms an essential part of regional health care and is committed to the further development of the "Continuum of Care" with and within the Hirslanden Group.

Do you not only want to operate IT, but actively participate? In this key role, you assume responsibility for the regional IT organization of Hirslanden Clinics in Central Switzerland. You will lead a team of nine employees, advise the clinic directorates on strategic and operational IT issues and drive forward digitization and transformation initiatives.

As a member of the Hirslanden IT Management Team, you represent the interests of the Central Switzerland region and ensure that the needs of the clinics are incorporated into the further development of our IT landscape. Together with the central IT teams, you will help ensure that modern technologies create tangible added value for employees, partner doctors and patients.

YOUR TASKS

  • Active maintenance of a strong stakeholder network in the clinics of St. Anna, AndreasKlinik Cham Zug and Meggen as well as the location of St. Anna at the train station through regular exchange with directorates, management teams and operational contacts as well as a high personal presence on site
  • Advising clinics on strategic, organizational and operational IT topics as well as digitalization initiatives
  • Leadership and further development of the regional team of around 9 employees in the areas of project management, application management and local IT support
  • Promoting a customer-oriented, solution-oriented and powerful team culture
  • Ensuring professional IT support for clinics in close cooperation with central IT teams
  • Responsibility for planning, prioritizing and implementing regional IT initiatives and projects
  • Steering and supporting change projects with a focus on sustainable benefits for clinic operations
  • Member of the IT Management Team of the Hirslanden Group and representation of the Central Switzerland region in strategic and operational IT topics
  • Support for critical incidents, escalations and operational challenges

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training with further or higher training (level HF/FH) in the field of project management, computer science, business and/or medical informatics
  • Several years of experience in project management, preferably in the hospital/medical computing field as well as management experience
  • Good communication with stakeholders at all hierarchical levels
  • Proactive, solution-oriented and cross-process thinking and acting also in complex problems as well as goal-oriented working methods
  • Business thinking, high service mentality and high quality awareness
  • Safer appearance and joy in the management of employees
  • Very good German and English skills in word and writing
