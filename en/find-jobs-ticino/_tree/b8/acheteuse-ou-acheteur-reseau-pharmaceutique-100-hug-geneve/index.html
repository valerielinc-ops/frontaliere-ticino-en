Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to see our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . Acting on behalf of the University Hospitals of the cantons of Vaud and Geneva, the role of the Biomedical Procurement and Engineering Centre (CAIB) is to structure and manage the entire procurement process, from the supplier strategy to contracting and performance monitoring.

Description of post

As a buyer or buyer you strengthen the team. In this role, you manage tenders commercially and administratively in accordance with the directives and the institutional purchasing policy, in accordance with the qualitative and economic requirements in force. Equipped or equipped with a common sense of communication and listening, you are a strength of proposals, ready or ready to invest in a changing group dynamic. You are required or required to manage the CAIB's procurement and pharmaceutical portfolio. You are responsible for monitoring and planning competitive bidding while ensuring quality of service.

Qualifications

You have an HES or equivalent graduate degree (Bachelor/Master level). You have an ease in the exchanges between the many interlocutors, you listen to understand their expectations and an assertive communication to enforce the purchasing policy. You have confirmed experience in the field of drug/hospital purchases. You are comfortable working through defined processes while at the same time being the driving force for proposals to identify sustainable improvement and optimization paths. You have excellent mastery and knowledge of computer tools. You have perfect command of the French language, both in writing and oral, and you have very good knowledge of English or German.
