Full description

Bachtelen is involved in the canton of Solothurn with a wide range of offers for the promotion, support and integration of young people. This includes special schools, various housing offers, individually coordinated therapy offers as well as consultations and daycare centers. Bachtelen employs around 550 people. In Grenchen, Bachtelen runs four daycare centers. The Kita Villa Kunterbunt, the Kita Märlihus and the Kita Teddybär as well as in Lommiswil a Hort and the Kita Lommiswil. These daycare centers offer the children a diverse learning and living world. For the Kita Teddybär in Grenchen we are looking for immediately or from 01.01.2027:e Group leader: in with Stv. the Kitaleitung 80-100 %

Your work includes

  • As a group leader: you take on the day-to-day responsibility of the children's group and are the contact person for your teammate:
  • You are responsible for the pedagogical development of the nursery and drive it forward in everyday life.
  • As deputy Kitaleitung you take on additional tasks of the Kita leadership and represent the Kitaleitung in absence

This is what you bring with you

  • You have your training for FaBe K. Completed 3 years ago and was able to gain experience in a comparable role during this time. Further education in childhood education HF is an advantage
  • You are ready to take responsibility and feel safe to guide the team in everyday life
  • High pedagogical competence in the promotion, care and accompaniment of children
  • Creativity, flexibility and high commitment characterizes your personality

This is what we offer you

  • An attractive workplace with plenty of room for initiative and participation
  • Flat hierarchies, short decision paths and an open, appreciative cooperation
  • Various employee events
  • Contemporary employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines
  • A meaningful work environment where your work makes a real difference
  • Generous support (money and/or time) for further training
