Full description

Every day, around 15,000 employees are involved in the Valora network to bring people on the road with a comprehensive foodvenience offer – close, fast, practical and fresh. Valora’s approximately 2,800 small-area sales points are located at high-frequency locations in Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Luxembourg and the Netherlands. The company includes among others k kiosk, Brezelkönig, BackWerk, Ditsch, Press & Books, avec, Caffè Spettacolo, Frittenwerk and the popular own brand ok. – as well as a constantly growing range of digital services. Valora also operates one of the world’s leading productions of lye pastries and benefits from a highly integrated value chain in the bakery sector. Valora generated external sales of CHF 2.9 billion in 2025. The Valora Group, headquartered in Muttenz, Switzerland, is the European retail unit of Fomento Económico Mexicano, S.A.B. de C.V. (FEMSA).
