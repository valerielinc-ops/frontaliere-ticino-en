Full description

Maintain, troubleshoot, repair, and keep records on production equipment, vehicles, HVAC, building and grounds and security system. See day to day functionality of manufacturing equipment and make/oversee repairs as needed.

Aufgaben

  • Performs trouble-shooting and diagnostic tests on all types of production equipment Investigates and executes economic repair options on production equipment
  • Executes preventative maintenance program
  • Maintains inventory of maintenance spare parts
  • Assists in the installation of new equipment, and relocation of existing equipment Performs maintenance on buildings, grounds and facility infrastructure
  • Coordinates with outside resources for delivery of repair goods and services
  • Maintains the highest quality of workmanship
  • Requires minimal supervision
  • Accepts/performs other responsibilities/duties as assigned by immediate supervisor
  • Complete tasks and operate equipment in a safe manner
  • Work at an acceptable and communicated level of productivity
