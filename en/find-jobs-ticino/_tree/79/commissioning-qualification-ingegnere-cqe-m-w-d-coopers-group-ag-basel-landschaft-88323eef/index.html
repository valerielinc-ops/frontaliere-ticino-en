Commissioning & Qualification Engineer (CQE) (m/f/d) — Coopers Group AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

For international projects in pharmaceutical plant construction, we are looking for a hands-on oriented Commissioning & Qualification Engineer. You support experienced CQEs directly at the customer and carry out operational IQ/OQ and qualification work on pharmaceutical equipment.

Hard facts

  • Start: By immediately or by arrangement, at the latest within 2 months
  • Duration: Initially 6 months, extension or later permanent employment possible
  • Pensum: 100%, 40 hours per week
  • Location: Europe, currently mainly Belgium
  • Work model: 100% travel activity, usually 2 weeks on site, then travel home
  • Expenses: Travel, accommodation and approved project expenses are covered

Your tasks

  • Implementation and documentation of IQ/OQ tests
  • Work according to existing test plans and templates
  • Implementation and support of calibrations
  • Ventilation, HVAC and clean room qualifications
  • GMP-compliant documentation of test results
  • Practical work on pharmaceutical plants and insulators
  • Collaboration with an experienced lead CQE on site

Your profile

  • About 3 years of experience in a GMP-regulated environment
  • Practical experience with IQ/OQ or Equipment Qualification
  • Knowledge of calibration, HVAC, cleanroom or facility qualification
  • German at least B2 and good English skills
  • Advantages of technical education or training
  • Hands-on mentality and physical strength
  • Readiness for Europe-wide missions and occasional additional hours

Insulator experience is beneficial, but not a requirement. Crucial are your practical GMP experience, reliable documentation and the willingness to work directly on the plants.

Before the first customer assignment, you will complete a one-week training course in the Basel region. Successful completion of the subsequent examination is a prerequisite for project deployment.

Interested?

Then we look forward to your CV and relevant certificates. Please also indicate your earliest possible start date.
