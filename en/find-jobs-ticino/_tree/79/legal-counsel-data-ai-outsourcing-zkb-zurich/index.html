80% - 100% | Legal / Compliance | Zurich | Experienced professionals Does your passion apply to new technologies? Are you proactively advancing change? Would you like to further expand your legal expertise in the field of data and technology law at a universal bank? Are you interested in cross-cutting topics and interdisciplinary cooperation? As Legal Counsel in the “Legal Data, AI & Outsourcing” team of the Zürcher Kantonalbank, you work at the interface between law and technology and thus keep up with the times. Your tasks

  • Advise all internal stakeholders on legal issues in data and AI law, related legal fields and governance issues
  • Analyze and develop new legal requirements, templates, processes, concepts and controls
  • Further develop the Group-wide legal data and AI framework and supervise the legal data and AI communities
  • Working in bank-wide change projects as a legal expert for data and AI law as well as related fields of law
  • Conducting physical and virtual training
  • Monitor and impact analyses of data and digital legal developments
  • Participation in internal and external consultations and the provision of services

Your profile

  • Completed law degree in law (lic.iur./MLaw), Swiss attorney’s patent beneficial
  • Good knowledge of data and AI law and related areas of law such as bank customer secrecy, contractual, competition, copyright and criminal law
  • Several years of practical experience in advising on cross-cutting topics in the financial industry or other regulated industries as an in-house legal or as an external consultant (e.g. law firm or audit firm)
  • You are strong in the analysis of complex, interdisciplinary questions and can show their impact and solution variants conceptually and precisely
  • Strong team orientation, personal responsibility and a solution-oriented working method
  • You convince with winning, address-oriented communication to different contacts and can contribute your concerns effectively
  • In a divisional organization with decentralized responsibilities, you feel comfortable and take on responsibility in a dynamic environment
  • You combine professional competence with a strong communication and network orientation. You shape your role by proactively seeking dialogue and personal exchange with relevant stakeholders to create new perspectives and synergies
  • You are curious, have no fear of touching technology-driven changes and an understanding of technical connections
  • Very good German skills (at least C1) and good English skills (at least B2)

Our offer

  • Agile, motivated team looking forward to working with you
  • Appreciative and dynamic working atmosphere and versatile tasks in your own area of responsibility
  • Opportunity to work with many different, exciting areas of our bank within this position
  • Your career is important to us as an employer and therefore we support and accompany you on your way
  • Modern workplace in the lively district 5 with very good transport connections (hard bridge station)
  • Comprehensive package, which in addition to the attractive basic salary and the variable compensation offers many additional services and benefits (e.g. sports offers, child care, health insurance, etc.)
