Full description

At the Beilngries site, around 360 employees in the Grain Quality and Supply (GQ) division are dedicated to plant and mechanical engineering in the areas of grain processing and malt & brewing.
This includes cleaning, drying, transport (including conveying equipment and ship loading), storage and digitization to process grain from the field to the consumer. Always with the focus on efficiency, product quality and sustainability.
