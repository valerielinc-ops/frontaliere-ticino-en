[Manpower SA]

Telephone adviser

Our client, a recognized player in his field, has commissioned us to recruit internal sales advisors in the area of telephony in the canton of Valais (M/F).

Post between 60 and 100% \- Monday to Saturday

Your missions:

  • Welcoming, advising and retaining clients with professionalism and enthusiasm
  • Identify customer needs and propose suitable solutions
  • Manage receipts and track customer orders
  • Take part in product development (merchandising, radiating, point-of-sale cleanliness)
  • Contribute to individual and collective sales objectives
  • Work with other team members to ensure excellent customer experience

Search profile:

  • Experience confirmed in sales or customer relationship (3 years minimum) and ideally in the telephony sector
  • Natural relational experience, active listening, developed sense of service
  • Autonomy, dynamism and team spirit
  • Careful presentation and positive attitude
  • Mastery of French, any other language is an asset (including English or German)

If you are interested in this position and you match the profile we are looking for, please apply via our online form with your CV, a copy of your ID (\+ residence permit if needed)

We look forward to receiving your application.
