Full description

Do you like working with people? Would you like to work for the Old Bombach Health Center immediately or by agreement with our care team?

Tasks

  • They accompany and look after our residents on a closed dementia department sensitively and competently in their everyday life.
  • They independently and safely carry out the planned care and support tasks and help with the care of residents in crisis situations.
  • They report observations, report objectively and document fully and comprehensibly.
  • They help with activating, reactivating and maintaining therapeutic care and care tasks and know the care and care-relevant needs and wishes of the residents and their relatives.
  • They supervise our learners during the entire apprenticeship period together with the main vocational trainers and those responsible for vocational training.

Profile

  • Responsible, reliable and exact working methods
  • Training as a health specialist EFZ with SRC recognition for diplomas from abroad
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Good IT knowledge and a heart for our residents

We offer

Enjoy an exciting field of work in a professional and team-oriented working environment with a du-culture throughout the company. The human being is at our center. That is why we support the reconciliation of private and professional life, different working models make it possible. In this position, you can actively participate in the choice of workload and service hours.

As part of the city of Zurich and due to the size of our organization, we stand for attractive development opportunities, progressive employment conditions and excellent social benefits.

Depending on the experience, the annual salary for this function is between 70'300.- and 93'936.- (100%).

About us

The Health Centers for the Age of the City of Zurich is one of the largest geriatric institutions in Switzerland. We are the leading service provider in the age and care sector - with competence, heart and a comprehensive offer from a single source.

The health center for the old Bombach in Höngg in district 10 combines various care and care offers under one roof and is characterized by many years of experience in age medicine. The comfortable, modern and bright rooms offer the residents a comfortable and safe home with inpatient care. Here you work together with 270 colleagues.
