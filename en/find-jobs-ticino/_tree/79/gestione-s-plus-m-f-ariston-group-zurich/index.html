Full description

ELCO offers premium heating solutions with first-class service designed to meet the needs of our customers through the entire life cycle of products and heating systems. The focus is on excellent consulting as well as convincing products and heating systems. ELCO has been a leader in burner, condensing and solar technology for decades and has 1.7 million installed heating systems throughout Europe. Since 1928, ELCO has been a leading European brand offering intelligent solutions and technologies tailored to the needs of private and commercial projects, ensuring maximum energy savings.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a responsible, independent and well-organized personality for our S-Plus warehouse in Zurich.

STORAGE MANAGEMENT S-PLUS (M/F)

Their tasks

With us, you can expect a versatile area of responsibility around our service logistics:

  • Support of our S-Plus regional warehouses in Zurich, Wädenswil and Altendorf SZ
  • Ensuring optimal storage organisation, stock management and order and cleanliness
  • Handling of incoming goods, outgoing goods and dispatch as well as support of service technicians in the procurement of spare parts
  • Coordination and implementation of annual inventories as well as revisions and calibrations of exhaust gas measuring instruments
  • Close cooperation and interface function between service logistics, service technicians and region

For our joint success story, bring with you

  • Experience in warehouse management or logistics, technical training beneficial
  • Knowledge of German at native level
  • Cat. B, ideally Cat. BE
  • Independent, structured and proactive working method with strong organisational skills
  • Communication strength and teamwork
  • Good MS-Office knowledge is beneficial
