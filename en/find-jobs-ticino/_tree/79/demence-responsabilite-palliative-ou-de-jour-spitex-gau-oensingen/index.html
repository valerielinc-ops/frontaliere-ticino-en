Full description

At Spitex Gäu, with around 120 employees, we see people – not just patients. In our family environment in Oensingen, we attach importance to sufficient time for individual care and thus provide professional and comprehensive care at home. Would you also like to support and accompany people with a meaningful task? We welcome applications from people of all genders, backgrounds and age groups – diversity makes us stronger! As a result of succession planning, further development and growth, we are looking for open, heartfelt and committed personalities for our constantly growing team. Dementia, palliative or daytime responsibility

Your tasks

  • Responsibility: You take care of a holistic, qualified and resource-oriented care and support of our customers and their relatives. You lead and control the entire care process and coordinate the interdisciplinary cooperation.
  • Stay flexible: You support, promote and accompany employees in everyday care, which is varied and brings exciting new challenges every day
  • Being strong together: You work in a dynamic environment where we keep each other’s backs free and bring your experience and ideas to bear

This is what you bring with you

  • Your foundation: Completed training as a nurse HF with appropriate experience and ideally further education or the willingness to train in the appropriate areas
  • Your know-how: Independent, resilient, thinking and emphatic personality with a high degree of professional and social competence. Category B driving licence is a must
  • Your vibe: You are a real team player, trustful and helpful with a positive attitude and keep your smile even in lively situations

This is what we offer you

  • A great team: With us you are not a number. We laugh together, support ourselves unconditionally and live a transparent culture at eye level
  • Work-life balance to measure : We can openly discuss your working hours – we find a model that fits your life!
  • Free space: An independent task in which you can actively participate and develop individually
  • Modern working: a contemporary infrastructure, attractive benefits and employment conditions according to cantonal guidelines

Ready for the next step? We look forward to meeting you! For questions please contact Rebekka Keller or Michaela Strub, co-team leaders specialist care or Romy Allemann, team leader dementia and palliative care (062 544 71 60).
