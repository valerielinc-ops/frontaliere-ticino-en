Full description

The Office for Labour Graubünden currently employs around 135 people in six regions in the canton. In addition to the public employment agency (RAV), we organize labour market measures for job seekers and run the cantonal unemployment fund. In addition, we perform various tasks in the field of labour market and occupational safety.

We are looking for an unemployment compensation specialist (80-100%) for the Unemployment Fund Graubünden as of 1 February 2027 or by agreement.

Tasks

  • Examination and processing of claims for unemployment compensation, including careful consideration of
  • Eligibility and calculation of benefits
  • Correct and timely payment of insurance benefits
  • Competent and service-oriented telephone, written and, if necessary, personal advice from insured persons on their claims, payment dates and other relevant questions
  • Coordination and exchange with other social insurers and with other departments of the Office (in particular RAV)

Requirements

  • Completed commercial training or equivalent qualification, ideally with experience in the social insurance sector (Federal certificate for social insurance professionals of advantage)
  • Flair for numbers and a precise, structured working method
  • Very good IT knowledge, especially in the use of MS Office
  • Friendly and professional appearance as well as the ability to explain complex issues easily and comprehensibly
  • Responsible, resilient and self-employed personality
  • Flexibility and reliability as well as teamwork
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Good knowledge of several cantonal languages is advantageous
