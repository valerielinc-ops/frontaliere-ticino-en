Full description

The Common Unit ORP-CSR, attached to the Regional Employment Office (ORP) of the Western Lausanne area, aims to take care of beneficiaries of the Insertion Income (RI) according to modalities that combine the skills of social workers from the Regional Social Center (CSR) and personnel advisors from the ORP. In this context, you organize and support the activity of the collaborators of the Common Unit. You accompany the creation of a professional practice based on the actions of two complementary professions and impact the deployment of innovative practices in the field of socio-professional integration. You ensure the development of the skills of the collaborators in your unit while controlling the quality of the service provided to the beneficiaries and compliance with the legal framework. You support the head of the ORP and the head of the CSR and contribute to the quality of the relationships between the ORP and the CSR. You follow and advise each beneficiary with the aim of optimizing job placement. You select appropriate placement strategies for each individual and have specific measures to develop their employability.

  • Lead and support the activity of the collaborators of the Common Unit;
  • Organize, optimize, and control the quality of the work of the Common Unit;
  • Ensure the development of the skills of the collaborators of the Common Unit;
  • Support the head of the ORP as well as the heads of the CSR and contribute to the quality of the relationships between the ORP and the CSR;
  • Ensure an activity as an ORP personnel advisor and substitute for the head of the office in managing the office. Key skills
  • Ability to lead and motivate a team of collaborators by adopting a participative leadership style. You value individual contributions while promoting a strong team spirit.
  • You demonstrate flexibility and adaptability in the face of changes while inspiring these qualities within the team.
  • Your listening skills and client orientation make you effective in individual interviews.
  • You are stress-resistant, rigorous, and precise in executing tasks.
  • You know how to support change initiatives and demonstrate an ability to understand and promote organizational changes. You actively contribute to the implementation of new practices and procedures.
  • Your relational and social skills serve a results-oriented approach. For more information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Ms. Teodora Ciobanu, HR advisor, at 021 316 61 93.
  • Federal diploma as an HR specialist, social insurance specialist, or personnel advisor.
  • Several years of experience in the fields of consulting and placement, socio-professional reintegration, HR, and management.
  • You have been made aware of the legal aspects of HR monitoring in your experiences.
  • You have a good knowledge of the job market and the economic fabric of Vaud. Position within the Vaud Cantonal Administration (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Apply online on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
