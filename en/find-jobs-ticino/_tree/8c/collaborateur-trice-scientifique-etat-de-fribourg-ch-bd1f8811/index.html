Who are we?

The aim of the Public Health Service (PHS) is to ensure the promotion, maintenance and recovery of population health. Health must be accessible to all at a bearable cost. The PHC is the centre of competence for all matters related to the broad themes of public health. It identifies the health needs of the population, ensures adequate supply and ensures the quality of services. He is also actively involved in the reflections on the evolution of the health care system in Switzerland, with its partners from the cantons and the Confederation.

Your Mission

As project leader, you are contributing to the implementation of the cantonal strategy in the area of vital and non-vital health emergencies.

Within the health emergency strategy and coordination cluster, you transform the strategic orientations into concrete projects and actions, in collaboration with the various actors involved.

Your main tasks will include

  • Pilot projects in the field of health emergencies
  • Analyze and assess population needs and changing care needs
  • Preparing reports and decision support documents
  • Design and implement communication and information measures
  • Supporting and monitoring the implementation of the various measures
  • To carry out organisational and administrative tasks related to the projects and activities of the Centre

Profile desired

  • University or equivalent training in health, public health, public administration or management
  • Confirmed experience in project management, ideally in health or public policy
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss health system and its main players
  • Excellent analytical, synthesis and writing capabilities
  • Ability to federate and advance complex projects
  • Initiative, autonomy and organizational sense
  • Use of IT tools and data processing
  • Excellent command of the French or German language with good knowledge of the partner language (B1-B2)
