Full description

Are you passionate about fresh foods, especially meat and fish? As a specialist salesperson* in a service counter, you can live out your craftsmanship and sales talent. You also inspire our customers with your tips and tricks. Apply now and become part of our team!

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Basel, Laufen, Bahnhofstrasse 4, 4242 Laufen

Important information

Working days/times: Monday - Saturday, between 06:00 - 19:30 We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Advising and serving our customers at the control counter (meat and fish) is one of your main tasks.
  • You prepare meat and fish for service sales and present them appealingly. You make sure that all hygiene regulations are adhered to.
  • You make sure that our goods are available at the control counter in the required quantity (goods disposition).
  • You regularly check the goods of the counter, fill them independently and sort them.
  • You are responsible for the freshness and shelf life of our goods and always check them at regular intervals.

Requirements

  • First professional experience: Meat / Fish / Charcuterie
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): as butcher, meat specialist vendor or cook
  • You are a customer-oriented personality and enjoy dealing with our customers.
  • You have a great skill in planning and organizing tasks.
  • You work reliably and have a high quality awareness.
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension fund with above-average performance: 8.5%, AG share 17%
  • Social benefits: In the event of illness, the salary for two years is 100%, in the event of accident we are insured SUVA
  • Cumulus points: Additional Cumulus points for employees
  • Club School Migros: cost share of 70% in club school courses up to max. CHF 1,000 per year
  • Leisure and cultural activities: various discounted or free cultural activities
  • Sports and fitness offers: cost share of CHF 500.- to a fitness subscription per year
  • Holidays: 5-7 weeks holidays per year depending on age/service age

Contatto
