The Position You shape the future of healthcare at the interface between German and European politics. As EU and International Affairs Lead (m/f/d) in the External Affairs team of Roche Deutschland and the global unit You are Roche’s strength with the European policy agenda. Together with a highly motivated, networked team, you will drive forward policymaking in order to sustainably secure the welcome culture for life-changing innovations.

This is what awaits you As a central link between our German organizational units and the External Affairs International (EAI) team, Strategic interests at EU and international level. After a thorough familiarization You have the following core tasks:

  • You will develop and implement powerful EU policy and advocacy strategies in close coordination with the German and international public affairs teams.
  • You design data-driven arguments and narratives based on Roche’s value chain in Germany (R&D, production, logistics).
  • You represent Roche’s positions in European and international debates (e.g. EU Pharma Legislation, IP protection, EU buses).
  • You build strong relationships with decision-makers, especially members of the European Parliament and representatives of federal ministries.
  • You will analyze business-relevant EU legislative procedures and translate them into concrete co-creation activities for the German organization.
  • You will create strategic briefings for Roche’s Senior Leadership and coordinate high-level interactions with European political actors.
  • You ensure full compliance with all compliance requirements (EU Transparency Register, German Lobby Register Act, FSA Code, Roche Code of Conduct).

You bring that with you For this demanding position, we are looking for a forward-looking personality with a strong drive to shape and sound political expertise. You have:

  • A successfully completed university degree (master or comparable) in politics, business, law or natural sciences.
  • Several years of proven professional experience in strategic policy consulting, government affairs or in public European institutions. You have already established a network with the relevant stakeholders.
  • In-depth knowledge of the legislative processes and functioning of the EU institutions as well as German health policy.
  • Confident knowledge of German and English in words and writing for convincing communication at management and political level.
  • Practical experience in the use of AI tools and digital applications to optimize analysis and work processes.
