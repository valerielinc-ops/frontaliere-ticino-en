Rheumatology and immunology

Job: By immediate or by agreement, for an indefinite period

Tasks

  • Administrative organisation of infusions
  • Creating/performing infusions
  • Vein punctures, subcutaneous injections
  • Vaccinations, implementation / Registration in the vaccination certificate / after doctor's indication
  • Subcutaneous administration of medication to patients
  • Filling / cleaning the syringe wagons / Wiegand, cleaning the examination beds in the examination rooms

Requirements

  • You are a flexible and service-oriented person and work reliably, quickly and independently, working together in a team makes you happy
