Full description

The RTS offers an induction course in the research profession for people in training, on the radio program "We talk about it" (RTS-First). An opportunity to discover the various facets of this investigative profession.

This course is intended exclusively for students enrolled in a certification course (Bachelor, Master or equivalent), for which the course is compulsory, validated by ECTS credits and supervised by a tripartite agreement.

Your Mission

You are involved in managing public questions and testimonials for interactive live programming, you are conducting various information searches with specialists, mainly by telephone, online and also in the field. You will search for specialists, witnesses and useful addresses related to the themes to be addressed in the program. You are also preparing survey files for the production of programs and syntheses for journalists, and finally, you are proposing program topics in the area of services and consumption.

Your profile

  • Be in training;
  • Very good general culture and curiosity;
  • Interest in investigation, investigation and radio material;
  • Interest in services and consumption;
  • Good knowledge of Swiss institutions;
  • Good knowledge of social networks and Internet research;
  • Mastery of office applications (e.g. Teams, MS Office);
  • Knowledge of German and English (read and spoken).

What we offer you

  • A motivating work environment in a captivating environment.
  • An immersion in a motivated and welded team.
  • An opportunity to discover a public service audiovisual media.
