Full description

  • After a gradual introduction and training, take on a central role in the operation of our fighter jets
  • F/A-18 repair, maintain and maintain – with later specialization on the most modern fighter jet generation F-35
  • Inspect and maintain engines as well as safety-relevant systems – from engine changes to stationary and test runs to controls of cable catch systems

  • Fascination for aviation – aircraft experience is not a must
  • Enjoy teamwork in our well-rehearsed small team as well as reliability and analytical thinking
  • Willingness to provide technical support for training abroad (2 x 2-3 weeks per year) as well as punctual piketting or night work (seasonal and individual weeks per year)
  • Swiss nationality and unobjectionable certificate of good repute (personal security examination)
