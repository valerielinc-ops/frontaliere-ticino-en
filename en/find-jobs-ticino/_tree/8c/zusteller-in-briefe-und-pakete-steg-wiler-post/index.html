Full description

Do you like to get up early and seek a physically challenging activity? Then you will fit perfectly with us! With you, dreams come unharmed and punctual.

You Can Make That Do

  • Early in the morning (approx. 06.00) you load the delivery vehicles together with your team and prepare your delivery.
  • Through your work you preface the street image of Steg or Wiler (Lötschen) and embody the values of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the punctual and flawless delivery of parcels and letters in your area.
  • On your tour you answer questions from our customers and contribute to customer satisfaction.
  • You also work on Saturdays, with the services being shared in the team.

You bring this with you

  • With your physical fitness you load and easily lift packages up to 30 kg.
  • You have the guide cards of categories A1 and B. If you do not have the A1 certificate yet, you will be willing to purchase it later.
  • You appreciate the work in the team but also like to be on the road independently and feel comfortable with large vehicles in road traffic.
  • You're reliable and you work on a long-term basis.
  • You communicate orally and in writing very well in German and have a well-versed handling of digital devices.
