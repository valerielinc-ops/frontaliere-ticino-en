Entry into office: 1 February 2027 Participation rate: 80% - 100% The Clinique romande de réadaptation (CRR) is a leading player in the field of specialized rehabilitation in Switzerland. A true pillar of the integrated care chain, it works closely with university hospitals and specialized centres to provide coordinated, innovative and high-quality care, focusing on functional recovery and the sustainable reintegration of patients. In order to strengthen our healthcare team within our para/tetraplegic rehabilitation sector, we are looking for: Nurse HES – ES (para/tetraplegic rehabilitation sector)

  • Activity rate: 80–100%
  • Place of work: Sion (VS)
  • Type of contract: CDI
  • Entry into office: 01.02.2027 Mission As a HES–ES nurse, you ensure specialized care for patients with spinal cord injuries, including paraplegia and tetraplegia, requiring complex care and individualized support in their rehabilitation journey. You actively contribute to the prevention of complications, the development of autonomy and the improvement of the quality of life of patients, in an interdisciplinary approach and focused on their rehabilitation objectives. As such, you:
  • Develop, implement and evaluate individualized care projects, taking into account the specific needs of spinal cord injuries.
  • Ensure specialized technical and relational nursing, including in the areas of vesico-sphincterian and intestinal management, prevention of pressure damage and monitoring of autonomous functions.
  • Identify, analyze and anticipate the risks of complications specific to spinal cord injuries, ensuring rigorous clinical surveillance and appropriate management.
  • Support patients in developing self-care skills by promoting self-reliance, active participation and reintegration into their living environment.
  • Coordinate and oversee the care provided by ASSCs in accordance with quality, safety and professional standards.
  • Participate actively in interdisciplinary clinical assessments and decisions, in collaboration with the medical profession, therapists and other professionals involved in rehabilitation.
  • Contribute to the accompaniment and therapeutic education of patients and their loved ones, taking into account the physical, psychological and social dimensions.
  • Participate in the supervision of learners, the integration of new employees and the continuous development of professional practices. Your profile
  • Bachelor of nursing HES, ES degree or equivalent recognized (recognition Swiss Red Cross for foreign degrees).
  • Hospital experience and strong interest in specialized rehabilitation and management of patients with spinal cord injuries.
  • Ability to provide complex care, to exercise relevant clinical judgment and to adapt its interventions to changes in patient health.
  • Strong sense of responsibility, professional autonomy, rigour and ability to define priorities in a demanding care environment.
  • Strong relational and psychosocial skills, allowing to establish a professional therapeutic relationship, respectful and adapted to situations of vulnerability and major change in the life course.
  • Team spirit, interdisciplinary collaborative capacity and ability to actively contribute to constructive work dynamics.
  • Stress resistance, emotional stability, and ability to retreat from complex clinical and human situations.
  • Openness to intervene on an ad hoc basis in other units in order to promote continuity of care and mutual assistance between teams.
  • Motivation to develop specific expertise in the field of para/tetraplegia and to engage in long-term patient support.
  • Mastery of French in writing and oral (minimum B2 level). Our offer
  • Support patients on the long term: Follow patient progress and contribute directly to their return to autonomy.
  • Enriching Environment: Collaborate with experts from various fields in an interdisciplinary approach.
  • Continuing training: Access a wide range of training courses to strengthen your skills and develop your career.
  • Dynamic team: Work in a caring environment where efforts are recognized and valued.
  • Benefits: High social benefits, many social benefits, free park space, and much more. Modalities
