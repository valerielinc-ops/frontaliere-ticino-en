Full description

Banking Operations is a strategic Avaloq service line providing Banking Operations outsourcing services to banks. We are organized as a global Business Area, running state-of-the art Backoffice services with industry leading automation, quality, and risk standards. We are experts in providing a true value add to our clients and thus, always look to add creative and client-oriented personalities with drive and passion to our team. The Corporate Actions Pre-Processing Team manage events coming from the market affecting the characteristic, the nature, or the value of a security. These events can be mandatory, mandatory with options or voluntary. The activity includes check of incoming messages and ending events, creation of the notification of the Corporate Actions and handling inquiries from clients. Your key tasks

  • Set up of Mandatory events
  • Set up and creation of notifications for Voluntary and Mandatory with choice events
  • Check of incoming swift messages
  • Handling of enquiries

Qualifiche

  • Economics studies, preferably at university level and with a focus in Finance
  • Proactive approach towards a highly challenging, fast-paced and complex environment
  • Good communication and organization skills
  • Good team player and positive attitude
  • Flexibility and willingness to learn
  • Fluent in English

It would be a real bonus if you have

  • Knowledge of Corporate Actions (even if only theoretical)
  • Knowledge of Avaloq Core Banking System
  • Knowledge of additional languages (especially German or French)
