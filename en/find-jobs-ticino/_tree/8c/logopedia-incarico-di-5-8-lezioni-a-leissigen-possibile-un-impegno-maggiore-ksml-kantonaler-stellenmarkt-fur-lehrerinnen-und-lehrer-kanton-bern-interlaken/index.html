Full description

Portrait The special lessons Jungfrauregion SJR warmly welcomes you. Our team is looking forward to meeting you! We are speech therapists, psychomotor therapists and school curative pedagogues under internal management. Some of us have been working on this established team for many years. All of us work both at a central main location but also share responsibility for the smaller locations / schoolhouses in the SJR area. This is possible on site and in part also centralized. Because we are exclusively committed to the SJR team, internal schoolhouse meetings, work groups etc. are omitted or are absolutely voluntary. Therefore, the daily mail flood is also limited. All the greater, however, is the mutual support in their own specialist teams.

Tasks

Independent clarification of the therapy needs of individual students. Self-employment in the assigned schools. Working days are freely selectable. Therapy and accompaniment mostly in children of cycle 1 and 2 (kindergarten up to 4th grade) at one to two SJR site communities. Cooperation with teachers, parents and specialists as well as interdisciplinary cooperation in the SJR team (sessions in Interlaken). No attendance at schoolhouse meetings required.

Requirements

Recognized Diploma or Bachelor in Speech Medicine. A car is not absolutely necessary, as the speech pedia locations are also easily accessible with public transport.

We offer

Permanent employment. If desired, the position in Leissigen can be supplemented with 4 L. in Habkern, 5 L. in Gündlischwand-Lütschental or 8 L. in Lauterbrunnen. Careful introduction to SJR structures and workplaces. Good support from the specialist team Speech Medicine and the management of the SJR. Mentorship for newcomers possible. Very autonomous and independent working method possible. Speech pedia rooms with a rich selection of materials are available as well as a generous budget for new purchases as desired. The total fees can be arbitrarily divided by the specialist speech therapy team by means of internal agreements. Applications are processed on an ongoing basis and interviews take place promptly.
