Full description

UniBE is renowned. With us, you benefit from the good reputation of a long-established institution throughout the world.

What you can look forward to In our stables, clinic facilities and office spaces at the Bern Farm Animal Clinic, we offer an environment that enables modern research. In collaboration with university institutions that support innovation and product development, a device is currently being developed that is intended to mechanically protect the calf from respiratory pathogens. It is currently being tested on a limited number of calves (severity of the animal experiment: low, 0 of 3; i.e. without burden). In a further step, namely the one to be undertaken as part of this work, the product's suitability for practical use will be consolidated.

Your tasks Transporting calves from practice farms in the wider area around Bern to the clinic, providing veterinary care for the calves with the support of the doctors on duty. Recording data using established methods from bacteriology, ultrasonography and clinical observation. The calves are subsequently taken to fattening farms. Training by the staff member currently working on the project is possible. The position is not yet funded. For this purpose, a corresponding application must be submitted in advance -without any guarantee of success-. After extensive review, it has been established that the following funding schemes are best suited. UniBE Venture Fellowship Bridge Proof of Concept You are the applicant. You alone are the recipient of the grant. You can count on comprehensive support in preparing it. Thus, the workload remains very (!) manageable. The application will be submitted during the month of November. For questions about the precise research subject, which is confidential, an in-person meeting or online meeting can be arranged.

Requirements

The requirements for your background are moderately specific

  • Open to those with a master's, doctoral or medical degree – as well as students whose degree will be completed within the next six months.
  • You have research experience as part of a bachelor's or master's degree that was completed less than 4 years ago, or
  • You will complete your doctorate within 6 months at the latest, or
  • You completed your doctorate less than 4 years ago.

Good knowledge of German or French is an advantage. Central location in the heart of Bern Application and contact With us, you create value. For yourself and society.

Project description Respiratory diseases in fattening calves are of great importance against the backdrop of animal welfare, antibiotic use and resistance, as well as economic considerations. It is urgently necessary to develop and implement measures that lead to improvements in practice. This project is intended to build on an ongoing project in which the calves are protected from respiratory pathogens during transport from the fattening farm. What you can look forward to
