Full description

Who you are

As a facility employee, you actively contribute to providing our employees, visitors and customers with a safe, clean, sustainable and functional environment. Facility services cover both the technical maintenance of infrastructure facilities and topics such as control of cleaning, waste management, pest control, active snow clearance, as well as energy, emissions and water management.

  • You will bring a completed vocational training in the technical or mechanical field (electric, specialist maintenance, HVAC, etc.) – ideally combined with pleasure and security in administrative tasks.
  • You have a great interest in the aspects of a building and the associated facilities.
  • You are at least fundamentally familiar with the MS Office applications (Word, PowerPoint, Excel).
  • You have good written and oral communication skills in the German language and are always able to assert yourself in security-relevant situations.
  • You are able to act calmly, thoughtfully and responsibly in stress and event situations (e.g. fire detection, water damage).
  • You support the service-oriented approach of the Facility Management team through your social skills and your polite, open and respectful nature.
  • You don’t mind working in the pikettdienst, you have to be able to work within 60 minutes.
  • You are basically available from Mo.-Sa. and in shifts from 07:00 - 21:00 (1-2 Saturdays a month)
  • Occasional work on a lift does not matter to you

You are responsible for

  • Support of the manager, in administrative tasks and coordination of orders.
  • Responsible for the daily execution of facility management routines on site (inspection, inspection and maintenance, repairs, improvements)
  • Monitoring and coordination of work performed by external FM suppliers, in accordance with IKEA requirements and local regulations.
  • Recording and reporting of findings, risks or damage.
  • Support in cost control through simple and cost-conscious work.
  • Implementation of maintenance activities and smaller projects either independently or in a team
  • Total workload: maintenance activities approximately 70% / administrative work approximately 30%
