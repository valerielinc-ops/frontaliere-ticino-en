Location: Hirslanden Clinic St. Anna | Lucerne

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 74950

Do you love personal contact with guests, have a feeling for first-class service and like to take on responsibility? Then you are right with us.

At Klinik St. Anna you combine your experience from the upscale hotel industry with a meaningful task in everyday hospital life. Together with your team, you ensure that patients and visitors feel welcome and well cared for from the first moment.

YOUR TASKS

  • You are jointly responsible for a smooth and professional process at the main reception of the St. Anna Clinic
  • You support the management of Réception & Guest Relations in the professional and personnel management of around 13 employees and take over the representation during absences
  • You work regularly in the services at the reception and are present in the day-to-day operations
  • You support the management of Réception & Guest Relations in team organization, planning and implementation of team meetings and projects
  • You are actively involved in the continuous development of the structure and process organization of the Réception & Guest Relations department
  • You take care of the management of the car park and the external parking spaces

Your profile

  • You have leadership and professional experience in the upscale 4- or 5-star hotel industry. Experience in the hospital or clinic environment is an advantage
  • You are a front-oriented, conceptually strong, contact- and communication-competent personality and have a commercial education as well as min. 3 years of professional experience
  • Even in hectic situations, you stay calm, friendly and solution-oriented
  • You have good oral and written English skills as well as knowledge in mind. another national language. Italian is an advantage
  • You bring in-depth MS Office knowledge and are open to getting to know more programs

Our offer

  • You can expect a family environment where mutual support and team spirit count
  • We value respectful cooperation and good cooperation
  • An attractive mobility concept and additional financial benefits for parents
  • Up to 4 extra free days per year thanks to paid changing time
  • For only CHF 5.00 a day you are provided with a meal in our varied staff restaurant as well as coffee, tea, mineral and seasonal fruits.
  • Further employment conditions and offers can be found here

Ready for the next step?

If you have any questions, Sylvia Balthasar, Head of Reception and Guest Relations, under T or Rahel Herzog, HR recruitment partner, under T are happy to help you.

For data protection reasons, we look forward to your online application.
