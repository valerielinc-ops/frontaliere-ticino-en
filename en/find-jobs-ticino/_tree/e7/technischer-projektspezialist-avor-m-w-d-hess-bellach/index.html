Technical project specialist AVOR (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company.

To strengthen our team in the work preparation of the Bus & Trolley division, we are looking for a motivated and qualified personality as a project specialist. You want to actively participate in the development of modern buses and are ready for the next career step? As a project specialist AVOR, you are the connecting element between sales, technology, purchasing, logistics and production. They make a decisive contribution to ensuring that our projects are implemented smoothly, on time and efficiently. Their tasks

  • Overall responsibility for your projects as well as coordination between project management, sales, technology, purchasing, assembly and logistics
  • Planning, control and monitoring of production orders – including on-schedule and volume-based loading
  • Implementation of pre- and post-calculations and completion of production contracts
  • Scheduling and implementation of deadline changes as well as amendments for ongoing projects
  • Continuous optimization of existing processes in work preparation and related areas
  • Collection and maintenance of articles and further master data in the ERP system
  • Periodic review of default and distribution times
  • Support for make-or-buy analyses and assembly procurement in cooperation with Supply Chain Management

Our requirements

  • Basic technical training completed
  • Further education as a process expert is advantageous
  • Analytical, structured thinking skills as well as in-depth knowledge in the vehicle or machine industry
  • Several years of professional experience in production planning or in a comparable environment
  • High customer orientation, teamwork and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Experience in project management and flow production is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German and secure communication in English
  • Good MS Office knowledge and experience with ERP systems (ideally Dynamics AX / D365)

We offer them

  • Exciting, varied tasks in an innovative, successful family business
  • Permanent employment with attractive employment conditions and flexible working hours
  • Design freedom: Bring your ideas and make a difference!
  • Active support for your professional and personal development
  • Collaborative and supportive team environment

If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
