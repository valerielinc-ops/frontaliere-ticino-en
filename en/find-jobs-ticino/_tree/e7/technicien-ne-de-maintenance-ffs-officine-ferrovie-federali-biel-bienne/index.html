What awaits you.

Diverse missions where you will be able to make available all your railway knowledge and know-how in the strategies for making trains available. We offer a dynamic working environment in which you can develop your skills and thrive professionally. You see irregular schedules and teamwork as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. At home, working at night and on weekends is entitled to extra pay, e.g., for example: CHF 16.– an hour for Sunday work. Time increases of 10 to 30% are granted for night work, which gives you more free time. To find out about benefits, such as free GA and reduced-cost overseas travel (for family as well), visit our website under Benefits. What you can move.

  • As a maintenance technician, you perform maintenance and inspection work on rail vehicles according to technical instructions.
  • You replace worn components and perform adjustment work.
  • You field of activity extends to the preparation of diagnoses concerning disturbances.
  • You carry out many control and modification mandates according to quality standards.
  • Your systematic approach allows you to identify gaps or possible improvements in daily activities.

What you bring.

  • Flexible and customer-oriented, you like to work as a team.
  • You consider irregular schedules, sometimes falling on weekends as well, and/or team work as an asset to organize your free time flexibly.
  • You benefit from a few years of experience in the railway field.
  • You have completed a professional apprenticeship in the field of industry (CFC or equivalent) preferably automatic and automatic editor.
  • You have basic knowledge of IT tools (SAP, MS Office).
  • You have very good knowledge of French (level C2).
