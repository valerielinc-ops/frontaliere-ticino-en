Full description

You have experience. You have attitude. And you want to make a difference. You know what successful gastronomy is and that sustainable success is achieved where people enjoy working, guests feel in good hands and economic thinking meets good cooperation. At ZFV, you and a colleague will take responsibility for the restaurants of SBB staff catering and shape their further development with vision, heart and entrepreneurial flair. Your businesses and customers are located in the cantons of ZH, SO, BE, SZ UR and TI, which you will efficiently manage with your travel. In addition to all the work equipment you need for mobile working, you will have a modern office workplace at our headquarters at all times. Are you our new team member who will support us as Head of Mandate (80 - 100%) as of 01.02.2027? Our task menu

  • Developing gastronomy mandates economically, recognizing potential and opening up new opportunities.
  • Maintain trusting relationships with our clients and strengthen cooperation in the long term.
  • Keep an eye on budgets and key figures, classify developments and derive effective measures from them.
  • Accompany managers, promote employees and support teams in being successful together.
  • Translating the ZFV strategy into concrete goals and driving implementation forward with commitment with the companies.
  • Strengthen cooperation between companies and specialist departments, exploit synergies and develop processes in a meaningful way.
  • Shaping changes and projects, for example in the areas of digitization and sustainability, together with those involved. Your recipe for success
  • Several years of management experience in the catering industry and enjoy taking on responsibility for several businesses or outlets.
  • Sound knowledge of community or system catering and a good sense of customer needs and economic contexts.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, analytical skills and the ability to turn ideas into concrete results.
  • Appreciative, clear communication and joy in empowering people and bringing different perspectives together.
  • Courage to initiate change, try out new ways and act in a solution-oriented manner even in challenging situations.
  • Good IT skills, especially in MS Office.
  • Stylish knowledge of German
  • Knowledge of French or English is a plus. Effective together – that’s the ZFV. For us, it’s not just the result that counts, but also the path to get there. Our leadership motto “We make others successful” shapes the collaboration and understanding of leadership in the ZFV. We give trust, communicate openly and create space for responsibility, development and effective action. With us you will find an environment in which your experience counts, your perspective is welcome and you can make a difference together with others. Because we are convinced that good results arise when people contribute their strengths and grow together. We create teams that support each other and in which you are encouraged. We work together and focus on everything that is good. We attach great importance to wage transparency and equal pay. That's why we communicate an annual salary for this position with a 100% workload, an annual salary between CHF 110,000 and CHF 115,000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary).
