Full description

Fixed Placement Agency and Temporary Work We are recruiting a mason chest to strengthen the teams of a recognized construction company in Geneva. You will intervene on sites where your technical know-how, versatility and rigour will be fully valued. Autonomous and vigilant, you carry out your missions seriously and accurately. Your missions

  • Read and interpret formwork and reinforced concrete plans
  • Organize and prepare the work site and work areas
  • Accomplish traditional wood and metal formwork
  • Formwork for walls, slabs and pillars
  • Decreasing the works and retouching if necessary
  • Lock anchor holes and mounting openings
  • Run different types of joints
  • Meet quality, safety and on-site deadlines

Your profile

  • Completed training in masonry
  • Minimum 3 years of experience as a mason
  • Mastery of formwork, railing and concrete techniques
  • Versatile, rigorous and with good physical resistance
  • Ability to work as a team
  • High adaptability

We offer you

  • Custom monitoring
  • A dynamic working environment
  • Various missions
  • Development and continuing training prospects
  • Compensation in line with industry and experience
