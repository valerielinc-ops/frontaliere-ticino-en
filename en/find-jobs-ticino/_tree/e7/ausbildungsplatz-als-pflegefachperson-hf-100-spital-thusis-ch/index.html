Full description

As a medical competence centre, the Stiftung Gesundheit Mittelbünden is responsible for the care of the population and guests of the region. A total of 300 employees provide personal and prudent care for patients and clients.

Are you looking for a responsible job? Do you like contact with people? Do you enjoy supporting people in their everyday life and accompanying them in the care process? Then the profession nurse HF is just right for you.

As of September 2026, we offer training places with direct employment for two or three years of full-time study in the hospital and in the Spitex as:

Task area

• Caring for and supporting physically and mentally ill people in the activities of daily life.

  • Health promotion and prevention of diseases.
  • Take over the entire care process of patients and clients as well as accompanying the relatives.

Requirements

• Completion of basic vocational training EFZ, secondary school or high school/vocational school.

  • Mental and physical resilience and flexibility.
  • Responsibility, decision-making and reflection.

We offer

• a varied and responsible activity in a family business.

  • Support and support by a competent specialist and training team.
  • Contemporary employment and working conditions with at least 25 days holidays.
  • a modern infrastructure as well as numerous offers and discounts in the leisure sector.
