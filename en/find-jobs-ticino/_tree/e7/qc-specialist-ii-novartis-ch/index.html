Job Description Summary #LiHybrid Location: Schweizerhalle, Switzerland. Novartis does not offer relocation support for this position: please apply only if this position is accessible to you.

At the Novartis manufacturing site in Schweizerhalle, which specializes in the production of oligonucleotide-based medicines, we are seeking a motivated professional to strengthen our Laboratory Equipment Qualification team. In this temporary role, you will support automation, artificial intelligence (AI), and laboratory equipment qualification activities, helping to ensure that equipment, systems, and tools perform as intended and comply with established standards and Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) requirements.

If you are a hands-on problem solver with a strong commitment to quality and continuous improvement, this is an opportunity to make a meaningful contribution from day one.

We value curious, ambitious, and collaborative individuals. In an inspiring environment, you will help shape the future of Quality Control (QC) in Schweizerhalle by contributing to innovative solutions and well-informed, forward-looking decisions.

Job Description

Major Accountabilities

  • Coordinate and oversee the execution of change requests.
  • Author and review qualification documentation in accordance with established GMP procedures.
  • Support improvements to laboratory automation systems and contribute to AI-related initiatives.
  • Coordinate qualification activities with laboratory teams and relevant stakeholders.

Essential requirements

  • Master’s degree in computer science, engineering, life sciences, or a related discipline with a strong technological focus.
  • Good understanding of chemical laboratory operations and workflows.
  • Independent, structured working style combined with proactive and solution-oriented thinking.
  • Experience in programming, automation, or the implementation of digital solutions.
  • Fluent in English and German.

Desirable requirements

  • Previous experience working in a GMP-regulated environment.
  • PhD Degree.

We offer a 2-year temporary contract for this role.
