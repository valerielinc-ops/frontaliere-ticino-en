Full description

Elektro Hunziker AG with around 100 employees is one of the leading suppliers for electrical installations, computer science & telematics (ICT), automation, photovoltaics, building technology as well as service and maintenance in the Thun, Spiez and Kandersteg area.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a team leader from now on or after agreement.

Their tasks

  • Independent execution of photovoltaic orders with your team
  • Preparation and termination of new PV contracts
  • Expansion and conversion of existing electrical distribution systems
  • Configuration and commissioning of inverters, energy management systems and ZEV's incl. Connection to the Internet
  • Instruction and support of customers
  • Analysis and resolution of incidents
  • Assistance in the training of learners
  • Opportunity to participate in reporting, planning and drawing up plans

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed apprenticeship in the electrical industry
  • Enjoy working with digital devices and applications
  • Motivated to work on the roof
  • Technical understanding and organisational skill
  • Independent, sociable and customer-oriented personality
  • Category B driving licence

We offer you an attractive environment with exciting challenges. In addition to interesting employment conditions, you can expect an established team and extensive development opportunities.

We look forward to receiving your application documents by e-mail to: info@elektrohunziker.ch

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
