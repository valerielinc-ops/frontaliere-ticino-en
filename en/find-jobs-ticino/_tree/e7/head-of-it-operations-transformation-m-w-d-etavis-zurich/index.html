Full description

Do you not only want to keep IT running, but really develop it together with your team? As Head of IT Operations & Transformation, you will take over the leadership of 12 employees from IT Services and IT Transformation. You ensure a stable, customer-oriented IT operation and at the same time drive the modernization of our services and transformation projects. As a link between the IT teams and IT management, you take responsibility and actively shape the further development of our IT. That's what you do.

  • You take over the personnel and professional leadership of our teams in IT services and IT transformation and develop your employees further.
  • You are responsible for resource, capacity and deployment planning and ensure clear roles, responsibilities and escalation paths.
  • You control IT services, service desk and support and ensure a high quality of service.
  • You set clear priorities in day-to-day operations and keep track of incidents and escalations.
  • You drive our IT transformation, from system launches to process optimization to standardization and automation.
  • You lead larger IT projects and ensure that new solutions and services are transferred to productive operations in a structured manner.
  • You translate strategic guidelines into concrete measures, prepare the basis for decision-making and represent IT topics to stakeholders, business units and committees.
  • In defined areas, you take over the operational and organizational deputy of the IT manager. This will make it easier for you to get started.
  • You have a higher education in computer science or business informatics or a comparable qualification.
  • You will bring several years of professional experience in IT Operations or IT Service Management.
  • You have already assumed personnel and professional leadership responsibility and experience in the development of employees.
  • You are familiar with IT transformation, service and process optimization and change management.
  • You’ll have a good knowledge of Microsoft 365, Intune, Autopilot and Entra ID, and a good understanding of cloud, hybrid and security issues.
  • You communicate fluently in German and English. Knowledge of French is a plus. This is what awaits you
  • With us, you will find a wide-ranging and challenging job with great scope for action in a successful company that is one of the industry leaders.
  • Structured and careful training is a matter of course for us – your successful start is important to us.
  • You can make a difference – you benefit from the exchange with committed colleagues and the strong team spirit.
  • We honor your contribution – by participating in the company’s success through employee shares and success participation.
  • We build on common values and live them in everyday life.
  • With us, you will experience the best of both worlds: the diverse possibilities of a global corporation combined with the advantages of an agile SME.
  • Our experienced leaders accompany you appreciatively on your way and create a professional environment for your personal development.
  • You are important to us - we offer you an open, appreciative and family environment. Did we convince you? Then apply to us today.
