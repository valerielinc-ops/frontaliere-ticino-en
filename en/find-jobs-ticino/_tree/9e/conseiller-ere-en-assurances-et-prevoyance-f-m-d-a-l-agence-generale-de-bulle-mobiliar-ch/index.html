Full description

What awaits you

Do you like human contact, personalized advice and business development? Do you want to accompany your clients in important moments of their lives by offering them tailored insurance and foresight solutions? Join our Bulle General Agency and actively participate in our development.

What you can do with us

Your main mission is to actively manage and develop your portfolio of individual and corporate clients. Of a thoughtful nature, you're always listening to your clients. You provide them with personalized advice and tailor-made solutions in the areas of insurance, foresight and also mortgage. As a social and communicative person, it is natural for you to build and maintain a network of clients. You also appreciate the open exchange of ideas within a team.

What you bring

  • A great professional ethic
  • Certificate of insurance intermediary AFA (any branches profile) or motivation to obtain it - we would accompany in this process
  • Several years of professional experience, in consulting, selling or other field in which customer contact is central
  • Excellent sense of customer relationship and business development
  • Regional network and local anchoring as a real advantage
  • Great professional ethics, with a responsible and resilient personality
  • Dynamic, positive and convincing personality of an authentic and empathic nature
  • Ability to work independently while enjoying teamwork

Working at home

A dynamic and motivated team awaits you, as well as a modern and attractive compensation system. We offer varied and demanding work, offering great autonomy, in an exciting and diverse environment.

The Insurance Supervision Act requires for the performance of this function: AFA Insurance Intermediary, All Sector Profile. Otherwise, we are happy to support you by offering you tailored in-house training.

Entry into service on 1 January 2027 or to be agreed.

What we offer

Participation in the outcome

Our success also benefits our employees. Each year we decide on the payment of a contribution to the result and its amount.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses. We contribute to continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and various discounts on cultural events.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Fribourg?

Border-zone rules and processing times vary by neighbouring country and canton, so confirm the specifics with Fribourg's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
