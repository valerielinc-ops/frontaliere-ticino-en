SBB CFF FFS You can look forward to that. Approximately 14,000 employees of passenger transport ensure that the more than 1.3 million Customers of SBB, which we transport daily, get easy access to public transport and travel from A to B both safely and punctually. The shunting staff as a central professional group makes a central contribution to this. An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you move the SBB together with your teammates, as well as a varied and paid education. You also have various internal training opportunities. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even for irregular working hours. Night and weekend work is also paid for with us - e.g. with 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night bets, you get additional time surcharges between 10 and 30%. This means that you have more free time than employees without night and weekend assignments. You can move that.

  • You prepare passenger trains for their journey, perform shunting movements on train compositions and drive various traction units with a maximum speed of 30 km/h in a limited radius of the station to the defined point of departure.
  • During the shunting movements, you monitor the road and thus ensure safety.
  • You create the driving readiness, enter data from the safety and information systems and perform brake tests.
  • If possible, repair minor defects or faults on the vehicle yourself or report them with the intended instruments of the competent body.
  • Assignments as a shunting specialist: in (Ai40) in full swing are a matter of course for you. You couple and uncouple rail vehicles, direct individual shunting movements and reset train compositions.

Here you will learn more about the job profile. You bring that with you.

  • You are attentive, focused and physically resilient to successfully handle this demanding work in 24-hour shift operations. You also like to work outside in any weather.
  • In your daily work, you operate a tablet, among other things. Dealing with modern work equipment does not bother you.
  • You are already trained:r train driver:in Kat. A40 or shunting specialist:in Ai40.
  • In German you bring in word and writing good knowledge (min. B2).
  • You have a perfect color sense (no red-green weakness) and have no entries in the penal and enforcement register.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
