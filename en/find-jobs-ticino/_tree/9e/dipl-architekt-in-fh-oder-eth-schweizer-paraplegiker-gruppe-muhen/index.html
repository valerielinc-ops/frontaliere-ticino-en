Full description

Become part of something bigger. Work with more than 2000 specialists from more than 100 professions and enable paraplegic people to live a self-determined life. On the modern campus in Nottwil, an inspiring atmosphere and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level awaits you.

As a non-profit organization we are looking for a dynamic personality for our team HFB in Muhen (AG).

are you looking for?

  • A new challenge in a different context, but still in your core competence
  • No processing of years of large-scale projects
  • Challenging tasks with high benefits for your customers
Tasks: • You take on individual construction consulting for obstacle-free construction to directly affected customers • You will work on construction projects in all phases from project planning to realisation (new and renovation - obstacle-free) • You maintain contacts with building authorities, contractors, external planners and rehabilitation clinics (specialize in paraplegia) • You conduct building consultancy for obstacle-free construction Requirements • You have completed your studies (FH or ETH) and are suitable for consulting due to your life and professional experience • You are experienced in planning, project management and management of smaller to medium-sized construction projects • You describe yourself as an organizational talent and are interested in working in a meaningful environment • You communicate with your interlocutors at eye level at all times and the needs of your customers are at the center of your actions. • Your very good language skills in German and French, good CAD knowledge (of advantage ArchiCAD) and a professional, winning way round off your profile • Ideally, you will manage projects in the linguistic border area between German-speaking Switzerland and Romandie. You are mobile and can manage an interesting area in your region We offer: You not only expect attractive employment conditions, but also numerous other advantages. Look forward to meaningful work in a modern working environment, flexible working hours and a wide range of further education opportunities. Come and experience our unique spirit. More information on parplegie.ch/benefits. Founded in 1980, the Swiss Paraplegic Association (SPV) is the national umbrella organisation of the paraplegics with around 11,000 members. It promotes, represents and coordinates the concerns of para- and quadriplegics and other members throughout Switzerland. It also supports 27 regional wheelchair clubs throughout Switzerland. The approximately 80 permanent employees and 170 employees working as freelancers as well as more than 2,000 volunteers are committed to the social, social, political and personal concerns of the members and are committed to their reintegration and to improving the quality of life. The services offered include legal advice, wheelchair sports Switzerland and leisure (promotion of top and grassroots sports, as well as our own travel agency), obstacle-free construction and social and peer advice. The SPV also publishes the member magazine Paracontact and two newsletters.
