Full description

The cooperative ZFV-Unternehmungen (ZFV) creates, in collaboration with its partners, inspiring meeting places that are enthusiastic about their hospitality and authentic experiences. Founded in 1894 by visionary Zuricho bourgeois women, the ZFV still attaches particular importance to innovation and sustainability. In more than 200 establishments in Switzerland, ZFV employees meet the needs of customers and guests. Areas of activity include restaurants, refectories, staff restaurants, hotels, mobile catering solutions, catering, bakeries and crèches. The cooperative actively contributes to shaping society and takes a stand for the environment, equal opportunities and inclusion. She lives hospitality for a society in which we all want to live. The RTS restaurant in Ecublens, operated by ZFV since January 2026, is open five days a week from 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The property has 200 seating positions and serves approximately 180 meals per day. The team consists of four people. Every day, the restaurant offers three different and varied menus, as well as a buffet of salads, desserts and snacks.
