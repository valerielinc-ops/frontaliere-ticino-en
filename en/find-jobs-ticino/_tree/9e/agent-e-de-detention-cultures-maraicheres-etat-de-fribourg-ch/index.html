Who are we?

The Friburg detention facility (EDFR) brings together prison structures in the canton of Fribourg. It has a status under public law and has legal personality. It is administratively attached to the Directorate of Security, Justice and Sport (DSJS), under the high supervision of the Council of State. The institution shall ensure the custody, accommodation, care and treatment of prisoners entrusted to it and shall contribute to the achievement of the objectives assigned to the execution of custodial sentences and measures.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. For more information, follow this link.

Your missions

  • Working in vegetable crops
  • Carrying out various work on a farm
  • Carrying out the various works of an agricultural holding
  • Provide care and constant supervision of detainees, taking into account the various detention regimes
  • Manage and organize internal and external movements
  • Collaborate on the resocialization of detained persons through positive action
  • Respecting discipline, order and security among detainees
  • Apply and enforce existing regulations and instructions

Profile desired

  • SWC market gardening, or training deemed equivalent , with several years of professional experience in the field of vegetable production
  • Being in possession of the federal patent of detention agent, an asset
  • Skills in the use of machinery and equipment, an asset
  • Good physical and mental condition, as well as flexibility and stress resistance
  • Meaning of human relations, communication and conflict management
  • Be willing to work in turns (irregular working hours including Saturday and Sunday)
  • Be willing to intervene outside working hours for cases of force majeure
  • Good knowledge of IT tools (MS Office)
  • Excerpts from prosecutions and blank criminal records
  • Mastery of the French or German language with good knowledge of the other language
