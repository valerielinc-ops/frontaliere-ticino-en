Full description

Information technology learning CIMO Compagnie industrielle de Monthey SA is a Swiss company founded in 1997 with 450 employees in 80 specialized trades. As part of its strategy for sustainable development and innovation, CIMO is active, inter alia, in the fields of energy production and distribution, waste treatment, technical maintenance, engineering, automation, hygiene and safety, environment, laboratory and occupational medicine.

  • Training plan
  • The learning takes place over 4 years and leads to the Federal Certificate of SWC Capacity.
  • Theoretical training:
  • Courses are given at the Vocational Technical and Trades School (EPTM) in Sion for one day to two days a week.
  • Practical training:
  • In-company practical training takes place at CIMO, under the responsibility of a trainer.
  • The inter-company courses (CIE) are organised by the Vocational Technical and Trades School (EPTM) in Sion in order to receive the practical bases of the trade.
  • The trade

As part of your apprenticeship, you will be required to ensure the functioning of the IT infrastructure and its services. You will install, configure, develop and monitor the network and its operation. You will implement, manage and ensure the maintenance and security of server systems and their services.

  • In order to protect these systems, you will also help secure data, manage access and implement backup protocols.
  • To carry out these tasks, you will be assisted by various programming tools and languages, as well as computer platforms.
