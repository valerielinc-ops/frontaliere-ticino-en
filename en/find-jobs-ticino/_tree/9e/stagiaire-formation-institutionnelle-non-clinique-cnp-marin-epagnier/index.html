Full description

Establishment of cantonal public law, the CNP has the task of conducting the health policy of the canton of Neuchâtel in the field of mental health, in collaboration with the partner institutions. The NPC aims to rehabilitate patients with mental disorders by adopting an integrated, community-based psychiatry model; implement public policies to promote and prevent mental health; it acts as a reference centre in the field of vocational training and psychiatry research. The Directorate of Human Resources and Relations (DRHR) is looking for one:

Internship non-clinical institutional training 80-100%

In connection with the development of the department of the Directorate of Relations and Human Resources, the non-clinical institutional training trainee participates in the daily activities of the unit, as well as in the various projects under way and contributes to strengthening the activities of the unit by providing operational and methodological support, while developing its professional skills. You are currently studying to obtain a Bachelor's degree (HEG, political sciences,...) or a Master's degree (human resources management, labour psychology,...) and wish to integrate a recognized institution specialized in mental health. You will contribute to the smooth running of the key processes by administratively supporting the non-clinical institutional training manager in their coordination, including:

Administrative support and coordination

  • Manage training requests in accordance with internal regulations, ensuring administrative follow-up (registrations, attendance lists, certificates).
  • Respond quickly and pedagogically to employees' questions about their right to training, with the support of the non-clinical institutional training specialist.
  • Process invoices and reimbursements for training.
  • Enter training data in the computer tools used.
  • Manage the generic non-clinical institutional training unit mailbox to ensure effective follow-up of applications.
  • To contribute to the logistics of training (agenda, preparation of rooms, materials, snacks).
  • Support the organisation of events related to the sector (hosting sessions, Future day of all kinds, etc.).

Continuous improvement of training

  • Collaborate in the development of non-clinical institutional training (supports, content, pedagogy).
  • Co-host development training and seminars.

Development of an institutional HR culture

  • Analyse training indicators (evaluations, participation rates).
  • Identify and implement improvement actions (literature reviews, benchmarks).
  • Launch and pilot projects to enhance the impact of training.

Process optimization

  • Actively contribute to continuous improvement of unity tools and processes.
  • Conduct a key project under the supervision of the non-clinical institutional training manager.

We are looking for a candidate with a good command of the following skills:

  • Good oral and written communication in French.
  • Knowledge of project management methodology.
  • Writing skills and understanding of processes and procedures.
  • Knowledge of MS Office tools (Word, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint).
  • Reception techniques and communication with various stakeholders.
  • Strict adherence to professional and functional secrecy in accordance with CTC requirements.

The candidate must demonstrate

  • Organization, focus and effectiveness.
  • Adaptability and openness to changes in methods.
  • Creativity and capacity for innovation.
  • Spirit of analysis and synthesis.
