Location: Clinic Beau-Site

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 73545

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

For Klinik Beau-Site we are looking for a committed personality with a wide range of tasks and attractive development opportunities. As a deputy for the surgical management, you take on responsibility in day-to-day operations, shape processes and promote the further development of our team. You can expect short decision paths, a flat hierarchy and the opportunity to actively contribute your ideas and shape changes.

We are an experienced, motivated surgical team that values open cooperation and communication, mutual support and the highest quality standards.

Your Advantages in Us

  • Self-organized service planning
  • Attractive workplace in the heart of Bern
  • Staff restaurant with discounted conditions
  • Up to two additional weeks of holiday (Leisure Plus model)
  • Four additional free days per year at 100% allowance as compensation for changing time
  • Participation in public transport subscription
  • Discount on fitness subscription
  • Staff room and parking space in front of the clinic
  • Selective damage in the form of free time or money

All further information about our employment conditions and benefits can be found under the following link: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • Deputy surgical management in technical, organisational and personnel matters
  • Ensure smooth and efficient operation of the operating room
  • Management, promotion and development of employees
  • Deployment and resource planning as well as participation in personnel planning
  • Operational coordination and ensuring optimal utilisation of operating room capacities
  • Instrumentation and service in various fields of expertise
  • Ensuring quality, patient safety and compliance with standards
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with surgeon: inside, anesthesia, surgery and other interfaces

Your profile

  • Training as a specialist/specialist surgical technology HF
  • Well-founded professional experience in the OR
  • High social competence and pleasure in the development of employees
  • Organizational strong, responsible and resilient personality
  • Economic thinking and networked action
  • Good MS-Office and SAP knowledge
  • Readiness for further training in leadership
  • Assistance in covering piquet services and late shifts

For additional information, Sibel Öztürk, head of OP, is at your disposal under T. If you have any questions about the application or the application system, you can contact Cindy Auf der Maur, HR recruitment partner, by e-mail.
