Full description

This is where the threads come together. In our modern corporate headquarters in Sursee, you actively shape the future of OTTO'S. You can expect varied tasks, short decision paths and an innovative working environment. Benefit from modern jobs as well as an attractive catering offer and a wide range of development opportunities.

Area of activity

  • Overall responsibility: responsibility for the vocational training of learners in German and French Switzerland
  • Strategic development: further development of the training strategy and introduction of new impulses for a modern and future-oriented learning culture
  • Recruitment & Employer Branding: Gaining new learners and strengthening the employer attractiveness of OTTO'S as a training company
  • Accompaniment of learners: supervision, promotion and support of learners throughout the training period
  • Support for vocational trainers: coaching, advice and qualification of vocational trainers in the branches and departments of logistics and central administration
  • Quality assurance: ensuring administration as well as compliance with and further development of high quality standards
  • Deputy: Full Deputy Head of Training and active participation in the further development of the training area

Benefits

  • Family, owner-managed company
  • State-of-the-art administration with car park and sports changing rooms
  • Modern and high-quality employee restaurant
  • Friday from 1 pm in weekend mode
  • Business car incl. Fuel card
  • Attractive shopping perks online and in retail
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 weeks from age 50)
  • Generous loyalty bonuses and legendary staff festival
  • Tickets for various games of FC Lucerne, EV train, HC Ambri-Piotta, SC Bern and Lausanne HC
  • Additional remuneration on the old capital of the BVG

Profile

  • Training: Completed basic training with further training in HR, vocational training or business administration
  • Professional experience: Several years of experience in vocational training in the retail trade as well as in the development and further development of training structures
  • Skills: Affinity to digital media and learning platforms as well as good knowledge of the Swiss educational landscape
  • Language skills: Very good knowledge of German and French
