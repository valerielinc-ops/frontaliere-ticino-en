We are looking for motivated veterinarians who want to gain their first professional experience and combine a clinical activity with scientific work in the field of MRI and/or CT diagnostics. They are firmly integrated into the workflows of our department and assume responsibility in clinical day-to-day operations. One of their main tasks is the anesthesiological care of small animal patients during MRI and CT examinations. In parallel, you will work on a scientific research project with the aim of writing a dissertation. Participation in the anesthesiological piket service is expressly desired and is additionally financially compensated. This is what they bring with them.

  • Good communication skills in German, French and English
  • Teamwork, reliability and willingness to take responsibility

About us

The Department of Clinical Radiology of the Vetsuisse Faculty Bern is a dedicated team of 24 employees, consisting of veterinary radiologists and radiologists as well as specialists in medical-technical radiology, veterinary practice assistants and doctoral students. We offer the full spectrum of modern imaging diagnostics. In addition to the diagnostic care and advice of our clinic-internal and external partners, we are involved in teaching and research. EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
