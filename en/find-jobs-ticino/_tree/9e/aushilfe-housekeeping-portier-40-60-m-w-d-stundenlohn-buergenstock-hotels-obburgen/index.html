Housekeeping Porter 40% - 60% (m/f/d) - hourly wage

Your daily work

  • Ensuring smooth luggage service for guests
  • Picking up and returning guest clothes on request
  • Acceptance and distribution of freshly cleaned hotel linen
  • Support for room cleaning (sucking of the floors, pulling off the bed linen)
  • Carrying out special work, e.g. regular window cleaning

What we want from you

  • Good knowledge of German or English is required
  • High quality and responsibility awareness
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Team spirit, organizational talent and physical strength
  • Willingness to go the “extra mile” for guests
