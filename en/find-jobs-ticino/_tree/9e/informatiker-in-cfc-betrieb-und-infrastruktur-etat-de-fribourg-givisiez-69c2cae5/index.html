Who are we?

The Information and Telecommunications Service (SITel) ensures the availability of IT and telecommunications resources necessary for the proper functioning of the cantonal administration. The security and evolution of the information systems of the State and all its partners also fall within its responsibility.

Your Missions

We are offering an apprenticeship position in IT Specialist CFC, specializing in Operations and Infrastructure.

During your training, you will discover various areas of IT and work with several teams at SITel. You will be supported throughout your apprenticeship and will participate in concrete work and projects.

What awaits you

  • Install and configure computer systems
  • Discover servers, networks, and workstations
  • Participate in technical problem-solving
  • Carry out practical work and projects
  • Discover various IT domains within SITel

Desired Profile

  • Completed or nearly completed compulsory education
  • Strong interest in IT and new technologies
  • Logical thinking, curiosity, and desire to learn
  • Ability to work methodically
  • Team spirit and sense of responsibility

We Offer

  • Varied and concrete training
  • Personalized support
  • Modern technological environment
  • Discovery of various IT domains and SITel teams
  • Opportunity to participate in real projects

Your Training

Duration: 4 years Professional Maturity: possible Diploma: IT Specialist CFC Operations and Infrastructure
