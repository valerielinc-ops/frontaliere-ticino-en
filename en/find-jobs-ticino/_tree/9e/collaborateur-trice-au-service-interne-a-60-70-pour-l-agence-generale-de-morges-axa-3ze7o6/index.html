What's waiting for you

What you bring

  • A strong interest in advice and sales, a strong motivation to train you on our products and internal processes

  • Positive and dynamic temperament, reliability, flexibility and ease of contact with others and excellent teamwork
  • An excellent mastery of French in both oral and written terms, your ability in English is an advantage, any other language is a complementary asset
  • A place to live in our area of activity and a good knowledge of the local economic fabric
  • Easiness with common computer systems and learning new digital tools

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
