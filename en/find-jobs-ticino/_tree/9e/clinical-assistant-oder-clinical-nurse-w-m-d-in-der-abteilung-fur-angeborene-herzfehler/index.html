We, the Department of Congenital Heart Defects, are an interdisciplinary team at the University Hospital of Zurich – just a few minutes from the central station of the bustling Zurich. The development of medicine has made it possible that today most patients with congenital heart defects reach adulthood. Our team is committed to providing comprehensive care for this complex patient group. We are proud that we can offer this support in high quality through our expertise. For that we need reinforcement! An exciting job in the Department of Congenital Heart Defects of Cardiology is waiting for you.

An insight into your main tasks

  • You are the primary contact person for the medical concerns of our patients
  • You take over the triage of patient requests, organize emergency consultations as well as hospitalizations and operations of patients in cooperation with doctors and responsible areas
  • You are responsible for requests from other hospitals or medical practices and process them
  • You are responsible for patient education, for supporting patients and their relatives in managing the disease
  • You will accompany our patients in the difficult transition phase from childhood to adulthood, plan appropriate visits with the children’s hospital and take part in it
  • After a comprehensive introduction, you will accompany ergometries and spiro ergometrites
  • You regularly take part in further education and training that continuously adapts your skills to the current requirements of patient care

What we expect from you

  • Decisive for us are your professional experience in healthcare (ideally in cardiology) and in the care of patients as well as your medical expertise.
  • As a basis, we imagine an EFZ training as a medical assistant (MPA), or a completed training as a qualified nurse HF/FH / qualified nurse HF/FH
  • Very good knowledge of German is absolutely necessary, combined with a self-confident appearance and a winning power of persuasion
  • This task requires a lot of personal responsibility and organizational talent, but also teamwork, coordination and empathy.
  • Experience or additional qualifications for the accompaniment of ergometries are desirable, but not a prerequisite - you can learn this with us

What can you expect from us?

  • A very exciting, but also demanding task area with beautiful design scope
  • A team that takes heart and soul to the patients – whose gratitude for the support in their diverse challenges of life is safe for you
  • Open collaboration and uncomplicated exchange opportunities in the team
  • An exciting collaboration with experts from different areas of highly specialized medicine
  • Regulated working hours (Monday to Friday) and a modern workplace
