Housekeeping employees/in (m/f/d) Flims From now on or after agreement we are looking for a new co-host for our housekeeping team. 40-60% from now until the end of November 80-100% Pensum possible from December or still 40-60% Depending on agreement Our traditional family business stands for warmth, passion and personal responsibility. With us, not only professional competence counts, but above all personality – an open appearance, joy in dealing with people and the proverbial heart in the right place. Our guests are as diverse as our everyday life: from marriage proposals and weddings to incentive travelers to family holidays. We don't get bored. In the summer and winter season, you work together with around 50 dedicated and charming hosts. Several times in succession we were voted the 75 best winter hotels and the 100 best holiday hotels in Switzerland. We see these awards as an incentive to continuously question and redefine our service. Our goal is to always surprise our guests with a real WOW moment. Their tasks Every host is equally important to us. Whether in front of or behind the scenes, we all contribute to the guestwow.

You are responsible for

  • Basic cleaning work
  • Room cleaning (Départ, Arrivée and Restant)
  • Cleaning of public areas
  • Cleaning of the indoor pool and wellness area
  • Laundry work (washing, drying, folding)
  • Control and filling of cleaning and consumables
  • Care and cleanliness of guest rooms and corridors
  • Compliance with hygiene and quality standards

That sets them apart.

  • An apprenticeship in the hotel or already first experience in housekeeping
  • Oral knowledge of German and/or willingness to deepen
  • You enjoy working with people in a team
  • You have a positive attitude to life and joy in giving other people beautiful moments
  • You pass on your knowledge to learners and new employees

Why you should come to us in the Schweizerhof

  • We are a dynamic, international hotel with over a hundred years of family tradition – we combine experience, heart and modern hospitality.
  • Enjoy an attractive workplace in the dreamlike destination Flims Laax Falera, with numerous leisure opportunities right on your doorstep.
  • Benefit from exciting benefits, in-house discounts and special conditions for overnight stays at our partners of Romantik Hotels International and Swiss Historic Hotels.
  • Experience a family working atmosphere characterized by respect, appreciation and a strong team spirit.
  • We promote your personal and professional development through targeted further education and training.
  • All hosts are welcome to participate in our active weekly programs (Yoga, Pilates, Shinrin Yoku...) and use the indoor pool for relaxation in the evening.
  • Regulated working hours with two days off per week
  • No partial service

Contact details We are happy to tell you more about this position and the philosophy of our company during a personal conversation. We look forward to meeting you!
