Full description

Attached directly to the Medical Director, you have a strategic cross-cutting function within the Arc Network. A true bridge between the Medical Directorate, doctors and healthcare teams, you are actively involved in the development of quality of care, patient safety and the implementation of innovative institutional projects. Quality of care and patient safety

  • Pilot and coordinate the Arc Network quality management system.
  • Develop and update patient quality concept, including for outpatient activities.
  • Monitor quality and safety indicators, including ANQ indicators.
  • Organize internal audits and support continuous process improvement.
  • Coordinate the medical-care quality team.

Integrated care development and innovation

  • Contribute to the development of the VIVA Quality System – Arc Network, an innovative model of integrated care.
  • Develop performance indicators in population health and integrated care.
  • Participate in research projects in collaboration with the University of Basel.
  • Support the integration of innovative solutions, particularly in the areas of artificial intelligence and coordination of care pathways.

Support to the Medical Directorate

  • Participate in the development and dissemination of institutional medical guidelines.
  • Contribute to the implementation of strategic projects such as TARDOC or TeamSTEPPS.
  • Support Medical Affairs in certain physician recruitment and integration activities.
  • Participate in the development of clinical and medico-economic piloting tools.
  • Coordinate medical teams, if necessary, in the absence of a Chief of Service.
  • Physician with FMH degree.
  • At least one year's experience as a clinical leader.
  • High interest in quality of care, public health and organizational development.
  • Excellent communication skills and ability to unite multidisciplinary teams.
  • Autonomy, organizational sense and adaptability.
  • High discretion and confidentiality.
  • A strategic function at the heart of the Medical Directorate.
  • Active participation in innovative projects with high impact for patients and healthcare professionals.
  • A recognition position for post-graduate public health training.
  • The possibility of maintaining clinical activity by agreement.
  • A flexible participation rate between 80 and 100%.
  • No custody.
  • An enabling environment for innovation, collaboration and professional development.

Entry into office: to be agreed Place of activity: all sites of the Arc Network

  • Moutier
  • St-Imier
  • Bernese Biel and Jura

The first integrated care network in Switzerland, the Arc Network is a key player in coordinated and community-based care in the somatic and mental health fields. With close to 1,300 employees, he is based on 2 hospital sites in Moutier and Saint-Imier, as well as a network of Medicentres, ambulance, EMS, radiology, a physical and cognitive therapy centre and a home hospital service. The Arc Network covers the medical-hospital needs of a population of nearly 80,000 and is characterised by its dynamism, excellent infrastructure and a constant concern to develop services of proximity and quality. In order to strengthen its Medical Directorate, we are looking for: Head of Clinic, Medical Directorate 80-100%
