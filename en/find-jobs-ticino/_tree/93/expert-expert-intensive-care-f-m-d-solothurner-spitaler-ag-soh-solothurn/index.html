About the Solothurner Spitäler AG

The Cantonal Hospital Olten (KSO) guarantees the extended basic medical care of the Olten-Gösgen and Thal-Gäu regions. Around 1,300 employees provide medical care and the well-being of patients around the clock. In addition to basic care, the KSO has the main offerings acute geriatrics, spinal surgery and urology.
