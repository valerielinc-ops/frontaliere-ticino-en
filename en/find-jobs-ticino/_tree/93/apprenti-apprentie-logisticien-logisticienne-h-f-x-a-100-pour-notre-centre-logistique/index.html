Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in vocational training, it gives an important place to the accompaniment and development of young talent, with more than 120 learners trained each year in its various departments. Workplace : Cheseaux-sur-Lausanne For our Event Logistics Centre, we are looking for an apprentice Logistician from 1 August 2027. Do you like to organize, move, work as a team and actually participate in the success of different activities? You want to learn a varied profession, which combines logistics, technical and event? Then this learning could be done for you! The missions that await you: During your apprenticeship, you will have the opportunity to:

  • Receiving, controlling and storing goods;
  • Prepare internal orders and arrange shipments;
  • Ensure computer tracking of stocks and participate in inventories;
  • Manipulating professional equipment and participating in the various logistical stages: pick-up, palletization, preparation and return of equipment, etc.;
  • Participate in the logistical preparation of our various events;
  • Contribute to decoration and visual production activities. Your profile:
  • You have completed or will soon complete your compulsory education;
  • You are motivated, curious and want to learn;
  • You have an interest in practical and technical activities;
  • You have an interest in event logistics;
  • You are organized and logical;
  • You appreciate teamwork and communicate easily with others;
  • You are flexible and enjoy a variety of activities;
  • Good knowledge of German is an asset. We offer you:
  • A healthy and stimulating work environment within a cooperative company certified as Great Place to Work;
  • Various, concrete and dynamic vocational training in the field of logistics;
  • The opportunity to participate in diverse activities, including logistics and events;
  • Support throughout your learning to develop your skills and autonomy;
  • The possibility of discovering the operation of a large insurance company and gradually building your professional future;
  • Opportunities for engagement at the end of your training. Interested? So don't hesitate to send us your complete application package including:
  • your cover letter;
  • your CV;
  • your last three school reports;
  • your possible certificates of internship. We look forward to discovering your profile and receiving your application!
