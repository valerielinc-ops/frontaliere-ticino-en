Salesman in motorcycles

Harley\-Davidson Valais\-Wallis is an official dealership based in Valais, Switzerland, dedicated to the motorcycle world and the Harley\-Davidson brand. The company offers a full range of motorcycles, accessories and services for a passionate clientele. It is distinguished by personalized service, strong proximity to its local clientele and community spirit around motorcycle culture. Joining Harley\-Davidson Valais\-Wallis is integrating a committed team into a dynamic and customer-oriented environment.

Description of position

As a motorcycle seller, you will accompany customers in all stages of their purchase project, from hosting in concession to personalized advice until the sale is concluded. You present Harley\-Davidson models, options, accessories as well as financing and insurance solutions, ensuring a friendly and professional customer experience.

You will participate in the showroom's presentation (organisation of motorcycles, merchandising, cleanliness) and follow the requests, quotations and commercial renewals in a structured way. You work closely with the workshop and the administrative team to ensure smooth service, while contributing to the events and marketing actions of the concession. This position is full-time and site based based in (Valais).

Qualifications

  • Experience confirmed in sales (minimum 3 years), in the field of motorcycles and/or automobiles, with a strong customer service orientation.
  • German/German required.
  • Interest in motorcycle and culture Harley\-Davidson an asset, basic knowledge of technical characteristics.
  • Mastery of basic computer tools (MS Office).
  • Very good skills in oral and written communication, ability to listen actively and ease in trade negotiations.
  • A sense of teamwork, reliability, autonomy and availability to occasionally participate in customer events (open doors, outings, salons).
  • Motorbike licence
