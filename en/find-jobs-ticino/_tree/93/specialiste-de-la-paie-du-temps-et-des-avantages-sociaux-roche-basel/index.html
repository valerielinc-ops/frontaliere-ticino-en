The Position

What we do

The People Support Solutions (PSS) department brings its purpose to life by providing our colleagues worldwide with integrated HR solutions tailored to their needs. We are a team that works very closely together to ensure good employee relations, anticipate our customers' future needs, and proactively develop solutions.

The Opportunity

As a Payroll, Time & Benefits Specialist - German speaking​ you are part of a dynamic, vivid and spiritful team supporting our German affiliates in Mannheim. This team delivers customer service and advisory support to HR and business partners on global HR systems and processes, ensuring end-to-end process completion in line with our customer-focused principles.

As a qualified expert, you are

  • Providing insight, support and explanation on HR system and administrative activities in the area of payroll, time & benefit services
  • Providing customer service on payroll, time & benefit related activities (inquiry resolution, request management, and troubleshooting)
  • Processing of leaves of absence, processing shifts, cooperation inquiries on the subject of working time, long-term illnesses as well as the associated compensation benefits
  • Answering employee requests regarding the pay slip
  • Supporting the embedding of the new ways of working, actively participating in the development and implementation of continuous improvement initiatives
  • Behaving as role model to team members, showing positive and flexible attitude
  • Building relationships with HR colleagues within the PSS organization as well as with customers to ensure ongoing service delivery effectiveness.

Please note that this position is a fixed-term maternity cover.   • Strong customer centricity, end-to-end accountability, problem-solving skills, growth & agile mindset in ambiguous situations
  • Multi-cultural acceptance, ability to work in a fast-paced and changing environment
  • Strong & precise work-ethic, good analytical skills, ability to deal with Excel tables and ability to focus on details and numbers
  • Experience in service delivery or customer care environment or HR Operations / HR Services
  • Experience in (German) payroll (advantage)
  • Fluent in written and spoken English and German.
