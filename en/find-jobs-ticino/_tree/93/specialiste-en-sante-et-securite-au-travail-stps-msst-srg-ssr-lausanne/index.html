Full description

We act as a UNIQUE team for the success of the SSR. Together with the other areas, Finance is accountable for strategic leadership and allocation of objective oriented resources, and creates transparency for sustainable decisions.

Your Mission

Within the Western Region Security Team, you help ensure a safe and healthy working environment, ensuring the prevention of occupational risks and the protection of the health of employees. Attached to the Head of Security – Western Region, you accompany the teams in their daily activities as well as in the preparation and realization of productions and events, both on the sites and outside. No one on the ground, you focus on a pragmatic, collaborative and solutions-oriented approach, proposing prevention measures tailored to the risks, operational realities and regulatory requirements. You work in a multidisciplinary team where complementary skills, knowledge sharing and the development of progressive versatility are essential elements. You also contribute to projects and initiatives of national scope, in coordination with teams from other regions.

Your Responsibilities

  • Prevention and risk analysis: identify, assess and monitor occupational risks related to the activities of different sites, productions and events, and propose appropriate preventive measures.
  • Operational support: to advise the teams as soon as the projects are prepared, to participate in the recognitions and to ensure the OHS monitoring of productions, shoots and events, including in the field.
  • Accident and near-accident management: follow-up with the persons concerned, analysis of the circumstances and causes, proposing corrective and preventive measures, and monitoring their implementation and effectiveness in order to reduce risks in a sustainable manner.
  • OHS implementation and project management: deploy prevention measures, contribute to audits and procedures, pilot OHS projects and develop awareness and training actions tailored to the needs of teams.
  • Collaboration and coordination: work closely with internal teams, providers, specialized partners and competent authorities, seeking solutions tailored to operational needs.
  • Teamwork and harmonization: collaborate with other specialists in safety, safety and fire protection, share skills and contribute to the continuous improvement of OSH practices, both at regional and national level.

Your profile

  • Recognized occupational health and safety training: a federal certificate for occupational safety and health protection (OSPS), recognized as a MSST specialist or recognized equivalent training.
  • Professional experience: at least five years of OSH experience in Switzerland, with strong skills in risk analysis, prevention, project management and support of operational activities.
  • Personal skills: excellent team spirit, relationship ease and sense of service. You show autonomy, discernment, flexibility and pedagogy, with a constructive, proportionate and solutions-oriented approach.
  • Communication and availability: mastery of French in oral and written language, good understanding of German and ability to collaborate with various interlocutors. Timely availability in the evening, on weekends and for trips related to productions.

Your assets

  • Experience in audiovisual, technical or event environments, knowledge of the constraints of external productions and mastery of English.
  • Complementary first aid skills (including IAS 3), experience in training and awareness-raising of employees or practical experience in demanding operational environments.

What we offer you

  • A dynamic working environment within an innovative public service company.
  • Various activities and stimulating responsibilities
  • Collaboration with committed multidisciplinary teams.
