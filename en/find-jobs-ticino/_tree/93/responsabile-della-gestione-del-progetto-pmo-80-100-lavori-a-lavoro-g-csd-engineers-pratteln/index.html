1,000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: The CSD Group is a dynamic, constantly growing engineering company. Inventiveness, competence and multidisciplinarity characterize our teams, optimal solutions for our customers with a plus for the quality of life and the environment are our trademark. They enjoy working in a professional and agile environment and attach great importance to quality and efficiency. Become part of our success! We are looking for a Project Management Officer (PMO) 80-100% (f/m/d) for our area of construction technology in Zurich, Pratteln or Frauenfeld. Your responsibilities

  • Project -, financial controlling and management
  • Establishment and maintenance of a uniform project controlling system for the current project
  • Monitoring deadlines, costs and resources with regular target/actual matching
  • Detecting discrepancies between planning, budget, effort and performance
  • Preparation of project status reports and decision-making basis for the GPL and departmental management
  • Risk management
  • Identification and evaluation of project and cost risks
  • Development and follow-up of appropriate countermeasures
  • Supporting project management in the early management of critical topics
  • Processes and standards
  • Development and further development of project management standards and best practices
  • Creation of templates, checklists and process documentation
  • Ensuring compliance with internal quality standards
  • Support for the introduction and further development of BIM processes
  • Resource planning and coordination
  • Support for resource and capacity planning
  • Coordination of staff assignments in coordination with project managers
  • Transparency on capacity utilisation, congestion and spare capacity
  • Your profile
  • Professional qualifications
  • Completed studies in civil engineering, industrial engineering, business administration or comparable discipline (FH / HF / KV with further education)
  • Several years of professional experience in a PMO function in a major project, ideally in plant construction, especially in the KVA or wastewater environment
  • Experience with hour reporting systems
  • Knowledge of NPK and BKP settlement structures
  • In-depth knowledge in project controlling, cost and budget management as well as auditing, contracts and reporting
  • Experience with project management software (MS Project, Primavera, or similar)
  • Knowledge of relevant standards and regulations (SIA regulations, KBOB, NPK)
  • BIM knowledge is an advantage
  • Personal competences
  • Analytical and structured thinking
  • High level of initiative and responsibility
  • Discreet and reliable handling of sensitive information
  • Communication-strong personality with a safe appearance
  • Team-oriented, resilient and solution-oriented
  • Language skills
  • in German: nehandlungssicher (C1/C2)
  • English: beneficial

  • Close cooperation with the overall project management and insight into all project areas

  • Modern working environment with digital tools and established project management processes
  • Individual training and development opportunities
