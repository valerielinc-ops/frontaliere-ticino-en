Designer Advertising Technology (m/f/d | 80 - 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 600 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. We also offer a comprehensive range of repairs and services for vehicles of all kinds (buses, commercial vehicles, passenger cars, campers, etc.).

To complement our team in the labeling department, we are looking for a motivated and experienced designer advertising technique.

You have a degree as a designer / in advertising technology, enjoy working creatively and are enthusiastic about vehicles and their appearance? Then we offer you a varied challenge with a lot of personal responsibility in a small and dynamic team. Their tasks

  • Capture orders and create the "good to print"
  • Planning and organizing orders and materials
  • Mounting of flat letters, panels, advertising banners, etc. on building envelopes
  • Labelling and foiling of all types of vehicles (buses, passenger cars, commercial vehicles)
  • Creation and editing of pixel and vector graphics
  • Assembly of signs and labels in the studio and at the customer's site
  • Assistance in the training of a Learning Designer Advertising Technology EFZ

Our requirements

  • Completed basic vocational training as a designer/in advertising technology
  • Technical understanding and material knowledge
  • Craftsmanship and a high quality awareness
  • Fun with creative work
  • Reliable, team capable, open and honest personality
  • Independent and solution-oriented working method
  • Good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Driving licence Cat. B

We offer them

  • Interesting, varied and challenging activity
  • Self-employed tasks with a lot of personal responsibility
  • Promotion of professional and personal development
  • Flexible working hours
  • Performance-based conditions of employment

If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
