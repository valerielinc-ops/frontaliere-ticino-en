Full description

You want to care for more than animals? Then you are right with us. In the Tierpark Bern you work every day for the welfare of our animals and inspire people for nature and species protection. Together we create a place that connects animals, people and nature – and offers more space for diversity. Tasks: • You look after the animals in the Dählhölzli and the BärenPark • You keep the plants (technique, restrictions, plants) • You do safety and cleanliness checks • You look after the guests on site (e.g. on guided tours, commented feedings, events) Requirements • Completion of a degree as an animal keeper/in EFZ (like specialisation in wildlife) and craftsmanship • Experience with wildlife • willingness to provide services and communication skills (D mandatory); E + F is advantageous • Joy in dealing with guests, affinity for nature and species protection • PW identification We offer: For sense and city life With us you change something. You help shape city life, carry responsibility and make Bern more liveable. In the city of Bern, more than 4,500 people work together across five directorates and more than 80 job profiles. We are committed with passion and conviction to the people who live here, who work here. Together we shape the community and the future of this city – visible, tangible, sustainable. for our city. For Bern.
