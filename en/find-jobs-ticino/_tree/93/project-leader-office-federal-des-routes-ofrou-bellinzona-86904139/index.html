Move Switzerland forward!

The Federal Office for Roads (OFROU) is responsible for the construction, maintenance and operation of the national road network and is committed to safe and sustainable mobility throughout the road network. OFROU is an office of the Federal Department of Environment, Transport, Energy and Communication (DETEC).

As a collaborator of OFROU, you are advancing Switzerland. You take responsibility for demanding projects that contribute to the development of different regions of the country by linking them together. OFROU offers you a modern working environment in which you will make a significant contribution to the country's success.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
