With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital ensures high-quality medical care for 150,000 residents – around the clock. Care with heart, mind and flexibility – welcome to Spital Emmental. What makes us special? For us, working in a modern way means: acting humanely, with creative freedom and an open ear for one another.
