Medical Secretary (a) — Spital Emmental

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

We are listening. We stick together. We work together. In our oncology secretariat in Burgdorf we are a well-established team that works with a lot of commitment, empathy and professionalism. At the same time, we take care of each other in the team, treat each other with respect and stick together. This cooperation is invaluable to us – it makes our work not only effective, but also human. To strengthen our team, we are looking for a committed personality as of 1 December 2026 or by agreement. The position is limited to six months for the time being, with the option of renewal.

Your responsibility

  • You have fun at work: You warmly welcome our patients
  • You bring order to everyday life: You coordinate the office hours and ensure smooth scheduling
  • You keep track: you organize examinations, create patient orders and keep the medical documentation carefully
  • You strengthen our processes: You prepare the tumor boards organizationally and ensure a structured process
  • You take care of the essentials: from ordering to material management – you make sure that everything is ready
  • You regulate the administrative: You keep the correspondence with health insurance companies and process cost-benefit applications. Writing the reports gives you pleasure
  • You work precisely: The billing of reports and tumor boards in IBI Care is your responsibility
  • You are our voice out: On the phone you perform empathically, competently and professionally

What you bring

  • A basic medical or commercial education with additional training as medical secretary:in or MPA.
  • Good knowledge of medical terminology and experience in writing dictates.
  • Experienced handling of the PC and common programs
  • You are capable of teamwork, resilient and enjoy working with and for people
