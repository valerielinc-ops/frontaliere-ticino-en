Full description

Location: Hirslanden Klinik Linde | Biel / Bienne

Occupation by: by agreement

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 51784

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments.

Clinic Linde is the leading private hospital in the Biel-Seeland-Jura area. With its high medical quality, its selected offers and the supra-regional reputation, it is the first choice for patients as well as for highly qualified medical doctors.

Are you looking for a varied job in the field of surgery – with clearly regulated working hours, a high level of independence and a motivated team? Then you are exactly right with us at the clinic Linde.

YOUR SCOPE

  • Need- and customer-oriented care and care of patients according to the care process in cooperation with our partner doctors and an interprofessional team
  • In this function, you work in the 12-hour service (11:00 – 23:00), divided into five weekdays from Monday to Friday. This regulation allows you longer leisure blocks and creates a clear structure in everyday life

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional care and care of patients
  • Implementation of medical regulations and participation in preventive, diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • High independence and responsibility in clinical decision-making and the measures to be initiated
  • Accompaniment of learners and students of nursing training

REQUIRED TRAINING

  • training as a qualified nurse HF/FH; or
  • nurse with diploma level ll (DN II) or
  • Dipl. Nurse in general nursing (AKP)
  • If you have a foreign diploma, recognition by the Swiss Red Cross (SRC) or at least a positive PreCheck is required. Further information can be found on the homepage of the SRK.

Previous experience

  • Professional experience in acute hospital desirable

FURTHER SKILLS AND KNOWLEDGE

  • Great flexibility, resilience and high organizational talent
  • High social skills and strong empathy
  • Teamwork and ability to guide employees
  • Good knowledge of German and/or French in word and writing (certificate B2) and understanding in the other language
  • Good IT user knowledge

Why is it worth working for us?

  • We work appreciatively and respectfully at eye level with each other
  • Possibility of working with flex and leisure plus models
  • Up to 4 additional free days per year as compensation for changing time (pension-dependent)
  • Above-average night and weekend allowance of CHF 10.- per hour
  • Many discounts and perks in the fitness center, over 100 service providers, leisure events and mobile subscriptions

Benefit as a Hirslanden employee: in many more benefits!

For additional information, Tanja Wyss, Head of Surgery, is available at tanja.wyss@hirslanden.ch or Jennifer Perez at jennifer.perez@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that we cannot consider paper dossiers (not returned) or applications by email. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via the tool on our homepage.
