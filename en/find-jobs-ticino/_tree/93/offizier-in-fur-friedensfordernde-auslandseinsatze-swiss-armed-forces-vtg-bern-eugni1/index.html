Put to the Point

Are you an officer, speaker, communicative and open to other cultures? Furthermore, do you have a high degree of empathy and would like to gain experience abroad in an international environment? Then this is your chance – we look forward to your application! The SWISSINT Competence Center is constantly recruiting specialists for temporary missions abroad to promote peace in the Swiss Army. For these assignments, interested women and men can apply voluntarily for various functions.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-7237

You can make this contribution

  • Deployment as an officer in NATO or EU-led contingents, for example as a location commander, team leader or staff officer
  • Deployment in UN missions, for example as a military observer to monitor compliance with a ceasefire agreement or as a staff officer at the respective headquarters
  • Capacity building as a training officer
  • Act as director of international courses in various training centers

This makes them unique.

  • Completed apprenticeship or study, practical qualification and good repute
  • Military degree of the Swiss Army as first lieutenant, captain, major or lieutenant colonel
  • Mental and physical resilience
  • Ability to put personal interests in the background; high willingness to work impartially for the mission in the international environment for several months
  • Civil ticket (category B1)
  • Very good knowledge of English, French is an advantage

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.
