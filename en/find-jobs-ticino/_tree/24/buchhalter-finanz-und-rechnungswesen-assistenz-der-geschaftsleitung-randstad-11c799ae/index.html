Accountant in Finance & Accounting with the assistance of the management (m/f/d)

Pensum: 80/100 %

Place of work: Upper Valais region

Type of employment: permanent employment

Industry: Total service providers

For our client, an established, leading family company from Valais with a strong market presence and family culture, we are looking for a versatile, structured and organizationally strong personality. In this interface function, you assume responsibility for the accounting and keep the management administratively free.

Your tasks

  • Finance and accounting: self-employed management of creditors and accounts payable
  • Current accounting and reconciliation of main and secondary accounts Preparation of the month, quarter and annual accounts Settlement and monitoring of daily payments
  • Preparation of periodic VAT accounts
  • Assistance to management: Reliable scheduling and calendar coordination for management
  • Support in varied day-to-day operations as well as cooperation in internal special projects Careful maintenance of databases, dossiers and internal files

Qualifications

Your profile

  • Successfully completed basic commercial training with targeted further training in finance and accounting (e.g. clerk/accounting or comparable)
  • Sound, multi-year professional experience in accounting
  • Secure and routine handling of the common MS Office applications as well as ERP / accounting systems
  • Independent, structured and exact working methods coupled with high discretion and strong team spirit
  • Stylistic German in word and writing
  • Knowledge of French is a clear advantage

Your profit

  • Long-term perspective in a crisis-proof, successful traditional company
  • Attractive employment conditions and financial participation in training
  • A warm, collegial team with lively culture and regular team events
  • Modern workplace with free employee parking
