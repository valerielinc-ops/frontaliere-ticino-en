Technical Quality Control - Q-team — MKS PAMP SA, Castel San Pietro (TI).

MIXI

For the expansion of the Quality structure, we are looking for a resource to be included in our Q-Team. The selected person will take care of surveillance and control along the production flow, working closely with the Production department to ensure compliance with company quality standards.

MAIN ACTIVITIES AND RESPONSIBILITY

  • Monitor the production process, verifying the conformity of products to quality standards.
  • Make measurements, controls and analysis of material composition, using dedicated instrumentation.
  • Report any anomalies or non-compliance to the reference team promptly.
  • Fill out the relevant technical documentation.
  • Preparation of samples with mechanical instrumentation (trapano, fresa).

TRAINING AND EXPERIENCE REQUEST

  • Experience in mechanical industrial contexts
  • In possession of a technical diploma (preferably mechanical or equivalent)

QUALIFIES AND COMPETENCES

  • Reliability and sense of responsibility
  • Precision and attention to detail
  • Teamwork attitude
  • Flexibility and adaptability
  • Proactivity in reporting abnormalities or non-compliance
  • Ability to comply with business procedures and standards
  • Working on three shifts
  • Familiarity with measuring instruments and control (caliber, micrometer, comparators)
  • Sample manipulation attitude (radially handling weights 5-10 kg)
  • Conformity analysis of the material
  • Ability to compile reports and technical documentation
  • Basic knowledge of the main IT tools
