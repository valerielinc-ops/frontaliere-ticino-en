Full description

The BRUGG Group is an internationally active Swiss industrial company with around 2,200 employees worldwide. The Group’s headquarters is currently located in Brugg and will move to the Birr site in February 2027. There you benefit from the immediate proximity to the subsidiary BRUGG Lifting AG. In the course of this move, she is looking for an organizationally strong, discreet and forward-looking personality.

The BRUGG IT

rethink. automatic machines. innovate.

As a company of the Brugg Group, the BRUGG IT department of Rittmeyer offers infrastructure services for the entire international group of companies. This benefits three organizations with a total of 1500 end users. The central service desk is the first point of contact for our users and interface to the rest of the IT disciplines.

What you can do with us

  • You support the Group CEO, the Group CFO and the Board of Directors in various organizational and administrative matters
  • You lead the Group Secretariat and ensure the professional organisation of Board meetings, General Meetings and other management bodies
  • You coordinate meetings, business trips and events and ensure smooth preparation and follow-up
  • You manage the share register, coordinate shareholder, KYC and ownership issues and ensure complete and confidential documentation
  • You create presentations, decision bases and correspondence for different stakeholders
  • You work closely with internal and external stakeholders and actively engage in HR, compliance and project topics at the group level

What you bring

  • You have a basic commercial education with a further education as a directorial assistant: in, executive assistant or a comparable qualification
  • You have several years of experience in a demanding assistant role at management, board or group level
  • You move confidently in an environment where confidentiality, professionalism and precision are taken for granted
  • You think with, ahead and connected, recognize connections quickly and keep an overview even in a dynamic environment
  • You communicate stylishly in German and convince in English in exchange with different stakeholders
  • Ideally, you will have experience in governance, shareholdership, compliance or comparable regulatory and administrative processes.
  • You work independently, assume responsibility and act securely and bindingly towards internal and external stakeholders
