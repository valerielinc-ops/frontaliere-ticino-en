fedpol in action for Switzerland – anytime and anywhere

fedpol in operation for Switzerland – anytime & anywhere. fedpol develops and operates national information systems and performs supervisory, licensing and enforcement tasks in weapons and explosives law. The Directorate of Police Systems and Identification (PSI) of fedpol ensures the secure operation and further development of central police information systems and databases, is responsible for the national hub for police information exchange and provides identification and forensic services for domestic and foreign partner authorities. The Department of ID Cards and Central Offices (AZS) carries out the statutory tasks of the Federation in the field of ID cards and weapons and explosives law. It comprises the two central offices for weapons and explosives, the area of ID cards and E-ID as well as the Competence Center for Security Documents and Counterfeiting and is responsible for oversight and market surveillance, the issuance and further development of ID cards and E-ID solutions as well as the analysis and fight against misuse and counterfeiting.
