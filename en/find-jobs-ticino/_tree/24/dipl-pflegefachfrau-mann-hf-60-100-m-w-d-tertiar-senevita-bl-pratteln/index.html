Full description

Senevita’s individual living and care services provide quality of life in old age. We are a private service company, successful in the management of senior citizens’ residences, retirement and care centres and assisted residential facilities in Switzerland.

For the Senevita Sonnenpark in Pratteln we are looking for a Dipl. Nurse/man HF 60 - 100 % Your tasks

  • You contribute to ensuring that our residents enjoy professional and individual care and care every day
  • You conduct RAI assessments and promote the autonomy of our residents
  • Emergency situations you recognize and master
  • You take part in the daily care according to the kinesthetic approach of our residents and train our learners
  • The electronic maintenance documentation you manage independently and seamlessly

You bring that with you

  • Training as a Dipl. Nurse HF, AKP, DNII, DNI or PsyKP (for foreign diploma: SRK recognition required)
  • You have professional experience in the inpatient long-term care sector and people with dementia
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Good RAI knowledge in classifying dementia patients is beneficial
  • You are an organizational talent and strive to make everyday life efficient

We offer you

  • Extensive training offer of the Senevita Academy, can be visited free of charge
  • Saturday allowances
  • Cheap food in the in-house gastronomy
  • Various perks (job ticket, fueling, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday (from 50 years 6 or from 60 years 7 weeks)
  • Good connections to public transport
