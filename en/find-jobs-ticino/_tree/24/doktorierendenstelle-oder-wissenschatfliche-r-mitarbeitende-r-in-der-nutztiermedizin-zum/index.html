Project description

Respiratory diseases in the fattening calf are of great importance against the background of animal welfare, antibiotic use and resistance as well as economic considerations. There is an urgent need to develop and implement measures that lead to improvement in practice. The present project is to follow on from an ongoing project in which the calves are protected from respiratory pathogens during the transport of fattening operations. You can look forward to that.
