Full description

Bethesda Spital AG | online since : 13.10.2026

As a successful Basel clinic with a private character, the Bethesda Spital combines top medicine with first-class service and a unique feel-good atmosphere. With its five focus areas: women’s medicine, pregnancy & childbirth, musculoskeletal system, rehabilitation and palliative care, our 6,000 inpatients benefit from state-of-the-art treatment centers under one roof – in the middle of one of the most beautiful hospital parks in northwestern Switzerland.

For our interdisciplinary private department with a focus on surgery, orthopaedics, rheumatology, musculoskeletal rehabilitation, internal medicine and gynecology, we are looking for a committed Dipl from January 2027 or by appointment. Care professional HF/FH (60 - 100%).

Their tasks

  • Control and implementation of the care process according to NANDA, NIC and NOC taxonomy and thus ensure structured, professionally sound and professional care
  • Creating a trusting and empathetic relationship with patients and their relatives
  • Co-responsibility for the quality of processes and results as well as active participation in their continuous development in the department
  • Participation and representation of care in interprofessional case meetings
  • Practical support for students and learners in cooperation with the main responsible vocational trainees

They bring with them

  • Diploma as a nurse HF or completed bachelor's degree in nursing (FH)
  • A noticeable identification with the guidelines of professional care and the understanding of the care process as a core task of nursing action
  • The ability to make relationships empathetic, appreciative and approaching
  • A pronounced willingness to learn, self-initiative and joy in shaping new things
  • The ability and willingness for open, cooperative cooperation in the interprofessional treatment team

We offer

  • An innovative, future-oriented working environment in which we continuously develop the quality of care together
  • Contemporary organizational structures that promote close interprofessional cooperation and short decision-making paths
  • Independent, responsible work in a team led according to the principles of shared leadership
  • Targeted promotion of your personal and professional development through an attractive further education and training program
  • Access to free sports facilities such as Pilates, Yoga and CrossFit as well as the use of our gym for your health and well-being

Have we aroused your interest? You are welcome to receive further information from Tamara Kamber, Team Leader Care, Tel. 061 315 20 78. We look forward to your online application. Our recruitment process has been defined according to environmental and economic aspects and does not provide for applications by post or email. Postal and e-mail applications will not be considered. For this post, recruitment dossiers are not taken into account.
