The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

The outpatient surgery center, AOP, at the station promises networked medical expertise and competence in a modern environment, needs-based and patient-oriented. Also for the stay after the operation is provided with a zone for additional insured / private patients. Are you looking for a new challenge? Are you motivated and interested in being able to work in a dynamic, small team? Then we look forward to welcoming you as a team member from 1 January 2027 or by arrangement as

Dipl. Expert/Expert Anesthesia Care NDS/HF, 20 - 60 %

You do.

  • Independent anesthesia guide in delegation by the responsible specialist
  • Preparation and assistance in all current regional and general anesthesia procedures
  • Application of up-to-date electronic documentation systems (Medlinq, KISIM)
  • Effective use of modern medicines and techniques
  • Good communication to all interfaces in the OR area
  • Interdisciplinary support of the team in interdisciplinary activities
  • Recognize patient needs and implement them competently

That sets them apart.

  • Diploma as Expert/Expert Anesthesia Care NDS/HF
  • In-depth, many years of experience in anesthesia, conveniently in outpatient setting
  • Very independent work with a great deal of room for manoeuvre, a pronounced sense of responsibility as well as high quality standards for your own work
  • Teamwork, flexibility and resilience as well as process-oriented and economic action
  • Communication in German or French (word and writing) with good knowledge of the other language

They benefit from it.
