Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Private Banking division, we are looking for a: Announcement Real Estate Finance Specialist – Acquisition and Development Your missions:

  • acquire and develop contact with real estate promotion managers and maintain the network of real estate brokers and agents
  • promote mortgage growth by ensuring a competitive watch and promote BCV as a partner
  • advising Private Banking clients, alone or in a duet with a Private Banking advisor on mortgage financing and available solutions,
  • support the credit requests of our customers Private Bankin according to the internal processes in force,
  • ensuring smooth and efficient follow-up of credit requests: be a proposal strength in the management of applications and ensure coordination between different stakeholders,
  • promote 360° Council by addressing the welfare and/or fiscal aspects,
  • monitoring and processing credit events,
  • mastering internal product lines and processes. Your profile:
  • front/advice and/or analysis of mortgage assets,
  • very good knowledge of the Vaudian real estate market and banking financing conditions,
  • 360° guidance (foresight, taxation, etc.),
  • rigor, solution orientation, negotiation capacity and interpersonal skills,
  • perfect command of French and good knowledge of English. If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
