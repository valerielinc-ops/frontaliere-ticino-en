Manager : Store Manager Direct reports (potential): Hospitality Ambassador

  • Welcome Host
  • Catering Host
  • Special Orders Referent; VIC Ambassador
  • After-Sales Ambassador General mission As a key member of the management team, the In-Store Customer Experience Manager guarantees an exceptional customer experience, faithful to Hermes values and perfectly aligned with business strategy. He/she leads hospitality, accompanies the teams and ensures that every interaction — in-store and beyond — is memorable, coherent and fluid. Key responsibilities
  • Excellence Client 1) Experience in Shop
  • Co-design the experience strategy with the Boutique Branch and lead a continuous improvement approach based on field observation and customer feedback.
  • Give the tempo during the morning briefings, centered on the reception, the priorities of the day and the highlights.
  • Ensure unimpeachable support from the first contact: elegant presence on the sales surface, precise accompaniment, constructive feedback.
  • Transform each return (mystery visits, verbatims) into concrete action plans and spread a demanding customer culture.
  • Fluidize traffic and routes to preserve the serenity of spaces and the quality of each exchange.
  • Support teams in sensitive situations (unavailability, claims, specific requests) with tact and sense of solution.
  • Contribute to the work of the "Customer Experience" community in close connection with the Customer Experience Manager. 2) Events and Appointments
  • Welcome and accompany guests during the events and visits of the House, with a sense of signature detail.
  • Pilot the schedule of appointments and assign to the Councillors for smooth support.
  • Design tailor-made appointments (advisor's choice, staging, coordination with the Visual team) for unique moments.
  • Imagine delicate attentions (gifts, personalized messages, invitations) that surprise and loyalty. 3) After Sale Service
  • Define priorities for improvement of the after-sales path and measure its effects.
  • To support Ambassador SAV and ensure that requests (repairs, returns, deliveries) are processed in an unimpaired manner.
  • Support complex files and reassure the client at every step, until the perfect resolution.
  • Customer Development
  • Secure reliable, complete and data collection in accordance with data protection rules.
  • Co-develop clienteling strategies and animate the local community based on customer analyses.
  • Maintain privileged relationships with loyal customers and support the development of sustainable portfolios.
  • Sale and Service
  • Help the team turn each demand into an elegant solution, even under stress.
  • Supervise all services: complaints, appointments, special and custom orders, reservations and "wishes", remote sales.
  • Ambassador of the House and Model Example
  • Incarnate the House's values: integrity, elegance, service excellence.
  • To create models within the team by example and a return of quality.
  • Share history, culture and know-how to feed the imaginary customer.
  • Infuse enthusiasm, generosity and emotion to make the shop a place of surprise and joy.
  • Team Management 1) Team piloting
  • Orchestrating daily life: planning, clear roles, readable goals.
  • Observe on the ground and offer a continuous, accurate and caring return.
  • Optimize the organization (staff, structure, rotations, recruitments) with the Shop Directorate. 2) Talent Development
  • Set progress targets and coach with demand and support.
  • Conduct monthly points and annual evaluation; propose targeted development plans.
  • Identify training needs and conduct sessions where necessary. 3) Commitment and Cohesion
  • Establish positive discipline, mutual respect and open communication.
  • Share course, results and successes; celebrate and activate the directions of progress.
  • Encourage gateways between teams and shops; create formal and informal exchange times.
  • Ensure active participation in key training, briefings and events. Performance indicators
  • Achievement of the store objectives (Sales, KPIs, Mystery Shopping).
  • Quality of customer relationship and progress of satisfaction indicators.
  • Ability to inspire, engage and develop teams.
  • Impact on operational fluidity and customer service management.
  • Exemplary presence on the ground and effective cooperation with all managers. Profile sought
  • Experience confirmed in the management of retail teams.
  • Strong service culture and excellent understanding of the luxury customer journey.
  • Natural leadership, sense of communication and ability to influence positively.
  • Elegance, dynamism, accountability and autonomy.
  • Results orientation, detail and quality commitment.
  • Excellent mastery of computer tools.
  • Agility, curiosity and solution posture. Languages
  • Excellent level in French and English
  • A third language is appreciated
