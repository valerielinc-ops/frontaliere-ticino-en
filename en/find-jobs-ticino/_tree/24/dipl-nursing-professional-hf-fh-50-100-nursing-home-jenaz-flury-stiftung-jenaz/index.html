Full description

With more than 600 employees in the Schiers hospital, the three retirement homes Schiers, Jenaz and Klosters, Spitex, living with service, the crèche, parental counselling and the medical center of Klosters, the Flury Foundation ensures comprehensive and integrated health care in the Prättigau between Grüsch and Klosters. The modern retirement home Jenaz in the beautiful Vorderprättigau offers around 70 residents a loving home. We are looking for a Dipl by 1 November 2026 or by appointment. Nurse HF/FH or Health Care Professional 80 – 100%

Your responsibilities include

  • A needs-oriented, individual care and care of our residents
  • Care based on the care process and corresponding documentation
  • Taking on nursing responsibility

Your profile

  • Completed training as a health professional or Nurse HF/FH or an equivalent foreign diploma recognised by the SRC
  • Joy and patience in dealing with old people, teamwork and reliability
  • High level of technical and social competence combined with a reliable and independent working method
  • Interest in new challenges and a good personal work organization

We offer you

  • A modern, timely and light-flooded living and care environment
  • A varied and challenging job in an innovative company
  • Working independently with a competent and motivated team
  • A collegial working environment
  • Attractive, contemporary employment conditions and generous social benefits
  • An own crèche and other interesting benefits for employees First questions will be glad to answer Anna Katharina Castelberg, Head of Care Service, telephone +41 81 308 01 07.
