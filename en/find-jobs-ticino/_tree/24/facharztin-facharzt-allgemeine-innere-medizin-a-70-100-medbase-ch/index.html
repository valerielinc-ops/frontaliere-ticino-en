Welcome to Medbase Baden

Our modern medical center is centrally located in the historic city of Baden, directly next to the train station. We look after our patients interdisciplinary and quality-oriented. Our team, consisting of around 20 employees, consists of specialists from the fields of general internal medicine, gynecology and obstetrics and ORL and MPAs. At Medbase Baden, we ensure a balanced work-life balance. Working part-time is possible in all functions and working hours can be flexible.

You can move that. As a specialist you offer comprehensive care and ensure a high quality of patient care. At Medbase, you are part of an interdisciplinary team that supports you in your professional development and sets high standards.

  • Self-responsible care and basic care of family doctor patients
  • Ensuring the best possible medical care
  • Collaboration in the team with collegial representation
  • Support for the further development of practical offers
  • Internal and external training for quality improvement

What you bring

  • Completed studies and specialist training for General Internal Medicine
  • Commitment to holistic patient care
  • Solution-oriented and team-capable working method
  • Familiarity with electronic medical history
  • Communication Strength and Empathy
  • Good knowledge of German (min. B2)

To strengthen our team, we search immediately or by agreement.

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in ambulatory healthcare. This means services ranging from health promotion to medication and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from different disciplines work hand in hand to provide people across Switzerland with integrated and world-class medical care. With logistics services and digital solutions, we support doctors so that they too can fully concentrate on patients.

The Medbase Group includes the brands Medbase Pharmacies, Zur Rose, zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare.
