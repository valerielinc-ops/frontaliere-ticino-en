Full description

For our outpatient clinic for specialized psychotherapy and psychosomatics in Baden, we are looking for you as tasks by agreement:

  • Taking responsibility for the independent assessment and treatment of adults with psychiatric illnesses, with the treatment focus being in the area of trauma-related disorders, personality disorders and chronic depression
  • Psychiatric support for the specialist psychologists working in the practice (arrangement model)
  • Close cooperation with colleagues in the outpatient clinic and across locations with the psychiatric clinic Königsfelden including the outpatient clinics, the partner institutions and established colleagues in the region
  • Participation in on-call services as part of the PDAG service concept (approx. 6 weekend days per year with 100% workload) Requirements:
  • FMH specialist training (equivalent) for psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • Preferably knowledge in the treatment of people with trauma-related disorders (EMDR, IRRT, NET, etc.), personality disorders (DBT, TFP, mentalization-based therapy (MBT), among others), chronic depression (CBASP).
  • Native language German or possession of a level C1 language certificate
  • Initiative, flexibility and good communication skills as well as skillful handling of psychological stress
  • Experience in the area of ​​outpatient psychotherapeutic treatment
  • Enjoyment of psychotherapeutic work
  • Confident use of common office applications and knowledge of billing according to TARMED We offer: Our employees are the heart of our company.
