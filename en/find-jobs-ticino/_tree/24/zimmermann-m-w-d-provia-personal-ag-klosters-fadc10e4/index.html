Full description

For our customers in the region, we are looking for motivated and reliable carpenters for varied uses in timber construction.

Your tasks

  • General carpentry work in new construction and conversion
  • Erecting and mounting of wooden structures
  • Assembly of roof trusses, facades and elementary structures
  • Interior fittings and renovations
  • Arrangement and assembly work
  • Work according to plans and technical drawings
  • Self-employment of assigned work
  • Compliance with safety and quality rules

Your profile

  • Completed training as Zimmermann EFZ or comparable training beneficial
  • Professional experience in timber construction
  • Good craftsmanship skills and technical understanding
  • Independent, reliable and accurate working method
  • Physically resilient and vertigo-free
  • Good knowledge of German is advantageous
  • Advantageous driving licence Cat B
  • Own vehicle is advantageous

We offer

  • Varied assignments with different customers
  • Contemporary and fair employment conditions
  • Attractive remuneration according to qualification and experience
  • Possibility of long-term temporary employment
  • Option for permanent employment if suitable
  • Personal support throughout the operation
  • Exciting projects in timber construction
