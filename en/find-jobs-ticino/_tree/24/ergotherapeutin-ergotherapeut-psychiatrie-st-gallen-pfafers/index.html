Full description

Look forward to an exciting area of work in which you will work in the day-to-day clinical and inpatient area. In a pleasant working atmosphere, a varied environment awaits you, in which you can contribute your expertise and help shape the future of psychiatry in St.Gallen together with us. Your tasks

  • Together with our interdisciplinary treatment team, you will support patients with subacute psychological and social problems in the daily clinical setting and actively contribute to their individual treatment.
  • After in-depth training, you will lead ergotherapeutic group therapies in the daily clinical setting and accompany the patients in a structured way through the entire therapy process.
  • In the inpatient acute psychiatric setting, you take over ergotherapeutic treatments in various individual and group therapies in the gerontopsychiatric area and bring your expertise specifically into the treatment.
  • As part of a dedicated interdisciplinary treatment team, you accompany patients with psychiatric diseases and related health problems and support them in their social and professional reintegration.
  • Your versatile day-to-day work covers the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases and offers you the opportunity to use your ergotherapeutic expertise in different treatment contexts and to coordinate closely with other departments and assignments. Your profile
  • You have completed training as an occupational therapist HF/FH and have a professional training permit from the Canton of St.Gallen or meet the requirements to obtain it before taking up the job.
  • Ideally, you will have professional experience in adult and/or gerontop psychiatry as well as experience in carrying out group therapies and enjoy a versatile field of work and appreciative treatment of patients.
  • Interest in the implementation of ZEPS groups (experience, perspective creation) with a focus on social participation and everyday activities is beneficial.
  • Creativity, innovation and design skills as well as the motivation to actively develop therapy offers and treatment pathways characterize your work method. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations.
