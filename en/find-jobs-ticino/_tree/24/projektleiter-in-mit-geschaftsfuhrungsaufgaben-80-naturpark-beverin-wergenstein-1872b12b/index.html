Full description

The Beverin Regional Nature Park in Graubünden extends around the Piz Beverin. Together with the population and the canton, we strive for sustainable economic and social development, strengthen the local economy and promote tourism close to nature and culture, and are active in preserving and enhancing the rich biodiversity, beautiful landscapes and high-quality cultural assets.

Want to be part of our team? As of January 1, 2027 or by agreement we are looking for:n

Project manager:in with management tasks 80%

In cooperation with local actors, municipal authorities as well as project and financing partners, you implement effective and sustainable projects and ensure the management for the sponsoring association of the project regional development (PRE) Safiental.

You can move this

  • Project initiation and development: Initiating and developing projects that contribute to the sustainable development of the Safiental. Preparation of financing applications and acquisition of third-party funds.
  • Project management and implementation: management and implementation of effective and sustainable projects in cooperation with the two clients, project and financing partners and authorities.
  • Advice and support: Project-related advice to stakeholders involved, participation in relevant individual initiatives and support to local organisations, for example in the organisation of events.
  • Networking and communication: focal point for project ideas from the region, networking of stakeholders and participation in working groups. Regular exchange and communication with the municipality of Safiental, the site development commission and the board of the association PRE Safiental.
  • Coordination and management: Ensuring the coordination, administration and management of the association PRE Safiental in coordination with the management office of the Beverin Nature Park.
  • Project monitoring and controlling: coordination and monitoring of the implementation of the PRE subprojects, monitoring of project progress, controlling and reporting.
  • Public relations: organisation of media events, drafting and editing of media releases and general public relations.

You bring this with you

  • Higher or higher education (agronomy, environmental sciences, etc.)
  • Knowledge of agriculture and food industry as well as experience with PRE projects
  • Experience in project management and business administration
  • Open-minded, initiative personality with good conceptual and organizational skills
  • Joy of developing interdisciplinary projects together with different partners:
  • Implementation-oriented and pragmatic working methods, reliability and teamwork
  • Related to Safiental and network in the region beneficial

Place of work: Versam and Wergenstein GR.
