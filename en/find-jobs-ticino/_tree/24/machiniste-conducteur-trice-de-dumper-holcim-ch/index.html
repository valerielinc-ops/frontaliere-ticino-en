Full description

Deadline for application: 13 November 2026

We are strengthening our team in our Eclepens career!

Do you enjoy driving heavy vehicles, are you comfortable on the ground and want to evolve in a dynamic environment where safety and team spirit are essential? Then this job is for you!

What awaits you

  • Mainly conduct dumpers for the transport of limestone and feed the crusher
  • Participate in the smooth running of career operations
  • Perform daily checks, first level maintenance and cleaning of equipment
  • Contributing on an ad hoc basis to other career-related work, as required
  • Respect and strictly enforce safety (H&S) and environmental protection rules

What you bring

  • Experience in the operation of construction equipment, ideally in career or in civil engineering
  • Licence M7 (dumper) required; M2/M3 permits are an asset
  • Good control over heavy equipment and safety sensitivity
  • Autonomy, responsibility and reliability
  • Good teamwork and communication skills
  • Flexibility, motivation and commitment to career activities
