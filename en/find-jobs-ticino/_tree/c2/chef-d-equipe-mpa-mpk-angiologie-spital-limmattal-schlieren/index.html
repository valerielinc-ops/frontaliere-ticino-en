  • Team leader MPA/MPK Angiology 80%, as of July 1st, 2026 Apply now The Limmattal Hospital thrives on interdisciplinary collaboration. The medical administration team members support the medical profession in all administrative matters and also work closely with nursing and all other areas. The entire medical administration is under one overall management, which further strengthens and simplifies interdisciplinary cooperation. In addition: LIMMI is the right place for you if a family atmosphere and a modern workplace are important to you.
