Full description

Tasks

Your tasks include regenerating and finishing the prepared food. It is up to you to prepare simple and seasonal food. Furthermore, the appealing preparation of the meals and the implementation of the lunch and evening service are part of your daily routine. This also includes compliance with and assurance of operational and legal hygiene regulations. Joy and knowledge in dealing with food and preparing food are a matter of course for you.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience in a kitchen of community gastronomy
  • Self-employed, careful and reliable work
  • Flexibility and teamwork
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Structured and efficient working methods
  • Understanding and appreciating the needs of older people

We offer

  • 5 weeks holiday, from 45 years 6 weeks, from 55 years 7 weeks
  • functional and performance-related remuneration, as well as attractive social benefits
  • Acting responsibly in a motivated and supportive working environment

Domicil offers senior citizens stationary services in care and care. With more than 20 locations and around 1,600 employees, Domicil is the number 1 in long-term care in the canton of Bern. Under the umbrella of Concara, we are working together with Spitex Bern for integrated all-round care.

For our kitchens at Domicil Alexandra and Egelmoos we are looking for a committed and reliable personality who actively supports our kitchen team. You enjoy cooking, a sense of good food and contribute to a tasty meal with your work. Together with the team, you ensure good food at the location and contribute to the satisfaction of the residents.
