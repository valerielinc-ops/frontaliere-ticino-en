Full description

Your tasks

  • You mount mechanical assemblies and machines using technical drawings and work instructions.
  • You set assemblies and make the necessary adjustments.
  • You perform functional and quality controls and document the work performed.
  • You ensure the careful handling of tools and resources.
  • You support the continuous improvement of our assembly processes.
  • You work closely with production, quality and logistics.

Your profile

  • Completed basic technical or craft training, for example as a production mechanic, mechanic practitioner, polymechanic or in a comparable profession.
  • Experience in the assembly of machines or technical assemblies is an advantage.
  • Good technical understanding and craftsmanship.
  • Enjoy practical work and dealing with technical products.
  • Independent, careful and reliable working methods as well as a good quality awareness.
  • Teamwork and enjoyment of working with different specialist areas.

Your benefits

Your new place of work in Dierikon is newly built and equipped with the latest technology and work equipment. Your workplace is located in one of the modern open-plan offices, where there are also inspiring meeting areas and a large terrace for various employee events. Komax AG offers its employees flexible working time models and home office. The further training of employees is important to Komax AG and offers, for example, free internal English courses or access to selected attractive learning platforms. At the Dierikon location, there are also modern break rooms and the possibility of a discounted lunch in a neighbouring partner restaurant.
