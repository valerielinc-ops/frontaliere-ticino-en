Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process.

Your tasks

  • You accompany and advise young people and young adults on the way to an independent lifestyle and include their individual living spaces.
  • You are responsible for two comprehensive, interdisciplinary case tours with a systemic focus and involving the respective systems of origin.
  • You work decentrally at various locations of partially supervised living in the city of Lucerne.
  • You take part in team meetings and supervisions and participate in systemic and psychiatric specialist and case counselling.

Your profile

  • Studies in social pedagogy or social work (FH/HF) or a comparable education.
  • As a trustworthy person, you meet people in an appreciative way and shape relationships that are engaged and clarifying.
  • Ideally, you will bring professional experience in counselling as well as in the partial or inpatient or therapeutic area.
  • You work solution-oriented, and also approach challenging situations with a pinch of humor and act calmly and decisively in conflict situations.
  • You value flexible, self-organized working hours and are open to individually planned assignments.
  • Fixed Fridays are possible in consultation with the team.

The Utenberg Children's and Youth Settlement employs around 90 people. With childcare and housing services, we support around 75 children, young people and families in their development so that they can lead a self-determined life.
