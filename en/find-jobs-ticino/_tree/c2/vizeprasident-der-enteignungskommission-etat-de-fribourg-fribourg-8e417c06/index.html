Position description

The Expropriation Commission is aware of all the questions of estimating that the Expropriation Act does not fall within the jurisdiction of another authority, as well as claims for compensation for material expropriation. It also exercises the powers conferred by other provisions of cantonal law on the expropriation judge, either formally or by equivalent expressions (art. 3), e.g. claims for compensation of a proprietor to his neighbour or neighbour under the Planning and Construction Act.

Profile desired

  • The Judicial Council is seeking a vice-president or vice-president who holds a lawyer's, a bachelor's or a master's degree in law, or has experience in the judicial field, with knowledge of the administrative law of the Fribourg region, particularly in the fields of construction and spatial planning (planning). Some availability for attendance at meetings and for drafting is required. This is an ancillary activity (approx. 10%) carried out irregularly
  • Citizenship active on the cantonal level. Persons of foreign nationality must hold an establishment permit and have been domiciled in the canton for at least five years. Candidates must not be the subject of an act of lack of property, not have been criminally convicted for acts incompatible with the function
  • Mastering the German language with very good knowledge of the French language
