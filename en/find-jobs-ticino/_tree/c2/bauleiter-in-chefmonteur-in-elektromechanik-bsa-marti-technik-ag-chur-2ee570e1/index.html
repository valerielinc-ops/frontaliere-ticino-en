BAULEITER\IN / CHEFMONTEUR\IN ELEKTROMECHANIK BSA Started in 1922 as a small family business, it has grown over the decades. Today, more than 80 Group companies from all areas of the construction industry belong to us. One of them is us, Technik AG. As an internationally active, innovative technology company, Technik AG offers various products and services in the fields of conveying technology, electrical engineering, formwork construction, special machine construction and infrastructure facilities. Our products and services are characterized by the highest quality, forward-looking technology and customer-oriented thinking. In order to strengthen our team in Chur, we are looking for a committed and motivated personality who, with passion and team spirit, advances our common goals. BAULEITER\IN / CHEFMONTEUR\IN ELEKTROMECHANIK BSA You can move it with us. You manage your building posts and ensure the professional and timely handling of your work. You represent Technik AG competently externally and against the clientele, planners and suppliers. With your competence and your performance, you gain confidence and thus follow-up orders. You lead ahead and plan your fellow workers’ resources. You promote and develop your team and make sure that the guidelines are adhered to. As part of the entire construction process, you take responsibility for AVOR, extent and billing.
