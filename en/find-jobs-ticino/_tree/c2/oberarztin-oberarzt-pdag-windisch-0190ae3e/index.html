Over the area

The Clinic for Forensic Psychiatry (KFP) consists of the expert opinion centre, the Center for Forensic Psychiatry Stationary (ZFPS) and the Center for Forensic Psychiatry Outpatient (ZFPA). The expert body produces a large number of expert opinions for administrative contracting authorities every year. The ZFPS comprises six wards with a total of 74 beds, on which mentally ill offenders are treated within the framework of court-ordered therapeutic measures (Art. 59 StGB) as well as crisis interventions are carried out on patients from prisons and investigation prisons. The ZFPA runs a forensic ambulatory and offers a prison psychiatric consiliary and liaison service.

Tasks

  • They are involved in providing basic care to the inmates of the JVA, triagulating and prioritizing prisoner concerns
  • You will maintain a collegial communication with the health service, the prison staff and the management of the JVA Lenzburg and/or the cooperation partners of the ZFPA, taking into account the medical secrecy
  • They supervise the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic training of the assistant doctors in the JVA Lenzburg and/or the ZFPA

  • Possibility of obtaining the main title Forensic Psychiatry and Psychotherapy
