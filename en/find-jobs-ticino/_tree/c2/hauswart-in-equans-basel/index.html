Full description

Do you want to take responsibility, look after varied objects and make a real difference? Then you are exactly right with us!

You can move that with us.

In this versatile function, you manage several objects on site and are the contact person for our customers.

Your tasks include

  • Operation and maintenance of several technical and infrastructural properties
  • Small-scale repair work for domestic equipment
  • Coordination and control of external craftsmen in maintenance and renovation work
  • Creating offers for our customers
  • Assistance in organising and implementing projects
  • Support for technical and organizational issues
  • Owner and management contact person
  • Maintenance and maintenance of building and outdoor facilities
  • Ensuring a well-maintained appearance of the property
  • Picetting missions

That's what makes you.

You live service and work with conviction for quality and reliability. You bring with you:

  • Completed training as a specialist:woman maintenance/housekeeper (or similar training) or craft profession
  • Independent, structured working method and sense of responsibility
  • Strong service mentality and organisational talent
  • Solution-oriented thinking and openness to administrative tasks
  • Very good German skills in Word & Writing as well as good IT skills
  • Readiness for Picetting Operations
  • Valid driving licence Cat B and residence in the Basel/Baselland region

That's what we offer you.

  • An exciting, responsible task with plenty of room for manoeuvre.
  • An experienced team with an open corporate culture.
  • Contemporary employment conditions and good social benefits.
