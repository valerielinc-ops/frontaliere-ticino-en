Full description

Who are we?

The SASoc Social Action Service, the competent authority in the canton of Fribourg, in particular for the compensation of LAVI victims, the collection and advances of maintenance contributions and the coordination of social assistance, offers persons at the end of their legal training and in parallel with their preparation for the examination of the bar, a position of legal trainee at 50-60% for a period of min. Six months.

Why join us?

The State-employer encourages the reconciliation of work and private life and offers an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

  • Drafting of LAVI Compensation Administrative Decisions / Collection and Advance of Maintenance Contributions
  • Representation of State interests before the judicial authorities and cantonal prefectures in civil and criminal proceedings
  • Legal issues in the processing of social assistance cases

Profile desired

  • Master's degree in law, legal training completed, preparation in progress for exams for obtaining a lawyer's certificate
  • Mastery of the French or German language with very good knowledge of the other language
