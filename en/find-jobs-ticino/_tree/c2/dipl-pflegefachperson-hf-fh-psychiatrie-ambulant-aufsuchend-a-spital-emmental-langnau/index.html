Full description

The psychiatry of the Emmental Hospital: small but fine – we offer demanding jobs for doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers and administrative staff. We attach great importance to personal development and consideration of our own resources. In our company, which is manageable in terms of size, the personality of each individual employee shapes the way we work together and deal with each other. We understand individuality as enrichment. We actively promote interprofessional cooperation. We are a successful training facility of the BZ Pflege.

For our outpatient care in the Ambulatorium Langnau we are looking for from 1. March 2027 or by agreement

Your tasks

  • You accompany and support people with mental illnesses as part of outpatient and visiting treatments
  • Together with an interprofessional team, you will design individual treatment processes and take over the case management
  • You plan and lead group therapies
  • You maintain a close cooperation with various professionals, referees, relatives and authorities

Your profile

  • You have a diploma as a nurse HF or FH with a focus on psychiatry
  • You like to work independently and are just as happy to join the team
  • You appreciate the interprofessional cooperation and enjoy the independent design of your work
  • You take responsibility, are proactive and meet different people and situations with openness
  • For your visiting activity you have your own vehicle
