Full description

We at CSS help people live a healthy life – whatever life has in store for them. Maybe our mission will soon be yours too? We would be delighted. We are a diverse group of ambitious people who want to make a difference. Some of us have done the KV, others come from the retail trade, the hospitality industry or from another professional corner. Everyone brings their experience. What unites us is our passion to be there for our customers. We help each other, stick together and laugh a lot. And we prefer to celebrate success together in a team. This is what your everyday life looks like

  • At the heart of it: For leads (e.g. from online inquiries or deals), you focus on the acquisition of new customers as well as the retention and development of existing customers in close cooperation with self-distribution.
  • From the first lead to the successful conclusion of insurance: you accompany our customers with commitment and find tailor-made solutions for their individual needs.
  • You can consult via video call, telephone and e-mail – in excellent German and French. Other languages are a plus.
  • With a targeted approach, you contact existing and potential customers – through marketing campaigns, follow-up campaigns or customer loyalty programs – and advance our sales initiatives.
  • You work closely with your colleagues and you support each other in achieving the common goals. You bring that with you
  • First things first: You realize that you like to be in contact with people – you approach them openly and your positive charisma is contagious.
  • Completed vocational training in the commercial sector, in the retail trade, in customer dialogue or another exciting profession – we are open to this.
  • You bring at least 2 years of experience in customer consulting and at most already have the VBV certification. If not, you can learn this in our training courses. What matters is your passion and perseverance.
  • You speak German or French at native language level and have good knowledge of words and writing in the other language (at least level B2).
  • You can empathize well with your counterpart, listen attentively and love to win others in conversation with convincing arguments.
  • Digitalization is familiar to you – you are well-versed in using the computer.
  • So that you can start optimally in Lausanne, you are ready to complete the training during a few months at our location in Root. Alternatively, it is also possible to work permanently at the root location.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Relax during the holidays: You work 41 hours a week and enjoy 6 weeks of vacation.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
