Field Service Engineer Eastern Switzerland (d/f/m) Workload: 100% Location: Losone Job type: Unlimited Your tasks

  • Carry out commissioning, repairs and maintenance work on milling machines directly at our customers’ sites in the

Eastern Switzerland region

  • Perform upgrades and overhauls on existing customer equipment
  • Analyse technical faults and ensure professional, fast and reliable troubleshooting and problem resolution
  • Work closely with our diagnostics team and maintain regular technical and professional exchange
  • You will primarily work directly at our customers’ sites in Eastern Switzerland, a few times a year, you will also

work at our production site in Biel Your profile

  • Completed technical vocational training as well as experience working with machine tools, ideally with modern

high-speed and high-performance milling machines

  • Knowledge of pneumatics, hydraulics and sensor technology
  • Knowledge of Heidenhain and Siemens control systems is an advantage
  • Good hardware and software skills, as well as proficiency in MS Office
  • Fluent German and very good English skills
