Full description

Motivated by an apprenticeship in polymechanics? Ready to become a reference in your profession through quality training in a leading company? Join us!

The polymer engineer makes mechanical parts, tools and devices using traditional or digitally controlled machines. On the basis of plans, he/she defines the processing stages of complex parts and uses industrial drawing software as programming aid. In collaboration with other people, he/she processes orders, monitors projects, develops construction solutions and creates technical documents.

This learning is suitable for young people who are interested in mechanics, like precise work and have good manual skill.

Profile

  • Confirmed aptitudes for mathematics, physics and technical drawing
  • Good sense of representation in space
  • Logical and methodical spirit

To apply

  • a curriculum vitae
  • a cover letter
  • your school reports for the last three years.
