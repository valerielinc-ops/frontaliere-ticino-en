Full description

Precise components are produced on modern CNC lathes. A supportive team environment and demanding technical tasks shape everyday work. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Manufacture of precise components on modern CNC lathes
  • Independent programming with CAM programming systems
  • Setting up and operating the CNC lathes
  • Monitor and optimize manufacturing processes to increase efficiency
  • Collaboration in the team for timely processing of customer orders

Requirements

  • Completed training as a polymechanic
  • In-depth knowledge of CNC technology
  • Ideally experience in dealing with CAM systems
  • Familiarity with Fanuc control and DMGMori machines is an advantage
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Offer

  • Diverse and challenging tasks with technically demanding components
