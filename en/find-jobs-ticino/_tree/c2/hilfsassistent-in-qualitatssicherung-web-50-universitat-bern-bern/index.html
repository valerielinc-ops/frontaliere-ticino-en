About us

With over 19,000 students and around 8,000 employees, the University of Bern is one of the largest universities in Switzerland. It is excellently networked nationally and internationally and at the same time anchored in the region. With her research she sets a worldwide accents – especially in medicine, space and climate research she is at the forefront. The Department of Communication & Marketing (AKM) is part of the Rectorate and is responsible for internal and external communication at the University of Bern. For the area of online marketing, we are looking for a dedicated assistant for quality assurance web. In this position, you are responsible for regularly checking the display of UniBE web content, ensuring accessibility and documenting detected defects. The term of office shall be two years.
