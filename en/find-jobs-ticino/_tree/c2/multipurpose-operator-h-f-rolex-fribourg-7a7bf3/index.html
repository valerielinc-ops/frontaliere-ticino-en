Full description

Introduction For our Production division, we are looking for a versatile machining operator (H/F) whose main mission will be the manufacture of certain clothing watch components in the machining industry. Responsibilities

  • Manufacture and monitor the production of different watch components on machining machines
  • Ensure dimensional and aesthetic control of the parts produced
  • Participate in continuous improvement efforts Profile
  • Professional experience in one or more of the following areas: CNC shooting, milling, 5-axis grinding, mono- and multi-brochite cleavage
  • Basic module in watchmaking, AFP of Mechanical Practitioner an asset
  • Ability to ensure settings and tool changes
  • Excellent visual acuity and rigour
  • Easy with the use of production software
  • Autonomous, organized and equipped with a good team spirit
  • Rigourous and procedurally respectful
  • Availability for shift work (2x8) Conclusion We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions and top social benefits. Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
