Full description

For an established, interdisciplinary Swiss trust and consulting company, we are looking for a committed and customer-oriented personality as a trustee at the Chur location. In an environment where team spirit and humanity are the focus, you will take on a wide range of tasks in the holistic support of SMEs and private individuals and benefit from the direct exchange with internal tax and legal experts. Immediately or by agreement, we are looking for you!

This is your job

  • Mandate support: Comprehensive support, advice and support for natural and legal persons.
  • Finance & Wages: Independent management of financial and payroll accounts and preparation of interim and annual accounts.
  • Taxes & Compliance: Creation of tax returns for individuals and companies including handling of all related compliance tasks.
  • Tax Law: Active support in tax appeal proceedings in cooperation with internal experts.

This is what you bring with you

  • Training & experience: Completed basic commercial training and/or practical professional experience in the trust or tax sector.
  • IT skills: Good MS Office knowledge and ideally experience in dealing with common trust tools such as Abacus or bexio.
  • Languages: Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Any other language is beneficial.
  • Personality: Strong numerical flair, exact and structured working methods, analytical thinking skills and an appreciative team and customer orientation.

This is what awaits you from the customer

  • Interdisciplinary network: Competent support by experienced professionals from the fields of finance & accounting, tax experts and lawyers.
  • Work-life balance: Flexible working time models with home office options and a healthy work-life balance.
  • Culture & Development: Exciting portfolio of dossiers, active promotion of your professional training and a motivated team atmosphere at the Chur location.
