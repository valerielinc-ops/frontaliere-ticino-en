Full description

A socioprofessional teacher Specialist TSA & complex situations at 80%, 80% Deployed at several sites, the Lavigny Institution consists of a neurological hospital with outpatient consultations at two sites, a housing department for adults with disabilities, intellectual disability or physical problems, a specialized school and a socio-professional department. For the "Eventail" socialising workshops sector, located on the Lavigny site, we are looking for a:

A socio-professional teacher Specialist TSA & complex situations 80%

Do you want to put your expertise at the service of people with disabilities and contribute to accompaniments that make sense? Do you like working as a team, developing creative solutions and changing practices? Then this post is for you!

Your Mission

In our socializing workshops, you will accompany people with disorders of the spectrum of autism (TSA) and behavioural challenges.

Your main responsibilities will be to

  • support individuals or groups, taking into account their needs and projects;
  • provide appropriate support for autonomy, participation and well-being;
  • collaborate with the network of accompanied persons and the various professionals involved;
  • administrative follow-up of the accompaniment and workshop: reports, summaries, computerised files, etc.;
  • contribute to the development and evolution of practices around autism and complex situations;
  • share your expertise and, depending on your skills, participate in the training of the institution's staff in the field of autism.

Your profile You are a committed, autonomous, organized and solutions oriented person. You appreciate situations that require reflection, adaptation and creativity.

You have

  • a diploma of socio-professional teacher (MSP), teacher ES or HES;
  • specialization in autism and/or complex situations;
  • at least 3 years of experience in accompanying people with ASD and/or challenging behaviours;
  • good knowledge of TEACCH, PECS or other tools adapted to the person with whom they are accompanied;
  • pedagogical skills and an interest in knowledge transfer;
  • excellent mastery of common computer tools;
  • Good adaptability and versatility;
  • a developed sense of responsibility, organization and collaboration;
  • a creative, open and innovative spirit;
  • an ability to challenge and work interdisciplinaryly;
  • the driving licence, category B.

We offer you

  • A varied and stimulating position within an engaged team;
  • a human and caring working environment;
  • the possibility of making a concrete contribution to the evolution of our workshops and practices;
  • opportunities for professional and personal development;
  • working conditions in accordance with the Convention on the Rights of the Child;
  • a business restaurant at preferential rates;
  • free parking available.

If you want to put your skills at the service of an institutional project rich in human values, we look forward to receiving your application.
