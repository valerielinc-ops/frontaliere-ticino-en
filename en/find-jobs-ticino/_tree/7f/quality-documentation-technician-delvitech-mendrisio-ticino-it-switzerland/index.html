Full description

Support quality in Delvitech's production: manage non-conformities and corrective actions and keep our quality documentation up to date. Job Summary The Quality & Documentation Technician will support the maintenance and continuous improvement of quality standards within the Production department, working closely with the Manufacturing Manager and collaborating with Production, Engineering and Supply Chain. The role includes quality inspections on components, assemblies and complete machines, as well as managing non-conformities, monitoring corrective actions and analyzing quality data to improve production processes. Key Responsibilities

  • Quality control: inspect components, assemblies and complete machines to ensure compliance with defined quality standards.
  • Process control: verify that production activities are performed according to approved procedures and requirements.
  • Non-conformities: record, analyze and monitor production quality issues.
  • Corrective actions: support the definition, implementation and follow-up of corrective actions.
  • Control plans: support the definition and continuous update of production quality controls.
  • Quality data: monitor defects, rework and key quality indicators.
  • Reporting: prepare and maintain production quality reports.
  • Cross-functional support: collaborate with the relevant departments to investigate and resolve quality issues.
  • Continuous improvement: propose improvements to production processes and quality controls. Requirements
  • Technical diploma, degree or equivalent education.
  • Previous experience in production, quality or quality control.
  • Ability to read and understand technical documentation and specifications.
  • Ability to analyze non-conformities and support corrective actions.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
  • Good organizational skills and ability to manage priorities and deadlines.
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
  • Good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Continuous improvement mindset.
  • Good knowledge of English. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
