Full description

Portrait Teach in an exciting city quarter near Uni and PH. Cycle 1 Länggasse comprises 10 kindergarten, 3 basic levels as well as five 1st and five 2nd classes with around 430 children. The school is central and very well accessible by public transport or Velo. For a first class in the Schulhaus Hochfeld we are looking for a motivated teacher from 01.02.2027 to 04.04.2027.

Tasks

29% as a deputy in a 1st class, on Tuesday 4 lessons in the half class and on Wednesday 4 lessons whole class, 1 February to 4 April 2027. (Attention: from 8 to 12) February are holidays in Bern

They create learning spaces in which our students can have diverse and challenging experiences. With encouragement and targeted support, you accompany them to break new ground. Trusting and appreciative relationships form the basis of your pedagogical action for you. Collaboration in the class team as well as with the parents is a matter of course for you. Respectful cooperation and active cooperation with other class teams, in the cycle and in the entire college are an important part of your school day.
