Full description

In line with our vision "We drive innovation to improve people's lives", Hamilton companies have been providing solutions for the healthcare industry since 1950. We are an innovative leader in the production of ventilators, automated pipetting and sample storage, as well as the development of process sensors. Our Business Unit Operations produces parts for all Hamilton companies, from injection-molded parts to metal and plastic parts. Our goal is to produce as many parts in-house as possible. This way, we remain agile, independent and efficient. ## Responsibilities

  • Responsible for the strategic orientation of the Electronics/Electrocomponents category in the industrial environment
  • Develop and implement the category strategy in line with the procurement and operations strategy
  • Participation in development projects and coordination of procurement activities within the strategic procurement
  • Responsible for a professional, global supplier management (evaluation, selection, assessment and development)
  • Conduct targeted procurement market research and observation, as well as identify new trends
  • Create and negotiate contracts, conduct price and condition negotiations
  • Ensure material availability in the development phases in close cooperation with the operational buyers
  • Adhere to processes, governance and compliance ## Requirements
  • Completed commercial basic education or technical education with commercial further education

  • Several years of experience in strategic procurement in the industrial environment
  • Reliable personality with entrepreneurial thinking
  • Autonomous, structured and meticulous working style
  • Personal initiative, negotiation skills and execution ability
  • Open, communicative and integrated team player
  • Negotiation secure in German and English, in word and writing (level C1) ## What we offer
