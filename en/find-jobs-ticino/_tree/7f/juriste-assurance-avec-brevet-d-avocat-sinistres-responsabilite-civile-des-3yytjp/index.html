Shape your future with us!

Do you want to work in coherence with today's world while opening up the paths of tomorrow with us? Are you passionate about the world of civil liability, or are you fascinated by the world of relationships? We offer you a work environment where reflection is a must, where teamwork is of great value, where flexibility is encouraged and where teleworking is partially possible.

In the Property & Casualty sector, you offer our individual and professional customers more freedom, thanks to innovative products, services of impeccable quality, and simple digital processes. We offer our customers a wide range of standard and individual insurance, legal protection solutions as well as financial products in the field of credit and bonding.

Highlights

  • As part of a five-person team, reporting directly to management, you will independently handle complex professional liability claims, particularly for lawyers, notaries, trustees, and IT service providers.
  • Your area of ​​responsibility will also include handling claims related to corporate liability, cybercrime, and breach of trust.
  • Your work will include clarifying the facts, legally assessing liability and insurance coverage, conducting negotiations with injured parties and their legal representatives, and, if necessary, supporting legal proceedings.
  • In addition, you will support the underwriting team in risk assessment, collaborate on projects, and be required to give presentations at seminars.

What you bring

  • You are proactive and a team player, you know how to assert yourself and negotiate, and you enjoy tackling complex cases.
  • Thanks to your great analytical skills and your ability to quickly grasp complex problems, you are able to solve them quickly
  • You have excellent oral and written communication skills in French. A good knowledge of German and English (spoken and written) is an advantage
  • You are a lawyer and have a Swiss lawyer's certificate, you have a few years of professional experience (preferably in the bar, the judicial field or insurance)
  • An affinity with IT as well as knowledge of civil liability law, corporate law and accounting are a plus that will perfectly complement your profile

What we propose We are convinced that satisfied employees achieve better performance. That's why we offer salary supplements that provide tangible benefits beyond the ordinary.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model), annual working hours, five weeks of vacation (six weeks from age 50), two additional weeks of vacation offered every five years, possibility to purchase up to ten additional vacation days per year

Work-life balance: family-friendly meeting times, 22 weeks of maternity leave, eight weeks of leave for the other parent, childcare subsidies, company family allowances, and days off to care for relatives or in difficult personal situations.

Pensions and insurance: an attractive pension fund offering extensive supplementary benefits and savings options, and up to 30% discount on insurance products.

Flexible benefits: AXA Shareplan (employee participation), CHF 500 per year on the Swibeco benefits platform, discounted lunch or Lunch Card, Smart Working allowance of CHF 200 per year

Mobility: reimbursement of half-fare travelcard, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free electric charging station, AXA bikes to borrow

The right balance at work: free health check-ups, annual health weeks, a variety of physical activity options (online and on-site), massage services, rest areas, Employee Care, an online health portal, and various seminars and events focused on health prevention and mental health.

Development: three days for individual training, wide range of learning and development opportunities, free coaching, financial participation in continuing education courses, promotion of internal mobility, talent development opportunities, seminars for those over 55 (AXA Horizon), involvement in communities on various themes, participation in innovative projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), two days dedicated to volunteer missions (environmental and social).
