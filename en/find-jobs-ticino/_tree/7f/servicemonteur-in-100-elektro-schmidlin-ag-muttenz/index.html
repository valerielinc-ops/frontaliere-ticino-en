Full description

We – Elektro Schmidlin AG – have been at home in the Basel region since 1983 and accompany our customers in the field of electrical engineering. At our headquarters in Muttenz, we employ around 35 people, 8 of whom are apprentices.

To expand our team, we are looking for an electric service installer immediately or by agreement.

Are you an experienced electrician EFZ or an experienced electrician EFZ (or equivalent) and looking for a new challenge? Then you are exactly right with us!

Your tasks

  • Execution of commercial and residential services
  • Implementation and support of smaller conversions and projects
  • Execution of e-mobility and PV installations
  • Active care of existing and new customers
  • Helping to train our learners

Your profile

  • Completed apprenticeship as electrical installer/EFZ
  • Independent and sociable personality
  • Own initiative as well as flexible working methods
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Category B driving licence

We offer you

  • Attractive compensation
  • 40-hour week
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • Own company tablet and company phone
  • Purchasing incentives
  • Various further development opportunities
  • Personal service vehicle
  • High-quality work equipment and modern workwear
  • Family working atmosphere with many long-term employees
  • Great team events

Interested? Apply to Marc Spühler today at m.spuehler@elektro-schmidlin.ch with your complete application documents. We look forward to seeing you.
