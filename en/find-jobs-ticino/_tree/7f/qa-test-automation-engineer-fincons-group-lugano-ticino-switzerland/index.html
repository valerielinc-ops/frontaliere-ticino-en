Full description

Fincons Group is an IT business consulting company that has been designing the digital future of leading companies on international markets for 40 years. Fincons Group built its reputation on foreseeing and interpreting new business models and the rapid evolution of IT systems by building a complete range of services: from research and consulting to design and development, from system integration of leading vendor software solutions right up to application management, supporting clients step by step along their digital transformation journey. Fincons is a multinational with over 2,600 people and several offices worldwide (in Italy, Switzerland, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Belgium and the USA), but above all a Family Company where the founders play a strategic role with commitment and passion, grounding the company in the same principles of a united and caring family.

We firmly believe in the value of cooperation and in the contribution that every idea and intuition can bring. We believe that everyone’s effort can make a difference. Transforming knowledge into a strategic resource is our company mission, and we pursue it with the help of our most strategic asset: our people.

For our Lugano office , we are seeking talented and experienced Senior Engineers with strong hands-on expertise in .NET/C# and Microservices to join our team and contribute to exciting and impactful international projects in the Banking/Financial Services sector.

Roles and Responsibilities

  • Deeply understand business requirements and proactively propose innovative solutions to help clients achieve their goals effectively
  • Develop high-quality software by applying best practices such as TDD, DDD, Clean Code, and SOLID principles to ensure maintainability and excellence
  • Collaborate with architects to define and develop the best technical solution and robust infrastructure
  • Drive quality delivery with a strong emphasis on CI/CD pipelines and seamless deployment processes
  • Ensure top-notch performance, reliability, and scalability of the solutions you build

Qualifications and Technical Skills

  • Bachelor's/Master's Degree, preferably in computer science or engineering.
  • At least 5 years of hands-on experience with C#, .NET, and .NET Core.
  • In-depth knowledge of Microservices Architecture principles and their practical implementation.
  • Solid understanding and application of TDD, Clean Code, and SOLID principles.
  • Experience with DBMS systems like SQL Server, Oracle, MySQL, or DB2, alongside strong SQL knowledge.
  • Proficiency with code quality tools, such as SonarQube, to ensure high standards in software development.
  • Experience with log monitoring tools, including Kibana, Elasticsearch, Grafana, and Prometheus, for system observability and troubleshooting.
  • Strong attention to and application of the main Secure Development Best Practices to safeguard code integrity.
  • Familiarity with the Agile/Scrum methodology is a strong advantage.
  • Fluency in both spoken and written Italian and English is mandatory.

Location Requirement

  • We do not offer a fully remote work policy
  • Candidates should be prepared to relocate to Canton Ticino (Switzerland) or Italian Border Towns (such as Como, Varese etc.)
  • Work-from-home days may be possible according to the Client's policy
