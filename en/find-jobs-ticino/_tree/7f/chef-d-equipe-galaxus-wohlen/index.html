Full description

In the role of Teamleader Major Parts Logistics, you are the central contact person for operational operations and lead your team in day-to-day business. You ensure that high-volume goods are handled safely and efficiently, develop processes further and actively participate in the development of our new large parts logistics organization.

Mansioni

  • You work on projects and actively contribute your team and logistics know-how to the continuous improvement of work processes

Requirements
