Start by agreement as a data manager: in Tumor Center 80 - 80 %

The Tumor Center is a leading institution in the fight against cancer in Canton Graubünden. Our goal is to support patients with first-class medical care and comprehensive care. Data is key to groundbreaking insights – and you can steer them in the right direction. Exciting, innovative projects are waiting to move forward with your expertise in data management. If you like working with complex data sets, then you are in the right place!

Would you like to strengthen our team of data managers with your motivated personality? Perfect, then you are exactly right with us!

These are your tasks

  • You identify and document the required patient data fully and correctly in Celsius37
  • You organize and structure data sets and ensure reliable and valid results
  • You create reports for certification and quality assurance

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as a nurse / nurse specialist or a comparable degree as well as very good medical knowledge
  • Experience in oncology and data management as well as good IT user knowledge
  • A reliable, precise and structured way of working, a quick understanding and enjoyment of working together in a team
