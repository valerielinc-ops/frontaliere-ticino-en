Full description

You understand the needs of the business and at the same time have a good sense of how suitable IT solutions are created from it? Then you are exactly right with us. As an IT Business Analyst, you are the central interface between our departments and IT. You analyze processes, take requirements and develop together with the participants solutions that really work in everyday work.You expect a versatile position with a lot of creative leeway, exciting projects and close exchange with departments, IT teams and external partners. Tasks:

  • As a central contact person, you advise our departments and support them in concretizing their requirements and needs.
  • Together with the departments, you will analyze business processes, identify optimization potential and develop suitable IT solutions
  • You translate technical requirements into structured IT requirements and thus create the basis for a successful implementation
  • For national and international projects, you take responsibility – from conception to implementation and testing to introduction
  • In Demand Management, you evaluate and coordinate new requirements and coordinate them with the relevant IT positions within the Bell Food Group
  • For new solutions and systems, you will accompany selection, feasibility and introduction, working closely with internal and external service providers.
  • You have successfully completed an education or study in the field of computer science, business informatics, business administration or a comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience as an IT Business Analyst, IT Consultant, Application Manager or in a comparable interface function you ideally bring with you
  • Requirements engineering and process analysis are part of your experience and you can collect business requirements in a structured way and translate them into IT solutions
  • You are familiar with project work: You have already accompanied or independently managed IT projects and have a good understanding of the different project phases
  • With different contacts you communicate sovereignly and can mediate between department, IT, key users and external partners.
  • Analytical thinking and pronounced process understanding distinguish you as well as a structured and self-responsible working method
  • You bring a very good knowledge of German
  • Good knowledge of English and enjoyment of working together in an international environment are beneficial
