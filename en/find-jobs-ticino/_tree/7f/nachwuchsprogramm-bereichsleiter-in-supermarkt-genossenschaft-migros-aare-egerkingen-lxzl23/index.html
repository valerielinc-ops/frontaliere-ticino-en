Full description

Start your career as a supermarket manager: With our young talent program, you will be optimally prepared directly in practice for 6-12 months. Develop from a generalist to a leader, either in the areas of food/non-food or checkout/services. Your application will be considered for all branches in the vicinity of your desired workplace.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Aare, Region Gäu, 4622 Egerkingen

Important information

Please submit CV, employment certificates and diplomas in full. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Customer-oriented management of an area
  • Responsibility for the leadership and development of department managers and employees as well as for the promotion of young talent
  • Achieving revenue and cost targets
  • Ensuring the defined processes in your area

Requirements

  • Completed basic or further training: in the retail trade or equivalent training
  • You want to take on responsibility and can already show initial leadership experience
  • Customer-oriented personality with pleasure in sales
  • Good PC knowledge
  • Willingness to work flexible hours
  • Ideally, more than 4 years of professional experience incl. First management experience
  • in German (negotiation certain)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Mobility: participation of an annual subscription to public transport, discount on fuel (Migrol), reduced annual contribution for mobility carsharing and entitlement to a fleet discount

  • Club School Migros: Generous financial participation on self-selected courses

  • Targeted promotion: Promotion of our employees at all levels with targeted personnel development measures

  • Occupational pension: Above-average benefits guarantee the financial security of employees in the third stage of life

  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
