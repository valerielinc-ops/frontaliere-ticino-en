Full description

Outpatient adult psychiatry with high self-determination and interdisciplinary cooperation at eye level. In Muri near Bern, Canton of Bern, we are looking for a senior physician for adult psychiatry and psychotherapy in the range of 60 – 100%. In a group practice of the largest private clinic group for psychiatry and psychotherapy in German-speaking Switzerland, you can expect a modern, interdisciplinary team and a wide range of outpatient treatment. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • They independently carry out investigations and treatments of adults from the entire spectrum of psychiatry and psychotherapy
  • They ensure psychiatric and psychotherapeutic diagnosis and therapy according to the current state of science and the quality requirements
  • You work closely interdisciplinary with the psychologists of the practice
  • You take over the service billing according to TarDoc

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist title for psychiatry and psychotherapy or equivalent foreign specialist title with federal recognition
  • Broad clinical experience and enjoyment of patient-oriented practice
  • Existing registration in the Swiss Medical Professional Register
  • Secure handling of MS Office and administrative software in the hospital environment as well as good knowledge of German in words and writing

That sets them apart.

  • Patient-centred, trusting basic attitude in psychiatric care
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation and independent case management
  • Responsible, structured working method with high professional standards

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Modern, appealing working environment in a team of psychologists, doctors and secretariat
  • Flexible and self-determined design of working hours
  • Careful technical introduction and supervision
  • Various training opportunities with the support of the group as a top training company
  • Exceptional social benefits and family-friendly and modern employment conditions

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
