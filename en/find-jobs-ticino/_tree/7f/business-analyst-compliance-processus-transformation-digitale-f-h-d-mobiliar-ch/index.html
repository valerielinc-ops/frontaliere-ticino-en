Full description

What lies ahead

As a Business Analyst, you are part of an interdisciplinary team that supports the Mobilière in its development in the foresight market through ambitious projects and agile methods.

What you can do with us

As a Business Analyst Compliance, you analyze, design and optimize the processes related to regulatory requirements in the field of life insurance (LBA, AIA, FATCA, LSA, etc.). You also monitor the evolution of reference applications that manage data and definitions common to the whole company, as well as their data architecture. You ensure consistency of end-to-end processes, especially in the areas of sales, advice and customer relationship management.

In close collaboration with Compliance experts, trades and IT within our agile organization, you identify the impacts of regulatory developments on customer journeys and business processes, define the appropriate solutions and accompany their implementation.

You also contribute to the continuous improvement of processes, controls and operational efficiency, while ensuring the right balance between regulatory compliance, customer experience and operational performance.

What you bring

  • University training in business economy, management information technology or training deemed equivalent
  • Mastering Business Analysis methods, tools and good practices as well as analysis, modelling and optimization of complex business processes
  • Strong understanding of compliance regulations and processes, ideally in the life insurance or financial sector.
  • Knowledge of Data Management, including metadata management, cloud, data governance and data products.
  • Excellent relational skills and ability to unite stakeholders with diverse interests
  • Analytical, pragmatic and solutions oriented
  • Current mastery of French and/or German with excellent English skills

Working at home

The Business Transformation Private Planning department is responsible for the conduct and implementation of projects in the field of foresight. It is composed of dynamic and heterogeneous teams of business analytics and project leaders working together in a collegial atmosphere.

Your occupancy rate is negotiable from 80% with access to flexible working time models. Below you will learn more about our commitment as an employer.

What we offer

Balance of working life - privacy

We apply flexible work models and allow 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. We also offer above-average family allowances and a sustainable pension fund.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of in-house courses and allow you to participate in possible programs abroad. We contribute generously to external continuing training related to work.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
