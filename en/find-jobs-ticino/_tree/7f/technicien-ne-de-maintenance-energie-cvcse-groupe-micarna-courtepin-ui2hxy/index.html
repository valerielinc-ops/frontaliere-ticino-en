Full description

Want to put your CVCSE skills at the service of a demanding and exciting industrial environment? At Micarna, a leader in the meat sector in Switzerland and a member of M-Industry, you are actively contributing to ensuring the availability, performance and reliability of our technical facilities. A varied position awaits you, with concrete challenges and lots of ground.

Luogo di lavoro: Micarna Group, Micarna SA - Courtepin, Rte de l'Industrie 25, 1784 Courtepin

Mansioni

  • Preventive and corrective maintenance of energy installations including heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold production, boilers, compressors, regulation and pre-treatment of the site's wastewater
  • Monitoring of the condition and operating parameters of the installations, detection of anomalies, carrying out diagnostics and implementing appropriate corrective actions
  • Implementation of planned maintenance operations, technical checks and safety and performance tests to ensure reliability of equipment
  • Participation in improving plant performance and optimising energy consumption through monitoring of consumption indicators and records
  • Application of internal procedures, WES instructions and regulatory requirements while contributing to continuous improvement of maintenance and operations processes

Required

  • 1-3 years: Experience in the maintenance of technical or energy installations; experience in the industrial, tertiary or on-site environment is an asset
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): CFC or technical training in industrial maintenance, electrical engineering, climate engineering, energy, thermal or automation
  • Good knowledge of multi-technical maintenance as well as HVAC, electrical, thermal or refrigeration facilities
  • Knowledge of safety rules and regulatory requirements for energy installations
  • Good analysis and strong diagnostic capabilities to effectively solve technical problems
  • Self-reliant, rigorous and responsive, enjoying teamwork and a common sense of service and communication
  • You are willing to provide 6-8 x picket service per year
  • You have experience in water treatment
  • A spa patent is an advantage!
  • French (Good knowledge)
  • German (preferably)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Maternity/paternity leave: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave / 8 weeks of paternity leave (4 weeks paid and 4 weeks unpaid)
  • Social benefits: 730 days of daily sickness benefits with full maintenance of net salary
  • Professional provision: Full coverage from the Migros Pension Fund – with first-rate and attractive benefits
  • Food: Balanced menu/buffet at the staff restaurant for a maximum of CHF 7.-
  • Migros Bank: Migros offers higher interest rates on private accounts and investment accounts to its employees.
  • Points Cumulus: Cumulus points are doubled on food products and quintupled on non-food items.
  • Migros Club School: 70% reimbursement of course fees, up to a maximum of CHF 1,000 per calendar year
  • Leisure and culture: You benefit from discounted tickets for selected cultural events
  • Sport and fitness offers: Contribution to fitness subscriptions: maximum CHF 200.- per subscription per year

Contatto

Candidature and contact Sabrina Zimmermann
