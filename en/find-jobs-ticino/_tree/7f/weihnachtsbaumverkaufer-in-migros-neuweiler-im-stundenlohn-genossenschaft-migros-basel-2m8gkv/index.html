Full description

For our Christmas tree sale this year we are looking for your support as a motivated Christmas tree seller for our branch Neuweiler in Basel. ►

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Basel, Neuweiler, Neuweilerplatz 7, 4054 Basel

The mission takes place on four to five days per week, each for six hours per day. We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.
