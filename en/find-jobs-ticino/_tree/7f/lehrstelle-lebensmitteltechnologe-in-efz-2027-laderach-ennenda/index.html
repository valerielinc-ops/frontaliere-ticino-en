Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate – that is what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business today employs more than 2,500 people from more than 80 countries across the Group, and the trend is rising. The company’s headquarters are located in the Canton of Glarus. In front of a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates here and constantly develop new, creative ideas in agile teams. In our production, we produce fresh couvertures from high-quality raw materials. Afterwards, we process and refine the liquid chocolates in our chocolate factory to form FrischSchoggi, pralinés, truffes, figures and much more. You will get the chance to complete a multi-year EFZ training in food technology at one of our production sites. There you will learn how food samples are taken and examined for their chemical and microbiological properties. We’ll show you how to operate equipment, monitor our chocolate production, and follow strict hygiene guidelines. Do you want to enter the professional world and learn new things? Then come to us.

This is what awaits you

  • You will be supported and accompanied during the transition from school to the professional world
  • You will be looked after individually and personally by our practice instructors
  • You will receive a diverse, practical education with very good prospects
  • You get freedom for self-responsible work and the implementation of your own ideas
  • You will be actively supported in learning and preparing for exams
  • You get to know different departments and develop a comprehensive understanding of our company
  • You receive regular feedback on your personal development and receive targeted support

You bring that with you

  • You like to work practically, are curious about technology and enjoy helping to shape production processes
  • You are careful, accurate and reliable – even if it is sometimes fast and hectic
  • You are motivated, like to learn new things and find it exciting to look behind the scenes of food production

This is what we offer you
