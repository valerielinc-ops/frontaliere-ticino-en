Full description

Movement is in our DNA – since 1936. As a family business, we value people who want to think, participate and grow with us. Become part of our family and shape the future of transport and warehouse logistics with us.

What you move

  • You are actively involved in picking
  • Chaos? Not with you! You ensure that the goods land safely and quickly in the right storage place
  • With your reliable and timely working method, you are responsible for the day-to-day processing of customer orders
  • The goods you move mainly with floor conveyors from A to B
  • You pay attention to compliance with packaging, shipping and labelling regulations
  • Together with your team, you are responsible for inventory
  • You always keep the camp in check and are open for additional tasks to ensure a smooth process

Why do you succeed?

  • You have completed vocational training as a logistician EFZ or have at least 5 years of professional experience in logistics
  • You are already a proud owner of a forklift card
  • Oral and written you can safely express yourself in German
  • Reliability, accuracy and rapid adaptability to new challenges set you apart
  • Even if it gets stressful, you stay cool and keep track.

Your Benefits

  • Attractive holiday regulations: With us you start with 5 weeks holidays and even benefit from 6 weeks from the 5th year of service!
  • Further education and development opportunities: With us you can develop yourself - we promote and challenge talents
  • Central introductory training: You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Internal personal store: Benefit from discounted prices in our internal personal store

Who we are For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
