Client Register Specialist (12 months fixed term contract) — Pictet Group

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your team

Operations ensure secure and efficient processing of Pictet's banking and investment funds. Our teams ensure that all activities comply with regulations and manage risks. By developing and maintaining a modern and robust infrastructure, we guarantee reliable and high-quality services for asset management, asset management and real estate. Manage all data related to account opening documents. Contribute to the management and optimization of customer data in banking business systems, ensuring quality service and maintaining an accurate, consistent and reliable database. You will play a key role in respecting internal procedures and regulatory requirements, while supporting the Bank's various lines of business.

Your role

  • Client Data Management:

o Support and process account openings of the various Business Line in our business systems. o Analyze and verify compliance of records and instructions related to claims for open accounts receivable. o Accurately record customer information, services and other documents in underlying banking systems. o Conduct quality controls, investigations and follow up on corrections required.

  • Support and collaboration:

o Provide proactive support to managers and relationship managers by answering questions and processing requests for information. o Collaborate with internal teams to ensure rigorous enforcement of existing regulations, taxation and directives.

  • Participation in projects and initiatives:

o Actively contribute to regulatory or internal projects to improve data management processes and tools. o Participate in data reviews and test campaigns to ensure quality and compliance of information.

Your profile

  • CFC, Bachelor/Master, ideally with first experience in the banking sector.
  • Ability to rapidly assimilate complex and transversal processes.
  • Customer service and excellent interpersonal skills.
  • Excellent mastery of computer tools, including MS Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint). The knowledge of Avaloq banking tool, an asset.
  • Team spirit developed and ability to collaborate effectively with different interlocutors.
  • Quality orientation, with an analytical mind and great attention to detail.
  • Curiosity, innovation and taste for continuous process improvement.
  • Current mastery of French and English, both in writing and in oral. Knowledge of German is an asset.
  • Residence in Switzerland compulsory.
