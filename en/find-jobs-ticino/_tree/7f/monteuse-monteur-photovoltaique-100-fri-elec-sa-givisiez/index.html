Full description

Participate in projects that shape the energy landscape of our region

Fri Elec SA, a Fribourgeois company based in Givisiez and member of the Burkhalter Group, has over 65 employees. We are active in electrical installations, building computing, security, photovoltaics and charging stations. Our company supports its clients in carrying out innovative and sustainable technical projects. To strengthen our Photovoltaic & Recharging Stations department, we are looking for a photovoltaic mounter/mounter for an entry-in to work.

Your missions

  • Mounting of roof and façade mounting structures.
  • Install photovoltaic panels.
  • Installation and connection of DC-side photovoltaic components.
  • Participation in the commissioning of photovoltaic installations.
  • Collaboration with project leaders and technical teams.
  • Completion of work in compliance with safety and quality requirements
  • Installation of charging stations for electric vehicles.

Your profile

  • Experience confirmed in the installation of photovoltaic installations.
  • Training of iron or roofer, an important asset.
  • Experience and ease in work on slope and height roofs.
  • Knowledge of different photovoltaic systems and brands

Inverters.

  • Knowledge or experience in the field of charging terminals.
  • Boat licence and height training, an asset
  • Experience in commissioning photovoltaic installations, an asset.
  • Independent, rigorous and quality oriented person.
  • Team spirit and sense of responsibility.
  • Driver's licence category B.

We offer you

  • Various projects in a future sector
  • A concrete activity at the heart of the energy transition
  • A dynamic working environment within an experienced team
  • Training and development opportunities
  • The benefits of a regional enterprise integrated into the Burkhalter Group

Want to make a concrete contribution to the energy transition with us? We look forward to meeting you and receiving your application by mail or email by November 13, 2026.
