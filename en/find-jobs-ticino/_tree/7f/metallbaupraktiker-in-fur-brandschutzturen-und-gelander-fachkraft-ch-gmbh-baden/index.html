Full description

In this order, you have versatile metal construction tasks from manufacturing to assembly. The company gives you insight into challenging projects. You benefit from working in an experienced team. For our client we are looking for one/n activities

  • manufacture and assembly of fire doors
  • manufacture railings according to technical specifications precisely fit
  • carry out welding work professionally and accurately
  • maintain and check metal components
  • undertake general locksmith work in the workshop
  • Requirements
  • Completed apprenticeship as a metalworker EBA
  • Practical experience in the field of metal construction
  • Precise and forward working methods
  • Safe and courteous behaviour among customers
  • Pronounced enthusiasm for metal construction offer
  • Collaboration in a routine team
  • Dedication on an hourly basis with attractive remuneration
  • Challenging projects in metal construction
