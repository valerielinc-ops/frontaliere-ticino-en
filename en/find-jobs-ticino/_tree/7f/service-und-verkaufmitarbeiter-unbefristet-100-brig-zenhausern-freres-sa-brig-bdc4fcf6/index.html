Service and sales staff (m/f/d) \- Perpetual \- 100% \- Brig

Discover with us the desire for adventure!

Right in the heart of Valais and with a 40-year history, our hybrid company, which operates with a love of pleasure in various areas, is looking for committed and motivated personalities. From crispy to heavenly pastries, every creation celebrates authenticity.

Become part of a team consisting of around 470 talents in which further education and career development flourish in a modern working environment. Reliable, dynamic and high-quality - our values form the corporate culture that puts people at the center.

Are you ready to actively shape your future and become your success?

Your tasks

  • You offer a warm welcome and impeccable service and are friendly to ensure the advice of our customers.
  • You present Zen articles attractively and highlight their characteristics.
  • You ensure proper stockpile management and promotion of new products
  • You master the checkout system (tablet) and ensure daily operation.
  • Opening or closing the branch is one of your tasks according to the daily shift.
  • You ensure a clean and organized environment in compliance with hygiene standards.
  • According to your assignment planning, you can ensure both the opening in the morning and the end of the guest activity in the evening. (Shops open daily from 5:00 to 20:30).

What you bring:

  • You have initial experience in the retail or catering sector (advantageous)
  • According to the internal branch planning, you are available on weekends (Saturday or ) and during school holidays.
  • Your interpersonal skills and positive attitude actively contribute to creating a friendly and warm atmosphere.
  • You show real pleasure in interacting with customers.
  • You have good knowledge of German, multilingualism is an advantage.

Your benefits

  • 5 weeks holiday
  • 13\. Monthly salary
  • 15% wage surcharge on Sundays and public holidays (sales and catering)
  • Free breakfast
  • Loyalty rewards
  • Ongoing training
  • 20% discount on our products
  • Discounts for various partners (fleet discount, fitness, etc.)

Date of arrival: now
