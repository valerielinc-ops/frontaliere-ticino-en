Senior IT Production Engineer – Scheduling / File Transfer / Monitoring (80-100%)

As part of the modernization of our IT platform (renewal of our platforms), we are looking for production engineers familiar with technologies around file transfer, scheduling and alerting, who will be able to support us throughout this transformation, while at the same time supporting the current installed base.

Management of an application pool within the IT Operations department (Technologies and Operations unit), in a team of 7 employees operating from Geneva.

YOUR MISSION

  • Responsible for implementing business requests on our alerting/scheduling/file transfer platforms
  • Qualification of requests, detailed documentation of specs
  • Actively participate and make proposals for the evolution of the platforms
  • Actively participate and make proposals to improve and automate existing processes
  • Level 2/3 support for existing workflows

Internal Contacts

  • Steering committee
  • Production team (Product-Support)
  • Integration team
  • All technical teams (Data bases, System, Telecom, Citrix,)
  • Development teams based in Geneva
  • Support teams (Service Desk and Application Support)
  • Service Management teams (Incidents and Problems)

External Contacts

  • Suppliers of external products and/or services
  • Technical sponsors of certain banking infrastructure customers
  • Technical sponsors of partners of our banking infrastructure customers

YOUR PROFILE

  • Higher education in computer science (Master's degree or equivalent)
  • 2 to 10 years in a similar position
  • Significant experience on file transfer: implementation of industrial transfer protocols, certificate management. GoAnywhere would be a plus
  • Scheduling: OpCon, Control-M or equivalent would be a plus
  • Monitoring/alerting: Service Now Event Management, Nagios, SCOM, scripting (Powershell, shell) would be a plus
  • Responsible, rigorous, versatile, flexible and with good interpersonal skills. Strong team spirit, desire for continuous improvement, proactive.
  • Fluency in French and English (spoken and written)
  • Residence in Switzerland or willing to relocate
