As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

For our interdisciplinary surgical bed department with a focus on cardiac, thoracic and visceral surgery, we are looking for a dedicated health specialist.

You Can Expect This When Us

  • Work-life balance: Look forward to scheduled working hours without night or shared services. So there is enough time for your hobbies, family and personal time out
  • At eye level and with each other: we support each other, meet with appreciation and do not lose our humor even in stressful times
  • Together it's more fun! You can expect a young, open-minded team with a lot of cohesion, joint breaks, regular team events and a collegial atmosphere.
  • The view is on top of it! Enjoy a beautiful view of Bern's old town at work
  • The benefits are also right: Remunerated changing time in the form of additional Fridays as well as a share in public transport costs and the fitness subscription

Here you can take a closer look at the benefits you can benefit from: Teamdiagnose.ch

YOUR TASKS

  • You nurture and look after our patients with heart and expertise. You respond to their individual needs and support in diagnostic, therapeutic and preventive measures
  • It's better together! You work closely with our attending physicians, nurses and other areas
  • You carry out medical engineering activities independently and take care of the maintenance documentation and performance billing
  • You accompany our learners and interns, share your knowledge and support them on their training path
  • Not just working, but helping to shape! You contribute your ideas, actively shape our processes and contribute to a high quality of care

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a specialist health EFZ. Acute experience is a plus, but not a must
  • The well-being of our patients is important to you – you care with heart and mind
  • Respect, appreciation and an open approach to people are a matter of course for you
  • You like to work independently, are a real team player and attach importance to a good quality of care
  • You bring good knowledge of German and feel safe in everyday linguistic life.
