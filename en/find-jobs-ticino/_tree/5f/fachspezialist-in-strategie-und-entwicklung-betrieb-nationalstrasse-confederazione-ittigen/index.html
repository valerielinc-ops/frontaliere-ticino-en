Put to the Point

They play a central role at the interface between the Confederation and the cantons and actively shape the future of the approval processes for exceptional transports. With your focus on the essentials, your determination and your assertiveness, you will find sustainable solutions for our customers.

  • Date of entry: 01.02.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20303
  • Place of work: Pulverstrasse 13, 3063 Ittigen

You can make this contribution

  • Assume technical responsibility for the holistic granting of authorisations for exceptional transports under the responsibility of ASTRA
  • Provide for measures to check the necessary timeliness and reliability of the data used and to participate in the development of data quality issues
  • Coordinate and further develop cooperation between ASTRA and the cantonal licensing authorities so that the processes can be carried out efficiently and effectively in line with customer needs
  • Responsible for the continuous development and optimization of business processes with a focus on the possibilities of digitalization

This makes them unique.

  • University of Applied Sciences degree in Technology, Economics, Computer Science or a comparable subject area
  • Further training in business administration, business informatics, change management or equivalent desired
  • Many years of experience in the cooperation and support of large projects
  • Experience / knowledge in traffic, construction and operational safety of road infrastructure and occupational safety
  • Competence for assessing operational processes and business relationships and ability to use them in interdisciplinary solutions
  • Active knowledge of a second official language

Move the Switzerland.

The Federal Office for Roads (ASTRA) is responsible for the construction, maintenance and operation of the national road network and is committed to sustainable and safe mobility on the road. It is part of the Federal Department of Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications (UVEK).

As an employee of ASTRA, you move Switzerland. They take responsibility in challenging projects that advance and connect all regions. ASTRA offers you the opportunity to make a significant contribution to the successful development of Switzerland in a modern working environment.
