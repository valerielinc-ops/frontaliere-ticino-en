Over the area

The Center for Psychiatry and Inpatient Psychotherapy is part of the Clinic for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy at PDAG (Clinic Director Prof. Dr. med. Marc Walter). It is responsible for the modern acute psychiatric treatment of people between 18 and 64 years of age, which includes biological, psychotherapeutic and socio-medical applications and is based on open-door and no-restraint concepts. The center is dual by Dr. med. Maxim Zavorotnyy and Petra Meyer, Head of Care, and includes five stations. There is a close network for interventional psychiatry. For doctors, the centre is a SIWF-approved training centre for psychiatry and psychotherapy category A inpatient (3 years) and for interventional psychiatry (rTMS and EKT).
