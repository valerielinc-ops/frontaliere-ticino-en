Full description

Portrait The Language Healing School Bern is a special elementary school for normally gifted children and adolescents with a severe speech disorder. We run classes from kindergarten to high school and teach according to the curriculum 21. Our goal is to (re)integrate the students into the regular school in the medium term.

Tasks

They eat lunch together with a group of 8 to 10 students and then spend lunch with them playing, crafting, etc. Wear together with other midday caregivers During this time, you take responsibility for the processes in the group and ensure the care.

Working hours are from 11:30 to 1:30 on the following days of the week: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday.

Requirements

  • Joy in dealing with children with special needs (areas of language and behavior)
  • Experience in caring for children and young people
  • Patience and assertiveness
  • Teamwork and independent working methods
  • Flexibility
  • Good knowledge of German

We offer

  • Lively working atmosphere in a committed and young team
  • Central, well-accessed workplace
  • 13 school holiday weeks (holiday plan of the city of Bern)
  • Employment according to city guidelines
