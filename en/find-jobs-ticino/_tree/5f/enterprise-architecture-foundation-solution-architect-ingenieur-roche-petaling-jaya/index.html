The Position Who We Are The Enterprise Architecture Foundations team acts as the backbone of the architecture function at Roche . We focus on community engagement, modern ways of working, and capability enablement . As Roche Digital Technology (RDT) shifts to a "Federated by Design" model, our success relies entirely on transitioning from static architecture documents to dynamic, data-driven insights . We connect and empower architects across our global, federated organization to design sustainable technology solutions using shared principles and active communities . To achieve our 2026 strategic goals, we need a visionary architect to build a resilient, fully integrated toolchain ecosystem that drives data-driven portfolio management and landscape consolidation . The Role: What You Will Do As the Solution Architect for the Integrated Toolchain , you will serve as the principal technical mind behind our core corporate platforms: ArchiMEDES (SAP LeanIX) , ArchiSense (our custom AI Co-Pilot) , and ITONICS (Innovation Management) . You will bridge the gap between architectural data and strategic execution .

Your primary responsibilities will include

  • Drive EAM Tooling Excellence: Serve as the technical owner for ArchiMEDES (SAP LeanIX) . You will configure core data models and drive advanced automated integrations with ServiceNow and cloud discovery tools to deliver a real-time, comprehensive view of our IT landscape .
  • Advance AI-Driven Architecture: Lead the technical scaling of ArchiSense, maturing it from a proof-of-concept into a production-grade AI Co-Pilot . You will build custom AI agents to automate queries, execute tasks, and streamline technology standard navigation .
  • Champion Data Democratization: Design, extract, and publish source-system-oriented data products directly into the Roche DataOne (Snowflake) environment and Data Marketplace (Collibra) to enable intuitive, self-service data consumption for downstream analytics .
  • Lead Technology Foresight Integration: Maintain technical ownership and oversee the integration of the ITONICS platform to effectively map external market trends and emerging technology phases .
  • Enable Data-Driven Portfolio Management: Architect a seamless toolchain that bridges corporate strategy and engineering execution, providing clear, transparent insights into dependencies, technical debt, and redundancies for senior leadership .
  • Embed "Decisions Over Documents" Practices: Partner with Architecture Practice Leads to embed practical governance guardrails directly into the tooling ecosystem, reducing administrative friction for engineering teams .
