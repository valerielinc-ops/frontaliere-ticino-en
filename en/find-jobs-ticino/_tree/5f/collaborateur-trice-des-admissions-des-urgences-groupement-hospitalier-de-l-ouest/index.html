Full description

GHOL is the reference hospital for the German West with its structures on Nyon and Rolle. Tomorrow, we propose to continue your professional development by involving you in your new missions and by participating in the development of GHOL, in its new hospital building at the Nyon site.

Description of position

In your new duties, your missions have an impact on the quality of patient care. Your role will include:

  • Be in charge of the admission process of stationary and outpatient patients (variable schedules, 7/7)
  • Updating administrative information related to patient stays
  • Be responsible for collecting and monitoring patient insurance coverage
  • Mastering the administrative procedures of your sector
  • Hosting, directing and ensuring internal and external communications, as well as patient relations
  • Manage service phone calls and email box
  • Collaborate with related services (receipt, billing, debtor, and medical care providers)

Profile required

We are convinced of your rapid integration into your future functions through your:

  • 3 years of experience in an equivalent position
  • Health sector interest
  • Sense of welcome
  • Good stress management
  • Flexibility and availability
  • Self-help
  • Team will
  • Proposal Force
  • Perfect mastery of French (read, written, spoken)
  • Good knowledge in English (minimum A2/B1)
  • Use of IT Tools (MSOffice Pack)
  • Opal Knowledge (an asset)

Level of training

SWC Trade employee or equivalent

Benefits

Integrating the Nyon Admissions team is a real integration plan to be quickly operational. You will evolve in a pleasant working environment, supported by a series of well-established procedures. Nyon Hospital is evolving to accommodate patients in a modern and welcoming infrastructure. A pleasant working environment within a human-sized team that encourages exchanges and your progress

  • Collaborate with innovative managers, active in their environment and creative to meet current and future challenges
  • The dynamic operation of a private company whose recognition of the public interest guarantees the benefits of the CTC applicable in the parapublic health sector.
