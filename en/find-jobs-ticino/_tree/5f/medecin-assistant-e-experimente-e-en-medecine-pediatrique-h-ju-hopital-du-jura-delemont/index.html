Full description

Attached to the Hospital of Jura, the site of Delémont, the Pediatric Service (total of 10,000 passages/year) is composed of several sectors. It includes 9 general paediatric beds, 3 neonatology beds, a maternity, an emergency area and a permanence - Mediped Delémont and Porrentruy Centres. The Paediatric Service is a training centre whose mission is to provide medical, psychological and social care for patients between the ages of 0 and 18. The team works closely with Basel University Hospital.

In order to complete the current strength, we place a position of:

Responsibilities

  • You provide quality, child-centred care.
  • You work with specialists, colleagues and multidisciplinary teams to ensure optimal patient care.
  • You are actively involved in pre- and post-graduate training and improvement activities.
  • Writing out reports, prescribing drugs, coordinating with other services, attending guards and multidisciplinary meetings are part of your daily life.

Qualifications

  • You have a Swiss federal degree in medicine or a degree recognized by the Federal Commission for Medical Professions (MEBEKO).
  • You are motivated and want to become a pediatrician.
  • You already benefit from clinical experience in paediatrics (more than 2 years) acquired in Switzerland or within the European Union.
  • Mastery of the French language (minimum level B2).

Benefits

  • A stimulating working environment within dynamic teams.
  • Attractive remuneration that takes into account your seniority.
  • Easy access to accommodation (room and/or studio).
  • A cafeteria restaurant with preferential rates
