Who we are

Novelis is driven by the desire to shape together a sustainable world.

As a world leader in innovative products and services and the world's largest aluminium recycler, we work in partnership with customers in the automotive, aeronautics, cans and specialty sectors to provide solutions that enhance the benefits of light aluminum.

We have a coordinated network of technically sophisticated 29-site rolling and recycling operations in 9 countries in North America, South America, Europe and Asia, with 12,750 employees worldwide. The county seat is Atlanta, USA.

Our customers include some of the most important and well-known brands in the world, including Coca\-Cola, Ford, BMW, Jaguar Land Rover, Airbus, Boeing, Nespresso, Samsung, and others. In addition, Novelis is leading the industry through its sustainability and recycling efforts.
