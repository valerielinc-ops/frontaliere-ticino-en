Full description

Entero Klinik is a psychiatric-psychotherapeutic specialist clinic for people with dependency diseases and psychiatric comorbidities. We work interdisciplinary, holistically and with an attitude that is characterized by openness, familiarity, individuality and solution orientation. We treat our patients with respect, empathy and on an equal footing and we also meet in the team. Are you looking for meaningful work in an interdisciplinary environment? In order to strengthen our team in psychotherapy, we are looking for you as

Psychologist:in / Psychotherapist:in (80%)

Place of work: weaning Egliswil

Your tasks

  • Self-responsible case management and psychotherapeutic treatment of patients: inside with substance-related dependency diseases and diverse psychiatric comorbidities
  • Diagnostic investigations
  • Structured documentation and reports for authorities, insurance companies and other bodies
  • Management and co-management of group therapies
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with psychiatry, nursing, social therapy, occupational therapy and social work
  • Co-creation of a development-oriented working environment characterized by ease and closeness

Requirement profile

  • Master in Psychology and Advanced or Completed Psychotherapy Training
  • Experience in treating addiction (or desire to delve into it)
  • Knowledge in ADHD diagnosis is an advantage
  • Enjoying clear, professional reports (e.g. exit reports, cost-benefit languages, etc.)
  • An appreciative, respectful attitude and desire for teamwork
  • Personal responsibility, humor and the ability to smile about yourself

What to expect from us In our manageable psychotherapy team, we live the values of loyalty, trust, reliability, as well as humor and ease. The close cooperation with psychiatrists: women, nursing, social therapy, work agogy and social work makes our work versatile and lively.

You get

  • An independent, responsible activity in a manageable setting
  • A team that holds together, strengthens each other and lives an appreciative atmosphere
  • Upon request, the opportunity to continue accompanying patients: insides on an outpatient basis
  • 5 weeks holiday
  • 1 paid training week per year
  • KTG contributions 100% taken over by entero
  • Free parking
  • Discount lunch (mo-do)

Sounds like you? Then we look forward to meeting you.
