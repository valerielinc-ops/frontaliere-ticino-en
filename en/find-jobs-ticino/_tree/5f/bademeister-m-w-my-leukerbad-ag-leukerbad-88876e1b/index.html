Full description

My Leukerbad AG is the operating organisation of Leukerbad’s most important tourist service providers, which are managed under one roof. These include Leukerbad Tourism, the torrent railways, the sports arena, the Snowpark Sportarena, the bus companies Leuk-Leukerbad and the Leukerbad Therme. As an integrated tourism company in a year-round destination, around 200 qualified employees develop, operate and market unforgettable and sustainable products and experiences for our guests.

The Leukerbad Therme, as Europe's largest alpine thermal bath, has 10 thermal water baths between 28°C and 43°C and a versatile wellness and restoration area.

If you like people, are looking for a variety of indoor and outdoor tasks such as supervision, technology, hygiene and safety and are a team player, then become our new colleague as a bathing master 100%.

Your tasks

  • You ensure a safe bathing operation and carry out supervision duties
  • You provide first aid services and life-saving emergency measures
  • You control and demand the bathing rules
  • You regularly monitor the water quality
  • You support the technical team with craftsmanship in maintenance work
  • A well-kept environment and a clean pool area are important to you

Your profile

  • Valid Brevet Pro Pool of the SRLG with BLS-AED, or valid Brevet igba Brevet Pro
  • Your experience in a bathroom or related technical fields is welcome
  • You meet our guests with good treatment, appear safe, friendly and consistent
  • You appreciate the benefits of shift, weekend and holiday bets
  • You can safely communicate in the German language, foreign languages in F, E, I are advantageous in direct contact with guests
  • Rely on you, you are sporty and very service-oriented
