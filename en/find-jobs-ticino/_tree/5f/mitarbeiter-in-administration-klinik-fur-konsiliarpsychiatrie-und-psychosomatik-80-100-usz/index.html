as of 01. February 2027 or by agreement

The clinic for consiliar psychiatry and psychosomatics of the USZ accompanies patients with various mental complaints both on an outpatient and inpatient basis. In this exciting hub function, you make sure that everything runs smoothly in the background: From the reception and care of our patients: inside through the scheduling to the support of doctors: inside and therapist: inside. You coordinate administrative processes, create medical correspondence and contribute significantly to high-quality patient care with your organizational talent. You can expect a varied and responsible job in a committed team.

Your main tasks

  • Ensure a smooth administrative process for our patients: internal and external interfaces in a lively environment
  • Patient: Interior reception with counter and telephone service
  • Planning and scheduling of consultation hours, opening of cases and maintaining case master data in KISIM
  • Writing medical and insurance reports as well as general correspondence according to dictates, submission and independently
  • Administrative support for the medical profession and therapists
  • Care KV learners:r 1st year of apprenticeship in job sharing

Your profile

  • Enthusiasm for direct patient: internal contact
  • Commercial education, professional experience in the health or health insurance sector and medical secretary: inside training beneficial
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, good knowledge of English and other languages are advantageous
  • Experience with MS Office programs (Outlook, Word, Excel), experience with KISIM is an advantage
  • Organizational talent with strong service and coordination thinking
  • Reliable, socially competent personality with friendly, courteous appearance and efficient and independent work
  • Federally recognised ID as a vocational trainer:in advantage
