Security worker/subordinate

Are you looking for a flexible side earning in the security area, don’t want to be just a “wheel in the gearbox” and appreciate a personal, family working environment? Then you are exactly right with us! We offer committed newcomers as well as experienced security forces varied assignments in side jobs. Majority with focus on event safety.

Your missions & regions

  • Working hours: Mostly on weekends (Friday evening to Sunday) as well as on public holidays and special events.
  • Winter: Focus in the region of Davos / Prättigau.
  • Summer: Mostly in the Glarnerland area and in the greater Zurich area.

Your tasks

  • Supervision of events, guaranteeing general safety & order.
  • Access and personal controls for events or objects.
  • Presence service, contact person for guests, organizers and customers.

Your profile

  • Experience: Welcome are both industry beginners and experienced professionals.
  • Personality: Quiet, specific appearance, joy in dealing with people.
  • Language & ID: Good knowledge of German, as well as an impeccable reputation (excerpts of enforcement and criminal records are required).
  • Mobility: driving licence Cat. B is advantageous

Our profile

  • Working atmosphere: Short distances, flat hierarchies and strong team cohesion.
  • Flexibility: Ideal combination with your main job or study by pre-planning the weekend assignments.
  • Wages: Attractive hourly wage
  • Training: A solid approach to the tasks is a matter of course for us.
