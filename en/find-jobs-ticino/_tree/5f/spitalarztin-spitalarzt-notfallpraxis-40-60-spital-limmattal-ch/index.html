In our interdisciplinary emergency center, we treat approximately 35,000 patients every year. A third of these patients are guided directly after triage to the bright and modern premises of the associated emergency practice with its own waiting room, laboratory and four treatment rooms.

Here they engage

  • in the diagnosis and therapy of all patients in emergency practice
  • in close cooperation with the specialists of the emergency department and the hospital as a whole
  • in a 2-shift operation, without night service
  • in cooperation with an own, motivated MPA team
  • Recognition for 1 year of ambulatory internal medicine category IV
  • Tutorat to achieve Abdominal Sonography and POCUS Emergency Sonography
  • are in possession of the specialist title General Internal Medicine, or are about to
  • have basic knowledge of wound care, or have a special interest in it
  • enjoy a versatile, varied work
  • Reduced employee parking
  • Motorcycle parking, lockable bicycle parking
  • With the ZVV Bonus Pass / Z-Bonus Pass we offer an attractive public transport subscription for employees
