Full description

Are you familiar with food quality management and would like to use your expertise in an international environment? With us, you take responsibility for the quality and food safety requirements of our trading platform Delica EU in the border-related garbage shelter (DE) and actively shape the processes.

Luogo di lavoro: Delica AG, Delica AG Birsfelden, Hafenstrasse 120, 4127 Birsfelden

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the quality management tasks of our trading platform Delica EU in Müllheim (DE) and ensure the relevant food safety requirements.
  • You are responsible for the IFS Broker certification of Delica EU GmbH and accompany the annual audit.
  • You organize and accompany further label and customer audits (e.g. organic) on site in Müllheim
  • You process customer inquiries, requests from local food authorities and complaints and ensure that the defined storage requirements are met.
  • Together with the Delica specialist areas, you ensure that product-specific regulations, such as PPWR, are adhered to in the sales countries.

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: in QM, QS or food safety in the food industry.
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in the field of food sciences and/or many years of relevant experience in the QM of food trading companies.
  • You can consistently enforce requirements and work solution-oriented with internal and external contacts.
  • You work independently and reliably, take responsibility and keep track of different Q requirements.
  • Very good knowledge of GFSI certifications as well as good knowledge of food product safety.
  • Experience with broker certifications is an advantage. You also bring SAP user knowledge and basic regulatory knowledge is available.
  • in German (negotiation confident)
  • English (negotiable)
