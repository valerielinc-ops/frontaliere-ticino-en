Full description

Restaurant the flow is the ideal place for a private or business lunch in an exceptional atmosphere in the heart of the city of Bern. It is located in the centrally located Welle7 near Bern station, which combines pleasure, business and education under one roof. Do you love to inspire people with your hospitality? We look forward to your application!

Mansioni

  • Customer service, advice and active sales as well as cash service including billing
  • Preparation of beverages and food
  • Reception and control of foodstuffs
  • General cleaning work, maintenance of kitchen equipment and compliance with quality standards and guidelines
  • Creating offers and e-mail correspondence

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in a comparable position
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship (EFZ): in the catering or hotel industry
  • German (negotiable): further knowledge of foreign languages (English, Italian, French) of great advantage
  • Independent, customer- and service-oriented personality
  • Careful working methods and high quality awareness
  • Readiness for the following work assignments: Basically Monday to Friday (8:45 – 22:30), 2-shift service, no part-service

Cosa of Friamo

  • Central location: Central locations with excellent public transport connections
  • Occupational provision: Very attractive pension fund with above-average employer contribution
  • Public Transport Participation: Public Transport Cost Sharing and Mobility CarSharing
  • Modern infrastructure: modern, bright office space with ergonomic workstations
  • Fair wages: Strong overall package of wages, social security and occupational pensions
  • Sports and fitness offers: Cost sharing in subscriptions to various leisure and fitness facilities of the Migros Group
