Full description

The LUKS Group with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee, Wolhusen and Stans is one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. More than 8,000 employees are committed with high professional competence, innovative strength and humanity to an excellent patient-centered care. The Clinic of Cardiology as part of our heart center combines medical excellence and state-of-the-art technologies with a lot of passion and personal care at the highest level for Central Switzerland. By combining the two main areas of coronary and structural cardiology, we continue to develop the clinic under uniform management.

For our team we are looking for 01. April 2027 or by agreement reinforcement. Tasks: • They carry the medical, personnel, organizational and economic overall responsibility for cardiology, combine the previous focal points with clear responsibilities, decision-making paths and common leadership culture and strategically develop the entire range of services with innovation and new technologies. • leadership of the leading doctors, the main responsible persons and the senior and assistant doctors; Active involvement of the focus managers in the further development of the clinic • Personal clinical and interventional activity in your own focus, inpatient and outpatient as well as interprofessional cooperation with care, cardiac catheter laboratory and functional diagnostics as well as maintenance of relationships with internal and external assignees • Promotion of clinical research and university teaching as well as responsibility for further and further training Requirements • Specialist title Cardiology with proven expertise and specialization in one focus • Habilitation and proven scientific profile with experience in research, innovation or quality projects as well as networking in national and international cardiology specialist circles • MBA or comparable management training is beneficial; proven experience of change and ability to win highly specialized teams for common goals and consistently implement necessary decisions • Clear, decision-making and appreciative leadership as well as promoting an open learning and feedback culture We offer: • Management of a large, highly specialized clinic with a full range of cardiology and modern infrastructure in a dynamic intercantonal hospital group with a clear future strategy • Great room for manoeuvre in setting up a uniform management and clinical structure • Collaboration in partnership in the interdisciplinary heart center with committed, experienced teams • Attractive employment conditions and an appreciative, collegial working environment
