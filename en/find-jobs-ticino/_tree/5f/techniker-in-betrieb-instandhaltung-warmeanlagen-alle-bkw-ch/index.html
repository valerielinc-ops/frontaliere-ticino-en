Full description

Do you want to ensure that heat flows reliably? With us, you will manage versatile energy plants, contribute your technical know-how and ensure efficient and sustainable operation.

Your field of activity

  • You independently supervise and maintain various thermal energy plants from wood chips and pellet plants to district heating, steam extractions to heat pumps and substations
  • For ecological, economical and reliable operation, you analyze plant conditions and optimize processes on an ongoing basis.
  • Systematically get to the bottom of disruptions and develop sustainable, forward-looking solutions to avoid recurring problems
  • Maintenance, repair and optimization measures you plan and coordinate independently and together with external companies
  • For start-up and shutdown operations as well as for new plants, you ensure safe trial operation and the quality of implementation
  • You support customers with technical questions and ensure a quick troubleshooting in the Pikettdienst

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic technical training EFZ as heating installer, electrical installer, mechanic or equivalent profession
  • Ideally, you have a further training as a heat specialist for heating systems specializing in firing technology wood, oil, gas or heat pumps, building technology planner heating, maintenance specialist or heating plant operator.
  • Your practical experience in the maintenance of technical equipment as well as knowledge of control and regulation technology distinguishes you
  • User knowledge in MS Office, control systems and maintenance programs as well as a category B driving licence is required
  • Your knowledge of German is safe to negotiate, every other language gives you an advantage
  • The willingness to take on responsibility is also reflected in your assignment in the Pikettdienst, which you currently take on for the plant portfolio approximately every seven weeks.

About us

BKW AEK Contracting AG offers innovative and ecological heat supplies. It plans, finances, builds and operates heat composites and individual plants. Founded in 2018 by merging the contracting competence centers of AEK and BKW, the company looks back on 20 years of experience

  • The portfolio comprises 75 heat generation plants. More than 30 employees are committed to innovation and sustainability every day. Make a contribution to society and our environment with us.
