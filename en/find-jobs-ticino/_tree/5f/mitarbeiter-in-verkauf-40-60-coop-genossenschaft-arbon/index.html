Employees: in Sales 40-60 %

Coop Cooperative — Arbon, TG, Svizzera

  • Job opening: immediately or by agreement
  • Specialty: Sales Food

You enjoy working in sales, are reliable and meet customers with a smile? In our Coop Pronto shop with gas station in Arbon, you can expect a varied day-to-day work with a lot of customer contact. We count on dedication, team spirit and a friendly attitude. If you like to sell, take on responsibility and keep an overview even in lively moments, you are at the right place with us.

Tasks

  • You advise our customers: competent, friendly and with a smile – and ensure that they are happy to come back
  • You sell our convenience products with conviction
  • You take on cash bets and process payments reliably and efficiently
  • You implement sales promotion measures attentively and effectively
  • You consistently ensure compliance with all relevant shop guidelines, in particular quality and youth protection regulations
  • You provide an attractive product presentation and a well-kept, inviting shop layout

Requirements

  • Ideally, experience in retailing
  • Completed basic education beneficial
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Customer-oriented thinking and acting as well as a friendly and well-groomed appearance
  • Joy in dealing with people and working together in a team
  • Flexibility in work assignments, including on Sundays and public holidays

What we offer

  • Attractive Perks with the Coop Personal Discount Card: 5x Super Points on Food
  • 10% discount on non-food in the Coop supermarket and benefits in other coop formats
  • Fair surcharges for Sunday and public holidays
  • Fair remuneration with an entry-level wage of CHF 4'200.- plus 13. Monthly wages
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • 41 hours Week
  • Flexible operational planning wherever possible
  • Early operational plans for better predictability
  • Option on part-time models
  • Update Fitness: Fr. 200.– Discount on annual subscription

We look forward to your application!

Mr Fredian Gashi
