Your Mission

At the heart of our Cartier operations Supply Chain department on the Villars-sur-Glâne campus, you will be in charge of managing the requests and orders of the markets for leather bracelets.

In interaction with international subsidiaries, production and multiple entities, your role will be to ensure optimal information in terms of timeliness, relevance and reliability.

  • You manage market requests on the leather strap perimeter:

o Preferred contact of markets/regions for any subject related to product availability, orders, especially on complex files (VIP clients, old models...)

o Management of Broken Reference Allowances Related to Planners Distribution

o Follow-up of emergencies by mobilizing all of the chain's interlocutors (markets, regions, central warehouse and manufacturing) to best meet customer needs and meet our commitment in terms of time

  • You ensure the reliability of the information in our strap configurator:

o Updating the strap configurator in terms of product and leadtime

o Coordination of different interlocutors (Technique, Planning, Digital) to correct any anomalies or inconsistencies

  • You are participating in continuous improvement projects on the distribution of leather straps:

o Participation in change of stream or performance review topics

What are the keys to your success?

Your rigor, your organisation, your sense of analysis and priorities are real assets to ensure a supportive role for our subsidiaries. In addition, your versatility, autonomy and relationship are qualities that allow you to fulfill your missions with skill and demand.

You have significant experience in the field of customer service and have at least a commercial maturity and/or equivalent training.

You are French mother tongue and your level of English allows you to speak fluently in both oral and written language.

Expertise on Excel, SAP SD module as well as skills for IT tools are required.
