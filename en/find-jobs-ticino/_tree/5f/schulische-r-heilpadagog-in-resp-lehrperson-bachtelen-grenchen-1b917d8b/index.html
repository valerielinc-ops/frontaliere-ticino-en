Full description

Bachtelen is committed to the individual promotion of children, young people and young adults in the canton of Solothurn. We are also committed to the integration of young people who are impaired in various ways: in behavior, language, communication, interaction or cognition.

In order to achieve our goals, we run day care centers, day schools, housing offers, offers of individual support as well as therapeutic and advisory offers.

In the day school Grenchen TAGRE, normally gifted students with special educational needs in the areas of behavior, learning and communication are holistically supported. Lessons take place in small classes and are characterized by individualized support following the curriculum 21.

We are looking for immediately or by agreement

School curative teacher / school curative teacher resp. Teacher (16 lessons)

Tasks

  • Teaching and supporting normally gifted students: women with special educational needs in the areas of behavior, learning and communication
  • Class leadership of an age-mixed class of 10 students:in cycle 1
  • Close cooperation in a multi-professional team consisting of the specialist disciplines of curative pedagogy, social pedagogy, therapy & counselling

We look forward to a personality that

  • Delights in taking new pedagogical paths, for example through learning in nature or action-oriented teaching
  • brings enthusiasm for the profession and promotes and demands children at their individual development level
  • is interested in further developing in the areas of trauma pedagogy, bond-led pedagogy and New Authority
  • enjoy working in a multi-professional team and taking responsibility

Among other things, we offer

  • Great room for manoeuvre in teaching
  • Working in a dedicated and supportive team
  • Support from team members and leadership
  • Various training offers as well as opportunities for reflection and intervision
  • Conditions of employment according to cantonal guidelines
