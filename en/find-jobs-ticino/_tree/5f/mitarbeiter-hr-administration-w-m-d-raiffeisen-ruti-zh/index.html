Full description

11.11.2026 Rüti ZH

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Rapperswil-Jona, a successful and locally anchored bank with headquarters in Rapperswil-Jona and offices in Egg, Hombrechtikon and Rüti ZH, we are looking for a committed, service-oriented personality as an employee HR administration (m/f/d). The place of work is in Rüti ZH, supplemented by the possibility of home office.

What awaits you?

  • Cooperation and support in all administrative HR processes from entry to exit
  • Administrative support in the recruitment process (job advertisements, applicant correspondence, contract preparation)
  • Preparation and management of employment contracts, contract changes, employment and intermediate certificates and other personnel documents
  • Maintenance and mutation of personnel data and management of personnel dossiers
  • Processing of wage adjustments, one-off payments and personnel-related mutations
  • Participation in monthly wage processing and support in social insurance and wage-related processes
  • Administrative handling of absences (illness, accident, maternity, military, unpaid leave)
  • Administrative support for managers and employees in personnel-relevant issues
  • Cooperation in other administrative and organizational tasks in the services team

What do you bring?

  • Basic Commercial Education
  • Further training in human resources (e.g. HR assistant)
  • Professional experience in HR administration
  • Knowledge of Swiss labour law and social insurance is an advantage
  • Structured, independent and reliable working method
  • High discretion and responsible handling of confidential information

Look forward to a versatile job in our services team. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Zürich?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Zürich's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Zürich's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
