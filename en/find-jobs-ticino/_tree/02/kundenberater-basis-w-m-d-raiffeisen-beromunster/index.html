30.10.2026 Beromünster

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank in Beromünster, a successful and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Beromünster and offices in Reinach AG and Eich, we are looking for an independent, committed and customer-oriented personality as a customer consultant.

What awaits you?

  • You advise our customers in the areas of numbers, savings, investing and pensions
  • You develop individual solutions and accompany our customers in different life situations
  • You create positive customer experiences and are the first point of contact in the reception and cash area
  • You recognize potential, communicate contacts with our specialists and thus help to actively maintain and expand the team customer portfolio
  • A team of competent, motivated and friendly colleagues awaits you - with your positive nature you contribute to joint success
  • In the spring of 2027 we move together into a new building in Beromünster. You can expect a working and consulting environment designed according to the latest criteria

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking education or equivalent with banking experience
  • Ideally sound experience in basic customer consulting
  • Strong customer- and service-oriented thinking and action
  • Sympathetic and winning appearance as well as good communication skills and pleasure in customer contact
  • Responsible and reliable way of working with which you support and strengthen your team
  • You are a team player and actively engage for common goals

You are an enthusiastic personality, enjoy customer contact and wish for a versatile, exciting activity with design and development possibilities? If this applies to you, we look forward to welcoming you to our team. Oriana Roos Team Leader Customer Consulting Why Raiffeisen?

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We live diversity and equal opportunities

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
