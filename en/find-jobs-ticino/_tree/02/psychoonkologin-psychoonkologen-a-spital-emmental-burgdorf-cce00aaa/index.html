Full description

The psychiatry of the Emmental Hospital offers demanding jobs for doctors, psychologists, nurses, social workers and administrative staff with the opportunity for personal development and takes into account their own resources. In our manageable company, the personality of each individual employee shapes the way we deal with each other and our cooperation. We regard individuality as enrichment. Psychooncology accompanies people with cancer and their relatives on their way through diagnosis, treatment and follow-up care. What to expect

A cancer never only affects the body – it also demands emotionally and emotionally. In this demanding phase of life, many people seek orientation, support and trust. This is where your work starts:

  • You accompany patients: inside and their relatives on their often challenging path – sensitive, present and competent
  • You independently lead the psycho-oncological consultation in the outpatient area
  • You are looking after inpatients: oncology and palliative care in consultancy
  • You are also regularly deployed at the Langnau site, ensuring psycho-oncological care throughout the catchment area.
  • You work closely with a dedicated, interprofessional team
  • Your work is characterized by closeness, professionalism and a deep understanding of what people need in crisis situations.

  • Ideally, further training in psycho-oncology and experience in the oncological environment
  • Empathy, resilience and enjoyment of interprofessional cooperation

  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
