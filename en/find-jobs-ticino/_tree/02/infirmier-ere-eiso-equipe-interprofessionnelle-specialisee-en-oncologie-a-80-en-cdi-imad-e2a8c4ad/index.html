Full description

In collaboration with the Department of Oncology of the HUGs and with a view to improving the outpatient and home care of cancer patients, the IMAD is creating a pilot project to develop a home care device specializing in adult oncology, based on a joint clinical route of the IMAD-HUG with a view to improving the continuity of care and home care of cancer patients in the course of treatment at the HUGs. The team formed will carry out a very broad and complex activity in the clinical, social, psychological and environmental activities of the client. The EISO team will also have the task of establishing close links with the specific Geneva oncological network (e.g. the Oncological Department of the HUG, the Cancer League, OTIUM). Your missions and responsibilities

  • Effectively respond to EISO team-specific care by providing autonomous and medically delegated, technically complex home nursing services to adult clients with oncological pathology, usually provided in hospital settings
  • Provide comprehensive personalized care to adult clients with oncological pathology who are part of a global vision, taking into account their direct and social environment, their psychological constraints. Appreciate any need such as dietetics or occupational therapy
  • Exercising a position reference role and acting in accordance with the principles of the nursing code of ethics
  • Participate in the institutional mission to foster home care in a stimulating environment for our clients by developing interprofessional and interrelational relationships with the various teams integrated into the project
  • Develop links and interactions with the actors of the Geneva specific oncological network (e.g. the Oncological Department of the HUG, the Cancer League, OTIUM).
  • Bachelor of nursing, permission to practice in the canton
  • Oncology CAS with proven oncology for the field
  • Minimum two years of work experience in home care
  • Dexterity in technical care
  • Adaptability, flexibility and responsiveness
  • Great relational ease, ability to manage stress and sense of responsibility
  • Knowledge of the Geneva network specific to oncology would be an asset
  • Ability to work independently and as an interdisciplinary and interprofessional team
  • Essential mobility (bike/car).
