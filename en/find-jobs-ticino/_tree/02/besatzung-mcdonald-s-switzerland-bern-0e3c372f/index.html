Job Description

As a Crew, you are at the heart of our restaurant. You work with great colleagues, serve our guests with a smile and create memorable moments. Whether at the counter, in the kitchen or supporting the team, your energy and enthusiasm make all the difference.

  • Collaborate with the team to achieve goals
  • Maintain a clean and welcoming environment
  • Adapting to different tasks and busy moments with a positive attitude
