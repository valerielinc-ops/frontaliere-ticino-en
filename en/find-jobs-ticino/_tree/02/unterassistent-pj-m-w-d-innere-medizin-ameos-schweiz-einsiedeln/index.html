Full description

AMEOS Spital Einsiedeln is a major healthcare provider for Einsiedeln, the surrounding region and far beyond, and stands for high-quality, needs-based medical care.

We continuously review applications for positions as

Sub-assistant /PJ (m/f/d) Internal Medicine

Your benefits

  • A structured and practical introduction
  • Close supervision by senior physicians
  • Active integration into the clinical routine with a steep learning curve
  • Regular internal training sessions
  • Family-like working environment with an appreciative team culture
  • Attractive workplace in the heart of Central Switzerland with excellent recreational opportunities
  • Accommodation available on site

Your responsibilities

  • Learning the treatment principles of Internal Medicine
  • Admission of internal medicine patients with assistance (medical history, examination)
  • Assistance in carrying out medical investigations (ultrasound, X-rays, punctures, etc.)
  • Ultrasound examinations under supervision
  • Interpretation of medical examination results
  • Proposal for preparing a treatment plan
  • Practising interprofessional collaboration
  • ....and much more

Your profile

  • Enrollment in Human Medicine
  • Interest in General Internal Medicine
  • Very good German language skills, spoken and written
  • Enjoyment of patient-oriented medicine and interprofessional collaboration
  • High willingness to learn, initiative and ability to work in a team
  • Responsible and patient-oriented way of working
