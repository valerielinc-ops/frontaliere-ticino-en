Full description

Policlinic, emergency, outpatient care, hospitalizations: from birth to old age, the Morges Hospital offers a wide range of care services. The establishment has 240 acute care beds, one maternity, with about 1,500 births per year, and nearly 30,000 emergency consultations. In all, more than 40 medical disciplines have been practiced in its walls for more than 150 years. Surrounded by vineyards, with incredible views of the mountains and the lake, the Morges Hospital welcomes you in a warm and dynamic setting.

Nurse graduated in midwifery training - Poolist on time (M/F)

Start as soon as possible or agree.

WE OFFER YOU

  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility to buy another week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time leave, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • A mobility plan including participation in your cycling, basketball or public transport subscription, with an environmental focus on an institutional sustainability plan.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience, as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Free pilates courses, financial participation at sporting events such as the 20km of Lausanne or Bike to Work, a catalogue of advantages offering discounts in the fields of sport, welfare, leisure, mobility, insurance and finance.
  • Hot drinks offered in the morning and meals at CHF 8.50 in our cafeterias.
  • A "Pâquis Plage" business drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Opportunities for internal change, according to your desire for development.
  • Many accessible in-house or certifying training.
  • A programme dedicated to managers to support them in acquiring the skills necessary to manage their teams.

YOU MAY

  • A HES Bachelor of Nursing or equivalent diploma recognized by the Swiss Red Cross.
  • Midwife training in progress.
  • A strong interest in maternity care, support for women and perinatal health.
  • Good coping, organizational and communication skills.
  • A team spirit, a sense of responsibility and an empathetic and caring approach in caring for patients and their families.
  • Availability compatible with the needs of the pool and your training course.

OBJECTIVES

  • Provide nursing care for patients in the areas of postpartum, prenatal and operational gynaecology, within your scope of competence.
  • Participate in the clinical supervision, care and support of women during pregnancy and after delivery, in collaboration with midwives and the medical team.
  • Contribute to the pre- and post-operative management of gynaecology patients, ensuring their safety, comfort and well-being.
  • Accompany mothers and their families in daily care, information and preparation for return home, in collaboration with the interprofessional team.
  • Provide advice for breastfeeding according to the UNICEF label "Baby friendly hospital"
  • Ensure quality, continuity and traceability of care, while actively participating in service dynamics.

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation facilities, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal transformation according to the aspirations and development projects of the collaborators.
  • More than 2000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
