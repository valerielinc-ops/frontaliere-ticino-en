Electronics Quality Engineer — AGIE Charmilles SA (GF Machining Solutions), Losone (TI).

100%WorkloadLosoneLocation Apply now Your tasks

  • Ensure quality control of PCBA production processes (THT, wave soldering, selective soldering, mixed technology) and

maintain control plans and acceptance criteria.

  • Support production teams in troubleshooting electronic failures and lead structured problem-solving activities (8D,

5 Why, root cause analysis).

  • Ensure reliability of electronic test systems (SPEA, Flying Probe, functional test benches) and implement corrective

actions on test failures.

  • Drive continuous improvement initiatives to reduce internal defects and support New Product Introduction (NPI) with

robust manufacturing and test processes.

  • Support supplier quality activities, define incoming inspection requirements, and contribute to the analysis of

assemblies returned from the field. Your profile

  • Degree in Electronics or Electrical Engineering or equivalent technical background; 3–5 years of experience in

electronics manufacturing, quality, or test engineering.

  • Knowledge of PCB assembly technologies, soldering processes, and solder joint quality; experience with electronic

test systems (SPEA, Flying Probe, ICT or similar) is a strong advantage.

  • Structured problem-solving mindset (8D, 5 Why) with hands-on, pragmatic approach and strong technical curiosity.
  • Ability to work cross-functionally with production, R&D, and supplier teams; clear and effective communication with

technical stakeholders.

  • Fluent in Italian
  • English B2 or higher required.
