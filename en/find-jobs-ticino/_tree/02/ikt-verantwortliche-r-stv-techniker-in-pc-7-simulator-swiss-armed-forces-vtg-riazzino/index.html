Put to the Point

Where computer science meets electronics and operational tasks in aviation. The Locarno Airport Command offers you an exceptionally varied and dynamic professional challenge. On the one hand, you are the ICT contact for civilian personnel, on the other hand, you work as an electronics engineer on the PC 7 simulator with military customers and take care of the management of flight effects to ensure the operational readiness of pilots, loadmasters and paratroopers. An extraordinary job, not only because of the high technical and operational level, but also because you can use your language skills optimally!
