Full description

You ensure that goods are available on time for shipping. The operation coordinates logistics processes and supports a well-established team. For our client we are looking for one/n activities

  • picking finished workpieces for shipping
  • provision of ready-to-ship goods on time
  • Managing stocks in the card and on pallet spaces
  • Coordinating empty goods with external logistics partners
  • Supporting the logistics team in case of absences
  • Requirements
  • Teaching as a logistician or logistician EFZ or many years of storage experience
  • Solid user knowledge in the computer area
  • ERP ProAlpha would be desirable
  • Valid forklift for everyday storage
  • Category B driver's license available Offer
  • Pensum on the basis of a 40-hour week
  • Free parking facilities at work
  • Reduced use of the e-charging station
