Full description

We are looking for a dynamic and fit diver to join our talented team at the Migros restaurant in Balexert. Do you have experience in cleaning and diving? Do you know HACCP standards and have good physical fitness? So maybe you're the person we're looking for!

Luogo di Lavoro: Migros Cooperative Society Geneva, Migros Restaurant - Balexert, Avenue Louis-Casai 27, 1211 Geneva 28

Important remarks

The use of the masculine aims at simplicity, but our recruitment is open to all.

Mansioni

  • You clean up the battery and the tunnel.
  • You keep the room clean and tidy.
  • You meet HACCP standards.
  • You are versatile and ready to make yourself available for the needs of the restaurant.
  • You organize your actions to optimize your work.
  • You handle and move heavy loads according to safety instructions.

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of experience: in a similar position
  • Good knowledge of HACCP standards
  • Stress-resistant and able to organize
  • Physical fitness for carrying heavy loads
  • No initial/continuous training required
  • French (Good knowledge)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Occupational pension: Primary contributions, contributions from 20 years of age doubled by Migros and early retirement from 58 years of age.
  • Catering: Meal discount of Fr.4 (from purchases of 10.-) in Migros restaurants.
  • Cumulus Points: Reduction of 2% and 5% depending on the assortments.
  • Migros Bank: Interest at preferential rate and free of charge for a savings account.
