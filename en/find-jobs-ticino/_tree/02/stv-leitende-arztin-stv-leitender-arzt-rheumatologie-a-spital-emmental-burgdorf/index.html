Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

Immediately or by agreement we are looking for:n

Tasks

  • You independently supervise outpatient rheumatological consultation hours at our Burgdorf and Langnau locations
  • Diagnosing and treating the full spectrum of inflammatory and non-inflammatory rheumatic diseases
  • You use arthrosonography specifically in diagnostics and monitoring
  • You work interdisciplinary with the other specialist areas of our company (including diabetology/endocrinology, nephrology, hematology, pneumology and pain therapy)
  • You participate in the further education of our assistant doctors
  • You are involved in the further development of our rheumatological offer and – depending on suitability and interest – gradually assume leadership responsibility.

Profile

  • You have the specialist title Rheumatology (FMH) – or are just before the specialist maturity
  • You bring the arthroscopy certificate – or the willingness to acquire it
  • You work independently, carefully and patient-oriented
  • You appreciate the interdisciplinary cooperation and the collegial exchange at eye level
  • You like to take on responsibility and want to develop professionally and personally
  • You have stylish German
  • Further language skills are advantageous
