Full description

At Endress+Hauser, progress is made through collaboration. As the world’s leading provider of measuring instruments, our approximately 17,000 employees are shaping the future in the field of process automation. Whether it’s innovating and implementing new technologies in a team, working together to develop new devices or strengthening important relationships with different global industries, our work is based on building trusted relationships that help everyone succeed. As a family-run company, we look beyond short-term goals and work to create a future-oriented vision for our employees, our customers and the world in general. We are looking for a layer conductor in Coriolis measuring tube manufacturing (f/m/d) to complement our team and help shape the production of a central core of our Coriolis flowmeters. In this role, you ensure that quality, safety and teamwork go hand in hand in shift operations and that our products are used reliably worldwide. What is at stake here? Are you excited about leading and developing people in the production environment? Do you want to take responsibility for smooth and high-quality production? Does it appeal to you to be close to the action and to make the success of your shift visible on a daily basis? In this role, you combine leadership, organization and technical understanding and thus make an important contribution to products that are used worldwide in the process industry. What tasks will you take on? They shall: • take over the management of up to 10 employees in 2-shift operations, including employee meetings and the continuous development of the team. • Ensure day-to-day operations, including employee deployment planning, focus on error-free assembly and active handling of bottlenecks. • Responsible for meeting the targets in terms of quality, dates and quantities. • Responsibility for compliance with quality and safety regulations and actively implement Total Productive Maintenance (TPM). • Perform active shop floor management to control the shift and transparently control targets. What do we expect from you? They: • have completed basic technical training. • bring at least 3 years of management and professional experience. • have experience in the application of lean methods. • have good user knowledge in MS-Office and SAP. • communicate very well in German, both verbally and in writing. • are characterized by communication and teamwork, a sense of responsibility and independence. What can you expect from us? • We are a family-run, committed company with a sustainable vision. • we promote lifelong learning with versatile training and further education opportunities, internal knowledge transfer and e-learning. • We support a healthy work-life balance with flexible working hours. • We provide support for sustainable mobility solutions such as public transport. • We promote family-friendly policies, including childcare places and childcare services during the holidays.
