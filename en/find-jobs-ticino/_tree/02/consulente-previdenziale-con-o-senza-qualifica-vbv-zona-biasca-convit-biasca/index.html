Private consultant – with or without VBV qualification (Biasca zone)

Are you a customer-oriented person and want to pursue a career in social welfare? Convit offers you the opportunity to grow in a dynamic industry and support our customers in their important decisions. We're looking for motivated people who want to make a difference.

Main information Employer: Convit International GmbH Workplace: Via al Mulino 22a, 6814 Cadempino, Switzerland Recruitment area: Biasca and surroundings – the workplace is our headquarters in Cadempino. Contract: Indefinite recruitment; degree of employment according to the approved position Retribution (OTE): CHF 1'500–7'500 per month (fix + variable part, depending on the degree of employment) Language: Italian B2 or higher

Activities

  • Analyze individual customer social security needs.
  • Prepare customized social security solutions.
  • Conduct advice within the approved framework.
  • Carefully document counselling activities.
  • Keep your professional knowledge up to date.

Your profile

A VBV qualification, at least in the Life profile, is an advantage, but is not required to start. If you already own it, attach the document and information about your professional update. Without qualification, the availability of training and the commitment to acquire the necessary qualifications are indispensable. Counselling and intermediation activities begin only after fulfilling the applicable requirements and internal authorization.

  • You have excellent communication and relational skills.
  • You are a reliable and result-oriented person.
  • You have a strong attitude to learning.
  • Work in an autonomous and structured way.

What we offer

  • We offer you a structured insertion and a constant comparison with experienced colleagues.
  • You will have cutting-edge digital tools and clear procedures for preparation and documentation.
  • If you still do not have the VBV qualification, a clear learning path with practical support is provided.
  • You will be part of a team that enhances collaboration and mutual support.
  • Hybrid working methods agreed, with presence in the reference location.

Training and qualification

If you do not still have a VBV qualification, the VBV admission exam preparation course is free for you: Convit supports course costs. The qualification requires overcoming the relevant examination and compliance with the applicable requirements.

Right to work

Applications of persons resident in Switzerland are welcome. For this campaign, those who reside in Italy must already have a valid G permit. The individual situation is verified before hiring.

Application

Send the resume and any certificates via the application form or to Recruiting@convit.ch.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status. Note: this listing allows remote work — teleworking above 25 % of your time can shift your social-security base to Italy, so confirm the agreed remote share with HR before signing.
