Full description

Tasks

  • Planning, declaration, ordering and cancellation of additional tours and organization of empty goods orders
  • Coordination of chauffeurs and coordination with internal and external interfaces
  • Communication with transport service providers including troubleshooting in the event of transport and shipping deviations
  • Moving bridges on the premises of the company by means of special vehicles as well as loading and unloading them
  • Provision of shipments and empty goods for the corresponding tours and destination areas
  • Support in the operational dispatch area and cooperation in the rotation in the event of appropriate utilization
  • Ensuring quality, order, cleanliness and safety at work as well as coordination of the Dispatcher Light if necessary

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience in logistics, ramp and/or transport coordination or a comparable environment
  • German language skills at level B1 or higher
  • Category B driving licence and readiness for internal training on special vehicles
  • Advantage: forklift pass and experience in handling change bridge vehicles
  • Strong organizational skills as well as the ability to keep an overview even in hectic situations
  • Self-employed, reliable and responsible work
  • Physical fitness and enjoyment of working with people, vehicles and logistical processes
  • Proper repute and readiness for the following working hours: Monday to Friday in shift operation from 05:00 to 14:30 or 14:30 to 23:00. In addition, a Saturday assignment takes place about once a month.

We offer

  • Perks: Attractive perks in the Brack online shop as well as lunch perks and staff restaurants in Mägenwil and Willisau.
  • Recreation: At least 5 weeks of vacation, in addition there is the possibility for holiday purchase and unpaid vacation.
  • Development: Internal training in the own Academy as well as financial participation in external further education.
  • Family: 18 weeks of paid maternity leave with extension and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.
  • Insurance: Supercompulsory – 100% continued pay and full financing of the sickness allowance insurance by the employer.
  • Mobility: free parking, financial participation in public transport and pool vehicles for business meetings.

We set ourselves ambitious goals, but it is very important to us that we act responsibly, promote and challenge each other and enjoy what we do every day.
