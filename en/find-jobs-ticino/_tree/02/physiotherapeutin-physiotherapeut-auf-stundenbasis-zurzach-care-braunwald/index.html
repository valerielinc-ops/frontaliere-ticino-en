Full description

In the idyllic landscape of Glarnerland is our renowned institution, the Braunwald Rehaklinik. We focus on the treatment of people with psychiatric diseases. Our team offers a versatile, interdisciplinary therapy based on a multimodal and integrative treatment approach.

As part of the therapeutic care, you support the team as required by the patients and help with holiday representatives.

Your tasks

  • Patient-oriented therapy: You treat our inpatient patients over several weeks and accompany them on their way back to a more independent life. You will treat in single sequences with a focus on psychosomatics / psychiatry and additional clinical pictures from the areas of musculoskeletal, neurology and pain.
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation: Work in an interprofessional team closely with other nurses, therapists and doctors
  • Documentation: You document the therapeutic measures in your area of responsibility and ensure the flow of information in the interdisciplinary exchange
  • Further development: You are involved in the further development of the therapeutic offer in the team

You bring that with you

  • Training: Completed training as a physiotherapist or equivalent with SRC recognition
  • Authorisations: Valid Professional Permit (BAB) or willingness to apply for it
  • Experience: Ideally, you already have professional experience in the psychiatric environment
  • Personality: You enjoy working in an interprofessional team, thinking in a networked way and always looking for solution-oriented approaches. Your positive, communicative nature and your pronounced awareness of quality characterize you as a personality.

What we offer

  • Mobility grant: Financial support for travelling by Braunwaldbahn
  • Further education: Paid days for your professional development
  • Working environment: In addition to the main place of work, assignments are possible at other locations in Glarnerland
  • Common breakfasts: Monthly breakfast invitations by the group of companies
  • Culinary offer: Three freshly cooked menus daily at special prices
  • Wellness: Free use of medical-therapeutic training and twice a week of the bathing area
  • Pension fund contributions: above-average participation and management costs with attractive pension options
  • Discounts: Use discounts from various partners, such as wholesalers, car dealers and pharmacies as well as free admissions and other discounts in the wellness spa FORTYSEVEN
