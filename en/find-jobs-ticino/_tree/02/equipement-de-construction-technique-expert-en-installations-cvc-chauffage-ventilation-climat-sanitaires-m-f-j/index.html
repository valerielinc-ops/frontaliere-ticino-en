The Position Responsibility | This is what awaits you As a Facility Expert Technical Building Equipment - HVAC (m/f/d) You are the strategic and tactical head for the safe, compliant and energy-efficient operation of our plants with a focus on sanitary technology, heat/cold supply, indoor air technology, sprinkler and conveyor systems. Your tasks in detail:

  • Maintenance Strategy: You develop and optimize maintenance strategies and keep plant data and maintenance plans up-to-date.
  • Energy & Utilities Management: As a driver:in for sustainability monitored You determine the energy consumption, uncover potential savings and track the relevant key figures.
  • Life Cycle Asset Management (LCAM): You create OPEX/CAPEX budget and investment plans for value preservation and recovery.
  • Project Management: Take over for investment projects You have the professional responsibility, act as operator:in/builder representative:in and plan and manage your own maintenance projects.
  • Records & third-party management: You define technical standards, ensure legally compliant documentation and control our service providers as Subject Matter Expert (SME).
  • Disruption Management: Preserved in complex technical challenges You have a cool head and intervene as a 2nd level support in trouble management.

Qualifications | This is what you bring You will bring a successfully completed engineering degree in the field of supply engineering / TGA or a qualification as a master:in / technician:in with appropriate professional experience. The following points round off your profile:

  • Sound practice in the management and maintenance of complex HVAC systems.
  • Proven expertise in thermodynamics, fluid dynamics and MSR technology.
  • Very good knowledge of the relevant standards and legal regulations (e.g. VDI 6022, EnEV/GEG).
  • Experience in the strategic and operational management of service providers.
  • You like to work directly on site at the location and bring the readiness for operational call readiness as well as occasional travel activities.
  • Negotiable German and good English skills round off your profile.
