The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their farms. Together with the brands Volg, TopShop and AGROLA, the LANDI cooperatives also provide the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy. The LANDI Furt and Limmattal Cooperative is successfully active in the Zurich West region. In the Volg Shop at the location Wettingen we are looking for a seller immediately part-time 20-50%.

You are looking forward to these tasks

  • They advise and serve our customers friendly, expert and with pleasure
  • They assist in goods management
  • They serve the cashier and create the cash account
  • They fill the shelves and provide an attractive shop image
  • They provide order, cleanliness and hygiene

We look forward to this profile

  • You have a completed sales apprenticeship or experience in food sales
  • They are flexible in terms of time
  • You have a friendly attitude and good knowledge of German
  • They enjoy selling.
  • You are ready to work in other branches as well

About us

Volg is the specialist for village shops and small spaces in German and French Switzerland. We live close to customers – as a close shopping opportunity as well as in personal contact. The employees are the heart of the shop and we attach great importance to individuality and personality. That’s why our motto is “fresh and cheerful”.

You can look forward to that.

  • Remuneration: Employees receive a 13th annually Monthly wages paid out. Depending on your function and responsibility, you also benefit from an individual participation in success.
  • Social benefit: Even in the event of illness or accident, you are financially well secured with us: During a total of 730 days you receive your full net wage.
  • Provision: Our company-owned pension fund participates above-average in the financing of savings and insurance contributions. From the age of 35, you have the choice of which pension plan you want to join.
  • Every five years we thank our employees for their loyalty and great commitment. Be it with a financial premium or with additional holidays.
