Full description

The workplace for this position is located in Visp, Switzerland. Relocation support is available if needed for eligible applicants and their families.

At the Visp site, we are looking for a Day Shift Employee (m/f/d) in the Energy and Utilities department to strengthen our team:

What we offer

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture
  • An inclusive and ethical working environment
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • A variety of benefits, depending on function and location

In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous benefits for lifestyle, family, and leisure time. You can find our complete list of local benefits below:

  • Benefits in Visp

Your tasks

  • Safe and timely execution of maintenance work in the energy and utilities plants
  • Expert and timely execution of piping and mechanical engineering work on the assigned facilities as well as their overhauls
  • You support the production team during the commissioning and decommissioning of apparatus and plants and contribute to efficient changeover times
  • You acquire knowledge and skills for the overhaul of operations-specific valves and apparatus
  • As a team player and reliable colleague, you contribute to the success of the team through your collaboration
  • Working with computers (Outlook, Excel, Word, SAP, as well as process control systems) rounds out your field of activity

What we look for

  • High standard for strengthening the safety culture
  • Training as a plant and apparatus builder specializing in pipe construction or an equivalent technical training is an advantage
  • Manual dexterity and professional experience
  • You are interested and ready to learn new technologies and continuously develop your skills
  • Customer orientation and innovative problem-solving are among your strengths
  • You are dedicated and flexible, working safely, independently, and reliably
  • You strive to ensure a high standard of safety and quality in accordance with requirements
