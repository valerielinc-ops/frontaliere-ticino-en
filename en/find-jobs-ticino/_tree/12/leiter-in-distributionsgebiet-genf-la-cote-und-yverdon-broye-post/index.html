Full description

Do you want to take on corporate responsibility, further develop the performance of a distribution area and accompany the teams in the upcoming transformations? As a manager: in the distribution area of Geneva, La Côte or Yverdon-les-Bains, you contribute directly to the quality of our services, the satisfaction of our customers and the further development of tomorrow’s postal service. With you, we drive forward the delivery in a sustainable, efficient and customer-oriented manner.

You can do that.

  • You manage a distribution area with around 250 to 350 employees and bear overall responsibility for the operational, financial, qualitative and personnel results.
  • Together with your leadership team, you ensure reliable, efficient, secure and customer-focused operations.
  • The leadership, development and coaching of your senior management are just as much a priority as promoting a culture of cooperation, responsibility and dialogue.
  • With the results analysis, you set priorities and implement the necessary measures to achieve the goals and optimize the processes.
  • You control organizational transformations and projects and accompany teams in the further development of processes, tools and working methods.
  • As a member of the Zone’s management team, you work for their priorities and represent logistics services to internal and external partners.

You bring that with you

  • You have several years of experience in the management of a larger operational unit and can lead over several levels of responsibility.
  • A higher vocational training or a study ideally in the areas of leadership, logistics, process management or business administration round off your profile.
  • Proven experience in change and project management is a big plus.
  • With an entrepreneurial vision, you translate goals into concrete priorities and take responsibility for their implementation.
  • Joint success guides your actions: You develop the skills of your teams, live the values and behaviors expected of a leader and communicate clearly and convincingly with your different contacts.
  • In addition, you communicate very well orally and in writing in a first national language and have good knowledge of a second national language.
