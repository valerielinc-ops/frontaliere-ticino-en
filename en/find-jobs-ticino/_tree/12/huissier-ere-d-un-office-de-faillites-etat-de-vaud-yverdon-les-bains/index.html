Full description

Do you hold the certificate of general training in enforced execution, or have significant experience in the field of prosecution and bankruptcy? To ensure a variety of responsibilities, notably by carrying out external operations in direct contact with users? You want to evolve in a stimulating work environment? Join us! Attached to the Vaudois Judicial Order, the canton's four bankruptcy offices have the main task of administering and ensuring liquidation by bankruptcy. In order to complete the existing team, the Office of Bankruptcy of the district of Broye and the Vaudois North , which employs nearly 15 collaborators, is seeking an office of bankruptcy.

  • Managing bankruptcy cases
  • Carry out operations related to precautionary measures, such as inventories, publication of the opening of bankruptcy, etc.
  • Determine active and passive masses (analysis of productions and decisions needed to settle the file)
  • Hearing the bankrupts
  • Establish and publish collocation statements
  • Proceed with the realization and distribution of the money (sale, statement of costs, distribution table, distribution of funds, reports and publication of the closure of the bankruptcy)
  • Collaborate in the management of real estate files, including review of productions, advisories to tenants or farmers and realization operations More information can be found in the specifications by clicking here . Do you have any questions? Ms Sabine Jamois, Bankruptcy Officer, is happy to be at your disposal at 024 557 78 00.
  • SWC of a commercial employee at a prosecution or bankruptcy office.
  • Certificate of general training in enforcement or possibly holder of the Apprenticeship OPF test or significant experience in the field of prosecution and bankruptcy with a commitment to follow modules 1 and 3 of the certificate.
  • Four-year professional experience in prosecution or bankruptcy.
  • Public service sense, very good stress resistance.
  • Good organization, initiative, speed, precision.
  • Ability to manage conflicts, assertivity.
  • Mastering spelling and ease with common computer tools.
  • Driving licence and availability of the desired private vehicle. There are many opportunities for professional development and continuing training within the Prosecution and Bankruptcy Office. As part of the recruitment process, applicants will be required to submit an extract from the prosecution register and criminal record for less than 3 months.
