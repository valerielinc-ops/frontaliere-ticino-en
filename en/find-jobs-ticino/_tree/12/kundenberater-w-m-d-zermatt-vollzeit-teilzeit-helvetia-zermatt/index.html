Customer Advice – Sales Pensum: 50-100% | Place of work: Zermatt Place of work: Level of experience: With professional experience FlexOffice: on site Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

What you do

  • Advice & support with foresight: You accompany private and corporate customers in all insurance issues with real commitment and ensure tailor-made solutions for every individual need. As a customer advisor: you are there when it matters.
  • Building long-term relationships: Trust is at the heart of your work – you maintain and develop sustainable customer relationships based on your excellent service.
  • Actively shape growth: You have a good network and bring your energy to the acquisition of new customers. Your commitment ensures that our customer base is constantly growing. How can we support you in this? By getting your own customer base with private and corporate customers.
  • Marketing & Sales Support: With fresh ideas you support our marketing campaigns, visit trade shows and events to strengthen our network.
  • Always on the pulse of time: Through our basic training and continuous further education, you stay up-to-date and offer the highest consulting competence.

What you bring

  • Experience & expertise: You bring experience in customer consulting or sales – ideally in the financial or insurance industry. Also ambitious lateral entrants: women who are ready to start quickly are welcome.
  • Communication talent: People inspire and win – that is in your blood. You shine through communication and negotiating strength. As a customer consultant, you can argue convincingly and have stylish knowledge of German.
  • Service is your strength: The field service is your passion and your high customer orientation and willingness to service distinguish you.
  • Commitment & initiative: With above-average motivation and entrepreneurial spirit, you are ready to achieve great things and exceed goals.
  • Regional anchorage: Your place of residence is in the Zermatt region where you look after your customers. You have a strong network in the Zermatt region and appreciate building and maintaining new contacts.
