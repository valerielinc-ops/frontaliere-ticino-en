Aluminium drawer 8-100% CDI (M/F/D)

Are you passionate about technical drawing and want to put your expertise at the service of various projects in aluminium carpentry? Integrate a dynamic company where technique, precision and collaboration are essential to the successful completion of the project

Responsibilities

  • Formalize manufacturing plans for the workshop
  • Provide technical follow-up of joint projects with a project manager
  • Develop approval plans and ensure their adaptation to project needs
  • Perform drawing tasks mainly oriented towards aluminium carpentry
  • Ensure the programming of several workshop CNCs and ensure consistency between plans and manufacturing
  • To carry out measures directly on the worksites in order to ensure conformity of the achievements
  • Work closely with production teams and project leaders to ensure successful implementation

Profile

  • Basic training in metal construction (CFC of metal builder or draughtsman in metal constructions), a major asset
  • Significant practical experience in metal construction projects
  • Good mastery of drawing software (CAO/DAO/3D), ease with the usual computer tools and with the programming of CNC workshop machines
  • Manufacturing/implementation knowledge (an asset)
  • Organizational sense, ability to anticipate and manage priorities
  • Autonomy, rigor and good team spirit

Benefits

  • A versatile position combining design, technical and coordination
  • A modern working environment with powerful and new tools
  • Attractive conditions with possibilities of evolution
  • Entry into office: immediately or to be agreed
