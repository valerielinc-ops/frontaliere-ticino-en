Full description

As a product owner in the field of clinical specialty systems, you actively shape the digital future of central clinical core processes at the University Hospital Zurich. Your contribution lies in understanding clinical needs precisely and translating them into effective, practical digital solutions that sustainably improve the everyday life of intensive care medicine, anesthesia and neonatology.

You work closely with specialist departments as well as IT and architecture teams and develop the PDMS and surrounding systems in a targeted manner. You combine clinical expertise, technological possibilities and strategic goals to create user-oriented solutions with real added value. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients. Tasks: • Professional and content-related responsibility for products and services related to the PDMS • Analyze and understand clinical needs and translate into features and user stories • Maintenance and prioritization of the team backlog • Close cooperation with clinics as well as IT, architecture and other application teams • Co-creation of product vision and roadmap and ensuring user-oriented and consistent product development • Participation in PI Plannings, ART Events and the further development of clinical processes • Readiness for 7x24 support and piquette use Requirements • Education or study in a clinical setting, ideally with many years of experience in intensive care or anesthesia • Affinity for IT and digitalization • Experience with PDMS or clinical information systems is advantageous • Understanding clinical processes and their translation into digital solutions • Interest in agile working methods and interdisciplinary cooperation • Structured, analytical and communicative personality • Experience with SAFe, Scrum or similar methods is advantageous We offer: Take on a central role in the digitalization of one of Europe’s leading university hospitals and actively shape clinical processes. An interdisciplinary environment with a lot of creative leeway, modern working methods and direct proximity to the practice awaits you. We offer you flexible working models including home office, an agile working environment according to SAFe as well as exciting clinical and technological topics. In addition, you benefit from a high level of personal responsibility, attractive development opportunities and an optimally accessible workplace near Stettbach station.
