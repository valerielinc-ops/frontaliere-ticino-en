Discover the LIEBHERR world. As a successful family business, Liebherr focuses on innovation, quality and reliability – especially in earth moving, mining and concrete technology. In this position, you act as a central interface between sales, customers and internal and external partners and actively contribute to joint success. Tasks:

  • Schedule monitoring and tracking of orders
  • Maintaining all correspondence with manufacturer plants, distributors and final customers
  • Checking and releasing incoming supplier and service invoices
  • Objective: Independent processing of orders for machine and spare parts orders from our international customers, in close coordination and cooperation with the worldwide Liebherr production companies Qualification: Completed commercial training
  • Professional experience in the field of international order processing / logistics, for example in an industrial or logistics company
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (s/m); French of advantage
  • Experience in dealing with ERP solutions (SAP or BaaN)
  • Good user knowledge in MS Office
  • Strong, resilient and flexible personality
  • Independent and responsible working method Our offer: expected at LIEBHERR You are self-employed with a workload of 80-100% as well as a varied and interesting challenge in an internationally successful group of companies with attractive social benefits.
