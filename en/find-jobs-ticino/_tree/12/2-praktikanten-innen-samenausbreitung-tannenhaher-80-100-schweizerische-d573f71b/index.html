Full description

The Swiss Bird Observatory is a non-profit foundation for bird science and bird protection that combines basic research, applied research and practical implementation under one roof. More than 200 permanent employees and 70 temporary workers are involved in a wide range of tasks for the benefit of birds.

In the research project "Seed Spreading by Fir trees", the role of young Fir trees in the establishment and spread of the Arve is to be investigated. For this purpose, firs are caught and equipped with GPS transmitters. The analysis of the movement data should provide information on the hiding behaviour of the fir chickens and show how hiding behaviour, migration, reproduction and survival of the young birds affect the establishment of argen. Their tasks

  • Assistance in the search for fir-hunter nests in alpine forests and assembly of nest cameras.
  • Assistance in catching and GPS transmitters of pine trees.
  • Search for dead transmitter birds and identification of causes of mortality.
  • Independent field work in a small team in alpine terrain and partly away from paths.
  • Application of various methodologies such as telemetry, habitat recordings, camera traps or acoustic loggers.
  • Data input and processing of various data types in R, QField, QGIS and Excel.
