Full description

Exciting tasks await

  • Personnel, specialist and organizational management of the department UMA (unaccompanied minor asylum seekers) with 15 directly subordinate employees, overall responsibility for the department with around 120 employees, as well as 12 internally and externally supervised accommodation with about 500 accommodation places
  • Responsibility for timely and appropriate accommodation as well as professional and trauma pedagogical care of UMA in a demanding, dynamic and politically sensitive environment
  • Ensuring and further developing performance and quality standards as well as continuously adapting concepts and processes to changing framework conditions
  • Management of cooperation with internal and external partners, negotiation of service offers and ensuring controlling of agreed services
  • Representation of the department vis-à-vis public authorities, institutions, specialised bodies and the public
  • Early identification and processing of risks and transactions with particular scope

What you bring

  • University degree (FH/uni) in social work, social education, business administration or a comparable discipline
  • Several years of management experience with a large number of employees, preferably in the asylum, social, care, security or judicial area, combined with strong decision-making and implementation competence
  • High resilience, pronounced independence and the ability to provide orientation and remain capable of acting even in complex, dynamic and sensitive situations
  • Very good communication, negotiation and conflict ability as well as a secure attitude towards different stakeholder groups
  • Networked and strategic thinking, business understanding and a strong political sensorium for working in the environment of administration and public

The population is at the heart of the activities of the Department of Health and Social Affairs. For the safety of their areas of life, we work sustainably and purposefully. The Cantonal Social Service is responsible for public welfare, asylum and refugee services and victim counselling and victim assistance. He is an appeal body in the field of social assistance and supports communities in the implementation of their family and age policies. We work closely with other administrative bodies, the municipalities, other cantons, the Confederation and third-party organisations.
