Full description

Würth AG supplies craftsmen of all industries with quality products and coordinated system solutions for workshops, warehouses and on the road. The versatile range comprises over 150,000 items: from screws, dowels and fittings to tools, machines, chemical products and occupational safety to vehicle equipment and warehouse management. Proven since 1945 and at home in Switzerland since 1962, Würth AG offers tailor-made solutions with the aim of making everyday life easier for its partners. Würth AG, headquartered in Arlesheim (BL), is a company of the globally active Würth\ Group. Tasks In this role, you are an important part of our dedicated teams in the Würth Shops in Zuchwil and Aarwangen. We are looking for a responsible personality with a pronounced service idea:

  • Advise and convince our customers through competent discussions to meet their needs and generate sales
  • Ensure a smooth process by recording orders in the system and taking responsibility for goods and warehouse management
  • Support the team by carrying out administrative office work to ensure the smooth operation of the Würth Shop
  • Implement sales promotion measures and keep an eye on the specifications in order to increase the sales and success of the shop
  • Be part of our commitment to customer loyalty and customer satisfaction by participating in customer events and supporting inventories
  • You also participate in meetings to contribute to the further development of the respective shop

Requirements

  • Completed training as a retail specialist EFZ in the field of iron products or similar.
  • A well-groomed appearance and a friendly personality are part of your professionalism in customer contact
  • Your strong sales mentality and the will to always go the extra mile make you a motivated and successful sales professional
  • SAP knowledge is beneficial and enables you to efficiently handle business processes

We offer In our training program we prepare you optimally for your start in sales, so that you are also successful in the long term, you receive regular product and sales training as well as individual coachingsYour guaranteed monthly income is enriched by premiums and incentives At the same time we offer a modern IT infrastructure and the values and sustainability of a family business.
