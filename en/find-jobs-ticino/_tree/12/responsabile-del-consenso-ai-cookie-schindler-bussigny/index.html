Welcome to the world of urban mobility! The Schindler Group, founded in 1874, is one of the world's leading elevators and escalators. The company owes this success to its approximately 70,000 employees operating in more than 100 countries.

Schindler vocational training

Schindler Vocational Training is a leading training centre with many years of experience and modern infrastructure. Throughout Switzerland, we offer practical training for nearly 300 apprentices in more than 10 different occupations. Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed.
