The LANDI cooperatives support Swiss farmers in the economic development of their farms. Together with the brands Volg, TopShop and AGROLA, the LANDI cooperatives also provide the population in rural Switzerland with food, everyday goods and energy. As a versatile LANDI in the lower part of the Emmental with three locations, we offer an interesting offer in the agricultural trade, fuels, garden products, green plants, tools, small animal food and drinks.At August 2027 we offer an apprenticeship at our location Hasle near Burgdorf.
