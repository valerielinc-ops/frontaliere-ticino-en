Full description

As Laboratory Manager you will refer to the Head of Quality. Your main mission will be to pilot and organize all the activities of the Watchmaker Laboratory in order to contribute to the quality, reliability and performance of the products.

In this context, you will be in charge of

  • Managing qualification activities for new products
  • Ensure the balance between load, capacity, testing and available skills by ensuring the versatility of each
  • Provide technical and analytical support to departments and other business sectors by actively participating in internal sessions and continuous improvement approaches
  • Collect and analyze customer and internal product issues
  • Propose suitable solutions and help secure future developments
  • Participate in the definition and development of the criteria produced in order to meet the Group's quality requirements, in particular by participating in the research and development of new measurement and specific tools
  • You have a qualification as a HES engineer (engineering/microtechnology/watchmaking) or an equivalent training
  • You justify an experience of at least 5 years in a similar position in the watchmaking field.
  • With a federative spirit, you show leadership and enjoy cross-cutting exchanges
  • Dynamic, you are a force for proposal, demonstrate organization and have a developed analytical mindset

We look forward to receiving your application and processing it as soon as possible.

What we offer

  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
