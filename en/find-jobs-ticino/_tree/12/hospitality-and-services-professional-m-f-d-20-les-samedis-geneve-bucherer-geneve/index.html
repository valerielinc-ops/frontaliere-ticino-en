Full description

Your role in our home

Book a 5-star welcome to our guests, you offer them an impeccable welcome and a high-end service experience, thus helping to create exceptional moments.

You prepare and serve drinks with care and professionalism, while supporting our Client Advisors in identifying and caring for our customers' needs.

You maintain and ensure the cleanliness of the various spaces, especially the bar.

You ensure the proper functioning of the operations related to the beverage service and take care of the reception of customers in the absence of the receptionist.

You guarantee a high level of service, with availability for missions, on Saturdays from 09:00 to 18:00, exceptionally outside these slots following agreement.

Your profile

  • You are a student, ideally in the hotel sector, in a hotel school.
  • You stand out for your keen sense of service and your sense of hospitality.
  • You are stress resistant, reliable and know how to keep your cool even in demanding and state-of-the-art situations.
  • An experience in customer reception is an asset.
  • You have a strong sense of responsibility, a good team spirit and flexibility.
  • Careful appearance you always deploy a professional posture.
  • In French, you have a very good level in English.
