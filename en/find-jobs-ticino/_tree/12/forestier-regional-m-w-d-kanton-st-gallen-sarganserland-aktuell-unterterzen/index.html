Full description

The forest region 3 Sargans is characterized by protected forests and covers the forest area of the municipalities Sargans, Vilters-Wangs, Bad Ragaz, Pfäfers, Mels, Flums, Walenstadt and Quarten. The 17'700 ha forest is mostly located at steep locations up to an altitude of 2000 meters above sea level. 80% of the forest area is owned by the public authorities and 20% is private forest. The average use volume is about 40'000 meters per year. The management and maintenance is carried out by the forest owners as well as other companies.

The Forest Council is responsible for the strategic management of the forest region. The Regional Forests Manager is responsible for the business. The approximately 12 district forests are under his responsibility.

  • Guided projects of the forest region and cooperation with projects of the cantonal forest office
  • Public relations and contact with the various stakeholders
  • Close cooperation with the cantonal forest office

  • Perennial professional and management experience
