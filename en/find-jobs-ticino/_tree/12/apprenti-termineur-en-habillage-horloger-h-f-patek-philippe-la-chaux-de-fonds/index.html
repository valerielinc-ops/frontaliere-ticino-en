Full description

Mission

  • The Watchmaker's Clothes Terminator is responsible for giving a perfect aesthetic to the constituent parts of a watch such as tiles, box bottoms, bracelet links, clasps or jewellery pieces using different media (cloth discs, brushes) covered with abrasive material. They perform their tasks manually using specific tools.
  • For 3 years, our dual training is provided internally by our experienced Training Manager, in connection with the technical vocational school in Porrentruy.

Responsibilities

  • Learning to organize and prepare work according to directives and operating ranges;
  • Apprenticeship of the careful preparation of machining parts, by deburring and correcting surfaces to obtain a perfectly clean and regular base;
  • Learning different types of surface finishes such as mirror polishing, satining, microboiling, using tools (cloth polishing disc with abrasive paste, satin brush, microbead compressed air) and specific machines.
  • Perform rigorous visual and dimensional checks to verify homogeneity of finishes, absence of defects and compliance with high standards.

Profile sought

  • Vif interest in mechanics, technical drawing and metalworking.
  • Perfect fit with our fundamental attitudes (Quality, Respect, Commitment, Cooperation, Opening up to Change).
  • Good visual acuity.
  • Good concentration capacity.
  • Dexterity and recognized manual skill.
  • Interested persons are asked to send us their complete file (a curriculum vitae, a cover letter, school bulletins for the last 3 years and certificates of previous internships if relevant) in response to this offer.
