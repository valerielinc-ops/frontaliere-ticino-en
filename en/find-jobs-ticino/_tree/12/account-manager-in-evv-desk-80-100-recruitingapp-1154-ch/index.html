Full description

«For our customers, we are the first bank». For us, the first bank means that we focus on their needs and constantly inspire them anew. Do you want to actively shape and develop your business with external asset managers at our headquarters in St. Gallen? Then you are exactly right with us!

This is what you will do.

  • As a first point of contact, you support external asset managers in their daily banking transactions, for example in account openings, account management and stock exchange orders.
  • You clarify customer concerns in various areas, from investment to credit business.
  • Our customer consultants support you in day-to-day business, especially in the preparation and follow-up of customer meetings.
  • For questions about e-banking, you support our customers.
  • You actively participate in projects to optimize work processes and further develop the area.

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed commercial or equivalent education in the financial sector.
  • You already bring your first professional experience in the banking environment.
  • An ongoing or completed further education at the level of HF or FH is advantageous.
  • The investment business interests you and you want to deepen your expertise in this area.
  • When dealing with customers, you convince by a respectful and sovereign attitude.
  • You have very good knowledge of German. Knowledge of English is an advantage.

Information Stefan Gasser, Head of eVV Desk St. Gallen, is happy to answer your questions by telephone 071 227 96 75.
