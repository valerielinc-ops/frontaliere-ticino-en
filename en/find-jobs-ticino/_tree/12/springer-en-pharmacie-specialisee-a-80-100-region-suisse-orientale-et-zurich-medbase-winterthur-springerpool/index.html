The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medical care and acute treatment to rehabilitation. The Medbase Group includes the Medbase Pharmacies, Zum Rose, Zahnarztzentrum.ch, WePractice and Bluecare brands.
