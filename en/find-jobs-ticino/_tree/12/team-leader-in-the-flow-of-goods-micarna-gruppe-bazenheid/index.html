Full description

As a team leader in the flow of goods in our leading meat production company, you ensure that all processes – from incoming goods to delivery – run smoothly. You optimize processes, keep track and ensure that quality and efficiency go hand in hand. Become part of our team and actively shape the flow of goods!

Mansioni

  • Leadership of the team in the flow of goods

Ensuring a smooth flow of goods within the cutting plant and fresh meat production

  • Responsibility for compliance with FIFO principles (First In, First Out)
  • Application and maintenance of the relevant SAP modules, to control and document the movement of goods
  • Planning and monitoring of production processes in the flow of goods

Support in optimizing workflows and processes to increase efficiency and quality Close cooperation with production and quality assurance teams Ensuring compliance with hygiene and quality standards in accordance with internal and legal requirements

  • Implementation and documentation of random checks on movements of goods

Close coordination with other departments such as purchasing, production and logistics Identification of waste (e.g. overproduction, waiting times, unnecessary transport) and implementation of CIP measures (continuous improvement process)

Requirements

  • Several years of experience: Goods Flow Management
  • Completed apprenticeship (EFZ): meat specialist or food sector
  • Knowledge in the control and optimization of goods flows, especially in the fresh meat sector

Experience with the storage and processing of fresh meat products Secure handling of SAP modules

  • Experience with further ERP systems or merchandise management programs
  • Understanding inventory management, supply chains and replenishment management
  • Knowledge of hygiene regulations and quality standards in handling fresh meat
  • Understanding durability data, storage conditions and cold chains

German (negotiable)
