Full description

For our renowned customer, we are looking for committed and experienced carpenters (m/f/d) who will support our customers in demanding construction and renovation projects.

Their tasks

  • Execution of timber construction work for new construction, conversion and refurbishment
  • Construction of roof trusses, wooden facades and interiors
  • Creation and assembly of wooden structures, such as stairs, balconies or carports
  • Repair and repair services of existing wooden structures
  • Cooperation with architects and construction managers in the implementation of building plans
  • Compliance with quality standards and safety regulations on the construction site

Requirements

  • Completed training as carpenter, carpenter or comparable qualification
  • Several years of professional experience in wood construction are advantageous
  • Craftsmanship and good technical understanding
  • Independent and precise working methods as well as teamwork
  • Good physical fitness and resilience
  • Easy communication in German
  • Class B driving licence desirable

We offer

  • Access to versatile construction projects with established and renowned customers
  • Attractive, performance-oriented and contemporary compensation
  • Long-term perspectives or project-based assignments
  • Personal support and support from our recruitment consultant
  • Opportunities for professional development
  • Possibility of permanent employment
