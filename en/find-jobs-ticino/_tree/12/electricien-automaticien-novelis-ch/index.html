Position Overview

Who we are Novelis is driven by the desire to shape together a sustainable world. As a world leader in innovative products and services and the world's largest aluminium recycler, we work in partnership with customers in the automotive, aeronautics, cans and specialty sectors to provide solutions that enhance the benefits of light aluminum. We have a coordinated network of technically sophisticated rolling and recycling operations at 29 sites in 9 countries in North America, South America, Europe and Asia, with 12.7 5 0 employees worldwide. The county seat is Atlanta, United States. Our customers include some of the most important and well-known brands in the world, including Coca-Cola, BMW, Jaguar Land Rover, Airbus, Boeing, Nespresso, Samsung, and others. In addition, Novelis is leading the industry through its sustainability and recycling efforts. Join our mission to create a sustainable world and start working on our website in Sierre, Switzerland as soon as possible Electrical/Automatician (100%)

Responsibility & Qualifications

Your responsibilities

  • You work alone or as a team to improve our production facilities, as well as to provide preventive and corrective maintenance.
  • As part of projects, you are responsible for the installation and commissioning of electrical equipment.
  • The sector of activity extends from strong current distribution to telematics and industrial automation.
  • Documentation of maintenance activities in SAP is part of your tasks.
  • You work in 4 shifts with a picket service (3 weeks a year).

How to convince us

  • Holder of an automatic CFC or electrical installer.
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in the industrial field.
  • Experience in industrial automation and supervisory systems.
  • Knowledge of SAP is an advantage.
  • Interest in self-employment, responsible, effective and teamwork.
  • Great flexibility, motivation and customer orientation.
  • French or German language with good second language knowledge.

Ihr Verantwortlichkeiten

  • Sie arbeiten allein oder im Team an der Verbesserung sowie an der vorbeugenden und krrektiven Wartung unserer Produktionsanlagen.
  • Im Rahmen von Projekten sind Sie für die Installation und Inbetriebnahme von elektrischen Anlagen verantwortlich.
  • Der Tätigkeitsbereich erstreckt sich von der Starkstromverteilung über die industrial Automatisierung bis hin zur Telematik.
  • Die Dokumentation der Wartungsaktivitäten in SAP gehört zu Ihren Aufgaben.
  • Sie arbeiten im 4-Schicht-Betrieb mit Pikettdienst (3 Wochen pro Jahr).

Wie Sie uns überzeugen

  • Inhaber/in eines EFZ als Automatiker/in oder Elektroinstaller/in.
  • Mindestens 3 Jahre Berufserfahrung im industrialen Bereich.
  • Erfahrung in der industrialen Automatisierung und in Überwachungssystemen.
  • SAP-Kenntnisse sind von Vorteil.
  • Interest an selbstständiger, verantwortungsbewusster, effizienter und teamorientierter Arbeit.
  • Hohe Flexibilität, Motivation und kundenorientiertes Denken.
  • Deutsche oder französische Sprache mit fundierten Kenntnissen in der zweiten Sprache.

Location Profile

Novelis的 Sierre is located in the Valais Switzerland employing around 550 people. It mainly focuses on production of aluminium sheet for the automotive market covering the entire process from casting to finishing. The plant also hosts a world-leaving Research & Innovation Center specialized in the development of new automotive sheet alloys surface treatment and joining technologies. In February 2022 together with research institutions and a local energy provider Novelis founded the Net Zero Lab Valais to develop carbon-neutral solutions for aluminum manufacturing. The lab focuses on identifying and implementing innovative solutions to neutralize the carbon footprint of Novelis The Sierre plant supports its community through direct sponsorship of many annual events promoting education sports and culture.
