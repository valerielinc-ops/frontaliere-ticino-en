Introduction

For our Buildings & Infrastructures sector, we are looking for a Technical Respondent whose main mission will be to manage the CUB infrastructure (copper and fibre optics) of the manufacture.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
