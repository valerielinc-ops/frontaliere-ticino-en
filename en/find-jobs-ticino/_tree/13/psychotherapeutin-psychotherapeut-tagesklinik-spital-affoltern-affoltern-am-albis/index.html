Full description

Psychotherapy is part of the Center for Psychiatry and Psychotherapy with the departments inpatient adult psychiatry, old-age psychiatry, mother-child ward, the integrated day clinic and the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic outpatient. To strengthen our team in our integrated day clinic, we are looking for a committed psychotherapist or a psychologist in advanced training. Their tasks

  • Treatment and counselling of patients and their relatives at the Center for Psychiatry and Psychotherapy in the semi-stationary setting
  • Independent planning, implementation and documentation of therapies in case management
  • Report preparation in accordance with the requirements of the clients (ordering doctors, health insurance, daily allowance insurance, accident insurance, IV and others)
  • Interdisciplinary and interprofessional exchange and cooperation
  • Cooperation with allocators and institutions
  • Participation in supervision, intervisions, case meetings and team meetings
  • Leadership of group therapies
  • Moderation of internal further education Your profile
  • You are a benevolent, responsible and resilient personality with a good dose of humor
  • You enjoy working with patients of all age groups of adulthood
  • They appreciate the independent work in the semi-stationary setting, but also the connection to a team and bring openness to different therapeutic directions.
  • You have great interest in interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation
  • You have completed further training as a Swiss-approved psychotherapist or are in advanced training
  • Several years of professional experience are beneficial, if possible also in the conduct of group therapies We offer
  • Very varied, interesting activity in our day clinic
  • Working in a very well-coordinated, supportive, open and creative team
  • Space for self-responsible, self-initiative work with options for co-creation
  • Training opportunities in the form of supervision, intervision and hospital-internal and external training
  • Family institution with appreciative work culture and flat hierarchies
  • Attractive employment conditions with good insurance services Our culture – Your benefits «Bi öis can mer no»: With us it is family and personal. As part of the Affoltern hospital team, you will also benefit from attractive employment conditions and numerous Fringe Benefits: Jokertag, shopping benefits and two free admission subscriptions to the FORTYSEVEN Wellness Spa are just a few examples of our Fringe Benefits. The Hospital Affoltern is the largest employer and training company in the Knonau Office. Around 550 employees work for the well-being of patients every day. Find out more about us in our portrait.
