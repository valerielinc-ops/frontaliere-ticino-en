Full description

Part-time internship (morning) 6-month duration We are looking for an intern with strong technical and interpersonal skills, a can-do mindset, and a strong willingness to learn, broaden their knowledge and expertise, and make a difference. This is an exciting opportunity for a student or recent Engineering graduate who wants to build a professional career in the Property Underwriting sector. About the Role As a Junior Property Underwriter Intern, you will support the team in daily underwriting activities and operational tasks, including risk research, analysis of risk exposure, documentation and file processing, analysis of policy conditions, and participation in commercial activities and negotiations. Key Responsibilities

  • Maintain the portfolio database and ensure it is kept up to date.
  • Follow up on portfolio renewals and related documentation.
  • Support underwriting analyses of new submissions, business practices, and risk assessments for new and existing clients.
  • Assist with the preparation of insurance proposals, documentation, marketing materials, and sales presentations.
  • Contribute to document management and organization to ensure files are complete and accurately processed in our systems.

About You

We are looking for a well-organized, open-minded, and curious individual with a strong interest in learning about insurance and a wide range of related topics.

You should have

  • Fluency in Spanish and English.
  • A proactive approach to problem-solving and ownership of tasks.
  • Experience working within a team and collaborating with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Good organizational and analytical skills.
  • Some experience with commercial insurance lines of business and related products would be an advantage.

About Swiss Re Corporate Solutions Swiss Re is one of the world’s leading providers of reinsurance, insurance and other forms of insurance-based risk transfer. We anticipate and manage risks, from natural catastrophes and climate change to cybercrime.

Swiss Re Corporate Solutions is the commercial insurance arm of the Swiss Re Group. We offer innovative insurance solutions to large and midsized multinational corporations from our approximately 50 locations worldwide. We help clients mitigate their risk exposure, whilst our industry-leading claims service provides them with additional peace of mind.
