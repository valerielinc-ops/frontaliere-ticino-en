The Position Team Lead Engineer SRE Clusters - Digital Operations and Reliability

As Team Lead (Senior Engineering Manager) for SRE in Digital Operations and Reliability (DOR)you will ensure and support the delivery as an entrepreneurial leader, setting focus, removingimpediments, managing stakeholders, and creating efficiencies in collaboration wherever possible.

You are an experienced and entrepreneurial SRE Technical Lead who drives the strategic development and delivery of our global Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) platform and operating model. This is a critical leadership role for a technical driver, who is passionate about creating immense value, pushing technical boundaries, and building resilient, scalable systems from the ground up - and mentor the talent needed to get us there.

You will develop the capabilities of your team members to align with the function’s vision and will encourage your team to self-define their career journey and seek ways to enable development opportunities in the direction of the journey. You will support them in removing roadblocks and by setting focus on their performance management, and make sure to develop your team with the right skills, healthy dynamics, and a sustainable pace.

Description of the Area

  • The Digital Operations and Reliability function ensures the proactive management and maintenance of the optimal performance, reliability, and availability of all RDT enterprise-wide digital platforms and Infrastructure, minimizing disruption to business processes across the value chain, and guaranteeing compliance.
