Full description

Are you enthusiastic about industrial maintenance and would like to contribute to the success of a leading company in Switzerland? At Micarna SA, a company in the Migros industry, you work in a dynamic team. Together, you ensure that our plants run reliably and that production works smoothly.

Luogo di lavoro: Micarna Group, Micarna SA - Courtepin, Rte de l’Industrie 25, 1784 Courtepin

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for the maintenance of gas and oil burners.
  • You perform the replacement of circulators, control systems and motors, installing the same models.
  • You are working on switch cabinets to replace certain components.
  • You take on various tasks in the sanitary sector.
  • You are involved in the installation of heat distributors and feed chains.
  • You take on a 24-hour piquetting service.

Requirements

  • 3-5 years: Experience in industrial maintenance, especially in the field of buildings. Knowledge in the food industry is an advantage
  • Completed basic or continuing education: You have a CFC as an agricultural machinery mechanic, electrician, polymechanic or an education recognised as equivalent
  • You are physically in good shape.
  • You show openness to change.
  • You like to work in a team and promote good cooperation.
  • You work independently and responsibly.
  • You have a driving license.
  • German (flowing)
  • French (good knowledge)
