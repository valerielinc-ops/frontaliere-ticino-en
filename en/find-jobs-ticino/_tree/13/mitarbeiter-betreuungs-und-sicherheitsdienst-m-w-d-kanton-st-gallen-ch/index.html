Full description

We create perspectives for people in a self-responsible life outside of crime and thus make an important contribution to a safe society. The Office for the Imprisonment of Justice of the Department of Security and Justice employs around 260 people in various professional fields. The Saxerriet prison is an open prison for men with a total of 135 seats. It has differentiated implementation concepts and a wide range of workplaces.

What to expect

  • Co-operation in the headquarters, in particular monitoring and coordination of the safety and operational processes as well as operation of the technical safety and communication systems
  • Care of the instructed persons, ensuring the daily routine, performing administrative work
  • Safety tasks such as cell checks, personal checks, etc.
  • Accompanying leisure and group activities
  • Accompaniment of external assistance (doctor, court, transfers, etc.)
  • Close cooperation with internal and external bodies
  • Management of crisis interventions
  • Weekend, night and piquet service

What sets it apart

  • Eidg. Certificate of competence, (specialised) maturity, secondary school card or equivalent
  • Good IT user knowledge, safe handling of digital work equipment and willingness to familiarize yourself with specific security and management systems
  • Stylistic German in word and writing, knowledge of foreign languages is desired
  • Positive attitude towards open prisons and integrity in dealing with people in prison regardless of their crimes and cultures
  • Communication, flexibility, assertiveness and psychological and physical resilience
  • Readiness for shift work
  • Possession of a driving licence for passenger cars
  • Minimum age of 30 years

What we offer

  • A multifaceted and responsible task
  • Professional and motivated working environment
  • Modern infrastructure and contemporary workplace
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Attractive further education opportunities such as the part-time further education as a specialist for the prison system with federal law. Specialist card
