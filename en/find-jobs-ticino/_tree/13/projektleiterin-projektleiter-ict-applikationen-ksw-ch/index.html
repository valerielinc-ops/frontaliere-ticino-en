Full description

You want to introduce applications and medical systems and integrate them into hospital IT in such a way that they work together reliably? At KSW, you lead projects from the first idea to operation. With the departments and ICT teams, you design solutions that support clinical and administrative processes and thus contribute to the best possible care for our patients.

Tasks

  • The introduction and further development of applications and medical systems you manage independently. You control project scope, deadlines, costs and risks.
  • Together with the specialist departments, you clarify and prioritize the requirements for the application so that it supports the workflows and fits the existing system landscape.
  • You develop solution variants and recommend one that fits technically, uses existing standards and can be operated reliably.
  • In the solution architecture, you determine with the ICT teams and suppliers how the systems work together and which data flows via which interfaces.
  • During the implementation, you create specifications, work on the configuration and, if necessary, check the technical feasibility with a prototype.
  • Tests, acceptance and introduction you plan with the participants. Then you hand over the solution with the necessary documentation to the company.

Profile

  • You have an education in computer science, business informatics or medical informatics or bring a comparable qualification.
  • For several years, you have managed projects for applications or medical systems and designed or co-responsible for their integration into an existing system landscape.
  • From the business analysis you know how to translate different requirements of the departments into a feasible concept.
  • You will bring practical experience from application operations and know how to take into account requirements for availability, monitoring and incident management in the technical concept.
  • With specialist departments and technical teams you communicate understandably. Your knowledge of German is very good.
  • You are familiar with the healthcare sector and familiar with the hospital application landscape and current standards such as HL7, FHIR and DICOM.
