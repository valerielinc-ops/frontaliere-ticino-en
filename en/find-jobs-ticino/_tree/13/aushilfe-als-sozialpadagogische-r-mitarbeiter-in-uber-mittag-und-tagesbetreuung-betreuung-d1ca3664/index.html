Full description

Are you looking for a meaningful task in which you can accompany and promote children in everyday school life? In our school-supervising care of the primary school Säli Steinhof, a varied environment, a dedicated team and the chance to bring your pedagogical skills to life with pleasure.

Tasks

  • You actively participate in the support team and create professional, appropriate care and support.
  • You take responsibility for the children's group and lunch.
  • You belgleitet the children in the children's restaurant and take care of various offers over lunch and afternoon
  • You are linguistically versed, orally as in writing.
  • You work mainly during school weeks, from Monday to Friday, from about 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. or until 6 p.m. as a temporary assistant/job representative

Requirements

  • You prefer to have a completed pedagogical training.
  • You have experience and joy in accompanying children from 6 to 13 years of age in groups, promoting them and supporting them in their everyday lives.
  • You feel in the children's world and are confidently dealing with culturally and age-mixed groups.
  • You convince yourself by your empathic, appreciative and team-oriented way and actively integrate yourself into our school life.
  • You work independently, reliably and communicate openly, clearly and transparently.
  • You have a completed German certificate B2
