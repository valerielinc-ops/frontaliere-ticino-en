Full description

Are you a seasoned lending professional with deep Lombard expertise and a strong sense of ownership? Do you enjoy leading people while remaining hands on in complex transactions and strategic initiatives? We are looking for a Director – Team Lead Lombard Lending & Deposits to lead the delivery of secured lending and deposits solutions in Taiwan. This is a senior role combining client-facing origination, product ownership and line management responsibility, with accountability for business growth, risk stewardship and people development.

In this role, you will be accountable for

Lombard Lending Origination & Structuring

  • act as the senior subject matter expert for Lombard lending in Taiwan, supporting Client Advisors and clients across the end to end financing lifecycle
  • originate, structure and execute complex and bespoke Lombard lending transactions

o act as transaction lead for higher-risk or strategically important cases o provide subject matter expertise and guidance for smaller/ less complex transactions

  • develop and promote leverage solutions to client advisors to generate client interest and leverage demand
  • drive sales momentum and conversion by identifying lending opportunities within existing and new client relationships
  • provide expert guidance on pricing, leverage, structuring and risk trade offs, ensuring appropriate and sustainable use of credit
  • participate in selected direct client interactions alongside Client Advisors to support origination, execution and client education

Deposits Business Ownership & Pricing

  • serve as the local functional owner for the deposits business in Taiwan, with responsibility for product oversight, pricing discipline and commercial outcomes
  • perform deposit pricing and balance sheet analysis, supporting business decisions and sales initiatives
  • design and drive deposit sales campaigns in close partnership with control functions and frontline leadership
  • liaise with Group Treasury and Investment Banking on funding considerations, pricing frameworks and group balance sheet preferences
  • ensure clear and consistent communication of deposit strategy, pricing guidance and constraints to the business

People & Line Management

  • line manage, coach and develop two team members, setting clear objectives and supporting their professional growth
  • allocate deal responsibilities and product tasks appropriately, ensuring high-quality execution and knowledge transfer
  • foster a strong team culture grounded in collaboration, accountability, risk awareness and client focus
  • provide structured feedback, performance input and career guidance in line with UBS people principles

Product, Risk & Control Responsibilities

  • maintain ownership of the local Lombard and deposits product shelf, including product maintenance, governance and pricing frameworks
  • ensure compliance with local regulation and UBS internal policies, fulfilling first-line risk management responsibilities
  • oversee portfolio monitoring, reporting and issue remediation in partnership with Risk, Legal and Compliance
  • stay abreast of market developments, competitor activity and regulatory change, proactively adapting local offerings

Strategy, Change & Stakeholder Management

  • develop and execute local lending and deposits growth strategies aligned with regional and global objectives
  • contribute to new product development and enhancement initiatives with Global Lending distribution teams
  • act as the local interface with IT and change teams on system enhancements, process improvements and digitisation initiatives
  • collaborate extensively across Wealth Management, Credit Risk, Investments and Banking to deliver integrated client solutions
