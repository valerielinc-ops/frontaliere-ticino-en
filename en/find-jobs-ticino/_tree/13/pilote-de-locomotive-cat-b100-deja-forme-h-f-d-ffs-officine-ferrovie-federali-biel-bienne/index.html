Cat locomotive pilot. B100 already formed (m/f/d) 60-100%

Move Switzerland with us. Cat locomotive pilot. B100 already formed (m/f/d) Biel/Bienne, 01.12.2026 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

At SBB Cargo, you are at the heart of the Swiss economy. SBB Cargo transports 185 000 tonnes per day, thus relieving the road of 15,000 journeys per truck per day and the environment of 460,000 tonnes of CO2 per year. An innovative working environment, in which you move the CFF freight with your teammates. At SBB Cargo, you are at the heart of the Swiss economy. Attractive working conditions are important to us, even in the case of irregular working hours. Night and weekend work are also paid at home - for example, 16 francs per hour for Sunday work. For night missions, you also benefit from time supplements of between 10 and 30%. This means that you have more free time than employees who do not work at night or on weekends. What you can move.

  • As a category B100 Cargo locomotive pilot, you travel at speeds of up to 100 km/h in your area and deliver and retrieve freight cars from customers. You circulate goods to ensure the quality of life in Switzerland.
  • You make sure along the railway lines of Switzerland that our customers' goods arrive at their destination safely and on time.
  • You take the lead in the convoy and respond to the safety and punctuality of freight trains on a busy rail network.
  • You take on the role of specialist in safe, appropriate (articulate) and ecological driving of different freight trains.
  • You manage complex manoeuvring movements, act independently, enter business and operating data into digital systems, and ensure the customer relationship in this context.

What you bring.

  • Team spirit and willingness to work according to irregular schedules and weekends.Physical and psychic resistance; independent and responsible working methods; ability to act correctly and deliberately.
  • In possession of the Cat. B100 Locomotive Pilot Licence.
  • Knowledge of the requirements specific to freight traffic is an asset, you are willing to acquire the skills necessary for the full execution of the transport mandate (radio remote control, VOT, EWC).
  • Oral and written understanding of the language of the place (mother tongue level); knowledge of a second national language (certified level A1).
