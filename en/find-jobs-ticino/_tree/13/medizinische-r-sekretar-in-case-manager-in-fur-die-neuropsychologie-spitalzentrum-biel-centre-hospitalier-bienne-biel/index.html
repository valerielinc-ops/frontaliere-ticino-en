Full description

The Biel Hospital Center is the public center hospital of the bilingual Biel-Seeland-Berne Jura region. Are you interested in working alongside our 2,300 employees for the health of our population?

The Department of Neuropsychology carries out independent and interprofessional investigations of diseases from the entire neurological and part of the developmental neurological spectrum. A memory clinic is also integrated into neurology. From 01.01.2027 or by agreement for an indefinite period we are looking for a

Medical Secretary - Case Manager for Neuropsychology

You do

  • You will take care of the administrative and organizational support of the neuropsychology team, in particular the processing of examination requests, scheduling and coordination of appointment calendars
  • You are an important point of contact for our patients and their relatives, whom you receive, advise and accompany throughout the course of their treatment.
  • You are also the contact person for our external and internal partners (referring doctors, therapists, universities, social services, etc.)
  • You prepare the consultations and files, take care of the administrative follow-up after the investigations as well as the sending and archiving of the reports
  • You will contribute to the implementation of the recommendations recommended after the neuropsychological examinations by facilitating processes and contacts with the professionals and institutions concerned
  • You support the neuropsychology team administratively in its daily tasks and participate in various projects and institutional measures

This sets you apart

  • An administrative or commercial education, a high school or vocational school or equivalent education
  • Additional training or experience in the medical, social or medical-administrative field as well as experience in dealing with patients are beneficial
  • Very good knowledge of French and German, both oral and written
  • Excellent organizational, planning and coordination skills, combined with care, independence, flexibility and initiative
  • Good knowledge of common IT tools and safe handling of various administration and clinic software programs
  • Strong sociability, empathy and trustworthy handling of data protection

You will benefit from this

  • A varied and challenging job in close cooperation with a team of neuropsychologists and numerous internal and external partners in an interdisciplinary and bilingual environment.
  • A central role in the organization of the treatment process and the care of our patients.
  • A position that offers independence, responsibility and opportunities to further develop your skills.
  • Safe and advanced working conditions.
  • A geographically very central workplace and a modern environment.
