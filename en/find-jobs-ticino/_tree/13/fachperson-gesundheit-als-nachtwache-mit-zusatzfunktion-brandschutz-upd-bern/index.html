University Hospital for Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

The University Psychiatric Center Bern (UPZ) offers a wide range of psychiatric treatment for people with different clinical conditions. Our employees work with expertise, commitment and humanity for the mental health of our patients. We attach great importance to interprofessional cooperation, mutual appreciation and an environment in which our employees can develop professionally and personally. As an additional night watch you support the professional care of our patients on the stations of the adult psychiatry with the Dipl. nurses. In addition, you take on the defined tasks within the scope of the fire protection project. You can expect a motivated, collaborative team that supports each other, cooperates competently and values an appreciative atmosphere. Do you feel addressed? We look forward to meeting you.

Your tasks

  • You support patients with psychiatric conditions with their needs during the night
  • If necessary, you take on tasks in 1:1 care
  • You will conduct cross-station inspections for safe maintenance of operations
  • You are responsible for the tasks that include the fire protection concept of the clinic

Your profile

  • You have completed an education as a health professional and already gained initial professional experience
  • You have a strong professional, social and self-competence
  • You communicate clearly and empathetically. You are a reliable and responsible personality
  • You have good knowledge of German
  • You are interested in measures for fire protection

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
