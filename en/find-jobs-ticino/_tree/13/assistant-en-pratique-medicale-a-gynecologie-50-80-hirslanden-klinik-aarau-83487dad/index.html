Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for cutting-edge medicine in a manageable, personal environment - true to our clinic goal "First-class medicine with personal treatment, as we wish for our loved ones".

For the reopening of our gynecological practice we are looking for a medical practice assistant (a) 60-70%:

YOUR TASKS

  • You will be involved in building up our gynecological practice, bringing your experience and ideas to bear.
  • You take over the telephone consultation of our patients and ensure efficient coordination and organization of the consultation hours including a warm welcome in everyday practice.
  • For gynecological examinations, pregnancy checks, ultrasound examinations and minor procedures, you are a competent and reliable support
  • Delegated medical activities such as blood sampling, urine analysis, laboratory work and CTG controls are carried out independently and with great care
  • You actively contribute to the compliance with our high hygiene and quality standards and take care of the professional preparation and sterilization of the instruments

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a medical practice assistant (MPA) EFZ or an equivalent qualification
  • Ideally, you already have experience in gynecology and obstetrics and have even participated in the opening, establishment or further development of a doctor’s office.
  • Laboratory analyses you perform routinely and reliably
  • You are familiar with practical software, as well as practical administration, performance recording and billing
  • You work very organized, structured and independent and keep an overview even in lively situations
  • You are a resilient, flexible and team-oriented personality who feels comfortable in dynamic everyday practice.

Does that sound like you? Then we may have just the right thing for you – because we have a lot to offer.We expect you to have a working environment that is characterized by real appreciation, strong team spirit and a good dose of humor. In addition, there is a varied job, attractive career and development opportunities, a family together, at least 25 holiday days, 4 additional paid days off as compensation for the changing time – and many other employment highlights.
