# Lecturer-researcher / Lecturer-researcher in conservation and restoration of mural paintings

The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI) puts in competition a position of

Lecturer/researcher in conservation and restoration of wall paintings

at the

Institute of materials and buildings

of the Department of Environment Construction and Design based in Mendrisio. Degree of employment of 100%, with fixed-term contract of maximum duration of two years and possibility of renewal. Entry into service 01.01.2027 or date to be agreed.

Responsibility and activity

The Conservation and Restoration Sector of the Institute of Materials and Construction, under the programme Raphael Legacy (2026–2032) in collaboration with the

World Monuments Fund

, a multiannual project of conservation and restoration of the cycle of mural paintings of Villa Imperiale in Pesaro (Italy) and the new Master of Advanced Studies in Wall Paintings Conservation, which forms on the field a new generation of conservative-restorers of mural paintings. To support the programme manager in the continuous research and training component, we are looking for a teacher-researcher/trice who combines practical expertise in the preservation of wall paintings, applied research experience and passion for teaching. The tasks envisaged are:

  • Collaboration with the design and implementation of the conservation and restoration programme of the wall paintings of Villa Imperiale: interpretation of study data, proposals for intervention and multiannual project.
  • Coordinate the activities of the conservation and restoration site: supervision of students in the field modules, management of materials and tools, safety, documentation produced in the yard.

Requirements

  • Master's degree in wall paintings conservation recognised in Switzerland and Europe

  • Experience in supervising or mentoring others and in coordinating fieldwork projects

  • Sound methodological approach, practical skills and up-to-date knowledge of conservation methods and materials.

  • Working knowledge of health and safety requirements for fieldwork.

  • Excellent command of Italian and English (C1); other languages are an asset.

  • Qualification as Conservator-Restorer of cultural heritage under Italian regulations is an asset.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
