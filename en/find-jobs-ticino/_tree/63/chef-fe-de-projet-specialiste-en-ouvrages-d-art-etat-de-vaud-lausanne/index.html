Full description

The Directorate-General for Mobility and Roads DGMR is looking for a project manager specialising in works of art for the Art and Damage section of its infrastructure division.

  • Planning and piloting projects related to bridges and support structures, building on internal and external resources.
  • Ensure compliance with applicable orders, laws, directives or standards in the context of the implementation of management-selected directions.
  • Manage the cantonal heritage of bridges, walls, stacades, etc. and organize monitoring by planning periodic inspections.
  • To monitor the services provided by different service providers and businesses.
  • Manage and issue special transportation authorizations. Please submit a complete application file (CV, cover letter, diplomas, work certificates, etc.). For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Stéphane Corthay, Head of the Art and Damage section of the forces of nature, at 021 316 72 22.
  • Diploma of civil engineer EPF or HES, or equivalent, in a field useful for the job.
  • Experience of at least 5 years in a civil engineering branch (technical offices, companies) for project or work management services.
  • Organizational and planning faculties; analytical and synthesis capabilities.
  • Knowledge of laws, regulations and directives in all areas in which the art specialist may be designated as a mission specialist.
  • Driver's licence (category B) required.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
