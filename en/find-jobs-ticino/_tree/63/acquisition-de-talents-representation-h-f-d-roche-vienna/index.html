Full description

At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. Our culture encourages personal expression, open dialogue, and genuine connections, where you are valued, accepted and respected for who you are, allowing you to thrive both personally and professionally. This is how we aim to prevent, stop and cure diseases and ensure everyone has access to healthcare today and for generations to come. Join Roche, where every voice matters.

The Position In this position, you play a decisive role in attracting the best talent for Roche in Austria (Roche Diagnostics and Roche Pharma). You act far beyond classic recruiting. You are consultant:in, networker:in and brand ambassador:in at the same time.

You will become part of a global recruiting team, share your recruiting and consulting expertise and work closely with local and global people & culture interfaces.

The position is for the time being limited to the duration of the waiting period.

What to expect

  • End-to-end recruiting: You control the entire recruitment process – from sourcing, interviews and selection to offering and negotiation
  • Decision support: You moderate the decision-making process together with hiring managers to hire the best talent
  • Stakeholder management & consulting: You advise colleagues & executives as a professional:r contact person:in at eye level in all questions about talent acquisition
  • Agile process design: You drive the further development of our recruiting processes towards more agility & digitalization, question existing issues constructively and develop solution-oriented improvement impulses
  • Teamwork & project work: You are actively involved in our People & Culture team and work in local and global projects in the area of People & Culture

Who you are

You will bring in-depth knowledge of talent acquisition, have strong communication skills and are enthusiastic about continuous exchange and relationship building with internal and external stakeholders. Furthermore, understand the business of your specialist areas and act on an equal footing with the business managers.

  • Several years of experience in the field of talent acquisition ideally in the multinational matrix environment
  • Very good communication skills and enjoy building sustainable relationships at all hierarchical levels
  • Fluent German & English skills in word and writing
  • Know-how with common recruiting and sourcing tools (Workday, LinkedIn, Phenom etc.) is an advantage
  • Independent working methods and flexible in cooperation with local and global teams

You can expect an annual salary of € 50,000.00 gross full-time, as well as a performance-oriented bonus and attractive benefits. Company restaurants, flexible working time models, company health promotion and a wide range of sports offers are just some of them. Depending on qualification and experience, there is a willingness to overpay

Let’s build a healthier future, together.
