Looking for a carpenter or carpenter job?

For one of our clients, a family company active in carpentry, we are looking for a motivated profile to strengthen its team in the Conthey region.

The position is mainly oriented towards laying and carpentry work on construction sites in Valais.

Responsibilities

  • Performing various carpentry installation works
  • Install layout elements
  • Installing doors and windows
  • Make the necessary adjustments and finishes
  • Ensure the quality of the work performed
  • Observe site instructions and requirements

Your profile

  • Training in carpentry, CFC or equivalent: an asset
  • Multipurpose, rigorous and motivated person
  • Good team spirit and good communication
  • Driver's licence and vehicle: an asset

We offer

  • A long-term mission
  • A fixed possibility of engagement depending on the course of the mission
  • Personalized support by our agency throughout your mission

We also support you in your professional development through ongoing training related to your profession, supported by the Temptraining Training Fund.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
