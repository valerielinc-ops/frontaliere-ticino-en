Rim painter (a) With the rim center, we offer a tailor-made offer for car dealers and workshops for professional rim repair. Supported by the latest technology, trained employees ensure maximum efficiency. They take care of the processing of the rims from paint stripping and cleaning to CNC turning, laser welding, painting and powder coating to safe packaging. To strengthen our team, we are looking for you rim painter (a). Task area

  • Coating and powder coating of rims
  • Independent quality control
  • Assistance in pre-treatment, cleaning, hanging, sticking, control, hanging, packaging

  • Several years of experience in the painting and powder coating of aluminum or metal parts, ideally of rims
  • High quality standards, operational readiness and flexibility
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
