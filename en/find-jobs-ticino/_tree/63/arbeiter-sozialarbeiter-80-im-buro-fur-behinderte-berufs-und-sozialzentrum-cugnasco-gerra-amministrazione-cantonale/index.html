Full description

Tasks

  • organize and promote individual or group observation activities
  • co-operate in the development of individualized projects for users included in the Professional Insurance and Orientation Area (Youth Insurance and Educational and Abitative Service) and apply the decisions defined during network meetings
  • Ensure insured persons included in the foyer an educational accompaniment and an individual development plan
  • accompany the individualised path of persons benefiting from a professional measure of invalidity insurance
  • to provide for the organization and operation of the Interaction and Professional Orientation Area
  • contribute to the drafting of the reports requested by the UAI mandate for the assigned user
  • actively participate in interdisciplinary work groups
  • ensure operational flexibility according to service needs

Requirements

  • bachelor SUPs in social work or equivalent securities
  • proven work experience with fragile users
  • knowledge of the territory and the social network of the Canton Ticino
  • motivation and spirit of initiative
  • organizational and decision-making skills
  • excellent editorial, communication and mediation skills
  • availability in the evening and night work
  • excellent knowledge of the Microsoft Office package
  • availability of continuing training
  • licence to conduct cat. B
  • good mastery of the Italian language (conversation and editing) and knowledge (conversation and editing) of a second official language
  • are preferential title:
  • knowledge of invalidity insurance and in particular its professional measures
  • experience with teenagers in ascertainment and in foyer
