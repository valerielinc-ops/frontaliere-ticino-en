Full description

Is it your pleasure to lead and promote employees? With you, we gain a motivated leader who actively tackles as a role model. Apply now and take responsibility!

You can do that.

  • As a team leader, you lead a team of about 10-15 employees professionally and personnel and are responsible for comprehensive communication and information in the team.
  • Together with your deputy, you will form a strong management duo: you will prepare the operational planning, hold discussions with employees and lead team meetings.
  • You create the framework for good team performance, attach importance to quality and compliance with safety regulations.
  • With your commitment, you lead and develop your team with concrete goals and you act as a contact person and coach. In order to live up to your role model, you actively participate in day-to-day business.

You bring that with you

  • Ideally, you have completed a logistics training and have several years of experience in delivery.
  • Your leadership experience helps you to quickly gain a foothold in the new function. Organizational flair, flexibility and the ability to keep track of hectic situations set you apart.
  • You put the contribution as a team above your own goals, set the right priorities and communicate objectively and transparently.
  • You are physically fit, like to take action and, if necessary, also question existing processes in a solution-oriented manner.
  • Confident knowledge of German in word and writing and advanced MS-Office skills round off your profile.
