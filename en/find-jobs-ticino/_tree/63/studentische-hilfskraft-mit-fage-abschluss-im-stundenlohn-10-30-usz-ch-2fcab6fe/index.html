USZ Campus

1. February 2026 by agreement

Are you studying midwife or nursing and looking for a side job? We are looking for students with completed FAGE training who would like to support our team on an hourly basis. The assignments are made by arrangement and can be reconciled well with the study.

Your tasks

  • You are the first contact and information point for the department.
  • You work mainly on weekends, in shortened late services (e.g. 16.00 to 22.00) and if possible in individual services during the week.
  • You independently take on administrative, organizational and nursing tasks.
  • You support the team in simple nursing and organizational activities, such as pregnancy checks or care of newborns.

Your profile

  • Completed training as a specialist / health professional
  • Ongoing studies as a midwife or nurse
  • Very good knowledge of German and good knowledge of English in word and writing
  • Maintained manners and high service orientation towards patients: inside and other contact persons

This is what we offer you

  • A varied activity in one of the largest perinatal centers in Switzerland
  • High degree of independence in the area of responsibility
  • Individual and targeted introduction to the field
  • The opportunity to develop both professionally and personally
  • An interdisciplinary and dedicated team that welcomes you
  • Operational planning in consultation with the team

Kita on site

In our in-house daycare centers, your children will be cared for lovingly. Long opening hours without company holidays and subsidized care places relieve you in everyday life.

Health

We support your health. In addition to personal consultations and courses of our health management that promote your well-being, we offer you access to over 120 sports with a subsidized annual subscription to the ASVZ.

Flexibility

We promote a balanced work-life balance: Almost half of our employees work part-time, and about 50% of the tasks can be done in the home office – if your working day allows it.

Family friendliness

We help you to reconcile family and work. Mothers are entitled to at least 6 months of maternity leave (of which 16 weeks are paid in full), fathers to at least 2 months of paternity leave (of which 2 weeks are paid in full).

Further education and development

We accompany you on your professional path and support your development with individual career and further education opportunities.

Insurance

We cover 60% of your pension fund contributions and offer attractive special conditions for supplementary insurance.

Mobility

You benefit from a personal ZVV BonusPass at a special price or receive a fixed amount of money for a general subscription (GA) or half-tax PLUS.

Telephony
