Don’t forget to submit the official application form available on the Justice Council website.

Description of the Office

Assistants hold a secondary judicial office and have judicial decision-making powers (Art. 4 JG). They shall be provided as required by the authority concerned. The attendants receive session allowance in accordance with Art. 79a JR. Members are elected by the Grand Council on the opinion of the Judiciary Council. The Judicial Council advertises the judicial offices for the Grand Council (Art. 12 JG).

Desired profile

  • The Judicial Council is looking for a person who has the necessary time availability to attend meetings during the day
  • Residence in the relevant judicial district (Saane District)
  • Voting rights in cantonal matters. Foreign nationals must hold a residence permit and have been resident in the canton for at least five years. There shall be no loss certificates against the persons. They must not have been convicted of acts incompatible with the judicial office
  • Mastering the German or French language with very good knowledge of the other language
