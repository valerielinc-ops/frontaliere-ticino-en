Full description

Corporate Development / Strategy Workload: 50-100% Location: Level of experience: Manager FlexOffice: on-site Do you want to help shape the future of Helvetia Switzerland's private and occupational pensions and make well-founded decisions based on data? You will lead a team at the interface of pensions, product development, risk management and digitalisation. Together, you will develop pricing strategies and improve the profitability of the pension business. In doing so, you will use technologies such as artificial intelligence and automation to create long-term competitive advantages. With strategic thinking and analytical skills, you will further develop our pension business and contribute to the long-term success of the company.

What you do

  • You will develop the pricing and profitability strategy for the Swiss pension business. In this way, you will ensure its long-term competitiveness and profitability.
  • Together with the departments involved, you will develop new pension solutions and improve existing products.
  • You will identify insurance and business risks at an early stage and take measures to ensure the long-term profitability of the pension portfolio.
  • You will be responsible for the development and improvement of tariff and pricing models and ensure their quality and marketability.
  • Together with IT, you will ensure that tariffs and provisions are correctly mapped in the management systems. You will also accompany migrations.
  • You will use data analysis, automation and artificial intelligence to further digitize the Pricing & Analytics department.
  • You will analyse the financial development of the pension business, identify potential for improvement and derive concrete measures.
  • You will implement regulatory requirements and contribute to internal and external reports.
  • You lead your team, promote the professional and personal development of your employees and create an environment for good cooperation and strong performance.
  • You will work closely with pensions, product management, underwriting, actuarial services, risk management, sales, IT, compliance and finance to develop long-term sustainable solutions.

What you bring to the table

  • Completed university degree in mathematics, actuarial science, economics, statistics, data science or a comparable field.
  • Several years of professional experience in the insurance, pensions, pricing or actuarial sector as well as in the management of complex, cross-divisional projects.
  • Sound experience in the management of specialists. You promote talents and accompany your team safely through changes.
  • In-depth knowledge in the development of tariff and pricing models and in the analysis of complex data, risk and business models.
  • Strategic thinking skills. You take into account the market, finances and risks and derive long-term sustainable decisions from them.
  • Experience with transformation, digitalization or automation projects, ideally with AI-supported solutions and data-based methods.
  • Initiative, decisiveness and entrepreneurial thinking.
  • You communicate clearly, involve the relevant contact persons and enjoy working with various areas such as pensions, product management, sales, actuarial services, risk management, IT and finance.
  • Very good knowledge of German and good English skills.
