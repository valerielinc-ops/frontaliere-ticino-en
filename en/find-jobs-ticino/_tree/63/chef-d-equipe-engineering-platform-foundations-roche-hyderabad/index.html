Description of the area

The Engineering Excellence & Experience (E3) organization builds the robust engineering bedrock that powers the future of medicine. We deliver foundational application lifecycle platforms, core developer platforms and AI tools required to build software seamlessly. By embedding application security and testing solutions directly into the engineering workflow, democratizing software delivery via low-code tools for non-technical creators, and surfacing data-driven insights, E3 accelerates secure, high-quality software delivery enterprise-wide. As part of the E3 organization, the Developer Platform team champions the provisioning of foundational, AI-enhanced tools and capabilities that empower developers across Roche. The Platform Foundations team, which is part of the Developer platform area, will offer solutions as per Roche enterprise standard in 3 main areas - Source Code Management, Artifact Management, and CI/CD Solutions. The team will provide a range of continuously evolving platforms fit for emerging technical complexities and accelerated development workflows, evaluation of market trends to efficiently incorporate new capabilities to support developers, full end-to-end lifecycle management of the platforms from new feature requests to delivery and maintenance, with on-going support through and after implementation to rapidly resolve user issues.
