Full description

This is what awaits you

In this function, you head the Legal & Compliance department and are responsible for the legal service function for the Insurance (Non-Life) management area for overarching topics outside of damage management.

This is what you do with us

As Head: In the Legal & Compliance department, you are responsible for the professional and human leadership of six employees. Together with them, you strive for a cooperative, customer-oriented and solution-oriented leadership culture.

One of your tasks is the strategic development of the department. You are responsible for various tasks such as insurance law, property insurance law, building insurance law, supervisory law, data protection law and compliance. In addition, you support initiatives in the area of non-life and implement new legal frameworks and regulatory requirements. In doing so, you actively shape the digital development of legal and compliance work and use the possibilities of AI and new technologies to effectively develop processes, consulting and knowledge management.

As a decentralized compliance function, you represent the insurance management unit in appropriate committees. You will advise the line on all legal issues regarding products and new offers and participate in projects.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed legal studies, possibly supplemented with a lawyer's patent
  • Proven professional experience in the legal sector in the field of the insurance industry, in particular in the non-life business
  • Leadership experience as well as project management experience are beneficial
  • High digital affinity and understanding of the potentials, risks and practical use of AI and new technologies
  • Experience with legal technology, data or automation solutions is an advantage
  • Customer-oriented, high-negotiation personality with a strong flair for cooperation with different stakeholders
  • Open, communicative and practice-oriented working methods
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, good French and possibly. Italian knowledge

Working with us

The Legal and Compliance team is waiting for you, who puts their expertise at the service of the organization.

Your place of work is Bern and the workload is negotiable from 80%.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
