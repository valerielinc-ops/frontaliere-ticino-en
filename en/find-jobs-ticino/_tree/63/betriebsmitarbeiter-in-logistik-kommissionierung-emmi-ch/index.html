Full description

Do you want to work in a dynamic logistics environment and actively contribute to our products finding their way to our customers reliably and on time? In this role, you are part of a dedicated team and take on a central role in our daily logistics processes. If you like to tackle, take responsibility and value structured and safe work processes, we look forward to getting to know you.

You can do that.

  • When picking, you make sure that goods from the warehouse are ready for shipping on time.
  • In your area of responsibility, you take over simple logistics services for external customers.
  • You keep an overview at all times and ensure that all products are available at the right place at the right time.
  • In everyday work, you pay attention to safe, clean and smooth processes.
  • With your ideas, you are actively involved and continuously shape our processes.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training as a logistician: in EFZ or EBA.
  • Readiness to work in 2-shift operation (early shift: Mo up to and with Sa 6:00 h - 14:30 h, late shift: Mo up to 14:00 h - 23:30 h).
  • You have good knowledge of German (at least level B1) and are able to read, understand and implement work instructions independently.
  • Team spirit and communication are not foreign words for you.
  • Working in the cold is no problem for you.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Sabbatical & Holiday Shopping: Give yourself unpaid holidays or extend your holiday entitlement by up to two additional weeks.
  • Service Anniversary: Loyalty pays off: Every five years, we reward your loyalty with a premium, which you can also receive in the form of holidays or as a one-time contribution to the pension fund.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Further education and development opportunities: Emmi focuses on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Lucerne?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Lucerne's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Lucerne's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
