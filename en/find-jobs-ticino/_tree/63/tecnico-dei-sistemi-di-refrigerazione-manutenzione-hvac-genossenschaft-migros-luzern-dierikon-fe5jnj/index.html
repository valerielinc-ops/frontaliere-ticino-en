Full description

Are you interested in many things and would like to develop further? In a small team you manage the refrigeration system of the plant headquarters in Dierikon. Due to the wide range of tasks, you dive deep into the technology and take on a responsible task. Don’t worry, QUALITY is important to us – a detailed training awaits you! Apply now.

Luogo di lavoro: Cooperative Migros Lucerne, registered office Dierikon, Industriestrasse 2, 6036 Dierikon

Important information

Fixed workplace and piket service only on weekends. Mediation fee: CHF 5'000.00

Mansioni

  • Responsibility for the independent performance of maintenance, repair work and troubleshooting of industrial refrigeration systems
  • Main responsibility with teammate for all refrigeration systems in the operations centre
  • Ensuring operational readiness with regard to operational safety, hygiene and economic efficiency
  • Coordination and management of external specialists
  • Participation in the planning and implementation of preventive maintenance work in the areas of sanitary, heating, ventilation and air conditioning (according to the existing concept)
  • Monitoring of input stocks

Requirements

  • Strong willingness to get involved: in the subject area
  • Completed basic or further training: as a refrigeration system installer and the willingness to familiarize yourself with a new discipline (CO2/NH3)
  • Reliable and accurate working methods
  • Communication and team skills
  • Self-employment and flexibility
  • German (good knowledge)
