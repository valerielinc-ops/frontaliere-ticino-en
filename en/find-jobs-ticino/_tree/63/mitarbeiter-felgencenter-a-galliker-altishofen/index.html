Full description

Employee Rim Center (a) With the rim center, we offer a tailor-made offer for car dealers and workshops for professional rim repair. Supported by the latest technology, trained employees ensure maximum efficiency. They take care of the processing of the rims from paint stripping and cleaning to CNC turning, laser welding, painting and powder coating to safe packaging. To strengthen our team, we are looking for you as an employee Relgencenter (a). Task area

  • Handling incoming and outgoing goods
  • Check rims for damage and set repair scope
  • Assistance in pre-treatment, cleaning, hanging, sticking, control, hanging, packaging
  • Wheel rims turn on CNC machine

Requirement profile

  • Basic training as a polymechanic or machine mechanic
  • solution-oriented, flexible, reliable and independent
  • High quality standards, operational readiness and flexibility
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Offer

  • Personal development opportunities in a future-oriented, versatile company
  • Top modern wheel center and advanced infrastructure
  • Cool, motivated and collaborative team
  • Good public transport connections and free parking
