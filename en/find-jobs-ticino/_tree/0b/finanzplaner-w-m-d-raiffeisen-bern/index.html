19.10.2026 Bern

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Bern, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices at the Orphanage Square and in Muri b. Bern, we are looking for a committed, proactive and service-oriented personality as a financial planner (m/f/d) 70-100%.

What do you expect?

  • Comprehensive advice on challenging issues relating to retirement, pension provision, property and inheritance law and asset structuring.
  • Development of individual and needs-based solutions taking into account the personal goals and life situations of our customers.
  • Identify and exploit customer needs and potential
  • Actively shape the future of our financial planning business and contribute our own ideas for further development.
  • Cooperation in the planning and implementation of market development measures, customer events and internal training

What do you bring?

  • You have completed the further training as a financial planner with a federal certificate and ideally have further qualifications, for example as CFP®, MAS Financial Consulting or comparable further training.
  • Several years of experience in the field of holistic customer advice and financial planning
  • Very good knowledge in the areas: investing, provisioning, securing and financing
  • You work independently, structured and goal-oriented and convince through your pronounced customer and team orientation.
  • Experience with Allfina/Logismata or comparable financial planning software is beneficial.

Our customers with their needs and goals are in the center. We advise them competently, comprehensively and with passion. Cédric Schmidlin Team leader investment client services Why Raiffeisen?
