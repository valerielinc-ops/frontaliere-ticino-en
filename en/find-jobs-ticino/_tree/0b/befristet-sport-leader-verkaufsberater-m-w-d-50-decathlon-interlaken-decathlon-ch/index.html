Full description

Mission Your Playing Field on Time: Decathlon Interlaken Are you looking for a dynamic job for the winter season and share our enthusiasm for sport and nature? Become part of our team at the Decathlon Store in Interlaken! Nestled between Thuner and Brienzersee and with the world-famous backdrop of Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau right on our doorstep, the ultimate winter sports paradise is right on our doorstep. This is where we equip locals and guests from all over the world for unforgettable days in the snow. Practical information:

  • Contract type: Temporary for 2 months
  • Job: By immediate or by agreement
  • Location: Interlaken, BE Your daily action on the sales floor You are not looking for an 08/15 desk job, but want to share your passion for sports? This is what awaits you:
  • In the middle of the action: Your everyday life takes place 100% on the sales floor. You advise beginners: inside and professionals and find the perfect equipment.
  • Taking action: From incoming goods to the attractive presentation of goods ("facing") to the lightning-fast preparation of click & collect orders.
  • Living a circular economy: You not only "sell" but also inspire our sustainable rental and second-life offers. This job is NOTHING for you if...
  • You prefer to sit quietly behind a screen all day.
  • It is difficult for you to approach people openly and actively.
  • You think unpacking pallets and racking up shelves is “not your job.”
  • You would rather have free Saturdays. Profile that you bring with you
  • Athlete DNA & Maker mentality: You are active, courageous and feel like tackling it every day.
  • Customer focus: Excellent manners and a real sense of service.
  • Language competence: Perfect German (dialect understanding is a plus). Good English or French skills are a great bonus. This is what we offer you (at 100% Pensum)
  • Attractive salary: A fixed gross salary between CHF 4,100 and CHF 5,000 (paid out in 13 monthly wages) plus a monthly team bonus (up to 10% of the salary).
  • Decathlon discount: 25% discount on all our products.
  • Your foot in the door: Even as a temporary team member, you gain valuable experience in a fast-growing, global sports brand – who knows what comes of it! The application process: Fast & uncomplicated No long letter of motivation necessary! Just send us your CV, answer a few short questions, and do our 30-minute personality check (AssessFirst). Afterwards we meet directly in the store to see if the chemistry is right. Ready for the challenge? Then click on "Apply now" and start with us!
