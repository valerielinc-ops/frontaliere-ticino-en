# Professional teacher in Ergotherapy

The Professional University School of Italian Switzerland (SUPSI) puts in competition some positions of for basic training at the Department of Business, Health and Social Economy (DEASS), based in Manno. Degree of employment: 40% Entry into operation: 1 January 2027 or convent.

Responsibility and activity

The selected person carries out teaching activities mainly in neurological and/or geriatric rehabilitation and provides support to students, contributing to the development of training and collaboration with the territory. In particular:

  • It manages teaching activities in the courses provided by the Bachelor's degree plan in Ergotherapy.
  • It participates in the planning of the training modules, ensuring the actualization of the objectives and contents and the reference to the principles and values of the Department.
  • It manages teaching and learning activities and chooses diverse and appropriate methodologies for the purposes, type of course and training context.
  • He cares for the pedagogical relationship and develops educational resources.
  • It maintains updated and is open to professional and pedagogical innovations.
  • It contributes to the general activities of the Department, participates in promotional activities, collaborates in the management of infrastructure and cares the institutional climate.

Requirements

The selected person has a solid academic and professional background in neurological and/or geriatric rehabilitation, with experience in teaching.

They are required

  • Title of study: Bachelor in Ergotherapy and post-training titles, preferably a Master of Science or equivalent titles.
  • Experience: significant experience in rehabilitation of people with neurological and geriatric diseases and in teaching, preferably at a tertiary level.
  • Transversal skills: strong communication, relational and social skills, with predisposition to team work and collaboration.
  • Digital skills: mastery of basic IT tools.
  • Language skills: excellent knowledge of the Italian language, good knowledge of written and spoken English and a second national language.

SUPSI offers

  • A dynamic and constantly changing working environment.
  • Entry into a participatory work team.
  • A network of local and national collaborations.
  • A context that enhances innovation and the quality of professional relationships.
  • Promoting diversity and equal employment opportunities.
