Cosmetic formulator Experienced

Since 1988, Cosmotec SA has been a reference in the field of Swiss cosmetic subcontracting. Located at the foot of the Swiss Alps, our laboratory puts its know-how and modern equipment at the service of many brands, offering them quality products valorizing the label "Made in Switzerland".

In order to strengthen our R&D team, we are looking for an experienced cosmetic trainer with a solid experience in cosmetic product development.

Are you passionate about the world of cosmetics? Do you like to develop new formulas, meet technical challenges and support the projects of our customers, from the formulation in the laboratory to their production?

This job is for you!

Your missions

  • Develop and implement formulae in the R&D laboratory according to specifications,
  • To propose the technical guidelines adapted to the various developments,
  • Select raw materials taking into account technical, regulatory, customer and environmental requirements,
  • Conduct and monitor stability and compatibility tests,
  • Analyze the results and make the necessary adjustments in case of problems of formulation, stability or compatibility,
  • Prepare client submissions and samples in collaboration with the various departments concerned,
  • Establish procedures and manufacturing specifications,
  • Participate in the industrial transposition of formulas and bring your technical expertise to the first manufactures,
  • Providing technical support to Quality Control and other departments where necessary,
  • Participate in supplier meetings, trade shows and training,
  • Share your experience and technical knowledge and contribute to the dynamics of the R\&D team.

Your profile

  • University training in chemistry, biology, physics or equivalent,
  • Minimum 5 years experience in formulation, development of cosmetic products and subcontracting,
  • Experience confirmed in the autonomous conduct of developments and their industrial transposition,
  • Knowledge of Coptis software,
  • Very good knowledge of raw materials, active ingredients, excipients and galenic form,
  • Good knowledge of the cosmetic regulatory environment,
  • Autonomy, rigor and analytical capacity,
  • Creativity, curiosity and taste for innovation,
  • Team spirit and ease in collaboration with different departments,
  • Mastery of French and English.

What we're looking for!

A person passionate about formulation, able to combine technical expertise and creativity, with sufficient experience to take charge of his or her development independently and contribute to the sharing of knowledge within the team.

Do you recognize yourself in this profile and want to take up a new challenge in the field of cosmetic formulation?

We look forward to discovering your application!
