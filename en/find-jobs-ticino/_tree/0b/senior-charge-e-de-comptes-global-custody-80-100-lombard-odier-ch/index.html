Full description

A career at Lombard Odier means working for a renewed global wealth and asset manager, with a strong focus on sustainable investment. An innovative bank of choice for private and institutional clients, our independently owned Firm is one of the best-capitalized banking groups in the world, managing close to CHF 300 trillion and operating from over 25 offices across 4 continents.

With a history spanning over 230 years, Lombard Odier is an investment house providing a comprehensive offering of discretionary and advisory portfolio management, wealth services and custody. We also offer asset management services and investment strategies through Lombard Odier Investment Managers and provide advanced banking technology to other financial institutions.

"Rethink Everything" is our philosophy – it is at the heart of everything we do. We have grown stronger through more than 40 financial crises by rethinking the world around us to provide a fresh investment perspective for our customers.

Senior Global Custody Account Manager (80-100%)

The Global Assets Customer Manager is responsible for the operational management of a portfolio of institutional and private clients in the field of asset servicing. It ensures the implementation of solutions tailored to the needs of clients and the issuance of exchanges. It plays a key role in the smooth functioning of daily processes and in optimizing the quality of the service offered by promoting and facilitating exchanges through the various support teams of the bank.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Manage stock exchange and FX operations, settlement monitoring, OSTs, payments, cash management, cash flows Private Equity (PE) and asset consolidation (asset aggregation).
  • Ensure the accuracy of operations. Accurately enter banking transactions within the specified time limits.
  • Open accounts and coordinate the implementation of operational setups with our support teams and third party providers. Maintain client records, ensuring complete and accurate documentation, in accordance with regulatory and internal requirements.
  • Review financial reporting to ensure accuracy prior to transmission to clients and stakeholders.
  • Improve follow-up of client requests: prioritise, acknowledge receipt, and deliver responses within optimal timeframes.
  • Maintaining an irreproachable quality of service by remaining solution oriented and demonstrating a great sense of customer service.
  • Propose and implement process improvement solutions while ensuring the efficiency of the team.
  • Meet and communicate proactively with clients to ensure satisfaction and meet their specific needs.
  • Establish and maintain strong relationships with our clients' external managers and agents.
  • 5-10 years of experience in the asset serving customer sector, preferably with an institutional and private clientele.
  • Good knowledge of financial operations (bourse, settlements, cash management, PE, transparent funds).
  • Common French and English. Current German would be an asset.
  • Ability to work under pressure rigorously and meticulously.
  • Intellectual curiosity and openness to learning new processes.
  • Great agility to adapt to changing customer needs.
  • Team spirit and strong customer orientation.
  • Be in line with the House's values: Excellence, Innovation, Respect, Integrity and One Team.
