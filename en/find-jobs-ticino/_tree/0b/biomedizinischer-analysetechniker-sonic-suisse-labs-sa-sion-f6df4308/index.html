Full description

Dianalabs Valais is a Valaisan medical analysis laboratory, a company of the Sonic Healthcare International Group.

For our laboratory in Sion, we are looking for

Technician in biomedical analysis between 80% and 100%

Your role:

  • You receive, support and record samples.
  • You perform quality control of the analyses.
  • You are involved in optimising, evaluating and implementing new methods and devices.
  • You are involved in continuous improvement of the quality system and management of non-compliances.
  • The work station includes Saturday morning hours (8am – 12pm) according to established turns.

Your profile:

  • You have an ES diploma in Biomedical Analysis Technician (TAB) or an equivalent qualification recognised by the competent Swiss authorities.
  • You have expertise in the fields of clinical chemistry and hematology analysis.
  • Knowledge or experience in transfusion immunohemology and/or microbiological analysis would be an asset.
  • You have good social and communication skills.
  • You are a motivated, dynamic person, with great interest in analysis and scientific development.
  • You show rigor, proactivity, organizational sense, versatility and autonomy.
  • You have a good command of the usual computer tools (Microsoft Office) and have the adaptability to use new software.
