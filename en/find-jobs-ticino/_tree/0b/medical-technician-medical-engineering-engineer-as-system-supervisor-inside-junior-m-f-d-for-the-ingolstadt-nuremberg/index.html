At Roche you can show up as yourself, embraced for the unique qualities you bring. The Position What to expect: As a system supervisor Junior (m/f/d) You are self-responsible together with your teammate: in the Ingolstadt/Nuremberg/Erlangen area for technical and application customer service. From installation to training, maintenance, modification and troubleshooting, you accompany our systems throughout the entire life cycle for your customer. You take care of analysis systems, reagents and their IT connection and have the following tasks:

  • In your care area, you are the first contact person for the service of our customers and maintain close contact with them in the private laboratory or hospital.
  • Build your technical skills Prove yourself when planning equipment installations, maintenance and repairs.
  • The analysis and correction of complex technical errors as well as the execution of test and routine runs are part of your daily business.
  • You are constantly expanding your knowledge in project management as part of automation projects.
  • You support Roche laboratory systems in the fields of preanalysis, clinical chemistry, immunology and clotting and advise the customers not only on technical, but also on application issues.
  • You accompany the digital transformation and contribute your IT knowledge in particular in network technology, installation and internal projects.
  • You work closely with our sales and in-house service and advise our customers together on the topics of preventive maintenance, service contracts and equipment detachment / new acquisition.
  • You document and invoice your customer services in our CRM system.
  • You bring with you the willingness to take over about 30% of the mentioned service activities across teams and thus throughout Germany. Who you are: For the exciting position as system supervisor Junior (m/f/d) You have completed a degree in engineering or a comparable technical training. In the fields of electronics, mechanics, mechatronics, automation technology, hydraulics, IT & network technology, computer interfaces and measurement technology, You can gain first experience. In addition, you are not only theoretically enthusiastic about technical questions, but also show practical skill. If you have initial professional experience in the service field or in the diagnostic environment, this is an advantage. The extensive training around our systems as well as our comprehensive training make you an expert (m/f/d) in our environment after your start at Roche. These other aspects round off your profile:
  • You are familiar with IT topics: You have fun setting up networks, servers, firewalls, interfaces, etc. and managing them.

  • You are very well organized, have a high level of initiative and communicate safely with different hierarchical levels.
  • You speak German and English fluently, also in the technical environment.
  • You have a driving license and are looking forward to business trips with your company car provided by Roche, which can also be used privately.
  • You start your activities from the home office and are ready to take on emergency and on-call services also on weekends and holidays.
