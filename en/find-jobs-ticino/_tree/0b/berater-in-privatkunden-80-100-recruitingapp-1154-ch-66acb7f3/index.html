Full description

«We are the first bank for our customers». For us, the first bank means constantly inspiring our customers by focusing on their needs. We need you for that! Do you want to support the Bazenheid branch as our new colleague and take over the deputy of the branch management? Then come to us!

This is what you will do.

  • You offer your customers first-class advice and attentive support and ensure goal-oriented market development in the region.
  • You understand how to inspire customers and distribute our products and services in the areas of investing, provisioning and financing.
  • You take over an interesting customer book, which you expand through active market development.
  • In the absence of the branch manager, you take over their deputy and ensure smooth business operations.
  • You are actively present in the market area (public events, events, etc.).
  • You work in a small motivated team and contribute your ideas to further develop the team and the office.

You bring that with you

  • You have a completed banking apprenticeship (or comparable basic training) as well as experience in customer advice, preferably at a bank.
  • You enjoy customer contact, bring good communicative skills and excel through degree strength.
  • You are a team player with a high level of social competence, exemplary commitment and personal responsibility.
  • Good knowledge of regional and local market conditions is an advantage.
