Full description

Directly attached to our Customer Service Workshop and under the responsibility of the Team Leader, your main mission will be to ensure the complete repair of our watches within a team motivated by quality and a productivity objective. In this context:

Responsibilities

  • You will be in charge of repairing recent and vintage watches by intervening on quartz, mechanical, automatic and chronograph calibers
  • You will also analyze the exterior of the watches and must provide useful information that can be transmitted to the client regarding the intervention
  • You will ensure the proper functioning and maintenance of work tools
  • Finally, you will collaborate on the continuous improvement of the workshop's functioning and constantly improve your level through training

Requirements

  • Holder of a watchmaking diploma or equivalent, you have a minimum of 3 years of experience in a Customer Service
  • You master quartz movements up to chronograph and are perfectly comfortable with adjustment, finishing and setting in motion
  • You have very good skills in dial setting and hand fitting
  • Passionate about your profession and with a good learning ability, you are recognized for your quality work as well as your analysis and problem-solving

What we offer

  • Coverage of health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 weeks of annual vacation and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year's thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The possibility, depending on the function, of working remotely
  • A company restaurant
  • An advantageous LPP
