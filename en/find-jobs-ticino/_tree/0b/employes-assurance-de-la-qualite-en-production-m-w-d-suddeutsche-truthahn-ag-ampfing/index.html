Full description

Süddeutsche Turkey AG is one of the leading German producers of high-quality turkey products. Sueddeutsche Turkeyahn AG is part of an international, rapidly expanding food group with around 12,000 employees. Tasks:

  • Implementation of quality controls on finished products (e.g. blank, weight, appearance, smell)
  • Control of cleaning and disinfection success
  • Hygiene checks during operation
  • Checking of operational hygiene through regular inspection
  • Training of colleagues on hygiene and quality
  • Monitoring of production processes with regard to compliance with hygiene regulations, recipes and process parameters.
  • Perform visual controls and measurements.
  • Identification and reporting of deviations.
  • Provision of working chemistry at washing plants
  • Support in causal research and the development of corrective measures.
  • Documentation of results
  • Creation and maintenance of quality control protocols, reports and documents Requirements:
  • Preferably completed vocational training or comparable professional experience in the food industry
  • Technical understanding
  • Readiness for shift work and different temperature ranges
  • Resilience, teamwork, high commitment
  • Independent, goal-oriented and reliable, accurate working methods and documentation
  • Enforcement capacity
