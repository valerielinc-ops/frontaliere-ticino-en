Travel Office Specialist

We are looking for an experienced figure in Travel Office, Executive Support, Office Management or similar roles to be included within our organization.

The resource, included in the Travel Office & General Services department, will provide operational and organizational support to management and various business functions, dealing with planning and coordination of travel, events, meetings and other organizational and management activities.

The role requires an autonomous, dynamic and reliable person, with excellent organizational, relational and analytical skills, able to simultaneously manage different activities and projects, ensuring precision, confidentiality and efficiency.

Main responsibility

  • Organization and coordination of business travel, travel, flights, hotels and transfers.
  • Planning and managing corporate events, meetings, conventions and meetings with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Operating and organizational support for managerial figures and various business functions.
  • Coordination of the logistic and organizational activities related to the business life.
  • Management of relations with suppliers, agencies and external partners.
  • Cost monitoring, budget management and reporting and analysis.
  • Support in the management of activities and transversal projects, ensuring compliance with timing and priorities.
  • Identification of organizational and operational solutions aimed at improving process efficiency.

Required Requirements

  • At least 5 years of experience in similar roles (Travel Office, Executive Assistant, Office Management, Event Coordination or Operations Support).
  • Excellent mastery of Microsoft Excel and creative AI tools, including Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT.
  • Great knowledge of English.
  • Explain organizational, analytical and planning skills.
  • Excellent communication and relational skills, with aptitude for managing internal and external stakeholders.
  • Ability to work under pressure, managing multiple tasks simultaneously, defining priority and respecting deadlines.
  • Operational automation, precision and confidentiality.
  • Flexibility, proactivity and excellent problem solving skills.
  • Travelling availability.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
