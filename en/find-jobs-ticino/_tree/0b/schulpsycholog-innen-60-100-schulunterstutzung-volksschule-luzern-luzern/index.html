Full description

We focus on proximity and openness: At the city administration we live the du-culture – already in the application process.

Your responsibilities

  • Psychological support at the interface of school and family systems
  • Counselling and support of children, adolescents, parents, teachers, classes and other school and educational stakeholders
  • Psychodiagnostics related to school and developmental issues (school career, potential analyses, behaviour, compensation of disadvantages, etc.)
  • Counselling and support of children and adolescents in learning, performance and behavioural problems
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with professionals and institutions

Your profile

  • Completed university or university studies (Master) with major in psychology
  • Knowledge and practical experience in child and youth/school psychology and/or teaching
  • Ability to work with complex systems and moderate processes
  • Familiarity with current school development and education issues
  • Resilience, communication and conflict capability
  • Willingness to engage in teamwork

We offer

  • Demanding and diverse tasks in the field of school and education
  • Independent work with a lot of design space in the allocated school buildings
  • Support from a motivated team
  • Current employment conditions according to cantonal specifications
