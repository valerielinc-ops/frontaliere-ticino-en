Full description

Introduction

For our Maintenance Equipment service, we are looking for a (M/F) Maintenance Technician, whose main mission will be to ensure the corrective and preventive maintenance of production and logistics equipment, while contributing to their optimization and process improvement.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure corrective maintenance of equipment by conducting a full analysis and preparing interventions
  • Accurately implement interventions in accordance with safety regulations and current standards
  • Manage orders and manage relationships with suppliers
  • Conduct preventive maintenance and propose equipment reliability solutions
  • Collaborate with the various internal services to optimize equipment and enhance the responsiveness and reliability of maintenance processes
  • Conduct reporting related to interventions and update work orders

Profile

  • ES technician in industrial or microtechnical systems or equivalent
  • Recognized skills in automation and digital controls (Fanuc, Siemens, etc.)
  • Experience in electrotechnical and mechanical engineering
  • Mastery of common computer tools, with good knowledge of SAP ERP and industrial computing
  • Methodical and rigorous approach with a spirit of analysis and synthesis
  • Excellent team spirit, listening and easy communication
  • English and German as an asset
  • Availability for shift work (2x8)
  • Availability for initial training in Geneva before continuing the mission in the canton of Fribourg

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
