Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated provider of services in old age and employs around 4,000 people. Outpatient and stationary offerings merge seamlessly. While the Senevita locations stand for independent assisted living and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading provider of private Spitex services throughout Switzerland.

For the Senevita Wydenpark in Studen BE, we are looking for an assistant in health & social care immediately or by agreement, Nursing assistant/in 50 - 100% Your tasks

  • You care for the residents individually, with heart, humor and mind in cooperation with the professional staff
  • You shape the everyday life of the inhabitants and orient yourself on their resources
  • Compliance with hygiene regulations and assumption of domestic activities
  • Independent management of care documentation

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as health and social assistant EBA or nursing assistant SRK
  • You have a weakness for older people and their life story
  • Teamwork, flexibility, resilience and responsibility
  • Good written and oral German skills

We offer you

  • Working hours with flexibility for work and family
  • Attractive further education offered by the Senevita Academy, which inspires ongoing development
  • Free intermediate meals (bread, fruit, mineral water, hot drinks) as well as discounted lunch - heartily prepared by our gastro team
  • Free SBB half-ax, discounts (fuelling, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
  • Interesting employee conditions such as free SBB half-ax and discounts for refueling, fitness, pharmacy, etc.
  • Good social benefits and at least. 5 weeks holiday
  • Very good accessibility (direct motorway connection, location directly at Studen station)
  • Our commitment: You as a person are important to us!

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed.
