Your role and responsibilities

In this role, you will have the opportunity to develop and implement people strategy through the execution of HR processes. Each day, you will provide HR support for a defined team or location, by partnering with respective managers. You will also showcase your HR skills by representing HR in the business and establishing close relationships with all business leaders and employees.

The work model for the role is: hybrid

This role is contributing to the local Motion Market and Factory Teams in Switzerland. Reporting to the lead division manager, additional important main stakeholders are other Local Division Managers, department and team leaders, as well as the HR community in Switzerland and beyond.

You will be mainly accountable for:

  • Ensuring an effective overall HR contribution and value-add to the business in partnership with the functions and Shared Services.

  • Supporting the business and the execution of HR processes involved across the end-to-end employee lifecycle by determining and delivering the most effective approach in the area of responsibility and executing the HR priorities while adding value to the business.

  • Partnering with respective managers to develop and implement people strategies.

  • Facilitating change management initiatives and supporting behavior change & continuous improvement across the respective business area / area of responsibility.

  • Support and contribution of Country HR Projects as well as Diversity & Inclusion Initiatives

• Qualifications for the role

  • Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, Psychology, or a related field; Master’s degree is a plus.

  • Minimum 5 years of experience in human resources, especially from an international company

  • Knowledge of the Labor Code and employment law

  • Solid business acumen with a customer-focused mindset which will enable you to align HR strategies with organizational goals.

  • Deep understanding of employee relations, coaching methods, investigations, and labor practices. Demonstrated ability to build relationships and collaborate with people at all levels of the organization.

  • Ability to handle several tasks and prioritize in a demanding environment.

  • You are at ease communicating in German and English, French and any other language in addition is a plus
