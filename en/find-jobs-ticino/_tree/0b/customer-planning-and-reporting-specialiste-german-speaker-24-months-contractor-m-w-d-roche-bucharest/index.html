The position Summary: As a Customer Planning and Reporting Specialist, you will support the Roche Diagnostics service sales team in various activities. This includes creating and monitoring service cases and reports, creating the service dashboard with the department’s key KPIs, and ensuring the interface between call center, customer and service field service. They monitor the stocks of the SpareParts (spare parts) and order them if necessary. For the service field service. The coordination, planning and implementation of inventories / inventory audits as well as the creation / cancellation and processing of workflows are also components. They create service offerings and maintenance plans, carry out data matching between CRM and billing systems and support initiatives to optimize the customer experience in the provision and evaluation of customer surveys.

Important: Very good knowledge of German in words and writing is absolutely necessary, as this team supports the service area of Roche Diagnostics Germany. The working time is between 07:50 and 18:00 (German time, or 08:50 to 19:00 Romanian time) and is organized in shifts. Shifts are split within the team, so you work some days from early morning to afternoon and most days from late morning to evening. Working hours also include some statutory Romanian public holidays, provided they are not statutory public holidays in Germany.

Their tasks Planning and reporting (about 75%)

  • Disposition / creation of service cases for proactive activities of the field team
  • Monitoring of spare parts orders
  • Initiation of the exchange process for third-party accessories (e.g. UPS systems, water stations, etc.)
  • Monitoring and adjustment of service spare parts stocks (vehicle stock and warehouses)
  • Support in reviewing, correcting and releasing Field Service Engineers (FSE) reports
  • Monitoring of spare parts and site management
  • Coordination, planning and implementation of inventories/stock audits
  • Training of new colleagues in the CRM system
  • Provision of requested documents to finance and sales departments
  • Creation of customer offers for spare parts and service activities
  • Maintaining relationships with third parties for case handling and installation projects and auditing
  • Provision and evaluation of event-based customer surveys
  • Preparation of recurring reports and proposals of measures to achieve the KPIs within the defined targets
  • Definition of the process for escalations from the surveys and follow-up of the initiated measures
