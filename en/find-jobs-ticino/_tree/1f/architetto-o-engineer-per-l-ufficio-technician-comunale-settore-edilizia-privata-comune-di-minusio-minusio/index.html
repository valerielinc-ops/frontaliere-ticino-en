COMPETITION TO RECRUIT AN ARCHITECT OR ENGINEER FOR THE MUNICIPAL TECHNICAL OFFICE (PRIVATE BUILDING SECTOR) The Municipality of Minusio opens the competition for the permanent hiring of an architect or engineer with an ETH/EPF or SUP diploma for the municipal technical office (part-time, between 50% and 80%) for the private building sector, under the conditions of Organic regulation of employees (ROD) approved on 11.05.2026 by the City Council and whose entry into force is scheduled for 01.01.2027.

  • Basic requirements > Diploma (Bachelor and/or Master) as an architect or engineer ETH/EPF or SUP or equivalent qualification > Swiss citizenship or a country signatory to a bilateral agreement, with "C" permit (domicile) > Healthy physical constitution > Clean conduct > Ability to carry out work suited to the function > Work experience in the construction or public technical/administrative field and on issues related to territorial planning > Ability to manage paperwork construction and complete knowledge of architecture and project evaluation > Knowledge of the theme of sustainability in the built environment and of the theme of the "Sponge City" > Ability to operate independently, sense of responsibility and strong sense of initiative > Strong organizational and coordination skills > Proactive and proactive person, with a predisposition for group work oriented towards solving problems > Availability for continuous updating > Availability for services outside normal working hours > Ease of contact with professionals and with users > Good knowledge of IT tools for technical office activities (Word, Excel, Autocad, etc.) > Good editorial skills (minutes, reports, technical reports, etc.) > Good knowledge of national languages, in particular the German language
  • Preferential requirements > SUPSI municipal technician diploma or willingness to obtain it within the deadlines set by the Municipality > Proven experience in the public sector > Proven work, organisational, managerial experience > Certificates / diplomas of post-training courses > Knowledge of the municipal territory > Municipality domicile or availability to transfer within terms to be defined > Type B driving license Hierarchical position The architect / engineer reports to the manager of the private construction sector.

Salary (gross annual, including the 13th month) The initial salary will be established on the basis of the new ROD and the new Municipal Ordinance on the classification of functions (under discussion with the Personnel Commission and the Unions, but which is expected to come into force on 01.01.2027) taking into account the requirements, suitability and professional experience: Grade Name of function Title Class RO II Technical collaborator I Bachelor 8 + any allowances for children The classes they refer to the salary scale of State employees (valid from 01.01.2026). Any changes to the law regarding the function, salary and organization, respectively any evaluation within the scope of the new municipal ordinance on the classification of functions, remain explicitly reserved. Considering that the new employee organic regulations and the new municipal ordinance on the classification of functions have not yet formally entered into force, it is deemed appropriate to also publish the salary scales of the employee organic regulations currently in force, and better: > Senior official 2 Class 15-16 (minimum CHF 79'154.72 / maximum CHF 106'108.86) Class 16-17 (minimum CHF 81'291.90 / maximum CHF 108'973.80) + any allowances for children Information Further information can be obtained from the secretariat of the municipal technical office (ediliziaprivata@minusio.ch / 091 735 81 41).

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
