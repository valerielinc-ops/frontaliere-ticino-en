The Opportunity Join our Local Service Center Switzerland as an intern in the Project Management team. In this role you will support project managers in Geneva while collaborating closely with colleagues in Zurich. This internship offers a unique opportunity to contribute to the development of a tool pool, ensuring that the necessary equipment and tools required for on-site execution are properly defined, available and maintained. You will not only define new tools, but also contribute to improving how we prepare and manage site-related resources and processes. A key part of the role includes visiting project sites to validate and understand how tools and processes are applied in practice, allowing you to directly see the impact of your work and contribute to continous improvement.
