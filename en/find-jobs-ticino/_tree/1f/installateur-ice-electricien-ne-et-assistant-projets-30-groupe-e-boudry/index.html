Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our customers to carry out their projects by providing comprehensive, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether in the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. As an exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2250 employees, including 200 apprentices, and the reconciliation between private and professional life.

Group E Tech , thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of electrical installations, tables and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as maintenance of installations.

Mission

You are actively contributing to the construction of general electrical installations, community works, troubleshooting and small facilities with our customers. In addition to this main activity in the field, you take on a project assistant role (30%) and support the Small Facilities project manager in the administrative, commercial and financial follow-up of the business of Boudry branch B2C. This complementary function offers you the opportunity to develop your skills in coordination and project management while maintaining close proximity to operational activities.

Your main responsibilities

  • To carry out general electrical installations in the residential, tertiary and proximity sectors, you master the reading of plans and diagrams of electrical installations.
  • Renovation, transformation and small electrical installations according to customer needs.
  • Ensure troubleshooting and maintenance interventions in accordance with quality, safety and timeliness requirements.
  • Organize, coordinate and participate in project sessions, draft and disseminate minutes of internal and external meetings.
  • Ensure rigorous monitoring of project actions, deadlines and decisions by ensuring smooth communication between clients, project managers, site managers and implementation teams.
  • Prepare and maintain all the documentation necessary for the successful completion of small electrical installations projects (metalwork, offers, planning, technical documents, correspondence, financial monitoring, etc.).
  • To ensure the commercial, administrative and financial follow-up of cases, from the preparation of tenders to the closing of cases, including the management of capital gains, amendments and items necessary for invoicing.

Profile

  • Installation-electrician CFC with several years of experience in electrical installations.
  • Interest in business management and project coordination.
  • Relationship and customer orientation.
  • Organizational sense, administrative rigour and ability to manage multiple files in parallel.
  • Mastery of common computer tools.
  • Proactive, autonomous and solutions oriented personality.

What we offer

  • An evolving environment conducive to work-life balance.
  • The social benefits of a modern enterprise, fair treatment, opportunities for professional development.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: "Together, we are moving towards success"
