Full description

As co-ordinator or co-ordinator of premises management, you ensure the co-ordination of requests related to the allocation of premises, the fit-up of workspaces and the relocation of activities and collaborators within the HUGs. A preferred interlocutor or interlocutor for staff, you analyse the needs expressed and ensure their consistency with the institutional real estate strategy as well as with the HUG master plan. You evaluate applications, propose suitable solutions and look for alternatives for efficient use of the surfaces and resources made available. You independently manage the low to medium complexity development and relocation mandates, from planning to implementation. As such, you coordinate the various internal and external stakeholders, monitor service providers, and ensure that deadlines, costs and regulatory requirements are met.

  • You are actively involved in supporting change with teams involved in activity movements or space reorganizations.
  • You also manage and update proximity signage in collaboration with the Communications Directorate, while ensuring compliance with the corporate chart.
  • Finally, you contribute to the maintenance and reliability of graphical and alphanumeric inventories of premises and to the quality of property management data.
