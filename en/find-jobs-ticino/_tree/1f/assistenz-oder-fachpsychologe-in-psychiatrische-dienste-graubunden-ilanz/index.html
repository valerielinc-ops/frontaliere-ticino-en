Full description

Your surroundings

They work in an interdisciplinary team at the Surselva Psychiatry Center in Ilanz and actively contribute to the region’s outpatient psychiatric care. You can expect a collegial working environment with good cooperation and close professional exchange.

Their tasks

  • You work largely independently in our psychiatry center Surselva in Ilanz
  • They are able to use the psychodiagnostic test methods commonly used in child and adolescent psychiatry.
  • In addition to psychotherapeutic investigations and treatments, they also carry out crisis interventions.

Our requirement profile

  • You have completed a degree in psychology and already have professional experience in the field of diagnosis and psychotherapy in children and adolescents
  • You have the psychotherapy training with eidg. Recognition completed or in advanced education
  • They work regularly with various people involved in the family e.g. teachers, social workers, youth advocacy, KESB etc.
  • They have in-depth competences in the field of child therapy as well as a high level of social and action competence
  • They are creative, flexible and communicative
  • Romanesque language is beneficial
