Full description

About us Anything but everyday. REHAB Basel, Clinic for Neurorehabilitation and Paraplegiology, treats and rehabilitates patients with paraplegia, brain injury and related clinical pictures after an accident or illness. Our ultimate goal is, together with the people entrusted to us, to achieve the greatest possible independence and an optimal quality of life. We treat successfully in the area of initial and subsequent rehabilitation. The clinic has 110 beds and an outpatient operation. Around 660 employees implement the visions and values of REHAB Basel in their everyday work. They work in a unique building in the attractive triangle of Switzerland, Germany and France. From the first day of the inpatient stay to the outpatient setting, we accompany our patients with our physiotherapeutic measures. Physiotherapy is part of the interprofessional team and, after a thorough examination of the findings, pursues the defined goals methodically according to house and other concepts. Our physiotherapists are specialized in various techniques and concepts. To complement our specialist team, we are looking for a specialist physiotherapy 60%-80% by 1 January 2027 or by agreement.

Job title Expert in Physiotherapy 60%-80% m/f/d

Your responsibilities

Professional team development

  • Development, implementation and evaluation of evidence-based best practice concepts for physiotherapeutic diagnosis and treatment
  • Support for the professional and clinical development of employees through supervision, mentoring and individual advice
  • Promotion of professional exchange and knowledge transfer in the team through the support of specialist groups as well as the planning and implementation of internal training

Clinical activity

  • Physiotherapeutic diagnosis and treatment of patients with complex neurological pathologies
  • Implementation of evidence-informed and patient-centered evaluation and treatment approaches in clinical practice

Data management and research

  • Organisation, preparation and evaluation of clinical data and research data to evaluate the effectiveness of treatment approaches and clinical processes
  • Collaboration in clinical research and development projects

Your profile

  • Completed MSc
  • Graduated physiotherapist in HF / FH
  • Several years of professional experience in neurorehabilitation and paraplegiology
  • Good methodological and didactic skills
  • Basic knowledge of data management, database structures and data organization
  • Experience with REDCap, especially in the development and development of projects, is an advantage
  • Very good knowledge of German and knowledge of English for the processing of specialist literature

You will find with us

  • An attractive, demanding and diverse field of activity
  • Interprofessional cooperation, which makes it possible to work together with the patients and their relatives to develop, pursue and benefit from the different expertise
  • An appreciative working atmosphere as well as a targeted familiarization in a committed and open-minded team

In addition, we offer a wide range of team-promoting and employee-friendly offers such as staff outings, free employee sports and much more. Our team is looking forward to you.

Position to be filled as of 01.01.2027 or by agreement
