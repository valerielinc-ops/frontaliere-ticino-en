01 Future employer

For an established company in the field of electrical engineering and automation, we are looking for a committed person who enjoys holistic control of switchgear projects and leads a team both professionally and personally. You will be faced with a versatile task with high personal responsibility, direct customer contact and exciting technical challenges.

02 Your task

  • You take over the independent management of several switchgear projects from technical and commercial preparation to successful handover.
  • You coordinate material procurement, resources and personnel deployment and ensure a smooth project run.
  • You lead the assigned employees and support your team with technical and organizational questions.
  • You can control costs, deadlines and quality at any time and introduce suitable measures early in case of deviations.
  • You have a professional contact with customers and actively develop existing business relationships.
  • By your technical understanding you recognize additional customer needs and contribute to the acquisition of follow-up and additional orders.
  • You work closely with internal bodies and other project participants and ensure efficient cooperation.

04 Your profile

  • A completed vocational training as an automatic operator: in EDC, switchgear fitter:in or in a comparable electrotechnical profession.
  • Training in switchgear construction, project management or electrical engineering is an advantage.
  • Professional experience in the planning, projecting or implementation of switchgear and controls.
  • First or well-founded experience in the management of employees and the joy of taking responsibility for a team.
  • Good knowledge of CAD applications and a pronounced A-resinity for digital tools.
  • An independent, solution-oriented and structured way of working.
  • The importance of organisation and good technical and commercial understanding.
  • A communicative, initiative and team-oriented personality who likes to move things forward.
