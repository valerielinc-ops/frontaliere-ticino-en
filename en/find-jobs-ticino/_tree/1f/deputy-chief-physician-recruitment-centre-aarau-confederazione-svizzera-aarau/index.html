Put to the Point

Here recruits are tested heart and soul – in the truest sense of the word! Medical recruitment in the context of alimentation is an extremely important process within the Swiss army. Take on this responsible task and enjoy a unique and varied working environment with development opportunities and a lot of quality of life thanks to regular working hours.

  • Entry date: 01.01.2027
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20527
  • Place of work: Barracks, 5001 Aarau

You can make this contribution

  • Represent the Chief Physician of the Aarau Recruitment Centre professionally and in terms of personnel
  • Carry out medical examinations independently in the German language for the persons responsible
  • Assessment of suitability for military service and civil protection
  • Lead the medical examination committee to assess fitness
  • Communicate and represent findings from the specialist areas externally and maintain a close cooperation with the heads of primary medical care and medical recruitment

This makes them unique.

  • Eidg. Diploma as a doctor or equivalent with MEBEKO recognition, specialist title Doctor / Doctor with clinical experience
  • Interest in military and military medical topics
  • Engaged and prudent leader with pleasure in the exchange in a motivated, interdisciplinary team
  • Enjoying contact with members of the army, as well as high social and communication skills
  • Good active knowledge of at least one second official language and passive knowledge of a third official language

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3'500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
