02.11.2026 Lausanne

Be part of our team!

To strengthen our team, we are looking for a personality as a specialist at the (m/f/d) Customer Service Centre, able to learn quickly, flexibly, service-oriented and with an excellent sense of communication. We offer a modern and flexible working environment with the possibility to do telework as well as interesting development options. Please send the complete file (CV, work certificates, diplomas).

What are you waiting for?

  • Respond to customer calls on various banking, digital and administrative topics
  • Identify the needs of clients, offer them suitable solutions (payments, savings, foresight, simple investments)
  • Participate in targeted outbound campaigns as part of commercial actions or customer information
  • Ensure active listening and transmit specific requests to relevant services
  • Follow regular coaching to maintain the high quality standards of the Service Centre

What are you bringing?

  • SWC Customer Relationship Agent, SWC Bank Trade Employee
  • 3 years experience in customer contact centre or banking
  • Perfect mastery of oral and written French, with excellent knowledge of a second national language (level B2)
  • Experience in the digital environment and strong interest in digital tools
  • Open mind, sense of service and enthusiasm to support our clients with professionalism
  • Flexibility in an expanded schedule (7.30 h – 21.00 h) according to a system of shift turns

Do you like human contact, have an excellent sense of customer service and want to evolve in a digital and stimulating environment? It's time to join our Customer Service Centre! Sabrine Du Pasquier Group Manager Service Centre Why Raiffeisen?

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
