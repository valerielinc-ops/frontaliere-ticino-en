Senior physician 60-100 %

Clinic of Angiology

Place of work St. Gallen

Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Independent care of angiological patients at the St.Gallen site; as desired and possible takeover of office hours at the Linth or Grabs locations
  • Varicose treatment by means of endovenous thermal ablation and sclerotherapy as well as modern wound care with skin replacement methods
  • Treatment of patients with chronic wounds as part of the interdisciplinary wound consultation session
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with vascular surgery and interventional radiology within the vascular center
  • Instruction, training and supervision of assistant doctors as well as participation in interdisciplinary boards and further education

What you bring for this job

  • Completed specialist title Angiology
  • Innovative, communicative and team capable personality with a high level of social competence
  • Enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation as well as a reliable working method with a sense of responsibility
  • Motivation for professional training and further education
  • Very good oral and written knowledge of German (level C1/C2 of advantage)

Our offer to you

  • Exciting and varied activity at a center hospital with all facets of modern vascular medicine
  • Team-oriented collaboration in a highly specialized and interdisciplinary environment
  • Close guidance and support by experienced and motivated cadre doctors
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
  • Various offers for employees in the field of health promotion, job and family, mobility, various benefits, etc.
