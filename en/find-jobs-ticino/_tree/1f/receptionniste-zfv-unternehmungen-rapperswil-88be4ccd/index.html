Full description

Welcome to the rose city on the picturesque shores of Lake Zurich. The newly renovated Hotel Speer is located in the heart of Rapperswil. The modern boutique hotel offers a centrally located workplace that provides plenty of room for personal development and your own ideas. Leisure and business travelers from all over the world stay here in the 56 rooms and discover the most beautiful places in the surrounding area from here. Our team ensures unique experiences and unforgettable stays. Are you our new team member who will support us as a receptionist as of January 1, 2027 or by agreement? You work 5 days per week from Monday to Sunday in early, mid, or late shifts and have 2 days off. Working hours are between 06:00 and 23:30. Your task menu:

  • Ensuring the smooth operation of reception
  • Welcoming, looking after, and bidding farewell to hotel guests
  • Fulfilling the role of host
  • Ensuring a high level of guest satisfaction
  • Handling and fulfilling guest requests
  • Checking hotel guests in and out
  • Operating the telephone and information center
  • Accepting and processing incoming reservations
  • Handling professional complaint management
  • Preparing and processing daily closing reports
  • Active room sales as well as maintaining the booking portals with optimal room rates
  • Handling various administrative tasks Your recipe for success:
  • Commercial basic training with an EFZ or completed hotel management school
  • Experience in the hotel industry and guest relations
  • Very good spoken and written German and English skills
  • Front-office and guest-oriented
  • Confident and resilient
  • Comfortable with systems and possessing good IT skills in MS-Office (Word, Excel etc.)
  • Goal-oriented and eager to learn What we offer you: We create teams that support one another and in which you are encouraged and developed. We make an impact together and focus on everything that contributes to well-being. Among other things, we place great value on pay transparency and equal pay. For this position, with a 100% workload and depending on your experience and qualifications, we offer an annual salary between CHF 56'000 and CHF 63'000 (incl. 13. Monthly salary). If you would like to share your passion for gastronomy with us, we look forward to receiving your complete online application.
