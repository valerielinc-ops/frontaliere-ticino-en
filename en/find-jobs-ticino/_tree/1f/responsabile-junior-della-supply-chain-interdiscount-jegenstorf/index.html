Full description

  • Job opening: 01.07.2026
  • Specialization: Category Management / Procurement
  • Place of work: Interdiscount, Bernstrasse 92, 3303 Jegenstorf

At Interdiscount, we offer our customers the best shopping experience in Switzerland. Engage in a dynamic and entrepreneurial working environment. Take responsibility, be proactive with us and develop further.

Tasks

  • You will analyze and control topics relating to the flow of goods, replenishment and demand planning along the product range, branches and promotions.
  • You are responsible for defined goods groups end-to-end in the area of goods flow management
  • You identify optimization potentials in forecasts, scheduling parameters, inventories, master data and merchandise availability.
  • You’ll create and develop KPI dashboards, reports, and analytics to monitor sales, inventory, service levels, and promotions.
  • You recognize risks and potential for improvement in the flow of goods and derive sound recommendations for action for management and specialist departments.
  • You work closely with category management, sales and data analytics and support the prioritization and implementation of optimization measures with data-based decision-making principles.

Requirements

  • You have a degree, a higher education or a relevant further education in business administration, supply chain management, logistics, business informatics, industrial engineering or a comparable field.
  • You bring strong analytical skills and enjoy working with numbers and data.
  • You have very good knowledge of Excel and can evaluate data in a structured manner.
  • You work independently, reliably and solution-oriented.
  • You can explain contexts in an understandable way and enjoy working with different specialist areas.
  • You communicate stylishly in German in word and writing.
