Full description

Curiosity. Training. Practice

The Kudelski Group is a global leader in digital security. Its technologies are used in a wide range of services and applications requiring access control and rights management. The group also offers cybersecurity solutions and services focused on helping businesses, their data and systems. Finally, it proposes solutions concerning the Internet of Things.

The apprenticeship of Emploi(e) de Commerce at Nagravision Sàrl - Kudelski Group, an entity based in Cheseaux-sur-Lausanne, allows you to discover and train in several fields throughout the internships, such as the assistance of a full department, Cybersecurity Marketing and Human Resources.

For this path, the mastery of English , oral and written, is a prerequisite (ideally level B2 - First Certificate).

We offer the possibility to register as a standard or mature CFC.
