International Markets Coordinator (M/F) back to list In order to strengthen our teams, we are looking for an international market coordinator (M/F). Job description

Your mission

  • Ensure the commercial coordination of several international markets and ensure the proper performance of sales activities
  • Support Brand Managers in day-to-day market monitoring and contribute to the implementation of the business strategy
  • Plan, coordinate and monitor market performance by ensuring reliable reporting to the Trade Directorate
  • Ensuring the proper flow of information between headquarters, subsidiaries, distributors and trading partners
  • Manage administrative transactions related to sales: orders, contracts, tariff terms and customer data within ERP and CRM systems
  • Coordinate interactions with different internal departments to ensure efficient processing of requests and quality service
  • Participate in market development and transformation projects, including openings, reorganizations or deployments of tools
  • Ensuring the reliability of business data, maintaining the monitoring tools and producing the analyses necessary to manage the activity
  • Develop and maintain trusting relationships with international partners, retailers and interlocutors
  • Ensuring a high level of service and managing sensitive situations with professionalism, responsiveness and diplomacy

Profile

Profile desired

  • Trade, economic or equivalent training
  • Experience confirmed in trade coordination, sales administration (SDA) or support to international trade teams
  • Excellent knowledge of the Swiss watch market, its distribution network and international trade issues
  • Current knowledge of English and French, both oral and written
  • Good control of IT tools and management systems such as ERP, CRM and Excel
  • Assistance in the analysis of commercial data and monitoring of performance indicators
  • Excellent organizational capabilities, with ability to manage multiple files simultaneously and meet priorities
  • Service sense developed and strong customer orientation
  • Excellent relational skills and ability to collaborate with diverse interlocutors in an international environment
  • Self-employed, proactive and able to take initiatives
  • Rigor, discretion and professionalism in information management and business relations
