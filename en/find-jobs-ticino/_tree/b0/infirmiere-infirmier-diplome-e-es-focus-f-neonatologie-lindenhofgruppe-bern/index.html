Full description

What you do

You will care for and care for our newborns and premature babies independently in all three shifts, with a high degree of personal responsibility and in close cooperation with our paediatricians and neonatologists.

  • You will independently care for and care for up to four premature or sick newborns and actively involve their parents in holistic, family-centered care.
  • You will carry out the nursing process according to the latest findings, supported by the clinic information system KISIM.
  • You take joint responsibility for achieving the divisional and departmental goals and are committed to the ongoing optimization of nursing processes.
  • You will also support the delivery room team in emergencies.

What sets you apart

  • You are a qualified nurse (KWS / PFF HF/FH with focus F) and have at least six months of experience in neonatology. Thanks to your professional and social skills, you will care for premature and sick newborns competently and sensitively.
  • You are a flexible, committed and motivated personality with a quick grasp of things.
  • Good interdisciplinary cooperation is important to you.
  • The well-being of premature and newborn babies as well as their families is the focus of your work. In doing so, you treat people with respect and appreciation
  • You work reliably, team-oriented and keep track of things even in stressful and changing patient situations.
  • You have a good knowledge of German, other language skills are an advantage
