Full description

At the Dagmersellen location, everything revolves around enjoyment, quality and real teamwork. With a lot of know-how and passion, various Emmi products are created here, which find their way to our consumers every day. You can expect a modern production company, in which everyone makes an important contribution. Become part of our team in Dagmersellen and shape the future of Emmi with us.

You can do that.

  • Ensuring a smooth process at the bottling plants – you ensure that everything runs smoothly.
  • Cleaning and preparation of the production environment – Cleanliness is your standard.
  • Self-conducting small repairs for technical problems – you keep the plant running.
  • Documentation and communication – you report errors, fill out logs and keep your team informed.
  • Quality control – you regularly check the production process and react to deviations.

You bring that with you

  • You already have some experience in a food apprenticeship at production facilities.
  • Seasonal 2- to 3-shift work is no problem for you (2.00 - approx.) 11.30 a.m. 4.00 a.m. about 1.30 p.m. and 11.00 a.m. - end.
  • You can communicate well orally and in writing in German (B1/B2).
  • Motivation, openness, flexibility, responsibility and reliability describe your person.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement savings above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Focus on your health: We offer occupational health management, preventive measures and free social advice from our partner Proitera for professional and private concerns.
  • Cheap meals in the staff restaurant: For breakfast, lunch and break meals, a staff restaurant with discounted employee prices is available.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.
