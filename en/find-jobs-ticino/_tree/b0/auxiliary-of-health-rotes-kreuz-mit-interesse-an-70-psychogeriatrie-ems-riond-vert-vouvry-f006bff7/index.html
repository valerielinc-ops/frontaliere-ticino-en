Auxiliary of health Red Cross with interest for 70% psychogeriatry

  • you collaborate in the development and implementation of projects accompanying residents and their relatives

  • you are oriented and have a common sense of human relations
  • you have a good command of French (spoken and written)

We offer

  • social and wage conditions according to the CLC SLD
