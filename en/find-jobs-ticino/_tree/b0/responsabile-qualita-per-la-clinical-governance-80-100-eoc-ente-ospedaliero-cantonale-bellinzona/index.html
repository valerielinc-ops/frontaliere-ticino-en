Full description

Responsible for Clinical Governance - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite Ticino hospital is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of more than 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team of the Medical Area of the Directorate-General we look for one: Quality Manager for Clinical Governance (80% - 100%) Purpose of the function: The position, hierarchically subordinate to the Head of Medical Area and functionally to the Head of Quality and Risk Service, aims to support the Doctors responsible for the coordination of Clinical Governance and Primaries in the exercise of the same, contributing to the improvement of clinical quality, appropriateness, security and outcomes of care. The function provides specialist methodological skills, tools and analysis, promotes the connection between the Clinics and accompanies the implementation of the cross clinical priorities defined by the Medical Area, working in close collaboration with the Quality and Risk Service EOC and the Local Quality Services. Main tasks and responsibilities: Support physicians of the Medical Area and Primaries in the definition and monitoring of clinical quality priorities, appropriateness, safety and treatment outcomes. Contribute to the analysis and interpretation of indicators and clinical results, favouring translation into improvement actions. Develop specialized methodologies and tools to support Clinical Governance, especially for clinical-assistance paths and clinical audits. Accompany Primaries in the definition and harmonisation of clinical-assistance paths, guidelines and transversal clinical protocols. Support the programming, implementation and evaluation of clinical audits as well as the implementation of M&M in Clinics. Collaboration with the use of clinical records data and the results of quality measurements for the improvement of clinical practices. Ensure periodic reporting to the Chief Medical Area and the Primaries on the areas of Clinical Governance, supporting reporting flows to the Quality and Risk Service. Contribute to the analysis of transversal clinical criticalities and to the definition and monitoring of the consequent improvement actions. Supporting certification, accreditation and development of clinical quality, encouraging the spread of good lawns

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
