Full description

The Gilly Hospital, a pioneering treatment and rehabilitation centre (CTR) in the canton of Vaud since 1989, has been serving the region since 1922. With 42 rehabilitation beds, this ideally located facility offers a breathtaking view of the Alps and Lake Geneva. In addition, our five-place temporary reception centre (CAT) offers activities for seniors four days a week.

We are looking for a

Nurse (M/F) in 80-100% CTR

Start as soon as possible or agreed.

WE OFFER YOU

  • Free parking spaces with your vehicle or care of the trip between Rolle station and the Gilly site by the EHC.
  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of buying back an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 free public holidays.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time work, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • A mobility plan that includes a contribution to your bike ride, sneaker ride or public transport subscription, with an environmental focus on an institutional sustainability plan.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your years of previous professional experience, as well as bonuses linked to seniority within the EHC.
  • Free pilates classes, financial participation in sporting events such as the 20km of Lausanne or Bike to Work, a catalogue of benefits with discounts in the areas of sport, well-being, leisure, mobility, insurance and finance.
  • Hot drinks offered in the morning and meals at CHF 8.50 in our cafeterias.
  • A company refreshment bar "Pâquis Plage" to share moments of conviviality on sunny days.
  • Opportunities for internal transfers, according to your development desires.
  • Many internal or certifying training courses available.
  • A program dedicated to executives to support them in acquiring the skills necessary to manage their teams.

YOU OWN

  • A Bachelor's degree in nursing or a level 2 nursing diploma, recognized CRS.
  • A first experience acquired in a medical, geriatric or rehabilitation department is an asset.
  • Good organizational skills (anticipation, planning, coordination), flexibility and proactivity.
  • An ability to work continuous shifts in 12h00 alternating days and nights.
  • The ability to take initiative and responsibility.
  • A sense of listening and interpersonal skills.

YOUR GOALS

  • To ensure the care of the patients entrusted to him in accordance with institutional values.
  • Systematically identify the patient's needs and resources.
  • Develop care objectives with the patient, his or her loved ones, and the multidisciplinary team, based on the data collected and taking into account an overall therapeutic plan.
  • Work in an interprofessional manner with the objective of quality care and respect for a global approach to the patient.
  • Collaborate with all the actors concerned to the continuous improvement of practices and the good organization of the service.

WE ARE

  • An institution comprising acute care and rehabilitation establishments, a private clinic, nursing homes, medical centers and permanences spread throughout the La Côte region as well as the 1st school of clinical hypnosis dedicated to the healthcare environment in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A care network allowing many opportunities for internal transfer according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2000 employees taking care of patients and residents in the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment that promotes the realization of projects.
  • A training company committed to the training of the next generation, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different professions, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.

TOGETHER, SIDE BY SIDE, BE THE NEXT TALENT IN OUR CARE NETWORK.
