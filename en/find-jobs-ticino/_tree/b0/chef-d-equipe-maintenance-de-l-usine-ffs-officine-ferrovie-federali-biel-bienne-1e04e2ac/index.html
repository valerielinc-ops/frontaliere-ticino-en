Team leader: in maintenance systems 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team leader: in plant maintenance Biel/Bienne, November 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. With your team, you make an important contribution to ensuring that public transport functions reliably. Thanks to your commitment, the systems in the maintenance facility and the service facility in Biel remain safe, available and ready for use. You work closely with the producing units, the factory management and various specialist departments and help ensure that the technical systems are operated economically and reliably. An exciting and varied task with a collegial working environment awaits you. You can find benefits such as free GA and discounted travel abroad

  • also for the family
  • on our website under Benefits. You can move that.
  • As a team leader, you lead and develop a team of seven to nine employees according to the SBB management principles and derive appropriate measures from key figures, which you implement in a targeted manner.
  • You plan, coordinate and are responsible for all maintenance activities in the factory and in the service facility in Biel, from preventative and curative operations to special orders as part of the operational processes.
  • You take on technical and safety-related responsibility for the systems, ensure compliance with the service level agreements and ensure sustainable solutions with effective fault management.
  • You further develop the maintenance strategy, form the interface between production and maintenance and contribute your specialist knowledge to shop floor meetings, management and specialist meetings as well as exchanges with site management. You will also support specialists with technical questions.
  • Together with internal and external specialists, you will optimize the maintenance of plant, building and railway infrastructure objects.
  • In close communication with regional management, you support audits, take on special tasks and participate in strategic and operational decisions. In this way, you contribute to the continuous development of the organization. This is what you bring with you.
  • As a team player, you actively approach people, lead and motivate your team and, thanks to your networked way of thinking, keep an eye on the big picture.
  • You will certainly be able to achieve addressee-appropriate and solution-oriented communication with different stakeholder groups.
  • You have several years of professional experience in industrial maintenance, including management experience in a comparable function.
  • At least four years of technical vocational training with specialization in the electrical sector (NIV Art. 15 or Art. 13 is an advantage), in-depth knowledge of mechatronics as well as production and process technology and successfully completed higher vocational training (e.g. federal diploma in a technical field, maintenance manager, facility manager or a comparable further training) characterize your profile.
  • You have experience in fault analysis and technical evaluation of systems and work confidently with Microsoft 365. Knowledge of SAP R/3 or SAP S/4HANA rounds off your profile.
  • Very good knowledge of German at level C1 and knowledge of French at level B1, both spoken and written, round off your profile.
