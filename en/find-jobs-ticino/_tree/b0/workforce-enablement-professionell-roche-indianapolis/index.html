The Position Workforce Enablement Professional

The Opportunity Are you passionate about bridging the gap between cutting-edge technology and day-to-day business productivity?

As a Workforce Enablement Professional, you will contribute to the Digital Employee Experience. You won't just be fixing technical issues—you’ll act as a strategic digital consultant, partnering directly with business teams to automate processes, drive tool adoption, and ensure our workforce has the seamless IT experience they need to thrive.

If you love a mix of strategic IT consulting, hands-on technical problem-solving, and driving digital transformation from the ground up, this is the opportunity for you.

Key Responsibilities Digital Workplace Ownership & Strategic Consulting

  • You will act as a digital enabler for our business colleagues and functions and be the owner of the digital employee experience for the respective site.
  • Present complex technical information to a non-technical audience in a clear and easy to understand manner. Take ownership of incidents and act decisively to ensure our customers maintain IT productivity.
  • Provide technical consultancy for specific groups and work with them on how to leverage the existing tool for their benefit.
  • Proactively approach business functions to understand their processes and suggest methods to increase productivity.

Hands-on Technical Support & Operations

  • Day-to-Day Operations: Manage day-to-day IT activities at the site, working independently within company guidelines to maintain seamless operations.
  • 2nd Level Support: Provide 2nd-level technical resolution (in-person or remote) for field-based and specialty spaces (GxP, Labs, R&D, etc.).
  • Incident Ownership: Take ownership of incidents, act decisively to restore user productivity, and manage troubleshooting across network and system infrastructure.
  • Vendor & Service Management: Work closely in conjunction with internal IT groups, Professional Services, and outsourcing partners to drive efficient solutions.

Mentorship, Training & Communication

  • Translating Tech to Business: Present and communicate complex, highly technical information to non-technical stakeholders in a clear, accessible manner.
  • Training & Onboarding: Design and conduct training programs to educate users on both basic and specialized applications of Digital Technology.
  • Team Guidance: Act as a go-to resource and functional mentor for colleagues with less experience.

Who You Are

  • Bachelor’s degree in IT, Engineering, Science or applicable discipline.
  • A minimum of 5 years of direct experience with IT/desktop support.
  • Excellent working knowledge of the ITIL v3 processes in IT service delivery.
  • Strong consultative mindset, proactive communicator, self-starter capable of working under minimal guidance, and a passion for elevating the employee digital experience.
