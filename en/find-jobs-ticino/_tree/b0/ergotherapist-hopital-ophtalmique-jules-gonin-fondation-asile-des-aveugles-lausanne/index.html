Full description

Founded in Lausanne in 1843, the Asylum for the Blind Foundation includes the Jules-Gonin Ophthalmic Hospital, university ophthalmology department, a research center, the Educational Center for visually impaired students (CPHV), the social service, rehabilitation and low vision and the Clair-Soleil medico-social establishment. More than 650 employees work every day in the visual health service to offer diagnostic, treatment and support services to people with vision problems.

In order to complement our social and low vision rehabilitation service, we are looking for a

Low Vision Occupational Therapist

Your mission

  • To ensure the development, maintenance and compensation of our clients' visual abilities in order to promote their autonomy and social integration.
  • Assess visual abilities and level of independence
  • Advise, adapt, design, in a multidisciplinary framework, optical means, auxiliary means and adaptations of the environment (lighting, developments)
  • Carry out assessments at home, in a professional, school or public setting and propose adapted accommodations
  • To ensure the performance and quality of administrative work, statistical compilation, etc.

Your strengths

  • Bachelor's degree in occupational therapy
  • Specialist training in rehabilitation in the field of visual impairment
  • Team player and ability to work in an interdisciplinary network
  • Comfortable with computer tools
  • Creativity in the therapeutic approach
  • Sense of relationship, patience and interpersonal skills with the client
  • Driver's licence: frequent trips away from home

We offer you

An enriching and varied activity in a dynamic environment, salary conditions in line with your skills.
