  • Move Switzerland with us. Lateral entry train driver: in construction train with manual experience Winterthur, from now on or by arrangement, 100% Apply now ## You can look forward to this. A varied, paid 9-month full-time training program awaits you. You will complete your theoretical training in Olten and gain your first practical experience in a regionally assigned marshalling yard. You see irregular working hours and shift work as an advantage in order to enjoy your free time while others are working. We pay additional wages for night and weekend work, for example with an additional hourly wage of 16 francs for Sunday work. For night shifts, you receive a time allowance of 10 to 30%, which gives you more free time. You can find benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad

  • As a train driver for construction trains, you make a big contribution to ensuring the renewal of the routes (tracks, gravel, etc.).
  • You carry out and carry out various and complex shunting movements of trains up to a maximum of 100 km/h independently, form and dismantle them and prepare them for the journey.
  • In order to ensure regional logistics, you draft, apply for and order driving orders on time and are responsible for supplying construction sites with the necessary materials.
  • You have a high level of responsibility for the operational safety of the construction trains. Checking workplace safety and creating reports round off the activities.
  • In addition, you will be deployed as a train driver for construction trains on the construction site and your technical skills will be required. This is what you bring with you.
  • For this demanding work, you are conscientious, reliable, a team player and willing to work in 24-hour shifts.
  • You are physically and mentally resilient and can act consciously and correctly with your independent way of working.
  • You have at least two years of recognized vocational training including a certificate of skills, ideally in the construction industry and with experience in track construction or have completed your high school diploma.
  • Fluent written and spoken German (level C1) is a prerequisite.
  • You have a category B driving license.
  • You have a perfect sense of color (no red/green weakness) and have no entries in the criminal or debt enforcement register. ## Your salary and your benefits.
