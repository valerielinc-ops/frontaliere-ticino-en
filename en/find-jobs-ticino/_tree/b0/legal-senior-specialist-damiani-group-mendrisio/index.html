Legal Senior Specialist

Head of the Legal Office

The Legal Senior Specialist will become part of the Legal function with a transversal role, in support of business and corporate functions in managing the different legal requirements of the company. Involved in a wide variety of issues, it will contribute to the management of national and international contractualism, the assessment of legal risks and the support of business and corporate functions.

The role offers a significant exposure to international interlocutors and design and requires a pragmatic, proactive and business-oriented approach, with the ability to translate business needs into effective legal solutions.

Main responsibilities

  • Business contract management (including Distribution Agreement, Agency Agreement, Franchising Agreement, License Agreement, JV Agreement), Real Estate and Supply Chain, from drafting and reviewing agreements to support business and corporate functions.
  • Provide legal support to different business functions, in relation to operational and strategic needs.
  • Support trading and management of relationships with customers, partners, suppliers and other stakeholders, including internationally.
  • Identifying and evaluating the main legal risks, proposing pragmatic and consistent solutions with business needs.
  • Collaboration with external, Italian and international legal advisers, where necessary.
  • Contribute to the development and updating of templates, guidelines and legal procedures.

Requirements

  • Degree in Law.
  • Skilling to the forensic profession and previous experience of professional practice will constitute a preferential requirement.
  • At least 6 years of experience gained from legal studies and/or corporate legal departments.
  • Experience in national and international contracting.
  • Knowledge of Retail and, preferably, experience in Luxury, Lifestyle&Design, Beauty&Cosmesi, Consumer Goods.
  • Great knowledge of English. The knowledge of the French language is appreciated.
  • Excellent trading capacity, communication and relationship.
  • Autonomy in the management of activities.
  • Ability to work in an international and dynamic context.
