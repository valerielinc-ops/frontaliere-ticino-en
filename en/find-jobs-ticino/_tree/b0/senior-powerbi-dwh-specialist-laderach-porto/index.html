Full description

High-quality fresh chocolate - that's what the Swiss chocolate manufacturer Läderach stands for. Founded in 1962, the family business now employs over 2500 people from more than 80 countries across the group, and the trend is rising. The company's headquarters are located in the canton of Glarus. Here, against a picturesque Alpine backdrop, we produce our fresh chocolates and are constantly developing new, innovative ideas in agile teams. Our Corporate Functions business unit encompasses a wide range of activities. Alongside our Finance and IT Services, Global Marketing and Sales Management as well as Human Resources are also located here. Fresh ideas are developed here every day, and together we turn them into success stories. Personal responsibility and networked work are particularly important to us. Want to be part of it too? Come and join us. What you can expect You create and optimize advanced BI solutions using Microsoft Power BI , including scalable data models and Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) formulas You build and enhance the Data Warehouse, design robust data models, and integrate diverse data sources such as ERP, CRM, and cloud services You lead ETL projects with SQL Server Integration Services or Azure Data Factory , ensuring high data quality and maintaining performance in Microsoft SQL Server environments You conduct performance tuning, implement security concepts, and establish best practices for data management You advise internal stakeholders and coach junior team members What we expect from you You have a minimum of 5 years of experience as a Product Owner or in a similar IT role, with strong knowledge of Microsoft Power BI (DAX, Power Query, data modeling), SQL, and relational databases You design and implement data warehouses, manage ETL processes, and work with cloud platforms (Azure preferred) You apply Agile methodologies, manage backlogs, and translate business needs into technical requirements You demonstrate excellent communication, stakeholder management, leadership, and teamwork skills, with a solution-oriented and stress-resilient approach You are fluent in English (additional languages a plus), willing to travel within Europe, and aligned with Läderach values and the Läderach Charter. What we offer you An exciting working environment with motivated colleagues A position with lots of personal responsibility The opportunity to contribute fresh ideas An open and respectful feedback culture In-house and external further training Unique career opportunities Generous discounts on our products Chocolate at work Free parking spaces Own it. Grow it. Make it fresh. Sounds interesting? Then come join us. Emilija Minova , Head Talent Acquisition & Employer Branding, will be happy to answer your questions.
