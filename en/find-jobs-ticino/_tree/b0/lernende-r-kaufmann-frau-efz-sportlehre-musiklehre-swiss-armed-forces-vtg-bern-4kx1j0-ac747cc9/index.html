Put to the Point

Start your future in Command Cyber During your four-year apprenticeship, which is compatible with your sports or music career, Command Cyber helps you to balance your commitment and education. You get to know different commercial areas, supervise challenging subject areas and take on a wide range of administrative and organizational tasks – for example in management, administration, finance, human resources and computer science. For inclusion in sports education, a valid Swiss Olympic Talent Card or a written assessment of potential of the sport partner of BWD Bern is required in the sports sector; in the musical field, an assessment of potential by an external music expert is required. More information about music or sports teaching can be found at: Vocational School BWD Bern.

Diversity, dynamism, perspectives – teaching professions of the Swiss Army The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
