Full description

As a Senior HR IT Consultant, you assume responsibility for SAP SuccessFactors Core Modules in an international environment, develop the existing HR IT architecture further and combine the requirements of HR and IT.

Your field of activity

  • You are responsible for HR IT applications such as SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central and drive new innovations in a complex system landscape
  • Together with central and decentralized HR teams, you will analyze requirements and develop suitable solutions
  • You design and realize system adaptations and interfaces and coordinate with the architects
  • From planning to tests to deployment, you accompany the implementation of your projects
  • In 2nd- and 3rd-level support, you solve complex application problems and act as a competent contact person for HR-IT issues

You bring that with you

  • At least five years of experience with SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central and in-depth knowledge of other SuccessFactors modules
  • Experience in consulting on HR IT solutions and a good understanding of HR processes
  • You like to work independently and in a team, proactively promote innovations and bring different stakeholders together
  • You communicate in accordance with stakeholders and enjoy the cooperation between IT and HR
  • You see new challenges as development opportunities
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
