Full description

The opportunity

At Hitachi Energy our purpose is advancing a sustainable​ energy future for all. We bring power to our homes, schools, hospitals and factories. You will join a collaborative and diverse team shaping innovative transformer solutions. As a Tendering Engineer you create preliminary design cost estimations for dry type transformers plus inductors according to customer specifications. A secondary objective is to provide sales and customers with technical support and act as a link between sales and engineering.

Your responsibilities

  • Review customer specifications to define technical tender requirements
  • Develop deviation sheets and propose suitable technical solutions
  • Create accurate cost estimates and define material requirements in ERP and define material requirements
  • Obtain accurate pricing information by collaborating with suppliers & vendors
  • Ensure on-time cost estimate submittal to the bid and proposal team
  • Maintaining accurate technical and costing product information throughout the entire deal lifecycle
  • Provide technical tender support during bid revision or negotiation
  • Ensure clear and timely communication with all tender stakeholders

Your background

  • University Degree in Electrical or Mechanical engineering
  • First experience in a similar role, especially in regard to the technical skills
  • Experience in CAD and Microsoft 365 tools
  • Strong attention to detail and communication skills as well as a self-starter mentality
  • Fluent in English, Italian would be beneficial
  • Willingness to travel up to 10%

More about us

We are a technology-driven company with a strong focus on innovation, continuous improvement, and sustainable manufacturing. We offer flexible working conditions, dedicated mentorship, and a supportive, inclusive environment where you can grow personally and professionally – empowering you to thrive while balancing life and work.
