Full description

Who are we?

Teaching, research, jobs, events: The University of Fribourg is an area of innovation and an important driver for the economic and cultural life of the region. It attracts students and researchers from all over Switzerland and the world, it marks the daily life of the bilingual city of Fribourg, since its foundation in 1889.

The Knowledge and Technology Transfer Service of the University of Fribourg supports researchers in industry collaborations, the protection and commercialization of research results, and the creation of spin-offs.

Why join us?

We rely on the complementarity of skills and the variety of perspectives because it is the diversity of paths and profiles that makes the state of Fribourg the strength. We promote work-life balance and flexible working conditions. To learn more, follow this link .

Your missions

The position covers responsibilities currently associated with intellectual property and licensing, specifically for chemistry, physics and the humanities.

  • Evaluate inventions for patent protection and commercialization
  • Advise researchers on IP protection strategies
  • Conduct prior-art searches and patentability analyses
  • Assess novelty, inventive step and patentable subject matter
  • Identify the essential technical features of inventions and contribute to initial patent claims
  • Coordinate patent applications with researchers and external patent attorneys
  • Identity licensing and valorization partners
  • Negotiate and manage license, research and collaboration agreements
  • Management intellectual property and support early-stage spin-offs
  • Provide guidance and training to researchers on IP, commercialization, and industry engagement.
  • Build and maintain relationships with industry partners, innovation ecosystem stakeholders, and investors.

What we offer

A varied and international working environment at the intersection of science, intellectual property and business. Flexible working hours, possibility of remote work, continuing education and good work-life balance.

Two-year fixed-term contract, renewable.

Profile desired

  • You have a PhD in natural sciences, engineering or related discipline, in-depth practical understanding of intellectual property, including prior-art searches and patentability analyses, initial draft patent claims and further development in collaboration with patent attorneys
  • Minimum 3-5 years of experience in technology evaluation, licensing and technology transfer in education, industry, a startup company or a patent law firm
  • You are proactive, analytical, independent and comfortable working with researchers, companies and legal specialists. Strong organizational and project management capabilities are your strengths
  • You have excellent communication holder management skills and a professional command of English and either French or German
