Full description

06.11.2026 Bishop's cell

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Amriswil Bischofszell, a successful and locally oriented bank with offices in Amriswil, Bischofszell and Muolen, we are looking for a personality as an employee / in marketing.

What awaits you?

  • You support in the planning, implementation and further development of our marketing, communication and sales measures
  • You work at customer events, member events and sponsoring activities from the idea to implementation
  • You manage our communication channels, such as website and social media and develop them further
  • You design content and marketing materials for online and offline channels
  • You work with internal departments, agencies and external partners and contribute to a professional market presence
  • You will take on administrative tasks for bank management, including keeping logs and statistics, creating presentations, and organizing meetings and events.

What do we offer you?

  • Flexible working time model
  • Training and development opportunities
  • Short decision paths

What do you bring?

  • Commercial basic education or training in marketing, communication, media or a comparable environment
  • You enjoy working with people, communicating at eye level and getting others excited about joint projects
  • Enjoy organizing projects and coordinating different tasks at the same time.
  • Creativity, initiative and an independent working method
  • Very good knowledge of German and pleasure in writing comprehensible and appealing texts
  • Good knowledge of MS Office applications, experience with Adobe InDesign
  • Experience in marketing, communications or events
  • Raiffeisen experience is beneficial

Behind every successful brand are people with passion, creativity and drive. It is precisely these characteristics that we are looking for for our marketing team. Together we develop exciting projects, strengthen our presence in the region and create real experiences for our customers. Become part of our team! Rolf Müller Chairman of the Bank Management Why Raiffeisen?
