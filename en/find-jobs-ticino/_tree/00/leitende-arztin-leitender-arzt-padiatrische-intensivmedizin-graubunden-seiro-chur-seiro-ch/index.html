Full description

In Graubünden, as a leading physician, you will assume a central medical leadership function within an established children’s and adolescent clinic with a workload of 60 to 100%. In this responsible management position, you actively shape the further development of pediatric intensive care and neonatology and, together with an interprofessional team, shape the care of seriously ill children, adolescents and newborns at a high professional level. She expects a demanding clinical environment with a certified child intensive care unit, SIWF-approved training centre and close interdisciplinary cooperation. At the same time, you will have the opportunity to contribute your technical expertise, your leadership skills and your experience in pediatric intensive care medicine in a targeted manner and to develop it in a sustainable manner. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • Medical co-responsibility within pediatric intensive care medicine and neonatology
  • Interdisciplinary care of seriously ill children, adolescents and newborns at the PICU and NICU
  • Supervision and professional support of assistant doctors in further education
  • Active participation in clinical processes and further development of the child intensive care unit
  • Collaboration in the general paediatric day-to-day business within the children’s and youth clinic
  • Participation in internal and external training events

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist training completed
  • Swiss Federal Medical Diploma and Specialist Title of Paediatric and Adolescent Medicine FMH and Intensive Care Medicine FMH or equivalent recognised degrees
  • Several years of clinical experience in pediatric intensive care medicine and neonatology
  • Experience in professional leadership and supervision of medical teams
  • High medical competence in complex intensive care medical situations
  • Interest in further development, teaching and interprofessional cooperation

That sets them apart.

  • Leadership Personality with high social competence
  • Responsible, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Open and cooperative communication within interdisciplinary teams
  • Resilience and overview even in demanding clinical situations
  • Motivation to actively shape processes and take on responsibility

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Responsible cadre function with professional scope
  • Certified child intensive care unit with modern infrastructure
  • SIWF-recognised training centre with attractive development opportunities
  • Engaged interprofessional team with appreciative cooperation
  • Possibility to actively shape the further development of pediatric intensive care medicine
  • Attractive place of work in Graubünden with a high quality of life and closeness to nature

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition
  • Assistance in organizing travel to Switzerland
  • Support in the search for housing by the internal rental department
