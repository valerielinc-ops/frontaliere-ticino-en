Full description

Since 1929, the Foundation Pro Senectute Valais-Wallis has been committed to the quality of life of older people with the aim of maintaining their independence and representing their interests. For this purpose, the foundation has a wide range of services in the fields of social consulting, socio-cultural animation and sports.

To complement our team, we are looking for immediately or by agreement:

a social worker 60-80% ##

For social advice and mandate management

Place of work: Oberwallis (Brig, Visp and Susten)

Your tasks: ###

  • Voluntary advice to persons of AHV age and their relatives on topics such as finance, administration, housing, health, lifestyle and law
  • Developing financial entitlements and support (social insurance, individual financial assistance)
  • Assistance on behalf of KESB
  • Advice, support or representation of the persons concerned in legal, administrative, financial and personal matters, including income and asset management
  • Cooperation with internal and external specialist bodies
  • Preparation of reports, statements and requests for KESB
  • Processing of clarification orders on behalf of KESB

Your profile: ###

  • Completed studies in social work at a university or university of applied sciences
  • Interest in working with older people and the challenges involved
  • Interdisciplinary and networked thinking, high degree of independence
  • German mother tongue and good French skills
  • Willingness to carry out administrative work, organisational capacity and ability to work independently, at best with practical experience in social counselling or in support
  • Driving license and personal car for home visits

You can expect a challenging job in an exciting environment in which you can contribute to the tasks and concerns of Pro Senectute with your commitment and commitment. Pro Senectute offers contemporary employment conditions and attractive, exciting training opportunities.

Please send your complete application documents by e-mail by 02.11.2026 to the following address.

The interviews will take place on 4 and 11 November 2026.
