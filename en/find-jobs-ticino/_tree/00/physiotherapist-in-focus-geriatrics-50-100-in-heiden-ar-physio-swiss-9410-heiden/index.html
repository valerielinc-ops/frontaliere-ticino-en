We are looking for a versatile, approachable personality to provide comprehensive and systematic care for elderly people. Not only should your patients feel comfortable - but also you in everyday work. We have created the best framework conditions for this. You can be empathetic, courageous and down-to-earth with older people, encourage them, motivate them and offer them the support that enriches their lives. You are used to assessing the functional, psychological and cognitive abilities of your patients, documenting them measurably and taking appropriate therapeutic measures. In addition to the main work on the patient, you get paid time for administration and interdisciplinary exchange. So you have time to discuss target agreements especially for complex patients with care / Spitex / Ergo / doctors etc. In addition to the work in our comfortable premises, you also look after patients at home and in the local nursing home, with whom we have a service contract. Ideally, you will want to actively shape and expand our practical offer. There are hardly any limits to your initiative: From age-specific group courses to interdisciplinary educational work to quality management, you can contribute and implement your own ideas. You can count on our technical and strategic support and get consciously planned working time for innovation and conception.

Why you will feel comfortable with us

  • Family working atmosphere: loyalty, support and support in the team
  • More time for you: 6 weeks of vacation and no overtime thanks to clearly regulated workload
  • More time for your patients: max. 2 treatments per hour and paid time for documentation, analysis and communication
  • Development instead of routine: strong professional supervision and cost assumption in further education
  • Unlimited access to HEIDENfitness also for your personal training
