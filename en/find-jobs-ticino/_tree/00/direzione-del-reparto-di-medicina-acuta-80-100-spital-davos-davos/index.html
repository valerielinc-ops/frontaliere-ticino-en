Full description

As Ward Manager of the Acute Care Unit, you are responsible for professional and personnel leadership and actively work within your team. You support staff in ensuring smooth daily operations and in providing nursing care in the acute care setting. As a regional hospital/acute care hospital with emergency services, nursing home and home care (Spitex), we are responsible for the basic medical care of the Davos population and visitors. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees. You will work in a manageable yet extremely important facility for the region. We foster a collegial work environment and enjoy the proximity to nature as well as the numerous sports and leisure opportunities Davos has to offer. ##

  • Responsibilities
  • Comprehensive nursing care for inpatient and outpatient patients
  • Planning and documentation of the nursing process in close coordination with qualified nursing staff
  • Planning, coordination and supervision of nursing care in collaboration with medical staff and interdisciplinary services
  • Responsibility for leadership, deployment and development of directly subordinate staff
  • Conduct regular performance appraisal interviews
  • Support and advise the nursing team in complex care situations
  • Ensure high nursing quality and continuous optimization of work processes
  • Responsibility for the cost center of the Acute Care Unit
  • Regular participation in nursing management meetings ##
  • Requirements
  • Completed tertiary-level education in nursing and several years of professional experience in an acute care hospital
  • Proven leadership experience or willingness to complete relevant further training
  • Enthusiasm for a seasonally influenced operation with an intensive winter period involving an expanded team, lively summer months and quieter periods during the off-season
  • Strong communication and organizational skills, high social competence, sense of responsibility, empathy and assertiveness
  • Independent, innovative and goal-oriented working style
  • Respectful and appreciative interaction with team members and patients
  • Excellent spoken and written German language skills ## What We Offer
  • Challenging, varied and interesting work within a professional and dedicated team
  • Autonomy with significant independence and trust
  • Attractive employment conditions and social benefits, hospital-operated day care for children up to kindergarten age
  • Diverse sports and leisure opportunities in the highest city in Europe ## Contact
  • For further information, please contact Bea Heeb, Head of Nursing (+41 81 414 82 50, bheeb@spitaldavos.ch), or our HRM Manager Gregor Bienz (+41 81 414 84 36, gbienz@spitaldavos.ch).
