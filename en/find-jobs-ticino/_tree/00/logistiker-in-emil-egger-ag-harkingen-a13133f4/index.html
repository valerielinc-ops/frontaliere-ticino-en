Full description

The EMIL EGGER Group is one of the leading Swiss logistics companies and offers its customers a comprehensive range of services in the areas of general cargo, heavy cargo and warehouse logistics. At 11 locations, around 800 employees currently work for well-known clients from industry, trade and trade. Your tasks Do you like working in a dynamic and exciting environment? At Emil Egger AG in Härkingen, we are looking for a dedicated logistics specialist who will enrich our team.

  • Responsibility for the reception, quality control and storage of goods.
  • Order picking.
  • Packaging and making available of goods for shipping.
  • Assistance in inventory work and ensuring stock accuracy.

Our requirements We are looking for a reliable and motivated person who has the necessary know-how and enthusiasm to play an important role in our logistics department. To be successful, you should bring the following qualifications:

  • Completed training (EFZ) in logistics with proven professional experience
  • Enjoyment of a lively and varied operation
  • Forklift pass, hall crane
  • Routine handling of hand scanners
  • Strong organizational skills and a high degree of accuracy.
  • Teamwork, resilience, sense of duty and an independent working method.
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
