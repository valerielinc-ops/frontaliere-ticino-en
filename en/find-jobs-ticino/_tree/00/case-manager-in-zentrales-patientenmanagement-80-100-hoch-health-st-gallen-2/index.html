Full description

Case Manager/in Central Patient Management 80-100 %

Clinic of Medical Oncology and Haematology

Place of work St. Gallen

Employee Disposition in Central Patient Management 80 – 100%

Your tasks and perspectives

  • General administrative work as well as planning of outpatient consultation and therapy appointments in close cooperation with the nursing staff, the doctors
  • Contact person for patients at the counter, by telephone and electronically
  • Visiting card of our clinic and responsible for the correct triage of our patients
  • Contact person for inquiries from assignees and upstream and downstream institutions
  • Active and service-oriented member of our disposition team

What you bring for this job

  • Education as a Med. Practice assistant/EFZ, medical secretary or commercial training with a medical background
  • Flexible and resilient personality with empathy for patients in difficult life situations
  • Independent working methods, a quick understanding as well as a sense of responsibility and quality
  • Networked thinking, openness and willingness to learn about change processes
  • Excellent MS-Office knowledge and talent in dealing with electronic working instruments, experience with the clinic information system KISIM is an advantage

Our offer to you

  • Regular working hours, excluding weekend service
  • Dynamic and well-functioning team
  • Versatile and varied work in interprofessional and interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Various employee benefits around health, work and family - including attractive perks and more
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
