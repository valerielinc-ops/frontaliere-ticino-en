Full description

Specialist Aftersales & Warranty Processing (m/f/d | 80 - 100%) HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 500 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company. To complement our LIFE-CYCLE MANAGEMENT in the Bus & Trolley division, we are looking for a qualified and dynamic mechanic for exciting tasks in the area of after-sales & warranty processing as an interface between office and service. You have completed technical training – for example as an automotive mechatronics engineer – are tired of workshops and would like to use your know-how in administrative customer service in the future? With us, you have the opportunity to take the next step. In our lifecycle management team, you take on administrative tasks, process warranty cases and support customer inquiries. Their tasks

  • Assistance in maintaining and creating technical excel lists (e.g. maintenance plan)
  • Preparation of project evaluations
  • Ensuring warranty processing in cooperation with suppliers
  • Assistance in creating complex cost calculations (including reporting)
  • Control and accounting of invoices
  • Creation of technical documentation for customers in the area of aftersales (e.g. service and training concepts)
  • Processing of guarantee applications
  • Answering customer inquiries
  • General administrative activities Our requirements
  • Basic technical training (advantage in the automotive sector) with commercial or technical training
  • Good language skills in German mandatory (French and English advantageous)
  • Good computer skills (Excel, Outlook, etc.)
  • High communication skills and independence
  • Good manners and a perfect appearance We offer them
  • Interesting and varied permanent position
  • Flexible working hours and opportunity for part-time work and home office
  • Performance-based conditions of employment
  • Working in a collegial team with very good cohesion If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
