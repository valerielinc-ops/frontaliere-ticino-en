Hirslanden Klinik Aarau stands for top medicine in the personal environment. Our clinical goal is: "First-class medicine with personal treatment, as we wish for our loved ones".

For Hirslanden Klinik Aarau we are looking for a committed personality as team leader / team leader ambulatory physiotherapy and sports center, who combines professional expertise with leadership skills, actively shapes the outpatient physiotherapy and training operation and jointly drives the development of our new sports center.

YOUR TASKS

  • You ensure a high-quality, patient-oriented and smooth outpatient physiotherapy and training operation and take into account the economic framework conditions
  • You take over the personnel and operational leadership of the assigned team, are the first contact person in everyday work and promote a constructive, appreciative cooperation
  • You are actively engaged in the direct clinical activity in outpatient physiotherapy and support your team surgically if necessary
  • You work closely with the department management and relieve them in a targeted way in day-to-day business: You coordinate independently within your defined competencies, inform regularly and consult at an early stage with relevant questions
  • You ensure compliance with existing processes as well as the quality and safety standards and, in consultation with your manager, work on their continuous development with

Your profile

  • You have a completed basic training as a dipl. Physical therapist, and ideally bring training in management or leadership
  • In-depth experience in sports physiotherapy and a pronounced affinity for sport are central to team management in our sports centre.
  • In addition, you bring broad expertise in other therapeutic areas and understand how to coordinate them with the responsible specialists.
  • You convince through your winning personality, your networked thinking, your implementation strength as well as your vision and creative will
  • You openly encounter change and understand how to accompany and actively shape change processes with clarity, communication strength and empathy
  • You value interdisciplinary cooperation and see it as an important success factor for high-quality and patient-oriented care

With the opening of our new sports centre at the beginning of 2027, you will have the exciting opportunity to actively shape the further development of our offer and to make an important contribution to the future of our sports physiotherapy. You work closely with our sports doctors and an interdisciplinary team you can rely on – in an environment characterized by appreciation, team spirit and humor.

We offer you at least 25 holiday days, 4 additional paid free days as compensation for the changing time as well as interesting career and development opportunities. All other employment highlights can be found here.
