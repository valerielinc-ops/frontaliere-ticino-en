The Position

Who we are

Roche Pharma AG at its site in Grenzach-Wyhlen manages, among other things, the medical activities, marketing and sales activities for the German market. The Events & Congresses division is responsible for the conceptual and organizational planning and implementation of Roche’s own events, Roche’s congress appearances and cooperative participations.

What to expect

During your internship You have the following diverse activities:

  • Support of the Events & Congresses Specialists in the planning and implementation of face-to-face, virtual and hybrid events as well as in participation in German and international congresses
  • Support and implementation of partial tasks in the areas of:
  • Project management of events and conferences
  • Research and booking of hotels, restaurants and other locations
  • Integration of digital channels such as Roche Fachportal
  • Participant management including contract management
  • Technical support for virtual events
  • On-site support of large events
  • Administrative handling of events in the Roche systems

Who you are

You are interested in event & congress management in the pharmaceutical sector and ideally already have initial experience in event & congress management and project management. In addition, you are customer-oriented and communicative, work in a structured, independent and solution-oriented manner and enjoy solving tasks in a team.

For the internship in the area of Events & Congresses You also have the following requirements and knowledge with:

  • General prerequisite for employment as an intern is regular enrollment during the entire internship or the internship is completed within a gap year, i.e. after the bachelor's degree in preparation for a master's degree
  • You are a student of (m/f/d) with a focus on event management, the hotel industry, economics or communication sciences or the like.
  • Good knowledge of MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Good knowledge of English and German in word and writing

Period: from 01.02.2027 or by agreement for a period of 6 months
