Full description

The Board of Directors of the Autonomous Body Lugano Social Institutes opens the public competition for recruiting one/one

Management of Human Resources

under the conditions of the Organic Regulation of Collaborators (ROCIS) of the Autonomous Body Lugano Social Institutes (LIS) and the competition chapter.

The position is open to candidates of all kinds.

General requirements, those provided for by ROCIS and in particular:

  • Swiss or foreign nationals with a working permit;
  • unexceptionable conduct;
  • healthy physical and mental constitution;
  • suitability to function.

Special requirements

  • Federal Certificate of Specialist in staff management or university education with address related to the function accompanied by specialized training in human resources;
  • Master in Advanced Studies in Human Capital Management is a preferred title;
  • Comprehensive and multiannual experience as responsible for human resources in medium or large companies;
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss legislative framework on labour law and social insurance;
  • Activities for the management of a team and experience in the management of groups or services;
  • Excellent mastery of the Office package and human resource management tools;
  • Italian mother tongue, knowledge of a second national language is preferential title;
  • Operational and learning rapidity;
  • Precision and discretion;
  • Ease of interpersonal communication, spirit of initiative, trading ability, conflict management and problem solving;
  • Relationship skills with different public;
  • Autonomy in work management and ability to respond to high quality standards;
  • Spirit of service, motivation and willingness to integrate into a dynamic team;
  • Decision, editorial and analysis capacity;
  • Open personality and leadership skills;
  • Good organizational and project management skills;
  • Flexibility in the management of diversified activities;
  • Ability to manage workloads, stress and complexity

Preferential requirements

  • Knowledge of Progel, EClinic, Arxivar programs;
  • Familiarity with the context of older and/or educational homes for minors;
  • Experience in public or parapublic organisations;
  • Experience of the management of complex situations concerning staff (relational and regulatory law in force);
  • Experience in counselling to managers, with analysis of issues and accompaniment in decisions
  • Management in the optimization of the main HR processes and in the management of organizational development projects.

Mansions

On the basis of the work programmes, quota needs and priorities defined by the Directorate coordinates and checks the activities and procedures related to the management of the Human Resources Service ensuring the implementation of the tasks envisaged in the expected terms.

In particular

  • It maintains regular contacts with the Directorate and the Managers (director, headset, department, capital and office);
  • Directs, organizes, coordinates and supervises the activities of the Human Resources Service for recruitment, inclusion, administration, wage management, training and staff development, ensuring the quality of performance, processes and the proper management of the resources allocated;
  • It plays an active role in assessing complex individual and collective situations, including relational issues, conflicts, administrative and disciplinary proceedings, in compliance with decision-making and applicable provisions;
  • Promotes and coordinates projects of improvement of HR processes, digitization and management of change, in accordance with the strategic guidelines of the body;
  • Check the correct application of ROCIS and the provisions and directives in force in particular in the field of wage policy, as well as their updating;
  • Implementing calculations, processing documents, studies, analysis, statistics and projections of business data by formulating proposals for action and long-term strategies;
  • In the field of health and well-being, in collaboration with the competent functions, the management of absences, case management, professional reintegration and prevention of psychosocial risks;
  • It manages administrative practices and forms, autonomously evading correspondence and other activities related to the Staff Service;
  • He is responsible for overseeing wage management and recruitment flows, coordinating the reference services;
  • Collaborate with the drafting of the estimate and conjunctive accounts;
  • It deals with implementing and optimizing workflows;
  • Takes other strategic and operational tasks according to the provisions of the Directorate;
  • It updates professionally and attends specific courses;
  • Human Resources Department responds to the Director.

It can also be called to perform other tasks compatible with its function and its skills.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
