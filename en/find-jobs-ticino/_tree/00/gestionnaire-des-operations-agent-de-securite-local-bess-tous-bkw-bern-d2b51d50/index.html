Full description

You connect project and operation and ensure that a project becomes an operational plant. As a central interface, you ensure that the requirements are incorporated early and that new BESS systems are technically, organizationally and safely ready for commercial operation.

Your field of activity

  • You coordinate the operational preparation of new BESS facilities from planning to transfer to commercial operation
  • As a link between project and operational organization, you bring in operational requirements and coordinate the cooperation of planners, operators and suppliers
  • Together with the project team, you define the operational readiness criteria, coordinate operational readiness checks and ensure the structured transfer to operation.
  • Operation, maintenance, safety and emergency concepts as well as the associated documentation are ensured together with the relevant specialist departments
  • You keep an eye on risks, open points and pendencies, drive their processing forward and escalate critical deviations early on.
  • As a supplementary task, you will take on the role of Local Cyber Security Officer together with another team member and support you in the implementation of relevant guidelines and measures.

You bring that with you

  • You have a technical education or study, preferably in electrical engineering, energy engineering, automation or a comparable subject area
  • Experience in the operation, commissioning or project management of complex energy or technical plants; BESS knowledge is a plus
  • Interface and stakeholder management are yours and you understand how to bring together different specialist positions in a targeted manner
  • You will quickly identify technical and organizational relationships and tackle challenges in a structured, solution-oriented and independent manner.
  • With your clear communication, your secure appearance and your assertiveness, you also represent operational requirements to different stakeholders.
  • A sense of safety, quality and responsibility shape your way of working and you think beyond organizational and departmental boundaries
