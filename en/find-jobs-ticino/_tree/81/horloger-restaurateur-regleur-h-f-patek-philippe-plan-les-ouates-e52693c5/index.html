Full description

Mission

As part of this function, which is part of the International Customer Service Division, you will be responsible for adjusting historical watches (produced between 1839 and 1970) during their restoration.

Responsibilities

  • Perform technical diagnostics on the adjustment part.
  • Ensure the start and adjust the balancers with flat spiral and Breguet by guaranteeing an optimal level of quality while respecting the assembly and control processes.
  • Ensure conformity of completion functions.
  • Ensure aesthetic, technical and dimensional control of the adjustment part in order to meet our quality standards.

Profile sought

  • CFC watchmaker Dresser or equivalent
  • From 5 years of experience in the repair of high-end watch products.
  • Control of mandatory setting operations.
  • Excellent sense of analysis, rigour and accuracy.
  • Ability to work in pairs, cooperation and mutual assistance.
  • Ability to communicate constructively and to question each other.
