Job listing

Your tasks and perspectives

  • Ensuring professional, professional and patient-oriented emergency care
  • Independent planning, organisation and execution of tasks of the emergency unit
  • Recognizing and acting independently in life-threatening situations
  • Installation of rail material and plaster casts
  • Care of relatives

What you bring for this job

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH with completed training NDS HF emergency care or several years of experience
  • High social skills, empathy and expertise in dealing with patients and relatives
  • Joy of working independently in a complex environment and openness to new things
  • Interest in the continuous development of care quality
  • Reliability and committed, responsible and team-oriented working methods

Our offer to you

  • Interesting and challenging task area in which you can apply and expand your expertise
  • Compensation for short-term intervention
  • Future-oriented and innovative company in a scenic environment
  • Family cooperation in a highly specialized, interdisciplinary environment
  • Broad spectrum of professional and personal training opportunities
