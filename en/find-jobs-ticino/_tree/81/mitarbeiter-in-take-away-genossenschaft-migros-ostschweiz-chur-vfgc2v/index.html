Full description

The gastronomy of the cooperative Migros Eastern Switzerland is diverse, modern and dynamic. Whether Migros restaurant, Migros take away, shared gastronomy or catering services – we offer every customer a tasteful experience.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Take Away, Gäuggeli (Take Away), Gäugglistrasse 28, 7000 Chur

Mansioni

  • You prepare with passion warm and cold food as well as seasonal specialties fresh and provide for enjoyable moments with our guests.
  • You actively serve and advise our customers at the food outlet. With your commitment, you contribute to making our customers feel comfortable.
  • You make sure that the hygiene regulations according to food safety are adhered to and consistently implemented.

Requirements

  • Professional experience: in gastronomy
  • Completed basic or further education: in gastronomy
  • You love being in touch with people and enjoy getting our guests excited.
  • Fresh foods are exactly your thing – you know how to put them perfectly on stage.
  • You are a team player, flexible and tackle everywhere.
  • Independent and accurate work is a matter of course for you
  • You always want to develop and learn new things
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of friamo

  • L-GAV: Attractive Land collective employment contract
  • Occupational pension provision: Attractive pension fund benefits with high employer participation
  • Insurance: Benefit Conditions at Various Insurance Companies
  • Cumulus points: Multiple cumulus points on food and nonfood items
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts on holiday offers and events
