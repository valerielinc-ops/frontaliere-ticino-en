Statistics count. Also for you.

The Federal Statistical Office (BFS) is the national centre of excellence for public statistics and a key player in the Swiss data ecosystem. It produces statistical information, works with the national data management for harmonised data and develops the knowledge gained from data using data science methods. The BFS thus contributes to the formation of public opinion and makes an important contribution to modern democratic governance.

Additional information

The Social Assistance Section is responsible for the collection, processing, analysis and publication of data on needs-based social benefits in Switzerland. It operates the social assistance statistics together with cantons, municipalities and other partners and provides up-to-date information for the management of social policy. In addition, the section develops and operates statistical bases, indicators, monitoring systems and analyses on the social security system.
