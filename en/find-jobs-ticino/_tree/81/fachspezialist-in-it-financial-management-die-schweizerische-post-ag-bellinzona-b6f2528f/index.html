IT Financial Management Specialist — Die Schweizerische Post AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Do you feel comfortable at the interface between IT and finance? Do you like to work independently and do you want to actively shape the financial management of our IT services? Then apply today. With you, we create transparency about the costs of our IT services and further develop their financial management!

Your advantages in the post.

We offer a fair wage, which ranges between CHF 84’000 and CHF 94’000 per year (100% fee) depending on experience and skills. Also applications of talents with different competencies are welcome, in such cases your wage can be slightly below or above the bandwidth.

With us, you work in a future-oriented, dynamic environment in which you are involved and with which you continue to grow. With us, you can expect various benefits, with which you can flexibly design your everyday life according to your needs.

Holidays

We will send you 6 weeks on holiday.

Education and training

We support you with training and further education, temporary job changes and offer you promotion opportunities.

Family friendly

We offer 18 weeks of fully paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of leave for the second parent. We also contribute to the costs of family-supplemented childcare.

Flexibility

We support you with individual working models. Mobile and flexible working, home office, third place, part-time work and job sharing.

Workation

We offer you the opportunity to use up to 20 days per year for a workation.

Mobility offers

We offer you a free half-tax subscription, a discounted GA or a commuter bonus. You pay only CHF 10 per year at PubliBike or Velospot.

PostMobil and PostFinance

Through PostMobile, we offer you an attractive employee subscription for CHF 12.95 per month. With PostFinance, you also benefit from numerous advantages, such as a free private account.

Advantages and rebates

We offer you numerous perks on brands and products. At the staff restaurants of the Post Office and other restaurants you also eat and drink at discounted prices.

Vouchers

We give you up to CHF 500 personal credit, which you can use for all products in the PostShop (including gift cards) as well as for products from SBB and Reka.

You can do that.

  • As a specialist: in IT Financial Management, you are responsible for the financial management of the operating cost elements and independently carry out the relevant tasks in our financial processes.
  • In close cooperation with the IT and finance teams, you will create detailed analyses and consistently implement the measures derived from them.
  • You advise and support your direct environment and thus make an important contribution to the cost-effective provision of our IT services.
  • You use AI and automation specifically to optimize, simplify and make our financial management processes more efficient.
  • You are actively involved in projects related to the IT Service Management of Post Informatik and thus shape the way into the future.

You bring that with you.

  • You have completed a higher specialist training in business informatics or business administration or have a comparable qualification.
  • You have experience in finance, ideally in controlling, or in IT and have a basic understanding of the other area.
  • You are enthusiastic about solving complex tasks and bring a systematic, solution-oriented mindset as well as assertiveness.
  • For the exchange with our customers and with your colleagues (including in Lisbon), you communicate very well in German and well in English.
