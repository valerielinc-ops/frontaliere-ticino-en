Full description

Do you feel comfortable at the interface between IT and finance? Do you like to work independently and do you want to actively shape the financial management of our IT services? Then apply today. With you, we create transparency about the costs of our IT services and further develop their financial management!

You can do that.

  • As a specialist: in IT Financial Management, you are responsible for the financial management of the operating cost elements and independently carry out the relevant tasks in our financial processes.
  • In close cooperation with the IT and finance teams, you will create detailed analyses and consistently implement the measures derived from them.
  • You advise and support your direct environment and thus make an important contribution to the cost-effective provision of our IT services.
  • You use AI and automation specifically to optimize, simplify and make our financial management processes more efficient.
  • You are actively involved in projects related to the IT Service Management of Post Informatik and thus shape the way into the future.

You bring that with you.

  • You have completed a higher specialist training in business informatics or business administration or have a comparable qualification.
  • You have experience in finance, ideally in controlling, or in IT and have a basic understanding of the other area.
  • You are enthusiastic about solving complex tasks and bring a systematic, solution-oriented mindset as well as assertiveness.
  • For the exchange with our customers and with your colleagues (including in Lisbon), you communicate very well in German and well in English.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
