His commitment to security and freedom

The Operations Command (Cdo Op) is responsible for all operations and military uses of the Swiss Army. It includes, among other things, the Air Forces, the Earth Forces, the four territorial divisions, the SWISSINT competence center, the Special Forces command and the military police. Approximately 40 percent of nearly 2500 employees are employed civilians: from engineers to aircraft mechanics to analysts.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 employees in the framework of four military occupations and about 200 civil occupations, in 110 locations across the national territory. It also provides new levers, forming apprentices in over 30 professions.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
