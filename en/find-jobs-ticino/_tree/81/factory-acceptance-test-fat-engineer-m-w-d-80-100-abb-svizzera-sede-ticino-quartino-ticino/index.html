Your role and responsibilities
In this role within the Customer Project Execution Team, you will conduct customer witnessed testing and verification activities for assignments of medium/high testing complexity of systems,
subsystems, or products effectively and cost-efficiently and in accordance with contract specifications, quality standards, safety requirements, and cybersecurity policies and standards.

The work model for the role is: onsite

This role is contributing to the ABB Electrification, Smart Power, Critical Power and Customer Projects Execution team in Quartino, Switzerland.

You will be mainly accountable for:

  • End-to-End Testing Accountability: Plan, prepare, and execute customer-witnessed type tests, including full documentation, while ensuring compliance with contractual, regulatory, and industry standards.

  • Technical Expertise & Support: Provide technical support during installation and testing, perform first-level troubleshooting, and collaborate closely with Project Management and Sales to ensure successful validation and delivery.

  • Leadership & Collaboration: Guide and support test engineers, promote best practices across projects, and collaborate effectively with internal stakeholders and suppliers to ensure testing readiness and quality.

  • Process, Risk & Quality Management: Apply and continuously improve standardized processes and tools, conduct risk assessments to ensure safe operations, and proactively identify and report improvement opportunities.

• Qualifications for the role

  • Minimum of 5 years of experience in testing and validation of products within the electromechanical field. Experience with UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) systems is considered a strong advantage.

  • Strong passion for technical excellence, with the ability and willingness to share knowledge and best practices across teams and projects.

  • Native Italian speaker with strong English (B1) communication skills; able to interact confidently and professionally with customers and internal stakeholders in an international environment.

  • Positive, collaborative, and proactive approach, with strong problem-solving skills and the ability to contribute to team performance and engagement.

More about us

ABB Smart Power provides energy distribution solutions for data centers, industrial and manufacturing plants, critical infrastructure and commercial buildings. The Division’s technical teams work closely with industry partners, delivering advanced solutions that support rapid growth, energy transition, and sustainability objectives. The Division’s portfolio includes industrial circuit breakers, low-voltage systems, motor starting applications, and safety devices like switches and relays. Its Power Protection unit supports the world’s largest data center companies with advanced energy-efficient UPS solutions. The Division’s ABB Ability™ Energy Manager provides a scalable, easy-to-use platform that helps organizations save energy and reduce CO2 emissions.
