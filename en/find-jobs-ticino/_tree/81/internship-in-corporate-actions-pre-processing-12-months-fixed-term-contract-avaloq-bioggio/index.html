Full description

Banking Operations is a strategic Avaloq service line providing Banking Operations outsourcing services to banks. We are organized as a global business area, running state-of-the art backoffice services with industry leading automation, quality, and risk standards. We are experts in providing a true value add to our clients and thus, always look to add creative and client-oriented personalities with drive and passion to our team. The Global CA pre-processing, is located in Bioggio and is responsible for the set-up and notification to eligible clients of corporate action events. You will be part of a global department located in 3 different countries (Germany, Switzerland and Singapore) responsible for managing Corporate Action business/processes (announcements, Instructions and Payments).

Your key tasks

  • Analysis of Custodian’s MT564/568 swift messages
  • Set-up of Corporate Actions events
  • Notifications in various languages to end-clients of the banks for elective corporate actions events
  • Resolution of client’s inquiries

Qualifiche

  • Economics studies, preferably at university level and with a focus in Finance
  • Proactive approach towards a highly challenging, fast-paced and complex environment
  • Good communication and organization skills
  • Good team player and positive attitude
  • Flexibility and willingness to learn
  • English language

It would be a real bonus if you have

  • Know-how in the Corporate Actions area (even if only theoretical) is considered as a strong plus
  • Knowledge of additional languages (German or French preferably)
