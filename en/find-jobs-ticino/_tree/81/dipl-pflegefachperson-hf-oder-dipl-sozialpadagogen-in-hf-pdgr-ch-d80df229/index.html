Full description

Your surroundings

The Salvorta psychotherapy station is an open-guided station with 30 beds. At the heart of our therapeutic concept is mentalization-based therapy (MBT). We treat women and men with depression, anxiety, compulsive, eating, trauma and personality disorders. Another focus of treatment is the treatment of pain disorders and decompensated tinnitus. In addition, the psychotherapy unit offers six beds for mother/father child treatment.

Their tasks

  • They accompany and support patients as part of an individual psychotherapeutic treatment process
  • As a nursing care provider, you take responsibility for planning, implementing and evaluating care and treatment
  • They advise and accompany relatives and involve them in the treatment process in a resource-oriented manner
  • You actively contribute your professional observations and assessments to interprofessional cooperation
  • You plan, guide and evaluate nursing group therapies
  • They network with internal and external specialists and contribute to a continuous treatment quality
  • They shape an appreciative, therapeutic environment and promote the individual development and stabilization of our patients.
  • They maintain an open, respectful and person-oriented communication

Our requirement profile

  • You have a degree as a dipl. Care professional HF or Dipl. Social Pedagogue / Social Pedagogue HF
  • Ideally, you will bring experience in the psychiatric or psychotherapeutic field
  • You meet people with mental illnesses empathetically, professionally and appreciatively
  • You work independently, responsibly and have a high ability to reflect
  • They value interprofessional cooperation and have strong social and self-competences
  • You are interested in psychotherapeutic treatment concepts and ideally have knowledge of mentalization-based approaches.

We offer you

  • You take on a varied and responsible job in a motivated, interprofessional team
  • You have the opportunity to shape and implement the treatment and care approaches professionally and organizationally
  • Take advantage of our internal training and further education offer – we also support your external training needs
  • With part-time work models, 6 weeks of vacation and unpaid leave, an optimal work-life balance
