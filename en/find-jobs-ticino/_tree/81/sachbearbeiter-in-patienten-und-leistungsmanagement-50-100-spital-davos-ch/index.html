Full description

With your commercial knowledge, your language skills and your calm manner, you ensure a smooth process in patient administration and a forward-looking admission of our emergency patients. In addition, you support our financial team with your pay flair and ensure that we can bill our services correctly and on time. As a regional hospital/acute hospital with ambulance service, nursing home and Spitex, we are responsible for basic medical care for the Davos population and guests. Equally important to us is the well-being of our employees.
