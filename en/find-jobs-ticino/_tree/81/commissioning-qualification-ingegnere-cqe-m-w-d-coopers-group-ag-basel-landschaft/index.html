Full description

For international projects in the pharmaceutical engineering industry, we are looking for a hands-on Commissioning & Qualification Engineer . In this role, you will support experienced CQEs directly on client sites and perform operational IQ/OQ and qualification activities on pharmaceutical equipment.

Hard Facts

  • Start Date: Immediate start or by mutual agreement, no later than within 2 months
  • Duration: Initially 6 months, with the possibility of extension or permanent employment
  • Workload: 100% (40 hours per week)
  • Location: Across Europe, currently mainly in Belgium
  • Work Model: 100% travel-based role, typically 2 weeks on-site followed by travel home
  • Expenses: Travel, accommodation, and approved project-related expenses are fully covered

Your Responsibilities

  • Execute and document IQ/OQ protocols and qualification activities
  • Work according to established test plans, protocols, and templates
  • Perform and support calibration activities
  • Conduct HVAC, cleanroom, and facility qualification tasks
  • Ensure GMP-compliant documentation of test results
  • Carry out hands-on work on pharmaceutical equipment and isolators
  • Collaborate closely with an experienced Lead CQE on-site

Your Profile

  • Approximately 3 years of experience in a GMP-regulated environment
  • Practical experience with IQ/OQ, Commissioning, or Equipment Qualification
  • Knowledge of calibration, HVAC qualification, cleanroom qualification, or equipment qualification
  • Good command of English or German (minimum B2 level)
  • Technical vocational training, apprenticeship, or further technical qualification is advantageous
  • Hands-on mentality and willingness to work directly on equipment
  • Physically fit and comfortable working in operational environments
  • Willingness to travel extensively across Europe and occasionally work additional hours when required

Experience with isolators is considered an advantage but is not mandatory. Most important are your practical GMP experience, reliable documentation skills, and your willingness to work directly on pharmaceutical equipment.

Before your first client assignment, you will participate in a one-week training program in the Basel region. Successful completion of the subsequent assessment is required before deployment to client projects.

Interested? We look forward to receiving your CV, along with relevant references and certifications. Please also indicate your earliest possible start date.
