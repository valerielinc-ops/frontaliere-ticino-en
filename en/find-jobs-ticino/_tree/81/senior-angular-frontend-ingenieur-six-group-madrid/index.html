Senior Angular Frontend Engineer

Madrid | Working from home up to 40% | Reference 8029

We're looking for a Senior Angular Frontend Engineer to join our agile, self-organized global engineering team building innovative products for our Repo customers. You will play a key role in designing and developing modern, scalable web applications within the financial markets domain, working closely with product, UX, and engineering teams to deliver exceptional user experiences.

Please apply with your CV in English. We’re looking forward to meeting you.

What You Will Do

  • Design, develop, and maintain modern web applications using Angular and TypeScript.
  • Drive frontend architecture decisions with a focus on scalability, performance, and maintainability.
  • Build responsive, accessible, and user-friendly interfaces.
  • Collaborate closely with product owners, UX designers, backend engineers, and other stakeholders.
  • Integrate frontend solutions with APIs and external services.
  • Contribute to code reviews, technical documentation, testing, and CI/CD best practices.

What You Bring

  • 5+ years of experience developing enterprise web applications with Angular, TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS/SCSS.
  • Strong expertise in modern Angular concepts, including component architecture, state management, routing, and performance optimization.
  • Experience building responsive and accessible user interfaces following frontend best practices.
  • Solid understanding of Agile methodologies, Git, CI/CD pipelines, Docker, and cloud-native environments.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a proactive and solution-oriented mindset.
  • Fluent written and spoken English, with excellent collaboration and communication skills.
