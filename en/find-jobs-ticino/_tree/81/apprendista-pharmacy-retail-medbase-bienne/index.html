Welcome to the Medbase Apotheke Biel Bahnhof

In our centrally located pharmacy, just a few steps from Biel Central Station, we advise our long-standing customers and passers-by. In addition to a wide range of products from conventional medicine, naturopathy and cosmetics, we offer services such as vaccinations, health checks or consultations. Our pharmacy is also involved in the training of apprentices and pharmacists in the assistant year. We at Medbase Apotheke Biel Bahnhof appreciate the participation in interdisciplinary pilot projects and the development opportunities in various specialist areas. We care for each other and love the lively exchange with customers.

You can move that

In your training at Medbase, you will be accompanied and supported by an experienced team. You will learn all the important facets of pharmacy life and develop the skills you need for a successful career.

  • Checks and receipts
  • Compilation of medicines according to doctor's prescriptions and labelling with instructions
  • Production of simple recipes and in-house medicines under guidance
  • Customer advice in the areas of health, disease and cosmetics
  • Attractive presentation of products in the pharmacy

What you bring

  • Good performance in science subjects of secondary school A / subdivided upper level E
  • Enjoyment of customer contact and self-responsible work
  • Reliability, empathy and careful work

#WeAreMedbase

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in ambulatory healthcare. This means services ranging from health promotion to medication and acute treatment to rehabilitation. Health professionals from different disciplines work hand in hand to provide people across Switzerland with integrated and world-class medical care. With logistics services and digital solutions, we support doctors so that they too can fully concentrate on patients.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
