Collaborator data entry \- CSD 3 years \- 80%

About the role

Within a team of 6 people dedicated to inventory and investment accounting, you will be responsible for integrating new hospital goods and equipment into the system. This one-year temporary role is crucial in expanding our client's infrastructure.

We offer you A contract of one year to 80% with flexible hours and offices ideally located 5 minutes from the station.

Tasks

Administrative seizure and tracking of capital assets and billing supplier for the extension of our customer's establishments.

  • Enter investment and asset data into the management system
  • Record vendor invoices related to new equipment
  • Create capital asset records following procedures
  • Ensure standard end-of-year billing
  • Work closely with the accounting team

We're looking for

  • Business employee CFCs or training deemed equivalent
  • Interest in finance and accounting
  • Excellent French skills
  • Autonomy, intellectual curiosity and rigor in execution
  • Ability to manage stress and work conscientiously

An asset if you own

  • Good knowledge of OPAL software
  • Good knowledge of German

To succeed in this function, your personal skills are as follows:

  • Stress tolerance
  • Organized
  • Stable
  • Responsible
  • Intellectually curious
