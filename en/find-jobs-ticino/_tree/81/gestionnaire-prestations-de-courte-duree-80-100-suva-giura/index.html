What You Do

  • You are a consultant and expert in the area of entitlement to the daily allowance under the Federal Accident Insurance Act (FAA).
  • As such, you fix or correct the amount of the daily allowances in accordance with the provisions of the LAA and pay them to businesses and insured persons.
  • You calculate the claims for reimbursement as well as the overcompensation in case of benefit competitions between different social insurances.
  • You are the reference person to answer questions about the payment of daily allowances. What you bring
  • French or German mother tongue, you have a good level in the other language.
  • You have completed commercial or equivalent training, and have professional insurance experience.
  • Ideally, you have knowledge of cash benefits in the area of social insurance.
  • You're meticulous, comfortable with the numbers, friendly, and appreciative of contact with clients.
