Full description

100%, workplace: Le Landeron and/or La Neuveville

Shape your future with us!

Are you an insurance professional? Start your career in one of the agencies of AXA, the leader in Insurance. We are the trusted partner of our clients, in all life situations. You too ?

Do you live in the Entre-deux-Lacs region and have you already been able to put your sales, negotiation and networking skills into practice? Are you a customer and goal oriented person? Are you serious about your work?

You have the opportunity with us to develop your skills and demonstrate your abilities! Our Main agency in Landeron would like to strengthen its team and, if you recognize yourself in this profile, we would be delighted to receive your file!

What awaits you

  • Directed and personalized advice on insurance solutions to customers you meet at home, at the agency or by telephone
  • Monitoring and professional development of your own client portfolio
  • Acquisition of new customers to guarantee the growth of the customer portfolio in your region
  • Placement of other AXA products (finance and protection, health services)
  • Carrying out administrative tasks related to your position, with the support of the entire AXA structure and modern monitoring tools

What you bring

  • With the benefit of a professional diploma and initial experience in consulting or sales, you have a keen interest in training in the field of insurance
  • You can demonstrate very good knowledge of the local economic fabric, a vast network in this region because you live there
  • Your personality is service-oriented and you have ease in contact with others, you seek solutions for your customers and you know how to adapt to any situation
  • You are fluent in French both orally and in writing, any other language is an advantage
  • Your working method is autonomous and goal-oriented, remuneration based on your own performance is an additional source of motivation
  • You are comfortable with common computer systems as well as learning new digital tools

What we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you the opportunity to flourish and fully exploit your potential. As a leading Swiss insurance company, we offer ambitious people interesting career opportunities with great freedom of action.

  • Up to 30% off AXA products
  • Five weeks of vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-fare subscription/contribution to general subscription costs
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Freedom of action and independent work
  • Innovative structures, processes and tools
  • Robust and professional training and development opportunities
