Full description

Who are you?

We are looking for a proactive, well organized and able to work in a frantic environment. A person passionate about the accuracy of stocks and the guarantee of having products available to our customers in the right place at the right time and who understand how essential this is for a positive shopping experience for our customers.

  • You have experience in logistics and/or retail processes.
  • You know how to use forklifts safely and efficiently and have a forklift license.
  • You like to work in logistics in a hectic and constantly evolving retail environment.
  • You're a self-disciplined, flexible person and you like to work on a team.
  • You are open minded, communicative and have a friendly way to help promote positive collaboration with your colleagues.
  • You are available to work early in the morning. The working time takes place mainly from 05:00 to 09:00, Monday to Saturday, for five days a week.

Your responsibilities

  • Working according to the established routines to ensure accurate inventory and availability of products for customers
  • Working in team with your colleagues to ensure that the store is well supplied, in perfect condition and ready for our customers.
  • Work carefully to ensure the safety and protection of customers and colleagues in your area of responsibility.
  • Share your knowledge and skills to support colleagues and colleagues in the department and other areas of the store.
  • Be available to support other areas of the store when required.
