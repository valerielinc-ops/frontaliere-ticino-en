Full description

Are you ready for a FaGe apprenticeship? Get your EFZ through an apprenticeship in nursing. With us, you will start a varied and meaningful apprenticeship with a future, because as a health specialist, you can expect a supportive working environment from day one, in which you can help people at the same time and are accompanied and supported by our experienced vocational trainers.

For the Tertianum Viktoria house in the city of Bern, we are looking for you as of August 1, 2027 for the

Apprenticeship as a health specialist EFZ

What you'll learn with us

  • Care, care and accompaniment of guests in everyday and demanding situations
  • Support with personal hygiene, getting up, dressing and undressing, eating and drinking
  • Medical activities such as blood pressure, pulse and temperature measurement, blood sampling, dressing changes and much more
  • Everyday accompaniment of the guests with games, design, singing, etc.
  • Housekeeping, administrative and logistical tasks
  • Compliance with and implementation of hygiene and safety regulations
  • Electronic care documentation

What you bring to the table

  • Completed school education (secondary school A or B) with good academic performance
  • Great willingness to learn and sense of responsibility
  • Empathy and patience as well as interest in dealing with our guests
  • Helpful, outgoing and team-oriented, with a strong sense of care and conscientiousness
  • Willingness to work irregular hours
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoyment of a variety of challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of written and spoken German

What benefits do we offer you? You are just as important to us as your training is to you! That's why we offer you the following advantages:

  • On your birthday you get a day off
  • Discounted mobile plans with Sunrise
  • Free SBB half-fare subscription
  • Each lunch for CHF 5
  • Discounts and offers for fitness, leisure and your wellbeing
  • Up to 10% discount on your supplementary health insurance
  • Regular team events
  • And much more...

You start right away with an exciting introductory day and halfway through your apprenticeship, we invite you to our Summer Camp for a whole week. Of course, your apprenticeship must be duly celebrated, so we organize an unforgettable graduation ceremony for all graduates. And maybe this is just the beginning of your career with us! We offer numerous opportunities to work within our company directly after completing your apprenticeship.

For more benefits and insights, visit our website.

Well, interested? As soon as we have your complete application documents, they will be reviewed by our team. If your dossier convinces us, we look forward to getting to know you personally during an initial interview. Afterwards, you can immerse yourself in the Tertianum world directly on one or more taster days and get an idea of everyday life with us.

What do we need for your complete application?

  • CV
  • School reports
  • Results of the interlocking test and, if available, the Multicheck

Could this teaching be something for you? Then click on "Apply now" and create your personal account to send us your complete application.

We look forward to receiving your application!

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
