Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to discover the highlights of 2025 by clicking here . The post is attached to the Department of Operations. The mission of this department is to ensure the stewardship and logistics necessary for the proper functioning of all HUGs, in the restoration, cleaning and disinfection of premises and hygiene of linen. It also manages the sterilization service, the biomedical service and equipment as well as Cressy-Santé. More than 1,350 professionals carry out their tasks on a daily basis with a constant concern for requirements, with a sense of service and environmental responsibility. The Biomedical & Equipment Service, composed of 52 staff, is responsible for the management of the medical equipment fleet of the University Hospitals of Geneva (HUG). Its main functions are technological and regulatory oversight, investment planning, prescription, acquisition and maintenance of medical equipment. In addition, this service implements and follows the requirements of materialovigilance (the head of service is the materialovigilance correspondent of the HUGs with SwissMedic).

Description of post

Within the Biomedical Maintenance Sector, you ensure the installation and preventive and corrective maintenance of all the equipment entrusted. You will be assigned to one of the groups with medical devices from different families: Group A (radiology equipment and laboratory),

Group B (Operating unit equipment),

Group C (anesthesia and resuscitation equipment),

Group D (maintenance management, contract and budget monitoring, GMAO, administration and logistics) You advise on the renewal, purchase, use and maintenance of equipment and facilities under the responsibility of the sector. You define the organisation of the production and monitoring of maintenance for any new medical device, in coordination with all the employees of the group. You contribute, develop and promote the organizations and improvements identified during the group's transversal working sessions. You collaborate on pre-project equipment purchases: you take stock of maintenance on existing equipment, taking into account the level of user satisfaction, normative aspects and implementation constraints. You are involved in the development of an installation schedule, its monitoring and the commissioning of equipment during care services projects (development or extension, park renewal, etc.). You set up and monitor validated session processes at the level of the attached group: indicators, working organization of the biomedical maintenance group and/or sector. You determine the material or resource requirements required to perform the tasks assigned to the group. You contribute, develop and follow preventive maintenance load plans for medical devices entrusted to the attached group. You participate in market research: back to existing equipment, accessories and consumables, taking into account the needs of users and interfaces with the premises, and surrounding services. You prepare and transmit the information requested and necessary for the technical park studies: time, costs, training and difficulties encountered on the maintenance performed. You inform and inform users and staff of any specific requests for medical devices assigned to the group. You ensure urgent interventions on request from the Control Centre during the picket service for long weekends (more than 2 consecutive days).

Qualifications

You are ideally qualified as an ES technician and technician in biomedical electronics or as an equivalent diploma, supplemented by training in project management. Your experience of at least 5 years in the maintenance and troubleshooting of complex electronic equipment, ideally in a hospital environment, makes you a recognized expert. You master electronics and have good knowledge of biomedical and computer science (Microsoft Office). Knowledge of a GMAO tool is an additional asset. Your knowledge of biomedical equipment is an asset. You have perfect oral and written knowledge of French as well as good knowledge of English. Basic knowledge in German is desired. You have a strong sense of customer service, you are a person open to change and with a strong team spirit. Thanks to your calm, quiet and discreet personality and your respectful attitude, you can easily establish a bond of trust with your interlocutors. You cooperate and collaborate to help colleagues while being sensitive to the patient's presence and hospital constraints.

Further information

Regular hours. Off-hourly availability including night, weekend and public holidays in case of picket service for long weekends (more than 2 consecutive days).
