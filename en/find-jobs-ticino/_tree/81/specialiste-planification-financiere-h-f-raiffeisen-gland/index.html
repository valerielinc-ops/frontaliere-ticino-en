09.11.2026 Gland

Be part of our team!

Want a new professional challenge? What if you accompanied a team of dynamic advisors at a human-sized bank? Based in Gimel, Rolle and Gland, in the heart of the Coast, we are looking for additional talent. Don't hesitate! We're waiting for you!

What are you waiting for?

  • Establishment of financial planning and asset analysis as well as support for counselling in provision of pensions, taxation and insurance for natural persons and entrepreneurs.
  • Support to collaborators in the organization and animation of customer events, as well as the conduct of training with our staff members in-house.
  • Support our teams in identifying needs and implementing foresight solutions, during customer appointments.
  • Internal Financial Planning Reference Interlocutor.

What are you bringing?

  • You are entitled to a federal certificate of financial planner or equivalent training.
  • You have several years of experience in financial planning, foresight and taxation. Ideally in banking.
  • You have a strong customer orientation and a good spirit of initiative.
  • You demonstrate good sales and negotiation skills.

What distinguishes us At the Raiffeisen Bank of Gimel, we cultivate collaboration, tolerance, team spirit, trust and fairness. Why Raiffeisen?

We offer flexible work models.

The balance between personal and professional life of our employees is important to us. This is why we create the conditions for flexible organisation of working time, with offers of mobile work, teleworking and part-time work.

We work in a friendly environment

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.

We offer attractive social conditions and benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages over banking products, various discounts from third parties, as well as above-average financial stability and old age insurance.
