Full description

Are you an open and ambitious person? Are you interested in working in a modern context in the heart of the real estate industry? With an exemplary customer orientation, from Livit you will take care of the needs of owners and landlords, contributing significantly to establish a lasting relationship with customers based on trust and promoting a positive image of the company. Do you have the right motivation to face the challenges of today and tomorrow and to make a difference in the real estate world? Then you're in the right place at Livit's. Your personal contribution is valuable to us. Aufgaben:

  • Active object management and minimisation of waste (residential, commercial and secondary)
  • Direct assistance to tenants and owners; coordination of craftsmen and subcontractors
  • Ensure proper maintenance of property according to the management mandate
  • Periodic inspections of the stability and drafting of the relative status reports
  • Control and issue of maintenance orders and verification of the correct execution of the works
  • Management of contractual changes, adjustments of the legions and application of the contracts indexed
  • Monitoring rental policy and consulting owners for legions in line with the market
  • Representation of owners to the competent authorities (discounts, evictions, etc.)
  • Control of heating and operating costs and additional charges
  • Supervision of employees operating on behalf of the owner (custody, etc.) Anforderungen:
  • Business apprentice with AFC or equivalent training
  • Swiss Federal Real Estate Manager Certificate (SVIT) is a preferred title (evtl. in progress)
  • Multiannual professional experience in the role of real estate manager; knowledge of building technology and plants is an advantage
  • Solid knowledge of lease law (CO, CCS and LEF) and basic accounting concepts
  • Expic professional competence, customer orientation based on collaboration and strong orientation to results
  • Good negotiating skills, safe behavior and resilience
  • Entrepreneurship and spirit of initiative
  • Italian native language, excellent German; knowledge of other national languages is a plus
  • Great mastery of the MS Office package (Word, Excel, Outlook) and opening to systems and processes digitized Wir bieten:
  • Internal offer of specialist courses and management
  • Financial and/or time support for external and specialized training courses access to e-learning platforms for online language courses
  • Individual career planning and growth prospects
  • Home- / Mobile Office
  • Bonus related to business results and personal performance
  • For 5 weeks of holidays and the possibility of purchase of a maximum of 10 days of holidays additional (Ferienflex)
  • Benefits higher than average: Livit takes 100% of the prizes insurance for daily allowances in case of sickness and insurance against sickness Accident
  • 14-day paternity leave We love the properties and we strive enthusiastically to preserve the value of the properties of our customers. If you work at Livit, you're part of our family. We appreciate what you are and what you do. In fact, we care a lot not only for your skills and experience, but also for you as a person. In our working environment you feel comfortable and have the opportunity to realize yourself. This is due, on the one hand, to our many professional profiles and the possibilities of growth within our company and, on the other, also to the unmistakable corporate culture, which has no equal in the real estate world.
