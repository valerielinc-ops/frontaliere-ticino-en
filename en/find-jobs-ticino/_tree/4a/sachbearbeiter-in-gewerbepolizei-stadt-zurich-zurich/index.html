Full description

The special department is responsible for the security police special care of the city of Zurich. The Commercial Police Department, within the Department of Enforcement, of the Administrative Police Commissariat, specializes in police, regulatory and competition law and complements the Department of Economic Police.

  • Processing of investigation proceedings in the case of counter-trials by private security service providers, in the case of counter-trials against the Prostitution Ordinance, in the case of criminal acts against the Federal Act on Money Games, the Trademark Protection Act and the counterfeiting of goods
  • Enforcement of numerous secondary legislations
  • Early, late, night and weekend services and week-by-week cooking services
  • Performing assignments at major events and actions
  • Close cooperation with the Economic Police Department and other internal and external agencies
  • Police officer with federal professional card, completed training course Kripo, several years of criminal police experience of advantage
  • Team-capable, socially competent, initiative and independent personality with high personal responsibility, flair for networked thinking, high resilience and joy of working in a lively environment
  • Enforcement in contact with different population groups
  • Interested in interdisciplinary work with close contact with other authorities
  • Ability in oral and written communications, good knowledge of German and IT

We offer you...

  • Within the motivated and supportive team, you can expect a demanding, challenging and versatile job.
  • They are carefully and comprehensively introduced to the task area, can look forward to a modern workplace near the station Stadelhofen
  • Attractive employment conditions such as targeted education and training, purchase of additional holidays, etc.
  • Assumption of 60% of the PK savings contributions, mobility contribution of up to CHF 600 per year and monthly up to CHF 100 food compensation
  • Cheap mobile subscriptions (private use included) as well as other interesting Fringe Benefits

The city police is a service department of the security department of the city of Zurich and, with around 2250 employees, the third largest police corps in Switzerland.
