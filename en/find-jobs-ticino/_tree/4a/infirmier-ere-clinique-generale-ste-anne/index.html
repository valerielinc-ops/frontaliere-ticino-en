Full description

Nurse CDI

  • Being an autonomous reflexive health practitioner
  • Responsibility for providing the patient with quality care on the basis of medical prescription or the criteria of his or her independent role
  • Be eligible for a HES diploma or equivalent training recognised by the Red Cross
  • Experience in surgery, an asset
  • You speak French or German, with good knowledge of the partner language
  • Ability to integrate into a group and team spirit
  • High professional awareness, motivation, flexibility and dynamism
  • Ability to manage stress
  • Self-reliance and leadership
  • A variety of work (several medical disciplines), motivating a dynamic and committed team
  • A pleasant work environment
  • Interesting pay and social conditions

Founded in 1932, the Clinique Générale has established a mission to provide excellence, accessible to all, in a human-sized facility. The Clinique Générale and its staff welcome patients in a modern and pleasant environment in the heart of the city of Fribourg. The Clinique Générale offers high-quality medical care and personalized follow-up in the following areas: orthopaedics, musculoskeletal trauma, hand surgery, neurosurgery, surgery, gynaecology as well as oto-rhino-laryngology (ORL), oncology and pain treatment.
