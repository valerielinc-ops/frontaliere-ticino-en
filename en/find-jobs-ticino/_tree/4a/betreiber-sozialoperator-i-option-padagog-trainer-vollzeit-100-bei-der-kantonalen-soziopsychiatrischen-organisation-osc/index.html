Full description

Class and annual salary (including 13th monthly): 6 68'456.-- 107'475.-- Tasks:

  • individually or in small groups users for whom social and educational intervention is necessary, within the framework of a therapeutic plan agreed with the team that concerns the minor or his family
  • follows SMP users for which educational or therapeutic inclusion is necessary in institutions for minors
  • cooperates with other bodies or institutions in taking charge of psychosocial situations affecting users reported at SMP
  • conducts consultations with entities or institutions dealing with minors and teachers and other workers working in contact with minors
  • participates in moments of sharing the clinical and formative activity of the team and entertains permanent training Requirements:
  • SUP (option: educator/trice) or equivalent qualification
  • if the SEFRI (State Secretary for Education, Research and Innovation) has a foreign qualification
  • availability and motivation for multidisciplinary work in children's psychiatry and adolescence teams
  • attitude and motivation to work with children and teenagers
  • experience in the field of children in the psychological and psychiatric field
  • knowledge of the territory, social services and the social network of the Canton Ticino
  • excellent knowledge (conversation and editing) of the Italian language and knowledge (conversation and editing) of another official language Special remarks:
