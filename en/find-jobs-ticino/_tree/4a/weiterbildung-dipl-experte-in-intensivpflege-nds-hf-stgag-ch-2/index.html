We offer a two-year part-time job

Further development Dipl. Expert in intensive care NDS HF

80 - 100%

During this time, you will receive theoretical lessons at the Cantonal Hospital St. Gallen and will be practically trained by pedagogical specialists at the workplace. Spital Thurgau assumes the training costs and time without a commitment period or repayment obligation.

You take over

  • Professional and high-quality care and care of intensive care patients
  • Assistance and accompaniment of relatives
  • Independent planning, organisation and execution of delegated tasks
  • Responsibility for physical and mental well-being and patient safety
  • Appreciative cooperation in the interprofessional treatment team
  • Management of performance and patient documentation

You bring with you

  • Degree as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF/FH or a foreign diploma in nursing recognised by the SRC, or an equivalent professional qualification
  • Professional experience of 24 months, in the acute care sector in a hospital
  • Motivation and willingness to offer patients a high quality of care
  • Accustomed to working independently and a personality with high professional and social competence who thinks and acts in an interdisciplinary and self-initiative manner
  • Open to the new
  • Teamwork, flexible and happy to contribute ideas

The Cantonal Hospital Frauenfeld introduces itself

The cantonal hospitals Frauenfeld & Münsterlingen are part of Spital Thurgau AG, a subsidiary of the thurmed Group. Our acute care hospitals serve around 32,000 inpatients every year and offer a comprehensive range of services in a high-quality infrastructure. Spital Thurgau is one of the largest and most successful hospital groups in Switzerland and has around 4,100 employees at various company locations. We offer attractive working environments, interdisciplinary cooperation and targeted development opportunities. Become part of our success story too!
