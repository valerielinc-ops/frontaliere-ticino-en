ASAP: Senior Accountant Accountant SAP S/4HANA (m/f/d)

For an internationally active company, we are looking for an experienced accountant with in-depth SAP\-S/4HANA\ knowledge at short notice. In this operational area, you independently take over the day-to-day business in the area of Accounts Receivable and ensure that the debtor processes run smoothly.

Our client is an established, internationally oriented industrial company with complex financial processes and high quality standards. Due to a short-term resource requirement, an experienced specialist is sought for an initial assignment of 3 months, with an option of extension and a possible later permanent employment.

Job description

  • Accounting and reconciliation of customer payments and open positions
  • Preparation, accounting and dispatch of invoices and credits
  • Maintenance and monitoring of accounts receivable
  • Implementation of the dunning and debt collection system
  • Reconciliation of bank, cash pool and intercompany accounts
  • Preparation of Intrastat and other international reports
  • Support for VAT declarations in several countries
  • Ensuring efficient and error-free day-to-day business
  • Cooperation with internal and international stakeholders

Profile

  • Basic commercial training with further training in accounting
  • At least 3 to 5 years of experience in accounts receivable
  • Sound practice in billing, payments, dunning and voting
  • Demonstrable experience with SAP S/4HANA (Must\-have)
  • Experience in an international environment is beneficial
  • Knowledge of VAT, Intrastat or international reports is desirable
  • Very good knowledge of German
  • Independent, exact and structured working method
  • High reliability and rapid comprehension
  • Available immediately or at short notice

The offer

  • Immediate entry into a responsible specialist function
  • Temporary deployment with renewal option
  • International working environment
  • High personal responsibility and short decision paths
  • Attractive opportunity for experienced AR\ specialists
  • Experienced team and structured processes
