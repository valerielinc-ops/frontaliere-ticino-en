Design, build, and maintain production-grade workflows on our self-hosted n8n Enterprise platform (AWS EKS), supporting business units across the company and helping grow our Citizen Developer / self-service automation program. Design and build custom AI solutions. Develop applications and tools that use AI to support key business processes. These solutions will be tailored to specific needs across different departments.
