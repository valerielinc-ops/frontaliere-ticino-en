Full description

Addetto/a ai trasporti - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite hospital of Ticino is present with its institutes on the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 employees and their attention to the human relationship, the EOC ensures quality health care by taking care of over 41, 000 patients who are deceased a year and providing over 600,000 outpatients. To complete our team of physiotherapy and ergotherapy service of the Rehabilitation Clinic (CREOC) Faido headquarters, we look for a(a) Transportation Attachment (40%) Profile: The co-worker/hour operates within an interdisciplinary rehabilitative context and guarantees the safe and efficient transport of patients between different departments, diagnostic services and clinical care areas. It carries out activities in compliance with internal procedures, ensuring punctuality, precision and attention to the assisted person. It is able to independently manage variable workloads, maintaining a high collaboration with physiotherapy/ergotherapy, nursing, medical, administrative and other services involved. It demonstrates good relational skills, sense of responsibility and a respectful and discreet approach to patients, family and colleagues. Flexible, reliable and service-oriented person.

  • Requirements: Excellent organizational capacity and priority management Excellent relational and communicative skills Availability to work on flexible shifts Great knowledge of the Italian language Knowledge of the main IT tools (electronics, internal systems) attitudes to team work and coordination with multiple services Comments: In the context of internal applications, profiles from services where special flexibility is not required in shiftwork in order to ensure a balanced organization of resources. Date of entry: 1 October 2026 or date to be agreed Applications, complete with curriculum vitae, copies of certificates of study and work, shall be formalizing online, by 28 June 2026, at http://www.eoc.ch/ -> Employment opportunities -> Job offers. As a result, applications sent by mail or e-mail will not be considered, as well as applications that do not meet the requirements. For further information, Ms. Michela Battelli, Head of Physiotherapy and Ergotherapy Service at the EOC Rehabilitation Clinic (CREOC), Faido's seat is available at +41 91 811 21 36.
