It combines flexible, location-independent distance learning part-time with an active, interdisciplinary research culture at Campus Brig. In five faculties – Law, Psychology, Economics, History and Mathematics & Computer Science – we have been shaping modern teaching, learning and research for more than 30 years and count over 2,700 students and around 550 employees. To strengthen our service Faculty Services, we seek a dynamic and dedicated personality as: Specialized Employee / Specialized Employee Faculty Services (50%) Location: Brig, with the possibility to make a day a week home office Start of work: 01.02.2027 The Faculty Services are one of the academic services of FernUni Switzerland. They support the research centre and faculties in achieving excellence in distance learning and research. They work closely with the management of the research centre LIFT (Learning and Instruction with Future Technologies) and the deanates of the faculties and are active at the interface between the research centre, the faculties and the academic and central services of the FernUni Switzerland. The specialist staff of the FACS coordinate the cross-departmental activities of the department and provide organizational and administrative support to the faculties and the research centre. Their tasks

  • Act as an interface between the LIFT management, the Deans, the Faculty Services and the other departments of academic and central administration.
  • Coordinate the organisation of face-to-face and online events and ensure their smooth running.
  • Coordinate the event promotion through the various communication channels.
  • Prepare the minutes of the LIFT meetings and the faculty meetings and ensure the implementation of the resolutions.
  • Assist in the preparation and management of the LIFT Centre’s budget.
  • Assist in the preparation of reports for internal and external purposes.
  • Update the data, documents and information on the various platforms and databases.
  • Prepare, organise and accompany the elections to the organs of the FernUni Switzerland as well as the appointment of Commissioners and supervise the communication channels.
  • Working on cross-faculty projects of Faculty Services and the institution.
  • Working closely with the other Specialised Employees: Faculty Services and representing each other.

Your profile

  • Graduate degree at bachelor level or equivalent education.
  • Proven experience in an interface function, preferably in higher education.
  • Experience in organizing and promoting events.
  • Strong service orientation, flexibility and the ability to work in a multilingual and interdisciplinary environment.
  • Strong social skills and teamwork.
  • Methodical, reliable, flexible and independent personality with high service orientation and self-initiative.
  • First language German or French, with the opportunity to work very well in the other language.
  • English at a fully professional level.

We offer

  • Research-strong academic environment: You work in a dynamic university context with proven scientific expertise and interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • Attractive employment conditions: You benefit from contemporary working conditions, generous social benefits and a wide range of training opportunities.
  • Dedicated team: You become part of a qualified team that actively lives innovation, quality and collaboration.
  • Inspiring working environment: Our campus in Brig is located in close proximity to the old town, the SBB railway station and attractive natural and mountain regions. It is a place of academic exchange with a high quality of life.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
