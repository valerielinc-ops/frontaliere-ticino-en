Your tasks

  • In the daily clinical setting, you take over the individual, need and resource-oriented treatment and care of our patients.

  • For expert care within the care standards, you coordinate the nursing therapeutic treatment planning with internal and external support groups.
  • An open and relationship-oriented working environment offers you the opportunity to use your skills in complex situations and to actively shape the therapeutic environment.
  • In close exchange with relatives and referees, you contribute to a trusting cooperation and an appreciative cooperation. Your profile
  • With your completed training as a nurse HF or FH and a required SRK recognition or a positive SRK PreCheck with a foreign diploma, you bring the necessary professional foundation.
  • Ideally, you have already gained experience in the psychiatric field and enjoy working with mentally ill people as well as working in a multi-professional team.
  • Your social skills, flexibility and resilience characterize you as well as an independent and responsible working method.
  • High commitment and creativity are among your strengths and are complemented by very good knowledge of German at at least C1 level.
  • You also bring good IT knowledge and can use it specifically to support you in administrative work safely and efficiently. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. We are on the road digitally and are committed to sustainability, so we look forward to your online application via our homepage. As soon as your application has been received by us online, you will receive a confirmation of receipt by e-mail. We are happy to get to know you based on your dossier and check whether your qualifications, skills and you as a person fit the advertised position and the psychiatry St.Gallen. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also get the opportunity to get to know your future team. You will receive a telephone confirmation from us if we decide for you after the second interview and ask you if you are still convinced of us. If we agree, we will draw up your contract. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. So you can get a differentiated picture of the company and already prepare for the job. On the first day you can expect an onboarding in your new team and with other colleagues. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also get your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
