Full description

Step into a pivotal leadership role where you shape how Financial Crime Compliance (FCC) operates at its core. As Head of Controls & Quality Assurance FCC, you will drive robust control oversight, strengthen compliance frameworks, and lead transformation initiatives that safeguard the organization. Acting as a trusted partner to senior stakeholders, auditors, and regulators, you’ll sit at the heart of decision-making—ensuring risks are effectively managed while championing a culture of accountability, continuous improvement, and operational excellence. What would your role be?

  • Lead and develop the Controls & QA team, setting clear objectives and fostering a strong control culture.
  • Oversee the FCC control framework, ensuring controls are effective, tested, and continuously improved.
  • Monitor control performance, identify risks/gaps, and drive remediation and resilience enhancements.
  • Manage internal/external audits, including planning, findings, and timely closure of actions.
  • Act as main contact for regulatory engagements, ensuring alignment with expectations and managing responses.
  • Establish MI frameworks with clear KPIs/KRIs and deliver actionable insights to senior management.
  • Oversee Quality Assurance to assess control effectiveness and ensure compliance with regulations.
  • Lead incident management processes, including escalation, root cause analysis, and preventive actions.
  • Drive training initiatives to strengthen risk, compliance, and control awareness across the organization.
  • Lead transformation and automation of FCC processes (e.g., AML, sanctions, transaction monitoring). What are we looking for?
  • Proven leadership experience within Financial Crime Compliance, Controls, QA, or Risk Management.
  • Strong expertise in FCC frameworks (e.g., AML, sanctions, transaction monitoring) and regulatory environments.
  • Solid understanding of control design, testing, and continuous improvement practices.
  • Experience managing internal/external audits and regulatory interactions.
  • Track record of building and leading high-performing teams and fostering a strong risk & control culture.
  • Strong analytical mindset with experience in MI reporting, KPIs/KRIs, and data-driven decision-making.
  • Experience in transformation, process optimization, and automation initiatives.
  • Excellent stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence at senior levels.
  • Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to manage complex risks and incidents.
  • Degree in Finance, Law, Business, or related field; relevant certifications (e.g., CAMS, ICA) are a plus.
  • Affinity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and a willingness to embrace AI-enabled tools and ways of working What do we offer?
  • Top Zurich location with collaboration spaces (including rooftop terraces) and free lunch.
  • Friendly, diverse team in an open-office space.
  • All the latest technology to help you excel and advance.
  • Agile environment in how we think, the way we work and our trust for each other.
  • Flat hierarchy with collaboration at all levels.
  • Campus feeling and a collaborative spirit. We are looking for talented people, not just a CV that ticks all the boxes.
