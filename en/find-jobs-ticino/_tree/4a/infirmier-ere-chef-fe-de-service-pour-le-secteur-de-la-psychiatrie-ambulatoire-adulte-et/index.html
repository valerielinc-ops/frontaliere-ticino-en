Full description

The canton's first employer, the Hôpital du Valais has 6,260 employees. Each year, 418,800 patients are cared for, and nearly 670 300 outpatient visits are provided.

The Valais Hospital is looking for the Valais Romand Hospital Centre.

90% Chief of Service Nurse (CSI)

for the adult outpatient psychiatry sector in central Valais and the child and adolescent psychiatry and psychotherapy service (SPEA).

This chefferie brings together the adult outpatient psychiatry of the central Valais (CCPP de Martigny, Sion and Sierre, psychiatric liaison unit) and the department of psychiatry and psychotherapy of children and adolescents (SPEA).

Mission

Ensure patient care according to the strategy of the Valais Hospital, the three axes of the Hospital Model (attention to the other and self, harmonious interactions, professional expertise) and the Professional Practice Model (MPP).

Participate in the service strategy with physician heads of department.

Ensure the proper functioning of services, quality and safety of care.

Pilot indicators and carry out service projects.

Supervises ICUS, recruits staff and supports employees.

Promote the development of nursing care through continuing training.

Contribute to Pôle projects.

Profile

Diploma of HES nurse or deemed equivalent recognized by the Red Cross,

Post-diploma training for the function of ICS,

DAS level management training required, or commitment to follow,

At least 3 years experience as an executive (ICS or ICUS) in psychiatry, ideally in outpatient or child psychiatry,

Leadership affirmed, meaning of decision-making and cooperation with medical and health care professionals,

Relationship qualities, ease in negotiation and communication,

Organizational sense, reliability, autonomy and mobility between sites,

Driver's licence and essential vehicle

Spirit of analysis and synthesis, mastery of indicators and computer tools.

Only offers responding to the profile will be processed.

Offer

A stimulating and diverse environment with the opportunity to leverage your knowledge and experience; attractive conditions of employment; opportunities for continuing training and further training. Find here all the advantages for our employees.
