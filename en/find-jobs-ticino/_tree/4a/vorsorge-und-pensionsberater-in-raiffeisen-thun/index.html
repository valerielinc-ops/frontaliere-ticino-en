Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Thunersee-Kiesental is a successful bank in the Thun region with a high level of customer confidence and a strong presence in the population. For our advisory bank we are looking for an entrepreneurial and professionally qualified personality as a pension planner: in (m/f/d). With your professional expertise, your holistic consulting approach and your sense of individual needs, you accompany people in crucial phases of life and create real added value. At the same time, you have the opportunity to actively develop our financial planning business and shape the future of consulting.

What awaits you?

  • You accompany customers in important financial life decisions and advise them holistically in the areas of retirement, pension, asset structuring and property and inheritance law.
  • You will develop tailor-made financial planning and sustainable solutions that are tailored to the individual goals, wishes and life situations of our customers.
  • You position yourself as a trusted contact for challenging financial issues and build long-term customer relationships.
  • You actively shape the further development of our financial planning business and contribute ideas, innovations and market impulses.

What do you bring?

  • Several years of experience in private customer consulting in the areas of investing, provisioning and financing as well as enjoyment of consulting demanding customers.
  • Experience in financial and pension planning is beneficial.
  • Degree as a financial planner with federal certificate.
  • In-depth knowledge in the areas of prevention, taxation and banking and insurance solutions.
  • Experience in dealing with Allfina/Logismata is an advantage.
  • A holistic understanding of financial planning and the ability to communicate complex relationships in an understandable, practical and customer-oriented manner.
  • A trustworthy, integrity and authentic personality with strong counselling and relationship skills.
  • Analytical thinking skills, conceptual strength and a structured and independent working method.
  • Enjoy the interdisciplinary cooperation and the development of sustainable solutions for the financial future of our customers.

This is what we offer you

  • A responsible and versatile activity with a high level of professional independence.
  • The opportunity to actively shape the future of financial planning and contribute your ideas.
  • A professional environment with short decision-making paths and a strong team.
  • Exciting customer relationships and demanding consulting situations with direct added value for our customers.
  • Modern and attractive employment conditions.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
