Full description

In brief

As an experienced leader, you will play a key role in the Swiss Armed Forces’ cyber defence. You will lead a highly specialised team, be responsible for handling complex cyber incidents and actively shape the further development of the CSIRT and cyber defence.

  • Start date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: permanent
  • Reference no.: JRQ$540-20833
  • Place of work: Papiermühlestrasse 20, 3003 Bern

You can make a difference

  • Lead a competent team of cyber security experts in organisational, personnel and professional matters
  • Ensure the operational deployment planning of analysts for the permanent service as well as for Armed Forces deployments
  • Ensure the planning and further development of the resources in use, as well as the continuous realignment of employees to current threats
  • Ensure full representation of the Head of the Cyber Fusion Center during their absence
  • Be responsible for, plan and coordinate threat hunting on the ICT infrastructure of the VBS in order to identify anomalies or undetected threats in the network
  • Conduct research on prototypes with the Cyber Defence Campus of Armasuisse and the Cyber and Electromagnetic Actions Division (CEA) in order to develop new detection methods and identify new directions in research

What sets you apart

  • University degree in computer science, information security or engineering
  • Additional training in cybersecurity (CAS, MAS) is an advantage
  • Several years of experience leading interdisciplinary teams in an agile environment, as well as sound experience in project and process management and agile methods
  • Officer training and several years of military leadership experience are an advantage, but not a requirement
  • Good active knowledge of at least one other official language and passive knowledge of a third official language

What we offer

  • Working for Switzerland We are committed to the Swiss success model and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Diversity in practice Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring different perspectives to the table.
  • Health at work We support and advise our employees on physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for expertise in the fields of cyber and digitalisation. We combine our enthusiasm for our mission with expertise, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Armed Forces and our partners in the Swiss security network.
