Full description

The Content Production Intern will provide hands-on operational support to the Photo Studio & content team, contributing to the daily organization and execution of e-commerce, lookbook and campaign shootings. The role will support the team throughout the production workflow, from shooting planning and sample preparation to freelance coordination, administrative follow-up and final asset delivery, ensuring that activities are accurately organized and completed according to seasonal priorities and deadlines.

CORE JOB RESPONSIBILITIES

Photo Studio Planning & Shooting Operations

Sample Management & Production Tracking

Freelance & Administrative Coordination

Post-production & Asset Delivery

Advertising Campaign & Location Shooting Support
