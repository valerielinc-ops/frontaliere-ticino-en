Full description

Pensum: 100%

Do you want to contribute to a sustainable energy mix and see nuclear energy as a method of achieving net zero? Together with Axpo, drive forward a climate-friendly future and support us in Switzerland’s security of supply. ⚡

The trainee program allows you to immerse yourself in three different departments and build up your know-how. Take responsibility for new projects every six months and bring innovative solutions.

The optimal start for your professional future! The traineeship starts on 1 April 2027 and is limited to 18 months with the option of continued employment.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • With your technical know-how, you contribute to secure electricity production and supply at Axpo. ⚡
  • You work on operational and/or strategic tasks
  • During the traineeship, you actively contribute to the daily business and take on responsibility from day one – the projects range from engineering, re-engineering of software or hardware and developing apps, as well as analysis and digitization of our processes
  • Work on innovation projects and make our processes more digital and agile
  • Start your first assignment in the [Electrical Engineering Department of the Beznau Nuclear Power Plant](https://www.axpo.com/ch/en/energy/production-and-distribution/nuclear energy/beznau/working-at-kkb.html?trk=public post comment-text). The team is responsible for operational IT, analogue and digital control technology, measurement and control technology, switchgear low to high voltage, and much more. 🔌
  • You can choose the second and third assignment within Axpo Group (including our subsidiary CKW), for example in other engineering teams, or at the Leibstadt nuclear power plant, or many more.

Profile & Skills

  • Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, IT or Physics (degree within the last two years) 🎓
  • Curiosity and interest in the changing energy industry
  • Good programming skills
  • First professional experience as an intern:in or working student:in
  • Good analytical and networked thinking and you are interested in agile methods
  • High willingness to take responsibility, proactivity and team players
  • Negotiable in German and English

About the team

You will be part of the 21st generation of trainees. Meet regularly for events, be it for lunch or visits to power plants. You work in an international, open and collaborative working atmosphere and make a valuable contribution to the future.

The innovative environment encourages us to contribute our own ideas, to further expand our knowledge of the energy industry and to achieve great things together. Happiness With us you work in Beznau, Baden and/or Leibstadt (KKL) or at our subsidiary CKW in Lucerne. More details about the traineeship can be found [here](https://www.axpo.com/ch/en/men-and-career/student-and-university graduates.html).
