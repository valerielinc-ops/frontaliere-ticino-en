Full description

The UniBE is typically Bern. With us, you can expect an appreciative culture and a healthy working environment.

Requirements

  • Very good doctorate in sociology, educational science, demography or a related discipline
  • Research focus in one or more of the following areas: education and social inequality, family complexity, family processes or intergenerational transmission of social inequality
  • Interest in bio-social perspectives on educational and inequality processes
  • Very good knowledge of quantitative methods of empirical social research, e.g. in the analysis of longitudinal or register data
  • First publications in international journals or correspondingly advanced manuscripts
  • Teamwork, initiative and enjoyment of scientific cooperation
  • Very good knowledge of English
  • Knowledge of German is desirable

We offer

  • Integration into an internationally networked research environment
  • Very good development opportunities in research and teaching
  • Support for further qualification, e.g. habilitation or own third-party funded projects
  • Conditions of employment in accordance with the guidelines of the University of Bern

International reputation Application and contact With us, you create value. For themselves and society.

Their tasks

The employment is initially limited to three years and can be extended by another three years after a positive evaluation.

  • Independent research on educational processes on the course of life and on the intergenerational transmission of social inequality
  • Collaboration in ongoing research projects of the professorship, in particular on educational inequality, family processes and bio-social approaches
  • Publication of research results in international journals
  • Participation in the acquisition of third-party funds
  • Participation in the supervision of students and in organizational tasks of the professorship
  • Teaching of 2–4 semester hours (SWS) in the fields of educational sociology and empirical social research

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Bern?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Bern's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Bern's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
