Welcome to Medbase Schaffhausen

Our modern, brightly furnished medical center with its own laboratory is located directly at the Schaffhausen station, near the Rhine and the picturesque old town. The team of around twelve members consists of specialists in general internal medicine as well as MPAs and covers the entire spectrum of general care medicine. The active co-design for the benefit of patients and a good work-life balance enjoy a high priority with us.

As a specialist, you offer comprehensive care and ensure a high quality of patient care. At Medbase, you are part of an interdisciplinary team that supports you in your professional development and sets high standards.

  • Self-responsible care and basic care of family doctor
  • Ensuring the best possible medical care
  • Team with collegial representation
  • Support in the further development of practical offers
  • Internal and external training to improve quality

What you bring

  • Completed studies and specialist training for general internal medicine
  • Commitment to holistic patient care
  • Solution-oriented and efficient operation
  • familiarity with electronic medical stories
  • Strength of communication and empathy
  • Good German knowledge (min. B2)

The Medbase Group is one of the leading companies in outpatient healthcare. This means services from health promotion to medical care and acute treatment to rehabilitation.
