At STRABAG, around 89,000 people at more than 2,400 locations worldwide are building progress. Construction Supervisor: Road and Civil Engineering Your Contribution with Us

  • Basic education in construction with ongoing or completed further training to become a certified construction supervisor and/or engineer or equivalent
  • Experience in road and civil engineering is an advantage
  • Cooperative manner and enjoyment of teamwork
  • Commitment, willingness to perform and can-do attitude
  • Winning personality with strong customer orientation
  • Proficient in handling common IT systems
  • Possession of a driving licence completes your profile

What Counts for Us

  • Independent management of construction projects in the region
  • Support of project or construction management in the handling and execution of construction projects, as well as in acquisition and tender preparation
  • Participation in calculation and tender processing
  • Support regarding site organisation, quantity surveying and billing as well as maintenance of work calculation
  • Supervision of construction progress and site result control
  • Execution and co-responsibility for work preparation and site organisation
  • Accurate and timely quantity surveying and billing
  • Involvement in cost, schedule and quality issues
  • Participation and shaping of innovation topics in construction (e.g. BIM)
