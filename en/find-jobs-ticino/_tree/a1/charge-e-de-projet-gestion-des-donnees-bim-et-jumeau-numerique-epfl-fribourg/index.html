Full description

Mission

Within the EPFL-FR Building2050 unit, the recruited person contributes to the structuring and valorisation of the digital data resulting from the construction of the Smart Living Lab (SLL) and to their availability using appropriate digital tools.

The function involves contributing to the management, validation and maintenance of digital data related to the BIM (Building Information Modeling) and site monitoring, as well as providing digital and administrative support for the DARE (Digital Assets for Research Experiments) interface, which centralizes access to building data, services and resources for researchers. It also includes the creation of dashboards and audio-visual media for communication and valorisation of unit activities.

Main tasks and responsibilities

Digital Data Management, BIM Models and Digital Tools

  • Implementation and monitoring of the process of validation of the digital data transmitted by the total company as part of the BIM submission and the site charter, including verification of the BIM models, their parameters, and technical documentation relating to materials and the site.
  • Management of digital models used for building research and monitoring.
  • Support on the automation of repetitive tasks
  • Support for monthly updating of the SUIVI project data (digital monitoring of the project site).
  • Contribution to the development of the digital twin by linking building monitoring data with available digital models and resources (BIM, virtual tower, etc.).
  • Maintenance of unit and building web interfaces.
  • Identification and evaluation of software, tools and digital solutions to improve unit functionality and services for researchers.

Development, maintenance and improvement of the DARE interface

  • Development and maintenance of the DARE (Digital Assets for Research Experiments) interface home page, which centralizes access to digital services, tools and resources developed by PL-MTI's IT-OT (Information & Operational Technologies) research team.
  • Gradual improvement of DARE functionality and ergonomics, based on identified needs and available budgetary resources.

Communication and audiovisual media of SLL

  • Production of dashboards and audiovisual material for the communication, monitoring and exploitation of SLL activities.
  • Contribution to the communication of SLL, in particular by updating web content.

Profile

  • Advanced training in computer science and database management, at the CFC, ES, HES/EPF level, or equivalent.
  • Knowledge of BIM, digital models and construction data management.
  • Mastery of web and digital communication tools.
  • Skills for creating dashboards using open source tools (e.g. Grafana, Inlux).
  • Skills in web interface development and maintenance.
  • Ability to collaborate with IT-OT and research teams.
  • Autonomy, rigor, sense of organization.
  • Current French
  • English an asset.

We offer

  • A friendly and stimulating working atmosphere.

  • The opportunity to participate in demanding and varied projects.
  • All social benefits of a large enterprise.
  • The opportunity to contribute to the development of a dynamic campus, at the heart of educational, research and innovation missions.
