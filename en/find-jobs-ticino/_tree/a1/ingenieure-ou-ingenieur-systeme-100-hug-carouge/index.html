Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to discover the highlights of 2025 by clicking here . The Directorate of Digital Transformation and AI (DTN-IA) created, integrates and manages all computer applications of one of the most efficient hospital information systems in order to support the clinical and logistical activities of the University Hospitals of Geneva. With a fleet of more than 12,500 posts, 1'300 servers for more than 250 applications serving all staff, available 24/7, the DTN-IA manages a HR information system for the 13,000 collaborators and collaborators of the HUG and the 7 other Geneva institutions and an ERP that is efficient for the logistics and financial processes of the most complex. DTN-IA creates an integrated patient record at the forefront of the sector and contributes to the outreach of HUGs through a recognized functional richness and close collaboration with all health professionals and professionals and their cross-cutting branches. In addition, DTN-IA integrates state-of-the-art biomedical equipment and daily life as well as specialized applications for all medical-technical platforms. The job of the DTN-IA is to support day-to-day activities and build the future of a modern and efficient hospital, according to the strategic directions of the Institution, based on the best practices and the most demanding standards.

Description of post

Evolved in a dynamic and modern technical environment at the service of patients and HUG collaborators, the Server Systems team is responsible for the administration of infrastructures hosting critical business applications. The team is hierarchically attached to the DTN-IA Operations Service's Infrastructure Domain and works closely with numerous internal teams, including Applications, Data, Security and Network. As a Linux/containers/IA/automation specialist within this team, you are contributing to the evolution of the Information System by providing innovative technical solutions and ensuring the reliability, security and performance of 24/7 systems. You design and administer Linux virtual and physical servers as well as containerization and orchestration systems (ideally Kubernetes Rancher, Docker Swarm). You implement the tools and procedures necessary for optimal production and quality (costs, reliability, performance, monitoring, standards and standards). You work with developers and developers to design and optimize technical architectures in terms of resilience, performance and scalability. You ensure the implementation, monitoring of systems and applications throughout the project life cycle as well as the technical coherence of solutions in collaboration with project leaders and managers. You are involved in the drafting of standards, standards and procedures, as well as technological intelligence and forward-looking actions, ensuring the proper use of the configuration management database (CMDB). You maintain hardware and software in operational condition by ensuring that end-of-life dates, performance and security criteria are met. You analyze and manage incidents, ensure that you return to service and take the necessary corrective action in a continuous improvement process.

Qualifications

You have a Linux/Containers/IA administrator profile/experienced automation and you have a Master's degree in Computer Science or an equivalent degree. You must master the tools of automation/Infrastructure As Code/GitOps (Ansible, GIT, ArgoCD...), as well as Linux (preferably Redhat/Rocky), VMware vSphere, the technologies related to containers and their orchestration (Docker, Docker Swarm, Kubernetes, Helm). You have experience implementing and operating AI platforms with GPU (NVIDIA GPU Operator would be a plus). You know the high availability technologies (load balancing, vertical/horizontal scattering), the technical, functional and organisational architecture of a large Information System, the production processes and their quality control. Knowledge and experience of IPAM/DNS/DHCP (Preferably IP-Effective) would be a plus. Moreover, knowledge of production tools (monitoring) as well as knowledge and experience of project management and methodologies (ideally Agile, Hermes) would be desired. If possible, you have knowledge of how public procurement works, automated testing and a DevOps and CI/CD approach (continuous integration/deployment). You have cold blood and reactivity to manage incidents and good stress resistance to cope with workloads or exceptional situations. In addition, you have a good analytical capacity to draw the right conclusions following incidents and good communication skills to interact with users at all technical and/or hierarchical levels. You can demonstrate a good adaptability with a proven experience of 5 to 10 years in a similar or equivalent function, if possible in a large company. You have a spirit of synthesis and abstraction, rigour, precision and creativity, as well as an excellent ability to work as a team. Finally, there is also a request for writing ease in French and an ability to communicate in English (oral and written).
