Full description

Our heart beats for the Swiss gastronomic sector. We are constantly committed to our clients. In our 31 self-service markets, through external service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a varied assortment, attractive clean brands, specialized and professional advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Suisse SA is part of the international group Transgourmet. The company is a leader in the delivery and self-service trade in Switzerland and has some 2'100 employees, as well as 100'000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals, as well as retail).

Mission

  • Ensure that the goods are 100% available (shelf, 2nd point of sale,

Skills and qualifications

  • Reliable, efficient and correct work
  • Careful presentation and pleasure of customer contact
  • Enthusiasm for new challenges
  • Physical resistance
  • Pleasure working as a team
  • Impeccable knowledge of French
  • Training in forklift and/or drawbar handling (SUVA standard) would be an asset.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
