Full description

The Lucerne Canton Hospital with its locations in Lucerne, Sursee and Wolhusen is a company of the LUKS Group and thus belongs to one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. The more than 8,500 employees are there with warmth, competence and commitment around the clock for the well-being of patients. The clinics and institutes of the LUKS Group offer medical services of the highest quality.

We are looking for our team on February 1, 2027 or by agreement. Tasks: • They receive our patients in a friendly and professional manner, advise them personally and by telephone and take on general administrative secretarial tasks. • They coordinate patient appointments, organize examinations in the outpatient and inpatient area and take over office hours and telephone services in the reception and secretarial area • Your varied responsibilities also include carrying out blood sampling and injections as well as competent advice, training and instruction from our patients. Requirements • With your completed training as an MPA or FaGe and your further education as a doctor secretary / doctor secretary You have ideal conditions for this position with • An appreciative, empathetic and service-oriented approach to patients and various stakeholders is a matter of course for you. • They work organized, keep an overview even in a lively environment and bring a high degree of flexibility • Very good knowledge of medical terminology and sound IT knowledge (MS Office) round off your profile We offer: • Enjoy an appreciative working environment in a small team with flat hierarchies, open communication and cooperation on an equal footing. • Regulated working hours from Monday to Friday enable a good balance between work, family and leisure time. • Our modern clinic information system supports you in managing all patient data and processes digitally and efficiently • As the largest employer in Central Switzerland, you benefit from numerous benefits – including internal educational offers and attractive social benefits that go beyond the standard
