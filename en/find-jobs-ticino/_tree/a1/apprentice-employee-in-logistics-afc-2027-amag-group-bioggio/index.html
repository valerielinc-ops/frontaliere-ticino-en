AFC - Mobilog Bioggio

AMAG is the leading car dealer in Switzerland and represents brands like VW, Audi, SEAT|CUPRA, ŠKODA, Porsche and VW commercial vehicles. We are proud to train over 800 apprentices in 18 different professional sectors. With us you can move and learn a lot. Are you at the last year of your compulsory training and want to find a place of apprenticeship as a logistics employee in early summer 2027? Apply to our Mobilog logistics warehouse in Bioggio, by clicking on the "Insertion" button at the bottom of the page. We offer you. .

  • the possibility to work on the most modern and known automotive brands
  • carry out responsibility tasks and interesting development opportunities.
  • 6 weeks of holiday a year.
  • a talent development programme
  • taking the costs of teaching materials (including software).

You learn to...

  • know our modern logistics center and you will be responsible for ensuring that about 120,000 warehouse items are stored correctly and available at all times.
  • manage incoming and outgoing goods and check accompanying documents
  • carry out inventory control and process orders using computers and scanners
  • plan and organize distribution according to the type of goods
  • deliver to customers
  • draft and manage inventory
  • manage various logistic activities of support

You...

  • obtained or obtained at the end of the year the middle school license
  • a good physical resistance and you like to put yourself at work
  • interested in working with the computer and the scanner
  • manual, organizational and ability to work under pressure
  • structured, tidy and precise personality
  • aptitude to work in group.

Did we arouse your interest?

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes, if you are resident in Italy within the 20 km border zone. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks, then automatic yearly renewal for as long as the employment continues. Weekly return to your Italian home is required to keep the status.
