Full description

Tasks

  • promote observation, training and retraining activities by applying the guidelines laid down by the Federal Law for Vocational Training (LFPr) and the relevant ordinances of the State Secretariat for Training, Research and Innovation (SEFRI) for trade training
  • co-operation with the development of an individual project for users in the business sector and the implementation of decisions taken at network meetings
  • accompany the individualised path of persons benefiting from a professional provision of invalidity insurance
  • to provide for the organization and operation of the sector
  • draw up the assessment, training and retraining reports for the assigned user
  • ensure the operation of the secretariat of the Centre
  • participation in working groups

Requirements

  • higher education in the field of economics (Sixth or equivalent) or training in the commercial field (Federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as an employee of trade) and training as accompanying person/social worker (APF) or equivalent
  • Basic course for trainers/trainers
  • experience in vocational training
  • proven work experience with fragile users
  • excellent knowledge of the Microsoft Office package
  • knowledge of invalidity insurance and in particular its professional measures
  • knowledge of the territory and the social network of the Canton Ticino
  • motivation and spirit of initiative
  • organizational and decision-making skills
  • excellent editorial skills
  • availability of continuing training
  • good mastery of the Italian language (conversation and editing) and knowledge (speaking and written) of a second official language

Special remarks

  • entry into service: as soon as possible

Application conditions

  • applications must be submitted online. Other forms of application shall not be deemed valid
  • in addition to the documents to be submitted at the time of the online application (letter of presentation and motivation, curriculum vitae, diplomas, certificates and certificates of work), further documentation may be requested at a later stage (judicial casellario with relative self-certification and self-certification on the health)

Terms of use

  • appointment or assignment: these are those provided for by the Law on the sorting of employees of the State and teachers of 15 March 1995 (LORD) and the relevant Regulations. Any amendment of the law concerning salary and organization remains explicitly reserved
  • working hours: 42 hours per week (for a 100% employment ratio), flexible or according to service requirements

21 October 2026
