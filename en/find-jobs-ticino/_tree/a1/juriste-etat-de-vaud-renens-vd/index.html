Full description

Join our HR entity where teamwork, collaboration and mutual support are strong values, at the heart of the service of nearly 500 people. In a challenging and dynamic IT environment, you will play a key role in administrative and salary management, while managing time and absences with your colleagues and contributing to HR and recruitment processes.

  • Management of HR administration and payroll processes, such as processing of information needed to establish salaries on the Vaud State HR Information System (PeopleSoft) and monthly salary verification
  • Time and absence management: tasks related to the time management tool, administrative management of absences from illness and accident, HR support for long-term absence situations
  • Active contribution to recruitment processes, follow-up of training requests and appraisal interviews
  • Support to employees in HR processes, including integration and departure
  • Active contribution to continuous improvement within the team including by proposing internal procedures to improve efficiency
  • The role of trainer at the workplace for the apprentice in a one-year internship with the HR team For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact the Human Resources & Administration Manager, Audrey Coat, by email audrey.coat@vd.ch We are happy to receive your complete file in French (CV, cover letter, work certificates, diplomas).
  • HR Manager Certificate Required
  • Confirmed professional experience in salary management and HR administration in a public administration or a large structure (about 500 pers.)
  • Professional practice in HR recruitment and development processes
  • In-depth knowledge of wage management, social insurance and Swiss labour law, and if possible of the legal framework specific to the Cantonal Vaud administration
  • Computer and digital skills Jurisdiction requested:
  • Perfect mastery of Excel, aptitude for numbers and reporting
  • Knowledge and use of an HRIS tool (Preferably PeopleSoft)
  • Ability to manage a large volume of data and a wide variety of tasks
  • Rigor and high organizational capacity
  • Strong team spirit, mutual support and collaboration
  • Excellent editorial skills in French Special Astreintes: - Planning leave days taking into account salary processing schedule requirements - Home in Switzerland required

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
