Full description

For our B2B Food Service Business Unit, we are looking for a committed and qualified personality with strong execution skills to strengthen our local Customer Marketing Food Service team and drive sales support in product and concept communication with creative energy.

  • Responsibilities:
  • Product management/support for the assigned product portfolio
  • Co-design of the marketing and media plan
  • Planning, coordination, and implementation of marketing measures
  • Adaptation of international assortment and product strategies to the local market
  • Creation of advertising materials and sales documentation
  • Development of arguments for gastronomy and institutional customers in collaboration with our Culinary Advisors
  • Preparation and active participation in conferences (preparing and delivering presentations)
  • Co-organization of trade fairs and events
  • Requirements:
  • Completed vocational training with further education in marketing
  • Initial marketing experience in the food sector, preferably in Food Service
  • Professional kitchen experience or completed hotel management school is an advantage
  • Confident German and good English skills, both written and spoken
  • French and/or Italian skills are an advantage
  • Digital mindset and understanding of social media is an advantage
  • Independent and flexible working style as well as strong team and convincing communication skills
  • Solution-oriented, creative, and strong implementation skills
  • Very good SAP and MS Office skills
  • Adobe InDesign is an advantage
