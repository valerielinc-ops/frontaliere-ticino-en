Full description

The entero Klinik with the locations Niederlenz, Neuenhof and Egliswil is a recognized specialist clinic for the treatment of patients with dependency diseases. In Niederlenz, we carry out qualified weaning treatments under medical supervision. With 15 beds, we are a manageable facility with a family character. To complement our team, we are looking for Certified nurse HF (60 – 100%)

Place of work: weaning Niederlenz

Your tasks

  • Care of patients in psychiatric setting
  • Accompaniment of patients in the therapy process (3 to 6 months)
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Co-design of individual care and therapy plans

Requirement profile

  • Completed training as a certified nurse/certified nurse HF
  • Professional experience in psychiatry (addiction area beneficial)
  • Humorous, resilient personality
  • Appreciative attitude
