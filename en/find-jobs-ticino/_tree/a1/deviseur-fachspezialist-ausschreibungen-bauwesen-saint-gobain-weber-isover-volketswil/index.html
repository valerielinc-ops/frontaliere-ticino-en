Full description

To support our development team at the Volketswil site, we are looking for an independent and self-responsible personality as:

Deviseur/-in / Specialist/-in tenders Construction, 100%

Their tasks

  • Precise tender documents for building envelopes (compact facades and curtain-ventilated facades) and internal plaster work
  • Development of specifications taking into account NPK, standards, building regulations and quality standards
  • Analysis and interpretation of technical drawings, building plans and project documentation
  • Application of construction software (e.g. SORBA or Messerli) for efficient preparation of tenders
  • Independent processing and coordination of tender projects
  • Close cooperation with architects, builders, entrepreneurs and the field service
  • Ensuring timely preparation and submission of tender documents
  • Support for technical issues and development of solutions
  • Ongoing update of call templates and work bases
  • Observation of new materials, technologies and construction methods and their integration into daily work

Your profile

  • Completed training as a draftsman or a comparable structural training alternatively also plasterer or mason with very good technical understanding and flair for technical drawing
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in the construction environment or in the tendering of construction projects
  • Experience with construction software such as SORBA, Messerli or comparable solutions
  • Good knowledge of standard position catalogues (NPK)
  • Secure handling of CAD programs as well as good digital understanding of plans
  • Strong technical understanding and enjoyment of reading and interpreting building plans
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Experience in creating tenders, ideally in building envelope
  • Good IT user knowledge and affinity for digital tools
  • Communication-strong personality with pleasure in working with different stakeholders
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing, French or Italian is advantageous
  • High quality orientation and a strong sense of responsibility.

Your perspective

  • Exciting tasks in a dynamic company
  • Corporate culture with space for personal responsibility and entrepreneurial ideas
  • A working environment in which openness and appreciation are a matter of course
  • Attractive employment conditions with above-average social benefits
  • Individual introductory programme adapted to the functional requirements
  • Support for your individual professional development
