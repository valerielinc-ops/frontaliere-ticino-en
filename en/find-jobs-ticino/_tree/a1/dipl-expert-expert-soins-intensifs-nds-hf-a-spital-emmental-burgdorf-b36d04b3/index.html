Full description

With specialists at the two hospital locations in Burgdorf and Langnau, the Emmental Hospital provides high-quality medical care for 150,000 inhabitants – around the clock.

We are looking for an immediately or by agreement at the Burgdorf location:

Task area

  • You support and monitor critically ill patients / patients of all disciplines competently and proactively – from admission to relocation
  • You carry out demanding intensive care measures on your own responsibility and ensure safe apparatus monitoring
  • You are an active member of the REA team and take responsibility in acute and emergency situations
  • You are responsible for the care process and its professional documentation
  • You work interprofessionally closely with the medical service and other specialist areas
  • You accompany students to NDS HF Intensive Care and support new employees with their familiarization

Profile

  • Completed postgraduate degree in HF Intensive Care (or an equivalent education recognised in Switzerland)
  • Sound expertise and pleasure in the demanding care of critically ill patients
  • High commitment, resilience and an independent and structured working method
  • Open, flexible personality with strong interdisciplinary team skills
  • Reflection and networked thinking and action
  • Willingness to work in shift work
