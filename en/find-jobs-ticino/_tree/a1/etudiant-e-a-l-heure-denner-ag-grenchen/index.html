Full description

Get things done. Move forward. Make a difference. Lots of warmth and a "hey, can I help you?" – team spirit. And always crossing the finish line together. That's Denner. That's our store. And soon, yours too. Join the fresh winning team now.

Tasks

  • Operate the cash register and settle accounts
  • Receive, check, and stock fresh products and all other goods in your store
  • Check the best-before dates on products
  • Ensure order and cleanliness in the branch and warehouse
  • Correctly implement store layout guidelines

Requirements

  • Ideally more than two years of professional experience: retail is an advantage
  • Completed vocational training (EBA): in retail (food) or a related profession is an advantage
  • Willing to work beyond "nine to five" – and enjoy free time when others are working
  • Physically fit, some parcels weigh up to 10 kilos
  • Familiar with PC, tablet, and smartphone – and you quickly get used to other digital helpers (e.g. for inventory management)
  • Enjoy being around people, a good dose of warmth and a positive attitude
  • German (good knowledge)

What we offer

  • Part of the Migros Group: Strong brand rooted in retail under the Migros Group umbrella
  • Healthy working: High standards in occupational safety, ergonomics, and hygiene
  • Maternity / paternity leave: 5 weeks extra baby break (19 weeks) for mothers at 100% salary. Fathers enjoy 4 paid weeks with their newborn
  • Fair wages: 13 monthly salaries and fixed salary increases for all instead of bonuses for a few
  • Mobile subscription: The agony of choice: With Galaxus Mobile, Wingo or Sunrise, you're properly connected from CHF 10.-
  • Migros Bank: Better conditions for interest and fees on your private, investment, or mortgage accounts
  • Employee offers: 10 percent off your next big purchase (twice a year)
  • Holidays: 6 weeks of vacation for everyone, and even more days for service anniversaries
  • Leisure and cultural offers: Various discounts for car rentals, hotels, events, and much more
