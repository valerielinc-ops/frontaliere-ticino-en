Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz, Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire construction customers and private customers with a great deal of commitment on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

To strengthen our team based in Bussigny-Nord, we are looking for an entrepreneurial and motivated personality.

Missions

  • Welcome and advise our clients in a friendly and competent manner
  • Providing technical advice in the field of wholesale
  • Preparation of delivery bulletins
  • Invoice preparation
  • Management of the fund
  • Inventory management
  • Arrivals, dispatch of goods

Your Profile

  • Own an EFA Retail Manager or equivalent
  • Experience in the field of sales confirmed (min. 3 years) and knowledge of the field of large works, dry construction, tools
  • Taste pronounced for sale
  • Have ease in contacts
  • Mastering common IT tools, Microsoft Office, knowledge of an ERP
  • Know how to work independently while easily integrating into a team

We offer

  • A challenge within a changing company
  • A stimulating, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where active collaboration is valued while encouraging autonomy.
  • An intensive integration programme is planned, allowing our new colleagues to benefit from product and system training within the company.
  • Support for the professional and personal development of our employees.
  • A well-structured network of craft centres and close collaboration with the sales department.
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 weeks from 50 years old) with the possibility of purchasing an additional holiday up to 10 days a year, additional benefits such as an independent and well-funded pension fund, as well as discounts on our entire product range and additional external benefits.
