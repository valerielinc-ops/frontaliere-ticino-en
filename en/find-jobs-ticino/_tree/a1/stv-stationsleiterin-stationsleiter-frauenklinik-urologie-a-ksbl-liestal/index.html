Full description

Admission by appointment · Liestal, Urology/Women's Clinic · Job reference 13281 · Permanent employment

Tasks

  • Support of station management in planning, organisation and management tasks on the station
  • Ensuring professional, patient-centered and high-quality care on the ward with a capacity of 24 beds
  • Empathetic and needs-oriented support of patients and their relatives within the framework of the zone and tandem model
  • Co-responsibility in the management committee of the station in the sense of shared governance
  • Active participation in the strategic and operational management of the station
  • Participation in field-related projects and participation in team development processes
  • Work on an interdisciplinary station for women and men using the principles of Lean Hospital
  • Optimization of processes, quality and patient orientation

Requirements

  • Diploma as nurse or midwife (HF, FH or equivalent)
  • Experience in an acute operation, ideally at least 2 years in the postpartum
  • Professional experience in a management position or willingness to undertake leadership training
  • Desire to take on leadership tasks and move forward with a positive attitude
  • Strong social skills, self-reflection, high motivation, teamwork, flexibility and resilience
  • Hearty and patient-oriented appearance
  • Empathy and knowledge of people as well as enjoyment of a varied and lively hospital everyday life
