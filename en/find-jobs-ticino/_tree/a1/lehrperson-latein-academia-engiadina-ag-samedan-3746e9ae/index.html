Teacher Latin (part-time)

The Academia Engiadina is a versatile educational institution in the midst of the inspiring Engadine mountains. It consists of a secondary school (gymnasium, specialist secondary school), a vocational preparation year, a secondary school for tourism & management, adult education and the viadalat observatory. In our high school area, we are looking for a motivated personality to strengthen our team at the start of the school year in 2027.

You can do that with us

You lead a modern lesson in the basic subject of Latin at 1\. and 2\. UG (7 weekly lessons).Since we are constantly developing as a school, you take an active part in projects with students and in the pedagogical field.

You bring that with you

Teaching diploma of secondary level 1 or 2 (Latin) or the willingness to achieve this,empathy and the joy of the promotion and development of young people,team spirit and interest in educational topics,knowledge in the use of AI\ tools in teaching or willingness to become familiar with these topics in a practical way.

This awaits you with us

A lot of space for creativity and ideas, an appreciative and collegial working environment that combines different strengths,the opportunity to increase the workload to the school year 2029/30,the unique quality of life of the Engadin – you work where others go on holiday.

Are you ready to help shape the educational world of Academia Engiadina? We look forward to your full application.
