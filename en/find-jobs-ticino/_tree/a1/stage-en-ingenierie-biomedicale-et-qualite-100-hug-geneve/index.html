Company description

With over 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international levels. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to discover the highlights of 2025 by clicking here . The post is attached to the Department of Operations. The mission of this department is to ensure the stewardship and logistics necessary for the proper functioning of all HUGs, in the restoration, cleaning and disinfection of premises and hygiene of linen. It also manages the sterilization service, the biomedical service and equipment as well as Cressy-Santé. More than 1,350 professionals carry out their tasks on a daily basis with a constant concern for requirements, with a sense of service and environmental responsibility. The Biomedical & Equipment Service, composed of 52 employees, is responsible for the management of the medical equipment fleet of the University Hospitals of Geneva (HUG). Its main functions are technological and regulatory watch, investment planning, prescription, acquisition and maintenance of medical equipment. In addition, this service implements and follows the requirements of materialovigilance (the head of service is the materialovigilance correspondent of the HUGs with SwissMedic).

Description of post

You are integrated or integrated into the biomedical service and equipment of the HUG, within the Sector of Biomedical Engineering (SIB), and are supervised or supervised by excellent and excellent professionals.

This course is part of two themes

Development of the biomedical network of French-speaking Switzerland and biomedical benchmarks.

Development of the Quality Management System (QMS) of the Service The service is based on the Good Biomedical Practices Guide and, as part of the continuous improvement and corrective action plan, you are helping to update the service's 2022 & 2024 self-assessments as part of its quality approach, and in preparation for the AFAQ Certification. In this context, you are also actively involved in updating quality documentation within the biomedical GED. The Swiss Romande biomedical network is organised around 3 Groupes Techniques biomédicaux romands (investments and technological monitoring, maintenance and operation, quality and vigilance), you actively participate in the development of this network by taking up the progress of the various works carried out by these GTs and consolidate the work to come (benchmarks and comparison between hospitals and clinics), create standards and make information available through communication tools to build and feed. In this context, you are required to work and exchange with all the hospital biomedical services in French-speaking Switzerland. You also have the opportunity to get involved concretely in several projects at different levels of advancement or coordination.

Qualifications

You are in the course of university training in biomedical engineering (Master in progress or eventually Bachelor's degree after the course), or in Quality System (preferably with knowledge of medical devices), as well as experience (stage or other) in the hospital or similar field. You have extensive knowledge of standard office software (MS Office, sharepoint) and are generally comfortable with the IT tool. Knowledge of communication tools (website or other) and/or database is a plus. You are comfortable in document management and in capturing technical information to feed the project's database management tools. You have a sense of rigour and organization. You act with methodology and efficiency and demonstrate communication and listening skills. You know how to work as a team and know how to manage projects in the field of medical devices (and related regulations). In addition to your spirit of collaboration, you are autonomous and discreet or discreet and ensure a fluid flow of information.
