System Engineer Operations 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. System Engineer Operations Zollikofen / Olten / Zurich / Work Smart, 01.03.2027 or by agreement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

As a small, distributed and digitally connected team, we are responsible for the further development, standardization and technical operation of SBB’s energy control systems. With this, you will ensure the safe and reliable power supply for rail transport and make a tangible contribution to strong, sustainable mobility. You work closely with specialist areas from energy, electrical engineering, telecommunications and computer science, get insight into challenging projects along the entire added value of the railway power supply and help design solutions that have an impact in daily operations. Work Smart and flexible working hours help you to combine work and private life well. You benefit from professional training and development opportunities in an environment that shares knowledge and takes responsibility. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a system engineer, you will manage SBB’s central energy control systems and ensure their reliable operation so that our trains are safe and on time. You keep an eye on the relationships between system components, interfaces and surrounding systems.
  • You develop tools that make the engineering process of control systems and plants more efficient.
  • You accompany new requirements from specification to production and ensure a clean implementation.
  • Your broad expertise in energy, electrical engineering, telecommunications and computer science is used to analyze events and derive effective improvements from them.
  • Together with the team, you will develop standards for data, interfaces and system images in order to optimize the overall system in a targeted manner.

You bring that with you.

  • In complex environments, you quickly get a reliable overview, approach problems systematically and work in a team-oriented manner.
  • Even in hectic situations, you stay calm, make structured decisions and keep your quality standards high.
  • A degree at FH or HF level in a technical field such as electrical engineering, computer science, telecommunications or energy is available.
  • Knowledge of hardware and software of networked computer systems including data structures, ideally in the environment of energy or power grid control systems or railway control systems, you bring along.
