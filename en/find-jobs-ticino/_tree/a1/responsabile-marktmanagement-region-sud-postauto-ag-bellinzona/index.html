Head ofMarket Management South Region — PostAuto AG

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you interested in the strategic development of public transport? Do you want to play a key role in market management and actively shape the transformation towards electromobility? In this challenging leadership role, you are responsible for market development in an important region and shape the future of PostBus together with a wide range of stakeholders. With you, the sustainable further development of regional passenger transport succeeds.

Your advantages in the post.

We offer a fair wage, which ranges between CHF 173’900 and CHF 193’900 per year (100% fee) depending on experience and skills. Clear and transparent employment conditions with a fixed annual wage await you. Also applications of talents with different competencies are welcome, in such cases your wage can be slightly below or above the bandwidth.

With us, you work in a future-oriented, dynamic environment in which you are involved and with which you continue to grow. With us, you can expect various benefits, with which you can flexibly design your everyday life according to your needs.

Holidays

We will send you 5-6 weeks on holiday.

Education and training

We support you with training and further education, temporary job changes and offer you promotion opportunities. We train drivers in our own training centre.

Family friendly

We offer 18 weeks of fully paid maternity leave and 4 weeks of leave for the second parent. We also contribute to the costs of family-supplemented childcare.

Flexibility

We support you with individual working models. Mobile and flexible working, home office, third place, part-time work and job sharing.

Vehicle benefits

We offer you attractive advantages when buying a vehicle: You benefit from fleet discounts and special conditions on new models. With Yellow Drive, we offer you attractive advantages on electric car subscriptions.

Mobility offers

We offer you driving discounts (FVP) as well as attractive additional offers for family members. You pay only CHF 10 per year at PubliBike or Velospot.

You can do that.

  • As a member of the management team Markt PostBus, you assume overall responsibility for market management in the South region (Central Switzerland and Ticino) and maintain relationships with internal and external stakeholders.
  • In addition, you lead the core process in regional passenger transport, negotiate offer and target agreements and ensure correct, timely and subsidy-compliant service billing, which makes an important contribution to the company’s success.
  • You are responsible for securing earnings in your region and primarily manage key account management for the Canton of Ticino in terms of personnel and organization.
  • In addition, in close coordination with the product and planning, you develop and optimize network and timetable offers, alternative drives and on-demand solutions and contribute your expertise in a targeted manner.
  • You coordinate cooperation with the various business units and actively contribute to the transition to electromobility and to increasing competitiveness.
  • You will also be responsible for regional marketing communications and align them with the overarching marketing strategy, leveraging your strengths effectively.
  • You represent PostBus in regional public transport bodies, create market and sales plans for your region and take on the organizational responsibility for the mandate of the Arcobaleno management.
