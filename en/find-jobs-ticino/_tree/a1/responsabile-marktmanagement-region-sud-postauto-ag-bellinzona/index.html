Full description

Are you interested in the strategic development of public transport? Do you want to play a key role in market management and actively shape the transformation towards electromobility? In this challenging leadership role, you are responsible for market development in an important region and shape the future of PostBus together with a wide range of stakeholders. With you, the sustainable further development of regional passenger transport succeeds.

Clear and transparent employment conditions with a fixed annual wage await you. Also applications of talents with different competencies are welcome, in such cases your wage can be slightly below or above the bandwidth.

With us, you work in a future-oriented, dynamic environment in which you are involved and with which you continue to grow. With us, you can expect various benefits, with which you can flexibly design your everyday life according to your needs.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
