Full description

Data is only valuable when better decisions are made. At BKW, you combine business and IT, improve the quality of business-critical data and create the basis for automated processes and a targeted customer approach.

Your field of activity

  • You are responsible for data-based use cases from business questions to prioritization to implementation and check their impact in the business
  • You identify data gaps in business-critical master and customer data, define quality rules and drive improvements together with process managers
  • You link customer, contract and usage data and thus create the basis for analyses and a personalized customer approach
  • Define meaningful KPIs, automate recurring reporting and enable self-service evaluations for the departments
  • As an interface between business and IT, you translate professional requirements into practical data solutions and accompany their implementation
  • You prepare analyses and concrete recommendations for action appropriate to the addressee – up to management level

You bring that with you

  • At least five years of professional experience in data analytics, business intelligence or a similar analytical role with direct business relevance
  • With SQL you know very well and work safely with a BI tool at author level
  • You have in-depth experience with master data, data quality and complex, grown system landscapes
  • Relational data models as well as the linking of customer, contract and usage data are familiar to you
  • You translate complex business questions into concrete data requirements and practical solutions
  • Your own initiative and entrepreneurial thinking distinguish you and you can convey data in an understandable and decision-oriented way.
