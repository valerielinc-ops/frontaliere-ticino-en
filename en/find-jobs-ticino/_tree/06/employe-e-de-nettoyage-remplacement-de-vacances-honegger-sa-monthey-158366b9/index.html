Full description

Honegger SA is a company run by its owner that offers comprehensive services for buildings in the fields of cleaning and Facility Management throughout Switzerland.

The traditional family company currently employs nearly 6,500 employees in 24 branches in the four language regions of Switzerland and Liechtenstein.

  • We guarantee Swiss values:
  • Reliability, openness to the world and, in particular, cleanliness.
  • We manage our employees in a respectful, fair and consistent manner.

We are looking for 8 employees to ensure the replacement of staff during the school holidays, from mid-December 2026 to mid-January 2027

Workplace: Monthey and Region

Work schedules: Various sites and schedules according to schedule transmitted during engagement

Your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for cleaning maintenance (commercial surfaces, industrial surfaces, offices, sanitary, etc.)
  • You ensure an irreproachable cleaning at our customers
  • You use our cleaning products according to art

What we expect from you

  • You have experience in cleaning and a neat appearance
  • You are autonomous, flexible and you like to take on responsibilities
  • You're an open person and you show how to live.
  • For you, reliability, discretion and quality go without saying
  • You have good oral knowledge in French
  • You have a valid residence/work permit

We offer you

  • Various and exciting tasks in an innovative company across Switzerland.
  • A well-structured and collective team.
  • Self-employment
