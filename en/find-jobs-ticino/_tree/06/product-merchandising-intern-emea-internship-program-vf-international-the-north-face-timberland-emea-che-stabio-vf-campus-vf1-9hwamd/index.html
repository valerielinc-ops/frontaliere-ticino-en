Be a dreamer, make the difference! Join #VFInternshipProgram in our EMEA HQ in Stabio, Switzerland as Product Merchandising Intern for The North Face . Learn more at http://vfc.com . Key information

  • Gross monthly salary: 1’850 CHF
  • Location: VF EMEA Headquarters in Stabio, Switzerland
  • Streamlined recruitment process: 3 interviews (Recruiter, Hiring Manager, potential Business Case) Eligibility requirements
  • You must be graduating after May 1st, 2026 OR you must have a student status as of June 2026
  • You must hold EU or EEA (European Economic Area) citizenship Let’s talk about the role! As a Product Merchandising Intern you will support the end-to-end product lifecycle by translating market insights into compelling assortments that drive sales and brand growth. Collaborate cross-functionally to ensure the right product is in the right place at the right time, maximizing customer impact and business performance.
  • Support the SMU (Special Make Up) line planning process by analyzing consumer insights, sales performance, and market trends to inform the FW/SS assortment strategy for Europe.
  • Coordinate the go-to-market process by managing timelines, tools, and product data accuracy to ensure seamless cross-functional alignment.
  • Monitor in-season product performance to identify risks and opportunities, providing actionable recommendations to optimize sell-through and margin.
  • Collaborate with global and regional teams to ensure correct data flows into the systems & assortments reflect brand direction while meeting local market needs and commercial goals.
  • Contribute to visual line reviews and product presentations by supporting the preparation of compelling tools.
  • Support data entry of PLM (Product Lifecycle Management) systems, support with transfer from PLM to Flex and update linelists to reflect correct information How you will make a difference To succeed as an Associate Product Merchandising Intern, you should be curious about products, eager to learn how assortments are built, and ready to support a fast-paced team with energy and precision. We are looking for someone who has demonstrated:
  • The ability to work with numbers and draw simple conclusions to support product decisions.
  • The capability to stay organized and manage multiple tasks with attention to detail.
  • A team player. Working x functionally is essential as a assistant product merchandiser.
  • An understanding of what makes a product appealing to consumers , especially in the European market.
  • Eye for detail to ensure accuracy across the tools & PLM systems
  • A basic knowledge of Excel or similar tools to help with data and product tracking.
  • A keen interest in fashion, retail, or product creation , and a desire to grow in this field.
  • A positive, can-do attitude , and a willingness to support the team wherever needed. What’s in it For You By joining our team, you will:
  • Learn how to build and manage a seasonal product line , from concept to market launch.
  • Gain exposure to real-time business decisions , using data to support product and commercial strategies.
  • Understand the role of merchandising within a global brand , and how it connects with marketing, sales, and product teams.
  • Develop your analytical and storytelling skills , turning numbers into product narratives.
  • Build a strong professional network , with opportunities to connect across teams, functions, and brands.
  • Receive mentorship and training , helping you grow your confidence and capabilities in a dynamic, international environment. The VF EMEA Internship Program is a 12-month journey designed to develop and empower emerging talents at the start of their careers. We are committed to unleashing your potential, so you can expect a structured learning path and rich hands-on experience , including:
  • Dedicated skill-building workshops & internal consulting group project
  • One day of work in-store gaining real insights about our brands
  • Networking opportunities with peers and senior leadership In this internship, you’ll gain hands-on experience in how seasonal assortments are built and brought to life across the European market. You’ll work closely with experienced merchandisers, gaining exposure to product strategy, consumer insights, and cross-functional collaboration. We cannot grant that the internship will lead you to a contract afterwards, but thanks to the value of the experience most of our interns are staying in the company after the internship period.
