The Position Network P&C Business Partner - Roche Diagnostics Global Operations

Who We Are At Roche, we are passionate about transforming patients’ lives, and we are bold in both decision and action - we believe that good business means a better world. That is why we come to work every single day. We commit ourselves to scientific rigor, unassailable ethics, and access to medical innovations for all. We do this today to build a better tomorrow. Roche is strongly committed to a diverse and inclusive workplace. We strive to build teams that represent a range of backgrounds, perspectives, and skills. At Roche Diagnostics Global Operations, we lead the industrialization of high-quality products that help diagnose disease and impact lives worldwide. Our teams manage a vital portion of the value chain—from late-stage development and material sourcing to mass production and global distribution. With a network spanning 13 manufacturing sites across four continents and three strategic supply chain hubs, our workforce of over 9,000 employees ensures reliable access to world-leading solutions for all Diagnostics Business Areas. Together, we deliver answers to patients everyday, fulfilling the Roche purpose of doing now what patients need next.

The Opportunity This role embodies the mindset of a business leader and entrepreneur, operating flexibly within a networked structure to drive the success of Diagnostics Global Operations. As a strategic partner and coach, you will inspire and empower teams within and beyond the P&C community, fostering end-to-end thinking and ensuring the organization is equipped to meet evolving global challenges through mindset and behavior transformations.

In this role, you will hold a unique position as a Network Business Partner, bridging global enterprise strategy with cross-functional execution. You will operate across the breadth of the Diagnostics Global Operations ecosystem to drive collective success.

  • You will act as a vital Network BP driving impact across our entire global footprint. You will collaborate deeply across the "Plan, Source, Make, Supply" value chain, partnering with diverse leadership teams to harmonize people practices and drive operational excellence.
  • Systemic Strategic Impact: You will translate overall P&C strategies into actionable, scalable initiatives that drive organizational health, performance, and a diverse, inclusive culture across multiple leadership areas. By fostering end-to-end thinking, you ensure that P&C solutions are fit-for-purpose for the entire Global Operations network.

As a core member of the GO BP Network Team, you will collaborate with the PCET. A key component of this role includes:

  • PCET Partnership: Partnering closely with the Diagnostics Global Operations P&C Enabling Team (PCET) to co-create and drive the functional agenda across the Global Operations footprint.
  • Strategic Alignment: Acting as a bridge between site-level execution and the strategic P&C roadmap, ensuring that local insights inform global enabling initiatives and site-specific needs are integrated into the broader global strategy.
  • Reporting Line: This position reports directly to a member of the Diagnostics Global Operations PCET, providing you with a direct line of sight into the P&C strategy for the entire organization.
