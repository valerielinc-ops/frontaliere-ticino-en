Full description

The Department of Culture comprises eleven multimedia teams, bundled in the four multimedia groups “Culture”, “Society”, “Knowledge” and “Fiction”. The teams work in an interdisciplinary manner, in accordance with the genres and topics defined within the framework of the strategy SRF 2024 for specific formats and content.

What are your tasks?

In the Multimedia Team (MMT) Health you are involved from the 1. January 2027 limited to six months for the following topics:

  • Journalistic research of various topics from the health sector for the programme PULS and other offers in the digital sector
  • Production of own contributions on all vectors (TV, radio, online)
  • Packaging of the programs EINSTEIN and PULS for Youtube

What do you bring?

  • You have completed studies, preferably in the field of science or health
  • You have a great affinity for health topics and already gained initial journalistic experience
  • Research is fun for you and you ideally bring first experience in the production of video contributions (camera, editing)
  • They have a good linguistic expression, write stylishly and have the desire to contribute and implement their own ideas

What can you look forward to?

  • You will get an insight into how knowledge contributions are created in a large media company
  • You will learn about processes and planning and gain valuable know-how in dealing with text, pictures and videos
  • You are part of a collegial environment and act in a creative team
  • You have the opportunity to contribute your own topics and ideas, to research them and to implement them into your own stories

What we offer

What does it take to reach the people in Switzerland with high-quality formats? Expert knowledge, enthusiasm and an inspiring and stimulating environment in which everyone can contribute and develop. For the best to get the best out of themselves, they need an inspiring home: Welcome to the world of SRF!

  • Making media: Quality journalism is our core business and our passion. We are editorially free, promote creativity and offer a lot of design space for new and high-quality products.
  • Shaping digitalization: Quality journalism is also digital for us. That’s why we explore new possibilities and experiment with new forms and formats: digital, interactive and cross-medial. We design on the pulse of time and on the nerve of the audience.
  • Being human: We meet each other collegially, work independently and shape our working atmosphere individually. Our success is based on the personal strengths as well as on the professional qualities of all employees.
  • Creating meaning: We reach and move the whole of Switzerland with our programs. In doing so, we act respectfully and independently; in this way we promote opinion formation and strengthen diversity. We are proud of our work and our contribution to Switzerland.

Requirements

  • Sie haben ein abgeschlossenes Studium, vorzugsweise im Wissenschafts- oder Gesundheitsbereich

  • Sie haben eine grosse Affinität zu Gesundheitsthemen und bereits erste journalistische Erfahrungen gesammelt

  • Recherchieren macht Ihnen Spass und Sie bringen idealerweise erste Erfahrungen in der Produktion von Video-Beiträgen mit (Kamera, Schnitt)

  • Sie verfügen über einen guten sprachlichen Ausdruck, schreiben stilsicher und haben Lust, eigene Ideen einzubringen und umzusetzen
