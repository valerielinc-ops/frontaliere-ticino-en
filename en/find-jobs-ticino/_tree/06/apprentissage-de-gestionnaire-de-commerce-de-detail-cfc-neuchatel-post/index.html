Full description

You enjoy being in daily contact with people and you like to advise and sell? So training in the retail trade is exactly what you need! During the initial three-year training, you will acquire the knowledge you need to successfully enter the diversified world of retail at the Post Office. Your training venue will be located in La Chaux-de-Fonds or Neuchâtel.

Your training

  • A real showcase of the Post Office, you will be in contact with our customers every day in the branch, and you will advise our customers on our entire range of physical and digital products at the counter and on the sales surface.
  • Receive letters and parcels, make payments and payments, sell products and services or provide advice as required: your tasks will be as interesting as they are varied.
  • The Post Office is increasingly positioning itself as a service centre for partner companies such as insurance, banks and health funds: here you will have the opportunity to gain additional knowledge of these sectors.
  • The range of offers offered in subsidiaries includes other products and services around specific themes (e.g. Habitat, Offer, Adventure): you will be at the heart of the action when it comes to supporting national and regional campaigns and creating a variety of shopping experiences for customers.
  • You will have the opportunity to acquire and deepen your operational skills within a team and in different subsidiaries.
  • Finally, inter-firm and in-house training courses will allow you to link theory to practice.

What you bring

We are looking for enthusiastic and motivated young people who want to make things happen and who show curiosity. The requirements are as follows:

  • You have completed your compulsory secondary school with good results.
  • You have a sense of numbers and you know how to handle money.
  • Human contacts are a source of development for you and you are able to express yourself by adapting your mode of communication to situations, both oral and written.
  • You stand out for your sense of organization and your taste for order.
  • You are also someone open, reliable and discreet.
  • Finally, you will gladly share your expertise.
