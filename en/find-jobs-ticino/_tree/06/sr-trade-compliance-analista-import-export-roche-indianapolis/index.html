The Position

Your Opportunity

  • Manage and monitor the company's compliance with global trade regulations, including but not limited to export controls, customs regulations, sanctions, and embargoes
  • Stay updated on relevant trade compliance laws and regulations, and provide guidance to internal stakeholders on compliance requirements
  • Conduct audits and assessments to identify areas of risk and develop strategies to mitigate potential compliance issues
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop, implement and improve trade compliance policies and procedures
  • Oversee the classification of products by ensuring accurate tariff codes, export controls, and partner government agencies (PGA) information.
  • Serve as a subject matter expert on trade compliance, providing training and guidance to employees across the organization
  • Handle import and export licensing processes, including applications, renewals, and record keeping
  • Effectively communicate with external partners, such as customs brokers, freight forwarders, and regulatory agencies, to ensure smooth and compliant movement of goods
  • Monitor and investigate potential violations of trade compliance regulations, and assist in implementing corrective actions

Who You Are

  • BS or BA degree and at least 5 years in an import/export position or 3 years with Masters.

Knowledge/Skills & Abilities

  • Knowledge of global trade regulations, including export controls, customs regulations, and sanctions
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a keen attention to detail
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with stakeholders at all levels
  • Proven ability to prioritize tasks and meet deadlines in a dynamic and fast-paced environment

Preferences

  • Experience in trade compliance, preferably in the pharmaceutical or medical device industry
  • Professional certifications in trade compliance (e.g., Licensed Customs Broker)
  • Detailed knowledge of Roche Diagnostics products and parts thereof
  • Familiarity with trade compliance software and tools

This is not a remote role. This role supports hybrid model of three days in the office and two days at home. Location is at the Roche US Diagnostics headquarters in Indianapolis.
