Full description

The company manufactures sophisticated metal components such as windows and stairs. You work independently with various welding techniques and ensure precise results. For our client we are looking for a Activities

  • Manufacture of components such as doors, windows, facades, stairs, balconies and canopies
  • Implementation of various metal and steel construction techniques
  • Cutting and welding of tubes, rods and sheets according to specifications
  • Independent execution of general metal construction work
  • Installation and repair work for production projects

Requirements

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as a metalworker with federal certificate of ability
  • Experience with welding techniques MIG, MAG and TIG
  • Craftsmanship and physical fitness
  • Accurate, careful and enduring working methods
  • Category B driving licence and team skills

Offer

  • Working in a small business with modern infrastructure and varied tasks
  • Possibility to take over workshop management and supervision of apprentices
