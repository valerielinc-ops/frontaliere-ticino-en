Their expertise is in demand. At the heart of Swiss social insurance and as part of a dedicated, well-established team, you will contribute to the development of concrete solutions and to the uniform, legally compliant application of benefits throughout Switzerland. We shape the social security for the Switzerland of tomorrow

The Federal Office for Social Insurance (BSV) ensures that the social security network functions properly and meets new challenges. In addition, the BSV is the federal competence centre for family, children and youth issues, intergenerational relations and social policy.
