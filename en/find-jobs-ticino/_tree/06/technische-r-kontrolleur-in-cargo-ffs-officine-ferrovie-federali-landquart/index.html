Technical:r Inspector:in Cargo 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Technical:r Inspector:in Cargo Landquart, 01.01.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You can expect a varied day-to-day work in the SBB Cargo team at the Lanquart location. You assess the functionality of freight wagons and cargo as well as operational safety and thus make a direct contribution to safe and punctual transport throughout Switzerland. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As a technical inspector, you take care of the technical inspection of freight wagons and loads and thus ensure trouble-free and safe rail traffic.
  • You recognize and report faults early and repair minor defects independently.
  • With your expertise, you make decisions about necessary measures and initiate repairs to ensure operational safety.
  • You will advise customers and forwarders on questions regarding cargo securing and compliance with regulations.
  • In collaboration with shunting and operations teams, you document findings and releases in the digital systems and contribute to the continuous improvement of processes.

You bring that with you.

  • You enjoy working in a team, taking responsibility and paying attention to quality and safety in your work.
  • With your careful and solution-oriented approach, you make well-founded decisions and communicate clearly with internal and external contacts.
  • You are attentive, focused and physically resilient to successfully accomplish this demanding work. You also like to work outside in any weather.
  • Completed training as Technical:r Inspector:in and a category B driving license you bring with you.
  • You are sure to negotiate in German (level C2) and ideally have knowledge of a second national language (level A1).
  • You have a perfect color sense (no red-green weakness) and have no entries in the penal and enforcement register.
