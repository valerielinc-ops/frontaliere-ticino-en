Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistique Romandie SA (MLR) to supply Switzerland every day. With modern technology and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow – networked and efficient.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Logistics, Migros Verteilbetrieb AG, Neustrasse 49, 4623 Neuendorf

Important information

Working hours: Monday to Friday from 06:00 to 15:30 (two Saturday assignments per month) We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Manoeuvring activities of various vehicle combinations
  • Provision of trucks for loading and unloading (trailer and semi-trailer vehicles)
  • Ensuring the correct load securing and load distribution
  • Loading of the vehicles with provided pallets from predefined lanes and standby spaces
  • Compliance with the requirements of the disposition
  • General cleaning work

Requirements

  • 3-5 years of experience: logistics/transport
  • Completed initial or further training: logistics/transport
  • Category C/E driving licence or training readiness
  • SUVA forklift certificate
  • A sense of duty and good manners
  • German (very good knowledge)

Cosa of Friamo

  • Occupational pension provision: Migros Pension Fund benefits are clearly above the law. All are retired at age 65
  • Food: In our staff restaurants, employees can cater at preferential prices
  • Migros Bank: Our employees benefit from particularly advantageous interest rates
  • Employee offers: We offer our employees many special services and discounts (e.g. fuel, mobile subscriptions)
