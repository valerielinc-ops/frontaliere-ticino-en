Full description

We at CSS help people lead a healthy life – whatever life has in store for them. Maybe our mission will soon be yours too? We would be delighted. We are a diverse group of ambitious people who want to make a difference. Some of us have done the KV, others come from the retail trade, the hospitality industry or from another professional corner. Everyone brings their experience. What unites us is our passion to be there for our customers. We help each other, stick together and laugh a lot. And we prefer to celebrate success together in a team. Put yourself in pole position for future vacancies!

This is what your everyday life looks like

  • The focus is on direct contact with people: With your convincing and authentic way, you win new customers for CSS and inspire them for our products and services.
  • Whether in person, on the phone, by e-mail or in a video call – your advice is always based on the individual needs of your counterpart. You communicate very well in German and English
  • Other languages are a plus.
  • Sales goals inspire you: You recognize opportunities, actively approach potential customers and use your sales skills to achieve new deals.
  • Also in the existing customer portfolio you will discover development opportunities. With cross- and upselling, you develop customer relationships sustainably and make an important contribution to the growth of CSS.
  • Need-based advice creates trust: You listen attentively, find suitable solutions and accompany your customers competently – even beyond the first degree.
  • Together we achieve more. As a team, you regularly exchange ideas, share your experiences and support your colleagues in achieving their common goals.
  • Variety is also part of this: For additional tasks and exciting projects, you are committed and like to look outside the box.

You bring that with you

  • Openness and a positive charisma distinguish you. You like to approach people, build relationships quickly and understand how to infect others with your enthusiasm.
  • The joy of selling and a strong will to succeed round off your profile. Ambitious goals motivate you, and with your own initiative and perseverance you pursue them consistently.
  • Completed vocational training in the commercial sector, in the retail trade or in another exciting profession forms a good basis – ideally supplemented by experience in customer contact.
  • You already bring experience from health insurance. You also ideally already have the VBV certification in your pocket.
  • In conversation, you convince with strong arguments, without losing sight of people. Active listening and a good feeling for the needs of your counterpart are among your strengths.
  • Digital consulting is a matter of course for you: Whether online meeting, digital sales documents or other communication channels – you move safely and professionally in the digital world.
  • A large relationship network helps you to establish new customer contacts. Ideally, you are also rooted in the region and live in the immediate vicinity.
  • Work Flexible: Adapt your work to your lifestyle annually with part-time options and other flexible models like home office and more.
  • Enjoy your wallet: Benefits such as bonus, Reka, discounted health insurance premium and others that quickly make up a few thousands a year.
  • Relax during the holidays: You work 41 hours a week and enjoy 6 weeks of vacation.
  • Improve yourself further: We like to see it if you educate yourself internally or externally and support generously with time and money.
  • Celebrate with us: Focused work and celebrate. We love this combination and maintain it regularly.
  • Stay fit: Ergonomic workplaces, promotion of mental well-being and many other offers around your health.
