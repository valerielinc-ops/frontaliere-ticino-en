WHAT WILL YOUR IMPACT BE?

Missions

To strengthen our expertise in digital Quality teams and to optimize our control processes, we are looking for an intern to accompany us in our strategic initiatives for digital continuity quality control processes. You will be an integral part of a young and dynamic team, in which you will be able to develop your talents and quickly develop skills on topics of high industrial importance related to quality and production. Your mission will be to carry out a technology transition project aimed at evolving our 3D technical data management methodologies to ensure its compatibility and optimal operation with our quality control systems. This project aims to optimize our control processes, improve operational performance and strengthen collaboration between the trades, from the study office to quality methods and production, ensuring the reliability and traceability of 3D data for quality.

Responsibilities

  • Implement tools and methodologies (application type) to ensure compatibility of enhanced 3D data with our quality control systems (programming and correction), based on the new 3D methodological standards applied by development and industrialization teams
  • Develop and structure new 3D methodological standards, in collaboration with CAD experts and trades (design, metrology, functional analysis, methods, quality), for their smooth integration into quality control processes
  • To ensure the digital continuity of the data produced, by promoting collaboration between teams and by eliminating information breaks and gaps, particularly for quality control applications
  • Deploy technological solutions around 3D to optimize access, reading and operation of models as part of quality control (control ranges, correction matrices, machine interactions)
  • Contribute to the integration of 3D data into quality processes by analysing technical and methodological gaps and proposing suitable solutions for programming and correction of controls
  • Pilot and accompany the change, following the performance indicators (KPI) related to the quality of 3D data and the effectiveness of controls, communicating the progress of the project and valuing the gains to the Management Committee

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE

  • Engineering training, with guidance in quality, metrology, research & innovation, mechanics or industrial engineering
  • Organised, rigorous, proposal-driven and well-analyzed and problem-solving
  • Team spirit, adaptability, autonomy
  • Mastery of 3D design and modelling tools
  • High interest in digitising industrial processes and managing technical data for quality and control
