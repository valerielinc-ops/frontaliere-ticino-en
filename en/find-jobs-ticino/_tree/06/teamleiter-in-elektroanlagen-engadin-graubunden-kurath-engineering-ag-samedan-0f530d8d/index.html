For our customer, a renowned Swiss company in the field of railway infrastructure, we are looking for a

Team leader/in electrical plants (South region \- in Engadin) \- (80-100%)

Your tasks

  • Personnel and technical management of the external service as well as coordination and organisation of the work of third parties and other specialist areas
  • Total resource planning and disposition
  • Ensuring high quality of work and quality standards
  • Responsibility for standby and malfunction, in-service troubleshooting and piketting
  • Ensuring occupational safety and health protection in the team, creating and controlling safety devices
  • Knowledge transfer in the team and to suppliers, representation of the interests of the customer as well as participation in coordination and construction meetings
  • Your profile *

  • High level of independence, initiative and resilience
  • Strong organisational and communication skills as well as team orientation
  • Readiness for occasional cooking
  • Place of work in Engadin – at least 70% on site
  • Knowledge of German at least level B2, knowledge of Italian beneficial
  • Good user knowledge Microsoft 365 (SAP beneficial)
