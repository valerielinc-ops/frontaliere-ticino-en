Pensum: 80% | Uzwil

With energy from biomass into the future! You like to get involved, work tech-savvy and keep track even in hectic situations? Then you are exactly right with us. For our biomass plant, we are looking for a committed personality who takes responsibility and together with the team ensures safe and efficient operation.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Co-responsible for processing the delivered green material according to the technically, economically and qualitatively specified criteria
  • Wheel loading and shifting work
  • Maintaining order and cleaning of plant
  • Carrying out maintenance, maintenance and repair work
  • Collaboration in the acceptance office, at the checkout and the telephone
  • Readiness to pay for holidays
  • Readiness to perform picket service
  • Dealing with customers

Profile & Requirements

  • Manual or technical vocational training completed
  • Basic knowledge of PC and Microsoft Office
  • Driving licence Cat. B
  • Advantage of possession of the truck and/or wheel loader test
  • Accustomed to acting and working independently and responsibly
  • Flair for maintenance and repair work on machinery and equipment
  • Place of residence max. 30 minutes from work location
