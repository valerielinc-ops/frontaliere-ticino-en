Full description

Engineer commissioning of electric buses (m/f/d | 80 - 100%) HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction As a leading Swiss company for the development, manufacture and repair of vehicles and kits, we employ 500 people in Bellach (SO). In addition to our local production, we support and supply license partners on five continents with our "Co-Bolt" modular system. As the only Swiss bus manufacturer, we move far beyond the borders with our high-quality electric and trolley buses. The innovation culture is the fuel of our company. In order to meet the growing demand for electrically driven buses, we are looking for a dynamic technician for the commissioning of our vehicles to strengthen our Bus & Trolley division. Do you have an affinity for electromobility, for drive systems and also for the software in vehicles? Do you also have self-motivation to actively contribute so that public transport is equipped with innovative and high-quality electromobility? In this function, you ensure that the vehicles from our production can go directly into line operation. Their feedback and insights are important and flow directly into the vehicle generation of tomorrow. Through your education and professional experience, you are the link between development and production. Their tasks

  • Commissioning of traction systems on electric buses, including high-voltage batteries, charging systems and communication systems
  • Install, test and parameterize the HESS vehicle software on the control units
  • Creating error diagnoses and implementing remedial measures in coordination with the respective specialist departments
  • Quality and functional control of the E/E system and its functionality in our electric buses
  • Documentation and release of commissioning carried out
  • Collaboration on new developments for the electromobility of tomorrow, their test methods and suitable process steps Our requirements
  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as an automotive mechatronics engineer, automatic engineer or comparable degree with E/E knowledge
  • Ideally, a further development in the field of mechatronics or vehicle technology
  • Enjoyment of diagnostic work with high quality awareness
  • Ideally, knowledge of high-voltage systems (automotives) and/or traction systems (railway) and their protective systems (e.g., insulation monitoring, emergency-off circuits)
  • Basic knowledge of the programming systems CODESYS, Matlab and Simulink (knowledge in communication systems such as CAN-Bus J1939 desirable)
  • Interest in learning new things
  • Collaborative and solution-oriented hands-on mentality
  • Written and oral communication skills in German and English (French skills of advantage)
  • Good PC knowledge in Microsoft Office We offer them
  • Varied, responsible work in a rapidly growing e-mobility environment
  • If suitable, a lot of development potential and exciting prospects
  • Large design possibilities with fast and short decision paths
  • Flexible working hours
  • Performance-based conditions of employment If you feel addressed, send us your complete application documents including letters of motivation via link (Apply now).
