Full description

Do you like to work on real products used daily by front office teams? Are you looking to gain hands on exposure to AI, analytics, and large-scale data platforms? UBS is looking for a Client360 Product Manager to

  • balance short term wins with long term modernization, helping evolve the broader Client360 and Distribution strategy
  • think about CRM not just as a tool, but as a product powered by clean data, strong models, intelligent automation, and AI ready foundations—surfacing insights directly into day-to-day workflows
