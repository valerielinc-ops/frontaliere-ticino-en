Main Tasks & Responsibilities

Engineering Project Support and Operation Readiness

  • Participate in new facility design and construction, including but not limited to SAT, IOPQ etc. to contribute to project delivery.
  • Support VI AM for the VI kits lifecycle management.
  • Participated in the implementation of VI operation readiness.
  • Support the initial development and optimization of VI processes.
  • Perform other duties as assigned.

Routine Operation

  • Ensure routine operation in compliance with Roche’s GMP/SHE and regulation requirements.
  • Performing the following operations: e.g. Vial Visual Inspection and VI kits handling.
  • Diagnos e and resolve equipment malfunctions promptly with the support of maintenance engineers to minimize downtime .
  • Execute regular manufacturing operations and GMP records filling.
  • Execute regular qualification activities: e.g. APS
  • Ensure data accuracy, integrity, and traceability in all VI actvities.
  • Implement cold chain management in the VI process.
  • Related IT system operations, such as SAP, MES.

Qualification & Experience

Education

  • Senior high school and technical School (Pharmaceutical / Biological is preferred) and above.

Job Required Competencies

  • 3 or more years’ operation experience in the injection pharmaceutical industry, MNC Bio-pharma preferred.
  • Experienced in strategic project of Visual Inspection including design, commissioning & qualification is preferred.
  • Strong knowledge of cGMP and regulations relevant to the pharmaceutical industry, especially in biological industry.
  • Fluent communications skills in English is preferred and the ability to effectively communicate, collaborate, and foster positive interactions with team members and stakeholders.
