Full description

Founded in Lausanne in 1843, the Asile des aveugles Foundation includes the Jules-Gonin Ophthalmic Hospital, university ophthalmology service, a research center, the Pedagogical Center for Visually Impaired Students (CPHV), social services, rehabilitation and low vision, and the Clair-Soleil medical-social institution. More than 650 employees are committed every day to visual health to provide diagnostic, treatment and support services to people affected in their vision.
