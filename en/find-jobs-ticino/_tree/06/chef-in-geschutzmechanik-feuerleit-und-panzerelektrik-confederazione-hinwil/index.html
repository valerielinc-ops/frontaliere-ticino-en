Put to the Point

You take over the central management and coordination of the assigned departments. Taking into account the high technical complexity of the systems, you ensure their professional maintenance and high availability.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21039
  • Place of work: Überlandstrasse 17, 8340 Hinwil

You can make this contribution

  • Overall responsibility for gun mechanics, fire control and armored electrics in the area of tracked and armored wheeled vehicles
  • Professional and personnel management of the various departments
  • Responsibility for ensuring efficient, safe and high-quality workshop operations
  • Analysis of damage pictures and decision-making as well as independent performance of diagnostic, maintenance and repair work
  • Planning and coordination of operational planning of external operations
  • Carrying out operations on modernly equipped external shooting facilities in various cantons

This makes you unique

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship as an automotive mechatronics technician with higher specialist training in the car industry or related professions
  • Comprehensive maintenance knowledge
  • Several years of leadership experience
  • Safe occurrence, assertiveness and a high load capacity
  • Clear communication and conflict capability
  • Solution-oriented negotiating skills

Your commitment to security and freedom

The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the materials, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3’500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
