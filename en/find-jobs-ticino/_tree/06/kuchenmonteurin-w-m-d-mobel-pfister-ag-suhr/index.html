Kitchen fitter (m/f/d)

100%

Suhr

By agreement

Share

We at pfister pursue a vision: We want to move our products into every Swiss home. We are seeking active support for this. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products in our range into the homes of our customers. Whether in one of the 18 branches or at the headquarters in Suhr: You can expect a unique environment, working with attractive products and, last but not least, appreciation and respect for your commitment. There are already around 1,400 people working at pfister today – will you join soon?

Your new tasks

  • Professional delivery and assembly of our attractive kitchen range in teams of two or three
  • In the team responsible for on-site assembly at our customers
  • Through optimal implementation of customer wishes, making living dreams a reality
  • Representation of the company to the outside world through professional appearance and solution-oriented working style
  • Completed apprenticeship as carpenter
  • Professional experience in kitchen assembly
  • Balanced and dynamic personality who can tackle, likes to travel and prefers to work independently
  • Good oral and written knowledge of German
  • Communicative personality that can quickly establish contact with our customers
