[Electro-Materiel SA]( )

Electro\-Materiel SA is the Swiss leader in the distribution of electrotechnical equipment. With an industry expertise centre "EM Industrie", lighting "EM Lumière" and photovoltaic and e\\-mobilité "EM Solar" distributed in 9 branches, more than 750 qualified employees contribute to the success of our company.

  • We offer an assortment of more than 250,000 items, specialist advice, high availability and fast delivery service to our professional customers. Electro\-Materiel SA is part of Rexel Group world leader.

Sales advisor 100%

As part of the development of our business in Romandie, Sion branch, we are looking for a

100% sales advisor

with experience in customer contact.

Description of position:

You are responsible for ordering, specialized technical advice and order processing. You process and follow orders and make clarifications from our suppliers. With commitment, you advise our customers over the phone on products, prices, delivery times and technical or organizational requests. Anyway, you're the link between our customers, our suppliers and internal services.

  • Qualifications:

We are looking for a person who can work as a team, stress-resistant and responsible, ideally with CFC installer training\-electrician and a few years of professional experience. You like to work independently and enjoy contact with customers. You have a sense of reflection and solid knowledge of MS Office. Your French, both oral and written, is impeccable, and your communication skills complement your profile.

Our offer:

In addition to an independent and diversified activity, a collegiate environment, a friendly team and attractive conditions of employment, including certified equal pay, await you. The benefits offered include at least 25 days of vacation, very good social benefits, individual development opportunities and much more. Are you interested in this exciting new mission and are you responding to our demands? In this case, we look forward to receiving your full application.
