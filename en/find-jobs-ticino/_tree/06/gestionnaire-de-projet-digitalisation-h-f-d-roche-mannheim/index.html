The Position This is what awaits you For our EMEA Customer Support Center in Mannheim we are looking for a Project Manager - Digitalization (m/f/d). In this role, you ensure that our customer service strategy is continuously developed and implemented proactively. You actively drive forward the digital transformation and successfully take our employees with you through targeted change management. In addition, You have the following main tasks:

  • You will take over the professional and independent management of projects, as well as the participation in large local and smaller international transformation initiatives.
  • You are responsible for the successful project implementation in terms of content, processes, resources, budgets and deadlines.
  • You agree Stay with all relevant stakeholders on a regular basis and advise them proactively.
  • You advance specific change and culture processes in customer support and ensure that employees are heard and involved.
  • You will develop modern project management standards and advise the project teams on the choice of the appropriate method (classic or agile).
  • You work closely with global and regional functions and represent the interests of the service in overarching projects.

You bring that with you A successfully completed degree (bachelor or master) in the field of economics, (business) informatics, industrial engineering or a comparable qualification. The following points round off your profile:

  • You have in-depth, multi-year (minimum 6-8 years) experience in project management and bring in-depth know-how in the operational implementation of digitization projects. (both in classic and agile setups)
  • Technical understanding: A strong high-level understanding of software architectures and AI-powered system integrations. You have a solid basic understanding of programming and can assess the feasibility of IT projects in a well-founded and goal-oriented manner.
  • Experience in the digitalization of business processes, ideally with a direct focus on customer-oriented functions.
  • Fluent German and English skills in words and writing for successful cooperation in our international environment.
  • A strategic mindset coupled with a pragmatic implementation competence, a high level of initiative and a passion to actively shape change.
