Full description

Introduction

For the Masses MP group of our Ebauches sector, we are looking for a (M/F) Group Manager, ready to follow the activity during its forwarding, on the Bulle site. The main task will be to supervise, animate and develop a production team in pairs.

Responsibilities

  • Ensure compliance with production plan, manufacturing standards and delivery commitments
  • Ensure product compliance with aesthetic, dimensional and functional requirements
  • Ensure strict compliance with management, traceability, safety and security rules for precious materials
  • Pilot the workshop performance according to the axes Safety, Quality, Costs, Timelines and Human Resources
  • Deploy and sustain continuous improvement approaches to optimize process fluxes, productivity and robustness
  • Organize daily team activity: burden sharing, resource allocation, priority management and risk monitoring
  • Animate production piloting rituals
  • Communicate objectives, results, gaps and action plans
  • Follow performance indicators
  • Ensure the application of standards to the post
  • Participate in the industrialization of new products through optimal production strategies
  • Supporting individual and collective change

Profile

  • Advanced SWC-type technical training supplemented by team driving training, federal patent, technician degree or equivalent training
  • Several years of experience in a precision production environment, including a proven experience in team management/framework, ideally in watchmaking, microtechnics, jewellery or precision metallurgy
  • Experience in the manufacture of watch components and/or the management of precious metals is a major asset
  • Mastering the fundamentals of production management: planning, scheduling, priority management and performance monitoring
  • Knowledge of the quality requirements associated with high added value components: visual control, dimensional, aesthetic and traceability
  • Good understanding of the manufacturing and finishing processes of watch components, especially for precious metals
  • Practice of continuous improvement tools
  • Current French; knowledge of English or another language is an advantage
  • Community leadership, ability to federate and provide a clear framework for the team
  • Rigor, integrity and a high sense of responsibility, essential for the management of precious materials
  • Organizational sense, responsiveness and decision-making capacity in a demanding environment
  • Clear, constructive and appropriate communication to various stakeholders
  • Spirit of analysis, pragmatism and orientation solutions
  • Ability to support change and advance workshop practices

Conclusion

We offer you a stimulating environment, excellent working conditions, flexible working time solutions and top social benefits.

Thank you for sending a complete application package (CV, cover letter, certificates and diplomas).
