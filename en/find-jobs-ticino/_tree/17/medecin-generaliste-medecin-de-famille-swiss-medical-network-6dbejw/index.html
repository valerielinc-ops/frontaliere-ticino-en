The VIVA project – an innovation in Switzerland As part of the VIVA project, the Arc Network is the first fully integrated health organization in Switzerland to offer its own insurance and care plan. VIVA is based on a modern vision of community-based medicine: a coordinated, continuous and patient-centred management that promotes collaboration among all actors in the health system. Accessible to the inhabitants of Jura and the Jura Arc, this model guarantees an optimal quality of care and a smooth and coherent health path. You ensure comprehensive and personalized patient care, including:

  • General medical consultations (diagnosis, treatment and follow-up)
  • Comprehensive health assessments and clinical evaluations
  • Longitudinal monitoring of patients in a comprehensive and preventive approach
  • Management of acute and chronic diseases
  • Rigorous maintenance and maintenance of medical records
  • Implementation of prevention (screening, vaccination, health advice)
  • Collaboration with other VIVA healthcare professionals
  • Coordination with specialists in multidisciplinary management
  • Participation in continuity and quality of care
  • Continuing training to maintain a high level of medical expertise
  • Diploma of general practitioner or family medicine recognized in Switzerland or in recognition (MEBEKO)
  • Experience in ambulatory medicine and first resort care
  • Interest in community medicine and integrated care models
  • Ability to work in teams and interdisciplinary networks
  • Excellent relational skills and listening skills
  • Mastery of essential French: German or German Swiss is an asset

We offer a varied and stimulating activity within a growing organisation, deeply rooted in its territory and population.

The Arc Network, which is firmly established in its region, is a major player in community care in the Jura Arc and its neighbouring regions. It ensures a comprehensive, coordinated and interdisciplinary management of the population at the heart of a rich and diverse territory. The network consists of two hospitals in Moutier and Saint-Imier and nine Medicentres. The provision of care is complemented by a vast regional ecosystem including relief services, two medical-social institutions, two radiology institutes, a physical and cognitive therapy centre and a hospital-at-home service. With nearly 1,300 employees, the Arc Network covers the outpatient and hospital needs of around 80,000 inhabitants, valuing regional wealth, human proximity and the quality of care. As part of the development of its Medicentres and future projects to extend and create new medical facilities in the region, we are looking for several: General practitioners / Family doctors
