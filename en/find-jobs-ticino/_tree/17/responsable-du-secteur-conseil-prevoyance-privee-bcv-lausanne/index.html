Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Put your managerial ambitions at the service of a healthy and sustainable performance by joining the canton of Vaud's first universal bank, one of the world's strongest banks, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Private Banking division, we are looking for a: Private Provident Consultant – Sector Manager Your Missions:

  • develop the sector as part of an established growth strategy to make it a reference in its area of activity,
  • promote and represent the Bank in the field of private provision (bank insurance, Epargne 3 and Free Pass foundations), by promoting inter-divisional and inter-departmental cross-selling,
  • develop and maintain a network of external relations (professional associations, partners, administrations, brokers, experts, etc.),
  • support the sales force in the field of private provision through participation in customer interviews and training of advisers,
  • be a main player in the Bank's strategic and operational projects related to private provision,
  • piloting the sector of about ten people at the managerial level: delineating objectives into concrete actions, optimizing the organisation of resources, mobilizing and supporting teams,
  • ensuring compliance with internal, legal and ethical rules on foresight,
  • actively monitor legal, judicial and policy developments in the field. Your profile
  • university or higher education (HES or equivalent),
  • experience in the conduct of a team of consultants and specialists,
  • experience in participating in Provident Foundation Boards,
  • excellent understanding of processes related to foresight, with good knowledge of the 2nd and/or 3rd pillar,
  • good relations, leadership and determination to develop new external and internal relationships,
  • excellent analytical, organizational and project management skills, critical mindset, result orientation to achieve business and business transformation challenges,
  • perfect mastery of French, another German or English language, an asset. If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
