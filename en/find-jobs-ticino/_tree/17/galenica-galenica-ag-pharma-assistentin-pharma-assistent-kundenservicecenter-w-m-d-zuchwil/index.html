Full description

Location: MediService AG, Ausserfeldweg 1, 4528 Zuchwil

Do you want to use your pharmaceutical expertise in a new environment? In our customer service center, you can advise customers by phone about medicines and health services. You support people as well as medical specialists and external partners throughout Switzerland competently and personally.

You can do that.

  • You will advise our customers by telephone on questions about medicines and health services and provide competent information.
  • You take orders, inform about products and services and ensure smooth processing.
  • You will competently clarify pharmaceutical questions in connection with doctor’s prescriptions and thus support doctors, patients and external partners throughout Switzerland.
  • With your empathetic and professional communication, you create trust among our customers.
  • You will also keep track of challenging situations and contribute to reliable advice.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed your training as a pharmaceutical assistant / pharmaceutical assistant EFZ or Expert / specialist Pharmacy EFZ and bring professional experience.
  • You enjoy the telephone consultation and convince with a high willingness to provide services and clear expressiveness.
  • You work reliably and structured and stay friendly and focused even under time pressure.
  • You have German at native language level and use it safely in telephone consultation. Ideally, you will also have French or Italian skills.

We fill this position without external personnel service providers.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks holiday, with the possibility to buy up to 10 extra days of holiday or take unpaid holiday
  • Pension fund with advantageous premium distribution - approx. 2/3 are taken over by us
  • Various discounts in our pharmacies and other top brands
  • Expand competences through funded and supported training and internal development opportunities
  • Family friendly with 16 weeks of maternity leave (100% of wages) and 20 days of paternity leave (100% of wages), as well as participation in children's funds
  • Registered shares at preferential prices - not only engage professionally in Galenica, but also invest

Benefits may vary slightly depending on area/place

About us

Individual pharmaceutical care Mediservice is a company in the Galenica network and supports people with chronic or rare illnesses through specialized pharmaceutical care - at home, but also by phone and video. As employees of Mediservice, we have the task of making the lives of our patients at home easier. Furthermore, we deliver complex and expensive medicines, offer various specialized services and impart health knowledge as well as tips and tricks for everyday use with medicines. Mediservice employs pharmacists, nurses and logistics staff. You make your very personal contribution to our patients to increase their independence and improve their quality of life.
