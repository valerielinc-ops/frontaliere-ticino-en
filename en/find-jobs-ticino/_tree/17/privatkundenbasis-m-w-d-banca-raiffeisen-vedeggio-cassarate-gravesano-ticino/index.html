Full description

The Bank Raiffeisen Vedeggio Cassarate, a Bank strongly rooted in the territory and oriented to local customers, is looking, for its network of agencies, a personality with a strong sense of responsibility and oriented to customers. You are responsible for the management in accordance with the strategy and development of our Private Base Client (CPB).

You will act as a competent and responsible advisor and take care of the recognition and exploitation of potential in contact with customers, its development and the transition to the target segment of Individual Customer Consulting.

You will take an approach to global advice and assistance and focus on the stages of life and important events of customers taking into account the specific offer of the segment.

You will actively contribute to the position of Raiffeisen as a competent partner on Finance (in particular Savings, Investments and Security).

What are you waiting for?

  • Global and oriented advice to the needs of private customers based on means of payment, savings products, investments and security, in compliance with the strategic, regulatory and objectives of the Bank
  • Recognition and development of customer potential through proactive advice
  • Targeted acquisition of new customers

What are you offering?

  • Degree of employee/business, preferably branch bank and professional maturity
  • Certification Adviser to private customers SAQ or availability to carry out it
  • Excellent computer knowledge and digitalization orientation
  • Intractive personality, with good relational and customer-oriented skills and sales
  • Ability to work independently and in teams
  • Active commitment to team success
  • Banking experience in a similar position, market knowledge and integration in the territory of Vedeggio Cassarate are preferential title
  • Domicile in Ticino
