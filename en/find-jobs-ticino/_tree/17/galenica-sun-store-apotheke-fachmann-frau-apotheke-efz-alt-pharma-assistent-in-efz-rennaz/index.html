Workplace: Sun Store Apotheke, Riviera Shopping Centre, Route du Pré-de-la-Croix 18, 1847 Rennaz

Are you interested in learning space as a CFC Pharmacy Assistant? Youpi!

Sun Store is part of Switzerland's largest pharmacy network and supports people on a daily basis on all health and wellness issues. Thanks to our numerous outlets throughout Switzerland, we are close to our customers and offer personalized and competent advice.

As a member of the Galenica network, Sun Store works with the strongest partners in the Swiss health market. Galenica is the first fully integrated health service provider in Switzerland. Our talents are working to develop solutions for innovative and safe health care in the future. The individual needs of our clients are at the heart of our commitment

  • Sale of all products of pharmacy assortment

Rejoice for the following benefits

  • Mobile subscription and smartphone at attractive prices
  • and much more...
