Place

Bellinzona, CH

Company

SBB What we offer you. In this position you will represent the interests of the client in complex infrastructure projects in the field of technical installations. Your operating area will include railway stations, galleries, technical premises and buildings. You will take the direction of technical projects with clear objectives. Management will be based on your technical skills and attention to interdisciplinary collaboration. A motivated team awaits you with a broad technical background, which will support you in your insertion and offer you a diverse and responsible work environment. With the "Work Smart" model, you can organize your working day in a flexible way: in the office, in smart working or traveling. The Stage will have a determined duration of 1 year. What you can move.

  • Represent the client's interests in all phases of the project: from design to contract, to realization and commissioning.
  • Ensure the achievement of the project objectives in terms of cost, time, quality, security and functionality, managing risks and opportunities in a timely manner.
  • Coordinate and manage planners, contractors and specialists, promoting interdisciplinary collaboration and effective interdependence management.
  • Check compliance with the rules, directives, legal provisions and requirements of the client during all phases of the project.
  • Manage contracts, changes and complaints (claim management) transparently and in accordance with the project objectives.
  • Apply "classic" and/or "agile" project management methods, adapting the partial objectives to the needs of the project.

What you bring with you.

  • Interdisciplinary work and promote a positive work environment.
  • You have strong analytical, organizational and communication skills, and act independently, structured and oriented to solutions.
  • You have gained experience in managing technical projects and are familiar with SIA performance models and contracts, as well as with the regulations of electrical and infrastructural systems.
  • You have a university degree (SUP/University/Politechnical) or higher education (SSS, federal certificate or federal diploma) in the branch of electronics or building technology.
  • Municipalities fluently in Italian or German, with good knowledge of a second national language (at least level B2).
  • Want to grow professionally and contribute to the success of projects and team.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
