Elsa Group SA, an expert in dairy products and cheese specialities, offers a range of around 2000 innovative and high-quality products, including a complete range of vegetable protein products, which are part of ambitious sustainability goals.

# Mansioni

  • Pilot projects from A to Z (burdens, planning, coordination, monitoring)
  • Manage project budgets, timelines and resources
  • Select, coordinate and track external providers
  • Ensure compliance with safety, quality, cost and timeliness requirements by external providers
  • Coordinate installation and commissioning
  • Ensure safety on construction sites and limit the impact of work on production
  • Organize the handover towards operation and maintenance
  • Provide documentation, training and implementation standards

Required

  • Initial or continuing training completed: electrician, supplemented by a patent in the electrical field
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss electrical standards and industrial electrical installations

  • Organizational sense, rigour, autonomy and ability to manage pressure and prioritize, with strong results orientation, safety, quality and performance
  • Excellent communication skills, teamwork and adaptable leadership
