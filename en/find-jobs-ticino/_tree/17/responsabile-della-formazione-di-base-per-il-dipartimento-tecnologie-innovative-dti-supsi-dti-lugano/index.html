# Head of Basic Training for the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI)

The University of Applied Sciences and Arts of Southern Switzerland (SUPSI) is offering a position as

Head of Basic Training

at the

Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI)

based in Lugano. The selected candidate will be a member of the departmental management and will assume strategic, organizational, and administrative responsibility for the department's basic training.

The position will be

Associate or Full Professor

Lecturer or Lecturer-Researcher

, depending on the selected candidate's profile, qualifications, and academic maturity. Employment rate: 80–100%. Entry into service September 1, 2027, or a date to be agreed upon.

Duties

  • Participate, as a member of the DTI management, in the definition and implementation of strategic directions and departmental decisions.
  • Define and implement the Basic Education development strategy, consistent with departmental and institutional guidelines.
  • Coordinate the Bachelor's and Master's degree programs, ensuring their quality, attractiveness, and educational innovation.
  • Ensure the management and development of the heads of the degree programs and the Basic Education Secretariat of the DTI, promoting the enhancement of skills and a culture of collaboration.
  • Ensure the management and continuous improvement of the administrative processes of Basic Education.
  • Ensure the planning and coordination of training activities and allocated resources.
  • Collaborate with the SUPSI Basic Education Directorate in planning activities and implementing institutional guidelines.
  • Represent the Department on the SUPSI Basic Education Commission and collaborate on cross-departmental projects.
  • Participate in departmental and interdepartmental working groups, contributing to the harmonization and improvement of training and administrative processes.
  • Promote relationships with the professional world and synergies between education, applied research, and innovation.

Requirements

  • Master's degree or equivalent in a field relevant to the Department's activities; a doctorate is an advantage.
  • Proven experience in university teaching and managing educational activities, preferably combined with recent professional experience in other university or corporate settings.
  • Solid academic record, with proven experience in research and development in a university context.
  • Experience managing academic and/or administrative staff, leading teams, and coordinating complex organizational processes.
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss university system, preferably of the Universities of Applied Sciences.
  • Leadership skills, strategic vision, planning, and change management.
  • Strong interpersonal and organizational skills, and an aptitude for interdisciplinary and interdepartmental collaboration.
  • Focus on quality, innovation, and continuous improvement.
  • Teaching qualification or willingness to obtain one, in accordance with Directive 6C of the Internal Directives for SUPSI Contract Personnel.
  • Excellent knowledge of Italian and English; knowledge of another national language is an advantage.
  • Residence in the Canton of Ticino or willingness to relocate there within an agreed-upon period after taking up the position, given the management responsibilities and the need to ensure an adequate presence in the area.

What we offer

  • The position of Head of Basic Training at the DTI has a term of four years and is renewable.
  • Responsibility as a member of the Departmental Management, with an active role in defining and implementing development strategies for professional university education, in a dynamic and innovative environment.
  • Competitive salary, in line with Swiss standards and the candidate's experience.

We Value Diversity

In all its activities, SUPSI guarantees an inclusive, accessible, and respectful study and work environment, with the aim of offering high-quality university education to all. More information on diversity and inclusion is available at this link.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
