Head of Basic Education for the Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI)

The University of Applied Sciences and Arts of Southern Switzerland (SUPSI) is inviting applications for a position as

Head of Basic Education at the

Department of Innovative Technologies (DTI) based in Lugano. The successful candidate will be a member of the Departmental Management and will assume strategic, organisational and administrative responsibility for the Department's Basic Education. The position is expected to be classified as

Associate or Full Professor ,

Lecturer or Lecturer-Researcher , depending on the profile, qualifications and level of academic seniority of the successful candidate. Employment rate 80–100%. Start date 1 September 2027 or by agreement.

Responsibilities

  • Participate, as a member of DTI Management, in defining and implementing strategic priorities and departmental decisions.
  • Define and implement the development strategy for Basic Education, in line with departmental and institutional priorities.
  • Coordinate the Bachelor’s and Master’s programme offering, ensuring quality, attractiveness and innovation in teaching.
  • Ensure the management and development of the degree programme heads and the DTI Basic Education Secretariat, promoting the recognition of expertise and a culture of collaboration.
  • Ensure the management and continuous improvement of the administrative processes of Basic Education.
  • Ensure the planning and coordination of educational activities and allocated resources.
  • Collaborate with the SUPSI Basic Education Directorate in planning activities and implementing institutional priorities.
  • Represent the Department on the SUPSI Basic Education Commission and collaborate on cross-cutting projects
  • Participate in departmental and interdepartmental working groups, contributing to the harmonisation and improvement of educational and administrative processes.
  • Promote relations with the professional world and synergies between education, applied research and innovation.

Requirements

  • University Master's degree or equivalent in a field relevant to the Department's activities; a doctorate is preferred.
  • Proven experience in university teaching and managing educational activities, preferably complemented by recent professional experience gained in other university or corporate settings.
  • Strong academic record, with proven experience in research and development in a university setting.
  • Experience managing academic and/or administrative staff, leading teams and coordinating complex organisational processes.
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss higher education system, preferably of universities of applied sciences.
  • Ability to lead, think strategically, plan and manage change.
  • Excellent interpersonal and organisational skills, and an aptitude for interdisciplinary and interdepartmental collaboration.
  • Commitment to quality, innovation and continuous improvement.
  • Teaching qualification or willingness to obtain one, in accordance with Directive 6C of the Internal Directives for SUPSI contract staff.
  • Excellent knowledge of Italian and English; knowledge of another national language is preferred.
  • Residence in the Canton of Ticino or willingness to relocate there within a period agreed upon when taking up the position, in view of the managerial responsibilities and the need to ensure an adequate presence in the area.

What we offer

  • The position of Head of Basic Education at the DTI is for a four-year term and is renewable.
  • A position of responsibility as a member of the Departmental Management, with an active role in defining and implementing development strategies for professional higher education in a dynamic and innovative environment.
  • Competitive salary, in line with Swiss standards and the candidate's experience.

We value diversity In all its activities, SUPSI guarantees an inclusive, accessible and respectful study and work environment that embraces all forms of diversity, with the aim of offering everyone high-quality university education.
