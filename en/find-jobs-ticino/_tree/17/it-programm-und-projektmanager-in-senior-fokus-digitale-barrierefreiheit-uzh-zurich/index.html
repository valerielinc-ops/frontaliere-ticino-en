Central computer science

Job as of 1.1.2027 or by agreement

Are you interested in working with us to make the digital future of the University of Zurich accessible? We – Central Computer Science – provide IT services for students, researchers, employees as well as institutes and faculties of the University of Zurich. As an IT Program & Project Manager: Senior with a focus on digital accessibility, you take over the management of complex and strategically relevant IT programs and projects and actively shape digital accessibility at UZH. You work together with different stakeholders within and outside of Central Computer Science.

Tasks

  • Program & Project Management: You lead complex, strategically relevant IT programs and projects with a focus on digital accessibility and ensure the achievement of the project goals in terms of time, costs, quality and resources. You will take over the professional leadership of the program and project teams
  • Project management: You are responsible for requirements and resource management, reporting, stakeholder management as well as quality and risk management and ensure timely and demand-oriented communication with the relevant stakeholders. You also carry out procurements in compliance with legal and internal requirements
  • Digital accessibility: You will advise on WCAG compliance, compliance and regulatory requirements, you will introduce the continuous e-accessibility improvement process, develop it further and ensure its operation, define and maintain standards, guidelines and process models, and implement monitoring and reporting tools
  • Accessibility Reviews & Audits: You lead accessibility reviews and audits by controlling their implementation and thus support the systematic improvement of digital accessibility
  • PMO & project portfolio: You work in the Project Management Office (PMO) with a focus on digital accessibility, design processes and interfaces together with the project leaders and the PMO project management methodology and integrate e-accessibility topics into project portfolio management
  • Consulting & networking: You involve relevant offices and committees of the UZH and support and advise project leaders on questions of digital accessibility, in addition, you contribute to the improvement of IT-wide project management through standards, templates, guidelines and training

Profile

  • Completed degree in business informatics or equivalent education
  • Further education in digital accessibility and readiness for continuous professional development in the relevant topics.
  • Many years of experience managing IT programs and projects in a complex environment
  • Demonstrated accessibility competence and practical experience
  • You are assertive and at the same time connecting, communicative and goal-oriented
  • You are characterized by high personal responsibility, initiative, performance orientation and integrity
  • You have fluent knowledge of German and very good knowledge of English in word and writing
