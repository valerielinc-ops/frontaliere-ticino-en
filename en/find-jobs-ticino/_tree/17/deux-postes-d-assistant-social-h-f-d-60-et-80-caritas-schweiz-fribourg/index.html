Full description

Just act. In Switzerland and in almost 20 countries on four continents. Get involved with us to realize the vision of a solidarity society, as Our Migration & Integration Department in Western Switzerland – a Caritas Switzerland office in Fribourg employing about 100 employees – is responsible for the support and integration of statutory refugees in the canton of Fribourg. On a cantonal mandate, we welcome new arrivals to our Matran home, help them find their own housing, provide personal and material assistance according to the standards of social assistance and support them in their efforts towards professional and social integration. In addition, we run an active community interpretation service in several French cantons as well as a listening and counselling service against racism for the inhabitants of the canton of Fribourg. The Social Assistance Service – composed of about 20 assistants and social workers managed by three team leaders and led by a service manager – is responsible for providing social assistance to refugees who are not financially self-sufficient. The teams follow them individually, draw up monthly budgets and provide them with material and personal assistance in accordance with cantonal law and the principle of subsidiarity. They also support them in their administrative processes and assess their integration needs. The Service works closely with Finance & Administration and Reception, Housing and Infrastructure, Integration and Legal Services. In order to complete our team, we are looking for two social assistants, one at 60% and the other at 80%. Your tasks

  • Welcoming, accompanying and advising statutory refugees on social assistance
  • Establish monthly budgets
  • Ensuring the principle of subsidiarity
  • Collaborate with a multidisciplinary team and within the external network
  • Active participation in symposia, intervisions and supervisions
  • Conduct home visits
  • Joint case management

Our requirements

  • Training of social worker or deemed equivalent
  • Knowledge and experience of the Social Assistance Act (LASoc)
  • Administrative and editorial powers
  • Excellent ability to prioritize tasks and manage stress
  • Use of IT tools
  • A sense of collaboration, negotiation and teamwork
  • Empathy and ability to accompany people in vulnerable situations
  • Mastery of German in addition to the desired French
  • Cross-cultural and/or affinity expertise in asylum and refugee matters

The workplace is Fribourg. Entry into office: 1 January 2027 or to be agreed. Caritas offers interesting hiring conditions and an open working environment in an international context. For the active development and implementation of digital transformation, we rely on employees with a digital and agile mindset who are willing to participate in the processes of change.
