Taxist

For you, taxation is not just tax returns, but above all a tool for advice and strategy? Here you can use your expertise!

For our client, a trustee who is firmly established in French-speaking Switzerland and has several offices, we are looking for an experienced tax expert at 80–100%. Active in the areas of taxation, auditing, accounting and business consulting, this structure supports a diverse clientele of individuals, SMEs and corporate groups in their financial and tax issues.

Your duties with your new employer

  • You advise a client of individuals and businesses on complex tax issues
  • You are developing tax planning strategies and accompanying business restructuring
  • You prepare and supervise the tax returns of natural and legal persons
  • You analyze tax decisions and follow up on complaints and appeals procedures
  • You write legal notices, tax studies and rulings
  • You are involved in the development and retention of the client portfolio

You offer to your new employer

  • You have a higher education in taxation, ideally supplemented by an expert degree in taxation.
  • You benefit from several years of experience in tax board, trustee or law firm
  • You have strong knowledge of the taxation of natural and legal persons
  • You are French mother tongue or have excellent command of this language, both oral and written
  • You are ideally fluent in German and/or English, which is an asset.
  • You stand out for your autonomy, rigor and ease in customer relations

You can also enjoy the following benefits

  • Parking spaces available
  • An opportunity for occasional telework
  • Various tax files, ranging from current taxation to more complex issues
  • Important autonomy in the management of your mandates
  • Opportunities for continuing training

We have awakened your interest? So apply today!

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed.
