Full description

The EVAM (Vaudian institution for the reception of migrants) is a major player in the voodoo public service. With more than 1,000 committed collaborators and an annual budget of 360 million francs, we ensure the reception, support and integration of migrants in the canton of Vaud.

Every day, our teams work with openness and responsibility to address key social issues. Joining EVAM means putting your skills at the service of a human mission and of public utility, in a stimulating, diverse and constantly evolving professional environment.
