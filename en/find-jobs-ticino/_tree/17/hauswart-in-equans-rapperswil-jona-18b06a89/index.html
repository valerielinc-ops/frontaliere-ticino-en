Full description

We are looking for a service-oriented, committed and motivated personality in the Rapperswil-Jona region immediately or by agreement.

You can move that with us.

In this versatile function, you manage several objects on site and are the first point of contact for our customers. Your tasks include:

  • Support and supervision of all technical installations in the building
  • Planning and organisation of repair, service and maintenance work
  • Monitoring, monitoring and control of the work carried out by external service providers
  • Support for projects and optimizations in cooperation with internal and external bodies
  • Implementation of client orders
  • Performing minor repair and maintenance work on equipment and infrastructure
  • Performing green and ambient works
  • Complete administrative tasks as part of your activity

That's what makes you.

You describe yourself as a service provider with innermost conviction. Positive feedback on your performance motivates you and spurs you on to further top performances. You also bring the following:

  • Completed training as a specialist EFZ or basic craft training
  • Several years of professional experience in building maintenance are advantageous
  • Place of residence in the region
  • Independent, structured working methods and a high sense of responsibility
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Category B driving licence
  • Readiness for Picetting
  • Good repute without penal and enforcement records

That's what we offer you.

  • An exciting, responsible task with a lot of creative freedom
  • An experienced team with an open corporate culture
  • Contemporary employment conditions and good social benefits
