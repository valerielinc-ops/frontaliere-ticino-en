Full description

Post "Infirmary, RFSM Marsens - Unit Aubépine" at RFSM - Fribourg mental health network, Marsens (FR). The RFSM is the mental health centre of the canton of Fribourg: adult psychiatry, children/adolescents, elderly people, addictions.

Who are we?

The Friburg Network for Mental Health (RFSM) is attached to the Directorate of Health and Social Affairs (DSAS). The RFSM is a centre of medical, nursing and psychosocial skills specialized in mental health.

Your missions

For a consecutive or agreed CDD

The Aubépine unit is a hospital unit on the Marsens site. It accommodates elderly people with mental health, including major neurocognitive disorders (demences). People in crisis and/or decompensated SCPD, come there for a review leading to a diagnosis, an adaptation of medication treatment or a period of observation with a view to a reorientation in a place of life adapted to their needs.

  • Basic/Nursing, Somatic/Medical/Delegated Care
  • Good knowledge in the area of elderly care, dementia and decompensated SCPD.
  • Participation in the clinical activity of the service (following care processes aimed at the quality of life of the person, taking into account their values, in collaboration with the family)
  • Clinical evaluation of mental state-physical examination
  • Analysis of professional practices, support for quality and continuity
