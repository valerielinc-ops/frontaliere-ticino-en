Full description

Type of Contract: CDI Rate: 30 per cent Start date: July 1, 2026 or to be agreed

This function plays a key role in the safety of actients, collaborators and visitors. Through your expertise, you actively contribute to reducing infectious risks and promoting a strong institutional culture of hygiene and quality of care.

Your missions

  • Define, deploy and evaluate infection prevention and control protocols and procedures
  • Analyse epidemiological data to guide concrete and measurable actions
  • Support professionals on a daily basis by strengthening their skills and vigilance in the face of infectious risks
  • Coordinate the actions of the health and infection prevention focal points
  • Prepare and host HPCI sessions with Management
  • Leveraging practices through targeted audits and recommendations for improvement
  • Participate in the management of risk situations (epidemics, infectious alerts) and coordinate necessary actions
  • Ensure regulatory and scientific oversight in relation to cantonal and federal recommendations
  • Participate in the safety and quality commissions of the RSBJ
  • Ensure compliance with hygiene standards in institutional projects (work, equipment, specific processes)

Your profile

  • Tertiary degree: Bachelor of Nursing or equivalent
  • Federal Diploma of EPIAS
  • Several years of experience in acute care
  • Interprofessional coordination
  • Self-reliance and organizational sense
  • Computer skills

Entry into office: 1 July 2026 or to be agreed Full application file to be sent to: jobs@rsbj.ch
