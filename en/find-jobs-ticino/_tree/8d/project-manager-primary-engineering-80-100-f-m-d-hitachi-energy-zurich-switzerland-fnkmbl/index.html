Full description

The opportunity

We are looking for a motivated and skilled Project Manager Primary Engineering to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will lead the technical planning and execution of gas-insulated switchgear projects and ensure that engineering deliverables are completed on time, within scope and according to the required quality standards.

How you will make an impact

  • Plan and manage the Primary Engineering scope in GIS customer projects, including cost, schedule and scope of delivery
  • Coordinate technical solutions with customers and internal stakeholders
  • Prepare the technical detailing of the high-voltage part of gas-insulated switchgear
  • Create the required documentation for ordering, fabrication, assembly and customer approval
  • Order and configure project-specific material
  • Manage technical communication channels internally and externally
  • Work on quality cases and support improvement processes
  • Provide technical support during manufacturing, installation and commissioning until completion of on-site assembly
  • Travel occasionally for customer meetings or site visits

Your background

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical or Mechanical Engineering or a related technical field
  • Several years of relevant experience in project engineering, ideally with gas-insulated switchgear or high-voltage systems
  • Good understanding of engineering project execution, including scope, schedule, cost, quality, documentation and material ordering
  • Experience with CAD tools such as Creo or AutoCAD, as well as SAP and Microsoft Office
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to identify risks, clarify technical issues and drive solutions
  • Experience in coordinating internal and external stakeholders in an international environment
  • Ability to work effectively within an international team environment and collaborate with other departments
  • Clear and structured communication skills, with confidence in technical discussions with customers and internal stakeholders
  • Fluent in English
  • German or another language is an advantage
