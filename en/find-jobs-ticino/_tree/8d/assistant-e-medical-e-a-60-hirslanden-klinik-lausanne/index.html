Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Clinique Cecil Permanent

Reference Number: 75143

The Hirslanden Group is Switzerland's largest medical network. We offer all individuals, regardless of their situation, high-level individual medical care to improve their quality of life - from birth to old age and from prevention to cure. As part of our corporate strategy Together We Care, our ultimate goal is to improve the quality of life of people who trust us.

MAIN TASKS

Within an oncology and surgery department, your tasks will be:

  • Support telephone calls; act as an interface for the entire service
  • Managing the agenda of three doctors
  • Preparation of medical records for consultations and billing
  • To welcome patients upon arrival on the first floor, to present the service, the contact persons, as well as the health care
  • Welcoming and responding to physician and visitor requests
  • Perform technical gestures (patient entry, blood test, injection, etc.)
  • Manage mail
  • Manage Service Orders (Papetry and Pharmacy)
  • Participation in maintaining a clean working environment

YOUR PROFILE

  • Medical assistant CFC with confirmed experience in a similar position
  • Knowledge of Mediway
  • Experience in near patient care an asset
  • Excellent mastery of MS Office IT tools
  • Customer orientation, sense of service, communication and interpersonal skills
  • Supporting teamwork and a high sense of responsibility
  • Autonomous, capable of performing multiple tasks at once and good stress management in a dynamic environment

WE OFFER YOU

In addition to an attractive workstation in the heart of Lausanne, you will integrate a dynamic team that looks forward to welcoming you into a human-sized structure.

You will benefit from attractive conditions of employment with, among other things, opportunities for continuing training and development throughout your career with us, as well as social benefits and other interesting benefits.

For an optimal balance between work and private life, we offer our employees various contractual models, including part-time and FLEXible contracts. There is also the possibility to enjoy up to 7 weeks of holiday thanks to our offer "Congé-Plus".

In addition, employees working in uniform shall be granted 4 days of additional annual leave in proportion to the attendance time.

See all the conditions of employment and employee benefits by clicking Benefits at the Cecil Clinic
