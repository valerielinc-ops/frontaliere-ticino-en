Full description

BMS Building Materials Suisse is a wholesaler of building materials for shell and interior construction. Our group of companies includes the renowned specialists Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced experts advise, support and inspire customers from the construction industry as well as private customers at around 110 locations throughout Switzerland. Close to everyday construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Quick, easy and competent.

Construction air or office life? With us you get both!

We are looking for 1. January 2027 or by appointment a dedicated personality for our location in Wil (SG), who is not only safely travelling by truck, but also keeps an overview in the transport scheduling. You like to be on the street, take on board and take over the deputy of our dispatcher if necessary? Then you are exactly right with us!

Task area

  • Delivery and collection of our products as well as orders from suppliers throughout German-speaking Switzerland
  • Loading and unloading the truck with the aid of the truck crane
  • Caring, maintenance and maintenance of the vehicle
  • Compliance with all legal regulations, in particular working hours and rest periods, traffic and safety regulations
  • Deputy of the dispatcher during his absence
  • Planning, coordination and monitoring of daily transports within the framework of the deputy

Profile

If your heart beats faster at the tasks described above and you If you have the following skills, you are our desired candidate:

  • Valid C/E driving licence and forklift
  • Driver qualification certificate (CZV)
  • Experience in handling crane vehicles
  • Experience in transport management or in the planning and coordination of transports
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Good geographical knowledge of German-speaking Switzerland
  • Responsible, independent and flexible working methods
  • Professional and customer-friendly appearance
  • IT affinity, especially when dealing with tablet-based transport management systems (TMS) and ERP applications

We offer

  • a challenging, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where the emphasis is on active cooperation and at the same time self-employment
  • intensive training is a program, whereby our new colleagues can benefit from product and system training within the company
  • Support our employees in their professional and personal development
  • No shift and weekend use (42.5 hour week), compensation of the hours
  • Basic wage gross x 13
  • 5 weeks holiday (6 weeks from 50 years of age) and holiday shopping up to max. 10 days, additional benefits such as an excellently financed independent pension fund and discounts on our complete product range as well as external premium benefits

We look forward to your complete application documents in electronic form.
