Put to the Point

Have you completed your apprenticeship as a computer scientist and want to start professionally? The ICT Warrior Academy is the Army’s competence center for civilian education and training in ICT and cyber. Here you have the unique opportunity to let off steam in the most modern IT landscape in Switzerland for 12 months and get trained to become an IT crack. At the same time, you enjoy all the advantages of a permanent position at the Cyber command and have the opportunity to build a career with us in the long term after passing the exam. Want to know more about the course? Then register for a virtual information event at www.armee.ch/ict-warrior-academy. Employing the Swiss Army makes sense and brings many advantages. Convince yourself of Army.ch/advantages. Women are underrepresented – your application is especially welcome!

  • Date of entry: 01.03.2027
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-19697
  • Place of work: Stauffacherstrasse 65, 3003 Bern

You can make this contribution

  • Learn how to use modern data center technologies (including Docker, Kubernetes, Openshift, Ansible) and become familiar with complex network, server and storage solutions and automation
  • Get to know the automation of Windows and Linux platforms and the use of system management and monitoring tools
  • Localize and fix hardware and software malfunctions and performance issues
  • A number of German-language basic training courses in network and server technology are completed (partly with recognized industry certificates)
  • Get to know the basic principles of cyber security and understand how Switzerland protects itself against cyberattacks
  • Develop and operate solutions in practical applications (DevSecOps) in order to apply what has been learned directly

This makes you unique

  • Completed training as a computer scientist/in EFZ in the area of platform development/application development/business informatics with 2-3 years of functionally relevant work experience, alternatively maturity or comparable training resp. Practical experience
  • Enthusiasm for complex systems from modern hardware and software as well as basic knowledge in the areas of network, server, storage, operating systems (Windows/Unix/Linux), virtualization, scripting and software development
  • Initial professional experience in the area of operations of large complex IT environments as well as in the use of system management and monitoring tools are advantageous
  • Willingness to learn, enthusiasm, good comprehension, "out-of-the-box" thinking and fun in teamwork

We offer this

  • We are committed to the success model Switzerland and work for the benefit of the population.
  • Thanks to equal opportunities, we develop our skills and bring in different perspectives.
  • Healthy at work We support and advise our employees in the field of physical and mental health.

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
