Full description

The Quartier LôDzé is looking for a 100% (M/F) Public Bath Supervisor to strengthen its teams. A true ambassador or ambassador of the place, you embody the customer experience on the ground and transform every visit into an immersive, sensory and memorable moment. Both operational, organized and endowed with excellent relational meaning, you supervise the daily operation of the baths, accompany your teams and develop activities and experiences related to well-being.

Your missions

  • Welcome guests with warmth, professionalism and authenticity, present the concept of baths and accompany them in their journey.
  • Create a lively, immersive and welcoming atmosphere, and ensure the general satisfaction of the clientele.
  • Manage claims and sensitive situations with diplomacy.
  • To ensure the day-to-day operations of the reception: reservations, receipts, cash closes, vouchers, subscriptions and forms.
  • Develop sales of complementary services and products and customer loyalty.
  • Supervise the smooth daily operation of the baths: atmosphere (light, music, atmosphere), cleanliness, hygiene, safety and comfort of the spaces.
  • Regularly monitor facilities, report anomalies and coordinate technical interventions.
  • Follow the management of linen and the availability of consumables and equipment.
  • Plan and coordinate external courses, activities and interventions, as well as collaboration with stakeholders and providers.
  • Design and organize well-being events and experiences (sound baths, thematic experiences, yoga, pilates, etc.) and participate in their promotion.
  • Monitor performance, sales and statistics, and propose ideas to develop activity.
  • Develop planning, mentor, train and support the team on a daily basis, including the integration of new employees.

Profile sought

  • Experience confirmed in the field of well-being, baths, spa or hotel.
  • An experience in team reception, reception or supervision is an important asset.
  • Excellent relational skills and developed sense of hospitality.
  • Careful presentation, positive energy and ease in interactions.
  • Organizational sense, autonomy and ability to take initiatives.
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously and prioritize emergencies.
  • Ability to mentor, motivate and unite a team.
  • Facilities with computer tools, reservation software and cashier systems.
  • Commercial sense and interest in sales development.
  • Team spirit, flexibility and orientation solutions.
  • Interest in the world of well-being and sensory experiences.
  • Flexible schedules, including mornings, evenings and weekends.

We offer you

  • Reduction for employees in the various outlets
  • 5 weeks of holidays, 6 holidays
  • Salary according to applicable CLC and 13th month
  • Dynamic and varied working environment

We will only examine the complete files (CV, cover letter, work certificates, etc.) of the candidates corresponding to the profile sought.
