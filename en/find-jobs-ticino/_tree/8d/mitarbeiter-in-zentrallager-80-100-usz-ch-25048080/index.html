Full description

Get involved and bring movement to our logistics! In a motivated team, you ensure that everything is in the right place at the right time – with responsibility, precision and the latest technology. >The University Hospital Zurich is one of the largest hospitals in Switzerland. Our more than 8,600 employees and around 700 trainees work every day for the well-being of our patients.

Tasks

  • Order picking from manual and automated storage zones
  • Warehouse organisation in SAP MM/WMS
  • Annual cut-off date inventory
  • Checking and recording goods incoming in SAP MM
  • Processing of returns
  • Packaging and shipment of national and international repair shipments
  • Active storage organisation (package management, replenishment, professional storage, MHD control)
  • Provision of the USZ transport vehicles according to tour plan
  • Loading and unloading of trucks for replenishment and return
  • Ensuring internal tracking

Requirements

  • Several years of professional experience in the field of warehouse logistics
  • Completed vocational training logistician* in EFZ
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Forklift test
  • Very good knowledge of SAP MM / WMS
  • Precise and precise working methods
  • Quality-conscious work

We offer you a versatile task in an advanced working environment, which is characterized by high quality orientation, independence and a sense of responsibility. A motivated and committed team supports you in all respects. As a modern employer, we apply exemplary and respectful management principles.
