Looking for reinforcement: Electrical installer (m/f/d)

As of now or by agreement

About Bergstrom Elektro GmbH

Since 2022 we as Bergstrom Elektro GmbH stand for quality, reliability and future-oriented solutions “Around energy”. Our success is based on our strong team. We believe in working together at eye level, where every opinion counts and real teamwork is paramount.

Your main tasks

  • Independent execution of electrical installations in the areas of high and low current (new construction, conversion, service, photovoltaics)
  • Commissioning, maintenance and servicing of electrical equipment
  • Troubleshooting and professional troubleshooting
  • Competent contact for our customers on site

Your profile

  • Electrical Installer training EFZ (or equivalent)
  • Reliable, clean and independent work
  • Joy of craftsmanship and direct customer contact
  • Advantageous driving licence Cat B

What we offer you (our benefits)

Your commitment is important to us – which is why we offer you attractive framework conditions:

  • Business vehicle: Modern equipped company vehicle for your missions.
  • Good remuneration: Performance-based and fair wage that values your commitment.
  • Attractive Incentives and bonuses for extraordinary services.
  • Individual training opportunities to promote your career.
  • A family team, flat hierarchies and a working atmosphere at eye level.

Ready for the next step?

Become part of our young and dynamic team at Bergstrom Elektro GmbH!

Send us your application documents very easily by email or contact us directly by phone. We look forward to meeting you!
