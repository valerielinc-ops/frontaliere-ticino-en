Do you match the profile?

You are strong from an initial experience on architectural/model-bulding issues of planning solutions. You master Anaplan and have a strong functional sensitivity. You also control data streams.

You are project oriented – agile methods and V-cycles.

Curious, you act with a view to improving and finding the optimal solution.

Analytic, you're comfortable with the numbers.

What do we expect from you?

Within the Technology & Data department and attached to the Senior Manager Data Engage & Planning Systems, you design, develop and maintain Anaplan models to support business planning and management processes, ensuring quality, reliability and value creation.

As part of your duties

· You are contributing to the roadmap projects. Thus, you participate in the framing phases and are the driving force for proposals on modeling choices and good Anaplan practices. · Participate in functional and technical workshops, contribute to cost estimates and deliverable planning. You also ensure alignment with existing modelling standards and governance. · You design and develop robust, efficient and scalable Anaplan models. In this sense, you build modules, lists, hierarchies, dashobards/UX and calculation logics as well as data integration flows. · You perform the tests, contribute to the recipe phases and document developments. · You analyze and correct anomalies and manage evolution requests. · You are involved in setting up a technological watch.
