Economist — Pictet Group

Full description

Your team

We are an independent investment partnership known for our long-term mindset, responsible approach to business, and entrepreneurial spirit. These principles have defined us since 1805. For our clients, colleagues and wider society, we always aim to do the right thing and honour our commitment to enduring quality.

Pictet opened its Tokyo office in 1981 to conduct research on the Japanese economy and equity market. It began providing asset management services to institutional investors in 1987 and entered the investment trust (mutual fund) business in 1997. As of the end of March 2026, the firm’s assets under management totaled 4.56 trillion yen.

Your role

We are seeking an economist to lead our global research and analysis on macroeconomics, monetary policy, interest rates, and foreign exchange.

Key responsibilities include preparing reports and presentation materials on macroeconomic and financial market developments; delivering presentations to retail and institutional investors, financial institutions, and the media; and providing clear explanations and interpretive support during client meetings.

The ideal candidate will possess strong analytical skills and excellent communication abilities, with the proven ability to convey complex information effectively in both English and Japanese.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct in-depth analysis of macroeconomic trends, monetary policy, interest rates, and foreign exchange in Japan and globally
  • Prepare reports and presentation materials for both internal and external stakeholders
  • Deliver presentations to retail and institutional investors, financial institutions, and the media
  • Provide clear explanations and interpretation support during client meetings

Your profile

  • Bachelor’s degree or higher
  • Minimum of 5 years of professional experience as an economist
  • Deep understanding of macroeconomics and financial markets
  • Native-level proficiency in Japanese and business-level or higher proficiency in English (reading, writing, and speaking)
  • Excellent report writing and presentation skills
  • Demonstrated experience in client-facing roles

Required Qualifications and Skills

  • Certified Member Analyst of the Securities Analysts Association of Japan (CMA) or Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA)
  • Bloomberg
