Full description

For BLS to function as smoothly as usual every day, it needs a thousand smaller and larger things—so there is no connection without the right goods. Admittedly, this may sound a little contrived at first glance. And yet: Our understanding of a modern mobility company is based on the conviction that nothing works without professional customs clearance.

This is what awaits you

  • Responsible for company-wide operational import and export processes
  • Create and check shipping, export and customs documents
  • Check customs, freight and VAT invoices
  • Ensure compliance with international customs rules and regulations
  • Work closely with freight forwarders, suppliers and internal departments
  • Create, complete and archive shipment-related customs dossiers

This is what you bring

  • Completed commercial training or comparable qualification
  • Further training in import/export/customs
  • Professional experience in operational customs processes (export/import)
  • Good SAP knowledge in operational processing (MM, SD)
  • Precise working style and excellent organizational skills
  • Languages: German & English

Your benefits – this is what you can look forward to

  • Get a GA 2. Great, a GA 1. Class or a half-fare travelcard.
  • Work wherever it suits you best. Whether working from home or in the office.
  • Arrange your working hours freely. We promote part-time work, top sharing and job sharing
  • You have the option of buying five to ten additional vacation days per year
  • Financial support for external training as well as an exciting internal range of learning and course offerings are available to you.
  • We support your health with seasonal events and courses.

With almost 4000 employees, BLS is Switzerland’s second-largest railway company. What’s more: We are also a shipping company, a real estate company, an international freight railway, a bus company and transport cars safely through the Lötschberg and Simplon. What unites us in all this? Pride in what we do every day. For us, mobility has many facets. And many exciting jobs.
