Job description Here you do something Without automatics, nothing works today. As an automatic engineer, you will be responsible for the development of the control software or electrical circuit diagrams of logistics systems and for the planning and realization of switchgear cabinets and electrical installations.At Swisslog, you will be part of the team during the apprenticeship in the realization, commissioning and maintenance of logistics systems. The basic training takes place at Libs in Baden. Our learning automatists go through several stations in their teaching. The tasks are broad and varied. In the 4 years you will go through various internships in addition to the various tasks in the automation department:

  • Basic education in the Libs in Baden
  • Maintenance with a Swisslog team on a logistics facility
  • Electrical installation of a logistics plant at a partner company Applicant profile This is what you bring with you
