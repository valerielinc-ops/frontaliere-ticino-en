Full description

The globally active Geberit Group is a leader in the field of sanitary products in Europe and celebrates its 150th anniversary in 2024. Geberit has a strong local presence in most European countries and can offer unique added value in both sanitary technology and bathroom ceramics. The production capacities include 26 production plants, 4 of which are overseas. The Group headquarters is located in Rapperswil-Jona in Switzerland and employs around 11,000 people in around 50 countries.

This results in a wide range of employment opportunities within the Geberit Group. Would you like to get to know us as an employer and work for us?

At Geberit, we believe in the potential of each individual and are always looking for talented professionals to join our team. If you believe that you could be an enrichment for our company but cannot find a suitable position in our current job offers, send us your initiative application. We will review your application and the available possibilities and contact you.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Fribourg?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Fribourg's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Fribourg's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
