Full description

The UKBB is the university centre of excellence for pediatric and adolescent medicine in northwestern Switzerland. Around 1,000 employees are committed every day with expertise, passion and passion for the well-being of children, young people and their families. Together we create the conditions for medical care at the highest level and actively shape the future of paediatric medicine. After many successful years, our long-term job owner is gradually retired. In order to facilitate the most pleasant transition and a good familiarization, we are looking for an engaged and qualified assistant to the Board of Directors (80-100%) for administrative support of our Chairman of the Executive Board (CEO) and the Board of Directors on 1 February 2027 or by agreement.

Entry Date: February 1, 2027 or by arrangement

Their tasks

  • They support and relieve the Board of Directors and the management in all organizational, administrative and coordination matters.
  • They shall manage the secretariat of the Board of Directors and senior management and shall ensure the smooth running of the business.
  • You are responsible for the demanding appointment management as well as the appointment and pending control for the Board of Directors and the management.
  • They organise, coordinate and record meetings of the Board of Directors and the Executive Board, including the collection and preparation of agendas and documents.
  • They maintain correspondence with internal and external stakeholders in a professional and stylish manner.
  • You independently take on confidential and demanding special tasks on behalf of the Board of Directors and the CEO.

Your profile

  • Basic commercial training with experience in health care or hospital administration
  • Further training as directorate assistant or comparable qualification is advantageous.
  • Several years of professional experience in a comparable assistant function at management or management level.
  • Strong organisational talent with a structured, forward-looking and independent working method.
  • High social competence as well as stylish appearance combined with discretion, loyalty and sense of responsibility.
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • English and French skills are beneficial.
  • Experienced handling of the common MS Office applications and digital work equipment.
  • Resilient, service-oriented personality who keeps an overview even in demanding situations.
