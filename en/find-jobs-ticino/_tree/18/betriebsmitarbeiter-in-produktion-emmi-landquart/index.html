Full description

From moulding to careful care in the cheese cellar: With us you take responsibility for important work steps in cheese production. Together with your team, you ensure that our products meet the highest quality standards every day and inspire our customers.

You can do that.

  • You shape our cheese carefully and efficiently into the fermentation hordes after the pressing process.
  • You carry out all work according to work instructions and document them correctly.
  • You take over cleaning work in the salt bath and drainage room according to the specified standards.
  • You recognize deviations in products, plants or processes and report them immediately.
  • You support the entire production operation and can be used flexibly in various work areas.
  • You make an important contribution to our high cheese quality and to our quality, environmental and safety standards.

Why do you succeed?

  • Ideally, you have experience in a production company, preferably in the food industry.
  • You are ready to perform physically demanding work.
  • You work reliably, carefully and with high quality awareness.
  • You are flexible and ready to work in 2-shift operation.
  • You have good knowledge of German in words and writing.
  • You know the importance of hygiene, quality and safety regulations and adhere to them consistently.

This is what we offer you

  • 5 weeks of vacation: Enjoy five weeks of vacation per year. From the age of 46, your holiday entitlement gradually increases to up to six weeks.
  • Attractive pension fund with flexible savings plans: Our Emmi pension foundation pays interest on your retirement balance above the legal minimum. Your contribution is based on your annual salary – without coordination deduction. Choose between standard or plus savings plan with 1% higher savings contributions.
  • Voluntary Success Sharing: Depending on the business success, you will receive a voluntary success Sharing. In doing so, we want to acknowledge your valuable contribution to the company’s success.
  • Further education and development opportunities: Emmi focuses on the further development of our employees: We offer talent programs, support training and further education and promote all skills for your professional growth. Find out more about our development offers here.
  • Discounts: Benefit from up to 20% discount on Emmi products, attractive leisure and travel discounts, special offers at top brands and fleet discounts when buying a car.
  • Free parking: We offer our employees free parking at the location.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Graubünden?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Graubünden's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Graubünden's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
