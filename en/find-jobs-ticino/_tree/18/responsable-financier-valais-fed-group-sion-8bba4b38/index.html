Financial Manager \- Valais

Our client, operating in a complex and demanding environment, is looking for a Financial Manager to strengthen its Financial Directorate.

Your responsibilities

\- Pilot all financial and analytical accounting. \- Supervise general accounting activities, suppliers, capital assets and billing. \- Develop multi-year budgets, forecasts and financial plans. \- Prepare reports and analyses to the Directorate and governance bodies. \- Work closely with Controlling and the various internal and external interlocutors. \- Manager a team of unit managers. \- Support the evolution of accounting processes and practices.

Your profile

Advanced Training in Finance/Accounting: Financial Expert, Accountant Expert or Federal Patent. \- Solid experience in a similar function, with a managerial experience. It is a common and good knowledge of German essentials. \- Excellent mastery of Swiss accounting standards. \- Leadership affirmed, ability to federate and support change. \- Excellent analytical, communication and writing skills.

We offer you

\- A strategic and highly accountable function. \- A stimulating and transformative environment. \- Real autonomy and proximity to the Directorate. \- Positioning ideally in January 2027\.
