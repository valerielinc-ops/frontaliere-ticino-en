Full description

For the temporary support of our Group Finance Team from January to August 2027, we are looking for an experienced financial personality who will provide valuable decision-making tools for the management and central stakeholders of the Bucherer Group.

Your role with us

  • Support of the monthly closing process as well as creation of management reports and performance analyses
  • Participation in budgeting, forecasting and long-term financial planning at group level
  • Advising executives through sound financial analyses, recommendations for action and the basis for decision-making

  • Management of investment controlling with focus on business cases, investment analyses and success checks
  • Further development and automation of reporting and planning processes as well as support for strategic projects and ad hoc analyses

This is what they bring with them.

  • Completed studies in controlling, accounting, finance or a comparable further education in finance
  • Several years of experience in FP&A, Controlling, Finance Business Partnering or Corporate Finance
  • Experience in the retail, luxury or consumer goods environment is an advantage
  • Sound understanding of budgeting, forecasting, reporting and marketing and investment controlling
  • Very good knowledge of Excel and experience with Power BI, Microsoft Dynamics 365 or comparable reporting tools
  • Strong analytical skills and a structured and solution-oriented way of working
  • Negotiable knowledge of German and English in word and writing
