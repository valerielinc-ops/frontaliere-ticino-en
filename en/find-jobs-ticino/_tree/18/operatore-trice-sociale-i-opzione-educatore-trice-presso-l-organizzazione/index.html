Full description

Class and annual salary (including 13th monthly)

6 68'456.-- / 107'475.--

Tasks

  • promotes and carries out educational activities aimed at the implementation of the programme, in a small group and/or individual relationship
  • maintains contacts with families to foster educational continuity and sharing of educational project objectives
  • participates in the decisions on new admissions and cooperates, after a first period of observation, to a synthesis of entry and to the elaboration of the therapeutic plan of intervention
  • on the cases of which it deals, prepares periodic synthesis to be discussed in team in order to verify the hypotheses of work and to update the therapeutic plan
  • permanently verify the work on the case with the team through regular updates and meetings
  • guarantees the correct compilation of performance and the management of the billing process

Requirements

  • bachelor of a professional university (SUP) in social work (option: educator/trice) or equivalent title
  • if the SEFRI (State Secretary for Education, Research and Innovation) has a foreign qualification
  • availability and motivation for multidisciplinary teamwork
  • multiannual experience and knowledge in the field of serious childhood psychopathology
  • aptitude for group work
  • knowledge of the territory, institutions and the social network of the Canton Ticino
  • perfect mastery (conversation and editing) of the Italian language and good knowledge (speaking and written) of another official language
