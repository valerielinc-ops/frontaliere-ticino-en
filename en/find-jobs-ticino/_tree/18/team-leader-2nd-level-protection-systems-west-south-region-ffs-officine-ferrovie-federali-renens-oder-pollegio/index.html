Team leader: in 2nd level security systems - West/South region 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Team leader: in 2nd level security systems - West/South region Renens or Pollegio, 01.02.2027 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You will lead a team that ensures technical 2nd level support for indoor and outdoor safety systems and thus makes a direct contribution to safe and reliable rail traffic. Together with specialists, they support troubleshooting and maintenance, monitor the plant condition and strengthen expertise through targeted training and knowledge transfer. In addition, you accompany projects with technical know-how and actively shape the further development of the security systems as well as the associated diagnostic and operating systems. Benefits such as free GA, discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under Benefits. You can move that.

  • As team leader, you will take over the technical and personnel management of the team’s 2nd level security systems for the West and South regions. You promote your employees, develop their skills in a targeted manner and create a motivating working environment.
  • In everyday life, you are responsible for operational 2nd-level support and ensure that the space teams are effectively supported in the event of disruptions and maintenance issues. You attach importance to efficient troubleshooting, systematic root cause analyses and sustainable improvements.
  • You plan, coordinate and prioritize human resources and operational tasks. You always keep an eye on plant availability, operational reliability and cost-effectiveness.
  • You promote the development of knowledge and the professional exchange within the team as well as across the regional borders. At the same time, you actively shape the further development of the 2nd level and are committed to uniform processes, clear responsibilities and efficient working methods.
  • You work closely with the RMO team (Middle/East region), the area teams and the internal and external partners. Together with your leadership colleague RMO, you promote cross-regional cooperation and create a nationally coordinated 2nd level with uniform standards, clear responsibilities and a common understanding of our tasks.

You bring that with you.

  • You convince through strong social skills, communication strength and joy in the leadership and development of employees. Also in change processes you give orientation and create trust.
  • Through your career so far, you have gained leadership experience or have the potential and motivation to take on a leadership function. You work structured, independent and entrepreneurial.
  • You quickly recognize complex technical and organizational contexts, set clear priorities and make well-founded and solution-oriented decisions even in demanding situations.
  • You will bring several years of professional experience and profound technical knowledge in the area of security systems, especially in operation, maintenance and troubleshooting.
  • As far as your education is concerned, you have a higher technical education (HF, FH, university or equivalent), preferably in electrical engineering or automation.
  • You have good knowledge of German, French and Italian, appreciate the cross-linguistic and cross-regional cooperation and are ready to work regularly at different locations in Switzerland.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
