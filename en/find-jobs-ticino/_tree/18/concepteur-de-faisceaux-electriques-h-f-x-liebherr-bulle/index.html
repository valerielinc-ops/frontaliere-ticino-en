Full description

Your missions

  • You are responsible for the design and optimization of electric beams for combustion engines
  • You prepare technical presentations and define, in collaboration with the E/E design team, the topology of electrical beams and the technological solutions to be implemented
  • You realize and manage the 2D planes and establish the list of parts of the electrical beams
  • You provide support in the PLM system (creation of the parts list, life cycle management and validation process)
  • You create and update the technical documentation and component specifications
  • You plan, perform and evaluate testing as part of the process of validation of prototypes and serial products
  • You collaborate with the various departments involved in the projects as well as with the component suppliers in order to clarify the technical characteristics of the components and to contribute to the resolution of manufacturing issues Your profile
  • You have an automatic CFC, electronics or mechatronics, or equivalent technician training. You justify several years of experience in the design of electric beams
  • You must have strong knowledge of Zuken E3. Mastering PTC CREO Cabling and the Albatross PLM system is an asset
  • You demonstrate autonomy, commitment and an excellent sense of organization. Flexible and stress-resistant, you enjoy working in a multicultural environment
  • You stand out for your innovation spirit and results-oriented approach
  • You are willing to work for defense application products
  • You have perfect command of French.
