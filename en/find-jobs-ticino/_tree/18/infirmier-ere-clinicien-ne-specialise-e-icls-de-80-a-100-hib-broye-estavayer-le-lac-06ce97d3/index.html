Full description

Through its 800 collaborators, the Intercantonal Hospital of the Broye (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation, and its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the Broyarde population. To strengthen the health care teams of the treatment and rehabilitation centre (CTR) d, Estavayer-le-Lac, the care management of the HIB is in search of:

Specialty Clinician Nurse (ICLS) from 80% to 100%

Your mission

The RTC has 47 hospital beds and is organized in two sectors. Its mission is to provide rehabilitation (musculoskeletal, geriatric and internal medicine-oncology) for adult patients in the Broye region. The service operates closely with the whole medical-therapeutic plateau and institutional multidisciplinary actors. L,care team is composed of about 35 EFAs under the supervision of two ICUS.

Your tasks

  • Perform a 40-50% clinical practice directly with patients
  • Evaluate and analyse complex care situations and propose the necessary actions
  • Manage institutional clinical projects under the supervision of the care management
  • Promote the integration of evidence into clinical practice to the benefit of quality, safety and efficiency of care
  • Provide clinical supervision supporting and responsible for the members of the care team
  • Contribute to the development of the skills of the team, the reorganization of the service and the

Supporting Change

  • Defining and monitoring clinical performance indicators for nursing
  • Participating in the implementation of the institutional strategy
  • Stimulating reflection on future projects and innovation in care

Your assets

  • You are benefiting from a Master's degree in Nursing and, if possible, from an experience in geriatric hospital care and/or rehabilitation.
  • Proactive in nature, your technical, organizational and relational skills allow you to offer quality care.
  • With a common sense of communication, you have a strong interest in quality care, multidisciplinary collaboration.
  • You are a person who is a person of understanding, of real clinical leadership and creativity.
  • Excellent mastery of French (spoken and written), scientific English and computer tools are essential.

Start: immediately or to be agreed.
