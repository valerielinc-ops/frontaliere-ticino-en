pad machinist:in / roller guide:in Your contribution with us

  • You serve modern pavers, rollers and other construction machines in topping installation
  • Together with your team, you ensure the precise and high-quality installation of asphalt coverings
  • You are actively involved in road and civil engineering work
  • You prepare your machine independently, set it up and check it before use
  • You take care of the maintenance, care and value of your machine
  • You bring your ideas and contribute to a smooth construction site process
  • You work consistently according to our safety and quality standards

What matters to us

  • You have an education as a road builder: in or practical experience in surface, road or civil engineering
  • Experience with pavers or rolls is a plus. Motivated young professionals: women with a technical flair are also welcome
  • You are enthusiastic about large machines, technology and working outdoors
  • You work reliably, independently and like to take on responsibility
  • Quality and safety are important to you
  • You are team-oriented, ready for action and keep an overview even on intense days
  • You have the driving licence category B
  • You can communicate well in German

Our added value for you

  • A modern machine park and contemporary work equipment
  • Varied construction sites and projects that you can be proud of
  • A collaborative team that holds together and passes on knowledge
  • A thorough training and support by experienced professionals
  • Clear development opportunities, further education and the opportunity to take on more responsibility
  • Attractive employment conditions, short decision-making paths and a family working environment
