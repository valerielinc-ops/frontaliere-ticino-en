Full description

Are you an organizational talent, do you love variety and would you like to get an insight into different areas? Then a way-up training as a businessman / woman in the service and administration industry is just the right thing for you! Have you just passed your Matura? Or will you do this soon? During the shortened two-year basic education, you will acquire the tools for a successful entry into the professional world as well as a federal certificate of ability.

Your education

  • As a Way-up merchant, you are responsible for handling numerous operational processes.
  • Depending on the location and task area, you write emails, letters, protocols and contracts, create reports and statistics, carry out analyses or optimize workflows.
  • In various practical assignments you get to know the versatility of the large company Post.
  • You will gain insight into varied and exciting topics such as personnel, marketing, finance, logistics and organization.

You bring that with you

We are looking for young people with enthusiasm, joy and motivation who want to make a difference. The following points should be met:

  • You have just acquired your high school maturity or will soon do so.
  • You are ready to perfect two foreign languages.
  • Dealing with people and working in a team gives you pleasure.
  • You are happy to work on the laptop and can express yourself verbally and in writing well.
  • You are reliable, honest and responsible. Careful and independent work is a matter of course for you.
  • In addition, you have a quick apprehension and a flair for numbers.
