Full description

Bell Schweiz AG, a leading food brand in Switzerland, offers you the ideal entry into professional life with an apprenticeship as an automatic technician. Supported by us, you will become part of the Bell Learning community and start your career in an exciting environment.

  • Contract type: apprenticeship
  • Pensum: 100%
  • Job opening: 01.08.2027
  • Language: German
  • Location: Bell Schweiz AG, Alsace Road 174 4056 Basel

Tasks

  • We owe to you that after the coin insertion, the selected product falls into the dispenser of the beverage vending machine. They ensure that automatic sliding doors open at the right time. And they make sure that in the sunshine the stores close automatically. Automatic specialists work in a wide variety of industries.
  • During your teaching, you will get in-depth insights into control programming, energy distribution, electrical machinery construction, industrial automation and process engineering. You will learn how to build or optimize electrical, mechanical or pneumatic devices, machines, plants and automation systems. You take care of their commissioning and carry out maintenance work. With the support of experts, you will work on exciting and educational projects and find suitable solutions for control and automation tasks. Furthermore, you participate in planning and monitoring production processes and create technical documents.

Requirements

  • Completed elementary school middle or upper level
  • Talent for technical drawing and craftsmanship
  • Good comprehension and pleasure in systematic-logical thinking
