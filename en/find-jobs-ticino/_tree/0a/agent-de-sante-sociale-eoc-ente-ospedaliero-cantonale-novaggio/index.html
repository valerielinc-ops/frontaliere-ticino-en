Healthcare worker/woman - Ausschreibung L’EOC the multisite Ticino hospital is present with its institutes on the whole cantonal territory for a total of 1’000 beds. The organization allows to effectively combine local approach and overall vision, ensuring the population a global and proximity hospital offer, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 5,000 employees and their attention to the human relationship, EOC provides quality health care to 380,000 patients per year. The following position is addressed to candidates wishing to apply spontaneously to the Nursing Service team at the EOC Rehabilitation Clinic (CREOC) for Novaggio. In case of availability of employment, candidates with profiles that meet the requirements will be contacted for a first interview. The following position is addressed to candidates who wish to apply spontaneously as a Healthcare Worker/Profile The future/cooperator has the knowledge and functional and technical skills to do their work obtaining high quality results; it has the ease of learning new techniques and is interested in innovations; it can work independently and uses its time effectively and efficiently.
