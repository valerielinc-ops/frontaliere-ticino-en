Tasks

The Heimberg School continues to develop its MR area with great commitment, expertise and a clear vision. For this purpose, we are looking for school curative pedagogues who are trained immediately or by agreement and who are not only looking for a job, but also want to help shape school. People who empower children and adolescents with special needs, recognize their resources and enable them to truly participate. People who contribute their professional experience while remaining curious. And people who want to go new ways together with us.

Thinking ahead with school – shaping together Our MR area is in an exciting development phase. We want to strengthen the proven and at the same time have the courage to rethink support and promotion. Since the school year 2026/27 we offer, among other things, an offer in horse-based curative education for students with special needs. At the same time, our structured psychomotor skills have successfully established themselves at school. For these and other developments, we are looking for specialists who enjoy contributing their knowledge, taking on responsibility and shaping a strong and future-oriented MR area together with others.

Requirements

What you bring Ideally, you have a recognised education in curative curative education and enjoy the integrative promotion of students with different learning and development requirements.

We would also like to see

  • A clear stance in favour of integration, participation and equal opportunities
  • Joy of working closely with class teachers and other specialists
  • Openness to new pedagogical paths and forms of support
  • Interest in professional and personal development
  • Willingness to take responsibility and actively shape development
  • Resilience, solution orientation and a good dose of humor

Also teachers with profound experience in the MR area and the clear willingness to a corresponding professional qualification are welcome to contact us.

We offer

What to expect from us With us, you will come across an MR area in which you can not simply take on an existing task, but think, develop and shape it.

We offer you

  • a dedicated and motivated MR team
  • a close and binding cooperation with the respective class teams
  • Technical advice and on-site support
  • a school management that enables development, shares responsibility and strengthens employees
  • Space for your own ideas, initiative and focus areas
  • innovative pedagogical approaches and construction work with real creative scope
  • Opportunities for personal and professional development
  • An appreciative togetherness in which humor also has its permanent place

Our common goal is to accompany our students with special educational needs on their way to more independence, self-efficacy and personal development in the best possible way. At the same time, we want to further develop the technically strong, innovative and clearly integrative MR area.
