HELIKOPTER – DISPONENT:IN 80% - 100%

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for an immediately or by agreement in Zermatt and Raron:

YOUR TASKS

  • You work at the front - daily customer support
  • Receiving client orders
  • Planning and organisation of tourist and commercial contracts
  • Assistance in fleet and flight planning and coordination of helicopters
  • Invoicing of flight operations and further administrative work

Your profile

  • Completed vocational apprenticeship with EFZ, preferably in the commercial sector or a corresponding further education of advantage
  • Open, friendly and communicative personality
  • Very good knowledge of dealing with MS-Office
  • Responsibility and organisational talent
  • Resilience and flexibility
  • Ability to set priorities
  • Geographical knowledge of the Upper Valais region
  • native German with good oral and written knowledge in English and French
  • Willingness to work irregular hours and weekend assignments

We offer

  • A collaborative team and a motivating working atmosphere
  • Exciting benefits from our strong partnerships
  • An activity with high responsibility and varied daily routine
  • Training opportunities

What are you waiting for? Take advantage of the opportunity to become part of Air Zermatt AG. Send us your application documents by e-mail to personal@air-zermatt.ch or apply directly online via our website.
