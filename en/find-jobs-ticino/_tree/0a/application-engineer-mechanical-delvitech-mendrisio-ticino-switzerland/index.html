Full description

Bring mechanical expertise to Delvitech's AI inspection machines: alignment, calibration and troubleshooting with customers and our R&D team. Job Summary The Application Engineer R&D ensures structured technical support for the Application Engineers in the field, while managing escalation processes, training programs and knowledge consolidation. The role acts as the operational interface between Field Applications, R&D and the PMO, with full ownership of product operational readiness across both software and hardware domains, and ensures clear and structured communication of software and hardware releases to all relevant stakeholders. This position is for a profile with a mechanical or mechatronic background. Key Responsibilities

  • Field support and escalation: manage technical escalations from Application Engineers through Jira workflows, perform technical triage, classify issues by root cause and coordinate with R&D to resolve them.
  • Installations: support complex installations and troubleshooting activities.
  • Hardware and system ownership: own the operational knowledge and support processes related to product hardware, act as reference point for hardware-related issues and field feedback, and ensure alignment between hardware and software behavior at system level.
  • Release communication: coordinate and communicate software and hardware releases to Application Engineering, PMO, Sales and Operations, ensuring release notes are clear, complete and aligned, and support deployment readiness in the field.
  • Training: develop and maintain technical training programs for Application Engineers, deliver onboarding and advanced product training, and improve their competency framework.
  • Knowledge management: maintain the product knowledge base in Confluence, document troubleshooting guides and best practices, and consolidate field cases and lessons learned. Requirements
  • Degree or diploma in Mechanical or Mechatronic Engineering.
  • Ability to read mechanical drawings and tolerances; experience with 3D CAD (SolidWorks or equivalent).
  • Experience in mechanical troubleshooting and alignment.
  • Experience in technical support or application engineering.
  • Ability to deliver technical training.
  • Structured problem solving and cross-functional coordination skills.
  • Fluent English. Nice to Have
  • Experience with machine vision, AOI, automatic machines, conveyors or linear motion.
  • Calibration of optical or mechanical systems.
  • Experience with tools such as Jira and Confluence.
  • Italian or German. What We Offer A permanent position in a fast-growing technology company that develops its own AI-based optical inspection systems, with strong opportunities for professional growth as the company expands.

Do I need the G permit to apply for this role in Ticino as an Italian cross-border worker?

Yes. For EU/EFTA citizens the G permit no longer depends on a border zone: you need residence in an EU/EFTA state, a Swiss contract and a return home at least once a week. The 20 km band matters for taxation, not for the permit. The Swiss employer files the G-permit application at the Ticino cantonal migration office after the contract is signed — first issuance takes 2-6 weeks. The permit lasts five years with an open-ended contract or one over a year, otherwise for the length of the contract.
