Full description

Your tasks

  • As a member of our interdisciplinary treatment team, you look after patients in the inpatient setting with dependency diseases and comorbid disorders and accompany them as part of their psychiatric treatment.
  • After a well-founded familiarization, you will take on case-leading tasks as well as individual psychotherapeutic work and group therapies with patients in close cooperation with our interdisciplinary team.
  • Your varied day-to-day work offers you insight into the entire spectrum of psychiatric diseases and thus enables you to gain diverse professional experience.
  • As an assistant psychologist, you coordinate the treatments, plan the appropriate therapies and carry out psychological evaluations.
  • In addition to your participation in rapports, case discussions and training, you maintain a close exchange with the other treaters and ensure careful documentation. Your profile
  • With a completed psychology degree, you have the necessary professional qualification, whereby a foreign degree requires recognition by the PsyKo.
  • Your Swiss-approved psychotherapy training is already advanced and thus forms an important basis for your further professional development.
  • For your work, you have a psychotherapy assistant permit from the Canton of St. Gallen for work under supervision or meet the necessary prerequisites to obtain it before starting your job.
  • Independent work is just as important to you as an open-minded and sensitive approach to patients, while at the same time you value cooperation in a committed team.
  • Very good knowledge of German at least level C1 round off your profile and enable you to secure and professional communication in everyday work. With around 1,400 employees, we provide accessible, personal and effective psychiatric treatment and care for adults in the cantons of St.Gallen and Appenzell Ausserrhoden as well as in the Principality of Liechtenstein at our nine company locations. We are on the road digitally and are committed to sustainability, so we look forward to your online application via our homepage. As soon as your application has been received by us online, you will receive a confirmation of receipt by e-mail. We are happy to get to know you based on your dossier and check whether your qualifications, skills and you as a person fit the advertised position and the psychiatry St.Gallen. Then we will contact you. During a personal interview we get to know each other and you will learn more about the psychiatry St.Gallen, the advertised position and the challenges. In the second round we would like to get an even better picture of you and find out together with you whether we fit together. You also get the opportunity to get to know your future team. You will receive a telephone confirmation from us if we decide for you after the second interview and ask you if you are still convinced of us. If we agree, we will draw up your contract. In the following days we will send you the contract by post in double. The contract also provides you with relevant information about your employment. Between the acceptance of the job and the first working day, you as a new employee can already access relevant internal information. So you can get a differentiated picture of the company and already prepare for the job. On the first day you can expect an onboarding in your new team and with other colleagues. You set up at the new workplace and get an overview of your next tasks. You also get your individual introduction plan, which helps you to find your way around quickly.
