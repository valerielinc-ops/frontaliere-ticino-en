Dietetic Cook

  • Job Posting EOC, the multi-site hospital of Ticino, is present with its institutions throughout the entire cantonal territory for a total of 1,000 beds. The organization effectively combines a local approach with an overall vision, ensuring the population has access to a comprehensive and nearby hospital service, regardless of where the services are required. Thanks to the commitment and expertise of over 6,500 collaborators and their focus on human relationships, EOC ensures quality healthcare, caring for over 41,000 inpatients each year and providing over 600,000 outpatient consultations. To complete our hospitality team at the Regional Hospital of Mendrisio, we are looking for a permanent Dietetic Cook (80%). Profile: The future collaborator possesses excellent technical and professional skills. They carry out all operational activities in the sector in compliance with defined programs, regulations, and work procedures. They effectively organize their work by setting priorities. They are a person who is available, change-oriented, and able to respond to challenges. They handle significant workloads promptly and seriously, maintaining good relationships in a multicultural context. Requirements:
  • Federal certificate of capacity as a dietetic cook.
  • Several years of professional experience in a similar position in medium/large private or public organizations.
  • Excellent knowledge of current hygiene regulations and procedures (HACCP).
  • Availability for flexible work shifts (7/7 and evenings).
  • Autonomy, flexibility, initiative, reliability, and creativity.
  • Excellent knowledge of the Italian language, spoken and written; knowledge of other national languages is desirable.
  • Good knowledge of major IT tools (Office package). Start date: May 1, 2026, or to be agreed. Submission of applications and information: Applications, complete with a CV, cover letter, and copies of study and work certificates, must be submitted online by March 5, 2026 (website www.eoc.ch). Applications received after this date will not be considered, nor will those that do not meet the requirements or those sent in paper form or via email. Further information can be requested from Mr. Giuseppe Maruca, Head of the hospitality service at +41 (0)91 811 31 60 or Mr. Carlo Giovio, Head Chef at +41 (0)91 811 31 83.
