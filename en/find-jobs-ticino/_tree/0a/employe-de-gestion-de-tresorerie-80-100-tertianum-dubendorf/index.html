The Tertianum Group stands for housing and living in old age at a high level. As the Swiss market leader, we aim to make the lives of senior citizens worth living every day with our around 6,400 employees in order to enable them to enjoy life. With our individually tailored services, we create a new home in over 100 locations across all phases of life in old age that offers a safe and self-determined life. What drives us every day is our respect and sense of responsibility for older people. Time is moving – so are we. The Tertianum Group is in a change process.
