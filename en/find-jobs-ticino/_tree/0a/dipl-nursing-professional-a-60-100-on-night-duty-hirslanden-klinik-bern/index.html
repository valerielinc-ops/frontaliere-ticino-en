Location: Hirslanden Klinik Beau-Site | Bern

Occupation by appointment

Type of employment: Permanent

Reference number: 49625

As the largest medical network in Switzerland, we set standards with our clinics, outpatient surgery centers and emergency departments. First-class medical quality and the well-being of people are our focus.

We are looking for you to support our night service team in the fields of cardiac, thoracic, visceral and oncology.

YOUR TASKS

  • Professional care and care of patients
  • Implementation of medical regulations and participation in preventive, diagnostic and therapeutic measures
  • High independence and responsibility in clinical decision-making and the measures to be initiated
  • Accompaniment of learners and students of nursing training

Your profile

  • Training as a Dipl. Nursing specialist HF / FH (for foreign diploma with SRK recognition)
  • Professional experience in acute hospital desirable
  • For this function in the night service you bring a lot of independence and self-responsibility

In addition to an attractive workplace in the heart of Bern, a well-established and dynamic team awaits you, which is looking forward to your support. You can also expect progressive employment conditions, attractive and extensive training and development opportunities as well as very good social benefits from us. Part-time employees also have the opportunity to conclude attractive flex agreements. For the optimal work-life balance, we offer you the opportunity to benefit from up to seven weeks of holidays with our "Leisure Plus model".

As an employee who has to move completely for his/her work, you will receive four additional paid days off with a 100% workload. With a reduced workload, the days off reduce accordingly.

As a Hirslanden employee, benefit from the following benefits!

For additional information, Melanie Schirk, Head of Care Services, is available at T, or Amina Prada, HR recruitment partner, at raphael.brand@hirslanden.ch. We look forward to your contact.

Please note that for data protection reasons we cannot consider paper dossiers (will not be returned) nor applications by email. Therefore, please apply exclusively online via this tool.
