Vehicle body repair technician (m/f/d | 100%)

HESS - The Swiss pioneer in vehicle construction

Carrosserie Rothenburg is a branch of HESS AG, one of Switzerland's leading vehicle manufacturers, and offers a varied working environment. In Rothenburg, our customers have access to a comprehensive range of repair and service options for vehicles of all kinds – from buses and commercial vehicles to passenger cars and camper vans. Our work orders are as diverse as the vehicles themselves.

To complement our Services division, we are looking for a qualified vehicle body repair technician for the Carrosserie Rothenburg branch (LU).

Do you have a passion for vehicles and would like to bring your expertise to our passenger car and commercial vehicle world? Then this is your opportunity! Apply today and become part of our committed team in Rothenburg (LU).

Your responsibilities

  • Independent and professional execution of a wide range of repair work on passenger cars, buses and commercial vehicles
  • Carrying out welding, straightening, dent removal, assembly and restoration work
  • Dismantling, repairing and assembling bodywork parts
  • Carrying out varied and challenging bodywork tasks

Our requirements

  • Completed vocational training as a vehicle body repair technician EFZ or another vocational qualification in a metalworking profession
  • Several years of professional experience is an advantage
  • High level of independence and customer orientation
  • Ability to work in a team, commitment and initiative
  • Motivated to learn new things

We offer you

  • Interesting, varied and challenging work
  • Flexible working hours
  • Performance-based employment conditions
  • Opportunities for training and further development
