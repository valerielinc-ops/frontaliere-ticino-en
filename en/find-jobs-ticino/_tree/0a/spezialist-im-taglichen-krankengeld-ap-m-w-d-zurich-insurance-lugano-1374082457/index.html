Full description

137334 | 80 - 100% | Lugano, Ticino

Are you fascinated by the health and social insurance system? Do you already work in the insurance sector of daily sickness and/or accident allowances or do you want to immerse yourself in this exciting specialist area? Do you have a talent for administrative tasks, speak French fluently and communicate in Italian and German in everyday work?

As the first contact point for all cases in Switzerland of insurance benefits in the area of daily sickness and accident benefits, the Zurich Switzerland support team ensures that cases are quickly registered in the system and managed directly in the defined segments. You will work at the Lugano regional headquarters in a dynamic environment and within a motivated team, which every day provides a top-notch service to our business customers.

If you have a strong ability to think interconnectedly, a quick understanding and if the team spirit, customer orientation and operational autonomy are among your strengths, then we are looking for you!

What awaits you in this role

  • You are responsible for managing cases relating to daily allowance insurance in case of illness in French language, ensuring proper performance processing.
  • Check insurance coverage and calculate daily allowances.
  • Collect written and telephone information from our customers throughout Switzerland in French.
  • Insert new performance cases into the system.
  • Within the team communicates in German, Italian and French.

What makes you stand out

  • Completed commercial or medical training or equivalent qualification
  • Interconnected thinking and rapid understanding
  • Teamwork-oriented staff with high availability and flexibility
  • Great motivation, initiative and enthusiasm
  • French as a native language or excellent language mastery (level C1/C2)
  • Knowledge of German and Italian (level B2)
