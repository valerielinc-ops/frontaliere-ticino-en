Full description

University Clinic for Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Psychotherapy

Consultation hours for refugee minors, Ambulatorium Bern Would you like to make a decisive contribution to psychiatric and psychotherapeutic care in the Canton of Bern? Our team at the Ambulatorium Bern is committed to young people and their families who are in difficult phases of life. We take care of a wide range of problem areas that burden children and young people as well as their families. In addition to basic care, including triage, diagnosis and treatment, we work closely with facilities for unaccompanied minor refugees. During this consultation, we provide care for young people who have just arrived in Switzerland or already have a chance to stay. Many of them show pronounced psychiatric symptoms. We treat these young people in the acute crisis and also afterwards. The focus is on a trauma therapy approach, which we consistently pursue and implement. In close cooperation with the teams on site at the centers, we are making an important contribution to the care of this vulnerable group. Are you interested in working with a group of patients who face many adversities, burdens and discrimination and do you want to ensure that they still have access to psychiatric care? Then apply with us.

Your tasks

  • You will conduct child and adolescent psychological diagnosis and psychotherapy as well as systemic interventions in the outpatient area
  • You will conduct initial interviews, crisis interventions and psychotherapy with refugees and work with the housing institutions
  • You will keep the electronic medical history, document the course and write all relevant reports both internally and externally.
  • With advanced training, interest and suitability, you can take over the professional guidance of psychologists in training
  • You have the opportunity for personal further education through participation in lectures, conferences, seminars, courses, training courses and subject-specific supervision

Your profile

  • You are in the middle of psychotherapy training or have already completed it
  • Ideally, you will bring previous experience in psychotherapy with migrants
  • You are distinguished by your interest and commitment to social psychiatric issues
  • You like to work according to evidence-based guidelines and show openness to academic work in the university setting
  • You bring flexibility regarding different clinical tasks and teamwork makes you happy
  • You bring very good German language skills

The UPZ

The Universitäre Psychiatrische Zentrum Bern (UPZ) is the leading centre of excellence for psychiatry and psychotherapy in the capital region. It ensures psychiatric care in the Canton of Bern along the entire supply chain, from early diagnosis to outpatient, day-care and inpatient therapies to rehabilitation and forensics. With more than 30 locations, the UPZ offers a comprehensive supply network. In addition, the UPZ makes an important contribution in the areas of teaching, training and further education as well as research.
