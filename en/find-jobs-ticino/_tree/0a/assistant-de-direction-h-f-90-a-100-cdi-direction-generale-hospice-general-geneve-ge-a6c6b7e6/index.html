Full description

Your Mission

Within the Directorate General Secretariat, you provide high-level support to the Deputy Director General and the Secretary General. You facilitate their daily lives, the flow of information and the monitoring of files within their perimeters. You contribute to the smooth functioning of the Board of Directors (CA) and the Audit Committee, support the Chair of the Board and ensure administrative support to the Internal Audit Service, while respecting its independence. You also organize seminars and institutional sessions. Proactive and reliable, you anticipate needs, follow deadlines and alert when the situation requires it. Your main activities

  • Provides secretariat to the Deputy Director General and the Secretary General: calendars, correspondence, mail, invoices, fee notes and current administrative follow-up;
  • Organize meetings of the Board of Directors and the Audit Committee: preparation of files, coordination of participants, minutes, signatures, documentation, follow-up of decisions and deadlines;
  • Provide administrative support to the Chair of the Board, coordination with the Directors and administrative support to the Internal Audit Service; contribute, as appropriate, to governance files (e.g. committees, powers and powers, review body);
  • Organize executive meetings, seminars of the Directorate-General and other institutional events; prepare materials and ensure the availability and archiving of documentation;
  • To ensure the administrative link with the guardianship department and with other government departments and services for cases within the scope of the post;
  • Anticipate needs, identify attention points, perform useful restarts and alert or request validation when the situation requires.

Your profile

  • Maturity supplemented by commercial or equivalent training - Management assistant training, an asset;
  • At least 5 years of experience in a management secretariat, ideally with an exposure to governance bodies, committees or institutional events;
  • Good understanding or strong interest in the functioning of the cantonal public administration and its institutions;
  • Very good writing convenience and ability to quickly produce clear, accurate and appropriate writings;
  • Excellent mastery of computer and collaborative tools, including Microsoft 365, and ease in information and documentation management;
  • Public service sense, discretion and discernment in the processing of confidential information;
  • High capacity for anticipation and prioritization, sense of initiative, reliability in monitoring and ability to quickly understand issues beyond the administrative task.

We offer you

  • Continuous skills development through attractive training policy;
  • Opportunities for career development and internal mobility;
  • A work-life balance - privacy;
  • Many cultural and sporting activities to live together within the institution.
